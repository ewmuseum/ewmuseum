I Will Not Tolerate This Prejudice.

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/03/02 Xavier feels insulted. Scientology is an important part of his life and along with other such celebrities as Tom Cruise and John Travolta he feels it is his duty to spread the word of the minority religion to the viewing public. Until Wednesday he had the support of the PCW fans which was noticeably raising the numbers of followers, but then he threw it all away. To make things worse, a new religion has sprung up in Primetime Championship Wrestling, his former partner the Rush and the Church of Crunk. The emergence of this rather questionable faith has caused Xavier to take extreme actions, feeling that PCW President Duke Willaims is sanctioning Crunk to spite Kannon for attacking his friend Jeremy Howard, Xavier has taken his protests to the Senate and has not found the agency he needs to punish PCW. We open up at the steps of a glistening corporate tower in Washington DC where Wrestling’s only Scientologist is located after protesting the Senate to oppose the newly found religion of “Crunk” invented by his former tag team partner the Rush, who is also the man he will face on Sunday along with the End. Xavier : “Well if the US Government has any sense then they will bury Crunk before it’s foul tide infects the mind of this nation’s already tainted youth....But then again they are the same Government that has tried to jail clean living Scientologists and even had the nerve to indite L Ron Hubbard before his death so the future of the world doesn’t look very bright.” As he speaks, Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon makes his way into the shot along with his preppy valet Eleanor Hall. Xavier : “But enough about what is past and what is to be decided on....And let us juxtapose ourselves into the present where I, Xavier Kannon, Gold Patron Meritorious in the religion of Scientology shall punish PCW for their actions of oppression.” The camera looks away from the 1st couple of Scientology to show the sign out the from of the building that reads “Office of Religious Prejudice”, inscribed into a pale Grey stone slab by the side of the stairs. Kannon is wearing a full length Brown leather coat that rests on top of the step above his feet. Under it he has an open Black silk shirt, falling open to reveal a Black t-shirt with “Asylum” written across it in Purple as an insult to the former members and a reminder of what he has destroyed. With it he has a pair of his Black jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. Xavier : “Finally I have found a group of people who will understand my angst....Who’s job it is to listen to the stories of oppression and prejudice that I have to face everyday from other wrestlers, my bosses and the world itself.....This building will set all the wrongs perpetrated upon me right again.” Eleanor : “Right.......A.......Gain!” Eleanor is wearing a Pink skinny fit t-shirt with Snapple~! in While lettering across her chest. With it she has a pair of tight Blue jeans with Silver sequinned stars down the legs. He long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is un-tied and bounces with natural volume with each step. They begin to walk up the steps, Xavier sporting the smug grin that seems to have been stuck on his face ever since he turned his back on Jeremy Howard thus putting an end to Asylum, PCW’s longest running stable. Xavier : “I am feeling at home already....Maybe when I retire from wrestling after completing the sweep of PCW Titles I will apply for a job at this place because lets think about it, I have seen with my own eyes what narrow mindedness goes on up and down the country.” The Scientologist opens the door for Ellie~! then follows her into the grand foyer bearing banners from every religion under the sun. He looks around the scene in front of him in awe. Xavier : “Wow....I’m impressed!” He walks towards the desk, turning through 360 degrees as he does so, gazing up at the walls and the lavishly decorated ceiling. Xavier : “It’s good to see all my tax dollars are going to good causes.” They approach the desk where the young receptionist wearing a navy Blue skirt sit and White shirt is dealing with a deluge of calls. Receptionist : “Office of Religious Prejudice....Hold please. Office of Religious prejudice....Hold Please. Office of Religious Prejudice....Hold Please....” Looking over the desk it is seen that all the lines are busy and glowing with an Orange light so she looks up to Xavier and Ellie~! Receptionist : “Ah, Mr Kannon, we have been expecting you....One of out advisors is ready to see you now so if you would like to take the corridor to your left then he is waiting in Room LC108.” Kannon and Ellie~! walk around the desk and follow the corridor, counting off the numbers on the doors. Eleanor : “So what exactly do you want to accomplish here today? Do you think they will ban all the other religions in the world so Scientology will be the only one left and you can rule the world?” As the Gold Patron Meritorious listens to her words a look of agreement crosses his face, thinking that her suggestion just might work before he rapidly shakes his head to get the thought out of his system. Xavier : “Well.....I was thinking more of getting them to put enough pressure on Duke for the religious prejudice and then exchange any cash settlement they might offer me out of court to default my match on Sunday and declare me the winner....Just something reasonable like that.” Ellie~! listens and nods in agreement at Xavi’s more obtainable desire. Eleanor : “Oh...Okay...Reasonable......Like......That!” They get to the door with LC108 in Gold lettering on and he knocks, then waits patiently for a reply. Voice : “Yes.....” Kannon opens the door and steps into the office. It is only around 20 feet squared but has enough room for a desk, sofa, chars, rug and wall to wall shelving that houses numerous legal volumes dealing with Religious Prejudice and the law. Man : “Mr Kannon, Miss Hall....” Behind the desk, and in front of the floor to ceiling windows looking out onto the Washington street stands a man of around 5’9 with a slight build, weaselly face and a Brown suit. Man : “My name is Kent MacNeill and I am an expert in Minority Religions, Oppression of Religious Minorities and how best to negotiate fall outs.” Xavier closes the door behind Eleanor and they walk in, standing in front of the Green leather sofa, impressed with the mans credentials. Xavier : “I am Xavier Kannon, Gold Patron Meritorious in the Church of Scientology, I am oppressed by my employers, my co-workers and the people they allow to frequent my work place....But I think you might be able to change all that...” Kent grins. Kent : “Well take a seat then...” The 1st couple of Scientology sit on the Green sofa as Kent relaxes back into his large Black leather swivel chair and turns a dictaphone on. Kent : “Okay, well start from the beginning and explain what is mailing you feel so oppressed in the work place.” Kannon clears his throat and composes him self, allowing a look of pity to drop down his face, playing the innocent party. Xavier : “Well it started on day one, but has been getting worse all week. First off, my employers actually asked if Scientology was a real religion! I mean, can you believe that?” Eleanor : “You......Believe.......That?” Kent listens to Kannon’s complaints and looks concerned. Kent : “Well Mr Kannon, Scientology is a minority religion so we can understand some employers who are not so learned to as- He is cut of quickly by Xavier who doesn’t seem too happy that the man is disagreeing with him so early. Xavier : “I hadn’t finished.....Then, even after over a month of dedicated service, sweating and bleeding for my company....And the President still cannot tell me the founder of Scientology or even spell my rank!” Kent : “But do you actually have any examples of how your religion has caused any prejudice in the workplace....We can’t really do anything about management’s lack of understanding of your faith.” The Gold Patron Meritorious sighs and shakes his head, believing that he has made some valid points, Ellie~! turns to him and nudges his arm, urging him to continue. Eleanor : “Of......Your.......Faith!” Kannon turns back to Kent and carries on with his attack of PCW. Xavier : “You wanna hear about prejudice? Well I’ll tell you all about prejudice!” Eleanor : “All......About.....Prejudice!” Xavier : “Right, after about 6 weeks I joined Asylum, lets call it moving departments, and was I given an equal voice? Er....No! Was I treated the same as the others? SO totally NO! I’ll give you an example of some of the menial tasks I was made to carry out...I had to help find the leader, Jeremy Howard’s brother to save him from being tortured...I mean, how oppressive is that? They might as well have had me cleaning toilets!” He turns to Eleanor for assurance and they both nod in agreement to one another, sneering at the mere mention of Jeremy Howard’s name. Xavier : “Then just last Tuesday, in my workplace, somewhere I should feel comfortable and un-oppressed I had over 20,000 people booing me....making me uncomfortable and incapable of doing my job to an acceptable level and that made me a Saaaaaaaaad Scientologist...” He pretends to wipe a tear from the corner of his eye as Ellie~! mocks consoling him, putting her arm around him. Xavier : “Then, just the other day I found out that they had booked me to work with someone who openly hates my religion and has even created his own faith...Crunk...Just to smite us!” Kent doesn’t seem to be feeling much affinity to Xavier’s complaints and twiddles his pen in one hands as he furrows his brow. Kent : “Mr Kannon, it seems that you are making too much out of this situation, a lot of it seems to be happening in your mind so I sug-” The Scientologist shoots right up out of his seat and furiously bellows at the man. Xavier : “In my mind? IN MY MIND?!?!?! Oh, so I am making up the constant oppression that I must face every time I leave one of my houses, having people shout insults to me in the street, having to put up with Churches of Crunk springing up all over the place intimidating my follo-...I mean Hubbard’s followers.” The advisor adopts a defensive stance, moving his chair back and sitting up straight, moving away from his casual pose. Kent : “Look, I don’t see what I can do he-” Xavier : “YOU CAN PHONE PCW AND TELL THEM TO AWARD ME MY MATCH BY DEFAULT BECAUSE THEY ARE DELIBERATELY MAKING ME WORK WITH PEOPLE WHO MOCK MY FAITH!” He starts to pace around the room, gesturing wildly with his hands as he paces back and forth. Xavier : “Have they got to you Kent....HAVE THEY?” The enraged Scientologist slams his hands down on Kent’s desk, forcing him to jump his seat backwards against the windows where people on the street who can hear the yelling of Xavier are staring that the office. Xavier : “Did the Rush sign you up for Crunk? Or are you lust as blind at the End to the peril a Scientologist has to face every day of the year KENT! Because from where I am sitting you have a pretty easy job, you listen to everyone’s problems...REVELATIONS STRAIGHT FROM MY SOUL before you try to politely fob us off and then I bet you have a good joke when we leave...I BET YOU LAUGH AT US BEHIND OUR BACKS!” Ellie~! then gets up and snarls at Kent in support of her man, her pale Blue eyes showing frosty hate. Eleanor : “Behind......Our......Backs!” Xavier : “Well.....KENT! Laugh all you want because I don’t need you! I am going to face up to my oppressors myself, I will take the Rush and beat his very belief system out of him until he screams for Hubbard to save his very soul!” Kannon turns on his heels and walks away from Kent and towards the door. Ellie~! then leans right over the desk so her face is a matter of inches away from Kent’s and speaks in a harsh, whispery voice. Eleanor : “His......Very.....Soul!” The camera then follows Eleanor as she walks over the Xavier and the first couple of Scientology slam the door closed behind them and the shot fades to Black.
