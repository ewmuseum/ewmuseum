Coming Out...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he and Vic Kevlar, his longtime in-ring sparring partner, go through a series of complicated power reversals and technical exercises in the cellar training room, complete with fully to specs ring. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum watches intently, circling the ring like a shark, brandishing her whip, and not afraid to use it. As usual, a couple of the Graces are scattered around the room to fill out the scene. With the Pay per View only a few days away, the two are working extra hard, and in saying so, that means that Big Al is working extra hard and Vic Kevlar is trying to stay alive. To emphasize the point, Vic Kevlar springs off the ropes and launches himself into a perfectly executed dropkick, only to have Steele step in between his legs and grab hold, whipping him down hard into the mat with a wicked power bomb that makes the ring jump from the impact. Steele stands up, puzzled, as Kevlar’s eyes are fixed and unseeing. The crack Grace medical alert team slides into the ring and implants a defibrillator on Kevlar’s chest… Steffi: “CLEAR!!” SSSHHHHRRRAAAKKK!! Kevlar: (screaming) “AAAAHHHHHH!!!” Kevlar’s body convulses and shakes, the electricity surging through his being. Smoke pours out of his eyes as he manages to grab at the leads and rip them from his chest. Kevlar: “For crissakes! For the last time… I WASN’T dead! He just knocked the wind out of me!! Stop shocking me!! Now I have to go and change my singlet again…” Steffi: “Sorry… but you looked so lifeless! And honestly, we want to do a good job.” Big Alan Steele: “You did fine, my pretties! A slight electrical jolt as such never causes irreparable harm, and your concern for Lord Kevlar is a delight to behold!” Kevlar: (grumbling, goes to change) “I’ll be right back.” Sue: “Keep moving in there… I don’t want you to cool down too much.” Suzi: “Man, this is so EXCITING! I can’t wait to be at your first PCW World Title Match! I bet your parents are sooooo proud!” BAS: “But of course! Tis true that Zeus must be immensely pleased with my methodical climb up the Mount Olympus of PCW…” Suzi: “Oh, no silly. I meant your REAL parents…” BAS: (confused) “Real…?” Whup-Crack! The whip cracks down on the Grace, sending her leaping away. Sue snarls angrily at the blonde. Sue: “DON’T go there, Suzi!” BAS: “Does thou mean my Earthy conduits? The mortal parents that raised me until the teachings of Zeus and Olympus could gain a foothold in my mind?” Suzi: “Uh… sure.” Sue: “Never mind her, Big Al. Just keep moving along in there…” BAS: (pondering) “They do not know of my accomplishments. In fact, its been YEARS since I’ve seen them at all…” Everyone kind of stops at looks at Steele in the center of the ring, deep in thought. Sue sighs. Sue: “Al, they just visited last week.” Terri: “Oh! So THAT was the guy who kept asking me to wash his car while he watched and took pictures!” Sue: “Wait a minute… they don’t KNOW? You know… about your… lifestyle choice?” BAS: “I suppose not. I had never considered telling them.” Kevlar: (re-entering the scene) “Oh man, dude, that’s TERRIBLE! They don’t know you are… super?” BAS: “Oh, they know that!” Sue: “Then do they know you are… you know?” Kevlar: (slapping forehead) “That’s even worse!!” BAS: (angrily) “Enough! Tis not the right time to explain my particular practices to my Earthly parental units! I have much preparing still to be done and I cannot be expected to divulge every aspect of my being to mere MORTALS.” Kevlar: “But Al… this could be very, very bad. First of all, you don’t want them to find out on their own. And if you win the title, it’s sure to be all over the news. Secondly, this is info that others can hold against you AND them in maybe a blackmail situation. If you don’t tell them, villains and wrestlers alike may be able to hold a distinct advantage over you! You have to come out!” Sue: “He’s right, Al. They can SMELL someone who is in the closet.” BAS: “By the gods! And Lord Kevlar, have YOU told your parentage of your choices?!” Kevlar: (indignant) “I don’t want to talk about it! Leave me alone!” Sue: “It’s nothing to be a ashamed of, Al. If I know your parents, they love you very much and will accept you no matter what. Even if you ARE… you know.” BAS: (deep thought) “Hmmm… do you really believe this could be a disadvantage, even for my upcoming match?” Kevlar: “I’d say without a doubt. If Snow or Nelson knew…” Kevlar draws an imaginary line across his throat. Steele gulps and pulls hard on the collar of his toga. Sue: “And you’ll be an inspiration to all the other guys out there who are wrestlers, but are afraid to let their families, and the world, know about their lifestyle choice.” BAS: (indignant) “Tis not a lifestyle choice, but a genetic makeup! The gods MADE me this way!!” Sue: (rolling eyes) “Yeah, sure. Whatever. I’ll call the Masochist and we’ll all go over for a visit. Then you can… reveal yourself. Vic, you wanna come?” Kevlar: “Ah, no thanks. I have to get to the hospital. I think one of my lungs just collapsed. (checks himself) In fact, I’m sure of it.” We re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he fidgets nervously on the front porch of his (Earthly) family’s home. Sue gives him a strong shove from behind, a few of the Graces watching with interest. Steele gulps hard. His crime-fighting partner, the Masochist, puts a consoling hand on his shoulder. Masochist: “Hey, Big guy. We’re all here for you. You won’t be going through this alone.” Big Al nods in agreement and stabs a meaty finger at the doorbell… DING DONG! The crew waits on the front steps, Steele visibly shaking. Steps can be heard behind the door. Big Al takes a deep breath, while Sue takes a long drag on her cigarette. Sue: (mumbling) “Here we go…” The door slowly opens to reveal a matronly woman. Her hair is bundled up on top of her head and she wears a traditional apron over a housedress. Upon seeing her son in the flesh, the dishrag in her gloved hands fall from nerveless fingers. Her face slowly lights up in joy, her blue eyes very much resembling those on our hero. She flies through the door, latching herself on to Big Al in a hug. Steele gets a look of discomfort as she grasps him tighter. Mom: “Alan! It’s so good to see you again!!” BAS: (panicking) “Get it off! Get it off!!” Big Al’s mom back away, tears of happiness making her eyes shine. Steele falters a bit and straightens himself up after the hug. He gives his mother a wane smile before Sue steps in front of him. Sue: “It’s good to see you again, Mrs. Steele.” Mom: “Ah, Susan! It’s good to see you again, too! So, when are you and my boy getting married, huh?” Sue: (abruptly) “Never. I’m sure you remember Steffi and Cindi, along with the Masochist…” Mom: (unsure) “The who?” Sue: “The Masochist. Big Al’s partner…” Mom: (gets a knowing look) “Oh, yes of course. Him. Please, won’t you all come in? I was just baking cookies and it would be a shame if they went to waste.” Sue: “We’d be delighted. Come Al…” The crew make their way into the house, Sue having to grab Steele by the toga and literally drag him inside. In the living room area, everyone has a seat on a piece of plastic-covered furniture, making uncomfortable crinkling sounds. Momma Steele is standing, making sure everyone is comfortable before yelling to her husband upstairs. Mom: “Harold! We have company!” Harold: (from upstairs) “I’m busy on the computer!” Mom: “But it’s your son and his friends!” Pause. Harold: (upstairs) “Did he bring the blondes?” Mom: (sigh) “Yes, two.” Harold: “Atta boy! I’ll be right down!” There is the sound of rushing footsteps down the stairs as Momma Steele takes a seat in her favorite rocking chair. Everyone looks pretty damn uncomfortable as Harold enters, rubbing his hands in delight, looking the blondes up and down. Harold: “Ha ha ha! That’s my boy, Alan! You make your old man proud.” BAS: (mumbling) “You are no father of THIS Greek warrior…” There are a few moments of Harold just ogling the Graces before Momma Steele tries to break the ice and start a conversation. Mom: “So… this is a rare treat! What brings you all here today?” Sue: “Well, I wish we could say that this was a social visit. But Big Al has something he would like to say to you.” Mom: “Oh dear.” Steele’s eyes go wide, and he quickly shakes his head ‘no.’ Sue snarls at him, and reaches inside her purse, brandishing the whip. Big Al groans and stand up, facing his parents. BAS: (avoiding eye contact) “Tis true. SUE felt it important that you should know more about my current lifestyle practices, though I felt that would NOT be necessary…” Sue: “Al! Just do it!” BAS: (deep sigh) “I, Big Alan Steele, your offspring delivered through the graces of Zeus, am…” Tense pause. BAS: “A wrestler.” Momma Steele squawks and then faints. Harold staggers back against the archway to the living room, holding his head in pain. Big Al scowls. Later, Momma Steele is lying on the couch, being fanned by Steffi and Cindi. Our hero sits on a small chair, hunched over and dour as his father yells at him. The Masochist is nearby, trying to smooth things over. Harold: “How could you DO this to your mother! You know how weak her heart is!! You KNOW how she feels about wrestling!” BAS: “Twas not my idea! The gods have made me this way!” Masochist: “Yeah, Easy Mr. S. It’s in his blood.” Mom: “Oh, Alan! Why couldn’t you have been gay, like all the other Californian men?!” Harold: “Yeah! But we should have known… those dresses! The floozies!! This little rat here!” BAS: “They are togas!” Steffi: “WE are not floozies!!” Masochist: “Rat?!” Mom: “Oh, how could you choose to be this way, Alan? Why? Was it something we did?” BAS: “THE GODS MADE ME THIS WAY!!” Mom: “Then I reject God!” Harold: “I’m so disappointed in you, Alan! I thought you had made something of yourself!!” Sue: “Excuse me, but what, EXACTLY, did you think he was?!” Harold: “Think about it! The clothes! The girls! The money! I thought he was a pimp!!” BAS: “Pimp?” Cindi: “A pimp?! So THAT’S why you kept asking me how much for the whole enchilada last week!!” Harold: (snarling) “And that’s why your prices were so low… I told all the guys at the bar how proud I was of you!” Mom: “And I thought with you running around with your little Masochist friend in all those dives looking for trouble that you were gay! I told all my friends at the bridge club how proud I was!” BAS: (standing and roaring) “This is what I am, Big Alan Steele, son of Zeus and defender of the innocent! I am a wrestler in defense of the old gods! I am a hero to the disenfranchised of San Jose! And I am soon to be World Champion of PCW! Hi-Keeba!!” Mom: “World Champion…???!!!” Momma Steele bursts into tears. Harold gets up and roll sup his sleeves… And our heroes find themselves outside Big Alan Steele’s ancestral home. Sue whips out a cig and lights up. Sue: “Well, that could have gone better.” Cindi: “I thought for sure they would have been more… understanding about you not being a gay pimp.” BAS: (sigh) “Alas, one cannot expect much more from mere mortal conduits. They know not on which they speak.” Steffi: “Um, Al? Harold gave me his number. Is that wrong?” BAS: “Deeply.” Sue: “Oh well, guess we’d better head back and get back to training. I hope this hasn’t soured you on the whole match.” BAS: “On the contrary, it has furthered my resolve! I know now that at least one advantage has been claimed from Snow and Lord Nelson, and that alone has good consequences! I shall win from my REAL parents in Olympia! This I swear! Hi-Keeba!”
