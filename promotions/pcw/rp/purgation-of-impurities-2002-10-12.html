Purgation of Impurities...

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/02 I hope Sky's alright. Just disappeared from the party, didn't even say goodbye... That's not like him. I mean, he at least left a note when he had to go on that weird Cannonball Run thing, whatever it was. Still not too sure, hasn't come up in conversation, but I'll have to ask him why he's galivanting around with his Turmoil opponent. He really should take more care, he's got a shot at the Extreme Title! And then even if that doesn't pan out for him the Black Friday Mall Brawl is for the PCW Continental Championship! What a boost to his career this would be. Listen to yourself, Sandy, talking about Sky's career. It was only a few months ago you had one of your own. Somehow they decided I wasn't pulling my weight as an interviewer and cut me! Cal Seaver, PFIK, and Tiffany Jennings did less work than me combined! And even so, if wrestlers don't want an interview I can't twist their arm to give it to me. Sky needs to get back soon, or else he'll miss the two biggest matches in his career. What am I talking about, he's never let me down yet. He'll come back... And I'll be right there with him... Right there for him when he does.... The End can't move his arms or legs. A shame, he has an itch behind his ear that's driving him nuts. The End:'Is it really necessary to tie me up?' Cyclonica, taking enough mind that The End is blind, doesn't nod her head like so many negligent mortals. She speaks in a matter-of-factly tone, as per her usual air of great wisdom. Cyclonica:'Your body will defend itself even if you wish to go through this. Shackling you is the only way to prevent it from lashing out.' The End's blindness notwithstanding, the room is completely dark. At least, the walls are. There is no floor; just a platform that is suspended above an empty pit that stretches far down into the Earth, seemingly reaching into another dimension. The open rotunda high above blocks out the dark blue night sky that is black against the bright white light emanating from underneath the platform and stretching out into space. The platform seems to be illuminated, but blocks enough light to prevent the entire area from being lit to the point of blinding. The Divine Prophetess Cyclonica walks around the platform, held up not by chains, but by pure magickal force. Her long and thin blue hair whips around behind her head, her white robe tightly wrapped around her body, tied with a blue cloth waistband to keep it tied over her body. She's naturally 6'1', but wearing blue heels adds another two inches to her height. Her arms folded behind her back, she slowly circles the platform counter-clockwise while inspecting The End's magickal chains, bright yellow bands of electric energy, holding him four feet above the platform. The End:'So... why is this necessary?' Cyclonica:'Short answer, to remove the human impurities in your system. Long answer, I'm curious about something and would like to do an experiment.' Cyclonica reaches into the light and brings out a bowie knife; brown-taped handle, six-inch blade that glistens and shimmers in the light around the circular platform. She's planning on doing a litt eamputating or something, and the quality of the cut is not an issue. The light, in addition to holding up the platform, may produce whatever material item one wishes. It may not leave the circle, but then again Cyclonica only needs the knife for a little while... He wouldn't be getting into trouble. It's not like him. Nice guy, peaceful. Would be just the kind of person to stay out of view. The spotlight really doesn't have to shine on him outside of the ring, he doesn't mind. Awfully calm, almost to the point of complacency, no matter how serious the situation... Cyclonica:'As you may know, I was revealed to be only a half-breed.' The End:'I prefer the less negatively connotated hybrid.' Cyclonica:'Quiet you. I have a theory that there can be a quick way to purge the body of its negative human elements. There could be slow, extensive blood-letting, but I haven't the time or patience for it.' The End nods his head in disinterest. The End:'Your problems aside, I'm important to this because...?' Cyclonica directs a bolt of energy out of the circle of light to strike The End. It hurts, but wasn't intended to kill. The End:'Yeah...' Cyclonica:'The... hybrid heart is separated into two sections. The right atrium and ventricle are Celestially aligned. The left atrium and ventricle are of purely human biological and magickal structure.' The End: *Paying half attention*'Interesting...' A master of knowing one's will, Cyclonica responds in kind to The End's patronizing remark with another bolt of energy from the circle of light. Cyclonica:'You'd do well to take more of an interest. This could benefit the both of us...' The End coughs out some black smoke. The End:'... Very well.' Cyclonica leans back against the magickal light, a solid wall against her form. She controls this force with an masterful expertise that would take many lifetimes to achieve. Cyclonica:'The blood sent through the right half of the heart becomes Celestially endowed. But travelling through the lungs and other major veins and arteries, and then through the human half of the heart, and that blood becomes diluted... less useful....' Cyclonica handles the knife in her left hand, spinning it in her fingers poking the blade into the index finger of the right hand. Cyclonica:'But I've come up with a theory [that you may submit to this experiment will help you to fulfill the Blood Pact of your contract with Him] based upon the regenerative properties of Celestially endowed bodies, even in the case of hybrids... that if I were to remove the human half of the heart, that a fully Celestial heart would replace it.' Cyclonica takes her gaze off the knife on her finger, slowly drawing black blood she's pushing it in so deep. Cyclonica:'That there would be the Purgation of Impurities...' The End:'But you don't know if it'll work like you want it to, or if it'll kill whomever does it to themselves...' The Divine Prophetess nods her head in affirmation. Cyclonica:'Yes... so who better to test it on than another confirmed hybrid than yourself?' Placing the knife back into the light, it becomes smaller, more of a fine single-edged dagger with a bony handle, ending in a small skull the size of a golf ball. Cyclonica:'But enough talk... I'm eager to see if this procedure truly works....' Stepping toward The End, Cyclonica takes the knife in her right hand and holds it up and away from her body. The End is already unclothed upon his torso, his chest lightly oiled to make cutting easier. She presses down on his left pec about two inches from the halfway-point of the sternum and inserts the knife...
