Animal Sex!!!!! Doggie Style~!

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/02 The Story So Far: In the past week, Flaymin kidnapped the infamous, talking Taco Bell Chihuahua. The dog, which has long been a prize possesion of El Diablo is still being held captive by Flaymin Aymin. Where is the dog?What is it doing? Well if you read the title of this promo i think you can guess where this is headed...... [Scene opens on a bright and early Sunday morning…. 2pm to be exact. The front door of a house swings open and out steps Flaymin Aymin and the infamous ‘dog-napped’ Taco Bell Chihuahua who is on a leash.Flaymin is decked out in a bright red jumper and err… matching aqua coloured pants. Flaymin stands outside the house and takes a nice, deep breath] Flaymin: Ah, nothing like good ol’ American pollution.Heck, at least this smog smells better than the people in this wretched country. [The Taco Bell dog looks at Flaymin and lets out a drunken burp.Flaymin glances at the dog and grins] Flaymin: Ha!I thought Mexicans could handle their tequila. [The intoxicated looks at Flaymin and burps once again] Flaymin: Eh, it ain’t so bad.At least you’re not farting out burritos like you were this morning. [The dog tilts it’s head to one side and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: At this point I’d usually be inclined to go for a jog for like….exercise and stuff. But the truth is I can’t be bothered. Taco Bell Dog: *Burp* Flaymin: That’s a good question. We really are connecting on a personal level here, can you feel it? Taco Bell Dog: *Burp* Flaymin: Are you burping or is that just a bad case of the hiccups? [The Chihuahua smiles intently and then lets out a loud, stench-filled fart] Flaymin: Burping.Farting.The Hiccups. I could’ve sworn you were pregnant. [A couple of Flaymin’s neighbours walk past the house and gaze in curiosity at the badly dressed idiot talking to a famous, stolen fast-food representative dog. Flaymin gawks back at the people and shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Don’t mind them, they’ve just never seen a pornstar before. [The overweight Taco Bell dog begins to sway from side to side as it tries to walk out the front gate. Flaymin holds on to the leash tightly and follows the dog out the gate. They both stroll down the quiet street, past several houses.Flaymin strides along the pavement while the dog zigzags from side to side, unable to walk in a straight line. Flaymin looks at the dog and chuckles to himself] Flaymin: You’re lucky there aren’t any cops around to make you count the alphabet backwards.I always seem to get stuck at ‘X’, do you have the same problem? [The dog yelps momentarily before it blindly crashes right into a tree.At that point, it just drops to the ground. Flaymin looks at the dog and sighs. Gently, he begins to kick it in the stomach but the dog doesn’t budge] Flaymin: Where are all the cattle prods when you need one? [Slowly the drunken dog raises its little head and looks at Flaymin before it drops back to the floor once again.] Flaymin: No.No.Don’t do this to me again. [The dog barks softly, yet it’s body remains perfectly still on the ground] Flaymin: Hey, if you think I’m going to pick you up and carry you home- you’ve got it all wrong. [The dog’s belly begins to heave in and out at a rapid, unhealthy pace. Flaymin looks at the dying dog and scratches his head distress] Flaymin: You can’t die on me now!I haven’t even got my ransom money yet.Just get back up and walk! [Flaymin suddenly clicks his fingers together as an idea pops into his head.He ponders the thought for a moment before he shakes his head in disappointment] Flaymin: No…. I don’t think the whole ‘Weekend at Bernie’s’ scheme would work. [In the distance Flaymin notices another dog playing around in the front lawn of a house] Flaymin: Ah ha!You stay right here, don’t eeeeeven try and go anywhere! [The Taco Bell Dog looks up at Flaymin and rolls its eyes. Meanwhile, Flaymin dashes away to hunt down the other dog in the distance. A minute later, Flaymin returns with a Collie in his hands. He places the Collie near the Taco Bell Chihuahua.Both the Collie and the Chihuahua look at Flaymin in confusion] Flaymin: Come on buddy, mount this bitch!! You can do it! If there was ever any incentive to get off your ass and back on your feet then it would have to be the chance to hump this foxy looking chicky-babe of a dog. Hell, if I was a dog I’d wanna mount her too! [The Collie yelps in fright and attempts to escape.Before it can flee, Flaymin grabs it by the tail and pulls it back towards him] Flaymin: And where do you think you’re going? [Flaymin grasps the Collie tightly with one hand.With his free hand he begins to poke his fingers into the Taco Bell dog] Flaymin: Get up you lazy bastard- this is your big chance to bone a hot looking dog.Do you know what they say about taller women…err… I mean bigger dogs? They’ve got experience! No wait…. That’s not it. [The drunken Taco Bell Dog tries to rise to its feet as Flaymin watches on intently while offering some words of encouragement] Flaymin: Get up on it boy!You can do it!Every dog has it’s day…..Every bitch needs a good lay…. This is your big chance to make it happen.You can gloat about this moment to all your buddies at the pound! [The Taco Bell Dog staggers towards the Collie. The terrified Collie attempts to break free but Flaymin holds it tightly in place] Flaymin: Hmmm…. I wonder if this counts as rape? Oh well, it wouldn’t be the first ti…um…ahh….err….uh oh.Did I say that out aloud? PCW Managing Director of Roleplay monitoring: *Nods head* Flaymin: Mental note: Organise Mafia-related bail money. PCW Managing Director of Roleplay monitoring: I heard that too. Flaymin: Where are you? PCW Managing Director of Roleplay monitoring: We hear everything~! [Meanwhile back in the realms of reality……. Flaymin continues to hold down the Collie.The little Chihuahua gets up close behind the Collie] Flaymin: Its showtime!Go easy boy- remember, you don’t wanna finish to quickly. [As the drunken Chihuahua attempts to locate the ,ahem, ‘opening’ of the female anatomy Flaymin gets down on his knees and watches on in interest. Flaymin suddenly bangs his fists into the ground and shakes his head] Flaymin: No, no, you got it all wrong!You’re aiming for the wrong hole! Aim for the bigger hole! [The Chihuahua looks at Flaymin in confusion. Flaymin sighs] Flaymin: Do I have to do everything myself?Here let me show you how it’s done. [A small crowd of people begin to gather around Flaymin and the two dogs. Not noticing the growing crowd of people, Flaymin grabs the Chihuahua by the ass and swings it’s hips into the correct position] Flaymin: Alright we’re ready for take off. [Without conducting a single thrust, the Taco Bell Chihuahua lets out a mighty burp before it releases its grip on the Collie and just slumps to the ground. Irritated, Flaymin throws his hands up into the air] Flaymin: Aaaarggghhh!!!! [Without saying another word, Flaymin grabs the passed out Chihuahua by the ears and drags it back to his house] Flaymin: The chance for free sex and he passes out…… I guess he’s probably gay. [Scene fades to black]
