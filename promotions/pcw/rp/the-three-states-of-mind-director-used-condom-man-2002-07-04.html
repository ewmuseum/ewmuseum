The Three States of Mind: Director- Used Condom Man?

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th July OOC: The rp is done in a kind of comic book or old 50's/60's TV show kind of way with the comments in italics being like a narrator. I thought I'd try something new, so if it's hard to get I hope this explains it. Does having three personalities really make you schizophrenic? Does having three personalities that coincide with each other on a daily basis make you insane? Does having three personalities inside your mind make you a subject of lunacy? And just does this apply to El Diablo a man of many states of mind? All should be revealed in this series known as 'The Three States of Mind' The scene is that of a street, a street that is baron and bare to the bone. All there is, in the street are sidewalks on opposite sides of the crumbling roadway and many old buildings that seem ready for demolition. Despite the bareness of it all, the sidewalks are full of dog faeces, garbage bags and other kinds of litter. Spray painted on walls are 'tags' of certain individuals of little relevance, the whole street holds little relevance. So why, just why would this Mexican Devil be out here? El Diablo is indeed out there and is sat on the sidewalk with his feet on the road. He wears his face paint obviously and has his hair loose. He sits looking down at the crumbling road wearing a white vest, showing off his trained physique and the red devil tattoo he has on his left arm. To finish off his attire he has black denim jeans on and black sneakers. Notably missing is his video camera, that he carries with him almost everywhere, but El Diablo seems upbeat for some reason. Diablo looks up and smirks as the white clouds float by in the blue sky just above him. 'Beauty, pure beauty,' he comments with that smirk still on his face. Is this lunacy? Talking to himself, it must be, perhaps he is insane He looks down again and picks up a part of the crumbling road and just crushes the soft rock in his hand into dust. Not much effort there. 'A beautiful day,' says Diablo while looking up to the sky, 'No, not today,' he quits looking up, 'Last Sunday, it was glory personified for the Mexican Devil, another marvellous victory for El Diablo,' he praises himself, 'And now I fight none other than Duke Williams on Friday Fusion,' Diablo continues talking to himself about past and future events. He finally stands up and begins to walk down the dirt infested street, aptly named 'Dung Road' or at least the street sign reads. 'Duke Williams, former Continental Champion! Duke Williams, PCW announcer! Duke Williams, soon to be El Diablo's bitch!' Diablo degrades the legend of Duke Williams in one sentence. Whether he means it or not, it is helping him prepare for one of the biggest challenges in his career so far, where he goes one on one with Duke Williams in a Continental Title #1 Contenders Match. 'El Diablo, could be THE future PCW Continental Champion,' he raises his arms with a power trying to get motivated for what could be if he wins, 'Imagine that, the little known Mexican becoming global superstar,' he sniffs the fowl air yet carries on his walk down this horrible street, 'I can smell it now,' finishes the Mexican Devil with a smile on his face. Suddenly Diablo stops walking and looks down at the little gutter between the sidewalk and the road, he kneels down to take a closer look. 'Excellent!' comments the Mexican. The object he sees is merely a pink colored used condom. Somehow you can imagine the comments Dave Kern and Jeff Marx would make about that. 'This has given quite possibly, WAIT FOR IT! The greatest idea ever!' he says with enthusiasm showing that he is not joking. In a disgusting movement he picks it up in his bare hand, brings it to his nose and takes a sniff. 'I imagine the Malice Man smells worse than that,' remarks Diablo on his Fusion opponent. He drops the condom back down and stands back up from his kneeling state. He rubs his hands together and smiles again. 'But enough about that, back to my amazing, incredible, perfect idea!' Diablo says convinced of the greatness of it, 'A new action series following the activities of, WAIT FOR IT! 'USED CONDOM MAN'!!!' shouts Diablo aloud. This causes the birds that were resting atop buildings to fly away, leaving the street with literally just El Diablo in it. 'This must be my cleverest idea to date!' the Director of Dictation states again to himself, 'And who better to play the lead than....hmmm, I don't know. The Malice Man? He smells like one, I'm pretty sure he acts like one and he always goes on about taking things Malice Style, whatever that gibberish means,' states Diablo on who could play used condom man. Despite his strange way of thinking, even for a 'director' he can't be serious can he? Can El Diablo really be that bad of a director? Can he be that insane? Just can El Diablo manage to get victory over Duke Williams? And finally does his recent sequence of activities prove that El Diablo is some kind of schizophrenic? Only time and Fusion will answer these questions. NEXT: El Diablo on Mescaline?
