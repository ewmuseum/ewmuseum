Mentally & Physically- Pt 3

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 4, 2002 ::Shawn Mosely is now more ready then any other time in his life. This past Tuesday Shawn won his second match in a row and overall by beating Andy Murray in which was a very weird match some would say. Shawn has now, supposedly moved past, his past, and is now moving forward even more now. Shawn is now just at the beginning of a journey to pure success in PCW and his life. We now see Shawn and James back in Atlanta, Georgia. They have traveled back from Arizona, the site of Shawn’s last match with Andy Murray, to Atlanta where James hopes to solve and advance the physical side of the game of wrestling with Shawn. He’s the owner of a masterminded Gym that is well-known around America and is located in Atlanta, Georgia. It’s also the site in which he brings Shawn now. Both men are right outside as they have both just gotten out of James old beat up car which is about 20 feet away from them as we speak. Shawn is dressed in some new apparel as he is sporting a silk shirt with black kahki pants. All these signs are still pointing to the fact that he is inching his way back to his old form, cocky & arrogant. Plus he backed that all up in the ring as he has done in PCW.Shawn start’s talking as they both have stepped out of the car:: “I really don’t get this part James” Shawn says as he holds his gym bag which is full of his wrestling equipment. James just smirks as he glances at the giant letters at the top of the gym and then at Shawn. “You need to get a training ritual down, Shawn.” James replies. Shawn just nods like he actually agrees with James. They both make their way into the building at the same time. Their first stop is at the front desk where long time assistant to James, Anne Redding, is standing behind. She gives a little wave and smile not even knowing that James hasn’t been there for over a week. James leads Shawn to the locker room area where his office is about 25 feet from. As they make their way to James office a few regulars notice James and give him a good old hand shake and a “How’s it going?” question right with it. So with that said, you now know that you have to have some money to get into a gym like this. James and Shawn pass the men and make their way to the door as James pulls out about 15 different keys and picks one immediately as they keys are in eye site. He opens the door and makes his way into the office. There it was, a 20 ft by 20 ft office mainly full of leather and accomplishments made of gold. There is also a man in the room by the name of Franky, and that’s all people called the man for some reason. Everyone has a last name you would think, but around here you just called him Franky. “Hello Franky!” James says right when he sees him. Franky barely reaches 5’9”, but the guy is built like a brick shit house. Although he is in his late 40’s as the grey hair indicates along with the wrinkles beginning to surface, you can definitely tell that he spends a lot of time at this gym. His black and grey hair is slick back and body is mostly covered in leather. Besides the black shirt which is covered by the beaten down leather jacket. “How you doin’?” Franky replies as they embrace each other with a hug as Shawn looks on quite shocked that two tough men like James and Franky would do such a thing. They embrace for a spilt second and then look at each other. “I’m just doing great, Frank. How’s everything going for you?” James answers then asks. “Things are great. I just got a little worried about your adventure you went on.” Franky replies. Franky answers that and then looks at Shawn. “So, uh. . .is this that kid you were talking about before?” Franky asks James. James gives a smile as he looks at Shawn. “Yes. That’s the Golden One.” James replies. “How you doin’ kid? I’ve that your doing some pretty amazing stuff up there in PCW.” Franky says as he shakes Mosely’s hand. Shawn glances at James and then answers Franky. “I’m doing great.” Shawn says. The room goes silent for about 10 seconds as no one knows what to say. James Creon breaks the silence quickly, though. “Shawn. This is my buddy Franky. He’s the one that’s going to help you out.” James says to Shawn as Franky looks on. “In and Out of the ring?” Shawn asks. “Yes. This is just something you have to do to further your career. It’ll also help you win that Rising Star belt at Spring Fling. James says. “Uh. .Rising Star what?” Franky asks. James turns his attention to Frank. “Yeah. The contract Shawn got from PCW, remember? Well he has an important match this Sunday.” James replies. Franky looks kind of shocked. “Really? He started wrestling already?” Franky asks. Shawn answers before James can. “I might have taken off some time, but come on. I easily stepped into the ring and beat Estep and Murray with ease.” Shawn says with a cocky arrogant swagger. ::James looks at Shawn as more words from the Golden One point out that he is coming back, he is definitely coming back. The rest of the time Shawn and James are their they set up a schedule in which Shawn will follow to get in tip, top condition. Franky goes over everything from food to actual training. And with that said, Shawn is now truly ready for the journey to the top of PCW::
