Alternating Realities.

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 30, 2002. Jackson Rust, Al, and Kimberly all stand outside of a port-o-potty, or…several feet away from it to be more precise. Al shrugs, and walks to the door, knocking on it…with force. Al: C’mon, Steve! You’ve been in there for half an hour! I need to friggin’ go! From the depths of the port-o-potty ‘o’ doom, Steve replies in a muffled voice. Steve: I-I-I’ll b-be r-r-ri-right o-ow-out…EEEOW! As Steve continues to scream in pain, almost as if his asshole was being penetrated, Jackson steps over to Al and Kimberly. Jackson Rust: You know…that same band has been playing for four hours nonstop… Al: Really? I hadn’t noticed…I sort of…well, drifted away from caring. Kimberly: Yeah, same here. Narrator: And same here, as well. Al: Are you going to be narrating every promo? Narrator: *nods* Al: What was that? I couldn’t hear you. Narrator: *nods* Al: Speak up, dude. Narrator: Oh, that’s right…you can’t see me, or hear nods. Al: Oh. Narrator: But yes, I will be narrating every single Jackson Rust promo…unless I get sick or something…but that happens as often as Will Smith becoming a good rapper and or actor before the year 2525; and about as often as Jennifer Lopez admitting in public that her ass is the only other man made object bar the Great Wall of China that can be seen from space. Al: Damn. Narrator: Yep…you’re shit outta luck. Al: *sighs* Steve: J-JE-JE-JEEBUS C-CH-CHRIST! Kimberly: Like…ewwzzies, and stuff. Jackson Rust: Since when did you become preppy? Kimberly: I don’t know. It gets over well here at PCW...I figured I could try. Jackson Rust: Oh. Steve: OW! JE-JE-JEEBUS~! Jackson Rust: Calm down, dude. It’s just a shit. All of a sudden, a very famous, and lovable piece of crap appears on screen. Mr. Hanky: HIIIIDDDDY HOOO! Narrator: Hey, Mr. Hanky. Al: Heya. Mr. Hanky: Now, Jackson…crap isn’t just crap…poo is everything. Jackson Rust: Huh? Jackson looks down to see the piece of crap, as cheesy South Park sing-a-long tunes start up from nowhere. Mr. Hanky: It’s the circle…the circle of poo! Kimberly: Please don’t. Cartman: HEY! YOU SCREWING WITH MR. HANKY?! Al: Oh, crap…it’s the fat kid. Cartman stares up at Al, and begins talking in his fat voice. Cartman: HEY! I DON’T NEED TO TAKE SHIT FROM A JACKASS! Narrator: Yeah? Well…you’re mom is on the cover of Crackwhore Magazine. Cartman silences himself for a moment. Cartman: What? Mah—my mom isn’t on the cover of Crackwhore Magazine, are you mom? The scene cuts to a bedroom placed outside next to a tree. South Park’s “Chef” is getting busy on Mrs. Cartman, as Eric curiously asks. Cartman: Mom…are you on the cover of Crackwhore Magazine? Mrs. Cartman: Of course not, Eric…now, let mommy talk to Chef here. Chef: What? Woman, you is on all da covers! Cartman: Son of a bitch! Narrator: Told you, you fat piece of crap animation. Stan: C’mon fatass…let’s go. Jackson Rust: Pleasant children, no? Kimberly: Too many of them. Al: Yeah… The trio begins to get eerily silent…as they just stand still, looking at the ground, kicking it with their shoes. Narrator: CONVERGE; CONVERSE…do something! Jackson Rust: Oh, okay…Al, do something. Al: Alright…Kimberly, do something. Kimberly: Jackson…make some weird, unintelligent comment. Jackson Rust: What do you want to do after Steve finishes dropping his liver? Kimberly: Very good. Al: Yeah…one of your best anti-logical comments ever. Narrator: You’re all morons. Jackson Rust: Am not. Narrator: Are to. Jackson Rust: You know by arguing, you’re just wasting time? Narrator: Am not. Jackson Rust: Al…you know what to do. Al quickly covers his ears. Narrator: Ha! You think THAT works? Jackson Rust: Well…yeah. Kimberly: Yeah…I did. Steve: Y-y-yeah…OUCH! JE-JE-JEEBUS CH-CH-CHRIST! P-P-PAIN! Narrator: Ha! Covering your ears is about as useful as a collapsible wine glass. Jackson Rust: So it should work… *SMACK* Jackson holds his head in pain, as Kimberly dusts her hand. Kimberly: Stop being stupid. Narrator: Oh…he can’t help it. Papa Smurf: Yah…he’s a reeeeal idiot. Jackson punts the little blue man across the field. Al: Why’d you do that? Jackson Rust: I don’t like Smurfs. Smurf: Racist. Jackson…also…punts the Smurf across the field, and resumes casual conversation. Al: Why don’t you like Smurfs? Jackson Rust: Because they’re all…small…and blue…small hands…and they smell like cabbage. Kimberly: …I can’t blame you for that. Narrator: Yeah…cabbage sucks balls…like Cartman’s mom. Cartman: Hey! Shut the hell up! As Cartman waddles his way toward the posse, Stan, Kyle, and Kenny restrain him, pulling him back. Kyle: C’mon… Stan: Yeah, fatass, come on. Kenny: Mmmbbmmpph. Stan: That’s a good one, Kenny. Kenny: Mmphffou. As they leave, Kenny is promptly run over with a go-cart, but the go-cart doesn’t stop, as it races along into the night, and the concert. Jackson Rust: Oh my God! They killed Kenny! Al: You bastards! Kyle and Stan let go of Cartman, and walk to Al and Jackson. Kyle: Those are OUR lines. Stan: Yeah…it’s the only way we can get paid. Kimberly: Then why don’t you say it? Kyle: Fine…we will. Narrator: Go ahead. Stan: Oh my God! They killed Kenny! Kyle: You bastard! No…wait…wrong lines, let’s switch. Stan: Okay. Kyle: Oh my God! They killed Kenny! Stan: You bastard! No…that doesn’t sound right either…we’re going to have to have a talk with the animators. Kyle: Yeah…definitely. Cartman: C’mon! I’m hungry! Kyle: You’re always hungry, fatass. Cartman: I DON’T NEED TO TAKE SHIT FROM A JEW! Kyle: Hey! Don’t belittle my people, you fucking fatass! Stan: *sighs* Al: *sighs* Kimberly: *sighs* Narrator: *sighs* Jackson Rust: *tunes in* Rust looks around for a moment. Jackson Rust: What happened? Al: Stan, Kyle, and Cartman got into a fight…Kenny got ran over…but you knew that part. Jackson shrugs, and walks back to the bathroom, holding his nose. Jackson Rust: Steve…hurry up! Steve: OUCH! Jackson Rust: So…you’re not going to be done for a while, eh? Steve: I-I’ll b-be ow-out in a m-m-min-minute. Jackson Rust: Okay. Jackson turns around, and walks back to the posse, who still is just standing there. Kimberly: So? Jackson Rust: He’ll be out in a minute. Al: Good…because I’m getting hungry… As Al finishes his sentence, two guys walk up, talking to each other, before going to the two story outhouse, one taking the top, and one taking the bottom stall. Bottom Stall Guy: Ah! Stop! Stop! Top Stall Guy: Huh? Bottom Stall Guy: Stop shitting! Top Stall Guy: I can’t! You stop shitting, and get out if it’s so bad! Bottom Stall Guy: I can’t! Top Stall Guy: Tough luck. Bottom Stall Guy: …die. As Jackson, Al, and Kimberly look on, curiously hits them, as they want to know what happened. Jackson Rust: Maybe it smells? Al: Maybe. Several moments pass before the sound of toilet paper ripping is heard, and the bottom stall door opens, as does the top stall. The man in the top stall walks down the stairs provided, as the bottom stall man trudges out of the little room, feces covering his head and face. Kimberly: Yuck! Jackson Rust: Still hungry, Al? Al: No… Narrator: Are you sure? Al: *vomits* Kimberly: Yeah… The door opens and out walks Steve…looking refreshed and ready for some action…that he won’t get. Steve: H-hey! The scene abruptly fades out…into a parallel universe known as reality.
