Last Stand In Open Country #3

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Last Stand In Open Country #3 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 2/12/03 -Duke sits in a leather chair wearing a black, Jon Kano shirt that looks to be fairly old. A PCW backdrop is behind him, and he has a small night table to the left of him that has on it a bottle of whisky and a glass. It’s the Malice pit.- Williams: A man like Jon Kano speaks his mind and does it both with honesty and sincerity. What does PCW do?? They bury it hoping you, the home viewer, will be up going to the toilet and getting a snack. The very foundation PCW was build upon rests on the shoulder of icons like Jon Kano. I’m not going to toot my horn too much but I’ve held up that foundation a time or two my damn self. Duke pours himself a glass of whiskey. Williams: But nobody wants to hear that. Nobody wants to hear what the old timers have to say. Nobody apparently wants to see us. They’d rather watch Syko the Goblin run around as GM. Damn that’s captivating television at its finest. Duke rolls his eyes in sarcasm. Williams: I don’t understand how the bookers of this federation can claim they have no angles for me. No story I can fit into. People don’t want to see me get a shot at the world title is what they tell me. Bullshit. Who’s a bigger face then myself? WHO?? -Duke takes a sip from his glass of booze.- Williams: Jon Kano has been shit on, and disrespect. I feel the same way. Am I whining or simply speaking my mind? That’s for you to decide. -Duke downs some more booze.- Williams: So this Friday I get to ref a match with a bunch of “big” names. If it were up to me I’d take a ballbat to each of their skulls and teach them respect. Respect for there elders, the business, and the history of PCW. But you see I have too much respect for the business then to ruin their heat. To bad they don’t feel the same way. -Duke pulls a cigar out of his pocket and lights it up.- Williams: No, the game now is all about me. That’s all these kids in the locker room care about is themselves. Nobody else fits into there equation if it doesn’t “get them over”. What a load of crap from a bunch of egomaniacal storytellers. -Duke takes a puff on the big Cuban stick.- Williams: Do the fans really enjoy the fantasy stories that these newbie craftsman carve out of words and rhyme? If I wanted Moby Dick I’d read a book. When I watch wrestling I’m not looking for intellect and poetry but rather barbarism and slander. -Duke puts down the cigar and takes another sip of booze.-Williams: They call us living legends but were a dying breed. Ask yourself how Jon and I have been treated. Have we been crucified? Have we been done in by the law? Are you satisfied that we may never ride again? Has the system treated us right and are the stories we tell true? If the stories are true, does it bother you my friend? Some day you too may become a fading star. Or simply put, a living legend. They said Mozart’s fame would never diminish but name three of his songs. -Duke toasts the camera.- Williams: Everything goes away in the end.The camera fades black.=w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
