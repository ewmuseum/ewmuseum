Funeral Arrangements Part I: Coffin Comfort

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6th, 2002 [They are made to be depressing, made to send an eerie sensation through anyone’s body but that is what funeral homes are for. The atmosphere would send anyone into a state of sadness, just having to chose a coffin for a loved one could take forever, due to the feelings and emotions in one’s mind. For Blayze, he has seen so many of his close friends enclosed in coffins that he is immune to the whole vibe a funeral home gives out. In Lubbock, Texas, Blayze decide to give the “Wilkins-Hurst Funeral Parlor” a visit, we all know that Blayze is not going to be ordering a coffin for a loved one but more like a close enemy. The environment is quite appealing, a huge golden chandelier hangs from the ceiling as you enter the Parlor, walking on solid marble tiles which match the color of the walls. Perfectly placed in the confines of the equipped shelves on the walls are different models of numerous urns, giving an idea for clients on what they can chose from. At the end of the long hallway is a reception counter where a young, vibrant brunette with reading glasses and a blue, collar-open suit sits on a chair reviewing the orders of certain clients. As serious as he looks, Blayze in fact acts like a normal and civilized person as he approaches the counter, mostly a way to attract the lovely receptionist.] Blayze: Um excuse me lady? [The receptionist slowly slips her glasses off her face and shakes her head in an erotic fashion. Blayze swallows hard and tries to keep a straight face.] Receptionist: Yes sir? Blayze: Yeah I was looking for the owner of this joint. I wanted to speak to him about a few coffin arrangements Receptionist: Just hold one for one minute sir, I will page him and let him know a customer is here [Blayze nods in agreement and the lovely receptionist gets straight on the phone, quickly dialing in the numbers and leaving a 5-digit code on the owner’s pager. In a matter of minutes, the owner makes his way out of a reception room behind the main counter where Blayze stands. He seems to be a very slick person, his hair well gelled to perfection, his Armani Suit perfectly fitted without one crease or wrinkle and the finger jewelry is more then astonishing. He sticks out his and Blayze shakes it with a grin on his face.] Owner: Welcome to Wilkins-Hursts, how can I help you? Blayze: I heard you specialize in custom-made coffins and engraving, is that true? Owner: Yes it it! We have a variety of coffins, different sizes and different styles, some can be altered and others can have the interior design changed. Also, we can engrave names on the coffins, again, different size and different style Blayze: Hmm, do you mind showing me a few of your styles Mr…. [Blayze glances at the owner’s golden plated nametag] Blayze: Cepicky? Owner: Not a problem, follow me [The owners opens two alpine woods doors with the golden handles, leading into a beautifully decorated room, and right when they walk in, Blayze eyes widen at all the coffins lined up on each side of the room. A variety of colors from platinum to copper, sizes ranging from 5’5 to even 7 feet. A few decorated with rings of flowers, others with clip on locks and soft white cotton interiors. Blayze seems like he has seen some he already likes but the variety keeps on coming. The owner stops Blayze in dead center of the room and points to all of the coffins.] Owner: As you can see, we are very dedicated with our work and try to bring our customers the best and most efficient coffins for their passing loved ones. Do you have any specific details you want to see on a coffin? Blayze: Well the platinum ones seems perfect, I need sometime with a very claustrophobic-type feel for the interior Owner: You mean with a quite large interior decoration that more or less seems like it closes in on the corpse? Blayze: You are a smart man [The owner guides Blayze to what seems to be their most prestige coffin. A real platinum coating around it, a grey-ish soft yet stuffy material that would almost suffocate a person, perfect head and leg room and comes equipped with a special rolling table to transport the coffin on. Blayze looks over at the owner and seems very pleased] This white cracker really knows how to please a black man. Too bad he doesn’t know the real reason behind me about to buy this coffin, and the S.O.B. won’t either. Blayze: This..this is perfect. Owner: You spoke about a possible engraving on the coffin before. Are you still thinking of going along with it? Blayze: Hell yeah! Right on the top of the coffin, right where the head would be on the inside, I’d like it to say “Clyde” written in Old English Lettering Owner: I will see about the Old English Lettering. Is this Clyde fellow a close friend of yours that has suddenly passed. [Blayze gives him a dirty look] Blayze: Ummm..yes..yes he is He is damn lucky I didn’t sucker punch this punk right between his beady little eyes. Clyde, a close friend? Ha! More like a soon-to-be deceased bitch. I will just let that question slip away, pretending I never heard it Owner: Im sorry to hear thatBlayze: Eh, no worries, he will be in a better place Rotting in the depths of fuckin hell, burning in Satan’s flames. Owner: You are very right sir. Would you like to enter my office and discuss this a little more? Work on the price, fix up the details, get everything down on paper so that there are no errors. Blayze: Sounds good enough, I just better be getting my money’s worth Owner: We would not be around if we did not service our customers with the best Blayze: True He better or he will be the one testing out the coffin before Clyde does Owner: Right this way then [Blayze follows the Owner to the end of the room where on the right hand side, is a door leading to the Owner’s Office. He opens the door and lets Blayze step in then, as they are both in, he closes the doors and the discussion begins. If everything falls through the way Blayze wants it to, Clyde will have a new commodity he can call home…inside of an enclosed coffin]
