Mr. Jones Must Go

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Mr. Jones Must Go Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 01/01/03 “I can feel you looking at me...” Our shot opens up on the fixed stare of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon as he studies his morning paper, almost looking like he’s trying not to have his attention distracted. His eyes move only to follow the print talking of the threat of war in the Middle East, but seem to be resisting floating left. Xavier : “...But it’s not going to work...” Still, his bright Green eyes and Black pupils move within the confines of the page, his face almost looking as if every muscle is locking to keep it from turning. Xavier : “... This is my apartment, and I won’t be intimidated by a walking flea circus like you...” As he turns the page, he’s careful to switch his gaze over-leaf in a split second, an instantaneous change to keep his attention where it should be. Xavier : “.... And no furball is going to turn Ellie~! against me.” The camera zooms out to shot Xavier at the breakfast table with a Bagel of the bakery bag we saw him return with on a plate infront of him. His forearms are mapped with thin scratches, from Mr. Jones’ claws and the very Cat that gave them sits on the table next to his Bagel, just watching him from just off the corner of his paper. Xavier : “I know you’re there, but you’re not getting my attention.” Mr. Jones, looking as innocent as a White fluffy Cat can be just licks his paw before returning his gaze to the Scientologist. “Meow.” It nearly works as Xavier’s eye start to creep off the edge of the page but Kannon snaps out of it, screwing his face up and returning it to centre page. Xavier : “You’re not getting me that easily Cat, don't get too comfortable here and don't expect me to want you.” Closing it’s eyes before turning it’s head in a way only Cats do, Mr. Jones looks down at Xavier’s Bagel and then back at the Scientologist. Xavier : “..... And don’t you dare.” Ignoring his words, the coarse tongue of Mr. Jones extends out from his mouth and licks the top of the Bagel, finally getting the attention of the CEO, and his annoyance too as Xavier just growls, the Cat continuing to lick the bread as if to try and cover it all. Xavier : “Fine, I wasn’t hungry anyway.” For a few seconds Xavier continues to read then finally lowers the paper onto the table to look eye to eye with Mr. Jones, the stressed un-shaven face of Xavier mirroring the White face of the Cat. Xavier : “WHAT?” Still, that Cat just sits on the table and looks at him, Kannon for once being bettered. Xavier : “You shouldn’t even be in here.... It’s un-hygienic.” The stare-off continues. Xavier : “You want me to make a fuss of you? That it? Now Ellie~!’s not here you suddenly don't want to tear my skin to shreds?” Innocently, the White Cat looks back at him with hopeful eyes. “Meow.” Rolling his eyes back and giving in, Xavier sighs and goes to stroke Mr. Jones... “HHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHH!!!!!!!” .... Only for the Cat to hiss and brandish his teeth at the CEO as soon as his hand gets near, the hissing continuing as a possessed look of pure eve forms on the Cat’s face. Sitting back in his seat, Kannon just observes the White Cat, in a way admiring it despite his thoughts of how to get rid of him. Xavier : “Fine, just wait until Ellie~! starts feeding you nothing but raw Carrots and Houmus next time she goes into one of her Yoga phases, I wonder who you’ll be purring to for food then.” Another short stare-off continues as the Cat seems to take in the information. Xavier : “Or when she decides she’s bored of White and wants to dye you Pink... I wonder who’ll save your skin, or rather fur, then....” Just sitting there, the Cat tilts his head. Xavier : “Ahh, finally coming around to the idea aren’t we?” As is to seal the deal, Kannon goes to pat Mr. Jones on the head but again has to dart his arm back. “HHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHH!!!!!!!” Another bout of rabid hissing and snarling continues until Xavier backs his chair back a little to distance himself from the snarling animal before in a split second the Cat goes from spitting demon Cat to cute, fluffy little White kitty. “Meow.” Xavier sits there puzzled trying to work the Cat out before the sound of the bathroom door unlocking is head and though the open kitchen door we see Ellie~! emerge with nothing on but a White towel wrapped around her, her long Blonde mane a little damp as it cascades down her back.. Eleanor : “Hewwo Mr. Jones!” Waving to the Cat, she walks across to the kitchen, Kannon starting to look jealous of the attention the Cat is getting while deliberately clearing his throat. Xavier : “Ahem.” Ellie~! looks concerned at the way Xavier cleared his throat. Eleanor : “If you have, like, a bad throat, you should WAY go back to bed...” The Scientologist flashes a smug grin at the Cat, boasting that Ellie~! is still his. Eleanor : “....You don’t want to give it to Kitty and stuff!” As Kannon growls softly in annoyance, that Cat looks right back at him, almost taunting before turning to Ellie~! who’s walking around the table. “Meow.” With a look as if her heart just melted, Eleanor picks up Mr. Jones and holds him up in front of her, their noses rubbing together before the Cat’s tongue reaches out and licks her to lip, much to Kannon’s frustration. Eleanor : “Hewwo Kitty, has Xavi been good to woo?” Rolling his eyes at Ellie~!’s way of speaking to the Cat, Kannon sighs and goes to pick up his Bagel before realising that Mr. Jones has licked all over it and he mutters under his breath in annoyance. Eleanor : “Well, Mommy SO wants some breakfast so you can go back to Xavi...” She places the Cat back on the table and it slowly stalks over to Xavier while Ellie~! looks through the fridge. As if to mock him, Mr. Jones rubs playfully against Xavier’s forearm where his elbow rests on the table. “Meow.” Annoyed at the Cat’s 2-facedness, Kannon gets revenge by darting his head forward. Xavier : “HISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHH!!!!” Hissing harshly at it, Kannon causes the Cat to jump up and scurry back across the table. “WWWWWWWWWWAWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!” Jumping down onto the floor, Mr. Jones runs over to Ellie~! and rubs around her ankles, purring as she closes the fridge door and looks over a the smug Scientologist, a look of anger on her face. Eleanor : “LIKE STOP BEING A MEANIE AND STUFF!!!” He holds his hands out innocently as a grumbling Ellie~! picks the Cat up and hugs him to her chest. Eleanor : “Was bad Xavi mean to woo, Kitty?” Kannon goes to protest but just decides it’s better to just stay silent as the towel-clad Ellie~! walks past him with the Cat, making a point of not acknowledging her husband. Eleanor : “Woo can come back to bed with Mommy away fwom that mean Xavi can’t woo Kitty...” The Cat’s head turns to look at Xavier. “Meow.” As Ellie~! walks out through the door and towards their bedroom, Xavier sits plotting in his silent anger, muttering to himself as he looks over to the wall, and more importantly, the phone as the shot fades out to Black. “You may have Ellie~! now Jonesy... But I have the number of the Cat shelter, game me.” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
