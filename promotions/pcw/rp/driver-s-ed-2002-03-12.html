Driver's Ed

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/12/02 'ARGH!!!!!!!!' And yet again the repetitive yet obnoxious sounds of a man yelling in frustration ring out. This time not in the dank confines of a dungeon, but in the smaller and more enclosed confines of an automobile. 'ARGH!!!!!!!!' To the left and to the right this car swerves, leaving black skid marks across the highway...........in the seat a tiny feminine hand with long and sharp nails clutches the steering wheel. She leans over it, peering...........boosted only by several old telephone books. The psychotic little girl known as Bethany looks over, to see her demon/lover in the passenger seat glaring at a tiny portable television plugged into the cigarette lighter and resting upon the dashboard. Clyde: ARGH!!!!!!!! Bethany: What the hell are you doing........... Clyde: Eyes on the road! With a grunt Bethany turns her focus back to the street before, muttering threats of death and dismemberment under her breath. Clyde: We're here to teach you how to drive. Bethany: But I'm eight.......... Clyde: (Sarcastically) Yes but as our friend Mr. Stormbringer so astutely pointed out you've been eight for years, you ain't getting any older honey. Bethany: (Raises an eyebrow) Clyde: So we may as well teach you now...............ARGH!!!!!!!! Yelling once more Clyde stares at his tiny TV, shaking it furiously. Bethany glances to him briefly............as soon as she does the car veers to the left, skidding off the road and bumping furiously. Bethany: DAMMIT! Up and down they bounce, the little girl struggling with the wheel which moves against her, overpowering her tiny arms.............a loud thud rings out, as a large cow flips up in the air from being struck by the vehicle and bounces off the windshield. Cow: Moooooooooooooooooo! Bethany: BAH MEH BAH! Finally with her insanity kicking in Bethany manages to tear the wheel to the right again, heading back onto the smooth road. She brushes her hair back from her forehead and glares at Clyde, who amazingly enough is still looking at the TV. Clyde: This Stormbringer moron STILL isn't mentioning me in his promos! ARGH!!!!!!!! Bethany: Goddammit I can't learn to drive with you watching jobber promos! Bethany attempts to reach over and turn off the burned man's television, only for him to swat her hand away without looking up. Clyde: Do you have any idea how exasperating it is to have to sit here and watch minute after minute of boring jobber promo, looking only for my name somewhere in it only to find it is NOTHING but minute after minute of boring jobber promo? Bethany: Which way do I turn? Clyde: (Still not looking up) Take a left. Shaking her head, the demonic girl does as told...........once more the car begins bumping as the panicked screams of men and women begin to fill the air. Clyde: Now then............what is the point of Stormbringer driveling about his useless match at the last PPV? He lost.........but that's not important. What's important is that it doesn't have anything to do with ME~! ME I SAY~! Bethany: (Watching a sign for Bloomingdale go by) Uh Clyde are you sure this is the right direction? Clyde: (Ignoring her) Why am I booked against the long lost Storm brother anyway? Didn't we get rid of all the Storms that were infesting this place? This is why PCW is sliding, it's because the Storms are coming back. Bethany: OUT OF THE WAY NIMRODS!!!!!!! The little girl stomps the gas, driving the car straight up an escalator. Frantic shoppers dive clear off the electric stairway, breaking bones but saving their lives............Bethany clears the top at last, driving along the top floor. Clyde: And what's with all the talk of brothers and sisters and crap? Didn't Jeremy Howard prove this kind of drivel is pointless and boring? Bethany: Maybe............hey look a Pic n Save, I wanna stop and get some shoes. Clyde: (Still not looking up) Keep driving! Bethany: Bah! Onward, onward through this building she goes, unlucky women and children flying up and off her glass. Their blood covers her field of view............grunting in irritation she yanks the lever for the windshield wipers, angrily clearing off the mess while spraying it with cleaning solution. Bethany: Which way now oh great and mighty teacher? Clyde: (Still not looking up) Make a left into this elevator. Shrugging once more Bethany does as told............driving inside the huge glass lift, holding the brake as the shocked passerby look on. Bethany: (Holding up her middle finger) See something green? Finally the elevator reaches the top floor...........once more the little girl hits the gas. Out she goes..............meanwhile Clyde continues to yell at his TV. Clyde: Mention me dammit mention me! Bethany: Uh Clyde........... Clyde: (Still not looking up) Bethany you're going at a snail's pace, will you speed up? You're holding up traffic. With another growl, Bethany steps on the gas.............with a furious sneer on her face speeds up. Faster, faster.............the clear blue sky racing by as she speeds along. Clyde: What the hell..........he's Aimz's BROTHER? She's his SISTER!? WHAT THE BLOODY HELL............... Unfortunately the burned man's cries are cut off as the little girl finally reaches the edge of the roof, going up a slight incline and launching off. Through the air the car flies, off the top of the shopping mall and into the wild blue y as we mercifully fade to black.................
