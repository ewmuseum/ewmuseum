Date: October 27, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank__33—********* Tides The Turning of Tides with moons and asteroids... HelloI haven’t seen you in a whileNot since I was thrown into this holeBut now that you’re backMaybe we can be friends ~Leigh Donovan Wednesday, October 23 - Day 112 It’s been almost two weeks since Vinny G, ‘Mr. Search and Destroy’ to his Mob friends on theoutside, realized that the team of Jason Snow and Tim McDonald aren’t going to be easy totopple. In McDonald, there is the unpleasant combination of deceptiveness and power - Vinny Ghas to walk on egg shells around McDonald, due to his position in the Italian mafia gang. InJason Snow, there is the dominating physical presence to go along with the fact that the longhaired lion is completely unpredictable - Vinny G thinks Snow the type who would cut a man’sthroat in a fit of rage, regardless of the consequences. Snow wasn’t one to go about deception,but he has McDonald for that. That’s what makes the duo so tough to crack. But last night, the bullish Italian formed a plan. Tim McDonald has the C.O’s in his pocket, soVinny G can’t make a phone call to inform his mafia companions of the real story behind DougHattoni’s death - a story that would surely lead to the death of McDonald, Snow, and the Aryanleader, Harvey Blake, along with their families and everyone associated with them. But justbecause Vinny can’t make the phone call doesn’t mean someone can’t make the call for him... “How would you like to make a quick buck?” Vincent asks Shane Hood, an eighteen year oldyoung man just three days into a twenty year sentence for vehicular manslaughter - he wasintoxicated behind the wheel. “What do I have to do?” Hood asks nervously. In his three days of imprisonment, he’s takennothing but abuse and harrassment from his fellow prisoners. This offer seems too good to betrue. But the offer is genuine. Vinny G just needs someone he can trust. “I just need you to make aphone call for me,” smiles the bullish Italian. “That’s all. Just a phone call.” Wednesday, October 23 - Day 112 With watchful eyes, Jason Snow and Tim McDonald secretly patrol the crowd that has gatheredat the telephones on this afternoon, like they have nearly every day for the last two weeks. Thanks to McDonald’s abundance of wealth and his ties with head C.O. Benjamin Graham, he’sallowed to spend the tireless three hours per day that the telephone lines are open in the phoneroom. “We’ve got to cut this shit out,” McDonald sneers at Snow, who’s blue eyes watch over thecrowd, making sure Vincent Giovanni doesn’t arrive. “We can’t watch these fucking lines dayafter day. We’ve got to get to Giovanni and cut his fucking heart out.” “Fine,” Snow says in a low, surprisingly calm, voice. “I’ve got no problems with you taking thefat bastard out.” Tim sighs in frustration at Snow’s mocking - they both know Tim McDonald’s not one to get hishands dirty. In all of his scheming, he always plays the part of the supervisor, and occasionallythe manipulator - never the labourer. In McDonald’s mind, that’s what Snow’s ever-growingtwo hundred and fourty-five pound body is good for. His own hundred and seventy pound framewould do little against the bullish figure of Vinny G. Snow has spent the past few weeks growing multiple ulcers. The stress of the lives of his familybeing in danger is something that has been eating at him every milli-second without so much asthe hint of mercy. Deep in Snow’s mind, however, he knows that at the eleventh hour, he wouldno doubt choose to do whatever it takes to keep his family safe - regardless of what penalty mayresult. What happened to Doug Hattoni must remain secret at all costs. Snow’s career has taken a surprising turn for the better. In all of the trauma of his personal life,he finds himself with a chance to earn a shot at the coveted PCW Championship in a week onTurmoil. Then again, at this moment, Snow’s career means very little to him. If Vincent Giovanni isallowed to make a phone call to his affiliates in the mafia, the truth will be revealed, and Snow,along with his entire family will wind up dead in whatever ditch the mob finds most convenient. “I’ll work something out,” McDonald says to Snow, his keen eye watching as a slim young manmakes his way to the telephone for his call. “And when I do, that fat motherfucker will dreadthe day he ever heard the name ‘Doug Hattoni’.” Snow shrugs, indifferently. Lately, he thinks Tim McDonald a talker more than anything else. Then again, when McDonald does strike up a plan, he can be brilliant, as he exhibited bymanipulating Doug Hattoni’s murder, using Jason Snow as one of his many pawns. Snowcouldn’t have chosen a better ally - talker or not. All the while, McDonald’s attention is locked on the young man at the telephone. He must be ateenager, McDonald assumes. The young man pulls a slip of paper from his pocket and beginsto dial the number. “Look McDonald,” Snow says impatiently, “Giovanni would be a fucking fool to step foot inhere. He knows we’re waiting for him.” McDonald doesn’t respond. His attention is locked on the curious young man at on the phone,waiting for someone to pick up at the other end. When they finally do, the young man speaksjust loud enough for McDonald to hear. “Hello?” the young man says, nervously looking overhis shoulder. “I got the number from Vinny. He told me to give you names.” “Holy shit!” exclaims Tim McDonald, his eyes wide and his jaw dropped. In an act of utterdesperation, he pulls back his right hand and strikes Snow in the face with a clean hard punch. Snow stumbles backward and rage fills his eyes while he glares back at Tim McDonald. After McDonald strikes Snow a second time, Benji Graham shouts, “Lock Down!” A slightsmiles flashes on McDonald’s face, only to be smashed into oblivion when he feels Snow’s righthand crashing across the left side of his face. McDonald collapses to the floor, semi-conscious -his plan, however, worked marvellously. Hesitating because of the motion behind him, the young man on the phone spits out the firstname on his list. “Harvey Blake...” he manages to say a second before he’s tackled and wrestledto the ground by a C.O. enforcing the Lock Down command. The phone hangs spinning on the end of its chord... Thursday, October 24 - Day 113 Harvey Blake, the one-eyed leader of the Tank’s Nazi gang, emerges from Geoffery Rankin’soffice - a look of shock spread across his paler than usual face. He takes a few steps beforestopping in his tracks to stare at nothing for a long moment. Behind him, Geoffery Rankinwatches from the doorway of his office with concern. “What the fuck was that all about?” asks Jack Hooper, Harvey’s right hand man who rarelyleaves the one-eyed Nazi’s side. Startled by the voice breaking into his world of silence, Harveyturns to look at Jake. “My family’s dead.”
