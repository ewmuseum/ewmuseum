Extreme/From a Woman

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/09/03 Strolling through the turbulent corridors was the misogyny manipulator, constantly rotating his head from side-to-side, curious to know which room is which. He'd earlier boarded the PCW Airship, and was found on the Third Residential Deck. Peering past the doors, he'd only saw the presence of the ordinary staff members, along with the joyous gamboling of the uproarious daycare center. Pestered by the annoying tones of.. 'Remember Stone.. Match on Friday.. Extreme Title.. Don't forget.. Important for your career build-up.' He just dashed forward back to his prosaic room pad. His room was placed in a split hallway and the disadvantage that came was that Lacuna Debris' had been the opposite section. It was like any other, a single bed, the joys of a semi-filled refrigerator (enough for him), a well-embellished and clean kempt bathroom, and a comfortable sofa before a television set programmed to view only the PCW events and happenings. Aligning himself in the center of the sofa, he snatched the remote off the arm rest, and flipped through the channels all having the transparent logo of 'PCW' to the side of it, indicating it being ONLY PCW. He came to a channel that coincidentally fell to his upcoming match with the PCW Extreme Champion, Manuel Labor. The narrator reading through the description and stipulations went on, as the word 'EXTREME' echoed loudly in Stone's mind. 'Extreme?' he questioned, looking behind him over to his bed where the sheath covering for his blade rested. He turned back over to the set and flipped it off, just musing to the word 'EXTREME'.. He thought further, lengthening the word and his concentration on the match. Flipping in to an abrupt flashback he said again.. 'Extreme?' [Piercing. The blade lead in to her flesh like a hot knife through butter. Appallingly entering, instantly imbruing her ashened skin tone with crimson blood. She squealed, but he blanketed her mouth with his white, pudgy hand, obstructing the shouts. She cringed in pain, moribund, moments later resting her voice, and ceasing her gestures. She died. Before her was Stone Burns, the somber and baleful misogynist. He extracted the blade FROM her chest, blood dribbling down the sharp points.] 'Was that Extreme?' he thought looking months back to his first known murdering of a woman by the name of Anna Blasch.. Leading to believe it related to the word 'extreme', he looked back at the sheath sheltering his blade. Stone stood up, walking over to the bed, obtaining the sheath, and cracking it open. He examined the glossy object, petting it having scenes rush in to his head, referring towards his upcoming opposition, Manuel Labor. [It shows Stone draping his hand over Manuel Labor's fallen body, in the diminutive arena located in the mid deck(s). The referee continues on with a count, screaming out and indicating the count with the pounding of his hand on the constructed ring. 'ONE... TWO... THREE!' Stone leaps up, his mien doesn't look excited but just rather bleak as always. Attaining the PCW Extreme Title from the referee, he hoists it in the air displaying his triumph.] Flashing back over Stone draws himself back, accompanied by his head. 'Me as Extreme Champion?' he pondered, riffling through and analyzing his evanescent and sudden clips of the future. 'Now that is -- Extreme.' he inaudibly remarked, placing the sheath along with the blade back abed and now beginning to think he actually stood a chance in acquiring the PCW Extreme Title. But hampering his thoughts and emotions came the malevolent image of Lacuna Debris, confronting Stone valorously and imprudently in the confines and remote areas of the past PCW arena. Running through his mind was that image, even though it was a women and thought of them as easy bait.. It managed to win him over and make him believe she would actually roil his match and his PCW career in general. Finally, he thought of the last words he had piped over towards Lacuna in the heated encounter.. 'Watch yourself.' -- and now Stone was beginning to believe more and more he had to watch himself..
