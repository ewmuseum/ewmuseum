Hunted Saga Part 20: The Death of a Loved one...

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/02 Hunted Saga Part 20: The Death of a Loved One… The mood around the foliage was sombre to say the least. The car wreck was still there, hidden away from the public eye, by the surrounding trees and bushes, but there was no clue what so ever of where the Thunderbolts took Crichton. Using the Avengers Quintijet it hadn’t taken them two hours to get to the actual sight of the crash. Aeryn wasn’t much good at this kind of thing and simply stood in the corner watching the Avengers feverishly look for clues. Captain America was searching through the white wreckage that was once her and Crichton’s car, Iron Man was flying above them trying to locate anything of significance and the Black Panther was inspecting the foliage. Cap walks over to Aeryn with a small metallic device and holds it up before her. “Recognise this?” Cap questions. Aeryn looks at him strangely. “Nope” She simply replies wondering what Cap was getting at. Cap smiles a little. “This is a tracking device. It was placed upon your car some time ago!” He states. Aeryn shakes her head in disbelief. “So that’s how everybody kept on finding us so easily? They were tracking us with that device!” She explains, more to herself than to Cap. Cap simply nods. “Can you remember exactly what you saw before the car crashed?” He inquires. Aeryn gets a thoughtful look upon her face. “I remember some kind of flying robotic device forcing us off road and then a man growing to an immense size before us and then… Aeryn trails off not wanting to relive the moment any longer and also not wanting to dwell of the thought of John Crichton. Cap nods thoughfully. “That sounds very much like Mach 10 and Atlas.” Cap states. Then The Black Panther wanders over to them slowly and successfully gains their attention. “Captain America, I believe that I have found the trail they left behind. I can track them down to wherever they went!” The Panther states. Cap raises his might arm in the air, to signal Iron Man down to the ground. Within a couple of seconds Iron Man lands within a couple of meters of the Quintijet, as Cap, Aeryn and the Black Panther enter the Quintijet. Iron Man follows them in the jet and they soon take off in search of the Thunderbolts and ultimately Crichton. ************ Crichton had been conscious for about one hour, when the dark room he found himself in suddenly became light. He decided that if the scientists didn’t know that he had regained consciousness that was one advantage he had upon them, so he immediately closed his eyes and waited. A second after the lights were switched on, Crichton heard the door of the room slowly open, by the slightly annoying creaking of the doors hinges. He sensed the people drawing ever closer to him and was disturbed at this. “How is the subject fairing?” One voice questioned. “He is at optimum condition. The results are better than we expected.” The second voice replied. “Good, we’ll check back in an hour to chart the progress.” The first voice continued. Crichton waited until he heard the familiar door hinges creak before he dared to open his eyes once more. The scientists had left the light on when they left the room this time and Crichton inspected the room for other possible exit points in case he managed to get out of this current pickle. Typical of the army, the cheap bastards couldn’t even afford a second exit. Then, the conversation that the two scientists had finally hit home for Crichton. What results? What Progress? Crichton hoped it wasn’t what he thought it was. Had they already conducted the super human experiment on him? He figured that it would explain why they gave him a shot to lose consciousness when he first entered the base. ************ The Quitijet silently lowered itself from the sky, down into an open area of grass, not too far from the army base that was currently keeping John Crichton captive. Cap, the acting leader of the Avengers, decided that if all of the Thunderbolts were present the three Avengers and Aeryn would be over powered, so a stealthier tactic was needed. They would go by foot to the fenced off perimeter, then they would get through and attempt to locate Crichton without too much trouble, or so they hoped. It didn’t take the four of them long to reach the fence and took them even less to break through the fence. Iron Man used his repulsor blast to tear the fence apart, without drawing any attention to themselves whatsoever. The four of them pressed up close against the dark green painted and Cap, who was ahead of the rest, looks around the corner of the building onto the main courtyard. “There’s a building at the end of this row, which is guarded more than the others. Logic would suggest that he is being held there!” He states addressing each of them. The other three nod in agreement. “But the question is Cap, how do we get across the courtyard to them, without being noticed?” The Black Panther enquires. Cap shakes his head. “We can’t get there unnoticed that’s for sure.” Cap replies with a grim look upon his face. “Perhaps if we Avengers attack, knocking the entire station off balance, while Aeryn tries to retrieve Crichton!” Iron Man suggested. Cap turns to Aeryn. “Are you sure your up to it?” He questions sympathetically. “Hell Yeah!” Aeryn replied rising to her feet. Cap shrugs. “Well okay if your sure! AVENGERS ASSEMBLE!” He shouts rising from his feet, breaking cover and entering the army court yard. “Make sure none of the soldiers are killed or seriously injured!” Iron Man shouts, before taking off into the cloudless air. As soon as the Avengers break cover, all hell breaks out. Aeryn wasn’t sure how long the Avengers could last, but suspected they would last as long as it would take, which meant she had time to pick the correct moment to make her way over to the building. Aeryn watched as Iron Man’s repulsor blasts blew chunks of earth into the air, not hitting the soldiers, but the blast of the shots knocking them unconscious. It isn’t long before the two armed guards, from the building in which they expected Crichton would be, joined in the melee, firing their fully automatic weapons at the various members of the Avengers. Aeryn half wanted to go now, but her instincts told her otherwise. Besides the Thunderbolts were undoubtedly guarding Crichton closely and she definitely couldn‘t take one of them never mind the five or six that there were supposed to be. Her patience was rewarded as the two Thunderbolts Aeryn recognised as the ones who initially attacked her and Crichton in the first place, exited from the building. Aeryn was tempted to wait longer, but since she had only seen these two Thunderbolts since the beginning, she had to assume that there were the only Thunderbolts currently present at the base. Aeryn took her chance and made a break for the building, stealthily and quietly, not wanting to draw unnecessary attention. She managed to make it to the doors without anybody seeing her and slipped in through the doors just as easy. Just as quiet and stealthy as she got to the building, she made her way through the maze of corridors that made up the gigantic building. Aeryn noticed immediately the red flashing light and screeching security alarm that was echoing through the entire building. Aeryn paused and listened out, confirming her suspicions, somebody was coming her way. Aeryn dived into a doorway and pressed herself up against the wall, as about six or seven fully armed soldiers run past her position not even glancing around. Aeryn watched as they rounded the corner in which she just came and made sure they were out of sight. When satisfied that they are gone, she exits from her hiding place near the doorway and makes her way in the direction in which the soldiers came. She wasn’t sure whether this was where Crichton was being held, but she had nothing else to go on. ************ The security alarm currently engulfing the military building was deafening and Crichton was mightily pissed off about it. Although, he guessed that this whole thing could have something to do with him and so his bad mood faded. His limbs were aching after being in the same position for so long. Crichton couldn’t even count the hours. He didn’t know how long it had been, it could have been a day or two, for he had no way to tell how long he was unconscious for. Crichton held firm in his belief that the Avengers would indeed come to save him, of this he was sure, or that he needed to do was wait. The worst thing about being in this situation was the waiting, so he began to occupy his mind with thoughts of his wrestling career. Just before the crash and the attack from the Thunderbolts, Aeryn had discovered that due to Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo’s hardcore behaviour, both Friday Fusion and Sunday Sabotage were cancelled. Bang went his dreams of the Television Title. However, not long after this he found that the match had been moved to the PPV entitled Supremacy. At the time, Crichton was pleased at this, it gave him more time to prepare for the match. Because his current situation however he hadn’t gotten any training time in and therefore had to be considered the outsider for the match. He couldn’t understand though, why the wrestler known as Jason Core was involved in the match. A couple of shows before hand he had kicked Core’s ass just to become the number one contender for the title, yet Core was still involved in the match. Despite having not training much for the match and assuming the Avengers were actually going to free him, Crichton was quietly confident. This was because on paper, he had beaten Jason Core before and he had battled Pharaoh to a draw before. He was almost looking forward to the match, to prove to the entire world. However, he couldn’t do anything while he was stilled caged up in this damn army base. Then without warning the door to the room, in which he was being held slowly opens and Crichton tenses himself up for confrontation with the damned scientists. The door opens agonisingly slow, but Crichton is relieved to discover Aeryn’s head creeping around the side of the door. Crichton’s face lights up with delight, as Aeryn steps into the room quietly closing the door behind her. “What took you so long?” Crichton questions sarcastically. Aeryn tuts slightly. “Now let me think, should I break you out or not? I’m going to be a while thinking ‘bout this one.” Aeryn jokes in response Crichton goes into a bout of fake laughing. “Your Funny! NOT! Now stop arsing around and get me out of thi…” Crichton’s words are cut off by the almost deafening noise of a gun blast. Aeryn simply stares on wide eyed, Crichton follows her gaze down to her chest to see the crimson red patch appearing in the middle. Aeryn looks back up again and simply slumps to the floor, revealing the shooter of the weapon, one Mr. Fleming. “Damn! I’ve always wanted to do that!” Fleming states insolently. “Damn you Fleming, if I ever get out of this I WILL kill you!” Crichton states voice full of anger. “That isn’t very likely now is it? For one your strapped up and two your going to be dead soon!” Fleming states fitting the evil villain stereotype perfectly. “Why are you doing this Fleming? How can this possibly benefit you!” Crichton screams. Fleming sighs. “You really are that stupid aren’t you! Because the Thunderbolts captured you and I didn’t I became the laughing stock of my agency, and since I no longer benefit from you being alive I now join Lyons campaign of wanting you dead. Except of course I will be the one who’s going to kill you” Crichton felt his anger reach maximum, he knew he had a temper, but hadn’t really had chance to explore it, his army life had taught him to become cool in a crisis, but this was different. Aeryn lay on the floor dying and he could feel something that hadn’t been there before he was captured. His muscles flexed and his anger grew to an almost unbearable level and without warning the metal shackles across Crichton’s wrists broke and his hands were now free. Crichton was as shocked as Fleming. Then the scientists run into the room, waving their hands about. “No! Your ruining our experiment, oh…” The first scientist trails off at the sight of the now free John Crichton. “I see our experiment has gone far better than we ever expected. He should only have just above human strength, now he’s got at least three times that!” The second scientist observes. Crichton didn’t register his comments at the moment and simply slowly paced forward intent on ripping Fleming’s throat out through his ass. Fleming snaps out of the shock and attempts to point the gun, however, Crichton quickly knocks the gun out of his hand and lifts him up in the air effortlessly. A couple of wild punches have no effect on Crichton whatsoever and he simply tosses Fleming, however, Fleming flies through the door and is knocked unconscious against the hard concrete wall. The scientists sensing danger attempt to run, but Crichton swiftly grabs them by their collars and lifts them up into the air. “What experiment?” He questions already knowing the answer. The superhuman experiment! We injected you with the serum as soon as you arrived.” The first scientist declares. Crichton simply grunts in anger as the scientists also find themselves flying towards the same concrete wall. Crichton turns back to Aeryn and lifts her up and drapes her over his shoulder he then goes in search of the armoury. ************ Outside the view was like a warzone. Dozens of soldiers were doing their best to battle the Avengers, but were failing miserably. The two Thunderbolts were still fighting though, keeping all three Avengers more than busy. “Do you think that Aeryn is fairing well Captain America?” The Black Panther questions. “I don’t think so at all look!” Captain America states pointing to Crichton as he exits the building with Aeryn draped over his shoulder. “What happened Crichton?” The Black Panther asks. “She was shot! And I would begin to run if I was you!” He states bluntly, running towards the exit with considerable speed. Cap simply shrugs. “Come on Avengers lets get our asses out of here!” Cap states making his way toward the exit. When they left the military compound they never received much resistance and nobody was following them. Cap suspected that the just wanted rid of them. As they reached the Quntijet, a huge deafening explosion rocked the very ground they stood on. Cap looked up at Crichton to see him wearing a very sadistic smile indeed. “What the hell was that?” Cap questioned walking over to Crichton getting face to face with him. “Justice!” Crichton simply replies. “Innocent soldiers were at that base and they were killed because of YOU!” Cap stated beginning to lose his temper. “That’s what a soldier signs up for when joining the army, you know that!” Crichton replies. “How can you possibly justify that!” Cap enquired. “I wanted to destroy all remnants of that serum destroyed before it ruins anybody else’s life! And the same way you can justify sending an unarmed woman into a highly guarded military compound to rescue me!” Crichton replied with venom in his voice. Cap just stepped back and walked away. “It was her choice Crichton! Her choice!” Iron Man stated. “That don’t matter none. She is still dead and for that I will never forgive you! Let me tell you that this ain’t over yet!” Crichton stated and with that he simply walked away.
