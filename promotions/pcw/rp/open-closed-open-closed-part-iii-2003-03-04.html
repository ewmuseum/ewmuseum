Open, Closed, Open, Closed part III

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04 march 03 The door to the tiny hall shut. Her reactions not being as fast as she would have liked, Lacuna lets out a grunt as Mani leans back on her, and her considerably smaller frame. Jade flips backwards and lands the toe of her boot into his chin, at the same time being forced forward by Lacuna. The smell of death still lingered heavily in Jade’s club and it was not some thing which she appreciated. Jade wanted this place clean and death free as soon as humanly possible. Lacuna wiped the blood from her chin and revealed the butt of the shotgun. Bluntly she forced the handle around the bald head of Mani. “There that’ll keep the buggar down for a while.” Lacuna beamed as her Violet lips presented the words. Jade falls backwards onto the stairs as good hearty laughter fills the room. She was ever so tired, psychologically and physically. She breathed out a sigh as she moaned. “I got a little bit of cleaning up to do. I don’t suppose a lady like you wants to help in such matters?” Lacuna’s smile faded as she tried to ignore her. She bent down and slapped Mani in the face trying to wake him up. -Anything- to avoid doing servant work. She began beating Mani about the face to see if he would wake up at all and unfortunately for her he didn’t. Instead she packed her shotgun back into her black bag and placed a handkerchief over her face. Apparently she didn’t need to wind down with a bit of light dusting, she was only just starting. “Lacuna? You gonna help me?” “I don’t suppose I am.” Her bright grin came back as she tittered queerly. “I still have training to do before Tomorrow, you know?” Jade rolled her eyes and let Lacuna out of the back door. After she had waved Lacuna and her servant Brook out of the alley Jade returned back into the apartment above the strip club and into the hall to deal with the mess. There was nothing there. Well, nothing but the mess and the dead people. Jade was some what shaken by the fact that the man could well still be here. Jade picked up the blue phone which was resting against the wall and lifted the receiver. Pretty soon the Police would be here to deal with this whole fiasco. -[Fade]- It had been four hours since Jade had been released form the police. Those cheeky bastards had the down right Gall to question her for the murders of her few workers and perverts which were trapped in her club. She had been interrogated for the whole night and was released at 7am to wonder the streets until her apartment had been thoroughly thrashed by those bastard’s who belonged in forensics. She had no where to go, little money to get there and a huge weight to get off of her chest before she went and suffocated the very existence out of some poor individual walking along the street. So she had decided to put her excess anger to good use and take a little trip to the gym. Of course that wouldn’t open for another three hours and this made her even more infuriated. She felt like she was going to blow her top if something didn’t happen to help take her mind elsewhere. Food was always the answer on this occasion, well falling swiftly behind harsh alcohol and training. And that is why she was now on her way to sit in a café at the crack of … 7am munching down a big greasy ‘English’ breakfast, the whole trimmings swallowed quickly like a pig at a trough. She licked her full black Hispanic lips as she smelt the food from the café being prepared. Her eyes chased the sign which was about to be put up welcoming customers. “Open, Yay! This is exactly what I need to calm me down temporarily.” Jade was enthused as she pushed a homeless man out of her way to be standing in the front of the queue. She ordered everything twice prior to getting to the cashier and handing over her gold card. Jade sat down in the red fake leather and wood chair and sat back. The knives and forks were attacked to a string under the table. To prevent people like the hobo she just pushed out of the way stealing them and swapping them for tin cans or something of the like. As the greasy food slithered down her throat she felt like she was in heaven. There was nothing like a good cheap meal to tide you over and boost levels of morale after you have had the biggest pile of crap thrown at you and that is exactly what happened. Jesus, Jade had spent so long trying to get her memory back after her little absence of mind thing, after the whole Chryste slaughter thing, she had remembered everything she wanted to forget. Responsibilities are the biggest bitch of them all. You seem to get them when you’re sixteen, earlier if you are unlucky, and don’t freakin’ ditch them to you are one with the wormies. A moment of reflection, if you will. Jade had enjoyed how easily she had taken to things in PCW. Sorting out old deals with Lacuna, and more deals with her cousin, whom she would no doubt later deal with. Lacuna and Jade now seemed to get a long better than they ever did in the Prime Time Central Tag Team Tournament. They seemed to know exactly what to expect from each other this time around and that helped a lot when things came to a head. Lacuna seemed slightly less sane that she had ever appeared before and in ways Jade knew that was why they were now teamed together in a higher class of partnership, above business, fun and love. Jade looked down at the water proof plastic watch which was resting on the cheap ply wood table. Her eyes opened in shock as she pushed the grotesque looking plate long the table. She had gone into one of her day dreams which she was actually renowned for, at least to what friends and family she did have left. Time had -really- passed and now she was looking at the watch in disbelief. “Christ. How the hell did this happen?” Jade shook the watch and pressed it against her ear. “It is eleven O’clock already?” There was already a vagabond munching hungrily at the remains of her breakfast and Jade barely gave it a second thought as she slapped a ten dollar bill in front of him. She felt some how satisfied. Even after all the death which has occurred in the last twenty four hours. -[Fade]- Jade stood in the middle of a blue mat. She wore a white cloth suit with a black belt tied tightly around it. Jade’s black and white hair fell loosely about her partially revealed, but very tanned, shoulders. She stood in a tight stance with her fist pressed tightly together. As the grunts of her tutor signalled her to start, also set a steady rhythm for her to follow as she hit various punches and kicks, capping it off with a high standing axe kick which landed her foot narrowly above the mat. Kerin, her Japanese lady tutor, walked into the light and didn’t show a fraction of emotion as she bowed in front of Jade. Jade knelt quickly for a moment to scoop up a towel and quickly wiped her brow and dropped her suit into her black bag. Her tutor tossed a huge medicine ball at Jade and did that animalistic grunt. Jade span on her heel and kicked the ten Kilogram ball against the wall. Jade nodded and went to finish her training. As Jade went into the other room she practically dived onto the treadmill. The speed was instantly turned on as she fiddled with the disc-man connecting it to her ears. Her head nodded back and forth crazily as her body writhed on the machine. Sweat dripping down her tube top crazily. Finally I get this out of me. The aggression I have expressed endlessly in the past few days is ready on tap. That’s a good thing when I face such a promising little wrestler as Young Tiger. Jade would have to be in the best to forms to excel as she did in the Fatal Four way match up which included Lacuna last week. Speaking of Lacuna, Jade needed somewhere to stay for the next couple of days and she seemed like the perfect target. That’s got to be best for me when I finish the next four way match up for the rising title. It is quite a way to her ‘house’ though, may be best for me if I get going soon. Maybe I can jog there. Jade giggles to her self. That didn’t sound that dumb when I said it in my head. Jade laughs as she dismounts the Treadmill. -[Fade]- Jade stepped up the grey stone gravel. Her army boots crunching heavily down, Jade holds the black rucksack over her shoulder. Her fingers twiddle the black strings between them. She nervously walked up the long steps leading towards the front door. Jade brushes the sweaty hair from his face and bangs the bottom of her fist against the front door. There is a slight delay and impatient as ever Jade beats the door again. The banging reverberated down the long hall as Brook jogged down the strip of red carpet towards the heavy oak door. Rings of sweat formed under his large arms and across his chest as the overweight balding man slowed to a walk, straightening his burnt orange coloured polo top. He had given the usual doormen the day off, so the responsibility of finding who was hiding behind the door now fell to him. He grasped hard on to the black iron handle and twisted, dragging hard as the heavy door heaved open with a loud scraping. “Hey Giles is ‘Cuna here?” “Brook.” He interrupted. Jade handed him her bag and walked right in, pushing through him. “Whatever. You got my bag, I do not intend on stealing anything. I do have this however.” Jade hands a silver chain she found on her first visit and hands it to Brook. She smiled as the ventilation caressed her soft skin. “How many showers do you have in this place?” Jade turned and asked. “Lacuna is training. Allow me to lead you to her.” Jade nodded graciously and followed him up the large split staircase. Lacuna was waiting ready on the lading after hearing the door bell. 'Jade' she growled angrily 'Why are you here? We agreed to tag, not to socialise' Lacuna was pouring with sweat, her exercise gear of black and purple lycra stuck to her like a second skin as she moved slowly down towards the bedraggled Jade. “You want to socialise? OK that is cool with me.” She wasn’t in a real listening mood today. “Are you training? Cool, Can I play?” Jade waited for a reply eagerly, her mouth hung open at the same time her eyes stare blankly, her nerves hidden. Lacuna couldn't believe her ears; she ran a hand through her hair as her mouth opened in awe. Her mind had become clouded and thinking hard. 'You haven't changed a bit have you?' She couldn't help but smile. 'You never trained before, what's come over you?' “Things change honey! I have a title to win you know?” She decided to wait before pouncing the second part on her. “I smell pretty bad. Would it be OK to get a shower first?” Jade asked. 'Jesus! You cheeky cow!' She smiled again; the Jade effect 'You turn up here, push Brook out of the way and then order a shower?' She shakes her head lightly, chuckling a little. 'sure, I might as well be polite to the lower classes' She leads Jade down yet another red carpeted, cream walled hallway and points to a smaller oak door. 'There's a shower, all the necessities are inside. Don't make a mess.' “Yeah this will be so cool living together for a week!” She smacked herself in the forehead. “Uhh. PCW project, help us to…become better tag partners, or something.” Jade quickly stepped into the shower room giggling. Leaving Lacuna shouting, she was confused and banging on the bathroom door. “Jaaaaddddddddeeee! Get your arse out here!” Jade turned on a radio in there and checked through the products hurriedly. -[Fade]-
