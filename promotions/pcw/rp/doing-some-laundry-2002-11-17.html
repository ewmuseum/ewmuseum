Doing Some Laundry

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/02 “Welcome to Texas Stadium, Mr. Nelson” greets a stunningly beautiful brunette woman as The Only Six Star Wrestler in Five Star Wrestling steps out of his long, black stretch limousine. The Greediest Player in the game looks the woman up and down, paying special attention to her firm legs that dangle down from her brown, tight fitting mini skirt. Her heels give her a few added inches of height, as well as accentuate her perfectly toned posterior. As he continues to make his way up, he gives pause as his eyes come across her ample bosom that hide behind a nearly see-through white blouse. Moving right along he finally reaches her face, something akin to a supermodel, with smart looking designer glasses nestled comfortably on her nose. Her silky brown hair is gathered neatly in a bun, completing the delicious journey. Tyler smirks and pops the cuffs on his black Armani suit. “The pleasure is obviously all mine,” he says smoothly. “Are you free for dinner tonight?” Nelson’s confidence catches the young woman off guard. She smiles and blushes, batting her eyes and quickly looking away from Tyler. “Ummm….,” stammers the assistant as she shuffles through her day planner. “Get back to me later,” states Tyler. “I expect an answer by the time I leave.” The executive assistant giggles softly. “Heheh….thank you, Mr. Nelson. Now if you will follow me, Mr. Jones has been expecting you.” Tyler looks down at her perfect ass again and grins. “Gladly.” The flustered assistant takes off into the stadium and Tyler follows, only taking his eyes off of her rear a few times to see where they are going. They hop on an elevator that takes them up to the skybox level. The gorgeous assistant can barely look up while Tyler stares straight ahead, knowing that he has the woman right where he wants her. The elevator dings and comes to a stop, then the doors slide open. The distinctive voice of Dallas Cowboy’s owner Jerry Jones is heard before the doors are even fully open. “Well I’ll be damned,” Jones says. “Tyler Nelson…how the hell are ya boy?” Jerry strides forward with his hand extended and a big smile on his face. Tyler steps off of the elevator and the men shake hands heartily. “Just fine after I met that fox of an assistant you have working for you,” responds Nelson. Jerry peeks over Tyler’s shoulder into the elevator, acknowledging the brunette. “Ah yes, Cassandra is a beauty,” Jerry says with a wink. “I assume you have a dinner date lined up already?” Tyler turns slightly and looks back at Cassandra. “She didn’t give me an answer yet,” he says somewhat disappointedly. “She must not realize that I don’t take no for an answer.” “It’s settled then,” Jerry says as he slaps Tyler on the back. “Cassandra darlin’, go ahead and make reservations for Mr. Nelson and yourself. Make sure you dress real nice for him, too.” Cassandra smiles and nods. “Yessir, Mr. Jones.” Just as the words leave her mouth the elevator doors slide shut. Tyler turns to Jerry and puts an arm around his shoulder. “Damn you always have some hot assistants!” exclaims Nelson. “Yeah, and you always get to fuck ‘em,” retorts the Cowboys owner. The two men share a laugh as they head over to a table next to the skybox window. There are two place settings on the table consisting of fine china and sterling silver utensils. Nelson and Jones sit down on opposite sides of the table, scooting their chairs in and getting situated. When they are settled Jerry begins the conversation. “So, what brings you out here today?” Jones asks. “I was just in the neighborhood and thought I would duck in and say hi,” Tyler responds with a sincere smile in his face. “Bullshit!” says Jerry. Tyler feigns shock for a second, then chuckles. “Okay, you got me. I need to run some money through you.” Jerry leans back a bit and squints one eye as he leers at Tyler through the other. “How much?” Tyler rolls his eyes back as he counts numbers in his head. “Let’s see……about….15 million.” Jerry whistles at the amount and rubs his face with his hands. “Jeezus, Ty.” “What’s the problem?” Tyler asks. “That’s a lot of cash to just dump on me all of a sudden,” explains Jerry as he rubs his forehead. “When do you need it turned over?” “A few days,” responds Tyler quickly. Jerry shoots up from his chair and begins to pace around the skybox. His mind begins to work overtime. Tyler explains further. “Look, Jerry, I need some money fast for a few things that I have coming up within the next week. You have historically been the best at turning over large amounts of cash for me,” says Tyler. “Yeah,” sighs Jerry. “But nothing this large.” Tyler pleads with Jones. “If anyone can do this, you can. You are almost as devious and deceptive as I am. Just run it through the football teams’ merchandise books or something like that.” Jerry frowns and retakes his place at the lunch table. “I heard about your little thing you got going on next weekend. You must be pretty excited.” Nelson smirks an evil smirk as he responds. “You don’t know the half of it. Next Sunday I have the chance to shit on everyone who has ever crossed my path, from Xavier Kannon to Ivan Stanislav to Kid Wonder to Duke Williams and the list goes on and on and on. Don’t think for one second that I won’t pay each and every one of those motherfuckers back for their indiscretions against me.” A chill runs down Jerry’s spine. “You are one cold son of a bitch.” He pauses for a second. “Send the money over. I’ll take care of it and have it back for you in two days.” Nelson smiles and rises from his chair. “I knew I could count on you.” He extends his hand to Jones and the two shake, although Jerry has a rather puzzled look on his face. “No lunch?” Jerry questions. “I had the chef cook up some lobster and steak.” “Nah, gotta run.” says Tyler as he checks his watch. “I need to run a few more errands before I take that knockout to dinner tonight.” Jerry Jones just smiles a crooked smile. “Don’t expect her in to work tomorrow,” Tyler cautions. “I never do,” says Jones. Tyler straightens out his suit and heads back toward the elevator as the scene fades out.
