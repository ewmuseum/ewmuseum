A Picture Not So Perfect

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 6, 2002 Beep! Beep! Beep! The siren on the alarm clock goes off as the radio begins to play. Jaden wakes up finding herself in a motel room. Whose room is this? Where am I? What is going on? These are all the questions that are running through Jaden’s battered head. “Ouch,” Jaden says as she touches her head to find a white cloth tied around her bruised head after being hit with the X-Bomb by Kannon. Jaden gets out of bed wear a big black shirt. She begins to search the room to find any clues of where she is. She walks over to a round table that is situated in the middle of the small room. On the round table is a note especially for Jaden. Jaden, I hope you find your stay here with me comfortable. I am glad you decided to stay with me and I hope that this relationship can work out. I know you said you where not read for a relationship but I honestly hope you change you mind. I know we had differences in the past but we will never know what tomorrow may bring. I have prayed for a new tomorrow and you have come to me like a knight in shining armor. Was it I that saved you from all your pain? We will never know until we walk forward in life together as one. I am not saying all of this because we had sex…. “We had Sex? Who did I have sex with…OH John,” Jaden says aloud shaking her head blushing as she continues reading the letter from John Crichton. “…but I am saying all of this from the heart because I…” Then, there is a knock at the door. Jaden sits the letter down and heads to the door. “Damn…Who is it,” she saying, standing on her tiptoes looking through the peephole seeing no face but flowers. “Umm…it is a deliver for me Ms. Jaden!” Jaden opens the door and the man hands her the flowers. Jaden is just in a lost for words and this is a first! She begins to think. Jaden rapidly shuts the door and continues reading the letter from John. “…Your friend, hopefully more,John Crichton” Jaden begins to reminisce. Thinking back when she came encounter with John at the gas station when he picked her up and took her to the PCW training area. As Jaden begins to think she also begins to wonder the house looking around the house seeing what kind of person he is. You can always tell how a person is from the way they live. As she walks she sees picture of a beautiful woman maybe in her thirties or younger. They are everywhere. “Either he is stalking her or they are still together.” At the bottom of a self-portrait of the woman has the name Aeryn. Jaden inflicts a perplexed look on her face. She wants to find out whom this Aeryn is and why her picture is everywhere. Is Jaden jealous of a person she is seeing on the wall before her? Eyes pure green with hatred hovers over her. The last time this happen she wound up in jail and lost her job. The scene ends with the camera doing a close up on the letter. “…Oh, Jaden, one more thing, I will be away for awhile so don’t go wondering off. You know you can’t after that accident the other day and be good while I am away.” “Sorry babe, I have business to take care of. Oh yes, I’m a jealous bitch! Today will be a busy day for me!” To Be Continued…
