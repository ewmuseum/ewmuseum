Date: 12/16/02 Racing through the mireIn an 18 wheeler truckOver the roads we goOver endless muck Entrails flap and swayMaking spirits brightAlong with the half empty bottle of Jack Resting on his dashboard tonight Oh jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the way Oh what fun to drive your truckOver an old woman's Chevrolet Hey Jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayOh what fun, to crush and maimOn this, oh Christmas day A day or two agoClyde thought he'd take a rideAnd soon little miss BethanyWas seated at his side They spread their wholesome cheerThey talked and talked aloudBut when the subject came to sexMisfortune came around Though Clyde had but one ballStill not his fault he claimedInstead her lack of a sizeable chestHe said it was to blame So over the seat she flewLanding in his lapA frenzied wail of teeth and nailsTo shut his big fat yap Oh jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayOh what pain it is to endureBeing bitten on your way Hey Jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the way A groin full of teethIsn't the nicest wayTo spend your Christmas day The wheel jerked left and rightAs Clyde did yelp in painHe bashed and thrashed the little girl's headTo break this grotesque chain But she would not be freedFrom her crotchal gripShe merely threw her head to the sideAnd things began to rip Spinning the wheel to the leftThe truck began to turnVeering off the overpassA squeal of tires was heard Oh jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayDriving skills are hard to keepWith a nutjob in your way Hey Jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayFalling down to mother earthTo the ground below today The truck came to a stopLeaking its gasolineSlowly creaking open nowThe door began to lean Bursting open with a snarlThe burned man did leap forthDragging with him BethanyAs heavy as a corpse A fateful spark then flewLanding in the leakOf flammable fluid on the groundAll he could say was 'eek' Oh jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayFlying through the frosty airTowards a tree not far away Hey Jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayBones are breaking, faith is shakingOn this painful day Laying still he wasBleeding from every poreThe burned man opened up his eyesTo behold a flying door The thought of this odd sightDid run through his mindIt left him just as quickly thoughAs pain flared through his spine A broken back he hadHe was unable to moveThe flying severed truck door thenCame falling for his doom The lights had faded outThe birdies they did soarBut even merciful darkness yieldedTo Bethany's mighty roar Oh jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayParalyzed under a moldy bridgeWith a girl jumping on his sack Hey Jingle hell, jingle hellJingle all the wayWith a smile the demon thoughtHe hated Christmas day
