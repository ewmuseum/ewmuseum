Date: 28.11.02 (#4 FF) “Shot of whiskey, please”, Abner mumbles in the general direction of the bar tender. Obediently, the young women turns, reaches under the counter, and pulls out an old bottle of alcohol. Leaning over the counter, Abner can’t help but notice the women’s tight little behind. Offhandedly, Abner remarks “The right stuff….” The women turns, and looking up, meets Abner’s lustful gaze. “Excuse me?” Quickly recovering, Abner is at no loss for words; “Whiskey….the right stuff…..” She shrugs. “Of course.” “Of course”, Abnert agrees. And turning his attention back to his fellow drinkers, he greedily eyes the prospects. So, who will it be? The biker, fat one sitting next to the jukebox, the pimp……no, I can do better than this, much better….. “Here you go, sir” “Huh?” “Your drink.” “Oh.” The lady places a clear shot glass filled with an intoxicating liquor on the counter. “Thank you kindly.” The women smiles back, “You’re welcome.” Under his breath, Abner ads, “And you’re very welcome…..” Fortunately, the lady doesn’t seem to pick this up and she turns around to tend back to her business. Abner lifts the glass up into the air, points it toward the stars, and grumbles “Here’s to you Third Party……may y’all burn in Hell……” And he meant it. Preparing to drink his shot, Abner lets out a sigh of discontent. His deep green eyes seem to have lost some of the liveliness obtained during his youthful days. With one hand he reaches up and strokes at the stubby colony of interlocked hair that makes up his dark, rough-cut goatee. You’d think that with all this time, the last four months out of prison, he’d find some time to get a proper makeover, but for Abner at least, this is not so. He’s had other, more important things on his mind the last few months; things like his future….. Running his fingers through the softness of his straight brown hair, a soothing comfort to Abner since early childhood, with the other hand he downs the vile potion in one gulp, and with that finished, gets to his feet. Pushing the stool forward, Abner pulls out an assortment of bills from his jacket pocket, and produces a twenty. Tossing onto the counter, he turns and begins to make his way through the crowd toward the exit. To hell with this place……as a matter of fact, to hell with the whole goddamn city of Boston! No one ‘round here would make a good club member….and by-God, I’d imagine that they ain’t very interested in it an’way. You see, our friend Abner has come to start back up a “club” which, by its rightful nature, the original name of which belongs to him: Third Party. Abner came up with the goddamn idea. Cornelius H., the current leader, was merely an “asset”, and in Abner’s opinion, was “nothin’ more than yuppie bastard from the city.” As Abner, half-mindedly of course, concludes, is that he was the Third Party! Pretty egocentric, but in many ways, also very true. Corn was almost certainly the smarter of the two, but Abner was a part of the brain (a small part that is, but nonetheless a part), and he was all the bronze. So in some sense, his opinion is pretty valid. Four years in prison…..bahh! That son-va’-bitch lucky I don’t go find his sorry ass and whup it from one side of Vermont to the next! He stole my club, he stole my friends, and that no-good, snot-nosed, hippie bastard stole my life! Abner clenches his fists in anger. Slowly, as the circulatory system begs for mercy, he gradually loosens his grip. Ahh! But to hell with it! It’s a big world, and there’s plenty of other clubs that need a leadin’ from a fella like myself. I ain’t gonna lose my mind over nobody, ‘specially from no goddamn hippie, and ‘sides, he’ll get his someday…..he’ll get his….. And with that, the former cult leader shakes it off, and makes his exit….. But he doesn’t quite make it out the door. “Hey! Stranger, ain’t you forgettin’ somethin’!?” The hickish accent is related to Abner’s, but it’s not exact. No….. Southnah’….. Abner struts out his chest, turns, and faces his opponent. Well I’ll be….. Surprisingly, the man is even bigger than he is. Abner stares intently down at the man’s feet, and making his way up, sizes up his fellow redneck. But there’s really no comparison: for one of the few times in his life, Abner is truly outdone. The muscular mass of southern pride is twice the size of him. Abner would guesstimate him at six-five or six, and roughly about 285, 290 pounds. But Abner’s not scared. As the fellow takes another step forward, Abner follows his lead, himself moving forward a few steps. Close now, almost face to face, the two men stare each other down. Ah, shit!..... But right before either man can make a move, the man extends a hand to Abner. The young PCWer gladly accepts it. “The name’s Burt, Burt Hurtley…..pleasure to meet ya sir.” Abner smiles at his newly made acquaintance, and in a friendly, yet cautious gesture, returns the greetings. “The name’s Abner, Abner…..”, the hick goes blank for a moment, “…..my friends call me Abner.” “I know who y’are.” “Well, that is ‘til now, ya see, I don’t have any friends no more.” “Yes, I reckon you don’t…..” The two then step back and begin to get a good look at each other in a formal, non-intimidating way. At second glance, the guy doesn’t seem too tough. A tall man, but a kind of tired looking one. He’s got the Texan/Cowboy kind of look going with long brown hair, an uncleanly shaven face, deep brown eyes, and rough textured skin. For attire he sports ripped blue jeans, a plain blue shirt, rattlesnake-skin, tan colored cowboy boots, and to top it all off, a nice brown leather cowboy hat matted down on his head. Abner on the other hand, doesn’t look all that much better. Our favorite hick, with his raggy old blue-jeans, brown steeled-toe boots, and roughly worn red and black checkered jacket, seems to also possess a taste for fashion-unfriendliness. His checkered shirt, long-sleeved, is worn over a plain white tee-shirt, one that besides for a small pocket on near his heart, is barren of all décor. But Abner’s defense, at least his clothing is clean. This cannot be said for his southern-fried friend. “So, you’re the famous ol’ Abner, eh?” “Yes, yes I am.” And suddenly, the connection is made. It becomes clear now, the significance of this person, for you see, he too is a former Third Party member! Yes, that’s right, a simple mark, one on the left side of his face, verifies this. A small, dark marks makes its way in snake-like fashion across the bottom of his forehead. The southern redneck is forced to constantly squint his left eye as the scar does, and probably always will, have a negative impact on the effectiveness of this poor man’s nerves. But Abner’s not impressed, and, to be honest, he doesn’t feel bad in the least. You see, when you’re in the Third Party, there is no physical evidence of your membership; the only membership confirmation, an oath that you take in the beginning. But as far as the marks go, these are given out only to individual who are, or who have…….misbehaved. Ones, such as a Abner, you know, someone disobedient, or someone that attempted to leave. Bastards. And this poor man obviously did something of that nature. That psychopath, Corn, as Abner heard, started this practice immediately after he had been sentenced to prison, and Corn had taken over. Apparently, everyone in the cult who had decided to side with Abner, were the firsts to bear these burdens……though he himself was officially the first. You see, that night when the final fight took place, when Abner went unconscious, something must’ve happened. When Abner woke up (in the hospital bed of course) the next morning, there was a new formation on his head. Now, a dark scar runs two-inch long: a gash running horizontally below his right eye. Something that the doctor’s (before the cops took him away), assured Ab was permanent. So, as one might surmise, Abner is not one to have mournful feelings toward this particular redneck. Anyway, Abner’s at the point where even his own scar, for the most part, doesn’t even bother him. If he has it, the bastard probably deserves it anyway. As if he’s reading Abner’s thoughts, the cowboy steps forward and prepares a speech. Abner, playing his part, makes his move on Q. 'So, um.....how'd you.....ah.....get your mark?' And of coarse, the very un-shy Abner is only faking his apparent concern. Or do I really want to know? The cowboy, tipping his hat to Abner, begins to speak. 'Well ya see, musta’ been ‘bout eight-nine weeks ago, when my club ‘eld a surprise meetin’.' 'Your club?' Abner remarks, specifically stressing the word ‘your’. 'Yes, my club.' And after a minute of thought, the cowboy adds 'Oh, you mean you don’t know?' Obviously, you stupid bastard….. But Abner’s dares not say it out loud. 'Know what?' 'Well, there ain’t just one club no more……ya see, Third Party’s gone out and has gotten itself too damn big…..' Third Party divided? Hmm…… This man is finally starting to peak his interest. 'How big?' 'Oh, I’d reckon about hundred, maybe hundred-fifty members……but to be quite shore, I don’t really know. See, the boss keeps all the stats to himself ya know…..don’t quite trust nobody with that type of info.' 'Well that’s understandable…..' 'Yeah, well there’s a few of us who didn’t quite agree with his 'rule the whole goddamn thing by my lonesome' policy. And word does get around you know. From what I’ve heard, sources tell me that there is three or so other clubs, all ‘eaded with leaders appointed by Corn.' So, three different clubs, over a hundred members, huh? That old bastard’s doing better than I thought……' 'So where are these……these 'other' clubs located?' 'I really don’t know. But I do know that Third Party is spreadin’ all over New England. Now ours be in Boston, and from what we know…..mainly to do with the legend we heard of the famous Abner……there’s one other in V’ahmont somewheres, and then there’s a’course one other too. But the one up north, Vermont-way, burned to the ground……' Yeah, I’d know…… '…..And I don’t reckon that they re-built it yet.' 'I see……' Abner, pretending to be interested, scratches his chin 'in thought', though in reality, he’s only entertaining this character. Deep down, all he wants to do is move on with life, pursuit his career in PCW, and maybe someday even get a little revenge on Corn. But as far as Third Party goes, they can go to hell. In Abner’s mind, he is……or was rather……Third Party, and now that he’s changed, the club has seized to exist. 'Anyway, that night when they called the goddamn surprised meetin’, I didn’t expect it. Matter of fact, I was out drinkin’…… Imagine that….. '…..And Corn left a message on the machine. Well, accordin’ to the Third Party rulebook……' More like a bible to Corn. '…..No matter what time, day night, come hell or high water, you gotta make every single meetin’, includin’ the surprise ones. So I got the message on mah answerin’ machine ‘bout, oh nine-ten clock at night, and the meetin’ was to be held at 10:15……calssified location you know…..and it takes me ‘bout twenty minutes just to reach the damn spot! So needless to say, stoned like a coon up a tree, I crawled down to that goddamn place, and you know what, I was late by six goddamn minutes……and……well…….' He never manages to finish the line. 'And so you got that.' Abner points over to the man’s scar. Nodding his head in agreement, 'And so I got this.' ? Ludicrous. With a heavy sigh, the man steps back and takes in a deep breath. 'But I reckon I done deserved it through……see, the club’s always right.' And then, thinking better of it, the man adds 'But you, well, I’m sure you didn’t deserve it neither…..' 'Damn right……You’re damn right…..' 'But anyway, sorry to bother you sir, but I’d best not discuss such issues of the club with an outsider……Sir, you know the rules.' 'Goddamn right I do, indeed, I wrote the frigin things.' The cowboy nods his head to show that he understands. And yet again tipping his hat to Abner, he turns to leave. But Abner calls out to him. 'So, what’s old Corny got to say about me!' The cowboy, obviously a little stunned, possibly even uncomfortable with this remark, turns to address Ab. 'Well, he told us all that you did for the club……the people you brought in, the ideas you came up with, the precedents you set…..basically, all that you did for the club…..Why?' 'So…..I, I’m good?' 'Well yeah……' The cowboy raises an eyebrow, 'Why wouldn’t you be?' Abner shrugs. 'Just wonderin’.' The cowboy takes a step forward and cocks his head to the side. 'May I ask you a question as well, sir?' 'Shoot.' The southern redneck seems somewhat agitated now. 'Well, you referred to the boss, Corn, as, well……as somethin’ you shouldn’t of. Soooo, I’s just wonderin’ if back in your day you was allowed to get away with such name callin’…..or was it just ‘cause of your position?' Abner is taken back. 'What? 'Corny!' The redneck shuffles from side to side. 'Uh…yeah, that’d be it.' Abner glares over at his southern friend. And then, in all-not-so-surprising Abner fashion, he loses it. Glaring up at the cowboy, 'What the hell are you talking about!? By Jesus Christ, I think, that if I want to call a chicken a chicken, a bastard a bastard......or a cocksuckin' whore a cocksuckin' whore, than I'm damn-well entitled to! See here, I ain't the frigin subordinate son-va-bitch to no Bastard, and that includes ol' Horny Corny, God rest his frigin, back-stabbin' soul! Now back when I was in charge of the party, we were cult of hicks and honor goddamnit! We all were treated as equals, and if you had a problem with authority, you settled in the parking lot! And by-God, that's just the way it should be. So why don't you just take your disgustingly loyal, southern-fried ass back to Corn, and tell that 'holier than thou' bastard to take this.....' Abner raises his fists in the air, '.....And shove it up his ass!' The cowboy is at loss for words. After what he did to you, after what that freakin hippie did to your face, and with you not standin' up for yourself, this is for your own good..... And so Abner lets his fist go. And well, 'cause I just feel like doin' it. Crack. With that, Abner turns on his heels ad makes his way to he exit, finally managing to leave the bar. If it were not for the near-flawless way in which Abner had laid out a six-and-a-half foot muscle-man, and especially because of the apparent ease in which the deed was enacted, perhaps the owner/authorities would have approached him......but not this time; Today, Abner would get away with it. Peering back over his shoulder, Abner officially the ends the conversation: 'So, why don't you quit the club and get a goddamn life!?' He was of course, and quite appropriately, half-way speaking to himself. Why, oh why did I ever want to get back into it? Cult, club......whatever you want to call 'em......they're not for me. But ya know, let's face it, they're not for me, but they're part of me. So for now anyway, I guess I'd better take some time before I think about goin' on another recruiting mission......maybe focus on more important things, like, oh, lets say......my career in PCW! But then again, who's to say? Maybe someday I'll be back......back in Third Party. As long as I control he party, and not the other way around, it'll be okay...... He stops and pauses for a moment, slowly letting his mind absorb the thoughts. In the final analysis though, all that shit is bad.....All it does is make monsters out of men...... Pausing for another moment. But then again, I like monsters......
