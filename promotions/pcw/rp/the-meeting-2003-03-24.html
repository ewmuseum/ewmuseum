The meeting...

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within the meeting room of Semore Smoke’s hideout. The usual groups of men that work under Smoke are seated at the table and it’s time for Smoke’s weekly briefing with everyone. The last time the group had convened, Smoke had given a few orders about a prisoner of theirs that may know something about the murder of Semore Smoke’s murder. Plus there were updates on the Mafia’s net worth in dollars and how much they had earned that day. Today will be extensions of those plus new business to begin the week with. Smoke enters the vast room from the far entrance that is across from the main entrance elevator corridor that leads up and outside to the ground level. The door that Smoke exits is obviously his office, but you can’t see the inside of the office as greenish brown smoke flows through the door as Smoke steps into the meeting room. Smoke is wearing a dress suit, in fact, it’s the same armoni suit that Smoke was wearing when “interrogating” the kangaroo that he kidnapped from AJ Trinidad to give Trinidad a scare and something to worry about. Smoke sits down at the head of the table, his two guards sit at the end of the table where he’s sitting but opposite each other. The older men at the table look relieved that it’s time for the meeting and wait for Smoke to begin the meeting with his questions to his own workers... Semore Smoke: Well, today is the day for our weekly report. So guys, tell me something, are we in set up with any allied families outside the state of New Jersey for the extension of the Bordon control outside the state? {{{{A man that is seated about three seats away from Smoke on Smoke’s left stands up to answer the question asked...}}}} Man: Yes sir. The Jones family in New York City has cooperated and we’re in talks with the controlling family of New York, the Stevens, about extension into their territory. But that might catch a hefty price for us to pay. As for other states nearby, Pennsylvania already has set-ups for our family to fortify and the same goes for West Virginia. {{{{Smoke strokes the hair on his chin from his goatee and looks as if he’s thinking about the situation...}}}} Semore Smoke: How much will it cost us in New York? {{{{The man looked like he was about to sit down but he stands back up to answer the new question...}}}} Man: It’ll cost us approximately two million dollars. {{{{Smoke raises his eyebrows from the high figure but then motions for the man to sit and turns to his financial aid across the table from the other man and motions to him with another question...}}}} Semore Smoke: How much money do we have today, after all we have earned, John? {{{{John stands up and stares at a piece of paper on the desk as he stands up. He then looks at Smoke with a grin to show that the figures are good as he answers...}}}} John: You alone right now are making two million a week, the entire family is getting forty million a week and there is another fifty million in reserve, so we have enough to probably buy half the country if you wanted to. {{{{Smoke breaks out into some chuckles as he hears this from his financial correspondent...}}}} Semore Smoke: Heh... good, real good. Then let it be so, give the Stevens two mill to let us expand to New York, finish the negotiations with the Jones family as well, but since Stevens is above them, once Stevens allows us in, then Jones will back down as well. We’re taking over gentlemen and this will be a time to remember in the history of the Bordon family. Now, has there been any headway in the investigation and how are the cops helping us? {{{{The man on Smoke’s right, who must be Smoke’s head of security issues stands up to answer the question...}}}} Man #2: Well sir, the person who we had subdued last week for questioning didn’t talk, so we did what you ordered, he’s now helping heat this building in the furnace. But what we have figured out is that the person was not a Mafia member or has anything to do with organized crime, in fact, whoever killed you father, didn’t even know who your father was. As for the police, the ones that we have on our payroll are working extra hard on finding any evidence they can on the murder, and the ones that aren’t in our back pocket are doing as minimal as possible, but are working the case in fear of you. Now, all that we need to do is wait until more comes up on the situation. {{{{Smoke nods in understanding as the lead guard sits down...}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, I’m sure we’ll get progression on that in the coming weeks, but right now, the situation at hand, are we all ready and set up for this Tuesday and Friday nights? {{{{Everyone at the table nods their heads in “yes” and says “yes” at the same time...}}}} Semore Smoke: Good, AJ Trinidad is on my mind right now and I’d love to really mess around with a bit like he thought he was doing with me last Wednesday night. Crespo will be the bait, let’s see how Trinidad reacts. Now, El Diablo will have to get the wrath of left over anger this Friday. I hope like hell he wins the PCW World title tomorrow night, because if he does, then our title could very well be a title match, and if not, then it will be great to say that I came back and a little over a week later, I defeated the World champion. My two guards here, you two will be joining me to the arena, just in case AJ wants to do anything in my match with El Diablo, but maybe I should talk with Flaymin Aymin about a possible job opportunity for him under the Bordon family this Friday night... Hmm... now, is there anything else that I should be told about? {{{{Everyone at the table shakes their heads “no” and Smoke stands up along with his two guards...}}}} Semore Smoke: Good, now I should get going, I’m gonna make an appearance on Turmoil to deliver Trinidad a special speech, and then once I get past Diablo, then Trinidad will be a synch. You’re all dismissed... {{{{The meeting seeming to be almost military-like is over and every sits back in their chairs relaxing and some discuss what just went on in the meeting and what their doing this week in assignment wise. Smoke exits the room leaving his two guards at his office door, this time you can see a bit more of the office and you see a few video games and a huge bong in the center of the room. The door closes and the scene fades on the nameplate on the door that reads “Mr. Smoke Bordon”...}}}}
