Inside the Prison Yard with the new Extreme Champ

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/02 Scene shows the prison yard at Amaranth Penitentiary, the maximum security compound in Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. Several inmates have gathered in the courtyard around 'Swamp Dawg' Karl LeBlanc. He is lifting weights while Warden Riley looks on. LeBlanc has chosen to wear his brand new PCW Extreme Championship belt around his waist while he pumps iron. Warden: Come on Swamp, push it! Now that you have that belt everyone's gonna be gunning for you. It's a whole new ballgame, and it starts this Friday against Mark Estep. I've already petitioned the PCW front office to consider making this a title match. Inmate #1: You're gonna put the belt on the line already? Warden: Swamp's made it very clear to me that he intends to be a fighting champion. Isn't that right, Swamp? LeBlanc nods his head as he pushes the bench press bar above his chest. The bar bends due to the extreme weight stacked on both sides. It takes two men to spot LeBlanc. Warden (points at LeBlanc and smiles): Look at that asshole! He's a tough sumbitch isn't he? Warden Riley picks up a bullpen and shouts Warden: DOES ANYONE HERE IN THIS PRISON YARD WANNA FIGHT LeBLANC??? He needs a little warm-up before Friday... Inmate #2: F*ck no, man! Inmate #3: HELL NO! Inmate #4: I ain't messin' wit Swamp! Warden: Jeez, what a bunch of f*ckin' p*ssies... Mad Dog, you killed four people down in Texas, decapitated their German Shepherd and set their house on fire. Are you tellin me you're scared to go one on one with LeBlanc? Mad Dog: Man, I don't back down to no one... but I ain't no dumb mother f*cker neither. I wanna be able to walk for the rest of my life. Warden: Fine, have it your way. LeBlanc doesn't need you. He'll be able to kick Mark Estep's ass whether he warms up or not. That sonofabitch will learn NEVER to interfere in LeBlanc's matches again. And as for 'Uncle' Jason Core... the cheap bastard that sucker punched me at Turmoil... if he so much as peeks his head through the curtain during this match, I will personally take my f*cking billy-club across the back of his skull. Then, I'll handcuff him to the ringpost and let LeBlanc make meatloaf out of his face. Scene fades as LeBlanc fires the loaded bench press bar off his chest. He lets out an earth shaking roar as the multiple 100-pound plates clang together.
