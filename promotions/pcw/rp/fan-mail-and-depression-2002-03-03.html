Fan Mail and Depression

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 *Monologue* My lady has spread her legs open to all the so called talent in wrestling. Just so happens my lady is a prostitute named Primetime Championship Wrestling. Life in the PCW couldn’t get worse. I hate to say it but she has opened her legs for Karl LeBlanc. Why would she do a thing like that? I have no clue. Probably sorry for him getting all that prison sex from Looks like Big Daveylicious hasn’t paid the prostitute. Sure, Big D has gotten numerous title shots but every shot he gets screwed. How so? In about most of the title shots he’s had their was a run in. Hell, Brandi used Rent-A-Hero and a Feminist group to retain the Extreme title, Big Daveylicious . Corey Williams almost lost his Rising Star title but was saved by Unstoppable Eugene. Every time he seems to get a shot he gets screwed some how. But instead of crying about it he holds his anger in. Just last Sunday Sabotage he lost to Tim Walden. After Walden had expressed that Big D hasn’t done anything in the past 4 months to be known for. Big D can’t help it if the Women’s division was lazy. But with them gone it angers him and he holds it in. The question how long can a man keep his anger to himself? (The scene fades to a walls on the hallway. Various photo's are along the highway. One of the photo's seem to be missing. We continue to stroll through the hallway to what seems to be a very large extravagant living room. As we enter the room the first thing we notice is a Sony Big Screen TV,on top of it has a VCR, also on top of the VCR is a DVD Player.In front of the TV is a Leather Lazy Boy recliner,on the left, right of the Arm Chair is two leather couches, in the middle of the room sits the glass tableIt's what's on the TV that catches our attention We zoom into it. On the screen of the TV is last week's Sunday Sabotage match between Big Daveylicious and Tim Walden. The match is nearly finished. We zoom into it as the match is being played. Big D gets to his feet and gloats about his recent move. He walks over to the fallen Walden who is still thrashing about on the mat in pain for his balls have just been moved up to his throat, and Big D smiles. Walden then delivers a drop-toehold on the unsuspecting Big D and Big D goes crashing hard to the mat. Walden gets up and reaches in his tights and pulls out a nut-cup a throws it to ringside. Big D gets up and is gets a Waldenplex for his troubles. Walden seizes the opportunity and walks to Big D's feet and locks him into the Syndrome.* Dave: THE SYNDROME!!!!!!! *Jeff makes a masturabatory gesture with his right hand.* *Big D tries to make it to the ropes as Bob Charlie is in position to see if Big D will submit, after several seconds of experiencing the Syndrome, Big D finally taps out. Missing his opportunity on a shot at the TV title. Suddenly, the match pauses, rewinds, and replays Big D tapping out to the Syndrome. The match pauses right about when we see Big D tap out in pain. Obviously someone else is watching this. We zoom away from the TV and turn to see Big Daveylicious sitting on the leather lazy boy recliner with the VCR remote in one hand and a beer bottle in the other. Clad in black pair of Tommy Hilfinger jeans, a blue fire printed poly shirt, Oakley sunglasses, and black boots. One could only think of what is going inside Big D’s head right now.) Big Daveylicious: If I only I didn’t showboat about that. Maybe I have done nothing in 4 months. Voice: What’s with all the pouting, Big D? (Big D moves scoots his chair to the left to see his lackey, Michael Graves carrying a mail bag over his shoulder. Yes, Michael has his gothic attire on with face paint of course.) Michael Graves: I thought you quit drinking? You can get lung cancer? Big Daveylicious: That’s smoking, idiot. What’s with the mail bag? Michael Graves: What I got in this mail bag is something to make that frown turn upside down! It’s fan mail! Big Daveylicious: Fan mail? I have fans? Michael Graves: Yes, you do. Didn’t you hear the chorus of cheers you got when you hit a low blow on Tim Walden last Sunday. Big Daveylicious: They’re not my fans they cheered because I hit Tim in the nuts. Plus, Tim screwed them over with all that CJS crap. Michael Graves: They would’ve cheered if your fist landed in anybody else's balls. Big Daveylicious: No, the gay audience would of... Michael Graves: Yeah, gay people are homosexuals! Big Daveylicious: Graves, what do you want? Michael Graves: I want you to read what A. Gay Canadian has sent you in the mail! (Michael takes out a envelope and hands it to Big Daveylicious.) Big Daveylicious: His name is A. Gay Canadian? Michael Graves: That’s what the envelope says, silly. (Big D opens the envelope unsurely. He pulls out a piece of folded paper out of the envelope. He unfolds it and reads it out loud.) Big Daveylicious:{reading out loud}“Dear Big Daveylicious, I am a very big fan of yours. As a matter of fact your my Canadian hero.” I’m not Canadian. “You and Brandi seem to not like each other. I want to know behind the scenes are you and Brandi good friends?” Oh, yeah. She is my special friend! We go camping, we go fishing, we go bike riding, and we go to the mall. We like the same clothes, same music and we talk about girls! Michael Graves: How come I’m never invited. Big Daveylicious: Because you were more makeup than she does. “Your special fan, A. Gay Canadian” That wasn’t bad. It was kinda heart warming that I have a fan. I mean with a name like A. Gay Canadian you would think he would of talked about spandex or my ass or something but no he didn’t so. Stay straight , A. Gay Canadian! Michael Graves: Here’s another letter from Dia Orea. (Graves passes Big D the letter. He snatches it.) Big Daveylicious: Graves, honestly if this letter came from diarrhea than it be covered in crud. (Big D looks at the letter.) Big Daveylicious: Damn, her name is Dia Orea. I take it Dia is short for Diane or Diana. (He begins to read it out loud just like the first one.) Big Daveylicious: {reading out loud} “Dear Big Daveylicious, I find you kinda cute and handsome. I want to know if their is a Mrs. Daveylicious? Love,Dia Orea”(Suddenly, sad music plays in the background as Big D takes off his sunglasses.) Big Daveylicious: Well, Dia Orea, there was a someone. But there just wasn't enough room in my life for romance and a career. You understand, I was on the road a lot being a wrestler and... set in my evil ways. But for one... brief shining moment ...in my life...eh...eh (Big D sniffles and a tear is about to go down his cheek but he wipes it away, puts his sunglasses back on looking down at the ground as Michael Graves pats him on the shoulder showing concern for his mentor.) Michael Graves: Are you ok? Big Daveylicious: Yeah. {sniff} I’ll live. Michael Graves: Good because we don’t want you to commit homicide! Big Daveylicious: That’s suicide you idiot. (Big D turns to the TV, turns off the VCR, and suddenly, a D-Kay Calyx promo which is entitled “Time For Review” is on.) D-Kay: {From TV}That's the Big Daveylicious of the group so far. As all the hype, but doesn't perform well. Next? (Big D throws his bear bottle through the big screen TV. The TV shatters, breaking the screen of the TV.) Michael Graves: Now, was that really necessary? (Big D gives Graves a sharp turn with that the scene fades to black.)
