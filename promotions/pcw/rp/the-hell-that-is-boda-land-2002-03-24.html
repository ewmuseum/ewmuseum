The Hell that is...Boda-Land!

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March 2002 The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#2 for Sabotage… Location: Boda-Land, in the depths of Chris Chase’s mind, in Albequrque, New Mexico. Time: Unknown, Probably early Afternoon on 24th March 2002. Musical Accompaniment: ‘The Fall of Man’ by Matthew Good Band – Download it NOW! Chapter Three: The Hell that is Boda-Land: Prologue. “Welcome one and all, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls…to the Biggest, Baddest place on earth – Boda-Land: The Celebratory Theme Park to the Greatest PCW World Heavyweight Champion in the history of the planet…” “Boda-Dogs, get your Boda-Dogs here…Get your hot Boda-Dogs fresh and ready to be eaten. Sir, you look confused…you could use a Boda-Dog…” “What? I need a Boda-Dog like I need a Hernia. Don’t you recognise me? I’m ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…you know, the real Chris Chase. Not some buffoon dressed up like Jesus parading around the front gate being spanked by a buffoon dressed up as Boda…” “Oh sure, you’re like the twentieth person to claim that you’re Chris Chase. Chris Chase could have potentially stopped all of this, but the fact remains that he never really existed…” “Sure, Chris Chase being a ficticious character…that’s good coming from a lackey of Boda-cakes. Now then…about Chris Chase…” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ You know cretins, sometimes I’ve wondered what the world would be like if I had never existed, if I’d never defeated Boda back in PCW2, if his ascension hadn’t been curbed by the likes of myself. Sure, call me weird for thinking that way…but sometimes you just have to ponder these things. Like, if I never existed, would the whole world be enveloped in boredom? On my 23 years on this planet, I’ve seen things the likes of the general public would probably never be able to fully understand. I’ve witnessed phenomena that goes unnoticed to the public eye. I’ve been subjected to Giant Demon Chickens, Giant Talking Celestial Heads, Rampant Russian Conspirators, Talk Show hosts, Midgets, Morons…you name it, I’m likely to have come across it in some form of another. And funnily enough, this has been happening for the past year, it’s been almost a year since I began my career in PCW2…laying the foundation for the months to come. If I were not here to experience such oddities, would you know of such things? For I to not have existed, this would mean that all traces of Chase-dom would vanish into thin air. The very city of Calgary, Alberta, Canada would simply just be a plain old city in Canada. Dustin Downey would be working in a fast food joint, Mark Estep would be in an Insane Asylum, Shawn Prodigy would have lost to Ivan Stanislav. PCW punters across the seven continents would be without their vital dosage of bonefide entertainment from the Celestial Cowboy…and they wouldn’t even know what that meant. Addictive catchphrases would cease to exist, Tim Walden would be without a scapegoat, and New Breed would be minus one overwhelmingly talent portion of its talent roster. And, most sickeningly of all, Chris Sloboda would be given a free reign over the PCW…calling the shots, dedicating theme parks to himself… The Hell that is Boda-Land, the Hell that is the non-existence of the singularly brilliant man in ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, and the Hell that leaves you with no other choice than to ponder exactly what would happen… A titanic statuette of Boda-Boy himself dominates the screen as we fade in, waving one hand in a mechanical motion in a near-friendly manner. It is late afternoon in Boda-Land, and the theme park is still pretty much bustling as the camera pans down from the statue dedicated to the embodiment of tedium. Children joyfully skip along the bricked paths of the park, wearing Boda merchandise…singing along to “It’s A Boda World After All” as they pass a hotdog stand. There I stand, at least, if I existed. Leaning on the Boda-Dog cart, I frown at the whole thing presented to me in front of my very eyes. With an annoyed snort, I watch the rides and rollercoasters wing their way around and around…people screaming in joy mid-ride. Mascots litter that park as much as empty drinks cans and food wrappers…as the pedophiles they are, molesting little children to a non-existent reaction from the parents. Beside me, the Boda-Dog vendor sighs, also leaning on the cart in pretty much the same way that I do….:: Children: It’s a Boda World after all, it’s a Boda World after all, It’s a Boda World after all!! Though Two Ton is Wide and Wild Deuces Divide…it’s A BODA WORLD AFTER ALL!! MGB Lyrics: “You Pray for the Sheep, I get closer to Hell, I stand on the hill…and then I run down…I look in your eyes, I look for your prize…you beg me to stop…” ::. The vendor glares at me with an over-confident grin on his face, watching the little children pass the cart. There is much malice and hatred in his chat, seemingly hating Boda pretty much as much as I do. Rubbing a hand through his medium length brown hair, he gesticulates with his free hand…explaining to yours truly the story of behind Chris Chase being a ficticious character….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: Oh give me a break, Boda’s a slave driver…he can’t do anything but satisfy his own massive ego. I tell you, if I had the muscular fortitude, I’d go take that nimrod on myself…now then, Chris Chase is a ficticious character made up by Boda to make his ego a little larger. He constantly makes gay references towards Chase, trying to mock an imaginary character in as best a way as he could, calling Chase his biggest challenge, or biggest pain in the ass or something like that. In other words, for everything Boda does wrong, he uses Chase as a scapegoat… ::. Grunting acknowledgingly, I smirk as this information comes to light. The inner-workings of Chris Sloboda, it’s more than what I originally had come to expect. Inside that minute little brain of his, I had presumed that nothing but the occasional swab of tumbleweed ever blew through his mind…stimulating his brain to actually do something entertaining. But when a man likes to preserve his own ego simply by trying to make a mockery out of a long-time enemy…then of course, it’s going to be something of this scale. Maybe I totally underestimated Boda…at least in that respect….:: Chris: So, that explains why the Mascot form of Chris Chase is continually being spanked by Boda-Boy. Heck, if I knew that Boda felt that way about me…I’d obliterate him with just a little more venom than usual. Back where I come from, Boda is a laughing stock, an imbecile who’s simply blind to the ridicule that’s being placed on him. Even HANK~! could beat Boda for crying out loud! ::. The vendor gives me a strange look, cocking his head to the side in almost exactly the same way that I do. A couple of mascots trundle by, halting the conversation for little more than a second. During this second, I take a moment to re-adjust myself to the surrounds, breathing in the polluted air of the park. Looking around me, Boda’s name entitles nearly everything in this park. From the Boda-Tron Wrestling simulator to the Boda-Rapids White-water ride…to the Boda Brand Coke. Merry jingles ring out over the park, increasingly applying annoyance pressure to myself and the Boda-dog vendor. After the Mascots have passed, he looks at me…before posing a question….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: HANK~!? Isn’t he on the Janitorial staff, and occasionally plays gigs as a drummer for the Boda-Band? Chris: Not that I know of…he’s actually a rather successful jobber in the PCW wrestling federation…at least from where I come from. ::.The vendor shrugs at my limited knowledge of either HANK~! other than the fact that he’s probably the greatest jobber that ever lived….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: Whatever. Anyhow, if you’re thinking about taking Boda on, you better beef up…you must weigh about 225-227 pounds, yes? Right, I weigh about the same…but I don’t really know if that’ll ever be enough to take on Boda himself. Now, Boda’s a behemoth, a gigantic man with a bad-temper…especially if you get on his bad side… ::.Knowing all of this already…I nod with an arrogant smirk on my face…gesticulating with my right hand whilst continuing to lean on the cart….:: Chris: Yeah, Yeah…I know all of that already, and trust me…he honestly isn’t really that hard to take down. Just aim for the knees and you’ll be well on your way to beating that nimrod. And this comes from experience…back, about 10 months ago…I was inflicting some sheer justification on his High and mighty self. He’s full of idle, worthless threats that seem more accustomed to a gangland movie than a professional wrestling promotion. These threats don’t ever amount to anything, and any hyperbole that’s conjured by his cretinous assistants is worth about as much as an 8-Track compiled by the likes of Rich Cannon and Stryfe. He’s nothing, and I can take him down as simple as that… Boda-Dog Vendor: The only problem that’s facing you now, is the park…here, let me show you a schematic…Mister Chase… ::. Never before have I been so pumped for a battle, a fight to the finish, whatever. Keen to strip Boda of his pride and the PCW World Heavyweight Title as easy as it is to take Candy off a baby, it’s no wonder that the confidence pumping through my veins is reaching unprecedented levels. I mean, face it…Boda’s hardly a Meanstreak, an Ivan Stanislav…he’s a pretender, he masquerades as a supposed “Big Bad Boda Daddy” destroying all in his path, when he never truly gets the job done. If he didn’t want me to be this enormous pain in the ass that he keeps talking about, then he should have got shot of me months ago, whereas I prospered in the lower reaches of the PCW talent roster. I won the PCW Cruiserweight Title, and defended it twice successfully…before flopping on the third attempt…that’s two more successful attempts than that he managed to accomplish with his original World Title Run… The Boda-Dog vendor indicates to a large map behind us, revealing the intricate layout of the park, which stretches for tens of miles into the horizon. Pointing to a position at the foot of the map, which I assume to be our current location, he subsequently indicates to an area a long way up near the very pinnacle of the map. Glaring at me, he begins to brief me on the situation as if this were a military operation….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: Twenty-four hours ago, Chris Sloboda…CEO of the Boda Franchise barricaded himself in the Behemoth Complex at the very apex of the park…isolated upon an island in the middle of Lake Sloboda. Chris Chase, your mission is to infiltrate this complex, and disarm Boda by any means necessary… Chris: Anyone going with me? ::. The Boda-Dog Vendor slowly nods, indicating to another spot on the map, not far from our current position. With a grim, yet highly cocky tone, the Vendor continues….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: As usual Chris, this is a two-man infiltration mission. Your partner on this mission will meet you at Waypoint alpha…after you manage to slip your way past the guards that patrol the Big Bo-dipper ride. You’ll be assisted by a mission contact at every point of the mission, however. You will be introduced to this mission contact in due time… Chris: Right, that’s all very well and good…but exactly who are you? Don’t you work for Boda himself? And what’s with the whole thing with the gesticulating just like how I do? ::. The vendor snorts and chuckles arrogantly, looking me dead in the eye. With a smirk, he rolls his eyes at the very sound of me being blunt for a change…stepping away from his cart with a strut….:: Boda-Dog Vendor: The name’s Chris Andrews…the very equivalent to… Both: ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase… ~~~~~~~~~~~Chris…Chris? Chris? CHRIS?~~~~~~~~~~~ ::. Albequrque, New Mexico…it looms in front of me sharply as I’m interrupted in the middle of a very thought-provoking dream, shaken and awoken by a very familiar person. I stir, glancing out onto the brightly lit street with squinting eyes. I look around, feeling the wooden bench underneath my ass as I sit around…rather dazed and confused. Automobiles pass by without so much as stopping, the sky has returned a pale blue…children cease to sing the praises of the most wholesomely sucky cur on the face of this planet. And, right in front of me, shaking my shoulders wildly is ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey…a rather annoyed and frustrated Dustin Downey, wearing a rather obscuring purple hat on his head… I look at my watch without saying anything, before recoiling at the time…it’s three o’clock on Sunday afternoon…little more than five hours prior to the airing of PCW Sunday Sabotage. Once more, I look around…confused and somewhat dejected at the disappearance of a chance to beat the living hell out of an imaginary form of Boda. Ah well, I guess the real thing would beat that hands down. Tapping me on the shoulder, Dustin chortles to himself…barely containing his hysterics as I continue to prove that Dustin’s easily imitated. Rising from the bench, stretching my hands into the air…I take in a deep breath of semi-polluted city air…watching the commuters pass by in their pedestrian pursuits….:: Dustin: What the hell did you think you were doing? Taking a nap just prior to a titanic encounter with Boda-bitchcakes himself, why it’s unheard of…unless of course your name is Dustin Downey, which in any case it’s only fit for ME! ::.Rubbing my forehead, I smack Dustin around the back of the head with my freehand. He reels back, although not very far. Taking my hand off of my forehead…I glare at Dustin with a berating stare…and an arrogant smirk firmly affixed on my face….:: Chris: Dustin, Dustin…it’s not like I’m already pre-destined to beat Boda for the PCW World Heavyweight Title, is it? Come on, you know me better than that. You know that I’d only sleep given times when I need some rest to recupperate and so forth. Thus, after three pre-celebratory shindigs and a couple of autograph signings, don’t you think I at least deserve a little bit of rest before I pummel Boda into oblivion. Now, if you’ll excuse me…my moronic cohort, I’ve got a match to prepare for… ::. And with that, I walk off…I don’t know where, and I don’t know why…but I just walk…walk to search for the end of the dream…and once that’s done, it’s plain sailing from there in…and you can Justify…that..::
