Juan's Back in Portugal

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/02 Time: 6: 38 P.MLocation: Shanghaied, ChinaScene: Prison Part 3 “Take Me Home, Manny.” The scene opens with Juan Borro, Jose, Paulo, Cesar, and Raul on a cargo ship. The men are all waking up around now, and they’re almost in Portugal. Juan shakes himself loose, and checks on the others. He goes over to Raul, and checks out the gun wound, to see if it’s all right. Raul- Don’t Worry, it’s red and hurts, but it’ll get better. For now I’ll be able to move on. A gun shot in the leg is ten times better than spending even another day in that hell whole they call prison. Juan- Good. I’m just happy were out of their, and how we’ll never have to go back. Once were in Portugal, everything will be fine and we can put China and everything going on over their in the back of our heads. Raul- Yeah….I just want to thank you for risking your life back their, to save me. It was a heroic thing to do, and I won’t forget it. I know were all friends here, but I know some of the other guys would never do what you did. Juan- Ahh…Forget about it, you would do the same for me also if I were in the predicament. Juan then goes over to a haystack and lays down over there. He sees a small whole opening in the ship. He moves all of the hay out of the way, and a beam of sunlight enters the cargo area. The men all hold their eyes from the glare and shake their eyes out. Juan pauses after seconds and then peeks through the whole. He sees land up ahead, and lets out a scream of joy. Juan- Yes! We’re almost back. Everyone celebrates, by jumping up and down, while Jose stays back and just stays still. Juan notices, and goes over to talk to him. Juan- What’s wrong Jose? Were finally about to make it. After our huge struggle, were back home. You should be thrilled. Jose- I know. I should be thrilled, but I’m not. You see I’m so accustom to that prison that I forgot what the real world was all about. You know my real world was never so great to begin with. I have no family and girlfriend to come back to. I just have an empty house with bad memories. So it’s really nothing big. Juan- You shouldn’t think of it like that. I mean would you rather still be in the prison. No matter what, you’re free now and no one can take that away from you. Jose- Yea, I guess you’re right. Juan gets back up and goes over to grab the things he brought. He puts his ear up to the side of the ship, as he can hear the other ships motors and engines sounding up ahead. The noise is music to Juan’s ears, and the other men hear it as well. Paolo- Ah……I’d take the clutter and noise of the Portuguese sea ports any day, over the screaming of some stupid Chinese officer. Jose- I’ll agree with you on that one. I guess it is pretty good to be free. I wonder if anyone will notice us. I mean it has been awhile. Raul- I know. To think it was only fourteen months since we left here the first time. It felt as if we spent our hold young adulthood in that place. I don’t even know how to act once I get back. So many things to do, but I don’t know what I should do first. Cesar- I’ll be having the same type of confusion so don’t worry. I just can’t wait until I see my mother again. I’ve missed her so much it’s beyond words. All I’ve wanted to do for more than a year now is just see my mother. Juan- I wish I could too, but I guess not everything is meant to be. The men pause for a few moments, until an odor comes flying in through the small whole. The smell of fish engulfs the ship, and the men just close their eyes and take in the surrounding. The smell of fish is almost like a “Welcome to Portugal” sign for every one of the men. Raul- This has to be one of the best days of my life. Jose- Yea, and we all have Juan to thank for it. If it wasn’t for his planning and determination we would still be rotting in those cells at this very moment. So every day I wake up and see sunlight, I’ll make sure to think of you Juan. Juan- Thanks man, but it took all of us. I could have never physically and mentally done this all without you guys. I can only pray to God in thanks that I met you guys when I did in that prison. Paulo- Hey to all of us, your like our savior. But why get all gushy about things. Were finally back in Portugal, so I say the first thing we do is have a huge celebration. Are you guys ready to have some fun tonight? Juan- Sure. The rest of the men also agree as they put their hands in and celebrate. The unity Juan feels is a unity like none other, a unity that he has never felt in International Incident or backs in the states. This kind of unity was unity by blood. Juan knew he built International Incident as a form of brother hood, but that brotherhood was corrupts. The men sought the same plan, but had different goals and intentions in the way. Juan likes to think he was not one of the men that led to the destruction, but knows his absence played a role. He knew International Incident was used for good, but the men within it were just too different. They were all international, but did not have the same blood. These men had the same blood and that’s why Juan got along with them so well. Men like Akiyama and Pharaoh took their envy and put it first, and that’s why things like this happened. Juan begins to think about Chef and how when he came Pharaoh and Akiyama slowly faded away. It’s almost as if they were jealous and envious that they did not take the role as leader. All it meant to them was something to make them look good. Juan knows he’ll never be one of the men that’ll do anything just to be at the top. He’s rather pleased with where he is now. After the hardship he had just been through, anything is better than prison. But the story goes on and International Incident is not yet dead. They may be weak and falling, but be sure to realize that they are not dead. International Incident will be back and on top one day, but first Juan has to take car of a few bigger issues. Juan knows his career has been sidetracked through all of this commotion, but he hopes that it will all soon pass him by. When he sits down and thinks about it, his life in the wrestling industry is on a huge plus. He’s doing rather well so far in the Global Title Tournament. He made his way into his first Main Event, none the less a Pay-Per View one, and he plans to do very well, if not shock the world and take the belt himself. The scene goes back to the ship, and it comes to a complete stop, the doors open, and the men go charging out on to the dock. After each man is coming out, they just stand at the center of the dock. They stand there and look over at all the people and ships. They look around, and take in the atmosphere, with complete joy. With all of the good feelings and happiness in the air the men are ecstatic. Jose- Yes! It feels so good to be back. Paolo- Yea, oh man I can’t wait until I’m back where I live. Once I’m there, the real fun would begin. All of the men continued babbling on, as Juan just walked down the dock, and looked at his beautiful Portugal. He stayed silent, but his eyes showed all of his feelings. Juan’s face was filled with a huge smile, and the next words out of Juan’s big smiley mouth were: “Finally, I’m back.” With that said, Juan is back in Portugal, and things should finally start making perfect sense just like they used to. Scene Fades to black. TO BE CONTINUED………………….
