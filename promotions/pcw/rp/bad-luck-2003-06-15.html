Bad Luck

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/15/03 Luck It was a four letter word that had seemed to be working against me as of late. It was my luck that my mother’s house had burnt down and she had guilt tripped me into letting her move in with me. It was my luck that since my mother moved in with me the only person with fault around the house was me, at least in her eyes. It was my luck that Allison would turn and kill her only son who happened to be my nephew right in front of the eyes of her daughter and me. Of course I always had the good luck of winning away the television title from Dorn Tracer and being able to call myself the PCW Television Champion and being able to go on a month long vacation aboard the luxurious PCW airship, but that was overlooked once my luck brought me to being booked inside a room in the honeymoon suite with the man who had made my life a living nightmare when he ruined what was supposed to be the happiest day of my life by marrying me, Gaz Green. And then to top that off my first two matches have been nothing but loses to my record thanks to that same man. WHY CAN’T HE EVER JUST GO AWAY? I pondered that question inside my mind as I scanned the room with my eyes and found that there were traces of him left all over the room. There was just no way to escape him. I should have learned that first hand when he ruined the marriage that was supposed to unite Chris Storm and me, but instead turned into an ambush from P.W.A. and resulted in Gaz and I being married. Now instead of a man I loved to death and wanted to spend my life with, I was stuck with a man who took every opportunity he had to toy with my mind and used it for his personal amusement. And now everyday for the next month instead of waking up to the pleasant “good morning Tessa Bear” as I used to, I have to wake up to the grin spread wide across Gaz’s face as he makes the situation worse with the simply greeting “good morning sweet cheeks” knowing very well that it would cause me to cringe and wish that I had not gotten stuck with this man who plays around with the emotions of others simply for his personal amusement. I took a seat on the bed as I tried to relax. I had to try to put Gaz out of my mind even if it seemed that every time I found a reason to be cheered up, Gaz would be there to knock me down with another one of his pranks. But right now I had more to worry about than Gaz; I had a four way match against three of my allies, and one of our common enemies, SOCIETY. Sure it didn’t seem like that big of a deal at first considering that both Lacuna and Miss Moonlight were friends of mine and more than likely we would team up to take out our common enemy, SOCIETY. But who would get the pin when this was all over? Surely all three of us didn’t want to suffer a loss. The last thing I could afford was another loss added to my record right now after losing to Gaz on Tuesday and Dante on Friday. Yet I was sure that both Miss Moonlight and Lacuna wanted this win as much as me. Perhaps it would be the end of a short lived alliance should we all grow selfish and want the win for ourselves. Or perhaps SOCEITY had something up his sleeve. He could easily even up his odds by having both Kirkorian and Stone Burns run in to save him and end the match much like the way the match between both Lacuna and Miss Moonlight had ended on Fusion. Then again maybe I would luck out and the match would be thrown out just as the main event on Fusion had been. “Hmmm, maybe I should just take a nap or something to get this crap off my mind.” I suggested to myself and decided that the would be a good temporary solution to the bad luck that seemed to be encircling me as of late due to a jester that never went away and a group of men that thought that annihilating women would rid the world of it’s problem. I crawled under the white sheets covering the large bed inside the honeymoon suite room and let my head collapse onto the pillow beneath it. Finally I’d be able to get a minute of rest with the two children at the daycare center inside the ship and Gaz floating around somewhere, more than likely pulling a few pranks on the passengers of the airship or getting ready for his match against Dante on Turmoil. “Peace and quiet at last.” I sighed as my eyes began to close and sleep began to sink in. Finally a moment for myself that I could enjoy, well that was until… “Well it seems someone wants some playtime tonight.” Gaz proclaimed as he entered into the room and looked down on the bed I was resting on with a large grin forming across his face, knowing that his comments would only infuriate me. “Damn it, do you ever go away?” I screamed out, climbing out of the bed I had not gotten to enjoy since arriving on this ship considering that every time I made myself at home on it, Gaz would be sure to crawl beside to me just to further agitate me. It was bad enough having to share a room with him, but to have him there beside me on a bed was worse than having an itch you couldn’t scratch. Instead of sticking around to further argue with him though I stormed out of the room in search or something else to do. With an airship this big there surely had to be more to do than just hang around inside a room filled with reminders of the man you hate. I just had to hope that whenever I went I wouldn’t encounter any further bad luck.
