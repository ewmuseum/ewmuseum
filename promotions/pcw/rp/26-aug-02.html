Date: not entered Perhaps this wasn't the right course of action. Perhaps I didn't think this thing through. Perhaps... Perhaps not. Trigger thinks he's a good American. The Ultimate Warrior-schtick. A role-model, maybe. The other guy, I didn't bother to check. A glance at this week's 'card', as the people here call it, revealed I was going to be in a Number One Contender's Match for the 'Extreme' title. I suppose that's good. I don't care about titles. Cheap vanity, your hubris inlaid in gold. What I DO care about is exposure. Granted, I haven't had an idea of how to express to the children of America how to be good Americans - but as long as my name is out there, my face is out there, a grinning rictus of sacrifice and patriotism... That is where my energies should be directed. As for 'Trigger'? I've seen guys like him before. I did a short stint being a Extra PT group leader. Since some guys had gotten fat or got out of AIT not being able to pass the physical standards (42 Push-ups, 52 Sit-ups, and 15:54 in the 2-mile), my commander volunteered me to whip these privates into shape. We would meet outside the company area in our PTs, typically around close of business. There, after work, I would run the shit out of these fat fuckers, assholes who wern't able to do a push-up right. I would run them until they puked, or got in my face, which I relished. Fort Bragg is Airborne Country, where being an NCO still means something, and I rode those fuckers ragged. There was this one guy, a broke-dick guy named Kilanski. Specialist Kilanski. He had been in the unit for about three years, and I had just arrived. He claimed he hurt his back on a jump. Now, I'm aware that back injuries are painful and hard to diagnose unless you got a vertebre popping out of your spine, but still. This guy was one of those guys who 'rode' a profile, trying to get out of going to the field for training, not having to go on long runs, always falling out of formation, claiming his back hurt. The fact that he couldn't physically do the work didn't bother me. I mean, if your back is spasming, then I suppose you can't really sustain a 7-minute mile pace. What did bother me was that this guy Kilanski, was a braggart. He claimed he was going to RIP (a kind of Ranger preparation course - they would starve you, deprive you of sleep, and smoke the shit out of you before you could be accepted into the Ranger course) before he hurt his back, how he was going to be a Ranger. He claimed that he was an MP before he transferred over to 11B (Infantryman), and he had went to Zaire, how he had to shoot a guy. I silently let him spew his bullshit, not really being able to rip into him since I wasn't in his platoon. Some gullible privates ate it up, believing this broke-dick asshole talking shit about this and that. So I went to S-1 (the battalion's records and personnel department) and checked out this guy's 2-1 Personnel File. Turns out, he WAS an MP... Working the gate at Fort Campbell. He reclassed when he hit his third year, trying to get out of Air Assault Land, and come here. He DID go to Jump School in Fort Benning after doing a bullshit AIT for 11B... But a few months after he got here, he feigned an injury. His PT scores, all barely 190. And he malingered here, in my company in the 3rd of the 504th Infantry, talking shit about shooting people and being a would-be Ranger. So when he landed in my Remedial PT program, I was pleased. Now I could stem the tide of bullshit coming from his mouth. Predictably, the first couple of weeks, everyone had the dog-shit smoked out of them. I would make them do arduous sets of push-ups, only to see SPC Kilanski doing arm circles 'Since my profile says I can't strain my back, Sergeant!'. He would walk while the others were having border-line heart attacks running at my 6-minute mile pace. He was a slacker, a malingerer, a shit-bag. But I couldn't do anything to him, since he was protected underneath the protective clause of the profile, which is basically a doctor's orders not do anything that might exacerbate whatever was wrong with him, in case, his back. And he would look at me with that smart-ass smirk on his face. And he would say 'Oh yeah, when I was in (insert bullshit unit here), I was maxing my PT test...' That fucking smirk. Like he thought he was better than me. So I bided my time. While Sharon was at home one night, I followed him in my Chevy S-10. He was going down to the clubs with his buddies. I waited four hours for him to emerge from the club, and miraculously, he was along, stumbling drunkenly towards his Ford Escort. Well, him driving a Ford was reason enough. I crept out of my car and grabbed my tireiron. And I snuck up behind him. Too bad his 'Ranger' reflexes didn't save him. Or maybe it was because of his back, I don't know. I slammed the tireiron in the back of his knee, toppling him. I wore my black PT cap and a balaclava, so he couldn't see my face. Of course, that wasn't a problem, after I crushed his nose, broke his cheekbones, his clavicle, his kneecaps, his wrists. He went unconscious after the cheeks went. The next day, at six o'clock formation, there was shocking news! The company commander had gotten word that Specialist Kilanski had been brutally beaten and put into the hospital! There WAS alcohol in his system, and it is believed that he was jumped for his wallet. He admonished us to stay in 'Buddy-Teams' when we go out, and to look out for each other. Kilanski got chaptered out of the Army, soon after that. I did my job as a Sergeant in my unit. I got a shit-bag like Kilanski out of the Army, and I made him regret ever lying about what he did. Trigger... He's my shit-bag in PCW. He's the shit-bag I'm going to take care of. He brags, just like Kilanski did. He's full of shit, just like Kilanski was. And I'm going to take you out of PCW, just like I took SPC Kilanski out of This Man's Army... Trigger... Trigger... Trigger... Trigger...
