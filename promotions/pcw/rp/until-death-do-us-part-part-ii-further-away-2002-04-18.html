Until Death Do Us Part, Part II: ... Further Away

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-18-2002 *When you take time to imagine how many things there are to see during one’s period of time on earth, it is quite astounding. There are so many things out there that each in their own special way connect mankind together and make up a part of our life. So many things have so many uses. There are two sides to everything little object in life, whether it being an inanimate object or a life-bearing being. Many simple household items can be quite useful and are quite a convenience. However, there are times they fall into the wrong hands and the darker, bleaker purposes for them are unleashed… Take for example, a knife. So many eyes look upon it as a helpful tool around the house for simple purposes such as cutting foods and other materials. There are however some people who see this as an entirely different tool; One for revenge, expression of hatred and cold-blooded murder.* *He sat on the floor staring at his own. There was something strange about the unusually bright gleam from the stainless steel. It drew him in and hypnotized him, paralyzing him with all the possibilities that could come from the correct usage of it. He spoke not a word nor showed the slightest expression on his face. As he rotated it in the light beaming from the window, it was as if the metallic shine took the words out of his mouth and did the talking for him. But what was it trying to say? No one could have possibly figured it out anyway.* *Jeremy still had nothing to say at all. It only seemed that with each passing second he was drawn further into its trance-inducing daze. Within moments he could hear it talking to him via its sharp teeth.* “You and I have deliberated for quite some time now, and we’ve decided that they must go.” *With that said, Stryfe came to again. He knew his knife, or whatever was talking to him was right. Blood was the only cure for his sickness. He stood up to leave to pick up his prescription. He finally spoke.* “It’s time…” *However, just before heading out the door he asked his holy figure for the time being for guidance.* “Please guide my way into Matthew Howe’s heart with your blade, sharp and true.” *It was official: Blood would stain the town tonight and heal the relationship of Stryfe and Sierra, even if someone had to die for her sins.* *Connie had found employment recently as the local Java Drive. Only nowadays can you find convenience in a small, drive-through coffee kiosk. Considering how many psychotic Twin Citians pass through every day, this day went rather smoothly for the trainee. One person after another came in for their morning caffeine fix. Of course, with everything that went on the night before, it was incredibly hard for her to concentrate on her work. She thought it would be best not to think about it though. The bottom line was that she found someone better for her, and that was that. Another car pulled up to find Connie standing still, lost in her train of thought.* “Hey! It would nice to get some service around here!” Shouted the man at the wheel. “Oh, I’m sorry.” Apologized Connie to him as she snapped back into the real world. The man continued, “Yeah. I’d like a double raspberry mocha.” *She went straight back to her work and fulfilled the driver’s request. She soon supplied him with his mocha as she counted up the total cost. During all her crazy adventures with her ex, her nerves had been completely shot. Knowing how the man snapped at her moments ago, she was reluctant to do so much as to even tell him the total.* “Three d-dollar a-a-and ten cents please.” She stuttered. *The male reached into a small cup holder with the green with a large “5” written on it, proclaiming its value to all. He reached up and slammed it on the counter.* He then told her, “Listen bitch, I’m in a rush and I don’t need to wait for your slow ass to count. Just keep the fuckin’ change.” *He took his coffee and peeled out of sight. At least she wouldn’t have to put up with someone like him again. At least that was what she hoped. Too unfortunate for her though that she these dreams, as small and possible as they may seem rarely ever come true. She went through another six hours of this however, which to her had completely stood still. It seemed to her that after a wild day of work, the hoopla would finally end. She was wrong again… At about seven she arrived home to hide away from the immense strains and pressures the world put her, like so many other under. She refused to be buried alive from it though. She was a tough one all right. She would struggle with all her might to stay above ground. She was finally home thanks to the local taxi service doing their own job which required contact with many crazy people of their own.* The driver announced to young Connie, “That’ll be $5.92.” She gave him a five and a one and told him, “Here’s six.” *With a ‘click’ the driver now held a nickel and three pennies in his hand, which he passed over to Wizzman.* “Thanks. Have a nice day, Ralph.” She told him. *She stepped out of the car onto the front steps to her lovely house. With the turn of her key, she was back in the hands of her creature comforts. She heard her television set again like many times when she walked in the door. From which, she heard those atrocious words.* ……… “Let’s get a party goin’. LET’S GET A PARTY GOIN’! When it’s time to party, we will party hard. PARTY HARD!” *There Matthew was, getting his mind fucked away by the horror known as MTV playing that damn Andrew W.K. video. Connie knew is was best to put a stop to this.* She yelled at him, saying, “Turn that shit off!” *He followed her orders and did as he was instructed. He sat up from the couch and walked over to his object and began feeling her up and down. Nothing different from any other day after work apparently.* “Hey baby.” He said softly to her. He then asked her just like every other time, “How was work today?” *Perhaps this wouldn’t be a day like any other. She responded by swatting his hands away from her. She knew that allowing his hands to rub all over would be yet another grave sin that couldn’t be forgiven.* “Please… don’t.” She then told him. “Why not?” He asked back, with a surprised tone in his voice. Connie simply replied, “I just wanna be left alone.” “Oh come on. You know you want it.” “LET GO OF ME!” *Across town, the sleeping Dark Age member woke up with a pounding headache. He was obviously very tired after just being interrupted from his precious slumber, but he knew there were many things that happen to be much more important… …That included the well being of a loved one. It was Sierra and he knew it. When two people have been bonding together for so long, they tended to comprehend each other’s feelings, even from ten miles away. He knew his head was the effect of Connie pressing her mental panic button and it was time for him to respond to it. The period of time it took him to wake up and leap out of bed seemed nonexistent. Time was not something he had on his hands when it was plotting against him. Before he walked the door, he thankfully remembered the one item sacred to him on his journey to the unholy land.* *It was a few months into his relationship and Howe’s true colors and began to seep out through his actions. Connie was backed into the corner of the shower, stripped completely naked for Matt’s objectification. What could the girl do? Cry and cry hard. She felt completely disgusted and useless by making the mistake of letting a convicted rapist into her heart. Perhaps the lessons her old boyfriend had taught her were not so ridiculous anymore.* “Quit yer bitchin’.” He snapped at the victim as his closed fist slammed into her jaw. *And how could she? She had been betrayed by what she thought was her new true love. No, he wasn’t a lover: He was a monster and she was the experimental rat to be eaten alive by it.* He shouted straight into her ear again. This time telling her, “Shut your goddamn mouth before I slit your throat!” *The race was on. He ran through the streets like a hurricane with the silver blade glistening in the rainfall. Not many people would be idiotic enough to actually hold it out in the open for all to see, but considering how fast Jeremy could run, not many would notice it anyway. But if anyone did in fact take note, he didn’t even care. His living, breathing drug was about to be shot into the hot veins of death and that was the only thing he cared about. In what seemed like an extended several seconds passing by in which he had no idea if time sped up or slowed down, he arrived at the slaughterhouse.* “Argh… stupid… door.” *It was locked, keeping Stryfe away from the horrors that lied inside to confront him. With every passing moment Connie was in more and more danger. He backed up to the bottom of the stoop, and with every ounce of strength managed to ram the door down. It was dark inside, and he knew Matthew was hiding somewhere in the shadows.* He shouted out, “Where are you, and what have you done what Sierra?!” Another strong yet strangely calm voiced echoed through the halls and asked him, “Sierra? Whatever do you mean?” “You know damn well what I mean.” After a few seconds of silence the voice boomed again. “I’m afraid you’ll have to show me.” It said. *Connors still couldn’t see thing in the extreme darkness though…. until Matt had popped out in front of him from out of nowhere. The two began brawling in the darkness. Punches, kicks and obscenities were rapidly traded between the two. On and on an on they fought and it seemed it would never end. Crashing through tables, off balconies and staircases destroying everything in sight. Finally Stryfe had pinned Matthew with his throat in one hand and a knife in the other.* “Please, you don’t know what you’re doing!” Howe shrieked out. Jeremy simply replied, “Oh, I believe in do.” “I beg of you, let me go!” He cried again with his usually powerful bass-filled English accent now serving as an extra yelp of desperation. “Shut up you pussy Englishman.” Stryfe shouted into his ear. “You’re no different than that Palithorpe prick.” *He slowly drew his blade closer to the demon’s flesh. Just before letting his blood loose, Stryfe shouted.* “In the name of the goblin called Syko, let this be an omen of things to come!” ……… *Within seconds, it was all over. The beast had been slain by unlikely hero. But it wasn’t over yet. He had to find his love. Was she all right? Would she take him back? The questions would finally be answered. He walked upstairs to the sound into the bathroom and turned on the light…* Connie Wizzman1983-2002 *He was too late that day. He found Connie dead in the shower. Although his only love was lost forever, he was at least at peace with a couple of things. First of all, she apparently went peacefully. He oddly did not notice any large wound that would have proven fatal on her. And second, on behalf of her Jeremy had extracted his revenge on her killer. But it was still very sad that so many would mourn him since he had so many people were close to him. Connie had no one in her life. She died alone. Maybe just of a broken heart. Apparently some questions would never be answered. He sniffed the rose in his hand. When he grieved over his loss, the beautiful scent was a great escape from the grim reality of what has just taken place. At least one person in this world cared for her and to at least one person she would never forget her. He placed the rose on her gravesite in remembrance.* “Goodbye my love…”
