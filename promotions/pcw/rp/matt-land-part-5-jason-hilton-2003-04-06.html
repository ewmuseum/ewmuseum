Matt Land, Part 5- Jason Hilton

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/03 “Is she going to be all right?” Jason Hilton asks, his voice filled with pain and remorse. “I never wanted her to get into this business.” Standing across the room in scrubs is a normal sized doctor, his jet-black hair pushed back and his long face scrunched up, as he begins to write Haley’s recent vitals on a chart. He looks up at her middle-aged father and gives him a smile, trying to let him know that everything is going to be fine and that he has nothing to worry about. The room is silent for the most part, except for the constant beeping of the heart monitor that is connected to the princess. “She is going to be fine, it is just with the beating she took on Friday and the action of earlier today, it seemed to take her down. I would say she just needs a little time to rest and to let her concussion heal,” the doctor says, with a reassuring tone. He makes his way over to the bed where Haley is laying and starts to check her over on more time. Her face is swollen and her blonde hair is covered in blood. She is a total mess and as the time passes it seems like things aren’t getting better. The beating she took from Lacuna’s shotgun left her with a mild-concussion and a lot of facial problems, but she ignored them and stood by Matt’s side in the Continental Title Tournament, not wanting to let him down. It only has made matter worse though because now she is going to be in the hospital for a few days, leaving him on the mission to destroy PCW alone. “Do you know how long it is going to be until she wakes up?” Jason questions, as he runs his hands through his grey hair. He is in his late sixties and all the hard hours he spent on his law practice is starting to show in the form of wrinkles on his face, but the master of anti-trust laws keeps his appearance nicely, always working out to make sure he keeps his slender figure. “It could be at anytime,” the young doctor, tells him, giving Jason a symptomatic look. “The best thing to do right now is let her rest and get her bearings back. In a few days I think she will be as good as new, except for a few bruises and bumps.” Reaching up his hands, Jason brushes the tears away from his pale blue eyes. He can’t stand to sit here and stare at his daughter, he is the protector of the family and at this point, he feels like he has failed. He left his little girl in the hands of a man he hardly knew and this is how it turned out. Even with Haley being an adult, he still believes it to be his duty to protect her and now he is going to assume that duty again. Nothing as bad as this will ever happen again, if Jason can help it. “I won’t let anything hurt my baby,” he whispers, taking her tiny hand in his and gripping it tightly. He hopes that she hears him and waits for some sort of response. Nothing comes though, not even a squeezing of his hand back. The tears once again surge forth, even with the aging professors tries to stop them. He feels like his princess looks and his feeling of responsibility is making it worse. Peering over the room, trying to get his mind away from how is daughter looks, he is pleased with it for the most part and is glad that they have more then enough money to cover the expenses. The private room isn’t very big, but it is large enough to house an oak stand next to the bed and two chairs for visitors. A television quietly sits on a stand coming out from the wall straight in front of the bed. Along the white plastered wall on the right is a window; it is shielded from the sun, by a pair of dark blue curtains. They don’t obstruct the view completely, as Jason is still able to see the rain falling outside. The pitter-patter noise it makes, softly touching the glass and then running straight down it, just like the tears that are currently streaming down his face. Each crying for their fallen princess and there is no signs of stopping. Jason was all alone in the room though and he didn’t care about looking strong now. No parent would in this situation and he wants to do more. The only thing he can do for her is hold her hand and hope for the best, but that isn’t enough for him. He wants to make everything disappear and let them go back to life before the PWA and before she met the animal that is Matt Jackson. “Honey, don’t worry. You will never have to see that bastard again, once you wake up. We will go home and I will take care of you,” he chokes out, pushing the tears away once more. Allowing his grey suit to wrinkle up. *** “Those bitches will not get away with this,” Matt bellows to himself, letting the anger in his voice take over his emotions. “She is going to pay and I am going to be more then happy to be the one to give it to her. Lacuna wants to bring guns into this? Fine, because when I play with guns someone usually ends up dead!” There is a truth in the ghost’s last statement, as he makes his way towards and revolving door of the hospital. The warm LA sun beats down on his angular face on this gloomy Sunday afternoon and his stone face shows that today isn’t a happy day. His cocky smile is replaced by a look of anger and hatred and his swagger isn’t present, only a walk of determination. The beating he took on Fusion is the last thing on his mind, but it is on the forefront of his body. The stitches in his head tell the whole story and the bruises under his brown eyes emprises the point. After everything that has happened over the past few days, he is still feeling rather well though. Minus Haley in the hospital, everything is going according to plan. It isn’t about helping Xavier Kannon get free anymore, but it is about shutting down the hell hole that is PCW because he hates it. Matt hates PCW now and he doesn’t care who tries to stop him, he is going to shut it down. Sarah Clarkson is also in the back of his head and right now that is where she is staying. He has been unable to break her up to this point, but soon he would get the DCI the information he craves. It was only a matter of time before the British snob breaks and gives up her father. It seems when she is gone and will give him what he needs; she comes up with another ploy and finds a way to hold out for just a while longer, causing him an unwanted hassle. Doesn’t matter because when she does break, that also equals the end to her impending death and she will be no more. For the first time all day a smile curls across his stone face. The thought of killing Sarah Clarkson actually makes him happy and he knows it shouldn’t, but after killing so many people, he just doesn’t care anymore. Their names and faces are nothing, but a blur in his memory. Nothing more than people who stood in his way, when he was trying to obtain a goal and now the rich posh is going to be added to the list. He doesn’t care about her or her family, just the destruction of them and doing his job. Stepping into the revolving door, he waits for it to slowly come to the opening of the hospital. Much like his life the door spins around and around, letting different people in and out, not allowing anyone to stay for any duration of time. It doesn’t feel the pain of loss or the joys of love, it is emotionless and only moving when it is pushed and has nowhere else to go. Stopping when it no longer has a use to anyone, it might as well be named Matt Jackson. Walking into the hospital, he takes a deep breath and makes his way across the glimmering tile floor to the front desk. He sets his elbows down on the wooden top and lets his head come to rest in his hands. His black suit gently moves, as the breeze from the fan to the left of him hits it. This also ruffles his brown hair a little and brings him some relief from the heat outside. His eyes softly scan the waiting area where people are quietly sitting; waiting to get news on there loved ones who have been rushed to the hospital. “May I help you sir?” A nurse asks, her voice full of sweetness. Matt quickly turns his head to her, looking at the young raven-haired beauty in front of him. Her white plain white uniform brings out her blue eyes perfectly and her smile reveals a set of white teeth, not a one out of place or order. “Uhhh… Yes, I am looking for Haley Hilton, I know she is a patient here,” he replies, giving a small smile of his own, trying to hide the fact that his normally perfect white teeth are caked in blood, from the recent events of today. “Well lets see,” she says, before flipping through a book that lists all the patients. Licking her luscious red lips, while Matt watches her every move, enchanted and locked on to her almost as if she is the piped piper and is playing is tune. Landing on a page, she looks up and gives him another smile, “Yes, Ms. Hilton is in Room 201 on the forth floor. Just take those elevators over to the right and then once you get upstairs, there will be a lady at the desk who can explain to you, how to get to room 201.” “Thank you,” he says coldly, making sure he walks away as fast as he can, so not to make a fool of himself in front of the beauty. That is the one thing the CIA never taught him, how to impress women and he wishes that it was something they offered. “I will have to talk to Peter about that,” he says to himself, laughing while he steps onto the elevator. *** “Can you hear me Haley?” Jason Hilton quietly questions into his daughters ear. They have been here since she passed out at the Continental Title Tournament and she isn’t showing signs that she is going to wake up anytime soon. Lacuna laid her out at Fusion and she might not fully recover from the beating that she took at the hands of the rich posh. Jason Hilton allows the silence to once again fill the room, as he remains seated next to his princess holding her hand. The river of tears continues to pour down the lawyer’s cheeks. His little girl is hardly recognizable through all of the swelling and dried blood that is on her face, acting as make-up to hide the cuts she received. He is almost startled out of the green padded chair by a knock on the heavy wood door of Room 201. Letting go of her hand, he stands up and wipes the tears away from his eyes with a swoosh from his massive hand and straightens out his grey suit on the way to the door. His black dress shoes click off the tile floor with each step he takes and he comes to a stop, jerking open the door. Jason’s eyes grow to the size of boulders, staring at the man on the other side of the door. “What are you doing here!? This is all your fault you son of a bitch,” he barks out at Matt Jackson, who is standing in front of him, his arms crossed over his barrel of a chest. He peers at her father, noticing the older man’s eyes are now full of fire. Matt knew part of this was his fault, he should have been watching over Haley at all times, but he didn’t and it cost her. “Get out!” Jason screams, pushing the agent back, but hardly moving him. Jason is a tiny man in comparison to Matt and there is no way he is going to push him around. “Look, I wasn’t meant to turn out like this, but she is a big girl and she knew what she was getting herself into,” Matt mutters, knowing that this is going to be pointless and there is no way the professor is going to step down from his stance. “I trusted you to protect her and look out for her and you didn’t! You let her get her face smashed in with a shotgun and then you let he get thrown onto her head by some super human man. Just do us a favor and stay away from her forever, because she is never going to be anywhere near you. That means outside of wrestling and wrestling itself.” “W-What?” Matt dumbfoundedly says, “You can’t take that way, it is her choice you bastard and so help me god, if you hurt her like that I am going to make sure, you end up in a hole in the ground.” “You think you can threaten me because you kick peoples asses for a living? Your hollow promises don’t scare me Matt. I could have you tossed in jail so fast,” he yells back. Jason didn’t know about Matt working for the CIA, he doesn’t know of the famed killer. “You would think that, being a big professor of law, but by the time you got a warrant. It would be too late. You don’t fully understand who you are fucking with Jason and if you weren’t her father I would have already done it.” “Don’t try and threaten me Matt, because my choice is final. Now get out of here before I call security!” His voice is filled with wrought and contempt. Matt stands unmoving in front of him, trying with every muscle in his body not to strike the man in front of him. “Security,” Jason’s voice booms out, followed by two guards walking up behind Matt. Each one in generic brown uniforms, they are both as big as Matt and grabbing his arms presents no problem for them, as they begin to drag him out. “Wait,” Jason tells them, the guards stop for a minute, still holding Matt in place. They are unsure of exactly what Mr. Hilton wants, but they are going to follow his orders. “Bring him in front of him, but make sure he can’t touch me.” The guards pull Matt in front of him and still hold his hands behind is back, as Jason reaches up his right hand and slaps Matt across the face with a smile. It brings him great joy to think that he just got some revenge for all the pain that Matt caused Haley over the past few weeks. “I never want to lay eyes on you again.” Matt tries to pull away from the guards, but is unable to. The only recourse he gets is a stray kick into the stomach of Jason, who doubles over for a second, before regaining himself and backhanding Matt one more time. He can’t believe what he just did, but it felt good to get a little pay back to the ghost and now all he has to do is remove him once and for all from Haley’s life. “Matt…Matt….” Haley moans, it is barley even a whisper. No one hears her though, as her father is busy making sure they get Matt out of the hospital and Matt is busy trying to fight free to kill him. She goes ignored again and now the two stubborn men are going to go back and forth until one gets what they want. Leave me battered and broken, Beat me down until there is nothing, That is when I am my best, That is when I am Matt Jackson
