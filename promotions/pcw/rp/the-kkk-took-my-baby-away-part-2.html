The KKK Took My Baby Away (Part 2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Black screen... Snow: “Damn it all to hell! Millions upon millions of dollars and I can’t find but ONE lowly QUARTER!?!?” The scene opens with the panicking PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World frantically searching the pockets of his jet black leather pants. He’s standing inside a cramped phone booth - his teeth clenched together and his long blond hair strewn wildly around his head. In a fit, he stomps down his Snake Skin boots and pulls hundreds of dollars from his leather pants and tosses it aside, still searching for a quarter... Snow: “Damn it! Why in the hinges of hell can payphones not give back change!? Or better yet, why are they not free of charge for Superior Individuals!” Snow finds that notion interesting. Still in his panic, he picks up the receiver and harshly presses down the “zero” button. After a few rings, our super-sensitive PCW microphones catch a female operator answering on the other end... Operator: “AT&T, how may I help you?” Snow: “I demand free service to this damned contraption!” There’s a long moment of silence... Operator: “May I ask why, sir?” Snow: “Because I’m a Superior individual! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” The operator nervously clears her throat before speaking to irrational young man... Operator: “May I ask, sir, who you’re trying to make a call to?” Maybe she sensed the urgency in his voice and suspected that he was acting in unusual circumstances. Maybe she somehow sensed that the PCW Champion was facing a major crisis - his daughter has just been kidnapped... Snow: “I’m trying to call the POLICE, damn it! And no, not your every day run of the mill doughnut bellied fatman in blue - I need the SUPERIOR police! I need the National Guard, damn it!” The operator pauses for a moment... Operator: “Ok, sir. I’m going to get you in touch with the local police.” Snow: “But damn it, I said the SUPERIOR police! Have you no ears to hear the Greatness that is spewing forth from my mouth!? Hello!? Are you there, wench!?” But she’s not there. The phone has already begun ringing as Snow has been put through to the local police. And indeed, the situation is an emergency. Snow’s daughter has been kidnapped by a pair of KKK members. Although the pair have displayed some obvious lapses in intellectualism, they must be considered dangerous... A receptionist answers... Receptionist: “911 - do you have an emergency?” Snow: “Do I have an emergency, you ask... By Greatness, I’ll have you know that MY emergency is unlike any that you’ve ever seen or heard of in the entirety of the pathetic existence that you call a life!” Receptionist: “Please state the emergency.” Snow: “My daughter has been KIDNAPPED!” Receptionist: “Kidnapped? Are you sure?” Snow: “Do you think you’re dealing with just another inferior peasant!? I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! Of COURSE I’m sure! I demand you send every man in this damn country to me and I’ll hunt the evil-doers down like the bastards they are!” With that said, we can’t help but notice the intensely serious look that burns in the Killin’ Villain’s blazing blue eyes. The scene fades to black... Later... The scene reopens near the park bench where little Haley Snow, daughter of the Killin’ Villain, was last scene. Snow stands atop the bench with a fiery expression on his face while two police officers take notes on the lawn. The first police officer is an older man with a thick red mustache and drooping eyes. He looks up and Snow and scratches his chin with the eraser of his pencil... Officer 1: “So... Mr. Snow... you’re saying that the man had a... ... ... six foot tall... baby?” Snow rolls his eyes... Snow: “Well I don’t know the exact height of the grotesque thing! Hell, I could barely take my eyes from it’s scraggly beard!” The officer’s exchange glances... Officer 1: “The... uh... the baby had a ... beard? Sir?” Snow stomps one Snake Skin boot down and glares at the police officers who are not sure whether or not Snow is telling the truth or if he is simply psychotic. In truth, those of us who have been watching Snow for some time know that it is a little bit of both... Snow: “Damn you! Are you going to stand around all day and ask questions!? Or are you going to find my Superior Spawn!?” Officer 1: “Sir, we can’t find your Sup... er... your baby until we get all the information. Now, listen. I’ll have my men keep an eye out for an old man with a ... six foot tall baby...” The old red-mustached officer jabs the second officer with an elbow after the younger man let out a snicker - the second officer believes the Killin’ Villain is just another psycho... Officer: “Ok? Now, I’ll have Sandy give your wife - the child’s mother - a call to inform her of the situation.” Suddenly, Snow’s eyes grow wide and he leaps off the bench to confront the pair of surprised officers... Snow: “NO! You CAN’T call the child’s mother! By Greatness, if you do that, I’ll wake up tomorrow morning missing one of my more Superior limbs!” The older officer shrugs, now leaning more toward the psychotic theory, and holds out his palms to the young PCW star... Officer 1: “Ok, sir. Have it your way. But look, we have to get going. We’ve got an important bust to make out at the old dock. We’ll keep you informed if anything develops.” Snow: “WAIT!” The Killin’ Villain steps around to block their paths, his eyes flaming with intense resilience. The officers exchange nervous glances now - at six feet, one inches tall, the young PCW Champion is larger than either of them, and is obviously in peak physical condition. They quickly realize that even combined, they are no match for the panicking young man... Snow: “What in the hell could POSSIBLY be more important than THIS!? We’re talking about MY SUPERIOR SPAWN for the Greatness sake!” The senior officer extends his hands in a calming motion... Officer 1: “Settle down, son, settle down. We got a tip that there’s a KKK meeting down at the dock tonight. Damn near everyone on the force is going to be there to get as many of those bastards as we can.” For a moment, Snow rages... Snow: “How can you even COMPARE the seriousness of the two situations! I believe that I mentioned that we’re talking about SUPERIOR SPAWN here! Forget about your little adventures with the K...” Snow hesitates. He stares straight ahead as something in his brain clicks - probably a memory of his previous experience with Earl, the senior kidnapper... Snow: “...K...” His eyes grow wider as the situation becomes clearer... Snow: “...K...” Suddenly, it all makes perfect sense. Snow grabs the pair of officers by the shoulders, and because he can find no better words, he exclaims... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!” Still holding the pair of startled police officers by their shoulders, the Killin’ Villain shakes them violently and screams... Snow: “Damn it all to hell! Why didn’t you tell me!? My spawn has been stolen by the damn KKK! Oh! The inferiority of it all!” The older, red haired officer breaks away from Snow’s grip, holding his arms out to slow things down. Slowing things down, however, is not an easy task - Snow is already jogging toward his car. Quickly, the police officers jog after him... Officer 1: “Wait a minute! What proof do you have?” Snow, without stopping, responds... Snow: “I don’t need proof! I’m a professional wrestler!” Odd response, but the officers accept it. When they get to the parking lot, Snow is about to leap into his trusty Viper when the senior officer gestures to his own, blue and white police car... Officer 1: “Ride with us. It’s the only way you’re going to get to the scene.” Reluctantly, Snow accepts the officer’s offer. He, along with the trusty PCW Camera man hop into the backseat of his car, and they speed away toward the kidnapper’s and Snow’s child, little Haley... The scene fades to black... Meanwhile... (the following footage was confiscated by the RCMP of Ontario, New Brunswick...) The scene opens at what we have no choice but to believe are the “docks” that the police officers have been talking about. The film we watch was recorded by an cam-quarter, is low in quality, and taken by what we can only assume to be an unprofessional camera man. Our view constantly shakes, but we can see both Earl and Billy Bob smiling for the camera... Earl: “Howdy!” His greeting is laced in his usual thick southern accent. For those who have yet to meet Earl and Billy Bob, please refer to the promos “Snow joins the KKK!?!?” and “The KKK Took my Baby Away.” Earl: “I’m Earl, and this here’s Billy Bob.” Billy Bob spits... Earl: “And we done got us some revenge today! Yessir!” Both men are dressed in their Klan uniforms, minus the headgear. Earl’s white hair has been messed from its time outside. Billy Bob’s long mullet is tied into a pony tail now, but his beard is as straggly as ever. The tubby sidekick spits, as usual... Earl: “Y’ever here o’ that feller? Jason Snow?” We hear the camera man speak... KKK Camera Man: “Yessir.” Earl smiles and picks a baby carriage up into view. The carriage, indeed, is the resting place of little Haley Snow, who thankfully is sleeping peacefully through her abduction... Earl: “Well I got me somethin’ that belongs to him!” Earl sways the carriage back and forth... KKK Camera Man: “I saw how he didn’t join the West Virginia branch and all - but what do you plan to do with his baby?” Earl, who we have come to learn isn’t the brightest fellow in the world, turns to Billy Bob. Billy Bob’s eyes, as usual, hold no additional wisdom. The lackey spits... Earl: “Well what’s it matter what I do with ‘er? The point is, I got ‘er! And he ain’t gittin’ her back! No way, no how! Next time, he’ll know better than to f(CENSORED)k with the Klan!” Stupid as he may be, it is clear that little innocent Haley Snow is not safe in the hands of this madman. Suddenly, the camera man shifts our view to a burning cross in the distance. As we watch the cross burn in front of the horizon, where the sun set not an hour ago, the scene fades to black... Meanwhile As the scene fades in, we find ourselves in the back of a speeding police car - sirens blaring. Our view is once again controlled by the trusty hands of our PCW Camera man, and the Killin’ Villain stares nervously out the window... no matter how he portrays himself, he does understand the seriousness of the situation. There are other police cars both in front of us and behind us, their red lights ablaze in the ever-darkening sky... Snow: “How much longer!?” Officer 1: “We’re almost there, kid. Don’t worry.” Snow: “Don’t WORRY!? Sure, maybe YOU wouldn’t worry if it were YOUR little peasant out there in the hands of those... those... BEASTS! But I just so happen to be the father of Superior Spawn, damn it! And I’ll not have a hair on her curly little head harmed, or by Greatness, there shall be hell to pay!” The officers say nothing. Although they think Snow is going about the situation in an odd way, they do feel for him - they probably have children of their own. To help ease the tension, the second officer turns on the radio and an old song plays loudly... Radio: “...Away for a holiday,Said she’s goin’, to LA.But she never got there, she never got there,She never got there they say-ay...” Oddly enough, the music does calm Snow to an extent. He leans back in the seat and tries to catch his breath, closing his eyes and trying to relax himself... Radio: “She went away for a holiday-ay-ay,Said she’s goin’ to LA.But she never got there, she never got there,She never got there they say-ay...” Though he looks more relaxed than before, Snow’s fists are still clenched tightly as he holds his eyes and mouth closed. With any luck, they’ll be at the docks soon... Radio: “The K-K-K took my baby away-ay,They took her away,Away from me-e.The K-K-K took my baby away-ay,They took her away,They took my baby a-way...” Snow: “DAMN IT ALL TO HELL!” The angered PCW Champion turns around and slams his fist into the back speaker of the police car, causing the speaker to attain an annoying, vibrating sound. The senior officer glances over at his partner, and the younger man quickly turns the music off. The damage has already been done, however. Snow looks as if he’s ready to explode. His cold blue eyes are wide and raging, his long blond hair is strewn wildly around his head, and his chest heaves with every giant, angry breath that he takes into his burning lungs... Officer 1: “We’re almost there, kid. Almost there.” Snow: “Drive faster, damn it!” Officer 1: “I’m driving as fast as I can! Don’t worry, we’ll get your girl back.” Snow: “Girl? GIRL!? You’ll treat her with more respect than that you lowly SIMPLETON! That child was born into Greatness, and for that fact ALONE she’s ten times better than you’ll EVER be! Her father just so happens to be the PCW Champion of the WORLD!” There’s a small pause while the officers take in this information. They glance at each other, and then back at the tension and stress-filled young man in the backseat... Officer 1: “You’re kidding!” Snow: “Why would I kid about it!? Have you ever seen ANOTHER Killin’ Villain!? Damn it! I demand you drive faster!” The senior officer looks to his partner and nods... Officer 1: “I’ll see what I can do.” We speed up a tad and our car passes the speeding police car ahead of us, hurrying along to the destination. Suddenly, and dangerously, the senior officer who’s driving the car pulls sharply off to a back road and continues flying along and breakneck speed... Officer 1: “It’s just up ahead.” Indeed, we can see the glow of burning crosses in the distance. Before long, the siren blaring vehicle pulls up on a surprised group of white-cloaked KKK members. There are nearly a hundred in total. As the officers leap out of the car and into action, the KKK members quickly cloak their faces with the pointed white headgear, making it impossible to determine which man is Earl, and which is Billy Bob. The second officer hesitates and then lets Snow out of the vehicle... Snow: “Where is he, damn it!?” A moment later, we hear the sirens of the other police cars as they approach. For the time being, however, the Klan members think they have a fighting chance. Several of them step in the direction of the two police officers and the PCW Champion. Snow wastes no time jumping into action. His first choice of violence is a devastating superkick, Snakeyes, into the jaw of one of the KKK members. The white-cloaked racist topples onto his back and lies motionless. The damage done in that one quick movement sends a message to the rest of the approaching attackers. They stop, dead in their tracks, realizing that their entire masses couldn’t stand against the Killin’ Villain in his current state of patriarchal fury... Snow: “DAMN YOU ALL TO HELL! I demand to know the whereabouts of the my Superior Spawn!” The only sound is that of the approaching sirens. There is a grim silence in the mass of racists... a silence that is matched by the Killin’ Villain - his silence is deadly. He overlooks the white-cloaked rednecks in front of him, searching them each for identifying traits... He says quietly, yet most of the terrified men in front of him hear perfectly clear... Snow: “I’ll rip through the whole inferior lot of you if need be...” The mass of racists exchange nervous glances. They know that the Killin’ Villain could do exactly that - rip through the lot of them. And judging by the actions of the police officers, who have done nothing as of yet, he’ll be allowed to as well. Soon, the KKK members realize that they don’t have much choice but to fight. A few of the larger men approach Snow, only to have a pair of their brave number dropped by a superkick and a strong right hand. When they drop, motionless, the other men lose their courage and back off. A devilish, hate-filled smile spreads Snow’s lips and the Champion drops his jet black leather jacket behind him... Snow: “I could do this all night, you know.” They know that if enough of them attacked, they would be able to make the young angry man a non-factor. But in order to do that, they also know that the first few who try will be left with broken bones in their faces, like the previous examples. They stand in the loose form of a line. A few of the police cars are in view now, as Snow stalks up and down, eyeing the racists over... Snow: “I know she’s here...” And then it happens. A sound draws Snow’s attention. As he snaps his head toward the source of the sound, his eyes catch a glob of spit flying from the breathing hole of a particularly fat racist’s mask. Snow’s eyes grow wide with rage and he exclaims... Snow: “YOU!” Obviously, the man who spit was none other than Billy Bob. The man beside him, who’s voice is recognizably Earl’s, scolds him... Earl: “Damn it Billy Bob! Why the hell couldn’t you just keep yer damn saliva to yerself!?” Then Snow spots it. At their feet, behind them, is the carriage where little Haley Snow has been resting all this time. Snow stalks toward them, his eyes full of rage... Snow: “Can’t you peasants READ!?!? NO SWEARING AROUND THE BABY!” With that, Snow delivers a powerful superkick to Earl, who’s elderly body crumbles in response to the blow. Snow then turns his attention to Billy Bob... ... ... Billy Bob spits. That action is the last thing Billy Bob will remember doing that night, because his consciousness is quickly deprived of him as the Killin’ Villain delivers a Snake Skin boot to his masked face. The white mask soaks with crimson before the large man falls to his side and remains motionless... Snow: “Superior Spawn!” Snow rushes up to his young daughter and looks inside the carriage - Haley is just now waking from her slumber, right on time to look into her caring - caring, in his own special way - father. On the side of the carriage, the sign that was left by Snow’s wife is still in tact: “NO SWEARING AROUND THE BABY!” Snow smiles warmly. She does look quite like her father... same blue eyes, blond hair, and determined look in her little jaw... Snow: “The peasants didn’t hurt you, did they?” Haley giggles... In the background, we hear that the other police cars have arrived on the scene and are now making arrests. The scene gets slightly darker, yet more comfortable, as the flaming crosses are extinguished. As Snow removes his young daughter from her carriage, the scene fades to black...
