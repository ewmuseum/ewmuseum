Betrayal

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-05-02 It had taken some time, yes, but the WITs were finally on Minion's side. He had lured them with kindness and treats, had then absorbed them with his dastardly plans, and at last stretched out the hand of friendship, the hand that would later become forbidden fruit of the garden. This was because, as we all know, with glory comes greed, and with greed comes violence. Right at the start, right when the WITs had crossed over the line to the side of The Insane One, he had demanded of them to make a pact. Nervous were they at going behind Trigger's back and committing their lives to Lance Minion, but as The Insane One had told them, it wasn't hurting anyone. ''Becoming a warrior is only the first step in the process,'' he had said the day before. ''Your trainer, Trigger, thinks it is the only step. He believes that being a warrior itself is the destined fulfillment of life, and that once achieved warriorism will make all peaceful and well. But you see, my WITs, that is not always true. I dare not tell the Infinite Warrior, but his theorizing is not accurate. Because we all know that Trigger is a warrior… one of the greatest perhaps… but is he happy? Are there not problems corrupting his life? Trigger thinks that being a warrior is all about giving. He is missing the fun.'' Minion had then explained to them the ancient traditions that The Insane One himself had followed, using powers of the warrior to manipulate and maneuver the brains of one's foes. The more Minion spoke, the more interested the WITs had become. They realized that combining the powers of a warrior with those of The Insane One's ingenious mind games, they could rule over any and all that they wished to. They would dominate and conquer and lead unprecedented lives such that they could give a new meaning to the term 'warrior.' With the benefits out-weighing the opposition, the Warriors In Training quickly agreed. The one thing they could not do, however, was tell the Infinite Warrior Trigger. Minion said that although Trigger was a wise man, he would not thoroughly understand the meaning behind this new approach on training. He would be forced into a mood of anger… one that could not be stopped. This new concept was worked well with the WITs, seeing as Trigger was home less and less, and Minion was always there to lend a helping hand. The WITs were happy. They thought The Insane One's intentions were pure, and that he was only furthering their training. They all loved it. All except for WIT 3. It is true that Trigger was hardest on the third WIT… that he was on the receiving end of punishment much worse than any of the others. It was true that at times WIT 2 had disliked the Infinite Warrior… even despised him. But in his heart, WIT 3 knew that he could never become a traitor towards Trigger… he could never become a traitor towards anyone. And with this, WIT 3 vowed to appear normal, continuing to be a part of Minion's extracurricular training, but never turn his back on Trigger, the man that got him this far. What WIT 3 didn't know, however, was that The Insane One already suspected this. When he had first begun to pull the WITs in, he knew that the third one was different from the others. For one thing, he had a mind of his own, and would never sink to let anyone control him. He was self-confident, knowing with all his heart that he could do anything and everything he put his mind to. Lastly, WIT 3 was brave. He stood up to challenges much greater than could be expected of one man. In the eye of disaster, he would not run away, but towards the trouble, looking for a way to make things right. These qualities… they enveloped WIT 3. They made him who he was, and it seemed as if nothing could change this. These qualities were ones of pure good that could never be penetrated by evil. But Minion was not afraid of these, no. He in fact saw them as delicate advantage. But he was afraid of WIT 3. Not because of the person he was… not because of the qualities he held, but because WIT 3 held the same qualities of another man… the only man that had a fair shot at Minion's destruction. WIT 3 reminded Minion of Trigger… and that was a thing that could not be tolerated…
