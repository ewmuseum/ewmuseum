Mall Of Autographs

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2003 [For the first time in years 'Totally Tubular' Tommy finally has a grasp on his destiny. He knew that he was to become a professional wrestler, to work his way through the ranks, and finally capture the World Championship. His father, 'Radical' Randy, was on his way to the same exact thing when Crapper McDoogal, a long time rival, ended his career and his life. At first it was labeled as an accident, but as time passed on it became clear that is was all just some sort of evil set up. McDoogal, the fat, stinky man, made the mat slick on purpose and lured Randy directly into the trap. As Randy fell onto his neck, killing him instantly, 'Far Out' Frank came to his aid. Frank, who was completely stoned, didn't notice that the mat was slick and he also suffered the same fate as Tommy's father. Frank, 'Heavy Metal' Harry's father, did something that he swore he'd do...he died with his best friend in the entire world. It was that night that Tommy KNEW his destiny was within professional wrestling. McDoogal did pay for his heinous crime in the end, but it never made Tommy complete. A week and a half ago, on Tuesday Turmoil, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy finally felt 'Totally' complete. When he felt the title in his hands and the announcement of the new champion a very warm feeling came across him. It was something that he honestly can't describe, but it's as if something was coming from above and hugging him. He completed his fathers rise to the top, just like he said he would do all those years ago. Another thing crossed Tommy's mind as he walked to the back with the title into his arms: More money] [Tommy walks through the largest mall in California, Los Angeles Mall, and he wears a smile across his tan face. His hawaiian dress shirt left open to show the ladies his totally bodacious body and his spandex blue shorts tighter than ever to show them his bitchin' behind. The PCW World Title fits snuggly across his waist as he walks past Spencers Gifts, glancing at some of the gnarly novelties. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's brother and manager, walks along with Tommy decked out in his Twisted Sister T-shirt and black leather pants. All of the mall patrons turn their heads to see the two men walking along as if they owned the place. The women look on with pure lust in their eyes and the men look on with jealousy. Tommy only stretches his smile further at the sight of the reactions that he's getting because that's exactly the way it should be] Little Kid: TOMMY!! TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY!! AWESOME!! [Tommy stops by The Gap and smiles down at the child who looks to be just a little bit over the age of nine years old. He's wearing a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles T-shirt and Tommy finds that to be most bodacious. The Turtles are a most excellent inspiration to the way his lives his life to this day. Michaelangelo is his favorite though, he's TOTALLY BODACIOUS!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Hey little dude! That's a most gnarly T-shirt. Little Kid: Thanks! The Turtles are AWESOME! [Tommy laughs looking up to the childs mother. Blonde, tan, and totally bodacious legs that keep going on forever. Her face isn't anything to write home about but she does have a cute look going for her. Maybe with a little bit of make up in certain areas she could really turn dudes heads ALL THE TIME! Tommy figures that she's just out for a radical afternoon with her son to do some clothes shopping] TTT: You know who is much more awesome than the Ninja Turtles? Little Kid: Ummm...Dragonball Z? [Tommy laughs loudly and looks over to Harry, pointing at the child. Harry has absolutely no idea what's going on and just decides to laugh along] TTT: Dragonball Z...that's cute, little dude. I was just going to tell you that the most bodacious dude in all of the world is none other than...me....'Totally Tubular' Tommy! [The child sort of stands in silence for a few moments, pondering it over in his little head] Little Kid: I don't know. Goku is REALLY strong and I don't know if you could beat him. Especially if he turns super saiyan on you because then it means your REALLY in trouble. TTT: I can turn super saiyan. Can't I, Harry? 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy looks up at the childs mom and sends her a smile. She smiles back brightly and looks down, trying not to meet Tommy's eyes. It's a sign of weakness to The Tubular One as he knows that this babe will be TOO EASY to bag. When a compadre, like Tommy, has everything he needs to be challenged. He needs the best of the best, people who won't just HAND him everything. Tommy needs to do some work, show some charm, AND THEN he gets it handed to him. Not too much work though, he doesn't want to get tired out] Little Kid: You cannot! TTT: Dude, look at my hair!! [Tommy points to his bright blonde locks and shakes then around for the kid] TTT: I'm blonde! I'm already in super saiyan mode! Little kid: Then how come your hair isn't all spiked up like Goku? TTT: It's resting. Little Kid: Hair doesn't rest. TTT: Yes, it does! Who would know more about it? Me, the totally bodacious World Champion and super saiyan or you, the little dude with bodacious taste in cartoons? [The child seems to be thinking things over again, which can turn out to be totally heinous. Kids are much smarter now a days then they were when Tommy was a child. They have all of these totally informative television shows now that really teach them a lot at a young age. It's kind of bogus because it's becoming harder for grown ups to outsmart their children and it's making it much more difficult to raise them. Not like Tommy would know about that, but it's obvious from this little meeting that is going on. Tommy is kind of jealous himself by the little kid. The only informative show that he saw was....MR. WIZARD!!] TTT: Your kid is most bodacious, Mrs....? Woman: Ms. Brown. TTT: ....MS. Brown. He's very smart and I can tell he's going to be a most successful dude when he grows up. Ms. Brown: Well, I hope so for my sake! He needs to take care of his mommy! Harry: I'd like to take care of her, man. Ms. Brown: What was that? TTT: Oh, Harry just says that he takes care of his mom now too. Ms. Brown: Awww, that is SO sweet! Little Kid: You and Xavier Kannon cheated on Tuesday. TTT: Cheated? That is SO TOTALLY OFF, dude! That heinous Chet King and Skylar Green were cheating the entire match, trying to run in and double team yours truly. Xavier and myself decided to take matters into our own hands and teach those heinous evil doers a lesson. I'll say after the way the bodacious Kannon nailed Chet King he won't be cheating anytime soon! We were also teaching little dudes, like yourself, exactly why cheating is a most bogus thing. You know that, right? Little Kid: Of course I do. I may be a kid but I'm not stupid. TTT: You better watch this Friday because it's going to be totally bodacious! I take on Chet King, El Diablo, and Violence Jack in a non-title match. I'm going to have another lesson there and show all the little dudes, like yourself, why it's heinous to HATE somebody for no reason. Little Kid: How come it's non-title? You said that you were going to be a fighting champion. TTT: Yeah...right.... [Tommy sort of remembers saying that but he figured that nobody would remember that. The little kid is really killing him right now] TTT: ...but it's not like any of those dudes DESERVE a shot at the title. It's just filler stuff between cards so I can rest up from my most grueling tag team match from Turmoil! You can't send a wounded animal back out into the wild or else all the other heinous animals will pounce on him. Little Kid: I guess that makes sense. [Tommy thinks about what he just said. He's not really sure WHAT he said but obviously it worked out] TTT: Of course! I'm super saiyan. Little Kid: I was wondering if I could have your autograph? TTT: Sure, dude.... [Tommy pulls out his autograph pen] TTT: ...but it costs thirty bucks. Little Kid: HEY!! Venneman Pendragon signed me an autograph for free. TTT: Well, that just shows how totally stupid that compadre is. [The mother pulls out a twenty dollar bill and a ten dollar bill and hands it to Tommy. He smiles brightly to her and puts the cash in the breast pocket of his hawaiian dress shirt. Tommy then scribbles some things on a piece of paper and hands it back to the kid. He looks at it and seems pleased with it] Little Kid: See ya later, Tommy. Good luck in your match on Friday...because your gonna need it! [The kid walks away laughing as Tommy doesn't really catch on to the child's well planned sarcasm. Tommy stops the mother and gives her a smile, he hands her something and her eyes brighten thinking that it's the money. As she opens her hand she notices the numbers typed up on the nicely cut paper. She then looks up and Tommy who extends his thumb and pinky to his ear and mouth. His mouth motions, 'Call me'. She just smiles and walks after her very smart son. Tommy and Harry walk on through the mall] Another kid: TOMMY!! TOMMY!! Can I have your autograph! [Tommy smiles] TTT: Thirty dollars, dude! Fade to black
