Hangin' out...

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 --- The segment begins with Ganker Ransolver and Hank Gunyon standing in the middle of the street intersection just talking as lines of cars honk their horns at them to move out of the intersection so traffic may proceed. Hank and Ganker ignore them and continue on with their conversation. Hank is wearing green cargo pants, black wife beater, long black leather trench coat, and a red bandana. Ganker is wearing a white plain dirty t-shirt, blue jeans, black zip-up sweatshirt, and over that a black leather jacket. They seemingly manage to to sidestep a car deadset on running them over with the license plate, Red-One. Hank drops on his back laughing, arms over his stomach. Ganker shrugs and lifts Hank backup and they continue their conversation.Ganker: 'So Hank, yo think Red Rock be pissed at Jobber Inc. and our personally selected finisher name?'Hank: (grinning diabolically) 'Oh yeah, it's more than half the reason I picked that name. My main goal in life is to piss off that sum bytch and I remember back in our UWF days, that this one dude by the name of Eric Boggs and his tag team partner Irbe teamed up and called themselves the Red Rock Fan Club, this really pissed of Red Rock at the time, and he swore that night when he made it out a live from the UWF, if he lived to see that day even, that he'd do anything and everything in his power to ban that name and copyright it himself. As yo can obviously see, he failed to do even that oh so simple task, like he will fail this Friday Fusion as he takes on Jobbers Inc.Ganker: 'That he did, but what's wit' our tag team name? I don't remember agreein' to the name, Jobbers Inc?'Hank: 'It was outta yo control boyo. This was strictly my decision and I thought, seein' as I'm the best PCW Jobber ever, that our tag team name should be based off of what my PCW career has mainly been. See, it's a runnin' gag that if I beat yo, yo suck pretty damn bad, but anyway, Jobbers Inc, that jus' popped in my head at the time and I there. Right there, I signed the name down on our contract.'Ganker: 'Don't I have to sign it if it's our contract?'Hank: 'No, yo have no say so in yo damn PCW career yet. Get it through yo head boyo. I am the veteran of PCW here and I know what makes the ratings, and quite frankly Team Nirvana, that can be our name we also go by, but the PCW name is Jobbers Inc. Yo don't have say so until yo have had as many matches as me and jobbed as many as me and won as many as me.'Ganker: 'It sucks to be the rookie.'Hank: 'Something that punk Red Rock will always be... A no good motha' f**kin' rookie~! He only wishes he could be like Hank~! The almighty Jobber King of PCW~! Red Rock wishes so damn much he was like me and 'That's A DAMN Fact~!'Ganker: 'Everybody wishes they could be like Ganker Ransolver, cause.' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' (grins) 'Whether Red Rock likes that fact or not or even chooses to acknowledge it, he knows deep down it's so damn true and needs respect it. He knows when it comes down to Ganker Ransolver versus Red Rock, he jus' don't stand a motha' chance of a f**ker.'Hank: (robot tone) 'Tryin' to compute... Not possible, impossible... CROAK~!' (laughs) 'Speak in english boyo.'Ganker: 'Si senior, el no comprende senior.'Hank: 'Comprende this.' (flips Ganker the bird) 'Speak f**kin' enlish before I end yo miserable stinkin' f**kin' life~!'Ganker: 'Red Rock was no match for me at March Madness, he was my own personal bytch and yo know what, tomorrow night will be the exact sameway, cause...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!'--- Janelle and Ana walk on over to their men. Ana puts her arms around Ganker's neck and Hank places his arm around Janelle's waist and kisses her lightly.Hank: 'I need an Ice House, know if Ray Lota got some boys up here who'd sell us?'Ganker: 'F**k no! Why yo think I hang around yo punk a**? Cause I like yo and yo a friend?' (Hank shrugs) 'Hell f**kin' no! I hang wit' yo cause yo get my liquor, get me drunk underage. Yo have the hook-ups bro.'Hank: (laughing) 'I know man, everyone of my friends use me, it's not like anything new.'--- The segment cuts out.OOC- Sorry this is short and crap, my computer's a bytch and my dad stayed on until 12:30am, which really pissed me off.
