Killer Chronicles Part 1: The Death Bell Tolls... and the Trumpet Sounds

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 The Killer Chronicles Part 1: The Bell of Death tolls, and the Trumpet Sounds. OOC: Yes, I'm still active... I’ve been known to have roleplays without the involvement of my character(s), especially with Rudolph. I believe it is more dramatic if I show how the events and actions of my characters effects the people closely involved to the story, so here goes… It’s been a while since we’ve seen the team of Fear Factory. The last time we saw the fearsome duo, Rudolph got into a little accident with the remaining part of Ivan Larsen. He found himself strapped in a hospital bed for a week, and after the discovery of what lies in the house of Rudolph, his location is pretty certain… [The scene starts to slowly fade in from darkness, showing the hospital room of Rudolph von Hackenmann. Rudolph is sitting up in his bed, after being hospitalized for over a week. He is scheduled to leave the hospital tomorrow and head straight for the local insane asylum. Rudolph has, however, decided that he does not need or want to go to a nut house. In the middle of the night, the security cameras are seen in Rudolph’s room, watching his every move. The scene we see is gray, resembling the picture that a security camera would take. Rudolph is just sitting there, doing nothing, saying nothing, but the evil expression on his face tells you that he has something in mind. Rudolph’s hand reaches over to the call button on the side of his bed and presses the big black button. The call buzzes loudly, and a young, beautiful blond nurse walks into the room. Rudolph’s expression turns sour, but it quickly regains its evil tones. Rudolph darts out of the bed and knocks the woman down on the floor. Rudolph searches all around the hospital room for something, and on the complete opposite side of the room he finds it. A scalpel. Rudolph slowly paces toward the woman as she clinches in fear. She opens her mouth as if trying to scream, but a large breathy sigh comes out instead. Rudolph jumps behind her and covers her mouth with his hand. The feed is suddenly cut, and the camera turns into a large, fancy room with blue carpeting. At a long wooden table in the center of the room, eight people sit. At the head of the table is hospital owner Johann Garregh, M.D. To HIS left is police officer Wolfgang Agrahm, followed by other officer Anni Korger. To her left is the nurse’s husband. At the foot of the table is the asylum’s director, Dr. Van Harra. To the right of Dr. Garregh are three doctors. All of the people at the table are talking as the feed is cut by a remote in Garregh’ hand.] Garregh: I think you can all guess what happens after that… [All the people at the table, including Garregh, shudder at the thought of that event.] Nurse’s Husband: Yes… But why did he do it? To her? Garregh: It wasn’t necessarily an action toward your wife. It was a plan of escape. [The nurse’s husband looks confused.] Nurse’s Husband: What do you mean? Garregh: We found her with her head cut open, but sewn back together. That suggests lobotomy. She was just sitting on the bed when we saw her. We asked her where the man went, but she didn’t answer. Finally, she just fell to the floor, dead. [The nurse’s husband looks as if he is about to cry.] Nurse’s Husband: I ask you… Hunt this sick bastard down and let me watch him in the death chamber! Harra: There will be NO need for that! I want you to find him and send him straight to my recovery home. Nurse’s Husband: First of all, it’s a damn asylum. Second of all, he cut part of a woman’s brain out! [Harra sighs.] Harra: If his case ends up in court, they will definitely win a plea for insanity, and he’ll be shipped off to me anyway! It’s obvious the guy fell of his rocker a while ago! Nurse’s Husband: They won’t! If they do, it means he’ll have a chance to go free again! If they do that, he’ll just kill more people! Harra: Yes, but if they kill him, they will be no better than he was! [Officer Korger slams both of her fists down on the table three times.] Korger: STOP ARGUING! I believe it is up to the police to handle this case, considering it IS a criminal charge. Murder is a pretty serious crime! [The nurse’s husband directs his anger toward Officer Korger now.] Nurse’s Husband: I realize this! I’m just trying to prove to this imbecile— [The nurse’s husband points to Dr. Harra.] That the courts will have him put to death! Korger: One can never tell… Just let us do our job. We’ll hunt this guy down and capture him. Then the courts can decide what to do with him… Garregh: So it’s settled. The courts will decide! Thank you for your time everyone. Meeting dismissed. [All of the people in the room leave, with the exception of Dr. Garregh. Garregh stays in his chair.] People wonder why I’m retiring next year… [The scene slowly fades away from this room. From darkness, the scene fades in to show the nurse’s husband sitting in the waiting room in the upper right corner, with a bunch of other people in the room around him. He is crying like his head was filled with salt and water. Nobody in the room stares, showing respect. They just tend to their own matters, such as waiting. The scene slowly fades away from him to show Officer Korger sitting in the driver’s side of a nice white car with leather interior, and Officer Agrahm sitting in the passenger side. The car is sitting in the parking lot of the hospital, not moving an inch. The two are talking about the recent events.] Korger: That guy looked pretty stricken in there. Agrahm: Wouldn’t you if your husband died? Korger: I don’t have a husband… Agrahm: I realize that, but just saying you did, wouldn’t you feel that way? Korger: I already know how it feels… [Agrahm lowers his head, feeling bad for what he has just said.] Agrahm: I’m sorry. I didn’t know… Korger: It’s okay. You didn’t know… Agrahm: Would you tell me about it? [Korger sighs.] Korger: I suppose… Well, my husband was Bevern Korger. He was a poli— Agrahm: Bevern! He’s a legend here! Korger: That’s because of how he died… He was in a high-speed chase on the highway, going after some guys carrying machine guns and bombs in their car. They were planning to blow up a truck with a shipment of medicines to Germany. They were terrorists. He was trying to stop them. He and his partner shot out both of the back tires of the car, and it swerved in front of the large truck carrying the medicine. The truck rammed it and send it rolling down the highway, off into the Danube river. The truck, about to drive into the river itself, tried to turn, but jackknifed right on the middle of the highway. Bevern’s partner slammed on the brakes, but the car went sideways under the trailer on the back of the truck, crushing Bevern but sparing his partner. Bevern’s partner stood on the highway for hours, waiting for the truck to be moved so he could check on Bevern. When he finally got his wish, he found Bevern, dead from suffocation, not the crushing or the impact. He was nestled down in front of the seat, barely avoiding being flattened, but the negligence of the driver of the truck and the road people caused him to die… [Agrahm sighs.] Agrahm: I’m sorry… That has to be a bad way to go… Korger: Never mind that… We have to stop this Rudolph guy before this turns into another event like that! Agrahm: Agreed! [The two sit back in their seats and buckle their seatbelts. The car speeds off out of the parking lot of the hospital, the scene slowly fading away… The scene opens back up in the meeting room of the hospital for the second time. In the room, Dr. Garregh is sitting at the head of the table, not alone this time though. Mr. Kach, the financer of the hospital, is sitting to his right. They are talking about Garregh’s retirement.] Kach: Johann… I realize that you are getting old, but you’re only 52! You’ve still got many years in you, and you were the best operator this hospital ever had! Garregh: But look how many people have died or disappeared under weird conditions! Kach: But look how many more lives have been saved! Garregh: Still, that does not excuse the fact that a man disappeared from a hospital late in the night without a trace, and was then arrested for killing his wife and child. He killed himself with a gavel during the trial, and during an autopsy they found a computer chip in his head! [Kach looks on with horror.] Kach: What? Something like that happened? Garregh: Yes, unfortunately. [Kach gets a strange grin on his face.] Kach: Remember the guy you are hunting down now? Garregh: It’s Rudolph von Hackenmann. Kach: Yes. He was suspected in the murder of… Ivan Larsen, that’s it. He was also accused of strangling his own father. He might’ve done this too. He has been known to do some pretty freaky scientific experiments… [Garregh’s mouth gapes in awe at the detective work of Kach.] Garregh: But still, this has nothing to do with my retirement. I want to retire because I can, and I feel that there is nothing more I can do for the hospital. Kach: You can keep up its good reputation of helping people live… Garregh: Not when nurses are getting attacked by patients… [Garregh gets up out of his chair and storms out of the room, slamming the door behind him. The scene slowly fades away from the meeting room… The scene starts to fade in again, this time showing the small office of Dr. Van Harra, director of the insane asylum, with oak walls and green carpeting. Dr. Harra sits at his dark wooden desk, cluttered with paperwork. He is busy writing reports, and talks to himself as he copies the information to his laptop computer from the rough reports.] Harra: Why did I have to be my OWN secretary? [Dr. Harra sighs and pulls a paper off the top of the pile to his left.] Patient, Andrew Reddings… Birthplace, Chicago, Illinois, United States… Age, 24… Disorders, Bipolar I, Bipolar II, Schizophrenia… Treatments… Calming medications, tranquilizers, counseling. Cell, 36… [Suddenly, a loud buzz is heard from the speaker above Harra’s head.] Speaker: Alert! Man down in cell 36! Tranquilizer stabbed into arm of guard. Send some backup immediately! [Harra sighs.] Harra: They’ll just beat him down like they usually do. Andrew Reddings… That guy just needs to be put out of OUR misery. [Harra grabs the next paper off of his pile.] Special alert bulletin… Rudolph von Hackenmann. Birthplace, Vienna. Age, Unknown… Disorders, Psychotic Rage and Mental Disabilities… Be on special alert for this man. Insert picture here… Done! [Harra goes to click the print icon on the top of his screen, when his hand just falls off the mouse.] That’s the guy! Holy crap! [Harra clicks the print icon on his laptop, and the scene slowly fades away… The scene opens back up in the office off Officer Agrahm and Officer Korger. They are both sitting at a wooden desk looking at a map of Rudolph’s castle. They are amazed at the size of the place.] Agrahm: Luckily they mapped the place out when they investigated the murder of John von Hackenmann. Korger: This place is huge! Agrahm: (pointing at the map) We’ll most likely find him in the laboratory. Korger: We can worry about where he is when we get there. We just need to memorize this map… [Agrahm sighs.] Agrahm: We’ll go tomorrow? Korger: Yes. I don’t want this guy out where he can hurt people for a few minutes, nevertheless more than one day… Agrahm: Understood. [They continue to chat and study the map intently as the scene slowly fades away from them for the last time. The scene ends in darkness…] To be continued…
