Why? Part 2 2 of 2 Rnd 2 Tourny

17 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/04 I woke up every night after Shanghaied with visions of Jon screaming into the mic……. Kano: I finally understood what PCW and this industry could do to someone. Look at Chris Sloboda here. He is laying here bleeding all over the place, making a mess of MY RING. And to me, this is payback. When Chris left PCW after losing to Miyagawa almost two years ago, he didn’t even say goodbye to me. When he signed with rival promotions, he never gave me a call. HE DIDN’T DO SHIT FOR ME. SO FUCK CHRIS SLOBODA. Dave: I still am in shock. Jeff: Shhhhh, our GM is speaking. Kano: Boda wasn’t there for me when I got injured. He wasn’t there for me when my career was nearly ended. I BUSTED MY ASS FOR THIS COMPANY, WHILE BODA REAPED THE REWARDS. I BROKE MY FUCKING NECK. IVAN STANISLAV CRACKED A VERTEBRAE IN MY NECK. HE DIDN’T EVEN COME TO THE HOSPITAL. BODA WAS, IS, AND ALWAYS WILL BE ABOUT BODA. Something about that last statement bothered me. After all, Jon made a valid point. I am all about me. Now. Of course, like I said earlier, it wasn’t always that way. Especially when it came to Jon and I. We were brothers…. Until the bar fight incident in Huntsville, Alabama. So like I said, I spent a month at home, waiting to hear from Jon. Never happened. Even when I was sent to PCW2, Jon never called me, never came and watched his ‘brother’. Jon Kano was a star in PCW, and it was because I covered his ass. BODA WAS, IS AND ALWAYS WILL BE ABOUT BODA. Everytime I heard that over and over in my head, I couldn’t help but think ‘why’. Why would a man that at one time was like my own brother, hold up all this rage, for this long a time? Why? Because he was blinded by the truth. And I’m not talking about the truth behind his statements. The truth he is blinded about is that I am better than he is. Let me run this back a bit for you. After PCW2 failed, and yes, it failed, I came back to PCW, looking to reinvent Boda. An ex-NBA star wasn’t getting me over anymore. I had won only a few titles my first year there. Extreme and Tag titles. Hell, I won more in two months of PCW2, then in a year of PCW. Oh well, I still got a shiny World title belt at home that reads PCW2 on it. Anyhow, back on subject…… I came back to PCW looking to make a name for myself, with or without anyone’s help. At that time, there was a new stable running wild in PCW. And the leader of that stable was the most impressive wrestler PCW had ever seen. Ivan Stanislav. He was a genetic specimen, purely built on old fashioned working out and eating right for life. And he was taking his Red Army and running all over the competition in PCW. That even included Jon, who was trying to stand up against the communist regime. So there I was, thinking of a way to reinvent myself, to make myself more marketable, to make myself a Superstar. And something clicked. I could either help out with Jon, and work against The Red Army, something I thought Jon would have wanted. Or I could follow my roots. After all, like I said almost 3 years ago when I joined Ivan and The Red Army, I’m Czechoslovakian, and my father was a spy for the Russian government. So, I found that little niche, I had hoped, to make a name for myself. And guess what, Jon finally came to talk to me. He caught up with me in the locker room the night of my ‘swerve’. He told me he needed my help, to ‘combat’ The Red Army. I told him I’d think about it, because I was busy getting ready for my main event match against another PCW monster…. Meanstreak. And beating Streak put me in the next round of a World Title tournament, one that I wanted to use to make an impact in my second PCW stint. So Jon left the locker room, to leave me alone, to get ready, and to, as he hoped, prepared to form a stable with him. Almost three months earlier was the last time Jon spoke to me. After a strong 2 year friendship, he couldn’t find it in his heart to check up on me when I was doing HIS suspension, paying HIS fines. I came back to PCW and Jon never once made an effort to come see me, to see how I was doing. But suddenly, again, Jon needed Chris to help him out. To make HIM look better. Right then and there, because of Jon’s own doing, I decided to DO WHAT IT TOOK TO MAKE MYSELF A SUPERSTAR. Looking back on it now, I know why. I figured out why Jon had the balls to say….. BODA WAS, IS AND ALWAYS WILL BE ABOUT BODA. Because I made a promise to myself, to my kids and to my then wife Stacey that I wasn’t going to settle on mid-carder salary. Hell no, I wanted to live life big again. I was use to make 6 figures a season, and dammit… I wanted that again. I needed that again. So, just like Kano did to me months prior, I chose to ignore him. And later that night, after a hard fought battle, going toe to toe with Meanstreak, I did it. I stood up to Ivan, standing next to Jon as he stood up to the big Russian. And just when Jon thought he was going to be able to use me again, I turned and laid him out. Much like he did to me at Shanghaied two weeks ago. Only I didn’t need a weapon. I did something to make myself a star, and Jon was the reason I did. My kids asked me two days after that night, why. ‘Why daddy, why did you hit Uncle Jon?’. I explained to them that it was part of a story we were telling, and that I didn’t hurt ‘Uncle’ Jon. Okay was the answer I got from them. I told them it was okay to still call him ‘Uncle’ Jon, and to still talk to him when they want to. After all, Jon did care about my girls, even if he didn’t care about me anymore. So after much contemplating the last two weeks, I finally figured out why Jon did what he started at Shanghaied. Because he was jealous of me. I didn’t help him continue to become a superstar. I took my own course in PCW, and I went on to become a Tag Champion again. I also finally won the Continental Championship, the same title Jon screwed me out of getting almost two years prior. And I was finally given the ball to run with in PCW. The World Title. A title Jon has never held here. After taking the fall for Jon I became a Legend, with out and before, him. That’s the real reason why. Not because I never called him when he was hospitalized. After all, he never called me, remember. Not because I didn’t call him when I went to work in FSW or OSW. Because Jon never helped me stay in PCW, like a real friend would of. I forgot about Jon, just like he forgot about me. After all, this is pro wrestling, you don’t become a superstar without stepping on a few toes. Any true legend will tell you that. Any poor schlep will simply beat down a one time best friend, and cry about never receiving a hospital visit or a phone call. I finally answered my questions. After two weeks of watching Shanghaied, revisiting my past in PCW, my friendship with Jon, I knew why. And Jon will soon find out why you don’t beat me down, with out killing me. That brings me not to asking ‘why’ anymore. It brings me to ask ‘what now’. What now becomes of Jon and Chris? Well, Kano and his boys mocked my career’s death over a week ago. So as far as I’m concerned, I’m going to show the world, and prove to everyone that you can not kill me, that I am far from deceased. I’m going to come back strong, translating what The Players call my dead career, into another World Championship. And it starts with Jon. My PCW career started with him, and now my next World Title reign starts with him. But I’m just a bit fearful of this match. I’m afraid that Turmoil will not be able to handle a match of this much ‘OOC’ importance. Sure this is a battle to see who gets to stay in the Face to Face tourney. But to me, it’s more important than that. It’s to finally prove to Jon that I am the Legend he’ll never be. But he’ll never truly accept that, after all, he’s got three monkeys that believe he’s the true Legend. And he also has them convinced that they ended my career. Or more so, he HAD them convinced. After all, I proved them wrong this past Saturday. And now this coming Saturday, Jon will find out why I am a two time PCW World Champion, an OSW World Champion and the better wrestler of us two ex-best friends.
