Put On Some Deodorant! (Part 2 is BorroW-A-National Hero series)

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 8: 15 P.M.Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Streets of Lisbon “Never Fear Team Portugal is here” Part 2 The scene opens with BorroW-A-National Hero and the rest of team Portugal soaring through the sky in search of the Smelly Frenchies and Zinedine Zidane. The team is ready for action and looking to stop the evil of the French once and for all. As BorroW-A-National Hero is flying he thinks about Rent-A-Hero his arch nemesis and most skillful combatant. He wonders if RAH could ever change around his life to conform to the great wonder The Golden Toe does. He’s showing everyone right now how easy it is to be RAH, but could RAH take the ball and show everyone how hard it is to be the Golden Toe. I think not, but who knows. Time flies by and Juan spots a small narrow street being bombarded by blue shirts and green air. BorroW-A-National Hero: Guys, I think we found our villains. Cunning Couto: That’s disgusting; we’ll die if we go down there. BorroW-A-National Hero: Couto, we may die, we may all die, but were doing it for our country and that’s all that matters. Put on the jockstrap get ready to fight. Are you guys with me? The men all let out a holler of satisfaction as they approach the area. When they arrive, the men strap on their face jockstraps and slowly make their way to the group. The group gets within a hundred foot radius and the smell starts to seek in so Juan and the team takes post for a while. BorroW-A-National Hero: All right guys, as you see this isn’t going to be easy. I thought the power of Montoya’s midsection would help us more, but I guess it doesn’t. This means we’ll have to make this quick with as few mistakes as possible. Pretty Boy Pinto: So how should we approach and what do we do if they start to attack us? BorroW-A-National Hero: Well the team isn’t that bad. All we have to do is put up with their greasy hair, and occasional maggot smell, but once we hit Zidane, we should all be ready for one of the worst experiences of our life. Furious Figo: What if they attack us? BorroW-A-National Hero: They won’t even think about attacking us, because they don’t even realize they’re own smell. They’re all so used to it that they think it’s normal. Meanwhile, Captain Costa looks at the scene around him, the women and children screaming trying to run out of the vicinity. He sees torture in the air, torture that even seeks through the heavy armpit aroma. Costa knows life shouldn’t be lived like this. He can’t stand around and wait, as his people fall one by one. The crying is driving Costa nuts. The air is becoming thick and Costa can no longer take it. Captain Costa: I can no longer take it! If I’m going out I’m going in style. Captain Costa salutes his teammates. BorroW-A-National Hero knows what he’s about to do and tries to stop him, but it’s too late. Just as Juan grabs his shirt, he runs off. That run is a run that will stick in BorroW-A-National Hero’s head for a long time. The young Captain Costa took each long step of courage and bravery. The smell didn’t bother him as he pushed through the crowd of French national team players, holding his jockstrap close to his nose. He arrives right in front of Zidane about two feet away. BorroW-A-National Hero thinks he’s about to do it and put on the deodorant, but right as the thought enters him Captain Costa falls. A fall heard all throughout Portugal. Zidane then continued to walk over him like nothing even happened. BorroW-A-National Hero: NOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Pretty Boy Pinto: CAPTAIN COSTA! I have to go get him he might die there. Pretty Boy Pinto goes after him, but Juan intercepts him, holding him back and trying to make sure he doesn’t do anything stupid. Pretty Boy Pinto: He’s gonna die Juan we can’t let him die. The emotions show throughout Juan, and for a moment Juan doesn’t know at all what to do. He looks over to Cunning Couto who is completely frantic because the smell is so strong it’s having his Afro wear away. Cunning Couto: My hair, the smell is ruining my hair. I have to get out of here. BorroW-A-National Hero: No we’re staying. Furious Figo: We can’t stay. We must go. This place is bad very bad. Furious Figo is sitting in a corner shaking back in forth looks around almost as if he’s crazy. He then starts munching away at his fingernails, until Juan comes over to him and shakes sense into him. BorroW-A-National Hero: You’re going crazy my fair crime fighting national icons. We must keep cool in the face of danger. It’s one for all and all for one, am I right? Now are you guys going to try and put our heads together, or just sit there and mope? Pretty Boy Pinto: He’s right man we have to think clear. Let’s put our hands in. Pretty Boy Pinto puts his hand in, followed by the hand of BorroW-A-National Hero. Furious Figo is the next to put his hand in as they all wait for Couto. Cunning Couto: All right, but you guys all paying for my blow-drying bill this month, I’m serious. Couto then puts his hand in and they all begin to think. BorroW-A-National Hero: First off we need powerful smelling things, but I don’t know what? Pretty Boy Pinto: We can use fish. Furious Figo: And the spray they have in Nursing homes, those have a real heavy smell, nothing can out do those. Cunning Couto: Don’t forget scented candles. Nothing has the great aroma of scented candles. BorroW-A-National Hero: Okay, Furious Figo use your Furious feet and lighting quick speed to get all of those items and report back here in a minute. Furious Figo: Check. Figo is then off in search of all of the items. BorroW-A-National Hero: Pretty Boy Pinto get ready we’re going to have to use your sparkling white teeth to distract the opposition. I’ll join you as I’ll try to hypnotize them with my golden toe. Pretty Boy Pinto then goes off to brush his teeth so they’re extra sparkly. BorroW-A-National Hero: And you Cunning Couto will fight the smell one on one with your ultra long hair. Cunning Couto: Aye Aye Juan. Couto then goes off and starts grooming his hair. A minute later all of the men are back. Figo has all the accessories and the attack begins as quickly as possible. BorroW-A-National Hero: Good luck guys, and remember this has to be extremely quick. The men all nod as they start the attack. BorroW-A-National Hero takes off his shoe and starts using his golden toe hypnosis while Pretty Boy Pinto uses his sparkling smile to blind the players. As this is going on Figo uses his lighting quickk feet to place the fish, scented candles, and spray the nursing home spray all throughout the area. All of the different smells start to where out the players smell, but Zidane is still standing strong. After awhile pretty boy Pinto’s teeth start to become yellow and BorroW-A-National Hero’s golden toe starts melting. When Cunning Couto sees this he grabs a deodorant stick and charges. He uses his hair to protect himself, but his hair then starts to dissapear. The smell is so strong as he gets closer it even starts to seek through his skin. When Couto sees this he tries to turn back, but passes out from the smell. Pretty Boy Pinto: Couto is down! BorroW-A-National Hero looks around before he grabs two sticks of deodorant. He throws one to Figo and keeps one himself. When Juan nods the two men kick the sticks of deodorant. The deodorant flies through the sky melting in mid air. BorroW-A-National Hero doesn’t think it will make it, but it finally does landing right in Zinedine Zidane’s armpit. The smell suddenly becomes much less lethal. BorroW-A-National Hero takes a deep breath before him; Pretty Boy Pinto and Furious Figo get the fallen Couto and Costa. BorroW-A-National Hero signals for Furious Figo to use his speed to take Costa and Couto to a hospital, Meanwhile, BorroW-A-National Hero confronts Zinedine Zidane. BorroW-A-National Hero: What are you doing, haven’t you ever heard of deodorant or taking a shower. Zinedine Zidane: Deodorant? Showers? BorroW-A-National Hero becomes mad and grabs Zidane by the collar screaming. The two scuffles with one another, when all of a sudden out of nowhere is Cantona, the retired English superstar. BorroW-A-National Hero: What are you doing here? Cantona: If you guys want to settle this, then I know the perfect place! Zidane: Where? Cantona puts his arms around the shoulders of both Zidane and BorroW-A-National Hero he then blows into the air and a picture appears, a picture of a cage with a scorpion inside. They then see two nets and field, with SKO written on it. BorroW-A-National Hero and Zidane both look at each other and nod meaning they agree. Cantona then twiddles his fingers and smiles. Scene then fades to black. TO BE CONTINUED………….. The Golden Heart Tales are not to be written or sold by anyone but Golden Heart Tales Production. Copyright 2002.
