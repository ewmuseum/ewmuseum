It's dramatic! It doesn't HAVE to make sense!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: sometime before just now. (We see a wind-swept rocky crag overlooking the pounding surf of the Atlantic Ocean. THE Multiple Man sits and broods.) Voiceover: “How long I sat there and watched the waves smash the cliff to gravel, I can’t say. Maybe a minute, maybe a year. It didn’t matter, it’s all the same anyway.” “Fine, I want my yearly salary paid to me every minute.” “Shush! I’m recording a dramatic voiceover, do you mind?” “But that line didn’t make a lick of sense.” “It’s dramatic! It doesn’t HAVE to make sense! Now be quiet!” (A low rumble of thunder accompanies the faint flicker of lightning upon the horizon.) Voiceover: “The air held a charge more electric than any natural weather phenomenon could explain. It was a certain tenseness… an anticipation of that which was coming.” “All you can eat wings at Garth's!” “Eh, the sauce quality lacks, and his bleu cheese dressing is runny.” “Who cares? Three beers and YOU can’t taste anything anyway, you lightweight.” “Stop that! Both of you!” (Rain rakes the terrain with needles of ice in a brief winter squall.) Voiceover: “The Messiah, Christopher Chase. A prophet of dubious providence, to be sure. What message did he hope to bring to the masses? To what paths did he seek to turn their weary legs?” “If you had bothered to look at the scouting report I spent TWO WEEKS assembling, you wouldn’t be wasting time asking those questions.” “I DID read it… or at least most of it, but that’s not the issue here!” “Hardly. You’ve been spouting useless rhetoric for the past ten minutes, all of which could be circumvented if you just knew what you were talking about, instead of shoveling it like an English Major.” “I’m setting mood!” “You’re spinning your wheels. At least if you insulted his mother, there’d be a POINT to it, however juvenile.” “Wow, she’s pretty much quoted Joe word for word.” “Joe who?” “Joe Momma! HA!” “Steve, leave the insults to the professionals, will you? Amateurs tend to get hurt when they step into the arena with the big boys.” “I’LL hurt him if he puts out another groaner like that.” “Heh.” “Now be quiet.” (Resolute, he stares into the inky abyss of the oncoming night, unfeeling to mere elemental matters, his every fiber focused on that elusive infinite.) Voiceover: “Though I could not fathom the form of the advancing challenge, I knew the reason behind it. Our love-hate relationship stretched far back into the murky recesses of my past, a cold and brutal mistress who I had not the strength to push away from me forever. A life draining succubus whose kisses were sweeter than the finest nectar.” “Sounds like someone needs a little action, or at least a few 1-900 phonecalls.” “He can’t, because YOU’RE tying up the lines at all hours!” “Umm… am not?” “Please, you’re such a regular that Madame Chatshka’s House of Unusual Telecommunication has given you a direct hotline!” “You’re just making that up!” “Then explain the autographed Visa card with the questionable logo on it. How she manages to do that with a telephone booth boggles my mind.” “Who does what with a telephone booth?” “Don’t listen to her, she’s lying!”“I dunno, I seem to remember seeing something similar from the Dehydrated Pleasure Company a few days ago…” “People! Stop this stupidity at ONCE!” “Thanks boss.” “What Steve does with his own money on his own time is HIS business and we are not to judge his choice of lifestyle!” “Hey!” “Now sit down and shut up!” (A thin tendril of pure light connects heaven and earth for the barest of instants. The echoing thunder sounding like the roar of some primeval beast.) Voiceover: “The ghosts of one’s past always rise up when they’re least expected. If you fail to properly put them to rest, that is. Regrets were of no use to me now, the past is gone, the future unwritten. All that I could do was grab now with both hands and…” “[Something in Spanish.].” “Not you TOO?! That’s IT! Screw this, and to hell with all of YOU, I’m leaving!” “Hmm… well, that was abrupt, wasn’t it?” “He really should get a better hold on his temper, it’s unhealthy.” “[Something in Spanish.]?” “I suppose we could finish the voiceover to him as a favor.” “Heh, interesting idea.” (The howling tempest is almost upon him now. In the measure of eternity, he is already enmeshed, but in the eyes of man, there is still a second to think, to blink, to be alive.) Voiceover: “And then it occurred to me… Doctor Quinn, Medicine Woman’s cancellation was perhaps for the best. Filled with new resolve, I decided not to stare any longer at the matte painting and wading pool in front of me.” (Camera fades out.)
