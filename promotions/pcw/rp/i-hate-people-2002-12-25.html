I Hate People

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/12/02 The following is a promotional video for Primetime Championship Wrestling: I Hate People © Anti Nowhere LeagueFootage © PCW The words 'Imagine your best friend, the one you thought would be there in ten year's time, turned on you.' appear on the screen in bold lettering on a black background. Next, an image of Pranks With Attitude walking to the ring with the Tag Team Championships around their waists is displayed, then fades to black and white, then to black completely. Another set of words then appears: 'Imagine everything declared null and void in that moment.' The screen then shows the slow motion footage of Gaz Green delivering the low blow to Nerezza and smirking, then standing alongside Xavier Kannon with a gigantic grin. The image turns to red, then the screen zooms out from the words: 'Now imagine what you'd do to them...' Suddenly, the song 'I Hate People' by The Anti-Nowhere League kicks in and along with the falling chords of the guitar, the screen shows clips of Nerezza executing his half of The Darker Side Of Comedy. 'God I wish that I could hide away and find a wall to bang my brains' Nerezza slams his fists against the side of P.W.A's metal container, yelling at Natalia Miyagawa. 'I'm living in a fantasy, a nightmare dream…..reality.' Shots of Nerezza using his Define Reality move, transforming into his true vampire shape and roaring loudly with cruel red eyes. 'People ride about all day in metal boxes made away' Footage of Nerezza inside the purple hearse, chasing after Akiyama and the time he posted signposts for a massive religious gathering in Hubbardville. 'I wish that they would drop the bomb and kill these freaks that don't belong!' Action replays from different angles of Nerezza executing Awe Of He on Statuz Quo at the Fusion before Alliances 2K2. 'I hate people……..I hate the human race' The camera pans past the front row of a PCW event, then pulls out to reveal the capacity crowd cheering. 'I hate people………I hate your ugly face' A close-up of Nerezza's mouth with bloodstained red teeth and his snaking pink tongue. 'I hate people………I hate your f***ing mess' The scene where Nerezza pulled down the scaffolding of the 16-Man No Ladder match, leaving a pile of debris and rubble. 'I hate people………They hate me!' Nerezza being punched in the face by the Void-Armoured Akiyama, taking the Hallmark from Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams sending him Whiskey Bent And Hell Bound. As a small instrumental plays, a small clip of Nerezza plays specifically for the video. He extends his index and middle finger on his right hand to inhuman length and gives an offensive sign, saying: 'Bloody humans.' The rest of the song then starts up, showing more footage. 'My mother thinks that I am a jerk because I hate my bleeding work' Shots of Nerezza punching the already bleeding face of Xavier Kannon and crawling from the cage that Skitzo sent flying through a sheet of glass, covered in cuts and slices. 'Be like daddy, he's sincere, but don't be true 'cos you're a queer' A replay of Nerezza dressed as Scorpion from Mortal Kombat, making Boca Del Inferno dances as he bursts into 'flames'. 'I'm working at my 9 to 5 with boring scum that give me jibe' Nerezza commentating on the Black On Black Violence match and mocking Statuz Quo 'talking of the love they give, they never give………they never give' Nerezza's failed date with Teresa Tomas, clutching his head in one hand as Teresa makes obscene gestures to a french maid. 'I hate people……….I hate the human race' Nerezza gives the classic Good Evening Western Consumers catchphrase to a huge crowd. 'I hate people………I hate your ugly face' A clip of vampire Nerezza digging his nails into the faces of Darkstar and Scourge during the No-Ladder match. 'I hate people………I hate the ****ing mess' Nerezza spraying a huge glob of purple slime into the face of Ellie~! 'I hate people……..They hate meeeeee!' Assorted clips of Nerezza falling victim to Mikey Marvelous at Alliances 2K2, being 'staked' in the chest by Skitzo with a black dildo and finally, a high speed reel of Miyagawa's head being rammed into the wall, Gaz being dropped onto a broken up steel chair, Skitzo being spun around on the end of the same dildo and to finish off, a clip of exclusive footage of Nerezza's open mouth lunging towards the camera which causes the screen to suddenly change to complete black, then Nerezza's trademark purple logo fading in. To wrap up, one last sound clip is played. 'I can't fly us back to Texas, you MOOK!'
