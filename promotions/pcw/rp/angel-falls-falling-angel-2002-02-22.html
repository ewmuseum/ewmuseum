Angel Falls - Falling Angel

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd Febuary 2002 Where ever you go, there you are. That was a quote from Star Trek who actually quoted it from the adventures of Buckaroo Banzaii if I’m not mistaken. I just thought it sounded like a neat intro. But speaking of intros, if I’m not mistaken, which I usually am... erm... or rarely not depending on who you are, then you’ll be wanting to find out where this promo is located. You see according to the rules of wrestling promos, the first three paragraphs of a promo should be talking about the location and what the wrestler is wearing, rather than the current fluctuation in the Argentinean Currency Markets, or how the Japanese Fish Stock is dwindling. So I guess I’d better get on with it. It’s a jungle out there... why? Because it’s literally a jungle, with trees and bugs and vines and more bugs and natives and more bugs and snakes and more bugs and more trees and more bugs and spiders and more bugs and vines and more bugs. By this point in this promo you should have a fair idea that Jungle tends to contain a lot of bugs. We join Miyagawa knee deep in... well... bugs. Well not exactly just like that because he’s being guided by several master trackers. Yes that’s right, it’s a nutty English guy who thinks he knows the jungle like the back of his hand, plus a native who has more sense than to tell the nutty English guy when he’s about to fowl something up. For example... last night... English (in an upper crust English accent, of course): You see in order to keep the mosquitoes away, you break open a termite mound and grab some of the red rocks and then put them on the fire... the smell it gives off keeps all mosquitoes away for the rest of the night. The trio walk though the jungle, cutting a swathe through the foliage with machetes. They eventually come to a termite mound and the English guy hacks at it with a machete and sticks his hand inside. Three seconds later, he pulls his hand out clutching at it and screaming in agony as a hundred soldier termites attack his hand with pincers. Miyagawa: You know, I thought that whole mosquito thing was Australia. Native: It is. Care for some insect repellent? The English guy runs around screaming while cussing and hitting his hand with various things trying to get the termites off... eventually he seems sense and sticks his hand in a puddle of water, virtually drowning the termites off his hand. Meanwhile, Miyagawa and the Native are spraying theirselves with insect repellent in a can. The English guy turns to them angrily. English: You could have told me. Native: I would have, but you had a go at me after I tried to tell you that vine was a snake this morning. So I kept quiet. English: But that vine WAS a snake. It was only that I hit it was a machete that it didn’t bite me and kill me. That thing was highly poisonous. Miyagawa: Aye, but it did taste all the better for it. Plus I’m going to have some rather classy red and yellow striped snake skin boots when we get back to the states. You know those animal rights activists that ask you if you would kill an animal to wear its skin... well now I can say that not only did I kill it, but I ate it too. So how much longer have we go to go before we get there? Native: We’re only a day away or so now sir. English: **Points to a nice open area** Good, we should get on at top speed and find somewhere to camp. I think over there would be good. Native: **Points to a rather secluded area** That’s an ant nest, sir. I would suggest over there, it’s dry and in shelter. English: Stop telling me what to do, I know what’s best, I am the explorer here you know. I’m camping over there where I say. Miyagawa: Let’s see... stay with annoying English person on top of ant nest and wake up with about three thousand bites all over my body... or stay with Native who knows how to create hallucinogenic drugs from local foliage. I think I’ll go with the native. English: Fine then, be like that. And there we have it. Now it is morning, and the journey’s end cannot be too far away. The English man got up early... because an ant bit his testicles... and that does tend to wake you up rather sharply. Meanwhile the Native and Miyagawa slept quite well in their hammocks, especially after several hours of locally produced hallucinogenic drugs had taken their toll on the duo. Miya and the Native are just packing up as the English guy approaches. English: Why the hell didn’t you people tell me about that Ant’s nest I slept on last night? Native: I did. English: And that there are crocodiles in the river just beyond those trees. Native: Yesterday morning I pointed them out. It’s the same river, it’s just long. Those crocodiles are the reason why we didn’t go by river, that and you insisted on going through the jungle, why normal people would go by... English: I am an English explorer. I do not go by boat. We always go through the jungle, across the icy wastelands, up the mountain, down the cliff... Miyagawa: Ever thought of just well... staying at home? English: Staying at home? And do what? I live for adventure! Miyagawa: Spend a year trying to find a bathplug in Russia, that’s an adventure... or a reasonably priced PC in PC World... that’s a mission, never mind an adventure. Try and find a piece of useful information about a certain former NTICW World Champion... that’s not an adventure, that’s just well... dire to be honest. English: Eh? Miyagawa: Are we going to these waterfalls or not? Actually I’m sure I can hear something... Native: Yes indeed you can sir; the waterfalls are about six hundred meters further on. Miyagawa: So why didn’t we go there last night? Miyagawa and the Native both stare at the English guy who just stares back in his eightieth century big game hunter’s outfit looking rather sheepish. English: How was I supposed to know? Miyagawa: By asking first before making presumptions? By not presuming that you are better than another person. English: But you’ve been talking about how much better you are than Drake Maxwell all this time we’ve been travelling in here. Miyagawa: That’s because I am better than Drake Maxwell. We’ve already fought and I beat him. When we thought again, he beat Clyde. When I fought Clyde on Tuesday I beat him. So therefore, I’m still better than Drake Maxwell. Not to mention charming, wittier, and more charismatic than him too... although my face isn’t as pretty as his is anymore. I wonder if it’s the bandages that does it, or just the occasional fluid seepage that does it. Native: It’s defiantly the seepage. Shall we get going then seeing as it’s right up ahead. I want to get a beer. Miyagawa: There’s a pub? Native: Oh hell yeah. I could really do with a pint of fosters. Miyagawa: And they serve proper beer there? Native: Sure thing. Shall we? Because the English and the Native even start walking, Miyagawa has sprinted off ahead in the jungle and the camera decides to follow him. After twenty meters however of running through dense foliage, he’s back at a walk. We rejoin them half an hour later just as they break through the edge of the jungle and hear the sounds of the waterfall... cut out by the sounds of an ice cream truck. Miyagawa adjusts his eyes to the sunlight through the clearing... well... he puts on sunglasses. As he sees what is here, he is amazed... at the drive through McDonalds, the pub, the waterfall and did I mention... the high speed motorway next to it all. Miyagawa: There’s a motorway. Why didn’t we come by car? English: You said you wanted a guide through the jungle. Miyagawa: Yes, but I didn’t expect there to be a motorway. You could have said something. English: Didn’t cross my mind, sorry. Do I get my thousand pounds now then? Miyagawa: Dammit. If you’re going to do something, get your research right first. Besides, it is rather pretty. Native: It’s called Angel Falls; it’s the heights unbroken waterfall in the world. You see unlike Victoria Falls or Niagara Falls, there are no double waterfalls, it’s all one thing. Miyagawa: Angel Falls huh? Now I remember the point to this promo. Native: What’s that? Miyagawa: Well it’s... And the screen fades to pink... with a fleur de lees pattern in green. What? This is an experimental film.
