Metal Gear Spoofage (part 2)

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/02/02 Standing over the second corpse of a person he was to be rescuing, our hero Solid Snake furiously clutches his ear and radios in. Snake: Okay folks, it was cute the first time but now this heart attack thing is getting old. Bethany: I don't know, why do you blame me for all this stuff? Snake: You're the craziest bitch in Alaska right now. Bethany: Complements will get you nowhere. Snake: Feh. Colonel, do you know anything? Snake peers into his monitor, where he sees what seems to be Colonel Campbell prancing about in a frilly frock. Abruptly he stops short and covers up, looking flustered. Colonel: Uh, this isn't what you think. Snake: You don't want to know what I think. Tell me what to do now! Colonel: Contact my niece. Her frequency is on the back of the CD case. Snake: .....what CD case? Colonel: You know, the CD case. The thing the CD came in. Snake: ....Colonel I don't own a CD. Colonel: THIEF! COPYRIGHT INFRINGER! THE IDSA KNOWS WHERE YOU LIVE! REMEMBER! THEY KNOW WHERE YOU LIVE! Snake: .....what? Colonel: Uh, nevermind, it's 141.52. Shaking his head, Snake presses his finger into his ear again, a female voice responding. Amy: Who're you? Snake: I'm obviously an idiot. I'm here speaking to you aren't I? Amy: ....well I can't argue with that. Snake: Yeah. Your uncle sent me here to save you. Amy: How do you know my uncle? Snake: He seems to get me into idiotic situations every so often. Like this one. Amy: Oh. Well, come get me then. I'm in.... Suddenly a voice erupts somewhere behind the female soldier. Voice: What the hell's that smell? Second Voice: Did someone slaughter a mackerel and leave it out overnight? Amy: .....damn they're onto me, gotta go. With that the young lady signs off, leaving a very confused Solid to wander in the direction he came. Through a door he trots, emerging in a huge snowfield. Snake: Ugh....uh.... Trudging through the ice, Snake looks on....then a huge explosion erupts underneath his feet. Up into the air he flies, spiraling through the air. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! Finally our hero crashes facefirst into the snow, laying motionless. A ringing sounds in his ear. Voice: Snake, watch out! There are claymore mines around there! Snake: (Lifting his head) Yeeeeeehoebra. Snake slowly climbs to his feet, shaking off the snow. He then looks up, just in time to see what seems to be a cannon blasting squarely at his feet and once again sending him up into the air. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! Solid crashes painfully off the ground, bounces into the air, does a complete flip and lands in a heap. Voice: There's a tank waiting up there too to ambush you! Snake: (Face down) Mmmmmmphahamaph. A rumbling awakens the hero, he sits up to see indeed a small tank advancing on his position. The hatch opens, revealing a behemoth covered in scars. Snake: Uh oh. The monster glares at Snake, squinting through one closed eye. He then throws his arms to the sky, roaring. Man: I R VULCAN MEANSTREAK!!!!!! Snake: I don't get paid enough for this crap. Eeeaaaagh! Solid charges forward, stepping on another mine and propelling himself through the air. He lands right in front of the tank, prompting a curious look from Meanstreak. Meanstreak: Snakes are animals to be feared....however, I think I can say you put no fear into anyone whatsoever. Snake: (Flat on his back) Shut up. Snake groggily stands once again....marches up to the tank's cannon, holding his arms out. Snake: Go on, shoot me. I dare you. Meanstreak: Very well. The huge man aims his gun directly at Snake's chest. Meanstreak: .....ya sure about this? Snake: SHOOT ME!!!!! Meanstreak: Yer funeral, cunt. With that Streak pulls a trigger.....however just as he does Snake moves. In one motion he shoves a finger into the cannon's muzzle, causing it to backfire and explode in a huge ball of fire. The vehicle flies into the air, crashes, its treads shattering into the snow. Snake: Sucker. Our hero stomps angrily to the side of the smoking tank, reaches down and plucks up a keycard. He then walks off into the distance, as the hatch again pops open revealing a slightly scorched Vulcan Meanstreak, who holds his ear in pain.Meanstreak: Well boss, I hope yer happy. He busted up the tank. Liquid Clyde: We'll play with him a little longer. Meanstreak: Whatever, shitbag. I'm only getting paid to do a cameo in this horrible spoof. Liquid Clyde: So hard to find good help these days. Meanstreak: Meh. A bit later, and Snake makes his way through a small corridor. As he opens a door a horrible stench assaults him, immediately identifiable as the smell of death. Snake: Amy? Are you here? Peeking his head in, the commando sees it is not in fact Amy causing this odor but several dead bodies. Their blood stains the walls, the ceiling....and around a corner, a girlish shriek catches his attention. Voice: Ah god no not there, NOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAGH! Running as fast as he can, Snake rounds the bend to see an odd sight. A soldier hangs in midair, blood pouring from his crotch. After an eternity a flash of light blinks, and the ninja Snake had seen previously stands, holding the poor man impaled on a sword. Snake: That's....ow. Noticing the presence of Snake, the cyborg withdraws his blade and sends the soldier crashing to the floor. He then enters the nearest door, Snake following suit. In this chamber, the warrior sees a man in a white coat backpeddling on the floor. Urine streams down his leg as he trembles before the huge ninja before him. Akininja: Where is our friend. Man: Our? Slowly turning his head, the odd creation notices Solid's entrance. His voice reflects a bit of humor, digitized as it may be. Akininja: Ah, we've been waiting for this moment. Snake: (Raising an eyebrow) What we? There's you. Only you. No one else. Akininja: Now we will enjoy the moment. We will kill you, or you will kill us. It makes no difference. Snake: What weAGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! Snake is cut off as a blow from the ninja's rock hard fist sends him flying across the room. He crashes into the wall, clutching his head and muttering. Snake: Someone help me. The Akininja closes in on our hapless hero, but then drops to his hands and knees. He slams his head repeatedly off the floor, hoarsely crying out. Akininja: Me.....medicine.... Snake: (Whipping out his handgun) Got your medicine, say ah! Snake begins firing his gun madly, bullets plinking off the metal hide of the cyborg. Finally he stands, rushing straight past the hero and bowling him over before heading out the door. Snake: (Shaking his head) God that hurts. Back on the far side of the chamber, the man in the white coat cowers. Snake makes his way over, looking down upon him. Snake: How long are you gonna.... The young man slowly pulls himself up on Snake, grabbing between his legs accidentally. Our hero backpeddles, slightly aghast. Snake: What are you doing? Man: (Looking up) Oh sorry. I'm Byron....I'm..... Snake: Look, whatever you're into, it's fine with me....just keep it to yourself. Byron: No, I meant I.... Snake: It's your business, really. I'll be going now. Don't look at my ass. Snake wanders away, glancing behind him occasionally to make sure he isn't being checked out. Byron: Wait, I have to tell you something. Snake: I know, I know....and it's your choice. Open mind, all that stuff. Byron: No! I'm just.... Snake: It's OKAY. Just....leave it be. See you later. Byron: Will you stop for a second? Snake: I would but I feel rather uncomfortable with....your kind. I know that's wrong, but.... Byron: NO! It's about my creation..... Snake: No doubt used for odd gay pleasures. I have no interest. Bye. Byron: (Sighs) Out of the room walks Snake, shuffling quickly. Once he leaves, the scientist looks up and shouts. Byron: I'M NOT GAY!!!!!! Only the young man's echoes answer him, as he slinks to the ground and buries his head in his arms. And shortly thereafter we open onto another chamber, as Snake continues his journey....stealthily, almost not there at all, he hides. Until a knock on the stall causes him to jump. Amy: Snake, what the hell are you doing? Startled, the warrior leaps up. Pulling his pants up with one hand, he grabs his ear with the other. Snake: Nothing....nothing at all. Amy: Why the hell are you in the women's bathroom? Snake: Uh.... Amy: Why are your pants around your ankles? Snake: It's not what you think. Amy: What's that smell? Snake: (Zipping) I think that's you. Do you EVER bathe? Amy: .....alright it probably is me. Let's go. Away runs the red haired soldier, following close behind Solid Snake. That is until she whips out a pistol and clocks him in the forehead with it knocking him to the floor. Snake: (Holding his head) The hell did you do that for? Amy: (Smiles) Snake: Ugh. Groggily the combatant finds his footing, only to be hit in the head with the gun again. Again he hits the ground, looking up in shock. Snake: Why do you keep hitting me!? Amy: (Smiles) Again Snake rises, and catches a swift boot right between the legs. Then another, then another, a series of fast kicks that cause his voice to rise several octaves. Snake: (Falling to his knees) Not....funny.... Voice: What's the matter, you don't like girls? Behind Meryl materializes an odd specter. Adorned in black leather and sporting a gas mask, he waves his pasty arms about in a theatrical manner. Man: I am Kreller Mantis! I know everything about everything. I am in charge! Snake: (Slowly rising) Do you even really run anything anymore? Kreller Mantis: ....shut up. I will read into your booking! With that Mantis waves his arms again, summoning ancient powers....breaking the very bonds of reality itself. Then....he moves quickly to a small laptop computer located on his desk, clicking the mouse. Kreller Mantis: Come on, load you stupid booking board. Snake: ..... Kreller Mantis: Damn....forgot the password. Uh....Snake, you wouldn't happen to remember it would you? Snake: .....uh, no. Kreller Mantis: Dammit! Oh well.....Amy! Stand right where he can see you, and....DAMN but you stink! Amy: (Wobbling to her feet) Rub it in why don't you. Moved against her will, Amy raises her pistol to her skull. Kreller Mantis: Now Snake, watch as she blows her.... Suddenly a large BLAM rings out. A huge hole rests in the female soldier's head, she slumps to the ground....before her an irritated looking Snake stands, his own gun smoking. Kreller Mantis: You killed my hostage. YOU KILLED MY HOSTAGE!!!!! In a furious rage Kreller flies at the legend, but Snake merely sidesteps and sticks his foot out. The psychic sails through the air, hitting his head on the wall and slumping painfully. Snake: Bitch. Solid slowly looks down to the young woman he killed.....then his earpiece blares. Colonel: Snake, is Amy okay? Snake: Uh....yeah. She's just fine. Peachy. Colonel: Let me talk to her. Snake: ....she doesn't want to talk to you. Colonel: What? Snake: Uh....yeah. She's tired. Let her rest, man. Colonel: Snake, I really want to talk to my niece. Snake: .....no. Colonel: What do you mean no....? Snake: ....no. Let's go....uh....Amy. Snake reaches down, yanking the corpse of the soldier up. Throwing her over his shoulder, he makes his way out of the chamber and down a set of stairs. There a noise catches his attention. Snake: .....I heard something. Onward he walks....and again the noise rings forth. Snake: .....I heard....a baaing sound. Shaking his head, the warrior lowers himself through a low overhead....squeezes. After a moment though he realizes he is quite stuck. Snake: Ugh....ugh.... Snake struggles, but it is to no avail. Then, the noise again rings out, louder this time. Snake: ..... Almost as if on cue a pair of eyes ring forth from the black, staring at him. Then another, then another....after an eternity they step forth. Snake: .....sheep? Snake's ear buzzes, the voice of Master Miller echoes in his head. Master: Those are Austrailian rabid homing sheep. Snake: Are they going to eat me? Master: No, don't be silly. They don't eat people. Snake: That's a relief. Master: No, all they do is wander dark caves until they're able to find suitable trapped prey. Then they mate with them while they're helpless. Snake: .....what!? The fuzzy predators close in on the warrior, who alternates trying to struggle and trying to cover his ass. Snake: NO! Ugh, ugh! The sheep move close to Snake, sniffing him in various places. Snake: No, bad sheep! Bad! ARGH! With a mighty struggle Solid wrenches himself out of the overhead, rolling through the terrible horny sheep. He then begins to run, but stops short....reaches behind himself, grabs the dead body of Amy and pulls her through as well with a huge effort. Snake: Damn you, come on! Hauling Amy over his shoulder, Snake sprints out of the cave. Faster, faster he runs until an impact hits his leg and sends him spiraling to the ground. Snake: AGH! AGH! AGH! Clutching his leg in pain, Snake looks on as a figure slowly walks toward him. Feminine, it sasheys in his direction.....he then raises his eyebrows in disgust as he sees it is in fact a man though, decked out in a low cut uniform but somehow sporting a magnificent pair of breasts. Sniper Argyle: It's hard to miss when you're this close. Snake: Who are.....WHAT the hell are you? Sniper Argyle: I am Sniper Argyle....and I never miss what I aim at. Snake: (Holding his shin) I noticed. Sniper Argyle: Well, I was aiming for your head but that's beside the point. Snake: (Wobbling) Uh..... Sniper Argyle: Getting dizzy? The sheep and I will keep you company, do not worry. Snake: No....must....stay....awake.... Groggily, the warrior falls to his face.....faintly, the feeling of motion pulses through his body. The feeling of being dragged.....dragged towards an unknown (and probably very uncomfortable) fate...... To Be Continued
