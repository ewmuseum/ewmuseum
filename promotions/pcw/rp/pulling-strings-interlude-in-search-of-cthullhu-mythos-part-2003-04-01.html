Pulling Strings - Interlude - In Search Of Cthullhu Mythos - Part One; Subsection B

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st, 2003 The scene opens up towards everyone’s favorite reflection spot, no matter whom or what you would be. A person comes here to find his Zen, to be his personal monk and train his mind in order to progress towards a higher level. If you're not knowing what I am talking about, its called a bathroom, and inside of the “House” as it is called, the bathroom is almost like the temple of Buddha. Inside the bathroom, a young man stands up, eying a mirror as he mimicks himself rapping. [He begins pacing up and down, his ears alertly focused upon the lyrics of “Shook Ones” by Mobb Deep as he uses his hand motions in cautiously panda miming the words to the song having his right hand be the microphone raised up to his mouth.] “To all my killers and 100 dollar dealers…” Soon there are knocks on the door of men outside, asking him and wanting him to hurry up and get out of there as he still panda mimes. Voice: Yo, hurry da fuck up! He still doesn’t hear however as his gray wool cap hangs over his head with the headphones steadily blaring. As he shouts from the outside, another man comes from behind the man and yells as well. Voice: Man, stop playin' with yaself! Man In Bathroom: ALRIGHT! HOLD YA FUCKIN' HORSES! The man flushes the toliet, pulling his pants up as he pushes through the door of the stall. Wearing a red jacket and black JNCO pants, he pushes through the door where two black men stand, looking at him suspiciously. Black Man #1: Who the fuck is you? “Spanish Man”: I got a stamp, I'm battlin' here tonight! Black Man #1: No you're not, you're going to have to enter through the front... “Spanish” Man: Dude you just saw me go to the bathroom! I have a stamp on my hand! Suddenly, a second black man scoops the Spanish man up by his legs and hoists him over his shoulder, before carrying out through the back door. As screams and shouts are heard on stage as a large crowd gathers around inside of the former housing slum found on many a politican's watchful eye. The two men emerge from the back door as the Spanish man fights his way down, pulling his hood down as he only walks off back behind a trash can. The man walking behind him has dredlocks hanging down past his forehead and ears as he wears a sweater to make sure he keeps warm on the outside, but heated while inside of the building. As it says on a white sticker name tag on his sweater, his name happens to be Omar with a cracky smile. Omar: Wait, you shitted on yourself again? Spanish Man: Fuck you O, I didn't wanna battle here anyway! Omar: Rome, you got the talent man, I just can't see why you won't battle Papa Glock tonight! Spanish Man: Maybe because if you beat him he puts a glock to ya POPPA maybe? Omar shakes his head in common shame as Rome continues to walk behind the garbage as he peels back a piece of the trash bag located and pulls out another pair of pants and underwear as he drops his pants before putting the other ones on. Omar: Look, you're not facing Papa Glock tonight okay? You're facing...Jazzy Jester G. Rome: You serious? That ain't a gargantuan 600 pound dude lookin' for a chance to whip my ass is he? Omar: No, everybody knows if you toch somebody of your color, babies pop out of nowhere, its natural. Rome: Same for Egyptians? Omar: I would not doubt it. Come on, let us go back in there, shall we? The words ring true inside of Rome as he shakes his head, cracking his neck as he walks back through the back door of the Home as the camera rotates towards a black man and a Egyptian/Hinduish man standing on the stage, the black man holding the mic, basically ripping the vernacular to shreds as he pounds upon the psyche and physical appearence of the Egyptians with brutal punches (disses), and personals (disses based on real life occurrences) The crowd marvels as the man as the Egyptian in a dark black Seattle Mariners baseball cap is unjustly hanging his head in shame as the man tosses the mic back to the man of festivites - Omar. Rome looks on from behind the speakers, chilling in a cool mode as he hears nothing on the stage at the moment. The man in a black pant and wife beater combo stares menacingly at the Egyptian as the crowd roars in approval of him, decimating the competition with vocabulary almost un-matched. Rome (under breath): And he wants me to battle him!? He can't be serious! That guy is like TOTALLY killing him! As Rome continues to stare at the black man on stage, Omar interrupts the raving fans. Omar: Okay, alright! Looks like we got a unanimous winner here! Papa Glock! He keeps his title! He'll be facing a winner of the tournament, next month. The crowd hisses and boos at the jester. Omar: Hey, I can't just let the man murder more MC's, the hungary ones want shit now too! Rome (from far distance): SHUT UP! Omar: Right now, its going to be a battle between Rome and Jazzy Jester G. Rome, step up to the plate man. Come on, let the battler through! Rome steps behind the sound speaker as Jazzy Jester G stands in front of him, smiling a bit before picking up a microphone handed to him by Omar as he speaks into it. He tests the mic out, before Omar steps in with the coin toss. Omar: Alright, since we are cheap and we will not play Paper-Rock-Scissors for this thing, its going to be a coin flip. Jester G, call it in the air. He flips the coin. Jazzy Jester G: Heads! The coin flips in the air a few times, before landing on heads, a perfect guess by the Jester as he cheers to himself, nodding as he elects the opportunity to go first. As Rome settles in, the beat drops for both battlers to begin with as Unbelievable by The Notorious B.I.G is the song of choice. Jazzy G cracks his neck before flowing into the mic, words which are too cheap to be repeated right now as the crowd continues to shout and cheer him own. Rome switches his stance, looking deadly into the eyes of the Emerald monster as he continues to lay into him rhyming. The monster rips off his shirt, exposing a white beater and hulking muscles. Rome can only stare an watch as the Emerald menace laughs ar him before slapping him off the stage, and into the crowd. Jazzy Jester G: RAH! The monster leaps from the stage, still raving and spitting before falling down to the crowd, shivering and just about shaking enough for medical help to arrive. He whispers to them, before kiping back up in traditional form, making it look like the entire shivering and seizure act was mere set up. Rome: That dude is going to KILLLLLLLLL ME! Rome tries to run away as the beat stops, shrinking the monster back down to size. Jazzy Jester G: Oops, did not mean to harm you there buddy... Rome eyes him as if he was crazy. Rome: Uhh, yeah. You just go ahead with that, I think I'll be ripping you right now! Rome leaps into the air, landing on stage performing some kind of Kung Fu leap before he drops back down, falling back onto his back as the crowd taunts him. Crowd Member #1: He beat his own ass up! The Spanish Dude With Attitude (he calls himself that in the mirror) hops back onto the stage, a smile on his face as he begins to talk into the mic, but there seems to be no sound whatsoever coming out of the microphone into the speaker. Rome checks the mic, seeing if there are any problems, but he notes none and looks back at the crowd, saying nothing. Crowd: CHOKE! CHOKE! CHOKE! CHOKE! CHOKE! Rome, dissatisfied with the outcome of the battle, drops the microphone and goes off the stage running. The crowd boos and hisses towards him as he leaves, leaving Jazzy Jester G to be the winner, or something. Omar checks on his friend outside as Rome stands behind the dumpster. Omar: What happened? Rome: I shitted on myself, and the mic wasn't working... Omar: Did you forget to cut it on maybe? Rome thinks about it for a second before... Rome: AWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!
