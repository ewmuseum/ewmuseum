Training...like so many others have before me...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Time and Space.... “That’s it, masster! A couple more and you’ve got it!” The sound of intense breathing is all about us, the off-putting stench of sweat lingers heavily in the air, and the spine tingling sound of muscle flexing and straightening back out seems to gently fade into our ears as we listen carefully. “I cannot go on much longer!” a strained voice murmurs… “But you must, milord! You have to!” About us is a strange room. Plain and bare it is, with only a few grey mats lying plainly upon the ground. The room’s walls are silvery and they shimmer in the artificial light from above. Still the sounds and smells come from somewhere in this place, but where is another matter… “If you are to win this Sunday you must train like never before!” “But why? It’s not as though my opposition poses an immediate threat to my title!” A distant glimmer catches our eye… we close in on it…a belt it is...golden and glorious…the same title that this Sunday a man by the man of Titu Xansiv will have to defend in the ring against John Crichton and Jason Core… “But if you train hard then you can make more of a mockery out of them than you were going to in the first place!” A pause…as though a distant thought is being contemplated… “I did not wish to humiliate then in the first place…” “But you must train nevertheless, milord…” A tiny drop of sweat hits the ground tenderly with a gentle patter. A man, dark in complexion and sweating from head to toe is to be found strenuously in a handstand position up against one of the room’s walls. With a deep exhausted breath he Slowly but surly performs a press up whilst a small, hunchbacked creature looks on eagerly… The Pharaoh and Wimlog… “I’ve had enough of this,” exclaims Pharaoh in agitation…”I’ve been training for hours, Wimlog!” “You need to train more, my Masster!” The hunchbacked freak watches as Pharaoh lowers himself down and stands back up in an upright position…Sweat flows like a river from his well-toned physique…He looks to be in the best shape of his life… “Your training is not complete yet! What are you doing?!” Wimlog seems to be quite demanding on this day…why is not known, but he is…that is for sure… “I’ve been training for six days solid, I need a break longer than an hour…” Wimlog mumbles something in anger… “You will not keep THAT title if that is your attitude towards training!” Mogre points down towards the glimmering belt lying seemingly unnoticed next to Pharaoh’s feet…once again a tiny drop of salty sweat falls from the Egyptian’s body hitting the ground with an imperceptible splash… “You worry too much, Mogre! Why, Jason Core and John Crichton are nothing compared to me…look at this body…” Pharaoh tenses his arm, flexing the bicep muscle under his dark skin to a great height… “There is no chance of me losing my TV title at Supremacy, Wimlog, so stop worrying…” Mogre sighs in concern… “You have one day left to train for your TV title defence at the Pay Per View, Masster…use it wisely…” Unhurriedly the hunched fiend begins to edge away from The Pharaoh and the place where they have been training, supposedly for the past six days… “Where are you going?!” asks Xansiv in confusion… Mogre turns back to his master and looks him up and down whilst shaking his head in pity. “I see that my words will have no effect on your foolish mind, milord,” Wimlog sighs… “So I shall leave it up to you now…train or don’t train…its up to you…” Wimlog turns his back on Titu and continues to make his way out of the room… Only now can we see that it is in fact many, many great feat in length and width. Pharaoh places his hands on his hips and frowns until Mogre is out of his sight. “The fool! How dare he say those things…” Pharaoh stops for a second as if thinking things through. “But perhaps he has a point…” Pharaoh turns to his right and walks a few feet in that direction. Soon a bench press can be made out, black it is in colour and a great amount of weight can be easily read from upon the huge slabs of steel that we find tightly screwed to the ends of the actual bar. Pharaoh gazes down on the equipment and sighs… “After all, I certainly wouldn’t want to lose you…” As he says these words he turns and looks down upon the glinting TV title belt. The Pharaoh lays down across the bench underneath the press and slowly lifts the huge weight up above his chest…He breaths quite plainly at first but after a few reps his breathing increasing in tone. “I will not be beaten by Jason core!” he bellows as if attempting to motivate himself to perform more reps… “I will NOT be beaten by John Crichton!” Now he is shouting… “I will NOT be defeated by either! Come Supremacy I will be on top form, and nobody will stop me!” We begin to pan out and slowly our scene fades to black. Only the sound of Titu breathing heavily and the smell of his sweat lingering about us can be made out now… Fade out…
