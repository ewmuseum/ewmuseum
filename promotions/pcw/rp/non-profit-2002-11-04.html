Non-profit?

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-4-02 Brandi lays on the comfortable looking couch with a glass of ice water in hand and one leg hanging off the couch. She brings her head off the armrest just enough to take a long drink out of a straw. Just as her head hits the soft fabric, there’s a soft knock on the door. Brandi sighs and sets the glass down on the carpet next to the couch. She opens the door to find a man holding a briefcase in one hand and a pamphlet in the other. Brandi- Can I uh..help you? Man- Hello! My name is Mr. Jackson and I’m here this morning to tell you about my organization and the great opportunities it can offer to you. I work for a company that is trying to develop better treatment and quite possibly a cure for diabetes. Currently, we are going door to door in a hope to get more people in the community involved and I’m asking for your help. I’m here today asking you for a donation. Any amount would be appreciated. Brandi stands there, staring at the man. She assumes that this is some kind of prank and starts giggling. Mr. Jackson stands there, not looking amused. He clears his throat, and Brandi’s giggles die down. Mr. Jackson- I assure you that this is a very serious subject. Perhaps you would like a little more information on this disease, which affects millions of people across the nation. I have some pamphlets here if you would like to look them over before making any type of commitment. Brandi- Commitment? Who said that I was going to give you so much as a penny? Mr. Jackson- Well, you haven’t…yet. Although, I am confident that if I can have a few moments of your time I can convince you that this is a very worthwhile cause. Almost everyone in this neighbor has been very helpful, and have proven to be generous in their donations. Brandi- I’m not a very generous person, Mr. Jackson. Mr. Jackson- Perhaps this is the time to become one. You could help us save the life of someone. Brandi- Save someone’s life? What has that person ever done for me? Mr. Jackson looks at the seriousness on Brandi’s face and shakes his head. It’s quite obvious that he’s disappointed and didn’t expect to work this hard to convince someone to donate. Mr. Jackson- Well, the person might have done nothing for you, but the person you end up helping might be someone you know. A loved one maybe. Brandi- Nah, I don’t think so. Mr. Jackson- Alright. If you’re sure that you won’t consider donating anything then I’ll just be on my way and won’t take up anymore of your time. Brandi- Why are you doing this anyway? I mean, wasting all your time going to people’s houses that only donate so you’ll leave them alone. What’s in it for you? The pay must be good or something. Mr. Jackson- Oh, no ma’am. We’re a nonprofit organization. Right now I’m a volunteer, so I don’t get paid a thing. Brandi takes a step backward and looks genuinely shocked. She looks the man over. He looks a little well dressed for a volunteer with the suit he’s wearing. Brandi- So let me get this straight. You go around and risk getting a door slammed in your face just because you want to? Mr. Jackson- You could say that’s correct. Brandi- Well, let’s just suppose that I donate something. What’s in it for me? I mean, are you going to come and support me at PCW shows? Are you going to buy our merchandise and flash it around when you go gallivanting around? Mr. Jackson- Once again, no. I’ve already explained to you that we are a nonprofit organization. That means that we get nothing in return for our time than the knowledge that we are helping someone in need. You would receive the same feeling, and perhaps gratitude from someone for life. Brandi- I don’t think that sounds like much of a deal. Maybe you’re going around asking the wrong questions here. Instead of asking me what I can do for you, maybe you should try asking yourself what you can do for me. Mr. Jackson- Excuse me? Brandi- Yes, that’s right. What can you do for me? I work my ass off for you people constantly, yet what do I receive in return? A thank you? No! Any sort of appreciation? No again. Mr. Jackson- I’m sorry but… Brandi- And now I can’t speak without being interrupted! Mr. Jackson- …I’m sorry. Mr. Jackson grips his briefcase tightly with both hands. The once crisp pamphlet is now a wrinkled mess as he stands there nervously. Brandi takes a step outside, shivering slightly at the feeling of the cold concrete on her feet. The startled man takes a step backwards, barely managing to catch his balance after losing his footing on the step. Brandi- You think that you people can just rule the world, don’t you? You think that you can just coast on by, leaving us to pick up the pieces you leave behind. We do all of the work, and you try and take all of the credit. Do you really think if the money you collect actually does any good, that the person who donated it would be recognized? Of course not! It would be the person that collected the money who would get all the credit. Mr. Jackson- I didn't realize that you'd feel so strongly with donating. I apologize for wasting both of our time. Brandi- Sure you say the words, but do they actually hold any meaning to you? You don't actually care that I have to be on a plane first thing tomorrow morning and that tonight was my first night to relax in weeks. Or that I won't have another opportunity for even more weeks to come. Mr. Jackson- Of, believe me. I do. Brandi- And I apologize as well. Mr. Jackson- You do? Brandi- Of course. Wait right here. Brandi turns around and disappears into the house, leaving the door open only a few inches. The man sets his briefcase down on the step, giving his now white knuckles a welcomed break. He tries to peak through the small crack in the doorway as he waits for Brandi to come back. The door swings back open, and his face is inches away from Brandi’s. Brandi gives him an odd look and Mr. Jackson resumes his position at the bottom of her porch step. Brandi hands a check to Mr. Jackson. He looks shocked and Brandi offers him little more than a shrug. Brandi- I’ve hard Mr. Walden say that you can use stuff like this as a tax write off. Mr. Jackson- Thank you. Brandi- Now I’m sure you have some other place you need to be. Before waiting for a response from the confused man, Brandi goes back into her house once again, this time slamming the door shut behind her.
