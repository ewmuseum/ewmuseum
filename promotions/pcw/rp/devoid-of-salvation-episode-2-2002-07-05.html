Devoid of Salvation: Episode 2

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 5 A wave of static permeated the air, leading Stryker to snatch the radio from his belt. Slade looked on despondently, his own instincts rising within to put his already tensed senses on full alert. “This is Squad Leader, report.” “Private Roeper reporting, sir ….ssss….. activity detected in northwest quadrant …… ssss …. of caverns. Source unsure…..ssss….” Stryker cursed under his breath, prompting Slade to stomp out of the chamber and down the westerly passage. “You’re sure its not our unit?” “No ….ssss…..sir. I checked in with the patrol posted further ….. ssss …. in that quadrant. They haven’t moved …. ssss …. since they arrived in that region of the caverns.” Stryker raised the radio to give his command, but felt his blood run cold as a rumble emanated in the lower level. Dust scattered in a cloud and loose rock crumbled down the semi-sloped walls. His eyes abruptly shot left as Slade came back into view. “What the hell was that?!” cried Slade with an anxious glance toward the easterly tunnel, where the elevator to the lower floor was located. “An earthquake?” “There’s never any seismic activity around here,” sneered the captain. “Coulda fooled me. Then apparently Godzilla just passed beneath us.” “Maintain positions until we find out what’s going on,” barked Stryker into his radio. “Don’t let your guard down for one….” “AAAAAHH!!!” His commands were interrupted by a frenzied burst of automatic fire from the lower level of the cavern. A thunderous crashing erupted from below as frantic cries permeated the air before being overshadowed by a further frenetic torrent of rifle fire. Jesus what the hell is it?! God Almighty!…..” The muffled exclamation passed ever so slightly through the floor and up the elevator passage as the chaos silenced below, Marcus taking several steps back beside the taut Slade. More static over the radio. The whir of the elevator rising. And all hell broke loose….. * * * A minimal ray of light glanced off the shaven head of Bruce, his eyes squarely leveled at the blood-soaked soil at his feet and his knees coated in grime and muck. Around him encircled ravaged flesh and the scarlet stains of spilled life, some of that scarlet fluid flowing to seep into the mud caressing his trembling hands. At the zenith of his lowered vision stood the booted feet of he who looked down upon him. Bruce need not raise his terrified gaze to know the entity’s own sinister glare was upon him. To lift his head was to risk locking eyes with the cosmic devil, and to lock eyes with one so wicked and so malicious was to play chance with his own fragile sense of sanity. “Dammit,” sputtered Bruce as the blood trickled over his fingertips. “You can’t be here. There’s….no way….it’s gotta be my f*ckin’ imagination!!” “Do not flatter yourself, Shanahan,” came the cold reply, permeated by a sense of echo as if simultaneously spoken by an entire clan of the Chaos. “You know as well as I do that my presence, and that of even my masters, is as real as the sun’s rays on your back as you walk the outer world. We are ancient, and We are eternal. And we are omnipresent. In these aloof caverns there is no Elder magic to imprison us. We wander as We please, and have done so in this ancient tunnels since the Earth was a veritable child in this universe. A universe created not by this ‘glorious’ Jehovah God, as your petty and infantile race refers to him, but by the will of the Creator Of All Things himself. That is the truth, and though your feeble heart presses you to believe otherwise, you know it to be so.” “Why….me….?” Shanahan weakly responded. “Why must you continue to…..follow me….?” The deity’s feet shuffled and left view, the Boston ruffian cautiously raising his eyes just enough to follow the path of his primeval antagonist. He caught view of the customary Egyptian-style headdress that typically sat perched on the head of the Chaos in this form and quickly lowered his gaze once again. “In the past I told you that you were a random plaything chosen to aid in Our passing the aeons in deathless slumber. But that is not entirely true, Shanahan. We have seen many an instigator decipher our secrets, then attempt to rise and seek the aid of the Elders to crush Us once and for all. And of those insolent troublemakers, a few stand out as particular thorns in the side of my peers and various Lords. The blood of one of those impudent mongrels flows through your veins, child. And the vengeance We could never reap on his accursed soul…..We intend to collect from you and all whom you hold so dearly.” “I hold no one dearly but myself!” insists Shanahan as the threat is breathed. “I love nobody! And I haven’t since I became a professional wrestler!” At that enraged statement the muscle-tinged divinity chuckled in jest. Shanahan watched only as the blood coursed through the thin muck of the chamber, its interior illuminated by some ethereal light source from which he could find no origin even by rapidly lifting his head to scan the room. He heard the clicking of the Chaos’ ancient footwear and purses his eyes together as they halted before him before giving way to an ominous snicker, again pervaded by that echo of a thousand voices. “Untrue yet again. Not only is your memory quite short-term, but apparently rather selective as well. Only weeks ago someone returned to your life. His appearance was brought about by a great rival, and upon the disappearance of that rival so was that of this lost brethren you once knew so well.” Shanahan’s eyes lit up, both saturated with the knowledge of whom the deity spoke and flooded with stark terror at the realization. His reason overcome by emotion, Bruce glared menacingly at that which stood before him. “Damn you,” he snarled through gritted teeth. “If you so much as look at Derek the wrong way, I swear to God I’ll…..” The eyes of the Chaos swung to meet the irate glower of Shanahan. Even under the cloud of hatred swirling through Bruce’s eyes and mind, the mere gaze of the incarnate blasphemy was enough to diminish his fury and return him to the brink of cowardice. “No, please finish your statement, mortal. What pain will you, a flash-in-the-plan even by Earthly standards, wreak upon one of my everlasting existence? Will you ‘brutalize’ me? Convince that weak-minded fool Akiyama to ‘banish’ me? Talk your pal Xavier Kannon into boring me to my final demise with his talk of that corporeal ‘Scientology’ rubbish? Please…..salvage your dignity and save yourself the shame of idle threats one of your stature in this universe could never carry out on one of my divine power. But no, Bruce, he is like you. He is of your bloodline, and as such carries in his tainted veins the infamy of attempting to destroy Us. You have collected sufficient protection to safeguard yourself from the full depths of Our wrath, yet he is naked to our bidding.” Nyarlathotep’s words again brought Shanahan to the brink of rage. His right hand was gnarled into a quaking fist and his breathing was becoming erratic. “Derek didn’t do anything, you son of a bitch!” he exclaimed. “I don’t know what or who you’re talking about!” “You shall bear the cross on behalf of your predecessor!” hissed the demon from beyond the stars. “His crimes are yours, and his sentence shall be carried out on you and your precious sibling!” Shanahan was helpless, and that realization was what held him to the ground. He held no item or attack which could so much as cause the Crawling Chaos to flinch, and in light of this was at the mercy of the most foul and spiteful of the Old Ones. There was no way out, no salvation. His only liberation was death. * * * Slade and Stryker raced through the slender tunnel, death shrieks reverberating in their wake between being drowned out by the intermittent hisses of whatever followed them. A broad shadow passed the wall at the rear of the tunnel from whence they came, the chamber inundated with a deathly silence as whatever it was pursued the horrified duo. “Damn that f*cking GeoCorp!” shouted Slade as they dashed around a corner. “Leave it to those ingrates to disturb a den of those things!” Stryker didn’t reply, only concentrating on the path ahead. His radio continually buzzed with cries from all corners of the caverns and the proximate sound of gunfire rarely ceased for more than a brief moment. He spun his head momentarily to watch as the tentacle-headed beast burrowed back into the rock to give chase. The ground vibrated with a perpetual trembling as a chant-like hum pervaded the ozone, the infamous monosyllable mantra that always accompanied the burrowing of the foul Cthonian race beneath the Earth crust. * * * Vanessa shook Bruce hysterically as he kneeled on the basaltic floor, his eyes still glazed over in the same distant trance and that vile chant continuing to flow forth from his lips. Ph’nglui mglw’nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn….” “Dammit, Bruce, wake up!” she pleaded over the gunfire emanating from the westerly passage. “What’s wrong?!” She lowered her head and grasped her weapon as the death hymn grew closer from below, pulling herself upright and staring forward as the rock beneath her feet exploded in a shower of debris…… * * * (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan) What will be my legacy? When I die, how will I be remembered? Bruce Shanahan…..the white trash street thug, bitter and hard as nails with a yearning to watch the world burn to the ground? Or maybe kinder, gentler and more ‘nurturing’ than my path leads to me right now? Maybe I’ll simply be known as Violence Jack…..my in-ring alias. And even then, will that legacy be that of a successful pioneer in this sport, or as an independent sensation that failed when opportunity finally knocked in a prestigious organization such as Primetime Championship Wrestling? The matter has been weighing heavily on my thoughts as of late in light of the mounting threats on my life. I’ve seen too many comrades fall lately to not believe my time is also close at hand. Somewhere, in some trade, I must make my mark. Whether it be an ‘underground’ society, such as the Blackhelm Coalition fraternity, or the masses in a forum such as PCW. I’ve done nearly all I can for the Coalition thus far, and presently feel I’m lucky to retain both my life and my sanity. Though I genuinely fear that it is merely by edict of the Old Ones that I still lay claim to both entities. I have had far too many encounters with that hideous abomination Nyarlathotep to trust that my survival and my elusion of madness aren’t favors; the Crawling Chaos and his brethren have turned over the hourglass many times by now and surely I’m living on borrowed time for their sheer amusement. So why have I been granted that time? For what purpose? Maybe it’s some obscene destiny they’ve unearthed in the stars. Or hell, possibly it’s nothing more than giving them some recreation as they sit in a state of incarcerated torpor across the world. Ah well…..either way I think it’s unfortunate for Flaymin’ Aymin. As hard as it is to focus on the task at hand, tonight I begin my long trek back from the gutter to redemption. Already I was forced into laying down so that putrid pretty boy Kramer Wild Wing could ‘get over’ (twice in a week actually) and I’ll be f*cking damned if it happens again. Obviously the incompetent PCW brass would rather put the ‘Extreme’ title on a pampered suburban chump than deal with the controversy that would arise from having a vendetta driven . As far as I’m concerned they may as well stick a new label on that championship….maybe ‘Rising Star 2’ would fit snugly, it fits a hell of a lot better than ‘Extreme’. Then again I suppose they wouldn’t be able to book all those comical little stipulation matches, eh? If they want comical I can oblige as well as the next guy. Maybe I’ll start by publicly castrating Aymin so his adult film career is truly ended once and for all, that oughta draw a few laughs. Maybe we’ll continue in a few weeks by pissing all over The Chef’s Koran and follow that up by burning down Kannon’s gorgeous little Hubbardville or even gathering some of the crew from the bar and having some fun with that little bitch he drags around with him. Sky is the limit of course, but I don’t foresee any happening soon. God forbid some cynical low-life from inner Boston outfoxes their precious headlining crew. Sort of makes me wish I was still being shredded by barbed-wire and glass back in Osaka. At least in Japan if they want to f*ck you up they do it directly in the wrestling arena. In America they call in a few favors and do it in the political arena. I guess it all goes with the territory of being the bastard son of Boston. Resentment is contagious…..
