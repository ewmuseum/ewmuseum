Date: PAY FRICKIN ATTENTION So far so good… They were on the plane…soaring over the earth on their way to Tokyo. The mission was going perfectly. Of course…Matrix had to take a latex stabbing for the team…but you gotta make sacrifices for the sake of the mission. Fusion the god had to team up with some low budget Americana reject against Harry Pothead and Ramses McGee. If this chimp comes walking out to “AMER…..ICANNN…..DREAAAMMM” ala Dusty Rhodes with the Extreme title painted red white and blue, the nVg will kick all their asses until they’re black, blue and red and call it a night. He has better things to prepare for… Red October being more important that teaming up with “The Beave” and hoping that we “gosh golly win it for the hometeam”. Spare the Celestial Champion your nostalgic days of waste. Cyclonica looked up as Matrix came walking down the aisle, a scowl on his face as he stopped before the Divine Prophetess. She smiled at him as he glared down at her. “My phone please,” he held out his hand to her. She placed the black cell phone in his hand and watched as he slid row across the aisle and slowly sat down, a painful exhale hissing out of him as he was fully seated. Cyclonica closed the magazine in her hands and turned towards the former Executive, her chin resting in her hands. “So…how was--.” “Shut up.” He cut her off before she could ask. She grinned at him wickedly as he folded his arms and scowled harder than before. “At least a woman did it, right?” Cyclonica asked him. Matrix nodded, but still kept his scowl. “I would rather have gotten a blow job…not a Snow Job.” “Snow Job?” Matrix nodded. “You know…Snow Job…derived from Snow’s time in jail, an act of anal penetration used to pass time late at night…it’s when you take your fist and--.” “That’s…more than enough.” Cyclonica’s face looked disgusted. Meanwhile, Sonata had her face pressed into the window as she stared at the clouds outside. “Wow!” she said with sincere wonder. “Every time I fly I just cant believe the beauty of the sky! Akiyama, take a look!” When she received no response, she turned back to find the “brown haired” god sitting there with his eyes closed and his arms folded across his lap. Sonata poked him in his ribs, which got no response out of him. Cyclonica turned towards them and waved her away. “Stop bothering the god. Cant you see he’s busy?” Sonata looked up at the sill Akiyama before looking back to Cyclonica. “Busy doing what?” “…sleeping.” That was the first thing that came out of her mouth. Sonata nodded slowly, understanding as she turned back to the window. The Divine Prophetess shook her head as she looked over to the nVg. “I swear if she jeopardizes this mission, I’m going to splatter her brains from the inside.” The god made no movement, causing Cyclonica to sigh as she turned back to the magazine in her hands. She opened to a spread about Red October, listing the matches for the night. As she flipped the pages, she stopped upon a page discussing the PCW Cruiserweight title match… …Anarchy versus Cyclonica… Lot of people were predicting Anarchy to take this one. What were their assumptions based on? Because she was a wrestler and Cyclonica was a manager? Cyclonica has had three matches in PCW…three fights in her life (not counting the huge riot a couple of months ago where she killed a number of people in one night), and in each of those fights she completely wailed on the person across from her. William S Powell…Bertha…Teresa Tomas and Cecilia Verucana….all of them beaten like the mortal dogs they are. Now she had Anarchy… Cyclonica stared down at the photo shot of the two of them. Anarchy was shown with the PCW Cruiserweight title across her shoulder, and her arms folded under her chest as she wore her black and blue attire. Across on the other page was Cyclonica, flashing the Celestial Sign at her exposed navel, also decked in matching blue and black attire. Across the red pages was the PCW Cruiserweight title, looking to be the link between the two black and blue haired goddesses. Cyclonica read through the article, scanning over the history of Anarchy and Cyclonica, starting from the first time she helped her against the incoming women looking to attack. Ever since then it’s always been about showing her the truth…whatever that may be. And ever since then…Anarchy cannot turn around without running into the Divine Prophetess with some ominous message of “the truth”. It was Anarchy who saved Cyclonica from the raging Akiyama after he lost the #1 contender’s spot during the 24/7 period, which followed by the allegedly unplanned ambush from Asylum, allowing Miyagawa to take the #1 spot. Then the story leads to the hit and run of the Neo Void God, that was assumed to be done by Anarchy by the god and the Divine Prophetess. That led to the infamous draining of Anarchy in that freezer, all of her red blood flowing from her body and that mystical black blood seeping out afterwards. From then the two of them have been wary of each other, but not hostile. Anarchy continues on with her matches, and Cyclonica watches from the shadows while committing dark, yet somehow erotic acts of violence. Their sexuality has been questioned many times by the press, but neither one of them chooses to voice their preference. Now…in Tokyo…the two of them would finally collide. The “TRUTH” as Cyclonica so called it…would come out in the open as the two of them would go toe to toe for the PCW Cruiserweight championship. But did the belt really matter at this point? Anarchy wanted the truth…and Cyclonica wanted to give it to her. But Anarchy was going to have to wait… They had to wait after their date before the fun would begin at the end of the night. There were smaller photos showing Anarchy and Cyclonica standing across from each other, both crouching and ready to strike. Showing various parking lot scenes with Cyclonica kneeling over the run over Akiyama, and Asylum peering suspiciously at Anarchy after their limousine was destroyed by the Celestials, the final two shots were of Cyclonica standing before the blood-soaked Anarchy, her hands pressed into her cheeks, and the final was of Anarchy and Cyclonica standing in the ring, standing on the same side, glaring across at their enemies in that tag match not too long ago. Cyclonica’s eyes were locked on Anarchy…she couldn’t help but lick her lips as she stared at the woman’s beautiful face. Then…something broke her concentration. “I’m bored!” Cyclonica glared over to Sonata, who sat with her arms folded over her chest and pouting. “When’s the in-flight movie coming on?” Cyclonica reached for her, but stopped herself, clenching her hand tightly. “How about you shut up? I was in the middle of strategizing for my match!” Sonata looked over and saw the picture of Anarchy and Cyclonica in the magazine. “Why’s there two pictures of you in that magazine?” “There isn’t! That’s a different person!” Cyclonica held the magazine up to show the mortal. Sonata looked harder at the photo and nodded with a smile. “Ah! You’re right! She looks just like you though! You could be twins! Or sisters at least!” The Divine Prophetess scoffed at her. “Nonsense.” “I think you like her!” Sonata blurted out. Cyclonica glared at her quickly, closing the magazine in her hands. “Hold thy tongue!” she hissed angrily at her. Sonata grinned as she took the magazine from her. “Yes you DO! I saw how you were licking your lips at her! You have a crush on her!” she raised her hands in the air and rocked back and forth teasingly. “Yooou like…” she stopped…realizing she didn’t know the other woman’s name. “What’s her name?” she whispered to Cyclonica embarrassed. “F*ck off, flat-scan.” Before Sonata could carry on any more, Cyclonica turned to Matrix, who was sitting there with his eyes closed. “Can I switch seats with you?” “Would you??” he asked desperately. Cyclonica eyed him strangely before snatching her magazine back from Sonata and exchanging seats with Matrix. She closed her eyes and exhaled deeply…relaxation finally sinking in. Being tense wouldn’t help her going in against Anarchy. … “Excuse me?” Cyclonica cut her eyes open narrowly as she peered over to her right. “Excuse me…Ex-Excuse me?” Cyclonica opened her eyes. “What?” Sitting next to her was a middle-aged couple; a Caucasian man with a full beard and slicked back brown hair in a blue cardigan sweater, and a middle aged woman with long dirty blonde hair and a yellow blouse. They both sat with their arm around each other, their other arms invisible under the black blanket draped about their midsections. “We were wondering…” the woman began, an educated tone spilling from her voice like intelligence overflowing from a fountain of knowledge. “Is this your first time flying Japan Air Airlines?” Cyclonica stared at them for a second, not sure if she wanted to answer them or not. “Yes…” she finally said after a minute of glaring. “Yes it is.” The man nodded with a little smile, barely visible past his thick beard. “And are you here without a lover?” That got the Divine Prophetess’s attention. “What??” what kind of question was that anyway? “We frequent Japan Air for our flying needs to…Japan.” He replied, turning back to the woman and gliding a finger across her cheek. “We find it satisfying for a quick…lover’s getaway.” Cyclonica gave them a quick nod before lying back in her seat. Before her eyes could close all the way though… “We are Professors Kenneth and Amanda Fountain.” Two different hands reached out towards her, and hovered in the air waiting for the handshake from Cyclonica. She opened her eyes again and shook their hands quickly before lying back. “…Ai. My name is Ai.” It was a lie…but at this point who cared? All the guys Akiyama were fighting were lying to themselves and to the public about winning…why couldn’t she lie about her name? “Ah…yes…” Kenneth reflected, him and his wife retracting their hands. “Ai, may I ask you something?” Cyclonica exhaled annoyed as she looked back up at them. “What now? I’m trying to rest for a match that I’ve--.” “I find that when one embraces the cramped quarters of the air-PLANE,” he said that word, accenting the last portion instead of AIRplane. “It is quite like you first encounter with a new lover.” Amanda nodded. “I remember the first time Kenneth and I made love.” Her “lover” nodded. “Yes.” She squeezed his arm and smiled. “It was after a long Sunday drive, that we pulled over into a beautiful meadow. Kenneth had laid me down on a fresh patch of green meadow grass gently. Then he proceeded to rub my nubile body with orange peels and…Oxy pads.” Kenneth reached for her cheek again, softly rubbing it. “Then he glazed my back with vegetable oil and…actual ham glaze.” She smiled and sighed, thinking back to that memory. “Until the ants and flies came, me thinks it was the finest lovemaking the world has ever known.” Kenneth began to kiss her fingers and humming to himself. Then he turned to Cyclonica, who had returned to her reclined position during the story. “Ai…are you listening?” Cyclonica glared up at him. “Yeah…I’m just trying to rest before I get to Japan…” “AH…” the two lovers exclaimed. Just then, the stewardess came walking by. She stopped when she recognized the two middle aged people, placing her hands on her hips and smiling. “Is that the professors…Kenneth, and Amanda Fountain?” she asked, her voice almost as educated as theirs. Now…what was a stewardess doing with an educated tone like she graduated from Harvard? Amanda and Kenneth gave a gasp of delight upon seeing the young Japanese woman. “Ah yes!” Amanda exclaimed, holding her hand up to present her to Cyclonica. “It is our good friend, NaoMI Tenjou!” She said it once again with a strange tone, highlighting the lower part of Naomi instead of saying NAOmi. “What brings you to frequent Japan Air?” Kenneth asked, unable to see the obvious reasons behind her stewardess outfit and pin. Naomi tightened her blue scarf and smiled. “The usual…the excellent in-flight independent films, the five star meals… the paycheck I get every first and fifteenth of the month.” Amanda nodded. “Naomi, are you still with your lover, Kohei?” “As you know,” Naomi replied, tugging on her tight blue skirt next. For some reason Cyclonica caught attention to the tightness of her skirt. “Kohei was indeed a skilled and creative lover…his love for me was exceeded by his greater love for wine…and whores. But now I can fall back onto my one true love…flight attending.” “Ai?” Kenneth turned to Cyclonica, preparing to speak with her. Cyclonica was leaned over towards the aisle with her back towards him, pretending she was asleep. Maybe he’ll leave you alone if he thinks you’re sleep… “Ai…” Some people weren’t that smart…no matter how they sounded. “Ai…” Some people weren’t smart…no matter how much they talked. “Ai…” Some people were just too Neo Void God-Damn dumb to know how to do anything else besides talk…ain’t that right Duke? “What the bloody hell do you want?” Cyclonica sat back up and shot evil from her eyes in his direction. Kenneth didn’t see the obvious in front of him…just like the Celestial’s opponents didn’t see the obvious beating coming their way. “NaoMI Tenjou is Japan Air’s top flight attendant.” Cyclonica nodded, her eyes half closed and weary. “That’s just Divine…” Just then, Amanda leaned over with a plate of steaming meat in her hand. Cyclonica leaned away quickly as she glared at the food. “What in the Void is that??” Amanda pulled off a piece, some of the meat hanging from it, and set the plate aside on her lap. “At this point of the flight, my lover and I frequently enjoy spiced lamb shanks.” The two of them gnawed at the gray slab, their mouths moaning in anticipation and deliciousness of the meats. Amanda returned the meat to her plate before looking back at Cyclonica. “We find that Japan Air is the only airline that will prepare the shanks for us pre-flight.” “NaoMI” nodded her head. “Japan Air is known for their…SHANKS!” “Wish I had a shank right now…” the Divine Prophetess muttered, turning away from the couple. “I’d stab the frickin lot of you in your faces until your noses ran with blood.” “What was that Ai?” Kenneth asked her loudly. Cyclonica glared back at him and shook her head. “I said I wish I had a sha—never mind.” She knew where that would lead…and she didn’t want some pasty piece of meat thrown at her. Anarchy was the only thing she wanted thrown at her…and she wanted her bloody and bound. Or did she…? “I should mention that although our dispositions above are calm…” Kenneth began slowly. “…Below the surface there is a frenzy of activity.” Cyclonica jumped away from them, squeezing herself as far away from the couple as she could in that chair. Kenneth sighed in bliss as Amanda patted the black blanket over their laps. “Hands groping…fingers twittering…hips buckling at the anticipation of lovemaking…that will take place in the bathroom in approximately…nine minutes.” Cyclonica couldn’t take any more. “I…have to go. SOMEWHERE…” Kenneth and Amanda stopped her before she could stand up. “Wait!” Amanda shouted, drawing strange and annoyed looks from the other passengers. “NaoMi, you are sans lover…and Cyclonica, you are a weary passenger. Maybe Cupid’s arrow has come to strike the two of YOU!!” “WHAT??” Cyclonica glared across the aisle, finding Matrix chuckling to himself. She reached up to psycho-choke him, but Naomi took her hand in hers. “Ai…don’t be alarmed by the professors.” She began tenderly stroking the back of Cyclonica’s hand. I remember my first time when I had met Kenneth and Amanda on their first flight to Tokyo. I was young, and after we arrived we spent the night in Shinjuku. After eating our fill of Okonomiyaki, we found ourselves sitting quietly on a bench in the park.” Kenneth looked off, as if he were looking in the distance. “Somewhere in the distance we heard the beating of classical Japanese drums….Was it in our minds? We don’t know…” Naomi nodded. “Then the fox spirit himself appeared before us and beckoned for us to make love. We gave into our ravenous desires…as the three of us became one with the great Fox Spirit.” “Turns out…” Amanda added. “That the great Fox Spirit was really an escaped mental patient by the name of Koji Hideo.” Cyclonica sighed, sitting up in her seat. “I don’t know if I’m tired from this flight…or from hearing you flat-scans ramble on like Duke Williams on a keg frenzy…but that story was indeed worth hearing.” Naomi smiled deeply at Cyclonica. “LOVER…would you care to walk with me to the bathrooms, where you can see my tattoo of Japanese pop singer Utada Hikaru?” “Hikaru Utada? Hot damn!” Cyclonica jumped out of her seat and ran for the bathroom. Kenneth and Amanda smiled as Naomi followed after her. They embraced each other, looking into each other’s eyes. “Seeing the two of them together has brought great joy to my heart.” Amanda whispered to her lover. Kenneth nodded. “I say we reward ourselves with a frenzy of lovemaking in one of the open bathrooms.” The two of the stood up and walked towards the bathroom, passing past Sonata as she made her way back to her seat. She pushed past Akiyama and Matrix and sat back down next to the window. She leaned past Akiyama’s lap to talk to Matrix. “Some guy just asked me to join the Mile High Club.” She said. “I asked him if he had an application, and he said meet him in the bathroom and he’d help me fill it out. What a nice guy.” She placed her yellow purse back down on the floor. “Do you have a pen?” Matrix pulled out a black pen and held it towards her. Before she could take it, Akiyama grabbed the pen and stood up, stepping out from his seat and walking towards the bathroom. Sonata smiled as she watched him disappear into the bathroom. “How sweet of him! He’s going to fill it out for me!” Matrix shook his head. Meanwhile…a man wearing combat gear walked past them towards the back of the place. He stroked his black beard before reaching to his waist and pulling out a .45. “AKIRA AKIYAMA AND CYCLONICA!” He exclaimed authoritatively. “THIS IS LIEUTENANT USHIO KUONJI, OF THE JAPANESE NATIONAL DEFENSE FORCE! COME OUT OF THE BATHROOM WITH YOUR HANDS IN THE AIR!” Two doors adjacent from each other swing open, with Akiyama and Cyclonica poking their heads out into the hallway. From Cyclonica’s bathroom, an unconscious Naomi Tenjou fell out, her eyes rolled into the back of her head. From Akiyama’s bathroom, a suave Japanese man in a white t-shirt and jeans falls out, his white shirt stained with red as a pen stuck out from his shoulder. Cyclonica held her hand to her head and purred. “Mmmm…. best thing about mind-f*cking…everyone’s a virgin.” Kuonji pointed his gun at the god and the Divine Prophetess. “PLACE YOUR HANDS WHERE I CAN SEE THEM!” Just then, another bathroom door flew open. Kenneth Fountain fell back into Ushio Kuonji, knocking him into the wall and to the ground. Amanda looked out from the bathroom with a concerned look. “All ya all right?” she asked, her educated Harvard tone gone from her, and a New Jersey accent replacing it. “MAH BACK!” Kenneth said in a matching accent. “Is it ya back?” she asked, either stupid, or reassuring herself. Kenneth glared up at her with anger in his eyes. “I said it’s mah damn back!!” he growled. Ushio pushed Kenneth off of him and jumped back to his feet. He pointed his gun and scanned the perimeter before finding “Ai and Masato Kibagame” standing at the front of the place…next to the exit. Cyclonica waved as she grabbed the emergency exit handle. “You…as well as the rest of those mortals…cannot stop us.” “Attention passengers!” the pilot said over the intercom. “Please fasten your seatbelts! We are now flying above Tokyo and will be preparing to land in five minutes. Put your tray tables in an upright position and turn off all laptops and cell phones.” Cyclonica’s dark smile crept upon her face…her brown eyes growing a cold blue…as if the color was slowly seeping back into her irises. “This is our stop.” The door flew open as she wrapped her arms around Akiyama, his brown hair fluttering in the suction of the cabin pressure dropping. The oxygen masks dropped down from above as everyone screamed in panic. Ushio began to run towards them, but froze as the Neo Void God held up his hand, beckoning him to stop. A silver barcode acted as a stop sign, freezing the officer in his tracks, a look of fear in his eyes. Akiyama held onto the Divine Prophetess tightly as he leaped out of the plane. Ushio glared at the empty doorway as Sonata and Matrix looked up at where Akiyama and Cyclonica stood. Sonata pouted, her feelings hurt. “…They left us.”
