Pulling Strings, The Tenth Part Of

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02. 13. 03 “Okay people, eyes and ears attentive!” the shrill, yet subtle voice of Jason Cunningham ran through a press conference gymnasium like a whirlwind tornado as members of the press, and silhouettes of waving pens, tipped fur browed hats and murmurs settled. Behind Cunningham’s short, yet stocky body was a banner like tapestry set above a black background. White walls tinted down various colors and velvet rope holding back anybody who seemed to not be upon the stage at the current moment. “What’s up your sleeve Jason?” a reporter from the Boston Globe struck up conversation as the pres relations guru laughed. “Oh nothing Red,” he pushed through a clenched face. “Just you wait.” He tapped the microphone gently a few times, testing out the speaker chords to see if they were hooked up or not. “Test…test...1...2…” he repeated before a reporter shouted out in a joking manner, “3!” before the caucus rose in attention, smiling. “Oh c’mon now, you guys are trying to be funny and I still haven’t finished!” Cunningham comment with a slight fixture of his tie before beginning his announcements. “Now, you members of the press are welcome to show up at the junket this upcoming Saturday, but now you just need to listen.” “To what,” Red sparked up once again, as the flaming red head codger was speaking guttery with a cigar wrapped in between his parched lips. “This better not be another Pluto Nash!” “Its not, its not I swear to you! Pluto Nash wasn’t my fault or its dumb script, just the script.” Cunningham reminded Red, hoping that his second attempt at comedy wouldn’t horribly fail like his first. “We’re here for Black Knight 2, set to star none other than…” he paused for a second, letting the reporters sitting down with pads in hand waiting and wondering who would be in the title role. “DANTE JACKSON!” A sudden gasp rips through the small auditorium before Red barks out, “Are you kidding me?! Dante Jackson? The man is merely a wrestler, a rapper, a THUG!” “Now wait Red,” Paul Gamete from the Los Angeles Times stopped the old reporter in mid step. “We’ve benefited greatly from black actors – see Tupac Shakur, or Jay Z, or Eminem!” “He’s right,” Jason interrupted the preceding, “but isn’t Eminem a common white guy?” Red nodded, “Exactly! White guys are not thugs!” “But he sounds like one!” Paul tried to cut back in the position that black rappers could be credible actors, but the truth of which couldn’t be said for everybody. “Even VH1 had a movie about them.” “They died like that Six Pac and Shorty Smalls anyway!” “Tupac and Biggie Smalls, they were Tupac and Biggie Smalls!” Paul re-iterated that Red, despite his knowledge and customary response on every single things his eyes caught, was just like Bush in the 92’ Presidential Elections, he strayed away from the happening. A new question popped up, “How in the world would Jackson even be there? Sources say that he has a match against Veneman Pendragon in PCW’s next show tomorrow night!” “In Idaho!” another reporter inserted. “There’s no way in the world that with a ten o’clock flight the next morning that he would be rested in time, there is possibly no way!” Jason stood there, listening to every single man and woman’s questioning, as if they were lines of bullets aimed directly at his head. Nobody could quietly tell him nor the rest of the people for that matter that he was completely misunderstood by Jackson being there. Jackson would be there, by hook or by death from a cop beating if Cunningham wanted it. And everybody would get what they want, one picture at a time. * * * “Shake it mommy!” the cat calls of Racks lights up the booth inside of a California strip club as the crew after so much time apart from one another have found it willingly to make their way towards the club, looking at butts and breast in no other way possible. “Smack it in my face!” AS Racks sat in a crop hair style and a pair of black Timberland boots, Kenny sat back, receiving some serious stock tips from a Los Angeles broker. His eyes were attentive, and his facial expressions showed he knew just what he had gotten himself into, but in order words – “WOW!” He was clearly lost. “Now look what you’ve done!” Will was heard shouting from the opposite side of the room, looking down at the small stain on his pants. “I didn’t want to do that!” “Excuse me!” the stripper in front of him sharply belittles him as Buddha sits right next to him, enjoying a smoke as always. But unlike the rest of the men inside of the room, Dante Jackson completely had his mind on other maters, not even the movie at this point which would begin filming as soon as March Madness weekend had concluded. His eyes met the room, sharply, slowly, before taking them all back in release, understanding that not a single person could help him. A crazed fear inside of him leapt up into his throat, prohibiting him to speak, still happy and excited about even going to March Madness in the first place. Jeez, there sure are a lot of horny black people in the world. He comments in his head. Including Racks, boy have I not seen him in such a long time. Has it been almost a year since I broke into the business, seeing not only these people go, my record contract being signed as well as two CD’s go #1 and stay there for a long time? Now I know what it feels like to be hunted by white men, my ancestors weren’t lying after all!
