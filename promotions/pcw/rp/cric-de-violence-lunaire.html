Date: Today That ingrate. He has the nerve to think that he's better than me, despite everything that I've done for him. When the bell rings and he steps in that ring, he thinks that he's going to beat me. That little punk. January 28th, Violence Jack makes his return to a PCW ring after I retired Rush Mason two nights before. That night, I faced Darkstar in the main event of Tuesday Turmoil. I'd be lying if I said that Darkstar put up a good fight. After I felled Darkstar, Jack makes his presence felt by hitting me on the back of the head. Not much, he just showed up. From there on, it was a constant nuisance. Jack did what he could to make himself significant to me. Attacks, promos, run-ins... he wouldn't give up. For whatever reason, I was the one that he needed to use to get noticed. He needed my attention. He carved a symbol in my chest, burned my houes down, killed the woman I loved... he did a number on me, he did. But that changed because he got my attention. He got me angry. He had 48 followers in his little cult. Every single one of them were felled at my hand. Crippled, killed, all of them. Shanahan still has a Brother Asenath as he's tried to rebuild his cult, but the righthand is always named Asenath. That's what the name means. At March Madness, Jack attempted to beat me himself, something he'd not been able to do the entire time that he and I were engaged in our feud. All of his best, strongest attacks were useless against me. I got right up, kickouts at one-count. I let him tire himself out attempting to harm me, before giving him the most brutal beating he's ever received. My flurry of offense didn't last more than 2 minutes, but I know it was Hell for him. Beaten, bludgeoned, pummeled... his ribs, nose, neck bruised. I cut and stabbed him. He's still digesting my eyes, shoved down his throat. All this gracious service I've paid to Violence Jack. All this attention he's gotten from me. I've made him more famous than he could ever have been on his own. The Earth is orbited by the moon. It makes a complete axial rotation every 27.3 days, and makes a revolution around the Earth every 27.3 days. Hence, the same side of the moon is always facing the Earth. The side that we see, the light side, is visible because the surface of the moon is illuminated by the sun. That surface, for all its craters and crevices, is merely a reflection of the sun's light. Without the sun's light, the moon wouldn't be known to the peoples of the Earth. Sure, the more observant would notice a spacial phenominon in which an area devoid of light and substance was covering up the bright lights behind it; there really couldn't be any definitive proof, however, of what would actually be in that spot where nothing was. This explanation of the heavenly body serves as a metaphor for my relations to dear Bruce Shanahan. You see, I am the sun to his moon. Here in PCW (which is the earth, metaphorically), I am the one that everyone looks up to, the one that everyone sees whether or not they're paying attention. I bring light to PCW. Without me, it would be dark, depressing, cold... Bruce Shanahan is unknown, not only because of his inherent lack of heat, but because he's taken the pseudonym of Violence Jack. He has a darkside that he never shows anyway, but even his light side is dependent on me. Without myself giving him constant light, without my attention and my guiding him through PCW, he is nothing. It's a bit of a stretch not to call him nothing now, but that's the affect that my grace lends to people. Rush Mason understood that, and tried twice to get noticed through me. I'm getting sick of Violence Jack now. It's only a matter of time before I plunge him back into eternal darkness. Back into the obscurity from which he came. PCW will have nothing to look to during their darkest hours, but then again that's not really time that they're paying attention to anything anyway. Violence Jack is his most visible only when everything else is resting, completely inactive. PCW fans aren't bored or on sabbatical, therefore Jack will never get the kind of appreciation time that he wants. It happens all the time. PCW is the Earth, and I am the Sun. Everything orbits around me. I didn't really ask for this, it just came to me. Being the focal point of PCW is an awesome responsibility. I make everything brighter with my very presence. With me gone, everything will cease. Looking at it that way, there's no choice but for my star to keep burning brightly, high above all the others. I've spent too much to giving light to Violence Jack. Now it's time to redirect it back to PCW itself. So far, Jack, the moon has been coming around the Earth, depending on the sun's light to keep it in everyone's view. But your time is drawing to a close, Jack. Soon the Earth is going to come between the moon and the sun, and the moon will be forever in lunar eclipsed as the Earth's shadow will be cast upon it, never the moon to appear again. PCW is going to take priority over you, Jack. Enjoy these last few moments in the spotlight. They are quickly fleeting....
