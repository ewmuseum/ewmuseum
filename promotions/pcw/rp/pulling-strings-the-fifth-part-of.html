Pulling Strings, The Fifth Part Of

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.01.03 Dante Jackson retains past experiences well. As his jet black deluxe transport maneuvers up several steep hills, passing signs informing drivers to yield to wandering animals, his mind wanders. 'Yo, where we headed?' his eyes zoom towards the rocks and various school children playing with other boys and girls. 'Not far,' the public rep, or PR guy Jason Cunningham peps as Dante leans back into the glossy black seats. 'Your father arragined this meeting...' 'Wait, my father? What does he hold in this company, in my career? He's my father dammit, not my damn devil and angel sitting on my shoulders.' Dante sputters out as Jason reminds him, 'Those are your consciences, who would want old guys for consciences?' 'Skylar Green.' the mutter in unison. Turning back to the clouds and trees appearing on the outside of stormy black tint, he tunes out those in his hefty entourage, lip synching Skylar Green's rap as if it were a battle chorus to their ears. Perhaps his thoughts are focused on tapering his temper, those knee-jerk reactions that, if not controlled, could destroy everything he has slaved to achieve. Or could lost industry friendships be what weighs heavy on his Caesar-cropped head? 'He's a tough dude,' Dante remarks. 'He's even died and come back, odd enough.' Jason speaks up, 'What that during his old gimmick or new one?' 'Old, because ever since he's dropped the name and gone to his real one, all the make believe that I thought of The End washed out of him.' 'So,' Jason stops in mid sentence. 'he's basically a walking mummy?' Dante shakes his head. 'No, he's kinda a walking kung fu mummy with no eyes, a camera, and no time on his hands so he makes a song with Ludacris.' 'Wait, you made a song with Ludacris!' Dante raises his eyes, 'So?! MY SONG WAS BETTER! IT EVEN HAD A CHORUS! His had a damn Chinese woman rapping...look at his accomplishments and compare them to mine!' Jason pulls out a tale of the tape monitor and sits it on his lap, his eyes flipping through each and every name of every PCW superstar that is not Dante Jackson, trying to find their strengths, weaknesses, and achilles heel. 'He's 6'8, you're 6'4, he's won two titles, you've won one. He's defeated Rush Mason and Legend Inc -' 'Stop right there, I beat Xavier Kannon, that wipes out everything he just put up!' Dante shouts with obvious glee. 'I WIN!' Jason shakes his head as he begins to even mutter the words of the 'Rocky' theme song. tapping his fingers upon his knee as if it were some old Nergo spiritual that you could groove to and sing during tough times. Dante slaps him, causing Jason to grow cold and slouch back. 'That was more off key than Totally Tubular Tommy trying to be...A SUPERVILLIAN!' 'But he's not,' Jason comments as he gets back up from the premature slap that Dante gave him. 'he doesn't even have his own bat cave!' He grows in silence as Jason made a point, yes Totally Tubular Tommy was not a common man of caves and things that usually make you a super-villian, as Rent A Villian to teach a class on it, and then you might learn something. He's learned much from former comrades but his pride and beefs won't allow him to thank them. That's just one to grow with he reasons. He could also be inwardly scolding himself for placing his faith in the machine. As a top-earner and overseas commodity, he assumed that his former label, the almighty Def Jam, would consider him a priority of Jay Hova proportions. But those who assume... One storied night a decade earlier, the terms of celebrity seemed much simpler to Dante Jackson. The place was the legendary Tunnel nightclub in New York City. Cloaked in a black hoddie, Christopher Wallace stood at the club's oversized bar, Remy probably in the system. Biggie as Dante recalls, received an occasional glance from party people, not because of his dark and lovely debut, Ready To Die, but because of the comapny he was keeping. Next to him stood a chisled Tupac Amaru Shakur entertaining a line crowded with adoring females longing for autographs, huges, a milliseconds's worth of eye contact. Dante remembers it well. 'When I first thought of rapping,' Dante begins to stir up another brew of words. 'all I could think was, I can't wait till people wanna give me the same type of love. I can't wait till people wanna wait on line and crowd up to see me.' The transport now crreeps at 10 miles per hour past a canary-yellow sign reading, 'Playmates At Play'. The town is Beverly Hills, home of exaggerated riches and prospering plastic surgeons. Final destination: the Playboy mansion. Accompanied by three busty blondes, Hugh Hefner, decked in a Lucifer-red satin smoker's jacket and slacks pressed just so, waits in line for Jackson, the guest of honor. Upon entering the hedonistic lair, the 24-year-old Houston native is greeted by paparazzi and Playbody Bunnies. How's that for a dream realized?
