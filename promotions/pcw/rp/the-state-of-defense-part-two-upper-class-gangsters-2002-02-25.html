The State of Defense: Part Two - Upper Class Gangsters

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/25/02 The State of Defense… RP#2 for Turmoil and March Madness…and #0.5 for Fusion Location: 43rd Street, Calgary, Alberta, Canada…outside the Downey Winter Residence. Time: 18:00 Canadian Time. Weather: Crap… Chapter Two: Upper Class Gangsters… “~(Posh Accent)~ Ha! That’s yet another smashing tale of yours, Edward…brilliant yet stunningly subtle. Say, did you hear that that crazy Downey fellow is back in town?” “Yeah, I heard about that guy, he gave up the good life for nothing more than a shabby wrestling career. That, and he spends an awful lot of time with…~(Stutters)~…M-M-Middle Class folk. By golly, his parents must be dreadfully ashamed of the mite…fraternizing with the lower classes…POPPYCOCK!” “And one of them even calls himself ‘The Messiah’, what a ridiculous thing to call himself, especially when you take into account that he wrestles. Bah…and it’s Pro Wrestling too…nothing like a good round of fisticuffs, eh Edward?” ::. ~(Voice of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase)~ Everyone at some point or another conjures dreams of great grandeur, of unattainable things far away in the heights of wealth and fortune. And then, of course, you have the wealthy, pretentious bastards who have nothing better to do than mock those who were less fortunate than they are. As far as I’m concerned, I may be Middle Class, but I don’t NEED to dream…nor do I WANT to dream. As much as you might like to think I do, I really don’t…after all, when you’re full to the brim with, for lack of a better word, talent…then it’s hardly in your best interests to dream, is it, cretins? The stereotypical upper classes are a dying breed, especially with the sudden incline in Dot Com millionaires sucking the woven carpet from underneath their feet. I can hardly be sympathetic to these guys, firstly because after all, why would I sympathize with a bunch of fox hunted morons? And secondly, with their hierarchy out of position, it leaves a void for the newer breed of top dogs to take their place… Ha! And you thought I’d wanted them out for a pure, noble…almost communist belief. Sheesh, you really better learn to read the signs with a little more fluency than is usually required of goof-off viewers who sit around doing nothing to entertain or astound. Anyway, what greater analogy as compared to the current state of the PCW, than to use the image of the Social class hierarchy? There are very few REAL Top Dogs in the PCW, especially now that some nimrods thought it would be better to slip through Tyler Nelson’s fingers straight into the somewhat hot grasp of Jane Reagan, former PCW Personnel Officer…to the sickening thought of Boda somehow managing to pierce the World Title division. What better time for the likes of New Breed to seize hold of the PCW? Wrenching it from the grasp of has-beens, flinging it into the all mighty heights of Controversy and Superiority above all that oppose it. And nothing…not you and your little cat too, can stop us… The Snow has subsided now, thankfully enough. When you last left off, it was at a point where Dustin had hatched yet another idea, for what purpose…I don’t know. That idea, is yet to be found out by the likes of yourselves, as the scene opens upon a parking lot in an aristocratic area of Calgary. Reading an issue of “Cocky Canadians” Weekly as I lean against the cold bars of an iron fence, I frown as a conversation between two young snobs goes on before me. Leaning against an E-Type Convertible as they converse mockingly on the lower classes, they seem quite oblivious to the overwhelming presence of a bold, arrogantly posed Professional Wrestler. Taking my eyes away from the magazine, I take a glance at my watch. It’s Six o’clock in the evening, and the twilight is upon the scene, as the iron encased street lamps begin to flicker on, as glossed vintage automobiles pass underneath them… I snort arrogantly, as I await the arrival of a friend of mine, obviously…as I continue to listen to the ranting of Edward and Wilhelm and ‘Middle Class Gangsters’ by Matthew Good Band blares in the background….:: Chris: Great…Dustin’s late, and I’m now subjected to these whining dorks…oh what fun it is to be the saviour of the modern world… Edward: HAHA! Why, yes my rather obnoxious friend, I have to say that you’re absolutely spot on. Fisticuffs? Why, it’s the man’s sport, it involves skill, wit and intelligence, stuff that’s barely ever present in that of a professional wrestler…HAHA! Wilhelm: HA! What a proverbial hoot, my good chap…yet again; you astound me with the sheer brilliance of those jibes against the lower classes, against good for nothing sports as a whole… ::. The pair laugh amongst themselves, in a typically snooty laugh particularly suited to the likes of snobbish rich folk. Also, in stereotypical snob fashion, they each wear finely cut and pressed tweed suits, whose dull brown tones add contrast to the snowy streets on which we stand….:: Edward: Wrestling, ha, it’s hardly professional…that’s for sure. It’s better off being the folly of beer guzzling red necks who are looking for a little cheap entertainment… Wilhelm: ~(Ripping off Kaientai completely)~ InDEED~! Thoughts of Chris: ~(Mockingly)~ By Jove, aren’t these guys up their own proverbial asses? Damn, even Xavier isn’t this bad…and he’s probably one of the most inept bags of federation ballast that I’ve ever seen since Chris F’n Sloboda. Meh…I wonder where that idiot Dustin’s got to… ::. The rich man on the right, otherwise known as Wilhelm, pats his acquaintance on the back, nearly knocking the weedy Edward flying as the pair continue to laugh at their own bad jokes. Again, I take a look at my watch as I await the arrival of ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey, the Rich Texan kid that I’ve known ever since last April…practically a year ago now. Dustin, although inept at real life outside his estate and other random homes around the globe, is a guy with connections in higher places… It’s not like you people ever expected that from him… Whilst not exactly being secure, safe connections that could possibly be in some way reliable…but it’d certainly be better bet than dealing with crazed ex-convicts, Russian Madmen and Television Executives. **Static** Damn Television Executives… I glance over my shoulder at the stately home behind me, which is literally surrounded by tens of acres of estate land, stretching back about as far as the trimmed hedges would allow me to see. Turning my attention back to my magazine, I begin to chuckle at the inclusion of an advertisement that brings back memories of an episode from twelve hours ago….:: Wilhelm: ~(Laughs)~ After all, old chap…there’s nothing much more idiotic than bashing another person with a steel chair, or clobbering Russians with penguins, or even beating the living snot into a Scientologist or two… Thoughts of Chris Chase: Been There, Done That, Won a Cruiserweight Title Belt. Sheesh, isn’t there anything this guy will mention that isn’t one of my glorious achievements? ::. Wilhelm laughs to himself, as I begin to read aloud the advertisement, barely holding the humour of it to myself…revealing it to the whole world, even if the pair of snooty rich kids ahead of me choose to ignore my reading from the magazine, a magazine devoted to the coverage of great young Canadian wrestlers such as myself, these frighteningly awesomely skilled in-ring competitors with that edgy look about them. Maybe I’ll let you read it some time….:: Chris: ~(Reading from an advertisement)~ Presenting…the ChaseTron 2002 Automated Defense System – The Perfect alternative to those conceited Messiahs and cocky Canadian Saviours. Order yours today from Downey Enterprises for only $20,000 Canadian! Great, it’s no wonder that Dustin got so DAMN emotional over this thing…it’s built by his friggin’ DAD! **You’d have need to have read the previous part to get this minor joke…well…go on then…** ::. As I begin to laugh at the sheer relevance of this lame advertisement to the events of 12 hours ago, there is a shout from behind me. Folding the magazine and shoving it into the inside pocket of my black leather jacket without so much as a care, I turn to find a staggering and stumbling Dustin heading in my direction. Unbeknownst to me at time, not only had I noticed, but also Wilhelm and Edward, who also turn their heads in curiosity….:: Edward: Say, Wilhelm…did you by any chance hear the weirdly pitched voice of a rich Texan? I swore I heard it coming from over by that stately home over there… Wilhelm: ~(Snorts snobbishly in agreement)~ Uh huh…and I swear that I can hear the ramblings of a Middle Class Canadian…you want to go-look-see? Edward: ~(Nods)~ Sure old bean… ::. Eventually, Dustin passes the gate on the long, gravel trail up to the house, finally reaching me after what seems like a minute’s worth of frantic running. With tired, yet moronic expression on his face, he looks up at me…puffing and panting, under the nosey eye of Wilhelm and Edward. Before Dustin can fully catch his breath, Dustin and I are approached by Edward and Wilhelm, whose stiff-upper-lipped, snooty expression causes myself to arrogantly smirk at the pair. In the back of my mind, there remains the constant thought of finding somewhere slightly more adapted to the talented tresses of yours truly….:: Wilhelm: Well…look what we have here…it’s Master Dustin Downey and…friend, Edward. What a great honour it is to have such an influential man here, isn’t it Edward? Edward: ~(Coughs)~ Yes, Yes…Old Chap…it certainly is… ::. As Wilhelm nudges the under-weight Edward in his tweed wrapped ribs, I keep getting the feeling that this is little more than a half-assed ruse to keep my moronic friend under their control. Well, don’t look at me like that…it’s not my task in life to look after this guy after all, is it? Dustin looks at the two with that same dumb-assed look, confused to the very teeth at his whole situation that had fallen upon himself, as I look on, rather cynical of their whole foolish approach to Dustin Downey….:: Dustin: Hoo boy, Trout Fishermen…say, did you catch any of those fascinating fishy creatures today? I mean, they’re scaly like Snakes and Lizards…but they can live underwater…why they’re almost as amazing as Hamsters! Thoughts of Chris: Gah, how on earth did he manage to come up with that rant? Of course, this is a guy who could come up with some random witticism at any given time…DAMNIT! Edward: ~(Stuttering)~ W-What? Fly Fishermen? I’m sorry old bean, but you’re beginning to take this the wrong way. We’d never fish, why; we have Jeeves to do that…our British Butler, about this tall…ever seen him? ::. With a confused look on their faces, Edward and Wilhelm glare in surprise at Dustin’s sudden inclination to talk about Trout of all things…walking away slightly, I begin to laugh loudly to myself…making sure that those two imbeciles manage to hear my cocky laughter at their foolishness…and at their hopeless attempts to put down the constantly excitable and jittering Downey…who continues to muse about Trout fishing….:: Dustin: Ah…don’t be silly. By the looks of that tweed you’re wearing, you’ve bagged at least twenty of those babies today, haven’t you? ::. Slightly perturbed by all this talk about Trout Fishing, the slightly more stoutly Wilhelm frowns at Dustin, in a malicious, almost gangland kind of way. Then, I begin to look around me…at the cars passing. Each plays host to a cussing young Rich kid or two, who spit on the sidewalk, and throw insults at their elders, whilst driving around in cars that are older than Duke Williams is.:: Wilhelm: Why shut up, you uncivilized no-hoper savage. Can’t you tell that we’re the cream of the very crop in this here neighbourhood, the top dogs of the Socially accepted youth around here. We symbolize all that’s great with the Upper Classes… Chris: What? The Posh accents? The Overly elongated use of long words that you probably know no meaning of? Give me a break…you’re not great, you’re just some unentertaining bastards who think that because you’re more privileged than those below you, that you’re better than they are… ::. Jeez…why do I get myself into this things? Turning around, Wilhelm looks at me, processing the rant that I had just unleashed…mistakenly. Bear with me, I rarely make mistakes, however…this could prove to be one of them…or not, as the case may be…eh?.:: Wilhelm: Why, it’s ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase himself, well, aren’t you righteous today? ~(Laughs)~ Well…I happen to know that you’re the type to mock those who are less TALENTED than you are…aren’t you? ::. I nod, with a cocky smirk once again developing on my face as I face Wilhelm, pushing away the trailing edges of my jacket to reveal the golden PCW Cruiserweight Title firmly wrapped around my waist underneath, glimmering in the light emitted by the street lamps above me and the rest of the scene. The prize of the moment, the prize that I’ll be defending this Tuesday when I come up against Xavier Kannon. After all my rambling of the past month or so, I’m sure that you’re now familiar with that auburn haired hypocrite of a Scientologist, who somehow managed to lose to known other than Seth Freakin’ Masters in the Global Title Tournament. Am I truly blessed with an aura that brings my opponents to their knees, days before I’m even due to come up against them? Now then, blatant mockery of a foe aside, let’s skip back to the scene….:: Chris: Well…I have to hand it to you…considering you mocked my chosen profession, you managed to figure at least THAT out. But, Mister Snobbish Nimrod, there’s one tiny little detail that you missed out…something so god forsakenly important, that I simply can’t be ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase without it… ::. Of course, Wilhelm looks at me, cynically…questioningly. What on earth could I be rambling on about now? I pause and watch as Dustin peels away from the pair, and reaches me. Tapping on my shoulder, whilst peering from them to me, Dustin looks troubled and worried, even more so than he was the time when he found out that Lucky Charms had gone bankrupt. I turn, and frown at Dustin, who had just stopped me before I could continue with what I was ranting about….:: Dustin: Err…Chris? Chris: What Dustin, can’t you see that I’m on the way to a winning rant? Dustin: Yes, I can see that…but now I remember, it was these two guys that were related with the Lucky Charms fiasco…they bought out all the advertising rights and killed the catchphrase…Can we go now? ::. Reluctantly, I nod….:: Chris: Oh sure…kill off my rant…just when it was getting good. Kill me now…god. ~(Sighs)~ Oh well…at least it’s better than having a duck thrown at you… ::. Trust me to say something that would eventually wind up happening….:: Wilhelm: TAKE THIS YOU BASTARD!! ::. So, as a featherless Duck is catapulted in my direction, this causes the whole scene, and screen for that matter, to slow down into a still image as I throw Dustin in the way of the thrown duck…as if suddenly it had turned into a suddenly shameless Matrix Rip-off. (Sigh) Ah well, I guess that I can call it a day, right here…right no-… **Fizz of Static** And you can Justify…that! Fade to a blank screen and absolute nothingness….::
