The Truth About Kramer (part 2 final)

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/03 The last we saw, Kramer had just revealed his feelings towards Brianna, which ultimately turned their time out together into a soap opera. He put her in a spot of disbelief and shock. The thing that did it was probably him saying he had quit the FBI just to spend more time with her. THE FBI FOR CRYING OUT LOUD! The cameras focus in on Brianna’s awestruck face as she is ready to give her point of view on the whole situation. “Listen, you’re a great guy…” “Oh I know where this is going.” Kramer throws out feeling embarrassed and upset that he wasted his time and professed his love for nothing. He turns sideways in his seat so he doesn’t have to feel any more out of place than he already does. “Hear me out please.” With a little defiance on his face, Kramer turns around as he realizes it’s not fair to not listen to Brianna’s reasoning. “I have had relationships in the past and they just haven’t worked out. I would do all I could to keep them intact but in the end it all blew up in my face.” Brianna says, feeling bad for rejecting Kramer, knowing it took a lot of balls to come and say what he said. “But I’m different then those other guys you’ve dated! We can make this work!” a look of hope dwindles in his teary eyes. “Can you be so sure? Can you promise me a hundred and ten percent that you won’t leave my heart shattered into a thousand pieces just because I won’t accept your marriage proposal? Do you promise not to leave because I am just not ready to have a family yet?” This leaves Kramer staring quite confused. “I…I never asked you to marry me.” Kramer says. “Of course you didn’t, but someone I loved once did. To this day I still think about him every once and a while. But I refuse to get myself involved in anyone until I am absolutely ready. Besides I have wrestling to focus on….and my gadgets. I just don’t want to get my heart broken again.” “This is all because of Thomas? Well I guess I can say I don’t really blame you. Just realize that all guys aren’t jerks. If you want to talk sometime just give me a call.” With that Kramer stands up throwing a card with his phone number down to Brianna. Before he walks by he gives her a light peck on the forehead showing no hard feelings and walks away calmly into the crowd of drunken people parading around to the beat of the music. A young man with boyish good looks stumbles over to Brianna’s table smelling strongly of liquor. He takes a seat where Kramer had been sitting and wobbles around while pointing a finger at Brianna. “You know….If I had the opper…oppertwenty….opper….chance…I would put you and I together in the alphabet baby.” the man laughs after his statement. The drunken man sits there waiting for a response from Brianna who is trying to hold back laughter. “That might have worked ten years ago, but it’s pretty old nowadays. You should really brush up on your pickup lines.” Brianna says standing up and walking away from the man who is so out of it that he tries to get up to follow, but falls to the floor with a sickening thud. Later On…… Brianna is sitting at her kitchen table looking through a wrestling magazine, reading an interesting article about herself (which only takes up a small section of the page). She finishes and flips the page. As she does that, Milo comes strolling into the house with a smile plastered across his face. He takes off his jacket and walks into the kitchen. “So how’d it go?” “It turns out he liked me and I had to shoot him down.” “You shot that fine Indian man down? What’s wrong with you?” he says while looking through the fridge, grabbing the carton of orange juice. “I just wasn’t ready for a relationship.” “You’re going to have to forget about the whole Thomas situation one of these days and move on hun! Sure things won’t always go great, but what happens if you pass up Mr. Right?” Brianna thinks about it and realizes that Milo is oddly making some bit of sense. “Mr. Right…” Brianna whispers to herself. Thoughts of a true Mr. Right bombard Brianna as the scene slowly fades to black.
