The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XII]

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6, 2002 We last left our PINK! heroes wondering if they would be ready for Friday's tag team title match against Cowabunga Connection. Johnny Heart was talking to the Mystery Man about getting rid of 'Smith' from the Rainbow Squad, who had defected to the PURPLE~! Jack Valentine was missing in action, and there was a whole lot of action going on with the Village People. How would everything turn out? Read on to the War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XI] to find out! The Mystery Man and Valentine had left the motel and were heading out to meet Johnny Heart. It appeared Valentine had just not gone out that evening...a rarity for him, considering he loves to party the PINK! night away. Jack Valentine: “Okay then what was the upshot of me going to that nightclub, did you find out anything useful.” The Mystery Man shrugged in a noncommittal fashion. Jack Valentine: “Was the PURPLE~! watching the place then?” Mystery Man: “I don’t know, hard to say, nothing definite.” The Mystery Man was thinking about something else. Jack Valentine: “You mean that I about got my head kicked in for the sake of nothing definite.” Valentine was now being particularly forceful. Mystery Man: “Oh come off it Valentine, we had nothing to do, it was worth a try. Anyway you did not get your head kicked in, and you got drunk. Not a bad night in my book” Jack Valentine: “That’s nothing to do with it, you asked me to go out there, the fact that I did not actually get my head kicked in is irrelevant.” Mystery Man: “Pfft, you just remind me of that next time I decide to save your life, if I knew you were going to be so petty about the whole thing, I wouldn’t have bothered. And no thanks for getting you drunk.” Jack Valentine: “Getting drunk as you so elegantly put it was nothing to do with you, anyway you got the more shit-faced than me. All I wanted to do was enjoy the scenery, have a few drinks, maybe think about my match against Cowabunga Connection, or even think up a few decorating tips.” Mystery Man: “Humpph, if you’re going to carry on like this I’ll be mighty tempted to kick your head in for you. Can we change the subject, while you still have all the requisite parts of your anatomy.” Jack Valentine: “Okay then, we need money for all these bills that you keep running up. The motel is after the tag team champions' money. I could use that cash card that Liberace gave me.” Mystery Man: “No you could not, you do and we’ll have half the Rainbow Squad in the area looking for you, anyway I told you to destroy it.” Jack Valentine: “I thought Liberace might want it back.” Mystery Man: “No he would not, destroy the bloody thing. You're a PCW wrestler, you pay for it.” Jack Valentine: “I guess that’s why I’m not in control like you were, I obviously don’t have your great planning abilities.” Mystery Man: “Fuck Valentine, gripe gripe gripe, I’m beginning to wish I had left you to fight off those thugs. Will you just shut up and tag along. Anyway you’re just pissed off because you lost your PINK! coat. Valentine huffed in silence. There was a certain element of truth in what The Mystery Man said. He felt out of place without his stylish overcoat. The Mystery Man had arranged that they meet Heart in the back room of a bar. Heart was sitting on a chair, his legs either side of the back rest, he nodded as he recognized The Mystery Man coming into the back room. The Mystery Man and Valentine also sat down around the table, The Mystery Man sitting forward like Heart, while Valentine sat back in his chair. Mystery Man: “How’s the room then Heart, they looking after you okay?” Johnny Heart: “Fine, no grumbles, but I’ll have to move, even with ten pages of motels in the yellow pages, it’s not going to take forever for Smith and the Village People to find me. They know where I'm supposed to be Friday night...right in the middle of that damn PCW ring against Cowabunga Connection.” Mystery Man: “Don’t worry Heart that’s what we’re here to sort out tonight. With luck this time tomorrow all your problems will be over.” Valentine sat forward... Jack Valentine: “Wait a minute, I thought that we had quite enough troubles of our own without going looking for more Johnny. This mystery guy...whoever he is...is really getting in our way of focus. We should be concentrating on wrestling now, not PINK! or PURPLE~!' Johnny Heart: 'You're right, but we've come this far...' Jack Valentine: 'I thought we were going to drop out of this whole war after Wrestle Vessel.' Johnny Heart: 'You thought wrong!' The tension between Valentine and Heart was growing, but they both knew deep down that they had to find a legitimate way out of this so-called 'war.' The Mystery Man once again turned to face Heart,... “We had agreed that if we can rid the PURPLE~! of Smith then your problems are over.” Johnny Heart: “Well my problems will not be over, but they will at least be solvable. As long as Smith is with them, I’m a marked man for sure.” Mystery Man: “Okay, we need all the information that you’ve got. Do you hold any paperwork?” Heart shook his head, then tapped his forehead with a stubby finger. Johnny Heart: “I’ve been following him for a month now, I know his routines better than he does.” Mystery Man: “Well that’s a start. What about the police computer, is it worth tapping in there to access stuff?” Johnny Heart: “Not really, we never bothered to load up most of the stuff, so I’ve got more of it in my head than there is in the computer. Is that likely to cause problems, I’m sure we can get access if it is important.” Mystery Man: “Not really, all we really need to know is itineraries and routes, that sort of thing. Someone with some on the fighting experience is the key to this. How long have you been following him?” Johnny Heart: “Months, we made a bust the day we met, but he had inside information and it all went pear shaped. Basically he landed another Village Person in it, and made monkeys of us. Mind you he had inside help to do that.” The Mystery Man pulled out a load of maps, and a pad of graph paper, and they got down to what was termed 'planning.' For a lowlife sleazebag Smith made an early start. The Mystery Man had established a point where Smith would pass by whichever subsequent route he might take. They were parked there for half an hour before Smith’s car passed them. Mystery Man: “How many of them were there in the car then?” The Mystery Man called out as they pulled out. Johnny Heart: “Whoa, whoa, that’s a PINK! car following Smith, let them pass.” Heart had recognized the unmarked boys in the Rainbow Squad. Mystery Man: “What, Christ, shit, they’ve got behind him now, bugger.” The Mystery Man was always at this best in the mornings. Johnny Heart: “We’ll have to let it go, if Smith has got a PINK! tail today we’ll never be able to touch him.” Heart was crestfallen. Mystery Man: “Only a slight impediment, I’ll think of something, you reckon you can tell me where Smith is headed for?” Johnny Heart: “Once we get beyond a certain point I’ll be able to tell you. In fact we can even get ahead of him if it’s where I think he’s going. He usually stops off for a newspaper, so you should be able to get ahead of him.” Mystery Man: “That will certainly make things easier. Right that PINK! car is one of them gay little British ones, isn't it.” Johnny Heart: “Yep new issue, quite nice actually. Man, how come you are driving this car without the benefit of an ignition key?” Heart had just spotted this little novelty. Mystery Man: “Johnny, I could have popped out and hired a car using a stolen credit card, but because it was you I thought I’d do things properly and steal a car for this one. What did you think I was going to do, pop into the PINK! headquarters and ask to borrow one of theirs?” Johnny Heart: “This whole thing is over the top, why did I ever get involved with you, I could have got protection, I’ve got friends.” Mystery Man: “Shut up Heart, this is the easiest quickest way to solve your problems. Besides, neither you or Valentine have any friends besides Valentine's so-called little girlfriend, but we all know that it's just a cover for your little relationship. Cowabunga Connection, Politically Incorrect, Pretty Cool Guys...they all HATE PINK! guts. They'd hate you with PURPLE~! too. That's why we have to remain unmarked for now. Now shut up and tell me where to go.” Johnny Heart: “About quarter of a mile along this road, once he turns at that junction with the traffic lights we’ll know for sure.” Mystery Man: “Okay then we just need to get ahead of that PINK! car then.” The Mystery Man accelerated wildly, till he pulled out level with the PINK! car, the PINK! car steadfastly refused to let them in in front, The Mystery Man started flashing his head lights, the PINK! car finally saw sense and let him get in front. By this time Valentine and Heart were cowering together in the back about dying of embarrassment. Mystery Man: “Okay all you lot you get one warning, put your seat belts on now and sit up straight.” The Mystery Man was letting Smith’s car pull away ahead of them. He watched it like an eagle, the traffic lights were nearing quickly. Smith indicated, moved into lane to turn right. The Mystery Man sped up, the PINK! car was close behind him. Smith’s car turned the corner out of sight. The Mystery Man gave the accelerator a final touch, then slammed the brakes on, the car was skidding to a rapid halt, and The Mystery Man had the gears into reverse, slamming the car back into the PINK! car. The PINK! car was trying to get out of the way but The Mystery Man steered into it. With a crash and splintering of glass their car connected with the bonnet of the PINK! car. The two Rainbow Squad men in the passenger seats, whipped forward, giant marshmallows of air bags filling the windscreen obscuring them. Valentine and Heart were still counting their arms and legs, as the Mystery Man pulled away as if nothing had happened. He bent forward to turn on the radio. Mystery Man: “Shit would you credit it, the radios broken. That’s why the car industry is in decline these days, just a shit product, not the Goddamn economy.” As promised they managed to get ahead of Smith and the Village People. Smith had stopped at a corner shop to send in the Cowboy to buy a paper and cigarettes. That left three of them in the car. The Mystery Man drove on ahead of them, and they parked where Heart suggested just out of sight of the building site. Johnny Heart: “Okay, this is a regular stop for Smith he will leave two of the Village People in the car and with one goon he will go into the building ahead of us. The person who he will meet will be on his own. The will swap a package of PURPLE~! gear for a package of money. They do this every week, but they have a variety of meeting places which they choose at the last minute. Have you got what you need?” Heart was standing with his hands on the bonnet of the car. The Mystery Man checked his watch, put his gear back over his shoulder and was ready to 'end' Smith. Mystery Man: “Boys, all I need is in my head, I could do this without the gun, the gear just makes it cleaner and easier. How long have I got before Smith gets there?” Johnny Heart: “Five minutes maybe, you’ll need it, you’d better get moving whatever you have in mind.” Valentine and Heart watched through the binoculars as The Mystery Man made his way to the building site. There was a ditch that headed towards the building passing close to one side of it. Having found a gap in the wire fencing that surrounded the building site The Mystery Man headed forwards quickly. He ran a low stooping run, silent and paced. Valentine realized how The Mystery Man had been able to surprise him in the woods that first day. Despite his size he could move like a cat, leaving no trace, making no sound. The Mystery Man skirted round a puddle, and swooped down into the ditch, Smith’s car pulled into the building site. The Mystery Man was barely visible, and moving closer to the building, unperturbed by the arrival of Smith. From their vantage point they could see The Mystery Man climbing the scaffolding that went up the side of the building. It was like some giant child’s toy with connecting pieces that bulged out wherever pieces connected. The Mystery Man had a rigid toe fixed on one such node, his hands reached up and grasped bare scaffolding, each arm finding a different part of the scaffolding structure to pull on. Despite his feline grace and strength he was a big man, and the scaffolding had been put up quickly, it was not designed to have people the size of The Mystery Man swinging from it. He was climbing the scaffolding with the steady rhythm of a man climbing stairs. The scaffolding was juddering with the wind, the legs of the scaffolding were planted on timbers in the mud, it seemed incredible that it could support The Mystery Man and his rapid ascent. But he was there, he was on the roof. The roof had been timbered. For once The Mystery Man seemed uncertain of his grip. His hands pawed the roof searching for an imperfection or an angle that he might use to gain some purchase. After some frantic pawing he found the necessary grip on the apparently smooth surface. With a cat like spring he leapt onto the roof, his feet landing on it, they slithered as he tried to run up the roof, his outstretched hands were also on the slippery roof trying to grip, he seemed to slither backwards fatally despite a pawing crawl up the roof. His eyes fixed on the edge to a dormer window that had not been finished, leaving a dark hole in the smooth wooden surface of the roof. With a renewed vigor he redoubled his frantic crawl, arms and legs moving to their own rhythms his limbs racing like a thoroughbred he suddenly shot up across the roof and disappeared into the hole. Valentine let out an audible sigh.... Wow! Will The Mystery Man be able to catch 'Smith' and rid The Heartbreakers of their problems so that they can be ready for Cowabunga Connection on Friday? Stay tuned for Part XII to find out!
