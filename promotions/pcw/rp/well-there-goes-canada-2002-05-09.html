Well there goes Canada.

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/9/2002 ~It is creeping up, isn't it? Can't you smell it in the air? The air is thick with anticipation for Supremacy. It is so thick you can cut it with a knife. Are you looking forward to Supremacy? Are you ready for the beginning of the end for the Ultimate Authority? Are you ready to see a revolution, a coup de ta, if you will? Jon Kano is ready. He is ready to hold class. He is ready to teach once again. Jon Kano taught Tim Walden everything he knows about the wrestling industry. He is the man who made Tim Walden. Without that education from Jon Kano, Tim Walden would still be a curtain jerking jobber. When you think about it, whenever a student challenges the teacher, 99.9 percent of the time, the teacher shows why the teacher taught and the student learned. Will Supremacy follow the trend or will it be the exception to the rule?~ *The scene opens up at the border of Canada (otherwise known as America, Jr.) and the United States of America. Jon Kano is driving his trademark jet-black 2002 Lincoln Navigator as he slows to be questioned by the border patrol. The rather large, disgustingly fatbodied border patrolman approaches Kano's Navigator as Kano rolls down the window.* Patrolman: Hello, sir. As standard procedure, I must ask you some questions before allowing you to enter the great country of Canada, eh. Kano: Alright. Patrolman: Do you have any baggage with you? Kano: Yes. I have a couple suitcases full of clothes and a laptop computer in a case. Patrolman: Did you pack your luggage yourself? Kano: No. Last night I called up Carrottop, Florence Henderson, and Martha Stewart and they came over to my room and we packed them together. Afterwards we drank a few beers and ran a train. They left near dawn, all satisfied. Patrolman: Sir, have you been in possession of your bags ever since you packed them? Kano: No. As a ritual, I set my bags down in the middle of the lobby overnight, just so the glare of the moonlight reflects off of them. Patrolman: You sir are a smartass. Kano: And you sir, are a fatass. *Kano cracks a smartass smile.* Patrolman: Finally sir, are you carrying any thing that could be considered a weapon of terror? Kano: Well, I have a tub of anthrax in the back, and I have a megaton a-bomb under my seat. Do those count? Patrolman: Just proceed into the country, sir. *Kano floors the gas pedal into Canada, spinning his tires all the way as the scene fades into the interior of his Navigator.* Kano: What a jackass. Yeah, if I was a terrorist, would I really tell him? Why the hell are they asking me those questions? Yeah, I, a worldwide personality and millionaire, am going to blow up some sh*t or kill thousands of people in a terrorist attack. I know this isn't politically correct, but I could understand them asking those questions to a man of my age that is from Saudi Arabia or Yemen. Well I guess I shouldn't say that, because now it looks like we have some domestic terrorists, too. Well, f*ck them. I still don't deserve to be treated like I am a potential terrorist. Speaking of jackasses, guess who comes to mind. Yes, that is right, Tim Walden is a jackass. *Kano reaches into his center console and pulls out a cigarette and lights it.* Kano: I admire Tim Walden. Now I know what you all are saying right now, 'Did Kano just say he admires Tim Walden?' Why yes, yes he did. I admire the fact that he is trying to be like me. He uses the word f*ck as much as he can, but he just can't use it with the flare and in the spirit of the word like I can. I mean I could say f*ck as every other word and everyone would know what I am saying. I communicate my thoughts effectively. Whenever Tim puts a microphone to his mouth and begins to speak, everyone says, 'What is this jackass saying? Is he trying to impress us with his thorough knowledge of the word f*ck?' Everyone loses interest. No one cares what Walden says. Can anyone remember a time when Walden has ever said anything that mattered or significantly contributed to the success of a show or PCW as a whole? I didn't think so. Hell, even in that whole little Lethal Lottery tag match he and I were in, he did the big J-O-B to the Violator. Now I know Tim will say, 'So what? Kano jobbed to the Sexual Harrassment Panda.' Yeah, that would be a great comment, if I wouldn't have had two cracked vertabrae in my neck, courtesy of that big, bad, Russian bastard. *Kano deeply inhales the cigarette smoke, savoring its deadly, cancerous smoke.* Kano: Is there really any doubt that Walden is going to be taken to school by me at Supremacy? Who the hell has Walden ever beaten of any stature here in PCW? Look at my resume. I have beaten Kriegman, Nelson, Boda, the Blood Brothers, AFL-CIO, Politically Incorrect, and the list of champions beaten by me just keeps going and going like that damn bunny. I am not some run-of-the-mill seasoned veteran. I am not some old has-been who can barely walk and is still clenching on to his delusions of grandeur. I am a twenty-six year old LEGEND. I have been to the promised land of PCW, and I have been to the bowels of hell. I am still here, still rocking as hard as I can. I am still putting the asses in the seats. I am still entertaining, and at Supremacy, I will beat Tim Walden, and I will become the number one contender to the Continental Title. I will then go on to become the two-time, two-time, my God, two-time Continental Champion. And that is why at Supremacy, I will show the whole world, and teach my former student why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano floors his gas pedal once again to head into the beautiful city of Toronto, Canada.* ~Are you ready? Is Tim Walden ready? It appears that Jon Kano is more than ready. And if Kano is ready and if he brings his A-game to Supremacy, may God have mercy on Walden because Kano sure as hell won't.~
