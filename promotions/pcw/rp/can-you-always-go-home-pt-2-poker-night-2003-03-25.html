Can you always go home PT 2: Poker Night

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/03 His eyes were burning from the cigar smoke that bedded the room’s atmosphere. The clicking of game chips had become like incessant buzzing of a pest. Knox carefully peeled back his two cards revealing an Ace of spades and a King clubs. Knox looked down at his stack of chips then looked across the table at his remaining opponent, his former tag team partner Derrick Diamond. The game was Texas Hold Em, Tonight’s game started with 6 competitors and 4 hours later it was down to just Knox and his former co tag champion. Most of the games competitors had since left, the only remaining spectator now was Sonny Villani, He was Ryan’s cousin and a soldier for one of Boston’s more well known “underground” organizations. His name carried more weight than all the Atkins diets in the world had burnt off and if you weren’t familiar with his name the 5 foot 8 mini hulk would be glad to enlighten you. If this game wasn’t held at his cousin Ryan’s house and wasn’t contested between some of his close friends, Sonny would be sure to take a share of the overall pot regardless if he won or lost.“Check” Knox barked outA smirk inched across Diamond’s face. A look familiar to many old school PCW fans. Diamond glanced at his chips. He had a small chip lead on Knox and if one of the two men didn’t make a power move for the others chips, this game would run endlessly. Texas Hold Em was the closest thing to a hostile take over in the game world. It was a war of the minds played out with a deck of cards.“Call” Diamond replied as he tossed in chips matching Knox blind bet.Villani dealt out the flop…three cards to begin the community in which each player could use to create the best five card hand. Knox looking away from the flop rubbed his sore eyes and took a deep breath trying to catch a second wind. He half heartedly thought about just dumping hand after hand to end the game. But his pride wouldn’t let him loose to Diamond, not only his former tag partner, not only a trust fund baby, but now a very successful sports and entertainment agent. If money grew on trees, then Derrick Diamond was selling the seeds. Knox looked up to see the three card flop….a run of hearts an ace, a king, and a 10. Knox had the strongest two pair in the deck. He paused for a moment to make sure his bet would be the right one, not one influenced by the pressure of leaving in the morning for his first PCW match in 3 years.“300”Diamond paused a moment to review his pocket cards with the community.“Ok, Call ….bitch”Years from being considered one of the greatest tag teams in PCW history hadn’t effected the charisma the two had outside the ring as best friends. Villani dealed 4th street, the 4th community card. It was a 7 of clubs… no help to either man…“300” Knox strung a series of chips into the pot.“Call”“You’s guys bet like a bunch of pussies. I ain’t staying here dealing all night ya know. I’ve got more pressing matters to deal wit, if you know what I mean. Aright!?” Sonny blasted into the two men as he tossed the fifth and final card…the river. It was another Ace suited in diamonds. Knox found it fitting that the final card gave him a full house Aces full of Kings, The strongest full house in the game and it was completed by a diamond suited card.“Diamonds are forever” Derrick said with a half hearted giggle.“There also for bitches and basketball players….and your no basketball player.” Knox said with a smile on his face…. “P.S….. I’m all in” Knox pushed the remainder of his chips in. Not even a flush could beat a Full house.“Call” Diamond quickly pushed his chips in.Knox thought to himself that Diamond must have caught the flush and figured at best Knox had the three aces.“Read em and weep”Knox flipped his cards over revealing the monster full house. “Oooofffff” Villani let out with a surprise.Diamond sat back with a sigh for a moment, then leaned forward flipping over his cards to reveal a Queen and Jack of hearts. Knox looked down at the cards and the arrogant smile quickly gaped open into a look of pure awe. Diamond had caught the strongest possible hand in poker, The royal flush. Knox slowly lifted his head up to see his best friend reaching for the chips with a cereal box smile from ear to ear on his face.“Ooooofffff, Can’t beat that Ryan” repeated Villani.Villani reached into his pocket and pulled out a large wad of money, about $6,000. They let Sonny hold the buy in money, He wasn’t going to run with the money and he didn’t trust anyone else to hold it.“ Maybe I’ll just run off with the money instead, Whatcha think about that” Sonny jokingly said“You’d never get away, eating all that pasta and shit and I’m far too pretty for you to bump off” Diamond snatched the money and stuffed it into his shirt pocket with a smile on his face.“I’ve got to drop the kids off at the pool, you better have something to read in that bathroom.”As Diamond made his way to the bathroom Sonny began to pack things up from the game.“Just leave it, I have a maid coming tomorrow and Little Tony is going to watch the house for me.”“Soooo … You’s leaving to go back to work tomorrow?” asked Sonny“Yea, that’s the plan. First time in three years. If it wasn’t for the occasional strong arming people with you I wouldn’t remember what work was. ““Know what I don’t like… The fact your not using your beautiful, given Italian name. What duh fok is so wrong with being Italian.”“Don’t start that again, Could be worse… I could be wearing feathered boa’s or Chef’s hat.”“This is true. Well, I’m going to shoot out, Call my cell when you land at the airport, let me know what’s going down. And if any of those other steroid monkeys like yourself give you trouble, you let me know…we’ll make em disappear”“You are completely crazy”“ I know, tell that goomba in the bathroom, I’ll call him tomorrow”“Ok, goodnight man”Knox guided his cousin out the front door, As he turned back he heard the sound of an aerosol spray can being used, instants after the smell of Glade Vanilla pine spray filled the room. Derrick Diamond re-entered the room walking toward the door he scooped his leather jacket off of the counter and headed for the door.“Well, good game man” Knox conceded to his one time partner“I wish, I could say the same for you and the 4 other bitches I cleaned out tonight…HA!” “Nice to see your still a sore winner”“yea, some things never change. So what time are you flying out tomorrow?”“Early evening”“Does the ex wife know yet?”“She’s not my ex, we’re just separated”“Oh right, right...does she know?”“I don’t know man, I tried calling her and before I can tell her we just get at each others throats. I ran into her sister at the market the other day and told her, so I assume she reported it back to Amy by now”“She was the only thing that kept you grounded, she changed who you were, but at the same time, she made you choose between her and the game and she’s at fault for making you unhappy by doing that.’“I don’t want to talk about it, So you sure your not interested in coming back with me? Knox and Diamond back in action?”“Ryan, I made 3.8 million dollars last year by talking shit to athletes, owners and directors. My pussy rate is at like a 9.7, The only people getting better ass than me is Colin Ferrell and seats at Paris Hilton’s salon. Sadly that outweighs running around beating people up with two divorced bitches like you and Boda”“Such tact”“Plus, I think you and Big Daddy Boda can handle Jon Kano, his never ever weres and that big Jehovah’s Witness. , Oh and when you get out there and get squared away, let me know. I want to fed ex you some business cards so I can get some clients out of PCW.”“I’m the only client you need”“Ha!...... Why are you even going back?”“I don’t know if I’m completely sure of that to be honest dude. It’s not because I need the money and not because I feel the need to get my body more bent than it already is.”“Then why? You’ve held titles, Beat the best that place had to offer, and after that whole incident with Kriegman and the hormone injection, you swore you’d never go back. So what changed?”What Derrick was asking was legitimate, and deep down Knox new the reason but just swirled a million other ideas to go back to complicate things.“When I was in the ring, living the fast life… I never felt a hunger for anything but the spotlight. I loved hurting people and I lived for the fame. After that life was over, I never felt the same until Amy came into my life, I loved her so much, I didn’t hunger for the spotlight anymore. Then when she left. I had nothing, So you ask me why… I don’t think I could ever fully explain.”“You’re a cheesy motherf..”“Get out”Knox opened the door for his best friend and watched him make his way out. Ryan turned back and made his way over to his couch, where he would sit and try and relax, hopefully falling asleep for a while. The things people have said about him were firmly repeating in his head, such things were only cemented in by Jon Kano’s recent comments. Knox was the greatest talent to never cash in. In fact if Knox were a NASCAR racer. He’d be the guy who reached the checkered flag long before the rest of the pack only to decide to veer into the wall rather than deal with the pressure of being the best. Why was he going back to the PCW? He knew in his heart why and that was enough to get him to help Boda battle the Primetime Players, it was enough for now at least.
