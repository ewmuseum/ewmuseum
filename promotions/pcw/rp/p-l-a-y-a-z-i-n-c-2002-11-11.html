P.L.A.Y.A.Z I.N.C

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.11.02 The scene opens up in a small, dark TV studio. Duzza has been given a short amount of TV time. He sits infront of the camera, his face looks tired, but it still has a youthful glow to it. His hair is set back in long cornrows. He is sitting right infront of the camera, staring right into it Duzza: “I got me some tv time. So with some inspiration from my man Statuz Quo, I’ma hit y’all up with an acronym for Playaz Inc… yeah y’all heard… here goes… P Pissin on y’all! Not literally… But, y’all getting’ pissed on like the bitch in that FAKE R. Kelly sex tape. Y’all always hatin, always runnin’ around talking about Playaz Inc. Man fuck y’all! Statuz Quo don’t care what y’all think. Duzza don’t care what y’all think! Playaz Inc don’t give a fuck! For real. We do this, just to annoy you. We ain’t tryin’ to make friends no more. I already got 4 friends, it’s time to make some mother fucking money! L Love. Love, is what Playaz Inc got for each other and from the hood. And I ain’t talking about PWA in their locker room backstage type of love either, naw, I mean respect. We get respect on the streets. Y’know, I heard them say “Duzza can’t go home” know where I heard that? Back in Harlem” A Amazing. For real. Ain’t no one doing the stuff we doing. Let’s take a look at, what’s the top fed in the world? PCW? Which stable has 3 members from the 3 last tag team champions? Playaz Inc. What’s the top, best known and undeniably strongest stable out here? Playaz Inc. We amazing. Y Y’all. Why y’all hatin!? This ain’t a black/white thing. I mean, all of y’all! We get so much hate! Don’t nobody care about us, all they do is doubt us. Fuck y’all. We don’t need y’all. Man, even vegetarians got beef with us! A Anger. Haters say shit like “they just angry black men, that’s original” What? I started this gangsta shit! Back in the day, it was all me. Then everyone else came around, claiming their hoods, don’t tell me I ain’t original, cos I’m the reason half of these Playaz Inc rejects are eating… Z Zenith. Shit, I bet half of y’all don’t even know what that means. It’s the highest point of success… or the highest point of power. And well, Playaz Inc ain’t there… yet. But we’re gonna get there. We don’t care who we have to burn and who we have to piss of and who we have to destroy to get there. Playaz Inc will get to the top…only a matter of my time my friend… I Ignorant. Some of y’all are ig’nant! You compare Playaz Inc to the Black Attack. Why? Because we’re black? Exactly. Let me just throw this out there, black people and white people are the same! We all the same. So how is a black stable different than a stable full of white guys? It isn’t. The only difference is how y’all perceive the stable. Your all ignorant. Now Black Attack, you wanna know why they was so unsuccessful? It’s because the are the exact opposite to Playaz Inc. Black Attack was put together just like a manufactured pop band. Half of them niggaz didn’t even know each other before they got thrown together. Playaz Inc on the other hand, man we was all down with each other before Playaz Inc got together in PCW. You understand? We ain’t manufactured, were real. N Never. Playaz Inc will never be stopped. Can’t no one stop us. Xavier Kannon wants to do an entire promo dissin’ us, do we care? No. That boy got issues, ‘cos we all know who’s running his relationship. Duke Williams wants to bad mouth us? Who cares? That hick has got a identity crisis. One minute he’s Garth Brooks, the next he’s Marylyn Manson. What’s that all about? An Ivan? Ivan whatever his name is, the penguin fearing Russian. Man, that boy needs to catch a kerbing like Mark Makonie did after he fucked with me. No lie… never fuck with Playaz Inc, unless your really down. Cos we’re petty mother fuckers. We won’t let shit go until your dead and buried. For real. You fuck with us, we’re gonna bring it to you until you can’t take it no more. CChampions, Crazy, Crackers… Whatever. None of us are champions at the moment, but it’s only a matter of time before we are. Statuz and Dante, the hardest working team up in here. We all know it’s only a matter of time before they get the belts back. Crazy? They say we’re crazy. Who cares? We ain’t crazy, we’re committed to the cause, to the struggle. We’re gonna get to the top of this mother fucking mountain. Crackers? Shit, I’m the mo fo that bought that word to this place and look at how it took off! But lets just take a look at the soldiers Statuz Quo, probably the most hated man in all of wrestling, but you know why he’s hated? It ain’t because of how he speaks, it’s because everyone knows he has skills. And he’s got my back. I could be in the streets, about to fight a group of 15 nazi’s all 100 times angrier than Makone and I know this mother fucker has got my back… the leader of the Playaz Inc no doubt..but also the releast mother fucker in the game Ali Khadaffi… that’s an angry black man right there. Don’t ever get on his bad side. Or you’ll be eating hospital food for a tube for the next 6 months. No lie. Dante Jackson… The brains of the group… Sure, he’s subdued and he’s quiet, but he’s as dangerous as Jackie Chan with a throwing ninja star… And Duzza? Well… I’m an R. Kelly fan. You don’t mess with R. Kelly fans. We’re dangerous. You never know exactly what is on our mind. We could be thinking about a classic love song, or about to give you’re a kerbing in a protest against all the hate aimed at the man… but whatever you think about Playaz Inc..y’all better recognize.. You Don’t Fuck With Us” Duzza then gets up from the chair he’s been sitting at, and as the scene fades to black, he walks away…
