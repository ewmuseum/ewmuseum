Strengthening the Mind

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered March Madness, the event that El Diablo will reveal his new self in the ring. El Diablo has already sworn his beliefs to ancient Babylonia and especially three mythical demons, Asag, Gallu and Namtar, but the reasons for his sudden change and sudden beliefs he has not explained at full detail but only left it sketchy. A big reasoning towards his change seems to be labelled on the headshot he took on Turmoil by the cruel Australian, Flaymin Aymin. Why Aymin did it is probably due to what The Mexican Devil did on Sabotage, leaving him to fend for himself in a match. But a fire extinguisher to the head is just pure callousness. Although that shot seems to be the trigger that caused El Diablo to start his cult following of ancient Babylonia. Whatever is going on in El Diablo's mind is definitely not stable or sane. El Diablo has definitely changed, he is no longer as loud as he was or acting how he used to, he seems more relaxed than ever and is more interested in living his new lifestyle than he was prior to his new beliefs. March 3rd will be the date that this new, strange, weird beast will lurk it's leery head out in front of thousands to main event at number eleven in that madness, but what madness will this Mexican Devil unleash upon those in the ring with him, or most importantly, what madness will he just do while he is out there. An eerie thought of such an eerie man, but El Diablo is just someone that cannot be explained and most likely never will be. These new beliefs of his, has really intrigued his mind. Already intelligent, El Diablo now is broadening his horizons, so to speak, and is pushing these new beliefs to unacceptable levels. His home has become a shrine to Babylonia and Angel, his loving wife, has no idea how to cope with this change. Not only is it El Diablo's change, but it will also be her change, a worrying thought for Angel, but nothing she cannot cope with. 'Can you believe this?' asks El Diablo in some sort of shock, 'can you believe that some say Asag is related to Zu?' he finishes. Angel watches on in utter amazement at him speaking from his book. 'What?' she asks sarcastically. However, being too caught up in his book and his own world around him he is oblivious to the sarcasm that Angel expresses. 'Zu was another myth,' explains El Diablo, 'he acted almost identically to Asag, hence why some say they he was related to Zu,' he finishes with utter interest in his voice. Angel sighs, knowing that El Diablo didn't even detect her sarcasm. 'How exciting,' she states sarcastically again while playing with her damp hair from the shower she just had. Her footprints that she left on the carpet have now faded completely and Angel, under her bathrobe seems to be practically dry with the exception of her damp hair. El Diablo is absolutely riveted with his book on ancient Babylonia; this can be seen with the sick smile that comes across his face every few pages, which he reads. 'This is amazing,' speaks El Diablo in awe, 'Asag, promoted sickness and disease to all those that would oppose him,' he says with a sinister smile on his face. Angel looks across at him with a look of disgust on her face. 'So?' she half inquires and half in sarcasm asks him. A smile fills El Diablo's face. 'I have the power to do the same,' he salivates, 'I can live just like he did. I can be just like Asag,' he says with a passion that he has not expressed in a long time. Angel attempts to swallow, but cannot, in a strange fear that engulfs her entire body, like that of and enzyme getting completely swallowed within the body. 'Why? Why would you want to do that?' she barely manages to ask in fear of the stranger she sees before her. Still with book in hand, he brings it into his chest, still open to speak openly with his life, who seems not to recognise the beast of El Diablo right in front of her. 'So, my vengeance can sweep away those in my way,' he masterfully says with a look of passion roaring in his eyes, 'and I will keep on doing it until my enemies see, that through time it will be realised that their is only one true darkness, El Diablo,' he roars as his eyes light up a red color. Angel jumps back in her seat at this sight. The power in his eyes die down, El Diablo appears like a man possessed. A man possessed by choice. 'Asag has chosen me,' he states in an unfamiliar voice, 'he was the earliest dragon legend and I will make sure that he is the last,' he preaches with pride, '7000 years of waiting to be free again, and his destruction will reign once more,' finishes Diablo in a sick voice. A look of horror crosses Angel's face. El Diablo again goes back to reading up on his Babylonian history and will no doubt educate her and the world some more on it and his plans.
