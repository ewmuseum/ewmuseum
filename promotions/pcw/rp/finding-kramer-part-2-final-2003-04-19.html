Finding Kramer (part 2, final)

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 19, 2003 The scene opens up and Milo and Kramer are still having a stare down. Milo grows rather restless and steps away from Kramer grabbing his side, shaking his head in disbelief. “After everything I just said you still wouldn’t let her know? She’s deeply involved with your life all by a huge mistake, and the thing she needs to know most you can’t share? That’s just ridiculous!” he sputters out from clenched teeth. “How do you think I feel? You think I wanted to drag her in this far? Sure I could have kept going when we went to the diner, but I didn’t. There was something about her that let me know I could trust her. That’s why I left Grimer with her, because I knew he’d be safe. Had I known that they would track her down I would have kept trucking. For that I’m sorry.” all of this anxiety makes Kramer slightly light headed so he takes a seat on a shelf cupping his head in his hands. Milo looks at him in his frail state and realizes that he does want to tell Brianna everything about himself, but something is keeping him from doing it. Milo lets out a sigh as he walks towards Kramer taking a seat right beside him. “I’m sorry for yelling at you like that…” Milo says sympathetically. “I know why you did it. Brianna’s like a sister to you and you’re just looking out for her. As much as I want to tell her, I just can’t. It would make her and my life a lot harder, thus it must be kept a secret.” Kramer looks down at his wristwatch and a look of regret crosses his face. He stands up brushing some dust from his backside and starts searching in his pockets growing very nervous with every passing second. “What’s wrong?” Milo asks with a raised brow as he rises into a stand up position. Kramer continues to check his pockets for several more seconds until he pulls out an envelope, which he throws at Milo. Milo doesn’t see it coming in time, and it winds up floating to the floor. “Please give that to Brianna.” Milo bends down to pick up the envelope and sees Brianna written on the front in big red lettering. “Why can’t you just…” Milo looks up as he is talking and notices that Kramer is no longer in the room. He looks around but sees no signs of him. Milo is a bit taken back by the quickness of Kramer dodging out of the room and walks out and back up to the counter. “Did you see a man rush out of that room?” He points to the “Employee’s Only” door and the woman shakes her head which confuses Milo even more. How did Kramer make it out so quickly, and why did he have to leave? Milo stands there in line just staring at the wall until an impatient old lady yells at him for cutting in line and just standing there. Milo moves out of the way giving her a mean look as he leaves the bar. “I can’t believe I found him….imagine the odds.” Milo tells himself as he starts walking down the street with the letter clutched tightly into his right hand, and the Snapple in the other. The scene turns to black, but quickly reopens inside of Brianna’s room, where she is busy packing her things for the Spring Fling pay per view. She rummages inside her closet, pulling out a pair of black boots with red flames along the bottom. She throws them onto her bed, and reenters her closet pulling out a black PCW duffle bag, which joins the boots on top of her bed. She closes her closet doors and sits in the chair near her desk, checking herself out in the mirror, not looking pleased at all. “In just one day I have one of the biggest matches of my career, and I look like total and utter hell! That’s great just great.” She’s referring to her frizzy hair, saggy eyes, and all around sickly appearance. The more she thinks about it, the more depressed she feels. Brianna snaps out of it momentarily as the doorbell rings downstairs. “Who could that be?” she asks herself as she grudgingly gets out of the chair and makes her way downstairs. She tries her best to put on a fake smile as she opens up the door, to see that her efforts were wasted as it’s just Milo. “I didn’t mean to make you come all the way down here. I couldn’t find my key for the life of me.” Milo throws out, knowing that it took a lot for her to drag herself downstairs. She gives him a small smile telling him it wasn’t a problem. That’s when Milo remembers the letter in his hand and pushes it in front of Brianna’s face catching her off guard. “What’s that?” she asks taking it from him. “Just open it up. I’m sure it’ll cheer you up. Well I have to go Bri, I’m supposed to meet Mat for dinner and a movie. I will be back before you leave.” He walks over and gives her a light peck on the head, exiting the house. Brianna looks down at the envelope and notices the handwriting, which fills her with excitement. Her frown flips and a huge smile comes across her face as she jumps on the couch and opens it up greedily. Dear Brianna,I know it’s been a long time since I have gotten in touch, and for that I’m sorry. You wouldn’t believe how much I’ve missed you and Grimer. You’re one of the greatest friends anyone could ever ask for, and I wish so much that I could just open up to you and tell you everything about my life. If I did however, it could jeopardize things. I’ve already dragged you through a lot and don’t want to make things harder for you. I just wanted to let you know that I am all right, and that hopefully I will be able to talk to you face to face again. Be sure to tell Grimer I said hi. Hope to talk to you soon….Kramer Brianna sets the letter down on her lap as the smile remains on her face. “This couldn’t have come at a better time.” A bigger look of glee overcomes her as she starts to think about the big mystery match at Spring Fling. Knowing that Kramer is fine and still kicking, takes away the huge amount of weight from her shoulders. No longer does she have to worry. Now she can focus fully on something that she has wanted for a while now, the Extreme Title. The scene slowly fades to black…
