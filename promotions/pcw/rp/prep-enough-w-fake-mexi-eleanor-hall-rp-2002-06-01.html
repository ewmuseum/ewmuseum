'Prep Enough' w/ FAKE MEXI!!! (Eleanor Hall RP)

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/06/2002 Wrestlers are all for newbies paying dues.... They have worked hard their entire life.... In Gyms, on the independent circuit, and then in the big Feds, working their way up from the bottom to the spots they would say they deserve..... Hence, they don’t react well when someone comes in that could “Steal their spot” as it were. Recently PCW has had an influx of new talent... Men, Women, Tag Teams.... All of whom will spread through the card.... But despite their hard work at the PWA and all the hoopla surrounding their signings.... It is the debut of someone entirely different that is making headlines.... For on Tuesday PCW will see the singles debut of Xavier Kannon’s valet Eleanor Hall... Now, Eleanor isn’t even a wrestler... She has never been to PWA and she suddenly finds herself higher up the card than those who have spilt their blood and broken their bones for a PCW roster spot.... You would think the least she could do was learn some basics? Well, as it said, you’d “Think” she would.... And thinking isn’t something that comes into your head when Ellie~!’s name is mentioned. “COME ON ELLIE~! YOU WANT TO WRESTLE? YOU WANT TO STEP INTO THE ULTIMATE AUTHORITY’S RING AT TURMOIL AND FIGHT? THEY YOU HAVE GOT TO SHOW ME YOU CAN!!!“ Our shot opens up in the Ultimate Authority gym at the secret U.A headquarters hidden in the basement of the PCW offices in New Jersey... Well, now you know where they are.... But you can’t come in... Kannon can because he owns 20% of PCW.... And you don’t... Unless you are P.I or Nelson... Anyway, on with this.... ....In the full size PCW ring we see wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon, clipboard in hand, whistle in mouth and towel around shoulders talking... Well, motivationally bellowing at his Preppy valet Ellie~!... Who on Tuesday will walk into a ring just like this to take in Jaden... ... Although the Prep wanted a match, and is even excited at the prospect.... This training doesn’t seem to be inspiring her much as she stands with her hands on her hips and a bored look in her eyes which are rolling back. Eleanor : “What-EVER!!!“ The Prep, dressed in a Black vest and Grey jogging bottoms looks back in defiance at Xavier, the wrestling tutor who sighs and drops his clipboard and takes the whistle from his mouth before trying to reason with the apathetic Prep... Well, more like plead... Xavier : “Eleanor.... If you are going to avoid being killed by Jaden you at least need to learn the basics... Try that Hammerlock thing I showed you... That was easy and takes very little effort!” She is now checking her nails, holding her hands right up to her face and paying a lot more attention to them than she is giving to her fiancé who is despairing across the ring from her. After about 10 seconds of viewing them from any and all angles she looks to Xavier, almost as if she didn’t hear what he was saying. Eleanor : “Can I, like, do the bouncing against the rope thing again? That SO ruled!” Kannon drops his head and starts to shake it, muttering to himself before looking up to Ellie~! who is excitedly waiting his order to bounce off the ropes over and over again. Xavier : “No..... You can’t bounce off the ropes.... In your match against Jaden you can’t do that or she’ll clothesline your head off.... Which may mess up your hair by the way....” She looks confused, trying to comprehend. Eleanor : “But, like, aren’t you going to be the ref though? How can I lose if you won’t count for her....” The Prep then scowls at him, hissing quietly. Eleanor : “... You WON’T count for her....” Xavier : “Of course I’m not going to count for her, I’m not a registered referee so I am not subject to their rules, regulations, code of ethics or the voice in their head telling them they have to be fair... But Ellie~!, if the match is simply going to consist of me X-Bombing her and you covering.... I might as well be fighting the fucking He-She Man Beast myself.” At Xavier’s words, his fiancé starts to fidget, stomp her feet and then talk in her extra annoying, super whiny voice that is saved for occasions just like there. Eleanor : “But I wanna give her ugly, masculine face the Prep Slap Of Doom~!” To this comment, Kannon’s face goes blank as he takes in his valet’s words. Xavier : “The.... Prep..... Slap...... Of.... Doom~!?” She nods, a happy grin on her face. Eleanor : “Well, now I have a match I decided I needed to learn all the important things so I don’t look bad.... So I started thinking about theme music and a finisher.... I’m torn on the music but I, like, SO have a finisher.... It’s a Slap..... It’s Preppy..... And it’s like, SO OF DOOM!” For a brief second, the Gold Patron Meritorious thought Ellie~! was finally beginning to realise she needs a bit of work on her ring technique..... But alas, Eleanor will be, and was Eleanor... Xavier : “Okay..... So what is so Preppy and “Of Doom~!’y” about this slap?” Eleanor : “Well... I, like, stand there or something.... Then make a joke about her awful hair, or her manly face, or her hairy top lip or her second hand-esque clothes... Then, like, REALLY hard... I SLAP her!” Despite her enthusiasm for the move, Kannon doesn’t seem to quite “Get” the principle behind the finisher. Xavier : “So it’s you being you then slapping her?” Eleanor : “Yup... That’s about it.” The Scientologist shake his head and sighs, wondering if it is time to bring out his ace on the hold.... His secret weapon... Plan B.... The one thing he has left to get his valet to wrestle. Xavier : “I only wanted him here as a last resort..... But I think we have reached that point...” The doors fly open and light floods the dimly lit Gym.... A figure is silhouetted as the doors stay open behind him and he strides valiantly.... Well, trundles forward until he stands directly under one of the hanging lights.... Revealing the grotesque form to be none other than.... FAKE MEXI!!! The huge Black belly that hangs over his ill fitting trousers wobbles from side to side with each waddle he takes forward.... An act that is copied by his multitude of chins that hang down from the bottom of his perpetually bemused face. Eleanor : “The wha?” The big, Red afro wig is illuminated under the light before the giant, flab encased Black man that is Fake Mexi stumbles under the weight of himself and rolls into the ring, his icky body mass squashing itself onto the mat before he strains himself, pulling his lardy form to it’s feet. The Prep runs her eyes up and down the man mountain of lardonia before her, a disgusted look on her face. Eleanor : “Ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwzies!!!” Kannon looks at the flabmonster, almost as if he is proud of the Red-wigged freak of fat nature... Xavier : “Now.... THIS is Jaden.... Think of hi-....He-.... IT as your opponent.... Fake Mexi is your Jaden, now.... Beat him...” The Prep is still running her eyes up and down the man, from the Red afro to the saggy face, to the huge belly down to his podgy legs. Her eyes then fix on his chest, his t-shirt tightly pulled against it. Eleanor : “Wow.... His breasts are actually more feminine than Jaden’s.... The rest of him isn’t far behind either!” Kannon grins as he sees his valet is starting to take a vague interest. Xavier : “Now do your Preppy worst.....” He pats Fake Mexi on the back, the immense fat reserves on the Black man’s back slapping into the Scientologist’s hand before Xavi walks to the corner and Ellie~! stands there looking at the big, wobbly, fat Mexi rip-off. Eleanor : “Take....... THAT!” With all the power of a ZX-Spectrum 128k.... That has been un-plugged.... Ellie~! swings a weak, open handed slap to the Fake Mexi’s gigantic belly causing it to resonate in pulsing waves that almost hypnotise Ellie~! Eleanor : “That is, like, SO cool!!!” Again they sends a weak, girl throw-esqe, slap onto the bemused Mexican’s belly and watches it shake.... And shake.... And wobble..... And shake to her enjoyment as Fake Mexi looks around, puzzled as usual... Fake Mexi : “Ghur?” The excited Prep then does it again and again, sending in little girlie slaps from left and right that smack onto his stretched skin or under-sized t-shirt, pulsating his flab. Eleanor : “Wrestling rules..... he’s like a big fat jelly!” The perplexed Fake Mexi turns to look over at Xavier who is standing in the corner, his head in his hands, not even looking at his valet slapping the huge belly with gusto. “Ghur?” As the excited sounds of Ellie~!’s frenzied slapping and the bemused mumbling of the Mexi wannabe are heard, we see Xavier’s troubled Green eyes as he mutters to himself... Xavier : “When Fake Mexi is your last resort and even THAT doesn’t work you know something is wrong....” The shot starts to fade to Black on his shaking head... Xavier : “Looks like Jaden is getting an X-Bomb!” We fade to Black on his defeated face as the happy voice of Ellie~! is hard in it’s all so preppy way through the darkness/ “Wobbling, like, SO rules!”
