...beckoning, the mind plays tricks on you...

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-3-02 'Rudeness is the weak man's imitation of strength.' 'This place might be the exact place to set out camp in the night.' ~[The Voice of Drake Maxwell]~ ::.Call it a gift from the thought of what to do in your life, but I've decided to starry off into the wilderness in search for something to my liking in the future. The darkness and the nights light falls upon me, the trees sway gently in the absent glory. A thunderbolt cuts through the clouds landing outward in the gavity and dampness of the clouds, light fires begin on the horizon as the cackle of fire upon the marsh continued to blaze. The eyes of the wanderer raise upward and down as my head rests softly on the tree branch. It was soft, yet honey like in essence as I let my ears do the talking towards the sounds as the crickets chirped loudly and freely without question a sound beep etched from their lips as the owls hooted with buldging eyes. Not in one step did it culminate without a great hush, before starting over again. In a whim, that silence had to be broken by someone's claws raking against the grass, causing my eyes to awaken to an appeasing look of interest, a look of searching where was the noise coming from. Lurking deep in the woods, an ogre laughs his mighty laugh..:: 'Argh, that last rack wasn't nearly as good as you promised!' he bellowed out with his meaty voice. 'So? What's wrong with this one, it looks no different than the last kind you picked up from the gypsy last time Strykar so don't blame the dog!.' the ogre's confidant walking along side of him as he shrugged about the trait of the goods he has taken in. 'You know Akarr, since you've gone blind, our choice of the latter has been nothing but worser and worser!' Strykar told him as he lay his large green body onto the trunk of a tree, obviously tired from the long walk. ::.Coming up behind him arguably a good twenty feet as he dragged his feet along the floor, constant scratching of his hooves was a nail like fixture along wood. I don't think I even anticipated but a canine, walking on his hind legs, back legs for those who cannot understand that. White in color and a black on his nose, his eyes were fire red, complete in package as one bad mofo who looked straight out of a trippy weed indulgence period..:: 'Si! Aye carumba senor, much timo por favor' the dog blutered out with a sense of trying to be humorous, and I think I loss all trained thought that I knew one things. ::.Most dogs speak English. I know you're saying I'm quite odd for saying this but bear with me, the dog spoke not so fleuently, but with some drunken rambling towards it so it happened to irk the giant at some point in time. He patted his head downward and continued on about his business. I shook my head without no reconnaissance and laid my head down against the tree, the fire cackling at a shortened pace, but longer once the rising from the wind began. Dreams are something we all have either they are for a yearn for a certain object in which you want or maybe even just for kicks you dream to make the blankness of hours transpire go a little bit faster. Knowing inside of wrestling, there are boyhood dreams of becoming the best among the best. The cream of the crop, that has come for Jay Sylo and I give him the congratulations for it. Yet when it comes to single comparrisons, there are no reasons to yearn for something larger in friendly compeition and Tuesday night is no different. I have no titles, just victories over the mind and peoples pride's in my hand. Is this a point to prove during the course of time that dreams are better left in the sleep? I guess so, I mean for my time to be worth it, some sheep have to be lead to slaughter and Sylo is the original slaughter. I dreamt, for the ways of playing games with some people were over. .:: [:: He found himself stuck within those illumination of lights, the feeling of sinking death among air conditioned pillars. The light swings over his head, the pitch blackness around him and a small child's crying voice, never does he actually care for it, but in a sense his mind happens to be upon it. The mirrors surrounding him weren't all the same, mostly appleasing towards common looks and gazes, but were more of a circus atmosphere. One showed off his look of being a short, plump man lacking definition whatsoever. Another showed him as a tall wide man who walked around like the Stay Puffed Marshmallow Man from the original Ghostbuster's film. He walked away from that, glazing over towards another room. ::] [:: Inside of the room, he saw nothing but an oasis of collided waterworks, his mind's own interworkings had conceieved a painting that he called Path: Untitled. The water steered clear towards it own side in a round pool as a woman, much to the image of one Amy Falan stands over the water, like Jesus and Peter walking on water. She stands with a heavenly sash over her body, the white dress showing her pure soul as the tree's are set ablaze and the swirling winds have caused the winds behind the image of Amy to circle in a funnel shape. Drake turns his head back to the center workings of the room, and sits downward on the floor, below his black sweat pants are the beating of speaker functions, thumping harder and harder towards the sounds of clashing swords. ::] [:: Thinking to himself, Drake thought that the sounds were to encourage him in the point of violence when it was needed. He barely balanced it into constant thought of his mind for really if he had then his soul would have the constant feel of darkness flowing through it, cutting the pure away. He says nothing towards it and continues to reach his mind, taking headphones set on a visually set placement in the thickness of oxygen and sets them on his ears. The thumping sounds of instruction causes him to lay down with his eyes open in trained thought. There is complete silence, only by the singing sonnet voice gaining its passage through the tunnels and bridges connecting the parts to which instruction are given. It was his own self meditation. ::] 'Drake...' [:: The voice of a male, deep and without fear steps into his mind, creaking and breaking through the barrier of restriction that causes somethings to be heard and not to be. He blinks his eyes only once, repeating this process every second. ::] 'Having a bad day? I know you aren't.' ::. 'You're the voice aren't you? That of which my mind is supposed to learn from prior mistakes.' .:: [:: The voice says nothing at all, causing Drake to sit up from his crouching position and stands upward blinking his eyes once more, letting his dry mouth feel the cool breeze of the air conditioned air. His eyes are dilated all in one single blink of the eye from complete silence to that of a large surreal feeling, that for something Drake cannot get out of his mind the saturated air feeling of being out there, in front of a crowd entertaining them. ::] ::. 'What is this?'.:: 'This is what you were doing, thinking and feeling all about a month, thirty days ago. This was your life wasn't it for I know it was. But now, it does nothing but seemingly play out of your life in nothing, just you're bored with everything else, like this is your life.' ::. 'Yeah, I know that and don't even cut that away from your memory. I was and still are a player in this game, they haven't taken me out.'.:: 'So?' ::. 'Doesn't that mean anything to you? I have no fear, I'm vulnerable to failure and yeah, I accept that with something new sooner along the way, you voices are like broken records. Never having a point...' .:: 'Still isn't getting to you is it?' ::. 'Is what not getting to me?'.:: 'You've been living in a false reality. A computerized game of some sorts created to entertain the minds of young teenage adults from spending time on the internet looking at diry things.' *** [:: His kunckles went white onto the handle of the steering wheel, the motor speed through the asphalt ripping away rubber from the ground as the Toyota continued its run. Wheeling inward and outward of on coming traffic like an olympic slaom skier, Drake let his emotions run wild as he sped downward into whatever. To say that his upcoming trip was going to be all smiles and nice movements was an understatement to say the least. He felt that urge, the surge of adrenaline running through his veins to try something daredevil style, but he didn't for better use of judgement. He slowled down his pressure on the accelerator and began to breathe. Amy sat right on the side of him, with her arms folded and her eyes accompanied on the upcoming and zipping by road.::] Amy: So why are we going to a shrink again? Drake: Purpose? Is trying to pick up some sanity on things a good idea? Amy: Well, its sorta a good case to dwell out on a psychologist for answers that you know you can answer. Drake: Hold up... [:: The brakes pull upward causing Drake up inch forward before the car jerks backwards and places him back with his leaned wall against the seat. He looks over at Amy with eyes attentive.::] Drake: Are you saying, I'm going for no reason at all? Amy: It's a possibility afterall, but did Sigmund Freud ever solve any one's problems? [:: Drake looks at her dumbfounded::] Amy: Well, did he? Drake: Of course he did, he solve some people's paranoia! How do you think they developed the term psychologist before Freud?All champions are like this, from Tag Team, Rising Star all the way up to Continental. Their knowledge fails once they become champions! Freud, he was the first head doctor they ever went to and I think he did a darn good job, better than some people you have today that lie, when the truth is something they can't comprehend! Scientology can tell you that, if you even look at the religion, you'll see its business oriented and Kannon's a non believer! Amy: You're kidding! Drake: If I were kidding then Kannon is telling the truth that he is the next Continental champion. In all actuality, I don't think Kannon can function without gold, it makes him sleepwalk. [:: A eerie sound that of a organ playing goes through the scene causing Drake and Amy to look around for whatever or whoever is playing that note to atleast stop because they aren't doing a very good job whatsoever. Drake just shakes his head and continues onward towards the doctor's office, pulling into the parking lot and shutting off the ignition. As he steps out of the car, a light breeze kicks up, blowing dead leaves into the air. Drake's coat flows through this as he continues to walk forward into the office pushing through the double doors and walking into the receptionist area. He glances at a few people, a plain looking woman whose eyes were an off beat blue color, a man standing straight up looking into the wall for nothing, and a child asleep on his arm. Drake says nothing to them and pushes a button on the elevator panel for it to come down to him and greet his service. ::] [:: Inside as the doors swing open and all there is solemnly is the gold framed bars to keep people inside , the freshly abstract painted wall, and the number coded panel. Drake looks down, before looking back up and seeing a host of people clamoring to fit inside of the elevator. Within such a rush, it causes Drake to change his mind. ::] Drake: I think I'll keep to the stairs. Voice from inside the elevator: Oh you don't want to walk so far up, the offices are seven flights upward. [:: Drake pushes himself forward to get into the elevator just as the doors are closing. ::] Drake: Phew! And I thought hospitals are hell. Woman: Wait till you see where you're going, it's worse. Drake: I don't like the sound of that for a reason. Woman: What, I thought you feared nothing. [:: Drake turns to look at the woman talking to him and spots her a few inches from his chin. A skinny pale woman with a high cheekbone and visably flat chest looks up at Drake in the oddest of manners causing Drake to move his feet backwards into another person, this time a man. ::] Drake: You got one of those things that help? You know, advice? Man: Hey bub, down here. [:: Drake's head pans downward to see a small wee man standing next to the larger man whose face is blank with no expression whatsoever. Drake looks at the smaller man, whose eyes seem nonexistent and his head a bit larger than his body. ::] Drake: He's the muscle and you're the talker right? Or do I have it different? Small Man: You have it different. You see, I am a medical miracle in fact, I was an abortion living inside that infernal womb and thought for death, but I lived and NOW THEY WILL PAY! [:: Drake looks at the man with an odd grin on his face, before turning back and looking at the doors of the elevator, staying the same while the car travels upward on balancing cable wires. He could have said out loud that the walking abortion could join the circus, but he knew it would be wrong to say that. The walking abortion starts to lift his head upward into the abysmal carbon air and suck it directly towards his lungs, before pulling outward of his mouth, a stick aflame. ::] Small Man: Damn thing, takes a while to get saturated in your mucus, but it burns!It is the only thing keeping me alive. Drake: By having a large stick shove up your ass? Small man: How did you know that? [::Drake smiles to himself with a laughing grin on his face. ::] Drake: Call it a guess. [:: With a smile on his face, Drake tugs on his jacket revealing a Red Wings hockey jersey with the name: Shanahan sewn into it and black jeans. His hair is different from what people usually are accustomed to seeing it as, the color of three as Drake has turned it back into its original style of jet black. As the elevator call doors pull open, he steps out and smiles casually at the assistant outside of the office who tells Drake to sit down on the burgundy colored couch stationed a few inches away fromthe door, leading into the office. Drake licks his lips and closes his eyes, before hearing the words... ::] Woman: Mr. Maxwell, the doctor is ready to see you.
