Title: Advice for the Lovelorn...gone horrifically wrong. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 30th Theatrics. They seem to the calling card, the modus operandi of Politically Incorrect as of late. They starred in their own big budget feature film. And now, they are in...... Kentucky. Even the SOUND of the WORD Kentucky makes Reginald V. Lovecraft and William S. Powell want to retch. Kentucky is, after all, the Jerry Springer capital of the world. Where the hell ELSE do you think they find all the sister screwing transsexual freaks? But no, it is not to the Jerry Springer show they head, but rather to a local program. It's title? 'Advice for the Lovelorn.' Why would Politically Incorrect agree to do this? They want to help out their good friend Rent-A-Hero.....errr, Rent-a-Villain. Getting over Amy, after all, would be a great start to his new villainy. So turn on your TVs! Change the channels! And get ready for some Advice for the Lovelorn. Oh, and it will be... POLITICALLY INCORRECT. I guarentee it! ::A small local TV station in Kentucky is the site for today's TV programming. Both members of Politically Incorrect sit on screen, immaculately dressed as always. They smile, as they appear to be awaiting something from off stage.:: Producer: 10 seconds! 10 seconds! ::They nod, and count down in their heads. As 10 seconds expire, a cutesy introduction theme plays, as 'Advice for the Lovelorn' fades onto the screen, a broken heart being used to dot the 'i'. A helpful chyron flashes on the screen, with 'William S. Powell' and 'Reginald V. Lovecraft' below the respective person on screen.:: William S. Powell: Hi, I am William S. Powell. Reginald V. Lovecraft: And I am Reginald V. Lovecraft. Welcome to Advice for the Lovelorn. Your regular host Ima Slute is out with a severe bout of herpes right now, so we're taking over for today! William S. Powell: We're going to start off taking calls today....first caller, you're on Advice for the Lovelorn. :;A caller comes in:: Caller: Hi guys, I'm Joe...22 years old, and from Baton Rouge, Louisiana. There's this girl I know....and I really like her, but I don't know how to introduce myself.... ::Powell smiles, confident he can answer the question: William S. Powell: Don't worry Joe...I can help, I'm going through a similar situation myself. ::Powell pulls a picture from his wallet. It is of, you guessed it, the Neo Void God's Divine Prophetess, Cyclonica. Lovecraft looks, and immediately groans, putting his head into his hands, undoubtedly cursing the fates for landing him with Mr. Powell:: William S. Powell: Now, Joe, the first thing you do when you realize that you like someone is assess your goals. What this means is you must determine what you want out of the relationship. In my case, I am trying to convince Cyclonica to accompany me home for an evening of passionate sex. You may be attempting to get your girl to go on a date with you, or be your girlfriend. Mileage may vary... ::The look on Lovecraft's face is one of utter shock.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: ARE YOU AN IDIOT?! She helped Akiyama kick the living hell out of us on Fusion! He gave you the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse onto a chair, and I was really hoping that it would knock some sense into you when your head hit the damn thing. William S. Powell: Yeah, but we got revenge on him later! Reginald V. Lovecraft: That was only because you, being the dumbass that you were, went to FLIRT WITH CYCLONICA after she set us the fuck up! William S. Powell: I can't help it! That outfit ruled! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And then what happened? William S. Powell: She touched me! Reginald V. Lovecraft: TOUCHED YOU?! SHE KICKED THE CRAP OUT OF YOU! You're lucky I sent Schultz out with that key, or her nail imprints would still be in your neck! William S. Powell: That wasn't that bad actually....I like a girl who plays rough... Reginald V. Lovecraft: I HAD TO SAVE YOUR ASS FROM A VOID BANISHMENT! AKIYAMA WOULD HAVE TRINITY BOMBED YOU TO THE VOID IF I DIDN'T HIT HIM WITH THAT CHAIR! William S. Powell: Ah well, I didn't get banished, you got Furman v. Georgia, I got Election Day, and we, thanks to Totally Tubular Tom, got Akijobber with the Executioner's Chair! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And Cyclonica AGAIN tried to kill us, but Roger came in, AND WE DROPPED THE CELESTIAL DIVINE WHATEVER-THE-FUCK SHE IS WITH THE THIRD PARTY! William S. Powell: Why did we have to do that to such an....such an....angel? Reginald V. Lovecraft: YOU'RE FUCKING DEMENTED! She's a dangerous human being! And, for that stunt she pulled on us on Fusion, I'm going to DESTROY HER! William S. Powell: Mr. Lovecraft, I have to respectfully ask that you let me deal with this situation how I may. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Joe, I apologize, but my partner is talking babble....let's just cut the show off now. :The director calls 'cut!', as William S. Powell takes off, dragging a cameraman with him. Lovecraft just sits, incredulous.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I've got an Extreme Title Match, and a tag title shot, and he's worried about engaging in sexual activities with a Divine Prophetess. THIS IS STARTING TO GET ME REALLY ANNOYED! Oh well..in that Trailer park brawl, I'm going to annihilate my five opponents, and then go into that tag match, and, whether or not Powell is fighting, I will take everyone in that ring and throw them over the top rope. Politically Incorrect WILL be two time tag team champions, with my partner's assistance, OR WITHOUT! ::Lovecraft smiles, a smile unbecoming of him.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: For, Sunday is Election Day. Politically Incorrect always wins on Election Day. You know the drill.
