Aura of Light and Shadows

Date: not entered The horror of it all, El Diablo remains lifeless despite the life in his blood red eyes, and in front of his motionless body, this strange bright light, like an aura is there. It isn't a person as there is no human features to it at all, it is just a bright light that came out from El Diablo the second he roared in some sort of agony. Angel is watching on in horror at the recent occurrences, she of course has no idea what to do as tears start running down her face. Meanwhile, the Babylonian book is open on a page on the floor that must have fallen from El Diablo when he got to his feet. Angel stretches her legs out to pull it towards herself, trying not to disturb the strange aura. The page it is open on is the first Tablet, and under a part where it says 'Illegible lines' there is a spot of blood, it must be El Diablo's blood. And seen dripping from his finger, which hangs over a side of the chair, is blood dripping from his index finger. But why would blood on the book cause that? The aura remains in the room doing nothing in particular, it is just there in front of El Diablo's lifeless body, as Angel watches in confusion and fear at the strange happenings occurring right before her. Suddenly the aura begins to move, it starts circling El Diablo's body slowly at first and gradually speeds up to a great force, the force of it begins blowing paper over and then books, the power of it gets stronger and stronger each cycle around El Diablo until again it stops in front of him, still lifeless. Angel stares at what is happening with lots of confusion and still sees her husband motionless but most importantly lifeless. His hands move slowly and gradually his body begins to move again, he stands up into the aura, and his blood red eyes fixed on Angel. 'Don't fear,' he exclaims, 'The host is still alive,' it explains. By the way he is talking it seems that El Diablo is not in control of body and that something else is. El Diablo could indeed be possessed. The aura of light still surrounds El Diablo's body, as it stands, not looking like it normally does, he is stood on tiptoes, near levitating. 'Quit joking,' Angel says in some shock, obviously thinking that El Diablo is playing a trick on her. However, she still watches on mortified. 'I assure you, that my presence is no joke,' the being enlightens her, 'I am here to see this world that you all see today,' it claims. Angel watches in confusion still, and in shock at seeing her husbands' body being used by somebody besides her. 'Are you for real?' she asks in fear. El Diablo's eyes flash a deeper shade of red, and then back to the blood red. 'Of course I'm for real,' the being states, 'I'm seeing if my host is worthy and if you are worthy,' it claims. His body has remained still for it's entirety, and Angel has been shaking ever since it appeared with tears still running down her face at this sickening sight. 'Is that you all you want?' she asks, this creature before her. She views it as a creature as it is no longer her husband at this point in time, and that is what scares her most. 'I want many things,' it proclaims. Angel gulps in tremendous fear. 'Who are you?' she asks curiously. The aura remains around El Diablo's motionless body as Angel stares on in complete and utter disbelief. 'I am the demon within this host, I am all that is evil within,' he states in an unfamiliar voice. She looks frightened at what is occurring right before her. It would appear that Asag, Gallu and Namtar are what is controlling El Diablo right now, the three demonic structures that he carries within have surfaced, and maybe not for the first time. His eyes roll back in his head and do a complete circle before re-appearing still looking blood red. 'I slew any man, and now my host will do all this for me,' it claims, 'I love the vision and smell of pure bloodshed,' it finishes in an evil voice. El Diablo's mouth opens as lots of blood begins to pour out from it, and it is not stopping, it is like the demon is trying to drain the host and kill it. 'STOP IT!' screams Angel loudly, 'STOP IT NOW!' she shrieks at the disgusting bloodshed. His blood stains the light blue carpet around El Diablo's feet. His mouth closes as blood stains his chin as well. 'We did as you wish,' the being speaks, 'but soon only the host will be able to command us,' it finishes. The sight that is being seen is quite sickening, the aura still surrounds his helpless body and Angel still has no idea why this is occurring and especially now. Angel keeps the Babylonian close to her and out of sight of the being in front of her. 'Terrible things will be unleashed,' it claims with a sick smile appearing on El Diablo's lifeless face, 'I am the new plague,' it states in a sadistic tone. The tears stop streaming down Angel's face, but are still very visible that she was crying. 'The plague starts now,' the being finishes. And on that last syllable, a loud and deafening sound can be heard within the confines of that room, the lights go out and an eerie, blood red glow comes from El Diablo, and some loud disturbing laughing is heard. Angel closes her eyes and ducks for cover as many objects in the room begin to fall towards the ground. Then in an instant the sound stops and the lights are back on, the evil laughing is over. The room is now in a large mess, El Diablo however is back in the chair, his eyes are his regular brown color and the aura that was here is now gone. Angel stares at El Diablo with complete disbelief. 'Are you ok?' she asks considerately to her husband. El Diablo looks at the blood stained carpet and then wipes his index finger across his chin, and tastes the blood with a sick smile on his face. 'Everything is falling into place, everything is as it should be,' says El Diablo with a look power and sadism on his face. Angel is still incredibly upset, but El Diablo seems to have got what he wanted all along. Now the sadism is alive and well within El Diablo.
