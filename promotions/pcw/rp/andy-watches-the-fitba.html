Andy Watches The Fitba'!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Is Today an option? The Match … Engand Vs. Nigeria. The Time … 7pm. The Place … The apartment of Andy Murray. Goodie! [Slowly our scene fades in to the apartment that is home to Andy Murray and his sidekick Rab, or to be more specific, Andy’s bedroom. The slug-like, sleeping form of Andy Murray lies tucked away under the “Glasgow Rangers” duvet cover, a digital alarm clock on the bedside table reads 7:28 in large, green illuminating … Stuff. The room is as poorly decorated as the living room, and the furniture; a chest of drawers with a TV resting on top, a large cupboard, the bed and the bedside table, are all in pretty poor condition. Ahhh, the joy of neds! The blue & white wallpaper is filled with posters of various Glasgow Rangers Football Club posters, featuring players from over the years … Brain Laudrup, Ally McCoist, Erik Bo Anderson, Jorge Albertz, etc. Suddenly, the door bursts wide open, as a hyperactive Rab, dressed in a red Adidas shell suit, bursts in, runs up to Andy’s bedside, and shake’s his shoulders violently.] Rab: ANDY! WAKE UP MIN! Andy Murray: Huh!? Wah!? [Andy sits up, rubbing the sleep from his eyes, before glancing at the alarm clock.] Andy Murray: Ya chube, what are you getting’ me up this early for!? Rab: Ye don’t remember? It’s tha England match the day. Andy Murray: England are playin’ fitba’ the day? Rab: Aye, it’s about tae start! Andy Murray: Pure, mad, mental man! [Rab darts out of the room, through to the living room area, as Andy quickly throws the RFC duvet to the side, and jumps out of his bed, dressed conveniently in a white & black Nike shell suit. Andy grabs a baseball cap from on top of the chest of drawers, and throws it on his head, before jogging through to the living romm area, and jumping into a chair. Rab, sits there, munching on a meal of delight from the local fish and chip shop.] Rab: Fancy a chip? Andy Murray: How d’ye get fish an’ chip thish early min!? Rab: I did’nae … I got it last night, just heated it up in the Microwave, min. Andy Murray: Mental … What ye got? Rab: Battered Mars Bar. Andy Murray: Geed on ya big man! [And reaches across, and grabs a couple of chips (fries), before leaning back in his chair, to watch the match, rooting, of course, for Nigeria. The team line-ups appear on the screen, firstly, the Nigerians …] Andy Murray: How they all got stupid names, min? Rab: Dinna ken min. Andy Murray: Baw bags. [Obviously, our favourite Glasweigians fail to realise that not everyone has a Scottish name. The English line-up appears on the screen next.] Andy Murray: Och, bunch a’ chubes min! Rab: Aye min. Andy Murray: Pawl Scholes? He could’nae hit a elephant’s arse wee a banjo. Rab: Trevor Sinclair, who’s that min? Andy Murray: Dinna ken min. [Andy and Rab sit silently, watching a pretty dull match overall. Julius Aghahowa hits a poor shot on goal that is easily picked up by David Seaman.] Rab: Pish. Andy Murray: Shite. [The match comes alive late on in the second half, as Michael Owen makes a golden run, slicing through the Nigerian defence, looking to score a wonder goal, only to be blocked out by a lucky Nigerian defender.] Andy Murray: HAHA! Ya wollaper! [A few moments later, Paul Scholes hits a thundering shot towards goal, only to have it deflected onto the post by the Nigerian Goalkeeper.] Rab: Thish is pure bangin’ min, these chubes are nae gonna score the day. Andy Murray: Aye. [The half-time whistle blows, Rab’s meal is long finished, and the two quickly get bored, after 10 seconds of the half-time break.] Andy Murray: Och min, this is pure hummin, I’m gonnae get a chippae! Rab: Mental! [Andy reaches into the left pocket of his trousers, and pulls out his Ned-O-Matic Mobile Phone, version 1.0. Andy holds in the ‘#’ quickdial button for the fish & chip shop, as the same, annoying worker that answered the phone two days ago, picks up.] Chip Shop Guy: Yes? How may I help you? Andy Murray: Oi you, ya’ chube, I’m pure wantin’ a chippae, and if ya dinnae give me it, I’m gonnae pure hammer you by the way. Chip Shop Guy: Are you the guy who phoned on Monday? I already told you, we aren’t open at this time of the morning. Andy Murray: How no? Chip Shop Guy: Because the demand for fish & Chips at this time isn’t exactly high, now is it? Andy Murray: I’m no wantin’ fish and chips, I’m wantin’ a battered Mars Bar, ya chube. [The chip shop worker sighs at Andy’s stupidity, you can hear him chuckling on the other end of the phone.] Andy Murray: What are ye laughin’ at? Chip Shop Guy: Nothing … Ya chube, ha! Andy Murray: Listen here ya toff, I’m gonnae come around- [Click. The chip shop guy hangs up, Andy jumps up from his seat, obviously he is angry.] Andy Murray: I’m gonnae go pure hammer that bampot! Be back soon! Rab: HOOF MAN! [Andy walks across the apartment, and heads off to the chip shop, to “pure hammer that bampot,” leaving Rab alone, to watch the football. We fast forward in time a little, to be precise, 50 minutes forward, Rab is still alone in the apartment, as the final whistle blows. Suddenly, the apartment door opens up, as Andy steps back in, looking just like he did when he left.] Rab: Did ye pure mash that baw bag up, min? Andy Murray: Och no, I got lost, I ended up some wee place called Toys ‘R Us min, and then this wee person, says he was 9, told me that the chube was in Argos, so I went there, and he was’nae there … Rab: Aye … Ye missed the fitba … Andy Murray: What was the score? Rab: 0 – 0. Andy Murray: Pish. [And … Fade!]
