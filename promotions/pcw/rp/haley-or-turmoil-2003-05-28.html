Haley or Turmoil?

28 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/03 “Matt…” the middle-aged man says, standing in the doorway of the operatives lavish office. Looking over papers on his desk, Jackson ignores the male voice coming from the male voice in front of him. “Matt,” the voice insisted with a bit of anger in it, from the ignoring that the agent is giving them. “I don’t have time for games,” he replies, not looking up, continuing to sit conformably in the leather. His brown hair spiked up, as usual and his grey suit fitting perfectly to his five foot eight inch frame. Silently he remains, his eyes scanning over the papers, not removing themselves, from the documents in front of him. This is his way of finding Haley, freeing her from the skinheads that are holding her hostage…his element of search and destroy. He is going to hunt down Manger, and when the time comes, he will pull the trigger and keep what is his. When he came into the PCW, he never thought things would get this out of control. He also thought he would never get the things he’s gotten…the fame, the title, and the record that he’s so close to achieving. Beating Kid Wonder’s 11-0, never looking back and not worrying…it’s all going to depend on Turmoil. This is where he makes his stand against James Edwards, a man who he has beaten three times before this…a man who dreams of being the first one to knock off Matt Jackson and take his title, claiming it as his own. After, three tries, one of which, was just a little under three days ago, Edwards thinks he can produce a new strategy, something to beat the new Raising Star Champion, something that he didn’t have three days ago. Now, he gets his fourth chance, one he’s not going to take lightly…one Matt’s ready for. It’s now the night before Wednesday Turmoil and Matt Jackson, is sitting alone in his office, trying to find the one piece of the puzzle that’s missing to him and that’s Haley Hilton…his one crowning jewel. She is the reason, that he’s where he is right now and she’s the reason he is going to keep his beloved Raising Star Title around his waist. James Edward’s has all the tools needed to carry the title to greatness, but his time isn’t yet…it’s not now. Everything has been building to the moment when these two would finally meet for the first time one on one…it’s something that hasn’t happened before this, but it’s something that people want to see. Everyone has been waiting for the moment; this would happen, but now it’s only a day away and then everyone’s questions will be answered…they will see the answer is Matt Jackson, there is a reason he is the Raising Star Champion and there’s a reason he’s undefeated and there’s a reason Edwards has fallen to him three times, before this. Looking at his Raising Star Title, that’s laid on the large oak desk top in front of him, he smiles, seeing the light reflect off of the gold face and the leather straps fitted to his waist, sitting there ever so ready for him to snatch it up and take it to Turmoil and defend it against his new found problem…James “Lunar” Edwards. The one man who actually has a chance to upset him. James Edwards, he thinks. You are the one man who can pin me in the center of that ring, but for some reason I don’t fear this fact…I embrace it, not worrying about your abilities, but trust in mine more…knowing that I can beat you again and continue my dominance over the Raising Star Division. There is one thing I don’t understand; why a man in your current position believes he can beat me. After everything that’s happened, you believe that you can stand toe-to-toe with me and take the title, the title I have so rightfully earned. A man such as Matt Jackson just doesn’t believe that he can just be brushed off and considered a win for James Edwards…not after everything that has happened so far in PCW. No matter what the reasons were, Edwards still lost the matches and the chances to claim Matt’s title, as his own, but in the end, the belt rests on Jackson’s desk and not Edwards and it seems to like it resting spot…not wanting to move anytime soon. It won’t…it can’t, it’s too soon since Matt won and he doesn’t want to go down in the record books for being one of the shortest reigning champions. Edwards is only doing this, because it’s his last stand…his final opportunity at greatness and that’s the reasoning behind him believing that he can beat Matt. It’s the only way that he can survive in the large pond that’s known as PCW. Eating the biggest new fish and making yourself the biggest, that’s what Edwards is trying to do…making himself look more determined then he really is, then he is going to be on Turmoil. Checkmate, James…I know now, Matt thinks, as he leans back in his black leather office chair…kicking up his feet onto the large oak top of the desk. Letting all his thoughts and emotions swirl into one huge storm inside, relaxing the few muscles in his body that he can. Soon it’s all going to be over and you will have nothing to worry about. Defending his Raising Star Title is just what he needs to keep his mind semi-controlled while he searches for his beautiful girlfriend. It’s also his opportunity to prove that he is more then just a man with a belt…it’s his chance to show that he is a champion. Too show that he is undefeated, because he has put in the time and the effort to getting to where he is…he’s not a fluke…he’s the champion! “Matt…we need to talk,” the middle-aged man speaks again, causing Matt to look up from his desk, just over the Raising Star Title, seeing the man, who is wearing a black suit, his wiry grey hair, just pushed to the side, encasing his temples, just enough not to give off a shadow, to ruin his perfectly anointed looking head. “Fine…” ~>…… This is going to be the man to save my daughter? Jason Hilton thinks, as his black opals focus on the young agent, who’s sitting in the large desk in front of him…Hilton sits silently in the small wooden chair, looking at Matt, who sits conformably in his office chair, kicking back in his seat, the smile crossing his angular face, matching his arms, which are crossed over his barrel of a chest. His facial expression is one of distain, as he looks over the desk at the masterful attorney…something the Chameleon rarely does. His look is always calm and cool, but now it seems like he’s divided, between finding his blonde beauty and beating Edwards…picking between the two things in his life he holds dear, not knowing, which one holds more value to him over the other. Everything in the PCW that he’s worked so hard for or Haley, that’s the choice…the salvation. Too must the answer would be clear-cut, but to Matt it’s not and Jason knows this, as he sits across from him, staring into his brown eyes, trying to find the answer he desires…hoping that Jackson is going to pick his daughter over the title, but in truth, he knows that Matt is going to try to do both. That’s why he’s not the Chameleon, but he’s Matt Jackson…the Raising Star Champion, a wrestler, just like Haley wanted…not the trained killer that Jason Hilton wants. ~><>…<><~ Matt made the choice long, before he gave it any thought tonight and he knows this, as he quietly sits in the leather seat of his midnight blue Dodge Viper…driving down the long dark road to the airport, considering all things and choices he made tonight, deciding that he would defend his title…face Edwards and hope that Haley is safe, knowing that Thomas Manger won’t do anything to harm her, it’s not in his nature and it’s not his plan. He wants the agent, not Haley…she will be safe…he hopes Driving down the black paved street, his face luminated by the dashboard lights, his eyes focused on the road, which is outlined by the full moon in the sky and his bright headlights. His mind is a blur, picturing the events of the past month, all his hardships…all his troubles, but at the end of each thought is Supremacy, when he held the Raising Star Title high, knowing that it was all over then…at least for the time being, not worrying about what’s going to happen next, just living in the moment. What’s going to happen next has come…it’s time is near, in just a few short hours, he will be defending his prize against James Edwards. A man he’s pushed out of his mind countless times, only seeing him as a mediocre threat instead of anything more, but he can’t do that anymore. Now it’s time that he is bringing the space man to his full attention. There’s something about Edwards, that makes him want to face him…he could have just handed him the belt and gone and searched for Haley, but instead he’s driving to Turmoil. Matt’s car continues to drive down the empty dark road, nothing, but farm land on either side of him, as his trek to Turmoil seems to be non-stop…getting closer to his first title defense, feeling his stomach start to tighten, as if a clamp had been placed on it and it was crushing him. He passes it off, considering it first time jitters, knowing that once he gets into that ring it will be all worn off and he will be able to defend his title in the way he wants…to keep it. There’s an emptiness inside of him though, and he would do anything to fill it in…wishing that it was different, he hopes that it will soon be gone, but his hopes fall on grey ears, it’s up to him to get out of this place…to get Haley back, something that he’s not going to rest on, until it’s done, but right now it’s not the main thing on his agenda and he can’t figure out why…why Edwards is the main thing on his mind and why a piece of metal is his only care. I understand you James, you hope that you can fulfill your dream of being a professional wrestler…by knocking me off of my throne and moving me down. Taking my place on top of the heap, but I can’t let that happen…I won’t let that happen, not now…this is my time and I am going to do whatever I have too…fighting to keep my position…to keep my title. That’s all that matters to me now. It’s the only thing that matters now, turning his back on his love to go to Turmoil. As he drives down the road, he still can’t believe the choice he made, he doesn’t understand it, but it’s the choice that he made…the only thing left is to try to understand the reasoning behind it, which he probably never will…even in beating Edwards, he won’t know why he’s in the ring. He will just know he’s there…going off of something else, some desire to hold onto his title a little longer. Too hold out beyond all hope, that he might just get the chance to beat Wonder’s record. None of these things should have bearing on his choices…it should be simple. No show Turmoil and save Haley, but it’s not that easy for him. He can’t just let this opportunity waste, he wants to add another win to the record and he wants to beat Edwards. He wants to be the only man to claim victory over him in a singles match, just as Edwards wants to do the similar, but he is missing the point. He doesn’t understand, that he’s running off nothing more then fear…hoping that in his last shot at the Raising Star Title, he can do the thing he’s failed at three times in the past. Claming a pin fall over the agent…pinning Kannon’s man, the one who was brought in based on his skill…Edwards is jealous of my accomplishments and hopes, that he can become a professional wrestler because of a win over Matt Jackson. Even if he does some how get lucky, it won’t change the fact…in the end he’s not the same, he’s not as good as me. It’s true, Edwards isn’t as good as Matt Jackson…he doesn’t have the record, the title, or anything else for that matter to back-up his claim for victory. Edwards is just to blind to take full blame for his pervious losses, chalking it up to someone else getting pinned by Matt instead of him, but when it all comes down to it, Edwards is in the challengers seat and not Jackson. This is my title; you are the one who has to take it from me…picking up where everyone else in the Raising Star Division couldn’t. Carrying the hopes and dreams of eight other people…including yourself. Hoping that you can beat me…taking something that doesn’t belong to you…this title is mine and it’s staying where it belongs. I hate you for making me pick between Haley and the title Edwards, Matt thinks, noticing how stupid his previous comment was, as soon as it started to echo through his thought filled mind. This wasn’t Edwards fault, it was his own for making the choice…a choice he is fine with making. Stepping on the break of the Viper, Matt quickly down shifts, letting the car slow down, before finally slamming on the breaks in full…leaving the car stopped dead in the middle of the empty road, his only friend is the Raising Star Title, which sits in the leather seat next to him…Haley’s seat. A lone tear falls down his cheek, as he lets his headrest against the padded steering wheel. What am I doing? He asks himself, looking back up at the road in front of him. Wiping the tears from his slender face, he puts the car back into first gear and continues down to the road to Turmoil. I’m going to defend my title…forgive me Haley
