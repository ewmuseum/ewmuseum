The Painful Past (Chapter 2)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/5/02 Revenge.Hate.Anger. They were three words I spoke of so much, but didn’t know a thing about them. I knew if I could have some type of emotion in me I could feel these words deep down in my heart as I spoke them. The night my mother lied there lifeless like some road kill I had the first real emotion I’ve ever felt. I had a moment where I was just pondering, “How could you do that”. I knew these men were animals, and people who do hateful crimes deserve to be punished, and by God I punished them. The lives of the two men didn’t mean much. They happened to be two pimps and my mother was a prostitute of there’s. The police recovered there bodies that night as I peeked over from a near a bush across the street. Right when we thought this city was being cleaned up we have more dirt bags and whores like these ones. Oh well at least there dead now. God I hate being called in the middle of the night just so I could discover some scum lying dead in the street. It should have no liked hearing those words they called my mother, but to me they were just words and these men actually spoke the truth. The next day I was off early and I arrived at the church to go to confession. Church was like a safe haven for me, whenever I felt lonely or like I needed someone the church would be there waiting for me. Bless me Father for I have Sinned it has been about one week since my last confession. Hello, my son what sins have you come here to tell me of? Well father I have done something awful. Something that even God himself couldn’t forgive me for. Tell me son, God is forgiving and hears your prayers. Father I have had the blood of men flow through my fingers in an act of rage. I have shown no remorse and feel but as if they had what they deserved. My poor child, are you telling me that you have murdered someone. Yes, my father but I know it is wrong. You know you must go to the police my child, for it is what God shall see as righteous. I can’t do that; I just can’t live a life of such restraints. I’m sorry but if you can’t, I’m obligated to do so. At that moment I ran out of the church in such a rush that I never saw the face of the priest. I knew it was a bad idea to tell a priest, but I had to so God would here them. Later that night rain began to fall and a storm was coming among us. Everyone was in their houses for shelter, so I used this time to return to the church. It was empty as I expected and I just sat there for hours pouring my soul out to the Lord. O good and ever so kind God, what shall you do with me. I am a fool among fools and I know that one day I will be punished. I then stood in silence and I stood in that silence for the next five years of my life. It was on a warm summer day where I was writing poetry in a journal of mine when I noticed an attractive young woman to the right of me. I looked at her and I smiled. She then nudged me on my shoulder and asked about what I was reading. I’m just writing some poetry for a café I read at. Would you mind if I read some. That’s how it all started. One thing went from another and then one day later I saw myself alone with her at her house. I never thought much of her, other than that she was a pretty girl and seemed to like my writing. Then she grabbed my leg and from there we began to make love. At that time I had so many things on my mind and so many problems that I was always rushing around and not enjoying life. For that small amount of time everything that was against me had disappeared and I felt completely cleansed. I didn’t so much love it for the perversion and lust, but more for the intimate and replenishing feeling. The next day came and I never saw her again. Within that year this happened a few more time, but I never turned it into something dirty or perverted. It was a way for me to release energy and I loved it. Plus, it was keeping me out of my other way of releasing energy, and that was fighting. Or at least it kept me out of fighting until Carlos Morales came along. One day I was at the park and I was going through some hard financial problems. Mr. Morales was holding an open audition for a fighting circuit in the park. He had one of his fighters present and guaranteed a contract to anyone who could pin him. The job guaranteed an amount of money I couldn’t refuse so I went for it. The fighter in the ring laughed at me, because at the time I was just about 5”9, but about one hundred and fifty pounds or so. I extended my hand to him and he spit on it, like I was not worthy of him. Then within an instance of a second I side kicked him straight in the chest throwing him out of the ring. Everyone in the park looked on in amazement as two minutes later he finally regained his breath. Lucky Bastard, now I’m going to get you. I just nodded, as he went to punch me I ducked and I then swiped at his legs. I followed it with a leg drop to the chest and a quick 1…2…3 followed. For me it was no big deal, but Mr. Morales loved me. It all happened so fast, that I never had time to actually think about what I was doing. Two weeks later I was in a ring battling for the top prize in the industry. And making his way to the ring from Barcelona is the Passionate Patriot El Romantico. I was so new to all of this and yet people loved me. I had fans from places I didn’t even know existed. I won that night and I was treated like royalty from that point on. The next few days were hard for me to cope with. I was no longer this alone simple man making it day by day. I was now a rich world-class fighter who Spain had loved and envied at the same time. Nothing was as overcoming as the woman. I’d have hundreds of woman at my door every night and sometimes I just didn’t know what to do. I felt bad about what I was doing and wondered if God would punish it for me. It’s not right for someone to be given so much, I should have to be doing more for what I deserve. Those first few nights I went sleepless and I repeated the same poem I had thought up of over and over. My mother gave birth to meMy father is a mysteryMy hands hold a wrathGod gave me a pathBut when I look backAnd I think of the pastIt gives me these chillsAnd for years they’ll last
