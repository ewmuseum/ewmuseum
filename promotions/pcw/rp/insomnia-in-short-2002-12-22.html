Title: Insomnia, in short. Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 22, 2002 My mind was fighting a war between anxiety and determination, parallel to that between the Trapezoid and myself, perhaps interdependent upon it....If my anxieties won, I would be endangered in greater sense, unable to wrestle to my full capacity and unable to defend myself sufficiently to avoid his wrath...This in itself created further anxiety, a rather vicious cycle of the growth of my negative energy...This wasn't working, this was just leaving me in worse position going in, laying on the bed at 2 in the morning, unable to sleep or even relax as I watched the promotional video for the match over and over.. 'HIT...Walden...No longer just victory, but a matter of survival....' ....Too true...I had to win. Why was I even being so bothered over this? It was obvious that I had to give it everything at this point, so I would undoubtedly do so. If I didn't, he could take advantage, he would win at the least...This was a war of attrition, both of us would lose in a sense of pain and suffering, but the losses were still not severe enough to be a deterrent from the greater hope that I could end this challenge to our authority, crush the HIT once and for all, make him a symbol of defeat for the rebellion... ...That sounded way too evil-overlord-ish. This was rather a high-stakes affair however, key to the momentum of the Front Office in the face of our opposition. The sinister shape had put too much effort into his fights to turn back now, and he wouldn't let up until he won or was unable to respond to defeat..I knew my job, I'd made my own bed with my arrogant contract signing and I was going to have to lie in it now..This was the moment of truth, but I wasn't sure if I was up to it, and this was my main fault...I couldn't step into the biggest match of my life underconfident, I knew I could beat him on his terms.. The Cruiserweight Tournament should've erased my doubts, I had gotten the better of him in a fair match and that was more than the public had expected, beyond their comprehension for the man who was fighting for what they perceived as right to fall...How could I make lightning strike twice? How could I make the veteran of the industry lose yet again to me when it mattered most? I had to leave behind my anxieties at the door, it was nearly the day of Decimation and without a decent rest, I would be unable to win... I grasped the remote control, shutting off the set. That would be enough to stop the influx of anxieties, the thought of survival being only a possibility was not helping me any when I needed grit and determination to win...I already had the essential rage within, I showed it by breaking the son of a bitch's arm for what he did to Brandi out of spite for me..I just had to expand it to the context of the match. I had to stay focused on beating him, I had to remember my training, all the maneuvers I'd learned from Saskatoon to America, around the world to my stint in Japan and back again...He was merely a difficult challenge to face, I'd near-proven my point but I would have to win this to prove it over once more...I could do this...I had all the inspiration I needed already, I just needed to put it to good use... Later. It would have to come later....I'd have to detach myself now from the situation at hand, just let the exhaustion take over and allow me the rest that might mean the difference between winning and losing... I'd think about what I could buy for myself...Though losing could kill me, if I ignored the thought then I'd be able to enjoy my dreams all the more.... There could be no pain in my dreams, and for that I was grateful... I'd need to absorb enough of it tomorrow anyway. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
