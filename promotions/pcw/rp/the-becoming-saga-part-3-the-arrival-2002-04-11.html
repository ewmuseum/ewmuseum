The Becoming Saga Part 3: The Arrival

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th April OOC: Grrrr … Revision is evil. Sorry, I had to cut this short, but like I said, revision is evil. Entry Date: 11th April Well, yesterday was shocking, to say the least. I just don’t know who to believe anymore … I mean, Clark Simons’s points did seem believable, but should I believe a man I have known for little over 24 hours? I don’t know, it’s all a blur to me now. Should I believe this whole World Peacekeeping Organisation crap either? And what the hell is the Digital Reality Corporation? None the less, I am going to go to the address that he gave me today, to see if I can find out more about this whole thing. I mean, why would these guys pick me out specifically? What’s so great about me? - Andy Start Tape The scene slowly fades in to downtown Milwaukee, at around about 11pm in the evening. The weather seems to have taken somewhat of a nosedive from the past two days, as instead of beautiful sunshine, the sky is full of grey clouds and rain pours down on the city. The streets and sidewalks are nowhere near as busy as they were over the past two days, but that’s probably because it’s late at night and most people are probably at home by now. The sound of rain hitting the ground fills the air, as very few people rush by, trying to get out of the rain, umbrella’s up high. The camera slowly pans around to the left, to reveal Andy Murray walking along the sidewalks straight towards us; he is just a few meters away. He is wearing the exact same clothes as yesterday, but this time the rainwater causes his leather coat to glisten. He passes us, the camera turns around with him, and follows him down the street. For a few moments Andy walks down the sidewalk, minding his own business. After a few seconds however, he stops in his tracks, and pulls out the small card that was given to him by Clark yesterday. He reads the address … “322 47th Street” … Well, he was already on 47th street, now all he had to do was find number 322. Andy slides the card back into his pocket, and looks up to the shop that is to his right. “Milwaukee Guns & Ammo” reads the large green sign. Through the windows, various types of guns can be seen, and it looks like the shop is still open for some reason. Above the door, painted in black, is the number “322”. ”Guess this must be the place” Andy says to himself, as he walks forward into the store. Andy looks around the store in awe at all the weapons and ammo that line the shelves, it’s amazing how such a small store could be filled with so many tools of destruction. ”You must be Murray” a voice calls from the other side of the store. Andy looks up, to see a short man standing behind the counter. The man is wearing a black & red shirt, and a pair of old scruffy jeans; obviously this guy works for the shop, and has some involvement with the DRC. ”Yes” Andy replies, slowly nodding his head. ”Follow me please” the clerk says as Andy starts walking towards him. The clerk goes through a set of tacky western style doors into a backroom of some sorts, Andy follows him. The backroom isn’t exactly the type of room you’d expect a shop to have, but it’s a backroom none the less. The room isn’t really decorated at all, it’s white wash walls and floorboards have been pretty much left alone. A few closed cardboard boxes litter the floor, but perhaps the dominating feature in this room is the huge silver safe that can be found along the back wall. The clerk walks up to the safe, and keys in some combination into the keypad attached to it. There is a brief click, as softly the large metal door slides open. Inside the safe, there is not money, or anything else you would expect to see in a safe for the matter, but instead there is an average sized wooden door. Andy glances at the clerk, who merely shrugs in return. Suddenly, the wooden door opens up; standing in its frame is Clark Simons, dressed in the exact same attire he was in yesterday. ”Glad to see you could make it, Mr. Murray”Simons says to Andy, who looks on. Behind Simons and beyond the door, we can see the metallic interior of what looks like a elevator of some sorts. Not the type of elevator you would see normally in a mall for example. This elevator looks a lot more futuristic in comparison to your average elevator. ”Lets go” Clark says, stepping inside the elevator’s interior. Andy steps inside the safe, before walking through to the elevator, closing the small wooden door behind him. As Murray steps away, two large metal doors slide shut behind him, just like any other elevator. ”Sub-Level One please” Clark says, as if he was speaking to the elevator. Smoothly, the elevator starts to descend, obviously it is voice activated, Andy remains silent. ”Where are we going?” Andy enquires, breaking his silence. ”Welcome to your new life, Mr. Murray” Clark replies with a smile and a chuckle. Suddenly, the elevator comes to a halt, and the doors slide open … As the scene slowly fades out.
