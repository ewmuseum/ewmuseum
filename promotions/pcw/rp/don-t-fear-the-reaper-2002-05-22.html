Don't Fear The Reaper

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/02 Time: 12: 53 P.MDate: 5/20/02Location: New York CityScene: Holiday Inn Don’t Fear the Reaper The scene opens with Juan Borro in a Holiday Inn. Juan’s had a long boring day awaiting a call to see if he’s been accepted back to the new Primetime Championship Wrestling. The time is close to 1 A.M. Juan is uptight and totally stressed over the decision. It has nothing to do with him being poor if he doesn’t make it or not being able to live without it. It’s just something Juan uses as an outlet, an outlet that is needed in his life. The minutes and hours have passed before Juan has finally come to a resolution. He can’t stay in his room all night sobbing near a phone like a ten-year-old girl. He has to go out and have fun. Sure it’s just a decision about wrestling, but today Juan is rethinking his whole life rather than just wrestling. The reason Juan has loved wrestling so much is because it was something dangerous. So dangerous he could even die doing it. That kind of risk is what drives Juan toward it, and now that he can’t take those risks he feels a bit incomplete. So today, Juan is going to take a risk, for today Juan will not FEAR THE REAPER! Juan can’t take it any more and jumps out of his bed. Juan grabs a coat and leaves his motel room as soon as possible. Next ting we know Juan is hitting the road with nothing holding him back. As Juan drives he thinks, “Where do the outrageous risk takers hang out?” At that point Juan sees a huge fire and loud music blasting. It’s almost like it was a call from heaven, for Juan has just seen the land we call RAVE! Juan parks on the side of the street and walks down a long dirt road until he sees a bunch of men in all black cloaks with a large tent behind them. “Hey are you guys like the head of this rave thing going on over here?” Juan asks trying to play it cool and smooth. “This isn’t just a rave, this is a new type of life. If you think your ready for this then take the next step. If not leave. Plain and Simple.” The man says barely moving with Juan a little hesitant to go. “Well what’s going on in there?” Juan asks wanting to know a little bit more before he takes the huge risk. “Life at it’s fullest is in there. Now are you ready to experience life?” The man asks in a pushy manner. Juan thinks about what he said about being all he could be and not having limits for himself. Juan builds up his spirit and soon enough takes that step inside a place he has never experienced before. The tent opens and Juan sees himself in a dark room. “What the hell is going on here?” Juan says to him self, as he can’t see a thing. Moments later he noticed three floating white object coming up to him. One of them spoke to him as she held out a pill in her hand. “Take this and the world will become your best friend.” Says one of the white figures with another giggling in the background. Juan looks at the pill and doesn’t think twice and he swallows it hole continuing his adventurous ways. A door to the next room then opens and Juan walks in. The room is like one huge trampoline and everything is different color. The air is filled with foam and everyone is totally out of it. Juan spots a woman standing next to him and he goes to ask her a question. “What kind of place is this?” Juan asks as he taps the woman on the shoulder. The woman then turns around and looks Juan straight in the eyes. She doesn’t say a word but instead goes on to unbutton her top. Juan doesn’t speak, and next thing he knows everything is completely changed around him and he is just in a bed with that same woman. She looks him in the eyes wearing just red lace panties. Juan gets excited and takes advantage of the moment. This heat continues, until Juan’s head starts to hurt. He closes his eyes and the next second when he opens it, he is in a bar area sitting a stool all alone. Juan wonders where the woman he was just with is right now. He looks around himself but sees no one. Suddenly in a distance he sees the woman. He walks over to her but she starts running so Juan chases. Juan chases throughout the whole bar, until he sees himself stopped by a rather huge man in front of him. He tries to get around the man, but the man doesn’t let Juan pass. Juan tries once again, but the man refuses to move. “Get out of my way man, I’m looking for someone.” Juan says angrily wanting to find the mistress he was just with. “I’m afraid, I can’t do that, sir.” The man says with a frown on his face. Suddenly the scene fades with Juan in a dark room with one round table. The only two men are Juan and the big man in his way. There are twenty shot glasses on table and Juan looks at them unsure. “Go.” The man screams and Juan doesn’t hold back and just goes for the drinks as quick as he can. The two men drink one glass after another not taking a breath at all. It’s chug chug chug, until they can’t chug any longer. Juan feels completely raged and goes quicker then he can. There is one more glass on the table and Juan takes it down like a pure expert. Right when he thinks it’s done, Juan finds himself in yet another place. This time Juan is in a factory type place where everyone is hooked up to these machines, getting something pumped into their body. Juan hears music at the end of the hall, and then sees the same woman from before. He chases her and the hall get longer. The people to the side of him are in a complete trance. Juan finally exits the rooms and enters one with thousands of lights flashing everywhere. Don’t Fear The Reaper is playing in the background. The sound is faint, and Jun keeps walking and it gets louder and louder. Juan continues to follow the sounds until he approaches an area with all bikers. Every biker has a lighter in the air, signing don’t fear the reaper at the top of their lungs. Seasons Don’t Fear The ReaperNor do the wind, the sun or the rainWe can be like they are Come on BabyDon’t Fear The Reaper Baby Take My HandDon’t Fear The Reaper We’ll be able to flyDon’t Fear The ReaperBaby, I’m your Man Juan hears the lyrics and feels the lyrics everything seems cool. He goes over to the bikers and speaks. “Hey guys, how are you?” Juan asks a bit confused and out of it. “Take a walk on the wild side.” The man simply answers as he throws the keys to his hog at Juan. Juan looks at them and doesn’t think twice. Next think Juan knows he’s driving free on the highway with the wind blowing in his hair. He feels completely free with his soul wandering. The beat to Don’t Fear the Reaper is repeating in Juan’s head as everything turns blurry. To him life is all about that one moment. He sees the bridge going up about half a mile ahead. The cars near him stop, but Juan knows he’s taking one more risk. The curtains flew then he appeared saying don’t be afraidCome on Baby and she had no fearAnd she ran to him then they started to flyThey looked backward and said goodbye she had become like they areCome on BabyDon’t Fear The Reaper Juan approached that bridge going over a hundred miles an hour. He hit the ramp and then he flew, like a bird in the wind and an airplane soaring above the clouds. Juan felt the wind on his face and smiled. Half way through something changed. A phone sounded and kept ringing. Juan tried to ignore it, but the louder it got the more he feel, until Juan feel all the way to the ground. At that point Juan woke up in his motel room with him phone right by him. Juan shakes it off and picks up the phone. “Who is this?” Juan asks. “You’ve made PCW” Juan didn’t even listen to the rest and just hung up the phone. He took a deep breath, and then laid back down. The phone rang once again and Juan picked it up again. “Who is this?” “You’re the new trainer/side coach of the Portuguese national Team, Congratulations.” Juan tried to make sure this was all real and hung up the phone. In just one night the two things he has been striding for happened quickly and easily. All Juan had left to say was four words. DON’T FEAR THE REAPER LalalalalalalalalalaWe can be like they areCome on babyDon’t fear the Reaper……………………… Scene Fades to Black.
