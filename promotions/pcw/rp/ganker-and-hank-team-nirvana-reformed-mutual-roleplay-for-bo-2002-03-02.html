Ganker and Hank, Team Nirvana Reformed, mutual roleplay for both matches at March Madness.

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-02 --- The scene opens to Ganker Ransolver gearing up for his PCW first ever Pay-Per-View, March Madness. Ganker is in the gym with Ana watching him work the punching bag with rights and lefts and a few martial arts kicks. Ganker is wearing black sweats and a white wife beater. Ana is wearing blue jeans and a red hoody, her hair tied back into a ponytail. Hank then walks over from working out on the bench press, gearing up for his PPV match against Mark Estep. He pats Ganker on the shoulder, then walks over to the squat machine and sets it up for 205-pounds. Josh Young then walks over with Jeff Marx in a headlock. Ganker: 'What'd that swine do to yo now Josh?' Josh: 'He challenged me, sayin' I was a worthless federation owner. He said the reason RSE died is cause I didn't know how to run it. So I figured I'd kick the bastards a** in the ring, which as you can obviously see, I did.' Ganker: 'Make sure to really hurt him.' (grins) Jeff: (gasping for air) 'I have you exactly where I want you.' (screams like a little girl) Josh: 'He's disturbing me, I'm about to throw him in the dumpster.' Ganker: 'No, no, no.' Jeff: (sigh of relief) 'Thank you Ganker, you're such a kind soul.' Ganker: 'Hang him by his feet from the upper level of the gym, out the window.' Jeff: 'Cruel bastard, who will always be a damn worthless PCW jobber.' Ganker: 'Former RSE Cruiserweight Champion, can't forget that now can we?' Jeff: 'The biggest jobb... Arg~!' --- Josh wrenches more on Jeff's neck. Josh: 'Shut up, I no give you permission to speak.' Ganker: 'As amusing as this be, I must retain my focus on my workout.' Josh: 'Cool, I understand. I mean you have to kick Red Rock's, Anton's, and Kramer's a** to become PCW Cruiserweight Championship #1 Contender.' Ganker: 'Yup, much like I kicked Angst's a** to win the RSE Cruiserweight Championship.' Josh: (laughing) 'That you did man, that you did.' Jeff- 'Argg~!' --- Josh wrenches on the neck of J. Marx once more. Josh: 'He, he, he~! I find amusement out of the misery and pain of others, mental and physical.' Ganker: 'Me too, now please, leave me alone so I may continue with my workout.' --- Josh shrugs as he takes Jeff Marx back to the ring and throws him in and starts to beat the living hell out of him. The scene then switches back to Ganker who is now practicing some martial arts kicks on the punching bag. He hits a back spinning heel kick, just a plain old heel kick and a roundhouse. He mainly focuses on the main kicks that he can hit fast and most efficient. Ana then gets up clapping as she hugs her man and he hugs her back, they then share a passionate kiss as Ganker then backs away and grabs the p. bag with his righthand and takes his left, forms a fist and takes it back and comes in as hard and fast as he can and nails the bag, making him lose his grip on it with his right. The bag then flies back as Ganker catches it when it comes back. Ganker: 'Damn I feel sorry for whoever takes on of my punches.' Ana: 'I feel sorry for whoever takes one of your martial arts kicks.' Ganker: (grinning) 'That too.' Ana: 'So, what are your plans for the rest of the night after you finish training?' Ganker: 'Grab a bite to eat wit' yo and then hangout in the bar wit' Hank, shooting about March Madness.' Ana: 'What am I suppose to do then until you and I are alone?' (grins) Ganker: 'I'm sure yo and Janelle can find somethin' to occupy yo time while yo men are out havin' a guys night in the bar.' Ana: 'By the way, you're a bit underage, how you plan to get in?' Ganker: 'However the fiz-uck I want.' (laughs) 'I might use my fake ID, or I might jus' beat the hell outta some bouncer or bartender until they allow us in to drink. Then again, I might jus' use the fact I'm a PCW wrestler and get in anyway.' Ana: 'Being on the PCW roster gives you right to drink underage?' Ganker: 'No, it's jus' most bartenders around now, they want all the famous they can to get drunk in their bars, mostly to make all the money they can off them after they're drunk, beggin' for more beer by the damn gallon.' Ana: 'They never seen Hank drink then I'm taking it?' Ganker: 'Probably not, Hank has a tolerance, a forcefield that protects the bastard from getting drunk. It's a good thing I have that bastard wit' me when I drink, I don't have such a good tolerarnce, four to five bottles and I'm plastered, can't hold my liquor too well.' Ana: 'I kind of got that.' Ganker: 'Cool, that's another reason I love yo so.' Ana: 'I know.' (smiles) Ganker: (small, slight laugh) 'Red Rock, it's kinda like old times huh?' (Ana nods) 'Who would've ever thought I'd get the chance to step once again into the ring wit' one of my most worthy foes? It's always been my dream to even the score wit' Red Rock and/or Duzza. I thought I had my shot wit' Duzza in the now defunct RSE, but that closed.' (clinges and shakes his fist) 'Now is the time for me to get back at Red Rock, to end the feud once and for all, to prove why Team Nirvana will always be the best damn tag team ever.' Ana: 'Nobody but you seem to have much to say about this match, they all seem to keep pretty much to themselves.' Ganker: 'Because they realize there is no hope when Ganker Ransolver is booked into the match.' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God' (grins) 'The only damn reason they don't speak, cause they know that it won't help them win. They know I'm the future of this company. They know I Ganker Ransolver will bring PCW backup to it's once great glory.' --- Hank walks over from squatting. Hank: 'We both will fool, we'll raise the dead PCW Tag Team Division up to new heights and form teams that would have never thought would have been born. We'll unite partners jus' to try and take us down, that's the only reason the AKR existed, was to eliminate Team Nirvana outta the tag team title division. They managed it for a little by takin' out yo leg man, but now yo back and I don't think we will be stopped.' Ganker: 'Think? I know bro!' Hank: (pauses and sighs) 'To leave the subject, Mark Estep cost me my long awaited for PCW title and now has a match wit' me at March Madness, what, does the damn little boy have a death wish? I'll hit the Off-Beat on little Mark Estep and pin his worthless a** to the mat for the three count. I am Hank and I make yo job better than yo ever have in yo entire life.' Ganker: 'I know, he cost yo the Extreme Title, but remember, next Tuesday we have a number one contenders match for the PCW Tag Team Championships, Red Rock and his partner.' Hank: (angered tone, while shaking a clinged fist) 'Red Rock!' --- Hank slams his fist down on a table sticking out of the wall and smashes right through it. Hank: 'Oh how I will enjoy making that little boy scream for mercy, oh how I will take great pleasure in bashing his head into the turnbuckle and making him bleed. His blood will taste so good. Ganker: 'There is no great team, but did yo notice, I, Ganker Ransolver, have two number one contenders matches this next week?' Hank: 'Yeah, I did.' Ganker: 'Cause...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' (laughs) Hank: 'Sure yo are Ganker, sure yo are. Ganker: 'Listen, jus' cause yo jealous that I'm better than yo are, don't mean yo have to deny the obvious truth.' Hank: (wiffs the air) 'What's that smell?' Ganker: 'I don't smell anything, what yo on bout?' Hank: 'I think it's, no. I know it's more bullsh*t comin' from Ganker's direction.' (laughs) Ganker: 'Ha, ha...' --- Smack! Hank: 'Thought I was the smacker?' Ganker: 'So I stole a move from you, not like yo don't steal from me on a daily basis.' Hank: 'I don't.' Ganker: 'Sure yo don't boy.' Hank: 'I don't and that's a DAMN Fact~! Now if yo don't mind, I have to prepare for Mark Estep's execution.' Ganker: 'I have to prepare to kick Red Rock's a** two times.' Hank: 'Lucky bastard, I wanted to kick Red Rock's a** too at March Madness.' Ganker: 'Well yo probably would have, but it's for the shot at a title that actually matters.' (laughs) Hank: 'Ha, ha, very funny jackass.' Ganker: 'I know.' (grins) --- From out of nowhere, SMACK~! The scene fades on that note. Note: I would like it to be well-known Red Rock only has one victory over me and I as Team Nirvana, the handler, have beaten him and his partner Duzza, on numerous occassions. In fact, I've even lost count. ( :P lol ) I added this in jus for Red Rock, heh, heh, heh~!
