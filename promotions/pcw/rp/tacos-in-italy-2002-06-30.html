Tacos in Italy?

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June Mocking blind children? Just how low will El Diablo go? (and we don't mean limbo) El Diablo is obviously a strange one and he shows that nearly every day in his lonesome life, so what would be next in the chapter? The outside of a fancy Italian restaurant is in view, with large windows showing the insides of this fine diner. Around the windows are rose beds and a small water fountain, with a long pathway to the main entrance to the restaurant. The sign above the restaurant is in flashing red lights, with a green backdrop to it 'Luigi's' A typical Italian restaurant name. Inside the restaurant is El Diablo seated at a table for one. On the table of the restaurant is a white cotton tablecloth, with a bottle of wine in the center of the table. In a basket nearby to it are breadsticks. In front of El Diablo is a glass of red wine and also there are silver knives, forks and spoons, two of each, with napkins underneath them. And of course on the table is a menu. The Mexican Devil is wearing his face paint still, and is receiving many strange looks from those on tables around him. Despite the face paint he is quite well dressed up, with his long black hair tied in a ponytail, and wearing a white shirt and tie and black pants with shiny black shoes. He is dressed well for an occasion that he is attending alone. Diablo is sat looking down at the cutlery. 'One, two...one, two...one, two,' he counts, 'Why are thee two of everything?' he asks himself quietly, as to not draw attention to himself.....for once. He waves his hand, indicating for a waiter to come over. A man with slicked back black hair and a frizzy moustache heads towards the table. He wears a purple waistcoat and white shirt underneath with a black bow tie. 'Are you ready to order sir?' asks the waiter. Diablo looks up to the man, and puts on his 'poshest' look. 'Garcon, why is there two of everything here? I'm only dining by myself. I'm not on the Arc am I?' queries Diablo, also trying to sound posh. The waiter looks a little impatient, but still answers. 'No sir, you have two forks, knives and spoons as one is for your meal, and the other is dessert,' he replies. El Diablo closes his lips and nods. 'Am I expected to use all of them?' he asks with a certain degree of caution. 'No sir' Now are you ready order?' the waiter asks once more. Diablo squinches his eyes and shakes his head. 'Not yet. You may go now,' states Diablo preparing to pick up the menu. The waiter starts to walk away and mutters under his breath, 'Idiot,' El Diablo of course does not hear a thing. He proceeds to pick up the large menu and opens it. He scans through the food listing and speaks out loud about some. 'Spag-hetti? What the hell is that?' he asks to himself and shakes his head in disbelief, 'Rav-i-oli? Meat inside pasta? That just isn't right,' he again comments, 'Olives? Bleh!' he finishes in disgust. Once he has read through the entire menu, he motions for his waiter to approach. Again 'Manuel' comes over with an annoyed look on his face. 'Are you ready to order yet?' asks Manuel. Diablo shakes his head. 'Garcon, do you prepare regular food here?' questions Diablo to the service. The radio playing in the background continues to play Italian music, as the waiter looks shocked. This is emphasised by the twitching of the mans moustache. 'What do you mean?' Manuel responds nervously. El Diablo just sighs. 'You know, tacos, burritos, enchiladas, cheese burgers, chilli con carne. Get the point?' Diablo now says angrily. Manuel looks angry too and in his mind he must be questioning El Diablo's intelligence. 'Sir, we are an Italian restaurant!' he politely replies. Diablo shrugs. 'So?' Manuel's moustache twitches again. 'So, we prepare Italian food, not Mexican,' Manuel states clearly. El Diablo shakes his head, grabs a stick of bread and takes a bite out of it. 'Is this Italian?' asks Diablo. The waiter shakes his head. 'Well if this isn't Italian, I'll have a beef meat taco, please,' El Diablo cunningly asks. The waiter begins to go red in the face. 'We don't make taco's here,' he says whilst gritting his teeth. Diablo places the half eaten bread stick back down in the basket and looks up at his waiter. 'I WANT TACOS NOW!!!' shouts the Mexican Devil with the whole restaurant hearing, and now watching his table. Manuel begins to sweat, and wipes his forehead with a cloth he pulls out from his waistcoat pocket. 'Sir, you're making a scene,' he whispers to El Diablo. El Diablo is now very angry, and it shows by the way he has torn the menu in half. 'I DON'T CARE IF I AM MAKING A SCENE, I WANT MY TACOS AND I WANT THEM NOW!!!' he continues to yell. The waiter slams him notepad down on the table. 'Listen, we don't freaking sell tacos!' yells Manuel. The customers in the restaurant continue to watch the events unfold in front of them. 'Give him his tacos,' says one man. The waiter Manuel ignores the man, and signals for the boss to come in. The boss walks in with slicked back black hair, and wearing casual clothes to fit his plump body. 'Is there a problem here?' he politely asks El Diablo. Diablo nods quickly. 'I ordered tacos and this poor excuse for a human being, let alone waiter would not take my order,' Diablo, now calm, states. 'Sir, we don't sell tacos here,' says the manager. The response only angers El Diablo again. 'What about the customer is always right?' asks Diablo to a series of cheers from the crowd of customers now watching, who seem to be firmly on his side. Manuel can't believe the support he has and almost walks away. 'I'm really sorry, but we do not produce, nor do we sell tacos here. Is that clear?' states the 'Luigi's' manager calmly. El Diablo smiles and looks down at the table again. 'Oh I see what's going on,' he says while nodding. 'You do?' asks the manager. El Diablo continues nodding, but nodding in frustration. 'You're oppressing me because of my race! Just like The End is oppressing me because I have sight,' comments Diablo looking back up and seeing the manager wipe his forehead with another cloth. The manager now looks nervous. 'We would never do such a thing,' he says. El Diablo replies hastily, 'Liar!!!! I will take my business elsewhere,' El Diablo stands up and rams the chair he was seated on into the table, knocking it over, causing a mess as red wine leaks onto the restaurants fine blue carpet. The Mexican walks away from the scene and through the crowd, muttering under his breath, 'Damn bastards, damn End, I bet he put them up to it,' Behind follow some of the customers who are just as disgusted as El Diablo. Diablo and several customers leave the establishment quickly, and remaining is 'Luigi's' are only half of the customers that were there before El Diablo arrived. 'We're ruined,' cries the manager.
