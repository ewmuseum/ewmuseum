Victory Or Death

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th October 2002 Finally some peace and quiet…. At least in my dreams I not being bothered by anyone, attacked by fans or followed by some Fat Japanese Man, I safe and on my own!! Today the day of reckoning….the day that I show my true colors and do what I have planned! Today everyone going to realize that the Vietnamese Viper is no one to mess with and no one to trust! They call me a Viper because I like a snake, quick to strike and quick to draw out, But what they not know is just as a snake devious….so am I! -=***=- I awoke to the sounds of banging on the door of my hotel room. Each knock echoing throughout the small set up, I not in the fuckin’ mood for this… ”Fat Man fuckin’ get the door!!” I screamed at the top of my lungs and pulled the bed sheet over my head, There was no answer but the banging at the door stopped as I lay underneath the blanket opening my eyes a little trying to tell if the knocking had stopped. I paused and held my breath for a minute, hoping that it would make the knocking stop and whoever it was go away….everything was o.k until I let out a sigh of relief…and then the banging begin to start up again! Du Mah!! I not take much more of this… I clenched my teeth and drew the covers over my head tighter and pulled the pillow around my ears trying to make the knocking go away, if I ignored it for long enough I figure they would get the picture and just go away….. The knocking continued to drown out as it finally pushed me too far and I hauled the covers off of my bed, “I fuckin’ coming!” As I yelled out I adjusted my mickey mouse boxer shorts and went over to the dresser drawer at the foot of my bed, pulling open the bottom draw and removing my prized possession…not the PURE Jnr. Heavy Weight Title but the machete which lay next to it, If they want wake me up this early…I going to make them wish they come a day later! I chuckled to myself as I felt the blade cold against my skin, making me shiver. Mornings aren’t my best times, I thought only people in New York wake up this muh-fuckin’ early?! Either way I didn’t bother putting my top on, instead I just walked up to the white door leading to my hotel room. Wiping some sleep out of my eye I pushed my face near the peep hole, trying to see who was making all the noise on the other side of the door, but all I could see was a huge dark dot as the vibrations began to run through it causing me to jump back. “You fuckin’ going to get it now…” I muttered to myself as I clutched the door handle and twisted it slowly, The person on the other side of the door must’ve noticed because the knocking stopped instantly and there was dead silence outside my door. Something not right… I gripped the machete tightly till my knuckles turned white before I released the door handle and took and pressed my ear against the door before speaking out loud to whoever it was outside. “Who out there?” I couldn’t think of anything else to say, There was some whispering before a grunting voice replied, “Uhh, we have a delivery here for Master Jung Loc, may we come in?” Who know I staying here… I took a step back from the door and thought to myself for a second before I took a deep breath. “Hold up, I opening the door now…but before I open it, fuckin’ apologize for waking me up!” I sniggered to myself not resisting to be a smart ass, There was some speaking going on in Japanese behind the door but after several long sentences the same voice grunted back, “We’re ver…I’m very sorry Master Loc” I know something going on now…. Something was up and I knew it wasn’t going to be good, I slid the machete behind my back and grasped the door handle twisting it slowly and tensing the muscles down my arm, Come on White Boy…I ready! With a grunt I swung the door open and stepped back holding the machete up against my arm before noticing that there was no one in front of my door….What the fuck?! I looked down the hall and there was no trace of anyone, no fresh tread marks on the red carpet, no sounds of a recent elevator….nothing. Taking some initiative I edged a little closer to the doorway before dropping the machete to my side and popping my head just a little out the doorway to take a look around. I looked to my left and saw nothing and was just about to glance over to my right when I felt something cold against my temple. “Don’t move child…don’t make a move!” A reasonably posh sounding voice spoke softly into my ear as I began to fume with rage, I gritted my teeth and was tempted to hit the guy when I snapped back, “What the fuck you want?! HUH?!” “I want you to go nice and slow back into that hotel room of yours and take a seat at the dining table…we have a little business that needs to be discussed!” The voice sneered towards the end of the sentence, This fuckin’ bull shit!! I grabbed hold of the machete tightly and edged my way backwards in through the hotel room door, being careful not to look at the guy with the gun to my head. As I edged back in through the door I felt someone grab my wrist as I winced with sudden surprise and pain, dropping the machete to the floor with a soft thumping sound. “Nice try Jung, but I guess you’re not as dumb as we thought you were!” Once again the man sniggered and then spun me around to face him, I not even know him… Looking at the guys face he seemed to be a well presented young business man, complete with a stylish haircut and glasses, not the type you’d even think of pulling a gun on someone…but looking up at his gun I noticed he was wearing gloves. This guy going to fuckin’ shoot me!! He waved the gun behind me and spun me around as another guy grabbed onto my wrist and escorted me into the dining area of my hotel as the first man with the gun went back and closed the front door.The guy who was escorting me to the table was bald with a long beard and a tattoo of a dragon on the back of his head….meaning only one thing, these two were Yakuza. He shoved me down into a chair before grunting something at me in Japanese, “Yeah Du Man to you to…stupid fuckin’ White Boy!” I looked him up and down and sneered. Once again he said something in Japanese before raising his fist signaling he wanted to punch me for one reason or another as I looked him in the eyes and held my chin up showing I wasn’t scared, Hit me White Boy…then we see what happen! “Don’t touch him Hiroshin…we need him to be in peak condition!” The man who had held the gun to my head moments before replied in the background, He pulled up the chair across the table from me and sat down, adjusting his white tie and black gloves before nodding towards his goon who crossed his arms and stood over me. “So what you want?” I couldn’t wait for him to ask questions, The man across the table from me didn’t seem to upset I was being cheeky, he gave a smile and leant forward, “I want you to get me 5 million dollars!” “Pfft, you think I get you money?! Do I look like White Boy to you?” I stared at him wide eyed, I not even know him and he asking me to give him $5 million?! I continued to stare at him as though he had a disease or something, and he continued to smile back at me from across the table. “Actually Jung you look like a horse to me….” The man began to chuckle, I continued to look him up and down, “What you mean?” “I don’t want you to physically give me 5 million dollars Jung…I know you don’t have that kind of money! That’s why I’m betting 5 million dollars on you…” The man trailed off, “What you talking about….” I didn’t like the sound of where this was heading, The man smiled and leant back in the chair before placing the gun, a glock 9mm in the center of the table and speaking, “I’m talking about you winning your match against PURE at PCW Red October and winning me a bet that will make me 5 million dollars richer!!” I couldn’t say anything, This guy pull a gun on me, then tell me he want me to win him money?! Everything was becoming confusing. I looked up over my shoulder at the goon who was still standing with his arms folded my machete laying on the floor at his feet, I so tempted to cut him up right now… “So why you wake me up so early and put gun to my head to tell me that?!” I became pissed off, The man chuckled once more, “Well you see Jung, I didn’t come here to tell you just that! Oh no, I came here to basically tell you that if you lose at Red October and I lose my money…I’m going to make sure that you pay a price!” “It not my fuckin’ fault you bet on me….” “You’re the Jnr. Heavy Weight Champion! You’re the brightest of all the stars going into that ring so I’m trusting you to win me my money!” The man became annoyed with me as he banged his fist down hard on the table causing the glock to jump, “So what I get if I win….” I smirked up at him trying to cut a deal, The man shook his head and pushed his glasses up his nose before leaning down towards me, “If you win, consider yourself lucky you’re leaving Tokyo alive! They said you were a feisty one but I had no idea you’d be anything like this!” “I not fuckin’ scared of you White Boy….” I smirked, The guy nodded his head smiling before grabbing the glock and pointing it at the middle of my forehead, “Are you scared now child?!” “No….” I winked at him as he became more pissed off and the goon from behind be clenched his fists, I going to play them like fools…. I scratched my neck and looked up at him trying to get the message across that I wasn’t going to be intimidated. All of a sudden the man lowered the gun and began to laugh, “You show no fear…this makes my faith in you stronger!” I leaned back a little upset I wasn’t getting on his nerves anymore, “You Nothing….” ”Just win the match tonight Jung and then Japan will welcome you as one of our own!” The man placed the gun away in his breast pocket and patted me on the shoulder before nodding to his goon. “Remember, I’m Yakuza and we don’t take kindly to people who screw us over…you’d better win for both our sakes! Otherwise you will lose out twice as bad!” The man walked up the short corridor and to the front door before opening it and walking out. The goon slammed the door behind him as I sat in the chair of the dining area confused, What the fuck going on?! This not make any sense… I shook my head as I reached down to my side and grabbed my machete on the floor, running my fingers across the smooth blade and thinking out loud to myself. “If I win this….I winning it for me!” ”No PCW, No PURE, No TokyoPop and no Yakuza going to scare me” I trailed off and I felt a sinister grin creep across my face, “They Nothing...”
