The Quest for Refreshment

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/02 The camera fades in… The door to the Chef’s apartment swings open and in trudges the Culinary Conquistador, home from a day at the rat races. The Chef: Clone Bertha, I’m home!!! “O-KAY Mr. F*cking Loud Mouth!!!” The Chef: The Chef, walking like a zombie from one of those crappy B movies, waddles over to the coat rack and places his camouflage chef cap on one holder. The Chef then makes his way over to the refrigerator and opens the door, looking for something to quell the tremendous hunger within him. “I f*cking ate everything in there and f*cking forgot to go to the c*ck assed grocery store too mother f*cker!!!” The Chef grits his teeth with anger, but quietly answers Bertha… The Chef: All right, I’ll just have a glass of Perrier. The Chef walks over to the cabinet and opens the door, revealing a cavalcade of glasses. He picks up a wine class and walks over to the sink. He grabs hold of the faucet with the Perrier logo on it and turns the knob, trying to receive some of that oh so refreshing flavored water. “I f*cking sucked the Perrier faucet dry too f*ck face!!!” The Chef, this time even more angrily, grits his teeth and slams his hand on the counter. The Chef: You finished a year’s supply of Perrier in 12 hours!?!? “I was f*cking thirsty!” The Chef shakes his head, still not believing that that much Perrier could have been downed in a short amount of time. The Chef: Well, what do we have here to consume!?!? “F*cking nothing!!!” The Chef nods. The Chef: Well I’m going to the local shop to pick up some staple products; I’ll be back in a jiff! “Don’t f*cking take forever, motha f*cka!!!” The Chef, tired and sweaty, grabs his Chef hat, opens up his apartment door, and bid farewell to his lazy house clone. The Chef: Adios… The Chef leaves the apartment and makes his way down the stairs of the apartment complex. The camera fades to black… The camera fades in… The Chef is shown walking down the street to the nearest food jobber. He politely tips his cap to each on coming male, and hoots and hollers at each on coming attractive female. The unattractive females get nothing. The Chef spots a store that looks to be suitable for his shopping needs. The Chef: Ah yes, “Frank’s Grub Hub”… The Chef casually walks through the door of the store, and peers around at the various confections, snacks, meats, produce, and beverages stashed around the small market. The Chef: Masturbating Mohammed!!! The Chef quickly runs over to the beverages and looks through the selection. His greatest fears were true…THIS SHOP SELLS SAN PELLEGRINO INSTEAD OF PERRIER!!! The Chef runs over to the nearest store clerk and interrogates the poor lad about the lack of good high quality French water. The Chef: What in the name of Allah is going on here!?!? You idiots actually think that your Pellegrino mineral water is going to sell more than Perrier!?!? Clerk: Um, sir, I just work here… The Chef: Well if you work her boy, you’d know why you have Pellegrino instead of Perrier!!! Clerk: Um, sir, why don’t you just ask the manager? The Chef hugs the young lad. The Chef: Ask the manager!?!? That’s a brilliant idea, thank you boy; you have done the world a great favor! The Chef slaps the poor little teenager in the back, and scampers off to find the manager of the store. The Chef: Must…find…Pellegrino…using…culinary…school…dropout!!! Suddenly as the Chef dashes through the potato chip aisle, a big burly man, unshaven, and fat as a Bertha Clone stands in front of the Ruffles with his arms crosses and his brow furled. Man: Can I help you sir? The Chef: Yes, are you the manager? Man: I am… The Chef: … Man: Frank! DUN! DUN! DUNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN! The Chef: Frank, eh, so let’s see here…”Frank’s Grub Hub” and Frank, so you are the manager!!! DUN! DUN! DUNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN! Frank: Yes, what can I do for you? The Chef: Well let’s be frank, Frank… I’ve been in the beverage aisle, and I don’t like what I see, and neither does Allah, cause he backs me up 100 percent, he’s my posse… Frank: OK… What’s the problem? The Chef: How about this…YOU HAVE PELLEGRINO INSTEAD OF PERRIER!!! Frank: Yes, we simply chose not to carry the beverage since its sales have been down this past month, which is kind of strange since it is the summer, and people want ice-cold water… The Chef: That can’t be!?!? Wait a second…I’ve been the spokesperson for a month…UH OH!!! Frank: Yeah, I’ve seen you on that crappy commercial where you kick your fat ass wife off a cliff or something… The Chef grabs the manager by the collar, obviously upset by his tone. The Chef: Listen Mac, You can insult me by selling some rat infested Venetian gondola water over crystal clear French Alps water, and you can insult my commercials… But Mac, you never talk smack about my girl…NEVER!!! The Chef pulls his arm back and thrusts his fist forward at the manager. SMACK!!! CRACK!!! KONK!!! BAM!!! CRASH!!! SMASH!!! “And stay out, ya Pellegrino hatin’ piece of garbage!!!” Frank and his cronies toss the Chef out of the store and into the street. The Chef: YEEEEEEOOOOOOWWWHHHHH!!! OUCH!!! The Chef lands with a thump outside the store and stands back up, wiping the dirt off his chef uniform and maintaining whatever dignity he had left. The Chef: Give my regard to Iblis, you infidels!!! The Chef walks down the street, ignoring all the laughing onlookers around him. The Chef: This really sucks prairie oysters… Suddenly, to the amazement of the Chef, he sees a Piggly Wiggly store with a sign posted on a window outside with the text, “Perrier 6 Pack and Pickled Pig’s Feet for $5.49!!!”… The Chef: Praise be to Allah!!! The Chef sprints down the street towards the supermarket as the camera mercifully fades to black…
