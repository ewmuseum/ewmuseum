Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part II]

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23, 2002 There are many dreary and dingy masses of ugly tag teams in the ranks of PCW, but there certainly could not be any team more ugly or dingier than Pretty Cool Guys. There were stories that they had once been more attractive, but that had been so long ago that no one remembered the time. They stood back in their gloomy, narrow strips of uncared-for, blemished faces, whose broken voices were supposed to take them to the top of the record charts. Did they really think that they would get there looking like that? Sure, all bands have ugly people in them, but that is why The Heartbreakers wanted to join. They were going to step up as the teen dreams, Pretty Cool Guys would be nothing more than their worst nightmare...backup singers. Pretty Cool Guys are the next tag team champions. At least that was what one boy thought as he stood near the iron railings watching the passers-by on the morning on which this story begins...keep in mind though, he was one of the ugly ones as well. He was a boy about twelve years old, his name was Justin Dentcool, and he was the kind of boy people look at a second time when they have looked at him once. In the first place, he was a very big boy -- tall for his years, and with a particularly strong frame. His shoulders were broad and his arms and legs were long and powerful. He was quite used to hearing people say, as they glanced at him, 'What a fine, big lad!' And then they always looked again at his face, and they would turn away in fear. This has to be the reception Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore get everytime they step into the ring, but hey, The Heartbreakers are nice guys, and they never pass up a chance at giving a good makeover, so why not try it in the ring Tuesday night? After all, what better place to give a makeover to someone than in the ring as the others wrestle. Of course while Jack, Johnny, and a Pretty Cool Guy of their choice stand out on the apron, the makeover can take place. Jack Valentine: “I’ll bring the eyeliner...” Johnny Heart: “...and I the lipgloss.” They thought to themselves. But that is a different story all together. Now, to return to the story of this strong, yet ugly boy, similar in characteristics to the Pretty Cool Guys. Ironically enough, he had stated in the past that he was the number one fan of the POP sensations, and probably the one and only. Justin Dentcool: “It is because they are strong...like me. And they sing so beautifully.” As Jack and Johnny passed by the boy like all of the others, they paused, picking up on the awkward comment thrown out by the boy. Jack Valentine: “Strong and musically gifted they may be, but are they beautiful enough to make it to the top of the charts?” Johnny Heart: (Interrupting, almost as if it is a sales pitch) “No, and that is where we come in.” Jack Valentine: “You see, we bring the band a sense of beauty, style, we bring the band what the kids want to see. After all, in today’s materialistic and cruel world, there’s no room for uglies now is there?” Justin Dentcool: “Who in the hell are...oh wait a minute, you’re those fags in pink that are trying to join up with my main men. Are you guys just plain dumb or what? Do you really think they will let you in the band?” Johnny Heart: “Well I have an inside source that says we have a 95% chance of making it into the band.” Jack Valentine: “95%? Where are you getting your numbers? My source says 96%.” Johnny Heart: “Oh...well, if we average them, that gives us...hmm...let’s see.” Justin Dentcool: (Angrily interrupting) “Enough. God you guys really are stupid in person. Don’t you get it? Don’t you see that you two are talentless fruits who have no right joining Pretty Cool Guys, and have no right being in PCW?” Jack Valentine: “Didn’t your mother ever teach you any manners young man? You should abide by the rules of the PINK! Always say please, and thank you.” Johnny Heart: “And don’t forget to brush your teeth.” Justin Dentcool begins pulling his hair out of frustration with the ignorant, and feminine Heartbreakers. Justin Dentcool: “I’m sick of this...get out of here!” Jack and Johnny look at each other, and then look back at the boy simultaneously. Again they look back at each other with a big smile on their faces. Justin Dentcool: “Oh my God! They’re going to try and put their evil gay ways in my head!!!” Jack Valentine: “Maybe, just maybe, we can help this young man, just like we are going to help Pretty Cool Guys when we join the band.” Johnny Heart: “How do you suggest we help him?” Justin Dentcool: (Interrupting again, nervously this time) “No, no...I’m fine...please, thank you. Just keep your gay hands away from me.” Jack Valentine: “We should sing him a song.” Johnny Heart: “Ahh...” Johnny pulls out the now infamous PINK! kazoo from his crotch and begins blowing some notes. Again, it is to the tune of their theme song, “Pink” by Aerosmith. Jack grabs a nearby stick and uses it as a pretend microphone as they sing to a now frightened boy. Jack Valentine: “You could be my flamingo...” Justin Dentcool: “No, no, that’s quite alright.” Jack Valentine: “...cause Pink, it’s the new kind of lingo.” Justin Dentcool: (Interrupting after each lyric) “Actually, Bye, Bye Bye is much better.” Jack Valentine: “...Pink, like a deco umbrella.” Justin Dentcool: “Like a what?” Jack Valentine: “It’s kink that you don’t ever tell her.” Justin Dentcool: “Oh my God! I’m outta here!!” As Jack begins to lick the stick that he is pretending to use as a microphone, the boy pushes Jack and Johnny out of the way and sprints down the road. Jack continues to lick the stick until Johnny taps him on the shoulder letting him know that the boy ran away. Johnny Heart: “Jack...Jack...he’s gone.” Jack Valentine: “Yes, but I really think we helped him. He probably ran home to by the latest in hair care products and the newest fads in fashion. By the way, great job on the kazoo today.” Johnny Heart: “Oh, thanks man.” Jack and Johnny gayly pat each other on their asses. Jack Valentine: “Aside from our newly converted friend, we should really buckle down and focus this week. We’ve got three huge matches in a row...well, at least I do.” Johnny Heart: “You’re right, and it all starts this Tuesday on Turmoil when we team up with our new bandmates...” Jack Valentine: “95.5% chance bandmates...” Johnny Heart: (Relieved that Jack finally gave him the average) “That’s it! 95.5% Thanks!” Jack Valentine: “And then Friday I return for the first time in nearly 3 months to single’s action in PCW.” Johnny Heart: (Laughing) “We all know how your single’s career went.” Jack smacks Johnny on the back of the head. Johnny Heart: “Ouch!” Jack Valentine: “Just kidding man.” Jack and Johnny begin to play around with each other, but before it gets too gay, they stop, noticing the public standing around watching in disgust. Johnny Heart: “And we top it all off on Sunday at Gold & Glory when we recapture the belts that are rightfully ours. I think we should spray paint them PINK! this time around...that might make for an interesting change.” Jack Valentine: “That’d be a great change! Good idea Johnny, and duley noted.” Johnny Heart: “We oughta get going though soon Jack, Christina’s probably waiting for us...” Jack Valentine: “Let her wait...” Jack smacks Johnny on the behind again, and the still un-confirmed to be gay or straight pair walks down the road to the beautiful sight of the PINK! sunrise. ...TO BE CONTINUED...
