They will cheer for me again

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/3/02 Betrayal…? A harsh word indeed, how can anyone class another as a betrayer unless they know the raw facts involved with the incident, surely you cannot judge someone. Innocent until proving guilty, a phrase used so much in modern society, usually to get out of trouble, but sometimes, it may just be true. You cannot accuse someone until you can prove they have committed an act of indignity. A wise man once said… He without sin cast the first stone. PCW Tuesday Turmoil, March 12th 2002. An ordinary day, in anyone’s life, but no, not in Anton McGonnell’s. He went into Tuesday Turmoil, he had been training day after day before it, his mind and body was 100% concentrated on the task at hand, and that was achieving his dream, and winning the PCW Cruiserweight Title. If he could somehow do that, it would be the greatest day it his life. He came out to a screaming crowd, as his theme tune, Paper Cut by Linkin Park hit. He fought the Multiple Man, until the very end, he wouldn’t give up, no matter what abuse he took, even when Jeff Marx putting boiling coffee in his eyes, which was ‘accidental’ of course. Though Anton didn’t give up. Then… two mysterious Luchadore’s came down to the ring, and took out Multiple Man, then placed Anton on top of him. The referee counted… 1… 2… 3! Anton was the new PCW Cruiserweight Champion, and the Masked Luchadore’s then revealed themselves as Golden Game AKA Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore, two members of International Incident. They then brought the tri-colour into the ring, which is the Irish National Flag, and give it to Anton. Anton was a new recruit of International Incident, and the new PCW Cruiserweight Champion. It had been three days since Anton the Kid had one the Cruiserweight Title from the hands of the Multiple Man, now he had a tougher challenge on his hands. He would have to take on the young Asian Star, Charlie Loc. Anton sat on the couch in his New York home. He had spent the last few days in New York with his family; they had planned a big celebration party due to him winning the PCW Cruiserweight Title. His family and friends all attended. He had had a wonderful day, and now, the day after, he felt very tired. He had been up all night due to the festivities. He was no quietly sitting in front of the television. He knew he had a big match the next day, against Charlie Loc, one of the best new talents in PCW. Charlie was an absolutely brilliant competitor, he practically dominated PCW when he was in it, and now he was here to do the same to PCW. Anton didn’t intend on making it easy for him though, Anton would give it his all, wing lose or draw. The room Anton was in was very big; it was basically to room in one. At one end there was the kitchen, and at the other, the living room. Anton was in the living room section, sitting in front of the black, 30-inch television. On the screen, was PTC TV 2. It was showing highlights of Tuesday Turmoil, where the masked Lachadore’s one the title for Anton. Anton couldn’t help but feel a little bad, that he didn’t win the match on his own, but he was confident that he would anyway. The crowd were on his side, and he was fuming, despite some The Multiple Man’s dirty tactics, like hitting Anton’s head on the floor, and then Jeff Marx spoiling boiling coffee onto Anton’s face. Anton joined International Incident because he wanted to make a name for himself, and International Incident were certainly doing that. He had for so long lain in the shadows. All he wanted was glory and recognition, he thought he would get it when he first came into PCW, but he didn’t, he lay in the corner jobbing, as he watched everyone else being cheered for by the fans. Anton wanted it so much, like it was in PCW, the feeling would be incredible, and he wanted the lust so much. So, when The Chef approached him about joining International Incident. He would then have The Chef, Juan Borro, Kevin Klaymore, Flaymin Aymin and Reeco Suave to watch his back, as Jeff Marx stated… “Just another jobber making a wise career move.” Anton didn’t want it to be seen like that but really… it was. Would the fans now boo him? He didn’t want it, he really didn’t but he knew they would cheer for him again. They had too! Anton sat on the couch, wearing a red jumper with a hood on it, and blue jeans, not usual attire for him. His hair was, as always, gelled to perfection. He had black sneaker on, which had the Nike logo on the tongue of them. On the television now, was The Phantom Booker’s speech on Turmoil about his bid to buy back PCW. Anton was extremely happy about this, as when he was younger, watching PCW, it was at it’s peak when The Phantom Booker was Owner, it was a lot fairer, and wasn’t being run down by Tyler Nelson. Maybe, with The Phantom Booker back, Anton would get treated better! He wasn’t sure though. Though, the matter of Charlie Loc came first to Anton, he couldn’t be left in a little dream world. Anton got up of the couch and began the stretch, he then yawned before making his way out of the room and into a corridor. He tuned right and entered his own room. Which had posters on the walls on ‘Linkin Park’ famous soccer players and wrestlers. He grabbed an already packed kit bag, which was black and blue and said ‘Nike’ on it. He put it over his shoulder and lazily walked out of the room. “MOM! I’m going to training now!” Anton shouts from the front porch of his house. “OKAY! Be back in three hours, you need to get your flight in time! His mother replies. Anton walked out of the house and into the street, towards the headquarters of his Martial Arts teacher Tsunami Chow-Lee, the greatest of all Martial Arts teachers. Anton was hoping he would have learned enough karate manoeuvres to use against Charlie Loc. He was hoping he could get a lot of training in as well today. For in five hours, he would have a flight to Omaha, Nebraska, were Friday Fusion would be held. He could only pray everything would go well in his match against Charlie Loc, Loc was such a talented competitor, and Anton didn’t know whether he would be able to cope with him. He was now heading towards the centre of New York now, as he walked swiftly up the sidewalk. So many people passed him, young old, working people, the unemployed, the law abiding, the not so law abiding. So many people passed, and a lot were PCE fans, some looked at Anton in disappointment, and some even in disgust for he had teamed with International Incident, a stable, many didn’t like, a controversial stable, if you will. Though International Incident was not about hating America and being bad, they were about standing up and believing in your country, for so long Ireland was unrecognised in wrestling, then, when Paddy O’Shea and Anton entered, it all changed, but not dramatically, Ireland still wasn’t getting the proper respect, and when Paddy was out injured, Anton was on his own, standing up for Ireland. Ireland had a special place in Anton’s heart, he loved it, the people were the nicest in the world, and it was a great place to be in. Anton didn’t hate America, not at all, he lived in America, it was a great place, but it was already the most powerful and popular country in the world. It had everything, yet Ireland, as famous as it was, still wasn’t getting the recognition it deserved, and Anton wanted to play a part in changing that. He would start on Friday; he would give Charlie Loc all he had, and would make sure Loc would not get off easily. For he had to prove his worth to the world, he just had to! Anton know that the crowd would probably boo him on Fusion, he didn’t want that, in fact he would hate that, but he knew he would earn their love and respect back again, he knew, how it would happen, for it was all part of the plan, the plan that everyone would soon find out, one that would shock the world and wrestling alike. This plan would be like no other, for it would bring Anton to where he always wanted to be… the top! Anton stopped for a moment; he was now in the very centre of Manhattan. He put his kit back down, kneeled beside it and unzipped it. Inside lay various pieces of attire, but one object stood out from the rest, it sparkled in the sunlight, it was a belt, more particularly, the PCW Cruiserweight Title Belt. It was beautiful, Anton smiled at the site of it, the carvings were so beautifully done, he loved it with all his heart, and didn’t want to let it go. This was what stood between him and having the fans back on his side again, having the adrenaline rush of cheers every time he entered the ring, well… Anton didn’t intend on giving either up… They Would Cheer for him again.
