The Golden Saga: The Long Road to Nowhere (Epilogue)

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-24-02 DarkStar sits in the front seat of the beautiful Porsche, he adjusted it. Blue Leather, it was perfect. He looked out the passanger side window and saw them putting a black cover over the beautiful Corvette, they loaded it into a truck to drop off at the estate until he got back. The car, in itself, was a material possesion. Therefor it never mattered to DarkStar, he didnt care that it could be faster, he didnt care that it could be prettier. It was the embodyment of spirit it represented. As a child he worshiped the ground that these machines peeled off of. His father had one. He hated his father, but he remembered it all. So now he watches it be towed into obscelesence. If this was any other time in his life, the mere sight would have made him burst into tears, it didnt this time though. He was too busy playing with the equalizer on his sound system. 'Kairos' by This Day Forward bumped out of the back of the car. It was loud, it was abrasive, and he could do what he wanted. The car dealership was aware who he was, of his stature. He was the pinnacle of life. The top. He was better then everyone. Talena walked over and knocked on the window, he looked over and smiled, unlocking the door, she got in holding her doll. 'Where are they taking your car?' He kissed her nose. 'Back home Talena, things are different now.' She didn't quite understand. This wasn't making sense inside her head in the least. That car was all she knew of him, this wasnt like him at all. The seats were comfortable though. 'How much did this cost?' He smiled like someone who won the lottery, 'Thats the best part, it was all free. Tyler arranged for it. They gave in to it. Were at the big time now Talena' He pushed his foot down and began to pull out of the dealership. His lips were still curled into that smile. It made Talena happy to see, she was still worried about him though, worried about how he was acting. He drummed his hands on the leather steering wheel and marvelled at the handling. You see, the car wasn't the problem. He didnt have a attatchment to this car. It was just pretty and it made cool sounds and went fast. He loved it though. The feeling of Power. He glanced over to his lover and his lips parted; 'Babe, we are going to live like rock stars, just like we always wanted.' He laughed, like he almost knew the next few words rang of more meaning then he believed himself. 'Things are going to change now.'
