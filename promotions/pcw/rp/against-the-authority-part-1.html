Title: Against the Authority- Part 1 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: -- The established authority. No one likes it. Unless, of course, you belong to it. In Primetime Championship Wrestling, this authority is The Front Office. But..the established authority does not just reign in PCW. It reigns EVERYWHERE. In businesses. In governments. And, oh yes, in schools. For, in Salt Lake City, Utah, a new regime has been established at Brigham Young High School. The old principal has retired...and a new man has come in. A new established authority.....brought in by the Superintendant of Schools for one reason. Tame Mr. Trapezoid. Yes, while the outrageously popular geometry teacher has proven himself an excellent teacher, the school board have some slight problems with him. He curses. He encourages pre-marital F.U.C.K.ing, a big no-no in Mormon land. Generally, to the board, the ends don't justify the means. And so, over the weekend, they did a little 'redecorating' of the HIT's room. The HIT is on his way to the classroom, looking to do a little bit of preparation for Monday's classes. What he finds is the beginnings of his greatest teaching challenge ever.. The HIT: Dum de dum dum dum...hmmm, boy, can't wait to get to work today! :Carrying a large pile of materials, The HIT manages to open the door to the classroom. Unable to see over the stack, he walks over to his desk, and places it down. He suddenly looks around his room, and realizes something is wrong..: The HIT: AH!!!! DEAR GOD! WHAT THE HELL HAS BEEN DONE TO MY ROOM?! :All of The HIT's trademark teaching implements have vanished. No giant 'F.U.C.K.' poser on the wall. No giant diagrams of spheres in the form of female breasts. No fun mathematics puzzles. But, worst of all....: The HIT: NO MATH BOOKS! SOMEONE FUCKING STOLE ALL OF MY SPECIALLY MADE GEOMETRY TEXTBOOKS! WHO THE FUCK WOULD FUCK WITH ME LIKE THIS?! I swear to GOD, if Brandi and Walden came over here and did this to me I will poke them both with a compass until blood starts streaming from every last PUNCTURE WOUND! :The HIT is still having difficulty comprehending the enormity of his loss. Years of his teaching philosophy and work on his own math books have been taken from him.: The HIT: Yeah, I can remake all this stuff.....but its the bloody PRINCIPLE OF THE THING! Still though....there's no way in hell Walden and Brandi could get access to this building! GAH! Fine week for this to happen to me too, Brandi and Walden to deal with Tuesday, and four people, including Walden and my tag team partner Teresa Tomas to go through on Friday in search of Venneman Pendragon's Television Title. There's way too much to think about right now for my own good....and yet, it's kind of hard to focus on wrestling when SOMEONE'S ROYALLY FUCKED WITH YOU! :Trapezoid collapses back into his seat, a seething anger written all over his expression. He is literally shaking, as he goes into his desk drawer, and pulls out his trusty compass. Jabbing the air with it several times, The HIT looks ready for war.:: The HIT: There's only one explanation....the school system of Salt Lake City finally got me. It's not enough for those ungrateful bastards that I've raised composite math SAT scores 50 points SINGLE-FUCKING-HANDEDLY! They want me to be one of those mindless drone teachers! Every since I've came here it's been, 'Trapezoid, don't do this!' or, 'Trapezoid, don't do that!', or, 'Trapezoid, you can't tell them to F.U.C.K. at their age!' Well, the school board can officially go F.U.C.K. THEMSELVES! I WILL NOT TAKE THIS GRAVE INSULT LYING DOWN! There's only one option I have left....one recourse...and that is....OPERATION DELTA PI! ::Resolve now lights The HIT's features, as he digs into his desk, coming up with a blueprint, which he lays out full-length on his desk.:: The HIT: I knew this day would finally come....so, I've been prepared for quite some time. Being 'different' in the teaching profession makes it inevitable.....I've never been one for staff meetings. The other math teachers hate me, I've always made them look like shit. Which is what they are, a bunch of old hags who haven't changed their teaching techniques in decades! I'm the threat to them...and their archaic methodology! I swear though......they will not beat me. I WILL NOT LOSE TO THE AUTHORITY OF THIS SCHOOL! Nor shall I lose to the authority of Primetime Championship Wrestling! :Trapezoid goes into his pocket, pulling out a cell phone. He smiles, as he looks down his class list....: The HIT: Operation Delta Pi.....begins.....now. My allies? My students here...and in PCW, The Teaser....Teresa Tomas. The quest to destroy my authoritatian adversaries commences here, in my classroom....room 203, Mr. Trapezoid, Geometry. Soon, my 25 students will arrive, and with them, I can begin planning my vengeance. They took advantage of me and my natural kindness, but NEVER AGAIN! No more Mr. Nice Trapezoid. Brandi, Timothy......I'll see you two on Tuesday, but if I haven't taken care of this situation yet......I suggest you two write out your wills..because the result will NOT be pretty in the least. :The HIT begins dialing the number of one of his students, as we fade away..: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
