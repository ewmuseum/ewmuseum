Celestial Realm Extremist

Date: not entered The god’s been silent lately... Ever since the Spring Fling, where many of you mortals would say that he jobbed not only once...but twice to Pharaoh. The TV title is out of his grasp...and so is Isiy Xansiv. But now Akiyama holds not only the godhood of his own...but the godhood of Pharaoh as well. He is twice the god he was before. Now, this is where you simpletons say something in the likes of, “Akiyama still lost the TV belt to Pharaoh! He still got shafted out of the match!” But you are wrong... Just as wrong as it is for Big Daveylicious to be the Extreme champion. If anything, Akiyama was the sole winner in that bout...Pharaoh holding onto some useless material he’s bound to lose, and obtaining a wife that’s just as defective as he is. The Neo Void God...however...took away a god’s existence...took away Titu Xansiv’s being...his entire identity was being a god...and now the NVG has stripped him of that, and now uses it to fuel his own Celestial© being. Have you ever taken a man’s reason to exist and used it as gas on the fire of your life? No...none of your mortals could ever do such a thing. Your minds are no where near experiencing such Divine emotions...and anything that you think could resemble the feelings...it’s just your mind playing tricks on you. So all in all...the true victor is Akiyama...while Pharaoh lost everything he has ever known about himself. So where has the god been all this time? Why did we not see anything from him regarding all that has happened at the Pay Per View regarding Ultimate Authority and the Syndicate? What are his thoughts upon all that has happened? What does he have to say about any of this? Nothing... ...for now... Ever since after his match with Pharaoh, the NVG’s been making some preparations...seems like those pricks Nelson and his corporate lapdogs can book Akiyama into an Extreme Title match with the former Five Star Wrestling star. Big D, formerly of Thugs-4-Hire...partner of Mike Frampton,.. Nothing of this man is even remotely impressive...not even his associates. Akiyama seems to have a magic touch with certain things...once the god takes a belt from someone...he makes it Divine...and then everyone wants to grab hold of it. You want results? Look at the Tag Team belts...look at the Television belt... Now...look at the Extreme title... Who held this belt lately? LeBlanc? Daveylicious? Core? At least one of them was skilled enough to hold this belt in the past few months. But even Core could not overthrow the stench that was left after a double reign from Karl LeBlanc and Big Daveylicious... But just like before...Akiyama was going to act as the disinfectant to sterilize yet another tarnished title. This would be the third belt he’s had to commandeer because of disgrace... Third time’s the charm, as they say... One after another...their green heads fell side by side amongst the group. With each crack, another fell, from the airborne view, it looked like a path was being made, each and every tree falling behind in a row. So much waste of good plant life... As if the god cared... This realm was already tarnished by the presences of mortals like Pharaoh and Big D...a few trees knocked down wouldn’t ugly the joint up any further. Below under the darkness of the wooded section, a large mass roamed. His trunks swatted against trunks, cracking through their bark and felling them in one swoop of his limb. The deafening crash of timber to the ground did not slow his pace as he stormed through the forest aimlessly. His mind was lost in its own thoughts, and only his physical presence operated to make sure nothing stood in his way while he was planning. His physical presence...it was kind of like running on automatic...destroying anything in sight...like a machine...a machine that was designed to demolish no matter who or what was the target. You know how God is said to be in more than one place at one time? Well...since Akiyama attained a new level of godhood this Sunday... guess who has a new Celestial power? Everything seems to fade into the blackness of the night... ---*---*--- And when focus returns, we find the Neo Void God standing atop of a five story building in a city of darkness. The sky was night, with no stars, and a moon that shined a sliver of silver upon the utopia. Dressed always in his black vinyl zippered pants and his black see through mesh shirt, he lifted his head and stared at the horizon of the city. Thousands of buildings and skyscrapers were the color of silver or black, intertwinding like a black and white television contrast. Lights that existed within the buildings were silver, with some faces of the buildings a solid, reflecting black glass substance. The god’s head tilted below, where pedestrians walked back and forth along the sidewalks towards their destination. Cars also lined this black and white town...their colors falling into the order that is silver and black. Who were these people? What were they doing here? Where was “here” anyway? Akiyama nodded slowly as he looked directly down below the building he stood on. As if by his will, a small gap in the flow of pedestrians opened up. His foot stepped on the edge before he bounded off the building. His silver ponytail fluttered behind him as he plummeted to the street, landing in a crouched position with a boom. No one turned a curious ear or eye towards him, all of them not realizing his presence... ...much as how PCW does now... But here, as well as there...his presence is dominant. The god moved through the flow of people until he reached the streets. The heavy flow of traffic suddenly came to a halt as he placed his boot in the road. He walked on, his eyes focused straight ahead on the target before him. He never even turned an eye towards the coupe that was speeding towards him. Looks like one car cared not to conform to his will. No matter... Akiyama did not even touch the auto, but his arm snapped up before it as if he were stopping it with the palm of his hand. The car screeched immediately before flipping backwards and crashing upside down in a wreck. That’s what happened when you stepped out of place against the Neo Void God... ...you got wrecked... He moved on with his face still frozen in emotionless peace. Then he stopped before the black mirrored office building before him. The citizens moved around him like water flowing around a huge rock. His eyes scanned the surface of the building up and down. He snapped his black-nailed fingers, and everything around him seemed to melt away in a blur. It was as if everything was stretching and twisting to the snap of his fingers. Reality around him finished bending, and Akiyama found himself gazing out through a large office window. Panning out behind him, the entire office was walled in silver paint, the desk and chairs the only things black within the room. With a focused sigh, he turned around and sat down at the desk and tossed his feet upon the desk. {- How do you like it? -} enter Cyclonica...the ever present Divine Prophetess that echoed through the mind of the Neo Void God. •-It is Celestial...-• he thought, knowing that she could hear his thoughts just as he could hear hers. •-Much work has been put into constructing this realm finally, and now the haven away from that stink infested realm called earth is finished.-• {- If you hate Earth so much, why do you stay there so much? -} The blatancy of that fact drew about a sour look upon the god’s face. •-Because, dear Cyclonica...after humanity was left without God’s intervention, they evolved into something far more worse than anything that He could have willed to create. The time has come for these creatures to once again take guidance...under our Celestial© authority. Take heed...we are tired of conforming to their childish rules and regulations. From now on...we give the stipulations...we make the laws...we decide the orders. We take what is ours...like Pharaoh’s godhood, and Big Jobbylicious’s Extreme title soon to come.-• “Excuse me...” The god looked up from his mental conversation with the Divine Prophetess to find a leggy brown haired woman in a short black skirt and a black silk blouse standing at his office door. His dark eyes trailed from her black pump up those long black nylon wrapped limbs, up her pelvis and torso all the way to her black lips and the tip of her crown. But every inch of her skin...was as white as snow...literally...unlike the god’s colored flesh. “Who are you?” the god asked suddenly, rather irritated at her interruption, yet intrigued by her physique. “Another question would be why you’re here?” She stepped forward, wringing her hands and shuffling her feet under the scrutiny of the NVG. “Well, sir...I’m Shizumaru Shido...I’m your secretary.” Akiyama stared at her for a moment. “What did you say your name is?”“Shizumaru Shido, sir.” “From now on...your name is Slave-Geisha #1. Got that?” Her eyes lowered to the floor, a submissive response nodding across her face. “Yes, sir.” He nodded, a slight smirk stretching across. “So, what do you want from us, Slave-Geisha #1?” “You have guests, sir.” she answered meekly. “Guests? Whoever it is, tell them we’re not seeing anyone without an appointment.” “Actually, they do have an appointment, sir.” she pulled out a small slip of papler and read from it. “Booked right here in the slot for 9:99' Akiyama sat up, drawing his feet from the table. “9:99?” The doors flew open, and the guests walked into the room uninvited. Akiyama took more ease when he saw the identities of his guests. Placing his feet back atop of the desk, he waved away Ms. Shido. “We’ll handle things from here, Slave-Geisha #1.” with a nod, she backed out of the room and closed the door behind her. Then the god looked out to his guests and held out his hand to the window behind him. “Ladies and Gentlemen...welcome...to the Void.” “Can the introductions!” the towering Darkcyde`! bellowed. “We know what the d@mn Void looks like!” Akiyama looked out at his fellow Void dwellers innocently, holding his arms up in protest. “Aren’t we touchy today?” “You think you’re so f^ckin’ superior, doncha?!” he growled, growing angry quickly with Akiyama’s calm demeanor. It was the tug at his black cloak that drew his glare from the NVG to the teenager at his side. The Daughter of Darkness stared hard into Darkcyde’s face before stepping back behind him again. He heaved a huge, angry sigh before looking back to Akiyama. “If it wasn’t for the Daughter of Darkness, Akiyama...you’d be ripped to shreds right here and now.” The god paid the threat no mind. He knew the difference between empty threats and promises. Mortals like Big D were always out there making empty threats about kicking this person’s @ss and tearing so and so a new one...but expect no threats from the Neo Void God here...if the god said he was going to drown you in gasoline and set you ablaze, you had better bring your swimming goggles. He said what he meant. “Well, in a way, we sure as f^ck are superior. After all...we are eons over humanity’s evolutionary development, and leaps and bounds over a single god or goddess’s powers. After taking Pharaoh’s godhood...we are at the top of the evolutionary scale. We rule everything...so we’re entitled to a little superiority.” The Void Assassin stepped forward with his arms folded over his chest, his blonde locks shining over his black shirt. “Maybe you do...but you have no right to call this place the Void...especially when you know very well that this place is most definitely NOT the Void.” Akiyama moved his feet from the table, sitting up to look him levelly in his eye. “ Why the f^ck not?” “First of all, this isn’t the Void!” Darkcyde`! interjected. “You created this place with all the power you drained from Pharaoh b!tch@ss!” “We’ve got no problem with you banishing people to the Void and all,” Symphony added in, the brunette stepping up from behind Shinnosuke, Darkcyde`!, and Hinageshi. “It’s just that you’re making things confusing by calling this place the Void while there’s another Void already existing.” “Wait...let us get this straight...” the NVG leaned forward, propping his elbow on the desk and scratching his head. “You all came here to get us to change the name of our realm to anything...except the Void?” They all nodded in unison. Akiyama shook his head with a small chuckle rumbling from within him. “We see...” his hand reached over to the black intercom on his desk. “Slave-Geisha #1?” “Sir?” her timid voice crackled through the intercom. “Escort our guests out of our office.” “We’re not through with you yet!!!” Darkcyde`! roared at the god enraged. The two Void Beasts, Mystic and X, stepped forward ready to fight at the sound of the Void Element leader’s animosity. “You’re going to change the name of this sh!tty realm NOW!!” The NVG drew the line right then and there. He leaned back calmly and stared at the tense Void Elements...before his eyes widened slightly. The wide metal black desk suddenly flew up from off the floor and pressed all eight of the Void dwellers against the wall! With his left leg resting atop his right thigh, his chair rolled forward by an invisible force towards them. Then once he had reached them, he stood up and glared Darkcyde`! directly in his eye...the rage way more intense than anything we’ve seen from Akiyama...ever. “You DARE to order US around?? Just who the F^CK do you think you are?? You think just because you get to boss around a bunch of flat-scan Void-Jobbers means you can give commands to US?? You have NO idea what you’re thinking when you challenge us here!! This isn’t an equal playing field like Earth or the Void...this is OUR realm...WE control this place...and everything bends to our will like our b!tch. We’re not the one to challenge right about now...because we’re not really that much patient anymore with a lot of people living these days! You test us...and we will make sure that you’re stretched out somewhere eating through a tube in your arm!! We know that you Void Elements aren’t human and all, but don’t for one second think that we wont figure out a way to have you all in crutches for a long time!!” The NVG stepped over towards Hinageshi, who merely stared blankly at the god through the pressure the desk placed on her. He could see the area above her chest turning a rosy hue on her otherwise pale skin. “Do you know who we are? We’re Akiyama-frickin-yama!! We’re the Neo Void God!! We’re the most Extreme individual in existence!! Big D is no where near as intense as we are!! We’re one hundred times mankind and ten times the gods!! Zeus is washed up! Horus should retire! Lucifer’s another b!tch on the corner! Allah should go rot in a cave with his Talibastards!! L. RON HUBBARD’S GOT NOTHING...ON US!!!” Still...the Daughter of Darkness stared into him with those hollow eyes of hers. The NVG bore into her gaze with his own...both of them lost within each other’s look. Then he stepped back and sat down in his seat. As if everything were going in reverse, Akiyama’s chair slid back to it’s position in front of the window, followed by the desk releasing the Void Elements and sliding back to where it sat before. The Void Elements fell to the floor, each of them affected by the force of the desk except for Hinageshi. She just continued to stare at Akiyama. The god snarled out loud before swiveling his chair to face the window. “We’ll change the name.” he muttered. “This is now the Celestial Realm.” Symphony coughed a bit before looking up with a questioning glance. “But wont the Celestials have a problem with this place being called the Celestial Realm?” Darkcyde`! glared at her before looking to Akiyama. Then he turned his back on him and stormed towards the door. “That’s their problem. We got what we wanted. Let’s go.” And like nothing ever happened, they all walked out of the office. Darkcyde`! led, followed by Mystic, X, Shinnosuke, Hinageshi, Symphony, and finally Kiyone. Before she closed the door, she poked her head back in and flipped Akiyama the finger. “I hate you!” the door slammed behind her as Akiyama continued to stare out at the city. The image before us zooms into the iris of the god...everything swirling about like a black vortex... Until... ---*---*--- We zoom out from the pupils of the Neo Void God as he sat crouched atop a recently fallen log. His braided ponytail draped over his shoulder as he stared down at his hands held out before him. He knew that it was those hands that would deliver violence upon the place called PCW...the last thing those insolent fools in management should have done was put Akiyama, the Neo Void God of PCW in an Extreme title match. Anything goes... The god knew that it would be those hands that would deliver the HAND OF FATE© to Big Jobbylicious, thus ending his short-lived reign as Extreme champ. Oh, you cannot forget about the HAND OF FATE©. Behind him...tons of timber lay in waste in his path...like bodies in a killing spree... Would that be the fate of PCW? Only the Divine Prophetess would be able to decide...
