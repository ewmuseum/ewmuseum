The Tank 6 - The Thing About Revenge...

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 15, 2002 Prisoner Profile File Photo: Coming Soon...Name: Jeremy Matthew DorisPrisoner Number: 596166Nickname(s): J-ThugRace: BlackBorn: 08-22-1972Height: 6'6Weight: 285Hair: ShavenEyes: BrownFacial Hair: noneTattoo(s): noneBuild: MuscularFrame: LargeIdentifying Characteristics: Massive physical presence, wears stocking cap down to his eyes.Current Sentence(s): 1995 - Two counts of Murder 1 (Life, without the possibility of parole)Previous Convictions: 1991 - Theft (Fined $1,000), 1992 - Assault (served three months), 1993 - Assault (served eight months).Family: unmarried, children unknown From the Office of the Warden “The inmates in the Tank are a lot like sharks,” says the Warden absent-mindedly as he flips through J-Thugs lengthy criminal record. “With most types of sharks, one shark becomes dominant. He becomes dominant because of his physical presence. Well, Doug Hattoni may still run all things political in the Tank, but the single most physically dominant prisoner currently dwelling within these walls is no doubt Jeremy Doris - J-Thug, as the inmates call him.” The Warden closes J-Thug’s file - he’s read it a thousand times anyway. “J-Thug is pro violence, pro mayhem, and most of all - pro black. The dominant black gang in the Tank call themselves the Thugs. They’re a much more ‘official’ gang than the Nazi’s or the Jews, who are basically a group of friends that share the same beliefs or stick together simply for survival. The Thugs are second only to the Italians in the actual declaration of Ganghood. The only unofficial thing about the Thugs is their leader... but when J-Thug speaks, the rest of the Thugs jump to attention... and no one - no one - dares question his decisions.” “J-Thug is a lifer, like the majority of the inmates in the Tank,” sighs the Warden as he puts J-Thug’s file back in the cabinet. “Some men go to prison for years after murdering someone and are then eligible for parole. Some aren’t ever eligible. Luckily for everyone out on the street, and unluckily for everyone who ever steps foot in my prison, J-Thug will never step outside of the Tank for the rest of his natural life.” “He was a small-timer early on,” the Warden says while shaking his head. “But they say he gradually turned more and more violent as he reached adulthood. By the time he was twenty-three, he was all mine - sent to me after he murdered a pair of armed police officers... with his bare hands. He took a gunshot wound to the abdomen... witnesses say it didn’t slow him down a bit - only made him angrier.” “I pictured a brute of a man when I heard he was coming to the Tank,” says the Warden as he returns to his seat. “But when he got here, I realized what made him more dangerous than I’d ever imagined - his brain is equal to, maybe even superior to, his brawn.” *** Friday, July 12 - Day 7 “What the fuck, man!?” Darryl Jarvis protests to the massive J-Thug, waiving his arms wildly in the air. “You said we was gonna pop that motherfucker, man! What the fuck!?” J-Thug cooly leans over the railing of the cell balcony, staring down at the main floor of Unit B - more particularly, at Jason Snow who’s being lead out of Unit B in handcuffs on his way to yet another PCW show. “Lucky bastard,” whispers J-Thug to himself - even his whispers rumble. “Are you even fucking listening to me, man!?” Darryl persists to J-Thug’s left. A smarter man would have realized J-Thug’s silence is dangerous. “I’m listening,” J-Thug says, never taking his eyes off Snow’s back. “Problem is, all I hear is you talking shit. What the fuck do you want me to do, Darryl?” “I want you to do what you said you would! I want you to make an example of that bitch!” Darryl Jarvis says. He’s starting to lose track of his tone of voice and it’s dipping into dangerously bossy tones. “Things have changed,” J-Thug says without looking at his mouthy companion. “Snow’s in bed with the Italians now. He may not be a full-fledged member, but still... I can’t fucking touch him.” “What!?” Darryl Jarvis says with a scowl. Ever since he took a vicious beating at the hands of Jason Snow a few nights back, he’s been eager to get revenge. He even goes so far as to slap J-Thug’s massive shoulder, “Are you afraid of the motherfucking Italians now? What the fuck is that all about, nigger? You backing down from the Italians?” Finally, J-Thug has had enough. “For you?” he says, turning his head sharply toward Jarvis, “You want me to start a fucking war with these motherfuckers? Are you forgetting who brings every ounce of drugs up in this motherfucker? You want me to tell all these motherfuckers that they can’t get high no more because I have to start a war with the motherfucking Italians? Over you? Fuck you, Darryl - Fuck you!” With that, J-Thug straightens himself and walks off toward his cell, leaving Darryl Jarvis angry, yet speechless. There’s no doubt that J-Thug would jump at the chance to harm Jason Snow, but at the present moment, he’s not willing to give up the only luxuries the prison - and more specifically, the Italians - has to offer. Jason Snow will be safe... at least from this massive monster... *** From the Office of the Warden “Politics,” the Warden sighs while looking through the headlines of a newspaper. “Politics are a necessary evil. They spring up everywhere. Church, business, and yes - even prison. You can think prison is chaotic all you want. A closer look will reveal... there’s a method to this madness...” *** Saturday, July 13 - Day 8 Jason Snow has learned that prison life is trying. Despite the fact that it offers more freedom than he first thought, it’s a constant reminder of the freedom he’s deprived of. Privacy is a thing of the past. He showers with other men on a daily basis. Women - in his case, to view only - are a thing of the outside world. The only woman he gets a chance to see is his anger management councilor, Jenny Vegas, and as beautiful as she is, there’s something about scheduled meetings that makes the situation seem somewhat odd. Everything has changed. And somehow he feels that long after he’s released from the confines of these walls, things still will never be quite the same. This place is going to change him. He knows that already. However, even in these walls, every man still has business that he must attend to. “I hear you know things,” Snow says, pulling the young red-haired man, known only as the Rat, aside. “Oh yeah?” The Rat smiles, “Where’d you hear that?” Snow doesn’t like the freckle-faced young man’s tone, nor does he like the cocky little smile on his face. “That doesn’t matter,” he says while walking with his arm around the Rat’s skinny shoulders. “All that matters is I hear you know things. I need to know things too.” “Ah!” says the Rat, still smiling. Snow may have an obvious physical advantage over the nineteen year old red-head, but the Rat has had plenty of time to learn the way prison life works. “Well, yes, I do know things. But how do you know I know what you want to know?” Snow stops walking, grabbing the Rat by his boney arm to stop his pace as well. “I got word a few days ago that a friend of mine - Miggy Sanchez - is in the infirmary. You know anything about that?” Snow tightens his grip on the Rat’s arm. “I might,” says the Rat, smirking at Snow’s firm grip. With ties to every major gang in the Tank, the Rat is not an easily intimidated young man. “But it’ll cost you.” “Cost me?” Snow says with a raised eyebrow. “Damn right,” the Rat sneers. “Information is a valuable thing in here, man! And I am the info man.” Snow sighs, “What do you want?” “A blowjob!” The Rat says with a chuckle showing that he’s not serious. Snow scowls because he knows the request would be serious if he were to consider it. It takes every ounce of restraint in Snow’s body to keep him from slapping the bushy red-haired young man. Seeing the fierceness of Snow’s eyes, the Rat’s chuckle fades off. “Alright, alright. Ten bucks for my guts, bud.” Still glaring, Snow reaches into the right pocket of his faded blue jeans, pulls out a pair of five dollar bills, and hands them to the Rat. “See?” the Rat smiles as he takes the money. “That wasn’t so bad, was it?” “Just tell me how Miggy got put in the hospital,” Snow sneers. “Do I have to?” the Rat chuckles, still not quite grasping the fact that he’s dangerously close to pushing Snow over the edge - an edge that will surely lead to a complete disregard for the fact that they’re standing in on of the most well-guarded rooms in all of the Tank. Snow’s piercing glare wipes the Rat’s smile away. “Christ man! Loosen up! If you’re looking for an answer, why not look right under your nose? Or more particularly, right over my shoulder.” Snow peers over the Rat’s right shoulder, past his smiling face, and finds himself staring directly at the floor’s pool table - occupied by the one eyed Nazi, Harvey Blake. Suddenly, it all makes sense to Snow. He now understands why Miggy was trying to talk him into joining the Aryans a few days back - Harvey Blake threatened him... and then... he made good on the threat. *** From the Office of the Warden “Doug Hattoni,” starts the Warden, “is the man who brings drugs into the Tank - everyone is loyal to him. The Rat knows that if he can keep providing people with information, they won’t kill him. In the Tank, providing a service keeps you out of the morgue.” “Some people suck dick,” the Warden shrugs, “Others can keep you out of trouble. But what might be the most valuable service a man can provide in the Tank is the ability to defend yourself and those around you. Everyone wants to have a friend who’ll kick some ass if the situation calls for it. That’s why everyone would love to be J-Thug’s friend and Miggy is pretty much left to himself. But is that so different than in the real world?” *** “Doug!” Snow bellows as he rounds the corner and steps into Doug Hattoni’s neat and tidy cell. The old man, surprised, looks to his left to find the young lion - chest heaving - staring through the half-closed bar gate at him. “Jason,” Doug says a little shocked, “I was about to send for you.” “Well you got me!” Snow says as he enters the cell and stares at Doug face to face. “So much fire, kid - settle down for a second. I have to talk to you.” “No, Doug, I have to talk to you!” Snow insists, leaning forward on Doug’s upper bunk, going so far as to glare up at the Tank’s most prominent mobster. His words catch Doug’s attention. “You said that if I joined up with you, I could have anything. I know what I want now.” “You do?” Doug blinks. “I want Harvey Blake dead.” After a long moment of silence, Doug begins to chuckle. “You want Harvey Blake dead? And I suppose you want to kill him?” Snow nods. “You see, young lion,” Doug says while shuffling a deck of cards that Snow thinks are always with him, “Things don’t work like that. You don’t just run off killing whoever you feel like in here. That’s my job - I give you the order, you do the job. But not without my say so.” “Then give me the order,” Snow says with teeth clenched. “You’re a spirited one, Jason - so full of life... so full of hate,” Doug says with a smile. “But you’re still a rookie yet. A hit like Harvey Blake... you’ve got to know what you’re doing. Harvey Blake is no pushover, and on top of that, he has allies... many allies.” “I’m giving you fair warning, Doug - I’m not going to just sit back and let that sick fuck walk around this place like he owns it.” “I own it,” Doug says seriously. “Don’t forget that. Never forget that. As for Harvey Blake, I say this - leave Blake to me. As a show of my loyalty to you, Jason, I promise you, Harvey Blake will be taken care of. And as for you...” Snow’s eyes brighten with curiosity. “Yeah?” “It’s time for you to prove your loyalty to me,” Doug says with an evil smile. “How?” “An old enemy of mine, Boris Valinov, was brought to the Tank a few days ago,” Doug stops shuffling his cards and looks directly at the young lion leaning on his bunk. “I want you to pay him a little visit.”
