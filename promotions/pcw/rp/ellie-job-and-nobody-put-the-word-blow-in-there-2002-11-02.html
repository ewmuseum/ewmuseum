Ellie~!? Job? And Nobody Put The Word 'Blow' In There...

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Ellie~!? Job? And Nobody Put The Word 'Blow' In There... Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 02/11/02 “Like.... SO bored!” To the sounds of a whiny, yawning voice our shot opens up on PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall sitting in the huge leather chair behind her husband’s desk. Eleanor : “Where’s Xavi? He needs to hire more people for me to fire.” She pulls the numerous bands and bracelets on her left wrist out of the way to see the face of her chunky watch. Eleanor : “Urgh.... For an Office this place WAY doesn’t have enough fun stuff... Where are those balls that you knock together and play with?!?” She folds her arms in a huff and brings her legs up so to sit cross-legged in the spacious chair... In answer to her question, the voice of the CEO himself is then heard, in a playful tone, by the door. Xavier : “Right here, my little Shag Squirrel.” The Scientologist winks as he walks through the door, holding his jacket open and gesturing to his groin. With giddy excitement, Ellie~! unravels herself and runs around the desk, leaping towards her man who catches her, the little prep wrapping her legs around him. Eleanor : “Like, what took so long? I was a bored Ellie~!” Holding onto her, Kannon walks over to his desk and unwraps her legs from around his waist, sitting her on his desk as he walks around to his chair. Xavier: “Why didn’t you just watch TV or something?” Kannon nods to the flat screen TV he has mounted on the wall while Ellie~!’s eyes follow him around the desk, turning to lay on the desk and keep staring at him with her Bambi-esque eyes before pointing the light Blue orbs down at the remote control. Eleanor : “Um... I SOkinda spilt nail varnish remover on the remote control and it wouldn’t let Ellie~! watch.” As she gives an innocent grin, the CEO picks the remote up and tries to use it... It is indeed ruined. Ignoring his annoyance, Ellie~! continues on about her day while the CEO looks at the paper work he brought with him. Eleanor : “So then I did my finger nails in Pink and Silver...” She excitedly shows him her nails, which he looks at with peculiarity. Xavier : “Um, there’re Purple and Gold, my little Poon Puppy.” Sighing, the little prep rolls her eyes. Eleanor : “I like, SO know! I did them and the I got bored again so made them just Pink but I didn’t like it so then I made them all Silver and had them for a few minutes then got WAY bored so then did them Purple and Gold then did my toenails too...” Rummaging through the paper work, Kannon furrows his brow. Xavier : “Is that all you did? I was gone like 3 hours!” Ellie~!’s face turns to one of annoyance and huffs. Eleanor : “Yes, I know! No Xavi makes bored Ellie~!” Leaning back in his chair, Xavier looks at Ellie~! who after a few seconds just blankly looks back, her lightly freckled face devoid of any make-up. Xavier : “So that’s all you did? Your nails?” The little prep does a double take and points to her face. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!? I like, SO removed my make-up... Then forgot I didn’t bring any with me to put it back on.... Oh, and I brushed my hair too!” Smiling at her husband till, she runs her fingers through he long, wavy light Brown hair with Blonde highlights and seeming proud of what she accomplished in the time she was without her man. Eleanor : “Then I played with that thing...” She points to the PC. Eleanor : “... And found this thing called the Internet where I could buy Yachts without moving, so I bought 2... And a Panda.... Oh, and a Villa in France... And a trip into space in 2043, you might want to mark that in the diary.” Thinking of his bank balance, Kannon shudders and tentatively contemplates a question. Xavier : “Um... Ellie~!, my little Fuck Ferret... have you ever considered, you know...” While the CEO is hesitant in asking, Ellie~! looks back at him with narrow eyes. Eleanor : “I’ve, like, SO told you before... You wanna put little Mr. Hubbard up there, you TOTALLY have to buy me my own Unicorn!” For once, we see Kannon almost blush as he shakes off what Ellie~! thought he was suggesting. Xavier : “Noooo, not that...Although would a Pony do?” She snaps back at him. Eleanor “Like... NO!“ The CEO gives a “Damn!” under his breath before continuing, still approaching the matter with caution. Xavier : “I mean, have you ever thought of... If you want to... Maybe.. Getting a job?” His bright Green eyes look to his wife with hope while she contemplates the thought, looking rather confused. Eleanor : “Um.... Aren’t I, like, Commissioner and stuff?” ‘Treading on Eggshells’ best describes how the Scientologist is approaching this. Xavier : “Um.. Yeah, but I was thinking maybe, something as well as that.... To get you out of the house, and the Mall, and my bank account...” Much to his relief, Ellie~! seems quite keen on the idea and swings her legs over the desk so she sits facing him, enthusiasm on her face. Eleanor : “I could be the President... He has a big chair like you and this WAY cool house in Washington that’s SO all White... I think it’s called ‘President Place’ or something!” The little prep fidgets with enthusiasm as she looks to her husband for support. Xavier : “Maybe something a little less demanding to start of with, less hours and travel... And effort.” She shrugs it off and keeps thinking, scratching her head and looking deep in consideration before snapping out f it and holding a finger up. Eleanor : “I could start charging you for Sex!” Kannon shakes his head with purpose and Ellie~! rethinks. Eleanor : “Yeah, maybe not.... If I’m only charging, like, one measly Unicorn for you know what, I don’t think I’ll earn that much from the rest.... Like, damnzzies.... I’d have SO made a great whore!” She looks disappointed as she thinks of more jobs. Eleanor : “Or, I could just get a job and charge you anyway... YAY PROSTITUTION!“ Kannon drops his head into his hands, this not going the way he planned. Xavier : “Um, or how about you get a paper and search the job section?” The little prep hops off the desk excitedly, scurrying over to the door. Eleanor : “Like, WAY good idea!.... Where do I get one of them from?” Xavier sighs. Xavier : “If you go back to the flat, I’ll have them sent to you.” She smiles, waving to him before skipping off through the door talking to herself excitedly. “Like, WAY cool... Ellie~!’s going to have a job and everything!” As the door closes behind her, Kannon lets out a sigh of relief as he leans back in his chair and kisses his Credit Card while the shot fades to Black. “Ellie~! with a job.... You are saved my plastic friend...” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
