Eternal Rewards.

Date: not entered We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he shoots toward the heavens, nausea setting in as the dimensions shift and clouds part, revealing the awesome golden gates of Olympia. Steele’s corporeal form is hurled through the opening gates, tumbling past cheering throngs of toga-ed spirit hailing the conquering hero. The twelve columns of the apex of the mountain loom before Big Al, the birthplace and home of the gods and goddesses of Greek mythology, all rising from their various thrones. Despite his fantastic speed, Steele can’t help but grin as he sees the beaming paternal face of Zeus, rising and holding a shining goblet of wine in toast to his son, who is coming in awful fast… SKREEEE!! Big Al skids across the dais on his face, the horrible sound of skin squealing in protest as friction quickly stops Big Al. Looking rather undignified, Steele slides to a stop into the holy chamber, his face contorted in pain, as the other gods raise goblets in tribute. He rises, smoothing out his toga, and stands before the assembled gods. “Welcome Big Alan Steele, son Zeus, and hero to Olympus! We drink to thee!” Big Al stands and grins, despite the strawberry forming on his cheek. A cherub flies up to him and presents a goblet of wine, which he hastily snatches at and holds aloft. Big Alan Steele: “And hail Olympia, who hath presented me with the opportunity! Hi-Keeba!!” All the gods: “Hi-Keeba!!” Steele quickly tosses back the wine and motions to the cherub to bring more. Eris leans into her husband, Ares, and whispers loud enough for everyone to hear. Eris: “He takes that two-drink minimum seriously, doesn’t he?” Zeus quiets her with a look, before breaking into a wide smile and strides from his throne. In but a few paces the huge god reaches Steele and wraps a meaty arm around his shoulders, forcing the air from Steele with his forceful, manly hug. Zeus: “Congratulations, son! You have once again brought honor and glory to Olympus in a time and age where we, the immortals, are looked upon as mere relics of a long forgotten society. Truly, an achievement worthy and befitting my offspring!!” Hera: (hissing angrily) “Bastard offspring of an illicit encounter!! His mere existence brings nothing but shame and…” Zeus: (growling) “Not now, DEAR…” Athena, goddess of righteous battle steps forward, ignoring the sputtering Hera. Athena: “Yes! In an eon not accepting of the powers of Olympus, you have showed all the strength and courage inherent in your beliefs… and your indomitable spirit! I hail thee, Big Alan Steele, newest god of Olympus!!” Other gods: “Hail! Hail! Hail!” Big Al’s eyes bug out wide, as the realization dawn on him. His hand strays to his midsection, where the PCW World Championship belt glints in the unfettered sunlight. BAS: (stumbling for words) “You mean… I hath succeeded?! I hath gained…” The Lord of Olympia, Zeus, laughs loudly and gives Steele another squeeze, squeezing the breath out of Big Al again. Zeus: “But of course!! You have become one of us, Big Al! You have become an IMMORTAL!!” Tears well up in Big Al’s eyes, the hopes and dreams of his dementia finally fulfilled! BAS: (weeping) “All the pain and suffering in the name of Olympia, all proven erstwhile! Now I, Big Alan Steele, shall reside in the heavens for all eternity, finally successful in my quest to become a god of Greek mythology!! (eagerly) What shall I become the protectorate god of? Righteous battle? Chastity? Wrestling?” Eris: (giggling) “Homosexuality?” Big Al looks daggers at the goddess of discord, who retreats behind her husband. Zeus looks around nervously as he disentangles himself from his son. Zeus: “Well, I’m sure by the time you are ready to join us we’ll come up with SOMETHING. In the meantime, you just establish yourself in PCW as a worthy champion, one befitting Olympia, as we all in attendance know you can be!” Big Al looks around, confusion clouding his visage. BAS: “But, I am not understanding. I have triumphed!! The day has been won! The PCW conquered!! I have earned my place at your sides, yet all is not ready?! What trickery is this?!” Zeus: “Well… it’s not that simple, son. You can’t just step into a position here. First we have to create the position, then it has to be ratified, then the legal department needs to look at the contract to make sure it’s proper. You know how it is. Then, the new god position has to posted for a period of ten days, I believe…” BAS: “I cannot believe what I am hearing!! I was led to believe that once I achieved ultimate success in the PCW, I would be accepted into the conclave of gods with open and loving arms!! I hath been deceived!!” Athena: “Nothing could be further from the truth, good sir Alan. You are clearly the most qualified demi-god for the job.” Zeus: “Yes! She’s right, you know. Once you die, you’ll merely have to submit your resume…” BAS: (eyes wide in shock) “DIE?!?! But I hath gained immortality! Godhood demands immortality!!” Zeus: “And you couldn’t be more right! Being a god shall grant you phenomenal cosmic powers! Reality will be yours to shape and bend at your every whim! The secrets of the cosmos shall be revealed to your mind the instant you taste the sweet ambrosia the feeds us all! You shall create and destroy worlds for pure amusement purposes! Followers of all race, creed, and species shall prostrate themselves before your awesome powers, wielded from your own throne here upon Olympus!!!” Zeus’ eyes dart back and forth as he quickly adds an addendum. Zeus: “But you have to be dead first.” BAS: “I am NOT understanding this! Immortality means imperviousness to death itself!” Hermes: “Well, look at it this way, Alan. In this dimension, you shall be granted immortality. But for you to pass into our dimension, you have to leave yours. Death is that gateway into this dimension.” Athena: “Plus, we still need to fight crime and be a beacon of hope in your crumbling world!!” Steele hangs his head, scowling, and kicks and a convenient rock for effect. BAS: (grumbling) “It still sucks.” Lord Zeus places his hands on his hips, looking down in consternation at his boy, Big Al. Zeus: “I’m sorry, my son. It’s all part and partial to the god process.” BAS: “Did YOU die?” Zeus: (laughing mightily) “But of course not!! I sprung forth from the creator Chronos, and in ultimate battle, dethroned him…” Zeus raises his fists in remembrance, getting into the retelling, only to finally notice the dour look on Big Al. Alan tries to get another god to look him in the eyes, but they are all kicking at dust bunnies or checking out the interesting art deco pillars. Zeus: “Oh…” Steele crosses his hands across his chest in huffiness. BAS: “Indeed. Only I shall have to die.” The air is again squeezed from Steele’s lungs, as Zeus tries to comfort his brooding son with another one-armed hug. Zeus: (forced cheerfulness) “C’mon!! Let’s go and check out your new kingdom in the Elysian Fields, huh? You know you wanna! That’ll cheer you right up!!” BAS: (stunned) “A kingdom?” Poseidon: “But of course! We don’t just hang out here all day! My kingdom, of course, is located beneath the waves!” Hades: (deathly whisper) “And mine far beneath the Earth’s crust… MUAHAHAHAHA!!” Big Al looks angrily at Hades before rubbing his beard thoughtfully, a slow grin finally spreading across his face. BAS: “Hi-Keeba! Such a trip may do my heavy heart good!” Zeus: “That’s the spirit!! Come, let us view you new estate in the heavens!!” Zeus grabs a hold of his son and hold his hand aloft. With a casual snap of his fingers, the world of Olympus dissolves into streaks of color, a vortex of streaks opening up and swallowing the two whole. Bright lights explode before the eyes of Big Alan Steele, and he vigorously rubs his palms at his eyes to ward off the searing pain of the white light. Just as suddenly, the trip ends, and all returns to normal. Big Al tries to blink away the blurriness, as Zeus gestures grandly before him. Zeus: (proudly) “Look, my son! Look at what the heavens of Olympus have afforded you for your courageous deeds!” Steele squints hard, his vision slowly returning to normal. The haze lifts off his eyes to reveal… BAS: (aghast) “Tis nothing but a dump!!” And indeed it is. Miles of miles of rank, steaming filth lay before god and demi-god. Zeus: “Correction! YOUR dump!!” BAS: (angrily) “I cannot believe the injustices done here this day!! I come as a conqueror of Olympia, only to be told of impending death and an eternity within a fetid dung heap!!” Zeus: (sighing) “It’s SYMBOLIC, Big Al. Everything here in Olympia, including the Elysian Fields, is related directly to your Earthly dimension. Your accomplishments have garnered you many bounties here in the heavens, including this kingdom.” BAS: “I fail to see this as a symbolic bounty.” Zeus: “That is because you are thinking in shortsighted terms!! Your godhood is almost assured! And your heroic deeds have claimed you this heavenly land that armies would battle for, which is based upon pure symbolism! Do you not see the possibilities?” BAS: (scowling) “You mean beyond the raging tire fires?” Zeus: “Okay, I’ll walk you through it. Your kingdom is representative of your PCW title. The PCW title was somewhat devalued recently…” BAS: (growling) “New Breed!!” Zeus: “Yeah… and as they kept violating the belt, well, Olympus produces a lot of trash. Heck, Ares alone…” BAS: (yelling) “SO YOU TURNED MY KINGDOM INTO A RAT’S NEST OF FILTH?! By the gods!!” Zeus: “Well, like I said, it’s all symbolism. Besides, it wasn’t like we were SURE you’d eventually win it outright…” BAS: (snarling) “Now there is faith, for you, in your own son.” Zeus: “Look, you have plenty of time to clean it up. An eternity, if you will.” BAS: “How shalt that appease me? I care not to shovel the trash of Olympia for eons and eons until then, and only then, I can build my kingdom upon reclaimed trash heap?” Zeus: “Remember… symbolic! As you infuse the PCW belt with your goodness, once again infusing it with the value it once had, then this area shall be cleared! And viola!! Good, clean land!!” BAS: “And how is that? How can this trash be cleared away as such?” Zeus: “Eh… I’ll just have Hades set his demons on trash detail. Or he can hire some illegal immigrants. Whatever. The point is, you have yourself a nice, long title reign full of righteousness and good old fashioned home-spun mid-Western traditional values, an I think, when you die, you’ll like what you see.” Steele frowns at the thought of his immortality… then freezes in horror. His face one of panic, he turns to Zeus and grips at the elder god’s flowing robes, shaking him. BAS: “Great Cesar’s ghost!! If I can only gain entrance into Olympia via death’s icy cold grip, then how have I come to be here now?” Zeus slowly reaches down and loosens Steele’s death-grip on his robes and gently speaks. Zeus: “Well… what do you remember?” BAS: (thinking hard) “Nothing, if that helps…” Zeus: “Then why is your toga all wet?” Steele looks down at his sopping toga. He smiles, his memory slowly returning. BAS: “Ah… I was taking a relaxing moment in the pool at my earthly home, Stately Steele manor…” Zeus: “Good. Then what happened?” BAS: “Steffi had wanted to show me her new high-powered hairdryer…” Zeus: “While you were in the pool.” BAS: (stopping) “With the chord in. (slaps forehead) Hi-Keeba!” Zeus: “Water and electricity are not a good mix. Hence your unscheduled visit. But do not fear, Alan, as your work is not yet finished in the mortal coil.” BAS: (shaking fist angrily) “Mortal! Why must you rub the word in my face!!” Zeus: “Look… let me just send you back, and we can work this all out another time. Remember that Olympia is proud of you, son. Never forget that.” BAS: “Even though my heavenly rewards smack of distastefulness.” Zeus: “Fare thee well, Alan! You do us proud!!” The world dissolves again, this time becoming like a flowing liquid, the images of the Elysian trash Fields washed away into clear blue rush of watery color… We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as his bohunkular form rests on the side of the pool, lower half in the water. A pole with a net attached rests on his head, the other end held by trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, wearing her usual look of disapproval. Steele coughs up a puddle of water, as Sue lifts the net from his head. A cluster of Graces rush to his aid, anxious to see if he is alright. Candi: (teary eyed) “Omigod!! Big Al, we’re so glad you are alright!! We thought you were a goner, fer sure!” Steffi: (choking up) “Yeah! I don’t know WHAT I would have done if Sue hadn’t fished you out of there!! I’m soooooo sorry about that hairdryer thing!” Suzi: “You can bet we probably… might… not ever let that happen again!” Steele looks over at Sue with bleary eyes, as she takes a drag on her cigarette. Sue: “Meh. I couldn’t let my meal ticket drown like that, now could I?” BAS: (weak grin) “By Zeus’s thunder, I thank thee, as there is much work yet to be done – on Earth as well as the heavens! Hi-Keeba!!!” Sue: “And more money to be made. Title reigns are good business.” Terri: “Hey Al! Now that you are alright, wanna take a look at my homemade Tesla coil?! Isn’t it cool?!” BAS: (waving arms frantically) “NO!! DO NOT…” Terri: “Here! Catch!” SHHHHRRAAAAAAAAAAKKKKK!!!! We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, upon the dais of Mount Olympus. Zeus frowns and wags a finger at Big Al. Zeus: “We have GOT to stop meeting like this!! I can’t keep sending you back all the time...” BAS: (sourly) “Agreed. Hi-Keeba!”
