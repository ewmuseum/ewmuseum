The CWS Goes To Stetson's

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-02 **Theme Music: 'Jump Right In' by The Urge** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Another day, another dollar, right? Lost my title. To LeBlanc, no less. Fired. Pulled a gun on one of my many superiors. Rehired. Some weeks are worse than others. I never wanted this run to last forever...but I wasn't ready to give up yet. LeBlanc, OG, and Daveylicious are going to have a VERY bad Sunday night. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [Alcohol.] Jason: You guys took me out to get drunk when I got fired...then you took me out to get drunk now that I'm rehired...you're just looking for an excuse, aren't you? Joe: Yep. [At Stetson's, a country-western bar in Tallahassee, Jason sits at the bar with Joe Stephenson, one of his CWS students.] Jason: Just wanted to know. [Joe flashes a grin at Jason.] Joe: You're welcome. [Jason takes a sip out of his Miller High Life longneck, while Joe pounds away at his Heineken. The two men rotate on their stools, and look at the line dance that's going on. In the center are Joe's best friend, Mark Farmer, and the cocky, self-centered 'star' of the CWS, Nick North. They both try to attract the attention of Ms. Sandra Davis, their fellow CWS trainee and gorgeous young woman. Mark's dressed out in his flannel shirt, jeans, black Resistol hat, and boots...while Nick's dressed in his urban contemporary black polyester and silver. Ms. Davis shakes her stuff in a tied-up plaid halter, daisy dukes, and boots. Joe just takes a long sigh.] Jason: What's up, kid? Joe: ...Mark likes Sandra. A lot. Jason: Ah, I see...worried about losing your buddy? Joe: Not exactly... [Joe's eyes dart around a bit, nervously. He struggles to change the subject.] Joe: So! Big PPV night Sunday, right? Jason: Yep. Joe: Gonna win the Extreme Title back, right? [Jason takes a long draw from his beer, and signals for another one.] Jason: Nope. [Joe looks kind of surprised.] Joe: Uh, no? [The bartender brings Jason his new beer, and Jason again takes a long pull.] Jason: Nope. [There's a short, moderately awkward pause.] Joe: Uh, you mind if I ask WHY THE FUCK NOT?? [Jason turns and looks at the moderately stressed out Joe.] Jason: Are you alright, kid? [Joe nods.] Joe: Can we focus on you, not me? [Jason kind of sighs, and drinks again.] Jason: Sure thing, Joe. [Brief pause by both men.] Joe: So? Jason: Well, I've had the ball. I ran with it. It's time I start goin' after bigger fish. My time with LeBlanc is just about over, people won't pay to see the two of us in a ring much longer. [Jason clears his throat with a sip of his beer. In the background, a loud 'Yee-HAW!' goes up.] Jason: LeBlanc and Grant a$$holes, but this Healey thing is getting me to think that we, the Malice Crew should be working a little higher up the ladder. LeBlanc and OG are Nelson's goons, but we should be going after Nelson himself. Fortunately, after Mexi takes him outta PCW, we won't have to worry much about him. But there are still other people. Frisco, Chef, Kannon... Joe: I see. [The music stops, and people go off to the bar or otherwise as a slower song comes up.] Jason: So on Sunday, I go after Big D for revenge. I go after OG since he's a ego-ridden prick. And I go after LeBlanc because I want to show him once and for all who the better man is. But if I have my way, The Rush will walk out of Arrowhead as the PCW Extreme Champion. Joe: But what if Rush gets eliminated early? [Jason shrugs.] Jason: A man's gotta do what a man's gotta do... [The trio of Mark, Nick, and Sandra run up to the bar, Mark following like a lovesick puppy, Nick looking her up and down like a Japanese buffet table, and Sandra just adoring all of the attention.] Sandra: Hi, Jason! [Jason waves a little at Sandra, who kisses him lightly on the cheek, drawing a blush. Joe is summarily ignored.] Nick: That's the most action Jase's gotten in god knows how long... [The four students laugh a little, and Jason even chuckles.] Jason: It'd be funnier if it weren't true. Mark: You know, Jason...I know this girl... [Jason holds up his hands in mock surrender.] Jason: Uh-uh...no way! Last time you set me up with someone, it was a 16-year old who STILL won't leave me alone. [Sandra smiles a little.] Sandra: She's not THAT bad, Jase! [Jason looks into a beer.] Jason: She's leaving the school. [All four of his students seem shocked.] Mark: You're kidding! [Jason shakes his head.] Jason: Nope, she's too young. I found someone else to take her place. [The four look around a little nervously.] Joe: Anyone else getting the axe? [Jason grins a little.] Jason: Not yet. Gimme time. [The bartender brings over a daquiri for Sandra, a beer for Mark, and a Manhattan for Nick. Mark raises his beer up.] Mark: Well, here's to the newly re-hired J-Core, and to the first Pay-Per-View in CWS history! Nick, Joe, and Sandra: Here here!! [The five intrepid Floridians drink to themselves, and to the future. Sandra avoids Joe's looks, while eating up Nick's and Mark's...while Jason stares down the barrel of his beer bottle...towards Sunday, and the impending explosion.]
