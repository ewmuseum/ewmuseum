Structure

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/02 Structure(n.) 1. The arrangement of all parts as a whole. 2. Something composed of related parts. 3. To pit together systematically. -Webster New Wold Dictionary1995 -Duke Williams is sitting in a leather chair in a wood panel room. He is smoking a punch cigar and glaring at the camera with an angry look. Duke is wearing all black from his Stetson hat to his black boots except for a shiny silver belt. Duke speaks directly to the camera while shaking his head.- Williams: America I’m telling you things ain’t kosher in Jew town. Look I’ve never been very good with structure. Hell, just ask Ty Nelson, cus when you get structure you get authority. The Malice Man doesn’t follow what the authority tells him to do I do it my own way. AS a kid I sucked at spelling and grammar and still do. I now have a college education but still suck at those things. Why?? Because its structure. Structure blows!! -Duke takes a big drag on his smoke as he shakes his head in disappointment.- Williams: People maybe I am getting old cus that old world way of thinking is changing. I mean kids today need some damn structure. Look at Xavior Kannon and Ty Nelson both of them are out for themselves. All kids are today. You want to see what I’m talking about, I’ll show you. Let me set it up for you. I was running late at JFK airport in New York trying to catch a flight back home to Montana. Well duh the flight was delayed and I had to wait for two hours for the next flight. The bar was closed which really pissed me off so I grabbed a Hustler from the gift shop and went to sit down in one of them uncomfortable plastic things they call chairs. A arrive in the sitting area only to find the place pack with young kids…..hippie kids. Just watch. -The camera cuts to Duke Williams standing in the airport lobby looking over a group of about thirty young men in there early twenty’s. Most of them are wearing tie-dye shirts and have on hemp hats. Some are doing yoga others are reading or talking loudly. A kid walks by Duke and bumps into his side. The camera cuts back to Duke in his chair.- Williams: Did you see that? Now hoss you know that ain’t right. But I’m a civil gentleman and I WAS going to let that pass. Let’s see it again in replay. -The camera shows the footage in the airport again then continues to play.- Hippiekid: Watch it man!! -The camera cuts back lighting fast to Duke in his leather chair in Montana. Duke shadow boxes the air and shakes his head.- Williams: IF this was wrestling I’d send him Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound…..Malice Style!!! -A video of Duke sending Xavior Kannon Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound is show. WE then return back to Duke in Montana.- Williams: That’s what I should have done. But I didn’t. You know why? -Duke sighs- Williams: I don’t know why. -The camera returns to the flashback of the airport.- HippieKid: Watch it man!! Williams: Oh hell no! HippieKid: You think just cus your tall and strong you can pick on the little guy man. Williams: You been hitting the mushroom’s son? HippieKid: Just cus I have long hair man….that mean’s I’m like a druggie man? Williams: If the shoe fits wear it. HippieKid: F*ck you man! Williams: F*ck me? You shouldn’t worry about sex pal. The first thing on your agenda you need to write down it too take a shower. HippieKid: Why, because society tells me too? Williams: No asshole, cus you stink!! Phew!! You smell worse then Ellie’s pussy. HippieKid: Your bent. Williams: At least I don’t stink. -The kid gives Duke the finger he walks away.- Williams: What a prick. -The camera cuts back to Duke in Montana.- Williams: You see what I mean hoss? Now I love my fans they’re all my Hairy Ass Hillbillies but these kids are scum!! I sh*t you not it smelled worse in there then a truck stop gas station. Parents you need to discipline your kids or they’ll end up like these turds. Hold on people, my nerves continue to get tested. -The camera goes back to the airport where the Malice Man has found a seat next to a bunch of hippies. He is listening to his diskman.- Hippie2: Hey man. -The hippie taps on Duke’s shoulder. Duke gets annoyed and takes off his headphones.- Williams: What hoss! Hippie: Can you turn down your tuneage. Williams: What? Hippie: Can you turn down your music dude. Williams: Do I look like a cowhand? Hippie: Huh? Williams: You even know what a Duke is hoss? Hippie: Hoss? Williams: Leave me alone while I listen to ole Buck Owens. Hippie: Buck Owens? Isn’t that the guy from lost in space. Williams: What do you listen too kid? Hippie: Where all Phish followers. Williams: You ain’t lying you all smell like fish. Hippie: You’re a tool. Williams: And how is that an insult? A tool is something one uses to better something else. A tool is a positive you greasy piece of sh*t. Buy a dictionary. -The kids move away from Duke who cranks up his hillbilly music and relaxes.- Williams: Score one for the guys with jobs. -The camera cuts back to Duke in Montana this time with a smile on his face.- Williams: Damn kids!! Anywho this Tuesday its Steele and Williams against Kannon and Nelson. What more can be said that hasn’t been said?? Nelson and Kannon don’t stand a chance in hell. I don’t care if they drafted the whole roster in the New UA. Nelson and his stables are worse then the Chicago Cubs. Year after year they come back and blow. I step in the ring with the world champion at my side against the swear waste of the UA. I can’t wait. I can’t wait to send them boys Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke takes a drag of smoke.- Williams: That kids is the honky tonk Truth! -The camera fades black and yellow.-
