Gimmick Search 2: Suprise Guests

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/26/02 The doors of the Gimmick search room open wide. From the darkness outside the room, Pete walks in. He's wearing a cape, and his face is painted a ghastly white. He looks around, and lurks into the room. Behind him, General Tso follows, walking slower, looking at the ground. Pete pulls the cape over his mouth and then down again, revealing a pair of fake plastic fangs. He pretends to turn into a bat, while General Tso reaches into his pocket. He walks up to Mr. Redd, and puts out his hand. Mr. Redd: What? Tso: ... Mr. Redd: Oh, ok. Mr Redd reaches down and shakes Tso's hand. Mr. Redd clutches the tiny hand and yanks his hand back. The Midget turns and runs, revealing a joy buzzer in his hand while Pete still prances around the room, thinking he's a bat. Mr. Redd: Next! This gimmick sucks! Pete turns around and looks like he's about to cry. he recovers and speaks through his false fangs. Pete: Misaw Wedd. I Vant to Suck yoor BLOOD! MUAH! Pete charges like an 800 lbs rhino, but even slower at Mr. Redd. Mr. Redd holds up a hand, and Pete stops. He puts his head down, and walks to the door, dejected.. Mr. Redd: Gentlemen, what do you say we break for lunch? Mr. Greene? Mr. Browne? Greene: Sounds good to me, sir. Browne: Excellent choice. Redd: Pete! Pete: Yes sir? Redd: We're going to lunch. We'll resume in about an hour. Pete: Can I go too? Redd: I'm sorry. The company only gives me a certain amount for meals. We couldn't afford you. The three men laugh as they pick up their briefcases and walk out the door. Pete mocks their laughter and walks over to the table, kicking it, and injuring his foot in the process. He hops around goofily, and hobbles out of the room... meanwhile, in the hallway... Pete: Tso, I don't know about this gimmick stuff... Tso: ...? Pete: I don't know. I mean, Mr. Redd is a nice guy and all, but the other two just sit there and look at me like I'm an idiot. Tso: ...! Pete: What do you mean I should be used to it?? Tso: ...! Pete: If the shoe fits! You little shithead! Tso: ... Pete: YOU ARE! Tso: ... Pete: YOU ARE! Tso: ... Pete: YOU ARE! Tso: ... Pete: I AM! Tso: ... Pete: I AM! GOD DAMNIT! Tso: ...! Pete: Yeah. You're damn right I am. Suddenly, a whisper is heard...'If you steal it, he will come...'. Pete turns around and looks at Tso, who's trying on a big goofy looking mask. Pete shrugs, and grabs a shirt. 'If you steal it, he will come...' Pete: What the...Did you hear that Tso? Tso: ...? Pete: A voice...it whispered something. Tso: ... Pete: No, it was not my stomach, thank you. This was a voice...and I think it's coming from the closet! Slowly, Pete walks over to the closet door. A green light shines from underneath it. Pete's eyes widen as the light grows brighter. From nowhere, a wind picks up, and blows the closet doors open. He sheilds his eyes, as Tso falls and gets swept down the hall. A figure appears, backlit by the green light. It's hair blows wildly as it slowly walks farther and farther from the seemingly endless closet. Pete backs up slightly, and watches for signs of humanity from the figure. The figure steps out of the closet, and Pete stares. His jaw drops to the floor as he stands in awe of the figure before him. He falls to one knee and reaches for the figure's hand. Slowly, perspective switches to his eyes, as he looks up the figure to reveal... Judas Steele? to be...you know...
