Date: --- {From the typings of Skylar Green) Last Turmoil, it was a tag match: Xavier Kannon and Rush Mason against myself. Though I tried, I lost the match for our team, won’t try to deny the obvious like some people. And then after that, I was booked to a singles match against Dante Jackson. I’m not in any way involved with Dante Jackson, and winning over him at this time really doesn’t do anything for me. So why the booking? Rush Mason, continually making requests to Xavier Kannon, has asked our boycotted owner and CEO to place me into matches in an attempt to tire me out, wear me down so that when I do meet Rush Mason at Shanghai’ed, he’ll be much more rested than myself and have an advantage. That’s why I’m now booked to wrestle Gaz Green. Another distraction attempt. Rush Mason will most surely try to interfere. Nerezza, at least, should come after Gaz. I suppose we’re even in that respect. What does this upcoming match mean to me? Nothing, really. It’s another match, another obstacle that Rush Mason tries to put in my way to keep me from being my best when we meet. Odd, he wants to prove that he’s better, but he wants me to be at less than 100% if he beats me, which ultimately proves nothing… Well, no one ever accused Mason of being too intelligent. Gaz Green is my opponent… Fair wrestler, can’t say otherwise. I’ve got nothing against him. This is a match. An exhibition match. A win by either of us will do nothing for us. Nerezza’s beating Gaz will mean more than my beating Gaz And that’s why I’ve declared Turmoil to be Nerezza Appreciation Night. What better way to show my appreciation for Nerezza’s efforts against Gaz Green, and by extension, the Front Office, than by not beating Gaz before he does? That’s right, for Turmoil, I’ll put up a fight just for show. But I WILL NOT put 100% into the match, and I WILL NOT actually try to win. If it happens, it happens; I won’t plan on it though. A quick match, I’m very, very tempted to lay down and let Gaz pin me. On the one hand, it’ll mean my getting on that plane to China that much faster. On the other hand, it’ll mean screwing with Mason’s plans since he expects me to tire myself out trying to defeat Gaz. But I’m not going to do that? Since when have I ever tried to give Rush Mason what he wants? So to you, Gaz Green, I congratulate you on your victory. And to you, Rush Mason, I hope you’ve turned in that OSW application by now. You’ll probably want to limit your unemployment time as much as possible.
