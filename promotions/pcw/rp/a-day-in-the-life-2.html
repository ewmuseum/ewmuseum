Date: Of a Goblin Sometimes, there are people who should be executed at gunpoint. I think that the HIT is one of them. I think the HIT is a retard that somehow comprehends math. Perhaps a lackluster Hellen Keller, at best. I also think Jesse Jackson isn’t really black, but like that guy in that one movie who pretended to be black. Whatever. I think a lot of things… …and anything I think is right anyway, so what’s the point in arguing? HIT, my only advice for you is to one-day wake up… Wake up, and smell the TRUTH. When I woke up this morning I had a ringing in my ear, so I slammed my head into the wall repeatedly until it went away. After I woke up a 2nd time today, ice pack held to my head, I felt it was time to get cracking on… …The Plan to destroy the HIT! “Ve don’t have a plan,” informed that old coot, LAB, readjusting his specs. Throwing my head back, and laughing at his senile idiocy, I said, “Plan B then!” He then countered this statement with the same answer as before. “Ve don’t have a plan B, Zire.” I rubbed my chin in deep thought, “Hmmm…” and almost immediately I came up with a solution, fit only to come from the likes of a mind as intelligent as mine. “VAT DO YOU MEAN ‘DO IT FOR YOU?!’” He asked, but I don’t answer stupid questions, for I answer to no one! I simply shoved him into the laboratory, shackled him to a table, and through punishment by the whip in my hand, forced him to calculate a diabolical scheme worthy enough to destroy the HIT. Eventually, he passed out from blood loss, but he left me with a simple message. “I hate you!!!” And he spat in my face!!! Well I hope he rots in hell for that! Screw him anyway. I can think of a devious way to take out the HIT on my own. Hours went by. It was hard work… But, finally I put something together. A little something I like to call… Chemical Y. “DUCK!” My loyal Duck heeded the call, coming to my aid with a, “Quack?” “Down the hatch, ol’ buddy.” I offered the green, bubbling solution I had in the beaker with a smile. … “Hey, where do you think your going?!” I asked it as it shook its head, taking cautious steps back. “I said suck it down yer goddamned hatch, ya fleabag! Or else!!” and with that kindly worded request, I hurled the beaker at the mongrel. How could it not trust me? The Duck moved, and the solution sizzled on contact with the floor, and burnt a hole all the way… Well, I’m not sure how far the hole goes. “Don’t look at me, you feathered freak! This is all your fault!” Said I, stating the OBVIOUS to the Duck. “QUACK!” it accused! The nerve! “I’m not the one who refused to drink it, wussie!” I explained, and it bet otherwise. Like hell am I going to let some Duck beat me in chicken! “How much you wanna bet I can’t drink it?” There always has been something about someone shining a light over your eyes while you happen to be sleeping. Opening my eyes, I see 6 surrounding me. “Mr. Goblin, you no longer have a small intestine.” They tell me, as I wake up for the 3rd time today. TO HELL WITH INTESTINE! I find me an ambulance with the keys still in the ignition… “HEY! Where are you taking me?!” cries a voice from the back. Some guy with a neck brace strapped to a stretcher. A hostage. I love my life. See you at Fusion HIT.
