Escape

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-1-02 'Honk' a horn blew through the misty air. It was a cold damp day, rain had been a problem all day long for those who wished to stay dry. The air was thick and breathing actually became a task when one roamed down the crowded sidewalks. One stood out though, even through the foggy mist; it was probably due to him standing 7'2', with muscles like an ox. He wore an ice blue type tank top with ice blue pants to match. They shimmered even though no sun shown through the dim gray skies. To his head he held a silver phone which he talked into as he talked. 'So what was the deal with what you said?' His voice was deep and dark as he spoke. From the other end came a soft female voice. 'I was afraid he would hurt the baby if I said anything else. You know I don't hate you, I hate that big freak. A god? Far from it, with that kind of breath he should be more concerned with finding a good mouth wash than getting his ass kicked by you,' The smooth voice reassured the giant beast known simply now as Sylo. 'The only one getting hurt is going to be Akira.....he has so much to answer for and his blood will be the only way to pay for it all,' 'Ok I under---' She was cut off and then screams were heard. A look of terror moved over the face of Sylo as he shouted into the phone hoping for some sort of response from the mother of his child. As he shouted a gray van with no markings screeched on it's breaks, flung open it's side sliding door, and five armed men leaped out dragging Sylo in as he tried to fight them off. It was no use because by that time they had opened Chloroform and pressed it against the giants nose making him pass out. The clock struck noon. He slowly opened his eyes which felt like they weighed at least fifty pounds heavier. His joints hurt and the constant sound of a beeping noise didn't help any in his ear. He tried to move his arms but couldn't, he tried his legs but couldn't, he tried his neck, and couldn't. This made his eyes spring to life as he scanned the metal surroundings. His body was now strapped to a large metal circle. He had tubes running from his chest and neck, his arms were strapped down along with his legs and neck. White coats decked out the entire lab looking area as they scurried from machine to machine; each with blinking lights and various sounds. A snarl emitted up from inside him as he tried to break free but his strength had been drained from him like motor oil from a car. He tried to shake free but the shackles wouldn't budge, not even show signs of weakness. Finally he gave up the fight and hung like a rag doll thrown away after a child was done playing. 'So your awake?' a voice echoed into his left ear making his eyes dart across the room. 'Where am I?!' Sylo snarled out into the open room making some scientist turn at the impact of his voice. 'Calm down. We are with the government and we had a call from an anonymous source which thought we needed to check you out. This is for your safety Mr. Sylo, we also got the mother of your child, Jaden but do not worry nothing will happen to her nor your child,' His voice was strong yet reassuring, but Sylo wouldn't buy into it. 'Akiyama did this didn't he?!' Sylo yelled out at the man. 'We do not know who gave us the call, we only know what we heard,' The man was young, with brown hair and a lab coat as well. He couldn't be more than thirty and no less than twenty eight. His eyes shifted back and forth against Sylo's as Sylo just hung there, plotting a way to escape. He found no weaknesses, no point of freedom, but what about Jaden? 'Let me see her,' He spoke with a low tone. 'Who?' 'Jaden! Let me see her....' 'I don't think we....' 'I SAID LET ME SEE HER!' His voice was demanding and loud. One could have thought loud enough to shake the entire room. 'Very well,' He said turning to another scientist nodding. The scientist wheeled out a gurney with Jaden strapped down to it. Sylo snarled and roared at what he saw. How could this be for their own safety when they were being treated as nothing more than animals? He had to keep his composure, for not only his safety but for Jaden's as well. 'Why is she strapped down?' 'Safety issues, we assure you she is completly safe,' His voice tried to reassure Sylo but it didn't work. Akiyama was a name buzzing around inside his head. He knew this was the work of that demonic force who claimed to be a god but was nothing more than a coward. He wanted so bad to be free right now so that he could tear Akiyama's limbs from his body and beat him with them. His thoughts of destroying Akiyama were only ceased by the voice of Jaden. 'They,' she started to speak in a groggy voice. 'They want to hurt the baby!' she screamed. 'What?!' Sylo yelled as he tried to break free once more. 'We assure you it's only for the best Mr. Sylo, you understand don't you?' The man's smirk stretched across his face as he turned to another scientist. 'Give this one 50 cc's of Demurral and give him anything that will knock him out, a horse tranquilizer, something,' 'You son of a bitch!' Sylo yelled. 'Were the government, we can do as we please.....Now take her away,' He fought to break free, every vein in his body stuck out. He began to shake and with one violent roar he broke one of the shackles holding his arm. An alarm went off as a scientist ran trying to shove a needle in his arm but Sylo quickly clocked him in the jaw with his free hand sending the small scientist flying into a machine. 'Someone stop him!' The one in charge yelled as he began to roll Jaden away in a hurry. Sylo let out another roar and broke the other shackle. He ripped the tubes from his body and unshackled his feet. More scientist ran for him but found themselves flat on their back in sever pain. Sylo leaped off driven only by his own adrenaline as he ran forward throwing another scientist into the wall. Armed guard rushed in trying to detain the beast but he would have none of it and quickly tore through them. He ran after the man who had Jaden and quickly found them going into an elevator. Sylo threw a hand in between the doors just in time and ripped it open as the man watched in fear. 'You liar!' Sylo snarled. 'Don't hurt me! Please we were paid to do this!' 'Akira Akiyama!' Sylo's voice boomed. He grabbed the man and slammed his head into the steel doors a few times making his blood run a streak to the floor. Sylo tossed him to the side and helped Jaden off the gurney and to the next floor. But once they got there the floor was covered with security. 'Are you strong enough to fight your way through?' Sylo asked looking to Jaden. 'I think so, yeah,' She nodded. And with that both rushed into the lobby area. Sylo tore through quit a few as Jaden held her own just fine. A metal door began to slide over the entrance but Sylo quickly grabbed it and using the last bit of his strength pushed it apart. He turned back to Jaden who was kicking a security guard in the head. 'And' Kick 'Don't' Kick 'Ever' Kick 'Touch' Kick 'Me' Kick 'Again!' 'Jaden we got to go I can't hold this door much longer!' Sylo's voice interrupted her kicking. She reluctantly kicked the guard once more and rushed between the steel doors. Sylo jumped through letting them slam together as alarms went off throughout the building. The two walked on the sidewalk like nothing had ever happened except for Sylo, who had no shirt due to them taking if off, which made people think he was just crazy. 'My hero,' Jaden said in a kidding manner. 'Akiyama will pay.....I'm going to split his skull open,' 'Down killer,' She smiled. 'Soon enough you will kick his punk ass,' 'Yeah....soon enough,' And with that they disappeared into a crowed of people. Sylo knew Akiyama would try to take him out many more times before the Pay-Per-View but he was ready, ready to kill. He wasn't only looking out for himself now, oh no, he was also looking out for Jaden and his child; and he would kill for all three.
