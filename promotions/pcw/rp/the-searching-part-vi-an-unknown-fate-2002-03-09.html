The Searching - Part VI - An Unknown Fate

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-09-02 NOTE: This features thoughts from Bobby. ICly speaking... let's just say that he put in voiceovers later on when he edited the recording. =) .Song listening guide: 'Blurry' by Puddle Of Mudd BOBBY STORMBRINGER 'The Searching' - Part VI - An Unknown Fate Then.... Randy Stormbringer has at least gotten justice. Randy Stormbringer has taken the family estate and most of his money back to him, where it belongs. He has a wife and a nice, easy life as a legal aide. So what left is there to do except living out the rest of days outrageously and having a grand old time with more freedom than any pitiful peasant could possibly fathom!? Well. Randy could forcibly level B.S.'s karma. Since dear ol' Bobby has had it too easy. Especially recently. Yes inDEED, the barely younger brother has chosen to make sure that he, Randy Thomas Stormbringer, is the sole cause of Bobby Stormbringer's Zero Hour. Emphasis on 'zero'! Underestimating a Stormbringer may end up being a minor moment for you. But why have any more regrets than the dozens you already have in your heart and soul..? Do not doubt that Randy will accomplish this task or you shall regret it. Randy will make him pay for what he has done to him and the people who care about him. In the very most negative of ways... Trust in me. And now.... * * * 'Every search for a hero must begin with something every hero requires. A villian.' -from Mission Impossible: 2 --3-04-02, Monday night, 10:23 pm, during the main event of March Madness!-- We join this 'pivotal moment' in Bobby Stormbringer's life, already in progress. In the melay Stormbringer got tangled up in the ropes and is struggling to free himself as Miya is going at it with El Diablo when the crowd starts to count. BS THOUGHTS: C'mon.. c'mon! Aymin could throw me out right now if he gets free! CROWD: 3... 2... 1!!!! The buzzer sounds and a worker monkey comes out with a hammer and runs down to where Aymin is nailed to the mat and starts to pry the nails free. JEFF: At least the worker monkey is helping undo Diablo’s injustice and let Aymin into this match. Aymin gets freed and charges the ring and goes right after Diablo. Miya slides out of the way and watch as the two trade blows and Stormbringer frees himself from the ropes. BS THOUGHTS: God, finally... wait a sec- that's not that smart. Two members of Asylum over here? Hello? DAVE: Wow, Aymin went for Diablo, there’s a surprise. B.S. glances over at Miyagawa and the wheels start turning. He thinks the following in two or three seconds: BS THOUGHTS: If I can get them out, Miya gets a rest. Where are we? Twelve? Thirteen?? Sh!t, that means Boda. But then Darkstar and Xavier will come help and it'll be three on one. No world title for me, but Miya should make it to the final five. Maybe Darkstar. What the hell..? Miya owes me a TV title shot for this though! Stormbringer sees the opportunity and charges at the two men with a cross body block and all three men go flipping over the top rope and land on the outside. Bobby Stormbringer, El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin have all been eliminated!!! DAVE: Stormbringer just took out three men including himself!! DUKE: And once again Miya gets a nice long break before Boda comes out. B.S. rolls away from Diablo and Aymin before sitting up. Even though he is a little dazed from the stiff fall, The Showstopper can plainly see back through the ropes and back into the ring to Miyagawa's stunned reaction. BS THOUGHTS: Heh. I bet he thinks that I'm pretty freggin' dumb right now. Bah. He'll appreciate it later. I hope. Ehhh.. got to elimate Chef, at least. That was fun. With those simple musings B.S. brings his sore-as-hell body back to a standing position and he staggers his way towards the backstage area. Not only is he worn out from his somewhat brief but intense battle for the World title but he also got creamed by not only Drake Maxwell but The End as well. For some reason or another B.S. concentrated too much on the goal and not enough on the means to get there. Mistakes like not finishing off a DDT quicker than that were things he just didn't do. The End of course was another sneak attack. B.S.'s intention all along was to get down to the ring before The End could get involved. Of course, he did anyay. Luckily for B.S., Skylar Green had some wrath for The Fallen Angel as well. THe Showstopper would of felt humiliated if he was the reason why Jeremy not only lost the Continental title, but would of never been reunited with his brother either. Thank goodness for The End? How strange... ...and just where was the rest of Asylum, anyway!? Mr. Prime Time slaps the hands of a few of his adoring fans while he contemplates the nuances of the Hell In A Cell title match. Those kids sure will have a lot to talk about at school tomorrow. KID: Bobby Stormbringer high-fived me! ME! That was so mother-f*ckin' kick *ss!! Hey. You know how young these cursing kiddies are these days? Such a shame there's a complete and total lack of parenting in this day and age. No really. I mean it. * * * --10:34 pm-- Several minutes pass. We see B.S. leaving a private restroom for the PCW crew. As B.S. slowly weaves his way back through the locker rooms for a smidge of comfort from his lovely Kira he passes by an arena monitor. After taking a glance at it, he keeps walking, right on out of the shot. One second... two- B.S. comes running back and leans over, sticking his face right into it with wild eyes.... On the screen, Amanda grabs Kid Wonder helping him to his feet. Then, the two begin to lay waste to the Krazed Lunatic, Jeremy Howard with kick after kick. KID WONDER: I told you, I'm a legend! You'd better start treating me as such! AMANDA: Why don't we take care of this once and for all!? A sinister look crosses Kid Wonder's face as he look toward the cinder block. Kid Wonder grabs it, lifting it into the air. The scene cuts back to the battle royal as Kid Wonder drops the cinder block across the head of Jeremy Howard, smashing it all to pieces as we cut back to the ring just in time for the countdown. STORMBRINGER: For Christ's sakes! B.S. stands up straight and he turns from side to side, trying to figure out where this conflict between K-Dub and his fearless leader could be happening. His face is contorted in a vision of pure disdain for Kid Wonder and especially Amanda, who was once held in high regard for her integrity by many people. Also mixed into the odd expression is concern for Krayzie's safety after that vicious assault. The shot follows from behind Stormbringer as he weaves his way through the narror hallways towards a sheer guess as to where Wonder's attack on the Krayzed Lunatic could of taken place. At first B.S. takes a right turn to another hallway.. then a left.. then one more left- But he is stopped cold in his tracks by a black steel chair right across his face! After the punishment that he took at the hands of Drake Maxwell and The End and then in his actual match and the total of about ten or fifteen minutes he just spent in there, Bobby Stormbringer understandably falls. B.S. falls backward in an awkward position with his legs trapped underneath him. Luckily he has the instinct to spring his lower legs out of the way before he reinjures his knees. B.S. also uses his survival instincts from so many years of being hurt or dazed in wrestling to rise back up to a natural sitting position. The view changes to one of ground level and slightly raised to see the stunned 'Bringer Of The Storms' from that lower perspective. From the left comes a couple of black boots that cme to a halt right next to The Showstopper. The sound of them tapping against the hard floor echo throughout the hallway. B.S. somehow raises his head to look back up at his presumed attacker. Then Bobby even manages an ego-filled smirk and a weak chuckle while he looks back down to the floor. STORMBRINGER: Sorry Jeremy... People need lung transplants.. kidney transplants.. even heart transplants. I think I need... I think I need a 'luck transplant'. And baby.... that's no- The only semi-concious Stormbringer gets his popular catch phrase cut off with a vicious chair shot right to the top of his skull!! Bobby's eyes roll up in the back of his head and the promising athlete falls over like a ton of bricks, all the way over this time. At least he doesn't have that semi-concious thing to worry about anymore. Sinister snickering is heard and the camera pans up and to its left to show.. the same man? No it can't be. The only difference is the lenth of their hair, but it is obvious who has perpetrated this unwarranted travesty. Randy Stormbringer, the man born from the same womb as Bobby some six minutes later on, now drops the now thrashed and dented chair right next to B.S. with a clang. Randy now laughs low in his throat as he grasps his twin by his dark silver trenchcoat and looks for a decent grip. This explains why Bobby Stormbringer couldn't help Jeremy Howard after Kid Wonder's unforgiveable actions against Krayzie. If Randy wants B.S. healthy enough to show up for the NTICW Pay-Per-View in their home of Orlando so badly, then why would Randy pull a stunt like this now!? Much has happened in the week after March Madness and Bobby will address the *ahem* 'turmoil' that has arisen in his professional life as of late. For now it is more than personal. This is family. But things are going to get more personal than he ever could of imagined. We show you these events of this past Monday while it is actually Saturday here in the western hemisphere. What will his thoughts be on Asylum's condition, his GTT opponent in Ben J. Pailthorpe, Drake Maxwell, The End, and the arrival of Politically Incorrect, most notably Mr. Powell?? When the time is right B.S. will be sure to clarify for not only for his fans and his peers... but also himself. The Searching is over. Let the Stalking begin. However, B.S. will be the one who is stalked. Until then we are left with the image of Randy Stormbringer dragging his brother's near lifeless body to an unknown location. An Unknown fate. Once the two twin brothers are out of our range of sight we cut right to blackness. WAIT FOR THE SECOND HALF. THEN AN EPILOGUE OR SOMETHING. THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002.
