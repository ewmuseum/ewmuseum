Bohemian Rapsody

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 26th, 2002 (The scene opens as Mike Frampton steps into a casino. He is greeted by a man in a tuxedo and handed ten free dollars worth of chips. Mike walks over to a black jack table. He puts down the ten chips.) Mike Frampton: Here you are my good man. Dealer: Sir this is a fifty dollar minimum game. Mike Frampton: That's outrageous! Dealer: Sir either put down forty more dollars or leave. Mike Frampton: Alright I will. (Mike puts down forty dollars in bills.) Mike Frampton: There! Dealer: Thank you. (The dealer deals Mike two cards. One card is a Jack. The other is face down. The dealer draws a queen. Mike lifts his card enough for him to see, it is a 10. Mike smiles.) Mike Frampton: Hit me. (The dealer gives him another card, it is an ace. Mike has reached 21.) Mike Frampton: Hit me. (The dealer puts down another card. It is a second ace.) Mike Frampton: 22. DOH! Dealer: Hold on a second. You had 21 and you asked for more cards? Mike Frampton: What are you saying? Dealer: I'm saying that was the stupidest thing anyone has ever done at this casino. Mike Frampton: I see. Dealer: I mean that was a bonehead move. Mike Frampton: Yes it was. Dealer: I mean what kind of moron does that? Mike Frampton: Yeah I get the idea. Dealer: So freaking stupid. Mike Frampton: O.K. I'm starting to get angry. Dealer: You must be Canadian. Mike Frampton: Alright that's it.(Mike grabs the dealer by the throat.) Mike Frampton: Yeah, I am Canadian. Got a problem? (The dealer slowly shakes his head.) Mike Frampton: You don't eh? Maybe you should. (Mike pulls a gun and puts it to the Dealer's head.) Mike Frampton: Mama, I killed a man, put a gun to his head, pulled the trigger now he's dead. Dealer: Dude no, please, I am sorry! Mike Frampton: After every encounter with me someone is sorry. Dealer: Please leave me be. Mike Frampton: Mama, I don't wanna die, sometimes I wish I was never born at all! (Mike pulls back the hammer.) Dealer: Oh God No! Mike Frampton: I'm just a poor boy, nobody loves me. Dealer: Please! Mike Frampton: He's just a poor boy from a poor family! Dealer: Let me go. Mike Frampton: We will not let you go. Dealer: Let me go. Mike Frampton: We will never let you go! Dealer: Please let me go! Mike Frampton: So you think you can stop me and spit in my eye? Dealer: No! Mike Frampton: Just gotta get out, just gotta get right out of here! (Mike shoves the dealer to the wall and grabs his money and rushes out of the Casino.) Mike Frampton: Nothing really matters, anyone can see, nothing really matters, nothing really matters, to meeeeee! (Fade to black.)
