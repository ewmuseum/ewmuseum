Date: Now This isn't about Anarchy. This isn't about my bitter contempt for authority or PCW. This is about my pride, the fact that this is where I have bled the same color blue for the last two 365 day cycles. So when pups start to get angry and nip at the heels of the legends, I get frustrated. Consider this a public service announcement Xavi, and you should take note of it too Tommy, because both of you despite difference in time spent here are in the same boat. This place used to have a aura surronding it, you see, literally hundreds of indy wrestlers threw their collective panties at the front office to join, some of them were really bad and never got in... and some of them were legends... and never got in. This place was that damn good. We were both hated and respected by every last person on the net who ever even uttered the words e-wrestling. What does PCW have now? Indifference. Noone cares, noone hates, noone respects. They have no reason to. Look at the roster, aside from four vets and maybe five other talented people, we are bush league. How bout we just offer the fans nudity and turn into GLOOW. That is about all we are worth. It's pathetic. Xavi, did you ever stop to think that maybe the fans hate whats happening now because it sucks, because PCW was a legendary force that fell into hardtimes and never recovered, maybe the fans are sick of it and dissapointed... see Xavi, thats why OSW is so popular, thats why that fledgling PWC fed from my hometown is growing so large, thats why these indy feds are getting closer to us... because we aren't PCW anymore.. Sure, the name is the same, but this isn't what people loved. So you say Fuck the Past, even though you weren't a part of it, you were wasting your time having biochips implanted in your body at that point, or winning a defunct leagues title while feuding with a guy who couldn't be original if he bought it at K-Mart. So, as a representative of the past, I say Fuck This. People are leaving because feds that aren't PCW can offer them what PCW once was, have you sat down and watched OSW? It's like i've stepped into a time machine, all it needs is Dan Hollywood, Putter Mayhem, and Chef and we've got 2001 all over again. PWC, that too... its what PCW started off as and its regionally as popular in the east as we are right NOW, and they play in Bingo halls while we 'sell out' arenas. This is a joke Xavi, and you know it. Tommy needs to face this now before he gets let down and burns out, he will never be as good as Jon Hull, he will never be as great as Ed, because when they were legends, they had to BEAT legends. There are no legends anymore Tommy, noone you can dethrone, noone you can defeat, there is no heat you can get. You went from a surfing Continental Champion to a surfing World Champion in three months... congrats... there will be a party for you thrown by half the roster in the unemployment line. Or in my case, the retirement home... Yes, you heard that correct. This was the straw that made me lose faith in the fed that gave me life, this is the fed that I once viewed as Lazereth, never being dead and always coming back, I've given up on it and that. This fed will never have the glory it once had, when the Legends walked the earth, when PCW itself was historic, when everything was actually credible and right, before you even read a L. Ron Hubbard book Kannon. None of you 'built' PCW, the people that 'built' it have already given up on it and have left for greener places. Except for a few of us, those of us that helped put the roof on and give it window dressing, the few of us left were the ones that painted the house that Troy, Nelson, The Phantom Booker, and Wonder built. Trust me on this Xavi, i'm not the only one that thinks this, I'm just the only one that won't be here in a few months, I'm the only one with the balls to say 'Stop fucking our past', Kannon, consider this my 'two weeks notice', i'll be out of your hair soon enough so you and Creative Control can put as much new talent here as you want, you can take seventeen surf boarding knights and place them in my spot where I've garnered no respect through you 'new' people. Have fun with that. I'll show the new people what PCW was really like, I'll give them a taste of it, whether I begin it on Fusion or wait until Turmoil to let the anticipation grow inside, i'll show you Kannon, i'll show you Tommy, i'll show all of you that weren't worthy to be the boot lick of what Primetime once meant, i'll show you what EVERY feud was like... and i'll end my career here too. So how about you people follow in MY footsteps.
