A field of shattered dreams

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/22/2002 ~He's back, back again, guess who's back, your best friend. Now some of you are probably wondering where Kano has been since Third Dawning. Jon Kano, the normally loud, semi-obnoxious, but always entertaining and speaking his mind, has been relatively silent since he wrote the last chapter in his storied feud with Corey Williams by sending Corey Williams on his merry way out of PCW. Jon Kano has been at his laptop computer for the past month writing his book, due out soon, about his life and PCW. Well, now his book is done, save for a few minor editings. So now is a time to get back to business. On last Friday's Fusion, Kano 'turned his back on the fans' and went back to his old ways. This Tuesday will be his first challenge as he teams with Blade D to take on Duke Williams and Ellie~! And on next Friday's Fusion, Kano takes on Kramer Wild Wing.~ *The scene begins with Jon Kano standing on the baseball diamond of John O'Donnell Stadium in Davenport, Iowa, also the home of Quad City River Bandits, Davenport's minor league team. Kano is dressed in a yellow Versace suit and gold framed sunglasses as he stands behind home plate.* Kano: Look at this ballpark. I can't believe this place actually houses a minor league team. Where is the vast seating? Where are the skyboxes? Where are the luxuries? You couldn't pay me enough money to come here and watch a bunch of glorrified sandlot baseball players. Hell I could hit a ball out of this park. *Kano begins to walk down the first base line.* Kano: Enough about this pathetic little ballpark, it is time to get back to business. Last Friday, I made my intentions for my future clear. Some people say I turned my back on the fans. Bullsh*t. I never turned my back on anyone but my true self. When I came here two years ago, I was an asshole. I was a man who drank fine wine, smoked the best Cuban cigars, and wore eight thousand dollar suits. I raised hell then. I kidnapped PhD, giving my good friend, TPB, ownership of PCW. I raised some hell with TPB, then with Nelson. All of a sudden, the fans got to me. I wanted to be cheered. I wanted to be loved by millions. I sacrificed my own well being. I began to run my decisions with what the fans wanted. It got me in situations where I got my ass kicked. Two cracked vertabra in my neck from listening to the fans and taking on Ivan Stanislav. Torn cartilage in my left knee from listening to the fans and taking on the whole damn Nelson regime. And lets not forget me almost getting killed by Corey Williams a couple months back by listening to the fans. It's not worth it. I could have ended up dead or a cripple and then what? My wife, my beautiful, loving, amazing wife would have to take care of my pathetic wheelchair bound ass for the rest of my life? My soon to be born child having to watch his father struggle every morning to get out of bed? F*ck that. I got to live my life here in PCW as myself. If the fans don't like it, tough sh*t. Now to Turmoil..... *Kano makes the turn and begins to walk towards second base.* Kano: Turmoil, the show that I made famous. A man who I really do like, Blade D, is my tag team partner. I like this young buck. He has skills, he has talent, and he has an attitude that I like. Then we have Ellie~! God almighty, when I hit her last week, I almost orgasmed. It was great. I didn't even mean to, but Jesus in heaven, I would do it again and again for the rest of my days and die a happy man. Then her tag partner, one of my former best friends, Duke Williams. I can't wait to hear what he has to say about me. What is he going to say? Probably some assinine comments about how I wasn't ever that good or how he never really liked me. Funny, a few months ago, he had no problem having me save his ass about twice a week. And he sure as hell didn't have a problem with me drinking him underneath the table on a nightly basis. I don't know what is going to happen when he and I lock up on Turmoil. Maybe he and I will begin to think of the good times, maybe we will walk to the center of the ring and hug. Yeah right. If I get ahold of his hillbilly ass on Tuesday, I am gonna knock the whiskey out of him. Hell, if I get ahold of Ellie~! I am gonna knock the slut out of her. Bottom line is if Duke disses me, he disses himself. *Kano makes the turn and begins to walk toward third base.* Kano: Then Fusion, the show that I put on the map. I get to take on the KFC spokesmoron Kramer Wild Wing. This guy must be an exception to survival of the fittest. I am amazed that he has made it this far in life. You can only eat so much grease and deep fried sh*t in your life before your arteries turn to granite from the cholestrol clogging them like a wet rat. I hope he keeps on eating the KFC, just will make it easier for me to spear his ass into next week. What the hell am I, a world class wrestler who has wrestled the greats of this industry and defeated most of them, doing wrestling a spokestool for a fast food restaurant founded by a failure of a businessman who was so senile at his old age that he believed he was General Robert E. Lee? Kramer Wild Wing can do all he wants on Friday, he can eat his fattening chicken, he can clog his arteries, because I am going to deliver a thunderous Kanoplex onto him like he has never felt before. And that is what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano finishes his trek around the bases by stepping on homeplate.* ~Well shit, the bastard is back.~
