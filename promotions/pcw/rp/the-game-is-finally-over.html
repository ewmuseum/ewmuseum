THE GAME IS FINALLY OVER

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: THE DAY THE GAME DIED {The scene opens up with Kevin Klaymore and Mandy at a local store in Kevin’s hometown of Detroit Michigan. Although Kevin is still within International Incident he still wishes to return home for a reason. Maybe it is the snowy weather, maybe it’s the wait five minutes if he doesn’t like it, or maybe he’s just a little homesick.} {Kevin walks out of the little store that is covered in graffiti. Looking through the blue sky with white fluffy clouds, it just takes Kevin to a place of love and care. While walking across the street where cars are zooming past him left and right, Kevin stops at the other end and wipes his clothes. The brownish dust catches the eye easily on his bright blue shirt with a red dragon in white trim on his back. The dust on his tan cargo khakis doesn’t really show but his white Adidas cross trainers easily catch it with little fight.} Kevin: Ahh finally away from the bright lights, and greedy bastards. Just me, Mandy, the green grass, and the blue sky. Finally we’re home. {Soon Mandy runs to the street corner and waits for the “walk” signal to flash so she can run and meet her fiancée. Her tan skirt that is down to just above her knee is held up with a black belt with diamonds on it. And her tight black shirt has “NAUGHTY” written on it in diamonds also. Her silky dirty blonde hair is down naturally as she hurringly runs to the other side. As she stares deep into Kevin’s hazel eyes the glimmer off her baby blue eyes and lips puts Kevin in some sort of a trance.} {Mandy holds onto Kevin and begins to speak softly, in a tender loving voice.} Mandy: Are you sure you want to come back here? If the guys found out you were coming back here they would have your head. {Kevin jerks back and gets all defensive.} Kevin: I’m not scared of them! They may be good wrestlers, some may be excellent, some even legendary, but they ain’t “The Bomb”! {Mandy brings her hand slowly up to Kevin’s biceps and rubs them. Hoping to calm him down, and that he won’t get worked up.} Mandy: Look I know how you feel. But there’s something I think you should know. {Kevin looks on at his love staring into her eyes lingering on every word she has to say.} Kevin: What’s that darling? {Mandy begins to get a little nervous and she can feel the butterflies fluttering all through her insides.} Mandy: No I can’t!… Kevin: What’s wrong? You know I’m here, you know I won’t let it take control of me. {Mandy looks down toward the ground and nervously replies.} Mandy: Well, umm… how can I say this… Kevin: Look Mandy you know me and you know what I’ll do… Mandy: That’s the thing, I know you and I know what you’ll do. I know you’ll get all upset, start yelling out profanity in Spanish and go off and not talkin’ to me in like forever. Kevin: Hun is it really that bad? You know everything will haunt you and tear you down piece by piece until it is off your chest, well breast in your case. {Kevin and Mandy begin to lightly chuckle at the joke, then Mandy just goes out with it.} Mandy: Half the guys in the back are badmouthin you and they want you fired. Kevin: ¿QUÉ? Los bandejos no me dijieron a mí cara. {Mandy looks on shaking her head, as she knew what would happen. As she begins to cry she interrupts him.} Mandy: See Kevin I knew what you would do, I knew you would do this. I know that from now on you’ll bust your ass off to prove every single one of them wrong. You won’t be home, there with me where you should. Instead you’ll be at the gym and at the ring, training and feeding that fire inside of you. Well I love you Kevin. I really do, and if you choose them over me then that’s what you’ll do. Kevin: How long have you known about this? I’m not mad I’m just frustrated right now. Why won’t they come up and tell me straight to my face? I mean if I have a problem with them I’ll tell them straight to there face that I don’t like you. I love you Mandy, and I love wrestling too. This hasn’t been a good time for me. Everyone and everything tempting me and pushing me back. Well this is it I’ve had it. Screw wrestling all together. I QUIT! Mandy: No Kevin! Don’t you ever say that! You have a gift, don’t ruin it because of ignorant people. Kevin: I’ve always wanted to get that house with the white picket fence, having a bar-b-q with the kids playing ball in the front yard. Well this is the time. This is the time where we get married, start a family, and do the normal thing. If you weren’t my valet I’d only see you a few days a year. Just like last time. Remember that? Mandy: I remember it well Kevin, I honostly do. But I wouldn’t trade it cause you were happy. Kevin: Well I’m not happy now. This is it. GOODBYE PCW, GOODBYE. {Scene pans in on Kevin as he chose his route. He wants the family life for now. Wrestling is out of the picture completely. Kevin wants out, and he made his choice final.}
