Nerezzident Evil (Part 4)

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th May 2003 Racing over to the doorway the Jester peered down at what looked to be a concrete staircase that winds in a circular fashion all the way down past the first floor and into the heart of the mansion. Dimly lit and looking somewhat a scene from a horror flick Gaz Green took his chances, unaware of the plastic sign post fixed to the left-hand wall with the message, “Laboratory…” Stepping atop of the cobbled staircase the Jester peered one final time at the fallen behemoth as he fended off swarms of ravens as they swooped down from the rafters, pecking at his pale and flaky face the Vampire waved his arms frantically in a flimsy defence. Stepping hotfooted from the crime scene the green tailored Jester continued to spiral from the second floor and down into the heart of the mansion and then as his foot stepped onto a level surface a long dark corridor stemmed ahead of him. “Why couldn’t this place be sign posted, is a four letter word like exit to much to ask?” sighed the Emerald Menace as he closed his eyes and waved his head left to right, his actions a clear sign that emotionally the Jester is still reeling from this whole ordeal. The corridor was an arc like chamber with cobbled walls littering both sides, most ridden with a green like fur that could be moss and the coated silence disturbed by the shallow sound of trickling water. Suddenly as the light seeped in its presence allowed shadows to creep along the walls and notably was a large figure making short sharp movements before blasting its tongue from it’s wide-open mouth. Standing petrified the Jester’s legs clamped and rooted to the spot in paralysis, gulping hard his only bodily function was that of his vocal cords, “Come any closer I’m going to kick you… Well if I still had use of my legs!” Suddenly the shadow crept forward with a sudden movement and the prankster could only retort with the closure of his eyes and wayward cringes of his angelic face as his mind developed nightmarish thoughts. Holding the pose the croak of a frog murmured literally at his feet and the Jester reluctantly opened his right eye ajar to investigate. Breathing a sigh of relief and tilting his head towards the heavens with a mime of his lips, “Thank You!” the Jester continued into the belly of the beast. Through the dimness the Jester ventured for what may seem like an eternity as his hands waved over the walls for coordination and his feet shuffled in small strides when suddenly the texture of the floor changed dramatically. Gazing down momentarily with a curious expression the floor appeared to glisten with fast sharp flickers of light and crashing a heavy boot to the ground the dense sound of stone failed to materialise, in it’s place, the clatter and clang of a metallic surface. “Huh?” he mourned. Squinting his eyes he focused ahead and the end of the chamber appeared to get brighter, most likely explained by an exit. Picking up the pace the subtle sound of footsteps could be heard faintly in the distance and coming from directly behind him, growing in decibels as the assailant traipsed forward. From shuffling small footsteps to larger strides the Jester now turned his movements in a full-blown run as he raced towards the illuminating light at the far end of the passageway. Clatter and clang went the ground as the pace quickened and finally the light radiantly lit up the corridor as it stretched along the walls allowing the Jester to see the flicker of a silhouette in pursuit. Standing before two glass-panelled doors a motor erupted and the doors parted horizontally allowing the Lord of Laughter to enter into a cleansed white washed room with unusual devices cluttering each of the work surfaces. Waving his hand over some of the contraptions all labelled with an Umbrella Corp insignias the Jester muttered, “If I didn’t know no better I would have some wild imaginative thought that Umbrella Corp are developing biological weapons or something!” A slight smile formed across his trembling lips at the thought of such an absurd concept before investigating further. Peering into a large metallic white container through a small window the Original Prankster cringed his face to that of puzzlement for aligning the top shelf was an assortment of flasks with coloured fluids contained within. “That’s it… Sight seeing is over it’s time to make like a tree and leave!” our host shuddered. Walking over to another door he stood waiting for entry but to no avail. Tilting his head slightly off centre the Jester noticed a plaque used for fingerprint identification and playing an open palm over its surface a yellow light roamed over both horizontally and vertically before a monotone voice erupted, “You do now have clearance for Level 2, access denied!” “I’m not in the mood for games…BITCH!!!!” hissed the former Front Office Entertainment Manager as an evil glint tainted his eye. Snatching a fire extinguisher off the wall the Jester heaved it backwards for momentum and as the canister came within inches of its target the comic stopped abruptly as a second thought clouded his initial judgement. Rummaging in the breast pocket of his velvet business jacket he removed his infamous hand buzzer that glistens from the illuminated ceiling and placed it onto his index finger. With a sadistic smile the Jester pressed the device against the fingerprint identification pad and instantly fireworks erupted as yellow sparks danced about before the shallow sound of a motor filtered and the door rose with a shutter effect. As the Jester entered into yet another corridor the sound of the glass-panel doors opening blasted and even before he could retort the distinctive voice of the Living Nightmare sounded, “Die Jester die… Must… Must kill…” With those fateful words the Jester raced from his attacker who charged in for the kill. The Prince of Pranks leapt forward with a baseball slide and in doing so passed under the shutter door at the end of the corridor to narrowly evade the bloodthirsty Vampire in this game of cat and mouse. Scrambling back to his feet the trickster was in a HUGE biological warehouse with instruments built up in the centre and various controls littering the outer-rim. Wandering aimlessly the sound of conversation filtered before a bloodcurdling scream echoed and then silence. “Hello, anyone in here?” whispered the Tag Champion. Creeping about with his head waving frantically the sound of gunfire blared, literally startling the Jester and in paranoia he shuffled backwards. The entry way he had just past echoed with one thud after another as the beast tries to prise apart the blockade then suddenly the Emerald Menace hits something solid. Turning around he has stumbled into another S.T.A.R.S operative fully kitted in camouflage and brandishing a small black pistol. “ARGH!!!!” Recomposing from the initial fright Gaz Green spoke again, “Phew… Not to put any pressure on you but your buddies have been dropping like flies so if you can show me the way to the exit then both of us may stay alive in this survival horror!” “We have a situation sir, I am Chris Red-…” began the soldier before his voice faded out and not surprising because from behind something organic has impaled him, leaving his carcase limp with blood spewing from his mouth. Reluctantly peering up a large green monster loomed and striking his claw like instrument backwards the S.T.A.R.S member collapsed to a heap on the ground and so the chase began. With a skip in his step the Prince of Pranks scurried away as the tyrant pursued waving his claw like hand as though it were a meat cleaver and round and round the duo went. Looking a little top heavy the Jester tumbled to the ground as the monster moved in to capitalise when suddenly a frightful growl bellowed when low and behold the Vampire had made his appearance. “Maybe I’ll just leave you two to it, huh?” muttered the comic edging away from the battleground as the colossuses angrily gazed over each other. Suddenly as the Jester made his getaway the floor shock and the walls trembled as the giants collided but only feet away the exit loomed and come hell or high water there would be nothing to stop Gaz Green. Entering what appeared to be an elevator the Jester continued to cringe at the bloodcurdling cries of the combatants as they duelled to the death but not discouraged he pressed a button on the control panel and the cables creaked into life.
