The Calling (Brandi/ Walden)

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 December With the tangible smell of polished oak furnishings, the cluttered twin desks that have been overloaded with writing utensils and disregarded pieces of paper it is clear that this opening scene is well within the confines of the Front Office's office. A fan rotated left to right on a medium setting, barely managing to edge the work load off the desk and the dim background appeared desolate with no activity when suddenly the fax machine did its impersonation of 'Star Wars' R2 D2 droid. A few random beeps, the flicker of L.E.D's accompanied the emerging of a piece of paper that slowly dropped into the loading tray, sitting neatly on a pile of other papers all of which addressed to the PCW stable. A murmur soon filtered the silence as two individuals conversed outside the door, their conversation muffled by the wooden structure, their identities unknown, only to be unveiled in due course. - THUD - The door crashed open, unveiling the two puppets of CEO Xavier Kannon in Brandi Wilson and Chief Reclamation Officer Tim Walden who has his Cruiserweight Championship on display as it sat limping over his shoulder. 'What a way to go into Decimation-....' sighed the blonde headed Tim Walden, stroking his title, his fingers covering every ridge of the PCW pristine including his name plaque. 'How do you mean Mr. Walden?' replied his stable mate Brandi. The duo sat, slouched into leather reclined chairs opposite one another, sitting relaxed. Brandi peered over various documents that sat ominously over her desk whilst Tim Walden merely gazed into the glistening plaque of his Cruiserweight Championship, almost to the point of showing affection. The minutes past and still no answer, again more minutes and again no answer prompting Brandi to intervene and ask her colleague what he meant by his previous statement. 'TIM!!! What do you mean when you say, what a way to go into Decimation?!?' The incoherent Front Office member snapped out of his affectionate trance and roamed his eyes over to Brandi Wilson who sat with a curious facial expression, finally opening his mouth and filling in the blanks. 'Well Ms. Wilson, CEO Xavier Kannon was not himself so to speak and then if you thought he was standing a little to close the edge well Pranks With Attitude literally threw him over-....' 'Arr-... But they have been taken care of Mr. Walden. We have yet to make an official announcement but it was agreed earlier that Gareth Green and Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, collectively known as Pranks With Attitude will be stripped of the PCW Tag Team Championship-...' said the PCW veteran Brandi Wilson with a gleaming smile, her sentence however interrupted upon by fellow Front Office comrade Tim Walden. 'How very true - But Xavier Kannon has out-done himself by forcing P.W.A to face one-another at Decimation, adding that demented vermin Skitzo as special guest referee was pure genius!' 'It begs the question, who in the Kannon family is really THE EVIL SUPER GENIUS!!!' injected the former Television Champion Brandi Wilson. The duo struck up a mass of hysterics, cackling like a hoard of hyenas as they laughed at Brandi's comments when regarding the Hubbardville couple of Xavier Kannon and his beloved Eleanor Kannon-Hall. With the rotating fan becoming an increasing annoyance, Tim Walden reached across, almost falling from his chair in what would have been a 'Candid Camera' moment. Retaining some sort of balance, his hands grasping the edge of his desk and the fax machine he returned to his seat panting and wheezing. 'THESE CHAIRS HAVE BEEN TAMPERED WITH!!! THAT BLOODY TRICKSTER TEAM-.... I'M GOING-... THATS IT I AM GOING TO ENFORCE A PRANKING TAX!!!' hissed Tim Walden, his face a bright red of embarrassment and putting on an unnecessary rage, aimed towards the victims of the Tag Team Championship scandal Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Nerezza. 'Oh, don't be silly-....' murmured Brandi trying to retain her laughter. During the course of Tim Walden’s display of desk climbing and acrobatics, the papers aligned within the loading tray of the fax machine had fluttered into the air and most of which now align the tiles of the cold floor. Gripping hold of the support bars of his chair, Tim Walden bent over, retrieving both his prized asset of a Championship title and a handful of printed papers. Repositioning the PCW title over his slender shoulder, Tim padded down the papers into a neat bundle with the all-important document right at the very top. 'So what is on today’s agenda Mr. Walden?' asked Brandi. 'Should we dive into some paper work Ms. Wilson?' retorted Byron-... Tim Walden with a smug grin, his eyes fixated over that of Brandi Wilson. Gazing at one another begged the question, 'Are we witnessing PCW's next couple?' a couple that may rival the love of Xavier Kannon and Ellie, possibly not as second place had already been secured by the smitten duo of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. With all this love in the air we could see something for February’s Pay-Per-View, maybe a PCW Saint Valentines Day Massacre featuring the connections of Kannon/ Ellie, Wilson/ Walden, Storm/ Windsor and quite possibly Green/ Miyagawa. 'HOPEFULLY NOT' Through these dense minutes of silence, the duo finally spoke in unison. 'Lets-....' With Tim grasping the top document from the sturdy pile at the corner of his writing space, the Cruiserweight Champions eyes grew large and glassy as they swayed from one sentence to another in a pendulum motion. Looking rather aggravated and uneasy, Brandi intervened and asked her co-worker about the substance of such a letter. 'What’s wrong Tim?' With a huge gulp, a look of fright and a pale complexion surfacing Tim Walden read the document aloud, a few wayward words due to stutter but the body of the letter was read to perfection. 'Dear both Brandi and Walden-....' 'Well, continue-....' roared Brandi, ushering Tim Walden to finish what had been started seconds earlier. 'Pranks With Attitude would like to welcome you and a few carefully chosen people to Shady Acres Mansion where you will under go the task at taking away what is rightfully ours in the PCW Tag Team Championship. We have proven to friend and foe that there is no way we can be beaten and we live up to the accolade of 'Dominance Personified' and this experiment will prove who will be the next rightful heir to this throne. No sinister master plan, no ulterior motives, a chance for a select few to rise above all others and take away our prize procession. The address and directions have been added on the back of this document, so we shall await your arrival with keen interest, Thank You Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A)' The Front Office duo look at one another, mouths ajar, trying to fathom the body of the letter, wondering whether they should involve the man labeled 'Evil Incarnate' CEO Xavier Kannon, especially in his state of mind. After a few minutes of silence, Tim Walden stepped up to the plate and informed his colleague of a course of action they will have to endeavor in bringing back the titles, gold that has been held by the best in the business, Blood Brothers, Goovil, Playaz Inc, Da Westsidaz-.... 'We take this to our grave, there's no way I'm going to inform and upset Xavier Kannon further, this is to fragile and would seriously play on his mind when he should be concentrating solely on Duke Williams!' 'AGREED!!! So what do you propose we do Mr. Walden!?!' asked the inquisitive Ms. Wilson with a look of concern knowing that if this came to light then it would be their heads on the chopping blocks if not then certainly their jobs. 'We shall play this childish game and combine our efforts to bring home the gold!' concluded the Chief Reclamation Officer Tim Walden. Both parties stood erect and raced over to the coat holder, retrieving their outdoor attire they flooded through the wooden door in a heap and out of sight as they turned left down a narrow corridor, bombing about like a pair of F1 cars. With the office returning to its once desolate state of solitude, the image drifted into that of a snowy white picture of no sound before ending this segment completely. - To be continued -
