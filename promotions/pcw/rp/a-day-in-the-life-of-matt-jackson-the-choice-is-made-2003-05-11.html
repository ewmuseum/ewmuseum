A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, The Choice Is Made

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/03 Matt Jackson has never questioned his own drive…his desire. After the events of the past few weeks he is starting to bring them into question though. Everyone is trying to keep him away from the one thing that he holds dearer then anything else…Haley. For the most part they are succeeding. Mark Makonie is succeeding. Being Jason Hilton’s henchmen seems to have it’s perks, getting to force himself onto Hilton’s beautiful daughter…beating down a man he knew nothing about until some money was thrown into his face. Matt could deal with it all though because he knew his princess was going to be back…his will wouldn’t stagger until the matter had been dealt with. It all changed on Tuesday, when his angel was tied to that bed…knowing the nazi was going to get to have his way with her and he was powerless to stop it…to protect her. Nothing was going to be the same between them now, he won’t allow it, not because he doesn’t love her, but because it’s the way he is trained. He would have been all right, just doing what Xavier Kannon brought him to PCW for, continuing his investigation until the downfall of the PCW, but now he can’t…there is something deeper. He can’t destroy part of something he owns, something that he has grown to love. Matt loves the PCW…all of it except for the people inside of it. The people like Stone Burns, Violence Jack, and more so Society. The men who are causing him the pain…the one man, Mark Makonie, the man that he hates and wants to destroy. I don’t know if she really is worth all this hassle…over the past few months, I have grown to love her, but all this bullshit just seems worthless. Jason Hilton was probably right in saying I don’t belong because I don’t…I am an outsider into their world and his only defense is to try to remove me, taking me away from his daughter…the one person I care about and the one person he knows nothing about. Matt quietly sits in the tiny plywood chair, sighing deeply as his brown orbs slowly scan the tiny room. He is glad that the ropes are no longer encompassing his body and that for the first time in weeks, he is able to just sit without anything holding him up or bringing more pain to his tormented body. The room almost feels like an entirely new one, except for the constant reminder of stale vomit and blood that swells around the room cutting his relaxation away…plaguing his thoughts. She was kissing Makonie and it didn’t even seem like she was trying to stop him. Does Haley even want me anymore? Is there any reason for me to keep trying to fight for her? I should just let her go and continue to my mission…to destroy Mark Makonie. It’s not about destroying the PCW for me anymore; I could careless what happens to it…just as long as I get Makonie. Jackson stands up and begins to pace the dirt-floored room, kicking up a bit of dust as his naked body glides back and forth. The room isn’t very big and doesn’t allow him a lot of area to move around in, but it’s better then the conditions he was last in. Pouring down his tanned body is a mixture of sweat and blood, which sends pain jolting through him. The operative smiles and keeps pacing…trying not to let the look of distain cross his angular face. Letting his face scrunch up, he swings his fist at one of the stonewall and strikes it…letting the pain rush through his body and the blood trickle to the ground, cocking back his fist…he releases it again, picturing Makonie, Violence Jack, and Society. Venting the anger that is building up inside of him…letting him get back to the killer he wants to be. In front of him, the white stone is now smeared in blood and more than one of his knuckles is probably shattered. “Damnit!” he screams, his voice cracking and his fist slugging forward again. Taking a step back, the agent collapses on the floor and rests his face in his hands…the tears he has been holding back on for weeks, slowly begin to fall down his bruised cheeks. I don’t know what to think anymore…who or what to believe anymore. My world is tumbling down around me and I am powerless to stop it. Every time it even looks like something is going to go right…something else comes up to make sure it doesn’t…Makonie. Lying in a heap, Matt lets the dirt stick to his sweaty 5’8” frame, not stopping the tears stop. Not caring that the dirt is infesting his open wounds. The pain is stricken from his mind as the operative lets the stream roll, holding nothing back. It’s the first time in weeks that he even considered letting his emotions show, but now that they are there isn’t any stopping them. I don’t care if I am breaking the main rule…not showing your emotions. Nothing matters anymore. I have nothing left; Makonie has taken it all from me. Being taken away from his thoughts, Matt quickly jumps to his feet hearing the door off to the left squeak open. Pushing his bloody brown hair back, Jackson brushes a few of the tears away and focuses in on the steel divider…the divider that’s blocking him from the outside world. He hates Jason Hilton for sticking him in this room and turning his princess against him. A faint bit of light shines in through the partly crack door…forcing the agent to swing his forearm over his eyes. Listening, he hears the door softly go back to its resting place and he removes his arm, letting his eyes refocus in the darkness. Standing before him is none other then the captor he has dubbed Nameless. The shorter man, who is dressed in a black suit, cracks a smile at Jackson…showing him his chipped yellow teeth and allowing his rancid breath to cut through the air. “I just thought I would tell you…before we were leaving the arena last night, I was told that you would have a match on this upcoming Fusion. It’s a four corners something or other against Shane Wethers, Stone Burns, and Dan Osbourne,” Nameless begins, tossing his greasy black hair out of the way of his pudgy round face. Taken back by his sudden niceness Matt stares at the man. “Interesting, but on to more pressing matters. Why are you being so kind to me all of a sudden?” His smile getting bigger, Nameless offers: “I saw the way you were acting all through Fusion. I believe that you are starting to change your mind…figuring out that the little slut isn’t worth all this trouble that she is putting you through.” Nodding Matt stands still for a moment. “You guys really don’t miss anything. The one thing I never thought possible…the CIA’s master operative would be broken, but at Fusion. I saw something that did the job countless people failed at.” “I don’t know what made you take this new frame of mind, but I must say I am pleased Agent Jackson,” Nameless says, as he scans over the operative…noticing the massive amount of damage Jackson has received over the past few weeks. The countless beatings he was dealt in the Raising Star matches, the punishment he took from Makonie and the two transporters and the damage Nameless himself dished out to the young agent. Holding his broken right hand Matt starts. “I guess it really doesn’t matter what made me change my mind. This way everyone seems to be getting what they want except me, but now I really don’t know what I want anymore. Everything is just a blur and it continues to get hazer by the day. Everything needs to just end and this is the only way to do it.” I can’t believe that I am telling him everything…giving him valuable information to use against me. Perhaps I have lost it…or perhaps I am just looking for someone to listen, to help me solve my problems. Even if this is the wrong place to look. “It will be best for all parties involved Agent Jackson. You get to go back to your cozy desk at the Central Intelligence Agency and you get to continue your current domination of the PCW. It really is a win, win situation if you look at it. I mean look at all the positives you are getting for close to nothing.” He is probably right, everything was going fine until Haley came around…tossing my life upside down. With her out of the picture I can do what I was originally sent to do. Gather the information and keep all ties severed. “I believe you are right and now that we have come to this decision is it possible that I can leave?” Matt asks, a hint of hopefulness in his voice. Rubbing his chin Nameless contemplates Matt’s question for a moment. “I would love to let you just walk out of here Agent Jackson, but we both know it can’t be that simple. A few things have to be done before we reach that step.” What? I agreed to their demands and now they are still going to hold me here. I can’t believe this, but I should have known there was no way out of this situation. There wasn’t going to be a way for me to make it out alive. “I will just have to wait for Mr. Hilton to give me the nod and then you are free to go…it shouldn’t take that long. I would assume you might have to meet with him to prove you are positive about this, but I foresee no major problems,” Nameless hisses, with a huge smile. “The sooner the better in this case because now I have a lot on my plate. Besides just the Main Event on Turmoil, I have to deal with three other people who are hoping to get their hands on me and I would be in a lot better condition if I didn’t have to be in this tiny room, not getting any food or rest.” “I understand completely Matt…you have put a lot out in your PCW matches all ready, it’s written all over your body. I don’t see why this week would be any different, except for the fact you are facing two of the biggest bastards I have ever seen,” Nameless says, trying to reassure the operative. “Yeah I think they are going to present quite a challenge. As for Fusion I don’t even consider that a match. I mean Stone Burns is a guy who I have beaten twice before…he doesn’t present a threat. The other two are nothing more then filler. No one wants to see Matt Jackson and Stone Burns again, so they just added something to spice up the match…the outcome is going to be no different,” Matt confidently replies, laughing a bit after this last statement. “That’s good, let me go call Mr. Hilton and sort this whole mess out. I am sure we can have everything handled by Turmoil and that way you can focus on the Main Event totally,” Nameless bellows out, turning on his heels and making his way towards the door. Matt softens his stare on him, as he exits the room leaving Matt alone once again. Haley I am sorry, but life itself seems to fade away…getting lost within myself, there’s no one else. I have lost the will to live. There is nothing more for me…I needed you to set me free.
