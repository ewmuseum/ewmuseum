Going for the Cruisernental Gold

3 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Going for the Cruisernental Gold Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 3, 2002 'THAT DAMN FRENCH SON OF A BITCH! I am SO taxing his retirement package!'Sounds of anger management through venting, contained by the walls of the elevator... Well, I wanted distraction...And nothing does that better than getting screwed out of something you earned. The Continental Title, by all rights, could have been mine within the course of a week, but the damn Chef had to go compounding my aggravation with his little 'title vacation' instead of having the guts to fight me...He didn't even have the sense left in him to give me my singles shot, just threw me into the mix of a bunch of guys who didn't earn their ways.. ...Worst of all, left me to deal with the sadistic shape on his own terms. A Cooking Utensils Hardcore match?!? That was something the Trapezoid would surely revel in..I wasn't used to having to go to quite such extreme measures as the usage of various tools of the trade, can openers, spoons.... ...The dreaded melon baller. I shuddered at the mere thought of what he could do to me...I wasn't used to being hardcore, I hadn't even expected to be in the match. I almost thought to get angry at Xavier, but for the fact that I wasn't meant to have gotten this shot anyway. This was Duke's shot, his chance to win, but the selfish hack had refused a shot at a title that 90 percent of PCW would be pleased as punch about..I had to see the positive side, I could be only the second Conti--no, Cruisernental champion, since Frisco did it at Supremacy 2001.. Of course, that was only if I managed to win both of my matches and keep the Cruiserweight gold around my waist, even though I just KNEW I'd get screwed by the Trapezoid, I always did...He was just going to tarnish another belt's image this week with a cheap loss on my part, I couldn't let that happen. If he was to attack me tonight, I would hold my anger for him in...In until the Continental Title match, where I could finally unleash levels of rage I might be incapable of otherwise. He didn't deserve anything better, but I didn't know how much worse it would get from my side... I wasn't in the mood to let go of the title just yet, I'd only just begun to fight for it by beating him at Alliances. The belt signified all I had accomplished since my return, I had won several matches to earn it, I had beaten a psychopath in a ladder match, I had come out victorious when faced with PURE's illicit antics in the ten-man tag, I had conquered almost all who had dared challenge me....But there was always someone new to give me a battle. Vilas Lavagna..What I knew of him was very little, just some Thai guy who'd shown up and beaten El Romantico. Perhaps he might have the sort of skills that Charlie Loc had learned in Vietnam, or perhaps he was just here under the minority-hiring policies..Whatever he was, he was trying to be a threat to my championship, and that had him on my bad side right there, hence the chair attacks that came his way post-match. If I was going to lose this title, I wanted to put him through hell while I did it, he needed to be fully aware that he would only be champion because his opponent had had the wrong enemies.. It was a rather selfish thought, but I had no qualms about making the man who would be champion feel the pain of inferiority. I had already played enough games with the Trapezoid to know full well what the deal was with his attacks, he wanted to be the 'hero', the man loved by the multitudes as a fighter for justice and liberty..Were only he sane enough to recognize how his methods disobeyed his own preachings, I might be rid of him out of pure shame, but no, I still had him to deal with.... ..Or did I? There were ways to seal the deal, I should've realized this, I'm the damn Financial Reclamation Executive after all... I pulled the miniscule cellular phone out once more, dialed in the number and waited...waited for the chance to prevent what would likely be the end of my brief-so-far championship reign....'I'm sorry, but the number you have dialed does not have a destination.' What? I couldn't have tapped it in incorrectly...I ended my call immediately, then tried once more..Same response....I held on a little longer, maybe there was more to it that I missed the first time... 'PCW is in the midst of restructuring their voicemail system, and your call's destination is temporarily unavailable.' No time now that I could spend to contact security about him...Maybe I would just have to deal with the after-effects of another failed championship reign, just like the sham with the TV Title, never getting a real win to solidify the belt after the initial momentum-launcher...Maybe there'd be no effect at all? After all, this wasn't getting a victory over the Krayzie One with the assistance of a Communist, an Amish and whoever else was in the mood to run in...I'd beaten the Trapezoid myself in spite of everything he put me through, I was stronger for it...But something still weighed on me...The belt was mine for the time being, but he could've changed the outcome.. He'd made the mistakes...The attempt at Calculus...The Times Tables to give me the win...But so had I. I could've won when I had the chance instead of going after him again, I could've been Cruiserweight Champ anyway...But I was now still, but not without luck, or at least opposition stupidity. This was not about stupidity this time...This was about how I could fight Vilas Lavagna. This was one-on-one, barring HIS involvement...If he came in, all bets were off. But I knew I could beat him..I was reasonably quick and well-versed in my wrestling skill, so his evasiveness would not be as effective as it would be around a larger, less toned worker..I wouldn't let anyone evade my grasp when it came down to it. There was more than one way to tax an opponent, and Vilas had paid his fiscally, but when it got to this point, it was suffering either way for him...I wouldn't let him get away with the belt. ..The Thai would try, but nigh, he could not beat me tonight.. ...For there was only so much of mine that his tax money could buy. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
