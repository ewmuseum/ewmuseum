The Calling - Conclusion part 2

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Christmas Day Prologue: Those following the series are well aware that Gaz 'The Jester' Green has put up the PCW Tag Team Championships as collateral. Offering a band of worthy members of the active roster the opportunity to be crowned the number one contenders position, the Jester's efforts were thwarted by the ruling of the ruthless Front Office, represented by its two fledgling members Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson. It has came to light that Pranks With Attitude are to be stripped of the PCW pristine with immediate effect so any dealings there after also would be scampered but desperate times, call for desperate measures and in this case desperate hits with a capital 'D'. As an act of revenge Gaz Green has imprisoned the seven-man party within the backdrop of a Victorian mansion, their only means of escape beside suicide is that of an electronic key guarded by the Emerald Menace himself. We last saw the escapades of this team as they agreed to disband on into their two men respected units and it is during this time we catch-up on Playaz Inc, The Front Office, The Black Dragons and 'The Mental One' himself Skitzo. This next instalment opens as the Front Office collective of Cruiserweight Champion Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson continue to hunt for the elusive Jester on the second floor of this colossal house. Kicking open various rooms with authority the duo clear the upper echons of the dwelling, however to no avail, when narrowing down the possibilities they stumble across one in particular room that has a murmur from within. 'JACK POT MS. WILSON!!!' cried the PCW veteran. Ushering closer with a touch of stealth, the Cruiserweight Champion stretched out his left hand grasping the doorknob in the process. Violently jerking as though he were having an electric shock, teeth chattering, Brandi watched on in horror, as Tim Walden lay foul to yet another devious prank before screaming for assistance. 'SOMEONE HELP! SOMEBODY, ANYBODY HHHHHEEEEELLLLLPPPPP~!' With a smirk Tim Walden relinquished his hold of the doorknob chuckling as he done so, obviously he had suckered in his Front Office colleague with his Oscar winning performance. Breathing a sigh of relief Brandi reversed her facial expression of sympathy to that of a snarl as she watched idly by as Tim Walden crept into the room. Shuffling forward the Financial Reclamation Executive stood motionless with a dazed expression; looking somewhat confused he peered at the ground and more importantly to the mat he was standing on. Gulping hard the Front Office representative found himself swallowed by the ground as the carpeted mat fell into the pit with him, his screams ignored by the sulking Brandi who stands with her back facing the events unaware that her partner has succumb to the prank of Gaz Green. Pivoting around, Brandi finds Tim Walden gone, vanished into thin air. Roaming her eyes as she inspected the room from the safety of the second floor landing, simply peering in from distance she glares down at the floor where the dark pit stares right back at her. Kneeling down at the edge of the pit, Brandi murmured into the darkness, her voice traveling off the walls and into the unknown. 'TTTTTTTTTTTTIIIIIIIIIIIIMMMMMMMMMMMM!!!!!!!!!!!!' Suddenly from no-where a foot came across, the tread of the boot kicking the Front Office Personal Director square in the back, forcing her over the edge and into the pit. Falling through the darkness the former Television Champion swung her arms in a swimming motion but to no avail as she vanished from view, another victim to Gaz Green or that's what we thought. Standing victorious, holding his triumphant pose was Skitzo who suddenly broke into an argument with himself, obviously one of his persona's unhappy with his devious dealings. 'The fucking bitch deserved it!' hissed Mr. Psycho before bouncing opposite himself, retorting his very own statement. 'Why do you have to be so insensitive all the time!?!' 'Why don't you get your fucking balls re-attached!' continued the multi-persona schizophrenic when suddenly he began to roll about over the landing, his clenched fist swinging violently as he appeared to fight himself. With Skitzo's head crashing down on the floor, a trickle of blood materialised, followed by a black eye and a bruise to his cheekbone. As quick as the charade started, it had ended with the battered Skitzo springing back to his vertical base panting and wheezing heavily before extending his hand calling for a truce. Wiping the sweat from his forehead Skitzo bounced into a position to accept the handshake, waving his hand up and down in the midst of thin air the storm looked to have past when suddenly in a Judo throw fashion Skitzo threw himself down the pit. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' Watching as the seven-man party is reduced to four the scene switched to the wandering Black Dragons who have elected to tackle the lower half of the house. Searching separately we first follow the antics of Chi'ing as he stumbles into a gym with its array of metallic equipment ranging from your standard bench-press to a tread mill. Connected by a concrete arch the room joins onto another with a laminate flooring giving it a dojo sense of feel and shroud in dim lighting, Chi'ing intrigue presses him on when entering this new territory he looks upon a silhouette. 'Who's that, Chen Lei-Chia is that you?' questioned the former PURE superstar twitching his eyes as he adjusts to the lighting. The man who has his head bowed to the ground and appears in oriental garments, has a bamboo cane clenched tightly, his face concealed and looks rather dubious with regards to recent events. 'Chi'ing son, please step forward-....' said the stranger, his voice lowered and barely recognisable. 'Mountain Lord Pai Po is that you?' asked the Black Dragon member. Stepping from the shadows the assailant attacked, wielding his chosen weapon, in this case the Singapore cane he snapped the bamboo across Chi'ing's legs before striking down over his back. One crash after another and Chi'ing could only yelp in pain, as his body became a piñata, his mourns bellowing to the point where the young Black Dragon lost consciousness. As though on queue the lights came up and the deadly hitman was unveiled as Gaz 'The Jester' Green who masqueraded in the darkness, the magnificent seven that had become the fantastic four was now reduced to its bare number of just three. Again changing scene we stumble upon the other half of the Black Dragon collective, Chen Lei-Chia who carefully roamed around, his eyes alert as they sway in a pendulum motion. Treading through the designer kitchen and browsing through the cupboards as he done so, the Asian performer found himself back at the start, facing the entrance he stood next to the stairs where he noticed at the base was a wooden door, notably a basement or cellar. Grasping the handle Chen Lei-Chia pulled back at the oak door with all his might and through his persistence the barrier creaked open like a moment from Resident Evil. Staring into darkness the martial artist was a little reluctant to venture onward and so he cried from his position down into the emptiness. 'HELLO?!? HELLO?!? ANYBODY DOWN THERE-....' After a few pain staking seconds the response he longed for filtered as the shallow voices of Tim Walden, Brandi Wilson and Skitzo could be heard. 'IT'S TIM WALDEN AND BRANDI HERE, CAN YOU GET US OUT OF HERE?' 'GET US OUT OF HERE!!!' followed up Skitzo in the darkness. 'HELLO?!? WHO'S DOWN THERE ALTOGETHER?' roared the former PURE performer when suddenly a voice came from over his shoulder but before the Black Dragon could question its source he found himself forcefully thrown into the basement too. 'SKITZO, TIM WALDEN, BRADI-.... AND NOW YOU SQUIRE!!!' chuckled the Prince Of Pranks Gaz 'The Jester' Green as he eradicated the competition further, leaving just Playaz Inc left on this extensive hit-list. Positioning a metallic chair against the basement door, wedged up over the door handle the Jester cackled to himself before venturing over to the stairs that have now been returned to their rightful position. As the former Tag Team Champion ascended the stairs he found his chosen path blocked off by the rage-fuelled partners of Playaz Inc who snarl, hissing with every bated breath. 'I'm gonna cap your mothafuckin ass Jester, me and my bruv'er Dante are gonna fuck you the right up, oh, believe!' snarled the behemoth Statuz Quo almost foaming at the mouth, Dante however standing like a mute his face voicing his anger. As Gaz Green turned in the opposite direction Dante tampered with the furnishing piece of the banister and the stairs rolled back creating the slope we saw earlier in the series. Sliding to a heap before the stairs, the angered Playaz Inc collective pursued the Jester; Gaz was about to head towards the kitchen area when out stumbled the Black Dragon representative Chi'ing. Staggering and holding his aching back he rose his head with an evil glint in his eye, the Puro guided superstar stalked his prey. 'YOU’RE GOING TO DIE JESTER-.... HORRIBLY!!!' With Playaz Inc coming from one direction and Chi'ing at another, the Jester frantically stepped back, pinning himself against the mansions entrance. Suddenly the basement door smashed open and emerging through the debris was the Front Office's Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson, Skitzo and Chen Lei-Chia who whilst grinding their teeth moved in for the kill like a pack of hungry wolves. 'Can we talk about this, huh!?!' pleaded the Lord Of Laughter as he continued to panic. Stalking their prey, Gaz was left to his one option. Brandishing the key, Gaz Green frantically fumbled it into the locking mechanism and like the gates of heaven the doors opened allowing the Jester to make his getaway. Setting hotfooted from the premises, scrambling from the scene Gaz luckily opened the door of the black Cadillac roaring its open top engine and fleeing under a fog of thick smoke, still however pursued by the vengeance fuelled mob who bombarded their vehicles, striking up a chase sequence. As each automobile drove off into the distance and out of sight the scene closed on what could have been a Benny Hill inspired conclusion. - The End -
