Oracle...

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/02 Kenshiro:'Who?' The black-haired Japanese man in a grey cloak with khaki pants, black boots, and a green long sleeved tight shirt stands up out of his seat on the park bench next to Rumiko. The park is rather empty for the early hour, save for the occasional female college student sunbathing in her shorts bikini top and the voyeur recording her from the bushes. Kenshiro looks up at the sun and squinted his eyes to block the bright hot rays it casts down on the green city park in. Rumiko, sporting a yellow bikini top of her own which doesn't actually highlight her slender, pale B-cup figure much, stands up and pulls her green denim shorts down over her upper thighs, they rode up while she sat. She brushes her long brown hair out of her eyes and frowns at Kenshiro. Rumiko:'I know you're trying to impress me by taking on this Oracle thing, but do try to pay more attention, please?' Rumiko, who has a strawberry ice cream cone in her right hand, takes a few licks of what's left of it from the melting thanks to the sun and starts to eat the cone. Rumiko:'The End. He's your new assignment.' The oracle Rumiko, who's mute but speaks psionicly, pats Kenshiro on the head. Kenshiro waves her hand away and steals her ice cream cone, eating the last bites of it. Rumiko:'Hey!' Kenshiro finishes it. Rumiko shakes her head and places her hands on her hips in a playfully disappointed manner. Rumiko:'You owe me a new ice cream cone now!' Kenshiro:'No, I owe you a 1/2 of an ice-cream cone; that's all you had left.' Rumiko:'I still have to give you your assignment. I'll shoot it into your head.' Kenshiro shakes his head. Kenshiro:'You really have to work on your terminology.' Rumiko:'It's pretty much what I'm doing, shooting mind bolts containing information directly into your brain.' Kenshiro:'Nevermind. Just go ahead.' When Rumiko brought me into the Oracle game, under the tutelage of Sho the Oracle... I didn't think it would be this unrewarding. I mean, sure, I've got more knowledge than anyone would normally learn and I can kinda travel to the Celestial Realm at will as long as it's on business, but really, all I got into this thing for was Rumiko poon and she's not even putting out! I'm only doing it for her. And I am so lucky she can only transmit information to my brain and not read my thoughts. I can't believe he thinks I can't read minds. He's lucky I'm so nice. I'd usually just give him an assignment that wasn't supposed to pan out well, but this looks like it'll actually be something. The End is becoming more a Celestial everyday. Kenshiro should go and help him discover his powers. It will be good for him, and I can get some freaking space. Sheesh. Sho:'I'll take this one.' The park changes to the Celestial Plane. Sho the Oracle transported them there. He was the must powerful of the oracles, not really doing much nowadays, but still keeping tabs on things. Kenshiro:'Not him...' Kenshiro resents Sho the Oracle, making shots at him behind his back, but overall respects and fears him. Rumiko brought Kenshiro to Sho when Kenshiro insisted that he be an oracle as well. Rumiko didn't know if Kenshiro could handle being an oracle, so she asked Sho to take him under his wing, so to speak. It is not an easy thing, being in Sho's tutelage, but Kenshiro made it. Not because he wanted to be an oracle but because he wants to impress his love, Rumiko. The Celestial Plane is rather... plain. There's nothing there, the Celestial Plane isn't exactly a paradise. Mind you, there are exquisite palaces and such, but the Celestial Plane is mostly empty space. You know, the way things were. Humans ought to try that sometime. Just LEAVING SOMETHING THE F*CK HOW YOU FOUND IT. The Oracles' bodies emit their own light, making them that much better than normal people. Out in the empty vastness of space, Sho appears. he is bald, in fact, the spitting image of David Carradine as a Shaolin monk. Except for the black leather jacket, boots, and biker jacket. But hey, Sho can dress how he wants. He strokes his goateed chin as his piercing hazel eyes look straight through Rumiko and Kenshiro. He brings up a full-sized image of The End to their left. It shows him in his ring attire, standing straight up. Kenshiro:'Who's this?' Sho:'Your assignment, Rat Brain.' Kenshiro:'You're not a Psychlonian.' Sho:'Quiet you. Listen, he's a Celestial from hybrid birth. Digo the Grigori endowed his mother.' Rumiko leans her torso forward, her feet not touching any ground. Rumiko:'Digo? The hero of the Celestial Wars?' The great Oracle nods his head. Sho:'The same one. Digo himself has ceased in existence. He only endowed one... The End.' The End's internal organs are shown. Sho:'He is a model Celestial in every way, save for the one thing that makes him weak. A human heart.' Kenshiro:'Greeeeeeeaaaat. Hey listen, ask me if I care about any of this?' Kenshiro gets a sharp nudge in the ribs from Rumiko. Rumiko:'Sorry, Sho. Please continue.' Sho shakes his head in the negative. Sho:'No, Kenshiro has a point. This is all a bit boring. So I'll cut to the chase. Kenshiro, The End is starting to develop his powers. And it's your job to make sure it goes the right way, and the beast within isn't unleashed.' Kenshiro:'And if it is?' Sho makes a trident materialize. It's made of sharp bone and has a screw cap on the bottom. Sho twists it, and the trident prongs split away into a triangle and retract to the handle of the device. Sho:'You know what to do...'
