Inner Balance...The Complete Warrior...

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-10-2002 (A shot of a starry night fills the screen. Illumined by a full moon, the night's sky is actually quite bright and the presence of so many stars in the luminescence is startlingly. The sound of nights is calming, soothing. The shot pans down and we are met with the visage of a vast lake. The lake is perfectly still and reflects the sky back up towards the heavens. As the shot moves to the shore where the water gently laps the sand, we see a man. By the sword he holds in his right hand we know him instantly to be Venneman Pendragon. He stands motionless as he holds the sword pointed down before him. His eyes are closed and he seems to be taking in the whole scene...Smells, sounds...Clad in a white shirt and black pants, he has a regal look about him, his blonde hair brushed haphazardly away from his face. He is the picture of a hero, a myth. Something almost too perfect to exist in the dirty world of today. The shot remains the same as a voiceover by Venneman Pendragon kicks in...) 'Absolute calm. To find balance inside, one must achieve absolute calm. My balance is what makes me whole. My balance is what allows me to stand above every single man or woman I come into contact with. Balance. The truest meaning of life that one can know. Balance between love and hate, good and evil...Light and darkness. Balance. It has taken myself years of dedication to finding myself to achieve balance...It has taken years to find that what I have been looking for was here all along...' (Eyes still closed, Venneman begins to work with the sword. He moves deftly along the bank of the lake and almost seems to dance away and into unseen foes. The motion is fluid; well practiced. The sword is almost an extension of the man...) 'What is important here is not that I practice the sword...It is the way in which I do it. Each movement ahs meaning...Each step, significance. I practice because it calms me. It is a way to harness the darkness from within and bind it to the light. It is a way for me to seek completion. That is why I do it; that is why I love it. It is an art...Granted, a forgotten art but one of beauty and grace nonetheless. It all goes back to balance. With my eyes closed, I flow through the forms, and I have balance...' (Abruptly, Venneman sheaths the sword. His eyes open and stare directly into the camera. Insanity burns deep within those eyes, but Pendragon doesn't seem to care. He strips off his shirt and straps the sword to his back, ancient warrior style. He then takes off running from the bank, towards the woods some mile distant. The camera matches him, stride for stride, and we are able to keep a frontal view...That's one hell of a tripod if I do say so myself...) 'Balance though, is not merely a mental thing...It is not just a balance of light and dark, but also of body and spirit. Just because the spirit is strong, it doesn't mean that the body can keep up. Likewise, a strong body doesn't dictate a worthy spirit. Harmony must be reached. If a spirit matches the body it resides in, then that body becomes an unstoppable force. I could run at this madman's pace for hours if I needed to. Endurance is a strong point of mine because I have the spirit to drive the body further than it would normally be willing to go...On the other hand, if the spirit ever were to give up, the body would carry on because of the inborn strength. That is balance. That is perfection...' (Pendragon reaches the woods and doesn't slow down. He runs straight down a path lined with trees on either side. Some are large, some are small. As he passes by, he reaches out and clotheslines a tree here, or he splinters a tree with a devastating spear takedown. After each impact, after he shatters each tree he hits, he bounds back to his feet and keeps running, seemingly untouched by fatigue...) 'I have never met a man who can put his body through so much as I. A lot of people can take pain...Some people even claim to thrive upon it...Neither describes me. I simply do not know what pain is. Is my body should ever feel it, I seal it off. I don't let the pain loose in my mind. Many people claim to be able to take pain, but in the end, they always succumb to the terror of the Last Crusade...' (Pendragon redraws the sword and takes his hacks at the trees as he passes by with one hand he chops, with the other, he doles out devastating punches. Each blow of his fist leaves a dent and sends shudders through the tree, each slash of sword chops the trees in half...) 'All mere men are just the same...They lack the fortitude of both body and soul to stand on the battleground with me. I have met many who claim to be warriors, but every one has come up short in some way. Some lack heart, some lack strength, some lack both...The problem with most, however, is that they are prone to worry. But how can worry be a detriment you ask? Everyone worries, right? Wrong. I do not worry, and I do not regret. I accept myself for who I am. It is because of that that I have been able to transcend the normal plane of the mind the body and the soul...It is because of this that I am not a mere mortal. I am a myth. I am a LEGEND! I am that which all aspire...Perfection. I am above the puny half men, or women to appease the few, who would dare do battle with me. I have been called many things in my day...Brash, cocky...Crazy. I guess I am a little bit of all three, but the word that should be used to describe me is merely...MASTER! It matters not where I end up; when it comes down to it, I rule. I always come out on top. It is only natural...How can a man who is above the half-wits he shares screen time with come out on the bottom? The fans know it. Why? Because the fans love the underdog. Many times, the fans will root for the man whom I am destroying. I cannot blame them. Perfection is hard to cheer, since it is lacked by all save me. I know that I can never cheer the underdog...Why wish success on the undeserving?' (Venneman stops and sheathes the sword once more. He looks directly into the camera and begins to speak. No voice over this time. His voice is haughty and confident, seemingly used to command...) Venneman: It is obvious that the powers that be in PCW do not wish success upon the undeserving either. Why else would they place gold in my grasp so quickly? My first match and already they give me the opportunity to take what is mine! I stand here, unestablished in PCW thus far, and they give me the chance to beat upon five unworthy competitors to take my first gold...Most people wait until it would be treasonous to keep me from the titles...PCW wastes no time. Were I a common competitor, I would be surprised. Were I one of the masses of untalented whelps in PCW I would be upset...I mean, why allow The Legend to stake his claim so quickly? Why not give the rest of us a chance....I am neither of those...I am the Master...And soon I shall rule....Prepare for the Pain....Prepare for the PAIN!!!! (The shot zooms in on Pendragon's crazed eyes as he starts bellowing with crazed laughter. Complete? Crazy? You make the call....Fine~)
