Saving Taco Bell

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th April Still Flaymin Aymin has the Taco Bell dog that is so precious to the Mexican Devil and still that means that Aymin has one over El Diablo. But no more, as now El Diablo has expressed his interest in finding that dog instead of paying that weasel, Flaymin Aymin the ransom he requested. It has now been weeks since Aymin took the dog and he now seems to be going to extremities to try get the ransom money. Instead though El Diablo has decided to track down this dog and take him back home. However, El Diablo has not seen the state of his dog and does not know where Flaymin is, so finding the dog thus far has been pretty unsuccessful, until an anonymous tip off he received. Flashback 'You have one message,' says the computerised tone of the cell phone El Diablo is holding. 'What the hell?' asks El Diablo to himself while surrounded by two Mexican Federales. The officers look at the seated Diablo and scratch their moustaches. 'I think it means you have voice mail, senor,' says one of the Federales. El Diablo looks up at him with a baffled look on his face. 'Voice mail?' he asks confused. The Mexican Federale with the moustache and distinguishing scar on his cheek kneels down next to Diablo where the phone is. 'Well, you see, you press this button,' he points to the big red button on the phone. El Diablo presses a black one. 'No, no, no,' says the Federale, 'The red button,' he yells in his heavy Mexican/American accent. Diablo still confused presses a green button. The second Federale shakes his head in disbelief while placing his right hand on his forehead. The Federale on his knees looks angered, and that is much emphasised by the throbbing vein on his forehead. 'I said red, red!!!' he shouts in anger. El Diablo points to the blue button. 'WHAT'S WRONG WITH YOU? ARE YOU COLOR BLIND?' he yells while snatching the phone from El Diablo and pressing the red button himself. El Diablo looks unphased as the Federale calms down and all three men move their ears towards the phone in anticipation for the message. 'Erm, yeah, I know where the dog is, I actually live nearby. It's in a house in California, somewhere near Sacramento. I live next door on Terwilliger Road. Hurry,' says the voice before hanging up. El Diablo and the Federales all look at each other. 'Lets get going,' they all say at the same time. That was the tip that sent them on their way to Sacramento, or most importantly sent El Diablo on his way to Sacramento, California. 'This used to be Mexican land,' says El Diablo aloud to himself. He sighs softly. 'Why did America kill my land and people?' he says under his breath as he walks onto 'Terwilliger Road' The street is a mess, lawns not mowed, cars left in heaps and used condoms on the streets sidewalks. El Diablo just nods. 'Yeah, this looks about right,' he says while peeling a used condom off his shoe, 'And they call Mexicans dirty,' he finishes discarding of the used condom. The houses don't look like much either, looks typical of where Flaymin would live, an utter mess. Suddenly El Diablo is tapped on the shoulder, he turns around sharply only to find it was one of the Federales parked in the van they came there in. 'Senor, we'll wait here, if you need us to shriek,' he says in his heavy accent. El Diablo nods. 'Yeah, I can do that,' he smirks as the Federale closes the van door and waits while reading a newspaper trying to look inconspicuous in a van that reads 'Mexican Police' El Diablo simply shakes his head and walks on, looking for the precise house that Aymin has. Sounds easy right? Not when he has to walk through heaps of used condoms, beer cans and soiled mattresses as well as just the usual garbage left out on the streets. El Diablo's clothing of black denim jeans and a black jacket soon turns into trash soaked jeans and a disease-ridden jacket. 'Aww, what have they done to our once precious land?' he asks himself while searching for the correct house. He stops in probably the cleanest aspect of the street currently and looks around, looking for what could be anything distinctive of Flaymin Aymin. 'Ah, ha!' he says in glee as he sees a house with beer cans in front of windows and a tractor parked in the driveway. 'How could I have missed that?' thinks Diablo diligently as he walks into Aymin's driveway. He walks to the front door and hears barking coming from somewhere, so he runs around the back until the barking gets louder and there he sees a door that leads down. It must be a basement. El Diablo puts his foot on one of the steps before recoiling it and looking around suspiciously as flashbacks of Home Alone come to him. He quickly realises there are no booby traps and walks down the steps until he reaches the door. 'Hmmm,' thinks El Diablo on how to get the door open, 'How?' he asks himself. He searches around in his jeans pockets looking for something to break the lock with, a gentle breeze then blows and the door opens to Diablo's astonishment. 'Of course,' he says in feigned realisation. He walks into the old basement, where it still has a concrete floor and many hundreds of beer kegs piled up in one corner. The basement is quite large and even has an old television in there that is actually on and is muted, on there is gay porn and tied around a beer keg in front of it is the Taco Bell Dog. The dog however doesn't look healthy, it is fat and barely resembles that of a Chihuahua and is pretty out of it. 'He's torturing my dog with gay porn?' Diablo questions out loud, 'That bastard!!!' he finished while looking around the basement to make sure Aymin hasn't set him up. Everything seems in order except for the many bikini tops ticking to the ceiling. El Diablo rushes over to his beloved Chihuahua. He gets down on both knees and looks over at his dog in distraught. He looks up and down at the fat torso of the dog and empty beer cans behind him as well as Taco Bell burrito wrappers. El Diablo looks utterly disgusted as he stares at his Chihuahua in concern. 'Oh Andy, what have they done to you?' he asks the dog in sheer horror and concern, 'I've got to get you out of here,' he says while untying the dog from the beer keg. Instead of getting the dog he rushes over the television and pushes it over, smashing it into pieces. 'That's for subjecting my dog to gay porn,' he yells loudly. The dog burps as El Diablo walks back over to him and leans down to pick him up. 'Doesn't he know how to treat a dog?' he questions aloud, 'Well, he does have Jenna,' he jokes to himself as he leads himself out of Flaymin's basement and back up into the street. He tries to run but fails miserably due to the Chihuahuas weight problems. It farts this time. 'Andy, he will pay for this I promise,' he whispers the dog in concern for it's well being. He finally reaches the van, with many used condoms stuck to his jeans. 'We need a vet immediately,' he shrieks to the two Federales in their highly conspicuous van. One of them opens the side door and lets them both in, the van door slams immediately as the engine starts. The vans tyres screech fast as a burping sound is heard as it drives towards the nearest veterinarian.
