Kalli, Take Me Back (Part 1)

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 6, 2003 Losing the one you love is never easy, even for tough, muscle bound wrestlers. Athletes such as these still experience the same weaknesses that common people do. They still cry. They long for the one that has left them. They even toss and turn in their sheets wondering if they will ever win her back. Tyler Preston had been dealt a rough hand. Deeming Tyler to busy for her, Kalli up and left him a week ago. Every motivation that Tyler had enjoyed now falls to the floor like dust, only to be swept under the carpet, but not forgotten. The door of the dark apartment cracks open, flooding refreshingly bright light from the hall to escape in. John Gargano pokes his bushy black head through the door to find a truly sad sight. Tyler Preston slouches in the stained white couch sporting a white tank top and a pair of athletic shorts. Cookie crumbs have called a meeting in the crevice of his tank top where they gather together in a bundle. He almost appears to have the mannerisms of an old man that fails to see the point in living anymore. He gazes in to the eyes of his new best friend, the television. The images reflect of his thickly glazed eyes as he doesn’t even give John as much as a nod of acknowledgment. John swings the door open with a smile and jumps over the trash and clothes that littler the blue carpeting. Tyler still doesn’t budge, so John begins to communicate with the ‘Tyler Preston Vegetable’. “Hey man, what are you doing cooped up in this dump?” John inquires as he hops over various particles or trash to approach the robed Tyler Preston who is in a glazed over state. He stares at the television with no emotion on his face. Every now and then, he flicks through the channels to find other programming. “John, just get out of here, I don’t really have the energy to do anything right now. I’ll be lucky if I’m even ready to go at Turmoil,” Tyler feebly fights off. He isn’t ready for company, and frankly, is embarrassed about the present state that his girlfriend has put him in. “Come on Tyler, snap out of it. She is just a girl. I’ve lost many girlfriends and I’m still keeping a happy attitude,” John attempts to cheer Tyler up. He puts a smile on his face, showing that the rejecting from the women hasn’t dampened his spirits. Tyler glances over at him, and without an expression, sets his eyes back on the flashing television. “You don’t understand John. This girl was different. She was the one. Now, I don’t know if I’ll ever see her again,” Tyler says as he runs his dirty fingers through his greasy blonde hair. He carries that same hand over his face, and for a moment, he almost looks like he is going to cry. John plops down on the off-white couch along side of him. “Did you try calling her?” John asks as he lays an affectionate hand on Tyler’s shoulder. Tyler keeps his hand over his face despite the friendly touch of someone who cares. “Of course, I’ve called her like a million times. She picks the phone up just to hang it up really quickly, or lets her machine get it. I’ll never get through to her,” Tyler confesses. It is true, he had been attempting to call his former love for the better part of the morning. He has no success. “Did you try going over there?” John continues the inquiry. Tyler just shakes his head as he removes his hand from his stubble harboring face. He sighs and throws his arms up in the air, signifying that it is truly hopeless. “I drove by her building a couple times but don’t really have the courage to go up there. John, she was really angry with me last week. I think its over,” Tyler finally admits to himself. This whole time he’s been grasping tightly to the notion that there may be a chance that they’ll get back together. At this point, he is throwing in the towel. John moves in closer and shakes his head, blocking out that idea. “That is Nonsense! The only way that you are going to become a loser is if you keep thinking like one. I’m sure we can find a way to get that Philly back,” John says in an energetic voice in attempts to knock Tyler out of his trance. Tyler perks up a little at his friend’s newfound interest in helping him get back the one he loves. “Take a time out for a second. Why are you so hells bent on helping me get her back? Just the other minute ago you were saying that she’s just another girl,” Tyler asks, making sure that John’s attitude is genuine. John has been known to do things for some pretty shallow reasons. He wants to avoid getting in a hostile situation such as that. “I’ll be honest with you; I don’t like to see you hurting. You’re my friend,” John says in an overly genuine voice as he rubs his hand on Tyler’s shoulder. Tyler looks bewildered, but then decides that it must be a pretty genuine comment. “Hey man, that’s a really cool thing to say,” Tyler says sincerely in appreciation for his friend. Not to many people have helped him out in this situation but John has really come through for him today, or so it has seemed. “And I guess it wouldn’t hurt that I want you back in time to retain the titles. Those tag titles are also my friend you know,” John says as he allows his voice to trail off. He had to be honest and tell Tyler the true, underlying reason for his aid. Tyler just rolls his eyes, knowing that John was doing it for all the wrong reasons. Yet, Tyler doesn’t care, as long as he gets his former girlfriend out of the deal. “I knew there was another reason like that,” Tyler mumbles, trying to score even more sympathy from John. John feels remotely sorry about the comment that he made, so he continues to give encouragement to his downed friend. “Tyler, don’t worry. When we get done with getting Kalli back, we’ll both get what we want out of the deal. We’ll stay PCW Tag Team Champions and you will have a warm body back in your bed,” John coaxes. Tyler sits up, coming to the conclusion that it isn’t a bad deal at all. John smiles, glad that Tyler is finally giving an attempt to move from the crater that he has worn in to the couch. “That sounds pretty good right about now,” Tyler says with warmth coming back to his eyes as he envisions the two of them back together. A smile even wanders to his lips for the first time this week. “I’m sure it does, so you better get dressed so we can head out. We have a lot to do before we can approach the brown haired goddess that is Kalli Stearns,” John instructs him as he points to the door of Tyler’s bedroom. It is half open with close protruding out from under the crack. “Get dressed? Where are we going?” Tyler asks in shock, as if he didn’t think his current attire wasn’t exactly elegant enough. Tyler looks down at himself and notices the stains, but still stands by his question. “If you are truly sorry, then you have to start dressing like you are. If you approach Kalli looking like a bum, then she isn’t going to give you the time of day,” John explains as he shoves Tyler in the direction of his room. Tyler resists. “That’s no different from now,” Tyler mumbles with dampened spirits once again as he fights to go sit back on the couch. Lucky for John and his training, he is strong enough to continue pushing Tyler towards the bedroom. “Listen, just get dressed because we have some shopping to do. Nothing says I’m sorry like a good gift. Be ready Tyler, because your princess is coming back to you,” John says as if this is going to be the most exciting thing that has happened to them yet. Tyler sighs a deep breath and walks in to his bedroom. He attempts to shut the door, but the clothes only allow it to close so far. Rustling is heard from the other side of the door as Tyler quickly changes his clothes. John hops on to the couch to catch some game shows on the television. He flicks it to the ‘Price is Right’ and sits back t wait for his friend. How is Tyler to trust John? He is no love doctor. Apparently John has some ‘brilliant’ ideas to win Tyler’s love back. How they will shape up, that’s another question.
