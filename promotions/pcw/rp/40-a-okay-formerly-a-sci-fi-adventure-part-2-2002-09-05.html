40 A-Okay [Formerly a Sci-Fi Adventure(Part 2)]

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 5, 2002 Comorragh, like a roach motel, a place where most humans come in, but they don’t come out. The sky is eternally twilight, and numerous spires (known as hives) clutter the landscape. Home to the intergalactic “pirates”, as the humans would call them, known as The Dark Eldar. The Dark Eldar themselves are perhaps the most evil, and the most feared race in all of the Terran Empire. They thrive on death, destruction, pain, suffering, and the service of their warriors, such as Eddie the Mandrake. Comorragh is also a place where desolation is like a good friend that you’d take on a trip. It accompanies everyone, everywhere they roam in the darkly twisted realm. Purple gas clouds, pulse slowly with light, and they swirl like a dark dance that they perform. Within a tavern, called “The bloody heart”, we see several of the warriors that were involved in the slave raid at a human colony; most of them still wearing their unclean armor, still sporting numerous piles of flesh that inhabit the spikes that is on their armor. Most of them are laughing it up, torturing the slaves, seeing how long they’ll last before they expire. One brutal test is to inject noxious chemicals into the bloodstream of the slave. The bet is to find out how long the slave will last before he or she dies. That was one of two main recreational killings that they were doing. The other, was throwing a slave into an airlock, and blowing the hatch to see how long they’ll hold on before being sucked out into the void of Comorragh. Both were going on, and it was mainly the bulk of the warrior squads committing the deeds. The elites were watching and making the bets. Many of the actual mandrake units were sitting there, drinking the blood of many of the slaves that already lost their lives previously. Some are drinking as heavily as Stone Cold Steve Austin after he wins and making a very large mess while they are at it. The slave waiters are trying to keep up with the demand, but they are falling…literally. “Hey Eddie, want to see something cool? Watch this.” One of the troops calls out. Eddie just leans back in his chair, as the warrior leads a sixteen year old girl out onto the floor. The troop grabs a metal rod with a rather sharp point on it. He plunges it into a plasma torch, the Dark Eldar’s main light source. The rod’s end heats up rather quickly glowing red now. The warrior pulls it from the plasma torch, and looks at the end. He eyes widen with anticipation. Two of his buddies grab the girl’s arms and holds them out. The warrior, now with an absolutely sadistic grin on his face, approaches the girl. Eddie’s face turns from enjoyment, to disgust. “Brethren, back off.” Eddie Barked. “Aw, come on…I heard is you stick a red hot poker into the eye of a human, their head explodes! Let me see!” “Back off now, or I’m reporting you to Lord Vect for disobeying orders. You know what happens…” “Y-y-yes, sir” The warrior concludes as the back off. Edie’s Thoughts: Eddie approaches the girl and leads her from the floor of death into a side room. The room is quite bare; it however, does have a cot, a sink, and a toilet, almost standard issue Prison quarters, except no steel bars. Eddie looks at the girl; the girl is shaking, because she knows how close she came to death. Eddie just looks at her with a confused look on his face. How the hell does someone so young be taken by us. Most of my kind don’t have limits, but I do. Why am I cursed with this self-restraint? “I thought your kind didn’t have a conscience” The young one spurts. “Is that your word for a self-restraint that prevents you from watching certain acts that absolutely repulse someone?” Eddie questions the young one. “In a word…yes, but my question is why?” The young one asks. Eddie ponders the question briefly. “Well…call it me having a ‘conscience’. You can also call it me not wanting someone so young taken. You can also call it the fact that I find you attractive…even for a slave” Eddie answers. The girl begins to breathe heavily, as Eddie eyes her young, maturing body. He slowly walks toward her, and she backs away at the same pace. Eddie tilts his head to the left. The young girl reaches the wall on the opposite end of the room. Panic sets in. Eddie’s eyes widen and he prepares for the big leap into the young one’s panties. Before he could do anything however, the door swings open with one of Eddie’s buddies standing in the threshold. “Eddie. Lord Vect wants to see you” he states. “Not now, can’t you see that I’m about to score, tell him I’ll see him later” “Wrong answer…NOW!” Eddie turns away from the soon-to-be-victim, and turns toward his colleague. He walks up to him and stares him down. His colleague is taller than him by about a good foot. Eddie simply looks up at him. “Get this slave cleaned and in my quarters in two hours, and if she is harmed at any time in between now and two hours from now…I will see to it that you are left to die in the middle of a Tyranid Genestealer brood” Eddie exclaims as he heads out of the bar. He walks out of the bar into the corridors of the station. It is a rather large complex, being the home of numerous Dark Eldar soldiers basically those who are in the service, and not civilians. He heads into an elevator, and pushes a button with a certain rune on it. It reads in the loosest translation, “Main Office”. The doors of the elevator close and it starts going up. Eddie’s Thoughts: This was probably my request to take on someone else other than the worthless humans. I am so sick of them; I want some new trophies to hang on my wall. I get intestines every damn ascension day, and I have a box full in my closet. **DING!** The doors open up showing a massive throne room, with slaves playing instruments with incredible discord, other Dark Eldar playing “hang-a-human”. Unfortunately for the human, he keeps getting hung, and revived every time. Eddie walks toward the massive pointed throne of the leader of the most successful chapter in the known history of the Dark Eldar, Asdrubael Vect. He is being seduced by several members of the female soldiers of the Dark Eldar, the Wych Cult. He peers through them and sees Eddie standing there. The tall overlord stands and throws the females out of the way, his armor is rather clean with the red and black hues shining in the torch light. “MY BOY! HA! Come in, care to indulge yourself with the Wyches?” Vect asks. “No, that’s fine my Lord” Eddie answers. “Some food and drink then?” “No, that’s quite alright, sir.” “I Insist!” Vect now demands “Alright, maybe a few bites” Eddie agrees. Eddie and Vect sit down at a table that looks like it was designed by H. R. Giger. Chairs taken from an Alien movie and the table look no better. The table itself has little or no space remaining on it from all the food and battle plans that are already on it. Eddie sits down first hoping that Vect won’t pull any sort of aggressive moves. Vect also sits down rather calmly. He grabs a leg of poultry. We don’t know what kind of poultry, but it’s a bird nonetheless. “You know something. I remember the time you took your first slave, and you brutally killed him, even though we had to egg you on. That’s how you got your name” Vect says. “I know, I was there. This call wasn’t for a social visit, I know that.” “Eddie, you are always so direct and to the point. I guess that’s why you rival Decapitator for the title of Champion Mandrake” Vect states as he sits up and straightens himself out. “I was talking with Archon Rapheel after the last slave raid you were on. He told me about your longing for better targets than the Humans. You see, humans are so fruitful in this galaxy, that’s why we attack them.” “What about the Tyranids?” Eddie inquires. “They are also fruitful, but they are no fun as slaves. We’d have to annihilate each and every last one of them in order to maintain the slave count. Hell, they’d try to assimilate the slaves and us into their bio-consciousness. They are just not feasible” Vect explains. “What about the Orks?” “Man you have got to be kidding? Those idiots would get lost in a single room with one door in it. Dumb as warp beasts, but strong as a Talos. They’d simply be no fun because they would be too stupid to know they are being tortured.” “Okay, how about the Tau?” “Now you’re being naïve. In order to even attempt a raid on the Tau, we would need 100,000 troops in order to get 10,000 troops close enough to initiate close combat. A slave raid on any Tau controlled world is suicide. If your next question is going to be about the Necrons, forget it. They are simply too cold and sterile to attack and capture.” Vect explains some more as he grabs a breast from the poultry. “Come on, you always see the bad side of the races. It’s true that the humans are plentiful, but we have enough humans to last us a few hundred years! We need to go after bigger game!” Eddie yells. Vect at this point stands up, throwing the chair backwards onto the floor. Eddie does the same. The Wyches look worried for their master, and the rest of the Dark Eldar in the room look toward the conversation. The tension is mounting. “Look, I see things from a perspective that you cannot. I have the battle experience of a dozen lifetimes, and you only have a few slave raids under your belt. I have takes slaves from all in combat, and all were not easy to deal with! If you want to challenge my authority…” Vect notices Eddie slowly reaching for his set of Poisoned Blades. “I wouldn’t do that if I were you. I have three Haemonculi with Stingers trained on you. Do as I say, and do not dare attack the other races. If you do, I will personally hold you responsible for any losses incurred.” Vect orders as he turns his back. “However, if you do bring back slaves, then they may square up your losses. You have been excused, Eddie.” Vect adds as two of the Haemonculi lead Eddie out of Vect’s room and into the elevator. Eddie’s Thoughts: Man, fuck that old piss ant. Why the hell do I have to listen to what he says? Just because he has a company of Wyches, and a Haemonculus on him, doesn’t make him any stronger than the rest of the troops. If I ever get the chance, I’ll slit the fucker’s throat myself…Hmm, Raw meat sounds good for dinner tonight. A few minutes later Eddie returns to his quarters high in one of the spires. He walks in to see that lone colleague with the slave that he wanted spared. Eddie’s apartment in Comorragh is always a mess. His floor is simply a shelf for all his clothing, weapons, armor, the occasional body part, and mostly garbage from the take out delicacies that he’d order from the numerous take out places near the complex. He walks into his place and immediately steps onto leftover bone. It cracks rather loudly, and he looks up. “Alright, here she is, cleaned, and ready for work, and not harmed as your asked for” He says with a very stupid grin on his face. “Okay, get the hell out of here” Eddie says as the colleague leaves. Eddie walks over to the newly found slave and inspects her. Eddie notices a tear in her clothing, made by a bare hand. “Stay here, I’ll be right back” Eddie says as he melds with the shadows. The slave shivers for a brief moment as Eddie leaves the apartment undetected. Eddie rushes down the hallway to the elevator. The colleague boards the car, and Eddie does as well just before the doors close. Eddie returns to the light, and immediately slits the throat of the colleague. He drops to his knees gasping for air. “You didn’t think I wouldn’t notice it? Hmm? You tried to make her do something…tried to force her to do something??” Eddie interrogates. The colleague simply gasps and dies leaving a puddle of blood on the carpet. Eddie The elevator reaches the ground floor, Eddie kicks out the body, and hits the button to return to his apartment. He walks back in, to see that she has disappeared. Eddie looks around, and finds his prize in his bedroom…the only room that seems to be clean. “What’s wrong?” Eddie inquires “He tried to rape me, luckily for me; you came in at the right time.” The girl answers. “Well…You won’t be hearing much of him. I got you a present though.” Eddie throws something into her lap. “GAAHHH! IT’S A HEART!” The girl shrieks. “Yeah, and?” “You can’t kill people!” “I have to! It’s them or me, and I always choose me!” “And what about this…mission that you want to go on? You are going to bring more people here in order for them to be your play things.” “AND WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH THAT?! YOU ARE A SLAVE, AND YOU WILL NOT QUESTION ANYTHING I DO! GET OUT OF MY SIGHT!” Eddie roars as the slave leaves the room. Eddie lays down in his bed, still wearing his armor. He stares up at the ceiling, and ponders. Eddie’s Thoughts: My new slave is nice. I should treat her differently from all the other skanks that I had in the past. I wonder whom I can get to lead this mission into the uncharted sectors. Maybe Rapheel might be up for it. Maybe Bozghoi…Hell, why the hell am I thinking about this…maybes some sleep will help. Yeah…sleep. With that Eddie drifts off to the nightmarish dreamland.
