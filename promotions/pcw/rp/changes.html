Date: 3-11-02 Last we saw of Reeco Suave, he had started on his journey to his first PCW show. His debut match against another PCW newcomer, the asian Charlie Loc. With only one bag filled with a few needed essentials.. wrestling attire, change of clothes, etc.. he set out in his black '72 Camaro. After a rather uncomfortable incident with a gas station attendant named 'Hoss', Reeco was back on on the road.. and so, we continue his path to Norman, Oklahoma - finding out more and more about the 'new' Reeco Suave with each passing moment. Today, we see the black '72 Camaro parked outside of a small bar just a few minutes away from the freeway. The scene progresses to the interior of the bar, where we see that it is a full house tonight. And amongst all of the people, we see one man stand out. Sitting at the bar with a bottle of Heineken in his hand, is Reeco Suave. He seems to be worn down from the long drive - pouches under his eyes, shaggy hair, and wrinkled clothes. As he takes a sip of beer from the Heineken bottle, we see someone sit down next to him. A guy with a dark brown beard, blue jeans, a white 'belly'-shirt, and a black leather vest. He seems to be a truck driver. Truck Driver: I'll have whatever he's having.. As the man points at Reeco, the bartender - seemingly fresh out of college, is a buff guy with his hair slicked back. He prepares the drink as the man is still looking at Reeco. Reeco Suave: Umm.. do you have a problem? Reeco looks at the guy, with his fat stomach hanging out, and the grease stains on his shirt with disgust. Truck Driver: Nope, no problem.. sunshine. Reeco, in the middle of taking a sip from his beer just stops dead in his tracks. He slowly places the bottle of beer on the counter, and we see a large gulp go down his throat as he looks at the truck driver. Meanwhile, the guy sitting next to Reeco is still looking at him, not breaking eye contact.. we see a smile creep onto his face. Reeco Suave: S-sunshine? *cough* Uh... umm, I think you have me mistaken with someone else. I'm not 'sunshine'.. Truck Driver: Heh heh, sure you're not.. hun. Reeco Suave: Umm.. rright. Well, I'm not 'sunshine', or 'hun'.. Truck Driver: We-hell.. you can be whoever you want to be, deary. Reeco just looks down at his drink and shakes his head. He then rubs his eyes and looks back at the truck driver, who now has a creepish smile on his face as he slides a piece of paper towards Reeco on the counter, all the while keeping eye contact with Reeco. Reeco picks up the paper and we see a phone number on it, along with the guy's name.. which seems to be 'Bubba'. Bubba: Why dontcha.. tuck that in your nice little back pocket there, make sure to not lose it.. sweet thang. Reeco Suave: What the who what where what?! What do you want from me?! I'm not like that!!! God! What is it with you people! Its like.. everywhere I go, I attract you weird ass people!! WHAT DO I HAVE TO DO TO GET YOU GOD DAMN WEIRDOS OFF OF ME?!! Bubba: Whoa, whoa sugah.. you don't have to hold back your feelings. Just let it go, man.. Let all those repressed feelings go! - Gosh you're pretty. With the beer in hand, Reeco looks down at the nearly finished bottle. He just blankly stares at it, while the 'Bubba' continues to 'flirt' with Reeco. Finally, Reeco chugs the rest of the beer down and calls for the bartender. Reeco Suave: I'll have another.. no, wait. Make it two more. Bartender: I think you've had enough for tonight, pal.. Reeco Suave: Two more. Reeco then turns to Bubba and looks at him with his beady eyes. He's only drunk one beer bottle tonight, but he is seemingly drunk as he begins to speak with a slur. Reeco Suave: Ya know what? I don't think you're really there! Nope.. sure don't! Just a figment of my imagination! Infact.. all of you are fake! Yep! Bubba: Sugah, I guarantee you that I'm real. An I'm all man... Reeco squints his eyes at Bubba, and he looks back at the empty bottle of beer. Just then, the bartender brings Reeco two more bottles of Heineken. After the 'torment' Bubba has put Reeco through, we see him pop the cap off of all the bottles and just chug away at all of them. After chugging down two more bottles of beer, Reeco drops everything and looks back at Bubba. Reeco Suave: Thats it... no more alcohol. Just then we see Bubba place his hands on Reeco's thigh... Reeco Suave: What the? Get your god damn hands off of me you fat, fucking... a-arhhh! WIth a look of complete and utter anger on his face, Reeco grabs a half empty beer bottle and storms off to a the other side of the bar, where he sits down in a booth.. amidst the people in the bar, Reeco has lost Bubba. However, as he now sits in a booth.. we see a medium sized man with a receding hairline and blood-shot eyes at a small table to the side of Reeco. In just two days on the road, Reeco has been some what of a 'weirdo'-magnet. And as he sits now, with half-full beer bottle in hand.. trying to drown the events of the past few days, he begins to think out loud. Reeco Suave: Why.. why me? Sure, I'm one sexy mother.. sure, I'm the kind of guy people often gravitate towards. But, by god - WHY!? First.. Hoss.. well, he was just weird. But, now I have.. 'Bubba'.. will it ever stop?! Reeco takes a sip of his beer as he looks up at the ceiling, almost as if pleading with god. As Reeco brings his head back down, he tilts it to the side and we see the strange medium sized man with a receding hairline sitting next to him. The guy, with bloodshot eyes and all, is staring at Reeco.. then we hear something over a radio speaker mounted on the corner of the wall right above Reeco. ::THIS JUST IN: Escaped convict Tony the Tulip has escaped! Be very cautious.. he is armed, and dangerous. Tony the Tulip is a medium sized man, wearing blue jeans and a white t-shirt, he can be clearly recognized by a receding hairline, and bloodshot eyes. If you have any information on the whereabouts of Tony the Tulip, or if you know where he is.. please, contact the local authorities. And run!:: Reeco looks up at the radio speaker on the wall. Then, slowly, he turns his head to his side.. where we see the guy still staring blankly at Reeco with his bloodshot eyes. Reeco Suave: No.. not.. again! This is just not my day. With a look of contempt, Reeco grabs his beer bottle and walks back to the front of the bar and sits down at the front counter. 'Bubba' is nowhere to be found, and to this we see a smile of relief come over Reeco's face. He drinks the last of his beer and calls over the bartender again. Bartender: Hey, look pal.. you're buzzed off your ass. I think you should just go back to your hotel room, or wherever it is that you're staying.. and get a good nights sleep. Reeco Suave: I am not drunk, damnit! I'm just a little pissed off. Now.. don't tell me what I can and can't do!! I said I wanted another drink, and I meant it! The bartender, who had a rag on his shoulder, grabs it and places it on the counter. He then leans in and places his elbows on the rag as he has a concerned look on his face. Bartender: Look pal, I can tell you're going through some hard times. Why don'tcha talk about it.. you must be dying to release some of that pent up tension. Reeco Suave: You.. want to know whats wrong with me? Fine! I'll tell you whats wrong with me! We begin to sense Reeco's temper getting ready to blow. He is starting to raise his voice as he is still speaking with a small slur. Reeco Suave: I just joined this wrestling promotion called PCW, and you see - The bartender's mouth drops open and he just cuts right in, in the middle of Reeco's explanation. Bartender: You're a wrestler?! Holy shit, man! I love wrestling! You say you're in.. PCW? Reeco Suave: Uh - well, yeah. Thats right. Bartender: Damn skippy! Ain't that some shit?! I got a professional wrestler in my bar! Er.. I'm sorry. I cut in, this is your time.. continue. Reeco Suave: Well, you see.. I just joined PCW about week ago. And, I have not actively wrestled in just a little under a year. I am under tremendous pressure to do good at my debut on Tuesday against some Charlie Loc guy.. and to add to that, I have god damn weirdos aggravating my pressure.. and just simply pissing me off!! I can't stand it! Reeco grabs the sides of his head, and pulls at his hair as he finally releases all the pent up anger and rage he had saved the past few days. Reeco Suave: I have god damn fat hicks with buck teeth going psycho at a gas station yesterday.. a few minutes ago, some weird-ass escaped convict was looking at me.. strangely. And just before that, I was.. sexually harrassed by a homosexual truck driver!! Bartender: ... Reeco Suave: So you tell me.. WHY THE HELL SHOULDN'T I BE PISSED OFF?! Bartender: Well..- Reeco Suave: No.. I know what this is. I tried to deny it before.. I tried to find other explanations. But there are none. Everything that has happened to me in the past few days.. its all your fault!! Reeco leans in closer and grabs the bartender by the shirt collar! Meanwhile, with his other hand.. he grabs his beer bottle and throws it at the wall behind the bartender!! We can see the fear literally roll down the bartender's face in the form of sweat as Reeco has gone off the deep-end. Reeco Suave: YOU! Bartender: W-What have I done?! God damnit! Something always happens.. p-please! Just take the money! Nobody needs to get hurt! Reeco Suave: Oh shut up, you pathetic jackass! I wasn't talking about YOU particularly. I was talking about you.. your people. This entire country! This is all your fault! Bartender: P-please.. I didn't do anything! Its not my fault! Reeco lets go of the bartender's shirt collar as the guy falls back. We begin to see a small puddle form by his feet... Reeco Suave: No.. I've had it. I finally pieced it together. All of this.. Bubba, the escaped convict.. Hoss.. it all adds up! Perfect examples of all that is wrong with this country! We see just about everything that Reeco has been holding in.. he is now more intense than ever, and he has just let out all the anger he had repressed for so long. However, he forces himself to calm down... he looks at the bartender with a quaint smile to him. Reeco Suave: Did you know that this country is the fattest country in the world? Heh.. who would have thought! Well I found that out the hard way! With fat fucks like Hoss.. the gas station jackass I ran into the other day. With Bubba.. who can't even keep his own god damn hands to himself. People don't even know the meaning of the word 'NO!' anymore! Everything that is wrong about this country can be summed up by the recent events in my life! Bartender: P-pleaseee.. just leave! We don't want any trouble! Oh, shit.. I want my mommy. Reeco Suave: There definitely needs to be a change around here.. - oh, yes. With that, Reeco gets out of the stool and turns around. He pushes through all of the other people in the bar, who were all watching what went down. As we look back at the bartender who has completely pissed himself, we hear the sound of Reeco's '72 Camaro starting up.. In a time of change, for Reeco.. we see him go completely off the edge of common sense. He has taken all that he had become since his 'Ideal 4' days, and multiplied it tenfold in tonights happenings.
