The Show That Never Made It To Air Part 2

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 19th, 2002 (The scene opens as Mr. Rogers sits at his chair. He is wearing his classic red sweater. He takes a drink of something inside a paper bag. He tries to take off his sweater but the production people stop him.) Mr. Rogers: What do you mean I can't take off my sweater? ITS HOT! *KNOCK* *KNOCK* *KNOCK* Mr. Rogers: Ah crap, it's probably that perve Mr. McFeely. Let him in. (The door is opened. Mike Frampton enters. Mr. Rogers cowers.) Mr. Rogers: Keep that man away. He tried to steal my peanuts. Not the ones I carry in this bag. (Mr. Rogers lifts a bag.) Mr. Rogers: The one I carry in this one. (Mr. Rogers grabs his groin.) Mike Frampton: Hold on. I was just trying to take your peanuts. Mr. Rogers: No SH*T! Mike Frampton: No your other peanuts. Mr. Rogers: Why on earth are you here? (Mr. Rogers takes a drink of his booze.) Mike Frampton: Damn it Mr. Rogers stop drinking! Mr. Rogers: I need it. (Mr. Rogers takes a large swig.) Mike Frampton: Why are you doing this to yourself? Mr. Rogers: Well... (Mr. Rogers takes a swig of his booze.) Mr. Rogers: It all started back when... (The camera pulls a flashback motif.) *Flashback* Big Daveylicious: Listen, I am warning you no songs. (Suddenly, a piano song of “It’s Just A Good Feeling.” plays. Big D holds his ears in rage. Big D walks right over to Mr. Rogers.) Mr. Rogers:(happy, singing) It's such a good, feeling To know you're alive It's such a Happy feeling You're growing inside And when you wake up, ready to say: 'I think I'll make a snappy new day It's such a good, feeling A very good feeling A feeling you know That, I'll be back When the day is new An' I'll have more, ideas For you. And you'll have things you'll want to talk about. I will too. (During the song, he takes his sneakers off again and slips back into his loafers. Then he stands up, takes off his sweater, and hangs it up in the closet. As he talks, he puts the suit jacket back on, keeping the leis on. And then Big D punch‘s him in the head. Rogers falls down and cries.) Mr. Rogers: (Crying in pain) Why did you do that?!? Big Daveylicious: I always make each day a special day. You know how? Each day I get a match my opponent always gets something special! A Daveylicious a$$ kickin’. (Mr. Extravaganza picks up Rogers and drags him over to the miniature town. Big D picks up Rogers so he can stand. Big D then kicks him in the gut and then gives him a Daveylicious Bomb!)*End Flashback* Mr. Rogers: Big Daveylicious took advantage of me! (Mike wipes away a tear.) Mike Frampton: How could he? Mr. Rogers: I never knew a human could be so cruel. Mike Frampton: Don't give up all hope because of it. (Mr. Rogers tries to take a drink. Suddenly his drink is knocked out of his hands.) Mike Frampton and Mr. Rogers: MR. MCFEELY! Mr. McFeely: That's right! And I have a special delivery for you all! (Mr. McFeely pulls a two shotguns from his bag.) Mike Frampton: What the heck? Mr. McFeely: It's time to take down Big Daveylicious! Mr. Rogers: MR. MCFEELY! Put that thing away this instant. Guns are meant to hurt! *BANG* (A piece of the wall is blown out.) Mr. Rogers: Be careful! *BANG*Mike Frampton: Sorry. *BANG* Mr. McFeely: Whoops! *BANG* Mike Frampton: Me again! (Mr. Rogers takes the guns.) Mr. Rogers: Stop that. *BANG* (Mr. Rogers takes away Mr. McFeely's second gun.) Mr. Rogers: Mike will take down Davey the same way he always does. DOES! Mr. McFeely: Hows that? Mike Frampton: A kick to the nuts? Mr. Rogers: No, no, no, no! Old fashioned blood and sw... *BANG* Mike Frampton: Me again. Mr. Rogers: Once you guys stop with the gun play we will continue our talk. *BANG* (Fade to black.)
