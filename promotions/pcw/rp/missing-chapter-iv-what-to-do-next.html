Missing: Chapter IV: What to do next?

undated

Date: TODAY I had my bag with wrestling gear in my hand as I walked out of the arena, scene of Friday Night Fusion to my limousine. I almost got lost in the mix of black cars but I eventually found mine and retreated back to my hotel for a break. I had my plan for the next couple of days. Tomorrow I would head out to Germany to the place where this Fredrick Thomas A.K.A. Fred Mystique, A.K.A. Ivan Chistov, my new enemy would be. He was hiding as a priest in a smal town in Germany and I needed to get back the fou million dollars that he had stole. You don't know what I'm talking about? Let me get you caught up. The Hare Krishna back in USA had told me about one of their former members, Ivan Chistov, who had stolen over four million dollars from the Krishna vault. The Hare Krishna needed the money back and I was determined to give them every penny. So, beginning another mission after I had vowed never to do under cover work again, I had set out to find Ivan. I used a database program I had that could give me information on anybody in the world. The program had certainly helped me out and gave me a small lead to work with. Using the program, I had first typed in the obvious name, Ivan Chistov. The database file on Chistov didn't have very much information, except that he is armed and had an alias, Fred Mystique. The part where is said that he could be armed was a no brainer so I had ruled that out. (duh) My only other option was to type in Fred Mystique and see what I could find. Luckily, I had found some information. Fred Mystique was an alias used by Ivan Chistov in one of his con-artist plans. He had disguised himself as a fortune teller, telling phony fortunes to anybody willing to pay cash. Mystique's business was soon found by the police and was abruptly closed. In this, Ivan only came out of the deal with a couple hundred dollars. The funny thing about Ivan being released from jail though, was that he was released on a five thousand dollar bond. The money was paid for by an unknown accomplice, Henry Claval. Since the fortune telling scam, Fred Mystique's name has yet to show up again. My guess is, Ivan dropped the name to have a clean slate after he was caught. Either way, Ivan was a free man. Having another name in Henry Claval, I entered his name into the database and finally found the information I needed. The database stated that Henry is a master mind at the game of con-artist. Henry's location has been unknown for a few years and only a few selected number of people actually know Henry at all. Below that information, it said that Henry's top 'student' was that of Fredrick Thomas, a name that wascertainly new to me. With yet another name that I was introduced to, I entered Thomas' name into the database and was certainly confused. The records showed that Fredrick Thomas was a man who had a background, but not one that made sense. Fredrick Thomas was trained by Henry Claval, just like the other record showed. This record had some more information though. It said that Fredrick Thomas was working for Claval a few years back but he was framed by Claval in a money-making scheme. Since then, Thomas was known to have gone back to Germany. In Germany, Thomas supposedly changed his ways and became a priest at a local German church. The funny thing about the biography was that under the past aliases column, it showed that Fredrick Thomas and Ivan Chistov were one person. This caught a blow in my plan. How could Fredrick Thomas be a local priest in Germany, when Ivan Chistov had just stolen four million dollars from the Hare Krishna? It didn't make sense and the database records on my computer, were recently just added and changed. There HAD to be two different people in this plan because Ivan couldn't be in two places at once, could he? I had went over all the information and records of all the names I had entered over and over again and there was no other lead that could help me. There wasn't any names that I had missed and no hidden information. So that brought me to here. I was going to head out to Germany tomorrow and goto the church, finding out if Fredrick Thomas actually is there. If he is, I can ask him questions and maybe gets money back. If not, I'm not to sure what I'll do. I'll think of something when the times comes but for now, I just got to think positive.
