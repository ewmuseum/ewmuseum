Transition Into Madness: Episode 1; Chapter 1

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11, 2002 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) Man, it’s like a shooting gallery. Andy Murray, Kramer Red Wing and Jack Valentine; three of PCW’s bush-league fluke signees. Thinking of this upcoming Friday gives me a nostalgic feeling in a way. Sort of like back in the day playing Nintendo…..watching those ducks burst up from the bottom of the screen, pulling up that light gun and sending them spiraling back to Earth. I feel no shame in admitting my defeat last week; Chris bailed, thus leaving me a trifle unprepared for the task of fighting two men the likes of Suburban Uprising. I’ll give the Devil his due and admit Christopher and Teddy fought well for a pair of uptown Gen-X babies. Miracles do occur. And Powtay’s beloved (and mentally impaired) sons should bow to their knees and thank God for His divine intervention.Alas, the trio of Red Wing, Valentine and Murray won’t be so lucky. They can thank those Uprising buffoons for creating the resolve now instilled in my black heart. And they shall collectively bear the cross for the sins of those insipid Knight twins. Lightning doesn’t strike twice in the same place, and it goes without saying this unfortunate trio in my crosshairs need to pray for a phenomenon like that if they plan on coming out victorious Friday night. Word has spread among the publications and e-zines that these latest set-backs to White Mexican and Suburban Uprising are anticipated as being the catalysts in finally putting my…..ah, how’d they put it?..... ‘titan-sized ego in check’. HAHAHA!! Damn, it’s a pity those fools put all their hopes in a retired-slash-overrated border-jumper and a tandem of ‘flavor of the week’ Civil Disobedience clones. And all in vain at that. Well I’ve said it about a million f*cking times now so there’s no harm in reiterating my point yet again: I’ve made a career of discrediting my detractors and force-feeding my critics heaping spoonfuls of crow. Politically out-played by Tyler Nelson’s now exiled ex-nemesis, and beaten two-on-one by the court jesters of PCW. Unfortunately for those ‘insightful’ cynics who hound me at every obstacle I face, the meeting at Fusion plays more to my strength. A four-way dance against three obscure personalities with careers that are, accomplishment-wise, murky at best. But enough on that. Business will be taken care of. I finally finished the important parts of Randall’s little manuscript. Gave me the willies. A lot of it I recognized as mythology; Sumerian specifically, I know that from a little of it I’ve read about in the past. But a lot of that mad gibberish can only be described as abstract philosophy and foreboding and malevolent prophecy. All sorts of crap about ‘death cults’ started for the purpose of worshipping those wretched demigods my Catholic pals seem to fear so immensely. Until I see one of these ‘blasphemous monstrosities’ for myself I’m taking it all hogwash. I said before I go as far as believing in telepaths, and ones that dislike my in-ring work and opted to screw with my head in retaliation I can even consent to believing. But omnipotent gods from behind space and time using my mind as a toy is where I draw the line. It’s f*cking ludicrous. Maybe when this ‘Cthulhu’ character rises from his crypt in the Pacific I’ll believe. I did find some eerie correlations elsewhere; explicitly in the packaged clippings and profiles of Randall’s little occultist crew. I didn’t know what he was getting at when he handed me that folder, but to say the connection was a bit weird after I finished is a massive understatement. Allow me to transpose a sample of what was entailed in this stuff: (Segmented from the ~Tacoma Daily Tribune~, August 13 of 2000 edition) A small-scale tanker owned by the international Exxon Corporation, the Red Banshee, went down early yesterday evening about 3.5 miles off the coast amidst a driving storm that caused towering waves and winds in excess of 60 knots. Nearly every member of the crew was rescued by the Coast Guard shortly after the vessel’s going down, though nothing of the ship itself was salvaged. Unfortunately three crewmen reportedly lost their lives before escaping the submerging vessel. Further details are sketchy, but it is believed the boat crashed upon a cluster of submerged rocks just below the surface. None of those aboard could be contacted for testimony about the incident at this point. Yeah, it sounds normal enough. But then I compared it to the report paperclipped to it. A joke? I’m baffled and still trying to piece it together: (Taken from a report authored by Marcus D. Stryker; dated August 16, 2000) To the Members of the Blackhelm Coalition, As the vast majority of the committee and the Coalition in general are aware, myself and the team I command executed what was originally a covert operation the other night against the minions of the wicked Sea-Devil himself, Dagon; the result was a double-edged sword in that our timing was simultaneously excellent and poor. As stated in previous reports, we have suspected the existence of a tribe of Sea-Shoggoths for quite some time but just recently confirmed our suspicions following a ‘recon’ operation under the guise of a fishing vessel. Our equipment verified the placement of a deep-sea contingent residing beneath the surface, just about three and a half miles from the Washington state coast. We pulled our operation back to the coast and planned a night-time ‘assault’ using armed submersibles with the ‘star-stones’ as safeguard onboard, as well as any of the primordial relics we could muster up from the Coalition arsenal. After obtaining the necessary materials, we launched our miniature fleet and returned to the strike point. By a stroke of luck one could call either beneficial or detrimental, depending on individual perspective, our operation coincided with the passing of an Exxon-owned tanker referred to as the Red Banshee. Upon reaching the coordinates of our destination, we spotted the tanker anchored over the sprawling aquatic metropolis constructed on the sea floor below. It was dark at the time, but we could clearly make out chaos aboard the deck. We pulled up alongside and sent the bulk of our force onto the deck. Again, our notions were proven and, in fact, the sailors were being held at bay in the locked engine room by a pair of Sea-Shoggoths. On deck, three sailors had been killed by the initial attack of the rampaging sea minions before the crew’s collective escape to their current stronghold within the ship. We destroyed all three of the beasts with relative ease, losing none of our division in the impending battle. After clearing the remains of their decomposing bodies from the deck (as all members of the Coalition recall, the decomposition process of the creatures is swift to an unearthly degree), and according to Coalition procedure, we incapacitated each of the delirious seamen and took them aboard our own ship just in time before the Red Banshee descended beneath the waves. From reports I have gathered on the incident, the Blackhelm Coalition psychics are using their abilities to effectively remove recollection of the event from the memories of all the individual sailors aboard the Exxon vessel. At this time I wish to apologize for the failure of the mission; the colony remains undisturbed as we lacked sufficient time to destroy the beasts, much of this due to those we now harbored aboard our own ship. Another operation is being planned however within the next few weeks, and at that time we plan on cleansing the area of the colony. Sincerely, Marcus Donovan Stryker Must I go into detail on this one? If this was not a joke then I must admit my entire view of mortal life has effectively been shattered. But then I’ve always been the sort to need concrete evidence before I’ll believe anything as outlandish as this. When I see one of these deities or their underlings I’ll change my mind. Until then, Randall and Reggie can feed me all that they want. This has been weighing heavily on my mind all day. I’ve decided to forego any further research into the topic until Friday night concludes; I opted to eradicate the peons the booking committee has tossed in my path as a distraction from this lunacy plaguing my life in recent weeks. Taking apart Wild Wing, Valentine and Murray ought to be good therapy…..not to mention the little upcoming Junkyard scuffle at Intervention. What a week for the God of Extreme, huh? For now I’ve decided to retire for the night. With some luck the dreams won’t come but that’s hard to foretell considering how erratic in regularity they have been lately. I’ll do what I usually do before big matches and say a few prayers in favor of my opponents. I just hope they’ve repented their sins and given their souls over to their Christ. Because either way, come Friday, they belong to me. * * * A shower of sparks rains from a swaying string of broken wire dangling overhead. Jack blinks rapidly several times before climbing groggily from his seat with help of the outside seats armrest. He felt a warm trickle of blood streaming from his forehead, and pressing his trembling hand to his forehead confirmed his suspicion. He rubbed his palm on the cushion in front of him, leaving a ragged streak of blood and with a groan dragged his sore form to an upright position in the aisle. A swift breeze caught his attention. “What the f*ck?....” he mumbled nearly inaudibly, glancing with glazed eyes at the wreaked interior of the bus he stood within. His eyes darted right, for the first time noticing the blood-matted hair of a female passenger hunched forward. He placed a hand gently under the woman’s chin and tilted her head back, but quickly pulled back at the sight of her dead eyes and the blood splattered across her pallid face. He rolled his head from side to side, scanning the silent interior for movement or life, met only with the grisly sight of others sprawled across the seats and many partly into the aisle, obviously victims of a similar fate. His gaze drifted to the wheel, noticing the driver’s limp carcass splayed out across the dashboard and hood of the bus, large shards of the shattered windshield acting as a primitive mat. A disembodied cackle echoed from the outside, dropping Jack into a defensive stance as if to protect himself from its source. He cast his eyes outside, the exterior darkness shrouding the origin of the phantom laughter. VJ scowled, ducking beneath the crackling wire overhead and moving through the aisle while avoiding those corpses slumped partially in his way. He gave the bus door a firm kick and cautiously crept out into the darkness, leaving it swinging loosely behind him on creaky hinges. His eyes widen, visage falling an ashen white as he collapses to his knees in disbelief. His surroundings, bathed in the shadow of midnight darkness, do little to hinder his view of that immediately surrounding him. Buildings crumbling with shattered windows and some leaning like the infamous structure in Pisa, concrete torn up by undoubtedly some inexplicably powerful force. There were even some bodies strewn about the sidewalks and leaning over railings and open window-sills, their escape forbidden by whatever malicious presence had evidently attacked from within their homes. “This place is…..familiar…..” pondered Jack as he gawked at the barren landscape. “I’ve been here…..but where?....” He cocked his head and elevated his eyes eastward, gaze falling on a multi-level tower with concrete steps rising from the cobblestone street running parallel at its base. The metallic sign above was cracked and partially collapsed, yet he could distinguish enough of the damaged notice to identify its origin. Primetime Central Offices “How f*ckin’ convenient,” mumbled VJ before crossing the street and dashing up the steps. Upon reaching the revolving door at the entrance, he heard a sharp crack in the background from where he had come. Pivoting around quickly, he squinted his eyes in the general direction the sound had derived from. “Yo, who’s there?!” he shouted brusquely. Again, dead silence. VJ tightened his lips together, turning back and giving a firm press against the revolving doors. At first they appeared jammed and refused to budge, but with a hard kick he successfully freed the lodged entrance and proceeded to make his way into the main lobby; he had only been in this tower twice, firstly as a ‘field trip’ for a survey on Anton the Kid for the Global tournament with the second and final time his contract signing with Primetime Championship Wrestling. The interior proved to be in complete disarray; the floor, couches and desktops were coated in a thin layer of white soot giving the hall an ashen complexion. Worse yet, corpses littered the ground, some already in early phases of decay and others stiffened in opening stages of rigor mortis. Some lay mangled and twisted in unnatural contours, yet others appeared to have no visible marks. VJ recognized none of the victims scattered in the lobby; if any were indeed wrestlers, they were employed by affiliates of the PTC empire as he had not seen any in the locker rooms of PCW nor OSW. Receptionists donned in their work-skirts slouched over the front of their desks, a small squad of security guards scattered about the room in their uniform blues, many with guns drawn from their empty holsters but laying several feet away. VJ crouched at the side of one of the down guards, plucking up their 9mm and checking the chamber in hopes it was loaded. “Thank God,” he said aloud, glancing around semi-nervously at his silent surroundings. “Got one of those damn feelings this may come in handy…..” (To Be Continued in Chapter 2)
