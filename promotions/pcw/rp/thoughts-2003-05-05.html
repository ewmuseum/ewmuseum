Date: 05/05/03 For a moment just forget about everything you know, what you think is right and wrong, what is good or bad and just listen to my words. Take the champions of this life, It matters not what profession they excel in, being a champion shows that you are the best, there are competitions for anything these days, gurning, belching the Guinness book of records even holds a spot for most semen pumped from a woman’s stomach. Disgusting but true. If we take these champions and convert them into animals they would all be at the top of the food chain, the lions and tigers where as those who were simply the pace makers would be at the bottom of the food chain, the gazelle who runs for as long as he can until he gives up waiting to be mauled and torn limb from limb this was the feeling that James was getting about Tuesday night. Skylar Green a man who had held four titles within PCW the cruiserweight title twice, the extreme title and the continental title. Titu Xansiv…the Pharaoh one-step down from Skylar winning three titles in his time the tag team titles, the television title and the cruiserweight title. Both men were now competing in the upper echelons of PCW headed towards World title battles. These men would be the kings of the animal kingdom, no one would dare oppose them not even en masse then you had James Edwards A man with the history in PCW less than that of seven days, a man whose single bout so far was a loss. If James were an animal he would be a rat or a cockroach, an animal that is small and tries to stay out of the way, someone who up until the end will look for a way out until there is no other option but to fight, however a fight that will not always be won. If you put a cockroach in a cage with a lion, the roach could hide but eventually it would be found, the lion could scratch away at the roaches carapace and the roach would not budge taking the damage waiting, just waiting for it’s chance to strike which might never come. Why then had a lion chosen a cockroach to back it up? I couldn’t help but feel all of the pressure of the world on me, if we lost on Tuesday not only would it damage my career, but also Sky’s he claimed to have seen talent in me, a form of potential that just needed to be tapped but when I looked into my heart all I saw was a man who wanted to achieve his dream and wrestling wasn’t it. It was a buzz there was no doubt about that the crowd, the cheering and to some extent the pain but it didn’t compare to how I felt about the possibilities of space. Not by a long shot. Whilst I hadn’t had the opportunity to leave the atmosphere, to feel the raw energy of the rockets thrusting me out into the great beyond, I’d gone through the whole training process I knew what the pressures were like, to feel your face feeling as though it was going to cave in due to the gravitational forces being implemented on it. I knew what it was like for the entire body to weigh less than a feather, to float around almost lifelessly without a care in the world, without a thought in your mind. People had often asked me whether I feel any ill repute towards my sister for stealing my dream, literally taking it out of my hands when it was down to just the two of us. I always thought I would be angry not at her but at myself the amount of times I’ve heard that stupid fucking question made me sick to my stomach, of course I didn’t hate my sister, she was my world and my all. Anyone who is shallow enough to believe that hate can be spawned over a dream truly has not felt the true intense bond that I share with Emily. One day I know I will fulfil my dream and it will be Emily’s turn to stand proud at the launch site. My dream had been put to one side for now in place of my life, a new life, which I had to grasp and take control of. Tuesday I wouldn’t just be fighting for myself but for Skylar Green as well. He had made a judgement call in choosing me as his partner, I was not about to let him or myself down. A win at Turmoil would put me in good stead for Fusion when I was to be teaming with two other men…men I knew little about against another three who once again I knew nothing about. But that is Friday. An entire world away from Tuesday. I couldn’t think too far ahead in this as if I did I would lose focus…losing focus would be the worst thing I could do.
