Where Everybody Knows your name

7 May 2003

Date: 07/05/03 As the camera fades up we see Manuel walking on cracked and broken streets. Washington always had the shittiest luck. It is the nations capitol but they cant afford to cough up the gold so the citizens could walk a straight line without having to escape the perils of potholes and manhole covers exploding. This town was a giant vat of steaming shit, and yet Manuel loved the city and the country as a whole. Sure it is cracked and broken but all of that gives it character, and most importantly charm. A certain geun ne ce pa. When he looked into a hole he could see people from the past bassing the bill of rights or amendments flying about the steps of congress. This place was a extension of Manuel. It was so much like him it could have been joined to his hip. He used to hate thinking about one place as home but now, now he is no longer a janitor, now he is a PCW wrestler. Now he is somebody. An old saying came to his mind; 'you can take the boy out of the city but you can't take the city out of the boy'...Usually Manuel hated sentimentality like that but right now it felt right. 'Can you spare some change sonny?' Quickly Manuel turned away forcing himself to swallow in order to hold back the vomit that was pushing its way up his throat. Spare change? Sonny? If he had more time he might help this man, cause he knew that without his big break that could have been him in two years time. He was a man on a mission today though, so this little man would have to find his own luck. How much luck? Manuel would never know. He kept walking, his feet felt a little sore, and his right shoulder was still giving him trouble, but he felt content. Even the cut on his forehead, now covered in a thick scab, felt fine. He had been worried for a while, waking up last Wednesday had not been the most pleasant of experiences but at least he had beaten El Diablo.::~ He pulled his jacket closed. It was windy today, but he pressed on. Every ounce of his being wanted to turn around, walk back to the car, and drive back home. But those would be the actions of a coward, he refused to let himself become one. He looked up ahead and saw the familiar sight. He smiled, he hadn't been here for years. He checked out the area, saw the lone smoker outside, saw the car on the other side with the driver resting on the hood. Still using the same old tricks. The progress started again, he walked right up to the old pub, not fearing any of the shady characters keeping close watch on him. This place held many memories, the good ones far outweighing the bad ones. He can still remember bringing some friends here for the first time, they turned their noses up when they saw the cruddy floor and the somewhat weird looking bartender, but by the end of the night they had fallen in love with the place much as he had. He unzipped his jacked as he opened the door, the smell of smoke and beer immediatlly permiating his nose, pushing into his nostrils and forcing the atmosphere down his throat...He loved it
