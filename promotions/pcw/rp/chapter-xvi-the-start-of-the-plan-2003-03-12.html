Date: 12-3-03 ::Introduction:: The competition is heating up with only three days to March Madness. It seems that more of the competitors right now want it more than others. Mark wants this as bad as possible as you can see his work rate has gone up and it just seems that this would complete his journey in the wrestling business. So gear up the competition because the Madness is about to start. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XVI: The Start Of The Plan… Mark wakes up again in the back seat of the taxi cab. This time with street lights shining in his face. The sky outside is pitch black with the half moon shining like a half glowing basketball. The blurriness is not over Mark’s eyes this time, it just seems that he hasn’t been sleeping that long. The road signs in Spanish have lights under them so that you can read them. The palm trees are swaying in the light warm wind that has come across the city at night. “Where are we spic?” Mark asks as he sets up in the backseat. “We are just outside of Mexico City.” Pedro says as he is still having his eyes on the road. “How far is that from the border?” Mark asks as he is calm and cool sitting in the back seat. “Its quite a ways, really don’t know the mileage.” Pedro says as he looks down at the clock. “We are down in the central plateau and all you can do is go north to find the border.” “Could you pull over real quick, I have to take a piss.” Mark says as he starts to hold his bladder like a little kid. Pedro nods his head as he pulls over on the gravel of the side of the road. The car comes to a halt as the gravel under the tires kicks up a bit to the side of the road, into the grass. Mark takes the door handle and he pulls it open. The car door opens up and almost hits the side of the car as the stopper in the door has been rusted off. Mark hops out of the car and onto the gravel stones. His boots hits the loose gravel which set him off balance a little bit. “I will be right back spic.” Mark says as he takes off jogging to the woods on the side. Mark jogs over to a patch of woods that has all sorts of kinds of trees in it. Evergreens and palm trees are the dominant trees in the woods. But who cares about that? The dew on the grass is spraying up behind Mark as his boots hits the blades of grass. Pedro is watching Mark in the dark as Mark heads under the street light and then disappears. “What in the fuck?” Pedro questions himself as he rubs his eyes. “I swear I am tripping or something because he just disappeared right out of the dark. I need to get some sleep or something, all this driving has messed up my vision.” Pedro leans his chair back into the resting position as he is going to wait for Mark and catch some sleep in the meantime. Mark is jumping over logs and tree stumps as he starts to get farther and farther into the back of the woods. Mark’s body is starting to tingle because of the sweat that is building up in his pores, but is not coming out in a liquid form yet. Mark stops himself as he jumps over another tree stump. He puts his hands on his knees panting as he bows his head right in front of a tree. “I have got to piss before I move on.” Mark says to himself as he goes up to the tree. Mark takes his piss on the tree. He zips his pants up and he fastens the black belt. He starts to head back to where the road is. He is looking on the ground but really can’t see anything that is there. He digs into his front pocket as he pulls out his flip cell phone. He presses some of the keys on the phone as it brings up the neon green back light. Mark kneels down and he puts the phone next to the ground. He searches around for a big enough stick as he finds one. He takes his cell phone and he puts it in his front pocket again as the back light shuts off. “This should do the job.” Mark says as he starts to walk towards the road. Mark starts a light jog as he wants to get back to the car in a hurry to get this job done. Mark can see the street lights as he is in the shallow part of the woods. Pedro is still in the car with his eyes shut, as he is licking his lips. He is fantasizing of his fifteen-year old cousin. “Ah yes Juanita.” Pedro says as the crotch of his pants start to rise up. “That’s the spot, just stay on top.” Mark comes out of the woods as he appears again under the street light. The evil look on his face can spell out what is in store right now. Mark walks up to the car as the front of his boots look moist from the dew on the grass. There isn’t a sound heard by Mark at all, just those of the passing cars on the road. There are not many this early in the morning while the moon is still out. Mark walks up to the car and he goes around the trunk and over to the driver’s door. The window is halfway put down as it was a cool night out. Mark leans into the door as he pushes on the power button to let the window down some more. “Don’t stop Juanita!” Pedro screams in his sleep as his pants are not up too much than they usually are. Mark is startled a bit as his hand goes back as the widow just needs to be down a bit more, but it will do for Mark. Mark takes the stick and he grips it like a baseball bat. But he takes the end and he sticks it right in the throat of Pedro. Pedro wakes up from his sleep being startled by the stick. “You damn spics just don’t learn.” Mark says as he starts to get a smile across his face. “You should never stop along a dark interstate where no one is around. You fucking dumb ass spic. This is why spics shouldn’t be given jobs, because they are this stupid.” Mark takes the stick out of the throat of Pedro as his face was turning red. Pedro’s face then falls to the passenger’s seat as he is coughing. Mark then opens up the driver’s door and he drops the stick. He takes Pedro’s bottom of his pants and he drags him out of the car by them. Pedro’s face hits the ground as Mark pulls him out quickly. Mark drags Pedro across the pavement road and takes him around the trunk and to the passenger’s side. Mark then goes over and picks up his big stick as he comes around to see Pedro lying there holding his face. “You wanted my respect?” Mark asks as he grips the stick like a baseball bat again. “Well dammit you are not going to receive it like no other spic is going to.” Mark takes a swing at the ribs of Pedro and he connects. That makes Pedro turn over on his back so that his whole stomach area is showing. Mark spreads his legs out and he takes a shot to the testicles. Pedro moans in pain as he grabs his testicles. He drags him across the gravel, as his shirt is getting torn up from all of the rocks. He sets Pedro up in the middle of the interstate as he starts to walk back to the car. Mark opens up the driver’s door as he bows his head so he doesn’t hit it. “If he lives, lets hope this teaches him not to mess with the whites.” Mark says as he puts his seat belt on and closes up the door. Mark starts up the car as he presses on the brake and he puts the car into drive. Mark then lets his foot slide from the brake to the gas and the car takes off. Its gaining speed as it goes down the interstate and Pedro’s body is coming up in the middle of the road. Mark pushes the gas even harder as he feels the car bump up off of the body of Pedro. Mark looks back and he laughs as he gets a smile on his face. The body back there looks as it if is broken into two pieces with blood running out of the middle into the road. “Yippie Ki Yay!” Mark says as he sticks his head out of the window into the cool air of the night.
