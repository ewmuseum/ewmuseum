Listening to advice is BAD!!!

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/March/02 'I don't believe this! Jesus Christ! I don't need this sh!t!' The scene opens on D-Kay Calyx pacing around the front room of his home in Chicago. He is dressed in a pair of Fila jogging pants, and a grey t-shirt that is soaked in sweat. Sat on one of the leather seats in the lounge is a face familiar to PCW and PCW2 history. Dressed in a pair of baggy pants, a black tp-2.com t-shirt, a pair of tan Timberland boots, and more platinum jewellery than you can shake a stick at; this man is undoubtedly D-Kay's former tag team partner, Duzza. Calyx continues to pace as Duzza looks around the room, not quite knowing what to say. 'Yo man. I told ya that they ain’t worth it. Chill man.' D-Kay stops and looks at Duzza. 'Chill? CHILL? How the hell can I chill? I have Boda, Mexi and Walden at each other's throats. I have a package to be delivered to St. Louis by LAST SUNDAY, and it STILL isn't there! And on top of that, I received THIS earlier today.' D-Kay stops by the cabinet at the side of him, and grabs an envelope off of the top, throwing it to Duzza. After opening it and reading it, Duzza stops dead, his mouth open, not knowing how to respond. 'She done that to ya man?' D-Kay nods his head, and slumps down in the armchair opposite Duzza. 'She did that. Went to England to find out who killed her brother, and then decides to stay there with a new man. Leaving me in Chicago to pay the mortgage on this thing on my own. There's me, worried sick about her, and she goes and tells me to f*ck off basically. Talk about having everything dropped on you at once.' Duzza re-reads the letter, and slides it back into the envelope, handing it back to D-Kay. They both sit in silence for a few seconds. 'Yo, who was it that said, 'The greatest trick the devil ever pulled was convincing the world he didn't exist'?' D-Kay looks at Duzza, and shrugs his shoulders. 'I don't know Duzza. Aphrodite used it on Dub Moods, but I don't know who originally said it. Why?' 'Cos that sh!t fits right wit' this. The devil don't exist, but still manages to f*ck wit' ya.' Duzza looks at D-Kay, who simply nods once. 'I mean. I kind of guessed, that after this time, she might stay in England, but to tell me she has a new man, and to do it by letter. It's low.' 'Women. Can't live wit' 'em. And you can't f*ck without 'em.' Duzza's comment forces a slight smile from D-Kay. 'Only you could think of that Duzza.' 'It's true though man. Think 'bout it. How many time's she treated you like sh!t?' 'To be honest Duzza, none. She was always good to me, and me to her.' 'Well in that case, I feel sorry for ya man. Ya lost a good one if that's the case. But, ya can't let it get ya down man. Ya gotta get back out there, and rock da sh!t.' 'What do you mean?' Duzza sighs and rolls his eyes as D-Kay looks puzzled. 'It means D. We get you back out this weekend. You got a gig ain't ya?' D-Kay nods his head in response. 'Well than my nigga. We get's our asses out to a club, you get your groove on behind the decks, then we get our groove on wit' the honey's. Ya dig?' D-Kay looks slightly confused again, but replies anyway. 'I think so man. You mean, we go out, get drunk, get laid?' 'For sho!' 'You do know I'm wrestling on Sunday?' 'Yeah I know! Chill man. We going out on Friday. That way, ya can take the day you'll need to get over this sh!t!' D-Kay looks a little less convinced. 'Hey man. Since when did I steer ya wrong?' 'Dare I start?' D-Kay lets out a slight smile at his own joke, as Duzza shakes his head. 'Man, I only ever got Red Rock into trouble. Never you.' 'No? What about that time when you decided to drive us up to a biker bar, and start a brawl?' 'You drove that day D! Sure, I started the fight, and we kicked those cracka's asses!' D-Kay shakes his head again. Duzza smiles, if acting a little hurt at the same time. Duzza suddenly gets up and leaves the room, returning a few seconds later with two bottles of Pepsi Max. 'Yo. Why d'ya only have Pepsi Max in here? Get some proper Coke man!' D-Kay laughs at Duzza again. 'You know Duzza. Who needs therapists or anti-depressants? We can just laugh at you.' 'HEY!' D-Kay bursts into laughter again as Duzza tries his best to look hurt. 'Seriously Duzza. If you're dragging me out on Friday, it has to be after Fusion. If Boda, Mexi and Walden are going to pull sh!t like this, then I better stop Red Rock and Boolie dropping themselves in it.' 'Red can look after himself, dawg. He's old enough, daft enough, and stupid enough. Let him.' 'True. But knowing him, he'll manage to drop himself in something. He always does. If those three want to implode like a time bomb, let them. They want to take Wild Deuces with them, let them. But I won't let them destroy me going for gold. And if that means saving Red Rock and going for tag gold, then so be it.' 'You mean ya gonna let 'em blow up?' 'Yeah. I can see where each one of them is coming from, but I'm not being caught in the middle.' 'You'll have to see what happens man. If those cracka's want to beat each other down, then let 'em.' D-Kay shrugs his shoulders, and downs his drink in one go. 'I don't know Duzza. Loyalty and all that.' 'Yo man. Loyalty should be to tha hood. Ya know, the black man stick together. I told you ya was wrong for joining wit' dem cracka's, and I still think I'm right. But I understand that sh!t 'bout loyalty. I hear ya man.' 'Yeah. So I have to make sure Red doesn't get them p*ssed even more.' 'See what happens on Fusion man. GO out Friday, have a laugh, have a ho. Saturday ya can train, and Sunday, kick ass again.' D-Kay nods his head, surprisingly agreeing to Duzza. 'Sure man. Sunday, Brock Newbludd get's his ass kicked. Time to let loose some aggression.' 'That's it man. Drop that cracka. Take no sh!t.' D-Kay nods his head again, and stands up, taking his t-shirt on, and dropping it in the laundry basket as he walks out of the room, Duzza close behind him. D-Kay grabs another t-shirt from the banister, and pulls it on as he walks out of the house. Duzza exits just after him, and D-Kay reaches back and locks the door, heading down the path. 'Where we goin' man?' 'Well. If I'm DJ'ing on Friday, I need to be prepared for Brock before hand. Let Mexi and Boda worry about Walden. I have Newbludd to face Sunday, and Red Rock to keep away from Wild Deuces on Fusion. Come on then Duzza. We can run into town, and get some new tunes for my set on Friday night.' With that, D-Kay takes off into a run, Duzza shaking his head and running after him. ------------- -Voice Over- So...D-Kay listening to Duzza's advice? What is the world coming to?
