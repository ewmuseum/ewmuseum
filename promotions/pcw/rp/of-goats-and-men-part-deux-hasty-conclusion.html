Of Goats and Men Part Deux: Hasty Conclusion

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-02 Previously, Downey was taken in by a couple of hicks who promised him a visit to a world famous goat. However, it was later revealed the two hicks were none other than evil spies sent to secrete the moron gland and turn the whole world into morons... It was beautiful. The white was so bright, yet strangely enough it didn’t even burn the eyes. “Peaceful” couldn’t even begin to describe the supremely serene feeling he felt. It was like he was outside of himself, watching as he stood still. Downey had seen this place before, he just couldn’t place where. A ride at Disneyland per chance? Oh, maybe it was that time at Uncle Phil’s house when he rode the pig. Heh...that was cool. The pig was all like “get off me dude” and Dustin was all like “make me pig” and...well...trust me it was sweet. Suddenly, like a cry from a distant shore the memory came back to him. He saw himself in a PCW ring, he saw Clyde and fire. Fire. All of a sudden it wasn’t so beautiful anymore. He saw himself running down a long corridor, where the end was never and the entrance was lost. He cried out for help, but none came. A searing pain shot through his head. It was gone. The white, the peace, the fear. In its stead was black. He felt groggy and alone. At length he decided to give speaking a try. Dustin: Hello, is anyone there? Voice: Yeah, over here. Dustin: Over where? Voice: Right in front of you moron. Dustin lets out a blood curdling scream. Voice: What in Danielle Steele’s name is the matter with you boy? Dustin: I’m blind! I can’t see! What have I done to deserve this cruel punishment? It was the time I beat up the girl scout wasn’t it? Well she totally deserved it.... Wavy lines overtake the screen signaling a flashback. We’re treated to a shot of Dustin sitting in his apartment in his underwear and watching the New Family Feud. He is laughing heartily at Louie Anderson’s amazing comedic styling. Then the unthinkable occurs. The doorbell rings. Dustin annoyed gets up and answers the door. He looks down to see a little girl scout. Dustin: This better be good kid. I’m missing Louie Anderson. I swear that guy kills me. I’ll bet he could eat a whole bus full of gravy. My hero! Girl Scout: I’m selling girl scout cookies! Dustin: That’s good kid. Listen I gotta run. Commercials are almost over. Goodbye kiddo. The girl scout looks up at Dustin and blinks innocently. Dustin: Fine, I’ll buy some of your stupid gay ass cookies. Got any Thin Mints? The girl scans her list, looks at Downey, then back at the list. Girl Scout: Nope sir. I just sold the last one. Dustin: (yelling) YOU STUPID BASTARD! You had the nerve to come to my house and sell me something that WASN’T Thin Mints! I should kick your ass. Dustin lunges at the girl scout, who quickly steps away. Dustin proceeds to get up and chase the girl around the yard. The girl flings her list up in the air and runs off screaming. Wavy lines bring us back to the darkness where Downey sits rubbing his chin. Dustin: Those where the days...wait a minute...something’s not right here. Voice: You were going on about how blind you were. Dustin: Thanks for reminding me. Where was I....(under breath) Cheesy flashback, check. Crying out to God, check. Whining, check. I’m missing something. Voice: Screaming “Oh the humanity.” Dustin: Oh yeah! That’s what it was. (screaming) Oh the humanity! Voice: Maybe I have a suggestion that’ll bring your eyesight back. Try opening your eyes. Dustin opens his eyes. Suddenly he can see again. What he sees is darkness with little holes of light shining through. They appear to be in the back of a large trailer, which is apparently rambling on down the highway. Dustin lets this soak in before turning to the man with a look of awe in his eye. The man is short, fat, and bald. Dustin rubs his eyes... Dustin: You’re.....you’re JESUS! You healed me! I thought I met Jesus once but it turned out to be this dude that lived in a box outside of my building. He mostly just pissed on the sidewalk but he was cool as hell. Voice: I’m not Jesus. My name is Todd. Dustin: So Jesus’ name is really Todd....trippy... Todd: No you’re not getting it...I’m not Jesus....never mind. Look, do you know where you are? Dustin: Yes as a matter of fact. This is that Pirates of the Caribbean ride, correct? Todd: (rolls eyes) Something like that. Where you’re at is the back of a large semi headed toward Canada. You were kidnapped by two evil spies. I’m Special Agent Todd Jeffries from the U.S. something or another. I’m here to help stop these evil men and there evil plan, but I’m going to need your help. Sound good? Dustin: Well normally I’m all about foiling evil plans, but I kinda have stuff to do. See, I’m a wrestler and I got a match against Reeco Suave on Turmoil that I can’t miss. It’s an easy win. Aside from that I got Juan Borro on Sabotage. Juan is actually a worthy competitor for me Todd. Juan once held the prize that’s avoided me my whole PCW career. Todd: A date with Brandy? Dustin: Why would I want to date Brandy? I’ve already touched her boobies. I’m talking about the Cruiserweight Title. I was born to hold that belt, however I still can’t do it so what makes you think I can help you thwart these evil fellas? Todd: Well Dustin, simply put, you’re a moron. These guys are out to take the gland that makes you such a moron, duplicate it and turn the whole world dumb. Dustin: They could also take the gland out of Reeco Suave and turn the whole world jobber. Or maybe they could take a gland out of Juan Borro and turn the whole world painfully boring! Or what about taking a gland from Snow and turn the world into best friend stealing jackasses. Todd: I’m sure they could if they wanted to, however they only want to turn the world dumb. Somehow making the world jobbers doesn’t appeal to the evil type. Look, are you in are not? Dustin:Sure...why the hell not. Not like I’ve got anything better to do...unless... Todd: No I will not give you a piggy back ride. Dustin: Damn. The scene shifts to the front cab of the truck. There we see Denny and Bill, however they are both wearing swank tuxedoes instead of the hick clothes we previously saw them in. Denny is driving. Bill: You know Denny, just when I think we can’t be eviler, you do something like this. Denny: Yes, I know. Driving an 18-Wheeler without a class C license. How fiendishly delightful. The two men laugh evilly. The scene then shifts to show an outside shot of the truck turning into a large warehouse. Armed guards surround the place. We are then back inside the trailer with Downey and Todd. Downey: ...and that’s how I saved Jimmy Carter with a curling iron, two ducks and a clay pigeon. Impressive huh? Todd: Wow. Silence, I think we’re here. Don’t say a word. The back slides open and two men with guns hop in the back. Todd gets up and judo chops them both. The scene shifts back to the outside of the truck where Todd and Downey, dressed as the armed guards hop out of the back of the truck. Todd: You just stick close to me and we’ll be alright, got it bub? No answer. Todd: I said do you got it? Still no answer. Todd turns around to see Dustin playing with a yo-yo. Dustin is giggling gleefully to himself. Todd snatches the yo-yo out of Downey’s hand. Todd: Where in the name of Yukon Cornelius’ hairy ass did you get a yo-yo? Dustin shrugs. Dustin: It’s more entertaining than a Juan Borro promo I can tell you that much. I get a shot to re-prove myself in PCW against someone like Juan. He’s a top contender to the Cruiserweight Title and if I can just beat him then a shot at the gold will be all but mine. I can taste it now! (Downey starts licking his lips) Mmmm....gold.... Todd: Dude, you’re drooling. Can we talk about your career AFTER we kill the evil terrorists? Dustin: But I was just gonna tell you about how much I hated Snow! Todd: I’ll give you one more tangent, but that’s it for this promo so use it wisely. Dustin: Yay! Anyway, after beating Juan I’ll be one step closer to my ultimate goal. The Cruiserweight Title. This title was held by the likes of Kid Wonder, Miyagawa, Chris Chase, and let us not forget Sean Matthews! Not only will beating Juan move me one step closer to the belt, it’ll give me some of that credibility stuff I hear so much about. Juan is a multi-time champion, so a victory over him would make me just as good as a champion. The only thing is I actually like Juan so destroying him isn’t gonna be as fun as what I’m gonna do to Reeco Suave on Turmoil. I don’t think I wanna waste anymore breath on Reeco or Juan. We can carry on with our mission now. Todd smiles. The two men walk around the front of the truck, Dustin mimicking every move Todd makes. Todd turns around to Dustin. Todd: It’s imperative that you be very very quiet or you’ll get us.... Suddenly a frog hops out from under the truck. This excites Dustin very much... Dustin: Holy F. Murray Abraham! It’s a FROG! Todd look at the frog. I’ll bet it’s all slimy and stuff. Todd smacks his forehead as a large group of armed guards comes running around a corner. They all point there guns at Todd and Dustin, however this doesn’t seem to phase Dustin who is coaxing the frog into coming his way. Armed Guard #1: Drop your weapons now intruders! Armed Guard #2: Dude, you sound so gay when you say it that way. Armed Guard #1: That’s the way they taught me to say it in Evil Armed Guard school dude. Armed Guard #2: Yeah, but why not just say ‘drop your weapons?’ That other stuff sounds so gay. Armed Guard #1: You’re calling me gay? This from the man who’s sole goal in life is to be a Diva on VH1’s Diva concert? While the two men are arguing, Dustin and Todd make a run for it. The guards immediately start shooting at the two men. Dustin ducks inside a building and Todd follows. Todd: We’re finally safe. Just then a light turns on, revealing an operating table, with several sharp looking painful operating instruments. Standing behind Todd is Denny and tying Dustin’s hands is none other than Bill. Cue hasty resolution... Denny: Now that you are subdued we’ll begin the operation! The lights go down and a bunch of trippy strobe light effects start to occur. Todd is taken away to another room, never to be seen again. Dustin is tied down to the gurney while Bill sharpens a large knife. Bill: You were a fool to think you could thwart our evil scheme! Dustin: I trusted you guys and you do this to me? I even let Denny borrow my Scooby Doo underpants, and Bill, remember that weekend we spent down in Galveston? Denny: Your feeble attempts at playing on our emotions are futile. Begin operations Billy boy! Dustin: But what about Sparkles? Bill: Who? Dustin The goat you guys were supposed to take me to go see! Denny: We just made that up because we know of your undeniable affliction toward goats! Bill is all set to insert the knife into Dustin when all of a sudden the whirring of jet engines can be heard in the distance. Bill and Denny both look up at the wall of the warehouse and a miniature WWI fighter plane comes barreling in. A few shots are fired, knocking the instrument out of Bill‘s hand. Bill: What the hell is that?!? Denny: It’s...it’s...it’s... Dustin: SPARKLES! The most decorated Goat in WWI! I knew you’d save me! The goat smiles at Dustin and gives what can only be the goats version of the thumbs up sign. Goat’s don’t have thumbs you say? This is an RP, anything is possible . The goat then drops a net over Bill and Denny. He lands the plane and hops out. He unties Dustin from the gurney. Sparkles: We'll let the police worry about these two. Dustin: You saved my life! I must now become your Wookie slave for life. Sparkles: There really is no reason for that kid. You just go and do your thing. You’ll walk in there and beat Reeco Sauve, then Juan Borro, and eventually capture the Cruiserweight Title. I’m usually pretty good about these things. Dustin: What are you gonna do now Sparkles? Sparkles: Fly into town, get all liquored up and pick up a cheap whore, what else?b With that, Sparkles takes off into the distant sunset. Dustin stands behind waving madly at him. Will Dustin defeat Suave and Borro? Will I ever improve my writing skills? Will Bert ever follow Ernie’s suit and come out of the closet? I dunno....with that the scene fades to a dancing donkey.... FIN
