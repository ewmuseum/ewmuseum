Extreme Shopping

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th May 2002 For the first time in some time we are not filming inside the Asylum Gym. Ok, I know they don’t get out much because of the costs of outside broadcasts, but hey they finally have gotten out, so don’t blame the narrator, kapiche? We join Natalia and Alexander Miyagawa in what appears to be one of these massive clothing superstores with everyone under one roof and everything under fifteen bucks. You know the ones that only foreign nationals and poor people shop in. Unfortunately, as previously mentioned, Natalia is Russian. She likes these stores. Natalia is also Alexander Miyagawa’s wife. And Miyagawa doesn’t like to get an earful of abuse when they go to shops he likes, so he goes to shops she likes to weigh out the balance. Life is all about balance. It’s also all about letting woman do what they want first, otherwise they tend to complain. A lot. A whole heck of a lot. An immense amount that you wouldn’t believe. Don’t worry, you’ll work this out when you get married, just like our dear intrepid hero has been befallen himself to right now this second. The duo step inside the doors, and they immediately sense that the place has no air conditioning, and what with it being in Anaheim and stuff, it’s warm in here. Pretty damned warm. Miyagawa strides forward, currently still confident that they only came in for the blouse that Natalia saw when he was out of town earlier in the week. Did I mention it was warm? Miyagawa: Do we need a basket? Natalia: I shouldn’t think so. Notice how the male of the species is being led into a false security that the female only wants one thing. What he doesn’t expect is this... the fact that she may only want that one thing... but she’s sure as hell going to look at everything in the shop anyway. Of course deep down the male of the species already knows this; after all it is what the female did last time. But unfortunately the male is completely optimistic. Notice how the female is carrying his child. The optimism carries over into this as well, you see he is under the presumption that she won’t turn into a sweaty wreck, and also that he won’t have to change any nappies. But he will, and on a daily basis. Natalia wanders over to a rack of clothing and starts looking through it. Although not a single item would fit her in her currently pregnant state. Miyagawa wanders over for a look also. He takes one look at them and then says the line that every male should know that they should never EVER say to a woman. Miyagawa: Aren’t those a little small for you? The reason being is simple; you see the female of the species isn’t insulted with that remark. However, she will use it as an excuse to purchase three to five times the amount of products she was going to previously. It is more commonly known as a “guilt trip”. Natalia looks up at him in dire shock, or in her case, completely fake dire shock. Natalia: You think I’m fat? Miyagawa’s eyes go wide. You are quite correct in his assumption that he just realised what he has let himself and his credit card in for. Miyagawa: Nonononono, I just mean that well... your pregnant. Notice the stall in his words in that sentence. Notice how the female’s ears prick up as she hears that and immediately takes upon a life of her own. Natalia: You think I’m fat... Miyagawa: No dearest, I don’t think your fat; in fact if it wasn’t for the unborn child, then you would still have your wonderfully slim figure. Natalia: So you do think I’m fat then! At this point in the relationship, the male of the species realises that there is no single way to get out of this, apart from simply take the abuse. At this point in the relationship however, Miyagawa takes a completely wild step that no man has previously done. He is about to say what no man has said before. Miyagawa: Ok, but only a little. You might presume death would shortly follow, as the female eats the male, but remember that the female was previously pretending to be enraged, and is now honestly shocked. At this point the male tries to say something to calm the situation. Miyagawa: But I still love you. Unlikely as it sounds, it works to an effect as the female of the species simply makes a strange “hmpth” noise with her mouth at her disappointment and continues shopping as before. The male of the species relaxes thinking that perhaps he has gotten away with it, but unfortunately in twenty years he will be reminded of this occasion where he called her fat in a public place while he talks to his friends with his two point four children at his side. The duo return to their previous thing where Natalia looks at things that are no where close to fitting her and Miyagawa stays completely silent in fear of upsetting her some more and that having a retro-effect on him involving a pair of industrial scissors and the middle of the night.
