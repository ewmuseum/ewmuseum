Can They Fix It? Yes, They Can!...Hopefully

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-06-02 'You know, I really could've done without this. Things were going so well...so very...well...somewhat shitty actually. But this is just the icing on the steaming pile of rancid pig vomit. How could something like this possibly happen to us? We were trained for this and we spent hours upon hours preparing ourselves for one simple operation.' (The voice stops and sighs deeply.) 'And now this...' (A black screen dissipates to show the inside of a large factory. The sounds of welding, clanging hammers, and electric drills echo throughout the dank, dingy building. Hundreds of mechanical parts, steel beams, and crucial machine parts are moved around in a hurry and many mechanics in yellow jumpsuits bounce around from station to station with their many tools. We finally come across the object of the voice's disappointment--a badly damaged Soviet-era destroyer. And in front of it, 2-time PCW World Heavyweight Champion and Argentine presidential candidate, Frisco. Our dictator shakes his head at the injured ship and stares blankly at a gaping hole in its hull.) Frisco: 'I had visions of this baby transporting our troops to storm the beaches of Brighton and Bournemouth...and now look at her!' (Frisco huffs and looks around at his half a dozen subjects.) Frisco: 'What the fuck is wrong with you people!? Can't you see that I am in deep emotional distress here? And not one of you will approach me?' (The stooges all look at eachother and quickly try to decide who will go console their heartbroken dictator.) Frisco: 'Don't you know I'm a human being!? I have feelings too! I grieve just like any other man who has lost a child. Just look at her! Look at what those donkey felching assbags did to my baby! Please, any one of you!....I just need comfort at this difficult time.' (Frisco's lip quivers as he looks on at his reluctant party members. Finally, one is pushed forward--Eduardo Perajol, PPA's Vice-Consul of Miami. Perajol glares back at his peers before he tries to figure out a way to approach his grieving boss.) Frisco: 'Señor Perajol, vas a consuelame?' Vice-Consul Perajol: 'Si, Señor Presidente...' (The short, balding Argentinean looks up as he stretches his arms out in preparation of a hug.) Frisco: 'Gracias...' (Frisco rears back his right hand and lays it forcefully into the Vice-Consul's gut.) Vice-Consul Perajol: 'UMPH!' The party member's lungs empty with a wheezing grunt before he drops to the floor. Frisco jumps up in glee and kisses his knuckles as his men look on in horror.) Frisco: 'Ohhhhhhhhaaaaaaaaa!' (Frisco kneels down to pat his winded employee's back.) Frisco: 'Thank you again, Señor Perajol. I feel better already!' (The cruel dictator snorts at his still wheezing stooge.) Frisco: 'Well!? Aren't you happy that I'm happy? Huh!? You greedy little fuck! I thank you for comforting me, and all you can think about is trying to get air back in your lungs. Why don't you say, 'You're welcome, my Lord. It was my pleasure to comfort you in your time of need. Thank you for letting me do so.'? That's what any other classy Argentinean would do. What disgraceful behavior. I hope nobody here tries to learn from your pathetic example.' (Frisco shakes his head in disgust as his victim coughs and wheezes and then looks up at the rest of his men to scold them.) Frisco: 'Such impoliteness and self-serving nonsense you people emit. Haven't you braindead fucktards learned anything from me!? Not one of you has picked up my beautiful traits of honesty, kindness, generosity, and self-sacrifice. That's why I'm going to be President of Argentina and you shitwits are still going to be polishing my boots and your wives will be polishing my knob.' (Frisco yanks up his victim and shoves him over to the rest of his stooges.) Frisco: 'Now, I'm going to converse with the mechanic and see what can be done about my ship's ouchies. You gentlemen stay here and discuss amongst yourself what valuable lessons I have taught you today and how you can best use them in the future.' (Frisco nods to his stooges and heads toward his ship. As Frisco walks away, the party members all give their dictator dirty looks and not very nice gestures. The man who had the wind knocked out of him a few minutes ago grumbles as his boss departs.) Vice-Consul Parajol: 'Pinche cabron. Lo odio!' (The rest of the group joins in the insubordination.) Politician #1: 'Puto!.' Politician #2: 'Pendejo!' Politician #3: 'Estupido asole! I want keel eem so moch!' (From 10 yards away, Frisco turns around to look at his loyal politicians. They quickly smile at him and simultaneously collapse their middle fingers and turn their thumbs up as if it's been practiced many times. Frisco picks and eyebrow up in suspicion, but keeps on toward the mechanics around his ship.) Politician #4: 'I steel hate de preek.' Politician #2: 'Si.' Vice-Consul Parajol: 'I always hate eem.' (One of the oldest of the bunch--an 85 year old Guatemalan representative grabs his crotch as he scowls at the mean, young whippersnapper.) Politician #5: 'Ehhhhh! Deekhead!' (Meanwhile, away from the unknown displeasures, Frisco approaches one of the mechanics as he wipes his right hand onto his metallic red FRISCACEMANI~! suit.) Frisco: 'Ahem...' (The mechanic accepts the extraordinary customer's hand as Frisco continues to speak.) Frisco: 'As you should know, I am Francisco Valerius, Commander-in-Chief of the military forces of El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina and I am the Admiral of this fair ship. If you read on the report, my beloved ship was brutally attacked by the United States Coast Guard for no reason.' Head Mechanic: 'Uh yeah...I saw that. Doesn't sound like something the Coast Guard would do.' Frisco: 'You've been lied to, my friend. The Coast Guard is a rogue naval force with nothing but criminals in their ranks. Simple boredom is enough for them to attack a peaceful ship like ours.' (The mechanic scratches his head.) Head Mechanic: 'Does that mean you don't want the 20 inch guns? Because the ones you've got on there now are pretty worn out. I'd say they've been used a few thous--...' Frisco: 'Yes, yes. But purely for self defense reasons. I don't want any incidents like this happening again. It's unfortunate that friendly gentlemen like us must stock up on such barbaric weaponry, but it is a necessity in these times. What with all the unwarranted attacks by the Coast Guard and all.' Head Mechanic: 'Uhh, right.' (The side of Frisco's face glows in a blue flame as a few men weld parts onto the ship. Frisco bites his lip as he watches his baby being burned by blowtorches.) Frisco: 'That's not going to hurt her, is it?' (The mechanic smirks.) Head Mechanic: 'I assure you, she is in good hands. We've done plenty of wo--...' (A huge crash rings through the garage as a piece of the ship's railing falls off.) Welder: 'Fuck!' Mechanic #1: 'Stupid ass! I told you not to move it!' (Frisco gasps.) Head Mechanic: *Laughing nervously* 'Well, we've never actually worked on a Soviet destroyer before, but we have worked on...similar types of vessels before.' (Frisco smacks his forehead.) Head Mechanic: 'Your ship will be ready by the end of the week if all goes well...' Frisco: 'Well, that's reassur--...' Welder: 'Ah! No, moron! Put that...no!' *CLANK!* (Frisco looks back at his ship and the dolts who work on it.) Mechanic #1: 'Hey, eat a dick! That's how it goes!' Mechanic #2: 'Shut up, Johnny, you don't know nothin'!' Mechanic #1: 'Like this...watch!' Welder: 'It's gonna fall off, douchebag!' *CRASH!* (Frisco's jaw drops.) Frisco: 'Eh?' (The petrified Admiral points at the bumbling mechanics.) Frisco: 'Wha?' Head Mechanic: 'Ehehehe! I assure you, it's just a little miscommunication. That happens in our field quite a bit. Everything will be fine and your ship will be back on the ocean in no time and ready to wreak havoc on wherever you plan to take her...' (Frisco rubs his eyes and shakes his head.) Frisco: 'England.' (The mechanic looks away from his idiot employees and back to Frisco.) Head Mechanic: 'What?' Frisco: 'I said England, shithead. I'm going to take it for Argentina and enslave its people. But first we are planning a few amphibious landings along the beaches of Florida and California. And if we have time, we'll sweep through the Mediterranean, take a few more countries, including worthless Egypt since somebody I don't like is from there and I want to piss him off a little.' *CLANK! CLANK! CRASH!* Mechanic #2: 'Now look what you done!' Mechanic #1: 'I didn't do that! You did!' Mechanic #3: 'No, I did it. Sorry.' Mechanic #1: 'Dumbass!' (Frisco cringes.) Frisco: 'Umm...' Head Mechanic: 'Look, I better go over there. Good luck with that stuff...' (The head mechanic turns around and heads toward his employees with a cold stare and a loud, course voice.) Head Mechanic: 'What the hell are you idiots doing! We have a good customer with a lot of money paying us to fix this boat! Stop screwin' around!' (Frisco sighs and heads back to the loving embrace of his party members.) Frisco: 'Gentlemen...' (The stooges put on their happy faces for the angry dictator who stomps toward them.) Frisco: 'Once my ship is fixed up, I would like you--my loyal friends to take the first reentry...without me. With all the yelling you put up from me, I think you deserve an unsupervised cruise.' (Frisco smiles warmly and his politicians perk up at the uncharacteristic gesture.) Frisco: 'Shall we...' (Frisco escorts his men out as he looks back to see a welder's head stuck through a railing and another mechanic picking his nose. El Presidente shakes his head at the ignorance as he exits the garage.)
