The State of the Onion Address

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens at a Cafeteria in the domestic terminal at the airport.Flaymin looks at his watch and realizes that his flight isn’t for another half hour. He walks over to the Cafeteria and sits down at a table and waits for the waitress. In the meantime, he picks up the menu and browses through it searching for any delicacies that suit his fancy] Waitress: Can I help you sir? [The waitress, a young woman with a lovely smile winks at Flaymin and brushes the hair out of her face] Flaymin: Uh, I’ll have a burger with extra cheese and onions, thanks. Waitress: No problem.Any drinks? Flaymin: Uh…. I really shouldn’t be drinking so I’ll just have two jugs of beer. [The waitress looks at Flaymin in surprise but jots down the order in her notepad anyway. She walks away to fetch the order while Flaymin remains seated and taps his fingers on the table. A few minutes pass before the waitress returns with two jugs of beer and a burger. She puts them on the table and smiles at Flaymin] Waitress: There you go sir. Flaymin: Thanks, love. [Flaymin picks up the first jug of beer and begins to chug it down as the customers around him gasp in horror. Flaymin then puts the jug back on the table and lets out a loud burp] Flaymin: *Buuuuuuuuuurrrp* [Flaymin then picks up his burger and takes a bite into it. Instantly his eyes pop wide open and he pulls the burger out of his mouth. He takes off the top bun from the burger and looks inside in an attempt to inspect its contents. He pushes the pickles out of the way and places them on the table. He swishes away the ketchup and mustard and wipes it off on his shirt. He takes off the lettuce and throws it on the floor. Flaymin then pulls off the two slices of cheese and squishes them up before tossing them over his shoulder in the direction of an elderly couple. Flaymin holds up the remains of the burger and inspects it closely. Angered, he then slams it on the table and raises his arm to gain the waitresses attention.Speaking in a stern voice he calls over the waitress] Flaymin: Hey woman, get your ass over here! [The waitress walks over to Flaymin. She looks on the floor and sees the lettuce.She also notices the pickles on the table and the ketchup smeared all over Flaymin’s clothes] Waitress: Maybe we should have gotten you a kid’s meal?Or a bib….. Flaymin: Don’t give me that crap. Do you remember what kind of burger I asked for? [The Waitress flips through her notepad] Waitress: Sure, it says here that you ordered two jugs of beer and a burger with extra cheese and onions. Flaymin: Precisely. Now tell me where the hell all my onions are? [The waitress peers over at the remnants of Flaymin’s burger] Waitress: You’ve got three pieces of onion right there. Flaymin: Three??You’re telling me that as a valued customer I’m only entitled to three freakin’ pieces of onion?!?!? Waitress: Valued customer?I only met you five minutes ago….. [Flaymin bangs his fists on the table] Flaymin: THAT’S NOT THE POINT!The point is I asked for extra onions! And all the onions I have here wouldn’t feed a family of flies! Waitress: Extra onions will cost you more. Flaymin: *gasp*More? You mean the $1.50 I so graciously paid doesn’t even count?? [The waitress sigh’s and then continues to reluctantly address Flaymin’s problem] Waitress: Look, do you want extra onions or not? Flaymin: Yes. [Flaymin picks up the jug of beer and begins to drink it down] Waitress: It’ll be a dollar extra. [Flaymin swallows on the beer and suddenly spits it all out. The beer comes flooding and sprays all over the table and waitress’s apron. Flaymin wipes his watering eyes and tries to speak over all his coughing and spluttering] Flaymin: MORE MONEY?? That’s the problem with society today- EVERYONE WANTS MORE MONEY! You park a car in a handicap spot- THE POLICE WANT SOME MONEY FOR IT! You get a lapdance at the strip club- THE SLUT WANTS MORE MONEY! You walk into a 7-11 to pick up some milk- THE DUDE AT THE CHECKOUT WANTS MONEY! You run over someone in a tractor- THE VICTIM WANTS EVEN MORE MONEY FOR IT! WELL THAT’S IT- I’M OFFICIALLY MONEY FREE…………. MONEY SHALL NOT RULE MY LIFE FROM THIS DAY FORWARD!! [Flaymin gets out of his chair and storms of in an angry mood. Meanwhile, a couple with their teenage son sitting nearby watch all the action unfold.The father nudges the son on the arm and speaks to him in a fatherly manner] Father: See son, that’s exactly why you should stay away from all those drugs…. [Scene fades to black]
