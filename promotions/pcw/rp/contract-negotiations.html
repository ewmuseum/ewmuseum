Date: not entered He didn’t care much about this brawl… It was nothing but a vent for him…something to allow him the opportunity to get his hands on everyone in PCW at one time…and give them all a taste of why he is this place’s noble redeemer… He knew the people feared him…he could see it in their eyes…he could fear it in their hearts…he could hear their souls shriek in terror. Spout out all the talk you can…try real hard to claim that you don’t fear Akiyama…keep telling yourself he’s not some god…convince yourself with all your might that he’s just as human as the rest of you… That’s it…deny what you know deep down inside of you…reject the feeling of helplessness that you all know you have, yet continue to ignore. None of you are any more original than the other. Mortal after mortal we come across…it’s the same thing…the same words over and over… “I don’t buy into this god bullsh*t.” “He’s just some delusional d*ckhead who thinks he’s better than everyone.” “If Akiyama is a god, then I’m the f*ckin master of the universe.” “I don’t believe in god…so I don’t believe in Akiyama.” Most of the deniers…most of those who take charge to blaspheme the godhood of the Neo Void God…they are shown their Absolute Destiny Apocalypse up close and personal…and their time on this earth grow shorter and shorter as the days add up after their encounter with the god. Akiyama’s torn through people like they were children…because compared to the eternal entity that the Neo Void God is, they are children…small…weak…meager…meek…pitiful creatures that are still learning of their lives, while the god has lived out thousands. PCW is filled with children…who think they can frolic through the mall and play tag with each other…eliminating each other until only one of them stands alone. Well children…the entire lot of you will be eliminated…as the grown-ups take their place at the top of the league. The Celestials… The chosen… The superior… The V I C T O R S I know what you’re all thinking… I hear it all the time. Humans are all the same when it comes to competition. Zealous thoughts and ideals…selfishness and greed…all of you thinking the same thing, repeating it to yourself like self-hypnosis. Trying to reassure yourselves of the same lie… “I’m going to win.” Everyone wants to win…there’s no doubt about it. Winning makes you feel good…winning makes you feel special…like you’re on top of the world. Winning gives you a reason to think yourself better than the other people around you. Winning…is a drug to you people… You’re all addicts…terrible addicts. The funny thing about you all…is that you go about the wrong ways to getting your fix. You cheat, you do ruthless things, and you do all you can be it good or bad to make sure you come out on top. But no matter what you do…you will fail each time…because there is only one way to get your filthy victory fix. Give yourself to the god…serve him with your soul…your heart…your mind…your body… …and everything you could ever desire will be yours…only if you are willing to do as he say. It’s as simple as that. And I know how you mortals like simple things…to fit your simple minds…and your simple desires. That’s what the motivation for success is all about, really… Trying to make your lives…simple. Simple…by being successful…by being rich and powerful enough to pamper yourselves…a comfort masturbation. Everyone wants the gold…gold has been a symbol of success for centuries. Even to this day, men are pushed by their ancestral instincts to own gold. Instincts … I think of them as predictability. Predictability equals weakness in my divine eyes. Do it more than once, and the god will punish with twice the force. And with the efforts that all the children have been making in their efforts to win the Continental title… Akiyama is going to have to quadruple the pain dealt out. =©= I hated white walls…they were so boring…so bland…they reminded me of BladeD, the efforts he’s made in this federation…empty walls. I’m sure twenty-five dollars could be put to good use in decorating these lifeless walls…or at least give the resident jobber something worthwhile to do. Taking my eyes away from the snow-white perfection surrounding us, I glared across the polished black table at the mortals before us. Sitting on the left side of the table was the Prime Minister of Japan, an old Japanese stuffed shirt dressed in a black suit with black-rimmed spectacles and thinning gray hair that looked displeased with this whole arrangement. I couldn’t blame him…everyone was displeased with the god…but no one could do a neo void goddamn thing about it. Sit back, enjoy the ride, and shut your b*tchpipe. It’ll all be over soon. On the right side across from the Prime Minister was the government’s watchdog that’s been a thorn in our sides since we got here. Yeah…I mean Japan Special Forces Commander, Ushio Kuonji. The black haired beard-stroking roughneck was dressed in his combat attire, bullet proof Kevlar vest and pistol at his side. He was supposed to be the muscle in this meeting…I take it. Maybe he hasn’t heard the tale about Inphino Blitz and the gun he pulled on me. They had like the entire Japanese Defense Force waiting outside the building for us when we arrived, and a platoon of the Japan Special Forces outside the door, just in case we were going to start Celestial Chaos or something. That would be a waste to do it here when we have an entire mall to do it in. At the head of the table was the Emperor of Japan…his name isn’t important, since neither my father nor myself consider him of any importance. He wasn’t a figure of power…just a figure of Japanese morale to keep the people proud of their heritage. The heritage that threw us away…yeah, something to be real proud of. They were lucky we didn’t put the collective effort into banishing the entire country…they were lucky that putting that much energy into the act would leave us drained for months…and we had better things to do. The worthless old man gave us a polite nod of his head as he folded his hands in front of him on the table. “Thank you all for coming here and meeting with us to talk about this serious situation.” On the other side of the table, the entire Celestial Collective stood in full force. At the head of the table were Akiyama and I, in our usual dress of black mesh and vinyl. On my father’s side sat Anarchy, dressed as well in her black in ice blue attire as mines. On my left sat Sandy Wong, dressed in an open black blouse with a white scoop-neck shirt underneath and tight black slacks and black thin wire glasses. Standing behind the god and I were The End and Shin Yagyuu. Shin was behind me, as I had assigned the Celestial Shogun to assist me in …certain endeavors. End was behind the god, as he and Akiyama has always been a tight unit since the pre-Celestial days with failures like Jay Sylo and Pharaoh. Both of them were in their battle gear, both of them with facemasks and blazing eyes acting as windows to show their Celestial existence. “We figure you’d like us to stop destroying your little country here…” I replied, folding my arms across my chest. “…So to show you that we’re not just monsters, we’re willing to negotiate with you.” He nodded as he looked to the prime minister. He adjusted his glasses before opening a manila folder before him and shuffling through the documents inside. “What we have here is a documented agreement stating that you will be allowed back into the country with full residency for the two of you.” He looked up at me and Akiyama, motioning his pen towards us. “Also on this agreement, we will grant you your own land that will be your own territory, and will not be in the jurisdiction of the Japanese government.” “What will we have to do to complete this agreement?” nothing came for free…even the simplest of mortal knew that. You’d have to give to take…a perfect example of that would be how the god would give punishment to those who crossed him…and afterwards he would take the belt for his own…just because they got in his way because of their meager desire for their ego ecstasy, victory. “All you will have to do is spare Japan from the relentless rampage that you’ve been on since you arrived here weeks ago.” The prime minister didn’t seem to have much to ask for. “Oh…and since you are the Neo Void God and all, we would require that you disband the terrorist cult known as CODE, as between you alone and their fanatical belief in you, the urban areas of Japan have suffered millions of dollars of damage.” Yep…nothing came without a catch. I gave my father a knowing look, which he matched with his own look of perception. Then I turned back to the three Japanese officials with a shrewd look. “We will be taking charge of the Celestial Order of Divine Existence.” “What??” Ushio looked up with surprised anger in his eyes. “What do you have planned for those religion nutjobs??” “Ushio! Calm yourself!” the prime minister snapped, trying to prevent a bloodbath if he were to anger the god. Then he turned to us with a sigh. “What is your reason for taking responsibility of them?” I looked at Sandy Wong. End’s manager nodded before turning to the men across from us with a smile. “You see gentlemen…” she began, her voice sounding quite professional. “The followers of CODE are in fact members of a select religion. In their actions against the country of Japan that you have considered terrorism, they have on the other hand been acting out what they thought were exhibitions of their faith. This was all at the hands of their leader, Shoichi Sato.” She slid an envelope containing his profile towards Ushio. He opened it and glared down at the old man’s picture before scanning over the bio info. “So this Shoichi Sato is responsible for all of the terrorism?” the emperor asked, starting to understand the situation. “Then we request that you turn him in to us for proper prosecution.” Sandy shrugged her shoulders. “I’m afraid we cannot do that.” Ushio stood up from his seat, his fist slamming into the table. “So you’re willing to AID a known TERRORIST?? WHAT KIND OF SUPERIOR BEINGS ARE YOU???” “USHIO!!” The prime minister was growing agitated with calming is subordinate down. I knew Akiyama knew the feeling…good subordinates were hard to find these days, giving his records with Itakagi Freeze, Kenji Hirose, Gaijin, Pharaoh, and Jay Sylo. Kuonji calmed himself before sitting back down and glaring into the folder before him. The prime minister looked at Wong with furrowed brow. “Why can’t you turn him in to us?” A smile slicked across my sister’s face. “He’s dead.” “OH!” the emperor said, taken back by the bluntness of the answer. Even I had to admit, Anarchy said it suddenly, and with an intention to shock them. Nice to know her sister was rubbing off on her. The emperor gained his composure back, “How did he die?” “Slow, with a lot of suffering.” Anarchy looked at me with her wicked look of pleasure growing. I matched her look, a soft chuckle rising up from me. “Now if they all could die like that.” She laughed at my comment as Sandy shook her head with a sigh. “Sato had some disagreements with the ideals of the Neo Void God and the Divine Prophetess…so they decided to terminate his services as a loyal follower.” The prime minister nodded, though his face was most uneasy. “I…see.” Sandy continued with providing them information. “To get to the point, we will be taking this group into our leadership, and forging it into a precise religion. If this is how they wish to live their spiritual lives…then we will not stop them from becoming followers of the Celestial collective. It pleases the god to know that mortals realize their flaws and seek perfection in his might.” The emperor nodded slowly, thinking over the proposal given to him by Wong. Wong moved on, sliding another folder to the prime minister. “This is OUR contract…that we request you to sign, granting CODE all rights and privileges as every other religion and sect in Japan. We have plans for temples, churches, monasteries, nunneries and so forth for our religion.” Ushio was the skeptic of the bunch. “And just what IS your religion?” The entire Celestial clan glared at him after asking us that. He looked down quickly, wishing he had never provoked our eerie eyes upon him. The last thing you wanted was to have yourself in our sights…because if you were in our sights, you didn’t last long. Lot of peoples in our sights this Mall Brawl…but we have enough eyes on the situation. “Everything looks to be in working order here.” The prime minister passed it on to the emperor to read as he turned back to Wong and passed her their contract. “I trust you’ll find everything there to be in order for you.” She took the paper and scanned over it. I leaned over to her, taking a peek at it myself. She whispered to me lowly while they were busy talking amongst themselves. “This seems legit. Are you ready to close this thing out?” She got the short nod from me. “Yeah, we have a lot to do for the days to come. Lot of sinners to strike down…Mandrake, Darkstar, Clyde, B*tch…maybe the rest of PCW too.” Sandy eyed me strangely. “B*tch?” I nodded. “Yeah…B*tch.” Anarchy leaned across the table with a half-pleased half-annoyed look. “She means Teresa Tomas.” “That’s what I said!” I responded, shrugging my shoulders. “I said B*tch!” My sister shook her head as Sandy sat back in her seat, a look saying “Oooo-kay” on her face. I don’t know why they couldn’t hear me when I said B*tch’s name. I mean, some flat-scan hits you with a sledgehammer for no good reason, and you remember their name like the back of your hand. Now if there’s a body to go with the name after I’m through with her…that’s a whore of a different corner. But enough about B*tch… “Okay. We’re ready to put this all behind us. I know you want us out of your faces as much as we want the government off our backs.” Wong handed me a pen, as the two suits across the table took out their own. We all stared at each other, watching the other sign the contracts on the table. After I had signed where my name was typed, I handed the pen to the god for his John Hancock. Once we were all sure that we had signed everything, making it all nice and legal, we ripped off the original copy. They handed us our original copy, while we gave them theirs. It was all settled. “Thank you for negotiating.” The emperor and the prime minister stood to their feet and bowed to us. “Maybe you’re not the demons that legend foretells.” I shook my head. “No…we ARE. It’s just that you were smart enough to make a deal with us instead of fighting us foolishly like the rest of humanity is doing. Consider Japan back under the favor of the Neo Void God for your cooperation.” “Domo arigato gozaimasu” they bowed again before leaving the room. Ushio got up from the table sulking as he walked for the door. Anarchy grinned at the back of him, his shoulders sunken in defeat. “Nice to see you again…commander.” He glared back at us, all of us staring at him menacingly. He clenched his fists before backing out of the room. =©= “It’s that simple. You learn to coexist with the god, and the god doesn’t kill you like babies in the womb.” It felt good to walk the streets for once…without having the police swarming about us shooting and yelling for us to CEASE AND DESIST YOUR RAMPAGE. Yeah, people were staring, pointing, and cowering as we passed by them…but that was normal for us. I mean…between Shin Yagyuu and The End’s eyes, Anarchy and my hair, and the mere presence of the Neo Void God, we stole the show, no matter what. Speaking of stealing the show…Fusion might just be a Celestial show this week…we’ll see… “I can’t believe they did all this just to stop daddy from tearing this trashy dump apart!” Anarchy said with a smile as she held End’s Extreme title. That belt…it was OUR belt…price of him entering the family. He was most worthy…but the sacrifice was needed. I wagged my finger back at her scolding her. “Now, now…this country isn’t that bad. I have to say that Japan is the most bearable place in this mortal realm, when in contrast to the other asshole countries on this rock.” Wong nodded as she walked by End’s side. Shin was on my left; I found he never left my left side. Akiyama walked at the back of the group, his hands folded behind him at the small of his back. It looked like he was thinking about something. Wong stretched her arms out over her head. “Well, I don’t know about you guys, but I think we should go check out this land that the government gave us. I want to see what we can do with it!” “That’s divine,” I agreed with her as she pulled the contract out and looked over it. “You see…mortals have this big affirmation that we’re just monsters…but we can be talked to…and we can be negotiated. Its just that most of the time…there’s no need for words, that’s all.” Mortals can break deals with us…if they have something to barter with… Just ask Xavier Kannon… “Wow…we have a castle!” Wong exclaimed. I nodded in satisfaction… …then I stopped dead in my tracks. “…That wouldn’t be…Jahana Castle…would it?” “Yeah! That’s right! How’d you know?” End sighed. “Does Divine Prophetess ring a bell?” I slowly turned around, finding the pulsing vein on the temple of the Neo Void God to match my own. Sandy went on to read the rest of the contract, but I knew enough to cause me a headache. “Jahana Castle, on Sado Island. HEY! Isn’t that where Inphi—OH DEAR…” =©= The roar of the demon god crashed through the air, amplified on the wings of the wind through the island. I sighed, sitting down open courtyard of the ancient Japanese fortress castle with its towers and fenced walls. The yard was unkempt, and was in need of gardening…I’d get some of the CODE to work on that later. Right now…the phone was ringing. I walked over to the canopied doorway leading inside to one of the many drafty hallways. Next to the door was a small table, where the black cordless phone sat. “Hello?” I said sighing, knowing full well whom it was. “Yes, I KNOW you can hear him all the way over there. This is the third time you called. I think that you would realize that he’s not going to stop…ESPECIALLY for you!” Pause… “Yeah…sure…whatever. You just stay over there and mind your own business. Goodbye.” I hung up the phone, looking up to find Anarchy watching me from across the courtyard on one of the upper balconies. “Who was that?” I sighed again. “Who else? The friendly neighborhood Black Dragon Master asking us to keep the god quiet.” Anarchy leaned against the railing, sighing herself. “Geez, daddy’s been doing that since we got here a few hours ago.” “Yeah I know. Who would think that such a pushover like Inphino Blitz would be such a b*tchy neighbor?” ugh…I could imagine those government dogs laughing it up now over their tea… Yeah…they got one over on the god…but they’d find the folly of their ways… Mankind always pays for their sins…at the hands of the Neo Void God… So says the Divine Prophetess. The ring of the phone brought another white-wisped sigh from the goddess, closing the scene out in darkness.
