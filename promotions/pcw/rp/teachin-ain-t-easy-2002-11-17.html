Teachin' Ain't Easy

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Teachin' Ain't Easy Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 17/11/02 “Wow, you’ve, like, brought me back to school! YAY HIGH SCHOOL!... Can I go and pick on some fat kids? Please?” Our shot opens up in a High School corridor where the 1st couple of PCW, Xavier and Eleanor Kannon-Hall are walking. The Scientologist searches around with his Green eyes, working out where he needs to go while Ellie~! just follows, revelling in her former stomping ground. Xavier : “No, we’re here for one reason and one reason only... To preach the wonders of Scientology to the nation’s future at an influential, and hopefully depressing, time in their lives.” Ellie~!’s eyes roll as if to say “Not again”. Eleanor : “Ohhhhhhhhhhhhh... Okay. That sounds kinda, you know, boring? I’ll meet you by the water fountain in.. Um.... Sometime today!” Before the Scientologist can get a word in to stop her, the little prep skips off leaving Kannon shaking his head as he searches for the class he is scheduled to lecture on the wonder of Hubbard to. Xavier : “Dear Hubbard, your 1962 works were easier to navigate than this Xenu-awful Peon factory!” His eyes keep searching around for navigation points as we cut to Ellie~! who’s revelling in High School reminiscence while waltzing down the corridor, a broad grin on her face. “Where’s Mr Frost?” “Who cares, no teacher, no lesson...” The little prep stops in her steps as she hears voices from the adjacent classroom, who’s door is slightly ajar. Eleanor : “Whoa....” Her pale Blue eyes light up. Eleanor : “I could, like, TOTALLY teach stuff.... And stuff! “Miss Ellie~!” like, SO has a ring to it!” Turning to the class room and checking her hair is okay, she walks into the class room as all the heads turn to her, girls sneering and guy’s tongues hanging out. She just stands there smiling and waves. Eleanor : “Like.... Hi!” Still smiling she skips over to her desk as the class’s eyes are all on her, the little prep not really dressed as you’d expect for a teacher in faded Blue tight embroidered jeans and a strappy Pink top.... .... Not that most of the males in the room care. “Hi to you too, ‘Miss’ I presume?” One of the amorous young men winks at her while licking his lips, Ellie~! looking back at him and still smiling while one of his friends pat him on the back and urge him on. Eleanor : “Thankzzies! That’s really cute in a ‘My Boyfriend can break your neck you creepy, horny little weirdo’ way!” Shot down by the Commissioner, the boy retreats as far into his seat as he can, the males in the room making their noises in his direction to rub it in while Ellie~! winks back at him, recalling all the times she got to mock people at High School. Eleanor : “Anyway, I’m, like, Miss Ellie~! and I’m going to teach this lesson and stuff!” Her eyes then look around the room for any hint at what she might actually be teaching... Drawing a blank. Eleanor : “But first, we’re SO starting off with a quick test.... YAY TESTS!!!“ As the little prep hops on the spot and applauds herself, the student body lets out a collective groan, their hopes of an easy lesson from the stand-in teacher dashed... However, this is Ellie~! Eleanor : “Okay.. The question is..... What lesson is this meant to be?!” The whole room looks confused, exchanging stares with one another and wondering whether it was a trick question or not.... Before one voice nervously offers an answer. “Er..... Trigonometry, Miss.” Ellie~! ‘Ahhhhhhhhhhh’s, now knowing what she has to teach as she points to the guy who gave her the answer. Eleanor : “You can, like, SO go to the top of the class! Now the rest of you can all mock him for being a nerd!” She grins as insults are whispered in the poor kid’s direction, opening the teacher’s course book on the desk and looking through it as if it was Ancient Latin... Or any other language she doesn’t understand. Undeterred however, the little prep soldiers on, trying to make it sound like she knows what she is doing, reading the first page under her breath. Eleanor : “Sin.... Tan.... Cos... Cos? Like the lettuce and stuff? Wow, this doesn’t look to hard, the important words are, like, SO three letters long!” She slaps the book shut and looks up at her students, a confident look crossing her pale and lightly freckled face. Eleanor : “Okay, can anybody tell me what’s the best thing to do with Cos?” Not quite understanding the question, the class look back at her blankly... Some having an answer in their head but not sure if it’s the answer that was wanted. “Er.... In a Triangle you can use it to wo-” Before he can get the answer out, the preppy Trig teacher cut him off. Eleanor : “Triangles? What are you, like, talking about? Did I get the retard group or something?” Her Bambi-esque eyes roll. Eleanor : “How many of your homes have wheels?” Only 2 raise their hands. Eleanor : “Hmmmmm, not enough to be the div kid group.... No, like, RIGHT answers? Nobody?” She sighs as the whole class looks bemused, wondering if they’ve been taught it all wrong. Eleanor : “The best thing to do with a fresh, crispy Cos is, like, SO CAESAR SALAD!!!” Grinning, she looks to her class who are all muttering amongst once another, a lone hand going up to be followed by a nervous voice. “Er.... This is Trig, not Cookery.” Hands on sides, head tilted and eyebrows raised, Ellie~! looks to the student, locked on and ready to retort. Eleanor : “This is Ellie~!, not someone you talk too.” She tuts, muttering about boy’s lack of manners before moving onto the next question, hoping her students will do better on this one. Eleanor : “Okay.... What’s a Sin?” Due to the fact she didn’t pronounce the silent “e”, and after the last question, nobody seems forthcoming with an answer. Standing at the front of the class, Ellie~!’s vacuous pale Blue eyes scan the room, looking for a sign that someone knows what it is. Eleanor : “Like.... DUH! Come ON?!?!? There’s examples of a Sin all around you.... That girl’s hair, his dress sense... Ewwwwwwzies, and those SHOES, and that ‘Shoes’ in the, like, SO loosest sense of the word!” A hand is slowly raised. “Er.... Do you know when Mr Frost is coming back?” Eleanor : “Luckily for you, he’s probably dead or something... And it looks like he SO hasn’t taught you anything. Now, someone tell be the best way to get Tan?” Following the pattern, one of the female students suggests a non-Mathematical answer. “The Sun?” Ellie~!’s head whips around and her eyes lock right on the girl as if she has just uttered the most severe insult about the little prep’s mother.... Like she has bad shoes or something like that. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!!?“ Her eyebrows are raised up his as she exclaims, the unfortunate victim sat in her chair like a rabbit in the headlights. Eleanor : “SUN?!?!? Like, SO no wonder you have such bad skin!” Shaking her head, she groans and lets out an URGHH!!! in preppy frustration. Eleanor : “I, like, SO give up on you all... Go dig Mr Frost up or something and, like, get him to teach you, Miss Ellie~! is leaving. The a class full of confused faces, Ellie~! skips with purpose out of the class and back onto the corridor where she hones in on her husband’s voice. “And so once the subject has focused in on his reactive mind after following the primary principles of Dianetics...” Hearing the Scientology jargon, the little prep yawns and turns on her high heels as the shot fades out to Black. “On second thoughts, I think Ellie~! will, like, SO give it another go....” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
