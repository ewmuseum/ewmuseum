Good Riddance Time of your life

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/03 Fuck it The scene fades from black to show James sat silently on a small black leather chair, his fingertips lightly holding up his tilting head as he stares blankly ahead of him. His face, which is used to containing the trademark smile is no longer there, underneath his eyes are bloodshot giving a clear indication that he has been crying. Another turning point a fork stuck in the road, time grabs you by the wrist directs you where to go. James lifts his head up and begins to fumble around in his pocket before pulling out his mobile phone after clicking a few buttons he is taken to the message screen, which is simply flashing up no new messages. So make the best of this test and don’t ask why, it’s not a question but a lesson learned in time. Letting a deep sigh filled with unhappiness James’ head and shoulders drop once again his breathing is slow and extremely light giving the impression that he couldn’t care less if he lived or died. It’s something unpredictable, but in the end it’s right. A small smile forms in the corners of James’ mouth as he begins to allow himself to think back…to think about her. Memories of the candlelight champagne picnic under the stars began to dance around James’ head and allowed him to remember just how happy being with Michelle had been. I hope you had the time of your life James nodded to himself and glanced down at his mobile phone once more before flicking casually through his address book to Michelle’s name and pressing the call button. ~ ~ ~ So take the photographs and still frames in your mind, Hang it on a shelf in good health and good time “This is the last boarding call for flight seven two six to Perth.” Spoke a voice over the tannoy system as Michelle’s face dropped as she glanced down at her watch unaware of the time, looking up with distain in her eyes towards the entrance she realised that James was not going to come, she had said her goodbyes and no matter how much she wanted him to arrive through those doors and tell her how much he loved her she knew it was not going to happen. Tattoo’s of memories and dead skin on trial for what it’s worth it was worth all the while. Michelle slowly rose to her feet, slinging her one allowed item of carry-on over her shoulder as she slowly ambled towards the check in desk, she could not have walked any slower if she tried she was hoping for something a sign of some kind but it was as though the fates had deemed it not so. It’s something unpredictable but in the end it’s right. I hope you had the time of your life The worldly known and annoying nokia ring tone began to emanate from Michelle’s pocket as she pulled at the phone expecting it to be one of her friends wishing her luck on the long arduous journey that was ahead of her however when she took time to look at the caller ID her hot stopped for a moment, a rush of emotion took over Michelle as the name James E was flashing before her eyes as the phone continued to vibrate slowly in her hands. It’s something unpredictable but in the end it’s right. I hope you had the time of your life Michelle nervously clicked the receive call button and raised the phone to her ears her heart rate well above normal as she waited to hear James’ voice. James: I’m so glad I caught you before you left look I have to tell you something that I couldn’t tell you before. I’ve never known anyone like you before, you make everything seem brighter when you are around and I can’t bare the thought of … It’s something unpredictable but in the end it’s right. I hope you had the time of your life. The phone went dead. Michelle looked in a state of disbelief at her phone, the battery was still fully charged meaning only two things he had either hung up on her or his battery had died during the most important phone call he would ever have made. Steward: Are you still going to get on this flight madam? Michelle looked up at the steward and then returned her glance to the phone waiting for a few moments wishing the phone to ring once more, it didn’t. With an unspeakable sinking feeling in her heart Michelle looked at the steward one more time before nodding sadly and making her way through the departure gate turning off her mobile phone as she walked through. ~ ~ ~ James: I can’t bare the thought of living without you, I don’t want you to go please stay we can sort something… James stopped mid sentence realising that he could no longer here the hustle and bustle of the airport in the background, he looked at his phone and nothing looked back the liquid crystal display had failed due to lack of battery but had not given him any sort of warning. James looked at his phone is disbelief not knowing how much Michelle had heard, if she had heard the most important aspect of the message. It was then the futility sunk in, James was fully aware that she had not heard the message, that the phone which was supposed to be the pinnacle of technology had let him down, clasping his phone tightly James let out a loud scream of anger, frustration and regret as he threw the phone the length of the room before it shattered into numerous fragments scattering itself across the floor. James dropped to his knees as tears began to flow from his eyes once more, punching the ground hoping that it would give way and swallow him up. ~ ~ ~ Michelle glanced out of her window seat as the engines of the Boeing 747 roared into life and began to propel her down the runway as the nose was pulled back and gently flew into the air soaring higher and higher as the ground became smaller and smaller to Michelle until only the clouds could be seen. It’s something unpredictable but in the end it’s right. I hope you had the time of your life
