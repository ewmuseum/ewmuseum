Visiting the Doctor...

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 June 2002 Doctors... You either hate them or love them. Some doctors care about their patients and try there best to get them better. Other doctors (and this is most of them) are in it for the money, nothing else they don't give 2 flying hoots whether their patients live or die, all they are worried about is their pay cheque coming through at the end of the month. It is a horrifying to think that these people hold our life in their hands. Sure there are some great doctors out there who have been pioneers in the field of medicine, but like everything in this world the field of medicine has become corrupt. What can we do? Nothing, just sit back and pray to god we don't get sick... Doctors, The Dark Messiahs, see the connection? No? Okay let me explain, now as we all saw at Tuesday Turmoil, PCW's Twisted Duo took on the mighty Akiyama, in a handicap match. Now you would have thought that this would have been a walk over for Steel and Stone but that was not the case. PCW's Neo Void God tore The Dark Messiahs to shreds and won the match with ease. However Akiyama didn't stop there, as he had a point to prove to the Tag Team division, and continued to crush Steel and Stone, even crushing Stone's throat. Indeed Akiyama showed how powerful and destructive he was, but what of the twisted duo, who on that night were made to look like jobbers by one man. Surely at this moment in time The Dark Messiahs are at rock bottom? But no, as The Dark Messiahs well know they have got what they wanted, a shot at the Tag Team titles at PCW's next PPV, Gold and Glory. Now with these thoughts in mind we join Steel and Stone as they wait for the doctor... The scene opens up inside a waiting room. Now this waiting room you would have expected to be buzzing with people, and paramedics whizzing by with crash car victims, but no. There is something different about this waiting room; it is very quiet and peaceful. The walls seem to be panelled with varnished oak wood, and the floor is a very fluffy red colour. The receptionist sits at a large oak wood desk, looking very professional, but not very nurse like. The big window behind the receptionist allows us to see that this waiting room is a few floors up from the ground. In fact the more and more you look at this waiting room it seems more like an office waiting room than anything else does. The 'patients' are sat on black leather chairs. Some are reading magazines, and others seem to be rocking back and forth?!?! The ones rocking back and forth seem to be muttering to themselves, it is too quiet to hear what they are saying but judging by the psychotic look in their eyes it isn't about fluffy bunnies. As we look around at the 'patients,' one of them who have been reading the magazine brings it down and throws it on the coffee table. We look up at the person and see that it is none other than Daniel Steel. He sighs and slumps back in his chair. He turns to the person next to him... Daniel Steel: (Bored) Hey Mike, how much longer do we have to wait? I'm so bored. Stone brings the magazine down and turns to face Steel. His face is very disgruntled as he points to his throat. Daniel Steel laughs and puts his hand on his mouth..... Daniel Steel: (Slight chuckle) Oh sorry man, I forgot you can't talk can you. Damn that Akiyama, he had no right to do that, but I guess it is me who is going to be doing all the talking for a while. Am I correct? Stone looks over to Steel still with a disgruntled face. He reaches into his jacket and pulls out a notepad and pen, and quickly scribbles something down and hands it to Steel. Steel looks at it... Daniel Steel: (Reading out note) What do you think!?! (Laughs and looks up at Mike) I was only asking man, no need to get touchy on me. Stone scribbles something else on the paper with a calmer face, he hands it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reading out note) I am just so p***ed off about what happened at Turmoil. I mean how could we have let one man beat us like that. (Nods head and throws note away) Yeah I know what you mean, how we could have let one guy beat us like that is mind boggling. Anyway we shouldn't dwell on it, we got some big matches coming up, and we got to get your throat better or you aren't going to be ready for your match at Fusion. Stone yet again scribbles something down, taking a bit longer this time. He hands the note to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) It doesn't matter how I feel at Fusion I’m going to put an end to our losing streak, and a end to those tag team rejects. Yes I may sound confident but that's only because I am sick and tired of seeing those freak shows on the TV. (Scrunches the note up and throws it to the floor) I know how you feel man this losing streak has got to stop, and at Fusion I do believe you are the man who can end it. I mean sure we have lost both matches we have been in but that is just down to preparation. We hadn't settled in the PCW, as injury in our first match put us on the shelf for a while and then we were thrown into the Handicap match. Sure our excuse may sound pathetic but it is the truth. Anyway enough talk of losing, at Fusion, you my friend are going to be the one coming out on top, and you know I’ll be at ringside if you need any assistance. Stone smiles and scribbles something down on the notepad and hand's it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads note) Who am I facing at Fusion again? I know I am facing members from The Heartbreakers, Pretty Cool Guys and Cowabunga Connection, but I don't know which ones. (Turns to Mike) Your useless remembering things you know that? (Sighs) Your going up against, Rich Sherwin, Jack Valentine, and Totally Tubular Tommy. Stone tips his head back and a strange huff comes from his throat, obviously attempting to laugh. He grabs his notepad and scribbles something down... Daniel Steel:
