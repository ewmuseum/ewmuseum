Finding Kramer (part 1)

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/03 Milo had found the root of Brianna's problems. Her worry and feelings for Kramer, whether they are friendly or more, was eating away at her. She didn't know where he was, and if he was even still alive. She had tried to get a hold of him several times, but the number he had given her had been disconnected. Seeing his friend in utter desperation, Milo decided to take it upon himself to find Kramer. 'Dead ends! They're all dead ends!' Milo shouted as he slammed the black cell phone closed, and pushed it into his coat pocket. He walked away from the doorway of the run down Deli and ventured into the current of people rushing up and down the sidewalks as though an unseen force drove them, going somewhere they didn't want to, otherwise known as work. Milo would be one of those people right now, had he not gotten fired for mouthing off to a customer at Dillard's several weeks ago. He has applied for job after job, but has had no luck. Several days ago he would have thought it a curse, but is glad that it gives him more time to hunt down Kramer at the present. 'All of this work has me dried out. I stop off and get a drink, and then start back again right after.' he says to himself, walking into a Fernelli's Bar. It’s a small joint, with very limited seating. But even though it’s dinky, it’s still a nice looking place with decent prices. And the prices are all Milo seems to care about as he only has a couple of dollars. In his mind he’s battling between plain, boring, old water, or a Kiwi Strawberry Snapple. In the end the Snapple prevails. “Next.” the sullen looking man proclaims behind the counter. “Yes could I please get a Kiwi Strawberry Snapple?” “Is that it?” he replies looking in the air very uninterested with boredom soaking up his eyes. “Yeah, pretty much.” “That’ll be two fifty.” Milo throws three crusty looking dollars onto the counter and the man picks them up reluctantly and hands Milo his fifty cents in change. Milo snatches up the Snapple and is about to walk away, until he hears a voice that is very familiar to his ears. He whips around and looks all over the place and hears it again, able to pinpoint where it’s coming from. Milo walks up to the man who’s back is turned wearing the Fernelli’s workers uniform. “Excuse me…” Milo taps the man on the shoulder and as the man spins around he is shocked at who he sees. “Uh..how may I help ya sir?” “You remind me of someone.” “I do?” the man says nervously. “Yeah, your voice sounds so familiar. OH I GOT IT!” Milo lights up as information sinks into his brain. “Oh never mind, I don’t. Well sorry to bother you.” “No problem Milo.” As soon as he says that he slaps his forehead. Milo stands there with an opened mouthed gaze. He backs up a few steps and opens his mouth to speak, with an arched brow of concern. “Kramer?” The man grabs Milo by the shirt and drags him into the back where an “Employee’s Only” sign is hung above the door. Milo is pushed into the middle of the room, and Kramer pulls off the face mustache and eyebrows. “Kramer I’ve been looking all over for you! Brianna is worried about you, and it’s real jerky of you not to tell her where you were. She thought you might have been dead for crying out loud!! You better be happy I have enough sense not to punch you right now, as I just got a manicure. So count yourself very lucky.” Milo says, ending in a red face as he was holding back the anger raging through him. “Is she doing ok?” Kramer asks. “Yes she’s fine, just a little down. So why didn’t you call?” “I can’t tell you.” “Don’t start that bull shit again!” “You don’t understand Milo. This is more then you could ever comprehend.” “You don’t have to clue me in, but you do owe it to Brianna. You just show up one day and turn her life upside down for the worst. Do you realize that she has gone from a cheery person, to a zombie just over you? She doesn’t eat, she doesn’t sleep, and it seems as though she just doesn’t care about anything but you.” “I want to tell her, god I do! But….I can’t!!” The two stand deadlocking each other with their eyes as the scene slowly fades out. To Be Continued…..
