A Visitor and a Way Out

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Visitor and a Way Out Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Nov. 23, 2002 I needed a break. The mess'd been a real bitch to clean up, and now, looking out the rather small window in my room with a sigh, I knew there had to be something to do...No better option for the time being but to take another can of A&W Root Beer from the well-stocked fridge and turn on the TV now that the mess of papers had been cleared up a little..'a little' meaning 'from utter chaos to mildly discernibly patterned chaos', not an average task, but this was no average office. I started flipping channels absent-mindedly, winding up on some sort of odd French game show, one of those boring things where the contestants sit there and try and make the biggest anagram from a bunch of letters or something...However, at this point I wasn't really cut out to try and understand a show with any more that I'd have to interpret to enjoy, so this was the closest thing to entertainment I could really get...The ho-hum life of working in Europe was getting me down, but I really didn't have anything I should be complaining about..I got to see the sights between shows, and the schedule was flexible.. 'Chat? Sonathe??...Er, Nostache?!....Bah, what the hell is an achetons anyway?' I couldn't be bothered with this...I just walked back to the table near the window, using a random PCW-4 form as a coaster for the can.. This wasn't helping. I couldn't go back to work for now, I'd taken enough time that I deserved a real break but there was nothing really that could be done..I had to think about something. Anything. There wasn't much to do between now and Alliances, but there weren't any sights to see..I was going to have to go back to America tonight, I already had the ticket booked for the flight..Texas might not be as scenic as most of the spots in Europe, but at least folks spoke Engl--... I heard a knock on the door...Couldn't be the housekeeping, they never came at this time of day. Well, whatever it was, it had to be something more interesting than watching the lights go on and off in the office building across the street..I put the drink down and walked across the room, not thinking to clean up anything here, might as well check the peephole first, after all I had to be sure it wasn't my ticket to Shirley Temple-dom, Teresa Tomas..She'd just love to get to humiliate me on her Southern soil.... It was Brandi. A sense of relief washed over me, I needed someone to talk to right now more than anything..I quickly fiddled with the lock and opened the door.. 'Hiya Brandi!' It was a bit of an overenthusiasm on my part, but after such a long day I couldn't help it..She was looking rather well today, must've not had quite so much to do at the office as I did.'How's it going?' 'Hey Tim, it's pretty good....' Her voice trailed off, as she seemed to be looking a little past me..'Got a bit of a messy setup here, don'tcha?' A snicker accompanied that comment, and I chuckled along with it. 'Yeah, it's in a bit of a, er, restructuring period ever since the whole Tomas thing, can't really go to the usual Tax Office places since she's gotta be aware of them..' Wearing a dress might not have been as horrible as I'd worried, but it was something I still didn't wish to participate in on a regular basis.. Brandi laughed a little, before settling back into a chair with a smirk... 'Oh, I don't know, I thought you made a pretty good Annie, though you might want to use a little more rouge next time.' 'Oh ha ha ha....At least I managed to make that little bitch tap out later on, hopefully we'll be doing more of that Sunday. A&W Root Beer?' I swung the fridge door open in what probably qualified as an ostentatious gesture, pulling out one of the remaining drinks. 'Sure,' she said, catching the can tossed her way and resting it on the TV-side table as I sat back on the bed, 'Those two have everything they get coming to 'em now, HIT cheating us out of matches and Tomas having the gall to take cheapshots and cheat to win. Well, can't say they aren't going to get what they deserve when we get through with 'em, then we can be Tag Team Champs and show them how it's done...' I nodded in agreement, as I generally couldn't find anything wrong with 'Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson, your PCW Tag Team Champions' as opposed to just 'The Front Office'. Of course, I sort of liked the ring of 'PCW Taggerweight Champion Tim Walden' a little better...'So, what brings you here anyway?' I shrugged, rather curious..'I mean, we do have tickets on the same flight back.' 'Well, actually, I have a proposal to make, and I figure I should tell you as early as possible...' I wondered what she might be getting at here, since both of us generally had a proper staff for the business and I didn't really have much to offer to Personnel...Obviously, there was going to be something asked of me to be needing to bring this up now..'All right, what is it then?' 'Well..' I hung on that word anxiously, as she paused...'Since you can't really use your usual office because of Teresa and all that, and this hotel thing isn't really working out...' Another little giggle, as I rolled my eyes at the paper blitz that had emerged in the room out of courtesy, though I was liking the sound of it..'I just thought that maybe if you want, you could work at the Personnel offices for the time being.' I was too happy to really say anything, just nodded..'Sure, that'd be great..I mean, there's no way she'd think to go there, and it's really convenient..Could we talk about this over dinner? I'm starting to get sick of living off of the vending machine for my coffee breaks..' She got up quickly, a little nervous in fact..'Well, actually Nathan and I had plans tonight and I'm already a little late for 'em, but I'm sure we'll be able to talk on the flight back. Anyway, the office is all ready to use, so just bring over all of the tax stuff and we can get it set up, okay?' 'Perfect.' She was halfway out the door when I said it, but I was halfway to locking the door, so it was an even trade....I sat back down, splaying myself over the bedding...This could either improve our chances or cause greater chaos... Did the team that works together really win together? ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
