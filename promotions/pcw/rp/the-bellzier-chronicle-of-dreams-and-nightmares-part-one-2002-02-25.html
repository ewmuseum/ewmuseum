The Bellzier Chronicle: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part One]

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 25, 2002 Good Luck SupermarketOakland, CA4:00 am Tired and fatigued could hardly describe me as I sat in the drivers seat of my now demolished Honda Civic. It was a wreck. An absolute mess of a car. Bullet holes littered the sides of the vehicle and broken and shattered glass was everywhere. I had burnt out the gas during the chase and now the car wouldn’t go above twenty miles per hour. That’s assuming that it could turn again at all. We were stopped near a small asian market – one that I remembered distinctly. We were in Oakland, California, just a few minutes outside of San Francisco. I remembered coming here as a child with my mother, watching her shop for asian delights to use in her cooking. All of the aromas, the sensations, the memories – they were all coming back to me. I sighed as I slumped even further down my seat. My mysterious passenger had fled the scene once more, saying that he had a call to make. I was too tired to argue. In fact, I began to fall asleep as I laid there. My eyes closed further and further until they were no longer open, and I was no longer conscious. Dream Sequence The scene opens to Tom Lexian, myself, sitting in the middle of the room. How sad…how pathetic. I guess there’s no crying in wrestling, but I wasn’t your normal wrestler, so I reserved the right to be different. I wasn’t crying, but I was sobbing. I took a severe tongue lashing from that cactus of mine, Bobby, and he told me to shape up or he would ship me out. Where exactly he would flop me off, I don’t really know, but you’ve never been scared until you’ve been threatened by a cactus. Yes, I said cactus. You see, in this world, there is no distinction between people and objects. Somehow, they all merge together into one entity until you cannot see one from the other. I don’t trust people so much, so I rely on a plant to guide me to victory. Oh, it’s a wonderful parallel universe. I was filled with sorrow, and filled with anger, yet I could do nothing but wallow in my own self pity. I decided to sing a song, and maybe someone would hear my cries. So I sang, “Nobody knows…the trouble I’ve seen….nobody knows…my sorrow…” The room was dimmed, but not as midnight dark as it was before during the promo. There was a small lamp at the table I was sitting at, and it was on, sparking a twinge of light for me to rest my eyes on. I was twirling a small spoon which was laying flat on the table in circles. How much more bored could I be? Suddenly, a distorting sound called out to me from the distance. QUACK! QUACK! I looked around the round, confused and perplexed, trying to find where that sound was coming from exactly. As we all know, “quack” is the sound a duck makes. Was there a duck in here? Duh. QUACK! QUACK! I suddenly pulled my head into the air, trying to figure out where these mysterious quacks were coming from. I turned the light on to a higher setting, illuminating the entire room even more. As the lights came up, I saw one of those clapping squirrels standing in front of me on the floor. He…or she, erm, it just stared at me with those big, beady eyes of his. I’m no animal expert, but squirrels aren’t supposed to quack, are they? “ Now, what do you want? Are you a talking squirrel, or can all you do is clap?” The squirrel shifted his head to the side, as if confused on what I had just said. Great. Not only was it a clapping squirrel, but it didn’t understand English either. My luck just never runs out. I began to wave my arms around like a crazed lunatic at the little critter. “C’mon, get out of here. You hear me? Scram!” The squirrel then smiled. Squirrels don’t smile. Now it was my turn to shift my head to the side, as if confused on what the squirrel had just done. Hell, if I knew it’s name, I would have cursed at it. I feel odd cursing at moving organisms without knowing their identity. Call me crazy, but I did. So what do I do? Of course, I get up off my seat, and I start flailing my arms in the air, trying to scare him away. Look at those advance tactics of fear I used. Yup, that thirty bucks I spent on that class at Vista Community College finally paid off, it looks like. But the squirrel wasn’t getting scared. It just got up on it’s hind legs, and looked at me oddly. Geez, this reminded me of that scene in Jurassic Park, with all those dinosaurs. Didn’t that one dino spit in that guy’s face and then eat him? A spitting squirrel…hell, he already claps. He’s evolving too quickly for me. “ Okay…well, I mean you no harm. I really don’t. Here, take this as a token of my appreciation. Eat it, it’s great.” I throw a chunk of spam at the squirrel. He sniffs it, but then hisses, showing me those big bucked teeth of his. “ Whoa. I’m sorry. Please don’t kill me! Look, I didn’t mean to give you that spam. Here, let me give you something more to your liking…” I checked my pockets, searching for something that might appease that crazy critter. Let’s see here…wallet, assorted pennies and change, a toothbrush, calculator, MTV sticker of the Backstreet Boys… …I decided to throw my wallet. I mean, money is valuable, but how valuable could it be to a squirrel? They probably barter in nuts and acorns anyways. I threw my ten dollar wallet at the squirrel, who sniffed it, and then took it in his paws. I enclosed my face in my heads and bellowed, “DAMMIT!” Ten dollars can buy quite a bit, you know? Magazines, pre-roasted chickens, pencils, pads of paper….that could have been three trips to In’N’Out! The squirrel then ran off, heading over to the wall, where there was a door. A door! Not the door to exit the room, but some really small one that only that pesky squirrel could fit in. He opened it, ran inside, and shut it. Did I mention the clapping whore also had my wallet? Argh. So what would you do? Just forget about it, chalk it up to bad luck, and go on with your day? If you answered yes, then you’re probably some poor, misguided fool like Harry Porter! My name’s Tom Lexian, and I always take the difficult road. So I decided to follow the squirrel, for the sake of getting back my wallet. There wasn’t any money in it, but it had my library card. My most valuable possession, my LIBRARY CARD! Whoo! For the sake of the children, save the library cards. So I ran over to the door, opening it with my thumb, and trying to find my way in. Hmm…it was obviously too small for me to fit in. What to do…what to do… Well, being the genius that I am, I decided to stick my fat arm down the hole and see what I find. So I did so. Unbuttoning the sleeve of my shirt, I pulled my sleeves up and stuck my right arm down the door. I couldn’t feel anything. It was just a lot of nothing down there. As I went down further and further, something finally caught my hand. I tried to tug it back up, but it resisted, and I was suddenly pulled down. Yes, my six foot frame was pulled into the smallish door. How? Don’t ask me, it just hurt a lot. “Hey you…wake up…” Something was tugging away at my shirt, trying to wake me. My head hurt so much that I felt like curling up into a ball and not getting up, but I realized that I still didn’t know where I was. That was never a good thing. So I opened my eyes, and everything was nothing but a blur. As I did, a figure was becoming more and more clear. I shouted out in terror, as I looked up at a giant rabbit man with a large top hat on. As I looked up to see where I was, I saw the figure of a giant rabbit standing in front of me. Being the brave, charismatic soul that I am, I did what I though was necessary. I screamed. “AHHHHHHH!” The giant rabbit man suddenly jumped into the air, hoping on one foot, and began screaming as well. “AHHHHHH!” I got a better look at this guy, and I couldn’t believe it. I mean, it was a five foot five talking rabbit. He had on a purple suit, with no shoes, so his little rabbit feet were showing. Well, little was an understatement. He had clowns feet, only furry and rabbit-like. His top hat really reminded me of something, but I couldn’t put my finger on it… “ Please! Please! Stop screaming! Please! If you make too many noise, the evil Man-Tiger will come and eat us!” “ The who? The evil Man-Tiger? What’s that?” I asked, sounding a bit more intrigued now. “ You don’t know? He’s the evilest, most vicious animal in all of the land. He has giant teeth…” The rabbit man opens up his mouth to reveal two bucked teeth, and tries to extend them out to represent vicious fangs. How cute. “….huge, terrible claws…” He then takes his furry paws, pounding them against his chest. “ …and he knows kickboxing! How terrible is that?! Jean-Claude Van Damme has nothing on him!” It’s about this time when I realized what a great charades partner this rabbit man would be. Friday nights at my house was the usual stage. We would really be able to kick some ass. “ Calm down!” I shouted at him, trying to get him to stop babbling. “First, tell me who you are and we can work things out from there. I promise, no tiger monsters are going to hurt you.” “ Okay, well, my name is Jax Rabbit, but my friends call me Fearful.” Name sounds familiar, but I can’t put my finger on it… “ Nice to meet you. I’m Tom Lexian, but my friends call me lame. Anyways, where am I and have you seen a clapping squirrel?” “Where are you? You don’t even know where you are? C’mon, who are you?” “ Look, the last thing I remember was chasing this squirrel. He ducked down into a door and I tried to follow him in. I stuck my arm down the hole and then…” “ …and then you woke up here, right? Ah, I see. Well, I think I should explain your squirrel phenomenon for a moment. Who you were chasing was the mysterious clapping squirrel. He’s always fooling around with other worlds, using that damn door portal of his to sneak up on others. He works for the evil Caterpillar, Maples. Maples owns this entire land, and he rules with an iron cocoon.” “ A cocoon?” Fearful jus shrugged. “Well, figuratively speaking. Anyways, this squirrel has been causing havoc in Lameland for ages now. I remember one day he brought in this one fat fellow…I believe his name was Presley or something. He had big hair, a huge gut, and these weird outfits. And he loved to sing these songs about Houndogs and such. Gosh, he’s nothing but a trouble maker as well.” “ You know what? This isn’t quite the world that I knew before. What is this place?” “Well, this is Dreamland. It’s magical place, almost as magical as Disneyland, only with less money needed to get in. I mean, I love it here. There’s so much fun everyday, and seemingly something happens all the time!” A flying buffalo suddenly flies past me and Fearful. “Yes, I think you’re right.” “ The only thing I hate is this tyranny that Maples has over the land. I can’t wait till he turns into a butterfly, and then gets eaten by a spider. Man, I’m just counting the days. He’s bound to start metamorphosing any day now!” “Wow, this place does sound great, but I really can’t stay here. I mean, I have another life in my real world. I have a career, I have a home…I HAVE A CACTUS TO WATER!” “ A cactus? We have one here too. Bobby, the giant Cactus Who Talks Too Much! He lives near here. He knows a lot, so if you need to figure out how to get out of here, I guess all you have to do is ask him. He’s pretty annoying though. I hate being neighbors with him sometimes, but he does keep away all of the pesky Kordics around here.” “ Kordics? And those are…” “ The meandering pigeon webmaster! He’s always pandering me for more CGI cookies. No one can bake a SHTML file like I can. I’m an award winner!” “ Wow, that’s great. Anyways, where can I find this know it all cactus? I think I really should talk to him.” “ Why, I’d love to hear more about this fellow. Hmm, I have a thought. It’s already pretty late…2 p.m. by my watch. We should start packing in to go to bed. Maybe you’d like to have dinner with me?” “ 2 p.m. is late?” “ Of course. In Lameland, everyone goes to bed in the afternoon and wake up in the middle of the night. It just makes complete and utter sense.” It does? “ Well, I don’t know. I really should be heading back…” “ C’mon, I’m cooking up some Macromedia Poser stew! Hot from the stove!” “ I guess I could stay for dinner then. It sounds delicious!” Okay, so I had dinner with a stranger. A strange, rabbit guy, that talks and cooks. Hell, I was hungry. A guy has to eat. Anyways, I was pretty sure my appetite would take over sooner or later, so I would eat, sleep, and then meet this cactus to find a way out of here. Besides, HTML is delicious. How could I pass it up? Tee-hee.
