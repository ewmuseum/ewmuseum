Reminders and a Festival

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April Heading into Spring Fling 2002, El Diablo has one up on his nemesis Flaymin Aymin. He has beaten Flaymin Aymin, whereas Aymin has thus far failed in any attempt to defeat El Diablo inside of a wrestling ring. It happened a few weeks back on Sunday Sabotage, El Diablo managed to pin Aymin and pick up the victory. Some thought that would end this rivalry, but it was only a taster of things to come. Sure enough things heated up with Flaymin's precious Afro being shaved off, soon followed by Flaymin blow torching El Diablo's wife, Angel, hair off. Now things started to get personal. El Diablo and Jenna in a room together, and this soon brought about brawls, truces and utter destruction of each other. Not to mention El Diablo's rants and dark speeches about Flaymin Aymin. It is now apparent that this feud goes deeper than El Diablo leaving International Incident, but what is it? And how deep does it go? El Diablo is not mentally stable and leading into Spring Fling with his mask on the line it only inches him closer to the brink of insanity. Recently El Diablo managed to retrieve back the Taco Bell dog for the good of all of Mexico, and even managed to get the dog back into shape from the slob it was while Flaymin Aymin held it captive. His reward you may ask? He gets to keep the dog and be the official spokesperson for Taco Bell. Now, I know what you're thinking, how would a masked lunatic be able to make Taco Bell more productive? Well the team of El Diablo and Andy the Taco Bell Chihuahua will work commercials together, that is his true reward for rescuing the precious Chihuahua and making him healthy again. So sure, there has been some glory for El Diablo that has come out of this rivalry with Flaymin Aymin, but mostly this rivalry has made tempers reach boiling point until the point of no return and that was only emphasised further when El Diablo's wife and Flaymin's valet, were caught in a sexual innuendo on Fusion. Who can El Diablo trust? Who can Flaymin Aymin trust? Are their relationships over? Hair vs Mask, who will win? All is unknown at this moment, but as the time draws nearer to Spring Fling 2002 there will be some realisation of what will happen. But for now, tempers are high, confusion is high, and thoughts of losing his precious mask are intolerable. Mexican Festival There was a Mexican festival held when El Diablo successfully retrieved the Taco Bell Chihuahua, thousands upon thousands of loyal Mexicans joined on the streets of Mexico City to make this one of the biggest festivals in Mexican history. And in typical fashion, everyone can speak English! El Diablo stands behind a red curtain and peeks out through one of it's gaps, what he sees astonishes him greatly, there are literally thousands of cheering and partying Mexicans awaiting his and the Chihuahua's presence. In the crowd of thousands, many sombrero hats are visible and many Tequila bottles in hand, there are streamers and balloons tied to the sides of nearby buildings. Many people have their tops off, including the Mexican women and are walking in either sandals or nothing at all on the hot sand on the hot summers day in Mexico. Of course many a moustache can be seen in the crowd, and loud music from speakers set up on the stage. Playing through the music is typical Mexican party music. It really is a special day for Mexico to have gotten its pride back in that Chihuahua and everyone out there partying knows it. For today is the day that the whole of Mexico celebrates the return of it's pride and joy in the form of the Chihuahua and it's rescuer El Diablo. El Diablo is wearing his mask as always, he has his top off and wears some long shorts with sandals while having the Chihuahua on a leash as he still peers through the red curtains on the set up stage that he is on. Suddenly he is tapped on the shoulder. 'It's almost time to go on senor,' says the voice of the stage manager. The stage manager is a well built man, with shaven hair and a dark brown goatee, he has many tattoos on his muscular arms, one most noticeably been the Mexican Flag. 'It's a proud day for us all,' he finishes before walking away from El Diablo. El Diablo nods and smirks and is obviously pleased to be home. The music ceases as the all the partiers stop talking and dancing and look up to the stage. On the stage is just a podium where the microphone is and the large speakers, nothing fancy, just typical that, of Mexico. Stood at the podium is the president of Taco Bell himself. 'It is a great honor to be back home,' he speaks with great pride. The crowd cheers loudly while jumping up and down. 'It is also with great honor that we have our Chihuahua back,' he shouts with a passion. The crowd cheers once more at this festival. A great feeling of happiness is felt around the whole festival, not to mention the whole of Mexico. 'It is with great esteem now, that I introduce to you, the man that single handedly rescued our beloved Chihuahua, PCW wrestler, El Diablo,' he says while motioning for El Diablo to come on stage. The crowd cheers and whistles for it's apparent national hero. El Diablo however is reluctant to get on stage, could it be stage fright? He is just frozen, his legs won't move for him to walk out on stage. 'Hey you,' says a voice. El Diablo looks around and sees no one. 'Down here,' the Mexican voice says again. He looks down and sees the Chihuahua talking. He shakes his head in disbelief to make sure he isn't imagining it. Meanwhile on stage the crowd are cheering while Taco Bell President looks nervous in anticipation for El Diablo to get on stage. 'Yo quiero, get on stage now, and prove your worth,' says the Chihuahua to El Diablo. El Diablo blinks and then stops still and thinks to himself before talking aloud. 'Hey, dogs can't talk,' he says suspiciously. He looks down at the Chihuahua. 'Bark,' the dog barks. 'That's better,' says El Diablo whilst smiling. Out on stage the crowd are starting to boo. 'I now bring to you, El Diablo,' the Taco Bell President repeats himself. The crowd cheers and applauds once more as El Diablo walks out with the Chihuahua on leash and heads towards the podium. He shakes hands with the Taco Bell President. 'You got here just on time,' whispers the President to El Diablo as he walks off stage. El Diablo gets onto the podium and makes sure the Chihuahua is in full view to the crowd. The crowd calms down, as El Diablo looks set to speak. 'It feels great to back in Mexico,' says El Diablo nervously to a great response from the crowd. El Diablo smiles. 'And it also feels good to have returned this Mexican treasure back home,' he says while pointing to the Chihuahua. The crowd whistles and applauds. 'I promise you and all of Mexico that his kidnapper will pay the price,' he says to boos from the crowd at the mention of kidnap, 'Aymin will be brought to justice and I will punish him like no other has before,' he shouts in patriotism. The crowd applauds and cheers him wildly. 'But today isn't about speech,' says the Mexican Devil to the astonishment of the crowd. El Diablo then picks up a bottle of Tequila that was left on the stage. 'Today is about partying,' speaks Diablo in pride. 'Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeah,' respond the crowd. Diablo smirks in pride of being back home in the country he loves. 'MEXICAN STYLE,' he shouts while finishing off his speech. The crowd starts cheering and partying again as music once more hits the speakers. El Diablo starts drinking the Tequila as he makes his way off stage to behind the red curtains again, where he meets with the Taco Bell president. 'We'll expect you to be on time to record our next commercial,' says the President. El Diablo nods as he still drinks the Tequila from the bottle. He then drops the bottle as he finishes it, and shakes hands with the Taco Bell President. 'It will be an honor,' he says while shaking the mans hand. El Diablo smiles, knowing what he has in store for Aymin at Spring Fling.
