The Handler's Day, starring The End

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/10/02 *Strange beeping music* 'It can't be 9 am already,' The End mumbles quietly as he stirs from his peaceful slumber, his head buried in his red pillow with the matching sheets somewhere down on the floor. The End gets out of his 5 & 1/2 foot tall bed (the beds are a pain to lower) and moves past the piles of dirty and clean clothes on the floor to his desk. The alarm clock is on the desk. He turns off the alarm, and checks the time: 9 AM. The End didn't get the sleep he wanted to get last night (probably shouldn't have stayed up so late watching cartoons; still, that's his fault). So The End gets back in bed, knowing he can sleep for another thirty minutes and get up himself, without the alarm. 33 minutes later, The End is up, and he goes to take a shower. “Great,” say The End, as he notices that Chauncey, who routinely takes 45-minute showers (and swears that he’s not obsessive compulsive about being clean), currently occupies the shower. The End goes across the hall to his friends’ room (who are gone for the week) and uses their shower. The End comes back to his room and dresses up in his standard ring attire (which represents the same shoes and pair of Sean John pants that the handler has worn for the past 6 days now) and gets his bag, wallet, and keys to go to work. Arriving early, The End finds that a class has already opened the computer lab for research purposes. The End moves in, sits down at the computer, and goes about his usual business. The End:'I can't use a computer, I'm blind.' Handler:'JUST GO ALONG WITH IT!' So, he's sitting down at the first computer in the 4x4 row in the computer lab, the only person using the computers at the moment. The room is a little chilly, but nothing The End can't handle. The End logs onto the Internet Explorer, and types in the America Online website address. *Types www.aol.com* The End types in his account information (you’re not getting that) and brings up his e-mail. Four porn site invitations, something that claims that his penis size is not big enough, and something about mortgage rates are going up. Six messages deleted. The second that those messages are deleted, another pops up about downloading a software that allows you to find anyone you want, anywhere. Another message deleted. Next, The End checks his hotmail account, which is more of the same. So, his e-mail cleared out for the day, The End goes to the PCW website and checks out what’s up. *Types www.pcw.ewmuseum.com/index2.shtml* The main index page comes up. Nothing new in the news or show updates since he was on the day before. The next thing The End does is go to the Roleplay Forums to see what has been put on the boards. Some stuff on the boards from Flaymin Aymin, Chef, Suburban Uprising is up there. “Ah,” The End says, “there’s an RP here from Stormbringer. Says Fusion. Odds are, it involves me.” The End checks back at the PCW website, and sure enough, he’s got a match with Stormbringer and Loc. Darkstar is The End’s partner. “Darkstar… ah well.” The End then goes to the PTC forums through the link under the Navigation Bar at the top of the PCW RP board. At the forums, he sees that Turmoil and Fusion assignments are posted. Being a backup matchwriter, The End looks to see if he’s on duty. The End is writing two matches on Fusion. “A match to RP for, and two matches to write for Fusion. I’m going to be working here for a while.” The End then checks his personal messenger for a message from Charlie Loc. After that, it’s pretty much just working on this RP. It starts off like this. The Misfits have returned, and they are joined by more former characters. So now, the roster of the Misfits looks like this. Richard Brogan: Height – 6’3”Weight – 242 lb.Stats: Scruffy-bearded man that looks like Jonathan Frakes when he first grew a beard on Star Trek The Next Generation. He plays a towel rack like a flute for money, refers to himself in the third person, and is a little dumb for his adulthood. Other than that, he’s a normal guy. He can, however, understand Slade’s incoherent mumblings. Slade MacGregor: Height -- 6’Weight – 205 lb.Stats: Slade, a Scotsman, is a terminal sleepwalker, which means he sleeps all the time and never wakes up. However, Slade is still able to operate in the conscious world as if he were awake. Slade is the smartest of the Misfist, though this isn’t really noticeable away from them, since he can only speak in mumbles and slurs that the Misfits (and them alone) can understand. Slade looks like Ewan McGregor. Lamar Lewis: Height – 5’10”Weight – 350 lb.Stats: Lamar is a leper; his body parts may at times just fall off. Making him even more of a Misfit, Lamar’s condition hasn’t changed his slow metabolism one bit; he’s still 350 pounds of fat! Lamar’s condition has also affected his mind, causing regression to a childlike state. His comprehension, speech, and actions resemble that of a 6-year-old. Lamar Lewis looks like an even fatter version of Wayne Knight. Drek Redaxe: Height – 3’8”Weight – 230 lb. (solid muscle)Stats: A dwarf from the Middle Ages, Drek is stuck in this time. If you need more info than that, I can’t give it to you, since Asa Fountain’s handler gave me this character. Drek Redaxe is the kind of dwarf you’ll find in Warcraft, but looks like Wee-Man from Jackass Lexicon: Height – 5’11”Weight – 184 lb.Stats: Lexicon is a master of the English vocabulary, and will often just use a word to show that he knows what it is, and in what context it works best. Lexicon is also responsible for breaking kayfabe so that Kay Fabe may be injured. Lexicon resembles James Brolin. Kay Fabe: Height – 5’Weight – 100 lb.Stats: The physical representation of kayfabe, Kay Fabe exists to be injured whenever Lexicon breaks kayfabe. Doesn’t even talk, he’s just there to get hurt. Imagine Gilbert Gottfried. Okay introductions over, the descriptions are all out of the way, and I don’t feel like going into what they wear from day to day. Just imagine that they’re all wearing silver jumpsuits or something. The Misfits walk into Chuck E. Cheese’s. Lexicon:“Indeed, this is quite a fun establishment; though my Domicile of Fluid-Exchanging Intercourse—” Richard:“You mean your bachelor pad?” Lexicon:“… Yes, but PLEASE! Do not interrupt me when I am about to go onto a Rent-A-Hero like monologue.” Drek:“I don’t think anyone would want that.” Everyone in the establishment ceases what they are doing, turn to the Misfits, and agree in unison. NPCs:“Yeah!” Everything goes back to normal, with the singing puppets on stage, the games, lights, pizza delivery to tables… everything that makes Chuck E. Cheese’s so great. Lamar immediately runs off to the stage. He jumps up to where the bird is manning the drums, and tosses away the animatronic puppet to bang on the drums. Guards in giant rat suits come to get Lamar, and rag him away. The guards return Lamar to the Misfits. Guard 1: (to Richard)“Excuse me, sir, but are you with the party?” Richard:“No.” Guard 2:“Then we’re sorry but you’ll have to leave.” The guards kick out the six Misfits. Sitting on the curb, Richard waves his fist back at the building. Richard:“Damn communist Smurfs!” The bus arrives at the stop in front of Chuck E. Cheese and the Misfits board. They all sit in the back. The bus riders all stare at the Misfits and wonder how six men in silver jumpsuits could be covered in pizza and tokens. Slade:“*Incomprehensible mumbles and slurs*” Richard:“Richard believes that The End shall defeat Stormbringer and Loc, even if Darkstar performs as he did last time he was in a tag match, namely walking out on his partner.” Lamar turns to a little girl sitting back facing the Misfits behind her. He removes a piece of food from his clothes and offers it to her. Lamar:“Pepperoni?” The girls’ mother slaps away Lamar’s hand and sprays him with mace. Lamar is blinded. Lamar:“AHHHHHHHHH! Why’d you do that? I was being good!” Slade:“*Incomprehensible mumbles and slurs*” Lexicon:“Yes, Slade is in the affirmative, and on his views I concur. The End, whom I have worked with in EW, is a very talented wrestler, and shall easily take this match into his win column.” Drek sees that a nun is eyeing them with acid. Drek runs up to the nun and shows his axe in her face. Drek:“YOU WANT SOME OF THIS?” Drek beats the nun over the head, and slaps her silly. Kay Fabe, who suffered being dragged along by the bus (earlier break of kayfabe not mentioned), listens in on the conversation and doesn’t think very highly of the Misfits at the moment. He speaks where he can without getting smoke in his lungs or bugs in his teeth. Kay Fabe:“*Cough* Morons *Cough* All of them….” What, you were expecting more? Selfish prick, get out of here! Just kidding, especially if this is a matchwriter. Hope you enjoyed the read.
