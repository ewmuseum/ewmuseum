Title: Des Moines City Ordinance #3657435 Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: April 6/7, 2002 The art of politics. In many ways, it is an easy one to learn, but it takes a lifetime to master, Whether you like politicians, or think they are the lowest form of life on Earth, you must respect them. The game they play is perhaps the most cutthroat in the world. Any of their past actions are fair game for mudslingers. To be a politician is to have your dirty laundry aired for your town, district, state, or even country to see. There is no privacy. Yet, men go into politics proudly. Some want to feel the sense of pride involved in helping out in the community. Others enjoy the feeling of power, to be able to aid in solving society's ills. Still others do it for the interns. Still, we must show respect for these men. Relatively few people have the cajones and will to step up and lead. William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft are two of these men. Despite most likely being corrupt and womanizing, they HAVE done good for their community in between spurts of the above. No politician likes to be mocked. Unfortunately, it may be the most mocked profession, other than that of an attorney. But political jokes are one thing. For Politically Incorrect, seeing the Cowabunga Connection running around impersonating politicians is a grave insult. And yes, in traditional PI style, there WILL be hell to pay. ::In the Des Moines Muncipal Building, activity is always going on. Des Moines' municipal court may be in action. However, on Friday, a town council meeting occurred here. A resolution was adopted by the Des Moines town council, with the mayor's approval. This resolution is, shall we say, somewhat odd. But, EVERYTHING Politically Incorrect does is odd, so why should this be any different? William S. Powell is seated in his chair in the town council's chamber, holding a copy of the passed resolution, now an official Des Moines city ordinance. Lovecraft and Roger Schultz sit in seats to his left and right.:: William S. Powell: It was a busy Friday for the Des Moines town council. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ummm, how do you attend these meetings when you're on the road? ::Roger steps in to interrupt.:: Roger Schultz: Videoconferencing. The chamber's wired to allow Councilman Powell to speak to and hear his colleagues. Reginald V. Lovecraft: So, what's this big surprise you've been telling me about ever since Friday? William S. Powell: Well, you know how Cowabunga Connection has been running around beaches like complete and utter morons trying to impersonate us? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes. Those two men are beginning to get at my last nerve. William S. Powell: I found a solution. You see, if they want to strike at the heart of what we do, I found it extremely appropriate to strike at the heart of what they do. ::Powell takes out a pair of reading glasses, and begins to read from the piece of legislation.:: William S. Powell: City Councilman William S. Powell introduces the following ordinance to promote safety in Des Moines' waterways. Section 1: The use of surfboards and boogie boards is hereby prohibited within the Des Moines city limits. ::Schultz has an incredulous look on his face.:: Roger Schultz: You BANNED surfing?! William S. Powell: YES! Now let me finish! ::Powell continues reading.:: William S. Powell: Section 2: Furthermore, surfing related activities such as windsurfing and bodysurfing are also banned. Section 3: To be caught surfing, windsurfing, boogie boarding, or bodysurfing will result in a fine not to exceed $250 and/or up to fifteen days imprisonment. Section 4: If passed, this ordinance shall be effective immediately. Roger Schultz: How the hell did you get the town council to pass this? William S. Powell: You underestimate the influence I hold on the town council...and on other political scenes, as well. I had a rather well known senator place a call on my behalf? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Wait, wait...you have influence in the U.S. Senate? Man...I thought I was a bigshot because I knew people in the Department of Justice. William S. Powell: Sweet! We'll have to increase our influence soon. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Agreed...wait a second, I just realized something. You know, in all the time I've spent here, I've never seen a single person surfing.. William S. Powell: Of course no one surfs here! THIS IS DES MOINES! We're over one thousand miles from the Atlantic! Why do you think the ordinance passed so easily? Roger Schultz: Then isn't it quite the hollow gesture. William S. Powell: It is no more hollow than those two towhead surfing bafoons wandering around pretending to be politicians! I mean, they're missing the most important ingredient! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Knowledge of the political system? Decent clothes? William S. Powell: No! INTERNS!! ::Lovecraft sighs:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Why are you always bringing up interns? Are you just jealous that Katherine and I have a loving relationship, and your interns never so much as look at you? William S. Powell: HEY! At least I don't have have to use Viagra to get it up! Your 49 year old dong can't be going much longer! ::Lovecraft shoots Powell a stare. The message is clear: F*ck you, and let's go on to another topic.:: William S. Powell: Fine, fine... Spring Fling is a rather important night. It's Fear Factory, Golden Game, Cowabunga Connection, and us. But those II problems seem to have silenced the Game, and Rudolph must be experimenting on Creeper again, so we're back down to us and the Cowabungas, for all of the marbles. You know, dudes, we just won these things a little while ago on Fusion, beating you guys with the Primary Election in the process. We don't want to give these titles up just yet. We like being tag team champions. We like the fame. Reginald V. Lovecraft: We like the fortune. William S. Powell: We LOVE the women! ::Reginald groans slightly.:: William S. Powell: Fact is though, you two can't beat us. You couldn't beat us after we wrestled three other teams, and you certainly can't beat us now that we'll be fresh. Later today will be Election Day. Roger Schultz: And, on Election Day, Politically Incorrect always triumphs! ::We fade out slowly, to the usual.....:: Paid for by the friends of the Tag Team Champs, Politically Incorrect!Election Day is tonight at Spring Fling! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
