Date: with minimal effort... Suave indeed… The scene opens up in the exotically bright, dazzling, and corrupt risk recreation center of the U.S., Las Vegas, Nevada. Right in the center of sin, the glimmering surroundings hide the BLACK HEART that lies within this cesspool of showmen who rob all the retired old people, the gambling addicts, and of course the “just married” couples that will break up in a year, or less, of their money. Cue the excitingly dramatic secret agent music, as a jet black Jag with an extended cab slowly sifts through all the poison mascara that makes this town seem so pretty. The driver passes every swanky hotel, grand casino, and “Charming” street corner swindler on the Strip, until… “This would be the place, Jimmy. You can let me out here.” …It pulls up to a peculiarly misplaced Laundromat called the “Laundry Strip.” The backseat door of the Jag opens up, and sliding one Armani attired leg out first, followed quickly by the other a handsome gentleman emerges from the car, and lustfully takes in the scent of greed in the air. Wearing a custom tailored, black tuxedo, with his hair slicked back, one might mistake this fellow to be one of those daytime television stars. He’s the really suave type. Suddenly, reaching out of the still open door a thin, elegant female hand clasps the classy guy’s hand, and gives a small tug. It catches the attention of the well-refined sir, but just slightly, as he gives off the impression that he has some serious business to get to. Female inside the Jag: “Oh, Johnny, (A small, flirtatious giggle.) When will I see you again?” Bringing her pretty hand to his lips, with its perfect manicure and expensive jewelry, leaving the beholder’s imagination to picture the rest of her, he kisses it, and grins from ear to ear. Johnny: “(English Accent.) Oh, my darling… (Tries to think of her name, cringing.) … Never.” And with that, the secret agent tune plays even louder than before, as she quickly withdrawals her hand, and he slams the door in her angry face. He pounds on the top of the Jag, and it starts to make its move… Johnny: “Well, Jimmy, you can take this one back to the street corner where she came from, I’m quite exhausted of her! (Chuckles with delight, waving ‘goodbye.’)” “Oh, Johnny, you have such a … ‘way’ with women.” Johnny: “(Mumbling.) Oh, delightful. Here’s the old hag now…” Sliding his jacket’s sleeve up to reveal a silver Rolex watch, Johnny takes a quick peak over his shoulder to see if anyone’s watching, then brings it to his face, and hits the “Light” button. In a heartbeat, the normal time display of the watch vaporizes, being quickly replaced by a small monitor image of a woman. Probably in her late fifties, the woman gives Johnny a rather stern look. Older Woman: “(English accent, also.) Report your status…” Johnny: “I’m just about to slip into the casino now… (A nervous tone setting in.) But, may I ask whether or not you think storming into an underground casino, to find information about some mysterious mastermind, completely based on the fact some weirdo sent in an anonymous tip?” Woman: “What? Who cares? It’s not my arse. Anyway, we need you to find someone.” The screen blips, and instantly a picture of a young brunette, with gorgeous red lips, appears on the watch screen. Johnny gets sprung by her looks, if you get the idea… “Her name… we don’t know yet. All we know is that she is the only one who knows the true identity of the scheming mastermind. You need to find her, since she’s on the inside…” Johnny: “Or, you mean this mastermind is inside of her, and you want me to seduce her so that I’m inside her, and therefore on the inside of this scheme, and…” “Well, not quite…” Johnny: “No, an order is an order, Mam!” She’d like to say more, but he cuts her off with the push of a button, and stealthily slithers into the Laundromat. Upon his penetration into the fake clothes cleaning facility, Johnny immediately uses years of training to assess the situation. Sure, it looks like a normal little convenience for the public, but he can see the secrets that lie deep beneath the exterior of the… “May I be of some assistance, buddy?” Completely taken by surprise, Johnny almost hits the roof… But, he must remain calm, act normal, and most importantly keep a suave appearance. He takes a collective look around the garment cleaning facility, until he finally spots the sentry who somehow was able to detect him. Laying cool eyes upon the man behind the counter whom had discovered him, Johnny sizes him up. Man behind counter: “Listen, if ya here for laundry, come back durin’ the daytime…” In a skipped heartbeat, the Laundromat clerk finds himself nose to nose with the oddly sweet scented stranger in the tuxedo, who stares deeply into the eyes of the slightly unsettled clerk. Johnny: “Oh, believe you know why I’m here… (Winks.)” OOC:rest to come later...
