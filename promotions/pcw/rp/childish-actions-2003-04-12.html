Childish Actions

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/03 Another day and once more I found myself sitting in my room all alone thinking. 'Man I really miss sleeping next to Tessa.' I found myself thinking silently. I knew that wouldn't be happening for a while since Tessa was still married to Gaz Green. I may be a former porn star but I do have my morals and sleeping with another man's wife is one of them. Then if neither of them wants the marriage and I'm sure Gaz wouldn't care if I cuddled up next to Tessa. But it was against the teaching of the Kami so here I remain in the cold alone. I sudden knock at my door ripped me from my thoughts. 'Come in' I hollered at the door. The door slowly opened allowing light into the room. I noticed a small figure standing in the light. I knew it had to be Evan. It looked as though he had something in his hands. The little one made his way over to my bed where I was sitting and looked up at me. I knew what he wanted so I picked him up and placed him on the bed. Little Evan lend against me, resting his head on my side. He handed me a piece of paper. I looked at it to see a drawing of a smiley face. The paint was mixed together but the picture still looked precious to Chris. A smile forced its way to Chris' face. I gave Evan a hug thanking him for the gift. Evan smiled but took the picture from me. I looked at him wondering what he was up to. Evan turned the picture around to show me the back of it. 'Dear Chris, please feel better soon.' I looked at Evan and gave him a warm smile. Despite all the shit that has been going on lately, it really did feel good to have Evan trying to make things better. He wasn't my son by birth but I loved him just the same. I noticed the card was from both Tessa and Evan. Evan grabbed my hand and hopped off the bed. I started to follow him as he led me out of my room and down the stairs. “Evannnn” I called from the bottom of the stairs wondering where in the house he had wandered off to, figuring that he was in his usual mood for hide and go seek. “I’m coming.” He answered, his voice echoing from the hallway at the top of the stairs. Surely enough in a matter of seconds he appeared at the top of the stairway with Chris following close behind him. Chris was smiling which was unusual considering that ever since the marriage of Gaz and I, the only sort of smile he had been able to give was a forced one that was usually not intended to be in my direction. “Someone seems happy.” I smiled slightly looking up at Chris who was holding the painting Evan had made for him a few days ago. 'Well of course I am happy. I have a wonderful son like Evan. What father wouldn't be happy?' I told her as I patted Evan on the head. I scooped him up and held him upside down as he giggled. I put him back down and walk over to Tessa and gave her a slight hug, but nothing too sincere. Taking a seat at the table I looked over at Evan who was climbing back into his chair to draw another masterpiece. I handed the picture to Tessa and gave her a smile. 'Tessa would you mind putting this beautiful drawing on the refrigerator?' “Sure Chris.” I answered in a false cheeriness. I could tell he was only acting happy to see me for Evan which I could understand but at the same time if he wasn’t happy to be around me anymore I wondered why he wouldn’t just pack up and leave to stop leading me on this false path of hope that someday everything would be fine between us. Walking over to the refrigerator I noticed that there was no room for Evan’s picture on it nor were there any magnets left for it. All the space was occupied by pictures that Christa and Evan had drawn for me during the five month period that they lived with me. I could have easily taken down one of their pictures to make room for Evans but it was a lot harder than it seemed considering that would be like ripping their memory from my heart considering that was all I had left of them since chances of ever seeing them again seemed slim. So I handed the picture back to Chris while trying to hold back the joyfully tearful memories of Christa and Alex that I had and said, “Maybe you should hang this up in the bedroom.” I looked at Tessa with concern, and then I noticed the refrigerator was full of pictures from Alex and Christa. I looked at the picture then at Evan, who had looked up from his water colors wondering why we weren't putting his drawing on display. 'Evan, would you mind if I kept this in my room with me?' I asked him hoping to get around the decision of having to remove one of the other children's pictures. Evan just smiled at me and nodded then went back to coloring his picture, which was a house under the sun with a tree next to it. I looked back at Tessa once more and saw her depressed look. 'Evan, would you mind having a nice spot for this picture in my room please? I think the master artist should fine the perfect spot for his work.' I asked him, looking for an excuse to get him to leave so I could talk to Tessa alone. Evan agreed and took the picture from me. Tessa just took a seat in his chair and let her head fall into her arms on the table. I put my hand on her back and gently stroked her back. 'Tessa, we need to talk. With Spring Fling coming up, I think we need to try and work out these problems between us and actually start acting like a real team otherwise we'll never free you from the grasp of that marriage.' “Oh you think we should cooperate now that the tag titles are on the line. I see how it is. But when I’m feeling down because I’m married to a man I despise you want nothing to do with me.” I snapped, not wanting to talk much about Spring Fling considering before Spring Fling occurred we would first have to deal with P.W.A. in a match that would place us and Flaymin Aymin against El Diablo, Nerezza, and the man who could manipulate me in pretty much anyway possible given he had half the power to decide if I stayed a Green or not, Gaz Green. I simply sighed. This was not going to be easy for me. Tessa had once again gotten in one of her moods where no matter what I did, I was wrong. Tessa just glared at me waiting for a reply. 'Tessa, do we have to get into this right now? I really did not want get into an argument with you. All I wanted to do was try and talk with so we would work out the problems. I know you don't want to be Mrs. Tessa Green anymore and the only way that can happen right now is for us to defeat P.W.A. at Spring Fling. But you and I both know we won't be defeating anyone if we are too busy fighting with each other like little children.' I told her. “Fine spill your guts and tell me what you’ve got planned.” I sighed, not bothering to lift my head out of my arms which rested on the table. I didn’t care much for talking to him right now considering it was only now that the time for the tag titles being put on the line was approaching, that he wanted to talk. I just wanted him to hurry up and get things off his mind so that perhaps he would leave me alone and let me think. I just shook my head. This wasn't worth it right now. 'Tessa, you don't want this relationship to work. I can tell by the way you are acting like a small child. So if you don't want to seriously try and work out our problems…' my words were coming out in fits of anger. 'Then I guess I can act like a little child too.' I promptly said as I walked away leaving her alone. I marched up to my room as Evan was just exiting. I gave him a big hug. 'I love you my son but right now daddy is a little upset so can you go downstairs and finish your drawing for me and let me think?' I asked him. Evan just frowned and nodded at me. He hated to see either Tessa or I depressed but we had both been deeply depressed as of late. Evan just walked down the stairs after another hug from me. He looks over to see Tessa in his chair with her head down. “Tessa are you and Chris going to break up?” Evan questioned quietly as he tapped me on the shoulder, surely confused as to why Chris and I were fighting at the moment.
