Date: 4-25-2003 She was a classy dame with the kind of legs you’d like to take home and suck on for a day. She came to me ‘cause she knew I was the best. She had a job for me. It was going to be dangerous I knew, but something she said intrigued me. This was about to be one of the greatest adventures I’ve ever had. We find ourselves in what appears to be a run down P.I.’s office with our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne. Strange as it appears, our scene in without color. Interviewer: Without color? You mean Black and White? How ridiculous! Dan: Hey, it’s my flashback. You butt out. We watch as Dan dons his trench and fedora and heads out the front door… And thus the adventure begins…. I had my objective from the dame, but I needed to do some reconnaissance to find out the details. I needed to find out where I was headed, and in order to do that, I had to speak with the man who was in the know. The one and only Jimmy the weasel. Jimmy was an old running partner of mine back when we were in ‘the business’ together. Jimmy and I instantly clicked the day we were DP on Juli Ashton. It’s amazing how secure two heterosexual men can be when they’re sharing the same space. Anyway, after discovering that we were identical size we became instant friends. Ahh, those were good years. Jenna, Taylor, Devon, Kylie, we had ‘em all. It was going to be good to see the weasel again. “What do you mean he’s dead?!” The waitress looks at Dan with a ‘did I stutter’ look in her eyes. “Yeah, he got smoked a few months back.” “By who?” “Some guy calling himself ‘The Hedgehog.” Said there was only room for one vermin in the business.” “I needed information that only Jimmy could provide. I knew what I had to do. I had to find his killer and get to the bottom of this.” “Are you talking to yourself?” “ Oh sorry, that was supposed to be inner monologue.” “ Okay, you’ve had enough. I’m calling the bouncers.” I found myself face down in the gutter. Not a place that was new to me. I had hit rock bottom a long time ago. Back when Molly and Mikey were kidnapped. I headed to the one place where a person like ‘The Hedgehog’ could be found: The Abba Dabba. The Abba Dabba was by far the lowest class strip club in town. Even ‘Porky’s’ was a five-star establishment compared to this place. This was the type of place that old porn stars came to die. The booze was cheap and so were the dancers. The brass poles were rusted, the mirrors were cracked and all the booths were sticky. There was no VIP room like in most joints, instead the Johns were serviced right where they sat. People sometimes wondered how a place like this could remain open. “Hey Mayor!” “ Hey Dan! How’s tricks?” “I was about to ask you the same question.” That’s how. The mayor and the chief of police were treated to freebies in exchange for them looking the other way. I knew ‘The Hedgehog’ had to be here. “He’s not here.” “But I just knew he would be!” A dancer wearing nothing but a G-string grinds her pelvis in Dan’s face. “Don’t whine. It’s unbecoming. Look. He never comes here when he’s shooting. Why don’t you try over at his place.” “What? No studio?” “It is his studio. He’s a bit of an eccentric. He refuses to perform anywhere other than his own home.” “I can understand that. It’s probably a cleanliness issue.” It wasn’t a cleanliness issue. The place was a real dive. After crossing under the Police DO NOT CROSS tape, I had to make my way around an endless mine field of beer cans and underwear. I stepped over what I can only assume were dead bodies and made my way to the front doorway. I say doorway due to the fact that there was no door. The door bell, which was in the shape of a woman’s CENSORED was barely attached to the wall. I pressed the CENSORED and was not amused by the sounds of a woman CENSORED. The butler, well actually it was some random house guest, answered the doorway. After expressing my desire to meet with ‘The Hedgehog’ I was led to a surprisingly upkept office. After about three hours I finally understood that I had been abandoned here. I took it upon myself to track down ‘The Hedgehog’. I found him in the midst of shooting a scene, THE FOLLOWING DESCRIPTION OF WHAT ‘THE HEDGEHOG’ LOOKS LIKE AND WAS IN THE MIDDLE OF HAS BEEN CENSORED FOR YOUR PROTECTION. BELIEVE ME, YOU REALLY DON’T WANT TO KNOW WHAT ‘THE HEDGEHOG’ LOOKS LIKE NAKED. After his money shot, I was finally able to meet him. You can tell by my previous description that this was not a pleasant experience. Really, just take a moment to really picture that description in your mind, then you’ll understand just what I was going through. I mean, really. Just.. the mole. Ewwww… Back in the studio, our interviewer has a grim, pale look on his face. “Wow, Dan. That was truly graphic and disturbing “ Don’t I know it.” ”And what was the deal with the Basset hound?” “Sorry, man. I just can’t relive that one again. If fact, I will never tell that description again.” “It’s a good thing we were all here for it this time.” “Yeah.” Back in ‘The Hedgehog’s’ house the conversation between the two of us was starting to get very heated. After all, this was the man that whacked Jimmy. I can’t remember exactly what was said (like I have with every other conversation) but it ended in a throwing down of the gauntlet. If I could beat him at a &$%#-off then he’d tell me everything I wanted to know. After the contest was over, The Hedgehog… “ Hold up. What exactly is a &$%#-off?” “What? I thought everyone knew.” “Sorry, Dan. We don’t.” “Oh. Well, it’s like this: the competitors take a 24 hour period to see how many women they can nail. It’s a true test of stamina and intelligence. It’s quite tricky actually. You have to be quick enough to get in a good finishing number, but not quick enough to embarrass yourself.” “And this happens often?” “Sure it does. It’s how two stars settle their differences. Unfortunately, many people have succumbed to the pressure and overexerted themselves to the point of death. That’s how Jimmy went.” “So what happened next?” “Well…” ”I can’t believe you beat me!” The Hedgehog was sweaty and out of breath. “What can I say?” Dan wasn’t even breathing heavily. “I’ve been in more &$%#-offs then you can imagine. Don’t mess with the king, Baby.” “Yeah, whatever. You got lucky.” “You think 87 is luck?! Buddy, that’s all skill.” “Yeah, whatever, just ask your questions and get out of my home.” “I want to know why you whacked Jimmy.” “Like I said, there’s only room for one vermin in this business. ‘The Weasel’ wouldn’t drop his title, so I challenged him. He accepted. I of course came out strong and never let up, but I’ll give the runt his credit. He wouldn’t stop. Even after defeat was inevitable, he just kept pounding out the numbers. The last few hours, I just sat back and watched him go. He had heart. Too bad it exploded.” “He had information for me. He knew the location of a place I’m searching for. Did he happen to mention anything in your talks?” “I think I know what you’re talking about. He was talking about it in the Abba Dabba.” “Good. Give me that information.” “I don’t think I will.” It was at this point that I knew I was in trouble. This was a big place with a large number of well-built men. As soon a The Hedgehog turned around to call his buddies over, I sucker-punched him and made a break for the closest window. I thought I was going to get out scott free until I heard The Hedgehog behind me… “RELEASE THE LION!” So there I was, with my head in the mouth of this huge lion. A lion! The crazy things people will buy just because they can. As I tried to remain calm the lion’s breath was really starting to get to me. He wasn’t chewing, though, so I wasn’t complaining. I was just wondering how long I was going to be stuck here. So there I was, with a porn star in my mouth. I don’t really like the taste of these guys, especially the sweaty ones, but I needed this job. The hairy guy hired me after that incident in the pen. I was happy to be out. After all, I never really belonged there. I mean, I saw that gazelle limping and it’s my job to thin the herd you know? At least that’s what Lorne Michaels always said. Next thing I know, I felt this sharp piercing and I was out. I woke up a few days later and I was behind bars. No trial. No nothing. I don’t suppose it would’ve really mattered. The evidence was all on tape. Stupid National Geographic. Just the thought of the whole thing was really starting to piss me off. The lion’s jaws were getting tighter. Something was agitating him and it was going to be my head that he bit off. I had to figure a way out of this mess and fast. This sucker’s head was mine. I breathed deeply and suddenly realized what a mistake that was. The little bugger was sweating profusely and I just inhaled at least a gallon of it. I had to get him out of my mouth as soon as possible. The lion must’ve known somehow that he was about to get a beat down because he let me go and over the wall I went. I caught a glimpse of the lion as I was scaling back down the wall. I must’ve had something in my eye because I could swear the lion was licking the grass. “Well Dan, we’d love to hear what happened next, but we’ve run out of time. We’ll pick it up right here tomorrow.” “Sure thing.” “Until tomorrow then. I’d like to thank our studio audience and all the people watching at home. God night, America.” Fade to black
