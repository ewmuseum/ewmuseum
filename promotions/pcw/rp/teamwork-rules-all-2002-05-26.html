Teamwork Rules All

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/02 “Teamwork Stories” * Prologue * The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore meeting together in the New York City Library. The two haven’t seen each other in a rather long time, and haven’t fought together in about two months, but they decided to get together this one day to do something that was a part of their past, being storytellers. Both unemployed they had loads of time to take out of their week and prepare a story for the young after school program. When Juan and Kevin put their heads together they thought it would be best if they had a story based on teamwork. After all teamwork is the number one key to they’re winning at the IPPV. Juan and Kevin don’t see themselves as the best tag team to ever walk in PTC, but they do believe they could be the best team. It takes courage for these two men to step in the ring with well trained and furnished tag teams that are still together, but the Golden Game is such a legend that the skill between the two men never disappear. The IPPV is something that is just a day away, but for now Juan has to concentrate on becoming the cruiserweight champion once again. His number one goal is that belt and nothing will stand in the way. Juan and Kevin do their fair share of training, but they prefer more mental training for a match. To Juan a match is all about the feeling you get when you walk down to the ring. At that moment you should already know if you’d win or lose. Kevin feels it’s all about being mentally fit. You have to be just as mentally fit as physically fit. With this said both men decided they’d do a story where mind prevails over force, because in the end they feel it has. With this said the story begins, but about what? They know that the children will be bored with another story of good versus evil or a story about how they got into wrestling and how they prepare. They knew teens want to here a story about other teens and their issues. Juan and Kevin put their heads together and came up with a story of extraordinary impact. They call the story “Unity Within Diversity”. *Prologue Ends * The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore arriving at the Library. Juan takes a seat, and twiddles his fingers for a while as Kevin paces around looking for things to do. “So how has life been? I mean it’s been ages since we’ve really talked.” Juan asks being a bit quiet and shy about the issue. “I’m not going to lie, the last few months have been shitty, but I’m not one to complain.” Kevin replies telling it exactly as it is as serious as possible. “That’s cool, we’ll have time to talk a little later after this whole storytelling thing, for now I think we should go into the main room, the kids will be here any minute.” Juan marks out. The two men pick their things up and make their way into the next room. The story will be kind of difficult because both Juan and Kevin have no type of final copy; they’ll just be improvising and trying to work off one another’s work. The children enter the room loud and rowdy as most teens are. The room is split into little groups the way teenagers usually do. On one side there are the popular kids, then there is the jocks mixed around, the druggies, the smart ones, the nice group, and the all out outcasts. When Juan notices this he knows exactly what the entire plot of his story is going to be about. “I think I know how this story of ours is going to begins.” Juan whispers lightly to Kevin who is waving hello to the entering teenagers. “Good, because I have absolutely no idea on what I’m going to do.” Kevin says as the two share a laugh and wait for all of the teenagers to settle down and take their seats where they are supposed to be. The head of the after school program then gets up and makes her opening announcement to the group of teenagers. “Hello children, today we have something out of the ordinary planned for you. Instead of the usual routine we’re going to have a little storyteller session.” The children show their displeasure with the usual rude screaming and uncalled for actions. The head of the program and the other workers go around screaming at the children to calm down and be more respective. “Like I was saying before interrupted. We have a storyteller program, but it isn’t going to be anything like your usual stories. We do realize that all of you are older now and middle school to high school ages so we thought we’d get more popular teen figures to recite the story to you. Maybe this way you can all pay closer attention and learn something from the lesson.” The director of the after school program says excited to announce the storytellers. Meanwhile, the teenagers get suspicious and start to chat between one another about how it might possibly be. “I’d like to announce the two time Primetime Championship Wrestling Champions known to you all as the Golden Game. Kevin Karlos Klaymore and Juan Borro both have apartments here in New York and were nice enough to offer up their busy schedules to speak with us today.” The director announces. The children become excited and start to cheer as Juan and Kevin make their entrance. The guys especially all shout out questions. A few of them even start GOOOOOOOOOAAAAAALLLLL~! Or GOLDEN GAME! Chants. Juan and Kevin blush over the nice compliments and strike a few poses to the delight of their fans. “Hey guys, thanks for the warm welcoming. As you already know I’m Juan Borro and I’m with my former partner Kevin Klaymore. Were here in New York together getting ready for the IPPV over at PTC we’ll be competing at. We decided to tell a story about teamwork to all of you, so hopefully you’ll enjoy.” The children ignore the whole story part as they go on shouting out questions like:Hey Juan, what’s going on with PCW and why is the whole roster fired?Do you guys still hang out even though you’re no longer a tag team?Can you get us free tickets to PCW?Can you get us autographs from Duke Williams and RAH? “Okay, I guess you guys aren’t going to let us go until we answer a few of your questions, so why don’t we get started. I for one can answer the “Do Juan and I still hang out?” question. We would try, but we have been so busy lately that time is something we have none of. I’m sure in the future we’ll have much more time to just hang out like the good old days. As for what I’m going now that I’m no longer in PCW, well I’m spending a lot of time teaching younger athletes to become great wrestlers. I’m also going back to get my Masters in school.” Kevin Klaymore announces without even one pause. “Yea, as for the other questions, I think I can answer them pretty quickly. The whole PCW issue is something I know nothing about. I know the Phantom Booker wasn’t happy about the way athletes were using their contracts so I guess he feels this is the best thing to do. II have returned, but who knows what will happen next. No, I cannot get you free tickets, but you can purchase them from me for the mere price of two hundred dollars. And hell no I will not get you Duke Williams and RAH's autographs. Don't you want an autograph from someone talented like muah. Well if you add on another hundred dollars to the tickets than I guess it’s fine. What do you expect I was unemployed for a whole two weeks?” Juan says as him and the kids share a laugh. Kevin comes over and whispers to Juan. 'You're still the ninth richest man in Portugal.' 'They don't know that.' Juan responds. Juan gets ready to begin, but before he can the scene fades to black.
