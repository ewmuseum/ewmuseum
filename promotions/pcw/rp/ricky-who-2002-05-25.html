Ricky Who?

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/25/02 Time: 8:17amPlace: Camden, New Jersey *Scene opens up, where Ricky Thunder is seen sitting on a bus stop bench, watching the traffic pass himby. There is very little grass and the smog from the surrounding cities have completely engulfed this quiettown. The #12 bus pulls up to the stop, and the flashing sign reads Downtown / 31st St. Ricky boards thebus and drops his toll into the dispenser, before making his way to the back and taking a seat. He re-situates his posture as the seats are the unpadded blue plastic ones. He begins thinking about the eventsthat took place at Fusion.* Ricky: Well, not exactly the impact I had hoped to make, but I'm sure the message was received. Now Iam sure that this UA, will claim we are all the Booker's lackey's and what not, but I will assure you this. Iplay lackey for nobody. I was paid to do a job, so I did the job. That job however is something that Ithoroughly enjoyed doing. I couldn't have wanted to laugh any harder, than when The Supreme Machine,my former tag partner in CCW, leveled Tyler Nelson with that shot to the jaw. I know he has beenwanting to do that for a long time now. However in the entire group, there was one person in particular,that I cared less for, than even Tyler Nelson, and that person was none other than Mark Makonie. *Ricky closes his eyes, as he remembers watching from backstage, the nazi symbol reflecting off the armoff of Makonie. The way Shabazz and Makonie wanted to get at each other. You could see it in both oftheir eyes. Ricky opens his eyes again, as a small smile comes across his face* Ricky: I didn't get a chance to focus too much on one individual on Fusion, however our time will come. The time when I destroy you for just being you. The time when I decide to show you what a true athlete ismade of. I will be watching your every move Makonie. You should know that when and if I decide tostrike, you will feel the presence of death. You will feel as though you are shrouded by these feelings ofdeath. That there is no way for you to escape it. All you will be begging for, is to survive it. Hoping thatthis feeling is wrong, hoping that it will not leave your lifeless carcass lying for the next man to spit on. Iwarn you Makonie, I will not refrain from trying to be the cause of your last breath, and if need be, IWILL be the cause of it. *Ricky reaches out and pulls the small rope that leads to the chime, that alerts the bus driver to stop. Hegets to his feet and move towards the front of the bus. As the bus moves to the curb the doors slide openand Ricky exits onto the sidewalk. He steps away from the bus and begins walking towards a buildingthat is basically encased in glass. The building is nothing but windows, aside from where the floor beamssplit them. Ricky enters the building, and notices the fine oak furniture lining the lobby. He makes hisway to the counter off to the right of the elevators, which are located on the far wall, and rings the smallbell on the countertop. From a partially closed door emerges a familiar looking man. He is about 35 yearsof age, and has a middle aged figure. His hair slightly thinned, but none the less, wearing a smile.* Man: Ricky!! How are ya boy!? Ricky: Hey uncle Tony, how are ya? UT: Not bad. Just enjoying my job as always. Of course, it may not be exciting as what your doing, butthat's ok, I don't think I could make it in CCW anyhow. Ricky: Oh you didn't hear uncle Tony? UT: Hear what? *The smile on Ricky's uncles face fades away into a look of concern. He had several times before warnedhim about wrestling after his injury when he was younger. He immediately started scanning Ricky,looking for casts or scars, indicating some type of injury.* Ricky: Don't worry uncle Tony, I am ok. I am still wrestling. UT: Not for CCW? That was your dream! To succeed where you brother had succeeded. *The slight smile on Ricky's face disappears. A blank expression replaces it, with Ricky eyes burning ashe stares at his uncle. Not speaking, but the anymosity is there.* UT: Ricky? You ok? Ricky: Yeah I'm fine. I am just tired of hearing about Shane and CCW. He won the title. True. He beatThe Supreme Machine for the title. True. He LOST the title. True. You see, when I first signed withCCW, nobody knew me. Nobody had a clue I was related to Thunder. Everyone may have thought, oh,just a coincidence, but no. They didn't need to know. Then SuMa came around and everyone found out. From that day forth, it didn't matter that I was 3-0, I was known as a 'shoe-in'. UT: Hey. Look, I didn't mean to bring up any hard memories for you. I was just shocked to hear youweren't in CCW anymore. Ricky: Well, it is something that people will have to deal with. I am gone. I'm not going back. Mybrother is dead, and I still couldn't get out of his shadow. My record upon leaving CCW contained oneloss. A disqualification, because Mr. Gobstopper felt the urge to interject himself into my match. I haveNEVER been pinned. EVER! My singles record, was 8-1 upon leaving. It would have been 9-0, hadGobber minded his own business. That wasn't enough. I set out at Group Therapy, in the King ofExtreme. I was beaten with boards, bats, both wrapped in razor wire. Powerbombed through tables, andspeared through 4x8 plate glass walls. That wasn't enough. I beat John Coens in a cage, for the CCWIndependent Title. John Coens has held EVERY title in CCW. He could be a future hall of famer, and Ibeat him. One on one, I beat him. That wasn't enough. No matter what I did in CCW, it wasn't goodenough. Everyone, compared me to Thunder. Everyone says I was only there because he was a legend. Ipaved my own way dammit! UT: Ricky. Go have a seat in the lobby. I will be getting off work in about 20 minutes. So go have a seatand grab a cup of coffee, I will be out in a bit. We can go have dinner and discuss things. I didn't meanto offend you by bringing up Shane, but Ricky, you have to acknowledge that he is your brother, andpeople did respect him before his death. Ricky: I know Tony. I'm sorry, I didn't mean to snap at you, but it just gets so damn frustrating. It seemseverywhere I go, everyone mentions Thunder, everyone accuses me of riding Thunder's coat tails intofame. I did this on my own. I didn't list Thunder on my application. Thunder didn't tell CCW to call me. Nobody told them I was his little brother when they came recruiting me. I have damn near broken myneck for this sport. The sport I love. The sport I have always thrived to be a part of. Only to be shotdown, by being told I am living in my dead brothers shadow. Do you know how it feels to live in a deadman's shadow? Tony: Well like I said Ricky, go have a seat, we will go grab dinner and discuss this whole situation. Probably another 15 minutes, unless I can get out of here early. *Ricky turns to where one of the leather chairs are on the near wall, he heads over to and takes a seatpicking up the latest edition of Wrestling Monthly from the magazine rack. He carries it to the seat withhim and as he sits down he glances at the cover of the magazine.* Supreme Machine and Thunder to PCW?Thunder's little brother leads his family name to PCW
