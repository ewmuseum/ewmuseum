The Spanish Inquisition! (Part 4)

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/02 (OOC-this is a continuation of an rp series I began in RUSH, The Spanish Inquisition! is a group of followers led by Stimpo, he doesn't have many members yet but he's planning to get members to prove that latinos, when in great numbers can in fact impact society, whether in a good way, or an idiotic way. This is part 4 of the series, you can read 1, 2 and 3 at the RUSH site, that is...if it ever works lol) Seven fifty nine eastern time. In a shabby house, creeking in every movement within it forms a creeking sound as Stimpo sits twiddling his thumbs seemingly awaiting someone to show up for his first Spanish Inquisition! meeting. Having already met with one man, and perhaps others not shown on television, Stimpo waits and waits until.... DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG Eight o' clock chimed as Stimpo jumped up scared out of his wits. He calmed down but jumped up and shrieked like a woman in fear as the door knocker sounded through the house. Stimpo: *exhales* The time has come. Stimpo takes a big breath and then walks to the door, opening it with a smile. Stimpo: Hel---huh? Fog rested outside making the air feel eerie like Halloween. Stimpo was confused to see that no one was at the door until he looked down. Stimpo: MY LATINO @$@#%@ GOD! On Stimpo's doorstep lay a terrible sight to see....a horror to all latinos everywhere....in a bloodied basket lay... Stimpo: *shudders, crying* A DEAD CHUIWAWA!??! NOOOOOO!!!!!!!!! *suddenly back to normal* Hey look there's a note. Stimpo calmly takes out a note from the basket on the doorstep, opens the folded piece of paper. Written in magazine lettering, the words are spelled out like so: The Latinos are not safe from us Monsiouer Ztimpo. If you dare to defy us, we'll have to use our...zecret weapon.Don't anger us Monsiouer Ztimpo, Zi French will show no mercy. Stimpo: Who? Stimpo sees a man running up and down the sidewalk wearing French clothing yell out in the distance: Man: THE FRENCH YOU FOOL!!!!! Stimpo: Thanks! Confused, Stimpo turns around leaving the dead chuiwawa outside and shuts the door. Stimpo ponders on this letter as he tosses it on his desk he was waiting on earlier. Then, Stimpo shrieks once again in a womanly voice as the sound of the door knocker sounds once again. Stimpo: About time! Necesito un reloj para mis amigos...estupidos. Stimpo mumbles the spanish as he heads towards the door, answering the door he finds that a man wearing a mask with puffy blonde hair is waiting outside. Stimpo: Come on in! The meeting is about to start. Stimpo opens the door to let the man in. Stimpo: How are you Mullet? Mullet: Fine thank you, where is everyone? Stimpo: Umm...erm....they're...well...on vacation! Mullet: Vacation? Stimpo: YES! Mullet: Where? Stimpo: No where. Mullet: Then how can they b-- Stimpo: HUSH! Another door knocker sounds as Stimpo once again shrieks freaking out Mullet as he heads toward the desk with the letter. Stimpo answers the door to find a woman spinning repeatedly around and around wearing a cape and a steel plated bra. Stimpo: Wonder Woman? Stimpo shakes his head as he answers the door letting this woman in. Dizzily, the woman staggers inside out of breath introducing herself. Woman: My name's...my name... The woman collapses on the floor exhausted and falls asleep. Stimpo looks up at the camera with a 'you gotta be kidding me' look. Stimpo: Well, this is going quite well! I have a man with a Mullet and a mask, a crazed woman who likes to spin and DEAD CHUIWAWA and a man in trouble with the French trying to form a Spanish Inquisition! WHAT IS A LATINO TO DO??!!?!? Mullet: Hey Stimpo, what's this? Stimpo looks up to see that Mullet is carrying the letter from the French. Stimpo walks over to Mullet with a gentle smile, takes the letter, stares at it evily and begins to speak. Stimpo: That Mullet...is the enemy.
