The Life of a Trainer

4 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-4-03 The train has been long and hard. Each day felt like a week. Each week felt like a month. It dragged on seemingly forever. The screams of pain fulled the house. It echoed off the walls and ran down the halls. Blood painted the ground. Yet within the blood, the screams, and the pain there was a champion being made. The wrestling sport was being getting a new star even though know one knew it. But before he could reach stardom he had to endure the hellish training that layed before him. The one named Chris Storm was being forced to suffer unike any human should. And the evil behind it was his mentor Genesis. The cold and cruel bastard that had made a name for himself in smaller leagues in wrestling. It was the fame that made Chris Storm suck all the punishment up. He knew if he just took it all and survived it that he could be as great as Genesis was known to be. And so Chris walked each day. His body slowly being broken down and his mind being raped by the harsh conditions he was having to live in. If he had to describe what it was like in one word he would have to say 'hell'. Each day it was the same thing. Storm would wake up on the cold mat. As soon as he woke he would have to start doing push ups, sit ups, and running. His body would hunger for food. Yet he was denied it until he was able to finish his morning training. If he couldn't finish it quick enough? He went without food. After breakfast it was time for his training with Genesis in the ring. It didn't matter how tired of weak Storm was. He still had to do it. If he wasn't strong enough then he bleed. And so the mat was covered in the blood of Storm. Or as Genesis put it, 'the blood of the weak'. After the live training Storm was allowed to rest. The rest was much enjoyed but it never lasted very long. Then after the short rest Storm had to let his body be slammed against the mat relentlessly. Doing front flips off top rope onto his back. Backflips off top rope onto him stomach. Hitting the ropes as hard as he could. Running into the turnbuckle full force over and over again. Then if he was still able to stand after all of it, he was allowed a lunch break. Lunch passes and he either ate or he didn't. It didn't matter if he ate breakfast or not either. With lunch break passed he was now allowed to learn technical wrestling. He had to push his body as hard as he could during this period so he would be sure he got in as many moves as he could. Storm knew the only way he would be able to survive the live training was to learn as many moves as he could in technical training. Technical training passed and it was time for Storm to build up his lungs. Was he allowed to run around beautiful New Orleans while he built up his lungs? No, he was only allowed to run in place inside that ring. For hours he had to run in place staring at the same ring he's been in all day long. Then after running he would be allowed dinner followed by more live training then sleep. That was his day. Each day, every day. For almost a year he did this. He survived his hell hole. Sleep was rare as it was near impossible to sleep in such horrible conditions on the cold, hard mat. Not even allowed a bed or covers to use. Food was a treat for him when he got it. The left overs from Genesis' meal is what he got. He didn't care though. The idea of actually putting food in his stomach was enough for him to over look the fact that it was merely the food Genesis didn't want. Yet dispite all the horrible moments and the blood he lost, Storm knew it would only make him a better wrestler. There was no way anyone else was allowing themselves to be put through this just to be the best. But maybe they did. Maybe this is what all wrestlers had to go through in order to be the best. So he would push himself harder then they could. So with every sit up, push up, and pull up and gave that extra inch of push. That little more power behind it. When he hit the bags he made sure he saw the enemy. The anger building up inside of him letting him release his anger onto it. The beatings he took during live training was just the small price he was paying to be a star. The blood lost would prove his loyality to the sport. He would be the best. He would be unstoppable. It was his mission in life. He would make it to the top no matter what the cost was. Heart, soul, mind, and body. All was just tools to be used to get what he wanted out of life. Storm stood in front of the mirror near the ring. Genesis allowed him to have it so he could look at himself in his miserable state. The image would burn in his mind and be a reminder of what it looks like to be a nothing. His face was beating and brusied. Dried up blood tipped his blond hair. His lip was swollen and his clothes was torn and bloody. He couldn't even stand up straight in front of the mirror. His body was so weak he had to stand with a limp. He tries to straight out so he can get an idea of his size but the pain cripples him back to leaning over. He started to get mad at his disability. The weakness he showed to himself. He passes a hand on the mirror to support himself. His head begins to throb in pain. With his other hand his places it on his head where it hurts. His knees get weak as the pain gets more intense. 'Weakness.....look at you. Your pathetic. You can't even stand up straight. So weak, so sad. Yet you think you can be successful? Go home!' Storm lifts his head up and looks around. 'Whooo...' his voice shakes, 'who's there?' he manages to get out. Looking around he can't see anyone. But who is talking to him? 'You cry yourself to sleep every night. I see you doing it.' He starts to get scared now. 'Who are you? How do you know that?' he ask, looking around hoping to find someone, anyone there. Yet he can not find the source of the taunting. 'Stop showing your weakness! Look at you scared! What is the matter with you. How can you be so pathetic. You make me sick. Someone like you being able to be part of something so great. You are suppose to be the perfect warrior! Yet you cry like a little baby. Where is that ruthless killer? Where is the man that wants to stand on top of the mountain?' Tears start to trickle down his face. His chest heaves in and out as he becomes angry. At the voice or at himself? The veins begin to stick out more as the blood is flowing. Blood rolls down the mirror as the hand on it turns crimson from the blood. It's almost as if the blood is pouring out through his pores. More and more joins the puddle on the ground under the mirror. Faster and faster it flows from his body. Storm's body starts to straighten out. He stands up completely straight. His hand off the mirror and at his side. The blood drips off his hand and onto the floor. A bloody hand print left on the mirror. 'I am not pathetic! I'm not weak. I am the Perfect Storm. I'll show each and everyone of you. I'll prove that no force in wrestling will ever be able to stand against the tides I'll bring. One by one their blood will be split and I will step closer and closer to the top of the mountain! No one can nor will stop me. I will break anyone that gets in my way. I will write my name in blood on the mat and the people will know I have been there.' Storm yells out. His voice cracking with anger and hate. A sick and twisted laugher breaks out in the empty room. Storm looks around, his eyes red with anger. He grabs the first thing he can find. A black steel chair leaning up against the ring. He closes his eyes and swings the chair when he thinks the sound is coming from, not sure as the laugher seems to be coming from everywheres. Crack! He opens his eyes to see the body of Genesis laying on the ground. The black chair now dashed with crimson blood. Genesis forehead is split open. Storm just smirks down at his fallen victim and throws the chair next to the motionless body. Storm steps on his chest and walks off. He closes his eyes again. His eyes slowly open. Chris looks around. He's inside his apartment. He quickly sits up in his bed. 'Was it all a dream' he ask himself. His body is sweaty. His heart is racing. Chris leans forward and rubs his forehead. 'What is going on? How long have I been asleep?' he ask himself. He can hear someone at the door. Chris gets excited. He nearly jumps out of the bed and opens it. He's still in his boxers. 'Tessa!' he shouts as he opens the door. Yet no one is there. Then he feels someone tapping him. He looks down to see it was Alex at the door. The little ten year old had been standing there the whole time and Chris had simply overlooked him. He smiled at Alex and sat back down on the edge of the bed. Alex just walked over to the bed and sat down next to him. 'Chris, do you think we'll ever seen Tessa or Christa again?' the little one asked. Simply frowning, Chris thought about that. Right now things didn't seem to positive between himself and Tessa. He had accidently blasted her with a chair shot to the head. Not to mention their already rocky relationship. If it was going to be saved one of them was going to need to do something and right now they were both being stubborn. Chris just looked at Alex. He was still waiting for a reply. 'Don't worry Alex, everything will be alright. We just need to wait.' Chris tells him. Not sure if everything will be alright. But another day rolls on and Chris must get ready again. For he must face them all once more.
