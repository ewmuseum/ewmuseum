Title: Against the Authority- Part 4 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: Today A tale of two locations.... In Europe, we have a Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, fresh off a victory with his tag team partner, Teresa Tomas. In Salt Lake City, we have The HIT's class, who, on Tuesday, were scheduled to run a protest against the school. Before he left for Turmoil, Trapezoid promised he would bring his kids in to Fusion, to watch him capture the Television Championship. And so, with the money he has saved from NOT PAYING TAXES, The HIT has flown his class into London, England, where they have taken over a wing of a hotel. All are happily gathered around the television to watch a surprise the kids brought. And no, it's not porn.. The HIT: You mean, you got the protest ON TAPE?! ::Trapezoid grins, looking around at his students. They all nod and smile demonically, one of them reaching into his backpack, and pulling out the tape. Wordlessly, they pass it amongst one another, and then slide it into the VCR The HIT has illegally hooked up.:: The HIT: This is gonna be some goooooooood viewing. Popcorn, anyone? :HIT passes around the popcorn, as the camera begins rolling, its film grainy... ::A group of rowdy kids sit in a classroom, as a teacher slowly walks in...:: Teacher: Okay kids, we've got a treat for you today.....since Mr. Trapezoid isn't here-- Jimmy: Why isn't Mr. Trapezoid here? Teacher: Because, he has a match! Susan: But he didn't have to leave yet. You all made him. Teacher: Well, errr, we-- John: Were afraid the next PCW Television Champion was going to kick your ass and make you cry? Teacher: I---I.....I DON'T HAVE TO LISTEN TO YOU BRATS! Anyway, its time to meet your new principal, Mrs. Susorch...come on in? ::The new principal, Mrs, Susorch walks in, to great and tremendous apathy from the students.:: The HIT: Dear God..that woman is just downright hideous. Jimmy: Yeah....although, your partner isn't.. The HIT: Oh, you mean Miss Tomas.....as I've already told you, we are partners. Nothing more. Susan: Oh, come on....you're the HIT! The man who's invented more mathematical references for genetalia than anyone! Jason: He's probably the ONLY man who's invented mathematical references for genetalia... The HIT: Duly noted. Drop the class. Jason: Sorry, but anyway.....seriously man! It's been a while since you've bisected a parabola! The HIT: Yeah, tell me about it...but still! She's my tag team partner! A co-worker! There shall be no use of the cylinderical object while I am PARTNERS with the woman! Kevin: Come on! She's attractive enough for you... The HIT: And I'm a freak who has something like four scars in his face, a nice amount of scar tissue, and TEACHES MATHEMATICS! The only girl that's ever been attracted to me is Amber, and she's in a mental institution right now! Plus, I have to fight her on Fusion! Jimmy: Well, that's how then! Just get in a situation where you have to touch her! The HIT: You're all perverts! I think the F.U.C.K. method's worked TOO well. Anyway, in that match, my focus is on becoming TV CHAMPION! And, that's not going to be easy. Venneman Pendragon...reigning TV champ, and a guy who outweighs me by enough to make my moveset difficult to use. A man in the mold of the Arthurian legend is not easy to beat in the least. Yet, yonder Pendragon has not faced a man with my abilities...a man with as many wrestling techniques as Sir Lancelot has manuevers with Excalibur..and so, like the mythic dragons of yore...Venneman shall fall before the blade that is Parallel Bases! ::Trapezoid smiles, about to press play, before another question stops him.: Susan: What about Rush Mason? The HIT: Ah, the voodoo warrior...and the man with the grudge against Pendragon. Rush, if you wish to take the Television title from around Pendragon's waist, I would advise thee.....DO NOT IGNORE THE TRAPEZOID! Mason, your voodoo charms and enchantments will not save you from the unmitigated might of my moveset! You may possess the voodoo charms, but I possess the magic of skills....and so Mason, Venneman, and everyone else......I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS! Jimmy: Good! Now, let's finish watching the video!! The HIT: YEAH! PASS THE POPCORN! Fade to black. ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
