A little break

Date: not entered The world still turned. Breath still ran through her lungs. She was still not American. Things weren’t all that bad on reflection. Sure, she had lost the one person whom she had ever felt close to, the only person she had been able to draw close to her without wanting to tear of their limbs and beat them to death with them. Jade was long gone now, and to be honest it may well have been a good thing in some oddity or another. Her legs dangled precariously from the cold and hard stone windowsill and out into the warm, still air. She kicked out randomly at nothing, letting her feet swing to and fro whilst still putting in the effort to stop them colliding with the old red brick of the wall. Her head was buried in a completely different form of cloud as thoughts ran around like kids on their school sports day. Closing her deep hazel eyes to the bright sun, she cocked her head to the side so it could rest softly on the brown plastic of the window frame so as not to have to hold it upright any more. She had been there for some time, just sitting and staring out of the window into the oak forest ever growing to the south of her mighty house. She found it quite relaxing, and the ability to scour and cleanse her mind of the impurity of confusion was very appealing to the young English woman, and she had found no better place than this so far. Her mind buzzed and twirled like some metaphysical maelstrom as the turmoil of her thoughts battled to organise themselves into the neat and tidy outlook she usually kept in her head. She liked things to make sense and also to be filed neatly so if she ever got bored she would just be able to find something to think about without ‘removing the ties’ so to speak. She had found a small amount of solace in the company of Tessa Windsor, whom she may well have previously despised, but things were different now, as she was on her own, and being on her own made her seem as if she needed to speak to someone. She had put a lot of thought into this. She was surrounded by morons, misogynists, schizophrenics and creatures that may or may not exist. It was hard to choose someone who you could befriend from that list, but Tessa was clearly the friendliest person around, and Miss Moonlight the most loyal. She was drawing in their trust, and strangely enough, they too were drawing in hers. She had found it hard to trust people, especially since Jade had disappeared on Lacuna and left her alone again, just as everyone else she had ever gotten close to had done. It had messed her up, but not now. She was up and running, grabbing at every opportunity. She was becoming positive! Or so she told herself. It was a way to keep her mind of how she really felt, deep down inside. She was empty, as if something had been taken from her and left out on purpose. She needed a break, and this airship thing was one that presented itself at just around the right time. A working holiday would be an interesting break for her, and she could do just about anything she wanted to from there. She had even tried to secure a room for her servants, but had failed as apparently the guests and fans were given priority over the staff of staff. Getting away, having a few matches, it all sounded great. There was nothing that really go wrong up there. She could even drool over famous people. It all sounded fine to her.
