Date: 4-4-02 A light is on, hovering over a desk in a underground office somewhere, a smallish girl is hunched over. Her black hair is less shiny then it was at the same point seven years ago. The date is March 25th, 2009... it has been almost Seven years since the antichrist rose in DarkStar's body, he has done many things both inside and outside of the wrestling ring since then. Almost single handidly bringing PCW and PTC together as one. It has been both a saving and damning performance by Lucifer... But here we stand... it is almost time to end his reign in PCW... The few of us that didnt fall victim to his little charade realized what was going on, and we tried to fight back, we have been harboring our aggression to this point, and are now ready to release it in one large explosion of liberation from the mark of the beast. Diary, this may be my last entry, and to whoever finds this... know that the cause will be avenged, freedom of our planet, freedom of my soul... and freedom... freedom of my lover. Goodbye, maybe. Talena. Alot of time has passed since they put the plan into motion. Since then, the PTC swallowed wrestling whole, creating a Global Network of Federations all under one ruler.... DarkStar. Lucifer. In that time, Talena and the few remaining Tribulation Wrestlers bided their time. They waited until they couldnt wait anymore... That moment was now. 'DarkStar' was asking that everyone renew their contracts, under the new contract you can never be released, no incentives, nothing special. This is signing away their souls, literally. The few that stood against him left, to the outskirts of Pro Wrestling. CZW. But even they couldn't hide there. So here they are, ready for one last volley against the Antichrist, against Lucifer, to pull the world back into place. This is the time.
