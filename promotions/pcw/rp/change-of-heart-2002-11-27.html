Change of Heart

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27th, 2002 November 27th, 2002Amarillo, Texas9:21 a.m. I could stand the thought of people anymore. Just the look or the pose of them turned me off, like a foul smelling egg that was left on the counter to long. They were all just horrible to look at, but its not like I had much of a choice now is it. The ruthless feelings these people hold so dear to them self will be there down fall. Each one of them has no choice but to play their role or suffer an UN timely fate. Vilas continued on his path towards his true calling. So far his American adventure was nothing like he had planned or hoped for. To him, the small mindless programming and business was just another piece to the puzzle, showing that America was yet just another country full of greed and hate. Over charging prices and storeowners whom “play the game” and barter their way to the top of the economy, well what was left of it anyway. If anyone had ever imagined Vilas would be here, most would just laugh in your face. It’s where everything changed for Vilas. He went from someone who cared to a man in deep regret. It’s not that he regretted being alive, but the simple facts of regretting to ever come to this small country or even sett foot on this soil. Now Vilas questions everything, and what isn’t questioned is already answered. Just look at this place. War on every front and an economy that is a shame to be apart of. How can this country even get by with killing each other off first? Simple answer is they destroy what they do not know. If something brings fear to their eyes, they whip it out before it has the chance to grow. It happened in the wars and it’s going on now. God blesses this country all right, because they sure as hell need it. Vilas kept his eyes on his target, which would be Romantico later that night. But to Vilas, Romantico was a key figure in this world. Not because he was some superstar in a greedy corporation, and surely not because he is well known by all. But for the simple fact that he represented everyone that ever set foot into Primetime. They all came for the same reason. Money… Respect… Power… They didn’t come here to entertain, and they sure as hell didn’t come to please anyone but them self. They came to make them self-a name off of others expense. They actually believe anyone gives any thought to what they actually do. To them it’s just a show, but to all the people who put on the show it’s more of a life that will reward in the end. Lets face it hard work and determination will get you know where fast. You have to be just as ruthless and just as greedy as the next guy, and that means breaking a few rules, bending the line in which the game is played on and stepping up the level of play to an area of UN-charted waters. There are no prizes for second and the strongest never survive. It’s just the matter of who can become something much bigger then what they are. “Vilas are you ready for tonight,” Vilas spoke to himself smashing his hands together on his forehead, trying to get the blood flow moving. I believe I am. There it no turning back now. Only one way in and one way out in the road of success. “Remember, intelligence over strength. Brains over browns. There is no turning back now. There is only one way.” Romantico better re-think his strategy fast, because this is not the same person as before. He has no idea who he is up against and the fact still remains he knows nothing about me. Romantico will die just like the others, in a world of greed and false hopes and desires. He better change his heart now, while his mind is still pure. But if he leaves it off till the very end, THERE IS NO WAY OUT!
