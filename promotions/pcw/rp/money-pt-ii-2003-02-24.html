Money Pt. II

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24 Feb 03 Jade rolled through the poorly covered vent and found her hanging 40 feet in the air with one hand held tightly on the edge. “Aaaw crap.” She squeals as she manages to clasp her other hand securely onto the vent. “This wasn’t in the blue prints.” Jade pulls herself up and slides back into the vent. “It took me thirty minutes to get myself here from the last junction in these damned pipes; it is getting awful cold in here. Whoever did this job really didn’t think it through. However much equipment they do have.” Amateurs really pissed her off. She grunted as she shuffled herself backwards through the network of pipes. -[Fade]- Jade finally found where she went wrong and took that turn. The tunnel which she went down seemed to be getting slimmer and slimmer, constricting her space until she struggled to breathe. What had kept her going was the thought of the money she’d receive from fencing it. She thought about the bills that would disappear from one night of good hard work. Still the tunnel got smaller yet she struggled on seeing the distorted light at the end of the tunnel. Jade reached out to grab the bars and does not reach it. She shuffles her body along a little further and something begins to creak. Jade tries to move onto her side to reach her other arm out and grab the bar but the shaft gives way and she falls the twenty feet to the floor below. Shadows are created on Jade’s body from the bright lights as three men stand above the fallen Jade. Jade is out cold as the three muscled men drag her into another room. Jade’s head is slumped into her chest. Her eyes are blindfolded and a small tub of what is presumed to be smelling salts is opened up beneath her she wakes up with a shiver up her spine and grunts. “Baby Face!” She refers to Carys. “Give me another hour. What time is it? Hey, who turned out the lights?” She is clearly disorientated. First she tries to move her hands to move the foul smelling substance away from her face but her hands are tied below the chair. Without thinking Jade kicks her legs out and falls backwards as she does so her head snaps back and it thuds against the polished mosaic flooring. “Ouch.” Jade mutters again as she loses consciousness. There was something comforting for Jade about being unconscious. Perhaps it was the fact that there was a lack of sensible thought running through her mind. It was either that or a searing pain which resembled a knife being forced through her forehead. When she had her silly and imaginative thoughts she did not have to worry about bills, money, heists, Carys, the club, work, all things like that which could really bring a girl down into the gutter. Jade was slightly panicked by the falling and darkness which clouded her thoughts when she awoke. Jade spluttered back to consciousness, gladly before they could waft that evil devil powder up her nose again. “Where am I? Oh here. Please don’t tell me I was caught by the amateurs.” The silence lingered in the room. “Oh great, lets finish my day off perfectly with a loss of earnings. OK guys you can let me go now! Joke is over I’m sure we are both here for different items exactly.” One of the figures pulled off her blindfold and her eyes close slightly as she gets used to the light. Jade pulls at her hands. “What about these? I have an ill hand you know?” The man uses the back of his hand to beat her delicate face into disarray. Jade grins showing the white of her teeth and the shiny jewel glued to her teeth, all tainted with the blood of her mouth. She hocks back harshly and spits her blood into her face. “You filthy little whore.” He hisses as he reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a flip knife. “Little whore’s should know their place. I’m going to be the one to teach you…” He holds onto the last word waiting to know her name. Jade turns her head and lowers it to the ground. “I like to know a whore’s name before I take her to school.” His hand squeezes her mouth. A tear trickles down her cheek as her cheeks hunch up. “Tell me your name!” He pushes her face backwards before beating it again. Blood spills from her eyebrow and she whimpers holding back a full blown cry. “Jade. Miserable woman beater! My name is Jade.” Through the hole in his henchman’s balaclava his eyes widen. He pulls his boss backwards and whispers into his ear. Their leader shakes his head and gives a brief look backwards to Jade who is fighting to keep her head straight and her eye sight in check, she desperately wants to go back to sleep. The man turns back to Jade and grabs her arms, he makes her flex and her fine toned muscles refine themselves in her skin. “Almost have a life of their own! Don’t they?” She jeered at the scrawny man. He snuffed her and turned his cheek. “Wrestling is just fake guys.” He bellowed to his friends. They laughed and Jade giggled at him. He turned on his heel and got back in her face. “Something funny whore? Who said you can laugh?” Jade began laughing from the very pit of her stomach her mouth opened wide and her throat chugged up and down. “You know what’s funny?” He looked mystified. “No? I didn’t really expect more from you, I guess. While you seemed so caught up convincing yourself that I could not knock the skin off a bowl of warm semolina pudding, I took of my bonds.” Jade snorted as her foot travelled up his inside leg, faster than anyone would like as she hit the jack pot. He flinched upwards and she planted her sore forehead on the nose of the person who constrained her. The two men who were with him run at her and she sits backwards before she falls she plants her feet into their chins and steadies herself back down with the force of the kick. Jade stood up and fell back down as their leader kicked Jade in the shins. Jade rolled over onto her back and tried stamping on the floor. The chief moved his legs and rolls backwards to his feet. His two henchmen are out cold on the floor. Jade stumbles to her feet and wipes her head with her sore wrists. “This is the part where we go to class then?” Jade demanded an answer. The man nodded and pulled of his balaclava. His face dripped with sweat, flushed. “Yip…Mani! You motherfucker! Don’t you ever learn? The days you spent in a coma last time were not good enough for you then?” She shrugged, this was another problem added to her list. “Missing your club yet cousin?” He lunged forward with a huge fist into the air where Jade’s head did exist. “This is not going to be any walk in the park like last time Jade.” He warned before Jade placed her fist in his kidney. He laughed and span back around and located his knee into her stomach. “Me and the boys are coming to take back everything. That is including the club. If I feel like it I may get a taste of your baby face.” Jade made a big mistake and got emotionally involved in this fight, he touched a raw nerve when he mentioned her friend. She tried to put her thumbs into his eyes and he bent down to his knees. In the confusion Jade did not hear the bang of the bullet flying into her cousin’s shin. He fell on the floor, a pool of his blood falling around him. “Bitch.” Jade looked up dazzled and Nathaniel stood with his legs spread and a silver handgun pointing at where Mani stood. “Nathaniel!” She shrieked as she fell to her knees, she was hurt not physically, but emotionally. She began to fiddle through her cousins pockets and pulled out a velvet draw string bag. She placed it in her pocket and looked hatefully at her relative, first cousin. She hated the threat she was given and it worried her so. Nathaniel put the gun in his pocket and ran to Jade. He lifted her up and simply took her back through the fire exit. The alarms were ringing out loud. Soon all of the alarms in the building would be activated summoning the police, fire service and the ambulance. All were adequate to take care of her cousin. Jade fell asleep in Nathaniel’s arms as he headed for the Chevette waiting outside. -[Fade]- Jade woke up with her head rested in her arms. She was sat in a luxurious leather recliner and she coughed as the smoke from the cigar the blonde haired man was smoking. Jade lifted her head and covered her mouth. Nathaniel was sat on a chair running hid fingers through Jade’s accounts. He looks to be in a terrible state as he rubs his hands through his hair. “What’s the matter Nathaniel? Are you smoking again? Something is wrong and I know it. Why don’t you just spit it out?” Jade didn’t even give him chance to speak, as soon as she felt the velvet bag missing from her pocket she began getting a little vexed about it all. “It’s bad honey. Nothing adds up, it is all out of hand. And then…” He holds up the velvet bag and empties the contents onto the table. All there was in the bag was a few stones, not your expensive kind, more of a pebble appearance. Jade slumps her head into the table as little pools of water form onto the pine of the table. She slams her fists into the table and pushes herself against the wall, her back away from the man sat on the table. “There is one answer to this whole problem Jade dear.” Jade turns in the chair and slouches herself against the back of it. Her eyebrows raised she attempts to pull herself up. However she ends up back in the chair, she feels far too delicate to play games. “What’s that?” Jade pondered what the response would be. “Your profession. What you are good at honey. Fighting, wrestling perhaps?” Jade looked a little satisfied compared to the out of this world thoughts she had running through her head. Gymnast, hooker, pimpette, car jacker, anything there she was capable of but the sensible thing came from his mouth, gladly. “Yeah! Wrestling! That sounds like a fantastic idea. I know exactly what I have to do and now all I have to do is go and ring Callisto Bane up and I can be on my way. Who knows? I may even be booked for the next show on Thursday. Yeah a PPV return would be fantastic. Getting to work with everybody again would be good for me.” Jade picked the phone form its place and brings it over to the table. She picks up the phone and rings Bane’s number. Nathaniel shakes his head and slams his fingers down on the hook, the phone is put down. “What are you doing? DWO must be the place for me right?” “No! Not DWO that is small fry sweetheart! The money could not keep you afloat for three weeks, especially the way you spend. Hmm, I have an idea.” He pulls out a black book from his other inside coat pocket. He fingers through the book and picks up the phone. “Yeah, the kind of money you need, does not come from the Independent feds, not from the National federations either, but the International feds are the one with the money. Oh baby, these contracts can be worth millions!” Jade’s face lit up. “Here is the number.” “What is it? Who is it?” Jade said as Nathaniel pushed the book into Jade’s sight, there is a number there but no name. “Hey ring them I said!” He looked excited as she wrapped her fingers around the phone, pressing quick and hard onto the phone pad she finally got the ring tone. “Oh this is really exciting!” She cheeped down the phone. Eventually she got the answer ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling.’ Jade stuttered, the red from her face leaks like a tap. “Excuse me? Could you repeat that please?” When the receptionist did repeat herself Jade looked up at Nathaniel. She puts the phone down. “Well, that’s a start! Now pick up the phone and give them your name.” Nathaniel ordered his friend. This was going to be a long night. -[Fade]-
