I feel like Tony Soprano

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/24/2002 *The scene opens up once again inside of John O'Donnell Stadium in Davenport, IA, the location of tonight's Tuesday Turmoil. Jon Kano is still there, this time sitting in the stands, with his feet kicked up over the seats in front of him. He is smoking a fine cigar. He is dressed this time in a royal blue Versace suit with a black silk tie. He just keeps watching the ring crew on the converted baseball diamond putting the ring up for tonight's event.* Kano: There he went, Duke once again. He just can't keep his mouth shut about me. I betrayed him? Horseshit. I was sitting backstage writing a chapter when he did what he did, joining forces with Kannon. Christ, he left me out to dry. We spent the better part of a year forcing Nelson out and Nelson ain't even gone a month and Duke decided he wanted to be part of something worse than Nelson. If I have my choice between the devil I know and the devil I don't, I choose the devil I do. *Kano takes a nice long puff off of his cigar.* Kano: I am behind with the times? At least I don't dress like I am from the old west and still listen to music from a man who reached his prime in the 1960s. Jesus man, you were like a brother to me, Duke. But right now, I am filled with this ruthless rage that I haven't felt since I ended Corey Williams' career. I feel like I want to seriously hurt you, not beat you, but seriously hurt you. You asked me for a favor? What the hell do I owe you? I never have owed you a damn thing. The only person I owe is myself. I owe myself glory. For so long, I listened to the fans and cared for others. The only people I care about now is my family, my friends, and myself. To hell with the fans, f*ck the homeless, and Kannon can kiss my ass if he thinks I am going to pay that stupid ass tax. If he wouldn't be such a cheap bastard and actually would pay the poor bastard ring crew a decent wage with some benefits, wrestlers like myself wouldn't have to pay his little innovative tax. Its not the money, its the principle of the thing. *Kano pauses to take another puff off of his cigar.* Kano: But I am getting a little off track here, back to Duke. I feel like Tony Soprano, who the hell can I trust if I can't trust Duke? But I am alright with that, it means that I have no strings attached. I can do what I want to whomever I want when I want without worrying how it will affect anyone. Duke was my last friend here. I love this. Duke wonders why I consider myself a legend? Motherf*cker, I didn't bestow the title on myself. It was given to me by a young man about a year ago backstage. His name? Corey Williams. It's ironic, isn't it? The name 'Real Legend' stuck. Duke still claims he is a legend and I am not. Well, he is, but damnit, so am I. He has titles he has won, so have I. He has entertained millions, and so have I. Do you see the similarities? If Duke is a legend, so am I, and vice versa. Duke and I are very much the same person, except he still wrestles for others, namely Xavier Kannon, while I wrestle for myself, and myself alone. Duke is going to go to school tonight, and he and I are going to kick it old school, with only myself and my tag team partner, Blade D, being victorious. *Kano takes another puff off of the cigar.* Kano: Then Friday, Kramer Wild Wing, 'The Wild One' if you will. He will step into the ring with myself, and there will be nothing Kramer can do about it. Jason Alexander can't help him. KFC's bucket can't help him. Not even God himself can help him because I am Jon Kano, the greatest big man to ever step foot in a PCW ring. I am better than Ivan, Boda, and Akiyama. None of them will be talked about and remembered generations from now. I will be. I am not some flash in the pan like Kramer Wild Wing has made a career out of being. I am the undisputed King of the Old School. I was here when PCW was barely started, and I will be here when it ends. I will be here, still standing amongst the rubble where others have fallen, and on Friday, another chapter will be written in my great legacy, and I will show Kramer Wild Wing once again and remind Duke Williams tonight why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano leaves his seat and walks away and out of sight behind the grandstand.*
