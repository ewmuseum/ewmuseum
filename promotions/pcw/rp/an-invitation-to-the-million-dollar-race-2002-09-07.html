An invitation to the million dollar race!!!

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 7, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within the living room of Semore Smoke’s house. Sitting on the far end of the room is Semore’s huge entertainment system, which consists of a VCR, TV, DVD player, PS2, Dreamcast, XBox, and a big screen TV for it all. Sitting against the far wall from the system is a single sofa where Semore Smoke is sitting looking over mail that he had just received that day… Semore Smoke: Bill… Bill… Bill… Bill… Indictment… Bill… Bill… Drugs… I’ll open that one later… hmm… what’s this? Invitation to the Cannonball Run… {{{{Smoke opens the envelope and looks over the letter inside, reading it aloud as he looks at it…}}}} Semore Smoke: “Dear Mr. Smoke. It has come to our attention that you’re a celebrity who might be interested in a race where the prize is one million dollars. Now, this race isn’t a normal race, we’ve taken the liberty to sign you up with another celebrity partner since we couldn’t get in touch with you on an earlier date. Your partner will be none other then The End, one of your fellow PCW wrestlers.” End? What the hell!? The guy is blind as a bat! “Others who have chosen to sign up for our Cannonball Run race from one end of the country to the other are RUSH wrestler Stimpo teaming with OSW diva Tempest, RUSH wrestlers D-Kay Calyx and Joey Flash, OSW wrestler Ivan Stanislav and his partner Alexei Ruslan, FSW wrestlers Timo Bolamba and Mike Marvelous, OSW wrestler Scott Taylor” Oh god! Now I have to join this thing, gives me a reason to kick Taylor’s ass again! “and RUSH wrestler Tyler Valentine, PCW wrestlers Satuz Quo and Dante Jackson, PCW wrestlers Gaz The Green and Nerezza, wrestlers Jim Surge and Ben ‘Nighthorse’ Campbell, OSW wrestlers Hessian and Minion The Imp, and PCW wrestlers Mark Makonie and Duzza. We hope that you and The End can join this star-studded event that has had two other installments in the past. These races included in the past the likes of Roger Moore and Jackie Chan, and now we hope to do a special wrestler version of the race. The race will start from Elizabethtown, North Carolina, just off the coast of North Carolina and will end in Las Angeles, California. More information will be given at the start of the race from Elizabethtown. We hope to see you there, sign, The Cannonball Run council.” Hmm… sounds like an interesting event. {{{{Smoke stands up from the sofa and keeps all of the mail on the sofa, except for the one letter. He then walks out of the room and into the next room, which is a kitchen area, which seems to be well kept, it’s complete with a refrigerator, stove, everything a man needs to cook himself a snack whenever he may be at home. But today he isn’t going to cook himself anything, but he’s going to use the phone that he has in his kitchen, and he’s going to use it to call his partner, The End. Smoke picks up the phone and then has to think a second for the phone number that goes straight to The End’s cell phone, the only phone that the blind wrestler himself keeps on him directly. Smoke then smiles a half smirk on his face as he dials the number that he remembers. After dialing he waits a few seconds as the phone starts to ring. It rings about four times, but in the middle of the fourth ring a man picks up, and it’s the voice of the man he was waiting for, The End…}}}} The End: Hello? Semore Smoke: Hey End, it’s me Semore Smoke. Did you get a letter about the Cannonball Run? The End: Yes I did. A buddy of mine read it to me, it says that I’ve been set to team with you against all these other wrestlers. I was about to have my buddy dial your number so I could talk to you about it, but I guess you beat me to the punch. I wouldn’t mind splitting a million bucks with anyone, because that’s another five hundred grand that’s in my pocket. So, I guess I’m in if you are. Semore Smoke: Same here. I’m willing to do anything for that much money. That’s as much as I usually get in a year. The End: Same here. Well I’ll have a plane booked to Raleigh where I’ll have someone drive me to Elizabethtown. Are you in Wilson? Semore Smoke: Yeah, I’ll just drive down tonight. I hope to see you there tomorrow. The End: Same here. See you then. Semore Smoke: See ya. {{{{Smoke hangs up the phone and smiles as he reaches in his pocket and pulls out an already been rolled joint. He sticks the joint into his mouth and thinks about how the race will be. Five hundred grand is on the line as he and The End team up to race several other racers, but what car will he use? He himself has about three extremely fast cars, but he’ll need the fastest to win a race. He’ll also need to get it over with before Tuesday. The race starts tomorrow afternoon that gives him three days to get from one end of the country to the other and then to his match against Anarchy for the Rising Star Championship. Time is against him now, so he must go prepare. Smoke lights the joint and the scene fades on Semore as he takes a puff in and lets the aroma and smoke intoxicate him…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED!!!
