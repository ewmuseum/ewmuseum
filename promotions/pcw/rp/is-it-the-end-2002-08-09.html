Is it the end?

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-9-02 Brandi and Lindsey walk quickly past the many booths and carnival workers who are calling out to them. They are all guaranteeing a win, but somehow people are walking away empty handed and with less money. This was another one of Lindsey’s “take a break” schemes. She had thought that the two of them would go out, ride some rides, eat some food, and take a break from all the chaos surrounding the rest of the world. As Fusion grows closer, Lindsey becomes much more worried about Brandi’s match with The End than she is. Lindsey tip toes around questions surrounding the match. She wants to ask them, but Brandi avoids them as much as she can. The more questions she is asked, the more nervous she becomes. The carnies continue to yell their promises, but for the most part they go in one ear and out the next. Lindsey looks at all the stuffed prizes as she walks past. Lindsey stops at one booth and watches as a man picks up a softball and heaves it at three milk containers. The ball hits them and two fly off the table, but the top one drops straight down and stays there. Carnie- Better luck next time. The man and his family walk away empty handed. Lindsey steps up to the booth and hands the man a dollar. She grabs the softball and tosses it at the milk bottles. It misses completely and hits the wall behind them. She hands the carnie some more money and steps back to try it again. Brandi watches and looks over at the prize. It’s a polar bear with goggles and a brown flying cap. Brandi steps back from the booth and leans against a pole. She hadn’t wanted to come tonight, but she couldn’t tell Lindsey no. Her head has been hurting again, and she took the last of her pain medication before they left the house. If Brandi told Lindsey, she would worry about her and perhaps insist that she see a doctor. Hopefully it’ll kick in soon enough. I don’t have to stay here long. Just long enough to make Lindsey happy and make sure that she doesn’t suspect anything is wrong. Brandi’s thoughts are interrupted with Lindsey’s squeaks as she finally knocks all of the milk bottles off of the table. The carnie claps and hands her one of the polar bears. Brandi walks over to her and flashes her classic smile. Brandi- Hey, good throwing. Lindsey laughs and hugs the bear. Lindsey- Yeah, it only cost ten bucks. Brandi joins in on the laughter and the two women walk down the midway. Lindsey spots the ferris wheel and points it out. The two get in the back of the long line of people and wait. Lindsey- So, what are you thinking? Brandi- Nothing. Lindsey- Oh, come on now. Don’t lie. You have to have something on your mind. Brandi is getting frustrated about Lindsey’s persistency. They both know that there’s one thing on her mind. That there’s only been one thing on her mind since last week. Her shot at Cruiserweight gold. Brandi realizes that it won’t be an easy match, and she’s been trying to mentally prepare herself for it. Brandi- Well…I was just wondering why the hell this line is so long. Lindsey knows that Brandi is lying, but she fakes a laugh anyway. The line moves forward slowly, leaving long awkward moments for the two high school friends. Brandi- Hey, Lindsey? Lindsey- Yeah? Brandi- I really do appreciate you trying to get my mind off Friday night. I’m sorry if I seem kind of…distant. It’s just that this is a huge opportunity for me, and I really don’t want to screw it up. Lindsey nods and smiles. The two have finally made it to the front of the line and they get in the next available car. As the ride starts up, Brandi suddenly gets a bad feeling about everything. She looks all around, but everything appears normal. She tries to shrug off the feeling, thinking that she’s just being paranoid, but it won’t go away. She lays her head back against the seat, and closes her eyes. Brandi jumps when the seat lets out a piercing squeak. She quickly leans forward and makes their car shake. Lindsey grabs Brandi’s shoulders and tries to pull her back. She allows herself to be rested against the seat, and tries to relax. Just as she is starting to feel better, something pops off the seat next to her. She leans forward again, and watches as some kind of bolt bounces down to the ground below. Brandi tosses a nervous glance over at Lindsey. Lindsey gives Brandi an odd look. She isn’t aware of what Brandi’s seen or heard. Brandi- The car is falling apart. Lindsey- What? Brandi- The car is falling apart! The ride is breaking! Lindsey hugs the bear close to her chest. It’s a long way down. Brandi is starting to make her worried. Lindsey- It can’t be breaking. It’s perfectly safe. Brandi glances down at the ground as another bolt falls from the machine. Then she looks back over at Lindsey. Brandi grabs the bear from her, and holds it up to her face. Lindsey looks on helplessly. Brandi- You did this, didn’t you?! You don’t want me to have the chance to take that title from you, so you’re trying to kill me off. You think this is going to work? It’s not! Even if I do die, they’ll find out it was you. They’ll know. You’ll have to give up your title. You’ll be sent to jail. Either way you lose. Brandi laughs and continues to stare up at the bear. Lindsey looks confused, but then she realizes what is going on. For some reason, Brandi thinks that the bear is The End. Well, maybe not him exactly, but it sure does seem like she thinks he is The End, or at least has some sort of connection with him. Brandi holds the bear up higher in the air. Brandi- Are you that scared? First you don’t want the match with me, then you try to kill me off?! Brandi holds the bear over the side of the seat by an arm. She looks down at him and laughs. Lindsey is slightly scared of Brandi’s unusual behavior, and she slides all the way over to the other side of the seat. Brandi seems to have forgotten that she’s there. Brandi- Why did you do it? Did someone pay you do it? Answer me! The bear’s shiny, black, unblinking eyes stare back up at Brandi. He, of course, doesn’t respond at all. Brandi brings him back into the car and holds him up high above her head. Brandi- Answer me, or I will let you fall. Don’t give me that smirk! You think I won’t do it, don’t you? Don’t try me. The bear is still unresponsive. Lindsey gently touches Brandi’s arm and tries to calm her down. Lindsey- Brandi, I… Brandi lowers the bear for a minute and turns to Lindsey. Brandi- Don’t you see what he’s trying to do? He wants me dead. He’s willing to kill you to get it, too. Lindsey- No, Brandi. It’s just a bear. Just a bear. Brandi- That’s what he wants you to think! Brandi turns her attention back to the bear. Brandi- I’ll give you one last chance to explain yourself. I suggest you take it. When the bear doesn’t respond, Brandi grows frustrated and heaves it out of the car. It sails through the air before coming to a rest on the ground next to a workers foot. Lindsey looks shocked. Brandi brushes her hands off, and leans back in the seat. Brandi- Well, that’s that. Lindsey- Brandi…are you ok? Brandi- Yeah, I am now. The car jerks to a stop at the bottom of the ferris wheel. A carnie escorts the two women off of the ride, and hands Lindsey her bear back. Carnie- Here ya go. I think ya dropped this. Brandi shakes her head and takes the bear from Lindsey, handing it back to the carnie. Brandi- No, we didn’t drop it. We don’t want it. The carnie looks confused. He looks to Lindsey as if waiting for a reasonable answer, but all she can do is shrug. Carnie- You sure ya don’t want it? Brandi- Yep. The carnie shrugs, and goes back to working his ride. Brandi walks deeper into the fair, with Lindsey at close tow. Lindsey- What happened back there? Brandi stops and looks and Lindsey, confused. Brandi- Whatcha mean? Lindsey- You like, flipped out back there. Brandi shrugs and points to another ride. Brandi- What do ya say we hit that one next? Brandi runs off to get in line, leaving Lindsey standing there alone and confused.
