The Evolution Of The Title Belt-The Stone Age

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Evolution Of The Title Belt-The Stone Age Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-24-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins as the NFL Hall of Fame music plays in the background, the lights coming up to full slowly as they reveal a mural of various wrestlers from Hulk Hogan, to Frank Gotch, to The Rock, to Keji Muto, to unknown indy wrestlers; showing off their various moves and holds upon a huge collage upon a concrete wall. The mural is a thing of beauty of a wrestling enthusiast, the music finally fading as a voice says from off camera..::/::..Voice:”And now your host…Rent-A-Hero.”..::/::..None other then PCW’s very own superhero, Rent-A-Hero, makes his appearance on screen with a wide smile. He is adorned within a vintage Grey Ghost costume, the PCW Crusierweight championship strapped tightly about his waist, and our hero finishing off his outfit with a silk necktie. He fixes the tie, interlocking his hands in front of him as he looks to the camera in his best attempt at a “serious manner” and says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Hello citizens, and welcome to “Great Moments In Wrestling History”. The sport of professional wrestling is an amazing and multi-faceted sport with many mysteries and acts that the regular fanbase are completely unaware of. Tonight, we will be discussing the administration of….THE TITLE BELT! Now with that in mind…where would I find a title belt to use as an example? HMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMMM….”..::/::..Our hero feigns confusion as he looks about for a title belt, knowing full well that he has the PCW Crusierweight championship about his spandex enwrapped waist. Finally tiring of playing dumb, our hero looks to his waist as his eyes widen with insincere shock. He undoes his belt and holds it up on his shoulder, pointing to it as he exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”OH WOW…I happen to have a championship RIGHT HERE! It also JUST so happens that I have a chance to win the PCW World title this Tuesday against nine fiends, bent on capturing the World gold I was meant to have. What a coinky-dink huh?! On another note, the title belt was erected for the sake of showing to the world who was the BEST OF THE BEST in the federation at the time. However….there was a time before title belts, where their were some “interesting” alternatives to set the greatest wrestler in the federation apart from others. Tonight, you will be witness to the evolution of the championship belt….and it all started long ago, when man was FRESH out of the primordial soup…and it didn’t taste like chicken noodle either!” 123864846843206306545047 B.C. (Before Cologne…and boy did those Cave-Men need it.) ..::/::..The world is hot and humid., the sand golden and searing to the uncovered foot….yet this does not stop the cheering and clamoring of a group of half-naked cavemen, bouncing and jumping about in excitement as they swarm about a circular ring surrounded by beautiful conch seashells. They chant in unison in a language that would hardly be considered advanced, watching on as two muscular cave-men, one in white tigerskins and the other in black bearskins, as the two claw and grapple with each other wildly. In the foreground of the wild chanting and brawling, is our hero, looking back to the action over his shoulder for merely a few moments before looking to the audience and saying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Ah yes…the era of the cave-men, where wrestling was truly tooth and nail. Around this time, wrestling was a means to prove dominance amongst the cavemen peers and to earn the only things that mattered to a real caveman….dinosaur meat, cave-babes, and sand…..come on guys, it’s not like they were rich with technology here! We are here at the very moment where Bulk Booga and Bronte The Giant did battle in front of a SCREAMING 35 cavemen, record attendance for their time. Little did the two know….that they would make history after the historic finish.”Fans:”BOOGA!!! BOOGA!!!! BOOGA!!!! BOOGA!!”..::/::..The cavemen are rabid, watching on as the caveman in white known as Bulk Booga beats away at his much larger opponent’s head with closed fists, Bronte barely phased by the hits as he boots Bulk in the face, following with a vicious choke as the fans continue cheering..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Notice the kick, punch, big boot, choke, and slam style would set the stage for EVERY other big man wrestler AFTER the Stone Age. I guess their just some style’s you can’t improve on…..and some just too crappy to BE improved.” ..::/::..We return to the fight as Bronte is now choking Bulk in front of the dozens and dozens of his fans, a cavemen who’s chest is covered in white and black paint checks Bulk’s arm, lifting it as it drops once. The caveman does it yet again, Bulk’s arm hitting the dirt yet again as the fans start to cheer for a comeback. The caveman lifts Bulk’s arm up yet again….the arm not falling as he shakes it with authority, rising to his feet and clocking Bronte in the face with a series of punches to free himself. Bulk then backs up, booting the colossus in the face before following up with a leg drop, the white and black striped caveman counting..::/::..Caveman:”OOGA….BOOGA….COOGA!!!” ..::/::..The surrounding cavemen cheer, Bulk’s arm being raised in victory as Rent-A-Hero scratches his chin, asking to the audience..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Now where have I seen that finish before?”..::/::..With the battle done, the referee looks about for something to present to Bulk, dropping to his knees and picking up a rock as he hands it to the tired and battered Bulk. The caveman warrior takes it within hand, roaring loudly as the cavemen roar back in approval. With his rock in hand, he proceeds to pose for his peers to their adulation as our hero says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”I CAN’T PUT A FINGER ON WHO HE REMINDS ME OF! Anyways, that’s where it all started….a rock. That one rock, being about the most rare item to cavemen besides the cavewomen, became the very item all cavemen warriors strived to possess…and it would soon start a shockwave in the sport of professional wrestling. On a side note, Bulk Booga shortly lost the Stone Age Rock Championship to the Ice Age….and NOBODY can politic or no-sell the Ice Age…well…maybe Meanstreak….or Triple H. We shall continue the evolution of the title belt in our next installment, “The World Heavyweight Whore”, the most coveted prize during the 1500’s! Until next time…SEE YOU SAME RENT-A-TIME, SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!” –slight shiver- “Whoa…it’s getting kinda cold around…..uh oh!”..::/::..The scene fades, everything coming to a sudden halt as a wave of ice overtakes the area, freezing the cavemen and our hero in his tracks, the scene fading to an auspicious black…::/::.. ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
