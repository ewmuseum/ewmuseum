The night after (Saga pt.10)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st December Deuce 00 Deuce § Kai woke up early the next morning. He still felt drained from his ordeal the night before. He slowly crawled out of his bed and was startled to find Cami liu asleep in a chair next to his bed. He sat at the edge of his bed just watching the sleeping woman. Her long brown hair was pulled back into a braided ponytail, which dangled over her shoulder. She was only wearing a long, light green nightshirt that ended a few inches above her knees. Cami liu was curled up in a 'ball' on the uncomfortable looking chair. A blanket had fallen around the legs of the chair. He carefully picked up Cami liu into his arms. She snuggled into his powerful arms. Trying not to wake her, he deposited her onto his warm bed and pulled up the covers around her to tuck her in. Giving one last look at the beautiful woman in his bed, he quietly left his room. Kai walked softly down the hall to Ben's room and lightly rapped on his door. Ben answered the door in his red bathrobe. 'I didn't expect you to be up for the usual morning exercise Kai. Last night was quite a night for all of us.' 'I did not get a proper chance to thank you for helping me last night.' Kai replied walking into the room. 'Hey! What are brothers for?' Ben said with a smile. 'Besides I still owe you quite a lot for pulling me out of some really bad situations.' Kai smiled at his friend, and walked over to the window and knelt down. The horizon was a brilliant colour of orange and red. It looked as if there was no rain in sight for the day. Kai closed his eyes preparing to meditate. Ben soon knelt beside him and closed his eyes as well. 'Kai?' Ben asked. 'Yes?' Kai replied, still facing forward with his eyes closed. 'After we dropped you off in your room last night, Cami liu asked me to explain what had happened. I told her somewhat about the various aspects of Chi. I also explained who Shoki was.' Ben said still looking forward. 'I see.' Kai answered back. 'Any idea as to why he has shown himself after all these years, and why he is hounding you?' 'I'm not quite sure of that myself yet.' Kai responded. They sat there quietly for a few more minutes in meditation. Ben finally spoke again. 'Cami liu was very concerned about you. She was emotionally drained after last night. I think she is in love with you. A girl like that does not happen along everyday my friend.' 'I know Ben. She is sleeping in my bed right now.' Kai replied not thinking of what he was implying. Ben broke his concentration and turned to stare at Kai. His eyes were wide as saucers and his mouth dropped, unable to mutter a word. Kai feeling Ben's stare upon him opened his eyes and looked over to his friend's astonished face. Kai gave him a questioning look as to why he was staring at him when it finally dawned on him. Kai turned a few shades of red. 'It's not what you think, Ben,” he stammered. 'She came into my room last night.'Ben's eyes got a lot wider. Kai felt as if he was incriminating himself further. 'I found her asleep in a chair by my bed Ben when I woke up! She looked so uncomfortable sleeping in the chair, so I placed her in my bed and came right over here. Honest!' Kai hastily added. Ben shot Kai a wicked smile. 'I believe you buddy.' Ben laughed elbowing Kai, and went back to meditating with a big smile on his face. 'This is too funny!' Ben snickered. Kai shook his head in defeat and went back to meditating. Well after sunrise, Cami liu awoke in Kai's bed. She rubbed the sleep from her eyes and looked around the room. Kai was nowhere in sight. Looking over to the chair she had slept in that night, she wondered how she ended up in his bed. 'Surely I did not crawl in bed with him?' she blushed thinking about it. She tossed off the covers, picked up her blanket, and walked out of his room. On the way out of Kai's room she passed a very sleepy Dan yawning as he walked out of his room in his pyjamas. 'Good morning Dan.' she said with a smile to him. Dan stretched his arms yawning again. 'Yeah, yeah, morning.' He replied. She walked down to her room and entered. Dan continued to walk down the hallway to Ben's room when he stopped. His eyes shot wide open and he looked back at Cami liu's door, then to Kai's door, and finally back again. 'How the H*LL does he do it?' Dan said aloud shaking his head. They all met in Ben's room later that morning to eat breakfast and to discuss what was planned for the day ahead of them. 'I plan on going down to the waterfront with Dan to see this movie he is the Star of.' Ben said between sips of his orange juice. 'Yeah Ben! Maybe I can get you a bit part in the movie.' Dan replied sarcastically. 'No thanks, Dan. I just want to watch you in action.' he gave a sly wink to the others. 'You'll be amazed, just as they were when they asked me to star in it!' Dan said with a big smile. 'I have no doubts about that.' Ben replied. 'What are you two going to do today?' 'I was planning on giving Cami liu her first lesson on mastering the chi today.' Kai said. 'That is if she has no other plans for the day.' 'Are you sure you are up to it after what happened last night? You should not push yourself so hard on my account.' She asked with concern in her voice. Dan was about to take a large gulp of his orange juice, while listening to Cami liu's statement, when he started to choke. Ben smacked him on the back and Dan started to get control of his coughing. 'You need to take small drinks, Dan.' Ben said looking over at him. 'I was! It's just that she...*cough*...she said...*cough*... ahh, never mind.' Dan said noticing that everyone was looking at him with questioning looks. It was about mid morning when Dan and Ben left for the shoot. They had all planned on meeting back up later that night for dinner. As they waved bye to the cab the two were in, Cami liu looked up at Kai. 'Where shall we go to do this? She asked. 'We should go to a secluded place away from the city.' Kai said looking around the busy streets. Cami liu thought for a second, then she brightened up. 'I think I got the perfect place!' she said §
