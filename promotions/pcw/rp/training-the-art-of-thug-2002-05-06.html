Training the Art of Thug

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/02 “How can one man be so silent after fourteen years of being in the dark? Finally after fourteen years Duzza finds out just who the murder of his beloved father actually is! And what does he do? NOTHING! He has not said one word! He has not shown any emotion! Why!? How!?” remarks an angry and dumbfounded Kid Krazie. “I know if my father was killed and I was left not knowing who the killer was for fourteen years I’d be pissed and more than likely have a lot to say once I found out just who killed him! But here Duzza just sits speechless and uncaring!” Krazie drives down the thuggish streets of Houston, Texas in the 5th Ward area where guns are common and drug dealings are even more common! Krazie is driving along with Lu-Lu sitting in the passenger seat next to her lover. “What exactly are we here for Krazie?” asks the inanimate shopping cart with mysterious abilities that allow her to talk to Kid Krazie. She sits there with the seatbelt strapped around her holding her in place and preventing her from rolling all over the seat. “I am looking for something to help me train…” replies a frustrated Kid Krazie. “I’ve got to find a way to beat Duzza…” Krazie slams on his breaks as he sees some black adolescents standing on a street corner wearing baggy blue jeans, some basketball jerseys, and some do-rags covering their braided hair. Krazie rolls down his window, as the group of six adolescent black kids look his way. “We don’t see s (censor) t to crackas!” says one kid. “Yeah so get yo honky ass outa here!” chips in another kid. “But I ain’t looking to buy anything…” replies a curious Kid Krazie. Krazie pulls off the street next to the group of thugs with his window down and the engine still running in case a speedy exit is required. “What do you need then cracka?” asks the first thug. “I need your help! All of you…” Krazie says to the group of dangerous looking thugs. “I need to learn how to fight like a nigger.” The six black kids look to each other and then look back at Krazie with hatred in their eyes. Krazie looks to Lu-Lu and then back to the kids who are now reaching in their pockets digging for something. “Oh s (censor) t!” screams a shocked Krazie. The black kids pull out some handguns pointing toward Krazie in a tilted thuggish fashion. Krazie punches the gas and peels out speeding away from the group of adolescent thugs with the kids throwing insults after the speeding Krazie. “I knew that was going to happen!” remarks an amused Lu-Lu. “Why?” asks a dumbfounded Kid Krazie. Lu-Lu begins to laugh, as Krazie looks her way. “You aren’t supposed to call them ni**ers! It’s ni**ers with an a at the end.” Krazie nods his head catching on as he pulls up to another group of black kids. They are older than the previous groups and more “thuggish” as they stand puffing and passing blowing some cowboy killa. They look towards the crazy cracka as Krazie smiles at them. “What are you smiling at cracka?” asks one of the four thugs. “Nothing…” replies a still smiling Kid Krazie. “You lost dawg?” asks another of the four thugs. “Nope…” responds Krazie as he gives it some thought. “I am looking for something.” “Yeah well we don’t got s (censor) t you Narc!” replies an angry big black guy standing with the blunt in between his fingers. “So get yo punk ass outa here!” Krazie pulls out his wallet flashing a couple of Benjamin’s towards the group catching their attention. They flock over to the side window of the ’32 Ford Coupe looking inside at the belted down Lu-Lu. “Yo dawg, you know you got a shopping cart in here with a seat belt around it?” asks the first thug. Krazie looks to Lu-Lu and smiles patting the handle bar with affection. “Of course! If I didn’t she would fall out when I turn!” “Whateva dawg, whateva!” says the second thug. “What do you want with us cracka? “I need some help! I have a match against this thug Duzza in PCW and well I don’t think my honky ways will be enough to beat his thuggish ways!” informs a still smiling Krazie. “So you come down here in 5th Ward looking for some of “us” to teach yo cracka ass how to fight like a street thug?” asks the first thug. “Well it wouldn’t be for free!” replies Krazie who is nodding his head. The four black guys look at each other and a smile forms on their faces as they formulate a mischievous plan. “Sure!!! We’ll help ya out!” says the second thug. “Why don’t we get started with the first lesson?” asks the first thug. “That’d be great! Where’d ya’ll want to train at?” asks Krazie who is very happy that these nice young men are willing to help. “Right here!” says the first thug who is pointing towards the alleyway. Krazie nods his head and climbs out of his car turning the engine off and following the four nice guys. “Ok so what is the first lesson in ‘Thug Fighting?’” asks a prepared Krazie. The four black guys begin to beat Krazie down as they brutally attack Krazie four on one with Lu-Lu watching from the car. They beat Krazie with their fists and vicious well placed kicks as Krazie tries to get to his feet. After a five-minute attack the four guys stop and all begin to laugh as Krazie lays there coughing up some blood. Krazie looks up to the four guys who stand above him with smiles on their faces. “What was that!?” asks a confused Krazie. “The first lesson in Thug Fighting!” remarks the first thug. “What you didn’t know the first rule was to always be ready to fight?” Krazie nods his head and smiles as he reveals a gap between two teeth in the top row of teeth. Blood is oozing down his face from some cuts received by the beating as he gets to his feet with the four guys just standing back laughing. As Krazie gets back up to his feet the four guys proceed to beat him once again! Krazie fights back some getting some good punches but is soon dropped to the concrete yet again as the odds are just too great. Soon the beating is over and an even more beaten up Krazie looks up at the attackers with a question look on his face. “Was that lesson two? Cause it sure felt like lesson one all over again!” remarks a lying and bloody Kid Krazie. “Lesson Two of Thug Fighting is to not trust thugs!” replies the second thug as he picks Krazie’s wallet up off the concrete ground. The four guys dig through Krazie’s wallet taking all the money and throwing the empty wallet back at Krazie. They turn to walk away leaving Krazie there to bleed. “Thanks for the lessons guys!” Krazie yells to the four departing guys. “Those guys sure were nice!” Krazie says under his breath. The scene fades as Krazie gets to his feet picking up his wallet and staggering his way over to the awaiting car. He is bloody and beaten but more knowledgeable in the art of Thug Fighting. “Get ready Duzza, cause I am getting ready for you! You ain’t taking my title dog” remarks Krazie as he climbs into the car.
