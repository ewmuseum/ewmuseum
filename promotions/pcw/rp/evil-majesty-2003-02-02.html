Evil Majesty

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/2/03 Evil Majesty The scene opens in a smoky room with steamy windows. The camera slowly fades by the lowered head of El Romantico, drinking a glass of crystal water slowly and lightly. He looks to his left and sees an angelic creature with her eyes wide shut. She lies on the pillow comfortably with her lip just barely touching the pillow covering. Romantico walks by her and graces her cheeks with his index finger. He then walks out of the room and down the long mystical staircase. He looks around at all of the possession he owns and just stares at the ground in a state of strong thinking. Romantico takes his hand and wraps it strongly around the golden braced doorknob, opening the door and letting in a shot of cool air. Romantico goes out in just his long fur robe and picks a strand of grass from his lawn. He sticks the strand of grass close to his face as it blows away from the wind. He follows the strand of grass with his eyes as it soars through the sky. Am I nothing? Years of gaining respect. They called me the world’s greatest fighter. They say I am the son of Spain, the Mysterious Lover, the Poetic Justice, but am I really any of that? I want to believe I am more. I wish I were more. By God I pray I was more. A woman. Romantico thinks back at the smooth beautiful creature that he embraced in love with. The woman he went deep inside and felt everything inside her. He looked in her soul and explored her heart, without her ever blinking. Could he turn his back on this miracle just to get back the embarrassment and shame at hand? Why? Why? Oh why? He cries to all the rivers and oceans, but his words are just blurs to everyone other than himself. He looks at his reflection in his own front window and falls to his knees. He takes a deep gulp and falls back on his back. She pushed me, but I just can’t push back. I look hard deep within myself to find a new more exciting and wiser person. I speak my soul now, and not just anything I think will take me to the promise land. And now I am with this brick wall in my path of success. I vowed that my emotions would not stop my goals. I would be ruthless and show no mercy, but how could I. If it were anything else it would be as easy as breathing, but it’s not. A woman. Could it be I’m losing my touch? I’m becoming soft and going back into this depressing pit I worked so hard to pull myself out of. Does she not know whom she is dealing with? Are my words jokes in her young and naïve mind? I love women. I swear I would not do anything to hurt such a beautiful creature. The past is behind me. I said what I’ve said and I meant every word. For a woman is a man’s pleasure. We are to love them and treat them well, but they are ours. They are not to be their own, but yet to be ours. Why does she not understand? Must I have to face this possession and beat it as if it were just another? Or do I walk away; turning my back on everything I’ve created. May she be DAMNED! DAMNED TO HELL! I did not intend for this to happen. I never sensed it, smelt it, felt it, or thought it. Is this my punishment? Well I’ll tell you this. If this is a punishment, then it is a punishment I will stand up to. The same strand of grass that Romantico watched float around in the cool Barcelona breeze is snatched with the palm of his once motionless hand. Romantico tightens his grip with his eyes becoming blood shot in madness. He jumps to his feet and clinches his teeth together. He paces stronger and stronger until he gets inside his house. He takes everything in his path and just throws it on the ground, breaking it. He screams and screams and screams some more until his heart in content. He takes his couch and throws it right through the front window to his house. He goes right up to hi shard wooden wall and just punches it over and over, until his hand is dripping with blood and can no longer be moved. You’ve turned me into this, and now I’ll act like what you’ve turned me into. I BLAME YOU! Romantico falls to the ground on top of the broken glass. His skin tears with cuts and blood, but his eyes do not move at all. He just stays there without moving, until he hears footsteps coming from down the footstep. Romantico! The angelic creature from before yawns as she looks over at Romantico and sees his in pain. She goes over to help as Romantico eyes suddenly darken and a scary smile moves across his face. She goes down and grabs Romantico’s wrist, but Romantico shakes her off and gets to his feet quickly and thoroughly. Oh my you are bleeding. Am I? She grabs the face of Romantico, wiping the blood gently off as Romantico just snaps, grabbing her shoulder viscously with a burst of strength. Romantico you’re hurting me? Romantico begins to shake and shake and shake her over and over. Romantico stop your hurting me. WHY DO YOU DO THIS TO ME? What are you talking about? The woman begins to scream and cry until she finally breaks lose and runs out of the house in complete horror. NO…. MORE…. PAIN…
