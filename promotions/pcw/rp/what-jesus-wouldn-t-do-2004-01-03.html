What Jesus Wouldn't Do

3 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/2004 Things had been getting frantic since Turmoil. I got home at seven thirty to find a large blob of lard and hair on Davey’s lap. Davey has that expression.... You know the one where you grind your teeth; close your eyes as tight as you can and moan with your chin in against his chest.... Yea, now you know the exact expression on Davey’s face. “Enjoyment” and “Horror” are the first two words that spring to mind.... The ball of lard rises about five inches, then falls down on Davey’s thighs. Davey.... Davey just grabs onto the lard and gasps. After getting sixteen stitches across the back of my head.... This is actually pretty soothing. Davey just sits smiles and says, “Watch the stomach dude.” What would Jesus do? “Jesus Christ man, how long you been there?” Davey gets up and tosses the bouncy ball to one side. I just stand there and rub my fingers against the stubble below my lip. Note to self: Buy a razor. “Err.... I mean, Terry I’d like you to meet Lara...” Then he grabs a handful of lard from her back so her face rises in my direction. “Lara, this is Terry...” And before she can say anything Davey holds a lobster claw over her mouth. I figured. Davey was just another one of those little scabs that just.... Well it doesn’t go away. The hooker slips on a black dress which is interspersed with a series of daisies stretches outs a hand.... Well more like the place where the lard from the elbow just caves in and from his pocket Davey produces a warm pair of silk panties. “Yo, you owe me forty bucks.” While this is happening I smile inexplicitly and think: Bouncey, bouncey, bouncey. Then picture rolling her down a flight of stairs. All the while Davey’s stroking the back of his neck, “You good for this one man.... It’s just....” What would Jesus do? And being caught up in the moment I hand him a wad of what could a hundred but could be a couple of dollars. Who ate all the pies? “Thanks dude,” and he hands me a twenty. The telephone rings while I’m imagining what her circulatory system must work like. And I’m thinking, for real, all the way over to the phone, Boingey, boingey, boingey. Hello? The voice on the other end of the phone says something that could be berry..... I can’t hear over lardy’s screaming. I’m sorry. And I push my index finger into my left ear and hold the phone to my other.... This is what Davey and I lived like.... Well it was all fake silence, other than when I come in to find him on ball with hair we’re rarely in the same room as each other. It’s your mother.... Well she isn’t doing too well. Oh, her, thought I told you not to call unless she was dead? There’s a long, belated sigh on the other end of the phone. “Well.... By the looks of things she’ll be moving to room 124 sooner than we expected.” 124 meant death. 1-50 meant crazy. 50-100 sick. 100-123 critical. You get the picture.... Well we thought we might need to talk to you.... As soon as possible... And my finger slips out of my ear.... Both Davey and lardy.... Lara rather are gone.... “I know this must come as a shock to you,” she says. “I mean.... Doctors say she never stops talking about you.” She must have forgotten the whole ‘don’t call again unless she’s dead thing’ since she actually DID call so I remind her. “Listen.... Just get down to the hospital...” What would Jesus do? Well looks like I’ll see the bitch in Room 124 then.... The word “death” comes to mind.
