Uh, Jon, where the hell are we?

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/7/2002 ~The time is now. What time? The time for Jon Kano to prove himself once again. Jon Kano has had one hell of a week. He was fired, rehired, and won a battle royal with eight other men. He has now been booked for the upcoming Pay Per View, Supremacy. Teacher versus student, one of the age old battles of human kind. The young tiger cub tries to oust the old tiger from the clan. Sometimes the young cub is successful, and other times the experienced old tiger foils the young tiger's plan. What will happen when Tim Walden and Jon Kano meet at Supremacy? Will Tim Walden finally be able to defeat his former teacher and mentor or will Kano once again take Walden back to school and show him why he is a legend? All of these questions will be answered at Supremacy. Kano is ready to step into the classroom and take his student back to school, but is his former student ready to brave the classroom once again? Will history repeat itself and will Kano kick the hell out of Walden or will Walden knock the old tiger out of the clan?~ *The scene opens up as 'The Malice Man' Duke Williams and 'The REAL LEGEND' Jon Kano are walking down a street late at night. The two men are staggering all over the place, from their escapades earlier at the bars of the fine town of Columbus, Ohio. Jon Kano is dressed in baggy jeans, a black t-shirt with 'REAL LEGEND' in white lettering across the chest, brown Doc Martin boots, and a long, black leather trenchcoat. Duke Williams is dressed in his trademark cowboy-western attire.* Duke: Damn, hoss, maybe we drank a little too much. Kano: I always thought that was a contradiction in terms. *Both men share a laugh.* Duke: Sh*t, hoss, I think this going to be the last time we are going to be able to get sh*tfaced this week due to our big matches at the PPV. Kano: Yeah, I know. We must keep our heads on straight, Supremacy could be the beginning of the end of the Ultimate Authority, if we all play our cards right. Duke: Jesus, what a great thought, the beginning of the end. Kano: I can't wait. At Supremacy, in one corner Timothy Walden, my former student. In the other corner, myself. Here we have student versus teacher. Oh what the hell is this? *Kano skips off subject when Duke and he are approached by a half dozen teenagers, with pipes in hand.* Teen 1: Gimme all your money, mothaf*cka. Kano: Eat sh*t. Duke: F*ck you. *The teens look surprised that they have been turned down.* Teen 1: Don't make me crack you two old f*cks over the head with my pipe. Kano: First off, I am only about ten or twelve years older than you. Second off, bitch please. *As Kano says this, he opens his trenchcoat, exposing the handle of a 9mm gun sticking up from his waistline in his pants. As soon as the teenagers see that Kano isn't messing around, they scurry away like a pack of rats.* Duke: Damn, the great equalizer. Kano: Sh*t, I don't even have this loaded. I just carry it around when I am out and about. I don't keep it loaded when I am drinking. Anyway, Duke, where was I? Duke: I think you just beginning a speel about Tim Walden. Kano: Oh yeah. My esteemed student, Timothy Walden, what a waste of flesh he turned out to be. I thought he actually had potential when I took him deep into the woods and put him through a physical and emotional hell. I destroyed him, and then I rebuilt him. I made him see things inside of himself that he had never seen before. I taught him about that fire that burns inside. You know what I am talking about? Duke: Hell yeah, hoss. Kano: I took his jobberific ass and made him a contender. I taught him the secrets of this industry and what it takes to succeed in this industry. I MADE TIM WALDEN. I took his ordinary, run of the mill straight out of wrestling school ass with no self-confidence and I gave him confidence, I taught him how to play to a crowd, I taught him how to refine his skills, and I changed him into a real wrestler. And how does he repay me? He turns his back on myself. He gets on Nelson's bandwagon and I refuse to let Nelson run this company again. Walden calls me out after I spend about six weeks kicking the living hell out of the whole damn Ultimate Authority and he fires me. I left the building and I smiled. I smiled as my cell phone rang from every major wrestling organization on the globe laying down offers for me to wrestle for them. Then you, Duke, you ran up to me in the parking lot as I was getting in my Navigator and rehired me with a nice raise. Jesus, I thought Jane was generous with her contract that I signed before she left. God damn, I can't believe how much I am pulling down and the benefits, my god, the benefits. And this contract truly is IRON CLAD. I would love to see Walden or anyone else try to fire my ass for the next 7 years on this contract. I would sue the living hell out of PCW and make Nelson cry tears of blood when he has to pay me the penalty. Duke: Yeah, I thought my lawyers did a hell of a job on that contract. Kano: They should for all the money you pay them. Kano: I still can't believe how nieve Timothy has become. He thinks that he can just go and fire my ass? I don't think so. I won't go away on anyone else's terms. I will only leave PCW on MY TERMS and that will be 15 or 25 years down the road when I decide that I have done all that I want to do in this company. Tim may be some big man now, but he never, EVER will be as big as myself, Jon Kano. I revolutionized this compnay. I showed everyone how to live the lifestyle, I showed everyone how to rock, and on Sunday, Duke, I will show the whole world and more specifically, Timothy Walden, why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Duke: I just have one question, Jon. Kano: What is that? Duke: Where the hell are we? *The scene fades to black as the two drunken PCW legends look around in a confused state.* ~It's time, it's time, it's time, it's Supremacy time.~
