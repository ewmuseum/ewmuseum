Ducks-Feed Them Or Else

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/02 The scene opens up on the outskirts of a lake located in Cincinnati Ohio. The sun beats down on the clean and bountiful lake leaving a sparkle on the water giving it a pretty glow. Along its edges are a slightly slanted pile of rocks which makes it easy for visitors to access the waters for fishing purposes. The whole quietness of the area is disrupted as two voices can be soundenly heard nearby. Jason: I don't see what the point of all of this is. Kramer: Look man, you are always in your polluted environment with the cars and so forth. I thought it would be nice if you got out and smelled some fresh air for once. Jason: Bah, screw nature!! Besides when did you become such a nature freak? Kramer: I don't know. A few weeks ago I woke up and got out and about and since then I have loved it. You might not believe it but it will calm you down and clear your senses because you are not trapped in the hustle and bustle of the so called real world. Jason: Maybe for you it works like that, but in all honestly I have more important things to do with my time than to waste them on.......wait what are we doing here? Kramer: We are going to feed some ducks. Jason: Ducks!? You could already be at the First Star Center wining and dining before tonights Turmoil yet you choose to feed some birds? What is wrong with you? You know you will never be normal. Kramer: Who's to say whats normal? Besides I like the way I am. Jason: Look I'll stay here for ten to fifteen minutes to feed these damn creatures, but then we are gone and on our way to Turmoil got it? Kramer: Whatever, just shut up and sit down. Kramer walks slowly and steadily down the rock slope leading to the lake and sits down. Jason follows him but goes ten times slower trying hard not to scratch up his two hundred dollar shoes. He finally sits down on the rocks and looks unamused. Kramer reaches into his pocket and pulls out a baggy with a whole piece of white bread in it. He opens it up and tears off a piece and hands it to Jason who snatches it out of his hand. Then Kramer reaches back in one more time to get his own. Jason: So now what am I suppose to do? Kramer: Just feed it to them like this. Kramer spots a duck waddling in the water about three feet away. He tosses the bread into the water which instantly soaks it up. The duck rushes over to get it before it sinks and then it looks longingly to Kramer wanting more. Jason gives his piece a launch into the water which sends about five or six ducks going after it. Kramer tears off more and more pieces taking some for himself and giving the rest to Jason. They continue to throw bread until the run out. Jason: That was awesome! ((he says sarcastically wanting badly to leave)) Kramer: Sitting out here for only a few minutes has already got my mind racing. Jason: Oh really? Staying out here for a few minutes has activated my bladder. Kramer: Its sad to think that this could be my very last week in PCW. Jason shoots a concerned gaze over to Kramer as he responds in a stern manner. Jason: How so? Kramer: Well I was one of the unfortunate people picked for the Roulette of Death battle royal on Sabotage. But that is totally understandable consider my work rate has sucked immensely. Jason: Well then the answer is simple. You are just going to have to win that battle royal! Kramer: Sure its easy for you to say because you aren't in it. I have to face some very tough people and I am just not sure if I can handle it or not. I never have done too well with battle royals in all of my history. I always slip up at the wrong moment costing me the match. Jason: So everyone who loses the battle royal is out of PCW for good!? Kramer: No...there are other chances throughout the night to keep your contract, but I would much rather just win the battle royal it be done with it, then to have to risk getting a partner who is crap causing me to lose more throughout the night. Jason: I thought you just said that the competition would be tough? So why would you worry about getting a crappy partner? Kramer: I can't really explain it. I know it doesn't make sense but to me it does. Jason: Whatever. All I have to say is worry about it when the time comes. Right now you have to worry about your match against the Forgotten Ones. Kramer: Never fear, I never forgot about my match. You know its kind of depressing when you get no response whatsoever from your partners in a match. I still have yet to hear anything from Leblanc and Grant which is making me sweat. Jason: You don't really have to hear anything from those guys. They are big fellows and I am sure they are getting prepared for the match up. Besides if you are going by that then also take into perspect that the only one on the opposing team who has said something is Crichton. Kramer: This I know. Yeah he goes on about wanting revenge for the unfair advantage I had when I last went against him. But like he said, this time the sides are even giving no team a real advantage. Although.......Leblanc was kicked out of UA which might cause some heat between Grant and him. If thats the case then I am in a real shit box and I can't find my way out. Jason: Ah you still got the power of chicken to guide you to victory. Kramer: GOD BLESS CHICKEN!! Jason: Enough chit chat, can we leave now? These rocks are starting to crawl up my ass. Kramer: Good point. I don't want to be late anyway. The two get to their feet and start to walk away. What they don't see are the ducks following closely behind looking pretty upset that they weren't given more food. A unison of quacking begins causing Kramer and Jason to turn around. As they do the ducks attack! We see feathers fly all over as the scene slowly fades to black.
