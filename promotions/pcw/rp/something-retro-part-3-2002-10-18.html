Something Retro (Part 3)

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 18, 2002 [We return to Gladiator Arena after a commercial for the Mazda Protégé, with that really annoying “Zoom, Zoom” kid. We see three platforms, painted in red and black, surrounding a giant boom with a big pile of balls coloured yellow at the bottom, blue at the middle, and red on the top, all fastened to the boom with Velcro. The boom is suspended about thirty to thirty-five feet in the air. All six people on the platforms are wearing bungee cords.] Mike: Welcome back, as we join into event number three of our primetime special, and that is Swingshot. Larry: In this event, the competitors have forty-five seconds to bounce off the ground with their cords, and gather as many of the scoring markers as they can before the time runs out. The person who gets the most, will win the event. Mike: That’s right, Larry, and the scoring breakdown is like this…The yellow markers are worth one point, the blue markers worth two, and the red markers worth three. Larry: Now, and interesting twist to this event, is the fact that there are three contenders and three Gladiators. Those three are Gemini, Turbo, and Hawk. Mike: The Hawk is going to soar here, we are ready to go, so down to our referee, Larry Thompson. Larry #2: Contenders Ready! …Gladiators Ready! (Blows Whistle) Mike: Everyone left, except for Eddie who is delaying? Larry: This may be good strategy by Eddie to get as many balls as he can carry. Mike: Wesley and Rico encounter all three Gladiators and it looks to me that they have no points off the bat here…but here comes Eddie! Larry: Let’s see how much he can pick up. [Eddie leaps off the platform to the floor, and then with a superman like leap, the crowd jumps to it’s feet to cheer the amazing effort.] Larry: Holy shit! Mike: THREE RED SCORING MARKERS! THAT’S NINE POINTS FOR EDDIE! Larry: Incredible strategy by Eddie to get those markers. Rico and Wesley now going, and it is looking like they are going to get a free pass from the gladiators to try to even up the odds. Mike: Up they go…. [The crowd comes to it’s feet again! This time with even more energy!] Mike: I can’t see…both bodies… Larry: Yup…Two Scoops and the Gay man are locked together with their bungee cords. Mike: However, I can see a blue…and a red for Rico…and two blue for Two Scoops….I think? POMF! (Whistle Blows!) Mike: What the hell? Oh my God! Look at Hawk! Larry: YIKES! SOMEONE GET THE EMT’S OUT HERE! WHO DID THAT?! Mike: Let’s take a look at the replay here… [The AG graphics fly across the screen as we now see a slow-motion instant replay of the events that occurred away from camera view. Eddie Mandrake went for another ball run, and Hawk pursued. When the met at the scoring boom, Mandrake with all his leg strength, kicked Hawk in the head, during the replay, you can see how far back the head snaps due to the force of the kick, rendering Hawk unconscious, he drops hard to the arena floor, making the sickening sound.] Mike: Oh my God. This is truly bad for us here. We have the trainers out, and the EMT’s out, attending to Hawk. Larry Thompson is yelling at Eddie Mandrake, but the crowd is at the loud murmur point, we can’t really hear them argue. Larry: I seriously hope that Hawk is going to be okay here. It would be a damn shame to see the event end like this. Mike: I totally agree Larry. I mean, that kick was vicious, and not only that, leading with your feet is illegal in this event. Wait…I am getting word that Larry Thompson has a decision on this…and I am pretty sure we know what it is, Larry? [The camera comes into Larry Thompson, who looks visibly angry.] Larry #2: Mike, the contender Eddie Mandrake has been disqualified from this event, it will be two on two for the remaining twenty seconds, and all of Eddie’s points will be placed back on the scoring boom. Mike: Well, there you go, Eddie Mandrake has been disqualified. Larry: Well, I can see that the stretcher is here, the trainer will have a preliminary report due for us at Half-time after this event. Mike: Okay, let’s get back to the game here… (Whistle Blows!) Larry: Everyone’s off and bouncing again…Rico’s got a blue ball…Wesley’s got a blue ball as well… Mike: Ten seconds left, time for one more pass…They’re up…THEY ALL HAVE RED BALLS! Larry: That sounds so bad, Mike…control your feelings. Mike: Shut up, moron! (Whistle Blows!) Larry: Well, that’s the end of the event…Wesley Berry scored…nine points in the event. Rico Costantino scored…eight points in the event, so after three events here…Wesley Berry takes the lead with nineteen points, Rico Costantino in a close second with eighteen, and Eddie Mandrake, due to his DQ, is far behind in fifteen. [The camera goes from the floor to the announce position where Larry and Mike are sitting with microphones.] Mike: It’s half-time here on American Gladiators, and what we have just seen is one of the most violent acts in the history of this program. Larry: A contender, completely disregarding the safety of the Gladiators. I think this was one reason why this show was cancelled. Mike: Well, let’s go to the Gladiator Locker-room where we have our trainer Pepe Cartman, and Nitro… [Camera switches to the locker room, where we see the trainer, wearing a black golf shirt with the American Gladiators logo on his left breast, and Nitro, still wearing his Gladiator garb, but with a neck brace on.] Mike: First Pepe, tell us the condition of Hawk. Pepe: Well, When I ran out there, Hawk had lost feeling in the lower half of his body, and has been taken to hospital for observation, and tests to hopefully confirm that the paralysis that he suffered is only temporary. Mike: Okay, thanks for that, and Nitro, you watching that, has to make your blood boil. Nitro: Mike, it does, seriously. If this asshole wants to play the games dirty, we will return that in kind. Eddie, I will get my receipt, that is a promise! Mike: Now, let’s head to the offender…This real butt-plug in my opinion… [The camera switches to Eddie Mandrake’s locker-room] Eddie: Mike, you obviously know a lot about butt-plugs. Mike: You shut your fucking mouth, what gives you the right to beat up the Gladiators?! Eddie: Simple, my survival instincts take over. If they hurt me, I’ll hurt them twice as bad… Larry: So them doing harm to you give you the right to beat the hell out of the gladiators?! Eddie: Your damn…GAH! [At that moment, Laser rushes into the room and begins to throw punches at Eddie. Eddie of course is returning fire, and the security rushes in and separates the two, there is a lot of yelling, and the tensions are high.] Laser: (Yelling) Bitch, I’ll see you in Atlasphere! Eddie: (Yelling) Bring it on, Fucktard! [The camera goes to a calmer place. The other contenders locker room, as they warm up.] Mike: Up next, the Joust so you don’t want to miss it! [Fade to commercial.]
