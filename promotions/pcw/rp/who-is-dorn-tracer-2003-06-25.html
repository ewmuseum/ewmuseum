Who IS Dorn Tracer?

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 25 - 03 So, just who is Dorn Tracer? Is he that egomaniacal asshole that graces our television screens every Tuesday and Friday night? Is he the devote lunatic that maims, harms, and destroys everything he can in the name of what some claim to be a false deity? Or is he a paranoid, insecure, dark human being with a burning vat of pain and hate sitting at the bottom of his heart? The answer: None of the above. So why do people think this about him? Contrary to what he believes, it’s NOT his religious preference. Some could care less whether or not he hailed En’Flarim, Allah, God, Buddha, or George Dubya’s toupee. But yet, they still detest the man. The mere mention of his name brings venomous words to their lips, crying out for critical harm to be bestowed upon his head and very LIFE! Do people hate him because he’s different from them religiously? No. Do people despise him because he doesn’t agree with THEIR religion? Some might, but not everyone. Then what is it about him? Sure he’s a prick most of the time, but that’s just on the inside. What does this man really think? What is he thinking when he walks to the ring to berate whoever? Is he thinking, “Man, I really hate this schmuck.”? Or is he thinking, “I hate this bastard because he hates me.”? The latter seems more likely. In all reality, Dorn is exactly like the rest of us. Dwelling DEEP in the back of the mind of every human being in an inherent evil. Some are born with a lust for material possessions, that which is known as greed. Others seem to be twisted and gnarled into serial killers just because their daddies made them clean their rooms. Others are prone to vicious anger when some don’t agree with them. This is called PRIDE: The defense of one’s beliefs and values against opposing views. And PRIDE is exactly the inherent evil that dwells in the back of Dorn Tracer’s mind. Does that make him a bad person? Absolutely not. After all, no one is perfect and you cannot call them a criminal that should burn in Hell because of that. Dorn merely has a stronger sense of pride than most, and its source is rather typical. Born to a family where his father never agreed with him and his brother constantly tortured him, Dorn probably felt worthless or degraded. His mother sided with him, but they were both constantly shot down. When Dorn would share a view of his on something, his father would scoff and forget Dorn even said anything. Why? Because Dorn is his son. His experience-less son. Born to an unknown world without any real knowledge. So how does he know anything? Dorn felt the same way and hated it. So he decided to go with something that made him feel good. Astrology was that cure. His religion. His passion. His sense of being. And when the Goddess En’Flarim bestowed upon him a great destiny and task, it filled his soul with beaming pride and purpose. He was no longer the nothing his father made him out to be. He was somebody, and he wasn’t going to let anyone make him think otherwise. His father’s words had such great effect because Dorn was young and was taught his parents were usually right. This hurt little Dorn so much. But soon, the wounds healed due to his new found glory. Some say his pride has gone to his head. They say he has an ego. He’s an asshole who has no respect for anyone. He’s the bane of society and shouldn’t be allowed to run free. These are rough views expressed by hypocrites. Who’s to say who’s right and wrong? Perhaps Dorn thinks THEY shouldn’t be allowed to run around. It’s all a thing of pride. “I’m not wrong; YOU are!” That’s exactly what it is. And honestly, it’s not a crime. It’s just a sense of being. It protects a young mind, casting a rock-hard shell over it. That’s why people are sometimes called “thick-headed”. They’re protecting themselves from feeling any worse. They don’t want to feel worse, they want to feel better. They want purpose. They want meaning. And they want their hearts to soar with self-pride and the love for oneself because that’s all that matters. If they can be happy with themselves, then that’s all that matters. So, is Dorn Tracers still a criminal? A psycho? An egomaniac? No. He just wants to be happy. Happy with life, happy with himself, and happy with his current standing. He has a strong sense of pride, and this is not wrong. Dorn Tracer is just a regular human being… … who wants nothing more… than to be happy.
