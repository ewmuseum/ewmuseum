From Nightmare To Dream

Date: Winter of 1996 Amongst the filthy garbage and clammy alley lies a man without worry in his mind. He lives his life day by day even without fame, glory, and perfection. With his grubby and disproportional garments, comes a sense of contentment that even the rich three twins of the city, Jackson, Chris, and Tessa, cannot elaborate on. The foul smell of the alleyway is also his scent, his fragrance. It is the foul stench of the hideous rats, thick mud, smashed beer cans, and spoiled food that until today the three twins haven’t smelled in their life. But just like what everyone say, there’s always a first time. The man rolls around the artificial turf he found on the dumpster two days ago and continues on his snoring. The turf was his king-sized bed and he has it for himself. No other scavenger could sleep in his property or even touch one fabric of it. This bed of his is a factory of dreams. Whenever he lies on this turf, happy thoughts come into his cerebral cortex. Perhaps, that’s one of the reasons why he is so gratified. Wait. Look. It’s happening now! Tessa! I will freaking kill you! He runs after her with a corndog and is about to hit her in the face. But Tessa, like a petite cat, scuttles away from the stinky man that is pursuing him. It was Chris! Erik, I swear, it was Chris! She maintains on running, letting her infantile voice travel beyond her back and hopefully reach Erik whenever it was possible. Without sign of tiredness, Erik continues on the pursuit. I know it was you, Tess. Chris’ hand isn’t that small! The corndog is getting cold from the wind hitting it, but it didn’t matter. The goal is to hit Tessa with it. Erik! Chris put that dung heap on your face, not me! I swear! It was you! With an angry look on his face, and well, manure also, Erik stops in his tracks. Tess, time to die. The corndog went flying into Tessa’s direction like a lance waiting to slaughter the enemy. The corndog was 2 inches away from striking Tessa in her spinal cord to completely kill her when… “Waaaiiiiitttttttt!!! That’s my corndog!” Erik exclaims after the atrocious nightmare. His body sits up and looks unswervingly at his surroundings, which was bestrewed by desertedness and two dumpsters on each side of the brick walls. The first dumpster was two feet away from him, acting as if it was his garage. “What the fuck happened?” he asks himself slowly and bewilderingly. “A girl puts shit on my face and then I was still kind enough to give her a corndog? That was a horrifying nightmare! It doesn’t get any worst than a bum giving food to a rich white girl!” “Will you shut the fuck up?” The lid of the dumpster swings open and an iniquitous voice comes out of a man that appears to have been disturbed from him sleep. “I’m trying to take a rest here, can you keep it down, Erik? Please?!” ‘Please,’ a request that came out as an order. “You shut up, Adam. And close that dumpster of yours. You obviously haven’t had any nightmares lately. Just all wet dreams, you perverted ape!” “Don’t call me an ape. I swear to God, Erik, I’m gonna tell Mayor Chris and Governor Jackson that you're a chaotic citizen that keeps disturbing the freaking peace!” Adam threatens as if the political officials are his acquaintances. “Oh, spare me the hazards,” Erik replies securely, with his horrendous nightmare still imprinted on the back of his noddle. “The only thing those two twins, Chrissy and Jackie, are good at is kissing their other twin’s ass, Tessa!” Erik lays his back down to his king-sized bed again - his hideous turf, in other words - and pays no attention to Adam and his senseless ramblings anymore. He closes his eyes shut like a millionaire taking a nap in his rooftop, safe and affluent. In the onyx background, he dreams again but this time, he is still fully awake. He asks Adam a question that had little to do with their discussion previously, “Adam, can you imagine you and I tagging up and beating the crap out of Mayor Chris and Governor Jackson?” He doesn’t provide Adam the time to speak but rather, he continues. “You know, like freaking wrestlers. You and I should be called the Dumpster Boys. Can you feel it, Adam? The Weedville New York Elites versus the Dumpster Boys! Oh man, that would be off the damn hook!” “Erik,” The guy on the dumpster summons his alley-friend. “What, Adam?” “Shut the fuck up.” ~ o ~ o ~ o ~ Three o’clock in a Monday morning. Still foggy and every soul seems to slumber in their own private nests still. The lids of the dumpster is already open and Adam is now on the look for his breakfast. But on the alleyway, a man lies still; snoring, waiting for a corndog to approach him automatically perhaps, like a breakfast-in-bed perchance. In the obscurity of the alleyway comes a man on a black trench coat and a business suit within, signaling no poverty whatsoever. Why he would come into this place, it is unknown. But he starts walking toward the area where the two dumpster lay inert on each side of the brick walls. “Damn! It fucking smells in here.” He swathes his nose with his wealthy finger, asking himself why he had come to this situate three hours just past midnight. But his curiosity and desperate search for the next man he would enslave and craft as a laughingstock of the city was just too much for him to handle. Two feet away from the opened dumpster on the east side of the alley, the man sees a green turf forming a geometry of a man. “Gotcha.” He looks at the street beyond where he came from prior to and starts whistling as if it was some type of signal. He turns back to the green turf covering an entity in shape of a man. With his intangible defiance overcoming his nervousness, he slowly pulls the turf off of whatever creature is beneath it. A flash of monstrosity is discharged from the sight of an individual with his eyes closed, mouth open, exhaling bad malodorous air, and beer bottles all over him, making the smell of the alley even worst. The man wearing a trench coat steps back in distress despite all the gallantry he once had. “Jackson, you still get startled by these kinds of swine?” a man wearing the same attire as Jackson asks disappointingly from behind, making Jackson shocked some more. The tone of his voice suggests that they have known each other for so long; long enough to orbit the whole planet whenever he wishes to. “You just don’t amend, do you?” Jackson steps away from the drunk man lying on the turf some more and responds – with his back still turned – to his twin brother. “Heh, Chris, you know I’ve never been in this part of city before. When I was elected…” “When you was elected,” Chris interrupts hastily, “you’ve always been in well-lighted places unlike this one. What’s wrong, Jackson, you scared of the dark?” The tease from Chris’ voice cuts through his twin brother’s manhood. “Just shut up,” Jackson merely responds with three words that don’t need a genius to comprehend. “So, are we going to do this or not? We haven’t done this for years. You think you can pull it out again?” Mayor Chris simply beams. ~ o ~ o ~ o ~ “What the fu–” Erik wakes up in his king-sized bed. Not in his green turf in the putrid alley, but in a real king-sized bed. The rays of the sun coming in from the colossal golden windows indicate a sign of serenity and pure tranquility. Around the gold-shaded wallpaper of the room were beautiful paintings of such artists as Van Gogh, Leonardo da Vinci, Pablo Picasso, Michelangelo Buonarroti, and many more of other various painters. Erik sits up and looks at his surrounding in absolute peculiarity. One moment he was in the streets, the next moment he was a prince. He stumbles down the bed and finds himself landing on the clean, carpeted floor with flourish and monarch designs. Just under the bed was a pair of slippers, new and elegant. Erik stands up and starts slapping his face to try and wake up from his dream. But it was reality. “DAMN! This is real!” “Sir, are you ready to eat your breakfast?” a servant, holding a silver platter in his hands, asks the emperor as he enters the room. “Are those corndogs? Wait, are you talking to me?” “Of course, sir.” “Wait up, dude, I’m not supposed to be here.” Despite the wealth before Erik, the honesty within him still overtakes. “Sir, I must inform you that you have been drunk for ten days. We were so worried about you. Even your wife.” “My wife?” the man from the alleyway asks with utmost deliberation. The servant nods reassuringly. “Yessir, your wife.” ~ o ~ o ~ o ~ Peeking through the door, Chris and Jackson laugh uncontrollably together with their plans going as planned. From the hallway, someone approaches them wearing a gorgeous dress, showing a little cleavage. The twins see the individual and lessen their laughters. “Are you ready, girl,” Chris asks and cackles again. “Of course, I am,” the lady wearing the lustful dress speaks in a low-toned voice! “Remember, your name isn’t Romantico! It’s Romantica. Now go ahead and act like Erik’s solicitous wife,” Jackson articulates his final instructions and cackles as well. The lady, or the gentleman rather, turns the knob and brings his long blonde wig inside the room.
