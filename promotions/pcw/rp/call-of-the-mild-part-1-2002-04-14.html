Call of the Mild Part #1

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 The camera fades in… The Chef and Bertha are shown hiking up what seems to be a nature trail. They pace up the trail of mulch and stone and take a gander at the fine scenery that makes a postcard from Yosemite National Park seem like a pile of crap. Rows and rows of evergreens are scattered throughout the landscape, and the pond some two hundred feet below them glistens with the bright reflection of the yellow sun above. The Chef: Praise be to Shamash for this wonderful day! Bertha: Uh, f*cko… Aren’t you supposed to f*cking be a mother f*cking Muslim and not a queer as f*ck Mesopotamian!?!? The Chef: Listen B, you call the sun Mr. Shine Shine; so I can call it anything I want! Bertha: F*cking whatever f*ckface… The Chef: This mulched trail is very coarse, what kind of mulch is it? Bertha kneels down on the ground and sniffs the mulch. She then grabs a few chips of wood and rubs them in her hands. She hops back up to her feet, ready with the scientific report for the trail. Bertha: It’s f*cking Cypress Mulch… The Chef: Ah, I see, well at least it isn’t horse manure… Bertha nods. The Chef takes a whiff of the crystal clear air, and reluctantly sighs. The Chef: Bertha, remember that spot in Spaceballs when Mel Brooks popped the top of that can of “Perri-AIR” to escape the pollution of Planet Spaceball? Bertha shrugs? The Chef: Well maybe we can do the same thing! Bertha’s face lights up. Bertha: And people could f*cking pay us mother f*ckers in sh*tty @$$ cheeseburgers!!! F*ck yeah!!! The Chef gives Bertha a stern glance. The Chef: I don’t think so… I was thinking maybe we could sell clean French Alps air in the American market…for money! Bertha hands her head. Bertha: (Mumbling) Always using your mother f*cking ideas… The Chef: I’ll make plans to fly to Alouette Manor at once! Bertha: Can I f*cking come!?!? The Chef: Um, well, you see, I don’t think you would be good for business… Bertha: F*cking why Chicken Thigh? The Chef: Well, in case you haven’t noticed, let me replay a scene to you from my past promos… The Chef reaches into his backpack and to the surprise of Bertha, he pulls out a portable DVD player. He slides in a DVD with the Title “My Promos” and lets the machine do its thing. ***A scene comes up in the middle of a Chef promo*** The Chef: The weapon, the weapon is here! The Chef tries to open the latch but the door is locked. The Chef grabs an old woman sitting near the back of the bus by the collar and questions her. The Chef: How can I get the door open!?!? Old Woman: I’ll never te-ell! The Chef: Jumping Jambalaya woman! This isn’t a Michael Douglas movie! This is real life, and you are all going to… Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock…Tick Tock… Before the Chef can finish his sentence… KAAAAAAABBBBBBOOOOOOOOOMMMM!!!!! In a rush of water and a nutty aura of smoke, the whole rear of the bus bursts and sends all the passengers flying in opposite directions across the county area. The Chef lands in front of a stop sign two blocks east of the explosion. The Chef: Why…why….why….? The Chef looks down at the ground, as if he was ashamed for his actions… The Chef: Why did I let Bertha eat at Long John Silver’s!?!? WHY ALLAH!?!? WHY!?!?! ***The promo ends*** Bertha: So what are you f*cking saying f*ckdaddy!?!? The Chef: I don’t want you polluting France’s air like you polluted New York’s, ok? Bertha lets out a big “F*cking HMMMPPPHHH!” and crosses her arms. The Chef walks over and puts his arm around Big B. The Chef: There there my raspberry tart. I’m sure you’ll be fine here on your own without my guidance. Bertha: I’ll be f*cking fine! I just want to come with you so I can f*cking get b*tch @$$ed f*cked in another country for f*cking once! The Chef: Even if I did take you, there would be no hanky panky. This would clearly be a business trip, end of discussion! Bertha: Bu… The Chef slams his right foot on the trail causing chips of wood to fly around. The Chef: End of discussion!!! Now please continue walking up the trail… I need a quiet place of solitude to praise Allah over and over… Bertha starts to walk up the trail. Suddenly, to the Culinary Couple’s dismay, they hear a wild howl. Bertha: What the f*ck was that? The Chef: Don’t get your Lane Bryant panties in a… But before the Chef can complete his witty remark, a vile creature hops out of the brush! Bertha: F*cking AAAHHHHHHH!!! The Chef: Good heavens! The Chef hops up and Bertha catches him.The vile creature, which turns out to be a bunny rabbit, hops along the trail and jumps into the brush on the other side. Bertha: F*cking Whew… The Chef hops backs down from Bertha. The Chef: (Laughing almost frantically) Hehe, we were, hehe, scared of that little, hehe, bunny! Bertha: F*cking HA F*cking HA! The Chef: Wait a second, that bunny hopped across that trail pretty fast, and it still doesn’t explain what was howling…uh oh… The Chef gasps. Bertha: F*cking run! The Chef: AAAAHHHHHH!!! The Culinary Couple sprints back down the trail towards their brand new camouflage BMW SUV, personally financed by Osama Bin Laden (unknowingly of course). Bertha tries to catch up with her mate, but the rows and rows of flab attached to her body impede her forward progress. Sweat begins to pour down from her head as she flops over onto the trail. Bertha: F*cking…gotta…run…f*cking…gotta…hide…f*cking…break on through…to the f*cking…other…side….f*cking break on through…to…the…other………..f*cking side…. Bertha lies unconscious on the ground. The Chef heard the violent thud on the trail and turns around. The Chef: Bertha!!! A thought then comes to his head. “Come on Chef, get out of there. She’s going to be bait! You can always make another clone, you’ll just have to unearth Bertha’s grave again!” The Chef ponders what to do… The Chef: I know, I’ll ask Allah! The Chef gets down on his knees and prostrates on the trail. The Chef: Gracious Allah, please hear my call for safety and…AAAHHHHHH!!! Suddenly, out of nowhere, a ravenous beast jumps out of the bushes and lands on the Chef! The creature violently tears at the Chef’s uniform and the Culinary Conquistador screams in agony. The camera pauses. Narrator: Will the Chef and Bertha survive their nature hike? Will the hungry beast eat the Chef? What does this have to do with the Chef’s upcoming match!?!? We’ll find out…Same Chef Time…Same Chef Channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The camera fades to black.
