Jessica, I have something to show you....

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 Jeremy: Now he's crossed the f^cking line! The hatred between Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon continues to grow deeper. Yesterday, while the Krayzie One chose to take a day off from the cameras that continuously monitor his life, his opponent for Spring Fling and tonight's Fusion decided to make a powerful allegation. To the Gold Patron Meritorious, as Kannon likes to call himself on many occasions, it was the Krazed Lunatic that sent Drake Maxwell to his death, not the banishment he recieved on Turmoil. For some people, that might be a befitting end to the Fallen Angel. And, while the Krazed Lunatic doesn't feel a bit of remorse for the events that transpired at March Madness, he'd never wish this upon another wrestler. What happened on Turmoil was Drake Maxwell's own fault and his only. Xavier Kannon blaming Maxwell's 'death' on the Krayzie, is just another way for Kannon to take a stab at Jeremy Howard. Sad thing is, this time he pushed a few too many of Krayzie's buttons. Which, is now becoming quite commonplace. Nevertheless, there's still something else the Krayzie One has to do today other than bitch about Xavier Kannon. So, as the scene fades in from black to the backyard of the Jeremy Howard's luxurious Elkhart, Indiana home, cameras pick up the Krayzie One and the lovely Jessica talking back and forth. This time though, Jeremy doesn't seem as hot as he was the last time he cut a promo in his Cadillac Escalade. He actually appears to be calm, cool and collected. Jeremy: Blaming me for Maxwell's death? What kind of sh!t is that?!? Jessica, whose body is leaning up against the white exterior of her home, tries to downplay it as best as he can. But, with Krayzie, thats easier said than done. Jessica: You know he's just trying to stir the pot as much as he can. Jeremy: He's really starting to get to my nerves. Jessica, wearing a light blue leather bodysuit that leaves little to the imagination on her curves, looks toward Troy. The little two year old child of Jeremy and Jessica is playing with his uncle Justin in the massive sized pool with a waterfall attached to it, having a great time. Jessica: I think I know just how to change that? Jeremy: How? Jessica smiles, still looking toward Troy and Justin. Jessica: They both sure are having fun with each other. Jeremy knods his head, agreeing with her. Jessica: So, why don't we just leave them be? The petite lady grabs Jeremy by the collar of his light blue Starter mesh shirt, pulling him into the house. Still smiling, Jessica plants a luscious kiss on his face. Jessica: Sometimes, even superstars need some time to just chill. She plants another kiss on his lips, being even more provocative. Even a man as tough and strong willed as the Krazed Lunatic can have his heart melted. Jeremy falls back onto plush blue couch seen many times before in Jeremy's office at Greed Inc. when he lead them. Jessica comes to a rest on top of him, continuing with the passion of before. This love filled kiss fest lasts for a few minutes, until the splashes of Troy and Justin is heard just outside to the back door. Jessica: Wait. We should go somewhere else. A sly grin crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: I know just where to go. Jessica: You do? Jeremy shakes his head up and down, signalling 'yes'. Then, he motions for her to follow him. To the immediate right of the back door, if looking at the floor plan of the house from the front door, is a door that leads to the eating and kitchen area of the house. And, that is where Jeremy leads her. He leads her right past the eating and kitchen area, directly into the garage. The garage? Jessica: The garage? Isn't this kind of dirty? The sly grin on Jeremy's face widens into a smile. Behind here, the rest of the monstrous sized garage is revealed, showing numerous expensive vehicles the Krayzie One has bought from his earnings with PCW. Or, some of the money he laundered from Tyler Nelson. Jeremy: Just follow me. I have something I really want to show you. Jessica: In the garage...... Okay. Jeremy makes a motion with his right hand index finger, 'follow me'. Jessica complies, passing by a black 2002 Cadillac Escalade, a silver 2002 Mercedes Benz SLK AMG, a silver 2002 Infiniti G5, a black 1999 Lexus RX300, a black 1999 Jaguar XK180. Finally, the last car seen is a black 1998 Mitsubishi Eclipse. Jessica: Wow! Its your very first car! Jeremy: I had it restored. I've had it stored away for quite some time now. Jessica's eyes light up. Jessica: I remember you buying this shortly after they took your brother. Jeremy: Yes, but thats not why I brought you out here. That is. Jessica follows the length of Jeremy's arm, looking to see where Jeremy is pointing. Right next to the Eclipse, is another car with a blue tarp over it. From what is seen, its a car of some kind. But what? Jeremy: I think you'll remember this one, also. Jeremy makes his way over to the car with the tarp on it. He stands right beside it, smiling. Jessica: What's under there? Jeremy: You'll have to wait and see. Jeremy reaches out, grabbing her sexy form with his hands, thrusting her to him. Their bodies meet, touching like lovers bodies should touch. Jessica: Honey, I know you're excited about showing me what's under that tarp, but I have something I have to confess to you. Jeremy: What's that? Jessica gets kind of tense, not so much the melting heart she was two seconds ago. Jessica: I'm scared of getting in the ring tonight at Fusion. I don't have any wrestling experience at all. Jeremy: I'll take care of it. I already know what I want to do. Jessica: Do tell. Jeremy: I will later. I still have the Chef to think about also. If anyone can remember back to the second 64 man tournament PCW held upon the restart of the company, they'll be able to remember the second round. For, it was in that around that Jeremy Howard beat the Chef to advance on in the tournament. At Turmoil, the two men meet each other again... for the Continental title. Jessica: You've already beaten him. Jessica falls back against the tarp, feeling up the car some to get an idea of what's underneath. Jeremy: Well, lets hope I can do the same thing on Turmoil. Jessica: Lets hope his girlfriend doesn't try to sit on you. Jeremy sighs. Jeremy: I don't know which is worse. The thought of the Bertha clone sitting on me, or having to endure a match with a Islamic rambling jackass. Jeremy shakes his head, shuddering at the thought. Jeremy: Enough about that sick sh!t, I have something to show you. Jeremy, showing off the muscles that helped him in his fight against the Russian Bear known as Ivan Stanislav, grabs the tarp and rips it off the car. Underneath it, is a red 1998 Ferrari 355 F1 Spider. Jeremy tosses the tarp to the side, turning his attention back toward Jessica. She's in shock. A good shock. This is the very first car anyone ever bought her. It was her eighteenth birthday present from her boyfriend at the time, Jeremy Howard. Jessica: It's..... It's..... my car! Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: Restored and good as new. Jessica: I love you with all of my heart. Jessica blinks, showing that raw emotion of love. Jessica: Thank you so much. Jeremy just smiles, soaking in the happiness his girlfriend feels. Jessica grabs the door handle, pulling the drivers side door open. Then, without any hesitation, Jessica latches onto Jeremy and pulls him into the Ferrari to resume the passion of earlier. As clothes begin to come off and more of Jessica's lovely body becomes exposed, the scene fades..... to black!
