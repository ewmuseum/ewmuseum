The Respect Saga: A Single Spark

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-13-03 'More for War! More for War! More for War!' the morons chanted in unision outside of Independance Hall, they were looking for more government support in the state for the War, Bush's War. That is all it is. Yet he has garnered enough support for it that mindless middle aged stock brokers and idiotic american 'patriots', and trust me, that is a insult to those in the 1700's who were ACTUAL patriots. So the thousands standing and pretending that their cause is right continue to think they are, indeed, right. Despite the fact the world is against them. The problem is that the more idiots involved in something, the more dangerous they are. The tension builds and the fact that the collective hundreds can only pronounce maybe one or two polysyllabic words on a good day (much like their leader) just adds fuel to the fire. A fire like this needs only a slight spark to set it off. A spark, if you really think about it, is a revolutionary. The powder keg is something that never changes, it sits there idle while most people just ignore it, it is consistent all the way through to the fuse that sticks out of its top. A flame could be lit, and its something the fuse and keg have never seen before, the fuse panics and begins to dissapear until the only thing left between destruction and safety is a small blast cap. Then, boom. Revolutionaries 1. Everyone else 0. The people part like a tide to the center of this debate, someone hotly argues with someone else, a young man with blue hair, he hands out pamphlets that have the truth about this war and this government inside them out to people who don't want them. He knows they don't care, but he does it anyway. 'You elitist capitalist PIGS! You complain to me about what i'm doing, you didn't ASK for me to be here, you say? Well did I ASK for the media too bombard me with your Oil War that is going to cost hundreds of thousands of lives.' DarkStar spits to the ground. 'NO!' 'Did I ask our government to create the most powerful bomb and test it in a show of penis size? Even though that SAME government just bitched and moaned like a cheap hooker who just got the dick slapped out of her mouth when North Korea pulled the same stunt? NO! It was brought down too us.' His eyes flamed up, angry, he knew within his body he had the power to influence these people with his voice, it came with the whole god thing. He also knew that he could easily kill each and ever person before they had time to sleep, that came with the whole evil thing. His powers, however, are not what he wants to use now. 'You idiots. Here, take these.' DarkStar chucked the pamphlets into the air angrily, 'You can have your war. You can have it all. You can also have the consequences of seeing your children's futures destroyed because of it.' The man who had been arguing spit at DarkStar while he walked away, before the spit even got close to him however, the man inexplicably drew a pocket knife out and slit his wrists with it, before he hit the ground his blood was flowing into a steady puddle. Noone understood why, but DarkStar did. He influenced him. He got into his head. Just for old times sake. 'Idiot.'
