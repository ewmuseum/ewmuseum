The Sage Of A Champion (Part 8)

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-15-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 8” Same - Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellSame - Day 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen ::The scene picks up from where we left off last time. Athens Mir has appeared out of thin air and he explained how he is still alive, then BladeD went on to talk about how he was originally on their side in a war back in 2000. Now talking about something called “Violent Ray” and all kinds of stuff:: Athens Mir: Yes BladeD you are correct. I was, in fact, are your side to begin with… See we have a few things in common. We are both infantrymen and we both have skills of an infantryman. You may be smarter here and there…. You may even have a lot more experience then me, but one thing is certain. When it comes to jobs as in spying and things of that sort. I can hang with people just like you. General P: I doubt that seriously, but go on. Athens Mir: I was going to retrieve the “Book Of Violent Ray” and it would have only cost us a few men here and there, but think of the lives it would have saved once Violent Ray was built like it is now. BladeD: Built all ready? WTF? General P: That was another thing I wanted to talk to you about, but forgot. I forgot to tell you that Violent Ray is almost finished. It won’t be too long before it is finished. That is why I was trying to get you to stop the nonsense about BattlePowers because I needed you to stop Violent Ray before it is complete or we are going have to use serious force to stop Violent Ray and it will put us in world war 3… Almost like what happened during the Metal Armour Age, only Violent Ray is ten times stronger then Metal Armour. BladeD: This is all insane. ::dead silence for a couple of seconds:: BladeD: Anyway, finish what you were saying Mir. Athens Mir: Well, see the thing is that I came here because I need your help BladeD. I couldn’t retrieve the book two years ago because I went into a coma, but there is still hope that we can stop Violent Ray. I just need you help me out. BladeD: No! I refuse to help you! You know full and well that I am a soloist. Athens Mir: Oh come on BladeD. Put your pride behind you for once and just let us partner up one time. I know that you love pride and don’t like giving up any of the spotlight, but come on BladeD, just this one time. BladeD: No Mir! All you would do is slow me down. That is why I wouldn’t do it! Athens Mir: Slow you down? Slow you down?! How can you say I would slow you down? Are skills are too similar for me to slow you down… You may be a little quicker here and there, but other than that we are just the same. BladeD: That “here and there” makes the difference… Because of that “here and there” quickness I have kept my life over “here and there” tactics that would get you killed because you don’t have, if it true that Violent Ray is that much more stronger then Metal Armour I wouldn’t team up with you for my life, because it would probably cost me my life trying to team up with you. Athens Mir: I can’t believe I am hearing this. BladeD: Athens. You are insane. You have truly lost your mind… These jobs can be done alone, but when becomes a group thing it always fails. Trust me! I have seen it happen with my own eyes! I know what I am talking about and I don’t need that happening again with something like this on are hands… Besides that I have heard the kind of crazy person you. You jump rooftops just barely landing on the other side, you just flamethrowers and try lighting up cigarettes with it, you even try going to prom in a tank top and shorts. General P: ::General P bursts out in laughter over the comment about he even tries going to prom in a tank top and short:: Man that is funny. BladeD: That may be funny, but it is the truth and the truth is that you are an insane man Athens. I don’t mean to hurt your feeling or anything, but you have to realize that a thing of this sort is not a group thing. It is totally a solo thing. Athens Mir: Fine then! If you don’t want to be a team, then we will both go, only I will go one way and you will go another. We will be on the same mission just spilt up in different ways. BladeD: WHAT!!! That is crazy talk! That could get us both killed and who even said I was going in the first place? I haven’t gotten BattlePowers settled yet. I just heard about this stuff today, so I would have to give it my usual thinking out, so I could get plans made and all that stuff, but before all of that I have to get BattlePowers straighten out, so I have no idea what you are talking about, but if you want to try and stop Violent Ray you can. I will just tell you this much…. You will not survive alone because I have seen you in action before and I know your weakness, so I know there is no chance you could tackle something like this on your own. Athens Mir: Even if that is true, that is one reason why I am trying to get you to go with me… Because I want to do this with a person like you that knows what he is doing. I mean, you have had experience with something like that… Everyone thought it was going to be the end of the world when Metal Armour came out and look what happen! You saved the world. Now we as a team have the chance to do the same thing all over again only this time we are tackling something greater then the first time around. BladeD: That is why I don’t want you involved in this. You haven’t had any kind of experience with something like the likes of Metal Armour, so there can’t be anyway you are ready for something like Violent Ray. I mean, I don’t even know if I am ready for Violent Ray. I am pretty sure if I am not ready for Violent Ray, then you are far from being ready. Athens Mir: If you are on my team I am ready for anything, but if I have to go in alone and the world is dead afterwards, it would all be because of you. BladeD: ::laughs: Naw, it would all be on you… but seriously, I would go in with you, but you would just slow me down and it could cost everyone there lives. General P: Ok! Will you two stop this fighting all ready? ::BladeD and Athens Mir look at each other for a second, then turn back towards the General. The room is silence during all of this and continues to be silent for a few seconds afterwards:: General P: Good. Now listen. I realize Mir that for some reason you want to be a part of this seeing as I guess you were in the beginning, but BladeD is right and you are right… BladeD would be slowdown by you, but at the same time you should be given the chance to be on this mission, since you have similar skills. The only problem is that BladeD is right on the fact that these things are solo mission, not group mission. He is also right about the dangers of you two going in at the same time, but BladeD. I think it is best that if you do decide to go on with this mission, that you allow Mir to be on the mission as well. You two would split up and do your own things. BladeD: General… you have got to be kidding me. I mean, if one of gets caught the other is sure to get caught… Or at least that is what I would assume. General P: I would assume that as well, but at the same time, you have to realize that Violent Ray is so much more powerful then Metal Armour and honestly, I don’t think you can take on Violent Ray on your own. I seriously believe that you need Mir there to assist you in this battle. BladeD: ::shakes his had in disapproval:: I would rather go on this alone, but you are the general, so I guess I have no chance, but to listen to you.General P: Good. Now that brings me to one other question on my mind. BladeD. How did you find out about Violent Ray and all those things about Mir being on our side? BladeD: Well Captain Kirk told me. General P: Where did he get the information? BladeD: He had said that a friend of his had classified information about Violent Ray and was good friends with Mir… but Kirk wouldn’t tell me who this friend was. General P: That is very interesting. ::The scene cuts from here… Slowly, but surely more and more information is coming out about all of these things that are going on. I don’t know about you… but the main question I have on my mind right now is what is going to happen to BattlePowers? I mean… Is BladeD going to drop it all for now and pick up on it later, so he can take this mission? Because he hasn’t accepted this mission yet and he doesn’t have to, so it would all be what is more important. The world? The BattlePowers? You would think the world, but at the same time he cares about BattlePowers being shutdown as well, so there is no telling what direction he is going in, plus is Athens Mir going to slow BladeD down in anyway? And can he truly be trusted? I guess you are going to have to keep up with the series and find out as the series carries on. Stay tuned for part 9!::
