Seduced by goggles...

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd of Sept 2002 “Look, I swear you won’t find any here!” “The Gods themselves have given me their word that I will find them here, Isiy! Do you dare doubt the Gods?” (Our shot open up in the ‘Regina Memorial Mall’ located in Regina, Canada. Panning out from the small enclosed area of shoe stores that we opened on, we find two shadowy figures peering about the crowded place; both of whom are currently divulged in a deep conversation.) “Actually, for once I do doubt them, Titu! Malls don’t sell goggles!” (Closing in a little, the two deliberating people are revealed as our Egyptian hero, ‘The Pharaoh’ Titu Xansiv, and his darling wife, Isiy.) The Pharaoh: “The Gods should never be doubted, my love! I can only hope they forgive you for uttering such a blasphemy.” (Isiy sighs.) Isiy: “Ok, sorry…” (Titu grins triumphantly.) The Pharaoh: “Excellent! Now come on, we must find them… before it’s too late!” (Isiy’s brow furrows.) Isiy: “Too late for what exactly?” The Pharaoh: “Well, too late for…” (He waves a hand in the air as though trying to shape his words.) The Pharaoh: “You know…too late for…for--…” Isiy: “Look, just come on…” (She grabs hold of his arm and pulls him further in the jungle of people.) (Our scene fades to black but re-opens almost immediately inside a pottery store. Brown clay ornaments and other small knick-knacks line the shelves of the shop as we venture further inside. Abruptly we come to the store’s counter where we rediscover the Egyptian couple arguing with a young man behind the desk.) The Pharaoh: “YOU DON’T SELL THEM?! What kind of a shop is this?!” Clerk: “Sir, we sell pottery and things to brighten up the garden. Unfortunately we do not sell…‘goggles’.” (The pottery clerk sighs.) Clerk: “Sorry--…” The Pharaoh: “Sometimes I just can’t believe the ignorance of you people!” (Titu points a long, unforgiving finger in the man’s direction.) The Pharaoh: “I, for one, can safely say that I won’t be shopping at the ‘Pottery Corner’ again!! I bid good day to you, sir!” (Xansiv storms out of the store, a furious expression brushed over his handsome visage.) Isiy: “Look I’m sorry about that. He’s a bit…eccentric.” (The clerk nods thoughtfully.) Clerk: “Heh, don’t worry about it. We get his sort all the time.” Isiy: Well, sorry again, anyhow.” (Isiy rushes out of the store in hot pursuit of her husband. She re-finds him standing in front of a portable ice-cream stall, chattering away to the gruff-looking man manning it. She catches up quickly but stops only meters away so as to overhear his words.) The Pharaoh: “And then I said ‘I, for one, won’t be coming here again, you little bastard!’. Personally I think I showed him who was boss, don’t you?” Ice-cream stall owner: “Look, are you going to buy an ice-cream, or what?” (Pharaoh looks hurt.) The Pharaoh: “But I was in the middle of telling you about my gripping tale!” Ice-cream stall owner: “And you can carry on telling me about it after you’ve actually bought something.” (Isiy steps up to the stall’s wooden counter and grabs her husband’s arm. She begins to pull him away, paying the man behind the portable work-pace a brief smile.) Isiy: “Sorry!” (Pharaoh tugs away from her grip and shakes his head.) The Pharaoh: “What are you doing, my love? I was in the middle of telling that man my story!” (Isiy forces a smile.) Isiy: “Unless you were going to essentially buy something I don’t think he was too bothered.” (Pharaoh’s brow wrinkles with contemplation.) The Pharaoh: “Bah! He was as interested in what happened back there as I was! But nevertheless we must keep going! I need to find those goggles! Follow me…” (Pharaoh speeds up to a slow jog as the cameras fade on Isiy’s frowning mug.) (We reopen in what looks like a typical department store. People are lined up in strenuous queues waiting, as patiently as they can, to be served. The lucky few at the front of the lines are in the process of being passed huge parcels in battered cardboard packaging; the toll of having waited hours to be given a new microwave oven easing from their minds. As for the rest of the store; well…they all gaze about in ‘wonder’ at the ‘amazing’ items on sale; contemplation on whether they should buy the new Hoover model arising in their skulls.) (Panning out a little, we pick up on two figures browsing through one of the store’s many catalogues. From the silk loincloth around his waist we immediately realize that these two are indeed The Pharaoh and his currently disgruntled wife, Isiy.) Isiy: “This is useless, Titu. This department store won’t sell goggles, either!” The Pharaoh: “You can’t spell hopeless without hope, Isiy!” (She lets out a deep sigh.) Isiy: “You’re making this entire trip seem dire! But what exactly is the relevance of these ‘goggles’, Huh? It’s not like you actually need them, or anything, right?” (Pharaoh flips through a few more pages.) The Pharaoh: “Alexander Miyagawa has been accustomed to wearing goggles for most of his PCW career, o goddess of my desires. In doing so they have brought him much luck and many victories! I swear down that there’s something special about them!” (Isiy ceases her actions.) Isiy: “You mean to tell me that we’re here so you can buy some of those idiotic…goggles?” The Pharaoh: “The Gods themselves have sworn by them, my dearest! They say they’ll bring me the strength I need to defeat him on Friday!” Isiy: “B.S!” (Titu turns in terror.) The Pharaoh: “WHERE?! SOMEONE SHOOT HIM!” (Isiy groans.) Isiy: “Not that B.S. I meant ‘Bullshit’! Those goggles he wears are just part of his gimmick, you idiot! And besides that B.S is dead, remember?” The Pharaoh: “Oh, yeah.” Isiy: “The Gods are wrong for once, Titu; can’t you see that?” (Xansiv looks shocked.) The Pharaoh: “The Gods are never wrong!” (He pauses as Isiy’s words sink in.) The Pharaoh: “Although now I think about it; going in search of a pair of goggles is quite inane.” (Isiy smiles.) Isiy: “So there you have it. Come on, we’re leavi--…” The Pharaoh: “Wait!” (Pharaoh stares transfixed on the page under his nose.) The Pharaoh: “Look! They actually have them!” (Isiy tilts her head to one side.) Isiy: “I thought we were going?” (Pharaoh sighs mournfully.) The Pharaoh: “Yes, but they have them! And the Gods did say--…” Isiy: “Titu, Come on. Seriously, now!” (She walks towards her husband; her light blue eyes catching the silvery goggles on the page below in the process. They twinkle.) Isiy: “Although…they are quite lovely--…NO! We must fight it!” (She hastily drags her eyes away from the page and grabs Pharaoh’s arm. He pulls away with forcefully.) The Pharaoh: “LEAVE ME BE!” Isiy: “Listen to me, Titu! I don’t know what those things have done, to you but you’re acting weird all of a sudden!” The Pharaoh: “I’ll be happy as long as I have my goggles! My precious--…” (Isiy rips the catalogue out of his hand and tosses it across the floor. People begin to take notice and gradually a crowd accumulates around them.) The Pharaoh: “NOOOOOOOOO!!” (He turns to his wife.) The Pharaoh: “What have you done, you fool?!” (Titu dives dramatically towards the catalogue but Isiy snatches his foot in mid-air! He SMACKS the ground face-first with a hard thud.) Crowd: “OooooOooo!!” (The Egyptian Beauty crawls to Titu’s side.) Isiy: “What have I done? I’ve just saved you from those…things!” (Pharaoh narrows his eyes spitefully and then eases.) The Pharaoh: “Thank… you…” (He gets to his feet and brushes himself off.) The Pharaoh: “I don’t know what those evil things did to me, but for a second I was completely seduced! They’re evil, Isiy, EVIL!” (The crowd starts to diminish as the couple leave the store.) Isiy: “Now I think we know why Miyagawa is how he is, eh?” The Pharaoh: “Indeed we do! That poor Japo-American” –he sighs – “he must be living in a personal Duat when wearing that demonic headwear. I will rid him of some pain come Fusion!” (Isiy glares at him blankly.) Isiy: “Rid him of pain? Don’t you mean you’ll cause him pain?” The Pharaoh: “Yeah, but…” (Isiy laughs.) Isiy: “Shut up while you’re still ahead, eh?” (Pharaoh frowns.) The Pharaoh: “Meh…” (The scene fades to black.)
