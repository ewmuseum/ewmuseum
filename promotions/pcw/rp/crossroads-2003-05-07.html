Crossroads

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/5/03 In life you will experience many crossroads, it could be something as simple as where are you going to eat today? McDonalds or do you go for the healthy option of a tuna and sweet corn sandwich bought from a cheap sandwich shop? Or it could be a life changing decision, which could affect you, and those you hold most dear to you forever. Have you ever sat at night and wondered what would of happened if? Life is full of ifs a pointless word which we will never know the truth of which leaves itself open to each individuals interpretation an example of which could be what if Hitler had won world war two? Everyone would have their own idea, which would be along the same sort of lines, but no one would know for certain. That’s the thing with ifs…you can just never tell. The night sky was surrounding James who was lying silently atop a hill staring at the stars as he had done some many times before staring at the constellations, the cool air whisping around him playing with his hair and brushing lightly against his skin. The stars were glistening light the bodies of a thousand angels all looking down on James, their gaze being returned by the lunar one who placed his hands gently behind his head supporting his neck breathing in deeply through his mouth. The sky tasted electric as the wind gained velocity and began to whip up small amounts of debris around James. A storm was brewing. Staring at the stars was a source of release for me, a way of helping me to realise my dream, keep the fire burning and the soul alive, once more the dream was going to be taken from me, for a short while at least while the elements danced their mystical dance. The storm would cleanse things make them real, for lately judgements had been clouded, decisions that had been made had not gone according to plan. I needed a storm in my life Out of nowhere the clouds were bought in on currents of wind, black and engulfing the sky. The stars, which were involved in a titanic duel, fighting trying to get their light to shine through and penetrate the clouds, it was all for nought however as the clouds won the battle and the stars for the moment dispersed, hiding waiting for their chance to strike once again. James’ link was now gone there was no point him lying there staring into the clouds, he would be deemed a mad man. He raised slowly and preceded to walk down the hill, his hands in his pockets of his dark blue denim jeans, trudging down each step being taken slowly in order for James to keep his balance. A few beads of rain dropped from the heavens and began to seep through James’ white t-shirt causing it to stick to his skin revealing his well-defined stomach. I have to make an impact, if I’m going to get the money together I have to step up my game. I need to make a name for myself, tagging with Skylar was just the first step I need to win gold…it will make people stand up and take notice of me. Look at Tracer, he held the Rising Star title for a record breaking amount of time he is now going nowhere but up…things had to be done I had to take a step in the right direction The dark gloomy sky was lit up with a huge bolt of lightning, which was quickly followed up by a large boom of thunder, which almost made the earth beneath James’ feet shake. It was as though the earth was angry with James…for what he did not know but something was wrong. James continued down the path, it felt as though the storm was chasing him wanting him to leave the hill and proceed on another path, maybe the weather was telling him something, maybe he had to forget his dream, for now at least in order to focus on the wait that was in front of him. I need to be able to focus more but that is not going to happen whilst I’m still dreaming about playing with rockets and going to space, I know that it’s my dream and I know that it is achievable for now though I have to be able to win wrestling bouts, I could lose every match for the next two years sure and maybe just maybe I would be able to fund myself, then maybe go through the training and then just maybe get accepted into a flight by 2009 or I can fight and I can win starting at Fusion. I don’t care how many other men I’m against or who my partners are if I have to fight all three by myself so be it. I will be victorious and I will make it to the stars. By now the storm was wearing off and the air was a lot clearer, the old pain and dirt had been erased and all that was left was a sense of calm and clarity. In sopping wet clothes James approached a fork in the cobbled road a left turn and a right turn. James had reached his crossroads; he knew that eventually he would get to the same destination it was just a matter of how long it would take. James glanced left and then right pondering his decision, trying to make some sense of it all before shrugging and making his decision. As James walked quietly down the road to the right the only noise the sound of his black leather shoes walking on the cobbled highway a small final burst of thunder boomed across the night sky. Maybe it was just the end of the storm or it was another message to James. Either way that night he had picked his path and for better or worse there was no turning back, as if you turn back in the middle of the night you never know what horrors you may see.
