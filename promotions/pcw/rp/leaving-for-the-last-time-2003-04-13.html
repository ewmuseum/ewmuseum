Leaving... for the last time...

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up on a bright sun shiny day on what seems to be the top of a building. You hear the sound of several blades slicing through the air as a helicopter is seated on the top of the building, waiting idly for its passengers. On the side of the helicopter you see the name “Semore Smoke” and a picture of the Primetime Championship Wrestling wrestler. From the rooftop of the building you can see the entire eastside of the city of Atlantic City from the gambling sector of town, to even the bad side of the tracks. The camera pans around the rooftop, which you can now see is huge, it’s about seventy stories up and about fifty yards by 30 yards base size. The roof of course is that size, so you could probably even play a small game of football on top of the roof. The camera zooms in on a doorway that leads downstairs and the door opens. You see Semore Smoke, two of his henchmen, and the kangaroo, Crespo, which is tied up in chains and the leash is being held by Smoke. The four of them walk towards the helicopter where you see one of the pilots get out of it and open up the back door for Smoke and the others. Smoke walks up to the helicopter and turns to his two henchman as he approaches the chopper… Semore Smoke: Guys, I’m gonna need you both to stay behind here. I know that the both of you have been accompanying me to the shows lately and you’ve met a bunch of the guys and enjoyed yourself while you met them, but I need to go at alone this week and with Crespo that will just make it even more dangerous on my part. So just stay here and hold the fort down for me. Try and make some headway on who killed my father, some information came in yesterday from London that could give us a lead and I want it analyzed pronto, so that could keep you two busy for the week anyways. {{{{The two henchmen nod their heads in understanding to their boss and turn away as they both say…}}}} Henchmen: Yes Boss. {{{{The two henchmen who are dressed in what seems to be second rate suits walk towards the door that leads downstairs and the two of them look a bit disappointed for some reason. They must have really wanted to badly as it seems to have put their hopes down. Smoke turns to Crespo and unchains the animal and Crespo looks back at Smoke as to ask “what the hell?”. Smoke just nods his head towards the helicopter…}}}} Semore Smoke: Go ahead Crespo, get in, this might be your last week in captivity and I know I told you that I was gonna make this week a living hell, but I lied. I wanted to get the boys back in the building before I did anything because I think you’re better to me as an ally then an enemy. And just because I hate AJ Trinidad’s guts, that shouldn’t give me the right to keep you tied up just because you’re his best friend. You get my meaning? {{{{The kangaroo doesn’t understand a word that Smoke says and Smoke just points to the helicopter and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: I’ll put it in simpler terms, you climb on chopper and so do I. We fly to Indianapolis where you see Trinidad again, and then we cut deal with Trinidad about maybe getting you back to Trinidad, I’ll use you for ransom at best now. {{{{The kangaroo somewhat understood Smoke and it gets on the chopper and so does Smoke in toe. Smoke takes off his suit jacket and throws it in to the back of the helicopter and then unbuttons his dress shirt to show a black shirt that has seemingly become a cult icon shirt now, written on it are the words “Got Pot?” and it’s the new Semore Smoke t-shirt that he’s worn around lately since his return to PCW just about a month or so ago. He takes the dress shirt off and then puts on a headset so he can talk to the pilots when the chopper is flying in the air towards Indiana. The pilot that had opened the back door closes the door and then climbs back into the driver seat of the helicopter. He puts on his headset and turns around to Smoke and smiles as he says…}}}} Pilot: Hello boss. I bet you didn’t know that some of us had pilot licenses. {{{{Smoke grins as he looks at the pilot…}}}} Semore Smoke: No, I didn’t know that. Where were you guys a year ago, I could have used these free trips instead of those expensive plane tickets. Well, it’s not free, I’ve still got to pay for the gas, but this is a fuel-efficient chopper, right? {{{{The pilot nods his head…}}}} Pilot: Yes boss. This chopper has the best mileage per gallon of any helicopter I’ve ever seen, I even think it kicks the ass of some military copters. {{{{Smoke shakes his head as to beg to differ…}}}} Semore Smoke: I was in the Army for a short time, trust me, the military gets the best of everything before the public does, I mean the military got jeeps first, then they got Hummer’s first, they always get the best first before us civilians do. But by the looks of things, this copter is top of the line, where did my pop get it anyways? {{{{The pilot shrugs his shoulders…}}}} Pilot: I didn’t work for your father, I just started working here about two weeks ago as a pilot, I saw an ad for it and I figured I’d sign on up for the job. Seems to pay well, I mean, I didn’t think I’d be flying you, but I’m glad I get to, you seem a lot nicer then I thought. {{{{A gunshot is heard away from the chopper, over the roaring of the blades in the air, so that means the shot was close. You see the two henchmen that Smoke sent back into the building coming out through the door again, firing as they come out, the FBI then bust out onto the top of the roof and Smoke just looks surprised as hell that the Feds are after him now. Smoke signals for the pilot to take off and the pilot waste no time to get started…}}}} Pilot: Off off we go, where we’ll stop, Indiana will be home. {{{{The chopper lifts off the top of the roof and Smoke looks down over the roof as it quickly flies away from his headquarters, which he’ll now have to abandon, after all the worrying of AJ Trinidad and Adam Stryker, he had forgotten all about the FBI and it’s blood lust of shutting down mafia families, that will surely put a slow stop to Smoke’s plans for a national crime family, one that could reach even to the White House. The scene fades on Smoke’s voice as he looks down upon the FBI agents who are shooting his men and at the helicopter, only to be missing the chopper…}}}} Semore Smoke: Why the fuck does all this bad shit happens to me? Looks like I’m heading back home with the new HQ… Wilson, North Carolina…
