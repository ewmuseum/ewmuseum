Tubular, For Men

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 30 [The scene opens on a sandy beach, in black and white. The tide rolls in very gently, the sound so soothing. The scene pans over to the left showing a very beautiful woman walking down the beach. Her brown hair curled to perfection, makeup done so perfectly, the sundress she wears fits perfectly on her body. To be blunt, everything about this woman is absolutely perfect. Her face is dropped in sorrow as she walks across the beach, trying to let the waves sooth her. The scene then changes to a young man waling up the other side of the beach. The hair pure white in this black and white sequence, his skin almost a shade of black, the Oakley sunglasses were simply made to be on his face, which they were. He wears a white buttoned down shirt, slightly open, which shows off his perfect muscular upper body, toned to perfection. The scene then changes to the woman, who is seen spying the young man coming up. She stops in her tracks, wanting this man to see her. The scene then goes to the young man, and he now sees this woman, perfection personified. He stops in his tracks, both only a span of 20 feet away from each other. The scene gives a close up on the woman's eyes, moving from side to side and then up and down, obviously sizing up this unbelievable man. The scene then shows a close up of the sunglasses on the man, then the scene moves down to his mouth, and smile cross his face. The scene is almost completely blinded with brightness at the sparkle of this man's perfect white teeth. His lips begin to move to spurt out words] Man: Tubular. [The scene then goes over to the woman, showing her eyes again. The scene then moves down and she has a smile on her face, the sorrow has been lifted from her shoulders. The scene now shows the two beginning to walk toward each other, slowly at first. The wind begins to pick up, making the woman's sundress blow around, the man's hair whips all around and his shirt follows the same rhythm his hair does. The two become closer than ever now, pressing their bodies against each other, they then embrace in a passionate kiss. Both of them look toward the camera now, smiling] Woman: Tubular. Man: Totally Tubular. [The scene begins to zoom out, showing just an upclose shot of a 56' television set. The two people disappear from the screen and a shot of a bottle of cologne pops up on the screen. On the bottle it reads 'Tubular for Men'. The scene then turns to a man on the couch, with long brown hair, a long goatee twisted in a braid, and wearing everything leather. This man is laughing like a madman as the commercial, featuring his best friend, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, fades out. The man named 'Heavy Metal' Harry rolls all over the black leather couch in bursts of laughter. The commercial wasn't that hilarious, there must be something else fueling the laughter, some kind of chemical] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Ha Ha! This is the best stuff I've ever had. Ha Ha! Tommy looked like such a weirdo, man. Ha Ha! [Suddenly a fly comes buzzing past and Harry starts throwing his arms around, in the process he wacks the fly and smashes it onto the large, handcrafted, coffee table in front of him. Harry stops laughing suddenly and looks at the palm of his hand. His eyes bug out at the sight of the smashed fly. Then the laughter continues even louder now] Harry: Ha Ha!! Stupid fly, man! I can't believe it flew at me, man. Ha Ha! I am the fly killing king, man. [In the background a door opens and the figure of a man comes stumbling into the large living room. Windows all over the place, especially the skylight above that gives the room its great brightness. This man wears boxers with little surfboards all over them. His tan skin glimmers in the morning sunlight, giving his toned body a much better look already. His bleach blonde hair is thrown all around, sticking up all over the place. The Tubular One, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, sits down on his leather couch near his best friend, Harry. Tommy, with sleep still in his eyes, stares over at his friend with a look of wonder and confusion] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Compadre, what's so funny? Harry: Ha! That cologne commercial you did was on, and I just can't stop laughing, man. Ha Ha! TTT: That was a most excellent work of art, dude. It wasn't funny. [Harry stops laughing and then shows Tommy his right palm. Tommy jumps back in fright, surely waking up now] TTT: Dude!! What bogus thing sat on your hand and died? [Harry starts laughing wildly again, burying his face into the couch, and then showing Tommy his hand again. Tommy turns his head in disgust, wondering if his friend is under the influence of drugs. That would be bogus if Harry is smoking weed again like he did when he was twelve, or so Tommy thinks] TTT: Come on, dude. That is most bogus to do that. Harry: Ha ha! I killed a fly just like the Karate Kid, man. It was rad, man. [Tommy grabs Harry by his head and looks deep into his friends eyes. Harry stops laughing and now looks a bit frightened by his friend's sudden motion] Harry: Come on, man. I know we are friends but I don't think I could handle a kiss on the lips, man. [Tommy then throws his friend down on the couch, seemingly not affected by the comment, but the fact that he didn't find bloodshot eyes. Harry sits up now, feeling a sense of victory, God Bless Visine!] TTT: I have to apologize, compadre. I thought that you were bogusly doing weed again, and just decided that I had to look. Its probably because I've been feeling completely bogus the past week. With those dreams that we've been having and the PCW tag titles on the line. There is a lot of heinous stress going around. Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry sighs and then moves closer to his friend. He puts his arm around Tommy. Tommy turns to look at his hand and sees the smashed fly. Tommy then tosses the arm off of him in disgust. Harry then wipes the dead bug on the coffee table, Tommy looks on in even more disgust but then quickly lets it go. Harry throws his arm around Tommy again] Harry: We're the Cowabunga Connection, man. Nothing can stop us. [Tommy looks at Harry, with very strong compassion. This is why Harry is his tag team partner, because he is there for him in his time of need. The same way that Harry's dad was there for Tommy's dad. He was there for Tommy's dad and helped him be the great wrestler that he was. Tommy is now slowly following in his fathers footsteps, gaining the acceptance of the crowd of PCW] TTT: Dude, your totally right! That match could have gone either way and I was just a very unlucky compadre. I fought hard, and thats all that matters! My dad would be most proud of me. Harry: Your damn right, man. Both our dads are up there hanging out thinking that we rule, man. Your dad is probably saying, 'Dude, my son is most tubular in the ring, his WipeOut is most innovative.' Then my dad would respond with, 'Far Out, man. I wonder where I can get more wee, er, where I can get more...food.' [Tommy smiles] Tommy: Your totally right, compadre. [Harry smiles and then pats Tommy on the back. Tommy gets up from the couch and begins walking toward the door that he came out from earlier. Harry looks slyly behind him seeing that Tommy is leaving. Harry goes into his pocket and pulls out a bag of brown stuff and then a small pipe looking contraption. Tommy then turns back around quickly and Harry quickly stuffs the stuff down the front of his leather pants, making a large bulge] Tommy: I'm totally stoked about today, dude. We've got a most bodacious workout session to get to... [Tommy stops in mid sentence at the sight of the bulge in Harry's pants. Harry just sits looking foward on the couch, acting like nothing is there] Tommy: Um, dude. We've been friends since we were little compadres, and, Harry, at least you could hide it. Harry: Uhh, I know, man. Um, your a guy too and you know how it can be, man. [Tommy smiles and giggles] Tommy: Totally! I'm sorry, just shocked me, dude. [Tommy then starts to walk away again going back toward that same door. Harry looks back and then takes the stuff out of his pants, feeling some relief. He smiles at his little toy, and his brown bag. Tommy then turns back around to say something again, Harry looks and quickly stuffs the stuff underneath his ass, his eyes bug out as the pipe seemingly goes some place that it shouldn't be] Tommy: If we win the tag titles then we don't have to face Misplaced on Sunday, but if we don't then we have to face them to see if we go to the most bodacious Spring Fling. Either way it's all going to be most... [Tommy stops in mid sentence again seeing the pain and humliation on his friends face. Tommy looks concerned, his friend is acting very strangely] Tommy: Are you all right, compadre? You look like you have a bug up your butt dude. [Harry looks over at Tommy with a very angered look. Tommy jumps back a little bit at the look and then begins to slowly back away] Tommy: Ok, dude, ok. [Tommy starts walking toward the door. Harry quickly pulls the things out from underneath him, his face shows of complete relief. He smiles and then gives the pipe a little sniff, and just shurgs his shoulders, thinking that its still good to hit from. Kind of Gross. He feels so happy now, he can enjoy his happiness. Tommy suddenly bursts back out of the door holding some clothes in his hands. Harry sees this and throws the pipe and bag across the room, breaking through one of the windows. Tommy stops and looks on in fright] Tommy: Ok, dude...nevermind. [Tommy then disappears back into the room. Harry sighs and buries his head into his hands] Harry: Not cool, man. That was my best stash. Once we win the tag titles I can get an even better stash, man. [Harry smiles brightly] Harry: Far out, man. [The scene then fades to black]
