Finding Reality

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th March I sit there depressed, my head tilted down towards the floor. The floor shines up with it having been freshly polished... at least that’s the signs of cultural improvement... a place that produces blood like a vineyard produces wine can have floors shinier than a bald man’s skull cap. My mind hasn’t adjusted to those thoughts around me, I just keep seeing flashbacks. A large blond man throwing her down at the floor and then laying into her with a steel chair in a completely brutal and unjustified manner; blood. Her blood is on my hands... I was... over confident. Protect your own. I should have known. All these thoughts drift into my mind and drift back out again and I can’t even begin to think about what to do with all of those thoughts mingling with each other, pressing against the interior of my skull and creating a dull ache that is going to last for the next five months. I tilt my head upwards and look across the corridor... the nurses seem oblivious to what I’m feeling, but then again I’m just another family member of another statistic in their ongoing search for the American truth... capitalism. If we were communist would this be any different? Sure we wouldn’t need health insurance, but would there be any difference? The nurses would be the same, the tax would be higher and therefore we would still be paying for healthcare, and my wife would still be in that room across from where I sit on this blue padded bench in a hospital corridor somewhere in America. I can’t even remember where I am... I just can’t... think. My head is numb with pain... with shock... with hatred. I’ve never had hatred for anyone before. I’m not sure who I even hate... Boda is a victim just like all others; he’s just a victim of his own pretty mindedness. Chris Chase however did it through choice, and that makes it far worse. To do it because your being tells you to do nothing else is not as much of a crime as to do it because you want to. Clyde is a burnt victim of an accident which changes him to do things to people which are at best described as “unpleasant”. Chris Chase is a pretty boy... he would probably be a centrefold in Playgirl if he really wanted to be, although I doubt that I would make the purchase because of it... I’m sure Boca and Peter would buy a copy each however. I’ve decided on one thing. I am going completely crazy sitting here in silence... how long have I even been here... I don’t even know. I left the Arena by cab and got here in a few minutes and then was taken up here and told to sit here and wait... but how long ago was that... ten minutes... an hour... a year? I don’t know... my mind is a whiz with complete trite, memories being dug up and resurfaced that I never expected to see again. Natalia’s smile for one... her want for a baby and my final act of giving in and trying for the new born life... but at what cost? What if Boda only attacked Natalia because she was pregnant... did he think that made it worse? Because I’m damned sure that it did. Would he have attacked her at all if she wasn’t pregnant? Is the child to blame for Natalia’s condition? I must not think that. It’s a new life, already five months old... I’m thinking like its still alive, I guess that is a good sign. It might already be dead, I just don’t know. Maybe I should ask someone... then again; maybe I should just sit here and wait for something to happen. That’s the problem... I always wait for something to happen, and then when it does I act all shocked. I should do *something* but I just don’t know what... I have a match on Fusion but I’m not even sure if I should go to Fusion at all... I should stay here with Natalia. For all I know she could be up and about by Friday... that’s right Miya, just keep looking on the positive side... don’t think about complications and her death. Her death... I never thought about that... Boda might have killed her... how long would they have me waiting out here before they bothered to come and tell me? No wait, there’s been no alarm sounded, no one has suddenly run into the room with a crash cart... as far as I know everything is ok. Nothing to worry about. Nothing to fear. She’s fine, she’ll be dancing around me before I can even grab both hands around Boda’s neck and strangle the life out of him for this. I should have done more... I stood inches away from him and I just threatened him... I should have done more... I should have acted on my thoughts rather than just say words. Sticks and stones may break my bones but words will never harm me. That was what my mother used to say to me... of course whenever I said it, people tended to throw sticks and stones at me and then promptly thank me for the idea. I believe in fate. Everything has a path and cannot move from the path. It was fate that led me to PCW... it was PCW that led me to Natalia... but what part does fate play in what Boda did to her? What’s more, if I want to try and get my hands on him in the ring again I’m going to have to win on Sunday night. Will fate allow me to defeat Alan Steele and the Chef... oh and Chase for the second time in three days? I respect Chef, I always looked up to him when I first came to wrestle in this company, and Big Alan Steele is pretty damned good himself... but Chase... what right does he have in all of this? He’s like a Hyena hanging around a diseased carcass taking any scraps that he can before the big cats arrive. I just can’t be bothered with him... Fusion is going to be over just as quickly as I decide it should be over... just so I can get back here to be with the only thing that truly matters in my life. Titles... victories... none are anything of consequence compared to this. I stand for a second and walk towards the door, my hand outstretched to turn the door handle to look in. Instead I look through the glass window at Natalia lying on the bed in a hospital smock with more wires and plugs in her than I thought I could possibly fit into something so small... so small... so weak... a tear rolls down my cheek at the sight of that, my mind drifting off once again... back to happier times... when we first moved out of that little flat and into the Gym... she was so happy... and so was I until I started getting decorators bills in. But she was happy and that is all that matters in life. To make someone else happy. I’m sure that Boda is probably very happy with his little back handed dealings at my expense, but I’m pretty sure that it has nothing of any consequence to what happens here... although it probably does help him in the matter that he has cut me deeper than anyone has ever cut me before. My hand clasps the door handle tightly but I do not turn it, the fear of seeing Natalia up close and personal could make me simply break down in floods of tears and pure unrivalled rage at myself. This is all my fault. I should have been there. I should have known Boda would have done something like this... something this depraved, something this callus. I have no allies to call upon... Asylum is gone... my mentor, the Trashcan Man is in OSW... Steve Jabowski has his own life, his own job... heck, his own contract over in NTICW now. Syko and Phoenix are wherever they want to be, although I doubt that I really want to see Phoenix now no matter what the grief is... he’s more likely just to try and twist the knife that is already impaled in my chest than to actually help. My Mother... my Sister... I hardly know them... I barely remember half the details of my childhood, so what would I say to them... I am truly alone and trapped in a corner. They say that a cornered animal fights ten times as hard, but I am not in the mood for a fight... I just want to lie down somewhere and let the pain in my heart engulf me until I no longer exist. A white coated doctor approaches me carefully from down the corridor. I don’t notice him until he gets right up next to me... instead I stare at my hand gripping the door handle... the pressure I’m exerting on the handle is turning my entire hand red. He looks me up and down and only then do I too, realising that I’m wearing my ring gear with that long black woollen jacket over the top; Natalia bought me this jacket... sure I paid for it, but she choose it because she liked me in it. She’s got such good taste... much better than mine... it should be me on that bed with all those leads attached to her. Doctor: Mr. Miyagawa? I turn to him and nod, not saying a word... not wishing to damage anything more with meaningless words that don’t tell the entire story. Doctor: I’m afraid it’s bad news. I expected nothing else. Bad news was my life. Fate just pissed on me. Doctor: She’s suffered major trauma to the liver. We have no shortage of donors, but that is not the problem. She’s got an extremely rare blood type... there’s no way we can operate until we have at least three pints of the correct type. I finally choose to speak after the long silence of a length I can’t even remember. Miyagawa: How long should the rare type take to arrive? Doctor: We haven’t had a donor of this blood type in over a year. We’re looking interstate for three pints now, but we’re not optimistic. Fate just crapped on me. Miyagawa: And the baby? Doctor: Your wife is an amazing woman... she took the fall across her neck and didn’t suffer any spinal damage from it which is amazing, and then she took all of the blows to the head. In other words, not a single ounce of damage was made to the baby. But, without the operation there is no way that she is going to be able to bring the baby to term. However, in six weeks time we can operate and take the baby out to place into a surrogate chamber that we have available. Miyagawa: And what about the blood loss to the mother? Doctor: There’s no way that without the blood, that the mother could survive such an operation. If we received the blood anyway, there would be no need to do this, as it would be all done in one single operation. The baby itself has a different blood type from what we can tell, one which is extremely common. And now fate is laughing at me. Unless three pints of Natalia’s rare blood type is found within the next six weeks, Natalia is going to die for the sake of the baby. Because I know her and if she wakes she is going to insist that they operate as soon as they can. I’ve been stabbed in the heart by Boda, and Fate just stabbed me in the back.
