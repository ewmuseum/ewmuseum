A Bad Night In The Park

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/01/02 The park is almost totally dark. The only light being given off by the few and far between light poles and the light of the moon. The paths leading through the park are very dim and the trees and bushes give off eerie shadows all along the path. Walking along in the darkness is Steve Johnson and Dawn Wilson dressed all in black. They seem to be talking and having a great time as they laugh and walk. They continue to walk until they reach a bridge. They begin to cross but stop and stand at the middle of the bridge and look out over the creek running through the park. The wind begins to blow a little heavier and louder now. Steve: It is a wonderful night out here tonight. Hard to imagine that this has been such a terrible week. But all the crap from this week will wash away this Sunday night. I can feel that Rising Star title in my grasp already. See the gold shinning in the bask of the arena lights and the crowds cheers and boos. So, how do you see this all playing out? Who is out of the match first? Dawn: Oh I really do not think it will mater who is eliminated first. The outcome will remain the same no mater who you face at the end. But if I were going to bet on any body being the first one out of the match-up I would have to say it would be... Lance Bell. Steve: The current champion huh. That is a fairly safe bet, since he is no more than a damn paper champion. He is only a transitional champion. Lucky for him that when Red Rock and him faced, Red Rock already had bigger and better things on his mind. If not, then Bell would be sitting at home watching this match on TV like the rest of the world’s biggest jobbers. Yes I believe he will be very easily disposed of. The second man eliminated would have to be... ah yes! Jay Sylo. Dawn: That is highly possible, but only if you keep him or yourself from eliminating Ripper second. You realize that Ripper is clearly the second least talented in the match after Bell. Hell he is only getting this shot because of his fluke of a win on Tuesday. To bad for him that ref will no longer be around to aid him in his match. Still he will need a lot of help to be the last man in there with you. Steve: It is true that Ripper has stumbled into this opportunity by pure luck. However on Sunday his luck will have completely run its course. But he will make it to the last with me. He is just talented and dumb enough to prolong the inevitable fact that he is not in the league with a true champion such as my self. His whole shitty life will take a turn for the worse on Sunday. The kind of turn that not even talking to his little head doctor will help. Sunday Ripper will wake up from his hypnotized state of believing he is talented and a threat to any one in PCW. The only person he is truly a threat to is the popcorn man. Dawn: At any rate Ripper will still need plenty of help. Besides as far as any one with a brain can see, the only thing he is any good at doing is beating up women with bottles. The two stand there and just stare down at the water. The creek is fairly dry for this time of year. There is only about a half foot of water in the bottom of the creek. A fall from the bridge would nearly kill a person how ever. They creek just seems to roll along real slow and easy. A few moments later a couple of people jogging along stop in the middle of the bridge where Steve and Dawn are standing. They have looks of great excitement on their faces. One is a male and the other a female. They both have short dark hair and are wearing bright white jogging suits. They have on Nike shoes. Steve and Dawn stand there looking at the two like they are nuts. Man: Look honey it is one of those Primetime Championship wrestlers! We have just started getting into the whole wrestling thing, and so we don’t know to many guys by name. What is your wrestling name? I do not go by a wrestling name. You have mistaken me for one of those men with no personality. My name is Steve Johnson. Woman: Oh yes I do remember you. You were the one that got DQ’ in his debut match. Yeah that was a terrible call by the ref and the PCW officials should reverse the decision. Any way I heard you have a match at this Sunday’s Pay-Per-View; March Madness. Who is the little woman? Dawn (Her face turning bright red): Little woman! I will show you who is a little woman. Why don’t I just take your little skinny butt and throw you off this bridge? Man (Stepping in front of his woman): Wait! Wait! There is no need for anyone to get mad here tonight. I am sorry for my wife. Sometimes she doesn’t think before she speaks. The woman smacks the man across the face. He quickly grabs his face and looks at her in shock. Steve and Dawn just watch with smirks on their face. Man: What did you do that for? Woman: You need to watch what you say about me. I am your wife. Not some dumb little bi##h that you just talk about like she isn’t there! You get what I am telling you? Steve just stands there and laughs at the man getting dogged by his wife. He has his hands on his knee’s holding himself up from falling because he is laughing so hard. The woman whips around and slaps Steve in the face. His head turns to the right, and then he slowly turns it back toward her with an evil grin on his face. Dawn starts toward her but Steve sticks his arm out. Steve: No wait just a moment Dawn. That was quite a slap you have there. It reminds me a little of the way Ripper punches. Now why did you have to go and do that? I was being a gentleman and talking to you two worthless no one’s. But no you had to go and slap me in the face. Dawn teach this woman a little respect. Dawn reaches over and grabs the woman by the hair and yanks her over toward her. The woman begins to yell and scream. The man begins to take steps like he intends to do something about what Dawn is doing, but is cut off by Steve who steps in front of him and just shakes his finger in the mans face. The man just stops dead in his tracks and has no choice but to watch and wait. Dawn slings the woman around by the hair and throws here to the concrete ground of the bridge. Then in one quick motion she leaps up on to the side of the bridge and jumps off with an elbow drop onto the woman. The woman screams in great pain as Dawn just simply stands up and dusts her self off as she looks at the woman and laughs. Next time bit##h make sure you know who you are messing with when you slap some one else’s man. Let’s get out of here Steve. These people are boring me a great deal. That is fine with me. I have been waiting very patiently to get those closes off of you any way. The man walks over and helps his wife up and they go off in the opposite direction that Steve and Dawn head in. Steve and Dawn begin to walk along back toward their vehicle. They walk along in silence and just seem to be enjoying the evening. Suddenly out of the shadows a man jumps out in front of them and pulls a knife. He is wearing a black stocking mask and black clothing. He holds the knife out and begins to yell orders at them. Masked Man: Don’t make a single fu##ing move and maybe I will let you and your little sl#t live. Now give me all your valuables, money, keys. Give them all to me right now and don’t make me ask twice. Isn’t this about par for the course. This has been a shit#y night. First I get attacked on Conan, and then two losers in jogging suits make stupid comments and slap me. Now some member from Lance Bell’s inbred family has decided to try and steal my money and stuff. I tell you this night can’t get much worse. Steve takes his wallet out of his pocket and hands it toward the man. As the man reaches out and grabs for the wallet, Steve brings his other hand around and hits the man hard in his temple. The man falls right to the ground and is out cold. Steve gives him a few kicks to the ribs before laughing at the man. Did you actually think I would give you my wallet? You represent the hopes of my three opponents on Sunday. Right now they hope, dream, and maybe even actually believe that they will obtain the Rising Star gold. But after the match is all over on Sunday they are going to end up just like you. Lying on their backs, out cold and not any closer to the desired goal then when they started. Steve kicks the guy a couple more times, then him and Dawn begin to walk toward the car again. As they walk along the park path they reach out and grab each other’s hands and the scene fades out to black.
