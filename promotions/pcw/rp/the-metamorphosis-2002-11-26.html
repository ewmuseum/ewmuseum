The Metamorphosis

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.26.02 Welcome to Dallas, Texas - the home of aftermath, just about as in inward reflection of how Dante Jackson carried himself, because Sunday night proved to be a lot for testament. For starters, he and Statuz Quo did not reclaim the Tag Team titles they had so wanted to gain back from Pranks With Attitude. It didn't stop from there. The Alamo Match, just about as ridiculous as David Arquette actually gaining a pinfall over somebody. The next thing you know, the very next thing - Gatorade, not even a soft drink wins the contract! Gatoade has nothing on Powerade I might add. But really not many smart people are wanted to win those type of matches anyway. Then, Alliances. Yes the sixty man, three hour clusterfuck put on annually for a November Pay Per View. Many are skeptical about who'll win, but when you get on the television a mere twenty six times and then screw RUSH into your pocket, then you know you're bound to win the match. Although, when you finish 13th, you instantly think that you're pretty good around the campfire, somebody who is on the rise. But when Rush Mason - an alter black man finishes ahead of you, those original thoughts go right down the shitter. 'DAMMIT!' The loud, blood curdling scream echoes throughout the IHOP as Dante sits inside of a booth, enjoying a pancake breakfast including hash browns and eggs. Accompanying him on the opposite side of the booth seat happens to be Dawn Browne, the traveling ace of Dante as he rolls on through another month in PCW. He snarls a bit as he stuffs down another stack of flapjacks, conscious of nothing but his own pleasure as Dawn stares at him with her mouth gapping, wanting some of the food Dante happens to be eating. She blinks, before sighing to herself, knowing she won't be able to taste a single morsel of the food he happems to be consuming at the moment. He chomps down on more, just not carring whether or not his friend or companion on the other side happens to care one bit. He stops, looking down at the pancakes with an odd glaze in his eyes. He drops the fork next to the place, before cracking his neck back. 'Well, what happened?' the female asks him as he stares at her without much comfort being in the situation. He dryly responds to her, 'No syrup.' She grumbles as she throws her head into her arms resting on the table as Dante grabs the syrup pitcher by his hand and grips it down easy. Pouring it all over the pancakes, he smiles before eating more and more. Looking up, Dawn spots the waitress who had taken their order originally, a short and stocky brunette woman with a few unpleasant moles and warts on her face. Holding a cigarette between her lips, she grunts towards Dawn, raising her awareness of the woman. Raising her eyes, Dawn wonders about the woman in front of her. 'Yes?' she asks in her most pleasant voice. The smaller woman happens to grumble as she turns to Dante, chomping down on his food ever more unconscious by everybody else. She raises a finger, trying to get his attention but it only makes the former Tag Team Champion eat more and more. 'This here boy,' her Southern accent running throughout her veins as her attention returns back to the patron in the booth who doesn't appear to be eating. 'he's been eating us outta house and home. Next thing ya know the boy's gotta eat you along wit it.' Dante batted an eye towards the woman, 'Yeah and maybe your warts will eat up the rest of your face.' The woman obviously heard that as Dante comes to terms with the words as her eyes cut towards the seated black man enjoying his pancakes with syrup. 'Now listen here,' she starts up, her voice getting louder by every single word or shout. 'nobody disrespects me like that!' 'Woman you're accent created ebonics so I suggest you just shut the hell up and let me eat.' Dante quipped. The woman stands back, slightly abused verbally from the former champion as he returns back to the plate of flapjacks in front of him. The woman turns back to her job, nearly close to sobbing her head off as Dawn raises her eyebrows at the woman herself. She returns back to Dante. 'That's an odd thing.' 'Shut up.' he responded, as he slammed his elbow upon the table, obviously still fuming after the loss at Alliances. 'I should be Cruiserweight champion right now, but the damn Front Office wanted to stick their noses in everything, including Timmy frickin' Walden.' 'Don't let that sweat you.' she replied, growing a sense of hope inside of him. 'Yeah,' he lied to her despondently. 'Yeah...' He was nothing near fine, all of those losses from the past weeks and then the thoughts of the past it began to eat up at him, slowly like a disease through a terminally ill cancer paitent, this pain wasn't going to go away quickly, it'd take a lot of soul searching. He picked himself up from the booth, the plate nearly eaten down from every single morsel, not a drop left for Dawn to even try and take a bite herself as Dante begins to trot out of the diner, before stopping at the door. 'Maybe I should just start off ranting right now. Because of this TYPICAL WHITE SERVICE, I've been cheated out of a quiet meal, and what do you inbreed low IQ having, no SAT taking morons have to do for yourselves? Hmm? Do you actually have jobs or do you sit here like the retarded baboons that you are and hoot and holler at the next piece of ass that you see?' Dante begins, his voice just unparalled by any other person trying to outdo him in speaking. A Plato quote pops into the mind, 'One must be heard over the others.' He clears his throat, 'Just because I'm black, don't call the police and say that a black man is causing ruckus in your diner because he so happens to want and speak his mind about things. For you should you, you've been visited by God ladies and gentlemen. You've been visited by the Black God himself, and none of you fucks even deserve it.' Many of the patrons don't even look at Dante, more of a sneer in a way, as Dawn looks on, a bit scared for a reason that a near riot may begin as soon as Dante utters anoter word. She looks concerned at it, but Dante doesn't he holds his position, strong and unclear whether or not he wants to stop. 'I see,' he looks over at the patrons sitting at the bar and the slave workers around the back area, near the cooks and janitors. 'all you know for yourselves that you can't do shit about me, because you're scared. You're scared of a minority holding position. So you know what? I'm the best, the fucking greatest black specimen in the history of professional wrestling. Better than your jobs, more wealthy than you harsh ideals and specters.' 'Will you please get the fuck out of here?' one asks under his breath and brown beard. 'For the love of God can he leave?' another chips in. Dawn taps Dante on the shoulder, grabbing his attention like a newborn with a rattle. 'Maybe we should -' 'Oh, you want to think like them now?' he strikes her oddly as he interrupts her mid sentence. When Dante's on a roll, you usual don't stop him when he's going. 'Oh, should I be like Nerezza and be happy with every little quote read out by every star you happen to come across? Or maybe its just him - dying to be human again. I'm quite sure the power of the human race out numbers them one million to one so its no problem. He wants to be special, fine he'll get that.' 'Dante,' Dawn starts up as she begins to heard towards the door. 'you're scaring me.' Dante smiles at the comment, before pressing the door against his thump, not a single person is going to stop him while he's in a zone. Not one person could do that ever, not even a re-awakened Vampire who happens to hold victory over Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo in a tag team match. Its just not happening. If you thought everything was fine with Dante before Alliances, this is the visual of AAD, after Alliances Dante. You don't lose three straight matches in one night without some self realization in the mirror. Its been in the blood of him, his head just boiling inward and outward of thought. Could he do this, could he do that? It never bothered him ever, not until now. 'Fine, be with them.' he utters. 'But you know what ladies and gentlemen? Let me tell you one last thing before I leave you. Don't come to the lower class people when you're lower class yourself, go find those who are higher than you. Go find those fucking top hats in the Wall Street Journal, hell find yourselves.' He turns out of the diner, dragging Dawn along with him as the two of them look inward at the prized car posession of Dante Jackson - the 2003 Nissan 350Z, decked out in silver highlights with Red base. He smiles at himself in the front side mirror, before pulling open the car door. Dawn stands there, obviously odd that Dante's previous actions earlier. Dante looks up at her through the tinted car glass window, 'What's wrong?' She tapped on the window, wondering what should she say now if there was anything to say. 'Nothing,' she whispered to herself. 'nothing.' Dante nods his head, before quipping. 'Well, atleast you're not going to be all bitchy about things like Nerezza.' Dawn lightly smirks before hopping in the car herself, shutting the passenger side door as Dante drives out of the diner's view. Inside, the men and women sit, either enjoying their food or stuck back on what Dante happened to say towards them earlier - an enlightening process in his eyes. Some of them sigh, while others make despondent remarks. They can't shake the simple fact that somebody like Jackson would take them on a more physiological matter. 'Where to now?' Dawn asks as they sit inside of the car her pen tapping deeply onto a notepad, before writing down more for her Source article to be published at the beginning of the year. 'Turmoil,' Dante responds. 'Gotta pay a certain Goth a visit.'
