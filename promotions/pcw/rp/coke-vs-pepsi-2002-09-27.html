Coke vs Pepsi

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Coke vs Pepsi Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 9/27/02 -The Malice Man sits in his office in the PCW headquarters where PCW referee sits in the chain in front of his desk.- Bob: Did you see Miya and JJ working out? Williams: Was it a work out of a Pepsi cola commercial. Bob: They plugged Pepsi like the US plugged Japan in WW2. Williams: I think he’s taking checks on the side shilling these products. Bob: Can you tax that? Williams: I can pull it off of the TV. We don’t shill products on our time. -Duke pours Bob a tall glass of Coke as he drinks some down himself.- Williams: ummm nothing beats an ice cold coke. Bob: Gods gift to soft drink, the original and still the best. -They toast glasses and drink down the cold fizz pop.- Williams: JJ was one of the worst wrestlers ever!! This match will be a walk in the park. Bob: Nothing like a Rosa Parks fight. Williams: I hope Miyagawa doesn’t mind losing cus he better get used to it. First I’m going to kick his ass on Fusion then less then a week from now I’ll beat his ass down at the PPV. Poor bastard. It must be hard being a consistent loser. Bob: I tell you what Buzz hates JJ. All the refs hate JJ he’s not one of us. Williams: Miygawa is a peasant, trying to create a cabal. That isn’t happening. The Front Office is stronger now then ever. When I look at him and his merry band of boners I just laugh. I laugh at how simple a man can truly be. Unimpressive pretty much wraps up his career when you get right down to it hoss. Bob: Amen and don’t even get me started on JJ’s career. This guys a bigger loser then a guy who gets tuned down by a whore. -The two men laugh.- Williams: I have fought must of my career as a singles wrestler. I didn’t do stables till I hit thirty-eight. Stables are for the weak who need back up. Miya’s like a street hood punk who needs somebody watching his back. Bob: JJ enjoys men watching his back. -Both men laugh.- Williams: This Fusion the team of Asylum will take a nice long trip. Bob: Where’s that Duke? Williams: Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound! =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
