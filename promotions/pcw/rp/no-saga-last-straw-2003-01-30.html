No Saga: Last Straw

30 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-30-03 The final straw has been broken by the camel on the television. I'm sickened. This is the end. I can't take this anymore... just watching him... just watching him stumble over these words, just watching him try and railroad the stupid american nation into believing in this little thing over seas. No. This isn't American. This isn't the country i'm a part of. The entire place is obese, minorities are taking over the majority, corruption and crime rise as the economy falls... and three-quarters of the population is too under educated to care. Theres noone for the people to blame it on this time, its not the 50's, they can't blame all the social ills on the blacks, although alot of the crime problems can be... its not the Great Depression, you can't blame things on Hoover. No. This is solely the uneducated masses thought. What a leader they have selected, one nearly as stupid as they are. I can't take this anymore. I... I can sit here in my house and not do anything, I can continue to use my powers just to keep the balance in the minds of humanity... Or... Or I can do something. Fusion, thats when it will begin.... Friday Fusion. Yes. It will all change then. Goodbye.
