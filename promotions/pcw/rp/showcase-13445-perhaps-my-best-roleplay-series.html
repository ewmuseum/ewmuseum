From Virtue to Vice: Element 2 – Courage/Valor (Part 1) March 4, 2003 Nestled in darkness amongst the hills of Vermont, a singular light illuminated the interior of accursed Wyatt Manor through the western window of the uppermost story. Hideous chants rose and fell with the howling wind from the recesses of that monstrous edifice, their blasphemous syllables drifting through the dreaded hills and raising the maddening song of the whippoorwills in the pines. The deplorable hymn swept through the shunned northern wood as consort to the winds, stirring reports from the near south village of Brayston of grotesque nightmares haunting its residents en masse and formless shadows lurking the avenues of moonless night. The night of March 4th brought unrest to the small village, one of the final outposts of civilization before giving way to the wilderness long feared by locals. One man, local grocer Jude Lambert, went mad that evening as he lay slumbering beside his wife on Wicker Street. Upon awakening he exploded into fitful bursts of dire terror as he recounted visions of Cyclopean cities erected of black metal and amorphous masses gliding in the skies overhead. The most notable of his recollections was the final revelation hissed by the quivering merchant before his ultimate lapse into insanity, his account of a vast flailing demon stretching the range of the heavens and so far beyond that his eyes couldn’t discern where its length terminated. Lambert was transported to Rutland for medical treatment when dawn arrived, and with him went three more of the village’s unfortunate denizens as they rambled of equally wicked images. The Brayston community was wrought with horror that day, as the identity of their notorious ‘neighbors’ hadn’t escaped their scrutiny. The Sect of Black Wisdom’s rituals weren’t unknown, but never before had their evil deeds and worship infiltrated the isolated hamlet from amidst the hills. It was an unprecedented breach by the foul theists into the society that ostracized their ways and gods, and the event cast the town of Brayston into alarm from daybreak until nightfall; none doubted the source of their neighbors’ collective madness after hearing the chants and mantras float from the woods that night. And certainly none doubted the madness of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan, the wicked high priest and instigator of the unspeakable faith. March 5, 2003 A moonlight vigil, organized by the First Church of Christ the King, formed a spiritual blockade against the evil that swept from the mountains the next evening. Again the blasphemous chants and hymns wafted down through the skeletal trees and forgotten trails into Brayston in the small hours, their blackest of intent countered by a congregation of men and women poised outside the sanctuary with candles and hymnbooks in hand to ward off the demons the song carried. From Wyatt Manor that cold night came hideous anthems praising various infernal deities, and the lot surrounding the church exuded repentant tones revering Jehovah God and his son the Messiah. Morning arrived and the nearly two-dozen exhausted worshippers finally relinquished their watch. A pious young woman among them, Janet Clement, insisted that she saw Bruce Shanahan, known leader of the black cult, lurking at the fringe of the woods while carefully surveying their vigilance from a trail just beyond the brush. Others in turn scanned the perimeter of the gnarled undergrowth, but her report went unconfirmed and deemed as the delusions of a fatigued mind. Contradictory reports had stated that Shanahan had departed the manor days ago to pursue his professional wrestling endeavors, but that again was an unofficial statement. Moments after the gathering broke reports flooded in of more nightmares and accounts of lingering madness amongst the residents. Lunacy pervaded the elderly amongst all else, and old Gladys Sutter had died of an abrupt heart attack moments after waking with screams of “Daoloth, the Render of the Veils!” and “Shub-Niggurath, that Black Goat and her spawn of the wood!” After hissing a series of hideous unintelligible syllables, Mrs. Sutter shuddered and her heart (mercifully, some would later say) gave out. Mrs. Sutter was never known for her knowledge of such dark things, and how one of her recognized ignorance and lack of education could spout such phrases perplexed those who knew her and especially those who witnessed the happening. Gossip spread that her face took on a joyous and even reverent expression as those final syllables were verbalized before her death. That account was shrugged off as a hybrid of hearsay and exaggeration. March 5th brought panic and outcry to the community, most of who were convinced Shanahan intended on expanding his spiritual territory through his unholy rites and that his efforts were directed at driving away the village of Brayston, if not worse. The First Church and town officials quelled the anxiety by reassuring the residents that Shanahan would be dealt with and told to restrict his activities in proximity to the village. A group of ‘delegates’ were appointed and sent to negotiate and speak with the high priest of the Sect of Black Wisdom, Bruce Shanahan, to suppress fear in the community and hopefully vanquish local sentiment of his diabolic intentions. The quartet of officials and businessmen, accepting their designated duty, ascended into the hills to meet with the wide-feared guru of the dark faith. Afternoon dwindled into twilight, and twilight became nightfall. Darkness encompassed the isolated village of Brayston, and the council hadn’t yet returned. Meeting again in the dimly-lit lot of the community’s lone house of worship, outcry arose for state police to be alerted and patrols to be sent into the gloom-ridden wood to track down the party. Their pleas were again thwarted by optimistic town officials, and plans were made to wait in hope until morning before taking further, and more drastic, measures; Chet Cole, a war veteran counted among the initial ‘delegates’, was schooled in the arts of survival and thus afforded the missing group a glimmer of hope. The assembly abruptly broke with growled curses from the majority and embittered shouts of “Their blood is on your hands!” arose among the more pessimistic of the crowd. The council, divided by dissension in opinion, opted to meet alone in the town hall. A faint trail of pitch-black smoke ascended into the sky from the blackened mansion, silhouetted against a waning blood-red moon. The townsfolk, caught up in the unmitigated fear spawned among their ranks, failed to notice the omen. Their conversation, tainted with panic and the presage of impending catastrophe, drowned out the inhuman howls that echoed from the summit of the mountain, the personal symphony of Bruce Shanahan; a symphony forged in a fathomless void beyond the range of conventional knowledge and mortal nightmare…… ---- 8 April 2005 From Virtue to Vice: Element 2 – Courage/Valor (Part 2) The door to the rickety old hall creaked shut on rusted hinges, the mark of the structure’s two hundred year lifespan as the community convention center. Typically it’s easy to say start at the beginning, but in this case I don’t think that rule factors in. Besides, beginning is relative. Who’s to say what beginning is? Certainly not me, and especially not you. There actually is no distinction, contrary to what ‘reality’ may tell you. Yes, I’m sure that confuses your feeble and unenlightened little mind. But it won’t in a bit. Stay with me here, child, for I intend on going rather quickly. Skylar Green’s past had been well documented; from his tenures in various lesser wrestling promotions, to his most heated rivalries, very little of The Artist Formerly Known As ‘The End’ is still an enigma. But nonetheless, I find it troubling that so many points have been neglected and dismissed about the past of the blind man. Whether this is out of memory blackout, ignorance or the sake of stifling outrageous truth from public eye is a topic that could be endlessly scrutinized. But for sake of time, let’s not get into that one, eh? This whole philosophy has incited me into thinking and I have other things to address here. So, as another offbeat theory, could it all be in the endless search for ‘realism’? Now that one requires some reflection. What does humanity know about ‘realism’? It was once said that we exist on a placid island of ignorance and were never meant to journey far, lest we drive ourselves mad with the bleak realities existing outside the comfort-glossed Utopia that is our own society. Some things are simply better left unsolved and shouldn’t be delved by human speculation or research. It’s as prevalent a concept in my profession as it is else where in civilization. And, unfortunately, it’s also all the truth. Yep, remember all those times you terrified your children with tales of the boogeyman and monsters in the woods, then hurriedly reassured them that there was no such thing as monsters? How about those tales of evil things composed by such disturbed minds as Edgar Allan Poe, Stephen King, or H.P. Lovecraft that we eventually laugh off knowing of their absurdity? Or better yet, how about that night you spent walking home in the dark along that remote road, all the while feeling that sense of dread inherent in the notion you were being watched by unknown eyes in the trees? You knew there was something there prowling in the shadows, but of course you shrugged it off when you reached the solace of the porch-lights and just attributed the whole scenario to too much of that Nightmare on Elm Street marathon. I could tell you to walk in there right now and kindly inform little Mindy and Tommy that monsters do in fact exist and that their favorite snack is human flesh. I could acquaint you with facts about many of the more notorious masters of the macabre and the mystical visions reported as plaguing their sleeping hours, the very dreams and nightmares that formed the imagery scrawled in their manuscripts. Through this means I could relate to you the nightmares inflicted upon one Howard Phillips Lovecraft, pulp writer turned both vessel and reporter of clandestine forces of universal evil. Better yet, I could burst your comfortable little bubble by saying that that feeling that chilled your spine in the woods that summer evening was no more your imagination than it was something infinitely worse than Robert Englund in a felt hat, striped shirt and razor-fingered glove, and that everything you pictured in your imagination wasn’t far from the truth (if not worse). But actually, I won’t tell you any of these things for sure. It’s better this way. It’s more to your advantage to believe you linger near the top of the cosmic food-chain, behind only God, his angels and the (imprisoned, of course!) demons and minions of Hell. It’s human nature to flee your fears and seek sanctuary for protection (hence the impact of religion on all culture, eh?). Fight or flight, the intuitive alarm system in us all; it’s nothing to be ashamed of. Unfortunately when Azathoth sired us from raw chaos he never believed we would go running to some second-rate divinity and a make-shift savior for refuge. A simple flaw in our creation by no fault of his own. Wait, wait……you still believe Jehovah God created us? Pfft, have you not been listening? Well…..I can overlook it on this one occasion. The next time though, you may end up taking a forced glimpse into the true nature of the universe. Daoloth himself will revel in your ensuing madness, trust my words. Anyhow, some people are simply content with their own evaluation of reality. They hear tale spun of Celestials, Great Old Ones, prophecy of Earth’s annihilation buried in folklore, and tomes of horrid knowledge penned by madmen in the distant corners of the Earth. They dismiss it all as insanity, the simple ranting of those poor demented misfits of the social collective. These so-called ‘lunatics’ are locked away in padded rooms as they gibber and speak in obscene tongues originated of dead races, and it’s all just “delusions manufactured in their twisted mind”. Forget warnings of the theological institution and let forces of the unknown be damned. Blame it on psychological regression, shoot serums into his arm, drown him in sedatives, wrap him in a straitjacket. I speak from experience, the system is a farce. Judgment contrived of bitterness you say? Alright, have it your way. How about some history, eh? Friedrich Von Junzt composed Unaussprechlichen Kulten and was deemed mad for its revelations, furthermore cast out as a pariah. For his desire to study those mysteries and blackest of cosmic perceptions which man could not solve through practical means, and his lack of faith in conventional science and logic, one of the world’s greatest theosophists was regarded as an outsider. Alas the infallible disciplines of science and logic failed to determine probable cause for his death following a return from Mongolia when Von Junzt, barred and confined in his lodgings in Dusseldorf, was found dead with the mark of talons around his throat. Alexis Ladeau, after piecing together the mutilated pages of his colleague’s final manuscript and reading their contents, burnt the documents into ash and cut his own throat with a razor. Never read that parable in your history books, huh? Applaud science. Another victory struck for the upkeep of ‘our ‘reality and the suppression of the ‘obscene’ reality. ‘Realism’, my friend, is just a red herring. What you know is a diversion from truth. The Matrix, that little Keanu Reeves diddy made a few years back? That is your life. This is all an illusion; your world is an elaborate hoax produced by proverbial directors and script-writers with identities and forms beyond our comprehension, all brought to life on a stage so immense we have yet to fathom its prodigious depths. Now, bearing all this in mind, what exactly separates the average Duke Williams or Chet King spiel from the documented exploits of this industry’s more ‘bizarre’ and outlandish denizens? Fear and denial don’t typically expel ideas from existence, but some are apparently near-sighted enough to believe so. They’re content in their reality, and who am I to shatter the little Zion where they ignorantly dwell? I sympathize with the common blue-collar workingman as much as the next guy, but hell….mongrels like Williams don’t even bow before the right altar. And you trust that yokel’s views? Sheesh. I should have put that boy out to pasture when I had the opportunity. Perhaps I could have redeemed you in the process. Those imbeciles can continue their verbal attacks from in front of a camera until they get some debilitating form of cancer as far as I’m concerned. I’ll be content with haunting your dreams with what I’ve come to know. The powers of technology mean little in the face of divine influence. Those cameras won’t be attached to their derrieres forever, god forbid. But I’ll be sure to keep you updated on my life as I see fit, and as long as you require sleep, you’re mine for life. How can I do this you may ask? Wipe the miserable tears from your eyes and I’ll tell you. Done? Good. Well, having access to the Library of Celaeno has its advantages, believe you me. Man, you wouldn’t believe some of the things Jehovah God himself made his vassals avoid discussing in the Bible. Trust me, no one would revere ‘His Holiness’ if they knew of his true ambitions. I just thank Azathoth I’m not among those irrational fools blindly having dedicated a life of service to Him. Sorta makes me feel sorry for both Duke and that degenerate offspring he sent off to defile ‘that other company’. Speaking of which…..ah yes, Skylar Green’s history. Well after seeing that he’s sufficiently bored you with enough trite stories to send Barbara Walters into a coma, I’ll digress and get to the meat of the matter here quickly. But first I thought I should note one detail of Green’s long-winded chronicle: wasn’t the Drake Maxwell he spoke so highly of the one and the same ex-PCW Continental champion caught up in a scandal involving deceit and fraud during one of his parents company’s more prestigious tournaments? There are smarks and fanboys out there, I’m sure they can back me up on this. The same Drake Maxwell hailed as a company saviour before being exposed as a sham and subsequently lynched and dismissed of his contract? Pfft. And people say I’ve never done anything profitable or productive for this company. Fuck the smarks and the horses they rode in on. If anyone knows the current whereabouts of Mr. Maxwell then please enlighten me. Last I heard he was headlining at a gymnasium somewhere in Peoria, Illinois with an asking rate of $75, a Latino whore with a foot fetish and a night’s supply of Jim Beam. But hell, I’m sure he’s well worth it and all. Just a shame he wasn’t blessed with the ‘assets’ of some of his peers to avoid such a fate. Speaking of which, caught any DWO programming lately? Anyhow, I’m getting off-track here…..Skylar Green. Well it appears Mr. Green has been labeled as the Sleeping Dragon, one of those fabled beings of ridiculous Celestial lore. According to the archives of the Library of Celaeno, a convention was held only months ago to discuss the crisis of “The End’s” existence and time-bomb capacity in this realm. It appears the freak has been imbued with monstrous power, and possesses the aptitude to render this universe back to its original state: a seething nuclear chaos where all matter is rearranged by the will of great daemon-sultan Azathoth alone, where all that we know has been extinguished from being. In essence, Skylar Green is the Celestials’ equivalent to a cosmic reset button. His existence is a hazard not only to all we commonly know, but to the sustentation of all the Old Ones across the vast nebulas. My purpose has become clear and the method is evident. Green must not be allowed to subsist on this world or any other. His wretched carcass will be sent screaming into the great beyond and deposited in the abyss where his own screams shall be all he hears for illimitable aeons. Once there the only thing that mongrel will capable of annihilating will be his own demolished husk of a body, as he will rot in solitude for eternity out of reach of this universe. A proper eulogy will be prepared in honor of his exile, rest assured. Ah, but I grow weary this night. You may rest now, dear child. Tomorrow is a new day, and with its dawn shall come new experiences and pleasures. They are yours in which to partake. But don’t forget your hours of slumber still belong to me. And bear in mind my words of earlier; know that few entities take pleasure in mockery of the human condition like the Render of the Veils. Pleasant dreams, heathen. Ia Shub-Niggurath! ---- 8 April 2005 The Dragon Stirs: Bruce’s Folly Revealed The bowels of illimitable chaos stretched eternally through three-dimensional space, black currents of matter shifting in perpetual disharmony. Between the rolling black tides rested a lone fog-ridden sphere, cold and barren of life, cast with a beacon of a faint, phantom radiance. Saturated with those elements both composing and sustaining life, this gloomy realm acted as a staging ground for the torture of enemies of the Old Ones’ more nettlesome foes where servitors and the gods themselves would render their enemies with agony and madness. It was also within this remote limbo that Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan regularly met through spiritual incarnation with his dark rulers, those deities of such vast power and dominion that they existed as the embodiment of cosmic principles and laws; omnipotent beings exhibiting the cunning and intelligence of the ordered universe. Where primordial chaos melded into the continuum of merging time and space, currents of tangible darkness drifted between the conscious dimensions and through unhallowed gulfs where light was taboo and sanity was an element verboten. It was here that Shanahan’s stooped outline wavered in incorporeal form before slowly materializing into flesh made physical, awaiting his conference with the soul and messenger of the Outer Gods, Nyarlathotep. He drew himself up, slowly gazing in a clockwise pivot at his surroundings. A wall of sound bore down on him from the ever-shifting darkness that encompassed the secluded rift in space, its rumbling tone like that of an approaching stampede upon the prairie and reflecting the incalculable stirring of matter infinite. His head bowed in sincerest of reverence, from his lips uttering forth those accursed syllables. “Ph-nglui mglw-nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn.” The tones echoed in that condensed void and beyond for minutes, reflecting back upon him as if answered by a droning choir of the dead and the damned. The Black Pharaoh had summoned him to this realm for his final instructions prior to the March Madness event. Exactly what the night held for the servant of the black gospel was yet unknown, but the one known as Boston’s Bastard Son hoped the god could shed light on his March Madness fate. Between Skylar Green’s impending annihilation and the World title battle royal itself, it would prove to be a busy evening for the head of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Shanahan became aware of a presence in the realm, foreign and unwelcome judging by its aura. Another abruptly popped up nearby the original, and he grunted. “I do not know who you are,” he snorted angrily without turning to view the culprits, “but you are not welcome in this sacred forum. I assume you’re merely passing through, or you warped into this region by error. I urge you to depart, lest face the consequences of your insolence.” One of the duo let out an audible scoff, the voice of a male. “No error I fear. We are here not of our own accord, but for reasons much like your own. However, our dealings aren’t with your gods. They are with you, High Priest Bruce Shanahan. And we won’t leave until our objective has been seen through.” Flabbergasted at the gall of the still-unseen intruders, Shanahan turned to gaze on the infiltrators. One was a young girl of Asian descent, resting cross-legged and barefoot on a cloud of mist, casually licking at an ice-cream cone. The other, a male of similar lineage and similar approximate age, stood alongside her. Bruce sneered, enraged at the boy’s comments, as the youth began to amble away from the girl to stand alone. “Boy….how dare you defy me and the Old Ones?! You are uninvited for a reason. That is because you are unwanted. Be gone, children. I have no time for your games.” “Then so be it,” uttered the male coolly. “But this is about Skylar Green. And your foolishness in provoking him.” The girl’s voice rang out loud and clear to Shanahan, but not through conventional sound; her words flowed forth through a combination of mental images and what the cultist deemed as a telepathic form of speech. “This is the most horrible error you have made throughout all of your evil activities, Bruce. You have single-handedly wrought possible doom on all of the Earth, and maybe the universe as it is known.” Her words further enraged the already seething Shanahan, who surged forward at the serene youth as she rested on a cloud of luminous mist. Feinting astonishment, her eyes fluttered and she feebly recoiled. Bruce’s hands arced from opposing sides toward the adolescent’s skull. “AHHH!” His arms swept through the air horizontally as he released a gruff shout, watching his bulky arms cross again in mid-air where the girl had been only moments before. He blinked once and sneered, pivoting at the sensation of a surging rush of energy. A vortex of swirling multi-color light spun in a cyclone of material energy, gradually traversing vertically downward to reform the tangible figure of the young girl. She appeared as calm as ever, and still lapping at her ice cream. “No force is needed here, High Priest Shanahan. We came here only wanting civil discussion.” “How gullible do you take me for, you snotty little bitch?” growled Bruce, his back to the girl and her telepathic banterings. “And barring that…..how the hell did you and your little friend get here? This is an isolated realm reserved for myself and the Black Pharaoh……” The girl drew herself erect, her pasty white fingers curled around the cone of ice cream in her right hand. The other hovered in the foggy background to Bruce’s left. “I don’t believe that is the main issue here…” “On the contrary, that is the issue here,” interjected the robed cult priest. “What’s important is who you are and your logic behind being here.” “No, wrong again….the real issue is the question of what prompted you to condemn our entire sphere of existence by lashing out randomly at Skylar Green?” Shanahan’s already contorted countenance twisted into a perverse half-smirk. The children were playing games, but they were on his terms now. He wouldn't be embarassed and outdebated here of all places. “There was nothing random about my targeting of Skylar Green. It was ordered of me.” “By whom?” Shanahan spun to face his accuser, the tranquil and subtly attractive Asian girl as she licked at a mound of ice cream sapphire in color. Her accusations still had not come in verbal tones, but seemed to reverberate in his mind as if by some telepathic rapport between the pair. “First things first, wench. Your identity and that of your friend. And your means of passage into this most hallowed of dimensions.” She seemed to sigh, though having yet to make a sound. The other came forward from out of the corner of Bruce’s eye, taking the side of the girl. “She is Rumiko, I am Kenshiro,” iterated the boy, resting a hand on the girl’s shoulder. “We are Celestial Oracles, stationed on earth to survey the activities of Celestials.” “I still fail to see your reasoning for breaching this sanctified domain,” spouted Bruce, a scathing demeanor etched across his face as he glared at the pair. “I know of Celestials, and I’ve come to learn Skylar Green is one of those impudent wannabe deities. So factoring that in…..it’s obvious your visit has something to do with displeasure about my appointed quarry.” The one called Kenshiro grinned. “And here you said you saw no reasoning behind our presence here. Amazing deduction, Sherlock.” “No, boy, you misinterpret me. I’m still oblivious to what business it is of two second-rate psychics if I decide to demolish that blind cretin.” Rumiko only sighed, but the tension between the temperamental Kenshiro and the hate-fueled Shanahan only escalated at the mad cultist’s snide comments. Faintly luminescent fog, begotten from a source unseen, continued to drift between the dwellers within the realm. Nyarlathotep had yet to appear, but Shanahan knew even now that the Chaos only lurked beyond the boundaries of detection. His awful presence, though veiled, permeated the small dominion. Shanahan had come to know it by now. But we both act as representatives of the Celestial convention.” The cultist rubbed the stubble of his chin, peering with a deformed grin at his visitors. “Sorta like senators huh? Gotta deal with both of you fucks then?” “Coarse language for such a ‘holy’ place,” commented Kenshiro dryly, fog wafting past his stoic form. Your conduct is absurd, just how do you justify it?” “You are no less mad than I am, you insolent little pest,” spat Shanahan. “I have seen the fate of the cosmos, and I have more sense than to vainly attempt to defy it. I’ve seen my destiny and chosen to go with the flow, so to speak. Fools of your caliber couldn’t comprehend such a thing. You, of course, are much like the one you’re protecting: simply blind. But even so….the situation with Skylar is much more vital at this time….” “What crimes do you speak of, mongrels?” breathed Bruce, gazing into the black eternity beyond the sphere. “For one, we know of the sacrifice,” came the mental transmission of Rumiko. “The mass murder you perpetrated on an entire community, and justified as the whim of the Old Ones whom you serve. I only took the lives of those I deemed worthy of such sacrament, and their spiritual fates I’m not privy to. Farewell…..” In a swirling vortex of light, the figure of Kenshiro gradually vanished from sight. Rumiko soon followed suit after her words had died away into the abyss. Accursed Celestials.
