The One Part 1

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5th, 2002 I'm not a machine. What do you want from me? Do you want me to bleed? Do you want to see the life being sucked from me? The price to pay is high. When I try I am shot back down. What do I need to do to make you people see? I'm not a machine, I'm not. WHY CAN'T YOU STOP? The love isn't there. The pride has been taken, my sophistication which once was high has been downgraded to gay jokes and ripping on the lessors. My life is a lie and meaningless in fact. Why do I exist, why am I here? DAMN IT TELL ME! Lost in the heat of battle. Never knowing which man to your left or right will go down. I never lived it, but I told people I did. Being something you're not can eat away at you like a cancer. It eats and eats at you. You want it to stop but it just won't. Why can't someone treat my cancer? WHY? Every night I am gawked at and insulted. How can I take it? People irritate me when they say that I am terrible and wish me death. Why don't I wish you death? Because I can never hate like you. YOU! You are the one! The one who laughs every time I fall. The one who looks at me when I lay my head to rest. YOU! You are the one who taunts me and pushes me away. When I want to succeed you are the one who drags me back down to the earth. I try and try, every time I try and it's you! YOU WHO DRAG ME DOWN! I need help. Why don't you help instead of hurt? I need your help. But when I ask you spit in my face and shove me to the mud. Where was your hate born? Now you walk up to me. The man in black. You're evil demeanor is ever so evident and you expect me to tremble at your feet. You're nothing. I ask your help in good spirit and you trash me. You are the problem with this world. YOU! How dare you say that to me. How dare you try and put the blame on me. I have never antagonized anyone unless I was paid. I never hurt anyone unless the price was right. I am not responsible for my own problems. You are the problem, but just my like, I am a problem solver. Just wait and see. Now leave me, I do not need your hate filled ways near me. GO!
