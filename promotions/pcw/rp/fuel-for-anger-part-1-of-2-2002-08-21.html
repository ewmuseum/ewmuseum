Fuel for Anger...(Part 1 of 2)

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/02 Moore is working over Cairns, still in the Pretty Cool Guys corner. Cairns can't even defend himself due to the close proximity of the other member of Pretty Cool Guys. Finally, Carey Nowski has had enough, and comes charging in from the opposite corner. Referee Sally Saddle tries to stop him, but Nowski knocks her down with a shoulder block. Nowski pummels the hell out of Moore and as Sherwin comes in Nowski and Cairns catch him with a double superkick. Cairns whips Moore into the far ropes and powerslams him hard to the mat. He makes the cover. *DING, DING, DING* Dave: What the hell? There was no count made. Clay: Ladies and Gentlemen the winners of this contest via disqualification are Pretty Cool Guys!!!!!! Jeff: Brilliant! Nowski assaulted the referee, therefore his team is disqualified. Misplaced was shot out of a victory last Fusion. They were finally dominating a match, but it took a turn for the worse. Carey J. Nowski snapped on the referee, and he snapped on The Pretty Cool Guys. For once he had shown physical abilities, but he lost his mental abilities. Something got in his head. He was fueling for anger. Stephen: “You lost the god damn match for us man. You lost it! I had the cover made, and we had a victory. Then I hear the bell ring without a count. You attack the referee! Why the fuck would you attack a referee?” Carey: “The god damn guys were making me mad. I could tell that they had no respect for me, and I wasn’t going to let it slide. I had to do something about it. We may have lost, but I beat their ass!” Stephen: “You’re becoming a twisted man Carey.” A fueled conversation is going on between Carey and Stephen. “Take me out to the ball game! Take me out to the crowd! Buy me some…” Carey: “Stop with the god damn singing!” Carey interrupts a few little boys who were singing the traditional baseball song “Take me out to the ball game”. The boys all frown and walk away to there parents. Stephen gives Carey an awkward look, but Carey gives him the middle finger. Carey gives Stephen the middle finger. That’s a site for the ages. Stephen: “What the hell has gotten into you? We’re at a baseball game. Those kids were just trying to have some fun!” Carey: “Fuck fun. Who needs fun? What is fun? How come I’ve never heard of this god damn fun thing?” Stephen: “I just don’t know what to do with you. I’m glad you actually show some talent, but you’re becoming a psycho!” Carey: “Eh…what do you know?” Stephen shakes his head, and they enter a gate to reveal a stadium. It’s the small stadium in Williamsport, PA where the little league world series is being held. On the field two teams of kids are warming up for a game, and the stands are filling up with parents and fans. The game is Louisville Vs Missouri. It’s a cheerful environment, and a beautiful day for a baseball game. The team of Misplaced walks around the stadium for a while trying to find their seats. Nervous parents are sitting anxiously for the game to start, and drunk fans are preparing to get the game going. We see Misplaced walking up a row until they reach their seats. They both take a seat next to each other just as the opening pitch is being thrown. “STRRRRRRRIKE ONE!” The Louisville fans cheer as the opening pitch is a strike. The next pitch is thrown, and the crack of a bat can be heard. It’s deep, it’s long, it’s gone! Louisville hits a home run right off the bat to give them a one to nothing edge. Carey: “I could do that. Those kids think they’re so good, but they really aren’t. That pitcher just sucks!” Stephen: “You do realize that many parents are surrounding us right? Carey: “Yeah, and your point is?” Stephen: “Nothing. I have no point then.” Stephen sighs, but he focuses on the game. The next pitch is thrown by the pitcher, and he beams the batter in his arm. Carey busts out laughing.Carey: “You suck kid! Learn to pitch you little ugly runt! You look like pig vomit!” The kid does hear Carey and gives him a look. A tear is running out of the kids left eye. Carey: “Ah…sorry about that! You’re doing fine out there buddy! Now quite crying a river up, and learn to be a man! Baby!” Many of the fans around Carey are giving him awkward looks rightfully so. He continues to laugh to himself, but Stephen is hardly amused. Stephen: “Do us both a favor and shut your mouth while your ahead!” Carey: “I can’t help it that little runt has an arm like my grandmother. She’s dead, but I’d take her on my team over him!” Stephen: “You’re pathetic. I'm supposed to be the twisted man between us, but you're taking that role!' The conversation comes to an end, and they both slouch down in there seats. Innings pass without conversation between the two, and not much action takes place on the field. It’s one to nothing Louisville over Missouri going into the top of the sixth. The Missouri crowd is very tense, but the Louisville crowd all sport smiles. The first pitch of the inning is thrown out, and it is a ball just outside of the box. Carey: “Way to pitch! Keep it up and you’ll for sure lose the game!” Once again the fans and Stephen look at Carey awkward. The game moves on, and the first batter is out. Another pitch is thrown to the next batter, and it is knocked foul. The ball goes soaring into the stands, and it hits Carey right in his stomach. Carey: “Son of a bitch! That little mother fucker just messed my whole night up! God damn that’s going to leave a bruise! You’re an ass kid! You suck!” Carey has a look of great anger on his face. He exits his seat and walks down the ramp, which leads to the field seats. Carey walks to the wall separating the fans and the field, but it doesn’t keep him off of it. He pauses for a moment and focuses on the one kid at bat. Carey: “That little brat hit me!” Carey hops over the wall and he runs at full speed towards the batter. The crowd gets noisy as Carey runs closer to the defenseless child. The boy looks extremely surprised, and he doesn’t really know what to do. In the heat of the moment he just swings his bat as Carey gets near him. Carey slows up not wanting to get whacked with a bat, and the kid stops his swinging. Carey notices security guards coming from all corners of the field, so he charges at full speed towards the kid. He goes to tackle him, but the kid rams the bat straight into his gut. Carey falls to the ground and grasps his stomach in pain. Security guards approach from all sides of the field. Carey: “You little twerp! That hurt!” Carey tries to get up, but a security guard pushes him right back down. They lock the handcuffs on the squirming Carey, and they drag him off of the field. The crowd cheers as they get this lunatic out of here. The security guard drags Carey through the tunnel. Security Guard: 'You know, it's bad enough that you try to attack a kid! It's even worse when the kid ends up hurting you!' Carey jerks his handcuffs trying to get out, but they aren't budging. Carey: 'It was luck you pig!' The security guard chuckles and he gives Carey a little kick to his guy. Carey gasps in pain, but the guard once again just laughs. Stephen gets out of his seat, and he rushes out of the stadium immediately. He notices Carey being stuffed into a police vehicle in the parking lot, and he runs over to him. A security guard puts his arm on his shoulder which slows Stephen up. Security Guard: “Where are you going big guy?” Stephen: “Are you taking him away? Security Guard: “We sure are. You don’t pull an act like that and get to walk away. He’s in some real hot water here.” Stephen: “Where is he going?” Security Guard: “I’m going to take him to the station. I’m sure you know where it is.” The security guard shuts Carey’s door, and he enters his side of the vehicle. The car starts and the sirens start to blow as the police vehicle rides down the road in the dark. So here we have Stephen Cairns standing in a parking lot alone, and his partner is being arrested. Stephen: “That guy is definitely changing. Something has to be done with him!” Stephen pulls out a set of keys from his pocket, and he walks over to Carey’s green Volkswagen Beetle. He enters the car. So Carey is showing anger, and he’s getting arrested for it. He’s screwed up a match for them, and he’s screwed up a baseball game. Yeah, he’s definitely changed.
