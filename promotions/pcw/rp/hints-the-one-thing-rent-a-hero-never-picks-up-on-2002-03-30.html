Title: Hints...The One Thing Rent-A-Hero Never Picks Up On Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-30-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins a few days after the events of the lab incident, where our hero found out that he was none other than a test tube creation....made for destruction, but somehow finding a heart within. The man who created him, Dr.Harkins, was killed after drowning in a river of mysterious chemicals...the seeds to the past of our hero lost in the doctor forever with the coming of his death. Another revelation for our hero is none other then the fact that he has a clone that stands against all the things he loves...a murderous clone who was bent on killing our hero and was thought to be dead in the lab wreckage...yet the question of if he lived or died still lingers. This evil entity who deemed himself, Rent-A-Villain, is also now another skeleton in the thought to be empty 'closet' of our hero. But he can't allow himself to be affected by these revelations, our hero soon to be on the road, reserving a hotel room and spending his night there before heading on the road to take part in the 8 man tag match on Turmoil where he along with Miyagawa, Jason Core, and El Diablo will battle the forces of The Pharoah, Boda, Flaymin Aymin, and his arch-nemisis at the pay-per-view, Juan 'The Golden Toe' Borro...his biggest rival for the PCW Crusierweight gold he brandishes around his waist. But a big suprise for our hero is headed towards our hero...that suprise being none other than the lovely Amy 'Aimz' Campbell, his beautiful OSW lover who just so happens to have a room at this hotel as well. She strolls down the green carpeted hall of the hotel slowly, her hands tucked in her black bondage pants with a red shirt reading in bold yellow letters 'Punk' complimenting her outfit. She carefully reads the numbers set on the wooden doors, our red-headed vixen seemingly looking for a certain as she passes up door after door of hotel room until FINALLY, she stops at room '303', walking over to the door and knocking lightly as she awaits an answer. Her face construes in a slightly peeved look, knocking on the door once again, but still no answer. Suddenly, she begins to hear the screams of a female from inside and an exclaiming Rent-A-Hero, yelling inaudibly from the inside. The usually calm eyes of Amy begin to raise up, a look of obvious anger on her face as she presses her ear to the door, hearing more screams from a female and the loud voice of our hero accompanying those wails as he exclaims behind the door..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'YEAH YEAH!!! Well let me tell you something....YOUR F**KING RIGHT!!'..::/::..Aimz backs up all the way to the opposie end of the hall, dashing and focusing all her might in her shoulder as she slams into the wooden hotel room door, the impact ripping it from it's hinges as it swings wide open, Aimz breathing heavily and probing the room, eyes filled with fury as she looks to be ready to brawl, exclaiming..::/::..Aimz:'YOU TWO-TIMING SON OF A BI**H!! Aimz simply grind the toe of her red and black Vans into the carpet and giggles nervously as our hero shut off his gaming system, standing up and looking to Aimz, the spandex material of his Black Panther replica costume scrunching up as he stands to look at Aimz before saying..::/::..
