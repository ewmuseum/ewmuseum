Cowabunga's Reloaded - Part 2 - History

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 19, 2003 [The Cowabunga Connection was a corner stone of the tag team division a year ago. They were the ultimate fan favorite against all of the evil tag teams that rose to the occassion. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry were looked at as astranged super heroes in a world of reality. Everytime their song would hit the fans would burst into a frenzy as their favorite heroes would trot through the curtain to give them ultimate entertainment. Through evil fiends as Politically Incorrect and Suburban Uprising they engrained a sense of heroism onto the fans of PCW. No matter what the Connection did the crowd would support them in their venture and make sure they pulled through with a victory in the end. It was a giving relationship between the Connection and it's fans. The Connection gave them entertainment and a hope that there is still something good left in this world. The fans gave the Connection hope that they could get through any situation and somehow come out on top. It was this type of relationship that drove 'Totally Tubular' Tommy into the madness that he has become to this very day. Tommy truly believed that the Cowabunga Connection could rise to become the GREATEST tag team in PCW history. Not only that but they would rise to put a stamp on tag team wrestling as how to conduct yourself as a great tag team. Unfortunately, through evil booking schemes and screw jobs the Connection really was never able to shine like Tommy thought they would. The evil politicians and evil skateboarders would run away laughing maniacally as the Connection would have to lick it's wounds over and over again. The fans wanted the Connection to succeed, wanted them to reach the heights that Tommy wanted, but it truly didn't matter to the fans. They just wanted the entertainment and the Connection NEVER failed at that] [One fateful night 'Heavy Metal' Harry faced off against Akiyama and The End during a battle royal. Harry is not the smartest man in the world and even at that moment he knew something terrible was about to happen. Akiyama charged with a brutal shoulder block and The End spun with a spinning back kick and it just so happened that both men hit Harry's chubby body at the exact same time. The only thing that The Stoned One felt was...well, he felt absolutely nothing as he toppled to the mat. It would be Harry's last time stepping into a wrestling ring for competition. The Cowabunga Connection had died on that night and many fans were devastated by the actions. Tommy went inward and couldn't cope with the fact that his brother was finished with wrestling. He wasn't going to let Harry give up on his wrestling career but what Tommy wanted just was not going to physically happen. Tommy went singles kicking and screaming the entire way but when he started winning his attitude began to change. He felt that unbelievable surge of energy he once had when he was a competitor in NTICW. He was on the horizon to truly change the face of wrestling with one fail swoop. It's at that moment Tommy's personality changed completely. He wasn't the giving and thoughtful person that all the fans made him out to be. He became ruthless and greedy in his quest to become the greatest singles wrestler of all time. See, when Tommy was part of the Cowabunga Connection he felt having the fans love you meant that you would slowly rise to power and win the tag team titles. He slowly learned that that is never the case in wrestling. Since he had a fresh start as a singles wrestler he knew that he had to only care about one person and one person only, himself. That's exactly what he did as he became Continental Champion] [One of the positives that came out of the Cowabunga Connection reign was that Tommy needed to take out his opponents. To rid evil of the world that evil needed to be pushed out of the federation. So, that's exactly what he did with his foes. The End was the first victim of Tommy's tyrad through the singles competition. Unfortunately, The End returned a new as the totally heinous Skylar Green, but that's all right because Tommy gets another chance at that dude. The next guy to fall at the hands of Tommy was the totally heinous Rent-A-Jerkface. He tried to take Tommy's Continental Title away but failed...TWICE! The pain was too much for him to bear and now Rent-A-Jerkface is gone, probably off to his homeland of I'm Really Crazy And Need A Therapist. After that dude there was the ever popular Venom Pentagram. Every guy in the locker room truly believed that Venom could defeat Tommy and end his World Title reign quickly. Were they totally fooled as Tommy not only defeated him but put him on the injured list for a VERY long time. No one has heard from or seen Venneman Pendragon since that night, and that's the way it should stay by Tommy's terms] [The Cowabunga Connection has one more fight in them. They step back into the ring together since that battle royal seven months ago. The fans will not cheer them on to victory as they did all those months ago, but they will only hope that they have the same fate that they had the fateful night they were ended. It's all simply because Tommy was out for himself and not to please the fans of PCW. It's pretty pathetic really. Most of the people in the crowd would easily step on another person to climb higher to the top of the ladder but when someone else does it...it's evil and wrong. When Tommy stopped listening to the whining fans he rose to the highest point you can go in PCW and yet they boo him for all that he's done. Tommy is all right with that and so is Harry. As The Connection steps through the curtain one more time they won't care about the fans but only about the heinous Pharaoh and Isiy. It will be a reunion of bodacious proportions and they will stand tall as the victors in the contest] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: GOOD LORD!! OH MY GOD!! YESSSSS!! [The moans of pleasure would make one think Tommy was with a bodacious babe, but when you watch the tanned, muscular PCW World Champion stumble out of a movie theater it's obvious that it's something else] Tommy: THAT WAS THE MOST BODACIOUS THING I EVER SAW!!! ['Heavy Metal' Harry stumbles out of the theater with his eyes as wide as silver dollars. He holds onto the walls trying to keep his balance. It's not because of the drugs he's on but because of the film he has just finished watching] Harry: Am I high from the weed or the movie, man? [Tommy looks at his brother with a serious expression] Tommy: Dude, I think that it's the movie...seriously. Harry: Yeah, man. [The brothers walk out of the theater and into the warm California sun. Both of them squint their eyes stumbling on the sidewalk] Tommy: The sun can be MOST BOGUS after a flick. [Tommy pulls out his Oakley's and puts them on] Harry: Yeah, man. [Harry doesn't carry sunglasses so he just squints his eyes together stumbling into the street, almost being hit by a speeding BMW] Tommy: Dude, I think that movie has just changed my life forever. I feel like I don't live in this world, that it's part of...dare I say it....THE MATRIX!! Harry: Yeah, man. Do you think that The Pharaoh and that Eygptian chick are agents? Tommy: No way, dude. Agents are dressed in a suit and tie and have one of those gnarly ear pieces. Harry: That's right, man. Tommy: Maybe they're really viruses in The Matrix trying to stop us, the bodacious good guys from Zion, from becoming free. Or in this case they are trying to hurt me before the gnarly PPV so that Pharaoh can win the title. That is most heinous, and it will TOTALLY not happen. Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men walk through the parking lot and to the 2002 blue Mustang convertible. It looks a little dirty but with Tommy's champion schedule it's hard to do the little things in life. This is why he needs to hire one of those maid people to take care of those things] Tommy: One thing that is for sure...The Cowabunga Connection WILL RIDE AGAIN! Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy throws the car into gear and peels out] Fade To Black
