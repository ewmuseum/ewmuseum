Sexaholics - Part 5 - Seashells

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 8, 2002 [This is totally bodacious, dudes. The Cowabunga Connection, a team that has been quiet of late, is getting yet another shot at becoming the tag team champions. This could be it. The time that the dudes wear the gold that they were born to wear. Half brothers. Best friends. Tag Team partners. They are ready] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Have any other tag teams even made a bodacious impact like we have? No, I think not. We've taken out more teams in PCW than those other dudes can even think of. The division is totally boring, a snoozefest if you want to really go that far. In fact, I think that PCW has become quite heinous in our abscence. Do you agree? [Sitting in the passenger seat of Tommy's gnarly blue and yellow 2002 Mustang is a little boy. His blonde hiar is parted to the right, looking like his mother combed his hair for him. You know...one of those spit in the hand things. Those were most bogus times as a little compadre. His Spider-Man T-shirt is worn proudly with a little pair of blue jeans. The little dude looks no more than 10 and seems to be most interested in Tommy's rantings] Boy: Ok. [The boy didn' really understand the ramblings of his dad's friend, but if he agrees with the guy maybe he'll teach him how to surf] TTT: See, that's what I'm talking about. We are most misused in PCW and it's time that we step up, dude. You better stay glued to that television set this Friday because we're going to be TOTALLY BODACIOUS in that match up. [The boy smiles] Boy: Sweet! [He really wants to surf, damnit] TTT: Your damn skippy, compadre. [Tommy pulls into his driveway, taking in the full view of his blue and yellow sided beach house. He's a most stylish dude, and nobody can ever get this gnarly with their home. Not even that Martha Stewart babe and all her homemaking crap. As Tommy throws his Mustang into 'Park' he begins to think about the hot brunette babe he saw coming back from Jimmy Hackman's house. Her body was totally radical and her ass was one of the best he had ever seen, that's saying a lot coming from The Tubular One. All he had to do was pull over, nod his head, and he'd be playing Yahtzee all afternoon long with that tight buttocks. It just can't be. He's recovering from sexual addiction. It's not like he can't have sex ever again, he just can't let it consume his life. Once he's done with a show he immediately begins to think about the bodacious babe he's going to be with that night. Even before the show he's thinking about that. Ok...you caught Tommy, he's thinking about banging bodacious babes while in the ring. It's totally bogus, really. Just like a junkie who wants his next fix of heroin, Tommy wanted his next fix of babedom. His career has suffered greatly from this addiction. The Connection should be walking around with gold around their waists, but instead they are bare. Left with empty hands. Poor. Looked down on. A pure disappointment to them all. You bet your surfboard that Tommy is strung out. It took every bit of strength he had the night before to not jump in the Mustang and go to Shailyn's house for a totally gnarly night cap. He fell asleep before he could get up, which was a blessing in disguise. That's why Jimmy Hackman's son, Billy, is there. Tommy knows that with a bodacious little dude running around will bring back those childhood days, refraining him from thinking about those totally heinous sexual thoughts. It's a large gravy train from here on out, dudes. Tommy has conquered his addiction and now it's time to go out there on Friday Fusion and get those PCW tag team titles] Billy: Tommy, how come there are a bunch of sea shell's outside your house? [Tommy looks at his front deck and there are dozens of sea shells sitting on the patio furniture, leading into the house. That's most strange] TTT: I don't know, dude. [As they walk onto the deck, looking over the very different sea shells, Billy damns himself for not opening his mouth sooner. He wants to surf, god damnit. The entire reason for this trip was for Blondie to teach him great surf moves so that he can be a very accomplished surfer, just like Tommy. In fact, Billy plans on breaking all of Tommy's huge records. Now if the dumb bastard would quit worrying about sea shell's and teach him how to surf none of this would be a problem] TTT: DUDE!! [As the two walk inside the house, it's apparent that the inside is much worse than the outside. Almost every square inch of the living room is covered with sea shells. Its as if a totally gnarly dump truck just rode in and dumped every sea shell into the house. Tommy and Billy carefully walk through the living room so not to break any of these quite gnarly sea shells] Billy: Where did all of these come from? TTT: Well, there is only one person who is capable of this bogusness.... [Just then 'Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's half brother and tag team partner, walks into the living room. His hair tied into a pony tail, his Twisted Sister T-shirt stained with beer, and his black leather pants with tears in them. Oh yeah...and those pesky sea shells are in his hands. His bloodshot eyes look at Tommy as if there is nothing going on. Tommy points at his brother with an accusing index finger] TTT: And that's the dude capable of this bogusness. [Harry looks around the living room as if there weren't any sea shells there at all] Harry: What are you talking about, man? [Before Tommy can retort, Billy does] Billy: He's talking about the sea shells, moron. [Yes, Billy wants to surf. He wants to learn. The faster he insults this stoner, the faster he gets out there on the waves] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Where did they come from? [a smile crosses Harry's face, as if Tommy should know the answer] Harry: The beach, man. We do live on one. [Tommy looks out of the window, then back at Harry] TTT: I know that, but what I really mean is WHY are they here? Harry: That's an interesting question, man... [Harry shuffles through a small pile of the shells and then points at a rather large one sitting on Tommy's handcrafted, oak coffee table] Harry: I really wanted to masterbate so I went outside and found this monster, man. I brought it inside and then I wanted to masterbate again. So, I went back outside and got another seashell. Then I put it next to the monster shell and had that same urge to flog the dolphin, man. So, I went outside... TTT: DUDE!! I get the point. So, everytime you want to masterbate you go out and get a seashell? Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy looks around the living room noticing that it's filled with seashells. Billy looks around as well, sort of puzzled by whats going on. He's ten, not really understanding all this talk of masterbation and seashells. He does understand the fact that he wants to freakin' surfboard though. Now if the blonde dumbass would stop trying to figure out that his brother is a gigantic moron they could get to that goal a lot sooner] TTT: There has to be thousands of seashells in here, dude!! [Harry shrugs] Harry: I think about masterbation a lot, man. TTT: But I've only been gone for 3 hours, dude!! [Harry looks confused, really not knowing what Tommy is trying to prove with his three hours mumbo jumbo. The new issue of Hustler comes out today! It's going to be a fun night tonight with Ginger...wait...he thought about masterbation....] Harry: Time to get another seashell, man. [Harry walks out in the warm sun to find another seashell to help him cope with his sexual addiction. Tommy guides Billy through the insane number of seashells to the back deck. The beach is most gnarly in the back, giving a nice personal space for the Tubular One to surf the waves when he needs that release. Billy feels satisfied, finally getting what he came here for] Billy: Am I going to learn to surf the waves, Tommy? [Billly makes sure he sounds surprised by this. He doesn't want to give it away that he's using Tommy or anything. Not that the dumbass would even notice] TTT: I don't think so, compadre. You've never been on a surfboard before and for you to go out there now could be most heinous. Billy: WHAT?! TTT: Dude, surfing is most complicated. You need to learn balance and the funadmentals before you go out there to shred, dude. Billy: This is bullsh*t! I'm giving up free time of playing Grand Theft Auto: Vice City to come over here and learn how to surf!! Now your telling me that I can't go out there and surf?! YOU F**KING PIG!!! [Tommy seems most frightened of this little dude. One minute he's like all quiet and happy, the next he's the son of Lucifer] TTT: Dude, I'm going to teach you how to surf, but it will be a little bit of time before you get on the waves. Billy: Screw you! I had to listen to you bitch and moan about how the Cowabunga Connection is getting robbed all the time. All you seem to do is bitch and moan about everything. You know what...you guys suck anyway. You do realize that all those teams you 'supposedly' took out were fired or had to leave for personal reasons. You did nothing to chase them away!! THe only reason your not on television these days is because PCW is thinking about canning you! You know why they are doing that?! BECAUSE THE COWABUNGA CONNECTION SUCKS!!! [As if the hand of fate drew it's gnarly hand, a seashell falls onto the child's head, knocking him unconcious. Tommy looks on in shock. He feels the childs carotid artery to make sure the little dude is still kicking, and it turns out that he is. Looks like he got something heinous in return for bad mouthing the greatest team in PCW] TTT: Ungrateful little dude. [Tommy walks back into the house, through the madness of seashells. Twisted Sister plays loudly throughout the house, which is a usual thing] TTT: DUDE!!!! [Tommy is at Harry's bedroom entrance, trying to get the dude to turn down his tunes] TTT: DUDE!!!!! [Harry doesn't hear him. He just stands next to his far out sound system holding one of the seashells up to the speakers] TTT: DUDE!!!!!!!!!!! [Harry turns around, frightened by the loudness of Tommy's voice. Harry turns down the tunes] TTT: What are you doing, compadre? Harry: An experiment, man. [Harry hands the seashell that was against the speaker to Tommy] Harry: Give it a listen, man. [Tommy places to seashell against his ear, and all that he can hear is the ocean. That's usually what seashells do] TTT: I just hear the ocean, dude. Harry: You can't hear Twisted Sister in the background, man? TTT: Nope. Harry: Damn, man. [Tommy shrugs and then walks out of the room. He needs to prepare for the upcoming match. It's going to be totally gnarly to step back into the squared circle. With so many teams in there at once it can be anyone's win, but Tommy knows that this is their moment. This is what a comeback is all about. Returning to gain the gold. To gain the titles that they so deserved since they entered the gnarly halls of PCW. It's time to put the belts where they belong]
