Girl Talk [A] - The Best Friend

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 23, 2003 This was a slow weekend…like time decided to take a lazy day. Laziness annoyed us to no end…it was a constant reminder of the type of people that manage to get ahead with their big mouths instead of with their wrestling talent. Names come to mind…but they know who they are. Time is an irrelevant concept to begin with. It was only created by humanity to scale their constant physical and mental degrading. That was the fact nonetheless… Sonata’s been busy all morning with something; cleaning the castle for some big get together she’s having this afternoon. We don’t care as long as her people don’t bother us…or if any of those people are the wrong type of people we don’t want to cast eyes upon again. We’re gonna sit down here in the basement and train for the match while she has her fun… We’ll have our fun when we get to destroy those nine men going against our will. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “When you walk away, you don’t hear me say “Pleeeaasee….Oh baaa—aaby! Don’t go! Simple and clean is the way that you’re making me feeeeel tonight! It’s harrd to let it go!!” Sonata harmonized to the voice of Hikaru Utada’s “Simple and Clean” dance mix as she straightened the living room up. Wearing a black skirt going to her knees, yellow knee socks, and a yellow turtleneck, she pranced around arranging pillows and making sure the hot water for the tea was still hot. She stopped in the doorway, looking at the tidy room and smiled to herself. “Perfect!” she held up two fingers and grinned. Then she dashed off towards the kitchen and began setting cakes and candies on the tray to bring to the living room. But before that happened a bell rang far off at the front of the castle. She looked back and with excitement in her bright eyes. “They’re here!!” She ran across the courtyard into the main hallway, sliding her feet into her slippers to walk across the front yard to the castle’s front gate. She pulled the oak door back and shot out a bright smile at who stood there. A tall woman in tight blue jeans, black silver buckled knee high boots, and a white tank top stood with her arms folded across her chest. Her sandy brown hair was braided in a thick ponytail down her back, and a thick sheet of hair hung down over the right side of her face. “Some place you have here, kiddo.” She muttered, looking up at the kanji written on the top of the front gate. “Must be rolling in the yen huh?” Sonata laughed as she swung the gate open for her friend. She pressed her arms at her sides and bowed deeply to her. “Commander. It’s good to see you again.” The woman popped her in the back of her head while she bowed. Sonata came back up and rubbed her head, finding the woman’s red lips pursed in intolerance. “I’m not your commander anymore. You quit the team long time ago…now we’re just pals. I’m your friend…Sayaka Mitsui.” “Probably the only one I got left.” she said with a grin. Friends don’t last long anymore in this day and age…everyone’s got their own agenda to work, to make sure their own benefit is kept. Selfishness under the cover of alliance. Despicable. They hugged before Sonata led her through the front yard. “So, how was the trip out here? You get lost or anything?” Sayaka rolled her eyes…well eye, considering only one was visible. “You kidding me, kiddo? After years of tracking down governmental officials and terrorists you think I can’t find my way to someone’s home on this rinky-dink island?” “Yeah, what was I thinking?” she stuck her tongue out at her friend as they entered into the castle. Sayaka crouched down to unbuckle her tall boots, glancing up at Sonata for a second. “How’s life been treating you?” “Same…still hiding from the law, still fighting the good fight with the Turbo Brigade.” Sayaka yanked her white socked foot out of one boot and started on the other. “You?” Sonata tried to hold back a smile as she looked around the area. “Oh…I can’t complain…tee hee.” Another roll of the “eye” from Sonata’s old friend. “Guess you’re happy with your life. Glad to see it after so long. You didn’t seem so happy when you joined our little group all those years ago. Fresh graduate and all and nothing working for you. What was the reason you joined us for again?” She shrugged her shoulders, looking away for a second. “Emotional breakdown…felt I needed a change of scenery.” “Oh yeah…that guy you were in love with left you at school. Whatever happened to him anyways?” “Uh…I think he became a wrestler or something.” The two of them walked down the hallway to the sitting room, where they sat on the floor at the low table on black pillows. Sayaka raised her knee up, propping her elbow on it as she reached for a pack of cigarettes in her back pocket. “Yeah, I started watching that wrestling stuff a few months back. A little PURE when it’s on, a few shows of OSW when they air it on cable. PCW every now and then.” Sonata looked up from pouring a cup of tea with a knowing smile. “PCW you say?” “Yeah, ever heard of it?” “Yeah, I hear a little every now and then.” Holding back her smile, she passed the cup to her friend and began to pour her own. “Who’s your favorite over there?” Sayaka sipped the tea, nodding satisfaction to Sonata before sticking a cigarette in her mouth. “Dunno. Lot of characters over there…like they’re all putting on a play or show or something. I mean real off the wall crap too. They say Japanese wrestling goes overboard with their masked characters, but these guys are intolerable.” “You don’t say? Every time I watched I personally liked Rent A Hero. Good old fashioned hero type.” She shook her head as she blew out a cloud of smoke into the air. “Nah, RAH’s pretty much sparked out as far as his career goes. He hasn’t done anything great since his feud with Snow…and that was basically because Brandi was a part of it adding the outside factor as the Black Kitten.” Sonata nodded intently. “Oh…I had no idea.” “Yeah, that’s what happens when you don’t pay attention to stuff like that. You gotta look at all the details so you can judge these people carefully.” Sayaka reached over for a cookie, almost knocking her tea cup over. “You see, nowadays PCW is all about characters, and how good can a character act on air. They don’t care about talent anymore. Otherwise, they’d have true names in their elite. Not just some clowns putting on a show to appease the people.” “Like who?” Sayaka shrugged at her question. “Uh, you got some a few wacky religion gimmicks walking around; Pharaoh, Dorn Tracer, Violence Jack…they got religions you’d never hear of unless you took a class at the university or something. And Jack’s religion I don’t even think exists. Then there’s the history fools, the guys who are acting like they’re from different era existing in today times. There’s Venneman Pendragon, Pharaoh again, outside of PCW there’s the Roman Empire in PWC. It’s basically stupid cross-era conflicts and old-timer talk. Next thing you know they’ll have a Miner 49er in PCW in his quest for gold.” “Actually…” she interjected. “That seems like a good idea for a historic wrestling character.” Sayaka cut her eyes narrow…almost closed somewhat. “You’re not helping the fact.” Sonata apologized quickly and stuffed a chocolate chip cookie into her mouth. “As I was saying, then you got some people who have these personalities about them, but they’re just average guys you don’t care about. There’s this one guy called Chet King…I think his gimmick is that he’s a loudmouth full of himself egoist…anyways this guy just does nothing but talk about everything that goes on around him…even if it has nothing to do with him. I saw not too long ago he was talking about a tag team wrestler…not even thinking about his own big event set to come up in a few days. That’s either the mark of someone with ADD, or a dumbass.” Sonata brushed a few crumbs off her black skirt, nodding in accordance to the conversation. “Seems like you don’t like anyone in PCW.” She nodded back at the observation. “Yeah…but out of all the characters there only one of them pisses me off the most.” “Who’s that?” She blew out more smoke…cigarette smoke, not Chet King rambling smoke…then she answered the question. “Akiyama.” Sonata almost choked while drinking her tea. Choke…good choice of action for what Akiyama’s opponents would do. She stared across the table at Sayaka with open eyes. “Who?” “Akiyama…some big goof trying to play off he’s a half god or something.” She snubbed out her cigarette and finished off the cup of tea in front of her. “It’s really annoying to watch him come on TV and completely degrade the human race the way he does. Like he’s not human or something…when we all know he IS.” Sonata sat quietly while her friend ranted on. “He personally has some skills under his belt, but the guy’s personality is totally retarded. Hates everyone…doesn’t care about interacting with anyone in the company, has no idea what’s even going on in the company that doesn’t concern him. He even hates working for PCW! If you hate working for the place…then leave!” “Yeah…leave.” Cutting her eyes into the hallway, she gave a nervous chuckle. “I’m serious!” Sayaka pushed her tea cup across the table at Sonata, throwing her hand up and shaking her head. “All this talk about being the best, having all this talent, and he does nothing for about a month and gets tossed out of a ring by 7 people. It’s just irritating!!” “Calm down, Sayaka…it’s all fake, remember?” Sonata’s goading managed to cool off the woman, her back arching over as she relaxed. “Yeah…thanks…I just get mad sometimes at stupid stuff. Mostly why I can’t look at most of those wrestlers I mentioned on air anymore…or get totally pissed off. There’s a big 10 man brawl for the world title coming up, but I probably wont watch that because either someone who doesn’t deserve it will win, or someone who doesn’t will by help of an angle. Then I’ll probably have a fit…” They had a quiet time between the two of them…Sonata sipping her tea slowly while Sayaka pulled out another cigarette. “So…who’s the new guy you’re shacked up with?” Sonata almost spilled her tea as she put it down, looking up to see her friend winking at her…or blinking…it was hard to tell with one of her eyes covered by hair. “Oh, he’s just some guy I grew up with. Real nice guy…you’d like him.” Sayaka nodded as she lit up. “Cool. Hope I can meet him sometime.” She shook her head roughly. “Nah, you probably wouldn’t like him.” “Come on now…don’t be shy with the guy now. I’m like your big sister…if the guy is treating you right, then I’m happy for you.” “SONATA!!!” Sayaka jumped to her feet, reaching behind her back for something as the booming voice of Akiyama roared out through the castle. Sonata stood up at her seat as Akiyama came walking into the room, black vinyl pants covering his lower body and his upper nude. Covered in sweat and breathing heavy, he looked at Sonata with a wrinkled brow. “Where are the towels?” he said in a tired voice. “Upstairs, in the linen closet.” She pointed to somewhere off in the castle before thumbing at Sayaka. “By the way, this is my friend, Sayaka Mistui. Sayaka, this is Akiyama.” Her jaw was to the floor as she stared at the god in awe. Aki raised an eyebrow at her before looking back at Sonata. “She f-cked or something?” “No…she’s just…amazed at your massive physique.” Aki nodded slowly, backing out of the room slowly. “Yeah…well don’t make Sonata upset, or you’ll piss us off…and we need to save our anger for our match this week. Other than that…nice to meet ya.” He walked away, leaving the girls alone. Sayaka looked at Sonata, mouth still agape and hand still behind her back. “You mean…?” Sonata nodded modestly. “Yep…that’s THE Akiyama. Same one I went to school with, same one I fell in love with, same one that hates PCW…and so on, and so on.” Sayaka sighed, sitting back down on her pillow. She placed what she was reaching for behind her back on the table…a magnum automatic hand pistol. “Girl…we gotta talk…” -=- To be Continued. -=-
