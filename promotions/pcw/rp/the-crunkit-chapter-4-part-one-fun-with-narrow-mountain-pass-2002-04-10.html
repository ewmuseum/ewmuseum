The Crunkit Chapter 4(Part One): Fun With Narrow Mountain Passes and Whiny Crunkits

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/02 While some of those lazy PCW superstars were watching Spring Fling this past Sunday, back in the other world, for who’s to say which world is real, well I am actually. So, back in the unreal world, Rushbo the Crunkit and his companions continued on their journey past the Last Homie House. Rushbo: Damn wiggers. Hey, don’t talk during my long intros! Like I said, they continued the journey. They found the right path thanks to the Portuguese direction instinct of Juandalf, and the fact that if they went the wrong way we wouldn’t have much of a story now would we? Long after they passed out of the valley from Elrond’s home, they continued to climb. Up and up, until they crashed right into the Goodyear Blimp. Wait, on second thought, they continued their journey and Rushbo thought about his poor little Crunkit hole. He wished he could be there, back in his lovely neighborhood with all the other jolly Crunkits. Back at Crunk End… Thief 1: Foo’, this place is loaded with shit. I’m gonna floss a pinky ring about 70 afta this. Thief 2: Damn right, this nigga been frontin’ on us. That’s why we gotta jack his ass up while he gone. Thief 1: Oh daaaaaaamn! Look at these pimped out chains! Oh, we got to take these. The two thieves continue to rummage through Rushbo’s things as we go back to the scene of the companions trudging slowly upwards. Every once in a while, a boulder would come flying down and scare the shit out of Rushbo, but the rest of the group realized that they were safe from danger because a 2/3 of a page RP really isn’t going to win a match, even if Red Rock never RPs. Handler: Ok, that’s it. From now on, I don’t care if you die. In fact, I’m going to write this story so that you have to die, and you’ll have to do work NOT to die. There. The group looks up at the almighty handler with a look that shows all of their feeling and emotions. A look that says, “Yeah right buddy, we all know you just want this RP to be over right now.” To show that Rushbo’s handler is serious, he sends another boulder crashing down and it lands in the middle of the group, increasing their upward incline speed. Now, back to the REAL story. When they slept at night, they were uncomfortable and freezing, which was not helped by Rushbo’s constant blabbering. They say by the fire one night while the small Crunkit shivered. Rushbo: Boy, I sure am glad I came with you stupid white people and your treasure. I’m just lucky you haven’t killed me yet. No wonder I don’t get any sleep, I have to keep my eyes open just to make sure you won’t pull out your white hoods. Coren: Burglar, why would we kill you when you’re our only way to recover our treasure from Smaug, as little of a chance as your mediocre thieving skills give us? Rushbo: I’m not the evil genius behind your plans. You’re probably in cahoots with that dark jobber dragon. Sethin: If we were in cahoots with him, why wouldn’t we have just killed you already? Why would we go out of our way just to kill you? Rushbo: Probably because you want my back to break from all this terrible sleeping conditions so that you won’t have to do the dirty works yourselves! Trevin: Dude, do you ever stop whining? Rushbo: It’s not whining, it’s pre-cautionary measures. This is the kind of shit that the group had to put up with, and to tell you the truth; the group was just about ready to actually kill the little Crunkit. Just smother him with a pillow, no one will ever know about it. However, they needed a thief, and there were no other burglars in the immediate vicinity, so they were stuck with Rushbo. But they kept silent mostly during the day, for they knew that at night the whining would come once again, but eventually even Rushbo grew tired of whining, so the group stayed silent all together, except for grumbling at meals or when a decision was to be made. Finally, one day while they rode their ponies and Juandalf rode his white Negro-oppressing racists! And I have a dream… errr…. Juandalf rode his white horse up the mountainside, Coren suggested a gloomy thought. Coren: It is summer nearby, for the blackberries are blooming, and there’s more streaking elves running around without uhh… limp bizkits, if you know what I mean. Rushbo’s handler mumbles something about a terrible band and a stuck-up lead singer but we can’t hear that. The group had left Elrond’s house with good intentions and feeling well about their ride through the mountains, which they hoped would be swift and easy. The gloomy feelings were about to intensify. Coren: It could very well already be Durin’s Day, though I have no idea without a proper calendar. Rushbo: Oh my Rush Mason! You white people don’t even know the date? Note to self: Not knowing date could be cover up for killing the black guy in the group. Juandalf didn’t reply to Rushbo’s comment or Coren’s, he just shook his head and continued to ride, and the grim silence returned from before. He knew the evil that lay ahead that was not easily surpassed, better than anyone else he knew. He could tell that something was going to happen, most likely something that would not be very good for this misplaced group. However, the group continued over peaks of mountains, in lands so barren that they had no proper name or ruler, they were just there. All was still going considerably well though, for a wet, cold journey on ponies, until the day that a thunderstorm struck in full force. Rushbo had never even contemplated how large a storm of this magnitude could be, and he showed so with a comment placed here or there. Rushbo: Oh, you’ve GOT to be kidding me. More of this shit? Soon, you’re not going to have to kill me to get rid of me, I’m going to heave my body of one of these cliffs. This is the single most NOT bitchhoss trip I’ve ever taken. Sethin: Dude, bitchhoss? Coren: Bitchhoss, indeed. Though you couldn’t tell from their conversation, they were indeed in grave danger. They were on a narrow pass and each strike of lightning threatened to cause any amount of possible damage to the mountain pass. The rain pelted them like bullets of water, ok terrible analogy, but it pelted them like something that pelts really hard. Coren: This is no way to travel, I must agree with the Crunkit! Juandalf: If you know a better place to travel, then be my guest, dwarf. With this comment, Sethin and Trevin, extremely tired of everything going on, went off to find another shelter, or slit their own throats, because they frankly were NOT going to be caught dead with the other three for another five minutes in this weather. They returned and promptly alerted every one of their findings. Trevin: Dudes, we found a cave. The other three members, who had been bickering the whole time and didn’t even notice the fact that the two dwarves were gone, despite the fact that Sethin and Trevin had walked right past each one of them. Juandalf: Thank god, well let’s go. Rushbo: Wait a second; don’t you care if this cave has been inspected? Who knows, it could be some evil guys hideout. Coren: Rushbo, the KKK doesn’t hold meeting in caves. Rushbo: In that case, call me bin Laden, put me in that cave, and dig my grave. Sethin: That made no sense. Rushbo, thinking himself amazingly clever, didn’t bother to respond to the dwarf’s comment and followed behind Trevin, who had already started his pony off towards the cave’s entrance. They arrived at the cave’s entrance and a small man popped out, wearing a large sand-colored robe. Man: Boo! Rushbo: You are? Man: Don’t you know me? I’m Lester “the Molester” bin Laden. Rushbo pointed towards his left, over the cliff face. Rushbo: Hey sand master; Chefbo the elf’s promos are thattaway. Lester: Oh. With that, Lester bin Laden heads off, confused. This leaves the group to file into the cave, each leading their ponies in single file, because of the tight squeeze to get into the entrance. However, Juandalf illuminated the cave with some sort of wizardy power, we’re not sure what the fuck he did, and frankly, we’re kinda tired, so we don’t care, but he searched every nook and cranny of the cave, making sure that it was safe. After he had approved it, everyone scattered into their bedding, because the last one to get in was the rotten egg. And instead of fading into sleep or doing what Rush’s characters in promos usually do, they all quickly passed out, exhausted from their long journey. They all had different dreams. Juandalf dreamt of shoving his wizard staff, well never mind. Coren dreamt of his not-so dwarf piece going right into some juicy a- never mind that either. Trevin and Sethin dreamt of getting some suburban chicks, bending them over and- well you know what, those dreams aren’t really important, and as much as some handlers *cough* Byron Calver *cough* would like it, we won’t continue that. However, Rushbo dreamt about something different. You see he dreamed about putting his, ahem, “Crunkness” into the mouth of- no wait, that’s another dream. His most fictitious dream was about a crack in the wall. He dreamt that it grew bigger and bigger, until the most horrific thing that had ever appeared in one of Rush’s dreams came out from the crack: White people spilled out like syrup onto waffles, ok another bad analogy, but the white people came out, and we’re talking a shit lot of white people here. They all burst out and kidnapped each one of the littler people, but Juandalf somehow was not taken. Then, Rushbo woke, but it wasn’t all a dream. There were white people, and lots of them… OOC: This is meant to be one of my longer chapters and I’m kinda tired as I mentioned, so I’ll probably add some elements from Hobbit chapters together, so don’t go all crazy on my ass because I splice chapters, besides, it’s a hate crime if you do anyway, so don’t try any funny business, buddy. :-)
