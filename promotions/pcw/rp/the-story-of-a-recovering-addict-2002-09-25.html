The story of a recovering addict

25 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/25/02 -A man in desperate shape walks into a bar in the middle of the afternoon. The middle aged man looks tired and warn out. Upon closer look we see this is not just a man but rather a Malice Man. Duke Williams who always looks like a million dollars now looks like death. The black under his eyes indicate lack of sleep and stress.- Williams: Whiskey line then up. -The bartender looks at the sorry state of the man in front of him and sees profit.- Bartender: You ok? Williams: Are you a bartender or Dr. Phil?? Now line them up. -The bartender pours the booze for the recovering alcoholic who hasn’t touched a drink in two months.- Williams: You believe in Karma? Bartender: The stripper at the deep hole on main? -Duke’s lip snarls up as he stares into the whiskey. The darkness of his youth. The crutch of his life. Sobriety versus reality a tough choice.- Williams: KARMA what goes around comes around. Bartender: Yea the more glasses I fill the more tips I get. -Duke is obviously not in the mood for humor. Especially from a quick witted bartender.- Williams: I take away peoples livings. I ruin lives. Now it comes back to me. My wife is gone, I said something to upset my best friend now he hates me, my chances of winning gold are fading, my fans are gone. -Duke just stares into the booze.- Williams: And I haven’t had a drink in two months. My mind is so clear yet so fogged up I don’t know what is up and what is down anymore. At least when I drink I new I had an escape. -A cold sweat coves the forehead of the Malice Man.- Williams: What the fuck is going on?? Why am I here? Is this worth throwing out the last two months of my life? Yea miyagawa has gotten under my skin but do I let him win by taking this drink? Do I let a kid of lesser intellect, skill, and personality take me out cus I’m not in my right state of mind? Or do I take the drink and have a clearer more focused mind? -Duke picks up the shot glass and puts it to his lips. He pulls back before pulling the trigger.- Williams: I haven't had a drink in two months. My eyes are clear and bright without that haze. I have also found a great preacher at the Church of Christ. But last night I actually laid on my hotel room bed and cried. -Duke gets a bit misty eyed as he tries to focus.- Williams: I can’t remember the last time I shed tears. I’m a grown man and grown men don’t cry. Is it weakness or recovery? I don’t know. -Duke again takes the shot in his hand.- Williams: Miyagawa will pay for the sins he has committed. I must stay focused. I must call my sponsor and stay clean. One day at a time a recovering alcoholic favorite saying. -Duke lays down a fifty dollar bill and walks away as the camera remains on the full glass of whiskey.- =w=
