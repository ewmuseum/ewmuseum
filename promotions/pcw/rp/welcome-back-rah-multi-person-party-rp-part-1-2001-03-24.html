Date: 3-24-2001 OOC:THIS PARTY IS ABOUT 2-3 RP'S SO IF YOU DIDN'T MAKE IT IN THIS TIME, YOUR SURE TO MAKE IT IN SOON ENOUGH, DON'T FRET. ENJOY!! ..::/::..When we last tuned into our whacked out superhero, he was finally found within a small town in Canada after becoming none other than a Royal Canadian Mountie. It was thought he would continue leading his life in that obscurity until 'The Krayzie One' Jeremy Howard and the beautiful Jessica found our hero and convinced him that he was needed within the ranks of PCW once again. After much deliberation and continued debate, our hero packed up his things and made his return with arguably one of the PCW's hottest couples....looking to save the dignity and prestige within the ranks of PCW. Little does our hero know what awaits him when he returns in a world that has changed since being absent for such a long time. As we now tune in, our hero, Jeremy, and the lovely Jessica are long gone from the one-horse town of Sova Ncotia, Canada and have landed up in the city, the bright lights and fast cars littering the streets as the night sky is its blanket. Their limo smoothly rips through the streets, the driver obviously having a beeline to a particular location as we go inside the lovely vehicle, Jessica and Jeremy all snuggled up closely on the plush red leather seat whereas our hero is busy playing with the interior limo privacy window which seperates the drivers part from the passenger part. Rent-A-Hero is dressed quite unusually, wearing a pair of overly baggy JnCO jeans, a muscle shirt, and a blue open up shirt with Dragonball Z character imprints all over it, replacing the full body spandex costumes of old as his only remnant of his old style is the Flash replica mask he has covering his face. He continues to play with the window. Jeremy looks over to our hero, bewildered by his actions as he asks..::/::.. Jeremy:'Having fun man?' Rent-A-Hero:'JEREMY, THIS APPARATUS IS INCREDIBLY WIZZER!! I JUST PRESS THIS BUTTON ONCE AND...' *presses the button and watches the window go down* 'And then AGAIN and it...' *he presses the button yet again and watches it go all the way back up.* 'I WANT ONE OF THESE FOR MY RENT-CAVE...when I get it off layaway of course.'..::/::..Jeremy looks over to Jessica, pulling down his dark shades just a little so he can look Jessica directly in her soft blue eyes, clearing his throat before asking..::/::.. Jeremy:'Was it REEALLY necessary we bring him back right away?' Jessica:'Jeremy, hush. You know good and well you like Rent-A-Hero, so don't go getting all upset, he's just having a little fun.'..::/::..Jeremy mumbles and grumbles as he folds his arms looking slightly peeved, Jessica resting her head on his shoulder as a slight smile takes over his face, knowing good and well he can't resist her charms..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Tell me Jeremy...where is it that we are headed my good friend? The suspense is eating me alive like the Suspense Eaters of Thetha 71 good friend. And why must I dress in this earthling style...' *shivers* 'Denim and cotton...EVILS I TELL YOU, EVILS!! I hate the loose fit and soft caress it has on my skin...' Jeremy:'It was either that or that corny Mountie outfit you had Rent, so don't start with me. Your the one who went and sold all your costumes on eBay for a bag of Peruvian Gummi Bears...' Jeremy:'SEX!! OH MY GOD RENT, THER'ES HOPE FOR YOU YET!!' Rent-A-Hero:'SEX?! What are you talking about, I was speaking of LASER TAG nimrod, duh. Jeremy, for saying you were a two time PCW World Champion, you don't act like it.' ..::/::..Jeremy continues his inaudible mumbling as the limo starts to come to a halt until finally stopping in place, the driver looking back and tipping his cap at Jeremy..::/::.. Jeremy:'Thanks bud, the money's been wired. All right everybody lets get out.' ..::/::..Jessica scoots over slowly as not to wrinkle her tiny pink dress and opens the right door, stepping out and waiting for Jeremy as he slides out shortly. She enwraps her arm in his and Rent-A-Hero shortly follows, striking an incredibly weird fighting stance as he bends his knees sharply and thrusts both hands out, looking about suspiciously as he quietly whispers to the couple..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Jeremy...I suggest you be careful, abrupt stops and dark nights mean only one thing....CYBER NINJAS!!' Jeremy: *sigh* 'Rent...will you come on inside already.' Rent-A-Hero:'Okay, but don't say I didn't warn ya. When your getting hacked up by a cyrebro-katana, don't come calling your hero to help you out.' ..::/::..Jeremy shakes his head shamefully as he walks in with Jessica inside what looks to be a club as blue neon lights shine above the entrance reading 'The Piledriver', loud music bumping from within as Rent-A-Hero shortly follows. Upon stepping through, the lights all turn out, shocking our hero as it is soon followed by..::/::.. SUPRIIIIIIIIIIISE!!!! ..::/::..The lighst come back on and standing on the club floor is a pleathora of PTC superstars, arms raised in the air and smiles on their faces, all being revealed as this being a suprise party set up by Jeremy. Their smiles turn to looks of confusion as our hero is atop PTC superstar Chris Matthews, thrashing away at him with girly slaps to his face as he tries to block them all. Jeremy immediately rushes over to our hero and pulls him off using all his strength, exclaiming..::/::.. Jeremy:'Rent...chill out!! He's just a guest!! A GUEST!! This is your suprise party man.'Chris Matthews:'Remind me to never try to hug someone in the dark.' *holding his face* Rent-A-Hero: *looks about* 'I'm, I'm quite sorry Jeremy...I was just shocked that's all.' *Jeremy lets him go as he looks about at the guests and the nicely decorated club* 'All this for me...thank you...thank you all. A hero is lucky....*sniff sniff*...VERY LUCKY to have friends like you. ' Juan Borro:'Yea yea Rent, we love you too...bring on the ladies!!!' ..::/::..PCW'SJuan Borro, dressed in slacks and a button up shirt and wearng numerous silver chains, rushes out of the crowd and straight to the side door of the lavish club. He lets out a loud whistle and shortly, a stampede of skimpily dressed females rushes into the club, Master P's 'OOoooooweee' taking over the sound system as the guests start to disperse and the party begins. Our hero can't help but smile, looking to Jeremy and saying..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'OH BUDDY....you know what this means right....MANLY HUG!!' Jeremy:'God help me now...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero tightly hugs his 'best friend' Jeremy, a few tears running down on his 'Disturbed' shirt as Jeremy stands their loosely, not even fighting it as he pats our heoron the back. Rent-A-Hero then quickly releases the hug, wiping away the tears and throwing Jeremy a thumbs up as he walks over to meet his guests. Jeremy lets out a loose sigh, taking off his shades and wiping them on his shirt as he says..::/::.. Jeremy:'He's like the retarded little brother I never had....' ..::/::..Putting his shades back on. Jeremy joins the party group as the club looks to be incredibly wild, no telling what all these PTCers could get up to, but we are surely about to find out..::/::.. At The Gaming Booth......::/::..At the gaming booth, PCW's Drake Maxwell and NTICW's militant extremist, Jordan Crazy are partaking in a game of DOA 3 for the X-Box in the well furnished gaming booth of the club. The X-Box sits on a see-through glass table with cushy rolling chairs for players to sit in as both Jordan and Drake tussle with the bulky X-Box controllers, the fight looking close between the two as the game can be seen on a wide screen flat TV mounted against the wall. Suddenly, Drake pulls out a devestating kick combination with his character, knocking out Jordan's character with ease. Jordan tosses his controller to the grudn and angrily exclaims at Drake..::/::.. Jordan Crazy:'YOUR CHARACTER ONLY ONE CAUSE HE WAS WHITE!! YOU THINK THAT X-BOX DON'T HAVE A RACE IDENTIFICATION THINGEE THAT MAKES SURE THE BLACK MAN LOSES EVEN THOUGH HE WAS HITTIN DEM' TIGHT COMBOS!! I'M ABOUT TO GET BLANK PANTHER IN HERE IF YOU DON'T GIVE ME A REMATCH, SALTINE CRACKER!!' Drake:'Ummm Jordan...your controller wasn't even hooked up...' *holds up the loose cord of his controller that was never plugged in* Jordan Crazy:'LOOK HERE SNOWFLAKE, DON'T BE TRYIN' ME SON OR MY FOOT IS GONNA HAVE A MEETING WITH YOUR PALE A**!!' ..::/::..Just as Jordan begins to calm-down, NTICW's Mark Makonie passes by waving none other than the rebel Confederate flag of the South and exclaiming whilest also pointing at his Confederate belt buckle..::/::.. Mark Makonie:'THE SOUTH SHALL RISE AGAIN!! THEY SHALL I TELL YA!!' Jordan Crazy: *slowly turns his head* 'Confederate belt buckle....Confederate Flag....Southern boy....that's an a** whoopin!' ..::/::..Within a matter of seconds, Jordan pounces on Mark with vicious intent and begins pounding away on him. the sound of hard punches heard from afar as Drake looks at the X-Box controller guiltily and says..::/::..Drake:'Mental note....never pick a white character and beat a militant black man...end note.' In The Champagne Room.... ..::/::..Things look to be getting extremely down and dirty in the 'Champagne Room' area of the club, the 'gentleman's' area of the club as a pair of scantily clad strippers with the bodies of Greek Venus's dance about together on a long metal pole impaled through a lighted stage as 'Shake Ya A**' by Mystikal plays in the background. PCW Tag Champion Juan Borro, Pharoah, PCW Extreme Champion Jason Core, and PCW Rising Star Champion Jay Sylo watch on seated in a circle around the stage as the girls tantilize them with their seductive dancing. Juan and Sylo toss insane amounts of dollars at the two females, smiling as they crawl and pick it up. Pharoah also tosses up some money of his own, and as the brunette stripper in a pink lace thong and top crawls to pick it up, she angrily holds it to the Egyptian PCWite and says..::/::.. Brunette Stripper:'What the he** is this?' *Holds up what looks to be a foreign piece of money.* Pharoah:'It's an Egyptian Rupie...don't you strippers have an exchange rate in this country?' ..::/::..The brunette stripper smiles at him playfully, nearing her lips towards his as Pharoah starts to get googily in the eyes, obviously turned on by her as he braces himself. Suddenly, she snatches Pharoah by his lion skin collar and angrily yells..::/::..Brunette Stripper:'NOW LOOK KING TUT, COUGH UP SOME GREEN MONEY OR YOUR GONNA GO FROM PHAROAH TO MUMMY AFTER I KICK YOUR A**!!'..::/::..Pharoah, too scared to even say a word, hands the stripper a wad of American money with a trembling hand. She stops to count it, holding it to the light to make sure it's legit, and smiles before resuming her seductive dancing for Pharoah and the crowd. Juan and Jay scream excitedly as they toss more and more money onstage, the exact opposite of Jason Core, as he turns away from the naughty dancing and covers his eyes with his PCW Extreme title, showing his obvious frigidness towards women as Juan turns from the pleasureable eye candy, tapping Jason and asking..::/::.. Sylo immediately covers J-Core, a referee rushing in from nowhere and making the count..::/::.. 1....2....3!! He watches on with a hint of envy as the two dance closely with each other, the love they have for each other obvious in their eyes as they mischievously trip other people up on the dance floor, sickening thuds heard every so often as the two of them laugh like high school sweethearts. Leaning against the bar table, our hero says aloud while still watching the couple in all their splendid happiness..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Say you were this spandex enwrapped superhero right...and you met this BEAUTIFUL red-head that made Wonder Woman look like a banshee....and you two got along quite well and everything....and when you started to REALLY have this feeling you never had about another girl before...she leaves you faster than The Flash runs....is it wrong to feel bad everyday of your life because deep inside your heroic heart you want her back?' ..::/::..The camera pans slowly to the left and sitting in the stool right next to him is none other than Tim Walden, looking incredibly pissed as he tosses back a glass of milk with authority as if it were gin before he exclaims at Rent-A-Hero..::/::.. Wait...Rent...isn't that Timo and Aimz of OSW over there?' ..::/::..Big Alan points to his right, Rent-A-Hero sharply turning his head as he spots OSW superstar Timo Bolomba with a neatly wrapped gift in hand and at his side...the only woman that made Rent-A-Hero have feelings of deep warmth inside...the only woman who ever succeeded in breaching the 'defenses' our hero...Amy 'Aimz' Campbell.
