Date: not entered [Scene opens in a Photographers studio. Flaymin, Jenna and Angel sit quietly in the waiting room anticipating their group photo. The waiting room walls are plastered with family portraits and the odd photo of little kiddies with no clothes… Flaymin looks at the photos and frowns] Flaymin: This guy sure does have a lot of pictures of children. Jenna: Yeah, and I still have a better body then all of them. Flaymin: They’re 12! Jenna: I got implants when I was 11. Flaymin: They probably sucked the silicone out of your head and put it in your rack. Jenna: What? [Angel interrupts the mini-argument by warmly clutching Jenna’s hand and smiling intently] Angel: I’m really looking forward to this family portrait. Jenna: We make a great couple, don’t we? Angel: Yep. Flaymin: You should both thank your lucky stars that I initiated this whole three-some operation.Without me, Angel would still be playing with El Diablo’s pin-dick. [Jenna kisses Flaymin on the cheek and thanks him quietly. The little alarm above the reception begins to ring and the number ‘33’ begins to flash. Flaymin looks at his ticket and gets up] Flaymin: Number 33.That’s us. Angel: I’m so nervous!! [All three walk into the photo studio where a short man with a massive afro is busy putting some film into the camera. The man turns around and grins instantly upon seeing who his next three customers are] Photographer: Oh my gawd!!!!Flaymin Aymin!! Flaymin: Hey dude, nice ‘fro. Photographer: Thanks, I did it just like yours! I can’t believe I’ll be taking your photo. Flaymin: Neither can I.Most of my photo’s usually involve me standing in front of measuring tape holding up some numbers. Photographer: This will be waaaaay better then all those mug shots! Angel: So how are we going to do this? Jenna: Naked I hope. Photographer: A naked family portrait? Flaymin: Yeah why not.A photo of myself naked with El Diablo’s wife will surely piss him off. Photographer: Well you’re in luck!I’m the finest photographer in the entire ‘Ho’s with no clothes’ magazine! Here take a look at this. [The Afro-endowed photographer hands Flaymin the latest copy of ‘Ho’s with no clothes’.Flaymin and Jenna flip through the magazine, nodding their heads in approval] Flaymin: There’s some nice stuff in here. Photographer: There sure is. First things first, what kind of background do you want? Ocean?Mountain?Farmyard animals?Rain forest?Jail Cell?Whore House? Angel: I think ‘The Rainforest’ backdrop would be romantic. Jenna: Yeah, we can hang off Flaymin’s vine! Photographer: So it’s settled! I’ll go get the wallpaper. [The photographer walks over to his cupboard and pulls out the Rainforest wallpaper. He hangs it up against the wall and then instructs Flaymin, Jenna and Angel to stand in front of it. The photographer then places his hand on his chin and thinks quietly to himself] Photographer: Hmmm.How are we going to do this now. Jenna: Naked. Photographer: Yes, but we’ll need some props. Gimme a sec, I think I have an idea.In the meantime, just get naked and stuff. [Flaymin, Jenna and Angel begin to strip while the photographer walks over to a nearby chest and begins to pull out some props. He returns a minute later with a toy lion, cuddly monkey and a woolly mammoth] Photographer: Here you go.I’ll get each of you to hold one of these toys. Flaymin: There aren’t any woolly mammoths in the rainforest. Jenna: I remember seeing woolly mammoths in my ‘Amazon Orgasm’ porno. Flaymin: That was a machine! Jenna: And I f*cked it anyway!! Photographer: Flaymin, ladies, we really need to rush things along.I have another appointment with Dennis Rodman and his freaky ho’s in 20 minutes. Aymin, just go and stand in the back.Jenna, get down on your knees in front of Flaymin…. Flaymin: Hey now were talking!!! Photographer: I want Jenna to hold the lion in one hand and stroke the monkey with the other hand. Flaymin: Can’t she stroke something else? Photographer: No.Angel, I need you to also get on your knees and pretend your giving it doggy-style to the woolly mammoth. Flaymin: How come they get to hold some animals and I get nothing? [The photographer sighs] Photographer: What kind of animal do you want? Flaymin: A snake. Or a crocodile. Photographer: I don’t have a crocodile, but I think I might have a snake. [The photographer walks over to his table and rolls up a piece of green cardboard before handing it to Flaymin] Flaymin: This is cardboard!?! Photographer: It’s a paper-tree snake! Flaymin: Really? Photographer: Yeah.These things run wild in the jungles of Antarctica. [Flaymin proudly holds up his ‘Paper-Tree Snake’ while the two women in front of him smile at the camera. The photographer picks up his camera and starts walking around in an attempt to find the best angle] Photographer: So how many copies do you want? Flaymin: One for us.And another one that I’m going to frame for El Diablo. Photographer: Ok, just remain reaaaally still.I’m about to shoot. Jenna: How many times have I heard that before! Photographer: Ok on the count of three, I want you to say ‘Jungle Fever!’ 1…. 2….. 3….. Flaymin. Jenna, Angel: JUNGLE FEVER!!!! [The photographer snaps the photo and then takes a second photo of the three in the same position] Flaymin: El Diablo is going to love this gift!! [Scene fades out to the rainforest wallpaper…….]
