In the End...No One Will be Laughing...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today These immature minded children…that’s what they are. All of them…looking for a cheap laugh, something to keep them from thinking about the pain, suffering, and triviality of their mundane lives. They knew nothing of true humor…they only knew of slapstick, sexual innuendo, and complete stupidity…but what more do you expect from Clyde? His whole existence is nothing but a joke…as well as his reign as world champion. The god will make it something to laugh at…after he takes what belongs to him on this Fusion. It’s a clean sweep, just like last time…Duke dies…Diablo dies…Demon dies…and he who stands left… Will be the DA GOD… Duke should retire now…that was the peak of his career right there. He will never get that close to being champion ever again. All that’s left for him is to change his name back to Dick Trickle and take a number in the social security line. Maybe take a bath…wash off that washed up stench. But you have to give it to Clyde…it took him long enough to finally gain a title that meant something more than Extreme. I guess he finally started paying attention in those PWA night classes and leaned how to do something that doesn’t require swinging a chair or something wrapped in barbwire. Takes a lot of skill to use a weapon…that’s why most of the greatest Extreme Champions were nothing but brain damaged loons. The god at least brought some class to the title when he held it before being screwed out of it as well. But then again…all Clyde did was poke his finger and get a quick count…not much skill there. Someone needs to hit the books… Speaking of hitting something…the god is ready to pummel on El Diablo yet again. Some people would think it would get boring after oh…a month of it up until Third Dawning. The string bean doesn’t get it…he can throw his body around all he likes, but all it will do is break it easier, and allow the god’s Celestial techniques to destroy your body quicker. But hey…it’s your mortal life…you do what you want with it. It will end soon enough anyway… Just like Clyde’s title reign… It will end at the end of the week…and there’s nothing that anyone can do to stop it. We are in Japan…the country that hates us…and we will destroy everyone and everything that stands in our path of retribution and of our mission. It’s comical if you think about it…the one thing that Clyde does to get himself pushed by is doing the dumbest thing he can think of. Can’t even come up with his own material…a USED concept with BORROWED characters from a TIRED series. Where’s the old Clyde we used to know…the one that struck fear into the hearts of his enemies with his insanely sadistic tales of madness and unreal violence? That Clyde is dead…like his title reign will be. What Clyde is now…is a clown, with scarred makeup. A puppet…dancing for you all letting comedy work his strings…that’s all he is now. He’s here to amuse you peons…to make you laugh and split your sides. He’s here to steal you away from the reality that hurts you…the reality that says you’re not good enough to be manager…the reality that says you’re not good enough for her…the reality that says you’re just a girl, so you can’t do anything except stay at home, get f*cked, and have babies. …The reality that you’re nothing but a loser sitting in front of a computer playing video games and wishing that your acne would clear up, or your vision would get better, or if you could drop say a hundred and thirty pounds… Clyde makes you forget…Clyde makes you laugh… Clyde makes me laugh…but for a different reason. He makes me laugh because I know he’s a hack, and I know he’s not worthy of being called champion. He makes me laugh because I know deep down inside that this is one pathetic being…not capable of being the man he used to be. I remember when he was talking with his father, Dark Gimmick, over dinner. He knew then that something was missing…and he still knows it now. There is most definitely something missing from his life…and that something is called life. Because when you compare the Clyde of old to the recent Clyde…DA MAN we know as Clyde is D E A D…what we have now…is a shattered, broken, useless FACE… And faces…are made…to be broken… The Clyde we knew…the Clyde that led the Dark Ages into chaotic rages of violence upon PCW…he doesn’t exist. In a way…he’s BANISHED…and I don’t think he’ll be back for a long time. Now he’s a dog for the masses…an entertainer. Make me laugh Clyde…you’re so damned good at it lately… Akiyama will not laugh at your foolishness…but he will laugh at your broken body…much like he laughed at your broken body last Red October…when he defeated you for the Extreme title. And he will laugh at you yet again…when he’s standing over you holding the World title in his massive hand…and you will ask yourself through pain why is the god laughing at me? It will be because Akiyama has been here for a year…and already he has maimed, slaughtered, and banished his way to the top of this business, and he has earned every bit of the gold that you’re holding right now. You…you were old when Akiyama first came to PCW. Now you’re rustic…an antique. Moths fly out of your hair when you come walking down to the ring. Dust rattles from your clothes as your body falls into the mat. Your bones creak like rotted wood… Even your Bethany is old…she’s not the hellfire she used to be. She has to take estrogen at this point to still squeak out at least a few “DIEDIEDIEDIEDIE”s like the good old days. It’s sad…relics that don’t know when to…………………………………….d i e . It’s because you’re a joke… You are made for laughing at … And while the laughing may be rolling through the air right now, while you’re shining up that title and spoofing your way into humanity’s hearts…you DO know humanity, right? They’re supposed to be the guys people like Akiyama and you are set out to destroy…well, people like you USED to be when you still had the killing fire in you. While the people may be laughing now… There will be no laughter when their clown is stopped dead in his tracks, brought face to face with his Absolute…Destiny…Apocalypse. Yeah…So says the Divine Prophetess. =©= “Where is my father?” i stood in front of the desk glaring down at the officer in that Tokyo police precinct. Behind me was my mother, Glacia, an older, but almost similar blue haired Japanese woman a foot shower than me. Behind the desk, an inept pudgy cop stared up at her, not knowing what was going on. In a way, his ineptness reminded me of Duke. “Um…who?” he said scratching his head. The senility of humanity, despite age difference, was a bother. “Where is the Neo Void God?” I rephrased the question. That got his puny little brain running. “Akiyama is being detained at the moment for questioning.” He told her, his focus becoming clear. Glacia huffed, as she looked away, her hands sliding down from her chest to her hips. “Heh, good luck.” She had a point. I bet I could imagine the trouble they were having, talking with he who does not speak to mortals. =©= The same two guards who questioned Sonata and Matrix were sitting in front of Akiyama while Ushio Kuonji paced in the dark room. “So, Akiyama…what is your ties to the cult CODE and yourself?” Silence… “What are your intentions on being in Japan? Is it true that you are out to destroy the country from within?” Akiyama stared ahead blankly with his arms folded, his dark eyes piercing holes into the two guards in front of him. “Are you responsible for the terrorist actions being led by CODE in YOUR name?” One of the guards raised his hand as the god refused to answer. Ushio glared at him. “What?” “Um…” he began, nervousness in his voice. “Can I go now?” The other man next to him nodded, “Yeah…cause he’s like staring at us…and I think if he does it long enough…our heads…will explode.” His partner looked at him with fear in his eyes. “You think he can do that?” “Dude…he IS the Neo Void God! He banishes people like US to the VOID!” Ushio stared blankly at the two combat-geared officers like they were crazy. “Are you serious?” When they both nodded, he sighed as he rubbed the back of his head. The help these days…no dedication… “Fine. Go…watch some TV or something. Get a couple of doughnuts.” They fled from the room, the gaze of the god following them the entire way with a small smirk on his face. As the door closed behind them, he felt Ushio sit down across from, his legs propping up on the table between the mortal and the god. “Now that we’re alone…” he said, putting his hands behind his head. “We’re going to sit here until we get some answers out of you.” =©= A black SUV rode through the streets of Tokyo. The windows were tinted, but inside, familiar faces sat with pleased looks on their faces. “I’m not pleased.” Reality muttered. Well…most of them were pleased. “I thought you were supposed to be the best marksman that JSF had. And you missed the shot!” Shift shrugged his shoulders as Reality glared back at him. “I had the shot! They just had…holograms.” “Jackass…you missed.” The Executive muttered. Illusion grinned as he turned the steering wheel, to send the vehicle around the tight corner. “So much for completing your part of the mission eh?” Reality wasn’t in the mood for his jest. “I’m not in the mood for your jest, Illusion!” Spice yawned, stretching her arms out as far as the roof of the car would allow her. “Who really cares at this point, boys? WE succeeded where you failed. So everyone’s happy!” Angelica sighed, her white sunglasses being pressed up onto her face. “I’m not. We’re far from over on this one. Akiyama and Cyclonica still need to be dealt with, and now JSF has him in custody, and Cyclonica is still walking around Scott focking free. That’s not good for any of us.” “I’m happy,” Shift grinned as he sat between the two beautiful women. Spice and Angelica turned towards him slowly, giving him a disinterested look. Shift’s grin slowly faded as he slowly slumped down into his seat. Behind him, the booming voice of Knight laughed at him. “Hold thy tongue, squire!” he chuckled. “These maidens may looketh fair, but they art lethal machines!” Reality looked back at the tall man all the way in the back seat. “How are our guests doing?” Knight smiled, “They art having the time of their lives! Aren’t thou?” Panning down, the mortified looks of Matrix and Sonata Kojima were low and droll. Knight patted them both on their backs with his large hands, shoving both of them out from their seats a bit. Sonata nodded slowly, “Yeah…fun.” “A riot…ri…..ot.” Matrix added in, causing Knight to bellow out in laughter again. So much for getting away… =©= Glacia and I walked through the precinct with about five officers following us. They knew who I was…and they knew what I was capable of. They led me to the questioning room, where after opening the door I found a staring contest between Ushio Kuonji and Akiyama. Looks like the questioning was going off without a hitch. “It’s time to go.” I said, stepping into the room. Ushio looked up at me questioningly. “What do you mean? We’re detaining him!” Glacia shook her head. “Not anymore. We’ve posted bail.” “How?” he asked. “The bail was enormous!” My hand went up as I counted off the reasons how we posted bail. “One: PCW pays us nicely. Two: We don’t spend 85% of the money anyway. What do we have to buy? Not a whole frickin lot. Three: Have you forgotten the value of the yen compared to the American dollar these days?” As Ushio pondered over this, Akiyama stood up and ruffled the hair atop of my head, like a father would a good daughter. Then Ushio jumped to his feet quickly, pulling a document from his pocket. “Here. Akiyama must stand trial in three days for his crimes.” I took the paper and looked it over, finding it legit. “Most likely the court will convict him…but that’s my opinion.” I shrugged my shoulders before handing the paper to my mother. “Yeah…sure…whatever. Thank you for your time, commander. See you soon.” We left him standing there like a fool…not the average type fool, but the Clyde “dance, monkey, dance” type of fool. As we were about to exit the precinct, we stopped when we heard the laughter of the officer at the front desk. I turned to him slowly, my eyes cutting slices through him. “What are you watching?” I asked sharply. He looked up from the small TV under the counter with tears in his eyes. “Some American program they dubbed in Japanese!” he said, holding back tears. “They’re totally ripping apart Metal Gear! WAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” Just then, the god knocked the cop from his chair quickly with a left cross. His rotund body hit the floor under the desk…but the act was not gone unseen by his fellow cops. I sighed, a cool vapor trailing from my blue lips as I shook my head. “I respect what you did, father…because I would have done the same thing…but NOW is NOT the TIME!” The three of us ran off as a swarm of cops came stampeding after us. But we made our point…and I’m sure everyone will know this on Fusion. No one will be laughing in the end…
