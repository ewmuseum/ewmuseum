Boy of Truth

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-29-02 In heaven above or hell below, no one knew the way he felt. If a man tried to invision the pains and woes he has endured, their spirit would be broken and they would simply give up hope. But it is his destiny. He is a storm. Going from place to place screaming for something more in this life. Destruction, chaos, suffering follow him like close friends. Like the mighty storms of nature, he too much walk alone in this life. But it wasn't always like this. In a former life long ago, or maybe it was yesterday, he wasn't alone. He knew real emotion and feelings. But the memory of it all seemed so far that he couldn't grasp it anymore. The only way he even knew it existed was the sting in his heart where he had opened up for others. He was now cold and bitter. Getting worst with each moment. But there was still a small place in the cruel world were he felt emotion. It was clear in the form of a small boy. Alex, the ten year old he watched over for the moment. The young lad was asleep in his bed, void to the harshness of the world. Meanwhile a single light in the apartment remains on, even at such a late hour in the night. There in the kitchen Chris Storm stands. A man who has just had his heart ripped out of his chest. The women he loved. The women he gave up everything for, left him without even saying good bye. Was it for good? He didn't know. Right now he didn't know anything. He was a child just like Alex. Stupid in the ways for the real world and the cruelness it carries with it. But Chris was going to rid himself of the pain. He was going to force himself to forget it and pretend it never happened and that everything was ok. So he stands in the kitchen. He is almost like a statue. Standing motionless, just staring at the drawers on the wall. His breath almost enaudible. His eyes never moving from their target, a wooden drawer. Silently in the night Chris starts to walk toward his target. He stands in front of the drawer and opens it. Moving the junk out of the way he comes to what he was searching for. A small black flask. A silver dragon is wrapped around the flask. Chris pulls it from the drawer and holds it up in the light. 'Charm' shines in the light in silver letters on the bottom on the flask. Chris walks over to the kitchen table and sits down at the head of the table. He looks up at a clock on the wall. 6:30 A.M. The sun was starting to shine through the darkness outside. But inside it was still so dark and cold. Not even the rays of the sun could change it. With a single motion of his hand Chris removed the cap off the flask. Already the potent smell of the alcohol could be smelt. Without a second though, Chris puts the lid to his mouth and tilts his head back. He gulps down the alcohol. After a few seconds and slams the flask back down on the table and lets his head fall onto his arms. He doesn't move. He just remains there, almost as if he fell asleep. Yet his eyes are wide open. 'Love...what a joke. What good is it to promise a women your heart if she can just leave with it in her hand the next day? Why put yourself out there like that. Defenseless and weak. So what's the point of it all? Why do humans even bother to care for one another? In the end we all face the judge alone. So why did I let myself care?' the words are scratchy and low. Almost as if it's painful just for him to even speak. Chris brings the flask back to his mouth and drinks more of the foul liquid. Finishing the rest of the alcohol off, he lets his head fall back into his arms. Only this time he lays his head down sideways. Chris closes his eyes and tries to let sleep overcome him. Just when the peaceful slumber falls on him the sound of little feet scrapping against the tile floor snatches him from the rest. Chris slowly opens one eye. Then the other. Alex walks over toward the table. He's dressed in his pj's. The little ten year old pulls out a chair near Chris and flops down in it. Chris just drags his head over to look at him. Chris hides the flask so Alex doesn't see it. 'Chris, do you miss Tessa?' the little one ask. Chris lifts his head and looks at him. Wondering why such a kid would ask a question like that. Maybe he was smarter then Chris gave him credit for. 'Why should I miss her? I won't miss cooking for her. Or washing her clothes. I'm not going to miss having to take care of her when she's sick. I mean I couldn't go out whenever I wanted. I mean she left me. I have nothing to regret.' Chris tells the child. He remembers all the times he cooked for her, washing her clothes, and stood by her side whenever she was sick. He tries to fight it but he can't help but smile. Chris hides the smile with his arm and acts like he is yawning. He gives Chris a look that shows he knows Chris was smiling. Alex ask him another question. 'Do you think you and Tessa will get back together?' Chris stops and wonders about that one for a second. I mean would Tessa leave Alex with Chris if she wasn't coming back? She might just come back and get him later. She hadn't even moved at her stuff yet. But then again, Tessa might just be so mad she might not ever come back. Her mother already doesn't like Chris. Him being a former porn star and telling her off. She might try and talk Tessa into breaking up with Chris for good and maybe even dating Jackson Rust. Chris just shruged it off. 'I don't know Alex. We'll have to wait and see what happens.' Thinking the questioning is over Chris gets up to hide the flask back in the drawer. 'If Tessa wanted to come back would you welcome her back?' Chris stops dead in his tracks. That had never crossed his mind. 'I don't know...' he said in a low voice. It almost sounded like Chris was shamed of himself. 'if Tessa wants to come back, she'll have to prove to me that she really wants back. If she can do that. I'll let her come back.' he states. Chris' voice picks up. He slides the flask into the drawer and turns back to Alex. 'Now come on, it's early in the morning. Let's get you some breakfast.' He walks over to the refridgerator and opens it up. He looks around for something to eat. He sticks his head out of the fridge and looks at Alex sitting at the table still. 'You want eggs and grits? Or toast and bacon?' Chris ask him. Alex just turns around in the chair and looks at Chris. 'Ice cream and chocolate.' somes the little ones reply. Chris stops looking around in the fridge and looks at him with a confused look. 'Ice cream and chocolate?' Alex just smiled and shaking his head. Chris looks back in the fridge. He grabs some eggs and milk. 'How about I fix you french toast and eggs and this afternoon we can have some ice cream and chocolate?' Chris ask. Alex just smiles brightly and nods in agreement. Chris places the food on the counter and grabs everything else he needs. He starts the stove and starts cooking everything. Alex hops out of the chair and walks over to Chris. He tugs on his shirt. Chris looks down at him. 'Yes?' 'Are you going to hit Tessa on Tuesday?' he simply ask. Chris is taken back by his question. He relaxes as he thinks about it and smiles to Alex. He puts his hand on his head and rubs his hair. 'Don't worry Alex. I won't hit Tessa. Not because she is a women. I have no problem with hitting a women. But I refuse to hit her because I just don't think I would be able to bring myself to harming her. We maybe fighting right now but I still have feelings for her. So don't worry, I won't hit her.' Chris gives Alex a warm smile to help make him feel more comfortable about it. However Alex's expression on his face doesn't change. 'Is she going to hit you?' he ask. Chris stops what he is doing. His mouth hangs open. 'I...' his voice is shaking. 'I don't know. I really don't know Alex.' is all he can say. He is just at too much of a lost of words. Chris just turns back to the stove and continues to fix breakfast for them. Alex slowly walks back to the table. The room is silent.
