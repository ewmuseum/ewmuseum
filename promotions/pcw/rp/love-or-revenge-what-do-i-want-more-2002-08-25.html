Love Or Revenge....What Do I Want More?

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-2002 After that whole Phantom of the Gothra ordeal....Brandi invited you over to her place to spend a little time together before the PPV.... To be perfectly honest, Brandi makes you feel so good about who you are, she fills a void within you.... She likes you for your dumb catchphrases... Your silly clothes.... Your silly interests... All the things that make you, YOU...and that truly is a rare quality lost among women these days who want to 'shape and mold' men into what they want them to be... Brandi doesn't want that from you.... She just wants you.... And it's amazing that you two have shared such a deep relationship, possibly as deep and loving as the one you shared with Aimz....and not a minute of it was swept up in sex. The most you and Brandi have shared is tight cuddles to each other and deep kisses....and through that....both you and ger stay fulfilled with this relationship....for the mental nourishment you both receive is more fulfilling and wonderful then any amount of love making..... It couldn't even begin to match it.... You love this feeling with her, but then you realize, you may never feel it again.... Not after Third Dawning....Darkstar and Snow could care less for how you feel about her, how she feels about you, or how 'warm and gushy' you feel inside whenever you share your world with her.... They want your blood splattered in as many places as possible and your bones scattered int he wind...If it were up to them, you would NEVER see Brandi again and your life would be put to a crashing halt... All this runs through your mind as you lacksidasically lay upon her plush pink couch, peeling back a bandage tightly clinging to your forehead....your face wincing slightly as you slowly peel back the band-aid....And you know that slow peeling feeling of a band-aid all too well.... The prickly, sharp pain that just keeps going and going until you finally can rip the tip away from your skin.... That's the kind of pain Snow and Darkstar specialize in....no way will they make it quick for you....but your promise not to make it quick for them either.... Suddenly, your thoughts of pain and carnage are thrown off slightly as you look to the hallway of Brandi's cozy apartment....the Queen of PCW standing in the archway of the hall wearing a provocative angel outfit, the white lace see through from the tiny skirt that started at the mid portion of her tight thighs.... To the mid section of her soft stomach.... She throws you that cute smile you've come to enjoy about her, playfully waltzing over towards you as you can't help but reply to the beautiful outfit and it's owner, short of breath as you say... 'Holy Shizznittlebam...' And she just said she was going to the bathroom....that little witch.... Not that your mad or anything.... You sit up and slide over on the couch, taking her in your strong arms as she leans back against you, her head firmly rested on your chest as she locks hands with you, wrapping your arms around her waist as she finds comfort within what you are....And you return the feeling wholeheartedly as you both just sit together, content in delirious pleasure with each other as Brandi pulls your hand up to her soft lips and kisses it, savoring the moment as much as you do as you run your fingers through her soft brown hair, making her giggle just a little at your touch... 'I don't want anything to happen to you....I don't want to lose moments like this Rent...' What could you say to her? I mean, in under 24 hours you would be in what people deemed to be the most brutal match in PCW history....could you REALLY guabeinrantee her the privilege of being well and intact to have moments like this with her again? You did the only thing you could do...tell her the truth... 'I can't promise you that Brandi....you don't know how badly I wanted to be like this with you...but two people may want to see it differently...' That statement obviously did not please her....you could feel her gripping your hands tighter, her care for you taking a tighter hold on you as she she turned over, now laying on you stomach to stomach as she looked you eye to eye....her coffee brown eyes to your sea blue eyes as she pleaded with you, requested with loving care, the kind of plea a Christian mother would make to her out of control son...'I don't want you to wrestle that match....I want you to bow out of it...please.' This plea came as a shock to you....You were torn at that very moment.... For TWO months, all you've been obsessed with is your revenge....the blood of the ones who made you this 'villain' you are now.... That match had it all...
