PCW...Should be fun!

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-9-03 [Adam is seen lying down, stretched out, under thestars. A dying fire lights up about half of his face.He looks around and smirks as his gaze falls upon AJand Hank. Both are sound asleep with Hank standingover AJ as if he is guarding him. Adam returns hisgaze up to the stars once more.] Stryker: That’s amazing…simply awe inspiring… [His mind begins to wander, he sees memories both goodand bad materialize in front of him. An older lookingman with white hair wearing a black suit and a pair ofwire framed glasses appears.] Old Man: Master Dyas I’m to be the one to tellyou this but while you were gone…your parents werekilled in an accident. Stryker: No! You’re lying! A-aren’t you?Please? [A tear runs down Adam’s cheek as the vision slowlydissipates and turns into another. This time its Adamsitting on a black leather couch with empty beer andliquor bottles all around him. Tears stream down hisface as blood begins to gush from his forearms. Abloody knife falls from his hand as he falls back ontothe couch. They hit at the same time as Adam stares upat the ceiling expecting death to take him at anymoment.] Old Man: Master Dyas! No! [The old man rushes to Adam’s side. The visiondisappears and transforms into one where Adam is lyingunconscious in a hospital bed. The old man is sittingat the bedside talking with a the doctor.] Doctor: You are lucky you found him when youdid Mr. Barne, with the amount of alcohol in hissystem coupled with the loss of blood…he surely wouldhave been dead in another few minutes. Barne: Thank God he is alive. Had I lost Adam,I don’t know what I would have done, he’s the onlyfamily I’ve got. Stryker: I…I am? [Barne quickly turns and notices Adam is awake. As hegoes to hug him the vision shifts to a dark, rainynight. A voice can be heard speaking garbled inaudiblegibberish. Adam is sitting in a black suit and looksas though he is completely out of it. He looks up at apriest giving a eulogy. Slowly the priest’s voicebecomes clearer and audible.] Priest: Nathaniel Barne was more than a man. Hewas a teacher, mentor, friend and father figure tomany in this community. His loss is a loss that wewill all feel for many more years to come… [The voice slowly becomes garbled and inaudible. Thatwas the last day Adam had set foot in a church. Hesnaps back into reality, opening his eyes as sweatbegins to bead on his forehead. The light of the firehas died down a bit.] Stryker: What the hell was that? Shit…fire isdying… [Adam gets up and grabs a few pieces of wood from apile and drops them into the fire. They quickly catchand the fire comes roaring back to life. Adam laysback down and looks back up to the sky. Once again, heslips into a dream state. A knock is heard. A door isheard opening as a small voice calls out.] Voice: Hel-hello? Mr. Stryker? The AWR sent meover. My name is Jacobs Winters… Stryker: In here… [A small man with black hair and brown eyes steps intoview. He’s wearing black jeans with a gray polo shirtwith the AWR logo over his heart. The notebook in hishand shakes as he approaches Adam, who smirks at thesmall man.] Stryker: Don’t worry, I don’t bite… [Jacob suddenly vanishes and is replaced by the AWRWorld Title belt. Adam’s hands materialize around itas it’s thrust up into the air. But is quicklyreplaced with a note simply stating, ‘The AWR hasclosed, your contract is now null and void.’ Adam’shands rip the note apart as it disappears.] Stryker: God damnit…I had just made a name formyself… [The face of a real sleaze ball comes into view. Hishair, clearly thinning, is slicked back. He wears acheap looking suit with the first couple of buttons ofhis undershirt undone. He paces around a crampedlocker room where Adam is sitting in a chair.] Sleaze: Goddamit Adam. You were supposed tolose tonight. Stryker: Whatever Biff, I don’t what it was Iwas supposed to do, the only thing that matters iswhat I did and that was win because I’m a winner notsome idiot who listens to you because you sign the paychecks. Biff: You’re old news Adam. Two knee surgerieslater and no one wants you. I’M the ONLY one who willeven bother with you. Consider yourself lucky that I’mwilling to even take up has been talent such asyourself. [Adam rises to his feet and towers over the sleazeball Biff. He pushes Biff into the corner.] Stryker: AND? YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT THIS TWOBIT PIECE OF SH!T OPERATION? I’M OUT OF HERE! [As Adam storms through the door it all disappears.Adam sits back up quickly and looks around. Sweat runsdown his forehead. He reaches up and wipes it from hisface. He takes in a couple of deep breaths.] Stryker: What the hell is going on? This isseriously freaking me out. Too much drinking Isuppose… [He slowly lays back down on the ground still lookingaround. He closes his eyes once again as the image ofCallisto Bane appears.] Bane: We’d certainly love to have you hereAdam, I think you’re someone who could really help theDWO regain the prominence it once held. Stryker: I’ll do my best. Bane: That’s all anyone can ask for, let be thefirst to welcome you to the DWO. Stryker: Thank you very much Mrs. Bane. [Adam stands to shake her hand when things suddenlyspeed up. DWO wrestlers past and present flash up fromArmandus and Jamie Von Eriche to Gina Muniz andJaslene Myst. AJ Trinidad’s face slowly fades in, hismouth is moving but no words are heard just yet. Asmore of AJ becomes visible words can finally be madeout. The scene fades in around him, it’s a physicaltherapy room. Adam’s working on his shoulder as AJstands next to him.] Trinidad: I’m sure everything will work out inthe end. I just couldn’t take anymore of it. I wasgoing insane without you there. [Adam keeps doing his exercises, not really paying anyattention to AJ.] Trinidad: Oh come on man! Don’t give me thissh!t, I don’t need it from you too. It’s bad enoughpeople I don’t even know are giving me sh!t about it,I don’t need my friends doing it too. [Adam stops for a moment and thinks. He slowlyreleases the bar allowing the weight to drop back downinto position. Adam stands and faces AJ. Staringintently into AJ’s eyes Adam cracks a smile and slapsAJ in the shoulder.] Stryker: Come on man, I was just playing withyou is all. I don’t blame you for leaving. You weren’tenjoying yourself and like we had said many times,when the fun stopped we should leave. I’m going tostick around a bit longer though at least until I getsome real DWO gold under my belt. Trinidad: Following in my footsteps eh? Stryker: Always it seems… [The two men embrace as everything falls intoblackness and then there is nothing. A loud roarbreaks the eerie silence as Adam and AJ are seenstanding next to each other. Adam is in his in ringattire and AJ in a hooded sweatshirt. Ropes begin tomaterialize around them as a gold belt hangs fromAdam’s right hand. AJ takes Adam’s hand and throws itup into the air.] Mike: Adam Stryker has finally reached thepinnacle of the DWO! HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMP! YEAH! [It’s quickly diminished as Adam sits behind a desk.He’s making the motions of writing a letter but for amoment nothing is there. Jaslene comes bouncing intothe room as a desk appears in front of Adam, alongwith a piece of paper. He leans back as Jaslene hopsup onto the desk and snatches the paper from thedesk.] Jaslene: Decided to go for ithuh? Stryker: Yeah well it was my goal all along.The DWO was merely a stepping stone. Jaslene: PCW…should befun! Stryker: Yeah in the end I guess AJ was right,no matter how much I wanted to deny it…he was dead on… Jaslene: About what? Stryker: That the DWO was holding me back. ThatI was too good for the DWO and there was nothing worthsticking around for… [He goes to stand but is suddenly enveloped bydarkness. And that’s it, no more visions appear.Until…] Trinidad: ADAM! COME ON! GET UP! WE HAVE TO GETGOING! [Adam slowly opens his eyes, it’s light out. Hesits up and looks around. Groggily he gets to his feetand stumbles forward.] Stryker: Huh? Wha? Why? What time is it? Trinidad: It’s mid afternoon, we have to getback, the girls are going to be wondering where wehave been. [Adam picks up a water bottle off of the ground andtakes a drink. He shakes his head and looks around asAJ packs up the Hummer.] Stryker: Hey AJ, have you ever had one of thosenights that you could have sworn was real but thenturned out to be nothing more than a chain of really,really f*cked up dreams? Trinidad: Yeah, happens all the time, come on,come on…we need to get going… Stryker: Keep your pants on… [The scene fades out as Adam takes his place in thefront seat of the Hummer.]
