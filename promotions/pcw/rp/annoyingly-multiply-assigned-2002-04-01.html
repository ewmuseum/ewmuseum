Annoyingly Multiply Assigned

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st April We stride forth from the abyss. We step forward onto the platform of life. We hire a narrator who didn’t previously work for Vampir Nosferatu. We are in a darkened room amongst several stuffed animals. Not the toy variety but the professional hunter variety. You know where a hick dresses up in camouflage and a mask with deer scents all over his body and ties himself fifteen foot up in a tree on a platform with a bow and arrow with a laser sight which has a range of three miles. You know the competitive ones rather than those who run out with a shotgun. Anyway we are amongst the stuffed dead as we spot Miyagawa... pose with a large piece of wood strapped to his back pretending to mix in with his motley... and probably rotting... crew. Miyagawa: I’m a wanted man. I’m a living trophy these days. Everyone wants a piece of me. Boda wants to kill me. Bobby Stormbringer just wants to beat me and Chris Chase wants to take me out for dinner. I’m like a deer, a prized stag you could say. He steps off the wooden panel that was on his back and steps forward wandering amongst the stuffed whole deers and bears. Miyagawa: I would like to thank the Natural History Museum of Anaheim for allowing me to borrow some of their exhibits. He wanders past a stuffed Mickey Mouse and Donald Duck. Miyagawa: Obviously these two weren’t employees of the month. The Disney Mafia is not to be messed with I’m afraid. Nor like me, because I’ll get Goofy on your ass. **Pauses for a second** Ok maybe it’s Chris Chase who wants to get Goofy on people’s asses... then again he probably wants to get on Goofy’s ass too... and Mickey... and Donald... not Minnie Mouse or Jessica Rabbit though, they’re members of the opposite sex and dear old Chase doesn’t work well with opposites. He picks up a small stuffed gopher from the floor and holds it up to high level before glancing over to the camera. Miyagawa: Oh Chase must have some memories of gophers, tubes and lubricants. Freddie Star ate my hamster, did I ever tell you that? He puts the gopher back down on the floor and moves on amongst the maze of dead. He stops by a large stuffed elephant and looks at its incredible bulk. Miyagawa: Nah, still not as large as Boda’s ego. Thereto I propose a solution. Boda is hyped up and overjoyed that he’s Primetime Championship Wrestling Champion... so the obvious solution is to strip him of that honour... let’s face it, he doesn’t deserve it. While I’ve been working my way through PCW in the last eighteen months Boda has been doing the same just in a very different manner... I worked through hard work, sweat, blood and tears. Boda however just sells his soul to the highest bidder... Media.... the Red Army... I’m surprised he didn’t find some reason to join the Dark Ages while they were still about. He strolls throw and suddenly he hears a ringing noise. He reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out a small mobile phone. He lifts it up to his ear... and unfortunately you only hear one side of the conversation. Miyagawa: Hi JJ, what do you want? What do you mean they said that Chris Chase wasn’t Gay? While everyone else is a Homosapien, he’s a Homosexual. Of course he’s gay, don’t be stupid.. **pauses for a few seconds, obviously JJ must be talking on the other end of the line** What do you mean Natalia’s sister is in town? Natalia doesn’t have a sister. Woah... slow down there... no you shouldn’t take her out for a date. **pauses once again for a second** What do you mean why not, because if it gets seriously and you get married then we would be brothers through some sick and twisted manner and I don’t want to damage my DNA by being close to yours in some bizarre family tree. Gah, shush... I’ll meet you at the hospital later. **pauses yet again** No, don’t wear a tux you daft plonker. **again once more** Because dinner wear is not a good thing for a hospital. And I mean it. **again yet again** Stop while your behind. I’m going to hang up now. Miyagawa puts the phone back in the jacket pocket from where it came and continues to walk... but this time reaches a... door. He opens the door and steps through to a corridor that is brightly lit. Two guys in overalls are standing there. Miyagawa: Thanks guys for letting me use the storeroom. Guy: No problem mister, did you bring the tickets? Miyagawa: The tickets when my team on Friday night defeats Boda’s team quite, quite badly? Yes of course I brought the tickets. What’s the magic word? Guy: Um... please? Miyagawa: I was going for Abra Cadabra, but that will do as well I suppose. Miyagawa fishes a couple of tickets out of the back pocket of his trousers and hands one to each of the guys in the overalls, before turning and walking off down the corridor away from the camera. Miyagawa: Now where did I park that bloody car...
