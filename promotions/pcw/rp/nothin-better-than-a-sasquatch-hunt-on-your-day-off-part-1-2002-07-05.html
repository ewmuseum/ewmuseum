Nothin' Better Than A Sasquatch Hunt On Your Day Off (Part 1)

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.7.02 The scene opens in the Hampton mansion that PCW star Duzza calls home. It is a four story, white building, with a long driveway leading from the Electric gates at the beginning of the driveway. Duzza’s estate is fit for a king. It has one full sized basketball court, which has a picture of Duzza in the centre circle airbrushed on, and each back board is made of a see through plastic and has “Duzza” written on in black graffiti style writing. The mansion overlooks the sea and there is a wall that you could jump off and land in the sea. Well actually you’d just land on some big ass rocks that lie right underneath the sea level. Duzza found out the rocks were there the hard way. The scene moves into the dining room area of the mansion. The room is very large, with framed pictures of famous black people along the walls. There is a large glass table that is in the middle of the room with chairs next to it. Duzza and his son Jimmy are sitting at the table. It is still early morning so neither of them are properly dressed, Jimmy is just wearing some Pokemon pajama’s and Duzza is wearing a old tight wife beater that exposes his muscular chest at crucifix tattoo on his shoulder and some baggy shorts. His hair is set back in long cornrows. He is sitting at the table, looking relaxed reading the morning paper, which is kinda shocking, considering most people figured Duzza couldn’t read anyway, but he’s not as dumb as he looks. He is sipping on some orange juice, reading the sport sections, when he comes across a advert that reads “Sasquatch! Myth of Monster? Capture real sasquatch and get million dollar prize” Now, Duzza loves money and for some unknown reason has a strange fascination for Bigfoot ever since he saw the famous footage of the bigfoot walking across the dry creek bed when he was younger. And once he read the advert he realized that this was the perfect chance to finally lay his eyes on the monster. As he isn’t booked for any matches in PCW till tuesday, he quickly picks up the phone and phones one of his closest friends, Pookie Duzza: “Pookie!! How are ya?” Pookie: “I’m good Duzz, ‘sup wit’ you?” Duzza: “Dawg, I just found something that will make us rich dawg!” Pookie: “Err Duzz… We’re both already rich, remember?” Duzza: “Oooh yeah, I broke my body in half wrestling and thuggin and you won the lottery didn’t ya…” Pookie: “Yeah… but what’s your plan?” Duzza: “Well, dawg this may sound strange but… we’re gonna go sasquatch hunting” Pookie: “Sasquatch?? What the fuck is a sasquatch” Duzza: “Man, don’t act dumb, y’know…bigfoot?” Pookie: “Nah… still ain’t following you” Duzza: “There was one in ‘Harry and the Henderson’s’…” Pookie: “Oooooh, dawg, now I understand… but bro, where we gonna find a sasquatch? And I thought it was just a TV show, ain’t real player” Duzza: “They are real now dawg, c’mon, are you in or are you out?” Pookie: “Errr… will we actually have to go in the woods?” Duzza: “What type of dumb ass question is that man? We ain’t gonna find a sasquatch in the hood are we?” Pookie: “Well, there’s always hope” Duzza: “Your actually dumber than you look. Why you scared of the woods?” Pookie: “It’s just… aww never mind, when do we leave?” Duzza: “Ok…well we gotta go to the airport, then we gotta get some tickets then we’ll get some guns and stuff and we’ll be off” Pookie: “I got plenty of guns here” Duzza: “Your not seriously gonna take a gun on a plane are you? You’re a fool Pookie, so just meet me at the airport in a hours time ok?” Pookie: “Aight dawg…aight” 8 hours later, Duzza and Pookie are in California, the place where there are more people of mixed race than there are black people, the place where more hip hop comes from than anywhere else… not including the east coast and most importantly the place where there are more bigfoot sightings than anywhere else on the planet. Walking through the airport you can see the confidence on the face of Duzza and the fear on the face of Pookie. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy camouflage pants and a green wife beater. He also has a camouflaged bandanna covering his forehead and some big black boots. Pookie meanwhile is dressed from head to toe in brown, and has brown face paint on covering his pecan brown skin. Everyone is looking at him as they walk through the airport to the car rental desk. At the car rental desk, sits a middle aged white woman, wearing a funny blue dress and a blue hat. She looks very bored, probably wondering what mistakes she had made while making her important life decisions to end up working at the car rental desk. Duzza walks up to her and starts to speak Duzza: “Hey yo, yo I was wondering if I could rent a car, hopefully a jeep, 4x4 type of thing” Lady: “Of course, can I enquire as to why you want a jeep sir?” Duzza: “Aww you know, the usual. Bigfoot hunt” Lady: “Not again….fine… ok, I shall be right back” As the lady walks away Duzza turns his attention to Pookie Duzza: “Why on earth are you wearing all this brown dawg?” Pookie: “Simple, to blend in with the trees” Duzza:” Ain’t you ever heard of leaves? Because you know the leaves are green don’t cha? Wearing brown won’t help you unless you dive against a tree trunk” Pookie: “Aww shiznit”The scene re-opens with a black, small jeep speeding down a small road, surrounded by woodland. There is nothing but trees for miles around and no cars in sight. Duzza is sitting in the driving seat, with his close friend Pookie, sitting in the passengers seat. It is obvious that they have been shopping while in California, because in the back seat, there are hunting rifles and a ton of camping gear. Pookie is looking out of the side window, just staring at the mass of woodland. Duzza: “What you lookin’ at man?” Pookie: “The trees fool. I’m tryin’ to remember what the city looks like” Duzza: “Dawg, we was in a city two hours ago bro! And they say I’m dumb” Pookie: “Exactly dawg, we’ve only been driving for two hours and we’re already in the middle of nowhere! We’re lost dawg” Duzza: “Man, we ain’t lost. I know exactly where I am, I just ain’t got a clue where we’re going” Pookie: “I know where we’re going, your gonna turn this car around and head back to the airport” Duzza: “Man, why you so scared? Didn’t think you believed in bigfoot anyway” Pookie: “Dawg, ain’t you ever seen that one movie Deliverance wit’ all them crazy rednecks? Where they try to kill the men? Man, we run into some rednecks fuck we gon’ do? Ain’t anyone raping me” Duzza: “Man, don’t nobody wanna touch your perverted ass anyway, you sick freak. Geez, and we’ve got guns dawg, we’re gonna be fine” Pookie: “Can I ask you a question?” Duzza: “yeah” Pookie: “Just say we bagged a bigfoot yeah. How the fuck are we going to get it out of the woods? Tell me that, how are we gonna get it’s hairy ass out of the woods? Because I’ve seen that videotape of the Bigfoot walking along, and ain’t no way us two could carry it out of there” Duzza: “We’d just drag it” Pookie: “You want us to carry it out as it no doubt screams for help, so we get every sasquatch in the area on are ass? Fuck that” Duzza: “For someone who don’t think they’re real, you sure got a lot of questions” Pookie: “Yeah well…” Duzza: “Hey, did you know that the amount of uncharted woodland here is large than the entire size of England” Pookie: “Well thanks for interrupting what I was saying for that completely useless piece of information” Duzza: “Anytime… A’ight we’ll park here, let’s go” Duzza parks the jeep and jumps out, he grabs a hunting rifle and a back pack. He waits for 2 minutes before Pookie finally builds up the courage to get out of the car Duzza: “Ahh Pookie, can ya smell that? That sick, disgusting, nasty, skanky smell? It smells like a dead animal rotting in the sun, that’s what bigfoot smells like that, that is the smell we have to follow” Pookie: “Ummm… I think I forgot to put on some deodorant” Duzza: “Good Lord…. Well at least yo stank ass might be able to attract some sasquatch… let’s go hunting” (End of Part 1)
