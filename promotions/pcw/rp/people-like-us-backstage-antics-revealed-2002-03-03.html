People Like Us: Backstage antics revealed!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 [Scene opens backstage at a PCW show. Standing in one of the many long and winding corridors is the host of ‘People Like Us’, Robert Burton. He takes a deep breath, looks into the camera and begins to speak in his professional, journalistic voice] Robert: Welcome to the third instalment of this ‘People Like Us’ insight into the world of professional wrestling. In our first to episodes we covered the road trip of a wrestler and the path to the arena, which every wrestler must take. But what happens when the wrestler arrives at the arena? Surely there is more to it then just taking off your clothes, rubbing on some baby oil, walking to the ring and fighting against some illiterate brute. Well today we find out exactly what happens backstage at a wrestling event. Now, behind this very door that stands behind me is our personal guide to the world of wrestling; Flaymin Aymin. Sure, he may be lacking some marbles in the ol’ noggin but hey lets face it- he’s more entertaining then all those other mind-numbingly dull junkies in PCW combined. Currently, Flaymin Aymin is in some serious preparation for his upcoming World Title Rumble at March Madness. We’ll be careful not to interfere with his strict schedule.Lets go and see him! [Robert knocks on the door and enters the room. He and the cameraman slowly walk through the door, doing their best to remain as quiet as possible. Inside the room, Flaymin Aymin and Juan Borro are in the midst of a heated discussion] Flaymin: No way!No f*cking way, you’re totally wrong on this one. Donatello is soooo the best Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle. Juan: Oh come on!How much talent does it take to run around with a piece of stick? Flaymin: It’s not a stick, @sshole!It’s a sawn off broom!! Did you see that episode where he wiped out seven foot-soldiers by twirling his broom just once? BAM!BAM!BAM! It was magic. Juan: That was only three “bam’s”.I thought you said he took out seven people? [Having heard enough, Robert interferes with the conversation] Robert: Sorry to disrupt this debate but Flaymin, I was wondering if you could show me around the backstage area? Flaymin: What’s there to see? You’ve got the toilet over there.The wrestling ring is a few yards that way. And the ‘roid cabinet is that huge thing the size of a fridge.Although there is a long queue outside that one. Robert: I was hoping you could show us around…. For the TV Show? Flaymin: Oh riiiight, The ‘People Like Us’ documentary!I forgot all about that. Alright, lets do this. Firstly, this is Juan Borro a fellow International Incident stable mate. [Juan waves at the camera] Flaymin: We were just discussing some err…. Tactics for our upcoming matches. Robert: But I heard you talking about Ninja Turtles. [Flaymin looks at Juan and shrugs his shoulders] Juan: Ninja Turtles are just some nicknames we’ve created…. Robert: Then who are the foot soldiers? Flaymin: Jobbers. Robert: Ok then…. Why don’t we head outside? [Flaymin leads Robert out of the room. They walk down the corridor.Half way down the corridor they hear a loud noise coming from a room] Robert: What on earth was that?? Flaymin: Ah don’t worry about that.It’s only Akiyama banishing another incompetent fool into the void. Robert: Banishing?Void? Flaymin: Yeah, it’s like a parallel universe.Haven’t you seen Bill and Ted’s Excellent Adventure? Robert: Wasn’t that just a movie? [Flaymin rolls his eyes] Flaymin: Movie….yes…..it was aaaaall…. Just a movie…. Robert: So where are we headed now? Flaymin: Well this room on your left is PCW President Duke William’s personal room. Robert: How can you tell that by just looking at the door? Flaymin: I noticed the spit bucket and empty whisky bottles on the floor. Ok, now I’ll show you the weight room. Robert: PCW has a weight room? Flaymin: Sure.Many, many wrestlers need to keep fit and stay warm for their matches. So they usually just do a few sets here and there on a couple of machines to prepare for their matches? [They walk into the weight room but find it totally empty] Flaymin: Actually, these days wrestlers don’t warm up much. Robert: I thought you said many wrestlers come here? Flaymin: Eh, I was wrong… Robert: Tell me, where do the wrestlers hang out? Flaymin: A lot of them seem to enjoy scrubbing each other’s backs in the showers.You’d be surprised how many wresters take 2 hour showers. Robert: Are you implying there is a lot of homosexual behaviour between wrestlers, backstage? Flaymin: Sure, why not. Robert: Aren’t you afraid that your comments might upset some of your fellow wrestlers? Flaymin: I let my tractor do the talking for me.That shuts ‘em up all the time. [Robert pulls a notepad out of his pocket and flips through the pages, looking for an appropriate question] Robert: So wrestlers take long showers, do some weights, pop pills and err…. Engage in homosexual activities backstage.Is there anything else they do? Flaymin: Nah that’s about it.Sometimes you have to sit back here and wait for hours before your match so it gets pretty boring. Robert: Out of all these wrestler characteristics, which ones apply to you? Flaymin: None.I spend most of my time sleeping or going through the bags of other wrest… um never mind that last comment…. [Robert shakes his head in disgust and then turns back to the camera] Robert: Well that ends this episode of People Like Us: The Professional Wrestler. Join us next week for our final instalment in which we provide our most in-depth analysis into the life of a professional wrestler! [Scene fades out]
