Title: Corners of the Trapezoid: Teacher/Wrestler Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: Today Complexity. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is a very complex man. He has three distinct lives, in a way. He is a mathematics teacher. He is a rap artist. And, he is one of the most hardcore son of a bitches in wrestling. Ever. He may not be schitzophrenic, like, well, Schitzo, but he is still a man of distinct traits. The three sides, however, bleed together, like a triangle. In The HIT's mind, the reason for this is simple. He is like a triangle. At each corner, two of his sides mix. Each side meets each other side. Wrestling, rapping, and teaching? The three sides. And when they meet? That's what HIT calls an angle. But is finding all of this mans angles possible? Doubtful.... Sitting in his chair, The HIT is writing up his lesson plan for the next couple days. In the back of the room, two students sit, taking makeup examinations. Looking up from tedious planning, Trapezoid inquires as to the status of his students. The HIT: How are you two doing on the Chapter 8 Test: These are Circles! GET USED TO IT? Student 1: Not bad...I just hate circumference problems! Student 2: Yeah, I'm almost done. The HIT: Good, good....lesson plans suck. Student 1: How so? The HIT: Having to plan everything out in advance sucks.....for the most part, my career in the ring is spontaneous. Sure, I study my oppopnents, but that doesn't work well when you fight three guys and chairs go flying everywhere! In a hardcore environment, you grabs yourself a weapon, and you swings it as hard as you can in hopes of hitting someone with it in a place it will cause damage. Repeat that about fifty times, and you have the average hardcore match. Student 2: Yeah, but you don't do that! The HIT: I know.....but, do you think I go into matches PLANNING to jump off twenty foot ladders on people? Hell no....it just happens that way. It's kind of like my lesson plans. I submit them to the principal, but I never follow the damn things! It's like this...... Trapezoid begins reading from his plan.. The HIT: Monday.....utilize spherical objects to measure area of spheres. See, what that REALLY means is I'm bringing in the strippers! LAP DANCES FOR EVERYONE!!! The two students taking the tests, both males, cheer. Student 1: Do we get happy endings? The HIT: OF COURSE NOT! THESE ARE HIGH CLASS WOMEN! Besides, I learned from last time......getting the hookers from Chinatown's not a good idea and only ends in pubic lice. Remember what happened to Jimmy? Student 2: Yeah.....they kinda took F.U.C.K. a little too literally. The HIT: ANYWAY.....back to what I was saying. Anyway, I do the lesson plan through the motions, then teach how I want. I prepare for matches by knowing opponent's tendencies. For example, Bob Vurdo's a mafia guy, so he's probably going to use a shotgun, blackjack, and long black limo. Student 1: How do you beat a guy with those things? The HIT: Hire cops, or you get a guy nicknamed 'The Bull' to rat on him. Both students nod in sucession. The HIT: Schitzo's a problem..he has two personalities! For all I know, I can beat the absolute shit out of him, and he can come back as the other personality and continue the fight! Believe me, schitzophrenics are no fun to fight. Potright was one...that damned Lunatic Pandora personality did more damage to me than Sam.. Still, Schitzo has nowhere near the talent of the deceased Potright...so I should be able to defeat him... Student 2: Then what about Rollins? That guy seems insane! The HIT: Yes, against his brother.....and he has a reason! It takes a dangerous man to fight with weaponry against those he has nothing against..to fight with equal anger and violence against those he loves or has no opinion on as those he hates. It is something that can only be acquired with time...a rare ability indeed. Rollins shall discover that. Student 1: Do you ever feel evil doing this? I mean, you can cause serious injury to yourself or others.... The HIT: I'm sure, as many of the other roster members would tell you, there is no 'evil' per se. We all battle for our own reasons...for our own pride. When you enter that ring, you take your own life and wellbeing in your hands. Just like when you step into my class...you risk your sanity, and your grade. It's really simple. Student 2: Ah.....I see, so, you foresee no problems? The HIT: Well, fighting three angry black people is not my idea of a good time, but I'm pretty confident I can beat Khadafi and Duzza. No style, either of them. Ali can't exactly pound me like a Fuck Rag if he can't catch me. I can outquick them both, outmanuever them both, and hell, I'll leave I. B. Black no choice but to count a three when I pin those two guys. No choice.....no remorse for what I do to them. Trapezoid looks out at his students, smiling. The HIT: So, you two done with the tests? The students rise to hand HIT their papers, as we fade to black... ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
