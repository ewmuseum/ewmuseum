Geared up for Turmoil and ready for the reast of the week...

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-6-02 --- The segment begins with Hank Gunyon and Ganker Ransolver backstage talking amongst one another, using hand gestures and pointing at things oozing down the wall. Hank is wearing baggy black JNCO jeans, white wife beater, long black leather trench coat, and a red headband. Ganker is wearing a blue polo t-shirt, black jeans, black leather jacket, and a multicolored beret. Ganker: 'So yo ready for later tonight man, yo know our match for the number one contendership for them PCW tag titles?' Hank: 'I've been born ready bro, and now it's time to prove once a---gain why Team Nirvana will never stop rockin'~!' Ganker: 'Golden Game, it'll be Golden Game who after the Nirvana Beatin' deliver upon their a**es!' Hank: 'Them PCW tag belts will look damn good around our waists, and be my second damn PCW title won.' Ganker: (lightly backhands Hank on the shoulder then points with index finger at his face) 'Yo bro, shouldn't yo be shortly in line for a shot at the PCW Extreme Championship, seein' as how yo never did receive yo title rematch?' Hank: 'Right Ganker, I was thinkin' of cashin' that in after those tag titles are safely locked around our waists and we prove to every damn tag team why we are the undefeated nZw world tag team champions. I still have the NZW titles back home behind special bullet proof plexy glass. NZW was my shining glory and really helped to make me what I am today.' Ganker: 'It's where we first started our feud wit' the A** Kickin' Rebels, now split apart by greed and self-glory.' Hank: 'I'd Off-Beat yo in a second for a shot at the PCW World Championship bro.' (laughs) Ganker: 'Not if I beamed yo wit' the Screw-Over then the RSE-Vengeance first bro.' (laughs) Hank: 'Would never happen!' Ganker: 'Because we be too damn united for a title to come between us?' Hank: 'Um sure, but I was goin' to say cause yo wouldn't have the chance, the Off-Beat would hit upon yo so damn quick yo wouldn't even know bout it until after yo out cold. Remember my friend, don't trust anybody, not even yo own damn tag team partner. I mean wit' yo as a tag team partner, it's hard to trust.' (laughs) Ganker: 'Who won the RSE Cruiserweight Championship and who foiled his oppertunity?' Hank: 'That'd be me who foiled the oppertunity bro.' (laughs) 'See, PCW was ready to put the Hank~! gimmick over and me in general over as one of the elite. They knew they needed me to be on top and carry PCW to the top once more. They knew Hank~! was the only one who could save it from it's ratings dive. So the federation I was workin', RSE, I dropped to go big.' Ganker: 'Sell-out...' --- Hank shoves Ganker back. Hank: 'It's not sellin' out to want to show off yo skill to the world and show to the world that yo have the heart to do what we do day in and day out. I have more heart than yo ever can imagine.' Ganker: 'Yo bro, jus' playin' wit' yo.' Hank: 'So was I man.' (laughs) Ganker: 'But on Fusion, man we have some damn good competition to stay on top of the tag team division.' Hank: 'Who?' Ganker: 'Red Rock and that one guy wit' a D in front of his name.' Hank: 'Who?' Ganker: 'I said... SHUT UP!' Hank: (laughing) 'What?' Ganker: 'Dumb sh*t says what, who, named Hank!' (laughs) 'Ha, beat that sucka!' Hank: 'I am a dumb sh*t yo moron! What? I said I am a dumb sh*t yo moron!' --- From a distance. ????- (yelling) 'GIMMICK INFRINGEMENT~!!!' Hank: (yelling back) 'JOSHY POOOOOOOOOO~!!!' --- Josh runs over and smacks the back of Hank's neck. Josh- 'There was a bug.' --- Hank stretches his arm back as far as it will stretch and comes back with a fury vengeance and smacks Josh off his feet, on his stomach. Hank: (smirking) 'Sorry, a very big bug, looked like those giant sloth, tick things.' Josh: (on ground) 'Oi...' Hank: 'Stop speakin' spanish yo fool~!' --- Ganker helps up Josh. Josh: 'Good to see the one true RSE member still keeps some class.' (glares at Drummer) 'I'm glad I fired you.' Hank: 'I quit remember?' Josh: 'Oh right, I must have dreamt firing you then.' Hank: 'A...' (smacks Josh) 'HEY~!' Josh: (rubbing neck) 'I really wanted to, but instead made the call to Tyler Nelson to tell him you were wrestling in RSE as well to make an extra couple hundreds and he demanded you quit and devote all your time to PCW.' Hank: 'Thank his soul, one more minute in that sh*t house RingSide Entertainment, as yo called it, I'd have gone insane. Not that I wasn't already.' Josh: 'People like you can't get anymore insane.' --- Hank head buts a cement wall and gushes blood all over. Hank: (licking some blood) 'I love the taste of my own blood... Destruction, death, suicide, pain, whatever else.' (psychotic laugh) 'It all can be beautiful, anytime.' Ganker: 'I tried to commit him yesterday, but the asylum would not take him back.' Josh: 'Can't say as though I blame them.' Ganker: 'Point taken man.' Josh: 'So you two ready for your match tonight?' Ganker: 'Yup, Golden Game will never be the same again after a typical Nirvana Beatin' we thrash out on them.' Josh: 'If you're anything like you were in the RSE, they won't stand a damn chance.' Ganker: 'Angst is such a joke.' (laughs) Josh: 'He's good, or else I would not have hired him.' Ganker: 'He ain't Ganker Ransolver though and everyone knows that...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Josh: (glaring at Hank head butting cement wall over and over) 'So Hank, are you ready for tonight?' Hank: (stopping and glaring down at Josh) 'Ready? Am I, Hank Gunyon ready to beatup The Golden Game for a shot at the PCW Tag Titles? Yo think I would be anything but ready? Those tag titles are as good as ours and we will arise the PCW Tag Team Division to new heights. We'll make them titles the best things to hold in the PCW. F**k the world championship, I'll hold it in due time, but as of right now, them tag titles are my main focus, then next would either be the PCW Cruiserweight or Extreme titles.' Josh: 'You should be in line for a rematch against Jason Core, you are a former champion who never did get his title rematch after losing the title unfairly on an edition of Sabotage.' Hank: 'Yo know what I'm really lookin forward to though Josh?' Josh: 'What is that Hank?' Hank: 'Gettin' into the ring wit' Red Rock and his new partner to deliver one hell of a thrashin' to the both of them, and let's see if Red can remember this, That's A DAMN Fact~!' Ganker: 'Still in the process of makin' him a people person.' Josh: 'I can tell.' --- Ganker and Josh help Hank back to the official Team Nirvana locker room, but are stopped by Dave Kern who is with the Pimply Faced Intern Kid. PFIK: 'Wow, Team Nirvana, I never thought I'd get the honor to actually see you in person.' Ganker: 'Now yo have kid, not please, let us on our way, we have a loose psycho and no telling when he's goin' to snap.' Hank: 'MONKEYS~!!! FLYING MONKEYS~!!! RED ROCK LET THE FLYING MONKEYS GO~!!!' Ganker: 'Apparently now I guess.' (sighs) --- Josh grabs a taser from his pocket and puts it in Hank's back and fries the hell out of him. Hank grins, but is as well unconscious. PFIK: 'Well I'll let you on your way then, but meet me and Dave back here in fifteen for a prematch interview.' Ganker: 'Maybe, but I don't wanna guarantee nothin'.' PFIK: 'Alright! Dave: 'At least you're not as bad as Jeff Marx.' --- Ganker and Josh continue to carry Hank back to their official Team Nirvana locker room filled with great furnishings of black leather and a flat screen television with a great cd sound system. The scene now fades out to absolute/complete/utter nothingness.
