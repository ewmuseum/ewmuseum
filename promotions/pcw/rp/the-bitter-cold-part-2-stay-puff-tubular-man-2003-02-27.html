The Bitter Cold - Part 2 - Stay Puff Tubular Man

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 27, 2003 ['Totally Tubular' Tommy looks sort of the Michelan Man. That bodacious looking dude made of tires that looks like he's a generic Stay Puff Marshmallow Man. Except Tommy has on a blue and yellow winter coat, with matching blue and yellow ski pants. It's not really TOO COLD in New York City right now. The mid-twenties or so, but to a surfer dude who is used to the ray's not getting any lower than fifty degrees, then there is a totally HEINOUS problem. Tommy is wearing four layers of clothing underneath the giant blue and yellow coat as he shivers on the sidewalk directly in front of The Plaza Hotel. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's brother, stands beside him looking very relaxed and warm in his long black trenchcoat that is bundled up tightly onto his body. The people walking the streets of New York, trying to get back to work after a gnarly lunch, would notice Tommy but with all the clothing it's hard to really tell who it is. According to Harry's brand new digital watch, which cost ten bucks at Wal-Mart, the limo is half an hour late. Underneath the blue and yellow scarf Tommy's rage grows with each passing second] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Mpft...Mmmm...blah. [Harry's face contorts in confusion, wondering exactly what the hell his brother is trying to say. On any other given day Harry would have just nodded and stared at the tall buildings, thinking that he were Spider-Man. But right now, he's sober, and he's completely aware of the situation that they are in. They are already five minutes late for that stupid Tubular Tiki meeting, and Tommy is losing precious time to get ready for his Champion versus Champion Non-Title match on Fusion] TTT: Mft!! BLAH!!!! 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Pull the scarf down, Rain Man. [Tommy tries to reach up and pull the scarf down but his clothing is so thick that his arm only goes halfway. Harry just snickers finding humor in Tommy's moment of misery. He watches as the puff ball of blue and yellow sqirms around, bumping into people walking by and then he ends up on the ground...and he can't get up] TTT: MMMMMM!!! DPFT!!! [Harry stands there for a few moments looking around to see if the other people are getting a good look at this scene. It turns out that there are a few people looking on, some in concern and others finding humor in it] Harry: Stop fooling around down there. TTT: RRRRR!! PFT!! [Tommy rolls around on the sidewalk a bit more looking like he's stuck in one of those sumo wrestler suits. The scarf comes loose and the motor mouth is able to be audibly heard now] TTT: DUDE!! Your TOTALLY ignoring me! I told you to pull down the scarf and let me hail a cab, dude!! Then YOU tell me to pull down the scarf...AND I CAN'T!! Then all I ask you to do is help me up, and what do you do? YOU ACT TOTALLY BOGUS AND JUST LOOK AROUND!! Harry: Sorry. I'm keeping an eye out for our limo. TTT: Dude...in speaking of that limo...WHERE IS IT?! I'm going to call that limo company and tell them how BOGUS that guy is so that they fire him. I'll tell them to give me the power to fire him so I can do it myself! Now...tell me THAT wouldn't be bodacious! Harry: It would be pretty cool if you did that laying there on the sidewalk. TTT: Yeah... Harry: You know...you sort of look like a blue and yellow, clean shaven Santa Claus from here. [Tommy's tanned face beams with brightness] TTT: Really? Harry: Yeah. TTT: That's gnarly. If I WERE Santa Claus I'd use my totally RADICAL REINDEER to guide my BODACIOUS SLEIGH to this BOGUS MEETING so that I can get with all the GNARLY NEW YORK BABES!!! Harry: Not from down there, Santa Blue Boy. TTT: Dude...WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU?! Your like...witty. It's becoming REALLY HEINOUS! You know...once this meeting is over I'm stuffing a bong down your throat and making you get high, damnit! [The limo pulls up and the husky Limo Driver from earlier in the day hangs out of the window with a grin on his face. He and Harry share a look as the limo comes to a complete stop. Harry decides to help his brother back to his feet, put him out of his misery of being stuck on the cold, dirty ground. Tommy would brush himself off if he could move his arms down there, but instead he waddles his way over toward the limo] TTT: It's about time, dude! You better give me your supervisors number because I'm totally going to call that dude and have YOU FIRED!! [Tommy tries to cram himself into the back seat but his coat is so thick that he gets stuck in the door. He pushes and pushes rocking the limo back and forth. Harry tries to push him in, but it's still absolutely no use. Tommy sighs loudly and he's already cursing the bogus weather for the current stink that he's in. If they were in sunny California he would have been in and out of the meeting already. At this moment he would be at an afternoon club hanging out with some of the most bodacious babes that the United States has to offer! BUT NO! New York has to be all cold and full of totally heinous traffic! With their small limo's and HUGE COATS! WHAT IS WRONG WITH THEM?!] Harry: Your gonna have to shed some layers there, Mr. Stay Puff. TTT: Dude, don't even joke. Harry: Sorry. [Tommy, still stuck in the door, begins to pull his arms inward, to get rid of the giant blue and yellow winter coat. He feels that once that is gone it'll be smooth sailing. As he finally gets his arms out of the coat he soon realizes somehting...] TTT: Har... Harry: Yeah? TTT: I'M STUCK AGAIN!! Harry: How? TTT: My arms are caught inside the two sweaters that I have on underneath. I CAN'T MOVE MY FREAKIN' ARMS!! Harry: Wow. I think that's some kind of record. Maybe we should call the fire department and have them pry you out of there. TTT: No way, dude! I'm not getting caught dead having some bogus dudes in the fire department use the jaws of life because my coat is stuck! Lets just go to the meeting. Harry: With you hanging halfway out of the freakin' door? TTT: I don't care. I want to hurry up so I can finally give this gnarly New York babes a break of bogus dudes!! I'm sure there are MANY babes here that need some Tubular lovin' Harry: Well...all right. [Harry runs to the otherside of the limo, giving the driver a thumbs up sign as if saying, 'Mission accomplished'. Harry is in and the limo takes off with a vengeance. Tommy is hanging halfway out of the back door and is quite prone to being killed by another car or even a light post. Tommy knows the risk, but all he's thinking about are the gnarly New York babes. Who cares about the meeting, that will just take care of itself, somehow. That's when Tommy begins to dwell on his gnarly match against PCW World Champ, Snow] TTT: DUDE!! Harry: Yeah? TTT: Do you think I have a chance of beating Snow on Friday? Harry: Hanging from a limo door? TTT: NO! In the ring. Harry: I don't think that they can fit this limo in the ring. TTT: NO! I'll be in the ring, without the limo. Harry: If your gonna wear all those clothes he'll just push you over with is pinky. 1...2...3, it's over. TTT: ARGH!! [A lamp post wizs by Tommy's head as the edge of the back door scratches it shooting sparks out. Tommy's fear begins to rise as he soon realizes that the door in front of him is just hanging out, waiting to smash off of something. It could be a most bogus affair if that were to happen, and how bogus would it be not to face the World Champ and give PCW a preview of what is going to happen at March Madness?] TTT: Harry! Tell this dude to stop the car! I think that I might die or something if I stay like this. Harry: You just thought about that now? TTT: Yes. Harry: Well. We're here already, so don't worry. [The limo slams on the brakes and somehow Tommy pops out of the back door and onto the sidewalk again. He squirms around trying to get up, but it's no use with his arms stuck in sweatshirts and the giant winter coat that covers it all] TTT: DUDE!! You mean to tell me that the building was only six blocks down from the hotel?! Harry: I guess so...interesting. TTT: THIS IS JUST BOGUS!!! Harry: I agree. [Harry walks toward the revolving door, leaving Tommy lie on the sidewalk. The limo driver just giggles to himself and speeds away and out of view. Tommy just lies there with people walking by, wondering what and WHO that poor homeless man is] TTT: Har? Dude? WHERE ARE YOU?!?! HELP ME UP! Man... [Tommy is silent for a few moments] TTT: THAT'S IT! YOUR TOTALLY GETTING HIGH AFTER...AFTER...I FREAKIN' GET UP!!! ARGH!!!! [The scarf falls back over his face] TTT: Mpt! PFT! BLAH!! MPFT!!!!!! [Fade to black]
