Domestic Dispute (spat is more like it...)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Inphino Blitz pretty much failed at what he said he was going to do, did he not? Last week, nothing but talk about showing Propaganda the sins of his actions, and instead, Propaganda’s sinful ways came out over Inphino. Barely at that… and usually Inphino’s competitive nature would push him into a rematch this week against Propaganda. But this is not so. In this edition of Turmoil, he faces against Luke Chadbourne. A newcomer to PCW who’s already advanced into the tournament by defeating Kris Kanton, it’s starting to seem trend for newcomers to defeat already established PCW stars… it seems… Luke was the task at hand… but Inphino’s mind wasn’t focused on that at the moment. He had something else on his mind entirely. Mina watched as Inphino sat across from her staring down at the oak table. They sat in the same spots as last time, only this time it was near nightfall. The orange hue of the sunset over the trees shone through the sliding screen doors in the parlor they sat in, a cozy feeling at times. This time the atmosphere was ignored over others. Mina leaned her elbows on the table as she smiled softly at Blitz. “Hey… come on. Cheer up.” Inphino didn’t respond, but merely stared at the steam rising up from his teacup. She sighed, sitting back in her chair. She was in more casual clothing this time; a red tank top and black sweats. Blitz always and forever in all black, tank top and pants. “So you lost, big deal.” She scratched her head as she looked to the side, looking off at something in the kitchen. “These things happen. Nothing to get worked up about, I mean come on. Everyone loses.” In response, Inphino shook his head slowly. Another sigh came from Mina, her pep talk obviously not doing any good. Some manager she was that she couldn’t motivate her only client. If she couldn’t get Blitz back on track then Luke would be going 2-0 in this tournament. And Blitz would be handing in his resignation to PCW. She shook her head of that thought. They were back in PCW, to leave so soon would be rather foolish. Even if Luke beat Blitz somehow (she scoffed at the idea lightly, unnoticed by Blitz) she’d talk him into sticking around. But truth be told, if there was a survey taken on who’d win out of Luke and Inphino, there’d be a lot of little Blitz bubbles filled up. “I mean, come on,” she started her talk back up again with another reassuring smile. “Even Ivan lost matches. Even MEANSTREAK lost matches!” “To Ivan,” Blitz suddenly interjected. Mina’s mouth was agape to continue, but she paused as she stared at him. “True…” she admitted. “But by no means is Propaganda Ivan Stanislav.” “I’m no ‘Streak either,” he added again monotonously. “Yeah, sure, whatever,” his little add-ons were getting aggravating. “The point is, everyone loses. No one is invincible. You’ve gotta take falls here and there.” He shrugged his shoulders, arms still folded across his chest as he stared at his cup. Exasperated, Mina closed her eyes and lowered her head with a deep exhale. Things didn’t look improving for her cause, nor did things look up for Inphino. Time for her to step up her game… “Look,” she said, placing her palm flat on the table leaning forward. “Luke Chadbourne is all set to go into this match, and despite knowing who you are and seeing what you’re capable of, he’s all gung ho for whipping your ass!” His eyes looked up as she raised her voice. Being they were in the country and there weren’t anyone around for a mile or two (not unless you considered their recently silent neighbors Akiyama and Cyclonica), Mina raising her voice wasn’t a hard task. But if she did start yelling at you… man, you had better pray for deafness. “Do you know what he’s hearing in his ear? All this talk about “Blitz is gonna beat your ass! You’re fighting Blitz? Why don’t you quit now? Aw man, you got Blitz challenging you? You’re shit outta luck man! Blitz Blitz Blitz!”” Mina stood up as she continued her speech, her brow wrinkling with anger as the forceful words came from her. “You gotta know more than anyone that when there’s negative propaganda buzzing in your ear about your opponent it’s downright infuriating! By the time the match comes around they’re gonna be bloodthirsty for the chance to beat you down and prove that all the hype was nothing but complete and utter BULLSHIT!” Inphino looked up at her suddenly, his eyebrow cocked at her questioningly. Mina paused in mid-pace, shrugging her shoulders at him with a sheepish grin. “Sorry… that Ukyo Matsuura IS kinda charismatic… and entertaining… and cute…” recovering from her moment of marking, she pointed at Inphino with a red tipped nail. “But you do understand me right? Luke Chadbourne’s gonna come in there with aims to prove he’s better than you! And we all know that’s just bullsh… I mean that’s nonsense! Luke’s got nothing on you, and do you know why?” His brow wrinkled at her question. Mina walked around the table and stood in front of him, putting her hands on her hips dominantly. “Because you’re Inphino frickin’ BLITZ! The Black Dragon Master, the star in the people’s eye. And this Luke’s gunning for that spot in the people’s eye. Now are you gonna let him get over on you and step all over you, grind you into the dust of retirement? I THINK NOT!” Inphino suddenly stood up slowly, looking at Mina. “Is that so?” He reached for his tea and started drinking. By this point Mina was getting irritated by his attitude. “I don’t know about you, but I have to make a living! And if you’re down and out what am I supposed to do then? Reopen PURE? Screw THAT!!” “Why don’t you manage Ukyo Matsuura?” Blitz finished his tea and lowered the cup from his lips, looking solemnly at Mina. “You seem to take to him pretty well.” Mina was left flabbergasted as Blitz walked around her into the kitchen. Then when the words had taken their toll, she followed after him quickly. She found him at the stove, holding the tea kettle in his hands. “What the hell is that supposed to mean??” she snapped. “What, you tryin’ to insinuate somethin’ here?” “Am I?” he responded casually as he poured a fresh cup of tea. “You tell me.” Mina’s eyes flared up at that response. Inphino watched calmly as Mina grabbed a cup from out of the cabinet and put it under the faucet, running water into it. As soon as it filled she turned and threw the water in his face. After realizing what she had done, the angry expression on her face dropped into an apologetic one. Inphino merely stood there with water dripping down his face. He finished off the tea as the cup was still to his lips during that “short shower” and set the cup on the counter. Mina blurted out an apology as she went to fetch a towel. “Sorry! I didn’t mean to--- I don’t know what came over me, I—I was just…” She found a white towel from the drawer and started to pat away the water from Inphino’s face. He looked down at her, a slight smirk on his face. “That was a lot of effort just to throw water in my face.” She stopped and thought about it, and afterwards she chuckled lightly. “Yeah, now that I think about it… but I guess I was so worked up over you losing all hope because of the loss you took to Propaganda. I mean ever since then you’ve been silent and gloomy and crap like that…” “Don’t I always come off like that?” he asked her as-a-matter-of-factly. “Yeah… yeah you do.” The situation was starting to turn for the better. “So you’re okay?” He placed his hands on her shoulders and nodded. “There are winners and losers always when one fights. It’s a part of life. Everyone must play the role at some point or another. I’ve accepted the fact a long time ago, so when it comes upon me I don’t bear the burden of defeat heavily. I merely shrug my shoulders and go back to training. It is others that make winning and losing into a form of power that energizes them, because with no control over their own lives they take pride in controlling others, in or out of combat.” “Like Eric Sagar being so weak, yet feels powerful in controlling Propaganda.” “Correct,” he nodded. “So you don’t have to worry, because if I win or lose, your job security is safe.” He winked at her as he went back to pour himself another cup of tea. Mina was in a better mood now, moreso cheerful than anything. She grinned like a schoolgirl as she leaned against the counter next to Blitz. “As long as everything’s okay!” she beamed. “So what, were you thinking all this time on how you’re gonna get over Luke and take another swing at Propaganda?” Inphino stopped pouring water into his cup. “Who?” “Propaganda, you know… tall…” she held her hand up high like she was measuring him. “…Looks like an extra on Revenge of the Nerds part 6 or whatever number they left off at…” “No, who’s Luke?” “Ohhh, Luke Chadbourne?” she nodded understandingly. “He’s your opponent for this Turmoil.” Inphino turned towards her slowly. “Okay… basic managerial roles require you inform me of matches I’m booked for in the future.” Her eyes widened in disbelief. “Are you kidding me? I was talking about him the whole time back in the parlor!” “Sorry, I wasn’t paying attention. I had something on my mind.” She didn’t believe that either. “But you WERE! You responded and stuff! What’s THAT about?” He smiled a little. “You like to talk, and I like to listen to people who like to talk. Listening requires at least keeping ONE ear open on the conversation… I just paid attention to enough of it to respond, as if I WERE paying attention.” Mina stared at him for a while… before throwing the damp towel in his face. Blitz allowed it to fall over his shoulder as he followed after Mina as she walked off into the parlor. “Look, I’m sure that I can beat this Luke Chadbourne on Turmoil… just lemme read up on him a little… what are you doing with the phone?” “I’m calling Ukyo Matsuura. I’m sure he’d appreciate someone of my managerial abilities.” “Aren’t you a little old for Matsuura? He’s barely 20.” SPLASH!! “MY TEA!” And so… the scene fades to black as Mina apologizes yet again for her temper…
