The Tank 17 - The Offer

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 13, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank __17—********* The Offer Taming snakesleaves you only the time to run away. ~Leigh Donovan Saturday, August 10 - Day 36 Jason Snow walks the main floor of Unit B, sure to keep his eye closely on anyone he thinks maybe a threat to his ‘employer,’ Doug Hattoni. Through a dominant physical presence, Snow hasbecome one of the most intimidating men in the Tank, next to only J-Thug and possibly HarveyBlake - each of whom have proven themselves time and time again in battle. So far in his stay,Snow has yet to do much more than slap around the odd inmate, making some of the tougher ofthe other prisoners suspicious that maybe he’s not as invincible as it may seem. Watching him from afar, Harvey Blake wipes a bead of sweat from the upper section of hishairless forehead. “Where the fuck is Jake?” he asks, not expecting an answer from theskinheads that surround him while they sit around a checkerboard table. “I’m not a patient manwhen it comes to information.” On cue, Harvey’s protege, Jake Hooper, curls around behind his back and takes a seat at his side. “Jesus Christ!” Blake exclaims, “I’ve been waiting for a God damn hour! I’ve got kitchen dutyin twenty minutes!” “Relax, Harv,” Jake says, leaning back in his seat. He glances over his shoulder at Jason Snowwhile the bearded young lion makes another round of the Unit’s Main Floor. “I put the Rat on it,so you know the shit’s reliable.” “Cut through the bullshit, Jake,” Harvey says in tones that are growing more and more impatient. “I haven’t got all fucking day.” With a cocky smile, Jake Hooper leans close to the table. The rest of the Nazi’s follow suit. “Alright,” Jake smiles, “here’s the scoop. It seems that little bitch who’s been coming to seeSnow isn’t what we thought. Not his wife or a mistress.” “Who is she then?” asks Blake as he leans closer. “Get this,” the completely hairless Jake Hooper says, holding up an informative finger. “It’s hisfucking sister-in-law.” “Sister-in-law?” Harvey exclaims a little too loudly. Immediately, he makes sure no one’s closeenough to hear. He then reverts to his former secretive voice. “What the fuck is his sister-in-lawdoing here when his God damn wife hasn’t even come to see him yet?” “The Rat tells me the motherfucker won’t let his wife come see him,” says Jake, hissmile growing. “He doesn’t even want his sister-in-law coming, but she comes anyway. Butthat’s not the real juice, Harv.” “What is?” “The other day, the bitch came to see him - you know, the day Snow went nuts and got put in thehole,” Jake says. “Apparently, she was stoned out of her fucking mind.” “We already know that,” Harvey sighs. “Yeah,” Jake chuckles, “but did you know that’s what set Snow off? Apparently, themotherfucker’s got a real hang up about drugs. And the little bitch in the visitation room is likea daughter to him. Thus, he went crazy.” Harvey leans back in his seat to consider the information he’s just received, and the possibleways he could put it to his use. One of his other companions makes a suggestion, “So what dowe do now Harv?” the anonymous skinhead asks. “You want us to rag on him about the bitchgetting stoned?” Harvey Blake holds a silencing hand up to the skin head and then turns his attention to thesmiling eyes of Jake Hooper - they’re already on the same page. “No,” Harvey announces, “Iwant everyone to ease up on antagonizing Snow, got that? Spread that around. I’ve got a muchbetter idea.” Harvey leans back in his chair and chuckles, prompting the much younger Jake Hooper to breakinto a chuckle of his own. “Jake,” Harvey nods at the young man, “I want you to set up a meeting with Snow. Just you, me,and the fucking lion himself.” “Where do you want to meet?” Jake asks. “The library,” says Harvey. “The rest of those greaseballs won’t know a thing about it if wemeet there, but it’s a place where Snow will know we’re not planning to jump him. I want menplanted just outside in the gym, just in case he fucking tries anything.” Jake nods. “Make sure he doesn’t tell Hattoni,” Harvey adds before giving his young skin head a seriouslook with his lone blue eye. “And Jake,” he says seriously, “I’m giving this assignment to youbecause I trust you - don’t fuck it up.” *** From the Office of the Warden “Doug Hattoni may not run as much on the outside as he used to,” the Warden begins sourly,“but believe me, he still is a powerful man. Every fucking week I see a new deposit in hisprisoner bank account, and it makes me fucking sick. But hey, I’m just the Warden, what can Ido? I’ve already got him paying room and board.” “But these days, the way all of the gangs are set back, just waiting for something to propel themat each other, Doug Hattoni is more dangerous than ever.” “Sometimes I ask myself, would it be so bad if someone were to just leave Hattoni’s cellunlocked one night? And another prisoner just happened to slip in and slit the fat fucker’sthroat? Sure, all hell would break lose in here... but the rest of the world would be a much betterplace.” *** Monday, August 12 - Day 38 Harvey Blake sits at a small table in the very back of the prison library, surrounded by walls ofbooks. He stares across the small plastic table at Jake Hooper. “You’re sure he’s coming?” “I’m fucking sure,” Jake snaps at Harvey. “He’s probably just trying to slink out of that fatfucking Hattoni’s sight. I know it took long enough to get alone with him in the first place.” “He agreed to come though?” Harvey asks. “I mean, you’re sure he agreed? For real?” “Jesus Christ, Harv!” Jake slams his hand on the table. “I know what I’m fucking doing, alright! I’m not like the rest of those bald-headed bastards! I can handle this shit. Now just calm thefuck down, Harvey. I took care of everything. He’ll be here, and Hattoni won’t know a damnthing about it.” Harvey sits back and sighs before reaching behind him and grabbing a book entitled “A Better UFrom A to Z.” Within a few minutes, Jason Snow appears at the far end of the bookshelf behindHarvey Blake and a long staring match ensues. “No trick, son,” Harvey says to Jason, holding his empty hands up to ensure Snow of his safety. “We just want to have a talk with you.” Hesitantly, Jason Snow makes his way down the long aisle of books toward the pair of Nazis,and takes a seat at the small round table. “You said you had some info about Krista?” Snowsays, looking at Jake. Jake turns and nods to Harvey, assuring him that, indeed, he did tell Snow that he hadinformation on his sister-in-law. He then turns to Snow, “Sort of.” Immediately, Snow begins to get up from his seat before Harvey holds out a hand to stop him. “Jason,” Harvey says in a non-threatening voice, “please sit down - hear us out.” “If this isn’t about Krista,” Snow says, eying Harvey Blake coldly, “what the fuck is this allabout? I thought I made it clear that I’m not going to burn any of your fucking crosses.” “It’s not about that, Jason,” Harvey assures him. “In a way,” Jake speaks up, “it is about - what’s her name? Krista?” Immediately, Snowregrets giving out her name like that. He never would have done that had it been his wife inquestion. “How so?” Snow leans back in his chair but doesn’t allow himself to fully relax. “Listen, Jason,” Harvey says, leaning forward over his folded hands. “I know you hate me. Idon’t blame you. If I was in your position, I’d hate me too. And you know what, this meetingis self-serving to me - I’m not going to lie to you.” Snow squints at Harvey, wonderingwhy the older man would admit to that. In a way, it makes Snow feel more comfortable - heknows Harvey’s being serious. At least, he thinks so... Harvey scratches his balding head and then looks over at Jake, who’s smile has long-since faded. “Jason,” Harvey starts, trying to align his words before he says them, “we know your feelingsabout drugs. And you know what, I feel the same way - I really do. I know that doesn’t make usfriends or anything, I just want you to know ahead of time that I believe in the same things thatyou do. It’s just, we’ve chosen different routes. I believe in purity - maybe I go a little fartherwith my beliefs on the subject than you, but I believe in it. And I believe in family too... not thesame way that your boss believes in it, Snow. That’s what I want to talk to you about.” “You want me to turn on Doug?” Snow raises an eyebrow. “I should have fucking known.” Snow shakes his head and runs his hand through his long blond hair. He does not, however,motion to get up. “You’ve got to hear us out, son,” Harvey says, nearly leaning over to touch Snow’s hand beforethinking better of it. “Doug Hattoni stands for everything you hate.” “So do you.” Harvey sighs and leans back. “You don’t work for me, son.” “What are you asking me to do here, Nazi?” Snow asks, purposely showing disrespect to theolder man across from him. To his right, Jake leans in. “We’re not as-” Jake begins before being silenced by Harvey’s strong left arm. At this moment,Harvey Blake realizes just how much Jake Hooper still has to learn. “I don’t want you to do anything, Jason,” Harvey says. “All I ask is that you stopprotecting Doug.” Harvey tries to nail his point home. “He’s not a good man, Snow. I knowyou don’t think I am either, but you have to see Hattoni for what he is. He’s a murderer, Snow. Not only in here, but on the outside. People are still being murdered in his name, byhis command. And you hate drugs? Where the fuck do you think Hattoni gets all hisfucking money, Snow? From drugs. Maybe not all of it, but most of it.” “And not just drugs in here, Snow,” Jake adds, making Harvey proud with timely input. “He’s right, Snow,” Harvey agrees with his young hairless protege. “That shit she was fucked upon - Krista - that probably came from Hattoni. At the very least, one of his outfits probably had ahand in bringing it here. He’s the real thing, Snow. That’s why he’s in this place. You’re herebecause you beat up some punk kid. You’re not like him.” “You think I don’t know all this?” Snow asks, his voice growing a bit louder and moreaggravated. “You think it hasn’t been playing over and fucking over in my mind since I talkedto her? But you both know, as well as I do, that if I leave Hattoni’s side, I’m a fucking deadman. And don’t even think that you can protect me from him. He runsthis whole fucking place. You know that, Blake - you’re barely able to keepyourself protected from him.” “Calm down,” Harvey urges Snow. “I know. But I tell you this - if you stop protecting Hattoni,he won’t live long enough to do shit to you. He may be inches away from taking me out, butright now, the only thing keeping him alive...” Harvey sighs, “is you.” Snow stands up from his seat, hearing all he needs to. He knows that Harvey Blake is right. Themoment Snow withholds his protection from Doug Hattoni, the fat Italian will be forced to eitherlive in a shell, thus losing his control over the prison, or the Nazis will make their move. Beforeleaving the library, Snow turns to Harvey, “I’ll think about it.” When Snow leaves, Jake turns to his mentor. “What do you think he’s going to do?” he asks. “He’ll do what we want him to,” Harvey snickers. “He doesn’t really have a choice now.” Before Jake can ask what Harvey means, the one-eyed Nazi gets up from his seat and exits thelibrary.
