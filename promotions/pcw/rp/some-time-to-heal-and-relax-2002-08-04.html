Some time to heal and relax...

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/04/02 It's a full week after the pay-per-view in Montego Bay, and The End and the Misfits are still on the cruise. The End took a few days following his surgery to rest in the hospital, and defending his Cruiserweight Title against Nicholas Pride of Dallas, Texas on Turmoil. But now, The End will wait until Fusion to wrestle Brandi for the Cruiserweight Title. He's boarded the same cruise, at the blessings (and with a fully-paid bill) of the bookers, and is now riding along with the Wrestle Vessel's host cruise ship on it's second tour of the Caribbean. On the cruise back from Montego Bay, Jamaica, The End and the Misfits are lounging in their room. Unlike Darkstar, who got the star treatment from Tyler Nelson, The End stays in the sub-sub-sub-sub economy class; that basically means that every now and then someone comes in with a shovel and tosses some coal into the fire. It's a shame the Cruiserweight Championship doesn't get anything more than this room. But it's even worse for the Rising Star and Extreme Champions. They would probably be the ones shoveling the coal! The End is still bandaged around his neck following the laceration of his throat in the brutally efficient PLC match with Charlie Loc and Miyagawa. His voice is a just as deep as it was, but is now a lot more scratchy and he breathes with a wheeze. Underneath the heavy bandages, rough sewed stitches hold the wound from his neck closed until the skin heals over. He is EXTREMELY lucky that emergency surgeons got to him in time. The trainers suggested that he take leave from active duty, but he went against their orders and will continue on the wrestling circuit, and work through the pain of the injury. It is foolhardy, yes, but at the same time valiant perseverance on the part of The End. It's something that Miyagawa saw in him and that's why he 'passed the torch' (so to speak) for The End to run with. Will he drop the ball, or exceed beyond anyone's wildest expectations? That's all on his shoulders now, as The End has taken on the weight of the world it seems to him... While The End rests on his cot, Lexicon, Richard, and Drek are playing poker in the light of the fire. Richard has on nothing but his boxers; Lexicon is down to his pants, undershirt, and socks. Slade is fully dressed in leather biker attire (not the gay-looking tight black leather, but the badass respectable worn-in brown dusty biker attire). Richard:'Whose idea was it to play strip poker, anyway?' Lexicon:'I suggested strip poker as a means of entertainment to pass the time as our trip has gone on longer than it should have. You would do well to note, however, that I also suggested that we adhere to the original rules and intentions the game implies by saying more than once that we should attempt to locate some FEMALES to accompany us in the game!' Drek throws down a card and picks up another one from the deck. Richard:'We tried. The old women didn't seem to like us, and there's too much competition for the young women. There's so many guys out there trying to get laid that the odds are against us.' Drek:'Besides, aren't you married to Barbara Streisand?' Lexicon shakes his head and gets upset, defensive in his voice and mannerisms. Lexicon:'YES, I'm married to Barbara Streisand as my alter-ego James Brolin, but honestly, who wants to see THAT naked? I married her for the money.' Richard:'Makes sense.' Lamar is trying on Lexicon's clothes. He has a cowboy boot over his head, and is trying to wear gloves like socks. He dances around with a blue towel hanging out of the back of his Ultraman T-shirt and makes 'whoosh' sounds. Slade just makes sure that Lamar doesn't run into the fire. Both of them have on khaki pants, but the pants are all that Slade wears. Stopping in front of The End, the 350-pound leper hops up and down to draw attention. Lamar:'How do you think this looks, End?' End:'Moron…' *SMACK!* Lamar gets a smack upside the head from Slade. Lamar:'Oh, right.' The End shakes his head, dumbfounded that one of his biggest (or fattest, anyway) fans forgot about his blindness. Lexicon:'Do not blame him for his lack of mindfulness concerning your condition. Remember that Lamar is an idiot.' The End shakes his head End:'It's my fault. Frankly, I'm not doing enough to push the gimmick.' In Kay Fabe's hospital room, the vultures peck away at his roasted flesh. The bandages burnt off in the electrical fire, but held in the bodily juices to seal in the flavor. Worst part about it is that Kay Fabe is still alive. For the purposes of the Misfits breaking kayfabe, Kay is immortal. Though at some times he wishes he wasn't. So, we'll give him a break today and let this be the extent of his injuries. Lexicon:'What more can you do?' End:'I don't really know.' The End sits up from his cot and steps forward; there is a spill and a tumble as The End trips over something that goes 'Yipe!' End:'Since when did we get a dog?' Drek speaks up, angered at The End's statement. Drek:'Ye've coll'd me soome ter'ble things, boot ne'er a doog!' End:'But you made a yelping sound.' Drek:'Ye stepped on me friggin foot! Woot'd ye 'spect me t'say: 'Thahnk ye, sir, may I hah' another?'!' Drek tries to massage away the pain from his toes. The End stands up and dusts himself off, the coal dust so prevalent that it forms a solid layer of soot on the floor. It rubs off on The End's black buttoned shirt and his blue jeans shorts. With his hands, he knocks off the soot as best he can. Meanwhile Patrick, a strong, broad-shouldered blond Irishman with fire for a cowlick and Brad Pitt for a face, comes into the room with a shovel and takes some of the coal that the Misfits are using as chips for their now regular game of poker. Patrick:'Don't mind me, I'm just here to keep us moving.' He tosses the coal into the fire with the greatest of ease and walks out the same way he's walked in for the past few days. After Patrick leaves, Lexicon ponders aloud, speaking to no one in particular save an aside to himself. Lexicon:'Perplexing, indeed a queer situation. The vast majority of major cruise lines use electric-powered turbines as the main source of energy. Thus the need for cooling towers and smokestacks on modern cruise lines is eliminated. This ship, on the other hand, not only has smokestacks and burns fossil fuels, but seems very worn and old, as if it weren't a modern ship at all.' The End dusts off his hands once finished with his clothes, and walks toward the door. End:'I'm sure that was very interesting, but you'll forgive me if I don't care at the moment. It's too stuffy in here, I'm going outside for some fresh air.' The End opens the door. It is completely dark beyond it. He steps inside and closes the door behind him. The Misfits are quiet for a moment, all just looking at each other trying to hold in their laughs. The End's voice comes from beyond the closed door. End:'I'm in the closet, aren't I?' Lamar, whose voice is growing more childlike in pitch everyday, responds. Lamar:'You are correct, sir!' The End steps out from the closet and goes to the other side of the room, where the exit door is located up a short flight of stepladder stairs. He climbs up the ladder and leaves the room, trying to maintain the same air of 'coolness' he had before mistaking the broom closet for the exit door. Outside in the night air, The End sits perfectly balanced in an Indian-style meditative position on the stern railing on mezzanine deck, flipping a coin. A JFK half-dollar, actually. He strikes it hard with his thumb, and the sound of the coin's distinctive 'ping' stays with it, so that The End can track its movement and catch it on the way back down. But that's not the only sound that The End tracks. End:'Hello.' The footsteps behind him stop. The jingling of the buttons upon denim overalls, and flip-flop sandals on the floor, along with the scent of Shanel perfume gave away his 'company's' presence. End:'Stop being alarmed by that, you know I have heightened awareness.' Sandy Wong:'Okay, but I swear that one of these days I am going to sneak up on you.' End:'Am I that important for you to swear by your word, or would it be just for fun?' *Simpsons fans be prepared to remember Abe Simpson's outburst as Homer's mother was about to be discovered living in the house.) [Grandpa Simpson:'I admit it, I'M THE LINDBERG BABY!'] [FBI Agent:'Are you creating a distraction, old man, or are you just senile?'] Wong:'A little bit of Column A, and a little bit of Column B.' Wong says that with a giggle. She's not as confident in her balance to sit on the railing; she just leans over it. The End catches his coin and ceases flipping it. He figures a post-PPV interview will follow the small talk. In fact, he's not really in the mood to drag out the inevitable. End:'Let's not beat around the bush. What do you want?' Wong:'Nothing, really. I'm just taking an extended vacation.' End:'How extended?' Wong:'I don't have a job with PCW anymore.' End:'Why?' Sandy Wong shrugs, forgetting who she's talking to. Then again, it could be just her own self-expression of cluelessness and confusion. Wong:'I don't know. They have Cal and Tiffany. That's enough, apparently. Even though I've been working more than both of them these past months, they deserve a job more than me.' Sandy releases a long sigh, hoping for some comforting words. Doesn't she realize who she's talking to? End:'This is the age of 0% job security. You have to get used to it. Every day on the job could be your last.' Wong:'That's a little pessimistic.' End:'Hey, I'd like to remain a naive optimist, but the world and all my experiences don't allow that to be so.' There is a moment between them of silence, as they just think the random thoughts that go through their heads. The ship is rocking back and forth in the waters, the traveling metronome guiding scores of passengers through the never-ending sheet of music that is the open ocean. Sandy is the first one to break the silence. Wong:'So what's it like?' End:'And by that you mean…?' Sandy, a person who makes clear her expressions through hand-gestures, can't help but wave her wrists in a circular motion to help her get her point across. Wong:'You know; what's it like being blind? In the seven months we've see- noticed each other backstage in PCW events, I don't think I've ever asked you that.' End:'… No.' The breeze tickles Wong's face and blows her hair into her eyes. She brushes it back. The End, whose hair is shaved low, is fine either way. Some bright white ghosts cover up the moon's light, though it still penetrates their semi-transparent forms. Wong:'So? What's it like?' The End stops and sighs. End:'You've been eating peanut butter. And pineapples.' Wong:'Yeah.' End:'The prevailing winds always blow in from the East in this area of the globe, so comparing that with our direction I would say that we're headed south.' Wong:'Looking from the moon and the time, I'd agree.' End:'The railing I'm sitting on is made of a lighter-weighted metal such as cobalt.' Wong: *Giggling*'I have no way of verifying that.' The End stands up and walks on the inch-narrow railing as if it were as wide and stable as a city street. End:'Being blind is like having sight. You just take notice of everything in other ways beside what you see. It's a mixed blessing. On the one hand, I wrestle better as a blind man than I ever did when I had my eyes. I've achieved more success now than in any of the independent federations that I've worked under in the past. The PCW Cruiserweight Title alone is more prestigious than any title I have ever won. 'And besides, I can do things like this.' The End cartwheels from side to side on the thin railing. Sandy Wong gets scared. Wong:'What're you doing? If you fall down, you'll hit the water and get sucked into the ship's propellor blades!' End:'IF I fall down. But I won't. Without having my eyes to get in the way, I don't see the fall. I can put it out of my mind, and trust in my awareness and balance to keep me alive. Besides, I studied and hand-walked the entire layout of the ship before we set sail for Montego Bay.' Wong calms down a little once The End puts her mind at ease. He still paces back and forth on the railing. Wong:'Then what makes it mixed?' The End, having removed himself by 5 feet to the left of his former position, does two backflip handsprings to the exact spot he was sitting in before. He stops himself there and goes back to a seated rest. End:'Drops of light misting rain falling on a leaf in the forest… A bright moonlit sky out in the desert… Waves crashing against break-rocks on the shore early in the morning… A flower-filled field in the middle of a pristine hillside, snow-capped mountaintops providing a dynamic skyline by no less than piercing the heavens above… I won't ever again get to enjoy such things. While I've gained a new perspective, and a new way of appreciating those wonders… what I wouldn't give just to watch one more sunset…' Wong:'That's nice. A little sad, but nice.' There's once again a silent moment between them. There seem to be words that they cannot say filling this gap, as evidenced by Wong's continuous stare directed at each other. There are slight rumblings, vibrations upon the bar. End:'Are you sure you're going to be able to balance yourself as well as I am?' Wong: *Sarcastically*'If not, you'd better catch me.' She doesn't move very quickly with it. In fact, she's so careful about stepping over the railing to sit on it that by the time she does get both of her legs over it two minutes have passed. Wong:'And don't tell anyone it took me this long to hop over the rail.' End:'You don't know that there is a promo-crew 20 feet behind us?' Wong turns around and sees the cameras. Wong:'Aw, crap.' The End would look at Sandy weird if he could. End:'Don't tell me that PCW has corrupted you?' Wong:'Nah, I just have to act innocent on shows. I liked it there! All my friends are there, it was good money, and I got to travel the country, and sometimes abroad. I'm gonna miss that company.' End:'I see.' Wong laughs at The End's joke. The End scratches the bit of his neck that his not bandages under his chin. Wong finally takes notice of the injury The End suffered. Wong:'How's your throat holding up?' End:'Barely. Emergency surgeons had to be call led to repair the damage. It's sealed up, but they say it could burst again at any moment.' Wong:'So you're going on hiatus from PCW?' End:'No. This means too much to me to sit at home at watch. Over time things might get worse. I'll take the risk, though. This is what I do. I'm a wrestler. I get paid to wrestle.' The breeze falls back a little, and ever-so-light drops of rain fall down on the two. They don't bother to move from their position; it's nothing serious. Wong:'Is the pay worth risking your life?' The End shakes his head. End:'No. I'd do this for free. Nearly do. I've got a special contract with PCW where my base salary is $5000 a year, plus travel and meals. It's all I really need to get by. There's a bonus you get for each week that you are a champion in PCW; but I don't care about that. I just want to wrestle.' The rain stops, but the breeze goes back to what it was. It's only slighter stronger than before, with the raindrops upon The End and Sandy Wong make it even colder than what it would have been otherwise. The End handles it just fine. Sandy Wong, though starts to shiver. Still, she does the personal interview she had been conducting. Wong:'They say you're a heel. 'A bad guy...' You don't act like a heel.' End:'I never said that I was. That's what others make me out to be.' Wong:'How does that feel?' End:'I'm indifferent to it. If they boo or clap, I'll perform the same.' A pause. Wong:'Let me ask one more question since the camera's behind us and this is like nostalgia for my old job?' End:'Shoot.' Wong:'You didn't defend the Cruiserweight Title successfully, but Miya still gave it to you. It could've just been because of his wanting to leave to be with his wife during the final month of her pregnancy, but he could have given that title to Charlie Loc. Do you think your performance played a part in Miyagawa's decision? End:'Both being equal. Miya couldn't stay on TV, and as far as I believe he was concerned, Charlie wasn't credible enough to hold it. So he gave it to me. I care about the title, and care about defending it and elevating it to a more prestigious position. The belt would have died of stagnation under Charlie Loc. Is that it?' Wong nods her head. Wong:'Yeah. Well, it's still pretty late. What're you going to do?' The End breathes in a deep, low breath. End:'Nothing really. Just sit here for an hour or so and... absorb.' Wong adopts curious facial expression, but a cute smile; not that The End would know, of course. Wong:'Would it be okay if I joined you?' End:'You already are.' Meanwhile, back in the cabin with the Misfits… Drek:'I quit.' Richard:'Richard does, too.' Lexicon slaps his hand down on his uncovered knee. In fact, Lexicon is completely naked, and had he lost one more game, he would have had to remove his foreskin. Lexicon:'By goodness, I knew my plan would work! I was not in the mood to lose one more time to a Middle-Earthen dwarf with a penchant for smoking and fighting, and a scruffy-bearded ruffian with mentally anemic faculties, the habit of speaking about himself in the third-person, and playing a broken towel rack like a flute defeat me. I am Lexicon, MASTER OF THE ENGLISH LANGUAGE! Certainly if I have the intelligence to master an entire mish-mash language thrown together on a whim, an easy task I found it to best you. Why a man of my… skill could…' Lexicon just breaks off into incomprehensible mumbles. He looks over at Slade. Lexicon:'What was I talking about again?' Slade:'Asdpom ejoinbq wnmb fiurhbn zdbfjk.' Lexicon gets up and walks to the door. Lexicon:'That's right! I shall celebrate my win over the others with a late night swim!' Lexicon doesn't even bother putting his clothes back on. He's going swimming after all. He runs around, hopping and shouting for joy. The others cover their eyes in disgust; Lexicon may be the Master of the English Language, but he's no Adonis. Even Lamar can't watch Lexicon's two-inch cock flap and jerk around while the rolls of his stomach jiggle like a mountain of Jell-O in a strong wind. Lexicon runs up the stepstairs, out the door, and instead of heading to the top Sun Deck where the pools are, he jumps right off the side of the boat. Hopping the rail, Lexicon is still ecstatic that he has won the game. But a moment later, he is hit with a realization. Lexicon:'Wait a minute -- I can't swim... I CAN'T SWIM!' *SPLASH!* Back on the ship, the Captain's voice comes over the PA system. Captain:'For everyone who's still awake on the exterior decks, I'd like to thank you for sailing with us on Euro-Continental Cruise Lines. Our next stop will be Lisbon, Portugal. The bar is closed but we do have a snack line on the interior mezzanine deck. Thank you once again for sailing with us, and have a good night. Goodnight, everybody.' The End tilts his head over toward Sandy Wong. End:'Did he just say Lisbon, Portugal?' Wong:'Yes.' The End snaps his fingers. He hops back off the railing and runs back to his room. *CLANG!* End:'Stupid waiters, leaving carts in the middle of the deck.' But if we're on the wrong ship, Richard will sort things out when we get to Portugal.' The Misfits all start kicking Richard hard in the shins. Drek:'Yood beh'er!' Richard:'Ow! Stop it!' The End walks away and closes the door behind him. Meanwhile Richard collapses into a ball on the ground while The Misfits continue to kick at his shins. Richard:'F*CK!'
