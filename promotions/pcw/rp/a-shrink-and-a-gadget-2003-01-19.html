A Shrink and a Gadget

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/03 Life has surely been different for Brianna ever since she received the acceptance letter from one of the best federations in all of the great United States, PCW. She has found herself more confident, and more excited about life. Some people say that when you are doing something you love, you find the most happiness, which she is doing. The question is how long before more opportunities open up? How long until wrestling doesn't quite suit her anymore? What happens if she does find a buyer for her many gizmos and gadgets? Thinking about that you can only try to achieve as much as possible in a short amount of time. If PCW wasn't ready for her before, they better get ready, because with all of these thoughts running through her mind, who knows what The Gadget is capable of? A soft, soothing form of classical music can be heard. Violins are being plucked away as piano keys are being slammed down quickly, yet very softly. The screen fades in from the black color, and we open up into a room which resembles a psychiatrists office. There is a big brown desk and chair located by the huge window looking down onto the streets, as well as a long couch sitting on the right of the room, with a big black chair next to that. From the entrance way of the room we hear talking, and the door is then pushed open. Standing there is a balding man of normal height and build, wearing a gray suit, with a clipboard tucked under his arm. He looks back and ushers in the person he was talking to before the door was opened. 'Come in Mr. Eadon.' The man walks in wearing slacks and a white button down suit. He takes a seat on the couch and reluctantly lays down looking towards the psychiatrist who takes a seat in the black chair. We see that it’s none other than Thomas who looks weary and droopy eyed. The doctor takes the clipboard from under his arm, pulls out a big bulky pen from his pocket, and pushes his glasses up from his nose as he stares in Thomas's direction. 'So what seems to be the problem?' 'I have feelings for a woman that I am no longer in a relationship with. After our break up we agreed to just be friends, but I can't stand not being with her. And to top it off, I am always a jerk to her whenever we come in contact.' Thomas spills as he starts letting everything flow from his mind. 'Was the break up really bad?' the doctor asks as he continues to scribble away on paper. 'To me it was. I had asked her to marry me one evening, and she turned me down. I blew up after that, and since I haven't treated her with any respect. Yet she is still willing to talk to me.' 'It sounds to me like you are just feeling bitter about everything. You loved this woman and she turned you down. Your heart is broken, and though you love her, you want to make her as miserable as yourself.' 'Yes that's it....I suppose. But why am I so mean to her, when I could be making amends?” “Your mind won’t let you treat her any way but poorly, until you believe you have won. You want her to realize that what she did to you was wrong and unjust. Until you can be more confident in yourself as a human being, and let the past behind you, you won’t get anywhere. And if that feels impossible, the only thing you can do is move on in life.” As Thomas continues on with his appointment, the scene slowly fades out and reopens in a familiar room where gadgets are cluttering shelves. The camera zooms in on Brianna, who is placing screw in a piece of black metal. She makes sure all of the screws are tightened on the triangular shaped object. She then picks it up and pulls the strap on the back, over her four fingers up to the knuckles. She stands up and faces towards a wall with nothing on or around it. She pulls her thumb into her hand and presses onto the triangular shaped object. As soon as she does, smoke starts pouring out of it and soon we see nothing as the smoke fills the whole room. A loud beeping sound starts piercing through the air as sprinklers go off in the room. Water pours down onto Brianna, clearing away the smoke. Brianna pulls a chair over and tries to quickly shut off the sprinklers before the mess anything up. She manages to switch it off and she just stands there, soaking wet, staring at her hand. 'Ok, so it was stupid to do that in a room with a fire alarm and sprinkler. But the good thing is that it works!' With excitement she jumps off of the chair and walks out of the room. Standing there is Milo. 'What in the world happened in there?' he asks concernedly. 'I got my handheld smoke machine working.' she replies happily. 'Oh so you plan on giving everyone an asthma attack when you wrestle tomorrow? Smart Brianna.' he says sarcastically. 'That's why I'm using fog you goof. If things get a little unfair, I pull it out and even things up.' 'Yeah that‘s great, Bri. By the way you got mail today. It's the card for the next Fusion.' He pulls an envelope from his pocket and hands it to the drenched Brianna. Brianna rips it open, and looks down the list, happy to see that she is scheduled to wrestle. 'First Tessa and myself are going to prove to Kano and Vurdo that women can in fact wrestle. Then I get another match on Fusion against Duzza! I love wrestling!' she kisses Milo on the cheek as she rushes away happy as a clown. “Girl I don’t really understand how getting beat up on a day to day basis can make a person really happy, but it’s really nice to see you this way. Although I figure you like getting rough handled by men.” he winks at Brianna as she chuckles, walking into her room. The scene fades out…… Intimidated obviously isn't in Brianna's dictionary. Sure she may still be a bit shaky about entering the ring with experienced wrestlers, but she is starting to get into the groove. Not only does she have the chance to extend her victory, but she also gets to show that women are as capable as men to do anything in the world. Though men claim to be dominant, women are catching up, and they aren't letting anyone or thing stop them!
