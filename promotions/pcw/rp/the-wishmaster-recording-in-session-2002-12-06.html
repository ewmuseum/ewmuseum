The Wishmaster --- Recording in Session

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/6/02 Open in a recording studio in Salt Lake City, Utah. There's much to be done here, the record company We'll Produce Anything is about to relocate. Not because of anything bad for business within the area, it's just that President Bush has juts declared Utah and Montana to be a part of the Axis of Evil and the country will be bombing it with all our stored nukes in a month. It is here that Dante Jackson has come with The End (who is still neglecting his training for his Continental Title match, but then again if he's not ready by now, he's not ready). Dante is finally in a warm room and he's shed his long coat for the jeans, gray turtleneck, and yellow Fubu vest. The End has paid for the services of recording artists Geometry Clazz. Sure, they work more for The HIT, but they do work for money, so The End contracted them for a single. The End got a note from Miles Williamette. It reads; 'Dear Mr. End, I'm a big fan of rap. I've got all of Bow Wow's and Little Romeo's CDs. I even wrote my own rap, but I've got no one to record it. I already asked the other two and they never got back to me. You're my last hope. Can you find a way to make my rap and put it on the radio? I'll be SO grateful. Your pal, Miles Williamette.' The End still has on his blue shirt and jean shorts. Sitting next to him at the equipment is Suge Knight; (who's here for some reason) fully decked out in his red suit, blue shirt and yellow tie. He's smoking a long Churchill cigar and has a 40 oz. bottle of Hennessey on the table by him. Sandy Wong is sitting in her black winter jacket and pants in between two of Suge Knight's black ghetto hoes, who are dressed... they're wearing pink thong bikinis. Big booties and breasts and all, Sandy sticks out like a sore thumb with her light pallor skin compared to the dark nubian ho-goddesses Suge Knight has with him. The studio is dark maroon in carpeting and the wall-paper. A couch sits right behind the recording equipment, which takes up most of the room. Suge Knight has set up all the recording equipment and playback for Dante Jackson, who's in the attached room behind soundproof glass. With him is the Geometry Class, the HIT's group clad in blue robes with caps and tassels, and Michelle Branch (dressed in another pink thong bikini because SUGE KNIGHT GETS WHAT HE WANTS). Dante takes a look at the script for the rap and makes audible curses. Dante:'Man, this rap sucks mo' than Lil' Kim at my House Party!' The Geometry Clazz release laughs at the expense of Lil' Ho... err-- Lil' Kim. Michelle Branch comes up from behind Dante Jackson and pats him on the shoulder. Michelle Branch:'It's going to make a little kid happy, let's just do it, okay?' Dante looks down at Michelle Branch... 's breasts. Dante:'Damn, you sho' is fine for a skinny ass white ho.' Michelle Branch shakes her head in disgust and goes back to her microphone. Sandy:'Hey, Sky, do you think you're spending a little too much time on Christmas Wishes before the Chef Memorial Continental Title Match?' The End shakes his head. The End:'No. The Fusion match is no problem. I've got it in the bag. I've already talked with The HIT, he knows my points are valid and irrefutable. Two months. It's been two months since I've defeated Rush Mason at Red October and caused him to get kicked out of Black Attack. Tim Walden was my debut win, and he's not gotten any better from then to now, he's just caught some lucky breaks. I've consistently outshined The HIT when we're in the same matches. And I'm due to beat Totally Tubular Tommy. I will be the Continental Champion after Fusion, giving the belt the first good champion it's had since the days of Chet King and Jeremy Howard before its downward spiral in the hands of Xavier Kannon began.' Sandy:'What about Dante? Is there a need to really pal around with him?' The End:'Well, we're teaming up on Adrenaline, might as well get used to each other's presence.' Sandy:'But you're also wrestling on Turmoil.' The End:'Yeah, but this RP isn't for that.' Suge Knight shushes the two as recording is ready to begin. He turns on the beat, a sampled mix of 'The Killing Fields' from Ice-T, only two octaves lower. Dante Jackson starts off with the rap that the End has brought to the studio for Miles Williamette. Dante:'there’s a fan swirling around and a band going on just around the corner so everybody get ready for a great ok get off the seat belt and have a little fun ok not just ladies other people too' Michelle Branch:'we see flowers blooming and we see the man eating also the birds and that bug flying in the house having a little fun so I saved you some for tomorrow' Suge Knight is nodding his head to the beat. He is, however, listening to some of Snoop Dogg's old work, he can't stand Miles's rap. The Geometry Clazz picks up the chorus. Geometry Clazz:'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah.' Michelle Branch:'dum bum fun bun that’s a boy not a girl ok everybody get out your you know what I’m not kidding yet one two three I like the big monkey and the man is very jumpy with a mole on his big old head oh Ah not like the old lady named Jannie with a giant fannie' There's a couple of beats where Dante Jackson isn't going along with it. Michelle Branch throws a shoe at him to get him to do it. Dante Jackson: *Shakes head*'so people I’m going up stairs to the fair in my little chair car thats shaped like a bear far in distance like my father' Michelle Branch:'so like a big star in the air I have a daycare that has a big ugly lair with men floating in the air and the sun shining at me with a dog named feefee and a cat named leelee and if you see a man named Tracyit’s a girl named Tammy' Dante:'but I have good food but it’s all chewed and drinks that is cool in cold so I have a tractor that’s at a factor and my big brother’s last name is Baxter' The ho's are wearing ear plugs to keep from hearing the attrocious song coming over the sound recorder. Suge Knight is still listening to Snoop's work. The End is just sitting there. Sandy Wong leaves her seat and steps at The End's side, putting her left hand on his right shoulder. The End take his hand and places it on hers. Turning his head up to hers, she leans over and they share a kiss (YOU HAPPY NOW, HIT! HUH?!). Sandy:'You're doing a good thing.' The End:'I hope so. While I look okay, on the inside this is wrenching at me just as much as with the others.' Back in the studio, Geometry Clazz hits the chorus again. Geometry Clazz:'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah.' Michelle Branch:'so I’ve had fun at the park when I saw a dog bark and the dog’s trainer was named Clarkbut then he had to fartand it smelled so I yelledI ran away but I felland so did my friend named kell' Geometry Clazz:'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah.' Dante:'I ain't doin' this.' Michelle Branch throws her other cleated shoe at Dante Jackson's head, prodding him to finish up the song. Suge Knight and the ho's are begging him to do the last verse so they can spare their ears any more pain. Geometry Clazz, the consumate professionals, buys more time by doing an extra chorus verse. Geometry Clazz:'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah.' Dante: *Shaking head in the negative*'No!' Suge Knight takes out a black brown-handled .25 caliber Raven semi-automatic handgun issued by Valor. He points it through the glass, which is not bulletproof, at Dante. Dante gulps and finishes up. Dante:'so I had funso I went homeand saw foamso I had fun with it.' Dante tosses away the headphones he wore and storms out of the room. Michelle Branch smacks her lips in disappointment at Dante. Dante grabs his coat by the red door near the left of the couch and leavfe the recording studio. Suge Knight rolls his left wrist to tell Geometry Clazz to wrap it up. Geometry Clazz:'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah. 'It's just a fun songyou know we just can't do no wrongsittin' pretty watchin' clouds all day longsingin, that happy happy fun song.Oh yeah.' *End*
