Another Price To Pay

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th March 2002 It seems that things took a turn for the worse last Sunday the 24th of March at none other than Sabotage for the self-proclaimed 'Asian Gangster' Charlie Loc…Not only did he confront the man who helped him against the II on Fusion, but he indeed had several 'Heart to Heart' words with him…which involved a few flying fists! Not only did Charlie prove that he wasn’t one to be messed with, but he perhaps made himself a most formidable enemy in the form of Bobby Stormbringer and just displeased the PCW Staff by getting himself thrown into the slammer for his 'unjustly' actions! Charlie doesn’t seem to know when to keep his mouth shut, but for some strange reason, his trip to jail might serve as a harsh reality and maybe make him think twice about reacting so fast next time….that’s if there ever is a next time once Mike Frampton and Bobby Stormbringer are finished with him! SLAM~! The rusty cell door lets out a small creaking sound before it snaps back in place, echoing it’s sound amidst the compact walls of the small cell. As with most cell’s there is a leak somewhere in the pipes and as the sound of the rusty door being slammed begins to slowly echo itself out in the confinement, the drip takes over it’s place and begins to infuriate the single person being confined within its walls…. Sitting on a frail, fold-out bed which is hanging off of one of the walls is none other than the man who 'had words' with Bobby Stormbringer…Charlie Loc! Through the gaps between the dripping sounds, the swings on the rather thin mattress begins to squeak as it becomes apparent that Charlie is rocking himself backwards and forwards, showing a sure sign that he’s indeed not happy! Who would be happy being locked in a cell? As the dripping and the creaking continue to persist the cell that Charlie is being held in seems to be very isolated and very dark. Like a lot of other cells the sweet smell of Toilet Cleaner can be smelt as the only other addition to the bed that Charlie is sitting on is a toilet, a small wash basin next to it, and a tiny mirror no big enough to see the reflection of one’s mouth in. DrIp~! DrIp~! DrIp~! CREAK~! DrIp~! DrIp~! DrIp~! Both the dripping of the pipes and the creaking sounds of the mattress begin to both become annoying in there own way, as Charlie who is still sitting motionless on the mattress, apart from his swings, doesn’t seem at all too concerned about it! As per usual, Charlie is ice cold! His head is hung between his hands and his hair is shielding what ever expression may be on his face, but knowing what the Asian is like, it’s probably just a stone cold glare that could shatter glass or pierce the soul! A few minutes pass, and the dripping and creaking fail to cease as the darkness in the cell begins to grow, engulfing everything except Charlie and the pale blue blanket and white pillow that lay on the mattress. Suddenly and out of nowhere, a white light shines from somewhere near the roof of the cell! A loud noise then cuts in over the dripping and creaking and the ever so familiar sound of a helicopters rotor can be heard as well as the usual 'chopping' sound that accompanies of it, as it’s wings slice through the cold night air. It becomes somewhat clear that a search light is being shone through a small, thin, barred window just below the ceiling of the cell behind the inmate. Charlie doesn’t seem to notice nor care and doesn’t bother to look up as the search light slowly disappears and the sound of the helicopter slowly fades out into the distance and is replace by the ever so annoying sound of the creaking mattress and the dripping water…. 'SHUT THE FUCK UP! STUPID WHITE BOY GET A FUCKIN’ PLUMBING JOB!' Charlie suddenly springs off of the mattress that he is sitting on and begins to yell at the top of his lungs as he is so frequently known to do.His voice seems to somewhat rebound off of the walls and echo outside the door, to which a distant mumble can be heard…probably some fat security guard eating donuts, too lazy to get up and reprimand him. For some strange reason, Charlie becomes infuriated yet again and grabs ahold of his hair and clasps his hands tightly over his ears before shaking his head and sitting back down onto the mattress rocking backwards and forwards mumbling something in Vietnamese with his face screwed up as the dripping and creaking begin to chime in with each other once more. DrIp~! DrIp~! DrIp~! CREAK~! DrIp~! DrIp~! Before the third drop manages to fall and splash somewhere inside the cell Charlie leaps up out of his seated position and lets out another loud roar. 'GET SOME FUCKIN’ HANDYMAN IN HERE WHITE BOY! THIS FUCKIN’ ANNOYING! AHHHHHHHH~!' If Charlie was a god, his voice would’ve leveled New York in seconds! As the screaming continues to echo in the walls of the cell Charlie begins to enter a fit of rage as every muscle on the young man’s chest and arms seems to flare, making him quiet the sight! All of a sudden, footsteps can be heard coming somewhere off in the distance. The footsteps don’t seem to be fast, but they seem to be heavy! As though the person walking is putting some force into each and every step he or she is taking. The stomping of the feet down the corridor leading to the jail cell could’ve caused tremors in the earth as they come closer and closer to the cell that Charlie is confined in…. As the footsteps come closer, the stomping becomes heavier and the sound of the boots hitting the floor begins to sound almost like the slapping of a wet towel on somebody’s naked back. Charlie continues to pace up and down in his cell, holding onto his head and looking like he is going insane as the footsteps of the Police Officer coming down the corridor become louder and harder. FLASH~! Just as the foot of the Officer’s boot appears around the corner of the cell, there is a loud flashing sound and a bright white light fills the cell and the corridor that the Officer was walking down! For some strange reason…everything has frozen! Not only has the dripping stopped, but the footsteps have too and the toe of the Officer’s boot is frozen in what seems to be mid air as it peeps around the corner of the cell! The only thing moving, or unfrozen for that fact is….Charlie Loc! The mumbling that’s coming from Charlie’s mouth doesn’t seem to stop as he hasn’t noticed everything freeze, but then again, Charlie usually is very slow to react outside of the ring… After a few minutes, he unscrews his face and the mumbling stops and the expression of Charlie’s screwed up face is slowly released as he opens his left eye and looks around, noticing that the infernal dripping sound has stopped and the Officer is no longer coming down the hall! A confused look crosses over the young Asian’s face as he walks to the dark blue bars of the cells and tries to stick his head out to peak around and see what’s happening to the Officer walking, but he doesn’t seem to be able to and instead he just takes a step back from the bars of the cell door and looks with a puzzled expression at the Officer’s foot hovering from around the corner! Charlie begins to shake his head, when suddenly an all familiar powerful voice, that can be recognized as none other than that of the almighty god Buddha cuts in out of nowhere and causes the bars of the cell to rattle and Charlie to fall flat on his ass on the hard cement floor of his cell. 'What a predicament you seem to have got yourself into here Mortal….what a predicament indeed!' The voice booms out of absoloutely nowhere. Charlie pushes himself up onto his hands and begins to pick himself up when the voice booms again, vibrating the cell and causing him to fall straight back down. 'MORTAL! DID I SAY YOU COULD MOVE?!' The voice boom’s yet again. Charlie opens his mouth to reply but is cut off immediately as the god responds to his own question. 'You are lucky to have pleased me Mortal! Not only have you gained your redemption and paid back my favor but you have put on quiet a show while doing so…' A cheeky grin crosses over Charlie’s face, who seems not to be so 'insane' after all! He opens his mouth to speak yet again but is quickly cut off! 'BUT! During the process you seem have to displeased me, as you Mortal Fools always manage somehow to do!' The voice echo’s around the small confined cell as Charlie screws up his face trying to keep his ears in one piece. 'I sorry Buddha….it not happen again!' Charlie quickly blurts out a weak reply. 'SORRY DOESN’T CUT IT MORTAL FOOL!' The voice literally rocks the cell yet again. 'Not only have you managed to displease me yet again, but you have somehow managed to end yourself up in the local jail, But….being the fair god that I am, and being as devoted to my religion as you possibly could be…I am giving you yet another chance to prove your apology Mortal!' The voice slowly fades off into the distance. A relieved look crosses over Charlie’s face and the only words that manage to escape his lips before being cut off yet again are 'How..' 'ENOUGH FOOL! IF I HAD WANTED YOUR PETTY QUESTIONS I WOULD HAVE ASKED FOR THEM….your first chance for redemption will come on Friday, where you will face none other than Mike Frampton!' The voice gives a soft vibration over the bars of the cell. Another of Charlie’s trademark confused look’s crosses over his face as he manages to squeak out a meek reply. 'What you mean ‘First’ chance? I get two?' 'THIS IS YOUR LAST WARNING MORTAL!…In answer to your question, Yes you get another chance to prove your worth to my religion, this time it will be against the man you savagely attacked….Bobby Stormbringer on Sunday!' There is a soft and sinister chuckle at the end of the god’s reply as Charlie props himself up against the back wall of the cell, with a dumbfounded look on his face. Just as the words are about to leave his mouth…Charlie stops and thinks before hanging his head low and replying to the god’s offers. 'I not fuck up again….' 'DO NOT SWEAR IN MY PRESENCE MORTAL!' The voice booms. 'OR I WILL BANISH YOU TO….' The voice trails off into the distance. 'Dol Mah Mae!' Charlie let’s out a smart ass comment in his own language. 'I AM VIETNAMESE MORTAL FOOL! ANYMORE OUT OF YOU AND I WILL MAKE SURE THAT YOU NEVER SEE THE LIGHT OF DAY AGAIN! NOW MEET MY DEMANDS AND YOU WILL LIVE…' The voice booms through the cell rocking it yet again making the iron bars rattle and roll before it trails off into the distance. 'I not displease you again Buddha….' Charlie hangs his head low trying to make up for the comments that he just made. After a few minutes of silence he raises his head and looks around the cell before yelling out to the god, and receiving no reply. He is about to yell something out when yet another flash of white light floods the cell…. Charlie begins to rub his eyes from the blinding flash of light as the dripping and the footsteps continue to pick back up from where they left off as the Police Officer appears in front of the door. He mutters something underneath his breath before fumbling around with the keys on the belt of his pants and clicking them into the lock on the barred door of the cell. 'Keep it down! Your getting out now you ungrateful little S.O.B!' The Officer makes a few choice words leave the side of his mouth after the comment. 'What the fuck?' Is the only response that Charlie manages to come out with. 'That’s right you little…you’ve been granted bail! Pick up your belongings at the desk and your free to go…' Once again the choice words follow his speech. 'Ha Ha Ha, Catch you later…White Boy! But who pay my fine?' Charlie is about to walk out the door of the cell before he stops in his tracks and looks with a puzzled look on his face. The Officer looks at the ground and mumbles something. 'Said his name was 'The Chef' or something…' 'HUH?!' Charlie looks at him with another angry/puzzled look on his face. 'Yeah that’s what I thought…Now get the hell out of here you ungrateful little S.O.B!' The Officer stops mid conversation and slams the door shut as Charlie jumps through into the hallway. As the Officer turns to lock the cell door Charlie gives him the one finger salute behind his back before muttering something in what seems to be Vietnamese before laughing and walking down the long corridor to freedom….. Well it seems that Charlie has been let off easily this time yet again! Only now he has to face two men who don’t seem like an easy win for young Charlie! And was that 'THE Chef' the leader of International Incident that bailed him out of the slammer?! If it is, there’s bound to be something going down! All good things must end sometime, and all good things must come with a price! Only questions left unanswered is when will it end, and how much will cost….knowing Charlie it will be sometime soon and a VERY hefty price….
