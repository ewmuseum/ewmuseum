What struck Skitzo as weird was that come Turmoil, the title and he himself could end up looking like this former shell, after the absurd match he was signed to. Who would be crazy enough face Clyde in a match as extreme as this? Who was crazy enough to face Clyde? Skitzo. The smell of fresh mahogany infiltrated Skitzo’s nostrils as he sat down in the spacious office, and Dr. McArthur smiled politely in his direction. Dr. McArthur: Now, Mr. Williams how can I be of service to yourself? As he turns to sprint down the street back to his hotel room, he skids to a halt and looks to his left. There, in a back alley is a giant dumpster, one of the lids was lying partially open, something jutting out and glinting in the sunlight. Catching his attention, the object glimmers brightly as Skitzo makes his way up the alley and stops before the dumpster. Peering in, the object seemingly stares at him, and sends Skitzo jumping back in horror. What is it he sees before him? With this weapon of mass destruction, Clyde has no chance in hell of surviving past Turmoil. With this vagina, Skitzo has reached a new peak.
