Mirror Images

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-11-02 Looking in the mirror I saw a man. His blond hair was cut short and tipped up into spikes. Those bue eyes stared back at me. He was rubbing his chin where a blond goatee once was. He shook his head at me. The man had been through some changes in his life. The road has been long and full of twist and turns. But his has made it through each with talent and luck. You could tell its taken it's toll on him though. His face seems older then what is should be. He can not even force himself to smile at me. His body was too tried to even try and lift the muscles up to make him smile. 'Chris Storm, what have you gotten yourself into now?' I asked the mirror. I looked at my reflection. Almost expecting it to answer back. I let out a sigh and touched my hair. My hand ran through my short spikey hair. 'You've changed. Just look at you. No more blond ponytail. No more proud goatee. But it's not just your physical appearance that's changed is it? No, you have been changed inside as well as outside haven't you?' I closed my eyes. The darkness consumed me as my body fell into a state of relaxtion. I hated sleep. I always have for as long as I can remember. When I fall asleep I leave myself open, weak. The images come back to me. The images I try to forget. For now I wanted to remember them. There was that small part of my past I needed to hang onto. 'PCW. . .' the words rolled off my lips. It was my new home. But it wasn't like the places I have been. The PCW is famous. It's known for being one of the hardest federations there is. Which is just what I was looking for. I wanted something from it. 'The best. . .'. To be the best. Wherever I have been I have always made a name for myself. I have always been a top draw. But they have all been called small time leagues. It wasn't really a great honor to be the WIW World champ or Grand Slam champion like I was. Sure I was proud of it. So was everyone in WIW. But did the people in the PCW care? No. Any why should they? I never beat any of them for it. I have not proven myself here. So to them I was just another green thumb coming in thinking he was hot stuff. But I was going to prove them wrong. All of them. My body hit the mirror. I just leaned against it as the images flooded into my mind. It was real but it seemed so unreal. I know it all happened to me but it was like I was watching a play. It was me I saw but it could have just as easily been another person there. Flesh meets steel as Chris brings a chair down onto the face of Genesis, his mentor and hero. Chris beings to smash the chair into Genesis' face, bending the chair. Chris climbs onto top rope with the steel chair in hand. He dives off the top rope and places the chair under his leg as he delievers a mighty Leg Drop to him, breaking the bones in Genesis' face. I opens my eyes. 'I was cold and cruel. Anything to get to the top.' The words hung in the air. I don't know why I said it out loud. But I just felt I needed to say it. It was like taking weight off my back. I lifted my head up, letting it hit the mirror. Anything to get to the top. That was very true for me. I have ended many careers in my past. People who could have easily been better then me, taken from the game because I couldn't bare to watch them outclass me. I have attacked my friends and allies. Anyone that was willing to open up for me and leave me room to strike. Even the man that brought me into the world of wrestling wasn't free from my venom. He brought me in and I took him out. I didn't care. I just wanted to be the best. And I was. My name was said with respect and fear. My opponents knew not to take me lightly and gave me the respect I deserved. I closed my eyes again. The sweet and innocent face of Tessa Windsor stares at Chris Storm. Her face slammed into a cake as she looked up at Chris. Fear is in her eyes. He stands over her with a steel chair in his hands, ready to strike down like a cobra. Tessa can not defend herself from the attack of Chris. He gets ready to bring the chair down onto her but he stops. The steel just mere inches away from her face. Chris pulls back and tries to delieve the fatal blow once more. He stops again. Tessa stares at him confused. Chris drops the chair and exits the ring. He is in a state of disbelief. He mutters something to himself as he walks backstage leaving Tessa face up in a ruined cake. 'I just couldn't do it.' I told myself. I said it over and over again. 'I just couldn't do it.' I could not bring myself to hurting Tessa. There was something inside of me stopping me. A wall I couldn't break down. It had never happened to me before. I felt something inside of me that wouldn't let me hurt my sweet Tessa. I didn't know it then but she was going to change me more then anything else ever has. I walked away from the mirror and over to a desk. There is a few pictures on it, along with different items. Looking at the pictures I picked one up. It was of Alex and Christa. I didn't know them at first. But they came along with Tessa. I guess it was sort of a package deal. Course I couldn't be happier. They are two of the most amazing kids I have ever met. Everything is a wonder to them. I could not image my life without them. To be honest they are not Tessa's children. Her brother died leaving Tessa to take care of them. I only hope to help her in raising them and making them their childhood magical. Mainly because I can not remember my own childhood. I have images of events I think happen. But I have no clue if they are real or just a lost boy trying to capture a little bit of a childhood, even if it means creating it. Putting down the picture I looked at the next one. It was of Tessa and I. Snow covers the area and we are wrapped up in sweaters and jackets. 'New York, October 17.' I said under my breath. It was the date the picture was taken. It was also a few days after Tessa had helped me keep my World title at a WIW PPV. After the PPV we talked. The talking led to our first kiss. That led to our current relationship. Our first date we went out on New York and had fun. In Central Park, where the picture is taken, we made snow angels and had a snowball fight with Alex and Christa. It was wonderful. It truely was the perfect day. It was also the day my cold heart melted in the warmth she showed me. I changed that day. And I have never been the same since. I put the picture down and walks back over to the mirror. I stared at my reflection. Studying it. I smiled and nodded to the reflection. I changed but I changed for the better. I am Chris Storm. And I am going to take the PCW by storm.
