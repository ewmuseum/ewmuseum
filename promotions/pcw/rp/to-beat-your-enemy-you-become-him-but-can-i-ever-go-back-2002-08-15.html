To Beat Your Enemy....You Become Him....But Can I Ever Go Back?

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-15-2002 (OOC:'I'll be mixing up some comedy, some seriousness, and a lil novel, a lil script. I wanna throw out a wide range and win or lose...this will be the BIGGEST match ever in my career...it's time to start showing my range ;) Enjoy and good luck Gav.) Brandi keeps telling you the things everyone has to say about your match against Darkstar. That whole night yesterday...all she could talk about was you and your health, just wishing you'd come out of Fusion in one piece. If she thinks what 'everybody' has to say means something... Think again... Your not real worried about what everybody has had to say lately about your match....'Your going to get mutilated...' 'You are going to lose so badly...' You will never survive him...' 'What are you thinking?' It's all you've really heard as of late and frankly.....you could care less. Even Brandi is worried about you.... It's funny because over the past few weeks, you've made EVERY attempt to get close to her.... Find love... Find that sweet peace within yourself that you once had with Aimz.But it's getting scary... Because it's not there. You keep trying to fill two voids now in your life, and neither one has been satisfied as of late.... And it's REALLY pissing you off. Those two voids your trying to feel are 'revenge' and 'love'. Revenge was hindered when Darkstar decided to stick his nose in your business with Snow..... You had Snow right there. You were ready to break him, make him pay for how he made you feel for so many weeks.... Make him wish that they'd NEVER let him out of that rathole prison ever again knowing you'd be there to completely decimate him. And 'love' is constantly hindered....between your obsession with revenge and the evil forces you are battling against, evil forces more sinister then you'd believe yourself to be...You don't want to see Brandi get caught in the crossfire of it all. This fact pains you...for you'd love to find solace in another person yet again, yet it's as if the world wants you to never have it... The cocktail of lack of revenge coupled with your lonely state has made you....ruthless....deranged even.Brandi said it herself when the two of you went walking outside the arena the night before... She looked deep into your sea blue eyes, always behind a mask just like you liked it....yet, the way she stared at you that night...it was unlike any other look you had ever received... Not that 'he's a freak' look you've been used to all your life...Or that obviously fake look Aimz would give you before you would kiss her... But a look of genuine concern for your soul. That night, she stared deep into your soul...and no mask, mental nor physical, could ever change that. She rubbed her thumb against your soft cheek as she cradled your head during the look....Brandi wanted to cry, you could tell, but you could tell how strong she was being for you...she wouldn't dare break down in front of you like that. She wanted you to be okay....she wanted you to survive the brutal monster that was Darkstar....'Rent...your scaring me. It's like...all you want is revenge, and your willing to risk ending your career to satisfy it...is it worth it, huh?'Could you really honestly tell her the answer without it hurting her... No. You knew what you wanted to say.... You wanted to tell her just how much you wanted to brutalize Darkstar, make him suffer for keeping you from your revenge....throwing up the hardest roadblock to hinder you from your true goal. From the moment he kept you from satisfying your urge....you haven't been able to get him OR Snow out of your mind. Now it doesn't matter which one you get....just as long as you make them both pay for their deeds against you...their hand in making you the monster you are now... The monster that killed Rent-A-Hero... A thought comes to mind as you keep talking....talking about brutal fighting, the brutalization of the sellout goth, and the insatiable thirst for revenge you've been harboring since the day Aimz left you... YOU ARE BECOMING DARKSTAR....Scary huh? Scary that you harbor the same deranged thoughts and shattered reality of the man who stands aside Tyler Nelson, selling out his so-called 'beliefs' for a platinum spiked dog collar....You are slowly becoming themonster he once was... You remember... the same monster that burned your face because he thought it'd win him the match for sure...The same man who threw you off the roof of a building just to see if you could fly... The same sick freak who would come up with an excuse to paralyze you and justify it within his mind, wholeheartedly making himself believe it until it was scripture to him... The more and more you revenge goes unfulfilled and the ore and more that void in your heart grows....the more like the 'Old' Darkstar you become. Your losing your natural smile...Your not joking as often.... You've never been more serious about your matches in your life...And every waking day, you wake up pissed off....because it is yet another day your new life's mission has gone unfufilled.... Friday....you have a chance to quench this insatiable thirst for revenge you've never been able to satisfy... It feels like drinking salt water in the Georgia heat....it may be water, but it does jack squat to quench you... All these matches you've been getting lately... Handicap matches....#1 Contender's matches....THEY ARE NOT WHAT YOU WANT!! You want Snow....you want Darkstar....YOU WANT THE REVENGE THAT IS YOURS TO CLAIM!! And Fusion...that's a start.... But then again.... It could be the beginning of what you fear the most.... Obessesion with revenge...They say 'revenge is quite the addiction'....will it feel so good when you finally get it, that you'll make up a reason to get more? Make up reasons to hurt the innocent and lie to yourself at the end of the day to somehow justify how 'right' it was? Your facing Darkstar...and to beat him....you will have to BECOME what he once was...Cold.....Heartless...Brutal....Unforgiving... But as most people who have lived longed enough have found out about some things in life... It's easy to get INTO things....but it's so much harder to come out of them.... If you become Darkstar, and avenge yourself....nail his HEAD into the mat....will you be able to stop....or will you become trapped with the same addiction Darkstar had been known for for years. You don't know... But there is no turning back now....The risk must be taken.... Let's just hope when the consequences come along.... You'll be fighting Darkstar again....Not standing by his side.... It is when THAT has happened.....that you have truly given in to this consuming obession... Brandi, pray for me..... If not her, somebody should.... I may die once again after Friday.... And what I become after that, may be more torturous the these past few months.Valhalla forbid there is anything that painful....
