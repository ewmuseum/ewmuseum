Date: Aug. 26, 2002 -Yesterday Afternoon- | The wooden ceiling fan circled slowly over my drugged body as I could see music in the air. I must have been pretty fucked up cause I started to hallucinate too. It's amazing what ecstasy does to a body. You get fucked up, start seeing music, and seem as if you were in a world of your own having a great ass time. I brung the weed to my lips once more, puffing on it before watching smoke ooze out from my mouth in black intoxicating clouds. The sun beat in from the window making lines of neon-light across my brown intimidating body with 'Piru' etched out on my chest. Buuday and Sheeca were lord knows where, I could give a fuck at the time. Ring...Ring...Ring... | 'Hello?' Like I wasn't quite fully in my body I pulled my shit together to reach over and answer the red phone which sat on my oak wood dresser. 'Who da fuck iz diz?' All voices that day came new to me. I was completely out of it. 'Wut? Man, you need to stop smoking that sticky-icky-icky shit partna'' Still in an extreme state of perplexity he continued to talk on about some things that didn't really stick in my mind. 'Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah, Blah blah blah...' It all mumbled like a school teacher on a Charlie Brown cartoon, I didn't understand it, but like the kids on the cartoon, I just nodded my head and acted like I knew. | High as hell, my mind started to ponder away. Thinking up shit that makes no sense to a sober person but funny ass fuck to a higher than a motha fucka person like myself at the time. | 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA' For no apparent reason to the person on the other line, I began to laugh at funny illusions playing out in my head. Of course, he just had to be saying something sad at the time: 'Wut da fuck?! Why da hell you laughing?! I jus' said my momma died!' Trying to go off on a nigga, he kept going on and on about how I was wrong for this and that but like I said, I was too high to care. 'Wait, who da fuck are you again?' After that all I heard was 'FUCK YOU' and the sound of the dial tone. Soon following, I let the phone slide out my hands hitting the red carpet floor below with a Thumb! | Feeling like the room was spinning in circles, I wiped the sweat which was seeping from my forehead at a extraordinary fast pace away and took another puff of my weed before setting it back on the ash tray which sat on the dresser besides me. Apart of me felt like picking up the dirty drawls, shirt, pants, and all other accessories worn on the naked body that laid around the room but then again I knew my black ass wasn't going to get up and do shit about it. A breeze brung the noise of Atlanta streets into my room while at the same time pushing forward the wooden blinds making a clacking sound as the blinds would soon come back and hit the wall because they were to big to hang inside the window area. As I sat there I could here the front door swing open, allowing Buuday and Sheeca to laugh historically at things ghetto woman would laugh at. | 'Yo AJ? You up in diz motha fucka?' Ghetto as can be, her voice shouted through the house grabbing my drawn attention. 'Wut da fuck ya'll want?' Or I thought I had said that, it could have been just in my head because Buuday busted in the room shouting my name once more before spotting me stretched out across the bed through the weed clouded room. 'Why da fuck you didn't answer me motha fucka?! You heard me callin' yo bitch ass name!' On and on she went about this and that, that and this. All it took was, 'shut up bitch' and she got quiet... Well maybe that came forth because she was searching for her gun... It didn't matter. 'Yo, did I get dat phone call?' Said to whoever heard me at the time, guess it was Sheeca: 'What phone call?' Her voluptuous lips colored with red lipstick spread apart making each word sound as sweet as pure chocolate. Coated with caramel skin tone setting off her incredible beauty with an ass and well sized breast to match. My lawyer/friend... Damn I wish I could tap that ass but she wasn't on that level with me, we're just friends, how she'd put it best. 'Wait, you mean that phone call from PCW?' I looked at the unsigned PCW contract and felt it was an aberration for me to go through with it. Fine, small words written elegantly, intelligently across a white paper with a yellow copy behind made up the PCW contract which lied on my silky texture red mattress. 'Uh...' Re-gathering my train of thought, 'Yea, wut they say?' As if I didn't know already. 'Oh, they just want you to turn on RUSH and join PCW. That's all' Blindly connive, she spoke of it with good sense of rectitude like it was perfectly ok. 'So, are you going to do it?' | Each word spoken so simply by her made it more difficult for me. It was like she was trying to encumber me with guilt for even thinking of turning on the place that made me without even knowing she was doing it. I puffed my weed again and again trying to stay high so I wouldn't give a damn. | 'MOTHA FUCKA!' BOOM! About twelve bullets leveled my bed but I was so high my reactions were slow to get up and see Buuday standing their with a revolver shotgun. 'CALL ME A BITCH AGAIN!' BOOM! | By that time I knew, 'this bitch was trying to kill my ass.' So I did like any smart brotha would do; grab my shit and dive out the open window before hauling ass down the back alley to my black Mercedes Bens with 20 inch rims parked outside. | ***7:47pm that afternoon*** | Like swallowed Ammonia, the guilt ate away at me inside. But I couldn't let that concern me now. I had Piru business to take care of. The dimmed now turned orange/red light from the setting sun projected barely bright blindly light off my platinum silver 20 inch dubs as I cruised down the street sipping on Gin and Juice. Broke down apartment buildings pictured on the outside with ghetto kids running ramped, red brick building up the structure, thugs thugging, and old people still trying to get their thug on all flew like a scene in motion through my heavily dark tented windows. I turned on Piru street and pulled up to my boy’s house, Darnel. Huge ass quantities of BBQ brought a hungry exodus of black folks leaving their home to the 'Annual Black BBQ Reunion' or what I like to call, 'get your grub on time.' Darnel's white wife-beater rippled in the low winds while he took off the white apron he was currently wearing. Dressed to thug in his black Fubu jeans and And 1 shoes, he greeted me with a pound(dap) and a blunt as I rose from my drop top. | 'Wut up nigga?' Said in the process of giving one another a pound(dap). 'I thought yo black ass wuzn't gon' sho'' Darnel continued as we walked to the backyard. 'Shit, I wuzn't gon' show up. But I got to much shit to get off my mind.' As if I didn't even speak, Darnel's attention went directly to a white guy with clean cut blonde hair, tight jeans, and a button up blue shirt that didn't help his image much. 'Yo cracka, you at da wrong motha fuckin' place. You needz to bizounce.' Making him leave as if he were a dog not wanted on the premises. I would have joined in to knock(diss) on his ass but RUSH and PCW were primacy in my mind. | The scent of meat floated in thin grey clouds while we continued to walk to the backyard. Music blasting as loud as possible, crazy ass black folks dancing on each other butt ass naked, sane black folks grubbing, and... oh shit... Wayne with his sorry ass set at a bench table with his crew. Nothing but gold teeth in his mouth. Said so his talk would never be cheap, but he's the cheapest motha fucka I ever ran across. His hair corn-rolled in a criss-cross pattern, dressed down in Fubu skeletons(cloths). Wayne and I go way back in the day when he dime-d my ass out for robbing that corner store. Of course I whooped his bitch ass and he had the nerve to tell everyone while I was in jail, that he whooped my ass. With Wayne and I in the same area, there was an inevitable altercation to go down. | 'I ain't scared to go to jail!' In the middle of some inspirational speech to boost his 'thug self-esteem'. 'I ain't scared of gettin' beat by da popos! Shit, they can lock me up in jail!' This almost to his self, 'I ain't afraid of takin' a shower with big men and watching the water drip down the crack of they ass, then you just want ride it all ni-' Realizing everyone is looking at him disgusted, he tried to come back to redeem himself, 'Ya'll kno' wut I mean.' 'No' Together, everyone shook their head while still looking at him disgusted. 'Wutever, letz get up outa diz joint!' Like 'follow-the-leader' everyone got up the same time he did. I was going to say something but after unmeaning fully ease dropping on what he said, I wasn't even going to bother. | I couldn't get my game straight. It was almost like I was pinion in guilt, but to get my mind off of this, my dogg Duzza and I chilled in the house playing cards. | 'Yo, how da fuck you gon' take dat hand like dat? I put a fuckin' spade down, u had a damn diamond. Tell me how da fuck you won dat hand? Cuz I'd like to hear diz shit.' I awaited Duzza's answer at which will justify his volition of action. 'See, what had happened was, my vision kind of fucked up.' Duzza looked over at a nigga next to the ice box. 'Yo playa, pass the Courvoisier' Trying to get more drunk than he already is. 'Stop drinkin' and maybe yo ass will see str8. Damn.' I drunk down a 40 between smoking Mari J. 'I'm high as fuck... DAMN!' 'What?' 'How you get so far away Duzz?' I was freaking out as I took another hit of my Mari J. 'Yo Duzza. I didn't kno' yo ass had a twin.' 'Wha?' Duzza busted out laughing. 'Yo ass needs to stop smoking that shit.' *continues to laugh* 'And you sayin' I need to stop drinking, man fuck you.' Duzza placed the whole deck on the table. 'I win.' 'Yo Duzza, why yo ass put a bird on da table?' With the Mari J fucking up my head, I began to trip out seeing things that aren't quite there. 'Why am I da only nigga 'n diz room dat got a problem with this bird Duzza jus' put on da damn table?' I Tried to quell the illusion at which was unfathomable to me; as to why no one informed me Duzza became a magician and have no problem with the bird on the table. | The laughing seemed to play a 'sing-a-long' tone in my head as I was so high I begun to laugh too not knowing what I was exactly laughing at. | 'I'm serious.' | [I must have been the clown that day because everything I said made the crowd of people laugh harder and harder. Through everyone who seemed to be far, blurry, and have twins that day, one person caught my eye; Darnel. Fidgeting with his hands uneasily, he sat there just watching with a faceless expression. Almost like something straight out of 'The Twilight zone' as I turned away to take another hit only to find Darnel missing after soon returning my bloodshot eyes in that direction again. It really didn't faze me to much because I didn't think anything of it. I just fixed my red Phat Albert shirt and pulled another blunt from my black baggy Phat Albert jeans and got my mind back on the party.] | 'When yo ass gon' put dat damn bird away?!' | [By this time I wasn't even playing, but the laughter grew once more and I continued to stay in a state of perplexity. Smoke reigned in clouds which hovered through the house with the kitchen being the producer of the smoke. The kitchen was very small if I remember straight, or there were just too many black folks in it, I forget. You know how black folks like to throw a party in a small ass place... Anyways, while everyone kept laughing, I got a phone call from Buuday...] | ***The phone conversation***7:59pm 'AJ!' 'What?' 'Oh lord... You won't believe wut happen'd to uz.' By the deep ghetto/Ebonics ascent, immediately I knew it was Buuday. 'Diz iz wut happen'd, Sheeca and I wuure in the jewelry sto' lookin' 'round fo' sum uar(ear)-rings or sumn' like dat when ol' bitch at da front desk startin' actin' a damn foo!' 'She started actin' a damn foo?' Tripping off how the front clerk tried to get crunk with the ghettoest girls to step foot out the projects, I asked quite amused. 'A motha fuckin' damn foo!' 'Like how?' 'Ok diz iz how it went down. I wuz lookin' at sum uar-rings dat wuure real expensive riight, and den all of a sudden, da bitch quickly approaches and says they cost too much! I wuz like, 'BITCH, YOU DON'T KNO' ME! BETTA GET UP OFF ME FO' I JUMP ON YO BITCH ASS!' Den she gon' tell me to calm down fo' she getz da police. Lil' Thug bursts out laughing 'Yo, where wuz Sheeca durin' all diz shit?' 'Wait, I'ma get there.' Buuday reminisced, 'After I cussed dat bitch out, people began to gather 'round and she started playin' it like it wuz crazy black lady attackin' innocent white lady and I wuzn't haven't it. So I continued cursin' her ass out till da police came and told me to leave the sto'. Datz when Sheeca came 'n. It wuz so funny as she came walkin' 'n to see wut wuz goin' on. The look on her face when she saw me wuz like... well actually, come to think 'bout it. She wuzn't surprised.' 'Yea, ok, then wut went down?' 'Well Sheeca stated she wuz a lawyer and wanted to kno' wut wuz happenin'. We explained da whole story to her, and then Sheeca did her lawyer skills on the officer to get me free when all of a sudden da white lady turned into Ms. Psycho bitch! Wait a sec. AJ-' | I listened on the other line as the real action threw down. Throughout the commotion and yelling I made out Sheeca beating down on the white lady. Buuday, who was still holding the phone in her hand, screaming, 'WHOOP HER MOTHA FUCKIN' ASS SHEECA!' and 'MAKE SURE DA BITCH WALKS AWAY WIT NO TEETH!' shortly followed by 'DAMN! DON'T KILL DA BITCH! Said like a true black person, cause you know how we like to scream shit in a act of provocation when a fight is on the break of the horizon. Black people are like guns, we kill without sorrow... Metaphorically speaking that is... Anyways, Buuday picked back up the phone.] | 'THUG! THUG! THUG! OH MY FUCKIN' GOODNESS! OH MY FUCKIN' GOODNESS!' Buuday still being a cheer leader for Sheeca who continued to whoop the ladies ass. 'THUG!' 'UH?' 'SHEECA JUMPED ON DA HO AND IZ WHOOPIN' HER MOTHA FUCKIN' ASS AS I SPEAK!' 'Damn... I knew Sheeca wuz ghetto as hell. Jordan X told me, 'Ghetto name, ghetto chick.' But I didn't believe his ass. Damn... No one pulled Sheeca off of her yet?' 'Hell no! They to fuckin' scuured!' Buuday laughed 'Yo Thug, I gotta go!' 'Wait! I wanna hear diz shit!' 'Not today! I jus' heard a cop sayin' they 'bout to bring 'n da S.W.A.T. team! Shit, I'ma roll up outa diz bitch fo' I catch a cap 'n my ass! Two Fingaz at a playa, bye!' Buuday quickly hung up the phone before yelling, 'SHEECA! BRING YO STUPID ASS ON! THEY BOUT TO CAP DIZ PLACE UP! You kno' how dem pigs don't give swash shit bout up black folks!' | Laughing, I hung up the phone. Darnel was doing crack, and... It hit me, CrossRoads was today. I knew what I had to do; lights, camara, action. | -RUSH Crossroads {Aug. 19, 2002} McGlocklan grabs hold of his own shirt from off Jackson’s carcass and stuffs it in his pocket, the stripes showing and dangling from off his side. The crowd cheers as Jackson’s head snaps against the ground with a Burning Hammer, the same move Caleb used nights before. I smirked adding a slight laugh at the end, 'Fuck-you-RUSH...'
