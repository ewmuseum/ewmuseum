Back To Old School

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06th June 2003 The illuminating light penetrates the two layers of glass of this double glazed window, a window that overlooks the smog infested streets of the bustling City of Sin. The apartment sits above the populous of Las Vegas Nevada watching humanity go about their daily lives but the activity of the outside isn’t the issue here, all relevance on today’s shenanigans lays within the dwelling itself. The laminate flooring glistens and the tangible smell of polished oak presents itself while the camera basks on the clutter of antique furnishings, items that lay home in this well-maintained living quarter. The creak of a wooden door murmurs, followed sharply by the shuffling of feet and finally the sigh of somebody yawning, “ARGH!!!!” The camera motions around to see Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester with his arms aloft and his mouth gaping open like a Venus flytrap. The trim Cruiserweight physique of the current Continental Champion protrudes from beneath his silky emerald bathrobe and to the gasp of a thousand singleton females he stands showcasing his boxer shorts. The Lord of Laughter creaks his neck and motions his shoulders in a circular fashion whilst his piercing green eyes adjust to the radiating sunlight of this beautiful Las Vegas day. “I should hit the gym!” mutters the Jester to himself, “But there again I only have my wife and a lousy janitor to contend with at Tuesday Turmoil!” concludes the King Midas of Tag initiating a cheeky smirk, one that develops into a full-blown smile. The Jester continues to stand in the midst of his bedroom door and after several seconds he finally traipses across the cold wooden flooring, overselling he looks to be walking through hot ashes. Truly a shelf of his former glorious self he plants his 226 lbs body mass into a black leather reclined chair and lounges in relaxation while contemplating single life, Single life being a career without the self-proclaimed “Living Nightmare” and not in the context of being without female companionship, after all, the green tailored Jester is a married man. Married both to his beloved Continental Championship and to one of his triple threat opponents, namely the newly crowned Television Champion Tessa Windsor-Green. A title he helped put around her waist but in doing so he infuriated the “Triumvirate” something he would like to rectify and Tuesday Turmoil may very well be the evening to right that wrong. Sitting in contemplation, the magnitude of the event came to his realisation, “Tuesday Turmoil, Triple Threat Match” “The Jester versus The Wife versus The Janitor” It looks pitiful on paper, a collection of oddities, that is until you weigh up the importance… “Continental Champion versus Television Champion versus Extreme Champion” A smile arced across the lips of the sadistic comic and a blush of red filtered into the cheeks of his angelic face as he realised that the contest is to be another opportunity to prove that the company’s second most powerful man didn’t came into being by the much argued fluke. Even in his state of relaxation his mind weighed up the pros and cons of being the Continental Champion and it was apparent that the positives overwhelmed the negatives. “I can now see why Dante Jackson is hell bent on reclaiming this title. It’s a confidence booster, an ego trip, hell I feel like the King of the World but… How would Dante know, he only wore the strap for a week!” mockingly proclaimed the Emerald Menace as he showcased a set of pearly white teeth. Giggling childishly towards his nemesis the Jester reaches over to his answering machine and allows his hand to freely roam over the control panel. “YOU HAVE NO NEW MESSAGES!!!!” roars the monotone voice of the answering machine thus allowing the comic to fall back into his chair and back into boredom on this, his day off. Standing erect the five time Tag Team Champion ventures over to a pristine wooden desk and peers down at a white envelope marked “To, Dorn Tracer” his eyes glazing over as he reminisces over it’s content. Clutching the envelope in his right hand the Prince of Pranks retraced his previous steps and passing the entryway of his apartment a sound startled him as he past, “WHAT THE…” Protruding through his letterbox was a bunch of letters all of which addressed to the occupier, in this case, one Gaz “The Jester” Green. Remembering the envelope in the palm of his right hand the Jester unlocked the door of his apartment and wandered outside onto a landing, before him stood the mailman. Clad in his corporate overalls he stood motionless before the metallic twin doors of the elevator, unaware of the superstar monitoring from behind, “Excuse me!” injected the Jester and the hired help turned with a curious expression etched along his face, his body language obviously questioning the Continental Champion’s wardrobe, “I have a letter here squire…” finishes the former Pranks With Attitude member as he handed over the article. Raising his eyebrows and rolling the pupils of his eyes he re-entered his penthouse pad slamming the door shut with a thunderous thud. The assortment of letters fell from their perch of the letterbox and fluttered to the ground creating a heap atop of the welcome mat these actions prompting the Conniving Comic to intervene. He hunched over and shovelled the envelopes into his hands and browsed through, “Rubbish, crap, pointless, bill, another bloody bill… Oh, and what’s this?” questioned the Jester inquisitively as he glared at an envelope with interest, the corner of said envelope baring a College crest. From left to right he tore open the suspicious parcel and his eyes roamed it’s content with a pendulum motion, again from left to right they swayed as he read the calligraphy of handwriting, - Dear Former Student, Time has passed by since you once roamed the halls of our school and hopefully this letter finds you living a good and prosperous life. Annually the school and its staff rally to reunite together at a function for former students/ pupils, so we invite you to attend one of these events. This year’s ‘Reunion’ is for those people in attendance for the semester of ’95 and would give you the opportunity to talk nostalgia with those you have since lost contact with, friends and pupils alike. Below are the necessary details of this event and hopefully we at ‘GM College Las Vegas’ will see you there at the date enclosed: 8th June 2003 Yours Faithfully, - Mr. John Tinkle (Headmaster) The Jester folded the article with a dumbfounded expression, soaking in the letter content thoroughly. Suddenly the camera picked up on an evil glint in his eye as a cringe curled across his baby soft face, his mind doing cartwheel’s as the planning of another series of mishaps began to bounce about within his active mind. The scene closed as those pranks and tricks began to be masterminded by the self-confessed Evil Super Genius of Primetime Championship Wrestling, snatching a pen and paper off a neighbouring ash coffee table the troublesome thoughts began to manifest. Scene finally fades to black. - To be continued -
