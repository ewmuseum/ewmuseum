Liquor Speaks Louder Than Words

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th September 2002 Question…. What make everything in the World disappear? When I say everything I mean everything pain, suffering, death, life and reality!! I bet all you sit there and try think of some long answer that you probably get taught in school, well let me tell you now before you think you got the answer …the answer is…. Alcohol That right, when you have alcohol all your trouble just drift away, nothing bother you, nothing harm you, everything peaceful…. I wish there some way to make this feeling last forever…. -=***=- I felt the wind run through my hair as I finally made my way through the dark gray door and onto the rooftop. The trip had been long I lost the feeling in both my legs but I guess I just too drunk to be able to care…either that or I just relieved to have finally achieved something worthwhile!! As I pushed the door open I clenched the brown paper bag inside my jacket to check that it was still there…I noticed that when you under the influence you a little cautious and I not wanting to walk all the way down 20 floors to get some more of this stuff….. So fucking cold… I began to shiver as I stepped onto the gray concrete of the rooftop and slammed the door shut behind me. Wait a minute…I not close that door! I stood still frozen in the icy wind that was blowing over the rooftop, trying not to make a move or a sound as my jacket and jeans began to ruffle in the darkness of the night sky. Someone was behind me, I just didn’t know who!! But whoever it was, I was prepared to give up a fight!! Everyone should know by now that Jung Loc not go down without one. The wind continued to blow as I closed my eyes and clenched the bag tight underneath my jacket. 1, 2, 3 “Ahhhhhh!” I roared as I spun around with a heel kick, missing whatever it was I was aiming for and slipping on the concrete, falling for what seemed an eternity on my back. My body made a thud sound, the sound that is made when someone breaks a bone or gets put through a table…no that more like a crack. Either way I not stay on my back for long as I leaped up onto my feet and looked left to right, trying to catch whoever trip me up. I couldn’t see anyone….. After looking around for a few moments I grabbed at the brown bag beneath my jacket yet again to check that I not been pick-pocketed…it was still there, but there was no time to celebrate! Someone else was up here with me and I wouldn’t rest until I found them!! I began to look around, trying to find clues…I seen them do this in those White Boy movies like Mission Impossible and Austin Powers when I looked down on the ground and spotted a puddle of ice. “I know you here….come out, I not going to hurt you!” I spoke out into the wind and the darkness that covered the rooftop. I knew that someone had planted that puddle of ice when I had my back turned, I just knew it. Heh, he come out sooner or later The wind blew in my face yet again as my hair began to trail into the night sky as I walked over to the edge of the rooftop and sat down, closing my eyes and opening them to see life below. A million and one flashing lights were buzzing around as far as the eye could see!! They were as small as ants but they were like something from a movie….it’s strange that the effect that alcohol has on your body isn’t it? Suddenly a thought hit me, “I guess I just going to have a drink now” I said in a loud voice trying not to chuckle and trying to keep a straight face as I put a plan into action. That right, I was trying to lure out my little friend on the rooftop, all I had to do was wait for the sign of him and then send a fist flying into his face. Well I said I going to have a drink…I not a liar My jacket ruffled in the wind as I shrugged my shoulders and removed the brown paper bag from inside. The bottle’s mouth felt cold on my lips as I began to shiver before raising the brown paper bag up almost verticle to the cloudy night sky. As the liquid ran down my throat it began to burn my mouth and suddenly I was plunged back into a happy land as the wind that was blowing stopped affecting me, I know it sound crazy but the alcohol touched me in ways that other things couldn’t…. I was in bliss….or I was drunk, one of the two. The bottle clinked as I slammed it down onto the cement of the rooftop and wiped my mouth with my jacket before dangling my feet over the edge. I suddenly felt content…then I figured that I hadn’t finished my henessey and took the bottle to my lips again. “Du Mah Mai!” What the fuck was that?! As I rose the bottle to my lips again something hit me square in the middle of the head and knocked me flat onto my back. Whatever it was that hit me was either strong or I was weak…I was betting that I had been hit by that stupid White Boy Super Man but I was praying that I had not lost my strength. “Yeah that right, hit me when I not expecting it! You really tough now White Boy!” I found myself raving on in again as I paused and opened my eyes…not believing what I saw. A voice boomed out of nowhere “Hello Jung” “How the fuck you get up there?!” I hiccuped. “I live up here Jung…I’ve been trying all night to get your attention and it looks like it’s finally paid off! Don’t worry you’re not dreaming, I am what you think I am” I think you a fucking moron who stuck in the moon…that what I think you are I couldn’t believe my eyes as I saw a strange face staring down at me from a huge and perfect 360° full moon. “I can hear your thoughts Jung…that wasn’t a nice thing to say!” the man snapped at me but his expression didn’t change from the huge dreamy smile and sleepy eyes he’d had since I first spotted him. “Are you Japanese?” The face quickly changed on the moon to that of a confused one as the man in the moon responded. ”No why do you ask?” I shook my head, “No reason…” Heh, because you look like a stupid Japanese…always happy and shit! If you down here I knock you in the head and then I… I suddenly remembered he could hear my thoughts. “What you want from me?” I sat up and raised the bottle to my lips. The man on the moon clearly irritated responded “I don’t want anything from you Jung, except for you to quit commenting on my appearance…You’re ugly but I don’t say anything!” Once again I banged the bottle on the concrete after swallowing a mouthful of Hennessey before replying to his rude comment. “What that supposed to mean? Say it to my face and we see who ugly after that…White Boy!!” “I am saying it to your face Jung….” I shot an ice-cold glare at him as he just sat up in the moon and began to chuckle to himself, knowing very well I couldn’t do a thing to him. For a moment I sat still pondering what to do or what to ask as the whole situation was new to me, just as I opened my mouth to ask a question I was hit by a barrage of “water” I going to fucking kill you….you wait! I shook my head before speaking alloud. I screamed up at him, “Why you keep spitting on me?! HUH?! You want to fight?! HUH?!” “I don’t control the weather Jung, I’m just the man in the moon! You’re the drunken idiot who thinks he’s talking to me….” I received a smart ass response. Followed by another, “How do you think I can hear your thoughts? I’m a figment of your imagination….or a character produced by alcohol one of the two!” “You Nothing….” I flipped onto my feet as more spit continued to fly from the night sky into me as I took the blows and rolled up the sleeves of my jacket preparing for a fight. The moon began to move up and down as if nodding, “That’s right Jung, I am nothing!” ”What you want then?! HUH?! You confusing the shit out of me!” I screamed towards him. I’d just about had enough of the shit that the man in the moon was giving me so I reached down to my side and picked up the bottle and began to drink as he began to softly shake and answer. “I’m appearing to tell you what you want to hear” The man in the moon smiled as he shook.He began to shake harder as he continued to speak, “You’re going to be a rich man Jung! Don’t worry everything will be fine! Just watch your back…the company won’t do it for you!” I shook my head to try and get him to stop shaking but it only made things worse, and made things harder for me to take in and understand. “Your house will continue to smell like rice until you hire a cleaner! Don’t be afraid Jung, I know you can do it!” The man in the moon continued to rave. O.k you really starting to shit me off White Boy…. I took another swig from my bottle before I began to swagger around and the man in the moon became all blurry. He continued to talk as I struggled to gain my balance, it was like he was using some kind of mind control I couldn’t control myself. Wait till I feel good…then we going to see who smell like rice! “Money will come your way….” ”You will not smell if you shower!” “The Company will look after you!” “Don’t be a typical gang banger, use a condom to protect your wanger!” “Use the force Jung….” Leave Me Alone!! A voice screamed inside my head as the man in the moon continued saying things that didn’t make sense, when suddenly I slipped back. I tried to wave my arms but nothing would happen, my World was going around me at a million and one miles per hour and it was plunging into darkness. The numbness in my legs began to flow throughout my whole body as rain began to pour onto me as I lay unable to move on the concrete of the rooftop. Heh, I managed a faint smile before my world plunged into darkness and I was transported away into another world. -=***=- “There will be no more nonsense Jung. You have a job to do, try to be professional or the company will punish you for your failure to act sensibly!” A familiar voice boomed around me as I tried to open my eyes, my body was feeling warm as light shone into my face and I closed them again. What the… ”Where I going….” I nearly finished my sentence when I felt a prick in my arm, my body convulsed before my world slowly plunged into darkness yet again.
