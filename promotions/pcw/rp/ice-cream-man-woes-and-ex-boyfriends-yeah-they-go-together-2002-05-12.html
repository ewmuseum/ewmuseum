Ice Cream Man Woes And Ex Boyfriends...Yeah They Go Together

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Ice Cream Man Woes And Ex Boyfriends...Yeah They Go Together Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-12-2002 OOC:'It's good to be alive and good to be able to RP for you guys another day. One love and peace.' ..::/::..Our story begins once again within the humble home of the Campbells, a nice 'Leave It To Beaver' style home built in a seemingly normal Nova Scotian neighborhood with freshly cut lawns and little children playing in the streets and accompanying yards. The sun smiles down upon all just mentioned as all looks to be well in the world. Shortly, a beautiful mettallic black BMW station wagon can be seen comign down the rolling hill leading into that particular block,the kids moving out of it's way before continuing their antics in the street. The car stops slowly in front of the Campbell's house, the doors opening abruptly as Forrester Campbell, the father of OSW's Aimz, exits the drivers side with a look of deep anger on his face, large red bruises covering his arms and head and anymore he could have unseen while wearing a 'The Gym-Where Canada Trains' T-shirt and a pair of worn carge pants. His lovely wife, Beverly, exits from the right passenger side, immediately rounding the car to meet her husband and trying to calm him down, looking at him endearingly with her deep hazel eyes before saying..::/::..Beverly:'Honey, don't you dare get angry at Rent-A-Hero, he was just having fun.'Forrester:'I'm gonna kill the kid honey. I'm gonna tear him into tiny pieces and recycle his Glad -Wrap a-...'..::/::..Before he can finish, none other than PCW's favorite superhero leaps from out of the station wagon, exclaiming various Rent-ism's of joy as he strikes a silly Whooping Crane pose. He removes the goggles over the mask of his Shinobi 2 replica ninja suit, the only difference being this replica is tight spandex and not soft silk, and grabs his PCW Crusierweight title out of the backseat of the car, smiling with great glee as he exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'PLUTONIUM PAINTBALLS OF POWER MR.CAMPBELL, that sure was a fun game. Hope I didn't bombard you with too many of those paint impact balls, but the rules were you gotta say 'I'm dead' and you never seemed to finish the line.'Forrester:'THAT'S CAUSE EVERYTIME I'D GET THE 'I' OUT, YOU'D KEEP BLASTING ME WITH THOSE GOD-FORSAKEN THINGS!!'Rent-A-Hero:'OOOOOH, we're supposed to give you a CHANCE to say it, gee wilikers Mr.Campbell, that's news to me. Oh well, it's not my fault you were too slow to spit it out. Oh well, we all can't be winners can we eh papa?' Forrester:*murderous tone* 'I'm gonna kill him...' ..::/::..Beverly shoots a mean eye to her husband after that remark, Amy's father doing all he can to retain his composure and will not to crush in our hero's skull as he happily prances about in victory. Aimz shortly jolts out of the backseat of the station wagon as well, removing her goggles and rounding to the side where Rent-A-Hero is at. She pulls undies the small ponytail made for the sake of playing paintball as her red hair flows wild and free like it should and she locks hand in hand with Rent-A-Hero, Amy smiling and saying..::/::..Aimz:'Now dad, don't get mad just cause Rentie lit you up like some weed at Snoop Dogg's house. My Rentie's got skills.' ..::/::..Amy leans in for a tiny kiss from her paintball warrior hero, but a sharp look from Mr.Campbell changes Rent-A-Hero's tune quickly, who stops his lean in and trades up for shaking her hand instead with a big smile..::/::..Rent-A-Hero: *Speaking in an over-exerted tone* 'PEOPLE WHO REEEEALLY LOVE EACH OTHER ALWAYS HAVE A GOOD HANDSHAKE!!' *Laughs nervously* Aimz: *Mumbles* 'Yup...dad's hard at work destroying happiness within my relationships as usual.'..::/::..Suddenly, a melody of rings begins to fill the neighborhood streets, a large white truck coming over the hill as the little children playing outside flock to the melody, caught in it's trance. The truck comes into better view, now being identified as an ice cream truck with decals of different frozen treats from Rocket Pops to Klondike Bars all over it, beginning to slow down as the driver starts to notice the pleathora of children flocking to catch up with him. Aimz, Beverly, and Forrester watch on at the ice cream truck with collective smiles on their faces, but the face oF Rent-A-Hero is construed into that familiar look of when he senses great danger. As the ice cream truck stops, our hero exclaims upon noticing all the children swarmed around the truck..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'NO CHILDREN, DON'T FALL FOR IT...IT'S JUST SINISTRO AGAIN ONCE AGAIN RELYING ON HIS EVIL ICE CREAM MAN PLOT AGAIN!! DON'T WORRY, I'LL SAVE YOU!!! IT'S MORPHIN TIME!!!' ..::/::..The Campbells try to stop our hero from rushing over to the truck, but he unleashes a burst of sudden speed as he races over to the ice cream truck, Amy turning her head in shame of the embarassment she knows is coming as Beverly and Forrester watch on in shock of the following stupidity. With the ice cream truck stopped, the ice cream man goes over to the counter area of his truck and then reaches down below it, but upon standing up, our hero can be seen leaping high into the air and straight towards him the kids and the man in shock as our hero exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'DON'T WORRY CHILDREN, I WILL SAVE YOU FROM THIS EVIL ICE SCREAM MAN!! PREPARE TO BE BOOTY-WHOOPERED EVILDOER!!' ..::/::..Our hero dives right on top of the ice cream man, rocketing right through the counter opening in his truck and spearing him. All that can be followed is the screams of pain from the ice cream man and various WHAP, PAM, ZOWWIE, OWWIE, MAAAAN symbols overtaking the screen as Rent-A-Hero is obviously putting a hurting on him. Shortly, our hero emerges above the counter, breathing heavily and dusting off his hands in a job well done as he addresses the little children, who stand before him collectively confused as to what just happened, the Campbells watching on from their lawn..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'FEAR NOT CHILDREN, THIS EVIL VILLAIN HAS BEEN DISPATCHED...the same way I shall dispatch Juan Borro at PCW Supremacy to hold onto my coveted PCW Crusierweight championship. The slaying of this villain is just the tip of the iceberg for what I'm gonna do for that soccer playing scoundrel who has beaten me twice....for at the pay-per-view, my greatest rival shall FALL to the Rent-A-Powers of the PCW's only and favorite superhero! Me and him are destined to be involved in the most classic crusierweight championship matches of all time...but there can be only one, much like the Immortals in Highlander. Our battle will rage and their will be a clear victor...that clear victor being the master of the KAMEHAMEHA ARTS OF BOOTY-WHOOPERING, your truly. Not only do I have fans watching all over the Earth, but over eleven galaxies are riding on Krypton's last son to reign victorious over that soccer fiend and I shall not let them down, for a true hero wallops any foe or challenge that dares to stand in his way! Juan will prepare for the greatest match of his life and when the ion rays have been fired and the Hadoken fireballs have been completed...the title will be around my waist where it belongs and the balance of the Crusierweight population of Earth's continum shall be restored! Ain't that great kiddies?' *The children blankly stare at him, having no earthly idea of half the words he's used* 'I'll take that blank stare of confuzzlement as a YAY in my favor children! Now, aren't you glad that I stopped that evil ice cream man from terrorizing you with EVIL, DESTRUCTIVE, PHOTONIC...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero reaches under the counter to see what unearthly evil the ice cream man had planned for the children...arising with two caramel center Drumstick ice cream cones in hand as he finishes..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'ICE CREAM CONES?! GREAT SLIPPERY OIL SLICKS OF SPYHUNTER....I really shoulda put my foot in my mouth before putting it in the ice cream man's butt...' ..::/::..The children look to Rent-A-Hero, dead silent for a few moments about what they've just learned. Finally, the silence is broken...the children breaking into a collective sea of tears and wails as one of the kids exclaims with tears in his eyes..::/::..Random Child 1:'HE BEATDOWN THE ICE CREAM MAN!!'Random Child 2:'THAT GLAD-WRAP MONSTER GUY HURT THE ICE CREAM MAN!!' Random Child 3:'LET'S KICK IN HIS SHINS!!'Rent-A-Hero: *Looks to camera* 'Eeep...'..::/::..The little children band together, storming the ice cream truck and toppling over Rent-A-Hero, slapping away at him and pounding at his shins with childish fury as Rent-A-Hero exclaims in agony, the ice cream truck shaking from all the violent movement within as the kids do their collective beatdown on our hero..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'NOT MY SHINS...PLEASE, NOT MY SHINS!!!' ..::/::..Back on the lawn of the Campbell's, Forrester and Beverly look towards Aimz, who is looking back at her parents with a nervous smile on her face, realizing this situation is going to be incredibly hard to explain on Rent-A-Hero's behalf. She just throws her hands up wildly in the air and exclaims..::/::..Aimz:'GOD, WHAT ELSE COULD HAPPEN?!' ..::/::..At that exact moment that Amy made that request, a long black stretch limo begins to come down the hill, slowing in front of the house of the Campbells until coming to a stop. From the driver's side, the finely suited chauffeur makes his way over to the passenger door far on the very end and opens it up wide, the chauffeur laying on the ground as two feet covered in black Stacy Adams wingtips use HIM as a carpet. The young chauffeur just looks to the camera, his giving a sligHT shrug before saying..::/::..Chauffeur:'Hey, if you were getting paid the money he gives you, you'd be down here too. Hell, don't let me tell you what happens when he needs to wipe his a**.'..::/::..Shortly, the man steps out of the car, revealing a tall, well built man with a clean shaven face and his hair cut short with gelled spikes in the front. His smile is the pearliest white and the only thing shinier than that is the gleaming platinum Rolex on his right wrist. His fixes his pinstripe suit collar as he walks slowly onto the lawn of the Campbell's, Amy's face construing angrily as she folds her arms and looks up to the heavens before saying..::/::.. Aimz:'You and your almighty Gods powers and stuff, GRRRR.' ..::/::..Upon seeing him, Forrester explodes wiht happiness, immediately going over to the man and shaking his hand merrily, both looking incredibly happy to see each other as Forrester says..::/::.. Forrester:'GLEN, HOW HAVE YOU BEEN?! It's so good to see you? How's Greed Bastard Enterprises going?' Glen:'Incredible Mr.Campbell...our stock rose by ten trillion and I've just bought some new land...it's called 'Afgagana' or 'Afghanistan'? Whatever the name, I'm just gonna nuke all those turbaned guys and make it a BIG shopping mall...there's gonna be no ugly bearded people at my mall.' *Looks over to Amy* 'Hello Amy...your looking lovely as usual.' Aimz:'Thank you...and your still the same enflamed a**hole I know you as.'Forrester:'AMY! BE NICE!'Aimz:'Sorry dad...he's JUST an a**hole then.' *smiles*'..::/::..Forrester immediately dismisses the dry insult by Amy, Beverly going over to her daughter as Glen and Forrester continue their talking..::/::..Forrester:'So what brings you by?' Glen:'Well...wait, it's kinda hot.' *Goes into his pocket and unearths a wad of hundreds, fanning himself with them* 'Ahhh...better. Anyways, I came to see how you guys were doing since I was in town for this big thing going on in Nova Scotia...plus I wanted to meet the man who beat me to you, I must meet him.'Aimz:'Well don't you worry Glen...he's big, strong, charming, handsome...'Glen:'And apparently getting his a** kicked by enraged toddlers...he sure sounds like a find Amy.' Rent-A-Hero:'MAKE IT STOP MAKE IT STOP!! NO, NOT THE JUMP ROPE!! GYAAAAH!! AMY, I'LL BE OVER THERE IN A MINUTE...I GOTTA WAIT TO FEEL MY LEGS AGAIN!!'..::/::..As the chaos ensues within the ice cream truck, the Campbell's and Glen just shake their heads about everything that has just happened, the scene fading as loud THWAP sounds rip through the neighborhood, accompanied by painful wails from our hero as this installment ends.
