Shabazz Dreams... (Part 2)

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/16/02 When we last left the Civil Rights hero known as Jordan X Shabazz he was knocked out from a punch by a bunch of homeless men in the Delaware ghetto. Jordan and his friend, Dante Jackson, decided to come to the area for one reason and one reason alone, to convert these druggies and crack heads into law abiding, good citizens. When Jordan was knocked unconscious he had a dream about his upcoming match on this weeks Friday Fusion. John Crichton, El Diablo, The End, and Jaden were all there fighting it out. When Jordan woke up the homeless men had split, and Dante Jackson was just standing around on the streets of East Benton, Delaware. Any time spent in this ghetto was dangerous, and to add to the danger, it was getting dark now. The area was dirty, crack heads slept on the floor, drug dealers prowled the streets looking for somewhere to peddle their drugs. Dante Jackson was waiting for Jordan to get up, out of the corner of his eye Dante spotted two men. They wore flashy mink coats and baggy jeans. They were drug dealers. Paranoid, rich, drug dealers who had run the streets of Delaware for five years. This was their area. Their hood. Their domain. Everybody and anybody who passed through the small town knew these men, they didn’t play around. They thought that anybody who came to town and didn’t meet them must have been drug dealers trying to make a quick buck and get away without paying the Delaware ‘State tax.’ That’s right, these men were so crooked that they made anybody who sold drugs in the area pay a 50% tax. They called it the Delaware state tax. If you decided not to play by their rules, you were snubbed out by one of their many guards. They don’t play around, that’s for sure. Shabazz was just getting to his feet, and Dante was leaning on the wall of a run down crack ridden shop, when the two men approached. They were bigger than they seemed. One of them wore a pink mink coat, and the other wore a blue mink coat. Gucci Sunglasses adorned each of their faces, and gold teeth was in their mouths. Blue coat wearer: “Hello children. My name is Juan Don, and my boy over here is Kevin Don. We’re brothers. We run this area, and if y’all is here to straight sellin’ yawls smack, we needs some tax. Fifty percent.” Dante Jackson and Jordan Shabazz look confused. Jordan has just reached his feet after being locked away in a dream wrestling match, and he didn’t know what was going on around him. Dante Jackson: “Don’t get it twisted playa, we just came through town because Jordan wanted to head over to the community center to say a few words to some of the crack heads around here.” Kevin Don looks surprised. Kevin Don: “What kind of words?” Jordan cuts in and answers for Dante Jackson. Jordan X Shabazz: “I’m a Civil Rights Activist, and I encourage African Americans around the world to get edumicated, and to not do drugs. I also encourage them to portray themselves as intelligent, non-violent people. So I was just going to head over to that community center and tell them that this area is not the key to happiness and the future success of African Americans in this world.” As Jordan finishes, the two men, Kevin and Juan Don, go into a small conference. They are contemplating on what to do with Jordan X Shabazz and his loyal friend, Dante Jackson. They finish their conversation and turn back to Jordan. Kevin Don: “We’ve decided that we’re going to take you both to our headquarters. We’ll search you, and if you’re clean, we’ll let you guys go.” Jordan and Dante couldn’t argue with that. They didn’t want to take the chances with these two gangsters. They two men waved for Jordan and Dante to follow them. Jordan and Dante had yet to speak to each other since following these guys, they weren’t looking, so they began to whisper. Jordan X Shabazz: (Whispering) “Dante, I say we start running back to the car and get the hell out of here. I think they might kill us. You got anything on you, a weapon or something?” Dante Jackson ignores Jordan, knowing that any plan this Civil Rights Hero concocted wouldn’t go over well. It’d probably end up getting those two drug dealers angry, and have Jordan and Dante each wind up in a body bag. As Dante, Jordan, and the two Don’s near the small community center, the intoxicating smell of marijuana hit’s the men. Jordan X Shabazz whispers to Dante Jackson. Jordan X Shabazz: “If I don’t make it out of here, make sure that you get John Crichton for me. And make sure you tap Jaden’s ass.” Dante Jackson looks at Jordan X Shabazz, and he shakes his head and sighs heavily. Jordan X Shabazz always says something inappropriate at a time where seriousness was needed. But I guess he couldn’t help himself, Jaden and John Crichton were weighing heavily on his mind. The attack that had busted his head wide open on Fusion. He was in trouble right now, but that was the thing that he kept bringing his mind back to. The opportunity to make John Crichton and Jaden pay for the attack that they perpetrated on him was what he wanted. He honestly didn’t know if he was going to hit Jaden. She smacked the shit out him, he was embarrassed on national television. But now he had to concentrate on getting out of the position that he was in, no matter what Crichton, Jaden, End, and El Diablo would do to him the following Friday. The entered the Community Center, and as expected, the place was like a crack house. Women who are obvious prostitutes are walking around freely, showing off their goods. Homeless men sleep on the floors. A game of pick up basketball is going over at the far net, the net has no mesh. The floors needed a washing, they were dusty and dirty. Dante Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz followed the two men up a flight of stairs and into the place they called their office. It was a nice, quaint place, much nicer than anything in the actual community center. There’s a soda machine, a large television screen, a food full of table. Three laptop computers, and a whole lot of furniture. Only a few people are up stairs, they are attired like Kevin and Juan Don. Mink coats, and gold teeth. All smiles. Jordan and Dante are lead down a dark and winding hall way. They had no idea where they were going, he could be taking them down this hall to interrogate them, or he could be taking them to a secluded room to torture them by making them watch hours and hours of John Crichton matches. As they reached the end of the hall Jordan and Dante realized that they were going to be searched, and then let them go free. As soon as they enter the room, they realize their surroundings, a group of people smoking marijuana are in the room. Jordan and Dante are told to sit on the couch. They do as they are told and relax on the couch. After a few minutes two large African American males come to them and ask them to stand up, both men are checked for drugs, none are found. Before Jordan and Dante are told that they are free to go, they are told that they can stay and smoke if they want. Jordan, being the one who wants the entire African American race to look intelligent and not fall into stereotypes that the white man has set forth. Telling blacks not to spend time in drug ridden groups, not to go to jail, to be a law-abiding citizen. Dante Jackson wanted to stick around to watch a baseball game, so Jordan decided to stay, as long as neither of them touched any of the marijuana, they were fine. After a while, the smell of marijuana became too much for Shabazz to withstand, the secondhand weed smoked knocked him out. Now we enter the dreams of a Civil Rights Maniac. Through the eyes of Shabazz. Argh, I’m trippin’ again. Haven’t been in this dream world since I was 14. I look around me, I see a battlefield. I’m crouched down on the ground. To my immediate right is Jon Kano and Rush Mason, both dressed in army style fatigues and both brandishing some semi-automatic rifles. To my immediate right is Duke Williams, smoking a cigar and holding a semi-automatic rifle. In my hands is a rifle. We are crouched low to the ground looking over a small hump of land. I peek over. About a hundred yards away I see Tyler Nelson and Xavier Kannon. Screaming at a bunch of men on the ground, trying to fire up their troops. I hear screaming and a group of horses trample the Ultimate Authority and head directly towards us. It’s The Political Machine, all on horses with pistols. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Five perfect shots floor the Political Machine. Each member down to the girl in a violent collapse. The horses squeal loudly as they are shot from the side. Ricky Thunder, Supreme Machine, ‘Scandalous’ Ryan Stephens, BloodAngel, Hiro Saotome, and the man leading the charge, that pantyhose wearing freak, The Phantom Booker. The immediately move towards us after taking down the Political Machine. They run on foot, almost nearing us, Duke fires first… and he misses. I turn to my left and see that Rush and Kano are now firing at the Phantom Phive. One of those two nails Saotome in his shin, he goes down. The gun battle continued for a few minutes with both groups trading heat. We had gotten up and tried to run to another area. As we were running I was watching or backs. I saw the Phantom Booker aim, he aimed right at Duke Williams. He shot, I was right beside Duke, so what I did was move over a few feet and step in front of the bullet before it hit Duke. I was wearing a vest, but it still hurt. End of Dream sequence. Shabazz wakes up, and immediately thoughts ring throughout his mind. Would he take a bullet for a fellow Malice Crew member? Would he risk his life to save another member of his troop? Would it be worth it if he did? Would they be grateful? Did Duke’s talking about the Malice Crew needing to stick together make Jordan actually care about people outside of his race? We all know that Shabazz can work with white people, but is he developing a tolerance for them as friends? He was technically high, so does that mean that in his dreams taking a bullet for somebody, and in essence being a hero, was his goal? Shabazz always wanted to be remembered as somebody who fought for his people, were Duke, Jon, and Rush now Jordan's people? He said it before, 'I fight for the black man, and the Malice Crew, that's it.' Dante Jackson was shaking Jordan. Dante Jackson: “Hey, the game is over, the Astros won!” That was insignificant, but it was signifying that it was time for Jordan and him to leave. Thoughts of The Phantom Phive, The Ultimate Authority, The Political Machine, and the Malice Crew rang through Jordan X Shabazz’s mind as the scene fades.
