The First (And Last?) Shill (AKA Golly, Momentum and Root Beer make a good combination)

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The First (And Last?) Shill (AKA Golly, Momentum and Root Beer make a good combination) Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 22, 2002 I sit alone at the table in the cafe down the street from the arena, still in a state of mixed emotion from the events of Turmoil, aching, embarrassed, but triumphant...Tomas tried to break me, thought that she could throw me off with the horrid Annie bit, but all it did was get me just a little more riled up to kick her ass..And once I got in that ring, I showed 'THE American Woman' what a Canadian man could do. And one more time, it was the same old story with her, she tried to get away from me, even pulled the ol' Trapezoid card when her weak little Southern Comfort couldn't do the job.. Once more, the sweet sound of an outmaneuvered opponent tapping her heart out...And what could be finer than getting to hear that sound once more with that hopeless Heavy Metal Harry? I really shouldn't write them off so quickly, but it's just so easy to feel superior when you win in such dominant fashion in the second match in one night... However, there's always a downside...Won two matches, got a Cruiserweight Title shot and a nice little boost to my pride, but for all the effort, still didn't manage to stop the damn HIT from getting the chance to rain on my parade again...He's ruined my chances at every damn title except this and the Extreme now, and missed on the Extreme only because he doesn't have the talent to beat Bob Vurdo... I've tried everything I can to rain on his parade and stop him from getting this far, and yet circumstances get in the way..He lucked out against Xavier, that damn Tomas helped cheat Brandi out of the chance to stop him by keeping me from being able to help stop Playaz Inc. from running in as they always do, he even managed to fluke out a win over one of them thanks to that bloody Gaz Green and his cheap buzzers. I'll have to ask that they be banned from the Tag Title match next time I see Ellie~!..Of course, one of the next times I see her I'll probably have to keep her from getting herself killed in the Alamo Match..Makes me wish that Xavi'd just decided to defend Snapple himself, instead of getting me to help out, but that's besides the point..It reminds me, and I slip a can of A&W Root Beer out of my bulkier briefcase, functioning in unique fashion as a cooler..The staff pay no mind, they've already been reimbursed sufficiently to overlook this breach of protocol as I pull a couple more from the case..'Who wants a free root beer? It's already paid for!' A 'me!' is heard, and a can tossed quickly, but most of the patrons are still befuddled...Damn French-speaking, it's like trying to advertise in Quebec...'Um...Tu n'est pas perde pour le bon Ah et Double-Vik, er, Beer de Root!' A rousing cheer bursts forth from the remaining early-morning drinkers, the message received as well as one could hope as several men got up from their seats, walking over to receive their complimentary drinks... Might as well do a decent job of shilling for the time being, not like I have to put the money into it myself when they're looking for the contract and I'm their means, even though to tell the truth, I don't know if I could win that match anyway, four in one night's awful difficult to deal with, though I did fairly decently with the double-booking on Turmoil..I was going to have to make a decision for the show here. There were four matches on my plate, and even I couldn't possibly win them all...I was going to have to give less than 100% at some point, and I didn't have much choice in the matter besides when to do so. If I gave it my all for the first three, I'd be positively burnt out by the Alliances match, and it'd probably just end up a world of pain at that point.. I needed to use the ol' reliable process of elimination here. The Alliances match was one of the foremost on my mind, for while most of PCW was entered in it, I had scores to settle all over the place..The hope for a measure of revenge against Tyler for firing me in the first place, in fact firing or trying to fire every member of the Front Office at one time or another..The bother that Valentine might put up, knowing that he was a good friend of the woman I had chosen to antagonize..And perhaps most importantly, the panic of having to deal with Team OSW if necessary. Mexi'd obviously never forgiven me for running him out of PCW, and I sure as hell wasn't going to apologize...Suffice it to say that if we met in the ring, I would have to be on my toes. Kid Wonder probably held great contempt for anyone in management positions that hadn't been him, Tempest had been seen with the Trapezoid recently, and then there was Ivan, the man who had Red Scared me on quite a few occasions..However, in spite of his rage toward the shape, he was dead-set on the Front Office and we were painfully aware...I couldn't afford to come in entirely out of it, or I wouldn't come out conscious. Thus, I had to keep some in the tank for Alliances.. This left three matches to chose from for my efforts...The Cruiserweight Title match was not a true option now, the Trapezoid's ego was sprouting out of his control from his earlier tastes of success against us and the only way to keep him in check would be victory, he could not be allowed to hold two titles at once or he would be an utter aggravation...This would be our first true one-on-one encounter, and I wanted to give it my all.. There was strategy required though, since he and I both had the Tag Team Titles on our mind in the match before, and neither of us would give up either without a fight, both of us fighting not only for the gold and personal pride, but also to help our respective partners gain the mental edge..One could argue that it's rather a gamble to put the full energy into the match, but we have a loss to make up for and momentum to gain going into the Cruiser tourney final should we win and inflict severe damage on their bodies and egos. Besides, I couldn't just let Brandi down, I was still worried she might be angry from the pin counted in error on my part, and that's no good for anyone.. There was no way about it. I wasn't going to win the Alamo Match, not with three other matches to give the maximum effort to on Sunday. In fact, I was probably not going to be in the running..I was only there in all honesty to keep Ellie~! safe, and so I would have to take only the measures necessary to ensure that. However, this was where the Trapezoid might be looking to take advantage if he realized that I wasn't in a position to put effort in...However, the more effort he put in here, the less effort he would have when it truly matters... It was probably worth the risk at this point. I just plain wasn't going to try my hardest in the match, besides, I already considered myself a winner for the arrangement..There might be a lot of free root beer going to the general public, but I could probably safely consider myself set caffeine-wise for up until the next endorsement match with the remainder I'd hoarded... In the management business, you tend to learn a few good tricks, after all, there's always gotta be some things passed down from one generation of executives to the next... But it's the ones you keep up your sleeve, much like the root beer itself, that are the sweetest treats of all. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
