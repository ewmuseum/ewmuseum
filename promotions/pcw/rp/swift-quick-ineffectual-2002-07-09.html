Swift, Quick, Ineffectual

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th July Quick OOC Note: This would have been longer, and sooner out…but given the circumstances forced by my computer, this is all I could achieve right now. Sorry. Messiah-ism is Dead – The ‘Not-so Surprising’ episodes…… RP#1 of 1 for Tuesday Turmoil, 9th July 2002… Location: The Jam – Local bar and Mecca for all manner of strange folk – Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Chapter One – Quick, swift and ultimately ineffectual… ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Some Parable. Some Story. It’s meaningless and dull, and my life has no true meaning. I’ve been forgotten, lost in the deep and foreboding shadows, where the goblins love to lurk, for three months. Obscurity is my pal, I know him well…like the eternal underdog that I am – consigned to a fate of watching self-righteous and undeserving cretins by the name of Xavier Kannon and his ilk stealing the limelight that was once mine. Three months ago, I was World Champion; I had everything going for me after winning at Spring Fling. How could it have all gone pear-shaped? How can it have gone belly-up like the careers of a multitude of former PCW jobbers? Let’s face it, it was quick, swift and ultimately ineffectual. It failed to put a large dent into the mass decline of the PCW, simply because it didn’t last, it didn’t linger. But what’s negative thinking? Dustin would call it ‘Stupid’, but on that notion, Dustin calls everything ‘Stupid’…even himself. This is my parable. My story. My story of how it all disappeared into the furthest reaches of Obscurity-Ville – home to a bazillion repressed former heroes….:: “DAMN THOSE INFERNAL SATANIC JOBBERS! They just had to rain on my parade, that beautiful return to the sheer brilliance of Chris Chase. Oh, who the hell am I kidding? It was shabby, hardly worth of any merit. And I was World Champion? Jeez…I need to work harder, push the very boundaries of space and time to produce something more amazing, even more so than talking to the God in my toaster. Yes, Yes…I know, I failed you again…what do you expect? Blasted deities…” “You bought ANOTHER toaster? Jeez Chris…you must definitely be suffering from post-returning trauma…or I’m a dancing queen in a leotard. Either those, or the laugh-out loud humor of Dustin Downey is beginning to wear a little thin on those gods of the populous. I hate to ask a stupid question, but are they watching now?” “~(Audible sound of hand slapping own face)~” “Alright then Chris, I’ll go fetch you yet another toaster oven, so you won’t have to result at throwing insults at me. Now, don’t you look at me like that…I know you didn’t do anything of the sort. But then again, punking yourself out like that is only another anti-moron trait of yours. Shall I go now? Yes, I think I shall…” ::. A jumbled piece of organic flesh and multiple attitudes, I’ve been baking slowly in the realization of the very fact that there’s been little more than a minor progression in the PCW promotion. And at four hundred degrees Celsius, the products finally turning brown on the top. Stuck between avenging the blatantly disrespectful attack by the Dark Ages on yours truly during my much touted return on the Friday Fusion card. A card that might not have been much more, if not for the reappearance of Chris Chase on PCW television. Perhaps a changed Chris Chase. Certainly – I talk to my toaster quite often these days. Obscurity has meant that the churches and cathedrals of this world have too denounced my formerly prominent position as a religious role model to the masses. So, these days, God contacts me through this toaster. He doesn’t have much to say at times, in fact, he remains silent until the chance comes to thoroughly berate me for the failures that I’ve made, tainting the name of the almighty. Sure, it isn’t my fault…it’s the fault of those damn Satanic Jobbers. Satanic Jobbers – the bane of Religious gimmicks across the board. They’ve crucified me before, why not you next? Relinquishing my hand from my forehead, I poke a piercing glance around my surroundings. The atmosphere of a beer drowned bar is enough to send the senses spiraling into a frenetic spin, flinging themselves around my head, dipping, diving…making my own semi-drunken state appear somewhat paralytic as my head hangs underneath the burden of my own brilliance. The signs of fatigue are already showing in a man that should be beyond healthy…given his lengthy vacation from all things wrestling. But with the intervention of the Dark Ages, I’ve been left at around 50%. Fifty percent – half of what I’d truly be capable of. At least that’s more than enough to beat a character such as Corey Williams. Beside me, sits the aforementioned toaster, gleaming in all it’s toaster glory – with a thin ray of light shining down on it. Wincing as I attempt a smirk, a shout of an all-too familiar idiot manages to find my ear. I spin around, if only to experience the true brunt of Dustin Downey’s moronic power….:: Dustin: Now then, I didn’t manage to come across another toaster oven in my intrepid travels. But LOOK what I found! It’s Billy Ray Cyrus…famed Country and Western singer. And it’s his job to give you good reason to laugh and feel all jovial again…just like you used to. Chris: ~(sighs)~ How was it that I used to feel all jovial, Dustin? Come on…was I ever in a undeniably happy and overly-perky mood? Face it Dustin, I’m just not cut out to be the kind of person who bounces off the walls whilst simultaneously doing the Macarena with a persistent grin fixed to my face. If anything, I was put on this planet to mope around and claim that I’m the greatest living wrestler ever…do you consider that JOVIAL? ::. Dustin shrugs, continually directing glances towards the toaster that resides beside me. The stench in here’s unimaginable. Well, maybe you could imagine the stench, but you’d be sucking food out of a straw for the rest of your life. Apparently, the toaster’s fond of Scrabble. I’ve never experienced the game myself, but suffice to say, I’d probably spend the majority of the time coming up with synonyms for ‘brilliant’ or berating statements of enough worth to bother Xavier Kannon with. Scrabble, Stenches, and seething morons aside, I gaze up at Dustin, through the thin fog that envelops us. An ill-fated transatlantic flight is what has bound the two of us together. The fact that we both managed to survive a verbal thrashing from a giant head is beyond the understanding of most people. In fact, most people are dumb. They fail to realize the significance of an existence of higher beings in this world, the giant heads being one of them. I hate to randomly rant like this, but it really must be done. The Giant Heads aren’t too fond of Scrabble, they aren’t too f**king fond of Satanists either. They consider the Satanists to be a little distasteful, somewhat of an understatement if you ask me. Understatements, are indeed, what I’m about. I’m an understated wrestler, in an understated federation…and I blatantly make understatements. To say that Corey Williams was mildly responsible for the degradation of the PCW World Title is probably the best example that I could come up with in a mere ten seconds….:: Dustin: You know Billy, ever since that time when I used you for live bait to lure in Josh Hale, I’ve been hanging around with thinking people. Like Chris here. As you can see, he’s thinking so hard, he’s not even paying attention to whatever I have to say. Chris: Yeah, it’s not like I totally ignore you anyway, my freakishly moronic comrade… ::. Dustin typically shrugs off this typical Chase-esque sarcasm, almost as if he had done so many times before. In the grand scheme of things, this matters little. It’s to be expected from a downed leader and his culture-shocked comrade. Holding up a pamphlet in his right hand, shaking it with much enthusiasm, Dustin deftly shoves Billy Ray Cyrus, the ineffectual distraction, away…pushing the thin fog away with a sweep of a hand. Coughing, he places the pamphlet down on the table, blabbering on with much aplomb, like a hyperactive salesman….:: Dustin: I’m glad I can be of service to you. Oh yes, and I took the liberty of ordering you the latest version of the ChaseTron Automated Defense/Offense system. The ChaseTron 2003 – the Cutting Edge in Chris Chase Based technology. All rights Reserved – Downey Corp. ::. With a cynical smirk showing across my face, I smack Dustin with a light backhand, sending the former PCW2 Cruiserweight Champ reeling….:: Chris: Why it’s nothing more than a glorified toaster! Do you know just how much I despise this fatally flawed piece of machinery Dustin? It was a totally futile replacement for yours truly, it wasn’t able to truly rid Calgary from Scientology…and Snow was never the same afterwards. And that was after you nearly destroyed the blasted thing by mashing it with your own SKULL. Dustin: Well…how was I supposed to know that was going to happen? Yes, I may be a moron, but I’m a professional wrestler and VCR expert too…and there was definitely no real warning that that was going to happen. Besides, wasn’t that whole thing just a set up to lure some Scientologist jobber out of Calgary? ::.I nod, a habit of mine that continues to be as incessant as the overly arbitrary comment made by my other former tag team partner….:: Chris: Don’t remind me… Dustin: Oh, and I forgot to bring this up at last week’s Leader-Sidekick meeting…is there any possible chance of a holiday whilst you thoroughly thrash the man that was responsible for those gay rumors? Chris: ~(Sarcastically)~ Oh give me strength… To be continued…
