E2 going to E3: More Spotlight Part 1

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 23 - 03 Every year a venue is held called the Electronic Entertainment Expo or E3 for short. At this venue numerous video games titles and other electronic devices are put on display. Some which are Madden 2004, Halo 2 and many other game titles for the many game systems. With the name of the whole show being E3 I think everyone should suspect who would be there or who would want to be there for this grand spectacle of games. E2 at E3? It’s only proper right? Guess again. The two men are left out in the cold on this one and do you think they would just take that lying down? Do you think they would just let it pass them by without a single care? If you answered yes than you must be completely and utterly clueless. If so then you’d be just like them. Preston: John can you believe this? I was just looking on this website and it says that tomorrow they are going to be starting an expo of some kind right here in Los Angeles and what? It’s called E3?! Gargano: E3? Let me take a look at that. John runs over to the computer and bends down to read the page. His face glistens in the reflection of the screen. John moves in a little closer to fully understand the paragraph. As soon as he finishes he sits up and looks at Tyler than points to the screen while he begins to speak about the expo. Gargano: [Reading] well it says here…….the Electronic Entertainment Expo has become the industry's most important annual gathering place. It's still the premier event for doing business with an ever-growing number of qualified industry players. In fact, because more commerce and promotion takes place here than during the other 51 weeks of the year combined, E3 is far and away the year's most concentrated gathering of qualified industry participants. Over 60,000 of the world's most influential retailers, distributors, developers, investors and media gather here for three days of intensive deal-making. There's nothing else like it in the world………..Dude it’s the media!!! They’re all going to be there! Preston: So... Gargano: Bro….this is our big chance, you know how you always say we aren’t getting enough spotlight? Well…..all of the media are going to be there. What better spotlight do you want huh? Preston: Great idea! You’re a genius squire! Gargano: I’ve been trying to tell all ya’ll dat!! The next day ……….. John and Tyler find out the location of the expo and in a blink of an eye they grab there coat and run out the door to there car. Gargano: You want to drive or do you want me to? Preston: I’ll do it. Tyler jams the keys in the ignition and turns it abruptly. [Booooooo……Booo…..Bmmmmmmm…..Pffffft] The car stalls leaving both John and Tyler sitting in there leather seats with a confused look upon there face. Tyler turns it again but this time no noise, no nothing just silence leading into more silence. Preston: John……Did you have this car last? Gargano: [Puzzled] Yea…..Why? Preston: [Angrily] because you forgot to put gas in the tank actor boy! John puts his head down and then looks back up with somewhat of a smirk upon his face trying to make the best of the predicament they are currently in. Gargano: Well Ty look on the bright side. Now we get to ride one of Los Angeles finest transportation systems so… What’ll it be? Bus or Cab? Preston: [Sarcastically] Oh! Let me think either we get mugged or we have to smell that rancid cab smell? Oh Boy! Choices, choices, choices! Gargano: You seem a little cranky today; I’ll just go get a cab for us. John get’s out of the car leaving Preston with a fuming look upon his slender face. John runs over to the curb and flags down a yellow and black hunk of metal that screeches and stops in front of John and another Japanese man. Japanese Man: Mush a Toshiba yon a kajiba a tote mush mush. Gargano: Huh? Japanese Man: Mush a Toshiba yon a kajiba a tote mush mush. Gargano: Oh this is like one of those Crouching Dragon Hidden Monkey things right? Ok, Ok Let me try! John looks into the guy’s eyes and then makes his lips move without any noise coming out of them. While moving his lips with no noise he dubs in his own voice like you see in Japanese movies. Tyler watching from the car looks on then get’s out of the car and runs over to John. Preston: What are you doing? Gargano: Playing Ninja with this guy! Hiyyyaaa! Preston: Wait a minute! Gaz gave me something for translating stuff; you know that thing he used for Miss Moonlight. Ah! Here it is, let me just turn this translator to Japanese and here we go. Japanese Man: Mush a Toshiba yon…….. [Words turn to English] You Mother@%$&! Move the hell out of my Mother&@%#ing way you @%^$sucker! Don’t make me choppy choppy your pee pee! John and Tyler just stand back out of the mans way in shock as the Japanese man hops into the back seat of the cab and the cab takes off suddenly leaving John and Tyler still standing there eyes bulged. Gargano: Well….I guess we are going to have to find another cab…
