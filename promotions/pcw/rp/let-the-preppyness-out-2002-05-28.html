Let The Preppyness Out.

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/5/02 There can be no doubt that having Eleanor Hall by his side has helped Xavier Kannon. From the day she joined him in NTICW he changed.... Inspired by someone with an even lower amount of respect for fellow human beings than he, he fell to even lower depths of humanity. But it doesn’t end at inspiring, she has also been his tag partner, getting involved in Kannon’s feud with Jeremy Howard, dragging Howard’s Girlfriend Jessica into it.... Then, at Spring Fling she went one step further.... By handcuffing Jeremy to the side of the Cage she took him out of the match and enabled Xavier to win the Continental Title. Come Supremacy it was a case of History Repeating as Eleanor made the distraction and brought the belt into the ring just as Duke was about to finish Xavier off.... One un-focused Duke Williams and one Drop-Out onto the Title belt later..... Xavier retained. There have also been the downs.... She has faced slurs by the likes of Jeremy and Duke, not to mention the accidental chair shot she took from the PCW President.... But somehow she always seems the scrape through... ...But has Ellie~! met her match now.... ....In her own Preppyness? Surely not.... “Like, what is with that White coat? White is SO not the new Black.... And that hair? Like... What...EVER!” Our shot opens up in the bedroom of Xavier Kannon’s Hubbardville residence, situated within the exclusive Kelly Preston Court development on the upper East side of the custom made City. In the king size bed lies Kannon’s valet Eleanor Hall.... But all does not seem well.... Eleanor : “Like...... Like...... Like..... So..... So.....So..... Like....” She is on her back, facing up to the ceiling with glazed over eyes and a cold sweat trickles down her forehead as she shivers under the sheets, muttering to herself.... Eleanor : “What.... EVER..... What...... EVER..... What..... EVER....” The hair falling over her forehead is matted down with her sweat, and her eyes have not blinked in nearly a minute as they keep fixed on nothing in particular. She begins to fidget more, snaking from side to side and nearly pushing the covers off the bed. Eleanor: “You.... Like..... SO.... Total..... LOSER!” Her head now starts to sway from side to side and she mouths words she doesn’t speak, her eyes starting to roll back. “I don’t think I have ever seen a case this serious in my life..... I don’t know if I’ll be able to help her....” We pan away from Ellie~! to show wrestling’s only Scientologist, Xavier Kannon looking on concerned at his feverous valet. Beside him stands a Doctor, bemused by the severity of Eleanor’s condition and not looking too confidant that he can cure it. Xavier : “There has to be SOMETHING right?..... She won’t last much longer unless we can cure her, her own Preppyness is driving her insane!” He Doctor shakes his head, almost in defeat at the situation. Doctor : “This is the single worst case of Preppylitus I have ever witnessed.... I’m surprised she is doing this well...” Kannon looks with solemn eyes towards Eleanor who is still shivering, cuddling the sheets around her and still muttering. Eleanor : “Like.... SO.... Like..... SO!” Kannon and the Doctor turn a shoulder to Eleanor and lower their voices to talk about her condition. Xavier : “What CAUSES this?” Doctor : “Well, as you know Preps need to vent their Preppyness at regular intervals... Make someone feel bad about themselves, mock their hair, the way they dress, their parent’s car... Things like that...” Kannon nods along, taking it all in. Doctor : “This isn’t just because they are bad human beings.... It’s a biological need.... A build up of Preppyness is a VERY dangerous thing.... I can’t recall the amount of times I have had to treat Repressed Preppyness by introducing a nerd to their room to “tap” their backlog of insults, mocking and general put downs...” Xavier : “There must be some advances in Prep medicine..... SOMETHING we can try, I can’t just give up on her....” The Doctor looks deep in thought, trying to think if there is anything he can do while looking with sympathetic eyes at the ill valet before him who continues to shiver and murmur. Eleanor : “Eugh.... Your.... Clothes..... Like...... So....... Suck.....” The Doctor closes his eyes and turns back to Xavier, opening them as he makes eye contact with Kannon. Doctor : “There is one thing we can try...... It’s extreme medicine but I can only think of one thing that could possibly cure a case of Preppylitus this potent..... But it will carry some risks.....” The Gold Patron Meritorious mulls it over, looking to his valet and then the Doctor before slowly nodding. Xavier : “....Go.....On....” Doctor : “Well.... If we can find something that she just cannot resist mocking.... It has to be more than a bad hair style.... More than someone wearing last season’s shoes.... Not even someone with a squint of a stutter will do it.... It has to be something SO ridiculous that she can let out ALL of her repressed Preppyness!” Cue dramatic pause.... *DUH-DUH-DUH!!!* .....Both men stare to each other, wondering in their heads what could be ridiculous to such high levels that it can draw out, by the osmosis of mockingness, the built up Preppyness inside his valet. Doctor : “Bur surely, with case this bad there can be nothing so mock-worthy to ridicule...” With his valet’s very life on the line, a light seems to go on behind Kannon’s bright Green eyes as he looks over to Ellie~!, hope in his eyes... Xavier : “Yes there is Doctor..... Yes there is....” Xavier hurries over to the bed and crouches, taking his valet’s hand and looking right into her eyes. Xavier : “Eleanor... Look at me.... Ellie~! come on, you have to listen...” She turns around, her eyes sleepy but showing that she can here him. Eleanor : “Like.... Like...” He pulls her long, wavy Brown hair from her face and talks right to her. Xavier : “Ellie~!.... You have to hear this..... This is who is in my match on Friday, the School’s Out Match..... The Rush...” With this one name something seems to click with Ellie~! and her eyes look like they are finally focusing on Xavier rather than staring through him. Eleanor : “Rush.... Like..... SO..... Job..... To.......ELLIE~!” The Scientologist turns to the shocked Doctor and mouths ‘It’s working...’ Xavier : “Yes, yes he did.... But there is more..... Anna Keyson..... Jaden...... John Crichton.... Streak....Miyajobber.... Andy Murray...” As Kannon reels off names Ellie~! can hold her Preppyness in no longer and finally, every snide comment, every mocking, every bitchy comment that has been stored up inside her is released in one huge whirling dervish of Prep. Eleanor : “KEYSON.... Like, hello? Lesbianism is SO not in, and Purple was like 2 seasons ago last season! And what is with her nickname? ‘The Darkness’... Take a little bit of the Darkness called EYE SHADOW, that way you’ll look like less of a guy!” Xavier gets up from his crouching position and looks over to the Doctor who is amazed at what he is seeing, the worst case of Preppylitus ever being cured before his very eyes. Eleanor : “...And as for Jaden... Like... EUGH is all I can say without the risk of catching some of her manliness..... Crichton? Like... WHATEVER? Has he HEARD of a smile or something.... EUGH.... Then, like, STREAK? Who is Streak? Someone legally change his name to “Generic Eric”... It’s something a lot more fitting!.... Please, these people are, like, SO nobodies!” As Ellie~! shoots off her mouth she seems to be cured.... Her pale Blue eyes are back to life and her cheeks have colour to them again. Her hair shines with life and goodness, and the cold sweat has gone from her forehead, she even sits up, huddling the covers around herself as she rants.... ....The camera pans to show Xavier and the Doctor, both nodding with approval as the shot begins to fade out. Doctor : “Wow..... I suppose the Rush, Keyson, Jaden, Crichton and Streak were the only things ridiculous enough to vent her Preppyness!” Kannon nods, looking with satisfaction at his valet of old returning with all her bitchy splendour. Xavier : “Yeah.... She’s back to her old self and she still hasn’t got to Miyajobber!” Our shot fades of Black as we pan back over to the full of life Ellie~!, still spurting out her pent up Preppyness to the room. “And, like, SO don’t even GET me started on MIYAJOBBER....” Black.
