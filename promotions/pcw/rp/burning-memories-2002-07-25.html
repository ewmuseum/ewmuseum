Burning Memories

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-02 6:00. My watch lit up, illuminating the time in the fading light. A brisk wind set in, chilling my bones, but I did not quicken my pace. I stopped for a moment to gaze upwards. Ah, the sun setting over the horizon was a wondrous sight. The orange-ish glow reminded me of the evil setting inside myself. The evil that would bring me to the top… As I rounded the corner of the block, my eyes fell upon a view seen so many times before. The abandoned shop. The shop that, just like my heart, had once stood out amongst the rest, had beamed with goodness. The shop that now lay in ruins. I walked closer until I was right beneath it's shadow, staring into what seemed to be the eyes of a demon. A demon that I knew quite well represented my past. But I wanted to forget this demon, to leave it behind as I trailed along on the path to glory, and so I knew what I had to do. I had to destroy it. Not only that, but I had to destroy everything it represented; all the memories and feelings that belonged to this building. The aforementioned is not excessive personification, for this building truly held a key to my soul. This building… this building had been the home of the greatest thing to ever impact me. The greatest thing to ever raise my spirits. The greatest thing to ever hurt me. This building… it was the home of the WITs. In the past, the WITs had been there. Although I looked after them, it was they who protected me from the dangers which to I was blind. I would have given my life to all three if asked; they were more than students to me, they were children. Thinking back, I remembered the first day of their training. I had dared not make the selection myself; in fact I sent the man least trustworthy to choose the reluctant learners for my program. Lance Minion. We had been enemies for as long as I could remember, but that bond of hatred was more of a bond than I could say about anyone else. And so it was he who became my right-hand man in the training of the WITs. They were angry at first… the WITs. Angry at having their identity stolen. Being forced to train day after day towards some goal they couldn't even imagine. I mustn't think. I mustn't go back to that time when pain was felt through emotions… that time that changed my life. I wish I had never met them! I wish I had never become a warrior. Why must my life be so burdened with pain and anguish that I can barely go on? Is life this difficult to all others? No, it could never be true. But me… my life and my career go tumbling down the road to hell. Only now did I think of my career. Or what was left of it. All of my glory was gone. My gold. Stripped from me by a pitiful excuse for an opponent. Venneman Pendragon. Nevermind that I would get another shot Sunday at Wrestle Vessel. I didn't want another shot at the Rising Star Title. I wanted another shot at life. I burst from my moments of thought, and found myself standing inside the abandoned shop. Not just inside… but in the WITs' quarters. I looked at their beds, still freshly made. The scoreboard, on which I kept tallies of their achievements and disappointments. The Voice of Reason had told me to go back to the way I as. To go back to the strong-willed, heavy-hearted warrior that everyone had come to know. To fight for the rights of everyone, never leaving a cause until it was settled. I destroyed my Voice of Reason. The Voice of Evil told me to change my lifestyle. To become fierce and bitter, to follow my intentions and to grab the spotlight. It told me I must fight only for glory and self-enlightenment, forget about others, and concentrate on conniving ways to get what I wanted. I followed the Voice of Evil, but when it led me to defeat, it was not there to comfort me. Now I was alone in this cold world. Nobody to lean one, nobody to lean on me. There was only one thing to do. I had to destroy all of the terrible memories, and move forward. Start a new life. Forget the name of the Warrior… I must begin fresh. My accomplishments? Null. My friends? None. My life? Over. And begun at the same time. I looked around the room. It was now covered in sticky gasoline. The can of inflammable liquid dropped out of my hand and to the floor. In my other hand lay a matchbook. I broke off one stick, and brought it hard against the booklet. Hot fire erupted from its tip, and as it dropped to the floor, the house, the memories, and my soul burned with it. 'I am a warrior…' I said with my last words, '…no more. No more.'
