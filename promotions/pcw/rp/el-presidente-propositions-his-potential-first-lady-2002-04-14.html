El Presidente Propositions His Potential First Lady

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-14-02 (We are introduced to the skyline of Portland, Oregon on this a relatively cool Spring Day in the Northwest. A few thin cirrus clouds pass overhead as the sun is occasionally blocked and unveiled, casting an increasing warm glow onto the land and a bright reflection off of the Pacific waters. Moving into the city, we see a high number of cars moving around the highways for a Saturday, and of course, the newly acquired campaign bus of Frisco. The beautiful luxury bus decorated with Argentinean silk flags and paintjobs cruises along the Portland streets very slowly. On the roof, the high caliber machine gun controlled by a gunner in fatigues and sunglasses moves around cautiously, looking for any dangers. We are brought into the bus where the loud whir of an air conditioner can be heard, and nothing else. The bus is extremely quiet, with only Frisco and 6 of his secretaries and party members working on their individual projects. A few whispers in Spanish from some of the passengers and rustling of papers can barely be heard, but fails to draw any angry responses from our future dictator, who snores softly in his chair. Frisco is dressed in one of his fine FRISCACEMANI~! suits with a pair of dark sunglasses hanging off the tip of his nose. He has a smoking Cuban cigar in his left hand that is almost burned to the end and a small stream of slobber leaks out of the corner of his mouth and onto his finely groomed beard. Frisco snorts slightly and shifts his movement. The party members watch him carefully so they don't wake him up. Unfortunately, a loud ring emits from Frisco's jacket pocket, which makes the Hall of Famer jump in shock and hurl a string of obscenities.) Frisco: 'God-fucking-damnit! Who the shit turned on my pinche phone!? Dios fucking mio, I'm trying to get a little rest here, you dumb fucking pendejos!' (The party members panic and talk all at once.) Frisco: 'Callete!' (Everyone shuts up as Frisco sighs and wipes the slobber off his face. He shakes his head and takes the cell phone out of his jacket pocket. He takes a puff of his stubby cigar and jabs it into a platinum ash tray. Frisco answers the phone.) Frisco: 'What!?' (In a split screen, a chubby Hispanic man in a purple suit speaks to the presidential candidate.) Man: 'Francisco!' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Let me reiterate....WHAT!?' Benito Ferrales: 'Es Benito Ferrales, tu Secretaria de...' (Frisco rolls his eyes, which are half open and crusted over.) Frisco: 'In English, you dumb fucking wetback. Don't you realize I'm on PCW television right now? When I have idiots talking to me in Spanish, the stupid Americans and Europeans have a fit because they don't know how to learn another language.' Benito Ferrales: 'Eng--Oh, sorry, sorry, Mr. President. My apalashees!' Frisco: 'What is your emergency?' Benito Ferrales: 'Jur covenshon en Seattle has been cancelled....' Frisco: 'Why is that?' Benito Ferrales: 'This whole Valenzuela situation. They think jur starting a coup.' Frisco: 'Are you serious? God-damn-motherfucking-shitheaded--....' (Frisco looks over to one of his secretaries.) Frisco: 'What was the name of the dumbass Valenzuelan piece of crap who was ousted a couple days ago?' (Jorge Gonzales, Secretary of Cereal, Milk, and Weaponry speaks up.) Jorge: 'Erm, Chavez, I think.' Frisco: 'Yes, yes, of course. Thank you.' (Frisco continues his verbal assault on the phone.) Frisco: 'Dumbass, shitbrained, fuckface Chavez! How dare he let a coup come to pass while I'm trying to run a peaceful, and orderly campaign!' (A loud rip of machine gun fire can be heard from the rooftop.) Frisco: 'What the fu--!?' (The whir of machinery is heard as the bus' gunner drops from his turret into the center of the bus. The psyched Argentinean in military fatigues thrusts his fist in the air and yells.) Gunner: 'I got the cabron, El Mero Mero!' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Which cabron are you referring to?' (The gunner points outside to a small burning vehicle and a man running away from it.) Frisco: 'Um, what did you blow up now?' Gunner: 'Som' kind of Breetish car or santing. It had one of dos' stupeed long licess' plas' on it and de steering wheel was on de wrong side.' Frisco: 'Oh...okay then. Good work.' (The gunner nods and grins.) Frisco: 'Back to your post. I'm trying to make a call here.' Gunner: 'Si, Jefe.' (The gunner rises back up onto the roof as Frisco watches the small car explode and returns to the phone.) Frisco: 'As I was saying, I'm trying to run a peaceful, orderly, democratic campaign here. We all know I could easily take over Argentina in a bloody coup, as I have the full support of the military, but it's just too risky and potentially damaging to my professional wrestling career, as useless as it may be right now.' Benito: 'Jes, Jes, I know.' Frisco: 'Well, Chavez is getting his job back, right?' Benito: 'Uh huh.' (Frisco thinks for a second.) Frisco: 'He seems like a pushover. Do you think we should take Venezuela once I am elevated to dict--president? My understanding is that they have quite a bit of oil over there.' Benito: 'Bueno, I'm not jur advisor, but jeah, that might be good.' Frisco: 'You're right. We should start with Venezuela, generate some income with the oil, and then we can start planning for the takeover of the United Kingdom.' Benito: 'Ehhhh, sure.' (Frisco smiles and motions to one of his secretary who goes to the back of the bus to retrieve a drink.) Frisco: 'God, I am so fucking brilliant. Why didn't I do this whole presidential thing years ago!? I could've been retired from PCW and relaxing back home in Buenos Aires by now instead of risking injury and traveling to crappy American cities to fight...who the hell am I fighting on Sunday anyway?' Benito: 'I don't know, sir.' (Frisco peers around the bus for an answer. Juan Paliendo, the new Secretary of Scheduling, Ham Sandwiches, and Tortillas speaks up.) Juan: 'Miyagawa, senor.' (Frisco thinks for a second.) Frisco: 'The goggle wearing fucktard....are you serious?' Juan: 'Jes, presidente.' (Frisco chuckles slightly and recalls memories of his conflicts with Miyagawa.) Frisco: 'Ahhhh, this will be a nice chance of pace...Well, not really, as this will be the 3rd time I defeat him, but it should be fun.' (Frisco nods and receives a vodka martini from a party member.) Frisco: 'Gracias....Hey, is he still wearing those stupid ass goggles?' Juan: 'No, I don't think so...' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Bah! There goes my plans. What about this other guy...Xavier Kannon?' Juan: 'Uhh, Continental Title match, smacked ju around on Fusion...' Frisco: 'Whoa, whoa. Stop right there. Continental Title? I've won that hunk of crap twice. It has no meaning to me anymore. I pumped as much credibility into it as I possibly can. And smacked me around? Please. The reason why I didn't kill those two was because security didn't allow my guards into the building to drag them off into captivity. They're giving me shit about the whole thing. I have to call them later so I don't have any difficulty with capturing and importing Kannon or Miyagawa. Hell, Miyagawa wants out of the business anyway. Might as well give him a job as a slave in the new Frisco-led Argentina.' Juan: 'Jes, sir.' (Frisco realizes that the man on the other line is still blabbing on phone. He puts his ear back to it.) Frisco: 'Are you still talking?' Benito: 'Jes, jes I am. What should we do?' Frisco: 'About what?' (Frisco takes a sip of his martini.) Benito: 'Seattle! Seattle! What about the convention there?' Frisco: 'Screw it. We don't need Seattle.' Benito: 'We have a chapter there! They all want you to speak to them!' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'If they want to support me, let them travel to kiss my ass. Since when do I have to appease the convenience of my followers? That's just a sign of weakness.' Benito: 'Okay then.' Frisco: 'Yes, okay. Goodb---...' Benito: 'Wait!' (Frisco snorts.) Frisco: 'Yes!?' Benito: 'I got the number of...ju know.' (Frisco's eyes light up.) Frisco: 'Seriously?' Benito: 'Jes! Es [detail withheld]...' Frisco: 'Beautiful.' (Frisco writes the number down and hangs up. He then looks around at his party members and giggles gleefully. The party members smile at Frisco's uncharacteristic smile...which quickly turns into a scowl.) Frisco: 'You people shut your holes. I'm calling my Shakira. If I hear one remark or laugh, I will throw you out of the bus and give our gunner some target practice.' (The party members hang their heads.) Frisco: 'Thank you.' (Frisco takes a big drink of his martini and dials the number. He giggles again as it rings. Finally, the beautiful Colombian singer is shown on a split screen in a pair of sweat pants and revealing crop top.) Shakira: 'Hello?' (Frisco smiles widely as he speaks to his object of obsession.) Frisco: 'Hola, mi amor!' (Shakira's face drops as she shudders.) Shakira: 'Francisco!? What the fuck are you calling me for?' (Frisco jumps back.) Frisco: 'Why, my dear, what kind of greeting is that?' Shakira: 'It's a 'go to hell' greeting, cabron.' Frisco: 'Oh, honey buns, you have so much hostility in you! Release some of it, so I can put some of myself in you.' Shakira: 'Ugh. You disgust me. How did you get my number again? I changed it 6 times so you'd be thrown off track.' Frisco: 'I have connections.' Shikira: 'Besides the one from your brain to your dick? I had no idea.' Frisco: 'Well, I'd much rather have a connection from my dick to your pu--...' Shakira: 'Stop it.' (Frisco smirks.) Frisco: 'So, what have you been up to, my love?' Shakira: 'Working, asshole, what do you think?' Frisco: 'Well, weeping and masturbating to me would be my first guess, but I guess you have the time to squeeze your horrid performances in-between.' (Shakira frowns deeply.) Shakira: 'Screw you, Francisco. At least I have a life. You're still wrestling for a living and getting your ass crippled. You're not even a World Champion anymore. Just some mid guy. Nobody likes you.' Frisco: 'Touché, my dear, but I have other things besides wrestling. Have you not heard about my campaign?' Shakira: 'You mean you're serious about running for president of Argentina? God, you're more stupid than I've ever imagined. Nobody in their right mind would want you as a president. You're just a pinche no talent wrestler! Not to mention a prick.' Frisco: 'I am dead serious, babe! This is not some publicity stunt. I'm in my tour bus right now. And you are not aware of my educational background and immense popularity. That's all I need to run a country.' Shakira: 'Well, hopefully a group of rioting Argentineans will kill you if by miracle you get elected.' (Frisco giggles insanely.) Frisco: 'Oh man, you're making me hard!' Shakira: 'You're making me queasy. I'm going to hang up now. Stop calling me...I mean it.' Frisco: 'No! Shakira, baby, I wanted to ask you if you wanna be my first lady?' Shakira: 'Hell no! I don't care about you or your stupid country.' Frisco: 'We can take over Colombia too. After Venezuela and England, of course.' Shakira: 'You are one stupid pendejo.' Frisco: 'The offer is on the table. You know you want a man with power. I will have much power after being elected president of Argentina and it will make your panties ever so moist. I urge you to act now before I get the choice out of millions of beautiful women from the country or countries I will own.' Shakira: 'I doubt any of them would even touch you. You're a disgusting pig.' Frisco: 'I love you too.' Shakira: 'Ugh!' (Shakira slams the phone as Frisco smiles brightly.) Frisco: 'She so wants me!' (The party members try not to laugh. Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Agree with me, you worthless asshounds!' (The party members assure Frisco with a scattering of 'Si's and 'Yes's.) Frisco: 'Fuckin' A!' (Frisco sips his martini and closes his eyes as the scene begins to fade out.)
