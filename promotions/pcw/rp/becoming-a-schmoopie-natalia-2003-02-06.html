Becoming... A Schmoopie (Natalia)

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th Feb 2003 Separate apartments, separate hotels, this vacation feels more like a dirty weekend rather than a couples holiday. There I go again using the couple word, it makes out that we have a relationship, be it one other than business, using such a word would be kidding myself. Technically I’m still a married woman but my marriage certificate is simply meaningless, I haven’t heard from Alexander in weeks and I hear he is pursing other avenues. Last I heard, Alexander was showcasing his aluminium encased cranium in Old School Wrestling, his handicap enforced by the man I am about to have dinner with. Speaking of Gaz Green, the man is an enigma. The Jester never ceases to amaze me, he along with that fiend orchestrated the fall of Asylum and be it love or revenge he proposed that I join forces with Pranks With Attitude. Maybe I was being used as a way of spiting the man who gave them their big break only to oppress them, but Alexander brought everything on himself. “Was Alexander set-up though?” this question niggled me from the moment Gaz Green suggested my new role within the company. Before the eruption I saw how Gaz would flirtatiously toy with that jezebel Anarchy, there was certainly chemistry from a spectator’s point of view and the Jester is like Pandora’s box, full of nasty surprises. Did Gaz Green mastermind this elaborate set-up? Only he can answer that but I must admit, life as peeked since Alexander was kicked to the sidewalk. His final words via the telephone, he couldn’t muster the courage to say in person at the time have tormented me though. “I need to speak with you, but not over the telephone…” Is my client about to inflict the same punishment that I did to my own husband and kick me to the sidewalk? Have I become dispensable? After all he seems to have found comfort in the bosom of The Front Office and like a runaway locomotive he’s been soaring up their corporate ladder. I peered into the mirror and my hands rummaged within my make-up bag, removing a tube of lipstick I applied a coat of cherry red as these questions splashed to the front of my mind. Happy with the end result I felt compelled to add a violet eye shadow and the years were soon fading off me. Gazing into the mirror I was amazed at the end result. My make-up was in place and my hair is the picture of perfect courtesy of a local salon, its array of colours almost camouflaged me, call it the Alan Ackart effect. Who? I giggled knowing that Gaz would have retorted that very line with the same witty one-liner; it is as though we can read one another’s minds… I highly doubt that. If that were remotely true then I would have foreseen the resurrection of Pranks With Attitude, more importantly I would have tried to prevent such an act. With Gaz winning at Shanghaied the P.W.A franchise became his and I genuinely thought that he would’ve offered somebody on the roster the opportunity to headline under that very banner, a banner that won every tag team award of 2002. How many people would have turned down such an opportunity? With the likes of Venneman Pendragon and Skylar Green gracing the same company, there are two names that would’ve relished such a daunting task. A task of restoring Pranks With Attitude to their rightful place, be it PCW Tag Team Champions. I shyly raced to my bedroom and wandered my eyes over the garment that aligned my door. I was out to impress and hopefully the combination of this low cut dress and my make-up would have my client panting. Taking hold of my lacy attire I slipped it on and zipped up the back with a slight struggle, a few adjustments here and there, namely the top so I could get a desired cleavage and I felt like Cinderella going to the ball. “Oh, that’s what I’m missing!” Like Cinderella I needed a slipper, actually some stilettos. I dived deep into my wall cabinet and found the perfect pair, glistening silver coated high heels. I gasped knowing that the last time I wore these were during my honeymoon, ironically that was the last time Alexander and I ventured away together. I had second thoughts but Alex was now a distant memory so I slipped on the shoes and waltzed back into the living quarters, notably over to the life-size mirror where I evaluated the final outcome. I gave a smile and accompanied it with a blown kiss, how I felt like a teenager again… I walked over to the door; taking hold of the brass handle something didn’t feel quite right. Maybe it was the fact that after this vacation I would be put second place behind that, that bastard Nerezza who kidnapped me. I really don’t understand why Gaz would take back that hellhound; I keep thinking that it’s a cryptic message saying something to the tune of ‘I don’t need you anymore!’ It would at-least explain why Gaz needs to speak to me in private, be it dinner in a man’s mind would probably soften the blow but if all this comes to fruition then I hope he has insurance. Ironically maybe I will smash something over his cranium forcing him to have a metal plate issued, as they say, “What goes around; comes around!” Well judgement day looms. I sneered thinking that this maybe my last day in the ‘Conniving Collective’ my replacement, a man, no a being with no pulse or complexion. I trembled as I set foot through the door and out onto the landing, I was a nervous wreck, I had built Gaz up on this pedestal and now he maybe about to make me unemployed. Life, huh?!? I stood waiting for the elevator, tapping my foot to pass away the time, fidgeting I was so inpatient. “Hurry up!” I kept murmuring through my grinded teeth, when suddenly it arrived, a few strangers vacated the elevator leaving just me to descend to the ground floor. I pressed the button and immediately the doors shut with a thud and I could feel my descent, a subtle ping as we hit the ground and the doors parted like the Red Sea in that Moses story. “Where is he?” I thought as I walked out into the lobby. Twitching I frantically looked around but to no avail, where was Gaz? I smiled for the onlookers, I must’ve made an impression, but not for the person that matters. I squinted my eyes to look through the glass entrance way and could see a long stretched white limousine just coming in to park, oh, how I prayed for it to be my White Knight in shining armour. Continuing to watch I was let down, the person entering was actually a green attired knight, I sighed it was indeed Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green. - To be continued -
