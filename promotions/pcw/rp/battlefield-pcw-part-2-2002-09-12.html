Battlefield PCW....Part 2....

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-2002 ''Twas dawn upon my second day in the land of Gauze. News of mine arrival had spread like wildfire through the normally peaceful realm of Summerset, where I was currently encamped. As such, the numbers in my ranks had risen dramatically merely overnight, though I still knew not whose idea it had been to summon me once more to the land.....' ****FLASH**** The scene opens into what would appear to be Venneman Pendragon's bedroom. Why? Well, because there is a bed, a wardrobe and a pajama'd Venneman Pendragon sitting upon the edge of the bed, reading a large book of some kind. His attention is abruptly drawn from his book by the sounds of a commotion coming from within the wardrobe. Pendragon rises to his feet, moves across the room to a mantle where he picks up and unsheathes Excalibur. Cautiously he approaches the wardrobe, and in his voice of command he speaks.... Venneman: 'Ho there! Who be the swine who has infested mine wardrobe? 'Tis late and I require peace! Speak or face the wrath of Pendragon.... No answer, but the commotion continues. Pendragon shakes his head and grits his teeth together as he opens the door. The site was unexpected. The door seemed to open into another land, and people who would appear to be soldiers mill about, consumed in some kind of daze. Shields and weapons lie here and there upon the ground, and the camera is drawn to an upturned shield that bears the Pendragon sigil: a golden Dragon upon dark blue. Pendragon's eyes widen in disbelief at what he sees through what he thought was a mere wardrobe.... Venneman: What sorcery is this? This cannot be! 'Tis the land of Gauze! But, who would have the power to draw me through once more? Pendragon shakes his head but does not hesitate as he walks through the wardrobe and into a different land. Indeed there were soldiers milling about, and at the sight of Pendragon, the men light up in a cheer.... Men: HUZZAH!! THE KING!!! HUZZAH!! Pendragon looks around and smiles, for these are indeed his men, and more seem to appear from thin air with each passing moment. None are armed, however as each man materializes, so to materializes a pile that seems to contain each man's armor and weaponry. Pendragon looks to his left and is surprised to see his wardrobe. Now though, his wardrobe holds a note. Pendragon snatches it and looks it over.... Venneman: 'Two leagues to the west lies a camp that has been made ready for your glorious arrival. A new scourge has devoured the land, and you are the innocents' only hope....' Pendragon folds the parchment and places it inside his robe that he still wears. He looks around in awe at his men, who now number around five thousand. He whistles and uses his voice of command once more.... Venneman: MEN! RALLY ROUND AND HEAR ME NOW!! The men instantly spring to action, each one grabbing his pile of belongings and filing into ranks. Pendragon stands at the head of the columns and addresses the men. They look upon him with a kind of reverence that one might reserve for a god.... Venneman: I know not how it is that we have been brought to this place, though it is a land that I know in passing. Men, this is the land of Gauze. 'Tis not as our own world. Here, the old ways have been adhered to and technology has not yet gotten into the way. It is a land that has known mostly peace, but evidently now has come under some kind of distress. We are here to rid the land of the scourge that has set up a new regime, and to protect the innocent peoples of the land. Intelligence tells me that we have a camp already set up two leagues to the west. We need to get marching.... Obediently, the men begin the trek to the west, loaded down with their varying belongings. Pendragon smiles at the directness and swiftness with which his orders are carried out. He then frowns at his wardrobe. He shrugs and opens the door. This time, all that is inside are his clothes and a suit of golden armor. No doorway to another dimension. He reaches inside and grabs up his armor and a battle sheath for Excalibur that he belts over his robe. He throws the mail suit over his shoulder and reaches in for a large bundle of blue cloth. He smiles and shuts the door before trudging after his men... ****FLASH**** Scene changes to show a large encampment that sprawls through a green valley. The shot follows a determined looking Venneman Pendragon as he makes his way down the center walkway. He stops at a huge tent that bears his sigil. He smiles and takes a deep breath as the flaps fly open. Richard Smith, Pendragon's second in command, strides out and relieves Pendragon of his armor... Richard: My lord, we feared you would not come. Since I was one of the first to arrive, I took it upon myself to make camp and to send out scouts.... Venneman: Good Richard...What of the horses? Richard: They are corralled at the rear of the encampment. They appeared all at once, in the space that we had set aside. Amazing how this has happened...Do you...Do you know how we got here my liege? Venneman: 'Tis not my intent to question this sorcery, but I do no a little of why we are here. Someone in the land, someone powerful enough to have pulled this off, fears for the safety of the land and her peoples. I have been here before, and it is unlike anything we have encountered. The Land of Gauze is a reflection of our own land, but most directly, PCW...My present employers. The land is everything that we have longed for our land to be....Non technological, the land has stuck to the old ways. Richard: So are we here to fight a war then, my King? Venneman shrugs and looks off towards the horizon. He takes a sniff of the air and shakes his head.... Venneman: We are here to stop an injustice. If forced to go to war to end it, then it is war we shall have... Richard's eyes glaze over with what can only be described as battle lust.... Richard: Very good sir.... Venneman: You spoke of scouts...Have any returned? Richard: Nay, none as yet My King.... Venneman nods and takes back his armor before entering his tent. The flaps close and momentarily Pendragon reemerges. He cuts a striking figure with his blonde hair, his golden armor and his sword. Today, though, he wears a blue breastplate that bears the Pendragon sigil... Venneman: We need to know the lay of the land. Have we any maps? Richard: Actually, we do. The camp had maps already when we arrived. We appear to be in the valley of Summerset.... Venneman: Show me these maps, good Richard... The two begin to walk away when a trumpet sounds and hoofbeats can be heard approaching fast. A sweat slicked horse carrying a young soldier skids to a stop directly in front of Pendragon. The fellow salutes and climbs from his horse, knees shaking the whole time. Venneman: Calm down lad. 'Tis safe here. What has you so spooked? Richard: And where is Jonathan? Speak up, Matthew! Matthew: My lords...We scouted to the north as we were ordered. Everything looked fine until we crossed a massive ridge. Beyond the ridge was perhaps the largest army encampment I have ever laid eyes upon. We tried to turn back, but it was too late. We were overtaken by dark skinned cavalry and escorted to their camp. We were tied to poles in the camp's middle square and their leader, a man who calls himself Crush Jason questioned us.... Matthew's speech is interrupted by his own sobs. Pendragon puts a comforting arm around the lad... Venneman: 'Twill be alright son....Richard, get the lad some water... Richard runs off to fetch the drink, and the lad smiles gratefully at Pendragon and seems to draw some strength from the great man. He goes on in a shaky voice.... Matthew: I was made to watch as Jonathan was put to the question. Crush pulled his intestines from him as he screamed over and over that he knew nothing, which was no lie. Finally, I could take it no more and I told them that we were your soldiers....It ended Jonathan's misery as he was promptly beheaded with my revelation. Venneman: And yet, thou were spared? Matthew: Aye. I was bidden to deliver a message. Crush Jason wants you to know that you are in direct opposition to Cravier Dannon, and that Skyler Welson holds no sway here now. Should you proceed with your forces into the land, you will be decimated by The Dark Lord Crush Mason....Commander of the northern legions. He bids you to leave while you still can.... Pendragon's eyes cloud with rage over the young fellow's words, but he holds his calm and nods. Matthew quivers in fear but Pendragon pays him no mind.... Venneman: He would torture my men.... >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
