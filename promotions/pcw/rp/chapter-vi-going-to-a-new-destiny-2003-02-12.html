Chapter VI: Going To A New Destiny

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-2-03 ::Introduction:: There really isn’t much to say here this time. Just that after Mark was fired from PCW he was jobless and he is now trying to find a way to survive on his own and also to try and get his beliefs out to the public since PCW wasn’t enough for his promotion and his plan. ::End Introduction:: Chapter VI: Going To A New Destiny The bus rolls up to the side of the street as the cold wind picks up some more and Mark is sitting on the bench with his arms around his legs still and his legs are in his chest. He is fast asleep as his head is tilted to the right side on his arms. The bus comes to a screeching halt as it lets out a big gust of air from the pressure. The man who is driving the bus opens up the door and a big gust of warm air is let in from the fronts colliding together. “Hey do you need a ride?” Says the white bus driver as Mark is awakened by his voice. Mark’s head rises as he rubs his eyes so that he can get rid of all the fuzz that is surrounding what he is seeing. He squints his eyes as he puts his stiff legs down on the ground. He tries to stand up on his legs, but the first time he falls back down on the bench because of the stiffness. “Hell yes I need a ride out of here.” Mark says aloud as he sits on the bench stretching his arms. The bus driver shows his old wrinkly hand as he waves him into coming on the bus. Mark stretches out his legs as he yawns. He stands up once more and he starts to walk towards the opened door. His walk is still with a limp since his right leg is still bad and not to mention still stiff as hell. Mark starts to walk up to stairs on the bus as one leg by one leg he makes it up to where the driver is sitting. “How much for the ride man?” Mark asks the bus driver as he is looking him into the eye. “For you I will make a special discount.” The bus driver starts to say as he is looking at Mark in the eye right back at him. “Five Dollars is the price you have to pay.” Mark reaches down in his ragged jean pocket and pulls out a roll of bills from his last PCW paycheck. Mark flips through all of his bills until he comes to the five dollar section. He pulls out a crisp newly made five dollar bill and he hands it right into the wrinkly hand of the old guy. “Thank you sir and please take a seat anywhere and feel free to get off at any place you would like to.” The old bus driver says as he closes the door to keep the cold air out of the cozy bus. Mark starts to walk back on the black gripped mat as the bus driver puts the bus into the drive position and starts to take off. Mark is shaken a bit since the takeoff was a little fast, but then he is holding onto the seats making his way towards the back. He looks around at all the people who are on the bus and there are people from a lot of different minorities. “Can’t sit here!” The black man says as he covers up the seat next to him. “Like I would want to sit next to a fuckin coon like your stinky self.” Mark says as he walks by the black guy. Mark looks and he sees there is no one in one row of seats so he limps back there and he takes a seat three seats in next to the window. Mark places his face against the window and he can feel the cold air come out of the crack of the window. The cold air hitting his face feels that he is fighting the wind on a mountain as he is climbing up. “What am I going to do now?” Mark asks himself as he is looking at the bright lights of the night. “I don’t have a job, I am running short on money, and every black person in this country hates me. Well that last thing is the best thing that could ever happen to me.” Mark starts to think about all the negative in his life and finally he can’t take it anymore. He starts to hit the seat in front of him and all of the people on the bus. “What in the hell are all of you people looking at?” Mark asks repulsively as he notices everyone staring at him. Mark blows the people off that are looking at him and they all turn their heads towards the front of the bus and out the side windows. Mark looks up towards where the driver’s mirror is and he notices there is a digital clock up there. “2:35 am.” Mark reads as he is squinting his eyes to look up at the clock. The bus driver dims the lights in the seating part of the bus as he gets on the highway. Mark leans his head up against the window of his seat and he starts to have some more flashbacks like he was having on the bench. Mark closes his eyes as yet again he is traveling on the road, but not to anywhere special, just looking for a place to go and stop for a while so he can straighten some things up in his life.
