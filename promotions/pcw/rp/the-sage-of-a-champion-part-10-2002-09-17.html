The Sage Of A Champion: Part 10

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-17-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 10” Same - Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellSame - Day 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen :: The scene picks up from where we left off last time. Now we have Violent Ray, “The Bullet,” and how Captain Kirk is in the mix of all of this. How will all of this unfold? Keep reading. General P: Anyway, Kirk. Explain to me how you know this Bullet guy that Athens Mir, BladeD, and myself have been talking about for the past twenty minutes. Kirk: Bullet? Oh you mean Gun Shot? Athens Mir: Yea, but for some reason I have a feeling you don’t know personally. Kirk: You are correct. I don’t. How did you know that? Athens Mir: Well that is because anyone that meets him personally doesn’t refer to him as anything, but Bullet. Kirk: Oh… I didn’t know his name was Bullet. General P: Long story. All we want to know is how you got the information that Athens was on our side at the beginning of the war, so we know how the rumors got around. Kirk: Well, I am still trying to figure out how Athens is still alive, but anyway you guys know my friends down at the bar that I am trying to stop going to right? General P: Yes, go on. Kirk: Well, I am not sure exactly how they know Gun Shot. Athens Mir: Bullet. General P: Mir. Please. Don’t interrupt again. Athens Mir: Sorry. Go on Kirk. Kirk: Gun Shot. Bullet. Whoever. Anyway, I am not sure how they know this guy, but they told me that the guy told them that Athens Mir was on our side to retrieve something called the “Book Of Violent Ray” or something of that sort. Anyway, that is how I found out about Athens Mir being on are side. Even though those guys are heavy drinkers at times and even though they can be very crazy at times, when it is time to get serious they never lie about anything. No matter how little or how big something is, they wouldn’t lie about something if it meant their life. That is why I began to spread the rumors because I felt it was true that the guy was on our side and I didn’t want him getting killed. I mean, I had no idea what this book was or how important it was, but if it was so valuable that someone on our team would switch sides as a spy for the purpose of this book I felt it was something close to the means of “Metal Armor” and I had to make sure that no matter what happened Athens Mir wasn’t going to be killed… Now in disbelief I am staring right at him which means he can’t be dead, but I really did think he died which made me upset because I had no idea what was going to happen to the world, since he didn’t get the chance to retrieve that book. Ever since a year after the event I have felt bad because nothing else was heard of this book or anything of that sort and I felt that for once my friends told me a lie. That is why it became so easy for me to try and stop going to the bar after you had asked me that I need to spend less time there. I felt that I didn’t need to associate with them after they almost risked my career with something stupid like that. General P: Well, they did tell you the truth Kirk. That is why Mir is here. As for him being alive, you will have to be told that story another day. I was just wanted to know how you found out about all this and I wish you could have stopped the guys from attacking him because if we can trust Mir which I am still debating over. That book is what he was trying to get. Kirk: No kidding? Wow. Man, that makes me feel bad. I am glad I haven’t been hard on the guys or anything because I started to think about just going off on them, but something kept me from doing so, and I guess it was God telling me that they were telling the truth and I didn’t realize it. What is this book anyway? Athens Mir: Violent Ray is much similar to Metal Armor. Think of it as the “Everything you need to know” – How to build Violent Ray. Book. BladeD: Only, Violent Ray is about ten times stronger. Kirk: Ten times? OMG! That is insane!! BladeD: Yes, it is. That is why it is taking so long to create the machine. The stronger something of that sort is, the longer it takes to build. General P: So far we have been told that “Project Ray” has been worked on for almost three years now. The project started one year before the war we went into and as the war progressed, so did “Violent Ray.” We have also been told that the machine is almost completed and we really need that book… See the people in Russia are not the brightest in the bunch. We have been told that they are going completely by this book and they have no back up of this book. This book is the only one of it’s kind and there are no back ups anywhere to be found, so basically if we retrieve this book, then the world won’t have to worry about this “Violent Ray” being finished. Kirk: That does sound kind of stupid on the part of Russia, but that also sounds like something they would do. General P: Basically, what Mir was trying to do was retrieve this book, so we wouldn’t have to worry about Violent Ray. He was on the opposing side because he was going to try and get a metal of honor or something where he would have the power to be in the area of this book, so that he could retrieve and be on a first class trip back home. BladeD: I think that was a dump decision on your part Athens… You had no set or permanent plan. You were just hoping for the best results possible and not thinking about what if you didn’t get those results. I mean, tell me what you would have done if your plan would have fail and you wouldn’t have gotten those best results you were looking for? Athens Mir: Well, I would have just had to find an alternate plan on retrieving the book. BladeD: That is what I am trying to say. You had no set plan. You either need to have a plan that you know would work, or back up plans to go along with the main plan. You cannot just be like… “Well, I am going to make sure that no matter what happens this all pulls through the way I planned it and not worry about anything else.” It is thoughts like those that have gotten many of the greats in our past killed. Because once that plan fails and you never have a back up plan, then you are stuck with forcing yourself to find an alternate which gets you all nervous and you begin to wonder what you are going to do. That has happened in the past and that has led to many deaths to the greatest the NMA has ever had. When you no longer have a plan to fall on you are completely vulnerable to your enemy. On top of all of that, you were trying to face an enemy greater then you except, so if you wouldn’t have passed out like you did and if you wouldn’t have gotten the results of your only plan. You know what would have happen? You would have been asking for death! That’s what! Plan “B” would be… “I am asking that I die now.” That is what would have happened. General P: BladeD. I am very impressed with you once again. It is because of things like this sort that make you the great person that you are. I couldn’t have put those words together like that so well in a million years. I mean I am almost speechless. I truly don’t know what to say. Athens Mir: Well, I have something to say. I guess you are right BladeD, but what you have to realize is that I was 100% sure that. :: BladeD interrupts:: BladeD: Don’t you get it Mir! You can never be 100% sure of crap when you are in a situation like this. No matter what you have done, what skills you have, or who you know. You can never bee 100% sure of something like this. Because when you are put into a situation where you have to cross into enemy lines for a long period of time and even go into their main headquarters the only thing you can do hope. That is it. You can only hope. You can’t ever be 100% sure that everything will turn out the way you plan it too because once you cross that line like that there is always room for mistake. On top of that you didn’t know the area you were in. It was all unknown terrain. I mean that puts your risk of death and your plan failing even higher. I would guess that there was about a 45% chance of you getting back home alive with or without the book. :: Once again we are left in the middle of an exciting chapter in this series, but this time I am not even going to hint any information to you, you are just going to have to stay tuned into this series to know what is coming up next. No matter what you do you don’t want to miss the upcoming episodes because they are going to shock you, make you laugh, and possibly make you cry. Wait a minute. I am hinting information to you. Oh crap. Well, it doesn’t matter just stay tuned. That is all that matters. Part 11 is coming up soon! ::
