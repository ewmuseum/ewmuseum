I'm back and I got some Snapple

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/02 Duke is standing outside of a large building in what looks to be California. Duke is leaned up against a big old truck with the Snapple logo on the side. He stands there looking at the building, which appears to be a church of some sort. He is smoking a cigar and has a big smile on his face. The Malice Man turns to the camera and starts to speak. Williams: Hey Kannon how the hell are ya??? I know your not used to a wrestlers talking straight into the camera, that style was lost in the late ninety’s along with your intelligence. I guess you can call me old fashion. Duke takes a drag on his Punch cigar. Williams: You know I’ve spent the last few days drinking, thinking, drinking, and just flat out getting red faced. I’ve been planing on how to kill you, your lady friend, Nelson, and the rest of the stooges. I thought maybe I could kidnap that douche you hang out with but that’s been done. I thought maybe I could cut off your fingers and toes just for the fun of it but that’s too much work. Maybe I could just kick your ass in the ring and take your pride away MR. Commissioner?? You think just cus you got a cute little title like Commissioner you should be respected? You think just cus you own some of this company I should give two sh*ts? F*ck you and your stock you three bit sally. But the more I think about it the more I realize your nothing but a dolt. Duke takes a shot of gold. Williams: You see your religion is like a big rubber dick. It brings you and your lady some pleasure for a while but you both know it isn’t the real thing. You know its just a dildo bringing you a taste of salvation cus you can’t get a genuine hard on. Hell hoss, if that’s your bag then hey that’s your bag. Just like you owning stock in the company that ain’t nothing but a farce. I know it, the fans know, your mother knows it, hell even that half ass actor Travolta knows it.… everbody knows that Nelson is in control and your just his little bitch. Duke spits out a big chunk of saliva. Williams: You know something while I’m talking about little bitches why don’t you say hello to Elli for me. Tell that little c*m net if she ever wants to hop on the Malice Express its all aboard hossssssss. You got too understand that I can see it in her eyes that you ain’t offering her enough pleasure. Everybody kneeling don’t be praying hoss. Maybe it’s time you go get your self something to eat south of the boarder, and if you ain’t willing, just send her my way. Next time I see he I’ll give her a little wink to remind her what I’m doing to her while I sleep and kids it ain’t rated R its rated XXX. Duke grabs his flask and drinks some Malice juice. Williams: You know people who rely on religion are very weak-minded individuals. Assholes who can’t think for them selves to put it blunt. Robots with their gears out of whack. Mental mid-gets with no self-esteem. Kid I bet your real proud of that there shiny title? I bet you showed it to mama, papa, uncle Xavior and even the homeless guy on the corner. Well piss on that title hoss cus it means sh*t till you can say you beat the Malice Man. Your whole career is null and void till you step in the ring with me. I’m not an amateur or a 21 year old just starting out I’m the real deal hoss. I’m somebody who doesn’t stop till its over and my opponent is broken down, beaten, and Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. Just then a little boy of about seven approaches Duke with a pen and paper. Boy: Are you Duke Williams? Williams: Are you the police? Boy: No! Williams: With the IRS? Boy: No. Williams: Peta or the dang ACLU?? Boy: No and No!!! Duke sticks out his hand and shakes the young fans hand. Williams: Then yes I’m Duke Williams!! How the hell are you? Boy: Good!! Can I get an autograph please? Duke grabs the note pad and signs his signature. Boy: Gee thanks!!! Hey, is it true you hate Scientology? Williams: Hate is a strong word little hoss. I dislike people who are brainwashed. The young man looks confused. Boy: I’m I brainwashed?? Williams: Are you a Scientologist? Boy: Yes. Williams: Then yes a little bit!! But if you’re a Duke Williams fan then its ok. But if you want to deprogram your self then read this here book. Duke hands the child a Crunk bible. Boy: Thanks Mr. Williams!! If you hate Scientology so much why are you standing in front of their church? Duke reaches into the Snapple truck and grabs two Snapples and hands one to the kid. Williams: I’m here to make peace with Kannon and deliver some Snapple. Boy: really? Duke guzzles down some Snapple just to spit it out. Williams: This stuff tastes like piss!! Maybe if I mix it with some JD, it will taste ok. Duke pours the rest of the Snapple out into the grass till there’s only a drop left. He then grabs a bottle of JD and fills up the Snapple bottle with the JD and the drop of Snapple mixing the two. HE then drinks down half the bottle. Williams: Much better!! The kid imitates Duke and spits out the Snapple. Kid: Your right it does taste like pee!! Can I mix it with that other drink? Duke laughs. Williams: Not a good idea hoss maybe in a few years. If you would excuse me kid I have to deliver this Snapple. Kid: OK thanks for the autograph. The young man scampers off as the Malice Man drinks down the rest of his booze filled Snapple bottle. He then throws the glass bottle onto the cement and laughs as it smashes into pieces. Duke then turns and starts to talk to the camera again. Williams: Kannon you want to play ball with the Malice Man, lets play. I see this nice church here seems like you criminals put a lot of money into it. The creed of Scientology states members must dedicate themselves to the eradication of injustice, constantly work to raise levels of ethics and restore sanity in all sectors of society. Duke scratches his mountain beard. Williams: Is that really what your carrying out Kannon?? Joining up with convicted criminals, is that really eradicating injustice? You’re a phony and it’s sad that the rest of the Scientologist’s have to suffer. No I take that back f*ck’em all with a big rubber dick there weak mental assholes anyway. The only one I respect is that fat, balding, bad skinned f*cker L. Ron Hubbard. At least the chunky con-artist is getting rich off of feeble-minded rich people like you Kannon. I think there should be more sick twisted con-artists like Hubbard to rip more rich people off!! I love watching you money hungry fags getting the proverbial shaft. It makes me feel kind of sorry that I have to deliver this Snapple but you wanted to play ball not me. Duke grabs a crate of the piss tasting Snapple and sets it on the ground next to him. Williams: You know the NFL draft is next week and the Texans need a QB. Sorry I missed the compound practice but let me show off my skills. Duke grabs a bottle of Snapple and drops back like an NFL QB and aims for the church. He then lets the bottle fly aiming for the window. He misses by a half an inch as the bottle smash on the side of the church staining it with iced tea and littering the side of the building with broken glass. Williams: Damn its been awhile, but remember I was a nose tackle not a QB. Duke grabs another bottle and rifles it into the side of the building. He then grabs another Snapple and this time it flies through the window of the church shattering the window and then the bottle inside the church. Williams: Touch down!!!!! Duke continues bulleting one after another breaking all the windows and staining the side of the church with Snapple. Williams: Your right Kannon this sh*t is pretty good. It has some good aerodynamics!! Come on you soft son of a b*tch lets play!!! Duke grabs another create and tosses some more at the building till he hears police sirens. Williams: That’s my cue to get the f*ck out of dodge. This is just the first round of a ten round fight bitch and just like I did to you on Sabotage I’m going to send you Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and me and Elli are gonna do it Malice Style!! HOSSSSSSSS I’m out. Duke hops into his Ford pick-up and takes off right before the cops show up.
