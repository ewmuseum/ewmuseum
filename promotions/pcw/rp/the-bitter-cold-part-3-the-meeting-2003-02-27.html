The Bitter Cold - Part 3 - The Meeting

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 27, 2003 [Miles Higgins is not a very attractive man at all. He used to be though. He was once a strapping young lad, with hair, and he was the guy ALL the ladies wanted to sleep with. Age sets in and now he's a balding, gray haired wrinkled mess of a middle aged man. Some people wished him good luck on his seventieth birthday a few months back. Unfortunately, the people that did that walked off too fast before Miles could explain that he's really only forty seven years of age. He is married, with four daughters. Two of them are in high school, and the other two, with are twins, are going through the awkward stages of entering womanhood. It's not a very easy life he's leading at home, which is why his face shows a look of impatience] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dudes. It was TOTALLY BOGUS! I'm stuck in this limo door, right. And we're doing like ninety down Fifth Street, and I nearly get swiped by one of those heinous lamp posts! It was MOST SCARY, dudes! Then some homeless dude attacked me....actually...it was an ARMY of homeless dudes! You should have seen them! They were most bogus with green teeth and they had...STICKS! HUGE STICKS! They didn't get anything due to me being a bodacious wrestler and all...but that's why I'm late. [Tommy sits at the large oak table surrounded by a bunch of old guys. Tommy wears a blue sweater and blue jeans while all of the board members are in the traditional suit and tie. The men just stare at Tommy as if he walked in the room half an hour late, wearing ridiculous clothing, and smiling like a jackass. Well, how about that...IT ACTUALLY HAPPENED!] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I thought that you were wearing so much clothing that you fell over and couldn't get up? TTT: DUDE! [Tommy's brother, Harry, just smirks as he looks to the other board members seeing what their reaction is. Unforutnately most of them are annoyed and impatient so they just give Harry a stone look] Miles: Tommy, that is a rough day. You need to understand that I have four daughters. One tells me that she is on the pill, the other tells me that he friends are strippers, and then my twins both had their period! Now, if that isn't a bad morning then nothing is! We need to get this Tubular Tiki underway. We just need your approval. TTT: I don't think that I'm capable of making decisions like that, dudes. I almost DIED today! It was a very traumatic experience for me. Plus, I have a HUGE match to think about this Friday. It's just too many things piling up, I'm not Superman or anything. I'm more BODACIOUS than him, but I don't have those powers. [Everyone at the table lets out stressful sighs and just look twice as impatient now. Harry looks a bit uneasy as all the old men are only a few steps away from lynching Tommy and nailing him to the wall. If that happens Harry has the cell phone on him and the limo driver will most definetly be on hand] Miles: Tommy, first thing we need to talk about are the figures that the other facilities have been drawing. I have to honestly give you some major kudos for really keeping business up as you play this 'super villain' role for the television cameras. TTT: Television role? Miles: Yes, television role. TTT: Is it on Baywatch? I'd be MOST BODACIOUS on Baywatch. Har, wouldn't I so totally nail all the babes? Harry: If they don't sniff any coke. TTT: Gnarly. [Miles looks lost as do the other board memberrs. Miles just shakes it off] Miles: People LOVE Tubular Tiki! It's numbers are climbing to HUGE rates and soon will overtake Starbucks if these numbers keep up. I guess it's just a matter of people enjoying the bad guy sometimes. TTT: Totally. Chicks dig me. Miles: We've been really scouting places all over the city to find out where the ideal location would be for the new New York facility. It's tough. But we've got three really great locations that nobody can really decide on. That's where you come in Tommy. [Tommy grabs the manilla folder in front of him and looks over the locations. It's just a bunch of pictures of old looking buildings surrounded by bodacious looking buildings. Tommy's face contorts in disgust at the sights of the pictures and he throws them aside as if they were watermelon seeds. The board members are confused now as Tommy goes into decision mode, ready to make his well thought out opinion] TTT: Bogus. All of them. Miles: All of them?! Have you gone mad?! They are all IDEAL locations where many teenagers and college students frequent! Any one of those locations will be PERFECT for the shop, we just need you to pick one. TTT: Dude, whoever picked these is totally fired. Look at these buildings! It looks like the Jolly Green Giant came along and took a totally heinous dump all over it! Who would want to go inside of place that looks like that? I know that I wouldn't want to!! [Miles sighs and rubs his eyes feeling a headache coming on strong. He already had one earlier today and it has just found a way to resurface, and all because of the dumbest man ON THE PLANET!] Miles: They aren't finished yet!! We have to do construction on them still! It's not the building that matters, it's the location you fool!! Harry: Tommy...just pick one. TTT: Har, tell me again why we let someone as totally heinous as Miles run our Tubular Tiki organization. [Harry shrugs] Harry: I honestly don't remember because I was too stoned at the time. TTT: Point taken. Well, I'll take the one near Times Square because that's near that MTV studios place and all those horny babes will want a piece of yours truly. [Suddenly Tommy's eyes get wide and Harry can see the Christmas lights getting brighter above Tommy's head] TTT: Dude...we should hang out there after the meeting and I can bag me some really quick babes that way. All those MTV babes LOVE ME! Harry: They're teenagers. TTT: Well...I'm 24. Harry: Exactly. TTT: So? Miles: GENTLEMEN!! Lets forget about MTV and girls for the moment. We need you to decide about ownership and management of the new facility. I have a few guys in line for the job and I just need you to look over their resumes. [Tommy takes the second manilla folder and squints his eyes at the papers in front of him. It's not that he has a sight problem or anything, it's that he doesn't really understand most of the words on the paper. He silently mouths the words that he's reading and his eyes squint even more the further down the page he gets] TTT: These dudes went to school too much. Miles: The man your looking over is named Richard Cunningham. He's ivy league and has a few years in the business world. I'd say he's pretty much a shoe in for the management position. TTT: Dude, it says here that he likes Tennis. Miles: That's right. I played a few sets with him the other weekend. A damn fine player. TTT: Well...Tennis is BOGUS!! Well, it's bodacious when the babes are on there. DUDE! [Once again Tommy's eyes light up as he looks up to his brother] TTT: Harry, we should check out the tennis courts too. MAJOR BABES there. Harry: Tommy.... TTT: Yeah? Harry: It's winter. TTT: Shut up. Harry: Your mother. TTT: She's your mom too. Harry: I know. Miles: GENTLEMEN!! This is just too JUVENILE!! Tommy, just say that you'll hire Cunningham and then you can get to your precious MTV girls and tennis players. TTT: I don't want to hire Cunningham, he's a heinous tennis player. [Miles gets up from his chair and leans his arms on the desk, looking Tommy directly in the eyes] Miles: He just does it for fun, Tommy. Now just say you'll hire him and we can all get back to work...FOR YOU may I add. [Tommy stands up from his chair and leans on the table just like Miles] TTT: Well, why don't I just go out and hire Andre Agassi, dude! Then we can have Pete Sampras serve the drinks!! IT'S BOGUS!! [Miles lowers his head having visions of himself stangling Tommy and then slamming his head off of the wall dozens of times. He can see the blood running down the wall and Tommy's empty blue eyes looking up at him. Miles can smell the death already like it's already happened, but he looks up and sees the smug smile on Tommy's face. Talk about a nightmare] Miles: Tommy, him being a tennis player has NOTHING to do with how well he can run the place. He's a well proven business man and he's a hard worker. He'll make you ALL KINDS OF CASH and that should cancel out the fact that he enjoys to go play tennis after work. Do you understand? TTT: I understand but I don't like the compadre. [Tommy looks over another resume and holds it up. It has a picture of a blonde woman with a very pretty face in the corner] TTT: She's a much better choice, dude. Miles: That's my daughter, you fool. TTT: Then whats her resume doing in the folder? Miles: It got in there by accident, give it back. [Miles holds out his hand, just waiting for the PCW Continental Champion to hand it back. Tommy just looks at the picture with a smile on his face] TTT: No way, dude. She is bodacious looking! Maybe I should stop by your bodacious pad for a visit. Miles: She's eighteen!! TTT: SHE'S LEGAL!! Miles: YOU WOULDN'T!! TTT: Dude, I would. And so would she. You said she was on the pill, right? [Miles sighs and pounds the table, his wrinkled face turning a dark shade of red] Miles: You will not even go within ten feet of my daughter, do you understand?! I'm hiring Cunningham, we're making that new coffee house in Times Square, and you can get the HELL OUT OF MY FACE!! [Tommy just stands there frozen, with no emotion on his face. Harry nods his head in approval of Miles' outburst] TTT: Fine. Have it your way, dude. Harry, it's time to find us some bodacious babes.... [Harry grabs Tommy's large winter coat and hands it to him] TTT: ...then we need to get you high again. You've become MOST ANNOYING! Harry: So have you. TTT: Shut up. Harry: No. TTT: YES! Harry: You first. TTT: Huh? Harry: Exactly. Now lets go. [The two men exit the room as all the old men laugh and shake hands with Miles. It was a job well done by a very damn fine business man. He's marketed Tubular Tiki very well and has gotten himself noticed by many huge companies. Soon he'll be out of the hell hole that he's in and into a much better atmosphere. That's when his cell phone rings] Miles: Hello? Miles' daughter: DADDY, I LOVE YOU!! Miles: Oh. Well, I love you too, honey. May I ask where this is coming from? Miles' daughter: I can't believe that you got me a date with 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. I know that you work for him and all, but I never knew you could have THAT MUCH power! YOUR SO GREAT!! [Miles drops the phone and is frozen in horror] Miles' daugher: Daddy? Are you there? Miles: Motherfu--- [Fade to black]
