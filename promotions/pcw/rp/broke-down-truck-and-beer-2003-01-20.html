Broke down Truck and Beer

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-20-03 RP 1 Turmoil 21st This afternoon, we find Teresa standing under the hood of her Chevy truck. The midnight purple step side is parked on the side of the highway. The winter sun has melted most of the snow though there are yet some hidden under shades and along ditches. Several cars and trucks have sped by. A few horns blow but no one has stopped to offer assistance. Underneath the hood, Teresa curses under her breath as she searches for the problem. A cold wind sweeps under the hood and across her face, which brings less comfort in this most unsettling situation. “Stupid cold weather. Stupid truck breaking down. Stupid me for not finding what’s wrong.” An agitated sigh exits the Television Champion’s lungs. From fingertips to elbows she is covered in black from wiggling this hose and checking that seal. Then suddenly she spots the alternator belt or lack there of. “That’s it! OMPH! Damn, that hurt like hell.” A mustang GT rushes by. The driver lays on the horn causing Teresa to stand to quickly and hit the top of her head under the hood. Watching the bright yellow sports car vanishing out of sight, she returns with a gesture of her own with two middle fingers and two unspoken words. After shutting the hood, Teresa decides the best bet is either walk to the nearest store or hitch a ride. Either way isn’t very save for a young woman. Then again, not every day you see a famous wrestler hitching a ride down the highway and you don’t want to make them mad. It would be her luck the first car to actually stop would be a small beat up wreck that looks like it has been driven right out of a junk yard. First the small blue Chevrolet Corsica passes like everyone else, and then it backs in reverse till it stops beside Teresa. The car’s clear coat is missing and the blue paint is almost chipped off. The car itself sounds like the Little Engine That Could when it passes by. She combs her fingers through her hair before realizing how black her hands are. “Well shit.” The Corsica stops and the passenger side door opens. A Burger King bag falls out onto the pavement along with an empty McDonald’s cup, which begins to roll down the road. The inside of the car doesn’t look much better than the outside. Trash is piled in the back floorboard from Mt. Dew bottles to beer cans. “Do I know you?” A deep Boston accent comes from the driver’s seat. Teresa peers in and sees a bald man a little older than her. He has a short stalky build and a bald head. She knows exactly who it is. She just barely remembers him. She tosses a few empty fast food bags in the back seat and about 5 empty beer cans from the front floorboard. She climbs in and shuts the door. “You might Jack. Mind driving me to an auto parts store? I need an alternator belt.” The driver gives Teresa a confused glance and glances back toward her truck. He takes in a deep sigh and puts the car in drive. “I never asked if you needed a ride. You look familiar and I only want to know who you are.” Teresa finds a semi clean grease rag on the dash and tries to wipe off her arms and hands. She is listening to the man she called Jack but she doesn’t look at him. “Does it matter who I am? If you don’t want to take me to the parts store, then I can walk.” Teresa reaches for the door handle but it’s missing. She uses the window crank to roll down the window. By the time she has her arm out the window to open the door the man speeds up. “Get your ass back in the car. Keep giving me that sarcasm and I’ll remind you why they called me Violent Jack. Besides, I’m out of beer and you’re going to buy me some after you get that damn belt.” Teresa chuckles and sits back in the seat. She begins to toss more trash in the back seat. Jack watches from the corner of his eye. He now remembers who she is. Of all people to pick up, the last person he’d expect to climb in his car is a woman. Just his luck, the one woman that actually sat down beside him is Teresa Jane Tomas. Jack pulls the Corsica into the parking area of the auto parts store. He watches Teresa step out of the car and walk through the double doors. She only is in her baggy Levi’s and a Titans sweatshirt. The sleeves are push to her elbows and her arms are still quite a bit black from tinkering under her truck hood. When she comes back, he wastes no time in turning the ignition key. He looks surprised when she actually climbs back into the car. Teresa places the purchased alternator belt on the floor by her feet and shuts the door. She catches Jack’s surprised look. “You still want that beer don’t you? I may be a bitch sometimes but I’ll never deny any person of their beer. Besides, I’m getting thirsty.” Jack pulls the car into the main road and heads toward the nearest gas station. “Wait a minute. I don’t recall ever sharing my beer.” Teresa sort of smirks. “You never invited me in your car either but I’m still here aren’t I? I’ll buy your beer but I have only one rule. You have to help me drink it. Despite your shitty car, you seem to be an alright drinking buddy.” The car parks in a nearby Shell. This time he doesn’t kill the engine. “You are one crazy woman Tomas.” After stepping out of the car, Teresa shuts the door and sticks her head through the window. She grins. “So I’ve been told.”
