If you say so hoss.

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/02 -David Kern and Duke Williams are at the site of tonight’s Turmoil going over some spots and sponsors when the conversation turns to Duke’s main even match.- Williams: So he thinks I don’t understand the fact that he’s insulting me? Dave: That’s the way I perceived it. Williams: For a guy who claims to be a God he sure is missing in the brain department. You think if he were so concerned with originality and repetition he would understand. He would understand that after being called old, dumb, or mortal every other promo from the so called elite of PCW I get sick of it. I get down right bored with it. Same old tune, fiddle, and guitar. He’s just a part of the drone like promos I hear every week. Their more or less carbon copies. Dave: Its does make you nauseous. Williams: I don’t believe he’s a God? Excuses me for reasoning that if God were among us he wouldn’t be a pro-wrestler. He wouldn’t hang out with a baboon broad with big boobs who talks to much. I don’t know when to shut up?? At least I know reality you fucking whack job. Dave: He does seem a little off base but he’s a great competitor? Williams: That’s good for him and I’m not going to take that away from him but come on a God? The guy gives me gas just thinking about him. -Dave makes a face as Duke lets out some mortal gas that’s down right repugnant.- Williams: A think this turd Akiyama downfall is the fact he’s spreading himself to thin. Akiyama the God of repetitive, insignificant, bitching lets to whine promo after promo about the whole damn PCW. El Diablo? Who gives a shit. Then he always cries about Kannon thinking he deserves a title shot? This down right dumb divine asshole needs to focus at what he has ahead of him not down the road Dave: You have a point Duke he does seem all over the place. Williams: He thinks this match is a waste of his time? Doesn’t he know I’m the president of this damn company? Why am I stuck in a match with HIM!! The walking hypocrite know as Akiyama is a real downer man. Dave: He does contradict himself a lot. Williams: The guy is so f*cking ridiculous its unbelievable. I see his last promo was a none specific filler promo. Dave: Sure he’ll chalk it up as character development. Williams: That is the trend isn’t it? -The two broadcast legends laugh it off.- Williams: So Pharaoh’s not only a terrorist but he’s now building up an army. Dave: If your serious they need to do a background check on this guy. Plus he stinks like urine. Williams: I know I asked him to shower but he didn’t give me a response. He must be one of those none mentioners!! Dave: Well he better do something or I’m going to call the personal director to have a talk with him. I gag every time he walks by me in the halls. Williams: Yea its bad PR. I’m going to call the CIA and have them look into his army of middle eastern terrorists. Dave: Is that PC? Williams: Who gives a shit this guy may blow up the PCW if were not careful! Dave: I guess your right. Williams: Damn right I am. Is he Middle Eastern? Dave: Egyptian yes. Williams: Were the September 11th terrorists? Dave: Yes Williams: Is he young and male? Dave: Yes. Williams: As were they!! Is he involved in some creepy underground army?? Dave: Your scaring me Duke cus your right. Williams: Something needs to be done. I’m hoping I can knock some sense into these weirdo’s!! Dave: I’m sure you will. Williams: The world depends on me sending them whiskey bent and hell bound. Dave: Lets not over emphases a situation or match. Williams: Oh no as announcers we would never do that. -Both men laugh as the camera fades.- =w=
