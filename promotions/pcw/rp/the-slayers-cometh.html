The Slayers Cometh...

Date: Earier in the Week (IC) They were all known for their skill...their technique...being beings of pure and complete conquest. That was their lives...not the one that they chosen, but the one given to then upon the end of theirs. So many times it has been said that there are more than you know of lurking in the shadows of this world. And in shadows, we do not mean the evil and darkness. We mean what you cannot see...the things that some of your minds are unable to grasp. There is yet another reality buried under this one...and in the days approaching, it will be clear for your understanding... But for now...they were the main focus upon this tale. They swarmed towards him like moths to a light. They totaled four, each approaching from different angles. They were different...but they all held the same look of determination within their eyes... Akiyama must be defeated. The NVG saw that look a lot in the eyes of others...especially those who looked to face him in the days approaching. That look belonged to the eyes of someone who wanted nothing more than to see Akira Akiyama lying face down in a pool of his own blood and sweat, having not been quick or strong enough to elude a tri-count, or to evade the attack that send him toppling over a top plastic covered rope. These were the eyes belonging to ruthless, bloodthirsty, and conniving goblins... ...but that didn't make them invincible. They were both ignorant as well as intelligent. They were blind to the ambitions of their employer, yet they studied the god well enough to know about him and his attacks. All they knew was that this man wanted Akiyama bad enough to place almost ten billion dollars on his head, and so far no one had been successful. When you want success...you call the Slayer Squad; they slay the bounty with no trouble. Akiyama wasn't one to let up from a good old-fashioned game of Outlaw and Posse. Outlaw and Posse, you say? You mean you don't know of the game from the early 1900s? It's as simple as its name...one acted as an outlaw, and the rest as the posse, and from there they tracked the outlaw until caught. Quite fun if you ever get the time... Akiyama knew of this game...but did not know how he did...yet another mystery. He took his trackers down a small alleyway. It was a dead end from the looks of it; a garage door closed at the end of the track. He just stood there and stared at the door as the footsteps approached more quickly. ~~~'A closed door...another closed door...we always had doors closed on us, because of our appearance or how we never acted like the other people. Just because we don't follow the set pattern placed down by management or the other rulers of society, we fail to receive the privileges of earning recognition. No title shots...more or less...but this time there is one. One World shot that we have fought to earn come Sunday. Nineteen other men facing off against us...it will be such a treat. But we have other things...that seek out our attention. How annoying these little insects become...showing up repeatedly throughout the day with their feeble attempts at success. It only makes us more familiar with your methods of battle...it only makes us more anxious to dismiss you from our presence...'~~~ The Neo Void God had been standing there for at least five minutes perfectly still. Those behind him were wondering of acting first, lest they lose their chance of gaining the upper hand. But the four of them all were caught off guard by the demanding raspy voice of the NVG. 'You children fancy telling us what you request of your god?' He turned from back to front, his black vinyl trench coat fluttering in the rotation. His black taped hands clenched in and out as he looked on at each of them. His brain acted as radar, marking each of their images with an imaginary red dot across their faces to keep his targets in line. 'We hope you know that your silence doesn't intimidate us. It only makes us more anxious to dismiss you from our presence.' Their faces were familiar to him. He knew their names, and who they were in general. They had been pestering him all day...and he was finally tired of their persistence. His eyes trailed over to the first one... ~~~ He was tall and well defined. Short spiky black hair and gray eyes along his face, and he wore a tight blue shirt with black pants and black boots. He had that stern look on his face from the first time he had laid eyes on Akiyama. The NVG had first noticed him when he was out trying to get something to eat. He sat at the table staring into his menu hard, the decisions racking his brain slowly. And he was across from the god...sitting alone chewing upon some noodles. Akiyama barked some orders to his inept waitress before sitting back and brooding some more. The man continued to watch him carefully, not trying to be inconspicuous. He wanted to seem more like an awed watcher than a crafty one. He would have passed off too, considering Akiyama's larger size against the rest of society around him. Akiyama watched as a woman approached the man's table and began talking to him. He smiled at her, getting up and walking off with her towards the back. By this time, the scatterbrained waitress had returned with his plate of food. He prepared himself to eat, but he realized that his hands were filthy. They were blood red...mainly from the intense training he put himself through earlier that day. Griping to himself, he left the table and walked off to the bathroom. He washed his hands thoroughly...and then stopped...something attracting his attention. They were moans...and groans of pleasure. Akiyama glared towards one of the farther stalls in the corner of the bathroom, feeling a sense of disgust for whoever it was. Then he looked down and noticed the black pants and boots...they seemed familiar. Stepping over towards the stall, he kicked in the door ready to dish out a sound scolding...he didn't know why he was going to yell at them, but it seemed like the right thing to do. When the door flew open, the man flew out of the stall at Akiyama. He landed atop of Akiyama's shoulders and began to punch away at his face. Akiyama was temporarily blinded by the man's sudden attack, which is until he took the initiative. With his hands wrapped around his attacker’s neck, the god pried him off and held him high in the air. Even as Akiyama proceeded to crush his windpipe, the man continued to kick at his face, nailing few direct hits. That was stamina... but all the kicking and attacks were cancelled when the back of his skull crashed through the porcelain bowl of the urinal across the room. Any normal man would have been dead after that. But this guy staggered back to his feet and shook his head in pain. Impressive...the god thought. He took a fighting stance and inched towards the NVG carefully. Akiyama turned to face him and lunged at him with a Danse Macabre. The man caught his kick, and loomed in to punch. Akiyama blocked, and crushed the back of his heel into his jaw with a reverse Enzeguri. Landing back on his feet, he flipped backwards and hammered his foot hard into the attacker’s nose. He staggered back and wiped the fluid from his nose away, raising his fists again. Before the man could attack again, Akiyama rushed him with a blizzard of punches and kicks. The man fell down on his back, but Akiyama picked him back up by the back of his head. Then he held him up and drove his head right through the wall of the toilet stall. He was skilled...quite skilled indeed. Upon other terms, the god would look to this man as a training partner, and quite possibly a friend. But no...he had chosen to go against Akiyama with his tainted reasons. He is just like the others...those who wish to take care of the Neo Void God, the barrier Akiyama represented between society’s evil, and the greed that they lust after... His name is Kamui... ~~~ The next one was fair figured, shapely from the strenuous training gained through a lifetime of practice. She was dressed in pink vinyl pants, black boots, and a tight black and pink glittered long sleeved shirt. Her hair was pink resembling the traces of color in her wardrobe, and her eyes were Coppertone. She entered the scene when she walked into the restaurant where Akiyama was awaiting his food glumly. She winked at him before approaching the table across from his left...where the man that watched him sat. The two talked...laughed...smiled...flirted...and soon the deal was made. Then they retired to the restroom t complete their business deal. Akiyama pushed the door in and stood over the sink to wash his hands. Then he heard the noises...an oral...wet...sound. Disgust ran through him, and he had no patience that night for nonsense. Storming over to the last stall, he pushed the door open and found a shock. Once the man was taken care of, he glared back at the woman to reprimand her. It was then that she struck out at him with a flurry of quick kicks to his chest and face. Akiyama stepped back to block, before grabbing one of her legs and swinging her whole body into the wall-sized mirror behind him. The impact shattered the entire mirror, but to his surprise, the woman rolled back into a crouching position once he looked back at her the second time. She then sprung off the side of the wall and nailed him with a roundhouse kick to his face. The NVG shook it off before elbowing her in her chest, doubling her over in pain. Then he leaped up and smashed her throat first into the edge of the sink with the Guillotine’s Fate. When she didn’t come back with a counter, Akiyama pried open her lips and wrapped them around the sink faucet. Another one...thinking they could underestimate the NVG. Their efforts were useless, just like all the others who think otherwise about success in the Madness. If it was the matter of who is putting out the most effort in the days approaching the battle...then Akiyama was far behind on that fight. But time was still free, and Akiyama still has time to deliver the final deathblow. Then with a twist of the hot water, he snubbed his nose and walked away... “Suck on that, child...” This was Sakura... ~~~ When looking at the next one, he looked out of place. His appearance was that of a teenager, ranging about 14-16 years of age. He was dressed in an urban fashion, with oversized baggy metallic blue jeans, large white tee shirt, a matching jean jacket, blue sneakers, and a blue baseball cap twisted backwards on his head. The glittering necklace he wore around his neck read “KJ”. He made his introduction into the scene when he stood next to the god outside the restaurant. Akiyama waited for the bus impatiently. He was already flustered from what had happened earlier, and he just wanted to go away and forget about what had happened. The teen bobbed his head up and down to the beat of the music within his CD player. Akiyama could hear the tune, and know that it was some variety of Japanese rap, yet unfamiliar with the specific artist. The NVG knew his music... it was something that he came across in his younger days while trying to stimulate his intelligence. Soon he too found himself nodding to the beat. The teen stopped the music and pulled out a CD case for a new selection. Akiyama looked down the street for the bus, wondering when it was supposed to arrive. As he turned back, something whizzed by his face, sending a streak of crimson oozing from his cheek. The teen was poised in a Ninjitsu stance, with his CD case wide open. Already there were CDs positioned between each of his fingers, ready to throw at Akira Akiyama. The NVG flipped backwards to gain some space between them both...and as he landed he barely dogged a razor sharp CD to his neck. The boy was tossing them out like a pro, maybe as well as Akiyama was at his age. The focus here was not if the child was indeed as skilled as the Neo Void God in his youth...it was how to deal with the teen now at the present time. And the god thought of one alternative...and acted... It was like everything went in slow motion. Three CDs flew towards him, and he could see each rotation of the bladed discs as they spun. But Akiyama was still in his normal speed, if it wasn’t heightened to ludicrous speed from the tension of the attack already. He plucked the first disc out of the air and used it to block the other two. Then he turned his back on the boy, and with his left hand threw the CD back at him. The aim was on direct target: the CD deflected all the remaining projectiles before embedding itself in the left of the child’s chest. He spun around in impacting pain before hitting the ground. Akiyama didn’t even look back as he walked away. Heh...another analogy, eh? Right you are...you see, it doesn’t matter how many you toss out at the Neo Void God, especially when you’re fighting with the wrong weapons. Effectiveness is the key here, and if you’re fighting Akiyama with condom water balloons against his Divine Weapon, the Saishukessen Ougii blade, it will not matter how many you throw in his direction...because he wields the most powerful, most effective weapon. Many of these children think that their linguistic weapons are effective, and some of them may just hold the right weapon every once in a while. But without the skill to use that weapon of language...they’re just as effective as whore who doesn’t know how to f^ck This one was Kyusaku Jinnai.~~~ There was one left in the matter...she must have been the brains behind the operation. She was dressed in all black like the NVG, but her outfit wasn’t vinyl and PVC like his. She wore wide legged black jeans, black combat boots, and a black spandex tank top with black tape reaching all the way up to her shoulders. Her hair was a vivacious fiery red color, more like crimson/ cran-apple than total natural red; brighter than nature intended. Maybe that was her life story...how she became who she is now... Akiyama walked off in a huff, his day having been nothing but training for him. It was such a thrill to have so many people wanting to do him harm...it always kept him on his toes. His cheek was healing up quickly, as most of his wounds usually did. He just loved the way he was able to seem almost impervious to injury at times in and out of the ring. He stopped and watched as the bus rolled past him. A sigh came to his lips...how ironic. Then from around the corner, a red Viper screeched. Akiyama looked back, noticing the sound, and was almost steamrolled by the vehicle. The NVG leaped out of the way just at the car sped past him on the sidewalk, his body sailing through the display window of a store. He exploded out of the window into the middle of the street, staring the car down right as it made a 180 to face Akiyama. The Viper revved its engine... The Neo Void God growled... The two machines gunned at each other with their roars meshing together into one, looking to meet the same point where destiny and fate dwelled. And once again, the actions seemed to transpire on multiple time frequencies. Akiyama jumped into the air in a horizontal corkscrew, rotating to aim a missile dropkick straight for the driver’s side of the tinted windshield. As his feet shattered through the glass, time slowed, and he saw the driver glaring at him through white sunglasses. She acted as quickly as he did, reaching down and grabbing the lever that reclined her seat. Akiyama flew over her and out the back of the car’s window, landing on his back in the street. The car screeched to a halt as the woman climbed out of the car. She stared at the NVG with wide eyes, surprised at the god’s attempted attack. She was even more surprised to see him nip back up and turn to face her. She took her fighting stance, expecting a heated rage to explode out of him. But she was wrong...Akiyama merely shrugged it off and walked away... There was confusion running through her...what was the meaning of this? You see...there comes a time when things just become uninteresting. It’s that time when you realize that hey...we’ve seen the same old people come here and there and say this and that in order to pick up a win against this person for that title...and it all becomes menial in the end. Yeah, we all know that people are trying to win, but no one’s going to the extreme as to put forth the true effort needed. Most of the time the outcome is determined by the presence...and never the truly skilled person. It’s a matter of the recognition that Akiyama’s never received in the entire time he’s existed here in PCW...but now thing’s have changed. This was his first title shot...the first world title shot. And he knew if he didn’t come through this time with the win, then he’d just get another chance later on. But he was definitely determined to NOT make himself into another Boda... There would be no repeated failures...there would not be any letting the BIG SHOT slip through his fingers... We’ve seen all of these men attack left and right for their chance to win this world title...but now it was the Neo Void God’s turn to take a shot. And he was determined this time...his motives were positive, and with the people backing him this time...maybe there was chance this time for success... ...Maybe there was a chance to have his Cursed status revoked... She...was Mai Miyamoto... She was the Slayer... ~~~ You would not know of the Slayer Squad...they were elite Japanese mercenaries so deep underground that contact was rare. Truth was, there were quite of number of underground groups that operated so swiftly and stealthily that they were hard to keep track of, but the Slayer Squad was one of the well-known groups. And of right now...they were after the god by order of someone else. Akiyama examined each of their faces and smirked slightly. “You children are the ones who have been pestering us all day. Do you not know that we have something important to train for, and you’re doing nothing but wasting out time?” they replied with steely glares. He shook his head, his eyes closing slightly. “But...it is fine. At least now we can get in line with the rest of those others we face at Madness who were wasting their time. At least we’re at the end of the line...so we can step out of line any time we wish. After this day though, we’re stepping back out of the line of wishful thinkers hoping to succeed. Then we’re moving on to the line of champions...the line that has started way before we even thought of PCW existing.” He heard a foot scuffle. His eyes snapped back up at them, his black nailed finger pointing sharply at them. “You had better keep your distance, flat-scans...we cannot be held responsible for our actions this close into the time of battle. If you think you’re so lucky, let’s see if your luck can help you escape the Void Banishment...that is, if the beating you long for doesn’t banish your consciousness to the darkness before then.” All four of them had moved up, cutting the distance between them in half from about twenty feet to nine. They were quick...really quick. Akiyama had to hold a grin back in his anticipation of a fight with them. “We already know who you are, and why you’re after us. So we just have two questions for you now...Who sent you, how do you want your remains presented...and what style do you want your @sskicking in?” Kyusaku Jinnai raised an eyebrow at him. “I thought you said there was two questions!” “Bonus, junior.” he muttered. “As BLIOTZ~! Would say...”Think before you speak; it prevents embarrassment.” As much of a wise philosophy it is, there are so many who choose to pay it no attention. Many of them are our opponents...but you really cannot judge them. If they choose to look foolish, it be not our place to question their judgment. All we can do is provide a barrier within their quest in the form of one called the Neo Void God. We are the gatekeeper... not that Snow guy...not that Nelson chap...not that Legend Inc gentleman...not that Boda b!tch...US. Just like you children have someone blocking your way to that three billion dollar bounty on us...but hey...we’re that person as well. Man...no wonder so many people hated us...we always get in the way of their progress.” Slayer folded her arms about her chest. She was growing impatient. “I’m warning you, Akira Akiyama. You’re totally underestimating us...just like your opponents. But you shouldn’t worry about them anyway, once we take you in to collect our money, you wont have to fight them at all.” Akiyama cracked his knuckles one by one. “You know...we admire your bravado...all of your courage...and all of their bravery as well. But there comes a point in one’s career when courage will not be enough to get you through. And while we most certainly will not doubt that you as well as our opponents have some skill...there is just one thing that you all are lacking, and you will continue to overlook it every time you try to outthink us.” Just then, the alley was flooded with a bright light. The Slayer Squad turned back to find a car with their headlights beaming at them. A quick turn back towards Akiyama found the god pouncing upon Kamui and Sakura. Then his arms stretched out at both Kyusaku and Slayer. He picked them both up from his crouching position and cracked their skulls together before throwing their bodies into the adjacent walls of the alley. Standing back up, he walked into the light of the car lights. Slayer rolled to her knees, her face etched in pain with a trickle of blood running down the side of her head. She glowered at the Neo Void God as he flashed his dark smile at them all, with one foot inside the silver-blue Matrix. “You know what you all lack??” he yelled back amused. “Godhood...finesse...and STYLE!” He blew Slayer a kiss before laughing out maniacally and jumping into the car. They all watched as Akiyama drove off, basking the alley once again in darkness. Slayer helped the others get to their feet, the smell of defeat seeping through her skin. Kyusaku punched his fist into the wall, a few chips of brickflying off. “D@mn!! I can’t believe this guy!!” he growled. “How does he always get away with that crap he pulls??” “It’s amazing how he does it sometime.” Kamui remarked. “All of a sudden he just has you defeated.” Sakura nodded. “It’s as if his style DOES make a difference.” Slayer shook her head. “Clam up. We’re going to have to take a different approach towards taking out the Neo Void God. Sakura, I’m going to need you to call the Legends.” The other three Slayer Squad members groaned in disapproval. “Aw!” Kyusaku griped. “Why do we have to call in those losers??” “Did you see who was driving that car?” Slayer replied. They all shook their heads. “Trust me...we’re going to need the Legends.” Slayer stared down at the end of the alley, where Akiyama previously stood trapped. Her mind was racing with ideas, plans...strategies. All of their minds were racing…planning…strategizing… A war was on the brink of breaking through the walls of society… …And there was nothing that anyone could do to stop it…
