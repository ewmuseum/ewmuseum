My Golden Sililoquy

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23rd, 2002 You never told me the truth. You never looked at me and you never even showed any respect. You were never ever there. Always alone, the pain is building, the lights are dimming, the night sky is overtaking my life and soul as the rain begins to patter down on my tin roof. The weather emulates my life. Dark, cold, dangerous. I see people outside running for cover. To live a life like mine is un-constitutional, Cruel and unusual punishment is banned. Then how can the government allow me to continue on the path that I am on? How can the guys on capital hill allow me to slip through the cracks? Why the hell can't anyone help me? End this cruelness? People may say that I have a great job and I get to see the world. Well I say that's a load of hoarse shit. My life may look peachy kin on the television but when I get back home it all falls apart, my life becomes what it was destined to be. My life means nothing. Why? Because you, YOU, were never there for me. YOU could never help me. YOU ruined it. Why do you stare back so coldly? Mimicking everything I do? Turn on the t.v. and what do you see? People running around killing each other. What happens when it turns out to be me? Is the man that you killed your brother? Casting out people like they were plain trash. You can't stop watching what they will do next. To do what you do just for the great cash. Worlds crashing because of a witch's hex. My life is a symphony at a death mass. My death mass. This is my final sililoquy. It is my chance to make my mark. My mark on this world. My mark in this dark sad world. You will not forgot what I have done for you all. Wrestling is a demon. People blame beer and cocaine for people's careers ending. I will tell you this once and for all. Wrestling brings down your career. Wrestling is worse than Cocaine, Heroine and all the beer in the world put together. Once you are in, and you see all the fans, you just can not get out. When you try to go cold turkey you enter withdrawal stage. This is when you begin to pick fights with anyone. Just as I prepare to retire, I receive more offers than anytime in my career. Why is it that people only care when you are about to leave? Why can they not care and let me leave just like I came in. Unnoticed and un-cared for. Before I leave I see the faces in the crowd. I don't just see fans, now I see faces of people and the people behind the fans. I don't see little Miyagawa fan there, I see Billy Joel age 14 who loves his parents and hates school. I have finally found my connection. My hatred for the people is leaving me at a startling rate. How can I deny them again? How can I accept this? For the last four years I have never accepted these people, but when I am about to leave they accept me. Wrestling is like a drug. It sucks you right back in when you are about to leave. The fans are my drug. I just had my first taste and now I can not quit. I need it, I need it worse than ever before. Now I see my chance. My chance comes just weeks before I retire. My chance is here and it is golden. With one victory I can un-do all of the things I have done in the past. With this victory I can finally earn all the respect that I have wanted. With one win I can become respected and an idol. Me an idol. Just with a single DDT, I can receive my shot at Television gold. The future is here and it is golden. My words speak volumes on how I feel about you. My words can say what a picture never could. If a picture is worth a thousand words than you will need about five thousand Michelangelo's right now. My life and yours are now one. You are exactly the same as I. I could tell you what I really think, but then you would go over the edge and commit suicide. Suicide, to kill one's self. Suicide, the act of total damnation. Suicide, relief. Suicide, your last resort. Can't you imagine it? The knife slowly is brought across your wrists. The blood flows smoothly out onto the floor as you take your final breaths? This is it. This is the big one. This is what will finally make you a household name. If you go through with it, people will never stop cheering your name. Everyone will know your name, everyone will scream it. Everyone will know the name of Mike Frampton.
