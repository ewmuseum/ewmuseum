Strategic Talks

Date: 11.3.02 (#3 TT, #2 FF) “C’mon….just meet him!” The voice of Rush Mason can be heard over the audio as the scene is still in black, beginning to fade in. It sounds coaxing, but also like it’s desperate, although the reason for that we will see soon. “No way….I don’t even like your kind.” The second voice is not that of Mason, and as we fade in, we can see that the voice belongs to none other than the Continental Champion, Syko the Goblin. Rush: He’s not my kind! He’s no more intelligent than a primate! Hell, I can’t guarantee he’s not one! Syko looks up at Rush, who shrugs, as the Continental Champ, who’s dwarfed by Rush, asks a question of Rush, while he stares down at his title belt. Syko: Why do I have to meet him then? Just because you found someone who’s like you- Rush quickly interjects again, trying to make it clear to Syko that 1) Rush is not an uncomfortably stereotypical black man and 2) Ray Ray is. Rush: He’s over 300 pounds! He can kill Pendragon and Chef in one fell swoop….well one fell whatever he does. I’m bringing him in to help. Syko continues to look at his reflection in his title belt, grinning like an idiot, and then finally turns to Rush. He looks at the door, then just mutters to himself, although loud enough so that Rush can hear. Syko: You know, I could always kill both of you. Rush shakes his head at the small man, then walks over to the door, opening it. He’ s rather surprised to spy Ray Ray standing directly in front of the door….and apparently engaged in some sort of scuffle with Syko’s Duck. Rush: Ray Ray! I swear to Bon Dieu, your brain must not function at all… The man looks up, holding the Duck by its two legs, and then drops the bird, letting it fall to the ground, but it flips over and lands on its legs, throwing an angry ‘Quack’ at Ray Ray, as the huge man tries to apologize. Ray Ray: Dawg…that duck was goin’ crazy like whoa….I was ‘bout to bust a cap. Rush shakes his head yet again, looking back once at Syko, who is now busy shining his title belt. Rush: Just come in….both of you. Ray Ray and the Duck both waddle, in surprisingly similar ways, into the room, as Syko looks up. Syko sees Ray Ray and hops to his feet, trying to look like the most intimidating 131-pound Goblin ever. Rush leans to Ray Ray, and “whispers” although Syko can hear. Rush: I know that Halloween is over, but don’t tell him. I think he likes that little world he lives in. Syko feigns a laugh, then looks back to his title, addressing the Duck as he continues to shine the gold. Syko: Duck, c’mere. We need a plan, because you and me are gonna carry those two to a win, and then we’ll have to kill them too. Over on the other side of the room, Rush rolls his eyes, while Ray Ray looks totally in space. Rush: Well….? Syko doesn’t even acknowledge the Voodoo Villain, he just continues to mutter under his breath and shine his title. Rush walks over to the small man and grabs the title out of his lap, once again bringing Syko leaping out of his chair. Syko: It would be a shame to have to fight a handicap match on Turmoil due to the death of my “partner…” Rush just chuckles to himself and holds the belt high above Syko’s head. Syko jumps once, but comes nowhere near the reach of the 6’4 Mason. Improvising, Syko snaps his fingers, and the Duck begins to peck at Rush’s ankles, while Syko leaps onto his back, trying to bite him. Rush: OWW!! CUT THAT OUT! Ray Ray! Don’t just stand there! While the trio of Rush Mason, Syko, and The Duck tumble around the room, Ray Ray just stands smiling, humming “Dilemma” to himself, and bobbing his head from side to side. Various exclamations of pain can be heard, and the camera finally settles on Rush, collapsed and panting on the floor, while Syko lies on the other side of the floor in the same state, and the Duck sits atop the Continental Belt. Rush: You…huge….buffoon! You’re….supposed….to….guard…me.. As Rush lets his voice out in gasps between huffs and puffs, Ray Ray snaps out of his near-trance and looks down at Rush, finally helping the Voodoo Villain up, while Syko hops to his feet as well. Ray Ray: Yo dawg…did I miss the shit go down? I would’ve had yo’ back and busted a cap, yaknowwha’imean? Rush dusts himself off, and then turns to Ray Ray, looking incredulously at the huge man as he raises his voice. Rush: Had my back?! You were too occupied with humming to yourself! That midget in a mask and his pet mallard almost disemboweled me! Ray Ray shrugs, and just returns to humming the song as Rush smacks his forehead violently against the wall for ever thinking Ray Ray would be helpful. Syko: So, I take it you had enough? You’re willing to fully recognize me as ruler of the universe now? Rush turns to the small man, who is puffing his chest out, and chuckles to himself, but doesn’t give in. Rush: Ruler of the universe? You tried to bite me, and then you gave me a titty twister. I hardly think a universal ruler would have to resort to such things. Syko turns to the Duck, and exclaims in a hushed voice. Syko: You know, I always did think that those trips across the Atlantic in those boats disabled their brains. The Duck nods in assurance, while Rush just sneers at Syko, who goes back, yet again to admiring his title belt. With that, Rush calls back to Syko as he starts to leave the room and Ray Ray follows in tow. Rush: Syko, when you finish your Halloween candy’s sugar high, come talk to me. Until then, I bid you and your filthy pet adieu. Syko makes no response, and as Rush and Ray Ray walk down the hall, they share a moment of enlightenment. “Dawg….that fight wasn’t my fault.” “And why not?” “Yo, that duck ain’t know how to keep it real…”
