Lord of the Allies

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 24, 2002 Horrified at what has just happened, Xamine Herr Kanyon backs slowly away from the still form of the Russian Bear. Mouthing silent words of outrage, he shakes his head back and forth with the debilitating realisation of the beast within himself. Bellie~! retreats to join him, and together they scamper back up onto their rock, trying desperately to increase the distance between themselves and the others, to fuel and support their growing denial. The others back up the other way, swaying insanely to some inner tribal music they all must hear, as close and comforting as conscience. The dead body seems to take on a fluorescent glow, lying there on the barren sand, free at last from the demons with whom he so unjustly shared the world. There is no regret or recognition in their eyes as they gaze down upon his surreal form, all remnants of sane thought and feeling having been long since abandoned. &#039;Look what you&#039;ve done,&#039; Herr Kanyon whispers softly, but in the stillness of this horrid aftermath his words carry to each and every one, accusing them of the foulest crime. Kanyon stares at them all with wide, unseeing eyes, scandalised at what he couldn&#039;t have done... Must not have done. &#039;Us?&#039; Nelson snaps, a strange smile curling his face. He raises an arm and points at Kanyon, naming him among them. &#039;I saw your hand rising and falling just as fast as anyone else&#039;s... Where is your blessed order now?&#039; He laughs, and the throng of evil spirits behind him joins in, howling at the moon like a depraved pack of wolves hot on the hunt. Again his mouth opens and closes without uttering a single word, his mind struggling for some defense, some denial that will set him free. Everything is falling apart, and Herr Kanyon knows it well. Time is running out for him, he can feel that as surely as his potential being squandered in his corporate position. &#039;Give us the pineapples, Xamine,&#039; Devil says in a low monotone, advancing a step in the darkness, regarding the man like an inanimate obstacle about to be ground to dust. &#039;Your time is done here.&#039; There is a general consensus from the mob, and Herr Kanyon hangs his head, defeated. &#039;Like, over my &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; dead body, Universal Peon,&#039; Bellie~! screams in a shrill voice, stepping in front of her husband in his unwavering defense. There is a tense moment of silence as the two factions face off, this contest of ambitions swirling like the solar wind. &#039;So be it,&#039; Devil says mechanically, a grin spreading over his face as he nonchalantly reaches into his breast pocket. His hand returns grasping a black object that glints and shines unholily in the fading light, and things move in slow motion for the married couple as he slowly raises the pistol to shoulder height. &#039;No!&#039; The anguished cry leaps from Herr Kanyon&#039;s throat, but is immediately drowned out by the ringing cry of a gunshot. With a sickening squishing sound, the slug tears into Bellie~!&#039;s supple chest, sending her hurtling backwards with the amazing force. She flies up and back, sailing over her disbelieving husband like a really good joke, and floats right off the other side of the rock. Kanyon&#039;s gaze follows her flight as if watching the world implode on itself. He drops to his knees and droops his heavy head into his hands, sobbing at his abrupt and irrevocable loss. &#039;The pineapples,&#039; Devil insists coldly in the ringing wake of the shot, aiming the gun at Herr Kanyon. Behind him the mob writhes and coalesces with unquestioning loyalty, certain beyond thought that they are doing right. &#039;I&#039;ll kill you,&#039; Xamine whispers, shaking with rage and adrenaline, then repeats himself with a mighty, vengeful shout, &#039;I&#039;ll kill you!&#039; as he leaps up from his kneeling position. Before he takes two steps the shot rings out clearly and sharply, hurtling a screaming bullet through his lower abdomen. Knocked off balance, he tumbles off the side of the rock and lands on his shoulder, which breaks on impact. A haunting and ominous chant of the single word &#039;kill&#039; begins somewhere in the crowd and is immediately taken up by all, rising in both pitch and crescendo like some illuminator&#039;s fireworks. The mob sweeps towards the rock like a giant oceanic wave, ready to bulldoze anything in its path, with Tyger Smells&#039;Em and Cruel Devil joyously taking the lead. Herr Kanyon grimaces as he forces himself up with his impaired limb, then turns just in time to see the wave come crashing down over the rock. Without even a passing glance for his leaking stomach, he turns and bolts across the beach and into the brush, zig-zagging his way along to avoid any steel equalizers that may cut his run short. His breathing is hoarse and rapid, but his mind makes it seem relatively calm and peaceful. Into and through the trees he runs, ignoring the branches reaching down to him like a mother&#039;s soothing arms, imploring him to stop and rest a moment. He crashes through the ruse and continues like a bat out of hell, smashing everything before him and so leaving a perfect path for his pursuers to follow. A mere ten feet behind him he can hear them, and they can see him. On and on this goes, with the pathetic shell that was once order fleeing before the insatiable hunger that is foundless greed. Herr Kanyon knows it must eventually come to an end, as he can&#039;t keep running forever, but something in his spirit won&#039;t allow him to succumb to the merciful demise that stopping would bring. All he knows is to keep running, fleeing from that which is himself but is at the same time wholly disturbing and unrecognisable. Like a wild animal before a forest fire he bursts from the other side of the forest, diving onto the open expanse of the beach as if into the cooling waters of some heavenly fountain. The pleasure is short-lived, however, as he hears the crowd crashing through right behind him. Leaking his life all over the sand, he rises hastily and begins to run, then stops dead in his tracks, an entirely new look of horror spreading across his sweaty, dirty face. Behind him the crashing and yelling abruptly ceases, and only the gentle whiffing of helicopter blades can be heard. Standing on the beach before them all is a grand man wearing an impressive black suit and an equally impressive scowl. His arms are folded across his natural barrel chest, and one toe taps slowly and rhythmically against the sand, the timeless display of knowledgeable impatience. His face is obscured by a thin layer of beige nylon, but from beneath the pantyhose two burning eyes stare out like those of a prowling wolf. The crowd before him shifts uneasily under that gaze, knowing that they&#039;ve been physically saved but at the same time realising they are forever changed. Devil and Tyger share a nervous, calculating glance as Herr Kanyon collapses at the newcomer&#039;s feet, grasping at his pant leg and sobbing out his frustration. &#039;Back!&#039; The Phantom Stalker roars, whipping the soiled leg away from the bleeding mess before him. There is a collective cringe at the scolding tone of his voice, but before he can continue a cry of deepest, mindless sorrow fills the air. &#039;They killed Bellie~!&#039; Xamine Herr Kanyon screams in strangled anguish. &#039;Bellie~! I say!&#039; He slumps down to the ground on his side, sobbing uncontrollably once more. Two dozen faces begin studying the ground with feverish scrutiny, suddenly finding it immensely interesting. The Phantom Stalker glares at each in turn, then pokes Herr Kanyon with the toe of his shoe. &#039;So what?&#039; TPS asks in a blunt and heartless tone, smirking away the unimportant matter. &#039;That&#039;s not any reason for you fools to be stranded here on this island, bleeding like stuck pigs. Do you know how much money you&#039;ve cost me this week? Do you know what happened to all my main events?! &#039; He demands of everyone in general, and there is shamed muttering from all. &#039;We had to rely on PTC for ratings, for fuck&#039;s sake. ...I&#039;m glad the little slut is dead,&#039; he declares, smiling down at Herr Kanyon&#039;s scandalised face. &#039;Who, like, said I was dead?&#039; Bellie~! demands in her haughty, preppy voice, coming free of the jungle without another sound. Every pair of eyes immediately zoom to her chest, which is slicked and shining with blood. She rolls her eyes and judges them all as one, &#039;like... Ewzzies.&#039; &#039;Bellie~!&#039; Herr Kanyon shouts, his voice full and rich with arrogant joy once more. He bounds to his feet and rushes towards her, arms wide open, but is ultimately deprived of his hug. Behind him, The Phantom Stalker groans and reaches into his own breast pocket, producing his own handgun. With a malicious grin he aims straight at Bellie~!&#039;s head and fires, sending the bullet tearing directly through her left eye socket. Half her head splattered over those around her, Ellie~! drops to the ground from a bullet for the second time tonight. Herr Kanyon turns around slowly, disbelieving. &#039;There,&#039; TPS announces, &#039;let that be a lesson to everyone.&#039; He pauses for effect, condescendingly eyeing everyone in attendance. &#039;Don&#039;t waste my time or money, or you wind up dead, just like little Bel-&#039; he cuts off suddenly and uncharacteristically, his own eyes going wide beneath the thin layer of fabric. His mouth hangs open, speechless, for the first time in his life. &#039;You, like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; can&#039;t kill Bellie~!, silly,&#039; Bellie~! giggles preppily, once again on her feet. The gaping hole in her head is slowly caving, filling in like some watery gelatin, or a liquid metal from some grand movie that shall remain nameless. There is a moment of profound silence as the word degrades into this senseless bullshit, and Bellie~!&#039;s eyes suddenly grow blood red. &#039;But she can, like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; kill you!&#039; She shrieks with mounting menace, turning and simultaneously decapitating three PCW superstars with the dreaded Prep Slap O&#039; Doom~! Horrified cries fill the air as the wrestlers scamper around like a bunch of headless chickens, futilely trying to escape the phenom that is Bellie~! Having seized the moment of confusion when she first sprung up, Tyger Smells&#039;Em and Cruel Devil leap into the helicopter and insist that the helicopter take off immediately. The Phantom Stalker only arrives as it rises out of reach, but manages to leap and grab onto a foot railing, from which he dangles precariously as it carries those three alone to safety above the high seas. Bellie~! continues on her rampage, Prep Slap of Doom~!ing everyone who straggles within reach, except for Herr Kanyon, who can only sit there with a stunned and somewhat aroused expression on his face. Her being a robot raises all kinds of bedroom possibilities, all of which will be immediately doable on this remote tropical island. The Lord - or Lady - of the Allies reigns alone and supreme at the acme of wrestling, having effortlessly destroyed all the competition in her path. From deep within its core the island bellows with laughter, having known all along that it would come to this, yet all the while stringing you happily along under the guise of something serious and meaningful. Don&#039;t you feel, like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; foolish?
