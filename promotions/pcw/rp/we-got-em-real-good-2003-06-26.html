We got em' real good!

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th June 2003 :: The PCW airship is full of people, so full, that if one wanted to have a party, one would have to do so in their own room if they did not want ‘gate crashers’. Sivasian Krikorian is smart, if he holds a party he wants it to be private, to only have the people he wants to be there. With the various ‘party animals’ around PCW, he couldn’t take any chances, and so ‘being adventurous’ and booking the on board movie theater was not going to happen. He knew how people like Skylar Green would more than willingly get to see the latest movie with all the food trimmings for free was something he would never pass. And so the small and cosy room of Sivasian Krikorian, which he has only had 2 days in so far, becomes the place where he is more than happy to allow the Society of Order and Logic into. And so, as Michael Chang and Society walk through the door, albeit with some problems for Society with his height, they bring with them party things. Food aplenty, or at least some sausage rolls. Sivasian Krikorian looks more than happy to see them, and is indeed dressed for the occasion. :: Sivasian: Welcome my fellow Society of Order and Logic members! :: SOCIETY nods and immediately heads over to the food table, where various assortments of drinks and food is set out. Cheese and pineapple on a stick immediately take his eye, but he finds it hard with his particularly large hands taking such small quantities of cheddar and sweet fruit from a small cocktail stick. He seems happy to Chang, who enjoys for once seeing SOCIETY enjoying various amounts of sausages, plain biscuits with cheese and small pieces of pizza. Krikorian walks over to Chang and speaks out of ear shot of SOCIETY. :: Sivasian: Does SOCIETY drink, Michael? Chang: He does not, he needs to keep sober in order to make the most out of making people conform! Sivasian: But yourself, you’re prone to the odd cocktail then? Chang: Heh, I’m human, ain’t I? Sivasian: Well, I wouldn’t.. :: Chang glares a dirty stare towards Krikorian who nods and smirks slightly. :: Sivasian: Of course you are my fellow Order and Logic member! And for this special occasion I have gone over the edge with food, which SOCIETY is enjoying and champagne for you and I! :: Sivasian walks over to the white draped food table before bringing out a bag with a holder and 6 bottles of champagne inside. He places it on the table, and slides 2 wine glasses from the table over to himself. He pops the cork and hands out the glass to Chang, both swig down the drink before tapping glasses. :: Chang: Indeed you throw a ‘mean’ party Krikorian. :: Chang pans over the room to see a messed up bed, himself, Sivasian and SOCIETY eating at the food table. Sivasian sighs but decides to continue with his speaking with Chang. :: Sivasian: Why, us three here are meaner and more intelligent than any 30 or more party goers, trust me on that. Chang: Indeed with my brains, you’re brains and meanness and SOCIETY’s power and meanness, we can rule PCW for years to come! Sivasan: I’m not getting any younger, but still, things will tilt our way this Friday when me and SOCIETY rip through Extreme Entertainment to win the tag-team titles! Chang: Of course, another party, this time in SOCIETY’s room would have to be in order. :: Sivasian nods as he listens intently to Chang. SOCIETY continues to chug down cola and gateux in the corner. :: Sivasian: Of course, the real reason we are partying is that the Society of Order and Logic have pulled the biggest SWERVE~! In PCW history! Chang: Of course, I can’t believe the whole world thought you and SOCIETY were honestly going to beat the living hell out of each other, without a care in the world, even though you’d gotten on so well for almost two months. :: Sivasian smirks as he pours himself some more campagne. :: Sivasian: That is , Chang, because no one else in PCW seems to have the thought and intelligent pattern that me, you and SOCIETY process. :: This is contradicted slightly as a suit wearing SOCIETY turns round, his massive frame, with chocolate around his mouth. Both Sivasian and Chang laugh slightly and cling glasses again. :: Sivasian: I hope my one shot one kill didn’t hurt the big man, sorry if I did. :: Chang waves his hand in the air. :: Chang: No need, the man CANNOT be and WILL NOT be hurt from now on in PCW, I think you can harness the monster inside him. Sivasian: Indeed, when we win the tag-team titles we will be unstoppable, and more and more people WILL conform to the way of the Order! Chang: You and SOCIETY completely outsmarted PCW, and you can do it again, not just for the tag-team titles, but once you get a fair shot at that title of Tessa Windsor’s, I think you can go out and make the Order bigger and better! Sivasian: What’s stopping such a talented group of wrestlers and managers winning title after title, and making the average fan and wrestler in PCW look like a dumbass? Chang: Precisely my son! :: Chang and Sivasian ching glasses again. :: Siavsian: A pork pie, Chang? Chang: Why not?
