The Grappling Gourmet: Part #2

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/07/02 The camera fades in… We see a still image of the Chef ready to pull out a culinary utensil of destruction to oppress the crowd. The image begins to play. The Chef whips out his wooden rolling pin and sets the container behind him. The Chef: Who wants a go!?!? Announcer: Just a reminder to our audience: each and every one of you has been given a replica of the Chef’s rolling pin as a free gift for joining us today! The Chef suddenly freaks out. The Chef: Leaping linguine!!! Every single member in the crowd looks under their seats for the rolling pin. Announcer: It’s in your gift bag on the side of your seat! The Chef: Shut up Charles!!! Announcer: Sorry Chef, just doing my job… The Chef turns and sees rows upon rows up angry food show patrons, ready to become vigilantes fighting for truth, justice, and mocha cake… “F*CKING WATCH OUT MOTHA F*CKAAAASSS!!!” Suddenly the backstage door bursts open, and Bertha Stewart, clad in a skin tight black leather dominatrix outfit stands in the doorway, holding a cattle prod, a pack of pork rings, another pack of pork rinds, and a Nerf Bow and Arrow set. Bertha: I’m here to f*cking save you f*ckface!!! As the crowd advances on the Chef, Bertha begins her mission. She fights through the endless rows of people, tossing them aside, stampeding over them, zapping them with the cattle prod Mountie-style, and shooting Poisoned-tipped Nerf Arrows at them. Actually they aren’t poison-tipped, she just like to stick them up her ass… The Chef: What is that stench… Isn’t dramatic irony great!?!? The Chef: Bertha, I can’t hold out much longer, the bone fragments are starting to accumulate on my rolling pin; it’s too heavy to swing!!! Bertha heeds the Chef’s urgent call for help and fights through the crowd with an even greater ferocity. The image of Bertha coming through the crowd pauses. Announcer: Last time we left Bertha and the Chef, they were in serious trouble! A gang of espresso-hungry crowd members has threatened to take out the Chef on his own television show! Bertha came to help her man, but the seemingly endless number of spectators to trample over has great diminished her rescue attempt. Let’s continue on with what seems to be another mind-numbingly stupid Chef promo!!! The still image begins to play again. Bertha: F*CKO!!! The Chef: Bertha! My time is dwindling!!! SMACK!!! The Chef takes out an elderly man grasping for the can of coffee with a rolling pin shot to the head. The Chef: Man, the stench of the corpses really drowns out my garlic cloves, canned anchovies, and microwave Taquitos… CRACK!!! The Chef accidentally takes out a food preparation aid who was just setting some half n’ half on the counter. The Chef: My bad! Sheesh, it is times like these when I wish I still had Osama Bin Laden’s Discover Card… As the Chef continues to struggle against the overwhelming odds, Bertha stampedes through the crowd like a mother rhinoceros chasing after her baby. Jeff: Quick, someone call some poachers! I heard ivory is worth a lot now on the world markets! Dave: You’re thinking of an elephant you nimrod, besides we aren’t even supposed to be in this promo!!! Jeff: Says you!!! Where’s Duke!?!? Dave: Well since Duke is the property of another handler, he cannot appear in a Chef promo without the expressed consent of Duke Williams’ handler in perpetuity throughout the universe… Jeff: Damn, whoever thought an “ol’ cowpoke” would be attached to such legal jargon… Dave nods and sighs. BACK TO THE PROMO!!! Bertha: I’m coming Lord F*ckington!!! Bertha runs over a small man, turning his once lively body into a bunch of inanimate paste… The Chef quickly hops up onto the counter and reaches out, trying to grab the hand of Bertha, who inches closer and closer to her man. The Chef kicks a few crowd members in the face, knocking the fillings out of Florence Henderson, who smelt the can of espresso from a Lifetime Channel Set two blocks away. The Chef: Tell Super Poli-Grip to fix that your Brady Bitch!!! Henderson: MMMMMMMPHHH HAAAMMPPPHHHH!!! The Chef though is HAMMERED FROM BEHIND BY A VENGEFUL RICKI LAKE!!! Ricki Lake: Give me back my virginity!!! The Chef: Ha! You should be thanking me! You would have never gotten that part in ‘Cabin Boy’ unless I won that Chris Elliot look-alike contest!!! Bertha: You f*cking lost your c*ck @$$ed virginity to that f*cking c*ck hungry b*tch @$$ f*cking skank ho!?!? The Chef: Ah yes, I remember as if it were yesterday… We were eyeing each other at that Bistro…I slid her a piece of my long French loaf and she let me try a fantastic fish taco… We then went behind a bush near the Eiffel Tower and made sweet sweet love until I climaxed prematurely… SMACK!!! But before the Chef can reminisce any longer, HE’S NAILED FROM BEHIND WITH A MEAT TENDERIZER BY TV CHEF EMERIL LAGASSE!!! The Chef: Emer…il? The Chef drops face first onto the counter. The Chef: Why are all my past enemies attacking me at this very moment? Bertha: Hey, when it f*cking rains, it f*cking pours… As a toothless Florence Henderson, blubber-filled Ricki Lake, and diabolical Emeril Lagasse wail on the Chef, Bertha comes to the rescue… Bertha: F*cking make way ya f*cking queer as f*ck mother f*ckers!!! Bertha DRILLS FLORENCE HENDERSON WITH A DEVASTATING CLOTHESLINE!!! The extra rolls of blubber on his arm twist and turns, provide a great increase in arm speed velocity as she overpowers the older woman and sends her flying into the air, landing head first on the cold unforgiving floor after a 480 in the air! Bertha: Who’s f*cking next!?!? Gillberg: Me!?!? Bertha: F*cking no! YOU!!! Bertha grabs Ricki Lake by the head and FORCES A PAIR OF KITCHEN SHEARS UP HER NOSE!!! Bertha then grabs the handles and PULLS THEM APART, STRECHING OUT THE NOSE OF LAKE!!! Bertha: Say f*cking d*ck @$$ uncle!!! Lake: Dick ass uncle! Dick ass uncle! Bertha: Wait a f*cking second, how come your f*cking cuss words aren’t f*cking censored like my f*cking ones!?!? Lake shrugs, as does Bertha. Bertha: Oh f*cking well… Bertha tosses Lake aside and concentrates on Emeril! Emeril: BAM! BAM! BAM! BAM! Emeril continues laying the Chef out with Tenderizer shots to the forehead! The Chef’s head begins to look more and more like ground chuck!!! Emeril: When I’m through with you, you’re face will be in between a couple of buns at the nearest White Castle!!! BAM!!! The Chef: White Castle!?!? At least give me some credit! My meat is Burger King quality, at least!!! Emeril nods, and THEN FEELS THE PAIN OF A BIG BERTHA BEAR HUG!!! Emeril: BAM! I didn’t even see that one coming! YEEEOUCH!!! Bertha lifts up the rather puny Lagasse and PLANTS HIM RIGHT ON THE COUNTER WITH A BEAR HUG SLAM!!! Bertha: Pour the f*cking coffee out now f*cko!!! The Chef does as he’s instructed, pouring the rest of the espresso on the maimed body of the TV chef Emeril, and then jumps towards Bertha, who catches him and books it out of the studio. The relentless caffeine buzz-driven crowd sniffs the air and recognizes the familiar smell. They begin to gang up on Emeril, grabbing and clawing at him to get just one spec of the oh so tasty powder… Crowd: We…want…mocha… Emeril screams in terror as the zombified spectators suffocate him to a certain death. Bertha and the Chef make their way out of the studios and are greeted by TV Food Network CEO Sissy Biggers. Bertha: What the f*ck are you doing here skank @$$!?!? Biggers embraces The Chef, who is still in the arms of Bertha. Biggers: Thank you Mr. Alouette for the best live ratings we’ve seen in years! The crowd deaths were a really nice touch! The Chef leaps down from Bertha’s grasp. The Chef: What are you talking about!?!? Those whackos went crazy when YOUR STAFF didn’t provide me with the proper ingredients to make a mocha cake! There is a serious amount of carnage in that studio thanks to you… Sissy smiles and nods as if she isn’t even paying any attention to the Chef’s remarks. She quickly shakes the Culinary Conquistador’s hand again. Biggers: Great ratings, keep it up! BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! Bigger: Damn, that’s my pager, gotta run! As the CEO sprints off to wherever she was called, the Chef and Bertha sit and ponder their future with the TV Network. The Chef: This is strange B, we’ve probably killed more people today then we did in my entire tenure as an Islamic Extremist… Bertha: Yeah, but the f*cking money kicks f*cking @$$!!! The Chef: You’re right, let’s give it another shot and try to do at least 11 episodes, that way we can collect royalties from the reruns!!! Bertha: F*cking hell yeah, b*tch @$$ mother f*cking c*ck sucking piece of shit @$$ed ho skank retarded @$$ licking ho riding f*cking @$$hole!!! The Chef and Bertha embrace. The Chef: I love you Bertha… Bertha: I love you too f*cking mocha, I mean Chef!!! Bertha pats the Chef on the back as they continue to hug. The camera mercifully fades to black.
