Hero vs. Hiro....Why You Will NEVER Mix the Two Up

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Hero vs. Hiro....Why You Will NEVER Mix the Two Up Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 6-4-2002 OOC:Excuse me slighta slow workrate in light of the RPG3 tourney, but it shouldn't effect my quality or what not and it will still be quite respectable so no worries PCW fans. Continue on. ..::/::..We begin our sto..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'CUT CUT CUT NARRATOR MAN!! YOUR DESCRIPTIONS OF INCREDIBLE DESCRIPTIVNESS MUST CEASE FOR THE MOMENT!!' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero, what's wrong...I thought we were supposed to continue the 'WWE/PCW Crossover' series....what's going on?..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Well...read this and it shall all make sense to you.' ..::/::..Our hero tosses up a thick white script into the heavens. I shortly take hand of the script, reading through the poorly written pages as I begin the 'new' RP. We find Rent-A-Hero standing against a plain white backdrop, arms folded and donning his black and white skintight Venom spandex replica costume. His usual happy-go-lucky facial expressions have been replaced by looks of disdain and anger as he stares directly into the viewing audience of those watching at home. Our hero clears his throat, waving to those at home before addressing them in his normal heroic tone that has become commonplace in a Rent-A-Hero RP..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Citizens...young and old....beautiful and mugly...alien and homosapien...girl an...' ..::/::..Get on with it Rent-A-Hero..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Oh yeah right, thank you narrator man. Anyways, I am appaled, shocked, outraged, and flabbergasted by the blatant gimmick infringement and confusion created by my Tuesday Turmoil match-up...in reference to the one I have against that no good, refutiable nemesis of mine Hiro Satome! Now of course we know that your hero shall prevail and give him a mighty smiting worthy of a loud gasp and a shocked look upon your faces...but the confusion between THIS Hero and THAT Hiro must cease. I am sick of going to the candy store and having little children approach me while saying, 'ARE YOU HIRO SATOME?! YOU SURE DON'T LOOK JAPANESE!!' ARRRRGH, BY THE POWER OF GREYSKULL, this must end. This is why I am prepared to show all the differences between me and this, Hiro, and prove why he will be nothing but a run through on Tuesday Turmoil. First off...STYLE. Now it's no question that Hiro has a very....how we say...BLAND style, but how can you confuse that with the cutting edge, Kryptonian supermodel style of one RENT-A-HERO?! SLIDES!!'Category 1-Style..::/::..The lights dim and a full screen picture of Hiro Satome comes over the white screen, our hero unaware he is obstructing the view as his shadow overcomes the entire projection. He turns about, looking at his shadow in the slide and costruing his hands to make a dog shadow puppet..::/::..Rent-A-Hero: *doggy voice* 'RENT-RA-RERO...RU RA RAAAAAAN!! RUF RUF.' *looks back to the audience* 'Oh oh oh sorry....hehe, kinda hard for you citizens to see with me in the way huh? Heh...'..::/::..Our hero steps off to the side, the picture of Hiro in full view on the white projection screen as RAH shakes his head in shame from the side..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'LOOK AT HIM!! Notice the sea-bass looking face...John Travolta wannabe haircut that is as ancient as Pong and G.I. Joe Under-roos...and the red and black Marc Mero rip-off boxers...Hiro...Hiro...Hiro...BY THE CLONES OF CASMARICK, if your gonna rip someone off...rip off someone successful, not 'Sable's husband'. Take notice of the supposed 'six-pack' he brandishes. I mean come on citizens, it looks like a radioactive mosquito stung him in six equilateral spots and he's waiting for the puss to drain out. There's a major difference between METORIC MUSCLE and FIENDISH FAT citizens. Now...let's go to the OTHER HERO...' ..::/::..We switch to a new slide, showing none other than Rent-A-Hero himself standing in full glory behind his PCW-tron as he X-Box Shox's Juan Borro. He is wearing his red and black Spiderman replica costume which accent his well developed body and all the while, our hero sits back and almost weeps at the sight of how heroic and nice he looks in the photo..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Now as we can see...THAT is the epitome of style! Tight spandex and built in web shooters make this the ultimate party, sleep, or formal attire for any occasion. Just look at those muscles and abs that can only be sculpted and crafted on the planet of Krypton. Don't even let me get started on the women who fall for the 'tall, masked, and spandex' type. What more can I say, I'm always ahead of the game, and I don't mean Playstation2, in style good Earthlings! Now for our next category...MOVESETS!!' Category 2-Movesets..::/::..Our hero snaps his fingers and a sldie switches to a printed listing...or lack thereof, of Hiro's moveset as Rent-A-Hero looks about on the slide...baffled by how much space is on it for saying it's a moveset page...::/::.. Hiro's Moveset Hanzako Combo (Left jab (face), right cross (face), left spinning back fist (face) followed up a roundhouse to the face (right foot) )Evolution (Cradle Brainbuster bridged into a pin) Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY MOVESET MALNUTRITION?! THAT'S IT?! HE DOSEN'T EVEN HAVE ANY COOL NAMES FOR THE MOVES!! That's all the offense this so called 'Hiro' has for me?! Oh please, he couldn't even defeat a Ganglorian space hog with the weak offensive. Hiro is gonna need a lot more then some silly Tae-Bo and a weak suplex to put Rent-A-Hero on the shelf! Now...let's get into MY moveset...' ..::/::..The slide switches, Rent-A-Hero's moves taking up a large section of the slide as he smile with pride at the many moves he is known for in the ring. He places his hands on his hips and pokes his chest out, putting his hand out so as to call attention to his moves before saying..::/::..Rent-A-Hero's Moveset The Final Fantasy (Sleeperhold Neckbreaker) The Gamma Ray Rush (2 Dragon Suplexes In A Row and A Sit Out Full Nelson Slam) The Krillain Buzzkill (Chickenwing release suplex) La Blue Girl Curl (Shooting Star Press) Sailor Moon Dust (Diamond Dust) Escaflowne's Ray (Springboard Moonsault) Anime Agony (Reverse Figure 4) The Sonic Spin (Sideways Corkscrew Splash) Twisted Metal Black (Full Nelson Front Face Slam) Spiderman Splash (Leap Frog Crossbody) Various Suicide Dives and Planchas The Green Lantern Rings (Butterfly Arm Lock) PS1-Plancha Sault 1 (Hurricanranna off the top rope Lita Style) Super Saien Scissors (Flying Head Scissors) Lasso Of Truth (Bow and Arrow Back stretcher) and Thor's Hammer (Cradle Piledriver) The Sinistro Series (Three jabs, spin in place into a split, and comes back up with a clothesline) PS2 (Gets his opponent in the position on the top rope like he;s going to powerbomb them off, but he sunset flips over his opponent instead and holds, which drives them off the turnbuckle with a powerbomb, which he holds for the pin.) The X-Box Shox (Cross-arm Bridging German Suplex ) The Gravity Defier (He grabs his opponent in a Diamond Cutter position and runs up a turnbuckle and backflips off, driving his opponent down with an elevated reverse DDT) Rent-A-Hero:'FEAST YOUR EYES ON THIS BUFFET OF BEAUTIFUL AND BRUTALITIZING MOVES! Hiro...how are you gonna get the chance to punch me and kick me wiht your girly 'My Little Ponytails' moves when I have this incredible dispensible arsenal you so called 'Hiro'! With names like the 'Spiderman Splash' and 'Twisted Metal Black' you know those moves mean business!! No longer will ANYBODY mistake me for you and your fiendish way for I am better then you in every way possible and today is gonna prove it. NEXT CATEGORY...CATCHPHRASES!! Category 3:CatchphrasesRent-A-Hero:'So tell me, when 'Hiro' gets ready to stand up to the forces of evil or and face adversaries that would make mere mortals quiver....what does he say to strike fear in there hearts?!'Hiro:'Hi. Yes I do exist.'..::/::..Eerie silence overcomes Rent-A-Hero as she shakes his head in disdain, appaled by the choice of catchphrase Hiro has used in this situation..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'I know what your saying citizens, 'Fear not, Hiro has something of INTELLIGENCE and STRENGTH to say to an evildoer' , correct? Well citizens...don't get your hopes up. Expecting THAT Hiro to have something exciting to say is like having expecting Billy Gunn to get an RVD workrate anytime soon. Hiro's secondary miracle catchphrase?!' Hiro:'So yeah…uhh…that’s about it from here…bye?' Rent-A-Hero:'I digress citizens...could this be anymore of a slaughter? HOLY SHIZZNITTLEBAM and BY THE POWER OF GREYSKULL are obvious catchphrases that you citizens enjoy and strike terror into the hearts of disposable villains! What have I next to smite Hiro with....awards and achievments!' Category 4-Awards And Achievements..::/::..The slides switch to various scenes of Rent-A-Hero in all his glory...winning the PCW Crusierweight Championship....defeating Snow while coming in as the underdog....his lengthy and resiliant Rising Star title reign. The moments run one by one on the slide, Rent-A-Hero so proud of what he's done as he used a Kleenex to wipe the tears running down the spandex cheek of his mask..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Look at my title history...look at the many fans I make happy on a daily basis with my wrestling and Super-Saien powers! I'm the FIRST EVER Wrestlingtattler ranked superhero ever! My accomplishments speak volumes and they say 'MAN HE'S GOOD!!' As for Hiro...well....I don't remember when pink slips counted for anything...unless you want to make claim to the record of most pink slips in a 3 month period you so called 'Hiro'. There's a catchphrase for you Hiro, HOLY PINK SLIPS OF UNEMPLOYMENT!! And face facts Hiro...after our match on Turmoil, you'll want to quit after beaing forced to go one on one with the POWER SPIRE that is Rent-A-Hero you fiend! You may have the power to be one fo the laziest men to ever coin a job in PCW, but you don't have the power to keep my shoulders down for the three you 'Hero' wannabe! Now for our next category...' Man Off-screen:'HOLD IT RIGHT THERE MR.HERO!!'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero looks to the right, the slides ending abruptly as a group of three burly officers upsurp and begin to force Rent-A-Hero to the wall, forcing his hands behind his back as a set of hand-cuffs are taken out by the lead officer..::/::..Officer:'You are in violation of PCW Copyright laws. On section 83746346346678954994 article 72432174874864454546 of the 87434543436639 copyright code, it says....'IF YOU STEAL SH** FROM THE SITE, YOUR GOIN' TO JAIL'!!! Now come on spandex boy, away you go!!' Rent-A-Hero:'BUT OFFICERS, I WAS JUST PROVING A POINT!! HOLY HANDCUFFS OF HANDLING, THIS ISN'T RIGHT!! DARN, WHERE ARE THOSE SPECIAL DONUTS WITH THE SLEEPING POWDERED SUGAR WHEN YOU NEED THEM!! OH COME ON GUYS, THE CITIZENS IN JAIL ARE MEAN...AND THEY ALWAYS STARE AT MY BOM BOM!!'Officer:'Just don't drop the soap or they'll do something else to your bom-bom. COME ON BOY!!' ..::/::..With that, the copyright breaking hero is hauled away by the three burly officers, the scene fading as Rent-A-Hero had laid down why he is not to be compared with the other 'Hiro'.
