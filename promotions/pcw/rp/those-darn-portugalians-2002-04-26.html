Those Darn Portugalians!

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/02 The camera fades in… The Chef and Bertha sit on the oh so comfy Motel 6 bed in their deluxe suite. The Chef watches videos of PCW action, including Juan Borro’s blasphemy before International Incident, well at least what’s left of it, and most importantly, Allah! The Chef grimaces and groans with each passing moment of video footage. Bertha sits quietly on the other side, knitting some socks for Manuel Hung. The half Asian/Mexican toddler is nowhere to be found, possibly hiding in a gym bag. The Chef, in a display of frustration and anger towards the images on the television set, tosses the remote control at the TV and dents part of the rabbit ear’s antenna attached to the contraption. The Chef: Is it my fault that culinary school dropouts like Juan Borro inhabit the PCW on such a large scale. Bertha does not reply, she continues knitting. The Chef: I’m sure as sugar that Allah won’t stand for this blatant infidelity! Bertha continues not to answer with a cuss word filled commentary. The Chef: And what’s worst of all, he never acknowledged anything I did for him. I fed him, clothed him, sang him chorales to Allah, told him Islamic Militant bed times stories, and bought him that Snickers one time in the grocery checkout lane! Bertha finally replies… Bertha: That wasn’t f*cking sh*tty @$$ Juan Borro, ho b*tch, that was Manuel, f*ckface! The Chef: Oh yeah, my mistake, I have trouble telling those damn Mexicans apart… Bertha: He ain’t no f*cking Mexican, he’s a queer as f*ck Portugalian!!! The Chef: A Portugalian you say? Then he hales from….BUT OF COURSE!!! He’s a native of…(Trying to hide his geographic ignorance) Paraguay? Bertha: F*ck no mother f*cker! The Chef: Hmmm, that’s the only country I can think of that starts with a “P”. Bertha: How about f*cking Panama, sh*tty as b*tch @$$ f*ck Peru, dumb @$$ Poland, and f*cking damn Portugal, that’s where the f*cker was born, f*ckdaddy!!! The Chef: (Smugly) I see, well I didn’t go to college like some people… Bertha: (Sternly) And it f*cking shows!!!The Chef: So he’s a Portugalian, that explains his infidel nature! Those Portugalians have always been a bunch of what us French call, “Retards”! Bertha: Well actually f*cko, the Portugalians have f*cking been the pioneers of the sh*tty @$$ amphibious navi-f*cking @$$-gation to the Americas!!! The Chef: Yeah, well while they were off in Allah knows where preaching their blasphemous and slanderous passages, the French were making the finest cheese and wine in the world! Bertha: F*cking true, I never had a piece of Brie I didn’t f*cking inhale, f*cking c*ck sucker!!! The Chef: And while most of us French haven’t received the true message from Allah, they are pretty swell people in this Islamic Militant’s book. Bertha: Would you f*cking suicide bomb them, f*cko? The Chef: In a second, but only because I have my priorities straight. I remember the emphatic words of the great Islamic prophet Abdul Mohammed Abdul Abdul Mohammed Abdul who once said, “Allah first, Tail later”, and I apply that to my life… Bertha scratches her head, wondering if the Chef would sacrifice her for Allah. Bertha: How about f*cking me!?!? The Chef: What about you? Bertha: Would you f*cking do away with me if that c*ck sucking @$$ humping b*tch ho Allah f*cking wanted me f*cking dead, f*cking huh!?!? The Chef: I…ummmm…you…hmmmm…me…ahhhhhh…but of cour…no…. Bertha anxiously awaits the Chef’s reply. The Chef: You know what my portly princess, my Big B, my ravishing rhino, I’d…darn it, I’d have to sacrifice you in the name of Allah, Allah, ALLAH!!! That remark quickly produces a smack to the face by Bertha. Bertha: Why did you have to say his f*cking name three sh*tty @$$ times, mother f*cker!!! The Chef: (Shrugging) Allah’s so nice, I said his name thrice??? SMACK!!!Bertha gives up her knitting and continues the discussion with the Chef. To the surprise of the Culinary Couple, Manuel Hung enters into their view, wiping the dried mucus from his sleepy eyes. Bertha: Little mother f*cker!!! Manuel: Hello Clone Mommy, Cheffy…I dis many years old!!! He holds up two fingers. The Chef: Tell me something Allah doesn’t know! Bertha rolls her eyes. Bertha: What the f*ck do ya want!?!? Manuel: Where are my socks Clone Mommy? I have been living barefoot for so long. Yakov does not believe in foot protection… The Chef: Darn Russians… Bertha: They are f*cking coming up, just give me a few f*cking queer as f*ck secs to f*cking finish the f*cking culottes!!! The Chef: You still haven’t finished those!?!? How am I supposed to pretend to be a teen girl on a suicide mission without culottes!?!? Bertha shrugs. Manuel: Can I have a pop? Bertha: F*cking sure! Big B tosses the tyke a few quarters out of her pocket and the little boy scampers off to the Coke Machine to grab undoubtedly an Orange Soda. The Chef: He’ll probably going to get another Sunkist or Minute Maid soda! Oh Allah, why didn’t you bless us with a strong manly boy who likes Perrier!?!? Bertha: Maybe because he f*cking ain’t real! The Chef: (Infuriated) Enough with your slanderous tongue, you whore of Flabylon! I haven’t heard a lie so hateful since they said Tom Cruise was gay!!! Bertha: But didn’t you, f*cking Chester Bin Laden and queer as f*ck Dick Vitale penetrate the sh*tty @$$ b*tching lair of Cruise and his f*cking homo army!?!? The Chef: Come to think of it, you’re right! I wonder why I forget such a thing… Bertha: F*cking El Nino? The Chef nods. The Chef: Possibly my handler is just THAT moronic! Bertha: Ok, hmmm I f*cking think this promo has go f*cking sh*tty @$$ nowhere! The Chef: Once again...the handler... Bertha nods affirmatively. The camera fades out on the Culinary Couple.
