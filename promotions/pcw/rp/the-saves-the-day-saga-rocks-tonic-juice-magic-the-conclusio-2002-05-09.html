The Saves the Day Saga: Rocks Tonic Juice Magic (The Conclusion)

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9th, 2002 DarkStar sat down with Talena on a park bench just off of the boardwalk. It was a warmer then usual Thursdya in May in Atlantic City. She held a ice cream cone in her hand, gingerly licking at it. DarkStar marveled at the fact that she could stay so perfect while holding a frozen snack made to look like a upside down Clown head with whipped cream and cherry eyes. DarkStar opted for the Bugs Bunny Bannana Split. He stared over it, wondering what the hell was going on. Five minutes ago he was complaining about his horrible draw in the card game of life. And now he was looking over a bunny in a plastic cup with bananas for its ears. DarkStar: I wish I was dead... Talena spoke with her mouth full. Talena: Whafth? DarkStar: Nothing lover. Talena swallowed the dairy treat and grinned, kissing DarkStar on the nose lightly. Talena: Thanks for taking me here. DarkStar gave her a smirk, but obviously had something else on his mind. She noticed and asked him. Talena: Whats a matter darling, something bothering you? He gave her one of those looks that you normally give someone who just asked the dumbest question on the face of the planet. DarkStar: Whats a matter? WHATS A MATTER? I am forced into a existence that even the dead would feel sorry for me for! I have to shuffle my way through this world as a extention of a deity pecking order that doesnt even exist in the rational human mind! I want to kill every last one of these worthless skin bags that plod along aimlessly in this life! DarkStar stops in mid arguement, his arms in the air, and looks around at the group of people that have stopped and nervously look on. DarkStar: ...erm... except for all of you. Your all fine. A small murmor of relief and the crowd walks away. DarkStar continues, but less animated. DarkStar: I can't even find relief at the work place, my main cause in my job is to physically injure those I am pitted against, I do it well, and get held back, mocked, disrespected. I'm sick of it. I have to look on as the world collapses on itself, as PCW collapses on itself, all because the important people dissapeared and noone payed attention to me! Talena tried to point something out to DarkStar, but he couldn't be silenced. DarkStar: First, everyone of any worth in PCW jumps ship like the rats they are, and I stay here and get NOTHING, then... to top THAT off, That selfish son of a bitch Death beats Fate in a chess game! And POOF! A eternity of Other World history is blinked away, and who is forced to clean it up? ME! I swear if those Gods werent all gone I would... Talena put her pale hand over DarkStar's mouth and turned his head to face the beach. His eyes opened wide at the sight. Every deity that DarkStar had so kindly been filling in for partaked in some sort of beach like activity. Talena took her hand away from his mouth and he stood there frozen to his spot in rage. God, Buddha, Jehova, Ganesh, Ozzy Osbourne, Death, Fate, all of them... anything Other Worldly, anything with a realm, sat and played volley ball. DarkStar walked over in a daze, Death greated him with her normally chipper smile. Death: DarkStar! Um... heh... what are you doing here... I mean... I thought your invitation was lost in the mail... God looked over and mouthed 'there goes the party' too Abraham. DarkStar: I had to do all your dirty work, so you guys could go to the beach? A flash as DarkStar's mind went into flashback mode. Fire burned in his eyes. At times I just think that all these other gods took this as a oppurtunity to go to Florida and lounge around on a private beach somewhere, while I am forced to stay here the entire time and keep everything in line for these jackass gods. Bah. Too bad my rational mind (or whats left of it) kicks me in the ass and tells me that when Fate loses something, this happens. Its in the handbook you get when your promoted to god status. God looked at DarkStar, who drew his special sword from his leather trench coat. God: Oh... shit! The view quickly changes to Talena who stands on the boardwalk leaning over the edge, the crowd behind her scatters and flees as the bursts of energy of the fight are accentuated on her face, her eyes show reflections of blood being spilled and aggression being taken out. DarkStar yells gibberish about not runnign away and facing their true maker. Talena goes into a fit of mad laughter. The screams and fire slowly comes to a stop, and the view pans out, showing the entire beach front area. DarkStar stands in the center of carnage, blood, gods limbs, everything lays in the torn remnants of what was once a Party of the Gods. DarkStar's jacket blew in the breeze, he whiped his bloody sword off and slid it back into its sheath. DarkStar: Get back to work you lazy bastards. He kicked the body of Azazel. DarkStar: I quit. Talena smiles at him. She acts normal for once. Her body language shows that she is in her 'normal' stage. She walks down and takes DarkStar's hand. Talena: I didn't know you could quit being a god? DarkStar picked her up and smothered her with a kiss, the sun set in the background as the waves rushed in. DarkStar: My love, you can quit being anything. Fade to black.
