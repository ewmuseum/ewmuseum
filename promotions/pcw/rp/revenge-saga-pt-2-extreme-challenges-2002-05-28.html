Revenge Saga pt 2: Extreme Challenges

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/5/02 Crichton’s dreams had been disturbed since his ordeal at the military base. The combined guilt over the death of his true love Aeryn Sun and all of the soldiers killed by the fire he caused through his fury was disturbing his conscience deeply. The problem was Crichton couldn’t listen to his conscience if he constantly chose to ignore it, and that was exactly what he was doing. He had to, as he saw no other way to accomplish his current mission of revenge. Maybe he should start listening to his conscience and cease his mission of revenge against all those who have wronged him, but the army would still come after him, trying to keep their little experiment under wraps, away from the public. Crichton doubted whether Dan Lyons had stopped looking for him either or Miss Zemoff for that matter. He had however, managed to kill of Fleming at the army base with explosion, as well as kicking Fleming’s ass prior to the explosion. He had reaped revenge upon him, to a point, but Crichton still wished he could have tortured him a little more. If he listened to his conscience would he cease his mission? That was the question that intrigued him then most, but he dared not find an answer. In reality this ‘mission’ he had set out to accomplish was nothing more than a vain attempt to cling on to the hope and the memory of Aeryn, if he ceased with the mission he would be forced to deal with the facts of the matter. The fact that Aeryn is dead and will never be coming back and the fact that as soon as his mission was accomplished or ceased he would be forced to grieve for his loved one. The result was still the same, except trying to accomplish the mission would prolong the inevitable. ************ The motel room Crichton was currently residing in was a real shit hole, but this was the status quo in his life since escaping the army all those months ago. The motel rooms reminded him of all of the good times with Aeryn, he knew that reliving these memories of her was just reopening the wound, so to speak, but he couldn’t begin mourning for his love until his revenge mission was complete, so he saw no real problem with his actions. The only illumination in his room, as he packs a black bag, was the moonlight shining in from the half-opened blinds. It was night time, more accurately the early hours of the morning, the time where John Crichton thrived and began to work. After the death of General Tranter, although the Army hadn’t public announced it, the other victims of his inevitable revenge would surely know of his death and would be very wary themselves. This would obviously make it harder for Crichton to get to them, but his determination allowed him not to think of failure of any kind. He had already decided that his next target was going to be the advanced weapons designer Miss Zemoff. The reason being, he knew where she would be, as opposed to the estranged Dan Lyons. He was purposely leaving Lyons until last because he was the one he could empathise with the most. Lyons had also lost the people he loved, and it was indirectly Crichton’s fault. He was even tempted not to go for him at all, but then decided that this would indeed break the vow he made with his recently dead love. Crichton removed the quilt from the upper half of his torso, then sat at the edge of the bed. Next to the bed is chair, with Crichton’s pants slung over the back. He reaches over and extracts a packet of cigarettes and a lighter from his pants pocket and lights a cig up for himself. He began to think about the current situation he found himself in. Killing Tranter had been easy for Crichton, but he doubted that Miss Zemoff would be as easy. She had her advanced technological weapons up her sleeve, and he very much doubted that even his new powers would stand up to that kind of weaponry. After a minute more contemplating all of his options and all of the possibilities of his options he came up with a final conclusion. Before he went up against any such competition he should test his powers before he even dared to combat Miss Zemoff. Crichton stared out of the motel room window, at his moonlight surroundings, thinking of such things he could do to test himself in combat, to test his limitations and figure out his own strengths and weaknesses since his transformation. The first challenge he set himself was to start a fight in the particularly rough looking biker’s bar, down the road from his very motel. He checked his wristwatch, it was only 2.48 am and he suspected that the biker’s bar was still very much rocking at this time of night. Crichton reaches down to the floor, retrieves his very creased pants and slips them on. He gave up caring about the way he looked at the very same time Aeryn died. He didn’t see the point in looking after himself anymore, after all he no longer had anyone to impress. He then takes his coat from the back of the chair and slips it on. While slipping the coat on, his jet-black pistol falls out of his inside coat pocket, onto the bed. Crichton eyes firmly fix upon the firearm, thinking about the situation he was about to walk into and whether he needed the backup of the firearm. True, the gun would be his insurance, if he couldn’t fight his way out of the bar, but that went against everything he was trying to accomplish from this little challenge. He fastened the last few buttons of his long black leather coat, then headed towards the door. As he turned the doorknob and opens the door slightly, he turns around to look at the gun one last time. No! He didn’t care about the consequences, the challenge was to test his fighting capability and taking a firearm into the hell he was about to create would inevitably ruin the little experiment he had devised. He closes the motel door behind him and doesn’t look back. ************ The extreme challenge, he had created for himself, would accomplish two tasks. It would test his limitations and fighting ability and it would also put him in the right mental state for Friday Fusion’s Extreme Title match. The match itself contained some of PCW’s best and toughest competitors and this kind of brawl in a biker’s bar would definitely train him up for this kind of extreme title match. Crichton rounds the corner at the end of the street and is propositioned with the daunting sight of the biker’s bar. The building itself was grottier than most decayed tombstone’s he had noticed, the peeling yellow paint added to that effect greatly. He was impressed however, with the all the different varieties of motorcycle that were parked in front of the building, he even recognised a few expensive looking Harley Davidson motorcycles. Crichton took one last drag of his cigarette, then throws it onto the floor and crushes it under his foot. What could be possibly do to get the whole building into one massive brawl? Immediately his mind filled with various options in that department, but one stood out to do the task undoubtedly. Crichton takes a deep breath and heads towards the door of the ‘establishment’. As he reaches out towards the door, he notices the dried blood upon the doorknob and decides that this was definitely the right place to come. He opens the door and steps in the bar. All eyes dart toward the door as Crichton makes his appearance. His long leather jacket helped him a little to pass him off as a biker, but not by much, for it appeared that biker’s know they’re own. Now that he had they’re undivided attention, it was the perfect time to implement the catalyst to a brawl. Crichton steps upon the nearest table, knocking some guys pint of beer over, and clears his throat for attention, not that he needed to. “I came in here, looking for a nice place to drink, for a place that I could feel comfortable getting pissed in, and I chose here. Let me tell ya big mistake! If I’d of known this was a gay bar before I came in, I would be at the bar down the road!” Crichton declares. The man behind the bar, the only one incidentally who wasn’t wearing leather at all, points his chubby little finger in the direction of Crichton. “You comin’ in here, callin’ this place a gay bar, are you lookin’ to get your ass whooped?” The bartender questions, already rolling his sleeves up for the inevitable brawl. “Not that I have to answer to you Village People rejects, but yeah!” Crichton declares booting the man in front of him, in the face. Crichton jumps down from the table and prepares himself. The nearest guy to him, clutching a snooker cue, swings for his head, but Crichton’s enhanced reflexes, allow him to respond in time to duck the blow. Crichton follows up with a blow to the guys ribs and then another shot to the face, grabbing the snooker cue off the guy as he heads south. And so the battle continued, blows exchanged and bodies beaten. Crichton sat upon the table he stood upon, not thirty minutes ago and looked upon the carnage before him. In truth he was a little disappointed with the challenge, it had been difficult, but once he started to fight the biker’s took the opportunity to start to fight each other, therefore Crichton hadn’t really been tested at all. He was starting to think that this was a gay bar he stumbled into, it was certainly where throwing punches were concerned. Crichton stepped over the prone bodies lay upon the floor and entered the toilets. Not the first place he wanted to be in this kind of bar, but it was necessary. The battle with the biker’s hadn’t daunted him at all, and they hardly touched his face. Crichton leant on the incredibly dirty sink and examined his jaw and upon extension the rest of his face. No bruising at all. In this day and age you’d expect lot more from fighting a group of biker’s, but hell they were all wimps from his perspective. Without thinking about it, Crichton jammed his fist down upon the sink in anger and the sink simply gave way, and the water pipe broke, flooding the toilets with water. Crichton stepped out of the toilet soaking wet. “It really is going to be one of those days!” he exclaimed, before heading towards the door of the place. Well at least it’d serve ‘em right for all being pussies, he surmised, before exiting the establishment and setting off for his next challenge.
