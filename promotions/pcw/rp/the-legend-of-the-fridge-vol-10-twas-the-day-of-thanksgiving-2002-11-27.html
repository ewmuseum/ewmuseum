The Legend of the Fridge: Vol 10. Twas the Day of Thanksgiving

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.11.02 'Twas the Night before Thanksgiving, and inside the Fridge,Not a Condiment was stirring, not even the Fudge.The lunchmeat hides in the crisper with Pears,In hopes Pete's Family, would never be there. Pete was all nestled, all snug in his bed, While visions of Turkey, dances in his head.And Tso in his kerchief, and Pete is his cap,Just settled down, for a pre-meal nap. When out on the Drive, there arose such a clatter,Pete sprang from his bed, to see what was the matter.Away to the window, he walked like a sloth,Tore open the shutters, threw on some cloth... The moon on the chicken breast of the new-fallen snow,Gave the lustre of mid-day to the objects below.When what to Pete's wondering eye's should appear, But a large Catering Van, and twelve trucks with beer. With an Army of drivers, so lively and quick,Pete knew in a flash, it Must be St. Nick's Catering.More rapid than Ramen Noodles his caterers came,And he moaned and shouted and called out their names! Now, Freddy, now Jack! now Robin, now Bob!On Joey, on Tom, On David, on Rob!To the steps of the porch! To the top of the stairs!Now Cook away, Cook away! Cook away alL! As dry spices that season the wild turkeys fly,When they meet with bird, Pete's mouth does go dry.So up to the stove the caterers they flew,With bags full of goodies, and some beer too. And then, when done tinkling, Pete heard on the lawn,His Father had hit the neighbor's child Fawn.As Pete walked outside, Dad was turning around,The two fat men ran, and fell on the ground. Dad was dressed, all in fur, clothes tight from head to foot,And his clothes were all stained from eating and soot.A twleve pack of beer, he carried in hand,Not knowing if Pete would buy his favorite brand. Dad's Eye's were all blood shot, His cheeks all red!If the cops would have nailed him, he sure would be dead!His drool in his mouth, was all on his chins,And the smell of his breath, made Pete Wince. And then there was Mother, climbing out of the car.She fell asleep, while Dad hit the bar.She had a broad ass, and a big round belly,That shook when she screamed like a bowl full of jelly.'' She was chubby, and plump, like a fine Jewish wife.And the look of her clothes, made you regret your life.A wink of her eye, and a slap to the head,Pete knew he was better off dead! She spoke lots of words, and went to the kitchen,She stared at the caterers, and started her bitchinAnd laying a hand right across a man's nose,It shattered, and splintered, and bled on his toes. They all sprang to their trucks, and drove away,Left Pete screaming about how much to pay.Pete yelled and he bitched, and shot at a truck!'DAMN YOU MOTHERF*CKERS! AND YES, YOU ALL SUCK!' Pete stood in the street, angry and out of breath.Cursing the drivers a long painful death.Mom went to the kitchen with glee, And Dad sat in the chair, waiting for Pete. Tso emerged from his quick little nap,While Mom grabbed him, and gave him a tap.Tso smiled and laughed as Mom gave a hug,He giggled and snickered when she gave him some love. Pete walked into the house quite peeved,But grabbed some beer and felt quite relieved.He sat on the couch and cracked it open,While Dad changed the channel, Pete sat mopin. Mom cooked in the kitchen and chuckled with Tso,Pete sat angered, and watched dad's show.It was football with the 'Boys and the 'Skins.Dad drank some beers, Pete enjoyed his kin. A few hours later, Mom made the call.'Come to the table, boys! Come eat it all!'Dad and Pete sat up with a startPushing and pulling, ripping each other apart. 'Now stop that! You'll hurt him!' Mom expertly said.She remembers the dinner that left her nearly dead.The boys hugged and they smiled, and each lead the way,Because Turkey, not fighting, was the theme of the day. They sat at the table, and said words of grace,Before it was over, Dad stuffed his face.Tso soon after stuffed his small mouth,And Pete needed a funnel, for his food went south. It fell and it tumbled down all his six chins,It landed in his crotch, and fell past his shins.Pete looked down and smiled at his feet,He shruged and laid down and started to eat. Mom started to yell, and then she remembered,Pete was special, He was born in Febtember.Yes, the author knows that that's not a month,He doesn't really care because you're a...bonth. So as the Pete family eats with their glee,Please remember this lesson, it teaches you and me:Never eat 2 pizzas, a Pepsi and marshmellows,Or you'll wake up like Pete, eating your Pillows.
