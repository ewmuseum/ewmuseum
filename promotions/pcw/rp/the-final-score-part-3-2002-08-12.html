The final score (Part 3)

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12, 2002 [Fast forward a day. Now morning, the strip is still busy…Hell, is there a time when it isn’t? We are, however, nearby a store. The store is on the very outskirts of the Vegas Strip. In the barred and reinforced glass case, an Army Surplus MP5, wood finished. That’s something rare to say the least. We go into the store, and we see locked steel racks, that are filled to capacity with various handguns. Desert Eagles, Berettas, Magnums just to name a few. The sign just above the cash register says… Welcome to AmmuNation The man behind the register is a rather large guy who’s wearing hunter camouflage. He looks like one of those who shoot coyotes in the Mojave, or Deer in Northern California. He’s standing at the register, filling out some paperwork. He’s behind a steel mesh, with only a hole underneath for the exchange of licenses, money, and receipts. There is a chime, and the door swings open. The exterior temperature hits the contents of the store like a brick wall. In walks our villain, Eddie Mandrake, who looks around the store to see if anything catches his eye. He is wearing the same clothing he wore yesterday when he checked into the Hotel. He heads to the counter, and the attendant cracks a standard “I’m going to give this guy great customer service” smile.] Attendant: Hi! Welcome to AmmuNation. How can I help you? [Eddie reaches into his wallet and pulls out his American Gun License.] Eddie: Here’s my license. [Eddie passes the license over to the attendant.] Attendant: Alright, let’s have a…Oh my. Eddie: What? Attendant: Well, according to this, you have a Class 4 license. Eddie: The problem is? Attendant: A civilian shouldn’t have a Class 4. That is one step short from using Military Surplus vehicles, such as tanks. Eddie: Damn…I don’t remember how much I paid for that. Attendant: All I can say that you must have paid a lot. Also, you must have paid off a military official in order for you to hold such a license. Eddie: Maybe I did. I can’t remember that far back. Attendant: Well, sir. We have several different options for you when it comes to the Class 4 consumer. [The attendant steps out of his cage and leads Eddie into a back room. It is poorly lit, but most of the cases are lit up. They have several different kinds of high-powered rifles. Eddie’s eyes glow with anticipation, and he begins to drool, slightly. Eddie regains his composure, and straightens himself out.] Attendant: Welcome to the high power section. This area is our Criminal Area. Here is where the criminals go to get their weapons. Eddie: Yeah? I always wondered why I’ve only used melee weapons when the coolest [BEEP] is here! Attendant: You have the hands-on approach? What the hell kind of criminal are you? Eddie: Not a petty one, I’ll tell you that. Attendant: Could’ve fooled me. Anyway, take your pick at our selection. [Eddie begins to take a look at certain cases. The first one he approaches has quite the large rifle in there.] Attendant: Ah, there is Sig SG-550 Sniper Rifle. This gun is great for taking out targets from a very long distance. The only problem is the price tag on it. It’s $4,299.99 [Eddie whistles in shock over the price tag on the gun. He moves on to the next case. He whistles in amazement again.] Attendant: This is quite experimental. The RC-P90. 80 round magazine. Super-high velocity rounds, however, its range is rather limited. Eddie: Can I give it a test drive? Attendant: Sure. [The attendant unlocks the cage, and allows Eddie to reach into it and take it out. It is rather heavy for an assault rifle. Eddie and the attendant head out back. Out back, is a section of the property, with a bulletproof steel door, and a twenty foot high concrete wall with a steel ceiling, which is also bulletproof. Eddie stands in the center of the room, and takes aim at a dummy…which has been cut to ribbons by other people who have tested their weapons. Eddie pulls the trigger. Immediately, ten rounds come out of the barrel, after only half a second of pressure on the trigger. The bullets go through the dummy; finally putting it out of its misery and cutting it clean in half. Eddie’s eyes widen at the sheer power of the weapon.] Eddie: Holy Hell! Attendant: That’s right, that weapon can take out a field of Police officers. What do you plan on doing? Eddie: Presidential Assassination. Attendant: Planning to kill him during that address aren’t you? Eddie: What? How do you know about that? Attendant: News goes around. I would recommend something light and nimble. Something like a Steyr Scout or even a MP5. If I were also you, I would pack my bags and leave, before you get caught in something that you don’t want to be in. Eddie: What do you mean? Attendant: Well…The security within a two mile perimeter is going to be extremely tight. The whole Two Mile Boundary thing. Eddie: I thought that was only for crime scenes. Attendant: Trust me; you’re looking at LVPD, FBI, and the Secret Service on constant patrol. If you get caught, you’re looking at Death. Eddie: I’ve stared in the face of death many times, and I end up stabbing death in the eye with a ballpoint pen. Attendant: Besides, trying to carry that, let alone running away from it. You go through with this. I will guarantee you; you will not make it out of the country alive! [Eddie then begins to ponder if the money is really worth it, $10,000,000 for committing perhaps the most heinous act since JFK’s assassination. And his shot isn’t even that good. Eddie then realizes that this can make or break his reputation as the most infamous bounty hunter on the planet! Oh, the decisions! Eddie however, using some sort of reasoning…most likely insane reasoning, decides on what to do.] Eddie: On second thought, get me an MP5. Attendant: You weren’t listening were you? Eddie: I was, but I have an idea on how to get my money, and screw my employer. Attendant: Alrighty. Can I interest you in our “evidence disposal program?” Eddie: I always take that when I shop here. It makes me a little less convictable. Attendant: A-ha. Alright, your total is $2,510.30 [Eddie presents his cash and his gun gets unassembled and placed into a steel briefcase. The briefcase is passed to Eddie, and he leaves the store. On his way to his car, he notices the two cronies, Carlo and Jimmy, waiting in a car that was parallel parked across the street from AmmuNation. Eddie delivers a nasty look at the car, then enters his own vehicle and heads back to Caesar’s Palace to enjoy his new toy.]
