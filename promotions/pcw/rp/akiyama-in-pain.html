'Dear Mr. Akiyama, According to the contract signed by you and your press agent, Ms. Seraphina Jameson, you are still signed to appear on a variety of shows upon our public access channel, Public Access Information Network. We here in PAIN hereby request that you come into the studio and complete your signed show rosters...lest we declare legal action. Thank you, and we'll be looking forward to hearing from you. Sincerely…Your friends in PAIN...' Akiyama crumbled the letter up in his grasp. 'Son of a b!tch.' Akiyama stared distastefully at the set around him. 'Why must it look like a clown threw up in here? You can't honestly expect US to go through with this.' The director smiled and handed Akiyama a small book. 'Well, sir, you DID sign a contract, which means that you agreed to be the host of 'Story time with Akiyama'.' Akiyama glared daggers at the director and took a seat on the only chair in the room-which is really sized for a 5 year old. In the meantime, 10 hyperactive boys and girls, all around 4-7 years in age plopped down in front of the black and silver haired Neo Void God and gazed up at him expectantly. Akiyama raised a brow at them and sighed heavily. Suddenly a sickeningly happy woman's voice starts singing, backed by chimes and a folk guitar. 'It's stoooory tiiiime! Yes its stoooory tiiime, with...Akiiiiiyaaaamaaa!!' 'By the Void, what have we gotten ourselves into?' Akiyama rolled his eyes and stared at the children. A plump, red-faced boy raised a sticky hand. 'What?' The boy pulled his sucker from his mouth. 'Can we call you Uncle Aki?' All the children began bouncing up and down on their bums excitedly. 'No you may not.' 'Why not?' 'Because you are mere mortals, while we are beyond mere humanity. We are Celestial, as well as the Neo Void God, so we outrank you on the existence scale by outstanding numbers. You are not worthy to lick the chocobo crap from our boot. Now shut up and let us get over with this.' Akiyama opened the book. 'This week's story is called Rumplestiltskin.' 'Ooh!' A blonde, pig-tailed girl jumped up in the back. 'I've heard this one before!' Akiyama glared at her until she sat back down. 'Once upon a time there lived a poor farmer and his daughter. The farmer's name was Kevin, and his daughter Juanita. They both got in the way of the all-powerful god, and he was left with no alternative but to banish them both to the Void. The end.' 'Um...' The same blonde spoke up. 'That's not how it goes.' 'YOU DARE QUESTION US?!' Akiyama balled up his fists and knocked his small chair over. 'Well you're wrong!' The blonde stuck her tongue out. 'INSOLENT-OW!' Akiyama looked down to find a small boy kicking him in the shins repeatedly. 'YOU'LL ROT AND PERISH WHEN WE BANISH YOU TO THE VOI--' 'AHEM!' Akiyama looked at the director who was tapping the contract. 'It specifically says in here no banishment of the children or any guests on the show.' Akiyama grit his teeth together, righted his chair and opened the book again. 'Fine. Once upon a time there lived a poor farmer and his beautiful daughter. The farmer was so proud of his daughter that he often would brag to his friends of her skills. One day, a young man overheard the farmer boasting that his daughter could spin straw into gold.' 'No one can spin straw into gold.' 'That's not the point.' Akiyama smoothed some of his bangs from his eyes and continued. ''Can she REALLY spin straw into gold?' asked the young man. 'Oh yes!' replied the farmer, 'she-'' 'That farmer is a liar.' 'Again, that's not the point. 'Well if your daughter can truly spin straw into gold, then-'' 'That guy's gonna get mad when he finds out she can't really do it. I bet he's gonna do somethin mean, like take away all their pokemon cards or somethin'.' Akiyama stared down at the child, glanced at the director, and promptly bopped the nuisance on the head with the FIST OF JUSTICE (The minimized version of the HAND OF FATE, pending lawsuits). The child slumped over onto the ground and the great NVG smiled. 'To make up for lost time due to our little distraction, the young man turned out to be the prince, and the farmer's daughter was taken before the king, along with her father. ''If your daughter can spin straw into gold on three consecutive nights, then she shall have my son's hand in marriage. If she can NOT complete the task, then I shall have you both beheaded for telling such lies.' 'That very night, the daughter was locked in the tower with merely a spinning wheel and a pile of straw. Overcome with grief, she began to cry. Much to her surprise, a small little man appeared before her. ''Why, who are you?' she asked.' A redheaded girl pointed and laughed. 'You can't do girl voices, your voice cracks when you try.' 'You,' he replied, 'We shall deal with later. The small man bowed, 'I am merely one who hates to see a beautiful woman weep.' Hmm...he should just run her through and be over with it. Is it commercial time yet?' The director rubbed his temples. 'This is public access television, Mr. Akiyama. There are NO commercials.' The boy began to cry and was quickly escorted offstage by his mother. 'Again, to make up for lost time, the girl gave the man her dead mother's pearl necklace, and in return, he spun the straw into gold. He returned the following night, and after she gave him her silver ring, he again spun the straw into gold. Everyone following us? 'The prince and the farmer's daughter soon became pregnant, and she gave birth to a son-' 'Wait, what's pregnant? Is that when the stork delivers the baby?' 'No, it's when people have unprotected sex and conceive a child.' 'What's sex?' 'Ask your parents. From the looks of you, it's the thing they're never having again.' Akiyama flipped through the rest of the book and sighed. 'Thank Cyclonica we're almost done. ''Fine then,' the little man smiled, 'I shall let you keep your son if you can guess my name within the span of three nights, starting tonight!' 'The farmer's daughter ran off every name she knew, but the little man simply laughed and disappeared. The farmer's daughter, sick with grief, sent her handmaid out to the forest to fetch her some soothing herbs, and told her husband of their troubles. The prince immediately sent out his knights to record every name in his land. 'While out in the evening, the handmaid spotted a strange little man dancing about a small campfire, singing 'Rumplestiltskin be my name' over and over, and laughing hysterically.' 'Sing the Rumplestiltskin song, Uncle Aki!' 'NO!' A whack, and another child was laid to sleep by the FIST OF JUSTICE (all rights reserved). 'Now, the third night was upon the farmer's daughter, and her handmaiden told her of the strange man she'd seen dancing the night before-' 'I think you're rushing this story.' A brunette boy adjusted his glasses and stuck his finger up his nose. 'Hmmm...One can only wonder why we would be...' Akiyama shuddered and continued. 'That night, the farmer's daughter guessed the name 'Rumplestiltskin', and the little man began to shake and steam until he became nothing but dust and blew away, leaving the royal family in peace. THE END!' He slapped the book closed and glared at the camera. 'Thank you for watching, when it's time to support your local public access channel, do NOT donate during our time slot.' With that he stormed offstage, throwing the book at the director and shoving a doughnut in his mouth. 'Never again will we sign our name to ANYTHING just to stay one step ahead of a flat-scan like Miyagawa...' Yet later... Akiyama gazed dispassionately at the set before him. The director jovially clapped the Neo Void God on the shoulder, grinning widely. 'Well, what do you think of the set, Mr. Akiyama?' '......' 'I take that as a good silence?' 'Do not touch your god of television.' Akiyama slowly blinked and stared at the man from the corner of his eyes. '.....this set's color theme consists of pastels. Glancing at the cue cards in front of him, he prepared himself for the inevitable. Behind her, Rampage came out in a red tank top and black jeans and boots, and Kyoshiro in a blue turtleneck and khakis. Cell smirked and twirled her hair while Rampage gave a simple nod of his head in greeting, and Kyo with his patented raspy chuckle. 'So what's the topic?' Akiyama's eyes returned to the cue cards.
