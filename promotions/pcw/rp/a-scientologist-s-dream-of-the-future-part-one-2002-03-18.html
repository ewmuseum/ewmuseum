A Scientologist's Dream Of The Future (Part One)

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/03/02 Society is going down hill. There is a moral and social decline happening in every nation of this Green earth. Developed countries are suffering rises in crime and lawlessness. Anti-Capitalists descend on every meeting of the World’s leading Nations. The 3rd World is struggling under the burden of debt, still faltering in overcoming years of famine and war. Decent people live in fear of fanatics while the Religious minorities scream oppression. The World does not need to be like this.... Things can get better.... One man knows how..... We open up in the bedroom of Xavier Kannon’s residence just outside of Miami, Florida. The White room and thick carpet are lit with a Blue tint from the moon that shines through the White net curtains of the open bay windows. “Mmmmm.....Get off......Damn security......Hmmmm.....I’ll get Howard.....Duke....Don’t oppress me....” We arc around the Scandinavian style pine bed to show wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon tossing and turning under the Black silk sheets, his Auburn hair falling over his face and the sheets being ruffles back to shoe his toned physique. Xavier : “Damn country.......No vision........Oppress us......We future.....They will see....” He talks in his sleep, oblivious to all that is going outside his bed as he tries to find a comfortable place both in his bed and in his mind. “Would you, like, NOT do that?” As the camera looks up we see Xavier’s valet Eleanor Hall sitting up in bed wearing a White spaghetti strap vest and Blue and White striped pyjama bottoms. She hugs one of the think pillows to her as she pulls her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights out of her hair allowing the moonlight to reflect off the pale Blue eyes. Xavier : “Mwa?.........Huh?” The Gold Patron Meritorious is plucked out of his un-easy slumber by his valets sharp tongued voice. He springs up into a sitting position, quickly looking to each side worriedly, a throw back to older times. As his Green eyes hit Ellie~!’s she stares back to him with raised eyebrows and a tone of annoyance infects her voice. Eleanor : “Um....Hello? I, being the courteous person that I am talk during the day because at night....I actually sleep!” She turns around and grunts softly and she turns to face away from Xavier, pulling the Black silk sheets over to her side and cuddling the pillow under her head as she exhales satisfyingly and drifts back to sleep. Kannon rubs his eyes and drops back onto the bed, attempting to pull some of the covers back over himself only to falter due to the vice-like grip that Ellie~! can obtain when she wants to hold onto something. He sighs and rolls to his left, spooning Eleanor as he tries to get back to sleep, whispering quietly to himself. Xavier : “If only you knew the troubles and dreams that run through the mind of a Scientologist my little Poon Puppy.” He closes his eyes and rubs his head into the pillow, trying to find a comfortable spot in which he can drift off in. “Really.........Don’t........Care!” Ellie~! replies in an aggravated whisper to Xavier’s words causing Kannon to chuckle softly to himself as he shakes his head and falls off to sleep. What things run through the mind of a Scientologist? Do Scientologists dream of Scientological money making Sheep? Or do they simply hope for the theoretical Utopia? Hoping that through the teachings of Hubbard society can find harmony both within itself and with other nations.....Only one thing can tell us, and we are about to go there. Xavier : “Mmmm....Perfect.....Helpfulness......Prosperity.....” We see the peaceful face of Xavier as he quietly mumbles to himself, any veil he shows in public cast off by the innocence of his near slumber. Xavier : “......It’s.......It’s perfect....” We then cut to a sun drenched street just past midday, the bright white sun burning through the Blue sky without a cloud in sight to tarnish the flawless sky. The street is free of litter with unblemished Grey slabs spanning along the eyeline with a litter bin, none full, every 5 metres or so. Xavier : “We.......We......Can make it happen....” On the roads the vehicles do not seem to be expelling any fumes and an advertising hoarding in between the towering Glass buildings reads “The New Toyota Hydra....The most efficient Water powered Car.” On the street we see an elderly lady struggling with her bags as she makes her way along the sidewalk, not having to worry about tripping on a broken paving slap. As she loses her grip on one of her backs her purse drops out and falls a few feet away from her. Elderly Woman : “Oh dear.....” As she bends her knees slowly to retrieve her bag a group of 3 teenagers walks out of the back alley behind her, walking towards her and watching the woman try to pick up her belongings. Youth #1 : “Shall we?” Youth #2 : “Yeah.” They approach here, 2 looking around while the other has his eyes focused on the purse. He reaches out to the old lady’s back. Youth #2 : “Here, let me help you with that....” He puts his hand on her shoulder for reassurance as he squats down and picks up her purse, dropping it into her bag for her. She looks up to him with a look of gratitude crossing her face. Elderly Lady : “Why thank you.....I’m not as young as I used to be back when I was 118!” She takes a note out of her purse and tries to hand it to the adolescent who holds his palm up, refusing to take her money. Youth #2 : “No, you’re welcome.....Imagine what we all would be like if Scientology hadn’t of sold off all the worlds arms for scrap metal and managed to convert all those nuclear weapons into a cheap, clean and renewable power source to fund a world wide free health organisation we would all be worse off.” As she puts the note back in her purse the youth walks off with his 2 friends who all pull a piece of fruit stamped with “Low in natural sugars.“ as they head into a door with “Give money to Charity here...“ above the door. Elderly Lady : “That is true my laddy, that is true....” She whispers to herself as she walks down the street, past a set of Marble steps that lead up to a glistening spire that seems to pierce the sky itself, the sun shining off it’s metallic tinted glass exterior making it appear a giant White spike though a mixture of reflections and perspective. “Look at the World you have helped create Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon.....A society built around our ideals.....And how it works.....” We cut to a room at the very top of the Spire where the familiar figure of Xavier Kannon is seen over looking the world at work below, grinning contentedly as humanity shaped in his image is working harmoniously compared to how it was at the dawn of the 21st century. Xavier : “When do you think this all started? Surely sometime in the past there must have been a watershed, a moment in time where the downward spiral was rectified and a new dawn was set in motion?” Kannon stands a foot away from the floor to ceiling windows, staring down past his seat at the cars following paths through the perfectly square city blocks and the minute citizens of the utopian society are seen going about their daily business doing no harm to themselves or their peers. He is dressed in a smart Charcoal Grey suit, the perfect cut for his slender body shape. Under it he has a roll-neck sweater in exactly the same colour and a pair of shiny Black shoes. She he turns away from the scene through the window and back into the room his face looks aged, looking in his early forties with he brow beginning to wrinkle and streaks of grey appearing on the sides of his long auburn hair which is neatly tied back, but one thing that has not changed is his eyes which still emanate from his face in their piercing Green way. “If I had to pick a date I would say the 22nd of Ellie~!, or March as it was called then, in the year of our Hubbard 2002....” The voice Xavier is turning to look towards is none other than his close friend and now Personal Advisor John Travolta, former Hollywood actor turned co-founder of Hubbardopolis, the greatest city on the face of the planet. Xavier : “Hmmm, I believe that was in my PCW era.....What happened n that day that was so important?” He claps his hands and a set of blinds roll down over the window which have commercials projected onto the them, shilling the latest products and showing that in this time and place, Snapple~! is not just the official soft drink of PCW but the official drink of the human race. John : “No.....I’ll give you a clue though....HI KEEBA!” Kannon hops onto the long oval Walnut table that occupies the centre of the ring and starts to think John’s question and clue over as Travolta sits at the end of the table, smug in the knowledge he knows the answer that Xavier is straining every synapse to recall. Xavier : “Hmmmm.....I am picking up a Greek reference there, was it the time we went to the Toga party at the White House and Ellie~! forgot to tie it in a couple of places and the pictures got all over the Internet?” He answers with a sense of doubt in his voice, trying to remember back to times of old where society itself was on the brink of collapse and his relaying of L Ron Hubbard’s teachings were the only things that could save it. John : “I’m afraid not G-P-M......Give up?” Xavier : “What happened at the start of the 21st century....It was only 22 years ago but it all seems to distant......I have an idea, I hope you don’t think this is cheating?....” The Scientologist and co-founder of the utopian society presses a button on a Silver panel attached to the table and a computerised voice is head, it’s virtual reality, picture perfect human form is then seen projected onto the blinds. The words MITCHELL X6-780 is written in the bottom left corner, the name of the unit. Mitchell : “Please state your enquiry.” Kannon looks to John who just shrugs not having any objection to Xavier running an archives search to find the answer for his question. Kannon then turns back to look at the image and asks it his question. Xavier : “Give me the major news worthy occurrences in the year of our Hubbard 2002.....List in ascending order from least to most impact on today’s society. Mitchell : “Searching....” The eyes on the computerised figure spin around to show the viewer that the time is being found and the news records searched. John : “You know, I thought you would get this...You did feature prominently in it!” The eyes on the computer figure stop spinning and it relays the information back just like a real person. Mitchell : “December 31st at 23-59 and 59.99 seconds.....Xenu returns to this earth as written in the works of L Ron Hubbard that founded the very faith of Scientology itself but due to the prejudice that Scientologists faced at that time by both the Government and the other major Religions, the 1st ever military alliance between the Arab States, Israel and the NATO powers killed him with 704 tactical nuclear weapon’s hits.” Kannon and John turn to each other, mourning the sad event in the history of Scientology that happened at the hands of the narrow minded religious scholars of the time. Xavier : “The less said about that the better......Next Mitchell....” Mitchell : “June 17th at 22-13 and 37.25 seconds Miss Eleanor Hall becomes the Governor of the 51st state in what was then the United States of America....Prepland, a land created due to the great prep backlash caused by aversions to the TV Show Popular.” Xavier : “This just reminds me of what kind of Society we had to endure back in the ‘Dark Ages’ of the dawn of the new millennium.....What is the last news entry Mitchell.” The image flickers out of pause. Mitchell : “Ellie~! or March as it was called then 22nd at 21-39 and 4.89 seconds Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon defeated Big Alan Steele at PCW’s Sunday Night Sabotage.” Now having fulfilled it’s required function it shuts off for a few seconds where an orbiting globe comprised of the word Hubbardopolis is shown before the commercials start up once more. Xavier : “Wow.......I would never have thought that one of my matches would have prompted the World to recover from it’s decade long spiral into immoral values and save itself through finally deciding to embrace Dianetics.” Travolta doesn’t seem surprised by the fact at all, his suspicion as to what it was being confirmed. John : “Think about it Xavier, if you didn’t beat that mighty Greek warrior, then you would have never got the shot to win the Continental Title at the right time and place in order to catapult you into the media spotlight allowing Scientology to be brought to the forefront thus waking the World from it’s post-Millennial apathy and turning the tide along the salvation of the whole planet.” On hearing this Xavier raises his eyebrows and shrugs, grinning smugly. Xavier : “Wow, I think you are right, I don’t know why I didn’t guess it earlier....If anyone where to be responsible for the salvation of 7 billion people it must have been none other than I, Gold Patron Meritorious Kan-” He is interrupted as a beeping sound comes from the blinds which are now flashing with Incoming Message. Beep Beep Beep Beep Beep Xavier : “Display Sender....” The blind then flashes up 7 words which seem perfectly natural to the pair of Scientologists who are now both staring at the projection of the sender’s identity.... PRESIDENT OF THE FREE WORLD ELEANOR HALL..... To be continued....As long as Xavi doesn’t wake up....Or Ellie~! elbows him for sleep talking....
