Sorry for the mishap.&lt;/i&gt; &amp;#65279;*********The Tank __36—********* &lt;b&gt;Mourning After Night&lt;/b&gt; &lt;i&gt;Worm pierces woodBlame is spread so wide it’s becoming thinTonight you dieTomorrow you sleep&lt;/i&gt; ~Leigh Donovan &lt;b&gt;Monday, October 28 - Day 117&lt;/b&gt; “Now remember,” Tim says as he escorts Shane Hood into the crowd in the telephone room, “ifwe hear you saying anything - fucking &lt;i&gt;anything&lt;/i&gt; - out of line, Snow here’s going to rip outa couple of your vital organs and choke you with them. Shane gulps hard, turning to his left where Jason Snow is lurking through the crowd like amonster. Hood knows McDonald isn’t bluffing. If he doesn’t make the all-important phone call,McDonald will have Snow kill him. In truth, Shane Hood knows nothing of the situation. All heknows is that he has a number chiselled into his brain and there are people waiting at the otherend of the line for some information. Whether or not the information Tim McDonald has toldhim to convey is true or not, he doesn’t know. All he knows is that he has no choice. Before long, Shane Hood finds himself at the front of the line, stepping up to the telephone. Hetakes a nervous look over his shoulder at Tim McDonald and then to Jason Snow - with hisbeard and golden blond hair, he really does resemble a lion, which is probably the reason for thenickname. “Hello?” Shane says as someone on the other end picks up. “I’m calling for Vincent Giovanniagain. I got cut off last time. You remember me?” “I remember,” the voice on the other end tells the young man. “What do you have for me.” “Giovanni’s not allowed to use the phones, so he has me making the call for him,” Shaneexplains, much like he did last time. “He’s a smart man,” the voice on the other end says. “Get to the point.” “I already gave you the name Harvey Blake,” says Hood, hoping his voice doesn’t crack underthe immense weight of his nerves. “He also wanted me to give you the names Jake Hooper andGeoffery Rankin.” Hearing the name Geoffery Rankin, Snow’s eyes dart to Tim McDonald andhis blood rushes to his face - why would Tim McDonald tell this young man to hand over Unit BManager Geoffery Rankin’s, life!? &lt;b&gt;Monday, October 28 - Day 117&lt;/b&gt; “You fucking monster!” Snow screams, now that he has Tim McDonald alone, just outside thetelephone room. He grabs McDonald by the collar and slams him hard against the concrete wall,not caring if anyone is to hear him and apprehend him. “What the fuck did you give those sickbastard’s Rankin’s name!? What the fuck has he done to &lt;i&gt;you&lt;/i&gt;!? I agreed to give him theother Nazi motherfucker because he doesn’t have anyone they can kill on the outside, but youdidn’t say a fucking thing about handing them Geoffery Rankin!” “Get off me!” McDonald exclaims, trying to push the overpowering young lion away -obviously, he’s unsuccessful. “We’ve got to make this shit believable, Snow! We can’t justhand them a bunch of fucking Nazi blow hards! Rankin’s got the power, Snow - they knowdamn well that left to their own devices, those motherfucking Hitler bastards would never beable to get past me &lt;i&gt;or&lt;/i&gt; you to get to Hattoni. With Rankin on the side of the Nazi’s, theHattoni family mob can form an idea of how the murder took place - and we’re both in the clear.&lt;i&gt;I&lt;/i&gt; might even get rewarded out of it.” Unable to contain his anger, Snow backhands Tim McDonald hard in the face, causing blood tosplash against the wall behind them. “You sick fuck!” Snow screams. “You’re no better thanthe Nazis! Geoffery Rankin’s a fucking innocent man, for fuck’s sakes! He might have fuckingkids! And in a couple of hours, some machine-gun toting goombas are going to bust through hisfront door and wipe them all out!” “Don’t give me this self-righteous bullshit, Snow!” fires McDonald as he wipes the blood fromhis lips. “It’s true. By this time tomorrow, Geoffery Rankin will be dead - you’re right. Hisfamily and his friends will be dead too. But you know what? Fuck that. Better him than myfuture in the Hattoni family. And better him that &lt;i&gt;your&lt;/i&gt; family, Snow! I’m only lookingout for &lt;i&gt;you&lt;/i&gt;, and this is what has to be done.” His words are harsher than any physical violence Snow can deal out. “And deep down,” McDonald says, “you know that.” &lt;b&gt;Tuesday, October 29 - Day 117&lt;/b&gt; Something’s not quite right... As the morning buzzer sounds, each of Unit B’s prisoner’s stalk out of their bunks, throw onsome clothing, and file out of their cells for roll call. Standing there patiently while each andevery prisoner is checked over - nothing is out of the ordinary. Each prisoner is counted andchecked off Benjamin Graham’s list. In terms of the prisoners, everything is perfectly normal... The door is slightly ajar in Geoffery Rankin’s office as two FBI agents approach, ready to gothrough the man’s personal belongings in search of clues. They’ll find nothing, and deep down,they know that. They turn on the light inside his office but the blinds in the windows remainclosed.
