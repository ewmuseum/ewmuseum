Giving Up?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec.3rd/02 Alliances was to be the greatest night of my life. I had a chance to make a name for myself, and blew it. Now I have to drink Gatorade and pretend to like it cuz that ass clown, Skitzo, won the Alamo Match. Then my team was eliminated from the main event, which forever ruined my chances of becoming one of the fastest rising rookies in PCW history. Oh well, what can ya do? ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ This past week, I was given another opportunity for glory, but I didn’t blow that time. I WAS SCREWED . . . and no one knows how or why. But will all know soon enough ... ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The room would be completely pitch black if weren’t for the pale, orange beams emanating from the street lamps pouring through an open window. Outside the rain is streaming down like God’s tub had overflowed, and every so often a tiny bead of rainwater found its way through the open window and onto my dirty, apartment carpet. You would never know that I was in the room if it weren’t for the constant rattle of beer cans. Man, I am so wasted, I can barely stand up. I guess the pressure had finally gotten to me . . . I guess the disappointment that had been growing inside me finally decided to show its ugly face, and it couldn’t come at a worse time. With a shot at the ‘Number One Contendership for the Extreme Title’ on the line at Turmoil and yet another tag match with that nimrod Mark Amulet, my career was most definitely on the line as well. I knew if I failed to get the job done on either one of the show, I was out . . . But deep inside me, the doubt and unwillingness to continue grew stronger . . . I had a hard time deciding what my next move would be . . . only because I wasn’t sure if it would involve wrestling or not. I knew I had not lived up to hype of Mark Michigan being part of PCW, and now that lack of enthusiasm was coming back to haunt me. Images of my debut replayed over and over again in my mind . . . and my attacks on Rich Rollins and Amulet also came into view . . . Those were my only shining moments under the PCW spotlight. Other than those few minutes of fame . . . I had been a complete failure . . . Now I’ve been stuck the lowest ranks as one could possibly imagine. The Front Office has me combating those of a lower class . . . a lower level of mankind . . . that’s right, I’m talking about those who fight for the Extreme Title. Could one be any more humiliated? Just look at the men and women surrounding me . . . all hoping that one day they will hold those precious pounds of gold plated glory. I am not an extreme wrestler . . . I am not a dog . . . I am the most intelligent man in professional wrestling, and I will not stand for such a travesty . . . But is the Front Office right? Am I only Extreme Title material? Am I not good enough for the Television Title . . . or even the World Title??? That is my real dilemma . . . am I just wasting my time? Am I going out every show and bustin’ my ass for nothing? YES . . . as a matter of fact I am! I’ve always been known to choke when the “big one” comes along. I’ve always been known as a laid back, nonchalant kind of a guy . . . not a hot headed, chair wielding psycho that the fans see in the ring each and every week. I don’t know if I can continue to be a man that I truly am not . . . NO! I’m not wasting my time! I am going out there every week and doing the best I can. I bring my ‘A Game’ to every match, even it’s not enough! I’m Mark Michigan dammit . . . I’d never quit? NEVER EVER! What the hell is going on inside of me? You know the old saying . . . “The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak” . . . well, I’m physically ready to go whoop ass each and every night, but lately, my mind and soul have just not been up to the task. So I am here now . . . sitting in this dim apartment . . . asking myself the same question . . . over and over and over again . . . DO I GO ON? The answer? ......................................... I don’t know ..............................
