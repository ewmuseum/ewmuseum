Nerezzurrection

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th April 2003 “Where’s that damn boat?” I thought as my piercing green eyes roamed the dockyard, left and right they swayed in the hope of catching a glimpse of the vessel that will free my partner from his metallic casket. The events of Sunday Night continued to haunt my mind, first and foremost we accomplished the benchmark of five Tag Team Championship reigns and even though I should be overwhelmed with positives I can’t help but feel the night was tainted. I never actually witnessed the demise of my partner first hand but members of the roster were only too happy to make the event common knowledge, their sympathy a camouflage for their true feelings. As the crime transpired over and over again the sound of an engine could faintly be heard, growing in decibels as the craft crept closer the Jester’s eyes widened as he peered into the darkness. Neighbouring spotlights flickered creating an eerie atmosphere and the above stars twinkled but visibility remained minimal as a white mist swept onto the wooden boards of the docking bay. The bustling bodies that would scamper about during daylight had long since gone home for the nature of this voyage was deemed classified. “A need to know basis, you know ‘cause you need to know…” were the actual words uttered during the transaction but the sea dog I had dabbled with seems reluctant to live up to his side of the bargain. I paced back and forth frantically, my impatience apparent as I tread over the wooden flooring that creaked under the pressure of my weight for I am not a large man but even my body mass tested its durability. The sound of the engine faded and so had my hope when suddenly a blinding white light shone down from the heavens with warmth and invite, for low and behold the fishing trawler had arrived. “What the fu…” was my initial retort to being blinded by the unknown but then came the rugged voice I had aligned alongside. The darkness had been vanquished and my emerald business suit glistened from the light source when from the ghost ship a voice called out. “Is that the land lover?” Even before I could yell back a platform had been lowered and I climbed aboard, like a tightrope walker I ventured over the few feet of water between the land and the vessel gulping hard as I gazed down at the crashing water that bombarded the side of the ship. One crash after another for the calm waters that once were had now decided to rage against us, almost dowsing the whole operation. I ventured onto the deck, almost tripping up over the loose rope that littered the floor and other assortment of death traps on show, there were nets and lobster boxes cluttering all available space. “All we need now is Captain Birds-Eye!” I giggled, remembering some white bearded Captain advertising frozen bread coated fish on British television when sadly the Santa Claus clone was really pushing illegally caught dolphin, even at that young age I was onto the scam. That sole image put a smile back on my angelic face when from no-where a creepy young man materialised, startling me in the process I almost died from fright but keeping a firm upper-lip I recomposed. The gentleman’s clothes were tatty and worn, his face dirt ridden and his hair netted, in truth he’d be deemed my polar opposite for I emitted suave and sophistication whilst he was nothing more than a commoner working his fingers to the bone. “The Captain would like to see you, mate!” Whoa, this infidel had the nerve to call me mate but I bit my lip almost drawing blood and ventured to the control room where the good Captain instantly greeted me. We exchanged a friendly handshake and I flinched as nightmarish thoughts invaded my mind, had this man even washed his fish laden hands? I quickly retracted my hand and tried desperately to reframe from looking down upon it in a smug manner, thank god the hired help had decided to interrupt as he stood slouching in the doorway. “We’re all set Captain!” A nod, wink combination and the Captain had put the pedal to the medal so to speak and as you can imagine the ship pushed off from its stationary position. This craft wasn’t huge so ship maybe a little politically incorrect but neither would I deem it a boat, a question that has no baring whatsoever but a niggling something playing on my active mind. Getting back on subject, we pressed against the waves yet as I looked from the gaping windows of my own crows nest, be it the control room I merely stared into darkness. The Captain alongside me tried to converse but his aggravating voice muffled against the monotonous engine that continually chugged as the towering chimney churned out smog into the Indiana night. I could hear the rattling of a chain and the clanging of metal coming from beneath but even though these flimsy faults offered unease I pressed on with the thought that soon enough I would be united with… My Shelby GT 500, oh and the Living Nightmare himself. “The young lad noticed who you be?” murmured the wrinkled old Captain with a croak. “I beg your pardon?” I replied, gazing at the Captain with curiosity as he stood navigating the vessel with a worrying smile etched along his bristled face. “Is he gay?” the questioned arose in my mind it must be the smile. “I was indeed questioning the guys sexuality, was he a homosexual and the lad below his wife away from land. You hear about those deprived…” I gulped hard and the colour filtered from my cheeks, as I stood not wanting to know when the sea-faring Captain finally spoke up. “The young lad below deck reckons your on the television mate, your into all that male grappling…” that was it I needed to voice myself and thus I interrupted. “I’M NOT GAY, HAPPY NOW HUH?” The Captains smile curled to that of a frown as his face retorted all expressionless as he stood in disbelief at what I had bellowed only seconds earlier, I had indeed jumped the gun and was looking somewhat foolish. The pause that followed was tense as both of us remained tight-lipped in our star struck pose downs when as I mumbled for a response the Captain disrupted with a soothing explanation. “Oh, that be a good joke!” cackled the old sea dog before concluding. “All you television people turn things into jokes but the lad was telling me that your one of those wrestlers… We weren’t to sure being as you’ve no make-up and you look smaller on the box but nobody wears green classy suits unless your Santa’s little helper right?” “WHAT!!!!” I ranted over in my mind, smiling falsely to keep things bobbing along in a gentlemanly manner. “Please to meet you Syko The Hobbit!” Unbelievably the decrepit old bastard has me mixed up with former Primetime Championship Wrestling performer ‘The Goblin’ oh how this ignited an inner rage, which I planned to unleash when from no-where a voice filtered indicating that we had finally approached our destination. Imbedded along the embankment was the nose of my once glistening Shelby GT 500 looking a shell of its former glorious self, the spotlight beamed over and we tackled the Mississippi River current to manoeuvre over. “There she is…” I told myself, arcing a smile along my cold face for the bitterness of the conditions I had finally succumb to. The vessel finally laid aside my pride and joy with just the tip aimed to the heavens and the backend where the Vampire resides remains isolated beneath feet of murky water. My fellowship stood staring in awe only aware that I would move heaven and earth to regain my property, never once questioning that in my celebrity status I could quite easily buy a replacement. The dynamic duo I sailed alongside in this journey hand signalled between one another and the young lackey stepped hotfooted up the metallic stairway towards the control room. Suddenly the HUGE winch beside me sprung into life, it’s movement startling me for this voyage had been one scare after another and the BIGGEST was yet to come, the grand finale so to speak. Creaking left the winch extended towards its target and taking a firm grasp of the lifting straps the Captain like a Superman stunt-double drifted across, balancing on the bonnet of my car. “Please God don’t scratch the paint work!” I whispered under my breath, my whispering creating a fog effect as the two different temperatures clashed. The bearded Captain tied the straps to the vehicle bumper and with a subtle nod the winch gave a hefty pull. You could hear the cogs straining but these seemed professional so I merely stood by and after several minutes of tug-o-war the silver Shelby arose from its burial chamber, I closed my eyes, fearing for the worse. Peering through my fingers the job had been successfully accomplished, even if the two hired for the task seemed idiotic at best but all that aside they had indeed resurrected my car and more importantly the behemoth known as Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. Unknown to them of its content I gave the order for the sealed boot to be opened, the youngster took a crowbar and with an animalistic passion he tried to chiselling the weld. Thud Clang After several nail biting minutes the seals defences fell and the boot sprung open like a casket lid and all three of us peered in with curiosity, peering into the depth of the vehicle boot that gazed back at us with nothing but darkness. Suddenly with a flash emerged the pale complexioned Vampire lunging from his tomb and towards the enticing throat of the winch operator… - Then silence -
