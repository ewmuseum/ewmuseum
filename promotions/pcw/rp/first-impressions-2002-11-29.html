First Impressions

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.11.02 The shot fades to a new scene, in the parking lot. Tiffany Jennings can be seen standing backstage. She motions the cameraman forward, and obediently he complies, the shot bobbling up and down as he trots along behind her. Suddenly, she comes to a halt as a vehicle can be heard approaching in the distance. A moment later, a large, rusty Ford F-150 comes into view. The driver of the old red truck puts on the breaks and she comes to grinding stop. Quickly exiting the door, it’s non other than PCW’s favorite ex-con/hick/cult leader, Abner. The crowd lets out a small pop as the newcomer trudges forward. He’s dressed appropriately for his ride: an old red, plad hunting vest, dusty blue jeans, and a torn pair of brown steeled-toe boots. With a grin on his face, he purposefully steps up next to Tiffany. Wasting no time at all, she begins. Tiffany: Welcome Abner, and welcome to PCW. Sooo, how does it feel to….(she gets cut off short). Abner raises his hand, turns on his heel and rushes back over to his truck. Tiffany: What’s he doing? The picture slightly wavers as the cameraman shrugs. A moment later, his intent his revealed. He proudly makes his way back to Tiffany, but now sporting a 24-pac of Budweiser under his arm. He nods toward the interviewer, giving her a quiet “Sorry, ma’am”. Tiffany sighs and then gets back on task. Tiffany: So Abner, what do you think the odds are of defeating your opponent, Kai tonight? Abner: (opening the case and pulling out a beer at the same time) Odds? Well, pretty damn shitty I suppose…..ya know, everyone’s bettin’ against me…..but I don’t care…. Tiffany: Yes?…. Abner: Well, see, I weren’t ever really good with numbers anyway, so math don’t mean a sheriff’s shit in prison to me! See, I ain’t really much of a good learner ya know……but you know what? Tiffany: What? Abner: I…..aight, hold on a second…..(opening a can of beer, Abner turns it up and downs half the can within seconds). Tiffany rolls her eyes. Abner: Ahhhh! Anway, like I was sayin’ ‘fore, I suck at math, heck, I’m worse at rithmatic than I am at obeying the goddamn law! And that’s sayin’ a lot. (Cheap pop). So as you might guess, I think it’s all (forcing his eyes to bulge out, he leans into the camera) horseshit! (Another cheap pop). Tiffany: How so? Abner: (finishing off the beer) How so? ‘Cause that goddamn hippie, Kai ain’t got a chance in Hell at hangin’ this hillbilly! See, I was raised a fightin’ man, and I’ll die one too! Like I says, “Brawlin’ bred in a brawlin’ bed; ‘twas brawlin’ born and I’ll be brawlin’ dead!” (Good size pop here). And you gotta know that it’s true. I survived the ol’ D-29 prison detention facility in Vermont, and I’ll survive a couple a sissy kicks here and there from some monk too! That goddamn monkity-monkey-monk, just wait ‘til I step in that ring…..I’m…..I’m gonna ……I’m gonna……(suddenly, he gasps, his face twisted in a look of terror). Tiffany: W…wh….what is it! Abner: Shit! I’m…..Goddamnit, I’m…..Goddamnit! Tiffany: Yes? Reaching down, Abner pulls up the case of beer: it’s empty. Abner: Christ-Almighty, I’m out of beer! He shakes his head in disgust, and turning his back toward the camera, starts for the truck. Tiffany: But…..but….wait! I’m not done…..it’s not over….. Abner shrugs as he tosses the empty case in the back of his truck. Angrily, he slams his fist into the side of his truck, leaving an imprint in the old door’s metallic frame. Tiffany: (giving one last desperate attempt) Bb-but, Don’t you want to be famous! It’s not the end yet….. This gets his attention. Cocking his head to one side, Abner swiftly spins around and with purpose, quickly walks back in the direction of the camera. With a fierce look of determination in his eyes, Abner nods his head in agreement. Abner: Goddamnit, Tiffany, you’re right…..how come I never thought of that! Pushing out her chest, her heart welling in pride, Tiffany Saddle addresses him. Tiffany: Well, Abner! After years of…..uh……um……Abner…..Abner…..Abner? Wh-wh-where are you going! Ignoring the women, Abner politely brushes by. Turning back just long enough to call out “Thanks darlin”, he disappears into the darkness of the night. The shaking reporter is almost in tears. Tiffany: (screaming) Thanks for what!? From somewhere out in the darkness, “Thanks for mentioning “The End”…..without you, I never would have thought of that…..” The voice is getting softer. “Now, where is that strange man……that son-va-gun owes me some beer……” Focusing back in on Tiffany. She shrugs, lets out a sigh, mumbling under her breath, “Ah, just another day at the office…..” Tiffany: (giving up) Back to you, guys. The view fades out and returns to ringside.
