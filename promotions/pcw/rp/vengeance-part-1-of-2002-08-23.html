Vengeance...Part 1 of ?

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-23-2002 'Sometimes, when adversity strikes, you really have to ante up and kick in like a man. I had finally gotten the opportunity to take back what was mine: The PCW Television Title. I had been the champion since the sixth of August, and had never even been able to wear the belt. Now though, that was about to change. Armed with an ancient scroll of prophecy, I had a mission that would determine the outcome of this climactic event. An event that would affect the shape of the entire PCW landscape....' The light of dawn was fresh on the horizon, and birds could be heard chirping in the morning breeze. The shot stays on the horizon and we watch a rising sun. Shortly, a small cloud of dust can be seen in the distance. Eventually, we are able to make out the form of a man on horseback silhouetted against the rising sun. As the rider approaches, the sun seems to reflect off of a gold colored shirt. As he rides closer, we see the man wears a shirt of chain mail armor that appears to be cast in gold. In his right hand he carries a sword, while he controls the reigns with his left. His medium length blonde hair flies back from his face, and his beard covered jaw is appears to be set in stone. His blue eyes are cold, but behind the coldness, if one looks closely, one can see that the fires of a deep insanity are stoked and burning. Venneman Pendragon, and he is riding hard into the morning. He takes a quick look over his shoulder and nods in approval. 'Here I am in the jungles of Africa, and I stick out like a sore thumb. I guess I should merely be glad that there is nobody following me. The locals here don't take to kindly to strangers. Especially those who are after what I am after...' Pendragon rides Xerxes into what appears to be a wall of dense foliage in the jungle. The camera follows and we can see that he follows what appears to be an ancient path. Once beneath the cover of the green canopy, he stops and unrolls a scroll that he has tucked away in his saddlebag. He quickly scans it, looks around and smiles.... Venneman: Glory from the ancient path; Glory in the aftermath..... 'Prophecy can be a dangerous thing. The Chinese master told me that all I will embark on to wrest my title from the darkness has been foretold, but why must it be pre-ordained? I am not one to argue with the supernatural, but what if I am on a Syko hatched goose chase? The feats in this scroll are rather unimaginable to have me perform without a thought. I only hope I had made the right choice by embarking on yet another adventure....' He shakes his head and places the scroll back in the saddlebag. He lets out a sigh and continues up the barely visible path into the jungle. He rides on and eventually comes to a clearing. He rides on until he reaches a cliff where the clearing ends. Across the way is another plateau, and on this plateau it would appear that a huge structure sits. Much like the 'pyramids' found in South America, it is tiered, and appears to be four tiers high. Pendragon shakes his head and dismounts. He walks Xerxes away from the edge and pats the horses muzzle.... Venneman: End of the road for you old boy...I have to go on alone. Give me two hours and if you don't see me coming, get the hell out of here. Too dangerous a place for you to wait much longer... Xerxes whinnies in understanding...Smart horse. Pendragon walks to the edge once more and looks down. The camera follows and we see a rope that leads across the drop and attaches to the pyramid. Pendragon sighs and walks back to his horse. He grabs the scroll and a pair of leather gauntlets from his saddlebag. He returns to the rope and shimmies out, stabilizing with his legs while using his arms to pull himself across. He gets smaller and smaller as he makes his way to the other side. Flash The Camera shot is now of Venneman coming towards us. Evidently, the camera didn't take the rope across. Closer and closer, until at last he heaves himself onto the plateau. He stands a moment and takes a deep breath as he views the huge structure in front of him. He let's out a whistle at the size of it and pulls the scroll from inside his shirt. He unrolls it and scans it again. He nods and places it back into his shirt. Evidently he has some sort of pocket in there. After a quick look around he draws his sword and makes his way cautiously to the pyramid. He reaches the base and finds a lever with an inscription writ next to it. What language is it? Who knows? Evidently Venneman does because he soon recites what it says.... Venneman: And to the world shall come the Goblin God. Taker of souls, eater of gold; enter, and find salvation.... 'This was getting worse by the minute. Here I was in the middle of nowhere and now I had to enter an ancient goblin temple. I just don't see how this is preordained. I feel like a fool for taking on the task, but I am already here. I might as well enter and see if I can get the talisman I seek...' Pendragon pulls the lever and a door appears in the rock. Pendragon has to duck to get in, but he does and the camera follows him. Inside, we find a large corridor lined with torches... Venneman: Evidently someone is home.... Pendragon cautiously walks down the corridor, sword held in front of him. He reaches an intersection and takes the right corridor that heads upstairs. At the top of the stairs, he looks to his left and sees what appears to be an ancient railing. He eases over and looks over the edge.... Venneman: Good Christ.... Below is a huge room. At the far wall of the room sits a huge statue of a Goblin. It sits cross-legged and holds a huge jewel in its lap. So what's the problem? The room is full of half-man sized creatures who bow and scrape before what appears to be a temple priest.... 'The place was full of Goblins. I didn't believe there could be so many in the world today! What the hell is that?' Pendragon looks over the edge once more and sees what the priest holds in HIS hands. A small statue of a man. Clad in golden armor, the statue also holds a sword.... 'Evidently someone was prophetic. These little bastards new I was coming. My only hope was that they didn't know I was already here. Evidently, this was a trap. How in the hell was I to get that jewel? I needed some kind of diversion....No way a clever disguise would work in here. I am just too damned big!' Voice: Halt infidel....Yes. A knight for a greedy goblin.... Pendragon turns and is met with the visage of a pair of small Goblins. One goblin holds a crossbow on him and smiles. The other holds a small battle axe and looks a tad nervous... Goblin 1: When the others find out....Then we shall feast, yes intruder? Goblin 2: Come Fryko. This man is a monster! Do not toy with him, let us take him down and get back! Venneman: You should listen to your friend, Fryko....I hope you are prepared to use that bolt, vermin. I am ready to use mine.... In a flash, Pendragon lunges forward and the Goblin fires the crossbow. Pendragon catches the bolt in his left hand and goes in swinging Excalibur with his right.... Venneman: All you shall feast upon this day is cold steel, worm! Venneman hacks the blade straight down through the Goblins shoulder, cutting him from neck to nuts. Green blood pours out and the stunned Goblin cannot even scream for help. Pendragon smiles insanely at the other goblin who quickly relieves himself down his leg. With another mighty swing, the goblin's head flies from his shoulders. Pendragon grins at his dispatch of the evil Goblins, who now hit the ground with twin thuds. Venneman kneels and picks up the crossbow. He removes the quill of arrows from the dead goblin and slings them over his shoulder the bow and quill over his shoulder. He then picks up the battle-axe that fits his hand more like a hatchet... 'I was outnumbered and I needed every weapon I might lay hands upon. These two would fight no more, so why not put their weapons to good use as take out their demonic brethren? Seems the right choice for me!' To his left is the way he came, and to his right stands another passage. Footfalls can be heard from the former, so he takes off towards the right passage. In a blur of gold he is gone...For such a large fellow, he does move quickly. 'I didn't need to meet a whole Goblin army at once, so it was deeper into the temple for me. I would choose my time to exact my vengeance on these foul creatures. Syko thought that this ruse would stop me. He has another thing coming. I would get that jewel from his temple, and Sunday, I would get my gold back too. I grew tired of my games with the Goblin, and my TV Belt had stayed with the villain for too long. It was time for me to get nasty about things. And to top it off, he sent a lawyer to try and take care of his dirty work. And goddamned if I don't hate lawyers! Oh yes. Syko would pay as I exacted my revenge. I only hoped the world didn't miss the goblins I was going to have to dispatch to survive this day. It is I or them, and I guarantee that the hero prevails in this one.....' >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
