Unbelivable (part 2)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26.04.02 The scene opens up in sunny, uptown Manhattan. There is a long wide road, with cars parked up and down the road. Along the sidewalk there are small trees lining the road. There are people walking around, up and down the sidewalk carrying there little plastic bags. There are a whole host of people sitting on the sidewalk on silver chairs, sipping on coffee, all of them white. And most of them are blonde haired females, probably never worked a day in their life, just living on daddy’s hand outs all their lives. Along the road are lots of big stores. All rich people stores like Versace, YSL and the occasional Starbucks. There is not a black, Asian or anyone else but white people around. The scene zooms into this big black BMW X5 jeep, which is parked on the side of the road. In side of the car is a big, muscular African American. The guy is, obviously, PCW wrestler Duzza. Duzza is dressed in a pair of very baggy white pants, a big black XXL FUBU Harlem shirt and has a black baseball cap and has a big black puffer jacket on as well. He has a large platinum chain around his neck with a cross at the end, but that is it for jewelry. For a gangsta and all, he is looking very nervous in this white area. It is something about being surrounded by them crazy star bucks drinking, sweatshirt wearing, cake eating white folks that makes Duzza nervous. After about 20 minutes another black guy walks up to the side of the jeep. He knocks on the door and Duzza lets him in Duzza: “FOOL! You done near gave me a heart attack boy” The other guy, dressed in a pair of baggy leather pants, and a black tank top covering a white t-shirt. He also has one a black cap. This is the recently made multi millionaire, Pookie. After his $300 million prize on the lottery. Pookie: “F*ck wrong wit’ you nigga?” Duzza: “Never and I mean never again, sneak up on a brother in a white neighbourhood and tap on his window. Scared the f*ck out of me bro” Pookie: “Have you been sniffing glue again? That ain’t cool man” Duzza: “No, I ain’t been sniffing glue again… Just, it’s something about these white people in rich area’s… I mean look at them” Pookie looks at Duzza as if he is a mad man, before looking around at all the rich white people. Pookie: “Umm… they look pretty harmless to me” Duzza: “Ahh, that’s what they want you to think… but I tell ya, there’s something about them star bucks drinking mother f*ckers that gives me the creeps” Pookie: “Man, has all that ice you wear gone to your head? You trippin’” Duzza: “Whatever. Man, you get your cash?” Pookie: “Oh hell yeah! I spent all morning getting it. Some white guy, he advised me on what to do. So I bought a house in the Hamptons, 22 East Way Drive, sweet place” Duzza: “What? What was that address?” Pookie: “22 East Way Drive, why?” Duzza: “Un-F*cking-belivable” Pookie: What?” Duzza: “That’s next to me. You’re my neighbor! I don’t believe it” Pookie: “Haha, who’d of thought, you spend all yo life working hard so you can afford a house in the Hamptons, and me over here, all I did was rob a liquor store to get the cash for the to get the ticket and now I got more money than you!” Duzza: “Yeah.. life’s great ain’t it….bastard” Pookie: “Haha, don’t hate, appreciate my brother. I told you I’d get out the hood, told you there was a easier way than doing all that studying and shit” Duzza: “Dude… are you gonna do anything apart from rub it in my face how I have had to work hard to get here and you ain’t ever done shit and richer than me?” Pookie: “Err… yeah that was the plan” Duzza: “Then you can get the f*ck out of my jeep, ya n-good, liquor store robbing fool” Pookie: “Bro…I was joking, now c’mon man, anyway, you said we’d go car shopping” Duzza: “Aww yeah..a’ight dawg, what car you looking for? The jeep? The porsche? Or the Bentley?” Pookie: “Brother, you know it’s the Bentley” Duzza: “Haha, I was hoping you’d say that, there’s a Bentley garage just around the corner, let’s go” Duzza and Pookie get out of the jeep and make there way up the street and around the corner. They come up to the Bentley garage. There is a big Bentley sign and there are Bentley’s parked all over the place. The actual salesroom is made out of glass and has a few cars inside. A tall white man in a very smart suit walks up to them, he looks a bit nervous and looks as if he doesn’t want them in the salesroom. Salesman: “Umm hi…Gentleman… how can I help you?” Duzza: “Fool, we hear to buy a car, why else would we be in a Bentley showroom?” Salesman: “I dread to think” Pookie: “So, yeah I want a car” Salesman: “Ok….well…there is a Pinto garage, on 53rd street. I’m sure you will be able to find everything you need there” Duzza: “Player please. Pinto! Don’t you know who I am!? Man, I oughta kick yo white ass back to Europe” Salesman: “Ok, now before I get security to escort you of the premises, let me ask, how would you be able to pay for a Bentley? Your another one of them rappers aren’t you?” Duzza: “Errr… we try…but no” Salesman: “You’re a basketball player then? Or is it football? C’mon what is it… your not a drug dealer?” Duzza: “Man… you ‘bout to get a beating… I’ma beat on Charlie Loc and then I’ma roll back on round and beat on yo ass mother f*cker…. I’m a pro wrestler and he just one the lottery a’ight?” Salesman: “AHAHAHAHAAHA a pro wrestler and a lottery winner! I’m on a hidden camera show aren’t I?!” Duzza: “Pookie, show this fool your money” Pookie, opens a bag which he has been carrying around with him. Inside are a lot of notes. The salesman stops laughing and looks at the bag of money with his mouth wide open. Salesman: “Ok….well, let’s take a look at the cars then shall we?” Duzza: “Thank you” About hours later, all three men are standing around a black, drop top Bentley Azure. Pookie: “I’ll take it” Duzza quickly pulls Pookie away from the car and whispers to him Duzza: “Nigga….it just occurred to me. Do you even have a freaking driving liscence!?” Pookie: “Well… no…” Duzza: “WHAT!? So we spent the last 3 hours looking around this showroom and you can’t even drive?! Damn dawg…I oughta slap you” Pookie: “Dawg, chill, I’ma get lessons… but meanwhile, how about you put the deposit down yeah? Let them thing it’s your car man, it’ll be cool” Duzza: “Man… sometimes I wonder why I’m still friends witchu” Pookie: “Ain’t it obvious? You know I got ya back” Salesman: “Yeah, I hate to interrupt but do you want to take it out on a test drive now? Only, I work on commission you see…and so far I haven’t sold jack” Pookie: “A test drive huh? Cool, yeah let’s do that, I’ll drive” Duzza: “Aww man” 20 minutes later, Pookie actually manages to get the car out onto the road. Pookie may be the worst natural driver in the history of driving. Even a child couldn’t of stalled the car as many times as he did in the first 5 minutes of being out on the road. Pookie can only drive about 10 meters before either stalling the car or breaking harshly. Everyone in the street stares at them as they drive past. Duzza in the passenger seat and the salesman in the back both cover their faces in shame. Meanwhile, Pookie just keeps on rolling around the area, throwing gang signs at everyone from thugs to old white people. Salesman: “ Err, you do know how to drive don’t you?” Pookie: “Umm… err yeah, for sure man, jus chill” Duzza: “C’mon Pookie, just put ya foot down on the accelerator man” Pookie: “Umm, are you sure I should?” Duzza: “Please do. C’mon, just pick up the speed man” Pookie: “OK… here goes nothing” Pookie slams the accelerator down to the ground and the Bentley speeds up, it keeps going faster and faster All: “AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH” Duzza: “Brake fool” Pookie: “You told me to go faster” Duzza: “Yeah 40 miles per hour fast, not 80 fool…BRAKE” But Pookie, who seems to be getting some type of thrill from speeding along a busy road. Duzza is holding on to anything he can while the Salesman is praying to God. Suddenly, Pookie skids while trying to overtake some white guy on a scooter and crashes straight into a Salvation Army charity store. The Bentley completely takes out the front window, and the glass total’s the bonnet of the Bentley. Both Duzza and Pookie are fine, but the salesman is knocked out cold. Duzza looks around… a crashed Bentley which hasn’t been paid for and a charity shop missing a front window… Duzza quickly comes up with the perfect solution to fix all of this Duzza: “Pookie… grab the bag of money…and RUN”
