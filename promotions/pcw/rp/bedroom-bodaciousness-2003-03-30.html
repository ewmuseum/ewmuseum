Bedroom Bodaciousness

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 30, 2003 ['Totally Tubular' Tommy is half naked on top of his waterbed. He's swaying his body back and forth slowly as if he were some sort of smooth jazz singer. The soothing sounds of 'This Must Be Love' by Phil Collins fills the bedroom with a very warm feeling. The dim lights above fit the slow moving love song to perfection. Tommy's perfectly defined abs crunch together as he sways his hips back and forth to Phil's wonderful vocals. The scene goes down to find a very beautiful young lady lying in the bed with a smile across her face. Her brown hair looks to have just been let out of a pony tail and she is in a pink, silk bathrobe. Tommy now kneels on the waterbed making both of their bodies shift from side to side like a ship on the ocean. Tommy keeps his head bobbing to the music as he leans into the woman and gives her a quick kiss] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: How was that, babe? [The woman giggles a little, grabbing a glass of wine next to her and taking a small sip from it] Brown haired woman: It was nice. TTT: But not perfect? Brown haired woman: Perfect would be pushing it, but it was wonderful. [Tommy ponders on that a few moments. He wonders to himself exactly how his swaying body was not absolute perfection? That's pretty bogus of her to just down grade a performance like that to 'wonderful'. That's like saying that his World Title victory last Turmoil was just decent and not perfection. Who does this babe think that she is? Well, she does teach ballet and jazz at the local dance studio, but that's to little heinous kids with absolutely no rhythm. Tommy has is the most bodacious dancer in all of LA, he's GOT the rhythm. In fact, he put the Thm in rhythm! He slowly reaches over to the nighttable where the lady picked up her wine glass and Tommy begins slowly raising the golden PCW World Title. He places it over his waist and begins swaying his hips to the sounds of Phil Collins once again. Tommy gives the lady a warm smile and nods to her] Brown haired woman: Now THAT is just perfect! [Now that's more like it, Tommy thinks to himself. Maybe he is having a bogus night with dancing, because it has been known to happen. It could just be all the totally rad partying that he's been doing since the victory on Tuesday. He just needed the gold to sort of inspire his dance moves to be sharp and precise. He did feel a bit sloppy for a few seconds there, but that could also be because of the gnarly love that they were making just moments earlier. That's when Tommy starts forgetting about his dance moves and thinks about a nice steamy evening with a dance instructor. Tommy now understands why it's a gnarly time with a dancer because of the defined muscles all over their bodies. Their strong grasps and fluid movements make the feeling ten times better. Tommy smiles to himself in the mirror above his bed, looking at the wonderful gold across his waist. He realizes that if he had dance instructors all the time it probably would lose it's most excellent magic. Would Thanksgiving be so bodacious if you would have a feast like that everyday? It wouldn't. But Tommy isn't going to not have a feast with bodacious dancer babes every year....maybe every week] Dancer instructor: You are just an amazing man, Tommy. I know a lot of other guys who would kill to have the life that you have made for yourself. Surfer, wrestler, money, women, and power...they'd love to have it all. Do you really know how lucky you are? [Tommy finds humor in the statement. Luck has nothing to do with how he got to the point where he's at] TTT: Babe, it's called hard work. See, these totally heinous losers your talking about are the compadres who want everything to HAPPEN TO THEM! Where as yours truly, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, goes out there and makes things happen. Just like this Venon Pandagram dude in PCW...he doesn't understand what kind of person that I am. He just thinks because I'm more BODACIOUS than he is that it makes me evil and heinous. He doesn't know about the long hours I put in at the gym or the extra hours I spend in the ring to perfect my skill. He's one of those bogus losers who believe that I cheated my way to the top and didn't even pay any dues to get there. Well, I just let them believe whatever they want, babe, because I'm here and NOTHING will take me down. Dance instructor: Maybe your right. Those same guys say that you treat women like sexual objects, like some kind of conquests of lust. I saw how you were always with different women in the papers and stuff, but the smiles on their faces didn't show that you treated them wrong. In fact, it seems to me that you go out of your way to make women extra happy. I think I've learned THAT first hand... [She laughs and they share another soft kiss with one another] TTT: I'm not going to lie to you. I'm with a lot of bodacious babes and I stress to all women I'm with that at this conjucture in my life I cannot be a one woman man. With all the traveling and press I have to do, it would be MOST STRESSFUL on a babe! I would never want to put another person through all that loneliness and frustration. Though when I see a babe I do treat them right and I give them the world because women are the most beautiful creatures on this earth. Especially the totally radical one that is on my bed. [The two share a laugh with one another and then it's another soft kiss. They look into each others eyes for a few moment, the woman taking in those hypnotic blue eyes. She pulls Tommy in for another kiss, this time a little longer. Her brown eyes are filled with such satisfaction and happiness. It's like she's completely high on life now, or maybe it could have been the radical glasses of wine she had] Dance instuctor: So, what's in the future of the new champion now? Are you going to rise up to be the best ever? TTT: Of course, bodacious babe! That's just a given with yours truly! I may be a super villain and a most unliked compadre but that isn't going to stop me from being a fighting champion. In fact, I'm tagging with a most BODACIOUS dude by the name of Xavier Kannon on Turmoil! He's a gnarly dude. Dance instructor: Isn't he that guy who is into Scientology? TTT: TOTALLY! Scientology is most bodacious!! John Travolta is into that too... Dance instructor: So, you and this Xavier man are going to win? TTT: Babe, why must you ask questions you already know the answers to? OF COURSE WE'RE GOING TO WIN! I am the current World Champion, and Xavier was ROBBED of his reign and should still have the title on his WAIST! But I guess it was just for my destiny that it had to happen though... [The woman looks at her watch and she is completely surprised] Dance instructor: Oh my god...it's nine now! I have to late night class to teach in an hour! TTT: That's bogus...I was hoping we could spend more time together. Dance instructor: I know, Tommy...I'm REALLY sorry. I have to go to this class because the ladies wanting me to teach are laying out BIG MONEY for me. I might be able to finally open my own school with enough lessons to these women. TTT: Gnarly... [The woman scurries through the dimly lit bedroom for her clothes and with quick grabs she collects most of her clothing. Tommy lies back on the waterbed taking a small breather from the moment he shared with the babe. She seems nice...really nice. Intelligent babe who really enjoys money a lot. Those are the best babes to have. The ones that appreciate the bodaciousness that money can bring! It's a great quality. As Tommy looks over to her she's already back in her red evening gown and putting some woman things back into her purse. She smiles and seems totally bummed about leaving] Dance instuctor: I'm just sorry about this. The date with you and the...well, the night here it just slipped my mind. How about we get together tomorrow evening? TTT: It's a bad time, babe. Lots of training and tape watching to do tomorrow. Then I have to travel to Turmoil the next day. It could be another week before we can even see each other! But...it's not like we'll never see each other again...right? [She wears a sad smile on her face] Dance instructor: Right. Goodbye, Tommy. [She walks out of the bedroom and her footsteps fade away down the hallway. The sliding front door opens and then closes. Tommy smiles to himself, catching his reflection in the mirror above himself] TTT: Harry... 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man? TTT: Send in the next babe, please? Harry: Ok, man. [Tommy's bedroom door opens and a blonde woman wearing a sparkling blue dress comes in smiling like crazy. Tommy nods] TTT: That's a bodacious color for you. Woman: Thank you. What were you busy with? TTT: Just a phone call...nothing important it turned out. But YOUR the important thing tonight...GET OVER HERE, BODACIOUS BABE!!! Fade to black
