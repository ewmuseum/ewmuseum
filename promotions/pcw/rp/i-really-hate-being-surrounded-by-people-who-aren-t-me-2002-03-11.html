I Really Hate Being Surrounded By People Who Aren't Me

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/03/02 We open up on the big Yellow “M” that symbolises McDonald’s, the world most popular restaurant chain. We zoom away from it to show the frontage of the building with a steady stream walking both in and out at what appeared to be a busy time of day. Xavier : “I don’t get it, we can afford to eat at the world’s finest restaurants, I can get reservations an hour before anywhere in the country....And you want to go to McDonald’s?” The camera pans around to show the first couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall, walking into shot, Kannon weighed down with shopping bags from stores that hint strongly it is his valet’s shopping rather than his. Eleanor : “Because I want at Vanilla Milkshake....So until your beloved Snapple~! starts doing them, then this place does the best...” Ellie~! is wearing a Pink skinny fit t-shirt with White collar and sleeves and Snapple~! is glittering Silver across the chest. With it she has a rainbow patterned fabric belt and Purple faded jeans with Gold sequinned stars down both legs to her Tan Converse sneakers. Her long Brown hair with Blond highlights is neatly ties back out of her face and on her wrists she wears a chunky watch and various bands and bracelets. Xavier : “Fine.....But if a single one of those ingrates in there tries to talk to me, or even recognises me you are getting a Snapple~! from the minimart next door!” The Scientologist opens the door and just as he is about to go through a scruffy dressed mother and her unwashed children pour out to Xavier’s disgust, both at their rudeness and appearance. Xavier : “I keep telling you....If you carry on coming here you’ll end up like that!” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a full length Brown leather coat with an un-buttoned Black silk shirt with Purple fractal pattern. It falls open to show a Black “Krayzie ‘til I Die” t-shirt like those worn by Jeremy Howard, taking another stab at his ex-Asylum team mate. With this he has a pair of his usual Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery depicting Scientological runes snaking down the left leg to his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. His long Auburn hair is only tied at the back, allowing the rest to fall down as a fringe and either side of his narrow face, flanking his piercing Green eyes. Eleanor : “Pur-Leez.....It would take a complete Trailer Trash transfusion to make me like that....Unlike Jessica, that lack of a hair brush and she’s basically there!” Xavier grins smugly as he holds the door open for PTC’s top valet as they enter the busy restaurant, thinking of his upcoming match for the Continental Title, the memory triggered by Ellie~!’s bitch comment about Jeremy’s girlfriend. Xavier : “I can picture the Howard family minus the wrestling money now....Some little basement apartment somewhere, drying washing in front of the one radiator under the mould covered ceiling. Banging on the walls to get the arguing couple next door to quieten down, kept awake by the dog howling outside all night....Well, I think from that we can see that money certainly doesn’t buy class, and there is no better example that Howard, his bitch wife and ugly brat kid!” They join the back of the queue and look forward, past the other 7 people ahead of them to the acne ridded worker who is hurriedly punching orders uncertainly into the till while trying to talk to the customer at the same time. Xavier : “Great, it looks like we are going to be here more than the 10 seconds I can usually stand. I tell you, being tossed over the top rope and punched by a Krazed Lunatic is far more enjoyable than being stuck amidst this cross section of humanity’s lower dregs!” Eleanor : “Humanity’s........Lower........Dregs!” They make no effort to mask their contempt for those around them and are met with evil looks that are countered right back with the 1st Couple of Scientology’s sneers of disdain. “I’VE TOLD YOU ALREADY........BEHAVE!!!!” They looks around to where an overweight woman wearing an ill fitting White t-shirt and Blue jogging bottoms is screaming at her son who is running around the restaurant, getting in people’s way. Xavier : “Hopefully when Scientology finally takes over this decaying country we can pass the three foot rule.....” The Scientologist looks in disgust at the family where the father gets up and grabs his screaming son, slapping him before dumping him back on the seat as the boy now bawls his eyes out. Eleanor : “Three......Foot........Rule?” They look around to where children are being allowed to run wild as their parent, giving up on them, don’t even care to notice. Instead they just get on with their value meals, occasionally looking up to shout through full mouths for their badly named children to come back to the table. Xavier : “The three foot rule is where you are free to kick any kid who strays more than three feet away from their parents...But then again, if the people who spawned me looked, talked and ate like that, I think I’d be running away.” Ellie~! pale Blue eyes come to life and she smiles, thinking of the implications of the rule coming into action and the amount of fun she would have lurking, laying in wait for a chance to kick a kid for six. Eleanor : “That would, like, be SO the greatest law ever! They could have contests at supermarkets where you get free stuff for kicking them into the freezer compartment, or hey can let you drops backs of flower on them or throw tins at their head....Why can’t Scientology rule the world?” She looks upset now at the realisation that what she has pictured will not come true. Xavier : “The world is still far too narrow minded my little Poon Puppy....But do not worry, we are the fastest growing faith on the planet so sooner or later, our voice of reason, our religion of the future will be heard.” WHAAAAAAAAAAA! WHAAAAAAAAAAA! WHAAAAAAAAAAA! WHAAAAAAAAAAA! WAAAAAAAAAAA! Their attention is now attracted to a family of 4 sitting around a table where the 2 children keep hitting one another until the younger bursts out into tears, more to the annoyance than concern of the family. Xavier : “They wonder why our country is in such freefall....That family is a just example of the social and moral decline of America....” Ellie~! tries to understand what he means and looks both to him and the family, still looking confused at his words. Xavier : “I bet you on Sunday that family will get dressed up in their discount store smart dress, the father will put the fear of their God into the two kids as the mother looks on wondering why they ever got married before they drive to church to show the rest of the equally declining society that they can put on a normal visage....I sickens me...” The father raises his hand at the crying child, scaring him into holding back his emotions as he sniffs and wipe the tears that still stream from his yes away. Xavier : “Sign that family up to Scientology and they might just stand a chance of being together by the time the oldest kid hits 10. Get them to stop spending their kid’s welfare on cigarettes and cheap whiskey to dress them properly...Educate them so that they can get off the sofa and into work...Even that group of 4 total and utter waste of atoms can become contributing members of society if they followed Hubbard.” Finally they get to the front of the queue and Ellie~! gets ready to order as the acne faced adolescent looks up, speaking in a bored voice. Kid : “Yes.....” Eleanor goes to place her order when Xavier holds his hand up, staring at the employee with his piercing Green eyes and a scowl on his face. Xavier : “Hey, kid....Your job....Your only purpose in life is to serve this lady, you contribute noting else an you probably never will....So ask her what she would like as if the world depended on it...because quite frankly, yours does!” The worker looks embarrassed as the people queuing around then look on at Xavier as he gives the reprimand and after he stops looking down at the floor the boy looks up to Eleanor again, shifting his gaze over to Xavier every few seconds. Kid : “Um....Hello Madam, how can I be of service....” As he finishes he looks to Xavier who gives him a stern nod to tell him that was better. Eleanor : “One large Vanilla Milkshake.....Please....” As she gives the order, she even manages to make the ‘Please’ sound rude and sarcastic. As he goes to get her order the 1st couple of Scientology are distracted by a man’s hollering voice. “IF YOU DON’T BEHAVE THEN I’LL GIVE YOU A SPANKING RIGHT HERE AND NOW!” The out burst attracts the attention of most of the restaurant and they turn to see the wild eyed father of the family of 4 leaning forward over the table to loom over his children, his hand poised above their heads ready to strike. Xavier : “Geez....The sooner you have to get a license to breed the better! I mean look at Jeremy Howard, how he was even allowed to keep his son let alone have him in the first place only Hubbard will know!” He shakes his head and turns around to face the counter once again as Ellie~! waits impatiently for her Milkshake. Xavier : “I mean, he admits he’s crazy, he calls himself the Krayzie One or the Krazed Lunatic....And he is allowed to be a father? Some social service is mucking up there! Maybe if they saw his un-provoked assaults on me at Fusion and Sabotage they might consider whether he is even fit to have a child in his home!” As Xavier ponders this over Ellie~! is served her Milkshake and puts a White and Red striped straw into the top and takes a sip. Xavier : “Come on my little Shag Squirrel we have business to take care of!” Eleanor : “Huh? I thought we were going home.....My feet were tired and I think I need to wash my hair after coming in here!” They start to walk out through the queues that have formed behind them and emerge by the door, looking back out into the bright mid-afternoon. Xavier : “Well, I just had an idea.....I don’t think I should even have to fight Jeremy for the Continental Title at Turmoil....I think all the oppression I have suffered is enough that PCW should just put the belt on me to stop me taking any further action....But failing that if Howard fails to turn up....I get his belt....” Eleanor looks him in the eyes as they make their way out, wondering that he is going on about this time. Eleanor: “Ya wha?” Kannon puts his arm around her shoulder as they walk off down the road as the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “Oh you’ll love this my preppy little valet....I am going to find evidence to have Howard’s kid taken away! Maybe he will have a chance of a better future away from his dad anyway. Plus, I have about 1 year of video evidence proving that he is un-trustworthy and down right Krayzie.....I am truly a genius!” The 1st couple of Scientology cross the road, both grinning evilly as the shot hits Black. Eleanor : “Truly.......A........Genius!”.
