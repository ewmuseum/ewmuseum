In D.C.

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In D.C. Featuring: The H.I.T. The government's come a calling. For most of his teaching career, The HIT thought it would be the FBI, arresting him for his unusual teaching methods. But no, it is the government in the form of the Department of Education. They have commissioned the use of The HIT's image in order to promote math. Now, HIT's always wanted to promote math.....but it makes for a hell of a travel schedule. Washington D.C. one day. Italy the next. No doubt a ploy to jet lag the Trapezoid before Friday by Xavier Kannon. Still.....if the Scientologist wants mind games, few play them better than the Sultan of Sides.... The Bishop of Bisection! THE MOTHER-F*CKING H! I! T! ::At a podium set up in front of an out of the way spot in Washingto D.C. is the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. Behind him is a giant banner saying, 'F.U.C.K. Math!' A steady stream of autograph seekers approaches him, despite the odd location...:: The HIT: This is incredible.....I've got my comeback match on Fusion, and that impudent square Xavier Kannon has me working an autograph signing....that man's power needs to be checked desperately, and that's where I fit in. Despite my detente with Rent-A-Hero.....the superhero has been nowhere to be found recently. Meaning that, I'm going to have to count on his innate abilities rather than a sound strategy to win this match.... :He pauses, signing his John Hancock on several programs, and slips of paper, before looking up to continue speaking.: The HIT: And then, even with THAT done....I get to go fight two newbies and a Schitzo for a shot at the PCW Extreme Title....at least there's no Ed anymore. But, the Front Office comes first and foremost. And, when I think of the regime of Xavier Kannon.....I think of taxing. Now, we're all taxed by the federal, state, and municipal governments. And remember, I'M A TEACHER! I HAVE NO MONEY TO START WITH! Why do you think I made Brandi sign me to a tax-free contract? I give everyone else enough damn money! :Trapezoid smirks.: The HIT: Besides, Kannon taxing us is illegal! The money goes to him and his damn Scientology! Hasn't he ever read the American Constitution?! First Amendment, Establishment clause? His economic support of religion is establishing Scientology as the official religion of PCW! HUBBARD WAS A MORON! HEY XAVIER! ::The HIT smiles, preparing to deliver the ultimate insult to the Hollywood loving owner of PCW.....: The HIT: BATTLEFIELD EARTH WAS HORRIBLE! ::Undoubtedly, L. Ron Hubbard is rolling over in his grave somewhere, as the Trapezoid continues to sign autographs, until a wrinkly-looking woman approaches, rage in her eyes.:: Wrinkly Woman: YOU! HOW DARE YOUR SLOGAN HANG OVER THIS RALLY! The HIT: Look, lady......what do you have against my slogans?! Wrinkly Woman: 'F.U.C.K. Math'.....Disgraceful! The HIT: Don't talk to me like that about my teaching system! Wrinkly Woman: Teaching System? The HIT: YEAH! F.U.C.K.! That's for Fun, Unviolent, Caring, and Kind, I'll have you know! Wrinkly Woman: .....but don't you know what the letters spell? The HIT: OF COURSE! FUCK! AS IN, GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE, BITCH! Wrinkly Woman: You terrible man! The woman attempts to swing with her cane, however, one of the young autograph seekers grabs it, and begins bashing the woman in the knee with it. The HIT: Ah! Now that's what I call F.U.C.K.ing.....wait a second, come here, young man... The young child, looking no more than seven comes over to the Trapezoid eagerly. Young Child: Yes? The HIT: You're doing a great job of F.U.C.K.ing, but, you can't F.U.C.K. an old lady like her! Young Child: I can't? The HIT: Nope. Nature prevents it. So, be more violent! Young Child: Wow! Thanks Mr. Trapezoid! The boy begins swinging with more force, smacking the woman in the kneecap, dropping her. The HIT: Bravo! Now, this young man is hardcore! Moreso I'd wager than Rollins....or that Sopranos reject I'll be fighting on Tuesday...although, that Schitzo worries me a bit....but there I go again! It all goes back to Walden! As a few intrepid autograph seekers step over the wrinkly woman's body, he happily signs their autographs, speaking again to the crowd. The HIT: Tim Walden.....the taxman of the Front Office...and the man source of my ire. Walden, you think you can tax everything....you believe yourself to be someone, when in reality, all you do is job to guys like Flaymin Aymin and THE Multiple Man. You have never been anything. Not as a Thug 4 Hire. Not as a Wild Deuce. And so, I SHALL SMITE THEE FOR BEING IN STABLES WITH NUMBERS IN THEM THAT SUCKED! Walden.....Xavier.....time draws near....until the Hero and I BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! WE'RE GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! AND I'M SURE AS HELL GONNA KICK YO' ASS! Trapezoid continues signing, as we fade to protractors.
