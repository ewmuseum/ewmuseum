Going To The Carnival [3] Matrix Stylin'

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24.12.02 BEEEEP! Honking horns screech down Toronto's busy highway intersections and motorways as our two players migrate their way from the north of the Canadian border all teh way out towards the sancity of home to them, America. They speed around, as if they were hunting some fictional creature hell bent on taking over the world or trying to destroy it like so many failed plots in the Star Trek saga. They're going down this road to chase only one thing, and that is to reclaim what they feel happen to be theirs in the first place - the Primetime Championship Wrestling Tag Team Championships taken away from them by those Pranksters and now 'dead' team of Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Nascosto Iruel Nerezza, also known as Pranks With Attitude. But as seen from Decimation which seems to be on the heads of every single people inside of the Carnival of Carnage match, the squadron of Playaz Inc. seem to not be phazed by people putting them out of the picture. After all, they haven't really been in serious compeition in two months, slightly after the end of the Black on Black Violence match two days before Alliances. The eyes of Statuz Quo, whether or not they might be darkened by not getting any sleep or just awakening from a very deep and personal marijuana incident, cloaks his eyes in dark rimmed shades as his compadre Dante Jackson opens himself out to the UV rays and various pedestrains trying to find their way to their destinations. 'Well...' The cloaked Quo sits back in the passenger seat as the female components to Dante and Statuz sit in the back, prepping themselves for visiting the vast majority of a white Christmas in Jefferson, New Hampshire. 'You said we had to come here.' The rap superstar's voice grows confident and boastful even more. 'Don't blame me if Canada is home to finding dead cows on the street corners, nobody but Kannon told us to come here in the first place.' Spanning in front of them and about fourteen cars in front of our travellers, a lone cow with most of its intestine spread out along the highway happens to sit, dead and all sorts of it. Meaning they're no forms of having twelve different forms of this cow pop up from anywhere in the streets. Police and highway patrol sit on the operation, scratching their heads as a local jethro is questioned by police. Dante turns to his partner in crime, a broad smile on his face. 'You ever wonder why cops never figure out the silliest things?' 'Naw,' report the crunkest playa alive with a smirk. 'ever wonder how white Tag teams always search for titles but neva find dem?' Dante nods, before turning his attention back to the wheel. 'Yeah. And they called us the epitome of bad tag teams.' 'It isn't funny you guys.' Leaning from the backseat, Tracy thunks Dante in the back of the head with a flick of her index finger and thumb. Dante jerks forward, clutching his head in a quick reflex notion. 'Because of ya'll, we've been looked on as people without a conscious.' Sheeca added in. 'Hoe, the fuck you talkin' bout?' Statuz sharply cuts his head backwards at his female confidant. 'Can't even spell conscious!' Dante shakes his head, 'Chill out, both of ya'll!' The three passengers inside of the car grumble as Dante turns to Statuz and taps him on the shoulder. The Boutiest Playa Alive jerks Dante's arm off of his shoulder, before the Lyrical Messiah shakes it so much that it finally gets Quo's attention. 'Ni - what the fuck do you want?!' an angered Quo turns aorund to face his tag team partner who as always has a cunning look upon his face.'Remeber how you always played Scavenger Hunt as a kid on Easter looking for eggs?' 'Yeah,' the meance from the ghetto responds, his eyes more focused into question now as Dante continues. 'That's what Carnival of Carnage is about, a scavenger hunt for the PCW Tag Team titles.' 'Meaning what?' the bald Piru demon asks Dante. With the question in mind, Dante looks out towards the outside, before spotting a gold piece stranded out in the middle of the road. Looking at it with an intense glare, he steps out of the car, slamming the driver's door shut. Walking over past the scene of the crime, he can barely hear one Canadian Highway Patrolman shout, 'Hey! That's evidence!' Dante bends over, arching his back as he looks deeply into the piece of gold with albiet a gaze in his eyes. 'The heck?' Dante questions as he pockets the piece of gold and walks away from the scene as the Canadian Patrol Man stops right in front of him. 'Hey budday, that's is police property, okey?' Jackson turns around, before delivering a thick boot to the face of the officer. He slumps back, not even taking time to clutch his chin before he falls down ontot he pavement. The officers in the scene in front of Quo, Sheeca, and Tracy in the car all look as the two officers pull out their weapons, aiming directly at the head of Dante Jackson. 'Dante, be like O.J. and RUN!' Tracy hollers from the car, but Dante doesn't pay her any attention. He reaches into a black leather trenchcoat, somehow forming out of thin air and pulls out two large HK-5 machine guns. 'Damn, this be like that Matrix -' Soon, gun bullets begin raining from the sky as Dante plugs the officers in the chest with various bullets, each one moving slower than the next in some sort of bullet time fiasco. Dante leaps in mid-air, before whipping out the HK-5's machine guns and pointing them directly at the officers in front of him. 'Oye...' A light sounded pop echoes throughout the scene as Dante plugs the officers full of bullets as they slump down, dead to the zillionith degree. Soon, Dante drops the guns and then casually trots back to the car in a Resivior Dogs type of fashion as he shuts the door behind him. Statuz Quo turns to him as Dante reaches into his pocket, starring at the piece of gold. 'You know what this is? A piece of the Tag Team titles. I must gain all the pieces before the others do.' Quo winds up, before slapping the taste out of Dante's mouth, sending him back into a reflex notion, only his head swaying gracefully towards the right. 'You and I both know, that you just pulled off some shit that's not even realistic, and yet yo dumb ass don' passed it as if it was. Motherfucka's need to stop watching The Matrix or them Anime shows that come on Cartoon Network, giving bitches too many ideas.' 'Yeah,' the smile on the Lyrical Messiah's face is from ear to ear now as the car pulls into the lane, pushing hte dead cow out of the way as they continue driving, farther and farther to New Hampshire as the road can take them.
