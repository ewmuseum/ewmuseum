The New Saga: Sorrow. (Prelude)

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-5-02 'There will be...' DarkStar tapped his fingers on the steering wheel of his '67 Corvette Stingray as if he was listning to music, although nothing was playing on the radio. He looked to the left and the right of him, a sick, twisted grin on his face. Wordlessly he puts his hand over Talena's, gripping it tight. 'Yeah... there will be...' The scenery sped by poetically, barely slow enough to tell trees from deer, signs from people, cars from... not cars. It was like the blur of a life that DarkStar has lead recently. Life hasn't been the same since he was forced to leave PCW all those months ago. It's been so long since he was deemed unneeded in the league that he bled for. Hypocrites, it is his time now. As much as he wishes he wasn't here, it is all him. Him and Talena had to sit through so much, so many problems, things they didn't have to deal with before he was looked over. Before he was expelled from doing the thing he loved. Before he was removed by Duke Williams on a whim, because he and Clyde were useless to them. Now how do they feel? Everything that has happened in the last few months has came back and bit them directly in their ass. Good and evil. No. This isn't good and evil. This is pride. This is revenge. This... This is Sorrow. This is the receipt for all the pain that was caused. 'Oh there will be...' Things change. DarkStar turns to Talena at a red light, she is half assleep, curled up in a ball like a innocent child. She is everything but. The blood has permanently stained her hands, as it has DarkStars. There is no returning from where they came from. DarkStar is a demi-god. She is his Pagan Princess. Thats how it works, and that's how it will stay for the rest of eternity. 'God I love her', he says quitely to himself, to not disturb the silent sanctuary that is currently the inside of this ton of metal. Safety. 'We can never look back my love,' he ran his hand down the back of her head, carressing her black and red hair, 'but at least I can always look forward with you... and to dismantling the people that caused our unemployment.' He spits at the mere mention of Duke Williams. He then wipes his window off muttering curse words. Probably should have opened that first. 'Sometimes I curse the day that I was born... but not today. I actually get to exact revenge next week on Turmoil.' The backwater sign for the Tad Smith Coliseum appears on the right of the highway. The lights blink to form the words 'PCW Fusion, Tonight'. DarkStar snorts loudly, pulling into the employee parking lot for the first time in months. 'Or maybe... I'll just exact my revenge right here.' 'Yeah there will be... Sorrow... No more...... End.
