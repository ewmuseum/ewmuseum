Date: not entered Knock, knock, knock! We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he opens the door to his luxurious home, Stately Steele Manor. His toga ruffles magnificently in the spring breeze, as he takes a slow bite from a monster-sized sandwich. A pair of men in tweed jackets stare at him from the doorstep with dead eyes. One with a pipe clamped between his teeth steps forward and smiles a cold and heartless smile. Man: “Hello, Mr. Steele. We were hoping you could spare us a few moments of your time.” Steele stares at the men, slowly chewing his sandwich. The second man clears his throat and runs a hand through his hair. Second Man: “Uh, we are doctors of psychology. We were told you were informed of our arrival?” Staring and chewing. The original man steps under the awning to shield his bald head from the glaring sun. Original Man: “We are psychiatrists from PCW…” Big Alan Steele: “Ah! There you are! The glare from your unprotected dome sent me into a tumultuous world, wherein I heard disembodied voices of gods claiming to be of PCW! Now, how can I help you?” The two men exchange worried glance before the second also comes closer. Original Man: “I’m Doctor Mendalin and this is my assistant, Dr. Weinrich. PCW management has sent us here to determine… erhem… determine if you are fit.” BAS: (grinning and flexing) “But, of course I am fit!! Just take a gander at my multiplicidous pulchritude!!” More concerned looks between the two doctors. Dr. Mendalin takes a long hit from his pipe before putting it out, waiting on the doorstep. Big Al just keeps on flexing for a few moments. Mendalin: “No, we meant your psychological wellness.” BAS: (frowning, ceasing flexing) “Oh.” Weinrich: “It’s really no big thing or anything, just a few simple questions and all so we can report back to our supervisors.” Another pregnant pause. The men straighten their Oxford ties uncomfortably. Mendalin: “May we come in?” BAS: (beaming) “But of course! Come and let us enjoy a repast whilst taking refuge from the elements. Sunlight dulls the senses…” Steele turns on his heel and enters his abode, leaving the men to shrug and step inside after him. Inside, Big Al leads them to his study, a favorite place for conversation. Steele grins at the men and drops himself into his favorite easy chair, the psychiatrists standing in the center of the lavishly decorated room. BAS: “Please! Ease yourselves into my plush confines!” Weinrich: (to Mendalin) “Is he coming on to us?” The other doctor hushes up his assistant before gesturing to a long leather couch near the crackling fireplace. Mendalin: (clearing throat) “Perhaps it would be better if YOU got more comfortable, in order to ease away any tensions or inhibitions that may hinder our little… talk.” BAS: (beaming) “An excellent idea!” Steele stands and flops himself onto the couch, the men dragging chairs over to better initiate their ‘talk.’ Mendalin: “Comfy? Good. Now, I just wanted to intimate to you that PCW is quite pleased with your work of late, and that your job is not in danger from today’s session, as stupid Californian law won’t allow it. No, today is about us making only recommendations to PCW on your behalf.” BAS: “Your words confound and confuse. How would lively debate enter into my Olympian wrestling pursuits?” Weinrich: (steepling hands) “You see, Mr. Steele, you have been granted a World Title match come Sunday…” BAS: (leaping up, dramatic pose) “InDEED, and I, Big Alan Steele, shall not fail in my attempt to once again bring glory to the gods on high…” Mendalin: (interrupting) “Yes, you have been granted a chance at the title. But the people higher up in PCW are concerned with specific aspects of your peculiar personality, which may make you a… undesirable champion.” BAS: (shocked) “Undesirable?!?! A man-god torn from Zeus’ thigh undesirable?! Inconceivable!!” Mendalin: “Yes, you see, wrestling is a profit-driven business, and those higher-ups wish that their champions possess the qualities that best benefit our company, hence our presence here.” BAS: (growling angrily) “And what if I should just TAKE said title…” Mendalin: (nonchalantly) “Oh, the usual. Interferences, unfortunate accidents, poisonings, firing squads… all the usual methods all clearly outlined in your contract. But we aren’t lawyers. No, we are merely psychiatrists, hoping to help you help us help you.” Weinrich: “Yes. Help us help you help us to help you.” BAS: (finally sitting) “I believe I see.” The two men exchange looks that say, ‘I can’t believe that worked.’ Pens and pads are produced and Steele lies back into the cushy leather as the men hover over his prone body. Mendalin: “Comfy?” BAS: “Indeed.” Mendalin: “Good. Now, management has some concerns mostly about your **cough cough** alternative lifestyle choices.” BAS: “My life of crime-fighting?” Weinrich: “No.” BAS: “My dedication to long-forgotten of an enlightened era?” Mendalin: “No. I’ll be blunt. You wear a dress and haven’t been seen with a woman since your arrival to PCW.” BAS: “Preposterous, for tis a toga!! In addition, I am surrounded by women muses on a minute by minute basis! Surely you jest!!” Weinrich: “But we haven’t SEEN you with a woman, if you get me. You have never been linked romantically to a female.” BAS: (snorting) “But of course not! Why would one such as I engage in such a fruitless endeavor?” Mendalin: “And that is precisely what PCW would like to know, Mr. Steele. Why indeed? You see, PCW caters to specific demographics. That target population is usually aged 13-29, male, very hormonally active, and has to relate to its champion. Also, the females that do follow tend to follow based upon selective traits proven year after year by research.” Weinrich: “The chicks have to dig you.” Mendalin: “They must want to have sexual relations with you, to be more precise. And we are here to figure out why you throw out such… asexual overtones.” Weinrich: “Yeah, like, what’s up not liking women?” BAS: “Oh contraire, yon gentlemen. I LOVE women!” Mendalin: “But you aren’t accessible to women. They don’t think of you as a ‘conqueror,’ but as more of a platonic friend that will keep them safe and warm.” Big Al pauses and scratches his beard, the men smiling at their efforts. BAS: “I do not understand. Would that not be a good thing?” Mendalin: “Let’s try the direct route… are you homosexual?” BAS: (confused) “Eh?” Weinrich: “GAY!!” BAS: “But of course I am! I am quite pleased to be battling the false prophet in order to assume PCW dominance…” Mendalin: (sighing) “No, no, no… we mean… well, do you get a… rise out of women?” BAS: “Rise? Your vernacular riddles me so…” Mendalin: “You know…” The Doctor makes a finger pointed downward slowly turn up to a no. 1. BAS: “I don’t get it.” Mendalin: (exasperated) “Dr. Weinrich… the photos.” Doctor Weinrich grumbles and reaches into a briefcase, pulling out photo after photo of the filthiest porn involving the most gorgeous porn stars ever. BAS: “She looks cold! … Besleeve yourself, strumpet! … By the gods! She must be crying out in pain, the poor woman! … Cindi?!?!” Mendalin: “Anything? Even a little bit there, hmmm? Any pent up… urges?” BAS: “A little what?! Stop twisting my brain with your confounded reasoning!!” The Doctor gain makes a finger pointed downward slowly turn up to a no. 1, then wiggles it for effect. Big Alan Steele roars in rage, standing and tipping over the couch. The two psychiatrists cower, unsure of what to do as the Greek man-god rears up to his full impressive stature. BAS: “REMOVE THYSELF from my presence, or face the wrath of Big Alan Steele!! I shall stand for your puzzling insinuations no longer!! Hi-Keeba!!” Mendalin: (grasping a concept) “Ah! I see it now! It’s all so clear! He is a victim of sexual repression!” Weinrich: “Excellent observation, doctor!” Steele pauses, not used to people reacting to his rages with such scientific probing. He scratches his head, looking on in distaste. Mendalin: “So simple! Mr. Steele surprises his urges, tamping them down within his body into a great, seething, roiling ball of lust.” BAS: “I do?” Mendalin: “YES!! So you try to channel that energy into other activities, based upon your lack of past success with women! You expend your sexual desires through physical activities, such as wrestling! It lends you great strength!! And that is probably why he keeps so many women around, to lend him strength… sexual strength!!” Weinrich: “I’m still not sure the fed heads will appreciate his using sexual tension in order to grapple with other, big sweaty guys.” Mendalin: “But at least he isn’t flaming, like previously thought!” BAS: (holding head) “Your words! My head aches from their verbosity!” Mendalin: “This is good news! But I still have one concern… do you ever, and I mean EVER, you know… think about women?” BAS: “But of course…” Mendalin: “And… get a rise? You know, nudge, nudge, wink, wink…” No. 1. BAS: (roaring) “GET OUT before I SHOW you out!!! NOW!!!” The psychiatrists squeal in fright and hurriedly grab their things, chased out by Steele, who SLAMS the door behind them. Frowning, he turns and walks back to the kitchen… The door opens a tiny sliver, Dr. Mendalin sticking hi s head partially in. Mendalin: “How about in the mornings? Anything? Even a little?” Steele bounds over and kicks the door shut, and loud ‘Ow!’ sounding on the other side. Steele shakes his head and begins to walk away. BAS: “Sometimes I wonder if it is the psychiatrists who truly need the help… Hi-Keeba!”
