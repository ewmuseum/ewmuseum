Title: Why? Featuring: Phoenix Date: 16 Dec 03 Location: Phoenix's home - journal entry The sound of running water filtered through from through the slightly open door in the north wall of the room, it’s gentle pattering echoing faintly as if a spring downpour was falling somewhere close. Muted light came through the gap between the plain white door and the wooden frame around it, the yellow glow diffused slightly by the plumes of steam that gently wafted in like banks of fog rolling in from the sea. The light from the next room, combined with the natural white light coming in from the almost full moon that can be glimpsed through the partially screened large windows that take up one wall, show the room. Sparsely furnished, and minimal in it’s direction, the room is no more than twenty square feet in size, with a closed door in one wall, a large leather couch alongside it, the slightly opened one in another. A third wall is almost completely taken up by two windows that stretch their reach from the ground to the ceiling, standing on either side of a set of patio doors that face out into the world outside. Diaphanous white drapes hang down across the windows and the door way, filtering the light that comes in through them, and illuminating small particles of dust that seem to bask in the spotlight they bring, weaving amongst each other in their eternal dance. The last wall is flat and broken only by a desk that sits squarely against it, a small green lamp, reminiscent of those that grace libraries around the world, sitting to the left edge. A comfortable looking black leather chair is pulled back slightly from the desk, depressions in it’s surface evidence of recent occupation. Above the desk, taking obvious pride of place is an ancient, but still mighty looking, bow, a ream of arrows beside it. Resting on shelves are golden title belts, trophies and awards … accolades from around the world, the one thing that they have in common is the word that crops up most of all upon their surface. Wrestling. With a rattle and then a click the patio doors suddenly open, the drapes blowing up and billowing out to almost envelop the room within a tender embrace as the wind rushes in, dying down almost as it begun, the drapes coming to rest again, their slight motion the only sign that the breeze is still coming in through the double doors. ... the same breeze that gently moves the pages of a now open book on the desk, a book that was forced open by the wind that moments before filled the room. As the pages flutter, vainly trying to turn themselves over but failing as the wind is no longer strong enough, words can be made out on the paper’s surface. … why am I doing this? Am I really going to throw myself back into a situation that I think … no I KNOW will bring me suffering and pain, both physical and mental? I remember my Grandfather telling me that it is only through the experiences gained in the journey of life that we learn anything, that we are able to make conscious decisions instead of simply reacting. Well, if that is true then I must be a slow learner, because I have experienced this before … twice … and yet I am still thinking about putting myself through this. Who am I trying to kid? I am not thinking about this any longer, I have decided, and all I am doing now is trying to make sense of that decision. … why go back to the PCW? I mean I have wrestled there before, right after getting to the finals of the first Global Title Tournament. That tournament … or to get closer to the truth the indignity that the so called winner Vampir Nosferatu put me through as he cheated his way to a title that should have been mine … was the turning point in my career that led to the PCW. Yes the invitation to go there was a heady experience, I mean who wouldn’t feel good if they were asked to join such a prestigious federation … but the chance to get my hands on Vampir, to face him in the ring, and to prove to him … to the World … to myself … that the way he beat me, by cheating, was the ONLY way he COULD beat me … THAT was the real reason for joining. I remember how good it felt when I turned up unannounced at the Mall Brawl event and took Nosferatu out of the competition, but it was to be a short lived feeling, as right after that my life became more complicated. The Phantom Booker took an interest. His ‘boy’, Vampir, had to be protected, and that meant ensuring that I never got anywhere near him again, in fact it meant making sure that my life was miserable while I wrestled for PCW. Flight information was wrong … hire cars not booked … even details about promotional visits were kept to me. … and when I was booked to wrestle, it was made sure that I didn’t get the information. I missed matches, I missed fan visits, I missed flights … and all the time, the Phantom Booker laughed in the back. At the end of the day I realised after a few months that I would not get anywhere in the PCW, I would not get my revenge on Vampir, so with a heavy heart and my head low, I did something I have never done before. I quit. … I still look back on that as one of my darkest times. Losing I can handle, getting beaten by the better man is something that I have faced before, even injury is something that I can understand and work through. … but I quit, I turned my back on something and walked away. … and then did it again a couple of years later in the PWC. Once again my path crossed that of the Phantom Booker when it was revealed that the owner of the PWC … Ron Starr … WAS the Phantom Booker, but by the time that this information was available it was too late. I had signed my name on the dotted line. What a mistake that was … right away Starr took delight in ensuring that while I was there I was his to abuse. He knew that my knee was injured, and sent his goon, Romulus, to ensure that it stayed that way. He sent Romulus to take me out of wrestling if possible, but to hurt me, embarrass me, and make me looks stupid for definite. … he failed. I did the job myself! I allowed Starr … through Romulus … to once again control me, and call the shots … I allowed myself to react to his machinations instead of playing the game my won way. … and when frustration became too great, I quit again. Twice I have allowed myself to feel despair great enough that I would walk away from something, and both times Ron Starr has been involved. … so why am I risking it for a third time? The fact that Starr is involved in the resurrection of PCW is one of the main reasons for my decision … I have a chance at something here that I didn’t even realise was still haunting me, plaguing me. I have had success … great success … since leaving the PCW and PWC. I have won titles, earned praise, grabbed accolades … but in the back of my mind there was doubt. Was I living a lie, or was I as good as I believed myself to be … as good as I told people I was. Was it false pride and vainglorious boasting, or the truth? When I heard the news that PCW was to reopen I was slightly intrigued … but when I heard that Starr was involved I was sure of my path. So, by this time tomorrow night I will be in Atlantic City, and I will be once again in PCW … and once again subjecting myself to Starr. So … why AM I doing this? Simple … because this time it is not up to him to decide my fate, it is up to me! He can put me through Hell and back, he can throw his army at me, he can do what he wants to … and fail! This time I do not quit, this time I do not walk away. This time I believe in myself, and my own abilities, I play the game MY way. … and THAT is a game that Starr does not understand, a game that he can not win. I will banish the doubts of the past, I will regain my path in life, and I will achieve that which I feel I need to … why am I doing this … …redemption.” A gust of wind once again blows strongly into the room through the open doors, the pages of the book fluttering madly before the cover is slammed shut, as we fade to black. ---- View Phoenix's Biography
