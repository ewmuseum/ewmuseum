Which Wrestler Is Selling Drugs To Eddy's Sister?

undated

Date: Read and find out Eddy, back in America and joyous over his win over Mark Makonie, even if the ref counted fast when Makonie pinned Eddy and slow when Eddy pinned Makonie, he managed to land his devastating 'The Apex' finisher and win. In happiness, he drove to Dallas to tell his sister of his redebut and drove many miles when he finally arrived at her house, joyous to tell her of the big news. Eddy noticed her house and pulled his red Durango near, and stepped out and looked around. He went up to the wooden door and knocked. 'Knock, knock, knock.' This proved to be pointless because there was no answer. He rang the doorbell, but nobody answered. Then, Eddy heard voices from the backyard. He heard what sounded like his sister’s voice, and a strange Italian voice he’d never heard before. Eddy, confused, decided to check it out. He walked through the grass green yard and walked up to the wooden brown fence, quietly. He had a peephole to look through, so he put his left eye in and looked around. His sister had a blue blouse and a black miniskirt. She was facing an Italian man, suited well, with a tux and a tie. He had a briefcase in his hand talking to her. 'How much do you have?', his sister questioned. 'I got an ounce here.' 'How much?', his sister asked back Eddy heard some price he couldn’t understand. 'That’s a good deal…', said his sister. 'I’ll take it', after this, pulling out some money of her pocket. 'Can I write a check?', his sister asked. 'Yeah. Pay to the order of Bob Vurdo.' 'Bob Vurdo?! Isn’t he in PCW?', Eddy thought. 'OK…', said his sister, puling the checkbook out. The Italian man crossed his hands and stood there, waiting. After his sister finished writing it out, she pulled it out and handed it to him. He pulled out a bag of marijuana, and handed it to her. 'Enjoy smoking.' 'I will. What’s your name?' 'Frankie Robertso. I work for Bob Vurdo of PCW.' 'PCW? My brother works there.' 'What’s his name?' 'Eddy Ramirez.' ‘I ain’t heard of him. Anyway, I’ll see you.' 'Bye', his sister said, tacking the bag into the house as the Italian man lifted his briefcase and started walked through the backgate out. Eddy, however, didn’t trying to stop him. He had to get to Honolulu to stop Bob Vurdo from selling anymore drugs to his sister. He was mad, but drove off to the airport once again.
