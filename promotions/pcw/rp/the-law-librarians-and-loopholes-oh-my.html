The law, librarians and loopholes.... Oh my!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in a large library.Thousands upon thousands of books line the shelves of this dusty, old library which is crammed full of people with nothing better to do. Sitting at a desk is Flaymin Aymin.He’s surrounded by a wall of Legal Books and Journals. He flips through one book and attempts to make some sense of its content. However, unable to understand all the technical jargon, he just slams the book on the desk and buries his head in his hands. Hearing the noise of books being slammed, the librarian looks up at Flaymin.She lowers her glasses to take a better look at him, before deciding to put down the books currently in her hand and approach him. The librarian walks over to Flaymin and taps him on the shoulder. Flaymin raises his head and looks up at the librarian] Flaymin: Yeah? Librarian: You shouldn’t be slamming books like that- you’ll damage the spine. [Flaymin simply shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Eh, you can get it a wheelchair. [The confused librarian shakes her head in bewilderment] Librarian: Is there anything I can help you with? [Flaymin points at the dozens of books scattered around his table] Flaymin: You could read these for me and then provide me with a book report. Librarian: Young man, you have to show some initiative! [Flaymin picks up one of the heavier books- a dusty old legal textbook with over 1000 pages] Flaymin: Do you have this on DVD? Librarian: No, I don’t recall ‘Hennessey’s Legal Framework Theory’ being adapted into a movie. Flaymin: Oh.How about the soundtrack, then? Librarian: Nope.The only way you’re going to learn about that book is the old fashioned way. Flaymin: Bummer.Well do you have the children’s version of this book?You know, with large letters, and pictures of talking teddy bears and evil witches? [Starting to question the sanity of Flaymin, the librarian stares at Flaymin with a look of concern] Librarian: What is your problem exactly? Flaymin: I’m looking for a loophole and I can’t find anything.Hell, I even went through the ‘2003 Scouts Guide to Knot Tying’ and there was nothing about loopholes in there either. Librarian: If I may ask, what is your interest in learning about loopholes? Flaymin: I need to find a way to legally wrestle my arch enemy- but I can’t because he has this restraining order against me. Librarian: Arch… enemy? Flaymin: Yeah, that’s right.He’s this little Mexican guy that wears face paint and shrieks like a woman. Librarian: I see….On an unrelated note, do you happen to receive any psychological treatment…. for your condition? Flaymin: No, but El Diablo is currently locked up in a mental hospital! Librarian: El Diablo? Flaymin: My arch enemy. Librarian: Oh, yes… right….him. [The librarian looks over the books on Flaymin’s table and then strokes her chin] Librarian: You see the thing is, I don’t think we have anything here to help your condition. Flaymin: What condition?!?I just want to find a loophole! Librarian: Have you ever thought about hiring a lawyer? Flaymin: Yeah, but she turned out to be a stripper. Librarian: My, my, what is the world coming too. Flaymin: I know.She charged me double for a lap dance. Librarian: Hmmm…I think I know what might help. [Flaymin looks at the librarian with interest.The librarian picks up a small book from Flaymin’s desk. She flips it open and points out a particular page to Flaymin] Flaymin: What’s this? Librarian: This should get you going.It has all sorts of information about how to void contracts and terminate restraining orders. Flaymin: Nice.How do I use it? Librarian: Uh… I think you should start by reading it. Flaymin: Do you have it on DVD? Librarian: No. Flaymin: How about the soundtrack? Librarian: Haven’t we already had this conversation? Flaymin: Yeah, I was just trying to fill up some more space. [Flaymin looks up and winks at the invisible camera] Librarian: What? [The librarian turns around and looks up in the direction that Flaymin winked, but sees nothing] Librarian: Well I have to go and put some books back on their shelves.I hope this book helps you find a loophole to take on your face-painting Mexican friend. Flaymin: Thanks for your help.I think I can see the light now. Librarian: That’s great to hear. [Flaymin flicks the ‘On’ switch of the lamp planted on his desk] Flaymin: No, I really do mean that I can now see the light! [The Librarian sighs and walks away as Flaymin continues to play with the lamp and the scene fades to black]
