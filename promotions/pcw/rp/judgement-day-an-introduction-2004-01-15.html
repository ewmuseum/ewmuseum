Judgement Day. An Introduction.

15 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th Jan 04. The White LDV Van screeches scratching the surface of the parking lot. The scruffy bumper scrapes along the cemented floor making a vile noise that is similar to the human finger nail being dragged down the surface of a blackboard. It halts. The rusty old passenger seat door opens. Very expensive designer Italian shoes hit the pavement with a sophisticated tap. The camera creeps up to see a very suave light grey, almost silver pair of trousers creased immaculately down the front. The trousers are short in length but big in width; perfect for a stout and little man. With his trousers he wears a matching suit. His plain and white shirt does little to hide his huge belly. The shirt has the logo of the Christian faith; a crucifix in navy blue embroidery on the left breast pocket with “E.S.” in elegant calligraphy written underneath. His tie is the same shade of grey as his trousers and jacket with very fine navy blue lines going across on a horizontal slant all the way down. His collar is done-up, but on the verge of bursting open due to the fat neck of this man. His many chins lie on top of the collar. His face is flabby, ugly and resembles that of a bulldog. He is virtually bald, with a horseshoe round the back and side of dull grey hair. It is “The Reverend” Eric Sagar. One of the most definitive manager’s in the industry. And who wouldn’t be. When he has a monster like Propaganda under his power. The driver’s door opens. The mammoth that is Propaganda steps out. His huge size 18 boots slam against the floor. He is wearing his usual wrestling attire, his white boots with a black toe and heel, his black wrestling tights with the plain white crucifix on his left leg and in block white capitals “PROPAGANDA” descending down his right leg. His white shirt is tucked into his tights but the sleeves are ripped to shreds around Propaganda’s bulging but hairy biceps. Over his shirt he wears a black tie. Propaganda’s face is a block of concrete, it’s shaped sort of like a cube and it is constantly the host to a cold, emotionless frown. He has wet hair with an orthodox parting in the middle. Propaganda is huge. He is a massive seven foot tall and of a rather stocky build. Some may say 'he is built like a brick shit house.' However neither Propaganda or Eric Sagar would ever think of such a vile, satan promoting word escaping their lips. Propaganda walks round to Eric’s side. They stand next to each other and start to walk. For every one giant sized stride Propaganda takes lifting his massive feet then throwing them across the floor Eric Sagar takes three small steps. The size difference between the two is huge. Eric Sagar who’s only just five foot and Propaganda who is an oustanding seven foot. After a few seconds. The silence is broken by Eric Sagar's husky voice. Eric Sagar: We’re here, brother Propaganda Eric Sagar’s points to the air to reinforce his point. Propaganda nods. Eric Sagar: PCW wanted a good opening. PCW wanted to spread around its tangled webs of sin and wrong-doings without any trouble or opposition. It never expected us. We will control it, brother Propaganda! Eric Sagar pauses and stops walking. Propaganda stops with him Eric Sagar: We already do, brother Propaganda. All we need to do is let them know then… “The Reverend” Eric Sagar claps his hands. Eric Sagar: We shall bring it to it's judgement day. Propaganda: We are it's judgement day! Eric Sagar: Very true. We are messengers from the lord…. …they continue walking… Eric Sagar: …who bring his unforgiving wrath with us. We shalt force his anger upon them. My mind and your sheer, intense power. We will have this organisation of sleaze begging for mercy. Propaganda: Our lord shall be proud. Eric Sagar: You speak the tongue of truth. They continue walking, there is a silence for a few minutes. Eric Sagar: Look at the sort of scum bags they employ. Inferno Blitz, or what ever he is called. (Don't Worry Marq, deliberate mistake) Propaganda: Bah! Eric Sagar: Indeed, just another jumping Jap that this sordid cesspool of an industry is filled with. Propaganda: He’s nothing new, I’ve crushed many of their sorts. Eric Sagar: I know, I just find his arrogance and ignorance towards the true way of life to be sickening. I will take pleasure in watching you draining force from his body with blow after blow of your divine fists. Beating him into a worthless pile of slant-eyed faeces! Propaganda: A-men! Eric Sagar: YOU SHALT CONDEMN HIM TO HELL! FORCE HIM TO FALL INTO DAMNATION! LEAVING THOSE OF US WHO ARE PURE AND GOOD NATURED TO LIVE FREE IN A WORLD OF CLEAN, HONEST HUMANS! Propaganda: Hallelujah! Eric Sagar is red in the face with rage due to barking incessantly. Eric Sagar: And the world champion! A CRIPPLE WHAT A JOKE! A supposedly world famous organisation has Skylar Green, nothing more than a sightless fool as it’s representative in this industry. Propaganda: He will be destroyed, brother Sagar! Eric Sagar: He is nothing more than a spastic, whose illness is a punishment from the Lord almighty. Propaganda: Praise the lord! The fat preacher gets a ghastly grin upon his hideously ugly face. Eric Sagar: But that is just the starter in this, God’s meal of punishment for Skylar Green, a sinner of this world. We are the main course! BRRRRRROOOOOOOTTTTTTHHHHEEERRR PPPPPRRRRRRROOOOOPPPPPAAAAAGGGGAAAAANNNNDDAAAAA! Sagar’s bellowing boldly echoes, bouncing back and forth. Eric Sagar: YOU SHALT DESTROY HIM AND SEND HIM TO HIS JUDGEMENT DAY! GOD HAS ORDERED IT! The Preacher is taking deep gasps of air now due to the exhaustion of this bellyaching. Eric Sagar: But… Eric Sagar pulls a face of disgust. Eric Sagar: …the General Manager… He twitches in repugnance. Eric Sagar: …The abhorrent Duke Williams. Propaganda: If only he was like his son. Eric Sagar: I very much agree, Brother Propaganda. This alcoholic’s life is just based around sin. He shall feel the wrath of the lord. They all shall… Eric Sagar stops. The camera pans into his face. Eric Sagar: PCW. We. Are. You’re. Judgement Day! And as soon he says “Day!” the camera shuts off with speed. Darkness.
