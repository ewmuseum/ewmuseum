Coping With Villainy - Part 1 - Dude, Where's Shai's House?

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 23, 2003 [Hero and Villain seem to go hand in hand if you really think about it. Without the villain, there would be no hero. If the villain didn't live by his or her diabolical ways then it would give no reason for the so called hero to even step up to stop the diabolical plan! Without evil, good would not exist. Without good, evil would not exist. The hero needs the villain and the villain needs the hero. Ying and Yang. There has to be a balance or else something totally bogus will happen. What is it? Nobody knows because the balance has never really been shifted at all. Or has it? Nobody really knows that for sure either. You know what, nobody really knows much of anything in this world. If your name is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy that holds very true, and ESPECIALLY if your name is 'Heavy Metal' Harry] [Carson Street, the main road of a small suburbian housing project in Southern California. The house all look exactly the same but with different colored siding. Trees are planted every ten feet. Everyone has a driveway. A mailbox at the foot of the driveway. A curved, stupid looking lamp post in front of the front yard. It's as if two houses got together and squeezed out a bunch of twins and put them in matching, but different colored, clothing. Quite bogus. Most people of intelligence would be able to make their way through the housing complex and find the destination. Now...if your name is Tommy, and your a surfer turned professional wrestler, the housing complex is a little more complicated then he thought it would be. The blond haired man with a ridiculous tan and pasted on smile, looks around the complex completely confused about where he is. He's looking a house with metal siding, a driveway, mailbox at the foot of the driveway, and a lamp post in front of the yard. And from Tommy's conclusion...] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, these house ALL look the same! [The surfing PCW Continental Champion turns to his trusty sidekick 'Heavy Metal' Harry, who has absolutely NO IDEA where he's at. His long brown hair is tied back in a pony tail, making his bloodshot eyes seem that much more suspicious. The stoned grin on his face tells it all once a person lays their eyes on him. The man is either extremely high or he's extremely retarded. And anybody that knows Harry will probably agree that it's a little bit of both] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy is battling the confusion and the horrible directions for a totally bodacious babe. A babe that Tommy considers his true love, but that's only to her face. Her name is Shailyn Higgins, and she's the good Christian girl that immediately stole Tommy's heart. To thank Shai, Tommy went and stole her virginity. Tommy feels that they are even now] TTT: What kind of bogus architect would build all the houses exactly the same? [Tommy is in his blue and yellow Armani suit, which is only saved for totally gnarly special occassions. Let's face it, dudes and dudettes, it is going to be a special occassion. Because this isn't Shailyns apartment, it's her parents house. That's right, the Tubular One is about to meet the parents. It's a most HEINOUS step in any type of relationship with a babe. Be it casual and friendly, or serious and long term. It's always a bother to meet parents when there is a totally bodacious babe at stake. Shailyn is what every dude wants in a babe. Smokin' body, flowing brown hair, a beautiful face, and an ass that anybody could bounce a quarter off of. Yeah, it's that firm! The Tubular One has been experiencing her 'loving ways' on a regular basis until a week ago when she brought up her parents. They begged to meet Tommy and wouldn't take the word 'No' for an answer. Tommy was most upset about this because he does have enough stress going on right now. Namely what The Tubular One and his bodacious bro are talking about...] TTT: Har, we don't need this heinousness! The Pay Per View is only right around the corner, dude. Rent-a-Jerkface is totally getting under my skin and I REALLY want to mess him up. I am just so BODACIOUSLY anxious for our match this Sunday so I can finally put him to rest and rid PCW of totally bogus heroes. Harry: In speaking of heroes, man....I got the munchies. TTT: Dude, I'm not even high and I have the munchies!! Harry: Grapes. Big juicy grapes, man. TTT: Dude, stop making me so hungry. I can barely concentrate enough with all of these totally heinous houses all looking the same. [Tommy stares at one of the off-white looking houses, having this radical sensation that this is the house that he must go to. That Shailyn, the bodacious babe that she is, is in that house whipping up a most gnarly feast. He walks up the cement pathway and to the white door equipped with doorbell] *Ding Ding* [Tommy waits for a few moments, a little impatient since he's starving. A bald man wearing clown makeup answers the door in his boxer shorts] Bald man with clown makeup: Yeah? TTT: Is Shailyn here? Bald man with clown makeup: No. But I can be her if you want me to. [Tommy's face immediately shows disgust] TTT: No way, dude. [Tommy quickly takes off down the path as Harry stands his ground, looking at the clown as if he's familiar or something] Harry: You know, you kind of look like one of the guys from Twisted Sister, man. Bald man with clown makeup: Like I told your friend, I can be anyone you want me to be. Harry: How about Tyra Banks, man? Bald man with clown makeup: Well...I could try but I'm no miracle worker. Harry: Meh...nevermind, man. [Harry walks away as the weird clown closes his door to go back to his horrible life. Tommy looks around at all of the houses and just shakes his head] TTT: Har, I guess that we're just going to have to knock on all of these doors until we finally find the right one. Harry: That could take forever, man!! TTT: But we must find our radical destination, dude. Harry: Yeah, man...before we frickin' starve to death. [As the two men begin their journey toward the home, a little girl's crying is heard in the distance. Tommy stops in his tracks as his baby blue eyes grow as wide as silver dollars and his chest sticks outward] TTT: It sounds like someone is in trouble, dude! Harry: Yeah, man. Lets eat. TTT: We must help them!! [Tommy springs into action without a second thought. Harry shrugs his shoulders and just walks slowly in the direction that his messed up brother is running in. Tommy runs up to the crying little girl, who looks like she has just lost her best friend in the whole wide world] TTT: What seems to be the problem, bodacious little girl? [She points up to the tree that they are standing underneath] Little girl: Mittens is stuck in the tree!!! [Tommy looks up and has a look of disappointment on his face] TTT: And that's it? Little girl: YES! He won't come down!! I WANT TO PLAY WITH MITTENS! [Tommy looks back up in the tree] TTT: That's pretty high up, little dudette. [She just sobs louder now. Tommy sighs and takes off his shining blue blazer] TTT: I guess this is a job for the most gnarly dude around, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. [Harry joins his brother finally as Tommy looks at him with a bright smile] TTT: Har, go up that tree and get Mittens. [Harry stares at his brother for a few moments and then up to the tree] Harry: Ok, man. [Harry begins slowly making his way up the tree. The keyword here is slowly, but at least he's making progress. Tommy gives his bro a thumbs up, and make sure that Harry's confidence is at an all time high. It's most important to serve ALL heinous problems if he's going to want to be a superhero. Even if it means getting bodacious kitty cats out of trees, then so be it] Little girl: How come your helping me? TTT: What's that supposed to mean, dudette? Little girl: Your the BAD GUY!! [Tommy seems taken back by the statement. But for some reason he feels as if a part of him is complete by being called a bad guy. He REALLY wants to be a superhero, make sure that he's the one and only true superhero of PCW, but there is something about the words 'Bad Guy'. Is he really a bad guy? He did stop the Tubular Tykes foundation to build a handful of water parks, that was kind of bogus. There was that whole thing with The End that seemed kind of bogus, but it wasn't THAT big of a deal. Wait a minute. It's all becoming clear now. The reason that the people boo him, and why everyone is always saying heinous things about him. It's the reason why that totally bogus dude, Rent-A-Jerkface, is going for Tommy's Continental title. Tommy is a SUPER VILLAIN!! He's a 'Totally Tubular' super villain] TTT: Hmmm...your right. Little Girl: Then why are you helping me? TTT: Harry... [Harry is about half way up the tree, hanging on for dear life] Harry: Yeah....man....? TTT: Kill the cat, dude. Harry: Ok.... Little Girl: NOOOO!!! [Harry spies the cat just hanging out in the try. Mittens kind of reminds him of that really far out cat Morris. That orange cat that sold all kinds of cat food. Yeah, that cat. Harry looks at the cat and reaches his hand out for it] Harry: Come here, Mittens. I only want to kill you, man. Mittens: HISS!! Harry: Chill out, man. Mittens: HISS!! Harry: Uh oh... [The little girl screams and cries as people begin coming out of their homes to check on what's happening. The green house that Tommy stands in front of holds a babe that has a striking resemblence to Shailyn. In fact, the babe standing in the doorway IS SHAILYN!!] TTT: BABE! Shailyn: What are you doing with my niece, Tommy? [Tommy looks down at the little girl, and then up at the tree] Shailyn's niece: He's trying to kill, Mittens!! [Tommy looks as if the little girl has no idea what she's talking about] TTT: Mittens? Kill mittens? Why would I do something so bogus like that? Little girl: Because your a super villain, stupid head. TTT: Your a stupid head. Little girl: No, you are! [Then the sounds of missing and rustling leaves are heard as Harry falls to the street] Harry: OOF!! [Everyone looks Harry's way] TTT: You all right, Har? [He doesn't answer, and just lies in the street twitching] Shailyn: We should call an ambulance!! Harry doesn't look too good. TTT: He'll be fine, bodacious babe! He's twitching so everything is cool. Shailyn: Oh. [The three begin walking into the house as the little girl sticks her tongue out at Tommy] Little girl: Stupid head. TTT: No way! Your the stupid head! Little girl: Am not! TTT: Are too!! Little girl: Am not! TTT: Are too!! Little girl: Are too! TTT: Are not!! HAHA!! [Tommy's face then drops in confusion] TTT: Wait... Little girl: See...told ya you were a super villain stupid head. TTT: Bogus. To be continued...
