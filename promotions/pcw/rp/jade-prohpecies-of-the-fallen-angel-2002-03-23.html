Jade: Prohpecies of the Fallen Angel

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-23-02 Chapter 1 He had read a long time ago about passages which stated that one day a man proclaiming to be the 'Fallen Angel' would arise and stake his claim by trying to make people believe the propaganda which seethed from his mouth. He needed to read some of these passages, he needed to know the truth. You see he now had to encounter 'The Fallen Angel' Drake Maxwell. He had never really paid that much attention to Maxwell until now, until he had become the slayer of sin and he would slay at any cost. The truth of Maxwell had consumed him so much that he had almost forgot about Laura, the woman he had met in the bar that faithful day. Soon he had thoughts of Laura, could it be he had actually found another mortal he cared about? Hogwash! Never! He wanted to call her, something nagging at him from deep down inside but soon he fought the urge off; if she wanted to talk she could call him, for now he had things to take care of. He had journeyed to an old library on the east side of town. The old library wasn't very appealing on the outside but on the inside it held a vast collection of knowledge. Knowledge which would accompany Jay in his goal of beating Maxwell and spilling his blood for imposing against the prophecies. After making his way through the old brass door, covered in age he stepped up to an old mahogany desk. An old man who's skin hung from his weary bones stood staring at the large Sylo. His eyes shifted to the old man's and seemed to be in a deadlock for many seconds. 'Could you point me to where I may find ancient scriptures?' Sylo's voice was heavy but yet quiet. 'In the back, the far shelf near the emergency exit,' The old man's voice was raspy but still had a soothing feeling to it. He only nodded to the old man as he made his way to the back passing books which held so much information that no possible man could ever learn it all. Finally, after making his way through the sea of fiction and other jargon, he found himself standing before a collection of very old books. By their appearance you would think by touching them they would simply turn to dust and blow away. Slowly he scanned the books until one caught his eye. On the spine of the book in large gold letters pressed upon the earth tone back ground were the words 'Ancient Prophecy'. He slowly reached forward pulling the book out, careful with his movements so he wouldn't harm this valuable book. This was his key to destroying Maxwell and he would be damned if he didn't do everything in his power to learn how to stop him. He opened the book, still very carefully and began to leaf threw the pages. He looked to the index and read aloud but still in a very low tone so not to bother others. 'The Antichrist, The End of the world, The scroll of war, ha! There it is, the prophecy of the fallen angel,' A smile had moved over his weary face as he read this. Slowly he turned to his destination and headed up in big, bold, letters were the words 'The fallen angel prophecies'. He was glad that it was all basic English and easy to understand. As his eyes bore down on the pages he began to read aloud in a whisper. It was written that when the sky turned to ashes and the world was still young an angle of god defied him, he looked for ways to rule heaven, to push god off his throne but every attempt was stopped by Macolies, the angel of punishment. Soon the fallen angel began to beg for mercy, he pled to god but god knew he would try his fiendish ways once more. A three day trial had been set in the court of heaven. The angels all gathered as God passed judgment that the angel would be banished to roam the earth, he would never die but those he grew attached too would eventually pass away. Death would never seek him, and he would roam the earth alone until the day of rapture and then, and ONLY then would god reconsider entrance through the heavenly gates. 'Amazing,' Sylo thought, astounded by what he read. What if Maxwell had been this 'fallen angel' the book had spoke of? He had to know more but the book seemed to be cut off, missing a page. Sylo franticly searched the old notes looking for the missing page, he searched the book shelf, he searched every where he could think of but nothing. He was angered. He felt a valuable note of scripture was now lost...how would he find the answer to Maxwell's power now? Chapter 2 What a day it had been. He finally thought the answer to his questions about Maxwell would have been answered but now he was left with even more questions. 'Could Maxwell really be the Fallen Angel?' His voice carried through the apartment. Sylo laid on an old leather couch staring up at the ceiling. Rolf who had just returned from a long vacation had already gotten back into the swing of things and now was laying passed out with a beer bottle in his hand. All diversions were gone, Sylo could no longer hide from his thoughts until the phone rang. 'Hello,' His voice sounded low and caught off guard. 'Jay? Is that you?' A feminine voice echoed through the phone. 'Laura?' Sylo asked, feeling a bit foolish knowing that it was Laura. 'Yeah! So you did remember me?' Of course! he thought but he would not say it. He could not reveal to this woman he barley knew that she had actually made a big impact on him in just one day. He was tougher than that and refused to give anyone that satisfaction. 'Yeah I remembered, so what's up?' Sylo asked getting to the point of the phone call. 'Well,' Her voice sounded like a steady summer rain, soothing and refreshing. 'I saw you on Television the other day. Ever since I met you I've sorta kept an eye on this whole wrestling thing. I noticed you have a match against someone by the name of Drake Maxwell,' 'Yeah....no pushover. I'm trying to get infor....' he stopped himself. 'What?' Her voice sounded intrigued. 'It's nothing really, actually quit foolish in ways,' Sylo replied trying to play it off. 'Try me,' her voice sounded stern so Sylo gave in and explained to her everything, down to him being the new age knight and the slayer of sin. They conversed for quit a long time. He felt a warmth from Laura but soon he would know something about her which would actually bring them closer. 'I have something to confess,' She said through the phone. 'Ok,' 'Not here, meet me in the park, we will talk there,' And with that she hung up. What could be this big news? He had no idea. Quickly he gathered a few things and left a note to Rolf letting him know where he was. With the note secure and all the lights off Sylo left heading to the park to find out what this news could be. Chapter 3 The lush smell of the park was a satisfying feeling. His senses tingled but in a good way as he took in the colors mixed together like an oil painting. Tree's covered a good portion of the park. On the far end were a few play items set up for children. Toward the large fountain in the center were a few park benches with even fewer people on it. He smiled when he saw Laura sitting on the bench. He walked over to her casually, still trying to play the strong man, but he seemed to melt in her presence, what was it about her that did this to him? 'What's up?' Jay said sitting next to Laura. She let out a deep sigh and continued to throw tiny seeds down toward pigeons which gathered to feed. She seemed more upset than the day they had met but he would not intervene...if she wanted him to know she would tell him. 'I couldn't tell you this over the phone....call me paranoid but you never know who's line is tapped. I haven't been fully honest with you...first off my name isn't Laura....it's Jade. I have been sent to help you with your journey's,' Her voice was quivering. 'But....how?' Sylo was so confused. 'In a counsel....your ancestors appointed me to be your companion so to speak,' Jade continued. 'So then your not human?' Sylo said still in shock. 'Yes and no. Yes I do carry a human body and no in the sense that I have lived in previous lives but my caring for you is sincere, please understand that. I have something for you...' She said reaching down in a purse. Oh great what now? She just dropped a bomb shell on him and now she wanted to give him something. He went along with it though but what she revealed changed everything. In her hand she held the missing page to the book. Sylo snatched it, trying not to seem to eager as he read in a whisper once more. And so when the masses cried out to put an end to the corrupt and fallen angel one man stood tall. The slayer of sin was predicted to strike the down with a great force ending the ways of the evil angel. With this slaying came the entrapment in hell but at the same time if this slayer wasn't careful, if his soul wasn't pure, he would be condemned. 'So Maxwell is the fallen angle and I now have the chance to slay him?' He said quietly looking to Jade. 'That is correct,' Jade's red lips pressed together as she stared up to him. 'And I am this 'slayer' the prophecies speak of?' 'That is also correct,' She said still staring into his eyes...she seemed to be the only one to ever do that. He stared down at the scrolls for a moment then back up to Jade. He slowly nodded his head while running his hand through his spiky ice blue hair. A groan moved from deep inside as he stared deep into the eyes of Jade. 'The I guess we should go, Maxwell awaits and if these prophecies are true I am the only one who can stop his wrath or at least slow it down,' She smiled and embraced him. This caught him off guard a great deal as he moved one of his massive arms around her, he wasn't sure what to do seeing as he hadn't been with anyone in years. She sat still hugging him as the pigeons flew off into the mid-day sun.
