Jail... the final frontier...

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 18, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up on an unfamiliar street location. Lining the street are several buildings and it seems to be downtown in a major city as well. You notice one building that must be a courthouse next to it is a police station for the downtown precinct. The camera turns around however and sitting right in front of you is the local city jail, which seems to stand about three stories high and on a high base of steps, proving that it has about two more floors down below the street level as well. The camera scene seems to glide up the flight of stairs as he walks up to the broad building. As the camera edges closer, you read a sign that states “Indianapolis Corrections Facilities” in broad Arial letters. The scene approaches the door and the door opens, and it seems to glide forward, turning left, past the front desk and seemingly just going through the next door, which leads to a flight of steps downwards to the basement levels. The camera glides down the stairs and makes a turn to the right, where it goes through a gated area and into the cells area. The camera stops in front of the first cell to the left and turns towards it, where you see Semore Smoke sitting on a bed, looking out the barred window, which is on the ground level. The camera melts through the bars and into the cell, and approaches to Smoke’s face, which seems actually sad for once… Semore Smoke: What a week… {{{{Smoke turns and faces the far wall and just stares it with a blank stare, as he continues to talk to himself about his current situation…}}}} Semore Smoke: First, my HQ is busted, now I have no one to help me but two people who are in hiding anyways… then I’m arrested for different charges then they are and I have no one left out there, so no one can bail me out from my current situation. Then AJ Trinidad and Adam Stryker get the last laugh at me… but I doubt that’ll last long when Trinidad realizes that I’m the only way he’ll see Crespo alive again. God, and to top it all off, if I miss Spring Fling, then I might as well kiss my ass goodbye in PCW. Damn… {{{{A guard walks by the cell and then stops look through the bars at Smoke sitting on the bed…}}}} Guard: Hey, Mr. Smoke. {{{{Smoke turns to face the guard with the same blank stare on his face…}}}} Guard: I’m sorry that we’ve had to arrest you I know how much you wanted to wrestle this Sunday against your rivals. But if it’s any consolation, I ordered the Pay Per View just to watch you wrestle, and now I might as well not even watch since I know you’re not gonna be there. {{{{Smoke nods and smiles for the first time in the promo…}}}} Semore Smoke: Thanks I guess. Well, it’s not all that bad, I knew that something like this would happen sooner or later, just was a matter of time, I kind of hoped that Trinidad wouldn’t be stupid enough to call the cops on me and press charges, I mean, how stupid can you be to know realize that if you call the cops on someone who has someone or something that you want, that there’s a good chance that you’ll never find what you’re looking for. But dumb asses will be dumb asses. Nothing I can do about that. Any word on the PCW officials and if anyone is willing to bring up the bail to bail me out? {{{{The guard shakes his head, but he seems to be a bit confused about something…}}}} Guard: Why don’t you just bail yourself out? {{{{Smoke hangs his head down low…}}}} Semore Smoke: I’ve got my reasons for that, which I don’t want to discuss. I’ll only do that if I have to, but you would think, that there would be someone out there who would be willing to bail me out, a political prisoner! Guard: Why now Jeff Marx? {{{{Smoke looks up at the guard with an alert look on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: That gaywod? I wouldn’t call him if he were the last person on Earth. Do you know that he’s stalking me? He’s taking pictures of me everywhere I go, and he keeps trying to get in my hotel room so he can take pictures of me when I’m in the shower, I swear, I saw him outside my room window Monday! {{{{The guard just nods his head…}}}} Guard: That’s understandable sir. Well, I better get going, have to make sure everyone is still their cells. If you don’t wrestle Sunday, then at least I can say I met you. I’ll see you later. Semore Smoke: Did you just come onto me? {{{{The guard just walks away shaking his head as he holds off laughter. Smoke looks over at the next cell beside him and sees a guy who’s staring right at him with an angry stare…}}}} Semore Smoke: What the fuck do you think you’re looking at? {{{{The man just shakes his head at Smoke and stands up and walks towards Smoke’s cell at the bars, with them being the only thing separating this man and Semore Smoke…}}}} Man: I don’t know if you remember me or not… but you’re the one who put my ass in jail a couple of years ago. {{{{Smoke shakes his head and walks up to the man…}}}} Semore Smoke: You’re lucky I’m in jail and I have nothing better to do but talk to your ass. What the hell did I do to put you jail? Man: Well, you were just starting to wrestle you were still in that damn academy, what was the name of it? Semore Smoke: PWA, so what? {{{{The man nods his head as Smoke edges closer to the bars and is now standing within reaching distance of the man in front of him…}}}} Man: Well, remember this little murder that happened around you? {{{{Smoke thinks back for a second… then remembers and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes I do, what about it. Some guy got whacked around me… {{{{The man’s smile becomes even wider as he reaches through the bars with his quickness and grabs Smoke and pulls him near…}}}} Man: Thanks to your testimony, I was sent to jail for being an accessory, but thank god for the United States Appeal court system! I was able to get my ass out of jail. {{{{Smoke smiles at the man…}}}} Semore Smoke: So now you want to kick my ass eh? {{{{The man nods, but as he nods, Smoke grabs the mans left wrist, and breaks the man’s grasp of himself, and then reaches through and grabs the man by the neck, and turns him around, holding the man in a choke hold against the bars…}}}} Semore Smoke: I don’t think so my friend. I’m in a pissy fucking mood right now, and not even King Kong himself could defeat me. Now, you better retard that anger of yours or I won’t let you go. {{{{The man is shaking wildly trying to break free, but he can’t, so he says in a worried voice…}}}} Man: Ok, it’s retarded I’m retarded! {{{{Smoke lets go of the man and steps back laughing his head off at the man…}}}} Semore Smoke: You just said you were retarded Ha!!! {{{{The man kicks his bed and sits down on it holding his wrist and neck in pain…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, nurse those wounds… {{{{Smoke turns around and sits back down on his cot in the cell and looks back out the window…}}}} Semore Smoke: I’ve got a feeling that freedom is just around the corner… {{{{The scene fades on Smoke’s face, which looks hopeful now…}}}}
