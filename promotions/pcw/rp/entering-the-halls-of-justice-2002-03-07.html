Entering the Halls of Justice

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Entering the Halls of Justice Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 7th Two newcomers to PCW. For months, newcomers have entered....made a mark, and fled quickly, either quitting, or being hung in front of a cheering crowd of thousands. But, they all have one thing in common. They stayed only for a short amount of time. However, PCW's latest additions have arrived. And a short stay is not on their itinary. No, these two men are a tag team, a dying breed in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Their debut in PCW was simply CONTROVERSIAL, as their first victims, Corey Williams and Chris Chase would attest. But William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft are controversial men. They are dangerous men. They are powerful men. They are.........POLITICIANS! Yes, politicians. PCW is indeed under assault by the most dangerous force in the hsitory of wrestling. I mean, we all know what happens when POLITICS interjects its ugly head into an issue...all hell breaks loose. And so it is that Politically Incorrect prepare for their debut...they prepare to take on two of PCW's best. And they know it. Yet, they are supremely confident.. PCW, prepare to enter the halls of justice.....for the very first time... ::A mansion on the outskirts of Des Moines, Iowa stands tall among the surrounding corn fields. The glimmering white marble used in its construction is a sharp contrast against the ocean of green and gold it stands in. A long silver limousine pulls up to the front door of the palatial estate. A butler, standing at the door, quickly jogs over to the door of the automobile, opening it up. For the first time, we see 'Da Guvna', or, 'The Governor', as he would prefer to be called, Reginald V. Lovecraft outside of a PCW ring.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I say now! Please, make haste Katherine! I have an important appointment inside with Councilman Powell! ::Lovecraft offers a hand inside the limousine. As he retracts it, a lovely young woman steps out. Her long brown curls spill out over her shoulders as she walks hand in hand with Lovecraft into the mansion.:: Katherine Willis: Why are we here again? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Very simple. I have my first Primetime Championship Wrestling matchup on Friday. I must consult my tag team partner to see what his views are. Katherine Willis: And what will I be doing while this is going on? Reginald V. Lovecraft: You will be sitting right here like a good little intern should. ::Reginald motions for her to sit, emphasizing his point by patting the girl on the rear end. As she does so, Lovecraft takes off down a far hall.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I must say, my partner here has quite the nice estate. Yet, I wonder where he is.... ::From behind a wall, a powerful, booming voice rings out.:: William S. Powell: Come Reginald! Enter the halls of justice! ::Locating a nearby door, Lovecraft throws it open, revealing a long hall. Seats are on either side of the long aisle, outlined in red carpeting. At the end of this hall sits the other half of Politically Incorrect, William S. Powell. He is seated in a podium, high above the hall. There are two chairs besides him. In the chair to his left sits Roger Schultz, Powell's loyal campaign manager. Lovecraft slowly ascends the steps to the dais where Powell sits, and slips into the chair to his right.:: William S. Powell: Good day Reginald. ::Powell extends his hand, as the two politicians exchange a handshake.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: So, what is it that we have to worry about this week? Roger Schultz: Your first tag team contest...you take on members of a splintering faction.....Bobby Stormbringer and DarkStar of Asylum. William S. Powell: Don't worry Roger...I know Mr. Stormbringer rather well.... ::The Master Debator smirks, recalling an incident in the Playboy Mansion match from a few weeks back.:: William S. Powell: Back in NTICW....I took the key to this great city of Des Moines, Iowa...and I hit that son of a bitch right in the back of the head with it. Why? Quite simple......he liked Aimz... ::Powell shudders:: William S. Powell: And then, up he went....only to come crashing down seconds later via Red Tape. I've already proven that I own the man..and it seems he has greater problems than I do right now. Reginald V. Lovecraft: What do you mean? William S. Powell: Stormbringer lacks decisiveness. With Kannon and Jeremy Howard battling for supremacy, he cannot decide which man to follow. So, he attempts to placate both. He is a lackey. As he always shall be. With his mind diverted, his attention is nowhere near Friday Fusion on, well, Friday. And then, he shall be easy prey for us. ::Lovecraft smiles.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Very well then....... ::Suddenly, the feed is cut off. A notice appears on the screen.:: Note: This Politically Incorrect promo has been cut off due to lack of funding. Any complaints can be sent to 25 School Street, Lexington Massachussets. ::Somewhere, Lovecraft and Powell are cursing, as we fade to black.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
