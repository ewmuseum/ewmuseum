Waiting for a ride...

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 June 2002 Control... Many people say they have 'control' of their own lives, but do they really have total 'control'? How do they know the decisions they make every day will affect what happens in the world? Do they control what people say about them behind closed doors? No because no one can be every where at one time. No one can control every aspect of their life, some aspects of life just happen; no one can control it. So with all these facts summing up in mind, do you really believe you are in control of your own life? I am one of those people who believe that things happen and there is nothing we can do about it. But that is just 1 person's opinion on life and control, what we are really wondering is what the opinion of The Dark Messiahs is... Last time we saw The Dark Messiahs was in a dream like manner, when they came face to face with themselves! Yes you heard me correctly the twisted duo cam face to face with their 'other' personas. Strange to believe I know but once you grab the concept that it was all just a dream, you realise it isn't that strange at all. However after the dream questions still remain. Questions like did the message of the dream get through to Steel and Stone? Or have they already forgotten it? Hopefully we are about to find out the answers of these questions as we join Steel and Stone outside the 24-7 Motel waiting for their cab... The scene opens up outside the motel where Steel and Stone stand. It is a beautiful day and so both men are quite warm in their black T-shirts and blue jeans. In Stone's hand he holds the notepad we saw him use before at the so called 'doctors',' from this we can tell his throat still has not healed from that Akiyama attack at last week's Meltdown. The Motel car park is not that busy only a few cars obviously just there for a night, if you know what I mean, wink, wink nudge nudge? If you don't then tough, because I am not going to explain it to you. Anyway the motel itself is a real dump with the sign 'Motel 24-7' actually saying 'Moel 27.' This is definitely not a place you would take your family, only your mistress, get it? Mistress? No? Ah forget it. As a bird flies across the sky Steel moves over next to Stone and picks up his back pack... Daniel Steel: (Looks at watch) God that Taxi driver better hurry up or we're going to miss our flight, and I don't want to spend much longer in this place, it smells like Pretty Cool Guy's wrestling talent, shit! Stone tries to laugh but all he can manage is a small huff of air. He tries to cover up the attempt by quickly scribbling something down and handing it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) Yeah, and it is following the same path as their music career, it's falling apart. (Laughs) Your right there Mike, but in all serious I had this strangest dream last night, it really freaked me out. I mean it felt so real it sent shivers down my spine. Stone taps Steel on the shoulder nodding his head and handing him a note... Daniel Steel: (Reads out note) Me too, I could feel the place and all of it's surroundings, but what does it all mean? (Shakes head) I don't know, it was just a dream surely it couldn't have meant anything. Stone hands Steel another note with a disgruntled face... Daniel Steel: (Reads note) I disagree, I mean when I hit that other me in the face it felt so real. And what the other you said about 'releasing the rage.' It seemed to make a lot of sense. (Shakes head again) They are just dreams man, we shouldn't concentrate on them too much we need to concentrate on our up coming matches at Fusion and Gold and Glory. I mean if we put all our thought to 1 stupid dream we aren't going to get anywhere. People can go mad studying what every dream they had meant, that's why we just brush them off as dreams so we can get on with our lives. Now Fusion is our first priority, how you feeling? Stone scribbles something down, taking a while before handing it to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reads note) I feel good, I mean it is a real pain not being able to speak, but when has speaking ever affected a wrestling match. It's not like words are as powerful as a chokeslam or a body slam is it? I wouldn't worry I’ve got Fusion in the bag, and Gold and Glory even though a tough battle I am confident we have the ability to come out on top. (Laughs) You certainly are confident my friend, but I wouldn't be too confident as there is along hard battle awaiting us at Gold and Glory. But remember no matter how much they beat us down or hold us back we will get straight back up and back in their faces because no one and I mean no one is going to stop us from getting our hands on the tag team titles! Another note from Stone to Steel... Daniel Steel: (Reading note) But remember Daniel that without confidence we wouldn't be able to get up in the morning. We would have no self-will, we wouldn't be able to keep going if we didn't have confidence. Determination and confidence is what we need and it is what will get us those damn tag team titles. (Nods head) Determination is what we indeed have, and something that the other tag teams don't. Sure they can say they have got all the determination in the world, but that's all they can do 'say' it they can't show it. I mean we have shown it time and time again, we could have packed our bags after the embarrassing defeat against Akiyama but we didn't, we stayed on and we will fight on. We won't stop until we hold those tag team titles in our hands. They can beat us down and beat us down until our face is covered with blood, but we will get straight back up because of determination. Determination is what will take us to both Fusion and Gold and Glory! Akiyama calls us simple humans, and yes we are just humans, but we are humans with a mission. A mission of destruction! Yes we may lose at Fusion, yes we may lose at Gold and Glory, there is always that chance but we will never give up no matter what. The odds may be stacked against... Before Steel can finish he and Stone grab their heads in pain. Steel screams in pain whilst Stone's expression tells the story of his pain. Soon the pain begins to subside as Steel and Stone get back to their feet. The evil glint we have see all so often is back in their eyes. Steel looks around before speaking... Daniel Steel: (Evil) Finally we are free, finally those morons minds have subsided and allowed us to see the light. Pain and torment is coming to the PCW! It's time to set the damn record straight about who is the dominant tag team in the PCW! Soon you will all soon realise why The Dark Messiahs are called psychotic! Respect us and fear us, because even though within us a battle is waging between which persona will be dominant, we are still fighting for what is rightfully ours, the PCW tag team championships! Fusion, Gold and Glory, no matter what place or what time you better be ready for us, because once the pain starts there is no stopping it! We are The Dark Messiahs and we are your future PCW Tag Team Champions! And that isn't just an opinion that is a fact! As Steel finishes a Taxi pulls up in front of them and the Taxi driver sticks his head out... Taxi Driver: Excuse me, did you order a taxi? Steel bends down and faces the Taxi driver still with the evil glint in his eye... Daniel Steel: (Evil smile) Why yes we did order a taxi, thank you for bringing it to us. Taxi Driver: (Thinking) Wait a... Before the Taxi driver can finish, Steel has grabbed him from the taxi and pulled him out straight through the window. Steel throws the taxi driver to the floor, the taxi driver crawls away in freight. As Steel laughs evilly and signals for Stone to get in the taxi which he does. Before Steel gets in the car he looks at the taxi driver with an evil smile on his face... Daniel Steel: Now what I want you to do is go home, switch on your TV, do something useful with your money and order PCW PPV, Gold and Glory and watch us as we take those damn Tag Team titles! And you know why we are going to win? Because we can! With that Steel enters the taxi and slams the door shut. As the taxi speeds away the scene fades to black with the taxi driver looking on...
