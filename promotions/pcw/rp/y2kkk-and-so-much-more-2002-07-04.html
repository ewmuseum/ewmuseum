Y2KKK (and so much more)

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/02 Richard:'Richard doesn't know about this...' End:'Trust me, you'll do great.' Richard:'But Richard doesn't believe in what he's saying.' End:'Just go with it.' Richard:'... Is this safe?' End:'I assure you that I won't be hurt in any way. Now please, put on your costumes, get out there and make me proud!' In a Jewish neighborhood market, Slade is dressed in a long robe. He walks down the street, wearing the gay little moustache that Hitler ruined for everyone (not that it was a popular style beforehand, either). The End has forced speech lessons upon Slade, so he speaks coherently around this group of working class Jewish people. Slade talks to The End, who is in radio contact. Slade:'I can't believe I have to do this.' The End's voice comes back to him over an earpiece that he has in his left ear. End:'For my plan to work against Mark Makonie, I need to do some research on how ethnic minorities react to him. This is the only way to do this accurately. Slade steps out from the alley and takes off his jacket, revealing a 1940's Nazi Party military uniform. With a smile (though ordered by The End) on his face, he casually strolls down the street. All the people look at him in shock and horror. Slade feels uneasy, all the eyes on him that are simply flabbergasted that he would do such a terrible thing. Slade:'They're watching me. Feels weird and unsettling.' The End comes back over the radio. End:'Go to Phase 2.' Slade takes out a piece of paper from his pocket and reads it alound, standing upon a crate of oranges. Slade:'Bomb the Jews back to Israel,and bomb them there, too!Because God doesn't like no stinking Jews! Cut em' with a blade, give them AIDS,That's all that'll do!Because God doesn't like no stinking Jews! Steam, broil, baking,Anyway to cook them through,But only dogs are low enough to eat stinking Jews! I've said my peace, hold your tongueFor I've only spoke the truth, You'll know why when you dieThat God doesn't like no stinking Jews!' In the Mexican quarter, Richard is dressed as the American wrestler Hulk Hogan (you all know he'd be at the undercard in Mexico). He's got a boombox playing the tunes from old Hulk Hogan's Rock & Wrestling playing, and he's even got a pasted on moustache and goatee like Hogan. Upon his neck is a number of old Lucha Libre masks that Richard uses for snot-rags occassionally. The crowd around him is furious. He's disrespecting an entire tradition. Richard:'American wrestling is so much better, Mexican wrestling sucks!In America I'm as rich as a king, here I couldn't make $20 bucks! Slow and bulky, that's the wayThat we beat Mexicans everyday! All you do is carnival tricks,You make us real wrestlers sick! I've said my peace, hold your tongueFor I've only spoke the truth, Now quit your yapping, clean up my garden,And get back on the f*cking roof! Drek Redaxe is dressed in a KKK robe, with stilts underneath that raise his height to six feet even. He carries a bag of miniature Bibles and crosses, and a lighter. The End has dispatched Drek to the predominantly black section of the city. And to make things worse, these are the racist ghetto thug blacks that are so prominently featured on the news. Drek has been given a speech of his own, but it doesn't rhyme. No, Drek has been given a podium to speak at, surrounded by police guard. Drek speaks in front of the black-dominated city hall, with a crowd of demonstrators forming to protest and shout him down. Just as Slade got speech therapy, The End coached Drek out of his accent for this 'experiment.' Drek:'You people want welfare? Why, so you can sit at home using MY tax dollars to drink, smoke crack, and get pregnant? And look how many of you are out here on a Wednesday afternoon to protest me; get some f*cking jobs!' A tomato flies in Drek's direction. He sidesteps it and continues. Drek:'Look at you wasting food like that! If you were still out on the fields, picking my crops like you're supposed to, you'd be killed for something like that! Get one thing straight, people. YOU ARE ALL INFERIOR TO ME! And Jesus agrees! That's what you all really need. Jesus!' Drek lights a number of Bibles and crosses on fire and tosses them into the crowd. Lamar isn't dressed like anything but Lamar in his tight jeans and wife-beater shirt. He doesn't remember his lines, he doesn't know what he's doing in the Asian quarter. He's just lost. Lamar has stopped inside the Fortune Dragon for some food. His waitress comes to get his order. He takes one look at her face and says, 'You're ugly.' It's not so much an insult, just his childlike honesty/cruelty. Everything in the restaurant stops, everyone looking at Lamar with eyes and mouths wide open. Lamar looks over at the only other white man in the restaurant sitting next to him at the counter and asks what's wrong. The white man, tied up and beaten to a pulp, weakly says, 'You just told the daughter of the head of the local Yakuza that she's ugly.' Lamar:'... And?' Running... The sound of feet, running... He pants, tired from running so long. He isn't even running, just launched down the street stuck inside of a garbage can. An intersection in the center of the city leads all four Misfits into each other. Slade, Richard, and Drek were running (Drek ditched the sticks a while back. They haven't even had time to remove their respective costumes yet. Lamar meets the other four courtesy of being send down the road in a locked trash dumpster. Of course, that doesn't keep people from chasing him. In fact, everyone's being chased at the moment. Richard:'Damn, trapped.' Slade:'I am so hating The End for this right now.' Drek:'Couldn't we just go down different streets?' Richard:'No, they see us together.' Drek:'We'll I'm not ready to die. I've faced more people than this is a barbecue. Wait till they taste my axe.' Drek reaches behind his back for his axe, but it's not there. Drek:'Dammit! Why do I keep forgetting that thing?' Lamar:'Are we there yet?' The crowds of blacks, Asains, Mexicans, and Jews gets ever closer to the position of the Misfits. It seems like they are just inches away from the Misfits. Closer, closer the angry mobs of stereotyped minorities come to the Caucasian Misfits, whipped into so much of a stir that they would actually kill the Misfits. There seems to be a threshold passed for when the crowd starts throwing rocks and bottles at the Misfits. With bats, crowbars, and pipes with trashcan lids, the front of the respective mobs form skirmish lines. It seems that this is it. After a good couple of months run, the Misfits are going to die thanks to The End's stupid plans. Ironic, isn't it? Only within a hundred feet now, the mobs gather around the Misfits and pick up to a run as all the people bear down on them. Just before the crowd reaches the Misfits, Juju flies down in a helicopter, there to scoop them out of danger. They eagerly grab onto the ladder that The End tosses down to them and climb up as the chopper climbs back up to an altitude where it can't be reached. Juju:'Wonk!' All the Misfits breathe a sigh of relief as they come up into the passenger cabin. Richard moves up front and kisses Juju on the head. Richard:'Thank you, Juju! Thank you!' Richard turns to The End. Before he can do anything, though, End stops him with his words. End:'Don't touch me.' Richard sits down in the back without another word or gesture. Drek:'Let's hope that we never have to do that again.' The End speaks back to the Misfits. End:'You're done for today, anyway. We've done enough experimenting and gotten enough research data. We know for sure that when provoked in extreme ways, even people that may be otherwise peaceable become a bloodthirsty pack of animals.' Slade:'You didn't know that already?' The End shrugs. End:'Well, a little part of me just wanted to screw with you guys. Anyway, this now presents us with more information that can be used to bring my plan against Mark Makonie together.' Drek:'You keep saying that. Is that just to shut people up so they don't ask about the relevance of these promos to PCW?' End:'Exactly. They do have relevance, but they'll have to just wait and see how it plays out. And as for Mark Makonie, well, he should just watch his back.' Juju:'Wonk!' They all laugh for some strange unknown reason as Juju flies them home.
