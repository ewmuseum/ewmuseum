Training Days.

Date: not entered Riiiiiiiiiing! Riiiiiiiiiing! Riiiiiiiiiing! The rather conspicuous special hotline phone in Stately Steele Manor flashes red with each intruding ring. A small, black box next to the hotline kicks in, a small LED light flashing. “ (click!) Huzzah and greetings! You have reached the Big Alan Steele hotline, gentle sir or madam! Unfortunately, events at this time precede me from taking you call, which I cannot apologize enough for. However, should you choose, leave a message at the ‘Hi-Keeba!’ and truly I shall return your call, post-haste! And, as always, should this call be an emergency, please do not hesitate to dial 911!! Excelsior!” “Hi-Keeba! Beeeep!” At the other end of the phone, a terrified woman at the First Bank of San Jose slowly replaces her phone. “BWAHAHAHA!! Soon I shall have not only the entire wealth of San Jose, but the head of Big Alan Steele as well!!” Huge and muscular, standing seven feet tall is a… creature. Giant bull horns sprout from either side of his head. His skin, red and thick. Flames lick off his supernatural body, the heat searing the bank counter. His men gather around, all wearing those shiny flame retardant suits firemen wear, hustling about and corralling the terrified populace inside as the evil Minion throws back his head maniacal laughter. Jets of flame streak from his body, punctuating his mirth. “He’s not coming.” The laughing ceases. Minions blood-red eyes narrow as he leans in close on the teller. “What did you say?” growls the demon. “He’s not coming,” is the matter-of-fact reply. “What do you MEAN he’s not coming?! We went through all this trouble to find the flame suits for my men, plan out the bank heist, rent the cars, I had to find a suitable pet hotel for ‘Muffin’… AND YOU ARE TELLING ME HE’S NOT COMING?!?!” The teller shrugs, “I got the machine.” “Well, this bites.” “Sorry. Don’t know what to tell you…” A disconsolate Minion waves her off with a clawed hand, “Naw, it’s okay. It’s not YOUR fault. C’mon boys, let’s head back to the hideout.” “I really am sorry,” she replies, “But aren’t you going to finish the robbery?” She holds out satchels stuffed with cash, but Minion merely turns away, disgusted. “Keep it,” he grumbles. “Hey boss?! Don'cha think we could maybe, you know, grab some cash now…” The monster’s hand shoots out, wrapping itself around the man’s neck, squeezing through the flame suit. Glistening fangs snarl into the face shield. “It’s NOTHING without him, you understand?! Money is of no consequence to me!! You and your ilk shall be duly rewarded when the time comes!!” A flick of the wrist sends the man flying back to the others, who all begin to hurriedly rush fro the door, followed by their burning master. Next day… Terror reigns in downtown San Jose, as Minion and his glistening followers release jets of flame upon the buildings and vehicles near the center square of downtown: the followers with flame throwers, Minion creating them from sheer will. He cackles malevolently as he lets loose a stream of heat into a gas station with catastrophic results. PHWOOOM!! “Run and hide, people of San Jose!! Minion cannot be defeated!! BWAHAHAHAHA!! Not even your PRECIOUS Big Alan Steele can save you from the Hell I can create!!” One of the flame suits trots over, dragging a small, teenaged girl behind him. Twisting around, he forces her in front of the towering Minion, who bares his fangs and poises his claws on the petrified young woman. “Do you wish to become cleansed by the purity of my flames, little one?!” Shrinking to her knees, the small girl tearfully shakes her head ‘no.’ Minion rises to his full height, and points a single talon at her. “Then DO IT!! Make the call to Big Alan Steele to attempt to rescue you from my clutches!!” The girl shakily reaches into her back pocket and produces a cell phone. Gathering her courage, she rises her head o face the terror above her. “But… but I don’t know his number!!” “Oh… it’s [detail withheld] for local calls, 1-800-BIG-ALAN for long distance…” The girl dials and brings the phone up to her ear. “Hello?” Minion grins as he rubs his hands together in glee. “Yeah… uh… we kinda need some help downtown. Yeah, some big red guy…” “Minion, Lord of Flame, servant of Ba’al, if you please.” “Okay, this MINION guy… I dunno, he mostly throws fireballs and whatnot. Uh-huh… Yeah, he’s got men with him.” “Well?! Is he coming?!” The teenager places a hand over the lower end of the phone. “The Grace on the other end says Big Al is too busy training for the Frisco PPV match to come out and fight. She asked if the police would do.” “The POLICE?!?!” huffs Minion, “What rubbish!! May I use your phone, please?” The girl shrugs and hands over the cell, dwarfed in Minions claws. “Hello! This is Minion, Lord of Flame, servant of… uh-huh… uh-huh… said he wouldn’t come out if it were Zeus himself, huh? No, I’m not Zeus. Listen, I don’t have long, because the phone is melting… No. I’m a servant of Ba’al, a greater demon among the legions of… yeah, that one. Well, could you just ask him? It would mean a lot to me and I’m sure if he’d heard… oh… uh-huh… Oh, he called earlier too? I see… no, no, it’s okay. We’ll just try again some other time. No, thank YOU.” Minion sighs and drops the phone, melted into an unrecognizable lump. “Let’s go guys,” he says heavily, “Back to the secret lair.” “You jerk!” cries out the teen, “You ruined my cell phone!” “Whatever,” mumbles Minion, as he and his cult members slowly begin to disperse. Next day… Riiiiiiiiiing! Riiiiiiiiiing!! Sue lazily picks up the hotline phone in the sweltering heat of the cellar training room. “Yeah? What?” The voice of a panicked Police Chief McCloud screams into the receiver. “Omigod!! The station is burning before my very eyes!! Some big demon guy is burning it down and there is nothing we can do about it!!” In the background, Sue can hear the diabolical laughter of Minion as he torches the places, as well as the popping of ineffectual police gunfire. She yawns before answering, “Yeah, so?” “SO?!?!” shouts back an incredulous McCloud, “We’ll all be toast if Big Al doesn’t DO SOMETHING!!” “You know, you guys rely on Big Al too much already. Maybe if you were to learn how to solve your OWN problems, things like a flame-throwing demon wouldn’t be such a problem for you.” “DEAR GOD, SAVE US… PLEASE!!!” “”I’m sorry, but he needs to concentrate on his meal ticket right now. Wrestling pays the bills and keeps the crime fighting overhead off our backs. You’ll just have to make do…” “AAAAARRRRRGGGGHHHH!!!!” “In the meantime, I’d suggest getting the Fire Department in on your efforts as well as maybe getting the Hall of Justice to help…” “They just disconnect the phone and leave town for two weeks when we call!!” In the background, Sue can hear the satanic devil yelling, “Oh, for the love of Pete!!” Sue shrugs, “Well, I gotta run. Keeping the Big Guy focused is a full-time thing, ya know. Tootles…” “WAIT!!!” Click! “…………………..” Next day… KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! Steffi, one of the lovely blonde Graces, hums an aimless tune as she skips over to the front door of Stately Steele Manor. She puts her hand on the brass knob… “Yeowch!!” And instantly brings it back, blowing on her scalded hand. Frowning, she uses the bottom edge of her shirt as an insulator for her hand as she again grips and pulls, opening the door. “Oh! Hello!” she says, staring, “Do you have an appointment?” The burning demon Minion smiles a feral grin, his sharp teeth gleaming. “Not exactly… is Big Alan Steele home? I was wondering if I could have a moment of his time…” Steffi looks around the flaming man, seeing a horde of cult followers in the yard, milling about with their flame throwers. “Sure thing!” she says, smiling sweetly, “Won’t you come in?” “Thank you so much!” “And YOU guys!! Stay off the lawn!!” The men all look up at Steffi’s scolding and hurriedly leap to the pathways provided. Minion has to duck as he enters the doorway, led to the training room steps by Steffi. “One, two, three, four, five…” Sue counts off as Big Alan Steele swings sparring partner Vic Kevlar off the ropes and catches him into side-buster, rolling off and quickly bouncing off the ropes again. “No!!” Sue cries out in anger, “You are a half-beat off AGAIN!!! Keep your timing, focus on your feet!! You shuffle too much as you get up any wasted movement is more time for Frisco to recover…” “Excuse me!” booms a demonic voice from the stairwell, “I hope I’m not interrupting anything!!” “By the gods!!” Vic cranes his head from the mat and gawks in fear. Big Al settles into a defensive posture within the ring. Sue stares a bit slack jawed. Meanwhile, Steffi leads the huge adversary deeper into Steele’s inner sanctum. “Steffi,” sighs Sue, “What have I told you about bringing odd people home?” “Oh no,” Steffi refutes, “He’s a friend of Big Al’s!” “Actually, more of an admirer of your work,” hisses Minion, “A pleasure to finally meet you in person!” Sue rises, getting perturbed at the training interruption, “Okay, so can I get you anything? A glass of water, perhaps?” “How droll,” snarls Minion, “But I come in peace on this day. I merely wish to speak with Alan. I figure after his affronts to me of late, it is more than a reasonable request.” “Affronts? I hath done no such thing!!” Bellows Big Al. “On the contrary, Steele!! You have rebuffed my attempts at battle thrice thusly! I have to admit to feeling a bit… unwanted.” “Sowwy!” chides Sue, “Did we hurt the big, bad monster’s feewings?” “It’s no joke, woman,” says Minion rather haughtily, “I have gone through much troubles to come to a confrontation with Alan, at great personal expense, and I have yet to do so. I perhaps feel he does not understand the full depth of his ‘heroic deeds’ among villainy’s caste system.” “I have to admit to not understanding your position, Hades-spawn,” replies Big Alan Steele, stepping over the ropes to the floor, “And I do sincerely apologize for any mis-intended slight… however I demand to know you presence here, at once!” “I’ll level with you,” Minion states, his tone lowering, “I NEED to fight you. I’m new to the scene and… well, I have to make a name for myself.” Big Al is dumbfounded as Minion continues. “Oh, it’s true. You see, you don’t learn how to be a bad guy overnight. You have to be taught. And where do you think you receive such training?” “I had always assumed the Legion of Doom spawned such programs,” mumbles Steele. “Not quite. They don’t accept just ANYONE, you know. You have to have some work experience. So, we have to fight YOU.” “ME?! By the gods!!” “Indeed. You see, you learn the basics of being a criminal, ironically, in prison. That’s where you meet the great villains. Sooner or later they all end up there. So I need to go to prison.” “But… then why not simply allow local law enforcement to capture and imprison you?” Minion snorts derisively, “Are you joking?! A SUPER-villain would be laughed out of Arkham if they were caught by mere cops!! No, unfortunately, our litmus test is MUCH more difficult… you, Big Alan Steele. If we fight you and, you know, kinda hold our own, then we are welcomed into the prison fraternity, almost assured a spot in the Legion of Doom after our stay!!” “Diabolical!! I have been used thusly without knowledge for how long?!” “Oh, come off your high horse!” snorts Minion, “We know you use US for wrestling training…” Steele looks down at the floor, a bit shame faced. “So, can we come to a sort of deal here? Maybe make an appointment?” Big Al furrows his brow, thinking hard. Finally, he smiles raising a hand in ‘friendship.’ “Indeed!! As most assuredly I could use a break in my daily routine!” The two shake with a sizzle, Steele yelping at the heat. “Excellent!” cries the demon king, “How about tomorrow?” “Dawn?” Minion cracks his knuckles expectantly, “I shall be waiting for you!” “Hi-Keeba!” Next day… Through the aisles of Arkham, a giant box is pulled along on wheels, caging the demon Minion within. The guards carefully wheel him down the hall to his asbestos cell. The laser grid lowers, allowing the men to push the entrapment inside. Once the men are safely away, the laser grid fires back into place, and the box opens. Minion, Lord of Flame, servant of Ba’al, slowly, painfully, limps from the cage and seats himself on the Spartan bed inside. Rubbing his bruised jaw, he slowly cranes his neck in order to stretch it out, his broken horn scraping the 4-foot cement wall. “Psssst! Newbie…” Minion turns at the sound of Dr. Doom, in the cell next door. “How’d ya do against… him.” ‘Him’ said with a venom not to be described. “I got a few good punches in,” the demon groans, “And reduced his garage to ashes.” Murmurs of approval. “At lunch hour, meet us at the far table, in the southwest corner.” Minion smiles, his broken fangs flashing in happiness.
