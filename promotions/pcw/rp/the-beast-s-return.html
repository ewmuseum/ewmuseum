The Beast's Return...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday evening (Chaos ensues in The Palace courtyard…) “You set of BLOODY fools! I thought I told you to get RID of it!” (Another hastily uprooted tree is launched at a troop of cowering guards, shards of green foliage raining down upon the ground.) “We did get rid of it, boss!” “Yeah! We threw it over the wall into next-door’s garden!” (We see the utterly shocked visage of The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv looking into the hopeful yet plainly stupendous eyes of one of his personal security force.) Guard, hurriedly: “It took us some time, I can tell you—” The Pharaoh, interrupting: Some TIME?! It took you SOME time?! Is that all you have to say for yourself, you idiot?! By the GODS!” (Huddled behind the wheelbarrow, the five bemused employees of The Palace hang their heads in shame.) The Pharaoh: “Sometimes I wonder why the DUAT I even BOTHERED to employ you people in the first place!” (Beyond their tiny shelter, the beastly creature continues to rip through the armoured men attempting to fend it off. Pharaoh looks to the heavens and shakes his head, dumbfounded.) The Pharaoh: “Give me strength, O powerful Ra…please, give me STRENGTH.” (A wave of uprooted pebbles blasts over the top of the wooden refuge as a vile shriek sounds out over the courtyard. The guards pay one another quick glances, sorrowful glances…) Guard, mournfully: “Sounded like old Bill…poor old Bill.” (A moment of silence.) Guard#2, conversationally: “To be honest I thought it sounded more like little Harry Blanket from #D sector.” (The third guard waves a hand dismissively.) Guard#3: “No, no…THAT was DEFINITELY Jimmy Harrelson from #G sector, mate.” The Pharaoh, interrupting: “By Osiris, pay WHOEVER it was some respect, gentleman! He’s met his maker now, there’s no need to dwell on the past.” (The troops take off their oversized golden crowns, place them about their hearts and nod politely.) The Pharaoh: “Now then, let’s try to figure out how this all happened, shall we? So start from the beginning, from the time I handed the beast over to you…” Guard#1: “Well, sir, after you caught Mrs. Livingston trespassing, and passed her over for execution—” The Pharaoh: “Not THAT beast! The one rampaging through The Palace right now!” Guard#1: “OHHH, right! Well, you gaze us the instructions to get rid of it, boss…so we did.” The Pharaoh: “Dropping it over Mrs. Heightneigh’s wall is NOT getting rid off it, man! She’s an old woman for the Gods’ sakes! What if it ate her…or something?” (The guard grins idiotically.) Guard#1: “Oh I very much doubt that, sir! She thought it was a cat for quite some time, you see… The Pharaoh, puzzled: “A CAT?!” Guard#2: Bad eyesight, sir. The Pharaoh: Ah. Guard#1, continuing: “…and then it ate the postman, and well…” (He pauses. Another shower of pebbles passes overhead as an echoing roar hits the airwaves.) The Pharaoh, ignoring the clacker of gravel: “Well what?” Guard#1, blushing: “Well she tried to boot the thing out of the garden, see, and well…it ate her too….” Pharaoh, sighing: “Well, THIS is just BRILLIANT, isn’t it?! I can’t WAIT for the lawsuits to start piling in! And I thought you said you very much doubted the possibility of her getting eaten!” Guard#1: “I overlooked that since she used laid out milk for it every morning…then of course I remembered what ACTUALLY happened.” The Pharaoh: “I pray to the Gods that your idiocy is overlooked when you die, my friend.” (The third wave of gravel flies by as a guard from beyond the makeshift shelter plummets to the earth a few feet away from the group.) Guard#4, in a matter-of-factly sort of voice: “Now THAT’S certainly Willy Taylor.” (Another roar.) The Pharaoh: “Great, this is just GREAT…in two days I have to fight Violence Jack, and at this rate I won’t even make it there! Brilliant. As if Fusion wasn’t bad enough!” Guard#2: “I thought Fusion was a good night for you, sire? You and Snow and everyone were left triumphant at the show’s end!” The Pharaoh: “Yes, but Clyde still managed to bring about another plague. I pray for my brothers in Egypt… And anyway, look at us now! I’m destined to meet my end here today!” Guard#3: “Not to worry, sir! ‘Least you’ll go out with a bang, eh? And looking at some of the corpses around here I’d probably say you won’t feel a thing!” The Pharaoh, dully: “Your words fill me with hope and prosperity, my friend.” Guard#3: “I mean, look at Willy. He doesn’t look like he felt a thing…” (The man picks up a moderately large stone and hurls it at the body.) Willy, unmoved: “Owww.” Guard#3: “See!” (Pharaoh shakes his head, his eyes filling with desperation.) The Pharaoh: “I can take this no longer. I’m going out there to battle this thing MYSELF!” (The others share a laugh.) The Pharaoh: “BAH!” (With a determined look about his face, Titu climbs out from behind the wheelbarrow and steps into the crumpled courtyard.) The Pharaoh: “By Horus! This is going to need some work!” (He turns his head and looks out over the horizon, his eyes narrowed. All around him are the moaning bodies of The Palace guard, but their attacker is nowhere to be seen.) The Pharaoh: “That’s odd…Where in the name of Thoth is it?” (BAM! Titu goes tumbling to the ground! He looks up, narrowly dodging the second spiked fist and rolls to his left. Regaining his footing, he begins to run, leading the beast away from the courtyard and therefore securing the safety of the five still-cowering guards.) Guard#1: “Reckon he’ll beat it?” Guard#2: “No.” Guard: 4: “Ha!” Guard#3: “Nah.” Guard#5: “…Yeah.” (He notices the sceptical stares.) Guard#5: “Errr…I mean no…” (The scene switches to show Titu running head-on with the horribly ugly, scale-backed monster hot on his heals.) The Pharaoh: “DAMMMNNN!!” (He turns a corner sharply and slips into an area of deep undergrowth. As he continues to run a thought strikes him.) The Pharaoh: “Wait a minute! Just up ahead I’m sure I dug out a trap—ARGHHH!” (Falling into the darkness of the pit, Titu screams out vainly until he comes to a stop with a hard THUD!) The Pharaoh, rolling eyes: “This is about as unfortunate a coincidence as the time Iris couldn’t find Osiris’ manhood! How the DUAT am I going to get out of this?!” (The scene fades, leaving our story unfinished…)
