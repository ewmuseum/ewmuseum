The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XIII]

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7, 2002 OOC: This is a short one, but it needs to be condensed considering it reveals the identity of The Mystery Man! A good episode in this series. We last left the boys in PINK! at an immense climax in which the Mystery Man was going to 'get rid' of ex-Rainbow Squad member Smith...here's how it continued... The Mystery Man slipped through the gap in the roof. The attic was not floored, but he easily moved across it stepping from one rafter to another. He reached a likely spot and dropped easily between the rafters to the concrete floor below. He landed, his knees bending taking his weight, till his hands too touched the floor, bending to absorb yet more of the force of the impact. He stood up and kicked his feet. The soles of his feet stung with the impact, but there was no serious damage. The building was a residential block though it made no difference at the moment, only the most basic of things were there. Still no windows, and bare walls. He walked down the bare concrete stairs and walked into the room. A man stood at the far end in a raincoat shivering. The Mystery Man took him in in a single glance. A drug addict, dealing to feed a habit, unpredictable but not particularly dangerous. Slow beaten eyes looking back at him. The Mystery Man had his gear in his hand. Mystery Man: “Take off the raincoat...and get out of here. Go up the stairs and stay there. If I see you again I will do something very bad to you.' The Mystery Man waved his 'weapon.' Mystery Man: “You understand?” Drug Addict: “Yes, yes I’m going, don't hurt me.” The man dropped the raincoat on the floor and headed out. After carefully watching him take off the raincoat, the Mystery Man headed over to the window where he could see the parked car, with two Village People in it, that meant that Smith and one minder must be in the building and heading towards him now. The Mystery Man shrugged the raincoat over his shoulders and tried to huddle in the corner the way he had seen the drug dealer huddle. He held his 'weapon' in his hands, with the gentle firmness that a craftsman holds a favorite tool. The Mystery Man heard them enter the room, leather shoes rasping against a concrete floor. He turned at the same time standing upright. Mystery Man: “Hello Mr. Smith, you don’t know me, but I know all about you.” The guard had his weapon is his hand in a single reflex action, but he was too slow. The Mystery Man had aimed and fired before the gun was aimed at him. The guard fell moaning and gurgling unpleasantly. The mystery man had shot him with a bee-bee gun. What, you thought the Heartbreakers were killers? Or even worse, you thought the 'weapon' meant something else? You perverts. Smith did not even spare a glance for the fallen guard. He was not armed, he never was. Smith: “What can I offer you, whatever you’ve been offered I can double it. I’ve got contacts, I’ve got this place stitched up, PURPLE~!, PINK!, you name it you can have whatever you want.” Smith was an operator, a handler, he was looking at The Mystery Man’s face all the time. Looking for that flicker that would show that he had touched a nerve, show what he could offer, what bribe would do the trick to take him away from The Heartbreakers. Smith: “PINK~!, yes I’ve got contacts there, anything you want you can get, a smart man like you we can do anything, stay with me and the PINK! will never touch you, why do you think I’ve never been 'touched' by them.” Smith was very convincing, The Mystery Man could almost believe him. Mystery Man: “It was the Heartbreakers that sent me here, well one particular Heartbreaker.” Smith: “That bastard Heart, I’ll nail him to the floor, whatever he offered you I can double it, it's is yours if you come over to me, he’s living on borrowed time anyway, I’ve got others that can do for him, he’s not as special as he thinks he is. The Heartbreakers will lose their titles this Friday anyways to Cowabunga Connection, so what does it matter?” Mystery Man: “No Mr. Smith...” The Mystery Man took off his mask and revealed himself to be ELTON JOHN~! He fired into Smith’s torso and doused him in PINK!. Then as he started to stagger he aimed again and shot him in the head. Smith staggered some more and fell. Elton John walked over to him coolly and searched him slowly and carefully for a PURPLE~! wire. No, Smith was not wired, then he checked the guard. He too was not wired. Elton John hauled the unconscious bodies over to the far wall, he surveyed the room. He took off the raincoat, there was money in the inside pocket. A dirty wad of notes bound up with an elastic band from the drug dealer. He put the wad inside his trouser pocket. There was no point in telling the dealer in the attic. He had seen nothing, and was an unreliable witness. However, Elton John checked quickly to see that he had not been within earshot. Standing at the empty socket of a window, he looked out. The second shot he had fired had been without a silencer. The two remaining Village People guards had become frightened and decided to flee. They were turning the car feverishly in the dirty rutted space before the scaffolding. The car that Elton John had arrived in had been parked over the exit. There was no sign of Valentine or Heart. As Elton stood there watching he could see the thick smoke starting to pour out of the car, one of the guards had got out of Smith’s car and was waving a gun in the air, he fired at the burning car parked across the exit, and then ran away in the direction of the site huts. The other guard also followed running quickly. Elton watched them disappear into the distance, they vanished among the site huts, then some minutes later he could see them still running beyond the limits of the building site. When people were armed they were dangerous, you always need to know where they are, especially when they are fruits like the Village People. When you don’t know where they are they are at their most dangerous. That's why The Heartbreakers have Cowabunga Connection scouted well for this match on Friday. When Elton John reached the burning car, flames were bathing it and with a stroke the paint vanished to leave dirty metal. Heart was using a crow bar to open the back of Smith’s car. Johnny Heart: 'ELTON?!? What are you doing here??' Elton John: 'I'll explain later.' Johnny Heart: “Did that bastard Smith offer you money, Elton?” Elton John: “Yep, they usually do, he was lying though, he wouldn’t have that kind of money even for a jumped up little PURPLE~! PUNK.” The Mystery Man was walking over to see what Heart was up to. Johnny Heart: “Don’t bet on it, he usually carries a lot of money about with him. He needs it to buy his MEN!” With a flourish Heart lifted a suitcase out of the back of the car boot and opened it. It was brim full of money and GAY PORN. A handful of dollars scattered on the wind. Nobody moved. Then Heart closed the case. Johnny Heart: “You can have the money Elton, means nothing to us as tag team champions. I’m going to head on in, see if we can get this sorted out before Friday's match with Cowabunga Connection.” With that Heart walked off, the crow bar hanging limp from one hand. Jack Valentine: 'What do we do with this?' Jack held up some of the GAY PORN~! Elton John: 'I'll take that!' Jack looked up mysteriously at Elton... Elton John: 'What? We need it...for...evidence, yeah...for evidence.' Fade out. So the mystery man had been revealed as Elton John all along. But The Mystery Man was originally seen on the PURPLE~! side when we first met him...what would this mean? How would it all affect the safety of The Heartbreakers on Fusion when they defend their tag team titles? Stay tuned for part XIV to find out even more in this never-ending story!
