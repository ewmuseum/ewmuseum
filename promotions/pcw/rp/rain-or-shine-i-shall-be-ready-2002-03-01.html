Rain or shine... I shall be ready!

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/03/02 So the faithful temptress has comeback from the bowels of hell, to once again plague and ruin the life of one. Though with love, bravery and lasting dedication, nothing can destroy you. No matter how bad things get, if you have people there for you, and you have the characteristics to deal with them yourself, then what else do you need? Nothing can stop you, if that is the case… Though, if that is not the case at all, if someone has no one there for him/her, and they do not have bravery, love and dedication, then what fate rests in their hands? The day was coming nearer, probably the biggest day in young Anton McGonnell’s career, where he would compete ion the PCW Pay-per-View. The honour was unimaginable. He would soon have the chance to grasp the PCW Cruiserweight title, from the tight clutches owned by Chris Chase, a man who is well respected through out PCW, and indeed all of PTC. He is one that will surely go onto greatness, and it would be a great honour, if Anton could some how take his title from his grasp, and place it around his own waist, to claim his first PTC Title. He fought for so long and came so close to winning the NTICW World Title on so many occasions, and there is no real relevant title in PWA. And as he was practically new in PCW, it would surely be the opportunity of a life. Though to achieve such as honour, he must first escape the vicious barrier that stands in his way. That Barrier consists of Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer Wild Wing. Three men who were highly respect, highly paid, and highly worthy of being PCW Cruiserweight Champion. Anton would surely do all in his power, to get the famous ‘1…2…3!’ on one of these great athletes, and get a title shot against Chris Chase. Though even if he did win, there was still the possibility that it would not be Chase who he would be engaging in war with. In fact it could well be one of Anton’s former nemeses from NTICW… Xavier Kannon. The former NTICW World Champion, who only won the belt thanks to Anton, could well stand in the way of Anton and the chance to be recognized and loved by the world, which would surely be an exhilarating feeling. Though in the end, it all rested on Sunday, at March Madness in Missouri, Anton would go head to head, in a 4-way match against; Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer wild Wing, for the right to be the Number 1 contender for the PCW Cruiserweight title. A PCW PPV was where dreams were made, could Anton’s be made on Sunday? Anton entered the dark alleyway in the streets of New York. The day was cold and damp, it was about 6:00 pm, and already the sky was getting dark, and the sun’s light reflected onto the moon, and shone brightly down on New York City. Anton wore a black Shirt, with an Asian symbol embroidered onto it. It was black, as were his baggy pants. He had a red belt around them. By this stage it is obvious that this is Martial Arts attire. Anton also had a blue Nike Sports bag, hanging from his right shoulder. His hair was as always gelled to perfection. He looked in front of him with his big blue eyes. As he entered the alleyway, he turned and when into a small door at the left. In here was a small corridor, Anton quickly went through this and came into a bigger corridor, he walked around like he knew the place of by heart, and every inch had been viewed him before. He walked quietly up and through a white door. Where he came into a medium sized office, it had a Japanese man in it, behind a desk, sitting on a big black chair. He had black hair with grey patches at the side. He wore a traditional white Martial Arts suit with a black belt. This was Tsunami Chow-Lee He looked up at Anton and smiled. “Ah, Anton-san, how do you do today.” He said in good English but with strong signs of Asians origins. “I am fine master Tsunami.” Anton replies, bowing down to his teacher. Tsunami replies this gesture by doing the same, but with less effort. “I take it you are ready for your Judo tournament to decide whether or not you will be a fully-schooled Black Belt?” He asked Anton solemnly. “Yes, I have been training Hard Master Tsunami, I have a dummy at home for which I use my skills on.” Anton replies politely as possible. “Good, but you must understand the relevance of this. If you get honours in the tournament, or even win it, and become a Black Belt, then we can go onto the next Martial Art, and you will be well on your way. For, even though most wrestlers would not admit it. It is a known fact that Martial Arts in the best form of physical war. No other form of fighting is as affective and as enlightening as it is.” Tsunami said with a strong passion is his voice. “I realize that Mater Tsunami, I am looking forward to getting onto the next form of Martial Arts, what will it be?” Anton asked curiously. “Ah, it is wrong for a Master to reveal to a student what is not needed to know, for it takes away mind space for things that you do need to know.” Tsunami said. Anton accepted this by nodding. “Well, I’ll be ready for the tournament, though I will need to be in peak condition for the Pay-Per-View on Sunday.” Anton said. “Oh yes, I understand that you must compete against the ones named Red Rock, Ganker and Kramer Wild Wing. All great competitors, but you are lucky it is the cruiserweight division, as you will be able to use your Judo skills to your advantage as they are not particularly heavy by average wrestling stature.” Tsunami tells Anton. “Yes I hope to do so, I will also use some of the kicking manoeuvres I have learned in my spare time.” Anton said, which seemed to impress Tsunami as he smiled at Anton. “Well, no matter of your bout this coming Sunday, for now, we will concentrate on your Martial Arts, be in the Gym in five minutes, we shall review some of your Judo skills, and if you are lucky, I may tell you your next Martial Art.” Tsunami said before leaving the room. Anton sat down on a seat beside the desk. The room’s walls were blue and there were pictures of different Martial Artists, World Champions etc. Anton hoped to one day have the success they did, only in wrestling, though he had a strong passion for Martial Arts. He knew it would help him, for he had been winning matches on his quickness and High-Flying skills alone, it was time he brought on a new technique. Anton put his head down to get his composure before he started his training. Sunday was only two days away, it would be two days before the biggest match in his PCW career would come true, a massive crowd would be coming, cheering Anton on, the adrenalines rush would surely be exhilarating, just thinking about it made Anton tingle. Though his focus mad to be on his opponents. Red Rock… a man who has ‘balls of steel’ Anton was not remotely a dirty competitor, so that wouldn’t be needed in the match. Though still the former PCW Rising Star Champion would surely be someone to compete with, he was an outstanding athlete, and was very young. Only 24 years of age, despite that, he was ten years older than Anton. Next came the mighty Ganker, a great competitor back in RSE AKA RingSide Entertainment. He was a real talent, part of Team Nirvana along with his good friend Hank~! He was a future talent, and had a real rivalry with red Rock, as the competed in a fed together and was never what you would call, friends. Anton hoped this would work to his advantage, though both would be out for the win as well, and they were professionals after all. Last, but most certainly not least, was Kramer Wild Wing, a PCW veteran I guess you could say. He had already been a PCW Cruiserweight Champion. He was a great competitor, and Anton would have a real problem getting past him, as he was a very experienced competitor, and would be the oldest in the match, while Anton would be the youngest, as always. “ANTON!” Anton hears, when he is broken from him trance. It is Tsunami calling Anton into the gym. Anton gets himself together and enters the quite large, windowless gym. It had a lot of equipment used for Martial Arts raining of source. In the room, Tsunami had set out four blue mats. Anton would compete against Tsunami on it as they did a lot of the time, to show how much Anton had learned. “Now Anton, this will be your final Judo lesson for now, and if you get the Black-belt, you will be well on your way, there are very, very few boys of your age who have a black belt in any Martial Art. You must capitalize on your opponents. So will be the same age as you, though since you are in the 14-17 category, a lot will be older, and maybe stronger than you, so use all that I have showed, you. Reversals could be crucial, that goes for the tournament and your match on Sunday.” Tsunami tells Anton, trying to get him as ready as possible. “I am pretty sure all my opponents on Sunday will be doing high-flying and risk-taking manoeuvres. What do you think will be the best tactics for me to take?” Anton asked his teacher curiously. Tsunami thought for a moment. “Well, they cannot stay up in the air all the time, so when the prance around the ring, you stay down and use all my teachings to your advantage. Then try some high-flying moves, use your amazing quickness too. It will catch them of guard and will give you time to take the advantage. All three men are fast, but you are faster. You will just have to make sure you don’t grapple with them too much as they have all superior strength to yours.” Tsunami tells him earnestly. “Should I go for the quick pin, or keep the match going until my opponents get tired?” Anton asked. “Well, you are well-known for your amazing endurance and heart, so keep the match going, and then end it with a quick move, for you can only pin one, but there will always be two other men there to stop you from doing so.” “Yes I realize that. That is exactly why I will have to use my quickness right?” Anton asked again. “Exactly, you are catching on fast Anton-san, soon you shall be an amazing Martial Artist.” Tsunami compliments Anton. Anton smiles at this remark. “Thanks master Tsunami.” Tsunami then suddenly claps his hands to bring them back to the task at hand. “Okay, now, get onto the mats and we shall practice some of the Judo I have taught you.” Tsunami said. The two then get onto the mats. The first time, Tsunami lunges first but Anton quickly sidesteps back and goes around to the back of Tsunami, and quickly takes him down. “YES!” Anton shouts in victory. “Finally, I have got you down for once.” Anton puts out his hand and Tsunami takes it, he then gets up and smiles. “Well done, once against you quickness has prevailed.” Tsunami said. “I just hope I can do that on Sunday.” Anton said unconfidently. They square up again. This time, Anton tries tripping Tsunami, but he quickly jumps and grabs Anton’s arms from behind, Anton tries to reverse it, but is quickly ended when he tries to jump over Tsunami and Tsunami simply lets go as he drops to the ground. “Oh, well. I guess you win some and you lose some.” Anton said. “You must realize, victory is not everything, you will always lose sometimes, but if you learn from it, it can be a great value.” Tsunami tells Anton. Anton would surely need to win on Sunday, for it could make or break him, it would be a massive moment in his career, he could only pray for victory against such opponents. They were all amazing, but was Anton’s crimson blood burning bright enough, was his heart large enough, and would the crowd bring him the mighty adrenaline rush that refreshed his body? Only time could tell, but Anton knew he would be ready, rain or shine.”
