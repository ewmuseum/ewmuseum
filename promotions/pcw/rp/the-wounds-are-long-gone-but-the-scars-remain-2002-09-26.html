The wounds are long gone, but the scars remain…

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26.9.02 The wounds are long gone, but the scars remain… “You’re doing well, you just need to practice it more.” Ever since Cyclonia became involved in my life I’d felt helpless and weak. She was easily capable of destroying me, and I knew that she would and could at any time. But it was, I felt, only her obvious fascination with Titu that stopped her from ending my existence. Deep down I felt that I couldn’t do anything to stop her from one day taking both our lives, and neither could Titu. I looked at him and wondered why he didn’t fear her. The ring, which he’d recently purchased to tone his skills, surrounded us and as he walked towards one of the turnbuckles a thought crossed my mind. Why do you want to help me? He was my husband and my sole mate, but why did he say yes when I came to him and asked if he’d show me some moves? So many thoughts, so many questions, so much to contemplate and take in. It was far too much to comprehend. I knew he loved me. But he had business of his own to conduct…right? And if that was so, why the heck was he teaching me the ways he knew so well? “Honestly, Isiy, you’re showing great improvement. In a few hours I reckon you could take out Cyclonia just like that!” said Pharaoh, nodding his head. “Thanks. Titu, why did you agree to help me?” A smile brushed over his mouth. He knelt down and picked up a bottle of water and took a swig. “You’re my wife, Isiy; that means whenever you need my attention I give you it,” he beamed a loving grin. “It’s because I worship you.” I knew that he meant what he said. And for that I trusted him with my own life. Once before, when Akiyama had attacked, I myself had had to deal with him personally. Back then, however, Cyclonia was nowhere to be seen. He’d tortured me and banished me to the void. I remembered o too well the horror of that desolate place and nothing on this earth would compel me to go back again. The terror that his relentless torment struck me with has never left me - I doubt it ever will. But every day I put on a brave face and I never utter a word about the incident. I know that if I were to say something it would drive Titu into a destructive rage. He loves me so much that he dares not see me in pain. The wounds have long gone, but the scars remain… “Come on then, Isiy; now I’ll show you how to throw a punch.” said Titu, a pleasant smile about his face. “Titu, I know how to punch.” “Oh really? Then show me one…” he said, a friendly amount of cynicism in his voice. I pulled back my arm and punched the air, stopping mere centimetres away from his jaw. He didn’t move, nor did he show that he feared my jab. I blushed. I was a bashful person, and though I’d been married to him for all these years his presence mortified me. Not in a bad way, mind you - he’d never purposely make me feel lower than him and some times he didn’t, it was just on certain occasions like this. People are themselves and that’s all there is to it. Titu was royal in appearance and brave in mind. His nobility was one of the key things that attracted me to him all those years ago, although his striking looks also played a part. I was simply a bashful woman and that was that. “Not bad,” he said at last. “Though, I’m sure you can do better.” Again, I threw my arm in his direction and again it stopped but an inch away from his face. “Once again it wasn’t bad. But come on…hit me this time. Imagine I’m Cyclonia. Do what you’d do to her, but to me…” Cyclonia. How I hated even her wretched name. How I wished she’d just disappear. Our paths had crossed only twice and already she’d driven hate and fear into my heart. It was an unusual mix. I despised her so much, yet her presence frightened me. I felt sorry for that poor girl Anarchy. PCW’s current cruiserweight champion was, in my opinion, Cyclonia’s whipping-girl. Just last week the sick beast had bled her dry, and the youthful Anarchy had enjoyed it, or refused to question it. She was said to be a divine prophetess - a being of absolute power… someone - something - as deadly as anything I’d ever seen. Akiyama was ranked bellow her, and the Neo Void God was an immensely dominant force. I hated her, however damned powerful she thought she was! I wished she’d disappear… I wished she’d die… I wished she’d leave Titu and I alone… I hated her… …so much… And there she was, standing in front of me like the light of day. My mouth twisted in anger, me eyes saw red, and my arm came back like the spring in a gun. My fist met Titu’s jaw with some impact. I watched as his face warped in pain and his form backed off and fell to the canvas with a thud. Gaining my senses, I quickly ran to him, knelt beside his dazed body and cradled his head in my lap. “Oh, no! Titu, I’m so sorry!” “By the Gods…what a punch.” He rubbed his jaw, wincing. “Could you pass me some water?” he queried. Without thought I edged towards the turnbuckle and picked up his bottle of water. Passing it to him, I wondered if I really had hurt him, or if he was just putting it on for show. I looked on curiously as he poured some water over his face and sighed. “By Amun-Ra, what a punch! If you ever did hit Cyclonia with an arm like that then on the good name of Isis she’d be down and out!” I blushed a little. “You think?” “I bloody know!” he laughed. “Ok, next we’re gonna see how your basic moves are coming along. To start off, how about you hit me with a suplex.” I glared at him, blank faced. How the heck could I possibly suplex a man like him?! He weighed well over two hundred pounds for the love of Horus! And besides, I only weighed around one hundred and twenty pounds; therefore there was no possible way that I could lift and throw him to the mat! His brow furrowed as I continued to stare in amazement. “What’s the matter?” “Titu, I can’t suplex you.” He tilted his head to one side, oblivious of the size difference between the two of us, and obviously the limits a one hundred and twenty pound frame has. “Why not?” he said, earnestly. Again, I glared. “Because you’re a Duat of a lot bigger than me!” “Bah! Come on, show me what you’ve got!” he laughed. I didn’t move, to his annoyance. “Well, at least try, then.” That was the only problem Titu had really. He couldn’t understand why people didn’t do things that they were told or asked to do. It annoyed him a little, but not to a point where he’d do a Tim Walden and ‘snap’. His brain couldn’t quite understand that the possibilities of me suplexing him were slim and in not even trying to perform the act I aggravated him. I shook my head to which he frowned. “Well Ok, I suppose if you don’t want to then you don’t have to,” he sniffed, “But you realize sooner or later you might have to fight Cyclonia?” “I suppose so.” “And in knowing what I’m willing to teach you you’d stand a better chance of combating her powers. Then again, it’s up to you.” He concluded flatly. What he said was right, I knew that. But Cyclonia could probably destroy me without me even making a move. There really was no point in learning the things he was willing to teach me. But that wasn’t to say that I didn’t appreciate the time he was giving up for me. After all, he did have Fusion coming up where he’d have to fight Jason Snow and perhaps not worrying over me would be better for him. I sighed. “Titu, let’s just call it a rap for today, Ok? You have other things to think about, remember? Your match for Fusion against Jason Snow, Akiyama, your Red October matches to name only a few. Maybe it’s in your best interests to concentrate on those for now, and not me?” “Isiy, you are the only thing that I worry about most in this world, but if you wish for me to adjust my line of thought upon my wrestling matters, then so be it. I need to get something to eat, so I’m going up stairs, Ok?” he said. Moments later, I was all alone in the basement. The ring surrounding me seemed bigger than ever and above all I felt trapped -trapped within a world that I couldn’t comprehend. The wounds Akiyama had once inflicted upon me weren’t there anymore, but the emotional scars still remained… Whether I’d ever find a way out of this world was something that remained unanswered… It was true that the wounds were long gone, but the scars remained… …and they always would…
