A Year In The Making - Angelo Deville (Part 4)

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 Jan 2003 Recap: The Primetime Wrestling Academy is the source of some of the industries most gifted individuals and continues to develop and groom talent but without a graduate since PCW’s alpha male Xavier Kannon then has this facility crashed and burned? A year has passed since the Scientologist etched his name on the graduation list and still nobody has been inducted but that trend finally comes to a tiresome end as four new names join those deemed ‘Best Of The Best’ be it Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, Angelo Deville, Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green and Amy ‘Aimz’ Campbell. Already we have witnessed Amy Campbell’s speech as she took to the stand in torrent of tears, we have also seen the vampire Nerezza voice his thoughts about graduating and with his David Copperfield vanishing act Hessian welcomed the hand groomed protégé of Tyler Nelson to the altar, Angelo Deville. “Between the emotional departure of Amy Campbell and the disappearing of the great Nerezza it would be nice if someone actually stayed… To the end… So I am putting in a lot of faith to our next guest, hopefully he will show us his party trick of concreting peoples shoes, maybe his own… Wrestle Tattler’s Universal Champion, Angelo Deville!” With the announcement of the controversial mobster the enclosure swept into a frenzy of murmurs only fuelling the ‘Biggest Free Agent’ into making an impact, something he has done since the first day he locked on his Soprano. The Front Office table locked in general banter as the ‘Conniving Collective’ of Xavier Kannon and Gaz Green converse through the darkness, yet the real story lay hidden in the dagger-like stares of Jason O’ Neil who’s cold expressionless face pin-points every move of his nemesis. With the lights dimmed a piercing white light shone from the rafters, capturing the erect Angelo Deville like a criminal… Ironically… The buzz of conversation immediately ceased as the cocky strut of the Universal Champion ascends the raised pedestal. As always Angelo’s hair is brushed back formally and accompanying his up market portrayal he wears a tailored business suit that has a noticeable glisten as the lighting parries off the material, a subtle cough and controversy personified finally begins. 'Gooooooooooooooooooooooooooooood evening, west-- er... Right!' coughed the former Mob-Rule stable mate, a moments hiccup and instantly he is recomposed to begin again. 'Hello everyone; welcome to my celebration. I'm glad some of you could make it, as you deserve to be here with me, and I'm even happier that one has already left, for the opposite reason. This isn't the place I'd choose to hold such a prestigious ceremony, and I'm actually quite appalled I bothered to show, but hey... Sometimes you've gotta humour the people you've stepped on to make it....' jovially continued Angelo toying with his audience. 'It's been a long time coming. A very long time. So goddamn long, in fact, that all of Aimz's STD's got together and found cures for themselves. So goddamn long that Minion is almost not a virgin anymore. And it's been so goddamn long that I've stormed a world-class federation and took it over, won the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament and become Universal Champion, and have now been unemployed for months. So goddamn long... Shit... What is this -- the government? Aimz's 'hit' list? I feel like I've been in line forever....' mocked The Crimson Angel. 'It doesn't matter, though, because we're finally getting our recognition. The successful teachings of men like Snow and Rush Mason are finally being acknowledged for what they've accomplished and become. The people -- and vampires, I suppose -- terrified and molded by the immortal Hessian for this vicious competition, and eventually carved out in his image to ensure victory. Many died in the process, running away like Jason O'Neil from the spotlight, while others -- while we -- saw the light within the horror and turned it to our advantage....' The cold hearted comments of Angie only stir the bubbling pot between himself and the Dark Messiah, Jason O'Neil who continues to look with intrigue as his nemesis fuels further their war with words, a slight pause of the proceedings and Deville is ready to go again as he bellows once more down the microphone. '...The world of Wrestling Tattler has the Primetime Wrestling Academy to thank for opening the Pandora's box that is Angelo Deville. And I have a few people to thank as well... Hessian, for owning everyone's ass and supporting me throughout my tenure and beyond... Minion, for never rubbing his balls across my face while he may have thought he had the chance... Me, for being me... And finally, to all the people who've stepped into my ring and jobbed like the inferior specimens they are, whether you call yourself Xavier Kannon, Nerezza, Inphino Blitz, or Jason O'Neil, you're somewhat responsible for my success... After all, my phenomenal ability would not be so regarded if yours were a match. Thank you for your incompetence. Yes... Thank you all for being incompetent and impotent in my presence. I appreciate it more than you know... Now let us have champagne and cake to celebrate our moving up into the real world... Even if it comes nearly a year late!' As the mobster concludes with a smirk that screams 'Evil Intent' a large novelty cake is pushed before the pedestal in front of the star struck onlookers, their jaws dropping in tandem, many foaming at the mouth knowing where this may lead. The general murmur seems that the audience are about to bare witness to the two bisexual valets, once the property of Cassius Deamonte, Kathryn and Erica. A rub of hands by the males, a snarl from the girls and everything is in place as the lights go down when suddenly the top explodes from the base... 'HANDS IN THE AIR WHERE WE CAN SEE THEM DEVILLE!!!!' What? No erotic dancing, just two bottle blue police officers brandishing pistols. A surprised Angelo Deville shuffles backwards before unveiling two automatics pistols of his own from a hidden holster that nestles under his business jacket, a few screams from those in attendance and the scene resembles a Miami Vice episode. With Hessian and Minion almost a cross hair away from death, Deville used their cover as a means to escape, the mobster firing off two wayward shots leapt from the raised platform in Max Payne fashion and with an accompanying roll he scurried from the scene as the officers flimsily chased after their target. 'YOU'LL NEVER TAKE ME ALIVE SUCKERS~!' A few re-composing seconds pass and the scene looked to have vanquished and the behemoth that is Hessian stepped forward, a sigh, a roll of his eyes and he clutched the microphone before hissing his final comment, almost reluctantly one would say. A real roller coaster of events. 'That's it... Fucking Jester... Office Jester, Corporate Jester, Child Molester... You get the picture, get your green ass up here and let's get this show done and dusted before Bobby Stormbringer invades with the new Phantom Phive!' -To be continued -
