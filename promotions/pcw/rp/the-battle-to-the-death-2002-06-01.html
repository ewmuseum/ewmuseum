The Battle to the Death

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 1, 2002 The Battle to the DeathPart 1 Stress is a terrible thing. When you are not over powered by stress you remain calm, collective and not taken in by heartache. You may have heard that stress causes people to have gray hairs or even make them die suddenly. Even the death of others causes a person to be stressed out and sometimes having regrets. Regrets that they never got the opportunity to tell that person how they felt about them, even if it was good or bad. Jaden has been going through different trails and tribulations in her life. She does not feel that power that allows her to be strong and be able to strive forwards anymore. She has been aggravated with Sylo when he calls begging for forgiveness, shoving the baby in her face saying, “lets put this all behind us and think about the Angel for a change!” Jaden even had to break up with her friend, lover, Davey because she is not ready now for another relationship, because she does not want to be hurt again. She is even troubled with beauty. Sometimes Jaden is not sure if Davey loves her for her, what she is, or her beauty. Some days she wishes her beauty were mediocre so people would not approach, mistaking her for a whore, hoe, and a non-educated woman because of the way she dresses. “What is wrong with you Jaden? You cannot keep going on like this. Do not let people take advantage of you and stress you out all the time!” “I know but I just don’t know what to do, kill myself, kill them, or should I hate the world for having society look at my beauty instead of my heart, mind and soul!” “You are showing signs of Man vs. Society. You need to change that and look within you and find out what it REALLY causing them to stress you. Just take a ride around the block or something to calm your nerves. Its 10:30 PM, the traffic should be light right about now. You really need to relax, you are getting dark heavy bags under you eyes.” “Yea, that’s what I will do, get away from it all, the stress around me,” Jaden says turning the faucet on and splashing some water on her face to keep herself up. She grabs the hand towel that’s next to the sink and dries her face, while opening the medicine cabinet in the bathroom, which the mirror is hanging on. She grabs her stress pills and pops a few in her mouth before shutting the cabinet leaving the empty container on the bathroom sink. She gazes back into the mirror one last time saying, “I really need a vacation.” Jaden has been talking to her innersole in the mirror the whole time, carrying on a fluent conversation. Unexpectedly, Jaden begins to stumble holding her head while she catches her breath, “the medicine must be kicking in, I feel like a bird flying away from its trouble.” Jaden’s mind becomes a ghost, nowhere to be found, however she continues to ramble not knowing what she is talking about or saying. Jaden leaves her house falling down her steps on to the pavement, bruising her arms with many scratches. A man walking down the street sees Jaden lying on the ground as it looks like she might have twisted her ankle but she can not feel the pain because of the pills she took before leaving the house. “Do you need any help ma'am,” he asked, being a polite and concerned citizen. “Leave me the fuck alone, you dirt ass mother fucker! I hate MEN,” she says yelling in rage as the man flips her off with the bird as he continues walking down the street. Jaden stagers along trying to stand up and getting into her car so she can take a relaxing ride. She rides along the highway for a few hours as sleep pulls her in, drooping her head at every beat. She literally has to pinch herself to keep herself awake. She finally falls asleep; however the car is still in drive and the car continues to move easily from lane to lane. Jaden’s car begins to scrap along the side of the railing when it comes to a complete stop. She is in the wrong lane while a car draws near her but the driver of the car is unaware that Jaden is in her lane because she has been drink heavily and everything is a blur to her. As the car continues to come closer she final notices, that Jaden’s car is at a complete stop. She begins to honk her horn acknowledging Jaden they are about to collide. Jaden finally wakes up and starts to speedy to make a head on head collision. “I JUST WANT TO END MY LIFE NOW! I JUST WANT IT TO BE ALL OVER AND DONE WITH,” those words began to play repeatedly in Jaden head like a tape-recorder. She tries to avoid colliding with Jaden’s car but she is unsuccessful as her car hits the front of Jaden’s car and it begins to flip over. When the impact hit Jaden’s car, she finally realized what she just did; she just really waked up, from a comma, a hypnotic state. This is what the pills put on her, putting different thoughts into her head. A few mintues later the police show up to find the cars that have just been in an accident, both cars bashed and beaten. “We have a code ten, we have an injured victim and I believe she is dead. Yea, she is dead; there is no way that she could have survived this horrible accident. We have an African-American woman round the age of 21, who has probably overdosed,” the police captain says, reporting his information in his little walky-talky. “You can’t tell if she overdosed without an autopsy,” the other police officer says, replying to the captain, wondering what he is talking about. “Well I assume she has overdosed because of all these empty pill bottles on the car floor.” “Sir, we have the car but no body! “What,” the captain says, not understand what the other office is saying. “Well, I mean whoever was the driver of this car is gone. They must have left after they got into the accident…” “Shit,” the captain gets back on his walky-talky and acknowledges to the other officers around that they have a hit and run… At the bottom of the hill, which the highway is located on is a gas station. At the gas station a tall slender model like woman is talking to someone on the telephone… “Hey you got to help me. I do not think I can walk. I still feel drunk, I do not think I can make me. I have just been in an accident and I left the scene. You do not understand I had to leave the scene. My criminal record is bad and I have been in jail for this before. Meet me at the gas station on Preston Street ASAP, bye!” The woman sits on the curb as she waits for her rid to come pick her up, seeing police cars speed past her to the top of the hill where the accident is located.
