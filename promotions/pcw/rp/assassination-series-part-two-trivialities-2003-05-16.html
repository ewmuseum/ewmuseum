Assassination Series Part Two: Trivialities...

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/05/03 Blind Rage stood on the cobble Brooklyn dock surface, waiting impatiently for the police riot truck to arrive. For the vigilante’s who are not paid for their heroic services for the community are, in some ways, better of than those Superheroes who are paid for their services. It wasn’t just Blind Rage’s opinion either, it was a fact, a fact he was painfully shown needed to be remedied. Whilst they were waiting to do things through the appropriate channels, two of their number had been captured by the assassin Bullseye. The ever so deadly assassin was attempting to lead them both into a trap, he and Warrior as such, but there wasn’t much else they could do about it. It situations like his the Justice Incorporated organisation were to be paid out of the police budget and because of this a member of the team needed to be there to sign the papers to make the payment official. Blind Rage couldn’t understand it, but Linda was not allowed to sign the payment over and so they were forced to wait. Despite this fact it wasn’t just material reasons they stayed behind, the police had further requested them to stay in order to ensure the pirates capture. For some reason the New York police force were afraid of pirates. Granted its not every day you see these pirates, but this was an exceptional day for everyone involved. The distant rumblings of a heavily weighted truck could be heard in the distance and instantly the two active members of Justice Inc prepared themselves for a quick getaway. The police officers manning the barricade forced the crowd to part for the trucks entry to the crime scene. Reluctantly, the crowd parted and the large police truck safely managed to penetrate the area. To hurry matters along Blind Rage and Warrior helped the police officers to half-heartedly bundle the pirates into the back of the truck. The police chief then handed the team the papers. They were signed and Justice Inc were finally on their way to walk straight into a deadly trap. Sometimes life wasn’t fair…but on the other hand life was good. There had to be a balance between the two, otherwise it wouldn’t be life in the first place. ***** “Ugggghhhh…” The Handbag Warrior regained consciousness with a very bad headache. Memories of what previously occurred slowly returned to him, although he didn’t really know what hit him when he did remember. However, he did remember the evil face of the well known assassin Bullseye laughing manically over him, just prior to him passing out. He knew something was wrong as soon as he opened his eyes. His enchanted handbag was nowhere in the immediate area, which basically meant his power was reduced to nought. He was strapped by the wrists hanging from the ceiling. He studied the area. The building around him appeared to be some kind of old establishment, but it was quite large. Why did the villains always pick the abandoned warehouses for their evil hideouts? He thought to himself, sarcastically. Before becoming unconscious Handbag Warrior had been investigating a tip-off about seeing some kind of owl-type creature in back alleys. Blind Rage had suspected that it was the ‘Outrageous’ Owl-Man, the guy that had hired Little Willie, so he sent the Handbag Warrior and Badger Man to investigate. It turned out to be an obvious trap. The Handbag Warrior looked to his left. Badger Man was in the similar situation as he, except he had yet to regain a conscious state. “Finally regained consciousness I see.” The Handbag Warrior glanced to the far side of the room at the source of the voice. It was Bullseye. “Damn straight, sugah.” “Hahaa…and you were supposed to be one the toughest of the team. You were easy to defeat…nearly as easy as the Badger thing there.” “Wait till Blind Rage arrives, sugah.” “Ahhhh…yes Blind Rage.” “He’ll tear you a new one, sugah.” “If your trying to suggest that I do not count on him showing up here today, you’re very much mistaken. In fact I openly gave away the address to this place.” “What was the point in that, sugah?” “Because Justice Inc have a price on their heads…and I intend to be the first one to collect.” “You mean there’s more than one assassin on our tails?” “Indeed. I just hope the other two manage to get here safely, so that I can kill them before anybody else.” “Don’t you worry about that, sugah.” Handbag Warrior said solemnly. “He’ll be here.” “Good” Bullseye replied, as he took a rather large looking knife into his hand, patting it menacingly on the other. “Because I have a little surprise for them.” Handbag Warrior rolled his eyes. “No much of a surprise dumbass, you practically told them it was a trap.” With a flick of his wrist and a blur of action the knife propelled it’s way across the room in the general direction of Handbag Warrior, but instead of the blade embedding itself within his vulnerable structure, the knife sliced through the rope that was holding him up, making the camp superhero fall fifteen feet to the unforgiving wooden floor. “Less of the lip, homo.” ***** Blind Rage and Warrior quickly strode through the crowd, who were still hanging around to see what was happening, despite the spectacle ending over an hour ago. Some of the crowd were decidedly disheartened because they wouldn’t stop to sign autographs, but a lot more was at stake than hurting a few people’s feelings. However, Blind Rage knew that it would come back to haunt them one day. The two made good time jogging a couple of blocks away from the docks, to where the Justice Inc van was parked. Upon reaching the rusty, petrol consuming monster, Blind Rage held his hand out, expecting Warrior to place the keys in his palm, however, Warrior simply stared at him. “I ain’t got the keys dude.” Warrior unnecessarily declared. “But I gave them to you as soon as we got out of the van.” “And I gave them back to you when we got to the docks.” “That’s a blatant lie…I mean…” Blind Rage was forced to silence, when the unexpected occurred. The van exploded with a massive bang. Luckily, Warrior had been covered by Blind Rage’s ample exterior, so he was covered from some of the blow, but that didn’t stop the blast from propelling the two hapless heroes across the street. Obviously the word had reached the dirty underbelly of the criminal under ground. The short journey would undoubtedly be a lot tougher than they expected if, in fact, they could survive the current onslaught.
