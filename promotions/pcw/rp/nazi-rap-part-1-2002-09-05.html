Nazi Rap (part 1)

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.09.02 The scene opens in the same suburban neighborhood as Duzza and Mark Makonie threw the first ever party where nazi’s were alongside thugs and no one caught a kerbing. The scene moves closer to Mark Makonies house. It is completely painted in white, with a white picket fence. The front door is painted white, the garage door is painted white and there is a white BMW parked in the driveway and the curtains are drawn, giving the idea that Makonie has yet to wake up. The sky is gray, but quite a few people, all white, are walking along the streets. In the background, loud obnoxious hip hop music can be heard, getting closer and closer until a big black jeep pulls up outside the house. This is where the music is coming from. A muscular, black man gets out of the car. This man is obviously Duzza. He is dressed in a pair of very baggy blue jeans, with one leg rolled up, a baggy Yankee’s shirt and a big fuck off platinum chain, with a diamond encrusted “D” hanging from the bottom of the chain. Duzza’s hair is cornrow’ed and he has black peak cap on backwards. After looking around, probably looking out for neo-nazis or other kerbin’ givers, Duzza walks up the white path leading to the front door and knocks on the door. At first there is no answer, so Duzza knocks again and again. Duzza: “I know this fool is up in here… mother fucker best open the door before I get ghetto on his ass and break in…” Duzza looks around and notices some stones. He picks them up and after looking around to see if anyone is looking at him, he throws the stones at the top window. Duzza: “OI MAKONIE!” Duzza picks up a bigger stone, and throws it hard at the window, smashing the window. An expression of shock comes across Duzza and he starts to hop around nervously. Duzza: “Awww shiiiit… he’s gonna kick my ass now…. Should I run….should I be a man and stick around… run..be a man…run or be a man… fuck this” Duzza sprints to his car and is about to jump in when an angry white guy opens a different window than the one that was smashed. This man, looking quite sleepy, is obviously Mark Makonie. He notices Duzza and hollers at him Makonie: “OI DUZZA” Duzza mutters “aww shit” under his breath, before turning around, putting on the best innocent expression he can do Duzza: “Errr… hi” Makonie: “I heard something smash. Did you see anything?” Duzza: “Errrr… naw dawg. Naw, I ain’t seen shit dawg. Naw, for real, I only jus’ got here bro. I ain’t seen anything man” Makonie: “Ok… Wait. What the fuck are you doing here? I know your scared of the suburbs” Duzza: “I ain’t scared of shit dawg. I just ain’t tryin’ to catch a kerbin’ that’s all. I got something to ax you, come down and let me in” Makonie: “The answer is no and your not coming into my house” Duzza: “Aww c’mon dawg” Makonie: “No. Now go away. It’s too early for this shit” Duzza: “It’s 11am dawg. And c’mon, let me in dawg” Makonie: “Look, I told you, your not coming in my house. Now what do you want” Duzza: “I’ve got a master scheme, to make us both multi-millionaires” Makonie: “Is this like your ‘master scheme’ where we tried to outrun the police so that you could get on that tv show ‘the worlds dumbest criminals’?” Duzza: “Heh, don’t act like you didn’t enjoy that shit… and naw, it ain’t anything like that. You won’t even have to hot wire a car or anything” Makonie: “Ok… I’m going to get dressed and then I’ll come down and open the door” Duzza: “Wait… are you saying that to get rid of me? Or are you actually coming down” Makonie: “Oh shut the fuck up… I’ll be down there in a minute” Duzza sits down on the front step and about 5 minutes later, Makonie opens the door. Duzza goes to step into the house but Makonie stops him Makonie: What’re you doing?” Duzza: “Err… trying to get into your house?” Makonie: No… We’ll talk out here” Duzza: “A’ite whatever” Makonie: “Ok, now what was your ‘master scheme’?” Duzza: “A’ight check this shit out. Y’know how hip hop has taken over the world and now your more likely to see a white boy dressed like a thug than a punk?” Makonie: “How could I fail to notice how the African American culture has taken over and crime rates have gone up and the general standard of living for decent white folks like myself has never been lower?” Duzza: “Err… was that a yes?” Makonie: “Yes, it was a yes” Duzza: “A’ite dawg, check this out. I used to do a lil’ bit of producing, back in the day” Makonie: “Where? In jail?” Duzza: “Would you let me finish? A’ight, so I get a white rapper, and I promote him in the south? Y’know, the southern, dirty south, racist states. Mississippi, Kentucky, like that. And we promote him to a white audience, it’ll be like a white Public Enemy, you know them right?” Makonie: “I’ve heard of them yes” Duzza: “Well, I thought, who could be better than you!” Makonie: “Me? A rapper? Skinheads don’t rap” Duzza: “Yeah well… yo shit will be groundbreaking” Makonie: “I can’t write songs. Ah well, looks like I’ll have to give this a miss, see you Friday Duzza, bye” Duzza: “WAIT! We got the lyrics done for ya. C’mon dawg, you’ll be famous, in Kentucky at least” Makonie: “I’m on tv every week you idiot. I’m already famous” Duzza: “Ah well… theres a lot of fine KKK bitches down there, sure they’d love to be wit ya… but that’s life… see ya Friday” Makonie: “Wait… ok… but I’m only doing this for money and women…” Duzza: “Err….that’s the only reason anybody does anything” Makonie: “OK… when will we go to the studio?” Duzza: “Now” Makonie: “Now? No, sorry, I got to be somewhere” Duzza: “No you don’t dawg, now let’s go dawg, C’mon, we gotta get to the studio before the women leave, nawmean? Hehe” Makonie cannot help to laugh, he then locks the door behind him and walks to the car. Duzza starts up the engine, just then Makonie notices the smashed window. Makonie: “Holy shit! What happened to my window? It’s smashed. Shit, I can’t go to the studio I have to go fix my home” Duzza: “What window? Naw, you trippin’ dawg” Just as Makonie is about to step out of the car, Duzza speeds off, cutting up a car and heads to the studio as Makonie looks back in horror at the smashed window at his house.
