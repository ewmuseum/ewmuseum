On the road to recovery (Part 3)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 9, 2002 We now arrive at the weight stations, many people are pumping iron…a lot of iron. Eddie, who is still reeling somewhat from the very quick and very long run on the treadmill that he had. Spider, who still holds onto the apple and mango juice that he ordered earlier while Eddie was exhausting himself. They both walk into the area, and Eddie lets out a massive groan. Eddie is sweating bullets and his workout shirt is laden with perspiration…well, even more than normal. Eddie seems very weary of everyone in the room. “Spider, I could kill all these guys in a split second” Eddie states. “Eddie these guys aren’t your sparring partners, even though sparring is next on the docket. This area, we will worry about strength conditioning. Upper body today, Lower body tomorrow…” “Look, when I was living in the death trap, I used sacks of concrete, stones, drywall, anything that I could get my hands on within my area in order to train. However, since I’d rather concentrate on agility and speed, as opposed to strength, I don’t train with weights that often.” Eddie says. “Is that so? Well, in pro wrestling, people would rather see large muscular guys display their power, as opposed to having 155 pound midgets go through tables. If they wanted that, they’d watch Raw, but they aren’t. They want to see you damn near kill people in the ring, while putting your own body on the line. Enough talk, you have a match to train for against Kramer Wild Wing, and Charles Loc…” “HOW THE HELL AM I GOING TO BEAT THEM IF I’M DEAD TIRED FROM ALL THIS FUCKING TRAINING?!” Eddie interrupts. “Calm Down, first off. Have some Gatorade. Then get on the Bench-press. I’ll set the optimum weight for ya.” Spider concludes. Eddie heads to a vending machine, which dispenses various flavours of Gatorade. Eddie inserts two dollars, and hits the button. A few quiet clunking noises are heard, and out spews a fruit punch flavoured Gatorade. Eddie cracks it open and chugs it down while walking back to the bench-press. Spider has it set and he’s ready for Eddie. “Uhm…uhhh….” “What, Eddie?” “How do you work this thing?” “It’s simple. You lay down on the bench, and you grab onto the weight. I’ll help you get it off the locking struts, and you do about twenty to thirty reps with the weight you have on there” Spider explains. “Ohh, whenever I did this, I had two large stones on Rebar, and I was on the ground when I did it” Eddie says. “I see, well?” Spider concludes once again. Eddie takes a seat on the bench, and sets himself properly. Spider helps him with the weight off the struts. Spider holds it up…then lets go. The rod comes crashing down onto the chest, and it rolls onto his neck. “GAHHHGH! GET…IT…OFF..ME!!!” Eddie chokes out. Spider quickly takes the bar off his neck and places it back onto the struts. Eddie sits up immediately, and coughs to get his breath back. “What was the weight?” “My standard, 765 pounds.” “765?! ARE YOU FUCKING TRYING TO KILL ME?!” “NO! EDDIE! AND CALM DOWN, YOU’RE GOING TO MAKE A SCENE!” Spider says assertively. “ENOUGH OF THIS! Get me some sparring partners. I’m not weight training today, just get me some people to spar with me.” “Already set in the next room. Let’s go.” Spider and Eddie leave the weight room and enter the real training room. We find out that the weight club is more than that. It is a multi-denominational dojo that supports numerous styles, such as Karate, Aikido, Judo, and a few others. It is abandoned right now, simply because Spider paid the gym a few bucks in order to book it for an hour. He also paid a lot of people to become Eddie’s sparring partners. There was a makeshift wrestling ring built as well…but it is still mostly blue mats. They enter and the sparring partners are in the other side of the room. “Eddie, I would like to introduce you to my personal troop of sparring partners. I’ve had these guys since the beginning of my career, after I became MWWF World Champ.” Spider said. “These guys don’t seem so tough.” “On the contrary, they are extremely challenging. Anyway, let’s get started.” Spider rings the bell, and the first competitor enters the ring. Eddie stands at the ready in the corner. His first partner charges in, but ends up eating the ring post. Eddie delivers a leg sweep to take down the opponent and immediately delivers an axe kick to the throat. The opponent rolls around holding onto his neck. “WHOA! HOLD ON!” “What?!” “NON LETHAL EDDIE!” “Great, so I have to go non-lethal. Okay, send in the next victim.” Spider rings the bell and the next opponent comes running in, Eddie is up, but drops to the ground again after grabbing an arm. He locks in an armbar, and quickly snaps the arm in two places. The sparring partner grabs his arm, and yelps in pain. He quickly rolls out of the make shift ring. “EDDIE! NON LETHAL AND NO INJURIES!” “GOD! YOU WANT ME TO MAKE STRAW FROM GOLD!?” “No, I want you to wrestle these guys…not fight them…we are not in a mixed martial arts federation. This is pro wrestling, and there are major differences. Now…Do exactly what you would do in the ring against the next two guys. But remember…Non-lethal…and releasing holds on tap outs. Got it?” Spider asks. “I’ve got it. Care to send the next opponent in?” Spider rings the bell and the next two enter the ring, and this time Eddie restrains himself, and actually acts like he’s in a real match. Eddie is performing head-locks, and arm drags, and many classic wrestling wear-down moves. “Good…Good!” Spider encourages. Eddie begins to go onto the offensive as he takes to the air with his high-flying moves. Planchas, Ranas, even a 6-1-9! “Wow! You’re better than I thought. Go to the ground now Eddie. Wear down!” Spider instructs. Now Eddie begins the ground attack. Going with old school headlocks, face rakes, many moves that you don’t normally see. They are effective nonetheless. “Eddie…END IT NOW!” He switches from one move to the Agoniser almost seamlessly. The opponent taps out immediately. Spider rings the bell. “You know Eddie, I haven’t seen anyone like you, you are ready for the match. Great job everyone, hit the showers!” Everyone leaves, except for Spider. Eddie is the most impressive specimen I have ever seen…There is only one way to prove his worth to the athletic community… Spider heads to a phone, and dials out a number. “Hello, Mr. Goldwyn? I have a proposition for you…” Fin.
