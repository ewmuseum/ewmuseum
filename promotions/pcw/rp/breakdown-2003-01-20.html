Breakdown

20 January 2003

Date: 1/20/03 Breakdown My weaknesses follow me and I don’t know how to make them disappear. It’s like my world has already ended and everyone’s playing a trick on me. I walk down the street and people murmur my name, when I myself can’t even bring myself to say them. I used to be the type of man that made me proud. I look in the mirror and I hate what I see. I used to be young, pure, and willing to have faith. Now I sit along day in and day out wondering how it all went wrong. I look at pictures from the past, but leave my thumb accented over the outline of my face. It’s just too hard…. I want to change, but I’ll never escape the thoughts in the back of my head. I tear my memories into pieces and let them burn to powdery ashes. Yet I still fear down the road they will haunt me once again. I become useless to the world. I pull my hair screaming then fall to my knees looking for some type of sign. Is the pain supposed to go away? Everyone tells me I’ll be okay and that I shouldn’t worry. Look in these eyes and say those words. My sclera is blood stricken. It cracks under pressure like it’s beholder and if it could speak it would be screaming infidelities heard in the highest of land. My iris shows how paralyzed my inner soul really is. It’s empty and the color fades along with all the life once in me. My pupil is black symbolizing the death of the young man once in me. The reflection you see in my eyes is the reflection of sadness. You can tell a lot by a persons eyes, but you can tell ever more by a touch. A touch I die plant on the chest of God’s most beautiful creatures. Not a touch of sin, but rather a grace of love. Love… It’s the word that torments me in my sleep. It wakes me like a bullet of lighting stretching through my chest. I scream, oh yes I scream. My lungs bleed for acceptance, my throat become weak, my hands are dry like dirt, my face is shaking in emotion, and the tear that should be rolling from my cheek doesn’t come to me. I hold my fists out and beat myself. I HIT AND HIT AND HIT! I blame YOU! I pray to you, I sing to you, I do everything you tell me to do, and this is what you give me. I save my pain bottled up inside for you my Lord. I’ve waited at least twenty years and you’ve shown me nothing, NOTHING! What will it take? I speak these current words with my voice trembling and fingers shaking. I look at these real life movies and wish, HELL I STARVE, to LIVE. Living isn’t being a wrestler living is being a human. I AM NOT HUMAN! I tear at my skin shedding blood from my palm. I let it drip on the wood paneling of the floor in my very own home. IS THIS HOW IT FELT? I don’t feel anything. I can kill myself and I won’t feel anything. I starve for pain, because pain seems like the only thing I was born to know. Give me a signal, or I’ll lose the little faith I’m left with. Hear me scream! AHHHHHHHHHHHHH! It doesn’t matter, I scream until my hearts content. Why? It’s because no one here’s it. And why does no one hear it? No one hears a thing because I am ALONE. I grow motionless in my speech as this word breezes through my lips. ALONE! Don’t make like you don’t know what I feel. I feel it every day, every minute, every second, every blink, breath, thought, and cry. I don’t want to see my name in lights. I want to see my name to the right of a woman’s that I love. I want to see myself a positive role model with children at home to tend to. I want to throw my past away and look to the future. YOU DON’T LET ME DO THAT! I ask for an answer I get nothing. I look for a fixture I get nothing. I pray for a sign I get nothing. What do I mean to you, because I’m starting to think I mean NOTHING. Is this how you repay me? I don’t have the strength to last any longer. I sit day in and day out. I search day in and day out. I can’t do it any longer. The last sting has been drawn. I clutch my fingers together and kneel in front of the trickling fire under a painting of the deep blue sea I can’t take my eyes off of. I see myself in the reflection of the fire and watch as my eyes change to a dark red color. I smile in unhdispleasure. This is a smile that will not disappear.
