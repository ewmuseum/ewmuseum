The City Awakes At 6am

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd October 2002 “What you doing…I said I want some fuckin’ food!” I screamed at the top of my lungs as the plump Japanese Businessman sat on the chair in the corner watching me. “What they paying you for?! HUH?!” I took a deep breath and began to scream again, “GET ME SOME FUCKIN’ BREAKFAST!!” I not believe this… It 6am on a Thursday morning in New York and I being woken up by some Japanese Businessman who has been hired to help me “take care of business” with PCW, and he not even get me food when I ask him too…What The Fuck?! I about to scream at him again when I realize that by the time I’d finished yelling I’d have the rest of the apartment yelling at me to be quiet. I not know about many other people but I enjoy my sleep and when someone wake me up early….I cut sick! Just as I about to get up I slump back down in the pillow my bed and realize that if I move the white sheet then I going to be half naked in front of some Japanese man who doesn’t stop smiling…. What the hell going on here?! for some reason I didn’t want to question myself any further into why this Japanese guy was watching me sleep….all I wanted at the moment was some privacy to “wake up” and some food. My stomach began to growl beneath the covers of the white bed sheets, Alcohol not sit well on an empty stomach!! I clutched at it to make it stop growling when I noticed the Businessman’s pair of beady eyes look at me from behind his thick glasses. “You hear that….” “Did I hear what Master Loc?” the Businessman finally spoke in a posh Japanese accent in fluent English. I smirked as I beckoned the man to come over next to my bed….no I not planning on doing anything sexual with him!! He cautiously got up out of his chair in the corner and approached the bed side as I lurched out and grabbed him around the neck with both my hands as he began to cough and splutter. “My stomach saying it time you GET…ME…SOME….FOOD!!” I swear I woke up the whole 10 stories of the apartment block… Finally as I released the Businessman he got the message, picking himself up off of the floor, adjusting his suit and glasses before bowing to me and ushering over to the door. Time to have some fun… As he slid the door open I heard him gasping for some air and I couldn’t resist the streak I was on. “Hey Fat Man, Come back here for second!!” The man paused at the door and slowly turned around grimacing and waiting for me to blast him again or try and throttle him once more. “What your name?” I calmed down a little and tried to be polite. The man began to stutter but he managed to stammer “U-u-udai “ “Well Udai…you going to die if you bring me some Chicken Laksa within the next 15 minute! You got that?!” I couldn’t help but smirk at the end of the sentence. “Y-y-yes Master Loc” he bowed once more and wiped a drop of sweat from his brow as I began to feel a little sorry for him. “Oh and you can get yourself something to….but nothing too expensive! I have to pay taxes you know!” I kind of regretted what I said. What the fuck up with this?! for the third time in a few minutes the man bowed again before scurrying through the door and closing it behind him, leaving me alone in my bedroom. I laid back on my pillow and spread my body out over the double bed as the sounds and breeze of the New York City below my window began to waft in, causing me to slowly drift back off to sleep…. BEEP~! I bolted up-right in my bed as I heard the sound of a horn from the street below which was soon followed by a rather menacing yelp from upstairs. “PEOPLE ARE TRYING TO SLEEP YOU INCONSIDERATE PRICK!!” I gritted my teeth before lashing back out at the angry resident, “BLOW ME…” Stupid White Boys…. I grumbled to myself as I shifted my body out of bed before moving over to the shower in my ensuite, for the first time in my life, my day had begun before 12 o’clock! -=***=- How I supposed to work with Fat Man sniffing my ass all the time?! How they expect me to help out PURE and TokyoPop when I made to fight for Xavier Kannon and PCW?! I not get how these Japanese or White Boys operate….this shit me of! I not know how long I going to have to put up with this shit…but if I wake up one more time with Fat Man in my room or near my door, I going to break his face! I already warned him to stay out of my way…. Maybe this just a test to try my patience, maybe it just something that TokyoPop done to make sure that I not going to get there money! Pffft, I going to show them!! -=***=- I just finish putting my jeans and tank top on when I heard a knock at the door. I seriously not get this…he rude enough to sit in my room, but not rude enough to barge into my apartment?! The handle twisted as I pulled the door open and the Fat Man himself Udai nearly fell through the door, keeping the Laksa from spilling his hands on his way through. In his hands he had a newspaper, my Styrofoam bowl of Chicken Laksa, a small plastic container of Fried Rice and a wad of notes, I not waste any time and snatched everything from his hands leaving the Fried Rice and making myself comfortable at my small breakfast table. “Sorry I’m late Master Loc” the man mumbled an apology as he closed the door and took off his shoes before pulling up the chair next to me. Not being one for conversation whilst eating I grunted towards him before commanding him, “Go press play on stereo…and pump it up loud! I want to be able to feel the bass through this table!”“Yes Master Loc” the man didn’t even begin to eat his Fried Rice as he got up and pressed play on the large wall unit I had situated across the room in the lounge area. The song “21 Seconds” by So Solid Crew began to blare out through the speakers as I steadily ate my Laksa and the Businessman began to eat his Fried Rice across the table from me. This not so bad… I thought to myself, At least he well-trained!! that second thought bought a smirk across my face. I not know why this “Udai” put up with the way I treating him, after all, all he have to do is report back to TokyoPop saying that I not doing things right and I out of a job….all the same I not complaining!! As far as I concerned he can slave me until I die…. My Laksa continued to be slurped down as Udai reached across the table and grabbed at the newspaper which was sitting in front of me. As soon as his hand was within an inch away from it I lurched out and grabbed it, stopping him in mid rest before picking up the remote to my wall unit with my spare hand and stopping the song, I such a lazy du mah! “What you think you doing?” I glared across the table at Udai. Udai gulped as the fear began to show in his eyes, “Reading the paper…..” “Shouldn’t you be ‘advising’ me?” I replied. “I’m only to advise you when you actually fight! Until then I’m to protect and serve you as you are the ‘X-Factor’ between PURE and PCW…being the only Champion in PURE” Udai trailed on. A thought began to cross my mind, “Where is my Title?” Udai didn’t take his eyes off me or speak another word, he just pointed a shaky finger towards my bedroom door as I nodded my head and took another sip of my Laksa. “Go and polish it…I want to be able to see my face in it by the time I finished here!” I ordered him and released his hand, “Then I let you read the paper!” I smirked as Udai nodded his head before scrambling out of his seat and into my bedroom before re-appearing with the PURE Jnr. Heavy Weight Title in his hands. My index finger extended towards the couch as Udai sat down and produced a white handkerchief from the breast pocket of his suit jacket, he began to rub the gold plating on the Title as I looked on with impress. I got to hand it to them, these Japanese….they know how to serve!! I smirked once more as I thought about the money and service TokyoPop was offering me to secure PURE’s win at Red October. Something soon snapped me back into reality as the phone began to ring and light up on the counter of my kitchen, the bling bling of every ring sounding out through my two bedroom apartment and blending in with traffic and noise from outside. I looked across at Udai as he nodded his head before quickly scrambling up and onto his feet and answering the phone as I waited on as he spoke. “Yes Master Loc is here….” His voice trailed off. There was some muffled speaking from the other side of the phone.“No I don’t think he is wanting to speak with you….” His voice trailed off again. This time the muffled speaking from the other side of the phone was loud as the man was screaming, Udai looked on in shock and was about to hang up the phone as I extended my hand. Yes that mean “Give It To Me”….stupid White Boy! I snatched the phone out of his hands as he went back to the couch and polishing my Title. “Who this?” I asked. There was a chuckle from the other line followed by a familiar mans voice, “We’re not going to be too happy if we lose at Red October Charlie!” “My name is Jung, what you want?!” I began to get aggravated. “We want you to get off your ass and start doing something! PCW is not going to let PURE walk out of Red October with a victory…if they do you are in deep shit! Understood?” the man sounded serious. “You fuckin’ with me?!” There was a sigh from the other side of the phone as the man replied, “This is your first and last warning Charlie, you make PCW win and you’ll be fine! We know everything, so don’t try to screw us over! You’re with PCW now Charlie….live with it!” ”You want trouble?!” I screamed into the phone. There was a chuckle as the man replied, “Have a nice day Chuck….” The phone began to beep as the man hung up and I slammed the phone down onto the hook as I glared over at Udai who quickly glanced down before polishing my Title in a hurry. I began to slow down my breathing as I got out of my fit of rage and began to think things through about the phone call I had just received. I thought out loud as I felt my face twist into a Sinister Grin, “Guess we just going to have to see who the higher bidder is…..PCW or PURE I not fussed, as long as I the one getting paid I not care who I fighting for….”
