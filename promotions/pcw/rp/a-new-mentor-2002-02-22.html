A new Mentor

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/02/02 Learning, it is the key to life, without it we would know nothing, you cannot know until you learn. Learning is probably the most important thing there is, for what other way would you have skills. People have many and varied skills, many don’t even realize they have them, but that doesn’t stop them from learning more about them. Sometimes we learn when we don’t even know it, and it is guaranteed that we will learn something new everyday. Learning equals skill equals responsibility… Anton was nervous, he had done many things, but this was new to him. His knees felt slightly weak and he felt quite hot. The sun was blazing mediocre, the sky was overcast but it didn’t look like it was rain. The day was neither hot nor cold, yet Anton felt extremely warm. The streets of Manhattan were packed; everybody seemed to be out on this average day. Anton made his way across the busy streets. He wore rather strange attire, he was wearing a black shirt with a Japanese symbol on it, and he also wore baggy black bottoms and what looked like black slippers. He also had a red rope around his waist; this indicated he was wearing Martial Arts Attire, which looked quite strange. He also had a blue Nike sports bag ion his right shoulder. He walked swiftly, taking in big breaths as he walked; a single stand of his was down of his forehead, a sweat gland stringed down it. He was now at a halt, in front of him was a narrow alleyway, blocked the light from it, made it seem dark and eerie. He entered it and then came to a side door at his left. He went through this door and opened it up into a small room with a desk, pictures of great Martial Arts Legends, and Asian writing over the walls. In front of a brown oak desk set a man of small stature, with a white robe on him. He had a Japanese face, with narrow eyes and tanned skin; his hair was black with grey parts starting at the sides. He had a black pen in his hand and was writing on white parchment. He e looked up, his eyebrow raised, and he got off his seat. “Anton, how nice of you to come.” The man says in good English, yet with a strong cent of Japanese in his voice. Anton walks over to the desk and puts his hand put, the Japanese man responds by shaking it. “Thank you Tsunami, I have waited a long time to meet you.” Anton says in his most polite tone. The man smiles and then sits back down. “Well, I have matured some of the greatest Martial Artist, why should I take you under my wing?” He says, obviously trying to test Anton. Anton thinks for a moment… “Well, as you know, I am a professional wrestler, and the youngest ever at that. I have great high-flying and athletic moves, plus I haven some Martial Arts skills, but I want to extend that so I can become more dominate in the ring, I find Martial Arts is the greatest form of fighting, plus it can release unwanted tension and help you with a lot of physical and mental problems. And due to my High-Flying and Athletic ability I think it would come quite easy to me.” Anton says as if he had just rimed it all off. The Tsunami seems impressed. He smiled and then hesitated. “That is a very good response Anton, I shall take you under my wing, but you must be willing to learn from me and do what ever it takes to succeed.” “I will I promise you Tsunami I will.” Anton says full-heartedly with almost a desperate tone in his voice. Tsunami stared at Anton, for what seemed like five minutes to Anton, his small, narrow brown eyes gazed into Anton’s big, bright blue ones. There was a tension in the room, though it felt like a good tension, Anton had been studying Martial Arts since he was six, he had learned all he could and had won many awards before becoming a wrestler. He loved it, it was a great way to express so many things, and a great form of fighting, Anton’s quickness, agility and athleticism would make him the perfect Martial Artist, and he hoped he could use it to a fuller extent tom reach the top in PCW. “I welcome you aboard Anton.” Tsunami said eventually, releasing Anton from his thoughts. Anton smiles… “Oh Thanks you Tsunami, I promise you won’t regret it, I will work my hardest!” Anton shouts. “You had better.” Tsunami says playfully, though with a hint of seriousness in his voice. “We shall start our first lesson today, be in the gym in fifteen minutes, if you want to get yourself together first, there is a locker room, over there.” Tsunami says pointing to a small door at the right of a short corridor. “Okay.” Anton says quickly before walking towards the Locker room. When he enters in he finds it is rather small. There are five hairs and five wardrobes; it is obviously a multiple person locker room. The chairs and wardrobe were made of what looked like a very old wood, and the walks were painted light blue. The floor was covered in green tiles. Anton put the Nike Sports bag that he had on one of the seats and sat down on the other, he was already fully kitted out for his lessons so he didn’t have to change, he would just stay in here to get his head together and get ready to be taught by the greatest Martial Arts teacher in the world. Tsunami Chow-Lee was a 51 years old former world Martial Arts Champion, though he was very young when he won the title. He finished his career early and started training people, one by one, so far he had secretly taken five under his wing and all of been some of the most successful men in Martial Arts history, he was a truly exquisite teacher, but not very well known, Anton only got wind of him through his Uncle, who had lived in Japan for most of his life and was a Martial Artist himself. It was a great honour for Anton to learn from such a man, and Anton was truly looking forward to it. It would be a new thing for Tsunami as well as Anton was the first wrestler had ever taught. There was one thing Anton would make sure, and that was that he would try his hardest, ads such and opportunity rarely came and when it did you must grasp t tightly, until there is no breathe left within it. Anton hoped to have a few good moves ready for Friday, as he would be facing the Mexican Devil, El Diablo in the final round of the March Madness tournament, if he could somehow grasp a win, it would out him into the tournament it’s self where he would have the chance to win the one and only PCW World title. It would be the greatest match Anton would probably ever have the chance to participate in, and he didn’t intend on letting such an opportunity pass with blind eyes. Though Fusion would not be the only time Anton would meet El Diablo, in Tuesday Turmoil, they would once again collide, in a standard match, one would be hoping the claim vengeance of the other. Anton was glad he would be facing the one opponent in a row, as it meant less scouting and more training. He knew El Diablo to perfection now. Anton now knew every move the Mexican Devil did, from the moment before he did it. He knew every move from the Lunasault, to the El Fantistico, to the Bomba. Though then came the deadly finisher, Diablo called it the Mephistopheles, which was a technical but amazing move where he spins and spears his opponent from the top rope, it was a lethal move that would surely win the match for Diablo if he hit that it on Anton. Anton would just have to stay out of the way of the Ultra Loco, which was his set-up move, and a very impressive style of Piledriver. All together, Anton thought that Diablo could match him is athleticism, but Anton might have the grasp on speed and high-flying moves, though a bit of Martial Arts could go a long way in the match. El Diablo weighed in at 207lbs, which meant he would have the strength advantage, but Anton didn’t intend on letting that be a big factor in both matches, his speed would hopefully keep him away from El Diablo’s superior strength. Anton came out of his trance to realize that his time was up and he had to make his way to the gym. He got up off his seat and stretches, and also did a few practice kicks, which cut through the air, like a sharp blade. He made his way out f the room, out of the corridor, into the office, and then into the gym. It was quite a large room, with no windows, and a lot of equipment and space for fighting. Anton saw Tsunami standing there, with his full fighting attire on; he had the traditional white suit with a black belt to symbolize his expertise. As soon as Anton entered he walked up to him. “We shall start with re most basic Martial Art, Judo. Judo is a great Martial Art, it helps you when you are trapped, and in a heads lock of submission move, and it also helps with a lot of technical fighting. Judo consists of different arrays of flips, reversals and takedowns. There is no punching and kicking that will come later. Now, have you ever been taught any Judo in the past?” He asked at the end of his speech. “Yeah, I learned some and have got to the brown belt stage.” Anton replies. “Well, that is very good, so you will know the basics. Now let me see some moves from you.” He says walked over to one of the mats. Anton walks up to him, he remembers all the moves he had learned, he tries one of his best, a reverse takedown through extended arm-lock. He tries this, but is immediately reverses by Tsunami, and Anton ends up on the ground. Tsunami extends his hand out to help Anton up, he takes it, and is soon on the found again through a simple trip. “First rule of Martial Arts, never extend your hand to your opponent during warfare, after and before, but never when you are engaged in it” Tsunami says hastily. Anton gets up, and mentally jots Tsunami’s words down. The two continue to battle with Judo moves, Anton rarely succeed in beating the former Martial Arts world champion, but any time he did, he knew he was coming one step closer to achieving his dream in PCW, an every time he lost he knew he was learning so he could succeed in the future. “A great warrior must always know what he is doing, the mind is the most vital part of warfare, the strongest of all opponents may be physically the strongest of all, but if he fights with his body alone, he will not succeed against the man who uses his head to fight. You are extremely smart Anton, so use your mind in your battle against the one they call El Diablo, he is known to be quite idiotic, so you must take advantage and try to be the more cunning. Then you will be victorious!” Were Tsunami’s last words before Anton left his first training session. It had been a hard day but a brilliant day for Anton, he had learned so much, and he couldn’t wait until the next day. He was now ready of El Diablo on both Fusion and Turmoil, he would give it his all, and could only pray for wins, he had learned a lot of Judo in one day and hoped to use it in his matches. The prize of winning of Fusion would be momentous, a great reward, a chance to win the PCW World Title, what else could any wrestler ask for. He would love it, but El Diablo, the Mexican Devil stood in his way, and he had to do his best to get passed him. Through his already present wrestling skills, his new Judo techniques, and his Crimson, Irish blood, he would rise to the top, Diablo had better be ready, for Anton’s day would come… Tiocfaigh Ar La!
