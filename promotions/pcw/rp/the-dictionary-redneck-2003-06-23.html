The Dictionary Redneck?

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/23/03 “What is the difference between PCW's resident redneck, Abner, and your average, typical ‘hillbilly’?” one might ask. Well the answer is simple. And furthermore, the answer is clear. You see, deep back in the luscious evergreen forests of Vermont, high above the hills, hidden almost cautiously, seemingly deliberately, behind the fruit of nature’s beauty, down the old dirt road, behind the old burnt-down, worn-away, crumbling wooden barn of their youth, grows a special brand of redneck, a rare sub-specie of hick: The Vermonter. And even more rare than this, an isotope of all isotopes in the human genome pool, is the Fighting Hick. A redneck so valued, and so rare, it’s only measurable equivalent in the elemental world is the rare Uranium isotope of U-235. And like U-235, the Fighting Redneck is highly explosive, carrying many times over an ability to destroy above and beyond it’s own “weight”, and indeed, like an atomic weapon, the aftermath of an explosion of this type of redneck, the fallout, is massive. But of all the comparisons, at a certain point, it ends. You see, unlike U-235, the Fighting Redneck, more specifically, the kind of the Vermont Hick sub-species, is not manmade. He is selfmade, and carved to perfection... “Damn straight” Abner thinks, leaning back and folding his hands behind his head. Sitting here, in the company of his lonesome, he tips back in his chair, and stares down at his desk, thinking of what it means to be a member of this rare, and malignant breed of hick. You see all across the country, and indeed, all across the world, this rare and endangered specie is losing members at an incalculable rate. Yes, it is true, to put it bluntly, that, in their own terms, rednecks “breed like rabbits”, but still, the birthrate cannot possibly measure up to the fatality rate, and certainly, this is due to unnatural causes. Progress, mainly. Progress is that which makes us humans. But it is also that which makes us robots. You see, the most well-known, most versed, quoted bible of them all -and indeed, the one that rednecks follow- is the bible of natural law. It is the governing document underlying all in our lives as human beings, and is written and forged in out blood -literally. It is encoded in our DNA, and lived in not mere thought, but in feeling as well. And this is the religion of the redneck: put this before everything, and all else. Even before logic. And in doing so, your average backwoods, inbred, beer-guzzling, tobacco-spitting hick, rejects the idea of society. And inasmuch as this, one would conclude that they are anarchists, but hypocritically, they are very much law-abiding at the same time. And indeed, this is very much the case. For instance, a redneck walks into a store, buys a can of tobacco. After chewing it down, spitting it out, he is satisfied. He did this because he wanted to, even though, in general, most people in society oppose “big tobacco”. He’s content with the outcome, so there is no hypocrisy, right? Wrong. For in following the rules set by the game masters of the establishment, by working for wages, contributing to a cause, accepting his paycheck for doing so, and for forking over the $5.19 at the local village store, he plays right into it...he lets the hand of society feed him, and in return, he works for it. Us, on the other hand, the “civilized” ones, have our own bible: reason. Now reason, at its core, is not merely logic and the mathematical components of it, which allow us to apply such formulas to our everyday lives, but include the aspect of emotion, too. We recognize emotion, and realize it to be a driving force in the ways of humanity. But we realize, that, just as in using absolute logic, it too can be dangerous. So we try to find a balance between the two-the balance between emotion and logic. But rednecks take the notion that emotion is God, and run with it, bringing it to a whole new level... But in this case, out of respect, and in the context of limited time, let us focus in on just one brand of redneck: The Vermonter. And so we return to Abner, sitting, peering intently out the window as the clouds float by in his room on the PCW Airship. Abner laughs, tilting upwards in his chair, allowing a smile to pass over his rosy cheeks. With a rough, powerful swoop of his hand, he swipes at a small container of pencils, strewing them across the desk. Setting the remaining office items aside, he plants his head down on the cool, smooth surface of the wooden desk and tries to close his eyes. But he can’t. He simply can’t. There’s too much on his mind. The German; this guy, and his vicious attacks, Turmoil; who will be there, what will happen, will Abner’s plan-the one he’s so meticulously been formulating in his mind over the last past few days-work?, and of course, Fusion; Tessa Windsor-Green, and the PCW World Television title...will it be his? Finally, Abner, on exactly his Seven Month PCW Anniversary: will hold some company gold? He hopes so. And so letting out a sigh, his mind drifts off to the conversation he had with the ship’s medical doctor, sometime earlier on in the day. “My word Abner, is all that drinking really necessary?” “Hells yes!” he replied, with a shrewd, backwoods accent. Shaking his head in disapproval, the doctor placed a firm hand on Abner’s shoulder. “Heh-heh...” Abner snickers, remembering the stern look on the doctor’s face, and the sincerity in his eyes when he said “Well it’s just not healthy, Abner...it’s just not healthy.” “Heh” Abner snickered, “and all that crap you guys eat, like those chemical-drenched, dog-shit on a stick granola bars, are?” The doctor was taken back by this. “Why certainly not, but-” “Then don’t be complaining’.” And with that, he turned to exit the room. “Abner!” he remembers the doctor crying out. But was it out of anger, or out of concern? He doesn’t really remember. “Now I’m telling you, you just can’t go drinking your life away like that!” “And you can’t just go thinking away yours” Abner replied, thinking-knowing that he’s not a very intelligent man-that the comment didn’t really make much sense...though, on some level, he was right.... The doctor let out an audible sigh before responding “...Fair enough...but still, just remember to get some rest...” And he held up his arms. “Now I know that times are hard-now trust me, I do-but there’s no excuse to miss out on daily exercise, and proper nutrition. If you take care of your-” But the redneck was already gone. Abner chuckles, resting his feet up on the desk now. In all his years, in all his encounters, he’s never really met a man who’s cared so much for his physical health. Well then, of course, here was his mother...Abner leans back, looks out the window, seeing, hearing and almost feeling as the clouds as they whisp by...but she’s gone now; long gone. See, back when he was growing up, living in one of the many of the rough small towns in rural New England, more specifically, Hicksville, Vermont, Abner did not exactly live an easy life. And when it came to personal comfort and care, the only one he could count on was himself. Yeah, sure, every once in a while Cuzin Ed, rollie-pollie and as rambunctious as ever, could provide some solid, lasting entertainment...but as far as the needs of a child were concerned, Abner had many, but few were met. His mother, a sweet, short old maid from Montreal, Canada, was his source of love and affection (and he was her love and joy) in his first years in life, but soon, Billy came, then Bob, then Frank...and then Ted. And after his other brothers, and the sisters, came, and after she (her name was Rose) had to start dealing with the drinking problems of his grumpy, although admittedly hard-working, and well-respected father, the attention originally designed for him, was then transferred to other mediums. The point is, sitting here now in the luxurious comforts of a corporate empire’s airship, Abner doesn’t feel at home...and yet, somehow, he knows he is. He’s not used to the kindness and affection of strangers, but somehow, deep beneath the rough exterior and rock-hardened skin of a self-made redneck, he knows it is right. But still, the doctor, care as he might, to Abner, cares too much,. He doesn’t like others meddling in his business, and certainly not on this occasion. A rednecks biggest weakness is that the more they are in need of assistance, the less they will accept it. The doctor is right. Abner has had too much too drink...not just today, but in his life in general as well. Getting a piece of the German is all that he wants. But at this point, Abner is biting off more than he can chew. The German has obviously, in light of his actions and precise, prestine planning, done excellent thus far, and has scouted out Abner perfectly. And so Abner, obviously, must be concerned with training now. But then, there’s the whole ordeal with Tessa Windsor-Green... Friend, or foe? Well, in that Abner greatly respects her and her efforts, and of course, her impressive in-ring abilities, she’s certainly not an enemy. And as for Sivasian, SyN...and the whole lot of the Order of Society of logic, well, in Abner’s own thought: “they can go straight to Hell!” And he means that, too. But still, a collective foe of Windsor-Green, they can indeed provide as temporary allies on Fusion... But Abner will not accept this. No. He has decided that if they do indeed decide to interfere, they’ll have more than just a woman to worry about... “As if” Abner thinks to himself, chuckling ‘that’s not already enough for those fools...” Everything can change on Fusion. Abner must concentrate now, and more than that, he must must also have the will to succeed. And for once, he does. Winning the Television Title means moving up higher on the card, climbing the PCW ladder, and of course, more airtime. And the more airtime, the more the fans get to know him; and the more the fans get to know him, inevitably, the more they’ll love him; and the more they love him, the more pressure on the Front Office to keep him happy by taking the Masked German....and HANDING...HIS....ASS to him on a silver plate. Abner leans back and coughs, now lying in his chair. Slowly, his eyes close, and he drifts off to sleep, the humming of the ship’s engines filling and nurturing his ears, soothing him off to a different world: a dream world in which a large, swastika tattooed Nazi ass is literally is given to him on a plate, hairy and covered in a barbecue sauce of blood from one of Abner’s coveted ass-whoopings, served with a side of whiskey, and a world in which he is the PCW Television Champion. He smiles, turning over in the seat. “Now that’s some motivation!”
