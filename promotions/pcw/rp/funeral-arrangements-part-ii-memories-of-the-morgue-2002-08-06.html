Funeral Arrangements Part II: Memories of the Morgue

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6th, 2002 OOC Note: Sorry if this is late but right after I posted my second RP, I began to encounter PC problems. Kept shutting down by itself, freezing and have internet explorer errors, which stopped me from getting my 3rd TT RP up on time [The last we saw of Blayze, he made a stop at the Wilkins-Hurst Funeral Parlor to pick out a specific coffin that he felt would be fit for Clyde or any other victim Blayze plans on laying to rest(Darkstar being the second target). This time, after finishing his meeting with the owners, Blayze had accept the asking price for the coffin and was ready to pick it up the day it would be prepared. As he awaits his day, he took a short travel down to the outskirts of Lubbock, Texas to find out more about buying a space at the Jaimeson Central Morgue. Blayze pulls up in the parking lot of the morgue in a taxi, he steps out and tips off the taxi driver, letting him drive off to another awaiting client. Blayze does seem a bit sketchy about stepping into a morgue since the last he did just that was when he was only 19 to view the gun shot wounded body of his former Crip member who was left to rest in peace at a morgue in Los Angeles. Gripping a hold of the entrance door’s handles, Blayze begins to shiver just a bit, he was not afraid of the funeral parlor but the morgue just revives certain horrific memories Blayze wants to forget. ] Blayze: Why am I even going forward with this. Ah fuck it, if it means sending some gothic punk to his deathbed, I am all up for it. [Stepping into the temple like complex, Blayze makes his way up the hard wooden three steps and enters the main hall. It has many different old-fashioned yet medieval type statuettes placed on the sides of the room, a dimly lit light fixture hangs from the ceiling, lighting up the room. He turns right and enters the huge room where all the bodies are kept, and that’s when the haunting photographic memories begin to circulate in Blayze’s thoughts. He tries to keep his cool but even as a street thug, he has his emotional side when it comes to his brothers. Along the walls, the steel slots with engraved plaques on them indicate the numerous corpses inserted into the walls of the morgue. All in Alphabetical order, Blayze examines each one of them but dares not to inspect inside of them. Creepy yet normal, a distinct aroma of death fills the room, just making things a tad worse. He becomes a bit curious though, wanting to see what the corpses look like since it has been a while since he’s seen one. He drops his fingers on one of the metal bars, gripping a hold of it, he begins to tug his arm bag and it opens slighty..] Voice: May I ask what you are going? Blayze: Jesus!! [Blayze slams the metal compartment shut and swings his body around to face a short bald-headed man with a slim goatee wearing a black suit and black tie] Voice: Well I am not Jesus but my name is Walter Kingsley, I am the sole owner and proprietor of the Jaimeson Central Morgue Blayze: You sure as hell decided to scare the living fuck out of me WK: What did you want me to do? You just began to snoop around, I was wondering what business you have in the morgue. Wanting to pay respects to a relative or friend? More like show no respect to the next corpse I am about to insert into this place Blayze: Not really, just looking for some info WK: Well opening the slots will not get you any info but will hand you a good dose of mental scars [He smiles with a cocky grin and Blayze eyeballs him with a look of confusion] Blayze: What I need to know, how much does it cost for a slot in this morgue of yours WK: It ranges Blayze: So you telling me..it will cost me more if the body is in perfect condition and less if it is massacred? WK: We can dress it up for you and give it special treatment such as cleansing and a proper death bed suit Blayze: The fuck? You play “Mr. Dress Up” with these corpses?? WK: At the client’s request [Blayze shakes his head, not agreeing with the weirdness] WK: Do you have the name of the deceased person ? Blayze: You see, the person is not currently deceased yet, but I would still want to rent out, not buy but rent out a space because his life is on thin ice, so you know WK: Ah I see, what is his name? Blayze: Darkstar WK: I am sorry but we do not insert nicknames, only full names Shit, the fuck was his real name again? [Blayze asks Walter to hold on as he struggles to remember the name] Ah fuck, what is it again? Gaydon..Garvon, nah it cannot be that. Umm, Gaffney..No damn! Oh wait..Gavyn! Yes that’s it! WK: Well?? Blayze: Gavyn Drazail WK: Positive? Blayze: Yes Who the fuck does this smart-ass SOB think he is? Another lucky soul, he should be glad it aint him I am inserting into those death slots WK: Well before it is all set and done, there have to be some paper work filled out. Making sure you understand our rights and priorities and…. [As Walter babbles on, Blayze begins to drift off into boredom] Can’t this fag see that I fuckin understand what the hell he wants? Jesus, just give me the slot and let me get me get my ass down to Turmoil and guarantee that Clyde and Darkstar ended up in their respected places: 6 Feet Underground and In a catacomb slot WK: Understood? Blayze: Yes! WK: Okay then, follow me this way and please, DO NOT touch anything else Blayze: Fine Fuckin Moron [Walter begins to head out the room into corridor and Blayze follows, taking him to get the paper work done. Now the only thing Blayze must worry about is if he can live up to his words and put two pathetic gothic souls to rest. If not, he just waste his time and money for nothing]
