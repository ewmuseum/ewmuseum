Titu Couldn't Last One Second in His Own Hometown

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 Location: Cairo, EgyptDate: August 24th, 2002Time: 6:28AM (The sounds of the morning Islamic prayers blast through a loudspeaker from a nearby mosque as the bright orange sun slowly rises above the Cairo skyline...) 'Allah-U Akbar!' 'Ashhadu Anla Ilaha Illallah!' 'Ashhadu Anna Muhammadar Rasullah!' 'Hayya Alas Salah!' 'Hayya Alal Falah!' 'Allah-U Akbar!' 'La Ilaha Illallah!' 'Haya Alal Falah!' 'Assalatu Khayrum Minan Naum!' (The sounds of the morning Islamic prayers blast through a loudspeaker from a nearby mosque as the bright orange sun slowly rises above the Cairo skyline. The beautiful chants continue until the sky turns to a lighter shade of blue and the sun completely rises above the towering mosques and buildings of this famous Egyptian city.) 'Ugh, what the fuck kind of morning music is that? Can't they play something decent like Mozart or...' 'La Britney Spears!' (Coming down from the skyline, we are now in a western part of Cairo close to the elaborate mosque that was previously playing the athan. Standing before it are an out-of-place group of Central and South Americans. We recognize Argentine dictatorial candidate and PCW Hall of Famer, Frisco and around 6 of his politicians. Also there are a few of Frisco's newly appointed Imperial Guardsmen, and surprisingly the failed assassins El Magnifico Busmuchacho de Mysterio and his transportation El Burro Ignatio de los Galvantes de Santo Maranito. Frisco snorts slightly and shakes his head at the masked luchador.) Frisco: 'No, moron. Not Britney Spears. The only way I would employ her as an alarm is if she were to wake me up with a blowjob. You want music that will put you in a good mood for the rest of the day. Not this kind of retarded chanting or teeny bopper crap!' El Magnifico: 'Pues, no?' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'However, I've already heard enough of this ethnic trash. It was cool at first, but now it's just annoying. No wonder The Pharaoh stays far away from here. Crappy music, crappy food, and ugly women....well, the parts you can see anyway.' (Frisco looks around at the many devout Muslims pouring out of the mosque.) Frisco: 'Well, that and the fact that the people here would probably put him to death for being a filthy pagan.' (The Roman Catholic Guatemalan jobber takes his silver cross out from his shirt and kisses it.) El Magnifico: 'Pinches paganos!' (The Central American does the sign of the cross and slips his religious symbol back in his shirt. A group of passing Muslims direct a stare at the Catholic luchador and one of them blurts out a hostile phrase.) Egyptian Citizen: 'Elif air ab dinich!' (El Magnifico frowns and looks over to Frisco who chuckles slightly.) El Magnifico: 'Que dijo?' Frisco: 'He just said something about a thousand dicks in your religion. I don't think it was very polite.' (The non-English speaking Busmuchacho still shakes his head.) El Magnifico: 'Que dijiste?' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'Un mil vergas en tu religion.' (Magnifico gasps.) El Magnifico: 'Verdad? Me dijo el cabron?' (Frisco nods and looks over to one of his politicians to ask for a cigar. El Magnifico shakes his fist and yells out to the disrespectful Egyptian.) El Magnifico: 'NO! UN MIL VERGAS EN TU CULO PORQUE YO SOY EL MAGNIFICO BUSMUCHACHO DE MYSTERIO....EL CAMPEON DE SUS CULOS~!' (From many yards away, the same Egyptian looks back to spout another phrase that makes his friends laugh out loud.) Egyptian Citizen: 'Bouse tizi, mara!' (Frisco receives his cigar from his Secretary of Tobacco and Health Care and shoots it in his mouth. Meanwhile, El Magnifico rushes back over to ask Frisco what was said.) El Magnifico: 'Que dijo?' (Frisco holds up his hand as he lights his cigar.) Frisco: 'Kiss my ass, bitch.' El Magnifico: 'Que!?' Frisco: 'Dijo...bese mi culo, puta.' El Magnifico: 'Ay! Eso pendejo!' (The enraged Guatemalan growls and hops on Ignatio's back to engage in his own private jihad.) El Magnifico: 'Ignatio, corra por esos pinches arabes!' (The reluctant Spanish donkey stretches and slowly begins to build up steam towards the offending Egyptian citizens. El Magnifico thrusts his hand into the African sky as he screams loudly.) El Magnifico: 'LE MATARÉ A TODO!' (The clicking of hooves brings the brave luchador and his donkey away from the safety of the group and towards the men who dared disrespect the self proclaimed 'Champion of Your Asses'. Frisco watches the beast and rider gaining speed on the Egyptians and shakes his head.) Frisco: 'Idiot. Why did I even bring him at all?' (Frisco looks at his associates as he puffs his Cuban.) Frisco: 'Well?' (None of the politicians or guards say anything.) Frisco: 'Which one of you is Secretary of Presidential Thoughts?' (Governor of Santa Fe, Ribeiro Mendoza speaks up.) Mendoza: 'Señor Presidente, shu fire im las week.' (Frisco ponders for a second.) Frisco: 'Did I really? Do you remember why?' Mendoza: 'Señor, I dink he gave shu a bad edea. Santing like buying ron' toile paypar.' (Frisco snaps his fingers.) Frisco: 'Ah yes, he did think of the wrong toilet paper. That roll was not comfortable at all. What a fucking moron. He had it coming for such a stupid thought that I would never think of. It's CHARMIN~! all the way, baby.' (El Dictator shrugs and points to his regional governor.) Frisco: 'You can be my new Secretary of Presidential Thoughts.' Mendoza: 'Ohh, gracias, Mero Mero!' Frisco: 'It's a very important job. You have to be on your toes at all time.' Mendoza: 'I will, Presidente!' (Frisco nods and begins to direct his group away from the mosque and through the streets of Cairo toward the spectacular Nile River which lies only a few thousand feet away. Frisco turns his head to look at both sides of the seemingly neverending river and then on to a small dock where a boat is guarded heavily.) Frisco: 'Gentlemen, we are going to take a short trip along the Nile to head towards Giza. That boat is for us and is already stocked with delicious Argentine cuisine and decent music. This will give us a chance to escape from this inferior culture until we land at our convoy.' (A murmur of approval goes up from the small group of protectors and politicians.) Frisco: 'As far as I know, our soldiers are in place and ready to flatten all targets. Once that is completed, most of us will go back home and I will be off to put and end to that annoying pagan slave rat, Xansiv.' Politician: 'Shu will kill im, Señor!' Imperial Guard: 'Shu kick is ass!' Mendoza: 'Viva El Presidente!' (The group of supporters echo the comment.) Group: 'VIVA EL PRESIDENTE! VIVA LA ARGENTINA!' (Frisco nods to the words and puffs on his Cuban once again as he smiles. But then his smile turns to a frown as he remembers something.) Frisco: 'Shit! Where's Busmuchacho?' (El Presidente turns around to see the luchador unconscious on the ground with Arabic text spraypainted on his tights and his donkey. Frisco slumps and shakes his head.) Frisco: 'I knew this would happen. Vamanos!' (The concerned dictator and his group rush over to the severely beaten Guatemalan and his desecrated donkey.) Frisco: 'What the hell happened to you!?' (El Magnifico opens one eye, spits out a bit of blood, and speaks.) El Magnifico: 'Me gane?' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'No, you didn't win. You got your ass kicked.' (Busmuchacho groans.) Frisco: 'Come on. We must head towards Giza at once.' (Frisco motions to one of his strong guards.) Frisco: 'Ayudale.' (The guard nods and throws the small luchador over his shoulders. One of the politicans grabs Ignatio's reins, only to be bitten on the arm in the process.) Politician: 'Ay!' Frisco: 'Ugh. Someone get Ignatio.' (El Burro Ignatio de los Galvantes de Santo Maranito growls.) Frisco: 'We're going, Ignatio. Don't start shit. I don't think one disgusting Egyptian here would hesitate in making you into a stick of khlah khalash. Donkey meat is a good substitute for pork, which they cannot eat. Think about it.' (Ignatio puts his head down and the dictator grabs the donkey's reins. With all men present, Frisco again leads his group towards the dock.) Frisco: 'You all are liabilities. If I see a decent pit in Giza, you fucktards are all going in it.' (The group members gulp as El Presidente begins his journey to ancient Egypt.)
