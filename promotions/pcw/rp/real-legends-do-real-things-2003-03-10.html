Real Legends do Real Things

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/2003 ~Redemption. Everyone messes up in life. It is the one thing that makes we, as human beings, human. Even back to Adam and Eve, they ate from the forbidden tree, and got kicked out of the Garden of Eden. Screwing up is part of life. It is how you redeem yourself is how you are judged as a person. Kano has made his share of screw ups. He would be the first to admit that. The one thing is that Kano always does something to redeem himself in his own eyes. He spent the last few months taking out newbies for his own personal amusement. Now he is making up for that by trying to stop PCW from imploding from within because of Kannon's own personal ego and greed.~ *The scene opens up in Jon Kano's backyard, which happens to be a cliff overlooking Jon Kano's hometown of Las Vegas, Nevada. The sun is setting on the other side of Vegas as Jon Kano, dressed in a 'REAL LEGEND' t-shirt and a pair of tan khakis pants, and his father, Marc, dressed in a green golf shirt with long khakis pants, over look the city that Kano has done so much for. Both men are holding a drink in their hand and are smoking cigars as they look over Sin City as night approaches.* Kano's father: Well, how does it feel to be home again? Kano: Pops, I am just going through the motions right now. I keep taking money out of my own pocket to fly in when I am booked, and I fly out back here after my match. Now, we are doing two shows in this fine city before we go to March Madness. Right now, it feels damn good. Kano's father: That is all good and well, son, but are you actually gonna get down to business this week? Kano: You mean at Kano Enterprises? Hell yes. I know I got to catch up on some of the shit you can't do. I am going to enjoy this week. Kano's father: You do have two matches this week. Kano: No, I don't. I got one match with a man I want to teach yet another lesson. My match on Tuesday is going to be Young Tiger's shining moment. I am going to make damn sure he wins. I like him. I really do like him. He has heart. Heart is a big part of what makes a really good wrestler. I plan on just holding him on top of me and letting him get that win. Kano's father: Why in the hell are you going to do that, son? Kano: I am getting concentrated on Friday. Timmy Walden has personal business with this REAL LEGEND. He claims I never taught him anything. That is amazing. Prior to me taking him out in the woods and teaching him everything HE knows, he was some no-name jobber who would have fallen to the wayside in a few months. After I showed him what being a wrestler was all about, he became better and better with each match, week after week. *Both of the Kano men pause to take a deep drag off of their cigars in a way that you can just tell they are father and son.* Kano's father: Jonathan, do you remember what I said to you when you were about twelve years old? *Jon Kano thinks about it a moment.* Kano: That you were going upstairs to fuck mom? Kano's father: No, other than that. Kano: That if I'd show you a tropical fruit, you'd show me a cocksucker from Guatemala? Kano's father: Oh Christ, son, why can you remember things like that but not what I am getting at here? Kano: It probably has something to do with all the chair shots I have taken in my career. Kano's father: Exactly, son. For the last seven years, I have criticized you for being a wrestler when you could make so much more money if you'd focus on Kano Enterprises. Now I have finally realized what you are all about. You don't care about the money, you just are out to have a good time. I respect that. Kano: You pretty much got it right, pops. There is a reason I only sleep for about four hours each day, because I have to balance Kano Enterprises and my wrestling career. It's hell, but well worth it. PCW is going to hell, but I am gonna fight it until I can fight no longer, right alongside my buddy, Duke Williams. Tim Walden is one of the ones causing PCW to go to hell, and he is going to learn yet another lesson from yours truly entitled, 'REAL LEGENDS DO REAL THINGS.' *The scene fades to black as Kano and his father begin to walk inside to Kano's house as the sun finally sets on Sin City.* ~Redemption, in Sin City? Odd, very odd.~
