Prepbonics Part 1

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/03 Often when times become rough there is only one thing a person can do, rely on their friends. For Tessa this was something that wasn’t so obvious though, until she stumbled across the number of an old pal, a man who was also once part of PCW himself, a man by the name of Jackson Rust. It had been nearly a month since Tessa had heard from Jackson and she was beginning to miss the charm and smile he always seemed to bring along with him wherever he went. Tessa didn’t love him the way she loved Chris, but she also realized that somewhere in her heart she would always have a spot for him just for all he had done for her in the past. The doorbell rang out a chime just as the short hand on the clock reached the number eight. Tessa gleefully ran towards the door knowing very well who it was. She had been expecting this visit from Jackson all day since she had called him just the other day begging him to come visit her just to bring that cheery smile back on her face. Surely enough looking through the pane of glass in the middle of the door was the semi tanned face of Jackson Rust. He bangs on the glass to gain Tessa’s attention and mouths something to Tessa that she seems to already know. Jackson: Tessa, can you open the door? He bangs on the pane of glass once more before pressing his face against the glass like a child leaning up against the pane of glass thinking they will get a better view. Tessa: I’m coming, I’m coming! Finally reaching the door Tessa swings it open and allows Jackson inside the Chatham home, but not before embracing him in a giant hug. Jackson: I guess someone missed me. Tessa giggles and nods her head. As she does so Jackson takes off his shoes and runs towards the direction of the living room as if he himself was a member of the Windsor/Storm family. Tessa: Gee someone sure feels at home. Jackson: You damn right! Jackson kicks his feet up on the oak coffee table that sits before him and grabs onto the television remote. Upon turning the television on Jackson runs into a station devoted to African Americans and their style of music. Jackson laughs as the host of the show on that station begins to spit out words that seem to make no sense to Jackson nor Tessa, both of them being as far from the so called “gangsta” type as possible. Voice from the television: YO YO WUD UP! Jackson hits the mute button on the remote as he looks over a Tessa, a grin on this face which tells that he obviously has something planned in the back of his mind. Jackson: You know Tessa, we should think of our own language for people like us that just speak plain old boring English. Tessa: Huh, why? Jackson: Because everyone has a language of their own except us average white folks. Tessa: What about those people like Xavier’s wife, whatever her name is. Jackson: You mean preps? Tessa: Yeah those type of people! Jackson: What about them?Tessa: Don’t they have a language of their own? Jackson: Errrrr, prepish? Tessa: Yeah! We should make a language like that! Jackson: But that would be so boring. We need to add something else into the mix. Tessa: What about that language thing we just heard on television? Jackson: Tessa, that’s regular old English just turned “gangsta” style. Tessa: But doesn’t it have a name for it? Jackson: It does? I think it’s nothing more than gibberish in some messed up form. Tessa: No, it has some special name that begins with like an E. E…bon…. Jackson: Ebonics! Tessa: Yeah that’s it! Jackson: Tessa, you’re a genius! Tessa: But all I did was… Jackson: I know what kind of language we could make! Tessa: What kind? Jackson: We shall call it….prepbonics! Tessa: Uhhh what exactly are we going to do with that? Jackson: Create our own language of course! It shall be even greater than your current tag team reign with Chris in PCW! Tessa: And how do you plan on doing that? Jackson: You shall see Tessa, you shall see. With that Jackson walks out of the living in search of a pen. Tessa merely shrugs and takes Jackson’s spot on the couch, taking the remote as well, in search of a station where she can understand what is being said, but while doing so a smile cracks across her face and she cannot help but laugh just knowing what kind of crazy idea Jackson Rust might be up to.
