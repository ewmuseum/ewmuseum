Kannon, you did something wrong.

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-24-02 In recent days, the Krayzie One has been silent. That seems to be the normal of late. After the events of Friday Fusion, which saw Xavier Kannon completely screw up Jeremy Howard's PCW Continental title match with Tim Walden, the Krazed Lunatic decided to skip Sabotage. Why? Knowing Xavier Kannon would be at Sabotage, why did he choose to not gain his payback then? Because, its a known fact that when you cross Krayzie, payback is twice as worse. With that said, a camera decides to spend a few moments of time with the Krazed Lunatic, hoping to get his thoughts on Kannon's recent actions and Jeremy's two upcoming tag team matches. As the camera comes into focus, the scene fades into living room of the Krayzie One's luxurious two story home in Elkhart, Indiana. There, standing before a black thirty five inch Sony television in faded blue jeans, black Airwalks and his Continental title strapped around his waist, is Jeremy Howard. Jeremy: I can't f^cking believe him. On the television screen, is the remainder of Xavier Kannon's recent promo. Or, to put it better, Eleanor Hall's bash of Natalia Miyagawa. The look on Krayzie's face..... pissed. Jeremy: They think they're so cool that they'll make fun of the misfortunes of a pregnant lady with twice as much class as Eleanor has ever had in her life. F^cking whore. Jeremy turns toward Jessica, whose sitting on the black couch behind him. Jessica: After seeing this, I can't wait until Friday. How does she do it? Wait...... Until..... Friday. Jeremy: Don't do that again. If I had the nerve to hit a woman, I'd lay that bitch out for this promo. Jeremy turns his head back toward the television, staring at the still picture of Eleanor Hall at the end of it. Just the mere sight of Xavier Kannon's valet brings a foul taste to Jeremy's mouth. It was only a weeks ago that Natalia Miyagawa was rushed to the hospital because of an attack perpetrated on her by the vile Boda, PCW World Champion. Now, the lady is being slapped in the face by Eleanor Hall. Normally, Jeremy wouldn't care, because it's not Jessica. But, Natalia is Alexander Miyagawa's wife. Jessica: Well, if you don't take the opportunity to, then I sure as hell will. A sly grin crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: You know, normally I'd mind abuse on a female. But this bitch has crossed the line. Jeremy pauses, trying to maintain his anger. Not happening. Jeremy: I'm getting so sick and tired of those two. Friday, Kannon has the damn nerve to screw up my damn match with Walden. Thats twice now I was just about to beat Walden! Twice! Jessica: It's sickening. Jeremy begins to pace the floor a bit, his anger beginning to boil over. Jeremy: Then, this promo! I can't wait until Turmoil. I'm going to break Kannon's f^cking face! Jessica: Honey, calm down. Jeremy stares her directly in the face. Jeremy: How can I calm down? I'm pissed. Jessica: I know, but we can't let Kannon and Ellie get to us. We have to pay them back. Jeremy's eyes glaze over, rendering him almost expressionless. He still keeps his sights set on Jessica, while choosing to take a seat next to her on the couch. She places her arm around his waist, trying to comfort the hotheaded Krayzie. If its one thing Xavier Kannon doesn't want, is a hot headed Krayzie showing up at Turmoil. No good can come of that. Jeremy: Pay them back, huh? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jeremy: I suppose you have something in mind? Jessica: Well, not really. Jeremy sighs, suddenly feeling a little bit calmer about things since the start of the promo. Jeremy: That's alright..... I do. Jessica locks eyes with Jeremy, noticing his lack of kidding. Either Jeremy is trying to throw a certain someone off, or this man is one quick thinking individual. Whichever it is, Xavier Kannon has some planning to do. Because, he's going to need some serious security if he wishes to remain safe from a psychotic lunatic on the prowl. Jessica: What is it? Jeremy: I can't say quite yet. It'll have to wait. Jessica: Wait for what? Jeremy stands to his feet, turning off the television that sits across from where Jeremy was sitting on the couch. A few seconds later, Jessica also gets to her feet, looking out the window. Pulling up in a silver Navigator is Justin Howard, with Troy sitting in the passenger's seat in a car seat. Jessica: Kids are back. Jeremy: Good. I'm sick of talking about Xavier Kannon and his bitch girlfriend. They'll be dealt with this week. Jessica knods her head. Jessica: I just can't wait until I get ahold of her. Jeremy: Just break her face. The two parents of Troy Howard continue to watch through the window, while seventeen year old Justin continues to make his way up the driveway from a day out with his nephew. Jeremy: Remind Justin to keep the television turned away from PCW shows this week. Jessica: Why? Jeremy grins. Jeremy: Because I don't want my son seeing the hell I'm about to bring on Scientology. A door can be heard in the background, while the two lovers continue to think of the evil they're going to bring on Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall. With Spring Fling only a few weeks away, Jeremy Howard is not going to keep letting the Gold Patron Meritorious push him around. Come Turmoil, things are going to take a complete one hundred and eighty degree turn..... to black!
