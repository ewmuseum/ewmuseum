Big Booty White Hoes?!

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.04.02 London, England Mixed Emotions No Ladder Match for the Tag Titles, perfect. *** He left Italy in a mix of moods, quietly he rode on the plane, only thinking of what he was going to do to the collective buffoons known as Pranks With Attitude. He felt betrayed, and made a complete fool of for losing to them, just to find out about the man he was facing Friday night. The train rolled down the tracks, clicking back and forth along the steel as he listened, deeply concentrated on Gaz Green and Nerezza, maybe unlike his friend Statuz Quo - he had something personal to deal with the both of them, and the antics of Pranks With Attitude. It was them they reformed and began in more plot of how to beat Playaz Inc. But, one woman's sudden arrival into the fold during the match happened to change all of that. They both thought that no woman, such as Natalia Miyagawa was going to help P.W.A, after the actions they had done towards her husband Alexander. They were wrong, an electric maneuver later, and the reign of Playaz Inc. was done. How was he deceived, stolen from that limelight to fight with good over evil. It bothered Dante to a passion, he uncomfortably fidgeted around in his seat, thumping away on a flip out tray and thinking more of what Friday impacted on him. The companion of his, Statuz Quo happened to be raving and jabbering towards a female attendant about how Fat Albert happened to receive a hernia for falling inside of a manhole. It didn't matter, just the fact. Closing his eyes, he placed his hands on his chest, reclined back and his mind in terminal hell. Was he just a one-sided man, who only thought about his own career and well being? He'd deny every thing that claimed any bad press towards him - it was heelish nature to do so. He was toying somewhat, with the mind of anyone who had dared to bring themselves towards him, that he didn't know - Total Violation happened to drop a significant match to the Playaz - pleasing him somewhat, but still he knew deep down he hadn't toyed with Andi enough. Maybe not one tag team wanted to feel the brush of Playaz Inc.'s wrath once more - they'd felt it enough already. Then it rose onto his mind - what's next? In his heart, almost as black as the death of a rose - the cat didn't feel like giving back. Not to anymore he felt didn't deserve it. Those people - the haters and self proclaimed 'next this and that' happened to be on his marking spot, wanting into his very own world. Not likely, he thought to himself. He may have been at time cowardice, and even malice towards people, never one of those men to back down from a challenge and this wasn't an exception to that rule. Pranks With Attitude had crossed the border of where people should stay, and he's was on terms with an agreement. Between himself, and the man standing across from him as the bell sounds. Here it was, on the heels of November and the Holiday season. A year wiser, and a year smarter. The reflections in his heart were damp, muggy as the air around the control car continued to swirl and rain drops peltered the car as it rode down the twisting track. He growled and removed his hands from the base of his heaving chest, bawling them up in a fist, and thunking against the cold plastic seating him. CLUNK! CLUNK! CLUNK! In all of his conflicts, maybe it was needed to stay and collect this thoughts about things. Maybe this would cheer up, and conjure up any steam of happiness in this man. Losing your very own sense of a smile can be damaging, and at times - mind wrecking. 'Stupid spoon.' he mutters his first words since getting on the train. Bending back a plastic spoon given to him by the traveler's on train dinner, he fights it, before snapping it in two. The meal wasn't kosher, rather stringy and out of date. 'Feeble people, stupid cracker made meals.' He ran his tongue along his bottom lip, before reclining back once more, sitting in a fitful rage over what he could make the most out of another trip to London, England. A place he hadn't seen in his life. 'God, how I wonder how those Big Ben nights will be,' he quietly quipped to himself, before lowering his head with a short gruff nod. 'The bullets riddling off cracked glass, the freaks out every night at Beckingham Palace making money their way. Tennis is such a pussy sport, yet the biggest major is played here...' he trailed off once more. As the railroad car tested its aerodynamics by twisting along with the matching track - the speaker built into his Walkman heads phones began blasting at a low rate, loud enough for people in the row to listen to it. 'This Means War', a play on Ozzy Osbounes' 'Ironman' hit with Black Sabbath. The only difference was, Busta Rhymes was its main performer and with a drum thumping beat to drop bars on, it was enough for Dante to relax towards. The words ran out in some odd oedipal essence to his mind - the right source of what he needed to be doing. Much like the chronicles of Saint Juan Diego, Dante was on a mission all his own and almost everyone had to experience from it. His daughter, his co-workers in both industries and the love of his life, but right now - only a few of those mattered. Those PCW Tag Team titles were deeply on his mind, as the train rolled down silently like a steel colored ghost, riding highways and seeking victims. 'Warriors, the silly shyster bastards. Those muthafuckas can't happen to monotone or profess just what I know.' he chuckled to himself, before the track 'Critically Acclaimed' from his Street Manifesto CD popped into his ears. Soon, on the side of him arose the other member of Playaz Inc., known for his brash confidence and almost giant sized ego, Statuz Quo had seen titles before, and loved them with a passion. When Statuz didn't have one, the best of him came out. He dropped down, pushing most of his body weight upon the small recliner seat, puffing away on a brown cigarette. His eyes dimmed down lower, before Dante nudged him on the shoulder, 'Now's not the time for you to get high.' Quo's eyes looked blood shot red, clearly out of his mind while taking short breaths of the cigarette. 'Your point?' he quitely asked. 'Soon one of the attendants are going to tell you to put it out.' He chuckled, before clasping onto the end of the cigarette, holding his hand over a arm rest as he took out the white smoke to leave floating towards the crowded train car. Various coughs roam through the car as Statuz looks around, almost in complete shock of his actions. 'What?' he shrugs his shoulders towards Dante. 'This 'No Ladder' match on Fusion this week has me more than buggin'.' he stated hesitantly. Quo rose out of his slouched position in the chair and let the head rest reach his neck, 'So what you're saying is....we have no chance of victory?' 'Our minds are on too much shit, that's what I'm sayin'.' Quo raises an eyebrow towards the former RUSH superstar's last comment, almost incited by it. 'Too much? Homie, we haven't even crossed the border of too much shit.' 'What I mean is,' Dante shortened his brief sentence. 'That over grown fuck Kannon is getting off on us! You know? Like having his fine ass wife stuff pillows down the back of her already gorgeous ass and act like she has a heavenly ghetto booty!' 'Its bull - wait, gorgeous ass?!' Quo asks, nearly coming to the terms of almost shouting. 'You aren't going for white booty now are you?' Dante chuckled, 'No! Of course not, I mean...' The two men sighed briefly, before coming to terms with the truth. 'She does have a great ass.' Shaking their heads out of the truth, they look above them, quitely noticing that a female attendant stands above them, holding a tray filled with steaks, Coca-Cola's, and Burberry items. 'Sir,' she spoke towards Dante in typical manner fashion. 'would you be able to take your itms from the tray?' Looking over at the tray, Dante's eyes bugged. 'Burberry! How in the hell -' 'We're England sir, we created the stuff.' Dante nods, before mumbling, 'Hm.' Turning towards Quo, he grows a wide smile on his face sending Quo into a fit, shaking his head, 'No, no, no, no, no! I'm not doing it!' 'You haven't heard what the plan is!' stated the former RUSH World title contender. 'Its better than Kannon's wife bending over in a thong.' Quo perks up a bit, 'Well, if it THAT good then, what is it?' 'We're going to the first country club that we see, then we're going on a white power trip, and then, we're going to be GOTH!' Jackson evilly snickered, before the woman interrupted him once more. 'If you're going to do that, can I come too?' 'Turn around a second,' Quo told her sharply. Listening with the words of a big tall black man who was crazy enough to send the entire train into mass hysterics, the woman complied. Both Dante and Quo raised an eyebrow, 'Daaaamn. Her ass is as flat as a pancake!' 'It is? Oh wait, I haven't taken my ghetto booty pills yet.' she smiled, before popping one in her mouth as if it were a Tylenol. Soon, Dante and Quo's eyes widened, before falling on the train floor laughing their asses off. 'MY GOD THERE IS SUCH A THING AS A WHITE WOMAN WITH AN ASS!' 'Okay, so can I come?' Dante nodded, 'We'll call you...Ellie~!'The woman stopped the both of them, 'But my name is Di-' Quo cut in front of her, 'You're Ellie~!, if you're not then you're not going to FUsion with us to see us beat Xavier Kannon and Pranks With Attitude for the PCW Tag Team titles, capice?' The singular mention of Xavier Kannon sent the young woman into a swooning fit, nearly fainting when the thought of her glimpsing the PCW CEO and Owner would have on her young body. 'Damn, Wisconsin dudes got major play over here.'
