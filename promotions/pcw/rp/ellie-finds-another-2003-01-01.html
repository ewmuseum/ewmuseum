Ellie~! Finds Another

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Ellie~! Finds Another Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 01/01/03 “I’m back....” With the familiar voice of PCW’s CEO, Xavier Kannon and the clunk of a door closing, we see the Scientologist returning to his wife’s apartment in Greenwich Village with a bakery back in his hand. His cheeks have that rosy tint of someone coming in from a cold morning, and his mane of Red hair is hidden by a Black and Purple ski hat. Xavier : “Ellie~!?” He looks quite concerned when there is no reply from his preppy little wife, PCW’s Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Removing the stripy scarf from around his neck and sliding off his long Black winter coat, Xavier hangs them on the wooden stand and walks into the hallway, stepping on a lavish Oriental rug with his still wet shoes. Xavier : “You haven’t seen it’s 2003 and freaked out because you thought you’d been propelled into the future have you, my little Poon Puppy... I remember last year.” Still there is no reply as he peeks his head into the White and Black decorated kitchen, not seeing any sign of Ellie~! He puts the bakery bag on the work-top and realises he still has his shoes on, then promptly kicks them off onto the Black and White tiled floor. “Shhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh....” A noise is heard from the lounge, Xavier just picking up on it as he slowly turns on his heels and treads quietly out of the kitchen and back into the hallway. Looking towards the lounge door he sees that it is pulled to, but not fully closed, just the smallest of gaps he can see through to make out a figure wearing Pink which he guesses is Ellie~! Worried there may be someone else in the room, he approaches slowly and announces himself. Xavier : “I warn you..... I’m a Black Belt in Karate... Okay, I’m not, but I have enough money to have you killed a thousand times over!” As soon as he finished his sentence, he then cringes as he realises he may have told a burglar that he’s a millionaire. He takes a few more steps towards the door, then Ellie~!’s voice is heard quietly whispering through the door. “Would you, like, shut up and stuff!” Concerned, Xavier tentatively reaches for the door and with one finger, starts to slowly push it ajar but then leaps back in shock as something small and White rushes through the door and past him. Xavier : “What the....” The Scientologist turns around just quick enough to see the small White shape dart into the kitchen before Ellie~! pulls the door open, not looking too happy with him as she puts her hands on her hips and tilts her head to the side while staring at him. Eleanor : “Like, SO well done and stuff!” Her sarcasm bites right through him as Xavier holds his hands out to the side wondering what he has done. Eleanor : “You scared Mr. Jones!” She skips past him, Xavier looking bemused as he turns to see his wife head down the hall, speaking in a soft yet chirpy voice. Eleanor : “Here Kitty Kitty!” The Scientologist does a double take as his eyes widen, realising one of the people on the earth who shouldn’t really be left to look after herself let alone an animal has bought a Cat. Xavier : “You bought a CAT?!?!” Ellie~! makes her way to the kitchen and disappears through the door. “Ahhhhh.... There you are Mr. Jones.” Shaking his head, Kannon mutters something unintelligible under his breath before Ellie re-emerges from the kitchen with a furry White Cat held in her arms, looking snug in her arms and held against her chest. Xavier : “A Cat?” Ellie~! does the usual schtik with the Cat that people do, talking to it as if it were a baby and then rubbing her nose against it’s little Pink one. Eleanor : “No, Mr. Jones.” Kannon looks at the animal oddly, it’s eyes not taking themselves off him and it’s face contorting to something evil. Xavier : “Who’s a Cat...” Ellie~! shrugs. Eleanor : “Well, yeah.... In that he’s a Cat and stuff! Come and say hello!” As Xavier cautiously approaches, Ellie~! turns the Cat’s face to hers and again talks to it. Eleanor : “Say hewwo to Xavi, Mr. Jones.” Stroking it’s head, Ellie~! turns him to Xavier who holds out a hand to stroke the pet, only for Mr Jones to whip out his right paw and unsheathe it’s claws, swiping at the CEO’s hand while hissing. “HHHHHHHHHHHHHSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHHH!!!!!!” In the blink of an eye, Xavier retracts his hand as the Cat keeps snarling and hissing at him. Xavier : “THAT CAT IS EVIL.... Which oddly, I should like....” Ellie~! doesn’t really seem that concerned with her husband and instead makes a fuss of the Cat. Eleanor : “Awwwwwwwwwwwww, did he scare woo Mr. Jones?” The Cat keeps a menacing and evil eye on the Scientologist as Ellie~! strokes and kisses him, Xavier’s bright Green eyes measuring up the pet, muttering ‘Evil’ under his breath. Eleanor : “Bad Xavi!” Xavier raises his eyebrows to exclaim as Ellie~! lets Mr. Jones go and the Cat scurries into the lounge again, leaving Xavier left looking at Ellie~! Eleanor : “Stop scaring him!” As she walks past him, Kannon just looks totally lost, and in a way a bit jealous as Ellie~! skips off back into the lounge and is head collapsing on the sofa. “Here Mr. Jones, come to Ellie~!” Shaking his head and thinking of all the better ways New Year could have begun for him, he follows into the living room where his wife is curled up on the sofa watching TV with the Cat curled up in her lap, purring as she makes a fuss of him. Xavier : “You DO know Cats take a lot of looking after don’t you?” Not taking her eyes off the screen or the White ball of fur nestled in her lap, the little prep replies. Eleanor : “HELLO?!? I, like, SO know that.... I’ll take it out for walks, buy it a hutch and get one of those wheels for it to run around in and stuff.” Rolling his eyes back into his head and muttering ‘Oh, Hubbard’ under his breath, Xavier walks around and goes to sit next to his wife but Mr. Jones has different ideas. “HHHHHHHHHHHHHSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHHH!!!!!!” His head shoots up and his set of impressive teeth show, snarling at the Scientologist while hissing. Eleanor : “Like, stop scaring him! Isn’t he a bad man Mr. Jones, did he scare woo?” Deciding to give up on it for now, Kannon starts stepping backwards, keeping his eyes on the Hell Cat himself as Ellie~! continues to pet it, not realising a vessel of evil itself is sitting in her lap purring as the shot fades to Black. “After this I, like, SO can take you for a swim and stuff, how does that sound?” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
