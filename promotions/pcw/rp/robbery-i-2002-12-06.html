Robbery (I)

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th December Time 1910 6th November Deuce 00 Deuce SA#1 Adrenaline RUSHKnowledge unquestionableKai hath fear anger So… the robbery commences. Security at the club had been tightened since the episode at Muffy Grant’s, as at every gym and health salon in New York. The number of uniformed security guards had increased to six. They patrolled the street level and the sidewalk around the club endlessly. Following the plan, Kai, George Pfister and Dennis Hollings walked into the loft building next door at about five o’clock, just as the shift of the furniture upholstering company was leaving for the day. Once inside, they headed for a doorway that led into the narrow courtyard between the two buildings, inaccessible from either sixtieth or sixty-first streets because of iron fences topped by three strands of razor ribbon. At six o’clock, they put their backs against one wall, and their feet against the other, and began the agonizing walk against gravity up the ten common flights of buildings. There were no handholds. It took an hour. When they reached the top of the wall, as previewed in the plan, the lighter of the three, Kai, turned over and poised shortly, his weight on George Pfister’s straining body. He slid onto the roof, then reached out, took Pfister’s hand, then Holling’s, and pulled them up. They lay near the parapet for ten minutes, their muscles shaking with the strain. The guard walked back and forth across the tennis courts twice as they lay there, then sat down on the stoop of the entrance to the building and lit a cigarette. It was the last thing he ever did. The single blow to the neck broke the atlas and axis bones at the top of the cervical column, severing the spinal cord, causing instant death. Kai glared menacingly into the eyes of Hollings who merely laughed, “Oops” Kai shook his head in abhorrence, repugnance. Kai looked deeper into Hollings who moved from side to side. ~**~ Kai…Kai…Kai… I’m coming for you… Your soul, your being, your essence… MINE. ~**~ Kai twitched his neck viciously. He looked up and shrugged. Ignoring the staring eyes of his victim and Kai, Dennis Hollings took the key ring from his belt and opened the door to the stairwell. George and Dennis wore maintenance uniforms under jackets that they shucked and laid next to the body of the slain guard. They had rehearsed their next steps a hundred times. As they walked down the stairs they could hear the reverberation if the hard rubber balls bounding off the squash and racquetball courts on the next floor. They passed two spectators in gym shorts and t-shirts on the landing overlooking the players. Kai, blended in with the horde of millionaires in simple athletic apparel. They people did not turn their heads a Pfister and Hollings walked by, however a half raised smile acknowledged Kai who returned the greeting with a curl of his lips at a half attempted smile. It was an act of procrastination. At first, it had seemed odd to Kai that the plan called for an assault on the women’s locker room. It would have seemed more natural to enter the men’s room wearing athletic equipment and blend in with the crowd. Perhaps, later in his current dress he would raid the locker room. When he put his mind to it, he saw that there was no percentage in robbing a few lockers and getting caught by the locker boy. They would be better off with more physically manageable women. He checked his watch. It was precisely eight-fifteen; one half of the seven forty-five’ ladies exercise class was over. The class was the club’s daily highlight. The women were the loveliest, and the exercises the most rigorous. There was always a big audience. Hollings did not hesitate. He slipped through the door of the ladies locker room, followed by Pfister and finally Kai. Kai knew that there were two attendants in the locker room. One who sat by the door and the other who dealt with towels. Acid rock vibrated in the air. The attendant at the door was a young black woman with close-cropped hair. She was seated reading a magazine. According to the plan, she was to be the second and last victim of violence. Kai struck her at the base of the neck with his fist. Slipping the magazine from her hand, he allowed her to lean forward on a pile of towels on the table. She looked as though she were asleep. Pfister and Hollings pulled on their stocking masks. Kai chose to pull his scarf up over his mouth and pulled his hat further to his eyes. An overweight woman in her forties emerged from the shower wearing a towel around her showers, hiding pendulous breasts, and other held tenuously between grasping fingers, not quite encircling her waist. Her first instinct to scream was stifled by the sight of the gun in Holling’s hand, and a loud “Shhhhh!” “Listen,” Hollings said, “you sit down on that bench and nobody’s going to hurt you.” Kai looked on objectively as George Pfister moved her to the side and took her trembling hand. He squirted her with a drop of Krazy Glue, and then held her hands against the bench. “You got the idea?” She nodded. Kai walked through the shower room till he saw the arch led into the back locker area. He took a glass from the top of the sink and smashed it on the floor, stepped into an empty shower stall, and waited. In ten seconds, a tiny woman in shorts shirt and pigtails came into the shower area. “Shit…” She tried to scream, just as her voice box trembled Kai shot out his foot so it was within millimetres of the woman’s face. She stood rigid, frozen with trepidation. “If you utter so much as a squeak, I will put my foot through your nose.” She nodded. Kai lowered his leg, ordering her to move into the other room. She did as she was told, her eyes widening even more at the sight of the gun in Hollings hand. Pfister glued her to the bench along with the other woman. Pfister had removed the locker skeleton key from the body of the unconscious locker attendant. Leaving Hollings to watch the door and the women, he opened the first row of lockers with gloved hands. Kai’s kismet was evident in his silence; something was approaching him, which he would have no hand in dealing. His cards were slowly being stacked, as premonitions went, strong took on another meaning. Hollings pulled a plastic bag from his back pocket and emptied the contents of purses and pockets as fast as possible, then did the second row. He motioned to Pfister, who tied gags around the mouths of the women. Kai looked on, his mind far away in a distant place. Kai, Pfister and Hollings walked through the shower into the rear locker room. They passed the women’s sauna, and through the glass saw two elegant nude female forms. Just past the door was the entrance to the service staircase. They stepped inside and breathed a sigh of relief. The second hurdle had been successfully crossed. Hollings returned his gun to his holster, and stripped off his stocking mask. Pfister threw his mask to the floor; Kai simply pulled his scarf down to around his neck and adjusted his hat. They climbed the stairs to the squash floor and retraced their steps to the roof. The door was ajar. Dennis Hollings removed the gun from the holster and crept close to the door. Kai was now a good hundred yards away from where Hollings was crouched. A young black gym instructor was kneeling next to the body of the dead guard, sucking air and trying to keep from vomiting. She was slim and wiry, but rather a tall woman. Hollings did not want to make a noise. He pushed gently against the door… ~**~ Kai looked on… perhaps his premonition was around the corner. He pushed his hands deep into his pockets and turned his attention back to Hollings. ~**~ Where I go from hence, let this be my parting word, that what I have seen is unsurpassable.”
