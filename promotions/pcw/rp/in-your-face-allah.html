In Your 'FACE' Allah!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Turmoil… …Was more or less… …DIVINE… In one fell swoop of the mighty Neo Void God’s HAND OF FATE, he destroyed an entire following in one night. Tuesday…was the day…that Allah died… Tuesday…was the day that the Neo Void God brought about the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse to the Tag Team Division of Primetime Championship Wrestling. One man is the champ…the champion of the masses… GOD OF TAG TEAMS… And now in less than a week’s time, the god must go into combat yet again, with men whose cause has been crushed, and men whose greed will be their undoing… Next Turmoil…the god must align with Politically Incorrect to take on the corpses of the United Allah Enthusiasts. PI better not step out of line…or we’re likely to see the breakdown of the Political Machine in PCW next… =©= Cyclonica: Don’t cry for them, Afghanistan… the truth is Allah never was with you. He’s just a figment of your false hopes… I declare Tuesday, July 9th, 2002 the OFFICIAL day that ALLAH DIED. The Divine Prophetess stood with her hands outstretched in front of a blank black wall. Next to her stood the nVg, with his arms folded across his chest and the PCW Tag Team titles draped over each shoulder. She lowered her arms and nodded to the god before looking back at what or who was before her. =©= The Divine Prophetess ducked into an alley as a huge roar of masses could be heard behind her. From the corner of the alley appeared Akiyama, swinging his massive arms left and right swatting bearded worshippers of Allah from his path. She glared down at her bloodied hands with eyes almost infused with insanity. Cyclonica: Bothersome ignorant mortals…you are all ungrateful of the truth!! She watched as a robed Arab falls across her path, thrown in her direction by the god. She looked back to see him taking chunks out of the crowd with the Clothesline from the Void, each swooping blow giving another nine feet between him and the gibbering horde. She watched the man’s hairy hand reach for her, and in an effort to keep his filthy touch off of her, she drove her spiked heel into his hand. The man screamed as she felt her heel hit the pavement. Cyclonica: You do not touch me, scum. No one has that right. Then she looked back at the god once more. He was handing out HANDS OF FATE and DIVINE SWORDS like it was Christmas for them poor bastards. She sighed and shook her head at the sight. Cyclonica: Are you done wasting your time? They’re peons anyway! Let’s go! Akiyama turned back and dashed into the alley. He wrapped his arms around a huge dumpster nearby and with his immeasurable might, he pushed it right into the opening of the alley. From behind the dumpster he heard their Arabic grunts and complaints. Cyclonica took the tag titles from off his shoulders and cradled them in her arms. Cyclonica: By the Void…that’s the last time we try to share the good news of your destruction of Allah at that place. The sign above them read: “Holy Temple of Allah”. Cyclonica: Nevertheless…we should be on our way. We have much to do with little time to do it in. They stepped out of the alley and into the streets… those human ridden streets lined with the aging flesh sacks going on whatever task they had to complete. The god stood tall amongst men, as he should alongside the Divine Prophetess. Their direction…unknown… Their destination…undetermined… But they did know one thing as they walked along that street… Cyclonica: Why are these dumbass mortals staring at us? Yes, it wasn’t really a new thing for the two of them to receive the stares and the points. It was mandatory for them to receive them…looking at their attire of PVC and vinyl, along with their solid black eyes and silver and black and blue hair, some would only glance over them as vile Goths. But being Goths aren’t enough to grab the stares they were getting… These were different stares…stares not like the stares of fear they always got. Fears still pumped through their eyes, don’t get the idea twisted… but there was something else as well. Fingers pointed…not knowing that they had a chance of being snapped by Cyclonica, or worse by the nVg. Whispers were uttered under breaths…not aware that the god hears all, sees all, and knows all that converges around him. She shot a look out at the god, getting only a short nod in reply. She knew he knew they were watching them as well…but they had to keep on their toes. He read her face and knew that these humans with their beady little eyes on them could be ready to attack them at any point just because of their superiority. And then…she felt someone… Her arm snatched back with her black talons ready to slice…then she halted… …The pitiful creature that touched her… …Was a child… Cyclonica: …What…the FREAK…do you want? Her hard black eyes pierced into the scruffy little boy, in his white shirt and blue jean overalls. The black haired youth peered up at her with big blue eyes…like one of those Precious Moments children. Child: (whispering) Is that Akiyama? Cyclonica: Speak up, ant! I cannot hear your whimpering. Child: I said…is that Akiyama? The Divine Prophetess glanced at the god as if she needed to check for herself. Then she looked back at the child with a nod. Cyclonica: Looks like it to me…now go ahead and run away in fear. Her words were not heeded by the youth…and she watched as the child did not run…but looked up at the god and smiled. Child: Thank you Mr. Akiyama. Whoa… All around the two Celestials the people began clapping their hands, softly and then growing ever so higher in a beat of thrice. Then the infamous chant began going… People: N-V-G!! N-V-G!! Cyclonica look confused…a first for the Divine Prophetess, who knows all and sees all outside of her vision. The god himself didn’t know what to make of it all. These people were…cheering for him? But why? Cyclonica: It’s got to be some form of diversion…a ploy to catch us off guard so they can destroy us… A blonde teenaged girl came rushing up to them in a white tank top and blue hip huggers. She let out a squeal of delight as she jumped up and down before the nVg, her abundant bosoms gyrating before the Celestials. Girl: Akiyama!! Akiyama!! I JUST CANT BELIEVE IT!! She held out a felt tip magic marker to him. Girl: Can you put your Celestial Symbol right….here? And up came the girl’s shirt, exposing her breasts before the two. Cyclonica and Akiyama stepped back from her with annoyed faces. A single slap from the Divine Prophetess knocked the marker from her hand. Cyclonica: What the FREAK is wrong with you people? The girl lowered her shirt with the same dumb smile, despite the insult of her hand being smacked. Girl: You two are like…national heroes!! Cyclonica: Why is that, mortal fool? Girl: Because you took a striking blow for the US against Afghanistan! Sure enough, the people around them were waving American flags with the letters “nVg” scrawled across the stripes. Some were saluting, and others were merely cheering and clapping for them. Cyclonica: Wait a second…you’re saying that just because we disposed of some trash that just so happened to cross our paths…we’re all of a sudden heroes? People: N-V-G!!! Cyclonica: …Come here, bosomy youth. The girl leaned into the Divine Prophetess so she could whisper to her. Cyclonica: You do know that we’re out to plunge this world into universal harmony, right? Girl: Yeah!! Of course! That’s why you two are the coolest!! You want to bring about world peace! Cyclonica: Um…I don’t think you understand OUR concept of universal harmony. You see… The crowd surged on them, two men lifting the Divine Prophetess up on their shoulders and parading her around the street. Cyclonica looked back at Akiyama, who watched with blank eyes. She made one motion with two fingers, slicing across her throat. Cyclonica: Akiyama!! Yep…right on cue… The god let out a huge roar, throwing his arms up in the air. That caught everyone’s attention. They did nothing but freeze, all eyes locked on him. They lowered the Divine Prophetess quickly, and she walked back before the god with her arms folded across her chest. Cyclonica: Now…I don’t know what’s up with you idiots today, but go on about your lives before the Neo Void God ends them! Yes, Akiyama did destroy the United Allah Enthusiasts single handedly, and he will go about doing so again in a few days…but if we ever find you people swarming upon us like that unexpectedly again…you ALL will be banished for your sin! Now…flee in fear before the god decides to Absolutely bring forth the Apocalypse to ALL of your Destinies!! SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS! The people…they stared for a moment… then they started cheering again. Exasperation came upon Cyclonica’s face as she slowly looked up at the nVg. Cyclonica: Akiyama… Another roar, and a few steps forward, and everyone scattered in panic. She nodded, with a job well done from the god. It would be just like that in a few days…another job well done from the nVg…single handedly, just like before… She cradled the tag team titles a little tighter as she turned around and walked off, the god following closely behind her. Amidst the people, a man in a black trench coat watched them carefully… Inphino: Hmm…the day that mankind exalts villains as heroes is the day that leads to their destruction. The Black Dragon Master lowered his head and walked off, disappearing into the sea of humanity fleeing from the Neo Void God… =©=
