Dong Zou's Story

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The week beginning 20th March, 2004 This is continued from “The Interview/Missing Immigrant”. Previously… We saw Eric Sagar have an interview off of a reporter for “Christians In The Spotlight” magazine. Sagar was very mysterious about Propaganda’s where-a-bouts and all the way through the interview an unearthly grinding noise was ricocheting through the walls of the ranch they were in. All Sagar said that Propaganda was having a special method of “training”. After the reporter left Sagar went upstairs to see that Propaganda is torturing the missing immigrant Dong Zou. Dong Zou’s view I hang from the wall held up only by the shackles that attach me to the wall, if it weren’t for them I’d fall. Willingly. The sheer weight of the amount of blood spewing from my body is enough to drop me to the floor. I try to lift my head but the weight of it just sends me unconscious. I black out… I’m back again. I open my blood soaked eyes to see a small square of vision, but all I can see is Propaganda tearing up the skin of my stomach with a blunt knife. The pain is unbearable he’s just ripping at my skin as I try and tense my muscles, he reminds me of how they slice the aromatic duck, back home in Shanghai. He finishes. He looks at me. Why isn’t he displaying any emotion he’s just holding that knife that blood covered? “Chink!” the other man hollers at me. I don’t speak English well but I know what that means. I’m hungry, I’m thirsty, I’m tired, I’m hurting, I want to go home. I wish I’d never come to this fucking country. What is there problem? Propaganda grabs the metal bar again he slices through the air and slams into my now numb ribs. I use the rest of my energy to shed a pitiful tear. Pain. I black out, again… Dong Zou’s story I was born and raised in Shanghai, it was a rough district. A lot of crime, a lot of gangs and a lot of drugs. I stuck way clear of it and got on with my work I was a good kid. I was an only child. And as I hit my teenage years I found a fascination in Western culture. I say fascination it was more of an obsession. I wouldn’t be interested in Asian girls; my taste was fixed on them. I got a bit of a ribbing at school for it. But I didn’t care. I’d just say “Whatever” like some moody American teenager, they wouldn’t understand what it meant and that would give me a giggle. I was determined to learn English, but I never did, the grammar was beyond me. Well like I said, I was fascinated with Western culture and would be interested in western sports like football, baseball, American football, cricket, rugby and wrestling. Now it was hard to keep up to date. We have a few football games now and again, three Chinese players play in the English Premier League for Manchester United, Manchester City and Everton and we have a national team. But games are expensive and I go to the odd one or two. The rest of the sports aren’t heard of, except for PCW Wrestling. Everyone in China knew PCW Wrestling. It was on the television and ever year they would have “Shanghai’ed” this would be one of the top sporting moments in the sporting calendar over here. When tickets would go on sale, those who could afford it would eat them up within an hour. I never even bothered. Except this year, there was this competition at a local radio station; you have to think of your own wrestler, think of his gimmick and his finisher. The most original would win two front row tickets to see Shanghaied. I jumped at the chance. I’ve been thinking of this for a long time. Here is what I said. ”Character Name: #4576 Age: 28 years since creation Hometown: Secret Army Testing Location, Arizona Height: 6'11' Weight: 304lbs Wrestling Style: Perfect. The ultimate wrestling machine. Flawless in combat. Catchphrase: 'I was built to kill you' Gimmick: The ultimate wrestling machine. In the ring he's virtually unstoppable. But has no personality what-so-ever. A very typical but abnormal monster gimmick. Appearance: So muscular he looks like he's about to explode. Every muscle in his body is huge. His muscles are on the point of unrealistic. He has a tattoo across his left peck with 'Test Experiment: #4576' on in a very military like stencil. Very strong jaw. Thin lips. Rather small ears. His neck is as thick as his head. Has an angry snarl on his face. His eyes are spooky; they have no iris, just a strange lime green-white with a small dot for the pupil. He has a dog chain around his neck and plain jet black trunks and plain jet black boots. He has black fingerless gloves on both hands. History: In the heart of the Arizona desert the American army have a secret plant. An underground nuclear testing plant. And in it they have the greatest scientific minds in the world. In it they do tests on humans. Criminals who were supposed to have been on death row. #4576 is a criminal. He was experimented, but due to a few very minor malfunctions (his eyes) he was rejected. In this plant they are trying to build the ultimate fighting machine for the army. #4576 was one of them. Unfortunately, there was a security failure. #4576 escaped and set himself free into the world. He made his way to Texas. Where he learnt that because of his eyes he was deemed a freak. He stole some sunglasses. He learnt new things by just wondering the streets. People often asked him if he boxed, played American football or wrestled. He didn't answer but thought he must be perfect for these jobs. He studied boxing and American football. Neither took his interest until he found wrestling. The art of hurting people with your sheer strength took his fancy. So he set himself a mission to go to one of these federations. So he set off to make his fortune.” Pretty good, huh? Well the radio station was impressed and accepted my think almost immediately, but considering everyone else just said standard stuff like “Captain Wrestler”, “Hardcore Harrington” and just phoned in to holler “SHANGHAI ROCKS~!” there wasn’t much competition. So to my sheer delight I was off to watch Shanghai’ed live. I was buzzing for weeks. Those who mocked me and tried to say I was committing treason for being fascinated for Western culture were now envious of me. Everyone wanted to join me for the extra ticket. I just shrugged them off and decided to sell it outside the stadium. 200 Renminbi. I was walking on the moon after this. I couldn’t believe it free ticket to PCW Shanghai’ed and I’ve profited off it. Could life really be this good? (No) I was on top of the world So the day came. Shanghai’ed. I watched the show, I loved every moment of it, and I embraced the atmosphere. I felt like I was part of the show. And from that moment on I knew I couldn’t stay here anymore. I must leave. Tonight. So after the show, after everyone went I snuck into the back, I went completely undetected, the security was appalling. You’d think they would take so much more care considering the 9/11 disaster and considering they were in a foreign land. While I was sneaking round the back, I got a glimpse of some of my favourite stars. The Buzz, Inphino Blitz, Skylar Green and I saw Boda. When I saw Boda I nearly screamed. He’s my favourite. I walked into the staff parking lot and then I saw my chance. These fat, lazy, donut eating, coffee slurping slops were slumped by the van in the middle of loading no one was attending it, it was just open. Anyone could walk in and take something out. Or anyone could walk in and put something in… like them. Without a moment of hesitation I sprinted. Faster than Dwayne Chambers. I leaped over obstacles, rolled onto the lorry; nose dived into a box and shut the lid on me. I was going to America. After an hour of buzzing off adrenaline we started to move. I waited in anticipation, I couldn’t wait. All the way I was humming “Coming To America” by Neil Diamond as discreetly as possible. “Everywhere around the world, they're coming to America Every time that flags unfurled, they're coming to America Got a dream to take them there, they're coming to America Got a dream they come to share, they're coming to America” I fell to sleep. I woke up. It seemed lighter. We were swaying. Hang on, I must be on water. I’m out of China! I’M ON MY WAY TO THE WEST! I couldn’t believe it. I stayed awake, trying to amuse myself. Thinking of what I would do when I got there. Thinking of things I could do. Places I could see. I looked in the box. Just some props, a bible, an empty blood pack, a few steel chairs, a baseball bat, the usual. I played a game where I could try and think who could have used each prop. I couldn’t wait. I just wanted this journey to end. Another day of this happened. I found my nutrition from lunch boxes around the truck. I never left the confides of this truck. I didn’t dare. Then I was there. Divided States Of America When we drove off the boat it took a few hours until we stopped again. After about half an hour. I lifted the lid of the box slightly to see the truck I was on was being unloaded. OH NO! I was going to be caught. I thought fast. I waited for a man to take something off. Then. I jumped out of the box. I ran for the door. I got out. I was in a big building with a lot of trucks in. It had big lifeless white walls that towered. And big doors that could be pulled down and I could see outside. I could see America. The beautiful outdoors. I made a fast walk away from my method of transport. I stunk like a dead badger. I was just about to head for the door when a fat old man approached me, he was very hairy. He said something. “Vwaw Mah Tennessee Sip Nashville Netto Country.” He smiled. He can’t have been interrogating me. So I just let off a tiny giggle and power walked away, into the open air. The land of the free. I walked around, I had a feeling I was in Tennessee from some of the signs I read. Night time fell and after all the walking I was tired. Where to sleep? The only money I had was in Chinese I shouldn’t use that. So I thought that another night of sleeping rough wouldn’t do me any harm. I found a back alley dumpster and settled. Just as I lay down I heard thirty or so foot steps. I sat up, I looked around and heading towards me were a flock of bald gentlemen, they were all wearing leather, holding knives and had weird tattoos over them. As they approached me I tried to think where I’d seen that dress style. They got closer and I tried to think. They were holding up their knives and I remembered it was from a film. But what film one of them grabbed me. Then I realised. American History X! That was the film. Oh shit. I was in fucking trouble. ”Skinheads!” is all I could say. Then they beat me. They beat me hard. They cut me. They beat me. They kicked me. They punched me. They beat me. I could have sure done with #4576. They beat me; they threw me in the back of a van. Then they drove me out to the country. To a ranch. Then I saw a familiar face. Eric Sagar from PCW. I thought I was safe! A celebrity! He would never hurt me. I was wrong. He and the skinheads dragged me up a flight of stairs and chained me to the wall. Then I saw him, Propaganda. They all beat me. They tortured me. And that’s where I am now. Being tortured. Why? Because I’m Chinese. Because I’m not American. This land I wanted to go to sure isn’t that great. Back to the present I’ve woken up. I’m face down. Which is a change? But boy am I sore. I have cuts, bruises and lumps all over me. I look a state. Everything about me is sore. I try to move, but my feet seem stuck. I look down. They are coated in cement. Oh no. What’s this mean? Then I felt a familiar feeling. It felt like I was on sea. The room I am in is dark. No light, except for a small ray coming from a window on a door. I look at it. I try to crawl but my block is too heavy and I don’t have any energy. Help me, what is going to happen to me? The door swings open. I look up. Two f the skinheads from before are there. They grab me by my arms, my arms are sore, they are swollen. They drag me out and throw me on the edge of the boat. I turn round to see standing over me the massive figure of Propaganda. Then I hear Eric Sugar’s voice. “DROP THE CHINK IN!” He commands to Propaganda. “DROP HIM IN THE NAME OF CHRIST! FREE OUR LAND OF THIS SCUM!” So he grabbed me. He lifted me above his head then threw me into the sea. Where I sunk to the bottom. I died. So in conclusion folks, I lived to go to America and when I get there I get beaten. I get mocked. I get tortured and then I get killed? For what? Being Chinese? Being different. Part of me felt like #4576. Part of me felt like crying.
