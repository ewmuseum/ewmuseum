Ragamuffins Revenge - Part 4 - Nothing But The Truth

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 21, 2002 [She waits on the beach for some kind of sign. A sign from God. She's shown love for him ever since she can remember. She is so thankful for everything that he created for the human race, especially thankful for giving her life. The cool wind wraps around her and it forces her to pull the pink, hand knitted sweater closer to her body. The beautiful Shailyn Matthews is completely lost now, for the first time in her life. She wasn't going to cope with it, but now she has no choice at all. Ever since she met 'Totally Tubular' Tommy she thought that it was her time to become truly happy. She had her career ready, she wants to be a lawyer...to defend the people of this world. She wanted Tommy to be her rock, the man who would care for her and show her true love. She found it, but he didn't. Were the words Ragamuffin...err...Randy said to her true? Was Tommy merely just going to use her for sex? To pick her virgin flower and place her up on his wall like a trophy? It's so hard to believe a man who kidnapped Tommy's brother because of name calling. But is it just name calling? Was Tommy somehow doing some sort of horrible mental abuse to someone who was considered a friend? The caring, gentle, and sweet man she knew as Tommy was not capable of those things. Though, after watching Friday Fusion and watching that gentle man beat a man with a chair, who knows what to believe anymore. So she sits on the beach, waiting for him to comeback home. That's if Ragamuffin...RANDY...hasn't gotten a hold of him yet. It couldn't be possible with the police in the Dakota's coming every inch of the states, making sure Raga--Randy is caught] WE'RE NOT GONNA TAKE IT, OH NO, WE AIN'T GONNA TAKE IT! WE'RE NOT GONNA TAKE IT, ANYMORE! [Shailyn can hear the loud music coming half a mile down the road. Harry's favorite band, Twisted Sister, ringing through everyone's ears that is anywhere near the beach. She doesn't see the point in the meaningless heavy metal music, but some people have a different way of escaping from their world. She stands up and begins making her way toward the blue and yellow beach house. The home of professional wrestler 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The place where he relaxes before his big matches, the place where he trains to become better. The place where Shai almost made love to him. In that weird Yahtzee room. She regretted not doing it at one time, but now she's having different feelings. Maybe she did the right thing by not sharing her body with him] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!! Twisted Sister can be MOST BODACIOUS sometimes! [The blue and yellow mustang screeches into the driveway along the left side of the beach house. The two men with their hair tangled up from useless headbanging and eyes filled with tears of laughter. After beating The End viciously Tommy has so much to laugh about. Laughing because he's going to be PCW Continental Champion, laughing because he's going to make sure that blind bogus dude is finished once and for all. He's laughing because his brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry will have Sandy Wong by his side] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man!! [Tommy is still dressed in his blue and yellow suit and, of course, Harry is in his Twisted Sister T-shirt accompanied by black leather pants and black cowboy boots. They pull a couple bags from the car's trunk and begin to enter the habitat that Tommy has called home for so many years. The place where his greatness began, and the place where he'll continue to be great] Shailyn: Tommy... [The beautiful woman walks through the fence opening, separating the beach from Tommy's home, and keeps her distance. Tommy looks up sort of in shock to see the bodacious babe Shailyn standing in front of him. Her pretty brown hair blowing in the cool wind. Her baby brown eyes looking so innocent and perfect. The pink sweater over her shoulders, leading down to her UCLA T-shirt. Her breasts are most underestimated as they are very voluptous, which Tommy has just noticed for the first time. The sunset behind her outlines her figure and the wonderful curves that she was so blessed with from God. Bodacious work, dude. Her tanned skin gleaming in Tommy's backyard as if signaling to him that there still is an excellent chance with Shai] TTT: BABE!! It's a totally gnarly surprise to see you here. [Tommy goes in to hug her, but she pulls back and shakes her head] TTT: What's the matter? Don't you want to give the most bodacious dude in the US a hug? Shai: Tommy, I need to ask you some things... [Tommy seems confused. Questions. What questions can this bodacious babe ask him? She cheated on him with Ragamuffin and never had the guts to admit she was wrong. She is the one who let a good thing go. She messed things up and she's the one asking questions? The Tubular One should be the one asking the radical questions, not her] TTT: Babe, I need to ask you some things. Shai: Tommy, I know that you think I cheated on you, BUT I DIDN'T! I was drunk and I didn't have any clue as to what was going on. Before you could even find out what was going on, and to see what happened, you were already gone. TTT: I saw what I needed to see, babe. Shai: Well, fine...but I watched Friday Fusion and I am really worried about some things. About who you really are. That's why I've come here to ask you some things. I want the truth, Tommy. [Tommy sighs and begins thinking back to the beating he laid onto The End. The brutality that he unleashed and how BODACIOUS it felt. Watching him bleed and groan in pain. Watching Sandy Wong crying in the corner as he bashed The End's ribs with so much force that they are easily broken] TTT: It's plain and simple, Shai. The End is totally bogus and somebody had to put a stop to it. Shai: Tommy, The End seems like a very nice person. In fact, he never had a problem with you. your the one who has had a problem with him. I just don't understand how the gentle, sweet man I once knew could have been that brutal. It's got me thinking about a lot of other things, Tommy. TTT: Like what, babe? Shai: Like you and me. Was I just going to be some sort of sexual trophy for you to hold up? To show off to all your other friends and laugh about it, knowing that I'm the one who's going to be suffering over it? Is that what you were going to do to me? TTT: You want the truth, right? Shai: OF COURSE! TTT: I'm am filthy rich. I'm a self made millionaire. I'm good looking and I'm talented beyond all belief. These are the bodacious gifts that have been given to me. I'm going to use them to the best of my ability. Because, Shai, life is bogusly short and you can't sit around waiting for things. You have to go out there and make the gnarly things happen. I've done that since day one. I've put in all the sacrifices that it takes and it's gotten me EVERYTHING I've ever wanted. Money, power, women, and FAME! Was I going to use you? Of course I was, babe! HAHA! I am 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the most bodacious chick magnet in the world. I could never settle down with one babe for the rest of my life, at least not right now. There are too many tanned chilas out there that need to feel my RADICAL touch. [Shai looks devastated. She never would have thought in a million years that Tommy would be saying these things right to her face. That this man she once loved was just putting on a show to get into her pants] TTT: Your a virgin and I haven't had one of those...SINCE I WAS A TEENAGER! I needed to feel that bodaciousness once again. I couldn't believe you came along the way you did and I was most grateful for what you gave. It's just...you were too damn religious, babe. You wanted to serve God and not disobey his gnarly ways. Well, I never pushed you to do it but you certainly went further with Ragamuffin then you ever did with me. So, Shai, meet THE REAL TUBULAR ONE! The man who uses babes, loves money, and will do anything to have what he wants! And right now, Shai, I don't want you anywhere near my bodaciousness. YOUR RUINING IT! GET THE HELL OUT OF MY RADICAL FACE!! NOW! [Shai breaks down into tears and runs onto the beach] TTT: GET OFF MY BEACH TOO!! [She doesn't care and just keeps running anyway. She runs for what seems to be miles as the tears stream down her face. Tommy, her first true love, said all those horrible things about her. He was just merely going to take her virginity away and throw her away like yesterday's trash. How could something like this happen to her? She never has done anything bad to anyone, just tried to do her best to obey God. Now she's feeling the most painful feeling in her life, heartbreak. As she runs down Broadmaker Boulevard, two hands come out from behind a tree and whip her behind it] Shai: AHHHH!! [The foul smelling hand covers her mouth and she can only gag. She looks up into the dark eyes of...RAGAMUFFIN!] Ragamuffin: Shai, it's only me...Randy. Sorry I had to pull you out of the road like that, but there was no other way. Shai: Randy...how did you get here?!?! I thought you were going to get caught by the police? [She then notices the police uniform that he's in, but that foul smell is taking over] Shai: Ugh, Randy, you really stink...I'm sorry to say that. [Randy shrugs] Ragamuffin: I'm the one who should be sorry, Shai, I spent an entire day sitting in manure. It wasn't fun at all, but somehow I got used to it. I'm guessing that you had a chat with everybody's favorite surfer. [Shai nods, wiping the tears from her eyes. Suddenly she feels her pain fading away, as if the manure filled hands of Raga...Randy...took away all of her pain. That somehow he was going to make sure everything was going to be all right. Randy is going to save the day] Ragamuffin: I'm sorry that you had to go through that. Your a very special person, with qualities that no woman in LA has. You need to remember that, because in a few short minutes I'm going to end 'Totally Tubular' Tommy...ONCE AND FOR ALL!! [Fade to black]
