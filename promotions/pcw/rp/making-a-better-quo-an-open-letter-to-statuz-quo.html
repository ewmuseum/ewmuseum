Making a Better Quo- An open letter to Statuz Quo

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today Sheesh, Quo, you really are boring as heck. This is a Pete production. See the stuff that those idiots tried to shut down. The stuff those idiots couldn't handle. The best damn making you think you’re better than you really are kind of things I can spit. If u didn't like me then, you’re going to like me now. To heck with the rules. That’s what is wrong with you Quo. Too much aggression, blurred by your mindless rage. As soon as you stop putting people off with your horrible attitude, and nasty demeanor, maybe it'll finally return to it's top notch production. Allow me to pontificate on the opinions you should have to make you a better human being. Marvin: Great guy. Both backstage and in the ring, he’s a consummate professional. I like talking to him and he’s a genuine human being. Spiffy: Again, another great guy. His command of the English language isn’t the best, but then again, either is yours my friend. Perhaps the two of you should take an ASL class together? It could do wonders for your terrible attitude. Chet: He is another good, quality human being, Quo. You could learn a lot from just sitting ant talking to him. Matthews: Sure, he’s in RUSH, but again, he’s a good person. He helped me fix a flat tire once, and I was eternally grateful to him. Aimz: I didn’t know her. She’s before my time, but from what I’ve heard, she was a nice girl. She was very wholesome and down to earth, with a good head on her shoulders. Deville: That’s not very fair, Quo, considering he’s the only Universal Champ you’ve ever seen, which would make him the best. Now, again, I’ve never met this individual, but I’m sure he’s a good, chivalrous kind of person. Skitzo: Now you’re getting personal here. Skitzo happens to be a good friend of mine, and is hurt DEEPLY by your comments. He’s only wanted to be friends with you Quo. We all have. It’s not our fault you’re so distant. We really do like you, and I know I speak for at least 9 Skitzos when I say we like you. We really, really do. Brock: I’ve never really dealt with Mister Newblood. Again, like everyone else I’ve met in the PCW, I’m sure he’s an excellent human being. Gaz and Nerezza: These two guys have set the standard for tag teams, until recently, and you, being a tag wrestler could learn quite a bit from them. Instead of degrading them, you should be embracing them. You should learn all you can from them, Quo. Ivan: Now, If it wasn’t for Ivan, PCW wouldn’t be what it is today. He gave quite a bit to this company, and in return is very respected. How a man as classy as Mister Stanislav is just beyond me. Now Quo, no one here hates you. We all care deeply for you. This is just wrestling. It’s not like this is the national hot dog eating contest. You want to talk about apathy for your fellow combatants! I could get into quite a few whoppers that would send you “LOL”ing all the way home, my friend. Someday, we’ll sit and talk and I’ll tell you a few stories about competition, and making friends. As far as being racial goes, I don’t think a national program such as PCW should be getting into race issues. After all, you should be more worried about making yourself an appealing job candidate, seeing as George Bush wants to get rid of Affirmative Action. I realize you’re part of a hiring quota, and that’s ok. Sure you put some hard working white men down into that God forsaken PURE abomination, but, you really should think about your future as it deals with the PCW. What am I asking? Nothing much, Quo. I just think that being a more approachable, easy to get along with human being will make you a better person. You should really stop holding a grudge against people just for the color of their skin, as well as beating you, and making you look silly in the ring. It’s just wrong to do, and could lead to you even having a heart attack! God forbid anything like that happens to PCW’s favorite man of color. We’re not all bad white men, Quo. Some of us, like Xavier Kannon are truly nice men. He gave me a shot when no one else would. I mean, let’s face it. Who would give a man as out of shape as me a job? But, being a kind-hearted soul that he was, I donated 50 thousand dollars to become part of the “Bladed Level” in his church of Scientology! I have friends, and I love it. Maybe you need to find God, Quo. Have you found Him yet? Has He touched your life? He should. And no, not the way Daddy used to touch you. In your heart, Quo. I hope I’ve at least given you a few things to think about, and maybe you’ll turn that frown upside down, tiger. There’s no reason to be a Johnny Appleseed, buddy. Because, as they sing in Toy Story…You’ve got a friend in me, Quo…You’ve got a friend…in me. Please, feel free to discuss further, my friend. In friendship, Pete
