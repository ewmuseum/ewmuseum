Jessica and Justin have thoughts?

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-19-02 Tonight, the night of Turmoil. Two weeks ago, the split between Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon began with a simple difference of thoughts. Now, two weeks after that confrontation, fans in the arena and around the world have to wonder, what's next? What could Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon possibly have in store for each other when Turmoil hits the air? And, how will their Fusion opponents, Tim Walden and Big Alan Steele, play apart in all of this? Only Turmoil can answer that question. What can be answered right now, is how exactly does Jeremy Howard prepare for what could be another explosive Tuesday? With that question in mind, the scene fades into the backyard of the Krayzie One's luxurious home in Elkhart, Indiana. Standing in the doorway that leads into the back, is the ever beautiful Jessica and Jeremy's younger brother, Justin. Jessica, wearing a white dress, appears to be talking over some things with Justin. Justin: Do you think he's walking into a trap tonight? Justin, clad in normal street clothes, stands with his back toward where former two-time World and current Continental Champion, Jeremy Howard, is seen trying to teach his two year old son baseball via a Nerf set. Jessica: I think anytime he walks into a PCW arena, its a trap of some sort. Jessica continues to stand in the doorway, her arms folded, staring out at her loving boyfriend and the two year old son that connects her to the man that's placed his life in danger more than once to care for her. In Jeremy's hand, he holds a Nerf wiffle bat, while trying to explain to a toddler the basics of baseball. It might seem odd, but this is Jeremy Howard. Justin: I don't see why he does this to himself. He should just take a break. Relax for a bit. Jessica: You know your brother won't do that. He's got too much pride in himself. Justin just shakes his head in disbelief. Justin: I would hope by now that he'd humbled himself a bit. Jessica: You know your brother. He has to just give everything his all. Justin knods his head, agreeing with her. Because he knows, as does every single one of his fans, that had it not been for the Krazed Lunatic giving his all each and everytime he applied himself to a task, he might not have ever became the World Champion, he might not have won the rights to his brother's freedom, and he his son might not have ever been able to come home to his rightful mother. Jeremy Howard is known for sacrificing his own well being for his family. A fact that Xavier Kannon should learn to accept. Justin: Well, I hope to one day repay him for doing that. If not for my brother, I might not have ever been free of that hellhole prison or Drake Maxwell. Jessica: You know, your brother beats himself everyday for letting that happen to you. A sorrowful look comes over the face of Justin Howard, younger brother of future hall of famer, Jeremy. Justin: He never forgot about me while I was in there, did he? It doesn't take Jessica one second to reply. Jessica: Not for one second. Your brother risked his life just to get Stan Hinkle to agree to the Christmas day match. He risked his life trying to find the whereabouts of our son. Justin: All that for us? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jessica: To be honest, I don't think there is anything he wouldn't do for us. Justin turns around, looking toward his older brother. Jeremy has his arms wrapped around Troy's body, helping him hold the wiffle bat in the air, as a white ball sits atop a black tee stand. Professional wrestling won't deter this father from being all that he can be. Jeremy barks a few more instructions to his son before stepping back. Two year old Troy swings the wiffle bat, nailing the black tee stand instead of the ball. A smile still crosses Jeremy's face. Jessica: I mean, it was only a few months ago that our house was set on fire by some deranged maniac. Now look at us. With the money he could've blown on cars, women and alcohol to live the great life.... Jessica pauses, staring out at the Olympic size pool with the water fountain and whirlpool attached to it just to the left of where she continues to stand in the doorway. Forward, she spots the basketball and tennis courts. To her right, what could be deemed best as Troy's playground with a wide array of 'toys' for his amusement. All of which, is seperated by various other things that fill this wonderland of fun known as the Krayzie One's backyard. Then, Jessica eyes move back to Justin. Jessica: ......He bought this palace for us. I love that man with every bit of my heart. Justin: Does risking your life helping him with Kannon apply in this case? Jessica frowns. Jessica: If he needs me, I'll be there. With all he's done for me, its the least I could do. He might only be twenty one years old, but Jeremy Howard has far exceeded the expectations of his life. That's evident by the way Jessica talks about him. But, with a Continental title defense on the horizon against a resurging Tim Walden, he can't rest on his laurels. It's apparent he's going to have to face Xavier Kannon at Spring Fling. In order to get there with the Continental title, he's going to have to get past Tim Walden and his snapping tendencies. Justin: I just don't want to see him get screwed. Jessica: I don't think anyone wants to. But, if it is to be, Jeremy will survive. He always has and always will. Jessica pauses again, turning his attention back to her boyfriend and son. Jessica: I'll tell you this though. If that bitch Ellie gets involved and hurts my man, I'll like so slap the taste out of her preppy mouth. Justin: It's going to be tough for him. He'll have Walden to deal with, while Kannon looms about. Outside interference isn't something the Krayzie One is a stranger too. The last time Jeremy Howard fought Tim Walden, it was for the Krazed Lunatic's Television title. In that match, as Jeremy was about to put Walden away, Ivan Stanislav and Frisco hit the ring, causing Jeremy the match and his Television title. Jessica: Kannon sucks. I hate everything about that man now. He's such a...... Jessica's words are suddenly are cut off by the sight of Jeremy looking right at Jessica. Jeremy, only being about fifteen feet away from where his girlfriend and brother stand, obviously heard a piece of the conversation that caught his attention. Jeremy: If Kannon even thinks about ruining this match with Walden, I'll make Spring Fling a hell for him that Scientology won't even be able to save! Neither Jessica, nor Justin have a clue as to what to think. For the entire conversation, he was quiet, allowing them to talk. But, as the thought of Xavier Kannon interfering in his Continental title defense came up, something sparked inside of the Krayzie One. Would the Gold Patron Meritorious actually consider stepping even farther over the line? With the look in Jeremy's eyes, he'd better think twice as the scene begins fading...... to black!
