TEC: Drake Maxwell

Date: 02/24/02 Drake:“You don’t know how much I hate you, Jeremy….” Drake stands alone in on a city rooftop, the night wind blowing through his short brown hair; his black trenchcoat flowing past him. With a deep breath, he exhales with the force of the weight of the world on his shoulders. He looks to the ground below, where busy humans are milling about, hustling and bustling and doing whatever they do at this time of night. With his hands in the pockets of his gray shorts (it’s not cold enough to Drake that he feel the need to cover up), Drake turns his gaze back to the bright night sky, twinkling stars greeting the “fallen angel’s” eyes. Drake:“In my thoughts, all I see is you. In the faces of those meaningless peons below, all I see is you. I hate you with a passion that you will never understand.” Drake takes his hands out of his pockets and stretches them out wide. His hands are at the level of his shoulders, with his feet together. Drake stands in a cross. Slowly, Drake lurches forward, leaning his weight over the edge. Drake:“But you will feel it…” Balance and gravity switch effect as Drake falls headfirst off the top of the building, down to the street below. Drake closes his eyes as he falls. Drake:“You shall feel my hatred, the hatred burning deep within me. When we meet at the pay-per-view.” Faster and faster Drake falls, reaching terminal velocity; the inconceivably tall building passing by so fast the windows all blur together into one long streak. The winds whips past Drake’s face like fire. Drake:“And your brother… you’ll see what I have planned for him.” The ground rapidly approaches Drake. The people below still have no idea that a body is gaining upon them, and shall soon hit the ground. The long fall nearly over, the hit almost arrived. And then, Drake wakes up, in a boat on the still waters of the ocean on that same starry night. Drake:“It is truly a shame I could not participate in the World Title battle royale at March Madness. It would be a pleasure to win and keep something you prize dearly, The PCW Heavyweight Championship, better than you have.” Drake notices two oars on either side of the small wooden boat. He takes them up in his hands and rows in some direction, though there is no land in sight to go to. Drake:“At least The End made it to the battle royal. Should he win, I get a title shot. It’s just on the principle of the matter. I helped him get to where he is now, so he owes me.” From the sea, many hands rise from the surface of the water, reaching out for Drake’s boat. Drake paddles his boat past, purposefully oblivious to the hands. Drake:“March Madness… funny, that’s exactly the feeling I get from the hoops you try to make me jump through to get to you, Jeremy… Look at you, hiding from me until the pay-per-view, sending your Asylum lackeys to try to soften me up beforehand. You are pathetic, and a coward, Jeremy.” A giant wave rises from afar and speeds toward Drake Maxwell’s little rowboat. He looks at it calmly, rowing on toward it. As the wave approaches, it forms into a large mouth with jagged teeth. Drake:“You understand that I’m going to have to kill you at the pay-per-view. Please don’t take it personally when I do. Then again, you won’t really be able to care, will you.” The wave engulfs Drake and his rowboat, and sends him down, deep down into the ocean… *Knocking* Drake:“You’d better have a good excuse for waking me.” Drake sits up from his red-sheeted hotel bed. He stands from it and moves past the dresser against the right wall, and the lamp in the corner of the room next to the window, to the locked and bolted door. He unlocks and opens the door, and outside the door stands a female escort. She is blonde, Caucasian but tanned, full breasts and firm buttocks. She comes inside and removes her dark coat; Drake closes and locks the door back. She reveals a thin green bra and lingerie skirt. As she tosses her jacket onto the floor, a piece of paper falls out of the pocket. It’s a picture of Jeremy Howard’s wife Jessica from the PCW 2002 Calendar. The woman smiles at Drake as she falls down to a seat on Drake’s hotel bed. “You’re cute,” says the escort genuinely. “Why do you need an escort service? You could get a girl to put out if ya tried.” Drake:“I have my reasons.” The escort looks around the room. No bags. The whole purpose of the room is the escort. “Well,” she begins, “can you at least tell me why you gave me that picture to look like?” Drake shakes his head in the negative. Drake:“I’m not paying you to talk.” The woman shrugs her shoulders. “Okay.” Drake takes off his coat and drops it on the floor by the door. The woman begins to stand and move to him. She reaches for his pants and begins unbuttoning them. Drake stops her. Drake:“No. Get on the bed.” “Okay,” says the woman, not really caring what she does so long as she gets paid. The woman slings herself face up onto the bed, expecting Drake to get on top of her. Drake stands as he is. Drake:“Turn over.” The escort speaks again. “Ohhh,” she says. “You’re one of those guys. Okay.” The escort turns her body over. She props herself up on all fours, and waits for Drake to make the next move. Drake moves over to the escort and runs his right hand down her back. He moves his hands up to her bra, and unhooks it, letting it fall to the bed. Drake then moves his hands down to the woman’s waist, grabbing the panties, and pulls them off as well. The escort is a little eager to get this over with, but still waits patiently. From behind her, she hears something unzip. She tosses her head up, expecting Drake to penetrate. Drake grabs her hair. “So he’s a rough one,” she thinks to herself. And then, Drake reaches forward with his other hand and slits her throat with a dagger. The blood pours from her throat. She gasps for air, and tries to call for help, but to no avail. Drake has her pinned to the bed so she can’t move. After a few moments of struggling, the movement stops; the heartbeats stop. Drake goes to the bathroom to wash off his dagger. Drake:“That’s not your woman, Jeremy… nor do I have anything against Jessica… but I would kill her; if only to get to you. That’s the extent to how much I hate you. I can and will destroy everything and everyone you hold dear, if but to get a moment in the ring with you.” He finishes washing the blade and his hands. He replaces the dagger in his left pocket and zips up the pocket. Drake:“Oh, would that were your woman whose neck I cut but so easily. Would that were your treasure that I deprived you of. To see your face; to hear your cries… it would be the greatest reward and the sweetest pleasure to me. And I could do that to your wife, imagine what I may do to your brother, Jeremy… savor your treasures while you can, “Krayzie One…” you will soon lose it all. A dead woman lies cold and alone in a hotel room. And Drake Maxwell walks out like nothing happened. Drake Maxwell wakes up inside of his own apartment, the sun creeping in through the closed blinds. Apparently, the rest was just a dream. But there is blood on his hands. What happened? Only Drake knows… or does he?
