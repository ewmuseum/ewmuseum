Chapter XIII: Getting to Oaxaca...

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-3-03 ::Introduction:: The meeting with Jax is just coming up, but this is not about that meeting in the introduction this time. This is about one person who is just not getting the whole point I don’t think. Chet King is just a big lousy bum who sits on his couch and doesn’t do anything. He just decides that one day he is going to sit and talk trash. Well let me think the last time I did that was about a couple of years ago. I don’t think that anyone does that anymore and plus all of this shit is in the past. There was not any PCW cameras back in the past following me. See if you would read the introduction in my stories then you would know that I am reliving my past. Plus, why would I just start out with a huge explosion on the spics? Why not work my way up to get them ready for a big happening that happened in the past. You just don’t have any room to talk because you are just trying to play mind games before Madness. I am going to say the same thing as Skylar said….stay the FUCK out of my life CHET KING! ::End Introduction:: Chapter XIII: Getting to Oaxaca…. Mark opens up his eyes to the sunlight that is shining right in through the back of the taxi cab. The blurriness is all throughout the car and Mark cannot see a thing through his perspective. Mark takes his hands and he rubs his eyes so that he can see where he is at and what is around him. Mark goes over to the side window in the back seat and he looks out at the big city that surrounds him. There are skyscrapers that tower the city as well as the little shops and bazaars around allies in the town. “Where are we at?” Mark asks as he keeps his eyes out the window. “We are at Oaxaca.” Pedro says as he looks back at Mark in his rear view mirror. “That’s the city that you wanted to goto right?” “Yes of course I do.” Mark says without hesitating. The car is going through the city without stopping anywhere. The ladies around the street have long colorful dresses on. As well as other people have jeans and the white Keds American shoes on as well. The men have their hair either slicked back or spiked up like any spic would have, as well as added color to their hair like everyone else has. Mark just shakes his head in disbelief at all the people around. “These people don’t even deserve to live down here in the heated weather.” Mark says as he starts to look at the tar on the road bubbling. “They should all be shipped on a boat and be sent down to Antarctica so that they can freeze and die off one by one. Then this world would be a much better place to live than what it is now.” Mark smiles at the remark he just made and then looks outside some more at the surroundings. Kids are running around the city without any shoes on and they are all playing a game with a little ball and a broken brook stick down in an alley way. There is a man running around the sidewalks in the town with a purse in his hand that he just stole from a poor old lady. Then Mark comes to a green forest of trees as the city is passed him now and in the dust. “Take the second right coming up here.” Mark says to Pedro as they go past the first road. “Then after that take the first right and then the first drive on the left, that is where I need to go.” “Will do sir.” Pedro says as he turns down the first right. “I like your attitude better now.” Mark says as he is leaning up towards the front of the cab. “You just seem to treat me with better respect than you did when we first met.” “Well I was hoping for some respect back.” Pedro says as he focuses on the road and not worrying about how close Mark is coming to him. “Sorry to burst your bubble, but you are just like the rest of all the spics around here.” Mark says as he now is leaning up in the front of the taxi. “Why should I treat you any differently now since you give me respect? No need for an answer since nothing you will say or do change my mind about how I feel about you since you just are a lame ol’ spic.” Pedro doesn’t say anything as he enters the drive on the left. They start to head down a road of gravel and the path is all surrounded by trees bending so that no light can get through them. The trees are very high palm trees with coconuts that are attached at the middle of where all the branches come and meet the trunk. “See you later Pedro.” Mark says as the car is stopped in the driveway. Pedro doesn’t say anything and he just gives Mark an evil look. Mark steps out of the car as his boots hit the gravel and make a crunching sound against the stones. Mark waves Pedro off so that he doesn’t see where he is going to. Mark then goes towards the north as he can see the old abandoned house up ahead. The thoughts are running crazy through Mark’s head as he doesn’t know what Jax is going to think of him and he doesn’t know what he should expect. The old abandoned house is made out of wood and clay is seems. There are wooden boards that are hanging down from their nails as they are dug into the sand at the bottom. Mark then walks up to the sand that surrounds the hut as his leather boots are all scuffed up from the places that he has been before Mexico. People can tell a lot about someone by their shoes, places they have been and so forth like that. “Well here goes nothing.” Mark says as he walks up the the door with the rusted hinges on it. Mark puts his hand on the door handle made out of a white marble stone. You can see the design still in the stone as some of the white has been worn away and is now an off white color. Mark turns the door handle and he pushes on the door as it opens with a creaking noise. There are not any lights inside, as the whole place around Mark is so dark. Mark can see a figure sitting down in the dark as he knows it his the man he is suppose to meet.
