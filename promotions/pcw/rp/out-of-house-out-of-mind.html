Out of House...Out of Mind...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Cyclonica: Geez…we get all of the dumbass mortals, don’t we? All in three nights of neat perfect pain… =©= Back in the usual dwelling of one Richard Seibzhen…AKA Baka, to you foolhardy mortals… the nVg and the Divine Prophetess are still gracing his abode with their presence. The thing is…our little victimized human friend here is not alone…looks like someone’s grown up and discovered girls…=©= =©= The scene opens upon the rec room of the Seibzhen household. On one side of the room…Akiyama and Cyclonica, standing in the doorway in all their creepy glory. They glare out across the room with their eyes locked on something or someone. And on the other side of the room…Seibzhen and the unknown woman that had interrupted the meeting between the Celestials and Seibzhen. =©= Seibzhen: Honey…what are you doing? I thought I told you to stay back here? Woman: But I heard commotion going on, so I thought I should check on you. Who are they, Richard? Seibzhen: They…are… um…former co-workers of mines. Yes…yes they are. =©= The woman shifts her eyes over towards them quickly, drawing them back when a growl rumbles from the god at her. =©= Woman: I dare not ask what line of business you used to be in before I met you. =©= Across the room…=©= Cyclonica: D@mn it all to the Void…it’s times like these that make me wish I was still in quarantine back at the Compound in Japan. Look at the lineup we have approaching this week…two flat-scans who want to die, and some skinhead Hitler mark. Boy…the bookers are sure scraping out the bottom to find competition for us. They’re trying as hard as they can to keep us busy before we put a crucial dent in the tag team division after everyone else gets put out of commission. That’s a long ways down to the ground from off your shoulders, eh? =©= A stare from the god gets nothing but a glare back from the Divine Prophetess.=©= Cyclonica: Yeah…I know…the Suicidal Tendencies don’t even know what the entire deal is going on with you. The things they’ve said made clear that they haven’t been around for the past…like…few months, let alone the past 2 weeks. They don’t know anything about you…and they don’t know anything about what’s going on between you and PCWs tag teams. They think you’re going to actually have a partner for your tag team match this Turmoil. What mortal fools… =©= Akiyama’s eyes narrow onto her as those words slipped from her mouth. She shrugged her shoulders at her slip. =©= Cyclonica: I digress…but even that betraying Pharaoh was correct about mortals being fools. Those suicidal monkeys even had the audacity to say that you’ve never been in a tag match before EVER. You…the Neo Void God…former PCW Tag Team champion, with the second longest tag team title reign and the NEXT TAG TEAM CHAMPION and the only individual to possess those belts! I believe you would know a thing or two about being in tag matches...but this time is completely different. You have no weak partner to keep you back in your path of destruction. You have no human waste attempting to be your partner and ride your train to gold and glory. It’s you and only you…and you’re ten times the man and a hundred times the god! Sending those insects over a rope will be like throwing monkeys from a boat. =©=Meanwhile…=©= Woman: Richard, if we’re going to be married, we have to stop keeping secrets from each other. Seibzhen: Yes, dear. Woman: Remember when I told you I did that porno tape with those three guys? I openly told you about that, didn’t I? Seibzhen: Yes, dear. Woman: And about the time I got drunk and stripped at that biker bar, and when I woke up my legs were wrapped around some hog riding off into the night? I never even knew that man’s name after we were finished. Seibzhen: Yes, dear. Woman: And here you are, completely ignoring the fact that you still have skeletons in your closet you have yet to share with me. I feel insulted, Richard. Seibzhen: Believe me…these guys leave more than just skeletons in your closet. That’s what they leave if they’re being nice to you. =©= Back at the other side of the room…=©= Cyclonica: Geez, what are those two simpletons talking about? They’re all rambling about on some Suicidal Tendencies-like dialogue…and it’s wasting our time. You need to be off preparing for this PPV match. It’s eighteen men against the nVg…your biggest test since that fluke March Madness. And we still haven’t paid back Lexian for that crap… but one thing at a time. Once we have those two belts across your shoulders, it will make beating some immortal morals into him all the sweeter. =©=She sighs as she perches on the edge of the piano against the wall. =©= Cyclonica: But before all that, we still have the matter of the neo-nazi on our hands. One, count…ONE…mortal by the name of Mark Makonie, member of Ultimate Authority. If being a member of that bastard group is supposed to be impressive anymore. After Tim Walden’s run in the group, the appeal fell like that whore’s panties across the room. This guy blows a load whenever he sees the swastika and orgasms whenever he sees the resemblance of old Adolf. This guy wanted to play god and build a master race? You cannot play god…you can only be god…just as you are Akiyama. How…the freak…can you be the leader of the blonde haired blue eyed Arian race when you weren’t too close to the physical requirements yourself? Just goes to show that mortals don’t think things through entirely. Just like this one…he hasn’t thought anything through about entering this match with you, nor has he thought about obtaining anything near being the physical requirements for facing you, though having the physical requirements is d@mn near impossible to achieve in any mortal’s life. =©= A yawn escapes her ice blue lips before she rests her chin in her hands.=©= Cyclonica: To sum up a worthless topic, if you’d rather serve a flawed human like Hitler than serve the nVg…you deserve to have your immediate destiny absolutely destroyed at your hands. =©=And yet again…across the room…=©= Seibzhen: Geez, dear. Could you please stop chewing me out? They’re getting restless over there! You don’t want them getting restless…that’s when they start doing all the crazy stuff that cause trouble. Woman: No one’s going to muscle their way into our house and cause trouble. Now Richard, I want you to go over there and tell those two to please leave our house quietly, or we’ll have no choice but to call the authorities. Seibzhen: Police don’t scare them. =©=She puts her hands on her shapely hips and stares at him in disbelief.=©= Woman: Then what does scare them? =©=Baka steps back for a second, his eyes looking off to the side.=©= Seibzhen: I honestly don’t know what scares them. All I know is they fear no human. Woman: No human, huh? That sounds like a bunch of nonsense to me. Now go over there and send them on their way. =©=With the “Super Spouse Eye Glare that Ends all Debates between Man and Woman” look…our poor friend Seibzhen treks across the room with his head lowered and his tail between his legs…metaphorically speaking. Cyclonica and Akiyama look over at him when he stops in front of them and sighs.=©= Seibzhen: …My fiancée over there says she wants you two to leave… =©=Automatic reflex tells him to guard his body with his life, but after flinching, he finds no blows pummeling upon him. He looks up slowly to find the Divine Prophetess looking at him with her cold, emotionless eyes.=©= Cyclonica: She…wants us…to leave? =©=After looking back at his fiancée, who nods supportively, he looks back weakly.=©= Seibzhen: …Yes… =©=He flinches again, but he still lives…what’s the deal here? Cyclonica nods slowly as she steps down from the piano ledge she sat on.=©= Cyclonica: Yeah…sure…whatever… Seibzhen: Really? Cyclonica: Really… Seibzhen: Just like that? =©=She looks him dead in his eye and nods, a small smirk creeping along her lips.=©= Cyclonica: Just…like…that. =©=With that said and done, the Celestial Couple makes an about face and heads for the door. Richard and his fiancée walk them to the door and watch as they head back down the sidewalk and around the corner silently. She then slips her arm around his waist, leaning her head against his shoulder.=©= Woman: See that? All you have to do is be assertive and polite, and people will do what you ask. There’s no need for violence in this day and age anymore. Seibzhen: Maybe you’re right, honey. Maybe there is room for decency and kindness…even within those two. =©= The whirls and whistles of the fire trucks can be heard all over the neighborhood. They all congregated in front of one small dwelling…one small flaming dwelling. Quickly they rush to put out the flames as policemen and medical personnel huddle around two figures wrapped in blankets. Officer: Are you sure you didn’t leave anything on before you went to bed? Seibzhen: I’m completely sure, sir! I just don’t understand what could have caught fire! Woman: This is completely disastrous!! =©=Just then…Seibzhen turns his head to look across the street. Walking along on their merry way was none other than our two favorite Celestials…Akiyama and Cyclonica. They stop dead in their tracks…much like all those tag team chasers will do in their pursuit for Gold and Glory (cheap PPV plug) and head straight for the couple. Seibzhen sighs as Cyclonica looks around at the situation.=©= Cyclonica: My, my, my…what’s happened here? Seibzhen: You… Woman: It’s horrible! Our house just came ablaze, and now we’re out of a house!! Cyclonica: Hmm…just like us, huh? Woman: Yes! Small world! Seibzhen: You… Cyclonica: No…my name is Cyclonica. Yuu is dead. =©=Akiyama and Cyclonica walk off on that note, leaving the couple to deal with the firefighters and doctors. Cyclonica nods slowly as she walks ahead of him.=©= Cyclonica: Yep…that’s called Universal Reverb, Akiyama. The galactic reflex that some people call karma…it’s nothing more than your actions and words coming back to bite you in the proverbial @ss. This Sunday it’s all about Universal Reverb on all those worthless tag teams. Squander your opportunity…and when a good one comes along it’s snatched up faster than Suicidal Tendencies can job to you. Faster than you can crush Mike Stone’s windpipe…faster than you can hurl the Pretty Cool Guys and Misplaced across the ring. This match is nothing more than a time limit test for you…let’s see how many jobbers you can toss from the ring in the given amount of time. When you succeed…you get yet another level of godhood added to your belt. =©=She turns around and faces the nVg as they walk.=©= Cyclonica: First…you have to invoke a little Celestial fear into the hearts of Suicidal Tendencies and one Mark Makonie. Three mortals who aren’t worth the god’s time…so waste them and be on your way! Don’t waste energy when you have at least 18 other flat-scans to dismiss later in the week! Now come along…time is of the essence…SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS! =©=
