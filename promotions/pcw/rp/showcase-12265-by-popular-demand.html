They knew, that…the fridge couldn’t handle it anymore.] [So they started playing little jokes. Celery in the Hot sauce, Lettuce in the milk. If anyone has the claim on the most bizarre life it would be Pete. But things are about to get more bizarre…] Pete: Ugh, I need a ham sandwich. [Pete walks into the kitchen. On the floor a can of sweetened pears sits. Pete walks over to it, and looks at it.] Pete: Pears? Who eats this shit? [Pete shakes his head and picks up the can, and places it on the kitchen table. He opens the fridge. It all happened in a blink of an eye.] Voices: VIVA LA RESISTANCE! Pete: What the… [Suddenly, Hot Sauce opens and squirts into his eyes. The Milk falls and spills onto the floor. A banana from ontop of the fridge peels, and falls to the ground. Pete slips and falls onto the floor. The cupboard doors fly open. Boxes and cans spill out ontop of Pete, burying him in an avalanche of cans. The food in the fridge moves to the edge of the shelf.] Cheese: Did it work? Hot: I don’t know. [Suddenly Pete sits up. He’s yelling at the boxes, falling out of the cupboard. He looks at the food in the fridge and dashes for the ham. He grabs it and tries to take a big bite out of it. The Spaghetti launches, spinning around his legs and arms, trapping him in it’s doughy vine. Meatballs fly at his head. He slips again, and falls onto the boxes. Hot sauce leaves the fridge.] Hot: Celery! Commence Part 1! [The Bottom Drawer opens. The Celery comes out and opens Pete’s mouth with Toothpicks. Pete looks over dumbfounded, and starts to scream…but it’s too late. He’s trapped in the Spaghetti Ropes.] Hot: Operation Fatty COMMENCE! [Knives begin to dance with forks on the counter tops. Lettuce, Carrots, and peas fly out of the bottom drawer. Knives start to chop the lettuce and carrots. One by one the forks pick some of the lettuce up and walk down to Pete’s mouth. They drop their cargo in the gaping hole, and force him to chew it. Pete screams, not wanting to eat this health food. They force him to swallow the salad. Spinach is next, filling his mouth as if he was popeye. More screams. The food in the fridge dances around in victory. Hot jumps onto Pete’s chest. Pete looks on, with sad eyes, not knowing what to think. ] Hot: You did well Pete. Salad’s not so bad is it? [Pete nods] Too bad. We did get something you might like, though. [Pete looks inquisitively.] You want it? [Pete nods furiously] Ok. Lemme get it. [Hot sauce disappears around the corner. The food in the fridge continues to dance around while Hot gets Pete’s gift.] Hot: Hey you like Chinese don’t you Pete? [Pete nods slowly.] Good. We called Hong To, your favorite. And asked for a big, heaping order of… [The familiar midget appears from the depths of the pantry.] …General Tso’c Chicken…enjoy. [Tso leaps into the air. Pete screams as Tso comes closer and closer with a look that means bad, bad things will happen to Pete.] Pete: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! NO!!!!!!!!!!!!! Voice 2: …? Voice: Me either. It was fun while it lasted though. Voice 2: … [Standing in the doorway of Pete’s Bedroom, is General Tso, with a bottle of Hot Sauce in his hand. They shrug, and walk away…into the darkness once more. Was Pete still dreaming? One things for sure. Don’t Play with your Food. It might play back.] ~Fin~ Legend of the Fridge: Volume 3 "Look at you. SO helpless. So defenseless." A rotund man stands in front of a bright light. He looks down, smiling a sickly disgusting smile. The light shines off his bald head, with only trace amounts of hair to keep his ears warm, and a thin strip to give the illusion that he still has a full head of hair. His teeth are surprisingly white, and clean. Puberty, must have been a bitch for this man. He's ugly, his face is acne and pock mark ridden. His clothes wear like a second skin. That’s not a good thing when you’re 373 lbs. Then again, at 373 lbs, no one’s really looking at your t-shirt that reads “This isn’t a belly. It’s a Gas Tank for my LOVE MACHINE!”. I’m lying. Yes they indeed do look at that. After all, how can you avoid the nearly billboard-sized print? In any case, he circles around the light, staring intently at whoever or whatever is in the middle of it. "Your friends have already given you up. I know your secret. I know what you're hiding. But I want to hear it from you." Silence. The man gets angry "You better tell me. Now. Or else. There will be trouble for you and your rebellious friends." Silence. The man gets even angrier. "TELL ME THE LOCATION OF YOUR REBEL BASE!" Again, silence. Cold, horrible silence. Normally, this is the part where someone loses a kidney, or an arm. However, this being Pete, less tact is required. He reaches down, and yanks up the object of his interrogation. A 2 foot Hoagie is in his pudgy hands. "If you won't tell me your secret, then you're of no use to me!" He cradles it, looking at it sickly, and lowers his mouth to it. He takes an enormous bite, and swallows, seemingly without even chewing. He rolls his head backwards on his fat, sausage like neck and screams out, food spewing from his amazingly large orifice. He's won this battle. But the war would hardly be his. He turns on the light brighter and looks down again. He speaks to an army of hoagies below him. “Let this be a lesson to you all. You will not disobey your lord and master. I am the almighty. I am Pete. And I will eat every single last one of you if I have to until I get what I want.” Pete looks down at one of the hoagies. He screams and picks it up. “WHAT DO I WANT? WHAT DO I WANT? WHAT DO I WANT?! I WANT…I…SHUT UP!” Pete stuffs the large sandwich filled with cold cuts into his mouth and walks out of the room. Silence again fills the room as the hoagies stay where they were. Pete. The Man. The Myth. The Legend. Able to eat a small train. Able to leap giant ant hills. Faster than a speeding burrito in a digestive tract. He’s every cliché and then some. He’s Pete. And you shouldn’t expect anything… Well, just don’t expect anything. Please. Legend of the Fridge : Vol 2. “Take a look at this one, mate! Ain’t it a beaut?” Pete, dressed in survival gear is out in the wild tundra. He’s crawling on his belly like a snake. A very fat, well fed snake. He’s speaking with a horrible Aussie accent, and his shirt is showing his gigantic belly. The stitches of his shirt could raise the Titanic, as his buttons are stretched to the max. Pete pushes himself up to his knees, and looks straight ahead at the Crock in front of him. “Let’s take a look at ‘er. Take note of the flowery belly! This is obviously an older Crock! Man, watch out! These babies can be dangerous! Take care when approaching one of them! You’ll know he’s no threat when you smell baked beans! That mean’s ‘e’s full, mate!” Pete gets back down on his belly, and moves to the next Crock. “Take a look at this amazing tail! It’s a beaut as well! It’s an Albino Croc, and those are becoming more and more common thanks to a genetic mutation! Boy, If I had a dollar for every time I’ve seen one of these, I could buy myself Ice cream for years and years!.” Pete slowly reaches up the tail, and holds it back. “Careful now, this thing can bite! You gotta be careful when hunting crocks! Only do it if you’re trained in the art of Culinary! Watch it mate! Watch it…you’re all right!” Pete grabs the tail, and reels it closer. He puts his hand on the glass lid, and struggles with holding it in place. “Look at the powerful jaws! If I’m not careful, it could take my hand clean off!” “Sir? What are you doing?” a man asks. “I’m hunting Crocks!” Pete answers, matter-of-factly. “Sir, this is a church picnic for Our Lady of the Bleeding Daggers through the Eyes. Are you a member?” Quickly Pete stands up. He looks left and right and notices the parishioners looking at him funny. He smiles and waves at them, a shy, John Candy-esque smile. A few of the older ladies stare on in disbelief while the children flat out laugh at him. Pete makes threatening glances towards them, but tries to keep his cool. “Uhm. Yes. I am. I donated 13 million dollars last year to this fine church. Hell, I probably bought this Crock right here!” Pete says as he points to the Albino Crock he was just looking at. “Yep. It’s mine. See? It says ‘Proctor-Silex.’ That’s my…uhm…Mother’s maiden name.” The man looks at Pete with disgust and pity. He shakes his head and holds his arm out, putting it on Pete’s shoulder. “Sir, Our Lady just opened last week. This is the grand opening celebration of the new church in town.” “Oh…Right.” Pete is flabbergasted, as well as blubber ridden. “Well then, I guess…I’ll just…uhm…take this Crock and liberate him!” Pete picks up the Crock and starts to run with it. He shrieks with success, happy that his captured crock could be free to live in the wild with other Crocks. Suddenly, after 4 steps he trips falls, and sends baked beans spilling all over himself. “CRIKE!” 'Twas the Day of Thanksgiving 'Twas the Night before Thanksgiving, and inside the Fridge, Not a Condiment was stirring, not even the Fudge.
