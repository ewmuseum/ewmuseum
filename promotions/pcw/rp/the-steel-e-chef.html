The Steel(e) Chef.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today! Wheeee! I got 3 rps out! A scene of an eccentrically dressed, yet dapper Japanese man, chairman Kaga, surveying various fruits, vegetables and other sundry ingredients. A voice –over dub accompanies his consternation. Announcer: “Five years ago, a man had a dream – to gather the finest chefs of various styles of cooking and put them in a format to stretch the limits of cuisine. He devised a place where battles between these various styles of cooking showcased in a battle format. So secretly, he combed the nation, looking for his unbeatable men of gourmet which he called the Iron Chefs!” Three men and a garishly decorated platform slowly rising from the depth of the darkness in an impressive manner. Announcer: “Iron Chef French, Iron Chef Chinese and Iron Chef Japanese here in his vision Kitchen Stadium! He then invited chefs from all over the world to do battle to see whether his men, the Iron Chefs, could maintain their supremacy. Will the challenger walk away victorious, or will the Iron Chefs prevail? Who’s cuisine will reign supreme?” The music rises, the man surveying his beautiful Kitchen Stadium, almost a tear in his eye. Picking up a ripe, yellow pepper he takes a huge CHOMP out it’s waxy surface. A slow grin spreads across his visage… Pan back, revealing hundreds of lesser chefs filling up the stadium, the music reaching a crescendo. Title flash: IRON CHEF. Kaga: (undubbed) “If memory serves me correctly…” The sequence of words subtitled underneath a rich baritone Japanese voice stir the soul upon a journey across fantastical trip across the culinary landscape of the East. Camera shots of gorgeous blue lakes surrounded by islands of trees with the occasional temple structure perched atop mountains green. Kaga: “There is a man in America quite skilled with his hands. A master of what some would call Western Martial Arts, he is a mountain of a man created in his long forgotten god’s identity. It has come to my attention through a letter that he wishes to do battle with my Iron Chefs, in order to prepare himself fully against what he calls a ‘dastardly foe quite skilled in food preparation.’ This letter also stated that he is fully versed in what he called ‘West Coast Neo-Cuisine.’ After reading his note, I have to admit I was quite intrigued.” Shadowy still frames of a huge man with bulging muscles and wearing a toga, stirring pots and flipping flapjacks high into the air. He looks pretty angry for no apparent reason. Kaga: “Who is this man who claims to be the Steel Chef, with thoughts of conquering my own Iron Chef’s? He is Big Alan Steele, most notably of Primetime Championship Wrestling. In his letters he claims vast superiority over my Iron Chef’s, and we shall not shy away from his challenge! Today, we welcome Big Alan Steele of PCW into Kitchen Stadium in order to prove his Bold statements!” Shot of Big Al, arms folded haughtily across his expansive chest. Big Alan Steele: “I shall crush your pathetic Iron Chef’s within the confines of glorious Kitchen Stadium, thusly proving myself the one and only Steele Chef!! Hi-Keeba!!” Overhead view of the kitchen area and polite applause from the gallery off to the right. The camera settles in on the announcer, a small Asian man with a voice-over terribly out of place with his mannerisms. Next to him sits a wise-looking older gentleman with white hair and small spectacle resting on his nose. Announcer: “Welcome, ladies and gentlemen to Kitchen Stadium, where today we shall see our 7th North American, a man who claims to be the ‘Steel Chef’ take on our own Iron Chefs. Which form of metal shall prove more durable under the intense heat of Kitchen Stadiums gas stoves? I’m Kenji Fukui and with me, as always, is Dr. Yukio Hattori.” Hattori: “Always a pleasure.” Fukui: “And our judging panel is filled by some actress who giggles whenever she speaks, a lower house Parliament member with seemingly nothing else to do, a Japanese baseball player, and a psychic. Now, Dr. Hattori, I hear that our newest challenger is… a professional wrestler?” Hattori: (almost unbelieving) “Well, yes he is and I find it hard to believe that he expects to come in here and be able to stand up to the pressure of preparing gourmet foods against our Iron Chefs in a mere hour. Though he claims to be a ‘Steel Chef’ of much renown, I personally could find very little about his culinary background.” Fukui: “Hmmm… so you think he’s in for a rude awakening?” Hattori: “To be blunt, I’m not exactly sure exactly what Kaga sees in today’s challenger.” Fukui: (excitedly) “Well, let’s skip over the mindless chit-chat with our brainless panel of judges and get right into the introduction of our challenger!” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as the intense percussion beginning of the Olympic Theme Song rumbles through the Stadium! Golden fireworks shower across the kitchen, as the horns blare through the loudspeakers and the curtains are pulled back revealing a troupe of gorgeous dancing blondes, tossing rose petals across the red carpet from wicker baskets! With trusted manager Sue Saksnussum at his side, he strides forth into the Stadium, a look of pure confidence radiating from his Bold visage. His finest toga ruffling around him, he slowly makes his way to his spot at the center of Kitchen Stadium, facing the chairman on his podium, who comes down and shakes Steele’s hand warmly. Fukui: (breathlessly) “An impressive entrance worthy of a professional wrestler, but will he be able to stand up to YOUR Iron Chefs! Iron Chef French Sakai, Iron Chef Chinese Chen and Iron Chef Japanese Morimoto!!” Kaga: “Ah. It is good to see you again. I hope your trip was fine?” BAS: (grinning, grinding a fist into his palm) “I shall travel any amount of distance in order to hone my skills against chef’s of your caliber!” Kaga: (beaming) “I enjoy your spirit! Do you feel confident today?” BAS: (thinking a moment) “I shall do my best.” Kaga: (clasps hand together in glee) “Excellent! I wish you well, now choose! Which shall it be?!” BAS: “I choose Iron Chef Japanese!!” The man in silver bows at the waist, eyes locked on Big Alan Steele. Fukui: (in a state of near asphyxiation) “It’s the Steel Chef Alan Steele taking on Iron Chef Japanese Morimoto in a cuisine dream battle! Now, let’s hear the chairman’s ingredient…” Kaga: “I wanted our challenger to feel at home, as he hails from the southern portions of California, but also one to challenge the disciplines of a chef. I wanted to test his skills with a particular vegetable that was a especially difficult to serve as a main course. Its very crisp and can also wrap things. I would like to introduce today's theme. Today's theme is: LETTUCE!!' Haunting music as Kaga rips a cloth from a rising platform surrounding by smoke. A good one hundred heads of fine, fine lettuce lie stacked in a pyramid. Fukui: (absolutely frantic) “Alright, the ingredient has been revealed and the battle is over lettuce, a difficult ingredient to work with, indeed!! It’s Iron Chef Japanese versus the Steele Chef known for a Southern Californian flavor in a battle to the culinary death! Who will take it?! WHOSE CUISINE WILL REIGN SUPREME!!!” Kaga: (grandstanding) “Allez cuisine!!” A gong sounds and the Japanese chef races furiously to the ingredient stand. Fukui: (possibly dying from the excitement) “There’s the opening gong, Morimoto racing to the stand, filling up his basket with lettuce heads…” Hattori: “Yeah, there are actually three different kinds of lettuce there. As you can see, there in Romaine lettuce, which is the dark green, broad leafed kind there, and…” Fukui: (confused) “Wait a minute… where is our challenger?” The camera angle pans back. The Iron Chef is still tossing head upon head of lettuce in his basket, but Big Alan Steele is nowhere to be found… Hattori: “Perhaps the strain of the battle got to him and he ran like a little geisha girl…” Fukui: (in the throes of a stroke) “Look!!!” The head of Big Alan Steele slowly rises behind the pile of lettuce heads. The Iron Chef doesn’t seem to notice him as he rises up, holding back a giant iron skillet like a Samurai warrior… CLANG!!! …PAUSE… We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits dejectedly in front of the television. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum stands nearby, the remote control in her hand. She looks awful vexed at Big Al, who sheepishly shies away from her glare. Sue: “Not your finest TV moment, I’ll grant you that.” BAS: (sputtering) “But… but.. they told us to BATTLE, to hold nary a thing back! I though the entire purpose was to battle whilst preparing yon foodstuffs of great deliciousness!!” Sue: (sighing) “I guess it’s my own fault. I should have known you would have gotten the concept all wrong.” BAS: “But I was victorious! Hi-Keeba!!” Sue: “Al, you made NACHOES and tossed lettuce on them. You only won because Iron Chef Japanese had to be carried out of the stadium on a stretcher from your little sneak attack. They had nothing to compare it to.” BAS: (huffy) “A gladiatorial win is a win. Now I am fully prepared to take on the likes of the Chef, as he carries no title such as mine, honorary Iron Chef!! Hi-Keeba!!” Sue: “I doubt that your ‘training’ really qualifies, bud. And besides, haven’t you forgotten someone?!” BAS: (thinking long and hard before answering) “Nope.” Sue: (another deep sigh) “Chris Chase? Hello? The new Messiah, or whatever?” BAS: “Wouldn’t showing up constitute my preparing for him? I do not fear the children of false idols…” Sue: (hmmm) “Yeah, I guess he isn’t much to worry about. It’s all those alliance we have to watch for…” BAS: “Leave all comers to me!!” Sue: “Yeah, right.” BAS: (thumbs up) “Hi-Keeba!”
