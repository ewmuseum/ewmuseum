The Arrival

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/02 Suicidal Tendencies are, in many eyes, the best tag team ever assembled. They have made stops to numerous federations, and now a stop to PCW. [fade in] The phone rings inside the office in me and Ron's home in Houston. I get up from the couch and walk into the office. I pick up the phone and start to talk. 'Hello?' I sit for a couple of minutes as I didn't get a responce from the other end. I quickly thought to myself that it was those damn telemarketers calling. But then I looked at my watch and it was 930 in the evening. So I thought to myself that it wasn't them. So I tried once more to get an answer from the other end. 'Hello, who is this?' Still no answer. 'I'm going to hang up if you don't say anything!' As I was about to put the phone back on the reciever I hear a voice. 'Yes this is the front office from PCW calling for Mike Andrews and or Ron Jacobs.' It was me now that was sitting and not saying a word. Then it sunk in that we have been looked at by these guys before and now they have recently fired their roster so now they are looking for new wrestlers. Or are they? It was the time that I found out what they were calling for. 'Yeah this is Mike Andrews.' 'Hey Mike like I said I am from the office at Primetime Championship Wrestling and we have been trying to get a hold of you guys for a couple of days now.' 'Is that right!' I replied. 'Yes sir. Now you are probably wondering why we have been trying to contact you and Ron for days now?' 'Yeah!', I said in a sarcastic way. 'That is what I am going to explain to you. As you know we fired the entire roster from PCW and now we are starting from scratch. Now we have heard a lot about you and your brother and we feel that you will give PCW the action that it needs.' 'Really!!' 'Yes really! So I guess what I am trying to say to you is, if you are still interested we welcome Suicidal Tendencies to PCW.' 'Yeah you can mark Suicidal Tendencies down to make an impact on PCW.' 'We were hoping that you say you will come?' 'Why is that?' 'Because we already booked you and Ron into a match this friday against the Cowabunga Connection.' 'That is the fastest that we have ever been booked for a damn match, and who are our apponents again?' 'The Cowabunga Connection!' 'I thought that you said PCW is going to start from scratch.' 'Well we are.' 'Now from what I heard just now. The connection were in PCW before. I'm not saying we have a problem with that because we will just do what we have been doing in Capital City Wrestling and that is whip every team that steps in our way. So you can mark us down for being there on Friday.' 'Alright. Thank you for your time and it is great to have Suicidal Tendencies aboard.' I hang the phone up and walk out of the office. As I enter the room I see Ron lying on the couch. 'Mike who was that,' Ron asked. 'That was a guy from PCW.' 'Oh...what did he want?' 'What does every federation want Ron?' 'The services of Suicidal Tendencies!' 'We are on our way to PCW bro. And get this we have a match this friday against Cowabunga Connection.' 'No sh!t!!!' 'No sh!t bro.' 'We are on our way to dominate yet another promotion.' 'Well let's get our asses over there.' Me and Ron walk into our rooms upstairs to get our bags packed to go down to PCW. We are going to have to travel alot as we have to get back for Mainstream on tuesday for PCW. [fade out]
