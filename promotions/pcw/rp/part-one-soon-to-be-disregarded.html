Date: Still working on it. Long ago, there was a time where darkness was a way of existence for some. Ancient shadows mark the beginning of a silent breed, conceived and birthed in those dark ages. They were loyal not to themselves, but to their numbers. Swiftly, they would go off into the night, cascading as if the breeze though the darkness. Haste was made. Each to his own would serve well, tapping into dishonor to maintain his own honor. They had no choice, yet willed it so, that they might become the perfect apparition of a shadow. Successfully, they made due… Never detected… not until it was too late. To see one of these shifting apparitions was to see your maker. Failure was never an option, unacceptable to the dark race… They would not live with themselves, having been shamed by failure. However, they solved the problem in their traditional manner. They were true to their clan, deceitful to their enemies. They were strong, graceful, and deadly. They were the enigmatic race of the Medieval Japanese era. They were Ninja. Ninja… The original Pokemon of Japan. In their day, they were huge. Every household had a horde of Ninja’s… They went like hotcakes 24/7. Hell, in that day… 99% of the population was Ninja. (Note: The suicide rate was through the roof.) But, today with all the modern technology, and what not, in this ever evolving world, there is no need, seemingly for Ninjas… (Except in movies.) But, how could they be replaced?! WHERE HAVE ALL THESE STEALTH ASSASSINS VANISHED TO?!?! Thursday, Sept 19th, 2002Glenview, IL135 Washington St.8:00 PM Nightfall has arrived for the small, well-established “Tree City,” USA. It’s quite calm out, notably warm for a fall evening. Inside this cozy little home, there’s an electric hum. An old woman residing here sits in the glow of her microwave, watching a bag of popcorn pop. There is a small creak above… The old gal looks up for a moment, at the slightly swayed hanging light fixture above. Unnerved, perhaps, she decides to ignore it. As the kernels cease to pop, she rises to stop the microwave, popping the door open… A rustle of leaves in her front yard catches her attention now… She scurries to her living room window, pulling a curtain aside… Nothing. Wait… A pair of blurs amongst the branches of the pine tree in her front yard scale into the depth of the greenery… Turning slowly, the old woman heads back for her kitchen. Little does she seem to know, is that the small circular, slotted vent for her attic has been disengaged from its place, allowing access for two NINJAS leaping from the tree, and diving head first through the small threshold, landing with in the attic as lightly as a feather. A pack of Ninja gathered at the rear end of the house quietly tiptoe in a straight line towards the suburban home. The first in the line whips out a grappling hook, which is quickly launched, to the roof. A quick tug locks it into place and in a heartbeat they all scale the home, climbing the slanted roof, and heading for the chimney… Flood windows become doorways for a flood of Ninja… Meanwhile, the old lady dumps the fluffy popcorn into a big bowl, but suddenly she hesitates again. This time, there was a definitely a sound INSIDE her home… in fact, right inside her living room… She lets the popcorn, bag and all, fall into the bowl as she creeps into he living room, the area seemingly empty minus herself. Old Lady: “You’re all late. Such a noisy pack of fools…” “FOOLS?!” “No, you idiot, wait! Ahhh!” “GAH!” “I’m slipping!” There is a loud succession of thuds as the Ninja in the chimney tumble out the mantle, to the living room floor, black ash fogging up the room. As they roll out, they bring a big mess with them. Old Lady: “INFIDELS!! I JUST STEAM VACCED THE ROOM TODAY!” The old woman’s eyes glow red. A pair of Ninja’s drop down from the ceiling, some worm in through a ventilation shaft, a few more from a closet… The Ninja from the chimney rise, brushing themselves off. Ninja 1: “Oh yeah, so BECAUSE IT’S SO hard sitting around playing old house wife all day…” Ninja 2: “Do I smell popcorn?!” Old Lady: “HOUSE WIFE?!” Suddenly, the old lady rips off her shawl, sweater, and blouse to reveal he muscular, bald headed, male body, shrouded decoratively in black. Big Ninja guy: “Do you idiots have any idea how hard it is to keep this place tidy, so no one suspects us?! I slave night and day keeping this home in tact for my Clan to come home to… All I ask is…” “POPCORN!!!” --- Basement8:30 PM “Alright, alright, mouths shut, ears open! Weekly SNC meeting has begun!” The murmuring of the Ninja slows, almost 40 or so squeezing into the basement. They kneel in rows, before a stage of sorts, the back drop being a giant bronze gong. The big ninja formerly referred to as “Old Lady” tries to shut the clan up… Soon they all are seated, passing the popcorn around. The Clan leader sets up a tripod canvas with a display. The lights dim, and a beam of light shines upon the lone ninja. Sensei: “Tell your boss what is wrong with this?” The Clan all squint at the grid on the canvas, on which a line graph is illustrated. A slight murmur circulates. Sensei: “You don’t know?” Silence. Sensei: “WELL THE PROBLEM SEEMS TO BE RIGHT HERE!!” The Ninja master attempts to pinpoint a specific location on the diagram, only he accidentally unsheathes his katana… The canvas hits the floor in two.
