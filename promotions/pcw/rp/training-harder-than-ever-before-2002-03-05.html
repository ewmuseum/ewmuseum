Training harder than ever before

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/5/02 (If you're a little confused, lemme just say that FOX is doing some sort of celebrity boxing special sometime very soon. And, umm, well, erm, yeah :P) -------------------------------- “Come on!” A male voice shouts out. There is no picture, but there are sounds. The sound that stands out, other than the screaming voice, is a man grunting. 'You're weak!' More grunting. There is also the sound of leather hitting leather. Like someone is hitting something. 'Amanda hits harder than you!' 'Hey,' shouts out a female voice. Fade in We enter the scariest place ever created by man. A place so scary that big, bad Bill Goldberg refuses to enter under his own will. A place so scary that only one man not nicknamed 'Lightning Foot' has the sack to enter. A place known all around the World as THE BLOCK~! Enter THE BLOCK~! Inside, there are three people surrounding what appears to be a punching bag. 'I'm hitting it as hard as I can,' says a male voice from behind the bag. 'C'mon Ice,' says another male voice that sounds a lot like PCW legend Kid Wonder. 'If that's the hardest you can hit, you'll never be like me. You'll never win.' We zoom in closer on what is happening. The three people around the punching bag become much clearer. Two are very familiar. They are, as you probably guessed already, Kid Wonder and Amanda. The King & Queen of PCW are in their traditional gear. Amanda is wearing her pink halter top with 'STAR' written in silver glitter across the chest along with her tight, black leather pants. Wonder, as usual, wears his bright red genie sequin pants and a black 'In Like Flynn' t-shirt. The third person there, the man punching the bag, hasn't been seen in a while. But he is also a man who needs no introduction. The greatest (white) rapper to ever rap a rhyme, Rob Van Winkle better known to you as Vanilla Ice. Ice is in a black tank top and some grey sweatpants. He has some red boxing gloves on his hands and punches away at a large, blue punching bag. Right hand. Right hand. Gunt. Left hand followed by a mean right hook. 'Can't you do any better than that,' asks Kid Wonder. 'What else do you want me to do,' asks the Iceman. 'I'm hitting the damn thing as hard as I can, yo.' Vanilla Ice hits the punching bag with a few more right hands as Wonder holds onto the thing from behind. Van Winkle is starting to gasp for air as he has been working for a while. Ice hits the bag with one more weak left hook before stopping. 'What are you doing,' stubbornly asks the teen idol to Vanilla Ice. Vanilla Ice takes a deep breath. 'I'm tired,' claims the Iceman. 'You're tired,' asks Amanda. 'Tired?' Wonder looks at Ice and isn't happy. 'How can you be tired?' 'I've been working and training all day, dawg,' Ice tells K-Dub. 'I need a little break.' Kid Wonder paces back and forth. He doesn't have a pleasant look on his face. 'You know,' Wonder tells Ice, 'I bet your opponent is sitting at home and laughing right now.' K-Dub shakes his head, then looks at Ice. 'You're pathetic,' screams KW in Vanilla's face. 'You, Vanilla Ice, a man who stood up to Suge Knight, scared of some pop tart wearing pussy gloves.' 'I'm not scared,' Ice tells Wonder. 'You're scared,' says Amanda to Ice. 'You're scared to death,' Wonder tells Ice. 'You're scared of someone you don't even know.' 'That's the point,' Vanilla tells Wonder. 'I don't know who my opponent is.' Kid Wonder, not amused with Vanilla Ice's sudden turn to cowardice, gets right in his face. 'I go into work every single day not knowing whether or not I'm gonna get screwed over,' Wonder tells Vanilla Ice. 'But I never go in scared. Why? Because I end up winning in the end. If someone tries screwing me over, I'll hurt them. I'll hurt them bad. But never,' Wonder stops and smacks a bottle of water out of Vanilla Ice's hand. 'Never do I back down. Never! Why? Because I'm a man! I'm the man! I'm Kid Wonder! I made PCW!' Kid Wonder paces back and forth. A look of stone across his face. Vanilla Ice sits there, somewhat shocked that Wonder has totally gone off on him. As Wonder paces, he grabs a chair. He takes the chair and chucks it half way across THE BLOCK~! Wonder stops pacing and gets back in Vanilla's face. 'Now get back on that bag before I kick your ass,' Wonder shouts at Ice. The two are nose to nose. Vanilla Ice sighs. 'Fine,' he says as the greatest (white) rapper to ever live reluctantly gets up and goes back to the bag. Vanilla Ice stands in front of the bag. Wonder holds it from behind. Ice hits a couple right jabs to the center of the bag. They're fairly weak. A look of unhappiness crosses K-Dub's face. 'Hit it like you mean it,' shouts Kid Wonder. Ice punches the bag a little harder. The punches, however, are still fairly weak. Wonder's really starting to get ticked. 'Hit it hard! Hit it harder than anyone you've ever hit before,' shouts the teen idol at the former teen idol. A few more lefts and rights from the Iceman. Wonder still isn't impressed. 'Geez,' shouts Wonder in disgust. 'This is pathetic!' Wonder sighs. 'I want you to pretend that this bag is Jeremy Howard,' Wonder tells Ice. 'Now hit it! HARD!!' Vanilla Ice hits the bag with a big right hook. The bag barely moves. Wonder stares in disbelief. He then screams. 'NO!! HARD!!' Wonder is screaming at the top of his lungs. 'I WANT YOU TO HIT HIM HARD!! I WANT YOU TO SMACK HIM HARD!!' Kid Wonder starts smacking the bag with both his hands really hard. 'I WANT YOU TO PUNCH HIM HARD!!' Wonder starts to punch the bag with closed fists as hard as he can. 'I WANT YOU TO KICK HIM!!' Wonder starts to kick the bag hard with both feet. Alternating between his left foot and right foot every kick. 'I WANT YOU TO KILL THE PIECE OF SHIT LIKE YOU'VE NEVER KILLED ANYONE BEFORE!!' Kid Wonder screams and lunges at the bag. K-Dub tackles the punching bag and takes it off its chain or whatever it is that keeps the damn thing up. With the bag on the ground, not moving, Wonder starts to go to work on it. He punches the bag. He claws at the bag. He tears at the bag. He bites the bag. He is totally killing the punching bag. 'You suck you piece of shit,' Wonder shouts at the bag. 'I'll kill you!' Wonder keeps working on the bag. Amanda slowly comes up from behind her man. She grabs K-Dub by the waist and yanks him off of the bag. 'Let me at him,' Wonder yells while trying to break free from Amanda's grasp. 'Calm down, baby,' Amanda tells her boyfriend. 'It's only a punching bag.' Vanilla Ice stands there watching in disbelief. Kid Wonder stops trying to fight is way free. He stands there while Amanda holds onto him. 'It's only a punching bag,' Amanda tells her man. She gives KW a little kiss on the cheek, but keeps a hold of the guy. Kid Wonder stands there and takes a few deep breaths. 'You're right,' he says in between breaths. Amanda lets her lover boy go. Wonder looks down at the bag and takes one more deep breath. 'That's all for today,' Wonder says. He turns and looks at a stunned Ice. 'I hope you learned something today, Robbie.' Vanilla Ice stands there, still stunned over what just happened. Wonder turns around. 'Let's go, babe,' calmly says KW to Amanda. Amanda grabs her boyfriend's hand. The happy couple slowly walk out of THE BLOCK~! We're left with a dead punching bag and Vanilla Ice with his stunned look as we fade out.
