Headaches and Thoughts

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26, 2003 There were many people around with cups full of quarters, pockets full of chips, and money ready to spend, preparing to burn deep holes in their pockets. Though the probabilities were stacked against them, they still insisted on betting huge sums of money, in hopes of breaking out of the statistics. Needless to say this wasn’t bad for the PCW airship as it was racking in loads of extra funds for the already prosperous PCW. Everyone in the casino seemed to be having fun, except for one person who was tucked away behind a small table, drinking glass after glass of Scotch and Vodka. “Just one more time honey. I almost have this thing.” an older man tells his wife who looks a bit peeved at him. He throws the dice at the craps table and yet again loses, making a total of ten grand down the drain. The camera cuts around them as the wife gives him a hearty slap in the back of the head, parking itself right in front of the visage of Brianna whose lackluster appearance shows signs of depression and sleepless nights. She shifts uncomfortably in the chair as she grabs her forehead, trying to overcome a headache that just hit her like a tornado. The waitress, who has been serving her drinks all afternoon, walks back over with concern on her face. “Ma’am are you alright? You don’t look too good.” “No I’m fine. Just a little headache.” Brianna replies, continuing to stare down at her table, which is littered with empty glasses and balled up paper. “Well is there anything I can get you? Maybe some aspirin?” the waitress offers clearing the mess on the table. “Now that you mention it, do you have any paper?” “Paper?” “Yeah…I had some but I’ve run out.” Brianna motions to the balled up pieces of paper all over, and the lady shakes her head in acknowledgement. “Yeah I think I may have some, I’ll be back in a moment.” With that the waitress takes off, stopping at another table momentarily to take a drink order. Brianna continues to clutch at her forehead with a grimace. She bends over and picks up the black person next to her chair, pulling it up towards her. She opens it up and rummages through it, pulling out a small bottle of aspirin and tiny bottle of water. Two aspirins plop onto her outstretched hand and she swallows them down with water. “That outta do the trick.” Brianna mutters to herself as the waitress returns with a small stack of loose-leaf paper in her hand. “This is all I could find.” “It’s perfect, thank you.” As the waitress walks away to continue her duties, Brianna plucks a pen from behind her ear and starts to work on the paper. She marks the date on the upper right hand corner of the paper, and starts writing fluently using well scripted lettering. Tuesday Turmoil proved to be yet another catastrophe. I finally thought I would be one up on Dorn, but things didn’t turn out quite as planned. His hotheadedness screwed me out of another deserving win. My intentions for requesting the Steel Masked Man to be special guest referee were simply to show the fear inside of Dorn. I knew the Steel Masked Man would play it fair, and being the ultimate dick he is, Dorn had to complain about it all intentionally throwing the match. I suppose I shouldn’t blame him for showing how much of a coward he is, and proving that him being number one contender to the World title is nothing but a fluke. Honestly I wonder why he considers himself as some sort of god. He can’t fight his own battles, and only wins when he sneaks up and steals it. All week he is bound to test my patience. On Fusion I have that big match for the Continental Title, something that I have dreamed of for a very long time. After all of my hard work and effort I am finally in a league with PCW’s finest. The thing that troubles me is the fact that Dorn is bound to destroy my dreams. I am about two days away from the biggest night in my career, and I know it will bring so much joy to that bastard Tracer, if he can take it all away from me. The more I think about it the angrier I am, and the less I can think about anything else. For the past couple of nights I have played out all sorts of scenarios making sleep impossible. Even if I do try I won’t make it through the night because I am just so antsy about it all. What could be the defining moment of my career thus far, might not happen, due to someone’s hate. But I have relayed a message over and over in my mind, in a sort of rhyme to help me remember. Friday is my day to shine…If Dorn takes it away,Come Tuesday his ass is mine,Because there will be hell to pay! Just thinking about it lightens the mood around me, but I just wish there were some way to ensure that the bout for that title would be untainted. At the moment I just don’t see that as a possibility. Having finally been able to put all of her feelings out in the open on the paper, she folds it up and places it in her purse along with the black ink pen she had been using. She stands up from the table and places a twenty-dollar tip for the nice waitress who had shown a very caring nature. Brianna then prances over towards an open slot machine, and plops down on the stool, ready to join the mass in blowing money. The scene fades to black….
