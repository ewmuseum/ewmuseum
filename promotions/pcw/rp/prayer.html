Date: Later Days... Any man can ask for what he wants...but it takes a more than a man to get down on his knees and pray to a god that he refuses to acknowledge… -Savage Tranquility, page undetermined. There was nothing but peaceful silence surrounding the Shinto temple...nature’s sounds were quiet strangely. No birds chirping...no wind blowing...no rustling of animals in the bushes...Not a sound from the surrounding forest... Just silence... Then...in the background a faint “Swish” sound could be heard, repeating itself back and forth. A priestess dressed in traditional priestess garb stood silently as she swept back and forth with a wooden broom made of straw. The lower half of the woman’s face was visible, while the long black hair of the priestess was shielded from sight. Her lips were rigid in a straight line...neither smiling or frowning. Footsteps were masked under the sound of the sweeping. But when the owner of the footsteps approached the temple entrance from the steps. It was a woman dressed in silver silk blouse, tight black slacks, and black heels with sandy hair with black streaks hanging down to her shoulders. On her eyes were a pair of black sunglasses, and the name card in her front pocket read as follows: “Yuri “Quest” Takayama: Freelance Reporter” Yes...PURE’s very own news-geisha...The Quest...standing right there in the flesh at that temple. But what was her purpose there? Yuri bowed to the priestess, who in return bowed slowly at her. Yuri then walked into the temple and knelt down at the altar. She pressed her palms together, her hands clapping together and bowed her head. “Please...grant me an audience so that I may ask a request from the spirits.” The tinkering of bells answered her. She looked up, placing her hands in her lap. “Yuri Takayama here...you know...The Quest...” she began...turning this into a conversation more than a prayer. “And boy...do I have a story to tell you! Guess what I found out while looking through the PCW information databases! Now...I know what you’re going to say...you’re going to say, “Yuri...what were you doing in the PCW databases?” Well sorry...I’m an info-whore, so you can punish me later. What I found out was that PCW’s very own Neo Void God...you know...Akiyama...disappeared from sight at Supremacy! No one knows were he is! What a scoop that would be! So I went to the first person I could think of to tell!” Sado Island... Blitz Dojo... Yuri stepped into the training hall, where the Black Dragon Master, Inphino Blitz, was training vigorously on a heavy bag. Yuri watched as he unloaded with punches and kicks all over the bag. Then he stopped... “You have news...” he announced without looking at her. Yuri nodded to his back. “How’d you guess?” “You never visit me without having some information you’ve uncovered.” he admitted. “Though most of your news is gossip to me.” “But this is actual news, Inphino! You’ll be excited to hear this!” The trademark sigh of the Black Dragon Master came forth. “Speak.” Yuri began as Inphino began working over the bag again. “Well...I found out that on PCW’s PPV, Supremacy, Akiyama suddenly disappeared in a flash of white light!” He froze in his place. Then he loomed up to his full height, his arms lowered at his sides. “Hmmm...so he is missing?” Yuri nodded at the back of his head again. “Completely vanished! What do you think?” Blitz didn’t answer really...he just stared forward. Then Yuri jumped back as he ripped his fist straight through the heavy bag. Sand poured from it like beige blood from a speared animal. Then he snapped his leg out and knocked the bag clear across the room with a kick. The bag smacked into the wall before sliding to the ground, spilling more sand out quickly. Then Inphino looked back to Yuri...his face in the calm, mellow look he always retained. “...Pity...” “So Inphino didn’t take it well...what do I know? Since he wanted to take this good news for him the wrong way, I decided to tell the next person who deserved to know.” “Like...what do I care if Akiyama’s vanished from the face of PCW?” Mina de Margo sat in the garden in the back of the Blitz dojo on Sado Island. In her ususal goth/punk mix of black bondage pant/hip-huggers, PVC halter, latex thong, and fishnet overshirt, she stared at the sun with her sunglasses on. Yuri stood next to her, breaking the news to her. “Um...you know you’re not supposed to stare at the sun, right?” Mina flipped her sunglasses up, showing her closed eyes. “Like I said...so Akiyama’s gone, huh? How come I wasn’t invited?” “To what?” “The celebration. I’m sure that the guest list’s down the frickin road for miles for this party.” Yuri sighed herself. “Mina...don’t you think that Blitz should be looking into his disappearance?” Mina sat up for a second, looking at Yuri closely. “And where’s he supposed to start...the Void?” “No! I was thinking more along the lines of PCW!” “Ahh... I see what this is...” she smiled, placing her sunglasses back on her face. “You’re trying to get Inphino to jump to PCW, and hopefully with you as well. Well forget it, Quest...Blitz is sold on PURE for now. Find your big break somewhere else.” “She wasn’t much help either. Seemed like no one wanted to know about Akiyama and what his disappearance could lead to. And here I thought people actually cared about the important things. Maybe all they want is to sit in their recliners and watch the dribble one of the nVg’s opponent’s would put out...ya know, like having to sit back and listen to one of Duke’s NC-17 rated promos, or Pharaoh’s depression trips since he lost his godhood…but meh, ya know? So I said forget it...but I showed one last person.” Yuri stood on a balcony with a white lace canopy giving shade from the sun. Her eyes were locked onto the person next to her, her eyes looking for some form of gratitude for her good news. Next to her was a tall, shapely woman, dressed in a thin black bikini top, a black cut thong, and a satin skirt tied around her waist. Her long blonde hair cascaded behind her, like a waterfall of golden locks. The woman’s eyes scanned vigorously over the letters on the document in her long nailed hand…she was the type of woman you wanted a lot of things from her done vigorously. Then her eyes shot up towards Yuri, her honey orbs glowing into her with emotion. “Have you shown this to Willow?” she asked, a bit of concern in her silky voice. “Nah, you’re like the third person I told.” Yuri nodded to the woman, her arms folding in confidence. “Why, what’s she care if Ak-“ “Don’t you dare say that name in this house!” the woman hissed at her, her voice lowering to only they could hear. “You of all people should know better than that!” “Oh….right. I forgot about the whole thing. How long’s it been?” The woman put up 5 fingers. “And she’s never changed since then…something about she wants to be the same as he remembered her back from the old days.” Yuri sighed. “Yeah, I know…now that he’s gone, people should learn to appreciate him now. I mean, before, there were guys like Pharaoh, Duke Williams, Sylo, and other guys hating the ground he walked on, but now maybe they can rest a bit more easier with him gone.” The woman handed her the papers. “I have to go check on her now. Come on if you like…but don’t bring those papers with you. If she were to find this out, we’d have a lot of trouble on our hands.” Yuri nodded, and tucked the papers into her back pants pocket. Then the two of them walked on through the building, a large dwelling with white walls, high ceilings, and Greek architecture designs. They entered a long hallway and turned down into the last door on the left. Opening…they found a neat little room, a bed in the corner, a dresser against the wall, and a vanity mirror at the far back wall. Yuri and the woman looked on as another woman sat in the chair at the vanity mirror doing her makeup. The woman made no notice to their presence, but continued to do her makeup while humming a tune to herself. “Will?” the woman called out to her. The sitting woman in the room made no acknowledgement. Yuri looked at the woman and raised an eyebrow. The other woman shook her head, waving her off. Then she made another gesture to call the woman before them forth. “Marquette!” she snapped out of the blue. The woman at the vanity table stopped dead in her tracks, her hand frozen as she applied lipstick to herself. She slowly turned around and looked on with wide eyes. “Is that you, mi amor?” the mousy woman asked, a glint of hope in her voice. Hope was everywhere…even laced in the words of men like Pharaoh and Duke…but their hope was nothing more than hopeless. This kind of hope was undying. The woman shook her head. “No, put on your glasses for a moment, Will. It’s me, Virtuoso.” Her identity revealed…Charlotte Alexandros… world famous supermodel/ actress. Also known to the more secret world that she calls her personal life as The Virtuoso… because she was a prodigy of beauty…how those media types came up with names will never amaze me….like the Malice Man…the Saint…the Pharaoh…bunch of crappy names that most certainly made the person behind them look more foolish. But the Neo Void God…? That wasn’t a name…that was a title…that was who he was…not some media given nickname to hype up… By the way, if you haven’t noticed by now… the new woman is named Willow Marquette…maybe you’ve heard of her before now? The woman called Willow slipped on a pair of black wire framed glasses, and looked back. After her eyes focused, she gave a cute smile. “Ah! ‘Allo, Virtuoso! And is this who I think it is?” Yuri stepped forward and held her arms out, “Heya, kiddo!” Willow stood up, dressed in a black gown with a tight fitting torso and sleeves, and embraced Yuri Takayama. Leaning back out, she placed two kisses on her cheeks and smiled at her. “Yuri! ‘Ow long ‘as it been, my friend?” Yuri shrugged. “I dunno…maybe a few years? Ever since I got that job in Japan I haven’t been around as much in France. How about you?” Willow tilted her head to the side, her smile never wavering from her pink lips. “I’m still waiting for Akiyama to come and marry moi!” Yuri’s face snapped into discomfort as the woman holding her broke out into a laugh. She looked over to Virtuoso, who let forth a sigh. Yuri felt no other option but to chuckle along with her, pulling away from her slowly. “Uh…yeah. Hope he comes back soon!” her hand trailed behind her, checking on the paper in her back pocket. Still there…good. “Will, would you like to go out to lunch with Yuri and me?” Virtuoso asked her. Willow nodded with her smile sticking to her face. “Oui! I’ll get my shawl and I’ll be ready!” Yuri nodded as the girl hustled around the bedroom. “We’ll be waiting outside for you.” With that said, the two of them walked out into the hallway, heading for the front door of the home. “Geez…she’s been like this ever since she left graduate school?” “Yep…ever since Akiyama left her…she’s been a mental wreck. She really thinks that Akiyama is meant to be with her…but after seeing what he’s been doing with himself now, I don’t think she’ll even recognize that monster he’s become.” “I know…the Akiyama we knew in school and the Akiyama that exists…or existed today are two different people. Willow might not even recognize him.” Virtuoso sighed, her head lowering. “Either way, she’ll be better off if she never hears hide nor hair about him ever again.” Just then…a scream ripped through the silence of the house, the high-pitched wail crying out nothing more than raged insanity. Virtuoso and Quest ran back towards Willow’s room, running into the room to find Willow standing there with her shawl in her hands. “What’s wrong??” Virtuoso asked excitedly. Willow stared at them for a moment…and then that same polite smile washed over her face like before. “I thought I saw a mouse…but CEST LA VIE!!” she broke out into a giggle suddenly, her hand going to cover her mouth. Quest and Virtuoso looked at each other, giving a knowing look of pity…you know that look…the doctors give it to every one of Akiyama’s opponents after they crawl away from their thrashing. “Well, are you ready now, Will?” Quest asked her. Willow broke from her giggle and nodded to her friends. “Oui…I am, my dear friends.” She walked towards them, ushering them out of her room. As Quest and Virtuoso turned to leave…a trained eye would have noticed her back pocket… “Willow ran off the next night…looking for Akiyama. It’s been a few months since then…Akiyama’s back in PCW, but Willow is still missing. Virtuoso and I have been looking for her since that day…and I think we’re hot on her trail!!” Yuri gave a grin as she continued her “prayer”. “I’m here today to ask you to bless Willow, wherever she may be…and to please bless Virtuoso and me on our search for her. It’s a lot of work…it seems hopeless at times…but we’ll stick to it no matter what. It’s the will of man to seek out their desires…and its our desire to find our best friend before she gets hurt…. or even worse…find Akiyama. Thank you…” Yuri stood up, bowed to the shrine, and walked towards the entrance of the temple. The priestess stood there ready for her, a slip of paper in her hand. At the bottom of the set of stairs stood Charlotte “Virtuoso” Alexandros, dressed in a pair of stretch blue jeans and a white halter-top and black sunglasses. She looked on as her friend came walking down. “What’s your fortune say?” Virtuoso asked Quest. Yuri unfolded her paper and read it aloud. “ABSOLUTE DESTINY APOCALYPSE.” Behind the shrine sat the Neo Void God…in his ever-ready battle attire. His arms were folded over his chest, and his eyes closed, as if he were sleeping. Gods don’t sleep though… The priestess hopped onto the shrine, peering down at the god from her crouched position. “I told you this prayer thing would be a bit interesting…a lot more interesting than hearing Titi and Duke run their mouths.” Her hand tugged at her priestess garb, and she ripped it off of her body, revealing her usual attire of black vinyl and ice blue PVC. A shake of her head let loose those ice blue highlights that we all know her for. “Inside all man, lies their will…and their will is what powers them in their tasks. It is the will of man to seek out their desires…and as of right now you have a lot of men desiring to stop you, Akiyama. Imagine all the energy you can sap from these monkeys just because of their hatred….now that’s a lot of power there being wasted on a task that will never happen!” Growls came from the god deeply, but the Divine Prophetess patted him on his head. “Hey there…don’t worry about it. You’ll teach them later that they’re a better use for their energy besides wasting it on trying to stop your PCW rampage. You’ll show them all…believers and unbelievers…and their only refuge for salvation from your destruction…is prayer…” Bells rang out…no, wait…that was the chuckle of the Divine Prophetess. “Pray, mortals…pray for your lives before your souls…pearls before swine…So says the Divine Prophetess…”
