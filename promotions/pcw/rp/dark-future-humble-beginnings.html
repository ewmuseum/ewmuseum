Dark future... Humble beginnings

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Just now... Turning on the TV, the curious viewer decides to take a look at the happenings around the world. (*Blip*) “Today, all across America, panic has engulfed the hearts of millions…” Like all televisions, the volume is activated first, while the picture takes little longer. “Chicago, New York, Detroit, Los Angeles… Almost every major city in the U.S. has gone under total evacuation.” The picture, slightly interrupted by bits of static every once in a while, shows live camera shots of the streets in New York, completely crowded with cars that aren’t going anywhere. Pounding on their horns, the civilians make racket that only a severe New York traffic jam could. “The President himself is in hiding…” The shot cuts to another city. Chicago, where once again un-moving traffic jams make the roads useless. Only this time, rather than all the cars having a noisy passengers blasting their horns, all the cars are empty. Abandoned. And in the bottom right corner, a big Channel 2 News logo. “With us now, Channel 2 News correspondent Joan Sung, who has witnessed some of the evacuation mayhem in Chicago, Illinois. Joan…” The picture on the set cuts to another lifeless street, where a short Asian woman with a Ch. 2 microphone waits for her turn to speak. The screen splits in half, and now we have a view of the newsroom where a male news anchor sits behind the desk, waiting for Joan to respond. Joan: “Well, Tom, things here in Chicago began just like everywhere else... (Points a thumb over her shoulder, at the street packed with cars.) …As you can see.” Tom: “I’ve noticed that all the cars are empty. What happened…?” Joan: “The Chicago police and fire department has herded most of the population out of the area on foot… (*Zzzt*)” Joan puts a hand to her ear, a slight cringe of irritation upon her face. Behind her, two men with a great deal of baggage between them, weave as quickly as they can through the empty cars. Tom: “Joan, I take it there are still civilians trying to get out… Joan? Are you still there?” Abruptly, Joan is completely cut off, thus leaving the lone newscaster to his own screen. Tom: “More on ‘The Syko Crisis’ to come.” (*Click*) Easily tired with Channel 2’s boring story, he takes a gander at the next channel… “So no one told you life was gonna be this way? You job’s a joke, you’re broke…” Indeed a turn for the best! Now this is quality entertainment! Joey’s the funniest, no wait, Chandler! Oh, he can’t decide, he loves them all! “I’ll be there for you! Cuz you’re there for me, too! I’ll be there for you! Cuz you’re there for me…” Just then, a new, much more serious tune begins to play, as the picture becomes a new one. “Channel 5 Breaking News,” is boldly worded across the screen and after that, it quickly cuts to a new scene, of a single podium in front a wall that is masked by a curtain and an emblem entitled, “The Pentagon.” Damn it! He really wanted to watch Friends, too. “We now take you to The Pentagon, where Channel 5 is getting live coverage of breaking news. After twenty-four hours of evacuation in many cities nationwide, the military has decided to share information about what has been known only as, ‘The Syko Crisis.’” Taking his place behind the podium greeted by an attack of camera flashes, a rather uptight looking man in uniform taps the microphone, testing it. His name, “Gen. I. R. Dummas,” appears in white letters beneath him on the screen. The viewer has to chuckle. General: “Ahem…” (Winces, as another sudden burst of camera flashes reflect off his face.) “…As you all know, there has been a severe evacuation plan in effect for the last 24 hours, code named ‘The Syko Crisis.’ Syko is the leader in a small legion of terrorists, who are rumored to be somewhere in the Hawaiian Islands, or in Alaska. With the entire nation on alert, you might be asking why we aren’t hunting this organization down instead of hiding from our own homes. Well, right now, the President of the United States may not have many options, and made it clear that it was dire to have all major U.S. Cities evacuated.” The General takes a moment while mummers erupt from those before him. General: “Without giving out too much top secret information, I can say this; they have stolen a top secret weapon from the U.S. and with it, no place on Earth is safe. Syko is supposedly in the process of making his demands… but until then, it is a waiting game. For now, we are in the process of warning our allies and our enemies. Japan, Russia, China… Everyone needs to be wary…” Worldwide now? (*Click*) Tapping into the power of his expensive dish network, with its 8,000,000 million channels, the somewhat aggressive channel surfer takes a look into some foreign affairs. “(Rapid Japanese rambling.)” A very edgy male Japanese newscaster pounds angrily on the broadcast desk, as he shouts into the cameras filming him. Behind him, the wall flashes different colors that have mind numbing effects, while in the upper right hand corner; a blurred headshot of Syko appears. Japanese News Anchor: “(More rambling, followed by…) BIG BANG!” (Pounds fist into open palm.) Too much flashing… feeling himself slip into a coma… (*Click*) The bottom of the screen displays a moving banner that announces the status of the stock market, which has taken a sudden crash due to the total chaos in the nation. “Cok: -12%, Xny: -3%… ect, ect…” “’The Syko Crisis’ has put the market at a total stand still.” (*Click*) “Da, that is correct. Silly Americans cannot handle their own terrorist.” (*Click*) “Today, the British Prime Minister announced that upon the destruction of the U.S., ‘we are going to take our bloody country back, you bloody b^stards! Finder’s keepers, losers…’ he then rambled a bit more, then had tea and crumpets. More on ‘The Syko…’” (*Click*) Some network from the other side of the world, where a news anchor tries to pronounce “Syko Crisis.” (*Click*)(*Click*)(*Click*)(*Click*) “Man, I hate when there’s some terrorist crap… It takes over every network in the world…” Thinks the angry viewer. One last chance; he’ll give TV one last chance. (*Click*) “And now, the Food Network presents Emeril live… DISCUSSING THE HORRORS OF ‘THE SYKO CRISIS!’” Suddenly, there’s a knock at the door. The scene is a rather small room, with a couch and a TV in front of it. We find the super villain supreme, Syko the Goblin, in a magnificent militia attire, laying across the couch, smashing a small, black remote control into his forehead repeatedly. Syko the Goblin: “Come in…” Opening the door, and stepping in, a young man in a uniform similar to Syko’s is followed in by a huge uproar of chanting. Holding the door, and making an “After you” gesture, he smiles. Minion of Syko: “Lord Syko, I think your people say it all…” It fills Syko’s ears like a chorus of the gods. “ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!” Suddenly, rolling off the couch to the floor, the evil lord of doom stumbles up to his feet, and makes sure he looks all right. Syko the Goblin: “Oh, right! My victory speech… I almost forgot! I can barely contain my self! And to tell you the truth… I was getting tired of this prison cell, if you know what I mean.” “ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!” Minion of Syko: “It’s a waiting room for your comfort, sire.” Syko the Goblin: “Yeah, but if I ever see myself on television again, I’ll blow my brains out. Now, to the podium, fool!” Minion of Syko: (Murmuring.) You’re the one going to it… fool. “ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!” Dashing out of the room, his black velvet cape flapping behind him, Syko emerges out into a massive indoor auditorium. An elaborate set-up of a stage, decorated with the emblem of Syko, where a single podium sits waiting to be mounted, all before a roaring crowd of uniformed men, standing in rows. Closed fists in the air, they only chant louder upon the sight of Lord Syko. Rushing up this his podium, the villainous Goblin thinks about how he should start. Perhaps, “We’ve got their weapon, and we know how to use it, oh yeah!” No. Maybe, “Those fools, we have them where we want them.” Hmmm… What about, “To fire, or not to fire… How about I flip a coin?” “We’ve made our demands, lets see if those punks dare to deny us of them…”? “ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!”“ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!”“ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO! ALL HAIL, SYKO!” He loves it… If he could stand here forever, and never start the speech, it would be heaven. Maybe he’ll build just a little bit more… [Elsewhere] …Anticipation. I wait in total anticipation. I can only wait now. My most trusted affiliates would stab me in the back, right now, only to prove that their solution to this crisis is the right one. “U.S.A. will not cooperate with terrorist!” “We have no choice!” “We’ll see the demands when they come!” “Gentlemen, do you know where we are? I don’t think there’s a safe room big enough for the entire U.S., let alone the whole world!” “WE NEED TO COMB THE RUMORED AREAS, AND ATTACK!” I say nothing. The 34-XR2 prototype is a weapon that combines the power of atom blast force with the rolling effect of a steamroller. Metaphorically that is. Basically, it could roll across an entire country, knocking it flat after it detonates. And what we aren’t sure of is if it will ever stop rolling once it starts. It’s just a prototype. They may argue, but they know as well as I do. We aren’t going anywhere until we have this bastard's demands. Damn you, Syko, whoever you are. I am the President of the United States. A new scene now of a simple enough room, long and rectangular. The walls are lined with what could easily be over one hundred feet thick. A long table, eight men seated on each side, and the President himself at the head, is where the action is. Every suit in the room is fuming, whether he is a high military official, or a highly ranked governmental officer. Everyone, except the grand decider of it all, the President, who sits in silence merely watching the chaos before him. They are the leaders of a country hiding in a secret safe room, deep below the ground. The Presidents eyes wander to the single door in the room. “This Syko is a menace! WE NEED TO ACT BEFORE HE DOES!” “What, and risk him using the weapon?” “We can’t run forever!” “We can’t risk it!” The President has just about had it when suddenly that single door bursts open, causing even more commotion than before. But, the President is the first to spring into action. Rushing in to meet the President, a small balding man holds out a single piece of paper, prouder than any man on the planet. He is followed by a group of men similar to himself that flocks around him looking for some glory. Man w/ Paper: “Mr. President, we have Syko’s demands!” Flock Member: “Yeah, we helped him pull it out of the fax machine!” Rest of the Flock: “(Murmur of “Yeah's.”)” The President greedily snatches the paper out of the hands of the intern, and begins eyeballing it thoroughly. Man who used to hold THE PAPER: “Your welcome Mr. President…” But, he is cut off, broken hearted, by the rest who gather around the aggressively reading President. After a few moments of thoroughly checking the listed demands, he finally looks up. President: “What the Hell kind of demands are these?! ‘Change all official’s names to something like that Dummas guy. He funny.’ ‘Supply my organizations with 300 gold teeth.’ ‘Take my mom out on a date?’ THESE AREN’T REAL DEMANDS!” Everyone erupts in anger… it’s then the President sees something in fine print that he missed. “You have five minutes to supply my demands.” President: (White as a ghost.) Oh, crap. “AHHH~!” Waking up, horrified by such an awful nightmare, Syko finds himself totally drenched in sweat. Syko the Goblin: “God, what a terrible dream… Wait. That’s the best damn dream I’ve ever had!” “I TOLD YOU, ‘NO MORE SLEEPING IN MY DUMPSTER!’” Looking around, and getting a grip on reality, Syko finds that he actually is in a dumpster full of trash, and that there’s a well-rounded Italian fellow yelling at him. The Italian guy wears a shirt that says, “Vinny’s Pizza,” and is holding an empty bucket that still has beads of water running down the sides of it. Syko the Goblin: “Oh, hey Vinny. I just had the best dre…” Vinny: “GET THE HELL OUT OF MY DUMPSTER!” Moments later, the dirty Goblin is back on the move, walking down a small town sidewalk. Syko the Goblin: (Hands in pockets, and head down.) “Stupid Vinny. Well, it doesn’t matter anyway. Last night was the last time I’ll ever sleep in a dumpster! I got me a P-C-W contract, and this time, I’ll laminate it! MUWAHAHA! And if Kid Krazie wants to mess with my awesome powers, he will meet his fate!” Syko stops for a moment, and takes a look up to the clear sky, thinking about the dream. Syko the Goblin: “Every Super Villain must start off with humble beginnings. Even the most powerful. (Eyes shift.) Now, to purge Kinko’s, MUWAHAHAHAHA!” fin
