Cowabunga Campaign '02: Alabama is weird, dudes

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6 COWABUNGA CAMPAIGN '02 - Alabama Is Weird, dudes. Support the most tubular, gnarly, and righteous dudes, the Cowabunga Connection, become the next PCW Tag Team Champions! [The scene opens on the large, blue sign that reads 'Welcome to Alabama!'. The scene turns to the highway and a white Chevorlet van zips down the road. It's an odd van with two very familiar faces on the side. Those faces are 'Totally Tubular Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the Cowabunga Connection. Tommy's rich, prize winning smile, tan face, and baby blue eyes just scream for the bodacious babes to come running that way. Harry's...well...his...stoned smile, no thats not it. Harry's very...bloodshot eyes? No, that's not it either. Well, Harry has his good qualities and when it comes to you'll be the first to know] [The Cowabunga Connection has been fighting hard the past month or so. They've taken on the baddest and the best the PCW tag division has had to offer. Spring Fling is right around the corner and the four way tag team match is looking totally righteous from a wrestling fans standpoint. The Connection has helped elevate a dead PCW division into one of the most exciting divisions. At one time the tag titles meant very little, just pieces of gold to two random wrestlers. Now with the additions of new blood and true tag teams, the titles mean more than just gold, they mean total domination of a strong PCW division] [Harry and Tommy, with the help of their skateboarding friend, got their pictures painted on the side of a van they purchased. This is their 'campaign' van to help spread their placement into the tag team title match at Spring Fling. They are going across America, spreading their good word, and most of all just trying to find some BODACIOUS babes. After ripping off Raggamuffin for the paint job, and gaining campaign managers, Will Preston Mesquire and Ned Theodore Hogan, the Cowabunga Connection is on their way to their master plan] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Don't they sacrifice virgins in Alabama, man? [Harry seems reluctant to go to Alabama as the four men are gathered around in the front of the van. The Tubular One wears his colorful Hawaiian button down dress shirt and a pair of Oakley sunglasses to shade the warm Alabama sun from his baby blues. Harry is in his usual attire of a Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. His long brown hair is let out of the pony tail and blows gently in the wind, mainly from having his window cracked. Will Mesquire and Ned Hogan lean against the driver and passenger seats from the back, listening in on the plan to attack Alabama with the most gnarly dudes around] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: They don't sacrifice virgins here, dude. They stopped doing that years ago. If they still did it that would be totally bogus. Ned Hogan: Most totally, dude. Will Mesquire: Yeah, talk about a most wasted use of a virgin babe. [All the men nod their heads in agreement, thinking about virgin girls and how...well, lets save that for a little later] [The van pulls off of the highway, entering a small town called 'Sacriavirgin'. It seems a bit odd for Alabama but its a good place to start the campaign. Tommy sits back in the driver seat, taking in the nice scenery along the small, country road. The farms, the fresh southern air, COUGH, well...it was fresh until Tommy got a wiff of that cow manure] Harry: That smell is awful, man. Mild Scallions: TOTALLY! [Harry smiles to himself as he sees the piles of cow manure on the farmland. He could make a small fortune if he had his hands on poop like that. You can get a killer buzz off of that poo, and people would buy and buy as long as they got high. Hey, that kind of rhymed, man. People would buy and buy, to just get HIGH!! Harry decides that he's going to go ahead and make a song out of that someday when he's a famous rocker/drug dealer] [The van takes a small right turn and begins to enter what this place would call a downtown area. A bank is what they meet first on their way down the main roadway. The bank is called 'Sacrifavirgin, get a grand'. Talk about an odd name for a bank. On Tommy's side, just past the bank is a local bar, a barber shop, and then little knick-knack shops. On Harry's side the grocery store, tailor, shoemaker, and burning cross store. BURNING CROSS?!?!] Harry: Tommy, why is there a store called 'Burning Cross', man? [Tommy looks over at the store as they slowly glide through the ghost town like place. Tommy shrugs] TTT: I don't know, dude. Ned Hogan: This place is most spooky, dudes. Will Mesquire: Yeah, its like something you'd see in those most frightening Halloween movies. [Tommy stops the van in the middle of the main road. He feels that this is the spot to begin the campaign. It's early afternoon, but he expected this place to be a little more hopping than this. Maybe everyone is just inside the places at once, which is a most freak accident if you ask Tommy. He grabs the CB Radio which will amplify his voice by five times with Raggamuffin's good technical skills. Harry seems a bit scared to go through with this here, in 'Sacriavirgin, Alabama'] Harry: I don't think this is such a good idea, man. There is nobody around. TTT: No worries, dude. With this megaphone system people can hear us from, probably, miles around. It's going to be righteous, compadre. Relax. [Tommy smiles, assuringly, and then presses the button on the side of the mouthpiece, ready to begin the campaign. His deep, moronic like voice comes off very loud and echo like] TTT: DUDES AND DUDETTES! [Everyone covers their ears for a second, not expecting that to be so loud] Ned Hogan: That was MOST HEINOUS. Will Mesquire: Totally. Since we are your campaign managers, we should speak for you, dude. [Tommy thinks about that for a second, smiles and then hands the handheld microphone piece of to the Mild Scallions. They begin the campaign in Mild Scallion fashion] Ned Hogan: This is Ned Theodore Hogan... Will Mesquire: And I'm Will Prestion Mesquire.... Both Men: And together....we are...THE MILD SCALLIONS!! [They play air guitars together and the loud sound of guitar solos rings through the small downtown area. Harry looks frightened and whispers over to Tommy, who is a bit confused by the guitar-like sound] Harry: How'd they do that, man? TTT: I don't know, but it was bodacious. Harry: That's scary, man. Ned Hogan: We are your MOST EXCELLENT campaign managers for the MOST EXCELLENT Cowabunga Connection of the MOST EXCELLENT PCW!! Will Mesquire: Most Excellent, Ned. We need your MOST EXCELLENT support for these dudes as they go into Spring Fling to take on some bogus dudes. Ned Hogan: Totally, Will. Like Golden Game. Those dudes are just a bogusfest waiting to happen. It was MOST EXCELLENT that they lost the tag titles, but they lost them to yet another bogus team, Politically Incorrect. Will Mesquire: Without a doubt, Ned. Those politicians reek of bogusness. [Harry looks over to Tommy with a scowl on his face, and he whispers to Tommy again] Harry: Reeking of Bogusness? That sounds familiar, man. [Tommy shrugs with a smile on his face, pleased with the great job his campaign managers are doing] Ned Hogan: Fear Factory...lets not even talk about those dudes because the Cowabunga Connection has wasted those dudes so many times! Will Mesquire: TOTALLY, NED!! Ned Hogan: So, lets meet your MOST EXCELLENT tag team, The Cowabunga Connection. TTT: I'm 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. I'm a Scorpio, I'm gnarly, and most of all...I'm TOTALLY TUBULAR! Ned Hogan: EXCELLENT!! Harry: I'm 'Heavy Metal' Harry...uhhh...ummm...I have a wide penis. [Everyone looks at Harry strangely for a few moments. Harry looks as if he hadn't said anything at all, just staring off down the road with his eyes open widely. The eyes of the other three men are still locked on Harry, and wondering why he'd say something like that to all the gnarly fans in this small Alabama town] TTT: That was totally bogus, dude! These dudes and dudettes didn't want to hear about your wide weiner! [Harry just stares forward with eyes wide] Ned Hogan: Yeah, dude. Will Mesquire: Without a doubt, homebro. TTT: Are you even listening, compadre? [Tommy then looks in front of them to see a very bodacious babe running full speed toward the van. The blonde woman wearing a red and white striped belly shirt, tight, ripped daisy dukes, and no shoes. Her tan legs go on forever and her fairly large chest bounces up and down with each long stride she takes. All four men seem hypnotized by the gnarly babe that runs their way. The megaphone certainly has its magic going for it, that's for sure] Bodacious Farmer Babe: HELP ME!! PLEASE!! [She pounds on the hood of the van, looking back behind her, and then back to the van. The four men just sit there staring down her shirt as the top of her chest is covered in glistening sweat, making it all that much better] Bodacious Farmer Babe: Are you going to help me or what?!?! [Tommy snaps out of it and gets out of the van] TTT: Sure, bodacious babe. What seems to be the problem? [She then points down to the end of the road. A horde of large dudes in red robes and red hoods begins trudging their way toward the van. Tommy stands in absolute shock, now putting the pieces of the puzzle together. Sacriavirgin is short for...well, not he doesn't have the puzzle put together. All he knows is that this babe in front of him is most bodacious and he needs to get her to a safe place, namely alone with him for a game of Yahtzee. Stop thinking like that, Tommy!! You need to move, QUICK!!] Bodacious Farmer Babe: They are getting closer!! [The horde now has burning crosses raised above their heads and begin chanting some weird stuff in a hick like language. Tommy grabs the girl and throws her in the back of the van with the Mild Scallions. Tommy hops back in the drivers seat and tries to start the car, BUT IT WON'T START!!!] TTT: BOGUS!! Bodacious Farmer Babe: Those guys grabbed me from my farm early this morning and they were trying to burn me alive!! MY FATHER IS ONE OF THEM!! [She is breaking into tears as Harry slowly turns to her, seemingly to console her, but decides to ask something only Harry would ask] Harry: Do you happen to be a virgin, man? [The woman seems dumbfounded] Bodacious Farmer Babe: What kind of question is that? If you think I'm going to be taken advantage of in this van, you've got another thing coming... [Harry smiles and begins laughing] Harry: Judas Priest RULES, man!!! [Tommy is still trying to get the van to start as the horde of red hooded bogus dudes is only five feet away] Ned Hogan: We aren't going to do anything babe. We just need to know if your a virgin to help you. Bodacious Farmer Babe: Well, yes I am...I am just waiting for the right man to share myself with... [Harry smiles to himself, knowing he was right all along] Harry: I told you they sacrifice virgins around here, man. [The horde begins banging against the van, rocking it back and forth, trying to tip it over] TTT: Hey bogus dudes!! Do you mind giving it a push!! [The van just rocks and tilts even more now] Bodacious Farmer Babe: I don't want to die like this!! There is so much I want to do in life. There is so much I need to accomplish! I need to make a man very happy, please his needs, and do anything that he asks of me. [All four men look at her with shocked eyes. Then they all look to one another. Tommy pushes down on the ignition and the van fires up] TTT: BODACIOUS!!! Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!!! Mild Scallions: MOST EXCELLENT!! Bodacious Farmer Babe: YEEEEEEE HAAAAAA!! [The van peels out and runs over a couple red hooded bogus dudes. Tommy races out of the town with ease, taking the babe to safety and adding another person to the campaign crew, and what an addition it is. Tommy can feel many great games of Yahtzee coming out of this] Cowabunga Connection: COWABUNGA CONNECTION FOR PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, DUDES!!! [The scene then fades to black]
