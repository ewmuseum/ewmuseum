Aboard the Wrestle Vessel

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/22/02 (PPV RP #2) Roleplay #2 for The Pay Per View takes place aboard the Wrestle Vessel, just keep in mind that this could take place on the weekend or something when all the wrestlers would have left the United States. *nod* The scene opens up on the boat known as the Wrestle Vessel. The site of the PCW's latest Pay Per View. They've been in motion for a few hours now, and Jordan X Shabazz and company are enjoying themselves partying. But now everything seems to have calmed. As they get later and later into the night, Shabazz, Rush, and Thug roC have all decided to stay cooped up in their room doing different things. Our scene opens up with Rush, Thug roC, and Jordan X Shabazz all standing in front of something. Something that they appear to be treating like the Holy Grail, nobody wanting to get too close to it, yet they still watch it with intent. Rush is the first to break their silence. Rush Mason: This... this friends... is beautiful. This is the way God meant for the world to be lived. We're on a cruise in the middle of the world's most beautiful place, and we stand here, in front of this... OH I LOVE YOU BON DIEU! Jordan X Shabazz and Thug roC look at each other. Thug roC mouths 'That's one CRAZY muthafucka' to Jordan X Shabazz about Rush Mason. Jordan nods in agreement. As Rush gets on his knees and begins praying to this 'holy grail'. Jordan X Shabazz grabs Rush by his shoulder and speaks. Jordan X Shabazz: Rush, it's just a bucket of chicken man. We now see that what they were treating as if it was the Holy Grail is nothing more than a bucket of Church's chicken. Thug roC: And it's not even the good kind of chicken, it's that ghetto shit. Who the fuck wants to eat Church's chicken? Rush Mason gets from his feet, now holding the chicken bucket close to his chest. Rush Mason: You think that's going to work on me? All those reverse phywhatawhatever. You know, them white people do it on Television all the time. Like John Crichton acts like he's in love with Jaden so she won't love him... you know. Jordan X Shabazz looks at Thug roC, and Thug Roc looks back at Jordan, equally as puzzled by what Rush is talking about. Jordan X Shabazz: I think we've been on the boat too God damned much. You need to calm down Rush, you're getting sick in the head. Besides, Crichton and Jaden make a cool Oreo couple. Jordan X Shabazz holds in laughter, but not for long. Shabazz, Rush, and Thug roC are soon all laughing like little bitches at the thought of Crichton and Jaden together as a couple. As they are laughing, Shabazz makes the first move towards the chicken that Rush clutches in his hands close to his chest. He doesn't get it though, because Rush was expecting the attack, and he sidestepped it. Now with Shabazz holding his head on the floor of their room, leaning on his bed, Thug roC eyes the chicken wearily. Rush tries to reason with him. Rush Mason: Calm down man. I need the chicken most to train for my Pay Per View match; I got to know Kramer Wild Wing's mindset. You know, that type of stuff. For instance, Shabazz over there has been trying to get into white women as a way of training for Crichton and Jaden on the Pay Per View, ain't that right Jordan? Jordan X Shabazz is ashamed of this. Jordan X Shabazz: It's true Roc. I've been searching the boat for some fine, white booty, and I can't find it yet. But you've got to understand, it's all for training. Thug roC puts his hand on his chin, and goes into deep thought. Pondering how he could steal the greasy chicken bucket from Rush's clutches, and pondering how he could get both of them to leave him alone with the chicken. Suddenly, the idea pops into his head. Thug roC: I GOT IT!! YESSAH! Thug roC motions for Jordan to get off and ground and come over. And that's exactly what he does. Thug then proceeds to tell Rush and Jordan of his plan... Fade.... *** We fade into another scene, on a different location on the boat. We see Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, and Thug roC sitting behind a table with the word 'Interviews' in front of them. They had posted handwritten fliers everywhere on the boat that they could. It was around 4:00 AM in the morning, so they were in for a long day, but it was worth it. Applications/Interviews were being taken for the job of being Jordan X Shabazz's 'personal assistant' for the day, in preparation for his match with John Crichton and Jaden. Sure the idea was ludicrous and unbelievable, but Shabazz was sure that several white women would be up for a little bit of lovin' with a superstar like himself. As Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC wait for the first applicant. Rush decides to torture his friends by slowly eating the Church's chicken in front of them... okay, who am I kidding? Rush tortures his friends by digging into the chicken like there is no tomorrow. The bucket that once had 40 chickens is now down to about twenty. And Rush is showing no signs of letting up. Sweat covering his forehead, and grease clogging his arteries, after a few moments, a woman finally shows up. She's African American, and apparently from Jamaica. In her right hand she carries what appears to be marijuana 'joint.' Shabazz eyes her wearily. Jordan X Shabazz: May I help you? The woman takes a hit from her joint. Woman: Yes mon. Me 'ere for da interview. Jordan X Shabazz looks down at the table. He realizes that he has only opened interviews for white people, because he wants to get into the mindset of John Crichton (yeah right) for his Pay Per View battle. He doesn't want to turn her away because she's black because it would be against everything that he has stood for in the past five months. Shabazz thinks quickly about what he could say to her that would make her leave, and would not be racist. Jordan X Shabazz: Uh.... no drugs over here. I'm sorry; you can't be here for the interview. Thanks for coming out today. The woman looks at Shabazz as if he's joking. What Jordan doesn't know is that in Jamaica, it is not unusual in the lest to be smoking substances that would have been illegal in the United States or Canada. She soon realizes he's dead serious, and she walks away. After a few minutes of nobody else walking by, Shabazz ends the madness. Jordan X Shabazz: Screw this Roc, Rush, I'm gone. Rush Mason, who has now downed the entire chicken bucket from Church's, tries to stop the Civil Rights Activist. Rush Mason: Where are you going? This was just getting good! It's almost 6:00 AM, the women should be pouring in soon. Shabazz doesn't turn around, instead, when he's walking away, he shouts. Jordan X Shabazz: I haven't slept all night. I'm going to the club to find some poon instead of waiting for it. Rush Mason and Thug roC, for lack of a better thing to do, begin to jog behind Shabazz as they follow him to the clubroom. The clubroom is a huge place. Usually full of professional wrestlers looking for a good time. Women from Jamaica and attendants of the boat usually frequent the place in the early hours of the morning due to the fact that they are working most of the time during the day. As Shabazz enters the place, he is immediately hit in the face with the smell of marijuana, the smell of cigarette smoke, and the smell of alcohol. Shabazz scopes the room, and heads towards the bar, where he sits and orders a Long Island Ice Tea. Soon enough, Rush Mason comes and sits beside him, Rush doesn't order a drink, but he speaks to Jordan. Rush Mason: Hey, tomorrow, I got the coolest idea on what we can do. Jordan X Shabazz looks at his long time friend/tag team partner/stablemate, perplexed. Jordan X Shabazz: And what is that? The excitement on Rush' face becomes visible. Before Rush tells Shabazz what he was thinking of, we fade to black. To be continued.
