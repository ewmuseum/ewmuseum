Poems of Inspiration (Part 1)

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/07/02 Poems of Inspiration by El Romantico Chapter 1 A lonely old woman is shown carrying a wagon with all garbage and cans inside. Snow fills the streets and her hands are frost bitten with the bitter cold. She’s sick and is pushing herself to every last movement. She bens her body over a rancid Barcelona garbage can to find anything that she can use to feed herself or to sell. Over and over she’ll look and find nothing, but she doesn’t stop. She comes out every day and feels the harsh defeat of LIFE. Today, today is different, because today she’ll get words of inspiration that may change her depressing outlook. She’s taking deep breaths that leave their impression in the air. Families pass pulling their children away from the woman, and then giving her dirty looks. What they don’t know is she was once just like them. Every person who you may look at as an outcast was once just like you, and no matter how different you think they are, there not. Everybody is human. So the helpless woman continues pulling out nothing but dirty bags and smashed food, until she notices a letter. She bends over to grab it, palming it in her hand. It has some garbage on it so she wipes it off, like it was almost new. She opens the envelope and the first words she sees is: “Your not alone” And so the letter continues with a poem. It’s a lengthy poem and she wonders to herself who had written this and if she was meant to find it. Was it destiny? Is someone trying to play mind games with her? Or is it just someone else’s garbage? She then continues by reading aloud to herself. When we started out in this world everything was given to usWe didn’t grasp having to work for anything, because our parents were there to give us it We were born greedy, like every child isWe didn’t know right from wrong because frankly we didn’t need to know Our lives were simple. We’d eat, sleep, and be loved for.Even though we don’t think back at it as the greatest parts of our livesBy God it was You may be able to go back and change how your life turns out, but you’ll never get back those great years From that point all it takes is one misfortune and everything changesWe no longer had people there to comfort us, for comfort would never be felt again As the poor woman continues reading she feels shivers progress down her spine from the powerful words. We were on our own, in a world that had taken everything we hadYou see everything we have doesn’t have to do with us, rather then the other people who make us what we are When those people are gone, life is just lonely challenge All you want to do is be angry, but you can’tYou have to fight to survive even though you can’t comprehend why it’s worth surviving Well you want that survival, because you know you’re worth itWe’re all worth it, but never realize it until it’s too late The digging through garbage, enslaving ourselves for pennies, degrading our souls for anythingBut none of it is worth it, because nothing will change Our outcome in life won’t change, and we won’t magically make money and changeThe only change we can make is within our self Sometimes it isn’t about our body survivingBut our soul surviving with it A person without a soul is just a stray animalAn animal fighting for nothing when they realize that nothing is what they are Your life can’t be changedBut your soul can be saved So whenever you feel like life isn’t worth livingDon’t forget about the afterlife you’re destined Because your afterlife is a whole new ball gameAnd anything you could ever think of may be granted to you So ask yourself, is life worth a hundred miserable day with no dignity and an eventual suicideOr is life better if you had just a great five days where God would release you from the pain you’ve caused. Keep fighting, hold your head up high an remember:That this December, is your December If you quitYou never gainYou may have had itAnd you’re going insaneBut you dignity should always stay with youAnd your heart should yearn for love so trueAnd when you finally reach the dayGod will have the final sayIn the end life’s worth livingLive it wellAnd avoid the thought of hellBecause is heaven anything goesThink of this when it snows. Signed: El Romantico. A cold frozen tear falls from the eye of the poor lady as she holds the letter tight to her bosom as if it were a child of hers. Her outlook has changed and show has her determination. Life will be changed through words, and she realizes it. That’s why these tears of joy enrich her. {Earlier that Day} The scene shows El Romantico writing the letter on his desk of his room. He writes with heart in passion. He looks over the window and sees the same homeless lady check through the garbage can in front of his house everyday. He knew this lady deserved more, and that she should remember that she’s not alone. That night Romantico dropped the letter in the garbage, waiting for her to arrive the next day and find it, before the garbage was taken out. Romantico’s act of inspiration one had been finished. And so he waited and watched, and on that night as he saw her grasp the letter tightly, he knew he had finally succeeded.
