Holy Shyte

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/02 Tappity-tap-taptap. Click, click. Taptaptap. 'This should give them something to celebrate.' The skeletal fingers of Nascosto Nerezza dance over the keys of a laptop computer. He sits in his favourite place - atop a building with his legs dangling over the edge of the roof. One slip means the end of the hardware. his heel hit one after the other on the stonework like pendulums. The glow of the screen reflects off his red lenses as he continues to type away on the screen. One final, hard poke onto the keys prompts him to spread a sneer across his gums. The smile carries past his eyes and almost back as far as his ears, revealing row upon row of fangs. 'Perfect. Now we just need a slight guiding hand.... wouldn't you agree?' Nerezza swivels his head to his left, looking straight at a stone gargoyle. The statue continues his glare out into the sky, dotted with pinpricks of light across the ocean of dark purple and indigo. 'Well, your opinion doesn't count. You're only made of stone.' he sighs. A slick, wet sound heard only by Nerezza's ears mingles through the air and his arms drop to his knees. Scratching his ankles with a sharp claw, he jumps out in what appears to be suicide. Twisting around in the air, he sinks his claws into the stonework and hangs there for a moment, tucking the laptop into his jeans and sticking his belly out in an effort to hold it still. Slowly, the vampire descends the face of what is revealed to be a cathedral. As he nears the bottom and public view, he searches for the window he gained access from and quickly steals inside, closing the majestic window with a long, tapering arm. The church would have to do for a resting place for the night. After all, God welcomes everybody with open (and inhumanly elongated) arms , doesn't he? * * * The next day, Gaz Green is waiting outside the Hotel Royale. In his green business suit with lime hair, he stands out against the otherwise normal looking clientele of the Hotel, but grins and basks in the irregularity he brings as well as the awkward looks he receives. Suddenly, he sees his ride has arrived as a purple hearse turns around the corner. The windows are tinted and a few of the passers-by mark themselves with the sign of the cross. Gaz simply steps forward as it pulls up next to him. Inside, a haunting melody plays mixed in with some mild drumming. The door opens to reveal the driver being none other than Nerezza, clad in a dark red tuxedo with his red glasses lowered to the tip of his nose. His hair hangs loosely about his shoulders and neck and no jewelry adorns his body at all. 'Nice ride. Isn't this a little bit... extravagant?' Gaz asks with a curious look. 'Actually, it's amazing what you can do to cause some confusion when you have a little technology at your claws. And they say vampires are the ones stuck in the middle ages?' Nerezza replies with a modest smirk. 'Get in. I'll explain on the way.' 'Where are we going?' The Jester slowly slides in, buckling his seat belt up. The smirk enlarges on Nerezza's face. 'Hubbardville.' * * * The pair are on the road to the aforementioned location. Suddenly, they pull into the side of the road and stop next to a sign with directions. Totally oblivious to the vampire's methods, the Lord Of Laughter undoes his seat belt and turns to question his partner. 'Okay. What in the heck are we up to? And how did we afford this custom-made car?' Nerezza simply remains silent, keeping Gaz in the dark as he exits and walks to the trunk. He lifts up the back door and removes a flat, round object that resembles a metallic pizza, a hammer and small plastic bag containing nails. Checking left and right, he calmly saunters over to the sign and holds the flat object up against the pole. Taking a nail and pulling the hammer bag, he pounds until the... whatever it is... has been secured to the post. Gaz still can't see as Nerezza's body obscures his view. Wiping the sweat on his sleeve, the inexplicably dressed undead finally moves out of the way to reveal the sign - 'This way to Christ-Con 2002.' Replacing the tools back in the trunk, Nerezza slams it shut and gets back into the driver's side, clipping his seat belt into place. 'Alright, time to reveal the master plan. Last night, I used a few of my connections to... ahem, tamper with the Front Office's online bank account. That's what paid for this car, not to mention this dapper little item.' he says, stroking the lapels of his neat and formal attire. 'Let's just say a Little Storm messed with their account and then gave me the key to our next great prank.' 'Which is?' Gaz said, still missing the point. 'Let me put it simply. I asked Tempest if she'd give me some help in retrieving some of the money we got screwed out of. Then she allowed me to borrow this laptop and post a message in virtually every religious forum, chatroom and message board that I could search for, stating that a major event in their prophecies or calendar would take place in the city center of Hubbardville tomorrow, where it will open it's gates to other religions of the world.' he giggled, pulling the laptop from the dashboard compartment. 'That means that the tyrant will have Hari Krishnas banging on his front door and storming the place, Egyptian and Greek mythology believers yelling to get in, Christians, Jews, Hindus, followers of Shintoism and Taoism and the worst of all... JEHOVAH'S WITNESSES wishing to preach! Think of the chaos! Think of the confusion! THINK OF THE SUPER-SIZED ASPIRIN KANNON WILL NEED!' Gaz's face remains as blank as the gargoyle Nerezza spoke with the previous night. Then contorts into a mass of cackling as he explodes laughing, beating his legs with his palm. 'Old Father Hubbard will need a garden shed to store the medication that size, forget the cupboard!' Gaz madly sneered in anticipation. 'In the meantime, I have signs to post for the other religions and a few suicide cults. Let's press on... it's going to be a long day for us and an eternity for that scientological Scrooge. The ghosts of Turmoil Past, Present and Yet To Come will hopefully make him change his ways.' 'It's going to take a miracle for him to live this down. Maybe we should keep this Hearse handy, he may not survive until Tuesday Turmoil!' Gaz chuckled, dragging a finger across his throat. 'Well, 'Miracles aren't something that happen. They're something that people MAKE happen', to quote Misato Katsuragi.' Nerezza adjusted his mirrors and glasses. The violet Hearse begins to pull back onto the main road, joining the stream of traffic. 'And it's time we put this prank of biblical proportions into action...'
