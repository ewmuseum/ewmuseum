Setting Up for The Sit Down Interview

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 10, 2003 [In Los Angeles Studio B, a small crew prepares for a momentous occassion: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's sit down interview. Tommy sat down with his agent, lawyer, and an unknown PCW official recently and they all agreed that Tommy needs to address the people about what's happening in his life. Shutting down the Tubular Tykes Foundation was a crushing blow to Tommy's totally bodacious reputation. People were devastated by Tommy's idea of turning the Tubular Tykes School houses into water parks. Granted, Tommy feels that giving the mentally handicap lifetime free passes would make up for it, the people just looked at it as a slap in the face. Then came the sex videos, drug use, and openly admitting that he was some kind of super villain. The local press has been having a field day with Tommy's activities as there is not a moment that goes by that isn't put onto some kind of film. It's not really hurting the Tubular One at all, emotionally nor in his pocket book, and that's where it counts the most. This interview is just something that needs to be done, to bury the hatchet about every detail once and for all. To put rumors to rest, and to just be straight forward with the public] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Tiffany Jennings is a hot chick, man. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Harry, she is most bodacious. I want you to be on your best behavior once she gets here. [PCW Continental Champion, Tommy, and his faithful brother sit in reclining chairs watching the crew fix the lighting over the gnarly interview set. It's nothing too fancy, but something that Tommy's agent put together the night before. A nice spider plant, a picture of an American flag, and a bodcious looking Benjamin Franklin statue. These are the objects that will appear behind the Tubular One as he answers the questions of PCW reporter, Tiffany Jennings. The crew works with scowls on their faces because Tommy has not made this day easy, not in the least. They provided Tommy and Harry with those fancy directors chairs, the ones with the names on the back, but Tommy refused to sit in something so 'bogus'. The crew then went to another set and found reclining leather chairs and these pleased the surfer turned professional wrestler. It dug into the time of getting everything together, thus putting the enitre production behind. PCW camera man Gregory Simpson has been doing his best to get the perfect angles for Tommy and Tiffany, because you can't go having them look terrible on camera] Harry: I'm always on my best behavior, man. TTT: Dude, your TOTALLY a liar! Remember that one time Kirsten Dunst was supposed to come to one of the gnarly Hotel California parties?? Huh? I don't hear you answering me? Is that because you called up her cell phone for a booty call? Is that the totally heinous thing you did? [Harry sits in his recliner completely dumbfounded] TTT: That's what I thought, compadre! I could have made the whoopie with Mary Jane Watson. Harry: Didn't you say Kirsten Dunst, man? TTT: But she played Mary Jane Watson in Spider-Man. Harry: But her name is Kirsten Dunst, man. TTT: DUDE! I know. But she played Mary Jane in Spider-Man and she had red hair. She looked most radical, very sexy. Harry: Tommy.... [Harry pauses for a few moments] Harry: ...her name is Kirsten Dunst, man. [Tommy rolls his eyes and sighs. He then turns his head forward toward the gnarly set, only now he doesn't think it's so gnarly anymore. Who is that totally bald, heinous looking dude statue?! And what's with the heinous spider plant?! BOGUS!! Tommy's agent, who is speaking with the director, looks extremely nervous as his tanned brow has beads of sweat appearing. He pulls at his navy blue blazer every five seconds, which is one of his bogus nervous reactions. He has reason to be nervous, because lets face it, Tommy usually messes things like this up. Just like at the Tubular Tykes announcement he went and pretty much destroyed his credibility in just five seconds. He did go on to win the Continental Title, but every person in the free world hates his guts. They no longer see the fun loving, caring young man. They only see arrogant, greedy dumbass] TTT: HEY!! What's with the statue? It's bogus. Get rid of it. [Tommy doesn't even bother getting up from the chair, he feels that it's not worth precious energy. He has a huge match against Skitzo on Turmoil and he needs to conserve as much energy as possible. Esepcially after taking out Skylar Green, Venom Penishead, Teresa Tomas, and Rent-A-Jerkface he's going to need his rest] Tommy's agent: Tommy, babe, the statue is fine!! Don't worry about it! It'll look GREAT on television, you just gotta trust me. Harry: It kind of looks like that guy who is my supplier, man. TTT: Totally! I don't want some heinous drug dealer statue behind me! LIGHT BULB!! [Tommy points above his head as if everyone can see a light bulb above him] TTT: Tell one of these heinous crew dudes to go to my beach house, grab the naked lady statue, and replace it!! IT'LL BE BODACIOUS! Tommy's agent: NO! No naked lady statue!! TTT: YES! Tommy's agent: NO! TTT: YES! Harry: YES, MAN! [Tommy's agent runs his hands through his thick, black hair. He knows for a fact that he's not going to get anywhere with this. Tommy will fight him until the bitter end never giving up, just so he gets that damn naked lady statue into the background. With a sigh the agent directs one of the crew members to do as Tommy wishes. He's not even on camera and he's already ruining this interview] Tommy's agent: I think now's a good time that we go over EXACTLY how your going to get through this interview. We have NO IDEA what Tiffany is going to ask you. For all we know she could have dug up some juicy facts about your past and will blind side you. On these cue cards... [The agent holds up a thick stack of tiny 4 x 6 cards. Tommy looks at them with distaste and keeps looking at the set, imagining his bodacious naked lady statue in the background. It only brings a smile to his tanned face] Tommy's agent: ...are pretty much how you should react and SAY in any instance she asks a question that we haven't gone over. TTT: You mean I have to read? Tommy's agent: Yes, you have to read. We can't have a friggin' translater come on set. TTT: Well, you should. That would be most excellent of you. Tommy's agent: Well, I'm tired of being most excellent. [Tommy's agent hands the cards to Tommy and he immediately shuffles through them. The agent needs to walk away, go outside and have smoke. He can feel his lungs ready to pop out and start smoking the cigarette themselves. His nerves are just completely burning] TTT: Harry, check this out... [Tommy hands the card over to Harry as he squints his eyes, trying to read the small writing on the cards] Harry: Does it say that we are gay, man? TTT: Well, not really. That's in case this bodacious babe asks if we're like gay lovers or something. Harry: Are we, man? [Tommy shakes his head not even dignifying an answer to his stoned brother] TTT: These are totally bogus questions. 'Is it true that you masterbate ten times a day?' Dude, when did this interview suddenly become about you?! Harry: I don't know, man....but I used to go fifteen, not ten. TTT: Too much info, compadre. 'Do you consider yourself sexist?' Now THAT is a totally bogus question, Har. I'm supposed to answer with something heinous like, 'I am a very big fan of women and I love the splender of their movement. It is like a life source for me to have a beautiful creature called woman with me.' DUDE! What kind of bogus compadre talks like that?! Harry: Only one of those politicians, man. Like Politically Incorrect used to. [Tommy throws the cards behind him and streches out with an arrogant gaze in his eyes. He knows that he's the biggest and the best going around in the wrestling industry today. All those months held down with his brother in the tag team division, held to just a spark of mediocrity. He never got the chance to prove himself as to what he did best, just like in his NTICW days. Now they are learning, REAL quick. They're trying to surround him with contreversy, say that he's some kind of heinous dude towards babes, and how he is the root of all evil. NO! The Tubular One will not go so gently into that night, not without the fight of his life. He's not going to lose his bodacious spot, or his prestigious PCW Continental Title. He is the one putting a sense of nobility behind it! It lost nobility the second it was won years ago. Now he's giving the belt some meaning, giving it a purpose. In fact, he's thinking about calling it 'The Tubular Championship'. That way it will NEVER lose nobility no matter what bogus compadre has it] TTT: I'm just going to wing this interview, dude. What is the worst she can ask? She's going to want me anyway. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Hey...there she is. [Tiffany Jennings, PCW reporter and TOTALLY BITCHIN' BABE! Her medium length red hair looking most shiny and bodacious on this day. Her pouting pink lips parting into a smile as one of the crew members gives her a compliment. Like she would give some totally heinous nothing a chance, when she's going to be interviewing the man of her dreams. NO CONTEST! Her body curves so wonderfully that even her shadow would give a man wet dreams. It's not like she's some kind of super babe that is difficult to approach. She gives off a very ordinary, girl next door kind of vibe but still somehow finding a way to stay more bodacious then the rest. She checks in with the director, gets some info and her next move is to have a small chat with the star. She's seen him around many times before but has never TRULY gotten aquainted with him] Tiffany: Hi, Tommy, how are you doing? [Tommy stands from his chair, showing the bodacious babe the respect that she deserves] TTT: Tiffany, you are looking MOST bodacious today. [Tiffany smiles, her face turning a little red] Tiffany: Well, thank you, Tommy. Your not looking too bad yourself. That's a very sharp suit that your wearing. [Tommy tugs at the collar of his blue blazer, and flattens it out showing off the blue and yellow silk dress shirt underneath. It does look sharp indeed, but too sharp so the agent said, but Tommy always gets his way] TTT: I know that this is going to be TOTALLY gnarly and I'm looking forward to it. I just have to wait until one of the crew compadres comes back with a statue that I want put into the shot. Tiffany: Well, I have all the time in the world. It will be great for me to get this interview with you. I know that it's probably going to be great. TTT: Of course. I'm mean...I'm in it. Right there it's gnarly gold. [Tiffany seems a bit taken off course by that comment but all she can do is giggle it away] Harry: Yeah, Tommy is waiting for his naked lady statue, man. Tiffany: Naked lady statue? TTT: YEAH! It's radical, babe. It kind of reminds me of you. Tiffany: WHAT?! TTT: The naked statue...it reminds me of you. I haven't seen you naked though, at least not yet. [Tiffany is appalled and the only thing that she can do is just look on in disbelief. She knew that he was very forward with women, but not TOO FORWARD!] TTT: Listen...this interview will probably be in and out...another thing I hope to do later...if you catch the gnarly drift. So, here's my cell number... [Tommy quickly writes down the number on one of the cue cards that he threw. He places it in her hand and gives her that million dollar smile that always melts the ladies. Only this time it doesn't work as Tiffany is too apalled to even notice it. Tommy then walks away to sit on the set, soak up his time in the spotlight. Harry stays behind in the chair and scribbles something on the back of a card as well] Harry: Here's my number too...lets get together have sex sometime, man. [Tiffany looks confused now, and knows that she is going to be in for a VERY long day] Fade to Black...
