Interview With A Jester (Part 1)

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 February 2003 ‘How many movies have you seen featuring a Jester? Probably none, after all these films are historical based and Jester’s were entertainment figures that rarely received credit, one lame line could result with the loss of a head and not in the Sebastian Toomes sense of the word. Jesters were the landmark of comedy, their existence paved the way of today’s batch of comic, those you idolise, people like Jim Carrey, Ben Stiller, Adam Sandler. So why hasn’t anybody bothered reversing the role and played the Jester at his own game, namely pranking? Fear? This could very well be the case. For seven months Gaz Green has always been one step ahead of the competition but what do people fear in a man with no real physical prowess, a man dressed in green? It is a proven fact that size simply doesn’t matter, take Xavier Kannon for instance, he isn’t the biggest superstar to grace the squared circle yet he is one of the smartest. Xavier Kannon watched idly-by, biding his time when suddenly he struck and from the aftermath he was crowned both the World Champion and Primetime Championship Wrestling CEO, not bad for a nights work one might say. Two very different men, however, two men who play by the same method, ‘You either do the walking or you be the one being walked on!’ During the Asylum stint, Pranks With Attitude were very much under the weight of Alexander Miyagawa, even though in the ring the role was very much reversed. Nothing thus far remotely challenges anything Xavier Kannon has achieved but being in the Front Office alongside him is quite the protégé rush. Still three PCW Tag Team Championship reigns is monumental, the acquisition of the P.W.A franchise a dream yet these seem so insignificant to my latest purchase, the capture of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The last time the Jester and Vampire headlined under the same banner we were untouchable to all those other than Xavier Kannon who maliciously stripped us of the titles but now we have overcome that hurdle because not only has the Conniving Collective resurfaced, they have seeped into the tightest circle of friends… The Front Office Now with Nerezza as my minion, with Xavier Kannon taking over from where Hessian left off, with Pranks With Attitude bathed in the light of the dark side we will re-ignite the tag division and join the side of greats, we shall ascend alongside both Da Westsidaz and Blood Brothers!’ “How was that?” said the voice again. Not just any voice, the distinctive tone could belong to but one man, Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green. Thrust into the limelight Gaz Green doesn’t shy from the camera, no he invites the publicity, the above speech another out-look on seven eventful months, another opportunity to self-promote. The swaying of the camera finally subsides as the technician unveiled herself, where Gaz Green roams, his faithful valet isn’t too far in toe, joining her clients side Natalia Miyagawa finally retorts the previous statement. “Don’t you think your playing a deadly game of cat and mouse, resurrecting old ties with Nerezza, not to sound cynical but he did kidnap me and force you into a first blood match!” Rubbing his bristled face, the stubble brown hair covering his normally clean shaven face he looks towards his trusty side-kick with sympathy, a look that would melt butter, a look Natalia has succumb to a thousand times. Waiting for the Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor moment to climax, something the two find difficult in fulfilling he unveils further information regarding his ultimate plan. “My war with Nerezza proved but one thing, and that is Gaz Green can cut it with the best. During the two months of P.W.A’s dormant role I single-handily defeated ‘The Has-Been Man’ Duke Williams and the Stevie Wonder of Primetime Skylar Green, not forgetting Nerezza himself, I beat the vampire in his own match!” The conversation flooded into an eerie silence, the statement of the Jester absolutely correct, be it a few exaggerated facts, but nonetheless, Gaz Green had cold shouldered his label of tag and ascended the Continental Championship division. Co-incidentally this surge could be traced back to Gaz Green joining the Front Office. This break in conversation opened the opportunity for the viewer to deduce that the happenings between the Emerald Menace and his female associate were taking place in the Jester’s lavish apartment. The command post set in the heart of the City Of Sin, namely his hometown of Las Vegas Nevada. “Gaz have you even thought that Nerezza may be playing you?” questioned the Soviet Scarlet, concerned that this alliance has paved the way for the vampire to conclude what he had started merely a week before Shanghaied, a loose end as it were. The green tailored Jester sat smugly, his facial expression enough to assume that he holds a retort for Natalia’s growing concern. “Think of the possibilities….” “Think how we can re-ignite the tag division….” “Persuade Xavier Kannon to throw random pairings together?” asked the Russian. “Reason with Xavier Kannon that the only course for the division to survive is for him to hire tag teams over those seeking singles success?” continued the separated wife of Alexander Miyagawa, seeking the answers. “I plan on creating a division by…. Getting under the skin of many of the roster. During our rise up the corporate ladder we stepped on a number of toes, well the same strategy will work here and with Nerezza abiding to everything I say then it shall be the bloodsucker to take the fall” injected the Front Office Entertainment Manager with a sinister grin. “Err… Arr… I don’t follow?!?” murmured Natalia. “Let’s take somebody languishing in limbo, err, El Diablo for instance. As from Shanghaied, the Mexican is so far down the pecking order for another shot at the World Heavyweight Championship that he requires something to pull himself back into the limelight. An opportunity to adorn Tag Team gold courtesy of Pranks With Attitude….” states Gaz Green before an untimely interruption from his beautiful assistant. “Aren’t you overlooking Storm and Windsor?” “Come Friday Night Fusion their title reign will be over, finished, dead and buried. Getting back on topic, someone like El Diablo would relish the opportunity so to draw him in we shall hit him where it hurts most, his own failings…. Maybe I’ll get Nerezza to adorn an NFL shirt and have him run around the ring trying to catch a few balls thrown to him, deliberately dropped of course!” evilly declared the P.W.A mastermind. “Imagine it somebody running around the ring with an ‘EL D’ shirt failing to catch a ball, it would be enough to boil his blood, all we would have to do then his find him a suitable partner, somebody of a similar calibre!” The cherry red lips of Natalia gaped open as her mouth dropped to the floor, metaphorically and the Russian peered at her client in awe before murmuring a response. “B-R-I-L-L-I-A-N-C-E” “You could force people together with this method and suddenly spark the tag division, the possibilities are endless, Akiyama and Skylar Green, Clyde and DarkStar, Jason Snow and Duke Williams….” “WHOA!!!!” interrupted the Velvet Trickster before fine tuning Natalia’s existing idea. “All them will most likely be doing their own thing, as much as I’d love to pummel the Neo Void God for his disrespect at Shanghaied I was thinking of teams like Chet King and Mark Michigan, Dante Jackson and Mark Makonie, Statuz Quo and Skitzo!” concluded the Corporate Jester gesturing his scam with some peculiar hand signals, looking more like one of those illuminated airport personnel who wave coloured flags rather than a man stressing his point. “Oh….” blurted Natalia. - The End? -
