The Epic- 'Finding Him'

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-25-02 ::It’s been over 7 incredible months in the world of wrestling . The sport has never been better , younger , and pure as it is today . But there is one faction , one man that disappeared off the face of the earth over a short period of time . His name you ask? A name that will be answered in the following Epic in which a soul was once lost , and now is trying to be found by numerous people:: ::It’s a Friday , in the great state of Florida . Of course it is drizzling out and the sky is dark blue and no one can be scene in the area where 29-year-old Scott Nolan is standing. He is standing right next to a bench looking down into his cell phone about to turn it on:: “Hello” he says right after he clicks it on. “Scott Nolan?” the voice on the other phone says. Scott Nolan is an owner of a very popular Sports Gym and Jacksonville, Florida. The gym was handed down to him from his father Willy Nolan two years ago. Before Scott got a hold of the family franchise he was an Amateur wrestle for 3 years before he blew his knee out . Sort of forced his dad into retirement a few years earlier. “Hey Scott” the other voice says “This is James Creon “ James Creon . A 39 year-old wrestling trainer . He’s wrestled and trained various young athletes years ago . Now he’s a man devoted full time to student development in Atlanta , Georgia . “James Creon . You ran Amateur Wrestling 1995 , right?” Scott replies quickly . “Yeah that’s me” James shoots back as he is ready to get down to business and talk about something serious in which his voice is indicating . “Sorry for calling out of nowhere , it’s just that I need your help tracking down someone” James says . Scott Nolan was named the wrestler of the year in James 1995 Amateur Wrestling program before blowing out his knee a year later . Scott is also just well known as his father owned many Sports Gym’s in which many Indy Wrestling Circuits used . “Yeah? Who?” Scott replies. “Shawn Mosely “ James says . Shawn Mosely . . once known as the Golden One . He’s the one this entire Epic revolves around . He is the man James Creon is looking for to turn around . “Uh . . *Clears Throat* I don’t know . After he disappeared I heard he went down to South America . Why are you looking for him?” Scott replies .Shawn Mosely left the wrestling seen in an instance . One match away from his dream . . the World Title . . .it was destroyed just like that after his opponent used a foreign object when the ref wasn’t looking . Shawn was hospitalized for 2 weeks after the chair shot to the head . Before anyone knew it , Shawn Mosely was gone from the wrestling seen just like that , the snap of your fingers . “He can’t be .I heard some rumors from some valuable sources that he was back in Florida “ James says . “Why would he be in Florida” Scott replies “ And what would you want with him . God knows what he is up to” Shawn Mosely and Scott Nolan wrestled together for a few months . Recognized as one of the brightest young teams, Shawn went his own way as his ex-manager Joe Armstrong came into the scene and offered him a true beginning. “I’m intrigued by him. I mean the guy is only 23 year-old kid and he’s already been considered a true contender. Has too much potential to waste. . And well. . you have all the contacts in Florida.” James says with crackles in his voice. “Heh .. You really think you can help this kid?” Scott replies with amazement to the fact that James thinks he can turn Mosely’s career around. “Yes . I do .*Pauses* Can you help me?” James asks . Scott takes a few seconds to reply but eventually replies with the word “Yes” . “Thank You Scott . I’ll be down there within the next few days . Just gather up all the information you can .” James says with a bit of excitement in his voice . ::Scott takes a deep breath as he clicks his cell phone off . He looks around at the dark side and hops in his car . You now can say the search is on . . and hope is possible:: ::Without any hesitation Scott looks down out his cell phone and clicks it on as he is driving down an empty highway not far from Jacksonville, Florida where he is heading . The call Scott Nolan is about to put in is just about 15 minutes after he stopped talking to James Creon . Scott dials the number and puts the phone on some connection that allows for him to speak and listen to the phone without holding it:: “Hello!” is shouted out on the other line . ::Scott takes a glance at the phone and then back to the road as he gathers his thoughts:: “Yeah. . .this is Scott Nolan. Can I speak to one Joshua Holden?” Scott replies quickly. ::Joshua Holden . . .one of the most popular wrestling characters in the state of Florida for the past 20 years . He is 51 years-old and owns/ runs his own Gym in Jacksonville . With all that popularity comes knowing a lot of people in general . With all his connections it seems like a smart thing for Scott to contact him:: ::With a few moments gone by . .Joshua Holden gets on the phone:: “Hey . . what’s going on Scott?” Joshua says as he gets comfortable in a chair near by. “This has to be quick since I have no time . So I need information on the location of Shawn Mosely.” Scott replies quickly . “O yes . . .Mr. Mosely . I’m just the man your looking for” Joshua says “He was last spotted in a motel called “Jaxx Inn”. It’s a pretty sleazy place located down the road from me” ::Scott Nolan’s immediate expression as Joshua is talking is a bright smile:: “Alright. Thanks Josh. I have to run. Later” Scott replies as he clicks off . ::The scene closes as Scott call’s up James Creon immediately after he hung up with Josgua Holden . The response to Scott’s news was exactly the same . .resulting into a great big smile . Only time will tell if James could get into the head of Shawn Mosely and bring him back to glory::
