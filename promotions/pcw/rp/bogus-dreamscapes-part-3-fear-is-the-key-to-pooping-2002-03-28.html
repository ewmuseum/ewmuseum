Bogus Dreamscapes - Part 3 - Fear Is The Key...To Pooping

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 28 *CONTINUED from Bodacious Dreamscapes Part II* [The scene opens inside the hotel room that the hottest PCW tag team, Cowabunga Connection, is staying in. The dull, light blue wall paper with the matching bed sheets. The ceiling fan is twirling above as 'Heavy Metal' Harry, on your left, and 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, on your right, peacefully fall into slumber. The hotel room door opens and a shadowed figure walks into the room. This prowler picked the wrong room to break into, considering these men are professional wrestlers. The figure stops in front of Harry, studying the man, as if sizing him up for something. The figure steps into the moonlight, which comes from the window just above Harry. IT'S JESSICA ALBA! Harry's eyes immediately open. The two lock eyes passionately as Jessica sits down on the bed beside Harry] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You've finally come for me, man. Jessica Alba: I have, my love. Harry: What about Tommy, man? Jessica Alba: I never wanted him, I only wanted a little HEAVY METAL. [Harry smiles and pulls out a Steeler's football helmet] Jessica Alba: Are we playing football? Harry: No, man. We're going to celebrate our love for each other, man. Jessica Alba: Oh. Harry: Take a drag off of Jerome Bongus and take me, man. [Jessica throws the helmet aside and puts her hand on top of his crotch] Jessica: Here I come! [WHITE LIGHT!!! Harry is no longer in the hotel room with Jessica Alba, but once again in that brilliant white light. He wears the jump suit and has one of the most angriest looks that a man can have. His long brown hair is hanging in his face as he breathes heavily, trying to control his anger. Somewhere that stupid Evil Echo is dwelling and probably laughing about taking Harry from one of the most far out dreams that he has had in a long time] Harry: Evil Echo, you better show yourself, man. Because I'm going to kick your ASS! [There is no answer to Harry's demand. The silence just pisses him off even more. He points upward, shaking his hand all around] Harry: YOUR GONNA DIE, MAN! Evil Echo: YOUR GONNA DIE, MAN...your gonna die, man... [Harry jumps up and down in a child-like temper tantrum. As he does another figure floats down from above. Harry just knows something is coming down and grabs the figure. He begins dropping elbows on the person, and slapping the person's face] Harry: RAH, MAN! Evil Echo: RAH, MAN...rah, man... [The figure then quickly turns around the assault, getting on top of Harry and grabbing him by the throat. The two men meet eyes and realize...that they are PARTNERS. The man who floated down is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the other half of Cowabunga Connection. Tommy, with his bright blonde hair gleaming in the sunlight, is also dressed in the gnarly, white jumpsuit] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: What has gotten into you, dude? [Tommy stands up, helping his best friend and partner to his feet. They then float upward through this odd dream world] Harry: That stupid Echo interrupted my dream, man. TTT: He interrupted mine too. Harry: I was about to score with Jessie, man. TTT: ME TOO, DUDE!! Evil Echo: ME TOO, DUDE...me too, dude... [The two men the look up, obviously upset about that last echo] TTT: This is starting to get really annoying, dude. Everytime we try and sleep this bogus Echo sucks us into a heinous dream situation. We tried reverse psychology, but that thing just threw it right back at us. We are going to have to try something else, something that is completely unexpected. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: First we were sucked into a bogus game of soccer with a bunch of imitation Juan Borro's, and we defeated those dudes. The second one was being arrested by some political compadre that reminded me of that Lovecraft dude from Politically Incorrect. [Harry giggles] Harry: Love...Craft, man. Far out. [Tommy smirks] TTT: We have to think who else he's going to throw at us. Could it be an episode of the X-Files or a game of cricket like those dudes from Misplaced? May I add we totally trashed those heinous dudes on Sunday. Harry: That we did, man. TTT: Could it be an invasion of bogus jobbers for those weird dudes Jobbers Inc.? Maybe being attacked by heinous metallic objects for those Metallyx dudes? We have to start thinking like this Echo. [Harry seems to be deep in thought about something, as if coming up with a game plan. Harry doesn't do that much and Tommy smiles at this rare occassion. It's times like these that Harry can get down right evil and get them out of tough situations, and this is one of those situations. With the PCW tag titles on the line and their most bodacious reputation this is go time. All bets are off!] Harry: I've gotta go to the bathroom, man. TTT: You should have went before you went to bed! Harry: I did, but it just suddenly hit me, man. TTT: There is no Port-O-Potty around for this one, dude. Your either going to hold it or go in that gnarly jumpsuit. I'm pulling for the first option, compadre. Harry: GOTTA GO, MAN! Evil Echo: FEAR FACTORY IS GOD...fear factory is god... [Tommy smacks himself in the head, not even thinking about Fear Factory. This could explain Harry's sudden urge to have to go to the bathroom. In a promo for their match with Fear Factory, Harry came down with stomach aches and eventually had to find a bathroom in a hurry. Tommy has to prepare himself for the worst in this case. Fear Factory is very crafty and very creepy. This one could be the toughest challenge that have faced in these Bogus Dreamscapes] TTT: RUDY POO AND MASON CRAPPER ARE BOGUS!! Evil Echo: TOMMY AND HARRY ARE BOGUS...tommy and harry are bogus... Harry: I felt a squirt in the jumpsuit, man. TTT: Hold it, dude! We can get you out of this and to a gnarly toilet in no time. Harry: FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, MAN! Evil Echo: FOR THE LOVE OF ME, MAN...for the love of me, man... [The two men's faces drop in disbelief. Is this God playing a sick joke on them? Tommy always said his prayers, took his vitamins...wait, that wasn't God. That was some other God. That aside, why would God want to put harm to Cowabunga Connections chances at the tag team titles? What did they ever do to God?] TTT: YOUR GOD?! Evil Echo: JUST KIDDING...just kidding... TTT: Bogus, dude. [The lights go out, and the Cowabunga Connection, for the third time, is left in the darkness. You can hear Harry moaning in the darkness from his sudden urge to want a toilet] TTT: Stop being a baby, compadre. Harry: Shut up, it feels like my intestines want to come out, man. TTT: BOGUS! [The sounds of running machines can be heard in the background. Gears crunching and turning together as one. Harry and Tommy soon realize that they are in some sort of factory. Molten metal is poured from buckets into large vats, making some sort of metallic like objects. They two men are dressed in dirty old factory worker uniforms which pale in no comparison to the gnarly jumpsuits] Harry: Can I poop in this thing, man? TTT: That's gross, dude. [The two men walk across a tiny catwalk, surveying the area. Both men are wondering exactly what is going to go on here. The other one's were semi-predictialbe, but this situation just doesn't seem right. Besides the machines churning away it is rather quiet in the factory, and not a soul in sight] Harry: This really sucks, man. TTT: Just stay cool, dude. Harry: How can I do that, man? TTT: I don't know, I'm thinking. [Harry hunches over the catwalk rail, trying to find a way to make his stomach feel better. The feeling is like no other feeling he's felt before. Everything is built up in his stomach as if he'd combined Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Easter dinner all into one helping. His insides are trembling so bad, just wanting to let out the contents that are inside. He can't crap in his pants though, how would he get Jessica Alba then? Plus, he hasn't done that since he was sixteen] [Suddenly a hand comes upward from the catwalk, grabbing Harry's ankle. Harry screams wildly as a tiny fart sound is heard coming from him. Tommy quickly turns to help his friend but a hand shoots up and grabs his ankle as well. Tommy begins beating the hand with his fist, trying to get free. Harry just lays on the catwalk like a useless log, trying to concentrate on the situation before this stupid hand] TTT: That was most scary, dude. [Tommy frees himself and then quickly frees Harry, carrying him further down the catwalk to a large metal platform with a crane beside it. Suddenly all the machines shut down and everything gets deathly quiet] TTT: This is most creepy, dude. I hope they aren't trying to kill us like on Nightmare on Elm Street. That movie gave me the most bogus nightmares ever. Harry: Yeah...man... [Suddenly a shadowed figure leaps out of the crane, hunched over Harry. The two men scream loudly in unbelievable fright, getting away from the shadowed figure. The thing looks like a regular man, but it's really just this odd shadow shaped like a man. How can that be? Well, this is a dream after all and anything is possible really. It still doesn't make a whole hell of a lot of sense] [The two men stumble to a dark corner, breathing heavily from being scared] Harry: That thing almost scared the crap out of me, man. [Tommy has a vision. He realizes what they have to do now. That bogus Echo was setting them up to have Harry hold in his dookie, but that's not what needs to happen. What needs to happen is for Harry to just let it out, just go. What evil demons and shadows figures would go near a couple guys surrounded by dookie? NONE!! TUBULAR!] TTT: Take your dump, dude. Harry: What, man?! TTT: Just go, dude. It'll fight that demon thing away. Harry: Oh, man. [Luckily Tommy finds to clothespins in the vicinity and hands one to Harry. The two men then put it over their noses and give each other a thumbs up] TTT: Go for it, dude. [Harry squats over in the dark corner and his face shows nothing but extreme relief. The sounds coming out of him don't sound anything of this earth. It keeps going and going and going. Tommy didn't think someone could extract that much dookie from their system, but this is Harry he is thinking about] TTT: Are you finished yet? [More disgusting crapping sounds come from the corner and just get louder and louder than before] TTT: That's a no. [The shadowed figure jumps down from a catwalk above, scaring Tommy. Then the figure stops and you can hear it sniffing. It then grabs it's own throat, pulls out a shadowed knife, and slices it's own throat. Tommy stands over the shadow and taps it with his foot to make sure its dead...and it is. Harry's head peaks out of the shadows] Harry: What am I going to use for TP, man? [Tommy ignores him and begins rejoicing] TTT: I was right! Your dookie killed the shadow thing! GNARLY! Harry: Thats far out and all but what am I going to use for TP, man? [Tommy shrugs with a smile] TTT: This is only a dream dude, just pull up the pants and go with it. Harry: That's gross, man. TTT: It is, but I don't have any TP. [Suddenly the demon jumps back up into Tommy's face. Harry screams and more dookie sounds are coming from the corner] TTT: ARGH!! [WHEW! Tommy wakes up in his bed in the hotel room. He sits up and wipes the sweat from his brow. He looks over to Harry, who is still in dreamland, and notices that he is farting in his sleep] TTT: Bogus... [Tommy then looks up at the Jessica Alba poster and smiles. What a bodacious babe. Once the Cowabunga Connection wins the PCW Tag Titles on Friday, he's taking Jessica anywhere she wants to go. It's on him] [Suddenly Jessica begins to fade from the poster and soon vanishes. Tommy walks to the poster to investigate but ends up getting sucked into the poster....] TO BE CONTINUED!!
