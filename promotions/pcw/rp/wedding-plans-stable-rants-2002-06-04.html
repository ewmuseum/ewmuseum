Wedding Plans & Stable Rants.

4 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/06/02 As most of PCW’s fan know, as announced on Tuesday Night Turmoil 1 week ago, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall are engaged to be married.... Normally in the wrestling world this is cause for celebration, but not in the eyes of Primetime’s followers who detest both parties with a passion. They hate Kannon because he turned his back on the man who did the most to help him.. Jeremy Howard, they hate him because he fell into the evil arms of Tyler Nelson to form the Ultimate Authority.... They hate Ellie~! because she loves Kannon, and helps him win the matches he will go on to brag about... ...However, one thing made the PCW fans cheer during the proposal, the interjection of Miyagawa into proceedings, ever ready to insult Eleanor and frustrate Kannon... But that still left one question.... How do Scientologists get married? “What do you mean you don’t know how Scientologists get married... I thought you were... Like, one?” Our shot opens up outside “Whatever the name of the Bar where Tom Cruise worked in “Cocktail” was called” in the stylish West end of Hubbardville, at the junction of Xenu Drive and Operatingthetan Avenue... “I mean, that I’m no exactly sure HOW I get married... Utah, there was a 21 page handbook just issued on how we should brush our teeth to avoid oppression by mint flavouring so I’m assuming it’s going to take some learning!” As the rather buff looking doorman in his Black tuxedo opens the door and says his pleasantries, wishing the couple a good night, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall emerge onto the well lit and lively street, where most of Hubbardville and therefore Wisconsin’s best theatres and most exclusive bars are located.... Immediately their hair begins to blow as a bitter wind blows down the thoroughfare causing Eleanor to cuddle herself tightly and tuck her head into the faux fur collar of her long Sheep’s skin coat and tuck her bare hands into her pockets... ...The Gold Patron Meritorious puts his arm around his fiancé’s shoulder and they begin to navigate through the crowds... Eleanor : “Well how did Tom marry Nicole then? They were both Scientologists then weren’t they?” As the camera gets lost in the mass of humanity outside the bar, waiting to get in, Xavier and Eleanor emerge from the crowd, cherishing the new sense of personal space they have on this more deserted section of the boulevard... ...As well as the streetlights they are bathed in various bright colours from flashing signs for the movie theatres and bars, causing their respective Auburn ad Brown hair to become highlighted with all the colours of he rainbow.... Xavier : “Well.... They got married by him becoming a top Hollywood star, the man directors love to work with and every producer’s dream.... Then by pure chance, although she probably planned it... He met a talentless, Gold digging whore from some backwards country who probably didn’t shave under her armpits so SOMEHOW got him to marry her thus giving her a career...” While the Scientologist does another of his personal attacks on the former Mrs Cruise who has since gone on to star in such films as Moulin Rouge and The Others, Ellie~!’s face goes pale, seeming never fearful... .... Kannon is oblivious to his valet’s feelings as her bottom lip starts to quiver, her elven face not looking to confident... Eleanor : “Oh no.... I don’t, you know, have to grow underarm hair like some Australian scrubber do I? I want to marry you and all but I am SO not becoming Jaden to do it!” The Gold Patron Meritorious looks at her puzzled, wondering what she is on about before he realises she was taking his words literally... Xavier : “No... You don’t have to do anything like that... In fact, be as far away from Nicole Kidman as possible...... Bad example..... Be more like.... Um..... Kirstey Alley...” Ellie~!’s eyebrows immediately shoot up and she turns to him defiantly... Eleanor : “Hey.... I’m willing to do, you know, that thing you like in bed but I am SO not putting on 50 pounds to marry you..... I actually like having a waist!” In Kannon’s mind he accepts defeat in trying to give his Preppy and naive valet an example of how a Scientological bride should be and gets right back to the matter of the Ceremony... Xavier : “I didn’t mean her weight.... Anyway, back to the wedding..... I’ll have a talk to John tomorrow and ask him all about it.... Then we can get working on the guest list....” This seems to perk up Ellie~! some more, the thought of sending out wedding invites to all her old school friends who shared her preppy ways brings a warm feeling to her cold heart... Eleanor : “I know... We could, like, SO invite Duke, Big Weirdo Al Guy and Miyajobber... Then when they turn up we can like, go.... HA! We didn’t really invite you.... That would rule and they’d look, like, SO stupid!” Xavi, not being the bravest man in the World doesn’t really like the prospect of being faced with 3 angry PCW’ers in the ring, let alone out of it where he can’t go for the count-out or disqualification... And especially against those three men... ....Miyagawa who still resents Xavier for ending Asylum, enough to interrupt Kannon’s proposal and mock both him and his fiancé.... Then there is Duke, who hates both the couple with a passion after Supremacy and the month before, then finally Big Alan Steele, the man who along with Duke Kannon will face in Tuesday, a man Xavier has beaten, but not cleanly... Xavier : “Those 3 will be easy to turn away from the wedding my little Poon Puppy.... I’ll simply get the Paramedics to wheel the stretchers in an opposite direction!” The evil glimmer that has so many times graced the bright Green eyes of the Scientologist returns as he pictures the scene, with power being returned to the Ultimate Authority he knows he can be even more abusive and devious... ...Beside him, Ellie~!, who has a similar evil streak to her grins in approval, willing on the injury of Duke and Miyagawa who have many times insulted her with everything from “Cum Net” to “Arse End of a Cow Faced Woman. Eleanor : “An....... Opposite....... Direction! Yeah! We’ll, like, hit them..... Then hit them again, and again, and again and again and again until they stop saying all those horrible things about me!” Xavier : “Don’t worry my little Shag Squirrel, we can do whatever we want and it would take the whole of PCW to stop us.... W have the CEO, Tyler Nelson, the KING of the wrestling world, the man who owns 50% of PCW...... Then me, Xavier Kannon, 20% owner of PCW, the man who drove Jeremy Howard from PCW.... We have Chef, the new prophet of Allah himself, we have Corey Willaims, the man with a PhD in Con-Tro-Ver-Sey.... We have “The” Megastar Aaron Goeren, the head of Goeren Enterprises and we have the Uzbek Enforcer, the man of pure muscle..... We can’t be stopped!” His eyes are alive with glee as he runs through the names of the New Ultimate Authority, the perfect example of Ultimate power corrupting Absolutely..... The perfect mix of greed, hunger and sell-outs.... Eleanor : “Like..... SO true..... Can’t..... Be...... Stopped!” Xavier : “When Duke and Big Alan Steele are being stomped through the mat tomorrow.... Who is going to save them? The Malice Crew? PLEASE?!?!?! The last time a rag tag bunch of nobodies was thrown together like that they called them the Chicago Bears!” They pause for a brief moment on the sidewalk before walking out into the road, threading the needle between the oncoming traffic from both directions... Xavier : “The Phantom Phucking Phaggots? COME ON!?!?!? 2 weeks ago they were the Janitor, the doorman at PCW Headquarters, the guy who did the U.A’s Valet Parking, Elmo the Ring Crew Guy and some Lighting Guy..... At least I think I’ve seen their talentless faces about here before!” Ellie~! sneers and agrees with him, muttering her opinions to exclamate his words while he pauses... Eleanor : “Ring..... Crew...... Guy... Yeah! Serve them right for hitting you!” Xavier : “Then the Political Machine? Can you say Ultimate Authority cast-offs? They can’t handle quite rightfully living in the shadows of me and Tyler so they create their own team? Utah, I’m sure there is some sweatshop worker putting CD’s on Boxes for Microsoft but they aren’t going to get far challenging Bill Gates! As I taught Lovecraft on Friday, treason is met by execution... And it isn’t Politically Incorrect’s Executioner’s Chair, it’s an X-Bomb onto anything I have to hand!” The 1st couple of Scientology turn to face back the other way and Ellie~! hails for a Taxi and the pair wait for a vacant vehicle to come by. Eleanor : “Yeah, have..... To.... Hand! Xavier : “To all the wrestling journalists who say there is a “Stable War” coming.... What the Utah planet are you living on? What I see is the Ultimate Authority and 3 thrown together bunches of no talent job-a-lot morons to try and stop us.... They’re pathetic... Ah... Here we are...” One of the highly efficient fleet of Hubbardville Black London-style Taxis pulls in and the driver, in a show of typical Scientologist politeness gets out and walks around to open the back door for them to get in... .... The shot then begins to fade out on Ellie~! sliding into the back followed by Kannon and as the door is closed behind them we hit Black... “Wait..... if Nelson is the CEO, why doesn’t he just fire that Sock-headed one?”
