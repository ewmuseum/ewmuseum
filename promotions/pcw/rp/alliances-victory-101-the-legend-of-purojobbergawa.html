Alliances Victory 101: The Legend of Purojobbergawa

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.02(PPV #3) “Ah yes, and to continue our learning… We are greeted by the voice of Rush Mason opening this promo and greeting us with a voice that sounds more chipper than usual, although that often means that some sort of devious act has been carried out. “To learn the true honorable ways of the…. The scene fades in as we see Rush Mason standing in front of a giant Buddha statue, where he seems to be wearing some sort of Karate training suit, fully-equipped with the white belt of a true beginner. Rush: …the Purojobbergawa! As Rush says this, he turns to the side, as if expecting something, while posing in a frozen kicking position, seemingly caught in time. However, Rush falls back into a normal stance and repeats himself, this sans silly pose and some of the previous spunk. Rush: I said the….Purojobbergawa! Again, Rush waits for some sort of response to these words, and again is greeted by nothing. He then looks off to a corner, and the camera follows his gaze, where we can see the root of the problem slightly poking out of the corner. Rush walks up to the corner, and screams to something off camera. Rush: Lardtard! That’s your cue! With a bumbling start, we are greeted by the sight of Ray Ray, who almost trips over himself, but makes it over to a small gong, and rings it quietly, albeit belatedly. Ding! However, Ray Ray’s effort is not greeted by praise, but with a scolding tone from Rush, who immediately tears into the huge man for his not so professional habit of sleeping on the job. Rush: You were supposed to ring that thing after I said Purojobbergawa…for bad kung fu movie effect! Ray Ray has to take a moment to digest this information, his brain moving at about three millimeters per hour. Finally, after a moment of contemplation, Ray Ray understands this condescending statement. Replying eloquently, as he always does, he throws Rush a shrug and steps into full view. Rush: Oh sweet Loa…. What Rush is seeing is quite horrific indeed. A huge 305 pound man in fighting attire much like the Voodoo Villain’s is the sight he sees, yet Ray Ray’s looks like someone played a bad joke and stuffed someone into a giant pillowcase. However, for urban appeal, Ray Ray has added his own touch, with multiple stains of various foodstuffs all over the outfit. Ray Ray: Dawg….this outfit is all they had left. If they’da let me fight in a velour suit, I’d be able to WHIZZAM and ya know….know them mofos out like whoa. Rush just runs a hand over his bald head in disbelief at Ray Ray’s immense inability to grasp many many things, and the rather goofiness of him in his current state. However, to break up such an awkward moment, what better than a small Asian man scurrying out in matching karate attire, despite a black belt. “My name Meowgawa!” The small Asian man’s hearty pronounciation and syllabic structure is quite interesting, but not so much as his grammar usage. However, Rush and Ray Ray do apparently happen to know why he is out there, and look down at his small form. Rush tries to cleverly whisper into the ear of the man, but ends up needing to bend far down to reach that lower level. Rush: You’re supposed to say Miyagawa….ya know? How I talked about spoofing effect? The small man looks up at Rush and then smiles, turning once again to the camera, and posing with a huge grin across his face. “My name Migayawa!” Rush just smacks his forehead and continues on, as though things are normal, as he addresses the small man as some sort of teacher apparently, which would make sense for a black belt. However, many have seen Rush’s inability to take direction from others, which might hamper one’s ability to learn from others. Ray Ray: Aite Mig…Miya….Migya…you! Show us some coolass flyin’ high stuff… Rush: Yeah….do some wacky stuff like crashing through a flaming table with live snakes writhing around your comatose body while you drift over the Atlantic in a death-defying blizzard! The small Asian man, whose name is not apparent, although he seems to be some sort of play on last year’s Alliance member, the goggled one, Miyagawa (d’uh), ignores the two men, and begins to grab onto Rush’s arm, shouting towards the camera as he does so.“Start with basics!” Both Rush and Ray Ray trade a glance of boredom, but give in for the moment, seeing as they’ve got nothing better to do. The little Asian man continues to hold onto Rush’s arm, going through smooth calculated steps involved in what is either a primitive armdrag or some sort of mating ritual. Rush: Err…Master Sensei….could you stop humping my arm? However, the tiny teacher seems to be off in his own land, going through some strange forms of something that might be martial arts, but could also be another mating ritual, you never know. Rush: You! Little Guy! Ixnay on the ouchingtay of the ushRay! And yet again, Rush is greeted by silence from his Asian companion, who is doing something strange yet again. Rush finally gets fed up and motions with his free hand for Ray Ray to bring something over. “This is basic armdrag! You must know basics!” Rush tries to wrench his arm away yet again, but is greeted by a ferocious grip from the little fellow. However, with a deft move, Rush quickly transfers the man to a small container, which Ray Ray has just brought over. “You canna do anything without basics!” Rush throws the container off to the side, with the small man inside, and walks over to the other side of this same Asian-motif room, which conveniently has a wrestling ring on the other side of it, already set up with a table in the middle. Rush: Now…I shall demonstrate a move that is called the….Flying Purojobbergawa Doom Attack of Death! Our protaganist begins to mount the turnbuckle slowly, while Ray Ray attends to the table in the middle of the ring, beginning to douse it with lighter fluid, while Rush perches on the top rope, looking towards the camera. Rush: Folks…we’re professional…uhh…guys and are the total supervision of this camera, so basically, if I die, I’ll just get Ray Ray…err…some other intelligent being to come to your houses and sue you for making me do this stupid spoof. With that “disclaimer,” we see Ray Ray throw a match onto the table, just as Rush readies himself to jump down to the table below, but his large manager interjects a surprisingly intelligent thought. Ray Ray: Uhh dawg….couldn’t ya hurt yourself, with all that wild flaming and stuff? Rush takes a look down at the currently flaming table, and then steps down off the turnbuckle, giving Ray Ray a congratulatory pat on the back for his notably good observational skills. Rush: Good play, my friend. And with that… The Voodoo Villain turns away from the camera for a moment, then turns back wearing huge blue goggles. Rush: We’ll have to end our wonderful hour. Dear Bon Dieu, I can’t see with these things on! And with that, Rush goes stumbling dangerously near the flaming table, while Ray Ray pokes his head out in front of the screen before it fades to black. Ray Ray: Uhh….th-th-that’s all….homies. With that, we fade to merciful black, but not before we hear of Rush’s unfortunate encounter with a deadly table due to those darn goggles. “YEEEEEEEOWCH!”
