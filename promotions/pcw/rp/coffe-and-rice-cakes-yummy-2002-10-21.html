Coffe and Rice Cakes .. Yummy!

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-21-02 RP 2 Turmoil 22nd The morning after is always the worst. In Teresa’s case, she didn’t sleep with anyone but her accidental over dosage on the booze right before Mall Brawl has done its toll on her. True, she had her moments but in the most part, it wasn’t her best and she knows it. She doesn’t know what gave her this headache, the bottle of Wild Turkey or the sumo wrestlers at the mall. What a way to go. Suits her right she supposes as she sips on a large cup of coffee. Teresa sits by a window of a coffee shop as the early morning fog fades into the afternoon sky. She slept little the night before due to body aches and beating herself mentally for being so stupid. Next time, she will buy the booze AFTER the show. A table of Japanese police officers speaks their Japanese gibberish. Their table is not far from Teresa’s and she gets an eerie feeling that they are talking about her. She probably sticks out with her black streaked blonde hair amongst a room of Asian men and few women. Teresa does her best to ignore them. She is not in the mood to show her temper. Speaking of tempers, considering she isn’t in the US, her anger management classes have been on hold till she returns home. Judge’s orders. What few classes she has attended she thought was boring. Those in the class with her she thought needed to admit themselves to the nut house. None the less, she did keep quiet out of respect and the fact that if she didn’t cooperate, she’d be doing community services till she agrees to do so. The Japanese officers continued to talk amongst themselves over coffee and rice cakes, (My RP here;)) while Teresa continue to watch the traffic outside the window. She places her coffee mug on the table as her baby blue eyes catch two heavy set Asian men approach the coffee shop. Oh shit The two men stop beside Teresa’s Chevy truck and begin to laugh in a mocking fashion. Teresa watch knowing exactly who they are. How could she forget two sumo wrestlers smash her like a pancake. She takes another sip of her coffee watching the two men enter the front door. All sumo men look the same so there isn’t much need for a detailed description except one is wearing baggy jeans and some sort of a red jersey. The other is wearing baggy black pants with a black silk shirt. Teresa didn’t realize jeans were made that big. The urge is there to get up and bust some nuts and crush a couple of heads. The urge is so great, Teresa finds herself standing up. Luckily in her case, she stops herself as her still aching body reminds her of the night before. The two men take a seat at a table a few behind the officers. They look around as if searching for someone till they spot Teresa watching them. The one in red winks while the other waves and smiles. In response, Teresa mocks a smile decides this is a good time to leave. She leaves a small tip and starts to exit when a deep voice calls her name. Tomas! Leaving already? Come, sit and we shall talk. Teresa turns around to see both men motioning her to join them at their table. To ignore them and walk out would be so much easier, but the taste revenge is much too great for our Southern Belle. Perhaps if she uses her head and not her fist, she will actually come out on top with these two over grown men. Instead of walking to their table, Teresa makes her way to the counter where the waitress stands. She reaches in her front pocket, pulls out a couple of bills and two tickets. She hands it to the waitress. Great coffee. Wrestling fan? The excited waitress nods instantly recognizing Teresa. Her black hair is put in a bun and she wears a while and red uniform. Her nametag reads ‘Mae’ Teresa smiles with a wink. That’s good to hear. Listen, inside these two bills is a couple of laxatives. The girl named Mae giggles finding it funny someone famous would need help in that department. Teresa waits with a blank expression till the girl quiets down. The drinks in front of you are for the two big guys at that table right? The girl nods. Teresa opens the bills and drops on laxative in each drink. You can have this extra tip and two tickets to Tuesday’s show if you don’t tell what I just did ok? The girl nods again and finally speaks. Teresa was beginning to think the poor girl was mute. I no tell Miss Tomas. Those men hurt you last night. Now you hurt them. The girl gives Teresa a wink and serves the men while Teresa makes her way out of the coffee shop with a huge grin on her face. Once again The American Woman has come out on top.
