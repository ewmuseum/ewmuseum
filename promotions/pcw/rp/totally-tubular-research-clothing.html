Totally Tubular Research: Clothing...

Date: Recently... [[Scene open.]] Titu Xansiv stepped into the store and looked around. It was like nothing he’d seen before. A HUGE selection of clothing lined the store, and the colours – well, the colours almost blinded him. He knew Tommy’s dress sense. The Hawaiian shirts, the bright short, the sandals. Pharaoh knew that if he was to take Azrateph’s advise on winning the World Title, he’d need to grow accustomed to Tommy’s dress sense, taste in music, and so on. He’d already witness the music – that Gods awful music - and now it was time to experience the clothing. He sighed. No matter how much of a ragtard he felt trying it all on. “Come, let us venture onward. Though a fear for this place slowly grows in my innards, I shan’t let this sudden terror take my heart and mind; the PCW Title shall indeed be mine!” Titu waved a hand towards the store’s entrance. The mall, as Titu had found, was absolutely huge. It certainly put Surfside Sounds to shame. But if Titu had his own way – and he knew eventually he would – he’d banish places like this. The slaves of Egypt never had this sort of freedom, that was for sure. As every day passed in America, and in this age, he grew more and more aggravated with the strange behaviour of the people who resided in it. “Milord, we must find a section of this strange place which sells Tommy’s clothing. Perhaps one of the staff members inside will be able to aid us in our conquest?” Azrateph knew damn well that Titu, unaided, wouldn’t be able to find what he was looking for. But he hoped, after the ‘Surfside Sounds incident’ that the great pharaoh’s temperament wouldn’t be tarnished by his words. Isiy, unlike the two ‘ancient’ Egyptians, wore a pair of tight fitted jeans and a low cut black top. It came to Isiy as a surprise that after such a long time spent in the US Titu hadn’t grown more familiarized with ordinary clothing. It sometimes embarrassed her…Titu’s dress sense. Sure, Tommy didn’t seem to have a clue either, but Titu really pushed the line. “Hmm,” Titu sniffed as he entered the clothes store, “The atmosphere in THIS particular place is MUCH different to that wretched Surfside Sounds slum.” Xansiv took another quick whiff of the air and grinned, “Indeed, THIS place is much more relaxing than that awful ‘shop’!” His voice, as everyone who knew him knew, was powerful. Titu’s tone, when something good was going for the royal Egyptian, was always full of authority, a hint of mockery hanging off every single syllable. Titu stepped further still into the store and peered about. It wasn’t long before Isiy took a step towards her wary husband and whispered to him, “Let me take care of this one, my love. I KNOW these places better than you.” For once, Xansiv didn’t argue. Isiy walked on ahead as The Pharaoh and the high priest Azrateph pursued slowly. In the back of Xansiv’s mind, he knew he wasn’t willing to actually try on any of the clothing here, but he did want to look at it. By studying the dress sense more, SURELY he’d realize what the Duat was going on in Tommy’s sickly twisted mind. The young, handsome man was the PCW Champion for a reason, after all. Titu had to unlock that reason and find a weakness. After that he and Skylar Green could eliminate The Tubular One from the Supremacy main event and go one on one for the Championship. “Hello, I’m looking for the menswear section,” Titu’s concentration was abruptly broken by these words. Isiy now stood talking with a woman dressed similar to herself, an inquisitive expression plagued upon her brow. Titu blocked out the sound of the other woman’s voice and looked onward, still considering the main event. Finally he’d get a chance to capture the PCW Heavyweight Title, and thus far, his chances of doing so looked slim. In comparison to the near 280-pound Skylar Green, Titu was small, weak and slow. Green was the model fighter. Fast, powerful, intelligent. Titu knew he could take Tommy, he already had after all, but Green was another matter. Over the past few weeks, the pair had faced each other in countless matches, and guess who came out the victor EVERY time? Green. Because of Skylar Green, he’d even jobbed to James ‘Lunatic’ Edwards! “Titu,” Isiy interrupted, “It’s this way, honey.” Titu looked up at Isiy and found the store clerk staring at him oddly. He looked himself up and down and shrugged. What was wrong with him? Why was the other woman, the damned infidel, looking at him like that? He paid her a disgusted glance and scoffed, “What in the holy name of Horus are YOU looking at, ragtard?!” The woman appeared stunned. He heavily doubted she’d anticipated that response. Isiy rolled her eyes as she usually did, and sighed, moving off in the destination the woman had pointed out. Titu kept that same disgusted expression on the woman for a moment until she walked away. In the back of his mind he was laughing at her – the idol SLAVE. “You know, it’s awfully embarrassing when you do that, Titu.” Isiy let out a lengthy sigh; she always did when she was angry. “I wish you’d give it a rest sometimes.” Moving through the expanse, racks upon racks of jeans, shirts, t-shirts and shorts passed the trio by. Titu looked back at Azrateph who peered about the clothing store with interest. It was indeed an intriguing place, even Titu could admit that, but it was something he wasn’t very used to. His clothing was simple. Aside from the jewellery and the royal crown and things, he only wore a simple loincloth and a pair of sand-battered sandals. But that was all he cared to adorn himself in. Most of the places Titu visited on the road were hot. He himself LIVED in California. It then dawned on him that California was home to a fair few wrestling superstars. Miyagawa of PWC lived there, Tommy, as he already knew lived there, he did and he was sure there were more. Alexander and Pharaoh even lived in the same district – Anaheim. “Master, look!” Azrateph’s voice broke the chain of thoughts this time. Titu turned back to face the high priest of The Palace and saw him pointing towards the distance. He followed his arm’s direction and saw not so far ahead a rack of extremely BRIGHT Hawaiian shirts. “By the Gods! Look at those things; why, they’re bloody HIDEOUS!” Pharaoh’s brow furrowed sickly. Upon these words, Isiy burst into laughter, the comment immediately catching the attentions of the other shoppers. Even the people addressing the shirts began to move away from the hanging stand sporting the unusual shirts. Titu jogged towards the brightly coloured things, and his footing quickly sped up to a full-on sprint. Putting on the breaks, Titu slid a few feet past the shirts, the soles of his sandals clearly worn, and with a curious expression moved back in their direction. “These things – they’re terrible!” he said with a shake of his head. Isiy approached with a nod. “Yes. They are. Tommy’s almost BLINDED me the other day.” Why Tommy wore these things was beyond The Pharaoh. He honestly couldn’t fathom the reasoning. Then again, ‘Totally Tubular’ Tommy probably couldn’t comprehend Pharaoh’s understanding for the whole ancient Egyptian garb. The shirts reminded Pharaoh of Tommy heavily. The vivid colours, the arrogance which bled from them – it all pointed towards the surfer. People who could wear these things HAD to be cocky bastards. The shirts were so vibrant and strange that only the most confident person would DARE to put one on. That was ‘Totally Tubular’ Tommy, though. Over-confident, self assured … the Champion. He really had everything going for him. But come Monday morning, Titu knew he wouldn’t. Xansiv WANTED the title SO much he’d go through anything to claim it. “Bah! These remind me of him TOO much of that infidel! We must LEAVE this blasted place NOW! IMMEDIATELY!” he bellowed, a few store assistants looking over at the angered Egyptian. “Will you calm down?” Isiy strained. “Master, we shall be cast away from this place if you do not.” Said Azrateph with a worried glance at the workers. But the tension was too great. He felt sickened now. It was a day or so away from the biggest night in his life and what the Duat was he doing here? In a stupid clothes store?! Nothing he did to study Tommy would give him the title, he realized now, only his WRESTLING would. “Sir, I’m afraid if you don’t calm down, we’ll have to get security—” “SPEAK NO MORE, FERAL WOMAN!” Pharaoh shouted at the staff member. “We’re already leaving!” Titu turned to Isiy and Azrateph. Both appeared shocked…maybe even a little disturbed. “Are you Ok, Titu” Isiy asked. He wasn’t of course. “Yes, yes, I’m fine. Come on! We’re leaving!” Skylar Green was already over in Afghanistan. Waiting for the PPV silently. Waiting to kick off the main event with a HUGE bang. Tommy was still here, in California. He, like Pharaoh, was pushing the time limit to get over to the war-stricken land. But a good sleep on the plane over there would soon loosen him up. He felt tense, unready for this. But he’d waiting SO long. He felt it was unfair for Tommy to hold the title. Tommy hadn’t gone singles for four months and ALREADY he held the title. Skylar and Titu had been here nearly two years and neither had had a shot at the title like this before. If anyway deserved the title this Sunday, Titu thought as he left the store hurriedly, it was he or Skylar. One or the other would attain the prestigious belt, but whom was the only matter left to consider. Perhaps he was going into the main event the under dog, but he wouldn’t go down without a fight. He wanted the title, and would do whatever it took to get it… [[Fade.]]
