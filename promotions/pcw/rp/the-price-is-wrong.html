Title: The Price..is wrong. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today Newcomers. Powell and Lovecraft know of newcomers. Incumbents often see newcomers rise to positions equal to theirs. To Politically Incorrect, newcomers are one thing. Weak. Malleable. Naive. Okay, so maybe newcomers are more than one thing.. The fact of the matter is that Politically Incorrect manipulate newcomers. They bend men to their will, and dispose of them when they are not needed. And now, two men, the Price Brothers, have the unmitigated gall to step into Politically Incorrect's PCW and dare to try and fight the former tag team champions? Laughable at best. Fatal at worst. Politically Incorrect does not appreciate intrusions. Especially when their tag team titles are involved. Price Brothers, you had best prepare... For Politically Incorrect are opening up the doors to the halls of justice once more.. ::The trademark Politically Incorrect limousine is where everyone's favorite politicians are on this day. William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft smile, seated in their chariot, sipping bottles of champagne. Both men seem to be enjoying themselves, as they speak of the new order of things.:: William S. Powell: It is a good thing that Tyler Nelson rehired us. It would have been a shame to have to file that multi-million dollar lawsuit against an old friend. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed...but remember, it would have been business. Friendships do not impact upon business. William S. Powell: Indeed... and it is time for Politically Incorrect to return to business. Our tag titles are on the line in a tournament...PCW's back up to eight teams. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Single elimination? No problem! William S. Powell: I'm afraid not..round robin. We have to face all seven others. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Damnit. Who have they brought in this time? Samburo? Fear Factory? William S. Powell: No, although, they DID bring back Misplaced! :Both men break into hysterical chuckles.: William S. Powell: Seriously though, Cowabunga Connection and the Heartbreakers returned, along with four new teams. The New Iconz, Pretty Cool Guys, The Dark Messiahs, and The Price Brothers. Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Price Brothers? What are their names, High and Low? William S. Powell: I don't quite know, but they are our opponents in the first round robin match. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah. They seem to be quite active early on..serious weasels they are indeed. Yet, they still seem to be in awe of their surroundings..in awe of the big show that indeed is Primetime Championship Wrestling. William S. Powell: Indeed, Mr. Lovecraft. The Price Brothers have yet to learn whom we are and what we do. I think we should inform them. Reginald V. Lovecraft: We are Politically Incorrect. The LONGEST REIGNING TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS IN PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING HISTORY! TWENTY PERCENT OWNERS OF THE COMPANY! We have destroyed EVERYONE! Misplaced! Fear Factory! The Cowabunga Connection! Civil Disobedience! Team CEO! ALL FELL BEFORE THE DYNASTY THAT IS POLITICALLY INCORRECT! William S. Powell: I am William S. Powell...one of only two men walking the face of the planet who can execute a Moonsault Senton, otherwise known as a 450 Moonsault. And goddamnit, I AM THE MASTER DEBATOR! ::Lovecraft chuckles briefly: Reginald V. Lovecraft: And I am Reginald V. Lovecraft...America's number one proponent of CAMPAIGN FINANCE REFORM. I am the oldest man in Primetime Championship Wrestling, yes, older than Duke Williams. I'm a traditional Southern Democrat, who has a weakness for pecan pie... :The two men grin, pride etched on their faces. Say what you will about the politicians, but they are fiercely proud men. The Politicians may indeed be corrupt, but they come about their corruption honestly...:: William S. Powell: This tournament is quite an endurance test. We must each face seven other tag teams. We must be capable of adjusting to all different types of opponents. I know that Lovecraft and I are capable of meeting this challenge. But, are you two? Can two rookies out of the Academy step up and hang with the best of PCW? We shall see...but I highly doubt it. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed, Mr. Powell..we make our living on taking men like you and breaking them down in the Halls of Justice. And there is no Price on earth that can change that fact. And with that, I welcome you to the Halls of Justice. For today, is ELECTION DAY... William S. Powell: And, on Election Day, POLITICALLY INCORRECT ALWAYS TRIUMPHS! ::Fade to..:: Paid for by The Ultimate Authority.We own you all. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
