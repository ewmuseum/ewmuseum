Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {3}

Date: December/March [Tuesday, December 11, 2002] They say that tragedy brings people together…if that’s true, then why does happiness not do the same? He could hear their voices from below, friends from the past, the few family he had left; the god bet Inphino and his woman was down there somewhere as well. At this point Akiyama could care less…if anyone dared to come up here and bother him then he’d have a problem. It was a reception for his passed parents, but that didn’t mean he had to sit down there and mingle like everything was fine. Things were far…FAR…from being fine. Someone out there is responsible for their deaths, and it was his task now to find that out. It’s a matter of principle…no one touches the Akiyama clan and walks away. What made matters worse was that PCW was cruising along without him, sitting there like an invalid while snakes and weasels that ran the company barely successfully were existing without punishment. Well…punishment would come in due time. “Hungry?” Glacia barely had a second to duck the fist coming in her direction. Akiyama glanced down at her, kneeling on the ground with a bowl of curry in hand. She stood back up when he turned away from her, staring back out of the balcony window we were sitting next to in our bedroom. “What do you care?” She leaned against the glass pane and shrugged at our question. “Beats me. Thought you might want something while you were up here…before they eat it all down there.” “We need nothing from you…” he muttered, watching the gray clouds roll through gradually. Glacia shook her head slowly, her ice blue locks waving across her face and a lump covering over her right eye. “You still have something against me after all these years? I thought a god of all people would be above holding a grudge.” “Don’t know us very well…” he replied, “or any other god for that matter.” Kannon would find out sooner or later, closer to sooner hopefully. Of course the Gold Patron Meritorious wouldn’t be alone… allies, enemies, bystanders…they’d all take the walk upstairs; especially if they had the gall at all to stand against the god. “Are you still mad that because we slept together you had to nearly kill one of your closest friends and drive him insane?” “That’s not something you forget about.” Betrayals over and over…friends, families, even your enemies…nothing but betrayal. Well no more… Glacia exhaled deeply, disappointment in her voice. “Well you had better. I’m in your life no matter what, so long as Cyclonica is. Learn to deal with it…better than you deal with everything else in your life.” “…?” The glare on his face showed Glacia that she had to explain. “Every time something really upsets you, you always isolate yourself away from people who try to help. It’s always something you have to do by yourself. Sometimes it’s not always a one man army. You can’t muscle your way through everything.” Akiyama rolled his eyes as he returned to the window. “Supportive is not your forte.” She raised a blue eyebrow at him. “Then what is?” “Disappearing.” It was obvious at that point where Cyclonica got her icy looks. Glacia sat the bowl on the table nearby and walked towards the door silently, but Akiyama could hear the anger in each and every one of those steps. She stopped at the door and turned around, her eyes glaring in his direction, but the god’s own eyes not cast upon her. “I hope you choke. Death is so suitable for you, my god. You search for it so much.” Slam, went the door. Akiyama didn’t bother reaching for his food; all he wanted at this point was to be one with his thoughts. He needed clearance, but given his mental dysfunctions that was a dream. One voice said live and let die, another said kill and let suffer. A third said live life rebuilt because of this incident…make something good of you. Become what your parents would have wanted you to become. The trouble with that statement…was that there was nothing good in Akiyama to start with. The Neo Void God…a destroyer of man, making pain his primary weapon in his existence. There’s no way that he can just reverse gears and become a good guy suddenly. It’s never been like that. Probably never will. He’s gone from mafia killer to hired muscle to fighter to the plateau of villainy…dark god. Screw greedy CEO…that was amateur position in comparison to where Akiyama stood on evil. There was no higher than that. And now here he was…wallowing in his spite and anger for the human race because some mortal had the gall to screw with his parents. You can’t play the good guy…it’s just not in your blood… He heard that inside himself, repeating over and over as the other voices argued about the course of action. It was the loudest of them all, bold and dominant. It was the same voice that stood out when he was chained up in the basement of his castle…the one that said everyone had to die. And just like then…it was making the most sense of them all. Why shouldn’t he? He had the ability…he had the right. It was basically all a matter of execution. He could go downstairs right then and there and slaughter the whole bunch of them…though most of the people down there wouldn’t go without a fight. Martial artist Inphino Blitz, psychopath Enzyme, former Japan Special Forces soldier Diametro, street fighters Iori and Passine, bounty hunter Botan, Cyclonica, who for some strange reason was an excellent fighter… Sonata… She wasn’t a master in Karate…had a black belt in Karate, but that’s only because her family used to run a school not too far from where Akiyama grew up. It was just that…out of all the people down there, Sonata, his cousin Ayumi, and strangely the Maxis daughter Hana he could imagine himself hesitating on. …compassion? There was a knock at his door. A gruff mutter from the god gave permission, and he watched as Hana Maxis walked into the room. Speak of the devil… “Can I come in?” During Akiyama’s time as the young girl’s bodyguard he never really could say no to her without a solid answer. He waved her in, and the girl shut the heavy door behind her before walking over and sitting on the edge of the god’s bed, facing him. Akiyama returned his stare to the sky as the honey haired girl tugged at the lapels of her shirt. “You heard we were all chatty, eh?” Hana looked up at the sudden break of the silence, more surprised to hear the voice of the man she used to know as Akira. “Yes,” she replied finally, “Sonata told me to come up if I wanted to speak with you.” Heh, that Sonata…always looking out for the god. Akiyama cleared his throat. “What’s going on down there?” “People, eating, staying on their side of the room.” Hana reached into her pocket and began to unwrap a piece of gum. “Mom and Dad are sitting in one corner, Inphino and Mina in another. They’re giving each other funny looks, and then your purple haired friend and her friends are in one corner talking. And Sonata’s walking around seeing that everyone’s okay.” Silence interjects as Akiyama nods slowly. Sonata looks down at her heels for a minute before looking back up at the silver haired god. “Your voice…” The god looked at her with a quizzical expression. “Nani?” “Your voice…” she repeated, “…it sounds different than how I remember it.” “…how do you remember it?” “Pleasant…at least not as serious around me. No witty sarcasm in your voice, like you used to have.” He shook his head slowly, thinking back to those days. “And now?” Sonata’s eyes lowered lightly. “Bitter, angry, like you don’t have anything left to live for.” Right on the nose. Akiyama sat up in his chair and folded his hands in his lap. “Well…” he began slowly, his voice still slow and steady. “The truth is…we don’t think we’ve ever been pleasant. We can’t remember a time that we were. But you were growing up at the time, and it was best that you get a positive attitude around there from someone at least…considering how you grew up with a mob boss and mad scientist for a ma and pa.” That brought a small smile to her face, despite the disappointing news. Akiyama looked down at the floor before continuing. “But maybe we was happy back then…and we just can’t remember.,,” a distant gaze overtook the silver eyes of the nVg as he looked back out to the cold gray sky. Hana looked over as well, leaning forward so her vision can fathom the clouds outside. Akiyama turned back to her, and something caught his eye. Hana Maxis had the top button of her shirt unbuttoned, the collars folded back against the rest of the shirt. Peering down her shirt he could see something silver flash in the faint light in the room. He nodded his head towards her with a curious look on his always-stern face. “Sore wa nan desu ka?” Hana looked around the vicinity for a second before Akiyama pointed a black nailed finger at her neck. She looked down and reached into her shirt, pulling out a silver chain with a small, rectangular pendant linked to it. The girl gave a soft smile as she looked down at it, rubbing the cold smooth metal between her hands. “Heh…you gave me this on your last day of work at Maxis Inc. You told me that as long as I wore this, things will never change…” It was her turn to grow distant as Akiyama leaned forward, reaching for the pendant. Hana leaned forward, allowing the god to rear his head down and inspect the silver casing. On the outside there was the numbers 999 etched onto it…the numbers tattooed onto the barcode on his right palm. He opened the pendant and found a reminder of his past… …A photo of the old days… A younger quartet standing together like family; in the front a young Hana with the same honey brown locks and bright smile, wearing a white sundress. Behind her was her parents, AJ Maxis in his gray pinstripe slacks and vest with white shirt and gray pinstripe tie, the gray haired and goateed man peering into the camera over half spectacles. Ryoko Godai was in her white laboratory jacket, the main source of color coming from her black/deep violet hair cascading on her shoulders. And behind them…now the Neo Void God…but then… Akira Akiyama… Still massive in size and stature, he stood dressed in a white Mandarin collar shirt and black vest with black slacks. His hair solid black and in a loose ponytail instead of the now tinted silver braided ponytail, he stood with his arms folded across his massive chest, his black taped hands gripping the sides of his arms. But that wasn’t the most important thing he was wearing… A smile A sigh came from within him, almost a growl if you didn’t know how to read Akiyama. Hana looked down at him, only seeing the top of his head as he leaned close to her to look at her pendant. “ “Change brings growth”.” The god’s voice was reminiscent as he read those words from her pendant. Hana smiled as she nodded once. “Departing words,” she muttered. Akiyama shook his head slowly, either disagreeing or in disbelief himself. “We’ve learned a lot over the years, kid… and one thing is for sure…change does bring growth. We saw more than a pendant down your shirt, if ya get me.” She gave a faint chuckle as Akiyama continued to stare into the picture, entranced somewhat by the past, the man he used to be or the life he used to live. “You’re growing up, kid, becoming a woman and the likes. Soon you’ll be getting yourself a guy and getting hitched…and next thing you know you’ll be having kids of ya own and taking care of Maxis Inc while ya ma and pa are off on retirement somewhere. You know where we’ll be about that point?” When she shook her head, he proceeded: “We’ll still be here…doing what we’ve done for probably more than our entire life. Committing violence…being a primary source of suffering for a good number of people. You know why?” Another shake of the head prompted progression: “Because…it’s what we do best. Change is growth…sometimes its for the better and worse. But we don’t change…we only continue to grow more and more powerful, more violent…more bitter, more angry. There’s a lot of people in the world that deserve punishment for their sins…and our growth will just continue in that direction until we’re done.” Hana looked a bit sad from that explanation, and as Akiyama looked up into her bright eyes he sighed softly. “Don’t ask why, it just happens. You shouldn’t worry about it anyways… all you need to worry about is who’s gonna take care of ya kid when you’re the big shot Yakuza queen mad scientist of Maxis Inc.” That brought the smile back on her face. “Oh? And what makes you think I’ll take over either of my parents’ work?” The god shrugged casually. “Gotta take over one of their jobs…or both.” She nodded in agreement with a laugh. “Yeah, well when that time comes…I’ll come to you for the job…since you did such a good job raising their mother.” It was Akiyama’s turn to chuckle now, a strange sound when not used all the time. His eyes shifted from Hana’s face to past her…at the door. Standing there with her arms folded across her chest was Sonata, watching with a smile on her face. Akiyama leaned back from Hana abruptly and rested his palms against his thighs, exhaling deeply. Hana looked back, and smiled at Sonata in a friendly manner. “Might wanna scoot down there and check on ya ma and pa, kid.” Akiyama suggested, leaning back in his chair again and looking up to the sky yet again. “Make sure they haven’t made any schemes or anything to take out Inphino before we get a shot at ‘im.” Hana took on a knowing look as she stood to her feet, nodding in agreement. “You know how they are with their schemes…mostly against Inphino…” She winked at the god before walking towards the door. Sonata patted her on her shoulder as the young Maxis left, closing the door behind her and leaving herself alone with the god. She walked over to Akiyama and stood close to him by his chair, looking out at the sky as well. “Nice girl.” Sonata stated about Hana observantly. Akiyama nodded slowly, his eyes pinned to the clouds. “We guess.” “You should know. You’re responsible for the way she turned out. You raised her as if she were your own daughter, and she turned out fine.” “No we didn’t…if we raised her like she was our daughter she would have put a sledgehammer in our skull as we were looking down her shirt.” Sometimes there were things that were left better worded. Akiyama looked up at the arched eyebrow of Sonata and shook his head. “Not as it sounds.” She shook it off for the moment, though it would come up again in a later conversation. “You can’t hold yourself responsible for Cyclonica’s upbringing. She was raised without you, or anyone else for that matter. She’s got a lot of pent up aggression and bitterness towards the world, and she’ll have to deal with it on her own terms. You’re only her father…you didn’t make her the way she is.” “We think her mother had something to do with that…still not sure about that.” Sonata giggled as she sat down on the arm of the chair. Akiyama’s eyes snapped to the slender thigh perched across the chair’s arm, delicately brushing against his forearm. Then his eyes grew narrow as he looked up at Sonata. She continued to stare out at the clouds, paying no attention to the look on the god’s face. Clearing his throat, he looked back out the window to the sky. “But we’re not gonna worry about that right now. All we want to do is just sit here…and think about what will be at this point…” Sonata nodded as she scooted back onto the arm of the chair, leaning back against Akiyama’s shoulder and resting her head against his neck. Akiyama’s body grew tense, as it always did when a physical presence grew close. It was his defense…not just in the ring was he on guard, but it was everywhere. It was part of what made him such a force to stray away from. But for some reason… …Sonata sitting there with him didn’t make him feel as tense…
