The Intruder Part 3

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-03 RP 3 Turmoil 15th “AHHHHH!!!” Bobbie screams watching Teresa blow a hole in the floor under his chair. The bullet misses his family jewels by a hair and his life had already flashed before his eyes so it seemed, his children, wife, and job. Then his chair falls back and his head hits the hardwood floor with a thud. The back of the chair mashes his tied hands underneath. He looks up and Teresa is standing over him. Her shotgun resting on her shoulders and she’s looking down with her head tilted slightly. “How did you find this place Bobbie?” Teresa speaks as if nothing has happened this afternoon. She can see his body shaking as he lies on the floor like a cold wet puppy. She reaches her arm toward the table and takes a Winston from the nearly empty pack. After the cancer stick is placed between her small lips, she takes a lighter from her front pocket of her Levi’s and lights the end. She does this all with one hand. “Your mother told me. I’m just wanted to ask you a few simple questions. Please Miss Tomas, let me go.” This time the end of her shotgun is forced in his mouth. The barrels still hot, burn his tongue. Bobbie attempts to scream, which causes Teresa to force the barrels further down his throat. He wriggles his wrists around against the ropes that bind him. “My MOTHER? You have the BALLS to bother my mother?” At this moment, Teresa throws her head back in laughter. How anyone could visit her mother and leave without wanting to kill oneself is beyond her. That poor woman would sit you down and kill you with stories of long ago and tell you her cooking secrets as if no one could cook better than she could. She would feed you homemade cookies and make you drink milk her farm hands milked from her cow Belle the morning before. “You’re some man to have survived my mother, but that doesn’t mean you’re going to be as lucky at my house.” She pulls the gun out of his mouth and drops her cigarette ashes over his face. He blinks to keep them out of his eyes. Teresa then turns her back and walks away leaving the reporter lying on the floor. She walks to a closet and opens the door. When the door shuts, she steps back with a box of shells. Her lips form a huge grin as she loads the gun. Her cigarette dangles from the corner of her mouth. Her cold blue eyes fixed on the bloody-faced reporter who is staring back in fear and confusion. The whole time the gun was in her mouth, it was empty. “Now what you going to do? You don’t have the nerve to shoot Me.” Teresa presses the Winston into an ashtray. What she doesn’t realize is Bobbie has finally freed his hands. Weakly, he pushes himself to a stand. His face, hands, and suit are a bloody mess. The brave reporter begins his first steps toward the door and stops standing in front of her. His hand grabs the shotgun and gives it a little tug. Teresa tugs back refusing to let it go. Bobbie tugs harder but Teresa’s grip is stronger. She points the gun to his gut daring him to grab it again. He wraps his hands around the barrel and tries to pull it away from him. Instead when he pulled, Teresa pushed and Bobbie stumbled back fell on his ass. BOOM!!!! Teresa sighs. “Stupid reporter broke my favorite beer mug.”
