Date: 05/30/03 (OOC: I apologize for the very short size, and description.. just came back from a harsh day at school and saw that I needed at least an RP for Fusion and had a very small amount of time.) 'PREPOSTEROUS!' a rough voice bluntly roared, muffled by the receiving end of the connecting telephones of another figure simultaneously speaking. Listening over the other end of the telephone, from the recipient was the dexterous misogynist, Stone Burns. Perched abed with the cradle in hand, and the phone in the other he returns the words from the recipient on the other end by the name of Vince Stapleton, a laboratory scientist of multiple tasks. 'I did not lose at the hands of a woman... but my fellow partner did.' Stone corrected, fiddling with the twisted cords attached to the phone. 'But seeing that I've got you as a friend-' Stone smiles and then adds.. 'You'll make this lethal bacterium so I can continue my campaign.' 'Just for women..?' Vince retorted questioningly -- 'Yes.. not towards men... yet.' Stone transferred back, bringing the feel of a nod from Vince to the receiving phone then silencing awkwardly for a moment, then being ended with the final words... 'I will call you in, precisely, three weeks.. then i'll be done... OKAY?' Vince ended, as Stone nodded approvingly. 'That'll be just fine... just fine.' Stone smirked, as an ending greet came to the close of the phone. The misogynist leaned over to the side of his Orlando, Florida rented; two star motel bed and placed the palms of his hands on his gut. 'What I am going to do for this world...' Stone gibed, leaning to the other side, where a drawer stood and up on it, his blade.. he went over with his hand and cosseted it while lastly inputting.. 'Dan Osbourne.'
