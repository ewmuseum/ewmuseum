The Missing Factor to Success (Introduction)

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/02/02 Determination. {Sweat dripping} Strive to win, strive to win, strive to win. {Punches, sweat, anguish, grinning} Must keep going, don’t stop, and never stop. You are the best; you can do this if you just keep going. {Punches harder, the sweat flows more, and the grinning becomes tighter} Just a little more, all it takes is the extra fifteen percent. The extra fifteen percent that certifies complete PERFECTION! {Stops punching, deep breath, sight of relief} Romantico steps back from the punching bag he had been beating recklessly at in the gym, and all that’s left is cotton and shredded concave all over the place. The punching bag was completely demolished with Romantico’s bare bloody hands. Everyone in the gym looks on in utter disbelief as they see carnage far beyond the boundaries of a normal man. Romantico is about to exit the gym, when a suspecting looking man stares him down from the corner of the room. He rises from his dark positioning and makes him way toward Romantico slowly and with slight footsteps. Excuse me, El Romantico. That’s my name, and you? Happy you asked you see. The name is Julio Alvarez, you can call me Al. You see I got a little proposition you might just want to here. {Winks} PCW, your current fed. I know it I know it very well. I know how the game is played and I can play it, play it to perfection. They call me the best, but I don’t think I’m the best I just do what it takes to win. Win you might be thinking to yourself, yes win. Winning as in titles, the fans, the federation, and the world. I go big, real big, so big that this entire universe looks like a shoebox. I’ve seen you wrestle, very nice style, I like what I see. You could be the best. Romantico seems to have heard enough but the short and stubby man who’s slightly balding holds his hand in front of Romantico. I can make you the best. Please take a seat, let us discuss. I have nothing to discuss. What about how you lost the number one contender match for the Cruiserweight Title? It’s none of your business. I had personal issues and was a little off that day. Off, I can make sure you never have an off day again. And how would you do that? Through hard work, determination, and goals. If your mind wants to win, you’ll win my friend. There’s no doubting it. I can’t help you build more strength or wrestling skill, but I can make you mentally fit. I’m not interested Julio quickly runs after Romantico. Here me out Romantico, we can make this work. I know your new goal; it’s to devote more time to your wrestling game. Let me help you rise to the peak of the competition. I guarantee success. I don’t need any help to start winning. You might not, but you will need help to move up the PCW chain. Your independent, no one has your back and is making sure you get what you rightfully deserve. I can do that for you, and I can play the mind games no other man would dare play. I trust your good at whatever this is you call. Professional Managers Company. We handle the best, train the best, and make sure our men stay the best. Only a select few are selected, you should be honored. Yea, well what ever, what I’m trying to say is I’m not the one who needs this right now. I like working independently. Is there anything I can do to change your mind? I’m afraid not. Well then it’s been an honor talking to you. Thanks a lot for the time, and if anything pops up, call me. Julio shakes Romantico’s hand and then slips his business card in Romantico’s pocket. Romantico then leaves the gym and is about to throw away the card as soon as possible, before he has a sudden thought. He imagines the Cruiserweight belt around his waist just like it was around his brothers waist twice before. He then takes the card and puts it right back in his pocket before getting in his car and leaving.
