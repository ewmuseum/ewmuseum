Sig Flags... [Part 1]

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/26/02 OOC Note: I was supposed to post this on Tuesday, but I just didn't finish it in time. Enjoy. [Tape delayed from Tuesday.] Jordan X Shabazz was tired. Mentally, and physically. He had flown all the way from South Africa, on a non-stop flight to Memphis, Tennessee. Jordan was being driven by one of his two butlers, Steve Armstrong. And I use the term ‘Butlers’ loosely. Jordan X Shabazz had hired Chris Bird and Steve Armstrong to chaperone him around town, doing whatever he asked. At the end of the day they were given a twenty dollar bill and a pool house in which to stay. Jordan didn’t allow them to eat any of his food, Jordan didn’t allow them to follow him to the arena, and Jordan didn’t allow them to meet any of his wrestling buddies. A while back, Jordan and Duzza, had an argument. With Duzza throwing Jordan out of a moving vehicle on the highway. Unrealistic? Maybe. Impossible? Not by a long shot. But now, Jordan and Duzza are friends again. Jordan couldn’t stay mad at a fellow African American for too long. Duzza was an older brother figure to Jordan X Shabazz, and now Jordan and him were going to go out on a day of fun relaxing, and hopefully, picking up some ladies… no… Duzza is not going to be looking for teenagers you sick FREAK! We zoom on a sign that states “Now entering Memphis, Tennessee.” ************* Twenty minutes later. We are now outside of a local Six Flags amusement park. Duzza was wrestling that very night against a returning Pharaoh. Jordan was wrestling that night with his buddy, Rush Mason, against Andy Murray and John Crichton, in a prelude to their Sunday battle that certainly would be a violent clash. But before then, both men just wanted to relax. Chill out, and have a good time. The pressure of a huge title match coming up on a National pay Per View, and the pressure of teaming to face two hated rivals later on in the night, were off and out of Jordan’s mind. Chris Bird and Steve Armstrong, Jordan’s two butlers, went to the hotel, they were to arrive to pick Jordan and Duzza up in three hours. Then they would head to the Pyramid and get ready to go into the ring later on in the evening. The sun beat down on Jordan as he slowly walked towards his long time friend, and one time enemy, Duzza. Jordan gives Duzza daps, and he smiles. Jordan X Shabazz: “You ready to go?” Duzza: “Hell yeah!” Jordan and Duzza walk through the gates of the amusement park. They pay the entrance fee, get their wristbands needed for proof of payment, and now they are in the park, ready to run wild on the unfortunate people in the park with them. Jordan and Duzza are like two children in a candy store, they stand in awe of all the spectacular and large rides. Jordan X Shabazz: “This park is unusually big. Before we ride anything, let’s go get some food.” Perhaps one of the stupidest suggestions ever heard, but nonetheless, Duzza agrees with Jordan and the two men begin walking inside the Amusement park, looking for the right place where they will spend their money on some food. Finally, Jordan and Duzza spot a… yeah, you guessed it, a KFC! Jordan immediately gets excited at the smell and thought of greasy chicken, fries, and Pepsi, so he begins running towards the KFC. Duzza isn’t that far behind. There’s a small kid, no older than 10, he’s also running towards the KFC, or at least in that general direction. The child was in fact running towards the ride that was only a mere 30 seconds away from the KFC. Jordan thought the kid was trying to run in front of him in line… and you don’t know black folks. Lesson Number One: DON’T FUCK WITH US WHEN IT COMES TO GREASY ASS CHICKEN!! Sadly, this kid didn’t know that. So Jordan ran full speed into this 10-year old looking kid, and he took him down an ‘accidental’ elbow to the head. The kid, who is white, and has one of those overprotective mother types, begins screaming, his mother is already screaming. Mother: “My BABY! NOOOOOO! MY BEAUTIFUL BABY!!” She looks up at the Civil Rights Extremist. Mother: “You bastard! HOW DARE YOU DO THIS TO MY CHILD?!” Duzza steps in, not wanting to get into any trouble, knowing that Jordan would take pride in slapping the hell out of any white women who disrespected him. Duzza: “Let’s just go get the chicken, then get on the rides. We each have huge Pay Per View matches coming up, and relaxation by KFC is one of the only ways to succeed in our goals of becoming champions.” Duzza’s remarks, aren’t usually something you’d expect to hear out of the mouth of this superstar. Jordan looks at him, and then he looks at the kid he knocked down and his mother, he laughs, loudly. Jordan X Shabazz and Duzza walk up to the KFC and get ready to place their orders. What Jordan and Duzza don’t know, is that this particular KFC is understaffed, and they are experiencing troubles servicing their costumers. Jordan X Shabazz sees that there’s nobody working the register, and he begins banging on the table. Jordan X Shabazz: “Hello, honkey’s? We’re ready to order!!” Jordan continues banging on the counter until a pimply faced KFC worker kid stands before them. PFKFCWK: “Hello gentlemen, how may I help you?” Jordan X Shabazz: “First off, whitey, you can take that smug smile of your face.” The PFKFCWK looks at Jordan weirdly, and he stops smiling like he was before. Like the people at KFC told him to. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now, I would like three 20 buckets of chicken, 10 buffalo wings, and 3 sodas.” Jordan turns to Duzza. Jordan X Shabazz: “Do you want anything?” Duzza is shocked. Duzza: “JESUS CHRIST JORDAN! Living in the south has turned you into a country black man…” Duzza sees the anger start to form on Jordan’s face, and he quickly places his order. Duzza: “Can I get a spicy chicken sandwich, and a large Pepsi?” PFKFCWK walks towards the back, where the food is being made, and he spends a good five minutes there. Jordan X Shabazz is banging loudly on the counter screaming of racism, and asking when they will get their service. When the kid returns, he’s saddened. PFKFCWK: “I apologize sirs, but we have run out of chicken.” Jordan instantly is angered. Jordan X Shabazz: “WHAT?! KFC RAN OUT OF CHICKEN? COME ON, I KNOW THAT THAT MANY BLACK PEOPLE DON’T FREQUENT THIS AREA, WHITE PEOPLE CAN’T EAT THAT MUCH CHICKEN!!” The PFKFCWK is dumbfounded and doesn’t speak. Jordan X Shabazz: “IT’S A DAMNED GOVERNMENT CONSPIRACY! WHY DON’T YOU CALL KRAMER WILD WING OVER HERE?! HE ALWAYS SEEMS TO HAVE CHICKEN WITH HIM! HE NEVER SHARES ANY WITH ME, THAT GREEDY BASTARD!!” Jordan X Shabazz screams, being the confident guy that he is, thinking that he already is going to defeat John Crichton for the Television Title and move on to face Kramer Wild Wing, Jordan has had his mind on the KFC spokesperson. Duzza doesn’t know what to say, he’s speechless. Jordan X Shabazz: “A GOVERNMENT CONSPIRACY TO MAKE THE BLACK MAN MAD! THEY KNOW WE LOVE OUR CHICKEN, SO THEY GO AHEAD AND…” Duzza cuts in and smacks Jordan, hard, across the face. Duzza: “*What* The hell is wrong with you? Relax yourself.” Jordan nods and thanks Duzza for snapping him out of it. Jordan and Duzza leave this section of the amusement park. As Duzza and Jordan roam the amusement park, a poster advertising Gold and Glory is seen. Jordan X Shabazz: “You think I could take him?” Duzza looks at Jordan, confused. Duzza: “Who?” Jordan X Shabazz: “John Crichton.” Duzza nods. Duzza: “Yeah, you could take any white boy.” Jordan X Shabazz smiles. Jordan X Shabazz: “How about Jaden? Think I could take her? In a different way, if you know what I mean.” Duzza laughs. Duzza: “We’ll see… we’ll see…” TO…BE…CONTINUED!!
