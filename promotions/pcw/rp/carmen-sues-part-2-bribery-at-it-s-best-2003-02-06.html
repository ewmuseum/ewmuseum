Carmen Sues - Part 2 - Bribery At It's Best

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 6, 2003 [Yeah, Carmen Electra is a bodacious babe looks wise. Once you get past her large breasts and smokin' body there really isn't much. In fact, most would think that her and 'Totally Tubular' Tommy would be perfect for each other. Carmen is evil personified when it comes to women. She's greedy and will do anything to make sure she has the cash. Tommy, well, everyone KNOWS what Tommy will do to secure his fortune AND his PCW Continental title. Yet another reason for Carmen and Tommy to be together forever, because they share so much in common. So people would think. It's this common bond they share that has them at each others throats at this point and time. Carmen, seeking to take Tommy for all he's worth because of the tape that he made of them having sex. Tommy is only looking to get out of there so that he can get to training for his upcoming eight man tag match and a singles match on Tuesday with that heinous dude Skitzo. What kind of name is Skitzo anyway? Isn't he that heinous dude who talks to himself? Sheesh, what is professional wrestling coming to when you have some dude talking to himself? This...coming from a guy who calls himself 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and thinks that he's a super villain. To each his own] Johnathan Roberts: Hello, Buster. I see that your looking much like you usually do. [The handsome, strong jawed super lawyer looks at Buster as if the man is ridiculous. Mr. Roberts does have a valid reason to do so because a short, fat man with spiked up hair like an Anime character and a red silk suit DESERVES a ridiculous look. As Buster just rolls his eyes, Mr. Roberts tosses a file folder onto the oak wood table. He's a shrewd man who likes to get right down to business] Mr. Roberts: Browse through the folder, Buster. Then just pay up the amount and everyone can go home. This fiasco can be put behind us and no one can be hurt any longer. If you don't wish to pay the amount, well, I'll just have to bleed surfer boy dry in court. He'll lose his fortune, his Continental Title, AND his stupid reputation...well, what's left of it. [Buster looks through the folder, not even LOOKING at the amount of money that has been requested. He tosses the folder back, ready for a war of words. Tommy smiles, knowing that the show is about to begin. He's about to witness a bodacious showing of lawyering skills by the Anime Tomato himself!] Buster: See, the money won't be paid, Johnny Boy! Because Tommy didn't tape the sexual acts... Carmen: Oh God! I watched him walk to the freakin' camera and pull the tape out. Then he said, 'Babe, I'm going to make some bodacious cash off of this!' Buster: First off, that was a HORRIBLE Tommy impression. Come on, slut, if your going to make fun of someone at least do it right.... 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It sounded pretty solid to me, dude... Buster: Shhh! SECONDLY....ummm.... TTT: Babe, it was my clone. Mr. Roberts: Your what?! TTT: My clone. A cult took my DNA and cloned me. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Did I get cloned too, man? TTT: Totally. Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! We can smoke together and rock out to Sister!! Mr. Roberts: Are you retarded? TTT: No, but my clone may be. Did you ever read Spider-Man comics? You know that bogus degeneration stuff? Well, that's what happened. Carmen: You are SO STUPID! TTT: In speaking of Spider-Man, that reminds me of this other BOGUS super hero named Rent-A-Jerkface. How I'm going to TOTALLY trash him this Friday in that eight man tag team match, THEN make him watch me beat the crap out of some heinous Skitzo dude. Carmen: What does that have to do with anything? TTT: Babe...it has EVERYTHING TO DO WITH THIS!! Kelly Slater: Your gay. TTT: Why don't you ask Carmen that...or I can sell you a copy of Tommy/Carmen. Slater: How much? TTT: Ten bucks. Slater: Deal. Carmen: KELLY! What the FUCK are you doing?! Johnny! He's doing it again! Right in a a court of law! He's selling the tape! The surfing bastard is selling the tape! TTT: Well, he asked. Buster: MR. ROBERTS! I suggest that we just forget all these silly antics. Seriously, we all know that Tommy can just stand up and kick your ass. So, save yourself the beating of your life and lets all go home. Mr. Roberts: Are you physically threatening me, Buster? Buster: No. Mr. Roberts: You just said Tommy would kick my ass!! Buster: Yeah...so? Mr. Roberts: YOUR THREATENING ME!! Buster: Pft, not even close. [Roberts just sighs and shakes his head. Buster Snyder is dumber than Tommy, and how the guy keeps on practicing law is just totally beyond him. He knew that when his former lover, Carmen, called that he should have just ignored it. Though the crazy bitch would have just keep calling and calling until he answer his cell phone. So, he really didn't have a choice in the matter. If he would have known that sticking his willy in Carmen would have done this, then he wouldn't have done it at all. He would have settled for Pam Anderson instead. Though, somehow, that would have gotten him into a situation like this too] TTT: Carmen, you know that you loved seeing that tape go for a cool five grand on E-Bay. You just want my money...well, you know what? You can have the money. [Tommy goes into his billfold and peels off the five thousand dollars and hands it over to Carmen. She takes it and looks at him oddly, not really sure what's going on. The Tommy that she gave herself to would NEVER have given in so easily. She was expecting Buster to do his thing, act stupid, and send this thing into a trial. That way she could take EVERYTHING and then not have to worry about bleeding men dry anymore. She would have enough of a fortune to live happily ever after. This is really putting a damper on things] Carmen: I DON'T WANT THIS! [Tommy opens the file folder and notices that the amount listed was the cost of the tape on E-Bay, and an apology. Obviously, this was drawn up by that other lawyer dude seeing that the money amount was so low. Yes, Tommy is a super villain and most wouldn't DARE give in to a heinous chick like Carmen, but Tommy is becoming more than a super villain now. He's learning from villains before him, the ones who are actually smart. He knows that if he just complies with the demands and walks out of there he avoids something terrible. He'll be able to rekindle the relationship and get Carmen back in the sack. That's when the REAL dream comes true. That's when Tommy makes the sequel and sells it for DOUBLE! It'll be amazing, it'll be radical, and most of all it's TOTALLY BODACIOUS] TTT: Well, that's what your going to take, babe. So, I'm most sorry for not consulting with you before selling the material on E-Bay. It was most unconsiderate of me and TOTALLY HEINOUS! I'd like to make it up to you even more by taking you out to dinner, and maybe pamper you a little bit at your favorite spa. I'd hate for you to have bogus feelings about me after this. [He hit Carmen where it hurts, he offered her a free pass to her favorite spa. That's the only way she can keep such beauty is by going there. Those people there give GREAT facials and keep her skin in such top notch shape. It's expensive but with a free session that gives her time to try and bleed another man dry! Damn Tommy, but he's SOOOO sexy!] Buster: Johnny, your going to just have to cope with the simple fact that your going to be on the receiving end of an ass kicking. I don't want to hear anymore of your whining, or your stupid excuses. Your going to HURT! It's going to be like a mafia beating! Or like when Goku totally tore ass into Freiza! YEAH! It's gonna be JUST LIKE THAT. Mr. Roberts: Our clients have obviously come to an agreement while you were babbling about nothing. It seems justice is done here, now I have another celebrity matter to attend to. Good day, gentlemen. Buster: You can't walk out of here!! We aren't done! We still haven't kicked your ass! TTT: Buster, it's over, dude. Your stuff just didn't work, but I was able to work it out. Carmen: So...Tommy, how about that dinner then? TTT: You bet, babe. [Tommy takes Carmen's hand and escorts her out of the room. Tommy is already dreaming about the great positions that he's going to put her through. Maybe introduce some bodacious toys into the mix. It'll be great for the camera this time! He'll just have to hide it better and be sure to take the tape out AFTER she leaves! It's going to be a grand eveing, dudes] Kelly Slater: Well, that just sucked...I didn't even get a hummer!! [Harry looks confused] Harry: A car, man? [Kelly and Buster laugh hysterically] Buster: You certainly are a babe in the woods, huh? Harry: I'm not a girl, man. [Buster rolls his eyes and grabs his briefcase] Buster: So, gentlemen...seeing that we are pathetic womanless losers...how about a Dragonball Z marathon at my place. Harry: Can I smoke pot there, man? Buster: Will you watch with me? Harry: As long as I can smoke, man. Buster: You got yourself a deal. You coming along, Kelly? Kelly Slater: I don't know, guys. I'm feeling pretty depressed. Tommy took my girl. Harry: Well, I WILL pass the bong around, man. [Slater's eyes begin to grow cheerful] Slater: COUNT ME IN, PARTNER!! [Fade to black]
