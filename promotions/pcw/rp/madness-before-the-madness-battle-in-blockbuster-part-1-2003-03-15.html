MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS!-Battle in Blockbuster Part 1

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MADNESS BEFORE THE MADNESS!-Battle in Blockbuster Part 1 Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-15-2003 ..::/::..Our story begins within the local Blockbuster video rental store, where we now find none other then PCW’s very own superhero, Rent-A-Hero, briskly searching the shelves of Blockbuster Video, home to a vast variety of movies from action to romance all at a reasonable price. Rent-A-Hero stands baffled in his Spiderman 2099 outfit and the PCW Crusierweight title about his waist, caught between the action and horror films in a daze as he looks about in confusion, exclaiming to the shock of the many store patrons who are startled by his sudden heroic monologue..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'This is more confusing then the maze of the Hydra in issue #234 of Danger Girl!! Even my Rent-A-Senses can't detect the whereabouts of that video within this emporium of movie marvels! DARN THE STORE SHELVE ORGANIZERS, they shall receive a BIZNITCH SLAP O' DOOM for their criminal confuzzeling of movie classifications! WHERE CAN I FIND “UNCHAINED HEARTS” AROUND HERE?!'..::/::..Irony truly sets in, for as Rent-A-Hero walks off to the left to further search for 'Unchained Hearts', he completely misses the flashing 'UNCHAINED HEARTS AVAILABLE TO THE RIGHT' sign behind him, once again proving that our hero is a few molecules short of a Kryptonian rod at times..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'I heard that narrator man...' ..::/::..*ahem* As Rent-A-Hero takes note of my saying, he turns about to the right to claim the tape, unwary of the fact that he is being trailed by a group of three ninja's, hiding behind a carboard cut-out stand up of the incredibly plump Sherman Klump for 'The Nutty Professor 3-DAMN YOU SO FAT!!'. Each ninja is decked out in identical silk ninja gi's, the only difference being one is in red, the other in black, and the third in blue. The ninja in black taps the red and blue ninja, sending each into separate directions as they continue their trail of the Kryptonian crime-fighter of the wrestling world. As our hero continues down the aisle, his eyes catch sight of the popular anime film 'Arimtage III', sparking his interest immediately upon sight...::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'BY THE POWER OF GREYSKULL, THE DIRECTOR'S CUT WITH KARATE SCHOOLGIRLS, MY PRAYERS HAVE BEEN ANSWERED!!'..::/::..As Rent-A-Hero bends down to get a better look at the film, the instant his head is ducked down, three shurikens rip through the air and miss him completely...yet finding a mark further down the aisle as a patron off screen yells..::/::.. Patron:'AHHH MY EYE!! THANK GOD I CAN STILL GOT THE OTHER EY' *another piercing sound can be heard*...GYAAAAAH!!!! WELL AT LEAST I CAN WALK...' *another piercing sound can be heard* 'WHY GOD...WHYYYYYYY!! I should have just stayed home for an HBO movie!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Mindless screaming...the sound of shurikens wizzing past my head....those three ninja's standing in front of the 'Unchained Heart's' movie booth....naaaah, I don't see what's wrong with this situat....NINJA'S?!'..::/::..Our hero turns to the large display shelf where just ONE copy of 'Unchained Hearts' sits all by it's lonesome on the rack...but impeding his quest of attaining the video are the three ninja's, standing shoulder to shoulder and with looks of murderous intent for our hero. Rent-A-Hero stands undaunted by their threatening demeanor and stance, raising his fists to prepare for battle...but instead of a fist fight breaking out, the ninja in black steps forward, addressing our hero after a hearty bow..::/::.. Black Ninja:'Ahhh yes...the famous Rent-A-Hero....' Rent-A-Hero: *poking his chest out in pride* 'You must have heard of my many PCW title wins...or possibly my upset victory over Jason Snow....or my Continetal title victory....or the fact I had recently won the PCW Crusierweight title for the SECOND time!' Black Ninja:'Well...actually I saw when you were heaved off the roof by the Dark Ages..but that's another story. We are here to make sure you don't live to take part in the March Madness battle royal, Rent-A-Hero. Our mission has simply entailed as to simply bring back your prostate.' Rent-A-Hero:'HOW DID YOU KNOW OF MY PLANS TO TAKE PART IN MARCH MADNESS?! My prostate?! Why HOLY SHARING AND CARING ninja type men people, I'm sure that shouldn't be a problem....ummm...what is a prostate?' ..::/::..The red ninja quickly approaches Rent-A-Hero, cupping and shortly whispering into his ear. After that short whispering, the red ninja returns to the side of his companions as Rent-A-Hero's face construes into one of total disdain, looking to the ninja's and exclaiming..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Change of heart...LEAVE MY PROSTATE ALONE!! And as for getting me to not go to March Madness, I believe that can't be a possibility for I must prove I'm the greatest superhero and wrestler on the planet by winning the PCW World title….and I must beat the SHIZZNITTLEBAM out of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy! If it means having to bring you three to justice, then so be it!' Black Ninja:'Well...have it your way Rent-A-Hero...but you shall not make it to that battle royal in one piece. Prepare for battle spandex one and prepare for the consequences of losing.' Rent-A-Hero:'Narrator man...action music please?' ..::/::..Stock hard rock music plays, the customers and the ninja's looking up to the sky in wonder of where it's coming from as Rent-A-Hero bends his knees slightly, striking a confident bright-toothed smile as he holds his hands up ready for battle...our hero summing up his readiness to battle in one word..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Groovy...'Off Screen Voice:'WAIT WAIT WAIT!!!'..::/::..As our hero prepares to engage the also battle hungry ninja's, a stout man with glasses, thinning hair 'hidden' by a terrible combover, and wearing a blue Blockbuster vest with 'Charlie-Manager' imprinted on the metallic name tag. He gets between both men, sweat beading off his cheeks and forehead as he exclaims..::/::..Charlie:'WAIT!! WHO'S GONNA CLEAN ALL THIS UP AFTER YOU GUYS GET DONE WITH ALL THIS FIGHTING!!'Rent-A-Hero:'Excuse me for a minute my ninja friends...' *turns to Charlie, putting an arm around him* 'Have you not forgotten good manager that this is a RENT-A-HERO RP!! By the next scene change, everything will be repaired and back to normal. 'Logic' and 'Rent-A-Hero RP' go just about as hand in hand as 'Lil Kim' and 'virgin.' Does that answer your question good sir?' Charlie:'Of course. Carry on good men.'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero throws up a thumbs up, turning his focus back to the ninja's as they throw up their fighting stance, the three of them rushing at our hero as he in turn rushes towards them. JUST AS THE FIRST PUNCH LOOKS TO BE THROWN, THE SCENE FREEZES, fading to black as we leave Rent-A-Hero to do battle with the three ninja's determined to kee him from competeing in the Wrestling God tourney tomorrow. WILL RENT-A-HERO SURVIVE THIS NINJA ONSLAUGHT?! WILL LOGIC EVER BE COMMONPLACE IN A RENT-A-HERO RP?! WILL RENT-A-HERO MAKE IT TO THE BATTLE ROYAL IN ONE PIECE?! FIND OUT, SAME RENT-A-TIME....SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
