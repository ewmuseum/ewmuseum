Adventures with Pete [4]

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20th, 2002 Comment-Allez vous? Je suis bien, merci. What’s up with this French, you ask? Well, if you must know, French is the language of the gamblers! Yes, anyone who gambles knows that Le Francais is the only way to bet your money. Anyways, when we last left our two heroes, they had just gotten into a dangerous accident with a Toyota Camry. We, of course, were driving stylishly in a Volkswagon Bug at the time, and were probably lucky that we hadn’t been crushed in the collision. Leave it to Pete to rent us a Bug, that cheap ass bastard. Anyways, what surprised us even more was the driver of the car…it was The Puzzled Bird Guy! Oh my gosh! I was surprised too! Man, what was he doing out here anyways? I still don’t know either, but it’s on my “To Do” list. Hold on a second…[puts finding out why The Puzzled Bird Guy is in Reno on his “To Do” list]…there we go. Now I feel much better. Well, shall we continue on? You see, this bird fellow is only one of many problems we have. We still haven’t spoken to R.L. Stine, the man who holds the possible location of the Amulet in his possession. We would be, of course, walking it to his palace since our car was put out of commission by The Puzzled Bird Guy and his Camry. Who knew the guy could fly, anyways? But most importantly, I would be stepping into the ring with not one, not two, but three other PCW superstars at Gold and Glory who all wanted that Continentel title around their waists. Doesn’t that sound lovely? And it’s not your average match – it’s a cage match. That’s like nine feet of steel that I would have to climb to win the match. And I have Miyagawa, the Japo-American, trying to pull me down. If not him, maybe Dukie Williams, my old friend. Lord knows he’s due for a great win, and I know he could pull this out, but I want it too much. If I have to pull his hillbilly bottom from the cage, I would do it. And then, what about Xavier Kannon? The tilde using, Ellie~! Abusing, no respectin’ former champion. I officially named the OBSCENITY against him, and now I’m expected to try and tear his rightful claim to the belt away. Could I do it? Would I do it? All I know is that this amulet would sure make things a lot easier. It’s time to time travel boys and girls (if you don’t remember, that’s what the amulet does). [The scene fades in with Pete Rose and Tom Lexian walking down the strip of Reno. Massive buildings surround them from all sides, and the lights are beginning to blind Tom. He began to just stare down at his feet, ignoring the bright glares of the lights. Pete, in the mean time, had placed a pair of sunglasses on his face and was confidently walking down the sidewalk with his bat slung over his shoulder.] Tom Lexian: [still shielding his eyes] For the love of god, hasn’t anyone told the people that own these buildings that there’s a power shortage? Pete Rose: Oh c’mon Tommy, are the lights really that bright? They’re beautiful, you have to admit… Tom Lexian: First of all, don’t call me Tommy. Secondly, they are way too bright. Beautiful, perhaps, but there’s no reason to have a thousand lights which spell out the word “Boom Boom Room.” [Pete stops in his tracks and looks up at the strip joint. The shop even has a mechanical woman wearing nothing but a bathing suit ushering patrons in. How wonderful.] Pete Rose: Hey, let’s check that out really quick, Tom. I’m sure Mr. Stine wouldn’t mind waiting a bit longer… [Tom grabs his arm and leads him away from the store.] Tom Lexian: You’re right, he might not, but I do. And don’t forget, I’m leading this little brigade of travelers to find the amulet. I’m Michael Jackson, you’re Tito. Pete Rose: [shrugs away Tom’s grip on his arm.] You watch too much Rush Hour. Anyways, I didn’t know two people constituted a brigade. Hell, we’re hardly a couple! [A few people nearby overhear Pete’s comments about them being a couple, and begin whispering to themselves. Pete gets upset and starts chasing people with his bat. That lasts for about five minutes. Tom, meanwhile, has sat down near the edge of a building, waiting for Pete to finish. A few moments later, Pete walks up to Tom, sweating like a pig, and dropping his bat to the ground.] Pete Rose: [huffing and puffing] Aww shit, I’m tired. These legs just aren’t the same as they used to be, Tom. I think I broke my colon… Tom Lexian: [shaking his head] First of all, your colon is in your stomach. If you broke it, you’d be passed out on the ground dying. Pete Rose: [kneels down on one knee.] Give me a moment and I can have that arranged… Tom Lexian: Secondly, nothings wrong with your legs. It’s that gut of yours that’s holding you back, buddy. Look at it…it jiggles more than those women you were gawking at in that strip bar. Hell, it jiggles more than Tyler Nelson’s man boobs! Pete Rose: [checking his gut] It’s not that bad. Sure, I could probably stand to lose a few pounds…well, okay, maybe more than a few pounds. I should get back to doing sit ups again. Hmmm…nah! Anyways, why were you so concerned about the lights earlier? You seemed pretty adamant about it. Tom Lexian: I live in California. Back there, electricity is more of a commodity. Of course, we were screwed by those damn energy companies for all we were worth. Now I see Reno wasting mega watts of energy on these lights, and I’m thinking it’s all for nothing. Pete Rose: I suppose you’re right. Man, you should be an environmentalist or something. I, myself, am pro-nature. Tom Lexian: [smiles] Is that right? Pete Rose: [nodding proudly and patting himself on the back] No one can say that Pete Rose ever hated nature. I’m down with Mother Nature. Tom Lexian: Gee, I didn’t know you and Mother Nature were “homies.” Well, if that’s true, then why did you cut up one of her children to make that bat you were swinging to kill those tourists over there? Pete Rose: [stares at the bat] Uhm, it’s a loaner? Tom Lexian: [shakes his head, standing up] C’mon, let’s get out of here before someone calls the cops on you again. [Tom and Pete head down the strip, admiring the beautiful starry night. There are a large amount of people on the strip at this time of night, mostly tourists or gamblers down on their luck. It made for a depressing walk, except for the stars, of course. Pete seemed to gawk at every single stripper or prostitute that crossed our path. I was getting a bit tired of it, but I wasn’t sure what I could do to make him stop. Finally, the last straw occurred. A woman dressed in a skanky red skirt and a yellow boa walked up to us. She had long brown hair, tons of make up on her face, and no shoes. She was walking in her bare feet. Made you wonder what else was bare. Pete seemed absolutely entranced by her.] Pete Rose: [excited] Well, hello my dear. How are you this fine evening? Hooker: [batting her eyes quickly] Fine, and yourself? My, your bat is so big…can I touch it? [Hooker touches Pete’s bat. Pete smiles broadly. I could only imagine what sick thoughts were going through his perverted mind. I, on the other hand, noticed something weird about the hooker.] Tom Lexian: [watching the hooker closely] Excuse me, ma’ma…but is there something in your eye? Hooker: [continues to bat her eyes] No, why? Tom Lexian: Well, I was just noticing that your eye keeps blinking wildly… Hooker: Ah, no. I was just batting them. The older girls say it makes you look ten times sexier. Tom Lexian: [looks relieved] Oh, thank god. I thought you were having a stroke or something. I remember one incident, where I was Christmas shopping in Frisco, and I was strolling through some of the local shops and stuff. Well, I had my arms full of bags, all of them from a very cheap store that sold clothing, and I was headed for the parking garage. Hooker: Do you always buy clothing at cheap stores? I mean, isn’t that a bit embarrassing? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Maybe for some people, but not for me. Hell, I don’t care at all. Then gain, I have a trick in my sleeve. You see, I bring my own bags with me. They’re usually bags from such stores like Gap, JC Penny’s, or one of those really expensive stores that I would never shop in. Anyways, I’d throw in my discount purchases in there, and voila, no one is the wiser. Well, let me tell you about that particular day, and what a day it was. You see, I was coming down on Embarcadero avenue, surrounded by another million other shoppers, and I just happen to be walking alongside this old man. The guy looked fit though. He wasn’t dying or really sickly looking…he probably worked out a lot when he was younger. Hell, I’d go as for to say that if I was his age, I’d like to look like that guy. He was built. Well, not that I check out guys or anything…no, I would never. Anyways, we were walking through the crowded sidewalk, and suddenly, the guy turned to me and began to start blinking at me, similar to the way you were doing a few minutes ago. I thought the poor bastard was trying to come on to me or something, so I shoved him away from me. Then he keeled over like a sack of potatoes. Poor guy ended up having a… Hooker: [interrupting] A stroke? Tom Lexian: You would think so, wouldn’t you? But it wasn’t a stroke. I went down to the hospital to check on the guy. I guess I was feeling guilty, since I did shove him away from me. I found out that the guy had a mild heart attack. He later told me that it was because he realized how much money he had spent on Christmas presents. Man, I didn’t know the holidays could do that to someone. Hooker: [saddened] That’s terrible… Tom Lexian: [nodding his head] I completely agree… [The hooker slides her hand inside Tom’s shirt and kisses him on the cheek.] Hooker: [begins to bat her eyes some more] So, you want to have some fun tonight? Tom Lexian: [raises an eyebrow] What you talkin’ about? [Pete stamps his foot in anger.] Pete Rose: [sadly] Why didn’t you offer that to me? He can’t have you, he’s got a girlfriend! A pretty one too! Tom Lexian: How do you know my girl’s pretty? Pete Rose: [shrugs] I assume so. Well, I’d hope so. Otherwise, you’d be a sad puppy, and since you’re so peppy all the time, I just figured you were getting laid 24/7… Tom Lexian: Hah!…[realizes that he doesn’t get laid 24/7]…Oh, dammit… Hooker: [looks at Tom sadly] So, you don’t want to have fun with me tonight? [gives Tom a sad, puppy dog face] Tom Lexian: I’m sorry, I probably shouldn’t. I’m very much in love with my girlfriend. When I come home, she cooks me eggs and waffles, and tells me how smart I am. I think she believes I’m smart because I chose her, but that’s just what I think. Of course, what I think doesn’t matter…..you know, because I’m a guy and she’s a girl…and she’s dominant over me…and I have no say in the relationship….and….and….[breaks down crying] Pete Rose: Look, obviously Tom’s not up for it. I am though! [eagerly rubs his hands] Tom Lexian: [stops crying for a moment] Pete, what are you doing? You have a wife, don’t you? Pete Rose: Well, sort of… Tom Lexian: I can’t believe you! You lying, two timing, beer swilling, cheating bastard! How dare you cheat on her! After how understanding she’s been with you, that entire betting scandal, that gut hanging from your body….I can’t believe this! Pete Rose: Tom, Tom, Tom…..calm down, buddy. Everything’s going to be alright… [The hooker watches the two exchange words, and shrugs.] Hooker: Let me guess…you both are lovers? [Pete gets an angry look on his face and picks up his bat. He then proceeds to chase the hooker around with the bat for a few minutes, while Tom reties his shoe laces. Finally, the two stop the pursuit, and the hooker ended up outlasting Pete. You have to wonder how long Pete lasts in bed, considering he couldn’t outrun a hooker with a bat, and is now sweating like a pig in the amount of time it took Tom to tie his shoelaces.] Pete: [huffing and puffing] I’ll get you yet!…once I catch my breath, that is…. Hooker: [falls to the ground crying] I guess I have no more customers tonight…again…[begins to cry uncontrollably.] [Some people stop to watch as a hooker begins to bawl and cry near two middle aged man standing over her. This really didn’t look good. Tom went over to console her.] Tom Lexian: [puts his arm around the hooker] Hey, don’t cry. Everything’s going to be alright. Look, you have nothing to be sad about. You’re a beautiful girl, you speak well….uh, you have no loose teeth….uh, your hair isn’t falling out….uh…Pete, help me out here… [Pete stares at the hooker for a moment.] Pete Rose: [in amazement] You’ve got really big knockers. [Tom nearly shoots himself of embarrassment right then and there.] Tom Lexian: What Pete means to say is that he thinks you have a wonderful personality. I think so too. I don’t understand why you’re a prostitute to begin with. You should be doing something better with your time….like, uhm, I don’t know, having kids or something. Pete Rose: Yeah, like lots of them! With different partners! Tom Lexian: Pete, you’re not helping. Pete Rose: Sorry Tom. It was the testosterone talking. Hooker: [blows her nose on Pete’s pant leg] I’m just so sad. I’ve never been good at anything in my life. I’ve been a failure since day one. I guess you’re right though. I’ve never had anyone compliment me before. I really appreciate it. I guess it is a good thing that I speak well, and that I have all my teeth, and that my hall isn’t falling out… [Pete reaches down on the ground, and picks up a loose strand of hair that fell out of the hooker’s head. Tom grabs it quickly.] Tom Lexian: [looks at the hair] Uhm, it’s mine…damn receding hairline…[throws the hair away] Hooker: [slowly getting back to her feet] Well, thanks for helping me out guys. By the way, I didn’t catch your names. I guess I was too busy hitting on you guys and trying to get you in the sack, huh? [laughs] Tom Lexian: [laughs] Well, my name’s Tom Lexian. This is my friend, erm, partner, erm…well, he’s Pete Rose. [The hooker suddenly gasps.] Hooker: You’re…you’re….you’re… Pete Rose: [suddenly steps closer proudly] That’s right, miss. I’m Pete… Hooker: [suddenly hugging Tom] You’re Legend Inc.! The wrestler! You wrestle for the PCW! Oh my god, I’m your biggest fan! Pete Rose: What the fuck? What’s wrong with you lady? You don’t watch baseball? Hooker: [laughing at Pete’s comment, still hugging Tom] Hah! Baseball is for wieners who can’t defend themselves. What happens when a hitter gets beaned with a pitch? He cries about it and runs out to the mound, pretending to be a tough guy. What happens? He gets held up by his teammates. What kind of shit is that? Pete Rose: I have to be a wrestler to get laid? Dammit. I’m in the wrong profession. Hooker: [staring up at Tom] I absolutely love you. I was watching the program and saw you almost pull that win on Turmoil and win a shot at the Continental title. Too bad the Authority had to break that up. But you were still awesome. It was great to see the good guys win for a change! Tom Lexian: It sure was! [Tom and the hooker exchange high fives.] Pete Rose: [rolls his eyes] Okay, can we get going Tom? We do have other things to do besides sit here on our ass trying to impress the local prostitute population… Hooker: Where are you guys going? Can I come? Tom Lexian: I’m sorry, you can’t come. We’re on a dangerous mission, and we’re seeking the help of R.L. Stine. If you come, I can’t promise that you’ll live through it. I couldn’t ask you to make that sacrifice. Hooker: [skeptical] R.L. Stine? He’s a living, breathing psycho. You can’t meet with him… Tom Lexian: We don’t have a choice. We have to go… Hooker: [grabs Tom’s arm] Just promise me one thing…promise me that you will win the Continental title at Gold and Glory….it’ll mean so much more to me if you promise me… Tom Lexian: [firmly grips the hooker by the shoulders] I promise you…I will win that title for all the good people and fans in PCW. There’s nothing anyone else can do about it. And you have really nice shoulders… Hooker: [taking off her shirt] Thank you…now, there’s something I want to show you… [The hooker takes off her shirt, only to reveal another one underneath. It is an Xavier Kannon t-shirt. Tom is shocked, Pete’s confused, and a few, dirty old men peeked over to get a free show. They were disappointed, obviously.] Hooker: Haha! Did you really think that I was a fan of yours? Haha! I hate you, you little good doer! I love Kannon, and come June 30th, Kannon will get his title back and destroy you! Haha! Now that’s OBSCENE! [The hooker takes off running, but runs into a light pole, knocking herself unconscious.] Tom Lexian: [shrugs] That’s an Xavier Kannon fan, for you. Shall we go?
