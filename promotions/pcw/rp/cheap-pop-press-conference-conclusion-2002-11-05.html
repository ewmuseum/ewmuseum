Cheap Pop Press Conference (Conclusion)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 - 11 - 02 Prologue: After an entertaining episode of cheap pops and verbal swipes by the current Tag Team Champions Pranks With Attitude, Natalie Miyagawa, now the Conniving Collectives valet has been asked to attend a Press Conference alongside the duo. The charade is basically a publicity stunt by Primetime Championship Wrestling in a bid too hold the biggest entertainment spectacle London has ever witnessed and the Main Event scheduled will certainly showcase such a boast. With a Pay-Per-View looming, alliances forming and a major loss of interest in the Tag Team division, CEO Xavier Kannon has masterminded possibly the greatest match of this year and in the eye's of P.W.A quite possibly the greatest successful title defence in the company's history. With eight teams participating, three referee's on hand and the Championships suspended fifteen feet above the ring, the event will undoubtedly live-up to it's 'Show Stopper' proclamation. From veteran Duke Williams and his Front Office colleagues to the two time PCW Tag Team Champions P.W.A. Add their standing nemesis’s Playaz Inc. who themselves have Front Office issues, mix thoroughly with deserving members of the roster in Darkstar, The End, Skitzo and you have the recipe for ratings. Standing aimlessly and open to the elements, Gaz Green and his business associate Natalie Miyagawa wait for their carriage to arrive, in this case a black limousine that pulls into view. 'Talk about showing up in style, it's usually Nerezza's purple hearse but I can live with travelling in something a little less key like this company limousine. Kannon must really want my title if he's sparring no expense, I'll polish it up enough to capture his ugly mug then show it up close and personal-....' proclaimed the Velvet Prankster. 'By wrapping it around his head-....' smirked the petite Russian. The car however has pulled alongside the path and the driver stands with the door ajar. Subtly ushering the duo into the vehicle, Gaz Green isn't eager to set this episode rolling and stands chirping on at his colleague Natalie Miyagawa. 'How did you know I was going to say that?' asked the star struck Jester, a little surprised at Natalie's response. 'Well Gaz, you use Extra Sensory Perception and I have Women's Intuition!' roared the valet with a dose of 'Girl Power'. The devious duo enters into the back of the long stretching automobile, the door closing under a heavy thud and seconds later jerking forward into motion. Driving steadily down the street, turning and heading through its only exit, Gaz Green emerged from the sunroof, bottle at hand and bellowing as the scene momentarily closed. 'I'M THE KING OF THE WORLD~!' Opening in the car park of Fusion's venue, the limousine stood at a halt and unmanned meaning that both parties in Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Natalie Miyagawa had already made their way to the conference room. Capturing the green tailored Jester and his personal valet waltzing into a large room buzzing with activity the place became bathed in flickering light as the media photographed the duo. 'So what are we publicising again-... An animal activist demonstration, or was it a debate on fossil fuels, or maybe-...' choked the Lord Of Laughter, overdosing on his own exposure, saved only by the composed Ms. Miyagawa. 'Keep it simple - TALK ABOUT WRESTLING!' 'Wrestling? Yeah I can do that' murmured the Championship adorning Gaz Green under his breath unbeknownst to the surrounding onlookers. Climbing a flight of stairs that lead up to a podium, the duo sit behind a table kitted with sound equipment and a draping tablecloth that hides the spider web of wires. Everybody dropped into their respected seats and the murmurs finally silence allowing Gaz to take centre stage and make his statement. 'Arr-... Err-... Well-... As you know Friday Night Fusion is being held here in London. Well there's going to be a number of matches taking place during the course of the night, which aren't worth mentioning in because they include a bunch of people who we regard in the profession as, hmm-... Jobbers!' Smiling and taking the bull by the horns Gaz looked confident, his jobber comment drew a few cold-hearted stares and a notable nudge from Natalie. 'Anyway the Main Event features P.W.A so that alone is worth the entrance fee. The match is an eight-team affair with the titles suspended fifteen feet above the ring and ladders have been made prohibited so God knows how we are going to manage this-... Maybe we'll hire circus acrobats from Russia or retrieving spider monkeys from ebay?!?' continued the PCW comic looking for help from the crowd of listeners. Suddenly an arm rises followed by its person and Gaz acknowledges with a nod, allowing the individual to speak. 'Clark Kent, Daily Planet - Mr. Green, what ambitions do you and your partner have for your PCW careers?' 'Whoa-... Well we want to end Politically Incorrect’s longest title reign and hopefully beat the holders record, currently held by Blood Brothers and The Westsiders-...' answered Gaz Green before an untimely interruption by the reporter Clark Kent. 'Da Westsidaz you mean?!?' 'Arr-... No. Do I look like a graduate of the Evan Levine School Of Dyslexia? It's God and not Gawd, or bitch, not biatch, I mean what the hell is holla back youngin?' roared the Emerald Menace with a look of concern as a mass of shrugging shoulders filtered the room. Peering around the room another arm rises. 'Ahem-... Peter Parker, Daily Bugle. What do you think of The End's new look?' asked the reporter as he tampered with his camera, that loosely swung from his neck. 'The latex cat suit, well it's kinder cool. However what as that got to do with this week’s Main Event? The guys blind, you could dress him up as a French maid and he wouldn't know-...' retorted the dumbfounded Jester as Mr. Parker blurted yet another comment. 'YOU PREDJUDICE BASTA-....' 'Sit down. Anybody else have a question?' questioned the inquisitive Prince Of Pranks acknowledging another raised arm, this time a black fellow, brandishing a microphone. 'Chad Brown brother representing 'Black T.V' tell me Gazzy - Is Statuz Quo indeed dyslexic or has he joined a religious cult like Rastafarianism?' 'That's the best question yet and in all honesty I haven't got a clue, so finally one more question, I'm a little strapped for time?' huffed the Tag Champion looking around. Standing to attention a middle-aged gentleman with white hair and cobias amounts of jewellery with a question of his own, this time aimed towards Natalie Miyagawa who's cringed her way through this charade. 'Yeah. Peter Stringfellow, reporting for The Sport - Well being almost a pornographic newspaper we would like to take a few topless shots of Natalie to give you a little publicity for Friday?' 'YOU WHAT?!?' hissed the Pranks With Attitude valet, astonished. 'Well Natalie it is for the good of the team, a few snap shots and-....' whispered Gaz 'The Jester' Green before being cut-off by the annoyed Russian. 'YOU CAN GO TO HELL!!! Now Gaz, how about doing what Alex couldn't and stand-up for your manager, valet or whatever you wish to call me' snarled the separated wife of Alexander Miyagawa. With a look of rage, Gaz Green turned to the assailant Peter Stringfellow, fists clenched tightly and almost oozing of fury; he retorted the previous comment with authority. 'Oh! Jimmy Saville I'll be seeing you out in the parking lot-.... I'm going to suggest you bring some identification because after the beating I'm going to give you, not even your dental records will help!!!' 'YOU RECKON?' cried Peter Stringfellow, held firmly by arena security, restrained with no-where to go. Ushered from the room by another flock of security, a task force even Gaz was forced into the corridor still a little upset by the proposition mentioned earlier. Cooling down, regaining his flagging composure, Gaz was tapped on the shoulder, only to turn and gaze upon that of Peter Stringfellow full of smiles and stretching out his hand in a gesture of goodwill. 'Nice show kid - I was hired by Xavier Kannon to add a little spice to this press conference. Give the event this Friday some real publicity, taking advantage of the gullible media. Front page in the morning on every tabloid, not bad for what may have been a boring afternoon!' said the jovial Mr. Stringfellow, shaking the hand of what maybe the Main Events victor. 'Arr-... I guess so-... thanks I think-...' stuttered the former NTICW employee as the masquerading hired help vanished down the corridor and out of sight. The scene finally closed on the confused Gaz 'The Jester' Green his expression simply dumbfounded by the events that transpired minutes earlier.
