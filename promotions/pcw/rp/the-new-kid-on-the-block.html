The new kid on the block

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/24-10-02 11.30am The sun is shining in Amber Hill for the first time in the last few days. The grass is a luscious green due to the water and healthy atmosphere of Autumn. Bob and Rosanna have both just been to see Sammy one of the captains of the Vurdo family. His leg is pretty bad and for the next month he will be getting around in a wheelchair. Bob and Rosanna both walk down the steps of the Amber Hill Hospital and look out onto the street which is very busy. Rosanna is a very beautiful woman she has dark brown hair that hangs just below her neck. She has natural Italian olive skin and a great body. Bob and Rosanna step out onto the main street and take a look around. Bob has planned to show Rosanna around his town Amber Hill. Bob and Rosanna begin walking east when they are interrupted by a voice from behind them. 'YYYYYUUUUUMMMMMMYYYYYY!!!' Bob and Rosanna turn around to see a black man licking his lips and nodding his head at Rosanna. Rosanna turns away disgusted as he whistles. Rosanna begins walking down the street but Bob doesn't follow. 'Who the fuck are you?!' Bob narrows his eyes and looks down at the black man in a threatening style. The black man appears to back down as he sees this big threatening man above him talking in such an intimidating voice. The black man looks like he is about to apologies when two other black men that appear to be his friends join him. The man then puts on a tough image. 'Im R-Dawg you fucking idiot, I am the man that owns this town.' Bobs eyes widen as he hears this and rage begins taking him over. Bob feels Amber Hill is the Vurdo families town and always has been. 'Do you know who I am?' Bob is now fuming and the man can see this he reaches into the pocket of his pants and smirks at Bob. His other two friends step up toward Bob but Bob doesn't budge. 'I don't give a fuck who you are you stupid fucking Bonzalli bitch. I should kill you right now for being such a smart ass.' The rage starts to take Bob over, he reaches into his jacket and puts his hand on his gun when Rosanna pulls him away. 'Ignore them Robert I don't want this it was meant to be a fun day.' Bob goes with Rosanna but doesn't turn his back he continues staring coldly at the three black men dressed in their homie gear. Bob has marked them out now and he will get revenge. --------------------------***1pm***------------------------- !!!Bring Bring!!! !!!Bring Bring!!! Bob pulls the cell phone out of his pocket and answers. He is sitting at a table in a park in Amber Hill eating lunch with Rosanna. Bob picks up the phone and stands up before walking away. 'Hello,' Bob greets the person on the other end in an annoyed tone as he was eating lunch. 'Yeah Bob its Tony.... I got a new recruit for us.' 'OK who is he?' 'Ron Jackson I think he'll be a perfect brute.' Tony seems fairly excited about this man and obviously thinks he could do big things for the Vurdo family. 'Good work Tony.... Look I got to go and finish lunch with Rosanna.' 'OK I want to meet that girl of yours. Sammy said she was very nice.... Maybe we could set something up for tomorrow? You can meet Ron and I can meet Rosanna. It would also be good to have a welcoming for Sammy when he gets out.' 'Sure that would be good. Call me later.' With the press of a button Bob ends the conversation and makes his way back over to the table where he sits back down with Rosanna. 'What was all that about?' You can tell Rosanna is angry with Bob by the tone of voice. 'It was a business call.' Bob doesn't know why Rosanna seemed frustrated with him. She scrunches up her forehead for a second before replying. 'Why did you need to go over there?' The looks on Rosannas face shows she thinks there is something going on. 'As I said it was a business call.' Bob has put on an innocent yet confused tone. Does she really know what the Mafia is like? Does she really understand the business? Bob is asking himself these questions now as the two continue eating their chips. He thinks about the time when he told her in Trappani.... She didn't even think about it much she just accepted it without even thinking otherwise. This could be a problem in the relationship if she finds out the wrong way. --------------------------***Same time different place***--------------------------------- We then see two familiar faces being Tony and the black man Bob was about to kill. Tony and the black man are both smoking cigars as they sit at the table. The black man is wearing long dark blue track pants and a lose hooded track suit with a black shirt underneath. He is rolling a joint as he talks to Tony. Who is looking sharp in his black tuxedo. They are both sitting at a table inside a room which is fairly empty apart from the table. 'Your a made man Ron if you complete just a few hits you will climb the ladder fast.' Ron is the black man Bob was fighting with. This situation is set to explode when Bob finds out he is the new recruit. Ron nods his head and smiles. 'You have the right attitude I think you are what we needed.... You also now your business don't you?' The Vurdo family has never had a black man in their ranks but they have never had anything against them. 'Yeah man I know a few people we could use to make some more money around here.' Ron takes one last long puff of his cigar before putting it down in the oversized ash tray which sits on the table. There is a pause as Ron puts the joint in his mouth. 'Some fucking bitch tried to start me today. I set him straight and he ran away like a fucking pussy.... Stupid fucking Mediterranean cunt asked who I was.' Tony seems to be quite offended by the last comment but Ron doesn't seem to notice. 'Everyone in this town should know who I am. I am the man that has made the impact around here in the last week.' Tony looks intrigued as to how he has made such an impact then it clicks. 'You where the man that killed those fucking Mexican bastards?' Ron grins and nods his head in a proud fashion. 'You get to meet the boss tomorrow.... If you make a good impression and you tell him about your ideas then this could be a big thing.' Ron nods his head and takes a drag of the joint.
