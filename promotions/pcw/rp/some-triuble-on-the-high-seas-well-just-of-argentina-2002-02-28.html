Some Triuble On The High Seas....Well, Just Of Argentina....

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/2/02 It’s happening again. Just as with Shang-Haied his route to the PPV is becoming complicated. After dining out at his Hotel’s buffet, he ate some dodgy Prawns and in the hallucinations which followed he took a rather long trip to the South Pole, aided by his valet Eleanor Hall who would have stopped it except she didn’t want to block his creative flow, thinking a trip to the seclusion of Antarctica could be good for him ahead of his first shot at the World Title. So now, with only 3 days to go until he needs to be in St Louis for the Main Event Battle Royal where he will enter at number 15 to fight alongside his Asylum Stablemates Miyagawa, Darkstar and Bobby Stormbringer, hoping to walk out with the PCW World Title over his shoulder. So far he has a lot of ground to make up, both in distance and preparation time, but with a valet such as Ellie~! how can he fail? Hmmm.....He’s in trouble. We open up on a small fishing boat somewhere approaching the coast of Argentina. On bard, standing at the bow are the 1st couple of Scientology Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall, watching the small spot in the distance that is the continent of South America get closer and closer. Eleanor : “So how much money do we have left now?” Eleanor is wearing the same outfit as when we last saw her in the Antarctic snow. She wears a Brown sheepskin coat with faux fur collar and hood trim with a White rollneck jumper underneath it and Blue, flared, sandblasted jeans. He long wavy Brown hair, highlighted with Blond is un-tied and blows all around in the sea breeze. Xavier : “Well, after the $900 it took for the Captain to take us this far....About $200.....We just have to hope that some Argentinean places take credit card!” Kannon is still wearing a long Brown leather coat. Under it he has a Black v-neck jumper with a Grey round neck t-shirt under it and a pair of his usual Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg. His hair, like Ellie~!’s is loose and blowing all around and he is starting to get stubble from his time without shaving, which teamed with the wild hair gives him much more rugged appearance than usual. Xavier : “At least I think it’s $200......I’ll check...” He takes out his Black and Purple leather wallet and opens up the main compartment to take his notes out but as he does so a massive gust of wind blows across the boat, sending all but a $10 bill clutched in his right hand blowing over the side and won into the water. Xavier : “.......Why didn’t you tell me that was going to happen?” The Scientologist looks to Eleanor who is giggling at the sight of Xavier holding his emptied wallet and single note. Eleanor : “I didn’t want to stem your creative influences...You must learn from your own mistakes!” Xavier tucks the $10 back into his wallet carefully and tucks it safely into his jeans’ side pocket, looking overboard to where his money is floating on the surface before taking his coat off and pulling his sweater up over his head. Xavier : “I can get it back....The sea doesn’t look THAT cold and it’s floating on the surface!” He the tosses his t-shirt aside and looks overboard again to his money before his eyes widen and he goes White in horror. Xavier : “My money! The Fish are taking my money!” In the water fish are coming to the surface and prodding the notes, breaking the surface tension causing the notes to get sodden and start to sink. Xavier : “That Fish right there owes me $20! I never forget a face, I will claim that back, I’m a Scientologist and I know all about hoarding cash away!” Eleanor walks over to where he is standing with his head in his hands watching his money sink. Ellie~! points down to the fish which are jumping out of the water. Eleanor : “I think they are mocking you...” She speaks slowly and turns to Kannon, pointing down at the sea with her finger and eyes. Xavier : “They are not mocking.....They are running scared...” The Gold Patron Meritorious then stops talking and just looks to something on the horizon, fear actually crossing his face as Ellie~! just studies him rather than what he is looking at. Eleanor : “Huh? What?..........What?” She then turns and looks to where Xavi’s eyes are looking and her jaw then drops as she leans forward, not believing what she is seeing. Eleanor : “Whoa......You’re kidding?......Cool!” Kannon turns to her with a look of pure surprise, wondering what is wrong with his preppy valet. Xavier : “How is THAT cool!” He points off to where they are looking, obviously not as pleased with whatever it is as Eleanor seems to be. Eleanor : “I dunno?” She shrugs and turns back to whatever it is. Eleanor : “It just seems kinda cool!” The camera pans around and follows their eyeline towards the horizon where the one emblem any captain dreads to see looms large, the symbol of danger and the warning of coming violence.....The Jolly Roger, the Skull and Crossbones....The mark of the Pirate. Xavier : “Believe me....We are a whole continent from where we want to be, we just lost nearly all our money to a shoal of Fish, this has to be the least secure ship ever.....And there are Pirates closing in on us.....Tell me how that is Cool?” Eleanor : “Um.....One of them might have a wooden leg or something.....Oh and a parrot...Parrots are cool, they talk and everything!” Xavier sighs and back away from the side of the boat and shouting to one of the ship’s crew while Ellie~! keeps looking out to the boat, a curious grin on her face. Xavier : “Um....Pablo?....Pancho? Sorry to interrupt you but.....Pirates!” The man looks him up an down strangely, wary of the stranger on board causing trouble. He glances at Kannon before getting back to his job and whistling his work song. Xavier : “Hello? Senor? Pirates!” Again the man look sup from his work, annoyed at the interruption before putting his head back down. Eleanor sees what is going on and traipses over with her hands on her hips and gets the man’s attention by clearing his throat. Eleanor : “Um...Hello! Slowey Gonzalez....Can you get the Captain....Um, El Capitano....Someone more important and a bit cleverer than you?” The man is still staring blankly. Man : “Que?” Eleanor : “JUST LOOK OVER THERE WILL YOU!” The man shrugs and rolls his eyes before stroking his beard and looking over to where Ellie~! is pointing before going White in terror and yelling. Man : “EL PIARATE! EL PIARATE! CEREVANTES! EL PIARATE!” On his call the entire crew are thrown into mass panic and throw down whatever they are doing and run around aimlessly for a few seconds before all converging near the life boat and dropping it down into the sea and leaping down after it. Eleanor : “Oh, wow, a little boat....let’s get on that one!” They go to follow but members of the crew yell and threaten them so they back off, Ellie~! looking none too impressed at their treatment as paying passengers. Eleanor : “FINE! We didn’t want to get on your stupid little boat anyway!” Xavier : “Um....Yes we did!” All the crew are now in the water surrounding the boat and crawling up onto it as it starts to run away, then Xavier sees that one of the men is holding a $20 bill in his hand, bribing the boat to let him on despite the cramped conditions. Xavier : “Hey! That’s my money! Give it back.........I might need that to buy the Pirates off!.......Fine, I hope there are sharks in the sea and the sea and they all eat you and then leap up onto this ship and burping out my 20 like in that book the Christians read!” The 1st couple of Scientology then watch the boat row off into the distance as the Pirate’s ship pulls up alongside them and the mostly bunch on board scowl at them, waiting to come aboard. Xavier : “I’m sure they’ll be reasonable people, not at all like the stereotypes you hear about....This is the 21st century after all so it won’t be all Shiver me Timbers or Walk the Plank!” Ellie~! looks almost upset at this. Eleanor : “Oh......I like all that, there will be a parrot though won’t there? I like Parrots!” As she finishes 5 or so Ropes with Grappling hooks are thrown across onto their ship and the Pirates swing over onto the ship, one takes control straight away and orders the others to search the ship while he looks over the 2 remaining people on the ship.....Xavier and Ellie~! Eleanor : “Excuse me......I hope you don’t mind me asking, but do you have a Parrot?” Xavier elbows her in the ribs as he spies the old fashioned pistol on the Pirate’s belt. Eleanor : “Ow.....What?” The Pirate clears his throat to regain their attention. Pirate : “No, I don’t have a parrot and neither do I work for Blue beard and I do not know Long John Silver and neither have I ever been to Treasure Island and followed a map I got from Ben Gun. Eleanor : “I liked Muppet Treasure Island.....That was fun-” The Pirate glares at her and she quietens down. 2 men on the Pirate ship then hastily erect a walk way between the 2 ships and a tall man in a Black cloak with Wooden leg clunking against the plank and he hobbles across, his long Black hair blowing in the wind. Both Xavier and Ellie~! lean in opposite direction to see around the Pirate in front of them and get a good view of them Captain, eye patch and all, approaching them. Pirate : “Captain on deck!” The others stand to attention and watch their leader walk onto the boat and past his 1st mate, standing directly in front of Kannon and Eleanor. Captain : “Shiver me Timbers! Pieces of Eight! Rum and Booty!” Xavier sighs and shakes his head, looking on disappointed at the anti-climax. Xavier : “Oh come.......On! Can’t you do better than that? I mean how long have you been flogging those things around? For ages! You need new material, you have ditched the parrot but you really need to ditch the catch phrases!” Eleanor : “And grow a new leg!” Kannon goes to speak then turns to Ellie~! and they exchange a quick “What?” and “Huh?” with their eyes in the way they have perfected. Xavier : “So what so original line is next? Walk the Plank?” The Captain lets out a hearty laugh which is mimicked by his 1st mate and within a few seconds the entire ship is laughing deeply at Xavier’s line. Ellie~! turns to Xavier, confused. Eleanor : “What did you say?” Captain : “WALK THE PLANK!” Eleanor : “Ah, that was i-.....HUH?” The Pirates rally to their Captain’s call and prod Xavier and Ellie~! with their swords, herding the couple towards a makeshift Plank which they step out onto and are prodded out to the end. Eleanor : “Wow......Walking the Plank is, like, SO 1794!” With one final thrust of a Cutlass the couple leap down into the ocean and hold onto each other to stay afloat while looking fore something else to hold onto. Xavier reaches out and grabs a piece of Driftwood, pulling it over o them to aid their buoyancy as the 2 boats start to sail away and the shot fades out, leaving them floating off the Argentinean coast. Xavier : “Whoa....One of my 20’s! The sound of Ellie~! holding Xavier’s head under water is the last thing we hear as we hit Black.
