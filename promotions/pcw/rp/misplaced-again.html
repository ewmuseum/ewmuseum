Title: Misplaced AGAIN?! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 23rd 'Again?!' Politically Incorrect are shocked. Suburban Uprising is gone. Fear Factory is gone. And yet somehow, Misplaced stays around. It's like the little brother that always hangs around you that you hate. You just can't get rid of him. And, seeing that Personnel Director Tim Walden has forgotten to fire Misplaced in his recent tag team purge, it appears the responsibility has fallen to Politically Incorrect to eliminate this problem. It's obvious that Stephen Cairns and Carey. J. Nowski have to go. And, Politically Incorrect's intentions are clear.. Like Chris Chase, Frisco, Hank, and Ganker.. Misplaced shall be Executed in the Executioner's Chair. And then, with any luck, they'll either have their necks broken, or Walden will fire them. Typical way The Ultimate Authority handles things.. And on that note...why are the Ultimate Authority fighting Tuesday? William S. Powell: Man.....Fear Factory's gone too. Reginald V. Lovecraft: They actually had talent at least.....this is such an injustice. William S. Powell: With tag teams like Misplaced around, why the hell would Walden fire Fear Factory? I mean, I appreciate the effort..but now we have to fight Misplaced! This blows goats! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah. Now we're down to us, the Cowabungas, wherever the hell they are, Heartbreakers, and the soon to be deceased Misplaced. William S. Powell: Oh, and S&M is back. Reginald V. Lovecraft: I never knew girls beating the crap out of guys while wearing skimpy outfits went out of style! William S. Powell: No, not the sex term. S&M. Syko and Miyagawa. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Don't worry....Miya will be gone after he loses to Boda, so we'll be down to just S, for suck, which Syko does. William S. Powell: Yeah....the vaunted PCW Tag Team division certainly seems to have been kicked firmly in the scrotum. Not that I'm complaining, mind you. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Every day we hold these belts cements us as the best ever. Better than Golden Game. Better than The Divinity. Better than the Blood Brothers! BETTER THAN DA WESTSIDAZ! ::Both men smile, looking around the club they are in. It's rather empty due to the daytime period, yet both men sip Screwdrivers and happily go on.:: William S. Powell: Our domination in the tag team division is established. Although....Tuesday, we have quite the unusual tag team encounter. It will be Mark Makonie and Xavier Kannon...vs. Tyler Nelson and Tim Walden vs. Jordan Shabazz and Duke Williams....vs. us! VERSUS POLITICALLY INCORRECT?! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I don't know what the bookers were thinking....in essence...it's all of the owners of Primetime Championship Wrestling in one colossal matchup. And, as much as I don't like having to do this...every so often...it is good to prove yourself against your equals. And, while Jordan and Williams do not pose much of a challenge, it is clear the remainder of The Ultimate Authority does. William S. Powell: Facing our comrades is not the greatest mission we could have..but it will help us cement our rising legacy. By defeating our own brethern, we show we are the best. While Tyler, Walden, and Kannon may be superior singles wrestlers, we are the masters of tag team wrestling. And, sadly, our colleagues do not have the skill in that arena to battle Politically Incorrect! ::Both men sigh, recling back and sipping their drinks. Both seem tired with the way things are going, and quite bored to boot.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I grow tired of this. Come William! William S. Powell: Yes.... Reginald V. Lovecraft: Friday shall be Election Day! William S. Powell: We always triumph. I know, let's leave. ::Fade out.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
