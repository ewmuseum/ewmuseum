You Lost That Shilling Felling, Oh, That Shilling Feeling, You Lost That Shilling Feeling & It's Gone, Gone, Gone...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/02/02 So the challenge has been laid down, at Tuesday Turmoil when Xavier fights Chris Chase it will be for more than just the PCW Cruiserweight Title, the belt Xavier lost to the New Breed member at Shang-Haied, it will also be for the Snapple~! shilling job, the duty of getting PCW’s official soft drink over to it’s audience. The day before Shang-Haied, Alan Kreigman, facing his retirement, handing on Snapple~! to wrestling’s only Scientologist, then, with what he thought would be a long GTT path ahead of him, Xavier passed it on to Ellie~! While Xavier, most likely due to the highly enlightened state of his mind due to his Scientology beliefs, remained un-changed during his tenure as Snapple~! shiller, but when it was passed on to Eleanor however, a change was noted. It seemed that not only Snapple~! can with the package, but the essence of Alan Kreigman, the Evil Super Genius seemed to have infected Ellie~! as she set about creating all kind of futuristic devices and contraptions that would have never been associated with her before. Then at Sabotage events took another turn when Snapple~! was driven, literally, out of Canada by New Breed member Chris Chase to took exception to some of the Scientologist’s remarks about winning back Cruiserweight Gold, how will she cope with not Snapple~! to shill in what could be her last days as spokesperson for if Xavier wins back his Title...He takes back his shilling duties. “Must shill Snapple~!....Need to shill.....I miss the joy of the shill....” mutters a voice, sounding infuriatingly familiar but a name cannot be put to it, “All I want to do is shill.....Shill the Snapple~!.....The Snapple~! was my friend....Must have one....One last shill....That’s all I need>“ The camera now fades in to show the corner of Ellie~! and Xavier’s hotel room in Calgary, the site of Sabotage, where the PCW Cruiserweight Champion Chris Chase deported Snapple~! from his native Canada, leaving a distraught Ellie~! with nothing to shill. “He took my Snapple~!....He put it in lorries.....He drove it back to America....Now I have nothing to shill....” she says, in a more strung out voice than normal, losing her happy yet whiny preppy voice to the depression of her situation. “They keep saying I can use another soda, that I should try to shill Mountain Dew or Sobe, but it is just a cheap stop-gap until I can get back on the hard stuff...” Ellie~! is not looking her usual self. Instead of one of her wide range of designer tops she I dressed in a baggy Grey woolly jumper, her hands not even emerging from the sleeves. Instead of her usual denim she is wearing a pair of Black jogging bottoms with a pair of Black house socks. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights looks a mess, only loosely tied back with one Black tie, the rest going haywire and the first ever evidence of a split end is present. The Preps pale Blue eyes are bloodshot and dark bags are viable under them and where she holds her head in her arms it shows that she is without her usual array of bracelets and bands. “I’ve scoured the streets, talked to the lowest of the low, the people who lurk in the shadows...” Ellie~! continues, hanging her head so that her face is lost under the loose locks of her wavy long hair, “But they have nothing for me....All the shopkeepers can offer is Pepsi or Cola....I need the Snapple~! I need it’s sugar flowing through my veins, I need it to pop the taste buds of my tongue with it’s wide range of fruit flavours such as Strawberry Lime, Pink Lemonade and Snapple~! Apple....” He head remains hanging, wallowing in her self pity as she has to face her Snapple~! withdrawal cold turkey, “Well Chris Chase...” he begins again in a seething, harsh, whispery voice, “I hope you are proud of what you have done....You have taken away what is precious to me just like you did to Xavier.” Ellie~! pulls the hair back from her face to join that tied behind her head, showing her panda eyes, her pale Blue iris’ being the only things that throwback to the Ellie~! of old...Or yesterday. “At that thing we went to in China....That begun with S...You punched him in the nuts and took his Cruiserweight Title, and now you came out yesterday and you took my Snapple~! away! Do you have any idea how many bottles you can fit in one of those trucks Chris? Enough to keep me shilling for months.....” she sighs, “But alas, as they should have sung in Top Gun....I lost that shilling feeling. The sound of the door opening is heard followed by Xavier’s voice. “Ellie~! could you make yourself look respectable? We have to leave for the airport in 2 hours!”. Xavier is for once without his usual full length leather coat and is instead wearing a Black Asylum t-shirt with Purple lettering over a charcoal Grey log sleeve t-shirt. With this ensemble he has a pair of Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and intricate purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to where a pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers are worn on his feet. In contrast to Ellie~! his long, Auburn hair is neatly tied back with a Purple tie and his piercing Green eyes are the focal point of his face, unlike the tied looking ones on Eleanor. “That’s just typical of you Xavier, you don’t feel my pain!” she moans, not making eye contract with him. “Don’t you care that Snapple~! is gone....Doesn’t it matter to you that trucks full of PCW’s official soft drink drove over the border last night....DIDN’T YOU LOVE Snapple~!?”. The Gold Patron Meritorious looks down to his valet, raising his eyebrows at her reaction to Chris Chase’s actions. “Ellie~! We can find you some more Snapple~!, it’s not like he was able to take every bottle of Snapple~! there was and have it taken out of the country!” he argues as she still stares at the floor, “So get ready will you, this match at Turmoil is important for me, I want my Title back.” He waits patiently for his valet to get up but she remains slouched in the corner. “Look, once we get out of Calgary there will be shilling to do, there will be billboards to look at and commercials on TV to watch. Soft Drink capitalism will flow through your veins again....” still the Scientologist’s words have no effect on his valet, “Okay....When we get back to America then you can shill all the Snapple~! you like...” Ellie~! looks up to him through her bloodshot eyes, “Well I won’t be able to will I?” she states to him venomously, “Because if you win your belt back you also take back your Snapple~! shilling deal and leave me with NOTHING....Only my own self pity to wallow in for the rest of my miserable Snapple~! free existence!” The Scientologist looks down at his broken valet with his piercing Green eyes and gives in. “Okay Ellie~!.....It’s yours.....You need it more than I do, and you seem to love it more than I do, therefore Snapple~! is yours forever....” he says passively, “Now, ill you get ready to fly to Turmoil with me?” At the reassurance that she has her Snapple~! shilling back, the preppy valet springs into life. “Yay me! I will, like, SO be ready in 10 minutes!” she says chirpilly, “Then we will get to Turmoil and totally win your belt back, and I’m going to find a bottle of Snapple~! and I’m going to shove it right in Chase’s face and be, like all Hey Chris! I got a bottle of Snapple~! so there! Oh, and by the way, your hair look greasy! That’ll teach him a lesson for screwing with the Queen of Prep!” As she speaks she moves behind the screen in the corner adjacent to the one she was slouched in and her jumper is slung over, followed by the pair of Black jogging bottoms. “When you think about it, I don’t even need Snapple~! to shill it, it like totally transcends it’s physical state, Snapple~! is all around us, it’s in out hearts and minds...” she philosophises as Xavier just looks confused. “Yeah....” he says cautiously, “But you do realise it still is a liquid don’t you? Something you drink?” Ellie~! then emerges from behind the screen wearing a vest top that has Red sequins on the front and Grey cotton on the back and a pair if dark Green cargo pants. her hair has been un-tied and brushed, regaining most of it’s original style and volume. “There, I SO look better,” she boasts, striking a little pose, “I won’t let Chris Chase defeat me, just as you won’t....We will both defeat him for us, Snapple~! and that skinny guy Title!” she rejoices. “Um....It’s Cruiserweight...” Kannon corrects, “Not skinny guy...” Ellie~! gives him a quick glance with her pale Blue eyes, now minus the bags, “Okay...” she says, none to pleased about having her momentum broken, “For Cruiserweights everywhere! You will become a 2 time Cruiserweight Champion and I will become a 2 time Snapple~! shiller....Yeah! He, like, isn’t going to kick your ass like those other times!” Kannon looks across to his valet and sighs at her naive words, seeing that she is still yet to regain all her Super Genius powers that come with her Snapple~! shilling. “Well thank you for your kind words of encouragement Eleanor, and don’t worry, this time Scientology will triumph over arroga-....Sorry, Christianity. Asylum with triumph over the New Breed, a stable so bad Timo was willing to take a beating to get out of it!” boasts Xavier, “I am the person that made the Title worth anything....If I hadn’t driven Juan Borro through the mat with an X-Bomb on New Year’s Day then the Cruiserweight Title would mean nothing...I put the work in, I built it up....Then Chase did the heist and disgraced it....Since he won that Title at Shang-Haied, how many times has he put it on the line? Once....I have been the number one contender for 3 weeks, and now he can run no longer, and I can cash in.” Ellie just nods, muttering “What-EVER!” under her voice as she grabs her suitcases and palms them off to Xavier. “Right, so you fight, you win, I have Snapple~! I shill.....I like this arrangement.” she says grinning, “Did you know that shilling is the national currency of some European countries?” she then asks him as he replies with a curious look. “Yes....The Shilling is...” he corrects. Ellie~! looks puzzled at the thought of this. “I wonder how that works?” the prep ponders, “You know....shilling as a currency. It must be a complete reversal, the guy who is selling it has to listen, and the one who wants to buy it has to shill it....How weird must that be?” she exclaims rhetorically, “Wow, when we go to Europe I’ll get free Snapple~! I am the shilling master, I’m a millionaire in shillings! I could buy the entire shop with my powers of the shill....Oooh, maybe you could move the match to Lithuania or something and I can buy the Cruiserweight Title for you?” she suggests. “I’ll keep that in mind...” he answers, “But I know I can beat Chase this time....The 1st time he brought an Iron bar to the ring with him because he knew that he was out of his depth....The second time it needed a virtual all-out Stable war to provide enough distractions for him to cheat and win....So what will he pull this time? Get himself disqualified? Get himself counted out? Have the New Breed assault me? Or will ‘The Messiah’ walk straight into an X-Bomb and allow me to walk into the Madness Battle Royal as the PCW Cruiserweight Champion.....Just like I walked into Shang-Haied....Chase, you are 2-0 up at the moment, but after your shoulders get counted down tomorrow and your feet touch the outside on Sunday....I’ll be even...” These are last words he speaks as he takes Ellie~!’s heavy cases and struggles through the door with them as she follows, pausing to sneer “He’ll.........Be.......Even!” before following the Scientologist out the door and slamming it behind her.
