Empire Saga™ Part Eight: A Thorn in the Side…

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/8/02 Chapter Two: A Thorn in the Side. [John Crichton] It had been almost an hour since John Crichton had sent his two henchmen, George and Reginald, to tie up all of his ‘loose ends’. He had discovered through various sources that one of his employees had got himself caught by the police performing an unauthorised robbery. Crichton couldn’t allow the police to get anywhere near him or his criminal organisation…so he had to ‘deal’ with it. After all it was this cretin’s own fault…he was the one acting against the companies rules…he was the one who got his buddy killed and himself arrested. Crichton was forced to break him out of prison and after a brief talk with him in the office, he decided that Barry would be tempted to rat him out. This was totally unacceptable…so it had to be dealt with. As far as he knew it was being dealt with…but it had never taken George and Reginald this long to disregard the unwanted trash…Crichton decided not to worry about it too much, he figured that they probably stopped off on the way home for some fast food. Very typical of those two, bumbling, clowns, sometimes he didn’t know why he employed them…but the answer was obvious…loyalty. They were too stupid to betray him and too scared to attempt a coupe de tat. He decided that it wasn’t worth worrying about…those two could handle themselves just fine…his thoughts shifted from his criminal organisation to his semi-pseudo wrestling career. After his defeat against the PCW Extreme champion Jack Violence he realised that more time had to be diverted from the running of this criminal organisation and transferred back to his wrestling career. Why did he still bother with his wrestling career? He often asked himself the very same question. He was backed by this criminal organisation, which was more than meeting his financial needs. His wrestling career however, helped him keep his sanity in his world of madness…giving a realistic element to his existence. Another reason why stuck with his wrestling career was the simple fact that he had been trained to wrestle by the father of the woman he loved. She was totally behind him all of the way…a small part of him felt that he owed her that much…even with the evil in control he still felt deeply for her… His next match was a tag match which would see him team up with his former friend and colleague Andy Murray…when he first came to PCW he was involved with stable called ‘The Forgotten Ones’…from then on he had more or less was involved Murray…he had tagged with Murray before and they were both a part of ‘The Political Machine’…recently they had no contact with each other…Crichton’s other ties had taken up too much time… Now…well now he wasn’t sure what was going on…Could he trust Andy Murray now?…The point was debatable…after all Murray had just quit the ‘The Political Machine’…But what did this mean?…probably nothing, maybe everything…one thing was for certain…he wouldn’t turn his back on him for a second. Davis Fury… The darkness of the night almost compared to the darkness he was currently involved in…through his source at the Chicago Police force he found the location of John Crichton’s ‘warehouse’…a rather poor attempt at a criminal front for his liking, but this was the place nevertheless. Fury decided that he was going to go in there, guns blazing, like John Wayne…this was purely a scouting mission…he came here hoping to find a weakness…a weakness of any kind that could potentially give Davis the upper hand. However, what he had witness here this day did nothing more than reinforce his initial fears…John Crichton is and would be a very powerful foe…Fury knelt on the concrete floor behind the warehouse, just in front of a window…the window led into the office of the potentially infamous John Crichton… By the looks of things he was giving one of his lackeys an ear bashing…Crichton looked extremely animated, which was inevitably emphasised as he placed his massive hand around the throat of the lackey, lifting him swiftly in the air without any exertion whatsoever…he then throws him violently into the more than capable arms of his two henchmen. Fury couldn’t hear what was being said within the room, but it was quite clear by the way the Henchmen were dragging the lackey out of the room, that this lackey had been sentenced to death…for what reason he wasn’t entirely sure, but he figured that this could be the opportunity he was searching for…Fury slowly rises from his kneeling position and stealthily makes his way to follow the two henchmen. Dan Lyons… He had been a spy within the ranks of John Crichton’s criminal organisation since he took over…no body question him being there…the employees that worked for the company before Crichton’s arrival assumed that he came with John Crichton…after all he had confronted John Crichton on numerous occasions….Lyons always came out second best, but this time he assured himself that he would succeed…Crtichton had finally shown the world what he was really like, so now if Lyons was victorious he would become a national hero. Crichton didn’t recognise him because the only time Dan Lyons had been in his presence was during a mass meeting…Lyons had made sure of this…Crichton would instantly of recognised him…Any opportunity Lyons could get he would listen into conversations and generally viewed all of the ‘business’ transactions he could…after all it was his only hope of taking Crichton and upon extension this organisation of his down… And so he found himself standing directly outside of John Crichton’s office, within the walls of the business front entitled ‘Pastelli’s Warehouse’…a couple of the general factory workers had informed him that something big was occurring and Lyons thought that he’d better check it out…so far he had managed to catch the majority of the conversation… This Barry fellow had got caught operating without the direct authority of Crichton and so he had to be punished…this was the exact thing Lyons was looking for…an opportunity to take Crichton and this organisation down…if he could ‘arrange’ for Barry to ‘fall’ back into police custody, it would essentially shake up the entire company, leaving Crichton vulnerable to attack… Lyons heard Crichton’s explicit order for his henchmen to ‘take care’ of Barry…he couldn’t let Barry be killed by the henchmen…his mind was made up…he had to prevent this tragedy from occurring it was his only hope… To Be Continued…
