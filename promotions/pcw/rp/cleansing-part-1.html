Cleansing (part 1)

Date: 01 - 01 - 03 (New Years Day) 'Tell me why we're going to this dive again?' said the sweet and innocent voice that belonged to the Soviet Scarlet Natalia Miyagawa. The next caper involving Primetime Championship Wrestling’s only ever prankster Gaz 'The Jester' Green opened as he conversed with his valet, hand in hand, both waltz along the sidewalk happily chatting. Dank buildings litter either side of the road, the street lighting flickers and grim passer-by's merely snarled at the upmarket couple that have dressed to impress. 'We're sending another message to our old friend, Nerezza!' retorted the Emerald Menace as his mouth arced into that of a cheeky smile, his eyebrows curling inwards, creating a devious facial expression. The two celebrities continued to blend in with the natives of this pitiful backdrop as they passed through hoards of people who dressed somewhat different to themselves. Whilst the Corporate Jester idly strolled by in his velvet business suit accompanied by an equally impressive Natalia Miyagawa who adorned a glittery yet tight fitting all in one evening dress, those surrounding wore leather jackets and continuous black coverings. 'This doesn't look your scene Gaz!' informed the separated wife of former PCW performer Alexander Miyagawa. The recently acquired Front Office member merely offered another cheap smile as those flocking around him hissed and snarled, obviously the Jester was in a part of town that he isn’t to familiar with yet as far as we know it could be somewhere in the heart of his native Las Vegas Nevada. This scene could be anywhere but with Gaz Green playing lead then something will surely materialise and with a murmur of an out-going message to Nerezza then that something may indeed be devious and under-handed. With a buzz of activity gathering outside what appeared to be a nightclub, the Conniving Collective flooded over to investigate. Two man mountains stood motionless outside in leather trench coats, ushering the guests in through the dwellings entrance, above the doors a neon sign glared with '6th Gate'. Joining a lengthy queue the dynamic duo waited as the line continued to snake behind them and eventually after several minutes of mindless waiting the people before them vanished into the buildings darkness. 'SHOW TIME~!' roared the Corporate Jester as he waltz by the bouncers when suddenly from behind Gaz Green found himself yanked violently back, turning the two behemoths towered over with intimidating stances. 'Where do you think your going PIP SQUEEK?!?' hissed a bald headed bouncer as he sprayed his saliva over the Front Office Jester. 'YEAH PIP SQUEEK WHERE DO YA THINK YOU'SE BE GOING?!?!' concluded his partner like an echo, a real mastermind. 'I'm going to P-A-R-T-Y squire-....' replied the PCW comedian with a cheeky smile, no signs of intimidation and his sarcasm not sitting well with the hired help who fortify the '6th Gate' with their shear size. 'IS THAT A FACT, PIP SQUEEK!!!' croaked the bald headed security guard once more, cracking his knuckles and rotating his head with some bone crushing creaks that make Natalia flinch. IS THAT A FACT-....' said the other bouncer when suddenly Gaz Green locked on his infamous arm-bar to the squeals of his victim. With howling yelps, the second bouncer stepped forward to dislodge the Jester when with some timely thinking Natalia bitch slapped him, but not any female slap, with the second bouncer having spasms on the ground, wriggling in pain, the Soviet Scarlet unveiled a hand buzzer. 'THE MOST ELECTRIFYING MOVE IN PRIMETIME~!' With the two giants incapacitated courtesy of the Front Office collective, the devious duo left their handiwork at the door and further embarked on their mission, which is still shroud in darkness. Like a knife, both Natalia and Gaz cut through the density of the smoke, which had been generated by a machine and into a large open space with various exits lit up on neon signs. Entering the bowels of the beast it appeared as though this place, the '6th Gate' is indeed a nightclub, coloured lighting dancing off the walls and high tempo dance tracks bellowing over the speaker system. 'LETS MINGLE BABE!!!' cried the Lord Of Laughter as he stepped onto the dance floor. With the continuous music blaring, the partygoers jigged and bopped away mindlessly, faces dripping with sweat and various sexual acts taking place, the whole scene looked like a Gothic swingers party. Bouncing about carefree it wasn't long before a few wayward women advanced over to the Jester who was strutting his stuff to the giggles of Natalia who fended off the approach of several men. Getting somewhat jiggy on the dance floor, the heat began to take its toll and without warning the sprinkler system hit, spraying red liquid over the crowd who react quite jovial. MEANWHILE-.... As the party were dosed in liquid, Gaz Green vanished through a fire exit with a hot chick who could quite easily catch her death with her minimum choice in clothing, I mean there was more fabric on a pair of gloves, fingerless gloves that is. Pushed up against a wall with some force, Gaz Green retaliated with a sarcastic comment, unbeknownst at the events that were to follow, or was he? 'You like to play rough, huh?' Accompanying his comment with an array of giggling the female bellowed alongside, then with a cringed facial expression done a complete u-turn as she glared hard at the Jester with dagger-like eyes. If stares could kill then Gaz would be as cold as turkey, yet the Front Office Entertainment Manager looked on unaffected, tapping his foot with impatience, almost foreseeing the inevitable. With a twist in her head the female suddenly sprouted a pair of vicious canine teeth that drooled with saliva, her eyes glazed over with red fury and an evil glint, her complexion somewhat pale and appearing as a creature of the night. An incredible sight but not for Gaz Green who had befriended such a being, it was apparent that this girl was indeed a vampire and like a wolf she lunged forward to feed. 'AAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHH~!' 'TAKE THIS BITCH!!!' screamed another, yet distinctive female voice from behind and as the vampire turned she found her back impaled with a wooden object. However no death scene followed as the vampire girl turned to her assassin who was unveiled as the blood soaked Natalia Miyagawa who now stares terrified as the creature goes to finish off her meal of Gaz Green, knowing full well that she will be left as the main course. Suddenly the creature of darkness vaporised into thin air, firstly pouring into a mist of smoke, her skin sizzled before being engulfed into a glowing red light and her cremated bones falling to a heap before their feet. 'HUH?!?' murmured Natalia trying to fathom the events that have unfolded. 'But-... But-... But how Gaz?' concluded the Russian valet to her client. Standing motionless, holding his arm a few feet from his valets beautiful face he twitched his finger and from within his jacket sleeve protruded a wooden stake set on a hydraulic device giving the weapon a pump-action effect. Almost frightening his assistant to death, the dynamic duo may have taken out one of these bloodsuckers but the '6th Gate' was actually ridden in them like a nest so to speak and if that could be deemed an obstacle so can the group of weapon wielding thugs standing behind them. 'OH CRAP-....' gulped the Prince Of Pranks gazing over to the danger. 'So tell me, do you have another bright idea?!?' questioned the inquisitive valet who cowered behind her client who's retort was to peer over to the rage-fuelled group of thugs with a half hearted attempt at a smile. The scene closed as the group ushered forward, weapons at hand and gaining on the PCW stars with every step under took, eventually as the seconds came and went the image slowly turned to black ending this caper on a cliff-hanger finale. - To be continued -
