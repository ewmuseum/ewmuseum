Visiting a friend.

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/2002 **The scene opens as a motorcycle is seen driving, at almost double the speed limit, down a lonely stretch of highway, in the middle of a forest. The sun shining in between the trees, almost making the darken areas come alive. As we get closer to the motorcycle, we see that it is one man in particular, driving alone, up the highway. Boda, riding a brand new Harley Davidson Fatboy, zooms past as the sounds of him shifting gears is the only sound we hear. Boda rides like a pro, wearing nothing in the form of protection, other than sunglasses.** [The wind blowing through my hair at damn near 100 miles an hour on a motorcycle is quite a rush. It gets the blood flowing, and the juices secreting. Flying up State Road 19 through the Ocala National Forest at damn near 100 miles an hour, a straight shot from Eustis to Palatka is almost orgasmic. Along the way, turning on 42 and taking it into Ocala, again, a straight shot where little traffic flows, and I can let the throttle of my bike wide open, is just as orgasmic. The only thing that can give me a better feeling is that of outlasting 19 others in a Royal Rumble setting and becoming the undisputed champion of the world. That, in itself is earth shattering. This is a trip I’ve taken countless times before, and every time, it gets better. Many times, I’ve enjoyed the scenery of this route, on my way to Ocala, to visit, and train with my friend, my mentor. Today, is no different.] **Boda drop the bike into a lower gear, as he is coming up onto a change in the speed limit. Another 10 minutes, and he will be in the presence of his mentor. Slowing down and pulling the bike up to the nearing stop sign, Boda thinks again about the upcoming match at March Madness** [Sunday, I get to finally prove to every one of my fans, my family, and most importantly, myself, that I am indeed the biggest star, biggest talent in PCW. For too long, I’ve been laid aside, and looked over. I made Boda the household name it is, on my own. Not because of Ivan, not because of anyone, but MYSELF. I’ve been cheated out of what everyone wants to see..... Boda as the PCW World Champion. Well, no more. I can not be cheated out of it this time. It is an impossibility. 20 men, all vying for the same thing. To toss each other over the top rope, and be the last one standing. And, who has the better chance of being the last one standing than I. The biggest man in PCW. The atmosphere of the match, is right down my alley. A brawl, an all out fight to survive. And survive, I will] **Chris looks up the road, and down the other direction, watching for coming traffic. Seeing he has the all clear, he steps on the throttle, and rockets the Harley Fatboy from a dead stop, and rooster tailing the back end as he crosses one intersection, and heads for the 42/441 intersection in the city of Ocala.** [All I’ve heard is ‘Boda is the one to watch at the PPV.’ ‘It’s Boda’s time to shine.’ ‘Boda has everything to prove this time around.’ But, the one thing Boda has heard more than a few times, is.... ‘The main event, the Rumble is Boda’s to lose.’ To me, that means I have to not slack, I have to stay focused. If I don’t, if I let myself slip up.... it’s over. The title will once again slip between my fingers, and fall into the hands of some one else. Some one not worthy, some one not capable of carrying a flame to the torch that is Chris Sloboda. But, dammit, I can’t let that happen. I won’t let that happen. Not this time. Not again. I’ve worked to hard, to long to get this, and by God.... nothing will hold me back from it. Mona tried, and failed. Miserably she failed. All her feeble minded attempt to keep me out of the PPV did was make me stronger, mentally. Something, that I actually must thank her for. Thanks Mona... you BITCH.] **Boda’s motorcycle winds down as he approaches the 42/441 intersection. Only a few miles to go, on the journey that Boda hopes will get him focused on the PPV. Chris hammers the throttle, and pulls out onto 441 traffic, which in Ocala is an adventure all upon itself. Swerving in between the cars and tractor trailers, Boda flies down 441, towards his final destination** [What a surprise it will be for Dory to see me. It’s been almost a year. How the time flies by. Last time I ran into Dory, I was in the Red Army, and he didn’t approve of my turning my back on America. But, a lot has happened since, and even Marti, Dory’s loving wife, writes me, and visit with Stacey and the girls when she can. I’m just hoping that coming back to the Funkin Conservatory, the Dojo, will help me gain that little bit more I need, that next level, I seek to win that World Title. Dory is and always will be that father figure that I lacked growing up, and now, I want to, and will, make him proud.] **There it is. The place that Boda learned the sport at. The home to Boda, along with others, that wish to make it big in wrestling. The Dory Funk Dojo, or as it is known to others, the Funkin Conservatory. Inside the hollowed walls, years of blood, sweat and tears have been spilled, all by those wanting to learn to grapple, step over toe holds and even submission moves, from the Legend himself. Inside the building, is where the career of Chris Sloboda began, and it’s here, that Boda wishes to regain the focus he needs to win the title at March Madness. Chris parks his Harley Fatboy in a parking spot reserved for The Legend, Dory Funk, and steps off it. He walks to the front window, and peers in, watching what goes on inside.** [This is like home. After losing my NBA career, I turned to Dory and Marti, and basically let them mold me into the monster, the pro-wrestling star I am today. And now, I return, to watch a new breed of up and coming want a be wrestlers. And maybe learn from them, remember what I might have forgotten along the way to my upcoming title match. Can the words of wisdom, instilled on me three years ago by Dory still stand the test of time, and help me with my goals? Can this visit to my mentor help over come the thought of the other 19 in the match, and focus on the one important factor... ME!!] **Chris walks in the font door. The lobby decorated in wrestling memorabilia of days gone by as well as of the present and future of the sport. Chris walks into the main hall of the lobby, and the unforgettable sound of screaming and agony can be heard. Chris chuckles, as he remembers his first few days here at the Dojo. The memories of the pain he went through, to get to where he is now. The distant screams, all the same that Boda experienced. Chris walks towards the back of the little hallway, and as he does, the screams get louder. At the end of the hallway, Chris looks through the little window on the door. There, he sees a small group of about 10 students, learning the ropes. Chris walks in the door, and watches. The students are being taught about hitting the ropes, and learning that they indeed do hurt, if hit wrong. Chris watches as Dory gets in the ring, and starts to talk to the students. The scene fades out as Boda starts to cheer and shout for Dory.** ‘Whoo, all right. Dory, you’re the best, Old Man.’ shouts Boda. **to be continued**
