Date: December 19, 2002 [Randy Raggagu, professional skateboarder and one crazy mofo. God blessed him with great skateboarding talent but seemed to have forgotten the man's looks. His mole covered face, unibrow, and nappy black hair puts any chance of getting a woman out of the door. His gangly, thin build only adds fuel to the fire when it comes to his looks. Even though he has one of the highest ollies in professional skateboarding, he's gone absolutely unnoticed. Tony Hawk, Bob Burnquist, Rodney Mullen...they all take his spotlight. The only reason that Randy EVER got noticed by anyone was because of one man, and one man alone...'Totally Tubular' Tommy. Since they dabbled in extreme sports and lived in Los Angeles, it was only a matter of time before these dudes crossed paths. Tommy was full of life and inspired all those around him. Next thing Randy knows is that he's going to the most premiere parties and hanging out with famous people. Happiness engulfed his tattered soul and began to open up Randy's heart that maybe there is hope, that maybe everything is going to be just fine. Then came the ridiculing. That word. That HORRIBLE WORD!!! RAGAMUFFIN!!! Tommy uttered the word at a huge beach party and EVERY person laughed until it hurt. Ragamuffin, Ragamuffin, Ragamuffin. Over and over again that damned surfer screamed it. Tommy's stupid sidekick, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, only knows how to follow. So, it was only out of following that Harry began his Ragamuffin onslaught. Then it was Kelly Slater, Tony Hawk, Bob Burnquist, and Lisa Burnardi ripping into him with that HORRIBLE name. Lisa...his true love, the only woman to have stolen his heart. He truly loved her and Tommy, that rat bastard, turned her against him. She called him Ragamuffin the most, she even forgot his first name. 'It's Randy, hone' he would say. 'Whatever, Ragamuffin' would be her reply. Crushed, broken, and alone. All his happiness and all he had built was totally destroyed with one HORRIBLE word. He was a man on the edge, with no hope, and with the dark void of loneliness rotting his soul. He tried to make Tommy's life a living hell, but he only added further to his own insanity. The ugly skateboarder sits in a California mental institution to get well. After the kidnapping of 'Heavy Metal' Harry he was placed here to get some help, the best help the state of California had to offer. It is, but Randy doesn't want to be helped. He doesn't want to be in that state of happiness ever again. That only thing Randy Ragamuffi...err...Raggagu wants is to watch 'Totally Tubular' Tommy rot in HELL!] Truck driver: Where are you going to, stranger? The only thing that is visible are the white's of the man's eyes] Skinny man: I'm going to Los Angeles. [The overweight, middle aged truck driver smiles, showing off his rotting teeth. Saddle up...you can check out December's playmate...SHE'S FREAKIN' HOT! [The semi roars, slipping from first gear to second gear with excellent handling. The man in the Independent cap picks up a Playboy magazine and begins flipping through it, looking at the tantalizing sights, but they don't interest him anymore. There is only one woman that takes up his perfect mind, and that's Lisa. Beautiful creature. No condoms, no birth control, just a hot night of lovin' and then on to the next broad. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy somehow fits in with these rejected locals. It's to where children are emulating what they see and hear, causing violent acts to other children. Parents have become worried about their children, and even with ratings systems they do not trust anything. TTT: You see, Har, this is why people like this are created by the bodacious God. So, every now and then a totally righteous dude like me has to give back to them. Harry: Yeah, man...there must be another guy here to have given me this free beer. Tommy, Harry...from Bonnie Ballstein, still waiting in Apartment 27, be careful boys. [Fade to black]
