He says die I say hoss!!

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/8/02 -The Malice man walks down the streets of New Orleans in a long black trench coat. His personal adviser former WWF super star Randy Savage walks along side him.- Williams: So wait a minute? He tried to kill Billy Shatner? Savage: Dats what I’ve been saying yeaaa. Williams: Thee Caption Kirk? Savage: Oh yea and rescue 911!! My favorite yeaaaaaaaaa!! Williams: Oh now its personal. Savage: Hold your rage till the end of the sentence yea! It gets worse. Williams: How can it? Savage: He cut off Shatner singing Covey. Williams: The CW McCall song? Savage: Ohhhhh yeaaaaaaaaaaaaa! HOSS HOSS HOSS Williams: His ass is going down!! This is beyond personal it’s…it’s….to the next level! Savage: Not done yet…yea. He then cut off the preacher from singing Johnny Cash “I’ve been every where man”. Williams: My god man that’s an American recordings favorite. HOSS HOSS Williams: If Clyde thinks he can mess with American institutions like Shatner and Convoy and get away with it. He'’ got another thing coming…. HOSS HOSS Williams: Yea exactly. Where the hell is that coming from? Savage: Am I a detective or an adviser? Yea. I don’t know. Williams: Its not as bad as the random die’s from the other guy. HOSS HOSS Savage: What in the name of mint jelly are you jawing about? Williams: In Clyde’s first promo back it kept saying….die….die….die….die….it gave me a damn headache. Savage: Its ring rust yea!! Williams: I’d love to cut his neck with a rusty ring from a soup can. Savage: That can be arranged umm-humm! Williams: So I have one guy who thinks he’s a poet and another guy who thinks he’s Neal Diamond. What a f*cking joke. Savage: And lets not forget your teaming with Mr. Rogers! Oh noooooo. Williams: Yea fucking Howdy Doody himself. HOSS HOSS HOSS Williams: Its not easy being the only normal one in the whole damn thing. HOSS Williams: What the fuck is that? -Both men turn around and find a homeless man hiding behind a garbage can- Williams: Can I help you man? Homeless guy: Hoss….hoss….hoss…..can you spare a dime? -The Malice man hands the bum a five spot and walks away.- Williams: Thank God I couldn’t take that Cylde style babble any longer. Savage: Dat young kid who hangs out with Freddy Kruger is not happy. Williams: Oh man a little trouble in paradise. Savage: She had to drag him out for a good time ummm-humm. Williams: Well by god I’d give her a good time with having to be dragged anywhere. Just some nipple clamps and my Malice stick would do. Oh and maybe that bamboo stick. OH YEA!! -The Malice Man gyrates his hips.- Savage: So are things cool between you and Mr. Rogers? Williams: I think Tommy and I can put aside are problems to battle these two spawns of Jerry Garcia’s waste. Savage: What? Williams: Ain’t that Austin’s line? Anyway I was reading I think in the readers digest that some samples of Jerry Garcia’s dung was stolden and some of them there medical doctors who work with jeans. Savage: Like Levi’s yeaaaa? Williams: No like medical genes. So anyway they created two humans. But since they used dung in the cloning process they produced Darkstar and Clyde. Savage: Oh YEA!!! I read that too I think it was the National Enquirer. Williams: Yea that was it. Savage: I dig that paper!! YEA!! Williams: Yea so anyway what did he end up singing. Savage: A song about you….yea. Williams: Really? I’m flattered. I think he’s into that group sex and wants me to join him and his lady friend. You can sing all you want you burnt pile of vomit but the Malice Man don’t swing that way. But truly thank you for the kind song.. Your like Cylde Dylan!! ‘ Savage: That’s what I was thinking yea. Williams: Yea know its sucks you can only get this New Orleans pot down in New Orleans. Savage: Yea but its da best. Williams: I hope so. So this Tuesday its time to party. Clydesdale and Darkstar versus the guy who fired them!!!! HA HA HA HA and I do it again hoss!! Give them the pink slip and a box. -The two men arive in front of a old building.- Williams this Turmoil sh*t will hit the fan as the Tommy and Duke destory Nelson’s plastic army of desperation and send them…… Savage: WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND OOOOOOOOHHHHHHH YEAAAAAAAAAAA!!! Williams: That kids is the honky tonk truth. -The camera fades on Duke’s flying “w” logo.- =w=
