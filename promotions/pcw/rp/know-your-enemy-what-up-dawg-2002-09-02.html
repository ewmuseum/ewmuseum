Know Your Enemy: WHAT UP DAWG!?

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd September [“Subterranean Living” by Crowd Of One plays gently in the background, as the scene opens up a suburban neighbourhood. A beautiful, sunlit street at midday approximately. As we pan down the street, we see families at war, children in gardens without a care, and even the odd pensioner throw in for good measure. The street is particularly quiet, and there are very few cars out and about. The houses are pretty much all identical – small and white. The homes are all single floored, and don’t even look remotely desiRossle. Some would be forgiven that we are in “The Hood”. As we pan along the street, we see two very familiar figures walking down the pavement, they are of course, everyone’s favourite Neds, Ross Cruickshank and Andy Murray. As usually, are Nedly heroes seem to be pre-occupied with something or other, and both are wearing identical white Nike tracksuits with black stripes, hair supplemented with 2 scoops of hair gel.] Ross: Haw! Where we gawn min? Andy Murray: We gunna go find some ‘o they ghetto people, ken? Ross: Ghetto person? Andy Murray: Aye, ye know … Like they chubes Ah’m facin’ on Fusion min? [Ross pauses for a second, but keeps on walking, trying to remember who exactly Andy is facing on Fusion. He scratches his gel-smothered head, before coming to a sudden realisation, as a light bulb appears above his head.] Ross: Oh aye, Status Quo an’ Michael Jackson? Andy Murray: Naw man, Status Quo an’ that Dante dobber. Ross: Aww … How can Status Quop wrestle anyhow? Aint they like, some crappy 80’s band or somethin’? Andy Murray: Aye min, I think they are … How come they’re in PCW min? It’s pure pish. [The Neds continue to walk along down the street, for a few moments, there is no conversation between the two.] Andy Murray: This is pure weird min, I wis told that they bawbags live aroun’ here or sumthin’. Ross: How does Status Quo live in a pissy wee hoose min? Are they no big rock stars? Andy Murray: I dinna ken min … [The two continue to walk along, jabbering to each other, as we cut to the front porch of one of the houses. The door is wide open, however one of those horrible, cheap, tacky bead veil things hangs down from the frame, preventing us from viewing inside the house. The wind gently rouses the beads, they hit together softly, causing a slightly relaxing sound. On the step of the porch, sits a young man, early twenties, wearing ‘Gangsta’ style clothes, stupidly tied headkerchief and all. The man is not African American, but is white. A wigger. The young chap sits, puffing away on a homemade cigarette. One can only guess what is encased in within its paper.] Wigger: Oh yeah! That’s tha sh!t right there! [He removes the ‘spliff’ from his mouth, and puffs the smoke from his mouth, chuckling to himself with a big goofy grin across his face. Suddenly, a shaggy dog walks by, and lies down beside the wigger, immediately falling asleep. The wigger notices the dog, and greets it at first with a look of surprise, which slowly merges into a look of happiness.] Wigger: Yo! What up DAWG!? [The dog sleeps on, not even twitching as the wigger takes another puff, before looking back down at the dog.] Wigger: Yo’ aint the talkative type then dawg? [Again, the dog sleeps on.] Wigger: Damn muthafucka, at least say somethin’! [Nothing.] Wigger: F^ck you! [As the wigger takes another puff from his cigarette, two figures begin to walk up the small, straight path that cuts between his dying lawn and up to the porch. Yup, it’s Andy & Ross. The Neds stop before the Wigger, looking down on him. As the wigger finishes his puff, he quickly notices the expensive Nike trainers on the feet of Andy and Ross. He quickly jumps up, a hostile look across his face.] Wigger: Yo whitey! Drop them Nike’s off yo’ ass befo’ I BLAST you MUTHAFUCKA! [Andy and Ross both look confused, before turning to look at each other, and laughing. The Wigger stares at both men, a confused look of discomfort across his face. Clearly, he is not used to people laughing at his attempted intimidations.] Wigger: What yo’ laughin’ at!? Andy Murray: Haw! Ye cannae speak proper min! Ross: Aye! Go get yersel’ a language teacher or sumthin;, cus yer’ a bawbag! [Andy and Ross control their laughter, as the wigger tries to figure out what the hell they just said.] Wigger: What you say crackah? Andy Murray: Are ye givin’ me cheek ya wee bampot? Cus Ah’ll make ye shit yer breeks we man, awlright? Wigger: Huh? Ross: Aye, and Ah’ll pure chib a brick aff yer noggin pal’. [Now it is the Wigger’s turn to look confused.] Wigger: You’se a couple of f^cked up crackahs! Andy Murray: Crackah? Wha? Like, Jacobs Cream Crackers min? Wigger: … Huh? [Andy and Ross share a chuckle.] Andy Murray: Anyway … Are ye Status Quo? Or Michael Jackson? Wigger: What you talk’n ‘bout!? Andy Murray: Y’know, Player Sink … PCW … [The Wigger scratches is head, before realising what Andy speaks of.] Wigger: Oh … Y’mean Statuz Quo an’ Dante Jackson? Playaz Inc? Ross: Whatever they called … Wigger: Yea, I know those playas! What of it dawg? Andy Murray: Tell them they’re pure pish min. Wigger: Pure piss min? Ross: Aye … An’ … Errr … LOOK! A WOG! [Ross points something out, the Wigger turns to face where he is pointing, while his back is turned, Andy and Ross quickly flee the scene. The Wigger suddenly realises that there is nothing there.] Wigger: Hey yo’ cra- [Before he can finish, he realise that the Neds have gone.] Wigger: Damn mothafuckas! Fin.
