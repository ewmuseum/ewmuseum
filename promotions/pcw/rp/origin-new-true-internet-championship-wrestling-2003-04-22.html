Origin - New True Internet Championship Wrestling

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd April 2003 The quickest root is unquestionably a straight line but in the career of the Jester, the Emerald Menace has had no such luxury. If anything, a Spaghetti Junction as the Las Vegas native has had to jump ship from a number of different promotions for a number of different reasons. During his tenure with the now defunct Dark Wrestling Alliance the man in question was fortunate to leave his mark on the company in being it’s only ever established Double Gold Champion courtesy of two important triumphs in both his Hardcore and Light Heavyweight opportunities. Under the alias of New Blood a gimmick that has since been committed to the ground the fledgling wrestling student managed to etch his name in stone but past accomplishments alongside the achiever remain buried for the name New Blood had been wiped. New Blood had been wiped away and the clean slate before him would only offered to his successor. Now under the guise of the Jester the comedic prankster would learn and apply his trade within the Wrestle Online flock in a bid to gain recognition amongst the elite of the industry. With four major entities haggling over talent in arguably a bidding frenzy then it’s simply a question of time before somebody snatches an individual before the competition. Assumption? Many a superstar has fallen at the first hurdle because they ‘ASSUMED’ too much and they naivety shows those watching their amateur traits. One could argue that the basis of Pranks With Attitude was based solely on assumption and why not? The duo formed under the influence that their concept would be highly looked upon by Old School Wrestling and the backing of their mentor Hessian would only substantiate that further but their name warranted nothing more than a mention on Malice. However Old School Wrestling’s loss would be another promotions gain and finally after several months under the microscope the moment to seize had arrived as the Phantom Booker publicly welcomed the duo to the newly resurrected New True Internet Championship Wrestling (NTICW). New True Internet Championship Wrestling A company that once flourished with talent only to be later frowned upon by those very same people who used it to serve the purpose of recognition. Once a name associated with greats like Xavier Kannon and Hessian, now nothing more than a memory too most and a bitter taste to those who endeavoured its final demise. Indeed the promotion that fed Old School Wrestling its United States Champion in Hessian and Primetime Championship Wrestling their World Heavyweight Champion Semore Smoke would choke. Maybe if the future could’ve been foretold then Pranks With Attitude may have opted to miss their NTICW era but such a luxury exists only in science fiction novels and the promise of talent flooding through its door only camouflaged what would become a doomed venture. Long gone was the cemented name of Dave Yoder and his successor a man only known as GOD but under his authority stood two able bodies in Mick Palmer and Alex Mann who had the necessary traits and credentials to make New True Internet Championship Wrestling a serious rating threat rather than the community joke. Everything stood in place firmly, two renowned individuals at the helm, overlooked by the head authority figure, much needed hype in preparation for their first televised event entitled ‘Brawl For It All’ and a mounting roster that adorned names like, Mordred Marcus O’Neil Teresa Tomas Jamie Celt And of course the dynamic duo of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green and Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, collectively known as Pranks With Attitude. In fairness the debut event of Brawl For It All was a major success and quenched the initial doubt of many. Simply the contest was a free for all conducted under elimination rules, so therefore if somebody were to be pinned then they would be deemed eliminated and would play no further part in the contest. However weapons and cheating were permitted and the match would not be confined to the ring, literally rules evaporated and so did moral as Jamie Celt stood as the company’s first victor. His victory etching the name of Jamie Celt into stone as NTICW World Heavyweight Champion. Pranks with Attitude made a good showing and the unveiling of a team known as the Great Latinos instigated what may have turned out to be a feisty feud except it never materialised. Immediately the cruiserweight collective vanished after their first defeat at the hands of P.W.A and so did all those there after, names like, Team Discovery Channel and Nu Era. None ever made an impact within the community and simply vanished into obscurity, kinder like the prospect of having a New True Internet Championship Wrestling Tag Team Division. For a promotion that once homed countless greats, talent had seized up thus forcing management to make the best out of a bad situation by juggling what superstars they already had. So where did it all go wrong? The concept of making it Extreme and Hardcore orientated with absurd match ideas. It was that simple. Management dreamt up the idea of New True Internet Championship Wrestling being bloodthirsty and violent by pitting its personnel in stipulated matches and by doing so filed its own fate. Nobody other than a patient in Shady Acres Mental Asylum wanted to watch a Barbed Wire Ropes, C4 Explosive Turn Buckle and Flaming Table Match because it would never showcase technical or aerial ability but management was right and the masses were wrong. The expression, ‘The customer is always right!’ applied here but there was no detour and so the birth of Submarine Matches. A question haggling over in the Jester’s mind was, ‘Where is this company going?’ and as much as anyone would want to stay in the relegation fight, facts suggested that New True Internet Championship Wrestling was going nowhere and fast. Being a straight ‘A’ student it was time for the Emerald Menace to evaluate the situation and two questions needed addressing, ‘Do you stay aboard a sinking vessel?’ Or ‘Do you jump ship at the opportune time?’ The vision that was the stable ‘Uprising’ had long been quenched and the answer was handed on a plate from an unlikely source, the management. The duo’s frustration had seeped onto television and their attitude was negative to put it mildly and the intervention of weapons had the roster worried for their well-being. The door was slowly closing behind them for Pranks With Attitude and as the wooden frame creaked shut another had opened from an unlikely source, Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW). Third Dawning The Asylum The Third Age… Just random words to the average person but to a fanatical wrestling fan they refer to the source of Wrestle Online in Primetime Championship Wrestling, however when coupled together in reference towards Pranks With Attitude their meaning holds the key to the opening of Pandora’s box. - To be continued -
