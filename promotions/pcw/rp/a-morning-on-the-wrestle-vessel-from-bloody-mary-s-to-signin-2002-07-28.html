A morning on the wrestle vessel, from bloody mary's to signing breasts it's got it all!

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-28-02 The vessel is huge. The boat is absolutely beautiful. The boat has a pearl white paint scheme with blue trim on the side with the PCW logo on the side and a banner that reads: “PCW Wrestle Vessel, Sunday July 28th, 2002, Live on Pay-Per View!” I stand on the dock bags in hand looking at this vessel in awe of how big and enormous this is. There is fan fare every on the dock. Wrestling reporters are everywhere taking pictures of the boat. PCW officials are all over the place looking for wrestlers making shore that they are at the boat. PCW staff and talent begin boarding the boat. Scorpion works his way up the ramp up onto the boat. The crowd in the back is going crazy chanting PCW. I make my way up onto the boat talking to a PCW official trying to find out where I will be stationed for the weekend. PCW official: You’re in room 207 Jeff. Scorpion: Thanks. After the talent and PCW staff make there way onto the boat the security allows the ticket payers to make there way up onto the boat. I look back for a brief second and notice all the fans on the dock going crazy taking pictures and waving there arms off. I make inside a portion of the boat where a sign points to the downstairs area that reads: “PCW only.” I make my way down the stairs and down into the hallway where the talent for the PPV will be staying. I make my way into room 207 and put my bags down only to find an itinerary for my stay on the boat. This afternoon, autograph session. Later tonight have dinner with the fans. Right now make my way out to the boat for photo opt when the Wrestle Vessel sets sail. Ok let me see what time it is. (Looks at watch) It’s 9:20 now and it sets sail at 9:30 I better make my way up to the deck now. I make my way back up to the deck now where there is a jammed packed crowd on the deck filled with wrestlers, officials and fans. I make my way to an open space near to bow of the boat. Flashes from cameras and cheers from the crowd is the scenery of the boat ready to take off. Holy shit, this is crazy. The boat’s horn goes off signaling the sign of the boat getting ready to take off. The fans begin to cheer wildly. A couple PCW wrestlers make there way at the bow of the boat with a champagne bottle breaking it at the bow of the boat baptizing the new boat. The crowd continues to cheer as flash bulbs from cameras go off with the baptizing of the boat. The boat begins to take off into the beautiful ocean water. I continue to wave my arm off and as we peer off the dock area and onto the ocean. Man I need a drink. I make my way down stairs to the PCW area and I head over to the bar only for the PCW guys. Scorpion: I’ll have one Bloody Mary. Bartender: All right sir. Are you with the PCW? Scorpion: Yes I am, I’m part of talent. Bartender: All right sir then this is on the house. Scorpion: Thank you very much. Bartender: No problem. Wow what a nice guy to do that. I wonder if PCW gets the bill. Scorpion sits on the bar stool as the bartender makes him a Bloody Mary. The bar area is filled with PCW officials and talent all over the place. The bartender finishes up the Bloody Mary placing it directly in front of Scorpion. Bartender: There you go sir. Scorpion: Thanks. Dam this is good. Scorpion gets off the bar stool and heads back to his room with his drink. He gets into his room and notices a note from PCW officials. Jeff you need to be at the autograph signing at 10:30. Originally you were supposed to be they’re at 1:30 but someone had to cancel. Thanks a lot. Scorpion looks at his watch. It reads 10:29. Dam it. Since I got on this ship I haven’t had a chance just to relax. I guess it’s better to get it over with now then later. Scorpion puts his Bloody Mary on the table in his room and makes it up to deck and into the meeting room where fan fare is being held. There are booths all around the area selling merchandising, there is a PCW hall of fame area that people can walk into and look around at the many memorable moments in PCW history, there is also a area where you can call a match with PCW announcers Dave and Jeff. Scorpion makes his way to the autograph signing area. Fans notice Scorpion walking to the autograph table and begin clapping. Scorpion is then approached by the Pimply faced intern kid. Scorpion: What do you want Pimply faced intern kid? Pimply faced intern kid: You got an hour Scorpion to sign autographs, don’t say anything mean or get into any fights with the fans or you will be fined. Scorpion: Got it. Scorpion takes a seat down at the table and flashbulbs begin to go off as Scorpion waves to the crowd. The line is filled with a decent amount of people waiting to get their autograph from the new guy in PCW. Fans from all around the world are in line waiting for an autograph. There is a small amount of fans wearing old CCW shirt of Scorpion and some fans where the new shirt that Scorpion has in PCW. At least I can say I gave back to the fans and that I wasn’t a stuck up son of a bitch like most wrestlers that don’t even bothering signing autographs these days. Fans come up to Scorpion shaking his hand and taking a picture with him. Scorpion notices a rowdy group of fans near the middle of the line screaming and hollering at Scorpion. Scorpion just waves to the guys, which gets a decent pop from the crowd. They must be drunk, they are stumbling all over the place chanting “I can’t feel my legs.” Scorpion gets the attention of the Pimply faced intern kid. Scorpion: Pimply faced intern kid, watch those drunks in the middle of the line and make shore they don’t get out of hand. Pimply faced intern kid: Got it Scorpion. The line continues to move suddenly faster as Scorpion begins to sign the autographs fast the group coming closer to the table continues to get more rowdy now as they get closer to Scorpion. God they are drooped faced drunk. This is going to be fun. You always get these punks at the casinos thinking that they are invincible and can beat you up. The groups of the drunks make their way to the table now stumbling all over the place. Good the group only consists about three people. The first two drunks make there way fine without any problem. They only stumble all over the place. The last person in their group is not as good. He is talking to himself, tripping and laughing like crazy. Scorpion: Hey what’s up? Drunk: The sky. Haha. Scorpion: Yes it is. Drunk: You suck. Scorpion: All right that’s nice. Drunk: Your going to get your ass kicked on the pay-per-view. Scorpion: All right whatever you say. Drunk: Kiss my ass. Scorpion: I rather not, ok next? Drunk: I’m not done. Scorpion: I signed your paper it’s someone else’s turn. Drunk: Sign my breast. The drunk pulls up his shirt showing everyone his hairy beer belly and his man boobs. Scorpion: Get the hell out of here. Drunk: Sign my breast now! Scorpion: Get out of here now. Security begins to make there way at the table. Where the drunk is beginning to get very mad. Security: Is there a problem? Drunk: He won’t sign my breast! Scorpion: I am not signing anything on your body. I already signed your paper. Drunk: Sign it. Security: Sir come with us. Drunk: No not until I get my breast sign. Security: Looks like you have had a couple drunks today. Your not getting your breast signed no matter what. Drunk: I haven’t had any drinks today. Scorpion: Then you must be the dumbest shit walking on the face of this earth. Drunk: Say that again. Scorpion: You must be the dumbest shit walking on the face of this earth. Drunk: Let me at him. Security: No sir you’re coming with us. Drunk: No. This is ridiculous I hope they throw this guy off the boat. A huge Security team takes the drunken man away in handcuffs as he continues to scream for Scorpion to sign his breast. Scorpion continues to sign autographs. He finally reaches the end of the line and is escorted by security back to his room. Scorpion heads back into his room and he finishes his Bloody Mary. Sign his breast, yeah right.
