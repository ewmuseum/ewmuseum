BSing at Parallel HQ

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: BSing at Parallel HQ Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 10/31/02 The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is best known as a wrestler. Probably second-best known as a teacher. But, how many of you know he's also a rap artist? If not, why do you think his famous theme song, 'I'm Gonna Kick Yo' Ass' is by The HIT f. Geometry Clazz? Yes, The HIT raps. Is it pretty? Not especially. But hell, psychotic wrestling fans the world over have made that song, and 'Masturbation', his cover of the legendary Red Hot Chili Peppers' 'Californication', best-sellers. He is outside the office building containing his label, Parallel Base Records. Okay, so it's not the entire building.... OKAY! IT'S ONE OFFICE! BUT IT'S HIS OWN LABEL, DAMNIT! He is here today to oversee the initial marketing meeting of his first CD, 'Why The Fuck Are My Angles Congruent?'. He hopes for it to be a best seller. We have seen The HIT in PCW as Mathematics teacher and hardcore wrestler. Now, we shall see him as rap artist and businessman... Sitting around a giant trapezoidal table are The HIT, his executives, and the Geometry Clazz, his band and backup singers. They sit with the Trapezoid at the table, as the men get ready to discuss important matters. The HIT: Alright, everyone! We're going to release my new album, but first, I want to know how the marketing has gone! Executive: Well, in the 100 people we tested, 30 said the album was 'bitchin', yo', 25 said, 'I can't believe he cursed that much in 12 songs!', 20 more said 'This guy ought to be shot!', 15 belived that 'The HIT is my God!', and, the remaining 10 individuals stated 'Why the hell am I listening to this?'. The HIT: ......And, what exactly does all of that mean, holmes? Executive: Out of the 100 people tested, 75 demonstrated a favorable response. The HIT: WICKED! Executive: Now, this doesn't mean they'll all buy it.. The HIT: No no, I understand. Just got a lot on my mind. I've got to face two black men who are intent on kicking my ass, with the referee also being a black guy who wants to kick my ass. Executive: Ouch, that just has to blow! The HIT: Yes....and then, I team up with a woman named Teresa against my mortal enemy Tim Walden, and Brandi, the Personell Director, I think. Executive: You ready for it? Some big wins would be great advertising for this CD! The HIT: Well, Friday's kind of punishment for my sins against the Front Office. Facing three pissed black men isn't a great way to do well....but so be it, I'll go out there and give it my all. The match against Walden and Brandi though, is for the #1 contendership for the tag team titles. Hmm...that gives me an idea! Executive: What? The HIT: Hold out on the album production...if Teresa and I can win the tag titles, I'll ask her to do a duet on the album! That'll REALLY bring in the revenue! Executive: Yeah! Great idea! Although, I've got another question for you, about the Geometry Clazz.. The HIT: Shoot. Executive: Well, we want to see if we should give them their own album..can we put their new single, 'Pyramidal Pussy', on the album? The HIT: Go ahead. The members of the Geometry Clazz smile, speaking in unison. Geometry Clazz: YEAH MAN! YOU WON'T REGRET THIS! The HIT: I know I won't..and, I also know I will reign triumphant in my matches! Which is why, I'm gonna sing for you guys! I call this little number, 'Dey Be Black!' With that, Trapezoid launches into one of his well known bad rap songs. The HIT: Well, this week on Fusion I be ready to mack, but Owner Kannon's gon' be on the attack! He's got three ready to beat me black, a bunch of angry fellows: oh Dey Be Black! :Trapezoid does a horrifically bad dance routine for five seconds: The HIT: They coming to beat me, they coming to beat me, trying to knock my ass back in to defeat me! But they won't, and that's a fac'! And why? Dat's simple! DEY BE BLACK! Executive: Um.....that was more than mildly racist. The HIT: Yeah, you're right....I apologize to all black people out there that AREN'T sent by Xavier Kannon and the Front Office to kick my ass! With that, we fade out, unexpectedly and mercifully. ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
