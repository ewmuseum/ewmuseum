ADVENTURES IN THE VOID! PART 1

6 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: ADVENTURES IN THE VOID! PART 1 Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 10-6-2001 ..::/::..Our story begins within the confines of 'City Park' located on the outskirts of the city, a beautiful and lush park filled with regal oak and cherry trees and a picture of innocence and serenity as little children run about having there careless fun, lovers walk hand in hand down the stone walkways, and the sun smiles down on all those below so they may truly enjoy the experience the park has to offer. And within the park...sitting quietly on a wooden park bench eating a pack of all clear Gummi bears to satisfy his hunger...is none other than reigning PCW Continental champion and all around good guy of the PCW...RENT-A-HERO. He childishly pops another clear Gummi bear in his mouth, his coveted Continental title strapped about the waist of his Spidreman 2099 replica costume proudly as he soaks up all the sun has to offer in utter peace. In front of him, skipping about merrily in her green Powerpuff Girls dress is Rent-A-Hero's new adopted daughter, Amy A-Hero, the innocent and loving little girl who was forsaken by Brandi and responsible for the transformation of our hero back to who we know him as now. The sun compliments her honey blonde hair perfectly, the little girl skipping back over to her 'popp-a-hero' and asking him inquistively, with her usual bright-eyed innocence..::/::..Amy:'Popp-A-Hero, why did we come to the park today? I thought you were gonna fight that mean ol' Frisco.'Rent-A-Hero:'Well Amy....the problem is, nobody knows of Frisco's whereabouts and contrary to issue #23663 of Alpha Females from the Lesbian Sector, it is IMPOSSIBLE to fight air...body slam it, yes. Fight it, NO! I am just as distraught as you my little Lois Lane, I was lookign forward to slaying that Argentinan brute for his evils against PCW and Big Alan Steele, but with his whereabouts unknown, he may very well have avoided the SUPER SAIEN SPANKERING I would have presented him had I had the chance! Lucky for him...cause I had that spankering SPECIALLY imported for him from Krypton too.' Amy: *giggles* 'You talk funny popp-a-hero.' ..::/::..Our hero simply smiles at his little daughter, tickling her chin which makes her giggle sweetly before he pops another Gummi bear within his mouth. Amy looks to her left, her eyes lighting up and the little girl jumping up and down repeatedly as she pulls at our hero's leg, asking repeatedly..::/::.. Amy:'POPPA POPPA POPPA!! CAN I HAVE SOME ICE CREAM?!?! PLEASE? PLEASE?! PLEASE!?!'Rent-A-Hero:'Ohhhh oh alright...you no I can't say no to your begging...what superhero did you learn that from deary?'..::/::..Our hero hands her a crisp dollar bill, Amy looking at it with childish disdain before saying to her superhero father..::/::.. Amy:'A dollar? What can I do with a dollar rent-a-poppa?' Rent-A-Hero:'Well...you can make a phone call with 10-10-220...only 99 cents for 20 minutes! It's the only number I use when I'm calling my little munchkin...whether it be from The City or Krypton.' *cheesy thumbs up at camera* ..::/::..A hand comes out of nowhere and hands Rent-A-Hero a large, white sack of money as the hero opens up the bag, eyeing through the cash eagerly as Amy stands confused, asking him..::/::.. Amy:'Ummm daddy...what was that?' Rent-A-Hero:'Honey...we like to call that 'cheap advertising'. Cheap advertising pays for that Rent-Cave I have on layaway and all those happy meals you like to eat sweetie. Now run along and get your ice cream while daddy counts his money.' ..::/::..Amy shrugs, skipping over to her left while humming a merry tune, making a beeline straight for the ice cream cart being wheeled by an elderly manin glasses. The 'star pop' for 99 cents is calling her name as she takes longer strides in her skipping, almost at her point of destination when suddenly...the little girl slams into the leg of a man in front of her. She falls on her butt, holding her head as she looks up....the sun blocked out for the little short princess as she looks up to find herself towered over by none other than the Black Dragon Master...AKIYAMA. Ordinarily, most people would piss their pants in fear of the Neo Void God, but Amy seems quite intrigued by the armored, entranced by his black armor and his gigantic presence as she asks..::/::.. Amy:'Hiya Mr.Giant-Man....' Akiyama:'.....' Amy:'Hehe...you didn't say anything. You got reeeeeally cool armor...' Akiyama: *grunts* Amy:'Teehee....I'ma go get some ice cream now...love ya bye bye...' ..::/::..With that 'intelligent' conversation done between our hero's Rent-A-Daughter and the Neo Void God, Amy approaches the ice cream vendor, ready to order her ice cream after the young blonde man in front of her....and in a suprise, Akiyama is also in the ice cream line, quite uncharacteristic of his destructive nature but a lot of uncharacteristics are known for taking place in RAH RP's. Anyway, the young man in front of Amy purchases his ice cream, happily turning about with his coffee brown eyes widened in merryment as he exclaims..:/::.. Blonde Guy:'WOOO, THE LAST DRAGONBALL POP AND IT'S MINE!!' ..::/::..Akiyama's eyes light up angrily as he takes notice of the frozen treat in hand of the young man, an orange sherbert ice cream pop in the shape and design of a Dragonball from Dragon Ball Z. He steps out of line, sticking out his tongue to partake of the sweet treat when he notices an incredibly angry Aki, his eyes piercing towards him...the Neo Void God obviously wanting the Dragonball pop that he bought the last of. With that same piercing stare....Akiyama begins to pulsate with energy, his eyes glowing sky blue with energy as the world about the young man begins to open up in black darkness, Amy scared out of her pants as the most sinsiter force begins to make it's prescence felt for the first time to her....the infamous VOID. Rent-A-Hero turns his head, his eyes bugging out with shock as the space around the young blonde man begins to rip open violently, scaring him like crazy until finally...Akiyama releases all his energy, the Void wide open as it's vacuum begins to suck him in, pulling him in furiously as he yells angrily as he is guzzeled down the throat of the Void....but a small casualty is also taken with it. Rent-A-Hero watches on in horror as Amy grips the ground below her, digging her fingers into the dirt and grass beneath her....but the force of the Void is too strong....and with enough time, it guzzles her down into the reaches of it with no problem, our hero racing over to the Void and screaming angrily at her loss. He then looks to Akiyama, distraughtly yelling..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'WHAT IN ELEKTRA'S NAME HAVE YOU DONE WITH MY AMY!!! ANSWER ME!!' Akiyama:'....' Rent-A-Hero:'BWAH!!! CURSES WITH YOU YOU BIG DUMB NEO VOID JERK!! I HAVE NO TIME TO WASTE...I MUST SAVE MY AMY!!! BUT HOW CAN I ENTER THE VOID WITHOUT BEING LOST MYSELF?! I KNOW!!'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero dips into the pockets of his costume, unearthing the thought to be gone.....YO-YO O' JUSTICE!!!! He brandishes the yellow and blue yo-yo, wrapping it around the trunk of one of the park trees while taking the rest and wrapping it about his waist. He then looks to the Void, taking in a dep breath before yelling..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'AMY...I'M COMING!!! GERONA-MAYBE!!!' ..::/::..With that, our hero dives off into the reaches of the dark Void, Akiyama just standing there watching him plummet down the spiraling dark hole, indifferent in his feelings towards the plummet. With the hero gone to chase after Amy, Aki stares at the ice cream vendor...not sticking around for Aki's wrath as he races away to safety, the nVg looking about before dipping his large hands into the vendor's cart and unearthing an ice cream sandwich, nodding before walking away with the sweet treat...::/::..
