ALF gives Duke some Advice

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/02 -The urban Cowboy Duke Williams walks down the street looking for love in all the wrong places. He walks the campus of North Western University hunting for some two-legged deer. His mission is not really that of hunting, this is just kind of a side trip as he makes his way to the health department. His personal trainer Sal is waiting for him. Duke looks out of place at 6’3” with a giant suede trench coat hanging off of him. His cowboy hat covers his eyes as he strolls down the path with a pimp limp. Duke talks to the PCW camera as he walks.- Williams: Ahhh Buckaroo’s its scamp Williams time again. Gold something I haven’t had in a long time. Glory something I already have achieved but would love to add too. Gold and Glory this Sunday live on Pay-Per-View. Why am I still here? -Duke spits into the grass and almost his some yuppie kid.- Williams: The fans love me. My peers respect me, however they don’t dig my style. When the votes come out I generally don’t do very well. People hate directness and view my style as too old fashion. To those people they can all go f*ck off. I’m not here to win popularity contest just gold and glory. -Duke pulls a cigar out one of his pockets and lights it up.- Williams: I don’t hide what I’m after. I’ll tell it to you too your face. GOLD AND GLORY. Say it with me hoss. GOLD AND GLORY. Put your weight on it. -Duke stops and turns around as he watches some hot young college girl walk by.- Williams: Umm sugar I hate to see you go but I love to watch you leave. -Duke sticks out his tongue and wiggles it in an obscene gesture.- Williams: You can’t go to the respect store and buy it. It’s something you earn. Something I’ve earned. But respect is an element of Glory and not the whole genera. -Duke lights up the cigar.- Williams:: What the hell I’m I talking about? F*ck it. I’ll move on. The double cage match this Sunday. You have to have balls to win this kind of match and daddy I have the two largest saddlebags in the PCW. Fear is not a factor when I step in the ring and the adrenaline starts to pump through my veins. Wild eyed and crazy is the correct term. Sure I’m scared sh*tless right now but come Sunday Duke goes to sleep and The Malice Man awakens. Let a sleeping lion lay is the old saying. Too late. -Duke smiles a wicked grin.- Williams: The Malice Man is truly suigeneris. A legendary career that expands too many years to count. My legacy is one of the oldest in wrestling today. Still going strong after all these years. Malice Till the day I day…… -Out of nowhere Alf walks up beside Duke.- Williams: What the hell? Alf: No the names ALF not hell. Williams: What is this? ALF: I’m like a Muppet only I’m not. Can we drop this? I came here cus your promo was putting me and the rest of the world to sleep. -Alf grabs a cigar out of Duke pocket.- ALF: I love these things. Williams: Is this some kind of joke? ALF: The only joke around here has been your last set of promo’s. I knew a cat back in melmack with more talent then that. Look nobodies promo stay in the real world anymore. Look at the other guy he’s running around with Pete Rose. The guy you fought before that thinks he’s a god. At least I still have a commercial career. You still a spokesmen for Budweiser? Williams: Yes. -Duke looks into the camera like why the f*ck is there a puppet talking to him.- ALF: 10-10-2-20 has really done something for my career. I was sleeping in a box after my TV show got sh*t canned. Williams: You went broke? ALF: No dummy I ‘m a puppet where do you think they store us. Williams: Right. ALF: YOU see I think you need a manager. You need ALF. Williams: Whatever. ALF: I’m just going to call you Willie I’m no good at remembering lines. -Duke just sighs waiting for the spy TV camera’s to pop out and say gotcha.- ALF: I’m good at working with your kind. Just got done working with Toby Keith and Terry Bradshaw. Both rednecks like you. Williams: I’m not a readneck. ALF: Willie face the facts you are a redneck and I’m an alien. Williams: This is ridiculous! ALF: Only if there is acutuly somebody still reading this garbage. Williams: Nobody reads or watches my stuff anymore. ALF: Well you couldn’t last forever could you? Williams: I guess not. -Duke and Alf sit down on a bench.- ALF: Maybe you should retire again. F*ck it you and I can share a bachelor pad in Hollywood and make movies. Williams: No I have nothing better to do. I hate retirement so its this or nothing. ALF: OK it was just an idea. So who do we have in this big show down? Williams: Miyagawa. ALF: I knew a spork vender back home named Miyagawa does he happen to wear goggles and have three heads?? Williams: Yea on the goggle no on the heads. ALF: That’s too bad cus he owes me some cats. Look this guy just needs a good tale whipping. Williams: You think? ALF: Yea just give him one of your Bent Bound and Whiskey Hell’s or what ever you call it and its all over. Williams: Easyer said then done. ALF: Ok then just say it. You do that all the time anyway. -Duke is getting angry.- Williams: Your beginning to piss me off. ALF: Hey like my mother always said better to be pissed off then pissed on. Who else is in this little rumble? Williams: Legend INC. ALF: REVENGE!! You have to love that! Williams: What? ALF: The guy got fired that’s not your gimmick you’re the whiskey guy. Revenge man, for him leaving your stable. Poke his eyes out. Williams: That’s not what this is all about. ALF: Bull crap man. That is what it’s all about. This kid is a first rate nerd and he dumped you. That Erkul kid on the TV was cooler then this guy. He’s cool as fire. Kick his ass he dumped you! The Malice Man. Williams: Your right Alf I should not let that happen. You don’t dump the Malice Man!! ALF: Damn Right. Who’s next that Kannon jerk? Williams: Yea. ALF: This guy is getting famous on your name. He keeps showing him beating you over and over. Time for revenge. Williams: He is talking all my heat. ALF: KILL HIM. Williams: Stab him till he’s dead! ALF: Amen brother! Where to now? Williams: The trainer for a work out. ALF: Lets go to Pizza Hut. Williams: I can dig that. -Alf and Duke walk away to pizza hut.-
