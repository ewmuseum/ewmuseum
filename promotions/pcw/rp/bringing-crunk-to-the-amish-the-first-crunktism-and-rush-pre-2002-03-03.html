Bringing Crunk to the Amish, the First Crunktism, and Rush Preaches Like a Pro

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 This scene opens up at the noontime, Rush is sitting down with Ezekiel at the table in Ezekiel’s plain home. Ezekiel is wearing black overalls now, with a gray collared shirt and his black top hat, which seems like it would be hot in the sun but he wears it, even inside. Rush is wearing his gray sleeveless shirt but he has on the pants he has had all of his trip, because he is very much more muscular than the tall, lanky Ezekiel, but the pants have been washed by Ezekiel’s friend’s wife. So, now with clean clothes and a new objective for now, Rush is ready to teach Crunk to the native people of Lancaster County, the Amish. Ezekiel slowly sips at his watery porridge, while Rush just picks at his, every once in a while pretending to drop some so that he doesn’t look like a total food waster. He looks into the porridge and sees an U.F.O(Unidentifiable Food Object) that looks strangely like a claw or a hoof, and he pushes the bowl away from him, cringing in disgust. Ezekiel: Are you not hungry friend? Rush: No my kidnappers uhhh… fed me a lot. They were cannibals and trying to fatten me up. Ezekiel: How strange this outside world is. You were kidnapped and then you escaped to Lancaster? Rush: That’s the short of it. I don’t feel like telling the whole story but you’re pretty much right on track. Ezekiel ponders this while going back to eating his delicious, exotic porridge. Rush thinks about March Madness and about being the only one left in the ring at the end, standing in godly victory over everyone else in PCW. Rush: Say, Ezekiel, do you think we could hurry? I still need to train because I’m not at a hundred percent and I need to be tonight. Ezekiel pushes his bowl over on the table and stands up, stroking his beard. Ezekiel: Indeed, I will call a town meeting. Then, you can show all the people of this town how to live like you. Rush: Okay, but just remember, I can’t promise anything if they don’t listen to me. Ezekiel: I understand. Besides, that won’t matter. Rush sighs in relief. Ezekiel: They will kill you if you can’t sway them Rush: Oh that’s good. WHAT!?!?!?! Ezekiel: That was what you call a joke. Rush: Oh, uhh…good one. I was just playing along, not that I thought your town was a bunch of white supremecist, racist, black people killing mob for a second or anything. XxXxXxX 10 Minutes Later XxXxXxX Rush is standing on a makeshaft podium, which is actually just a tall desk, where he is standing. No microphone is there of course, because these people don’t have one. Rush looks around, very nervous at making his speech. He starts to quickly crack his knuckles, putting his hands in and out of his pockets, trying to, and not succeeding, mask his anxiety by keeping himself somewhat occupied. Lots of people have come out to watch Rush, almost the whole Amish town. They quiet down as he steps up to his speaking point, and takes a deep breath. He looks out at all the unhappy faces, and realizes that he wants to make a difference. Rush: Well hey there people, a funny thing happened to me on the way to this town. A guy riding on a horse, with a dog by his side came up to me. All of a sudden, his dog talked to me. So, I asked if the guy’s dog was magical, and the horse said, no he’s always running his mouth. A cymbal bang is heard out of nowhere, and Rush looks around confused, but then realizes that no one in the crowd is laughing and Ezekiel is shaking his head. Rush: Hehe, great crowd you guys are today. Anyway, I’ve seen how you live, and I’m from the outside world. I’m from a place where people can buy any type of food they want, and they don’t have to grow it. The crowd still is not responding to him at all, and a woman yells to him from somewhere in the crowd. Woman: We like the way we live! The crowd starts to make noise in agreement as Rush smacks his forehead in disbelief. Rush: Okay, maybe you do like the way you live. I’m not saying it’s a bad way. I’m saying that there is a better way to live. A way to live that is more…Crunk. Hit it boys. Out of pretty much nowhere, three large black, singing gospel ladies pop up besides Rush, as he begins preaching, his last resort. Rush: Now I’ve seen lots of trouble and problems in my time. This town is one of the few clean places I’ve seen. I’m offering you the chance to become the first official Crunk Town. Women: Cruuuhhhuuunk Tooooown! Rush: That’s right, I’m telling you that with my powers as the Crunk as Hell God, I can make everything better. Your children can live just like the other kids, and even better than the city kids. Women: Better than the ciiiiiiiiitteeeeeeeee! Rush: If you let me show you the light of Crunk, then you will all live better. I can make the changes today, and all I’m asking for is that you give me your eyes, ears, and your hands, and that you give me a horse to get my ass to the Lou. Women: Get his assssssssssss to the Louuuuuuuuuu! Rush: Now, everybody join in. Clap your hands like this if you wanna be crunk. Clap your hands! The people in the crowd start to liven up and clap the beat. Not applause but clapping in a smooth rhythm as Rush continues his preachin’ ways. Rush: Everybody clap your hands! Don’t even think about clappin’ your hands just do it. Clap your hands if you’re a little kid! Clap your hands if you’re a grown up! If you’re happy and you know it clap you hands! Everybody, do we we wanna be crunk? Crowd: WE WANT CRUNK!WE WANT CRUNK! Rush: That’s right! Everyone wants crunk, but you get it first! I’m willing to help out the little people, because the Church of Crunk cares about you! My right hand man, Rolf~! The Monkey is out right now, getting some monkey poontang on the beaches, but he wants you to have Crunk too! Women: Moooooooooonkeyyy Pooooooooontang! Rush: So, are we gonna turn Lancaster into Crunkcaster?! Crowd: YES! Rush: Are we gonna turn the Amish into the Crunkish people of the Crunk State of America? Crowd: YES! Rush: Are we going to show equal opportunity to all peoples, no matter what color, as long as they’re not Jon Kano or that annoying Jason Snow? Crowd: YES! Rush: Are we gonna get naked and run around, wild and free like the animals?! No sounds can be heard from the crowd, and crickets chirp. The people all look around, and suddenly they reply. Crowd: … YES! Rush is carried off of the podium by the people, and they carry him right into the sharp ledge of a low-hanging roof and he falls down to the ground, clutching his head. Ezekiel: Whoops, sorry about that. The people pick Rush up again, hoisting him like a horny kid to a girl’s skirt, and start to carry him through the town, chanting. Crowd: WE WANT CRUNK! WE WANT CRUNK! XxXxXxX One Hour Later… XxXxXxX Rush is standing in the front of the town, with a white horse saddled and bridled ready to ride, with the townspeople. Ezekiel stands at the front of the party, ready to send Rush off to the Lou. Ezekiel: Thank you Rush. You really have brought Crunk to the Amish. Rush: Ezekiel, you deserve your place in the Church of Crunk as the Crunk Co-Ordinator of Crunkcaster. Please, kneel down before me. Ezekiel confused, whispers to Rush while going down to one knee. Ezekiel: Rush, this isn’t that gay thing you were talking about, is it? Rush chuckles at the man and replies, as he picks up a pitcher of water. He begins to pour it over Ezekiel’s head as the same three women appear out of nowhere. Rush: Ezekiel, you’ve taught me something when I was up there and preaching today. Women: Preeeeeeeeach it Rush! Rush: I learned that though we may be different, we’re all the same if we have Crunk in our minds and hearts. Women: We alllll gotta keep it cruuuuuuunk! Finally, Rush is done performing his first Crunktism, instead of Baptisim. Now, Ezekiel rises and faces Rush, his first friend from the outside world. Rush: Ezekiel, you’ve done very well and I thank you and your people for bringing me in. There’s one final thing I must do before I go. Rush begins to whisper in his ear, and Ezekiel gets the same very look of being shocked and surprised. Then, he smiles as Rush pulls back. Ezekiel: So that’s what reaming is. Thank you Rush. You’ve brought so much to our little town. Ride hard. Rush: Thanks man. The church of Crunk will not forget your town. Rush kicks himself up onto the white stallion, and turns around to face the people of the town. He waves once and then he kicks the great white horse into a trot, heading off down the dirt road, towards the great city known as the Lou. Rush laughs to himself while riding at a trot. Rush: Who would’ve thought? The Amish go Crunk and Rush returns in time to beat everyone else in the Brawl for It All senseless. Look’s like it’s time to go. Hi-ho Silver. Horse: My name is actually Barney. Rush: Oh sorry, my bad. Our hero rides off to the west, towards the Golden Arch, no wait that’s McDonald’s. Our hero rides off towards the Giant Arch, the welcoming to the city of St. Louis. He rides off towards his destiny at March Madness. We can see movement on the horse, and that’s Rush falling out of his saddle. He gets back up and continues riding. Ahh…yes that’s our hero.
