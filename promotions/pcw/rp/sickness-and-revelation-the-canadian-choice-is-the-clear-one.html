Sickness and Revelation....The Canadian Choice Is The Clear One

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.22.02(#6 PPV, #9 Team) “Ray Ray…where the hell are you? We’re supposed to be filming a commercial for this god awful Canadian stuff!” The obnoxious and sarcastic voice of Rush Mason greets us for this promo although the connection seems to be shaky, as if he is contacting the scene, but not exactly there. “You’re the one who made me do this because that crap was SO good!” The scene fades in from blackness and we can see the huge manager, sitting on a couch long enough to fit him, sniffling loudly and then replying into the phone, in usual Ray Ray fashion. Ray Ray: Dawg…I’m mad sick. Some muttering on the other line can be heard, most likely Rush cursing the fact that Ray Ray got him into such a predicament and is now sick. Rush hisses back at Ray Ray over the phone, sounding very unhappy. Rush: Sick? Sick?! I’m on my way to the commercial set-up, and you’re sniffling away? Ray Ray nods his head, but realizing that Rush can’t exactly pick up on this, he just replies with another short answer, his nasal congestion apparent from his sniffs every other word. Ray Ray: Dawg…*sniff*…I’m too sick. I think I mighta gotten Michael Jackson’s disease. Let me enjoy my last few hours of Negro pride. The reply that comes from the other under, at first, is nothing more than a ‘Pfft’ from Mason, but then Rush replies, his tone sounding quite urgent as he tries to reason with his manager. Rush: C’mon, you retard! You can’t leave me hanging like this. How can one shill without knowledge of what said shill is supposed to entail? And as the words go beyond that boundary of two syllables, Ray Ray is suddenly lost, fumbling for words, as he sniffles loudly again, blowing his noise like a trumpet call. He then just gives yet another excuse. Ray Ray: Uhh….Dawg, I gotta go. You just cancel or don’t show up, that’s what I always do. Making an indignant ‘Hurramph’ on the other line, Rush quickly clicks the phone off, leaving Ray Ray to sit alone, sniffling to himself. The large man then hits the remote control and switches the TV on, finding himself mired in the purgatory that is afternoon television. Ray Ray: I missed Judge Joe Brown. Can’t a sick homie get some quality uncomfortably stereotypically black television? Feeling quite sorry for himself, Ray Ray waddles over to the small area that supposedly is some sort of kitchen, dominated by a huge refrigerator. As the large manager opens the fridge, light pours out and his smile grows. Ray Ray: I think I found an antid….anito….andit…medicine! As the camera pans around 180 degrees, we can see the contents of the refrigerator include KFC leftovers, in the custom large red bucket. Ray Ray picks up the container and then sets it down onto the counter. Ray Ray: I know a way I could feel better real fast… With that, Ray Ray ferociously opens up the box and begins devouring the contents, ravenously throwing the contents, including what seems to be fully solid mashed potatoes, into his open mouth. The sounds coming from the kitchen are reminiscent of some kind of warthog as loud grunts hang in the air. Ray Ray: Must…continue…to eat… We can now see Ray Ray leaning on the counter, his girth seeming more prominent than ever as it hangs out of his t-shirt. The KFC carton is toppled over, with only a slimy gravy substance’s residue left as evidence of what was once a meal that could’ve fed three families…or one Ray Ray. Ray Ray: Damn, yo. I think I got food poisonin’: the overconsumption type. Of course, that is not a type of food poisoning at all, but more of a logistics thing. It’s the simple principle of knowing how large your stomach is and how much it can hold. Luckily for Ray Ray, that amount is high, but apparently not enough as he stumbles across the kitchen to the fridge. Ray Ray: Man, I sure wish I had a mad tight refreshin’ beverage that not only quenched my thirst, but also brought the cold of the Canadian wilderness right to my own mouth… Not realizing the shill for a product he has signed up to represent at Alliances, Ray Ray ponders this for a moment. Only a man of such a low caliber brain wouldn’t recognize his own riddle, but as has been pointed quite a few times before, Ray Ray is not the most well breaded piece of chicken in the bucket. Ray Ray: Oh snap…Dawg, I know what to drink to relieve this mad wack cramps and aches! Although he is speaking to no one in particular, he acts as if he has made a fantastic discovery as he fumbles through the now open fridge, looking for his salvation in a bottle. Ray Ray: Here it is! The bottle seems to gleam as he pulls it triumphantly out of the fridge, the scene playing in slow motion for Ray Ray’s emotional moment of victory. Then returning to real time, he twists the cap off the fizzy drink and lifts the clear drink back, taking in the bubbles one at a time. The label, ‘Clearly Canadian’ can be seen as Ray Ray chugs down the entire bottle, finally finishing it with a deep breath. He places the bottle down on the counter, and feels his stomach with a hand, an enlightened look on his face. Ray Ray: My stomach….it’s….not mad wack! Rush’s manager raises a triumphant hand in victory as he speeds off to use the phone, as fast as a 300 plus man can speed. With phone in hand, the scene begins to fade to black, but the voice of Ray Ray hangs in the air as the scene cuts out… “Dawg…I found a tight way to show of this drink…” “Wrong number, sir.” “No, I just dialed a number. I really had to tell somebody and couldn’t remember Rush’s number…”
