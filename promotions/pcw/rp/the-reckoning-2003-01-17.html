The Reckoning

17 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 17th The flickering shadows laughed at him from beyond the hazy twinkling gleam of candlelight. Their voices echoing silently, as the darkness danced in symbiotic rhythm to the wavering light reflected in the gold of the belt which hung before him. It distorted his own reflection in the keenly polished metal into a myriad of blurred color and motion, wrenching the once pure image into a twisted mass of shame and regret. Even in this quiet sanctuary, meant to calm and appease the malicious intentions that arose within, his own reflection stank of shame and fear. This small temple, fashioned after a shinto shrine in Fujikawa, was meant to sooth the rising 'dragons' that would torment Kevin Sandusky's dreams; but now it seemed a cauldron that held both he and they together. Locked away under pressure, until a reckoning could be reached. Biting stings of regret tore at his every pore like fire, the gripping shame of inaction squeezed the breath from his lungs with icy fingers. It was as if entire being had been contradicted and compromised. He has stood by and done nothing. The very traditions he persued and preached had been violated, the sanctity of the art washed away by the green blood of money. He had stood by and watched the institutions be weakened and overwhelmed by manpower and money. The dreams and aspirations of others discarded as 'things' into the abyss of neglect. PCW... Kannon... They ruined it all... Artistry... Honor... Integrity... All, sacrifices left at the pagan alter of power and greed... But there was one thing that remained overlooked. One tradition still held in reserve. One fierce black strain of hope that lived in his name and his bloodline. The catylst encapsulated in a stainless steel auto-injecting syringe. Beyond the alpha and the omega, is the Noir. It is not a weapon. It creates weapons. And the shadow's laughter grew silent as the needle pierced the skin. It's potent serum, a synthetic amalgam of atrapine, endorphin, HGH, anabols and adrenaline spreading rapidly through the tissues. Kevin Sandusky's eyes rolled to the back of his head as every muscle in his body contracted and grew rigid. Convulsing, spasming, he fell to the ground. His veins pulsated with the pressure of his blood now racing through him, his muscles spasming to the point where individual sinew strands strain outward against the skin like bundles of steel cable. His back arches in a precarious rainbow as his spine tries to break itself in twain. As the violent rapture crushed his lungs he screamed. Starting low in the manner of effort, before rising to a torturous wail before passing the threshhold of intelligent thought and speech and reaching out into the domain of animalistic rage. The echo reverberates like thunder down the walls and into the ground. Miles away, dogs howl and flocks of bird take hasty flight. Slowly the convulsions cease, the spasming stops, the reckoning complete. Power and greed are games, played at by men. Genocide is played at by one. Fear the Killer without Cure... Indiscriminate Reaper... Ebola...
