The Exlanation of Yahtzee

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 4 [The scene opens. Shadows corrupt the small studio that can be seen. Through some dim lightling a figure of a man and a stool can be made out. The lights in the studio begin to brighten, and now the man is no longer hidden by the shadows. His black hair is slicked back perfectly, not a hair out of place. He wears a suit and tie, navy blue sports coat, grey dress pants, light blue dress shirt, and a blue and grey striped tie. He looks to be in around his fifties or maybe his sixties, but age can't do anything to the smug, overconfident look this man possesses. He leans against the stool, smiling to the camera as if he's been around it for so many years. He begins to speak, his voice very deep and outspoken, like he was some sort of announcer at one time] Man in Suit: Hello, my name is Johnathan Spokes. I have been in such films as 'Space Babes for Hire' and 'Mamacita in a Hot Tub'. I've done many voice overs for radio, such as '2000 Flushes Blue'...but my crown jewel is 'Rogaine'. [The camera changes to a different camera, changing the positioning of the scene. The man turns his head just as the camera's change, showing a close up just on this man who starred in two popular B-Rated movies. He throws the smug smile away, and throws on a face of seriousness] Johnathan Spokes: Yes, I've had many great things in my career, but we are here to talk about something much more concerning than my career. We are here to talk about the dangers and hurt that can come from Yahtzee, a game of Luck, Quick Wits, and Strategy. [The camera zooms out a little showing Johnathan's full body now. He is no longer leaning on the stool, and takes a few steps toward the camera, letting his hands flow freely] Johnathan Spokes: Many just think of the game of Yahtzee as a game you can play with the kids, or a way to keep the kids occupied. Its a lot more than that. Yahtzee is played between many couples, at a serious level, and can result into long loves, or long periods of hurt and deception. [The camera's change again, the man once again turns with quickness and is facing the newly changed camera. It is just a shot of his upper body and face. He still has a serious, compassionate look on his face. Out of the blue he holds up a Yahtzee box, and is showing it off to the camera as if he was selling it on a home shopping network] Johnathan Spokes: This is the game that has caused so much heart break and lost loves. This is a game that can take love, passion, and happiness to its knees. This game is either the best invention ever made, or it is the worst. Lets take a look at exactly how this can impact a life. [The cameras change again, and Johnathan sets the Yahtzee box on the stool, opening it. He digs in and pulls out the red, plastic cup that comes with the game, it makes it a lot easier to roll the dice. Johnathan throws the 5 dice into the box, and his eyes bug out in a very surpirsed expression. He looks up to the camera with a smirk] Johnathan Spokes: Well, it seems that I have experienced the luck of this game. I've scored a Yahtzee!! With me, it could have been luck, but Im Johnathan Spokes, and that was absolute skill. [He looks off set for a second and then he shows a look of a scalded dog. Obviously someone off set was upset by the comment. Mr. Spokes looks back to the camera, to his adoring public, and moves on] Johnathan Spokes: You can see where the erotica pleasures can come into play with a game like this. The score of the Yahtzee, sort of the climax of the game. All the five dice must match in order to score a Yahtzee, but thats only half the battle. You can get Large Straights, a sequence of 5 dice, you can get Small Straights, a sequence of 4 dice, a Full House, which is 3 matching and then 2 other matching dice. Those are just foreplay considering to the Yahtzee, or climax. [The cameras change for yet another time, the man turns to the lit up camera, now smiling with the smug, overconfident smile again] Johnathan Spokes: Now we must step into the world of hurt and deception. One of the most publicized Yahtzee incidents in the country, and the most recent, is of professional surfer, and now wrestler, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He is the greatest surfer in the world, has won many awards, and recently started a career in professional wrestling. He feels that he must follow in his fathers footsteps, make his father, whom died in the ring, a proud man. Right now, Tommy is sidetracked by Yahtzee. So, lets take you to my interview with the Tubular One himself. [The scene fades. A new scene opens onto a different man, a much younger man. His bright, blonde hair, baby blue eyes, perfectly tan skin, Hawaiian button down shirt, blue swim trunks, and black sandals. This man is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He stands by a window looking outside with a look of hurt, a look of sadness. He stares out wondering why it had to happen to him, a man who doesn't ask much from life, just happiness. Tommy turns from the window and walks into the luxurious living room. Johnathan Spokes sits in one of the leather chairs with a notepad in hand, and the smug, overconfident smile on his face. Tommy then takes the leather armchair just adjacent from the interviewer. Tommy holds his head down, still deep in sadness. Mr. Spokes decides to start the interview] Johnathan Spokes: My dear Tommy, you certainly felt the wrath of what Yahtzee can do to a couple, and to a man. How do you feel right now? [Tommy still sits in sadness, his eyes gazing off into another world. Mr. Spokes moves closer to Tommy and begins snapping his fingers in his face, trying to wake him up from his deep, awake sleep...if thats possible] Johnathan Spokes: Tommy! Tubular One! Come back to earth and talk to me, Johnny S!! [Tommy quickly snaps out of his small trance and looks around, as if he didn't know that he was in the room. Spokes smiles now, his interview isn't down the drain now] Johnathan Spokes: Now, Tommy, as I asked before...How do you feel right now? 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Bogus... Johnathan Spokes: Of course you feel bogus, and who wouldn't? Now this fine woman, named Jessica Alba, went and just stepped all over your heart. You were falling in love with this woman, you shared Yahtzee with her, you made it more than just a one night stand, you made it into a long series of games with her, ones that you thought would continue forever, am I right? [Tommy looks at the man with confusion, wondering just who this guy is] TTT: Its something like that, dude. Johnathan Spokes: Jessica Alba is that very hot actress who is in that Dark Angel series on FOX. She is one hot tomale to play a game of Yahtzee with. I commend you, Tommy. TTT: Sure, compadre. Johnathan Spokes: I just am spellbound by the lips on that woman. Did you get a chance to try them out at all? TTT: A couple times, dude. [Spokes smiles, trying to get a feel for how exactly those lips were used on the Tubular One] Johnathan Spokes: Now the reason you feel 'bogus' is because you caught Jessica playing Yahtzee with three other men? I mean...THREE MEN!! How can you compete with three guys at once? [Tommy jumps up from his chair, pointing in the man's face] TTT: Compadre, I've had just enough of this!! I'm feeling TOTALLY BOGUS and your asking all these questions, which are just unradical statements! I know what happened, dude, I was there when she told me. Now you get out of my abode before I take you out myself. I lost that tag team gauntlet, which me and Harry were totally ripped off from, and now we're back in the hunt for the tag titles! And Fear Factory is going to have a bogus time with us on Friday!! Because the Cowabunga Connection is going to be THE NEXT PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, COMPADRE!!! [Tommy then turns and rushes toward a nearby door and slams it behind him. Mr. Spokes looks to the camera and shrugs his shoulders with a look of fear on his face. He has never been threatened like that before, and of a man Tommy's size, it could leave a little trickle in the pants, and by the looks of it, it has] Johnathan Spokes: Damn, I peed myself. [Behind Mr. Spokes appears another man. He has long brown hair, a long goatee tied in a braid, and he wears all leather. His eyes are bloodshot and his face has a ridiculous grin on it. It can be none other than Tommy's manager and best friend, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. He stands in the background of the scene laughing and giving a thumbs up to the camera. Mr. Spokes sits there wiping his crotch with is hankerchief. Harry looks curious over the man's shoulder and sees the small spot of urine] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Ha Ha! I can't believe you peed yourself, man. What a loser, man. [Mr. Spokes turns back quickly with a look of embarrasment, like he was caught in a very wrongful act. Harry laughs and giggles, stumbling around the living room. Mr. Spokes begins to hyperventilate and signals to the cameraman to leave the place. They begin trudging out as Harry follows close behind them, singing] Harry: The man who peed his pants...The man who needs to do the pee pee dance, man....You took a pee pee...and 'Heavy Metal' Harry got to see see, man...Ha Ha! [Mr. Spokes runs down the steps of the large deck, and out of sight. The camera man stays showing Harry laughing wildly and falls to the floor. His face bright red from laughter, tears building up in his bloodshot eyes. The scene then fades to black]
