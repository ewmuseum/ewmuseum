Back In Black: American Born PCW By The Grace Of God

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Back In Black: American Born PCW By The Grace Of God Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/02/02 -From his executive office next door to Kannon’s in PCW headquarters. Sits the President and ex -commentating legend Duke Williams. His hair rests on his shoulders as he’s dressed in all black with just the PCW logo sewn in yellow on his polo shirt. No make up on his face just a neatly trimmed beard. Duke kicks back in his big leather chair.- Williams: Ahhhh Buckaroo’s it’s scamp Malice time again. Time to let the cat out of the bag. Time to blow the whistle on this dog and pony show. Better know as Kannon and Ellie….I’ll let you the people decide who’s the dog and who’s the pony. YOU HEAR ME TALKING YOU WALKING ANAL WART!!! -Duke throws a baseball hard at the wall he shares with Kannon.- Williams: I may be too dumb for New York and too ugly for LA but hoss I’m just right for PCW. Unlike those no talent RUSH hacks taking up our airtime I tell it like it is. Unlike our lack luster champion I give it to you straight. Unlike our asshole GM I have personality and commitment. -Duke smiles into the camera and winks.- Williams: I seized the Presidency from Tyler Nelson. That’s right I said Tyler Nelson on my airwaves with out getting beeped. I don’t follow your rules you ball less hoss. He got out of hand I took his power away which gave you the opportunity to take him out. Now I must do the same to you. -Duke shakes his head.- Williams: I’ve watched promo’s from those Rush leeches and let me tell you its more enjoyable watching a turtle shit then listen to them whine. I ain’t lying hoss. -Duke shoots the camera with his finger.- Williams: Kannon you have to understand your only a splinter in my slide down the banister of prowrestling. You beat me once with a little help from the dingbat but hoss I’m a tad older and a shit load wiser this time. You should have left this lying dog lie. Cus now hoss no E-meter or lawyer can save you from being laid out ass chief. -Duke looks over some papers.- Williams: I have added some laws to the PCW law book. Any reference to Scientology or Hubbard is now to be taxed by 80% of one weeks pay. How do you like me now??? -Duke sings some Toby Keith as he continues.- Williams: New law number two. If that walking fat ass heifer of yours Ellie Kannon does anything to cost me a match she will be suspended for a year. I can’t fire you or tubby tiny brain but I can use some of my Presidential powers. The voice of the average people has returned. -Duke points at himself with a smile.- Williams: Not only am I going to do kick your ass, rid Rush of PCW, and do it all with a smile. But I’m also going to finally give the people a champion they can cheer for. An all American boozer with bad breath and a mean malicious attitude that don’t like folkal named Kannon, Clyde, or Justin for that matter. -Duke gives the camera a country snarl.- Williams: Hoss I’m American born and PCW by the grace of God. Now I’m not going to go all out and bitch and yell any longer. The words are just starting to formulate. But I can promise you this Kannon. I’m no Jeremy Howard and I’ll take it even farther. I’m the Mean Mean Malice Machine with a quart of whiskey in my motor and revenge is my destination. PCW purists unite as we rid our world of Kannon, Rush, and cock-a-roach champions. Baby that’s the Honky Tonk Truth delivered Malice Style. -The camera fades.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
