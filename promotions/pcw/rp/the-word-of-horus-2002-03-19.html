The Word Of Horus

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/3/02 The Word Of Horus… The scene opens up in what can only be described as a colossal white temple. Stone pillars, white in colour and as tall as the tallest tree are erected about the area casting down great dark shadows upon the white and cream marble tiled floor. The pillars hold up a vast cream roof constructed from strong stone, untouched by weather. Patterns and swirling designs of great craftsmanship and magnificence swim upon the columns of this strange place and all that can be seen in the distance is the blue sky and the snow white clouds that obscure it. Suddenly two figures, one a man of a dark skin colour and the other a woman of a lighter tone than the man walk into the scene. The man is quickly identified as our hero and resident Egyptian God The Pharaoh, and the beautiful, curvy woman is quickly acknowledged as Isiy Xansiv, Pharaoh’s wife. Isiy: This place, Titu, it’s amazing! Isiy Continues to look about the almost blinding sanctuary in wonder. The Pharaoh: That it is, Isiy…the sky sanctuary is indeed a place of great beauty … Isiy: I agree, my love! The Pharaoh: But enough of this idle chatter, we are pressed for time and Horus is seemingly nowhere to be found… he should be just over their, but… Pharaoh points into the distance towards a white marble throne positioned towards the far end of the colossal hall. The throne is of excellent craftsmanship and many patterns of great extravagance are carved into its backrest and its arms. Nobody sits upon this amazing seat and only a large, comfy-looking light-grey cousin is to be found upon it. Pharaoh folds his arms over his chest and gives out a deep sigh of inconvenience. Isiy: Well if he is not to be found upon his throne, then where is he? Pharaoh looks around the great place in search for the Egyptian God, still to no avail. The Pharaoh: I haven’t the slightest idea. Every time I have come here before he has been seated upon that very throne, but today I cannot seem to spot him anywhere… Isiy: This is a huge place though, Titu, perhaps we have simply not seen him… The Pharaoh: Ah, Isiy, we would have seen him by now, trust me…his all seeing eyes are everywhere; if he is indeed here he will know of our presence… Suddenly a low thud rings out, echoing off of the sanctuary’s vast stone pillars. Isiy spins around to see what’s going, but Pharaoh seems to ignore it. Isiy: What was that, Titu? The Pharaoh: I shouldn’t worry about it, darling…many strange things go on here in the sky sanctuary… Isiy looks around in confusion. Isiy: Well what on earth was that thud? The Pharaoh: Most likely a cloud passing over the sanctuary’s roof. Isiy: Clouds are not even solid things… The Pharaoh: And neither is this place! Isiy: What do you mean? The home of Horus is made from pure stone and solid marble… Pharaoh smirks. The Pharaoh: Not everything here is as it seems, my love… Isiy turns back around in the direction Pharaoh is looking. Suddenly the two Immortals are startled when a long, thin arm grasps them both on the shoulder. Titu and Isiy spin around only to be confronted by an old man, wearing a long grey robe and walking aided by a thin, shimmering black cane. The old man smiles and his bushy eyebrows and long white beard seem to flinch as he sees the face of the Pharaoh. Old man: Titu! The Pharaoh smiles happily. The Pharaoh: Horus! The two embrace in a hug before Horus begins to speak. Horus: Titu Xansiv! How long has it been since you last came up here to speak with me? The Pharaoh: Far too long, old friend…far too long… Horus: Yes, yes it has… Horus begins to lean his full body weight on the staff in his aged hands. Horus: And what may I ask brings you up here to the sky sanctuary? Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: I seek your advice, old friend…many strange and unlikely events have taken place since we last spoke and now is the time that I feel I require your guidance… Horus: Yes, I see, I see…and may I ask you to fill me in on the events that you name as being so strange? Pharaoh smiles grimly. The Pharaoh: For one, Horus, Akiyama and I have gone our separate ways. Horus’ bright blue eyes light up in shock. Horus: On good terms I’ll hope? Once again Titu sighs. The Pharaoh: Alas, it was not meant to be…Akiyama seems to have lost control of his Godly powers, banishing people to the void on request and ending the careers and lives of innocents…last Sunday I stood up to him and complained that I didn’t like the way in which he was going about his business…things went sour from that moment… Pharaoh pauses for a second before continuing. The Pharaoh: The Neo Void God struck me and I was not going to be one to back down from him…I struck him back and the Divine pair of the world was no more… Horus shakes his head in disappointment. Horus: You know that Akiyama is a well thought of being amongst the other gods, don’t you, Titu? Pharaoh nods his head in agreement. The Pharaoh: That I do, Horus. Horus: Then you will know that if I was to go against the judgement of the higher Gods in your favour then I could be banished from the Godly realm and the sky sanctuary to die on Earth as a meagre mortal…so is the punishment of a God who goes against the word of the higher beings…your fate could be even worse… The Pharaoh: I have made my own decision and I believe it to be the right one, and I did not come here today to speak with you on that matter anyhow! Horus looks confused. Horus: Then why have you come here, Titu? The Pharaoh: I have come here to seek your advise on a different matter altogether…I have already challenged Akiyama to a match for his PCW TV title… Horus: A match of the Gods! The Pharaoh: You could call it that…but to be able to call myself a worthy opponent for Akira I must first defeat a selection of opponents this Friday in a match to call myself the number one contender to the voltage fighter’s championship! Horus: And being a God is not enough to be called a worthy opponent? Pharaoh shakes his head. The Pharaoh: It appears not. And so finally onto the reason of my being here today, I have come to ask you a question in all truth, Horus. You see back down on earth, in California where I am currently situated, a great storm has fallen over my town; Anaheim…one of my opponents and possibly my biggest threat in this match is someone named Bobby Stormbringer… Isiy interrupts. Isiy: The Crappler! Pharaoh turns to his wife and gives an angry look to which she cowers. The Pharaoh: I wanted to know whether there is a connection between the storm and Bobby Stormbringer; is the storm a sign that I must not underestimate him, my teacher? Horus sighs and gives Pharaoh a sarcastic look. Horus: Do you really think that I, or any other God can truly be bothered with sending down Storms and such to warm our friends and pupils, as you are, of possible threats? Pharaoh shrugs. The Pharaoh: Well, yeah… Horus: Don’t be so stupid, Titu! Of course we don’t, the storm is most likely a tropical storm passing over California by chance… The Pharaoh: But it was snowing moments ago… Horus: Then maybe it’s not a tropical storm then…but trust me, Titu, the storm that seems to have dismayed you has nothing to do with me, or any of the other Gods… Isiy begins to laugh… Isiy: And there’s you thinking that it was sign, Titu! I hate to say this, but I told ya’ so! Pharaoh shudders. The Pharaoh: Please, Isiy, reframe yourself from using Mortal catchphrases such as that… Horus: I’m sure that your match will go just fine, Titu, if you have the determination to win then you will win! Believe in yourself, my pupil, and you will come out victorious with the right to call yourself number one contender to the PCW TV title! The Pharaoh: You have always taught me well and protected me from the evils of the world when I was but a meagre mortal…but now Master Horus I request that you come back to earth and help me train for my biggest threat yet, Akiyama, I fear that I cannot do it on my lonesome… Horus: Titu, I can only help you in the slightest, old friend…I do not know if my skills are still up to their finest…it has been such a long time since I last fought… The Pharaoh: Then do you accept my invitation? Or do you throw in down to the ground in a disgraceful manner? Horus: I accept… Pharaoh smiles arrogantly. The Pharaoh: Excellent! Akiyama will have much trouble in disposing of me now! I am practically invincible… Isiy: Not as invincible as Tim Walden… Pharaoh frowns. The Pharaoh: Why do you always have to ruin my moments? Isiy is about to speak but Pharaoh cuts in before she has a chance. The Pharaoh: And you call yourself a wife? Pharaoh sighs deeply while Horus chuckles at the couple’s feeble argument. Horus: And you call yourself a God? Horus chuckles again. Horus: Come now, you two, if we are to get you ready, Titu, then we must act quickly, we have little time to spare… The Pharaoh: That is indeed true. Horus: Then come! Let us go… The scene fades to black
