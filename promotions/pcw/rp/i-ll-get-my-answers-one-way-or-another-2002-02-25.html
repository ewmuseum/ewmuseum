I'll get my answers one way.... or another!

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 Better bring your weapon, when steppin', bring on that ammunition, trip and don't slip, not to mention, never knew no competition. An excerpt from Thuggish Ruggish Bone by Bone Thugs N Harmony The first of three important matches this week are in the books for the Krayzie One. In the main event of Sabotage, Jeremy had to battle Clyde for the second time in as many weeks. And, just like the week before, Clyde tried with everything that he had against the Krazed Lunatic, only to come up short. Which, just goes to show, that when you push Jeremy Howard too far, he'll respond back with deadly intentions. Just ask the man they call Clyde. He felt the wrath Sunday. But, he's no longer a matter in the life of Jeremy Howard. For, coming up on Friday Fusion, is Chet King. Yet, its not so much Chet King that the Krayzie One is thinking about. Its the shiny gold hardware that sits around the waist of Chet King that's caught the eye of the former World Champion. That title belt represents one third of Jeremy's goal at a title sweep in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Then, six days from now, the man that some say is a future hall of famer, has the chance of his lifetime to free his younger brother from a h#ll far worse than prison ever was. Six days from now, Jeremy Howard faces Drake Maxwell in a hell in the cell match for the rights to Justin Howard's freedom and for the Continental title if applicable. It might seem like a lot, but this is Jeremy Howard we're talking about. This man welcomes trouble, for reasons only known to his twisted mind. Which, just might be the case as the scene slowly fades into an ice cream truck rolling down the road. Conveniently placed on the right side of the road, is a massive green sign that reads, 'Terre Haute, Indiana'. Terre Haute, the same city that the Krayzie One has learned his son is being held in. Could Jeremy Howard be going after his son only six days before he has the chance to get his brother back? Jeremy: Are you sure an ice cream truck was a good idea? Moments later, the scene picks up the sight of the Krazed Lunatic, Jeremy Howard, and his wonderful girlfriend, Jessica, sitting in the front of a standard ice cream truck. Jessica: If it gets us close enough to ask a few kids questions about where Keith Lipowitz is, then it was a great idea. Kids aren't believable. No one is going to believe them if they blab about us looking for Lipowitz. Jeremy sits back, kind of in shock at his girlfriend's straightforwardness. Jeremy: I still don't understand your logic, but if it gets us back Troy, then I'll try it. Jessica: Because if you're going to get Troy back, you're going to need to surprise this occult that's holding him. Just walking in there as you are, isn't going to work. Jeremy sits forward in the driver's seat, wearing his normal name brand blue jeans and a black t-shirt with his likeness on it. The eyes of the Krazed Lunatic are set on the inner city of Terre Haute, Indiana. Jeremy: Ah, I get it. You don't think I can kick all of those sick freaks to h#ll? Jessica sighs. Jessica: No! I just don't want you to get injured before two of the bigger matches in your career. Jeremy: I can handle Chet King and Drake Maxwell. That's no worry. Jessica: You won't be able too, if you're laying in a hospital because of over-confidence. A disdainful look crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: It took seventeen police officers to hold me down the last time, didn't it? Jessica turns to Jeremy, looking him dead on with a serious look. Jessica: If you'd quit letting your mind wander off then you'd remember that our research said there were well over fifty members of this occult based right here in Terre Haute with hundreds more in neighboring cities. Jeremy: I remember what I read. Once I find out where this f^cker is, I'll handle him. Jessica: That's what I'm afraid of. Jeremy just shakes his head, as he makes it into the inner city of Terre Haute, Indiana in an ice cream truck. The Krayzie One spots a group of three pre-teenage kids walking down a sidewalk on one of the side streets. Jeremy clicks on the cheesy ice cream truck music and drives down the road. The music catches the attention of the kids quickly, as they turn toward the ice cream truck, signalling for it to stop. Jeremy complies, stopping only feet from them. Jeremy: Yes? What can I get you kids? Jeremy sticks his head out of the driver's side window. Kid One: I'll have a push pop, mister. Jeremy: Sure thing. And you? Jeremy turns his head to the only female in the group. Female Kid Two: How about one of those strawberry shortcake things. Jeremy: No problem. Jeremy turns his head toward the last kid. Jeremy: You? Kid Three: I can't have ice cream. I'm lactose intolerant. Jeremy shrugs. Jeremy: I'm sorry to hear that. Jessica hands Jeremy the two ice creams for the pre-teenage kids. Jeremy: Tell you what. How would kids like to have these ice creams for free? Female Kid Two: Well, my mommy always told me not to accept things from strangers for free because they might want to kidnap me. Jeremy smiles. It's good to know some parents these days still care to teach there kids the basics of life. Jeremy: I assure you, I'm not going to invite you into my truck. I'll give you these ice creams for free, if you can help me find a Mr. Lipowitz that lives in this city. All of the kids shrug, not having a clue as to who exactly Jeremy's talking about. So, the first kid pulls out some one dollar bills and pays Jeremy for the ice creams. But, Jeremy being the true fan favorite that he is, denies the money and gives away the ice cream anyways before heading off to find another group of kids that might be able to help him. Six blocks away, in a completely different neighborhood, the loving parents of Troy Howard stumble onto another set of teenage kids. Jessica: There! Jeremy: I see them. Jeremy clicks on the ice cream truck music once again. Jessica, wearing a modest white t-shirt and normal name brand blue jeans looks on, as Jeremy drivers the ice cream truck down the avenue toward the set of five kids. It's not long before they hear the music of the ice cream truck. Jessica: I just hope that guy we took the ice cream truck from doesn't break free from those ropes, that you tied him up with, yet. Jeremy moves the ice cream truck closer to the kids, who are seemingly ignoring the obvious ploy. Jeremy: If these don't know anything, then we need to ditch this ice cream truck and get to the BMW. Though, I'm sure everything'll be just fine. Jessica: It won't be if this ice cream doesn't lure those kids to us. Jeremy makes a motion with his hand for Jessica to silence her voice. Jeremy: I have an idea. The five kids continue to walk down the sidewalk, not caring at all about the ice cream that is coming upon them. Jeremy slows the ice cream truck down, going at the same speed the kids are walking. While driving still, Jeremy ducks his head out of the driver's side window. Jeremy: Hey! How would you kids like some free ice cream? All you want to eat. This catches the attention of the kids. A black haired Latino kid steps forward. Latino Kid: What's the catch? Jeremy motions for them to come closer. Being there's five of them, they comply. Jeremy: I need a favor and you don't even need to break the law. A pudgy white kid steps forward, next to the Latino kid. Pudge White Kid: What do you need, mister? In the background, Jessica holds up a whole box of chocolate bars as well as a box of push pops. Jeremy: For all the ice cream in this truck, I just need the address of a man..... Keith Lipowitz..... Four of the kids looked at Jeremy dumbfounded, not having a clue as to who the Krazed Lunatic is talking about. The fifth kid, another Latino boy, stands all the way in the back, pointing at a black Trans Am one block away in the parking lot of a 7-11. Jeremy: That's Keith Lipowitz car? The boy knods his head, 'no'. The Second Latino Kid: That's a friend of his. My mom and dad hang out with Mr. Curry and Mr. Lipowitz every Thursday and Sunday night at Mr. Lipowitz house. Jeremy: Do you know where Mr. Lipowitz house is? The boy's head falls forward. Second Latino Kid: No. They leave me at home with a babysitter. Jeremy: Thanks kids! Jeremy steps out of the ice cream truck as does Jessica. While Jessica is showing the kids to the ice cream, Jeremy makes a call on his cellular phone, presumably to one of his Asylum friends to bring the BMW. Whoever this Mr. Curry is, he's got some vital information that he needs to tell Jeremy. With that said, the scene begins fading away to Jeremy talking to this certain someone on the other end of the phone..... to black!
