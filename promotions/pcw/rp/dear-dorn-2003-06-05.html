Dear Dorn...

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05th June 2003 The scene opens on the unforgettable apartment suite of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident comic and current Continental Champion Gaz “The Jester” Green who sits in thought at a polished ash writing desk. The smell of polish waves tangibly around the open-plan dwelling, literally circling the green tailored Jester with a touch of annoyance. A pen is firmly grasped in his right hand and a clean sheet of paper looks up from its wooden perch, a smile arcs at the corner of his lips and the City of Sin native allows his arm to motion across the paper and soon follows line upon line of calligraphy, Dear Dorn, I can’t help but feel for you in this turbulent time in your career. If your career were to be measured by a Richterscale then it would have surely been off the chart but in recent weeks not one but two women have humbled you thus begging the question, does ‘Misogynist feel more appropriate of a religion than that tripe you currently worship, Astronomy?’ Now you’re conscious, free of the blinding white light illuminating from the rafters it is time to assess the aftermath of Tuesday Turmoil. What you may have came to realize is that the glistening gold plaque that once nestled against your shoulder, once being the operative word, has since been installed unto it’s new heir, namely Tessa Windsor-Green. The whore as you so elegantly put it. ‘What is a whore?’ Is it somebody with a bad reputation? NO! Is it a wench with her legs parted to the hours of ten and two? NO! The term ‘Whore’ with regards to Tessa Windsor-Green lies within the surname. I tarnished her wedding day as a malicious act but everything has now come full-circle and that my darling wife is actually basking in the name of GREEN! The same could be said for our World Champion who as The End languished in mid-card limbo, that is until he jumped aboard the bandwagon, Dorn, you come across as a philosophical person so it isn’t past you to realize that the Jester has defeated two thirds of the Triumvirate or the Three Stooges, as you’ve become known in seven days. That’s three hundred and eighteen hours in which you have lost both the Continental and Television Championships, more importantly is the loss of respect from the roster. The loss of your golden assets can be digested but the loss of respect is the reason why the Triumvirate aligns with a false Messiah like Dante Jackson. You can look for excuses all you want… Dorn, you were preoccupied with the gadget-wielding harlot Brianna Washington… Pharaoh continues to obsess with the World Title… BUT The truth of the matter is that you never once had the support of Dante Jackson. Not once did the Lyrical Messiah intervene, why? Because the all singing and all dancing black fool remains bitter that he himself could not dethrone your Violence Jack from his Continental pedestal. Dante cracked Bruce across the head with a lead-pipe and ushered him to the ring yet he still couldn’t get the job done, his frustration towards your stable mate manifesting during the Main Event. Dante Jackson is coming to the realization that the Continental Championship is simply a ‘Bridge to Far’ but, by leaving you high and dry he could earn himself the accolade of ‘The Man Who Single-Handily Ripped Apart The Triumvirate’ Quite an accomplishment! ‘What is the point of all this?’ I hear you cry. As far as I’m concerned your aligned to a man with an IQ fit for sand castle building and another with the body art of a leather clad biker, Dorn you the brains of the outfit even if I do believe your heads in the clouds, Turmoil was a case of you seeing stars… Anyway we both want wrongs righted. I want the man who watched idly-by at your demise to pay for his injustice and in return I shall bachelor myself by sacrificing our new Television Champion, the heat leech Tessa Windsor-Green! I have no quarrel with the Triumvirate, you’re no, Front Office but this is to be a case of you scratch my back and I shall scratch yours, Or in the numbers game of three to one, ‘Immunity’ Actions speak louder than words. - A Clown The Emerald Menace sarcastically signs the document ‘A Clown’ in response to Violence Jack’s perception of the Jester and wide-eyed he stares down, happy at the content. In a pendulum motion his eyes venture from left to right as he inspects the letter one final time, a smile confirms his approval and snatching at a nearby envelope he discards the letter within the sleeve. Waving his moistened tongue over the seal the ‘Dorn Tracer’ intended parcel is concluded, “I love it when a plan comes together…” openly mocks the Lord of Laughter taking the infamous catchphrase of A’ Team’s Hannibal into the bosom of his own twisted vocabulary. Sadistically he smiles, pleased with himself that he can buy immunity from the wrath of the Triumvirate and more importantly deal with the devil, a deal that may eliminate the thorn in his side, The Lyrical Messiah, Dante Jackson
