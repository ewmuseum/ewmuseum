I Try To Help You Pathetic People & I Get Treated Like This

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/03/02 We open up at the Unemployment office in Norman, Oklahoma where the town’s inhabitants are lined up ready to collect their Welfare cheques. One hatch is open in the dank Grey room where the Blue carpet is worn down through the 1000’s of feet that have treaded it since it was fitted. In the long line that stretches out of the door and onto the street are a selection of undesirables and those who look like they might actually stand a chance of being employed once in their lives. Some have even gone to the effort of wearing a shirt and tie ready for a trip to the job office, while most seem to think a pair of old jeans and a White vest is suitable dress to go out in. Xavier : “Worry not oh unwashed unemployed folks for your salvation from the slumps of worthlessness has just entered the room in it’s human form!” Walking through the door and alongside the queue are the 1st couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and his valet Eleanor Hall who is glaring at the people in the queue, her face contorted in disgust at their appearance. Xavier : “I know you must be feeling embarrassed setting of at the same time on Welfare Day, coming to collect this meagre cheque that will keep you in Beer and Cigarettes for the week ahead, but all that will be changed out of the goodness of my own Scientological heart!” Xavier is wearing a long, Brown leather coat and under it he wears a Black -t-shirt with Gold Patron Meritorious written across the chest in Blood Red. With this he wears a pair of Dark Blue worker-cut jeans with Blood Red embroidery down the left leg. His Auburn hair is tied at the back to give a ponytail while the rest falls down the sides of his face. He walks to the front of the queue and leaps up to pull down the shutter of the hatch to the shock of the woman behind it and the annoyance of those queuing. Xavier : “Yes, I know you want the free money, money that you haven’t earned....And no, getting up before midday and actually walking here does not count...” Ellie~! stares down the line of people, all looking frustrated at the delay in getting their much needed funds while she simply turns her nose up at them. Eleanor : “Does.......Not........Count!” Ellie~! is wearing a Brown suede jacket and matching short skirt with a White, Purple and Black geometrically patterned top and Purple tights. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle brown highlights is loose and cascades down her back to the bottom of her jacket. The front of the line has now backed off so Xavier exploits the space to move into, standing in front of the closed hatch to address the room. Xavier : “Now, I can see you are stuck in a rut, set in this routine.....But hey, a few of you are actually wearing pants which has to be a step in the right direction am I not right?” His hopeful look turns to one of mild frustration as none of them replies with anything more than a mumble to the person next to them. Xavier : “I see you need some convincing, but surely scrubbing this from your schedules should be inspiration enough. Well, enough beating around the bush, I am going to give you all a new chance in life, give you all employment and funds enough to pay of the mortgage, buy a new car, take a holiday.......I will GIVE you all of that!” This finally puts some life into the crowd as they talk amongst themselves, wondering what this strange Auburn haired man could mean. One man, one of the better dressed steps up. Suited Man : “So are you offering us a job?” Taking his queue another, minus the suit but still one of the smarter dressed among them questions him as well. Second Man : “Did you buy the factory up on Walker Hill?” The Scientologist looks confused, exchanging looks with Ellie~! before looking back to the assembled group in front of him who are eager to ask more questions. Xavier : “What in Hubbard’s name is a Walker Hill?” Another burst of chatter occurs while Xavi ponders the previous ones. Suited Man : “So are you a businessman, looking to invest in Norman?” Kannon doesn’t cope well with these questions being levelled at him and keeps looking to Eleanor for support who just gestures with her eyes for him to turn back to them. Xavier : “Who is Norman? Does he live on Walker Hill?” His answers do not inspire confidence in the crowd who only came here to collect their Welfare and those who were queuing on the street have found their way in as the orderly line has disintegrated. Man in Vest : “So tell us what you want buddy so we can get our money!” Several voices in the group agree with him and they chat amongst themselves quickly, all wanting to get this distraction out of the way. Xavier : “Is that any attitude to have? Is that what you say at your job interviews? If so then it is no reason your wives are all on the road to leaving you and you’ll only see your kids one weekend in 3.....Like a certain person in Elkhart, Indiana....Oh wait, he’ll be stuck with that bitch wife and NEITHER of them will get to see his kid!” Eleanor : “See.......His........Kid!” His new tone of voice does not sit well with them and mumbles on un-rest start up, spreading through them. Xavier : “Now, back to me saving you from your pit of wasted existence......I am Gold Patron Meritorious, Xavier Kannon, I myself have more money than I know how to spend, I could literally BUY this town, if I want something I get it, if I want something made it’s made....I am even going to have a flavour of Snapple~! named after me, Gold Patron Berrytorious....While I am guessing that none of you people can say that about yourselves can you?” A hush descends in front of him as they listen to his words, envious of his success. Xavier : “Now I already know hat I am more enlightened, more evolved and more successful than you.....But I don’t just want to rest on my laurels, I don’t want to simply bask in the glory that is me, I don’t want to have a kid, move to the middle of nowhere and live the life of someone who might as well just retire and let those more worthy inherit my throne as they rightfully should....Oh, I seem to have gone a little off topic....” Confusion seems to be the new trend in the assembled group, bemused by this charismatic man with a slightly unstable side to him that stands before them, blocking their route to their welfare cheques. Xavier : “Now, to my point......And your salvation.....” Eleanor : “And........Your........Salvation!” He nods along with Ellie~!’s repeating of his words. Xavier : “I am here today, in this dying town full of declining businesses to talk to you about Scientology.....” The mere mention of the controversial religion that Xavier subscribes to casts doubts over the crowd who mutter to themselves in disapproval as soon as they learn a minority religion is involved. Kannon, now in the zone ignores them and continues. Xavier : “.....Now, by signing up with us for only a small amount of money, I’m sure the bank will loan you it if you ask them and let the manager sleep with your girlfriend.....I believe that is how a certain long haired Lunatic from Indiana got his mortgage....Anyway, for this small investment your family can have a new life, free from this weekly trip to embarrassment, you will have you own income, maybe you could even inherit some class after a few years, and th-” He is then interrupted by one disgruntled voice in the crowd. Voice : “Hey, we got a Church buddy!” Xavier’s look of false friendliness is immediately replaced by one of disdain. Xavier : “OH THAT’S NICE! THAT’S FRIENDLY! WHY DON’T YOU TRY TO OPPRESS THE MAN WHO HAS COME TO HELP YOU JUST A LITTLE MORE?” They are taken aback by his sudden change in tone, his false mask of sincerity is cast aside and a new image of rage and bitterness brought in to replace it. Suited Man : “Um....We didn’t mean to insult you, we just don’t like things being forced upo-” Xavier : “DO NOT SAY FORCED UPON YOU! I SWEAR IF YOU SAY FORCED UPON YOU I CAN GUARANTEE THAT YOU WILL BE FIRST TO GET YOUR WELFARE BECAUSE I WILL PUT YOUR HEAD THROUGH THAT SHUTTER MYSELF!” Nobody really knows how to respond to the Gold Patron Meritorious’s outburst. Xavier : “Fine, I don’t HAVE to be here, I was doing this out of the goodness of my own heart, something I can see that NONE OF YOU HAVE! Well, you’ve blown it now....” Eleanor : “Blown........It........Now!” He walks from the front of the group to take Ellie~! by the hand and they walk along the side of the queue, a look of rage enveloping his face. Xavier : “SO GO ON AND GET YOUR MONEY! AND THE NEXT WEEK AND THE NEXT WEEK AND FOR THE REST OF YOUR PATHETIC LIVES AND I REALLY HOPE THAT COMES TRUE!” They get to the door and turn back around with spiteful looks on their faces as the people inside turn to follow their path. Xavier : “So go on, and live out a meagre existence, THINKING OF HOW IT COULD HAVE BEEN! You people make me sick and I don’t think my stomach can stand being around you much longer....While you carry on stuck in your own pitiful existence I shall be bettering myself by winning the continental Title from Jer-.....Wait, you pathetic people don’t even deserve to know!” Eleanor : “Deserve.......To........Know!” The 1st couple of Scientology turn away, shaking their heads in disgust and leave behind a bunch of shocked locals as the shot fades out.
