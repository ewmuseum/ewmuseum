It's Time...

Date: For My New Title Our scene opens, strangely, without seeing Rush Mason. The shot appears to be in some sort of pool, but there is no light, except for a tiny stream of white light coming from somewhere off-camera. “It’s Time….” The voice, presumably Rush Mason’s comes from somewhere within the confines of this…room? Is it a room? “After Six Months…” Six months since Rush Mason came to PCW, back then he was the Rush. Now Rush Mason is a member of the Uprising, his newest hope for gold and glory. And no, that’s not one of your shitty puns. “The Taste Of Crimson…” Rush has tasted the blood running down his face, from the multiple beatings that he has received. Has there been anything worth fighting for though? No one can feel the pain… “To Fight For?” What can be considered worthy enough to fight for? Blood is shed in war, but now the war begins. Now, there is another war. This time, a win means fame. Yet another loss means failure. “Such A Weakling…” Such things have been said about the Voodoo Villain. Right now, right here, he’s ready to disprove all of those pieces of shit who said that. “To The Critics…” Rush came in with such promise, the partner of the current #1 contender: Xavier Kannon. Xavier Kannon can blow him like the rest of those fucking moron critics. “Fuck…You” That’s right. Screw all the goddamn critics. Suddenly, Rush’s head is lifted from the pool, and we see, from the white light, that the Voodoo Villain is laying back in the pool, eyes closed. Fuck The Past… That’s right. Right here, right now, I win. I’m not stopping for friends along the way, because I have no friends. Jordan and RoC, they’re brothers. But am I Cain or am I Abel? All brothers turn. My Choice… Now, they are just my brothers. Fuck Cain and Fuck Abel. They follow God. I follow Bon Dieu and his black light, white light, whatever you want to call it. I follow him and his aura. Love Me… Some people will still love me. I don’t care if you love me. Hate Me… Some people will still hate me. I don’t care if you hate me. Know Me… But I’m not getting off of this fucking boxcar that we’re on. This ride doesn’t stop until the very end. The final stop is where everything tastes like gold. This Time… The gold is finally mine and I’ll use every bone in my body to get it. The playground is that way, you morons. The mental institution is coming to that ring when I step into it. Get Extreme… Extreme is bullshit. This is carnage. Limb from limb, flesh from bone, veins from skin. It’s mine now, Kramer. I’m going to take everything from you. Rise Up… That title is all mine. The Uprising wins and you lose, Kramer. Go fucking cry after I clean the blood off my belt. It’s Time…
