Date: W/ A Gimmick Change on the Horizon Our present scene opens up on Rush Mason hunched over, breathing hard, and apparently sorting through something. Back from a short disappearance apparently, Rush is wearing a long-sleeve Ecko gray shirt and silver jeans with regular-colored Timberland boots. On the other side of wherever Rush is, two construction workers can be heard chuckling to each other. “Ey Ernie, whassat guy doin’ over dere?” “I dunno, le’s go get a calzone. The two men walk off as all we see of Rush is more frantic sorting as his friend and Malice Crew co-member Jordan X Shabazz walks up. The camera pans out to show, strangely enough, that both men are standing in the middle of what seems to be a large dump, and no not like the fetid wasteland of Eleanor Hall’s snatch. Jordan: Rush, what in the name of Crunk are- Rush: STOP SAYING THAT! Rush’s response startles Jordan and the smaller man looks down at Rush with wonder in his eyes. Jordan: Saying what? Rush: Cru…Crunk. Rush spits the word out, but then begins to apologize to Jordan, still not looking up from his sorting. Rush: Sorry…I’m not really myself ever since I got back from my trip. Jordan: What trip? Is that why you were gone? Rush: Yeah I was in- Suddenly, as Rush tries to recall the details of his trip, a searing pain flashes through his entire head, causing him to kneel down, holding his head intensely. Jordan: Rush, what’s wrong, dawg? Rush stands up, sweating from his forehead now and breathing hard once again. Rush: Umm…it’s nothing, just a migraine headache. Jordan raises an eyebrow to question the statement, but just shrugs his shoulders, leaning against a nearby pole while Rush continues to sort. Jordan: Rush, what are we here for anyway? Rush thinks for a moment back to his trip and how someone told him something about coming to this dump and looking for something, but something inside tells him that not even Jordan can know… Rush: I’m…looking for a weapon to use when I get my shot at the Extreme Title. Gotta bring gold back to the Crew, ya know? Jordan nods but quickly responds with yet another question. Jordan: And why exactly are we in a dump? Rush bites his lip, thinking of another lie, strangely looking and acting like a certain former Governor of Arkansas. Rush: Well, I don’t wanna use those pussy weapons that Nelson gives people, I wanna use the real shit! Jordan holds up a toothbrush. Jordan: Rush, this is the only thing you’ve found so far. Okay, it would probably do some good for certain members of Ultimate Authority, but it’s not going to win you any matches, even against that old bag. Rush looks up from his sorting, finally stopping for a moment. Rush: Jordan, you’re right Jordan: I am?! I mean, yeah of course I am. So let’s get- Rush snaps his fingers, as if having an idea Rush: We need to be searching over in the larger area, there’s probably some nice rusty razors and broken glass shards. Rush gets up and jogs over to another area of the dump, but Jordan continues to follow behind, calling out to his friend. Jordan: Rush, you don’t even need the Extreme Title. You’re better than that trash, dawg. Rush looks up, disbelief in his eyes. Rush: You know Jordan, why don’t you ever think about something other than yourself?Jordan looks down at his friend, now he is the one with the look of disbelief in his eyes. Jordan: Hey, just chill man. What’s going on? Are you pulling a Bobby Stormjobber on me or something? Rush looks up once more…and grabs his head as searing pain goes through him once again. Rush: What the fuck is going on? Jordan: Rush? Rush continues to grab at his head and collapses onto the ground while Jordan kneels down to try and help him. Jordan: Damn…I gotta figure what the fuck is going on. So ends a strange promo. Maybe next time we’ll figure what the fuck is wrong with Rush. No but seriously, what the fuck is wrong with Rush? The answer comes soon; No really, I mean it.
