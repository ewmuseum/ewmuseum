Airship Conference

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.9.03 He seemed almost overly calm for a man who had just been notified a day earlier that he’d finally been employed with Primetime Championship Wrestling. He seemed extremely understanding, especially for the pompous prick he’d been coming off as recently, when he was told to pack his bags because he was heading to ‘Cloud Nine.’ And now, as he stood before the sea of humanity on the fifth deck- the entertainment deck- of PCW’s air ship, he appeared as collected as ever. Public speaking, while not an area of particular expertise, was tolerated and he often did very well when the opportunity arose. Today would be no different, hopefully. The podium that he stood behind, sat on an elevated section of stage at the front of the room that ultimately added another five inches to his already tall height of 6’ 4”. Beyond the stage, there was a large open space that would serve, normally, as a dance floor and schmoozing area to anyone who felt obligated to do so. Now it was filled with reporters, rabid little hyenas, wrestling over the best and closest spot. Their cameras sat on little bi- and tripods, set up like camouflaged snipers who were more than ready to send a bullet whizzing through the face of the enemy. They had their pens drawn like miniature swords. Silvio chuckled to himself in thought. The pen is mightier than the sword? No, they both serve the same purpose… They cut you into pieces. Everyone’s a God damned critic. Yes, they were hyenas, every one of them. They were all hungry and willing to fight fiercely for what they felt they deserved but truthfully didn’t. And Silvio was the prime cut of the menu. He was well aware of all this, as he glanced at his fantastically shiny Silver Rolex watch and snorted with disgust. Two minutes late. Typical! Yeah, like these bastards have nothing important to do. They’re losing valuable story time! Think, just now, they could be ripping some other man to pieces. Or perhaps sneaking a cheap snapshot of Julia Roberts who’s got something in her eye and while she’s wiping it away, oh my God, hit story! …Let’s get on it with people; you’re wasting my time. The rehearsed, plastic smile stayed fixed on Silvio’s face despite the negatively charged thoughts running through his mind. Image was everything. And as long as Silvio kept to himself, there would be no problems. And that’s exactly what he did, even as his deathlike grip on the sides of the podium grew stronger as his anger grew. Indeed, image is everything. He’d worn his best black Armani suit, an exact replica of the suit that he’d worn to PCW’s pay-per-view Supremacy, the first time he’d appeared on Primetime Championship Wrestling broadcast. It had been a nightmare, Silvio recalled, ending with a trip into a dumpster and receiving a coating of camel dung and God knows what else. What had been his best Armani jacket at the time had been reduced to nothing more than an oil rag. What a disgusting waste. “Okay! We’re going to begin now!” Silvio’s voice erupted over the commotion and was immediately followed by a hush, with the exception of a few last minute notepads being fumbled from pockets and the clicking of pens. All the reports—hyenas—sat in their little green folding chairs and prepared to take notes. Good, it was his turn. He cleared his throat, flashed one last smile, revealing his superbly white teeth and began. “As you should all know by now, I’m Silvio Rochelli. But that’s probably the extent of your knowledge about me and that’s exactly why I’ve set aside this time, to answer any questions you might have. So let’s get to it and please, be orderly.” A dozen hands flew up in the air all at once but a blonde-haired woman dared to do one better and rose from her seat. She had shoulder length blonde-hair and was smacking noisily on a large piece of chewing gum. A pair of shorts that could have barely passed as underwear dressed, if you could call it that, her long, tanned legs and matched perfectly with her black belly shirt, stretched over her humongous breasts. “Hey Mr. Rochelli! Like, my name is Kate and I was like wondering… Um, okay, how does it feel to finally be in the PCW?” She sat back in her seat, pushing the gum on the end of her fingertip and stretching a long rope of it outwards before bringing it back to her mouth. Rochelli smiled weakly and didn’t break his attention from the blonde bombshell. “The pleasure is all theirs, I’m sure. For me, personally, I feel like the kid who wanted something really bad for Christmas and he found out he got it before they could ‘surprise’ him with it. I knew it was all a matter of a time. Too bad it took them so long to do it. Next question.” Silvio nods to a lanky man who is dressed in a casual business suit consisting of a white shirt, tucked in, burgundy-colored tie and a pencil behind his ear. He introduces himself as an employee of the Associated Press. “Mr. Rochelli, can you inform us a little on your background? I understand you’re fresh out of the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Can you tell us a little about your experiences there?” The Italian snorted and cracked his knuckles lazily. His answer sounded just as lazy, very nonchalant. “Certainly, I can outline my accomplishments. I enrolled in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, which is basically a breeding ground for future talent for the large federations, in February of this year. On April 1rst, I won what they call the ‘Coming Storm’ title, which is like any other title really. In winning that title, I solidified my position as the cream of the crop, the true ‘Coming Storm’ out of all those little hopefuls down there. And last week because of my appearances on PCW television, I got expelled from peewee wrestling hell and I ended up here. Might be important to be point out that I never lost the title, either. Therefore I am STILL the ‘Coming Storm’ and I always will be. Thanks. Next?” Silvio leans his weight on the podium as the next reporter rises with his question. The reporter is a messy-haired kid, seemingly fresh out of college, dressed in a pair of dark colored khaki’s and a black t-shirt. He pushes his hair out of his eyes and reads his question aloud as printed on his notepad. “It’s been revealed that you’ll, uh, be facing Matt Jackson on Friday Fusion. This would put you, um, at a real disadvantage… because Matt Jackson, er, has more experience and stuff. What are your thoughts on your upcoming match on Fusion?” Ah, the single question he’d rehearsed his answer for. Rochelli ran a hand through his black hair while his tongue ran along the inside of his lower lip. He answers smoothly and confidently. “Matt Jackson… He’s really been a thorn in the side thus far in my career and for no logical reason that I can explain or see. I’ve heard the rumors going out, and the little whispers behind my back that I’m not supposed to know about. My ears have been invaded with the little snickers and chuckles. ‘Oh, that new Silvio Rochelli guy is going to get crushed by Matt Jackson!’ You all pretend like I don’t hear it, like you’re just one step smarter than me. You all have this unique impression that Matt Jackson, just because he’s gotten in the ring a couple of more times, is going to put me in my place.” The murmurs began in the very back of the room and worked their way forward. Silvio paused and examined the crowd and was immediately offended by just how rude the reporters really were. WHAM! A mighty closed fist comes crashing down on the podium and the room dives back into silence immediately. Silvio leans forward, in a shouting voice, and lets everyone know. “IT’S NOT GOING TO HAPPEN! Get the idea out of your head; shut your God damned mouths! Every little cocksucker who thinks that Matt Jackson has ANYTHING over me just because he’s stepped between the ropes a couple more times better not count their chickens before they’re hatched! What has to be done WILL be done. And Matt Jackson will be put in his place. You think it can’t be done? Yeah, well who bet on Funny Cide to win the races? Who thought the underdog could come through? No one, but the horse did it. I know my self-worth; I know what I am capable of! FUCK ALL OF YOU FOR JUDGING ME! Do you people not know that Matt Jackson was once new himself? Do you not remember that he, too, is a product of the PWA? Did HE ever win the ‘Coming Storm’ Title? No, he didn’t! He is nothing! Fuck every one of you in this room for being a judge! May the first person who is sin free throw the first stone but if you’ve sinned, shut your God damned mouths!” Rochelli showers the audience with obscenities and saliva thoroughly before he finally catches himself in the throws of a verbal rampage. Oh well, he had no regrets. It was people like these that hissed among themselves, that fired the rumors that reached him and ultimately threw the gasoline on his fiery aggravated mood. The all stared back at him, offended, a little intimidated. They didn’t realize what they’d done. Idiots, they were all idiots. Leeches. Bloodsuckers, wanting nothing more than to spread the lies and mistruths, whose middle names were all ‘indecency.’ “What about Tyler Preston? He is in the match too!” The call came from an unknown voice from the back of the room, piercing the tension-filled silence. “What? Oh. The body cannot work without the mind, can it? Tyler Preston is nothing without his tag team partner. A non-issue.” An immediate follow-up question ensued. “What about the Warrior?” Rochelli laughed, obviously fake and began stepping off the podium. “Yeah, what about him?” The last minute-questions begin flying rampant as the newly hired Italian walks towards the door. They all bounce off of his turned back, off the fine material of the black Armani suit jacket and plummet into nothing. Thanks for the welcome. The pleasure is all yours.
