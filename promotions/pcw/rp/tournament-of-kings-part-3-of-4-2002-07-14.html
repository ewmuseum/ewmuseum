Tournament of Kings...(Part 3 of 4)

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-14-2002 (The scene opens with a breathtaking shot of a beautiful sunrise. The sun is just starting to poke from beneath the rim of a large hill. In the distance, a small trail of dust is discernable against the reddening sky. Soon, we hear the sounds of heavy hoofbeats, and a rider comes into view. It is almost as if he is riding straight out of the sun. As the rider gets closer, he almost appears to glow a bright golden in the light. Closer and closer, until we are finally able to see that it is Venneman Pendragon. The gold shine was his golden chain shirt. He wears black pants and high boots, and Excalibur is sheathed upon his left hip. Though a small smile covers his face, his eyes burn with the insane light that we have come to expect from the self proclaimed King. He rides his horse hard, but not so hard that the animal seems overly fatigued. Looks like Venneman just has Xerxes out for his morning run...) 'Xerxes needed the run, and I needed a break from all the blathering wannabe nobles I had gathered at my Tournament of Kings. I mean, I know I invited them, but there is only one who comes close to being able to compete with me at any event...Stern. One of my only friends...Why do I even bother? I mean, what is it that I get out of this whole thing? Glory? Honor? I guess it just boils down to the fact that I love to dash people's dreams...Many undeserving people exaggerate their own greatness...Maybe it is my calling to just set those people down a notch. And I will admit, it is fun. I was merely born during the wrong time. This whole tourney is my way of bringing grander times to myself, since I cannot take myself to grander times.' (Venneman reigns in the horse as he approaches his tents. He walks Xerxes into the stable setup and straps a small oat bag onto the horse's muzzle. He strokes the animals snout and pats his back as he whispers to him...) Venneman: Big day today, boy...I gotta feeling you'll do fine though...I just have to hang on. (Venneman leaves his horse to his oats and walks into the back of his tent. He exits through the front and heads down the row of tents belonging to the other 'nobles'. He smiles and waves at whomever acknowledges him, though the crazed light has not really faded from his eyes. Anyone who gets to close as if to say something instantly backs away. Venneman passes a group of minstrels joined in a chorus of song. Edward is among them and Venneman gives him a nod. The song comes out strong and fine, and Pendragon cannot help but listen a little to the talented group. They perform the oldest song ever written in English, 'Greensleaves'..) Chorus: Alas, my love you do me wrongTo cast me off discourteouslyAnd I have loved you so longDelighting in your company Greensleaves was all my joyGreensleaves was my delightGreensleaves was my heart of goldAnd who but my Lady Greensleaves. I have been ready at your handto grant whatever you would crave;I have both wagered life and landYour love and good will for to have I bought the kerchers to thy headThat were wrought fine and gallantlyI kept thee both at board and bedWhich cost my purse well favouredly. Greensleaves, now farewell! adieu!God I pray to prosper thee;For I am still thy lover trueCome once again and love me! (Pendragon is normally passive to things like this, but the rendition of perhaps the first song to be penned in his English tongue dims the insanity behind his eyes. He gives a small clap before moving along his way. Finally, he reaches the end of the strip where a crowd of the nobles has gathered. They face the horsemanship course, which is set up as such: The course opens with a jump over a brick wall, followed immediately with a sharp turn to the right. After a short straightaway, the course takes a left and on the right hand side is a watermelon on a pole. The object of this obstacle being to cut said watermelon in half. Almost right after this is a jump over a stream that cuts through the course. Ahead on the left is another watermelon, this one a little lower than the first one. A sharp right brings us to yet another jump, this one over a log. A final straightaway passes six watermelons, three to a side. The first on the right, head level...First on the left, leg level...Second on the right, leg level, second on the left, eye level. The third on the right is high and the last one on the left is on the ground. The object is to pick this one up, not chop it. And then, the finish line The men listen to the rules being spouted by a man in a white wig...Best go all the way, I guess.) Wigged Man: And the course run shall be timed. Each miss of a specified target shall result in the addition of one second to your total time. Good luck gentlemen...Try not to get hurt. (Most of the crowd disperses to their respective areas, but a lanky fellow sits upon his mount, a brown and tan mare. Pendragon walks over to him...) Venneman: SO..You are up first, hey Thovian? Thovian: Aye...Feel like a damned guinea pig...This course is tougher than last year. Venneman: So it is...And for you, that bodes ill...Seeing as how last year proved to much for you. (Thovian starts to reply but he stops with a look at Pendragon's eyes. A slight shake emerges in his knees and he quickly reigns his horse away from Pendragon. Pendragon barks a laugh and then returns to his own tents, through the strip and once more past the minstrels whom he pays no mind this time... The scene cuts to show Pendragon sitting his mount, Xerxes. He wears only black breeches and high leather boots. His massive chest glistens in the sun as he surveys the course one last time. He looks around at the different watermelon setups, deciding beforehand exactly how he plans to attack the course. At last he smiles and gives a nod to the timekeeper. The timekeeper wears the traditional garb of a peasant, and the start signal is merely a hammer sounded to a large bell. At the first hint of that bell, Xerxes is off, with Pendragon riding one handed, his right hand clutching his sword, Excalibur. Xerxes is over the brick wall with ease, and he is heading to his right seemingly before he even touches ground. His hooves do not seem to touch the ground as he flies through the straightaway to the left turn where Venneman slices the watermelon in two with his sword. Venneman holds his sword towards the left of his body as Xerxes leaps the stream. As they approach the next watermelon, Venneman leans to the left and deftly slices the low placed obstacle, and Xerxes veers hard to the right...Pendragon's body merely shifts with the motion. As Pendragon adjusts, Xerxes jumps the log and heads into the last straightaway. As he flies through, Venneman hacks the head level watermelon on the right. Immediately, he shifts to his left and leans down to maul the watermelon at his left foot level. He switches to the right and hacks the upcoming watermelon at leg level and immediately swings with a high backhand slash to get the watermelon on the left. Two to go...As they approach the one on the right, Venneman tosses his sword through the watermelon as he switches the reigns to his teeth, He slides down the side of Xerxes, holding tight with both teeth and knees as he reaches down and plucks the final watermelon from the ground. The two cross the finish line and Pendragon leaps from the saddle, still holding the watermelon...) Wigged Fellow: Best time today...20 seconds, and the bonus for retrieving the final watermelon. If this stands until tomorrow, you win again Lord Pendragon... Venneman: But of course! And my best event, the most honored event, the lance, is yet to come...That is where the glory is...That is what I wait for. Glory and Honor good sir. The driving force behind any great man such as I... (The scene fades as Pendragon goes to retrieve his sword from its place in the watermelon pole. He pulls it free and cuts a large hunk from the watermelon he is carrying before tossing the rest away. He seems to be humming 'Greensleaves' as he munches and walks. Edward comes in from out of nowhere and kicks in with another song...Damn that minstrel!!!) Edward: And of the horse, my Lord is best; no one can match, him from the rest! For the lance awaits, the man so great; for therein lies his fate!! Lord PENDRAGON!!!! HE is Lord PENDRAGOOOOOOOON!!!!! (The scene fades before Edward can get any more of his melody out...) >>>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
