The Choice of a New Generation

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10/02 Sleep. That little taste of glorious death, sweet and merciful oblivion. The only time in one's life one may experience the ultimate in relief from the unending misery that is existence.........for the normal human, that is. However there exist those on this planet who are beyond........ 'Bah.......' Mumbling and grumbling, a man attempts to hold on. To hold to this sweet peace, respite from the unending pain...........however like all good things, it is not to last. 'Wake up nimrod!' A sharp object protrudes into his dreams, piercing them and scattering them to the four winds of the mindscape. His eyes open to see, and what he sees is a high heeled shoe screaming towards his forehead. 'BAH!' Grinding, grinding and ripping the last vestiges of oblivion from him. She buries her heel into his skull, cursing her curses and spitting her venom. Clyde: Get the hell off me! Bethany: (Grinding) Get up! Clyde: Leave me alone! The burned man attempts to roll over, not that it helps. Again his young lover's heel lowers, mashing into his temple this time. Bethany: Come on lazy ass! Clyde: What? Didn't we just screw last month? Bethany: Not that moron! Get the hell up! Clyde: No. Clyde pulls the blanket over his head...........we pan out a bit to see it is no bed he rests upon, but instead the hardwood floor of an old and abandoned house. Muttering to himself he attempts to rest, to block out the shrieking beyond his safe covers. Clyde: Why can I not just sleep.............and what's that smell. Beneath the thick blanket the demon sniffs..........his keen senses detecting a familiar odor. Clyde: Bethany are you cooking out there? I told you I'm not hungry, just put the pieces of nun in the fridge, I'll heat it up later. No response to his words...........finally Clyde looks over, noticing at last it is not a nun which cooks this day. Clyde: (Eyes widening) Son of a......! The burned man scrambles to a sitting position, swatting at his blanket which is now engulfed in flames. The hungry fire moves to consume him, leaping to his dirty rags as he rolls to and fro attempting to extinguish it. Clyde: BAH! With his flesh cooking to an even greater degree than it's usual state, Clyde thrashes in the fire............finally pushing it out, killing it to the hardness of the floor. Sweating he looks up to his child lover who merely glares. Bethany: If you're done messing around we need to get started! Clyde: Get started with what? I just want to sleep. Again the killer of so many lays upon the ground, pulling up his blanket which is now little more than a tatter barely reaching past his shoulders. No sooner does his head hit the floor though it is yanked back up forcefully. Clyde: Gah! Bethany: You're not sleeping! Get up I said! With a handful of his hair, the little girl begins dragging Clyde across the room..........to a chair resting against the far wall. Clyde: Let.........GO! Mustering an inhuman amount of strength, Bethany literally hurls the monster into the seat where he crashes unceremoniously. Blinking his sleep filled eyes, Clyde looks upon her. Clyde: What's this all about? Bethany: Look at you! What the hell's wrong with you? You're the laziest sack of crap I've ever seen in my goddamn life! More venomous words escape the girl's lips, however they fall on deaf ears..........after several seconds Bethany stops short. With her mouth still open she stares.........then reaches over and claws Clyde directly in his closed and once more sleeping eyes. Bethany: (Scratching) Nyaaaaaah! Clyde: GAH! Desperately Clyde attempts to wrench Bethany's nails from his face..........finally succeeding and throwing her off. However this does not deter her in the slightest. Bethany: You signed up for a stinking soda match and now you're gonna sleep through it! Clyde: So? We're not winning anyway, we don't win anything anymore. Nor do I care, all I care about is how many idiots I drag down with me and maim before the bottom falls out. Bethany: That's the point turdburgler, the bottom IS about to drop out! They're onto your lazy ass Clyde........ON TO YOU! Clyde: .............soda match? Bethany: Argh! Full of rage, Bethany stomps from the room.........and after a minute returns, carrying a video camera in one hand and leading a massive man by the other. Bethany: Now Violator, we just need you to stand by Clyde..........WAKE THE HELL UP CLYDE! Clyde: (Snoring) Huh? Wha? Bethany: Violator, stand next to Clyde and hold this bottle of Sobe. When I give the cue say your lines and drink it. Give me a thumbs up and a smile at the end, don't screw up! Violator: I am the Violator. The huge and slightly retarded fellow makes his way to the sadistic but extremely lazy killer...........pulling him up as he tumbles out of his chair, he looks to Bethany's camcorder. Bethany: Remember, we need to SELL the Sobe........BE the Sobe! Our careers depend on it, if we don't get this one we're fucked! Violator: Prepare for total violation? Bethany: WAKE UP CLYDE! Clyde: (Snoring and drooling) Mommy I don't want to go to school.......... Bethany: VIOLATOR! Violator: (Smacking Clyde) I am the Violator! Clyde: Wha? Huh? Bethany: Alright I'm taping now...........go! Bethany kneels, finding Violator through the camcorder's lens...........holding it steady she tapes as he raises the bottle of Sobe, stopping briefly to yank up the dozing Clyde and saving him from a nasty spill. Violator: I am the Violator. Prepare for total violation. I am the Violator. Behind the camera Bethany raises her head, gives a forced smile. Just as quickly it vanishes as she ducks behind the lens again. Violator: (Holding up the Sobe) Prepare for total violation. I am the Violator. The Violator stands.........and looks on at the camera. Silence.........and finally......... Bethany: Smile and drink dipshit! Smile and drink! Violator: (Shaking his head) I am the Violator! Realizing his error, the mammoth jobber holds the tasty drink in a pose beside his face.........grins heartily. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Giving a thumbs up, the Violator drinks........downing the entire contents of the bottle in one fell swoop. Wiping his lips with a dopey expression he looks on..........belches mightily. Violator: (Smiling) I am the.......... Suddenly a massive convulsion strikes the body of the behemoth.........his skin turns a sickly shade of green. Clutching his throat he coughs and sputters, finally flinging himself backwards upon the ground with a thud as Bethany's plaintive voice rings forth behind his image. Bethany: Poison Sobe. It really hits the spot. Violator vomits upon the floor, spreading the contents of his stomach along Clyde's feet as the camera image slowly fades. Bethany: And cut! Clyde: (Mumbles sleepily) Bethany: Goddammit wake up asshole!!!!!! Violator: (Coughing weakly) Prepare for total......... Bethany: GRUUUAAAAAAHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!
