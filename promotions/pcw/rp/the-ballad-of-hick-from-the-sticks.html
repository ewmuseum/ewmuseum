The Ballad of 'Hick From the Sticks'

Date: 01.12.02 (#2 TT) Let me tell you a story ‘bout a man named Ab,A poor, ex-con, hick, Doesn’t know how to add. One day he went down to Texas, Lookin’ for a win,Lost his match to Kai,Probably due to gin. So goin’ down to Mexico,His heart was kinda’ sad,His ego was unhealthy,And his brain was kind of mad. Met a hippie psychiatrist,And boy she taught him crap,Drank a couple beers,Got fed up with that. Movin’ down the road,Down to Mexico,Bust a lot of faces in,And bought a lot of hoes. Ready to right the “forbidden fruit”Or was he lookin’ for a drink,Why’d he lose to Kai,A man with a three-inch…..finger. Hoppin’ from bar to bar,Inebriated every day,Kept on askin’ locals,“True that Rommantico’s gay?” So happy with The End’s gift,Kept ‘em drinkin’ with his friendsEnd may have got him beer,But didn’t teach him how to swim. So pukin’ out his guts,Forgets ‘bout Tuesday night,Verse Amber n’ El in three-way,So can this redneck still fight? And even if he can’t,Ain’t no problem at all,See he wasn’t born for wrastlin’,He was born to brawl. Like he tells the people,“Brawlin’ born and bred,And by-God when I die now,I’ll die brawlin’dead!” So a lifetime supply of Bud in one hand,Fist folded in the other,Head on down to the ring,And spread them boys like butter. Romantico may not be a hippie,And Token may not have a dick,And you may think that Abner’ll lose, But hey….That there's just HORSESHIT! *And then the narrator cries*
