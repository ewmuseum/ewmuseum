Kidnapped and no where to go...

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-5-03 Mark is walking along the streets of an unknown city like always. The bright sun beats down on his bald head as it makes a reflection on people’s faces. There are all kinds of people mixed in the crowd. From being a coon to a mick to being a spic, they are all there on the street now. How many times will it take for Mark Makonie to show off his skills to be recognized? It all started with Shabazz, they both had a great feud going until the roster change came. That was the only thing that stopped from having Mark go up in the ranks. Then there was Duzza and Makonie, the only inter racial tag team champions to ever be in PCW and then Duzza goes and fucks it up by hurting Makonie and putting him out for a little while. Now its time for Makonie to step up to the challenge and face this so called “Agent” Matt Jackson and see if they can make a good run together. Now this is a stupid mick that doesn’t know when to stop or doesn’t even know what is his limits. ”What must be going through Jackson’s mind that he knows that I have Haley on a leash. He knows that I can make her do anything I want to and she is a very good person if you all know what I mean.” Makonie says as he looks up to the bright blue sky. ”Fuckin spics, they steal all our fuckin jobs and make us go back to school or some shit just to learn a new field so that we can actually make our money.” Mark thinks to himself in his head as he is looking at all the minorities going around the town. ”Damn coons, they all think that they are better than us but in reality they are just a bunch of pot smoking junkies. They all don’t know in reality the only reason God put them on this country was to pick out the cotton from my damn fields.” Mark thinks to himself again as all these topics run through the brain of Mark’s. Mark continues to walk past people and the sweat off a big fat coon flies off of his body and it touches Mark right in the arm. Mark feels the wet drop come across him and he looks around and can see the sweat pour down the fat man’s neck and the droplets can be seen running through the lines that make up the fat rolls. The big coon takes his towel off from his shoulder and he wipes his forehead off as the sweat drips down onto the front of his neck. Mark recognizes the big guy from somewhere and can feel that he has done something in the past to Mark. Mark sees all the happy faces of the male coons and Mark starts to walk with his eyes closed. The sense of his body takes over and he can feel himself walk in the right direction and he starts to have flashbacks of the coon community. People throwing items at Mark as he is helpless on the corner of the street not being able to do anything about it. Mark remembering paying his dues to do what he believed in, not letting down to anything that came to him from the coon community. An old rusted out van strolls down the main street of the town. No one notices anything wrong with the van or any weird things, so they all continue with their shopping and wondering around on the sidewalks. Mark is still thinking of the flashbacks about how he tortured out the coon community and finally thinks that they all know the white power is the best. For some reason they don’t know when to stop and realize they are just trash in this country. Mark gets to the alleyway that he is looking at and he looks up to notice the gas powered lights that border the white sign. Mark heads down the alleyway so that he can get to the pub that all the white power go and socialize at. The van strolls down the alleyway as well and it follows Mark. He turns around to look at the van, but he does not notice anyone in it. Inside the van there are three men wearing black suits and look like they are from the CIA or some agent company. All three of the men are wearing black sunglasses, dark enough lenses so that no one can see their eyes through the lenses. The men park the van as Mark gets closer to the pub. All three of the men get out of the van and they start to walk towards Mark. He starts to walk faster so that he can get to the door of the pub quicker. “We need to talk to you!” One of the suited men say as Mark looks as if he didn’t hear what they said. “Its about Matt Jackson and the Haley situation.” One of the other men says loud enough so that Mark can hear them clearly. Mark doesn’t turn around any more as all three of the men start to run towards Mark. He is too busy turned around and looking at the three men that he doesn’t see the stack of tires ahead of him so that no one comes down the alleyway with their cars. The men see that Mark is going to hit the tires. Mark turns his head just in time to see the rubber and it hits him right in the face. His head snaps back as his body falls loosely to the ground. It hits with a loud thud as if someone dropped a brick off of a building. The blood coming out from the nose of Mark is a dark red color as it drips down to the rosy red lips of Mark. The three men in the suits go over to the laid out Mark and one of the men kicks him right in the ribs. He does not show any sign of movement as the suited men go and pick up Mark. The three men pick up Mark since it takes all three of them to pick him up cause of him being knocked out. They carry him back to the van as the blood starts to run even more fluently out of his nose and it is now going down to the neck of Mark. “That teaches your nazi ass to never run from the captors ever again.” One of the suited men says as they open up the back of the van. “He cannot hear you so why do you bother talking to him?” Another suited man says as he just shakes his head. The three men throw Mark in the back of their van as they shut the back door hard. They all go up to the front of the van as they get in their respective seats as the driver starts up the van. “Now we have in our hands the prime of our plan.” The driver says as he gives a grin in the rearview mirror and drives out of the alleyway.
