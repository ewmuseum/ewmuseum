Back in the Saddle pt 4.5 - The conclusion

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-26-2003 “They are in the adjacent room. But you’ll never get to them in time. Not without my help.” “What are you playing at now, Elizabeth?” When we last left our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, he had just thwarted the plan of Elizabeth Montoya. Here we are going to assume that you’ve been following our story and not explain all the details. Have fun. “Well, as soon as the door opens, it begins a chain of events that will lead to their demise.” “Aren’t you supposed to throw your head back and cackle now?” “I don’t understand.” “Well, it’s what any good evil madman, such as yourself, would do in a situation like this.” “Really? Oh well then… ahem… MWAHAHAHAAAA!!! How was that?” “Good, for a beginner. Now about this death machine you’ve got set up. How do I stop it and rescue my friends?” “I’m not going to tell you that.” “You’re supposed to. You are supposed to be so secure in the fact that I will never be able to get to them that you tell me everything, your plan, your history, and what the fatal flaw with your death trap is.” “Really?” “Yup.” “Well I guess you’d know better than I would. It starts like this…” And with that, Elizabeth goes into a huge, borings, dreadfully awful soliloquy about her life, what drove her to evil and all that crap. But since even I think that’s too boring, we’re just going to skip it. Instead, I was to talk about eggs. Does anyone know if they’re good for you or not? I was able to keep up with the constant changes, but I got lost somewhere. Now I’m just not sure. If anyone knows, please send your mail to: [detail withheld]. “… and that’s the whole story.” “Wow, that was truly awe-inspiring. But you forgot about the fatal flaw in the design of your death trap.” “Oh that. Well, in order for the trap to be sprung the door has to be opened very quickly. Otherwise it just won’t work right, but you’ll never… HEY! What are you doing?” Dan opens the door very slowly to reveal a string attached to a match. When the door opens quickly, the match is struck, which in turn, sets a parallel rope on fire. The rope is supposed to burn through, which releases a boot on a swivel that would kick a bowling ball down a ramp, running into a cat that is watching a goldfish in a bowl very intently. The cat, staring too hard at the fish, is hit with the bowling ball causing it to screech and attract the attention of a sleeping dog, which then would begin running after it causing the treadmill to begin its cycle. The treadmill, which is attached to a pulley system, then would lift a pail of water into the air, bumping into a ledge, thereby pouring it’s contents down yet another ramp. The water would then run down the ramp into a large sponge that would, of course, absorb the water thereby increasing its height. After absorbing the entire contents of the pail, the sponge would then be tall enough to release the switch on a spring loaded board which, attached to it, is a ping-pong paddle. The paddle would swing up and smack a ninja on the butt. The ninja, upon being smacked in the butt with the ping-pong paddle would promptly shoot Mikey and Molly in the head. Upon seeing the ninja with the gun, Dan walks over to the ninja and smacks her in the head causing her to drop the gun which inadvertently fires a bullet that propels itself past the death machine straight into the chest of Elizabeth Montoya, striking her dead. Dan unfastens the restraints, which bind his friends. After a brief discussion, ending with a handshake, with Tommy Hilfiger, Dan and his friends pile into their car and drive off into the sunset. “Oh my God, that was beautiful, Dan.” “Thank you.” Back in the studio, ‘ooohhhs and aaahhhs erupt from the audience followed shortly by wild applause. “Dan, I would like to thank you for this exclusive interview. As always you have been larger than life. It has been not only a pleasure, but an honor.” “Thank you, and thank you to your studio audience. But I have one more exclusive for you.” “Oh my. What in the world could top that epic story, Dan?” “I will be making my return to the Primetime Championship Wrestling ring this Tuesday at Turmoil.” “That is news! We can’t wait.” “Now if you’ll excuse me, I have to go find out who I’m beating up.” “Ladies and gentlemen, Dan “Big O” Osbourne!” Fade to black
