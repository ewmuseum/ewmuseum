Death of a salesman

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, December 10, 2002 Dillon Groban is a simple man. He lives in New York. He drives around in his car selling Snapple to people. If someone is thirsty, he sells them a drink. It's part of business. Dillon's job takes him up and down from New York to New England. He's away from the family most of the day. And when he gets home, Groban is too tired to do anything. His day is spent driving and his nights spent sleeping. That's the life of Dillon Groban: Snapple salesman. However, on this night, we find ourselves in Mahopac, New York... not too far from the big city of New York. Driving down the road, in a beat up Buick, is our Snapple salesman. Bottles of Snapple sit in a cooler in the back seat as Dillon Groban drives home. He has the car radio on and beats on the steering wheel to the beat of the music. Dillon: (singing to himself) Pour... some... sugar... on me! In the name of love. Pour... some...... As Dillon is pretending to be a member of Def Leppard, he stops paying attention. On this cold night, there is ice on the ground. Dillon hits a patch of this ice. He skids his car. it skids and skids until...... BOOM! Dillon Groban crashes his Buick into a guardrail. Dillon's air bag works. He shakes some cobwebs out of his head, but shows no signs of being hurt. The minimal traffic on the road just drives by. Groban opens the driver side door and steps out of the car. Dillon: What happened? Dillon surveys the damage to the car. The right side of the car is pressed up against the rail. Groban looks at his car and shakes his head. Dillon: How the hell am I gonna get home? Dillon pulls his right foot back and kicks the car. Dillon: God dammit! Groban turns around and looks back at the area in which he skidded. Dillon sees the skid marks that his tires caused. He also sees the ice that he hit that cused him to crash. Dillon: Damn ice. Come out of nowhere. Cause me to crash. Fuckin thing. Dillon turns back around. When he does, he's startled. Dillon: What the... Standing in front of Groban's car is The Dark Gimmick. Dressed in his cloak, Gimmick stares an evil stare at Dillon Groban. The stare from the mysterious man causes Groban to get a bit spooked. Dillon: May... Maybe I'll wait in the c... car and call for help in... in there. Yeah. Dillon opens the driver side door and hops back into his wrecked Buick. Dark Gimmick, on the other hand, continues to stand roughly five feet in front of the car. DG stares at the man inside the car. Dillon: Maybe if I stay in here long enough, he... he'll go away. As Dillon looks on with some fear inside the car, Gimmick stares a blank stare outside the car. he stares at the man... then the car... then the man again. Gimmick stares at him and nods. Then...... POOF!! The car lights up in a blaze of fire. We hear screams as there's some movement in the car. We assume it's Dillon Groban. There's some banging on the door, but the door doesn't open. Dark Gimmick just stares at the inferno in front of him as Dillon Groban suffers inside the car. Gimmick's blank, emotionless look remains as the car burns and burns through the night. Fade to black
