Sci-Fi Theatre(Part 2)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 15, 2002 [We return to see Spider in his chair again, turned toward the camera. This time, he is ready for the camera, as opposed to last time when he was caught off-guard. He remains in the chair, still wearing his standard managerial attire.] Spider: Welcome back to Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre. As we last left our…well…character, Eddie has been sent to Arrakis on business of the war nature. The question is, can Eddie pull off the high expectations of the Executrix? Let’s find out as we continue with “Dune: The Ordos Uncovered. [Spider hits the button to play the film on his chair, and the movie returns. We now return to Draconis 4, the elaborate palace of the Ordos, and Bay one, the area where the forces will rendezvous for boarding. Eddie walks into the bay to see the troops getting acquainted with each other, and some dust scouts that have been shut down.] Sergeant: Overseer on the deck, ATTENTION! [All the troops snap to attention in their companies. Eddie looks around, and seems a little flabbergasted. One of the troopers walks up to Eddie; he has three stripes on his armour. He has blond hair, radioactive green eyes, and is taller than the Overseer.] Trooper: Overseer? Eddie: That be me. Trooper: I am Lieutenant Citadell, your X.O. for the expedition. A Pleasure to meet you, sir. Eddie: Thank you. I’ve never had to lead a military mission before. I have plenty of experience for the administrative portion of the mission. Citadell: Indeed. The people tend to like you; I’ve visited to Western province before and everyone is so happy compared to the others who seem to be freezing their asses off. Eddie: Of course. Anyway, what are the statistics for the mission? [Both men begin to walk down the rank and file of the companies.] Citadell: Alright, we have ten full companies of troops that will be boarding with us. The Heavy units, such as the Kobras and the Deviators, will come with us, but on a separate transport. Eddie: Are MCV’s going to be a part of the heavy transport? Citadell: No, they will be with us, as our job will be to make the beachhead at our last reported position before the idiot you replaced retreated off the planet. Eddie: I see. [A person, not wearing any armour, just the military uniform runs to Eddie and the Lieutenant.] Officer: Sirs, the transports have arrived. Citadell: Excellent! Overseer…do you want to give the order? [Eddie nods and turns to the rank and file.] Eddie: Okay folks, Maintain rank and file, and board the transport. MARCH! [All the troops turn and begin the boarding process. Within ten minutes everyone is on board. The Lieutenant and Eddie are the last two to board. A member of the Spacing Guild, a very skinny man, wearing navy blue robes and a conical hat, approaches Eddie after the doors are closed and pressurized.] Guildsman: We have confirmed the cargo. I just need your thumb print right here. [He holds out an advanced PDA, Eddie places his thumb print on it and it beeps. The guildsman walks away back to the administrative areas of the transport, and all the transports begin moving to orbit. After three minutes, the transports are in a very busy orbital path, leading to the cylindrical heighliner, the cargo vessel which is the backbone for the Space Guild’s monopoly on space travel and trade. The transports lock themselves in, and the space around the transport becomes quiet. Meanwhile, inside the large vessel, the dropships containing the Ordos vehicles are still, but the infantry are using all the amenities that are open to them. Eddie just looks out a window watching all the other frigates begin locked down, or still locked down from previous stops. Lieutenant Citadell walks toward the newly named Overseer.] Citadell: Solaris for your thoughts sir? Eddie: I’m just wondering…how many of those transports carry our enemies? Corrino, Harkonnen, maybe some other minor houses…I know how they work. Citadell: They will work to whomever gives them the most Spice. We used whatever we had left to pay for the trip back to Arrakis, I hope that there are no Harkonnen spies among us. Eddie: I hope not as well. Otherwise, our plan is ruined. *****MEANWHILE ON ARRAKIS***** Big man: WHAT?! [Shouted a large man, who weighs at least 280 or 290 pounds. He has a buzz cut, but it is dyed red. He wears little armour, and he is perspiring heavily. He is sitting in a room with another even larger man, who needs some sort of hover garter-strap in order to move around, and a very small man, who looks to be wearing some sort of cloaking armour. He is quivering with fear as the large one stomps around the room like an ork.] Big man: You have got to be kidding me? The Ordos is coming back to Arrakis?! Man #2: Y-y-y-yes, Lord Rabban, Our spies on board the heighliner J725 have reported the boarding of ten companies of Ordos troops, and several companies of vehicles. Rabban: DAMN! I thought we drove the Ordos out of Arrakis, and now they want to come back?! Have they plotted a landing site yet? SPEAK! Man #2: No they have not. Rabban: Very well, LEAVE! [The skinny scout leaves] Rabban: What am I going to do? With all the troops tied up in trying to pacify these godforsaken Fremen, These bastards are going to walk in and take it over! Fat man: Rabban, they will not do anything here. Rabban: Are you kidding Uncle Vlad? They have a force that is even more than us, and at the rate we are losing troops, they will run over us with relative ease! Vlad: Look…The Ordos, all they want is the Spice, money and power…if we contact the Executrix, and inform him, that we will give them full reign to the Imperial Sardaukar, twenty-five percent of our current spice holdings, and ten percent annual tribute to begin immediately, then they will turn tail and go home. Rabban: If that is what you think, Uncle. Then I will try to remain calm about the return of the Ordos. Vlad: Don’t worry, my Nephew. I have everything well in hand. *****BACK IN SPACE***** [Back aboard the heighliner…we see the Sergeants of each of the companies, and Lieutenant Citadell hunkered over a map of Arrakis.] Eddie: I told you already, Sergeant, we land there, we’ll be wandering the desert for months before we even get close! Citadell: The way I see it, if we land at Arrakeen, the Capitol city, we are dead as the Harkonnen will be right on top of us. I say we land at our outpost near…Carthag. The rest of the way, we should walk at Night, and rest during the day because it can get rather hot. Eddie: Well, duh, it’s a desert planet. [The sergeants laugh at their CO’s comment. Then a loud noise is heard throughout the cargo vessel…] PA System: Attention all personnel, now approaching Planet Arrakis…All personnel are required to return to their transports for unloading…estimated time of arrival is ten minutes. Eddie: everyone, get your shit together, assholes and elbows, get moving folks! [All the troops begin to get organized for the ride down to Arrakis, as we zoom out to Spider and the Enterprise again.] Spider: Wow, this is getting exciting isn’t it? Stay Tuned, you won’t want to miss what’s next! [Fade to commercial for Iron Chef.]
