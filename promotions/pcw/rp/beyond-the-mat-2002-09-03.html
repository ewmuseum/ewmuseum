Beyond The Mat

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03 - 09 - 02 The stage is set in the confines of a standard bar as the dynamic duo; Pranks With Attitude lay the building under siege with the final touches of what appears to be a party. Everything appears present and correct with colourful balloons roaming freely and beautiful drapes aligning the tabletops that lay home to a variety of tasty snacks and delicious dishes. The DJ assigned to this event tests his equipment with high tempo dance tracks and allows the strobe lighting to flicker aimlessly with its crimson reds and neon blues, which illuminates the full capacity of the spacious room. Our jovial Jester has decided to go with the whole surfers persona has he adorns knee length shorts, accompanied with a Hawaiian shirt and his more accustomed features of spiked hair and sunglasses whilst his partner in crime had opted for the subtler of approaches. Standing motionless and overlooking the proceedings, Nerezza wears a long flowing black cape that hides his simple white shirt. Continuing his portrayal of the infamous 'Dracula' the Living Nightmare brandished his canines, wiping the excess saliva with his tongue the duo finalize the decorations. 'What is the purpose of this charade?' groaned the behemoth with a hiss. 'Public Relations my friend.... We've just entered a new environment where everybody will be looking down upon us and I thought it would be a good idea for those individuals to get to know the real Pranks With Attitude!' retorted the devious comic, offering his partner a sinister glare that appears to gain Nerezza's approval. 'And there's me thinking this was a taste the competition party, a senseless blood-bath!' roared the goliath with a dose of sarcasm. 'No trying to eat the active roster?' ordered the worried Jester. The high tempo music unleashed from the sound system only appears to infuriate Nerezza with his patience wearing thin. The whole event at present could be deemed a waste of time and notably effort as the tag team acquisition wait for their guests, even the bar staff stood in a daze as they await the potential custom to seep through the doors. 'Jester where's your newfound friends, huh!?!' hissed the Angel Of Death. 'Hmm.... Look we have guests!!!' screamed Gaz Green with an out-stretched arm. Suddenly a horde of PCW superstars waltzed into the building, immersed in conversation and all adorning various costumes in keeping with the themed night. The End cockily strutted up to the bar with an expensive tailor made suit and continued his charade of 'James Bond' whilst Miyagawa opted for the Goth approach with a striking resemblance to OSW's Corg. Teresa Tomas strolled in looking all eye candy with a long dark purple silk dress with a slit running along the leg showcasing her slender physique accompanied by the corporate dressed Rush Mason. 'Shouldn't you greet your guests....' questioned the cynical vampire. With those encouraging words both members of Pranks With Attitude set hotfooted across the dance floor to greet the former Cruiserweight Champion The End who stands against the bar, Martini at hand. 'GREAT COSTUME~!' proclaimed the jovial Jester. 'Why thank you....' replied The End before an untimely interruption from the PCW comedian. 'I mean, even without the make-up you do a real good impression of Stevie Wonder!!!' cried Gaz 'The Jester' Green to the distraught looks of his recipient. 'Gaz, I don't.....' injected Nerezza to no avail. 'I just called to say I love you, I just called to say how much I care, I just called to say I love you and I mean it from the bottom of my heart~!' sung the Lord Of Laughter, unbeknownst to him that with every line that goes by he digs himself a deeper hole. 'And I've just called to say.... You’re an idiot and that to is from the bottom of my heart!' bellowed the irate End as he stormed off to the confused glares of Gaz Green. 'How rude?' said the emerald menace to his dumbfounded partner. The Asylum members gaze upon the dwelling with interest as they single out yet another individual who would reap the benefits by socializing with the hottest property to enter the federation since Xavier Kannon. Looking over their flock the duo nod one another’s approval at their latest victim, who was none other than the Voodoo shaman Rush Mason. The two man party confidently walk over and announced their introduction to the unimpressed witch doctor who appears quite contempt to simply listen. 'HELLO I AM GAZ GREEN AND THIS IS NER....' yelled the Prince Of Pranks slowly. 'Nerezza. You would be surprised how much English black people know!' grinned Rush Mason. 'I foreseen your arrival and now the future looks bleak for the company, everything is shroud in darkness' continued the Bon Dieu follower with a touch of sarcasm. 'PCW's future looks bleak because of our arrival or was you referring to the tag division?' asked the King Of Komedy. 'Gaz does it really matter?' groaned the creature of the night. 'Not really but as a token of good-will we offer you this gesture....' Gaz nodding his head in approval towards the towering Nerezza was a slight indication for the unveiling of their gift. Nerezza, placing his talons beneath the draping black cape revealed a voodoo doll shaped and crafted to resemble Bobby Stormbringer. 'Hmm, something I've always wanted, I guess!' proclaimed Rush Mason with a soft smile of acceptance. Leaving behind a conversation of interest with Rush Mason, the trickster team continued their greeting mission but this venture centered on more familiar faces, namely Miyagawa who masquerades as Corg. With fake piercing aligning his face and a thick white complexion, the Asylum leader welcomes his comrades with open arms. 'Great Party! I'm impressed but where's the prank....' asked the inquisitive Miyagawa. 'Sore wa himitsu desu!' replied the sinister Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. As the team immerse themselves in the conversation with Miyagawa the gorgeous Teresa Tomas steps in creating a quartet and instantly becoming the centre of attention with her flirtatious awe. 'I'm... I'm...Gaz... The... The Jester... The Jester Green!' stuttered the instigator of injury as his eyes wandered over the curves of Teresa Tomas who stands in a catalogue pose, attracting a great deal of interest. 'I'm up here - And it's nice to meet you!' murmured Teresa as she toyed with the Jester in a flirtatious manner. 'And I'm Nerezza' cried the Angel Of Fear, trying to draw some attention of his own. Whilst his comrade’s eyes gazed upon the splendour before him, Nerezza couldn't keep his own eyes from fixating over her enticing neck area. 'Gaz - I think it's time for your speech, which you've put a lot of time and effort into' continued the spawn of evil as he ushered his partner towards the DJ set-up. 'One moment... Teresa, you couldn't hold my sausage?' whispered the PCW comic to the amazed glares of the onlookers who have just happened to over hear. 'I BEG YOUR PARDON!!!' bellowed the irate diva. 'No, I literally mean my sausage....' croaked the embarrassed Jester, brandishing a string of sausages from within his knee length shorts, which Teresa took reluctantly with a firm grasp. Gaz waltzing over to the sound equipment with a skip in his step emerges from the dance floor and composes himself with a microphone in preparation for his big announcement that touches the surface of his introduction. Everybody looks in unison at the Jester as stands on a podium with his mouth ajar, awaiting his queue. 'I'm going to keep this short and sweet, kinder like me....' began Gaz Green, as the small note he held descended into a full-blown document. 'This party is a little thank you from the team of Pranks With Attitude for giving us such a warm welcoming since our arrival only a week ago. I know many people were unable to attend for various circumstances and that the batch before my very eyes show me that you to are glad to see our induction....' continued the conniving comic, as many of the congregation began nodding off into dreamlike states. Looking at his watch he is about to continue his flowing speech when suddenly the doors swung open unveiling a genuine task force of police including their blood thirsty hounds who strike forward still held tightly by their respected handlers. The enforcers shuffle forward, wary of their surroundings when suddenly a recognisable voice ascends over the riot. 'GAZ GREEN I KNOW YOUR IN HERE!!!' yelled one officer, masked by his standard headgear and plastic visor. 'LISTEN - GREEN I KNOW YOUR IN HERE, YOU AND THAT NEREZZA' bellowed the officer again. Nerezza making haste across to his partner realised the voice belonged to the officer they played a prank to only days earlier. Both men cower in the darkness still unable to react, when suddenly one dog jerked forward towards the swaying sausages, still held by the terrified Teresa Tomas who stands in petrification. 'Nerezza..... NOW~!' screamed Gaz 'The Jester' Green, as the duo made their escape.
