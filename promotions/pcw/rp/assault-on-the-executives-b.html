So says the Divine Prophetess... Red lights flashed through the corridors in a strobe effect. The siren blared like a wailing child in a panic, warning of impending danger. Down the silver-paneled corridor lined with wires and circuits fused into them, a bright line of lights shone. There was a side corridor on the left of the hallway. Silence flooded the air... Suddenly, blood splashed against the walls...like a painter throwing a can of red paint against a blank canvas. The side corridor went dark as the lights shattered from above. From around the corner stepped the god...slowly, like he was following a beacon. From behind a blast of laser hit his back, sending him sliding into the wall in front of him. A armored soldier came running up from behind and aimed his advanced pistol at the back of Akiyama again. The NVG suddenly whipped his arm back, swatting the gun away and catching the throat of the man in one movement. Then as he stood up, he threw him around and smashed the back of his head into the wall. Sparks flew as his helmet fell cracked to the ground. His eyes rolled into his head as he slumped onto the floor, a streak of blood following his skull. The god moved on... 'We need to hustle people!! Who knows how far Subject #3-9 has gotten inside Sub Basement Omega!!! Our time is already cut in half since he took out the elevators!! If he reaches the core of the building, we're through!!' The scene switched to show a dark stairwell where thick cables ran up the walls towards a light at the top of the stairwell. Running down the stairs were the Executives, with Reality at the point. They all dashed single file down in an ordered fashion, like they had trained numerous times to do such. Knight brought up the rear, his lance draped over his shoulder. 'Thou should only hopeth that the knave hasn't reached the core yet!' he called ahead to Reality. 'Well if someone had done their job of neutralizing him at the gate...we wouldn't be in this situation!!' Reality growled, slightly directing his glare back at one individual Illusion had strapped his two huge rifles across the back of his yellow Zoot Suit. He held the brim of his yellow fedora to keep it from blowing off at the speed they ran. 'Hey!! If you had just let me do my usual job instead of making me use these crappy rifles, then #3-9 would have been done a long time ago!!' 'We need him neutralized...not driven insane.' Angelica replied from in front of him. Illusion stuck his tongue out at her as they ran. 'And keep your tongue in your mouth. Save it for later...preferrably for Spice.' The red mini-skirted vixen gave a cold stare at Angelica from behind Illusion. 'I'll ignore that remark, Angelica!' 'And for your kindness, I'll ignore you.' Angelica was really curt with them at this point. Chitchat was irrelevant. She sighed as they cleared another couple of floors. 'Simmer down, guys! We're wont be at Sub Basement Omega for a few more floors! Get ready and be on guard! By the looks of things, we may have to get rough with #3-9!' he snatched a phone from his jacket and pressed a button. -*-*-*- A load of technical equipment, monitors, and advanced computers were crammed into a small room. They all surrounded this wide desk, where at least 5-6 monitors sat before someone lounged back in a desk chair. His black boots were propped up on the table, his long black vinyl covered legs stretched out one over the other. His black nylon covered arms were over his stomach, while a black bandana was draped over the man's face. Suddenly a phone rang. After a few rings, the man stirred. With a groan, he removed the bandana, revealing jet black hair hanging messily over his face and black sunglasses. He shoved documents, disks and CDs out of the way as he searched for the ringing device. Then he plucked the phone out from the mess on his desk and held it to his ear. 'Yeah...' he answered lazily. -*-*-*- 'Matrix! Wake your @ss up and lock down the core!! Subject #3-9 is making a beeline straight for your area!!' -*-*-*- His brow wrinkled...he was a bit confused. 'Subject #3-9?' he questioned. 'I thought he was banned from Japan...how'd he get back here?' -*-*-*- 'That's besides the point, Matrix!' Angelica now had the phone to her ear. ' Are you going to tell him that?? You just make sure that the security perimeters are set around you and the core. Gather all the data on disk and delete the hard copies!' -*-*-*- Matrix scratched his head with his black nails. 'The data? I doubt that he's coming all the way down here for some data on Universal Negative and Positive Energy.' -*-*-*- 'He's after Subject #3-6, moron!!!' Illusion yelled into the phone angrily. -*-*-*- 'Oh...her...why didn't you say so?' Matrix stood up slowly and began clearing off his desk. He tossed a few papers aside before he finally unearthed his cordless keyboard. Then he pulled out a palm pilot from his desk and began downloading information remotely from his hard drive. 'Don't sweat it, Illusion. I'm downloading the data as we speak. I'm even getting those nude pics of Spice I promised you in the shower.' -*-*-*- 'In the shower???' Spice replied with a tint of surprised anger in her voice. 'You little perv!' -*-*-*- 'Oh...well...um...there's nothing to be ashamed of, Spice.' boy, was he caught in a situation. Of course after this situation is over, I'd be glad to help you find that...itch...' -*-*-*- 'Thou shant be scratching anything, squire! Thou maketh sure that thine area is secure, lest you meeteth with the sharp end of my lance!' And with that said, he hung up the phone, passing it back up the line to Reality. He pocketed it and pressed on down the stairwell. -*-*-*- Matrix looked at the phone before setting it down. 'Geez...what a grump.' Typing upon the keyboard, he brought up an image on the screen. It was a camera feed of a hallway, most likely in front of the area he sat in. It was empty. On the next monitor he brought up another camera feed, showing a darkly lit room. In the center of that room stood a capsule of sorts with wires and cables connected to it. On the front of the capsule was a label reading 'Subject # 3-6'. Around the top of the capsule was a small window, allowing vision inside. Matrix watched as a thick bubble gurgled up before the window. Turning back to the other monitor, he was just in time to see the camera crushed by fate's hand. Gasping, he jumped up and ran for the door. Too late though...the thick gate flew open from one boot of the god. There was no time for protest or mercy pleas as Akiyama seized him up by his neck and held him in the air. In shock, he did nothing but watch as the god roared in his face. Blood dripped down his neck...moist and warm from the stained hand of Akiyama. It looked like the end for him. Then the NVG brought him around to the back of the room, where a scanning system sat. Palming the back of his skull, he pressed his face to the optical scanner. Then he snatched the sunglasses from his face, allowing the scanner to do its work. 'SECURITY PERIMETER A CLEARED.' a robotic voice called out. Akiyama then pressed his hand against the DNA scanner...and soon the sensor reported clearance. The door before them hissed open, steam from the cracks as it slid into its crevices. Akiyama dragged Matrix into the room and stood before the lone metallic capsule seen on the monitor before. He reached forward and touched the label where 'Subject #3-6' read. Then he snatched his hand back when a jolt of electricity shot out at him. He growled as he looked around. 'You are not classified to touch the capsule!' a female robotic voice called out. It sounded youthful, with a bit of warning in it's voice...maybe programmed to sound like that. Akiyama glared down at Matrix, pulling him up to his feet and holding him before the capsule. 'Hey, Hoshimi, baby.' he called out in a comforting voice. 'Hello, Matrix.' the program seemed to take a lighter response to him. 'What is going on?' 'Nothing, Hoshimi...could you please open the capsule for me? I need to check on Subject #3-6.' 'Whatever you wish, Matrix.' And with that through, motion was set. More steam hissing...more automated unlocking...and draining of the capsule. Soon the front door flipped open, steam flowing out like a sauna. Akiyama waved the cold white mist out of his way and stared at the contents. She was on the brink of consciousness... her slender legs, the curve of her hips to the firmness of her chest...fully developed physically as a grown woman. Her long black and ice blue hair fell down to her knees from quite possibly years of growth. She was totally nude, if that wasn't mentioned before. Matrix stared with his mouth gaped open. 'Dude...the Divine Prophetess is hot...' With that inappropriate remark aside, Akiyama reached into the capsule and pulled her out. He cradled her in his arms, ignoring her appearance. He shook her a bit, rousing her to consciousness. Her arms were around his neck, beginning to cling to him like a child. She then looked up into his eyes... Matrix stared on at them. They both stared at each other for a good while. It was as if they were talking...but without words... Then she turned towards him...and he became lost in the solid ice blue that were her eyes. Clarity enveloped him... The Executives rounded the corner, finding the door off its hinges. Reality cursed as they barged into Matrix's lab and found the door leading to the core of the building wide open! 'D@mn it!!' Reality and Angelica stepped into the core, the other three Executives behind them. They saw nothing but the back of the god, blocking anything in front of him save the capsule. '3-9!!!' Reality yelled, pointing his electrified nightstick at Akiyama's back. 'Drop 3-6 and surrender!! Akiyama turned towards them, a glare on his face that could frighten blind people. The woman labeled Subject #3-6 in his arms held a identical gaze. Behind them, Matrix stared on in amazement. 'You do not possess the role to order the god...learn your place...and prepare for enlightenment.' The voice came from all around them, in their ears and in their minds. It was not the computer Hoshimi...it was a more familiar voice...one Akiyama had been hearing for a long time now... 'So says the Divine Prophetess.' Before Reality can respond, a black abyss opens below Akiyama, #3-6, and Matrix. They all disappeared into the Void before anyone could respond...
