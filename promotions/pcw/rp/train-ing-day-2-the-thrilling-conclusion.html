Train-Ing Day #2 (The Thrilling Conclusion!)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was in the midst of an intense training program, whipping The Pharaoh’s bodyguards back into shape.His plan?Simply put, it involved Flaymin instructing the hopeless guards on how to tackle oncoming trains. Well the time has come.The bodyguards are ready for the test…And the steaming train is well on its way…. Toooot!!! Toooot!!!!! The ending of the last episode: [Suddenly in the distance Flaymin notices some shooting steam in the air.Slowly, the undeniable figure of a train begins to appear….. coming closer towards the group- and more importantly young Stanley who stands on the train tracks, shaking in fear with his knees knocking repeatedly. Flaymin steps off the track and yells out some final words of wisdo… well words of idiocy actually, back at Stanley] Flaymin: Remember kid, just relax and go with the flow. Oh and don’t bend your neck like that or you’ll probably get a scratch on it. [Stanley straightens up his neck to avoid possible ‘scratching’.He then widens his arms into the ‘bearhug’ position and waits for the train. Gradually the train grows closer and closer…. CHUGGA! CHUGGA! CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [Stanley gulps in fear one last time as the train closes to within 20 metres of him….] CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [The chugging sound begins to heighten and the roaring vibrations of the train intensify, as the train is suddenly only a few metres away from Stanley…] CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [Stanley closes his eyes and grits his teeth expecting the oncoming train to blast right over the top of him.However, the train rushes past…. On the adjacent train track, thus totally missing Stanley. Flaymin rushes over flapping his arms in the air] Flaymin: YOU’RE STANDING ON THE WRONG TRACK!!!JUMP IN FRONT OF THE TRAIN!! [Stanley tries to dart in between the rapidly moving train carriages but keeps pulling himself back in fear. Fighting off all the flying gusts of dirt and dust Flaymin jumps onto the train track and picks Stanley up by the back of his shirt before launching him into the path of the monstrous train.Stanley is elevated into the air and then….!!!SMACK!!!! His body hits the side of the carriage and bounces off it- back onto the grass. Stanley grabs his broken nose and tries to wipe away the gushing blood. Flaymin furiously rushes over to check up on Stanley] Flaymin: What the hell was all that about?!?!?I told you to jump in front of the train and instead you end up humping the side of carriage! [Stanley gets up and grabs his throbbing head] Stanley: Sorry Boss, please do not tell The Pharaoh of my failure.He will have me booted out of the squad! [Flaymin strokes his chin before nodding his head agreeably] Flaymin: Alright, I’ll let you off. [Flaymin turns back around to face the rest of the crew] Flaymin: Right, who’s next? [The other guards all shake their heads hesitantly] Flaymin: You, you’ll do fine! [Flaymin yanks out the infamous ‘Fake-It Harry’ from the group] Fake-It Harry: Please!Please no! I have gingivitis! Flaymin: Is that anything like Hepatitis? Fake-It Harry: Actually its more like arthritis….. Flaymin: Close enough, now get over here! [Harry reluctantly steps onto the train track besides Flaymin] Flaymin: Right, now you’ve been paying attention haven’t you? [Harry grabs his ear and winces] Fake-It Harry: I’m sorry I have a hearing disorder! [Flaymin grabs Harry by the ears] Flaymin: Listen you little pussy, if you don’t stop creating diversions I’m REALLY gonna tear your ear out of its socket.Now get on the damn track and wait for the train!! [Harry steps onto the Train Track and starts shivering in fear. Meanwhile, Flaymin raises his binoculars to his eyes in order to spot any oncoming trains. A moment later he notices a large one in the distance] Flaymin: One…. Two…. Three…. Four…. Five…. Six…. Seven…. Eight.Eight Carriages!!This should be good! [Harry gulps in fear as the train slowly progresses towards him. As the train steadily approaches Harry, he turns back to face Flaymin] Fake-It Harry: Sir!I think there is a plumbing problem!!! [Flaymin looks down at Harry’s feet where a little, yellow puddle of urine has suddenly appeared] Flaymin: Hey, you’re not getting in my car like that!I just had it steam-cleaned last week! Harry: Do you think I could get off the train tracks and put on a clean pair of trousers? Flaymin: Ha!Stay there and tackle the freakin’ train! [That indistinguishable smell of a rampaging train suddenly fills the air (overpowering that rotten stench of fresh urine).The train zooms along the track, closing in fast on Harry. Harry’s eyes boggle open and without any further thought, he decides to turn and run. Harry sprints along the train track as fast as his piss-drenched little feet will take him. Meanwhile, Flaymin watches on interest while the other guards start bumbling in hysteria] Phillip: Why the hell is he running ALONG the train track, when he can just step off it and onto the grass? Clifford: And sir, how can you let him runaway like that!?! Flaymin: Guys, relax.He’s not running away, he’s just running all the way back there to get a good lead up.Then he’ll turn around and charge back at the train- kinda like how a plane uses a runway. Clifford: He doesn’t seem to be stopping…. Phillip: And why is he trying to jump over that barbed wire fence? Flaymin: Hmmm, this doesn’t seem right.Phillip, go to my tractor and fetch my blowtorch. We’re going to have to teach Harry a lesson. [Flaymin casually steps onto the train track and lifts his binoculars back up to his eyes.He scans far and wide in search of the fleeing Harry but is unable to locate him.So he walks further along the train track, still searching through the binoculars. Suddenly he hears a noise….. CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA! Flaymin: Uh oh…. [Flaymin’s smile quickly turns upside down.His eyes boggle open and he slowly turns around] Flaymin: OH SHIT!!! [A coal train unexpectedly appears directly behind Flaymin Aymin. Flaymin drops his binoculars and begins dashing for dear life. In the meantime, the rest of the guards watch on in enjoyment] Clifford: Where does he think he’s going? Horacio: See that big train behind him? Clifford: Yeah. Horacio: Well its three seconds away from planting Flaymin into the train tracks… permanently. Clifford: Oh. Phillip: No, no, no- He’s just doing a run up like Harry! [Back on the train track, Flaymin continues to run as fast as humanly possible as the train closes in fast. Flaymin turns around and notices the train is suddenly only a few metres away.But with his attention focussed on the train he fails to take notice of a loose plank in the train track.Flaymin trips over the plank, causing it to detach from the tracks and fly into the air while Flaymin himself comes crashing down hard on a nearby grass patch. Meanwhile, the train continues to cruise along the tracks- until it abruptly hits the void where the loose plank used to be.The train unexpectedly dips into the ground causing it to jerk forward.Inside the train, the driver immediately slams on the brakes which leads to the train lunging forward even further. Without notice, the train begins to shake from side to side until…… !!!!CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH!!!!The Coal Train topples over and lands on the grass below, causing the coal to scatter everywhere.The glass shatters and the carriages all dismantle from one another thus wreaking even more havoc. Still in shock, Flaymin gets up off the ground and wipes all the soot off his clothes.He looks at all the carnage and grins] Flaymin: Oh my god, I just tackled a train!!!!! [The other bodyguards rush up to Flaymin in awe] Phillip: Boss, that was great!!! Horacio: You gotta show me how to do that!! Flaymin: Wait ‘til the Insurance Company hears about this screwup- they’re gonna shit themselves!!! Which reminds me…. Where the hell did Harry go? [Scene fades to black]
