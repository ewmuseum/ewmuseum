BorroW-A-National Hero and Team Portugal Take on the Smelly Frenchies and Zinedine Zidane

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/3/02 Tales of the Golden Toe: The Golden Heart as passed and the Golden Toe has come back again. The Golden Toe is the old Juan Borro, the fun loving foreign soccer player who did his all to help others. Through the tales of the Golden Toe Juan gives us insight into present experiences with his life and duties and encounters with people. The Golden Toe has seen his calling, and that calling is to do good be good, and be a power for good. No force will stop Juan’s GOAL~! Time: 6:23 P.M.Location: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Juan’s Laboratory/ His Apartment “I’m Up Up and Oh shit there’s a tree…BOOM!” Part 1 Lately we’ve seen our national hero trying to solve the great mystery of El Romantico, A mystery that is still yet to be answered. Juan knows the answer will come along soon enough, but for now he won’t stress over it. Juan is preparing early for his Pay-Per View match up against his arch nemesis Rent-A-Hero. It’s not like RAH is a bad guy, Juan actually thinks of him as a good wholesome person, but that doesn’t mean he’ll go easy on him at all. Juan and RAH are both without a doubt going to go all out, trying to produce the best ever Cruiserweight Championship match ever. In the long run Juan of course will win, because we all know Juan always goes for the GOOOOOOOOAAAAAAALLLLL~! Their could have been many matches and stipulations the two could choose from, but they decided to keep it as a good old one fall match up. Not because it’s the simplest, but it’s a type of match that they can use to teach the upcoming cruiserweight of this federation. Juan and RAH are probably up there with some of the best Cruiserweight ever along with Miyagawa, Kid Wonder, etc. In less then two weeks, Juan will show why he deserves the title of Best Ever Cruiserweight in PCW. In a plan of preparation for Juan, he’ll be trying something a little different. Usually wrestlers like to train in gyms with personal trainers and other kinds of strength building methods. But for this PPV, Juan is not following that path. If you look at past PPV’s where Juan has done the ordinary training, it hasn’t paid off. Juan Borro has NEVER won a PPV match up, but at Supremacy he hopes to capture his first. He’s going to do this by actually becoming his opponent. YES! I SAID BECOMING HIS OPPONENT. Juan’s plan to success is to turn into RAH. He figures if he acts like RAH, he’ll already know what to expect. This means you should all not be surprised if you see Juan using RAH’s own moves against him. Just goes to show how versatile of an athlete Juan is. Let’s check in to the Laboratory where BorroW-A-National Hero is standing by with his trusty sidekick and butler Furious FigoFigo. Furious Figo: I’m afraid there’s a problem, sir Borro! BorroW-A-National Hero draws his attention to his sidekick Figo who is standing in front of the mega multatron with a nervous look on his face. BorroW-A-National Hero: What is it Furious Figo, is their problems in grace land. Is a communist party overthrowing Portugal? Are aliens invading? Are the wonderful people Portonite in jeopardy. Oh please tell me Furious Figo what is wrong? Furious Figo: I’m sorry Juan, but it’s something of greater proportion then any of those. And no it isn’t the distribution of the JJ Bear. It’s even worse than that. BorroW-A-National Hero: BLASHPHEMY! Nothing could possibly be worse than that. I’m afraid I won’t be able to handle this. Furious Figo: I’m sorry to do this Juan, but I have to. The scene goes into slow motion as Furious Figo grabs a bottle of some type and when he turns around and shows it to Juan it’s completely empty. Juan’s mouth drops in horror, as the camera zooms in at the last drop of liquid dripped from the bottle. BorroW-A-National Hero: NO! We’re out of FANTA! How could this be, I thought I bought more last week. What’s a life of crime fighting without the official beverage of YOUR National Hero? Furious Figo: I don’t know Juan, but it’s not a world I want to live in. Suddenly, Juan’s head starts aching. Furious Figo: Is their something wrong? BorroW-A-National Hero: Yes! My Soccer sense is tingling! My soccer sense is tingling! Furious Figo: What do you see? BorroW-A-National Hero concentrates long and hard as different images pop into his head one after another. BorroW-A-National Hero: Oh no! This is a great travesty. Somebody has let the French national team in here. And it’s turning into a kill frenzy. I see people falling to the ground holding their noses. Now I see a picture in my head of Zinedine Zidane in a tank top. Furious Figo: No not a tanktop! BorroW-A-National Hero: Yes! And he isn’t wearing deodorant. The camera then zooms into headshots of both Figo and Juan as they are seen straining their faces with an amazed look. BorroW-A-National Hero: You know with this means? Furious Figo: I sure do. This is a mission for TEAM PORTUGAL. Figo and Juan both grab some vodka and drink it. They then begin an OLEY OLEY OLEY OLEY chant. The chant gets louder and louder, until it echoes throughout the community. Then, out of nowhere come some of the Portuguese National Soccer team members. We see Cunning Couto with his deadly hair, followed by Captain Costa who has the power of lodging soccer balls from his rear end, and finally Pretty Boy Pinto who blinds people with his sparkling white teeth. BorroW-A-National Hero: Hello Team Portugal. I’m here to warn you about our next mission. This can be extremely unsafe. It seems the French national team led by Zidane are parading through the streets leaving people unconscious in their path. All of Portugal is in our hands. If we don’t get some deodorant on Zidane, the consequences can be unbearable. Cunning Couto: How are we supposed to avoid the smell of Zidane? BorroW-A-National Hero stands and ponders for a few moments before he thinks of something and a light bulb appears over his head. Furious Figo: Holy Shit! How’d you get that light bulb over your head? BorroW-A-National Hero: I didn’t the thing just hangs low from the ceiling. The men all stare up and notice the cord it’s connected to. BorroW-A-National Hero: guys I’ve come up with the perfect protection for our noses? Pretty Boy Pinto: What is it? BorroW-A-National Hero grabs a box and takes our five objects that appear to be jockstraps. BorroW-A-National Hero: Wear these over your faces to protect yourself from the smell. These are the official Alvo Montoya jockstraps I received at my party. They have so much cologne and other things sprayed in them along with the occasional alcohol and seamen that the smell of Zidane has no chance of seeking through. Captain Costa: This is great. Captain Costa seems to be enjoying it more than the others until he is slapped aside his head and has the jockstrap taken away from him. BorroW-A-National Hero: Don’t abuse your privileges. Other than that let’s get set to go boys. Team Portugal gathers together and goes outside, they then charge down and screams the same words. We’re UP UP AND AWAY………. Two seconds later all of the men go tumbling to the ground face first. BorroW-A-National Hero: Oh yea, we can’t fly. I know what to do though. Furious Figo, get the booty pack. Costa take position and when I say go, you know what to do. Everyone straps on their booty packs. BorroW-A-National Hero: Let’s try this again. We’re Up Up and Away. GO! Captain Costa kicks each man straight in the ass as they go flying into the sky. All men are set off soaring above the clouds. Meanwhile, back on the street Costa is left alone. Captain Costa: Umm guys, whose going to kick me. Captain Costa takes a few moments of thinking before he attempts to kick his own ass. Eventually is pays off and he’s seen catching up with the rest. The camera zooms into BorroW-A-National Hero who sends out a quick message to his billions and kuzillions and fratillions of fans out there. BorroW-A-National Hero: Everyone make sure to tune into Part 2, to check out if you’re favorite National Hero can defeat Zinedine Zidane. Same BorroW-a-Cable Access Network, same BorroW-A-Time.
