Let The Games Begin

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/1/02 After seeing the reaction of some of the PCW wrestlers to Rush’s death, we may soon find out what is happening, if anything in that car. However, in the mean time, Rush still takes a breaks from his own promos. No one but Rush knows the true story, which means his captors may well get off with no punishment if Rush is dead. Strangely enough, they may have gotten more punishment for kidnapping then for murder. Don’t think it was anything else. Rush’s death was a murder, no matter the circumstances. His kidnappers placed him into a situation where death was almost certain, no matter what he did. The kidnappers have now gone unnoticed though, and if they’re to punished, then Rush must’ve survived, but we will find out later whether the Crunk as He!l God made it through the terrible accident. Now, we must see what the plans of those responsible for Rush’s kidnapping are. The scene opens up on an old-looking stone house. This house has been seen before, and should be familiar by now. This is the headquarters of Rush’s captors. This is where Rush was brought before he made his daring escape, which ended up in his own death. The architecture of the building is Greek, and the sun beats down on the house heavily in the noontime, as if it knows what goes on inside. As if can see inside of their souls. As if the sun is trying to punish them… Inside of the house is a new sight however. Screams are heard from inside of the room, sounding like those of a woman, who has been greatly frightened. These continue for a couple of minutes, until finally they turn into quieter sobs that cease to be as loud as the time passes. The shot goes to inside the room. It is very decoratively furnished, with silk curtains and lots of large furniture, including a king-sized bed. On the bed, the sheets are moving with a lot of speed and motion. From underneath the sheets, a familiar face can be seen popping out from underneath. Damien, one of the kidnappers is in the bed. Also, coming from the bed is the sobs of the woman heard earlier. As the shot pans in to the strange scene, the woman can be seen in front of Damien, being the unwanted recipient of Damien’s love. With each movement of Damien’s the cries sound out a little bit loud, but the smack of a hand against flesh can be heard with that. It can be seen now that the smack is Damien’s hand against the woman’s face. Finally, Damien gets up, facing away from the camera, which is now focused on another part of the room, but the sounds of the room can still be heard. The girl begins to cry and flops down on the floor. Damien yells at her a few times, but she doesn’t get up. All that’s heard next is a click. For a long time, there is silence. Then, Damien begins to laugh an evil laugh and the sound of a gun firing is heard, resounding throughout the building. The camera goes back to Damien, who has the gun in his own hand. He looks at the gun and the bloody corpse of his rape victim on the ground, and strangely, points the gun at his own head. However, he gets a fiery look in his eyes, and steps out of the camera’s view, but then the sound of a door opening and closing is heard. The mysterious gun-wielding figure leaves the corpse of the young girl, entrails from her brain splattered all over her naked body. The shot switches to another bedroom, though this one is much more peaceful. Inside the bed there is a figure, whose face is turned away from the camera. The sound of an opening and closing door is very faint, but it can’t be heard in the silence of this bedroom. Into the room walks the crazed, gun-wielding Damien. He stands about ten feet away from the bed, and he points the gun at the figure in the bed as he speaks in a grave voice, waving the gun about with each word. Damien: Oh Lucifer. Master, as they call you. Father, as I call you. This is a very interesting finding that Damien is the son of the Master, who is named after the devil himself. Damien begins to grin as the figure tries to escape from under the sheets. Damien: Oh I see that my servants, My servants, already did you the favor of not having to move. You needn’t worry either, because I will do what you couldn’t do while you were alive after you die. Of course, you might be thinking now about some last way to beg or plead to save yourself. But there is no way for you to escape. Your servants, formerly, who you treated like garbage for years, none of them even want to save your sorry soul. So you see, there’s no point in life if you don’t have friends, isn’t that right father? The figure is now trying ferociously to escape from under the sheets, but he has been tied, which was the servants did their “favor” for him already. Damien just laughs at the feeble attempts, which are just getting the man more tangled into the sheets. Damien: Father, I believe there are many people who you’ve killed in your life. You always used the pathetic way to kill, by sneaking around and never even speaking to your victims. However, I’m a new type of a leader, a new type of killer. Good-bye father, say hi to the devil for me. With this, Damien raises the pistol and fires 5 shots right into his father, each shot splattering blood on the white sheets. Each shot brings a high-pitched scream from behind his father’s gagged mouth. Damien, very pleased with this, pulls back the covers to see the end result of his work. Damien: But?! How?! Damien looks at the body of his partner in kidnapping, whose name we never learn. However, a look of fear is frozen into his face for the rest of time, his eyes wide. 5 perfectly circular bullet holes are spread throughout his abdomen, blood oozing slowly out of each wound. Damien clenches his fists and begins to punch the wall with all his might, slowly embedding the imprint of his fist with each shot. However, the shot switches to outside as this is happening to show another event that is going on at this very moment. Outside, in the forest behind the house, showing a black clad cloaked figure hurrying through the trees, barely noticeable in the dark forest. He stops once, in a small shaded clearing, under the cover of a large knobbed oak tree that hangs very far out on all sides. This man whispers to himself, but somehow his voice echoes throughout everywhere in the forest. Master: Son, you are a ruthless killer. However, you learned from the Master, and until you can kill me, I am just that. I know what you will do now, and I will do everything in my power to stop you. My life is no longer worth anything, and death will be peace for me. You will be my last victim. You will learn how a true killer brings death. This is just a game of Cat and Mouse, Damien. And you were bad at games as a child, you never could beat me. You never will beat me… The cloaked man now hurries through the clearing back into almost invisibility in the forest and the movement can be seen until he clears a small hill, leaving the clearing empty. No sound is heard, except the buzz of locusts and the chirping of birds. Two very contrasting sounds with the scenes we have witnessed so far. And what we are about to see. The shot switches to a large office room, the same one seen when Rush was first brought to this dreadful place. Now, however, it is differently finished. All around the room are paintings of corpses. A portrait of Damien stands on the far wall, amongst the photos of the dead. The shelves that were used for storing papers inside manila folders now hold many weapons. Quite a few of the servants who formerly served Damien’s father now stand in front of him, silent and loyal. Damien paces irritably in front of the five men. Damien: You all saw the corpse of dear Rasby. That was what my father was all about. He was a man who didn’t care for anyone but himself, and you all know that. He only cared about his personal gain. Now, I have a different policy. I am more ruthless than that old bag, but I care about each one of you. You will be my five loyal horsemen, not like second tier workers. The men all nod and smile at this announcement, and Damien turns toward the wall and smiles evilly, then turns again to address the five men. Damien: Each one of you will search out that man who dares call himself a God, Rush Mason. I will finish what I am afraid my father didn’t. First Man: Sir, wasn’t he killed in that car crash? Why would we have to kill him if he’s already dead? Damien: Who knows if he is dead? In any case, I want him back here, dead or alive. So, get to it. Take the best five vehicles you can, but the first person back here gets one hundred thousand dollars and will become my personal bounty hunter. The five men run and push to get through the door as Damien leans back on the desk and laughs. Damien: They will pick off each one but the strongest, and then I will have one personal bounty hunter, and not have to deal with those pathetic weaklings. This will be a game for them, and Mason will die another death, if he has been granted a second chance at life. Let the game begin… Damien sits down at the desk and begins typing away, as Rush’s handler does for his next RP for March Madness, but the screen fades to black…
