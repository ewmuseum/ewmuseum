Just So Damn Confusing (RO #1 - Repost From Boards)

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-29-2002 The call, was most definitely a shock to me. I had never expected this, nor had I ever expected to be one of the chosen few. With everything I say, and all the hatred I spout towards Jamar, and Sean, they fools have actually chosen me to represent their organization against Prime Time Championship Wrestling. Slowly, I placed the phone down on the receiver, and leaned back in my chair, trying to gather my thoughts on, why. Why I was chosen? Why did the expect me to function with them? Why did they think I would actually care? It was all so damn confusing. 'What's wrong, Cianan?' The light voice, of my darling crossed the room, into my ear, and I turned my head, looking towards the one, I had accepted into my life. The once, mother of my child: Akiko Hiroshima. I gave her an uneasy smile, as I still tried to sort out the details in my mind. Something just didn't feel right about all of this. 'Well, I just got a call from some PURE officials.' I started slowly, trying to chose my words carefully. To be honest, Akiko's actually had some part in PCW, one of her best friends, Savanda Carlson, a.k.a. Lady Cat, had been a PCW commissioner for a small time. And, well, let's just say that no one involved in this story has any love lost for PCW. She tilted her head to the side, her black hair cascading like a raven colored waterfall down past her ear, hanging with a beautiful shine. She was the picture of beauty to me. 'I'm participating in a PCW Pay Per View.' I said slowly, thinking it better to just say it, then drawing it out, and having her kick my ass, despite how fun it would be. The slow raven brow quirked. I gave a small nod, 'They apparently want me to defend PURE's honor, seeing as PCW is arriving on their turf. It's basically a big pissing contest between Sean and Xavier Kannon, that I'm being dragged into.' She bit her lip lightly, 'So, Cianan. What are you to do?' I smiled, despite all her time in America, and wrestling for CCW, she still sometimes struggled with English. She hated when I teased her about it. 'Well,' I started slowly, rubbing my cheek. It had been a while since I have shaved, and there was a light fuzz there. I had been so busy training for PURE, and to take out my aggression, and hopefully work some things out of my system, that I was losing a bit of sleep. That and I've always rather wondered what I'd look like with a bit of gristle on my face. 'I don't know.' 'What do you mean you don't know?' Akiko frowned, and slapped me on the arm, causing me to wince in reflex. 'You go, and kick they asses!' 'Their asses,' I corrected, trying not to sound snotty about it. 'Yes, that what I meant!' 'Right, well.' I hesitated, this was definitely something to think about. I disliked how PURE was going, but I also hated PCW. I REALLY hated PCW. Since my time in CCW, where we always tried to get the best of them, when they stole our talent, I felt it. It seemed like a friendly rivalry, but I wasn't just the talent, I was talent and management at CCW. I knew, and was affected more by this then most on both rosters. I hated PCW, and it wasn't really that much of a secret. 'I just don't know, it's on their turf, it's their PPV. I wouldn't be surprised if Kannon has really stocked the 'Mystery Fifth Person.' it will probably be someone like Akiyama.' I snorted, in a bit of disgust. She just stared at me. 'What?' I blinked, and looked back at her, frowning. She continued to stare, there was a certain fire in her eyes that I had only seen a few times. I blinked, now growing a bit nervous, this is the look that she gave Sebastian Toomes, after he called her 'Just a girl.' She kicked his ass after that, and took his Independent Title. 'I can not believe you say that.' She said slowly, a bit of disbelief in her voice. 'You can not believe I said what?' Her glare was heavy, almost paralyzing, it was almost hard to believe it came from the beautiful brown hues from before. 'Are you still same man from before?' She stalked forward a few steps. 'You! Being afraid of PCW? Big Bad PCW? Few weeks ago, you say that you take on anyone from PURE, now you afraid?' 'Of course not.' I snorted, it was quite the insult to me. 'Then why not do it? Why not take on PCW?' 'You want me to Akiko?' I damn near roared, as I rose from my seat, glaring right back at my ladylove. 'You want me to fight? Fine, I’ll fight. I'll fucking rip them apart, would that make you happy?' 'Yes, it would.' She said solemnly, 'You make me proud?' 'Of course,” I said, as I walked down, and grabbed my jacket. She blinked, then started after me, smacking me on the back of my head. 'Where you think you go?' She demanded rather forcefully. 'To the gym,' I smiled sweetly. 'Oh no! You train with me!' Again, she demanded, and I knew there was no talking her out of it. Martial Arts practice it was, now to find my sparring gear, which was somewhere in the house. This was going to be a pain in the ass. And this was only the beginning, but how little did I actually know it would be. But, as everything, all pains start small and work into sores, and throbs, and pains, usually in the ass, or neck. To be continued…
