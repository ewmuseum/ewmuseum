'...Don't waste our time, b-tch.'

Date: not entered We never thought we’d be back here again. These rusted ruins, decayed and molded with age since most likely the dawn of time. We remember spending a portion of our life in this desecrated domain…a place of darkness…a place of evil… The Void Springs. Sonata was close to me, her first time here, probably be her last as well. Her thick black heels the only sound being made as we trekked through those damp halls of night, onyx the composed material in the floors, walls, and ceilings. It was a spectacle nonetheless…any archeologist would dream of finding such ruins. Ruins… That’s all we wanted really…wanted left of our opposition. Ruins… We could never question our hatred for anyone standing across from us in that ring. It was an infuriating rage that just dwelled within us…waiting to be unleashed on the poor fool willing to face us one on one. It wasn’t even like we had a personal vendetta against them or anything…we just wanted to see them defeated, their confidence in their own ability crushed, their self value deemed worthless… their bodies in pain… Wonderful appearances… Some would call us a bully for wanting to see people in pain so much. That…is most certainly not the case at all. We deal out the pain to whomever deserves it for sins committed… because leaving retribution to destiny and fate would be like leaving PCW in the hands of Xavier Kannon to run… It would die… And for his futility to be a good provider of PCW…he will die… But all in given time…as he’s shelling out recycled material he calls a feud with Krayzie Howard. His impending doom shows with his lack of creativity and talent… you saw the process begin when he tried to destroy us a few months back. He is a constant pest… but we will not exterminate him. His time is short anyways. He has been whittled down to nothing…and that’s where he shall fade… Nothing… “…Void…” Symphony looked back at us, a knowing smile on her black-lipped face. Looking at her was like looking at a Gothic Sonata, same face, same body, same voice…but Symphony was a lot darker than her twin. Wearing tight black pants and a black halter top with yellow sleeves and black boots, you saw the fashion sense ran in the family between the two of them. Sonata clutched on our arm tighter, quite fearful of the unknown. “What’s she mean?” she asked softly. She got no answer as we took our arm back and proceeded down the dark hallway. Her boots could be heard shuffling behind Symphony and us… she was always a step behind. We like the thought floating around backstage that we have fallen behind in the new pace PCW is taking. New talent arising almost every show…it seems in Howard’s control he couldn’t change the idea of the revolving door policy PCW has for new talent. Yeah, there’s open people available for us to throw a spot of focus on…pick a fight with…but they’re rookies. We don’t give a sh-t to start something we can’t finish, especially since their ass will be back in the indies right after the first ADA we dish out. Overweighter? Matt Jackson? Hailey Hilton? Dorn Tracer? F-ck are they?! They’re sh-t…they’re nothing… We…we’re the closest thing wrestling has to a god. Closest thing this world has to a god… “It’s right down these stairs through the door.” Symphony motioned forward with a flick of her hand. We followed her down the stairwell, careful for any sort of ambush possible. You had to watch your back nowadays…never knew where an attack would be coming from… A toothy grin came to our lips thinking on that situation… we went from popping Rent-A-B-tch to chasing off Tim Walden and then to dealing a Hand of Fate to Darkstar. We got busy that night…and rest assured that was just the beginning of the anger we got bundled up. Everyone pays…remember that. We grabbed Sonata by her wrist and pulled her ahead of us slowly. We wanted her to enter first…just in case. We knew they would have something brewing for this encounter…Symphony didn’t come to visit her sister without a reason. The Void Elements were waiting for us… and we were set to take the whole place down with us if so. There’d be a pile of bodies after this deal is done if things go sour…like that tournament’s set to go once we get in that ring. As soon as we entered the room, the door slammed shut behind us. The fists were already ready to swing. It was automatic…just like the sound of that bell. The big hall was just as we remembered it; black onyx pillars lined around the room, black marble reflecting those standing on it. The open plain in the middle of the room, where the Void Elements were gathered…Darkcyde, Chronic, Mystic, Soteria, X, Kiyone, and the Daughter of Darkness Hinageshi and her Void Assassin, Shinnosuke. All eyes fell on us, and they grew agitated. Last time we all got together…hell, we can’t remember. All we know is it was something real bad…and half of them got bashed because we were pissed. Chronic was always the ambassador. The bald Void Element stepped forward, his arms stretched, chest sticking out through his black mesh shirt. “Well if it isn’t the NEO VOID GOD HIMSELF!” he grinned at us as he closed in, eyes turning towards Sonata. “And who’s this Symphonic looking beauty we have here?” He got dealt a Hand. Symphony glared down at Chronic lying on the ground across the hall. “I had to admit, I saw that coming for him.” “Then you should’ve saved his dumb ass the pain.” We pointed at him, eyes snapping up at the rest of them. “He didn’t even do anything yet…let that be a lesson for you f-cks!” We had a knack of giving warnings…and we knew full-blown well that no one listened. People were just fools to take advice from us because they thought we were just saying stuff to scare them. We’re serious as sh-t when we say back off or we’ll make you thank whatever literary god you worship for the feet you stand on…because after we break them you’ll learn to appreciate verticality. Soteria rolled her eyes as she leaned against the black column, inhaling her cigarette and turning her head away from us. “By the Void…first Blitz, now you. Chronic can NOT get over with you two.” “What about BLITZ??” The temptress had our attention now. She looked back with a small smile on her face, blowing out smoke past her black lips. “Oh? You haven’t heard? You’re next door neighbors with the guy and you haven’t heard yet?” “Not like we speak to the little sh-t on friendly basis.” Kiyone glowered as she turned her head from the sixteen-year-old Hinageshi to us. “That ingrate Inphino has gone dark again.” “And what would you expect?” Symphony added in, walking ahead and sitting next to Soteria on the floor. “The universal balances have to realign, and with Akiyama getting himself a sense of honor and duty suddenly Blitz had no choice but to revert back to his repressed self.” Them’s be fighting words. “We have nothing…just like the rest of those fools have on us. They’re dumbasses for even wasting their breath cheering us out there…just because we turn our attention to a person the people hate doesn’t mean we’re any less evil.” “Nevertheless…” Soteria inhaled again, and blew out before finishing. “He’s gone rogue this round. No allegiances…not with us or the Aura.” “Could ya blame the f-cker?” we sneered at her, folding our arms across our chest. “That’ Void sh-t don’t go so well out there. It’s like living a life based on books no one’s read…by an author no one gives a sh-t about! You’re out there flappin ya gums off, and all for F-CK!! No one knows a sh-t about what the f-ck you’re saying! The people are f-ckin morons! They don’t know the Void from their brains, or the assholes they screw every night!” “I’d keep a civil tongue about the Void if I were you…” Oooh…we’ve waken the sleeping giant. The big Darkcyde rose up from his seat and walked towards us slowly…his size growing as he grew closer to us. We looked up at the over seven footer like he was stupid. Because of course…he was to approach us with the balls he had. “You think that dark ministry Void sh-t scares us? We’ve done it out there in the real world chump…and people look at you like you’re a f-cking fool!” A heavy growl came from within the heavyset fellow. “You’ve been disconnected from the Void so long that you have lost faith in it! You have NO right to call yourself the Neo Void God!” “Actually…we’ve got all right to claim that name.” We took a look at Sonata, who was backed against a pillar keeping her eyes on the rest of the Void Elements. She was scared, but that was inevitable. “Out of this entire bunch of f-ck-ups and Goths WE’RE the only one that actually made anything of ourselves! It was US that took YOU out before we even became a Void Element! And since we’ve been out there we’ve gone on to bring suffering and destruction to numerous lives…including waging war on this entire country! And you think YOU made US? WE MADE US!! And unless you want me to unmake the lives of you and your f-ckin friends here we suggest you back up out our face!!” “So what, you’re rough and tough now?? You think you’re the bad because you’ve accomplished all you have without the aid of the Void that made you? You know nothing of true power! And you will NEVER know anything of power until you submit yourself back to the Void!” “Convert some weak minded jackass who gives a sh-t. PCW’s full of the bastards.” we raised fists balled and grounded ourselves. “Right about now, unless we get some answers, we’re about to embed our foot in your Void!” A toss of his cloak exposed his muscular torso, his thick trunk of an arm pressed against his side. “Wage your effortless war on us…on your PCW…on the world if you will. You will get NOWHERE.” That was the line that got the fist connecting to his jaw. His head turned…but the man didn’t move. We took another swing, and Darkcyde just took the bastard. Now we were getting pissed. “GO DOWN MOTHER F-CKER!!” Three more punches went to his face, along with one into his midsection…but the giant never budged. His dark eyes just pierced into us, his black hair swaying as he turned his head back facing us again. “Now you know how it feels…facing the immovable monster.” “Monster OUR ASS!!” We Spiked that whore into the ground hard. Bastard didn’t see that one coming… getting up from the tackle, X and Mystic were racing towards us, feral growls emptying out from their lungs. We countered with a roar of our own before dropping X with a Judgment Blade kick. Mystic tried to come at us from behind, but got a kick in her face for the trouble as soon as our foot hit the ground. Symphony and Soteria watched from their seats as Shinnosuke lunged out with his blade… One swing was all he got. Like most people get on us in a match… We ducked and spun him around, picking him up and launching the f-ck across the room with the ADA. Kiyone was all that was left standing before us, the five footer glaring hard into our eyes. She had rage in her…but that was nothing compared to ours. A shove was enough to move her out the way…and locking a hand around the girl’s throat…Hinageshi was pulled into the air from her throne. “DON’T YOU DARE!!” Darkcyde growled as he rose to his feet. Sonata rushed to our side, trying to keep save as the rest of the Void Elements began to rise up again. They were strong, we’d give them that much. “We came here for a reason!! Your psychic said she could fix our mind, get rid of all the damn voices we hear! Unless all that was just like you, your Void, and the rest of the f-cks in PCW and just complete and utter bullsh-t! If you sh-ts can do what we want…let’s get down to business!!” We yanked the emotionless girl around and held her out before her followers. Hinageshi stared out, not even struggling at the choke set to end her life. We stared hard in her eyes, and from her emotionless we felt our urge for destruction rage up. “Otherwise…DON’T WASTE OUR F-CKIN TIME, BITCH!!” -Bitch- We looked into her solid black eyes…and saw many definitions of that name… Xavier Kannon… Teresa Tomas…Tim Walden…The End, Skylar Green, Sky Blue, or whatever the f-ck he calls himself now…Violence Jack…each and every one of those bitches thinking they could stop us in this tournament…each and every bitch that thought they could stop us PERIOD… We were here to fight…we were here to destroy. We’re here PERIOD…so either let us do our work…do something that will keep us busy…or stop wasting our time, you bitch. Symphony stood up, handing Soteria her cigarette back. Brushing her hands off on the back of her pants, she stepped forward and stared up at us. “Well come on…we haven’t got all night now.” -=-=- ToBeContinued -=-
