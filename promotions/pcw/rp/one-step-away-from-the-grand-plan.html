One Step Away from the Grand Plan...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Our Scene slowly begins to open outside what would appear to be an Office Block, its 'Grandness' being able to shine through the picture and captivate all of the business people sitting in their homes, watching this segment with intensified glory. The recording device slowly begins to pan across to the left of the building, bringing a very large sign into view, we see this sign as reading 'PCW' and immediately think of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling', but looking at some very small text underneath, we see the words, 'Pretty Cultural Women'. The design of the sign looks nothing like that of the Wrestling Company and it really would take an idiot to get mistaken by the two... but like in the case of our friends... I mean, enemies over at the 'WWE'... regardless of how much you look different, and regardless of that stupid agreement, there were supposed to be people who apparently got confused with the distinguishing of the companies. One would think... an honest mistake, but everyone else would think, 'Hehehe... what a diltweed.' The recording device begins to slide down the building front, coming to the main entrance before stopping for a few moments. We look on as women flood in and out of the establishment, some of them being Chinese, some being American... others being French and Russian. At the time of speaking, the only thing that made them a collective was their gender.. for those of you not reading this but skimming through, they're all women. The recording device moves again, pulling toward the source by looking down at the pavement, floating into the road before passing a veichle resembling that of an 'Army Jeep'. The view stops in its tracks and begins to move up to see what kinds of characters could be waiting within this 'Jeep'. As the slow moving device floats up, we hear the voices of our favorite singers, Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... collectivly known as the 'Pretty Cool Guys'. Sherwin : 'Wow... this feels like it could be the beginning of a great fight Ryan... can you smell the air? Can you just sense the excitement of doing something that could be essentially effective for all the Tag Teams around 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'? Can you... feel the vibes of all the people walking into the complex, and looking out of their little windows at us... wondering what kinds of mystical and magical mayhem will be wreaked... by the Greatest Tag Team that 'PCW' has ever been able to get its hands upon...' Sherwin was beginning to sound excited at the prospect of doing something that may very well change the kind of light that is spread upon the 'Tag Teams' of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling.' His voice had gradually reached a high-squeaky like point, once more, reflecting the natural aura of 'excitedness' that was following him.A small period of silence followed as the recording device got both Sherwin and Moore in view. The 'Cool Guys' had decided to lose their preferred rags of PoP Attire and gone for something a little more intense, a little more patriotic and... something of a little more to the idealistic nature of their esteemed cause. Ryan had decided to come to the location dress in a pair of baggy combat pants, a white vest and a Red Banana while Sherwin had made sure he was wearing baggy combat pants, an camo-style shirt with short sleeves and a black cap, this one not deplicting the image of the 'Pretty Cool Guys' insignia. Sherwin takes a pair of binoculars from the 'Glove Compartment' of the 'Jeep' and stands up from his seat, looking over at the complex, using the items to take a glander at all that is walking into the building. Sherwin : 'Hmmm... there seems to be a delicious taste of people walking into that building for the time being. I'm highly against finding somebody like the 'Heartbreakers' or the 'Cowabunga Connection' around these here parts... and to be honest, I'm damned happy we won't have contend with both of those... totally uncool teams before that huge #1 Contendership Tag match this Friday.' Ryan Moore scratches the back of his head with his right hand and looks up to Sherwin, a slight look of confusion and worry starting to form on his face. For those of you who didn't get time to come through the first part, Rich had just told Moore that he had planned something big for the time ahead... but didn't come through to telling Ryan what the exact details of the plan were. For all we knew, they could be something quite sadistic... quite mean... or generally, quite annoying. There was small talk of a concert being one of the sole purposes of this schduled 'adventure' although with the current circumstances, that wasn't even looking like a possibility. Moore : 'Erm... Rich, are you sure that this is even the right place? I mean, look at all the cute girls walking into the place. Unless I just happened to see Jaden through the masses, do you really think that this is where Mr Nelson and his friends do all the business stuff that they have to do?' Sherwin's facial expression showed a look of shock as Ryan Moore finished saying what he needed to. The amazement about the lack of support from his Tag Team partner was begining to show as the seconds flew past us. Anyone else around the world wouldn't have been surprized at the that Ryan is so 'unsure' about the real identity of the building... but with Sherwin's ego being bigger than all the states of America, its pretty impossible to tell him when a mistake's been made on his part. Sherwin softly shook his head from side to side, physically showing his dissappointment at the lack of support. Sherwin : 'Ryan Moore... I am not a very happy person when it comes to you trying to insult my supreme intelligence and intellectual advantage over you. Both you and I can see the huge letters on the side of this bloody building... 'P'... 'C'... and of course, 'W', now... in my mind, that spells out as 'PCW'... and please, correct me if I am wrong... but I thought that 'PCW' represented the company that we work for!' Rich was beginning to sound a little aggitated at the way that his friend was doubting everything that followed along with this plan. He looked around the immediate area for a few moments, trying to bring reflect his current emotional condition. The silence slowly began to hover over... sliding in like vultures at every given opportunity. Sherwin stepped out of the 'Jeep' and was all but ready to walk over to the building... when Ryan Moore's voice broke through the silence like a hot knife through butter. Moore : 'Rich... I'm sorry for doubting your obviously supreme intelligence, I didn't know that this was going to be THE actual place because all the people going in are Women... but the more I think about it now, they're probably holding a draft of female talent or something.' Ryan still sounded a little worried about the legitimacy of the location that they were at, but it was hidden enough to satisify the aggitation of Sherwin, who quickly hopped back into the 'Jeep' and pointed over to the 'PCW' building... which, if you bothered to read the introduction to the piece, was actually known as the 'Pretty Cultural Women' building. The two members of the 'Hit Tag Team' sat ready in their 'Jeep' when another body clumbered up onto teh back of the veichle. Nobody turned around at first, but a familiar voice broke over them... Voice : 'Ehrrrm... *Burp*... can you... take me over... deeeeree... please?' Rich and Ryan both span around from their seats at 'Command Control', looking over to see what stranger had decided to catch a ride on the 'Pretty Cool Express'. The recording device soon turned around after the two 'PoP Icons' had done so... and to their amazement, an old and grizzily looking Ronan Keating was resting on the back of their ride, gazing in towards our two heros and flashing them both a pathetic grin. Sherwin was the first to react to the arrival of their new friend, his face contorting into a sincere glare of disgust. The Boppin' PoP Icon started to scold Ronan in the harshest of manners known to man. Sherwin : 'RONAN KEATING?! What in Gods name do you think you're doing... clambering onto the 'Jeep' that will roll the ball forwards for the 'Pretty Cool Guys' within PCW... how dare you come along to this place of Greatness and try to steal what is rightly OUR SPOTLIGHT!' At the end of his line, Sherwin started to get a little frustrated with the way that Ronan Keating was still trying to clumber up into the 'Jeep'. The member of our idolic Boy Band hopped out of the 'Jeep' and made his way through to the back of the veichle, not even allowing Ryan Moore to have any say about the course of action that was going to follow next. Ronan couldn't believe his eyes as Sherwin made his way to the back of the 'Jeep' in record time. Ronan : 'What?.. Why ya coming round to the back? All I'm asking for is a trip over there... is it too hard to ask?' Keating tried to protest his innocence of the problem but Sherwin wasn't have any of what was being laid forward to him. Ryan Moore continued to look on upon the situation and waited for a few seconds before saying something to break the obviously intense vibes surrounding everything. Moore : 'You know Rich... I've just been thinking about something...' Sherwin turned his attentions to his Tag-Team partner, an obvious look of interest spreading across his face. Eager to hear what his friend has to say, Sherwin nodded his head somewhat, a sign to show Ryan that he was ready to hear whatever idea was on his mind. Moore : 'Well... you know how all of those causes around the world have concerts to generally annoy certain people into doing rights? Well, I was on the lines of thinking that all three of us could sing a few of the old 'Pretty Cool Guy' hits... hits from when Sean Smith was hanging with the group.' Rich looked away for a few seconds, engrossing himself deep in thought. Ryan looked on, patiently awaiting whatever judgement was to come from the other half of the 'Future PCW Tag Team Champions'. Ronan just looked up into the sky, giggling at whatever sight he could see pass by... whether it be a small bird... or a light aircraft, this man just found everything to be 'funny'.A few more moments had passed... Sherwin was still looking into the dirt on the floor, thinking of an answer to the idea that had been posed to him by Ryan Moore. Ronan Keating... well, he was still looking up into the air... although, now there was nothing flying past.. nothing of any context, and still... that freako was still laughing like a little confused school girl. We waited patiently for whatever answer that Sherwin was going to give... and THEN... it happenned... Sherwin : 'Oh My God... I don't know why I didn't think of it soon... hell, I don't know why I didn't think of it period... Ryan, you must have stolen my brain for a few moments or so there to steal my idea or something, but you know... starting to sing a few of those old school tracks will really put things into perspective... and force those chiefs in that 'PCW' office building across the street to listen to the demands of us... and us alone...' Moore : 'I thought that this whole entire scenario was supposed be us coming along to help all of the Tag Teams around the world?' Sherwin looked over to his young friend and laughed loudly, obviously forgetting everything that had been said earlier on in the first episode of it all. Moore looked confused but Sherwin comforted him with quick signalling of the 'Tag Team Championships'. Rich looked at Ronan Keating for a few moments before pounding his hands upon the boot of the 'Jeep' and saying what was the very last line of the piece... Sherwin : 'Ryan... Ronan... tomorrow, we leap forth into the world of music and we give all of the chiefs a showing that will not only confirm our positions as automatic gainers of the #1 Contendership of the PCW Tag Team Titles... but it will make sure that we get all the respect in the world!!!!' The picture faded out into the confinments of black, the 'Pretty Cool Guys' totally unaware of anything that had happenned on the fronts of both 'The Heartbreakers' and the 'Cowabunga Connection.' Will they finally realize the evil acts that had been carried out by the Connection in their last showing? Or will the world continue to flow around with our boys left to wonder why Justin Timberlake's face is turning into pure dirt? All the questions to these answers on the next episode of 'Pretty Cool G!' ... Gah, I mean Answers to these Questions... or... meh, whatever...
