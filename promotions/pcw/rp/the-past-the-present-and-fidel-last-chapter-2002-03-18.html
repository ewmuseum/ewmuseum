The Past, The Present, and Fidel (Last Chapter)

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/18/02 Time: 2: 45 P.M Location: CubaScene: Fidel’s Mansion “ The Past, The Present, and Fidel.” The Final Chapter The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore entering the Fidel Mansion. The two men have just arrived there and are in complete awe of Signor Castro’s huge place. Juan: What have I been saying, Kevin. This man is my complete idle. This is the kind of fame and fortune I want to receive. Kevin: You’re almost forgetting Juan, now that you and me are tag team champion, we have to carry around a superior attitude. Juan: Oh believe me Kevin. I can do a superior character role. They don’t give me the name Golden for nothing you know. A servant of Fidel’s comes walking toward the two men slowly. They notice the man and he greets the two. Castro’s servant: Hello visitors, I welcome you both to Castro’s mansion. You will notice the acres and acres of exotic terrain and beautiful skylines. We also are home to some of the largest crop fields in the world. (Juan speaks silently to Kevin.) Kevin: It’s nice to know that he took the time to memorize a speech about this place just for us. Juan: Yea, and the part about the crops I can believe, but it isn’t carrots and grapes there growing over there. Kevin: Then what is it? Juan gives Kevin a stupid look and smacks him in the back of the head. The servant points for the men to follow him, and then goes walking off. Kevin follows, but Juan holds Kevin back, and whistles to grab the servant’s attention. The servant speaks no English and doesn’t know a word that’s coming out of Juan’s mouth. Juan: Excuse me servant guy, but what you did right there was totally unacceptable, and will have to be done over. The servant shrugs after not understanding. Juan: Don’t make like you don’t know what I’m talking about. Now you just remember you are talking to the PCW tag team champions and we can get you fired with the flicker of our fingers. The servant shrugs once again and turns around. Kevin kicks him in the back for him to continue walking as he does some kind of curse in Spanish, but Kevin didn’t hear it in full. The servant brings the two men straight up to Fidel’s room, which he is not in at the moment, but will be coming very shortly. Servant: Signor Castro will be with you in a few moments, but for now make yourselves comfortable. The two men walk around and look at all of the photographs and paintings as the servant leaves the room. Juan: Wow! Check this painting over here out, it had Fidel above a dark cloud, with people begging for mercy underneath him. Kevin: I thought he wouldn’t like to be depicted like, how do you say, umm…..satan, but I guess he does. Juan: Hey, if you got the power, then why not flaunt it. I find no problem in flaunting thing, do you? Kevin: No, not at all. They then notice a gold plated flag of Cuba above his desk. The gold is colored in certain spots with the colors of the Cuban flag. Juan stares at it for a few moments, until Kevin snaps his fingers to grab Juan’s attention. Kevin: I’m guessing you dig the flag, right Juan? Juan: That is pure beauty. Do you know what I would give to get something like this of the Portuguese flag? Kevin: It is pretty sweet, just goes to show if you have a lot of money, you can do what ever you really want. Juan: Man, do I regret spending all of my money from my soccer career on my beach houses and nightclubs. The door then slowly opens as Juan and Kevin stop everything there doing and draw their attention to the door. In from the door comes the one, the only, the master of all, the king of extreme, and the strength of the country, Fidel Castro. Juan and Kevin pause for a few seconds and let Fidel say the first words. Fidel: Ahh! So you are the boys, I hear so much about. You are from the American fighting association, am I right? Juan: Yes! PCW, it’s an American fed, but we’re trying to change that all, sir. Fidel: Oh please don’t call me sir, just call me Fidel. You two seem tense, take a seat and grab a cigar, only the men that owe me money should be worrying like the two of you are right now. The two men grab cigars and sit down as Fidel takes a puff and look over at the two men, sitting in front of him. Fidel: So I hear the two of you are in need of my services. Kevin: It’s more like we want your support then we want your service. You see we run an organization called International Incident. It’s goal is to bring pride to the international world, and to show the best fighters in the world are the foreign ones. Fidel: Interesting. Juan: Yea, we also work a lot in different countries with campaigns. Were more than just a group of wrestlers, rather than political figures? Fidel: Yes, I realize that. I believe one of your other members had been taking part in a little uproar around here over Elian Gonzales, you must of heard of him? Juan and Kevin both begins to sweat in horror of what’s going on as they answer. Juan: Which member would that be? Fidel: He calls himself The Chef. Juan: Oh I see well I’m really sorry Fidel, we’ll definitely make sure he stops what ever he’s doing the moment we get the chance. Fidel: Oh no, don’t even thing of doing that. The man has a certain air about him that I seem to like. Juan: Oh I see, so would you like to take us up on our offer and come over and check out of one of our shows one of these times. Fidel: I never refuse, so of course I shall. Juan: Great! Fidel: I’ll see you guys next time you defend that gold around your waist. Keeping looking up to me, and soon you’ll have enough gold as in my flag over there. As Juan and Kevin leave, Juan takes one last glance over at the flag, the pure gold and success that it stands for. It represents, everything that Juan ever wants to be or become in his life. With that the scene fades to black.
