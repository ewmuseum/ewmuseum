The Truth Revealed!?

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/02 ‘The question will finally be answered!’ remarks a determined Krazie as he drives a POS (piece of shit) rental down the quiet streets leading to Port Ewen, New York heading towards his parents house. ‘Lu-Lu, thanks again for coming with me.’ Lu-Lu the inanimate shopping cart sits in the passenger seat with the seat belt strapped across her, preventing her from sliding across the makeshift leather seat of the POS. Krazie looks her way as he drives past the Welcoming sign that every city or town has posted welcoming all the visitors. ‘It’s ok babe. I am glad you took me along. It’s a lot better than going out training in the art of thug, that’s for sure!’ Lu-Lu replies to Krazie who slides his left hand across his face feeling a few bumps and scabs from the beating he took a week ago. ‘No matter the pain, no matter the scars, I still think it was a valuable lesson I can use in the match against Duzza at Supremacy,’ remarks Krazie as he keeps his hand on one rather large scar that covers his cheek. The Rising Star champion makes his way through the quiet streets of Port Ewen as he makes his way to the heart of the small town. ‘Look over there!’ Krazie says urgently as he slows the POS rental down. There Krazie points to a corner where a small local corner store sits with some trucks and jeeps are parked outside. Neo-Nazi flags are put as decals on some of the jeeps and trucks as Krazie creeps past remembering the fateful day fourteen years ago when his father murdered Duzza’s father. ‘We better hurry!’ remarks Krazie as he speeds down the road making his way through the neighborhood and to a medium sized red bricked two story house with a two car garage connecting to it. ‘Here we are…’ Krazie states the obvious. Krazie pulls his POS Rental halfway up the driveway and parks it behind a white jacked up Ford 500 extended cab with a Neo-Nazi flag decal on the hood of it. ‘Brace yourself! I don’t think my parents know that we are “dating”….’ remarks a nervous Kid Krazie. ‘You never told them about us!?’ asks an offended Lu-Lu. Krazie climbs out of the car and walks around to the passenger side to help Lu-Lu out like the loving gentleman he is. ‘Nah, I haven’t had the time to tell my parents about us,’ Krazie says as he puts Lu-Lu on the concrete driveway. ‘But I guess it’s better now than never right!?’ Krazie pushes Lu-Lu up the drive way some and across a side walkway that leads to the front door. Krazie pushes the button and listens as Dixie plays as the doorbell. ‘Here we go,’ remarks Krazie as the door slowly opens. ‘Krazie….!?!?!?’ responds an elder woman standing in the doorway holding the door open with one hand and holding a cigarette in the other. She is wearing some sweatpants that stretch to fit her wide rear end and a white shirt that hangs loosely on her shoulders with some pit stains under her armpits. ‘Hi momma…’ remarks Krazie as he goes to give his mother a hug. She gives him a half hug with one arm patting his back as she smokes the cigarette in her other hand. ‘Is pop home?’ The woman walks from the doorway into the house as Krazie pushes Lu-Lu in following his mother as the three make their way into the living room. Krazie parks Lu-Lu next to a black leather recliner as his mother plopped down on the couch that is facing a large 32’ big screen television that is displaying Operah. Krazie’ mom looks at Krazie as he parks Lu-Lu next to the recliner. ‘What the hell do you have a shopping cart for?’ remarks a dumbfounded Mother. ‘Oh I forgot to introduce you to my girlfriend… Mom this is Lu-Lu… Lu-Lu this is my mom…’ Krazie introduces the two with a smile on his face. ‘What is wrong with you boy!? That is no girl, that there is a basket with wheels!’ remarks an angry and confused Mother. Krazie pats Lu-Lu on the handlebar trying to soothe Lu-Lu down. ‘Yes I know baby, yes I know, I’ll tell her…’ Krazie looks to his mother as she begins to laugh at him. ‘Lu-Lu says, don’t hate because she is better looking than you!’ Krazie’s mom begins to laugh even more slapping her thigh as she laughs. Her laughter is cut short as she begins to hack and cough. She stops coughing and looks from Lu-Lu to Krazie with a look of disbelief on her face. ‘If this is some sort of cry for help tell me now!’ remarks Krazie’s mother. ‘If not, then boy you really need some help!’ ‘No mom, this is the love of my life and I plan on marrying her someday! She understands me like no one else can!’ cries a wounded Krazie. ‘Now where is pop!?’ ‘He’s out back in his office… I wouldn’t mention the whole Lu-Lu thing to him just yet!’ Krazie’s mom remarks before bursting out into another fit of laughter. Krazie leaves after giving Lu-Lu a kiss heading out back, passing through the back door and through the backyard and into a wooden shack where Krazie sees all four sides of the small shack covered in Neo-Nazi propaganda. His father is sitting in a white colored recliner with his feet propped up as he watches Andy Griffith on the television. ‘Hey boy, I thought I heard you pull up!’ remarks his father without even looking his way. ‘What brings you up here to Port Ewen boy? I thought you were ashamed of the people who live in Port Ewen?’ Krazie walks up and takes a seat on a couch perpendicular to the recliner that his father is sitting in. Krazie looks at his father with a mix of respect, fear, and awe. ‘Nah pop, I’d was never ashamed of anyone in port Ewen. I just thought I’d come down and see how you are doing before I fly to Canada for Supremacy.’ Krazie’s father pulls out a beer from a mini fridge that is positioned next to his recliner in arms reach. ‘Yeah well I am doing fine… How’s your wrasslin going?’ Krazie shakes his head as he notices that his father is still an isolated and cold man. ‘It’s going good pop. I got a title and made a couple of friends. I got a title defense on Sunday against a guy named Duzza.’ Krazie’s father nods his head half listening to his son speak as he watches Andy Griffith solve another crime in Mayberry. ‘So this Duzza sounds like a n!gger…’ ‘Yeah pop he is….’ Remarks Krazie. ‘You going get beat by a n!gger boy?’ asks Krazie’s dad as he turns and looks his way. ‘Nah pop, I ain’t going to lose…’ replies Krazie as he looks his father dead in the eye. Krazie’s father nods his head and turns back to watch his show. Krazie gets up and walks along the back wall looking at newspaper clippings reporting on White Supremacist attacks on African Americans. ‘Hey pop you remember what happened fourteen years ago?’ Krazie’s father takes a swallow of beer as he just sits there thinking. He turns around to face his son as Krazie stops walking and looks back at his father. ‘What about it boy?’ replies his father. ‘Just wondering if you remember what happened to that n!gger who drove through the neighborhood that night,’ remarks Krazie. ‘Yeah, I remember what happened to that n!gger! He deserved what he got!’ Krazie’s father replies as he turns back to the television turning his back on his son. ‘Any n!gger who thinks they can come into my neighborhood will learn their mistake!’ Krazie father takes another swig of his beer as Krazie begins to laugh. ‘You know what pop, I never thanked you for that night…. Thank you!’ Krazie’s father stops drinking and doesn’t move for quite some time. As seconds go by he finally stands up and walks to Krazie who has braced himself for a confrontation. Instead his father walks up to him and warps him in a great big bear hug. ‘You know what boy? I have been waiting a long time to hear those words!’ remarks Krazie father as he hugs him with teary eyes. ‘By killing that innocent man, I learned that not only are white people superior to colored people, I also learned that my father is the biggest p^ssy in the entire WORLD!’ Krazie replies as he steps back breaking the hug and expecting to get hit with a punch or something. Krazie’s father just stands there unblinking and dumbfounded as his son backs away a step or two. ‘What!?’ asks his father. ‘Why? What was the point in killing an innocent man? Just because he was a different color or was it because he spoke different than us?’ Krazie asks angrily. ‘By killing that innocent man did it prove your manhood? What was the real reason? Why did you really kill that man!?’ Krazie father’s shoulders slump as he just looks at his son with an evil glare in his eyes. ‘Why!? You want to know why? Did you know that man!? Did you know that that same man was the same man that rapped your mom!?’ At this time Krazie could be knocked over by a light gust of wind as he stands there shocked. ‘Yeah boy that’s right! That man was not an innocent man! He rapped my wife while I was at work… So was that killing justified? I’d say so! If I could go back and do it differently I’d make him suffer even more!’ Krazie’s father walks over to his recliner and sits down putting his face in his hands. Krazie walks over and plops down in the couch doing the same thing his father is doing by putting his face in his hands. ‘I can’t believe this…’ remarks a dumbfounded Krazie. ‘Yeah boy, you want to know why I hate n!ggers? That is why! He broke into our home one afternoon while I was at work and as he was stealing some of our sh!t your mother came home from the grocery store.’ Krazie’s father remembers the event like it happened just yesterday. ‘He grabbed you mother and after beating her a bit he proceeded to….’ Krazie’s father stops his story not able to continue. ‘Why didn’t anyone tell me this before!?’ asks a shocked Krazie. ‘What would you have done?’ Krazie’s father looks up at Krazie. ‘There was no point in bringing it up with you… You didn’t need to know and I didn’t want to tell you!’ Krazie just looks on at his father as his father gets up with clenched fists and a burning look of hatred behind his blue eyes. ‘So now that you know what truly happened what are you going to do!?’ Krazie smiles and begins to chuckle causing his father to turn and look at his son. ‘What will I do?’ asks Krazie. ‘I will destroy that n!gger’s son!’
