Defining the Corporate Giant

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd October Miyagawa is standing near a blue backdrop in a studio for no real reason other than to stand him. Suddenly your viewpoint switches from the crappy handheld to the camera in front of Miyagawa. Miyagawa: Greetings friends. Welcome to tales of the Unwanted. Suddenly we change cameras to a famous big rotund black bloke with a cock-eye and a shaved head. Bloke: HE DID WHAT?! We switch back to Miyagawa in the studio. Miyagawa: Now what I want to do now is to tell you a little story… I call it Duke Williams versus the Space Aliens. On the blue screen behind him an image of Mel Gibson in blue face paint, a kilt and armed with a sword appears. Miyagawa appears to stare at a monitor off scream. Miyagawa: Brave yes. Wrong one however. The shot changes to the opening sequence of the 80’s children’s TV show “Bravestar”. Basically think of a cyber cowboy with a robotic horse and a laser gun and so forth. Miyagawa: Now Duke in his earlier days used to be a cartoon. What? How can someone be a cartoon and be real? Well I dunno, but here is a technical demonstration from Walt Disney on how to do it. The camera changes to a stock image of Walt Disney smiling… you know, the age old black and white image of Walt Disney they ALWAYS use. After three or four seconds it switches back to Miyagawa. Miyagawa: There we go, thank you Walt. **looks off screen** You’ll dub in the bollocks later, won’t you? The camera pans around to where JJ is standing dressed as a director, including a stick on beard and a pair of glasses whilst wearing an “ET” t-shirt. JJ: Sure thing, no problems. Say is Steven Spielberg Jewish? Miyagawa still on screen shrugs. Meanwhile in the background, Bravestar is fighting some zombies. Miyagawa: Oh yeah, anyway, Duke was out one day and started fighting some Zombies. Why? Because he’s a bastard, that’s why. Pause the footage a second. The footage is paused and Miyagawa waves towards his side like a weathergirl. Miyagawa: Look, see that Zombie there with a bulge in his pocket… see… well that’s obviously a packet of cigarettes. The Zombie just wanted a light! And Duke went all Hitler on his ass and started firing his laser gun off! Well that’s just like an upstart Hitler white supremacist that Duke Williams is. Suddenly the image on the screen changes to Hitler at the famous rally where he’s standing in front of the tall desk and waving his arm about. Miyagawa: And see, this is Duke Williams placing his lunch order at McDonalds. McDonalds! The most evilest corporate giant of them all! Suddenly from off screen comes a nerd. It’s Microsoft’s Bill Gates. Bill Gates: I hate to interrupt but I really must stop you there. Miyagawa: Huh? Bill Gates: We’ve copyrighted “The Most Evilest Corporate Giant of Them All” I’m afraid that you simply cannot use it. Miyagawa: So it’s Microsoft, The Most Evilest Corporate Giant of Them All… right? Bill Gates nods happily. Miyagawa: So now that you’ve got so much money that you cannot feasibly spend it… and you actually beat three quarters of Africa’s national output… ever thought of dropping the cost of Microsoft Office to say… under a hundred bucks? Bill Gates looks shocked. Bill Gates: No way, that would be like… a nice thing to do! Corporate bastards like me never do nice things ever! Suddenly the camera goes to black and white and JJ wanders on holding a large cardboard cut out of Liam Nielson from “Rob Roy”. Miyagawa: What now? JJ: Going for the Schiendler’s List look. Miyagawa: Son of a Bitch. Bill Gates holds his finger up. Bill Gate: Gonna have to stop you there. That’s the head of Apple’s current CEO title. Fully copyrighted, can’t use it. Miyagawa: Look, piss off, before I call security. Bill Gates turns and walks away and leaves Miyagawa standing on screen in black and white next to a cardboard cut out of Rob Roy whilst Hitler’s rally plays in the background on a blue screen. Miyagawa: Ah bollocks it to it all, this has gone to shit.
