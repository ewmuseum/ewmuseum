The desperate quest for a bigger piece of the glory

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The desperate quest for a bigger piece of the glory Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Dec. 27, 2002 ....It was over....All in the course of an hour or so, of heaven and hell compacted into the length of a match, pain still surging through my body from every harsh impact that had come to pass...My head ached greatest of all, having endured most of the punishment, they said it might've been a grade one concussion, I'd just have to wait and see... Wait and see? Not on my life. I'd never participated in a Carnival of Carnage before, and with the tag gold I'd come so agonizingly close to within reach if I could only find it before anyone else did, I wasn't just going to let it slip once more...Every day was just another dream, you had your chance to go for it, though in doing so you were risking great loss as a result..But if you could just survive it all, you would be able to look back on the moments that summed up your dream and keep them for the rest of your life, happy and accomplished. I was already living quite a few of my dreams at once at this moment, but I still had the hunger for more, there would always be the hunger for more..It was there when I was part-owning RUSH but being overshadowed constantly by egotists like Nelson and Stanislav and the 'big names', all I wanted to do then was get back into the damn ring and get my spotlight back. It was there when I came back to PCW, I thought I had all I wanted for a moment with the Front Office and the tax job...But I, like most wrestlers, just had to get ahold of the gold. This was a sore point for me for a while thanks to the Trapezoid, and so when I defeated him for the Cruiserweight Title, I was starting to come around. No matter how much I did, there'd be something more I could do to keep building my fame...It was infinite wanting, but I could worry about retirement later hopefully.. This was my career, I could take a break for the holidays if I wanted to, but there'd always be something to have to bother with..... (What with all the Christmas celebration, Turmoil went up while this promo was bein' written, so let's just pretend that this sudden timeframe jump isn't awkward.:-P) ......And last night, I took the brunt of the bother. This was what it was all about, absorbing what you needed to and dealing enough to get the job done, and I did the best I could there, I managed to come back from an awful cheap bunch of caneshots and STILL take out Duke, that egotistical little prick always thought he ruled the roost since he came in and I've beaten him twice in a month now, but he still crows on about how he can handle this guy and that, that he managed to beat Kannon on the basis that he had more run-ins for their Decimation match... And how did it end? A display of utter brutality from Nerezza, legitimately trying to end my career....or just taking all precaution to make sure he would make the pin, either way giving me just another near-concussion experience to pile onto the Trick or Treat pain..At least come Friday and Sunday, I might have matches that wouldn't involve me in the ring for the most part, maybe then I'd be rested up to defend the Cruiserweight Title against half of those new Tag Champions in El Romantico....Of course, if it was a non-tag format being employed for the 6-man Continental shot match, perhaps I just might win yet another shot at the belt I had yet to get my first earned opportunity at...I was still waiting for the right time, the right man, but having two shots would be better than one, and the opposition was comprised of the same guys as usually seemed to get involved. I'd beaten the Trapezoid so many times that it no longer really mattered how I fought against him, a loss just plain wouldn't faze me..The End, Chet, Dante, they had their chance at the Pay-Per-View but they were still going to provide competition...It was Mason that pissed me off, him and his attempt to make a mockery of me whilst fixing the Versailles match...If he'd survived Decimation with the belt intact, I'd have taken it right off his hands the next Sunday, screw #1 contendership, but now it was tougher..I'd have to defend my most prized possession of the moment against a hungry competitor who wanted the same glory that had been taken from me by the Trapezoid..I should have been Taggerweight Champion as Juan Borro could claim to have been, and I didn't want to lose my title just to see this Romantico guy who'd lost two matches for the chance to fight for my title get the same glory in such questionable fashion.... This was different than the other times. I would fight not just for my own immediate glory, I fought to keep others from that which I felt I deserved to have gotten before them, a sort of delayed glorification taken to an extreme..He wasn't taking this title, I'd already seen lesser people than me rise up the ladder ahead of myself, and this was my time to shine.... I was a mere 10 days or so away from a new record title reign for the Cruiserweight Belt...This would get me into the books, at least for another few months, if only I could get through him I would be a record-holder in the annals of PCW history... ...If only I could get through this... ...If only I could get through this... ...Gotta help me get through this, ooh, ooh... (Well, looks like it's time to wrap this baby up, he's gotten into the Daniel Bedingfield again..Damn late-night promos.) I gotta get through this, oh I've gotta get through this, I'm gonna make it, gonna make it, gonna make it through... (Sorry folks, g'night!) ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
