Dear Big 4....MAKE ROOM!

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-1-2002 OOC: These comments are strictly thoughts of the character being represented, NOBODY take them personally for they make perfect sense with the character. Just enjoy the RP for what it is. Thank you. Dear Big 4...OSW, RUSH, FSW, and PCW, I was told my first week of media economics 101 at Morehouse University that you never really make your mark in the world until every person walking the planet... Young and old... Weak and strong... Beautiful and ugly...Knows your name, and either fears....respects...or revers it.Well listen up cuntlick, because my name is Jamar Wilson.I'm that guy over in Japan who took the failed project of your very own Alexander Miyagawa....laid his hands on it...and made it a money-maker. I've noticed something about you 'Big 4', an annoying trait that lit the fire in me to even prompt the attack against PCW itself. Is it written scripture that you 'big boys' ignore everyone other fed out there that poses a threat to you. You like to pretend those other feds out there don't exist, maybe hoping they'll disappear. OSW does it everyday.... PCW are the KINGS of it, getting raped of talent by OSW and STILL failing to even lift a finger to strike back... RUSH, you guys have no chance of smoking anyone because by the looks of things, your just learning to roll the blunt my boys. FSW, you seem to think you can't be touched on your steady rise up. Then there's PURE....we're that little kid on the playground the other kids are too scared to talk too... We're that street in the neighborhood that nobody DARES drive down in broad daylight or the dimmest of moonlight... We're the personification of in-ring and out of ring excellence and entertainment.Yet all we are to you is a 'Jap fed'....a fed only known through tape traders and 'puro' fans.... What's the deal, networks too scared to air us? You Big 4 too worried we might make you look bad? Heh, figures....we didn't get handed what you four were just served on a silver platter... OPPOURTUNITY With that....there is no end to how far you can climb up the mountain, but it seems as if PURE can't get a break, no matter what we try or how hard we work... So we did the one thing we could do...the only thing we could do to get our message across... We invaded. We came on your programming and made a STATEMENT...THANK YOU, for allowing us the privilege of coming on your programming and screwing it up royally for the benefit of showing the world that we can't be ignored much longer. We've stayed in the shadows long enough. While we put out quality shows that are only confined to Japanese arenas and gyms, the staff and roster knowing FULL WELL we can sell out bigger and better venues than that....the 'Big 4' continue to float around, putting out the very epitome of crap show after show. A 'Jane Says' match for OSW ending with Poindexter Jr., Howard Porter, landing the already sulied and sh**-buried OSW World Heavyweight title....'Jamar Says'...BULL**IT. That, and next time 'Jane Says' anything....smack that ho.FSW....they suprise us with ALAN KRIEGMAN AND HOWARD PORTER, two jerk-offs who obviously can't cut it in OSW and are trying to get seen in a fed that's already way over-rated....not to mention FSW si responsible for getting Frampton ranked on 'The News'...I know I wasn't the only one who spit out his coffee to say, 'What the f**k' after he made it.RUSH....you telling me your most recognized superstars are a Lucha-whore who sadly has more charisma then half the roster and a lackluster Black Attack refugee as your President. I'll see you all in the unemployment line in...I'd say 2 months. Then....there's PCW. Run by a man that'd I'd actually respect if he wasn't my competiton and if I didn't think he was a bigger a**hole then...well me....Xavier Kannon. WAY TO GO GIVING YOUR SAVED BY THE BELL REJECT MORE TV TIME THEN YOUR ACTUAL TALENT!! Taxes? I'm sure the people tune in to see wrestling and drama...not a CNN financial report. All the Big 4 have a major problem....They don't know what the people want. They take a shot in the dark, hope it hits something, and if it does....stick to it whether it's destined to fail or otherwise. It pisses me off that the 'Big 4' run about, looking at their ratings sheets, battling it out for 'wrestling supremacy' when really....there IS NO fed supremacy without the forgotten fed....PURE. CHZ, we crushed em' eh? We sent them packing back across the border and they can take Tom Green, Canadian Bacon, and frickin' Avril Lavigne with em'. NTICW, please....when your lead by incompetance, expect nothing less. Like anybody ever gave you boys a chance. That leaves PURE....and we were basically gievn two choices... Survive or die. Dying has NEVER been an option in my life, and it doesn't look to be the option now. PURE will do more than just survive...we will rise from the ashes and ANNIHILATE those who try to hold us down. YOU CAN'T HOLD US DOWN ANYMORE! PURORESU ULTIMATE RING ENTERTAINMENT, we refuse to play second fiddle and be shafted out of this war any longer...Red October is more than just an event for us...it is a revolution, a decleration of our power and strength.Alongside the Chosen 4....I will risk it all, in a high stakes game with the chosen 5 of PCW...and either we get our asses handed to us... Or we send the Primetime boys home with their asses via Express Delivery. PURE has kept quiet long enough....maintained silence for as long as possible...we're hungry, and we want what we were never meant to have... The spotlight. For now, we're stealing someone's spotlight in only the way we know how... But one day, when we arise victorious over the PCW boys at Red October.. We begin our rise to crash the 'party for 4' that we weren't invited to... And when we're top of the mountain, kings of the hill, the federation that everyone can't help but talk about, three things are certain... Xavier Kannon, Rush Mason, Howard Porter, and ELLIE~! will ALL be working for PURE as the official clean up crew... The spotlight will forver shine on us... And one day, some small fed is gonna try to invade us from nowhere, because by then, we'll be the big dogs. Adrenaline pumps through our veins RUSH... Why have five-stars, when we can pump out ten stars sh** at the drop of a nympho's drawers FSW.... We're the new school, and we're gonna teach you old bastards a thing or two OSW... And we most certainly, will be fought over for that precious 'PRIMETIME' spot you all covet so much PCW... Red October is our night, the night PURE stops fighting from within and we fight the world... We battle the biggest and baddest you all have to offer until finally...we stand victorious or in a pile of ashes. Welcome to our homeland PCW, enter the jungle and leave with a new a**hole the tigers rip for you. We will not be denied pur time to shine... Sunday, we strike a blow for the held down feds everywhere... And I'll make sure the first blow is on that knee-burned, Canidan tax-fiend bastard Tim Walden....Just looking at him makes me wanna jaw him in the grill one good time...see you Sunday PCW... As for the rest of the wrestling world, BE READY...Sincerely, Jamar Wilson, PURE Vice President AKA the guy's who leading the guys who are gonna kick your a**es XOXO
