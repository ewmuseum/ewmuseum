Future Destinies

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th August 2002 We arrive at our destination, a small psychic carnival presumably somewhere in California, Miyagawa’s native state. We see Miyagawa standing there wearing a grey v-neck t-shirt along with a pair of black shorts. His dear wife Natalia is standing next to him, pushing a three wheeled pushchair carrying their three week old son, Christopher. The duo look perfectly content to just look around at the various stalls with it not being overly busy, at all the knickknacks and the various runic pieces of rubbish that at any other fair would simply be disposed of. They pass a tarot reader and Natalia stops and catches Miyagawa by his shoulder. Natalia: You should get your tarot read. Miyagawa turns about and smirks at her. Miyagawa: You know I don’t believe in that sort of stuff. Natalia seems rather insistant. Natalia: But you know I do. Do it for me, I want to see what you get. The duo lock eyes in a format where only the most determined will succeed. And we all know that isn’t one Alexander Miyagawa. Miyagawa: Alright, alright, I’ll get some gypsy to play patience with a pack of fancy cards for me. The duo and the pushchair make their way over to a small tent just off the beaten track. Miyagawa ducks under the tent and steps into the gloom where an old woman sits hunched over a crystal ball while she holds a pack of cards in one hand. She shows him the chair in front of him and he sits down just as Natalia enters the tent holding Christopher in her arms, having removed him from the chair. The young baby is awake, but only barely noticing his surroundings, as of course his eyesight isn’t fully developed yet. Gypsy: Please cross my palm with silver. Miyagawa fishes out his wallet as the gypsy holds out her hand. He drops four silver dollars into her hand… well notes anyway. She looks down at the money in her hand and smiles as if it was the correct thing to do. Gypsy: What may I ask is the question? Natalia butts in. Natalia: Will we have a long and happy life? The gypsy woman looks back to Alexander and across to Natalia and Christopher. Gypsy: Who is the reading in question of? Miyagawa: Me I guess. The gypsy woman looks back to him. Gypsy: Then what is your question then? Miyagawa: Well… will we have a long and happy life? Gypsy: Very well then… let us see what the cards say about this… The gypsy starts dealing the cards out, with the back of the cards facing up in what basically can the described as a cross with a line down on side (tarot buffs will know that is the a celtic cross). She turns over the card in the center of the cross. It’s an image of a naked muscular man holding a lion’s mouth open. Gypsy: Ah, that is a good sign… this surrounds the central issue of the situation and this is the card that symbolises strength. You two have a bond of you. Miyagawa and Natalia nod at each other, Miyagawa now seemingly buying into this gypsy witchcraft stuff. The gypsy pulls another card out from underneath the center of the middle card that was just revealed and reveals it on top of the strength card. It’s image is of a woman in a flowing dress holding a large coin. Miyagawa: They say women always want the money. Then he realises he’s in a tent with two women. He coughs and quietens down again. Gypsy: The Queen of Coins… she symbolises things such as fertility, a new born baby I see before me, and in this position it talks about the atmosphere around the central issue… what you are thinking about. Miyagawa and Natalia nod at each other once again. She turns over a card at the top of the cross, showing a card with a cup on it. Gypsy: The ace of cups… love and overflowing joy is what is below the surface. She turns over a card at the bottom of the cross revealing it to be seven swords. Gypsy: Ah the deeper level shows that uncertainty and disagreements are abound. She turns over the card to the left. Gypsy: The four of staves… there are things in the past, or things yet to be revealed of preoccupations and discord. Miyagawa and Natalia look worried at each other. She turns over the card to the right of the cross showing someone on a throne. Gypsy: There is a person of sharp insight in your future. Be wary. She turns over the card at the bottom of the line or called staff. It shows four swords pointing upwards. She looks at Miyagawa. Gypsy: You feel that you are in self imposed exile, like you have no place in the situation. Miyagawa coughs nervously. The Gypsy overturns the next card up and shows a card with seven staves on it. Gypsy: The person, group or environment you are interacting with is of splendid award or valuable prize. Miyagawa: Sounds like PCW indeed. The gypsy turns over the second to last card, the next one up showing a man holding a scythe, but not death. Gypsy: Judgement. The unexpected thing that could happen is that you will be judged. You will either be rewarded, or punished for your actions. She overturns the final card revealing it to be… the lovers. Gypsy: The final outcome however will be romantic involvement. Passion. Love. Miyagawa and Natalia look at each other and smile. Miyagawa: That’s good right? Gypsy: It is up to you to decide if it is good or not, I am merely the messenger. Miyagawa and Natalia get out of the tent and stand outside for a moment to look around while Natalia puts Christopher back into his pushchair. Natalia: Well that went well, don’t you think? Miyagawa: I think so… not sure if I like the judgement thing and the person of wits. But I don’t think it could have gone any better to be honest. Natalia: Oooh… gypsy charms, hope you’ve got your wallet ready. Miyagawa: Like I said… women holding coins. And with that, we leave the duo.
