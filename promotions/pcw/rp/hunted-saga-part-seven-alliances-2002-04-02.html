Hunted Saga Part Seven: Alliances...

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/4/02 The camera opens up, in a birds-eye view on a road, somewhere on the outskirts of Chicago, Illinois. The camera zooms in, closer to all of the cars passing by at high speeds, until it focuses in on just one car, a jet black, Mustang. The familiar car of the man assigned to hunt down David J. Constantinople for the army, one Mr Fleming. The camera cuts to inside Fleming’s car, as we see him driving with one hand on the wheel, the other hanging outside of the car. He is dressed in his usual work clothes of a black suit, a white shirt and a black tie, looking particularly like a federal agent. A lot had been going through his mind of late. It had almost been a week since he had been assigned to the hunt for David J. Constantinople and so far he had found nothing, not even a bit of clue. Fleming was beginning to hate the man named David J. Constantinople, even though he hadn’t met the man yet. The reason, simple, Professional Pride. Fleming held his job up first and foremost, he held his job before anything else, and this ‘amateur’ as he liked to call David J. Constantinople, was making an idiot out of him. Yeah okay, he was trained by the military to the best of their abilities, but truthfully that hadn’t stopped him before. Fleming had hunted far better trained men, far deadly and incredibly more resourceful men than this man. That was what it said on record, but Fleming’s instincts disagreed with this trail of thought. If this Constantinople fellow was an ‘amateur’, Fleming would have found him long before his predicted one-week period, but alas his deadline would end in precisely one hour and he would be an embarrassment. An embarrassment in his own eyes at least. He was just itching to get hold of this man that had eluded for this amount of time. Fleming was brought out of this almost trance like state by a faint whirring sound. At first Fleming looks around with a disturbed look upon his face and then extracts his small, silver, mobile phone from his pocket, presses the ‘receive’ button and then puts it up close to his ear. “Yeah!” Fleming greets bluntly. After a faint crackling noise we are able to hear the voice at the other end of the line. “Hello Mr. Fleming, this is your current employer General Tranter.” Tranter begins trying to sound a little domineering, “I have recently acquired some information on our target.” “Go on.” Fleming prompts. “Well have you ever heard of Zemoff Indutsries?” Tranter probes. “Yeah. Hasn’t their contract to develop weapons for the US Government just run out? And the government don’t intend on giving them another contract, am I correct?” Fleming answers, making sure he hasn’t left any vital information he knows about the company out of the conversation. “Yes, Fleming that is indeed very accurate. Well somehow or another, Ms. Zemoff, the owner of Zemoff industries still has contacts in high places within the government and she recently learned about our little ‘AWOL’ case.” Tranter explains. “Yeah, go on.” Fleming prompts, wishing Tranter would get to the vital information. “Well in an attempt to curry favour within the ranks of the military to get her weapons building contract renewed, she used her resources to find the location of the man we’re looking for. He has apparently changed his name to John Crichton and is currently shacked up somewhere, with a woman named Aeryn Sun.” Tranter fills in Fleming leaving no detail unmentioned. Fleming felt the blood boil in his head. How could a mere woman, with no training whatsoever in the skill of hunting, have found the target before he. Fleming was enraged, but also bitterly disappointed. Some untrained ‘industrialist’ of all people had more information on his target than him. “Yeah and what happened?” Fleming inquired, genuinely intrigued. “Well this was the part that she wasn’t totally clear about, but from what I can gather, she had John Crichton and Aeryn Sun in their custody…” Tranter began before being interrupted by Fleming. “THEY EVEN HAD HIM IN CUSTODY?” Fleming shouted, truly enraged with himself. “Indeed. However, as Zemoff’s men were taking Crichton and Aeryn Sun back to the Zemoff building, Crichton somehow, bust the car door, jumped out onto the streets and consequently disappeared into the back alley’s in downtown Chicago. Later on he returned to the Zemoff Building and despite all of the building’s security precautions, made it all the way up to Zemoff’s office, kicked her ass, then left with his woman Aeryn.” Tranter explained. Fleming giggled a little. He escaped from their custody, then broke through all of their security precautions and then walked out of the building all unscathed Zemoff was weak or John Crichton was more resourceful than he ever gave him credit for. This was a very interesting predicament indeed. “I see.” Fleming stated. “Fleming, I’ve set up an appointment for you with her at Zemoff buildings, set for one hour from now. Can you make it?” Tranter both informs and questions. “Yeah I’ll make there, but just what promises have you made in exchange for her help in this matter?” Fleming probes, attempting to get a better picture of the man he is currently being employed by. “The usual, giving her the impression that her co-operation in this matter will lead in an inquiry into the rejection of weapons deals with her company. I haven’t actually made an inquiries or in fact intend to, but what she doesn’t know can’t hurt me!” Tranter replies, closely followed by manic laugh. Fleming simply nodded to himself and decided that his current employer was a nasty piece of work. “Okay, Fleming out!” Fleming stated before pressing the disconnect button on his small, silver mobile phone. The camera cuts back to a birds-eye view of Fleming’s, sleek, black, Mustang, as it spins 180 degrees and heads back in the direction which it came, back to the center of Chicago. The camera cuts to in front of the Zemoff building as the black Mustang screeches up toward the curb, by the entranceway of the building. Fleming casually opens the drivers side turn and steps out onto the Chicago pavement, simultaneously extracting an expensive looking pair of Oakley sun glasses out of his jacket pocket and putting them on his face. Fleming then looks up at the Zemoff building and then shrugs, “I’ve seen bigger!” The camera cuts to inside Ms. Zemoff’s luxurious office, the one with scarlet red carpeting and the massive windows, which gave the viewer the sight of most of Chicago itself. Then the office door opens and chaperoned by Carlos, Ms. Zemoff’s own body guard, Fleming enters the office. Before sitting down at the new mahogany desk in the dead center of the room, he walks over to the windows and takes a look out into Chicago. After a couple of minutes he sits down on his allotted mahogany chair, just as Ms. Zemoff enters the office using a different door, opposite Fleming’s eye line. Fleming decided that this was supposed to be some kind of mind trick to tell Fleming that she was firmly in charge here. It didn’t work, as far as Fleming was concerned. However, Fleming did rise from his seat at her presence. “You may sit Mr. Fleming.” Ms. Zemoff simply stated. Fleming smiled at these last comments she did think that she had control of this situation, Fleming put it down to delusions of grandeur and nothing more. However, he left the ‘demand’ as he saw it slide by and simply sat in his allotted seat. Fleming decided that he didn’t want to be here any longer than he had to be, so he decided to try and expedite matters. “I believe you have some information on our mutual target?” Fleming probed getting directly to the business at hand. Ms. Zemoff sat down upon her chair and simply smiled at Fleming’s oh so predictable reaction to the situation. “Yes, but I’m afraid only a little more information than you undoubtedly have already heard. I can give you a starting point towards your search and probabilities of how far he could have got in the so little time he has had.” Ms. Zemoff explained, realising that Fleming came here searching for far more information. Fleming blinked in shock and felt himself fill with rage. “That’s all? That’s all the addition data you promised General Tranter? What a waste of my time.” Fleming stated rising from his seat and heading to the office door. Carlos simply walks in Fleming’s path and stands there menacingly. Fleming looks up at Carlos and begins to laugh slightly. “Don’t make me hurt you boy!” Fleming threatened. Carlos never budged an inch and Fleming realised that he couldn’t control his anger anymore and simply hits Carlos with a lightening quick punch to the jaw, sending him reeling down the scarlet carpeted steps and then lands with a thud at the bottom of the stairs. “Thank you.” Fleming simply commented opening the office door, he was about to exit, but was stopped by Ms. Zemoff’s voice. You may be able to get past my body guard, but how do you plan on getting past all of the security in reception?” Ms. Zemoff asked with a dangerous tone in her voice. Fleming simply shrugs and develops a small smile upon his face. “Well if our target could simply walk in here, rescue his woman and walk out again, I’m sure that I can also accomplish the same feet.” Fleming replies with relish in his voice. Ms. Zemoff sits upon her desk and simply stares at Fleming before a vicious smile begins to appear upon her face. “At least we have some information on the target Fleming, it is far more than you have obtained in your time within this case.” Ms. Zemoff simply stated. “Yes and what valuable information have you accumulated? You captured him and then let him escape, that’s not information that is you wanting favours from the US Military.” Fleming replied with venom in his words. Ms. Zemoff simply nodded. “Well I found out that he no longer goes by his real name anymore…” Ms. Zemoff began before being cut off by Fleming. “Well that was predictable! In my experience that is one of the first things that the escapee does.” Fleming simply stated in a very patronising manner. “Yes Mr. Fleming but I am aware of his new name, as you well know.” Ms. Zemoff replied the smile widening on her face. Fleming shut the door and walked closer to Ms. Zemoff, kicking Carlos in the head as he walks by. “Are you trying to say that you are a better hunter than me?” Fleming questioned. “No Fleming, but I am a smarter hunter than you, a hunter fuelled by her own predicted profit margin.” Ms. Zemoff added. Then without any warning whatsoever they both embrace in a passionate kiss, they both look like they’re trying to suck each others faces of, with the use of just their lips. After a couple of minutes the embrace is stopped and they both step back a little, both as bewildered as the other. “Well thank you for your information Ms. Zemoff, I’ll get back on his trail immediately.” Fleming simply said heading back over to the office door. “Keep me informed of any details that you might acquire on your journey’s.” Ms. Zemoff requested. Fleming simply smiled and replied with “Hell no” before kicking Carlos again on his way past. The camera fades to black.
