The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 1

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/26/02 {From the hand of The End} I'm writing this down, typing at the keyboard. I managed to memorize the placement of the keys on this computer and am using it to record my own words. I just need Sandy to read back anything that's not mine that pops up. Anyway, I've got a lot to say and a lot to say to people, so let's get started. First, look at this. Singles MatchNon-TitleReferee : Sally SaddleChet King v Totally Tubular Tommy Chet King... You'll be in the match at Adrenaline, competing for the #1 Contendership to the Continental Title. You know why? Look at the match listing I've had Sandy provide. IT'S A NON-TITLE MATCH! I don't know why you're worried about winning anything more than a singles match this week anyway. Go ahead and defeat Tommy. I've got everything planned out. You see, I'll win the #1 Contendership this Sunday, and then once I retire, I'll hold that position in limbo to choose whom I please to face the champion for the Continental Title. And let me tell you, it won't be you. So you came back in shape after an 8 month hiatus. Forgive me if I'm not impressed. Frankly, you've disappointed with your comeback, sinking into the same mediocrity and losing streak that you suffered before you left. I'm not surprised. I was one of the people who beat you in that time when you sucked before your injury. Remember this night? Remember March 26th? Remember the time after you lost the Continental Title after you hit your peak by defeating the likes of Vampir Nosferatu and The White Mexican, both of whom were anything but wrestling gods? Probably not, you most likely blocked it out of memory. Here's a little reminder. Chet King effortlessly PLANTS CLYDE WITH A DIVING REVERSE DDT and goes for the cover….1……2…..SAVE BY THE END!!! Dave: This is turning into an all out brawl! The End locks King in an armbar and grabs him by the hair, ushering him over the top rope to the outside! The End turns around AND IS CLOTHESLINED OVER THE TOP BY THE RUSH!!! You hit your finisher. THe match was yours. But it didn't turn out that way. It seems that it was becoming a trend, more and more the matches weren't turning out your way. Well, those you were booked for. I think there was a period of about a month before you were actually injured where the only thing you did was wait at home, hoping not to be booked. It was in this time that you managed to defeat Clyde, but at that same time in PCW's history, who didn't? Oh, yeah, Bobby Stormbringer. He doesn't count. But let us go back to the teletype, which will take us further into the match. Clyde, unsuspectingly, is SPEARED TO THE CANVAS BY THE RUSH!!! Jeff: NOOOOOO!!! The Rush raises his arms in the air, celebrating the devastating move, when out of nowhere, he’s surprised from behind by a returning Chet King! Elbow to the back of the head by Chet! Chet sets him up for a CHATBACK! Dave: It’s over now! As soon as King looks up he sees two heels coming straight towards his face! Dave: FLYING DROPKICK ON CHET KING BY THE END!!! Jeff: Get up Clyde! The End quickly gets to his feet, kicks the Rush in the gut, and PLANTS HIM WITH THE ONE LAST TIME!!! Cover….1……2……3!!! Winner: The End I dominated that match, kept you from winning twice and stole your pin. Pathetic. And to think that you haven't improved much from that point. The question begs asking: Why come back? You were at home getting paid not to come back... okay, injured pay, but still. No one asked you to come back. PCW certainly didn't gain anything by your return. It was best left alone. You had a safe legacy, but you had to come back and ruin it. Because the new fans and workers who's memories don't go back as far as when I was here with you means that they won't know or care to know what you've accomplished in the past. They'll just know that right now, right at this moment in your career, you're a loser; one of the 'good-ole-boys' who came back into spotlight they didn't deserve, to relive long-past glory days. The Continental Championship will be pointless. Indeed, your reign was rather uneventful, the most spectacular things about it being that you lost it to Jeremy Howard. I remember single-handedly saving that title for Howard by throwing Drake Maxwell off the top of the cage. It probably made you mad that you had to watch Howard carry the belt. Or that Drake Maxwell supplanted himself into the spot vacated for but a moment after you lost the belt, not even getting so much as a few chances to get it back. The Tag Team Championship with Chef for C&C Television... all for naught. It was good at the time, but Chef's moved past it and will arguably be remembered as the better wrestler out of the two of you. Of course, he also drew my ire in screwing me out of the Continental Title at Versailles. The swerve was pointless other than that he wanted the final match, the final thought of the match, to be about him. The match being dedicated to him wasn't enough. Darn him. Tthe multiple Cruiserweight and Extreme title reigns... all for nothing. Then again, just holding the belts does nothing for anyone's career. I should know, I've held the same belts. Extreme Title didn't do nearly as much for me as my performances in Mall Brawl and Alliances this year. I elevated myself more through those matches than holding the Cruiserweight Title twice or winning the Extreme Title. Being in those divisions is pathetic. I had to get out. That's why I let Brandi pin Mark Makonie in the three-way match for the belt. That's why I didn't bother getting out from under the rubble that fell on me in my match with Bob Vurdo. No point. It would just drag me down the ladder. Being a former PCW President? Duke Williams is more memorable as that position, having done more as President than anyone before him. Heel or face, he did his job and did it well. You did, also, but it was a different kind of JOB. Winning the Revelations Main Event at Fedwars to help destroy that godforsaken place and help to further put PCW on the map as THE place to be. Heh. Did anyone think that Fedwars was a threat to PCW? Didn't think so, I'm not impressed. Media.. Tyler Nelson made that stable and has gone on to do better things. You were a tag-along. A glorified groupie. All of those things... no one will know. No one will remember, no one will care. No one but you. But that's the problem. You're not Chet King now. You're the Chet King of early 2001. You're the Chet King who still feels that he's the best. When you've proven in recent history, when you've proven in 2002 that you're not. And how about this, I haven't had any problems with the woman I'm with. Even in that way I'm better than you! And you know what the worst thing about your returning is? Because I'm retiring, the fans will need to turn to someone else to be their 'good guy,' and in the Continental Title division that would most likely be you. Oh, if I could keep that from happening. But unfortunately it looks like that's what's going to happen. Maybe Venneman Pendragon can come back at some point and help the facesof the division save face. I wouldn't entrust that job solely to you. Maybe Pendragon and Rent-A-Hero being in the division with you, they can keep you out of trouble and keep the heels from running amok. Pharaoh is up in the World Title scene now where he deserves to be. If there's anyone I'd tell to keep the faces in good standing in PCW, it would be him. Maybe even Mark Amulet, he's showing promise. Kai is displaying talent in the early days of his career. If he keeps it up he'll surpass you in no time, Chet. Pretty much anyone could. You're not here for more achievements, you're here for us to remember you. So in the present you're destined for disappointment. You've already started on that track. So to you, Chet King, I finish merely by saying this: Don't screw up too much. I have more to say, but shall reserve it for later postings. Good day.
