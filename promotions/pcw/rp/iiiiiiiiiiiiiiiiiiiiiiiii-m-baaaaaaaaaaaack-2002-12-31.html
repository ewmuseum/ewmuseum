IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII'M BAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!!

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII'M BAAAAAAAAAAAACK!!! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 12-31-02 ..::/::..Hello Rent-Heads watching all over the world. You may know me as the narrator of perhaps the most whacked out, crazed, and bold superhero there has ever been in wrestling history, let alone the world. He has been gone quite awhile....many wondering if he'd ever find his way back to the television sets that graced those who followed his exploits ever again. Well, now you have your answer....he's back, in the flesh, and it is an honor for me to be able to describe and narrate his exploits yet again on a regular basis. As long as their as breath within my lungs and strength in his body....the adventures will never end. Now...without further ado, I can finally get back to my job. So without further ado..... Our story begins in the graces of the overwhelming....the screen engulfed in never-ending incubus ink darkness as hard breathing can be heard in the background, accompanied by the sounds of heavy footsteps in rapid succession....a sign of fearful running. The breathing is desperate, their lungs drawing in as much air as humanly possible as their steps continue in succession until finally....they simply stop. All that remains is eerie, uncomfortable silence as the breathing grows steadily slower...as if whoever this person is was trying to stop breathing altogether. The silence continues....long seconds passing by until like most silences....they are broken..::/::..!~CLICK~! ..::/::..At the sound of that quick click, the lights flicker on, revealing an all blue-walled room....and the tired and fallen body of none other than PCW's very own superhero....Rent-A-Hero. He looks spent for air as his blue eyes stare at the screen in a cocktail of simple amazement and awe struck fear, sitting up against the wall with nowhere to run and nowhere to hide as sweat begins to dribble down the spandex fabric of his Flash replica mask. Spent for air, he barely manages to expel from his mouth..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Please....no...more....I can't take...it anymore...' Female Voice:'Hahaha, no way your getting out of this one Rent-A-Hero....PREPARE....TO....DIIIIIIIIIIIIE....OF STOMACH PAINS AFTER EATING ALL THESE COOKIES!!! GET HIM KIDS!!'..::/::..What seems to be a vicious villain stalking our PCW superhero in fact turns out to be a rambunctious mob of children, stampeding like cattle towards our hero as they dogpile him with all their tiny might, laughing and giggling as Rent-A-Hero lays buried under the pile. Standing in the doorway of the room is a lovely young woman, adorned in hip hugging blue jeans with ye dye flowers upon them, a white t-shirt tied at the waste, with coffee brown eyes and lush, short honey blonde hair. She smiles, enjoying the sight of the children overpowering Rent-A-Hero, letting it last for a few more seconds before beckoning to the children..::/::.. Female:'Okay kids...let Papa-Hero up, I'm sure ya'll have worn him out. You guys head to the play room and we'll all watch a movie okay?'..::/::..The little boys and girls turn their bright faces to her, hat group a beautiful sight to see....black, oriental, white, hispanic, and other races of children all playing and enjoying their time together happily as they all respond in unison, followed by huge smiles..::/::..Kids:'YES MISS ROSARIA!!'..::/::..With that, the kids all recede from the dogpile, skipping and racing out of the room....and with the smoke cleared, lays Rent-A-Hero on his back and devoid of breath. Rosaria giggles under her breath, walking over and standing over top of our hero as she asks playfully..::/::..Rosaria:'Have we learned a lesson today?'..::/::..Our hero pushes himself up as best as he can with the palms of his hands, his mask sitting lopsided. Before he can even attempt to fix it, Rosaria kneels down and pinches each side of his spandex mask, fixing it straight to his face with a smile as Rent-A-Hero replies..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Never try to get out of trying all the kid's baking projects....but come on Rosaria, Chad had worms in his.' Rosaria:'Oh come on Rent, you said you'd try EVERYONE's cookies....besides, don't act like you didn't eat worms when you wee young.' Rent-A-Hero:'HEY, that is untrue....I devoured silly putty and crayons my blonde sidekick of child care...BUT NEVER WORMS! That is below my heroic standards.' ..::/::..Our hero nods as Rosaria just giggles at is answer, helping him up by the arms as our hero dusts himself off. With his breath collected and and a smile upon his face, Rent-A-Hero and Rosaria walk side by side as they exit the room...and enter a HUGE room filled with TV's, PS2's, plush animals, assorted action figures, coloring books, and other endless amounts of fun items a child could preoccupy their time with....a virtual wonderland for a child. The walls are painted a bright blue like the room before, windows adorning each wall as sunlight seeps in and illuminates the area, the world outside pleasant and bright as Rosaria stares about in marvel at the area while walking beside a winded Rent-A-Hero, saying..::/::..Rosaria:'This is an amazing thing you've done for the kids Rent. A daycare for the orphaned....it's just amazing. I'm glad I couldbe apart of it.' Rent-A-Hero:'Oh it's nothing Rosaria....these kids deserve a lot better then what has been handed to them and I'm here to give them just that. It's not like I don't have the cash to handle it.' ..::/::..Rosaria shoots an inqusitive eye towards our hero at the mention of money, his lovely assistant asking..::/::..Rosaria:'But...you've been injured for months? You haven't wrestleed in quite awhile. Where did you get the kinda cash for THIS place? And isn't your boss that Xavier Kannon guy? I mean, he'd find a way to make coin-operated stalls for public bathrooms if he could....how'd you manage to evade his taxs?'Rent-A-Hero:'Oh come on Rosaria....Xavier's not THAT smart. How dare you even compare him in the same villainous league as Doctor Doom or The Red Skull when he fails to even have the brian power of the monstrous Rhino! He can't tax anyone on injured reserve...so, I just kept finding ways to stay longer and longer on the list....sprained ankle, tennis elbow, reddened fingers from button mashing....with the money I saved...I put it into the one and only fun-stop for orphans...THE RENT-A-DAD SCHOOL AND DAYCARE FOR ORPHANED CHILDREN AND OTHER FUN STUFF!!....now tha name just screams 'simple'.Rosaria:'Among other things...by the way...Rent...umm, what are you doing later tonight?'font color='blue'>Rent-A-Hero:'Well you know....what any self-respecting daycare owning superhero bachelor would do in his spare time....play isane amounts of PS2 and eat Gummi Bear until I'm lying face down in my X-Men bed.'Rosaria:'I'm sure your the envy bachelor out there...but umm...how about and I...'..::/::..At the cusp of Rosaria's sentence, our hero is blindsided by the young little girl he's come to know as his sunshine...none other then Rent-A-Hero's adopted daughter, Amy A-Hero. Excited and filled with joy, Amy leaps upon her father and hugs him tight as Rent-A-Hero reciprocates her hug with much love, Amy creating quite the gap between Rosaria and our hero. She pushes some of her cinammon brown hair to the side, smiling brightly as she asks..::/::..Amy:'Daddy...me and the rest of the kids wanna watch Space Jam! Can we, can we pulllllllllllease?!'Rent-A-Hero:'Anything for my little angel.' ..::/::..Amy hugs her father tightly, looking back to Rosaria when our hero's eyes are closed as she sners at her, Amy's soft green eyes turning into piercing daggers for Rosaria...almost in a jealous fashion..::/::.. Amy:'And your not gonan do anythign tonight right? Cause you know I can't go to sleep without a bedtime story right?' Rent-A-Hero:'Of course Amy.'Amy:'YAY!! I'll tell everyone else.' ..::/::..Little Amy is let down by our hero as she skips to the video room with the rest of the children....yet on the way she 'accidently' steps on the clean pair of blue Vans worn by Rosaria, snickering after leaving an ample sized footprint on the left one as she feigns sorrow, saying..::/::.. Amy:'Ooops...I'm sorry, I didn't mean to step on your ugly shoes. I'll be more careful next time.' ..::/::..Amy smiles devilishly, skipping into the video room as Rosaria holds in her anger, just brushing off the dirt and looking back up to Rent-A-Hero as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'She's such an angel isn't she?' Rosaria:'Oh yeah....that aluminum halo over her head sure fits her. By the way, what will we be teaching hekids before the movie?'Rent-A-Hero:'I think it's high time we taught them Michael Jackson and R.Kelly safety....no kid should be withou...'!~BEEP~! !~BEEP~! !~BEEP~! ..::/::..Rosaria and Rent-A-Hero's eyes shoot over to the indoor sandbox sitting dormantly next to the Pre-SKool play table, the box lightinh up from snow white to fire truck red as the beeping blares through the room. Rosaria then turns her attention to Rent-A-Hero, who has that look of serving justice upon his face as he then turns his eyes to Rosaria...::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Let the kids watch the video and tuck them in DIRECTLY after it....I've GOT BOOTY-WHOOPERING TO DO!! Take care Rosaria.'Rosaria:'Be careful Rent.'Rent-A-Hero:'I ALWAYS AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA...' ..::/::..As our hero dashed towards the sandbox while saying that, he clumsily trips over a set of strewn on the floor, our hero smacking face first into the ground, Rosaria holding back the urge to giggle as Rent-A-Hero shakes off the pain..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Umm yea....I meant to do that...yea. TO THE RENT-CAVE!!' ..::/::..With that Rosaria just admires as our hero races to the sandbox, leaping highinto the air and diving arms forward as the sand gives away to a large tube underneath, our hero racing through as the tube seals up and the sand replenishes itself within the box..::/::.. Rosaria:'Be careful Rent...' ..::/::..With that, Rosaria heads into the video room with the kids....the scene fading in the middle of that state of emergency. IS RENT-A-HERO TRULY READY TO TAKE CARE OF NOT ONE....BUT MANY CHILDREN?! WHAT DANGER IS THREATENING THE CITY?! HAS HE FINALLY GOT HIMSELF A RENT-CAVE?! FIND OUT....SAME RENT-A-TIME...
