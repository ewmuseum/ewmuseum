The plan unfolds(Smokie Tang #3)

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 7, 2002 OOC: Not my best, but hey, anything wil work, :-D. {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens into a still night outside the Calyx Incorporated company building. Everything is so still around the building, except for one light that is on up on the top floor. The scene moves up along the outside wall of the building showing all the empty dark offices. And then reaches the window with the light, and enters the room. It’s a huge office with several products on shelves throughout the wall. D-Kay Calyx is sitting behind a huge desk in a luxurious black leather chair. He’s wearing a dark suit with a white dress shirt and black necktie. On the desk sits a computer and several other things, including pictures of family, and books for logs and such. In front of the desk sits two chairs, and in the chairs sit Clean P Corg on the left and Sloppy Makonie on the right. D-Kay Calyx is holding a picture of Smokie Tang in his hands and he’s throwing it around in the air. He then lets it drop the floor, letting the frame break and glass shatter everywhere. He looks at Clean P Corg and Sloppy Makonie and says… D-Kay Calyx: That’s what I want done to Smokie Tang pretty much. I want his image to the children shattered and I want him selling my products. {{{{Clean P Corg strokes the stubble on his face and answers with a curious look on his face…}}}} Clean P Corg: I’ve been trying to get that Smokie Tang to go legit for about a year now, it’s just impossible. D-Kay Calyx: That’s where I’ve got the edge right now. You see, as we speak, Smokie Tang is receiving a visit from one of my very own on my payroll… {{{{The scene fades from the office building, and into Smokie Tang’s apartment flat. Smokie is smoking on his golden bong in the smoke filled living room of his apartment. Sitting next to him is Dante Jackson and across from him are three whores that just walked in after a night of street walking. Smokie takes a long puff off the bong and then hears a knock at the door. He gets up and walks out of the main room towards the door. He opens the door and standing there in front of him is Charlene Loc, the virgin that is on the payroll of Calyx Incorporated. As if it were kryptonite at his front door, Smokie drops to his knees in pain. Charlene walks into the room and helps the fallen Smokie up to his feet. Looking confused at what just happened Smokie feels a huge pain in his chest. But he pulls out a pill and takes it, and the pains go away. He looks at Charlene and asks her with the confused look on his face…}}}} Smokie Tang: Who are you? Charlene Loc: I’m your worst nightmare. {{{{Smokie looks at her with a smile on his face and he catches his balance letting Charlene let go of him. They walk into a bedroom and close the door behind them, without a word as if Smokie couldn’t resist himself, he locks the door and jumps onto the bed. Charlene looks at Smokie and says with an embarrassed look on her face.}}}} Charlene Loc: I’m sorry Smokie, but I’ve never you know… been with a man before. {{{{Smokie’s smile turns into a grin now as he realizes that he’s got a virgin on his hands. But what was it his father told him about virgins? He can’t remember, so he just pats the part of the bed next to him. Charlene walks over to that part of the bed and sits down right next to Smokie Tang. Smokie starts to kiss her arm, and gets this funny sensation in his mouth and head, it could be the marijuana reacting to his senses, so he just ignores it. Charlene pulls away from him as if she doesn’t know what she’s doing and then she stands up looking around as if she’s scared of something. Smokie gets up and starts to caress her body with his hands. He then pulls off his shirt and rubs his body against her arm. As if she couldn’t resist she falls down onto the bed, Smokie leaps up and falls on top of her. He starts to move into kissing her on the lips and as his touch hers his body falls limp. He can’t move and he’s in her complete control from then on. She pushes him over and stands over him beside the bed. She pulls a piece of paper out from her pocket and places it on the bed in front of Smokie. She puts a pen in his limp hand and tells him…}}}} Charlene Loc: Now sign that. {{{{Smokie moans as his hand moves towards the contract and he then asks right before he writes a word…}}}} Smokie Tang: What is this for? {{{{Charlene Loc just grins a huge grin from ear to ear and says…}}}} Charlene Loc: Now you’ll be selling stuff for Mr. Calyx, and the second you sign that contract I’ll also be getting my pay check. {{{{Unable to control his body at the moment, he signs the contract, even after knowing what it is for. He signs his full name as Smokie Tang and drops the pen right beside the contract. Charlene picks up the contract and the pen and stuffs them into her pocket. She then pats Smokie on the back and waves goodbye as she says…}}}} Charlene Loc: Nice doing business with you. {{{{Smokie just moans as Charlene unlocks the door and walks out the door and does the same with the front door…}}}} Narrator: Well Smokie just signed his soul to the devil, literally. Man oh man, I can’t believe what Smokie has just gotten himself into. Now onto that meeting that was taking place at the huge business building owned by D-Kay Calyx. Well, seems that Clean P Corg and Sloppy Makonie where very interested in Calyx’s proposal. And with Smokie signing that contract, Smokie’s image was torn apart for good. Well when things looked as if they couldn’t get any worse, they did, how you ask? Well, you’ll need to find out in the next part… TO BE CONTINUED…
