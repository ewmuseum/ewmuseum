Andy Beats Up Chris Sutton

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th August [The scene slowly opens up in a small, quiet residential area of Glasgow, which makes a change from our usual scene, the chaotic, hectic city centre. The houses are all detached, and are all made out of a dark-red coloured brick. They each have their own lush little gardens, driveways and garages, making for a highly desirable residence. A few of the driveways are occupied by expensive looking cars, the afternoon sun glimmering off their perfect paintwork. There are no cars parked by the pavement at all, and there appears to be next to no traffic or pedestrians. Except, from one strangely familiar vehicle, which rolls slowly down the street. It’s a dark-red coloured Vauxhall Corsa, it’s rusted frame give away the fact that it is indeed, Andy Murray’s vehicle. We cut to the inside of the car, Andy Murray and Ross Cruickshank, who sits with a map of Glasgow on his lap looking very confused, occupy the driver’s and front passenger’s seats respectively. In the back, sits Rab all on his lonesome. Ross holds the map up, trying to figure out just where in the hell they are.] Rab: Haw! Aye ye sure this is the way to the fitba’?! Ross: Aye … Ah think so. Rab: Then how come yer lookin’ at that map like a dobber min? Ross: Ah know wut Ah’m dayin’! Rab: Awlright muckah. [Ross scratches his head one more time.] Ross: GRRRR! [Eventually, the frustration overcomes Ross, as he grabs the map, and scrunches it up into a tiny little ball, before rolling down the window, and throwing it out onto the road.] Andy Murray: Wut did ye dae that for!? Ross: Map’s pure pish min. Andy Murray: How we gonna get tae the fitba now!? Ross: Dinna ken … [Rab sighs.] Rab: Pish … Ross: Shut yer mowth, or Ah’m gonnae chib ye. [Ross rolls the window back up, for a few moments, there is silence between the Nedly trio. Andy then reaches down to the radio, and presses the “ON” button, before resting his hands back on the steering wheel.] “I just can’t get you out of my head, boy your loving’s all I think about!” Rab, Ross & Andy: ARGH! PISH! [Andy quickly reaches for the radio again, and turns it off, saving his sanity from the devil that is Kylie.] Andy: Kylie’s pish min … Ross: Aye, pure crap min. [There is silence again for another couple of seconds, before they car rolls past a familiar figure on the pavement. He’s pretty tall, and has black hair. He wears a “Glasgow Celtic Football Club” green & white training jacket, and a pair of white sports trousers, with a black shoulder bag slung over his shoulder. Rab immediately recognises the figure.] Rab: HAW! IT’S CHRIS SUTTON MIN! Andy Murray: BRIWLIANT! AH’M GONNAE CHIB HIM! [Yes, it is of course Chris Sutton, Glasgow Celtic striking reject, and English football’s favourite donkey. Andy quickly hits the breaks, and stops the car, as Chris Sutton goes walking by. Quickly, or Neds throw open their car doors, and jump out of the Corsa. Stealthily, Andy walks up behind Sutton, and makes obscene hand gestures behind his back, much to the amusement of the Neds. He then steps forward, and grabs his shoulder, before quickly hitting him with a quick right shot to the forehead. Sutton falls to the ground unconscious.] Andy Murray: Fuckin’ chibbed ye. [Ross and Rab quickly run up to observe the fallen football player.] Rab: I think ye killed him. Andy Murray: Fuck it, he plays fer Celtic anyway. Ross: Aye! [After a couple of moments staring at the downed football player, the Neds eventually think of something to do.] Ross: Haw! Lets take stuff fae the bawbag’s bag! Andy Murray: Briwliant! [Andy, Rab and Ross kneel down, and remove the black shoulder bag from Sutton’s body. Andy pulls the zip, opening it up. The Neds begin to rummage though the bag. Inside, we can see a football, a Celtic shirt, and some other things. Andy pulls up the shirt.] Andy Murray: Hoho! Toilet paper min! Rab: Briwliant! [Andy quickly stuffs the shirt into his oversized pockets, as Ross pulls out the football, looking at it in a sense of awe.] Ross: Whoa … Andy Murray: Pure magic fitba min! Ross: Aye! Ah’m gonnae put it in the Corsa! [Ross stands up, football in hand, and quickly throws the football into the back of the Corsa, as they continue to search the bags.] Rab: How come there’s so much pish in this bag? Ross: Cus the chube plays fer Celtic min! Andy Murray: Aye … [Andy pulls out a small packet, the words “Glasgow Celtic FC – Official Condom” printed on the label attached to it. Andy scratches his head, confused as to what is inside it, and opens up the package, pulling the condom out. Then, it clicks in his head.] Andy Murray: Aw, balloons min. Ross: Balloons? Andy Murray: Aye, I don’t want Celtic balloons min, they’re for kids … What a chube … [Andy throws the condom over his head, before getting to his feet, pulling the bag up with him. Ross and Rab also rise, as Andy overturns the bag, various small items fall out, pens, pencils, a notepad, training sheets, and various other stuff useless to a Ned, they grimace in disgust.] Ross: Nothin’ but pish … Andy Murray: Aye … Rab: Haw, lets go min … Andy Murray: Aye, lets go steal some more sweeties fae the Spar … Ross: Magic … [And with that said, the scene slowly fades out, as our Neds enter the car, and drive away with their newly acquired football.]
