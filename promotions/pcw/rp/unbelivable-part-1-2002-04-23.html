Unbelivable (Part 1)

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.04.02 The Hamptons, upstate New York. One of the richest places in North America. Everyone who is anyone in the entertainment business has a house in the Hamptons. From Sean “P Diddy” Combs to Sly Stallone, all have a crib in upstate New York. Duzza also has a home in the Hamptons. The scene moves closer to the house. The house is a 3 story, white house. With a large drive way leading to the house, with automatic gates at the edge of the drive way. There is a 3-door garage, with all three of the doors closer. There is also a parked BMW X5 outside one of the closed garage doors. The property is surrounded by pine trees and green grass. But beyond the tree’s is a small private beach that Duzza owns. Around the side of the house is a full-sized basketball court, surrounded by a chain link fence. To the far right of the basketball court are two dog houses, where Duzza’s two dogs “Buster” and “Lady.” At the back of the house is a swimming pool and a conservatory. How Duzza affords this place no one actually knows. The camera moves into the house and into the living room. It is a very large room, with a African theme going on with the wall paper and design of the room. There are small wooden African figurines all around. On the walls are pictures of the family. There are pictures of Duzza’s parents, grandparents and the same for his wife’s family. In the corner of the room is a large television, which has the lottery on. There are two leather sofa’s in the room and one big single leather sofa. Duzza’s ebony queen/wife Brittany is sitting on one of the couches with her and Duzza’s son Jimmy, who was named after one of Duzza’s fallen homies. Brittany is dressed in some tight blue jeans and has a old football jersey on. Duzza, dressed in a pair of baggy black slacks and a white wife beater stumbles into the room, holding his lottery ticket Duzza: “Did I miss it? Did I miss it?” Brittany: “Miss what?” Duzza: “The lottery, the draw, have I missed it?” Brittany: “Nah, it ain’t happened yet, the host is just talking… and talking… and talking” Duzza: “Good, I got a real good feeling about this” Brittany: “Malek, why do you waste money on these lottery tickets? If you haven’t noticed we’re loaded. We’re out of Harlem now Malek, we got cash, we don’t need more” Duzza: “Ahh Brit, your so young and naïve. You can never have enough cash” Brittany: “Well, I seem to remember reading in the Bible that it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than a rich man get into Heaven” Duzza: “What? Hmmm, I gonna have to check that out one day… but now I’m trying to win the lottery, it’s like a super-dooper roll over” On the TV the host finally stops talking about nothing and starts to introduce the draw Host: “There has not been a winner of this lottery for almost two months now. This is the biggest cash prize ever heard of, with $300 million available. But now remember, you have to get all 6 balls, all 6 to win any money. Now let’s get the balls rolling” Some flashy music starts playing and the balls start spinning. Duzza meanwhile, holding his ticket close sits down on the couch. Host: “And the first ball is…. 7” Duzza: “YES! Haha, c’mon” Host: “And the second ball is… 34” Duzza: “Wooo! Who da man?” Host: “And the third ball for the super-duper roll over is… 29” Duzza: “HAHAH! That’s three! I’m too good” Host: “And the forth ball is… 17” Duzza: “Good Lord! Four balls! We’re gonna be rich-er” Host: “And the fifth ball is… 1” Duzza: “Hell yeah! Nigga what?! Yeah, 5 balls. C’mon one ball left, God, please let this be, c’mon one ball, one more ball please be 18, please be 18, please be 18” Host: “And the final ball for the $300 million cash prize is… 19” Duzza: “ Yea…. NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! No, this can’t be, I was so close, no, why, why is this happening to me?” Host: “And let’s just check those numbers, that’s 1, 7, 17, 19, 29 and 34, and reports are saying that there is just one winner, who will scoop the entire $300 million” Duzza: “Well… F*CK, I was this close, this close?! Damn, damn who ever won that money, damn them to hell” At that moment in time, the phone starts ringing, Duzza answers Duzza: “Who this, and f*ck do you want?” Voice: “DUZZAAAAAAA!” Duzza: “What tha f*ck? Who is this?” Voice: “It’s me! It’s Pookie! Ya n*gga” Duzza: “Pookie! What do you want?” Pookie: “Well dawg, y’know that super-duper roll over yeah?” Duzza: “Yeah I know about that no good, bitch ass thing, why?” Pookie: “Well…. I WON!!” Duzza: “WHAT?” Pookie: “Haha, that’s right, I’m a multi-millionaire, just like you” Duzza: “I don’t belive it, you! How did you even afford the ticket?” Pookie: “I like robbed a liquor store…but still, I’m a multi-millionaire! Now I can get us all out of the hood man” Duzza: “That’s nice man but, why did you ring me? To rub it in my face? And how the f*ck did you get this number?” Pookie: “I got connections y’know? And man, I called ya cause man, you be the only homeboy that out of Harlem and livin’ it up man. So like, I need your advice, what to do with the cash and stuff” Duzza: “Pookie, I’ll give you one quick bit of advice for free. If n*gga’s find out you got cash, your in trouble. Get the f*ck out of Harlem now man, I’m serious” Pookie: “Ok… I can do that… hey Duzz” Duzza: “What?” Pookie: “Do you think, like tomorrow we could go shopping?” Duzza: “What? Shopping? That money gone to your brain? I ain’t going shopping like no fag man, geez” Pookie: C’mon dawg, go buy a couple of cars, maybe get a house, c’mon it’ll be cool” Duzza: “Oooh car shopping! Sorry man, thought you meant like girly shopping” Pookie: “Hell no! Man, I ain’t no bitch n*gga, I just wanna spend my cash bro, simple as that” Duzza: “ Ok dawg, I’ll come and pick you up aight? Tomorrow morning, nine am. We can go pick up your cheque or whatever they give you, then we’ll go off and get some cars yeah?” Pookie: “Ok Duzz, I’lll see you later then” Duzza: “Yeah, peace” Duzza puts the phone down and slowly walks into the living room. He has a shocked expression on his face. Brittany: “Malek, what is it?” Duzza: “It’s… It’s… Pookie” Brittany: “Pookie? That lil fool? What has he done this time? He’s got himself shot hasn’t he” Duzza: “ No… He… I can’t say it” Brittany: “C’mon Malek, it’s best to talk about these things. Now what has happened to Pookie?” Duzza: “He…won… the… lottery…” End Of Part 1...
