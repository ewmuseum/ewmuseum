New Year High

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31.12.02 Dante Jackson is many things in life, about enough money to resurrect Enron from the dead, but in all arrogance - he'd never do it. I mean, when you have money from different things such as an outstanding music career with Shady Records, a wrestling contract which will keep him wealthy until he falls off the face of the earth and a soon to be wife and child, what was missing? To him, winning was. He'd come close to victory in the Continental title matches, but you knew for certain that since 'Xavier Kannon' won, he'd be the first in line for a title shot. 'Attention please, ladies and gentlemen - WE HAVE ACHIEVED VICTORY!' VICTORY! With those words echoed into the air, the clinking of glasses rings into the ears of the people as the scene opens up towards the Hilton Hotel in downtown Houston Texas. A gather of people have come out just to celebrate Dante Jackson's some what victory, as he stands around, pacing and joking with friends. His wife Tracy isn't here tonight, busy babysitting Simmone. He chuckles, before walking over towards a Spanish man, laughing while sipping a bottle of Tequila. Dante chuckles, before speaking to the man. Dante: 'Hey, if it isn't my lower immigrant friend!' Mexican Man: '!Que?!' Dante: 'Just what are you sipping on? (looking at the bottle's label) 'Ol' Cheap Mexican Wine That Looks Like Tequila?!' Mexican Man: '!Si!' The Lyrical Messiah swipes the bottle out of the man's hands, before shoving it on the floor. The Mexican looks at the bottle, then looks up at Dante with his face red in anger. Mexican Man: '!MIERDE! PUTO MAOQUEVOS!' Dante: 'What did you just say?' Mexican Man [in Speedy Gonzales voice]: 'Nothin' boss.' He shakes his head, before moving out towards the bar, looking at the latin woman serving drinks, before growing a smile on his face. Dante: 'Let me have a Bacardi Dark.' Latin Woman: '!No, de nada!' Dante: 'Why?' Latin Woman: '!Justice League novos push de WWE!' Dante: 'You aren't serving me because Rent A Bero's superman stable collapsed and you were his biggest fan? WHY THE HELL ARE YOU AT MY PARTY ANYWAY?!' Latin Woman: 'I suggest, that you take that up with my deportation officer.' Dante [rolls eyes]: 'Oh forget it, I was going to pour this for my past homies, but no you deny me that. Now they're burning in hell in the land for Mexican Whores and AIDS carrying people! Since I'm not drunk, I good a bid evening to you - puta.' The Lyrical Messiah sighs at the woman, before stepping away from the bar and almost towards the exit of the doors, overviewing everyone inside of the room. Here he ends up finding whites, black, latinos, and even albinos. Dante steps through the rotating door, finding himself in eighty degree heat, and traffic during the Summer nights. Dante: 'Ain't this a bitch? My own party, BUNK!' Dante shudders to himself, before a man behind him in a burgundy trenchcoat and a matching hat steps out. He is black, a grey and black goatee and a bony figure. The Man: 'You are in need of help...' Dante: 'What?' Hearing the voice, he turns around trying to find the man, but only happening to see clear smokey air. Shaking his head, he turns his head; only to find the man appearing out of nowhere! What nowhere? Maybe the one with a factory, and a tennis court for black men who think they can play. I've heard it has all day whitey vision, and Rodney's Revenge, where the four cops get beat up themselves!? HA! Anyway... Dante: 'AH! Damn Bill Cosby for making that shitty movie Leonard Part 6!' The Man: 'Or maybe, ITS THE CONFORMIST WAYS OF THE VAST BLACK AND WHITE CONSPIRACY!' Dante: 'Just where are you getting at old fool? You kinda look like -' The man steps out of the darkness, to turn out looking like Bill Cosby from the movie The Meteor Man! The Man: 'I am not Bill Cosby, and remember that.' Dante: 'But you really do look like -' The Man: 'I'm not Bill Cosby.' Dante: 'You look exactly like him...' Man: 'I AM NOT THAT RASPY FREAK BILL COSBY! I AM NOT THAT FAT CHESSY RETARD MOTHERFREAKER FAT ALBERT! GET THAT SHIT FREAKING CORRECT!' The Lyrical Messiah raises his right eyebrow at the man, before continuing to walk down the street, with the man following behind him. The Man: 'You can call me Jules.' Dante [sensing recognition]: 'Where's Vincent, Jules? Where's your bible quote Ezeikel 25:17, Jules?' The Man: 'Fine! Call me the Man Who Looks Like Famous Black Actors, or maybe just M.W.L.L.F.B.A.' Dante snickers at the name, before continuing his walk. M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby) staggers around, his own mind warped by being on the streets and brought up by a house filled with no hope, no progress, and nothing but profanity. Dante: 'How do you come up with a name like that?' M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby): 'Well you know how you've seen Hollywood and black actors aren't the people you once thought of? That's how.' Dante: 'So your pissed at Hollywood?' M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby): 'No, I'm pissed at the motherfreakers who shotty @ss botches thought I wasn't freaking good enough for their motherfreaking shitty botch ass movie!' Dante: 'Ah, just like Magicians?' M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby): 'Those are the biggest freaking fakes I've ever freaking seen in my freaking life, the shotty freaking freakers!' The Lyrical Messiah only nods his head at the judgement of his newfound friend, as they try to hail a taxi cab, busy running on these sunny nights. Another person, a few stores down happened to be carrying a quart of rum in his right hand. Dante thought to help the man, calling out his name a few times. Dante: 'Hey!' The drunkard, riases hsi head, turning towards Dante and a drunken glee of happiness or depression, or whatever. He wore a flamingo shirt, bucket hat and a pair of non matching kahki shorts. Drunk [mumbling]: 'WHAT?! GET AWAY FROM ME YOU BEJEEZUS ANIMALS!' In his eyes, he saw Dante and M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby) as huge monster serpents trying to crawl into his view and eat him alive. He waves around the bottle, in a druken sense, clocking Dante in the face, with about a pellet of Mescaline dropping into his mouth as he hits the concrete. M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby) didn't say a word, yet congradulating the man. * * * On mescaline, it was one of those 'perfect drugs' for New Years, and how I wished we happened to be there - I'd end up looking like the town drunk found in an ol' Irish novel. Here I found myself stuck in a hotel room, bright and early in the morning. With the largest freaking hotel bill I had ever seen in my life. Then I heard a voice, saying something like... Dante[simeltaneous with narration]: 'Holy crap, I've landed in King country.' Stepping into the elevator, I was high as a kite, soaring majestically on a mescaline high that I hadn't done before and probably didn't choke upon. The door pulls opens, and I look to be the stupidest man inside of this hotel. I had a suitcase of about 35 illegal narcotices ranging from ether to marijuana - all of this happened to be in the owner M.W.L.L.F.B.A (Also Known As Bill Cosby); it was his. Dante: 'That rat fink!' I mumbled to myself, trying to stay quiet. In front of me, a Wisconsion police chief happened to be arguing with the clerk. In my imagination, I was hearing this man ramble on about how he'd beens screwed over by crazy police chiefs wanting rooms and now it was his turn. Me? I prayed for him to make it out alive, and of course myself. Dante: 'Hey, how ya doing?' I stepped right in front of the wild beast, his neurotic wife and the clerk. Nice people I thought, high on so many drugs Marion Barry would have called heaven. Dante: 'Look, (turns back and faces the couple) sorry if I've stepped in your way or anything, but you people REALLY are taking too much time. Now (looks at the man's nameplate) Sven, I have my bill paid and I want to get out of your registered hell-hole for the mortally retarded.' Sven: 'Well sir, I thank you for choosing our hell-hole, please come back soon!' Was this man out of his mind? Or did the cop do him in so many places verbally, he just snapped? I was out of there, climbing into an Ol' School Shark; candy red convertable and drove backwards at 45 mph. I could have asked for the hydraulics hook up, but that would have been getting more gadets and crap that only the fakes that work the crap happen to know. It freaked me out, the whole scene of me stumbling out of a Houston hotel, flipping off the man who I was to pay the bell, before finding a sweaty albino man chuckling his head off. Dante: 'Holy hippopotamus!' Did he hear me? Or was a thinking to myself? The main thing a man like myself could hope for was did Rent A Hero happen to notice my own skills? Maybe yet, he shares the same vain expression I have towards people! Yeah that's it! Wait a minute, what if he hates me? I never thought of that, but if he does and screws up like that idiot Chet King did in our tag match then...he'll become a freakin' hoe - I put in check. Dante: 'Frick off...' I drove off into the sunset, close to the nearest airport I could find. You drive until your foot tingles with frozen bloon and asleep toes. The I drove some more. Happy New Year, and more drugs to be passed around tonight
