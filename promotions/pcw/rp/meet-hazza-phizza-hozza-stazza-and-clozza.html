Meet ‘Hazza, Phizza, Hozza, Stazza and Clozza’!!

Date: not entered Last week on Turmoil there was a serious security breach at a local recording studio where Flaymin was busy recording his duet with Teenage Sensation, Michelle Branch.Violence Jack was behind a series of sabotage ploys aimed at stalling Flaymin’s music career. So Flaymin sat down and thought long and hard about it.…..And 3 seconds he came up with the answer:‘Hazza, Phizza, Hozza, Stazza and Clozza’. [Scene opens outside a Sony Recording Studio in Manhattan, New York.A white van with blacked out windows pulls up outside the building.The driver’s side door flings open and out jumps Flaymin Aymin decked in his favourite tight, bright orange ‘WHAM!’ t shirt. Flaymin walks over to the side of the van and opens the rear sliding door.One by one, The Pharaoh’s Bodyguards in Training emerge- Harry, Phillip, Clifford, Horacio and Stanley. The five inept guards stand around scratching themselves as they wait for Flaymin’s next order] Flaymin: Right.First of all welcome to New York, Ladies. [The men all look at the surrounding environment, checking out the bustling city life and New York skyline] Flaymin: Now I have brought you here for a reason. Harry: You want us to get you some food from KFC? Flaymin: No.I want you all to assume the position. [The five men look at one other before they all kneel on the ground with their asses hanging out] Flaymin: Sheesh, you do that in this part of town and you’ll get more than a foot shoved up your backside. Harry: Then what position did you want us to assume? [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I don’t know, I’ve just always wanted to say that. [The five men get up and wipe their shirts and pants clean of all the dirt and grime that was on the sidewalk] Flaymin: Now, last week there was a major problem when that bald headed fool disrupted my vocal lessons. Clifford: Kojak was here? Stanley: No, no, it was Anthony Hopkins! Flaymin: Idiot, I said BALD not OLD!!Haven’t you ever heard of Violence Jack?? Clifford: Sorry boss, we don’t watch gay porn. Flaymin: You don’t know what your missi….err… I mean neither do I…. Yeah… Ahem, now the thing is I have enlisted you five men to guard this building from any attempts by Violence Jack to break in. So I want you to all stand outside the door and only allow the important looking folks.Got it? Harry: Do you qualify as important? Flaymin: Of course I’m important- not just anyone wins the Extreme Title three times, you know. Stanley: And also jobbed it three times… Flaymin: Hey! [Flaymin reaches out and slaps Stanley across the back of the head] Flaymin: Now remember men, you must guard the building with all your might.Remember how last week I took you on that training expedition of tackling trains? [The men all nod their heads] Flaymin: Well forget all about that because there aren’t any trains here. Phillip: What about the subway? Flaymin: Does this look like a time to eat? Phillip: I didn’t mean that kind of sub… Flaymin: Shut it, Steve! Phillip: You mean Phillip…. Flaymin: Yes, that’s another thing we need to address- your crappy names. Phillip: But sir, I am named after English royalty! Harry: As am I! Flaymin: Dirty Harry was a monarch? Harry: Prince Harry! [Flaymin frowns] Flaymin: Never heard of him.But the fact of the matter of this fact of that matter is the fact that this matter must be resolved. [The five bodyguards all stare at Flaymin in total confusion] Flaymin: Got it? [Still lost in bewilderment, the men shake their heads. Flaymin sighs and then scratches his afro] Flaymin: Well look, I’m going to have to give you all new names.Australian names. Harry- you will now be known as Hazza. Phillip- You are now Phizza Clifford- You will be… [Clifford interrupts Flaymin] Clifford: Clifton? Flaymin: Uh no…. you’ll now be addressed as Clozza. Horacio- Your new name is Hozza. Stanley- Congratulations you have earned yourself the eternally cool name of Stazza. [Harry…err…. ‘Hazza’ steps forward and puts his hand up] Flaymin: Yes? Hazza: Does that mean we can call you Flazza? Flaymin: Flazza?How on earth did you come up with that!?!That doesn’t even make the slightest bit of sense in comparison to the slick names I’ve given you all. Hazza, Clozza, Hozza, Stazza and Phizza!Now you sound like a force to be reckoned with!There isn’t a single man on earth who would dare to mess with the might of guards with names like this. Stazza: What about Superman? Flaymin: Fool.Superman is make-believe. Phizza: Batman? Flaymin: Batman is a pussy cartoon character too. Hazza: He-Man? Flaymin: Damn, I forgot all about him.How the f*ck are we supposed to stop THE Master of the Universe? Clozza: Maybe we could try and track down Skeletor’s whereabouts?I’m sure there’s a phone book lying around here somewhere. [Flaymin strokes his chin in serious thought] Flaymin: Hmmm…. No that wouldn’t work because we don’t have that much time.Does anyone know where Captain Planet lives? Hozza: I swear I saw him buying a cheeseburger at McDonalds not too long ago. [Flaymin looks at his watch] Flaymin: Gee, look at the time, I gotta go and start recording soon.I’ll leave you guys to it. If anything happens, just use kryptonite to protect yourselves. Phizza: But sir!Where are we supposed to get kryptonite? Flaymin: I don’t know….Go buy some gum and colour it with a green crayon or something.You figure it out. [Flaymin looks at his watch one more time before dashing into the building, late for his recording session. Meanwhile the five bodyguards all stand around, busy formulating a plan of attack, should He-Mean unexpectedly drop in for a little rendezvous…….] Scene fades to black
