Breakfast Meeting

16 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 16, 2003 Begin Transmission. (The day is an important one. For SOCIETY, it is his most difficult and strenuous test to date. He must face two members of the Rising Star Division simultaneously. Michael Chang is inside, rapidly dialing and yelling away into his cellular phone, as SOCIETY is content to sit at a table in the restaurant, consuming his early morning meal of eggs, sausage, and pancakes.) “…I will not tolerate this embarrassment!” (Chang was infuriated, as SOCIETY simply took another bite of his scrambled egg.) “SOCIETY, stop eating and listen to me!” (The big man dropped his fork temporarily, and looked up at his ally.) “Yes, Mr. Chang?” “Our original plan for post-Supremacy has, shall we say, fallen through…” “Blast!” (SOCIETY was not pleased in the least bit. The plan Michael had given him was sound, very sound indeed. But, all was not lost…) “It could be worse though, Mr. Chang. I’ve known you a long time now, and I know that you ALWAYS have a backup plan.” “Indeed I do, SOCIETY. THAT is why I have been on the phone all day, making calls to various people that may be needed for this to work.” “Has Mr. Krikorian been notified?” “Yes. He’s a full member now. I keep him up to date on all of our actions.” “Good. His donation to us was quite, generous, wouldn’t you say?” (Chang smiled, recalling the amount…) “Yes, it was indeed. The money he put in will be repaid tenfold after we gain more victories. We will raise the funding and the surveillance needed to find his parents’ executioners, and then, he can go on his merry way.” “You mean, we’re kicking him out of the Order then? “Kicking him out? He’ll ASK us to leave. You said it yourself. You and he don’t especially like each other. This is an arrangement of business and convience. We get an ally that will work for the cause, he gets the resources of the Order of Society and Logic to help him find his parents murderers.” (The egg gone, SOCIETY begins work on his pancakes, to Chang’s approval.) “Eat well. You have a busy day today. It is important that you win this match to keep up in the standings.” “The standings. How tired I am of the standings! I wish I could be like the rest of the superstars, and not have to worry about standings!” (Chang’s response was simple, effective, and pretty much true.) “Well, if you do well in this round robin tournament, you won’t need to WORRY about standings. You will exit the Rising Star Division. You will be your own man.” “Ah, for that day to come would be excellent indeed. Supremacy beckons, and with it, my chance to get my hands on a championship. To become a champion would vindicate my work thus far.” (Michael looked over at SOCIETY, who was back to his pancakes, and even beginning to nibble slightly on his bacon.) “Even titles, in the long run, will not be the greatest measure of your success. Neither will victories or defeats. For you, success will be measured by your ability to instill fear in the hearts of foes and fans alike. When “Suite-Pee” begins to play, the man in the ring waiting for you must KNOW, THEN AND THERE, that he has no prayer in his contest with you. Once you instill fear into the heart of this roster, there is NOTHING that you cannot do!” “I certainly hope so,” SOCIETY said, “Because I’m facing two guys later today, and I really can’t afford to lose.” (Chang sighed.) “I know SOCIETY. I know. And you’ve got a busy day on Tuesday as well. But you can handle it.” “I have been trained to handle anything. That’s what you told me before we left the Falklands.” “And it is equally true today, SOCIETY. You have all the necessary tools. You have only truly fallen once in combat. Last Tuesday was not your error that cost your tandem the match. But keep that in mind. One day, you will have the chance to avenge that loss. One day, soon, you will be facing the likes of Brianna Washington and Violence Jack in one on one competition. An environment you are much better suited for, because, let’s face it…SOCIETY doesn’t play well with others.” (At that comment, SOCIETY grinned..) “You’re right. I don’t play very well with others.” “And that is exactly why you are to be feared.” “And that is exactly why others will CONFORM…TO…SOCIETY.” (Fade out, as SOCIETY finishes off his meal.) End Transmission.
