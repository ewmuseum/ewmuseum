Coping with Villainy - Part 3 - Get My Henchmen, I command it!

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 25, 2003 [Tommy knows that there are key elements in being a very good super villain. First, is to be as dastardly and evil as possible, which is something that he has pulled off quite nicely. Second, it's good to be a wealthy super villain, which is something that Tommy has accomplished when he was just a sprouting teenager. Third, henchmen. That's right, evil henchmen. He can't always do the dirty work when it comes to his super villain ways, and what better way than to have large and in charge henchmen to do that work for him. That's why he needs to money AND the henchmen will make him more evil. So, it works out for everybody in the end, and totally bogus losers get a chance to make money for being brainless and strong. It's going to work, and all Tommy needs is to find the perfect henchmen] [The henchmen idea came to Tommy as they searched for the car. The odd Southern California suburb has become quite an oddity to Tommy as everything looks EXACTLY the same. The houses, the driveways, the front yards, and even their dogs have the same distinct features! With that aside, Tommy and his stoned brother, Harry, have finally found the 2002 blue Mustang convertible. If Tommy's memory would have been working at all he'd have known that he parked the car at the main gates of the housing project. He even tipped the security guard one dollar to make sure no birds pooped on the car. Harry felt one dollar was too much, but Tommy felt it to be reasonable. There was no bird poop, the security guard was nice, and Tommy took his dollar back. Why? Well, he IS a super villain after all!] [As Tommy, in his blue and yellow Armani suit, arrives back at his beach house, he's immediately greeted by the ever so popular Gregory Gastion, super agent and businessman. The dark skinned Gregory looks to be quite upset at the doorstep of Tommy's home. After the phone conversation earlier in the night, Greg needs to get a few things straight to his client before he ends up in a straight jacket] Gregory: We need to have a talk, Tom. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Of course, compadre. But you have to watch out when walking through the front door, I have a series of totally bodacious laser beams ready to kill any intruders. [Tommy unlocks the lock on the sliding glass door and enters the home cautiously, as if there REALLY ARE laser beams in the house. Gregory and Harry just walk in behind Tommy as if there is nothing going on, just staring at the PCW Continental Champion as if he were absolutely losing his mind, which he probably is] Gregory: There are no laser beams. And your not a super villain. TTT: Yes, I am. Gregory: No, your not. You need to freakin' drop this act right now, because it's already getting old! I'm your agent and I know EXACTLY what is good for you and what's not. I know that you need to relax, get some sleep, and shake this stupid delusion that your having. Then it's getting down to the nitty gritty of your training to make sure that your ready for Rent-A-Hero on Sunday. TTT: Dude, I was totally born ready to take that loser out. Gregory: That's good. You have some confidence in you, which is NEVER a problem. Because I need you to keep your head in this, Tommy. Your the PCW Continental Champion. Your like an ambassador of PCW and we're getting GREAT marketing from this. If you LOSE that title then you won't be nearly as marketable, you get it? [Tommy has thrown his shiny blue blazer onto the leather couch, and is browsing through his CD collection for 'The Beach Boys Greatest Hits'. If he didn't need to hear their soothing sounds he'd pay more attention to what the bogus agent is saying, but even if he were it still wouldn't matter to him. He's a super villain, he's not supposed to listen to people, only to himself. And right now he wants to recruit evil henchmen and go beat people up at random. Why? Because it sounds like it will be bodacious fun] TTT: Greg, I need you to get in contact with those totally heinous mob dudes. Get me some henchmen. Greg: Your NOT a super villain. [Tommy walks over to his agent and seems to be consoling him] TTT: Dude, it's ok. I know it's hard to cope with, but sometimes these things just happen. Harry: Yeah, man. [Gregory can't believe that he even associated himself with Tommy. He stole The Tubular One from right out under Semore Tishky so that he could get those chain of water parks going. He knows that Tommy is a gold mine and he can only keep producing the gold if he is kept in line. With his fixation on Rent-A-Hero and wanting to prove his superiority he's come under some messed up delusion that he's a freakin' super villain. That Rent-A-Hero is the star of the comic book, the hero that everyone looks up to, and the guy that saves the day. Tommy spent years believing that he was that guy and seeing Rent-A-Hero, and hearing the boos he gets, Tommy has finally lost it. He's given in to his stress and flaws. He may be a villain of sorts, but he sure isn't a super villain] TTT: Dude, Harry can be my main henchman. Greg: That's stupid. TTT: You know, your right! It is stupid! Harry will be my SIDEKICK! Greg: Super villains DON'T have sidekicks. Only heroes do. TTT: Well...that's just totally bogus. He'll be my EVIL TWIN BROTHER! Greg: He's not your twin!! TTT: If it's what I want, then it'll be done, dude! I command it! I'M THE SUPER VILLAIN! NOT YOU! [All Greg can do is bury his head into his hands and watch his dreams go up in smoke. That future that he wanted with a supermodel and a house in the woods? It's all gone now. The child he wanted to have that would carry on the mantle of Gastion's? That's NOT gone, but the child will end up ugly if Greg isn't filthy rich. But for some reason he has doubts and feels that Tommy is going to blow this oppourtunity that he has. The guy has wanted to prove himself ever since he stepped into a wrestling ring, mostly to his dead father. Now he's finally gotten that chance to prove himself and to gain what his father never did. He's going to throw it all away for his foolish pride] TTT: I want a big henchman and a REALLY little henchman...you know one of those little wiry guys. Those dudes are TOTALLY bodacious. [The is a long pause as Tommy is obviously contemplating something] TTT: UNIFORMS!! They need bodacious uniforms. Harry: Twisted Sister, man. TTT: Meh...I was thinking something in blue and yellow. Greg: God, save me. [Fade to black] To be continued...
