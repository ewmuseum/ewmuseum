Date: Season I, Episode 2 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason IEpisode 2************************************ The Task at Hand Snow has a hard time finding his way through the dark of the mysterious room as he gropes atthe walls for support. Suddenly, he runs into the firm hairy body of Shena, his talking horse. “Damn it!” he curses as he falls to the ground. “I didn’t see you there.” “I can’t see nothing at all!” barks O’Reilly, the leprechaun, from some unknown area in themysteriously dark room. “It’s like the inside of an elephants twat in here, I tells ya! I don’tknow how you come up with these things, Snow!” “Me either.” “How do you feel about going to New York?” Shena asks him in her softess of voices. “I’m not sure,” Snow sighs. “I mean, on one hand, I think it’s what I have to do. On the otherhand, it’s some scary shit, you know? I mean, I don’t even know who these people are that I’mgoing to see. They might just be waiting there to put a bullet in the back of my head. I don’tfucking know.” “Why do you surround yourself with bad people?” Shena asks. “I’m no fucking prize either.” “Ya got that right, ya bastard!” O’Reilly sneers from the opposite side of the room. “Bad things and bad situations follow me,” Snow shrugs as he makes his way away from Shenaand the wall of the room, the only support that he knows. “Sometimes I worry about Haleythough. I mean, she’s my daughter and this shit that keeps happening is obviously going toaffect her someday, if it hasn’t affected her already.” “You care for her deeply,” Shena nods. “Of course I do,” Snow says as he finds himself further away from the horse, yet their voices areas loud and clear as ever. “I always have.” “You missed her first birthday, you know,” Shena says to him. She has always had a way ofreminding him of things he would rather not think about. “While you were in prison.” “What the fuck!?” Snow snaps at the horse. “What are you trying to do here!? I know this shitalready! You think this doesn’t eat at me every fucking day!?” “I know it does,” Shena says calmly. “Then why the fuck do you keep bringing shit like this up?” “It’s my nature.” she replies. Suddenly, Snow feels something crunch beneath his shoe - it’s O’Reilly’s tiny foot, and theleprechaun doesn’t take kindly to it. “AAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!!!” he shrieks. Snow feels a tiny fist punch him in the leg. Then another, then another. “I’ll fuck yer mother and stab her cold heart with me pecker!” he curses. “I’ll slash yer throatwith a mules arse, God damn ye!” “Easy!” Snow commands as the leprechaun begins clawing at his leg. “Don’t make me hit youback, you little bastard!” “Stop fighting!” orders Shena from across the dark room - her voice is distant now. “You bothknow that fighting one another is... destructive.” Reluctantly, Snow steps away from the tiny leprechaun, who swings away at the air in the darkroom. “Come back here, ya sissy bastard!” he says in his thick Irish accent. “I’ll rip yer skinfrom bone and feed yer innards to the that fuckin’ horse, I will!” Snow tries to hold back his anger as he stumbles through the dark room. Suddenly, a realizationstrikes him - what if there’s a staircase in the room? It’s too late. No more than the thoughtcrosses his mind, he finds one, falls harshly down it, and dies. Location: New York CityDate: Monday, November 25, 2002Start of Scene Time: 9:35 pmShot: Limousine - Interior Snow felt uneasy in the back of the long black stretch limo, staring across from his seat at threemen in the most expensive suits he’d ever seen. Well, two of them were expensive - the otherlooked like something he remembered his grandfather wearing to church on Sunday when theold man was too stubborn to wear anything other than the suit he’d been wearing for twentysome-odd years. On either side of Snow sat two large Italian men with their arms crossed over their chest. Obviously, these men where there for Justin Hattoni’s protection, in case Snow turned out to besomething other than what the late Don of the Hattoni family described him to be. Justin Hattoni, the new Don, sat directly opposite Snow in the back of the limousine, staring athim with a smile meant to be gentle, but to Snow, was nothing short of menacing. He looked somuch like his father, the man Snow protected in his first two months in prison. No one left aliveknew that Snow eventually turned on Doug Hattoni, the old Don, and delivered him into thehands of his enemies. He deserved it, thinks Snow, but no one will ever know whatreally happened in Doug’s final days on Earth. “Mr. Snow,” Justin’s said in a voice that eerily rang of his father’s raspy tones. “I thank you forcoming all this way. My father spoke extremely fondly of you in his final days.” Snow expectedformality. “He was a good man,” Snow lied. Justin nodded. His grey hair betrayed his youthful face, and if Snow didn’t know Doug’s age, hewouldn’t be sure if the man sitting across from him was twenty-five of fifty. He wore littleglasses that were so small that one might not notice at a glance, and his mouth was almostalways held in that familiar purse - just like the old Don, Doug Hattoni. “So Jason,” the man to Justin’s left said, “or should I call you Mr. Snow?” “Just call me Snow. It’s what I’m used to.” “I’m Max,” the man said with a smile. “Max Hattoni.” Snow nodded to Doug’s second son. Max looked young. He was definitely no more than thirty, likely a few years Justin’s junior. “So tell me,” he continued, “you don’t happen to be Italian? I mean, no Italian blood at all?” “Not a drop,” Snow answered. “One hundred percent Irish, by descent.” “Ah,” Max said, leaning back in his seat. He glanced over at his brother and nervously asked:“Does that change anything?” “It changes nothing,” answered Justin, his eyes never shifting from Snow’s face. “Our fatherspoke highly of him, Max. That’s the only thing that matters.” “Of course,” Max said with a pleasant smile, turning his attention to Snow. “Forgive me,” Justin suddenly said to Snow, “I haven’t introduced you to my associates. To myright, as he’s told you, is my brother Max. To my left, Johnny Rosalini, known in the family asJohnny Silver.” He made no mention of the large figures on either side of Snow. Johnny Silver was the one in the cheaper suit, and Snow had no doubt where his nick name camefrom. His hair was one complete tone - a unit - of silver, and combed completely forward tocover the fact that he had obviously been bald for years. He was the oldest of the men in the car,probably nearing his sixties. When he smiled, Snow noticed one of his two front teeth had beenreplaced with gold. “Hey kid,” he said. “You probably recognized my name - Rosalini.” Snow shook his head. “You know,” Johnny persisted, “Rosalini spaghetti sauce?” Snow shook his head again. “Ah come on,” Johnny said, “everyone knows Rosalini’s spaghetti sauce! My mother startedmaking it over thirty years ago! It’s all over the country now!” “I’m more of a lasagna person,” Snow said. “You don’t know what you’re missing kid,” Johnny silver said, leaning back in his seat. “I’m sure your curious about why you’re here,” Justin said, getting things back on track. “Iwon’t beat around the bush with you, Snow. In our business, a man who doesn’t need a gun tointimidate someone is a very valuable commodity.” Snow remembered Justin’s father, Doug Hattoni, saying the same thing when Snow had firstentered the Tank. “I want you to come to work for me,” Justin said. “Should you accept this offer, you can restassured that you’ll be under my own personal watch. You’ll start at the bottom of the family,like everyone else, but because of your service to my father, you’ll be put on a much faster trackto the top.” “You want me to join the Hattoni family?” Snow sounded surprised, though he had reallyexpected this. “I’m not even Italian.” “That doesn’t matter,” Justin told him. “Mr. Snow, I’m a very powerful man. And I know - Iknow - you crave power as well. In my father’s letters, he told me that beneath your coldexterior, you are a cunning man who does what’s in the best interest of his family. Now Mr.Snow, I want you to join my family.” “This is a bit startling,” Snow said, “to be honest.” “I’m sure it would be,” Justin nodded. “But should you accept this offer, rest assured that youwill be protected. Both you and your wife and child. In the Hattoni family, we take careof each other.” He sent Snow a pleasant smile. “Do I have time to think about it?” Snow asked. “You have until the morning,” nodded Justin. “At that time, Johnny Silver will come to youwith a job. You understand, I can not tell you the task here, because I don’t know you wellenough. Before I can give you dirt on me, I need dirt on you. That’s the system.” “We’re going to the hotel now,” added Max. “You can get a good night’s sleep. Think thingsover. If you decide you’re game, wear a suit - there will be plenty in the closet of your suite tochoose from. Look sharp.” “But remember, Mr. Snow,” Justin said in a finalizing voice, “this offer will not renew itself. Should you refuse the task that Johnny comes to you with in the morning, we’ll fly you back toCanada, where we assume you’ll make no mention of this meeting.” “So are you going to do it?” Shena asks. She already knows the answer. “He offered to protect my family,” Snow says. “How can I refuse that?” They walk slowly through the heat and trees of the jungle while mute animals appear from timeto time. The sky is a bright red and blood clouds are forming just above the tallest of the trees. Snow steps over a log, keeping his eyes on the ground, instead of on the sky. “These aren’t good men you’re dealing with,” Shena says to him. “They be mighty rich though,” O’Reilly comments. “This can be a way out for me,” Snow says to his companions. “For my whole family, youknow. If this works out, I can give my family happiness and protection.” “And if it doesn’t work out?” Shena’s voice is cynical. “They’ll kill me, most likely,” Snow sighs. “But then they’ll take care of my family. At least,that’s the way it works in the movies, right?” The blood clouds clash together in the sky, sending a flashing red lightning bolt to the groundbelow. It strikes Snow, and he instantly dies from the shock. His body lies in a puddle in themidst of the jungle. Shena and O’Reilly have completely disappeared.
