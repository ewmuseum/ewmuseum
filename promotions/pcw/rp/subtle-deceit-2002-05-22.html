Subtle Deceit

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-22-02 PREVIOUSLY: After Trigger promising his Warriors In Training a reward the next day, co-trainer Lance Minion shows up instead, taking the WITs away. They sense some evil-doing, but are eager to get their reward, so ignore the feelings. They arrived at the diner around 7:00 AM. The parking lot was fairly empty, with only a few customers inside. Minion had claimed this would be the perfect spot to begin their day, with a hearty breakfast away from the crowds. The diner was like many that would be found in a quiet neighborhood, with its clean white paint and colorful blue roofing. Red letters cascaded over the top reading the name of the breakfast restaurant, and banners taped to the door read prices of special deals. The WITs didn't care however… they just wanted to eat. Inside the diner, atop a metal platform stood a sign that said 'Seat Yourself,' so accompanied by the WITs, Minion headed towards a booth near the window. He sat down, and beckoned the trainees to do as well. One by one they shuffled in, although all was feeling somewhat uncomfortable. There was definitely something wrong here. From the moment the WITs had met The Insane One, they knew something was wrong. He was wrong! He would use his sick mind tricks to manipulate any- and everyone. No life form was safe from Lance Minion. Even the way he spoke would send chills down your spine… and that is what he intended! Long ago Minion had learned that he was different from other people. Long ago he had learned that he had the power to do things no other man could. And long ago he had accepted this power, using it for cruel and sadistic torture. Nobody really knew much about The Insane One… just that he was bad and his plans were worse. Nobody really wanted to know much about him. To even think about delving into that pit of torture that was Minion's mind could send men running. The irony of the whole matter is, that although nobody really knew Minion, he was inside of them all. He was the kind of person that you regret meeting, and remember for the rest of your life. Minion was evil… the kind of murderous evil that made Dracula and Frankenstein look like children's fairy tales. His attitude and behavior was scary enough-- but what really made The Insane One frightening was the ability he had. All three WITs knew of the memorable battles between Trigger and Minion. They knew that the two had been bitter enemies for years in the past, and both claimed to be foes now. Lately however, things had not been the same. Trigger and Minion-- they were working together! They were acting friendly toward each other, even exchanging compliments. Trigger had taught the WITs about animosity long ago. Blood feuds were not gradually developed; they were immediate reactions of two humans meant to hate. That, Trigger said, was the case with he and The Insane One. They had been adversaries for the longest of times, and would be always. He claimed that Minion and he had simply set aside their differences for the interest of the WITs; for the interest of the training. The question was on the minds of all the WITs. Why does it seem like Trigger and Minion are genuinely getting along? Deep down, they all knew the answer. Someone was up to something, and judging from the character of each individual, it probably wasn't Trigger. So that left one man. Minion. What was he planning, and why was he planning it? The WITs hated the fact, but only time would tell. 'What can I get y'all today?' Everyone glanced up. Being lost in thought, nobody had realized that the waitress had come to their booth, ready to take orders. She had frizzy brown hair, tied in the back with a bun, and talked in a heavy southern drawl. Being unprepared, the WITs quickly snatched the menus as Minion ordered. 'I'll have the scrambled eggs dish,' he said, looking up at the waitress. As she got a glimpse of his face, she jumped back, frightened at the clown-like paint. She wrote down his choice, and faced WIT 2, not turning back to The Insane One. 'Umm… I'll have the same,' said the second WIT. Nodding impatiently, the waitress moved on to WIT 1. She wanted to get out of the situation fast, because as mentioned earlier: nobody wants to meet Minion, and those who do never forget him. 'I'll have an omelet, please,' answered WIT 1. WIT 3 spoke up without being asked. 'I'd like four slices of French toast with two pancakes, a side of sausage, and some eggs.' Out of the four men there, WIT 3 was by far the largest. His six foot eleven body towered over the others, and furiously bulging muscles made him stand out more. Without even writing down the order, the waitress almost ran away from the odd table, surely not to return. In the mean time, WIT 3 dared to strike up conversation with The Insane One. 'So why exactly are you taking us for our reward instead of Trigger?' he inquired. This was apparently not a good idea. With lighting speed, Minion's eyes darted into the third WIT's, chilling his body like a fever. When he spoke, his voice quivered with anger and even the brave WIT 3 slunk back in his booth. 'That is not of importance. Trigger asked me to do this, and so I shall! There will be no more questions.' Even to the WITs, who were used to this kind of menacing reaction, Minion's outburst was surprising. There was definitely more to this story than that which Minion was letting on. All the WITs were dying to know more, but were afraid to ask The Insane One anything else. After waiting nearly half of an hour, the attention of the party was grabbed by an altercation taking place in the kitchen. They heard the worried voice of a woman, and then a middle-aged man walked out into view, nervously heading towards the booth with Minion and the WITs. In both hands, he carried a large platter containing three plates. On one was a set of eggs, on another lay a steaming omelet, and on the third was a variety of foods. The server placed down the meals in front of WIT 1, 2, and 3, then hurried back to the kitchen without a word. Not pausing a second, the Warriors In Training dived into their food, and were devouring it quickly when they were interrupted with a loud bang on the table. Looking up, all three saw that Minion had pounded in anger. It seemed the server had forgotten his food. The WITs watched closely as he abruptly stood up from the booth, and paced heavily towards the kitchen. Ignoring the 'Employees Only' sign, he marched in, where a loud commotion followed. First a shrill scream, and a clatter of plates and broken glass. A moment later, The Insane One walked calmly out again, as if nothing had happened. He did not sit down, however; instead he addressed the group of WITs. 'There's been a misunderstanding. We must go.' And with that, he shuffled the three WITs up and out the door of the diner. Minion took one last look around the place, and then closed the door behind him. Back at the abandoned shop, Trigger had just entered. He walked to the back and opened the door to the WITs' Quarters, expecting to find his three pupils hard at work. Instead, he found…nothing. He looked around more closely. Still, there was nobody there but him. 'How could this be?' he thought to himself. 'The WITs aren't supposed to leave their room without my permission. They have behaved perfectly for two months. Why have they suddenly disappeared?' This was especially odd, since it was the day when the WITs were to get their monthly reward. He had told them the night before… why now would they leave? Trigger thought they should be overwhelming with joy. Could they be hiding? Training was very serious, but perhaps the Warriors In Training were playing a joke on him. He backed out into the main room, heading towards the most probable hiding spot: the closet. He crept slowly up to it, and then in a single movement flung the door open and looked inside. All he found was an antique television and dusty cleaning supplies. Now the panic began to really set in. Where could they possibly be? 'WITs? Where are you?' he tried calling to them, but no response came. 'WITs? Minion? Is anybody here?' Minion! His name shot into Trigger's mind so quickly it hurt. He didn't have a clue to what was going on, but he had a terrible feeling that The Insane One had something to do with it…
