Airport! joint rp w/Aymin,

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in a limousine parked outside the airport. The limousine is parked in the VIP section of the airport parking lot, surrounded by other high priced, fancy, European and Japanese cars. On the other side of the parking lot are all the crappy cars, the poorly designed cars, the cars that move as swiftly as a semi-trailer, the cars that are about as comfortable as a bare-camel ride throughout the Sahara Desert, the cars that are as confusing as a computer manual in Swahili, the kind of cars that run for five miles before they break down a.k.a ... The American cars. Inside the limousine sit Flaymin Aymin and Jenna Jameson as they wait for El Diablo to arrive from a flight. Jenna casually knocks back some vodka while Flaymin continually looks at his watch and frowns. Flaymin peers his head out of the open window and looks directly at the 'Arrivals' section of the airport] Flaymin: Where the f*ck is he?? He said he'd be here an hour ago. Jenna: Maybe the plane is late? Flaymin: Can't be. The plane landed an hour and a half ago. [Flaymin presses the button that lowers the window between the driver and the passenger in the limousine. Gradually the window winds down. Once the window is fully wound down, the driver turns around to face Flaymin] Driver: Can I help you sir? Flaymin: Is this the right gate? What if he came out of a different gate? Driver: I'm positive this is the gate. I double-checked the flight details. [Flaymin shakes his head in frustration and sits back in his seat. He begins to tap his fingers on the whisky bottle in front of him as he attempts to play a very bad sounding version of 'Living La Vida Loca' by Ricky Martin. After a minute or so he stops tapping the bottle. He peers his head out of the window, this time however he begins to smile] Flaymin: Hey look at that! Jenna: Has he arrived? Flaymin: Nope, but I just noticed that there are no shitty American cars in this entire V.I.P. Parking Lot! Nothing but fine European Cars like Volkswagen and Mercedes... and Ford and Chevrolet. Jenna: I thought they're American cars? Flaymin: Nope. Ford is Swedish. In Swedish 'Ford' means: 'Very good fast car with lots of leg room' Jenna: Are you sure? Flaymin: Definitely. And then there is the whole 'four-letter' thing. Swedish people always name their things out of nothing more then four letters. For example there is Ikea and ABBA. Anything more then four letters and they'll confuse themselves. Jenna: But the name 'Sweden' is more then four letters? Flaymin: And that's exactly where the confusion all began for them! Jenna: Riiiight. [The driver interrupts the stunning Automobile/Swedish History lesson and begins to talk to Flaymin] Driver: Sir I'd just like to remind you that you've only booked my services for an hour. Very shortly I'll have to attend to other commitments, unless your partner arrives soon. Flaymin: All right buddy, I don't think he'll be too much longer. [15 more uneventful minutes pass and El Diablo still hasn't shown up. At that point the limousine driver regretfully tells Flaymin and Jenna to exit the car. They both walk into the airport and continue to wait for El Diablo in there. As time wears on their frustration grows and they begin abusing all the little children that approach them seeking autographs.] Flaymin: I've had enough of this sh!t. Even I don't spend this long brushing my awesome hair in front of a mirror. Jenna: Wait, I think I see him! He's coming this way. [El Diablo and his wife Angel casually stroll over to Flaymin and Jenna] Flaymin: Where the hell have you been? [El Diablo just seems to ignore Aymin and Jenna] Flaymin: Diablo? Where have you been? [He looks at Aymin and smiles] El Diablo: Oh there you are, I've been looking all over the airport for you. [Aymin looks a little bewildered] Flaymin: You sure? El Diablo: Definitely. [Aymin and Jenna look at each other in shock] Jenna: Is this true Angel? [El Diablo looks at his wife] Angel: Um, yeah, my feet are killing me we did so much walking. [Aymin and Jenna don't seem to believe a word of what El Diablo is saying] El Diablo: We even put out a radio call for you in here. Flaymin: Oh really? [El Diablo and Angel nod as this meeting in the airport goes on] Flaymin: Got any proof? [El Diablo sighs] El Diablo: What? You don't believe me? [Jenna and Aymin stand arm in arm and don't look like they believe Diablo.] Flaymin: Well you knew we were waiting outside. We even had a limo. El Diablo: How could I have known? I was on an airplane. Jenna: But Flaymin told you before. [El Diablo and Angel look disgusted at the disbelief from El Diablo's Sabotage tag partner.] El Diablo: Well, I guess it slipped my mind. El Diablo: (under his breath) I'm sick of this. [Aymin hears Diablo say something but isn't sure what.] Flaymin: What was that? [El Diablo is a little stunned that the afroman even heard him.] El Diablo: Oh, nothing. [Aymin just nods, still obviously not believing him.] El Diablo: So you don't believe me about anything? [Aymin just shakes his head, indicating no.] El Diablo: At my wedding you turned up drunk, yet have protested you weren't ever since. Want to know something? Angel: We don't believe you. [Aymin just smiles and watches El Diablo's tirade go on.] El Diablo: You never trust me! How do you expect to tag with me if you don't trust me? [Just as El Diablo says that a man walks up behind him with a drink and taps El Diablo on the shoulder.] Man: Here is your drink sir, sorry about the two hour delay for it. [The man hands El Diablo the drink as El Diablo looks a little embarrassed and Aymin looks satisfied that he was correct.] El Diablo: Lets just get out of here now. [El Diablo drinks down what the man gave him, as all four of them head outside and signal a taxi.] Flaymin: Taxi! [A taxi stops by as Angel, Jenna and El Diablo sit in the back and Aymin sits in the front.] Taxi Driver: Where to? Flaymin: The finest hotel in Boise, Idaho. Taxi Driver: You got it. [The taxi starts to drive heading for the hotel.] Flaymin: So, are you ready for Sabotage? [El Diablo stops looking down at Jenna's cleavage and looks forward at Aymin.] El Diablo: Of course. Akiyama will pay for Sabotage, Turmoil and Fusion. [Aymin smiles. El Diablo has to remove Jenna's hand from his crotch as his wife Angel is sat next to him also.] El Diablo: Oh and I have a surprise set for you on Sabotage also. [Jenna and Aymin both smile.] Flaymin: Sounds good. [The taxi continues driving along. The scene fades out]
