The Tank 2 - Insomnia

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 8 Sunday, July 7 - Day 2 Miggy hurries toward the library pushing a cart of books in front of him - he’s been a loyal member of the prison library staff for years. Every day, he reports to work minutes after the morning buzzer and does his rounds, pushing his little cart throughout Units A and B asking the prisoners if they would like a book. Now, at nearly six-thirty, he’s just finished his return rounds - where he gathers the books and magazines that the inmates have finished - and is heading back to the library. This is the part of his job that he hates the most - to get to the library, he has to get through the gym... The Aryans usually pile in around six-thirty and work out as a group at the near side of the gym, while a group of large black men - they call themselves the Thugs - usually gather at the far end at around seven. And in the moments that the two groups are in close quarters with each other, the tension is thicker than the space between Meanstreak’s shoulders. Born in Mexico, Miggy can easily become the center of either groups torment, which is why he always hurries himself to drop off the books and get back to the main floor of Unit B before the groups show up. He wishes he had someone to protect him - to walk him through this hell whole of sweat stains and testosterone - but at his age, it’s too late to join the gang of Latinos who have recently been gaining a lot of notoriety around the Tank. “Well, well...” a familiar voice nearly shatters Miggy’s old nerves, “If it isn’t old Miggy...” The old Latino turns behind him to the front entrance of the gym where Harvey Blake, leader of a gang of White Supremacists, has just entered with a towel slung across his broad shoulders. “J-just bringing back the books,” Miggy says quietly to Blake - his nightmare - while pointing to the far door which leads into the library. “Books?” Harvey Blake says with a mock sense of seriousness, staring at Miggy through his one horrible eye. Behind him, several of his swastika-tattoo’ed followers are entering, curious to see why their leader is talking to little Miggy. “I like to consider myself an,” he pauses for dramatic effect, “avid reader.” Harvey reaches into Miggy’s cart of books. “You can’t!” Miggy says, reaching for Harvey’s hand - regretting both the words and the motion before a moment passes. Blake looks down at Miggy, mock confusion all over his face. “They have to be back in the library by six-thirty...” he says quietly. “Oh, do they?” Blake asks, turning back to his large companions - all of them are laughing at the helpless little Latino by now. “Y-yes,” Miggy says quietly, searching for a way out of the situation. In vein, he tries: “But I can bring one to you - in your cell, if you’d like - first thing tomorrow morning.” “But I want a book now, Miguel,” sobs Harvey Blake - his friends laugh harder at his words. “There’s nothing I can do for you, Mr. Blake,” Miggy says in a near-whisper, his eyes lowered to the floor. He wishes he could give Blake a book and run to hide in the library, but he knows the Librarian will find out that a book scheduled to come back was left behind, and Miggy might be transferred to work in another part of the prison. He doesn’t want that. A few books have been misplaced in the past, but Miggy has always been able to explain what happened. If he gives a book to Harvey Blake and then tells the Librarian he was bullied into it by Blake - he won’t live through the week. “Well, if that’s the best you can do...” Blake says. Suddenly, he picks Miggy’s little cart of books up, raises it high above his head, and hurls it to the concrete floor. The flimsy cart can’t withstand the impact - it shatters, scattering books and magazines all around. The action terrifies Miggy. He stands stunned for a moment, unsure of whether or not he should start picking up the books. “Fucking shotty craftsmanship,” Harvey sneers. The noise of the crash prompts the entrance of a Correctional Officer from the far entrance - the library entrance. Upon seeing the mess of books, he pulls his nightstick out, “What’s going on here, fellas?” The C.O. is a rather large man in his late thirties, wearing a full beard and a few teeth are missing from his top row. “Why don’t you ask the people who fund this fucking place,” Harvey fires back, “This little spick’s cart is flimsy as shit - you think you’d given him some real equipment to work with.” Slowly, under the watchful eye of the C.O., Miggy kneels down and begins piling the books. In a way, he’s glad Harvey destroyed the cart. If he hadn’t made that noise, there’s no telling what horror might have been unleashed. “Oh, and spick,” the tall, balding, one-eyed Nazi sneers, “I’ll be expecting my book in the morning.” *** From the Office of the Warden “In it’s natural state,” the Warden begins, leaning against his desk as usual, “energy - electricity - is wild. Uncontrollable. Over the years, man has learned to harness that energy, and now, instead of lightning bolts, we have batteries.” The Warden produces a battery from the pocket of his sharp black suit, “Batteries take this energy and channel it so it can be released in the appropriate place. So what happens when that energy is channelled to the wrong place? The wrong places?” The Warden smiles in a bitter sort of way, “Well, that’s the kind of people they send to me. People who fire electricity from all sides. People who don’t know - don’t understand - how to control their own, most powerful, of energies.” “Harvey Blake,” sighs Gavin Miller, the Warden, “is a source of uncontrollable energy. You may be wondering about that eye patch he wears - makes him look like the devil himself, if you ask me. He lost that eye back in the early eighties, when he was establishing himself as the leader of the organized whites... the Aryans... the Nazis - whatever you prefer to call them. He was serving a few years for a domestic incident back then. After he organized the whites, the stubborn bastard took things a step further and killed one of the black leaders of the Tank at the time - got another twenty years tacked onto his sentence.” “Now,” the Warden says while shaking his head, rounding his desk and heading for the large leather chair on the other side, “I pray every day I’ll be able to catch the bastard doing something else before he gets out on parole. As much as I hate having him in here, I’d hate to see him on the streets even more...” The Warden leans back in his chair, giving one last thought to Harvey Blake before moving on, “Unlike Harvey Blake,” he says, “some people don’t understand why they channel their energy to the places they channel it to. They don’t see it coming - it just happens. They explode, almost at random... and eventually, these people are usually sent to me too. My job is to try to teach them how to channel their energy...” The Warden takes the battery in his hand and places it in the back of a clock. Instantly, the clock begins ticking, “...into its appropriate place.” Tick-tock, Tick-tock, Tick-tock... *** Monday, July 8 - Day 3 Jason Snow hasn’t slept a wink since being introduced to his new bed in Unit B. It’s not so much the hard, uncomfortable, bed that’s keeping him up nights - it’s his cellmate. Darryl Jarvis, who claims ownership to the questionable nickname of “Harding,” is no match for Jason Snow physically. However, the slender black man’s warning about his situation - life - in comparison with Snow’s - eleven months - favoured the physically inferior of the two. As roll call ends, the prisoners are free to roam around the Unit. Snow, still buttoning his black shirt - trying to recall where Miggy told him to take his laundry - begins down the staircase toward the recreation floor. Maybe he’ll watch television - Miggy didn’t mention any groups or gangs controlling that. “Jason Snow?” a low voice, laced in surfer tones, calls for Snow’s attention. The young lion turns around and finds himself looking down at a man about his own age. The man’s head is completely shaven and there’s a swastika sloppily tattoo’ed above his left ear. He wears a thick black goatee and has a fairly good build to him. “Yeah?” Snow says cautiously. Since coming to the Tank, the only person who’s spoken to Snow at all has been Miggy. And Miggy only approached because Snow was looking particularly lost and gentle at the time. He noticed later that Miggy is usually afraid of his own shadow. “Harv wants to see you,” the young man says. “Harv?” Snow asks. The young skinhead doesn’t say another word. He simply gestures with his head for Snow to follow him. With great caution, Snow does. Harvey Blake leans over the pool table, and carelessly takes a shot before looking over his shoulder and seeing that Jason Snow is in his presence. “So you’re the great Jason Snow!” Harvey chuckles and extends his hand. Snow hesitates somewhat - a little concerned about the red swastika on Harvey’s eye patch - and then accepts the handshake. “Call me Snow.” “I’ll do that,” says Harvey, pushing himself up into a sitting position on the pool table. “You wanted to see me?” Snow says, trying to move the conversation along. All the swastikas and iron crosses drawn onto the bare skin of these men are making him uncomfortable. “I sure did,” Harvey says with a grin. A moment later, his tone turns serious, “Tell me, son - has that cellmate of yours been giving you any trouble? What do they call him?” “Harding,” says the young man who escorted Snow over to this group of Nazi’s, “His name’s Darryl Jarvis but they call him Harding for all the ass the faggot’s fucked over the last few years.” Harvey looks to Snow for an answer to his question. “A little bit,” Snow says quietly, but confidently. He turns to look up at his sleepless cell, “It’s nothing I can’t handle.” Harvey ignores Snow’s boast of confidence, “What’s he saying to you, son?” He asks in a voice full of concern, “Is he making threats? Telling you how he’s a lifer so he can do all the fucked up shit he wants?” Snow’s eyes grow wide, “How’d you know?” he asks, wondering if this Nazi can somehow offer an end to this sleepless hell. “Fucking niggers,” Harvey sneers, holding his head low and shaking it, “All the same. You know, when I first came to this shit hole, I had one pull the same shit with me? He took my eye, but I took his heart.” The aging one-eyed Nazi winks at Snow, giving the young lion-like wrestler a sick feeling in the pit of his stomach. The other Nazis laugh. “I can handle Jarvis,” Snow says, backing away. “Whatever you say, son,” Harvey chuckles, jumping down from the table and approaching Snow, “I don’t doubt it. I’ll bet you could lay a beating on that cocksucker like only his boyfriends have given him. But the fact is, son - this ain’t no fucking wrestling ring.” “Huh?” Snow doesn’t understand where Harvey’s going with all of this. Harvey chuckles, “You can make that little nigger your fucking bitch! We all know that!” He points up to Snow’s cell, “Shit, even he knows that - why the hell do you think he gave you all that shit about being a lifer? He doesn’t want to end up sucking your cock for the next couple of months.” “What’s that got to do with wrestling?” Snow asks with curiousity. “Take a look around you, son!” Harvey says, gesturing to the other prisoners, “This is your new reality. This is where you live and these are the people you’re going to have to deal with. That little faggot you’re rooming with ain’t shit around these parts, son - he ain’t shit. But you’ve got to understand, for every little cocksucking nigger in this joint, there’s a much bigger nigger - and he wants you to suck his cock.” Harvey puts his hand on Snow’s shoulder in a manner meant to be comforting - that’s not the feeling Snow gets. “I think,” Snow begins, brushing Harvey’s hand away, “I can handle it.” He turns his back to the aging Nazi, genuinely afraid of everything the man stands for. “You think so, prag?” Harvey fires before Snow has taken three steps, “Prag - that’s what you’ll be known as pretty fucking soon, son. You can handle one big lonely nigger? So fucking what? What are you going to do when you’re dealing with three? Four? Five of the bastards? I’ll tell you what you’re going to do, son - you’re going to come back to me. And I’ll be waiting. Waiting with open arms...” Snow continues walking away from Harvey Blake and everything the old Nazi offers. Snow doesn’t want protection that’s spawned from things he can’t believe in - can’t even understand. Every time I look at the guy, I know there’s not a chance in hell he would - or can - hurt anybody. He’s harmless. But from what I’m told, that wasn’t the case back in 1958.” “Miggy was nineteen years old when he caught his new wife with another man,” sighs the Warden, “He walked in on them - caught them in the act. They were living in a one room apartment at the time. Miggy came home to find his wife in bed with her lover and his three month old child in a crib beside the bed. He didn’t think. He grabbed the shotgun from beside the door and opened fire. No one in the room - save Miggy - survived.” The Warden stands up from his chair and turns to the window behind his desk, “Miggy drowned himself in guilt for years. It wasn’t until he was put to work in the library that he could go through a whole day without crying. Gradually, he became human again. He found a group of friends - harmless, like him.” “Every man,” the Warden turns around, “has the instinct to kill. Every ...human... has that instinct. Even those who you would never expect to have it...” *** “Hey spick!” Harvey bellows when he sees Miggy nearby with a new cart of books. Reluctantly, Miggy approaches. “You brought me some good reading, didn’t you?” Miggy gently pushes the cart toward Harvey. “You think I’m going to upset your cart?” Harvey asks the old Latino in a voice meant to be gentle - it’s hard to sound gentle when your eye patch has a swastika sewn onto it. “No, I’m not going to do that to you - not today. I do want you to do something for me though...” “For you?” Miggy gulps. “Yeah,” Harvey smiles, taking the old man under his arm. “I see you’ve gotten all buddy-buddy with Jason Snow. You see, I want to be buddy-buddy with Jason Snow too. You’re going to help me with that.” “I am?” “You are,” Harvey nods. A moment later, his tone turns stern, “And if Jason Snow’s not on my team within the next few days, spick... it’s your ass.”
