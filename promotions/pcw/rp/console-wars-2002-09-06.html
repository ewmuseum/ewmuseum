Console Wars

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th September 2002 We stand quietly outside a suburban house, a rather large one. It is what was once again the Asylum Gym; formerly known as the Anaheim Dungeon. All seems quiet and peaceful until all of a suddenly a small pink and white box lines through the window from the inside and down onto the small amount of grass on the outside. The camera zooms in, revealing it to be a Nintendo Gamecube. Suddenly from inside the house a loud voice beckons. Voice: AND FUCKING STAY OUT YOU PIECE OF SHIT! The door swings open and a man wearing a pink and white shirt with “Nintendo” on the back in red lettering is shoved through the door by none other than Alexander Miyagawa. The Nintendo chap stumbles down the steps before Miyagawa slams the door on him. Nintendo Chap: Dammit… that’s the fifth house this week… all I did was tell him what games were coming out for the Gamecube… The chap wanders over and grabs the Gamecube before walking past the camera, presumably to a Nintendo Van parked somewhere behind the camera. The door opens once again, and this time a guy wearing a pair of jeans and a white polo necked t-shirt walks out. Voice: AND TAKE YOUR DAMNED XBOX WITH YOU! A large black box wider than the door slams against the door setting. It gets turned onto its side and slid through the door slowly as it scraps against both the floor and the top of the door mounting. Finally once it’s through, the door is slammed once again. The apparent Microsoft chap looks at it in despair. Microsoft Chap: Jesus… anyone would think that this thing is big… but look at the crystal on the top of it… plus it’s got an “X” in it’s name, so it must sell because of the “X”. I still think if PCW changed its name to “PCX” it would be much better than it currently is. We don’t stick around for the Microsoft chap to try to pull the double bed sized X Box back to his transit van, but instead using the power of overpriced special effects, the camera passes straight through the wall and into the Asylum Gym itself and into the TV lounge where Miyagawa is sitting on his couch playing on his Playstation 2. There’s a noise to the side of Miyagawa, just out of camera view as we look over his shoulder as he plays. Miyagawa: You know what JJ… I can’t believe I went this long without a games console… what games I’ve missed… Metal Gear Solid Two… although they’re re-releasing that because half the game was missing… only a real sucker would have purchased that. ---- We suddenly change to a house in London, England where a handler wearing a green shirt whilst writing a promo on Microsoft Word suddenly stops and looks at the sky towards God and says… Handler: YOU FUCKING BASTARD!!!! I HATE YOU!!!! ---- We cut back to the Asylum Gym in the Television lounge once again to our previous position as Miyagawa continues to play the game. JJ: You know what’s really good on the Playstation? Miyagawa: What’s that then? JJ: Tomb Raider. Silence ensues for a few seconds. JJ: What? You don’t like Tomb Raider? But we went to see the movie. Miyagawa: I liked the movie. JJ: But not the game? What’s wrong with you… those bouncy computerised breasts… that long…? Miyagawa: Stop trying to pretend that you’re not gay. Silence ensues for a few more seconds as Miyagawa shoots some twerp in a white winter jacket with a sniper rifle. Miyagawa: I just really hate platformers. I can’t stand them. I played Tomb Raider three on the Playstation one and I totally hated it. It just really pissed me off in a major fashion. JJ makes a rather huffing noise. Miyagawa: And what is that meant to mean exactly? JJ: Everyone knows Tomb Raider Three sucks big hairy donkey balls… AND don’t you even think about making some sort of comment about that as if I were gay. Miyagawa turns his head towards JJ as he pauses the game. Miyagawa: Do you honestly want to know why I think that you’re gay? JJ: Go on. Miyagawa: The total reason that proves without a shadow of a doubt that it is not only true but the one hundred percent truth and has always been true. JJ makes another huffing noise. JJ: Out with it already, tell me! Miyagawa: Alright. Because you’re dressed as Lara Croft, and got dressed as her as soon as you heard I bought a Playstation two. The camera pans around to finally show JJ, and indeed the guy is dressed as Lara Croft. Even down to fake tits, he’s dressed as her. The gun belt… the tight latex shorts, the tight halter top over two hefty melons… a ponytail hair extension… the tank boots… and two see through plastic pink water pistols holstered in the gun holster. Miyagawa: So is that proof enough for you? JJ looks down at himself, and then realises that one of the melons has come out of place and adjusts it slightly. JJ: They don’t make comfy bras anymore, do they? Silence ensues for a few more seconds. Miyagawa: And you’re wearing a bra. JJ looks at Miyagawa with a devilish smile. JJ: Well I heard that Natalia got you to wear a pair of her panties once. More silence ensues. Miyagawa: Damn, she did another interview didn’t she? JJ: Hello Magazine, United Kingdom. They also took a load of photos of this place, and of Christopher. Miyagawa raises his eyebrows, since he knew nothing of that happening. Miyagawa: God dammit, I wish she would get herself a press secretary. Hell, I need to get one. Wait a fricking minute, you’ve changed the subject. JJ: Only slightly. JJ grins at Miyagawa. Which is rather disturbing since JJ is currently dressed up like a woman. Suddenly at the door behind JJ appears a third figure… a figure that hasn’t been seen for some time… the figure of… Steve…. Jabowski….. Presumably he was doing a tour on the Disneyland Bus as Mike Wzowski (the little green one eyed guy from Monsters Inc.) and is now back. He looks at JJ’s latex clad ass and slaps it… hard. JJ nearly jumps out of his skin. Miyagawa manages to stifle a laugh. Miyagawa: Perhaps I should leave you two alone, although if you want me to be best man at the Johnson -slash- Jabowski wedding, I’m certainly willing. Steve looks surprised. Steve: Jeremy has a sister? Jeremy turns around, causing Steve to nearly jump out of his skin. Steve: God dammit Jeremy, why have you dressed yourself up as a girl… wait… I slapped you on the ass… no… urgh…. I feel so dirty… And with that we leave you for the moment. Today’s song for the day is “House of Fun” by Madness. The letter is “O” and the number is 3.16820939. Have fun kiddies.
