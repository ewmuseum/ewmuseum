Our Future Dictator Voices His Economic and Foreign Concerns

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-02 (The faint light of a lit-up projection screen reflects off of the smooth tiles of a building. In it, you can distinctly see the face of our future dictator and sole PCW Title Sweeper, Frisco. As you look at the images bouncing off of the pearl white tiles, you hear the commotion of dozens of people near by. As the view comes off of the floor, it catches a huge banner that reads 'FRANCISCO VALERIUS - NUESTRO PRESIDENTE SIGUIENTE DE ARGENTINA' and Mao-esque posters of the man himself all around it. We turn around to see at least 30 rows of chairs, all filled up by men and women in suits. The first row is filled up with members of the press and many cameras focusing in the front of the room. We are obviously in some kind of convention hall for a presentation. A projector produces images of Frisco on his campaign trail onto the front wall and onto two screens in front of it. Impatient murmuring from the crowd is distinctly heard as the podium that sits in front of the main projection wall is only accompanied by two large Argentinean flags, but no living being. This soon changes as a tall Hispanic man in a brown suit walks down the aisle and onto the podium. A relieved expulsion of breath from the audience can be heard as the presenter walks onto the podium and begins to speak in a thick Spanish accent.) Juan Galvanto: 'Good evening and thank you for coming to this very important conference for the election of Presidente Frisco.' (The audience stands and applauds.) Juan Galvanto: 'I am Juan Galvanto, Chairman for El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina - North America Division. Please take yur seats and our presentation will begin shortly.' (The audience sits and a few flashbulbs go off as the lights in the room are shut off and Juan Galvanto walks over to the projector. A minute or two passes before Mr. Galvanto turns the changing still images of Frisco into a moving film. The audience murmurs and the chairman sits down as the film starts.) (Frisco's PCW theme music 'Ode to Joy' plays as a dramatization of Frisco being presented a bejeweled crown and a purple tunic in front of a seemingly endless pool of cheering Argentinean citizens rolls to the delight of the party members in the room. The well-produced opening moves around to towering bronze statues of Frisco holding his 7 different PCW titles in one hand and the Argentinean flag in the other and back to a huge balcony where Frisco presides over his people and does a dramatic Castro-like speech that is inaudible in the film, but excites the thousands of computer generated people in front of him. A narration begins to play as Frisco's fiery green eyes are zoomed in on.) Narrator: 'What makes one man become a leader?' (As Frisco's eyes remain on the screen, the marble busts of Alexander the Great, Augustus, Trajan, and Napoleon appear and disappear.) Narrator: 'How have so few individuals held so much power over so many?' (Old black and white footage of Hitler giving a passionate speech at a podium is shown just as troublemaker in the audience yells out something.) Troublemaker: 'Bloooooowwwwwjoooooobbbssssss!!!!!!' (Loud laughter erupts just before 6 men in dark brown suits and sunglasses quickly move on the heckler and forcefully remove him from the room.) Narrator: 'What does it take to become the speaker of people, the decider of fates, and the guider of lives? (Argentina's own Evita Peron is shown on a split screen with Frisco. The audience applauds.) Narrator: 'Strength?' (Footage of Frisco's straining face is shown as he F-Bombs Meanstreak at a Friday Fusion event from last year.) Narrator: 'Balls?' (Frisco is shown doing a 630 Senton off the top of a cage.) Narrator: 'Ambition?' (The footage from Frisco's campaign announcement in Buenos Aires plays with a disheveled Frisco pounding his fist on the podium to the cheers of rowdy citizens.) Narrator: 'Wealth?' (The images fade to a drunken Frisco humping a large mound of $100 dollar bills. Giggles are heard from the audience.) Narrator: 'Or just the common touch?' (Frisco is shown smiling and shaking the hand of a poor Argentinean boy before he immediately wipes his hand on an associate's suit. The audience 'awwwww's at the heart-touching scene.) Narrator: 'Whatever it takes to make a worthy leader, you know that Francisco Valerius is sure to have it. The future president of Argentina has spent his life devoted to the progression of his own intelligence and success in order to enhance the same onto the people he wants to lead. Frisco cares about you and he cares about your happiness.' (The audience applauds as footage rolls of Frisco sticking out of the roof of his limousine and blowing kisses to the residents of a poor village in Argentina. You can briefly catch a guard beating the shit out of a farmer who tries to touch the shiny vehicle before the scene again switches--this time to Frisco dressed in a radiation suit and getting hugged by a mob of school children.) (A female audience member speaks out in an infomercial-type praise.) Audience Member #1: 'Awwww! How adorable!' Narrator: 'The world is tired of politicians. Leaders from common backgrounds with common beliefs for the common man are a dying breed. Francisco Valerius is not a politician but a common man with an extraordinary background and a rich history of success. He is what the common man dreams of becoming--he is the common man evolved and he wants to serve you to make you who you want to be.' (Frisco is now shown laughing and posing with a citizen who stands behind a cardboard body of Frisco with the head cut off to allow the person to enact his fantasy of being the popular figure.) Narrator: 'No more will the citizens of Argentina have to be afraid. Money will be worth something again, but still never the value that that the citizens themselves will come to be. Coinage and people alike will appreciate in value under his rule and the country will prosper. Just as the wrestler we have watched in PCW, Francisco will give credibility to every aspect of Argentinean life just as he has given credibility to every title he has touched.' (Frisco is shown holding up each and every title he has won in PCW and finally an Argentinean baby.) Narrator: 'We trust and love our future president and support him as he realizes his own dream and the dream of the citizens he will soon guide. Help him into our arms so he may help us out of our stresses, for he is the light and the way to what we dare to dream. Francisco Cassius Valerius will be the savior of Argentina and the savior of each and every life that inhabits it.' (One last slow-moving piece of footage is shown as Frisco stands in front of a widespread crowd of smiling citizens, with his arms rising up. Frisco and the people are presented before a beautiful scene of golden castles and a rainbow that stretches over Frisco's risen arms.) (A few scoffs from the press are heard before the rest of the crowd drowns them out with cheers. The chairman Juan Galvanto stands up and turns on the light, which temporarily blinds the people. He walks up to the podium and taps on the microphone.) Juan Galvanto: 'Now, I hope ju enjoyed that as it will be played all throughout the Americas for the next few months.' (A few scattered claps are heard.) Juan Galvanto: ' So, now that you've seen our new commercial, we will bring out the man himself...' (The cheers rise up loudly.) Juan Galvanto: 'Here he is, the next president of Argentina and PCW superstar....Frisco!' (The audience stands and the press quickly pick up their cameras to get ready for Frisco, but instead of Frisco walking out, he appears on the screens sitting casually on a chair in a gold FRISCACEMANI~! suit and a Cuban cigar in his hand. The audience groans in disappointment. The on-screen Frisco flashes a million dollar smile and tips his head towards the camera.) Frisco: 'Greetings...(Frisco's mouth says something but an overdubbed 'Tucson' takes its place)...' (A few scattered boos and scoffs are heard.) Frisco: 'I'm sorry I couldn't be there for this event, but I am extremely busy at the moment this is being shown...' ----------------------------------------------------------SCENE CHANGE---------------------------------------------------------- (The scene switches to a hotel room where Frisco is in his bed snoring loudly. The alarm clock near by reads 2:29 and clicks to 2:30 where it rings loudly. Frisco's eyes open and he grits his teeth.) Frisco: 'Goddamnit!' (Frisco sits up and looks at the 4 suit-clad guards who stand around his bed with AK-47s and walkie talkies.) Frisco: 'Someone take care of that fucking thing!' (One of the guards immediately reacts.) Guard: 'Yes, sir!' (The guard points his AK at the alarm clock and blasts it to kingdom come.) (TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!-TAT!) (The ringing ceases as pieces of the clock fly all over the room. Frisco cringes at the even louder sounds of the gun and angrily screams at the guard.) Frisco: 'What the fuck is the matter with you!' (The guard begins to stumble over his words.) Guard: 'I-I-I...' Frisco: 'I am a complete fucking moron? Is that what you were going to say?' Guard: 'No, I w-w-wa...' Frisco: 'No,no, no! Let's start again. Say I.' (The guard looks down at the ground.) Guard: 'I?' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Now say am..' Guard: 'Am...' (The other guards begin to laugh at the large bald man who is close to crying.) Guard: 'Sorry! Sorry! I am so sorry! Ok!?' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'No! Stick to the fucking script!' (The guard nods rapidly.) Guard: 'I am...I am...' Frisco: 'Fired...' Guard: 'I am fired?' (Frisco nods and smiles. The guard begins to pout.) Guard: 'No!' (Frisco motions to the rest of the guards to the offender. The remaining 3 guards point their guns at him.) Guard: 'I mean yes.' (Frisco nods and points to the door.) Frisco: 'Please put down your gun and remove your uniform.' (The guard puts his gun at the end of the bed and strips off his uniform. What remains is a fat bald man in a pair of boxers. He is escorted out by the rest of the guards and Frisco plops his head back onto his pillow.) Frisco: 'Now everyone shut up and don't wake me up for another 2 hours.' (Frisco digs his face into the pillow and yawns as the scene switches back to the conference room where his previously recorded self is on the screen.) ----------------------------------------------------------SCENE CHANGE---------------------------------------------------------- (We are now back in the conference room where the large audience views the 3 screens displaying a generic interview with the presidential candidate. Frisco speaks about the current state of the economy in Argentina.' Frisco: 'See, what I plan to do here is scrap the peso. Why Argentina has used this worthless monetary unit for so long is beyond me.' (The voice of an interviewer off screen asks Frisco a question.) Interviewer: 'So what do you propose?' Frisco: 'I propose a new monetary system with the main unit called a 'Frisconote'.' Interviewer: 'A Frisconote?' Frisco: 'Yes, it will feature a very nice portrait of me and will have a nice design. I would show you a prototype, but I had it sent back to the lab for further work.' Interviewer: 'You plan to put your own portrait on the money?' Frisco: 'Uh huh. The coins too, which will be called 'Friscoins'.' Interviewer: 'Frisconotes and Friscoins?' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Yes! Cool, eh? I envision Americans trading in their worthless dollars and cents for Argentinean currency and Europe quickly scrapping their stupid little Euros for this revolutionary type of money.' Interviewer: 'How in the world do you think this will improve the economy?' Frisco: 'It won't, the war will.' Interviewer: 'War? Frisco: 'Yes, upon my ascension, Argentina will get its rematch with punk ass England, and we will prevail!' (A few members of the audience burst out in cheers...others in laughs.) Interviewer: 'You want to go to war with England?' (Frisco puffs on his cigar.) Frisco: 'Indeed I do, as does the people of my country.' Interviewer: 'Why would you want to do that?' Frisco: 'Are you out of your f(bleep)ing mind!? The Falkl--...f(beep)k that, Las Islas Malvinas are ours and the British are a bunch of pussies who shouldn't be able to own a pair of shoes, let alone my islands! I don't know how in the hell we've let this unlawful ownership by those dickheads go for this long.' Interviewer: 'Well, Argentina were defeated by those 'pussies' 20 years ago.' Frisco: 'Exactly, that was 20 years ago. They were led by the menacing duo of Margaret Thatcher and Queen Elizabeth. Those two bitches are out of the way now! England has no power left! They're totally screwed!' (Frisco cackles and pounds his fist on the arm of his chair.) Interviewer: 'Umm..okay. How do you think this will improve the Argentinean economy? The last war cost 2 billion dollars and damaged the already declining economy...' Frisco: 'Why are you asking me these questions? We're not here for a debate.' Interviewer: 'Ok, I'll sw--...' Frisco: 'No, I'll answer that. It will improve the economy because we will get our islands back which are rich in seals, whales, and sheep. Animals which produce lots of products for export. Not to mention the possible oil in that area. It's a gold mine, I tell ya! We will make great use of those islands.' (Frisco sucks on his cigar.) Frisco: 'But that's not all, once we defeat the British we will occupy their own land and enslave their people!' (A few scattered claps are heard in the room.) Interviewer: 'That's....a very unique response.' Frisco: 'Slavery will be brought back in style and the British people will make excellent slaves. However, they will be cared for and given toothpaste and such.' Interviewer: 'That's good to know.' Frisco: 'Yes, I'm not a barbarian, I will treat the new British slaves with respect. They will have the same rights as any common pet has. You know, like being able to walk freely around the yard, their own chew toy, and the occasional drink out of the toilet.' Interviewer: 'How very humane.' Frisco: 'Indeed, slavery will boost our economy big time. Rebels of Argentina will also be sold into slavery. People such as Jason Core and Legend Inc.' Interviewer: 'Who?' Frisco: 'They are enemies of the state. By trying to bring down the future ruler of Argentina, they're trying to oppress the people. Jason Core will be dealt with later today and Legend Inc. will be dealt with next week. Both men will be punished. I intend on having them dragged from the ring after I render them unconscious and have them transported to Buenos Aires for detainment. Once I am elected president, they will be the slaves of the highest bidder.' (The look in Frisco's eyes grows crazier.) Frisco: 'As will any other enemy of myself and the Argentinean people. This aggression will not stand!' Interviewer: 'What aggression?' (Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'You're one of them!' Interviewer: 'What?' Frisco: 'You are trying to oppress my people!' Interviewer: 'No!' (Frisco stands up and begins to point at the unseen man behind the camera.) Frisco: 'Él es el enemigo! Mátele! Mátele!' (A struggle is heard as unintelligible voices in Spanish yell at once and boots are heard connecting with the floor's wood panels.) Interviewer: 'Ahhhhhhhhh!!!!!! Hellllll--mmmmmmmmmffffffff! Mmmmmmfffffff!' (The interviewer's voice is cut off and only a muffled voice can be heard as it gets fainter and fainter until the only sounds audible are a loud pounding and a slight squeal.) Frisco: 'Heh.' (Frisco straightens his suit, puffs on his cigar and winks at the camera before he walks out of view. The feed shuts off and jaws of people in the audience are dropped to the floor. The chairman begins to clap loudly and the rest follow. He walks up to the podium and taps on the microphone.) Juan Galvanto: 'El Presidente!' (The audience is still in shock, but applauds.) Juan Galvanto: 'We're sorry ju couldn't see him in person like ju thought ju were going to, but he is busy man. However, his new Secretary of Scheduling, Conventions, and Sponge Baths tells me that he plans a live presentation here in a month. If ju want to contact Frisco with any questions, contact his Secretary of Questions, Chicken Nuggets, and Paper Towels.' (A few members of the press stand up and begin talking at once.) Juan Galvanto: 'Sorry! Sorry! No questions! I tell ju to go to the Secretary! Thank ju and have a nice day!' (Scattered applause is heard along with angry grumbling as the chairman walks off the podium. The scene fades into Frisco still sleeping in his guarded bed as it then fades out to a waving Argentinean flag.)
