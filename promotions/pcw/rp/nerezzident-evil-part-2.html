Nerezzident Evil (Part 2)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th, April 2003 Raccoon Town, Alabama April 28, 2003 “What the fu…” the prankster mutters, holding his chest cavity as he tries to recompose his breathing. Finished the blood stained face of Nerezza gazes up at the once jovial Jester with the look of hunger tainted in his eyes and curling his talons he sprung forward like a wild animal. Latching onto the slender neck of his colleague the perked cheeks of the Jester fade to a pale complexion rivalled only by that of the Living Nightmare who’s in the midst of attack. WHACK!!!! With improvisation the self-proclaimed ‘Evil Super Genius’ pulled the trigger and with a hefty thud the brass candelabra cracked the behemoth in the cranium sending his mountain of flesh to the ground in a heap. Lying atop of the existing corpse of S.T.A.R.S Alpha teams Barry Burton the Jester spluttered from his ordeal and after several quick minutes to recompose his breathing he back peddled towards the dining room entrance. Grasping at the locking mechanism with a trembling left hand the Emerald Menace tilted his head towards the crime scene but to his astonishment his vision become obscured by the goliath standing directly opposite, merely a question of inches rather than feet. “ARGH!!!!” Stumbling through the door the Jester crashed backwards onto a crimson carpet aligning the reception area floor. Pursued by the bloodthirsty Vampire the conceited comic scrambled across the ground with frantic movement and fear etched across his angelic face. The game of cat and mouse continued to unfold as the Jester pleaded to his assailant in a cowardly manner, his negotiations falling on deaf ear as the beast edged closer, looming over his proposed victim. Towering in intimidation the Vampire slowly shuffled forward as the green tailored Jester raced backwards and directly into a sideboard and using the wooden legs for leverage he pulled himself back to his vertical base. Seeing his reflection in the glass of the emergency box imbedded into the sideboard the Jester sparked his counter attack by firstly crashing his elbow through the glass-panel and doing so he yelped in pain but laying before him was the assortment of weapons. Looking back at Angel of Fear the Jester’s eyes fixated on the beast’s movements as his hand fidgeted within the emergency box when finally he smugly grasped at the banana unaware. “Yeah, you want a piece of me?” hissed the comic as he unveiled his weapon of choice. The banana generated nothing more than a twitch in the lips of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Vampire as he moved in for the kill. Dumbfounded the Jester slung the banana at the trench coat clad behemoth that parried it with an open palm before murmuring his vocal retort. ”I am… I am… A carnivore, not… A vegetarian!” Toying amongst the other weapons the Jester’s hand rummaged further before taking hold of the butt of the knife and with matrix style reflects he lunged forward burying the implement into the heart region of the Living Nightmare. Putting pressure on the forward thrust the Vampire absorbed the blow and gulping hard as he collapsed to one knee in a melodramatic sequence. ”Aren’t you suppose to turn to dust or something?” inquisitively sighed the prankster as he peered down at the Creature of the Night who clutches the imbedded blade as he continues to over sell. Suddenly the Vampire looked up in puzzlement and grasping the blade from his chest cavity he realises that the knife is a special effect implement with the blade rigged to press into the handle on impact. Throwing the object in disgust the Jester can only cringe as he tries to flee but as he passes the fallen Vampire, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza throws out a hand tripping the escapee. Nose-diving to the ground the Jester cushioned the impact with his hands and scurrying away he ventures to the second floor where he is suddenly surrounded by a cluster of wooden doors. Investigating each door the keys must be marked by symbols rather than numbers as some doors have shields etched under the locking mechanism whilst others have helmets and armour. In desperation the Jester kicked a door marked with a shield and the wood crumbled from the blow and the door swung ajar allowing our hero to enter. “Huh…” The flimsy reaction is from the first sight of the room. Entering there appears to be a young female decorated in camouflage with a S.T.A.R.S insignia stitched into the arm standing in front of a large three-dimensional jigsaw with a single piece astray that sits on a neighbouring coffee table. The girl appears deep in thought as her head sways in a pendulum motion whilst following the lines of print of a clue etched on a brass plaque below the actual puzzle. The commando doesn’t pay her guest any mind as she goes through the motions of dealing with this puzzle thus allowing the Jester to read her nametag ‘Rebecca Chambers’. ”Hey sweet cheeks, let me assist you!” politely gestured the comic but to avail as Rebecca Chambers continues to strain on a puzzle that looks incredibly straight forward from first inspection. ”Rebecca, hope you don’t mind me calling you that and not to steal your thunder but why don’t you simply place the last piece into the puzzle!” questions the Lord of Laughter with a glint of curiosity as he tries to fathom the simplicity of the task at hand, which the S.T.A.R.S member Rebecca appears to be failing miserably at. No response materialises and Rebecca Chambers goes about re-enacting her puzzlement role of not knowing. As the young attractive brunette goes about unable to deal with the jigsaw the Jester seizes the opportunity to read the glistening clue engraved on the brass plaque, ‘Form a picture and further doors will open’ Giving the S.T.A.R.S operative a star struck glare the Jester lifted the three-dimensional piece from the polished ash coffee table and without thought on the consequences placed it into the vacant space. Suddenly the faint sound of a motor murmured from behind the puzzle and the clanging of cogs turning filtered when without warning the brass plaque twisted horizontally to unveil an iron key. Clutching the key firmly in the palm of his hand the Emerald Menace inspected it closely to see the shape of a helmet engraved. “Who’s the man, who’s the man?” triumphantly bellowed the five time PCW Tag team Champion as he showcased a jovial smile that has been oppressed since his arrival at this establishment. As the comedian stood in triumph the monotone Rebecca Chambers began to wander the room in a jog stance. Bumping into everything from furniture to walls the whole scene look rather comedic because as the calamity driven commando clatters into a object she doesn’t fall or flinch but merely strokes up against it Resident Evil style. ”Things just get stranger…” muttered our hero. Taking a hold of Rebecca’s shoulders the gentlemanly Jester manoeuvred the soldier from the confines of the room and onto the landing where she continues to bounce off the walls and safety rails due to her poor control system. Clutching the energetic female as her legs continue to shuffle with a mind of their own the Jester peers over her pert shoulders and down to where the Vampire was last seen but his body has long since vanished. Breathing a sigh of relief the Prince of Pranks was about to turn when suddenly a white murky mist filtered around their legs and down at the top stair before them where it looked to settle. Paying the fog no mind it soon grew in volume and raising vertically it personified into the Angel of Fear who stands with his canines brandished and his arms outstretched. ”Sorry babe, every Jester for them selves!” worryingly yelped the cruiserweight as he cowardly nudged Rebecca Chambers into the danger of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The animalistic Vampire snatched the S.T.A.R.S operative and with a heavy bite he clamped his jaws around her neck. Sickeningly her neck snapped back broken and the Vampire continued to feed as blood seeped down onto the laminate flooring, trickling down each step like a waterfall. Flowing freely the Jester peered down squeamish towards the bloodbath and with a tint of green occupying his perked face he set hotfooted from the encore performance. Placing the iron key into a door merely feet away the latch clicked open and the Jester fell through as his journey takes him into another side of this nightmarish Victorian Manor House…
