Blue Tentacled Thing

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (It’s a dark day in the history of THE Multiple Man, the hounding dogs of PCW management are hot on his heels and the threat of imminent release hangs heavy upon the horizon. By rights, the mood should be just short of suicidal in his secret lair.) “Morning. Now get off the ceiling.” (Nope, just another normal day. Tmm blinks awake and looks upwards towards the floor overhead.) Tmm: “Ah, good morning, blue tentacled thing. How are you?” (The glistening, faintly iridescent slime around its gaping mouth tumbles to the floor as it speaks.) B.T.T. “Uh-huh. How many tentacles is it today?” (Tmm squints in concentration against the blood rushing to his head as he counts.) Tmm: “Five… and a half.” (The gelatinous thing slowly blinks its huge compound eye as it preens the sharpened quills on the apex of its head knob.) B.T.T.: “Hmm… three and a half less than yesterday. I hope that means you’re improving. Does it?” (This last is directed at the pink cube on a pogo stick that has just bounced in.) Cube: “You think I know? Half of the stuff I injected him with didn’t have a label. I could get arrested if I hadn’t had that permanent waiver he gave me when I signed on.” B.T.T.:”How does he stay on the ceiling like that? That can’t be humanly possible, there’s no real handholds!” Tmm: “Oh, that’s easy. I took off my shoes and got a good grip with my toes.” Cube: “I’d bet it’s a variant on catatonic or PCP strength. I’d know more, but he won’t let me put the electrodes on him. See?” (A crab’s claw emerges from the cube.) Tmm: “AAAAHHHH! SNAKES!!!” Cube: “It’s been like that since Tuesday. He hasn’t let me get within twenty feet of him when I have them.” B.T.T.: ”Well, he’s gotta do SOMETHING. Grapevine says we’re slated for the chopping block if he doesn’t perform.” Tmm: “My enemies are moving against me… but they don’t know of my secret stash, the fools! Mwahahahaha!” B.T.T.: “He has a stash somewhere? Maybe we won’t have to sell the lair while looking for employment?” Cube: “Don’t let him fool you, it’s just a tractor trailer stuffed with PCW staplers and stationary.” Tmm: “Plus canned food and shotguns. We’ll live like bandit kings!” Cube: “Bandit accountants, more like. I haven’t ever seen so many bottles of white out in my life.” Tmm: “Well, when you’re a bandit king, there’s no room for error y’know…” B.T.T.: “Wow, that sounded pretty normal for him. I think the drugs wore off.” Cube: “He’s still on the ceiling, remember.” B.T.T.: “He’s done it before. I’d bet he’s cured.” Cube: “Hey! How many tentacles do I have?” (Tmm barks laughter.) Tmm: “That’s silly! You don’t have any tentacles!” B.T.T.: “Ah-HA!” Tmm: “I’d see them with my X-Ray vision if you had any!” Cube: (slyly) “I forget, how much did you bet?” B.T.T.: “We never shook on it.” Cube: “How convenient… for you.” B.T.T.: “That’s not important. What IS important is that we get him down so he can churn out some crowd fodder of a promo before we get the axe! Have you tried cookies?” Cube: “No good, he starts screaming about snakes.” B.T.T.: “Cut a hole in the ceiling around him?’ Cube: “Relax, it’s taken care of.” (A stained glass window with a picture of Bob Dylan in it enters.) Window: “Which first, crowbar or pistol?” (Camera fades out.)
