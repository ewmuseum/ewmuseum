A formal letter to a young infant

8 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/02 Dearest Kanoi... I hope by the time you finish reading this note, some form of enlightenment may have passed through that young and misguided head of yours, and awaken the rotting and hibernated brain cells of common logic and make you realise that that your so called anguished time on this earth will come to an immediate close soon. It has come to my attention that over the past few weeks, you seem to have taken a keen taste to bring pain and suffering to me. This, in my case, if absolutely fine Kanoi. I live with those two emotions and feelings everyday, so if you feel you have some new form of punishment spawned from them, please feel free to bring it to the table ,and I will dabble in it willingly. Yet, if my memory serves me correctly, out of the two times we have already met my young friend, I've gained the advantage. That's leaving me standing 2-0 over you, but obviously simple statistics are not something you pay attention to, but then again who am I kidding? I don't pay attention to them either. Then again, allow me to refresh your memory. Adrenaline, you and I, one on one. Do you remember the feeling of laying across the cold hard wood of the table on the outside, dazed, disorientated, praying for it all to end, when suddenly a Shade of a figure comes soaring over the ring and crashes through your young and fragile body smashing it through the table to the hard floor. Do you remember that Kanoi? I do... Our second encounter, Decimation. Do you still have flashbacks of falling through the air unprotected, only for your head to collide with a set of tables followed by the concrete floor? Does your shoulder still occasionally weep with puss and fluids from the crossbow fired right it? Does your young mind allow you to realise you are battling someone who can not be tortured by a novice in suffering as yourself? So young, I remember being 19 Kanoi. It seems strange even to just write that, 19 years old and already your facing death, The Avatar Of Death. Although I admire your attempts, you have displayed signs of strength at me and have seized your opportunities in a most sadistic manner against me, I like that. Reminds me of myself when I was your age. Yet your pain seems to commercial for my liking. Looking dark and feasting on peoples flesh was made cool and popular by mid 90's MTV and graphic novels sold to Warner Bros to turn into blockbuster motion pictures. My pain is real, my suffering is a harsh reality. That is what separates me from you. When we eventually draw the battle lines in the sand and square off one last time, it will be reality that determines who survives. Don't bother reading that sentence again, I sad survives, because lets face if one of us merely survives our encounter physically and mentally it will be a miracle. Yet it's a sacrifice I'm willing to make. Yet you have to make a decision. Are you willing to sacrifice your mere commercialised image and lifestyle and put it to the test. I dearly hope you are. Until then rest easy young Kanoi, because the campaign and conflict between you and I looms sadistically on the horizon. Yours faithfullyGideon Daray Poe
