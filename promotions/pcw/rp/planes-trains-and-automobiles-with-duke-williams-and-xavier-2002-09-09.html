Planes, Trains, and Automobiles with Duke Williams and Xavier Kannon.

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/9/02 -It’s a street corner in New York, New York and standing on it is a one Duke Williams, and the CEO of PCW Xavier Kannon. They just got out of a meeting with the board of directors of MTV.- Kannon: How can those peons at MTV not let us tax their workers if they perform on our show? Williams: Damn if I know. Besides we would have to use a lot of loud, crappy, music. -Kannon looks at his watch then the dirty street.- Kannon: Where in the name of Reverend Lovejoy is MY limo? Williams: Those bastards at the limo company here in New York are always fucking up. Kannon: My little poon puppy and I have a special week planed. Williams: I wish I could say the same of my little lady. Kannon: I’m calling the limo company and if they don’t have a car here in five minutes I’ll fire them!!!! Williams: Hold it hoss, it will take at least an hour for a limo to get here in this heavy New York traffic. I have a cab coming any moment you can ride with me. -Xavier puts down his phone but looks disgusted.- Kannon: A cab?? Williams: HA HA HA you know a taxi. It’s like a limo only not as long. -Just then a taxi pulls up and pops the trunk.- Kannon: A commoner’s limo? Williams: Do you really have an alternative? It’s just like a limo just not as roomy. Kannon: I JUST WANT TO GET OUT OF THIS CITY!!!!! -The cab is show pulling away into the New York traffic. A few minutes later we rejoin our traveling Front Office friends. Both men are in the back seat of the taxi with their knees firmly placed against the security wall. Due to both men’s size there is zero ball room as the two men wiggle just to breath. The drive who is of middle eastern decent and Duke are engaged in conversation as Kannon just sits there in disgust.- Williams: I watch that show all the time!! Cabbie: OK, OK, Ok, so I watch show and a piggy was on it. Dis piggy saved da life its tis owner. OK? Williams: I saw one where a cat detected a gas leak in the back yard and saved a whole family. The cat threw its self up against the door until the owners followed him. Amazing. Cabbie: Vhat I like about the show is the kitties stories. I have cat. Williams: HA HA HA what’s his name? -Kannon shoots Duke a look begging him to shut the fuck up.- Cabbie: I name him Clinton after the then president. I love America!! Williams: Amen hoss. Hey X you have any pets. -Xavier looks at Duke then shakes his head.- Williams: Not even a fish. -Xavier ignores Duke and reads the Taxi’s rules.- Williams: As a child did you have anything? Kannon: WHAT THE NAME OF HUBBARD IS THAT SMELL??? -Duke leans over to Kannon almost crushing him and points his thumb in a hush-hush manor towards the drive. He then whispers to him.- Williams: Its called curry its in there cooking. -Xavier opens a window and hangs his head out of it.- Kannon: It smells terrible. This is hell. Williams: HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA -A few minutes later the taxi pulls into the air port and the two sardined wrestlers exit. Duke pays the driver as Kannon looks over at a women exiting a limo in jealousy.- Williams: See that wasn’t so bad was it? Kannon: LETS GET TO THE PLANE. -The two men look at the screen and see a final call for their flight. They race through the airport till they find the boarding area. They get patted down by security and before they know it they are getting on the plane. Kannon pulls Duke to the side.- Kannon: Our seats our in different sections I just want to apologize for being a bit nasty during the cab ride. I’m tired and want to GET OUT OF THIS GOD AWFUL CITY!! So thank you for getting us here. Williams: No problem I understand. -The two men shake hands as they board the plane west bound.- Williams: See you! -The camera follows Duke into the general seating area where his ticket is for due to a last minute booking from a now fired PCW worker. The camera then cuts to Xavier Kannon as he argues with the flight attendant named Shanee.- Shanee: Sir your not getting back here. Kannon: Listen you mental retard my ticket is for first class. Shanee: We are over booked SIR. Kannon: So un-book it MISS!! -The two go head to head.- Shanee: Sorry no. You can get a refund for the difference now will you please go to coach. Kannon: Do you know who I am?? I’m the CEO of PCW the number one rated wrestling organization in the world. I make more in a minute then you do in a year. Now you WILL MOVE OR I WILL BUY YOU AND FIRE YOU. -The flight attendants neck starts to gyrate as he attitude matches Kannons.- Shanee: I don’t care if your James Brown you ain’t sitting back here. Now Mr. CEO you have a decision you can sit down in coach or get off MY GOD DAMN PLANE? You dig baby? Kannon: You’ll be hearing from my lawyer. -Kannon goes to sit down and finds his seat. He is sandwiched between a priest and a smiling Duke Williams.- Williams: Hey buddy your back! -Kannon gives a half ass smile as he just nods and squeezes him way to his seat.- Williams: So Miya thinks he funny trying to impersonate the Malice Man? Does he not know I’m a one of a kind Cajun clipper? Does he not know that I’m president of PCW? -Kannon reads the vomit bag as Duke goes on one of his famous rants.- Williams: He can have all those filthy hairy assed hillbillies behind him. I don’t need them. I think Miya is jealous that I was more popular then he was. Well boo-hoo. I think he likes the cock. I think him and his pranking buddies are all homosexuals. One of them weird cults or something like that. I saw something about goggle wearing homo’s on 20/20 one night. Hoss I just know something isn’t right. -Kannon just nods his head in disinterest.- Williams: I mean come on I’m the Malice Man. What is his deal anyway? He pranks people and people like that? Isn’t that called a Juvenal delinquent? His gimmick is being Juvenal delinquent? That is a joke in its self. Who is he running around with a bunch of jobbers from other feds. Come on! This guy is a first rate loser. When God was passing out head he thought he said beds and said give me a big soft one. The guy is about as bright as night. Something is fucked up with his name or something. Miyagawa isn’t a normal name. I bet he’s one of them Satan worshipers. BOO to him. You know what I mean hoss? -Kannon just sighs.- Williams: Well its bed time for me. -Duke slides off his 900 dollar ostrich skin boots.- Williams: Woo doggie my cattle are dropping fodder. -Duke slides off his socks as Kannon just looks away in pure disgust- (The camera rejoins Xavier Kannon on the phone with Ellie~! in Iowa as the plane was not able to land in Chicago.) Kannon: I won’t be able to make it home tonight. I’m stuck in some hick village with corn EVERY WHERE!!! If I get into Chicago I’ll take a limo to Hubbardville but don’t wait up for me. Sorry my little poon puppy but there isn’t anything I can do about it now. -Kannon hangs up the phone and standing next to him is Duke Williams.- Williams: You get a hotel room? Kannon: What? The delay won’t be that long. Williams: Oh yea, they just went on strike in Chicago. No flight are going that way. As far as its backed up were stuck here for a week. I got the last hotel not booked up in town. If you come now I can get you a room. -Kannon looks at people sleeping on the floor and follows Duke out front where he hops on the back of a Harley.- Williams: One of my old fans lent me his bike he’ll pick it up at the hotel. Hop on. Kannon: NO WAY!!! I don’t do death bikes. Williams: Oh come on I’m a great driver. Kannon: I don’t care I’m the world champion and CEO of PCW I don’t do bikes. Williams: Ok then that cab over there will take you to the hotel. -The camera rejoins the two Front Office road warriors as Stephenwolf’s “Born To Be Wild” plays from the bike. Kannon is hanging on for dear life as the laughing Duke Williams pops a wheelie. The next scene shows Duke and Xavier at the hotel room talking to the clerk.- Cashier: I’m sorry we are booked up. All I have is the room you booked Duke. I’m sorry Mr. Kannon. Williams: Can we share my room? Cashier: I don’t see why not. (The camera pans the small hotel room then does a double back on the one queen sized bed.) Williams: Oh boy. Kannon: DUKE!!!! (A few hours pass and the camera zooms in on Duke sound asleep snoring like a chainsaw taking up two-thirds of the bed. Lying next to him with a single blanket is Xavier Kannon just staring into the darkness. Every time he closes his eyes Duke lets out a loud snort. Final Kannon rifles up and yells.) Kannon: In the name of L. Ron I can’t take it anymore. WILL YOU PLEASE SHUT UP!!!! Williams: HUH? Kannon: Your snoring and talking in your sleep. Williams: What? Kannon: Duke I respect you but I can’t stand this. You have driven me crazy since New York. Didn’t you get the picture when I was reading the vomit bag. Here’s an idea when go on your LONG rants can you please come up with some new material? Not EVERYBODY is a homo, or in a cult, or stupid. It’s the same stuff over and over again. AND FOR THE LOVE OF HUBBARD WHAT THE HELL IS A HOSS?? Does anybody really know….no…wait…..does anybody really care? -Duke just stares at Kannon obviously hurt.- Williams: Oh and you think your so perfect you do things that drive me crazy too. Kannon: Like what? Williams: Your balls. Kannon: What? Williams: You fidget with you nuts all the time. You keep checking if their still there. Plus everything is Hubbard this and Hubbard that. You know I was just trying to help you get home to your wife. That’s all I was doing. I’m in no rush. -Kannon has look like he wishes he could take some of what he said back. Duke start to exit the room.- Kannon: Look I lost my temper please come back. After all we our the Front Office and nobody is better then us. Were a team. -Duke thinks it over then walks over and shakes Kannon’s hand and gets back into bed.- (The camera rejoins the duo first thing in the morning. Duke is curled up with his arm around Kannon. Kannon kisses Dukes hand in a loving manner thinking it’s his wife’s hand. His eyes suddenly open and realizes that isn’t his wife.) Kannon: Duke?? Williams: Yea. Kannon: Why is your hand on me? Williams: Its not its resting on a warm pillow. Kannon: THAT’S NOT A PILLOW!!! -Both men get out of the bed likes its on fire.- Williams: Dare God. Kannon: THIS ISN’T RIGHT!! Williams: Jesus. Kannon: Hubbard. Williams: How about them Bears winning last minute Sunday? Kannon: Yea the Packers too. Williams: With all do respect I think your wife has a great set of tits. Kannon: Yea she does have a wonderful rack. Williams: You get the new Playboy yet? Kannon: OH yea and the new Hustler. Williams: Great pictures. Kannon: Yea, yea……. -Both men regain their composer from the unfortunate mistake.- (The camera then rejoins them on a train with the next stop being Hubbardsville. Duke is leading a sing along as Kannon sits with fingers in his ears praying for the end.) Williams: On the road again….I just can’t wait to get on the road again….the life I love is making music with my friends and I can’t wait to get on the road again. -Everybody claps but Kannon.- Williams: You feeling ok? Kannon: I just can’t wait to get home and be with my wife and away from these diseased people. (The train starts to pull into Hubbardsville.) Williams: I hear you man I remember them days. Kannon: What do you mean I thought you were going home to Montana to see your wife. Williams: She just filed for divorce this will be my sixth. Kannon: So where are you going? Williams: I don’t know. Conductor: Last call for Hubbardsville. -Xavier thinks long and hard.- Kannon: We have a guest room in our home your welcome to for a few days till you can find a place to live. Williams: You mean it? Kannon: Take it now or I will change my mind. -The two men exit the train and walk into America’s only perfect city. The only city home to the CEO and world champion of the only true wrestling organization in the world PCW. The wonderful Hubbardsville, Wisconsin. God’s city.- Williams: Its been a long journey but this was worth it. =w=
