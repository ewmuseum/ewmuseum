Revenge Saga Part 23: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine...pt3

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 23: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine…pt3 The…third…time. Crichton’s mind was a very fragile place…one single event or action could tip him over the edge and into potential madness. The thing was, Crichton could see the event coming…It was his match against Jordan Shabazz at the Pay Per View entitled Wrestle Vessel. As the old saying went ‘third time lucky’ and Crichton couldn’t help wondering that applied to this case. Yeah okay, Crichton had managed to defeat Jordan Shabazz two times before, but those matches were well fought and very tough. The first title match, at PCW’s previous Pay Per View named Gold and Glory, the match was arguably the best on the card. Both he and Shabazz fought their asses off in that match…hell, they both lasted almost fifteen minutes…the standard time limit for a TV title matchup…Yeah, Crichton won under ‘dubious’ circumstances, but nobody could say that he hadn’t deserved to win the match, especially after being screwed out of the title on the night of the PCW roster split. The second match was just a couple of weeks ago, on Tuesday Turmoil. Leading up to the match Shabazz hooked up with some ‘unsavoury’ characters in the form of The Rush and Thug RoC and began to take part in cowardly sneak attacks against him. From there Crichton had slowly begun to lose his mind…in a way the wheels were already in motion, just a simple catalyst was needed. He predicted that losing his title to Shabazz of all people, could be the catalyst, in essence the dark impulses would take over and he would finally lose his mind. At the end of the second title match it was evident that Thug RoC intended to do everything he could to make Shabazz the champion…he was just a little too late that’s all. And what happened afterwards proved his point entirely, Thug RoC and The Rush viciously attacked him…they were poor losers that was obvious, but what Crichton couldn’t understand was that must be used to it by now! Surely they should adapt to losing, and they should try to lose like men in the future. ************ Wolverine was pissed off to say the least. It was his day off from ‘adventuring’ and here he was assing around Westchester looking for yet another nut job. Some days he just asked for it. His hyper-senses were the most form of tracking he knew of and he still hadn’t found the man named John Crichton. About 30 minutes ago, he had pulled up next to a house, it had the scent of Crichton all over it, but there was also a more recent of him, a scent that led away from the house and deeper into the centre of New York City. Resisting the temptation to just leave the hunt and get on with his holiday, Wolverine reluctantly began to follow the new scent. He was drawing ever nearer to Crichton…it was only a matter of time before he found him, but being so close brought more and more questions that he didn’t even wanna think about, never mind speculating an answer for them. What exactly was he going to do when he found John Crichton? Rumours were that he once fought off the entire roster of the Avengers, so how could he alone defeat him? Wolverine was never the one to be intimidated by anyone, not even Galactus, ‘The Devourer of Worlds’ and he certainly wasn’t the one who would back out of a fight. Wolverine placed one of his cigars into his mouth and lit the end with a stylish metal lighter, before placing more pressure upon the accelerator pedal, speeding off into the midday sun. ************ Fleming sat uncomfortably upon the bare wooden chair, squirming a little attempting to get into a comfortable position. He had been led into this primitive ‘office’ by the Thunderbolt known as Moonstone. She ushered him rather impatiently and told him to sit, their ‘boss’ would be along soon. This place was in no condition to hold any kind of meetings, especially the kind of business meetings that would be taking place today. The more he studied the bare interior of the room, the more uncomfortable he felt probably the intention. It would, after all, give whoever was in charge around here the upper hand in ‘negotiations’ not that he was in any position to negotiate. Still, they wanted something from him otherwise he wouldn’t be here today, they simply would have rejected the job offer and went on their way. The bare stone walls left him with a desolate feeling inside, the bare wooden chairs and the damaged desk added to this desolation. This long wait was also an attempt to intimidate him further, but he wasn’t scared, he wasn’t daunted, he didn’t really care. Fleming’s concentration was broken as the equally desolate wooden door, slowly creaks open. Fleming stares at the door…waiting for whoever was on the other side of the decaying wood. When the door opens fully Fleming cannot believe the man was still alive. The other mind games, the desolate room, the long wait intimidated him less than the mere presence of the man confronting him now. He had been assumed dead for a long while and was one of the most dangerous men on the planet. His evil had been well known throughout the world, never mind the United States of America. His name? His name was Baron Helmut Zemo…the son of Captain America’s greatest foe, Baron Zemo. ************ Crichton had got bored of the Television in Jaden’s place and decided to go for a wander around the city. It was always dangerous for Crichton to walk around in public area’s, especially in New York City – it was the pinnacle of all Super Hero and Super Villain activity in the USA, but anything was better than Oprah. Normally he would find something else to do, something to occupy his mind and body, but all he could think about was his match against Shabazz at the PCW pay per view, Wrestle Vessel. It wasn’t long before the novelty of the walk wore off, and the thoughts further pierced his mind. He now realised that he could never escape these thoughts, so he decided that a new tactic was needed. To drown these thoughts with the amber nectar known as BEER! He made his way to the nearest back alley bar, he really didn’t know where he was going, but he trusted his instincts. After all he was a man he would eventually find a bar sooner or later. Within mere minutes of his wandering he was sat on a stool, in front of a bar with a beer firmly fixed in his left hand. For the first time that day he was utterly at peace. Predictably, as soon as the thought of peace entered his mind, trouble came through the door. Literally came through the door, as The Thunderbolt known as Mach III crashes through the door, landing elegantly upon the white tiled dance floor, his metal wings retracting into his backpack. Closely following him were the other Thunderbolts known as Jolt and Songbird. Crichton stood up, the stool falling upon the floor behind him, he quickly drank the last of his beer before backing up against the back wall. The other civilians, a few old drunkards and the landlord, began to flood out of the doors to safety. Crichton was mildly relieved that the Thunderbolts didn’t try to maim them as the civilians left, but Crichton realised that they were business men/women not slaughterers, although it was a very fine line. He was outnumbered three to one, this didn’t look good, this didn’t look at all. ************ Wolverine parked up his jeep next to the curb and exited his mode of transport and stepped onto the pavement. Once again, he pulled his mask over his head, in preparation of battle. Wolverine circled a square block and Crichton’s scent was coming from the exact centre of the block, therefore Crichton had to be somewhere in the back alleyways. He would have to go by foot from here, Wolverine cracked his knuckles a little. After all of this hunting, Wolverine quickly hungered for a fight…looks like he was going to get that fight. He grinned a little and headed towards the alleyways. ************ Baron Helmut Zemo grinned at the reaction of Fleming at his mere presence and simply walked past him, without giving any eye contact. Zemo sits upon the chair opposite Fleming. Zemo: I see that you’re familiar with me Fleming. Fleming: …Uh…yeah…your Helmut Zemo, right? Zemo: Indeed I am. Fleming: …so…uh…what do I owe the pleasure of this…uh…meeting? Zemo: I have a business proposal for you! Fleming: Really? I assume this is in exchange for the services of your Thunderbolts. Zemo: No, actually I’ve sent some of my Thunderbolts to ‘deal’ with Crichton as we speak, to [pausing a little] sweeten the business proposal. Fleming: Okay, can I hear your proposal? Zemo: [Smiling] I thought you’d never ask! It’s simple really, let us use Zemoff industries to both fund our criminal actions and give us the technology to perform our criminal actions… Fleming: And what’s in it for me? Zemo: …Well, if you co-operate, I won’t kill you! TO BE CONTINUED…
