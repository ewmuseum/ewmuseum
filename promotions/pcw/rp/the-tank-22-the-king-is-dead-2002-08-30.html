[detail withheld] The Tank __22— The King is Dead Broken shards of heavy crownswill cut the veins of those on their knees. A King’s wrath is seen only in demise. ~Leigh Donovan From the Office of the Warden “When the dust settled, twenty-five men died in the Unit E riot,” the Warden begins with a sigh. “Sixteen black, five white, one Italian, one Hispanic. The other two were CorrectionalOfficers.” The Warden pauses to take a sip of water. Newspapers lie scattered about his desk with theheadline bellowing - ‘Inmates Riot at Peter J. Tankerton!’ With a heavy heart, he continues -“Dead... The riot began when one of Harvey Blake’s men stabbed three of Jeremy Doris’ Thugswith a knife he sharpened out of a broken piece of a plastic chair. Who said prisoners aren’tcreative?” “Ironic part is, of the twenty-three prisoners in body bags, only seven of the blacks were Thugs,and only two of the whites were Nazis. The other thirteen, and the two C.O’s... just got caughtup in the confusion.” Tuesday, August 27 - Day 53 Tim McDonald smirks at the television screen as he sits alone in the television area on the mainfloor of Unit B. His spiky blond hair is still drying from his morning shower, where he met upwith most of the Italian gang - they’re all completely in the dark about Tim’s manipulating handin Doug Hattoni’s death... ***FLASHBACK #1*** Tim McDonald, Doug Hattoni’s young cellmate and protege, runs his fingers along the back of arow of hardbound books in the small library that Unit B prisoners are allowed to use during thelate hours before lockdown. ... “I already explained to you what kind of man you’re protecting,” says the gruff voice. TimMcDonald pulls a book carefully out of place and finds himself with a great view of a meeting inthe next aisle. He’s surprised to find Jason Snow speaking to the arch enemy of Doug Hattoni -Harvey Blake. ... “Hey, whatever you fucking want,” Harvey says, glaring at Snow through his lone eye. “Butremember this you God damn guinea-fucker hippy, you’’re going to regret this decision. Youmark my fucking words.” ***FLASHBACK #2*** “So Doug actually sets him up with drug deals,” Snow says, a bit disgusted by the entire system. Somehow, deep within, Snow can tell that Tim McDonald is pleased with his disgust. “Nowyou’’re getting it, Lion,” Tim says with an evil smile. “You see, this guy, this,” McDonald pausesto look inside the envelope, “Gino Lopano - the best thing he ever did was smack thatbitch. Now he’s got the fucking life. He pulls in damn near ten grand a week.” McDonald pausesagain. This time, when he continues, he speaks slowly to assure that Snow hangs on his everyword. “Gino Lopano has it fucking made.” Snow looks to his left, directly into McDonald’s eyes. Somehow, after this conversation, hetrusts the slick-looking man less than ever, and something inside him is sure that Doug Hattonishould trust him less than anyone. ***FLASHBACK #3*** “Listen, Harv,” Jake starts in a tone that indicates he’’s about to change the subject. “The shit inUnit E is about to fucking explode. I don’’t know how much longer our boys can hold off. Thefucking niggers in there are pulling all kinds of shit. One of our guys is in the infirmary rightnow with broken ribs. ...They want you to give the order Harv - they want to start a fuckingbrawl.” “Be patient, son,” Harvey says as he turns his attention back to Jason Snow. “You have torecognize an ace in your sleeve when you feel it.” Little does Harvey Blake know, his words are not only reaching the ears of his fellow Aryans. Sitting at the table directly behind him, among many of the older prisoners in the Tank, is noneother than Tim McDonald - he’s been intently listening the whole time. ***FLASHBACK #4*** “Listen,” McDonald says, finally getting down to business. “Something’s going to go down inUnit E in a little while.” “And?” “And I want you to take all your buddies up there to get things back under control,” McDonaldsays with a sly smile. “You want me to lead all the C.O’s out of Unit B?” the large man smirks. “Fuck that, Timmy - Iain’t losing my fucking job over one of your scams.” “You won’t lose anything, trust me,” McDonald says. “The Warden’s going to call you guys upthere anyway - I just want you to make sure that you’re on your toes when it happens, and bringa couple of extra boys up there when it goes down. Trust me, you’’re going to need them.” Withthat, Tim reaches into his pocket once more and adds another pile of bills to the one alreadyresting on the control desk. ***FLASHBACK #5*** As he leaves the cell, Snow feels no remorse for what is about to happen. To him, it’s justice. Justice in this place may not be as technical and supervised as justice on the outside, but it’’sswift, harsh, and final. “Hiya, Doug,” Harvey Blake says with a smile as he steps toward the old mobster, grabbing apillow off the lower bunk of the cell. “Were you expecting our company?” At the opposite end of the cell balcony, staring across the large gap that exposes the main floorbelow, Tim McDonald watches all of this - an evil smile painted to his young face all the while. Tim snickers at the thought that Hattoni is now out of the picture and he alone owns the Unit Bdrug trade. Soon, he’ll be dealing to the entire Tank, just like Hattoni did. It’ll take a littlework, of course, but sooner or later, the Italians will look to him for leadership. He was, ofcourse, Doug Hattoni’s closest friend at the time of the aging fat man’s death... if only they knewhow large a part Tim played in Hattoni’s demise. Tim turns his attention to the television screen where Doug Hattoni’s death is being featured inthe headline story... The underworld mourns today as one of the main figures in organized crime in thepast thirty years was laid to rest,” a female news reporter begins from behind her desk. She triesto look professional, though deep down, she’s excited to have the chance to report such a largestory. “Doug Hattoni,” she continues, “Don of the infamous Hattoni family of New York City, waseight years into a twenty year sentence at Peter J. Tankerton Maximum Security Prison in LosAngeles when he was found dead in his cell late Saturday morning and is suspected to be thevictim of inmate violence.” “Doug Hattoni was fifty-two years old, and his lawyers were working toward getting him paroledwithin two years.” “Hattoni’s eldest son, Justin, thirty, who’s been handling the family business since DougHattoni’s incarceration in 1994, is believed to have taken over the title of ‘Don’ in the family.” “Gavyn Miller, Warden of the notoriously violent prison, admitted that he’s received warningfrom the FBI and says that although he’s not particularly concerned with Mafia retaliation, hewill take necessary precautions. In a press conference last night, he announced that visitationprivileges have been revoked for all prisoners that in Doug Hattoni’s Unit, Unit B, for their ownsafety. He added that visitation privileges would be restored at a later, undisclosed,time.” “What the fuck!?” bellows J-Thug, sitting behind Tim McDonald. “They can’t take away ourmotherfucking visits because someone off’ed that fat bitch! I’m going to see Rankin about thisshit! They can’t fucking do it!” J-Thug angrily gets out of his chair and heads toward GeofferyRankin’s office. Tim McDonald’s still smirking - visitation privileges mean nothing to him. By the timevisitation privileges are restored, he plans to have every Italian in the Tank writing home aboutwho the Hattoni family should really be looking to for leadership on the outside. Parole’s just a few short months away, and Tim McDonald can taste the power already. There, in the middle of the main floor of Unit B, Tim McDonald pulls out one of Doug Hattoni’scigars and enjoys the satisfaction of a job well done. *** From the Office of the Warden “Doug Hattoni, unfortunately, was probably going to be walking the street within a few years. His lawyers were pushing for a parole hearing, and with the amount of grease Hattoni has forpalms, chances are, his days in the Tank were just about numbered.” “The world is like a wheel. Grease turns the wheel... grease of greener nature turns theworld. *** Tuesday, August 27 - Day 54 “For fuck’s sake, Rankin!” Snow exclaims, staring at the smaller man in his messy office. Rankin, playing nervously with his thick brown hair doesn’t know how to respond. “Snow,” Rankin finally sighs, “there’s just nothing I can do. If I could I would - you know that.” “No!” Snow fires back. “I don’t know that! All I know is my sister-in-law got fuckingraped a few days ago, and you’re the man telling me that I can’t see her!” Snowpauses to catch his breath, his fiery eyes half hating and half pleading with Geoffery Rankin. “Damn it, Rankin!” he fires desperately, “You’re the one who talked me into letting hervisit in the first place! And now you’re saying I can’t see her? That’s bull shit!” “It’s not my call, Snow,” says Rankin, sitting on the edge of his desk. “There’s really nothing Ican do. You have to understand, Snow - since Doug Hattoni got killed, we’ve got every fuckingdepartment in the legal system warning us that one of his hot head goons is going to try toretaliate.” “Retaliate against who?” Snow asks angrily. “I don’t know!” Rankin snaps back. “Nobody fucking knows, Snow! All we know is that a manwho was very important to the underworld died in your Unit last weekend. A man who hadsome very dangerous, and very loyal, friends. We’re not going to take any chances.” Snow leans against the far wall of Rankin’s messy office, accepting his defeat - there’s nogetting around this. Now, when he’s finally ready to support Krista in the time she needs himmost - he can’t. These walls have never seemed so solid until now... “Rankin,” Snow says quietly, trying to let the Unit B manager see his desperation. “She needsme - you’re the one who made me believe that. There must be something you can do - somestring you can pull.” “Well,” Geoffery sighs. “I’ve been keeping an eye on her.” Snow immediately glares harshly at the authority figure. The last time someone ‘kept an eye’ onKrista, he ended up being her rapist. Though he realizes Rankin has only the best of intentions,he can’t help but feel sick at the sound of the words. “She’s been going to therapy,” Rankin continues. “And she’s been going every day - here,actually.” “What!?” Snow exclaims. “She’s been coming here for therapy!?” “Don’t worry,” Rankin says, gesturing for Snow to calm himself. “She’s far away from theprisoners. It’s just... she refuses to go back to Canada. After you left her in the visiting room lastSaturday, I set up a session with Jenny Vegas for her. And now... well... she won’t talk to anyonebut Jenny.” “Jenny Vegas,” Snow frowns. “That same woman who spends every Saturday evening shakingher tits in my face, claiming it’s anger management?” He shakes his head and speakssarcastically, “Great.” “You may not like her methods, but with most of the scum in here, it works,” shrugs Rankin. Hethen steps over to Snow and tries to be empathetic. “Look, Jenny Vegas may not be apsychological genius, but as of now, she’s the only one Krista will talk to right now. She’s asensitive woman - that’s what Krista really needs right now.” “What about me?” “She needs you too,” Rankin sighs. “She’s seen you as a protector for so long now...” “But I can’t see her.” “Snow...” Rankin says softly, wishing Snow would just understand that there is nothing he cando. Then again, Rankin agrees with Snow completely. “Look,” he finally concedes, “don’t takethis as a promise, but I’ll see what I can do.” “That’s all I want,” Snow answers as his eyes fill with hope. Without another word, he makeshis exit from Rankin’s office, hoping he can focus on the task at hand tomorrow on Turmoil. *** From the Office of the Warden “But with Doug Hattoni in a box,” the Warden sighs, “all bets in the Tank are off. Suddenly,every major gang in the prison is peaking its head into the picture, wondering if it can be the oneto take control.” “At the forefront,” the Warden says coldly, “are Harvey Blake and Jeremy ‘J-Thug’Doris.” *** Wednesday, August 28 - Day 55 Harvey Blake, like everyone else in the Tank, knows nothing of Tim McDonald’s hand in DougHattoni’s murder. He doesn’t realize that if McDonald hadn’t bribed the Head C.O of Unit B atthat perfect moment, there would be a good chance that Harvey and a few of his followers wouldbe spending a large portion of their days in solitary confinement. Harvey Blake, however, is happy to believe that he was the sole mastermind behind the famouscrime boss’ death. To him, it feels a lot like it did when he murdered the leader of the blackstwenty years ago. For the second time, he’s put his Aryans back at the top of the heap. As he leans against the pool table on the main floor of Unit B, Harvey can’t help but chuckle. He owns this Unit again, as far as he’s concerned. The Thugs may have something to say aboutthat, he knows - but for now, he’s the man in charge. Suddenly, he spots J-Thug’s heavy eyes on him and approaching quickly. “Well, well, boys,”Harvey says with a smile to his companions. “Is it just me or is it getting dark in here?” The massive J-Thug walks alone toward the one eyed Nazi, Harvey Blake, his face contorted in ascowl. Harvey stands up straight to meet the enormous black man. “What do you want, Doris?” Harvey asks, glaring up at J-Thug through his lone terrifying eye. “You know what I want,” J-Thug growls. “What the fuck was that shit over in Unit E? Youwant to make a motherfucking habit of jumping Thugs, you one-eyed bastard!? I’ll make a habitof putting my foot in your white ass!” J-Thug takes a step toward Harvey, prompting all of theNazis in the area to take note and get ready for action. Harvey notices that most of the Thugs in the Unit have their eye on the situation. “You think Iwas going to let my boys act like punching bags for your nig...” Harvey hesitates. “Sorry - yourcoons?” J-Thug’s face ripples with anger. “For a week I was hearing about the things you fuckers were doing to my men in Unit E,” Harveytells J-Thug. “All I did was tell them to fight back.” “Seven of my men are dead!” J-Thug snaps. “Sixteen,” Harvey corrects. “Only seven were Thugs,” says J-Thug. “Sixteen were niggers.” J-Thugs eyes grow wide with a mixture of shock and anger. “Watch your ass, motherfucker,” hesays seriously to Blake. “This ain’t over, bitch - this ain’t fucking over.” With that, J-Thug turnshis back and walks back to his empty cell.
