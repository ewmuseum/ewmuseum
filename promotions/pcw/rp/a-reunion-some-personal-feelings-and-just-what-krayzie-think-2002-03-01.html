A reunion, some personal feelings and just what Krayzie thinks....

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 If it's one thing that can be learned from the Krazed Lunatic, its that the love for one man's family can and will overcome the worst of things. Even evil. And now, with Jeremy Howard on his way back to his hometown of Elkhart, Indiana, after taking his son back from the dark forces of evil that held took him at birth, a family will be reunited. Now, the love of a mother, Jessica, can nurture a son that has never gotten the chance to know just how great his father and mother really are. To that, the scene fades in from black, to the very front of Jeremy Howard's two story house in suburban Elkhart, Indiana. The camera stays focused on the front door, built of glass, not moving away. Suddenly, a honk can be heard in the background. As the camera begins to zoom backwards, revealing more the picture, the front door opens. There, Jessica, dressed in white pants and a navel bearing white shirt, steps out of the front door to the sight of her baby boy, Troy, standing in the driveway with his father and former two-time World Champion, Jeremy Howard. Jessica: Troy! The mother in her kicks in, sparking the lovely blonde beauty to race the twenty four or so feet to the driveway and hug her son. One might wonder how a two year, whose never really met these people before, can attach himself so quickly to Jeremy and Jessica. But, there's no love like a parents love. And, this two year old little boy is getting his first taste of that love. The Voice of Miyagawa: We're going to head out for Fusion and get ready. Jeremy turns around toward the yellow hummer that sits behind him, facing his longtime friend and Asylum stablemate, Alexander Miyagawa sitting in the driver's seat. To Miya's left, sits another of Jeremy's stablemates, Darkstar in the passenger's seat. Jeremy: No problem guys. Thanks for everything. Miyagawa: Just take that of that little boy now. Jeremy knods his head to Miya, before the PCW Television Champion begins to back the yellow hummer out of the driveway. Jeremy turns back toward Jessica, whose flooded with tears of happiness. Jessica: How'd you do it? There had to be over a hundred people there when we last saw them. Jeremy smiles. Jeremy: Lets just say that two men though enough of me, to risk there chance at winning the World title at March Madness by placing themselves in danger. Jessica: Darkstar and Miya? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: I promise, I'll tell you the whole story some other time. Right now, I just want to focus on our son and my next three matches. Jessica: I understand, honey. This man never rests. He had a full five days to rest up for his challenge for the Continental title against Chet King. But, not a single one of those days did he rest. Not one. Instead, the Krazed Lunatic spent every single one of those days trying to find and get back his son. And now, with Troy back in the loving arms of his mother, Jeremy can focus on Chet King. But, that's not the only match he has to think about. There's still Drake Maxwell at March Madness in two days and Snow on Turmoil in five days. Jeremy: Let's go inside beautiful and show Troy his new home. A bright smile graces the face of PCW's loveliest lady according to the opinion of Jeremy Howard. Jessica: I think that's a great idea. Jeremy reaches down and picks up his son. Jessica puts her arm around the waist of the Krayzie One, as the family, all members present, heads for the front door of 187 Belmont Ave., Elkhart, Indiana 46514. Once there, Jessica gets the door, opening it up for Jeremy to introduce Troy to his new home. Jeremy steps in, standing in a twelve by ten foyer. Jeremy: Son, this is your home. The little boy seems apprehensive at first, not really understanding what's going on. His little mind seems lost. Does he really know that Jeremy and Jessica are his parents? Or, were Keith and Mary Lipowitz that bad of parents, that young Troy clinged to the first person that showed him the slightest bit of love? Choice two might an awful thought, but a real possibility. Jessica: Is he alright? Jeremy: Yeah. I'm sure he's not used to us yet. The smile on Jessica's face fades away to a look of sorrow. Jessica: Are you saying he doesn't really know us? Jeremy closes his eyes, knodding his head, 'yes'. It might be sad, but it's the truth. He reopens them. Jeremy: Don't worry. I don't think anyone is going to want to go through the h#ll of taking Troy away after all the things I did to him. Which means, we've got all the time in the world to teach him who we are. Jessica: It's just sad that our own son doesn't know us. Jeremy: No, what's sad, is that I keep having people taken from me because I can't be pushed around. How one man can withstand all that the has happened to the Krayzie One, is beyond imagination. But, Jeremy Howard has done it. The past two Governors of Indiana, Stan Hinkle and Richard O'Bannon, have each taken somebody away from the Krazed Lunatic in there tenures. Stan Hinkle took Jeremy's brother, Justin, just because the Krayzie One didn't throw a match. Richard O'Bannon placed Jeremy's son, Troy, with an occult, after he was already taken by Stan, because Jeremy botched one of his underhanded deals. Jeremy: I'm sick of letting people try to push me around. And, this Sunday, at March Madness, the Krazed Lunatic has the chance to win the freedom of his other family member taken from him, when Jeremy steps into a hell in the cell with Drake Maxwell. Jessica: What are you going to do? The three person family begin to make there way into the living room, that sits just to the left of where they presently are standing. Jeremy: I know we just got Troy back..... and I really want to start rebuilding our family..... but I really must begin to think about what lies ahead for me. Jeremy sets Troy down on the black nicely designed couch that sits just inside the doorway entering the living room. Jessica grabs his hand, sharply, turning Jeremy toward her. Jessica: I don't want you feeling bad for having to leave..... Jeremy blinks his eyes, trying to make sense of it all. Jessica: ....the fact that you thought so much of your son, to risk your own life and save him, speaks volumes for the type of person that you really are. Jeremy: I just worry about your safety. Jessica: Jeremy, you don't have time for worrying about me right now. You have a Continental title to win. Before tonight, Jeremy Howard and Chet King have never faced each other before in singles competition. To Jeremy's knowledge, there's not even a conflict between the two men. But, when 'Down with the Sickness' blasts out over the speaker system tonight, the Krazed Lunatic is going to have one thing on his mind. That being, getting one step closer to his ultimate goal of having a reign with each title in PCW. Jeremy: But you and Troy are my life. Jessica: We'll be alright. You just go and prove to all those people backstage, that say you've lost a step, why you're a two time World Champion..... Jeremy: ..... And why most of them will never get out of the mid-card if they keep worrying more about my business and not enough about there own..... I'll see you before March Madness. I have something I need to do. This man's life is almost always embroiled in some kind of turmoil. It comes with the life of being a PCW superstar. But now, the Krayzie One has to go from being a hero of a father at home to being a fading star in PCW. Can this fading star capture the Continental title? The supposed fading star reaches down and lifts his on up, hugging him. Jessica wraps her arms around the two men in her life that mean the most as the scene fades away..... to black!
