A Look Into The Past: 'From Her Fury, Came Man'

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 - 06 - 03 Come to me, my star-born warrior… Wha…? Who’s there?! The time for my reincarnation is nigh… Your…reincarnation? Who are you?! I am you, I am She, I am everything… I don’t understand. Please, my head… Awaken, my warrior… awaken and feel the fury of En’Flarim… Please…stop…the light!! AHH!! Bursting from the soft mattress of his bed, covered in his own sweat, Dorn Tracer is quite a sight. His hair is in matted tatters, obviously from days of keeping it unkempt and unwashed. His bare chest heaves with exhaustion and fear as the Astronomer struggles to build some sort of sanity to protect himself from his strange dream. For many nights now, Dorn has had to sit through strange, prophecy-like dreams of some woman calling out to him, calling him her ‘star-born warrior’ and ‘the one to save the stars’. It’s been all too much for Dorn’s panicked mind to handle, and now it’s gotten out of control. “I’ve got to find some way to make it stop… I’ve got to see a doctor,” Dorn sputters through his labored exhaling. He draws his forearm across his brow, collecting the cold dampness of his own sweat and removing it from his face and eyes. Dorn throws the ratty, thin blanket off his body and swings his legs over the edge of the bed. With a limited amount of energy, having just woken up, Dorn rises from his state of slumber and rolls his head about, trying to get the pounding his temples to stop. He rises groggily to his feet and stretches his limbs out, letting out a prolonged, tired groan. “Mmmm… it’s wonderful having strange dreams that wake you up at 6 in the morning,” the Astronomer groans, with a hint of malice in his voice. “I suppose it wouldn’t be so bad if I wasn’t practically swimming when I wake up.” Dorn turns and gazes at the huge damp spot on his bed, shaped like the cracks and crevices of his cut, muscular back. Shaking his head in dismay, Dorn leaves towards a door on the opposite side of the dark, cramped apartment bedroom. He swings the door open without any regard for things around it and steps in. Dorn flips on the light and covers his vulnerable eyes from the overwhelming light. “Damn, I should have put on my shades. I can already tell this day is going to be another shit sandwich for Dorn Tracer…” After finally adjusting to the light level, Dorn steps over to the small, rusted shower stall. The bathroom itself isn’t much to look at; there are used towels hanging everywhere, toothpaste stains in the sink, a dirty toilet, and the mirror is fogged and greasy. The Astronomer apparently doesn’t notice, having given in to his accommodations due to being short of income. He flips the opaque glass door of the shower open and turns the hot water on. After waiting for a moment or so, Dorn throws off his white muscle shirt and black boxers and steps in the shower. And in a rather comical way, Dorn begins to sing to himself! “Ooohhh, who lives in a pineapple under the sea? Spongebob Squarepants!” His singing is atrocious, but he’s the only one to hear it, so it’s not like it really matters anyway. He continues showering and singing for about 5 minutes before shutting the shower off. Dorn opens the glass door slightly to grab the red towel hanging on the rack and pulls it into the shower. He steps out a moment later, dripping wet with his jet-black hair hanging all in his face. The Astronomer begins whistling to himself as he approaches the mirror, but his foot catches something, better not to mention what, and he loses all trace of balance. Letting out a roar of panic, he falls flat on his face with a sickening crack. “OW!! My nose…” Dorn groans in pain and tries to rise to his feet while nursing his throbbing nose. “By the stars, this REALLY is going to be a shitty day…” Finally rising to his feet, Dorn approaches the mirror groggily and wipes it clean of fog with his damp hand. His nose is red and even a little swollen, which adds insult to injury seeing as how his nose is a little big already. He rubs it gingerly, almost like he’s trying to heal it, and gives a exasperated sigh. Dorn picks up his toothbrush, dabs some Crest Extra Whitening on it, and begins to aged old ritual of good tooth hygiene. He just stares blankly in the mirror… his mind blank… his eyes tired and expressionless… until… My child… speak to me… Dorn’s eyes shoot open and his mouth drops open, with the toothbrush still inside of it. He slowly turns his head from side to side, looking for any intruders. ”Who’s… who’s dhere?” Dorn sputters, mouth full of toothpaste. Come to me, Lunis Superia… “What the… What’s going on?!” Dorn promptly spits out the toothbrush and whips his head from side to side, frantically searching for the source of the strange voice. It rings in his head and echoes from one ear to the other. It’s soft, yet firm in a way. It’s also a woman’s voice, which definitely sends Dorn into a bit of a panic, since the voice in his dreams was also woman in nature. “Whoever you are, leave me alone and GET OUT OF MY HEAD,” Dorn roars, absolutely panicked into being a basket case. Do not be afraid, my child… It is I…En’Flarim… “WHO OR WHAT IS AN EN’FLARIM?!” I am you… I am She… I am the Eternal One… “This is crazy…” Dorn thinks to himself, in a state of confusion and fear. His breathing is back to being labored and beads of sweat begin to slide down his forehead, collecting at his nose and eyebrows. Gaze in the mirror, my star-born warrior… “No way, I’ve seen the Exorcist! I won’t be possessed by some stupid demon!” Gaze into the mirror… believe in me, my child… for I am you… Just about fed up with riddles and strange voices, Dorn slams his fist against the porcelain sink with a crushing blow and grits his teeth in rage. “SHOW YOURSELF, ALREADY!!” Your destiny is mine. Mine is yours. Gaze upon the mirror if you wish to know… “Fine, I’ll look in the damn mirror!!” Dorn steps up to the mirror angrily and blatantly stares deep into it, nearly burning a hole in it with his anger. For a moment, he sees absolutely nothing but the bathroom behind him and himself. Feeling rather retarded and confused, Dorn sighs and collapses on the sink, cradling his forehead in his head. “What is going on… what IS this CRAP…” Gaze into the mirror, Lunis Superia… “I DID, STARS-DAMNIT!! I DIDN’T SEE ANYTHING,” He roars into the sink, never taking his gaze off the dirty porcelain. Gaze into the mirror… Dorn lifts his head weakly from his hand and gives a sad, confused look to the mirror… but this time, he doesn’t just see the bathroom and himself. ”Oh…my…WHAT THE?!” Dorn cries out in panic, for instead of himself in the mirror, he sees a flaming visage of what appears to be a reptile-like creature. You see me now, my star-born warrior… The creature has no skin, merely flames. Two dark, swirling holes make up its eyes and it sees as if parts of the flames just die out when the creature is speaking, then relight. Dorn cries out in surprise and staggers backwards into the wall, gripping it for dear life. His body shakes violently as a wave of emotion runs through his body. ”What the… who…who…who are you?!” I am En’Flarim, Goddess of Destruction and Matter, I am You, I am She, I am everything… “I…don’t understand…” I am the supreme ruler of positive and negative. Your very make-up is mine to command, and mine to wear. I rule from on high, hidden within the Great Star… “The Great Star? What in the name of Jupiter is that??” A great ball of fire and gas, the source of my power… and my captive form…I have come to enlighten you, my loyal follower… Dorn can barely compose himself. He wants to run. He wants to run FAR away. A strange, burning spirit claiming to be Queen of the Universe has visited him and now wants to ‘enlighten’ him, and by the looks of her fire, that’s not a very good thing. Dorn decides he isn’t going to listen this and begins to hightail it out the door, but some strange force slams it closed just before he can get out. He spins around, slams himself against the door and tries to back away from the burning spirit. “GET AWAY FROM ME!! WHAT DO YOU WANT?! I DON’T WANT TO BE ENLIGHTENED!!!” Dorn screams, wanting nothing more than to dive out the nearest window. Calm yourself, Lunis Superia. You have been chosen to undergo a titanic, worthwhile adventure of epic proportion… “I don’t want to go on an adventure! Wrestling was bad enough!!” I’m afraid you’ve got no choice, but you dare deny your own Goddess?? “My own Goddess?? Astrology doesn’t have –“ You shall see in time, Lunis Superia. Now, you must embark on a familiar journey, down a path you’ve treaded on before… “Say what? I’m not embarking on anything!! Can’t you just leave me alone?! Go pick on a junkie at the prison!!” You have no choice, Astronomer. You are my chosen Knight, and I shall assist you on your endeavor to spread The Divine throughout the world. “QUIT SPEAKING IN RIDDLES! WHAT’S THE DIVINE?! WHAT KNIGHT?” You follow the faith yourself. You’ve embarked on this quest before… but this time… you shall succeed… “Wha-“ Unfortunately he has no chance to finish. With a burst like a supernova, the spirit comes crashing through the mirror and soars into the air like a great dragon. Dorn shakes violently with fear and just stares as the spirit circles above his head, staring down at him. After circling him three times, the dragon soars through the ceiling, without breaking it or anything, and comes right back down towards Dorn. Dorn lets out a roar of terror and covers himself, and in a flash, the world he knows is gone… A rushing wave of flames flashes before him and he finds himself in some great black void, floating in mid-air. He uncovers his himself and gasps in surprise, turning in all directions. “What the…WHERE AM I?!” And like a great, pounding bell, the familiar voice of the spirit reverberates all around the void. PREPARE YOURSELF, DORN TRACER! Suddenly, the spirit appears out of nowhere in front of Dorn, in the form of a great, golden, glowing dragon. Its mighty wings flap and slash through the blackness like its nothing. Thick, blue pointed spines trail down its muscular, meaty back. Its titanic head roars so forcefully that Dorn is sent flying backwards through the void. The spirit’s humungous body levels itself and flies after the poor, defenseless Astronomer. The dragon opens its great mouth, sending huge, spiraling forms of anti-matter slashing through the blackness. Having recovered, Dorn can only watch as the dragon comes closer and closer, about to swallow him whole… BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! “AH!!” The dream instantly ends and Dorn shoots to a sitting position, surprisingly in his bed. His face is terrified and sweaty, his chest heaving with fright. He throws his ratty blanket off his body and sits on the edge of the bed. Dorn closes his eyes and shakes his aching head, trying to make sense of what happen. He raises his right hand to his forehead, and to his surprise… “Huh? AH! My hand!” Dorn’s eyes shoot open and he finds himself face-to-palm… with a leather-gloved hand. The glove is black and the wrist of it is lined with shining steel. He gasps and brings his other hand up, and guess what, same exact thing! He leaps out of bed and hears a mysterious CLONK, as if he’s wearing boots. He looks down, and… ”Wow, what a freakin’ surprise…” He’s wearing black leather boots, also steel-lined. More to his surprise than that could ever be, he’s wearing strange armor-like greaves. They’re black with silver armor over the front of the bottom part of his legs and over the front of the upper part of his leg. The same format is lies on the other leg, and to complete the outfit, he also seems to be wearing armor on his torso. The armor is sleeves with silver, rounded shoulder pads meeting in the middle of his torso at a point. “Wow… one minute I’m naked in my shower, next I’m wearing armor. What in the name of the Sun is going on?! My head hurts so badly…” Dorn groans, clasping his forehead with his cool, gloved hand. “This is going to be one VERY strange day…” He stands up and stretches out, trying to get his joints to pop. Not really wanting to wear the armor anyway, he tries to pull the torso part off, but to no avail. He then tries to take the gloves off, but again, they don’t come off. Thoroughly frustrated, he raises his fists in the air and throws them down just as quick. “WHAT IS THIS?! I’ve forever stuck in a tacky suit of armor!! I’m not even going to be able to take a piss! What a sad state of affairs…” Dorn sighs and turns around, only to be greeted by a black leather trench coat hanging on the wall and a book on the stool by his bed. The book has a small, yellow piece of paper sticking out of it, like a bookmark. He stares at the jacket for a moment with a puzzled look on his face, trying to decide just why its there. “That wasn’t there yesterday…” Dorn says to himself, almost like he’s trying to convince himself that it’s not really there. “This is insane, I need to see a shrink or something. I think I’m going crazy from an over indulgence of Chinese food…” The Astronomer then looks down at the book and picks it up. It’s thick, with a dusty brown cover with no words on it at all. He removes the yellow piece of paper from the middle of the book and sets the large, dusty thing done. He stares at the piece of paper for a moment before EVER so slowly unfolding it… his eyes widen and his jaw slowly drops at what’s written… “Oh my stars… no, not again…” It says: Call PCW. [End]
