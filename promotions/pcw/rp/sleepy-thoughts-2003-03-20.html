Sleepy Thoughts

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/03 Lying in bed I tossed and turned. I wanted to sleep just like the others who abided inside this Chatham Massachusetts home, but my mind just wouldn’t let me. It was far too focused on the thoughts of many issues that had been plaguing my mind for nearly a week. A match with Lacuna on Friday, the constant threat of P.W.A. and the threat of losing the children I had been caring for since my brother’s death six months ago. It seemed like my life was going on a downward slope and there was nothing that could fix it. Nothing but thinking about it constantly and hoping that maybe if I thought about it hard enough the thoughts would get too intense and my mind would finally explode and I’d forget all my cares, but I knew that wish was far too good to come true so instead I looked over at the digital clock which read 10:00 in glowing red numbers and hoped that sometime within an hour I would fall asleep, but until then I would just let my mind wear me out. …Ten o’ clock and you are still awake. Why are you still up Tessa, you need sleep!.... …Then again who could possibly sleep knowing P.W.A. is out to make their life a living hell… …I wonder what they have plotted for Chris and I next….. …Hopefully it’s nothing too bad… …Speaking of bad, last time I heard of Lacuna and Jade they wanted revenge on Chris for some heated words he had said to them. I hope they don’t take this out on me… …Then again if I really loved Chris wouldn’t I take any kind of beating for him… …But if I really love him then why didn’t we just run off and get married somewhere else since P.W.A. ruined what was supposed to be a perfect wedding… …Hmmm, I’m sure we’ll think of someway to be together. Until then I should just worry about Lacuna… …Oh yeah, and P.W.A. Last thing I need is yet another ass kicking from them… …But why do they want to kick our asses so bad it’s not like they are proving anything in doing so? The stipulations been thrown out and the tag titles around Chris and my waist are not going to be theirs anytime soon, why don’t they just realize that and give up?... …Then again who am I to talk about giving up? I never give up myself so I guess I’m just as guilty… …Ah well I guess I’ll just have to wait to see what happens Friday… With that final thought my eyes close and finally I find myself in a peaceful world of sleep, but one can only wonder how long will this peace last? Probably not long with people like P.W.A. around.
