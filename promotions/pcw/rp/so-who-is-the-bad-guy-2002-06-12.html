So who is the bad guy?

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/02 -A bus is parked on the side of a road in the middle of a cornfield somewhere in central Iowa at 4:30am. The bus is decorated with the colors of the Malice Crew in yellow and black. A PCW logo stands out on the back of the Bus along with a picture of Duke Williams decorating the side. This of coarse is the infamous Malice Express. After September 11th Duke has not flow very much. About a half a mile into the field a figure is seen in the Iowa Dawn. He stands their with nothing more then a pair of sandals, long brown robe, shades, cowboy, and a golf club. Next to him is a bucket full of balls. The man is Duke Williams he is dropping balls and hitting them away with his club.- Williams: Ahhh nothing like slapping your balls in the Iowa Dawn. -Duke whacks a ball way up high and deep into the flat lands of the corn field. As he’s doing this he talks as the world listens. Williams: Mistakes? The world is full of them isn’t it. Hell your birth is the perfect example of that isn’t it Mr. Akiyama? ZING RIGHT OFF THE BAT HA HA HA HA!!! Good morning America how are ya?? Don’t you know I’m your Malice Man. I the one they call sin central. -Duke tips his hat to the camera before hitting another ball high and deep.- Williams: Sure Mr. Akiyama you can list my mistakes all you want. I can do the same of you though. Sure I can. It was a mistake for you to come back to the ring against me for one. Bang baby….bad move. The second mistake you made was an assumption. I guess we really are getting to know each other aren’t we killer? FORTE! -Another ball goes flying as Duke voice fades instead of echoing against the flat lands- Williams: You talked of a mortal taking on an immortal. You assumed I’m the mortal one. I’m I? -Duke smiles at the camera as the sun reflex off his chrome shades.- Williams: I also heard you speak of my flaws. My vices if you will. Women, weed, whiskey, a warped sense of violence, and of coarse Malice. Kid I’m just warming up. You really want to know how bad I can be? So I’m a good guy? You call your self a god damn it you should know these things. -Duke hits the ball a tad harder.- Williams: As I tried to explain to your simplistically overstated mind that PR is everything. How good of a man is Bill Clinton who is worshiped with in his party? Who cares about his principles cus he has personality. A little of this and a little of that and the world is your kid. First you have to gain the trust of the people. Once you have that trust you do not push your issues and agenda outright. No, you use slight of hand and subliminal trickery. Just ask the kind folks at Nike how they got the kids of America to kill for those half ass shoes. -Duke reaches into his pocket pulls out a flask of whiskey and gulps down a good drink. He winks to the camera when he’s finished.- Williams: So Mr. Akiyama am I a bad guy?? I don’t think so. I give the people what they want. I fight for their rights to be as bad as they want. Tell your boss to f*ck off!! Go ahead and drink that whiskey it won’t hurt you. F*ck her in the ass till it hurts, she’ll thank you for it when its all over. HA HA HA HA -Duke laughs as he hits another golf ball.- Williams: America just needs permission and I’m the guy to give them that. Nelson wants structure. Kannon wants power. Booker wants respect. What do I want? Admiration for giving the people what they need. Your right humans only live for a short while so why not live it up. F*ck till your unit turns green and falls off. Drink till your liver explodes. If you don’t…..guess what your still going to die. The Judaic scriptures, the Muslim writings, the Christian bible tells you to be good. What a f*cking joke isn’t it Mr. Akiyama. No matter how good you are you will still die. The God these people believe in is a f*cking bore. He may have kicked me out of the Promised Land but I’m f*cking up his little science experiment ain’t I? HUH?? HA HA HA HA Oh wee a little slight of hand. You see while your getting booed and ostracized…I’m influencing and changing the world all the while being called a hero. -Duke throws his hands in the air and shrugs his shoulders.- Williams: SO who has the dark gimmick as they call it in the wrestling community? Well that’s easy….you do. Everybody knows that. Damn your smart. We don’t see you coming a mile a way do we? You’re a regular f*cking Pearle Harbor aren’t you? Well just call me Mr. Nagasaki motherf*cker. And hoss you know how that turned out. -Duke hits the golf ball while yelling “bang”.- Williams: You talk about failure. You know I was talking to Jesus last night and he told me he’ll be coming back like he said. However we both agreed Mr. Akiyama’s career won’t. Him and I don’t agree very often so you can take stock in that killer. -Duke reaches into his pocket and pulls out a punch cigar and lights it up.- Williams: Hoss you talk about my words hurting me. Have they really? Do you want to bet on that? You’re a “god” what do you have to show. What have you accomplished? I f*cked four different Miss America’s!! HA HA HA -The smoke flows out of the cigar as Duke holds up four fingers and gyrates his hips at the same time.- Williams: Dark and deep don’t get you anywhere young hoss. Don’t your realize half of America isn’t literate enough to understand you?? That’s why I speak simple buckaroo. I understand my people and they understand me. What more do I need? -Duke pulls a cell phone out of his pocket (these must be some deep pockets).- Williams: HELLO??? GET MY lawyer on the phone and cancel my will!! Mr. Akiyama will not be sending me to the void. -Duke hangs up the phone and drops it back into his pocket and picks up his golf club.- Williams: I’m glad you cleared up the Void threat. I’ll sleep a lot better at night tonight. No really I was worried. I was. HA HA HA HA Give me a break. I’ve been in this business long enough to know bullsh*t when I hear it. You kid are full of it. Let me get my fishing boots out of the bus before you start talking. -Duke mimics like he’s walking though a river of runs.- Williams: Mr. Akiyama as you’ve observed I’m not one to shy away from words. Since nobody in the back will tell you like it is. Allow me. You’re a f*cking joke. Look at you!! Really…..look at you. Pathetic with a capital P f*king U. I ain’t lying hoss. That and you like to f*ck bunny rabbits. You heard me right. I didn’t say f*ck like a rabbit I said Mr. Akiyama likes to f*ck rabbits. -Duke gives him self bunny ears with his fingers as he gyrates his hips.- Williams: You said it your self Mr. Akiyama. When you don’t know the truth make it up!! You f*ck rabbits as a hobby. Do I need proof? I’m a respectable PCW journalist what more do you need. This will make the news tomorrow hoss. -Duke hits another ball deep.- Williams: So you too were a jock in high school huh? See we have something in common after all. I tell you what. After I kick your ass and send you whiskey bent and hell bound on Fusion. After the show I’ll take you out to Denney’s for some nice ice cream and apple pie. I told you I wasn’t such a bad guy!! See no hard feelings. Don’t you feel better already? -Duke hits another ball deep, the sun is really starting to come up now.- Williams: I know you think you’re that much smarter then me. So riddle me this batman. You asked your self why anybody would want to be a part of this flawed species? My reason is simple I’m getting back at the kid up stairs who kicked me out of his clubhouse. But aren’t you here interacting with this flawed species also? Are you not wasting your time breathing the same air as all my fans?? Doesn’t seem very logical to me. Maybe there is a motive I’m not seeing. Whatever the case my be your comments were very hypocritical. Don’t be hypocritical when dealing with the master of spin. I’m a journalist and I will pick you a part if I have to. -Duke hits another ball.- Williams: As you said Duke Williams…the essence of humanity…That I’m guilty of. The essence…of error…sure but sometimes the loss is just a part of strategy to win the over all war.So says the Divine Prophetess…yea?? F*ck them and f*ck you!! Just have your bags pack and be ready to be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND….HOSS!! -Duke grabs the rest of the balls and starts heading back to the bus as the camera fades on the Duke Williams picture on the side of the bus.-
