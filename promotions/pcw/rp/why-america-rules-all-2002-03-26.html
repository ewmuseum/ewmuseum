Why America Rules All

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Why America Rules All Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 26, 2002 Manifest destiny... In the minds of many historians, manifest destiny is the most powerful idea in the history of the United States. From the days of British rule, Americans have always wished to move onward, forward, and westward. With the aid of American ingenuity, an undeniable pioneer spirit, and brute force, America conquered a continent. Ingenuity came in the Louisiana Purchase. American diplomats talked Napoleon Bonaparte into selling the vast Lousiana Territory for three cents an acre. Pioneer spirit came in the frontier moving west. People packed up, intrepidly living in the Great Plains and the Far West, scrapping for every inch, seeking no quarter, and giving none back. Brute force? The conquering of the Indians and the Mexicans, the Spanish and the Filipinos, anyone who tried to stop our expansion got a can of Uncle Sam brand whoopass opened up on them. Manifest destiny.....it can be applied to wrestling as well. For some, it is simply their time to shine. The ecstasy of gold...combined with manifest destiny.... Such is what drives men to greatness. Such is what makes champions. Such is what drives.......Politically Incorrect. ::The Mojave Desert of Arizona. A barren, sandy wasteland. About the only things for miles are cacti and Gila monsters. Well, that, and a 1000 foot high mesa, rising above the majestic Mojave. And it is here, in the absolute middle of nowhere, that Politically Incorrect sit, with a lineup of four people standing near them.:: William S. Powell: Welcome to the Mojave Desert. As you heard our paid propaganda man in the introduction....we are manifest destiny embodied. And to prove it, we're going to run down a member of the race of each member of International Incident? And why? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, let's be honest here. America rules the asses of all other countries. Well, Japan's close, but lets be honest. Latin America and Europe both suck. And that's why we have this lineup of...'Both Men: PEOPLE FROM COUNTRIES INFERIOR TO THE UNITED STATES!! ::Powell strides over to the first man, a British individual, dressed in a redcoat uniform.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: First up, the British. Here we have a fine strapping young British soldier. At one time, this guy was a member of the most powerful army in the world. Although, probably also the dumbest army as well. I mean, these guys walked in a STRAIGHT FRIGGIN' LINE! HELLO! That has to be the WORST military strategy ever! And yet, the British army won battle after battle! Just goes to show you how much the rest of the world sucked for the British to be kings of the world like that... ::Reginald grins, before nonchalantly shoving the Redcoat over:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: And then it all went pants. The Americans and the French teamed up to kick some redcoat ass. Who's the British international incident member? Anton the Kid. William S. Powell: I thought he was Irish! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ireland used to be a part of the British Empire though! ::Powell simply sighs, dropping the subject, and moving onto the next man, this one of French decent:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: And now, we have ze French. After the Revolution, they got a tad pissed at us, and then they tried to take advantage of us in the XYZ affair, so our ships started fighting. We've bailed them out of almost every foreign war they've entered, except the Franco-Prussian War, in which Prussia handed Napoleon III his ass on a stick. And of course, this French guy corrolates to the Chef. Former PCW Champion, current Islamic psycho. And, a man who, if he interferes AGAIN, will be reduced to pate. You may think it was funny to run in and hit us with the cheapest heat-garnering finisher in wrestling history. Sure, it knocked my partner out for the count, but the TaliBANG? Come on...we kicked the crap out of the Taliban already. The hatred's been lessened over time. What's next? The Saddam Hussein Stunner?The Iraq Craq? The North Korean Nosedive? Next time Chef, this Friday, it's a gauntlet match. No II runins. And, under those rules, there is no one here who can stop us.....NO ONE. ::Lovecraft kicks the French guy squarely in the nutsack. As he doubles over, Powell steps forward, staring squarely into the eyes of a Guatemalan.:: William S. Powell: Now, I THINK we got this one right. If I remember correctly, Frisco IS Guatemalan, but I honestly can't tell all of those damned Latin American countries apart. That's mostly because pretty much all of the Latin American countries are sheep to us here in the U.S. Back in the 1900's, people complained that President Theodore Roosevelt had turned the Carribean Sea into a Yankee Lake. Well, for all intents and purposes, that's really quite a true statement. We practically own Central America. We own South America. As it is, President Bush is in Peru convincing the Peruvian leader to vote against Cuba's human rights abuses. The U.S.'s influence over Central and South America is absolutely astounding. It's been documented for years. We own the Western Hemisphere. Pretty much an accepted fact. Sorry guys. ::Powell smiles, swiftly knocking over the Guatemalan. Finally, Politically Incorrect come to an American...:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: And lastly, we have the American...who has moved elsewhere. It is the rare person who emigrates from America, but this person does so for several reasons. They do it because they have family. They do it for a woman they love. Or, lastly, they do it for greed. Men like Kevin Klaymore and Reeco Suave have turned their backs on America for the sake of furthering their careers. And quite honestly, that disgusts us. International Incident, we may not be the greatest people in the world. We may indeed represent what is to foreign eyes the worst America has to offer....but damnit, we're politicians. Along with the usual power trips, we get into politics, because goddamn it, we love this country. We pledge allegiance to our flag, and we damn sure mean it. William S. Powell: And so, Golden Game, Friday.....we WILL run the gauntlet! We WILL become the tag team champions. And, all you other teams, don't worry, we'll get to you soon enough. ::Fade to...:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically IncorrectThe Next Tag Team Champions of PCW. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
