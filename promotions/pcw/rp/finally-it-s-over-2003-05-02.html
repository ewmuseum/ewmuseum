Finally, it's over.

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-2-03 Many hours have passed since our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, and his companions, Molly, Mikey, Liz, and Jack, laid waste to the combination of Tia Leoni and Matt LeBlanc, better know as Tia/Matt. Their guide through this adventure, Mikey Rooney, has been neither seen nor heard from for quite a while. Okay, everyone caught up? Good. Let’s continue then. “Okay, Mikey, since you seen to know more about what’s going on here than the rest of us, where to now?” “Well, Dungeon Master… I mean Mickey Rooney, said to lure Viniger to the Dragon’s Graveyard. We should head there.” “And how are we going to lure him there?” “I’m not sure yet. I’ll figure that out on the way.” With that, the band of adventures begins their trek. For what seems like days, the party crosses the Desert of dry mouth, the Forest that can not be seen, because of the trees, and the Ocean of that looks a lot deeper than it really is. Finally, the Dragon’s Graveyard is within sight. Jack comes up with an idea. “Hey guys, if we’re going to be fighting tomorrow, perhaps we should make our camp here for the night so we’ll be refreshed for tomorrow.” The group stands in stunned silence. “What? It’s not like I don’t come up with ideas all the time.” “Yeah, but this is the first one that makes sense.” “Oh bologna. I’ve had good ideas before.” “Yeah, like the time you suggested we all sell our kidney’s on the black market?” “Hey I got good money for mine.” Stunned silence again. “Okay, I can’t believe these words are coming out of my mouth, but Jack has a point. I agree that we should stay here tonight.' The rest of the group nods in agreement and they begin preparations for making their camp. After getting tents pitched and a fire started, the group gathers around. “Wow, what luck that we happened by these tents.” “Yeah, it was almost like fate intervened to fill a plot hole.” The group all looks at the camera. “Hey, I know how to pass the time. Let’s tell ghost stories.” “Great idea. Who wants to start?” Mikey starts to speak. “Well, there was this one time…” The group listens intently until he begins to wrap the story up. “… and there I saw… his ass!” GASP!!! “Mikey, that was truly horrific.” “Yeah, Man. I could never survive that.” “I suddenly want to call my Mother.” With a bright flash of light and a large puff of smoke, two people stand in front of the group. One, an attractive woman with large white wings and the other, with flame red hair and an aura of annoyance begin to speak. “Need to make a call? Use 1-800-CALL-ATT.” “Don’t listen to that idiot. Use 1-800-COLLECT.” Without warning, the ‘angel’ hauls off and kicks the annoying man square in the crotch. He doubles over in pain. Unfinished, she grabs two handfuls of his red mane and bring his face down to meet her knee. With blood covering his face, the red-haired man falls to the ground, weeping. The angel dives on top of him and proceeds to pound his face with her fists. She then grabs him by the shirt collar and hits him with a vicious head butt. The man slumps down into the ground, unconscious. “Now what are you guys going to dial for all your collect calls?” The entire group shouts out with no hesitation. “1-800-COLLECT!!!” The angel stands up, brushes the dirt from her clothes, smearing blood at the same time. “Good choice. By the way, Viniger is at the Dragon’s Graveyard waiting for you.” And just as suddenly as she appeared, she vanishes. Completely ignoring the now dead body on the ground nearby. Molly breaks the silence. “Now that was odd.” “Odd? That’s an understatement.” “I agree, Dan. That really made no sense. Almost as if the fates were watching E! True Hollywood Story on Jim Varney and saw a phone commercial.” Again the group all looks at the camera. “Well regardless, let’s get some sleep. Now that our plot hole has been miraculously filled, we know Viniger will be waiting for us.” “Goodnight, Molly.” “Goodnight, Jack.” “Goodnight, Liz.” “Goodnight Dan.” “Goodnight Mikey.” “Goodnight Molly.” “Goodnight, Jack.” You see where this is going… “Goodnight, John-boy.” Sorry, couldn’t help it. The cool morning air is a refreshing wake up call as the group begins to rouse awake. A few minutes later the group gathers for breakfast (Please don’t question where they get food. Just go with it) then gathers up their weapons and heads towards the Dragon’s Graveyard for their epic battle. Upon their arrival, the witness their dismal surroundings. The ground is littered with bones, both large, presumably dragon, and many smaller ones, mostly humanoid. From behind one of the gargantuan skulls steps a large, cloaked figure. Dan is the first to notice him. “Viniger!” The man draws back the hood of his cloak to reveal his face. Dan seems to recognize him. “Wait a minute! I know you! You’re Stone Burns! I’m supposed to fight you this Friday. What are you doing here?” The man throws his head back and laughs. “Ha ha. You are mistaken, Mr. Osbourne. I am not this man of which you speak. His power pales in comparison to my own. Quite frankly, it pales to your as well.” “How do you know what kind of power we possess?” “That is my secret.” “Fine, whatever. Can we just get on with whipping your ass? As you apparently know, I have only a few hours until I have to whip Stone Burns on Fusion.” “I am afraid you will not be making your commitment this evening as you will not be leaving here alive. The Dragon’s Graveyard will soon be your HOME!” Viniger leaps through the air and the battle ensues. It was a great battle. You should’ve been there. Everybody jumping around and vanishing, and bashing things. Oh man it was great. But eventually, it all came down to Viniger and Dan. Dan won. (Don’t you just hate me? Hehe) So Dan won. Mickey Rooney showed back up, took the weapons back, forgot to say ‘Thank you’, and sent everyone home. Jack and Liz found themselves comfortably at home. Dan, Molly, and Mikey appeared the back of their Limo in front of the stadium. Ready for Fusion, they piled out of the car and headed inside. Will Dan be affected by his ordeal? For that matter, does he even remember it? Will he be able to beat Stone Burns in just a few hours? No. No. Easily. Burns is nothing more than a thug. He said it earlier. Enjoy the match.
