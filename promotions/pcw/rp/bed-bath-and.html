Bed, Bath, and...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: That day that&#039;s named after that alien movie... I forget which. (We once again zoom back to the hidden lair of THE Multiple Man, a place so secret that it appears on satellite imagery only about half of the time. The other half? Well… all they can see is the tarp. It’s late night, and trouble is brewing…) Tmm: “Where the hell is my towel?!” (Tmm digs deep within the closet of the lair’s living room. Pogo sticks, scattered plates of old medieval armor and month-old Tastycake pies are piled in heaps around the door.) Tmm: “Dammit, I’M the only one who does laundry here! Towels don’t just up and walk away!” (Across the room, the big screen Television set clicks on for no discernable reason, showing only a test pattern of green sine waves.) Tmm: “C’mon… I know I put a spare in here for emergencies… where is it?” (More items are tossed from the small storage space, a squeaky frog, sticks of dynamite, a pneumatic catapult, that holographic projector that almost never works right… SNAP!!!) Tmm: “Stupid bear trap…” (The metal jaws clatter to the floor as the TV silently changes stations to a channel normally filled with noisy static. This time instead of white noise, up on the screen is a dark haired, serious looking man dressed in a suit. The man stares out for all the world like he can see into the room…) Tmm: “Ugh! We’re going to the beach tomorrow and I haven’t a towel in the place! And the stores are all closed…” (The man in the TV gives a ghost of a smile… like he’s just heard his cue.) TV Man: “Imagine if you will, a store unlike any other. This store is not shown on any map, nor listed in any directory, yet it is very, very real to those that happen to wander through it’s doors. To step between these doors is to venture past that which is known to man. It is the middle ground between low price and high quality-- A store of beds. A store of baths. A store of… beyond. We invite you, if you dare, to step directly into...” (Tmm half turns around, mildly surprised to see the TV on.) Tmm: “Feh. Fat lot of good it does me, it’s past midnight!” (The man on the TV stops briefly before resuming.) TV Man: “Here, time has no meaning. All the clocks have stopped… mysteriously… at 2:35 PM. There is meaning to this, meaning behind meaning veiled in meaning. But all that is commonplace here, in…” Tmm: (muttering) “Eh… just get to the location.” (Another pause, almost as if the man was marshalling his thoughts, but only almost, for we all know that this is an impossibility…) TV Man: “It is often said that all roads lead to Rome, but if you happen to stumble upon the way, you might just find yourself inadvertently slipping through the cracks and passing through… Bed, Bath, and Beyond.” Tmm: (pleased) “Oh, I know where that is.” (He instantly turns and heads for the elevator leading to the lair’s underground parking garage, never once noticing the TV clicking off unassisted. VROOOM!!! Cough! Cough! Sputter! THE Multiple Mobile dives into the night.) Tmm: (muttering) “Towel, towel, towel… need to get me a towel…” (BA-BUMP! The tiny Volkswagen Jackrabbit shudders violently.) Tmm: (muttering) “Potholes…” (Minutes pass… and a faint scratching can be heard over the ‘sneakers in a dryer’ sounds of the motor. Tmm sees a brief flicker of motion from the corner of his eye...) Tmm: “The hell?!” (The hideous, goblin-like creature ducks back down below the window line, causing Tmm to crane his neck awkwardly in a vain attempt to see the thing again. More scritching noises…) Tmm: “So it’s like that, eh?” (Calmly, Tmm opens the glove compartment and withdraws a pistol… one with a disturbingly large bore. He cocks back the hammer with the smooth assurance borne of long familiarity.) Tmm: “C’mon you little bastard… I know you’re there…” (The shriek of tearing metal echoes through the night sky as the goblin-thing crawls upon the hood of the car and begins to tear away strips of metal from the hood. Tmm sights the gyrojet pistol through the glass.) Tmm: (softly) “Bang.” (The creature turns it’s bulging, weirdly glowing eyes at Tmm and hisses. WA-BOOOM!!! The propellant ignites in the hand portable rocket launcher and the armor piercing round punches a spiderweb through the safety glass like it was made of wet paper. The micro-rocket catches the spindly-armed thing in what would be the collarbone on a human. The force of the impact knocks the thing spinning off the front of the vehicle. BA-BUMP! BA-BUMP! Roadkill under the tires has a feel all its own.) Tmm: “Ahhh!!” (The air from the suddenly depressurizing cabin wails like a banshee as it gets sucked out through the freshly created hole! Tmm loses his grip on the pistol and fights to keep from being vacuumed out into space along with it!) Tmm: “Wait a minute!” (Desperately, Tmm cranks down the window. Almost instantly, the pressure equalizes and the danger passes.) Tmm: (muttering) “Keep forgetting that this is a car…” (The little car takes the ramp leading up to the massive Bed, Bath, and Beyond storefront, but the camera does not follow it. Rather, it stays at the rampway and does a slow turn, bringing the same serious looking man from the television commercial into view. Far behind him, we can see THE Multiple Man parking the car and walking into the shop.) Man: “Portrait of a man in desperate need of a towel. Sometimes the easiest things to find are the hardest things to get, and sometimes they’re just down the street. Keep your wits about you and you might just avoid ending up all wet, here… in Bed, Bath, and Beyond.” (Eerie percussion music as the camera points up towards the starry sky and fades out.)
