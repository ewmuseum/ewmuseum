Oh god, Kano is behind the wheel again

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12/2002 *The scene opens up on a wide strech of open interstate going from Louisiana to Texas. As the scene shifts to take in the emptyness of the interstate, a brand new, jet-black Nissan Altima comes flying past like a bat out of hell. The scene fades out and fades back in inside of the beautiful North American Car of the Year. And here is a big surprise, 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth' Jon Kano, is driving. Kano, in all of his Malice Crew attired glory, seems very relaxed driving at speed in excess of one hundred miles per hour.* Kano: Its amazing. I love this car. I mean I got one at home, but this rental car hauls ass with the little modifications I made. *Kano laughs.* Kano: I love the way driving relaxes me. I don't know why, but it just does. Nothing is more therapeutic than to take a nice car out for a spin. It lets you clear your head. But my head is not clear right now. I keep thinking about 'Controversial' Corey Williams and Semore Smoke. I get to step into the ring with those two poor excuses for human beings. Jesus, God had an off day when he created those two. As the late, great, Bill Hicks once said, 'I guess God shouldn't have smoked that joint on the third day.' God had to have been high to have created those two. First, Corey Williams, has the wrestling ability of a chimp, and the intellegence of a muskrat. It took him two pairs of handcuffs to my legs to the ropes to keep me from getting up to kick his ass. Corey knows, as well as everyone else, that he ain't shit. He keeps on coming trying to be the king of controversy, but you can't be controversial unless you mean what you say or do that causes that controversy. He wants to spout off and talk about how old I am and how I am over the hill. Motherf*cker, I am only three years older than him. He just can't stand to know in his heart that he will never get to the level I am as quickly as I got here. I started this Old School era. I am the man who began every era in PCW. I did something to cause a new era. What is Corey Williams' biggest claim to fame? He will be known as the Shadow Man. He has always lived in the shadow of someone his whole career here in PCW. He just doesn't have the in-ring talent nor the mic skills to stand on his own and shine. Now he will probably come back and say I play second fiddle to Duke in the Malice Crew, but that is a load of shit. Duke and I are partners who confide within each other our plans for the Malice Crew. Duke does his thing at the announce table and in the ring. I do my thing by making people realize why I am the man who rocketh the mosteth. The thing with Corey is he wants to be the man now, but as long as I am here, at the top of my game like I am right now, he will never, ever be the man. You see, Corey wants everything now. I worked damn hard to get where I am right now to let some little bastard who is about as over as Ned was. *Kano begins to slow down and pull over to the shoulder of the interstate, behind a stalled car. Kano gets out of the Altima and walks up to the car. Two beautiful girls are sitting in this car, a 1982 Chevette, in awe of the massive Kano.* Kano: Hi. Having a little trouble with your car? Blond girl: You, you, you, you, are, are, are, Jo, Jon, Jon, Kano!!!!!!!!!! Kano: Why yes, yes I am. Brunette girl: We are such huge fans of yours, Mr. Kano. Uh, hows your wife? Kano: She is good. Brunette girl: Do you love her? Kano: Of course I do. Listen, I am happily married. If I wasn't, maybe this conversation would go where you want it to go, but for now, pop your hood and I might be able to fix your car. *The two girls look a little disappointed, but pop the hood to the 1982 Chevy pile of shit known as a Chevette. Kano goes to the engine and you see Kano stick both arms in, fumble around a little, and uses the trademark Kano family method (heel-stomping the shit out of whatever is broken).* Kano: Fire it up!!!! *The car starts up with seemingly no effort. Kano shuts the hood, and walks back to his Altima.* Kano: Damn, I hope my wife appreciated that. Those two girls looked like they could suck the tin man through a key hole. Anyway, where was I? Verbally kicking Corey Williams' ass, ahhhh yes, onto Semore Smoke. Now there is a guy that makes the United States' drug policy make sense. This guy was born fucked up. I mean Jesus Christ of Nazareth, I don't think I have ever seen a man who is permanently high, well Hix. *Kano shudders at the very mention of the name of the disgraced former PCW World Champion.* Kano: Semore and I didn't have a problem with each other. He wanted to kick Corey's ass, I still want to kick Corey's ass after kicking it repeatedly. We had something in common. I liked him. I respected the fact that he wanted a piece of Corey, as did I. Then I hit him with a right hand, on accident. He got all pissy and decided he wanted a piece of me. That is a sign of inexperience. You see when you have been around for as long as I have been, you realize that accidents happen and you shake them off. He, however, went apeshit and decided he needed to attempt and get over on me. Well now he has signed his own death warrant. Walden wanted to make a name for himself at my expense, after I taught him everything he knows. He didn't want to wait for the course of events to run themselves as do Corey and Semore. And look at him now, he is living next to Big J in some homeless shelter, making his living by sucking off hobos for quarters. And why is Tim Walden where he is now? Why will the same thing happen to Semore and Corey? Because I showed him and I will show them why I am what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!! *Kano puts the Altima in gear and burns rubber leaving the shoulder of the interstate as the scene fades to black.*
