Hallo, Tarot!

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/11/02 The scene opens in a room decorated with red velvet curtains and candles of purple dripping wax. An aromatic scent of violets fills the room and the flickering flames give an eerie illumination. In the middle of the room is a circular table with a soft dark red cloth covering it. A simple wooden chair keeps it company and a small deck of cards sits on top of it. The trio are joined by a fourth - but this object is animated. Or more appropriately, RE-animated. Quiet footsteps take the man towards the chair, where he sits and exhales, causing the flames to dance wildly. He wears a white ruffled shirt beneath a dark red tuxedo jacket. A black bow tie sits neatly at his neck and pristine white gloves slip over his fingers. The flames of the scented candles reflect off his red circular glasses and light up his pale, dead skin. Slowly, he stretches out a hand towards the cards. More ruffles and frills emerge from his sleeves, making him look like a 19th century nobleman. He grips the deck in his right hand and begins to shuffle the cards. He then begins to talk in two distinct voices. One of them, heard the majority of the time. The other, the voice that projects a loud roar on rare occasions when Nerezza is more... Nerezza than usual. 'Why have you brought me here?' the quieter voice asks. 'Instinct needs guidance.' the growl comes out, speaking no more than necessary. 'Learn.' 'What have you to show me?' the inquisitive side gasps. 'LEARN.' the true vampire hisses. His breath makes the candles frenzy on their wicks. From the pack of cards, he takes one randomly. It's a picture of Gaz Green in a medieval Jester's outfit. He carries a small wooden puppet in his image. The voice growls inside Nerezza once again. 'The Fool. The starting point. The faith you have. You rely on this... HUMAN... faith can be your hopes and your weaknesses. In the end, you will depend on him. Make sure that you CAN...' Nerezza's hand removes another from the deck. The End's image has been painted on it, but he wears the robes of a sorcerer and a wave of colour streams from his hands. 'The Magician... why has The End been dressed as a conjuror?' enquires Nerezza to his less human side. 'Because he is the surgeon, the craftsman. He is full of initiative and he will not allow his weakness to be one. Do not doubt him. Now, remove the next one yourself...' he emits in a deep snarl. Doing as he is told, Nerezza selects another card. Cyclonica emerges, wearing outlandish attire that looks like some sort of voodoo ritualistic clothes. Her hands are outstretched towards the sky and her icy, cold stare has been replicated to the nearest detail. The lower section of the card reads out the description - The Priestess. 'She is the one that guides, although her intuition will cause her to be independent. Beware, for she is as you are. Not of this world, entirely. Do not get too close to her, Nerezza. I see you want her..' 'BOLLOCKS!' he shouts at himself. 'She's ... well, she's not bad but...' 'WE know the truth. You want the power she symbolizes. Don't try to bring human desires and lust into this. Draw the next card!' Grumbling, Nerezza takes one from the bottom of the pack. Ellie~! appears with a crown on her head and the same ditzy look as always. 'How appropriate. Another Queen that nobody cares about except the person she married' Wisecracks the vampire. 'That is The Empress. The is symbolic of creativity, as you have learned...' the hiss exclaims. Nerezza thinks back to the times that Ellie~! has found revenge for her ongoing receipt of Gaz and Nerezza's pranks. 'Then let me guess... if she is The Empress, then next to her on his own throne will be...' Nerezza smiles as he pulls the next one from the deck. 'King Kannon himself, The Emperor. So what's he all about?' 'Dominance and control. He has PCW by the throat. It's a grip that has not failed thus far and one that not even YOU have been able to weaken.' Nerezza objects to the statement with a growl of his own and removes another card. He smirks to himself as he removes it and stares at is with a smile that is accompanied by slanting eyebrows. A look of sick satisfaction spreads across his lips . 'The Hierophant... also known as Alexander Miyagawa.' Nerezza moves it towards the candle closest to him, ready to immolate the card but a force inside him yanks it backwards. 'DON'T!' the creature inside screeches. 'The Hierophant is the avatar of the teacher. He is a mentor and based in tradition. You should look at it to remember your roots and your basic knowledge.' 'I need no knowledge from that man... he is an example of why humanity will be saved by my hand or damned by your claw!' Nerezza says, drawing a new card. For some reason, it has a picture of the PCW Tag Team Championship Belts on it and is named The Lovers. Nerezza prepares to fly into a rage but the forces inside him prevent a transformation. 'What is this... who made this deck? That's the oldest, cheapest insult in wrestling history about Tag Team partners...' 'It shows your desires for the belts themselves. Don't judge in haste!' his other half interrupts. 'Or you will end up as HE has!' it shrieks, drawing a card using Nerezza's left hand. Skitzo is shown in a roman Chariot, with his face split down the middle, each half representing one side of the tragedy and comedy masks seen in theatre. 'The Chariot warns of an uncontrollable aspect, that which cannot be regulated or moderated.' 'Like you, perhaps? I don't even know your name... or why I was drawn to rename myself. What do I call you, anyway?' 'I am you and you are me. We are one and the same. If you need a name for me, then call me by the name inside yours.' 'The name inside mine?' 'Call me Ireul...' '...just get on with the card display.' Nerezza draws another card. On it's face is Akiyama, clad in his Void Armour. 'What's this one mean?' 'Strength. As in, internal strength. Akiyama is as physically strong as he is mentally. His knowledge of The Void manifests itself in that form the same way as your anger manifests as... well, me.' Turning the next card over, Nerezza's hand is taken over again by this mysterious other side. The flames on the candles seem to ignite even more. 'What is this?!' he cries, startled by the sudden burst. 'This is you, Nerezza.' the low voice whispers. On the card is Nerezza himself, a precise mirror. Nerezza holds a card to his face on the illustration with curious eyes. 'The hermit represents searching inside oneself, a questioning of the being. You ask too many questions for your own good, Nerezza. Accept what you are. You broke from both human and vampire authority but now you lack a guide. Just be what you are. No more of this ridiculous inner searching.' Suddenly, the Nerezza on the card leaps through the plastic lamination and hurtles towards the Nerezza in the room. He closes his eyes and raises his hands in front of his face to protect himself. Moments pass... and the adrenaline stops. He slowly parts his fingers to find himself inside his rented hotel room. His tuxedo is gone completely. He wears no more than a blanket and a shocked expression. Then something touches his back to and makes him jump out of bed with a yelp. Nothing behind him... then he realizes what it was... he reaches back and pulls one of his wings forwards, investigating the sharp spine on the tip. Sighing, the shocked vampire shakes his head and slumps back down into bed, yanking the blanket back over himself and closing his eyes again with hopes that other thoughts would slip between the molecules of his brain. * * * Later the next day, the tinkle of a shop's bell above the door invites the vampire inside. A quaint little curiosity shop adorned with various artifacts of presumably exotic origin surrounds him. Incense burns on the counter where an aging woman looks at him curiously. The situation reminds him of the time he bought an odd looking mask as a souvenir back in his NTICW days, where the store owner said that a horrific monster would take the mask from it's owner. How ironic... and perhaps prophetic... that Nerezza was the one to purchase the mask. He looks towards the woman with an inquisitive glare. She finally takes her nose from a book in front of her and glances up at him through thick spectacles. 'Can I help you?' she asks in a somewhat odd manner. 'Actually, I was looking for a set of Tarot Cards... ' he politely replies. The woman disappears behind a curtain and some loud rummaging can be heard fro behind. She emerges with a deck in her palm and sets it on the counter. 'Anything else?' the lady says, scratching behind her head and causing her earring to dangle to and fro. 'Nothing in particular.' he whispers as he takes the cards and fishes in his pocket for his wallet. 'Although, I'd appreciate if you could tell me if you've ever heard the name Ireul before.' The woman's lip begins to quiver the second she hears the name. 'You... know about Ireul?' she trembles, standing up. 'Well, I... I had a dream but' 'PLEASE! Take the cards and leave!!!' she cries, shrinking into the corner. Not one to be told twice, Nerezza turns and shrugs. The bell tinkles once more as Nerezza marches off down the street. He looks back to see the woman pulling down a shutter on the shop's door and rotating the sign to 'closed'. 'Bloody hell...' Nerezza mutters to himself. 'The way some people act, it's like I'm some kind of monster...'
