Death... the final frontier....

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens in yet another big city that Smoke has control of crime wise. The scene zooms back and you notice that the scene was actually that of a shot out of a window of a building. The camera turns and you see that the building is actually an airport. Security guards walk by the scene and you see several other people who are obviously travelers. The camera zooms in on a plane terminal and you see Semore Smoke step through the terminal with his bags in his hands. It’s obvious that he didn’t check any baggage in fear that it might get lost. Smoke turns away from the camera and looks ahead, looking for someone. But then when he notices that no one is there, he walks forward toward a sign that says “Baggage Claim” and is sure to have an exit close to it. Smoke walks pass a few people, who are conversing outside the Subway set-up eating subs as they chat. As you look at Smoke more and more, you notice that he’s wearing an all green suit, in fact, it’s a dark green suit that looks like green smoke in the way it sways as Smoke walks. Smoke turns the corner of a corridor and walks down a flight of steps. He sees the baggage claim right in front of him, and an exit close to it that leads to the parking lot. Smoke walks up to the window and takes a look outside, sitting right outside the door is a white 1996 Toyota Minivan. Smoke exits through the door and walks forward to the van with a smile on his face, the people in the van look to be about seventy or eighty years old and could very well be Smoke’s grandparents. Smoke walks up to the back of the van and slides open the back door. He climbs into the back seat and sits down behind the man and woman. Smoke smiles as he sits down and says... Semore Smoke: Thanks for coming to pick me up Grandpa. {{{{The old man turns around and smiles back at Smoke...}}}} Grandpa: It’s our pleasure, welcome back to North Carolina. {{{{The old man turns around and puts the Van in drive and drives around in the parking lot and heads toward the exit. As he drives, Smoke speaks...}}}} Semore Smoke: It’s great to be back in the south where there’s not half as much people and traffic. I thank you guys for picking me up, you know how I feel about renting cars and such. {{{{Smoke’s grandmother in the passenger seat nods her head in understanding...}}}} Grandma: Well, it’s nice to see you again, your mother would be proud of you for sure, how’s everything going with your dad’s old business? What was it exactly, a few nightclubs? {{{{Smoke chuckles at the question and then answers it...}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, that’s about it and it’s going well, I’m bringing in some new entertainment, and I’m expanding the nightclub chain into New York. I hope to have some in Raleigh, Wilson, Cary, and maybe even Rocky Mount here in North Carolina. {{{{Both grandparents nod their heads as the grandfather pulls the van into traffic headed out of the Airport parking lot. It’s now obvious that Smoke is at RDU International Airport in Raleigh, North Carolina and is there to visit the reminisce of his family. As the van merges into traffic, Smoke turns to look at his grandmother and asks...}}}} Semore Smoke: Where exactly are we going tonight to eat? {{{{The grandmother turns around with a grin on her face...}}}} Grandma: I’ve got a home cooked meal already prepared at the house, when we get there you can eat your fill. {{{{Smoke nods his head, giving a unique expression of understanding. The grandfather driving pulls the van into the right lane and lines up to get off at the next exit and head home. As he merges over into that traffic, another car there speeds up and the van slams into it. Both vehicles spin out of control and the van slams head first into a tree. The van begins to smoke and it’s obvious that it all happened in a matter of seconds. Smoke is still conscience in the van but is trapped in between the two front seats and his back seat, he coughs a little but seems to be ok, he looks up to where his grandparents are and asks in a low voice, almost a whisper...}}}} Semore Smoke: Are you two ok? {{{{No answer is heard from the two, and Smoke then sits up as much as he can and notices that the two of them had slammed face first hard into the windshield and had died on impact. Smoke shakes his head and a tear from his eyes and then tries to climb out from in between the seats. As he climbs out though, he sees people climbing out of the car that caused the wreck, and other people stop by. But these people aren’t here to help, but the crash was a set-up and planned. Smoke looks around desperately for an exit as he sees men walk up to the van with cans of gasoline in their hands. Smoke tries his hardest to push the seats forward or push his back, to try and make an escape, but they won’t budge. He then reaches down for the lever that reclines the entire back seat, and finds it, he then sits it back and gets enough room to roll out of the pinned in area he was in. But it’s too late, the guys are now dumping the gas on the van and one is holding a match in his hand. Smoke can’t make their faces, but notices that one of them looks to be big and strong, aside from the other smaller figures and he seems to be the leader. The man with the match drops the match onto the van and the gas ignites swiftly into an inferno ball. As it envelopes the van Smoke screams in horror, the scene then changes to the outside of the van and you see it enveloped in fire with no exit, and the fire is heating up the gas tank of the van as well. All five or six of the men around the van run away and get in their own vehicles and drive off speedily. Smoke is scene through the window banging on the glass, trying to get out, but then the van explodes! And Smoke wakes up in a puddle of his own sweat in his bed at home in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Smoke breathes heavily as he sits up and cuts on the lamp beside his bed, he looks over to see a new woman in his bed and shakes his head. He stares at the mirror thinking about the dream, he then speaks out loud...}}}} Semore Smoke: That’s not a good sign... I just visualized my own fiery death... {{{{The scene fades as Smoke stares at the mirror with a look of shock and horror on what he just dreamed...}}}}
