Date: June 5, 2002 Episode One Suzie's Flower Shop 6:30 pm It was near closing time in the little flower shop, which was cutely placed in between an auto repair shop and an adult movie store. It was fairly ironic that something as pure and innocent as a flower shop could be placed between two testosterone filled, man only type stores. The sky was beginning to darken, foreshadowing a possible storm that was impending and going to crash upon the fair city. It was not too uncommon for storms to be forming at this time of the year, and many people were prepared for such freak weather. The local news had predicted incredibly beautiful skies for the next few days - well, screw that. Small droplets of water had already begun to fall to the sky, peltering the ground with their spear-like drops. They splashed on the ground, making sharp pitter patter sounds as they arrived. The shop itself was a cute hue of pink, with a large sign at the top of the door reading, 'Suzie's Flower Shop.' The words were emblazed in yellow, with little flower symbols enveloping the words itself. The shop looked like a club for middle aged women, who wore ankle length skirts and believed in abstinence until the age of 45. The lawn in front of the store was a beautiful green, and was like an ocean of vegetation for the entire sidewalked. It ominously shadowed and contrasted with the endless amounts of concrete which seemed to harken from the auto shop or pornography store. Heading for the door, there was a very pretty little rose which hung from the handle. Although on most days, some villan would steal the rose to use for their own purposes, today some unknown force had protected the flower and allowed it to survive outside without being stolen all day. The remainder of the sidewalk was oddly silent, only filling up with a few people rushing to their cars or to some other form of transportation to escape the pending rain. Suddenly, a midnight black Cadillac pulled up to the shop's store front. The engine hummed to a stand still, and a man emerged from the car. He was about five foot eight, with dark black hair, and a neatly trimmed moustache. He had a pale white skin complexion, and it appeared that he wore nothing but leather from head to toe. A leather jacket, leather pants, leather shoes...the only non leather article of clothing that he wore was the white t-shirt underneath the jacket which read, 'Change Blows.' The man's hair was slicked back rather hard, and it looked like it had been forced down using a ton of hair spray. The man took out a cigarette and lit it, puffing out a bit of smoke and locking up his car. He turned towards the shop, taking his sweet time heading for the door. He looked at the store hours sign, which said that they closed at six o'clock pm today. He checked his watch, seeing that it was already half an hour past closing, but he didn't care very much. He was preparing to smash the door open, when he decided to at least try the door handle first. Much to his own surprise, the door opened. 'Stupid ass people,' The man said laughing. He found it funny that with all fo the security people had today, something as trivial as not locking your door still occured. The man swung the door open, immediately being hit by a wave of perfumes. It smelled like the perfume section at any department store around the country, only the scent was multiplied and compounded by over a million times. The man left the door open a crack, while he headed for the leafy green plants. The leafiest plants were always kept in the back, and in the back was where we found our 'Breaking and entering friend.' He took a stem of leaves, and stuffed them in his mouth, chewing them down. Once the stem was clean of leaves, he moved on to the next, not really enjoying the bitter taste that swallowing and bititng into stems gave. Just as he was enjoying yet another leaf, a small, old woman appeared from the corner. She had on a flowery apron, and was carrying a box of small magnolias. She was rather old - maybe fifty or sixty years old, with bright, white hair and had a slow movement about her. As she bent down to put the flowers on the shelf, she slowly turned her head to see the man, chomping down on leaves. 'Excuse me, sir...' She started to say slowly. 'But we are closed right now...' The man finished chewing the leaves that were in his mouth, and threw the stem over his shoulder. 'And who, may I ask, are you?' The man had an amused look on his face, like the answer didn't really matter to him. That's when his eye caught a sign near the purchasing table. 'Oh wait...let me guess! You must be Suzie, right?' Suzie nodded slowly, still not taking her eyes off of the strange man who had a taste for plants. 'Haha, that's fucking awesome!' The man said, pumping his fist. 'I must say, you have a wonderful selection of vegetation here ma'am...' 'Thank....you...' Suzie replied, trying to break free of her stare. '...would you like to purchase the plants that you just...consumed?' 'You know, I'd rather not,' The man said, rubbing his stomach. 'I mean, I don't appreciate having to pay for plants that really should be out in the nature where everyone can enjoy them. I mean, these all should be free.' Suzie got up from where she was crouching, and put her flower bed down. 'I'm sorry, but I'm going to have to call the police. Please just wait there...' The man got a puzzled look on his face as he watched Suzie make her way to the cashiering table, where there was a telephone at her disposal. He walked over to her as she had her hand on the phone dialing 911, and placed his hand on the hang up button. 'What are you doing? You can't call the police...' 'I...I...I...can't?' Suzie replied, stuttering and terrified. She wasn't even blinking - her eyes were locked on the man's eyes. 'No, you can't.' Suddenly, Suzie grabbed something from underneath the counter, revealing a twelve round shotgun. The man stepped back in surprise, not expecting to see a deadly weapon being shown to him in front of his face. He put up his hands, as if he was trying to surrender or give up. 'Now, just put that down before you hurt yourself...' 'Shut up, scum bag!' She exclaimed, pumping the shotgun. 'Now, who's the scary bad ass now?' 'Well, at the moment you are,' The man replied, shrugging. 'Then again, you do have a deadly weapon in your hands. Might I suggest something?' The man's eyes began to flutter, and suddenly they were completely yellow, and his tongue flickered in and out like a snake's tongue. Suzie screamed, firing the shotgun. The man flew up in the air, avoiding the shot, and landed behind Suzie, taking control of the gun. He grabbed it from her arms, and crushed it against his knee. 'Now, who's the bad ass?' * * * * * * 'Hey Sour Puss, you gotta check this out!' Agent Sour Puss sighed for what seemed like the thirteenth time that day, as he watched Agent Chopsticks staring into the window of the porno shop. He looked up at the store's sign which read, 'Hardcore Movies, Magazines, and Assorted Goodies!' There was no name...well, one could suppose that that was the name of the store. It was honest, if not terribly creative. To say the least, it summed up everything that the shop was about in six or less words. All of the windows were blacked out, except for a small stand of magazines which they kept at the store front window. They were fairly revealing, which was a shock to Agent Sour Puss, who was certain that the city would soon cite the store for such a showing. He was a little surprised that someone hadn't already ratted the store out, considering it was only five minutes away from a local church and ten minutes away from a school. If not, perverts like Agent Chopsticks would be perverse enough to stare into the window. 'Close your mouth Chopsticks,' Sour Puss advised, 'you're drooling all over the pavement.' Chopsticks didn't look back, only admiring whatever he was looking at in the store front window. 'Huh?' Sour Puss sighed once more. If you're keeping count at home, that was number fourteen. Agent Chopsticks was a Korean american. He was aboout five foot six, medium build, with a light tan. His hair was slicked back, but it rarely ever stayed that way. It had done a complete reverse, flowing back up and sticking into the air, almost like spikes. He was sort of short, although average for your normal asian person. It wasn't a stereotype, it was the unfortunate truth, which even Chopsticks had admitted. He had only known Chopsticks for a few months now - he was a rookie. He had been assigned to him to teach him the ropes, and hopefully he would learn some things. 'I told you to close your mouth, Chopsticks. You're staring at pornography, not making love to a beautiful woman.' 'And how would you know?' Sour Puss got an amused look on his face. Sour Puss was a middle aged caucasian male, about five foot nine, with brown, but graying hair. He was not special in any attribute, except for his knowledge of the game. That is what kept him in the hunt. He realized Chopsticks' intellect, as well as his unbearable speed, but he could not stand the man's immaturity or lack of strength. Thankfully, those qualities could be replaced with others. Mental capacity could not. Of course, sexual prowess was always on a man's mind, never more often than in men that could never engage in such intercourse. It was blatantly, and cruelly banned by The Agency - they said it had too many consequences and offered far too many complications. Sour Puss obliged, although he used that as an excuse for the reason he was code named Sour Puss. 'Look, can we get back to business here?' Sour Puss asked, beginning to get annoyed. 'We're hear to catch the bug, not to stare at naked women.' 'Naked women?' Chopsticks said, looking back at the magazine. 'Who said anything about looking at women?' Sour Puss looked back at Chopsticks with a horrified look. He nearly drew his gun from the holster, blasting away his four month old partner for the mere thought of homosexuality. Oh no, he wasn't afraid of a gay man. Forbid that thought! In fact, he has seen enough alien and 'weird shit' life forms to make him accepting of many things in this life. But Chopsticks had said it in such a way that he made it seem like he was proud, and where pride went, confidence came. Confidence with Sour Puss was like mixing hot wax with your own skin - it hurt. 'Whoa, whoa!' Chopsticks said, throwing his hands in the air. 'Calm down, I was just joking. No need to pull out the 9mm bud.' 'You're luck I didn't shoot off one of your legs.' Chopsticks laughed, in that god awful high pitched voice of his. Sour Puss swore that his laugh was like a banchee's straight out of Ireland itself. 'You would kill me just because I was gay?' 'No, I would kill you because you never told me before and there was a chance that your sorry ass might come on to me.' 'Pssh!' Chopsticks said playfully. 'I would never look at another man that way. I mean, look at that fine woman in that picture. Do you think I would dump women for a guy like you?' Sour Puss looked back at the magazine, finally taking a glance at the photo. 'Yes, I'm sure she's very well rounded...' Chopsticks began to laugh hysterically. He fell to the ground, clutching his stomach. Sour Puss began to look around, noticing that people were staring over at the raving lunatics in the black suits. Sour Puss grabbed him by the arm, getting him to stand on his own two feet. 'That was a great joke, Puss...' He had tears in his eyes for laughing so hard, but Sour Puss was not amused. 'Do you always have to laugh like a lunatic when I tell a joke? Am I that hilarious to you?' 'Yes, you are.' Sour Puss shoved Chopsticks ahead of him, ignoring the urge to shoot him right in the back. 'Can't you see we're attracting attention? Don't you remember the first cardinal rule?' 'Never let people notice you...' Chopsticks replied, like he had repeated that phrase over a million times over. '...because people wouldn't understand what we are, or why there is even a need for suits.' Sour Puss said, finishing the sentence. 'Now, let's get back down to business, which is catching the bug.' 'That's what I don't understand, why are we chasing this bug anyways? I mean, he's only a level 3 Butterfly right? He can't really harm anyone.' 'That 'level 3 Butterfly,' as you so calmly put it, is one of the most dangerous butter cups in the country. Good ol' Sporty has been alluding us every since he made bail for that bank robbery he did a few years back. Now he's running around with an emerald crystal...' 'Remind me what an emerald crystal is...' Sour Puss rolled his eyes. 'Weren't you paying attention during the briefing?' 'Of course not, I was flirting with the other female suits.' 'The emerald crystal allows some of the bugs to reproduce, without having actually have contact with another or be on their own world for that matter. He's got it, and we need to stop him.' 'Isn't that his car up there?' Chopsticks asked, pointing over to a black cadillac that was parked in front of a flower shop. Suddenly, a loud shriek could be hard echoing from the flower store. Chopsticks and Sour Puss exchanged glances, and suddenly drew their guns, running for the flower shop door. Chopsticks lead the way, adrenaline pumping and head forward, while Sour Puss lagged behind. Although he wasn't as old as he thought he was, he still couldn't keep up with the fleet footed Chopsticks, who was ready to throw himself head first into danger. Another scream could be heard, and Chopsticks busted into the shop through the already opened door. As he came in, gun drawn, he saw Sporty holding an old woman over his head, ready to drop her. Chopsticks instructed Sporty to drop the woman and prepare to be taken into custody. 'Drop the woman?' Sporty asked, nodding his head back and forth. The woman shrieked again, obviously terrified. 'Would you stop nodding your head like that? You look like a damn mime. Just drop the lady!' 'If you say so...' Sporty threw the woman at Chopsticks, causing them both to spill to the floor. Sporty stuck his tongue at him, making a mocking laugh resound from his lungs. That's when Sour Puss arrived, firing off his weapon. Two bullets caught him in the chest, and Sporty staggered backwards. He ran for the window, but Sour Puss chased him down, tackling him before he cold take off. Sporty slammed his elbow into Sour Puss' face, causing him to hit the floor once more, but not before ripping open his leather jacket. The torn material fell to the ground, and Sporty looked on in horror. 'You ripped my brand new leather jacket, old man!' Sporty cried out. 'Tough shit, leaf brain...now give up...' Sour Puss said, still confident although he was the one laying on the ground. 'Let me think about that....uhm, NO!' Sporty suddenly took off, wings ripping away from the jacket, and suddenly flying off, crashing through a nearby window. Sour Puss sighed, number fifteen, as he realized that Sporty had gotten away again. Soon, Chopsticks had gotten back to his feet, and was helping Sour Puss up. They had lost Sporty... ...but what they didn't see was the emerald crystal, which now laying in a package of flowers that was to be sent to a San Francisco apartment the next day. Yes, the emerald was safely hidden away in the arms of several large stemmed roses, and were addressed to one Natalie Dischner.
