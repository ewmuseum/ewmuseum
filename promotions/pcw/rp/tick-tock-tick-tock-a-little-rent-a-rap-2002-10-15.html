Title: 'Tick Tock...Tick Tock'-A Little Rent-A-Rap Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 10-15-2002 Tick Tock Tick Tockby Rent-A-Hero.... Tick tock tick tock... All my Rent-heads are forzen with shock... Tonight I fight the Duke Williams, Hank Williams Jr.'s son, HOLY SHIZZNITTLEBAM.....that's not even the START of my fun.... Because after that, it's on to Friday Fusion... And the dreaded Black Friday Mall Brawl, PCW's tradition of mass destruction and confusion... And who do I have to thank for this SPECTACULAR schedule plannin... Everyone, give a hand to Hubbardville's biggest biznitch, Xavier Kannon.... See he's the evil villain at Red October who tried to kill me with his deadly Hallmark.... One on the floor...one through a stretcher....and the world went dark... But all the while when the world was dark, I counted the hours on the clock... Tick tock....tick tock...tick tock... But before Xavier Kannon, comes the 'Goth Cowboy' in the flesh.... Duke Williams is gonna wish he NEVER got into this mess... See he prides himself on the win/loss scoreboard...he's one and I'm nothing...But remember Gothy Tonk Hero....there's a first time for everything...What happened Duke, having an identity crisis? Those 12-year old Asian whores raised their prices? This Goth thing is a stretch for a Honky Tonk Hero... And let me remind you...your chances for taking my belt are the number of times Lil' Kim has said no to a lay....ZERO... How can you be President when you don't know if you want a ten gallon hat or studded earrings? Cowhide vest with leather chaps or frilly shirts with nipple piercings? Oh, speaking of history lessons you had to bring up the night when I debuted...you got the win... But did you forget it took more cheating then Bill Clinton with an intern for you to get a pin... I'm impressed by your selective memory you scary Goth man you... Your as scary as those other child rapists....MICHAEL JACKSON AND EVEN R.KELLY TOO! We fight tonight Duke, haven't you been watching the clock... Tick tock...tick tock...tick tock.... We will dance in the ring yet another time...Grrrrr, what can I find that rhymes with time? Oh well forget it, I'M BOOTY-WHOOPERING YOU TO THE MAX...Tell me, is there anything in PCW about a 'BIZNITCH SLAP O' DOOM' TAX? You want my prize...my gold...my love Duke, come ake it like a man.... But being a man requires testicles....so you've got a few hours to find them if you can... You better become the Duke you used to be... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
