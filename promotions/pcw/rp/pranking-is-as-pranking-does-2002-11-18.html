Pranking Is As Pranking Does

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18-11-02 The anxieties and suspense is high as Alliances 2K2 rolls around the corner in just over a week. The main event is filled with the top talent…and the lowest form of talent from all walks of wrestling, one team of which is THE team…the one…the only… TEAM BASTARDOS!!! Nerezza, Gaz Green, Skitzo and Vilas Lavagna have combined their mental attitudes and skills to create a force greater than a Firesnatch orgasm. P.W.A., Mr. Psycho and Vilas together make the team that will hopefully walk out of Alliances the victors, but to do so they must be at peace with each other, get to know each other…think like each other…unfortunately Skitzo happened on this fine day to choose P.W.A.’s Gaz “The Jester” Green…………… ---- Skitzo: WALKIN’ DOWN THE STREET, SMOKIN’ JOOOBBERS, SIPPIN’ ON MY GATORADE!!! Skitzo: Laiiiid back! Skitzo & Skitzo: WITH MY MIND ON BASTARDOS AND BASTARDOS ON MY-… Gaz Green: EASY TIGER! Team Bastardos is many things, but it is NOT a barbershop quartet! Strolling down a little side-street minding their own business, tag champ Gaz Green and The Mental One, Skitzo gaze over the people doing their own thing, while all around them the buzzing and groaning sounds of technology work away. Stopping before a junction, the two men slouch against a shop wall, Skitzo sipping away on his cool, delicious Gatorade while Gaz runs a hand through his slick head of hair, winking at a nearby squaw who walks off giggling at the Prince of Prankage. Gaz turns to Skitzo glumly. Gaz Green: Man, that was some fine ass, but no girl is gonna look twice when they see me standing next to Steven Hawking substitute.... Straw Boy… Skitzo: Well 'scuse me Mr. Universe, if you be looking for a quick screw off the streets then I regard myself as your saviour! Skitzo: She coulda had AIDs… Skitzo: Or cancer Skitzo...OR A PENIS! Gaz Green: Great deduction there! If she had aids then the best thing she could've done is to sleep with you and cleanse this god forsaken world of your stupidity or better yet slapped you to death with said penis, I CAN SEE THE HEADLINES NOW?!? Skitzo’s jaw drops at The Jester’s daring comment, and a look of flustered anger graces his face. Clutching at his Gatorade, Skitzo sighs and takes a deep breath. Skitzo: Y’know I thought to myself, Skitzo, why don’tchu take out your stablemate and treat him, get to know him, so we got each other’s backs at the Pay-Per-View, but chu ain’t makin’ it very easy for me, comrade… Gaz Green: Ok - Hey, I'm an animal lover and like animals I have to adapt so I tell you what, “PAL”, how about we start again? Gaz extends his hand with a sly grin on his face, to wish Skitzo replies with a boisterous handshake, only to suddenly scream out and pull his hand away to reveal Gaz wearing his trademark buzzer! Smoke rises up from Skitzo’s head, and he moans, shaking the excess volts from his body. Looking at Gaz, who is giggling quietly to himself, Skitzo manages a very small smile, and cocks his eyebrow. Skitzo: Be that so, Gaz? Check dis out then… With that, Skitzo sups the last of his Gatorade, and shaking the empty can, turns from Gaz and begins fiddling with it out of The Jester’s sight. Turning back to Gaz, he holds the can up, and Gaz examines it, noticing the opening has been fused shut again, and the ring pull bent back to its original position. Scratching his head in confusion, Gaz watches as Skitzo lifts a finger to his lips, then points to an elderly lady sitting on a nearby bench. Gaz smiles and watches as Skitzo skips silently towards the old woman…and planks himself down next to her rather heavily saturated body, covered in sweat as the sun beats down. Skitzo: Nice weather we’re having ma’am, wouldn’t ya say? Old Woman: Oh son, for a winter fast approaching this suns beatin’ down like Stone Cold on Debra! Skitzo: Who did what now…? Skitzo: NEVER MIND! Old Woman: Oh…k… Skitzo: Say ma’am I tell you the reason I be here is see I thought thinking to myself you be lookin’ mighty warm here, I was just wondering if you’d like a nice refreshing can of Gatorade to quench your thirst? The old woman takes the can from Skitzo and looks it over, before a smile appears across the wrinkled old wenches’ face. Skitzo gets up and nods before skipping back off to Gaz, waiting with a look of confusion still strewn across his face. Skitzo continues to walk past him as the old woman opens the can, and lifts it to her mouth…but stops halfway and looks into the can. Skitzo: I’d duck if I were you… The second Skitzo finishes his sentence, the old woman screams out as the can explodes in her face!!! A thick cloud of black smoke envelops her, which as it rises up and up into the atmosphere reveals the old woman, covered from head to foot in tar and feathers! The can still held in her hand, exploded open. Gaz’s eyes open in horror as he gazes over the fiendish prank. Turning to Skitzo, the PCW tag champ points in all directions wildly, while blurting out nonsense. Gaz Green: HOW THE-…WHAT-…HOW-…YOU-………………HOW IN THE GREAT GOOGLY MOOGLY DID YOU DO THAT?!??!!?!? Skitzo: The tar and feathers………………elementary my dear-… Gaz Green: Don’t pull that Arthur Conan Doyle shyte with me!!! I’ll-…you-…GAH!!!!!!!!! That does it!!! With that, Gaz reaches into his bag of tricks, and pushes Skitzo aside, heading for a nearby manhole. Kneeling down over it, The Emerald Menace begins fiddling around with the cover, out of Skitzo’s view, watching for people passing by. Eventually Gaz stands up and claps his hands together. Gaz Green: Beautiful! Gaz steps back and leans against the shop wall casually, as he turns back to Skitzo he notices Mr. Psycho has a bottle of Gatorade this time, drinking away at it as though it were going out of fashion… Gaz Green: Y’know if you drink enough of that it dyes your stomach?! Skitzo: Gatorade provides a healthy, delicious and refreshing alternative to the paperweight drinks of the corporate world! Gatorade is simply worth every cent! Mmmm…Gatorade! Skitzo: Can you tell I’m going to OWN the Alliances Alamo Match yet? Gaz Green: Based on the fact Nerezza shoved a can of Rezz Bull up your jacksie, I’d say you’re at least five whoopin’s from the top boy!! Skitzo: I’ll show you…what, do you really think Xavier Kannon is going to let some shitty little concoction like Pepsi or Dr. Pepper or Root Beer be the official drink for PCW??? Gaz Green: We-… Skitzo: EXACTLY!!! There can only be one-… Gaz Green: The Highlander? Skitzo: DRINK!!! Skitzo: There’s a lot of hot contenders in this match, but ya’ll gotta understand Gatorade is THE drink! I might just be hyping up for a big fall, but dammit Gaz… Suddenly the Star Spangled Banner begins to play ominously in the background. Skitzo: Gatorade is a drink for the people! A drink for the nation! A DRINK FOR THE WORLD!!! If there’s a thirsty child somewhere, he shalt sup of the Gatorade! If there is a wounded soldier on the battlefield in need of healing, he too shalt sup of the Gatorade! If there are a disease-stricken people in need of comfort, THEY SHALT SUP OF THE GATORADE!!! If Jesus Christ himself returns to us in need of a refreshing alternative to that crappy wine…HE SHALT-… Gaz Green: GETOUTOFTHEFUCKINGWAY!!!!!!!!!!!!! Suddenly, Gaz grabs Skitzo and pulls him to the ground as the same elderly woman, tarred and feathered and looking for help steps onto the manhole that Gaz had rigged. Skitzo waits for her to fall through the faulty cover, but instead Gaz’s twist on the prank proves to be even more fatal as the manhole explodes with fireworks and rockets and zooms high up into the air, before flipping out of the way and sending the old woman crashing back down, and through the manhole into the sewers below! Skitzo: ……………………Fucking intense… Skitzo: I see how you’re gonna play me, cracka! A’ight, game on! Skitzo looks around for something to corrupt, his line of fire passing through a hot dog vendor, a gang of youths, a lone horse and cart, a huge billboard with a bra advert on it, a sex shop, a street-… Skitzo: A HORSE!!! EUREKA!!! Skitzo glances at the horse, but before he can move to pull a prank on it or its owner, he glances over to the sex shop to see a certain low-cost highly-valuing-of-his-virginity guy walking in… Gaz Green: This isn’t good squire…not good at all… Skitzo freezes, watching as Byron Calver whisks his way into the pornography outlet. As soon as the door closes, Skitzo zips across the street and fiddles around with the cart, detaching it from the horse, and guiding the old mare across to Gaz. Skitzo: You’ll have to help me on this one, boyo… Gaz Green: How is it possible to bring a big old Clydesdale like that across a busy road with no mention of traffic?!?!?!? Skitzo: Elementary my dear-… Gaz Green: OK SHUT UP, what have you planned? Skitzo moves closer to Gaz, in the non-Boca way and whispers his plan to the Emerald Menace. All the while the Clydesdale looks on dozily as Skitzo and Gaz Green put their plan to action, whilst Byron looks at porn inside the shop. About ten minutes later, the shopkeeper kicks Byron’s horny ass out of the shop, and as the Viscount of Virginity turns to leave, with a pack of nudey cards hiding in his pocket for “playing” with later, he hears a familiar voice… Voice: Uhhhhh…hey Byron, uh-hu! You wanna, like, y’know, come do some drugs and get fucked? C’MERE BIG BOY!!! Byron turns around to see the face of his one true love. Staring back at him, through a thick mane of red hair is Firesnatch! Byron runs across the road, dodging cars and people and leaping the final few feet to embrace the wonderful wench. Wrapping his arms around her, Byron hears the voice again. Firesnatch: Kiss me, stud! Byron can’t refuse. He puckers up and plants a long, steamy one to Firesnatch, only to retract in horror and look closely at her. Cocking his fair little head, he begins to actually peel away the red spray paint serving as hair, which is actually a tail, and sees the lips for what they are……………….a horses arse… Byron looks down the street to see Skitzo and Gaz Green sprinting off into the distance, laughing their fucking Asses off. Byron: DAMN YOU SKITZO!!! DAMN YOOOOOOOOOOUUUUUUUUUUUUU!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Skitzo: Alliances is going to rock the fucking socks off of everyone!!! Gaz Green: Just keep running, lack of sex has resulted in Byron’s testicles growing to a large size so he can’t catch up with uuuuuus… Skitzo: WHERE’D MY GATORADE GO?!?!?!?! The horse turns around and makes a cringing expression as the bottle of Gatorade suddenly flies out of its ass in full force and knocks Byron to the ground…as this scene finally and thankfully fades to black!
