HEY!!! That's my....

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-25-02 HEY!!! That's my car!!! Ricky rushes towards the men who are hooking his car to the back of a tow truck. They pull away from the curb as one of the men yell out for him to contact the police impound. Ricky dejectedly kicks a rock across the ground and looks around for a cab. He sees one coming his way and as he hails it down a guy rushes in front of him and jumps in. HEY!!! That's my cab!!! Ricky watches as once again he is left standing on the sidewalk without a ride. The yellow cab disappears into the distance as Ricky decides to make his way across the street. Once across he notices a travel agency, he decides to step in for a brief second just out of curiosity. As he enters the building he makes his way across the waiting room towards a chair in the corner. An old man exits the restroom and takes a seat in the same chair. The last chair. HEY!!! That's my chair!!! Ricky takes a deep breath and flops down in the floor. Right in the center of the waiting room. He lies back and spreads his arms before letting out a yell that destroys the silence of the waiting room. Everyone in the room jerks to attention as a man leans over the counter and spots Ricky lying in the floor. He rushes around the counter and kneels at Ricky's side. Are you ok sir? I will be. As soon as this day comes to an end. As soon as I can get a flight to Florida. As soon as I can get away from myself. As soon as everything, again becomes....everything. Ricky exhales a huge sigh as the man gets to his feet and steps behind the counter again and motions for Ricky to follow him. He slowly gets to his feet and follows the guy back. They enter a small cubicle in the far corner of the office. The guy takes a seat at the desk while offering Ricky a seat in the chair on the other side. Ricky takes the seat and places his elbows on his knees resting his head on his hands. Been a rough day? Been a strange day is all. I know thing swill be better once I get on my way. I can then relax and not worry about nothing except my upcoming match. Match? What kind of match? Wrestling. I work for PCW. Never been a fan of wrestling, sorry. I thought you looked more like a soccer player. Actually I never cared much for that. Odd eh? Ok, here is what we've got. We have a flight leaving non-stop flight to Miami, in roughly 2 hours. Or we have another flight to Key West leaving in 3 hours. Which would you prefer? Key West sounds like a plan. I'll go ahead and book that for first class, is that ok? Even better Will you be needing accomadations upon arriving? Rental car, hotel, anything? No. I will be catching a ride to Montego Bay via cruiseliner once I arrive. Ahh, now THAT is a vacation. Not for me. This is a title shot for me. WrestleVessel. PCW's pay per view. I will be facing off against a couple guys I used to know from back in CCW then, some tramp named Jaden and some midget named Syko. Though, aside from him being a jobber I have yet to determine why he is in the Rising Star title match. Ahh, here ya go sir. Tickets and reservations have been made. Your total, $1,235.00. WHAT THE FUCK!? It's how much? Is that is Yen, or Rubles or something? How much did you expect it to be sir? How will you be paying? I don't suppose washing dishes is an option? Ricky cracks a brief smile as he reaches for his wallet. He feels around in his back pocket but can't locate it. He looks out into the waiting room where he was lying and sure enough it is laying there in the floor. He makes his way back out to the waiting room as a kid snatches the wallet up and bounces out the doors. HEY!!! That's my wallet!!! Ricky chases the kid out the door, and as soon as he rounds the corner the kid is standing there holding his wallet up. Ricky stops on a dime and glares at the kid who hands him the wallet. The kid turns to face him... They are WAY overpriced. I did you a favor. You want to get to florida, follow me. The kid takes off dashing through the alley as Ricky looks around. He shrugs his shoulders, as if his day could get worse. He decides to follow the kid as they both high ball through the alleys of New York. Straight ahead is a chain link fence that the kid scales with no problem then sommersaults off of. Ricky on the other hand takes his time, almosty struggling to get over the fence. Finally once on the other side the kid is leaning against the wall smoking as Ricky comes down the fence. Joey Kase at your service. Ricky... Thunder. I know. Anyhow, down to buisness. I have a contact on a small liner that travels the coastal areas of the eastern border. He can take you all the way down to Florida. It may take a few days, but you have the time. What's in it for you? Everything. For each 'ticket' I sell. I get 1 months worth of free lodging and food. It's worth it. Deal. Let's go meet this friend. Well, uinfortunately, you have to be blindfolded in order to go. It is part of the agreement. If I were going to rob you I would kept running with your wallet. So here, put this on. Hmm...aight...I guess. Ricky takes the blindfold and positions it so he can't see. The kid makes his way over to him and waves his hand in front of his face. He grabs his arms and leads him through a few more alleys before Ricky is suddenly clubbed from behind with a bat. They continue beating Ricky until he is motionless. They take his clothes, his wallet and his shoes. The people leave him blindfolded and lying in the alley in just his boxers. He raises his haed, and slightly mutters..... hey.....that's......my.....everything...... Ricky's head slams back to the ground as he is encompassed in darkness.
