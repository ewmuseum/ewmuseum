Class Reunion

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-29-02 Brandi walks through the parking lot arm-in-arm with her old high school friend, Lindsey. The two have dressed alike, although each wears a different dress for this occasion. Both women have on backless blue dresses, without straps. Brandi’s dress is a deep blue. Her high heeled sandals match the color perfectly. Lindsey has a very similar dress, although hers is a much lighter blue. The women walk closely together, whispering and laughing as if they were back in school, gossiping in Ms. Mitch’s English class once again. Actually, they might as well have been in Ms. Mitch’s English class all over again. They look up and see the familiar sights around them. The high school hasn’t changed much since graduation. After all, it’s only been a few years. The gym has been lit up, and decorated for tonight. Lindsey follows Brandi’s stare up to the many lights and black and gold balloons. She doesn’t pay attention to where she’s walking, and suddenly trips on the curb Brandi’s just stepped over. Lindsey almost falls, but manages to catch herself in time. Brandi watches her friend and tries to hide her laughter. Lindsey- Oh, you be quiet. Brandi looks on as Lindsey tries to regain her composure. She brushes herself off, although there’s no need to. Brandi can’t help but giggle at Lindsey as she fusses over her dress, and her hair. Lindsey shoots a glare in Brandi’s direction, but this just causes her to giggle even more. Finally Lindsey is by Brandi’s side once again. Brandi- I’m sorry. I was just remembering prom all over again, that’s all. Lindsey moans and walks towards the gym, hoping that Brandi won’t go into detail about that night. Brandi smiles and quickly follows her friend up the walkway. Brandi- What? It’s not my fault that someone spiked the punch and that you tripped over the curb, falling into a mud puddle and ruining your dress in front of everyone. Brandi flashes Lindsey a quick smile and hurries ahead. Lindsey stops for a moment, laughs, and goes after Brandi. Lindsey- You just had to bring that up, didn’t you? Brandi- Why, of course. You know you’ll hear about that story from me for years to come. Lindsey- Well, I’m going to make sure everyone bugs you the rest of the night about being famous. Brandi- Being famous? Lindsey- Well, yeah. You’re in PCW. That’s being famous. Brandi- Well, in that case, fame isn’t all that it’s cracked up to be. Lindsey- What do you mean? Brandi doesn’t hear Lindsey’s question, or she just chooses to ignore it. Instead, she pushes open the door to the gym and walks inside with Lindsey close behind. The two stand there for a few minutes, their eyes adjusting from the night, to the brightness of the room. A large banner in the back of the room reads, “Welcome Back, Pirates”. There are even more black and gold balloons in this room than outside. A small table is set up in front of them. The woman sitting behind the table looks familiar to Brandi and Lindsey. When they get closer to the table, they realize why. Brandi- Ms. Mitch! The woman looks up at the two women, trying to remember their names. When it clicks, the woman is up in a hurry and hugging the two girls. She stands back and looks them over. Ms. Mitch- Brandi! Lindsey! You two haven’t changed a bit. It looks like you’re still joined at the hip, too. Brandi and Lindsey smile. Ms. Mitch walks scribbles the girls’ names down on two nametags and hands one to each of them. They barely have time to stick them on their dresses, before Ms. Mitch is dragging them across the room. Lindsey- Ms. Mitch, you’re just the same as I remember you. Ms. Mitch- I know. I keep telling them that they need to space these things further apart, but you know how the school board is. Always stubborn and never willing to listen. The trio stops in front of a large group of students and teachers. They’re in the middle of a conversation, but Ms. Mitch doesn’t seem to notice, or care. Ms. Mitch- Hey, look who came, everyone! Brandi and Lindsey feel their cheeks turn red when the majority of the people in the building turn to see who Ms. Mitch has found. Brandi smiles sheepishly and waves. She turns back to Ms. Mitch and whispers in her ear. Brandi- I don’t even know half these people. Ms. Mitch smiles, pats Brandi on the back, and turns to leave. Ms. Mitch- Well then I suggest you get to know them quickly, don’t you? Brandi starts to say something else to her former teacher, but she’s already left to find another old student to embarrass. Brandi is relieved when the group goes back to the conversation they were having minutes ago, and ignores Lindsey and Brandi. One man pushes his way through the crowd and stands in front of Brandi, holding his arms out. Brandi looks shocked to see the man. She throws her arms around him and gives him a big hug. Brandi- Dave! The man smiles, revealing a set of perfectly white and straight teeth. He has the tan, and the build of a surfer, but is dressed in a suit for the night. Dave- I told you I’d make it to all the reunions, didn’t I? Brandi- Yeah, you did, but you also said you’d keep in touch. What’s up with that? Brandi grins and playfully smacks the taller man on the arm. Dave- Yeah, I know. I’m sorry about that. I’m here now though. How’re you doing? Brandi- Great. What about you? You look great! You went to that college in Hawaii that you were so excited about, didn’t you? Dave- Actually, no I didn’t. I went to one over in New York. Just don’t let anyone else know that. Dave winks at Brandi, getting a giggle out of her. Suddenly he realizes Lindsey for the first time. She’s been kind of avoiding the man since he made his way over. His good looks and outgoing personality have always intimidated her. She’s also always had a crush on him, but only Brandi knew that. Lindsey manages a smile and a small wave. Dave- Hey there, Lindsey. How are you? Lindsey- Pretty good. I think I’m going to go get something to drink. Lindsey leaves to get a drink, and probably more importantly a chance to relax. She needs a little time to convince herself that she can talk to Dave without having to worry about making a fool of herself. Lindsey grabs a cup of punch, and settles into a chair in the corner of the room. Dave- Ms. Mitch ran up to me earlier and told me that you’re here, so I just had to come and see it for myself. I thought you didn’t really like being around crowds of people? Brandi- Yeah, I didn’t. I kinda had to get over that awhile ago though. Dave- Yeah, I almost forgot that you’re a wrestler now. How’s that been working out for you? Brandi- It’s alright, I guess. So, what did you major in? Dave laughs. Brandi feels uncomfortable talking about careers with people. She’s always felt that they judge you based on them. Dave- Wait just a second. I want to hear about this wrestling thing first. Brandi- There’s not a whole lot to hear about. I wrestle. There ya go. That’s the just of it. Dave- Well, I want to know a little bit more than the just. You have anything big planned? Brandi- Maybe. I do have a match on Turmoil to see who the #1 contender is for the Cruiserweight Title. Dave- Oh, really? Who are your opponents? Brandi- It doesn’t really matter. It’s just another match. Dave- I think it matters. You have a shot at winning a belt. That’s pretty good. Brandi- Yeah, I know. It’s just…I don’t know… Dave- Well, who are you against? Brandi- Ricky Thunder, Syko, and Trigger. Dave- Well, I’ve never heard of any of them. Dave looks quite serious. Brandi looks up at him and laughs. Brandi- Yeah, well you don’t count. You don’t follow wrestling. Dave- Hey, I think I count. I could have heard of them even though I don’t follow wrestling. You never know. Hey, do you want to go to dinner later tonight? It’d be a great chance to catch up. Oh, and remember you promised me you’d go on a date with me? You still owe me that date. Brandi laughs, but Dave is pretty serious about the date. Brandi is thinking it over when Lindsey comes back over to them. Brandi looks at Lindsey, then over at Dave. Lindsey- Sorry I took so long. Ms. Mitch caught me again. Brandi- Don’t worry about it. I won’t be able to make it tonight, Dave. Dave- Oh, that’s too bad then. Brandi- But, I’m sure Lindsey would be glad to take my place. Dave- Hey, yeah! That’d be great. What’d ya say, Lindsey? Free for dinner later tonight? Lindsey looks over Dave’s shoulder at Brandi. She’s already slowly making her way towards the door and the safety of the parking lot. Brandi winks at Lindsey and smiles. Brandi- The two of you have fun now. Brandi throws them a little wave. In the corner of her eye, she can see Ms. Mitch rushing over to her. She’s probably desperate to find out why someone is leaving so early. Brandi flashes her a smile, before making the final dash for the parking lot.
