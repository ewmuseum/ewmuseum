The Anarchy Saga: F*ck Off and Die

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-1-03 Australia was a very good time. It was like a home away from home for DarkStar, he could easily be himself here without having to worry about the censorship of the American government shutting up his views. They could do nothing about it. It might not make sense to people, he thought, but it is exactly what he wanted to be. The god thing was nice, but this is really DarkStar, the hatred he feels... this is what he lives by. No longer an American, no longer a nationality, no longer a silent pawn in a huge chess game played by old rich white guys in realms of power across the world... he wanders into a show in New Zealand. The accents are almost enough to drive you insane, not quite enough to make your ears bleed... until the moment you hear the band. They aren't quite Australian, they aren't quite British, but their message is clear. 'Fuck off and Die.' DarkStar looked at Talena with a glimmer in his eyes. It was Extreme Corporal Punishment. It was like walking out of 2003 and stepping into 1985 filmed in 1996, except without Matthew Lilliard or Heroin Bob. The point was clear though. Neither he nor Talena needed to hide under their hoods anymore, they didn't need to hand out anarchist propaganda below the radar. They were apart of the cause now, not just simple followers, but LEADERS. Talena smiled, almost as if she read his thoughts, 'Well, you never were one to take things slow.' DarkStar grabbed her hand and laughed, dragging her up through the crowd, 'Hell no, come on, we've got preaching to do.' The camera moved up and out, watching as they dissapeared and taking in the whole scene, foul mouthed goths and punks congregating for one cause. The band plays loud and obnoxious... truth is in the air. 'One of these days I'm coming out slow, and one of these days their heads will roll.'
