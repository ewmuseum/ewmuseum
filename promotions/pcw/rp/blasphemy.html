Date: By Dorn, again @*$&*!@&%!!!!!!!!!!!!! I can’t believe it!! I LOST! I was fucking robbed on national TV by a pumpkin pie hair-cutted freak and his chair-swinging whore! It’s insane! It’s blasphemy! It’s just not fair! So here I sit, dazed and confused as the back of my head throbs like a heart on mega CCs of speed. The reason for it all still evades me, and I’ve been here for about an hour. En’Flarim, my lovely and malevolent Goddess, has long since retired to the shrine built outside. A rather rickety structure, but its reasons are only temporary. She had no comment on my lost, but I have derived from her silence that she can’t be happy. And why should she be? Her prime warrior just lost to some dork that wears white dresses in order to appease some Shitto deity. Not only was it on national TV, but it broke my proverbial winning streak that could have gone on for ages, Tiamats willing. But no, not tonight. Tonight was different. Someone wasn’t happy with me. I appeased Tartorus and En’Flarim, shouldn’t be enough? Shouldn’t that be all that’s required to make sure their prime warrior comes out victorious against the likes of CHRIS STORM?! I mean, come on, it shouldn’t take the genocide of a million newborn infants in order to achieve victory against the likes of the World’s Millionth Storm and his shmoopie-loving fuck bag. DEATH TO SCHMOOPIE, I say! Their fucked up, cheating ways cost me my chance at achieving the most coveted trophy in sports entertainment. But perhaps it’s not entirely their fault. Perhaps their cowardly, cheating ways weren’t the cause of tonight’s downfall of the Lunis Superia. It could have fucking been the fact that I’m sick as a dog. I’ve got a version of the flu where I piss blood, sneeze blood, swallow blood every time I try to take a drink, and ultimately, shed blood. It’s put me in a blind frenzy because I actually DESPISE being sick. It shows weakness, and that cannot be a good thing. But maybe that’s not it either. It could be… What I’ve feared most for quite some time now. Every time I go to pay homage to the Tiamats, I feel something different. Some other presences, smashing my psionic link with the Astrological Gods like a piece of glass. Could it be someone tapping into the very depths of my mind? Could it be the Tiamats trying to actually severe their ties with the Lunis Superia? Or could it be the taint of stupid, blasphemous religions breaking up my connection? That’s got to be it. I can’t help but have a feeling of painful diarrhea when I think of the other religions running a mock within the bland halls of the arenas PCW visits. Such drivel like Chtulu Mythos and the long-dead faith in the Egyptian Gods. Shinto…Scientology… What IS this CRAP?! They do nothing but congest by righteous mind with their own aura, poisoning my praise of the Tiamats. It must be stopped. I cannot think. I cannot speak straight. I can’t even take a dump without wanting to vomit blood at the thought of speaking to Ra. It’ll have to end soon… Soon… [End.]
