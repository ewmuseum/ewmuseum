Becoming: Isawa Miyagawa The scene opens to a small bridge inside of America's major monuments and one of the leading places into maybe the laughing stock of the state of California, Anaheim. The bridge is suspended by relaying support beams and the man stands on top of it, looking down into the sea of which he is about to plummet. He can only hold his breath as the point of view from him is only of bleak blue skies and dank coloreed waters below. 'Miyagawa': I am useless to the people, all I am is a wrestler. A JOBBING TO BAD BATTO WRESTLER! I can't believe I let every other member in Asylum over shadow me in greatness. I mean every member from Asylum has made more of an impact than what I have and I am never getting out of it, no. He whispers the final words of himself, before starring into the abyss known as the deep. He looks back as the drizzle continues to pound the floors of the bridge causing him to change his mind about the entire thing, contempting suicide for what he happened to be. 'Miyagawa': I know this because I barely beat Clyde on Turmoil to get towards March Madness. I don't even think Clyde wasn't trying for is there any fun when you defeat me? No, I mean can there be? As many people think I'm funny, I'm just a corny, and non existent person on the face of the earth. He jumps backwards and onto the ground steel. The man known as 'Miyagawa' has his hair sticking upward in a mixture of red and black coloring. His trenchcoat hangs onto him like glue to paper and he sulks down with his eyes focused upon trying to find something new in his days. As the stores along the wooden bridge begin to close up since no customers are coming to their shops, one by one each man and woman put on their respective rain coats and continue to mind their business while the man walks through the harbor, paying his mind. A fan comes up to him, a little boy with short blonde hair and a size that reminds him of a small pee wee football fullback. He walks up to 'Miyagawa' and begs for him. Fan: Please sir, please, sign my autograph. 'Miyagawa': You wouldn't want it anyway kid, I am just not funny these days with my quips about people and their problems of society. I wish I were a Angel right now, but sadly I'm not for I'm Isawa Miyagawa and its because I'm...Excellent. Fan: Excellent? You are the equivalent of shit along my foot when I step in my backyard. I mean come on, have you given atleast one single funny thing in the last four months? Have you? No, then you aren't excellent and that is why Drake Maxwell is going to beat you for the television title. 'Miyagawa' sobs like a hysterical child coming out of it's mother's womb as he runs away shaking his head. The fan just shakes his head and continues his walk with his father, their hands combined together just so the boy won't get lost. The words echo throughout the ears of 'Miyagawa' as he bobs his head inside of a Asylum. 'Miyagawa': I reek of Verbal Diahheroa, I reek of Verbal Diahheroa. I reek of Verbal Diahheroa because I cannot spell it properly. For woe is me....WOE! He begins laughing hysterically and convulses onto the floor, white foam coming out of his mouth as orderlies come into his cell as his eyes roll back into his head. As they try to shake him back into life, a recognizeable needle has been shoved into his arm. Fallen Angel Medications- Enough to Take Down A Japo American from Anaheim. Becoming A man whose intentions are unknown up until this point in his life, as he was born into this world without the knowledge of what he was to do in his life, just like many in the world. He first came towards the place of Primetime virtue of acquaintance the Academy, and then the land of Eternal also known to be a mountain filled with feces and the matter of which no one wants to go back to except the implanters of chips. He came to us by accepting a voyage into the land and now, we don't know if he will succeed or fail but because of his friendships with Jeremy Howard, we can only suspect the worst. This is the life of him, the life of... Becoming: Bobby Stormbringer Gone... Everything he had worked for from the beginning had either been shrouded from the thoughts of actual progress, or his own buffonery. It was once thought of Bobby being something later in life, whether it be a 80's music guru, or wrestler. He chose the latter and now it begins us to then. 2-23-02, 1:37 PM EST, Saturday Afternoon 'Bobby' looks to be completely out of it, celebrating in the glimpse of his victory into the March Madness Battle Royal. Laying under his bed, pulling the sheet covers over his body, he dreams peacefully with the sound assurement that he will enjoy the weekend, and the rest of the month. A flock of his salvia comes from his mouth as he continues to sleep calmly and peacefully with his mind on other things as a euphoric wave of thought comes under him at the moment. As his jaw begins to relax and the pillow on his head comfortably rest, he can only dream and not wake... A song from the generation new, 'Video Killed The Radio Star' plays on into his mind as many look on from inside of the ring of hope, his dreams are being confiscated by our view and he isn't very pleased with everything. The quiet calm of rainwater drips back into the inside of his bed as the camera moves in closer to his breath, 'The Showstopper' as many call him smiles with delight as a rainbow overshadows him while he is in his dream world. He continues to sway backwards and forwards in a bunk as his dream continues over. The singing of the sea plays in his mind over and over again loud enough for him to open his eyes and see where he happens to be. On the hull of this ship, 'Bobby' finds himself looking away and trying to find something new about himself. Always the joker, 'Stormbringer' picks up a rain jacket out of a open locker and pulls it onto his body before walking out of his quarters. He is received with mixed emotions by his fellow officers as they continue work on a depleated engine room before 'Stormbringer' hears his name being called out on the public announce system. ' Lieutenant Junoir Grade 'Stormbringer', you are requested in the captian's quarters for a de-briefing and a breifing from your last and next assignments.' 'STORMBRINGER': Sh!t, now what do they want? I defeat the Hank Army and they want to place me somewhere else?... Stormbringer trudges along, his boots clank along the metal flooring as he turns open a metallic hatch door leading to the captian's quarters. There he see a old man, a full lock of gray hair on his head in crew cut fashion, his medals and accomplishments decorated on his left breast pocket and a faded brown colored shirt and pants combination to match. He looks at 'Stormbringer' with intentions of doing something to make the world a better place. CAPTIAN: Look 'Stormbringer' we were all happy that you came back unscathed from the Turmoil mission and advancing in further endeavors later on in your career. You defeated the Hank Army which brought us one step closer to the top of the nation, and now you must bulk down for a lower placement mission. 'STORMBRINGER': Lower placement? CAPTIAN: Yes, lower placement not for any titles, but for hiding behind a coward of a man to do duties. 'STORMBRINGER': You mean...I have to cover the back of Howard? CAPTIAN: Yes. A grimance comes on the face of 'Stormbringer' as he nods his head looking over to something different in the air. 'STORMBRINGER': So, this a solo job? CAPTIAN: No, more of a co-op between you and a man with the code name of 'Scientologist'. He is a former spy whose looking to shake off a failed mission and climb back to his original glory. 'STORMBRINGER': You do understand that I work alone right? CPATIAN: I know 'Bobby', but we cannot afford to lose you. You have a future, the 'Scientologist' does not. 'STORMBRINGER': And let me guess, Sabotage? CAPTIAN: That's right and you're objective is to obtain victory and defeat the legion of Maxwell and End who've taken over the minds of everyone involved and have taken a hostage. 'STORMBRINGER': Can't do it. A look of shock comes across the captian's face as 'Stormbringer' shakes his head no. CAPTIAN: No? Why not? 'STORMBRINGER': Because, with a Scientologist on my side, I am just a hostage in my own right. I cannot accept this mission. CAPTIAN: Very well, return back to your quarters immedietly for your Dishonorable Discharge... A smirk comes on the face of 'Stormbringer' as he begins walking out of the captian's quarters. He turns his head back slightly and out of the corner of his eye looks at the captian. 'STORMBRINGER': Beats teaming with a 'Scientologist' doesn't it? He walks out of the room to a disgusted Captian as we fade.
