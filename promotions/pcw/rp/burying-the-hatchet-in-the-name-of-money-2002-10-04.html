Burying The Hatchet...In The Name Of Money

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th October 2002 The gravel began to spit out from underneath the tires as the sleak black limousine cruised down a deserted highway with its high beams on full. I couldn’t see or tell too much else as the windows were tinted and the night was pitch black outside. I took a look across the seat from me at Udai or also known as Fat Man. He seem a little nervous when I looked at him but hey, that can be expected since I been scaring the shit out of him since the minute I met him. Just as that thought raced through my head I nearly bumped it on the roof of the limousine as it veered off onto a smoother surface, What going on here…. “Where we going?” I glanced over at Udai, I knew that we weren’t going to the airport. Udai tried not to seem to nervous as he put his head down and replied in a meek voice, “To Japan…” ”I know that but I hate to break it to you…but…we nowhere near the fuckin’ airport!” I shouted my last words at him. I could tell that Udai really started to get hot under the collar because he make no eye contact with me after that, He really starting to piss me off! “I know Master Loc…” his voice trailed off as the tires could be heard rolling on the smooth surface outside. I couldn’t believe he just said that, “What you say…” “I said I know Master Loc” he repeated the exact same words he spoke moments before. Something was wrong, I could sense it in the way he calmly spoke back to me and the way that the car was all the way out in the middle of nowhere….for once in my life I decided not to use violence and just play it cool,I hurt Fat Man later!! “Then were we going Fat Man?” I couldn’t help but add the insult to the end of my question. Udai didn’t even look in my direction, he just sat and stared straight ahead before reaching into the pocket next to him and producing a bottle of water before beginning to drink it…ignoring me completely. He going to get it soon, I thought to myself before I asked him again, this time trying to make myself sound more psychotic, “Where…we…going….Fat…Man” “To Japan” he gave me a reply before taking another swig from the bottle of water, I began to feel my face flush red. “You trying to fuck with me? HUH?!” my temper began to flare…. Udai still didn’t look over at me, instead he paused for a second as the sounds of the car driving could be heard making me feel uneasy. I turned my head to the side and gritted my teeth trying to restrain myself from hitting the stupid White Boy when I looked out the window and saw…nothing. Darkness as far as the eye could see!! Something really not feeling right about this…. “So we catching a Private TokyoPop jet out in the middle of nowhere to go to Japan? Why we not just go to the airport, I too famous or something?” I almost mocked him. “Don’t flatter yourself Master Loc,” my eyes grew wide with shock after that comment.I not believe he fuckin’ standing up to me!! I gritted my teeth and grabbed onto the arm rest by my side as I felt the blood rush out of my knuckles and the muscles on my forearms tense. “What you mean?” I couldn’t think of anything else to say. Udai turned around with a smile from behind his glasses and fat face, “Nowhere near as famous here as to what you are in Tokyo Master Loc” He calmed me down with that one comment as I felt my muscles relax and I stopped gripping onto the arm rest to restrain myself. I let out a weak smile, “Then how we getting to Japan…smart ass!” Udai didn’t reply, I was getting fed up with his games but I still controlled my temper. Instead of replying he wound down the electric window to his side, the motors in it whirred as it slid down. As it finished sliding down a fresh sea breeze drifted in through the window and filled my lungs. Taste like fuckin’ shit but at least I know I taking a boat… Udai must’ve figured that I going to ask what kind of boat we going to be taking to Tokyo because before I even open my mouth to ask he said, “Don’t worry, it’s not a junk” You lucky then Huh… I felt relieved but traveling by sea over to Japan was going to bring back some haunting memories of how I arrived in America in the first place. Stupid boat…better be the Titanic or something just as big! The sea breeze continued to waft through the open window as the car continued to roll on what I could now recognize as the pier because I felt a bumping sound every few seconds, must be the planks of wood the car is driving over. As I leant back in my chair and let the air run through my long hair I not have enough time to enjoy much of it as the car stopped and I had to hold my leather seat to stop myself from lurching forwards. I peered out the open window, “I not see any boat….” “Oh don’t worry, it’s here….” A voice drifted through the air outside as I turned my eyes onto Udai who was now breaking into a sweat. The Fat Man managed a weak smile, “We’re here Master Loc” As the words left his mouth he must’ve realized I was going to wring his little neck and make a pancake out of his plump face because he adjusted his suit, opened the door to his side and stepped outside. Leaving me alone in the Limo with a chilly sea breeze wrapping around me. Stupid Fat Man…. I adjusted my jacket and opened my side of the door and got out onto the pier, slamming my door as I saw the light turn off from inside the Limousine. “It fuckin’ cold!!” I said out loud as I glared over the roof of the limousine at Udai who wasn’t paying attention, instead he was conversing with a rather large black figure. Suddenly I knew something was going on. I reached down the side of my leg and began to pull up the machete I kept tucked away in my pants, Always knew that TokyoPop going to fuck me over sooner or later! I had been expecting a double-cross since the deal had been made, but I didn’t know they were going to carry things out Yakuza style…wait a minute, they Japanese so I guess it make sense.I pulled the machete from out of the side of my pants and held my hand behind my back as I made my way around the back of the black limousine to where Udai was talking to the huge black man. “I’m sorry Master Loc, he told me he needed to do this to apologize!” Udai gulped and backed away from the man who turned around to face me, Heh, I smirked and moved the machete into the moonlight, letting it glisten off of the blade as Udai continued to back away….but I soon saw the mans face and rage began to fill inside me. “You…I going to…Fuckin’ Kill You!!” I roared the last part of the sentence and pointed the balde of my machete in the direction of the tall dark man. The man responded with a chuckle, revealing himself as Clyde, “Heh, I’m already dead so try something new!” Before I had a chance to respond he spoke again, “Listen Chuck, I’ve been meaning to apologize for cutting off your foot, so I offered you and your little friend here a ride to Japan….” Clyde didn’t seem to care about the machete I was waving in his face…He fuckin’ stupid?! Instead he just pointed behind him at a huge Cruise Ship which was docked amidst some warehouses, my mouth drooped as I wavered the machete. “I see you’re impressed…I guess I need to tell you what you owe me for this baby!” I could see a smile form on Clyde’s burnt face. Udai backed away behind him in the darkness trying to smile at me, “So you saying that you paying me back by telling me you taking me to Japan with this boat…but I need to do something for it?! I should fuckin’ kill you!” “For god sakes you idiot, I’m already dead! All I need you to do is prove there is no hard feelings…or at least till we’re in Japan anyway, as I can’t afford air fares with the taxes and all…” Clyde trailed off. I shook my head and gave Udai daggers as I nodded my head at Clyde, still pointing the machete towards him, “I listening….” “To prove there’s no hard feelings and there’s a truce, I need you to hijack the mother fucker…and before you ask yes it is The Love Boat” Clyde began to chuckle again. “Why you not do it?” I shot back at him glaring, Clyde shook his head, “Because I want you to prove that we can be truced! No killing until we meet again in Japan!” I thought about his offer for a second before the sinister grin crept over my face, “I not wanting to spend the money I earning, I take care of the captain, you take us to Japan” Clyde looked puzzled, “Fuck no, I’m making your little fat Asian man pilot us to Tokyo….” “Sound fine by me…” Both Clyde and I chuckled in unison into the night as Udai began to look startled. Clyde extended his burnt hand as I shook it, agreeing to a pact until we got to Tokyo. I pulled the hood of my jacket over my head as I tucked the machete back away before making my way over to The Love Boat followed closely behind who had Udai slung over his shoulder. -=***=- I not know why I just shake his hand…the White Boy try to fuckin’ kill me! But then again I not one who wants to burn money on something I can do or get done for free!! If I have to work with my enemy or the devil, so be it…at least I ahead in the game! Fuck Udai too, If he want to follow me around and make deals without talking to me, he can be Clyde’s sex toy I not care! As I said once before, I in this for me and the money! Nothing or nobody else going to be able to change that fact, The sooner we get to Japan the sooner I get rid of both the Fat Man and the White Boy so I making this quick…. -=***=- Fuckin’ heavy piece of shit… I let out a grunt as I hurled the body of The Love Boat’s captain overboard from high up on the deck into the icy water below, I heard Clyde chuckle behind me. “Why this so funny?” Clyde continued to chuckle, “Because we’re taking The Love Boat and I just made you do all the dirty work….” I grumbled as I tucked my machete away as Clyde and Udai made there way into the control room to pilot the ship and get us out of the country. This too easy… I thought to myself as I heard the huge Cruise Ships engine begin to churn underneath, This way to fuckin’ easy…. Clyde could be heard laughing like a maniac from the control room as “Love Shack” by the B52’s began to get pumped out over the speaker system. Stupid White Boy music…can’t wait till we in Japan! Clyde continued to laugh as Udai took the Cruise Ship out from dock and land and New York City slowly drifted off into the distance. “When we get to Japan I going to laugh about this!!” I not believe I just said that….
