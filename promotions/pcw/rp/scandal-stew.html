Date: not entered THE Multiple Man stands in his secret lairThe starting place for many affairActs without thought or careWhither next? A poetry dare! Travel he to someplace new?Trying finally to get a clue?He shrugs and says, “What I’ll do,”“Is whomp me up some Scandal Stew!” “Scandal Stew!?” the crew exclaims“You’ve flipped your lid! You’ve scrambled your brains!”“There is no plus! There are no gains!”As Keira said, “It’s pretty damn lame!” (Tmm looks downcast.) Tmm: “Really? Oh. Then Haiku is what I’ll do!” The match approachesI will train with diligenceHe doesn’t like me. Keira: “Ick. Do the other thing.” Steve: “I feel a headache coming on… I’m gonna go… lie down.” Tmm: “You people are no fun! How am I gonna keep my creative edge if I don’t experiment a little?” Keira: “Stick your head in a pencil sharpener?” Tmm: “Bah!” A dash of Ryan, the scandal man,A couple carrots, a spritz of Pam!Some strange nuts found in Japan,Noodles lots, a dozen clams! Keira: “That’s a stir fry.” Soon piping hot,Tossed into the pot, (Tmm sticks his tongue out at Keira. She makes a face in return.) Some water we got,And boil the lot! Add some beans,Then the scandal ‘zines!With Elvis drag queens,Holding laser beams! Keira: “You’re gonna eat paper?” Tmm: “Don’t worry, the ink’s non toxic, I checked.” Keira: “Errrm…” And if you pleaseA bit of cheese… Tmm: “…” Keira: “Well?” Tmm: “I think I’ve blocked.” Keira: “Cheese’ll do that to ya sometimes.” Tmm: “But this is terrible! I’ve completely blanked! I had it all figured out just a few minutes ago! Interesting ingredients that would make a tasty dish that I could relate to wrestling in a brilliant manner!” (Keira merely looks skeptical.) Tmm: “It would have! Somehow… in some vague way I can’t quite remember at the moment…” Keira: “If it makes you feel any better, that concoction in the pot looks like it would make a great caulking compound.” (Tmm dips a finger into the goo and tastes it.) Tmm: “Absolutely terrible.” (Keira hands over some other sheets of paper.) Keira: “Try adding the sports section. Maybe there’s a few goofballs out there that think that wrestling is a sport?” Tmm: “It’s a loose association… but it might work.” (He stuffs the newspaper into the sodden mass of ingredients and mixes it around for a while before tasting it again.) Tmm: “Nope. Still terrible. There’s gotta be a lesson we can take from this experience…” Keira: “People still love a scandal even though it leaves a bad taste in your mouth?” Tmm: “Good enough for me.” (Camera fades out.)
