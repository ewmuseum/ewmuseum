THE GAME - Part X - The Third ENDGAME

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-02 Necessary OOC Note: Just to clarify this ENDGAME thing first... I consider B.S.'s e-fed career to go into four acts, much like a novel. Every Act ends in what I call an 'Endgame.' I try to make these my very best RPs. In truth, this time the ENDGAME is this entire series. With this last RP I'll try just to wrap it all up into a nice package. The usual 'endgame quality' (I hope!) is in my last two RPs with Aimz and Kira, Parts 8 & 9. Act I: July 1998 to Autumn, 2000. Majority of Indy Fed Career. Ends at pretty much the point where I thought I had quit E-Fedding for good. Silly me, eh? =P Act II: August 2001 to May 2002. Basically, the so-called comeback tour. HWA indy fed, PWA, Eternal Wrestling, the GTT, and these four months of PCW. (Damn, it's been THAT long already!?) Act III: ? ? ? (Should start sometime in early June, July at the ABSOLUTE latest, I promise. Heh. Always freggin' starts in July, doesn't it??) Act IV: ? ? ? Now that that's all cleared up. On with the show! Song Listening Guide: Choice #1: 'Points Of Authority' by Linkin Park. (My old PCW theme song. It's an MTV video now! WOOO!) Choice #2: 'Man In A Box' by Alice In Chains. (Old Tommy Dreamer theme, ECW) Choice #3: 'Who We Be' by DMX. (Old Eternal Wrestling theme) ((C'mon, damnit, ya gotta have ONE of these! =P)) LEGAL DISCLAIMER: Viewer/Parental/Match Writer/Judge discretion is STRONGLY advised. In other words? {Beavis Voice} Huh huh huh. This is gonna be cool. Huh huh huh! BOBBY S. CAMPBELL THE GAME - Part X - The Third ENDGAME Game n. A contest governed by specific rules; a way of entertaining oneself; a calculated way to do something; animals, birds, or fish that are hunted for sport or food. gamer, gamest adj., gamely adv., gameness n. (Definition Credit: Webster's Dictionary, Paperback Edition. Text Copyright (c) 1990, K. Nichols.) * * * Then.... A look of concern grows over Aimz' face in the realization that Bobby just may be heading into another trap. One that wouldn't find Randy this time, either. 'I don't want you getting hurt. That's all... don't forget all those names you called 'er, by the way. I'm sure that didn't help.' 'What? Like... oh, I remember. Young Tiger'll change the same day that Mona quits cold turkey at being a sick f-f-f-f-fffffffreak!' 'Heheh. That's the one!' 'Don't sweat her, sis. She ain't nothin' but a piece of peasant gutter trash. No really.' 'You... mean it?' Kira runs her fingers through her hair and winks to Bobby before holding her neck in some slight discomfort. 'Sure do.' Bobby sits back while the three share a short laugh. The rest of the afternoon... they talked about everything. Movies, games, sports, politics, Boy Hits Car, Old Metallica versus new Metallica, Linkin Park, how dominant Boda was at the PTC basketball game, Disney World, how dirty Daytona Beach is lately, Timo's strange habits, how much Amy can't stand being around Eugene, shopping (Bobby went to the bathroom), Byron Calver, Rent-A-Hero, Bre, Boy Hits Car for the fourty-second time, and anything else under the sun. Anything but wrestling. Everyone needs a break. Everyone needs peace and quiet. Everyone needs to feel loved. Everyone needs someone to talk to... Everyone just needs someone to listen to what they have to say. Just listen. And now.... * * * Perhaps one last long promo. Just for 'old time's sake.' We are left with unanswered questions. However, there is no issue to be had with this, since life deals us unanswered questions on a nearly routine basis. Here are the answers to a few, however. The special guest official was former GTT number one seed Tyler Davidson, and the judges for the 'PTC Street Ball GAME' halftime Slam Dunk contest were the Orlando Magic's Tracy McGrady, a gimpy Grant Hill, and Darrell Armstrong, as well as Orlando resident and slapstick comedian Carrot Top. The fifth judge was the 'big surprise' that B.S. had been gloating about and teasing with for weeks on end before hand. The fifth judge happened to be none other than the very man who discovered Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer in the first place all those years ago before either one of them was even close to being 'well-known.' Stormbringer was his final student before hitting the big time. The same man who would go on just two days later to win the Heavyweight Championship Of The World in Sports Entertainment, without dispute. Except Sebastian Toomes and Boda, at the time. That man is 'The Game' himself. Triple H. (Hey, if PTC Intervention can feature New Jack and then Stevie Ray popping out of a pizza box, then I can do THIS, damnit. ;-P) 'Trip', as B.S. annoying calls him, bolted through the back door with Motorhead blaring off the PA system. Everyone had to sign the necessary legal documentation to witness a non-PTC entity in the business step onto the premises. As the story goes, these two Connecticut boys hadn't even spoken since The Showstopper's very first days in the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Both knew full well who they were each employed by. Tyler Nelson being one, and we all know what the initials 'VKM' stand for! Either one of them showing up on each other's shows was damn near criminal and they both were liable to be fired if they were ever caught. But thanks to these waivers... the fact that Bobby Stormbringer independently produces these promos doesn't hurt, either. Circumstances are NOT the same anymore. This wasn't like some Cesspool of a wrestling company that would lay down and die for the opportunity to have the almighty Triple H himself on their local cable program, even just once a week. This is WWFE and PCW we're talking about here. A mentor and his charge. Anything that happens to the charge in a positive light.. and sometimes negative.. can always be traced back to the mentor. Whether it be Ultimo Dragon to White Mexican, Bret Hart to his brother Owen, or even Obi-Won Kenobi to Luke Skywalker, these are all necessary relationships. Some are like fathers and sons, while others are simply like brothers. In Trip and B.S.'s case... they would be blood brothers. The most ironic part is that when Trip first debuted as the spoiled blue blood 'Hunter Hearst Helmsley', the gimmick made Stormbringer absolutely sick to his stomach, knowing that 'Helmsley' was capable of tons more. However, this is when Trip explained to him the need to be patient. To pay your dues. Bobby was young, predictably brash and arrogant. It took him until several years later to gain full clarity on just what Trip was saying. All losses still hurt. But, just for an example, would B.S. rather beat Clyde soundly in the ring only to lose the match? Or would he rather get his arse totally kicked around by Clyde and then somehow get a fluke three count over him? The latter scenario would of been just fine with him just eighteen months ago, but now the only victories that he even cares about now have to deal with federation championships. Even then, if you don't manage to pull it off? There is always next time. Now when it comes to Sports Entertainment as opposed to Pro Wrasslin', Mr. Prime Time knows only one thing for sure. For the sake of money, respect, love, t-shirt merchandise sales, TV ratings, and anything else you want, one statement is true: 'All that matters now is how good you look out there!' After a brief but meaningful staredown with then PCW Champion Boda, Triple H and his longtime pupil shook hands at THE GAME and shared a private conversation. It became private, thanks to the heavy adulation and cheering from the local Orlando fans. That and the booing from Trip's peers out of once again, being envious of him... since pretty much everyone in the industry wants to be like that guy. The advice was sound and simple. That B.S. was getting soft. With or without Kira York, Bobby needed to keep his exercise schedule diligent and demanding, something that even Marines would be proud of. No more unnecessary plane flights, the less jet lag the better. If Bobby was prepared to bring Kira along, then he needed to be prepared for the consequences. Trip was a sort of prophet... since Kira was just hung up by her neck and nearly KILLED some three and a half weeks after that advice was given. The point that Trip emphasized the most was that B.S. had to stop using The Perfect Storm (as in his finishing move, not the secret power). As effective and deadly as it was, it simply took too long to set up. Bobby agreed since that was one of the few things, besides his flu, that cost him his most important match yet. His Rising Star Title Match with Red Rock. Recently it also cost him a match just as important with Miyagawa. The bottom line is that was a match that cost him over seven hundred and fifty thousand dollars! Not to mention a lot of pride against an old hero of his, and now someone he couldn't respect LESS after getting to know the way he truly operates. Chris Chase just had to get involved, didn't he? Some people just don't know how to keep their big noses out of his business. 'Noneya!' Bobby is prone to say at times, a saying his mother used to say constantly. As in... 'None of your f*ckin' business.' Bobby is still convinced that Red Rock simply isn't half the technical wrestler that he is. Red Rock has been recently fired by new Personnel Director Tim Walden. Stormbringer? It's going to take more than just Tyler Nelson's hateful and personal vendetta against The Showstopper to keep him out of the spotlight. Believe in that. But Bobby had a problem. If not The Perfect Storm, then what could he do? That's when Trip did something that he was younger... this would be totally unimaginable. Triple H gave Bobby Stormbringer permission to use the Pedigree. When you think about it, it makes sense. Not just a 'passing of the torch.' But also they are, after all, employed by two different wrestling companies. There's no real harm in it. Besides. Trip is by no means done yet. So instead of 'passing the torch'... he's simply sharing it. * * * The Slam Dunk contest itself was quite entertaining. The two surprise entrants turned into three at the last minute. Drake Maxwell, commentator Asa Fountain, and Meanstreak's handler himself, Mo~! Already entered were one half of the so-called 'Greatest Tag Team in PTC,' William S. Powell, former PCW Rising Star Champ Jay Sylo, The Rush, OSW's Tempest, Duzza, and the person who came up with this idea in the first place in the form of a challenge to Duzza, CCW's Blayze. Young Tiger temporarily took over as head of security as head of security. Duzza and Rush alternated DJing duties whenever possible. Hey. All three signed up for both assignments, so they better be ready to do both, right? *narrator winks* All nine dunkers were good and entertaining, with Maxwell and Powell doing the most ordinary dunks such as three sixty one handers, normal two handed windmills, and other semi-basic stuff. However, the force of their dunks were most impressive. Especially for Powell, who is known for his technical expertise in the ring, but not really his power. Asa Fountain's dunks included one with his fro!? Don't ask! Sylo's included a reverse with the left hand after banking it off the backboard, as well as the Minor Dunk, dropping far down between his legs in mid air, almost touching his ankles as he drove a reverse through the basket, net, and almost the carpeted floor below it! Mo~! hit a double windmill as well as the 'stick your arm halfway through the basket' one-handed dunk trick. Amazing, isn't it? A handler jumping that high?? Gotta love fiction. *narrator snickers* The Rush, Duzza, and Blayze, however, all pulled out the best dunks. double spin reverses, the Cedric Ceballos blind two-handed dunks, and so on. The best dunk of the preliminary round was the God Of Crunk dunking it not from the free throw line, persay, but from... THE THREE POINT LINE! How unfair is that!? Must be nice to not have to be mortal, I guess! 'PCW! PCW! PCW! PCW!' chants from about half of the crowd in attendence. Obviously, a perfect score of fifty to the leader of a real religion around here. Hmph. But did he use that dunk too soon..? Even more exciting than that normal dunks, however, was Tempest's. On her final attempt after two misses, The 'Little Storm', the shorter-than-five-feet-eight-inches-tall Asian cutie dunked the ball! With two hands!! What may also seem like an impossible feat really should be with someone without all those fancy dancy powers. Bobby Stormbringer could only fold his arms and shake his head in amazement, like usual when it comes to Tempest. He's seen the 'impossible' from her before along with the birth of this millenium. Winning match after match after match, mowing down guy after guy after guy. Never.. ever.. EVER underestimate Tempest. If you don't? It might end up being one of the worst mistakes in your life. Now if only Meanstreak had listened before he was tarred and feathered back in March... Tempest was given straight nines for it and a more of respectable fourty-five. However, the scoring was the best two scores in three dunk attempts with a maximum of thirty when you miss, so she could not advance. The top three scorers that made it to the finals were The Rush with ninety-seven, Blayze with ninety-five, and Duzza with ninety-four. 'Jump' by Kris Kross is played. When it all shook out The Rush was extremely unlucky. After missing a repeat of the 'three point line' dunk, he settled for an Isiah Rider 'windmill between the legs' dunk. This gave him another perfect score. However on his third attempt the ball inexplicably slipped right out of his right hand and hit the top of the rim! It slowly rolled around the rim and finally fell through, taking all of the impact of the move away. That is what happens when you try a.. get ready.. a TRIPLE windmill. A score of one hundred is unbeatable, but there is no doubt nothing less than another perfect score would of been warranted. Instead, he recieves a fourty-one. Ninety-one total. The door has been opened. 'Last Resort' by Papa Roach is played. Blayze follows up next with a strange but awesome dunk. The constant combatant of K-Dub in the 'PTC Complex Discussion Rooms (or the TT Board)' flips the ball up from behind his back, letting it fly over his head. Then he goes straight up and dunks it home with his left hand! This earns him a score of fourty-six. After that he hikes the ball like a center in football up against the backboard! Then he turns quickly and hits a two-handed 720 degree dunk to the roar of the crowd! Even commentators Robbie Decker and Asa Fountain leap out of their seats on this one. This earns a score of fourty-nine. It is revealed that Carrot Top was the lone holdout with a wierd score of nine! He is booed almost out of the building. Blayze gets a final score of ninety-five. Only the original two entrants, Duzza and Blayze, can win the Contest. Duzza comes onto the floor, ironically, to the old EW entrance theme of 'Who We Be' by DMX. Duzza's first dunk is a 720 windmill reverse attempt, and understandably, he misses to a shocked 'Oooo!' from the capacity crowd. 'No more misses or we can just hand the trophy over to Blayze! This sucks... Tempest should be in these finals.' If Robbie Decker had an OSW badge on, he would display it proudly every chance he got. 'I don't think there be an actual trophy, jive turkey,' Asa Fountain responded after returning to the commentator's table. 'but either way. I can't watch!' Asa pulls his fro over his eyes and whimpers slightly. 'Whatever. Go to the showers, you stink!' Duzza didn't hesitate any further, CARTWHEELING TWICE up the lane before springing back up into a massive two handed dunk from behind his ears! When the din from the audience members dies down, Duzza gets a score of fourty-six, with Grant Hill and Darrell Armstrong giving out nines and that DAMN Carrot Top giving him an eight! Triple H, quite frankly, seems to be ready to hand out tens on that day... 'He needs a fifty to win! No chance, none at all. Ha!' Robbie Decker will hardly ever change, that's for sure. The crowd hushes down and Duzza takes a few deep breaths. The now PCW star drives from the middle of the court from the half court line and does the most spectacular thing ever seen on a basketball court. In fact, now his plain mortality has to be called into question. Eyes closed. From just inside the free throw line. One handed. Three-sixty front flip dunk. 'Nuff said. The crowd, by the way, explodes. Metaphorically. No, no one was stupid enough to bring a bomb, much to B.S.'s surprise. Darrell Armstrong. Ten. Grant Hill. Ten. Triple H. Ten. Tracy McGrady. Ten. Carrot Top. Oh God, here we go.......... TEN! Immediately, Duzza's friends and even a couple family members rush the court in stupifying celebration in the winning score of ninety-six, lifting him onto their shoulders like he just won the PCW World Title. Blayze meanwhile, just chases Carrot Top right out of the building with murderous intent in his eyes. The scene fades out while leaning against the side wall behind Fountain and Decker and watching from his vantage point- Bobby Stormbringer just smiles and applauds an old friend. ONE.. MORE.. TO GO.
