Date: 19/06/03 11:20 PM ‘HADOKEN!’ James: Yes come on have it you’re going down! The sound reverberated all around the room followed by the constant tapping of buttons, a few strained noises coming from James Edwards who was stood in front of the video games machine ‘IT’S MY SUNDAY BEST! PROTON CANNON!’ Another computer generated voice screamed out of the large machine before the screen, barely visible through the back of the person in front of him exploded into a mass of colours and flashing lights which, had the person in front of the machine been epileptic would more than likely taken care of business. In a huff the figure kicks the base of the machine before walking off limping slightly trying to disguise the throbbing pain in his foot unsuccessfully. James: Bloody War Machine and his bloody Proton Cannon every time he gets me. James sighed slightly and made his way through the seventh floor of the airship looking at the numerous video games which it held some old some new but all serving their purpose to entertain, passing Pac Man James stopped momentarily and looked at the aged machine, he could remember when this game had first come out how revolutionary it was how everyone wanted to play it, now it was placed in the corner of the room, out of harms way collecting dust. Is this really what happened to things when they became obsolete? Were they simply put out of view, away from public eye? Would the same thing happen to him one day? When he could no longer perform the way people expected him to perform, when he was unable to achieve his dream? James shook off his brief moment of depressive thinking and continued to wind his way through various video games until he saw the elevator at the far side of the room, he approached it slowly, he was in no rush after all and with it being reasonably late at night James knew he wouldn’t have to worry about fighting just to got on it. Pushing the button with the arrow pointing up on it James leaned backwards against the wall as he heard the whirring of machine click into action as the elevator slowly grinded it’s way down the numerous floors of the airship to James’ destination. Since his all be it brief run in with Matt Jackson, James had been taking a much more different approach to life on the airship, PCW had gone to all the expenses of hiring this thing yet nobody seemed to be using it, simply choosing to stay hidden in their rooms, in the hope that they would not be seen by their respective enemies. A loud ‘PING’ followed by the sound of metal being opened as the elevator had reached the games deck interrupted James’ thoughts once more. James pushed himself up off from the wall and entered the elevator pushing the button to take him to the first floor. ‘Observation Deck First Floor. Elevator Going up’ With that the huge metal doors closed, interlocking and trapping James inside the metal cubicle, which had started its slow accent. It had taken James a long time to realise that Matt Jackson was just another wrestler, for the best part of his brief career in PCW James had seen Jackson as the point of where to set his sights, never wishing to look past him. He counted his pin fall over Pharaoh as simple luck, that Skylar Green had done most of the work. He always felt that he could do no better than Jackson simply because he had been beaten by him so many times. Now though it was as though things had changed. ‘Observation Deck First Floor’ The huge metal doors, which had formed the brief prison around James, opened to reveal the huge Observation deck situated at the very top of the airship. A gentle whirring could be heard of the air conditioning machine keeping the room at a reasonably cool temperature as James stepped out of the lift and sat himself down on a large sofa and stared blankly out of the Plexiglas windows, which surrounded him instead of walls. The view was phenomenal as the airship soared majestically just above the cloud line giving James a picture perfect view of the night sky. The moon was shining brightly through the windows on the observation deck filling it with more light than would be expected for the time of night. Numerous constellations, some, which usually couldn’t be seen, too well from ground level shone brightly twinkling in the void of darkness called space. Just a few decks below him Edwards knew that the rest of the roster would be sleeping, a group of men and women who had recently experienced more than their fair share of problems, it seemed to come with the job, as a general rule, wrestling bought pain, tears, heart break and more psychological problems than Sigmund Freud would care to shake a stick at but at the end of the day he knew that they were all doing what they loved, for some reason or another destiny had bought every member of the roster to PCW. Edwards reason to complete his dream. James rose from his sitting position never being able to remain in one place for too long, something which his instructors had always complained about back at NASA, how he could never remain in the main section of the shuttle without fidgeting and bugging the hell out of his mission companions at least in the training simulations anyway. He moved slowly towards the edge of the room placing his hands lightly against the Plexiglas leaving a number of finger prints on the reinforced plastic, he closed his eyes and let out a short intake of breath before he let his head hang down. If it wasn’t for this window I’d be able to touch you, I would be able to become one with you, I know it’s my destiny and I will go through whoever I must in order to get there, my time is not yet there but I will become what I always wanted to be, I will follow in the footsteps of my sister and become an astronaut. I know I won’t be as famous for it but I want to do this for myself, I have to follow my dream and I will be there with you sooner or later. James removed the hand from the Plexiglas and returned to his sitting position, never taking his eyes off the moon, staring at the visible craters on it, the indentations into the form of the land thinking of the previous men who had stood upon the moon not just Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin the others, who had done what so few men had been able to do, yet never received acknowledgement for their success. Charles Conrad, Alan Bean, Alan Shepard, Edgar Mitchell and the remaining six who’s names even James forgot. James let his head fall backwards as the plush cushions of the sofa softened the blow as realisation flowed quickly into his mind. Just like Pac-Man eventually things were forgotten, no matter how great or ground breaking your accomplishment, eventually you just became a page in history, only bought to life again by a kid doing a research paper, or an over enthusiastic man wanting to prove something to himself. The fact I couldn’t remember their names made me feel ashamed of myself, not being able to remember twelve small names…names, which should mean the world to me. Names that were more than that but people who had been able to walk on the moon. I guess at the end of the day the name is not important but the action. After all a name is just a name.
