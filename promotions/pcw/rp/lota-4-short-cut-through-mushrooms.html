LOTA #4: Short-cut Through Mushrooms

undated

Date: ..... (I'm once again cutting 'A Short Cut Through Mushrooms' into two parts. The chapters in the book are way to long to make one rp that isn't 12 pages.) The sun was shining on my face. Should I get up? It probably wasn’t late, most likely it was still early but there was a big day ahead of me and my team of AJ Palmer and Jesse Ramey wanted to make it to Brickethollow as fast as they could with me. Nah, nobody else is probably up, I’ll just rest my eyes. I closed my eyes and rolled over, the sun was shining on my face through the trees around me. I let the sun shine on my face for about a minute when he sun was gone and replaced with shade. I thought that it was just the sun adjusting but then I heard some quiet rustling on the grass a couple of feet away. I opened my eyes and saw the outline of Sam Ramey. He was taking his sleeping bag and rolling it up. He looked tired but rested for the time being. “What time is it?” I mumbled through the pillow that I slept on. Sam turned his head toward me, continuing to pack away his stuff. “Still early sir, but I couldn’t sleep.” I kinda nodded but Sam didn’t see me. I turned over onto my other side and closed my eyes for a few minutes. About ten minutes, I was up and dressed, ready to go with my sleeping bag packed away. AJ ‘Pippin’ Palmer was waking himself up. I went to the bathroom in the woods and came back to see Sam laying on the soft grass, some juice and toast with peanut butter in his hand, eating. I don’t remember bringing the kind of peanut butter that Sam was using. It was chunky and we had only brought regular. Also, we didn’t bring orange juice, that was for sure. “Where’d you get the food?” I asked Sam, walking over to him. He looked at me and squinted because of the sun that he was looking to. “The elves left it. They also left some leftover food from last night for us. When I woke up this morning, they were gone and nowhere to be seen. They left the food on a tray with a note wishing us a good journey.” That was nice of the elves to leave good food. Our own supply was running low and even AJ, who doesn’t worry very much was questioning whether we would have enough food to make it to the house where I would be ‘living’ in. “Cool.” I sat down on the grass beside Sam and put that peanut butter on two slices of bread, putting them together and eating eat. Soon, Pippin was with us, eating. We didn’t talk much and instead, just admired the surroundings. “How long till we get to Brickethollow do you think, master?” asked Sam. Wow, my secret had lasted a long time. I thought by this time, everybody would have known that I wasn’t planning to stay in Brickethollow for very long but it had worked perfectly. “Uh, we have to take a small ferry and from there, it’s no more than a days walked, so just under 2 days or so.” Soon, thereafter, we had packed up the remaining supplies that we had with us and were on our way, down the main road. We all were thinking about the black rider from a day before but didn’t say anything to each other. A little ways up, Sam started a conversation. “Master, it may be better if we take a shortcut to get to the ferry. If we turn off here, we can follow this mushroom field and take off a lot of time, and also stay hidden.” We all looked to the left off the side of the road where a mushroom field as far as the eye could see was. “All in favour?” I asked. Everybody nodded at me and we were off through the mushroom field. The terrain inside was more hilly and dirty but in between the tall mushrooms, we could manoeuver quite easily. A little ways in, Pippin suddenyl stopped us. “Look!” Pippin’s ton of voice was one that I didn’t like. He pointed up onto a ledge that was one or two hundred feet up, about 100 yards away. Looking off the edge was a huge, black horse and on the horse was a man, clothed in black cloth. “The black rider!” exclaimed Sam, continuing, “We better run!” “Wait!” I stopped Sam from doing anything stupid. “If we run, we’ll make noise and he’ll see us. He can’t see us in the field standing up, and there is no way he can see us if we crouch a little. We are safe.” I paused shortly, “For now.” We crouched slightly and continued walking through the field, our eyes not leaving where the black rider was standing on the hill. Soon, he turned around and went away. “That is good news.” Pippin whispered to all of us. “Not really. Now we don’t know where he is or where he is heading. We must hope that he passes.” Sam had a good point and Pippin nodded, understanding. We had continued walking. Pretty soon, lunch came and went without food. It was two o’clock already and we were still in the mushroom field. The ground was too dirty to be taking out food to get ready. We were all hungry but it would have to wait. We had been walking non-stop in the field for almost four hours when all of a sudden there was a rustling noise in the bushes nearby. We all stopped dead in our tracks, listening ahead. “What is that?” Sam whispered as quiet as we could as we all got closer together. Once again there was rustling in the bushes and a figure moved across the bushes. “The black rider?” AJ asked what was on all our minds. Could it be the black rider coming for us? “Uh-oh.” Sam didn’t get to finish his sentence as all of a sudden, two gos started barking at us, coming straight for us. About five feet away, the dog got ready to pounce on all of us. The look on the dogs face wasn’t a happy one. The dog looked mean and hungry. The dog barked and lowered his head, ready to pounce. We all turned to run the other way when we saw the other big, black dog, ready to pounce as well. The snarl was scary and not nice. “Hutch, Fang, down!”
