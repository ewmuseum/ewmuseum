The Transformation: The Empire Has Begun

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/22/03 The Parties Just Begun Music blares from the Gothic style 18th century mansion of El Romantico. What makes this cryptic portal different on this foggy January night is that El Romantico isn’t the only one in there alone. The people have come together and just overnight; Romantico has managed to change his life with a complete one eighty. The door opens as a group of five to ten unknown faces walk through with smoke coming out into their face. Eyes wander around inside as everyone is either smiles of tears. Reckless and with no compassion the hundreds of people go as wild as they can. El Romantico in the meanwhile sits back on his silver lined chair with red velvet comforters. He has women at his feet and is smiling a sick smile of satisfaction. Anything I can get you Romantico? Nothing you already haven’t given me precious. Romantico smiles showing his full deck of white sparkling teeth. He rubs the arm of the beauty in front of him and then lets out a deep long and luxurious breath. He then stands to his feet as the multiple women wipe the dust from his solid black pants. He enters the main ballroom and watches the happy faces dancing and having fun. He let’s out a loud sigh. Now this is the life my friends. Romantico then went to the middle of the room as everyone’s eyes drew close to his. He ha the DJ stop the music and waited as the people settled down. In a world imperfect, we find perfection. In a country of sin, we find purity. We are not the outcasts, my friends but they are. My eyes bleed for acceptance, and I am pleased to have you all as my people. We shall lead a country of devastated believers into a reality of happiness. May the meek in heart leave now, for the real party has just begun! Romantico’s eyes light up in flames as the room circles around him. They join together and cheer for Romantico, not knowing what they are getting into. The dancing and partying continues as Romantico decides to go on his own little adventure. Ladies, follow you master. Romantico snaps his fingers and leads the ten gorgeous women into a huge majestic bathroom fit for an army of people. You are no longer normal women who need to be seen fit in the eyes of foolish men with foolish minds. Lend me your bodies and you shall become one of Romantico’s possessions. Do not deceive me show me your love. Look in my eyes, and I’ll wipe away your cries. The woman looked at each other uneasy at first. Sure they had this strong attraction for Romantico, but were they willing to give themselves to Romantico in full. That’s when Romantico uses the tactics he’s kept locked up for years. He approaches one of the uneasy woman and whispered in her ear: Look in these eyes; these eyes will not hurt you. The woman stares in the colorful eyes of the handsome El Romantico. Her body becomes weak and mind uneasy. It’s almost like magic where Romantico is hypnotizing this beauty into feeling this way. She falls as Romantico catches her in his arms. He then places her back to her feet and she speaks softly. I’ll be one of yours Romantico. The rest followed one by one, never taking their eyes off of the Poetic lover. They trusted his now tainted soul, but they had no idea how they’d be used. Romantico takes out a rod of hot iron. It was shaped with an Italic R. He pets the fiery solid and licks his lips, as his finger turns red from the heat. Now my ladies, it is your turns. One by one Romantico puts his symbol on the woman signifying his ownership over them. He feels bad in no way whatsoever, but instead he loves every second of it. The doorbell suddenly rings, but Romantico ignores it. It rings five times more and he uneasily paces over to the main door. A few drunken party goers have already answered the door and are mumbling words, but Romantico just throws their bodies out of the way and checks to see who it is. The man who lives in the house right across the street is at his doorstep with an unpleasant look upon his face. Sorry to make a scene. I know you don’t have many of these and this is the first time it’s happened, but I’d appreciate it if you lowered the noise level. My children are trying to get to sleep. I’d appreciate it if you left my property. Excuse me. Romantic puffs smoke in the man’s face from his vintage cigar. I’ll call the police on you. Oh I don’t think you want to do that. Romantico then shows off a solid silver knife with a gold and burgundy ring around the handle. No need for violence. Let’s keep it that way. Romantico slams the door and then looks back at his adoring female admirers. Where were we ladies? THE EMPIRE HAS BEGUN!
