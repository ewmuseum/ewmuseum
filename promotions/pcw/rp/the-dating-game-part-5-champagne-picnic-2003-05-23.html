The Dating Game Part 5 (Champagne Picnic)

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/5/03 It had been a short journey to get to the place James had taken Michelle on the secluded picnic that Sharon had arranged for the both of them. What is that location? I hear you say well here goes. The night air was calm and refreshing, with the moon shining brightly down through the clearing in between the trees where James’ and Michelle were sat quietly staring into each others eyes, listening patiently to each other, sat on a tartan design blanket there are a number of empty plates in front of them which indicate that their picnic has finished, both of them are holding onto a crystal glass containing bubbling champagne, the only noise that can be heard is a few crickets chirping in the background and the slight breeze causing the branches and leafs overhead to rustle gently and melodically in the wind. Michelle: That was a lovely dinner James, the whole evening so far has just been so romantic, the flowers, the wine, the champagne and just being here with you it’s all been so amazing I don’t want the night to end, there is one thing that I still want to know about you though James. What do you actually do? I’m guessing that from the look you gave me earlier you aren’t a rodeo rider like Richard said. James laughed whilst taking a sip of his champagne never taking his eyes away from the deep pools of hers, he placed the glass down lightly on the tartan blanket and took her hand in his lightly rubbing the top of it with his thumb. James: Well when I was young well for as long as I can remember I’ve wanted to go up there. James raised his head and looked to the night sky, the stars glistening brightly accompanied by the moon in their beautiful ballet across the sky. James: My very first memory was with my parents just sat on a large rock at the beach, I don’t remember which beach but I can distinctly remember the sound of waves crashing on the rocks as I stared at the night sky, my mother and father hugging each other with me in the middle, it was the most secure I have ever felt in my life and staring at the stars just made me wonder what was out there, from that moment on I knew I wanted to be involved in space in some way shape or form so I got my degree in astrophysics and then I got a job at NASA. James noticed that Michelle was now rubbing his hand in return, her soft silky skin gliding over his as a feather glides on the wind. Michelle: That’s beautiful, so how did things go in NASA did you get to live out your dream what happened? James let out a long sigh, it had been a long time since he had been forced to relive the memories of his failure, the memories of how his own flesh and blood had taken the dream away from him. It had been over half a year since Emily had been sent into space and he still had not heard anything, either from her via video link or by NASA itself. James: Well I was put through training unlike anything I had ever imagined, they try to break you both physically and mentally making sure that you are ready for every possible situation that could occur. It got down to myself and another astronaut as to who was going to get the final spot on the shuttle but it wasn’t my time and the other person was deemed more appropriate for the mission. It was then that NASA had decided to tell me that my funding had been scraped and I would have to pay for my own training if I ever wanted to accomplish my dream. James stared towards the sky one more time before lying back on the blanket gazing into the void like abyss above him, he hadn’t told Michelle that it was his sister that had beaten him to the spot, he’d seen no need to. It was only their first date there was no need to lower the tone from what had already been a relatively magical evening, he knew that if he started to talk about it he would just get depressed and that was something that he did not want to happen, not tonight. Michelle: Then what happened presumably you haven’t given up on your dream have you? James shook his head lightly from side to side before slowly parting his lips. James: No, when I watched the shuttle that I could have been on breaking through the atmosphere I knew it was my destiny to some day reach the stars but I had to get money to do it, so I became a wrestler, don’t ask me how the transition was made as it was hard, I’ve had my head punched and smashed more times than I care to remember but as long as eventually I can get into space I don’t care. Michelle nodded her complete attention fixed upon James, taking in every word that he was saying, a small smile formed on her delicate lips as she leaned in and kissed James lightly on the forehead, James shutting his eyes as she did so and sighing happily. Michelle: You will get your dream, I can tell, it will come true. Michelle glanced down at her silver watch, the hands indicating that time had flown by and it was now two o’clock in the morning. The tiredness hit her almost instantaneously when she allowed herself to be grasped by the concept of time once more. Her eyes became heavier her breathing more strained a difference that James noticed and glanced towards her. James: Are you ok there? Michelle: I’m just a bit tired it’s two o’clock in the morning I didn’t expect to be out this long, I’m afraid that I should be thinking about leaving, I just want you to know that I didn’t expect tonight to be so magical, I had the vision of the you taking me to some hoity toity French restaurant where the portions are so small for the price you pay, but you didn’t you went out of your way and prepared a wonderful evening for the two of us and I want to say thank you. Without another word Michelle leans forward towards James and kisses him tenderly on the lips, for that moment the world seemed to stop for James, feeling a connection to another human being like he had never done before, her soft lips pushing against his sending him into a state of euphoric ecstasy. And then it was gone as she pulled away and smiled at James one more time before climbing up off the tartan blanket brushing a few fallen leafs from her dress before walking along a cobbled path through the woods and away from James who was left lying staring towards the moon, revelling in how he felt when he had experienced that kiss.
