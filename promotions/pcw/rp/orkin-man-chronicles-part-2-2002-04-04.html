Date: 4/04/02 ***As we last left the Chef and Bertha… Bertha: Do you f*cking make queer as sh*tty @$$ f*ck house calls? The Orkin Man, always a suave individual, grabs Bertha’s wart infested hand and kisses it softly. He speaks… Orkin Man: Anything…for a lady… Bertha practically melts in her Lane Bryant exclusive sweat pants. Bertha: ooooooooooohhhhhhh f*cko… The Orkin Man smiles at Bertha, but realizes that he is needed to vanquish some horrible pests. Orkin Man: It’s go time! The Orkin Man puts some shades on and presses the trigger again when……….. The camera pauses. The images on the screen stay still as if time was miraculously stopped by Allah, and Allah could do that since he’s so strong, wise, powerful, graceful, intelligent, a good break dancer, makes some kick @$$ blueberry muffins, and is appearing at your local mosque…YOWZA!!! ***And now…BACK TO THE STORY!!! The Chef: Stop!!!! Orkin Man: What? I’m the freaking Orkin Man here! The Chef: You can spray my arm and kill the ants, but won’t the poison eventually seep its way into my veins through the open wound and poison me!?!? Orkin Man: Well…I think you’ve got a point there. How do you know so much about noxious gases? The Chef: (smirking) Ahem, well I am an Islamic Militant…AAAAHHHHH!!! The Chef grips his arm tightly and cringes in pain. The Chef: Their pinchers!!! They are pinching me with their pinchers!!! Orkin Man: Yeah, they’ll do that. So what do you want me to do about it Mister? Curator: Well, the museum closes in ten minutes, so if you can’t get the problem fixed by then, you’ll have to go outside! The curator points towards the doorway. Bertha intervenes on the Chef’ behalf. Bertha: Well that’s a f*cking nice way to treat a mother f*cking celebrity with a b*tch @$$ quote in your f*cking sh*tty as f*ck museum, f*ck face! Orkin Man: Ok, then let’s get this party started! The Orkin Man points his spray gun at the Chef’s arm again. The Chef: NOOOOO!!!! Bertha: F*cking NOOOOOO!!! Their cries don’t sway the opinion of the fanatic pest killer; he pulls the trigger and sprays the Chef in the arm!!! The Chef: AAHHHHHHH, THE PAIN!!!! THE PAIN OF IT ALL!!!! AAAAHHH…huh? I’m not hurting anymore, the ants are dead and falling to the floor, and I’m not choking on my own vomit…I’m cured! The Chef hugs the Orkin Man. Orkin Man: Easy fella, if you grab any area below the waist, I’ll have to spray you in the eye… Bertha: Why aren’t you f*cking poisoned skank @$$ f*cko!?!? The Chef: I guess living with you and your natural other’s noxious and toxic bodily gases for these past few years has given me an immunity for what would be a lethal doses of pesticides! Bertha: I knew f*cking eating beans would come in mother f*ck @$$ humping handy! Bertha and the Chef hug, the Chef grabs her butt cheeks and squeezes them. The Chef: Thank you Bertha…for saving my life, even though you did so involuntarily… Bertha: And thank you f*cking Orkin Man!!! Bertha plants a kiss on the cheek of the Orkin Man; he then cringes with disgust. Curator: Well, there is five minutes left before the museum closes, would you like to see your statue? The Chef, wrapping a bandage around his arm from his Militant Islamic first aid kit, replies… The Chef: I wouldn’t miss it for the world! The trio of the Orkin Man, Bertha, and the Chef all follow the curator to a remote location in the museum. Bertha: Dang, these f*cking quotes ain’t so c*ck @$$ funny, f*cko… Orkin Man: “Because I’m your mother, that’s why”? The Orkin Man looks at the nameplate at the bottom of the statue. Orkin Man: There’s a statue of “Your Mom”? Curator: Yes, yes, it was all the idea of that radical curator; he thought it would be a joke… Bertha: It ain’t f*cking funny! Curator: Here’s another inductee from the radical curator’s years… The trio passes a statue of Mary Poppins. Bertha: Superf*ckingcalab*tch@$$fr*ckinexpimothaf*ckingsh*ttyasf*ck@$$alado…what the f*ck? Curator: Yes, I don’t believe anyone has pronounced it except for Miss Andrews… The Chef: These statues stink like rancid sausage! Curator: Yes, the curator’s tenure here was short-lived. Bertha: What’s his f*cking name b*tch @$$!?!? Curator: Well, I would be violating the swore secrecy of the curator’s code, but he called himself the “Visionary”… Bertha: F*cking JJ!?!? The Chef: Hehe, I wondered where the master of the “L-Bow” went!Curator: Yes, indeed, it seems that this JJ fellow thought of himself as quite the director, but his work, as we often say in the curator trade, sucks a lemon… The Chef: Yeah, and to think he won the TV Title… The Chef rolls his eyes. Curator: He tried to get a statue of himself put up, but we didn’t find his antics any more amusing than a David and Goliath promo… The Chef: Man…that’s bad… Finally the group stops before the Chef statue. A proud Chef is shown, with his Military Islamic garb, and a bright light shining forth above his head. Bertha: What’s the f*cking light for!?!? The Chef: It’s Allah you dimwit! Curator: Actually no… Bertha sticks her tongue out at the Chef. The Orkin Man just rolls his eyes. Curator: Actually it’s a prison searchlight, the setting of your statue is supposed to be Guantanamo Bay. The Chef: Oh! How do you know I? The Curator just nods and winks at the Chef. Curator: I have my sources. The Chef: Hmmm, must be cool to be a curator! Curator: Yes, it’s a life few would dare pass down. Women flock to me, the money flows into my bank account, and I’m surrounded by great quotes like this all the time… The Curator points to a statue, oddly enough, of Manuel Hung. The text bubble reads, “Adios Enemigos!” The Chef: Wow, I wonder who else has had a statue inducted before me? Well, I guess this is a good way to end the promo don’t you think, B? Bertha: F*cking fine with me, as long as I pick our mother f*cking dinner destination! Orkin Man: Are you thinking what I’m thinking? Bertha: If you’re f*cking thinking about the Wafflehouse, I’m f*cking thinking what you’re f*cking thinking! Orkin Man: Last one there’s a sausage pellet! Bertha: F*ck yeah! Bertha and the Orkin Man race towards the door. The Chef looks back and shakes his head, sighing at Bertha and the Orkin Man. The Chef: Well Mr. Curator, are you in the mood for poorly cooked breakfast food? Curator: Sure, you’re buying? The Chef: Huh? You think I’m made of money? I’m no curator! The camera mercifully fades to black…
