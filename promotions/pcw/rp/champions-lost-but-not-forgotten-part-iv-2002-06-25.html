Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part IV]

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 25, 2002 The relentless pursuers were drawing nearer and nearer as Sunday became closer and closer, so close that it seemed that it would be an utter impossibility for Jack and Johnny to regain what was taken right out of their hands nearly two months ago. These men were not relishing in fright, nor hiding in fear from the Heartbreakers, but they were in a sense coming together. Standing strong in a clusterf*ck full of greats in a battle to once again steal something from The Heartbreakers. Not so much steal the titles this time, but steal an opportunity. Lost but not Forgotten. Although they did capture the belts, it was only for a short time, and their reign will not go down as one of the greatest. However, they cannot be forgotten in this match, as it is their opportunity to prove once and for all that they belong with those belts, and they belong to the chance at greatness. They have proven a number of times; yes, tens and hundreds of mortal combats with men and beasts. And so they knew that there was another reason than fear of loss behind their fight, as they knew that a greater power than pride or honor spurred them to surpass these so-called tag teams. What was it in their case? Contrary to what many may believe, it isn’t the power of the PINK! Although the will and desire to serve the PINK! proud may guide them in many ways, it wasn’t that. It was mere love. Not love for one another, but a love for this sport, a love for this game, and a love for those titles. They possessed the desire, probably moreso than any other duo in that match, to become legends amongst their peers and amongst their division. Lost but not Forgotten. When their time comes to leave this great federation, they hope to go down as champions that are lost from competition, but definitely not forgotten. Time and again the ferocious battles are fought, and forgotten, and time and again wrestlers fight, and then are forgotten. That’s just the way it goes, power shifts, talent shifts, wrestlers come, wrestlers go; but every fight has a purpose, every battle has a prize; and for Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, that prize was nothing more than regaining something that they had lost. Jack Valentine: “You know something buddy?” Johnny Heart: “What’s that chief?” Jack Valentine: “I feel like a champion today.” Johnny Heart: “Well, at least you feel like one today, because you sure as hell won’t feel like one Sunday.” You see, Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart, if you hadn’t realized already were having their doubts about this match on Sunday. But even the two best swords in a world of fighters can avail not for ever against overwhelming numbers of fierce and savage brutes that know not what defeat means until cold steel teaches their hearts no longer to beat, and so, step by step, Jack and Johnny are forced to have these doubts. After all, they have been to the plateau, and some say that they may not want it as much as the other teams, the majority of which has never seen the light of this tag team gold. Jack Valentine: “Day in and day out you sit here, and you whine, and complain, and bitch and moan. And frankly, I’m sick and tired of it.” Johnny Heart: (Kicking his feet up) “Well, good luck finding another partner.” Jack Valentine: “If you’re going to have an attitude like that, then maybe I will!” Johnny smiles, and then thinks about what Jack just said. His smile quickly turns into a grin of disconcert. Johnny Heart: (Taking a big gulp) “You’re not really going to find another partner...are you?” Jack Valentine: “How many times are we going to have to go over this...it’s not going to be that tough on Sunday.” Johnny Heart: “So...you are keeping me as your partner? I’m confused.” Jack Valentine: (Breaking out into one of his big speeches) “It doesn’t matter if we are fighting a God, or PoP stars, or Heroes, or Political Giants...all that matters is that we have heart, and we abide by the PINK!” Johnny Heart: “Ok...sure.” Jack Valentine: “You don’t sound very confident Johnny...let me further explain this to you. Did you hear what Akiyama was saying?” Johnny Heart: “What’s that?” Jack Valentine: “He said that PINK! was only a color!” Johnny Heart: “That heartless bastard!!!” Jack Valentine: “That’s exactly what I said.” Johnny Heart: “He may call himself the GOD of PCW, but we are the GOD’s of PINK! and if you ask me, two GOD’s are better than 1!” Jack Valentine: (Sighing) “Sure Johnny, whatever gets you motivated.” Johnny Heart: “I can’t believe that little punk had the nerve to say that...if I get hold of him in the ring Sunday night, it will be him that will be punished to the void of the PINK! Someplace where no straight male wants to go!” Jack Valentine: (With a look of shock and disgust on his face) “Whatever Johnny, whatever.” Akiyama had the advantage in this match. After all, it is almost like one on one competition for if a partner is eliminated, then the other must vanish as well. Symbolical bars blocked The Heartbreakers’ further progress, but beyond they could see the light of day. They could see the championship belts in their grasp, and all they had to do was find a way...a way out of those invisible bars, and into the glory. Akiyama was right about one thing...the Glory will be winning the tag team championships, but it will not be him doing it, for it will be two men that abide by something far greater than his god stature, the PINK! The interval is closing. In a short time the opportunity to break free of the bars will be given to Jack and Johnny and whether or not they follow through with their opportunity lies in the hands of the beholders. Johnny Heart: (Enraged) “I will be the one that destroys a GOD!” Jack Valentine: “Hopefully you will do it as a member of the greatest band ever to hit PCW since the Steven Smith quartet...Pretty Cool Guys!” Johnny Heart: (Shifting his mood to cheerful and gay) “Oh god I hope I am...that would just be a dream.” Jack Valentine: “It’s too bad that band members will have to fight in the ring on Fusion...oh well, I don’t care who wins, as long as myself and Rich Sherwin can ‘team up,’ in a ‘good way.’” Johnny Heart: “Ah yes, that’s right...Jack Valentine’s big return to single’s wrestling this Friday night. I hope you remember what you’re doing.” Jack Valentine: (Pointing to himself in a cocky manner) “Me...I was once one of the most dominant single’s wrestlers in this sport!” Johnny Heart: (Hysterically Laughing) “Ahahahahaha.” Jack Valentine: “What’s so funny?” Johnny Heart: “You almost jobbed to HANK~! HANK~! I SAY!” Jack Valentine: “That’s not to be mentioned ever again...but I will prove to you Friday against Sherwin, Tommy and Stone that the original Heartbreaker...Picture Perfect Jack Valentine has still got a few hard knocks in him.” Johnny turns Jack around and looks at his butt. Johnny Heart: (Being stupidly gay again) “He sure does.” ...TO BE CONTINUED...
