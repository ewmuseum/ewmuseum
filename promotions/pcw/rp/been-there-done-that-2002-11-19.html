Title: Been There Done That Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11/19/02 -The two time Continental Champion and evil Goth God Gangsta chef sits in his evil lair at a dark mysterious table with rookie PCW reporter Porter Cole. Duke is smoking some evil goth herbs as the reporter interviews the brash wrestling legend and good looking sex machine.- Cole: Thank you for the opportunity to be the first person to visit the evil goth lair of a one Dukestar The Goblin for this is official PCW one on one interview. Coming here I was blind folded and placed on a plane and driven here. Dukestar is a Goth God and his location must be kept under wraps. I was the only reporter willing to make the trip and now I sit one on one with Dukestar the Goblin. How are you Duke? Dukestar: Fuck you Porter I’m whack yo. -Duke flashes some goth gang signs. Cole: Tonight we talk alliances only one week away. What are you thoughts? Dukestar: My thoughts right now are pondering how hot you mother is. I’m willing to be she’s pretty fucking hot and I’d like to do her in the ass Malice Style. Those are my current thought. That and I want some hot coco. Ahem evil GOTH GOD HOT COCO!!! -Duke hisses at the young yet stern reporter.- Cole: Ok let me rephrase that. What is your opinion on Alliances. Dukestar: My opinion? Kid opinions are like assholes everybody has one and they all stink. But let me offer you my outlook on Alliances if I can yo. Cole: Please. Dukestar: Has beens, homos, kids, crooks, cons, cock suckers, pussies, pansies, pedophiles, pill pushers, punks, pricks, hell that’s just Team FSW!! You see there really isn’t much to say except that the Front Office has assembled the greatest alliances team in the history of PCW. Cole: What makes you better then any other competitor on the map. Dukestar: Fact is hoss I’ve Been there, done that. Every spot on the map. See these lines on my face and these bones out of place. -The camera zooms in on Dukes rugged face, which reads like the red river valley.- Dukestar: I got Shit on my hat, been blessed and cussed at. No braging hoss, just fact. You see kid I’ve been there and done that. It’s nothing new to me. Cole: You’re not the only veteran in the Alliances main event what makes you unique. Dukestar: I have the best cast around me and I’m better looking then the rest of them scrubs. I’m Duke Williams err Dukestar The Goblin. How many can say they are a God? Cole: Only like one or two. Dukestar: How many of them are gangsta’s? Cole: Five or Six? Dukestar: President? Cole: One. Dukestar: You see kid there is only ONE gangsta Goth God and that is me. Not Jon Kano, Rush, Statues Quo, Snow, Akiyama, none of them. Just me. I’m the only one who wears old black jeans and boots made of brass and who has kicked a whole lot of ass, smoked some grass, but never been out classed. I've been up and down, both sides of the town. Heel and face you know the facts. Cole: So what keeps you motivated if your so perfect? Dukestar: Guess there's only one thing that keeps a hoss like me going. Gets my attention right now. Well, you know what I'm talking about, a pretty green eyed ho in goth cloths and a gold strap around my waist. It makes my heart beat fast, kid and gets the Malice juices flowing…hoss…flowing!! Cole I don't put up with no crap, like I said kid I’ve been there, done that. Cole: Ready for some word association. Dukestar: Pussy!!! Cole: I’ll take that as a yes. Dukestar: If you ever said no I’d have a few questions for you hoss. Cole: Tyler Nelson Dukestar: Who’s his daddy??? The front office. Cole: Jason Snow. Dukestar: Prison bitch. Once you become the culture the culture controls you. Thoughts, dreams, goals, wishes, wants, all come from concrete and gray walls. No man can over come that. Not even a talented prison bitch like Jason Snow. Listen to Johnny Cash’s Folsom Prison Blues and you’ll understand. Cole: Tim Walden Dukestar: My hommie in da hood. Great talent and a star. Cole: Akiyama Dukestar: A god but not quite as good of a good as me. Sad really. Plus the big bitch don’t talk. Not even a peep. What kind of God is that? He was a great like Charlie Chaplin but his time is long gone. New gods like me are moving in. I speak and am a bad ass so that makes me Pacino. Cole: El Diablo Dukestar: What a fucking joke. Not a good joke either just a real shitty one. Maybe he’s an inside joke and somebody somewhere is laughing cus hoss I ain’t. Cole: Dante Jackson. Dukestar: He’s black right? Black like color not like goth black like me. But I bet I’m more ghetto then his ass. Cole: Rent-A-Hero Dukestar: dipshit Cole: Darkstar Dukestar: Wannabe Dukestar. Not nearly as Goth as me. Cole: Thank you for you time. Dukestar: So long hoss and remember this has been the Honky Tonk Goth God Front Office Presidential Truth. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
