Hippies, Horseshit, N' Holiday Inn

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29.11.02 (#5 FF) Sometimes your hopes…..fears…..dreams, you just have to let them go. In a world where society dominates, and the individual, in an attempt to survive, is forced to bond permanently with another to survive, all natural being, all true humanity, is lost. And indeed, when you conform to the group, to the masses, your soul, your being, may be lost; lost indefinitely. And when the laws of nature, the laws of God, are broken, someone is then needed to enforce the law. Justice must come to society. An individual, an individual, individual that is, must compensate for the needs of society, and indeed, the quest of this person becomes quite a paradox…… An individual, an outcast of society, is forced to save society…by destroying it? Yes, the good of the people as whole is obtained by the serving of justice to the individual person. One by one, each must be treated. One by one, we must all, under God’s grace, regain our individuality. And so, an outcast is called upon by the powers that be. One that might sacrifice his/her ability, and spot, to “fit in” in with society, in order to counter the blow of the masses, and, as consequence, set us free…..saving us in the process. So to enforce the law, one might ask “how do you go about doing this?”. Simple: by breaking it. You see, in order to restore nature’s law, you must destroy man’s. This is the truth. And so be it… And thus, the torch was handed to Abner….. And thus, our adventure begins….. ---------------------------------------- Abner, an ex-con, ex-cult leader, and barely literate hick, is suddenly thrust forward into a position that’s previously unknown to him. Foreign and void it is……and possibly unfriendly…..but unexpected? Yes, that too. And there is no foreseeable solution. But luckily, for Abner’s sake, none is needed. You see, a solution is only needed to cure a problem…..as antidote or antibiotic is to an illness…..but to Abner, there is no problem. You see, Abner may be least where he expected, but he’s right where he wants to be….. IN OTHER WORDS….. Come Friday Fusion, pull out the chew, the ol’ bottle o’ moonshine and the case of shotgun shells, ‘cause hicks, anarchists and prison inmates of America, your fellow outcast is ready to hand out an arse-whoopin’! That’s right, in PCW action, Vermont-born , Abner, takes on the Chinese-born monk, Kai….. But we’re overlooking one very important thing. Abner, is he ready?…..Well, does it matter? You see, Abner, refusing to answer these so blatantly obvious questions, asks one of his own: “Who wants to know?” In other words, popular opinion: the media, professionals, and fan-based organizations, mean nothing to Abner, nothing at all. You see, to Abner, all this talk about Kai, about the experience factor, and about in-ring knowledge, is well, in his own words “horseshit!” Kai may still be a rookie, but at least he’s been in a ring before…..Abner on the other hand, well, he’s never stepped foot in one…..even in training! But as he sees it, and justifiably so, the situation can be summed up in an old saying conjured up in the backwoods of Hicksville, Vt: “You can buy a huntin’ license, or you can go out kill somethin’!” And to Abner, Kai may have bought the license…..may have, but he’ll be damned if he himself is not the one who carries the gun! And here you me, this disturbed young hick is looking down the barrel of his weapon, and Kai is in his sites…...and as you know, Vermont hunters don’t miss their shot…… So with a monk in his sights, and “impure” thoughts in mind, who’s to say what kind of an impact this rookie will make on Friday Fusion…..the world, maybe? Does the world even have a clue? Nah, not in Abner’s book….. ---------------------------------------- “Where is that goddamn hippie anyway?” Abner conducts a mental search in the back of his mind. “Last I knew, all the P-C-Dubers were ta be situated in the same hereabouts……’round here somewhere anyway.” He scratches his head. “Well, guess it just ain’t so…..” Pauses for a brief moment. “All came ‘ere on the same damn plane by-Jesus Christ……well, ‘cept for them snooty bastards……but still, we y’all should be in the same goddamn hotel…..or at least same frigin area for God’s sake! Ah well! Lame corporate bastards; can’t expect nothin’ outta’ them. Never do anything right, I guess. But no matter, ‘cause come hell or high water, on Fusion, Kai’s ass is mine! And a speakin’ a Fusion…..Fuse my goddamn foot up his monkity-monkey-munk ass!” Coming out of the hotel room, Abner lets out a sigh. His gray tee-shirt moves in and out, copying the movements of the firm chest. His large, loose, brown, leather steeled-toes clud heavily on the floor as he trudges on down the hallway. Abner is looking for Kai, and he’s not in a very good mood…… Clumsily, a rough rhythmic pattern slowly works itself out as his blue jeans flap up against the top of his roughly worn footwear. Brushing aside his thick brown hair, and quickly running his fingers through his rough-cut “makeshift” goatee, Abner steps up to a set of doors: an awaiting elevator. After pressing a smell red button with a black “down” symbol, the thick gray doors give way. Abner steps into the confines of the empty, open elevator…… Good…..empty….. He lets out a sigh of relief: he’s alone. Publicly, a natural-born “people person”, privately, Abner only wished to be left alone. To him, in solitude comes happiness. And can you blame him? ‘Cause you know…..well, people do kind of “suck”, right? So the thankful hick lets a small, but merely temporary grin sew its way into the fabrics of his skin. Peace, peace at last. Well, sort of. His deep green eyes flash in a mischievous stream of delight as he pushes another elevator button. What’s he up to? Let’s wait and see. And so now, Abner’s going down…..down, down, down. Down the elevator shaft that is; quite the opposite of the direction he’ll be heading come Friday. Yeah, that’s right, Kai’s a dead man….. Anyway, the steady motion and the gentle buzz of the descending compartment seems to comfort this usually very intense character. For a brief moment in his life, he smiles. This is well. For in little or no time at all, comfort will hold a second-class passenger’s seat on the bumpy ride of this man’s life. I’ll find ‘em, I’ll find ‘em…..I’ll get ‘em….. And they’re off. But where?….. Looks like that’ll be all she wrote. The elevator comes to grinding stop. Even in such a well-maintained hotel, things of great value still seem to crumble and decay in Abner’s presence. But it’s not his fault. Maybe that’s the way society is supposed to be: you know, in tatters. So quickly regaining his senses, Abner bolts out of the elevator, only a step ahead of the others waiting to board. He then turns and intently tries to scan their faces. Damn. No such luck, for none of them can help him. Not one of them is Kai, and not one is even remotely affiliated with PCW. “Horseshit!” he mutters under his breath as he strolls forward toward the main lobby. Casually, casting a glance at his surroundings, he also finds this area desolate. No sign of PCW life here either. The room, quite ugly, is seemingly decorated by the dullness of a corporate hotel chain hand: plain, simple…..and extremely cheap. Abner can’t stand the thought of men so powerful, with so much money, that are somehow so cheap that they barely manage to furnish they’re own property properly!…..Or simply aren’t willing to spend much on it….. Still, no one of importance in sight. But ah well, Abner can deal with it……for now. “By-God, if I didn’t know any better, I’d bet my Bud that that hippie-son-va-whore, Kai, is afraid to show his goddamn face in public! That yellow-bellied monk just may own a bean or two!….. But no fuss, ‘cause he’ll get his soon ‘nuf. See, soon as I get up a mind to go up there and meet that boy in the ring, I’ll show him a thing or two.” Digging deep into his soul, “I’ll show him why I’m the only guy at D-29 to never get corn-holed! Yep, that Asian’s one dead deer…..er….an Asian deer that is…...” Mumbling by this point, “…..Or somethin’…..” And then Abner finds himself just where he wants to be: the front desk of the Holiday Inn’s management department. Looking up from his desk, a tired young man unveils his face from a pile of papers. Smiling, he begins peering intently at his new visitor. And with his undivided attention, Abner does the same. Well if it ain’t a hippie….. The man is not quite of the Abner “breed”, and as a matter of fact, quite the opposite. His main attraction: a full head of long, black hair pulled back in a neatly tied bun, that’s gently sloped over his shoulders. This greasily pasted object stands in sharp contrast to the young man’s almost Abner-like goatee. Not nearly as neatly cut, the beard and mustache seem off color from the man’s hair. The curly black strands are uneven and tangled, and on some level, remind Abner of men he knew from childhood. But there’s no time for daydreaming…. Abner also notices a sharp pair of twinkling brown eyes that stare through a sophisticated set of glasses. A smile coming to his face, the attendant dutifully responds to the wrestler’s presence. “Good evening sir, what can I do for you?” Rudely, Abner lets out an unrestrained sigh. Well, for starters, get a haircut hippie. “Well, I was just wonderin’ if Kai was ‘round…..know ‘em?” The young desk clerk does not seem to understand. “Excuse me?” And so, the battle begins….. Muttering under his breath, the irritable hick takes a different approach. “Kai, you know…..a goddamned PCW wrestler roomed here…..know where the son-va-bitch is?” The young man is about to reply, pauses, and then thinking better of it, finally gives out his originally intended response. “I’m sorry sir, but all of our boarded people are guaranteed their privacy…..protected from the public…..” Seeing that that the redneck is obviously unamused, adds “…..And all of their information as well.” Abner still doesn’t seem to comprehend this. The desk attendant is starting to become agitated. “Company policy. I’m sorry sir…..” And then pausing for a moment, “…..Is there anything else I can do for you?” That’s horseshit! The PCWer takes a step forward, pushes out his chest, and leans over the nervous young desk attendant. His purpose: to intimidate. Unfortunately for Abner though, his six-foot-one, two-hundred-twenty-seven-pound frame doesn’t quite get the job done. So instead of wasting time, he begins speak…..a more diplomatic approach. “Yes, yes there is…..” The attendant holds his breath, anxiously awaiting the hick to finish off his statement.. “…..You can tell me where that goddamned monk, Kai is!” The attendant, obviously used to the situation, calmly, but obviously nervously, attempts to restore order. “I’m sorry sir, but as I stated earlier, I simply am not allowed to do that.” Trying to redeem himself, “Company policy, not mine…..sir…..I’m sorry, but there’s really nothing I can do.” Now Abner, fuming in the brain, grits his teeth together in determination. Poison is in his thoughts, his blood, and soon, his tongue….. “If you don’t tell me where that monk is, I swear to God, I’ll rip through every goddamned door in this entire shit’ole of your’s ‘til I find that bastard’s room myself! And then I…..and then I….and then I’ll mess up his room too! An’ if he ain’t here, I’ll get that monk yet….um…..uh…..see, I’ll smash through every damn synagogue in El Paso, if that’s what it takes, damnit!” So take that! Obviously giving up on the now-hopeless situation, the young man quickly gets up out of his chair. This aggressive action does not go unnoticed by Abner. The desk attendance is obviously a little heated. Rolling his eyes, he fails at giving a clam response “Sir, I cannot, I repeat, cannot give you that information….and…..and furthermore, I cannot allow you to damage private property…And, just for the record…..monks belong in monasteries, so you’re “monk” must be a Jew.” An enraged Abner slams his fist onto the desk, catching the young man unawares, and sends him flying back into his seat. Staring wild-eyed into the man’s face, Abner too gives up all hope of avoiding conflict, and sets to work on what he does best: fighting….. “What!?” He reaches up like a madman and repeatedly slaps himself across the head. “What!?…..What!?…...How dare you call a PCW wrestler such a shameful term! A “Jew”, A “Jew” huh?…..” The confused young attendant doesn’t seem to understand the basis of this remark. “But I uh….uh…..um, but I didn’t mean to…..um….uh….” And then snapping out of it, “Excuse me?” “Ha!” Abner starts, “You sit there all nice and pirdy and pretend not to know what you just said, eh? Well, shame on you!” “But….” “No! Shame on you!” “But…..” “Uh-uh, shadup!” Abner begins to point his finger at the accused, yet innocent, man. “Shame, shame, shame on you!” The man blinks his eyes…..and then Abner makes his move….. Suddenly, seizing the opportunity, Abner reaches down and swiftly grabs up a pile of papers sitting on the corner of the desk. He quickly attempts to justify his actions. “Fine. If you won’t give me the room number, than by-God, I’ll find it myself!” Still not completely recovered from the last moment of confusion, the man is slow to reply, and even slower to react. “Ss…ss….ss-ss-sir! You can’t take those!” He finally begins to regain a little bit of control…..enough that is. And in protest, the attendant lunges forward at the stack of papers, reaches out with a determined hand, and latches on to something. Unfortunately, he’s grabbed the PCWer’s hand. Surprised by this, Abner, with his instinctive reflexes, wastes no time to retaliate. But then, knowing what’s at stake, Abner’s cautious enough not to be too physically aggressive. Simply pulling back, Abner recomposes himself in an intentional defensive position. “You goddamn tree huggin’ hippie! Get yer environmentally friendly paws off a me!” In a spur of the moment reaction, the attendant bursts forward and again attempts to reclaim the papers, and again only manages in finding the wrestler’s hand….but this time, the results are not so forgiving. Almost boiling, the hick makes no attempt to disguise his native Vermont accent. “Stupid ‘ippie ‘ore….howdaya like me now, ah!?” The puzzled attendant doesn’t have to wait long to find out what this question meant…… With a mighty shove, Abner knocks the attendant backward. The poor guy crash-lands in his chair, and tips it over in the process. And when it’s all said and done, he finds himself sprawled out across the floor, momentarily at the mercy of the enraged hick. But Abner merely lets out a satisfactory grunt, and then calmly begins to collect the pile of papers. This takes him a few valuable seconds though: mainly due to the fact that they’re now strewn out across the desk. Finally, he’s got them all and prepares to make his escape. But his ego gets in his way….. Turning back to the fallen victim, “How do you like ‘at, hippie?” But suddenly, quickly recuperating, the attendant somehow manages to get to his feet. Wasting no time, he rushes forward and dives for a particular spot on the desk. He manages to hit his intended target (a small red button), and within milliseconds, the security alarm goes off….. Hahaha….. Unknowingly, the attendant has just done Abner a favor…..but he doesn’t know it at the time….. Within moments, they’re surrounded by a handful of uniformed guards. With PCW in the house, security has been tripled, and reinforced by some PCW’s own. Instinctively, they rush toward the larger Abner and prepare restrain the wrestler. “Manager, manager…..I demand to see the manager!” And then Abner gets an idea….. This sudden outburst seems to unsettle the guards, and for a moment, they froze in place. But not for long. “Security, security…..this man attacked me…..I….I….I demand, demand that you take him away at once…and….” Abner shrugs, raises his eyes in confusion and pleads, “But….but….” But it’s too late. Security finally have their minds made up. Better safe than sorry, they cautiously approach the Vermonter. Shouting at the top of his lungs, Abner pleads for help. “Manager, manager! MANAGEEEEER!” The security guards, six in total, can quickly, but not easily, subdue their foe. Moving forward in a steady mass of black shirts will yellow lettering reading “SECURITY”, it looks as if Abner’s about to be overtaken by a group of killer bees. And so the question is, does Abner have the Raid?” Shit! Shit! Shit! God, don’t let me do this….. But then, right at the last second, the seemingly inevitable bloodshed is prevented. Backed into a corner, help has finally arrived, and the entrapped wrestler is no longer forced into making such unpleasant decisions….. “What’s the meaning of this!” A small Latino man dressed in an expensive black suit comes into view. Spreading his arms, the hotel manager shoves his way through security (though not without resistance) and bravely steps up to Abner. Making eye-contact, the two quickly size each other up. And before either can speak, the desk attendance rushes forward. Reaching his boss’s side, he points over at Abner. “Sir, sir, this man is a monster! He attacked me, and stole my papers….and….and…and…” Raising an authoritative hand, the main softly but firmly commands his employee, “Enough.” And so, obediently, the attendance lets out a sigh and stands back. The manager than turns to Abner. “And you, what do you have to say for you-self?”, no longer hiding his foreign accent. Abner has already prepared his speech. In a confident tone, he starts “Your….desk attendant here….well…..he’s…..he’s a biggot!” “Excuse me?” The Hispanic cocks his head to the side. “He…he….well, see….I just come down for a drink a water…..ya know, ‘cause I was trainin’ real hard….and then…and then I decided I’d stop by to find out where one of mah friends was stayin’…..ya know, just because. But then, this here fella refused to tell me where my friend were…...and I was just about to walk away, I swear it…..when he outta blue called a fellow PCW wrestler, and a personal friend of mine…..a…..a….a Jew!” The manager is taken aback. Then, turning to his employee, he unleashes a bombardment of curses in Spanish. “But….but sir…..I didn’t mean it like that….he….he…he said that his friend was a monk….” “Michael!” …..An…..and then he said….” “I’m ashamed of you! After all these years….” “….and then….and then….well….” “So I have no other choice…..you’ve left me with no other options…..” “….The…..the whole bit about the synagogue….” “You’re FIRED!” “……….” Not giving him time to speak, the manager quickly grabs the poor, innocent young man by the ear, and then tugging forward, drags him toward security. Angrily, “Security! Take this man away! I want him out, out, out! Out of my sight! Now!” They pause momentarily, thinking over the situation. “Now!” Without flinching, they suddenly turn on the man and begin dragging him away….. And off into the distance he goes: kicking, screaming and crying. “You can’t do this to me…..I still work here! I’ll…..I’ll…..I’ll…..I’ll sue you!! Do you here me, Rick, I’ll sue you!…..” Bing. Abner gets another idea. Sue him, huh?” And even before he can speak, the manager is on his knees. “Oh, sir! I’m so sorry….sooo incredibly sorry! I assure you, it won’t happen again…..and…..and…..and as for him, he’ll never step foot in this Holiday Inn, or any other, again!” Literally pleading with the hick, the manager grovels at his feet. “I’ll give you anything! Anything! Just name it…..but please, please don’t sue me sir! I….I really try to run a nice, clean hotel….I….I really do! And I swear it, name your price, and I’ll do anything…..but I beg you, please, please, please don’t take this to court…..I’m sure we can work something out…..” “Reaaaaly?” Abner scratches his chin in thought…..---------------------------------------- And so, from that time on, PCW and all of its affiliates stay free, that’s right, absolutely free of charge at any Holiday Inn they please. And that may not be a big deal to the snooty big-timers, but hey, to the rookies, it’s a dream come true: more company cash in their wallets….. But as for Kai, well, we’ll just have to wait and see…..
