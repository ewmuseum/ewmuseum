...To Persist...DEVILISH...1/2

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Persistence is ever vigilant… Duke’s determined to defeat Akiyama… Determined… One would think that all his hopped up conversations with “Mary Jane” would have gone noticed by the authorities by now…last time it was checked, that stuff was still illegal in the US. Shows yet again the lapse of morality within humanity these days. It’s a shame…so much potential for a species, and it’s going all down the drain for personal pleasure… =©= The scene opened upon the city, as always… zooming in further, we open onto a shopping mall crowded with people. The mortals…as we know them…scurried back and forth like ants to their labors. It was a big mall…no doubt about that…more than four levels of consumer greatness. In the center of the vast mall stood a huge marble fountain. A platoon of cherubs stand poised with their trumpets, blowing out water into the pond below. Amidst the trickling of streams of water…the melodic voice of the Divine Prophetess could be heard. =©= Cyclonica: It’s places like this that make me wish I was still in captivity. =©= Standing on the other side of the fountain was Cyclonica and Akiyama, both dressed in their usual attire; Akiyama in his black vinyl pants, black mesh shirt, black tape, black shades, and black boots, while Cyclonica in her black vinyl pants, ice blue PVC shirt, black mesh skintight over shirt, black knee high heeled boots, and her ice blue shades. They both leaned against the edge of the fountain looking strangely out of place in that atmosphere. =©= Cyclonica: You see this place, Akiyama? Swarms of mortals come here to fulfill their desire through material possessions…how ignorant. Money cannot buy you happiness. No…let me catch myself before Duke comes along and makes a “dumb, redneck spin of words” from my dialogue. What would he say? Oh yes… =©= Cyclonica cleared her throat as she tried her best to put on a Duke voice. =©= Cyclonica: (In bad Duke Wlliams voice) “Money can’t buy you happiness…but it can get you an ounce of weed, a pint of JD, and an hour with Keisha…and you had better be a happy hoss after all that or you’ll be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND!” =©= Akiyama looked down at her slowly, his right eyebrow raised slightly from behind his sunglasses. Cyclonica shrugged her shoulders as her emotionless face came back into play. =©= Cyclonica: But that’s just an assumption…and Duke’s the king of them, as we know by now. With him assuming that we’re making assumptions off his assumptions…he’s sounding like a total jackass. Hypocritical…yeah…sure…whatever…we know what we’re doing and what we’re saying; unlike he does once he climbs on “Mr. Daniels’s wild ride”. Spin doctors aren’t anything but whacked out idiots that think everything can be turned towards their favor by tossing in some jargon they think is fact. Here’s a fact: The Laundromat called…they said he’ll be washed up by the end of Friday night! =©= She looked over to the nVg and snapped her fingers. =©= Cyclonica: But now let’s be on our way. Let’s do what we came here to do and get the hell out of here. =©= They pushed themselves off away from the fountain edge and prepared to walk away, but then a small group of teenagers dressed in black with pale skin and metal jewelry approach them. =©= Goth: Hey…do you mind if we hang out with you? =©=Cyclonica glared down at the Goth teen in wide legged bondage pants and black Slayer shirt. =©= Cyclonica: …Why? Slayer Goth: Because…you two look hella good. =©=Cyclonica stared down at the pale Goths for a few seconds. Then she turned to the nVg slowly. =©= Cyclonica: Akiyama? =©= The nVg lets out a growl as he stomped towards them with his arms raised up to strike. The Goths scattered off in a cry of fear as the god took two steps forward. Cyclonica nodded as Akiyama glared off at them. =©= Cyclonica: Ratty Goths…come along, Akiyama. =©= The two Celestials walked through the plaza, scattering the pedestrians as they started climbing the escalator. =©= Cyclonica: And people wonder why we’re in such foul dispositions… it’s because we’re stuck in this hell hole where all these humans live and crap. It’s frickin purgatory in here. Duke’s asking us about why we’re even here and why we’re interacting with these foul creatures. He acts like we have a choice. He needs to learn that there are some things in this world that he will not understand…including US…so he should just say “F^ck it!” and go off and smack his sack. =©= A sigh came back from the god as he listened to the Divine Prophetess go on. =©= Cyclonica: He’s so much of a non-believer in all that is religious…that’s no problem because there’s no denomination or religion for the nVg. We have the good end of the stick. I’m sure that if Duke was to hear our views on religion…we’d be such good friends. =©= Stepping off the elevator, they headed walked past Waldenbooks, where a cutout of Tim Walden stood with a book in hand labeled “Swearing for F^cking Dummies”. They snatched up a copy of PCW Weekly (via shrinkage…shoplifting for you dumb@ss mortals), and walked on without even breaking stride. Cyclonica flipped through the reading material as they pressed on…finally stopping before a Radio Shack. =©= Cyclonica: Look at this crap. =©= She turned to the god and held up the cover of the magazine…which coincidentally had a picture of Duke “Malice Man” Williams in all his cowboy-hat wearing glory. He sat on a black horse in the middle of a wrestling ring. The headline… “MALICE MAN RIDES AGAIN.” Akiyama stared into the picture as Cyclonica held it before him. Cyclonica: This is the man you’re to defeat Friday. Duke Williams…you’ve seen him before jibbering his mouth over at the announce table with Jeff Marx and Dave Kern. He’s the one always yelling “VOID BANISHMENT, HOSS!!” and whatever. Now…we need to analyze this man. Let’s look at him…pot head…that can’t be it, because OSW’s Aimz was a pot head, and she was successful for a point. Alcoholic…Scott Hall was an alcoholic…and he’s pretty much done now in wrestling. But there’s still people like Austin and the Acolytes who are drinkers and still function. =©= Akiyama looked off to one of the televisions in the display window of Radio Shack. It was showing a scene with Stone Cold and Debra sitting backstage in their dressing room. The blonde in her leather Rattlesnake attire and Austin in his trademark shorts and black t-shirt sat in the same chair, the wife in the lap of the husband. Akiyama turned back to the magazine and stared harder. =©= Cyclonica: Is it the cowboy hat? Does it give him like a public appeal of miscreantic tomfoolery? Or does it just make him look like Curly from City Slickers? =©= Akiyama looked back at the television, where Debra was rubbing Austin’s chin. Then he looked back at the magazine. Then he looked over to Cyclonica. She was rubbing her chin in contemplation. =©= Cyclonica: I hope it’s not the stupidity thing…if so humanity’s worse off than I imagined. And it’s not PR that’s pushing him into facedom...we’re not going back down the whole Public Relations route with Seraphina Jameson again. Then what could it be?? =©= Akiyama took one more look around the vicinity. Debra wrapped her arms around her husband with a smile. =©= Debra: Oh, Austin, you’re so rugged. =©= Akiyama looked over to Cyclonica, who was still rubbing his chin. Then he tapped her on her shoulder. She looked up at him quickly. =©= Cyclonica: What is it? I’m thinking here… =©= Akiyama turned the magazine around and showed her the picture of Duke Williams. Her solid dark eyes widened as she came to a realization. =©= Cyclonica: THE BEARD!!! =©=She looked behind Akiyama, where the group of Goths were sneaking up on the two slowly, trying to act inconspicuous, loitering around against the walls and looking aloof. Cyclonica shook her fist at them with an angry face. =©= Cyclonica: BEGONE, PESKY GOTHS!!! AKIYAMA!!! =©= The god smashed his hand into the Radio Shack display window and palmed a TV set in his hands. Then he chucked it at the small, frail Goths, who ran off like girls screaming with tears running down their faces smearing their makeup. Akiyama roared at them as he gave chase, leaving Cyclonica standing there thinking amidst the carnage. =©= Cyclonica: I just so happen to know the perfect person to involve into our little experiment….yes…and this will be a Divine thing once actions have been made. So says the Divine Prophetess! =©= She sighed as a terrible scream came from further ahead. The crowd of people formed behind her all winced in pain. =©= Cyclonica: Watch for the spiked collars…they really tear into the skin when you choke them, Akiyama! Geez…snotty gothic sissies… =©=
