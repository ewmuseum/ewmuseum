New Year...New Change

9 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th January Due to technical difficulties on Tuesday's Fusion, the following segment wasn't seen on-air. Here is the whole excerpt in full. The arena is suddenly covered in darkness and there are a few whistles and faint cheers from the crowd. Jeff: Darkness...wonderful darkness! PFIK: You were locked in the basement as a child, weren't you Marx? Dave: As far as my memory serves me, only one person in the entire PCW/RUSH roster enters the arena in darkness Moments later, the now infamous countdown begins to a negative reaction from the fans Zehn...Neun...Acht...Sieben... Dave: I don't want to say I told you so... Jeff: Well don't, just sit back and bask in the glory of the German language! Sechs...Funf...Vier... PFIK: We'd all be talking German now if it wasn't... Jeff (interupting): Yeah great...write a book Drei...Zwei...AUS! Jeff: Get ready for das booooooooom!!! Dave: It's amazing how that sentence doesn't annoy me. Jeff: Heh. Dave: I didn't say that you don't annoy me, Jeff. There's a short silence as the booing continues and the arena is still in darkness. Dave: I wou... The exploding fireworks at the entranceway cut off Dave in mid-sentence as Rammestein's 'Sonne' blares into the arena. Jeff: Yeah...yeah...rock...on! PFIK: You are simply an idiot, Marx Moments later, the RUSH Network Champion Matt Hunt soon appears at the entrance way to a chorus of booing from the locals. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Dave: I actually think he loves to be jeered and booed. Jeff: Well, why would he want to be cheered? It's easy if people cheer you, but if they boo you...then you know you're doing your job as a 'baddie'!! PFIK: Baddie? This isn't some 1980's Viacom cartoon, you know. Hunt walks out dressed in a smart three-piece blue suit and holds the folded up title belt in his right hand. He glances behind him and soon enough, his five-man security workforce march out to some continued heat. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Jeff: Das Bundeswehr!! Dave: For those of you who don't know, The Bundeswehr is actually the German Army! Jeff: No shit, Sherlock. PFIK: I see that Hunt used all his creative power to give his lackeys that moniker. Instead of walking down the aisleway and towards the ring, Hunt stops at the top of the entrance way, while his Bundeswehr line up horizontally behind him. Dave: I have to say that I wouldn't like to cross this collection of Germans, what with that shaved head and aggressive demeanour. Jeff: You really are being Mr Obvious tonight, eh Kern? PFIK: He's got a point, but this also shows that Hunt is nothing more than a cowardly sell-out. Getting five big men to watch his back...pfft. Jeff: Watch his back? What are you on about? They are the Front Office security, you moron! As the house lights soon reappear, Hunt scans into the audience with that smug grin on his face. Crowd: ASSHOLE! ASSHOLE! ASSHOLE! Hunt holds his hand up while proceeding to talk. Matt Hunt: Meine Damen und Herren...bitte... Dave: Here he goes with the German again. Jeff: That's because he IS German! Matt Hunt: People, people...is there any need for such negativity? That comment only seems to aggravate them Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!! Matt Hunt: I mean, I look around this scheiBeplatz and I see so many angry faces! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! Dave: What do you expect? Matt Hunt: Is it because this Resistance fotze aren't really putting up much of a fight? PFIK: As far as I could see at the conclusion of Adrenaline, they sure as hell are. Matt Hunt: Or is it because you're angry with yourselves and rather than take it out on yourself, you'd rather direct this pathetic tirade at everybody's favourite German? Dave: Heh, that's actually pretty funny. Jeff: Tongue in cheek, Kern, tongue in cheek. Matt Hunt: You're angry with yourself because you are surrounded by dismay and there's nothing you can do about it. Your morals and family values are so low, that you'd sell your own children down the river for a quick sale. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!! ASSHOLE! ASSHOLE! ASSHOLE! Dave: Where the hell did that come from? PFIK: Nothing that this moron says surprises me anymore. Jeff: The truth hurts and that's why these idiots are booing. Matt Hunt: That's right, you would sell your own children and further more, I wouldn't be surprised if the majority of you are from a broken home. You see, that's what makes you angry, not to mention unstable. So you come to these wrestling events and your vent your personal anger at guys like Xavier Kannon, Tyler Valentine, Cassuis Deamont, etc, etc, etc. Dave: Hunt really is laying it on pretty thick tonight. PFIK: He's getting way too personal. Matt Hunt: It's not our fault that your family is full of selfish individuals who care about nothing and nobody but themselves. You only cheer and get wet for this resistance rubbish, because you feel that they represent the goodness of today's society, fighting the good fight and standing up for what they believe in. Jeff: He's got a point. Dave: That's exactly what the Resistance are doing! Matt Hunt: Well just because there's no harmony in your broken home, doesn't mean that you can cheer the next thing that is totally the opposite of your own pathetic situation to make you feel good about yourselves. PFIK: Where exactly is Hunt going with this? Dave: That's what I'd like to know. Matt Hunt: Lack of respect...that's what it's all about. For yourself, for your own morals and for your family. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! Matt Hunt: Take the Resistance for example. They don't work as a collective unit, like a strong bond. Every single one of them wants to be a hero and they're displaying their individual selfishness in the process and that's the core of their downfall. Dave: What total crap! Matt Hunt: But the Front Office work together as a family and we are systematically breaking down this resistance day-by-day. Jeff: Oh yes they are! PFIK: Pfft, don't believe the crap. Matt Hunt: This is why I am so much a better person than any of you will ever be. I have respect and I give it where's due. I have morals and I use them intelligently. I also have a family that showed to me that they are there for each other and the strong blood bond continues to grow. PFIK: He's not going to introduce us to some crazed cousin, is he? Matt Hunt: While I was recuperating from my injured back in my homeland, I realised this display of unity in the Hunt family and it inspired me. Dave: He's gonna leave to return to his family? Matt Hunt: It inspired me to take a look at myself and realise that I don't show enough love and respect to my loved ones. Jeff: This is beautiful; I'm going to cry. PFIK: This is pathetic; I'm going to puke. Matt Hunt: I've decided to show this newfound respect and make a change...an important change. Dave: Now this is getting interesting. Matt Hunt: I have decided to go back to my roots; go back to the two people who brought me into this world. Hunt pauses and motions as if he's going to break down in tears, he manages to 'compose' himself and continues. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!! Matt Hunt: Firstly, the name 'Matt' is a shortened version of my birth name 'Matthias' and from this moment on, I will use this name. Jeff: Eh? PFIK: Sounds more like a German rentboy. Matt Hunt: Kuntz, pronounced Kur-ntz, is my Mother's maiden name and to show respect and to honour my Mother's family, I will use this name from this moment forward. Crowd: CUNT! CUNT! CUNT! CUNT! Jeff: Hey, show some respect you idiots! Dave: I thought 'cunt' is a British term. I know what it means, but you don't often hear it on this side of the world. PFIK: Heh, I know what it means! Hunt is about to continue, but notices the sudden chant from the fans. He stares into the crowd and shakes his head in disgust. Matt Hunt: Yeah, you people really have a lack of respect. Dave: It maybe is a little harsh, but I don't know what Hunt really expected. Matt Hunt: Finally, I will continue to use my Father's family 'Hunt' and just like my Mother's family, I will show the respect needed by using this name from this day forth. Jeff: No more Matt Hunt? Dave: Nope, I believe it's... Matt Hunt: So now I will call myself - Matthias Kuntz-Hunt! Hunt holds his arms aloft in delight, which provokes the fans. Crowd: BOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! ASSHOLE!! CUNT! ASSHOLE! Dave: Do we still refer to him as Hunt? Matt Hunt: I've had with you people... Hunt uncharacteristically drops the mic and motions for his Bundeswehr to return backstage, which prompts cheering from the capacity crowd. Crowd: YEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!! Dave: Where's he going? Jeff: Now see what these morons have done! PFIK: Temper tantrum!! The Bundeswehr follow the instruction and march off the stage with Hunt himself soon following. The delighted fans continue to cheer as Turmoil cuts to a commercial. COMMERICAL! We return from the break and there's a camera backstage. Moments later, the Bundeswehr are seen marching across the screen with an infuriated Matthias Kuntz-Hunt behind him, cursing the locals under his breath. Dave: We're back folks and it's seems that Hunt is... Jeff: He's pissed off, Dave. Wouldn't you be? PCW reporter Jake Smith has a microphone and rushes up to the ticked off German. Jake: Matt...Matt...just a quick word... Hunt stops and stares at Smith with an angered expression, he then barks down the mic. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: HALT! Smith leaps back in shock as the Bundeswehr stop marching and hold their position. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: Did you not hear me? Jake: I..err... Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: It's not 'Matt' anymore, you mooch. It's Matthias...or Herr Kuntz-Hunt to you. Dave: So he really is changing his name! Jeff: Well he didn't say it for a bet! PFIK: Could have fooled me! Jake: Erm...Herr Kuntz-Hunt, it seems that the people here in Washington have hit a nerve. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: What do you expect? Here I am showing my respect and honouring my roots and all these scheiBekopfs can do is hurl more abuse. It's disgusting! Jake: So with this new name change, how... Matthias Kuntz-Hunt(interupting): How do you refer to me? Well first off, it better not be 'cunt' or I'll rip off your head and shit down your neck. PFIK: Now that would be interesting to see! Dave: He really has grasped the concept of the English language very well! There's an uncomfortable silence before Kuntz-Hunt continues. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: You can refer to me as Matthias; Herr Kuntz-Hunt or plainly Hunt...as I know that most North Americans are too lazy to pronounce a name with a hyphen. Jeff: Isn't that true, Kern? Jake pulls the mic back and is about the end the interview, when Hunt grabs it and pulls it back towards his face. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: I'm not finished... Jake: Sorry... Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: And of course, not be forgetting...Das Dangerous Technik, DDT! Seeing as I am the best damn athlete to have ever come out of Germany and the best RUSH Network Champion of all time! Hunt then storms off and walks past his work crew before barking in German again. Matthias Kuntz-Hunt: SCHELL FUSS...EINS...ZWEI...DREI! The Bundeswehr follow the order and continue their march as Hunt storms alongside them. Dave: So we can call him Matthias; Herr Kuntz-Hunt, Hunt and DDT? PFIK: Too many alias' for my liking, I'll stick to calling him Hunt...maybe DDT. Dave: I'll follow your lead. Jeff: Typical.
