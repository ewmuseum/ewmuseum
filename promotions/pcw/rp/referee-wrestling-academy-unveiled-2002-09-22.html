Referee Wrestling Academy Unveiled

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 - 09 - 02 As though a moment of de ja vu surfaced, when a purple hearse drove into an unoccupied parking space before the PWA Training Complex. Parked in the midst of prestige the hearse's purring engine fizzled out, not that anyone would notice with the deafening roar of a cluster of musical artists all played over the sound systems of the neighbouring vehicles. As the compound of drumming persists both driver and passenger doors of the funeral car swing open in unison to unveil the conniving collective known as Pranks With Attitude. Gaz 'The Jester' Green brushes his hand down over the velvet fabric of his three-piece business suit before drawing his hand up towards his face to adjust his sunglasses, catching the glare of the sun in the process. Nerezza however opts to cover up with a hooded coat that drapes across his vampiric face and other exposed body parts as they embark on yet another devious caper. 'I remember the days of originality in the academy but now everybody seems to be....' groaned the Jester, his sentence cut-short by the accompanying behemoth Nerezza. 'A GANGSTER!!!' hissed the Angel Of Fear, protecting his face as both members stepped hotfooted across the open top car park and through the glass panel doors, Nerezza's reflection stranded in limbo. 'Talking of gangsters, what happened to that Angelo Deville, didn't he once beat you?' questioned the Emerald Menace as he looked upon his comrade, who retorts his curiosity with a snarl and a short showcase of teeth. 'A distant memory... A thought lost over time but forever will scare you kinder like your first encounter with Charlotte!!!' replied the Living Nightmare as both sides of his mouth curled up into an arc. Solemnly the duo walked into the reception area, a neatly furbished area, rather inviting. An attractive receptionist hides behind her computer monitor, head down into some serious grafting as her nimble fingers waltz over the keyboard peering up momentarily she lays eyes upon the trickster team. She stands erect to greet her advancing guests allowing the roaming eyes of Gaz Green to wander over her slender body as she plays with her flowing blonde hair in a flirtatious manner causing the Jester to almost foam at the mouth. 'How can I be of service?' said a sweet, innocent voice belonging to the receptionist. As Nerezza ushered forward, his fellow Asylum member cut-in with a subtle side step. 'I can think of a few services that may need your assistance sweet cheeks?!?' retorted the Lord Of Laughter, eyes wide and glassy as though he had witnessed Christmas Day through a child. The receptionist playfully smiles simply to amuse her newfound admirer and Gaz Green reacts by leaning forward to whisper sweet nothings but his efforts are thwarted by the titanic frame of his colleague who squeezed in on the proceedings with a firm shoulder. 'We're here to see Scott Taylor concerning one of his students?' bellowed the Spawn Of Evil. 'Err-... Well-... Mr. Taylor is away on Wrestle Tattler business, Can I take a message?' asked the dizzy blonde, grasping a pen firmly, almost teasing the comic into submission. 'Well its lucky for us that Mr. Taylor as you put it has shown us around this establishment so you wouldn't mind if we simply go about our business?' cried Nerezza with an authoritive tone; pivoting on his foot he embarks unto the bowels of the building unbeknownst to his trailing partner. Pushing open a door they stand motionless before the elevator, almost having heart failure, well Gaz anyhow at the sudden 'Ping' of the doors sliding apart and both step into the booth, pressing the button assigned to the basement they descend. The journey eventually comes to a halt in the midst of hardcore elements, barbed wire fencing, wooden tables and chairs, most notably was the blood curdling screams from those students undertaking a dark gimmick as the hall stretched ahead with minimum visibility. 'The sound of bliss!' murmured a nostalgic vampire. Gaz remained wary, his eyes frantically swaying back and forth as they headed towards the military compound. Again the cries of men whaled through the corridors, almost drawing a smile from Nerezza whilst Gaz could only creep in the darkness, a little on edge. The towering figure of Nerezza leant over to browse over the commando's indulging in various exercises, some were midway through an assault course, some trained on nearby weight equipment and the rookies were made to absorb the surrounding information. 'How this place has changed, I mean Hessian was a hard teacher, he dismantled us and re-assembled us into what we're today but this....' whispered the Prince Of Pranks before an untimely interruption by his co-worker. 'THIS IS WONDERFUL!!! The future of tutoring' Gaz simply nodded his approval sarcastically, anything to keep the peace really. 'Where to next squire?' said the inquisitive Jester. 'We shall keep to the same path laid before us by Scott Taylor!' retorted the goliath, shadowing the velvet prankster in a stance of intimidation. Again Gaz simply nodded and they furthered their progress by reaching the Nursery where the Neanderthal antics of the students have created a scene of chaos, with furniture tossed aside and debris littering the floor. 'Hmm, Nez, Err-... It appears we have a situation, where's the EvaNerezzion?' stuttered Gaz Green, holding his head with a look of pure panic splashed across his once jovial face. 'Very good question' The two individuals scarper like a shawl of fish from a predator and run along the halls trying to find their lost equipment but to no avail. The duo rejoin at a flight of stairs leading beyond the basement and down into a concealed chamber, with no other options available they descend under a torrent of creaking from the rusted steps and guard rail. 'I wonder what’s down here, maybe it's Wrestle Tattler's version of Area 51 or the cloning of Xavier Kannon or maybe....' screamed the King Of Komedy as Nerezza cut his speech short. 'Or maybe PCW are developing a brain for you, huh, ever thought of that?' Pranks With Attitude concludes they’re squabbling and find a whole new teaching layout used for the development of referee's. In one class 'Kaiser' Florien Liebig bellows in a absurd French accent various techniques as his students watch in awe as they sport tight stripped shirts with bands of white and blue. 'VIVA LE FRANCE!!!' 'UN - DEUX - TROIS' Embarking further the Asylum collective stumble upon another room, peering through the window they witness a class instructed by Jeremy Johnson, a recent graduate himself. They witness the infamous two count as he screams in bated breath then decide to conclude their journey by roaming the rest of the complex when suddenly the cries of terror filter over the dense silence. Obviously feeling curious they shuffled to the source and to a door marked 'Bob Charlie' the personal referee of the Front Office stable lead by Xavier Kannon. There was a group cowering in a corner, some running with their hands in the air and others fleeing the scene via the open door but the dynamic duo wanted to see the source of this mass hysteria. 'Just as I figure, the EVA NEREZZION!' groaned Nerezza looking upon the carnage of the room, whilst his creation simply continued its handy work by placing its metallic arm through the concrete wall. The robot was destroying anything related to Bob Charlie, his room could be substituted for a junk yard as the EvaNerezzion murmured it's objective with yet another table reduced to wood shavings with nothing but its frame crashing down on the structure. 'Primary objective - Nobody screws with P.W.A' 'Secondary objective - And certainly not some tax evader, called Bob Charlie!' Nerezza looked on with disgust as Gaz 'The Jester' Green beamed a bright red, holding back his tears of laughter. 'DID YOU HAVE ANYTHING TO DO WITH THIS?' yelled the commanding figure of Nerezza. 'Hmm, Err-... Well... Oh, look at the time?' Gaz said as his head tilted down at his forearm, which could be deemed watch-less. The human form of Nerezza began to take shape into it's gruesome reality of a vampire, huge wings sprouted from his broad back and his face simply cringed to brandish his two piercing canines now on show for all to witness. The transformation was almost momentarily but the Jester wasn't going to stick around and see the aftermath of his own demise and so like a track sprinter he fled the scene with his final and concluding comment. 'WHAT WILL THE WESTERN CONSUMERS THINK~!' - To be continued... Maybe -
