Assassination Series Part Seven: Hitting the Bullseye

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/5/03 The rather large stone, boulder effortlessly ploughed through the wall and entered the main complex of the warehouse. The boulder continued to roll through the wall opposite this new hole and consequently ventured out into the streets, undoubtedly causing havoc amongst the people of Brooklyn outside. Blind Rage eyed up the building warily. How the hell did he get the boulder in the warehouse in the first place? He thought to himself idly, as he and Warrior hesitantly stepped through the hole. The warehouse before them was extremely large and in the centre of the room hung Bullseye’s main show piece. The two captured members of Justice Inc, Badger Man and Handbag Warrior. A flare of anger quickly rose deep within the pit of Blind Rage at this sight, but he tried to suppress it. He had to. He had to keep his head and keep cool. Bullseye was a dangerous character. Unstable. Deadly. There would be no room for error in this one and thus he was forced to quell the unbridled rage deep within his being. In essence, this rage would force him to act blindly, and thus lead him to commit actions that were not going to help whatever situation he faced. That was precisely why his name was Blind Rage. From this distance, it was decidedly difficult to tell the exact severity of their team mates condition, but it was quite obvious they were not conscious. Blind Rage quickly scanned the rest of the room in search of the ever illusive Bullseye. He could see no sign of him. His team mates were being used as bait, but that was something they had managed to figure out from day one. Blind Rage sighed. Why was this job never easy? He thought to himself as started to make his way towards Badger Man and Handbag Warrior. He felt a firm hand rest reassuringly upon his shoulder. “What are you doing?” Warrior asked, with a concerned look chiselled on his face. “Going to get them down!” Blind Rage replied. “But it’s obviously a trap.” “It has been a trap from day one.” “This is different. They want you or the both of us at the centre of the room, ready to attack.” Blind Rage nodded grimly. “Yes. I am aware of this.” Warrior sighed. “Good just making sure, before signing our own death warrants!” Warrior started towards the centre of the warehouse, but Blind Rage firmly took a hold of him and dragged him back. Warrior’s legs squirmed in the air, like a child attempting to escape from his mothers claustrophobic grasp. “What the hell are you doing?” Warrior asked, as he was finally placed directly next to the door way. “You said it yourself. Bullseye wants the both of us in the centre of the room. We’re going to defy this principle in order to gain the upper hand,” Blind Rage rationally answered. “But, surely you should be the one to stay behind!” Warrior said. “You’re the only one who actually stands a chance of beating this clown.” Blind Rage shook his head. “No. You are more than capable of beating Bullseye, if you believe in yourself. We have no more time to waste, so I must pull rank on you to assure your compliance.” Warrior nodded. “I don’t like it, but you are the boss,” He replied through clenched teeth. Blind Rage nodded in compliance and slowly ventured into the middle of the warehouse. 20 feet away from them - nothing. 15 feet away - nothing. 10 feet - still nothing. Soon he was stood below them and still nothing. Blind Rage then hesitantly reached up to get Badger Man’s foot. Only whilst doing this, did actually notice the severe limpness of their bodies as they hung there motionless. Upon reaching the foot, he discovered the very essence of the trap. The bodies weren’t Badger Man or Handbag Warrior at all. He had hold of a couple of dummies. There was a large rumbling noise, which swept the warehouse, engulfing the attentions of everybody present within the room. Warrior stood there helpless as a perfectly circular section of the wood and brick roof collapsed in about a 10 foot diameter on top of Blind Rage who stood there and watching hit him. Warrior grimaced at the sight of this tragedy, but his attention was quickly refocused to the other side of the warehouse. Bullseye emerged from a hidden corridor with about half a dozen pirates. They had both Badger Man and Handbag Warrior, tied up of course, with them. Upon seeing Warrior, the pirates simply dropped the helpless members of Justice onto the varnished wooden flooring and formed a protective circle around their boss. A wide smile developed upon Bullseye’s face as he began to clap his hands in an almost mocking fashion. “Very well done Warrior,” Bullseye said. “I must admit I didn’t expect you to get this far.” “Well you can expect me to come over there and introduce your face to the very colourful wall,” Warrior replied. “But that’s not what we both want…is it?” “It isn’t?” “No. You release my two buddies there and I’ll left you leave without tearing your face off.” Bullseye started to laugh, almost uncontrollably. “You know what? I like you. I’ll let you live of you like!” Warrior sighed. “So I take you’ve decided to take the longer route, the outcome will be the same.” “Yes. Yes it will. Your friends are here Warrior. If you can get past my pirates and then me, you can have them.” “No shit. Well that was blatantly obvious numb nuts. No wonder you just became an assassin and not some super villain bent on taking over the world - you simply don’t have the mental capacity.” Bullseye grew visibly angered by this last statement. “Kill him.” All of the pirates grinned and slowly advanced towards him. Warrior counted two teeth among them - at most. Double the brain the amount o brain cells they had. Warrior smoothly extracted his two laser axes from the side of his legs, as the pirates extracted their sabre-like swords. They were walking to slow. Warrior wanted to end this quickly, try not to use too much of his energy up on the super villain’s lackeys. That was one of the major rules in the super hero business. Warrior gently flicked the ‘on’ button on the side of each of his laser axes, then charged at the pirates. He took the first two by surprise, swiping them across the stomachs. The third one managed to strike a blow, but Warrior blocked it with the left laser axe and cut the pirate across the chest with the right axe. The last two struck out simultaneously, but Warrior managed to block both blows with his axes and with a brief flick of his wrist the pirates were effectively disarmed. Warrior finished them both off with a swift karate kick to the chest of one and an elbow to the jaw for the last one. Warrior faced Bullseye with a grim, determined look etched upon his face. Bullseye’s smile widened. “Ah…I see your skills have developed somewhat,” Bullseye declared. “Indeed. Can we please have less talk and just get to the gratuitous violence?” Warrior replied. With that Warrior lunged forward laser axe first, a move that was easily dodged by the very nimble Bullseye. The assassin followed this up by a quick blow to Warrior’s gut and a throw that sent the hapless superhero across to the end of the room, dropping both laser axes on the way. Bullseye slowly followed him across, picking up one of the axes along the way. He stood over the still conscious Warrior, playing around with the power switch the side of the metal frame. Bullseye put his foot upon Warrior’s chest and raised the active laser axe high above his head. “Say a prayer to your deity…you’ll need it!” Bullseye declared, as readied himself to strike. Before he could strike there was a primal scream emanating from the centre of the room. Both Warrior and Bullseye glanced to see the cause of the problem. The rubble at the centre of the room slowly started to move and soon debris flew out at all angles and the massive frame stood from the rubble and made his way towards Bullseye. “Aw shit!” Bullseye exclaimed, hurling a metal knife at the mammoth’s huge frame. Blind Rage effortlessly batted away the deadly knife. Bullseye took that as a sign of failure and instantly departed back through his secret corridor. Blind Rage slowly bent down and lifted Warrior soundly up onto his feet. “What took you so long?”
