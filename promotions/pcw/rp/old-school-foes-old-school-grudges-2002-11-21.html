Old School Foes, Old School Grudges

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Old School Foes, Old School Grudges Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 11/21 The HIT's PCW career has spanned two tenures. In the first, he came in as a member of Neurosis, the ill-fated IWO based stable with Kent Anathason and Nuke. In the second, he came in under his own right, as the 10/18 Assailant and foe of the Front Office. But, it is neither of his entrances that upsets The HIT. It is his exit. In a story he has told many times, 'The Russian Bear' Ivan Stanislav eliminated him from PCW in a match against Rent-A-Hero with one well placed Red Scare. Trapezoid still loathes Ivan for it, despite HIT's later victory. But....Stanislav is not the same man. The Red Army is no more. Does HIT still hate the massive Russian? And what of Kid Wonder? The Quintessential Teen Idol, and the man HIT beat for his first PCW Television Title reign. But, more importantly to Trapezoid, the man who beat HIM in the first round of the Wrestling God tournament. Vengeance is on the mind of the Bishop of Bisection come Alliances.. And, like the federation coming to dance, the grudges are damn sure Old School.. :As the bell rings to end the long school day, students pour out of Brigham Young High School from seemingly every angle possible. As the students leave, one man with a large red afro steps out, drinking a Mountain Dew, and holding the PCW Television Title around his waist. That man, of course, is the HIT..: The HIT: Mmmm.....nothing like a nice refreshing Mountain Dew on a hot Utah day to cool things off....but I digress. I've got a lot on my plate to worry about at Alliances. Tag Title match with Teresa, match to make this here Mountain Dew the official soft-drink of PCW, then my Cruiserweight Title match with Mr. Walden....with all of that, I've almost forgotten about the Alliances Match itself! And, I really shouldn't be forgetting about it. Not only is it a chance to face all of PCW, it is now even a chance to face the greatest superstars from OTHER promotions as well! And, one of those promotions just happens to be Old School Wrestling... ::Taking another swig of the Dew, HIT stretches out tiredly, accidentally tripping an overly eager student running down the steps to his bus.:: The HIT: Oops! Sorry about that man....now, where was I? Oh yes...Old School Wrestling! The invention of one Jane Reagan, and, while I don't know a damn about Old School Wrestling versus New School Wrestling, I DO know some of the men representing OSW. Ivan Stanislav.....and Kid Wonder. Two men whose paths I've crossed before... ::The soda chugged, Trapezoid carries the can with him, stepping out into the parking lot, not the brightest of ideas considering teenage drivers.....:: The HIT: HEY! I'M A PEDESTRIAN! I HAVE THE RIGHT OF WAY! NO! DON'T RUN OVER THE FRESHMEN! GAAAAAAHHHH!! ::Some nimble dodging manuevers later, Trapezoid has made it to a nice grassy patch, the closest thing to a quad type area the high school has.:: The HIT: Ivan...you remember me, da? INTERvention? Much has changed since I hit you with Calculus....you dropped the Red Army, formed a stronger bond with Tempest, and killed the whole Communism thing. But Ivan, while you may have fooled everyone......you cannot fool the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid! One day Ivan....once no one expects it, the Army returns, and you take over OSW like the Soviets took Nazi Germany territory at the end of World War II. Not that I give a damn about OSW, but Ivan, your new act makes me sick. Still...one must wonder, can you still handle it? Xavier Kannon, Duke Williams, Walden, El Diablo, Clyde....all of PCW's best, and you can't even beat the lowly Television Champion! So now, you return to PCW...a PCW STRONGER than it was when you left. I advise you....don't even bother. A man with as much pride as yourself might resort to suicide when defeated by the likes of BladeD! :A slight chuckle escapes the Trapezoid's lips, as he places the Mountain Dew can on his head in a bad attempt at advertising.:: The HIT: And then, Kid Wonder. Geez, no wonder they call the place Old School Wrestling. And no, that's not an age joke.....I'm above that. Wonder, you've been around for probably about as long as me....a couple of years. You've had chance after chance...and yet, you could never step up to be counted. The PCW World Title...eluded you every time! You're a legend here in many ways, but are better known for your failures than your achievements. You are defined by failure...and that is why you went to OSW. To revive yourself...to make that name elsewhere. And, you became OSW Cruiserweight Champion. Good for you, hell, if I beat Walden on Sunday, I'll be the PCW Cruiserweight Champion, and then I can show you exactly which federation has the best cruiserweight kingpin... ::The can begins to fall from his cranium......sensing the posible profaning of the soft drink's name by touching the comment dirt, HIT lunges, splaying out full length to catch the can, cradling it gently in his arms. Realizing what an idiot he must look like, he attempts to look tough, but fails miserably....:: The HIT: Old School Wrestling....welcome home. Your old home, Primetime Championship Wrestling, is welcoming you back...but not in a friendly manner. The Chef, Clyde, DarkStar and I.....we all have long memories. And all of us, to a man, have a problem with The Russian Bear. Wonder I will handle myself, but Ivan? Watch your back.....comeuppance is a bitch, isn't it? ::Rather than deliver his catchphrase, HIT simply walks off, solemnly, into the parking lot.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
