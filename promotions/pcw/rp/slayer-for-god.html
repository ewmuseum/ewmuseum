Slayer for God

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Right Now I laid flat on my back like a prom date the next day. My eyes felt dilated as I stared up at the whirling blades of a brown, rusty fan that seemed it could plummet and kill me at any moment. I never moved my eyes though, I only watched, mesmerized like a small child with a new toy but yet I didn't know why. The sheets underneath my massive body were soft as silk but were made of cloth which hung down to the white carpet. Crumpled piled of clothing laid on the floor as bandages replaced where they usually would be. My ribs were bandaged, my thigh, a gash on my forehead. Needless to say I felt like shit. I had picked dry blood out of my spiky blue hair for the past week now and my eyes seemed dim now; had I lost my power? I looked to my arms which were still their normal size, I was still standing as a tower, and everything seemed fine except for my eyes. Akiyama did this to me.....and he would pay dearly. Now I let sleep take me into it's grasp. I was so tired, it had been such a long day and sleep sounded so good. I drifted off as the clock blinked over and over with red numbers. The fan stopped but I didn't notice as I slipped into a coma type state. Things then began to get weird. He stood in front of an old dusty town with buildings that seemed to be crumbling under the hot days sun, an old cement fountain in the center, and wooden markets on parts of the streets. Children ran through the streets playing, dressed in sack clothing. The parents were no different. They did their daily business dressed the same and it made him wonder; Where the hell am I? Slowly he moved through the town but no one seemed to notice him. It was as if he was an enigma. He passed through the crowded streets until he heard the sounds of laughter. People threw stones in one direction but he couldn't see what was going on. After making his way through the sea of people he saw men carrying crosses on their shoulders. They were dressed in nothing but cloth with a crown of thorn on their head. One stood out the most. He had long brown hair and a beard. He showed no sign of pain as he drug the cross which was 2 times bigger than him across the stony ground. People taunted him and tossed stones as armed guards with swords passed behind. They walked like royalty in their silver armor with the red clothes underneath. They laughed at some of the remarks the people made and when one with one cross tried to stop they kicked him along like a filthy animal. 'What's going on?' He asked himself but no one could hear his question to give a reply. A feeling made him follow and soon they stopped at a hill. The men laid their crosses down and were forced to lay upon them. Their arms spread across two sides of the old wood and their feet laid one on top of the other. Two of the men wept for their life but still, the one that stood out, said nothing. He was surprised, shocked, horrified when the guards drew hammers and spikes and drove them through the hands and feet of the men. Screams lashed the atmosphere but still one man did not scream or beg for his life. Those town folk kept pelting them with stones and a great thunderstorm moved through the air as slowly the life escaped these men. He couldn't move his eyes off this one and it was almost like he could feel his pain, feel his mission, and anger swept over him. He knew who this was and what this was.....This man was more than a man... He was Jesus Christ.....and he had died for these people's sins...... Like a flash he was now standing in the midst of a city. People ran screaming for their life as riders rode on horses with heads of lions and tails of serpents. The riders were large knights who rode with purpose and dignity as they slay the ones running by. They fell to the ground dying, they ran with bursts of fire, and the horses added by killing as well. 'Who are you?' He managed as fear seemed to paralyze him. The sea of riders sat there just staring. He watched their every movement as small beads of sweat began dripping down his face. Finally one pointed up to the sky and with that the riders were gone. And the flash returned, blinding him. I awoke back in my bed, fear still clinching my being as I stared up. 'What was that?' I asked myself knowing I wouldn't get an answer. 'You are one I seek, for I am god all mighty and I request your serves my child,' I leaped from my bed shocked as the voice boomed through my room. My heart beat against my chest as I looked around grabbing a pistol from my drawer. 'Do not fear me, for I am the one who sits most high,' 'What kind of sick joke is this?' I had asked. A beam of light seemingly went through my roof striking me into the chest. I was paralyzed now and couldn't move, talk, or even blink. Images of the riders filled my mind, of Christ being hung on the cross, and it all became clear to me. 'I....I understand,' I mustered out. The beam dropped me to the floor with a thud and now I knew what I had to do. I was now a rider of the seal, the one who was chosen to punish the one's god marks and my first mission would be....to take care of the False God.
