Date: Love=PAIN Dearest Bertha, How beautiful thou art to me… When the wind blows I think of you… When there is a burning house It’s you I see… When I see my own unworthy visage in the mirror It’s your name I see carved into my chest. With love, ~Syko--- Dearest Bertha, Please, grace me with your siren caw… Don’t let the chains of bridal status lock your heart down forever, depriving you the chance to test the admiration of others. Open up you heart, for there are voids that must be filled… There are so many unsoiled, empty closets around, it hurts to think about it. With love, ~Syko--- Stupid whore Bertha, Hey. It’s me. The Chef. You are a stupid skank. I never loved you. Syko is better. With hate, ~Chef --- Dearest Bertha, In case you are wondering why my handwriting has been red, as of late, it is because I ran out of ink. Before I slip into the black, I just want to say --- Dearest Bertha, I think I understand why you have yet to answer my letters. But, believe me when I say this; In all my heart, the dead cat shoved into your mailbox was a testament of love!!! LOVE!!!! It means I’ll kill for you! And salvage road kill, too. With love, ~Syko--- Dearest Bertha, Smell this letter. --- Dear …misfortunate human being, I must apologize for my employer’s, how should we say… Terrible mishap. You obviously were not Bertha Stewart, at least not the one whom is referred to in these letters. Wrong address. Please except our most humble apologies. Sincerely, ~Syko’s 2nd. PS: Sorry about the chloroform laced note.
