Catching up

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-11-02 Bob Vurdo puts the key to his house in the front door and opens it up slowly. He walks into the lobby and toward the stairs when the door from the kitchen opens and out walks Mrs.. Vurdo. As she sees Bob she smiles and walks over to him. Bob drops his suitcase and gives his mother a hug. &#039;Oh Robert it&#039;s so good to have you back.&#039; Mrs. Vurdo continues hugging her son tightly when Grimgor walks through the door and into the lobby. &#039;So this is the house of Bob Vurdo.&#039; Mrs. Vurdo lets go of her son and turns around to see Grimgor. She looks at the dwarf in a puzzled manner as he enters the room and approaches Mrs. Vurdo. &#039;Don&#039;t worry mum that&#039;s just Grimgor... He&#039;ll be living out back for a while.&#039; Bob&#039;s mother smiles at the dwarf as he grabs Mrs Vurdo around the waste and latches on giving her a hug as well. Mrs. Vurdo can&#039;t believe what&#039;s happening but she gives the dwarf a hug anyway. &#039;I&#039;m going to go and um pack my suitcase mah.&#039; Bob picks up his suitcase and walks back up the stairs leaving Grimgor and Mrs. Vurdo alone. &#039;So where are you from Grimgor?&#039; Grimgor stays latched onto Mrs.. Vurdo who is trying to end the hug in a polite manner. &#039;I have to go and finish the ironing.&#039; Mrs. Vurdo pries the little dwarf from around her waste and walks back into the kitchen. Grimgor walks over to the stairs slowly and sits down. Bob emerges from his room and walks down the stairs to sit next to the little dwarf. &#039;Want to see your room?&#039; Grimgor looks up at Bob eagerly. &#039;Yeah that&#039;d be great.&#039; Grimgor jumps up energetically from his seat on the stairs and looks back at Bob who slowly gets up from the stairs. They walk to a door in the back left hand corner of the room behind the staircase. They enter the hallway and walk down to the second last door on the right. Bob turns the door handle and opens up the room which has been vacant for a few months now. Sunlight shoots through the blinds in the dark room making lines of sunshine on the carpet. The room is rather blank with only a bed, a bedside table and a wardrobe. Grimgor enters the room and his eyes light up. &#039;Is this really my room?&#039; Grimgor jumps onto the bed and backs himself up to the pillow. He seems amazed by how comfortable the bed is. &#039;Well this is it I suppose.&#039; &#039;I love it thank you Bob Vurdo.&#039; Grimgor smiles at Bob who just ignores him. &#039;I was noticing your mums a good catch.&#039; Bob flares his eyes in rage and looks down at the dwarf. &#039;Her breasts were all nice and soft.... I think I might go and touch my willy over that.&#039; Bob can&#039;t believe what he is hearing. &#039;Excuse me?&#039; Grimgor sees the fire in Bobs eyes and realizes he has said something wrong. He decides it would be better if he backed down. &#039;Is your mum good at catch?&#039; Grimgor&#039;s attempt to make Bob think his hearing wrong isn&#039;t successful. &#039;You stay the fuck away from my mother or I&#039;ll kill you!&#039; Bob walks out of the room and shuts the door behind him leaving Grimgor by himself. Bob walks down the hallway and opens up the door which leads him back into the lobby. It&#039;s good to be home he thinks. Even though he didn&#039;t spend much time in LSD land he has realized how much he has. Maybe that was the whole point of it, maybe he was meant to realize to be thankful for what he has. Bob walks over to the phone besides the kitchen door and picks it up. He has a lot of people to call the first on his list is Tony. Bob punches in Tony&#039;s number and the phone rings a few times before Tony answers with &#039;Hello.&#039; &#039;Heya.... Tony it&#039;s Bob.&#039; Bob sounds a lot happier than usual, the trip to LSD land must have done a lot for him. &#039;Hey Bob.... Look man we got a lot of catching up to do a lot has happened and we need to talk NOW.... Meet me at my house in half an hour.&#039; &#039;OK.&#039; Tony hangs up and Bob begins wondering why it was so urgent that they meet at once. He doesn&#039;t usually rush to speak with Bob unless its something important.
