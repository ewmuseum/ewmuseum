The Great Escape

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21st, 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen being dragged into a dimly lit back room. A chair is set up with ropes on the floor. Doctor No stand infront of the chair. Three men in white coats and pants drag Frampton into the chair and sit him down. One man breaks away and ties him around the waist, arms and legs. Mike tries to escape but is unable to. He looks a Doctor No with pure hatred as the three men stand behind Frampton's chair with their arms crossed and eyes beaming down on him like a hawk who smells a wounded animal. Doctor No slowly closes the door and turns a lock. He is in a black suit, he wears a red tie and still has his grey hair. Doctor No walks behind Mike who turns his head to keep him in view. Doctor No spins Frampton's head so he faces the door. Doctor No comes back around and sticks his face in Mike's face. Mike spits on him. Doctor No wags his finger and pulls on the rope tightening its grasp. Frampton gasps and stops struggling. Doctor No pulls up a stool and sits down. He eyes Mike with evil intent.) Mike Frampton: What do you want with me Doctor No? Doctor No: Tsk, tsk, tsk. How would you no if we wanted something with you? Mike Frampton: I would be dead if you didn't want anything now speak up. (Doctor No smiles. His yellow, crooked teeth reflect the light into Mike's eyes.) Doctor No: Tell me Mr. Frampton, who are we today? George Bush? Dan Rather? Ted Turner? Cher? Out with it. (Mike begins to shake rapidly in the chair. His head drops down and he stops shaking. He looks up and sees Doctor No.) Doctor No: Damn it man, who are you? Mike Frampton: The names Bond, James Bond! (Doctor No's jaw drops.) Doctor No: You have to be kidding me. Mike Frampton: I never kid unless it can get me laid. Now then Doctor No, if you really are a Doctor, tell me your plan and I will go easy on you. (Doctor No stares on with a look of total disbelief upon his face.) Mike Frampton: What's wrong No? Cat got your tongue? (A creased frown appears on Doctor No's face.) Doctor No: If your so smart Mr. Fram... Mr. Bond, then why don't you tell me my plan. (It's time for Frampton to shine. A grin crosses his face.) Mike Frampton: This is just my rough draft but here it goes. First you plan to highjack a nuclear submarine. You will then remove the Nuclear Material and place it into a bomb device. You will then swallow the bomb. This way you can get through any security check point. Next you will walk up to the front of the White House. Following this you will detonate the bomb and blow away half of Washington D.C. You don't need to respond, I know I am right. (Doctor No again is staring in disbelief as his jaw has yet again dropped. Doctor No then composes himself and does a circle around Mike an sits back down.) Doctor No: So if you are James Bond, why haven't you used one of your gadgets to escape? Mike Frampton: You don't want that. Doctor No: Oh but I do. Mike Frampton: In due time No, in due time. (Doctor No walks up to the three men. He whispers something in their ears then hits his wrist five times. He then walks back and sits down.) Doctor No: I think we got off on the wrong foot Mr. Bond. Could I get you something to drink? Mike Frampton: Yes. Doctor No: I think I know what you want. A martini, shaken not stirred, correct? Mike Frampton: Nope. I want a Manhattan with a lime and one of those curly straws that allow me to watch it come up to my mouth. Also I want an umbrella and a plastic sword in it. (Doctor No looks at Frampton surprised at his request. He snaps and points to the man on the left. The man walks out the door.) Doctor No: While we wait why don't you tell me where you are from. (Mike Frampton looks at Doctor No. Frampton then puts his hand into his pocket. He pulls out a switch blade which is well hidden. He presses a button and the blade flies out.) Mike Frampton: Me? Well I'm from England. (Frampton begins to saw slowly at the ropes. The two men behind him seem not to notice.) Doctor No: England you say. What do you do for a living? Mike Frampton: I am a double 0 agent. (Frampton cuts through one rope. He then switches hands and begins to cut the second and final rope.) Doctor No: Really are you? (Doctor No looks on with a false interested face. He nods at the man on the right and he exits the room. Frampton cuts himself through the last rope still unnoticed.) Mike Frampton: Oh yes I am. Doctor No: Who do you work for? (Frampton hesitates.) Doctor No: If you believe you won't get out of here alive you may as well tell me. (Frampton waits.) Doctor No: Well? Mike Frampton: If I told you, I would have to kill you. And where the hell is my drink? Doctor No: I'll send Franky here to check on it. (The last man exits the room as well.) Mike Frampton: That's odd, why did they all leave? Doctor No: I really hadn't noticed. Mike Frampton: I'm onto your game. Those guys are waiting outside the door. Doctor No: I assure you they are n... (Frampton dives at Doctor No and covers his mouth. Frampton then smashes his hand into the face of Doctor No rendering him unconscious.) Mike Frampton: That'll leave a mark. (The great 007 music begins to play as Frampton slowly crawls over to his chair. He then shoves the chair underneath the nob to lock the door. Frampton then places Doctor No on the chair. Frampton looks up and notices an air duct.) Mike Frampton: Going up. (Frampton jumps and knocks out the front of the duct. He then pulls himself in and disappears as the music fades out along with the scene.)
