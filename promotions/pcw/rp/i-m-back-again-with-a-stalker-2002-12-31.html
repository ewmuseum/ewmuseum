Than he just gave up and joined up with the Supreme Court. A group of wrestlers whom hated the FSW for the reasons of nearly destroying their careers. They seeked to destroy the FSW and they did. They bought some 10% of the FSW. But that wasn't enough. Big D was still hungry for revenge along with them they destroyed it. Which brought him back to his new home: Primetime Championship Wrestling. In the PCW, he had accomplished a lot.Now, he's back to ruin the careers of those who've tried to ruin him.
