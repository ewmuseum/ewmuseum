Some pre-talk before the big Showdown!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 Sometimes, certain events in life are unexplainable. For the Krazed Lunatic, that statement couldn't be more truer than of Friday Fusion. Going in, a substantial number of critics believed that the Krayzie One couldn't pull of win against Chet King, since his mind has been anywhere but on the match itself. But, it would take one mistake by the Continental Champion for him to be dethroned by a man that is now one step closer to his dream of completing the title sweep in PCW. Yet, as much as Jeremy Howard would like to savor his victory over Chet King and his newly won Continental title, he can't. Because, tonight at March Madness, the Krazed Lunatic has to face the man that has been holding his brother captive for nearly two months now. Drake Maxwell, simply put, has angered a man some say is a future hall of famer. And now, within the confines of a hell in the cell cage, he has to pay for his sins against the man he couldn't beat with help on New Year's day. But, before the Krayzie One heads for that showdown, the scene fades into his stylish European designed two story home in Elkhart, Indiana. A camera comes to the front door, watching as the door opens up. Like a voyeuristic male creeping through a brothel, the camera slowly makes its way into the foyer of the home. The camera has three choices. Left to the living room, right to the dining room or straight to the den. It chooses straight, passing by a flight of stairs before making it into the twenty by sixteen foot den. Jeremy: How does he like it? Within the room, the lovely Jessica is seen watching over two year old Troy, whose playing inside of a Fisher Price jungle gym playground toward the left wall. Today, Jessica wears a pair of black leather pants that fit snug to her rear end and white t-shirt. Jessica: He's taking right to it. Jeremy: That's a good sign. Jeremy, clad in name brand blue jeans and a black 'Krayzie Till I Die' t-shirt, stands in the doorway to the backyard across from where the camera sits. In the background, part of what is an Olympic size pool is within sight. Jessica: One day, he'll get to know who we are. Jeremy steps out of the doorway, getting closer to where his girlfriend and son are. Jeremy: I'm just glad that he's back with us. That's all that matters. Jessica: Thanks to you..... my hero. Jeremy gets to the little jungle gym and bends down next to his son. Jeremy: Hopefully after March Madness, he has an uncle to play with while I'm away. If Drake Maxwell hopes to have any chance against this man, he'd better devise a devious plan to gain the upperhand. Because, he's walking into this match against a man going on pure emotion. And, given his past, those emotions can carry the Krayzie One to put his body on the line in a way that most men don't get back up from. Or, in Ivan Stanislav's case, couldn't overcome. He is the Krazed Lunatic. Jessica: Troy and I will be watching.... Rooting for ya. A smile graces Jeremy's face. Jeremy: That's the kind of love that carries a man to victory. Jessica: I won't be there this time, so Drake can't blame his loss on me this time. Jeremy: He only b!tched about losing the last time, because his little plan with Vampir failed miserably thanks to a good friend of mine. As it stands, the battle between Drake Maxwell and Jeremy Howard stands even at a win and a loss each. In the first match, the Fallen Angel picked up the win by default when the Krayzie One left him laying in the mat after a Krayzie Death Slam to chase after Jason Snow. Then, in the rematch for the Krazed Lunatic's World title, Drake Maxwell came to 'A Psychotic Ending', after he tried to cheat his way to victory. Tonight will serve as the tiebreaker in this heated fued. Tonight, the real winner in this fued will stand tall. Jessica: Are you going to have anyone watching over you in the match? Jeremy shakes his head, 'no'. Jeremy: I told Kannon that I wanted Maxwell all for myself. Jessica: I really wish you'd rethink that. Jeremy leans forward and plants a kiss on her luscious lips. Jeremy: I have to get going. I gotta make a stop before St. Louis. This stop can't be good. As per usual, whenever the Krayzie One has a 'stop' to make, it involves him going through a window, or chasing someone with his car through the streets of Terre Haute, or sending someone to the hospital because of an altercation. What could it possibly be this time? Jessica: Where? Jeremy grabs Jessica around the waist and pulls her in close. Troy, still playing on the jungle gym, looks up to his hugging parents. Jessica lays her head on the shoulder of the Krazed Lunatic, feeling that security blanket of love has always provided for her. Jeremy: I'm going to see my mother and tell her about Justin not being the one who attacked me after I beat Drake Maxwell. Jessica: You know, she's going to ask where he's at. Jeremy pulls back just a bit from Jessica's body, while still holding onto her waist. Jeremy: I know. Jessica: What are you going to tell her? Jeremy: I'm going to tell her the truth, then give her the money to order the pay per view. It's going to be hard to tell his mother that her youngest son has been trapped inside of a dark cell for the past two months, but it's something Jeremy Howard has to do. Probably the only thing that saves this woman from having a nervous breakdown, might be when the Krayzie One tells her that he's going to fight for his freedom tonight against the man that is holding Justin Howard. Jessica: Good luck, honey. Jeremy: Thanks. Jeremy turns to his two year old son, whose just learning who these people really are for the first time, and gives the little man a tight hug. It has to feel so nice to be able to finally hug your son. Then, he turns back toward his beautiful girlfriend. A loving embrace ensues before Jeremy leaves for what could be the biggest match of his life, emotionally wise, as the scene fades..... to black!
