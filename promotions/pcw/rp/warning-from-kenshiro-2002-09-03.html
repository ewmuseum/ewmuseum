Warning from Kenshiro

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/03/02 The beast within... Every human has one. For some, it's that feeling that you get when you want to steal that diamond the clerk left out in the open. For others, it's the instinct to fight when surrounded by four men, instead of possibly trying to talk them down. And for a smaller few, it's what makes you mutilate and mangle others, possibly even eating them. Some persons develop a sexual appetite. While others kill themselves for no apparent reason. Pretty much any gray area known to modern human psychology can be explained simply by saying that the beast within activated. There are different triggering mechanisms that activate the beast within. It's different for every person, don't need to go into it. So what does this matter? Well, to one Rush Mason, it matters a great deal. Allow me to introduce myself, Mr. Mason. My name is Kenshiro. Last name is irrelevant, just listen. You are fighting a developing Celestial with a beast within. A very dangerous thing indeed. You see, The End has powers of a Celestial, powers that he's learning. I'm helping him with that. But he also has that beast within. Should the powers and the beast be linked... then suffice to say you're f*cked. Normally I really wouldn't care. But Rumiko got me involved, and Sho's watching me like a freaking hawk... nevermind. What does this mean to you? Well, short answer, it means that The End is more dangerous and powerful than you may think. Long answer, he could perhaps be more unpredictable than anyone could imagine. And I don't think if that beast is activated that he could be controlled. Also, while I'm at it, I think you should know something. Don't perform chants. In such a case, the victim must be able to see and hear you for anything to happen. Now, back to the matter at hand. I've studied The End, and his powers and beast are very closely linked. They both have to do with the threat of harm. The End's beast within is unleashed during times of extreme personal physical danger. One case in point... Charlie Loc covers The End to pin. 1-2-3No! The End somehow manages to kick out of the pin. Charlie Loc is livid, slapping the mat and cracking the pieces of glass into even smaller pieces. Loc turns The End over and puts him into a Gedo Clutch to try for another pin. 1-2-3NO! Dave: MIYA COMES BACK WITH A VENGEANCE! Miya ran to the ropes and snapped Loc back with a flipover neck-whip, carrying right over into a reverse sunset flip, covering The End. 1-2-3NO! Once again, The End barely gets a shoulder up! Duke: Close pins from Loc and Miya. They really want that PURE title. But aren’t they closed? Dave: I think it’s opening up again under new management. Jeff: DAMN THEM! Following Jeff’s awkward outburst, Dave and Duke look at Jeff before double-smacking him. Miya pulls The End back to his feet and whips him into the corner. Miya calls for a THREE panes of glass to be sent down to the ring. Per his instructions, they stop about two feet off the mat. Miya sets The End on the top rope and follows up, bringing them both to a stance on the top rope. Duke: Miyagawa better be damn sure this doesn’t backfire on him. Miya sets The End up for a DDT. Charlie Loc comes back to life and sees them on the top rope. Loc runs to the corner and sets himself under Miya. Using his leg strength, Loc takes them all back. Miya holds on for the DDT, and holds Loc over the glass with his legs. Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! All three men fall through the glass. Miya and Loc sort of just lie there. The End rolls around and squirms, flailing his legs and body wildly. Duke: The End may be the most expressive. Jeff: He’s just overselling the move. Dave: No he’s no-- Oh My God... The End stops over by the ropes. Dustin Downey checks on The End and just backs up, speechless. He waves to the back for help. Dave: Ladies and gentlemen... oh my, I just... The camera moves in close and shows The End gasping for air... a large piece of glass lodged into his throat. That night, The End came close to dying a complete death. Cut a Celestial's head off, and it's dead, period. Some would've said he was lucky; after all, any normal person would have been killed by such an injury, right? But The End is Celestial. Threatened with death, his beast activated and among other things it manifested its abilities in hyper-active healing faculties. But minutes into that very match after suffering that life-threatening injury and came very close to defending his PURE Jr. Heavyweight Title; fighting with far more ferocity than he had before... That was one example. Now another example would be The End's Celestial powers. He can learn to control them, but for now, they have a trigger. It's been done but once before... Robert and Akiko Green ride down the forest-surrounded Route 66 with their son, Skylar buckled into the backseat of their emerald green Mercedes Benz. The sky is changing to an orange hue, shades of pink sneak out from behind the mass of cumulus clouds that hides the setting sun. They had been at the park all day, the city of Fairfax, in joint with nearby Westbrook, hosted a Fourth of July picnic for all to attend. There had been many games: a softball tournament, volleyball... toy boat races in the lake... General fun was had by all. There was a reckless drunk driver on the road that day. He drove down the wrong side of the highway. Skylar's father swerved to avoid the driver, but the car spun out of control. By the time he got it back under control, it hit a car in front of it at high speed. Skylar didn't know it, still doesn't, but his powers activated for fear of his parents being killed from getting thrown out of the car. Of course, at such a young age, he couldn't do very much. The only ability he could muster was to hold his parents in the car. That turned out to be a cruel twist of fate: they would have died if they were thrown from the car, but being held in the car, they were crushed in the 21 car-pileup. I warn you of this in particular, Rush Mason, because I know that you will try to do something to Sandy Wong. And that won't be a smart thing. The End will only get angered and his powers would probably activate and there's a chance that he'd kill you. And remember, this is when he's NOT possessed... Take heed, Rush Mason... What I say is for your own benefit...
