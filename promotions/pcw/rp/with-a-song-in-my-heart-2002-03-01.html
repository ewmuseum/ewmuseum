With a Song in My Heart

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01 March 2002 Once again we rejoin the once again intrepid trio in the once again BMW Z3 in British Racing Green Metallic. Yes, they’re still driving... well...Miya is driving while Steve-o and Natalia are doing the whole passenger thingy in the passenger seats. They’re doing it pretty well; Steve has managed to shove Miya’s mother’s flowers between his knees and has thus freed up his hands to drive the nectar of the gods... Lizard Lightning, part of the Sobe range. Heh, you can only do so much shilling on an empty stomach, right? Anyway, as I was saying, the car moves swiftly on down the Anaheim landscape... although strictly speaking several dozen large malls and a handful of theme parks doesn’t exactly make much of a landscape, but I’m just laying out the pure facts for you right here and now. The car swerves around a corner as it continues on its path. Steve: How comes, when we live in Anaheim and all, that we’ve never once been to Disneyworld? I like Disney; I’m even wearing Donald Duck Boxer Shorts. Natalia: I don’t exactly want to hear about the state of Steve’s underwear, but he does have a point Miya... how comes you’ve never taken me to Disneyworld? I always wanted to go to EuroDisney, yet now I have the chance to get to the proper one and it’s on my doorstep. Miyagawa sighs quietly at the pair who has basically double teamed him. Miyagawa: I’ll make you a deal. If I win the world title on Sunday at March Madness, then we’ll go to Disneyworld on Monday. Deal? Steve: But you might throw the match because you’re a tight bastard. Miyagawa: I am not a tight bastard. I own three cars... how the hell is that tight? I just paid thirty dollars for flowers... how the heck is that tight? I am not tight, Scotsmen are tight; hence the joke... who invented copper wire? Steve replies immediately much to Miyagawa’s disgust, proving that that joke is neither new, nor amusing in anyway... in fact it’s verging on racist in some parts. Except this author is half Scottish, so he can get away with it. Steve: Two Scotsmen fighting over a penny, I’ve heard it. It isn’t particularly good, but I’ve heard it all the same. Besides there isn’t a Scottish person in PCW... although one did apply... and was rejected for being a little on the... well... Miyagawa: Shite side? That’s the good thing about this wonderful company we work for... a nice high talent rate, no matter what anybody says. And come Sunday at March Madness, hopefully I’ll be the top of it all. Which will be nice. Besides we’ll take Mom... Mother... Mum... what the heck do I call her? Natalia: Call her whatever your heart tells you to call her... how much further to go is it anyway? Miyagawa: According to this little Bosch Satellite Navigation gismo, it’s just around the next corner. The car turns and goes around the corner down a residential street. Miyagawa pulls up just outside number fifteen to a stop. The undoes his seatbelt but stays seated in the car. Miyagawa: Well... this is it. I never thought it would come to this... I never thought this opportunity existed... I still can’t believe I actually have a mother, I didn’t think something like this could possibly happen... apart from on Television. Steve: I hope you were going for some irony there. Miyagawa: I was, yes. Did it work? Steve: I thought so. But then again I’m not much of a character witness since I’m watching this in the first person, not actually on Television. But then again, just like Ice Hockey, it’s much more enjoyable watching it live and in person. Miyagawa: LET’S GO BLUES!!! Steve: Minor question... well... how comes we all live in Anaheim, and yet you support the Saint Louis Blues in Ice Hockey... the Saint Louis Rams in Football... and the Saint Louis Cardinals in Baseball... and not one single Hockey team. Miyagawa: Erm... well... erm... I just thought that the Ducks were kinda like... selling out to Disney with those movies about them. Like Kid Wonder sold out to Tyler Nelson and thus destroyed his integrity. **pauses for a second** What the hell am I saying?! Kid Wonder never had any integrity, when I kicked his ass not once, but TWICE for the Cruiserweight Title, I proved that completely and utterly. Kid Wonder is not only a joke, but a complete... why am I sitting in a car talking about an over eager under spoken over the hill even though he’s younger than I am walking talking nest of buttfinches, when we came here to find my Mom... Mum... Mother... whatever to find my female parental unit. Let’s do this. Miyagawa opens the door of the BMW and steps out onto the road. Natalia opens her passenger door and steps out onto the road also, standing next to Miyagawa (although she left her bunch of roses on the passenger seat where she had sat. Steve passes the roses over to Miya. Steve: I’m going to wait here. Even though we’re best buds and we’ve been through hell together... well... this is a family thing. And I respect that. Go have... is fun the right word... go find out the truth. That’s a better way of putting it. Miyagawa smirks and looks and Steve and nods his head slightly in the knowledge of Steve trying his damnedest to do the right thing in this situation. Miyagawa: Ok man, thanks. Without you, we wouldn’t even be here... although don’t think of it as a reason for yet another gimmick change. Steve: How can I? I’m Mr Southern Comfort, haven’t you seen me in those poster ads? Sure they only show my hand around a glass of the nectar, but it’s me all right. Anyway, time is a-wasting, you go and see her. Just remember that although it might seem new to you, she should remember you. Miyagawa: Well... she hasn’t seen me since I was seventeen, so that is nine years... but it’s time to make this right, to finally meet her again. And you never know, it might help restore my memory. With familiarity and all that; you know. Miyagawa and Natalia walk around the opposite ends of the BMW and meet again on the pavement on the other side. The house itself is much like the others down this particular street, kinda ornate... red tiled roofs and white painted walls. All on one floor the houses down here are. As Natalia and Miyagawa walk down the garden path they look at all the little pottery animals... the little hedgehogs and the sparrows and the turtles... and a painted garden gnome with a fishing rod dipping the line into a small pond with a sprinkler system on it. Miyagawa: Maybe we shouldn’t do this. I might be better off doing this on Monday after I’m World Champion... then again if she was a wrestling fan, then surly she would have seen me on Television or something. Natalia: It’s not too late to go back you know. Miyagawa: Nah... there’s a song that seems quite right for this situation... you probably haven’t heard of it. It’s by the Righteous Brothers and it’s called “You’ll Never Walk Alone”... it goes something like this... **sings** When you walk... Through a storm... Hold your head, up high... And don't be afraid, of the dark... 'Coz at the end of the storm... Is a golden sky... And the sweet silver song... Of the lark. Natalia pauses for a second as Miyagawa for some reason breaks out into a song, just for her... a song she has never heard before too. Miyagawa: Walk on, through the wind... Walk on, through the rain... Though your dreams be tossed... And blown... **breaks from singing** and here’s the chorus **sings again** Walk on, walk on... With hope, in your heart... And you'll never walk alone... You'll never walk alone **drags out “alone” for about five seconds** Natalia waits to check that he’s finished before speaking first. Natalia: Is that how you see us then? Miyagawa turns to her a nods at her. Miyagawa: Yes, yes I do. Natalia: In that case let’s play no more games... of course I’ll marry you. Miyagawa: Again. Natalia: Da.
