The Little Fetish Shop Of Horrors

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/02 (FF#1) “Hmm…do you think this would look nice embedded in Darkstar’s jugular? As usual in a Rush Mason promo, we are met by a strange opening, and a black screen, while we can get only audio, and hear some very strange things as we do. “Well, I’m not a big fan of leather, but it certainly would look nice around the ‘Mourning Star’s’ neck as he leaves on a stretcher.” The scene finally begins to fade in as we see Rush Mason holding what appears to be a leather choker, with spikes extending in both directions and what is called an “easily adjustable size changer” that is basically a belt loop. He’s standing in a large store with strange objects all around, and some odd-looking people as well. Rush: You over there….freaky! Every single person who seems like an employee turns to Rush, all of them with multiple piercings and hair that seems to defy the laws of gravity. Rush tries again. Rush: Umm…Satan-Lover, come here! Rush says this to no one in particular, and each person starts to walk toward Rush. Rush starts to yell again, feeling almost trapped against the wave of weirdos. Rush: I meant….YOU! The product of a Doberman and an Arabian woman named Bashmal who lives under the bridge and eats pond scum for breakfast! In a realm of comedy, every single person would continue to walk towards Rush, but not one of the freaks continues to walk, although one appears to be thinking as if it could possibly be him. Finally, Rush just points to a tall, fairly normal looking man, his only real strangeness is that his eyebrow is pierced. Rush:Just come over here, I need some help and I don’t want to waste my whole day on a fetid wasteland such as this. The employee makes no notice of Rush’s pissy mood, which is really more his personality than a mood. The employee’s voice is noticeably raspy as he approaches Rush. “How can I help you, sir?” Rush scratches his chin for a moment, then points over to a wall and begins to jog/walk over. Rush: What’s that thing? Pointing up at what appears to be a giant black leather straightjacket with spikes on the outside, Rush’s eyes light up at the thought of the potential violence that could be inflicted with said “weapon.” “That, sir, is our special. It’s made in the Antarctic, and is fashioned from the purest snow in the world, refined into bl…Sir?” Rush has pretended to drift off into sleep, and is “snoring” loudly, but pretends to wake up and turns to the employee. Rush: Okay, here’s how things are going to work. I don’t want the history of your little geek world company, I just want to buy some things so I can kill a dark jobber and amuse myself watching him bleed. Is that SO much to ask? The employee looks blankly at Rush, not acknowledging him, and then turns back to the object on the wall. “It’s a PleatherPleasure5000 model of the Jakket Of Luv. It’s intended to be used on people who are less than 160 pounds, and is only for recreational use. You must be warned before using this product that…Sir?” Once again, Rush has drifted off into a fake sleep, and awakens once again to give a mock smile and another scolding to the tall employee. Rush: I do not want to hear about you and your little nimrod exhibition. So I repeat….I don’t care. The employee rolls his eyes, and turns back to Rush, who pretends as if nothing has happened. Rush: Okay, I’ll take that thingie then. And uhhh….whassat thing over there?….And I don’t want to hear that it’s the Charlie Loc nunchucks of doom and that’s it’s made in Cambodia by alien beings. The employee obliges, pointing up to an object that is reminiscent of some sort of long silver oblong object. The employee begins to explain, in a sympathetic voice. “I’m sorry, sir but this one does require some explanation, because it’s rather complicated to use. And you might not want to use it once you hear…” Rush’s eyes just glare at the man, who stops talking as Rush just looks up at it once, and then replies. Rush: Sure, whatever, I’ll take it. Just put all that stuff in a bag for me. The man sighs, but then walks off, as Rush walks over to the window, speaking to himself in a very strange monologue. Rush: You know, a long time ago, I thought that dark jobbers were no better than decaying corpses. Rush looks out the window, giving off a philosophical aura as he continues his strange soliloquy. Rush: Now however, with Clyde holding the World Title, and that weirdo little kid stealing the Continental Title, it seems as if they actually do matter. Rush turns and looks back at the PleatherPleasure5000, and then smiles a smile that normal people would associate with something glorious, like the birth of a baby. Rush: But I lie not, when I say that making that make-up wearing faggot bleed all over the ring and beating him senseless will be one of the greater moments of my life. Rush wipes an imaginary tear from his eye as he finishes, imagining in his mind’s eye the moment in which he destroys DarkStar. However, he is interrupted by a raspy voice calling out. “Sir, umm…, if you stop talking to the window, your bill is up over here.” Rush turns rapidly and shouts back at the employee. Rush: I was having a moment there! Bon Dieu, I need to get out of here. Maybe I’ll go down to the 7/11 and hit random Goth kids who walk by with a baseball bat. Ahh yes…that sounds just grand. With that, Rush walks over to the cash register and signs the receipt for his credit card purchase of the two weapons for use against DarkStar. He gives a cold look to the tall employee who helped him as he walks away, whistling at his own good fortune as he closes the door, while the two employees chat as the scene fades out… “What the hell did that guy buy?” “The PleatherPleasure5000, of course, and then he bought the Stimulator?” “The Stimulator?” “You know, that new electric dildo we got in.” “Pfft…we get more weirdos in here every day….”
