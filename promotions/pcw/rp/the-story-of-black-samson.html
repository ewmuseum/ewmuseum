The Story of Black Samson.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Just in time! Birds chirp in tall trees lining the San Jose suburban lane, Maple Street. Up ahead, Stately Steele Manor shines on it’s small hilltop like a lighthouse of goodness and righteousness, spreading forth its, dare I say, love across the suburban landscape. An ice cream van dishes out all manner of frozen confectionaries to happy little children. Dads mow their lawns to golf green consistency, pipes stuck in their mouths, waving to any and all passersby. Moms in frilly dresses bake scrumptious apple pies, setting them out on the window ledges to cool while scheming little kids tray and snatch them away. Old folks stroll down the street, chatting the day away idly, laughing at the memories of times long past but not forgotten. A huge black man dressed in blood-red robes drags a coffin up the street, happily… Wait a minute. That’s not right. Muscles rippling under his crimson finery and long dreadlocks cascading down his back, he grunts with each pull of his gnarled rope, noisily scraping the oaken coffin along the once calm boulevard. Mothers quickly usher children away. Old folk clear the streets. Dads get their guns. The black man is angry. Very angry. His eyes dart this way and that, drinking the entire scene in. Reaching his breaking point, he drops his rope and points at anyone in sight, screaming in rage. Black Man: “You crazy crackas! Ain’t you never seen a fo’ sho’ black man before?! I ain’t comin’ fo’ you, so don’t give me a reason to, ya suburban pussies!!” Some of the feint of heart women swoon! The men dive behind bushes with fright! Children cry! Snarling, the black man grabs his rope and continues his journey, dragging the heavy wooden piece behind him with each pull. He slogs his way up the street towards the goodness that is Stately Steele Manor. Sweat running down his dark skin, the man turns his head, finally seeing the full glory that is the Manor. There is an audible grinding of his teeth as he turns and crosses the street, the sound of wood scratching on pavement. The bronze gates (turned green with age) of a ‘BAS’ with a lightning bolt stare him in the face. Fire burns in his eyes as he grabs the gates with both hands and throws his head back in maniacal laughter. The metal groans under the force of the mans jerking, a testament to his power. Teeth gritted, hands clenched in anger, the great black man heaves and seethes with intense fury!!! Ding! Dong! He presses the intercom button. Still moments pass. Intercom: (crackle – a female voice) “Hello?” The black man swings his fist, smashing it into the concrete pillar, sending a shower of fragments flying! Black Man: “Oh yeah! It’s about damn time cracka!! Now, send out that Big Ass Steele so I can whup up on the bwah!!” Intercom: (crackle) “Uh… do you, like, have an appointment or something?” Black Man: “Appointment?! Listen sista, when you have waited as looooong as I have to get your hands on that racist piece of white trash hidin’ all up in there, you don’t NEED no mutha fuckin’ appointment!! Get me Steele now or I’ll bust the place up around his ears!!!” Moments of silence. Intercom: “So, you DO have an appointment.” The man goes into a rage, screaming profanities and grabbing at the gates. With unbelievable strength, he rears back and strains at the wrought bronze, the horrific noise of shearing metal upon metal. The hinges snap off into the air, and the gate is picked up into the air by the monster of a man, tossed aside into a row of garden flowers lining the walkway. Breathing heavily, the man begins to stomp forward… Intercom: (crackle!) “Okay, Big Al says he’ll see you there. Just hang on a minute. It’s his lunchtime you know…” The huge man slams a fist into his palm, grinding his knuckles against the skin of his hand in anticipation. None too long, we rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he steps from the comforts of Stately Steele Manor, and closes the door behind him. He strolls passively down the front walk, a slight smile plastered on his big mug, a cool breeze rustling his white toga. In his right hand, a mammoth sandwich – turkey on wheat, lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, Swiss and gobs of mustard and mayo. Three large hunks have been ripped from the Herculean-sized morsel, as he contentedly munches at his latest efforting. The huge black man tenses all his muscles, his body clenching itself into a huddled mass of extreme raging anger. Steele slows his walk, stepping through the rungs of his former gate, now torn aside by the fuming black man harnessing the power of hatred before him. Craning his head, he can see the oaken coffin behind the great man, half in the street, half on the sidewalk. Veins begin forming on the forehead of the black man, filling up like strange blood balloon animals… OF DEATH! Big Al smiles widely, though keeping his mouth shut due to the unfortunate amount of food in his face. He waves at the man, a flick of mayo shaking from his fingers and hitting the man square between the eyes. It sizzles against his heated surface. Suddenly, the man levels a shaking finger at Big Al, his voice a force behind his words. Black man: “Whassa matta’, DOMINO?! (snaps his finger) Forget me so soon?!?!” Big Al’s eyes narrow in confusion. He then shrugs, still trying to process the food in his mouth. Black man: “It’s ME! Black Samson! The man you WRONGED so long ago! You know why I drag this coffin around e’rywhere I go?!” Steele again cranes his neck at the plain wooden coffin. He shrugs an answer of “go ahead.” Black Samson: “Well, I ain’t gonna tell ya yet! I’ll bet you don’t even remember what you did to me, do ya?!” Big Al swallows hard, passing a huge bolus of sandwich. Big Alan Steele: “Dear friend, if I have wronged you in any fashion…” Black Samson: “Wronged me?! WRONGED ME he says!! I should say you have, cracka! Just two weeks ago, at the Sav-Rite Supermarket…” Flashback to the store. Black Samson and Big Al each arrive at the front door simultaneously. Big Alan Steele then reaches forward and grasps the door handle, pulling it open. He then steps back, making a grand gesture to allow Black Samson through. Steam shoots from Samson’s ears as he walks by… Back to current time. Black Samson: (screaming) “Oh, sure!! Hold da door fo’ the BLACK man! Make sure you hold that door wide open for the BLACK man! Because that will surely make up for 400 YEARS OF SLAVERY by your white-devil ancestors!!!” BAS: (confused and hungry) “But surely, my Earthly mother claimed me of Polish descent…” Black Samson: “And look at yo’ cracka ass! Living the high life! Surrounding yo’self with all these women of ARYAN descent!! Not ONE black character in yo’ stable of characters fo’ yo’ promos!!” BAS: (through clenched teeth) “Kayfabe!” Black Samson: “To Hell with yo’ precious kayfabe!! All these years, OPPRESSING my people… and now I’m gonna take out 400 years of my brutha’s aggression out on your lily white Caucasian ass! NOW! Do you know why I drag this here coffin around?” BAS: (sighing) “By the gods, no I do not…” Black Samson: (snapping fingers) “It’s ta bury your cracka ass in!! BWAHAHAHAHA!!!” Black Samson rears back and roars with laughter, just as a moving van rumbles down the street and OVER the coffin, splintering it to the four winds. Samson stops his laughing and sputters in, what else, rage. Steele grins and looks up to the heavens. BAS: “A sign from the gods…” Black Samson: (screaming) “I’ll give you a sign from YO’ MOMMA!!” Black Samson cocks back his fist and aims a haymaker at the jaw of Big Alan Steele… Hours of intense battle later, the lawn of Stately Steele Manor is a desolate war zone. The grass is gone, burned away. The trees have no leaves. The Manor’s façade is in shambles, pieces of siding torn off, and plaster chunks ripped from the foundation. Huge holes are now where once the mighty fence surrounding the manor once stood. The Army Reserves have been called in, encircling the compound with all manner of heavy artillery and armored equipment, acting as the surrounding wrestlers in a lumberjack match. The smell of embers hangs heavy in the still spring air. Big Al sits back against what used to be a ivy-covered statue honoring Aphrodite. His once white toga is now in tatters, torn into shred from the intense combat just taken place. Black Samson sits just across from him, resting against a statue that used to honor Athena. Both gladiators look over warily at each other. And grin. BAS: (booming) “An excellent spar! You are quite the combatant, Lord of Blackness!!” Black Samson: (wry) “Yo ain’t so bad yo’self, brutha!” Steele reaches into the folds of his toga, pulling out the sandwich, still in perfect three-bite missing condition. Smiling, he looks over at Black Samson. BAS: “You realize, I haven’t had a steady sparring partner in quite some time!! Perhaps YOU could be that said man!!” Black Samson Stops grinning. He stands and turns around. His shoulders begin to shake violently. Black Samson: (rage building) “Oh, NOW I get it! You want me, a BLACK man, to work fo’ you, the MAN, huh? We ain’ts gots no black men in our roleplays…” BAS: (hissing) “Kayfabe!!” Black Samson: “… so let’s go and BUY OURSELVES A NEGRO?!?! So you can look like the big man, huh?! The Big slave owner on the block!! Oh, you’ve gone and done it now!!” Samson turns, his eyes burning with fury. Black Samson: “400 HUNDRED YEARS OF SLAVERY, comin’ right outta yo’ ass, ya stupid cracka ass biz-nitch!” Big Al sighs as he tucks his luscious turkey sandwich away in the folds of his toga. BAS: (mumbling) “At least I shall train well for the Lord of False Prophesy!” Black Samson: (winding up) “Don’t be usin’ that white man’s tongue round me, punk bitch!” BAS: (preparing to go for round 2) “Hi-Keeba!!”
