When Two Worlds Collide: Alexander Pryce Vs American Idol

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [The scene opens in Alexander Pryce’s luxurious Beach House which overlooks Huntington Beach- the very beach made famous by Pamela Anderson’s bouncing bosoms and David Hasslehoff’s remarkable acting abilities in that hallmark of Television Greatness- Baywatch. Lounging back against the sofa watching television is none other than everyone’s favourite Pop Star- Alexander Pryce. Sipping on Orange Gatorade, he casually flicks through the TV channels using his remote control. [Alexander flicks to a channel that features Oprah talking about her miracle weight loss] Oprah: It feels so great to have lost those few pounds!” Alexander: Fantastic, just a few more hundred to go. [He then flicks stations to a channel that is explaining the lifecycle of the cockroach] Alexander: That reminds me- I need to fumigate the house….. and clean the fridge. [Once again Alexander changes the channel on his remote control until he comes across the financial channel. A middle-aged man with a bad comb-over is reading the latest financial results in a dull voice] Man: The All Ords. Finished 10 points higher, while the Dow Jones and NASDAQ finished weaker on the heels of economic uncertainty throughout the world. [Alexander looks at the television presenter in confusion] Alexander: Nasdaq? I thought we were still bombing Iraq? [Before Alexander can finish pondering this thought, his mother, Marie, walks into the room] Marie: What are you watching? Alexander: I don’t know, but I think we’ve just started a war with another country. Marie: Which one is it today? Alexander: Some place called Nasdaq. Marie: Never heard of it- must be somewhere in Africa. Why aren’t you watching American Idol? Alexander: FOX sucks. Marie: Isn’t PCW on FOX? Alexander: I thought we were on CBS? Marie: No, that’s CSI. [Alexander shrugs his shoulders. He then switches channels to FOX where an ugly and overweight 15 year-old named Dianna is belting out a tune much to the delight on the crowd] Alexander: Look at this crap. If I wanted to see warthogs I’d watch Animal Planet. Where are the real women at? [Having finished her song, Dianna begins to receive some feedback from the judges] Randy: Dianna, I was feeling that, dawg. It had a great vibe aiight!?! Alexander: What exactly was he feeling? Looks like he just finished jacking-off. [The camera then shifts to Paula who is also smiling] Paula: That was awesome. You’re pitch was great right throughout. I think you’re a real star in the making. Alexander: Hey mom, isn’t that the slut who works the tables at Hooters? Marie: No, Paula is the washed-up ho who works the Sunset Strip in that Pink Thong. Alexander: Is that the one that runs around saying “Titties for a $20! And $50 for a Facial!” Marie: Yep. [The camera then shifts to Simon Cowell. He looks at Dianna for a moment before speaking] Simon: I think it was a relatively decent performance. I thought you were losing it in the middle, but you came home with a strong finish. Alexander: Is this a horse race or a singing contest? Marie: Judging by the size of her head, she probably is a descendent of Mr. Ed. [The crowd begin to cheer as Dianna leaves the stage to talk to host Ryan Seacrest] Marie: By the way, why didn’t you enter American Idol? Surely it would have gotten you a lot more publicity? Alexander: Yeah, but those monkeys aren’t in my class. Marie: There’s only way to find out…. [Alexander turns away from the TV and looks at his mother] Alexander: You want me to be on this show? Marie: Well it’s too late to compete in it now, but maybe we can get you a cameo appearance or something similar? We could do it as a FOX Cross-Promotion between American Idol and PCW. [Alexander considers the thought of appearing on one of the world’s biggest television sensations] Alexander: Do I get my own changing room backstage? Marie: Sure. Alexander: With Coke that has no more than 3 ice cubes? Marie: Anything for my boy. Alexander: With a bowl full of M&M’s that are only blue and green? [Marie begins to grow irritated with the petty demands] Marie: That can be arranged. Alexander: As well as a- Marie: DO YOU WANT TO BE ON THE FREAKIN’ SHOW OR NOT!?! [Alexander takes one more look at the TV where the vignette of the next Idol Competitor is being shown] Alexander: Yeah, lets do it. And if the judges don’t like my performance, I could always beat the crap out of them. Marie: Or give Paula a $20 to flash her breasts. [The scene fades to black as Alexander grins at the prospect of being on American Idol]
