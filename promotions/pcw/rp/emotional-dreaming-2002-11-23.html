Emotional Dreaming

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/23/02 Emotions. What’s going on? Why am I feeling this way? What is it that’s bringing me to this conclusion? Am I weak? Oh God, tell me what’s wrong with me. I’m dying in your arms, and your just watching. LET ME BE, GOOD LORD! LET ME BE! Tears… roll down my eyes of misery as if my existence is of no essential purpose. Tears, tears I once wanted, but now possess. I’m standing there, alone high above everyone else. *Whispers* Who stand there? Come out and show yourself. *Tension* The urge to just pull out all of my hair, and scream to the top of my lungs races through my veins like a speeding bullet. End this misery end THIS misery, I ask of you. What have I done to forsaken you? Why me? Let’s go, get your brother…. *Whispers* *Quietness* Who are you? Show yourself. These words are so tormenting to hear. Why am I hearing this? Who is controlling these thoughts that race through my mind? WHY AM I BEING TREATED LIKE A TOY? WHY ME? *Echoes**Laughter* Is this some type of sick game? I’m asking you a question. My veins bulge and my mind wanders. I stand there, looking into the air. Look down! *Whispers* Where is that voice coming from, what is it talking about. Then I look down. Nothing. I see nothing, nothing but an eternity of pain. I stand there atop a building with wind blowing across my chest. My fingers are aching in the numbness of the cold air. I CRY! I CRY FOREVER! Help me! *Voice fainting* Where do I shift my blame? Is it my mother? Is it my father? Who did this to me, my body, my soul, no control. I WANT CONTROL! *Screams* WHY???? I HATE THIS. I fall to my knees. I’m inches away from death, but almost like I’ve been living in a hell anyways. *Crack* Part of the building ledge cracks and I slip. I almost fall, but I don’t. Something keeps me up, I don’t know what, but it’s there. What’s there? Why is it there? I WANT TO KNOW WHY IT’S THERE? *Paranoia**Shaking* Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Why? Shh… Come out! I demand you! I know you’re here! Mommy, can I have more salad. Sure son. Who is this, what food, what son, what woman? *Breath* Am I losing it? Am I dying? Am I dead? What is this? Is this my punishment? Is this what I have lived for. I LIVE TO DIE! *Echo* Daddy’s home Go give daddy a hug, Juan. JUAN! JUAN! Where are you? Eyes bulging, body shaking, fingers twitching. Breaths, breath, breath, breath, exhale. A…A…Are you here Juan. Daddy has presents mommy. I sure do sport. I have presents for everyone. Juan, I can hear you name. I sense your spirit. What is the meaning of this? Tell me you haven’t turned on me to. Please I can’t…I can’t…I can’t…I can’t…I can’t…TAKE ANYMORE! Looking down, so far down. Dark hole, empty hole, it’s calling my name. Romantico (HUSH) Romantico (HUSH). I have to do it; they want me to do it. There’s no turning back any longer. One foot off, one to go Wait mommy I forgot one thing. *Freezes**Reflections* What are you doing here; mom wants us in the car. M………me? Yea, let’s go. Then the reflection of a young Juan Borro comes to me haunting my soul. They all follow. Every single one of them! Mom?Dad? It’s really you. Let’s go we have to get this picnic done with before it starts to rain. Take mommies hand. Joy, I feel nothing but joy. We’re finally together, my mother, father, Juan, and me. We’re one big family that’s happy. Am I in heaven? I reach for her hand and grab it. We smile, it’s majestic then it happens. NO! Dust, the hand turns to dust, the faces turn to misery. I fall, fall in a pit where there is no end. I’m dead to everyone, and dead to myself. THEY LAUGH! Laugh at me, like I’m a fool. NOOOOOOO!!!! *Alarm clock sounds* I throw myself up from my bed in a deep cold sweat that sends chills down my entire spine. IT WAS JUST A DREAM! *Deep breaths* JUST A DREAM! To be continued…………
