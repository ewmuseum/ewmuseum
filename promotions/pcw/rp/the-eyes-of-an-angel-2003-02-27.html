The Eyes of an Angel

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/03 The Eyes of an Angel Soft blue luscious round eyes, so tender they look as if they are not even real enough to be on such a perfect creature. I look in these eyes and see everything I have ever sought to be in my life. Her eyes are so alive and so vibrant and I wonder why my eyes cannot behold the same characteristics. Romantico slowly walks over to the mirror and graces his hand against his face until he holds his index finger near his eyelid. When I look in these eyes I see nothing but an empty void starving to be filled. I wonder to myself what they could ever possibly be filled with? Inspiration? I have none. Fortune? It’s come and gone and come again. Happiness? It’s just a word that could never fit in a sentence with my name. Why? I may never know. I have everything I could ever desire, but I still spend every night looking deep into my soul and finding absolutely nothing. Romantico continues to look into the mirror before pounding his fist into it with full impact. The glass shatters to right near his bare toes. His knuckles are bleeding from several open cuts, but his face never changes. Romantico just takes some time staring at the blood as it slowly drips to the golden-laced rug. Is this what they call pain? A cut isn’t pain, a physical wound isn’t pain, and nothing of any harm can lead to pain. Pain comes from you yourself. When you set out to accomplish a goal but know you’ll never get it. It was yesterday night when I stared into those wondrous eyes and realized everything I ever desired is just a figment of my imagination. Oh Romantico, please please please…Romantico, oh Romantico. I was in a moment of lust. Everything was so intense and physical that I never took the time to realize the beauty of the specimen I was with. It was dark with the lights out, but I still managed to see those blue eyes shining at me. I paused. Is something wrong, Romantico? I continued to just stare in her eyes as I went into a sudden euphoria. I realized all the promise and potential she had. Women have always said I was too good for them, but at that moment I realized it was the exact opposite. What was I? Was I but a simple man who happened to have good fortune and no future. This gorgeous figure had much more than just fortune, she had everything she could ever dream of at her fingertips. What is she doing instead? Wasting her time with a lost soul. I stopped and sat at the right end of the bed with my head down and hands crossed. I just took a deep breath and let everything slow down. What am I doing? Is something wrong? Did I do anything wrong? Oh I’m so embarrassed. I should be the one embarrassed. Why? You were, well you were fantastic. I just stared into her eyes again. I saw everything I wanted and I was jealous. This girl I once saw as just another name to the list had something I was jealous of. I could make like I have all the power in the world, but this one simple girl had ten times more then what I had. I should be the one chasing after her, not the other way around. Maybe you should leave. Oh God, I’m so embarrassed. Don’t be, it’s me not you. Isn’t that what they all say. I slowly went toward the princess with the bright blue eyes and I softly tapped her shoulder. I picked her head up with my fingers and kissed her softly on her lips of perfection. I don’t deserve you. What are you saying? If anything I don’t deserve you. You don’t know what you’re talking about. You have something that can’t be duplicated. I’m just another man with a pretty face and fortune. I have nothing to look forward to, and nothing to look back on. I’m a book without words. You’re not a book without words; you’re just a mystery, I wish I could say that, but I’m afraid it’s all empty. Then she did something I never expected. She looked in my eyes and she said just a few simple words that will stay with me for a long time. I see a future for you. She took the time and instead of unbuckling my pants, she looked in my eyes and told me what I saw. She saw something that I could never see. One question still remained: Do I have no future or was I just blind to seeing it?
