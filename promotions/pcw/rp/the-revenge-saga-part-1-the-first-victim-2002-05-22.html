The Revenge Saga Part 1: The first victim...

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/5/02 As if the weather was reading his mood, the beautiful sunlight that shone brightly not long ago, turned into a dark, grey skied, rain. John Crichton stood, blank faced, looking at the grave below him, the rain simply pouring off his motionless form. It had taken him about an hour to dig the grave, he then put Aeryn’s even more motionlessly, cold body into the hole and filled it back up with dirt. He had also fixed together two rather large sticks together to resemble some kind of rudimentary headstone. Crichton regretted not being able to get a grave at a cemetery nearer where she lived, but people ask questions when you have a dead body in your arms asking for a burial space and besides for all he knew the army was still after him. He looks over to his left, at the smoke rising in the distance, the aftermath of John Crichton’s fury. The only two things that occupied his mind was grief and the need for revenge. He wanted revenge on all the people that had wronged both he and Aeryn since his escape from the army. The people who hadn’t allowed him and Aeryn to lead a normal life, but some would argue that Crichton had sacrificed that when he joined up with the United States Military. However, that wasn’t the way he saw it and probably never would see it that way. One thing he did realise though, was that now he caused absolute chaos at the military base the Avengers and the majority of the worlds super hero’s would be after him now too. This worried him a lot, he didn’t wanna go up against these, hell he even respected most of ‘em, but he realised that the thirst for revenge was too great to resist. He was well aware of the powers of many super hero’s and ultimately knew that he could not beat many of them, but he was going to t least try to avenge Aeryn’s death. It didn’t matter to him whether he died trying or not. No it did not matter one bit and he had made up his mind, he would go ahead with this no matter the cost. He had already picked the next target for his vengeance too, the one who started this whole thing in the first place General Tranter. Crichton knew that Tranter hadn’t been on that military base, because he would have come and gloated before him. In fact, Crichton didn’t think that he was even in charge of the operation anymore, because a General by the name of Bernstein had come to check up on the progress of ‘the experiment’. This didn’t matter much to Crichton, General Tranter was the one who started the operation so he was going to be the first of Crichton’s targets. Crichton looks up to the sky as the rain slowly begins to stop and the sun escapes the shrouding, dark, clouds. The first place he would look for General Tranter was at his old military base, just on the outskirts of Chicago, Illinois. Crichton realised that Chicago was a long way away from Ohio and the thirst for revenge was too great, to waste any more time. She was gone and wasn’t coming back, Crichton had to accept that he knew, but it was the most difficult thing he had ever had to do. He kneels down at the foot of her grave and places a single floor upon the obviously un-earthed soil, lowers his head a second, tears dropping from his eyes and then simply walks away, without looking back. ************ From where Crichton was standing, hidden behind some bushes, directly opposite to the only clear entrance way, the Military base hadn’t changed much at all. The security box, near the barriers that prevent cars entering the base, seemed empty, but Crichton knew otherwise. Crichton moved from the push to his left, walking and crouching at the same time, not taking any chances. He didn’t know what kind of security measures the military base had accumulated since the last time he was three months ago, but he knew for damn sure that new security procedures had been put in place after his escape. He moved over to the exact place in the mesh wire fence, from which escaped all that time ago. Crichton wanted to make sure that he wasn’t seen until, he got into Tranter’s office, for he wanted some time alone with Tranter, to ‘teach him the error of his ways’ as Crichton liked to put it. It didn’t matter whether he was spotted after that, because of his new found powers. Crichton had to wonder whether he was relying too much upon his strength, after all he hadn’t had chance to test his abilities to the maximum level. In retrospect he should have come prepared, but his rage clouded his judgement greatly. At the military test centre in Ohio, where the super soldier serum was injected into his bloodstream, he remembered hearing the scientists talking about certain insurances that they were going to implement upon him. Apparently the scientists had planned to get Crichton addicted to some kind of specialised drug, which in theory would keep him in line, do what they wanted him to do. The army knew full well that once they had implemented the super soldier serum into Crichton’s bloodstream he wouldn’t play ball, and so needed something to prevent their own creation turning upon them. Once Crichton was addicted to the drug, he would need regular injections and once Crichton stepped out of line they would pull the plug on the specialised drug and to hell with the consequences. Luckily for him Aeryn came for him before the drug was sent into his bloodstream. Crichton inspected the mesh wire fence and the area around it, from a distance, he couldn’t see any new security measures taken by the army. This didn’t surprise him too much at all. He glanced around the general area and saw no sign of army officers. He then sprinted from the cover of the bushes and with one leap, cleared the mesh wire fence, landing smoothly upon the concrete floor. This surprised him greatly, he jumped expecting to get no further than half way, then climb the rest of the way, but he cleared it. He promised himself that he would be carrying out further tests to calculate the full potential of his new found powers, that was for damn certain. He then navigated himself through all of the back alleyways of the barracks, ensuring that nobody either saw or heard him. He assumed he had been unnoticed up to the wall by the side of Tranter’s office, hell he hadn’t seen anybody and no alarms were going off so he considered it a good thing. He quickly exited the back alleys of the barracks, then entered the reception area, again unnoticed. The receptionist, sitting behind a large pine desk, lets out a yelp in surprise then swiftly gets up from her seat and attempts to press the alarm button under the table. Crichton’s hand quickly extends, grabbing her wrist just before it touches the alarm. He hated hurting women and as an adolescent teenager he vowed he never would, under any circumstance. However, he no longer cared for his principles or the world’s view on doing the ‘wrong thing’, all that he cared about was revenge, pure and simple. He then grabs the woman by the throat lifting her high above his head. The pressure of gravity and the strength of Crichton’s grip around her throat forces her to gurgle a little. “I’m sorry I have to do this!” Crichton states honestly before throwing her against the wall. The impact with the wall is almost instantaneous and as she slides down, unconscious upon the reception floor, there is a clear imprint in the plasterwork. What surprised Crichton the most about this uncharacteristic act of violence was that he felt no guilt for his actions. The thoughts about this and his own morality soon dissolve from his head, as he realises that he finally has the opportunity to confront Tranter. Then Tranter’s office door creaks open and the General steps out into the reception begins to walk to the receptionists desk, before seeing John Crichton standing before him. “How…how did you get in here?” The general questions, fear showing in his voice. Crichton remains silent, and simply begins to casually stroll forward in Tranter’s direction. With each step Crichton gets closer to him, Tranter takes a step back. “Wha…what did you do with my receptionist?” Tranter questions. Crichton remains silent still and remains upon his vector – directly for Tranter. Tranter however, has nowhere else to go and his backed up against the wooden doorframe. Crichton then attacks, punching Tranter in the jaw, sending him through the door and into the office. Crichton follows him into the office and closes the door behind him. Tranter manages to lift himself up from the ground, using his mahogany table as leverage. “NO! Stay back! I’m sorry Crichton, I was only following orders! I…” Tranter trails off. “Be quiet, maggot and accept that your judgement day has finally arrived!” Crichton declares. Tranter shakes his head in disbelief. “You plan to kill me? Your no killer Crichton, you have morals, principles, you have a conscience.” Tranter replies. “Don’t even think that you know me anymore! Being hunted can do stuff to a man’s mind! Having your true love killed can also do stuff to a man’s mind! And being used as a lab experiment to implement a super soldier serum, can do stuff to a man mind!” Crichton shouts. “Yo…you mean that they actually captured you and administered the serum?” Tranter questions. “Yeah! Can’t ya tell?” Crichton replies grabbing Tranter by the throat and hoisting him up into the air, far above his head. He holds him for a further second before launching him across the room. Tranter violently impacts upon the wall. Tranter somehow manages to stay conscious. “There is no need for this Crichton! If you kill me you will hunted more than you were before!” Tranter states defiantly. A sadistic grin forms upon Crichton’s face. “You know Tranter, I really don’t care anymore! I have nothing to live for, now that Aeryn is dead!” Crichton replies in strong tone of voice. “No Crichton, don’t kill me! I can help you avoid being captured again, please don’t hurt me!” Tranter pleads. Crichton then breaks out into a manic laugh. “Don’t try to appeal to my good side Tranter!…[Walking up to the general, who is now upon his knees] I no longer have one!” Crichton declares, before snapping the mans neck with one swift movement. “Paybacks a bitch ain’t it” he adds. Crichton then walks to the other end of Tranters luxurious office and sits upon his desk. He then places the palm of his hands before his eyes. The revenge had been delivered to the first of his victims, now that this barrier was broken, he could no longer resist the search for more revenge. The next victim of revenge was unknown to him…for now.
