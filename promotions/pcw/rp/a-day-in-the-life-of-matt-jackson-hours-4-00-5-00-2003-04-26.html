A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 4:00-5:00

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/03 I am going to die in this small room and no one is even going to know about it. No one is going to miss me and no one is going to care. Those who do are going to be glad that the murder is dead and those who actually know me are going to pass it off as a hazard of the job. Those in the PCW are just going to be glad to have no less thorn in their sides. Stone Burns, James Edwards, and Sivasian Krikorian are going to rejoice that they have one less person to deal with at Turmoil and they are going to be glad that they don’t have to face the growing power that is me. Probably better for them. This isn’t the way I pictured dying. I never thought I was going to go out bloody, beaten, and alone. I never wanted to die alone; I wanted to die with some sort of legacy, with a family and friends. Instead I am going to die hanging by a rope, because I was stupid and made a mistake. I knew when I took the job working for the CIA could be troublesome at times, but I never envisioned this. If this is the way it must be then so be it. I would rather die in the position I am in now, then struggle to make my rise to the top and watch others step over me. All my hard work isn’t going to be wasted. I am going out doing the thing I love, thinking about the person I love. I love you Haley, I am not dying alone, because without you knowing it. You are here; you opened me up and made me no longer feel alone. Thank you. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 4:00 am Matt stands quietly in the room, letting the sweat and blood drip down his body. The time is passing slowly and he is no closer to escaping. The room still smells of stale vomit and the smell is beginning to make him sick, as he is forced to inhale the air. It doesn’t matter to him because he is glad that he is still able to breathe, after the damage to his lungs. His life is coming to an end so quickly and he doesn’t know how to react to it. His whole 5’8” frame is screaming out in pain, wishing it would get a few moments of rest. Nothing comes of it. Matt is forced to stand his whole weight on his legs and hope they don’t give out on him anytime soon, because if they do then his arms are going to be pulled and wretched. The game is ending and it doesn’t look like Matt is going to be the winner in this one. You have to get out of here Matt. You have to get out of here for Haley...she can’t survive on her own. You have to be the one to protect her from her father. You have to be the one to protect her from all the evils in the world. His brown eyes begin to softly scan the dark room looking for the source of the rope. The room is too dark for him to get any real idea of where to even start. Taking the top of the rope in his hands, he lets out a sigh, glad that the pressure of the tight twine isn’t cutting into his flesh anymore. These small reliefs were the only things that were keeping him going… the only things that were keeping him alive. There has to be a way out of here… there has to be a way to survive. I will get out of this. His body trembling, Matt moves his feet around trying to find where the rope is tied. He only ends up feeling the sopped up blood and vomit that has been absorbed into the dirt floor. The pain in his feet flares up, as the dirt gets taken into his open cuts. He quickly pulls his feet away, not letting his determination waiver. Nothing is going to stop me from getting out of here…from getting back to Haley. I will not die alone. Shaking his head, tossing his sweaty brown hair from side to side, Matt allows his feet to feel for the rope again, believing that the rope is anchored to the floor some how. Not finding anything Matt straightens out his body and allows his muscles to relax a tiny bit, the strain is still there though and his legs won’t be able to support him much longer. The ex-Ranger’s survival skills are beginning to kick in, as he feels even more blood spill from his body. He is used to it now and the feeling of pain, as the sweat fills the open wounds is quickly blocked out by his own drive for freedom. For the first time in a few hours, his body is feeling rejuvenated and he knows that he can get free. Looking frustrated Matt tugs on the rope, wishing that he could break it off of its anchor. Nothing comes of it and Matt just goes back to his hanging position. “Damnit!” He screams, kicking up some of the dirt under his feet, wincing in pain as he does. There has to be a away out of this. Something I am missing and something I have to find. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 4:30 am Tugging on the rope, Matt starts to think this whole thing is pointless, with the little strength he has left; Matt grabs the rope above his head and pulls himself up, inch by inch. At the top, he reaches up with his left hand and grabs the steel poll. This is it… this is where the rope is anchored; just a few seconds and I will be out of here. I will be free! Giving it everything he has, Matt pulls on the rope and loosens the knot, but it still isn’t enough. Shaking, Jackson lets go of the poll and begins his free fall towards the hard ground, only a few feet away. Buckling under his weight, the knot snaps under his weight… he is free now and quickly unties his hands and tries to regain his senses. His arms drop to his sides, feeling like they are being tied down by weights. He wants to just collapse on the floor, but he must keep going… he is so close to his goal. Making his way around the room, Matt slowly allows his legs to stretch out and gain there bearings back. His body is even sorer then it was when he was tied up, but he has to act quickly if he wants to get out of here, rubbing the sweat out of his eyes, it really doesn’t help any… only letting more blood and sweat to fill his eyes. Dragging himself over to the door, Matt lets the pain flow through out his body, doing everything in his power to block it out. Standing in front of him is the gateway to the outside world…all he needs to do is pull on the handle. This flimsy door, is his ticket to freedom, and the brass handle has never felt this good in his hands, pulling the huge door open, Matt feels the cool air hit is broken body… it feels good allowing the sweat to dry on his naked body. I wish I would have been able to get my clothes, but I guess you can’t have everything you want in this situation… Taking a step out, Matt rests his feet on the cold cement, letting them get a break for the first time in a while… cuts and bruises mean nothing to him right now, he is so close to his goal… he is so close to getting his freedom back. Darting his eyes around the new room, he can’t tell the difference… the darkness won’t allow him too. No matter though, he knows he is out. Making a run in the general direction of moonlight, Matt reaches the door… here it is the exit- the fix to his mistake. Pulling open the glass doors, he steps outside, letting the cool night air hit him like semi. Taking a few deep breaths, Matt starts to jog out the door, and into the moonlight… his body feels crisp in the newly found air and the cuts and bruises are getting some much-needed relief. Giving his body a second to loosen up a little more, Matt takes off toward his Explorer hoping it will still be there. “STOP!” a voice yells out, turning around Matt notices the man standing there…pistol drawn and pointed at him. Run damnit run! You worked too hard for this…don’t stop now! Matt tries to run, but his body just won’t let him… his body is to worn down and he can’t push it any more then it all ready is being pushed. Just standing is a task in and of itself, but he does everything he can to push on. His legs feel like two gigantic rocks, as he takes each step…each step harder then the first and straining his over worked muscles more. Snapping his head over his shoulder… Matt looks back seeing the man gaining on him more and more, getting closer by the second. Without a second look over his shoulder…Matt feels the electrical voltage flow through him, slowing him down and taking away his remaining energy. Hitting the cold pavement with a thud, Matt grinds his teeth and falls victim to unconsciousness once more. I am sorry Haley, please forgive me. 5:00 AM
