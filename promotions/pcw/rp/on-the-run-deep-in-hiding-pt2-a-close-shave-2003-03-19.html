On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt2- A Close Shave

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th March During March Madness With a menacing stare behind him, El Diablo quickly jumps into his parked vehicle and hurriedly speeds away from the arena, leaving all the carnage he has caused behind him. Returning at March Madness was a lot of work in the making from El Diablo, and by leaving March Madness he's sure to feel slightly better about things knowing that he has seriously hurt his mortal enemy Flaymin Aymin. Days Later Now that a few days have passed since that Madness, El Diablo is still in hiding from Flaymin Aymin, perhaps he is fearful of him or maybe this is just part of his master plan, if he even has a plan. Diablo's black van is parked outside of a bar somewhere in San Jose, California. The bar isn't a popular one, but more of a local bar, so of course El Diablo will be out of place just upon entering the bar. Inside the bar, there are several stools placed at the main bar and then just a few tables based around the two pool tables in the center of the room. There must be twenty-five - thirty people in the bar this night, including El Diablo who is sat directly at the main bar drinking out of a bottle- the contents of the bottle are of course Tequila, Diablo's favorite drink, afterall it's Mexican. Also around the bar is a darts board and a hallway that leads to the restrooms. Behind the main bar are two people, one male and one female. The male is middle aged with a large bald spot on his head, and a grey horseshoe like haircut around it. The woman on the other hand is young and has long blonde hair and is quite attractive. It seems the people behind the bar are father and daughter, the father seems to own the bar. 'So, what'll it be now?' asks Curtis, the bar owner, with his blue eyes staring straight at El Diablo. The now red headed Diablo slowly looks up from his bottle Tequila, and gazes at Curtis a moment before looking back down at this drink. 'How about a shot glass and as many slices of lemon and sugar as you have,' Diablo responds before raising his bottle of Tequila to his mouth to drink away some more of his sorrows. 'Coming right up,' Curtis responds. Curtis promptly slams a shot glass down in front of El Diablo and soon enough has placed several slices of lemon with sugar on it by the glass. Talk about service. 'Just put it on my tab,' Diablo remarks while pouring some Tequila into the shot glass. Curtis looks baffled, and with his slender arms raises his hands to wipe his sweating brow. 'You're new here, you have no tab,' he retorts back to the Mexican Devil. The Mexican looks back up at Curtis, and wipes his own brow while he's at it. 'Well give me a tab for tonight then- my name is Flaymin Aymin,' Diablo lies to get out of paying for his drinks. Curtis nods and starts to take note of what El Diablo is ordering. Diablo can only smirk at his fairytale before taking another shot of Tequila. Overhearing El Diablo, the young blonde at the bar wanders over to him. 'Hi, I'm Tracy, welcome to our bar,' she's most definitely a bubbly blonde, not a dumb blonde but just someone who is always happy. El D looks up and just shrugs. The blonde haired, blue eyed, well-defined Tracy however still remains smiling. 'Awww, you're looking very depressed, what's wrong?' she asks him, with a genuine look of concern in her eyes. Diablo runs his right hand through his short dyed red hair and sighs. 'My life is what's wrong,' he responds in his typical Mexican accent, 'Ever since last year I've had problems all because of one man, he's my mortal enemy and has been out to get me ever since,' Tracy looks baffled by his story, yet intrigued. 'In fact, I did something bad, real bad and that is why I'm here and laying low,' Diablo continues. Why El Diablo is opening up is really baffling considering that he could get in serious trouble for what he did to the real Flaymin Aymin at March Madness, but it seems that he really doesn't care about the ramifications anymore. He truly hates Flaymin Aymin. Tracy looks on and smiles at El Diablo, despite the fact that he has his head down and is not looking at her. 'Well, things could be worse,' Tracy responds. El Diablo now looks up at her and smirks. 'HOW THE HELL COULD THEY BE ANY WORSE? YOU DON'T KNOW ME!' he yells at her loudly, smashing his bottle of Tequila in the process. Tracy takes a step back looking a little scared, meanwhile her father Curtis steps forward. 'Now I don't care about that broken bottle or the spilt drink, but no one yells at my daughter like that. So either get the hell out or buy drinks all round and apologise,' Curtis states sternly. Diablo looks down at the floor, where his smashed bottle of Tequila lays. 'You know, that broken bottle and spilt drink represent my life, I am broken and my life is bleeding away out of me. My only way to become whole once again is to destroy that mortal enemy I have, Flay...um El Diablo,' he mourns and continues to hide his lie. Curtis looks across at him, from behind the bar once more. 'Apologise now or get the hell out,' he reiterates as he stands in front of his daughter Tracy to protect her. El Diablo looks back up. 'I'M THINKING OK? IS A MAN NOT ALLOWED TO THINK?' he yells once more. The angry yet depressed El Diablo drops to his knees and starts to pick up the shards of glass from the broken bottle. 'He ruined my life, he ruined me,' Diablo rambles on, 'Drinks all around, I'm so sorry,' he finishes in a worrying tone of voice, even for him. The Mexican Devil slowly gets to his feet and places the broken shards of glass on the bar, but behind him a man shouts. 'HEY, WE DON'T TAKE KINDLY TO YOUR TYPE AROUND HERE!' the voice says. El D quickly turns around, to see a man holding a pool cue in his left hand. The tall, well built man steps forward, 'How about a game of pool, mister,' he says while running his free hand through his thick black beard. El Diablo smirks and then steps backwards. 'Give me a break, I'm Mexican,' he responds to the challenge. 'You just don't get it, I wasn't asking you, I was telling you. Besides you should feel right at home then in California,' the tall man says right through his teeth. Diablo takes another step backwards. 'Look I don't play pool, I wrestle,' he calmly states. Diablo's contrasting moods really are worrying, especially for someone in a career such as his. It is a real problem. 'Well, it'll be easier to hustle you then,' the man replies. Suddenly the barmaid Tracy interrupts the proceedings. 'Look Jim, he didn't mean any harm, he's just a bit down on his luck currently,' Tracy tries to convince Jim. Diablo looks back at Tracy and nods in agreement. 'Yeah, I'm down on my luck, that's all,' he tries to convince him as well. El Diablo succeeds in backing down, as little did he know Tracy and Jim are seeing each other and she has a massive influence over him. 'OK fine, just go then and don't come back,' Jim says whilst holding the pool cue in an offensive way. Diablo raises his arms like in a way of surrender and slowly backs away out of the side door of the bar. 'Awww dammit Jim, that Flaymin Aymin guy didn't even pay his tab,' Curtis says angrily to Jim. Jim shakes his head and lowers his pool cue, 'I'm sorry Curtis,' Suddenly the main door to the bar bursts open, and a man wearing a large natural afro stands there. 'I'm Flaymin Aymin, now have you seen a short Mexican guy?' Flaymin asks the people in the local bar. Curtis just simply smiles. With Flaymin Aymin so close on El Diablo's tracks, how can Diablo seriously expect to run and hide from Flaymin forever? Can he remain a desired distance from his enemy? Time will only tell. Outside the bar, El Diablo's black van can be seen leaving with El Diablo saying a few choice words as he leaves. 'Turmoil, it will all start over again.'
