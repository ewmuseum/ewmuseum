Flaymin: I didn’t like it, so I scrapped it and moved on to Plan C. Hirraani: But since you scrapped the original Plan B, shouldn’t Plan C just simply be called the new Plan B? [Frustrated with the bombardment of questions, Flaymin looks over at Hirraani] Flaymin: For a gypsy you sure do ask a lot of questions. [Flaymin leaves Hirraani and walks over to the ‘performers caravans’.He looks at the dozen or so caravans and scratches his head.
