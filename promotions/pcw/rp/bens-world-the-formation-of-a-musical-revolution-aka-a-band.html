Bens World - The Formation of a Musical Revolution aka a Band

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (7th April) I feel as if I am achieving something. All the years of searching and endless nights of wandering, are starting to make sense. I look around and I can feel it all coming together. It was here that I came to be and it is here that I will cease to exist. I feel the end of my search is near, for I found them, and I believe it is they who hold the key. I will follow. I will always follow. My friends surround me, Andy Murray, John Crichton, Fazz McLovely and my self. Im sitting in a room, with my friends, in my lap an acoustic guitar, in my head a tune. I begin to strum, one chord, two chord, three chords, four. My left hand moves nimbly up and down the neck of the guitar, my right hand keeps a perfect rhythym, strumming..... Words escape my lips, lyrics, 'Please come now I think I’m falling, I’m holding on to all I think is safe' My friends sit silently, listening, unaware of what to do, not wanting to disturb me, my thoughts, my dreams, my words borrowed from the song book of Creed, their words borrowed from the songbook of Heaven. 'I cried out heaven save me. But I’m down to one last breath' The words are not mine but they are familiar, the song is not mine but it fits, it relates to my life, it relates to my emotions, it relates to my musical taste. Andy Murray looks up, his eyes light up. 'A band' he cries. 'We could form a band.' The outburst causes confusion. 'A band?' replied Fazz 'A band.' says Andy smiling. 'A band?' says Fazz McLovely. 'A band.' says Andy smiling broadly. 'Sad eyes follow me, But I still believe there’s something left for me, So please come stay with me, ‘Cause I still believe there’s something left for you and me' I continue strumming and singing quietly as the conversation takes place. 'Ben can play guitar.' says Andy. 'and he can sing.' 'But thats not a band.' replies Fazz 'Thats just a man with an acoustic guitar.' 'But I can play drums.' says Andy Luckily Chris Matthews walks in. 'Hi.' he says. 'I can play guitar.' 'Sweet.' says Andy. 'I CAN PLAY BASS!!!' shouts Fazz. 'Calm down.' says Andy. 'Your disturbing Bens karma.' 'Shut up!' says Ben. 'Well...we have guitar, lyrics, bass, I guess I can play...' says Andy. 'AIR TUBA!' shouts Fazz. 'Calm yourself down, I was about to say I can play drums.' says Andy. I tear myself away from their riveting conversation to continue strumming the acoustic guitar, the resonating sound rings in my ears, my voice trails away into melody, im losing myself in the music. And thus a band was formed......
