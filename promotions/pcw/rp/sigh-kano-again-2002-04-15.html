*sigh*.....Kano again?

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/15/02 Darkness. Giggling. Moans of pleasure. You’re all perverts. As the scene comes into view we can plainly see that the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson, is at the health spa getting a pedicure. He sits in a snow white bath robe with ‘TN’ monogrammed on the front left breast. A look of ecstasy blankets his face as a beautiful, and well endowed, blonde diligently scrubs away at his feet. Her all white uniform is ill-fitting in all the right ways, allowing her ample bosom to be plainly seen from the CEO’s vantage point. “You certainly have nice….,” Tyler pauses for effect. “…hands. That feels great.” The blonde, her name tag reads Elizabeth, peeks up at Tyler and giggles once again. A reddish hue colors her cheeks from embarrassment. “Why thank you, Mr. Nelson,” purrs Elizabeth. “That means a lot coming from a man of your stature and greatness.” “I’m sure it does,” agrees Tyler. Elizabeth takes a brush and begins to exfoliate Tyler’s feet, getting all of those dead skin cells and whatever else grows on feet off of them. “Your feet are almost flawless, Mr. Nelson,” says Elizabeth with a hint of shock in her voice. “How often to you have pedicure’s?” Tyler rolls his eyes back in thought. “Hmmm…..I’d say about four or five times a week.” Elizabeth stops her work and looks up at Tyler, seemingly impressed with the frequency of his pedicure’s. “Four or five? Wow!! Why so many? Normally guys only have a pedicure a few times a month.” A cocky smirk crosses Tyler’s lips. “Well sweet cakes,” oozes Tyler. “I am the CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling.” Elizabeth’s face lights up. “That wrestling place?” she squeals. “One in the same,” says Tyler confidently, sensing that he could be on to something with the blonde. “ I own the place…..along with a few other people but that’s beside the point. I’m the CEO.” Elizabeth has totally stopped what she was doing and has all her attention focused on Tyler. The CEO has her hooked, and is about to reel her in. “You must have a lot of money and power,” coos the beautician as she looks up at Tyler with stars, and maybe dollar signs, in her eyes. “Money, Power, and Respect,” corrects Tyler. “And lots of it. You see, being the CEO of PCW is a more prestigious position to hold than the President of the United States, King of France, or even Player of the Year at the LA Players Ball.” Elizabeth looks somewhat puzzled at the last part of Tyler’s statement, but the CEO quickly moves on so as not to cause the blonde to overheat. “With all that Money, Power, and Respect also comes a great deal of responsibility,” explains the CEO. “As owner of the company it is my duty to maintain a quality roster of talent. When we have a worthless jobber on the roster taking up space, it’s my duty to cut him loose and free PCW from said jobber. Elizabeth can only muster up a light “Wow!” Tyler can see that he now has the young, busty, beautiful woman eating from the palm of his hand, so he decides to go in for the kill. “Now normally I would just terminate the contracts of the pieces of shit and be done with them, because frankly that is all they deserve. This is the method I used to cut bait on the likes of Kevin Klaymore and Brock Newbludd.” “Who?” wonders Elizabeth. “Exactly!” exclaims Tyler. “There are, however, special occasions where I feel it is my duty as CEO to make an example of the most worthless of jobbers. Take for example one Jon Kano.” “Who?” wonders Elizabeth again. “Exactly!!” exclaims Tyler again. “This guy actually has the nerve to utter my name and think he has the right to say it. I am so infinitely better than this schmuck that it’s laughable. The crowning moment of this jackasses career is when I, Tyler Nelson, literally gave him the PCW Television Title. It was a weak moment for me personally and for some reason I thought that Kano could actually serve some sort of purpose.” Elizabeth frowns. “I know, stupid me,” concedes Tyler. “I had a lot of things going on….there was Media, and Fed Wars, and…..anyway, back to the moral of the story. Every once in a while I feel it is my job as CEO to go out and take care of personnel situations personally. To sort of make an example of the jobbers that think they are better than they actually are.” Elizabeth looks lost. “So….what does a pedicure have to do with all of this?” Tyler calms the young beauty. “I’m getting to that, don’t worry.” “Okay,” says Elizabeth joyfully as if she was promised a treat for being good. Tyler notices the density of the blonde and semi rolls his eyes. “I get all those pedicures because I put my foot so far up those jobbers’ asses when I kick them, that I have to have my feet cleaned to get all the mess off of them.” A look of discovery pours over Elizabeth’s face. “Aaaaaaahhhh….I see. So who’s mess are you getting cleaned off today?” “This dirty, slimey, stinky, no good, backstabbing, burrito eating White Mexican,” seethes Tyler with contempt. “I did like I just explained to you and kicked the living shit out of him last Sunday, then sent him packing for the unemployment line. I just haven’t had the time to get a decent pedicure since.” A close examination of the CEO’s feet reveals something interesting. “Hey….as a matter of fact I think I see a piece of refried bean on your big toe,” Elizabeth points out. She quickly picks up her exfoliating brush and begins to scrub away with a purpose. “There….all done!” Tyler looks down, stopping briefly at Elizabeth’s cleavage, at his feet. A smirk crosses his lips. “Looks good sweetheart,” compliments the CEO. “You know….I have something else that could use some TLC, if you catch my drift.” Tyler winks at Elizabeth, who returns the advance with a high pitched giggle and more blushing. “I get off work in about fifteen minutes,” says Elizabeth lustily. “My limo will be waiting out front,” says Tyler as he stand up from his seat. He heads off toward the dressing room as Elizabeth hastily cleans up her workstation. “Heh….looks like I’ll have to come back in a few days after I kick the shit out of Kano. Just a little added motivation….as if I needed it. The Ultimate Authority has no room for worthless people like Kano and he will be dealt with accordingly.” Tyler strolls out of view and the scene fades to black.
