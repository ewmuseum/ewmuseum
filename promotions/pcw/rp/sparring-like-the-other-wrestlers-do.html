Sparring like the other wrestlers do.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Okay, pick me up at eight, but remember the candy and roses... (WHUMPH!!!) “Oww!” (We see THE Multiple Man flat on his ass in the middle of the seldom-used training room built underneath his secret hideaway. Keira Marshall, dressed in loose gym clothing, looms over him with her arms crossed.) Keira: “Seriously, are you even trying? ‘Cause this isn’t helping me one bit.” (Tmm picks himself up and falls into a relaxed combat stance.) Tmm: “What do you mean? This is some of my best stuff!” (Tmm lunges in a scything kick aimed right for Keira’s head… or at least where her head was. His leg is swept out from underneath him with snakelike speed. WHUMPH!!!) Tmm: (getting up) “Stop that!” (Keira’s face scrunches up in confusion.) Keira: “Stop what?” Tmm: “The stuff! The ninja stuff! Act like a real wrestler for just once in your life!” Keira: “Huh, if you insist.” (She tilts her head, crosses her eyes, and sticks out her tongue.) Keira: “Wook at me, ah is a big dhum wesler. Taste mah furhy. And dat iz da bahttom line.” (Tmm moves in, POW! A right hook cranks his jaw around good!) Tmm: (screeching) “Yee-ow!” Keira: “C’mon! You walked right into that! Suck it up, you baby!” Tmm: “You said you were going to act like a wrestler!” Keira: “Wasn’t that what I was just doing?” Tmm: “Haven’t you ever sparred before in your life?!” (Keira looks thoughtful.) Keira: “Mmm…” (Tmm attacks! WHOOF! The air in his lungs explodes out and he staggers back from the boot to his gut.) Keira: (sweetly) “… no?” Tmm: (gasping for air) “Sparring… is… just… practice!” Keira: “Pfft! What’s the point?” Tmm: (gasping less) “Didn’t Ninja… Bob Roberts… do it… with you?” (Shrug.) Keira: “Naw, it was mostly like this. ‘Cept he was me and I was you. You wouldn’t believe how fast I picked up his lessons when the alternative was getting my butt whipped.” Tmm: “Not even in the very beginning?!” Keira: “Well, we kinda did, I guess. Didn’t use weapons then.” (Tmm grimaces.) Tmm: “Tell you what, this time you attack me.” (WHAP! WHAP! WHUMPH!!! Tmm drops to the mat like a rock.) Tmm: “Ow.” Keira: “Give up?” (Tmm, sprawled on the floor again, gives her a sour look.) Tmm: “No.” Keira: “Have it your way.” Tmm: “Look. The whole point of sparring in front of the cameras is for you to intimidate upcoming opponents by seeming to be an invincible warrior!” Keira: “Right. That’s why I’m kicking your ass.” (Tmm slaps an open palm to his face in exasperation.) Tmm: “NO! By ‘you’, I mean me! You’re supposed to throw the fight to make me look good! Like those guys Supreme Machine was wrestling! Don’t you get it?! It’s psychological warfare! Public relations stuff!” Keira: “Me.” Tmm: “Yes.” Keira: “Throw the fight.” Tmm: “Yes!” Keira: “To make you look tough.” Tmm: “YES!!!” (Keira looks at a fist reflectively.) Keira: “Maybe that last hit to the head was a little too hard, you’re cracking up. Next thing you’ll be wanting is for me and Val to accompany you to ringside in thongs or something.” (She snorts briefly in laughter and turns to leave.) Keira: “We’ll continue this later, I think.” (She bounds up the steps spryly, not even winded in the least. Tmm drums the mat with his fists in frustration.) Tmm: “Why in the hell can’t my damn employees do what they’re supposed to do just ONCE in my fricking life!?” (Heavy thumps on the steps announce the arrival of Steve.) Steve: “Yeah? Keira said you wanted to see me?” (Tmm goes from horizontal to vertical almost without bothering with the positions in between.) Tmm: “Yessss, that’s right, I did. Come over here, I want you to see something…” Steve: “Okay.” (Steve walks to Tmm and the camera fades out.)
