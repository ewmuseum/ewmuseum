Bob Vurdo, huh? Fine, but first CUTTING A PROMO AGAINST JON KANO!

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/2/02 A sunny day, not a cloud in the sky. Atlantic City, or as the natives call it, 'DONALD TRUMP OWNS US ALL!' This city is home to THE MAN WHO RULES, Jim Onak! Only 27 years old, he is both an full-time professional boxer and the head of his own company, Onak Enterprises. Though if you asked anyone, even Onak, what his company does, you'd get a confused shrug at best, the inquisitee scratching his/her head and furrowing his/her brow in an attempt in vain to give an answer to the question. Indeed, Onak Enterprises has created an entire workforce of characters like 'Tommy' on Martin. They're supposed to have jobs but no one knows what they do. Here we find Jim Onak. He has a large presence, standing 6'11' and weighing 297 pounds. He has blond hair that's slicked back, solid from the 10 pounds of styling gel and activator he puts on it this and every morning. Oh, and he's not even blond. Wash that stuff out and he's as tow-headed as Carrot Top. Wearing a yellow blazer over a light blue dress shirt, with khaki slacks and brown Ralph Lauren loafers, he is an odd sight, grizzled from battle but refined for the fact that he has to dress that way for work outside of the ring. He's muscular, but is still something of a pushover. Men who have had glaring weight disadvantages have still kicked his ass because he can't take a hit. So, of course, despite being so large, he hasn't really won much in his near two-year boxing career. Again, we find Jim Onak, lounging out in front of a local Starbucks. To the left of the establishment is a Starbucks. To the right, a Starbucks. The next left and right establishments, and the next ten after that down the road are Starbucks. Across the street, Verizon and a Kinko's... no, wait, they just became a Starbucks. And next to that Blockbuster Video is about to become a Starbucks in 10 minutes. But anyway, Jim Onak is sitting out in front of a particular Starbucks when the camera spots him. Everything's recording, and he turns to the camera as though he's surprised they found him even though he called them to do this. Jim Onak:'Well, I was trying to relax and enjoy a sunny, yet breezy day when a camera crew stalked me for about two hours. Finally, I had to just let them approach me and cut another God Promo. Not that I really enjoy doing these things... no, I'm lying, I love these things. I get to talk and you have to listen to-- me....' The camera pans away from Jim Onak. Actually, the cameraman is already bored and is leaving. Jim Onak:'HEY! GET BACK HERE!' The camera turns around, and Jim Onak is running after him. Onak catches up and attempts to yank the camera away, but the smaller 5'7' 140 pound cameraman shoves Onak to the ground. He leans up on his elbows looking up at the camera. Jim Onak:'Can we edit that part out?' The camera shakes from side to side. Jim Onak:'Dammit! Well, let's just start over, and I'll give you $5000 to sit there and record me?' Once again, the camera shakes from side-to-side. Frustrated, Onak takes out a wad of cash. Jim Onak:'Then I'll give ya 10 thousand to leave the camera with me.' The camera drops to the ground, and the cameraman runs off with $65 thousand, snatching Onak's whole cash clip. Only an idiot would flash the whole thing. Now with no money on him, he resolves that he'll find the cameraman and do... something to him. But in the meantime he does have a promo to shoot. He picks up the camera and sets it up on a table across from where he was sitting. Onak goes back to his seat, picks up the newspaper he was reading, and grabs the 12 oz. cup of Starbucks double mocha cinnamon almond capaccino blast freezer. His eyes go to the paper and he moves his eyes as if reading it. Then he counts down to the promo again. Jim Onak:'3... 2... 1...' Onak turns to the camera after taking a sip from his Starbucks drink. Once again, he acts surprised over what's going on. Jim Onak:'Oh! You want some words from me? Well, then, I suppose I could take a few moments out of my busy day to talk to you. I mean, I was trying to relax and enjoy a sunny, yet breezy day when a camera crew stalked me for about two hours. Finally, I had to just let them approach me and cut another God Promo. Not that I really enjoy doing these things... no, I'm lying, I love these things. I get to talk and you have to listen to-- me....' Someone decides it's a good idea to steal the camera. Of course, Onak gives chase, but once again the faceless person, though much smaller than Onak, shoves him to the ground with ease. *Later* Sitting in the same cafe with the Suburban Ninja Clan running interference to keep everyone away, Onka can finally get to his promo... with a new camera. Jim Onak:'Twenty months. That's how long I've been in Primetime Championship Boxing. You hear that? There goes another flash in the pan who couldn't make nearly the impact of someone like me, THE TRUTH LEGACY. I've won TWO championships! Even though my opponents were sick and I paid them to take the fall, and neither of my reigns were in any way, shape, or form long and/or eventful... I retired that annoying championship-whoring waste of oxygen Cory Wilson! Then again, it took me months on end to do it, and that's after I faked retirement for the fifth time, the fifth of which happened after I got my ass kicked by Wilson and Smokemore Weed in a triple-threat boxing match I had the ingenious idea of suggesting after a night of drinking hard Smirnoff... 'BUT THAT'S NOT THE POINT! I AM THE MANETH WHOETH RULETH! That doesn't make any sense but it works. I AM A TWENTY MONTH VETERAN OF PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP BOXING! Even though my t-shirts have only released 10 units in all that time; 9 of which I bought for myself and 1 which as stolen, but later returned after being used to wipe up an overflowed toilet...' *Thirty-seven minutes later* Jim Onak:'AND THAT IS WHY I SHALL WIN MY MATCH! Now, if you'll excuse me, I have to go pay someone on the street to take a beating from me. I might sell the video on Bumfights....' Back at Onak Enterprises, Jim Onak is wearing the same thing as before. There is a conference table in the center of the 45th (top) floor room. Onak is sitting at the head of the long black metal rounded-edge table. No one is in the room, save for a man about 6'6, 265 pounds, with shoulder-length blonde locks and wearing a gray business suit. Well, the jacket, anyway. He's wearing yellow sandals and white Bermuda shorts as well, showing his pale skinny white legs that don't impress anybody. Yes, to the near left is Onak's business partner and former boxing opponent CORY WILSON! Yeah, no matter how hard he tries, he won't be able to get away from the guy. I mean, they are pretty much the same person, right? Onak:'So, Cory, how are we doing in the numbers?' Cory stands up from the table and walks over to the wall behind Onak. Hard plastic-metal over drywall. He hits a button near the door on the wall and a large projection screen comes down from the ceiling near the wall. Cory turns off the lights and closes the blinds in the room. Taking a remote for the computer projector hanging over the center of the table out of his pocket, he presses the button to turn it on. The projector shows the heading at the top of the slide that says 'Net Worth of Onak Enterprises.' Of course, there's nothing else on the slide and Onak points that out. Jim Onak:'There's nothing there.' Cory Wilson:'EXACTLY! WE'RE FLAT FREAKING BROKE BECAUSE YOU, THE OWNER AND CEO, ARE TOO BUSY GETTING YOUR ASS KICKED IN BOXING TO RUN YOUR DAMN COMPANY!' Wilson regained his composure, clearing his throat. He calmed down and addressed his partner. Cory Wilson:'There's no hope for things the way they are. There needs to be a change... a change in management.' Jim Onak:'You'd better not be going where I think you are.' Cory Wilson:'You're damn right. I'm running this company now. Hostile takeover from inside, biatch!' Jim Onak:'Bastard! I retired you from boxing and I'll retire you from Onak Industries!' Cory Wilson:'BRING IT ON!' With some hesitation, Onak grabs his coffee and throws it in Wilson's face. Wilson wipes his eyes while Onak stands there and laughs at him. Jim Onak:'Never forget, I AM THE TRUTH LEGACY!' Wilson stands up, unharmed but with coffee dripping off his face. Cory Wilson:'The coffee was lukewarm, jackass. Now, I've got some boxing that'll put you on the floor in 2 seconds!' Jim Onak:'Impossi--guk!' As Onak is about to go into a 5-minute tirade about his self-delusion invicibility, Wilson throws a quick snapping superkick to Onak's jaw, knocking him flat on his back. With his former business partner incapacitated, Wilson takes a black Nokia 8300 cell phone out of his right shorts pocket and dials Security. Cory Wilson:'Yes, this is your new owner and CEO, Cory Wilson. I need you to meet Mr. Onak in the top conference room. Escort him to his office so he may remove his items.' Snapping the phone shut, Wilson looks back at Onak, lying back on the floor. Onak is in just enough sense to ask what happened. Jim Onak:'I thought you said you had some boxing moves?' Cory Wilson:'KICKBOXING, dumbass. Now wait here until security comes to get you.' Wilson straightens his jacket and pulls a hankerchief from inside the jacket pocket, wiping his suit jacket with it where the coffee has made stains. Walking out the door, he turns back to Onak. Cory Wilson:'You have an hour to clean out your office. I won't fire you; no, I'm demoting you to the mail room, without possibility of advancement. In an hour, you'll be ousted and have no power or shares within the company. DEAL WITH IT.' The door slams behind Wilson. Immediately, it opens again and Don King sticks his head into the room. Don King: 'ONLY IN AMERICA!' The door again slams and reopens in less than a second, this time Bill Gates walks in wearing the same dorky blue shirt and gray slacks that he wears everywhere, hair looking as stupid as ever. He's accompanied by two large bald Irish men, wearing balck suits and gloves. Bill Gates:'I'll be blunt, Mr. Onak. I saw your internet add as I was surfing one of my many computers looking for potential competitors; but I can't figure out what, if anything, CompuGlobalHyperMegaNet--' Jim Onak:'Onak Enterprises.' Bill Gates:'--Whatever; actually does! So, rather than compete with you, I've just decided to buy you out.' Onak asks for a moment to think about it and turns to the camera, thinking out loud. Jim Onak: *Thinking aloud, in fact everyone outside of the conference room can hear him*'I'm going to lose the company anyway. If Wilson's trying to take over this will be a great way to get back at him. I'VE BEEN BOXING FOR TWENTY MONTHS! And I NEVER got a fair rematch at either title I won that was stolen from me! I realize that this has nothing to do with the situation I'm currently faced with but I just can't for the life of me go 5 minutes without launching into a spiel about my career. But Bill Gates is looking at me so I'd better come up with a decision fast. Screw it, I'll just go with his deal.' Onak turns back to Bill Gates. Jim Onak:'I've decided to take your offer for a buyout.' Bill Gates folds his arms and steps back to the door. Bill Gates:'You heard him. Buy him out, boys!' The two thugs start throwing chairs and overturning the tables in the room. Onak tries to stop them but they in turn punch him to the ground and start kicking the crap out of him. Onak curls into a fetal position as he covers up from the blows, each impact drawing a girly 'Ow!' from Onak. Jim Onak:'Why OW! are OW! you doing OW! this?' Bill Gates laughs. Bill Gates:'You don't think I got rigch by writing a lot of checks, do you?' More laughs. *Outside* Glass shatters as Jim Onak is thrown out of the conference room window by Bill Gates himself (stronger than he looks... nah, Onak's just a pushover) and falls the 45 stories to splat on the ground below... The End It's hard to be the best when you're stretched too thin...
