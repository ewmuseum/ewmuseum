Scientology's Own... 'Claims Direct!!!'... Hubbard Help You All...

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/06/02 Oppression is a very subjective thing. Throughout the month of March Xavier Kannon was screaming it at everything.... Every loss was due to a referee’s fast count due to his Religion. Every attack against him a direct oppression of his faith... Even Duke declaring Crunk the official religion of PCW rather than Scientology was nearly met with court action. Court Action by Scientologists is not a rare thing, the faith has taken the US Government to court almost as many times as it the Government has taken Scientology to court... Numerous Newspapers, Magazines and Websites have found themselves on the end of legal action.... If they print one bad word or a slight mistruth it is pounced on and the source faced with huge legal fees if not a hefty payment. It takes a lot to get a Scientologist’s mind of money and causing annoyance and upset to others.... But if one man is getting close it’s Alexander Miyagawa.... It looked for a brief second as if Miya had turned his back on the PCW fans when he handed over his Extreme Title to Kannon’s valet.... But to the relief of the crowd he managed to sucker Xavier and Eleanor in and well and truly duped the couple, leaving Kannon fuming and Eleanor faced with the prospect of fighting Reginald P. Lovecraft... ... But the Scientologist must look past Fusion as he has to face Miyagawa, possibly to avenge his valet for whatever happens on Friday... But the winner will obtain something that could never be construed as oppression.... A shot at the PCW World Title... “...Frank from Hubbardville is one of many to benefit from our service...” Our shot opens up on an office scene, rows of uniform desks set out, each housing a PC, In/Out Tray and other stacks of paper work amongst the employee’s personal items such as family photos, and paperweights probably bought for them as a present when nobody had a clue what to get them. As the generic voice over stops we see a suited man striding through the office, holding a wad of cash in his right hand and speaking to the camera. Frank : “I went for a job I wasn’t qualified enough for, but it was given to a Christian because I am a Scientologist.... With the help of Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones I was able to take legal action on a no win, no fee basis and I won $67,000 compensation!” He unfolds the stash of notes into a fan and holds them up as grins cheesily to the camera as the voice over starts up again. “....George from Hubbardville used us after an airline dispute....” A corporate looking man with a Black briefcase in hand walks into shot, looking happy with himself... George: “I was flying Economy and they chose to upgrade one person to First Class.... But as I was the only Scientologist they chose one of the other 200 non-Scientologists.... I sued them from Religious discrimination and won $42,000 compensation!” He opens the briefcase to show it full of currency as the voice over is heard again... “..... Larry from Hubbardville also benefited from our crack team of lawyers....” Another man, dressed a little more casually in slacks and polo shirt walks through the tables, the workers at the desk not looking up from their terminals. Larry : “I was stopped by traffic police for going 86 miles an hour in a 40 zone, but with Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones behind me, I sued the entire police force for $450,000 for Religious harassment!” From one of the desks he picks up an oversized novelty cheque from the Police to him for the value of $450,000 and his displaying of it is accompanied by another Cheshire Cat-esque grin. “......Greg from just outside Hubbardville was able to use our branches outside Hubbardville to tackle Religious hatred in his neighbourhood.....” As the camera moves past Larry we see another man, seated on the edge of one of the desks who’s face shows an aggressive, not too friendly expression and resting on the desk, his fists are clenched. Greg : “My neighbours had the nerve to put a cross up in their living room, in clear view of someone looking through their window so with the help of Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones’ Lawyers, I was able to sue them for Religious hatred to the amount of $150,000 causing them to lose their home and live on the streets.” He grins to show he is missing half his teeth as he fans out a wad of cash, half smiling half sneering as a more familiar voice is heard as another voice over... “Now you have seen what Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones can do for you, call us on 1-800 HUBBARD, that’s 1-800 HUBBARD...” The camera pans across the office, showing all 4 clients until it focuses in on Xavier Kannon who is lounging in a large Black swivel office chair to the side of all the rank and file workers... ...The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a smart Charcoal suit and grinning smugly, his long Auburn hair tied back in a ponytail... Xavier : “... So if you have suffered any detrimental effects, intrusions on your life and oppression from narrow minded peons for following and practicing Scientology.... We are the company for you...” As he speaks the company’s 4 spotlighted clients walk behind his chair and display their “Winnings” as the number flashes up on the screen with a enormous amount of small print and disclaimers that appear for half a second at most before fading off... ...Over the space of 2 or 3 seconds the polished look of the shot fades into a more grainy one as we fade out of the “Ready for TV” shot and back into real time as Kannon gets up from the chair, looking like he has a sour taste in his mouth while the 4 men behind him walk off in their own directions... Xavier : “Ah, the legal system.... I hate it but what better way for Scientologists to punish the other peons that we have to share this armpit of a country with...” He wanders through the set to where the cast are dispersing from their desks and walks past the left wall to show it as merely a bit of board held up by a wooden frame... ... Off the set even more people are milling around, with a few odd people stopping Kannon and shaking his hand, congratulating him on his hardly taxing part.. “Wow...... Do people, like, actually GO for that stuff?” Into the picture walks the PCW Extreme Champion Eleanor Hall, as is her way proudly displaying the Title she didn’t even win on her shoulder after no doubt showing it to every member of the backstage crew that would take the time to see it. Xavier : “You’d be surprised.... A few commercials trigger a few minds, they start to wonder whether or not it was their own inadequacies or oppression.... But of course unlike me they have no real grievance so they blame oppression... Take the case to Hubbard, Hubbard and Jones.... They win the case, I get 15% of anything they make for appearing in these things and they take another 65% off the top of that... It serves us all.... Well, apart from those we are taking the money off...” Eleanor : “The........... Money......... Off!!!” The couple navigate through the post-filming hive of activity to a spare sofa where they crash down, Ellie~! making sure to drape her title over her lap as she spots a person walking by she hasn’t seen.. Eleanor : “Hi... I’m the PCW Extreme Champion..... Good huh? YAY ME!!!” The man really doesn’t pay much attention to her but breaks into a slight smile, walking hurriedly past. Eleanor : “So can I sue Miyagawa for calling me an arse end of a cow faced woman?” As soon as she asks the question Kannon’s face looks uncomfortable as our shot starts to fade out on his face... Xavier : “Er.... These things are kinda subjective....”
