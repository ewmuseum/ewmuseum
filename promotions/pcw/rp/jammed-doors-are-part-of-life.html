Jammed doors are part of life

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Last Time: Chet King suddenly started talking to Cindy Bi about how he was unhappy with life. It was very strange as he gave no reason why he was upset with life, and his change in mood happened very suddenly. (The scene opens up outside Chet King and Cindy Bi’s hotel room. Chet King is trying to open the door, but it appears to be jammed. Chet yells out in annoyance to Cindy.) Chet King: This bloody door is jammed! (Cindy Bi seems a bit taken back by Chet King’s yelling as she’s standing less then a foot away from him.) Cindy Bi: There’s no need to yell Chet, I’m right next to you! Chet King: I’m sorry, but this f*cking door is jammed. Cindy Bi: Are you sure? (Chet King seems angered by Cindy’s question.) Chet King: Of course I’m sure, I’m not a dumb ass who doesn’t know the difference between a jammed and un jammed door. If I’m trying to open this door and it won’t open because it’s jammed, it means it’s jammed! Cindy Bi: I didn’t mean it like that…. (Chet King seems to be offended by Cindy Bi’s comments.) Chet King: Then how did you mean it? Did you mean I was a dumb ass in the nicest way possible or what? Cindy Bi: I didn’t mean you were a dumb ass, I just meant that maybe you should check it again to make sure it was jammed. Chet King: What’s the point of checking it again if I already know it’s jammed? Tell me the answer to that, bitch. (Cindy Bi becomes startled by Chet King’s comments. She stalks to Chet King in a hushed voice.) Cindy Bi: Did you just call me a bitch, Chet? (Chet King has realized that he made a stupid mistake and begins to deny insulting Cindy.) Chet King: No, I didn’t say that, you must of misheard me. Cindy Bi: I didn’t mishear you, I know what you called me. Chet King: I can assure you that you misheard me. Cindy Bi: I know for a fact that I didn’t. (Chet King once again lashes out and yells at Cindy.) Chet King: WELL YOU’RE WRONG! YOU MISHEARD ME! (Cindy Bi puts her arm around Chet King and tries to calm him down.) Cindy Bi: Why are you yelling? What has gotten in to you? In the car you’re talking about how you’re sick of life, and now you’re yelling for no reason. Chet King: I’m yelling because this door is f*cking jammed. Cindy Bi: That’s not the real reason is it Chet? What’s going on? Chet King: The door is the reason, or should I say it’s part of the reason. The door is part of life, the thing that I’m sick of. (Cindy Bi sighs as Chet King punches the door in frustration.) Cindy Bi: I think that you best get some sleep and get over this sudden displeasure with life like I suggested in the car. Chet King: I can’t get to sleep if we can’t get in the hotel room. And it doesn’t look like we’ll be getting in the hotel room as the door is jammed. Cindy Bi: I’ll go talk to the front office and try and get it fixed straight away because you really need some sleep because you’re in a very strange mood today Chet. Chet King: I’ve been in this mood for a couple weeks. Cindy Bi: No you haven’t, I would of known. You’re in this mood today because you’re tired or something, and sleep will fix that. Chet King: You’re right, I am tired, tired of life. (Just like in the car, Cindy Bi still can’t understand why Chet King is in this mood.) Cindy Bi: And why are you tired of life? You haven’t explained it to me, and if you don’t explain it to me the only explanation I can come up with is that you’re tired and need sleep. (Chet King shrugs as he leans against the wall and slumps to the floor slowly.) Chet King: What ever, maybe I am tired and just need sleep. Cindy Bi: Then I’ll go get the front office to fix it now. (Chet King suddenly has a burst of energy it appears as he jumps to his feet and starts to kick and punch the door in rage, much like he was in a minute or two ago.) Chet King: F*ck that, I’ll just break this mother f*cking door down! (Cindy Bi leaps on to Chet King and tries to restrain him as he tries to break the door down. Cindy manages to restrain him as she once again tries to calm down.) Cindy Bi: Why don’t you save your energy for Jeremy Howard on Fusion and the battle royal competitors at March Madness? Chet King: Because I want to kill that door because it’s a part of the horror that is life. Cindy Bi: Chet, calm down! You’re in an extremely strange mood today, and I hope it’s the result of tiredness. Now if you can just calm down for two minutes and I’ll get some one to fix the door then you can get to sleep and get out of this mood. Chet King: The only way you’re going to get me out of this ‘mood’ is if you can improve life, and that’s something you can’t do, isn’t it? Cindy Bi: I don’t know what you think is wrong life. Chet King: Everything. (Cindy Bi sighs in frustration.) Cindy Bi: I already told you this in the car, I don’t know what everything is! Chet King: Everything. (Cindy Bi rolls her eyes.) Cindy Bi: What the f*ck ever. I’m going to go get some one to fix this door and then you can get some much needed sleep and get over this mood. And then when you wake up perhaps you can train for two of the biggest matches of your career which are coming up in the next few days. Chet King: What’s the point of training when life sucks. Cindy Bi: Because life doesn’t suck, and you want to be able to say you’ve defeated Jeremy Howard and you want to be the World Champion. Chet King: I do, and I will, but I also want life to get better. Cindy Bi: As with many other things, I already said this to you in the car, but you confuse me. You’ve got a positive outlook on your wrestling matches, but you’ve got a negative outlook on life today for some reason. Chet King: And like I said to you in the car, there’s more to life then wrestling. Cindy Bi: If only I knew what more was making you upset. (Chet King looks at the ceiling and doesn’t say a word.) Cindy Bi: Alright, I think you’ve calmed down now. I’m going to get some one to fix this door, so stay right there because I’ll be back soon. (Chet King doesn’t say anything, but just continues to look up at the ceiling. Cindy Bi then walks down the stairs to go to the bottom floor of the hotel to talk to the front desk. As soon as Cindy Bi is out of sight Chet King lets out a yell of unexplained rage and starts to attack the door again as we leave the scene.)
