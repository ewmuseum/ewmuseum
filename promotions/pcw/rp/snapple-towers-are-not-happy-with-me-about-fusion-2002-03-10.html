Snapple~! Towers Are NOT Happy With Me About Fusion!

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/02 Snapple~! The Official soft drink of PCW made is first appearance since Chris Chase banished it nearly 2 week ago. Unfortunately for Miyagawa it’s return in his PCW Television Title match with Akiyama where any doubts about Asylum’s future where solidified when Kannon broke the bottle over Miya’s head, starting the series of events that lead to Akiyama becoming the new TV Champion. The acts of wrestling’s only Scientologist went further towards angering his former mentor Jeremy Howard, who had hopes of calming the temper of PCW’s rising star but seemingly called it a day in trying to bring Xavier back around and instead snapped at the Scientologist’s betrayal of his former Asylum Stablemate and decided to beat a lesson into him instead. With Miyagawa reeling around, blinded by the blood from his cut forehead, he lashed out at Darkstar who un-like Xavier had put the heightened emotions of Wednesday behind him and come to help Miya and in return took the Pressure Drop that finally beat, the 2 telling blows coming from 2 men who a week ago were prepared to help him....Leaving Asylum finally confined to the records and Miyagawa left with just his World Title. We open up At Snapple~! Towers, the building in Dallas, Texas that houses the Chief Executives of PCW’s official soda. The building is a towering tribute to the drink in dark tinted glass with a huge Orange and Yellow checked sign above the main doors with Snapple~! written in Green italics across it. The camera which is looking at the front doors, showing the crowd of people entering being mirrored by those leaving the busy building. The camera then moves up the outside of the building getting a brief snippet of what is going on through each window as we move up to the 9th floor and stop, looking through in a voyeuristic way to show Xavier Kannon and Ellie~! standing opposite a group of Snapple~! executives and loud voices are heard....Not coming from the Scientologist or his preppy valet. “WHAT IN THE BLUE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE DOING USING OUR PRODUCT LIKE THAT!” We cut from the outside into the office, decorated lavishly right down to the Snapple~! colours of Orange and Yellow checked blinds and rug. PCW’s resident Scientologist reacts calmly to the harsh words and ties his long Auburn hair back as he explains. Xavier : “It’s called ‘Product Placement’.....I got your fine product, a bottle of Peach Tea Snapple~! and I placed it over Miyagawa’s head.....It just happened to break it, slash his forehead open and blind him, but it made good TV!” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing his usual full length Brown leather coat with a un-buttoned Black silk shirt under it that falls open to reveal a Black t-shirt with a picture of Miyagawa’s bleeding face on, under the words ‘YOUR World Champion’ written in Purple as an insult to his former Asylum leader. With his he wears a pair of his trademark Black jeans with Green stitching and intricate Purple embroidery running down the Left leg to his pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. Eleanor: “Made........Good........TV!” Beside him as always stands his valet Eleanor with her hands on her hips and her head tilted in boredom at the dressing down they are receiving. She is wearing a skinny Pink t-shirt with White collar, sleeves and Snapple~! written across her 34B chest. With this she wears a pair of Blue sandblasted jeans with sequinned stars down the legs and a rainbow patterned fabric belt. Her long wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is loose and cascading down her back nearly as far as her belt. “THAT ISN’T THE POINT! THE POINT IS THAT YOU USED OUR PRODUCT TO CUT SOMEONE’S HEAD OPEN ON LIVE TELEVISION!” The camera pans around from the 1st couple of Scientology to show the 5 less-than-happy Snapple~! executives, all dressed in matching style suits only varying slightly in colour between Grey and Dark Grey and all 5 men have matching Orange and Yellow checked ties and Snapple~! cuff-links. Kannon holds his hands up to them to try and calm them down as he takes a seat in one of the Yellow and Orange checked chairs and Ellie~! reciprocates this and gets seated beside him, staring nastily at the men trying to tell them what to do. Xavier : “Look, when you put the Snapple~! thing on the line and Alan Kreigman took it, winning you the EXCLUSIVE contract as PCW’s soft drink....You knew he wasn’t some clean-cut goody two shoes who was going to bring a great reputation upon your drink....No, you knew that he was going to get YOUR product on television for free! That is what I did, in the main Event, always the highest rated segment of the show and the commentators screamed Snapple~! and to me, that is everything you could ever want!” The 5 executives in front of him do not look impressed with his attempts at reasoning it, more concerned that Xavier’s newly found hatred from the fans may effect their sales. Executive in the lightest Grey suit : “Look, Mr Kannon....Did you hear the fans when you hit that other guy with that bottle? They were booing, they were disgusted....Disgusted is not a great mind set for probable Snapple~! drinkers to have about our product! Not all TV time is good!” The last statement confuses Xavier who looks to Eleanor and exchanges shoulder shrugs, not quite sure what was meant. Xavier : “How do you mean......Not good?” He furrows his brow, trying to emphasise with the feelings of the executives, wondering it was about Snapple~!‘s appearance on Fusion in front of 1000’s of fans and millions of viewers. Executive in the darkest Grey suit : “What we mean is if they found out the make of the gun that shot JFK it probably wouldn’t have it’s best year! If a car ran over Lassie the firm would be bricking it about dog lovers up and down the land boycotting their vehicles....And Miyagawa fans are hardly going to buy the fruit drink that cost their hero the Television Title!” With this explanation Xavier works out what they are on about, seeming unconcerned about their revelations, assuring him self that he did the right thing. Xavier : “Okay....Hehe is how I see it. People watch, camera on, Snapple~! appears on came-....Wait, I think I’ve got you....You were upset that I broke the Snapple~! bottle aren’t you?! All 5 Executives : “YES!“ The Gold Patron Meritorious seems to understand where they are coming from and nods in recognition, seemingly assuring them that their point has been made. Xavier : “So next time I don’t break the bottle....I’ll try and catch him with the side of it or something when it’s less likely to break....In fact, this helps me out as well because then I can hit them more times! You Snapple~! guys are sure sly when you want to be aren’t you?” The Executives all stare at each other in disbelief not knowing how to get the message through to Kannon who believes that he is doing the right thing by PCW’s official soft drink. He fails to acknowledge their looks of despair and carries on oblivious. Xavier : “Well, it was rhetorical so no need to reply....Wanna hear my idea for Sunday? I have some more great stuff to keep Snapple~! on TV!” They stay silent and Ellie~! stars at them, stating Xavier’s words to them. Eleanor : “Snapple~! on TV!” Still their is no reaction from the 5 suits who are each trying to work out ways in their head to get out of their Snapple~! agreement with both Xavier and PCW. Xavier : “Well, don’t worry. That one was rhetorical as well so I’ll continue....I was thinking a Snapple~! airship that floats around the arena, but only it isn’t floating because Ellie~! here is flying it...” This seems about at new to Eleanor as it does to the executives and nudges Kannon to get his attention and points to her chest. Eleanor : “You mean...Me?” He pats her on the shoulder to assure her then turns back to the suits, trying to convince them of the benefits of his plan. Xavier : “Yes my little Poon Puppy...Anyway, so during my match with the Rush and that Blind Guy...Or TBG as I shall call him, the Snapple~! ship moves into position the using state of the art targeting equipment taken from Scientology personal arms hoar-....I mean, which we bought for this purpose only, she will drop bottles of Snapple~! down into the ring, knocking them out and giving me the win!” The blank faces stay well and truly riveted to their faces, wondering what they let themselves in for the day they signed Kreigman’s papers to pass the shilling onto Xavier. Xavier : “Okay.....I can see you have reservations, so I’ll make it so they don’t break thus keeping the label intact as to get the maximum exposure of the brand....There, who says I cannot be flexible, that covers all the bases. Xavier relaxes smugly in his chair as Ellie~! mimics him, crossing her legs and leaning back with her hands behind her head. Eleanor : “All..........The..........Bases!” Finally ones of the executives replies to Xavier’s plan. Execute in the 2nd to lightest Grey suit : “Look Mr Kannon, we are not sure that the direction you are taking Snapple~! in is the right one for the company and ideas like this just go to pro-” As the man voices his concerns the smug looks on the faces of the 1st couple of Scientology turn to scowls and Xavier leans forward with his chin resting on his fists and replies in a harsh, quiet voice, his Green eyes burning with repressed anger. Xavier : “Well maybe if you are no happy with my work you should try and find someone else because let me tell you this, you will not find ANYONE BETTER!” The Scientologist breaks out of his vicious whisper into a full blown rant as we have seen him do many times since Turmoil. Xavier : “BUT EVEN IF YOU DID THEN IT WOULD NOT MATTER BECAUSE YOU ARE LOCKED IN TIGHT HERE, YOU AND THOSE STUPID TIES YOU WEAR! YOU ARE THE OFFICIAL SOFT DRINK OF PCW AND SCIENTOLOGY AND AS A PCW WRESTLER AND SCIENTOLOGIST I AND ONLY I WILL SHILL IT AND I WILL SHILL IT HOW I SEE FIT! The suits aren’t exactly sure how to respond to the outburst and sit in stunned silence as Kannon gets to his feet, his short fuse well and truly spent. Xavier : “SO DO WHAT YOU LIKE! TRY AND BECOME THE OFFICIAL DRINK OF CRUNK OR SOME OTHER CRAPPY RELIGION, HEY, WHY DON’T YOU SPONSOR BLIND PEOPLE WHILE YOU ARE AT IT AND HAVE PEOPLE IN STUPID GOLD MASKS IN YOUR COMMERCIALS!” Ellie~! now rises to he feet and joins in support of her man, leaning forward at the men with her hands still on her hips, shouting in her high pitched, preppy voice. Eleanor : “IN......YOUR......COMMERCIAL!” She shakes her head at the disapproving faces of the Snapple~! executives and looks to Xavier who is ready to leave. Xavier : “JUST DON’T YOU FORGET...I MADE SNAPPLE~! AND THE MOMENT I STOP SHILLING IT YOU SALES ARE GOING TO GO DOWN LIKE RUSH’S LITTLE CRUNK BITCH IN AN ALLEY! So say what you want....You owe your jobs and your kid’s college fund to me....SO DON’T FORGET IT!” With these words, the familiar site of Xavier and Ellie~! storming out of a room is seen as the shot fades out on the bemused faces of the 5 Snapple~! executives.
