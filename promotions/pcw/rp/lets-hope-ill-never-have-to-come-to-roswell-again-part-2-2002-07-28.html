Lets hope I’ll never have to come to Roswell again (Part 2)

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th of July, 2002 Time – it’s a thing that we never seem to have enough off. It has many meanings, and different people use it in different ways. Some, for example, spent it like there’s no tomorrow. Others use it carefully and wisely, and some simply consider it as just been there – like the air, or the ground we walk upon. To Titu Xansiv time has a special meaning at the moment. As it does, time has its way to bring the weeks around so quickly that one may consider certain days to have never come. And as it has done this week – just like all other weeks - Sunday has come around in no time at all. To Xansiv Sunday has an extremely BIG meaning. Sunday, you see, is the day that PCW’s annual summer PPV ‘Wrestle Vessel’ airs, and its come faster than Titu ever expected. Now Pharaoh needs to get out of Roswell and travel down to Tampa where he’ll board the Wrestle Vessel Cruise Ship and later on in Jamaica he has the chance to win PCW’s most prestigious title. But of course, two things stand in his way. Time and Xavier Kannon… Now Xansiv needs to find a way out of the strange town and he may get help along the way from a very strange character indeed. But, of course, Tampa is still a long, long way off… And first he has to get out of Roswell… The last time we saw The Pharaoh he’d just clambered onto the roof off a small café in a vain attempt to escape the crowd of insane Roswell natives below him. To get a better idea of WHY he’s just done this I’m afraid I must briefly recap our story thus far… It’d been a usual three days in Roswell. After all, many, many strange things had happened since he’d arrived. Upon arrival Xansiv had made quite the impact. The people had gone mad, shouting things as strange as ‘He’s the Alien king!’ and ‘Whoa! Would ya’ look at that silk loincloth! THAT’S A REAL SILK…LOINCLOTH!” Things could only progress from there though as Pharaoh ventured further into town and unexpectedly met an apparently SANE farmer (unoriginally) named ‘Farmer Giles’. After a friendly introduction from the old man Pharaoh was offered to stay at his house and so – like any other visitor who’d only really met one native of a strange place - he took up the opportunity. ALAS Giles turned out NOT to be as sane as Pharaoh had first thought. The old man shot at Pharaoh when a mysterious bright pink thong fell from the Egyptian’s loincloth. Giles thought it belonged to one of his ‘Daughters’ and so - taking offence – he began to chase Pharaoh from his land. Pharaoh ran and ran, and then he ran some more until FINALLY he came to the town square in Roswell. As he’d thought something strange was going on – which wasn’t really that unusual in Roswell. A mass group of people were chasing a small man around the town. Pharaoh learnt that they believed he was in fact Master Yoda from the Star Wars movies! Pharaoh merged with the mass of people and as he struggled to break free he was selected to climb the roof to find the small man. Pharaoh took the opportunity as a way to escape the crazy townsfolk and so - after climbing the café’s roof - he ran towards a ladder – his only way down from the café and quite possibly from Roswell. He stopped in his tracks, however, for standing in front of him was…YODA… Oh! And before I forget, Pharaoh also discovered that a man with the same surname as him resided somewhere in Roswell… COULD THIS MAN ACTUALLY BE PHARAOH’S DAD?! WHAT’S GONNA HAPPEN NOW THAT PHARAOH’S ACTUALLY MET MASTER YODA?! AND WHO THE HELL PLANTED A BRIGHT PINK THONG ON HIM?! Answers to all these questions and much, much more on the road ahead as we now return to our grippingly sad tale… “YODA?!” Pharaoh repeated as he looked down at the small, wrinkly green-faced creature in front of him. Yoda: “Right you are, young fellow…” Pharaoh stared in disbelief as Yoda, master of the Jedi, looked up at him with a solemn expression. The Pharaoh: “But, but, but--…” Yoda: “I’m not ‘real’?” Pharaoh laughed hysterically. The Pharaoh: “YES!! You’re just a puppet, aren’t you?! Not some actual REAL living…thing! George Lucas created you in his mind, for the love of Horus!” Yoda’s nose twitched, his lip to stick out and an expression that can only be described as disappointment merged on his stubby visage. Yoda: “Clouded, your mind is, my young friend. Much to learn, you still have…” Pharaoh frowned. The Pharaoh: “What the DUAT ARE you TALKING ABOUT?!” Yoda began to walk away from Pharaoh towards the ladder at the other side of the café’s roof. The Pharaoh: (Jogging after him) “HEY! I asked you a damned question!” Yoda hopped onto the ladder and began to climb down from the crown of the building. Pharaoh followed, talking as he went. The Pharaoh: “Look, OK! Maybe we got off on the wrong foot, but cummon, Yoda, tell me how you can be real. You ARE, after all, just a character in a movie, right?” Yoda shook his small green head, his big ears flapping gently in the wind. Yoda: (Sighing) “Stolen from Degabah, my home, I am. Forced, I was, to play the part of a Jedi!” The Pharaoh: (Puzzled) “So you’re not a Jedi, then?” Yoda chuckled. Yoda: “Indeed, a Jedi I am.” Yoda dropped to the floor from the last step on the ladder and, aided by his walking stick, set off on foot down the murky alleyway behind the cafe. Pharaoh quickly followed after him. The Pharaoh: “So why are you running from the people, back there? Why not use a Jedi mind trick or something on them?” Yoda stopped abruptly and turned to Pharaoh, a look of astonishment about his tiny face. Yoda: “CRAZY, those people ARE! Mad, I am not! To enact Jedi powers upon so simple minded as they would be like trapping a bee inside a balloon…” The Pharaoh: “Ah, I get your point…but, as you say, those people really are mad!!” Yoda nodded thoughtfully and then continued to walk down then alley. The Pharaoh: “Err, Yoda?” Yoda: “Yes?” The Pharaoh: “Where exactly are you going?” Yoda sighed. Yoda: “Too long I have stayed in this dreadful place. A way out, I look for!” Pharaoh frowned. The Pharaoh: “You’ll never make it out of here without help! Yoda, those people are like a pack of wild animals! It won’t take them long to find us!” Yoda stopped in his tracks. What Pharaoh said was true. The people of Roswell were like nothing either of the two had ever seen, and Yoda HAD seen a LOT in his time! He sagged a little and then turned around, facing Titu. Yoda: “And you would help me?” The Pharaoh: (Considering it) “Well I don’t see why not. I want to get out of here as much as you do!” Yoda shut his tiny eyes as though reading from the inside of his eyelids and nodded at length. Yoda: “Escape, we must! And together we shall!” The Pharaoh: “Come on, then! We have to keep moving…” Pharaoh jogged a few metres forward and then stopped, realizing that he didn’t know where they were OR how they’d get out without the people seeing them again. Yoda: (Slapping his head) “Lost, we are…” The Pharaoh: “And if we go back down the alley then the people’ll--…ah SHIT!” Yoda: (Laughing) “Worse, things could be… Come, I believe I know a way out of here!” Yoda hobbled towards the end of the alley where a huge stone brick wall faced him. Pharaoh winced, groaning. The Pharaoh: “We’re going to have to go back, Yoda. There’s no way out!” Yoda ignored Pharaoh and thought logically about the walls height compared to his, its weight compared to his and of course whether he thought Pharaoh could manage such a task that he had in mind. Aided by his walking stick he turned on his heels and stared solemnly up at the Egyptian. Yoda: “Watch and learn, you must!” Titu looked on in confusion as the tiny green alien walked up to the wall and pounced at it. Grabbing a small brick’s edge, he pulled himself up and before long the Master Jedi had scaled the entire wall. Pharaoh gazed on in astonishment. The Pharaoh: “But, but, how could YOU possibly have done THAT? You walk with a walking stick?!” Yoda smiled and then jumped down to the other side of the wall with a grin. “Come on, then. Keep up, you must!” were the words he heard from the other side and – as you may have already guessed – they were spoken with a subtle amount of sarcasm. Xansiv groaned before walking towards the wall. It did indeed take Pharaoh three goes before he finally managed to scale the wall. Yoda had grown quite inpatient in the time it took him and so when Pharaoh got down to the other side of the wall it came with no surprise that Yoda gave him a good SMACK with his twisted old cane. The Pharaoh: “Oww! What was that for?!” Titu rubbed his arm in distress as Yoda turned and began to walk further into the gloomy passage ahead. Yoda: “Time wasting!” Pharaoh rubbed his arm one last time before he set off after the strange little creature. The Pharaoh: “Well sorry, I can’t help it if I aren’t as nimble as some peop--…err, some…things…” Yoda looked to his side where his newfound friend walked alongside him. The Pharaoh: “I sense that there are a few things on your mind, young Titu--…” The Pharaoh: “Wait! Wait one minute! I know you’re a Jedi and all but you can’t actually read minds…right?” Yoda nodded in response. The Pharaoh: “You can only sense things, right?” Like before, Yoda nodded. The Pharaoh: “So how did you know my name?” Yoda smiled. Yoda: “When I heard you were staying with Farmer Giles I knew that you would be the one perfect to help me get out of this terrible city… Days I have been trapped here, and it was only until yesterday that I devised my plan to get you into the town again…” Pharaoh’s brow wrinkled as frown formed on his face. The Pharaoh: “WAIT! So YOU were the one who planted that BRIGHT pink thong on me?!” Yoda winced. Yoda: “The only way us to meet, it was. A plan, it was…and a good one at that!!” Pharaoh put his hands on his hips and rolled his eyes in anger. The Pharaoh: “Well YOUR damned PLAN almost got me frickin’ killed! What kind of a plan was it, huh? ‘The Get Pharaoh Shot’ plan? Pharaoh shook his head in disbelief. The Pharaoh: “I thought you were a Jedi master?! Not some--…” Pharaoh waved his hands in the air as though trying to catch the words from his very surroundings. The Pharaoh: …--err…goddamn …thong…planter, yeah! That’s it; you’re a damned Thong Planter! Not some Jedi! You, my friend, are a frickin’ Little Green Thong Planting MAN!” Yoda frowned. Now people frown in certain ways that make others think ‘Hey that guy’s really pissed off, let’s leave him alone for a while and then he’ll be fine…”, some certain frowns make people think “ BLOODY RUN FOR THE HILLS!”. Now at this particular moment Master Yoda frowned in such a way that someone might be rendered to blurt “Fuck me, he’s gonna’ blow!” which, in fact, is far worse than either of the other two mentioned. Yoda: “Little…Green…Man?” Pharaoh looked down, an expression perfectly picturing sarcasm on his mug. The Pharaoh: “Oh, now you’re starting to sound like Ellie! Yoda let out a low growl and leapt up into the air. With the speed of a cat he took out what appeared to be the end of a sword from the belt around his waist and wielded it around his wrist. With a flick of the switch a beam of bright green light shot from the end of the metallic object… Yoda: “ARHHHHH!!!!!” Titu ducked as a green blur swooped over the top of his head. Yoda bounced back off the other side of the wall and slashed again at Pharaoh with the deadly light-sabre. The Pharaoh: “Hey, hey, HEY! Just calm down, will ya’. What’s wrong?! YOU were the guy who planted that thong on ME?! Not the other way round! Yoda: (Calming himself) “DO NOT call me a little green man! OK?!” Pharaoh backed off. The Pharaoh: “O…k…” Flicking the same switch that turned the light-sabre on, the beam of light diminished in mid air and faded to nothingness. Yoda picked up his walking stick and began to walk down the other side of the alley. The Pharaoh: (Following after him) “Where to now?” Yoda sniffed the air. Yoda: “What says we go and get a bight to eat?” Pharaoh frowned. The Pharaoh: “And…the people?” Yoda looked up at the puzzled brown eyes of Xansiv and grinned. Yoda: “Worry not. I know someone who can help us…if think you may know him…” The Pharaoh: “Oh?” Yoda: (Walking off) “Oh yes…he’s your father.” TO BE CONTINUED…
