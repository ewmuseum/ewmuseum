Hunted Saga Part 15: A new day, a new mortal enemy

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/4/02 It had been three days since the incident with the former diner owner Dan Lyons and Crichton had only just simmered down. His dark impulses had subsided for now, concentrating on the battle with his conscience and he had managed to stay out of trouble since then, so there had been no call for these impulses, which plagued him so. Since the Dan Lyons incident he and Aeryn had been laying low, which Crichton suspected was the reason no trouble had found him, but now he had decided to come out of hiding. Temporarily at least. He figured that he owed Aeryn at least one night on the town to make up for her seeing the dark side of him, the side of him that he had little control over. He gave Aeryn the choice of where to go and she chose China Town, and although Crichton hated that part of the city, he obliged nevertheless. He had also decided not to drink, so that he could drive there and drive back, a great sacrifice for Crichton. This was indeed a great sacrifice for him, because he used alcohol as a means to escape reality and go to a place of his own creation, away from his troubles and strife. The pub chosen was chosen at random, so Crichton didn’t know what to expect upon entering this pub. He just hoped and prayed that it wasn’t a gay bar. The entrance to the pub gave him a few ominous signs from the start and he was thinking about turning back, the dried blood upon the doorknob didn’t help matters whatsoever. The inside of the bar itself was definitely not reassuring and gave Crichton the stereotypical image of what kind of people entered an establishment like this. He even went as far as to predict that if they stayed in this hellhole, it would take but five minutes for them to get mugged. “Ya know Aeryn, this may not be the best place to drink, I mean it looks kinda rough.” Crichton states. Aeryn smiles a little. “Nah, I think we might as well stay here now. Anyway if any trouble starts I’m sure that you can handle it!” Aeryn declares strolling forward towards the bar. Crichton eyes up to hefty looking guys sitting on bar stools near the edge of the bar, as they begin to leer at Aeryn. He sighs. “Why does this shit always have to happen to me?” He mutters following in Aeryn’s footsteps to the bar. “Okay John, I shall have a Malibu and coke.” Aeryn demands. “What? In the same glass?” Crichton replies seemingly shocked at Aeryn’s demand. Aeryn looks at him in bewilderment. “Yes, in the same glass and don’t be so sarcastic.” She says. Crichton simply shrugs and calls the barman over to him, with one simple gesture. “A Budweiser and a Malibu and Coke, please.” The barman looks at him Inquisitively. “What in the same glass?” The barman inquires with a disgusted look upon his face. Crichton simply looks at Aeryn with an “I told you so’ look upon his face. “That’s exactly what I said! But yeah she wants that.” Crichton remarked. The barman’s face becomes a little stern. “I’m sorry there’s not much call for Malibu around these parts. We’ve got beer and whiskey and that’s all!” The barman states. Aeryn’s face adopts a rather shocked feature. “In hate to tell ya this pal, but the wild west era is over and there are more alcoholic beverages being made nowadays.” Aeryn shouts, picking a fight. ‘As if I’m not in enough trouble’ Crichton though ‘I’ve got the Army, Dan Lyons and Ms. Zemoff after me and now Aeryn’s trying to pick a fight.’ He had to act quickly if he was to avert a fight. “I’m sorry friend, It’s just been a long day. Two Buds please.” Crichton comments, steering Aeryn away to a cubicle near the doorway. “What the hell are you trying to do to me?” Crichton questions, with an extremely angry tone in his voice. “Well Crichton, the place only sells Beer and Whiskey! That deserves an ear-bashing!” Aeryn states. “Yeah, but don’t you think we’re in enough trouble with the army, to go around and simply pick fights in extremely rough looking bars? And I told you this was a rough bar when we came in, so did you really think that they would sell Malibu?” Crichton questions. Aeryn simply shrugs. “Even the rudimentary of bars should stock Malibu.” Aeryn states regardless of Crichton’s argument. Crichton shakes his head, not wanting to go further into the argument with her and in the corner of his eye saw the three mean looking oriental figures enter the bar. They walk directly up to the bar and the barman nods in Crichton’s direction. Crichton simply turns to Aeryn with a worried look upon his face. “I told you this would cause trouble! Look at three guys coming our way now, thanks to you and your Malibu comments.” Crichton states. Aeryn simply tutts, thinking that Crichton is joking, but nevertheless looks towards the bar and focuses in on the men walking towards them. “Oh hell! I didn’t really think that I would cause this much trouble!” Aeryn states a little bit humbled that she made a mistake. Crichton shakes his head and giggles a little bit. “Don’t worry, I’ll explain the situation and get them to leave us alone.” Crichton stated rising to meet the men. “Listen guys, the lady didn’t mean anything by the comment, she just expects…” Crichton breaks off as the lead man executes a perfectly placed martial arts kick, directly to Crichton’s chest sending Crichton reeling backwards and through the table, hurling a glass ash tray into the air, until it ultimately smashes upon the floor, glass shards covering the bar floor. The oriental man who delivered the blow simply clicks his fingers and within an instant the man on his left quickly seized Aeryn by the arms. The lead man then casually strolls forward grabs Crichton by the throat and forces him to his feat and forces him up against the wall. “Wha…ugh…wha-what do you want with me? I already apologised for the Malibu confusion.” Crichton pleads, the hand around his throat making it difficult for him to speak. Then all three men begin to laugh uncontrollably, “Do you really think that we would even acknowledge you for such a petty incident?” The man with his hand around Crichton’s throat questions in a very patronising manner. Crichton’s eyes move from side to side a little, his brain attempting to figure out what is actually going on. “Um…Yeah, what else would you want me.” Crichton replies. The man with his hand around his throat laughs a little to himself. “Don’t play coy with me John Crichton! I want the same thing as The US Army, we want to develop our own little superhuman soldier experiment!” The man states. Whilst Crichton kept the man talking a plan had been developing in his mind. The only shot he had of getting himself and Aeryn out of the current situation was to free himself from this oriental mans vice-like grip, and then it was debatable whether he could take three highly trained martial art warriors in hand to hand combat. The problem was that this was his only shot however feeble it might be and he had come up with a theory, which could potentially put his plan into fruition. It was now time to act… Crichton visibly sighs upon hearing the leaders latest comments. “Well hell, join the cue, pal. I got a shit load of people after me and each and every one of them are more dangerous than you, you tit!” Crichton prompted. “How dare you speak to Shun-tu of the Chinese Triad in that manner! You shall pay for your insolence!” Shun-tu bellows. All in the blink of an eye, Shun-tu pivots around and throws Crichton against the opposite wall. Crichton slams hard against the wall, leaving a visible dent in both the paint-work and the wood work. This had been Crichton’s plan, in retrospect it was so bad that it had to be his plan. Crichton grabs the wooden leg from the now broken table and swiftly gets to his feet. He then hurls the leg at Shun-tu in order to catch him off guard, but his reflexes prove too fast and he manages to duck out of the way. The piece of wood however, connects with one of the other triads forehead, knocking him clean unconscious. At that very point, Aeryn jams her elbow into the final Triads gut, making him keel over, then smashes his face in with her knee. As soon as the piece of wood was thrown Crichton charged Shun-tu and as he ducked the piece of wood which flew past his head, Crichton managed to catch him with a tackle sending him down. Crichton then grabs Shun-tu and throws him at the wall, creating an even larger bodily imprint and ruining the wood work. “Good-god damn I’m good.” Crichton states profusely. “That’s debatable and I was right!” Aeryn replies, a smile developing upon her face. “Oh and what were you right about?” Crichton questions inquisitively. “I told you we weren’t going to get into a fight over the Malibu incident, it’s you and list full of enemies!” Aeryn states. “Whoa…whoa…whoa… you can’t be blaming this on me! I didn’t know the Chinese Triads were after me and it wasn’t me who insisted on going out in public, we have been safe for the last couple of days.” Crichton replies leading Aeryn towards the door. “Yes Crichton, but you know damn well that if I was here on my own right now, I wouldn’t of had any trouble at all.” Aeryn states. Crichton walks over to the bar and simply cracks the barman on the jaw, sending him stumbling backwards smashing up a load of whiskey bottles. “Aeryn, you wouldn’t be in trouble, because your not being hunted…” Crichton trails, off as guns being cocked stops them in their tracks. Crichton and Aeryn immediately put their hands up in the air, fearing the worst, especially with their luck, this was vital. They slowly turn to see the Chinese Triads, who previously had their ass kicked, pointing guns at them. Shun-Tu steps forward. “I really should have realised that you would not be taken easily John Crichton, after all you have managed to evade the army for a very long time as it is. You got lucky, because I underestimated you. I assure you that will never happen again.” Shun-tu states menacingly. “Fan-fucking-tastic.” Crichton shouts out at the top of his voice. Then without warning a deafening noise filled the bar and the whole front of the pub gave way, under blinding yellow-orange flames. ‘This really is one of those days!” Crichton thought.
