The deadline! The deadline! Beat the Deadline!!!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in the members lounge at the airport. Flaymin is fast asleep on a comfy recliner as other passengers walk past him on their way to their departing gates. Despite all the noise and hustle and bustle of a day at the airport, Flaymin seems to be in a deep sleep with a beer in his left hand. Suddenly, the computerised voice over the loud speaker begins to speak.As a natural reaction, Flaymin's body jerks forward and he spills the beer in his hand all over his face. Flaymin slowly begins to awaken and wipe all the beer off his face as the computerised voice in the background continues to speak] Computerised Voice: *Ding* *Ding*This is the final boarding call for the 9.30am flight to California departing from Departure Gate 12. Once again, this is the final boarding call for the 6pm flight to California departing from Gate 12- Can all passengers on this flight please make their way over to Gate 12 immediately. [Flaymin picks up his ticket from his pocket and looks at it. Suddenly his left eye pops wide open…..Then his right eye….. ] Flaymin: That’s my flight!!! [Flaymin quickly jumps out of the recliner and almost trips over his bag which is lying on the floor.However he gets right back up and makes a mad dash for exit of the members lounge, all the while screaming and yelling at the top of his voice] Flaymin: Out of the way!Out of the way!I’ve got a deadline to beat!! [Along the way, Flaymin zooms past a group of elderly people and accidentally knocks one over. Flaymin suddenly stops to consider his options:Should he help the old man he knocked over?Or should he do the RIGHT thing and KEEP RUNNING!?!? Flaymin looks at the old man with a guilty look on his face.He then walks over to the man to help him up] Flaymin: Sorry ‘bout that Gramps, but I’ve got a deadline to beat! [However, the grumpy old man picks up his walking stick and swings it violently across Flaymin’s knee cap] Flaymin: AAAAAAARRRRGGGHHHH!!! [Flaymin falls to the ground in pain as the old man gets back to his feet and extends his two middle fingers in Flaymin’s face] Old Man: Take that asshole!! Flaymin: F*cking old people!Bad Teeth.Bad Bones.And now….. Bad Attitude?? [Flaymin winces in pain as he tries to get back on his feet and towards Departure Gate 12. He hobbles along towards his destination but is in an awful amount of pain with his knee already bruised and throbbing. He walks over to the wall and begins to thrust himself forward by holding the railings. However, Flaymin soon realises that at this rate it’ll take him forever to get to Gate 12. Tired and hurt, Flaymin looks up at the clock in the corner of the room and notices that time is ticking away fast. Suddenly an idea pops into his head…. As quickly as a crippled pornstar can possibly walk, Flaymin makes his way over to the clock and pulls it off the wall. He turns it over and begins to turn back the switch that controls the hour hand] Flaymin: If I can put this clock back an hour, then I’ll have plenty of time to get to Gate 12! [Little does Flaymin realise that the Airport is scattered with hundreds of clocks and that changing one clock won’t make a difference. However as Flaymin continues to turn back the clock, a security guard walks over to Flaymin and begins to question him] Guard: Sir, what do you think you’re doing? [Flaymin looks up in shock and begins to stutter] Flaymin: The clock… I’m…..doing….. the clock….. Guard: You’re doing what to the clock?? Flaymin: Daylight Saving!I’m turning the clock back one hour!! Guard: Daylight Saving isn’t ‘til Sunday. Flaymin: That’s the problem with you people- you always wait until the last second. Here I am, a good citizen, doing the right thing and turning back the clock early and you’re actually upset?? That’s the problem with this country today- EVERYBODY’S LAZY! They’re sleeping on recliners…. Or drinking beer…. And the world is just passing them by!! [The guard begins to back away having conceded defeat] Guard: Sorry Sir, I didn’t see it that way. Flaymin: That’s ok buddy, there’s still time to change your ways. [The guard looks at Flaymin’s bleeding knee] Guard: Is your leg ok? Flaymin: I had a run-in with a…. big… strong…. man.It was ugly. Guard: Do you need help with that? Flaymin: Well actually I’m trying to get to Gate 12 for my flight. Guard: Why don’t you hop on my buggy and I’ll take you there. [Flaymin happily obliges and he gets in the passenger seat of the buggy. The guard gets in and quickly begins to drive it to Gate 12. A minute or so later, they arrive at Gate 12. Flaymin gets out of the buggy and hobbles over to the counter where a Flight Attendant is processing tickets] Flaymin: The deadline!!Did I beat the 6pm deadline!! Flight Attendant: You JUST made it! [Flaymin sigh’s a sigh of relief and hands the woman his ticket] Flight Attendant: Did you have any bags? [Flaymin’s eyes suddenly boggle wide open again. His mind instantly rushes back to a few minutes ago in the members lounge where he tripped over HIS BAG…… and accidentally left it behind in a fit of panic….] Flaymin: Uh oh….. [Scene fades to black]
