Date: Another boring roleplay brought to you by me The sky was pink and orange, a clear idea that it was twilight. Eddy walked the streets of Dallas aimlessly, no idea where to go but to see humanity around him. He ahead of him and saw a black man in tattered clothes playing a guitar and singing with his guitar case opened for change. Eddy felt sorry for the man and dropped in 2 dollars worth of money as the man's eye lit up. Eddy passed him before he could say anything, but he had a sympathy for the homless. People walked by, and Eddy took notice of each one. Women with babies, men with women, women with men, men with tattoos, tattoos with colors, colors with- You get my point anyway. Eddy knew that everyone was different, but the same.Eddy knew some weren't as lucky as others. A reason why he respected everyone. He loved everyone and treated everyone equal, even if he didn't like them. It was his nature.The sky was getting darker. The clouds with getting darker. It began to sprinkle. Eddy walked through, just like the other people around him.
