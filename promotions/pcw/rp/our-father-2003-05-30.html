Our Father...

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/30/03 “Our Father…” The male voice speaks up from the head of the lavish church…standing on the lip, his voice like angles, as he begins to recite the Lord’s Prayer. His voice booming over the almost packed house, each person packed into a wooden pew…each wearing generic clothes that just blend together with the persons next to them, at the helm of it all is the master of this celebration…Fr. Jim Snow, wearing white robes, with a blood red shawl, he continues to the prayer. Everyone in the congregation following him, the voices sounding off in chorus…all of them, but one, kneeling in the back, dressed in a grey suit, is Matt Jackson…in his head, there is a wooden rosary, with an Irish cross at the end of it…it’s tightly grasped in his hand, as his head is bowed in prayer, his brown eyes closes, deep in thought. His slender lips move with every passing second, but no words come out except the ones he mutters to himself… Whosoever shed last blood. By man shall his blood be shed. For immunity of god make he the man. Destroy all that which is evil. So that which is good may flourish. And I shall count thee amoung my favoured sheep. And you shall have the protection of all the angels in heaven. Down the pew from him, is a little girl, dressed in a white dress…she sits silently staring, kicking her small legs back and forth, just watching the young agent in amazement. Her parents constantly try to get her to stop staring, but Jackson doesn’t notice…as he’s too far lost in his own thoughts. Wondering how exactly he lost the title…why exactly he lost something that belonged to him and no one else. He didn’t lose it, it was wrongfully taken from him and he will get it back in his own due time…just when the man thinks he’s gotten away from his plan, the dark angel will strike…bringing down his hammer of vengeance. Without a sound…Matt raises to his feet and places the wooden rosary upon his neck and with a few strides from his leg, he frees his five foot eight frame, from the pew and makes his way up the long aisle of St. Peters, almost like he is making his long trek back to the top of his throne…to claim his universe back. Not giving it much more then a mere thought, he keeps walking to the alter, step after step on the light red carpet. Not giving a first look to the people, who are now staring at him, not caring enough to give them a second out of the corner of his eye. Reaching the alter, the agent takes the first few tiny steps, in one enormous stride…wanting to show everyone, that he really isn’t that fall off from becoming the King of the Raising Stars once again. Snow gives Matt a look, pushing his grey hair back a bit, but making nothing of it, as he continues on preaching. Jackson doesn’t even notice, as he walks past the marble white table, which sits in the center…housing the soon to be body of Christ. This causes a smirk to cross over his angular face, but it’s quickly removed as he stands in front of the huge dark oak cross…resting on it, is a huge figure of Jesus. Matt leans forward and kisses the side of it, the wooden rosary he put on his neck before, dangling down now…tapping against the dark blue marble tabernacle, the resting place of the body of Christ. Turning on his heels, the agent walks back down…taking each step this time on it’s own, reminding himself, that he’s not quite there yet, just on the brink of gaining it all back…getting back the things that were taken from him…wrongfully. Some of them can be replaced, others will never come back…Kid Wonder’s record is gone, safely tucked away once again. It all starts on Turmoil, he thinks…walking down the aisle once again. He’s right; everything starts back over on Turmoil. He is no longer champion…ruler of the universe of new comers, which belongs to James Edwards for the time being. He isn’t going to let Shane Robertson slow him down. Robertson is nothing more then a religious fanatic, not ready to take it to the next level…like Jackson. He’s just staggering in the back, hoping that God’s good graces will shine down on him and give him the power to stand up to the beast that is Matt. I know you Robertson…I have seen your kind, before. I have beaten your kind before…including you, he thinks, snarling at the notion he is forced to face a man who’s not even the same caliber as him. Then again, no new comer is…not even Edwards with his fluke victory; something that Matt will repay him for later. The Raising Star Title, meant nothing to Jackson, it was just a piece of metal, one he is sad to see turn into nothing again. His other opponent isn’t even on the PCW radar…much like she isn’t on his own. She’s no threat to him, and he knows this. It’s the reason he’s not worried about her, when she finally steps up to the plate, he might take notice, but for now…she’s nothing more then a mere name, one that he could repeat countless times and it would still be nothing more then a name…a wantabe like Edwards, who hopes he can now leach off of the fame of the former Raising Star Champion. Reaching the doors of the large oak doors of the church…Jackson steps out into the sunny streets of Boston, pulling his black trench coat tight, to fight off the nagging breeze, which he feels running through his brown hair…everything is different now, everything is new on this glorious day. ~>…… Driving down the streets of Boston, the agent carefully steers his mid-night blue Viper…feeling as the tires hug the black pavement, each turn a step in the right direction. Getting closer in his goal of finding Haley. With the break for Turmoil, he is back in full swing now…forcing the manhunt to a level he never thought it would reach so quickly. His goal to get her back, before the air ship takes off will be shattered…she will be his once again by tonight. He can feel it surging through his body, as he rests in the leather seat of his car. These people picked the wrong person to mess with, he yells out…careful to keep his thoughts in his own mind, making sure that no one gives him a second look. He wants to keep this an in and out job, that’s why he is the Chameleon…the best at what he does and that’s dealing out revenge with an iron fist, giving no regard for the people around him…it’s times like this, when he is most like his favourite wrestler…Angelo Deville, but he hates himself for it…he doesn’t mind Angelo and it would be nice to be him sometimes, but he doesn’t want to be a heartless killer…at least not anymore. His phone rings, jeering him from his thoughts…forcing him out of the fantasyland he was in. Grabbing the black phone from the cup holder between the seats, Matt flips it open and answers it, not giving a hello right away, but letting the breathing on the other end linger in his head for a few moments. “Hello?” he finally answers…hoping that he can get rid of the annoyance on the other line. Even not knowing who it is, he knows he doesn’t want to talk to them…he just wants everyone to leave him alone, to let himself get focused on the task at hand, which is reclaiming his glory. Not in the ring, that would be better left for Turmoil, but the glory of revenge…a dish best served cold in his mind. “It’s Jason…I think we just got the break we needed,” the voice on the other end booms out, almost causing Jackson to go deaf in the process, but with the news it sounds like Jason has, Matt doesn’t much mind…he could get his payback sooner then he expected and that’s always a good thing. Only stopping a moment for breath Hilton continues. “Manger isn’t the one with Haley…” That’s not possible, I saw him take her out of my hotel room, his mind echoes, in a total state of chaos. Not believing the words he is hearing coming through his phone…he’s suddenly distraught, more so then after he lost the match to Edwards. It’s like his world is coming down around him and he’s defenseless to stop it, not the position he wants to be in, at a time like this. “Manger passed him off to the man who funds his skinhead movement,” Jason starts back up, not giving Jackson a second to even gather his thoughts, as he continues to drive…hoping he doesn’t hit anyone in his current frame of mind. “He’s a business man named Stephen…he imports and exports manure from Germany.” Great, the man I am about to go kill…shovels shit for a living. I can’t believe the utter bullshit of this situation, he thinks…his anger building with each word that rolls off the tongue of the powerful attorney. This is nothing like he expected, but this is a turn for the better…even if it’s out of the ordinary. “His warehouse is in Boston…pretty close to St. Peter’s actually. Three blocks down and to the right, his will be the first office on the left, as soon as you get in there. The man you are looking for a man, who has dark hair, much bigger then you. Sorry I can’t give you more, but that’s all I have,” Jason dreadfully says, waiting for the backlash from the Chameleon. There is none though…Matt is just happy to get the information he did…glad he is going to be able to get Haley back. “Thank you Jason…I will be in touch.” Are the only words he lets slip out his lips before closing the phone and putting it back in it’s place in the cup holder. Taking his petite hand, Jackson shifts the car into third and gathers speed down the tiny Boston Street…hoping he can be at his destination soon. ~><>…<><~ Standing inside the metal doorway of the massive warehouse, Jackson jams his hands in his black trench coat…taking a few steps forward, eyes peeled for this first office he’s going to see. With each step over the cement floor…his wooden rosary smacks against his massive chest, but he pays no mention too it, as his mind is busy considering exactly what could go down here…giving himself second thoughts, but he quickly pushes them away. He took something, that belonged to me…I can’t let him get away with that, his mind booms…pushing him closer, closer to killing Stephan and getting back his precious girlfriend, but everything needs to be flawless, he can’t make any mistakes or he could loss her to a man who knows nothing, who made a bad choice and decided to back the skinhead movement. It was his mistake, and now he’s going to have to pay the price…the toll to Charon…atoning for his sins. Taking a few steps forward, Matt’s grey suit swishing like a sail in the wind, he believes he has found his goal…the office, Stephan’s final resting place. Jackson’s explored all other options, but this is it…there’s no other way to handle this situation then with the iron fist of vengeance, known as God. This is his answer, his salvation and shortly, he’s going to be sending Stephan to whatever god he so wishes. Looking past all of the wooden crates, which seemingly go on forever, he sees the office…just up and off to his lift, blinds pulled, but lights on…just screaming for him to walk in and take what belongs to him. Building up his speed, Jackson sprints across the floor, dodging all the shit that’s in front of him, not letting his mind waver even for a second, as he gets closer and closer. Reaching the medium sized wooden door, he grabs the handle and jerks it open, taking one large step in…not showing any regard for if Stephan was even wielding a gun or not. His eyes scanning, he notices nothing out of the ordinary, in the middle of the room is a large wooden desk, a small computer in the center of it, sitting behind the desk, is a man with dark black hair and a cheap business suit…not even close to the agents in look or price. In the right corner there is a large filling cabinet, probably filled with all his bullshit business with the skinheads. Next to him, tied to a flimsy wooden hair, his the blonde beauty Haley Hilton…she looks a little worse for the ware, as her jeans are ripped and her pink tee shirt has holes in it. For the general most part though, she looks unharmed, but he can’t ask, due to her mouth being gagged. “You must be Agent Jackson…I have been expecting you,” Stephen says, letting a sinister smile cross over his slightly aged face. He leans back in his office chair…showing no fear in the choice he made, placing his hands behind his head and watching. “I am sure you have, but this all ends now…you helped take something from me, that doesn’t belong to you…now it’s over,” Jackson cockily says, smiling bigger and bigger, as each word passes his lips…showing that he only came for one thing and that’s to end this man’s life. “See Jackson, that’s all fine and good, but the thing you are missing…is you have to learn how to play the game. I helped take something from you, because it was of benefit to me…you are just not playing the game,” Stephan yells, slamming his fist down on the desk and sliding his chair back, so he can stand to his full six foot three height…trying to bear his teeth down on Matt like an animal. The only thing on Matt’s mind is: “Whosoever shed last blood. By man shall his blood be shed. For immunity of god make he the man. Destroy all that which is evil. So that which is good may flourish. And I shall count thee amoung my favoured sheep. And you shall have the protection of all the angels in heaven.' “No, it’s you who isn’t playing the game right…for the sheer fact, you don’t understand the rules, you sought to make things easier for Manger, when he came to eliminate me, and in the long run make things easier for you, but the fact of the matter is, this is my game and it’s my move!” Matt barks out…reaching into his jacket pocket and pulling out the silenced Beretta 9MM. Stephan almost instantly backs up…knowing he’s lost control of this situation, hoping now that he can just escape with his life. Mumbling some prayer…Jackson pulls the trigger. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! With the three bullets inside of him, and his blood flowing from him like a river, Stephan falls to the ground. Matt bows his head and places the pistol back into its holster. Without a look or word to Haley, he walks over to the body and takes a knee in front of it. He digs his right hand into his pocket and pulls out two pennies, carefully placing them over the eyes; he continues to keep his head bowed. Then in one swift movement, he crosses the mans enormous arms over his massive chest, his rosary hanging down all the while. Looking over to Haley, he sees her tied down, being kept away from something she wants most…much like the position he is in right now with the Raising Star Title, but she will soon be free to get to what she wants, just like Matt…
