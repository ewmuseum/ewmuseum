Oh Brother Where art Thou?

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-5-03 RP 2 Fusion 7th And ye shall hear of wars and rumors of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet. For nation shall rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places. All these are the beginning of sorrows. Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you: and ye shall be hated of all nations for my name’s sake. And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another. And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many. And because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold. But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved. And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come. Matthew 24:6-14 Dear Teresa, It has been months since we last spoke. If you’re reading this, I know that Mom has found you. I would’ve called you, but I know you wouldn’t want to talk to me. I know I stabbed you in the back and for that I apologize. Enough about the past. Let’s talk about the future. The reason Mom hasn’t heard from me in a while is because I’ve been away. With the trouble brewing overseas, I knew if I told her, she’d loose it. Yes, dear sister, I joined the Army. If Dad was still around, he’d say, “Eric son, be a real man, be a sailor”. You remember how sea sick I get don’t you? The real reason I am writing you Teresa is this. I completed Basic Training a month ago and in two days I will be leaving the US. I am not permitted to tell you much, but I will say I will be gone a long time. In case something happens to me, I want you to know that you are still my favorite sister and I’ll miss you. I wrote Mom too so she knows. That’s why she came to you despite any reason she probably already had given you. Congratulations on your title win. You worked hard for it. Take care of Mom for me and tell Scott he’d better take good care of you or he’d be answering to your big brother. I love you little sis. Love, Eric Dwayne Tomas A deep sigh exits Teresa’s chest. Within the folded sheet of notebook paper is a picture of her only brother, Eric. He is dressed in full uniform and smiling proudly. She can see on his face, he had finally found his calling. Yes, Momma Tomas will probably loose it but deep down she will be proud of her son. Captain Tomas, Teresa’s father gave his life to serve his country. Now Eric has chosen to do the same. May God keep him safe. “Teresa, you ok?” A hand rests on her shoulders. She sits at the kitchen table holding her brother’s picture. A natural guilt rushes through her veins as she thinks of the last words she spoke to him. So full of hurt and rage, she didn’t realize those could be the last words she’d ever say. Standing behind Teresa is Scott who is looking down on her with concern. No response leaves her lips as she stands and turns to face him. With the letter and the picture lying on the kitchen table, Teresa wraps her arms around Scott’s neck. His own arms embrace her in the silence of this winter morning.
