Title: My Last PCW RP? Not if I can help it Featuring: White Mexican Date: April 7/2002 You didn’t expect me to no show a Pay Per View did you? In a match where my career is on the line? I may have been the match writer but what type of example would I set for all the MexiMarks if I no showed? I’d have to fire myself. That wouldn’t be fun. I’m not saying this will be the greatest RP because frankly it will likely suck but I’m putting an effort in. It’s also bound to be short too but I hope it gives you an idea of the frame of mind that Mexi is in. With out further ado… Well I have to ask myself, have I lost my mind? Am I’m nuts for putting my career on the line tonight? As I sit here in my locker room only moments before PCW’s Spring Fling and possibly my last ever PCW Pay Per View, I know you are wondering what I am thinking. “I wish I knew” I mutter in the room that I have decided to keep dark and free of distractions. No I’ve got a plan for the match, one that I hope will be fool proof but how can one underestimate Tyler Nelson. I would be a fool to not expect a trick or two from Tyler. “Bastard.” Well I know one thing is for sure. Unlike no other I have gotten under Tyler’s skin. I have thrown him off his game and I have made these last two months for him a living Hell. Damn I can’t help but smile at that. Then again, my life hasn’t been the greatest these two months either. Hell technically I’m still Tim Walden’s lackey for about another forty minutes. That makes me shake my head. “That Backstabber.” All I ever did was give Tim a PCW career. When he couldn’t get in on his own, I brought him in. When he left for Thugs 4 Hire and almost jobbed out of PCW I helped him back to grace with Wild Deuces. And now when I could have used his help he takes advantage of me and SNAPS! “Ingrate.” But why focus on him, my mind needs to be on the task at hand. The greediest player in the game. Tyler Nelson I know you will never admit it but I am your biggest adversary. I have with out a doubt done to you what even the Phantom Booker himself couldn’t do. “I took PCW from you.” And you want it back… PCW will die with you in control… I did PCW a favour freeing you from the reigns. “Fuck you Tyler… Tonight you quit PCW… Bitch.” ---- View White Mexican's Biography
