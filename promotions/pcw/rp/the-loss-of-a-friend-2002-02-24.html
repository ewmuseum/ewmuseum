The Loss Of A Friend

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/02 The gym is small and damp. It looks just like those old ones you see in all the movies, like Rocky and stuff. There are weight benches and squat machines and just about every other kind of machine you could think of, crammed into the small gym. Over in one corner is the PCW’s Steve Johnson jumping rope. He is wearing a pair of plain gray shorts and sleeveless T-Shirt. He is wearing some black Adidas shoes. He is jumping this rope at a very fast pace. Sweat is dripping off his face and is covering his shirt and shorts. He looks as though however he isn’t even tired yet. A few moments later we hear the door to the gym behind us slam closed and someone is walking up behind us. When the person comes into view we notice that it is none other than former wrestling great Michael Cade. He is wearing a pair of blue jean shorts and a black t-shirt. He stands where he is for a few minutes while Steve finishes up his set. When Steve stops and reaches down and picks up a towel to wipe off his face, Cade walks over to him. He smiles at Steve who just seems to look past him. Cade: Hey man how are you doing today? Steve: I am just fine Cade. I am getting ready for my first match in PCW on Tuesday. You know, the company that I got myself into and even had you a spot and again you turned and walked away from the business. So what exactly did you come here to bother me about today? Now come on Steve I already told you I was just ready to walk away from the business now. I am ready to enjoy retirement. Can’t you understand that? Any way I have seen who your first opponent is and he seems pretty focused. Are you prepared? Cade your right it was time for you to retire. You must have took one to many chair shots to your head to ask me a stupid ass question like that. Have I ever gone into a match not fully prepared? No I have not. I am always more than prepared to walk into any kind of match at any time and whip my opponents butt from one corner to the next. No difference on Tuesday night. Besides what has this Ripper guy done to impress me or any one else for that matter? He is clearly intimidating and appears ready for you. Intimidating? Why, because he can scare a reporter and a shrink. Well woo fuc##ing hoo. So he can intimidate two absolute no bodies. That is about as impressive and scary as…your wearing a big, fuzzy, pink bunny suit Cade. He better be getting ready and focused for Tuesday night. And I do not mean by running around showing us all how big and scary he is by screwing with a doctor and a reporter. Do you want some advice Steve? Advice from who? Advice from me of course. Who else would I be talking about today Steve? Steve just looks at Cade and shakes his head. Cade looks at Steve confused and seems to wonder what is up with Steve today. Cade at one time I would have valued your opinion. However right now in this situation, I really do not care what you have to say about anything that involves wrestling. You are in no position to tell me how I should handle a situation in the ring. Besides, your retired remember. This is a little out of your league now. What is your deal today Steve? I am your friend remember. I have gave you advise a ton of times. What is different about today other than me being retired? Steve just looks at Cade with fire in his eyes. The big reason for the change Cade is not your being retired. It is the fact that I am far greater and smarter than you in or out of the ring now. You were a great champion. Now you are just a washed up, wished he was. You never were good enough to be hanging around with me. I am glad you retired. I was getting damn tired of carrying you around all the time. So you can take you and your tired ass the he## out of face. Cade just looks at Steve in complete shock. He stands there a moment before Steve motions for the door to him. Cade finally turns and slowly walks out of the gym. When the door slams shut, Steve motions for the cameraman to come over closer. He looks straight into the camera. Ryckman you may think that you are convincing every one in PCW how crazy you are by attacking and playing mind games with Cal and your shrink. Great. It is a good thing you already have a shrink. Now you won’t have to go out and retain one after the humiliating loss you will take on Tuesday. On Tuesday night I will give you hours and hours of more things for you to cry your eyes out about to your shrink. Ryckman you need to be paying more attention to focusing on our match Tuesday and less time writing love letters to your cousin Cal Seaver. I am not at all fazed or impressed with your ability to scare two little pu##ies. Steve begins to walk toward the bathroom where the showers must be located. He smiles all of the sudden. He stops and looks back toward the camera. You know your name is rather fitting for both you and your career starting on Tuesday. The name Ripper, it will be very fitting. At least the first few initials anyway. On Tuesday after you are done tapping to my Tormented Soul, you and your career will…. Rest In Peace!!! Steve turns and walks back toward the bathrooms. He walks into the shower area and the scene begins to fade to black.
