Admiral Frisco's Infamous Pirates of the Atlantic

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-02 The whistling of aerial objects pierces the bright, blue sky as the sloshing of a boat on water can be heard along with indistinct chatter. Seconds later we hear the results... *WHHHHISSSSTTTTTTLLLLLLE SPLASH! SPLASH! BOOM! WHHHISSSSSTTTTLLLLLEEEE SPLASH!* (The yelling of men frantically follows the explosion, with a leader's voice directing above all.) 'Avancen! Avancen!' 'La playa esta 90 kilometros adelante!' 'ARRIBA! ARRIBA!' *WHHHISSSSTTLLLLLLLEEEEE.....* (A desperate yell echoes across the air as a seagull passes through the view.) 'A LA IZQUIERDA! A LA IZQUIERDA!' *BOOM!......SPLASH!* 'VUELVEN DISPARAR!' (A high-calibre machine gun opens fire somewhere on the ocean, but all that remains visible is the summer sky.) 'DESTRUYALO! DESTRUYALOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!' (The machine gun stops firing after only a few seconds and crewmen's voices call out.) 'NO TENEMOS MUNICIONES!' 'TRAIGAME MUNICIONES ESTE MINUTO!' 'NO TENEMOS MUNICIONES EN EL BARCO!' (The leader's voice cracks as he screams out.) 'WHAT!?' (Another sailor screams out an apology.) 'LO SIENTO, MERO MERO! NADIE TRAJO LOS MUNICIONES!' 'Wh--I th--!...You fucking morons!' (A smacking of a soft object onto someone's head is heard...and we are finally brought down to see Frisco, dressed in a naval uniform with an Admiral's rank, smacking one of his sailors over the head with his cap.) Frisco: 'What the hell is wrong with you? Did you fucktards not know we were staging an important exercise here!?' (The sailor covers his head and leans back. Frisco finally ceases his abuse and throws his cap on the ground.) Frisco: 'Bah! To hell with this!' (Frisco storms off the deck of the ship, cursing in Spanish.) Meanwhile, we are brought back from the deck to see an old Cold War era Soviet destroyer slowly advancing along the waters of the Atlantic Ocean. Frisco's clunker of a ship is a dark, steel gray with the emblems of El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina on its hull, and the Argentinean flag flying atop its outer stations. In all its glory, however, the ship still shows signs of abuse, with dents and scratches on its hull and thick smoke puffing from its engines. Many sailors, dressed in dark blue uniforms dart back and forth, tending to their duties. Going farther away, many small boats surround the destroyer, firing blank shells into the air to make this exercise a realistic one. The blanks fall into the ocean, producing huge splashes. (From the mock battle, we return to the advancing destroyer and the captain's office, where our brave Admiral Francisco Valerius grinds his fists into his head. El Presidente is clearly stressed at the omission of an ammunition supply for the exercise. After a few more seconds of stirring in anger, Frisco finally yells out.) Frisco: 'Someone, get in here now!' (The traffic on Frisco's floor that could've been heard a few seconds ago stops suddenly. Frisco raises an eyebrow.) Frisco: 'Goddamnit, I want someone in this office right now so I may yell at his worthless ass for screwing up my exercise!' (Frisco waits patiently as the nearby area stays silent.) Frisco: 'I will give you all 10 seconds to either jump the fuck off my ship, or come into my office to get yelled at before I go out there and cripple the first man I see!' (Frisco eagerly looks toward his door and picks his ear up attentively to hear a rustling and a few whispers. Finally, the door swings open and a young sailor is pushed into the office by his fellow seamen. Frisco grins slightly.) Frisco: 'May I ask who you are, son?' (The scared sailor immediately gasps at seeing his boss standing in front of him, and bolts for the door, but is not able to open it due to his fellow seaman holding it closed from the other side.) Frisco: 'Looks like your friends talked you into this...' (Frisco comes a bit closer as the sailor flattens up against the door.) Frisco: 'Some friends, huh? I bet you really didn't want to come in here...' (The sailor tries to think of a response as he sweats profusely.) Frisco: 'Am I right? Did you not want to come into my office to see me? You know, I thought it would always be an honor to meet your boss. Hell, son, I'm going to be in charge of a few million people in a few months. Isn't it not a good thing to have met the future president of Argentina? You get to tell everyone back home that I'm your friend.' (Frisco puts his hand on the sailor's shoulder who flinches in fear.) Frisco: 'You do want me to be your friend, now, don't you?' (The sailor begins to sob. Frisco frowns.) Frisco: 'Is that a no?' (The sailor continues to sob as he speaks up in broken English.) Sailor: 'Ju goeeng tu make I suck jur penis, sir...' (The sailor bursts into tears and shamefully gets on his knees. Frisco unhands the sailor and stands back in horror.) Frisco: 'WHAT!?' Sailor: 'I will do eet...for my country, sir.' (Frisco shudders and scratches his head.) Frisco: 'Who said I was going to make you suck my penis?' Sailor: 'Por favor! Just get eet over weeth, sir! I have a family!' (Frisco walks away from the sobbing sailor and gives out a big sigh.) Frisco: 'What is happening here? My men are spreading rumors about me. If they're spreading such horrible rumors, they can't respect me! If they don't respect me, they won't vote for me....' (Frisco puts his head in his hands.) Frisco: 'What did I ever do to deserve this treatment!? I have done nothing but served my country and my loyal men with dignity and honor and pride and love! Never have I done such horrid things to make them think I was a homosexual who likes to have his military personnel doing my sexual favors! I have groupies for that, for fuck's sake!...Female ones!...The ones with with--vaginas!' 'This is all too much to take. I'm not even in the mood to yell at you anymore.' (Frisco sits down at his desk and drops his forehead onto it.) Frisco: 'Why can't things go smoothly anymore? Why, why, why!?' (The sailor finally perks up.) Sailor: 'Ju mean, jur not goeeng tu make me suck jur dick?' (Frisco springs up and grabs the sailor by his neck.) Frisco: 'No! The only reason I wanted someone in here was so I can yell at him for screwing up my training exercise. I don't know what in the hell you fruity sailors were up to out there, but I do not want sexual favors from my sailors, nor my soldiers, nor my marines! This mission was strictly to see how we could manage an amphibious assault on Miami!' (The sailor's eyes dart around.) Sailor: 'Oh...' Frisco: 'Oh!? Oh!? Oh!? Is that all you can fucking say!? Had this been a real mission, this ship would have been resting at the bottom of the ocean right now and your bloated corpse would be on its journey to a shark's intestine!' (Frisco reaches for his head, but realizes he left his hat on the deck.) Frisco: 'You stay right here! I have a little more business to take care of and I'll come back to yell at you some more when I'm done.' (Frisco shoves the sailor into the wall and opens the door to see the seamen who pushed their unlucky fellow into Frisco's office.) Frisco: 'Well, what have we here?' (The group of four sailors prepare to run, but are stopped by the stern look of their leader.) Frisco: 'None of you pathetic rats are going anywhere!' (Frisco takes a deep breath before he lays down another verbal assault.) Frisco: 'Do you know what that young man was thinking I wanted him in my office for?...Don't even answer, pinche mentirosos! That young man thought I was going to proposition him for oral sex! Did you hear that? He thought I wanted him to put his mouth on my genitalia...' (Frisco looks to see if any of the sailors crack a smile, but resumes his scolding.) Frisco: 'I do not, repeat, do not want or desire oral sex from my sailors or any of my military personnel. I am not sure which of you shitbrained morons came up with this rumor, but I can assure you it is totally unwarranted and disgusting.' 'I wanted that young man in my office so I could yell at him for my exercise's failure. A simple, harmless request that you sick pigs ruined! I now realize I should have gone out there myself and first asked every man what he thinks of his captain, so I could find the true assbags who were in need of verbal discipline.' 'Now...' (Frisco reaches out and picks the cap off of an ensign and smacks him over the head with it.) Frisco: 'I will continue yelling at the poor bastard you heartless punks led to the slaughter...' (Frisco smacks the rest of the sailors over the head with the cap and makes a motion for them to leave.) Frisco: 'Get back to work!' (The sailors run and trip over themselves. The last man who stumbles gets a kick in the ass courtesy of a pissed off El Mero Mero.) Frisco: 'Next time, I'm throwing you sons of bitches off my friggin' boat!' (Frisco huffs and charges back into his office to find his victim playing with a globe on his desk. The sailor quickly turns around and braces himself.) Frisco: 'Ahem!' (The sailor stands at attention and salutes.) Sailor: 'Si, señor!' (Frisco fronws and scoffs.) Frisco: 'Don't give me that crap! You should've done that when you came through the door. It's too late now.' (The sailor stops his salute.) Frisco: 'Hey! I didn't say stop saluting!' Sailor: 'Si, señor!' (The sailor resumes saluting.) Frisco: 'Now, where was I?' Sailor: 'Ju were goeeng tu let me go, sir?' Frisco: 'Do not tell me what I was thinking! I know what I'm thinking! I was asking myself what I was thinking so myself could tell myself just what I was thinking when I was thinking it!' (The sailor frowns in confusion.) Frisco: 'Oh, right!' (Frisco picks up the cap he took from the ensign and smacks the young sailor over the head repeatedly.) Frisco: 'You stupid idiot! You screwed up my exercise! Why didn't you remember to bring more ammo!? What the hell is wrong with you!?' (After rapid beating, Frisco alternates his smacks with words.) Frisco: 'WHY!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'DID!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'YOU!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'FUCK!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'UP!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'MY!?' *FWAP!* Frisco: 'EXERCISE!?' *FWAP!* *FWAP!* *FWAP!* *FWAP!* *FWAP!* *FWAP!* *FWAP!!!* (Frisco finally stops beating the poor kid over the head with the ensign's cover and breathes heavily. The sailor peeks out of his protective position.) Sailor: 'Lo siento, Señor Presidente!' (Frisco looks up and catches his breath. He walks behind his desk and picks up a SHARPIE~!) Frisco: 'Eh, don't worry about it. These things happen.' (The sailor's eyes brighten in surprise.) Sailor: 'Ah?' (Frisco straightens out the cap and autographs it.) Frisco: 'Wasn't your fault anyway.' (Frisco hands the autographed weapon to the young sailor.) Frisco: 'Best regards to your family, and I'll be promoting you to officer within the next few months.' (The sailor clenches the cap that was used to beat him a few seconds earlier and smiles.) Sailor: 'Gracias, señor!' (Frisco takes a seat and gives dismissal gesture.) Frisco: 'Yeah, yeah. Get back to work.' (The sailor stands at attention and salutes. Frisco gives a half-assed salute back.) Frisco: 'I said get your ass out of here before I reconsider!' (The sailor quickly darts out off the office with his new autographed item and honorable promotion. Frisco picks up a Cuban cigar and lights it up as he plops it into his mouth.) Frisco: 'Now that this day has been screwed to its full extent, I can just relax an--...' *HONK!* *HONK!* *HONK!* (Frisco closes his eyes.) Frisco: 'No, please don't tell me...' (A loud, booming voice penetrates the walls of Frisco's dingy, naval office.) Coast Guard: 'THIS IS THE UNITED STATES COAST GUARD! PLEASE STOP YOUR ENGINES IMMEDIATELY AND PREPARE TO BE TAKEN INTO CUSTODY!' (Frisco forces a smile and sarcastically blurts out...) Frisco: 'Oh, this is going to be ever so fun!' (Frisco spins around in his chair and stands up forcefully. He then walks out of his office and through the halls, with his men all lined up and saluting. Frisco darts his eyes left and right and scowls.) Frisco: 'Goddamnit! Did you hear that!? The Coast Guard is attacking! Man your battle stations and find whatever ammunition you can find on this ship!' (The saluting sailors scramble as Frisco puffs on his Cuban, determined to take down the threatening force.) Seconds later, we are now on deck of the ex-Soviet destroyer and Frisco stares a hole through the Coast Guard who dare threaten his party's navy. On deck, sailors rush back and forth as the ship's alarms scream loudly. (Frisco leans himself up against the metal bars of the deck and calmly puffs his cigar as a voice booms through the Coast Guard's loud speakers once again.) Coast Guard: 'WE SUGGEST THAT YOU DO NOT FIRE. PLEASE SHUT DOWN YOUR ENGINES AND COME TO A COMPLETE STOP!' (Frisco frowns and finally screams at the top of his lungs.) Frisco: 'CAN'T YOU DICKHEADS SEE THAT I AM TRYING TO CONDUCT AN EXERCISE!?' (Frisco flings his cigar at the ship and flips off the Coast Guard. He then turns around and screams out another order in Spanish.) Frisco: ¡SIRVEN SUS ESTACIONES DE LA BATELLA! ¡ESTAMOS ATACANDO!' (Frisco spits and walks off, still staring a hole into his new opponent as the feed cuts off with a blast.)
