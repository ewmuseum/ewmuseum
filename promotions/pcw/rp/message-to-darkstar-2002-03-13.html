Message to Darkstar

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/02 Dave: Back from commercial, and now time for The End….. Jeff: Its time for the End of the show already?? Duke: It is a bit early? Dave: Its now time for Darkstar versus the End. Jeff: I wish the show would just end so we could all hit the bars. Duke: Amen to that. Clay: The following contest is scheduled for one fall with NO time limit. Introducing to you first from Philadelphia, PA he stands 6'5' weighing in at 239 pounds he is DARKSTAR!!!!!! Dave: Here comes the monster know as Darkstar. Jeff: Yea that’s what the ring announcer just said. Duke: This is the only Dark style hoss I dig. Jeff: That’s a ringing endorsement from Duke. “Parade” by Lifers plays as out walks the dark prince know as Darkstar with his princess Talena. The crowd react negatively. Dave: Darkstar is a smug individual. Jeff: So who cares at least he’s bring a chick down to the ring with him. Duke: I’d like to take her down a trip of Malicedom. Yes, Darkstar. You were smug. You knew as the veteran, you were favored to win. You'd seen much action in a PCW ring by that point, whereas I was only on my third match. Everyone's a rookie when they first get into PCW, but I've had but a few months experience, so I'm still learning. You're pretty well versed at handling yourself in the ring. I've already faced you, I know that. Then again, I was inexperienced when we first fought. The camera shows The End back stage stretching and getting ready to enter the ring. Dave: Whats this all about? Jeff: Informative video on how to prep for a match. Duke: Really why wasn’t I informed on the making of this documentary. Jeff: Drinking a bottle of Jack before a match is not great show prep. Duke: Worked for me! In the mist of a quality stretch a super kick enters the screen nailing The End in the head. The Kicker is Juan Borro, with him is Kevin Klaymore the two start working on the End. Dave: What is this? Jeff: Looks like an ambush to me. Duke: Alright nothing like an old fashion thrashen. To this day, I don't understand why Golden Game attacked me at that point. I challenged Juan to a fight before I came to PCW. Perhaps he was afraid of me. Lord knows he couldn't beat me on his own. While I hold a clean win over him, his win over me was made possible by a triple-team with Klaymore and El Diablo assisting him. I tried to get even with Borro in the #1 contender's match for a Cruiserweight Title shot, but Juan couldn't fight alone. After that, he decided that messing with Ivan Stanislav was a smart career decision, so I left him to be killed by the Russian Bear. In the ring Darkstar sits with a smile on his face as he watches the monitor of the Golden Game beating on The End. Dave: That bastard is enjoying this! Jeff: He should its like a half a day of work. Duke: I wish I had a half a day of work. You enjoyed seeing me be attacked. You knew it would mean less work for you. Apparently though you were a face, you don't mind condoning heel tactics by other wrestlers so long as you benefit from it. Then again, you've always been a little screwed up in the head. But you're not the only one working for PCW that has been or is crazy, so they let it slide and allow you to work there. Well, also, you were in league with Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard, an affiliation that got you much more than you may have deserved. The closest you've gotten to the World Title in recent months is by merely screwing Clyde out of title shots. Must be fun, you do it so much. The End rushes the ring and spears Darkstar sending him to the mat. Dave: This thing is finally under way.... Darkstar is getting his face pounded by The End. Dave: Good he deserves it! Jeff: That’s a hostel comment from a pacifist. Dave: Pacifist my ass I’m speaking my feelings. Duke: You’ve been watching Oprah again Dave? Dave: Shut up. Having been beaten pre-match by the Golden Gays, I thought it would be best to try to take the quick advantage and possibly get a win early in the match. Of course, you were better than that, and it didn't quite work out as planned. Darkstar gets out from under the attack and starts up an assault of his own. Dave: Darkstar back on his feet. Jeff: Good I hope he punks this bitch out. Duke: What in the name of May berry did you just say? Jeff: Never mind Opie!! Duke: Bite me Goober. Those were just punches. Little technical or exciting about that. Just to stun the opponent, that's what wrestlers are taught in the PWA. Stun your opponent, and then go for the big moves. It's at this point that you hit me with one of your signature maneuvers. Take a good look, because this was the first time, and the last time, you hit this move on me. Darkstar picks up the end and tosses him in a unique style. Dave: Oh man theirs the tearchaser!! Jeff: Duke. Duke: I got it lets chase the tear chaser down with some whiskey! Jeff: Light it up! Darkstar calmly heads over to the End and goes for the pin. Dave: I think its all over. Jeff: Thank God!! Duke: Lets chase that down with some more booze! Jeff: Now that sounds like a plan! The ref goes for the count but falls short of the three. Dave: Very, very, close. Jeff: I think he got screwed. Duke: Ain’t nobody getting screwed around here if I’m not invited. I kicked out. The move's more for show than actual damage, even you'll admit to that. You were calm as you ppicked me back up to continue your attack. Few people can do that. Most freak out if they don't get a pin, wanting the match to be overwith as soon as possible and whining if things don't go their way. I wasn't done, however. Darkstar shows no signs of frustration as he brings The End back to his feet. He goes to grab him but gets nailed with a low blow sending both men to the ground the ref sees nothing. Dave: Both men down after The End goes for a desperate move. Jeff: If Darkstar would have done that it would have been a cheap move but since the End did it, you call it a desperate move. Duke: Boo!! Dave: I’m just trying not to be repetitive. The ref starts his ten count both competitors not moving. Dave: This thing can end it a double count out. Jeff: Just get it to end. Duke: Now your cheering for the End? Jeff: No, just the ending. Duke: Ahhh ok. The End gets up to his feet on the count of seven and stumbles over and drops an elbow on Darkstar the goes for the pin. Dave: Lets see if the End can end it here. HA HA HA HA HA HA Jeff: This is purgatory. Duke: I’ll drink to that!! Say hoss lets cut out of here and go grab some tacos. Another kick out just before the three count. Dave: Another close fall. I scored a few moves, though nothing much, and even made a pin attempt on the 'great' Darkstar. I thought I was doing well. Silly me. Jeff: What do you want on your taco? Duke: Everything hoss!! Both men get up to their feet and start exchanging punches. Dave: Now the weak fists start flying. Jeff: Dave, what do you want on your hot dog? Dave: Nobodies LEAVING!! Duke: Damn hoss take the Prozac. The End goes for a right but Darkstar uses the momentum to get behind The End and drop him with a full nelson, reverse DDT. Dave: The Pressure Drop!! There’s his finisher. Jeff: Great just finish it. Duke: Ok I’ll take two double decker taco’s……… The ref goes down for the pin this time scoring the three in favor of Darkstar. Dave: That’s all she wrote Darkstar takes the victory. And with the Pressure Drop, you won the match. I took this tape out for you, Darkstar, because it will not happen again. The score will be even after Turmoil. 1-1. The Tearchaser, the Pressure Drop; you'll be lucky to even score those punches. I'm different from when we first fought. The inane comments from the announcers, your attempts at an offense, outside interference... nothing will save you from losing at Turmoil. I'm better than I was before. I've got an actual repertoire of moves, more experience under my belt, and now that I'm a Celestial with Akiyama... now there's no way I can lose. Congratulations on your win over me on Friday Fusion, January 11, in Roswell, New Mexico. It was the last one you will get over me. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- And now, random quotes from The End's handler. 1) Ashes to ashes, dust to dust, the soul of the departed rises to-- ooh, is that chicekn pot pie? 2)'My car's on fire AGAIN?' 3) 'Don't step in that. It doesn't know where you've been.' 4) 'When you come to a fork in the road, die.' 5) 'Look out behind you!' 6) 'I have placed a bomb in your computer. When your computer accelerates past 500 mph, the bomb will be armed. When it decelerates to a stop, it shall explode.' 7) 'Is it just me, or AIR COW!' -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Remember, Darkstar... you won't win at Turmoil.... You won't.... Handler: 'I'd like to take this time to thank all of you who have read my RPs day in and day out, and basically have been fans of mine for so long. I do this for myself, not you, but it's nice that you appreciate my work.' *Akiyama appears* Akiyama: 'How did you get out of the Void? We didn't release you.' *Akiyama opens the Void and tosses the handler back in.* Akiyama: 'Bitch.' *Akiyama turns to the computer and types where the handler would* Akiyama: 'Yeah, The End is going to win against Darkstar. Not so much that he's even better than Darkstar, though he is, but because WE will it. He works for us now, so there's going to be a lot expected of him, and he'd better deliver.' *Akiyama pauses his typing* Akiyama: 'On a lighter note, we are very pleased with Jay Sylo scaring Ripper Ryckman out of PCW. That adds to list of pretenders he's sent away from PCW. The list already includes AFL-CIO, and others. And with his... Celestial Star Title, he's going to be even more of a force.' Suddenly, everything blacks out. The handler wakes up, his keyboard covered in drool. The screen shows 247 pages of 'gggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggg' since he fell asleep at the keyboard. He wipes his eyes and kicks himself, having promised not to fall asleep. The handler deletes the 'gggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggg' and gets back to work on this RP. Wait a minute, it's done now. That was fast. Okay, now I just have to go and post this thing. Good day.
