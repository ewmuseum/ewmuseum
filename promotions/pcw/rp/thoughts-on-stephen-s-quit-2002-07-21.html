Thoughts on Stephen's quit....

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-21-02 ::Introduction:: Mark has had the whole week off from wrestling in a match. But in those two cards he did show the world how he was dominating over the average African American and that he is trying to get himself out in the world more. Now Mark has to face probably yet his biggest challenge on Turmoil this week. He has to team with an African American to take on two other African American. Now how is this fair to Mark? It is not, three African Americans to one white man. Now whoever the booker is must not like Mark and would much rather see Mark get made fun of in the ring instead of having Mark compete. Mark is now thinking how he is going to deal with this match and how this match will affect his segments. Will the African Americans take over Mark or will he prove to the USA that he is once again the dominating race? ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to a little cafe outside of Huntsville, Texas. In a little cafe named Patty's Pantry inside there are people hanging out and sitting in the bar stools. The waitresses are running back and forth up on the bar getting coffee and breakfast going. Outside of the little cafe Mark Makonie is shown looking at the local Sunday paper. 'Wrestling hero is out of wrestling.' reads the headline on the paper as Mark puts some change in the coin slot. Mark then pulls down the handle and pulls out the little door that keeps people out of stealing the papers. Mark pulls out a paper and then he shuts the little door as it locks. Mark heads up the stairs to the red door. Inside of the cafe Mark goes and he takes a seat down at the bar. 'Hello sir, what can I get you today?' the waitress at the bar says as Mark checks out her lacy collar. 'How about you start me off with getting me a menu and a Pepsi.' Mark says as he looks at the waitress still and he sets the paper down on the bar. 'I will be right back sir.' says the waitress as she walks away from the bar. Mark then sets his elbows up on the bar as he unfolds the paper. He looks at the headline once more as it shows 'Scandelous' Ryan Stephens's picture underneath the headling. Mark smiles as he starts to read over the article. It is talking about the run in on Fusion that Mark Makonie is the one who is responsible for the beating down of Stephens. 'Here is your menu and your Pepsi, just call me over when you are ready to order.' says the waitress as she makes Mark look up from the paper. Mark takes a drink of his Pepsi and he sets it back down on the glassy bar surface. He then picks up the menu and he checks out what they have for breakfast. He goes through the eggs, waffles, and omlets that they have. Mark doesn't worry about breakfast as he goes back down to his paper. 'So here they are saying that I am the reason why Stephens quit PCW.' Mark says as he goes back to looking at the article. 'But I am for sure that it was on the doctor's will that he should stay out of wrestling, but I made him goto the hospital so I was me that put Stephens out of wrestling. Mark smiles as he keeps on reading the article on Stephens. 'It says here that the town boy could've made it to the top of PCW if it wasn't for me.' Mark reads as he is skimming throuhg the article. 'Yeah right, Stephens hardly had a gimmick when he came into PCW and he just came in to take up jobber space. Now he is all done I am going to go and find someone else that I can actually work with on a feud with. Instead of just beating each other down show after show I can maybe do something more productive.' Mark puts the paper back down on the bar as he picks the menu back up for the resturant. He goes through the breakfast menu once again and decides on what he will have. He signals to the waitress to come over as she starts to head over there. Mark takes another drink of his Pepsi as the waitress gets to him. 'Are you ready to order now?' asks the waitress as she has a cute glimmer in her eyes. 'Yes I will take the Farmer's Omlet with sausage links and a piece of toast.' says Mark as he orders straight from the menu. 'Would that be all for you sir?' asks the waitress as she is still looking across the bar at Mark. 'Yes that would be all for me.' says Mark as he is looking away from the waitress at a picture behind the bar. The waitress takes the menu away from in front of where Mark is sitting. Mark then goes to the sports section of the paper and he starts to look at the scores. Although Mark is not much of a sports fan it keeps him busy to just check out the scores, but he does like the sport of boxing. Then a man comes into the cafe and sits next to Mark. Mark looks over at the man and he has barely any hair left and looks as he is in his seventies. He is wearing long blue jeans with a blue zip up fleece. 'Hello young gun.' says the old man looking at Mark. 'Hey.' says Mark thinking that this guy is weird for starting a conversation with him. The waitress comes back over to Mark with his plate full of steamy food. Mark takes out his silverware as the waitress heads over to take the order from the old man. He just orders some decaf coffee as the waitress heads back over to the coffee machine. Mark starts to dig into his omlet as he looks over to the old man. The old man is just looking at Mark that he wants something from him. 'Do you need something?' Mark asks to the old man as he puts his silverware down on his plate. 'Well have you ever thought of having the world go back to how it was in the fourties?' asks the old man as he checks out what Mark is wearing and everything. 'Well I didn't really know how that was so I couldn't answer you on that one.' Mark says as he starts to pick up his silverware to eat again. 'That was when it was fun for us to just go out and pick a random black guy.' The old man starts to talk as he gets Mark's attention. 'We would then go back to a high tree or something and we would just hang him and would get away with it. See back then no one would care what would happen to them since no one cared about them. I still feel about that today, that they are not as equal as us white people.' 'I feel you man.' Mark says as he is like looking into the mirror at himself. 'I am a wrestler and this Tuesday the booker put me in a tag team match. But guess with who?' 'Who?' The old guy says as he watches Mark take another bite of his omlet. 'He puts me to tag with an African American with two other African Americans.' Mark says as he is chewing up his omlet. 'I mean how set up is that. Three African Americans vs one white guy, who do you think will lose? Me not my team, but me because you know those African Americans don't care if they win or lose. ' 'Neo-Nazi?' asks the old man who gets his coffee as the waitress goes away looking at Mark on the way by. 'Yeah I am since back in the past of mine, I was picked on a lot by the African Americans since I was a geek.' Mark says as he finishes his meal. 'How do you know about all of this stuff?' 'Well back in the fourties I was also picked on as a kid by African Americans.' says the old man as he takes a sip of his coffee. 'And just like you I wanted to get revenge so that is what I did. I started to kill off African Americans in my town which there were a lot of. So then I was finally caught after going state to state killing off African Americans. I was put in an insane asylum until I was released just a few years ago.' 'Wow that is some mad shit to get into.' Mark says as he finishes up his Pepsi and the whole breakfast. 'Well yeah I didn't know how serious I was until I started to go on that spree.' says the old guy as he is taking another sip of his coffee. 'Well it was nice talking to you.' Mark says as he picks up the check and he gets up from his seat. The old man waves good bye as Mark goes over to the cash register. Mark then leaves the little cafe as he is thinking about Turmoil. About how he is going to cope with facing Jordan X Shabazz since he got into PCW. How will he cope with tagging with an African American and will Duzza lose the match for the team? We will soon find out in two days.
