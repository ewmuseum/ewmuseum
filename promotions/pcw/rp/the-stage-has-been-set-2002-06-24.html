The Stage Has Been Set

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-24-02 Voice Over: It all started when Political Machine Member Mark Makonie hit Phantom Phive member ‘Scandalous’ Ryan Stephens in the face during a stable brawl near the end of show. Stephens decided he wouldn’t stand for such a cheap shot, and took it upon himself to seek revenge from Makonie. During a Makonie match with Frampton, Stephens hoisted a javelin into the ring issuing a challenge to Makonie. Makonie accepted and so the stage was set. The two men would fight one another in a standard match on Fusion. In preparation for the match Stephens vowed to virtually the whole United States that he would rid America’ television of the Nazi clad Makonie and all his hate. Voice Over: Stephens was determined and focussed on the task at hand, while Makonie’s mind rested on his trial, convicted of killing a black man. To Stephens the trial seemed staged, and Stephens’ suspicions became stronger when Mark Makonie magically got out of jail and showed up at Fusion without anybody knowing how he got out. Stephens felt that he would prevail on Fusion, but Makonie never let the match get to the ring as he attacked Stephens before the match, and the two brawled it out to a double count out draw. Now the stage has been set; Stephens versus Makonie at Gold and Glory. Who will prevail? Stephens is inclined to think he will despite what the majority of the critics have predicted. [Fade In] Voice of Stephens: Damn! I’m sick of this crap! [Stephens looks up from his lap top computer venting is frustrations aloud. He’s dressed, as usual, in comfortable attire for sitting around. The usual baggy mesh shorts, Phantom Phive T-shirt and low cut ankle socks. His laptop is set up in front of him on top of the hotel table within his room. His wife, Dana, is over near the foot of the bed. She too, is dressed comfortably. She has various drawers open, the ones underneath the television, and a suitcase open on the foot of the bed. It’s quite obvious she’s packing.] Dana: What? Sick of What? Ryan: [obviously frustrated] All of these critics picking Makonie to win. What in the hell do I have to do to make the people realize what I’m capable of? I mean I come into PCW, and only a few weeks later finish fourth in the extreme title battle royal. I defeat a talented wrestler like Rush Mason, I practically carry the Phantom Phive on my shoulders, and still no love from the critics. [She’s carefully re-folding clothes, and laying them into the suitcase. She doesn’t look up, but calmly answers her husband.] Dana: You know, as well as I do the critics opinions don’t mean jack squat and you shouldn’t worry about what they think. Ryan: I know, I know! It’s just frustrating because from my stand point, I feel that I’ve come into PCW and done everything I can to garner some respect from people that watch the PCW product, and yet I feel I haven’t been getting any. Only two of those critics picked me to defeat Makonie, TWO! I feel like if I defeat Mark Makonie on Gold and Glory, they’ll come out and say, “Well Stephens upset Makonie,” but then predict against me in the very next match I get scheduled in. Dana: Seriously though baby, what difference does it make what they think? Just train hard, and focus so that you do beat him. If you keep winning, eventually people will start becoming more aware of what you’re capable of, you’ll start getting noticed. PCW is a place in which respect is earned, not given, and because it’s loaded with such talented names, sometimes it just takes a lot of time to earn the respect. [She sighs, and shuts a drawer that she has just emptied of her clothes. He merely sits in the chair at the table by the window with a questioning look on his face.] Dana: You’re smart Ryan, you’re aware of all this. You only ask me questions like that so you can hear me say what you already know. You shouldn’t need me to reinforce anything for you! You’re a great wrestler, and you should be proud of yourself. The bottom line is you have to stop carrying about what everyone thinks. Do it for yourself, not for them. [Ryan gets up from his station at the laptop, and makes his way over to the little space that resembles a closet. Lying on the ground in the said space is another suitcase, his suitcase. He drags it out and tosses it on the bed near his wife’s. She looks up, somewhat startled that it just came flying through the air, but then she turns away and begins folding more clothes and inserting them into her suitcase.] Ryan: You’re anxious to get down to Texas and look for a house aren’t you? Dana: You know I am baby. If we leave right after Turmoil we might can find one before Gold and Glory on Sunday. Ryan: Perhaps, but I intend to be at Fusion on Friday to watch Makonie against Akiyama. I still haven’t got much of a chance to spot any of his tendencies, especially since we never got to fight last Friday. [Ryan shakes his head in disgust. It still pisses him off to think he didn’t get to get Makonie in the ring where he could physically and mentally dismantle the Nazi.] Dana: Well then I don’t know why we can’t leave tonight or tomorrow morning. You still haven’t told me why you have to be at Turmoil. It will be good for you to take a few days off anyhow. Ryan: Baby, I have to be at Turmoil. I can’t tell you why yet, but you’ll see. [She looks a little agitated, or perhaps disappointed is the word. He never keeps anything from her, and she’s not sure why he won’t tell her why he has to be at Turmoil.] Ryan: Baby, don’t get mad at me. Everything is going to be better after Turmoil, you’ll see. Dana: I’m not mad baby! Ryan: Yes you are, I can tell when you’re mad. Dana: I’m not mad I just don’t understand why you can’t tell me. Or why you won’t. Ryan: Fine, Fine I’ll tell you! Dana: No forget it, you don’t want to, you don’t have to. Ryan: Baby don’t be that way, just let me tell you. Dana: Fine! [Ryan walks over to her and cups her ear with his hand. He reaches in with his head and whispers it into her ear. As he whispers her eyes light up, and her mouth drops open. After he pulls away she looks at him and says…] Dana: Are you sure that’s for the best? Ryan: [he smiles at her] I’m positive baby! [He hugs her tightly in order to reassure her, similar to the way she reassures him by telling me what he already knows when he conjures up conversations that he’s already discussed with himself in his mind. They make a solid little team you know! Young and in love, not corrupted by the business or put together for show. They love each other, and they help each other, she helps him, she is his guiding light.] [She hands him a shirt of his, and begins to fold it. He looks at her and smiles. She smiles back, and they know that everything is going to be all right. Come Turmoil Stephens’ has a surprise to unveil. How will the fans react? How will PCW react? Who gives a shit about how the critics will react!? The stage has been set, the competitors in their corners, but who will prevail?]
