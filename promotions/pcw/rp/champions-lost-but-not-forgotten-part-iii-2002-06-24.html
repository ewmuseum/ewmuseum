Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part III]

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24, 2002 As Jack and Johnny stood upon the bluff before their PINK! Cottage on this clear, perfect night in June, the noble tag team flowing like the crystal blue waters of the river below them, they each felt again the strange, compelling influence of the team that once was, the beloved champions, which now for almost two months have been naked from the bodies of these two sculpted men. Not since that day in May at PCW’s Supremacy, when they had stood tall as the number one force in tag team wrestling, where they hoisted the belts high above their heads had they felt such a god-like presence. With arms outstretched toward the sole PINK! star that shined bright in the sky that night, they began hoping...wishing if you will, upon that very star that they would once again become tag team champions. Two long months they had waited and hoped that the chance would come again. Well, it wasn’t so much hope, as they knew the chance was coming, but their patience was growing dim and their relationships with the other tag teams in the federation were falling out as well. Suddenly a qualm of nausea swept over them, their senses swam, their knees gave beneath them and they each pitched a rock to the ground upon the very verge of the dizzy bluff. Instantly their brains cleared and there swept back across the threshold of each one of their memories the vivid picture of the horrors of mishap in PCW; it seemed eerie that both of The Heartbreakers were feeling the same feelings at the same time...but after all, they were like soul mates, it was like they were married...in a spiritual sense of course. Their muscles refused to respond to their wills and again, as though they were in the ring, experiencing those losses again, they could hear the awful moans and rustling of defeat that had halted their careers dead in their paths over and over again. The very losses that had threatened their existence in PCW...the very losses that they could not experience this Sunday at Gold and Glory. What would come of them after Gold and Glory? After all, with the editions of three new tag teams to PCW, and the existence of up and coming teams, the Heartbreakers would have to start all over again if they didn’t accomplish what they had to do this Sunday at the pay-per-view. Of course, the Pretty Cool Guys would be a factor, and even though the Heartbreakers are friendly enough to try to help them, Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore are two POP stars that could hit a high note on The Heartbreakers anytime during that match and eliminate the hopes and dreams of all that is PINK! With scarcely a parting glance they turned their eyes again toward that PINK! star way up in the sky. Again, hopes and dreams, along with failures and nightmares flashed through their minds like a racing cheetah. Thoughts of them pinning Politically Incorrect, Crichton and Pailthorpe, Misplaced, Fear Factory...along with many other great teams in PCW raced through their head. Along with the dreams came the nightmares...Miyagawa and Syko, Cowabunga Connection, Frampton and Daveylicious...the teams that destroyed The Heartbreakers...slowed their dreams, and although they didn’t end them, they loosened the foundation and made it more vulnerable for a tear. This brings us to the meaning of this series. Champions: Lost but not Forgotten. A very fitting title as the belts are lost from their possession, but also not out of their minds. But, the title has much more meaning than that, as we can see from the thoughts of the Heartbreakers on top of this beautiful bluff. They are lost...lost for meaning...and with a loss in this match, they will be at a loss for hope. However, they will not be forgotten, as they are doing everything in their power to make an impact in this match on Sunday...they are doing everything possible to win, and win they must. Jack Valentine: “You know, it’s been too long Johnny...” Johnny Heart: “I know...me too. Maybe we should hit up the bars tonight.” Jack Valentine: “Not that you numbskull...besides, I already have Christina. It’s been too long since we’ve seen those straps around our waists.” Johnny Heart: “Oh, you’re damn right about that too.” Jack began to outline the figure of the PINK! star with his finger, going round and round in circles until an idea popped into his head. Jack Valentine: “You know something...” Johnny Heart: “I know a lot of things.” Jack Valentine: “Well, I bet you don’t know this. We’ve been sitting here for the past hour, worrying about this match on Sunday, but why should we be worried...” Johnny Heart: “Um...because there’s about 10 other great tag teams that want to win these belts just as much as we do.” Jack Valentine: “But who are we?” Johnny Heart: “Is that a trick question?” Jack Valentine: “No! We’re the Heartbreakers! Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart!” Johnny Heart: “...and your point is...” Jack Valentine: “We were the last PCW Tag Team Champions. We ARE the number 1 contenders for these belts. Why should we be afraid? It should be all of them that are afraid of us...afraid of The Heartbreakers, afraid of the PINK!” Johnny Heart: “Yeah...It’s all about the PINK!” The Heartbreakers stood there on that bluff scrutinizing the situation. Jack was the optimist, Johnny was the pessimist, and as all great duos of our time, they find a way to debate and compromise. Jack Valentine: “On second hand, there are great teams like Dark Messiahs...” Johnny Heart: (Interrupting) “Been there, done that.” Jack Valentine: “You did?” Johnny Heart: “We did...remember...it was their first match.” Jack Valentine: “Oh, you’re talking about...oh ok.” An awkward pause comes across the scene before they continue. Jack Valentine: “Well how about Misplaced, Suicidal Tendencies & Politically Incorrect? They are all great teams in themselves.” Johnny Heart: “Strike 1, Strike 2, Strike 3...they’re all out. We’ve beat all of them as well.” Jack pauses a minute to think. A nervous aura comes over his face and beads of tiny little sweat begin running down the side of his cheeks. Jack Valentine: “That still leaves Akiyama, Chef and Parshin, Pretty Cool Guys, Rent-A-Hero & Snow and Cowabunga Connection!!! There’s no chance in hell of us getting by any of them.” Johnny Heart: “Didn’t you just tell me a minute ago that we shouldn’t be afraid..” Jack Valentine: “Well...” Johnny Heart: “We’re the number one contenders!” Jack Valentine: “Yeah!” Johnny Heart: “We’re the last champions!” Jack Valentine: “Yeah!” Johnny Heart: “We’re the team to beat!” Jack Valentine: “Yeah!” Johnny Heart: “AND IT’S ALL ABOUT THE PINK!” Jack Valentine: (Screaming at this point) “Hell Yeah!!!” As the two men take one last look at that PINK! star in the sky, they run that list of teams through their heads one last time. Each of them takes a nervous gulp as the scene fades. ...TO BE CONTINUED...
