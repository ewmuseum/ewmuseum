Dear Kristin

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/05/03 The scene opens up on a beautiful marina on the coastline of America's fiftieth state and one of the most awe-inspiring places in the world, Hawaii. The sun is setting on the horizon and plunging itself into the crystal clear Pacific Ocean. This sight cannot be described by words and still do the sight justice. Underneath a palm tree, sitting in the sand and leaning against the palm tree, is Jon Kano. Kano is wearing no shirt, no shoes, but has a pair of long, tan khakis pants. Kano has a gold pen in his right hand as he writes away at his notebook in his left hand being propped up on his right thigh. His thoughts are being broadcast instead of his audible words. 'Dear Kristin, Honey, I just received your last letter this morning. I do believe it is a great idea for us to begin this corespondence rather than e-mails or cell phone calls. It is more heartfelt. I cannot believe how beautiful it is here in Hawaii. I have been here so many times, but everytime I am here it takes my breath away. I think while I am here and since I am not on the show here, I will look into buying us a house here. That is if you want a house here.' Kano pauses and looks off into the sunset. 'I can just picture ten years from now, Kristin. We have a lovely beach home here and our villa in Las Vegas. We come down here with our children, if we have any. My career in wrestling is winding down and Kano Enterprises is continuing to grow and expand. We lay here out on our piece of the shoreline and sip Mai-Tais and watch the sunset. Kristin, it will be beautiful. Do you realize that this is going to be the first Valentine's Day that we have been together on in four years? I already called and got reservations at Mikey's. That is just the beginning, I got a lot planned for you. You have been so good to me, while others would toss me aside. I remember when we were just getting serious back in high school and your parents wanted you to go out with the star quarterback rather than me. They thought I was dirt and that I would never amount to anything. Look at me now, less than ten years later and I am worth 70 million dollars, according to Forbes. That quarterback, I think his name was Cade is a 35,000 dollar a year CPA. Life is odd, ain't it?' Kano stops writing once again. He grabs a pack of cigarettes out of his pocket and lights one. He takes a few drags off of his trademark Marlboro Menthol Light, deeply inhaling the cancerous smoke that will one day probably claim his life, and then begins to write again. 'Friday night, I have to wrestle an old friend. I have wrestled friends before. I took Chris Sloboda to the limits, beating him once, and he beat me once. Things have changed. Duke and I have had our problems, but we still have history as friends. Right now, honey, Xavier sold the company that he didn't build to a man who will destroy it. Duke and I can save the company, but we might end up destroying each other on Friday. He and I are competitors, first and foremost. We are very intense and we both want to go to March Madness and have that fine opportunity to be the World Champ. He and I are going to give a show on Friday. He and I could beat each other on any given day, its just going to come down to who make a mistake first. I just hope, regardless of the outcome, Duke and I can still get our shit together and take this company that he and I revolutionized and prevent its downfall. Honey, I got to get to the airport and get back to the mainland. I'll see you soon. Love, Jon.' Kano takes a few more drags off of his cigarette and then flicks it into the distance. Slowly, the scene fades to black as Kano rises to his feet and vasks in the glory of the beauty of tropical Hawaii before he leaves to the airport.
