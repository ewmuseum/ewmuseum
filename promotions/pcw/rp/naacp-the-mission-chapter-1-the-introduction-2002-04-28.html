NAACP- The Mission [Chapter 1. The Introduction]

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 OOC- This is going to be the start of a series of RPs known as NAACP-The Mission. Enjoy reading... I was sick in every sense of the word. Physically, mentally, and emotionally. I had been assaulted by a bunch of racists, and now all those claims about white people were proving me right… All cops deserve to die. Anyways, last night wasn’t all that bad. Even though three KKK Klansmen attacked me while Michigan State Troopers watched, I made my debut in the PCW, and I proved that I could hang with Duke Williams and Xavier Kannon. Also, I received word from the NAACP that they wanted me to be their new spokesperson. I immediately accepted and now it appears as if I’m just a few minutes away from the press conference they had called for 3 PM this afternoon. The scene opens up on a dirt country road. We see a black Jeep speeding down this dirt road erratically. The driver doesn’t seem to care for human lives by the way he’s driving. We now enter the vehicle to see one of the PCW’s newest signees, Jordan X Shabazz, driving with a sadistic grin on his face. “Too Much Money, Too Much Cash” by the Freestyle Kings plays through the CD recorder in Jordan’s car. I got me a job, booyah! I really don’t need a second job right now since I’m already working three cards a week, plus house shows and promotional tools, but it’s all gravy, as long as I get to put some stupid ass white people in their place, I’m happier than the average man! There’s no use in sugarcoating it, I hate almost all white people, and I think they are the scum of the earth. But since I’m teaming with Duke Williams, Jason Core, and Legend Inc these days, I guess I should learn to deal with their type. It’ll be hard though, because they urge will always be there to seriously injure a cracka that looks at me wrong. Jordan turns off the dirt road and into a residential neighborhood. Jordan’s two-way pager suddenly goes off. Jordan fumbles around his pockets for it, and he finally pulls it out, he reads the in-box Email thing on it, and it states. “Jordan, the press conference will take place in Dallas, the street name is Crestwood, and the number is 1921, so 1921 Crestwood Drive is where you are to meet at, it shouldn’t be hard for you to find, it’s right beside the Reunion Arena. Jordan nods and smiles yet again slowly, he’s confident that he’ll knock ‘em dead at NAACP headquarters, he really was looking forward to telling everybody about the attacks his suffered, and he was looking forward to getting his name out there to the general public again. Before Jordan had joined the PCW Jordan was wreaking havoc in the NTICW, he was a true rising star, and he was quickly becoming the most popular and recognized man in the NTICW. He was enjoying himself, but sadly, the NTICW had decided to cut back on Saturday Showtime, and they would only be running one card a week, that just wasn’t enough for Jordan. Jordan needed to make his money, he needed to get that money that we all seek and pray for. But now that Jordan has joined the PCW, and it’s three cards a week, Jordan is finically set. But there’s one problem, since Jordan joined the PCW, he’s changed. Changed a great deal. Before he was a no nonsense, trash talking, and white man hating SOB. He was definitely going to go straight to the top of the NTICW, he was bound to eventually dethrone the current champion, Scott Taylor. He knew that the white man was inferior in his mind, and he knew that there wasn’t a white man in the entire wrestling world that could defeat him… But now in the PCW, Jordan is no longer that man. Jordan is no longer the abrasive, proud black man who wasn’t afraid of a single white man in the world. He was no longer the man that would literally, be involved in drive bys with the gang that his brother, Drew Hostyle, was once a part of. Instead, Jordan was a timid, unmotivated, loser. He had lost his first match in the PCW, but it was nothing to be ashamed of, yet Jordan still didn’t feel himself. He was too nice to white people, and they were taking advantage of him. It started with Xavier Kannon, but it’s going to end with Mark Makonie. Jordan pulled onto the industrial area of ‘D-Town’, he was closely checking out every sign that stated the street, and every building that was tall enough to be a Sears Tower. He didn’t spot the NAACP headquarters. He kept driving slowly as traffic had slowed him down, the music in the car no longer played. Jordan sat there… Thinking at a red light. Hmmm…. I’ve changed…. Too much for my likings. I’m just another run of the mill black guy who hates white guys… I’m nobody special…before I was special…before I was the most hated man in the NTICW, but now I’m one of the most loved… I’ll be honest, I do enjoy being loved more than I enjoy being hated… But still, it’s time to get back to business. No longer shall I stand idly by while the white man makes a mockery of my people, no longer is the white man going to step all over my good name, and step all over my people. Xavier Kannon, Mark Makonie, Tyler Nelson, the entire Ultimate Authority made a mockery of me and my peoples when I was Xavier Kannon’s SLAVE! I wasn’t a damn servant, it was like they had set the black man back another hundred years, I was a bitch, a fan, a phony, a fake… The light turns as Jordan cruises up the block. He passes a large building with the words NAACP on the top. Jordan X Shabazz: “OH SHIT!” Jordan turns at an intersection and he drives into a large parking lot, which obviously belongs to the large NAACP building. There parking lot is packed, which leads Jordan to believe that this press conference is rather important, and that he is a big coup for the NAACP. Being a black man, and being a victim of racism numerous times, Jordan’s juices are flowing. Jordan parks his Jeep and he gets out. For the first time we can see what he is wearing, though you’d think he dress formally for an occasion such as this one, Jordan is dressed in typical baggy blue Pure Playaz jeans, a large baby blue FUBU football shirt, with the number 05, and on the back, Copeland, his real last name. He wears light brown untied Lugz boots on his feet, and finally a Toronto Maple Leafs baseball cap over a black bandana. Jordan wipes some beads of sweat from his forehead as he begins walking towards the entrance of the large two-story building. The office is full of people quickly moving about, looking very important. Jordan looks around, then he walks to the front desk where a secretary is typing madly away at her computer. Her desk is completely covered in paperwork, and she wears a smile on her face as Jordan taps the counter. Secretary: “May I help you?” Jordan looks at the secretary and nods. Jordan X Shabazz: “Yes, I’m Jordan Andrew Copeland, I was called her to be the new spokesperson. In fact there’s a huge press conference coming up where I’ll make a speech soon.” Secretary: “Oh, I apologize.” The secretary gets on the phone and she dials three digits. Suddenly two large security guards follow a tall black man dressed in a black suit. The man walks hurriedly to Jordan X Shabazz and they share a hardy handshake. Man: “Mr. Shabazz, I’m Doctor Scott Robinson, I am the CEO of the N double A CP, and I would like to welcome you to these offices. Let me take you upstairs to your office.” Jordan nods, but doesn’t speak. He follows Scott Robinson up a set of stairs and into a quiet area of the building. It was as packed as the others, and it was decently spaced out. Large offices with see through glass showcased the offices well. It was very well lit, and Jordan walked with intent behind Scott Robinson and his two bodyguards. They finally come upon a place in the corner of the hall that was secluded from the offices. There wasn’t a see through glass window, but there was a glass door. It was half covered up with some sort of green metal. Scott Robinson used a key to unlock the door, and he led Jordan into the office. The office was beautiful. There was a large nameplate on the nicely carved wooden desk. It stated in bold, gold letters “Jordan Copeland, NAACP spokesperson.” Jordan grinned when he saw it, and he grinned even wider when he saw the red, leather swivel chair that was his. Also on the large desk was a black Dell computer that looked brand new. Beside the Dell was a black Macintosh laptop computer. There was a large completely packed bookshelf, we could see that most of them had something to do with race. The office was rather large, and it allowed space for a Television, a decent sized sound system, and several chairs. Scott Robinson directed his guards outside the office and he directed Jordan into the chair. Jordan relaxed and he put his feet up on the table as Robinson spoke to him. Scott Robinson: “Mr. Copeland, you are expected to work here everyday that you get off from the Primetime Championship Wrestling. I suggest you move to this city for easier travel. Also, you can have interviews for a secretary next week. Also, make sure you dress more appropriately next time. Your speech will be in about ten minutes. Good luck.” Jordan stood up and shook the hand of Scott Robinson and smiled. He didn’t speak, he turned on his Dell computer and got onto the Internet within seconds. He didn’t really need to use the Internet, he just wanted to have the feeling that he could surf the web while at work. He smiled. This is the most perfect job a guy could have! My office is excellent, nice, and big, a lot of space for me to move about. I’ve gotten some great tools, and though I don’t have a day off working, I’m sure I can handle it. I love the NAACP. Jordan left the computer on, reclined in his chair, and turned on the large Television with the converter that was on the table. After what seemed like an hour, but in reality was like 8 minutes, a young white woman came to the door. She smiled at Jordan, and Jordan smiled back. Woman: “You’re up Mr. Copeland.” Jordan nodded and he stood up. He followed the young well-dressed woman down two flights of stairs, and into a large, packed conference room. Light bulbs flashed as Jordan walked to the large podium. He wasn’t prepared for a speech, but he was willing to make one up as he went along. Jordan looked at the faces, all eyes were on him. Jordan X Shabazz: “Ladies and gentlemen, the NAACP would like to announce that they have signed me as their new spokesperson.” Murmurs and whispers began. Jordan waited until they settled down before speaking. Jordan X Shabazz: “My status in the PCW helps not only myself and the NAACP, but it helps black people everywhere.” More light bulb flashing. Jordan X Shabazz: “I will do everything in my power to make sure that the black man is NOT oppressed by corporate white America. It’s a new age ladies and gentlemen, and it’s time for a REVOLUTION!” Jordan screams out the last words as some of the people in the large room look worried and question Jordan’s sanity. Jordan X Shabazz: “There will be NO more accidents like Police Brutality, if there EVER is another thing like what happened with Rodney King, you can bet your ass that those cops will get theirs. There’s nothing left for me to say, but thank you all for coming, and good afternoon.” Jordan smiles, and he walks off the stage to a decent reaction from the media and the civilians who went inside the offices to hear that short speech. Two bodyguards lead Jordan back up to his office where he relaxes. He reclines in his chair and he goes into his pants pockets. He pulls out a plane ticket, it’s coach of course. Jordan X Shabazz: “Time to head to Sabotage.” Jordan says to himself, as we fade…To….Black.
