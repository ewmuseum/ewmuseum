On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt1- The History

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th March Upon the mess caused at March Madness, El Diablo fled in fear of the Flaymin One and eliminated himself from the Madness Rumble. The Mexican Devil's return to PCW after near three months away sent shockwaves to just one man, Flaymin Aymin. For weeks El Diablo sent messages via the Jumbotron promoting his return and what he was returning for, only it was done anonymously. While he did gain retribution like he promised, Aymin will now be looking for the same retribution. Call it his 'Rebirth' if you will, as El Diablo has returned with a new look and a new attitude yet still beneath the surface he is the same man that hates corruption and ridicule. With his now short dyed red hair and goatee, it really is a new Mexican Devil on the outside, and perhaps by his actions at March Madness on the inside too. Diablo is paranoid, paranoid about life. Sure, it sounds strange, but it's easy to see why when looking back at his career in PCW and even how his life was before he entered Primetime Championship Wrestling. The paranoia began shortly after the death of his brother Pedro, and that spark was magnified in PCW when it was revealed beyond a shadow of a doubt that Flaymin Aymin was his killer in that fatal car accident. Thus begins the history of Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo. Being involved in International Incident, El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin actually became good friends at a time and were often inseparable; in fact Flaymin was his best man when Diablo married long time love Angela (then known as Angel). It all went sour from there on. During a standard Tag Team match, El Diablo left Flaymin to fend for himself and publicly announced his leaving of the International Incident, Flaymin of course was angry and a general bitterness was born between the two. Was El Diablo bitter with Flaymin? Who knows but the man himself, but Flaymin was very bitter and rightfully so, and there began several confrontations which resulted in the burning off of Aymin's esteemed afro, the blowing up of his beloved beer and the loss of one of his testicles. To Diablo, he discovered it was Flaymin that killed his brother; Flaymin then stole his wife and the loss of his beloved mask. Oh, how El Diablo loved that mask, as he claimed at March Madness it was his heart and soul and heritage, and with one defeat it was all wiped away and erased, intensifying his paranoia and his need to hide his face. It was a severe blow to the Mexican Devil who had prided himself on that mask, it definitely caused a changed in him and he literally hit rock bottom. Strange ramblings soon followed aimed at Flaymin Aymin, the public was meant to believe he was rambling about his mask, but it soon transpired that it was really about his dead brother and how Flaymin had killed him. The straw that broke the camels back though, was when during an El Diablo interview, Flaymin came out dragging the tombstone of Diablo's dead brother and shattered it into dust. For El Diablo it must have been heart breaking, and it changed him forever. No longer would El Diablo be outgoing, no longer would let his emotions rule him, no longer would he feel any pain, all he would feel is a numbness and that numbness has continued to this very day. The turning point of not only the Flaymin Aymin/El Diablo hatred but also PCW and its history came when they literally destroyed one of PCW's shows, Sunday Sabotage. It was literal sabotage and mayhem, nothing could stop them, not even then owner, Tyler Nelson. The hatred had boiled over so severely that it had affected PCW and PCW broadcasts. So what would follow? It had to end somewhere, and that somewhere was at Spring Fling 2002 when in an unsanctioned street fight, that spread from sick bumps outside of the arena, to men's restrooms and buffet tables, to the ring where Diablo slammed Aymin through several tables to gain victory, and in the process eliminated Flaymin from PCW for several months. It was finally over.....or was it? For weeks prior to March Madness, it was known someone was making a return for redemption and retribution against an old enemy, and as was discovered it was El Diablo assaulting his mortal enemy, Flaymin Aymin to reignite the fuel of their hatred for one another. Is El Diablo really the bad guy in all of this, or is he just misunderstood by the fans? One thing is for sure though; PCW is about to bear witness to further unadulterated sickness and brutality by this rivalry. PCW may never be the same again....once more. So, until the day comes when El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin square off one more, El Diablo will run and hide and deliver his messages to Aymin through other means. Turmoil is simply the beginning, and Rent-A-Hero a victim of Diablo's rebirth. Flaymin Aymin has created a crazy Mexican in El Diablo. Welcome to Flaymin Aymin/El Diablo 2, the history begins all over again.
