'MEH!' Now, with the days passing by for both PCG, PCW and all the people of the world... I have to bring up the point of this promotional feature being shown as.. yet another PCW 'Presentation.' ACTION! ** 'Dragonball Z' recap music begins to pump out of those same magical speakers that made their debut within the second installement of the current Chapter. ** Previously... on Pretty Cool G's...Our heros finally came face to face with the menacing menace known to the world as the acceptor of PCW Contracts... and boy, were they in for one of the roughest times in their short lived PoP career after being told that getting the papers for the job... wasn't as easy as getting a Chesseburger from that delicious vender... What does this man want PCG to do for him?...Why is this episode being taped and shown after their first match had occured?...and finally... Since when was I told to announce that PCG's very own Ryan Moore will be making his singles debut next week?The answers to all of these questions and more... today... on Pretty Cool G... ** The recap music plays its way through before finally fading out into nothingness... the viewers, the fans and everyone bothering to tune into this broadcast... able to get their senses back and pay full concentration on the movie ahead. ** | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 1 : The Beginning of a PoP era...Installment III : A hidden task and a pair of special guests?!... Bonjour...Bonjour...and Bonjour...Welcome to the third installment of the third most pathetic series since we were all subjected to the crapfest that was better known as 'The Avengers'... which first aired in something like... 1920... but yeah, boring facts from somebody who knows how to party on alot better. Once again, as you are able to notice from the way I have introduced you all to the show... the intellectual intellect that I like to call my personality, has decided to grace you with a lesson in the arts of French. Now, to be perfectly honest about the way that I'm greeting you with every episode... don't you think its an innovative use of international language... besides, I'll be teaching you all the very languages that I managed to learn on my travels along the world... as we performed with some of the greatest bands in the history of PoP... The Backstreet Boys...A-1...N'Sync...and... who can forget the very good, the almost God like group called 'Wetslife'... Erm... Rich, you seem to have made a mistake with one of our brother bands... you called them 'Wetslife'... when we all know them by the name of 'Westlife'... Oh shut up you bloody rabbit.... just because you managed to kick bottom on Friday night... you think you can run around the place telling me whats right and whats wrong? Look buddy, I used Audiogalaxy the other day and when I downloaded 'Uptown Girl'... it was by a band called 'Wetslife'... understand... kids... or young wrestlers... they think they know so much.. You do know... that people on that little thing, change the names of the song artists so the tracks aren't banned from being downloadable... Look... I am the most intellectual man in the history of the world... and you... you're just a boy who managed to get through customs instead of Sean Smith... who would probably be saying the same thing... but in tense so advanced that neither you or somebody like Mike Frampton would be able to understand! Now, will you just leave me alone while I get this bloody thing started? You said I could do the video... ... Yeah, and I think I said that you're going to be fighting for the Extreme Title next Friday Night... No way... you didn't Rich... you know how scared I get of all those brute men running around... screaming to their hearts content with weapons and what-not.... you bastard... Well, thats what you get for concerning yourself with something that doesn't really... and shouldn't really have to include your being at this very moment in time. Now fans, people around the world... its time for me to shut up with the intros... and step forth, with 'Episode III'... | - ^ - ^ - | The scene slowly begins to open up for what could and should be classed as the third installment of a story that could quite possibly never have an ending to it. For the better half of the more recent times, we'd seen myself and Ryan Moore get stuck in some crazy office with this freaky old guy who tried to make us sign PCW contracts... well, I think that story has really run its course over the last week... What do you mean... I loved that story... it was my favorite... How the hell could it have been your favorite story... the bloody thing hadn't even even finished yet... and... wht do I even bother trying to explain these things to you... you're so... lame! Fine.. I'm going to call Mamma Sherwin and tell her exactly how mean you are to everyone around you... Fine... call my mamma you silly boy... she'll just tell you to turn to the Horoscopes and look up what it says for Scorpio... I'm a fire breathing dragon baby and I ain't scared to bitchslap your ass all the way back to Atlanta... hmm, the silence would go to indicate that Ryan Moore has decided to run along and call my mamma. However, besides all of that embaressing segement there with myself and my friend... I think its time we move onto better... more advanced things if you will. Sure, the episode of the story will keep its same Title... but I think I'll use the power of PoP, to reach on down to the bottom... and just pick off that 1 and that III... and then change a few things to grab this Title... Chapter 2 : The time has come... we think...Installment I : Special Guest #1?!... See... can you even begin to place emphasis on how good that Title looks in the light... ah well, if you're unable to do that... well, too freaking bad... its my shot and I like it.Regardless, the picture begins to open up inside the confinements of a small house... all the way on the West side of a city... which was ironically called 'West Side City'. The day was one that resembled everything that Summer should be like around the world.... decent heat, nice conditions and babes on the beach, waiting to be picked up by a 'Sex God' like myself... an icon for females to look at and drool upon... oh yes, the power is mine.We finally get around to joining our heros in the safety of their own home... all three of the special characters at this time are sitting at the 'Breakfast Table' in the kitchen of their cosy complex. Ryan Moore is seated on one end, I am seated upon the other end... and the first special guest of the night... Nick Carter, is busy eating some Cheerios in the middle. Quite the combination you'd say? Hell Yes, I say... the most powerful PoP tandem in the world! 'Wow fellas...', Nick started to say, taking his eyes around the room, picking up every little detail with regards to the scenic view. He waited for a few moments for the feeling of his line opener to sink in before continuing with, '... you guys really have one of the best houses across all of Storytown..' 'What the hell do you mean by that?' I asked, in a tone that would not only reap the destructive powers of pain and 'No Gain'... but the sour excuse that my 'Horoscope' says this is the way I am. Ryan Moore looked at me with a hint of 'Da hell?' in his face... I swallowed my pride and didn't say anything more... dropping the subject if you will... sweeping it under the mat... throwing it... Yes Rich... we get the point... Yeah... well, I basically acted like that was the end of the conversation... and it was, until Nick Carter tried to be bad ass about it... he was starting to talk smack to be about how rude I was being... and he got up... and I kicked his ass... singing Britney Spears is definently one of the reasons why I became a wrestler... Oh Rich... that didn't exactly happen did it... No... you're right Ryan... that little bit about the talking smack didn't quite happen... although it was the best sounding part of every single PCG installment so far... wouldn't you say so yourself? Probably not... Fine... be mature about it all and don't laugh when I'm trying to be funny... but remember who the 'Intellectual' member of the Posse is mister! Getting back onto our details, Nick was sitting at the table...he had stopped eating his delicious cereal when I made that really uncool comment to him. At the time, I didn't want to believe that such a town existed... especially since it would make everything I'm saying now seem... a tad fake if you will... but alas, I grow to terms with that fact... and my friends say I should forget about it in about several months time. 'Oh... I'm sorry Rich...', Nick responded, in a tone that could only go as far to suggest that I'd ripped his heart from out of his chest... stabbed it with a knife a couple of times before grinding it in a mincer, frying it into a burger and handing it back to him before asking for $24.95... damn emotional artists... they think they're so good because they can cry about something as lame as that... I guess thats why MTV and TRL still think I'm the most handsome topic of general womens life... Don't you mean the most pathetic? Nick... how the hell did you get into the darkness recording room? Oh... I didn't know this was the dark room... eww, you could be doing something that I don't want to know about... I'm outta here... Good... and don't ever think about coming back you little piece of poop.. hell, if I had a dime for every time that somebody would invade my time as being God of the PCG Promotional video creation... I would seriously be a millionaire and have no need to wrestle within Primetime Championship Wrestling. However, the topic of this video isn't about money is it... infact, its about the current character that found their way into the PCG home... namely, Nick Carter of 'The Backstreet Boys'. 'Nick... I'm sorry for shouting at you... I didn't really know that you were trying to be friendly about everything... forgive me?', I asked, looking like I was ready to sheer a tear for one of my apparent friends... obviously, I took Joey Tribiani's(sp?) advice from 'Friends' and used that good ol' Tweaser trick to make the tears form in my eyes. Nick looked at me... yes, the dude looked at ME... and he believed that I was shedding tears because I felt bad about the thing that I had said to him... meh, why am I going to feel bad about speaking the truth... when I can feel poignant and fresh. 'Oh Rich..', he started before going off into a session of tears (without the tweasers mind you). I looked around the room and wondered how long I had to continue inflicting this pain upon myself before he spoke once more... and about five minutes later... he finally splurted out everything else, '... I can't believe that you're so kind and warm... it almost makes me want to leave 'The Backstreet Boys' and become the third member of 'Pretty Cool Guys'.... oh, I see this as being the beginning of a beautiful friendship.' 'Oh Joy' I thought as Nick began to mop up his tears... and I released the pressure being applied to my groin from those freaking tweasers. Hell, that had hurt... and it had all been for nothing really... I though Carter would have shown something of extreme hate and disgust toward my attempts at being mushy... but in actual fact, everything had backfired and shot back in my face without due warning. I guess the proper term that I should use for this situation is 'Uh Oh'... but well, maybe things can be a little bit better over the course of time. 'Well...', Ryan Moore began before his voice slowly began to fall away like it usually does... and hell, thats one of the most annoying things about my Tag Team partner... always trying to say something about somebody... and then, just stopping without even getting to the end of whats said... sometimes I wonder... what would life be like if Ryan Moore was able to finish every sentance that he ever started... and then I realize how much more annoying he would probably be in relation to arguements about my 'Intellectual Advantage' upon him. 'Well... this pretty much ends everything that needed to be brought up.', I said, pushing back from the table and getting up from the chair. Ryan and Nick looked at me with an astonished eye peering on my every movement. I looked down at the two of them... and flashed my trademark 'Mocking Smile' before walking away from the foolish people... Nick was here to stay... DAMN... and well... I was able to leave the table with a smug smile hovering over my face like nothing seen before me. I continued walking away from the table toward the entrance of the house, my smile still showing on my face... the door was opened on my side when... A FIGURE STOOD IN THE WAY... I cowered in fear... I screamed for my mommy... Oh No... what was going to happen now?!?!?! The picture ended as we left me... cowering in fear from something standing infront of me...
