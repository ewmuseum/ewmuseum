Eyeballs of steel... admit it- you're jealous!!

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/07/02 When we last left off….. Flaymin was in the midst of a life-threatening testicle operation aimed at having Red Rock’s Balls of Steel implanted into his nutsack.While the operation was in progress, Flaymin’s soul drifted skywards where he ultimately had a rendezvous with The Grim Reaper.Having successfully helped The Grim Reaper get his groove back, thanks to a vibrating Cattle Prod, Flaymin’s soul was returned to his dormant body in the operating theatre.Upon being awoken, Flaymin was overcome with joy since he assumed had Red Rock’s Balls of Steel inserted. But the surgeons got it wrong.Horribly wrong. Instead of Balls of Steel… Flaymin now has EYEBALLS OF STEEL!!! [Scene picks up where we last left off. Flaymin is frantically running around in his hospital gown trying to comprehend what has just happened] Flaymin: EYEBALLS OF STEEL??? HOW COULD YOU CONFUSE MY EYE SOCKET WITH MY NUTSACK?!?!?!?!? [The incompetent surgeon shrugs his shoulders. Flaymin continues to act in a hysterical manner as he attempts to find a mirror so as to see a self-reflection of his eyeballs of steel] Flaymin: A mirror!!Get me a damn mirror!!! [The surgeon cautiously takes a step back] Surgeon: I really wouldn’t recommend that.You know what? Flaymin: WHAT???? Surgeon: Why don’t you hop back on that operation bed and I’ll try and fix the problem. Flaymin: Are you kidding me?You’ll probably chop off my nose and replace it with my donger! Surgeon: Heh, heh… a dickhead! [Flaymin hyperactively waves his index finger in the air] Flaymin: Oh no!Oh no you don’t!You’ve got no right to be cracking jokes, smart-ass! [The Surgeon strokes his chin] Surgeon: Smart ass, huh?Yeah I’m sure we could sew your brain onto your buttocks, or visa versa. No, no, that wouldn’t work… because then you’d just be a dumb-ass rather than a smart-ass!Ha, ha, I think I’ll leave you to it… dumb-ass. [The surgeon walks out of the operating theatre chuckling while Flaymin remains in the room in a frustrated state. Suddenly in the corner of his eye, Flaymin notices a hand-held mirror.He dashes over towards it and picks it up.Slowly he holds it up to his face and begins to move it around slightly to gain a better reflection of his new eyeballs of steel. Suddenly the front door of the operating theatre swings open and in walks Flaymin’s gypsy valet- Hirraani] Hirraani: Who was that woman shrieking? Flaymin: Um… that would have been me. Hirraani: You could hear it all the way down the hallway.You gave the Heart Attack patients a stroke and the stroke patients a heart attack. Flaymin: Who cares about them!Look at me! [Hirraani looks into Flaymin’s eyes and raises an eyebrow] Hirraani: What happened to your eyes? Flaymin: Look closely. [Hirraani leans in and gazes into Flaymin’s eyes.She then yields her head back and frowns] Hirraani: I didn’t know you wear contacts? Flaymin: NO, you stupid crack-whore!I had the balls of steel inserted into my eyes!! Hirraani: Eyeballs of steel, huh?Cool! Flaymin: I look like a freak.Here, just look at this mirror. [Hirraani looks at the ‘mirror’ in Flaymin’s hand and smiles] Hirraani: That’s not a mirror.It’s a piece of glass! [Flaymin clutches his eyes and then falls to his knees in despair] Flaymin: Oh my god!I’ve gone blind!! Hirraani: I guess constant masturbation does make a person go blind… Flaymin: It wasn’t that!It’s these freaking eyeballs of steel. [Hirraani grabs a hold of Flaymin’s hand and helps him up off the floor] Hirraani: Here, let me have another look. Flaymin: Alright, but be careful- this disease might be contagious. Hirraani: *Sarcastically* Oh yeah, there is that nasty outbreak of ‘eyeballsofsteelititis’ going around… [Hirraani places her fingers on Flaymin’s eyes and then pushes up his eyelids to garner a better view at the steel eyes] Hirraani: Wow, these look great. Flaymin: Really, you think so? Hirraani: Yeah, next time you’re wrestling and someone throws a bag of powder at your eyes, you won’t feel a thing. Flaymin: Hmm, you’re right. Hirraani: And now you can easily win all those staring contests without having your eyes water as much.No wait, the water will probably make your eyeballs go rusty.Rusty Eyeballs of Steel!! Flaymin: Sheesh. Hirraani: And I bet Stewart Hix would love eyes like this. Flaymin: Why? Hirraani: Finger-poke of death.If he had these eyes, he wouldn’t have jobbed to a finger poke. Flaymin: I don’t care about that pot-smoking, donkey-f*cking bitch!We’re supposed to be talking about me!! Hirraani: Me!, Me!, Me!It always has to be about me… err… I mean you. Flaymin: You’re not the one with eyeballs of steel! Hirraani: You’re right; I really should be more caring in your time of need. Flaymin: Damn straight! Hirraani: So what are you going to do now? Flaymin: I guess I’ll have to keep searching for a new method to get a second testicle. The dream about having balls of steel is over. Hirraani: Don’t worry, I’ll help you find a new nut. [Scene fades to black] Where to now??What ploy will Flaymin come up with next in order to gain that elusive ‘perfect testicle’? You’ll have to tune in next time to find out!
