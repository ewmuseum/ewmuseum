The Adventures of Jeremy J. Johnson: Finding the Truth about Chris and Corey Chase-Williams... aka Triple J visits a Gay Bar

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29 March 2002 The bar is seedy. And I don’t mean gardening. Leather clad men are strewn all around the place, bikers... people wearing a pair of jeans and some suspenders... and a Russian style hair-do. But if JJ knows one thing about gay men, it’s to avoid speaking to him. He can’t help it if he’s a gay icon, he’s stuck with the notion 24/7 and he only has Steven Smith to blame for the entire thing. He stands in a darkened nightclub wearing a black leather jacket over one of his normal Hawaiian shirts, and a pair of sunglasses on his face that he borrowed from Miyagawa. Ok, he stole both the jacket and glasses from Miyagawa, but let’s face it, Miya did ask him to come here to try and find out some stuff, and he’s not about to wander in touting himself as Jeremy “The Gay Sex Icon” Johnson for fear of being molested by Bob or Jeff or even Doug the Biker who likes to be whipped while eating ice cream. JJ: Urgh... Says the apparently straight man as he spots someone at the bar kissing another man. The pit of his stomach turns to disgust as he makes his way to the bar as well, just going around the “couple”. He spots a cigarette machine in the corner of the bar and realises something from his trip to London earlier this year. JJ: Heh, a fag machine. He steps up to the bar and pulls out his wallet... and puts it away realising the fact that it’s probably the only G.I. Joe wallet to have been pulled out in this bar ever. He glances around and notices a couple of guys dressed in camouflage gear tongue wrestling... ok, maybe not then. Barman: What can I get you darling? JJ looks up at the four hundred pound man built like a brick shithouse... if brick shithouses were made from pink bricks, that is, and feels complete worry. JJ: Um... can I get one of those alcoholic mineral waters, like they drink in Hollywood, you know? They’re kinda hard to get a hold of. Barman: Which type? JJ: What do you mean “which type”? Barman: They’re big in “our” kind of scene, so we import them from Canada, the gayest country in the world. So we have six different types... we’ve got Alcoholic Perrier, Evian... and a few others. We’ve got a rather nice Sex on a Beach as well, it’s the house special. JJ: Sex on the Beach? How is that like Alcoholic Mineral Water? Barman: Sex on a Beach... it’s fucking close to water, isn’t it? **SILENCE from JJ** Barman: You know you should take those sunglasses off; it’s pretty dark in here. I doubt you can see anything through those lenses. JJ: That’s why I’m wearing them. Barman: Come again? JJ looks stunned for a second. JJ: Did I just say that out loud? Erm... He whips the sunglasses off and shoves him in his inside coat pocket. The barman instantly recognises the young man as Jeremy J. Johnson... Barman: JJ~! The entire bar goes silent as they look at the barman and then at JJ’s cute ass. JJ covers his ass up as reflex action, which makes them all stare at his pert mouth instead. JJ: Awww crap. I’ll just have a Diet Coke then. No, make it a Pepsi, they might have cancelled the sponsorship deal after I wouldn’t have JJ Bear drinking a can of Soda, but I got used to the taste. Barman: Was it a salty taste, JJ? JJ: No it was not a salty taste, and my name is no longer JJ. It is Jeremy J. Johnson, or you may call me Triple J. I’m an investigative reporter for PURE, that’s Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment. Barman: Oh we all like all things that are pure here. Pure... innocent... virginal... JJ: Aww crap. Barman: What’s with the extra J anyway? JJ: I added the extra J because I thought it would be hardcore. Barman: I'm all in for hardcore, all the time. JJ: Awww crap! I’m not gay dammit! I’ve never been gay, I never will be gay! It’s all Steven Smith’s fault, he just kept saying it until people started to believe it! And it’s not true dammit! JJ is getting visibly upset... mainly because he’s a poor defenceless little jobber in a society of gay sexual fiends. Hank would love this, but alas for poor JJ, I knew him Horatio. Horatio? Dammit, I’ve lost him, quickly get the CPR team in here again... sorry, I’ve been doing lines for Hamlet and E.R. at the moment, and I keep getting them mixed up. Barman: Funny, that’s what most Gay people say just before they admit they are Gay. JJ: That’s because they’re lying, but I’m not! Barman: And that when they’re confronted. Anyway, I promise not to chat you up if you tell me what the middle J is for. JJ: Jobber. Barman: What? JJ: That's right, Jobber is my MIDDLE name. Barman: Ah. Silence resumes its forthright hold in the bar once again as the conversation crawls to an un-dramatic halt all of a sudden. JJ: Listen I came here because I heard that one gay person can recognise another gay person. Barman: Just like I recognise you sweetcheeks. JJ gets annoyed again. JJ: I’M NOT GAY DAMMIT! Barman: Alright, but you’ve got to promise to work through that denial issue you have there. JJ just shakes his head in frustration at the situation. JJ: Right. Are Chris Chase and Corey Williams gay? Barman: Nope, they’re straight. Now Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore are gay. Hell, Kevin is more of a fag than I am. JJ: What? They’re not gay? I’ve come all this way for nothing... oh screw you guys, I’m going home! JJ turns and storms back out the bar, slamming the door behind him. Another punter walks up to the barman and asks him a question. Punter: So Chris Chase and Corey Williams aren’t gay then? Barman: Of course they’re gay. I just really wanted to see JJ’s ass.
