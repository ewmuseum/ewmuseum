Cowabunga Campaign '02: The Totally Tubular Elvis

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6 COWABUNGA CAMPAIGN '02 - The Totally Tubular Elvis Support the most tubular, gnarly, and righteous dudes, the Cowabunga Connection, become the next PCW Tag Team Champions! [The scene opens on the large flashing lights of...Graceland. Memphis, Tennessee, the home of the king of Rock N Roll, Elvis. The streets are filled with many middle aged men and women, whom were young kids at the time of the kings reign, all fill the streets looking for Elvis memorabilia. The most peculiar thing on the main drag in Memphis is the white Chevorlet van with two large painted faces on the side. People are stopping in the streets taking pictures of the van and smiling brightly as they walk by. It seems that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the Cowabunga Connection, have already made their mark in Graceland] [Actually the Connection has been making their mark throughout the south of the United States. Tons of cities have been met by Ned Theodore Hogan and Will Preston Mesquire's great campaign for the Connection. People have begun believing in the hearts of Harry and Tommy, and many believe that once the smoke clears in that four way tag team match, the most tubular dudes from California will walk away as the NEW PCW tag team champions] [The campaign began in the Los Angeles, CA garage of Raggamuffin. The professional skateboarder who made the lovely portrait on the side of the white van. Raggamuffin wanted too much cash, and got fake cash, knocking himself unconscious as Harry and Tommy escaped scott free. They met up with the Mild Scallions, Ned Hogan and Will Mesquire, and they became the Cowabunga Campaign Managers. The first stop was a small town in Alabama, which just spelled trouble to the Heavy Metal One. The town seemed like it was dead until a very hot, farmer's daughter came running toward the van. Unfortunately, right behind her was a horde of evil red hooded virgin sacrificers. These KKK like dudes wanted to sacrifice the farmer's daughter for the sake of...well, nobody knows what for, but it is messed up to sacrifice a bodacious babe as hot as that. The campaign was able to escape the clutches of the evil horde of virgin sacrificers and they gained a new friend, Missy the Farmer's daughter. Oh yeah, she is a virgin, of course. Unfortunately, Tommy has yet to play any games of Yahtzee with her, but one must be patient] [Graceland is just what the doctor ordered for this campaign. These people have worked hard and it's time to kick back and enjoy what life has give to you. The Mild Scallions have gone to a local music store to show off their air guitar talents, Missy is participating in a stripping contest at a local strip club, and the Cowabunga Connection stands on the main drag of Graceland completely undecided as to what they want to do] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Want to go see the amazing Elvis monkey's, man? [Tommy, dressed in a blue and yellow wifebeater, khaki shorts, and black sandals, seems a bit down] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: No, I've had enough of monkey's, dude. [Harry looks down and kicks at the ground, kind of let down. He wears a Ratt 'Touring Round and Round' T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He really wanted to see the amazing Elvis monkey's, because monkey's are freakin' hilarious, man] Harry: Well, we've got a night to just enjoy, man. TTT: Yeah, dude. This is Graceland, but I'm just feeling so bogus all of a sudden. Harry: Why's that, man? [Tommy sighs to himself, knowing that Harry will just get mad if he brings this up. Spring Fling is getting so close and his nerves are starting to tense up. It's a very bogus feeling to doubt yourself getting closer to the biggest match of your career, but that's what is happening to The Tubular One] TTT: I'm just not sure about this match at Spring Fling, dude. I know that we are the hottest and most bodacious tag team in PCW, but do we really have the skill to match everyone else in the match? Are we truly bogus when it comes down to wrestling skill and talent? Harry: We have great wrestling skill, man. We learned from the best there is. TTT: Yeah, we did, compadre. Harry: Our dad's kicked ass, man. [That's right. Before their fathers died in the wrestling ring from a...sexual mishap, Harry and Tommy were taught the sport that is wrestling. Even at a young age the boys excelled and for being little tubular tykes they picked up quickly. They were only five years old but even nine year olds feared them on the beach. Never forgetting that skill and always improving through the years, the Cowabunga Connection is born and ready to take their skills to a whole other level] TTT: Harry, your totally right, dude. Fear Factory has fallen at our hands many times already! Misplaced has gotten a most GNARLY beating from us not once, but TWICE. We bodaciously have dominated every team that we've faced coming into Spring Fling and now its most excellently our time to take the titles. We've been building up for this moment for over a month, and now it's time for the most tubular tag title reign in PCW History. Harry: YEAH, MAN!! [Once again Tommy is pulled out of the depths of depression and doubt. Harry, with his bright outlook on life, has once again done a great job as being a friend. Now Harry wants a little repayment for being such a far out friend] Harry: Now can we go see the amazing Elvis monkey's, man? [Tommy smiles and gives Harry a thumbs up] TTT: Monkey's are most bodacious, dude. [The scene fades and then changes to a little monkey with sideburns glued to the side of it's head. The monkey is dressed up in a glittering jump suit, just like the one Elvis wore. The monkey jumps up and down like a crazed animal...wait, it is a crazed animal. So, that analogy makes some sense, for once. Well, the monkey is doing it's work as a large crowd, whom paid 20 dollars to see this, gathers around the cages to see the monkey's just act like monkey's. Though you have to remember these monkey's aren't just acting like monkey's, they are ELVIS MONKEY'S! Never forget that next time you visit Graceland] Harry: LOOK AT THAT MONKEY GO, MAN!! HA HA! [Harry is right in the front, getting all of the monkey action. His eyes are more bloodshot than ever, showing that he had a little of the good stuff just before throwing twenty bucks down the drain. Harry loves monkey's because they are the coolest. Why does he think their so cool? Well, they are just like people except stupid and they make cool screeching noises. So, its basically like having a really, really stupid child in a sense. Harry can certainly relate to that, could explain why he loves the monkey's so much. Harry also likes spanking the monkey, just thought that would be a good addition] Harry: GO MONKEY, GO MONKEY, GO, MAN!! [Tommy is in the back of the crowd, just looking over the people around him. It's bodacious being here in Graceland to enjoy where Elvis was from, and kicking back. This is the life, dudes. Once him and Harry win the big time tag titles they are surely coming back here to party just like The King would] [Suddenly Tommy is grabbed from behind and pulled into a little golf cart. Tommy seems a bit upset by this and wears a scowl on his face as he regains his balance in the fast moving golf cart. He sees that it is two women dressed up like Elvis. Now Tommy is assuming that these babes would look bodacious if they didn't have sideburns stuck to the sides of their faces. Babes with facial hair just isn't doing it, in fact, it shouldn't do it for anyone] TTT: What are you babes doing? Elvis Babe # 1: We know that your 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, and we're going to help you get more exposure here in Graceland. Elvis Babe # 2: Call it more of a gift from us. [The two Elvis Babe's smile to each other and giggle. Tommy just smiles himself, feeling that this three way game of Yahtzee is going to be a little extra kinky. He's up for anything though] TTT: So, what is the gift, bodacious Elvis babes? [The cart stops in front of a small blue and yellow tent. The Elvis Babe's quickly get out of the cart, grab Tommy by the arms and drag him to the front of the tent. They smile to each other] Elvis Babe # 1: Oh your about to find out. Now just lay back, stay still, and enjoy the ride. [Tommy smiles brightly to himself as the three disappear into the blue and yellow tent] [The scene then changes back to Harry, just with two small children, watching the Amazing Elvis monkey's. The monkey's all seem very tired and annoyed now. These three idiots have been standing there for over an hour looking at the monkey's sleep and scratch their crotches. One even ate it's own poo, that seemed to be a major highlight to the three morons] Harry: It's going to eat it's poo again, man. Kid # 1: No way! Kid # 2: POO POO!! Harry: Come on, monkey, eat the poo, man. Kid # 1: EAT THE POO!! Kid # 2: POO POO!! [One of the monkey's decides that enough is enough. It sticks it's little hand up it's butt and slams Harry in the face with a handful of poop. Harry is thrown backwards and goes through a small table behind him. He knocks over a few stacks of hey as a crowd of nearby on lookers laughs hysterically at the poop faced bandit] Harry: ARGH, MAN! Kid # 1: HE FLUNG THE POO!! Kid # 2: POO POO!! Harry: This sucks, man. [Many people come over and begin taking photographs of Harry trying to wipe the poop crust out of his eyes. Most of the people keep their distances because Harry stinks to all hell] Harry: Someone get me some air freshener, man. Kid # 1: POOPFACE BANDIT!! POOPFACE BANDIT!! Kid # 2: POO POO!! [Just then the distant sound of 'Most Excellent' is heard as the Cowabunga Campaign Managers, Ned Theodore Hogan and Will Preston Mesquire, come through the crowd to see what is the problem. They see their MOST EXCELLENT campaign dude lying in hey with crap in his eyes. That's pretty bogus, wonder how that happened] Ned Hogan: What happened, dude? Harry: A monkey stuck poo in my eyes, man. Will Mesquire: Bogus, dude. What really happened? [Harry stomps his feet like one of the small children near him would do] Harry: I'm telling the truth, that monkey smeared its poop in my eyes, man. Kid # 2: POO POO!! [Will and Ned pull out paper towels from absolutely no where and wipe the excess poop from Harry's face, giving him the ability to see once again. Unfortunately, Will and Ned doesn't have any deoderant or cologne they can magically pull out from nowhere] Both Men: MILD SCALLIONS RULE!!! [They both play the air guitar and it actually sounds as if they really have guitars. Many of the tourists begin taking pictures, amazing at the two men. Harry scowls over at the two men, his hatred for these two growing larger with each passing moment] Harry: Idiots, man. [Suddenly the lights on a nearby stage start flashing, signaling the beginning of another show. The large crowd of people begin to rush to the stage, surprised because the program didn't say anything about an extra show. Talk about a major surprise! The blue and yellow lights shine brightly down to the middle of the stage as a figure begins rising out from underneath the stage. The crowd begins cheering and anticipating whoever this person is. The figure has on a glittering jump suit, just like Elvis, and you can see the black, large hair through the shadows, and of course the every so popular sideburns. The crowd explodes knowing that The King is here! Well, not really The King, but one of it's best impressionists recreating a concert of one of the greatest entertainers in all of time] Harry: That Elvis guy looks familiar, man. Ned Hogan: Of course he does, it's Elvis, dude. [The lights beam down brightly now to reveal...THE TOTALLY TUBULAR ELVIS!!!! The crowd begins going crazy as 'You Ain't Nothin' But A Hound Dog' begins blaring loudly. The Totally Tubular Elvis begins dancing the best that he can, giving the crowd exactly what they want. Behind him the two elvis babes, sideburnless and wearing red glittering Elvis suits begin backing him up] Harry: It's TOMMY!!! FAR OUT, MAN!! Mild Scallions: MOST EXCELLENT!! [As Tommy finishes the most bodacious version of Hound Dog, the crowd of women begin trying to rushes the stage. Tommy's sexiness has been tripled and it's about high time that he get out of there before the horde of women tries to do him in for good] 'Totally Tubular' Elvis: Babes, I need to get out of here. Elvis Babe # 1: Where's your van? 'Totally Tubular' Elvis: It's just a few blocks from here, babes. Elvis Babe # 2: Hold on. [The women strap a jet pack on Tommy and hit a button. The women grab onto Tommy as they fly into the air just in time. The horde of Elvis admirers missed out on loving the Tubular Elvis to death. Harry and Mild Scallions begin rushing through the crowd] Will Mesquire: They are going to the van, dudes. [The begin running down the street toward the white Chevorlet van. As they get there the Tubular Elvis, with his Elvis babes in hand, land at the back doors of the van. Mild Scallions climb in and help the Elvis babes in the back. The Tubular Elvis takes to the driver's wheel and Harry jumps in shotgun position. As Tommy starts the van up he realizes that they are one person short] 'Totally Tubular' Elvis: Oh no!! We're missing Missy, dudes. Mild Scallions: BOGUS! Harry: Shut up, man. You guys suck. [Just then a woman wearing a very revealing string bikini comes running down the street with a horde of horny men behind her. She carries a bunch of money in her hands and some is stuffed in her bikini. Mild Scallions and Harry look on in awe as her breasts bounce up and down viciously, just like when they first saw her. Obviously she won that stripping contest, and a lot more] Missy, the Farmer's daughter: YEEEE HAAAA!! Let's get out of here. [She hops in the back and the Tubular Elvis peels the van out, getting away from the horny men and the horny Elvis admirers. The scene then fades slowly to black]
