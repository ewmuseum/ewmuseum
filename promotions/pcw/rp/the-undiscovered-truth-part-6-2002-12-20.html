The Undiscovered Truth (Part 6)

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 6 Sometimes decisions need to make in the blink of an eye. You have no time to think about them or decide if it’s the right thing to do, but you just do it because you have no other choice. I wonder to my self if it’s not better this way. Sometimes we’ll spend our lives on a decision and never be able to make it. When the decision is forced on you, you have no second thoughts, you just go with the flow and whatever happens just happens. And so I made my decision, leaving no time for extra thought. The door creaked open and my mind became an instant blur. I felt like screaming at the man to the side of me, for never telling me other people had the key. I looked toward him as the two men dressed in security uniforms entered the room talking to one another. I leaped like a frog across the room with the stun gun in my hand and even though we may have confided in one another before, it was all about myself now. The two men turned around, and then they see the man who was supposed to keep me confined tied up in the corner. I watched as they drew their weapons and held out the man’s body in front of me. He screamed for mercy as I whispered in his ear. “Everything will be fine.” I knew they wouldn’t shoot, and if I was going to get out of here this was something I had to do. I quickly made my demands and I made them clear. Drop your weapons and get in the corner. No, you drop the stun gun; we will fire on you if you don’t surrender now. Don’t play games with me; I know what I’m doing. If I were to hold this stun gun to his head for more than five seconds I’d literally fry his brain like he was in the electric chair. So if we all want to stay safe, drop the weapons and get in the corner. I felt the sweat dripping from his head as his heart pounded 50 miles per hour. The two security guards looked at each other and I noticed one reaching below his waste, so I took action. Get you hands in the air! “Play along.” I quickly whispered as I pushed the stun gun on and forced the man to shake as if he was being shocked. The security officers quickly put them in the air. They then looked at one another and realized that I wasn’t joking around. They dropped their weapons and placed them by their feet. Now kick them over into the corner and don’t make a sound. The officers followed the directions and kicked the guns over. They looked hesitant but I made sure they executed it as planned. Now go toward the other corner and sit their Indian styled. One-officer stares down Romantico with a look of disgust as the other nudges him and tells him to just do what Romantico says. Romantico tries to think of what to do next, but is a little unsure about what’s best. He doesn’t know how he could possibly get out of this without harming anyone. The last thing he wants is to be in prison for killing a man and two security officers. If I were seen in a trial again, they’d lock me up for life. I decided I’d make a go at the door, then ditch the by standard and make an all out run for it. It might have been a hard task to pull off, but it was early in the morning and not a lot of people were probably in the center. So I stood up and slowly walked to the door, I saw one of the officers get to his feet and quickly took control. Don’t get up. I will go to the door and then release the man, but if you make a move toward me, I kill him, no second chances. The security guard gets back in sitting stance when he notices a white rose on just lying there on the floor. Romantico looks at his eyes and follows where they are directed and then sees his white rose sitting there. He reaches at his chest pocket and feels the emptiness within it. He then knows he has to get back the flower and he makes another quick decision. For Romantico that rose symbolizes everything for him. He’d be empty inside without it and it’s kind of a symbol for him to keep living on. It also possesses the remembrances of his dead brother and mother, and he won’t let anybody else have it. He sees the officer make the run at the rose knowing that if he gets his hand on it he’ll destroy it without a second thought. Romantico chucks the body of the man he’s holding and dives at the rose getting it a second before the officer does, but now he’s faced with a new dilemma. Romantico pops up as one officer tackles him to the war, and the other draws his gun. The first officer drops his gun while tackling Romantico and it slides into the middle of the room. He grabs Romantico’s arms and holds them behind his back, waiting to apply the cuffs. Romantico worries knowing that this can’t go anywhere but down. This moment right here could be the end of an unfulfilled life. He thought of making a fight, but knew it would just end with him being shot. The officer took out his cuffs ready to apply them when out of nowhere a gunshot was fired into the wall that stunned everyone. I turned around and saw the same man who’s life I threatened risking it all just to save me from a life of pain. The officer with the gun turned as well and I knew it was time to make my move. I back heeled the officer behind me in the midsection knocking him off of he. The other officer turned back around and let out a shot but I dodged out of the way. He shot two more as I raced out the door, not looking back. Each bullet was millimeters away from my skin. I saw the nearest window in the hallway and jumped out of it, right as the officer let out another fire. Luckily more me I landed in a bush and I was only one floor up in the research center. I sprinted down the street, as I was no longer within the distance of the security guards range. And like that, I was away from danger. FOR NOW! (Back in the research center) What the hell just happened! What were you thinking about when you shot that gun. I don’t know, I just wanted to shoot the man for taking me hostage like that, but I got nervous and missed. The man knew he was lying, but it was the only way to save himself. Wait, I found something. The officer goes near the window and sees a sample of blood on it, from when Romantico jumped through. We may not have caught him, but we might have all we need right here.
