Chapter XII: The Fist Spic Torture....

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-3-03 ::Introduction:: Seeing everyone down in Mexico has really changed his output on how he views spics. First of all they smell and they are a bunch of bull shitters. They laugh at every American that walks the face of this earth and they need to be taught a lesson. Mark is the guy to teach them that lesson and he is going to start in a little bit. The Spic Dieing Era is going to come upon us real soon and it could be happening in a matter of days or weeks or even months. After all Mark does have to get some tips from his former friend Jax. Now we continue to look back into the past at Mark Makonie. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XII: The First Spic Torture… “Makonie.” Mark says into the cordless phone as he is trying to hurry the words as he can hear the spics in the other room talking. “Whats up man?” Jax finally says as a pause is on the phone. “Nothing much I need to get together with you.” Mark says as he is pacing around the back room. “When and where at?” Jax says as he sounds worried about his friend. “I don’t care man just somewhere and its has got to be quick.” Mark says as he is now standing in a little corner in the backroom as all the spics are shouting in the main room. “Well how about you meet me in the city of Oaxaca in a little bit.” Jax says as he still sounds a little worried but is trying to not have it get to him. “Can you do that?” “I will do anything possible to talk to you.” Mark says as he is now going to the front of the back room to take the phone back. “Meet me at a little abandoned hut outside of the city.” Jax starts to say as he is explaining what to do. “Go north through the city and go the the outskirts of it. Then turn on the second road on your right after you get out of the city. Go down that drive till you come to the first left. There is where the abandoned house is at Mark. I will see you here no later than an hour. Good day.” Mark takes the phone away from his ear as he hits the talk button to hang up the phone. Mark starts to walk towards the swinging white doors as he notices all of the coffee mix and the creams are stored along some big racks. They are not held down by anything and they are just standing there freely. Mark goes over to one of them and he starts to bring it towards him. It is not as heavy as it looks and he starts to tip it even more. Then he just brings down the rack and he goes to the next as they are coming down with a steel against the concrete sound. The boxes of creams and coffee are going everywhere and Mark then goes over to a couple of bags of coffee and he rips them open parading around the back room proving a point. “This will teach them clean up after themselves.” Mark says as he continues to parade around the back room. Mark then drops the coffee bag as he storms through the swinging white doors. He takes the phone with him as he is dialing the numbers to the taxi cab on them. “Hell this is Pedro, how can I help you?” He says in his Spanish accent. “This is Mark and I need a cab outside of the café right now.” Mark says as he runs through the café giving all of the spics the middle finger. “Alright be there in two seconds.” Pedro says as there is a click on the other end. Mark heads to the front door to the café as he is jumping tables that they people have set out to try and catch Mark. The cash register boy yells something at Mark as he touches the door handle and looks out to the street. Mark looks back at the boy and he then reels back and lets go of the phone out of his hand towards the boy. Mark then turns the door handle and he exits the building in a casual way. The taxi cab is sitting out in front of the café with Pedro sitting in the driver’s seat. Mark runs toward the back and he opens up the yellow door and hope inside. “Go, Go, GO!” Mark says as he raises his voice in the car. Pedro puts his foot on the gas as Mark shuts the back door himself. The taxi speeds out of the town as quickly as possible since Pedro feels that Mark is in a hurry and wants to make something up to him. Mark just sits back into the back seat with his eyes closed huffing and puffing like there is no tomorrow. “What happened back there Mark?” Pedro asks as he looks back into his rearview mirror. “First of all you don’t call me by my first name.” Mark says as he opens up his eyes with sweat running down his forehead. “You don’t have any right by me to call me that. Second of all it is none of your business to what I did to your damn family.” “Alright fair enough.” Pedro says as he is not going to argue with this man. “You see there is a difference between Americans and you Mexicans.” Mark starts to say as he looks up at Pedro. “Whats that?” Pedro asks as they finally get out of the town they were in. “That you are related to everyone around in this whole country and us Americans are only related to a couple of people in the city.” Mark says as he is starting to laugh at the comment that he made. “See you all are smelly shit as well and us Americans are all nice and freshly cleaned.” “What city do you want to goto?” Pedro asks as he needs to know so he knows where to drive. “Oaxaca.” Mark says as he closes his eyes again for the hour long drive there.
