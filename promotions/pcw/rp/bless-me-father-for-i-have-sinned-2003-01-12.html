Bless me Father, for I have sinned

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/12/2003 'I am a complete, utter f*ck up,' confesses Jon Kano as he sits in a pew next to a priest. Kano, dressed in his traditional Armani suit (this one happens to be a solemn black with grey trim), seems to be a hollow man instead of the flamboyant, defiant original King of the Old School that we have all come to know and love. The priest is sitting by Kano, intently listening to Kano baring his soul. 'My son, there could have been no deeds you have done that cannot be undone by the grace of God,' comforts the priest. 'What kind of non-sensical, total bullshit is that?' asks Kano, whom is getting a little tense. 'Jon, God can forgive you,' explains the priest. 'I am not worried about God forgiving me, I am worried about me forgiving myself. I live to wrestle, but ever since I lost my Television Title, I cannot focus and I am making mistakes like I have just gotten out of wrestling school. I am making rookie mistakes, and I still wonder what is wrong with me,' Kano angrily confesses. 'Jon, my son, do you not have bigger things in your life than wrestling, like your wife or your unborn child?' questions the priest. 'Father, my wife called me earlier today, the child is dead. She felt an awful pain in her stomach last night, but thought it was the food she had last night because she threw up afterwards. She woke up this morning and she knew she miscarried.' reveals the Man who Rocketh the Mosteth. Suddenly, there seems to be tears in Kano's eyes, but he chokes them back. 'Let it out my son, you will feel better,' advises the good father. 'Emotion is weakness. I plan on going to see my wife after Adrenaline and then it will be business as usual.' 'My Son, it might appear that you are unfeeling.' After these last words by the priest, Kano leaps to his feet, enraged. He stands over the priest, barely restraining himself from striking a man of the cloth. 'I feel, my child, never had a chance to live. Not one breath did it breathe. Not anyone on this earth can tell how I felt then or how I feel now. Tonight, I have a match against a man who has never had any grief in his life, Jackson Rust. Mr. Rust has no idea what pain is, but tonight I will define the word for him. And Tonight, Father, I will show Mr. Rust, yourself, and everyone else why I am what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!' screams Kano at the top of his lungs as he storms out of the church to a stunned priest. 'Give them hell, son.' the priest says under his breath as the scene fades to black.
