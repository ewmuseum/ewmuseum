Back On Track

2 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/2/04 Introduction: It had been a long day for Cal Roberts. April 1st, 2004 would be the day his recent displays of caring about others more then himself died. During the day he had arrived back to his apartment after being on the road for 2 weeks straight to see it was completely made over by Melissa. While he enjoyed being together with her after all the time they'd been apart, Cal Roberts had began to think the cons outweighed the pros. She was controlling of the weigh he dressed, ate, what he watched on tv, even what he said on wrestling. Which was part of the reason why he had been sending her home earlier every show, to give himself some freedom. The apartment was the last straw, however. So he told her to pack her bags and move out as quick as she moved in. She shed her tears and he left the apartment as quick as possible to see his agent about PCW. He knew his heart was weak, and if he stayed at the apartment, he might change his mind. So it was for the best that he'd received a call earlier in the day. He drove his new 2004 Corvette to the offices of Dames D. Jillon, his agent for most of his professional wrestling career. He had recently purchased due to two 'jobber' tag teams destroying his precious 95' black Corvette, which he had driven since he started his career. It was probably time he traded the car in anyway, to many memories. 'Back On Track' The cool wind blew on my face and through the thin tank top I wore beneath my unzipped leather jacket. I bent back into the car, and turned the ignition off, then pulled out the keys, which I threw into my leather jacket's front pocket. I moved away from the door way and pushed the door shut, and headed for the building. The lettering on the side of the 3 story brick building read 'The Offices of Dames D. Jillon and Friends'. I crossed the parking lot that was filled with cars, and thought sarcastically to myself. 'I better not have to wait...' As I reached the door, I reached into my coat pocket, and pulled out the keys. After alarming the car, I returned them to the pocket, and opened the door. The reception lobby was warm, and the heat coming from the air vents could be heard throughout the room. I looked around, and saw no one in the waiting room. It'd been about 6 months since I'd been to the building, and that was when my PCW contract had been faxed over to Dames. We'd stayed up until midnight doing negeotiations with the now incarcerated Ron Starr and Tyler Nelson, and finally got a great deal for my services. 'Can I help you, Mr. Roberts?' I heard the voice of the secretary purr from behind the reception desk. I looked her up and down, and admired Dillon's taste in women. She was a brunette, wore black rimmed glasses, and had full pouty lips. A woman like that didn't just shuffle papers. 'Why yes you can' I stated with a smirk on my face, which caused her to smile back. I stared into her eyes for a few seconds before the silence was broken. 'With?' she playfully asked. I laughed a bit before answering. 'Unfortunately I'm not here to play with you today. I have to see Jillon about some PCW matters.', I stated while recollecting the last time I was at the offices. You see I wasn't actually waiting by the fax machine like Dames during the negeotiations six months ago. I was attending to some 'secretary' work. I placed my hands on the counter and leaned in. 'Well, head right back, sweety. He's expecting you.' she purred. 'Come back and visit me sometime, I've missed seeing you.' 'Don't worry I'll be seeing you real soon.' She smiled and I turned to move towards the elevators that lined the wall perpendicular to the receptionist's desk. I pressed the up arrow's button, which causes it to fill with light as I waited for the elevator. I whistled and examined the room around me as I waited for the elevator. Various paintings lined the white painted walls, and some flowers were places on tables by the waiting chairs. In the middle of the room was a large aquarium that housed many exotic fish. I watched as one large fish chased a few of the smaller ones, and was surprised when I heard the doors open behind me. I was even more surprised when I saw a famous wrestler walk out from the elevator. El Exotico the famous mexican ...midget...luchadore. He was dressed in a black business suit, and sported sunglasses. To my surprise he also sported a beautiful blonde woman who had about 2 1/2 feet on him, height wise. He looked up at me with a smile, and must have thought we were friends since we teamed one time when I wrestled in Mexico. Teaming with a midget in a match is why I didn't work in Mexico often. 'Fuckin midgets...' I mumbled as he held his hand up for me to shake. I gave him a slight hand shake before getting into the elevator and quick as possible. I pressed the 3 and watched as the doors shut, and felt the elevator jerk as it moved up. After what felt like thirty seconds, the elevator stopped, and the doors opened. I felt a slight breeze of air fill the elevator and I stepped out into a long hallway lined with doors. I cracked my neck and tried to remember which door belonged to Dames, and soon found myself walking down the hallways checking each door. It wasn't until I reached the last door that I saw his nameplate. 'Figures it'd be the last door..' I slammed my hand down on the door three times TAP TAP TAP 'Come in!', I heard his old raspy voice yell. I opened the door, and Dames jumped from his seat with a smile. He walked around from his oak desk and shook my hand. 'Cal Roberts, how the hell are you doing!?' He asked with a genuine excitement in his voice. We moved back towards the desk, and as he moved back around to his seat, I sat in the chair placed in front of the desk. 'I'm doing good, but I've been better man.' I stated while laughing slightly. 'That paycheck I get from PCW at the end of the week makes me pretty happy though.' '20,000 dollars a week should make you feel pretty damn good. Well, I'm not going to waste time about why your here. Those nice checks you get every month might not be coming soon.' I raised my eyebrow as Dames finished his last sentence. 'What do you mean?' I asked with concern. Dames tooks of his glasses, and a solemn look spread across his face as he leaned in on the desk. 'They want to release you...' 'WHAT!' I was shocked. I mean I wasn't necessarily causing ratings to shoot up, but I was getting rave reviews all over the place, and I was one of the top heels in the company. Dames shook his head and looked down, and his head began to shake up and down. He reached for a little black calendar, and turned it to face me. I read the date. 'April 1st, 2004', it took me a moment to realize that it was a joke. I started some fake laughter, and Dames joined in with me. I then abruptly stopped and looked at him with a grimace. 'That was so funny that I forgot to tell you something.', he looked puzzled. 'You're fired!' I got up from my seat and hurried out of the door before busting out laughing at the priceless look on his face as I had said the words. I waited and heard footsteps, and then the door swung open to reveal Dames with a look of worry on his face. 'APRIL FOOLS, BITCH!' I screamed and he stared blankly at me. I laughed and slapped him on the shoulder before heading back into the room. I walked over to the chair I had sat in minutes earlier. I leaned against it and faced the door, as Dames shut it and reentered the room. 'Now, why'd you call me down here man? You know I got things to do when I'm not on the road.', he walked past me and sat back down behind his desk. He motioned for me to take a seat, and looked me in the eye. 'I've been worrying about you for the past three or four weeks, Buzz. You had such a great start in PCW. I mean you rose from an indepent worker to one of the top guys in PCW. You're already a former World Champion, but lately it seems you've lost focus. I'm wondering if you've lost sight of what you're really after.' I was confused at what Dames was getting at. 'What are you trying to say?' 'To be honest you went from trying to show the world how great you are, and going after World titles to being in a mid-card rivalry. I know you like to hold championships, but should you really be focusing on the PCW Continental title?', the look of concern on Dames' face was genuine, and I was ready to put his worried to rest. 'The thing going on between Skitzo and I was never about that belt. That belt is just a hunk of gold as far as I'm concerned. This was about the disrespect he showed me after I vacated my title. This was about him thinking I was trying to steal his title, when I was simply trying to look at the waste of space belt. I don't want the belt for my career, I want the belt to prove a point. Skitzo is not on my level in the ring, and when I beat him for it. He's going to know how it feels to lose something precious.' I took a deep breath after my long rant before continuing. 'Besides I'm focusing myself back on the World Title. I had a minor setback, but I think it's running smoothly now. After my last two matches I don't think anyone is going to have the balls to challenge me.' I sat back and smirked until Dames began to speak. 'That's another thing I had to tell you. PCW officials couldn't get ahold of you on your cell, and I've been informed that you've been challenged. In fact it's by a legendary or maybe I should say infamous pro-wrestler.' I was not happy at what I had been told, but was curious. 'Who is it exactly?' 'The Abyssmal Warrior' I started laughing at the nerve of PCW for allowing the match to be booked. 'The Abyssmal Warrior!? He hasn't been good since 1988. That's when he was actually muscle and not roid juice. I didn't even know he was still alive!', I said jovially. Dames did not join in however, and began to speak. 'I wouldn't underestimate him. From what I've heard he's been training for the last year to come back, and have another run like his 1988 run you mentioned. He could be a legit threat, not only to you, but to everyone going for the gold.' I began to laugh as Dames finished speaking. 'Dames, I know it's April Fools, but you can't be serious about telling me to take this guy serious. The guy comes back for one match every five years. The first one he was actually still impressive, last time he barely won. There is no way he's going to take me out. I'm one of the best technical wrestlers in the world today, not to mention my history in boxing.', I said while standing up. I reached over and shook Dames' hand before walkin towards the door. 'Don't worry man I'm back on track, and I know for sure that PCW ain't gonna' be able to handle that.' I turned the nob and opened the door before turning back to nod at Dames. 'I'll be seeing you on the 12th for my birthday present....that little secretary of yours.', We both laughed as I walked out of the room, and let the door shut behind me. I walked down the hallway, and began to think of PCW. Now that I was refocused on my career...I knew the Era Of Buzz was soon to begin.
