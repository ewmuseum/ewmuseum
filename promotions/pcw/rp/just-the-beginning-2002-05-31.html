Just the Beginning.......

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-02 Just the Beginning.... Heaven Rayne’s childhood bedroom comes into view as she is waking up Friday morning at her parents’ house and is about to prepare to go home. She has been staying with her parents for a week because she had some things to clear up. Since she has been here, she has had a talk with her father, confirming the fact once more that she is an angel; they also discussed the way her dad’s career had taken all of his time for the last ten years. Heaven now has better understanding of him, and has found it in her heart to forgive him. Along with all of the things Heaven has found new and better understanding of, Heaven has accomplished a few things on her visit as well. Heaven completely conquered the hard feelings she once had toward God for taking Jason’s life. Heaven attended church last Sunday morning for the first time in two years. She felt inside that the sermon that the pastor of the church taught on was meant for her. She felt that God spoke to her through the preacher, telling her that she is now ready to accept the calling she was blessed with. Heaven still is not sure what all she is required to do, but she knows that she will soon feel led to help someone, and that she will understand what to do as it comes. She thinks that the feeling she got when she realized god was trying to speak to her about this, is the same feeling she will get when she is being led to help someone. She feels like she is going to accept her calling now. While she was at church she saw Sarah and Mick’s parents. She stated her sincere condolences to both families and they appreciated it and told Heaven they were happy for her for moving on with her life and they were proud of her wrestling career. This felt like an accomplishment to Heaven and a weight off of her shoulders. For the past two years she had been trying to get up the nerve to see how they were doing. She knew that Sarah’s parents were close friends with Jason’s parents, and she asked them if they had heard anything about them. They said that Jason’s whole family had disappeared after the crash. They assumed that either Jason’s father got a work transfer, or the family was just trying to escape bad memories. As Heaven reviews the events of the last few days in her mind, she is still laying in bed. Her thoughts are interrupted by a small rat-a-tat-tat on the door to the bedroom. Without waiting for Heaven to answer her knock, Janet Rayne, Heaven’s mother walks into the bright pink room. Heaven’s bedroom is the same as it was when she left two years ago. The walls are a soft, pale pink, but the bold hot, bright shades of pinks in the bedspread of the large, Queen sized canopy bed, accented by the morning sunlight streaming in from the two large windows on either side of the canopy makes the room seem extremely bright. The only things along the wall where the large bed is placed are the bed itself and the two large windows on both sides. Along the wall to the right is a gold colored dresser, a glass vanity table with gold trim, and a gold book case. All of the furniture in the room is a gold shade to match the large golden frame of the canopy bed. On the floor is fluffy white shag carpet. Janet Rayne walks across the fluffy white, and approaches Heaven still lost in her big pink mess of a bed. “What time do you plan on leaving, Honey,” she asks. Heaven looks at the gold framed clock on top of the dresser and sees that it is 9 a.m. “Soon, mom. I need to pack and then be on my way. Cloe is probably going crazy at home all alone.” “That cat can wait,” says Heaven’s mom, “You haven’t had any breakfast, and you can’t leave on an empty stomach. What do you want….some biscuits and gravy?” “Sure, mom that sounds great. I’m going to go ahead and get a shower and then start packing.” “Ok Honey,” Heaven’s mother says as she turns to walk out of the bedroom and down to the kitchen. “Oh, mom,” says Heaven quickly as a thought occurs to her suddenly. “Yes?” “Make sure you tell daddy bye for my when he comes home tonight,” she says in a sad voice with her head down. “Heaven, he’s home. He has decided to stay and have breakfast with us since it is your last morning before you leave.” Heaven is speechless. She hardly knows how to react, because her dad hasn’t done anything like this in the past ten years. At first her eyes appear blank as she doesn’t know what to think, but eventually a smile appears on her face as she realizes how worth while this visit really has been. Half an hour later, Heaven is seated at the breakfast table with both of her parents. The kitchen has recently been renovated. The floor is white tile with a dark green diamond shape in the middle of each square. The walls are painted white, but there is a gingerbread man cookie border around the center of the walls. The table is the same as before, but the place mat sitting in front of Heaven is now covered by a design of a gingerbread man instead of ducks with country blue ribbons around their necks. The aroma filling the kitchen is of homemade biscuits and gravy, and of bacon that Heaven knows will be crispy to her liking just by the smell. As the food is placed in front of her and Heaven begins to pour her orange juice into a small clear glass, she looks over at her father who is laying aside his “Wall Street Journal.” Heaven’s heart becomes light and happy to see her father is actually going to communicate instead of sticking his nose into business life. Breakfast goes smooth as Heaven talks excitedly to her parents about her wrestling career. “I’m in PCW now, you know,” says Heaven, obviously proud of her achievement. “And Last week for Friday Fusion, I had a triple threat match against Jaden and Anna Keyson. It was my very first match ever in PCW, and I won! I was so excited to tell you guys, but by the time I got her last Saturday I had a lot on my mind. Plus, this is the first time, besides when we were at church that I’ve had the two of you together in the same room, and I wanted to tell you at the same time.” “That’s wonderful, Heaven! Isn’t it Russ?” Heaven’s mom asks her father. “It sure is…you know Sugar,” he says to Heaven, “I am really proud of how far you have come. I remember when you were little, how you used to watch wrestling every week and tell us that one day you were going to be just like the pros…ha-ha……,” his voice trails away in a faded laughter as his eyes look far away and a content smile remains on his face.” “Well,” says Heaven, breaking the silence after what her father has said, and bringing him out of his thoughts, “I have a match tonight, for this weeks Friday Fusion against Kid Krazie, Mark Makonie, and Trigger for the PCW Rising Star Title. I’ve really got to get going.” By this time, everyone is finished eating, and Heaven is anxious to leave the table and finish packing. “Go ahead and go upstairs, Heaven, don’t worry about the dishes. I’ll get them while you get packing your things. I’ll be up to help after I finish down here,” Janet Rayne says to her daughter as she begins collecting plates from the table. Heaven gets up from the table and walks across the tiled floor to leave the kitchen. She goes forward and then makes a right to ascend the stairs leading to her pink bedroom. As she’s packing she hears a loud scream, sounding like it is coming from a woman, and the woman sounds very frightened. Next Heaven hears a deep manly voice sounding angry and violent. Heaven hears glass shatter and then more screams as she looks out her bedroom window to see where the noise is coming from. Heaven feels like she is in a dream, because as her eyes shift toward the small brown two story house across the street, her eyes focus in as though she is using binoculars, and she can see straight into a bedroom window. She can see a plain looking room with a twin bed and a night table at both sides. An ornate glass lamp is at on top of the night table to the far side. The other table however, does not contain a lamp, and Heaven’s eyes shift toward the floor where a lamp lays shattered into pieces on the ugly, ragged, tan carpet. Heaven sees something underneath the blue blanket on the bed. A woman with mangled brown hair sticks her head out of the cover. She has been laying face first, crying into a naked white pillow, with no pillow sham to cover it. As Heaven looks closer, she sees that there is a blood stain on the pillow and when the woman sits up, her nose is bleeding along with a large open wound on her right cheek. Heaven’s hand goes up to her mouth as a surprised gasp escapes her mouth. She looks around her own bedroom, again aware of where she really is. For a moment she felt as though she were inside the house across the street. A funny, sick feeling comes to Heaven’s stomach as she remembers the way she felt five days ago at church when she sensed that the lord was speaking to her. Her head begins to pound and her extremities feel heavy as she realizes why she could see so clearly into that house. She is an angel; these people must need help. Just then, Heaven’s dad comes into the room to talk to Heaven as she is packing. Heaven asks him if he heard people screaming, and when he acts as though he has no clue what she is talking about, she realizes that she is the only person that heard. Heaven seems to forget that her father is in the room as she thinks out loud to herself, “These people must need my help………..” TO BE CONTINUED…
