The Eleventh Commandment

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15th 2003 My body had still been twitching from the high that stayed etched in my mind from Turmoil. My knuckles cramped into a stiff claw, the bell had been ringing constantly in my head, the words of Dave Kern, the saints and the sinners, the birds and the bees, incest, Ireland, the refs hand smacking the canvas mat sending shockwaves all around me. I suppose these things happen. I’m in jail again. I’m in jail and I’m listening to the whistling and senseless slurs they inflict on me as I walk limply through the narrow hallways. I’m in jail and I’m pushing the closet behind the bars. Creek. Creek. Creek. I’m in jail and I’m blocking out their sounds. Creek. Creek. Creek. I’m in jail and nothing’s ever seemed so clear. Creeeeeeek. I’m in jail and everything’s gone away. According to the logistical sciences of Gaelic Ireland not owning a hamster makes me a wanker. It done? Mikey. Mikey owns this city. Mikey’s penis feeds other penises for miles around... This is my life.... Feeding from the fruits of Mikey’s penis is life... I live off Mikey’s old, wrinkled penis. It’s done, I say, I did it. I work for Mikey by mistake. Mikey dialled a wrong number whilst trying to get hold of one pawn or another.... This is how I became a pawn in Mikey’s game of chess... Mikey calls me, Mikey leaves a message, I don’t hear from Mikey again for weeks... To Mikey I’m just #43 Pawn #43 Penis #43 Makes me wonder what it’s like to be my penis. What’s this he keeps pushing me into? This is me, this is my life A mistake of a mistake of a mistake Mikey’s like a God is to the people, we don’t know he exists but he has an overwhelming power that ever the great almighty would acknowledge. If the task involves me travelling long distances I just lock myself in my room for days and wait for Mikey’s call and the parcel with the pillow of green notes. Mikey doesn’t notice whether or not #43 picked up his groceries while someone happened to get shot in the same place at the same time. Mikey doesn’t notice whether or not pawn #43 has been pushed enough by his almighty finger this month. Mikey doesn’t notice if penis #43 is still alive. A number. A statistic. And Mikey’s happy. Mikey’s calls were my period, only it could be days, weeks even months, it all depended on how long it takes Mikey to get from #1-#43 and from #43 to #79. I was on the rotation scheme of Mikey’s chessboard. Now Mikey says he wants to meet me.... Mikey wants to meet #43.... The same #43 that purchases a screwdriver at the same place as a man happens to get his eyes gouged out. Mikey wants to meet #43 the instrument... The penis.... The pawn.... Oh well, things could be worse. It took me weeks to become more than a statistic in America. You’re really Irish? Yes. Is it true that in Ireland ye make human sacrifices to your God’s? You’re thinking of the Aztec’s. How d’you say ‘fuck off’ in Irish? Fuck off. Wow, and what does ‘Ich heisse Patrick’ mean? You’re thinking of Germany. And so on.... According to the literary works of ancient Ireland, drinking salmon juice will make be invincible. And according to Mikey, I should have been dead two months ago.... Weeks ago numbers one through forty eight disappeared; according to Mikey they must have somehow missed me this time around. Mikey wants to kill me, and now Mikey wants to meet me. Mikey says that the element of surprise means nothing anymore, that I might as well know I’m about to die. Mikey wants to meet me but I don’t want to meet Mikey.... See it’s a complex situation, if I run to New York, Mikey tells me #6-37 are stationed there and that they’d hand him my ass on a silver platter. When you’re about to die it’s surprising the thoughts that run through your head.... Intercourse. Mikey’s wrinkled penis. Mikey’s wrinkled penis having intercourse with young girls. Mikey. Things appear through a crystal vase of life. Suzie tells me these thoughts are called ‘pre-ordan’ thoughts. Mikey calls me at home yesterday. Mikey tells me to come around to his house at six to cook him dinner. Dinner with no carrots, broccoli, aubergines, chilli, nothing grilled, nothing fried, oh yeah, and especially no red meat or fish. And I have to be out of there by quarter past six, that’s when he gets home. Mikey calls me and he says blah, blah, blah, Mikey wants to kill me. I ask, so what does he want? I get home from work at the bowling alley and the phone is ringing off the hook... It’s Mikey... Mikey has a job for me.... Mikey tells me I’m a wrestler... Mikey tells me the PCW is a wrestling company. Mikey adds two and two and gets five. 2+2 is to five is to PCW is to Mikey’s old wrinkled dick. Then again, things could be worse.
