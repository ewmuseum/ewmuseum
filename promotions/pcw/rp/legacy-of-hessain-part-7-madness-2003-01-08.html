Legacy Of Hessain part 7: Madness

8 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/03 Recap: Hessain has breached the sanctuary of the psychic Skitzrapto, who has been driving the entire world insane. After learning that the madman seeks self destruction, Hessain has appeared to grant him that. However, as he approached the slumped figure of Skitzrapto, a phantom knight blocks his path. Not just any knight - this is a Serafan knight. A sect of knights trained specifically for one thing... killing vampires. * * * Legacy of Hessain Part 7: Madness ''Skitzrapto - when I have cut down this phantom, I shall take you next!'' I cried, readying my blade. The figure on the throne, looked up. A pointless action, as he had sewn his own mouth and eyes shut to blot out the outside world. However, his eyeballs were not needed to grant him sight, nor would a simple needle and thread silence him. 'Magnumek, you grade-A loser' boomed a voice that seemed to come from my left. 'He's no loser, you're the loser!' came another from my right. 'He failed the circle once before, bee-hotch!' From above?! 'Magnumek, getcho' ass gone, foo!' Four voices?? Magnumek... the name stirred the tiniest pool of recognition in my head. A ghostly Serafan knight named Magnumek... nevertheless, whatever or whoever he was, Skitzrapto would have none of his aid. The figure dissipated into nothingness as quickly as he had come before me. I was alone with Skitzrapto... if a man who was now harbour to many ancient wise men, now all torn with hatred and rage, could be considered one man. He was a curiously dressed fellow. For a man of his rank, he had no armour, simply a grey robe and hood. His face was unshaven and his eyes and mouth showed the black bindings that closed him off, physically at least, from the world. As I raised my sword again, he then rose up and approached me, not by the use of his legs, but floating... 'Skitzrapto! I have come to bring your head to the lady Aimziel and right your wrongs!' I bellowed. 'Your wanna play mind games, boyee? I'll show yo ass head games!!!' he replied. The man had an odd, simplistic method of speaking. Perhaps some things may only be said with lips.My thesis would have to wait, however, as my opponent began to shudder before me. Such violent shaking would snap the bones of any normal man, especially this frail oddity. But what happened next only proved that Nosgoth is a land of any possibilities. Skitzrapto's cloak shredded open as something tried to press through the fabric, bursting into.... another head? Skitzrapto had spawned another of himself, sewn shut and disfigured, into his own shoulder! The two headed beast laughed beneath the cotton and had it allowed much more movement, I'm sure a smile would have spread across those bound lips. The abomination continued to laugh as a pair of arms burst out of the bottom of his legs, spreading the toes of each foot from the wrist to the elbow... if I were human, my guts would have revolted against me at this sight. I watched on as more and more limbs and faces and parts and bits grew on the blasphemy's skin, until it was only human in face. The beast stood thrice my height and writhed with hands and mouths in places that no creator would allow. 'NOW WHICH HEAD YOU BRINGING BACK?' they spoke as one. How hideous it was... I could not permit such a twisted creature's existence any longer. I lunged at the foul thing, swiping my sword at it. My rewards were little, as only a few fingers and a kneecap were severed. My mistake was punished with en elbow... or was that a neck... to my nose, sending me staggering across the courtyard. I tumbled upon the filthy floor with the grace of a slug. Such immense raw power! I picked myself back up and ran at the thing, yelling as my sword divided its feet and jawline. No good did it do me, as I was once again sent reeling into a wall. For those who say death has no pain, I am a testament to the falsehood of that... The beast then gripped me in a hand that had a face on each finger, each one laughing and taunting me, spitting in my face and chewing on any exposed spot. As strong as I was, I knew I was not invulnerable. I could feel myself fading as my ribs were crushed by this unmerciful force. If only I could grab another weapon and hack away at it... I looked around and saw a suit of steel and mail with a shield... wait... In the highly polished shield's face, I saw myself, squirming in the grip of no more than the air, while the original form of Skitzrapto hung above the ground, his shoulders bobbing up and down in laughter. This was a trick of the mind. The form he had taken was one that my mind decided would best fit such a twisted personality. I had to slay the real thing in order to release this crushing grip before I once again met Morgazius... using the shield for guidance, I aimed true to the real Skitzrapto and in one hefty swing, sent a message from the undead to the insane, plunging the blade into his heart. The creature in front of me immediately dropped me and I plummeted to the ground with a heavy thump like a sack of stone. Rolling in agony, the beast began to unravel into thin air with a smoke of fleshy colour, leaving only a bleeding, broken and pathetic Mentalist under it's fading veil. 'How...' 'How...' 'HOW DA SHEEYOT!?' thought the dying psychic. His thoughts were but a whisper now, as more and more of the legion of chaos inside his head became silent. 'Because poison such as yourself must be cleansed from this land, Skitzrapto!' I answered. I raised my sword above my head, ready to strike it down onto his throat. 'Let justice be done!' 'Justice... words of a hypocrisy.' said the last voice in his head as one mighty stroke ended the thoughts of Skitzrapto forever. Grabbing it by the hair, I inspected it closely... well, not too closely. Satisfied with my progress, I looked to the sky and recalled the feelings of taking flight as I once did an hour ago... the air and wind on my being, the beating of wings, the chill of the night air. It was time to go back to the pillars and show Aimziel that I had gotten rid of the scourge of Nosgoth's thoughts. * * * 'Well done, Hessain. The Pillar of the mind has been healed.' Aimziel said as I placed Skitzrapto's head at the foot of the giant stone column. Skitzrapto's gruesome face turned to dust and was absorbed by the pillar. The cracks in it's stonework and crumbling facade begin to close and recover as though fine marble. 'Then with the source of the poison gone, I demand my cure!' I commanded. To my chagrin, Aimziel shook her head. 'Like a spider biting her victim, the poison shall stay even once she has been killed. You shall not be offered your release until the circle itself has been purged and each pillar stands anew.' She said, half in apology, half in a command of her own. 'WHAT! I was...' I began. But I recalled Aimziel mentioning only what I had wished to hear, not what I had really heard. The truth stood as firm as the Pillar of the mind. In order for me to finally gain release, I would have to hunt down each member of this circle and return something that belonged to them as a token to the pillar they served. 'Very well, shade... who is the one I must slay next?' 'The Pillar of Conflict demands that you bring before it the helmet of Magnumek, the Serafan Knight'. Magnumek! The ghostly knight that would have confronted me in place of Skitzrapto had the senile one's thanatos been less. With one pillar restored, I had to journey Northward to the stronghold of Magnumek... the protector of the Circle.
