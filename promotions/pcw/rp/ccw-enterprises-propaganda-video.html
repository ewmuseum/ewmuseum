Date: Now Note: I wrote this rp three hours ago and I only realised just now that I had forgotten to post it. ::Our scene opens up in the CCW Enterprises building in New York. Our first shot is of an elevator door, with two thriving pot plants either side of it. Above the door is the numbers one through five, with the number '1' currently highlighted with a red light. Suddenly the light behind the number goes out and reappears behind the number '2', shortly after it changes and goes to '3'. As it hits '4' we hear a short 'ping' and the elevator doors open revealing Corey Williams dressed in a suit and tie, his arms folded behind his back. Once the doors have opened to their full extent Williams walks out casually and heads for a large glass door. Once he reaches it he surveys the words written upon the door; 'Production.' Corey smiles and nods before opening the door and stepping through. The room is not your standard office building; there are no cubicles. Instead the room is lined with various computers and TV screens, including a large projection screen on the wall furthest from the door. In the centre of the room is a large meeting table which contains a projector as well as various papers. Sitting on swivel chairs at the computers and TV screens are employees of Corey Williams' company known as CCW Enterprises. Williams walks up to one particular employee and stands behind him.:: Corey: Well hello there. ::The chair swivels around to reveal the employee as none other than Corey's butler, Charles. Charles is dressed, as always, in a tuxedo and is wearing a headset carefully so as not to wreck his perfectly combed blonde hair.:: Charles: Ah, Mr. Williams, good to see you, as always. Corey: Is it done? Charles: Yes, of course. Have I ever been known to fail you? Corey: Well boot it up and gimme a look-see. ::Charles swivels back towards a nearby computer and manipulates it with a few mouse clicks. He suddenly turns towards the large projection screen and looks at it expectantly. Williams points towards the screen also and raises his eyebrows slightly, on his face he wears an inquisitive expression, as if to ask 'am I looking here?' Charles simply nods and Corey too looks to the screen. Our camera focuses in on the screen as a video seems to start up.:: ~!The video starts with a black screen and a background music which starts slowly and begins to pick up momentum. The first thing we see is the word 'Corey Williams' which slowly fades out. Narrator's Voice: Corey Williams, an open-minded man whose goals include economic reform and helping the homeless. The screen switches to show a small picture William's handing a homeless person a five dollar bill, whilst smiling to the camera of course. From that photo we switch to the words 'John Crichton' in white, which slowly begin to fade out. Narrator's Voice: John Crichton, an angry and frustrated man who believes in violence and oppression of rights. This time we cut to a photo of John Crichton, however it is not a very flattering photo. Crichton’s face is contorted into an odd shape, eyes closed and nose scrunched up as if smelling a foul aroma, and judging by the fact that his mouth is wide open it seems that this photo was taken of John right in mid-speech. Boom! In simulation with the drum beat a picture of Williams walking along the beach is shown before quickly fading out. Narrator's Voice: He enjoys walks along the beach. Boom! The same out of context picture is shown of John Crichton is displayed as the drum beat hits, this time it fades much quicker though. Narrator's Voice: He wants to slaughter our precious marine animals. Boom! We see a picture of Corey Williams holding a baby in his arms with a fake smile upon his face. Narrator's Voice: He is a great lover of children. Boom! Back to the John Crichton shot again. Narrator's Voice: It is rumored that he has an illegitimate daughter, whom he sent so-called 'hush money' to in order to keep her quiet. The background music changes slightly, it has a more eerie presence now. Staying at the same picture of Crichton, the screen begins to zoom in on it whilst the narrator talks. Narrator's Voice: John Crichton? Can we really trust John Crichton? In fact, how much do we even know about him? He has been in our great organization PCW for a few months now, but still he is a mystery. Do you know that during his entire tenure here at PCW John Crichton has YET to deny that he is a member of the Al Quaeda network!? John Crichton? Or perhaps Osama Bin Crichton, as he may be better known as among his comrades. Can we really trust this man? BOOM! The zooming is stopped and a large black question mark in placed over John Crichton’s face. From that shot we cut to the word's 'Corey Williams.' Narrator's Voice: Corey Williams, the future of this company and this industry. A man who in the past has rid us of evils such as Kremmen Von Smackdown, Badge, Timo Bolamba and more recently Jon Kano. We cut back to the words 'John Crichton.' Narrator's Voice: John Crichton, oppressor, child abuser and member of the Al Quaeda network. We cut to the word 'Williams' Narrator's Voice: Williams..... Now to the word 'Crichton' Narrator's Voice: ....Crichton. And finally to the words 'Corey Williams Vs John Crichton. Tuesday Turmoil.' Narrator's Voice: Williams versus Crichton, live on Tuesday Turmoil............ (Narrator's voice changes to an almost sinister tone)…… Better the devil you know. Our last screen to cut to is a bunch of small print, Charles the butler's own voice kicks it. Charles: OrganisedbyCCWEnterprisesNewYorkinaCoreyWilliamsproduction. Warning,rumoursmayandmostcertainlyarefalseandwereservetherighttomakeupandshitwewanttogetyoutobelieveinwhatwearesaying. ThankyouassholesIcancallyouassholesforyoucan'tevenhearwhatIamsayingbecauseIamtalkingsodamnfast.!~ ::As we leave the projection screen and return to the reality around us we see Williams with an extremely satisfied smile on his face.:: Corey: Great work Charlie m'boy, great work indeed. Now I want you to take this tape down to NBC and make sure they show it once every hour from here on until Turmoil. ::Charles nods and flicks his hair back. He swivels back towards the computer and after a few clicks turns his chair once again, this time towards a nearby VCR which has begun to make some strange noises. Charles waits as the VCR does whatever the hell it's meant to do, his eyes wondering around as he begins to absent-mindedly drum his fingers on the table. However once his eyes meet Corey Williams' he receives a strong glare which quickly shuts him up.:: Charles: Ah! It's done. ::Charles ejects a video from the VCR holds it up for Corey to see.:: Charles: And now it's ready to go. Corey: Impressive. ::Charles makes for the door.:: Charles: I'll just go get this over to NBC. Corey: Wait a minute. ::As Charles passes Williams, Williams sticks out a hand and grabs Charles by the cuff. The very cuff of the arm that is holding the tape. With his free hand Williams reaches into his pants' pocket and pulls out a golden ring. He lets go off Charles cuff and places the ring on his finger, admiring it from every angle.:: Corey: I've been meaning to try this... ::Williams holds up the ring on his hand, which we can see has the CCW Enterprises logo on it (that being a star with the letters 'CCW' written inside it. Williams slowly brings the ring down towards the videotape and presses it against the top of the casing. After pushing fairly hard for a short while Corey pulls his ringed hand back towards his body and surveys the video tape.... nothing has happened.:: Corey: Damnit! Those bastards in marketing said I could have my own goddamn seal! Charles: Uh sir.... Corey: 'Just press it against something and hey presto!' they said! Charles: Sir.... Corey: What a bunch of goddamn fucking bullsh-- Charles: Sir! ::Corey pauses for a minute, to both calm down and to catch his breath after his long-winded rant.:: Corey: What is it? Charles: Well sir, I'm no expert or anything but I'm pretty sure you need wax. ::At first Williams is confused.:: Corey: Wax? ::It dawns on him.:: Corey: Oh! Of course! Wax! ::Williams breaks into small laughter.:: Corey: How could I forget the wax? What an idiot I am, aren't I? ::Charles looks at Williams suspiciously, Corey eyes Charles back, putting him under even more pressure.:: Charles (nervously): Uh... I-uh, I mean I'm.... err... I d-- ::Williams doesn't wait for Charles to finish.:: Corey: OF COURSE I'M NOT A FRICKING IDIOT! I'M THE FUTURE OF THIS INDUSTRY AND I ENDED JON KANO'S GODDAMN CAREER FOR FUCK'S SAKE! NOW GO AND GET ME SOME FUCKING WAX BEFORE I HAVE TO FUCKING PUNCH ME SEAL ONTO YOUR FUCKING BALLS! Charles: Y-y-yes sir! ::Charles scrambles off in search of wax. The pretence that Williams had gained some sort of feeling of affection for his new butler, that they had made a bond and started a friendship, is shattered in an instant. Corey Williams does indeed have no friends, only associates and business partners. The scene fades out.:: !The End!
