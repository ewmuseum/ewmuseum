Assassination Series Part Five: Sound of the Underground...(Not I repeat not a Girls Aloud Parody)

23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/05/03 The dimly lit underground platform was unusually empty. The two members of Justice Inc had just missed a ride and were impatiently waiting for the next one to arrive. After fifteen minutes of bothering him, Warrior had finally talked Blind Rage around to his way of thinking. It was logical after all. What use would they be, in taking out Bullseye, thereby saving their friends if they were too tired to fight? It was counter-productive to say the least. Warrior didn’t know whether Blind Rage had come around to see his point of view, or whether he was feeling a little tired himself. He probably would never know. They stood, just barely behind the yellow waiting line. Blind Rage leant against the study pillar, tapping his left foot impatiently. Warrior was stood just in front of his mammoth team mate, keeping his eye on the crazy old woman who had been talking to an old, empty can of sliced carrots for the last five minutes or so. Probably, even before that. “This isn’t a good idea.” Blind Rage declared, completely out of the blue. “I know. That old woman seems to think the can is her son. We shoulda just got the bus.” Warrior replied, still eyeing the old woman up. “No you idiot. We should have just carried on running. It’s easy to find a stationary target, but a moving one is much harder to find.” “Not necessarily.” “What do you mean?” “It is better to handle all of the other problems now, leaving us focused on the primary target.” “Elaborate.” “Well if we never stopped, all of the hired hands would eventually catch up to us at the warehouse during the fight with Bullseye and it probably wouldn’t have gone well for us.” “I see your point, but there is always a way out of such situations.” Warrior considered the point for a moment and reached a very valid conclusion: he didn’t know what the hell Blind Rage was yapping on about. “What in the name of Greek buggery…are you talkin’ about?” Blind Rage sighed. He hated it when Warrior showed his brainless nature in public. “It’s called tactics.” “No, it’s called bollocks!” “If we went to Bullseye to try and release our comrades and then other assassins showed up wanting a piece of the bounty, do you think he’d just let them come and take it?” Warrior considered Blind Rage’s words again. Now he understood. “Ah…now I see.” “Finally…” Blind Rage exclaimed. “Shame it’s too late,” the voice bellowed. It came from behind the two heroes that was for certain. The tone and the dramatic pronunciation suggested that it came from some kind of super villain. Blind Rage and Warrior slowly, but in synchronicity to see the very formidable vigour of the mighty Doctor Octopus stood there. Blind Rage noticed that the mighty super villain had positioned himself between them and the exit. Essentially there was no option but to fight. Warrior discretely fingered his laser axes in anticipation, whilst Blind Rage’s mind tactically planned a few manoeuvres ahead. A fight was inevitable that was a certainty. Who would come out on top? That was still up in the air. Technically the good Doctor was only one man, but his four extremely tough metallic arms seriously out numbered the heroic duo. The tension built up to almost phenomenal levels, this was the intensely formidable super villain that the incredibly powerful Spider-Man barely was able to put down. This was seriously brown trousers time. “Well if it isn’t the defiant Justice Inc duo…” Doctor Octopus bellowed. “Are you aware just how difficult it was to track you down?” Blind Rage sighed. “Why with all the drama? Why can’t we just get down to fighting so we can get this thing over with now?” Doctor Octopus seemed to mull this over in his mind. “Yes…why not?” In a blink of an eye two of his metallic arms shot out and roughly took Blind Rage by the shoulders and effortlessly lifted him up into the air. Doc Oc then slammed the mammoth superhero into the side of a concrete pillar, denting the formidable surface a great deal. At the same time, Doc Oc’s other two arms shot out for Warrior. But he was ready. Using his agility, he dived forward through the air, dodging the first arm effortlessly. He then performed a forward roll, finishing it by leaping up into the air, thereby avoiding the second arm. There was no tactical planning of each movement, he was relying upon instinct, pure and simple. Whilst leaping into the air he extracted his two dormant laser axes from the side holster, just before performing a smooth summersault. He hit a concrete pillar feet first, almost in a dropkick-like motion, then pushed himself away from the pillar using the very capable muscles in his legs. Warrior heard the concrete pillar shatter behind him - obviously feeling the full force of one of Doc Oc’s metallic arms. The direction the push-off from the concrete pillar propelled him directly in the path of Doc Oc, and Warrior was well aware that the nefarious super villain that he noticed his acrobatic lunge. Warrior flicked the switch on the side of his steel dormant laser axe handles and consequently, green lasers came from the very tips of the steel devices, in an axe shape. Warrior noticed one of the metallic arms shoot out and block his path in a very defensive way. Carelessly, Warrior lunged his laser axe at the metallic arm, effortlessly slicing it in two, consequently causing the good Doctor to scream in agony. The way Warrior heard it, the Doctor had some kind of mental link with his arms. They were a part of him. Because of the slicing of the Doctors arm, Warrior’s direction changed somewhat and he fell like a sack of spuds to the floor. Meanwhile, because of the Doctors pain Blind Rage had been released from his grip and as a resultant made his way towards the super villain, intending to case serious harm. However, before he could reach him, thee good Doctor had regained his bearings and shot two of his arms back at Blind Rage. This time however, the events took a decidedly different turn. Blind Rage stopped the arms just before they made contact with his near indestructible frame. Warrior was faring no better. By the time he had regained his composure after the far sized fall, so had the good Doctor and this time he wasn’t so fortunate. As soon as he stood fully upright, Doc Oc lashed out and the metallic arm struck Warrior clean on the chin. The velocity of such an action propelled him backwards, forcing him to drop both of his laser axes. He landed uncomfortably on the rails. This left a third arm free, which consequently lashed out at Blind Rage, who managed to duck the blow. The metallic arm hit the concrete block and effortlessly shattered it. Only just realising what happened to Warrior, Blind Rage’s anger grew and therefore so did his strength. It was his mutant power, basically the blood flow forced the use of extra adrenaline and thus his muscles were supplied with adequate resources to get the job done. His strength even surprised the evil Doctor Octopus, who felt himself being pushed backwards to the far wall. Effortlessly, Blind Rage threw the metallic arms into the air and rolled towards one of Warrior’s dropped laser axes. He lifted the already active axe up and threw it with incredible accuracy. The axe indented perfectly in the Doctors shoulder, making him squirm somewhat. The Doctor glanced at the blow and an uneasy expression filled his face. He tenderly pulled the laser axe out of his shoulder and threw into the floor. “Well played, hero. Well played.” He said, before retreating up the steps towards the streets above. Blind Rage briefly contemplated following him and bringing him down, but quickly decided against the idea when he remembered Badger Man and Handbag Warrior at the mercy of the mad man. Her heard the distant squeal of the tube train, and quickly remembered his fallen comrade. He quickly rushed to the side of the track and jumped down. He effortlessly hoisted Warrior up onto his shoulders and jumped back up to the platform. This had been one hell of a day and the worst was undoubtedly yet to come.
