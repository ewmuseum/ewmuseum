Back to Montana away from you all.

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/2/02 -Duke digs through boxes looking for something with urgency. Boxes are everywhere and furniture is scattered through out the room. A Punch cigar hangs from his lips as he rips and throws.- Williams: Where the hell is the toilet paper? -One of the movers points to a box that reads bath room. Duke quickly rips up the box and looks inside.- Williams: What the fuck is this?? It’s filled with fucking pictures. Pictures of my greatness. Look at this…HEY VATO look at this…. -Duke gets the attention of the Spanish mover and shows him a picture of the ICWA title around Duke waist.- Williams: That’s what I call gold hoss. You never will reach that level of competition. You will never be more then an eight dollar an hour mover. What a pathetic life isn’t it? Mover: Ah fuck you man at least I can spell Xavier man. Williams: Up your ass it was a typo which this isn’t…..you just got the ax. Get out of here. Mover: Good luck finding your TP puto! Williams: I’m going to buy this moving company and fire you again. I’ll buy your whole family and put them to work in the fields. Send them back to Mexico where you people belong. You prick. -The mover exits the colonial Montana mansion.- Williams: Back home again in big sky company. No dealing with stupid fucking people, ignorant foreigners, or anybody besides nice white farmers. This is God’s Country hoss, which is perfect for a man of my stature. A man who has survived in this business longer then just about anybody else. A men who knows the difference between excellence like the Front Office to low life scum like everybody else. Can you even name any of the presidents of PCW before me? You know why; because they were weak. PCW’S history is full of weak champions like the preacher to guys like the Chef. Nobodies who blew their way to the top on their knees the whole ride. You see I’m a strong man. A man of great wisdom and street smarts who has witnessed it all. I’ll screw you twice before you can even get your prick out to screw me. So don’t try it. -Duke goes back to ripping open boxes.- Williams: How could you low life losers cheer for an alcoholic? Did you really think you were living your dreams through me? Trust me I didn’t mind cashing the checks and having the top T-Shirt sales. Hell I’d even go out there and call you hairy assed hillbillies and you cheered!! You cheered!! Are you people that f*cking dense? Ahhhh what a pudddddddddddddddd. -Duke finds a Chicago Bears football signed by the 1985 team.- Williams: Pharaoh You are proof that God has a sense of humor. Cus you sure as shit don’t. Your about as exciting as a dictionary. You know before I fire you I want to give you a rise so you can lose a better job. Do you really think you can contend with Malice?? Not only Malice but also Front Office Malice. Pure as the snow is white. -Duke finds a box full of records he pulls out a Jerry Jeff Walker album from the late 70’s.- Williams: Great Gonzo’s is a wonderful album and will be played from now on before every PCW show. I know how much you folks love the country music so I’ll play it extra loud. Give you sloth’s some culture. -Duke finds a box full of gold rings and belt buckles.- Williams: Pharaoh you realize there is more value in this box then there is in your whole body? How do compete with perfection? -Duke smirks into the camera.- Williams: You don’t. PCW is going to change like its never changed before. Every drop of water shapes the stone and kiddies the Front Office is the water and you maggots are the stones. -Duke finds his TP and heads to the bathroom as the camera fades.- =w=
