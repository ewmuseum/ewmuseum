Tipping the Pizza Boy!!

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/11/2002 Boda: “Whatever.....listen... I’m here at the Reunion Arena now. Got a match tonight. So, I’ll call you later Rich. Yeah, I know you want to hold a meeting this weekend. I’ll try to be there. I know, as part owner I’m required to be in attendance. But, Rich.... I’m on the road right now. I’m in Dallas. So, yes there is a point. I’ll be in town on Sunday. One week, okay. We can have the meeting next Monday morning. Okay, let me get going. I’m hungry, gonna grab a bite to eat before I get to go out an entertain the masses. Bye Rich.” **As Boda ends his phone conversation, he puts his cell in his jacket pocket, and steps out of a Chevy Suburban. As he steps into the cool Dallas night air, we see he is indeed standing in the rear parking lot of the Reunion Arena in Dallas. We see various trailers with PCW written on them. We see the PCW production trailer right next to the building. As Boda grabs his duffel bag from the back, he reaches for his cell phone again, as he is embraced with a hunger pain. Boda dials three digits** Boda: “Hello, information? Yeah, I need the phone number to Domino’s in Dallas. Yeah, thanks. Can you dial it for me? Thanks......” **Chris walks to the back entrance, holding his cell phone in his right ear, and carrying his duffel bag in his left hand. He knocks on the door with his foot, and waits as a security staff member opens the door for him. He makes Chris wait till he checks his credentials. Meanwhile Boda finally gets through on the phone with Dominos.** Boda: “Yeah, Dominos, I want to order a large pizza, and an order of wings. Yeah, it’s for delivery. To the Reunion Center. Just ask for Chris Sloboda at the security desk around back. Okay. 30 minutes. Okay. Thanks.” **As Boda hangs up the phone, he manages to hand the guard working the desk a photo id. The guard lets Boda pass, and Chris walks into the backstage area of the Reunion Arena.** Boda: “This place hasn’t changed much. Still the same from when I came here to face the Mavs. Well, lets find my locker room.” **Chris walks down the hall, and stops upon seeing a crewmember. Chris asks about the whereabouts of his locker room and he given directions to it. So, off goes the man that will once again regain the World Title in a little over 2 days from now. Making a beeline for his locker room, as to avoid being bothered by anyone, Boda walks past his tag team partner for tonight, as well as D-Kay Calyx. The two unseemingly affected Deuces. Aside from the ultimatum handed to them on Fusion. Red Rock tries to get Boda’s attention, but to no avail, as the former World Champ opens the locker room door, and steps inside, and out of site of the rest of PCW. In the process, he shut the door in the face of Red Rock, who turns to talk to D-Kay** Red Rock: “Man, I don’t know what to do. How am I suppose to wrestle in a tag match with Chris, if he won’t talk to me. What really gets me is I respect and look up to Boda, but lately.... he’s become some on else.” D-Kay: “Well, he lost his title, a title he’s fought for for so long. He thinks it’s because of Mexi. And, he gave us a choice to answer to this week. On top of that, he’s got a rematch with Miya on Turmoil, so he’s got to be focused on that. Give him some space, I’m sure he’s going to come around soon.” Red Rock: “I’m sure. But I just have to ask him something.” D-Kay: “If you must, but I’m out of here before he blows up at us again.” **D-Kay takes off, looking to not enrage Boda. Red Rock on the other hand grabs his set of steel balls, and walks to the locker room door, and knocks on it.** Boda: “I’m coming, let me get my wallet.” **This somewhat confuses Red Rock, but he knocks on the door again. This time, Boda sounds more angered. Finally, the door swings open, and a towel clad Boda stands there looking down at Red Rock.** Boda: “You’re not the pizza boy. What do you want?” Red Rock: “Sorry to bother you, but I wanted to talk about our tag match tonight. I just want to make sure we are on the same page, and that you’ll be there for me.” Boda: “Red.... man, listen. I hate Chris and Corey. I owe them a bunch of ass whoopings for the abuse they delivered on me this week. So don’t worry about it. I’ll be there. Just you and D-Kay better decide who your going with. Me or Mexi. Okay? Now, go get ready. I’m waiting for a pizza to be delivered.” **Before Red can continue, Boda shuts the door in his face. As Red walks away dejected somewhat, we get a shot of the inside of Boda’s locker room. He is not in view at this time, but we do see a TV monitor, a couch and a fruit basket. As we get a closer look on the basket of fruit, we see a note there. Suddenly Boda walks back in, and picks up the note on the basket. He reads it out loud.** Boda: “To Chris... sorry about the way you were cheated on Turmoil. I never wanted to win it this way. That is why I gave you a rematch this week. And, don’t worry, I got something for you after you laid me out on Fusion. Your PCW World Champ, Alexander Miyagawa” **Chris picks up the fruit basket, and stares at it. He talks to it as if the basket were Miya in front of him.** Boda: “So, you want to be cute, huh? Play games, huh? Well, I don’t play games very well. And, I don’t appreciate you trying to cover up for the fact that the only way you could beat me was to have your lackey Darkstar knock me out with a baseball bat. Miya, I don’t buy it for one second, that it was not a plan. And, you can whine all you want about me not eliminating you at the PPV. Fact is this, you goggle wearing freak...... it didn’t matter if you came in first or 20th on Sunday.... I was still the last man in the ring. End of story. Now, if I were you, I’d stop whining, and start worrying about how I’m going to run buckshot on you this coming Turmoil, and regain my title. So, what you going to do when a 7 foot monster tears into you?” **And with that, Boda chucks the fruit basket at the wall. It makes a fruit collage of a mess as the remains slide down the wall, and come to rest on the floor. A knock at the door is heard, and Boda walks to it, opening it to get his pizza.** Boda: “Danny? Are you delivering pizzas?” Danny: “No, you ass. I’m here to say hi.” **Indeed the pizza boy still is not here. Instead, at the door is Dallas Mavericks center Danny Manning. He and Chris shake each other’s hands.** Danny: “Chris, long time no see. I heard you were in town, and I brought my kids to come watch the matches. So, being I have some pull, I came back here to say hi.” Boda: “Well, Danny. Man, it’s nice seeing you again. Tell ya what, let me get you an autograph for the kids. I’m sure as big fans they’d like that.” Danny: “Actually..... my boy is a Miyagawa fan. He thinks you are weak for losing the title so soon.” Boda: “Is that so? Well, tell you what Danny... just remind your boy who was the one that always outscored his father, out rebounded his father, hell, outplayed his old man. ME. BODA. I’m the better athlete. I’m the better champion. And this week.....in two days... tell your boy to watch closely as I win back my title. Now, I got some thing to do, good bye.” **Boda slams the door in Danny Manning’s face. The frustration in his eyes grow larger. Boda starts to pace around the room, when he stops to look at the clock on the wall.** Boda: “33 minutes. Damn he’s late.” **As if by cue, the door is knocked upon. Boda walks to it, and opens the door. It is finally the pizza boy, with the pizza and wings Boda ordered.** Boda: “It’s about time. I’m starving.” Pizza boy: “Sorry man. Traffic was a bitch. That’ll be $15.45.” Boda: “Excuse me? I don’t think so. I believe the policy is 30 minutes or less and it’s free.” Pizza boy: “What... come on man. It’s not my fault dude.” Boda: “Hey, sometimes in life, you have to play by the rules. Now, give me my pizza and wings for free.” Pizza boy: “Fine. Here.” Boda: “Okay, thanks. Have a good night.” Pizza boy: “What.... no tip?” Boda: “Sorry.... late pizza and wings, means no tip for you. Try to have good night.” **Boda starts to shut the door, so he can go eat his food, when he hears the pizza boy mumble.....** Pizza boy: “Well, you big jerk, I’m glad you lost your title after three days. That was the funniest shit I ever seen.” **Chris yanks the door back open, and grabs the pizza boy by the scruff of his neck, and pulls him into the locker room. The pizza boy tries to scream like a little girl, but nothing can come out of his mouth. Boda shuts his door, and picks the boy up, holding him against the wall at eye level. Boda looks the boy up and down, before settling his glaze right in the kid’s eyes.** Boda: “So.... it’s funny that I fought for 18 months to finally win the big one, only to have it taken away in three days time. Well, sorry if I’m not fucking laughing with you son. Because I don't find IT THE LEAST BIT FUNNY. Matter of fact, I FOUND IT INSULTING, AND DEGRADING. And, you want to know what else I find insulting and degrading? YOU. So, here’s what’s going to happen. I’m going to give you a chance to apologize. If you do, I let you go, no problem. But if you don’t.... I’m kicking your ever-loving ass. Here’s your chance.” **As the pizza boy starts to speak, Boda shoves two slices of the pizza in his mouth, preventing him from apologizing.** Boda: “So, you don’t want to say you’re sorry. That’s okay.... I need a warm up for tonight anyhow.” **Boda sets the boy down, then proceeds to slap him around. Due to the graphic violence, the scene shifts to the outside of Boda’s locker room, where passing crewmembers stop and listen in on the actions going on inside the room. After a few minutes, the door flies open, and the pizza boy comes sliding out into the hallway, face first. The scene fades out to black as we see Boda standing in the doorway eating a slice of pizza** Boda: “You want a tip boy? Bet on Boda to win back the title at Turmoil.”
