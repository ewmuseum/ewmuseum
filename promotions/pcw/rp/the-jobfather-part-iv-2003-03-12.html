The Jobfather - - Part IV

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 March 2003 The roller coaster that is Pranks With Attitude continues to soar and dip, but recently they have had their fair share of twists to contend with. Eclipsing Politically Incorrect’s longest title reign should’ve set up another ambitious year as they venture towards the bench mark of five individual Tag Team Championship runs but number five appears to be the teams unlucky number. Thwarted by the love sick collective of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor opportunities are beginning to fade fast, meaning that the team of Pranks With Attitude may vanish into tag team obscurity alongside names like ‘Blood Brothers’ and ‘Goovil’. And with a dip in form the Main Event for March Madness maybe another case of ‘Chances Wasted’ as the duo hopefully drop their tag team label and accomplish what many deem as the impossible for both Gaz “The Jester” Green and Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, World Heavyweight status. However their preparation is another activity being interrupted upon. Losing a Pranksters Paradise match to a pair of under dogs isn’t a promising start but neither is having your life intruded upon by somebody from ones past. For almost a week now the Jester has been singled out by an unknown entity that has a bitter hatred for The Front Office. Using the Front Offices Entertainment Manager as their own pawn they have dived deep into the pranksters mind and have disrupted his March Madness preparation with a series of underhanded tricks. ‘The hunter has now became the hunted’ With that said this entire incident is about to close. It appears the games have stopped and now both parties are to be acquainted via an orchestrated meeting. The last chapter saw the Jester in possession of a briefcase loaded with Front Office funds, taxed from the workforce he plans to pay-off his tormentors who feel justice is to prevail. ‘Or is it?’ the Jester may very well have a trump card still to play. We open with random flashes of scenery, the flickering of images as the landscape passes by. It was apparent that the cause of this confusion was the Jester’s barely conscious state and the fact that the ominous long black limousine from the restaurants car park had undertaken the journey. Raising his green tailored sleeved arm the Jester patted the back of his head, checking for blood from the clubbing blow he had received he murmurs to himself, “Where, where am I?” Quickly coming to he gazes out of the tinted window to see acres of fields and a large Victorian Manor House looming over the tree line. A utopia of beautiful scenery lay before our host but the tranquillity of the countryside was far from his priorities as he checked to see whether his cargo of loot had been removed during his slumber. “Oh, that doesn’t make sense?” whispered the Front Office Entertainment Manager. “They could’ve cut the chain and taken the money without having to bring me along for the ride!” concluded the Jester as a voice interrupted. “SILENCE~!” The Manor House meanwhile had drawn nearer. Almost a stones throw we could soak in this well designed masterpiece, with it’s beautifully preserved exterior that has been littered with statuettes and its large gaping stain-glassed windows, which allow the over-hanging sun to pass in. Suddenly the vehicle jerked to a halt and the door swung open to unveil the collection of thugs Gaz had already acquainted himself with. Standing on a gravel path before the Manor House Gaz knew too well there wasn’t any escape as the thugs formed a protective circle encasing the Jester within. “This way Mr. Green!” hissed the leather-clad henchman from the restaurant who then proceeded to usher the green tailored prankster into the dwelling. Entering they passed beneath two large oak doors, which led directly into a reception area that individually tunnelled off into a labyrinth of different parts of the house like a series of catacombs. Besides the maze of rooms the elegance of the exterior had seeped inwards as the walls were bathed in oil paintings and the ancient furniture stood pristine, in some cases decorated further with ceramic vases. However there wasn’t anytime to sight see as the hired help bullied the Velvet Trickster up a flight of carpeted stairs and onto the second floor. “So guys… Thanks for the tour but it’s getting late… Would love to stay and…” the sarcasm of Gaz Green fell short as a bristled face bully stepped forward tapping a small wooden club into his open palm. “Well on second thoughts!” said the Pranks With Attitude member half-heartedly. The adventure continued as Gaz Green was taken across the landing still under a watchful eye. The whole scene resembled something from an Al Capone flick or possibly something closely related as the collective walked over to another set of double doors. Parried to one side the Jester stood mouth ajar, his face the personification of shock, his lips twitched as he tried to respond but the words simply wouldn’t materialise when finally through his persistence he spoke. “WHAT THE FUC…” Standing either side of a large leather chair, which was turned away and over looking the outside grounds was two ghosts from the pranksters past. On the left stood the Asian assassin who had a brief spell with Primetime Championship Wrestling, namely Kai and a man who had a much shorter stint but still managed to have a BIG farewell present Alan Ackart. “I know you two couldn’t have orchestrated this so where’s the third stooge, huh?” On queue the leather chair swung hundred and eighty degrees to unveil yet another parody of Primetime Championship Wrestling, indeed a bigger name than the two other jokes but still a dot on the talent radar. The mastermind behind this entire charade turned out to be none other than Jamie Celt, the artist once known as Anton The Kid. “Welcome Mr. Green!!!” said the former NTICW World Champion. “You must be kidding me! I’ve been abducted by a victim of bestiality, a graduate of Callisto Bane’s School Of Copying and Jamie ‘No Show’ Celt?” giggled the Corporate Jester as he tried to fathom the events unfolding around him. “YOU SHOULD NEVER DISRESPECT THE FAMILY~!” retorted Ireland’s Jamie Celt. “Even if it’s a dysfunctional family?” injected the Emerald Menace. “I see you have brought us our redundancy money, money that was taken from us by your beloved Front Office. Now with these funds we shall extract our revenge on Primetime Championship Wrestling by firstly updating our equipment… With Windows XP at my disposal I shall TAKE OVER THE WORLD!!!” Tapping away on a laptop computer Jamie Celt began to show signs of impatience as he twiddled his thumbs and fidgeted about as he awaited for the PCW homepage to load in. “Note to self – Must upgrade from 56k modem to broadband” As the former World Champion concluded he gave off a subtle grunt towards his two henchmen of Kai and Alan Ackart and they began to advance forward. Ushering backwards the Jester caught the slogan imprinted onto the t-shirt of the conman Alan Ackart, ‘Will Do Anything To Get Into A Wrestle Online Fed’. Lunging forward the bitch of Pendragon’s horse took hold of the black leather briefcase, which was still handcuffed to the Jester’s forearm. “Open it Mr. Green!” Removing a key from within his emerald business jacket, Gaz proceeded to unlock the handcuff and almost immediately Kai swiped it from the Jester’s possession. Adjusting it to view the content, Kai opened the briefcase ajar when suddenly a cloud of white smoke erupted from within, causing a blanket of fog, which coincidentally allowed Gaz Green to make his escape. Pivoting on his foot Gaz turned to make escape but unfortunately his efforts were thwarted as he waltzed directly into the mountain of flesh that is Two Ton. Unable to side step the goliath, Gaz cowered before the behemoth that threw himself over the Jester in a pancake as the recipient cried for his life, “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!” Suddenly the scene faded to black, only to re-open within the confines of the original setting of a grim Motel room. Springing to life, the Jester sat up from his slumber in a flash. Wiping away the sweat from atop of his brow Gaz breathed a sigh of relief as he realised that the episode had been nothing more than a dream. Standing to attention in his creased clothes the Lord Of Laughter headed on into the bathroom where he bathed his dirt ridden face in water, eventually gazing into the mirror to conclude, “What a nightmare, the Jester being bullied by Jobbers Inc…” - The End -
