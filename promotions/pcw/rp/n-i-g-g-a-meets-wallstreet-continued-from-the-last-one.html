Date: By Jordan... ah screw that, I&#039;ll put the real date: 07/07/02 When we last left Jordan X Shabazz he, along with stable mates Thug roC and Rush Mason, went into a taxi and they began their drive to the New York Stock Exchange, where Jordan’s group N.I.G.G.A. (Negroes International Growing Group Association) is going to hit the market for the first time ever. Now it won’t be a private family owned business on Shabazz’s behalf. But instead it would be a business that is partly owned by the investors. At first the thought of white people holding stock and partly owning his organization scared Jordan. He just didn’t like it. His organization was one of the things that he used to combat the racism that the white man was spreading around the world. But then again, they were paying him. In his mind he wanted everybody to think that he was running N.I.G.G.A for the black man. They believed he was doing this for the furthering the black man as a whole. Making people believe black people as intelligent, trustworthy, and the complete opposite of the rappers “Bling, bling” “I Pimp Hos and sell drugs” thing. The scene opens up with Jordan X Shabazz, Thug roC, and Rush Mason getting out of the cab that drove them to the street that houses the large building where the NYSE is housed. Jordan is the one picking up the tab on the white taxi driver, as they leave the vehicle the driver shouts something at Jordan. Jordan turns around and walks back to the door of the cab. Jordan X Shabazz: “What the fuck did you just say?” Jordan reaches into the cab and he grabs the taxi cab driver by his t-shirt. The traffic continues around the men as the smoldering heat beats down on Shabazz and crew. Jordan is obviously angry because the taxi driver decided to scream something at him about a tip. The driver is afraid that the much larger professional wrestler is not happy with him, and is now holding a handful of his cheap discount Wal-Mart bought t-shirt. The driver stammers an answer. Cab Driver: “I j-just said that I-I n-need to feed m-my family, you cheap prick.” Jordan opens the passenger side door and sits back in with the cab driver. The cars behind this cab are honking their horns at the car in front of them. Thug roC and Rush Mason look on at Jordan in complete shock. Jordan X Shabazz: “Let me tell you something you white devil, YOUR people held my people down. YOUR people made sure that my people were seen as nothing more than common drug dealers and crack addicts, YOUR people did all of that to my people. And for you to ask for a tip on a job that you already got paid on, that’s just… just…” Jordan smacks the scared cab driver hard in the face and he gets out of the passenger side door. He walks up to Rush Mason and Thug roC that are standing right by the front door of the NYSE headquarters. Thug roC looks at Jordan and he smiles. Thug roC: “Good to see that I’m rubbing off on you.” Jordan X Shabazz nods and they walk into the sea of madness that is the New York Stock Exchange. People are running around trying to organize everything before the trading day begins and the place gets more hectic. The first thing The Uprising notice is the lack of African Americans inside of this working place. Most are Caucasian, Asian, and there are a few Hispanics scattered throughout the place. Jordan doesn’t have any more time to think as a large and powerful person immediately grabs him on his right arm. Jordan, upon instinct, fights it. Jordan X Shabazz: (Shoving) “Hey, get the hell off of me!” When Jordan sees that it’s nothing more than a security guard, he feels amused. They had hired him to take care of Jordan and his two business associates while he was on the premises. The guard leads Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, and Thug roC through the madness. They pass by groups of people comparing numbers and notes, pondering whether they should buy or sell. Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, and Thug RoC walk up a flight of stairs and onto the deck where the bell will be rung and the day of trading will begin. The Uprising all seats on different swivel chairs in front of computers that are usually used for some of the workers to check the plunges or rises of the day. Thug RoC is the first one to decide to use the computer. He goes up into the web address part of the computer and he types in www.roCTV.com, which is his Website, and he begins to check the message board for some the messages that could have possibly been posted by his legions of fans. Rush Mason immediately types in www.VoodooGirlsNude.com and begins getting his jollies off, so to speak, by looking at women of all shapes and sizes naked doing black magic. Jordan X Shabazz immediately goes to search on the search engine known as “Google.” Jordan X Shabazz, being an intelligent African American, types in “famous black heroes” into the search engine. The first thing that Shabazz sees is… Web Images Groups Directory Searched the web for famous black heroes. Results 1 - 10 of about 189,000. Search took 0.04 seconds. Category: Society > Death > Suicide > Notes CNN - OJ Simpson Trial... The pool of prospective jurors in the OJ Simpson civil trial split along racialLines Tuesday, with whites saying Simpson was probably guilty of murder and ... Jordan X Shabazz looks at his screen in shock. He screams over the loud crowd towards Rush Mason and Thug roC. Rush’ hands are already nearing his… uh. Pocket. And Thug RoC is typing a hateful flaming message at the internet miscreants who decided to hack his website and redirect it to www.primetimecw.cjb.net which obviously is known as CLINK~! Through the Internet world. When they don’t hear him again, he screams again. Jordan X Shabazz: “GUYS, GET OVER HERE!!” This time Thug roC and Rush Mason hear Jordan X Shabazz, and they immediately look at the screen where Jordan is pointing. Rush and Thug roC look back at Jordan at the same time, completely confused. Rush Mason: “Hm?” Jordan points to the part of the screen where it says “famous black heroes” and then he points towards OJ Simpson’s name, right besides a website entitled www.whenwillojkillagain.com When Will OJ Kill Again?Top of Form 1Bottom of Form 1We all know it is going to happen again! Rush Mason: “When will John Crichton job again. Try that one.” Thug roC laughs. Thug roC: “Friday night, playa.” Jordan looks at the men, not getting the reason they are laughing. Jordan X Shabazz: “This is what I’m talking about. This is why we are a group. This is what we have to fight against. Instead of having Martin Luther King Jr, Malcolm X, Mohammed Ali, Jordan X Shabazz, you know, somebody who’s done something good for the black community, up here, they have an accused murderer. Who WAS innocent I might add.” Thug roC and Rush Mason look at Jordan weirdly. Jordan took himself WAY too seriously. Jesus Christ… it was just a freakin’ gimmick… or was it? The same husky man as they saw earlier now came up to them again. He motions towards them to follow him up another flight of stairs. Jordan looks over the railing on which the huge bell is hung on. It’s the bell that Jordan is getting ready to ring. He’s angry, inside. The thing about OJ angered him. The fact that he lost to John Crichton at the Pay Per View angered him. The only comforting thing in Jordan’s mind for him, was the fact that he had the opportunity to smash Crichton’s head open on Friday. He had been waiting since the Sunday of the pay Per View. Sure Rush, Thug RoC, and Jordan X Shabazz did a number on him at a restaurant the night before Fusion, but it wasn’t enough. Jordan wanted to pin John Crichton in the middle of the ring, one on one, so he can prove to Crichton that he’s a better wrestler. In Crichton’s mind, he thinks that he’s earned the title. He thinks that he won the title because Jordan couldn’t handle the offensive onslaught that he possessed. But in reality, the only reason John Crichton defeated Jordan X Shabazz for the Television title is because his whore of a girlfriend, Jaden, decided to stick her nose into the match. These are the thoughts that went through Jordan’s head as he neared the bell. He heard the speaker say something, but he wasn’t really listened. All he heard was… “Now the CEO, President, and Owner of N.I.G.G.A, Jordan Andrew Copeland, will ring the opening bell.” Jordan flashed his best fake smile, and he stepped up to the bell. Jordan rang the bell, and the words “Let the trading begin” were heard. Jordan smiled. “Give me all your money, crackas.” Jordan thought to himself. Fin~!
