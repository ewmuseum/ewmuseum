A brief history of Bodaciousness

Date: not entered A brief history of Bodaciousness Written: July 15 [The Cowabunga Connection can be described in one single, solitairy word...BODACIOUS! 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry are argueably two great wrestling technicians glued together for the long haul of tag team greatness. It always wasn't that way as Tommy worked on a gnarly singles career in NTICW, taking down all those that crossed his path. Harry stood beside his, at that time, best friend and was the greatest manager in the history of NTICW. With PCW in need of tag teams the NEW dynamic duo of America approached the PCW big wigs for a chance at tag team stardom] PCW Headquarters March 8, 2002 [The Tubular One circled the beautiful oak desk of a shadowed man. Obviously this man did not want his face to be seen, or else he'd most likely have shed a little light on his face. Tommy looked to have a fire in his eyes as his passion for becoming a PCW superstar was evident. Harry just sat slumped in a black leather chair absolutely stoned out of his mind] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Most excellent head dude, you have to hear us out. Harry and I are a most cohesive unit... [Harry's giggle breaks through Tommy's explanation] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I have a cohesive unti, man.... [Tommy ignores the comment] TTT: ...and we are life long best buddies!! He may have been just a manager but Harry can BODACIOUSLY throw down and kick some bogus butt. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: With my most agile, radical high flying skills and Harry's most FEARSOME power moves we'll not only take this heinous looking tag division to a new level, we'll TEAR IT UP!! Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: We've already got a name. PCW Executive: What is it? TTT: Surf's Up. [The executive sighs] PCW Executive: Change it. TTT: Really Radical Compadres!! PCW Executive: No. TTT: Bodacious Bombing Dudes!! PCW Executive: No. TTT: Gnarly Named Wrestlers!! PCW Executive: Stupid. TTT: Tubular and Far Out. PCW Executive: Getting warmer. TTT: Stoned and Sexy!! PCW Executive: No and No. Harry: COWABUNGA CONNECTION, man!! [The PCW Executive is silent for a few moments and you can see his shadowed head nod] PCW Executive: I like that. Though I'm not enjoying the 'man' at the end. How about Cowabunga Connection? [Tommy shrugs and Harry nods wildly with a very proud smile across his face] [That was the beginning of the greatest tag team alive. They not only tore the tag team scene apart, they ran many of them out of the federation. Fear Factory, which was an evil scientist and a creepy, most heinous smelly guy. They didn't last long as the surfer and stoner tore through them like a Ginsu knife! Misplaced was next on the list, making the Conspiracy geek and the Cricket player pay for not being a more 'cohesive' unit like they were. Misplaced returned to PCW after feeling the horrible beatings of the Connection, at least showing they can swallow their bogusness and live with it. Suburban Uprising was a large conquest for the Tubular One mostly. They were two punk skateboarders that looked down on surfing as a wimpy extreme sport. Tommy took acception and made it a mission to prove to PCW and THE WORLD that surfing was totally bodacious in a most far out way. Suburban Uprising didn't even last through the first series of matches. The Connection defeated the skateboarders and showed the world that Surfing was an extreme sport to be reckoned with] Tommy's Private Beach April 14, 2002 [The evening is fairly warm for this time of year. The regulars have had enough of catching rays and riding most gnarly waves. They have gone to enjoy the LA night life and hopefully get a bodacious hook up or two. Tommy sits alone as the sun begins setting over the horizon. His blue surf board with the yellow stripe down the center sits beside him like a pet would to it's master. Tommy's bright blonde hair is slicked back from the ocean water pouring down from above him as he caught some ripin' waves in the late afternoon] [As Tommy is about to leave his spot on the beach a very, very FAT woman in a two piece bikini approaches the most, and only, tubular dude on the beach] Fat Woman In Two Piece: HI!! I noticed that you were 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. [Tommy forces a smile, looking past her to something more interesting...like a large mossy rock] TTT: That's me, babe. Fat woman In Two Piece: Can I have your autograph. [Tommy shrugs] TTT: Sure. [She giggles wildly as her large jellyrolls, which are hanging over each other and over her bikini bottom, bounce back and forth. She is standing there with sort of a sly smile on her face. Tommy is waiting for her to produce some sort of notepad and pen, but she is just standing there with a red marker in her hand] Fat Woman In Two Piece: Well? TTT: I need something to write on, babe. [She then slightly lets down her bikini top to show the top of her fat, jello-like breast. Tommy begins choking as he throws up all over his own beach] TTT: BOGUS!! Your fat!! GET OFF MY BEACH FATTY!! [The woman begins crying as she begins running away, which isn't at all a good sight. She then trips and falls flat on her face looking like a beached whale. Harry, wearing his Twisted Sister T-shirt and black leather pants walks up to her and pokes her with a giant stick] Harry: We're gonna have good eatin' tonight, man!! [The Connection has been screwed many times out of rightfully becoming PCW Tag Team Champions. It was against Politically Incorrect that Xavier Kannon, a man whom the Connection looked up to, interfered to help PI. At that time PI were learning about Scientology and becoming quite tight with Xavier Kannon. The Connection vowed revenge against Kannon and PI, but were never given the chance as Executive politics pushed the Connection down and The Heartbreakers up into their tag team title shot] Jo Jo's Diner, Los Angeles, CA Early May [The diner is packed to the roof just about. The interior is a reminder of a time that is long passed, the 50's. The floors are checkered black and white with an old styled jukebox in the corner playing all the oldie hits. The diner is usually populated by many middle aged people and old timers that try to relive their pasts and bring back something that can never be brought back again. The Tubular One and The Stoned One frequent the place because all the waitresses are total babes and dress in really tight mini-skirts. Always bodacious for a quick peek, and sometimes a squeeze, but usually Harry gets slapped in the face or kicked in the sack] TTT: Pink sucks, compadre. Harry: I once had a dream that Dumbo was pink, man. [Tommy seems apalled by this comment] TTT: DUDE! Harry: I'm sorry, man. But it just popped in my head and Dumbo turned pink. TTT: Those pink dudes stole our shots and our titles. Harry: What pink dudes, man? TTT: Those bogus Heartbreakers!! [Harry shrugs] TTT: Johnny Heart and Jack Valentine. The two heinous compadres that dress in pink and look TOTALLY GAY. [Harry shrugs again] TTT: DUDE...I HATE PINK!! [Tommy grabs one of the waitresses pink outfits and pulls down hard. The entire top and skirt rip off into his hand leaving the very attractive waitress standing in her bra and panties...which happen to be pink as well. Harry smiles brightly] Harry: I HATE PINK TOO, MAN!!! [Harry goes to rip off the rest but is met with a swift kick to the crotch] Harry: Not far out...not far out at all, man. [With the new restart and the acquiring of many new tag teams, the division has grown into a large melting pot of competition. The Connection has stood tall as two of the most fearsome dudes in the division, showing their true dedication to the division. While many tag teams have bailed and quit, the Connection has stayed strong and fought through the politics, the pink, the evil scientists, the geeks, the cricket players, the skateboarders....and NOW...TERRORISTS?!?! TOTALLY BOGUS!!!] [The Connection is not going to go quietly into the waves, not even close. They may not have held the tag team titles on many occasions, or even on occassion, but it remains to be seen that the Cowabunga Connection keeps on winning key matches at key moments and showing PCW that they are here to stay. That the Cowabunga Connection are TOTALLY BODACIOUS IN A FAR OUT WAY!! So bring on the International Terrorists because it'll just be another category of bogusness to add to their trophy room] Harry: YEAH, MAN!!! [Fade to Black]
