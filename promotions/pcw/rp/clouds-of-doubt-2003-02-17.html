Clouds of Doubt

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/03 As the snowflakes trickled down outside the window I stared out the window wondering how much longer this horrible weather would last. It seemed that as of late I had been having the worst luck with just about everything from my career, to my health, and now the weather around me. My luck could not sink much lower but I knew inside my mind that things would start to rise once again soon. With each second of the day passing it came closer to the moment that Chris Storm and I would be bonded forever through a pair of rings, but I could not help but worry. Sure we were engaged, sure we had been through hell and back together, but what if he decided that at the altar that day that I was not the one. What if this was all a joke to him? I'd never thought about it but I had all the time in the world to think about it today considering my body had fallen victim to the flu and I was not going anywhere anytime soon. Gazing at the snow reminded me of all the fun times Chris and I got to spend together both alone and with the kids this winter but what about the season before? In the fall we had just met and it did not exactly start off on the right foot. In fact for me it did not start on me feet at all. I spent the first time we met flat on my back lying unconsciously in the splinters of a broken table after being power bombed by Chris in his attempts to get under Erik Erratic's skin. Boy had the tables turned. Now it was Chris and I against Erik but it had never really occurred to me why this sudden change of heart had occurred. How could you go from hating someone so deeply that you would go out of your way week in and week out to make sure they would be put through hell to loving that person so much that you would go to any length to protect this person from any sort of harm. It made no sense. I will be the first to admit that I had loved Chris ever sense I had met him. I was a fool in love. But what about him? I thought back to early October and one vision came to my mind. In the middle of the WIW ring I stood staring at a massive strawberry flavored cake. On the cake the words 'To the greatest tag team partner: Tessa Windsor' were written in pink icing. I stared at the cake as my blue eyes glittered in the light of flashing cameras. Beside me stood the manager of Chris Storm, Arizona. He knew what I didn’t know. He knew exactly what would pop out of that cake and what would happen afterwards as I stood there totally ignorant to the events that would occur. Instead all I could do is stare at the cake before me as I notice the cake’s top layer raise higher and higher up into the sky until out of the cake just like a true chip n’ dales male popped Chris Storm. Of course in my innocent mind I saw everything changing for the better here, but what happened? Of course things couldn’t go right for me. What happened? I got my head smashed into the top layer of the cake. Some sign of affection. Yet for some reason when Chris tells me now that he loves me I believe him. Why is that? How do I know this couldn’t be another one of his many mind games that ended revolved around me being his victim, just like him trying to get into Erik Erratic’s mind, and just like him actually leading me on to believe that the cake he made that day was actually made as a present for me. It seemed likely and I hoped for my sake that this would not be the case. Rolling over in bed and lying on my other side I looked at the picture that sat on the dresser top and felt a sudden calmness overcome me. In the picture were the children, Chris, and I. It was a family portrait that we had taken not too long ago. His love couldn’t possibly be a hoax. Not after all the trouble we had been through together. From the disappearance of Christa to losing the PCW Tag Titles together and getting the hell beaten out of us. It had been a long ride and yet still he remained true, how on earth could I possibly question his loyalty to me yet still I found doubt in my heart. But why? But just as I began to ponder that one name came to my mind. El Romantico. His constant mind games as of later had me wondering about the married life. Supposedly when you got married it was to be to the person that was the love of your. The person that you would be with for eternity. But would that hold true for Chris and I? I had my doubts the more I analyzed it. Chris too was like El Romantico at one time, a one night stand type of man. It made me wonder how he could suddenly find the supposed love of his life in me out of nowhere and how did it all start in the first place? I was clueless as to why he had a sudden urge to change his life all for me, or if that was even a case. For all I knew this could be some sick mind game that focused on taking the innocence of yet another girl who happened to me and then moving on once again. I sighed with all these thoughts being far too complicated for me to think about being as sick as I was. I did the best that I could to block the thoughts from my mind by deep inside my heart I could not help but wonder, once the big day came, would I be overcome by these doubts and make the biggest mistake of my life in saying no, or would I say yes and make an even bigger mistake in becoming another victim of Chris’ mind games and living proof that Romantico’s thoughts were right, or would we just go on and live happily ever after in a fairy tail life?
