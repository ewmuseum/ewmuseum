Title: Continuations Featuring: Tim Walden Date: October 25, 2002 The scene opens upon the reception area of an office structure, as the 20-something blond receptionist puts down a phone, looking up past the desk at something while the camera pans away from him to show the line of chairs across from him, mostly empty but for a person sitting nearby the door, youthful with dark brown hair and casually dressed with a black duffel bag resting on his feet, though facing downward and hiding the identity somewhat, looking through a magazine it appears.. 'Mr. Calver?' Byron Calver, WrestlingTattler's most irregularly appearing reporter peeks his head up, looking back across the room at the receptionist with an enthused expression... Byron: Yeszzies? Receptionist: Mr. Walden will see you now.. Byron: All right then, thanks for telling me! Calver gets up from his chair, slinging the duffel bag to the side as he places his arm through the shoulder strap, turning with a mild sashay as he begins walking down the hallway with a step that is somewhere between a confident strut and something from the Ministry of Silly Walks....Suddenly, Byron stops, putting his hand to his chin...He turns around, befuddled. Byron: Er, where is Tim's office again anyway? The receptionist stands up, exasperated as Byron steps back toward him, but raises his hand to cut him off.. Receptionist: I told you, it's the one with the large gold nameplate on the door.... Calver slaps himself in the forehead before turning around and walking back down the hall, a sigh head turning back and forth as he looks at each door he passes in turn.. Byron: Here we are! Byron spins around to face the door, before turning the knob and entering, as we see Tim Walden sitting at his desk, pushing a stack of paperwork to the side and straightening up his casual business attire of a dark green golf shirt and a pair of blue jeans..Tim looks rather subdued today, the curtains closed on his picture window letting precious little light through on him. Tim: Take a seat, Byron... The tension in his voice is evident, considering the manner in which their last meeting ended prematurely...Byron does as is requested, pulling a wooden armchair from the side of the room.. Tim: I've got a flight to London to catch in about an hour and a half, so we'll have to make this interview short..I don't want to be stuck somewhere in Azerbaijan waiting for a replacement connecting flight during Fusion. Byron: Fine by me..So let's start right now, okay? Tim: Sure. What would you like to know? Byron thinks it over for a second, then leans back, notebook in hand. Byron: Firstly, what's your reaction to the recent events with the Hardcore Isoceles Trapezoid and his so-called '10/18 attacks'? Tim: I have to say, I'm rather disappointed in him...I wouldn't have minded having him in PCW for a couple of matches if he would just have been fair and taken his taxes like everybody else, instead he goes around sneaking backstage and declaring himself the man who's going to stop the Front Office's 'reign of terror' without having the guts to show his damn face..Then, when he finally does something of any value beyond beating up referees and having his students give me tokens of his lack of esteem, he decides to damn near kill an innocent intern, then brutalize our Personnel Director Miss Wilson for a contract! He is a cancer, a blight on the federation with his immature antics, and I'm going to have to excise him.. Byron: How do you expect to accomplish this when you couldn't manage to beat him in that two-on-one situation last Friday in the Grandberry Mall? Tim seethes for a second, as in his eyes memories of the defeat almost seem to play back, his face seeming less awake with energy as it goes on...He blinks, and seeing Byron taking notes, he shakes his head quickly. Tim: Oh...Damn caffeine. Now anyway, about the Mall Brawl. That was nothing but an absolute fluke at best! He didn't have the guts needed to finish me off, he used a damn schoolboy! A FREAKIN' SCHOOLBOY, FOR HUBBARD'S SAKE! IT'S NOT EVEN A REAL MOVE! THAT NEVER WINS MATCHES!....He had to have had his legs on a shelf, or a handful of tights, it's just not right that I didn't kick out of that! Well let me tell you something, Mr. I-Can't-Wrestle-And-Only-Get-These-Interviews-Because-The-Editors-Pity-Me, and let me tell the HIT something himself. I know I should've beaten you there, you didn't earn your victory in the least..You fought with cowardice, kicking me in the groin early on, using a ridiculous roll-up to get the tainted victory....You realized something that I already knew, that you couldn't fairly win in this sort of match with two opponents whose first priority was revenge! You chickened out, and I'm sure you'll do it again tonight in fact...You'll probably try and keep Rent-A-Twit in the ring for most of the match, taking advantage of that loser's trust and only getting involved when you have a cheap shot to make at me again..But this time, your trickery won't work against me, for with Xavier Kannon by my side, I overcame the Violator himself to get the win, and I can surely secure the win here, as the Front Office cannot be stopped in this case..Merely delayed as we have been, and though you may have escaped the taxes for now, HIT, you know that you will eventually reach the point....Of no evasion. Byron: Sheesh, a little overcompensation for your inner though-- Suddenly a snap is heard. Byron: Hey, my pencil broke! Mind if I borrow one of yourszzies? Tim cocks his head to the side.. Tim: Sure, but there's a 37-dollar graphite tax. Byron: ... Tim: I'd better get going anyway, it's probably a good enough interview as is.. Walden gets up, waving a hand to shoo Byron out of the room as the scene fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
