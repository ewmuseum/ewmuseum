Hunted Saga Part 4: Memory...

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/3/02 The camera opens up on a cold, drizzly day, in the busy streets of Chicago, Illinois. The camera then pans to the left, past the hundreds and hundreds of people hastily walking to wherever their preferred destination is and focuses largely upon a very murky looking alley way, leading into the very bowels of the city. The camera moves forward past all of the people, across the road, to this featured alleyway. The camera continues, entering the alleyway, slowly moving past all of the grim the husky smell of piss surround the place, past all of the over turned garbage cans and past the so many homeless people living rough, in cardboard boxes, on the streets. The camera then slowly grinds to a halt, in front an unmoving body, lying square in the middle of the alleyway, strange even for this part of the city. The light is very bad, but we can make out black designer jeans, a metallic grey T-shirt and an expensive looking pair of boots – not typical attire of a homeless man. The camera cuts and reopens in a position on the other side of the unmoving figure. The camera focuses in and again even though the light in the alleyway is incredibly bad, we can make out the familiar face of John Crichton. We can also see a dried up patch of blood at the top of his head, with visible signs of the stuff all down the right side of his face. Then a spasm seemingly rocks his whole body into one sudden violent movement and Crichton’s eyes slowly and almost reluctantly open. An inner voice, thick with spite, snickered quietly in his head. ‘Embrace the moment’, it whispered. ‘Hang on to the amnesia, because this tiny moment of warmth will be the best thing that will happen to you for the foreseeable future’. His simple curiosity predicted he would not hang onto the warmth of his unconscious state. The scene around him slowly focused, as he pushed himself up into a sitting position. He leant up against the wall and that’s when the pain it him. Rising into this position his head just felt like a lead weight, but now the pain hit him and nearly sent him back into the darkness, the warmth of unconsciousness. He felt up to the pain and drew his arm back down to see the familiar sight of the warm, sticky substance he knew very well. Blood. Then the seriousness of the situation struck him like a sledgehammer. He had no actual recollection of…anything. He didn’t remember where he was, his name and more importantly who he was. He couldn’t remember what had happened to him either, but he didn’t have to be a private detective to figure out that this head injury of his, was probably the reason for his long-term memory loss. With no memory he felt vulnerable, too vulnerable for his liking. His paranoid thoughts, had begun to bother him too. Questions like ‘Did he actually want his memory back?’ ‘Had he done this to himself to avoid reality?’ Crichton pushed these thoughts to the furthest reaches of his mind, it wasn’t a time for speculation only the bare facts of this would be good enough to give him the answers that he so required. It was very clear to him, he would survive for very long, without his memory. The only thing for him to do now was to keep on moving and hope that different scenes stimulate his memory, because his memory sure as hell wasn’t going to come back to him while he was sat on his ass, in what appeared to be some kind of alleyway. Plus the smell of piss was getting to be unbearable and was beginning to send him back to the warmth of unconsciousness. Crichton seemingly using the wall as leverage, pushes himself up to his feet. He decided almost instantly that it wasn’t such a good idea after all, as the sudden rush of blood, sent him incredibly dizzy. Luckily he managed to prop himself up on the wall, preventing him from falling flat on his face. Crichton took a second and then realised what a picture he must paint, dried blood all along the right side of his face. No. He didn’t want to draw unnecessary attention to himself in the vulnerable state, he currently found himself. Crichton looked to his left. The alleyway led to the very busy road of whatever city he was in. That wouldn’t do, too many people would see him. Crichton looked to his right and saw that the alleyway continued further into a whole maze of alleyways. Crichton decided that the undeniably strong stench of piss was preferable than being hassled in his weak state. Crichton began, using the wall to balance himself, he staggered to his right, toward the maze of alleyways that stood before him. When he reached the end of his current alleyway he found himself in a kind of clearing, but it was still enclosed to most of the public, however the was another problem. There were at least three alleyways leading to their respective direction. Which way should he go? . He decided not worry too much about this and left it to his own blind luck to lead him in the correct direction. He decided upon the alleyway nearest to his right. Crichton staggered around the corner and headed toward the alleyway. “Hey. Hey you!” A voice boomed from behind Crichton. Crichton decided that this wasn’t a good thing and prepared himself for the probable trouble ahead. He slowly turned to see three homeless guys, all with sticks about a fifty yards away. “Yeah what can I do for you fella’s?” Crichton questioned The guy in the middle steps forward. “Those are some fancy threads you got there. I think I’ll take them!” The man replied with an evil look forming upon his face. For some reason Crichton had to fight the urge to simply laugh in the guys face pure and simple. Crichton couldn’t understand why though, for this was no laughing matter. He was but a couple of seconds away from getting mugged. However, Crichton’s mind was already made up. His clothes were all that he had of his past and he refused to give them up so easily. Crichton decided that he would fight, even though it would probably result in him spilling even more of his blood. “Listen guys, I don’t think I’m gonna be doing that I…” Crichton trailed off as the homeless man in the middle plunged forward, with very rough plank of wood in hand. Crichton didn’t have time to think however, his instinct and reflexes took over almost immediately, as he side steps the homeless man’s lunge and executes a very sturdy and stiff kidney punch. The homeless man lets out a little squeal, before Crichton follows this up with a forearm across the face, sending him south. Crichton grabs the plank of wood from the floor and looks back at the remaining two homeless guys. “Guys if you would just leave me alone I…” Crichton trailed off once more, pointing the wooden plank in a threatening manner at the homeless guys. However, the homeless guys obviously didn’t take his comments to heart, as they too began to charge at Crichton. Again Crichton stood his ground and prepared himself to let his instincts and reflexes take charge. “Well I suppose they got nothin’ to lose.” Crichton whispered dryly. The homeless guy on Crichton’s left charged first, the plank raised above his head ready to administer a killer blow. Crichton simply strolled forward a little and hit him in the stomach with plank of wood he retrieved from the first homeless man. The second homeless man was now bent over clutching his stomach in agony and Crichton swiftly brought the plank down hard on the homeless guys back, sending him south too. Crichton simply turned and faced the single remaining homeless guy, who had already begun to charge, in an attempt to catch Crichton off guard. The homeless man swung the plank of wood, but Crichton simply moved and as the wood hit the floor, Crichton stepped upon it and in one swift motion kicked the man across the jaw. Crichton began to compose himself and as his blood lust died down, he looked upon the three unconscious bodies lying prone on the floor. He looked at his hands. How the hell did he do that? The way he moved when taking out the homeless men, his actions were smooth, almost fluidic, like had been doing that sort of thing for a while. He figured that he had to have had at least some training in hand to hand combat. Then he realised something, a thought brought on by the imminent danger of the muggers. Crichton checked his pockets, he figured that he might have at least something on his person, which could give him a better idea of who he was. Crichton found nothing in his front two pants pockets, so he tried his back pocket. He carefully extracted the leather object and discovered that it was indeed a wallet. Crichton unhooked the button press and opened it up. There at the very front of wallet was a picture of blinding beauty. A picture of a beautiful black haired woman stared back at him. Crichton subconsciously recognised her, but couldn’t how or why however; he realised that he had very strong feelings for the lady. Scuffling near the entranceway of the alley directly before him broke Crichton’s concentration. Crichton looked up to see another homeless man getting up off of the floor. “Hey what’s all this damn commotion out here…” The homeless man began as both their eyes locked. “Oh god it’s you!” the man exclaimed before turning back the way from which he came and heading off a t high speed. Crichton eyed the man warily, “He seemed as if he…Hey wait! Do you know me?” Crichton shouted as he began to give chase to the homeless man through the alley. As the homeless man turns the corner at the end of the alley, throwing bins to the ground. Crichton not able to stop in time trips over the bins and lands in a pile of garbage that, to Crichton, smelled far worse than the stale piss in the previous alley. Crichton spat out the few pieces of rubbish from his mouth then pushes himself up off the ground and gets back to his feet in a flash. He removes a banana peel from his shoulder than carries on in the direction that the homeless man began. Crichton rounded the corner to see the homeless man backed up against a wall. A dead-end. The homeless man is cowering, almost shaking at the sight of Crichton. “I had to do it! I needed the money. It ain’t easy living out here in the streets.” The homeless stated, still quivering. “How do you know me?” Crichton questioned with a very stern look upon his face. “You mean you didn’t come here looking for me?” The homeless man replied eyeing Crichton warily. “No, but I need to know how you know me.” Crichton repeated, not wanting to give too much information away. “I don’t know you. Damn I’d thought you’d found out.” The homeless guy said with a laugh and began to walk past Crichton. Crichton grabs the man by the wrist and hurls him back up against the wall. “What the hell are you doing?” The homeless man said kind of shocked. “I saw the look on your face when you first saw me. That look told me that you were frightened of me. I need to know whether we’ve met before.” Crichton demanded. He knew this was a risky question, because it gave away a little too much information. The homeless guy could be acting this way because he could be one of the people responsible for his current memory loss. The homeless man eyed Crichton warily and gave a little disturbed expression on his face. “Oh course we haven’t met before, otherwise you’d remember, right?” The homeless man stated, almost patronising Crichton. “Yeah of course I would. How do you know me?” Crichton quickly retorted. The homeless man visibly sighed. “Okay, but you didn’t get this from me, right? The homeless man quickly replied. Crichton simply nodded and waited for the man to carry on. This morning a couple of massive guys, wearing suits was flashing a picture around. It was a picture of you.” The homeless man began. “Go on.” Crichton demanded. “He was asking any homeless guys whether they had seen you. They were offering money for information. About an hour before I told him that I saw you entering your hotel. They thanked me and gave me fifty dollars. That was the last I saw of them.” The homeless man explained. Crichton had a far out look upon his face. Then nodded as he attempted to digest the information that had currently been presented before him. “Okay, that could explain a lot.” Crichton began “I need to know everything you know about what happened, especially after you told them about my position.” Crichton demanded. “You mean OUR position right?” The homeless man ventured. “Huh?” Crichton simply replied. “You weren’t alone. That woman was with you.” The homeless man stated, obviously getting a little suspicious. That would explain the picture of the woman in my wallet Crichton reflected silently. “Of course. Just tell me!” Crichton once again demanded getting a little too impatient. “Okay, Okay, calm down. I don’t anything more about the men, except they looked more like body guards than anything else, and the car that they arrived in had ‘Zemoff Industries’ on the side of it.” The homeless man replied. “Ah…the plot thickens. Yeah thanks man. Appreciated it greatly. One more thing, do you remember what my name is?” Crichton asked. “Yeah…he said your name was…John Crichton…” The homeless man answered. “John Crichton eh? Interesting. Very interesting indeed.” Crichton began. Although to him that name didn’t seem right, didn’t fit him at all. Must have been a phoney name, those thugs used. Yeah that’s it. “And where was this place where I was staying?” Crichton questioned. Crichton decided that he would go to this place first and regroup, try to find anything else that could trigger his memory. It was also a good place to rest and then to find ‘Zemoff Industries’ base of operations. The camera fades to black.
