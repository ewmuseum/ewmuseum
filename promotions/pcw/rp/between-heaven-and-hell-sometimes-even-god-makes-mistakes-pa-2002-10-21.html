Between Heaven and Hell...Sometimes Even God Makes Mistakes...Part 2

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-21-2002 The scene opens back into the pure white office of the man we all know as God, but who insists he is merely Bob. He sits behind his white desk in his white suit and smiles a mischievous grin at the camera that has for some reason found its way through the pearly gates and into Heaven… Bob: Ahh yes. Welcome back, my children! It is truly a sad day when I take to amusing the likes of you folks rather than it being the other way around. You were, after all, created for MY amusement. But, I digress. I guess that I should throw you mortals a bone every now and then just to spice things up. After all, if I don’t take care of you, you may fall victim to my despicable….Enemy, as it is. Do not fret though, we will discuss him directly. Hmm…Now where were we? He thumbs through a leather bound book upon his desk. The brown book looks quite out of place in the whiteness, but elegant none the less…. Bob: Ahh yes. Here we are. Pendragon in Hell. Not a pretty picture, but it does get better. Hell, it has to. The man has caught the eye of God, or Bob. Anyhow, we better get moving. I do tend to get off of the subject anymore. The Lord King had already witnessed his first horror of Hell, but it would surely get worse. As he drew his sword and continued his trek through the fiery pits…. ***FLASH*** The scene cuts back to the flaming confines of Hell. Pendragon walks the stone path between walls of flame, sword drawn, face set against the heat of the place. “Good CHRIST! What have I gotten myself into now? Hmm…Perhaps I should not invoke the Lord’s name in vain, even if I am merely thinking it. BAH! I cannot believe this! There must be one down here who can give me some answers….” Just then, as if on Queue, a demonic voice calls out from behind…. HALT! We have orders to take you with us…The boss wants to meet you… Pendragon snarls and turns, Excalibur flashing black in the firelight, and sees what he is up against. A two headed, three legged tripod of a demon leading a three headed demon dog by a thick chain. With one hand, the giant brandishes a huge club, and his two sets of eyes glare pure hate at our hero… Venneman: Then thy boss is a fool! I do not belong and thee knows it. I go only if your…Boss…Rectifies the situation… Head 1: You go because we say you go. Now, get going. Keep moving forward along the path! Head 2: Better listen to him, half-man. Sometimes he can get mean, and when he does, Cerberus here gets a little ancy….. The dog snarls and jerks on its chain. Pendragon snarls right back as he rushes in, his sword a blur in the firelight. He hacks a head from the dog and then an arm from the Tripod. The dog runs off in obvious pain. Evidently nobody fights back down here. Pendragon then drops into a defensive posture waiting for the blow that the giant shall surely strike… Head 2: My pants…You got blood on my pants…You bastard! These are my favorite pants! Damn…My pants! Head 1: Let’s go get em cleaned….Maybe the blood won’t set yet… The creature, in shock, shambles off in search of something with which to clean its bloodied pants. Pendragon merely gapes as he watches it go, completely flabbergasted that it would merely take its beating and leave. Finally, when the creature is out of sight, Pendragon sighs and turns back to the path that he was following. He shakes his head but carries on… “Damn. I know not what that was, but I am surprised to have dealt with it so easily. Surely the demons of hell do not expect one such as I to merely lie down and take what they give me! This is a mistake, and I shall rectify it no matter the cost. I can’t be dead. I have a federation to rule!” Pendragon trudges on until finally he comes to a door. A huge iron door set into the face of an even huger cliff. Pendragon stares up before banging on the door. Nobody answers so he bangs again. Venneman: Open I say! The Lord King Venneman Pendragon demands an audience! “Surely this was the demesne of Lucifer. Surely he would be able to see that Hell’s boundaries are not made to contain a warrior…Surely he could. This can’t be false hope!” Suddenly, the door swings inward on great hinges into an all consuming darkness. HAVE A SEAT Pendragon starts as a beam of light falls upon a stone chair, but quickly shakes off his jumpiness… Venneman: Thanks, but I believe I shall stand… SO BE IT. WHY ARE YO HERE, CRUSADER? Venneman: If I knew, I would not be here. Why am I here? Do I truly belong? Does the great Lucifer, most beautiful of the angels truly want a Crusader of Truth and Light in his midst? Does the infamous Lucifer want the soul of a good man when he knows that Hell cannot contain it? You tell me. I have no answers, only questions. SILENCE, INFIDEL! YOU DO NOT BELONG, BUT DO NOT BELIEVE THAT I HAVE HAD NO HAND IN YOUR LIFE AS OF LATE. I SENT YOU SYKO. I HAD YOU STRIPPED OF YOUR HONOR BY THE HALFLING. YOU DEFEATED HIM TO GAIN IT BACK, BUT I SLAPPED YOU WITH SOMETHING THAT I AM SURE EATS AT YOU…AFTER YOU BEAT HIM, I GAVE HIM THE CONTINENTAL TITLE THAT YOU SO RIGHTLY DESERVE! IT WAS I WHO CLOUDED THE MINDS OF THOSE YOU WOULD CALL “BOSS” INTO LEAVING YOU TO TOIL WITH THE NEVER WERE’S IN THE TELEVISION DIVISION! GREAT MEN ARE TRULY MY FASCINATION. NO, I DO NOT WANT YOUR SOUL, PENDRAGON. I WANT YOU ON EARTH, LIVING HUMILIATED IN YOUR PRECIOUS PCW AS MEN SUCH AS SYKO THE GOBLIN, TIM WALDEN AND “HIT” GET THE ATTENTION THAT YOU SO RICHLY DESERVE. NAY, CRUSADER. I WANT YOU NOT. YOU ENTERTAIN ME WITH YOUR WOES IN LIFE, NOT YOUR ACTIONS IN DEATH… Venneman: Then send me back, bastard! If you fear me so, send me back! IT IS NOT SO EASY AS YOU MIGHT THINK. ONLY THE MAN UPSTAIRS CAN GET YOU BACK INTO THE WORLD, PENDRAGON. AND I FEAR NO MAN. BUT….BOB DOES OWE ME A FAVOR…PERHAPS I CAN GIVE HIM A CALL…AFTER ALL, I AM A FAIR MAN! The voice of evil stops and Pendragon sits stunned. The continental division, seemingly out of his reach, has in fact been heavy on his mind as of late. Did Lucifer speak the truth? Was evil to blame for the lack of respect Pendragon received? Or was it mere chance…An odd coincidence? Venneman: Bah….Why listen to the father of lies? PCW knows where it’s bread is buttered… ***FLASH*** The scene switches back to the office of Bob, only this time he appears to actually be working on some paperwork. The phone rings and he eyes it suspiciously. He picks up the receiver and smiles… Bob: Why Lucifer….How have you been? You don’t need more sunscreen do you? ……… Bob: A problem? Someone you are not supposed to have? Impossible. We both know I make no mistakes. ……… Bob: Hmm….Tell me about it and we’ll see what I can do…. The shot zooms away and we see that the screen had been encompassed by the leather bound book that Bob had been leafing through. He flips a page and smiles into the camera… Bob: Oh yeah. Lucifer was going to owe me for this one. I do have ways of getting what I need. Now I just needed to find out what I was going to do with the Crusader….But that is a story for next time…Until then, good-night my children! >>>TO BE CONTINUED
