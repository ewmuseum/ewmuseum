Something Retro (Part 2)

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 18, 2002 [We return from the commercial for Cocoa Puffs to Gladiator Arena, and an overhead shot of the arena, showing the layout for the next event. Also, some graphics float by the bottom of the screen.] Mike: Welcome back everyone to American Gladiators, our next event is our half-football, half-wrestling event, Breakthrough and Conquer. Larry: The breakthrough portion is essentially a red zone dash. The contender starts at the twenty yard line, and they have to rush in for a touchdown, however, as always, there is a Gladiator in the way to block their path. If the contender gets the TD, they get five points. Mike: Then they enter the conquer ring. All they have to do is get a part of the Gladiator’s body outside the ring within ten seconds and they will receive five points. Larry: This is also a survival event, so whoever is in last place at the end of this event, will be eliminated from the competition. Mike: Up first is “Two Scoops” Wesley Berry, he is in last place right now after taking one for the visiting team, thanks to the head shot by Laser during Assault. We head down to Larry who is on the floor standing by with “Two Scoops” [The camera then goes from the action shots to the floor, to where Wesley Berry, and Larry Csonka stand by.] Larry: Two Scoops…You took an outstanding hit in assault from Laser…You think that’ll wake you up? Wesley: Larry, I have to kick it up to 190 percent, and blow by both Gladiators to stay alive here. Larry: I hope you’ll stay alive, good luck. Mike: Well, Two Scoops needs a good showing here in order to stay alive. And he’s facing the Gladiators Hawk, and Nitro! Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Two Scoops runs in…jukes, jives, pivots, and GETS BY HAWK FOR THE TOUCHDOWN! FIVE POINTS FOR WESLEY BERRY! Larry: He finally got on the board there, Mike, let’s see if he can continue it in the Conquer Ring. Both men are ready to go here… Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Two Scoops goes low on Nitro…he’s got him on the run…and he trips Nitro up, and his arms flew outside the ring! (Whistle Blows) Mike: THEY ARE GIVING THE POINTS TO HIM! [The crowd comes to life as Wesley Berry celebrates his victory.] Larry: Listen to this crowd they are going crazy for Two Scoops! [The camera peers to Hawk.] Hawk: They aren’t getting by me again! [The camera peers over back to the action at hand.] Mike: Next up is Coz Worthington who only has one point thus far after firing one weapon in Assault. Larry: Let’s see if he can add to that here against Hawk and Nitro. Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Coz trying the same thing that Two Scoops did, and Hawk didn’t fall for it this time, as he takes Coz down at the two yard line, No points for Coz here. He enters the conquer ring now against Nitro. Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Coz tries the same thing at Two Scoops tried, and Nitro isn’t having anything of that. Nitro in counter mode here, and with three seconds left…well…it looks like that Coz is going to pack his bags and leave Gladiator arena as the first one eliminated from the competition. (Whistle!) Larry: Yup, that’s it. Coz Worthington is the first to be eliminated. Let’s go down to Mike for comments. [The camera then goes from the action shots to the floor, to where Coz Worthington and Mike Adamle stand by.] Mike: Coz. I have the unfortunate news to tell you that with only six points over the last two events…You have been eliminated. Coz: Well, you win some, you lose some. I’ll tell you, it was fun once again, just like the last time. Mike: Well, I wish you luck in whatever you do, and keep in touch. Ladies and Gentlemen…COZ WORTHINGTON! [The crowd cheers for the valiant effort that Coz put up for the first two events. The camera returns to the action shots.] Larry: Next up is Rico Costantino, WWE Superstar. He is one of the favourites to win the conquer portion of this event, but let’s see how he does in Breakthrough. Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Larry: Rico jogging up…makes a move to the left and Hawk saw it coming…and he gets taken down at the one yard line! No points for Rico! Moving now to the quote-unquote “main event” here, the Conquer ring. Nitro versus Rico…Here we go! Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Larry: Both men putting up a good effort here. Rico making the move and Nitro making the counter move…5 seconds left…RICO WITH A SUMO STYLE SLAP MOVE! TAKES NITRO OUT OF THE RING!! FIVE POINTS! [The crowd makes a cheer again as Rico celebrates with them.] Rico: YEAH! WHOO! Mike: Look at Rico go here! He’s making a lap around Gladiator Arena he’s so ecstatic! Larry: Must be those homosexual drives doing that. Mike: That wasn’t called for! Larry: Funny, I thought it was. Mike: Anyway, moving onto our final competitor here in Breakthrough and Conquer, Eddie Mandrake. We all know of what’s expected of Eddie in this event. Larry: Absolutely, Eddie is expected to get ten points here. Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Eddie sprints for Hawk…that is the wrong move…Hawk picks Eddie up…SPINEBUSTER! Larry: Say what? Mike: Hawk picked Eddie up and dropped him on his tail-bone, in wrestling they call that a spine buster. Eddie may be hurt here, but he’s getting up and headed for the conquer ring, without stopping. Both men are ready to go here… Larry #2: Contender Ready! ...Gladiator Ready! (Blows whistle) Mike: Eddie’s not making any moves, while Nitro is just trying to keep Eddie at bay. Five seconds left on the clock…Eddie sneaks in under Nitro…Waist lock, and… BOOM! (Whistle!) Larry: HOLY SHIT ON A SOCCER BALL! Mike: In pro wrestling…they call that a German suplex, and Nitro has gone limp because of it, but Eddie receives the five points nonetheless. [Eddie salutes to the crowd as they cheer very loudly.] Larry: Nitro may be seriously hurt here, but Eddie doesn’t even seem to care. [Eddie points to Hawk.] Eddie: YOU SEE NITRO?! YOU’RE NEXT BIG BIRD! OH YEAH! YOU ARE FUCKIN’ NEXT! [Larry approaches Eddie.] Larry: Eddie, why do you not care for the welfare for the Gladiators? Eddie: You see what Hawk did to me? If they want to play that rough, I’ll return the favour in kind! BOO YA! Larry: Mike back to you. Mike: Well, after two events…Eddie Mandrake still leads with fifteen points, Rico Costantino and Wesley Berry are tied with ten points, and Coz Worthington, with only six points, is eliminated from the competition. Larry: Coming up Next…SWINGSHOT! Don’t go away! [Fade to commercial]
