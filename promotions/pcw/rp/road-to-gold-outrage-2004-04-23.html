Road To Gold: Outrage

23 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/04 There are three things in the world that can put me to sleep slowly but surely: a good bottle of whiskey, a good woman, and traffic. Most people get pissed off at the site of dozens of cars piled behind each other for miles, but not me. It could be due to the fact that I had never been the type of guy to worry about showing up on time. Just so I showed up early enough to get the job done is what I worried about. I reached down and pulled my can of Coca Cola out of the cup holder that sat between me and the passenger’s seat. I heard it fizz as I pulled it out of its spot, and took a sip to quench my thirst. I put the cup down, and looked ahead at the cars that stretched for about 80 feet, and then in the background at the Detroit-Metro airport. You've never experienced traffic, or a good time to reflect until you've sat in airport traffic. That was my theory anyway, and the place where I got my best thinking done. There wouldn't be much thinking on this trip, however, as I heard a digitized version of Metallica's 'Master of Puppets' echo from my cell phone that laid face down on the passenger seat. I noticed my lips were chapped as I muttered, 'Who could this be.” I lifted the phone off the seat and pulled the antenna out before folding it open. I held the phone up to my ear and answered. 'The Buzz's House of Love, may I help you?' There was no answer for a few moments, just static. I moved my head around to clear the phone, and once again spoke into the phone. 'Hey, is anyone there?' I heard the voice come through and recognized it as the Pimply Faced Intern Kid from PCW. His voice cracked as if he was still in his teens, 'Mr. Roberts? Is that you?' I stared out of the windshield in utter disbelief at the question I had been asked. I shook my head before answering. 'No, it's Duke Williams . . . You called me, dumb ass. Who in the hell do you think it is!?' I heard the young man's voice cracked as he answered, 'Sorry sir . . . I have some bad news to inform you of.' I sighed and shook my head before putting the car in drive to pull forward a half an inch. I put the car back into park before questioning the statement made by the PFIK. 'What do you mean . . . bad news? What now . . . is PCW going to say I have to vacate my seed in the tournament?' 'No, but you are going to have to vacate two thousand dollars into the pocket's of PCW.', I heard the PFIK chuckle somewhat. My voice filled with slight anger as I began to speak. 'You trying to get cute with me, kid? How about I evict my foot from the floor, and move it into your ass!' The sound of fear filled the Pimply Faced Intern Kid's voice as he began to apologize, 'I'm so sorry, sir. I did not mean to offend you in any way. I shook my head, 'Yeah..yeah . . . why are you guys fining me?' There was silence for a few moments. 'Well, that’s the amount of damage caused by a group of unruly midgets that you locked inside of your locker room.” I slammed my fist into the steering wheel and my car horn honked, causing a portly fellow in front of me to poke his head out of the window and yell obscenities. I rolled my window down and poked my head out. 'WHAT DID YOU FIND A PIECE OF CANDY IN YOUR POCKET!? JUST SHUT THE HELL UP, BACON BOY! IT WAS AN ACCIDENT!' The man did nothing but return his head into the car, and roll the window back up. I did the same; having won the argument, and heard the PFIK’s annoying voice still speaking into my ear. 'Mr. Roberts . . . Mr.Roberts are you there?' The PFIK's voice filled my ear and I thought about the midget's Skitzo had sent into my locker room. I started to laugh somewhat before answering. 'You know what man..those weren't even my midgets . . . I mean I don't even like midgets. I hate small people . . . ' 'Well, I am just the messenger. You need to pay when you get to the arena or . . . ', his statement was not completed as I hung the phone up. I shook my head at the nerve of PCW for fining me for one of Skitzo's shenanigans. I had to be mad to even think the word shenanigans. Once again, I popped the car into drive and drove forward. This time I did not stop, but continued to move slowly behind the cars as we pulled past the security station one by one. It seems as though security had pulled aside a car full of men and was searching for drugs. Once inside the airport I drove around looking for the Smith Terminal. What felt like a half hour, but was really only ten minutes, later I found the terminal and pulled into a parking spot. I’d be back in a day so there was no need to park in the secure car drop off area. The rumbling of the exhaust of the Corvette died the instant I turned the ignition off. I brushed a spec of dust off the counsel of the car and then got out. A few minutes later I found myself walking toward the entrance of the Smith Terminal. 'I Gotta' get some sleep . . . ' I approached the doors, which slid in opposite directions and had to side step a couple as I was entering and they were leaving. After readjusting my bag on my shoulder, I looked around for the Spirit Airlines section. Luckily enough it was right in front of me about twenty feet ahead. The line for Spirit only consisted of five others. I placed my sports bag on the ground beside me and gave everyone in line a look over. A Hispanic couple, two business men, and an old lady stood before me. The two business men seemed to be engaged in a lively conversation, most likely about stocks and the like. My attention was caught when I noticed the old lady had turned, and was staring up at me with a glare in her eyes. I raised my eyebrow and wondered what the hell she wanted. 'You need something?', I questioned with an obvious tone of annoyance. The woman reared her hand back and slapped my chest. I moved backwards and she continued to slap. I was surprised at what was happening and brought my arms up to absorb the blows. 'WHAT DO YOU WANT YOU CRAZY OLD FART!?', I yelled as everyone in the airport began to stare. The woman composed herself, and brushed off her flower patterned dress before answering. Her voice cracked and the jowls that hung off her old face shook when she spoke to me, 'I thought you needed to be taught a lesson for locking those children in your locker room last week.” I felt my jaw drop as I registered what the woman had said. 'They weren't even children!...THEY WERE FUCKING MIDGETS! Trust me. Midgets are no reason to get your Depends in a twist.', I yelled with a slight crack in my voice. The woman gasped and brought her hand to her mouth. I saw security begin to move toward us, and felt the hair on the back of my neck stand up. I'd been nervous before big matches before, but when you have airport security approaching you; it's a whole new experience. I had to think quickly, and as they approached I knew what I had to do. 'Sir, is there a problem here?', a female security guard asked me with a stern tone. 'This woman assaulted me, and threatened to kill me . . . I . . . I can't believe the type of people you let in the airport now. I thought the security was better than that . . . I may just have to complain to..', I said with an acting job some horrendous it'd make Hulk Hogan blush. The female guard interrupted me with a hand on my shoulder. 'I've heard all I need to hear' I felt myself grow nervous, and as the guard began to talk once more I hoped for the best. 'Ma'am we are going to have to ask you to come with us!', she spoke loudly and grabbed the woman by the arm. 'Tony, grab her bag . . . we are going to have to search this woman.' The tall guard that stood next to the female guard reached down and grabbed hold of the bag. The old woman's cracked face lit up with fear, and she began to try and fight loose. 'Well, I have never heard such a lie in my life! I did not threaten a soul! Ask everyone around!', the woman's voice was high out of fear. 'Ma'am, save it.', replied the guard who began to drag the woman out of line. The screams of outrage of the old woman could be heard for several seconds and then nothing as she was drug into a room by the two guards. I turned my attention back to the line, and to my surprise no one was even looking any longer. After waiting for another 25 minutes it was my turn to check in. I approached the counter and gave the woman behind the counter a look over. She was very beautiful...beautiful enough for a quickie in the airport bathroom.... 'Why hello beautiful.' My greeting and smile received a reaction, but not the one I was hoping for. Instead I received only a raised eyebrow in return, and harsh retorts. Her pouty lips slowly spelled it out for me while she spoke, 'Sir, give me your ticket, and I will check you in. Then, give me your bag, and I will check it. End of story...save your bullshit for someone else.' 'Well I'll be damned...just calm down honey....I wasn't hitting on an ugly wildebeast like you.', I felt myself smirk as I made my comeback. The woman smiled back at my response as if she knew it couldn't possibly be true. 'If you don't give me your ticket right now...I'm going to call security and suggest they perform an anal cavity search on you.' My eyes grew as wide as saucers and I began to fumble around my pockets for the ticket. I brought the slip of paper out of my back pocket, and handed it to her. 'Didn't have to get all touchy about it...' About ten minutes later I found myself sitting next to the flight entrance with my bag next to my foot. I thumbed through the most recent issue of PCW Magazine. It featured a highlight on the final eight competitors in the Face to Face world title tournament. Not to mention a small blurb of personal opinion on each. I skipped through all of the blurbs except the one that sat underneath a picture of me holding the World Title. I felt the blood rush into my cheeks as I read the blurb. 'While 'The Buzz' may be one of the most technically proficient and charismatic wrestlers to work in PCW in recent memory. He is easily distracted away from his goals, including becoming a two time PCW World Champion. His over abundant ego, and big mouth have caused him trouble in the past. They will definately do so in the future. Probably keeping him from his true potential along the way.' I slammed the magazine back into my open bag, and thought about what I was going to have to do in one day. A huge obstacle stood in my way. An obstacle that I had faced twice before, and come up victorious against. He was beatable in the past. However, this was before he'd be humiliated by being beaten by a cripple. This was before I had beaten him to win my first PCW World Championship. In the present day...Propaganda may be unbeatable. To Be Continued.
