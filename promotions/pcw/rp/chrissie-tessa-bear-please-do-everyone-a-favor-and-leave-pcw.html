Chrissie, Tessa Bear, please do everyone a favor and leave PCW

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th March &#039;03 This promo opens in a scene we’ve become accustomed to over the last few Chet King promos. Chet is sitting on a chair in front of a grey wall staring directly down the barrel of the camera set up in the middle of the room. Chet King: Ugh, what did I ever do to deserve having to put up with Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor? I’m forced to put up with them when I’m at work, and when I watch their promos. Has there ever been a more annoying couple? I can’t think of one. Not even Tim Walden’s secret relationship with the Pimply Faced Intern Kid is as annoying. No one wants to have to suffer through listening to the astoundingly stupid and annoying conversations between Chrissie and Tessa Bear. Actually no one in PCW, nor the fans at home, wants to put up with the individual antic of Chrissie and Tessa Bear, let alone when they’re together, they’re just so annoying. Whenever I see anything involving these two I just cringe in embarrassment for them, and the image that they’re sending casual PCW fans. I mean if I had never watched PCW before, and I turned it on to see what it’s all about and I saw Tessa Bear and Chrissie doing their usual unbelievably stupid routine, my intelligence would be so insulted that I’d never want to watch PCW ever again. Tessa Bear and Chrissie, you’re embarrassments to PCW. I think it’d be appreciated by everyone if you left PCW, and went to some other place like OSW, before you cause PCW to lose any more viewers. Just to prove my point about their frustratingly annoying behaviour, take what I saw when I watched one of Chrissie’s promo a few days ago. Chrissie is trying to do some voodoo trick on a Gaz Green and Nerezza doll, which on a side note is really sad. I mean when you have to use voodoo in an attempt to have a chance at defeating a pair as pathetic as Gaz and Nerezza, then you know you’re struggling. But anyway, Chrissie is trying to do voodoo, and then Tessa Bear comes in and that’s when I started banging my head against my TV. Tessa Bear pretends to be a vampire, biting Chrissie, which apparently she and Chrissie found hilarious. Tessa Bear even said 'NO I R TEREZZA, HEAR ME RAR!!'. If that in itself doesn’t want to make you destroy your TV, then what was to come must of as Chrissie’s feelings got hurt when Tessa Bear insulted his ability to make voodoo dolls. Chrissie then said something, which made me cringe almost as much as Tessa Bears previously mentioned comment, 'Terezza, I am the vampire hunter Chris Storm! Prepared to be slayed!'. This was inevitably followed by Tessa Bear’s annoying voice saying 'Oh nooooooooo, you got meeeee.' While all this stupidity was taking place, Chrissie and Tessa Bear were in hysterics, while the rest of us were left wondering ‘what the fuck?’ Chrissie, Tessa Bear, like I said before, please do us all a favor and leave PCW so we don’t have to sit through any more of crap like that. As soon as I heard that Chrissie Storm had signed with PCW, I knew things weren’t going to be good. After all, despite their being five Storms before Chrissie, there had been no real successful Storm’s out of the lot. Now that I think about it, Chrissie is probably the worst Storm out of them all. I have no doubt ‘Amazing’ Mike Storm, AJ Storm and Jay Storm could of all taken Chrissie. Even someone as terrible as Kory Storm would have been able to take out Chrissie. And the other Storm who’s name has split my mind because he was so bad, would have been able to beat up Chrissie. Chrissie, seeing how no Storm has ever made it big in PCW, being the worst Storm ever to have graced PCW just reflects how truly terrible you are. Tessa Bear, I’ve seen trailer trash whining on Jerry Springer about going out with a cross dressing goat, who are less annoying then you. Everything about you annoys me, from your voice to the fact that you think you have wrestling talent. Tessa Bear, may I give you a suggestion? Keep your mouth shut so no one gets a headache. Remember Tessa Bear, keep your mouth shut and everyone will be happy as no one wants to hear your voice, your laugh or what you have to say. Chrissie and Tessa Bear, please can you consider that favor I’m asking of you? Please can you leave PCW? You don’t want PCW to suffer because people refuse to watch us because of your intelligence insulting behaviour and conversations. Everyone in PCW will be happy. Thankyou to the both of you in advance for listening to my request, on behalf of everyone who hates wanting to destroy their TV after watching the two of you, and leaving PCW.
