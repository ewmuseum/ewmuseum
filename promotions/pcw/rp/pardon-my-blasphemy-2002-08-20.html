Pardon My Blasphemy...

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 Quick OOC Note – It’s time I apologized for my poor performance of late. I’ve been plagued with a little thing called undue laziness, and it’s about time I put a stop to it. I keep letting little distractions get in the way of actually working – with the exception of work and family occasions – of which I’ve had to plough a lot of hours into of late. Again, I apologize and promise to try a little harder for the next month or so. This Roleplay’s pretty poor by my standards, and the fact stands that I’ve been having a fairly rough time mentally…I give to you perhaps the most cynical roleplay I’ve ever written… The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles - Book Five: The Deluded State of C.Chase…… RP#1 for Turmoil (Versus Pharaoh/Akiyama/Duke Williams)… Chapter One: Pardon My Blasphemy... “JESUS FUCKING CHRIST!! – If you’ll pardon my blasphemy…” ::.~(Prologue {as read by Chris Chase})~ ALL HAIL THE KING! –Don’t look at me as if I’m some kind of ridiculously dressed cretin that runs around half naked, spouting out Spartan phrases and generally causing much disturbance to the peace. No, look at me like the frail little idiot like you’ve always done. The GREAT underachiever – the guy who could talk the talk…but lacked the walk, and any utensil that could carry myself across the abyss of success. I suppose I should become one of those self-mutilating morons that depression seems to follow. Maybe I shouldn’t be so quick to leap down my own throat. It’s not MY fault that I’ve been a little lackluster since my return to PCW action, it’s not MY fault that I’ve become that gross underachiever that I’ve always been, again. These people could barely remember me, even after a short period of approximately THREE months. A month and a half in to my return, and I’ve done so much as beat Duke Williams. Hoo boy, that’s something to be proud of, isn’t it? Someone shoot me already, I’ve had enough of my own whining… Tremendous self-pity aside, it’s been yet another uneventful week in the land of ‘Saint’ Chris Chase. I should have got off my own lazy ass a long time ago, the kind of thing that I would of usually been so quick to do, considering that I’ve just been dragged through the bush of eternal obscurity. I’ve been thrown again, into a match with the kind of lowlifes that I used to be naively happy in delivering a gargantuan beating to. It was once second nature to me, a habit that I couldn’t kick until I decided that laziness was more enlightening than taking a stick to a mightily huge jobber. What a mistake. The Pharaoh, Akiyama, Duke Williams. I’ve got the tremendous talent required to beat each and every one of these, my so-called fellow athletes. If that could ever be applied to a trio comprising of one washed-up excuse for Boda-Fodder, one guy that seems to have a more self-mutilating influence on himself than the entire collective of so-called Progressive musicians… And then there’s Titu Xansiv, The Pharaoh. The Egyptian Civilization was past its prime millennia ago and in this modern world ruled by the mighty fist of Computers Titu decides to bring up the whole forgotten philosophies and tendencies of the Egyptian Civilization – before unleashing his hordes on the rogues of a certain Argentine’s personal army… And I thought the whole deportation of Snapple was harebrained… So why is it, when faced with the diversity of my opponents, that I make my way to a semi-religious fair? It’s almost as if I’ve got an inbuilt homing mechanism….:: “JESUS FUCKING CHRIST!! Is it really THAT hard to understand? No, really, is it THAT hard? You know, you people haven’t really gotten what it takes to be religious icons, have you? Look at you and your second rate costumes…even Barney the frickin’ Dinosaur had greater appeal to the enlightened masses!! Jesus Christ…what am I getting myself in for here?” “Err…Mister Chase?” “P-LEASE! Do you seriously know how many people have called me ‘Mister Chase’ in the past month? Forty-Fucking-Five! And that’s COUNTING the twelve Caribou Scouts that tried selling me dried Caribou droppings disguised as Cookies! It’s becoming even more clichéd than frantic outbursts of angry egomaniacs!” ~(Lengthy Pause)~ “Oh all right…I’ve been a extremely intolerant idiot, haven’t I? Let’s get this show on the road!” ::.Phlegm. Yes, Phlegm, Chris Chase has almost choked on the Phlegm that is his own laziness. A frail, yet domineeringly talented being he might well be…but a pragmatic, hardworking laborer of society he isn’t. Ye Olde Christian Fayre isn’t held all that far from Chase’s home of Calgary. Being in the town for little more than a day prior to his clash with three of the cream of the crop from the PCW on Tuesday, the young and impressionable Celestial Cowboy was co-opted into managing the Christian Fayre with much persuasion that involved the promise of yet more bragging rights for the eternal underachiever. Such things have avoided Chris Chase for months now, and it’s time for he to finally catch up with the rest of the world and get ahead of current terms. A job that could well be a greater challenge for Chris Chase than any of his opponents on Turmoil, with no real disrespect to them of course. Being holed up for a period of two weeks has left ‘Saint’ with little more than a sliver of sanity… Yet that never stopped some of the true visionaries of our time…until they either gave up, or died. Far from a visionary, Chris might just be the last living hope for a non-derivative in this world where being born means being born a derivative. Which is blatantly unavoidable due to the fact that most people are derived from their parents and their life-experiences. Being more random than the Uzbekistani Government’s Taxation policies in recent days – Mister Chase, as he’s been frequently dubbed of late, possesses the qualities needed by a true non-derivative… If only he’d avoid the flagrant clichés that he’s so focused on putting an end to….:: “People, People of the Alberta State. Welcome to Ye Olde Christian Fayre. A blatantly foolish act of domineering cliché-abusing Christianity, right on your very doorstops! Yes, my name is Chris Chase, and I’ll be your Captain for the duration of this promisingly tumultuous voyage!” “When did Christianity ever become tumultuous, Chase? Before you began to represent it, or afterwards? Give me a break, you two-bit excuse for a cocky whiner…you can’t save Christianity from your own wrong-doings?” “Do I hear an non-believer? Or someone who fell off the ugly tree, hit several branches, before taking a wrong turn towards the alley of despair?” “Add to that that he’s FUCKING JEWISH!!” “A Jew at a Christian Fayre? Preposterous! And I’ll have you know that I’m so damned vague that I even abide half-heartedly towards both religions…” ::.Enter a scene atop a soap box. A stage in the face of the wide blue yonder, an open exhibit for the religiously inclined to gather for a day of enlightenment and the belief that everything will be alright and that my wife won’t run off with the next-door neighbor. About twenty or so miles into the Canadian Wilderness from Calgary, Alberta…nestling in a forest clearing lies Ye Olde Christian Fayre, devoted to the local practitioners of the religion, an arena of sorts for all manner of divine god-loving activities that would pass a festival day in a particularly pedestrian way. Yet, in this seemingly tranquil environment, the typical yelling of regular city folk can be heard echoing over the conifers as Chris Chase blunders his way into another predicament… And this time, he pretty much deserved the revilement from his fellow spiritual beings. An angry crowd of patrons bustle in front of a wide stage area, on top of which stands a quite unexpressed Chris Chase. Chase isn’t usually this bewildered by the reactions of his peers, and after pushing the followers of an age-old religion over the edge for a second time in the space of eight months. He groans to himself as he realizes that it might just be too late to reconcile the hordes that bay for his retirement to the dressing area. Concern, regret…emotions that Chase hasn’t felt for months, sweep over the former Messiah like a Tsunami – I bet that was a surprise for him… Yanking on the collar of his shirt feverishly, Chris pokes glances to his right and left, gaining some bearings in case he has to make a sharp exit. Rubbing his forehead in a semi-nervous, semi-furious manner not befitting to his character…he plants one hand on the stand in front of him…steadying himself as he ducks an unidentified flying missile, which knocks down a technician after hitting the poor cretin with much force….:: Member of the crowd: So what’s it going to be Chase? Judaism or Christianity? I mean, what’s the difference huh? In one, circumcision’s mandatory…in the other, it’s optional. How about it Chase, have YOU been circumcised? Have you put your manhood ahead of your religious obligations? ::. Another missile is thrown and misses Chris, only to fly straight through a pane of glass belonging to the window of the dressing room for the Dancing Girls troupe. A few faint screams can be heard from the trailer, followed by the sight of a half-naked woman shaking her fist at the pulsating crowd. Ignoring this, Chris shakes his head…his eyebrows falling to a distinct frown as he peers across the seething crowds with a worried glance….:: Chris: Who fucking wouldn’t?! After all, this society is one that looks away from people who spend their Sunday afternoons giving the old One-Two to the odd choirgirl. Pardon my blasphemy, but for the love of God – you people represent the typical religious community. Why is it impossible for you people for embracing the concept of being a non-stereotyped religious crowd by letting loose the dogs on those who are too blatantly religious for their own good? Crowd Member: Because it’s beneath us, you dolt! Do us all a favor and take your Pro-Jewish ass out of our Fayre, and back to Professional Wrestling where you so rightfully belong. Fade into obscurity…we don’t care! Chris: Have it your way…be like that. I don’t particularly care either. In fact, I care so little for this little religious tirade that I’m even considering Islam for crying out loud! ::. And with that, Chris storms off the stage, leaving the crowd with much to ponder for the fifteen or so minutes of the aftermath….:: Exactly ONE day Later… ::. That was Monday, a pressured time for an already mentally fragile Chris Chase. It’s incidents such as these that have been forcing the former Messiah further down that long and winding road leading to the eventual self-depression and other worst-case scenarios that wouldn’t be exactly befitting of our hero. ~(Narration by Chris Chase)~ I didn’t mean to say that I’d practice Islam or any other religion for that matter – it’s even enough to suggest that I’m a false figure of the religion that I supposedly believe in. Everyone forgets that side of me – they see me for the ridiculously arrogant guy that I am, and essentially forget about the man behind the “arrogance”. It’s the time-honored tradition passed down from generation to generation of people throughout this world – even the dog managed to pick it up along the way. This society is becoming stranger by the day, or is that me? You know, it’s becoming clear now. I’m doomed, doomed to spend half my life underneath such ludicrous beings as Akiyama and the Pharaoh…I’m doomed to spend my life beating the living tar out of Duke Williams…and for what? That’s perhaps the most depressing thing of all…that fact that I’m not enough any more. The fact that I’m probably one of the biggest failures in PCW, that I’ve never truly accomplished what I set out to do in the first place. Cry me a fucking river… Nobody buys that kind of crap anyway. Nobody seriously believes in the fairy-tale wonders of being a successful Professional wrestler. Nobody is seriously inspired by the fantasy of the Void – if so, they’d probably spend the majority of their lives caged like the monkeys and other inhuman apes that have gone before them. No-one would be caught watching the tired old frolics of Duke Williams. Yes, indeed…if I’m the most boring wrestler ever…then does that make Duke the Grand Overlord of Tedium? Let’s face it, at least there’s a whole lot of variation in my promotions. I haven’t been INTERVIEWED in FIVE MONTHS. Just because I’m not over, doesn’t mean that I’m boring…right? I just haven’t had enough airtime of late… And there’s the Pharaoh. Strangely enough, I don’t think that Private Armies would be allowed under the Geneva Convention – or was that Thermal Nuclear Armaments? Finally, there’s Chris Chase, or ‘Saint’ Chris Chase. Is this guy for real? Does he really consider himself to be a living saint? Or Messiah? Or whatever it might be this week? Yeah…he’s a right one all right…making out that he’s some eternally devout being when he’s nothing more than an arrogant slacker. I AM ONE WITH THE SLACKER!!.:: This abrupt, cynical promotion was brought to you by C.Chase Enterprises.
