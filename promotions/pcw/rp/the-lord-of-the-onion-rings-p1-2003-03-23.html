The Lord Of The Onion Rings (P1)

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/03/03 (The camera opens up. You can see a bright light. The light is so bright you can’t quite see what it is. The camera zooms in. The camera slowly goes back into focus. Once the camera goes back into focus you can see a banner above a building. It says Big Mouths Burger Bar. The camera goes close up onto a big window. You can see a counter. There is a Spotty Teenager behind it. A phone rings. A Spotty Teenager answers it.) Spotty Teenager: Hello? Quiet fussy voice: Hi, can I order something for pick up? Spotty Teenager: Yes go ahead. Quiet fussy Voice: Okay, I would like 50 Onion rings. Spotty Teenager: Did I hear right? You want 50 Onion rings? Quiet fussy Voice: No I don’t want 50, I want 70! Spotty Teenager: 70 okay, what’s your name sir? (A road washer drives past and makes a loud noise.) Spotty Teenager: Okay, it will be ready in 10 minutes. Quiet fussy Voice: Okay, I will be there. (Ten minutes later. The door to the Eastern Delight opens, a big man walks into the takeaway. He is looking at the ground. You can’t quite see who it is. He looks up you can see who it is. It’s The Overweighter! He walks over to the counter.) The Overweighter: Hi, can I have some onion rings please? Spotty Teenager: Yes, it will be 10 minutes you can wait over there. The Overweighter: Okay thanks (The Overweighter walks over to some seats. He sits down and waits for he’s onion rings to be cooked. He starts to stare outside the window, it’s starting to rain. It’s very heavy, there is water forming a mini river by the curb and it’s rushing towards the drain. The Overweighter is watching the water doing down the drain. Suddenly everything goes white, you can’t see anything. Everything is blank. The blankness starts to change into a cloud. You can see a cloud, along with a mountain top sticking through it. You can feel the suns rays pounding down on you as you begin to sweat. You can smell the heat in the air as you breathe it in through your nose. You suddenly see a big thing come falling in front of you. It’s The Overweighter. He’s falling from the sky. He falls into a jungle and you can see a load of flatten trees from where he landed.) Voice: Sir…? Sir…? Are you okay? (Suddenly The Overweighter wakes up from he’s daydream. The Spotty Teenager behind the counter is shouting at The Overweighter.) Spotty Teenager: Sir, sorry we haven’t got anymore onion rings left. The Overweighter: You what!?!! Spotty Teenager: We have no more onion rings left. The Overweighter: Who took them all? Spotty Teenager: I can’t tell you that. The Overweighter: Tell me! Spotty Teenager: I can’t! The Overweighter: Why? Spotty Teenager: He will hurt me! (The camera zooms in on The Overweighter’s eyes. He’s eyes change shape and starts to make The Overweighter look like his thinking. He goes to say something but he hears a noise in the corner of the takeaway.) Noise: pssst, over here. (The Overweighter looks over his shoulder. He can see an old woman in the corner of the takeaway. She is dressed like a tramp, clothes ripped and filthy. The Overweighter walks over to her.) The Overweighter: What do you want? Old Lady: I can help you. Meet me in the alley in half an hour. (The old lady runs away, leaving The Overweighter standing there confused.) -30 Minutes Later- (The Overweighter walks down the dark alley looking for the old lady. All of a sudden a puff of smoke rises from the floor. You can’t see anything within 5 meters away. An old lady walks through the smoke, she has a huge smile on her face and her teeth are black as a room with no windows. She starts to talk to The Overweighter in a wicked voice.) Old Lady: The one you seek is The Lord of the onion rings! The Overweighter: What??? (Another old lady walks up behind The Overweighter.) Old Lady2: He goes from takeaway to takeaway and takes way every onion ring there is. He has already taken all the rings there is in this city! (The second old lady scares The Overweighter.) The Overweighter: Ah, where did you come from? (A third old lady walks to the side of The Overweighter and puts her hand on he’s arm.) The Overweighter: Ah, another one! Don’t touch me! Old Lady3: He has already drained this country and many others. He has gone back to his home to absorb all of the powers from them. The Overweighter: What is this home thing you speak of? Old Lady: MARVINNN!!! (A spotty teenager, with broken glasses and dribble coming out of his mouth, walks up to the old lady. Marvin says in a geeky voice with spit coming out of his mouth.) Marvin: The definition of ‘Home’ is the place where one lives; the city, etc where one was born or reared. The Overweighter: Where is this home? Old Lady: Marvin you can get lost! Old Lady2: The Lord of the Rings lives on top of a mountain! Old Lady3: There is a jungle at the bottom of this mountain! The Overweighter: Look, I haven’t got much time! I want my onion rings back, just tell me where to go to get them! Old Lady: Okay, India. The Overweighter: Where in India? India’s a big place you know. Old Lady2: Now that would be telling. (The Overweighter is beginning to get frustrated with the old ladies.) The Overweighter: Of course it’s bloody telling! Why the hell did you say you could help me if you’re not going to tell me! Old Lady: Only I said that. Old Lady3: You don’t need to know where about in India. The Overweighter: …? Why? Old Lady: Because you’re there now. (It suddenly goes white, it’s all blank. The Overweighter is walking on a cloud. There is a mountain top sticking through it.) The Overweighter: Hang on this is just like my daydream? (All of a sudden he falls through the cloud. He hits the floor. Luckily for The Overweighter there was a load of trees to break his fall.) Voice: Sir…? Sir…? Are you okay? The Overweighter: Ouch. (The Overweighter looks up and sees a little Indian boy, he is really small. The Overweighter doesn’t really need to look up when he is laying down.) Indian: My name is Throwdough. The Overweighter: Umm Dougghhh. Throwdough: Where did you come from? You just fell from the sky! The Overweighter: I’m an angel. Throwdough: Are you from heaven? Did it hurt when you fell from there? The Overweighter: YES! Throwdough: Why are you here? The Overweighter: To get my onion rings back! Throwdough: The lord of the onion rings has stolen from heaven! Finally someone has been sent from the all might powerful god to save us from him. Have you been to the meeting? The Overweighter: what meeting? Throwdough: Yeah, there is a meeting being held so we can over throw the lord of the rings. Follow me I will take you there. (The Overweighter follows Throwdough. He takes him through some woods, you can smell something funny and there is a weird creaking noise. It’s all dark and damp The Overweighter is looking at the trees.) The Overweighter: Why does it seem these trees are watching me? Throwdough: Because they are, haven’t you heard the stories about these woods? People believe that these woods are alive and trees can talk to each other. Someone once told me they saw the trees move and try to grab him. He went back into the woods the nest day and he hasn’t come back yet. The Overweighter: How long ago did he go? Throwdough: About 5 or 6 I can’t remember. The Overweighter: 5 or 6 days!?!! Throwdough: No years. (The Overweighter hears another creak. He stops and looks where it came from, he sees a tree on a mobile phone.) The Overweighter: That tree is using a mobile! Throwdough: Where? Tree: Hey, I need to text my mate! Do you mind!?! (The Overweighter and Throwdough start to run through the woods.) Tree: Hey no running, I’m going to tell my mate about you! (They run about 1000 meters. The Overweighter is knackered. Suddenly you hear a mobile do off, it’s the same annoying tune that everyone has for text messages. The Overweighter and Throwdough look at each other.) The Overweighter: Oh no. (You can hear and noise, it’s another tree.) Tree2: What, a couple of idiots running in are woods! There they are! (The tree begins to move and it grabs Throwdough.) Throwdough: AHH! (The tree grabs The Overweighter, it goes to lift him but Overweighter doesn’t budge. The tree tries again but nothing happens. The tree throws Throwdough away. The tree grabs The Overweighter with both branches and tries to pick him up. Nothing happens until a few second after.) Tree2: Yes, I picked you up! (Suddenly the branches snap and The Overweighter hits the floor. The Overweighter starts to runs towards Throwdough.) The Overweighter: Let’s get out of here! (The Overweighter and Throwdough begin to run, but the tree start to gain on them. The Overweighter trips over and falls to the ground. Everything freezes, nothing is moving an inch.) Is this the end of The Overweighter? Will he get up and carry on running? Or will his laziness take over? Find out in Part 2!
