Rent-A-Dad

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-5-2002 I looked at last week's Turmoil footage OVER and OVER.... Not for my betrayal.. Not for the 'entertainment value'..But for the little afterthought to one Brandi Wilson... Amy... She was crying... Broken... Distraught at the loss of the figure she thought she could trust... For Amy, Friday Fusion last week replayed OVER...and OVER....and OVER in her head.Amy still could not release the nightmare that was Tuesday Turmoil when she was sacrificed like a virgin priestess, all in the name of the creation of the Front Office.A tool....a pawn....a piece of meat to Brandi as she ripped into her....Destroyed her emotions...And raped the very soul of Amy that night as she disowned her, sent the pure orphan away crying in her drunken stupor of power. Xavier Kannon seduced Brandi with power, and both Amy and I took her for better....sadly, she gave into that one human emotion that can make or break relationships....destroy bonds....send people to the coldest of graves...GREED We both wanted her to fight it...but she fell to it with little inhibition. Brandi must have needed some source of power in her life and it must not have mattered where it came from....even if it was being handed to her on a silver platter by the devil himself....His name just so happened to be Xavier Kannon in this lifetime... Brandi took a knife and created deep wounds, the deep kind a Band-Aid is too frail to do anything about.... It'd be like using tissue to plug up a bursting dam. Those cuts left Amy and I numb, numb to feeling and care.... Numb to the notion of EVER wanting to share our feelings for others... Amy had been scorned twice unjustly... As had I...Amy's scorn stemming from the neglect of a parental figure in her life...Everytime she needed someone to hold her hand, guide her, protect her against the snowstorm we call life... She was left out on her own, no jacket to protect her from the hailing shards of ice as she was left to find her own source of love.....She thought she found that blanket of comfort in Brandi... We see how that went. Myself...well....I needed love. My heart has always been receptive to the intake and reciprocation of real love.... I thought I had it with Amy Campbell, we knew each other in and out...I gave her a piece of me that I can NEVER get back again.... And when I wanted her to be with me, share her world with me... She ran away and left me alone to transform into the hateful being I am now.... I thought I found comfort in Brandi as well...You see how that went, sensing the deja' vu' here? Fact of the matter ise....I can't keep running around HATING everything... Hate is an easy emotion to display... It takes no time at all to find a reason to hate someone, and it's simplistic to base your goals around gaining personal revenge for wrongd done to you...But does the hate EVER stop with the person you originally began with?All it does is grow....pulsate....intensify until finally.... It is your life. I never wanted revenge....hate....bloodlust to be my life.... The same way Amy never wanted loneliness and feelings of inferiority to plague hers... The more and more I thought about Brandi....Kannon...Duke...Frisco.....the neverending plans of revenge formulated for them... The more and more that little six year-old girl, that I came to know through Brandi, creeped into my mind. What bond do me and a six-year old orphan have in common?A bond of pain and suffering...A bond of neglect and hopelessness... A bond of distress... All our lives, we've called out to others for their love....for their blanket of comfort and we get brushed aside....as if we were unfit to know what love is.I know more then my share of pain. From constant teasing as a child...to being forsaken by those you thought loved you... Amy would NEVER grow up to be a bitter, angry woman who never knew what it was to have love and protection in her life. No matter how brutal and sadistic I became due to the slow chemical that was revenge flowing through my veins......my heart was interwined with Amy.... The pain we both shared was similar, only mine had progressed to stages it should never have gone.If nothing else, I want to save her from hating what life has to offer her....I want her to know that she could grow up to be a successful, happy woman..... She will know love that lasts.... She will experience the joy of children... She will know all these pleasures and joys if someone would step in for her....If someone would hold her hand so she could make that walk to being a graceful, happy young lady.... And that someone was me... Which is why I never regretted signing the papers for Amy. From the moment I woke up this morning, all I could think of was saving her....holding her hand...being the blanket she was never entitled to... She would know what love is... She would stop being pricked by the thorns of life and begin to smell the fragrant roses, the joys life was full to the brim with. I looked at her, laying limply with her seatbelt strapped across her chest, her face brandishing the most innocent smile... She was in a slight slumber, her head drooping off lazily to the side as we made the trek home, I dared not race down the highways at my usual high speed....she was too cute to wake...All I could think about now that I had her, was how I wanted to give her EVERYTHING....Then take it all away.... Just so I could give her more.Amy and I are kindred....we both have felt our share of pain.... Today begins the time we feel pain together.... And when that ends....we will soon know what it is to smile together. I love you Amy.....and I mean it. The day I forsake you is the day life is pulled from my body and my soul joins the heroes of Valhalla... From this day forward, you are flesh of my flesh.... Blood of my blood... And you will forever be daddy's little girl... I will be your blanket int he snowstorm... I will be your hand to hold in the dark...I will guide you, why?... Because I love you Amy....Thank God I saved you in time. I just wish someone saved me...
