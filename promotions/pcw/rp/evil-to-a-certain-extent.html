Evil...To A Certain Extent

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.23.02(#8 PPV) “Couldn’t we have gotten a job a little more evil and a little less smelly?” The whining tone of Rush Mason’s voice so cheerfully opens this promo as we can hear strange noises as the audience is both literally and figuratively in the dark as the scene is all black. “I mean, I want to be evil, but there’s a limit I would’ve put, had I known this was going to happen.” With that, the scene comes in full view, and we can see Rush and Ray Ray walking along what appears to be a sidewalk in some sort of enclosed building, with water somewhere making that sound that water makes. Rush: This is a waste of time and effort. We came to see your buddy and we got stuck doing some retarded errand. Ray Ray, walking in front of him, stops and turns back, looking down at Rush, then shrugs as Rush mutters to himself and starts walking. He calls back to Rush in an un-sympathetic voice. Ray Ray: Yo, you wanted evil, and I got you it. Muttering and spitting underneath his breath, Rush just trudges on, following blindly behind his manager, who for some reason knows his way around this place, which looks very much like a… Rush: I still can’t believe we’re in a sewer. The next Alliances winner is in a sewer the day before his victory. Pathetic. Ray Ray doesn’t respond, although the Voodoo Villain doesn’t expect him to. They continue to walk along the narrow passageway, and Ray Ray almost trips into the water as he slips on the cement. Rush shakes his head and slowly makes his way along the wall, calling out to his manager. Rush: If you down here, I’m leaving you to the rats, I hope you know that. However, his employee is too busy making sure to keep his footing than to reply back, and Rush just follows along until they reach a large pipe that is filled with water and Ray Ray looks back at Mason. Rush: No fucking way I’m going down there. Ray Ray looks back at him, his face uncertain, and then just pulls his usual shrug and steps down into the murky water that covers the passageway. Rush just chuckles to himself as he leans against the wall. Rush: Heh, can’t wait ‘til you come running back here all covered in shit and rats and stuff. Suddenly looking around, Rush realizes how very alone he is in the sewer, with many of the aforementioned rats and God knows what else. He glances around, and then calls out to Ray Ray, trying to seem like he’s not terrified. Rush: Scared out of your mind yet, ya big retard? Probably pissing those XXL pants of yours? Hearing a screeching sound, Rush tromps into the water and dashes up to where Ray Ray is standing. When he arrives, he tries to look as though nothing is out of the ordinary. Rush: I just..uhh…came along to make sure you don’t fuck this up. He looks up at his manager, who is probably too dumb to realize Rush’s bluff, and then the two just continue to slosh through the water as Ray Ray calls back to Rush. Ray Ray: Dawg, we almost done anyway. It’ll only take two minutes. We’re almost there. And, so they are there. They end up in a large clearing, where they step onto a platform and see….nothing. Rush:So…? Ray Ray looks around, apparently supposed to be seeing something here, but not. He then spies a small blanket on top of what appears to be old trash, but he lifts up the blankets to reveal an old man sleeping. Rush: Eww…homeless people. Move away, I wanna get a good shot in. His manager ignoring him, Rush walks over to the homeless man, and picks him up, throwing him against the wall. Rush: Listen, we’re gonna beat you up now, so if you could start groaning, it’d make me feel particularly evil. The old man looks at Rush, and then up at Ray Ray, speaking to Ray Ray in a very educated tone. “This is the fuck they sent to beat me up?! BAHAHAHA!” With that, the homeless man begins to walk away, and Rush calls after him, trying to sound as if though he just chased him off. Rush: Yeah, run, pussy! The large manager taps Rush on the shoulder, and tries to mention an obvious fact to him. Ray Ray: Dawg…you didn’t do anything. I was supposed to just make sure he's still alive cuz he's not really a homeless g- Rush realizes this and goes tearing after the bum,ignoring Ray Ray's final words and looking to get in some good homeless people beating, but Rush slips and goes sliding around, tumbling off the edge towards the murky water as the scene goes black… “Dawg….you alright?” “Ray Ray…remind me to stab you when I get dry.” “Aite…nah, you remember, I’ll forget, yo…”
