Date: Season I, Episode 13 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 13“Maybe Something’s Wrong”************************************ Location: Snow Residence - New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 7:45 am The day of the hit came, and Snow hadn’t slept to well. He caught a few hours of sleep on theplane ride back from Adrenalin, but other than that, the night was full of surreal thoughts and dryhours. What if things went wrong? What if he failed to get the job done? And who, exactly,was this partner that he was supposed to go in with? Johnny Silver was supposed to show up today with the details, and that would round out the totalnumber of men who would know of the plan. Justin Hattoni, Petey the Vice, Johnny Silver,Jason Snow, and of course, this mystery man who was going in with him. Everything was imploding, it seemed. There was too much pressure, and it was all pinpointedand targeted on a single span of time shorter than a week. Between the job on Mossilano, thePCW Championship match, and the news about Jeremy Howard, Snow’s nerves were frayedjagged. He set an empty coffee mug down on the kitchen table and took a seat. The table was set next toa rather large window, and he was able to keep his eye on the drive way. Johnny Silver’sdefinition of ‘morning’ seemed to be sometime around noon, but for a job this size, Snowdecided that Johnny would likely show up a lot earlier. “You’re up early,” his wife’s voice broke the silence. Startled, Snow whipped his head aroundand stood up from the seat. When he saw Raven, his heart sank back down from his throat. Being the morning person that she was, his wife already looked ready for the day. Snow hadalways admired that about her. He hated mornings. “I’m going out,” he said casually, trying to hide his edginess. “I’ll be back sometime tonight, Ithink.” “And then Turmoil tomorrow,” his wife sighed. “Jason, you said that moving down here wouldlet us spend more time together - but we never see you.” “I’ve just got to sort some things out,” he said without meeting her eye. “I’ll be around more. I’ve just got some things to do first. That’s all.” She came close to him and rested her head against his broad back, putting her left arm aroundhim. Her right arm went instinctively to his hair - she loved it now that it was short. It wasgrowing so fast, she thought, that it wouldn’t be long before she was pestering him to get it cutagain. And of course, he never did tell her why he got it cut in the first place. Feeling her warmth, Snow closed his eyes and took a deep breath. He began weighing theseriousness of his task for the thousandth time. The reality seemed almost unbearable,especially now, after he had just been freed from prison and gotten his life, at least on a familylevel, back to normal. There was so little margin for error. Suddenly, he felt the urge to head upstairs and hold his daughter. “Try not to stay out too late, Jason,” Raven said, still clutching him tightly from behind. Shecould sense something was troubling him, and it troubled her as well. But she knew there wasno point in pressing toward the source of his worry. “I want to talk to you about Krista.” Snow sighed - Krista was a hot topic around the house lately. “Alright,” he nodded. He would have pressed the matter further, but he realized that it wouldonly add more stress, as issues with Krista always did. Her fingers continued through his yellow hair. It was combed straight back, though there was alittle bounce to it, and her fingers broke the mousse shell. He didn’t seem to care. When he opened his eyes, Johnny Silver’s black Benz was coming up the street toward thehouse. It was time to go see his daughter, Haley. *** Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment - New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 9:25 am Krista enters Marty Verrelli’s apartment slowly, and he leads her over to the sofa with a brightsmile. He was always happy when Krista was able to see him, though lately, those occasionswere becoming more and more rare. Of course, she blamed it on her overbearing brother-in-law,who had taken it upon himself to take the role of her protector, but Marty couldn’t help feelingsuspicious that there might be other reasons. “So how are you?” he was trying to make idle conversation once they were sitting. “I haven’tseen you in a few days.” “I’m ok,” she said, looking up at him with a sheepish smile. He knew something was wrong. Instinctively, he pulled her close to him and they leaned back together. “Good,” he said, even though he didn’t believe her. “I missed you, you know?” She nodded. He didn’t see it, but he could feel her head on his chest. He began to stroke herhair lightly. “My father says he wants to meet you,” he said to her, trying again to make conversation. Itseemed she wasn’t in much of a conversation mood. So why did she come, he wondered? “Hedoesn’t come around so much these days. He’s a busy guy, so I’m not sure when you’ll get achance to meet him. He’s actually working a job with Snow sometime soon, or at least that’swhat he told me. Something big - your brother-in-law must be moving up quick. Really fuckingquick. I don’t think I’ve ever seen someone so fresh working with my old man.” Krista closed her eyes and tried not to think about her brother being involved in a crime family. And soon, to her surprise, she tried to ignore the fact that Marty was involved in the samefamily. How strongly did she feel for this young man, anyway, she wondered? At this point, maybe her positive feelings toward Marty were a good thing. *** Location: Abandoned Warehouse - New York CityDate: January 20, 2003Time: 10:30 am When the large warehouse doors opened, Snow got his first look at the man who had heard somuch about - The Dragon. He wasn’t physically impressive as Snow had imagined. He waslarge, but looked like he hadn’t been to a gym in at least a few years, and his beerbelly hungdown a few inches over his jeans. Still, Snow could tell instantly that this was a dangerous man. Thick black smoke rolled out of his mouth from the thick cigar he clenched in his teeth. Hishead was completely shaven and his bare arms were heavily tattoo’ed. He reminded Snow of alot of the people he had met in prison. Somehow, he imagined that The Dragon had his ownprison experience as well, and he doubted that the man was anything less than dominant whilehaving it. He stood tall in the center of the empty warehouse, which was a building that all parties involvedhad agreed would be safe, and kept his eye on Snow for the first few moments. Johnny Silver, of course, was only the messenger. Snow had long since realized that Johnnywasn’t the ‘important’ man that he would have Snow believe. Johnny was important, of course,but Snow had intuitively learned that one day soon, he would outrank the man with the silvercombover. “Alright,” Johnny began as he closed the large wooden doors. The vast room became ominouslydim. “We all know why we’re fucking here, and we know we ain’t got no fucking room forerror. So if either of you are doubting something, or maybe you think its immoral, or maybeyou’ve got last night’s blow job on your fucking mind - now is the time to say so.” Both men were silent. “This isn’t fucking rocket science,” Johnny continued. “I’ve got a list of John Mossilano’s dailyappointments, right here. You find him at one of these things, and you follow him throughoutthe day. If he notices you, don’t fucking back off - just let him think you’re the Feds. How you do the job is at your own discretion, just get it done today! And remember this,above all else - this can not be traced back to the Hattoni family. Capice?” Snow nodded. The Dragon nodded. “There’s a new blue lumina waiting for you across the street,” he said as he pulled a set of keysfrom the inside of his jacket. “Get going.”
