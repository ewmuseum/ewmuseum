The Secret Treasure of New Jersey

Date: Uprising Once Again Well, there it was. The Third Age of PCW is upon us, and the age is one without Tyler Nelson or the Phantom Booker. A man with any caring bone in his body last night might’ve hated Xavier Kannon. Most people would’ve cursed the Scientologist Champion and his disgusting excuse for a wife, wished them both lying six feet under. Not Rush Mason. Not by a long shot. Since the day he came to PCW, Nelson has always held him down, always kept him from succeeding while his former NTICW partners like said World Champion and Pharaoh moved up. No, Tyler Nelson wouldn’t have Rush up there, because he knew that the Future would eventually take him out. But now, there’s no need for that, because Xavier Kannon took out the garbage. Now it’s time for the real game. Now it’s time for revenge and to run amok in PCW, because there’s no more Tyler Nelson, and there’s no more protection for the little circus sideshow known as the Dark Ages. The real dark ages of PCW are coming upon us… “Bon Dieu! It reeks of sub-par in here!” Of course that is the voice of Rush Mason, always having something insightful to say about his current situation. However, the shot has not opened up fully yet so we can only wonder what Rush can be complaining about now. “How do those goth losers live like this?” Rush’s talking would lead one to assume that he is somewhere with another person but the Voodoo Villain, but it is not unknown for Rush to talk to himself for long periods of time. Rush: Hey Doctor Idiot…where the hell are you taking us? Just as Rush addresses his companin every so respectfully, the scene finally opens up as both Rush and another unidentified man wearing white lab coats and surgical masks, which makes Rush almost as unidentifiable as the other man, but Rush’s sarcastic and obnoxious voice is unmistakable. “My name is Doctor Karalynzski. It’s not that hard to remember and I don’t appreciate you calling me an idiot. Even with Rush’s surgical mask, it’s plain to be seen that as the doctor turns his head, Rush makes a little mocking face and mutters under his breath something while the Doctor continues to walk down a long, steel-walled hallway. Rush: Dr Catlicksi, excuse me again, but where in the sub-par name of your institution are we headed? The doctor shakes his head and pulls up his surgical mask a little bit as Rush looks around at the bare walls, wondering who in the name of Bon Dieu would design a hallway so hideous. Doctor K: Mr. Mason, my institution is one of the top of its kind in the country, and you have requested a special viewing session, so I would like to see some more respect. Rush shakes his head and does his mocking motion again, pretending to be the doctor, and continues to walk behind the man, trying to seem as unhappy as possible, because what fun is being a sarcastic asshole if people think you’re in a good mood? Rush: I’m pretty sure I’m not speaking Polish, now I will ask you again, you second-rate quack. Where are we going? This would be the last straw, actually the last straw would’ve been a while ago for anyone but Rush’s connections. However, the doctor only lets a small amount of his annoyance show in his voice as he replies. Doctor K: We are going to the room that you requested we show you. And Mr. Mason, let me remind you that while we are fans of your wrestling, we demand respect as much as any other profession. Rolling his eyes, Rush doesn’t bother to mock the doctor this time, but just follows the doctor, who continues to lead him down the steel-walled hallway as they near a *gasp* steel doorway! Rush: Wow, who’s your decorator? I’ll need a nice little steel box for Clyde’s ashes. Shaking his head, the doctor replies but with even more edge on his voice now, as he almost growls back to Rush as they get nearer to the door. Doctor K: His name is Little Dylan Steel. Rush snorts a laugh as the doctor ignores him, and they stop as the reach the door. The doctor begins to type in some codes for the door, and after a minute or so, Rush begins to tap his foot irritably as the Doctor fumbles with the codes and growls again back at Rush. Doctor K: Being annoying doesn’t get the door open any quicker. Rush takes this with annoyance and a sneer, as would be expected from someone who has proclaimed himself the future of PCW. The doctor pokes around with the numbers a bit more, and finally, with a rush of air, the door opens as Rush chuckles to himself and mutters in a robot voice. Rush: Doors being de-pressurized, Spock. The doctor just continues to ignore Rush, and they step into a large open room that feels a bit chilly to Rush, but he looks around at *gasp* more steel! The doctor turns around and asks Rush in a more relaxed voice now, probably because he could kill Rush in this room and no one would hear. Doctor K:So, is there any special reason that you’ve come to our institution today? Rush first looks at the doctor with a blank stare, as if the doctor is joking, then asks the doctor in a strangely calm voice. Rush: C’mon, you may be a quack, but you know what I came for. The doctor looks around, looking scared for a moment, like Rush might’ve brought him here to rape him or some crazy thing like that. In fact, the terror makes him forget for a moment that this man just called him a quack. He responds in a trembling voice and only now realizes how much stronger and taller Rush is than him, and probably faster, preventing an escape route via running… Doctor K: Well umm…actually…could you be a little more specific? Rush looks around for a moment, and then surprisingly speaks in an ever softer tone, barely above a whisper as he also motions with his hands. Rush: I know it’s here. It’s okay, you don’t have to tell anyone, I have to see it though. The doctor’s hand begins to tremble again as he begins to unzipper his pants and… Rush: SWEET BON DIEU! Get your damn sub-par wang away from me! The doctor looks rather relieved though, as Rush turns away disgustedly while the doctor fixes his pants. Doctor K: Oh good, I thought you might rape me. Rush sticks out his tongue from his surgical mask and shows disgust on his face as he looks over the doctor. Rush: Please, just get on with it, you second-rate buffoon. The doctor now looks very confused, and finally decides to flat out confront his problem. Doctor K: What are you exactly here to see, sir? Rush opens his mouth in surprise and looks at the doctor as if he just declared that squirrels were making war on the world. Rush: Why the hell else would I go to a morgue in Jersey? Where the hell is Hoffa, you quack?! Rush almost screams as he ends his sentence, and now the doctor is the one who lowers his head and looks at Rush with a glare of pure contempt. As the scene fades out, the doctor shakes his head and Rush spits in disgust and walks out of the room. “I guess I’ll just have to check under Giants Stadium to find him.”
