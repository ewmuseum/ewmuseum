Time, what a wonderful time

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/22/2002 ~'Here I go again....' No, its not Whitesnake but the words people are using who were here when everyone was more or less fired last June when PCW completely restarted itself, merging with PCW2. Jon Kano was one of those men. He has been in PCW for the longest time of anyone else. The Chef began his PCW career earlier than Kano, but the Chef was gone for a long period last summer. Kano has been here continously. Has anything changed? Well the fat has been trimmed once again in PCW. No more self-righteous, overpushed jobbers holding titles. And Kano is THE MAN. He isn't living in the shadow of anyone else. Today is Kano's day. The present is Kano's era. NOW IS KANO'S TIME TO SHINE. What is Kano's goals with this new PCW? The same as what his goal has been for the last six months of his career. He wants and will oust the corrupt owner and CEO of PCW, the bastard known as Tyler Nelson. The only difference is now Kano has the backup he needs in the Malice Crew. Nelson's Ultimate Authority group is falling apart at the seams, and the Malice Crew gets stronger by the day. The time is near to the end of Tyler Nelson's reign of terror over PCW. God bless this time.~ *The scene opens up just outside of the PCW Pavilion in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Jon Kano, decked to the nines in a black Armani suit with matching shoes and golden framed Versace sunglasses, is standing in front of the Pavilion, sizing it up.* Kano: This is where it started. Right here, in that very building, the dynasty was born. PCW began here, and it will probably end here. I wasn't here when it began, but I may as well have been. I have been here and done that. I have done everything in PCW that I have ever aspired to do in PCW except one thing. That one thing would be to oust that son of a bitch Tyler Nelson out of PCW. *A man approaches Kano, dressed in various PCW merchandise, including an Ultimate Authority t-shirt.* Man: Mr. Kano!!! Do you think I could get an autograph? Kano: What the hell is this? Man: I'm a huge fan. Could I please get an autograph? Kano: You know, normally I wouldn't mind giving you and autograph or two but you see I don't like you. *The man gets a surprised look on his face.* Man: Why is that? Kano: Well, you see, you are an ass and you are wearing the enemy's shirt. Man: The enemy? Kano: Yeah, the Ultimate Authority. Nelson's lackeys. The group that I, along with the rest of the Malice Crew, are trying to destroy before they destroy PCW. Man: Oh who cares, you are nothing but a has-been anyways. *Kano becomes incensed and grabs the man by the collar of his shirt.* Kano: What did you say? What the f*ck did you just say? Man: Noth, nothi, nothing, sir. Kano: You know, I know why you are a fan of UA. You are just a little bitch like all of them. *Kano lets the man go and begins to walk down the legendary boardwalk of Atlantic City, New Jersey.* Kano: What a little bastard that guy was. He reminded me of Nelson. He thought he was a real man only to realize when he got nose to nose with this REAL LEGEND that he was nothing. He was pimple on the ass of history, just like Nelson should be. If Nelson wouldn't have had the good fortune of purchasing PCW from The Phantom Booker after that crazy Aussie damn near killed a dozen fans, then he would be scrubbing toilets in the mental ward of some third rate hospital in Georgia. Yet with all of the good luck Tyler has had, he is probably the most ungrateful man I have ever met in my life. He always wants more, he is never satisfied. With him in charge and him being the way he is, its no wonder that the average lifespan of a newbie wrestler in PCW is about 3 weeks. Tyler is consumed by greed. Greed is moderation is alright, we all are a little greedy. But Tyler has set out from day one to be in PCW for the sole purpose of increasing his own interests and good. Nelson is the only man in my illustrious, legendary career that I could honestly say I HATE. I am not just talking about my PCW tenure, but my four and a half years previous. In those four years, I have been run over by people, wrestled zoo animals, electrocuted myself in a steel cage, and taken more chair shots and gone through more tables than I can even guestimate. What has Tyler done? He has gotten lucky. He has been lucky for way too long and it began at Supremacy, and it will continue on this Friday's edition of Fusion that his luck will run out. He wants to run Duke and the rest of us out of PCW, but that will never happen as long as he is still as corrupt as he is. Duke wants him in our match Friday, and he can have him. I can got someone that I want to beat the piss out of on Friday. *Kano looks at a beautiful young brunette woman walking past him in virtually nothing.* Kano: Jon, you're a married man. Jon, you love your wife. Jon, you are two months into your marriage and you have never been happier. Where was I? Oh yeah, I thought that at Supremacy I would have enough of kicking the hell out of Tim Walden, but no. I still want to beat the hell out of my former student. I am not satisfied with beating the hell out of him and all of his homogay anal butt sex buddies at Supremacy. I want more. I am like a crack addict needing another hit of the crackpipe. I need to spear him again. I need to Kanoplex him again. I NEED IT. I WANT IT. I GOTTA HAVE IT. I will get it on Friday when Supremacy repeats itself. And on Friday, I will show everyone why I am still here and why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano continues to walk down the boardwalk of Atlantic City, New Jersey.* ~Nelson is so screwed. The Malice Crew is ready to rumble, and the Ultimate Authority is falling apart. Who do you think is going to win? My money is on the Malice Crew.~
