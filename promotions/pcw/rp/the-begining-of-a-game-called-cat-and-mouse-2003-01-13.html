The begining of a game called Cat and Mouse

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-1-03 Last Thursday the results came in for the Amber Hill election to decide the mayor of Amber Hill. Bob Vurdo won the election and became the newest mayor of the city. Residents believe he had ties with the Mafia to help him win but the police never proved that any of the participants were involved with the Mafia. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob Vurdo sits back on his chair as he looks accross the table at the old man who sits on the seat nervously. The old man's green eyes dart around the room from object to object but not staying locked on a particular one for longer than a second. Bob doesn't know why he is so nervous but he doesn't like it one bit. If the old man is nervous it means he has something to be nervous about. Bob has to be weary of this type of thing as the new mayor of Amber Hill and also the boss of the Vurdo family his life is often endangered. 'What can I do for you Mr. Hanley.' The man looks around for a second before looking up at Bob with his aging green eyes. 'Ahhh.... Hmmmm.' He is hesitant to say what he wants as he looks back down at his lap where his hands rest neatly folded. 'Get to the point.' The man's body jolts as Bob adds an annoyed tone to his voice. 'Well.... I..... Ah....' The man hesitates for a second before gulping and continuing. 'I have a complaint about the litter on Amber Hill's streets.... Ah do you think we could get more rubbish bins put around the place.' The mans old face wabble's around as he talks. Bob leans over the desk staring the old man directly in the eyes. He pauses as the old man grows more uncomfortable by the second. 'You wasted all this time to ask for more rubbish bins?' The man doesn't reply he just sits there looking at Bob through those green eyes full of fear. 'Well did you?' The man nods his head slowly as he begins looking toward the ground. 'I'll see what I can do but next time write a letter.' The old man smiles as he gets up from his seat and walks out of there as quickly as he can. Bob then turns his chair around to face the window that looks over the main street of the city from the council building. He leans back and looks out on the city when the buzzer to the phone on the desk goes off followed by the polite voice of the councils receptionist. 'Mr. Vurdo a friend of yours Tony is here to see you.' Bob turns his chair around and presses the button on the phone. 'Send him in.' The two wooden doors at the front of the office open as Tony walks in with his usual gray suit under a brown trench coat attire. Tony smiles at Bob as he sits on the seat opposite Bob where the old man was sitting. 'So this is it.' Tony smiles as he takes a look around the office with various award for city achievements city against the walls. 'Yeap this is it.' 'Lets get down to business.' Tony brings his chair in closer to the desk and then leans across the desk to get closer to Bob. 'We have set ourselves up to make millions.' Tony smiles at Bob who smiles back. 'I own this fucking town in more ways than one now.' Bob laughs to himself in a sinister manner. 'The only problem is.' Bob hadn't thought about any downsides to the plan and as the words came out of Tony's mouth he knew straight away there was going to be a downside. 'Now that everyone knows who you are and what you are you can't be seen hanging with us.... It also means you can't come out with us on business trips.' As Bob thinks about what Tony says he is slightly relieved. He doesn't enjoy going out on 'business' trips anyway because of the chance of getting caught. 'It also means we are going to have to be clever with how we do this. We have to use your power as mayor of Amber Hill but we can't involve you directly.' Bob thinks for a second before smiling at Tony. 'All right we can work that out but what do you think we should start off by doing?' 'All right well lets get the food industry sorted out.... We have 'shares' in three restaurants and two cafe's in Amber Hill. We can set an example to their rivals using the health inspectors and be able to get a little more money on the side with threats of regular health inspections.' 'Good idea.' 'We can also make sure there are no business's that pop up without us making a bit of money off them through building regulations and the like.' Bob nods as he mentally takes note of the plans. 'That's what we will start with anyway.... Now you just need to continue running this town in a way that brings up no controversy.' 'Easily done.' 'All right glad we got that cleared.' Tony smiles and leans back in his chair. Bob then leans back when he notices the red light flashing on his speaker. He takes a closer look and realizes the speaker has been on the whole time which would allow the secretary to hear the entire conversation. 'Fuck.' Tony's attention snaps from the view of the town behind Bob to Bob himself. 'What?' Tony can sense the urgency in Bob's voice. 'The secretary has heard this whole conversation.' Tony's eyes widen as he gets up from his seat and pulls a gun out of the inside pocket of his jacket. He begins walking toward the door when he hears the bing of the elevator arriving at the level. 'She's in the elevator.' Tony then runs out the door of the office as Bob gets out of his seat. Tony opens up the two large doors and enters the secretaries room to see the elevator close and head toward the bottom floor. He looks across at the secretaries desk where no one sits. 'Shit.... Bob we have a problem.' Bob runs through the two large doors and stares at Tony for a second. Tony rushes over to the closed elevator doors and presses the button beside it to call the elevator to this level a few times. He looks up at the gauge that tells which floor the elevator is on and watches painfully as it continues to head toward level one. 'Take the stairs.' Bob puts his gun back in his jacket and heads toward the stairway which is in the far corner of the room. Tony follows as the two begin running down the stairs as fast as they can. They emerge in the lobby to see the secretary running out of the council HQ as fast as she can with her high heel shoes on. Bob an Tony follow but walk as they don't want to cause a scene. The lady looks over her shoulder and continues running faster. As Bob and Tony walk out the electric doors they begin jogging after the lady who is trying to unlock her car as she looks over her shoulder at Bob and Tony in fear. Bob and Tony both run as hard as the can toward the secretary as she gets in her car and puts the keys in the ignition. She starts up the car and speeds off as Bob and Tony near the blue car. 'Now we have a problem.' Bob says in an exhausted voice.
