Date: ... “Avoid exposure at all costs, lest your existence be noticed. Then when you are known to exist...will they be ready for your attack.” ~~~Savage Tranquility, page undetermined Miyagawa...the Japo-American freak. PCW’s Television champion, taken away from Flaymin Aymin before him...by an act of god specifically speaking. It would be such a pleasure to yet again step into the ring with this man after the Madness Sunday. He has surely had himself a long...extensive run within Primetime Championship Wrestling...way before Akiyama even thought of the federation. This fight would only prove to beyond great...two mighty Japanese...the ONLY Japanese in PCW...fighting against one another for the honor of being the most dominant Nihongo in PCW...for the right to be called the champion of television. “It’s the Television title, what better way to gain positive exposure on TV outside of PCW coverage?” The NVG has proved before that he can go head to head with the Goggled one. Red October was the time and place last year. He, along with OSW’s commissioner Edward, and the Dark Ages founder, Clyde, were the victims of an @sskicking with style up on top of those towering scaffolds, and Akiyama proved his dominance. His first PCW belt...and from that day forward, Akiyama would go on to prove just why he was a Celestial being...from then...to now...and beyond... “Bah! We don not smile upon gaining a televised appearance outside of battle. It is strictly promos and shows for us!” Television can be looked upon in two manners. The first is as a means of entertainment. Its receivers go out and snatch the signals broadcasted, and brings the images straight to the viewer at home. Cable...satellite...delivering stations and channels at random variety. Comedy, drama, children, education, news, action...adult...anime...all sorts of programs to soothe our craving for blissful entertainment. “Come on, Aki! Consider this a consolation prize after March Madness. I know how disappointed you are because you jabbed!!” The other way of viewing television may not be to entertaining. The television...is a tool...but not one of fun. It is more of a corporate device used to sway the minds of the populous. Commercials...sponsors...products...”This show is brought to you by...” Subliminal messages sewn into the inner linings of catchy jingles, sales pitches, and eye-appealing spokespersons. Pepsi: For those who think young... Do the Dew... 7-up yours... Obey your thirst... Snapple: Made from the best stuff on earth... They are all directed towards lassoing the youth of society into drinking beverages known for blemishing their sensitive adolescent skin with acne...using society’s own leading figures to condemn these children into facial disorder. Fast food...junk food...meaningless and worthless items...they’re all sold over the TV. You do not control it...it controls you... You do not turn it on...it turns you on... It steals your precious time away...time otherwise spent living your lives worthwhile. “Yuu...last time we listened to this woman, she had us dressed up like the Good Humor Man giving away @sskickings with style. We were hoping to never have to deal with her again, but you had to find her number...didn’t you? And the correct tern you’re looking for is jobbed...not jabbed...” Television...invention of the twentieth century...or mind stealing banality of humanity? You decide...but also think about who you think would do better as it’s champion...it’s representative... “Heh we all make mistakes, don’t we Mr. Akiyama?” “Hey...you’re lucky we didn’t banish you on sight.” Anyway...the scene opened upon the office of public relations and press agent Seraphina Jameson. Like last time, her office was decorated in that new age futuristic design of steel gray furniture. The huge bay window behind her desk was opened wide, allowing the sun to shine into her sterile work environment. The African American woman was dressed in a silver stretch pant suit with silver thick heels. Her straight black hair was parted down to the left, styled in a blunt cut bob, hiding her ears and a portion of her right eye. Yuu and Akira Akiyama sat across from her in the metal chairs. Yuu was wearing silver satin Capri pants with a silver Camisole v-neck blouse, silver cardigan elbow length sweater, and a rhinestone belt with silver tone heeled leather sandals. Her lengthy light brown hair was crimped from root to tip, and parted down he middle to drape over her shoulders. DA GOD for once was garbed in his civilian attire, and not in his battle gear of black vinyl and PVC. He wore a sleek short sleeved black mock shirt, with black faux fur jeans, and silver buckled black boots. Like usual, his hair was tied up in a high ponytail with a yellow satin ribbon, and his forearms were still covered in that black vinyl tape. Covering his eyes was a pair of black rimmed sunglasses with silver reflecting lenses. Good thing too...cause then the glare in his eyes would have been noticeable. A sigh floated out of the NVG as he reached for his steaming cup. “Anyway...what is it thayt you have planned for us, Seraphina? Another hair-brained scheme that’ll make us look like a fool and job to not only Miyagawa...but Frisco and Legend Inc too?” She chuckled slightly. “I had nothing to so with your loss to Timo in Tokyo.” “The freak you didn’t.” he retorted. “You were the one who was directing our promos at the time. It was because of your directing that we lost to him, was disowned by our family, and was banished from Japan.” “Wow, I did all that?” she looked down at the file on her desk, her voice apologetic. “I guess I ruined your life, eh?” The more optimistic Yuu shook her head. “Not really! You gave him a reason to ditch the International Incident, and now he’s loved by the fans!” “We would beg to differ after the Madness...” that night, his semi-face status was questionable as he interfered in Jay Sylo’s match, and picked a fight with Legend Inc. “Oh, so I DID succeed in improving your public image!” Seraphina’s face brightened back up, realizing her success. Akiyama sipped his tea slowly, hesitating to respond to her with yet another smart remark. “Yeah...sure...whatever... what’s really important now is dealing with the task at hand. Miyagawa is the Television champion...and we need to dethrone him to gain back some of our godly creditability...therefore building up some steam before taking out those two old school foes on Sabotage.” “I have a few ideas.” the god’s hand stopped her as she reached for a memo pad on her desk. “Just for the record, we’re not going to become a Japo-Canadian freak... a Columbian drug lord, or a self proclaimed legend or whatever else stereotypes they hold.” She pulled her hand back in a sense of defeat. “Hmmm...well I could call in a couple of favors from some of my contacts from the networks....get you a few guest spots on some shows to show your dedication to becoming a TV icon.” “Great idea!” Yuu exclaimed. The god eyed Seraphina carefully. “What kind of guest spots?” “We swear...that girl’s getting banished for this.” “It’s not at all that bad, Aki!” “Y.S.W.” “Huh?” “Yeah...sure...whatever...” A voice called out to them. “Quiet on the set! Cue the music!” “CAN YOU TELL ME HOW TO GET...HOW TO GET TO SESAME STREET??” There they were...Akiyama and Yuu...walking along the pretend alley called Sesame Street. The god grumbled to himself as the cameras rolled. “Lousy rotten P-R b!tch. We swear...she gets us into more embarrassing sh!t than Miyagawa has face lifts.” “Aki!” Yuu hissed under her breath. “Kayfabe!” “Screw that! Miya’s the one talking about us prancing around in the Void Armor. And do you know why?? Cause some lazy-@ssed Poser photographer wont shoot us an updated photo! We tell you...that Poser guy and Miya are probably in cahoots!” “Shhh!” they finally reached the front of the apartment building, where all the kids were sitting around wit the Latin star of the show, Maria, and Elmo. “Oh. Look kids!” Maria called out with over-enthusiasm, pointing over to Akiyama and Yuu. “We have visitors!” “GOODIE!” the red furry monster yelled out. “ELMO LOVES VISITORS!!” “Why are you yelling? Can you not control the pitch of your voice?” Akiyama asked while grabbing his ears. Yuu jabbed him in his ribs before leaning over to address the children. “Hello, children! It’s nice to meet all of you today!” Yuu said with a smile. “My name is Yuu.” “Who?” one of the children asked. “Yuu.” “Me?” another child asked confused. Yuu shook her head with a giggle. “No...my name is Yuu!” “U?? U IS ONE OF THE LETTERS OF THE DAY!!” Elmo exclaimed. Akiyama gritted his teeth at the sound of the monster’s voice. “What is this, Abbott and Costello?” he muttered. “Who’s on first, huh?” One of the kids raised his hand. “You?” Yuu shook her head. “No...I’m not on first! I’m Yuu!” “You’re me?” the child responded, more confused than ever. “No, not Mii...Yuu!” Yuu’s tolerance must have been stocked high. Too bad the NVG’s wasn’t. “AUUUGH! Enough of the rolling puns!! We’re Aki-frickin-yama, not Miyagawa! We leave the foolishness to him and him alone! Move on with the show!” Yuu looked at Akiyama with a nod. “Say hello to the children Akiyama.” Akiyama leaned over like Yuu, looking each of the young ones dead in their faces with a smile. “How you little sh!ts doing?” “Aki!” Yuu was shocked just as much as everyone else. “Stop showing off!” He shrugged. “Sorry...it slipped.” Maria looked uneasy, not knowing what to do. She laughed weakly before moving on. “So, Akiyama, wasn’t your birthday this past Sunday? How old are you?” The god’s answer was blunt. “Not as nearly as old as you are, you wrinkly whore. Shouldn’t you be dead by now?” The Latina started gibbering rapidly in Spanish as she reached up to snatch off her earrings. Yuu stepped in between the two before a fight broke out on public access television. “Maria...calm down...and Aki...you behave yourself!” “It’s not our fault that she still knows how to give a good enough slob job to keep the producers from replacing her with a younger Shakira type.” “AMEN!” someone shouted from off camera. Yuu sighed as she leaned over to talk to Elmo. The children were more confused then before about what was going on. “At least you’re still the same cute and lovable Elmo, despite what’s happening.” her smile shined through the bleak situation. The Muppet nodded happily. “ELMO ALWAYS CUTE AND LOVABLE!” he yelled out. Just then...Akiyama turned his attention from the ancient Latin American woman cursing him our in Spanish to Elmo. “What are you staring at, creature?” he asked. Elmo looked his head around innocently as Yuu straightened back to her full height. “Aki, what’s wrong now?” she asked, growing a bit tired of his behavior. “That little bastard was staring at your cleavage.” Yuu sighed hard. “Akiyama...his eyes are made of felt. He cannot focus clearly upon direct objects!” Akiyama ignored her, pointing his black nailed finger down in his face. “We catch you disrespecting her like that again, and we’ll kick your furry little @ss all over Sesame Street!” Elmo raised his hands, signaling to bring it on. “ELMO NOT AFRAID OF YOU PCW CHUMPS! ELMO ALREADY PROVE HOW HARDCRE SESAME STREET IS!” “Let’s see how hardcore you get when we toss you through Mr. Hooper’s display window!” the NVG replied in a near growl. “We’ll tear apart those gay lovers Bert and Ernie, and as far as Linda...well, it’s pretty frickin hard for a deaf woman to hear an attacker approach, now is it?” “That’s it!” Maria whistled with her two fingers. The children all backed out of the area as the lights dimmed, they must’ve known something Aki didn’t. Maria stepped up to Akiyama with her fists raised. “PCW is about to get another beat down from the Sesame Street Barrio!” Akiyama was caught off guard as Maria leaped up and nailed Akiyama with a hurricanrana. The NVG rolled over into the round clearing at the end of the street, getting back up to find Maria running at him again. She jumped up on his shoulders for another hurricanrana, but Akiyama grabbed her arms, crossed them over her chest, and lair her out with the Trinity Bomb!! Suddenly, from out of nowhere, a caped individual in a black top hat dived into the fray. He landed near Akiyama as he had Maria in a powerbomb pin position. “1...2...3!!!”“The freak??” Akiyama looked over to the man in black with suspicious eyes. Underneath the cape was the usual referee outfit, but the dark haired man wore a white mask over his eyes. “Who the freak are you?” “I am he who appears out of the night to make the call that is right!” he said, pointing his finger to the sky. “I make the pin, so that you get the win! I AM...TUXEDO REF!!” “Son of a...” Akiyama shook his head in disbelief. Then...a huge yellow creature ran out towards him. “WE-EELLLL!! IT’S THE BIG BIRD!! IT’S THE BIG BAD BIRD TONIGHT, YA’ALL!!” Sure enough, the giant canary came storming towards the god. Akiyama laughed at him as he punched him straight in his face. The bird no sold the punch. Akiyama punched him again...but Big Bird still didn’t move. “Oh, hell.” A high pitched roar came out of Big Bird as he raised his hand high into the air and clamped it around Akiyama’s neck. Akiyama struggled hard as the Big Yellow Machine hoisted him into the air. Then, Akiyama suddenly kicked the 8 foot tall fowl in his beak, causing him to drop back down to the ground. Akiyama worked his knees over with kicks, dropping him down to the ground before nailing Big Bird with a Divine Sword! He made the cover... “1...2...3!!” Tuxedo Ref whipped out a small pocked ring bell from his cape and rang it. Akiyama stood back up only to be dropped back down by a shuffling side kick. He looked up to find Gordon standing there with one baseball glove on in his face. “What’s up now, suckah??” the bald black man began shaking his hand before dropping down to one knee. All the children began cheering. “Booker G with the Gordoroonie!!” they screamed! “The most electrifying move in Sesame Street!” Gordon rolled on top of his head and began spinning atop of his cue ball skull. He rolled back to his feet, right in time for a HAND OF FATE!! He slammed into a wall and fell on his face. Akiyama rolled him over as Tuxedo Ref made the count. “1...2...3!! Akiyama...3...Sesame Street...ZERO!!” Right on cue, the next entrance played. “YOU LOOK SO....GAY TO ME!!” From around the corner pranced the original tag team...Bert and Ernie. Both donning white bathrobes and headbands, they danced towards the NVG, doing a strip tease with their robes to the children. Just then, Bert dived at Akiyama and flashed him with full frontal felt nudity. “Auuugh!! Our eyes!!” Akiyama grabbed both of the Muppets by their throats and slammed them down with a blind Neo Void Tribulation! With temporary blindness, he draped his arms over the two dazed, half naked roommates. “1...2...3!!” Tuxedo Ref called. Akiyama rubbed his eyes as the Ream Team came and carted away Bert and Ernie. “Akiyama...4...Sesame Street...ZERO!!” Before the god could get his vision back entirely, a huge brown mass toppled him. He fell back against the wall, as Mr. Snuffalupagus backed his way in. The brown elephant type thing let out a trumpet toot from his trunk. Akiyama regained his sight just in time to catch a face full of Snuffy @ss! “Oooooh!” the children laughed. “Snuffalupagus Phatu with the Snuff-face!” Akiyama rolled out from under the monster coughing up a storm. He turned around to see Snuffy charging head on for him. Akiyama suddenly turned back, leaned backwards, and locked his hand around the monster’s huge throat. His feet skidded against the ground from the force, but Akiyama proved to stop the charging Snuffalupagus. Then he leaped backwards and clocked Snuffy right between the eyes with a Crescent Moon Knocker!! The mammoth tilted over to his side, and Akiyama made a loose pin on him with his foot. “1...2...3!! Akiyama ...5...Sesame Street...ZERO!!” As Akiyama turned around to face Yuu, he was knocked flat on his back by a green spinning heel kick. Above him stood none other than... “K-D-F!! K-D-F!!” Kermit D. Frog...sure enough croaking out the trash talk while thumbing to himself. Then he leaped up on top of a second story windowsill, and thumbed out K-D-F one more time, before sailing off with the FIVE STAR FROG SPLASH~~NO!! Akiyama raised the knees before impact! Kermit rolled around on the street in pain as Akiyama climbed up on top of a nearby dumpster. He mocked Kermit with the thumbs pointing to himself. “N-V-G!!” he shouted before flying out with the Aurora Akiyama!! He hit it, and hooked the leg on the frog for the pin. Tuxedo got to work right away. “1...2...3!! Akiyama...6...Sesame Street...ZERO!!” Elmo was livid. He stormed up to Akiyama and stared him straight in his face. “ELMO HAS HAD ENOUGH!! NOW ELMO TAKE CARE OF YOU PERSONALLY!!” “What are you gonna do against us...little man?” Akiyama chuckled at him, but his chuckle died down as the red monster pulled something from behind his back. “Hey...what the freak??” Elmo strapped on a pair of goggles... “GET READY FOR THE MUPPO-AMERICAN MONSTER...ELMOGAWA!! WE’RE GOING TO KIIIIIIILLLLLL YOU!!” Akiyama and Elmogawa began trading punches back and forth. Then Elmogawa began gaining the upper hand with leaping drop kicks to his chest. Akiyama grabbed the legs of the monster in mid-dropkick and began spinning him around in the Cyclonica’s Flight. Before he could toss him away, a blue blur came running out from nowhere and clipped the god down to his knees. The kids all gasped in shock. “Gonzo!! He’s back!!! For the tenth time!!” Gonzo and Elmogawa were busy putting the boots to the ribs of a god on his knees. Akiyama suddenly let out a huge roar before grabbing both puppets by their throats and standing back up to his feet. Then... “EAT THE COOKIES NOW!!!!!” Ahead of them all stood the blue haired beast with his back turned to them. He held his arms stretched out, a cookie in each hand. “COOKIES INC!!” the kids gasped. Munching away on the chocolate chips he held, Cookie Monster ran into the fight and speared Akiyama down to the ground. All three of them began to kick away on the NVG street style. Then each of them take a turn with their finishers, Gonzo with the GONZO ASEHINO...Elmogawa with the TICKLE ME THERAPY...and Cookies Inc with the CHOCOLATE CHIP DROP!! Akiyama is out in the middle of the street, all three muppets standing over him. Elmogawa grabbed him by his ponytail and lifted his head up. “AND THAT’S WHAT WE CALL A SESAME STREET BEATDOWN! WHY?? BECAUSE...ELMO’S...EXCELLENT!!” Suddenly Akiyama sat straight up, his eyes blazing up and glassy. Yuu watched on horror. “Void Trance!!” she cried. Akiyama blasted all three Muppets with HAND OF FATEs before he got back to his feet. Gonzo ran right at him, but Akiyama caught him in the AKIYAMA LOCK in mid run!! Gonzo tapped right away, and Tuxedo Ref rang the bell. “Gonzo’s eliminated!!!” Then from behind, Cookies Inc tried to clothesline him. Akiyama swung him around in a 360 by his arm before locking him also in the AKIYAMA LOCK!! Cookie Monster tapped immediately!! “Cookies Inc’s eliminated!!!” From behind Elmogawa leaped on Akiyama’s back with the TICKLE ME THERAPY!! Akiyama tried to shake him off, but found it to be useless. He then fell back into the ground with the goggled Muppet on his back. He nipped back up and smashed him into the ground again. He did this a few times before getting Elmogawa off of him. Then he pounced on Elmo and began tearing him limb from limb. Tuxedo Ref rang the bell finally. “Elmogawa’s elmininated!! Your winner of the Sesame Street Slobberknocker...AKIYAMA!!!” There was no applause of cheer...only sobbing and crying. The children all stared at their former furry friend...his snow white guts spread out all over the street... one of the kids even had the audacity to puke at the sight. Another fainted as the god ripped off Elmo’s head. Yuu ran up to Akiyama along with the stage crew and security. “Cut!! CUT!!” the director called out. “You’re just calling cut now??” Yuu asked. The director shrugged sheepishly. “It’s a live show...gotta put out something!” “Akiyama! Snap out of it!!” Yuu shook the god as he reached into the Muppet’s head and pulled out the cotton brains, holding it up like a trophy. “Filthy stink children!!!” he growled. “We’ll make them all pay! We aren’t giving in this easy to the fogies that think they can muscle out the new generation of PCW!!!” Yuu helped him to his feet, and a small growl from the god scattered everyone except Yuu from around him. He glared down at the stuffing in his hands and tossed it aside. “Meh...some things aren’t different...both of them have fluff for brains...” dusting his hands off, he looked down to Yuu with a confident face. “Now...we have a press agent to banish... come along, Yuu...” With that said...the two walked off silently, leaving the carnage where it lied. From that day forward...no one would forget about the Legend of the Sesame Street Slobberknocker...it was an unmentionable day forever lived in infamy... But just like Fusion...Ha... Just like Sabotage... The Neo Void God was determined to beat the stuffing out of the opposition...
