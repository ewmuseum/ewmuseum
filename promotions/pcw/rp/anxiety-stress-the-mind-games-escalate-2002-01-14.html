Anxiety...Stress....The Mind Games Escalate

14 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Anxiety...Stress....The Mind Games Escalate Featuring: Tim Walden Date: January 14, 2002 Seething with the anger of robbery...The theft of my Cruiserweight Title, at the hands of two men, who to my knowledge had had little to do with each other but formed critical parts of what would have been a clever plan.... El Romantico had been a difficult opponent in the ring to this point, his skill obvious in our original confrontation and coming to the forefront once more on the night that my reign had ended...I knew I hadn't entirely earned my victory from him yet, but I at least showed that I wasn't going to stay down for three for anything less than the fullest that he could bring. I didn't count on his targeting what little heart I had though...I let him get to me enough to give him what he wanted to try to get him off my case, keep him from using my emotions against me any more, but I took the challenge too suddenly..I wasn't thinking clearly enough in the ring, the match went a little too near his way, and when I delivered the German Suplex to him...I thought too much about earning the pin on him, I let my own shoulders slip from my attention..Just once... Once was enough. That damn Duke had seen the opportunity, he wanted to do whatever he could to hurt the Front Office now and he'd probably planned upon the possibility an opportunity like this..Saddling me with the pain of having to deal with Romantico another couple of weeks, the aggravation of a title reign ended so early, it just wasn't fair....But how could I have expected fairness from one of the Rednecks? He was in this business for our pain now, and with his closest companion besieged constantly over the TV belt, he had to strike back on whatever fronts he could, and it just so happened that I was the weak link of the chain at the time... ...Was I the weak link of the Front Office? Gaz and Xavier won the tag titles, Brandi was getting the better of Teresa, and I...lost my belt. I hadn't managed to get my head straight then, but emotional weakness was still weakness, I'd gotten too distracted and left myself vulnerable....But I wasn't the weak link. I couldn't be. I'd taken my share of trouble before and gotten into a losing run thanks to that blasted shape, but I came out on top then...Romantico hadn't gone for me physically though, he went straight to making it personal, he almost knew that it was his best opportunity to get me enraged enough to battle, and that I couldn't fight at 100% then.. This wasn't good for me..I had so much stress to deal with now, it wasn't a matter of things that were out of my hands like Duke's selfishness in the Office or the HIT's being responsible for a losing streak...This was about my emotions, this was about doing well for them, as the taxman I was mostly responsible for the poor finances of PCW, and in combination with my lack of big wins lately, I was going to look like the weak link either way...I had to turn things around, Xavi hadn't said anything about replacing me but I knew the possibility was there, when someone gets useless you just ditch 'em... I couldn't afford to be useless, I knew that they wouldn't hesitate to dump me if I wasn't bringing the skills they needed, Brandi wouldn't fight for me at this point, Xavier wouldn't keep me around with the money I was making now, I'd be stuck, out on my own, caught between an Office that didn't need me and a federation full of people who would hate me by association... I had to make myself useful. If I could beat El Romantico, I would be useful..Hell, to put up a good fight in next Tuesday's main event would be enough to show I could still beat people, I was still owed a Continental Title shot and that meant that I was a great wrestler, I beat the HIT every time the two of us had a decisive finish on Pay-Per-View and THAT meant I was a great wrestler..El Romantico wasn't better than me, he didn't DESERVE to fight for the Cruiserweight Title...I'd had my time with it, but it wasn't worth hanging onto forever, there were still titles with higher historical value..Nobody remembered Jake Keeton's title reign, but everyone remembers the Kannon-Duke Continental feud even today, people remembered Ivan-Jeremy-Frisco for the belt, Ivan Stanislav and the Chef clashing for it, the Mexican and Chet...the Continental wasn't that far away from the top level of notice... It could wait another while. Romantico is still on my case no matter what I do, he'd just cost me the opportunity, those title scenes are so clogged that I'd never get back into the hunt anyway..I still had the Cruiserweight Title in reach, I could become a two-time champ...Just like so many of the greats, Kid Wonder, Miyagawa, Mexi, Juan B--Well, he wasn't actually 'great', but still, I could be remembered as a two-time champion if I could win this... Every belt just makes for a new opportunity to get the spotlight, impress the crowds and gain the fame.... ...I could use some more fame right now. But the only way to get there was to fight for it at this point...And the only way to win the fight was to try and keep Romantico out of my head...Or was it? He'd aggravated me...Maybe I could use the anxiety, the irritation of having my emotions somehow visible to my enemy and spoken to the masses, no confirmation or denial or anything beyond mere rumours to their ears...But everything to my mind..The paranoia that had lingered so long of having it revealed was finally brought to fruition, but while I could do my part to lie to those near me in my angst, I also had the ability to bring this agony out against him, only my intense emotions would translate below the surface into intensity in my wrestling, a viciousness not seen from me since Trick or Treat IV...Yes, that's what I'd do..He had only fought to his relative success when I was distracted, I could still destroy him if I blocked out the distraction and fought WITH the pain... And thus doing, I would bring the pain to him, pulling the title nearer to my grasp and bringing me back to the forefront... ...And he would know it. He would feel my pain coming right back at him...He had chosen the wrong man to mess with. The mental war could be won on his part, but it would be little more than a war of attrition... I would have his defeat physically. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
