Tubular Tykes Banquet - Part 1

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 12, 2002 [The Tubular Tykes Foundation was started in 1999, via Tommy's agent. Ralph, Tommy's agent, said it would look great for Tommy's image to sponsor less fortunate children. During that period of time, our Tubular dude was freshly retired from the surfing world and had made many millions during his surfin' days. The public was very upset with Tommy because he was basking in his millions, as if he were some kind of selfish pig. Tommy WAS being a selfish pig, but it's not like it was being done on purpose...it just sort of happened. He spent money on things that never would matter to life. Televisions that could be voice activated, motion detector lights, a waterbed that is a massager, stereo system that has speakers all throughout his beachhouse, and, of course, he bought his own private beach. He still had plenty of millions left over to just sit and watch. Ralph knew the key was to get into the hearts of the general public, show them that Tommy was a HECK OF A NICE COMPADRE! It was just a foundation at first, but somehow it grew. The people of California spread the word to everyone who would listen to a sob story. Pretty soon they built a complex in downtown Los Angeles that would house many 'tykes' and give them a real shot at becoming future, radical leaders of the world. There are two more centers in the works in San Francisco and Sacremento. Ralph is pleased, and so is Brandon Higgins III who is the real brains behind the operation. He makes sure everything is in place and that all the paperwork gets finished. Tommy signs papers, stamps stuff, and takes credit for it all...as if he even cares. Tonight is a big banquet dinner thrown by the Tubular Tykes foundation to give a yearly report of what's in store. Expansions, new programs, and success stories. The media is all over this, because who doesn't like to see a retard succeed?] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Look at the dude in the football helmet...HE'S TOTALLY GNARLY! [The banquet hall is huge as there are tons of people on the dance floor, swaying back and forth to Whitney Houston. The men in their black and white tuxedo's and the women with different colored evening gowns. Rich people just trying to make themselves feel better by donating a large hunk of cash to an organization that was started to make a certain surfer look like he cared. Tommy DOES care, whether he looks gnarly or not...] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: The one that looks like he's screwing the punch bowl is the best, man. [Tommy, dressed in a blue blazer, with a yellow dress shirt, and blue slacks, looks across the hall to notice the very slow child doing a pelvic thrust toward the table leg where the punch bowl is located] TTT: Dude, I think he IS humping the punch bowl!! [Harry's eyes bug wide open] Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!!! [Harry is sitting next to his half brother wearing a T-shirt that has a tuxedo spray painted on it. A pair of leather pants accompanies his distasteful T-shirt, and the cowboy boots really aren't helping it much either. Tommy doesn't even really notice as he takes a swig of his bourbon on the rocks. Harry sits on the bar stool, looking as if he's anxious about something, with his hands rubbing on his knees, rubbing sweat off his hands] TTT: Dude, what in the name of nervousness are you worried about? [Harry looks at Tommy with a very frightened look, almost like a timid dog] Harry: You know what I'm thinking about, man. [Tommy nods with a smile, now knowing what Harry is thinking about. The announcement that Tommy will be making in a short half an hour. Everybody is dancing around, happy, and not knowing what the impact of the announcement will have on everybody. The dancing is going to stop and the smiles are going to fade. Could there be a totally bogus riot? Could there be butcher knives thrown immediately after the announcement? Could Chewbacca really look like a human being if you shaved him? Those questions will be answered, Harry thinks to himself, all except that last one though. That one won't be figured out until he gets his hands on the dirty Chewbacca!] TTT: Har, there is absolutely nothing to worry about. I make the bodacious announcement, I walk out of here, and then we go cruisin' for babes. [Harry holds up his right index finger to Tommy] TTT: I'LL go cruisin' for babes, while you prepare for Sandy Wong's coming. [Harry looks confused] Harry: I'll be the one coming, man... [Tommy rolls his eyes and shakes his head, taking another swig of his bourbon] TTT: Of course, compadre. [The two men stand in silence for a few moments, just watching the banquet. Whitney Houston has faded and now a classical piece, something by that Beethoven dude, is playing. The people sway more slowly...if that is even possible, and they smile. Babes holding tightly onto their dudes, knowing full well the night of whoopie that will be happening. The fact that there are children in this place that will go back to their Tubular Tyke headquarters to be medicated and told that they are 'special'. Well, after the announcement that may not be happening, dudes and dudettes] TTT: I really hope to get this over with quickly, Har. I have a most BOGUS set of matches coming up and, of course, that big match against The End at Decimation. I have a lot on my plate now and I don't need to be at this thing. Ralph could have made the heinous announcement while I was at home working on the treadmill, making sure my endurance and stamina stayed at an all time high. [Harry chuckles to himself, and has decided to turn on 'Corny Mode'] Harry: I had a lot on my plate earlier...then I ate it, man! [Tommy looks over at his former tag team partner and rolls his eyes once again. That's all you can really do in a situation like that. Whether you even open your bodacious mouth and tell Harry the joke sucked, it's not going to work out that well. Harry just won't listen...because...let's face it...he's too damn stoned] Harry: That humping kid is coming over here, man!!! [The little boy with an odd limp begins dragging his way over toward Tommy and Harry. His large green eyes are filled with such joy, obviously the table leg must have been good. Harry tenses his entire body up, ready to lash out if this unbitchin' kid makes a wrong move. Tommy has had four bourbons already and is much too relaxed to worry about what the little compadre is going to do] Humping Boy: Hi, TOMMEEE!! [The boys voice somehow carries over the classical music, making all the people look with happy faces. All of them thinking it's cute that the boy has approached the man who has made all of this happen. The tubular dude who put his money on the line and built them facilities all over California so that they can have it good, have a REAL chance at life] TTT: Hey, little dude. How are things going for you tonight? [The boy gives a crooked looking thumbs up with a stupid smile on his face] Humping Boy: All DAT!! AND A BAG OF POTATO CHIPS, TOMEEE! [The people laugh as if a famous comedian just said the most hilarious thing in the world. The child looks all around him, realizing that he's just done something cute and funny. Now he's got them wrapped on his little, retarded finger] TTT: BODACIOUS! How are things working out for you in the Tubular Tykes center? Learning a lot of radical things? [The boy nods, still holding up his crooked thumbs up] TTT: Tubular. [Harry leans over to Tommy and whispers into his ear] Harry: He's looking at you like he wants to.... TTT: Har, stop now...because that's just BOGUS. [Harry leans back to his tense position, keeping close watch on the Humping Boy] Humping Boy: I know the A, B, C's, TOMEEE. Wanna hear dem? [Tommy shrugs] TTT: Sure. Humping Boy: A...B...C...DEE...E...F...GEE...H...I...J...K... [The boy then goes from his stomach down to his crotch area] Humping Boy: LMNO PEEEE!!! [Everyone laughs hysterically and the boy jumps up and down as if he had just hit the lottery. The laughter just continues as some of the old people look like they are about to take a dump in their adult diapers. The boy laughs and swings his crooked arms around wildly. Tommy just nods with a smile as Harry still sits tensed up, waiting for that wrong move] Humping Boy: Q..R..S...T..U..V..W..X..Y..and Z. Now I know my AB CEEE's! Next time won't you sing with me!!!! [The people clap loudly as if he were The Dave Matthews Band and had completed a 4 hour set in Central Park, with a worldwide audience watching. The boy gathers in the moment, sucking up all the cheers of the crowd. Tommy lightly claps as he takes a drink of his bourbon, and Harry stares at the child with contempt] Humping Boy: SING WIT ME, TOMEEE!! [Tommy looks up at the giant clock above the main entrance of the hall and notices the time reads '8:30'. It's time for the totally gnarly announcement. Soon he'll be at his beach house with three hot babes at his side. A night of Yahtzee, a night of lust, and a TOTALLY bodacious, much needed massage from his waterbed. He's been looking forward to that all day] TTT: I have to make my announcement, little dude. Once I'm finished we'll have to do a most excellent rendition of the ABC's! [Humping Boy nods as Tommy begins making his way toward the stage. The boy walks away and Harry completely loosens up with a large sigh. Whew, the little far out humping kid is gone...he doesn't have to worry. A middle aged man approaches Harry with his empty glass held out] Middle aged man: Can you get me another bourbon, bus boy? [Harry looks at the man as if he's insane, picks up his beer, opens the man's shirt, and fills the shirt with beer] Middle aged man: I'VE NEVER!!!! [Fade to black] The fate of Tubular Tykes hangs in the balance. Tommy's big announcement will shake California forever!
