The King and I in the Dairy Queen.

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The King and I in the Dairy Queen. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 1/14/03 Duke Williams is at a dairy queen down in Nashville, Tennessee. Duke’s dressed like Johnny Cash at Folsom Prison in 1963 wearing nothing but black. He orders up his favorite cone, which is vanilla dipped in chocolate. Duke drools with anticipation as he waits for his soft serve treat. This little trip is going to run him a little late in meeting up with Tomas for a game of pool at Tootsies Orchard Lounge. Williams: Damn hoss I love how you’ll do that little twirl at the top. Dqdude: We our trained professionally to make that happen. Williams: Damn hoss where do I sign up to go to DQ school. I got a soft serve machine in my house but it ever comes out right. DqDude: It’s all in the wrist. Williams: My ex-wife always told me it was all in tongue but what do I know? DqDude: Two dollars. Duke pulls out his custom made =w= money clip and delegates the appropriate sum to the cashier. Duke wastes no time diving into his treat like a nympho would a twat. During his deep dive, out of the corner of his eye, a starange site stuns him. He quickly turns to the left and does a double take. Williams: Well I’ll be damned. Sitting in the back corner booth is a man in his sixties who looks exactly like the king of rock and roll Elvis Presley. Duke walks over to the believed to be dead king. Williams: Can I sit down? The stranger with the long side burns snaps his fingers and points to the booth. Elvis?: Help your self there man. I can use a little conversation. The Malice Man sits down slowly as he looks over the aged Icon. Williams: Are you……. The side burns wearing stranger snaps his fingers to stop Duke. Elvis?: Don’t be cruel man, I’m whoever you believe I am. Like Santa Clause baby only with more shuck and jive. The stranger curls his lip as he fixes his gray hair. Elvis: Now shut your mouth and lay your burdens on the king man. Williams: This jerk off Xavier Kannon has really been pissing my the fuck off. Elvis: Whoa man the king doesn’t need that language. Why is this Kannon fellow burning your britches. Williams: He thinks he’s better then me and he abuses his power. Elvis: I knew this cool cat named Jerry Lee who thought he was better then the king. Nobodies better then the king. In the end everybody remembers me and he’s just a faded 45. Williams: So what are you saying? Elvis: I’m saying you and skyler need to pummel Kannon and Rush to prove a point. Let the world know your number one baby. Don’t forget it man. Number one. The king snaps his fingers and uses his index finger to show number one as he heads off to the bathroom never to return. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
