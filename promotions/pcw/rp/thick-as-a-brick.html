Date: not entered With the untimely death of Michelle Branch, Flaymin found himself in a very long period of mourning. Yes, they were the longest 6 hours of his life….. [Scene opens in the foyer at PCW Towers.Flaymin sits back against the comfy lounge near the reception swaying his head from side to side. Meanwhile, the elevator doors open up and out steps The Pimply Faced Intern Kid.He notices Flaymin in the distance and walks over to offer his condolences] TPFIK: Hey, I’m sorry about your loss. [Flaymin nervously begins to search through his pockets] Flaymin: Dammit, where the hell did I put my car keys? TPFIK: Uh… I wasn’t talking about your keys.I was like, you know referring to Michelle… and stuff. Flaymin: What? TPFIK: Look dude, I understand that you’re trying to be all macho and all that but its ok to show your feelings. [Flaymin reaches over and slaps the The Pimply Faced Intern Kid over the head] Flaymin: Dammit boy, what did I tell you about watching Oprah?You watch one episode and all of a sudden you’re experiencing a freakin’ spiritual awakening! TPFIK: No, no.I meant the fact that Michelle has… you know, passed on. Flaymin: Oh right!Between you and me, I think that’s a real problem of hers.I mean she’s passing gas faster than Rosie O’Donnell woofs down 12 cheeseburgers. [The Pimply Faced Intern Kid pauses.He frowns at Flaymin for a moment…. And then frowns some more] TPFIK: Are you sure you’re ok? Flaymin: Well my head’s spinning a little but its nothing major. TPFIK: Nothing major??After everything that’s happened!?! [Flaymin raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: Are we talking about the same thing here? TPFIK: I sure hope not! Flaymin: Uh… I think I’ll be going now. TPFIK: Alright dude, you still need that time to grieve. Flaymin: You’re right, that was some of the best booze I ever had. [Flaymin walks away from The Pimply Faced Intern Kid and heads towards the elevator. On the way he is stopped by a woman who gives Flaymin a hug and hands him some roses. Woman: I’d like you to put these on her coffin. Flaymin: I think they’d look better in a vase actually. Woman: You mean Michelle is going to be cremated? Flaymin: For sure, she just loves my cream- full of natural goodness…. Not to mention a little stickiness! Woman: How many urns do you own? Flaymin: I didn’t earn anything and if I didn’t earn it I don’t see how I could own it?Geez, woman you just don’t make any sense. Woman: You poor thing, you must be still coming to grips with the facts that she is gone for good. Flaymin: She went where? Woman: Hopefully to see St. Peters at the pearly gates. Flaymin: That little tramp! Hmmm, ‘Pearly Gates’….. is that the new nightclub that opened downtown? Woman: I doubt it. [Angered, Flaymin punches his fist on the wall] Woman: Good…. Good, let out all that frustration. [Flaymin storms off towards the exit of the building but is abruptly cut-off by some teenage girls] Flaymin: Out of my way ladies, I’ve got to get to the pearly gates ASAP! Teenage Girl: You can’t do that.Not at least until St. Peters gives you authorization. Flaymin: Who does this guy think he is?Ain’t no one got the right to pimp my woman except me! Teenage Girl: I just wanted to know when the funeral will be held? Flaymin: Well I’ll have to find him first, but it shouldn’t take me very long to get the job done. Teenage Girl: You mean you are actually digging the grave? Flaymin: I’ve already built the damn coffin! Teenage Girl: That’s so sweet. Flaymin: Now would you know which way it is to the pearly gates? [The teenage girls point up] Flaymin: You mean on the second floor? Teenage girl: Heaven. Flaymin: That new burger store near the highway? [The girls give Flamin an odd stare.Flaymin simply rolls his eyes] Flaymin: Idiots. Teenage Girl: You do know what happened….don’t you? Flaymin: Oh no!Oh no!I should have seen the signs! Teenage Girl: I’m sorry. Flaymin: So am I, I sure as hell am not ready to be a father again!All the planning….Putting together the crib….The sleepless nights….The bruised nipples…. Then again, the baby food is pretty nice! [Scene fades to black as Flaymin rushes out of the building and heads towards the nearest ‘Baby’s R Us’ for a frantic shopping spree unaware that Michelle is already deceased….]
