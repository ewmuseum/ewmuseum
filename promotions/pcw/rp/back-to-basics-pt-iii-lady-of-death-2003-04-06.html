Back to Basics Pt III: Lady of Death

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th April 2003 -[Continued]- “No!” Jade had to cover her mouth to stop herself from alerting the whole dingy alleyway. The rain bounced down from the heavens as Jade let her head fall back down to eye level. Speaking of eyes Jades white one seemed to be finally settling back down to its dull glow rather than shiny sun brightness. Jades breath relaxes back to a small hum as she realises her surroundings once again. The thing which remained dormant most of the time in her head had decided to pay one of its helpful visits. Mani is in the truck and well on his way from the scene and Jade is still alive! After receiving what could only be explained as the mother of all premonitions. That voice which rested inside her was not always one to work at the convenience except when the spirit knew the vessel, Jade, would be broken, destroyed if it did not intervene. Then the most realistic and brilliant light would engulf her mind and allow her to see things a little more clearly than perhaps she was. It certainly increased the activity of incite within her normally clumsy mind. Of course now it was a lot less of a problem with the whole possessing thing she used to have to put up with. These little visions and momentary outburst of strength, speed and insight were kind of little rewards she had, it could also explain a supernatural horniness which does not need to be mentioned right now. Now Mani would not have the chance to strike at Jades friends while they were all in a convenient same place and Jade could sleep a lot easier knowing she was alive, knowing she would still wake up in the morning. Still something did not settle right on her head and that was the fact that he was still breathing, he was still walking, he was still ruining people’s lives and it tore her apart on the inside. Just thinking of all of the anguish that man causes daily ripped at her innards. Jade decided, similarly to what Mani his self had decided to bring her here with the brick through Lacuna’s window, she knew she would have to send him a message. Something which would get him riled up like the proverbial cat. She had just the plan. The four men who escorted Mani out of the building and into the army vehicle also accompanied him wherever he was going as the car burned out of sight, flicking gravel and dust up into the air and huge skid marks along the floor. Jade now had the opportunity to get a better look of the area before carrying out her intentions. There were only two clumsy oafs on the front door and perhaps a way to get a better idea of how many people are in there you need to eliminate the people from the front door, perhaps get them in a panic. “Now the party really starts.” Jade sighed collected her thoughts and tapping her fingers on her perched legs. Jade grabs a stone from beside her and throws it down an alley to her side. The stone rebounds off a bin and both men go to investigate, expecting some punk kid to be able to beat on. Jade bends over with her arms straightened behind her back. She leaps off the ground and grabs the bar above her head. Her legs fold and move above her as she rolls onto the building of the grocery store which is on the other side of the building. Her eyes look with focus at her target; that being the roof opposite her which led into the club probably infested with these cheap little ‘Panthers’ people checking her entire building. These were really the lowest of the low when it came to odd job men, the cleaners as they are often referred to. Mani was obviously spending more money on his Armani suits and designer head wax to be bothering about a silly little thing such as the club. “I see I am wearing my climbing boots, how convenient.” She looks on with concern at her knee high heeled boots after seeing the ten foot gap between buildings. Jade looked around at the area and there seemed to be only one way she could get there…jump. Well that was until she saw the telephone wire at the top of a large mast, it led right to the building. Jade skulked across the top of the roof as her overcoat flapped in the wind. Her eyes glowed with excitement as she grasped the poll of the telephone mast and shimmied up the poll to the top. Once there she set about taking the belt to the overcoat and throwing it over the top so she could simply slide down the telephone wire to the roof of the club. Jade rolls when she hits the veranda and rolls with a gentle thud against the wall. She would have wallowed in the pain had it not been for the thud which probably had alerted the slimy little odd job people. Her head lifted as she slinked across the gravel stone slabs Jade had made up here. Her eyes seemed a little off focus to say the least but she needed to be out of sight on the bare veranda and it was not going to be easy. Jade first tried to jump onto the roof of the third floor and could not reach, even after jumping from the railing. She decided to settle with a safe dangling from a balcony above two meat heads. The door opened and four men rushed onto the veranda with their Mexican accents and silly little Walther hand guns. “Do they all have guns? I’m gonna get shot if they really don’t put those things away.” Jade thought to herself as an aching in her arms began to set in. The door slammed and Jade finally pulled herself backwards over the bar and to her feet. There was a man with a gun pointed right between her nose. “Oh crap. You wouldn’t dare! Do you know how much it costs to get this thing fixed?” She pointed to her nose abruptly pushing the gun out of her face. The mans hand is shaking as he points the gun back at her face. “You know you are pretty insistent with that thing. Anyone would think you’d develop some testicles and actually shoot me instead of being a little cowardly whore. I mean you point that thing at my head and you haven’t even took the safety off it you dumb little bitch.” The man had in fact taken the safety off but it was the distraction she needed to slam the heel of her boot into the bridge of his nose. “Fucking bitch!” He cries as he holds his nose in its excruciating pain. Blood pouring through the holes his hands have. “Love you too sweetie.” Jade grunted as the side of her toe blasted the crony under the chin, sending him reeling over the top of the balcony. Jade looks down at the men below her as her eye begins to kick in again. The men squint to look up at her. Almost as if there was the very light of a flashlight shining right next to their retina. Jade took a deep gasp of breath and swung herself to the alleyway below. When she lands on the two men she instantly struggles to her feet and jams a can into one of the men’s throat. The others knuckle is shatter between Jade’s boot and the door before Jade uses the hand as a push off to sling her leg into the head of the remaining man and knocks his head sideways, another loud crunch follows. The metal rusty door is forced open as a barrage of these filthy little runts comes out at Jade guns emblazing her arms are thrown up in the air to protect her but to no avail. Jade falls into another crimson pit of eternal rest. -[Fade]- The scene opens up at the side of a deep grave. A white haired woman beats the ground furiously as Mani her cousin throws her friends into the grave, their own blood spills over the engraved coffin. Once again it reads ‘Jade.’ -[Fade]- Jade gasps to life as her head raises from her head smashing against the door. Her eye slowly settling. “Again?”
