...school daze...

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.02.02 'For thy brother, you pull out all of the stops, for evil, you pull everything to hurt thy brother's keeper.' CIV: Voices 'Long ago, you were the right one starring down this path were you not?' I could not answer the voice with words, rather I nodded my head in acknowledgment of where I have been and what I have done. I remembered being confronted by such a voice with the outlook of a beast resembling a demon, the devil itself. I remembered the talks, every time a new subject brought along a new faith to place myself into. 'I fear the time grows near for you, when this entire existence swallows you whole and then your back at step one asking questions of who, what, when, where, why, and how. The time where you happen to truly discover what you are...' 'What do you actually mean by that? My true purpose? Who I truly am?' 'Of course you don't understand what I am or who I am, but in due time you will and it all will come full circle. You have to use your time wisely and not make judgments on petty nonsense and take that extra second to look at both sides of the looking glass. There is a cause and an effect for everything we carry out upon this plane. The challenges you have experienced in the past were nothing but test to see if you would actually get here. You will learn the answers to the questions that you have asked in the back of your mind since day one. That is what I mean by the time grows near. That is why I say you will discover what makes you who you truly are…' 'What do you mean by the challenge that I'll face is not a battle with the darkness from afar, but a battle that will test me and push me to the edge of sanity and driving focus? I don't get this, I don't get anything you say now. I wish I knew just what you were talking about.' 'Those are answers you must seek on your own. In time you will understand the words that I speak. Now it is time for you to walk your destined path.' 'My Path?' 'Yes, you're path to success over your victims. Haven't you told me that before?' 'Well yes, but never has success come to me. The last time it did was November 30th. And even then I didn't know where it would end, I know this.' 'You took the road of evil and made it a playground, making your victims more precise and even for your own gain. Take Jeremy Howard for example. You kidnapped his brother right after a match that the two of you were having. Then out of pure rage to get you inside of the ring, he calls for a World title match. When you make Krayzie angry, he is playing right into your trap.' 'Seems like this trap is becoming more of a burden on myself.' 'What? You used to enjoy this with a passion. Remember Xavier Kannon and his blunders back in NTICW?' 'How could I forget? It was the talk of the entire federation and at one point, I think the offers for my acting skills got me some low budget roles in flims.' 'You don't have the chemistry you had once before.' 'Because, it's a whole new playing field.' 'Stop making excuses for yourself, you know what you can or cannot do and in this case, you seem afraid to take after Jeremy Howard and take gold away from him.' 'No, fear will not conquer me over another human being I promise you that. Howard knows defeat for titles, you can ask Tim Walden and if Krayzie gets the job done Friday, it's my turn Sunday.' 'This is your path?' 'Exactly, and Krayzie is the one to start it off.' The voice was gone. I stood in the darkness I had been in ever so many times before. I turned around to see nothing. When I returned my attention where it previously had been there were three paths in front of me as I suddenly found myself in the center of an unfamiliar area. It was wooded and as I looked around myself, I found a total of twelve different paths. Each of them led down a different road and in a different direction… I hadn't the slightest clue to which direction I should go down. A feeling surged through his body as I looked up at the sky, seeing it had become night. I looked down at my wrist, seeing my watch as both hands moved onto the twelve. It was midnight and I didn't know which path I should choose. As I looked back up at the stars I saw the North Star, Polaris. I decided to go north, not knowing why other than the fact that I could follow the North Star the entire way. Unknowingly was that this was just the start of a long and treacherous journey. **** What reason did I have to smile, after all I should be mad at Amy for costing me the Television title for cripes sakes. I mean I know she didn't mean bad things by it but her eye sight because of it caused a delayed reaction from the referee and me a titleless man. 'Shit.' I told myself trudging along the ends of concrete to the outside of the Wisconsin. The hills only were noticable as a car rolled past them en route to Illinois and finally to Missouri and the fabled town of Saint Louis, named after King Louis XIV and founded by the explorer Robert La Salle, who also received a college to be named after him. What happens to him? His crew kills him. Amy walked right beside me trying to make up for lost apologies when it should have occurred time ago. 'I'm sorry, I didn't understand that you had the title won. I'm so stupid.' she continued repeating to herself, I placed my arm around her, citing she had done nothing wrong but hit a mistake button. 'It's okay, beside I get to go for the Continental title Sunday at March Madness. It's a step up from Television and is the second rated belt in the entire federation.' she smiled when I told her that, always realizations in my own success. Against Jeremy Howard, I forgot to tell her that since well, I wanted to get it all out of the way, this Miyagawa nonsense, and the fact that its the rubber match between myself and Jeremy. We aren't putting people to sleep as the match begins. But I wonder, will Jeremy even be ready mentally for me at Madness? The last time he remotely was inside of a Hell in a Cell was a cage match with Ivan Stavinslav, whose picked up his tail and ran out of PCW. But Ivan had power, and I have speed and finess. True I might be skinny, but look at most of the skinny guys you know that can pull the insane things they do. Krayzie could do them, with breaking bones in his body. But, for Justin? I make damn sure Krayzie brings his best towards my attention Sunday. Krayzie must, or his brother will never be in his care... Bobby Stormbringer, well I proved to him at Sabotage what a force I am to withstand his best attempts for victory, yet I come back and out do him at his own game. It's Stormbringer's turn to face me and later who knows? Darkstar? Kannon? The possibilites are endless from those two names and Stormy, he's gettting something Tuesday night, I guarantee it. Maybe it was good for her to understand what somethings meant and didn't mean, but for whatever reason, I was thinking to pay a visit to Justin atleast one more time before he is strapped to that chair cheering his brother on. The family bond thing is something I envy within them, they have something I didn't love from a family. Soon I'll have my own and then, we won't be equal anytime soon, but we will be equal in a part. It was falling apart, the unity that first brought us together, but when I understand about it, I couldn't smile at that day, not for a simple second since I was just coming back from a hard day at N.Y.U. listening to a Jeremy Howard esque sensi - teacher in Japanese who spoke of great quotes of the twentieth century. When he spoke of the things in which many thought were passe' and were of old generation, I nodded along with them. We barely gave glances at each other, something like the Jeremy Howard/ Jessica thing, but soon we found ourselves talking regularly and myself using inexperienced charm since I hadn't gone out with anybody since junior year of high school (constant time on the old Playstation as in playing Metal Gear Solid) So it was new to me, the college thing, just like wrestling...it was something I'll barely forget... CV: Back in the Day The sun rested high beneth the swelling clouds and the weaving winds as I stood outside of the university front office clutching to the handle of a black Jeans Sport backpack. My hair wasn't the same mixture of colors it was right now and my eyes didn't hang droopy like they are now, rather cool jet black hair and attentive eyes were more my look then. The song, 'Kryptonite' by Three Doors Down was becoming a smash and all I could think about was how the hell am I going to get to first period. New York University held an opus to any person who was able to preform at the highest of expectations and this was no different. Speaking from today, I could truly say that there were a ton of jock's who would walk up to girls and entice them to make love, especially if a senior on the football team walked up to an unsuspecting freshman. It was common mating season for two semsters four years. Wearing a white Offspring T-Shirt that was long around the sleeves and some black FUBU jeans which were baggy, I hopped on a Hawk endorsed Skateboard and started pulling off tricks along the rails and staircases of the place causing some to get attention to me. As soon as I heard a long group of people cheering, I know I had them in my hand, only he wasn't apart of that. While I was busy doing my thing all along the school on my board, she was leaned back against the wall of the Gymnasium where the basketball team practiced, talking to a blocked headed boy who looked a few years older than her. She wore her hair a little shorter than then and proved that she was a definite red head and she wore a red long sleeve shirt that showed a perfect wasit line and mid riff, flocked by a long blue skirt ragged with the ends tied up along the waistline. My god, she was a knockout from the start. I asked one of my friends one of the guys who looked at my skating preformance. 'Whose the girl talking to the block head?' He replied to me simply as if the words rolled off of his lips and he'd been dreaming the girl in his mind nightly. 'Amy Falan.' I smiled to myself, thinking of walking over there and conversing, wooing her away from block-head, but soon as I saw his jacket compared to my skinny size, I knew it would take more than that to woo her. But as the sun came forward and the bell ringed for our next class to begin, I was beginning to wonder just who is the beauty that comes into my life right now? I wonder....
