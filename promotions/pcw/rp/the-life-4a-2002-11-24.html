The Life - 4a

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.24.02 He left Houston in a mix of moods, quietly he rode on the plane, only thinking of what he was going to do to the buffoon known as the PCW Roster. He felt betrayed, and made a complete fool of for going down to Houston, just to find out about the man he was facing tonight. The train rolled down the tracks, clicking back and forth along the steel as he listened, deeply concentrated on the matches at Alliances, maybe unlike his friend Statuz Quo - he had something personal to deal with them!, and the antics of Pranks With Attitude. It was them they reformed and began in more plot of how to beat Playaz Inc. The companion of his, Duzza happened to be raving and jabbering towards a female attendant about how Fat Albert happened to receive a hernia for falling inside of a manhole. It didn't matter, just the fact. Closing his eyes, he placed his hands on his chest, reclined back and his mind in terminal hell. Was he just a one-sided man, who only thought about his own career and well being? He'd deny every thing that claimed any bad press towards him - it was heelish nature to do so. He was toying somewhat, with the mind of Gaz Green, Nerezza, hell everyone that had a match against him tonight - pleasing him somewhat, but still he knew deep down he hadn't toyed with them enough. Maybe they didn't want to feel the brush of Dante's wrath once more - he'd felt it enough already. Then it rose onto his mind - what's next? In his heart, almost as black as the death of a rose - the cat didn't feel like giving back. Not to anymore he felt didn't deserve it. Those people - the haters and self proclaimed 'next this and that' happened to be on his marking spot, wanting into his very own world. Not likely, he thought to himself. He may have been at time cowardice, and even malice towards people, never one of those men to back down from a challenge and this wasn't an exception to that rule. The roster had crossed the border of where people should stay, and he's was on terms with an agreement. Between himself, and the man standing across from him as the bell sounds. Here it was, on the heels of Alliances, another Pay Per View spectacle put on by Primetime Championship Wrestling. A year wiser, and a year smarter. The reflections in his heart were damp, muggy as the air around the control car continued to swirl and rain drops peltered the car as it rode down the twisting track. He growled and removed his hands from the base of his heaving chest, bawling them up in a fist, and thunking against the cold plastic seating him. CLUNK! CLUNK! CLUNK! In all of his conflicts, maybe it was needed to stay and collect this thoughts about things. Maybe this would cheer up, and conjure up any steam of happiness in this man. Losing your very own sense of a smile can be damaging, and at times - mind wrecking. 'Stupid spoon.' he mutters his first words since getting on the train. Bending back a plastic spoon given to him by the traveler's on train dinner, he fights it, before snapping it in two. The meal wasn't kosher, rather stringy and out of date. 'Feeble people..stupid cracker made meals.' He ran his tongue along his bottom lip, before reclining back once more, sitting in a fitful rage over what he could make the most out of another trip to San Antonio. Odd how you could be around the world, and yet never experience the short obstacles in front of you. 'Championships are won in the trenches,' he quietly quipped to himself, before lowering his head with a short gruff nod. 'Tonight is going to be no different my friends...' he trailed off once more. As the railroad car tested its aerodynamics by twisting along with the matching track - the speaker built into his Walkman heads phones began blasting at a low rate, loud enough for people in the row to listen to it. 'This Means War', a play on Ozzy Osbounes' 'Ironman' hit with Black Sabbath. The only difference was, Busta Rhymes was its main performer and with a drum thumping beat to drop bars on, it was enough for Dante to relax towards. The words ran out in some odd oedipal essence to his mind - the right source of what he needed to be doing. Much like the chronicles of Saint Juan Diego, Dante was on a mission all his own and almost everyone had to experience from it. His daughter, his co-workers in both industries and the love of his life, but right now - only a few of those mattered. The entire roster was deeply on his mind, as the train rolled down silently like a steel colored ghost, riding highways and seeking victims. 'Warriors, the silly shyster bastards. Those muthaf^ckas can't happen to monotone or profess just what I know.' he chuckled to himself, before the track 'Critically Acclaimed' from his Street Manifesto CD popped into his ears. The lyrics stuck out, a reason why he made it the second single off his album 9.1 million copies sold in nine months - debut album. Its what the roster wanted, something Dante Jackson happened to have. Critical Acclaim.
