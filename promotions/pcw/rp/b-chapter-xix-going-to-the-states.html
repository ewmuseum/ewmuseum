b>Chapter XIX: Going to the States…

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-5-03 Chapter XIX: Going to the States… The pig smell in the box car lingers in the air as Mark gets up from beneath the hay, the only thing that can keep him warm. His legs tingle like nails are in them as they have fallen asleep. He waits for the tingling to go away as he can hear the pigs getting up as well. The brain of Mark’s is still fuzzy as he gets up from the wooden boards that act as a floor in the box car. Blood starts to ooze down the backside of his head as Mark can feel the warm liquid go down his neck and touch the little hairs. The color stains the little white hairs as they act as a symbol of pain. Mark outstretches his arm as he feels along one side of the box car. He feels for a sharp object, something that will do damage to whatever he is going to be hitting. The steel is cold against the warm hands of Mark as he feels along the side of the car still. He reaches a wooden stick it feels like as it is leaned up against the cold, silver steel. Mark grabs the wood as he helps himself up to his feet. He picks up the stick and it feels weighted at the bottom. Mark runs his hands carefully up the wooden stick as he runs his hands up his mind is thinking about what is weighting the bottom of this? The sharp side of an ax cuts a little slit in Mark’s index finger as he gets to the top of the weight on the stick. Mark takes his hand off the ax’s end as the cut shocked him. The red blood starts to come out of the cut as it blobs up and is trickling towards the side of the finger. Mark can’t see the sight of the blood but he can feel the warm liquid move out of his finger and down the skin. The guards outside are talking amongst themselves at the start of the train. They are talking nonsense about the train cars in Spanish so that the conductor could not understand what they are talking about. The conductor is up in the front of the train as his hat covers his face so that he can try to get some shut eye. This is one of the only stops that he is able to make for the night and he has skin puffed up under his eyes that look like pillows are underneath. It seems that the guards are not going to check the back of the box cars, but Mark can hear the shoes against the wooden floors of the deck as they are coming towards him. Mark picks up the ax as he makes his way across the wooden floor as it creaks as he walks across the boards. Every step is Mark walking even closer to the state of danger. The ax drags across the floor like a dead person’s body is dragging across the cement. Mark can feel the hog’s warm breath as it like wind in the summer time. The condensation inside of the hog’s mouth can be felt as well as they breath right towards where Mark is. Mark stops as he reaches out with his left hand, that is free, and he touches the warm skin of the pigs. The heat can be felt from skin to skin as Mark’s hand starts to burn up. Mark takes his hand off of the hog as it is restless as it sits down on the hay. Mark takes the ax in both hands and he brings it up over his head and comes down right across the heart of the pig. The pig is silent as it drops over onto the wooden boards of the box car. Mark takes the extra hay and he covers up the pig so that none of the guards are able to find what just happened. Mark drags the ax back over to where he found it and lays it there so that the dark red hog’s blood could drain down into the wooden boards. Mark quickly goes back to the corner that he was in to begin with and he takes the hay to cover up his body. “There is one box car left.” One of the guards says as he takes his hand on the handle and slides the door so that the light from the building could shine in on what is in the box car. “I smell something suspicious in here.” The guard says as he comes running over from the back of the train. “No I think that’s the smell of *sniff * fear.” The first guard says as he hops up on the box car platform. “Lets find out what is hiding in here before we let this train roll.” The guard takes his black combat boots and he starts to kick around all the hay in the middle of the car. He then works his way over to the opposite corner that Mark is laying and he kicks up the hay to find just sleeping hogs. “I thought you said there was something in there boys?” The head captain screams as he sticks his head out of the window. “There is fear in here.” The guard in the car says as he makes it loud enough that his boss can hear. “Shut up boys and get the fuck out of there, we have more trains to check.” The captain says as he shuts his door to the building. The guard moves across the wooden floor so that he can get out of the box car. The pig’s condensation from their noses is getting on the guard’s boot as there are little water spots on it. The guard hops down from the box car and he puts his hand on the handle of the door. The guard looks in once more and he can see a leather boot sticking out of the hay in the corner that he didn’t check. The boot moves as the guard puts his hand up in the air signaling for the other guard to hold on. “What is the hold up now?” The second guard asks as he is getting tired of waiting. *sarcastic* “Do you smell fear again?” “No there is a person in there I can see him.” The guard says as he looks closer to where Mark is laying at. “Why don’t you just get out of the way so that I can shut this door?” The second guard asks as he stands next to the other guard. The guard turns his back as he gets out of the way so that the second guard can come and slide the door shut. Mark moves his boot in under the hay so that no one can see the boot out in the open. The second guard looks into the box car and he sees nothing as he slides the door shut so that the train can roll into the states.
