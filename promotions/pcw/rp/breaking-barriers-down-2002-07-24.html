Breaking Barriers Down

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 24, 2002 The water felt cool as I stroked again through the pool. Amazing how soothing it feels when you're swimming through a pool on the middle of a cruise ship. I guess it harkens back to the womb. Bundled up, warm, protected, and taken out of the one place we want to stay. We can spend the rest of our lives trying to get back, but we'll never get there. I had loved swimming since I was a little boy. Taking lessons at the local YMCA gave me enough skills to take it to the next level and try my hand at more competitive levels. That was the thing with me - I always loved competition. No, I needed competition. It's what drove me to be better than everyone else. But what do you do when you have more competition then you can deal with? What happens when the competition will stop at nothing to hurt you? I pulled up to the side of the pool, sliding off my googles and looking over at the rest of the lanes. No one else was swimming. I guess they thought it was stupid to be swimming when we're in the middle of the ocean. Suit yourself, land lovers. As I grabbed the ladder to get out of the pool, I spotted Thinh Alexander, otherwise known as The Crimson, walking over towards me. He was wearing khaki shorts and no shirt, showing off his fairly buff midsection. With such little clothing, his painfully obvious vampire pale skin was hanging out for everyone to see. Good thing he didn't care. 'I was wondering when you two would come knocking,' I said, grabbing my towel and drying off. He glanced at me. 'Speedos? I thought they outlawed those things.' 'Only in certain countries,' I joked, putting the towel over my shoulder. 'So what's up?' 'Just came by to see how you were doing,' He replied, looking distant. 'I hadn't seen you since we arrived on the boat. Me and Rob are staying in a sardine cane for a room. You would think PCW would put up the cash to keep the inmates happy.' I smiled. Even as a vampire, Thinh wasn't happy. He preferred amenities that only us 'humans' would beg for, and yet here he was, proving to me again that he wasn't completely the undead. He took a deep breath. 'So how are you holding up?' 'Not bad. I haven't killed anyone yet, so that's a good sign. Looks like they're keeping both Duke and the Dark Dogs from coming into contact with each other, or me for that matter.' 'You guys would probably sink the god damn ship.' I shrugged. 'Probably. But we'll see what happens when I step into that ring, see the fans pop out of their seats and cheer, and then watch that Continental title come back to where it belongs.' His eyes sank into mine. There was a hesitant motion in his body. 'Are you that confident that you're going to win?' 'I have to be. You don't get anywhere in this sport for being down on yourself. I was the champion before and I'll be the champion again. That's as simple as it can be.' Thinh was unconvinced. 'Listen, Rob knows what's going on. He knows why you broke up the Crusaders and what could happen after the pay-per-view.' 'But how could he have-' 'We met Raymond Burris.' That scoundrel. He was afraid that I would drop out of the plan so he went to the Crusaders to fill them in. 'Thinh, it's not how it looks...' 'I understand. Look, I don't trust the guy as long as he's wearing that nice suit and tie. You know me. I don't take kindly to those in power, but this plan worries me. You're putting a lot into this match, and it could all go down in a ball of flames with your career. I hope you're ready for that.' 'Hopefully it won't have to come to that.' 'So do I.' He reached into his pocket and grabbed a cell phone, handing it to me. 'Go ahead, take it.' I grabbed the phone confused. 'What do you want me to do with this?' 'Call him up. He's waiting.' Thinh walked away, not explaining his actions or his motives. I looked at the phone and realized that the number was already inputted in the phone. I just had to dial. The dial tone began to ring... 'Hello?' My voice was parch. 'Hey, long time no see. So how's it going, Mike...'
