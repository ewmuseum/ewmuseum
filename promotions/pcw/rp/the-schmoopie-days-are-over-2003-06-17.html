The Schmoopie Days Are OVER!

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/03 Love and Hate It may seem ridiculous to say but there is a fine line between the two. They seem like such intense opposites, but there is no denying that at times you hate the person you claim to love because of their actions, or it could be the opposite you love the person you hate and just can’t admit to yourself and others your feelings for them because you are afraid they will think down on you for it. While Chris was around I could relate to this saying well. At times I hated him for death for failing to realize that even though Gaz and I were bonded by last name I didn’t want to be with Gaz, but even though I would hate him for this I knew deep inside my heart that Chris could easily manipulate me into listening to his view of the situation twenty times over simply because I loved him with all my heart. It may seem foolish to say but even now the saying comes into play in my life. The wedding vow that Gaz and I were bound by said that we were supposed to love and to cherish each other for all the days of our life, but in reality I despised him for causing me for having to live by that vow by screwing up the prank he and his former tag partner Nerezza decided to pull on Chris and I the second time we attempted to get married. It wasn’t just like this for me though. Many married couples realize how true this saying is the first week of their living together along with just plain couples who aren’t bonded by a wedding band around their ring finger or by a bed they share together. The same happens once they are broken up as well. It seemed that love and hate were nothing more than a continuous cycle of events that you could not avoid no matter how hard you tried or how big of a celebrity you were. “Going somewhere sweet cheeks?” The voice of Gaz “The Jester” Green rang out from his spot on the couch inside our honeymoon suite where he sat reading an article inside a magazine with his feet kicked up so that they rested conveniently on one of the couches arms. “Yes, somewhere far away from you.” I answered him back with little intention to be patient towards him. It was bad enough being stuck in the same with him, but with him trying to play the husband in my life just because we shared the same last name it only made matters worse which is why I wanted to be far away from him every moment I had the chance. Instead of giving him the chance to retort with some sort of rude comment I left the room, slamming the door behind me as I began to stroll down the hallway of deck three. As I strolled down the hall I recognized one of the VIP guests who had come here just as a guest and not to perform on stage in front of an audience as he usually did, Justin Timberlake. “Well if it isn’t Ms Windsor, the woman who is going to be responsible for the beating of Krikorian and his little buddy once she gets a hold of him on Fusion.” The member of the popular boy band N-Sync said with a small chuckle following his line as he walked up beside me. “Finally someone has to courtesy to call me by my actual last name!” I sighed with relief after him referring to me as Ms. Windsor instead of Mrs. Windsor-Green which many people had chosen to do either to please Gaz or to just get on my nerves. “And yes you are right I will be the one to get some revenge on Krikorian and his buddy on Fusion, but of course I’ve got to share them with Lacuna and Miss Moonlight considering they want those two just as gone as I do.” “Of course.” He agreed as he smiled, catching the attention of the woman he dated at one time, Britney Spears who simply smiled in his direction and then walked towards the both of us. “Well hello there darling.” She said as she walked over to Justin hugging him as if the two were still together. Obviously trying to be a gentleman Justin embraced Britney as well before letting go and exchanging a greeting with her. “Hi, what brings you here?” Justin questions the pop sensation who often tried to be much like Madonna. “Nothing, just figured I paid you a visit. It’s been awhile since I’ve seen you.” Spears said with a twinkle inside her blue eyes each time her eyes came in contact with Justin. “Yeah it has…I’ve missed you terribly.” Justin said as his puppy dog eyes twinkled back at Britney, you could feel the love floating in the air. “Well I’m going to head back to my room now.” I said cutting in between the two, not caring much for the mushy stuff between the two who supposedly broke up because they were cheating on each other. I guess I could really see now that the thin line between love and hate could be crossed easily with the presence of that person you both love and hate but in the mean time I could careless about the rendezvous of these two. If they wanted each other so bad let them deal with each other alone, there was no need for me to stick around, especially since there was hundreds of other things I could be doing right now instead of listening to these two go on and on about how they loved each other and all that mushy stuff. I had enough of that mushy stuff with Chris and there was just no way I wanted to hear it anymore, especially if Chris wasn’t here to say the same to me. “But Tessa…” Justin chimed in, giving me sad puppy eyes as if I was supposed to care about him and Britney’s past relationship and their turmoil’s. But how could he expect me to care when I could careless about love anymore with all that had happened within the past two or three months. I simply shrugged him off and headed back towards my room, even putting up with Gaz would be better than putting up with this mushy romantic crap which I didn’t want to go back to. “I don’t want to hear it! My Schmoopie days are OVER! They’ve been over! And don’t even try to say that Chris and I were like this one time or another because I couldn’t give a shit! He left me because he obviously didn’t want to be outshined by a girl just like Krikorian and his two little buddies, SOCIETY and Stone Burns.” I cursed at the male pop sensation, sick of all this mushy stuff. I had to get away from it. Sure I liked romance but this was just plain bullshit which I did not want to see anymore, before I left though I felt the urge inside of me to pull out a line which usually my soft heart would not allow my conscious to say but I had to spit it out anyhow to feel somewhat better about the whole situation, or maybe it was just the fact that perhaps if I said something like that that might please Gaz I might be able to get him to leave me alone and stop haunting me . “Oh yeah and I hope that this relationship lasts longer than Kriky and his pal do on Fusion, though I seriously doubt it.”
