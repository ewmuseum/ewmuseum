Death to Cheffy: Part 4

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/02 The camera fades in… We see a still image of the previous Chef promo with the Chef and the Director getting back to their feet after checking out the dead cat Richard Grieco was eating. Narrator: Welcome back to another mind-numbingly stupid Chef role-play! In the last promo the sinister “Jingle” Jackson was taunting the Chef and the Director. His slanderous tongue was not the only card he brought to the table, though… The still image begins to play and we see the Chef’s face getting red with anger as he prepares to verbally assault Jackson. The Chef: You tricked us, Jackson! You aren’t in that dead cat’s body!!! Jackson: You are fools for thinking that!?!? Haven’t you taken elementary math!?!? The Chef stands forward, growling angrily at Jackson. The Chef: In France we don’t learn Math…the only things important in France are the periodic tables of Wine and Cheese, and Puff Pastry 101… Jackson: What about Descartes!?!? The Chef: Not de Carte…it’s pronounced “a la carte”… Jackson: Quiet you!!! I’d have enough of you insolent behavior!!! It is now time to face your destiny and receive your just desserts for negating my right to sexually abuse children on live television! HE HE HEEEE!!! The Chef: Oh yeah, and what are you going to do about it!?!? “CHEF, HELP!!!” The Chef: Wha!?!? That voice…sounds familiar… “I DIS MANY YEARS OLD!!!” Jackson: He’s holding up two fingers! The Chef: …MANUEL!!! YOU WILL NOT VIOLATE MY ADOPTED SON, JACKSON!!! Jackson: HA!!! Then meet me at Never-Neverland! And Bring your TV Contract!!! The Chef: Or what!?!? Jackson: (Quietly) Jingle…bells…Jingle…bells…Jingle…all…the…way…TO NEVER-NEVERLAND OR MANUEL WILL BE MY LOVE SLAVE!!! HE HE HEEEEEEE!!! Director: He really needs a woman…that’s over the age of 13… The Chef nods, and then lets out a Vince McMahon-esque Adam’s Apple gulp. The camera fades to black… Narrator: Wow! This is some really dramatic shit! Will Jackson have his way with Manuel!?!? For the sake of the people reading this promo, I hope to God not! Let’s continue on with this saga… The camera fades in once again… A white van pulls in front of the gates the Never Neverland, Jackson’s “theme park” for kids. Jeff: It’s the sniper! Get him! Dave: We aren’t supposed to be in promos you moron! Jeff: Oh, my bad… A deliveryman dressed in a chef’s uniform jumps out of the van and presses the doorbell button. A voice comes over the intercom. “Who is it!?!? HE HE HEEEE!!!” The man answers… “VegaNA-GO-GO Catering Service! We have your steamed broccoli florets, celery root and mango-lime salsa, and a package of frozen bananas!” “Sweet! HE HE HEEEE!!! Come on in!!!” The gates open and the figure gets back into the truck, driving it past the gate and into Jackson’s compound. The van goes past the Merry-Go-Round, the Ferris wheel, the gallows, where many Sony Record producers are hung, and LaToya’s shack. It finally stops in front of Jackson’s colossal mansion. There are too giant marble statues of the King of Pop standing in front of a magnificent garden path, which leads to the entrance. The man jumps out of the truck and opens up the back, wheeling out a cart most likely filled with the vegan entrees Jackson ordered. Delivery Guy: Dum Dee Dum Dum Dum… He wheels the cart through the garden towards the front of Jackson’s mansion. He passes a giant hedge sculpture of Jackson doing the moonwalk AND IS SUDDENLY YANKED BACK BEHIND THE SCULPTURE…CRACK!!! Jeff: That was definitely a rolling pin shot! Disqualify him! Dave: Marx… Jeff: Oh yeah, we aren’t supposed to be in promos, that’s right… Dave sighs. Suddenly, out from the giant sculpture steps the CHEF!!! The Chef: Your @$$ has just been flambéed and sautéed by the finest Chef this side of the Atlantic, and the other side too!!! The Chef takes a quick look around the garden and hops back behind the hedge. The Chef: Now to disguise my appearance… The Chef reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a cheap, rubber Richard Nixon mask and sticks it over his head. The Chef: Cheffy, you’re a genius! He then hops back out from the sculpture and begins to wheel the cart up to the front door. He rings the doorbell. “Lisa Marie!?!? Have you finally come back to me…and brought your nephew!?!?” The Chef: No…this is the catering guy from the…um…catering place? “Ah yes…come on in then…and although you might be tempted, don’t feed Macaulay Culkin”… The door opens and the Chef is let inside the mansion. He gazes around in awe at the sheer number of child porn flicks Jackson has in his ownership. The white tiger, music studio, Rembrandt painting, Ark of the Covenant, and the extra never-before-seen episode of “Police Squad” on VHS are also pretty cool too… Jackson notices George…er…the Chef admiring the tape… Jackson: Leslie Nielson is my lover… The Chef: That’s…um…swell, where should I put your food?Jackson: In the dining hall! There will be a big celebration tonight! The Chef plays dumb. The Chef: Really, what for? Jackson: Tonight I will have my way with a boy of mixed ethnicity, and then head down to Democratic Party Headquarters to flirt with old Jewish chicks, and their young nieces and nephews! HE HE HEEEEE!!! He grabs his crotch, and then moonwalks back down the hallway. The Chef smiles, and lifts up one of the trays, REVEALING THE HEAD OF ELIZABETH TAYLOR!!! The Chef: JACKSON!!! The Jingle man turns around as the Chef throws off his mask. The Chef: You want to play hardball!?!? The Culinary Conquistador then whips a carving knife right out of his other pocket. The Chef: Show me where my son is OR LIZ GETS IT!!! Jackson: … The Chef: Tell me!!! Jackson: … The Chef: Tell me or I’LL GUT HER LIKE A RAINBOW TROUT!!! Oh!!! And I’ll add a touch of lemon for zest!!! Jackson: Liz… All Taylor can do is weep. The Chef: (In a Mel Gibson from “Ransom” toned voice) GIVE ME BACK MY SON!!! LIZ SUDDENLY HOPS UP, BUSTING THE TABLE IN HALF AND REVEALING THAT IS WASN’T REALLY LIZ!!! The chubby figure tosses away the XXXXX Large Dress and lets down her dark black hair to REVEAL HERSELF!!! The Chef: Behold the Queen of Mean, my rotund reason for living, my portly princess…BERTHA STEWART!!! Jackson gulps. Bertha: Give my back my c*ck-@$$ed son MOTHER F*CKER!!! Bertha immediately charges at Jackson. Jackson: UNLEASH THE CULKIN!!! Suddenly the cage with the Home Alone star drops to the ground, breaking itself open and letting the malnourished and mistreated child star out. He smacks his cheeks with his hands and lets out a skin-crawling shriek… He turns and looks at Bertha’s leg, which looks as if it was chock full of cottage cheese. Culkin: Food… The Home Alone star charges at Bertha and LATCHES ONTO HER LEG, BITING AND NIBBLING AT HER FLABBY FLESH!!! Bertha: Get the f*cking f*ck off of me you piece of sh*t f*ck tard!!! The Chef immediately chases after a fleeing Jackson. The Chef: Come back and fight like a man, Jackson!!! But the Thriller himself manages to scurry away, shouting in a victorious tone, “HE HE HEEE!!!” The Chef looks around the giant dining hall and sees a giant centerpiece in the middle of the table. The Chef: Manuel!!! The Chef recognizes the hog-tied child and rushes over to save him, BUT SUDDENLY A GROTESQUE CREATURE COMES SWINGING DOWN FROM THE CEILING!!! HE LANDS ON THE TABLE RIGHT IN FRONT OF MANUEL AND LETS OUT A HUGE ROAR!!! The Chef: Gah!!! I know you!!! You’re the ELEPHANT MAN!!! But the creature doesn’t answer the Chef; it slowly approaches the Sultan of Sautéing and lets out another giant roar!!! The Chef: This is going to be quite interesting to say the least… The Chef backs up quickly to a corner of the room as the Elephant Man approaches and gets closer and closer. The Chef can smell the rotten breath of the sideshow freak as his hideous faces comes ever so near to the face of the Culinary Conquistador… The Chef: Bertha!?!? Little help? The scene suddenly pauses. Narrator: Will the Chef and Bertha survive their battles inside the Jackson mansion!?!? Or will Manuel become the Thriller’s next victim!?!? Will Jackson share his method for hog-tying children!?!? We’ll find out…same Chef time…same Chef channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!!
