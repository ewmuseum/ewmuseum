Just about time to head to Mexico

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/2/2002 'Mr. Amulet, ironic isn't it,' questions Jon Kano as he sits on the hood of his trademarked, fully customized, jet black Lincoln Navigator. Mr. Kano is dressed in blue jeans, black Doc Martin steel toed boots, and a 'REAL LEGEND' t-shirt. He is in Godknowswhere, Texas, watching the sunset in the oil fields of the Lone Star State before he leaves to go to Mexico City for Turmoil. 'Mark, irony is such a funny thing to me. I, myself, the constant defender of tradition here in PCW. Why? I have been here for so long, working on my third year now. I have seen all there is to see here in PCW, nothing surprises me anymore. I was the one to bring the first regime into power. That regime was just two men, The Phantom Booker and myself. Then the bastard Nelson came to power. Finally, the even bigger bastard Kannon is in power. I liked Xavier for a awhile. He had balls but now he is bringing in all of this new blood and trying to usher people like myself out of PCW. I STARTED THIS PRIMETIME SHIT!!!! I DEFINED WHAT PCW WAS ALL ABOUT!!!' Jon Kano screams with echoes of his words in the distance of the oil fields. Kano stands up on his hood and steps up to the roof of his luxury SUV. 'Amulet, you were my teammate at Alliances, thats ironic. The three of you disappointed me so much, but at the same time I expected it. The three of you; yourself, Michigan, and Semore Smoke were out of the match within the first three to five minutes. I was there for a hell of a lot longer. I hit anything that moved and it all happened to be newbies. In that bloodbath of newbies, I rekindled my lust for the blood of the new. Mark, on Turmoil, you just happen to be a newbie in the wrong place at the wrong time. It is nothing personal, Mark, just extremely pleasurable business,' 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth' says with a rather sadistic smile across his face. 'Mr. Mark Amulet just happens to be the Extreme Champion. Now, Mark, don't fret because I know right now you are soiling yourself in utter fear of what awaits you in our match. I am probably not going to win the match. I am not big on winning matches right now, I am more about making sure newbies realize what is at stake here in PCW. You can keep your little championship. God knows I have had my share of championships in my illustrious career. I have held titles in every single federation I have ever wrestled for, holding titles on six continents. If I never again hold a title, its fine with me, because I am content with making you and the rest of your newbies pay your God damn dues. On Turmoil, you may escape with a win or your Extreme Championship, but believe you me, you will pay your dues. And on Turmoil, I will show you and every single newbie why I am what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!' yells Kano as he slides into his SUV through the open sunroof and drives away as the scene fades to black.
