Only Seperated By A Plan

20 November 2002

Title: Only Seperated By A Plan Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 20/11/02 He may not be PCW World Champion. He may not be a Hall of Famer. But Xavier Kannon. CEO. Owner of PCW, is the ‘King of the Mountain’. “From where I am...” His wife, Eleanor Kannon-Hall is the Commissioner, the whole world knowing that the ditzy little prep only has the position to appease any whining... It is Xavier who pulls the strings and his wife playing the role of puppet. Beside him are the Front Office, each with their own tasks but with nobody above them to answer to. They make the rules and the buck stops with them, all thinking along the same lines there is no disharmony. Same goals. Same methods. Same thinking. “... There’s only down.” While in business, many a boss who thinks he’s something special will talk about how he mingles with his work force, jesting that he’s ‘One of the guys’... At Alliances that is exactly what Xavier, Duke, Walden and Brandi will be doing. The ‘Management’ and the ‘Workforce’. But not many people have a 5 ring, multiple cage structure as a workplace. “Every week we have a ‘Casual Friday’ too.” Only problem is, while Xavier Kannon is above them, there will be no ordering them about, just like there isn’t when he steps into the ring on Turmoil or Fusion. Even from the top of the mountain, there are some things he can’t control. “It usually involves beating the shit out of each other.” The woman who controls the hiring and firing. Miss Wilson, the Personnel Director, founder member of the Front Office will be fighting in the same match as Team CTMB. The team thrown together by the Front Office, or rather a bored Commissioner stuck by a Fax Machine. That’s the diversity, the spread, of the Alliances match. Brandi, a woman who could rid Jamie Celt, Trigger, BladeD and Eddie Mandrake of their contracts and hire in any other 4 she saw fit, gets no head-start over PCW’s enhancement talent. Far different salaries. A Universe apart in power. No comparison on impact. No favouritism in Sunday’s workplace. “I know, even this match is below us.” Tim Walden. The money man. A PCW employee who has the power to dictate how much each wrestler will take home each week. The Financial Reclaimations Executive is the man who effects how much a wrestler can spend on his family each week, how much goes in their child’s College fund. He is in charge of the purse-strings, and the strings connected to every worker in PCW. They might as well give him their wallets for safe keeping. But that’s the wonder of Alliances. The man who takes the wrestler’s pay cheques and rewrites them, who has filled the PCW bank vaults once again, steps into the same cages as Kano and 3 Other Guys. At Alliances, Tim Walden is stripped of that power. Out of his office and in the cages there can be no taxation. Tim Walden will have to fight just like the rest of them. “Then again, he can strip their pay packets bare for the next year, it all balances out.” Duke Williams and Rent-a-Hero, 2 very different men. The PCW President has a solid, loyal team behind him. He can always focus on the match knowing that there will be Kannon, Walden and Brandi watching his back. The Spandex clad Hero has it different, his ‘Punishment Team’ hand-picked for him. Avalanche, Revenant, David Arquette. Same situation. Both men will still walk onto the giant structure with 3 people at their side, both men will still have 14 other teams to fight against. For the first time in 2 months, the Front Office cannot use their power to win, nor even get any favouritism.. “I wouldn’t say that.” And there’s the catch. From the day they formed, the Front Office have never put themselves in a match they didn’t have a advantage in. They always tried to have the extra man, they always had Duke at ringside ready to leave his desk... They even have their own referee, Bob Charlie. Does anyone expect them not to have a plan? “Ah, there’s always a plan.” This week, Kannon has been taunting some of the biggest names in wrestling. He wants Ivan Stanislav and Tyler Nelson to come for him, he wants Rent-a-Hero to exact revenge, and he hasn’t gone to any lengths to curry favour with Playaz Inc. and the Celestials. On Friday, Playaz Inc. will have to face one another in Black on Black Violence. Last Friday, it was Akiyama v the End - Last Man Standing. “Maybe I just feel lucky?” Nobody could call Xavier Kannon ‘Brave’, nobody would ever say he has the ‘Heart of a Tiger’. But they would say he’s conniving, and they would say he’s as sly as a Fox. There must be a plan.... Mustn’t there?” And so the problem begins.... How do you devise a winning strategy in a match where anyone of 56 wrestlers could roll you up for a pin? Or where the merest slip will send you falling off a walkway to the ground... And elimination? It must take a really brilliant, and even more so cunning mind to devise such a thing. “Moi?” Correct. Xavier Kannon’s. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
