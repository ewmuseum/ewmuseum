The Tank 31 - Absence Makes the Heart grow Cautious

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11, 2002 ﻿*********THE TANK __31—********* Absence Makes the Heart Grow Cautious We used to be so closewhen you let me cut youand fuck youand kill you Where did you go when I was bloody and bruised? ~Leigh Donovan Tuesday, October 8 - Day 96 “Where the fuck is your cellmate anyway!?” demands Tim McDonald as he uninvitedly bargesinto the cell that Leigh Donovan usually shares with Jason Snow. Just days after learning thatTim’s own cellmate, Vincent Giovanni, is actually an undercover mob hitman in search of thetruth about Doug Hattoni’s death, Tim has tried to stay as close as possible to the much largerJason Snow - as much in hopes that Snow will guard him as in hopes that he may be able to get aread if Snow gets, as he puts it, ‘unpredictable’. “You didn’t here?” Leigh Donovan, the Australian rocker says with a snicker. “Jesus, mate -they ain’t expecting that oaf back for quite some time.” “What are you talking about?” Tim squints. “Red October,” shrugs Donovan while lying on Snow’s upper bunk, reading an issue of ‘GuitarMagazine.’ He folds the magazine closed and looks over at Tim. “Your chum got his headcracked by the skinny bastard he was fighting.” “What?” McDonald says in disbelief. Leigh nods. “I’m not fucking lying to you, if that’s what you’re thinking,” he says. “Best partwas, them two fucking morons who escorted him every where were in on it. The big blondbastard probably would have won the whole fucking thing had they not started cracking himwith their billy clubs. Fuck - what I wouldn’t give to have Pay Per View in this hell hole.” Leigh shakes his head while smiling. Tim McDonald psychologically snaps. He finds himself in a position remarkably like DougHattoni’s - Jason Snow appears to be his best means to hanging on to his life in the face of thisnew danger that comes in the form of a bullish Italian named Vincent Giovanni. Tim knows thatVincent is just biding his time, looking for some final nail for the coffin, before he decides tosnuff out his life. He also knows that Vince will then proceed to punish Jason Snow for his partin Doug Hattoni’s murder in much the same fashion. Of course, Harvey Blake will be next, andthen the Aryans. At least Harvey Blake has the Aryans to hide behind. Tim McDonald will find no refuge in theItalian gang that he leads. If Vincent tells what he knows to the Italians, there’s no telling whatmight happen. Tim knows that even if the Italians didn’t believe the story, the seeds ofsuspicion would be planted, and it would only take one hot head with a blade to seal his fate -and his future. That is, after all, what it’s all about to Tim McDonald - his future. “So where the fuck is he then?” Timothy demands. “The infirmary?” “Fucking Tokyo,” chuckles Leigh. “Being fed by God damn Asian nurses and shit.” “Jesus Christ!” exclaims Tim, a hint of desperation in his voice. “He’s that fucking bad!?” “They don’t know,” shrugs Leigh, opening his magazine back up. “Fuck if I care either. ThatGod damn nigger lover can rot in hell before I turn an empathetic eye.” Tim, a man who fights his battles with brain instead of brawn, also happens to be a man drivenby passion, and in this moment, it takes over. Uncharacteristically, Tim doesn’t think - he acts. With a right hand with strength that even surprises himself, Tim clubs Leigh in the face, throughthe magazine. “What the fuck, mate!?” screams Leigh, rolling out of Snow’s top bunk and landing hard on thefloor. He cups his hands beneath his nose to catch the blood that’s spilling from it. Tim rainsdown a second blow and then a third to the back of Leigh’s unprotected head, driving the Aussietoward the concrete floor. “Fuck!” screams Tim while firing yet another shot down on Leigh. He’s not angry with Leigh -he’s angry with the situation that Leigh has been laughing at. A second later, the momentum swings. Pouncing like an animal, Leigh Dovovan leaps from hiscrouched position on the floor and sends a hard right elbow crashing into the left side of TimMcDonald’s skull. The young mob leader stumbles backward and soon finds himself corneredin the small cell as Leigh closes in and begins striking at an alarmingly fast pace. Leigh is angry as well - but not with Tim McDonald. Three months ago, when he entered theTank, he would have allowed Tim McDonald to beat him until the Scottish descendant Italianprison mob leader tired of striking. But now, Leigh Donovan has transformed from a cautiousherbivore into a carnage-lusting meat eater. With horrid intentions, Leigh fires blow after blow at Tim McDonald’s arm-covered head - afew shots penetrate through Leigh’s defense and blood soon splashes out from behind his arms. “Fight motherfucker!” Leigh says, not loud enough to arouse attention. “You guinea wanna-befucker!” Leigh reaches through Tim’s arms, grabs his throat, and begins to squeeze. “You thinkyou can push me around!?” Leigh says through clinched teeth. “No one pushes me around thesedays, you fucking guinea lover!” With his arms now useless to protect himself from Leigh, whose attack has changed, Timfrantically pulls at the knob of the water faucette to his left until it finally breaks off. With onelast burst of energy, Tim drives the chunk of metal into the side of Leigh’s unsuspecting face. “Christ!” exclaims Leigh as he stumbles backward, finally releasing his death grip on Tim’sthroat. Immediately, Leigh’s right cheek begins to turn black and swell. “You broke myfucking cheek bone, you cocksucker!” Instead of saying another word, Tim tosses the knob into the sink before laying one final lefthook into Leigh’s throbbing cheek bone - his mouth begins to fill with blood from a gash createdwhen the inside of his cheek was slashed by his molars at the time of impact. “Bastard,” Tim sneers as he staggers outside, trying hard to catch his breath. Upon leaving theyoung Australian’s cell, Tim immediately looks around for on-coming authority figures, but tohis pleasure, he finds that no one saw the incident. No one, except one man - when he saw Tim emerge from the cell, Vincent Giovanni ducked intohis own cell, the one he shares with Tim, and out of the sly Scottish descendant’s sight. Now,his aging hard eyes peer out from the dark, waiting for McDonald to return. Thursday, October 10 - Day 98 Tim McDonald realizes that every day Vincent Giovanni is tightening the noose - there may notbe much time left before Giovanni decides to finally allow Tim’s neck to crack. If only he couldtalk to Snow, things would be better. McDonald is confident that he has the brains to outwitGiovanni, but the only man he knows willing to provide that muscle is Jason Snow, whosefamily is in danger. “Damn,” sighs Tim, as he sits at an unoccupied checkerboard, staring up at Geoffery Rankin’soffice while the prisoners of Unit B await the presence of Rankin, the Unit Manager, who hasrequested the prisoners to gather for his announcement. Tim knows that he can’t set up a hit on Giovanni through his own Italian gang. He realizes thathe hasn’t gained enough trust with some of the higher ranking members to have them kill anItalian by the command of a non-Italian, even if he is their leader. Tim knows all toowell how quickly things can change in the Tank. From here on in, it’s a chess match, he thinks while staring down at the board in front of him. Anticipation is key. Yet, at the same time, one must never be too afraid to play the gameaggressively... “May I have your attention?” Geoffery Rankin says loudly as he steps out of his office andmakes his way to the cell-balcony staircase - he climbs a few steps to make himself moreaudible. “I have some good news for you.” “Oh fuck,” Tim says under his breath - he knows what’s coming. “The prison, and Unit B in particular, has been taken down a couple of levels of alert, and as oftomorrow, all normal telephone privileges will be restored,” Geoffery says loudly. He receives amuch expected round of cheers from the prisoners. “No...” Tim shutters - this is not good news to him. This means Vincent Giovanni will haveaccess to the outside world - and his very powerful friends. “Of course,” Geoffery adds before retreating to his office, “visitation privileges are still beingwith-held until a later date. But as of tomorrow, the telephones are yours.” Another cheer rises. Through the corner of his eye, Tim McDonald catches a glimpse of Vincent Giovanni’s smirkingface.
