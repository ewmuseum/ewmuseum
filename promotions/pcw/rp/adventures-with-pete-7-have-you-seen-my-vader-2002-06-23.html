Adventures with Pete [7]: Have you seen my Vader?

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23, 2002 In a time when all we want is some peace and quiet, there is always evil to stop us. It never fails nor ceases to amaze me how many times I can’t stop and think about how I’ve been screwed. Not in the literal sense, mind you, but in life. Sleazer had just given his solemn vow to help us in our quest for the amulet, and I thought we had a strong bond. He even told us his embarrassing prom night story that only a guy light Sleazer could have pulled off. I mean, a green suit? But things were different now. What seemed like an earthquake turns out to be a gaping hole in the middle of the café. All of the people were now strewn about the ground, unconscious or close to it, except myself. Oh, and Pete was alright too. But of course he was…he was a baseball player, ya know? The inevitable question that would be asked was if I would ever be able to get to Gold and Glory. After all, Darth Vader is not your average, run of the mill villain. And while he did turn back to the good side at the end of Return of the Jedi, this was no redeemed jedi knight. Nope, this was the same evil, maniacal mad man that first destroyed all the good guys in the first place. Reminds me of Xavier Kannon, only Vader didn’t cry after he saw Lilo and Stitch. Oh yeah, XK cried after seeing that. Sad little aliens. Tom Lexian: [looks over at Pete] Pete, are you alright? Pete Rose: [rubs his head] Do I fucking look alright? I have a bump on the side of my head the size of a cantaloupe, and you want to know if I’m alright? Tom just smirked. Pete was always the exaggerator, in fact, if he was ever on that show American Gladiators, that would probably be his name. But his belly would have given him away. Tom Lexian: Is Sleazer alright? Pete Rose: I don’t know. His eyes aren’t open. Tom shakes Sleazer’s arm, which is slung over his chest as if he had been knocked out. He began to stir a bit, but was still not waking up fully. Although I was about to give up, I suddenly had a brilliant idea. Tom Lexian: Pete, hand me one of those fried kumquats that Sleazer was eating earlier. Pete Rose: [has a disgusted face] Dude, hell no. I’m not touching one of those. It might give me cancer. Tom Lexian: Pete, just hand me the damn kumquat! Pete Rose: [grabs a kumquat using a napkin and throws it at Tom] There you go. I hope you two are happy with one another. Tom Lexian: [wipes the kumquat juice that splatters on him] Stop being a child, Pete. [places the kumquat underneath Sleazer’s nose. Within a few seconds, Sleazer’s eyes are wide open.] Sleazer: [coughing] Oh my god, I’m dying…I’m dying…someone help me…. Tom places his hand over Sleazer’s mouth, causing him not to talk. Sleazer began to spit out some green, gurgling liquid, which Tom immediately began to wipe away on Sleazer’s shirt. The green pus somehow patched the sky blue shirt he was wearing. Irony, apparently, held no prisoners. Thank god. Tom Lexian: [hissing] Keep your voice down, nimrod. We don’t want to attract that Darth Vader guy. Pete Rose: Was that really Darth Vader? I mean, I heard the WHHOOOOOP EEEEER sound, but I didn’t really think it was him. Sleazer: Yeah, I thought he was just a movie character. Tom Lexian: [sighs] I learned that you can’t take anything lightly in this world. I’ve seen too many weird things in my lifetime to say that this can’t be possible. Pete Rose: Like that puzzled bird guy we saw earlier? Tom Lexian: Exactly. How often do you see a life sized bird driving a Toyota Camry anyways? Besides that, how many times do you see a game named Sunshine after a world cup game loss? This world is unbelievable sometimes! Sleazer: [confused] You guys saw big bird? And who’s named Sunshine? Tom just shook his head, trying to analyze the situation. The man was a solver, and he had to find a way out of here. Most of the people were unconscious. Very few were moving at all. And at the far end of the store was Mr. Darth Vader himself, stalking about the café. And Sleazer didn’t want to know the whole story about Sunshine. God knows I didn’t, and I really knew! Pete Rose: Just our luck that we’re the only people conscious. Now he’ll probably kill us. Sleazer: Oh my gosh. Do you think he’ll use his evil dark Force tricks on us? Maybe he’ll strangle us to death using his mind powers. Tom Lexian: Shut up, you two. Don’t you remember? I’m a wrestler. I can handle this guy. Pete Rose: [skeptical] Yeah right. Like you’re going to stop Darth Vader. Need I remind you that he is the evil prince of darkness. Tom Lexian: Dracula is the prince of darkness, you moron. And besides, you can’t believe everything in the movies. We don’t even know if he even has any special powers. Pete pointed over at the huge hole in the wall. It was an inescapable truth that, yes, there was a humongous hole in the wall, and the man that put it there was trying to take our heads and rip them from our necks. Not a pleasant thought, but it pays to live in reality every once in awhile. Leave it to Pete to cast a very dark cloud over my life. Pete Rose: He just put a god damn, mother fucking hole in the side of the building. Does that need any more convincing to you? [8===0! OBSCENITY!] Tom Lexian: I suppose not, but I’m just saying, don’t think he’s invincible. Anyone can be beaten. Pete Rose: Psssh…suuuuuuuuure Tom. We believe you, don’t we Sleazer? Sleazer: Yeah right. Tom just shakes his head, cursing the doubters and picked up a fork. Now, forks are a terrific weapon. While pointy and easy to handle, they are also fun to say. Just try it – FORK IT! And while a sword probably would have done just the trick right about now, let’s face it, how often do you see spare swords just lying around in a blown up café? I didn’t think so. Pete Rose: Please tell me you’re not going to fight him with a fork. Sleazer: [scared] Yeah, don’t you know he has a light saber? It can cut people in half! Those forks couldn’t even cut a kumquat! Tom Lexian: Guys, calm down. I know what I’m doing. If we all use our heads, we can take this guy out. Pete looked doubtful, and I didn’t blame him. I was trying to be the confident, strong leader, but even I knew this was going to be tricky. A lot was just riding on the idea that he didn’t have any special powers. Lord knows we didn’t. But once again, I would rely on my superior fighting skills, perhaps unrivaled in this side of the galaxy. Why, I imagined myself slamming a hard right hand into the side of that dinky helmet he wore, and then slamming my knee right into his groin. Oh yes, even dark sith lords have a groin. Tom Lexian: Here’s the plan. I’m going to stand up and confront him. You two swing around and try to take out his legs. When you guys do that, I’ll get him with the fork… Pete Rose: Could you at least use a knife? Tom Lexian: The fork’s blunt fool, it’ll hurt more. Sleazer: Or bounce off his armor. Tom Lexian: Shut up, Sleazer. So there was the plan. Only thing left was to execute. Sleazer and Pete made their way around the tables, getting ready to flank Vader. I took a deep breath and tried to calm myself. This would be an epic battle, and I might not make it out. Here’s to living and dying. Tom Lexian: [standing up] Yo Vader, I got your amulet right here. DARTH VADER: Oh really? Hand it over, and I will make your death swift. Tom Lexian: [laughs] Don’t make me laugh. Oh, too late. You’re not getting the amulet, my friend. Do you know why? Because it’s mine. Now, can you dig that? Yep, I tried my best Booker T impression. It didn’t work too well, and I couldn’t do a spin-a-rooni. And I practice everyday too! DARTH VADER: The only thing you’re digging is your grave, human. Now, hand over the amulet. Tom Lexian: [busts out his fork] Don’t take another step forward, I swear to god I’m going to let you have it. Vader lifts up his hand and the fork goes flying into the wall. Oh shit, he does have powers. DARTH VADER: Was that your only weapon? I fear you’re near death. Now, hand over the amulet. Tom Lexian: I…I…I don’t have it. [begins to stammer backwards] DARTH VADER: Don’t lie to me. I can sense you have something in your possession. NOW GIVE IT TO ME! Tom’s clothing comes flying off, and suddenly a green object flies into Vader’s hand. It’s the kumquat. The fruit suddenly explodes in Vader’s hands, oozing green goodness all over his black, metallic space suit thingy. He does not look happy, and if we could see his face, I bet there would be a nasty scowl on it. DARTH VADER: What in the blazes is this? This is not the amulet. This is some type of hoax! You will pay! Pete Rose: Leave him alone! Pete slams a metal chair into Vader’s back, shattering it into a million pieces. Vader takes Pete by the throat and lifts him off the ground. As Pete chokes, Sleazer comes around and grabs Vader by the neck. Vader lets Pete go and kicks Sleazer off of him, unleashing his red bladed light saber. DARTH VADER: That was a mistake. Now you will die. Sleazer does a crane kick type of maneuever, but completely misses Vader, kicking away at the air. He comes down hard on his back and moans in pain. Sleazer: Don’t move…ugh, or I’ll call kill you. DARTH VADER: You are pathetic. [raises his light saber to strike Sleazer] Tom Lexian: [running forward] NOOOOO! Vader causes Tom to go flying backwards using his force powers. He then looks back at Sleazer who has fear written all over his face. Beads of sweat made their way down his face, probably mixed in with a few tears. The song “Don’t Cry for me Argentina” suddenly came to my mind. That, and Madonna in that “Like a Virgin” video. Hell of an entertainer, that Madonna. Sleazer: Please don’t! Spare me! DARTH VADER: I didn’t spare the rebels. Why should I spare you? Sleazer: [crying] I’m pathetic looking! DARTH VADER: Yes you are. But even that does not spare you from death’s grip. Now, prepare to die! Suddenly, two men are at the hole holding two large guns. They were not rifles, nor shotguns, nor any sort of conventional weaponry. In fact, they had a sci-fi twist to them almost, as if they were taken straight out of a Steven Spielberg flick. While one gun was probably larger than my entire body, the other was actually quite small, with vibrant lights flashing from its sides. I was mesmerized by the gun, perhaps more, if I didn’t think that they would vaporize me the second I opened my mouth. Sourpuss: Drop the light saber, Vader. You’re under arrest. Chopsticks: Do what the old man said, or I might have to roast you. DARTH VADER: Infidels! [uses force powers to toss both men to the ground and runs out through the hole] Tom gets up to see Vader is gone. That’s when he spots Sourpuss and Chopsticks – the Black Suits. Of course, the suits are one of the reasons that Tom doesn’t believe in reality anymore. You see, in reality, there aren’t huge alien butterflies trying to take over the world, or aliens that always want to blow the planet up, nor conspiracies about how Elvis is really George Bush without his makeup and a loss in his dance step. Reality doesn’t have these things, yet they continue to occur. Tom doesn’t wakeup from these dreams, and so he believes he lives in DIS-REALITY. Could it be true? Is this new world that Tom Lexian has discovered a portal to unheralded fun and mayhem? Tom Lexian: [rubs his head] Oh god, not the Black Suits. Anyone but the black suits. Chopsticks: [looks over at Sourpuss] You think he’s happy to see us? Because he doesn’t look very happy. Sourpuss: [smiling] Of course he’s happy to see us. We’re good buddies. Oh goodness…
