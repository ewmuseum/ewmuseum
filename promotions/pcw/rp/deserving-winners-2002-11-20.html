Deserving Winners

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Deserving Winners Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 20/11/02 Alliances. 60 Men. 15 Teams. 5 Cages. 3 Levels. “Any man can win.” Last year Jeremy Howard emerged victorious, and we all know where that went. Could Rent-a-Hero overcome the odds of his ‘Anchor’ team and be the last man standing? Maybe Akiyama can power his way through 59 other men and stand tall at the end? Or even Jon Kano could show why he’s the REAL Legend and get a World Title shot, couldn’t he? “Any team can win.” Can Mismatched find a common bond and work their way to the end? Perhaps Team CTMB, thrown together by a bored Ellie~! could overcome the odds and divide up the shots. What about team OSW? Team FSW? Rush and the Extreme Crackas? Playaz Inc.? “Only one team deserves it.” Amidst all 15 teams, one has been a solid rock for over 2 months in PCW, holding the Fed in it’s vice-like grip and squeezing everything they can from it. Playaz Inc. were brought together by race and lifestyle. The Celestials have a common energy that flows through them. Team RUSH are driven by their underdog spirit. “That’s us.” The Front Office. After many months as #2, Kannon finally inherited what he thought was his. The whole of PCW was his, every staple in every office bearing the PCW logo belonged to him. Then, he threw us the swerve. “What can I say? I admired his fighting spirit!” All through April, Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams waged war. Opposite ideals, opposite principles and opposite lifestyles clashed violently... Both in words and actions. Then, Duke made a deal with the Devil, or worse. Xavier Kannon. “And so the Front Office was born.” Duke Williams had sat back and watched PCW since he became President in January. He’d seen a string of wrestlers come through and make their play for Titles and Power... One caught his eye, at that time, for the wrong reasons... And that man, that Scientologist brought him out of retirement. But Kannon beat Duke, and even an old hoss like the Malice Man had to admire that. As President, Duke knew that another war with Kannon would benefit nobody... After all, the President knew ultimate power was only an alliance away. “Ultimate Power Rules Absolutely.” Was it all about Power for Kannon? Was his alliance with Duke simply about being on the same page as the one man who could thwart him? For any other man it probably would be, Kannon is different. Duke was a man of the people, and Xavier saw how they loved him. Everytime “Honky Tonk Heroes” hit every PCW loyal fan would rise from their seat and cheer, waiting to see what profanity laden rant he would deliver, ready to pop at the mention of Malice style or roar when he raised his JD bottle and yelled out “I’LL TAKE YOU WHISKY BENT AND HELLBOUND!” The boss of hoss had millions of fans. “So I took him from them.” And that sums it up right there. No amount of insults could have hit PCW fans as hard as when Duke turned his back on them and joined up with Xavier. Duke went from being loved to detested in one second. The moment he stood beside Xavier he betrayed them and everything they stood for... But in his frame of mind, Duke was only encouraged by their hate. He could make money from it. “It wasn’t only Duke though, remember?” The night the Front Office formed was Xavier Kannon showing the middle finger to PCW fans in the biggest way possible. They hated him for destroying Asylum. They blamed him for Jeremy Howard’s firing. They were angered when he ended Big Alan Steele’s run in PCW. They detested him for robbing Tyler Nelson for control of PCW. He just hated them for being who they were. “Nobody likes to see a Super Hero cry.” One man wasn’t willing to let Big Alan Steele be forgotten. The big bold hero had a true friend in Rent-a-Hero, and the hero wasn’t about to let Kannon piss all over Big Alan Steele’s legacy by just letting the Greek God fade from people’s minds.... But he didn’t figure that someone so close to him would be taken away. As if his friend Jeremy going wasn’t bad enough, Big Alan Steele’s exit from PCW rubbed salt into the wounds... But that still wasn’t enough for the mind of Xavier. “Yeah, like she even thought twice about it.” Just as he took Duke from the fans, he took Brandi from Rent-a-Hero. The hero’s true friend. The un-masked ‘Black Kitten’. The woman Renty would have layed his body on the line for... Was then by Xavier’s side, smirking at him, rubbing it in, the man he detested commanding the girl everyone could see he loved. “He needed to be kept in check.” The night the Front Office formed, it sent a chilling message to Primetime Championship Wrestling. No matter how anti-Kannon someone had been in the past, they were only one persuasive word away from siding with him. No matter how much someone detested his ideals, they were only a sniff of power away from turning their backs on all they had for a longer smell. No matter how close a friend they were, they’d stab them in the back for the lure of the CEO. PCW’s fan favourite President became Kannon’s ally, the Tax-Happy President Duke Williams. Rent-a-Hero’s sidekick, Brandi became the arrogant and un-caring Personnel Director, Miss Brandi Wilson. “Hey, Taxin’ ain’t easy you know?” The money poured in. Merchandise was shoved down fan’s throats. Being a PCW fan was more expensive than ever. Loyal followers were finding their seats replaced by Executive reserved sections of the crowd. Wrestlers found their salaries booming only to be earning less than before by Taxation and other various ‘deductions’. Hate them people did, but the Front Office sky-rocketed PCW back to where it should be. The Front Office saved PCW. They deserve to win Alliances. “Wait, that’s only three...” So much money stealing and Taxation was going on, they even had to sign someone to deal with it all. Xavier needed someone who he could trust, someone he had a common bond with, someone who detested the man Kannon took PCW from. And he chose the man he was part of the Ultimate Authority with, Tim Walden. “Make that “Financial Reclaimations Executive” Tim Walden, if you please.” The man fired by Nelson... Twice. There didn’t seem to be any more money the Front Office could squeeze from PCW until Walden arrived on the scene with his new edicts and budgets. If a wrestler found $5 backstage you could be sure that by the end of the show that Tim would have at least $3.50 of it. It was unfair. It was harsh. It worked. “And so, OSW, FSW and RUSH will be guesting on the TOP Fed’s Pay Per View.” It’s true. The Front Office’s ‘tough love’ policy towards PCW, stretching it as far as it will go without snapping, pushing it to it’s limits of patience and loyalty.... Worked. A new golden era has dawned on PCW at the beginning of the Third Age, and love them or hate them... Or REALLY hate them... They did it. “And what thanks do we get?” Maybe winning Alliances will be a small measure of payment for making PCW what it is today. After all...Xavier Kannon, Duke Williams, Tim Walden and Brandi... They deserve it. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
