Meeting Tomorrow?

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/03 “You know this totally sucks,” Haley says, placing her hands on the table. Her icy blue eyes stare impassively on her sister seated across from her, dressed in a dress for the classy dinner Haley had invited her too. The other people around them are dressed just as nicely, each engrossed in their own conversations, paying no attention to the people around them. The waiters and waitresses wearing their standard uniforms rush to bring the hot food to the people that ordered it. The dim lights make it hard to tell who is getting what and they seem to be confusing orders. Behind them a lone pianist causally plays some relaxing music, his face contorted and his fingers sought the keys furiously and frequently in an attempt to set the mood for the onlookers. Across the way, others talked loudly at the bar with menacing enthusiasm as the alcohol was starting to set in and cut all their inhibitions away. Each drink making them forget about their problems and bring them some enjoyment, even if it was just for a little while. Some happy couple, having a great time and enjoying the company of the person in front of them, occupied each table. A few of them were probably married, but most were single and having a great time on their dates. All of them except Haley Hilton, who was forced to be here with her sister Laura, instead of her new boyfriend Matt Jackson. He had canceled on her at the last minute for some important business at the agency. She had hopes of being able to go with him, but he had told her that it was just to complex for someone just starting out. “You know he could have come up with a better reason,” Haley begins, pushing her golden locks out of her face. “I just can’t believe he would like cancel right before our first date. He has been so busy at work getting ready for everything and this was like our first real chance to go out.” “I am sorry Haley, I don’t really know what to tell you,” Lauren tells her, trying to show Haley how sorry she really is. “What is it you two exactly do again because I thought you worked together and I figured if he was at work, you would be too?” The question puzzles the blonde princess for a while, as she twists some of her hair on her finger. She had been told exactly how to handle that question by Matt, but she wasn’t paying attention when he told her. Her mind was racing for a response, that would best put her sisters worries at bay. Lauren hadn’t liked the idea of Haley wrestling, but she also hadn’t been able to talk her out of it. That meant she really would like her working for the Central Intelligence Agency. “I am like totally Matt’s assistant at the PCW,” she quickly lies. “I just like make sure he does everything legal and we get rid of those pests who are infecting it.” ‘Interesting, do you like it there?” “Like no, that place sucks so much. Last week we were almost assaulted by some security guard because he was jealous that we weren’t poor like him and then this past Friday, some fans like threw trash into the ring when we were in it. It is going to be great when Matt closes that place down.” “I don’t know if I like you working in that place Haley. You are so small and most of those people are so big and on top of that it sounds like Matt is kind of a marked man. No one is going to like him shutting down their business.” “It is like so safe. I only have to wrestle if Matt is with me and then that like means he is going to do all the work and I just get to stand and look cute. Even though none of those morons should be allowed to look at someone like me.” Her words have a very serious meaning. She hated the PCW now, it was keeping Matt from her at this very moment and that made her angrier then anything else. Little did she know that it wasn’t really the PCW that was keeping Matt away from her. It was something that Matt saw more important than her in his eyes and that was getting his objective completed. Haley didn’t have enough experience to go on a mission of the caliber that the ghost was on right now. Maybe it wasn’t just a lack of experience, but it was a lack of common sense that was keeping her away from going with him. She couldn’t be positive, but whatever it was, she wanted to correct it so they could be together. “Even though I don’t like the idea, I still want to look out for my little sister, so when am I going to get to see you on TV?” Lauren says, with a certain protective in her voice. She has always been able to look out for Haley and now she had to leave her in the hands of some rogue agent, who thinks he is larger than life and the worst part about it was that he was starting to rub off on Haley. “Well like this Tuesday, we are fighting some chicks. They don’t like stand a chance against my man though. He is the best and I am just there to like support him and every now and then come in and slap the bitches,” Haley tells her, smiling wider then ever. She is actually excited for the upcoming match on Turmoil. This wouldn’t be like the PWA where she was forced to take a beating and keep on going. She would be able to sit back and watch, as Matt disposed of Jade and Debris and then they could go about their mission. She still didn’t understand the whole plan, but she did understand that they were going to close PCW by any means necessary. The idea has been seeming better and better then when she first heard it. Now it was only the matter of putting it into place. Matt told her he had a lot of favors that he could call in, but she didn’t know what any of that meant. All she knew is that she had to look out for him and make sure that he didn’t make any mistakes. In truth, she wasn’t just there to watch him; she was there for the spotlight. She was loving the fact that she got to hold power over people and she would be the one to play a hand in the destruction of an organization like PCW. Everyone would remember when the PCW fell and they would remember the blonde princess that played a part in it. She leans her elbows on the small table, feeling the cloth of the fabric below. “You know Lauren, this Tuesday is going to be a lot of fun. I am sick of everyone saying I like can’t do stuff and I like lack common sense because I am like going to make sure those chicks like know I am the best women in the PCW.” Haley wants to believe the statement she just made, but it was made because she is scared. She doesn’t want to disappoint Matt on Turmoil, but she also doesn’t want to look bad. She wants to garner the respect of her new co-workers so they will allow her to do things. Everyone in her life has always held her back because she wasn’t as smart as everyone else. The only reason she even got by was because of looks, though she would never admit this fact to herself. She is drawn out of her thoughts at the sound of her cell phone ringing and quickly she grabs it from her pink purse that is hanging on the chair, that she is currently sitting in. She presses the talk button hoping to hear Matt, but instead it is his handler Denis. “Hello,” she whispers into the receiver, her voice is low because she doesn’t want her sister to know exactly what is going on. “I was sorry Matt had to cancel, but we needed him to do some back work here,” Denis lies, knowing that Matt would kill him if Haley found out what he was really doing. “We have a meeting tomorrow and you need to attend. Basically we need an update on the PCW situation and we need to give you some more things to go off of. Nothing big, but it is a required meeting.” The man on the other end of the phone says no more and hangs up. Haley quietly sits there staring at her sister, hoping that tomorrow morning could come sooner. She would get the plan and then bring down PCW piece by piece until it no longer stands.
