DC/LX/VI Saga: Happy families

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/11/02 Shelly peeked her head around the edge of the small cave they were hiding in, sure enough her suspicions were now facts, the police were asking the various angst ridden teens on the back questions, questions that involved herself and Gideon, and as popular as Shelly was, she wasn't popular with everyone on that beach, and it's hard not to notice a tall dark man with long black hair and long black leather trench coat looking as miserable and misguided as Gideon at a concert like this. 'Gideon?' She looked around the cave looking for where The Sinister Paladin had vanished too. Her response was of Gideon emerging from the shadows around the other side of the cave. 'Sorry, force of habit somewhat' '............Riiiiiiiight, anyway, we have trouble' 'I noticed. Are they getting any closer?' 'They sure are' snapped a voice from behind them. Shelly turned around sharply while Gideon slowly grinned with calmness, but rolled his eyes in annoyance and turned his head slowly to face the owner of the cold bitter voice. 'Lieutenant , over here' The man in uniform yelled before placing two fingers inside his mouth and whistling into the night sky to make certain he caught the sergeant's attention. '...... and get this band out of here!' came the fat of voice of the sergeant as he approached the location at which Gideon and Shelly were located. As he drew closer it was evident that the man was in his late forties, with greying greasy hair slicked back. 'Well well well, what do we have here? A romantic midnight rendezvous? What would your father think Shelly?' 'Fuck you!' The Lieutenant looked at the sergeant, while Gideon looked at them both suspiciously. The Lieutenant than smiled maliciously, a smile that Gideon had seen thousands of times on Jeremiah O'Barr. The Lieutenant than reared his hand back and brought it smashing across the face of Shelly sending her stumbling to the ground. 'Grab him!' The oncoming charge of Gideon was spotted immediately and the sergeant who spotted both him and Shelly moments ago grabbed him instantly and hooked his hands behind his back. 'Soooooo, we have a hero here do we?' shouted the Lieutenant menacingly. He followed up the comment by driving his fist into Gideon's face. The solid cold ring on his wedding finger only added to the eventual force of the blow. Although nothing measured to the force of immeasurable amount of pain of when the Lieutenant began focusing in on the still tender and seeping gash in Gideon's forehead which he suffered from Steph O'Connor. His new found hate for her would have to be put on hold however for now. Eventually the Lieutenant stepped back, rubbing his now aching fist while Gideon's head hung loosely, blood from the gash in his forehead trickling down his nose and dripping off into the sand. 'Well son, I guess it's about time I told you why I'm here for you, or who sent me here for you. Then again I guess you already know that don't you? Gideon tilted his head up slightly to gaze at the Lieutenant. 'I'm assuming you already know this, which is why I'm not gonna waste my breath repeating information you already know.' He then slowly began upholstering his gun from his side. 'So that's why I'm just gonna get to the....' His comment was cut off by Shelly pouncing on the back of the Lieutenant, sending him stumbling down onto the sand, his weapon skidding off further into the cave in which Gideon and Shelly had been hiding in. Shelly quickly ran after it, but the Lieutenant easily tripped her up, the sound of her face smacking unmercifully against a cold hard rock on the brim of the cave made Gideon flinch. Thinking quickly, Gideon manoeuvred his elbow forward and shot it back into the ribs of the sergeant holding him captive. This was enough to allow him to break the hold he had on Gideon. Just as he did the sound of a trigger being pulled back reached Gideon's ears, and he automatically grabbed the sergeant in front of him and spun him round in front of him as a shield as the Lieutenant got trigger happy with his sidearm and pumped bullet after bullet into the sergeants body. As Gideon let go of his hold on the now deceased limp body of the sergeant, the Lieutenant was quick to grab Shelly up to her feet and draw her into him and point his pistol right at the side of her temple. Gideon stopped abruptly. He wished he and Shelly had hid closer to the shore, this cave was too far away from the other civilians, they couldn't see a thing. 'Car, now' uttered the Lieutenant, and gestured for Gideon to get into the dead sergeants car, away from the other teenagers. * * * * * * * * * * * 'You know what annoys the shit out of me?!' The Lieutenant was now driving the motor vehicle with Gideon and Shelly cuffed in the backseat. 'A fucking hero!' Gideon was anything but that. He glanced a look at Shelly, she was scared, worried, panicking. He was surprised to find himself feeling sorry for her. The small tear trickling down her face making her mascara run added to the guilt and sadness he felt for her. 'That is what you tried to be tonight Shelly, and that's what your friend, THE SAVIOR OF THE WORLD, Gideon Poe is trying to do now anyway!' The Lieutenant now began looking into his rear view mirror as he talked to Gideon, his eye's reflecting onto his. 'Well tonight I'm gonna play the villain, and tonight, there aint gonna be no happy ending like there are in the films' Gideon looked right back in the rear view mirror, and maybe it was the sadistic ness the Lieutenant saw in his eyes that forced him to finally look away. Eventually the car came to an abundant stop. Outside it was pitch black. The area was in some deserted woodland, trees covering the surrounding area casting their intimidating shadows over the ground. 'Clarkson, what is the situation?'. The voice came booming statically over the CV radio. 'Dad?!' yelled Shelly! 'Everything is under control here chief' replied the now identified Clarkson. 'Gideon?' 'Maintained chief' 'My daughter?' 'Maintained also?' 'Daddy this fucking psycho is lying, he's going to kill us!' '.............................. How much has she told him?' 'A lot I'd imagine chief. Whatever she told him it was too much if you ask me.' 'Daddy!?' '...............................' 'Daddy he's going to kill us?!' An eternal daunting silence fell between the responses. His final response was one that even filled Gideon with a cold stab of shock and horror. '.............I know' With that the CV transmission cut out, as did Shelly's relationship with her father. Her face a caption of grief and undeniable shock as Clarkson turned round with a sly edged smile on his face as he pointed his pistol at her. All Gideon could do was watch. '......DADDDD-' The gunshot was the exclamation point on her life on this earth as it fried through car sending a muffled breath from her lungs as it tore through her body. She was dead. All Gideon could do was watch on, not with horror or pain, but just with regret. He had been through to much horror and pain in his life already. Yet he had no time to contemplate his emotions. Clarkson had his sights set on him next. 'You ready freak boy?' Gideon didn't reply. Instead he flipped the latch on the inside door and rolled backwards out of there onto the cold wet grass outside, using his knees to get to his feet due to his hands being cuffed behind him. 'Bastard!' He was now the emotionless Avatar Of Death he had been morphed into over these last few years. Every action he committed next was theoretical and automatic. From kicking the driver's door back into Clarkson as he opened it. From kicking him in the ribs as he stumbled to the ground. From smiling grossly as he watched him crawl on his hands and knees clutching his broken wrist that occurred when the door slammed shut on it. From kicking him repeatedly in the back of the neck as reached the edge of the nearby lake. From looking into his eyes, and seeing a mind that had been manipulated by some higher power that went by the name of DC/LX/VI to such an extent that he would kill an innocent girl without thinking twice. He saw a man who had been overpowered by a force to become a killer. Gideon saw himself in Clarkson. He saw his relationship with Jeremiah as to what Clarkson's relationship with DC/LX.VI was. 'I will release you' This time it was Gideon's foot stamping down poetically onto Clarkson's throat that was the exclamation point on another persons life. This time it was Gideon playing the part of the author jotting down the exclamation point. * * * * * * * * * As the gleaming moonlight glimmered down onto the lake, it made Gideon appear nothing more than a black pawn on a chess board. His head hung low, his long black hair obscuring his handsome troubled face. He slowly tensed his body and began channelling it into his cuffed wrists. Slowly and methodically the faint grinding sound of him gritting his teeth together was replaced by a gurgling moan as he raised his head to face the moon. The metal links bending even more so until they flew apart into the night majestically. Gideon let out a soft moan and placed his hands into the lake his wrists had been badly cut and scarred, but nothing seriously wounding. The crimson dashes of blood on his wrists came across black in the lake under the moonlight, yet he didn't notice. Right now he thought he was in no mood to notice anything. But he was wrong. 'Clarkson, are ya done there yet?!' Gideon turned his head slowly to the police car, it was the CV radio. 'Clarkson?!' Thinking automatically Gideon hauled himself up and jogged to the car, before sliding into the drivers seat and responding. 'I read ya' 'Good. Did you get rid of them okay' '.....It's a dead issue' 'Heh, I'm glad. Now you are aware of the situation, we gather tonight, 11:00pm sharp, the warehouse just out past the interstate....' 'I know the one' Truth be told there was only one abandoned warehouse within 200 miles, it wasn't hard to assume which one he was on about. 'Not the most fancy and suitable location for our event, but it will have to do. It is away from the public eye, and will avoid suspicion.' 'I understand' 'Remember, worship and believe in all that is he' 'You too' 'Farewell' 'Farewell' With that the signal cut out and Gideon slowly rested his head back against the leather of the drivers seat. He now had a time and a location, and only had a few hours to get there. Whatever was being planned was either going to bring something to a bitter corrupt end, or a vile and sadistic new beginning.
