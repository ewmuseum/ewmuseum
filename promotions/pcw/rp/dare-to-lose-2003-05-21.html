Dare to Lose

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/05/03 :Look upon this world::::Fell the hatred and the fear::::As it glides through the air of uncertainty::::This air, the air we breath:: ::Feel the cold sensation of failure::::Feel your body shiver uncontrollably::::It bites at your skin with no mercy::::Could this really be what you've been searching for:: ::Or is this just the price we pay to reach our goals::::We put ourselves through it all for just that moment of glory::::Only a few seconds is what we crave for::::What a massive price to pay for so little:: ::But that warm feeling of success::::After starving without it for what seems like an eternity::::Finally tasting it reaches the heights of heaven itself::::However others strive for exactly the same thing:: ::I've not been starved for a long time::::However a shocking defeat has fallen upon me::::One that I never saw coming until it was too late::::I don't plan on being starved twice:: ::Yet that's what everyone else sees::::A man who's on the road to failure::::I'm facing the man that none of you give me a chance against::::I might as well be facing the all mighty god himself:: ::Well I plan to prove you all wrong::::I plan to show that immortal this man isn't::::And the cost I'm willing to pay is high::::All so that I won't be buried alive::'Only those who dare to fail greatly can ever achieve greatly.' -Robert Francis Kennedy Just a few days ago, I decided to look within myself. It's been a long time since I've plucked up the courage to look within myself and it felt twice as long as it actually was. I was at home, sitting on my couch as I watched the TV as pictures of PCW glared at me. I don't remember what match it was, it's competitor's are irrelevant now. I just listened to it all, my eyes were open but they were not watching. Instead I let my ears tell me a different story. Bodies hitting the mat over and over again as the fans took pleasure from what I could hear was pain. The voices most clear where those of Jeff Marx and Dave Kern. The men that watch what we wrestlers go through every night. They see the determination within us as we display it to the world with the best seats in the house. I listened to them and what I heard from them was the same doubt I had heard from so many others. Usually I'm the man who says that words, or more specifically words that are meant to bruise my ego do not effect me. I brush them off like they are nothing and the really words that have effect aren't spoken but sent in the ring. Well this time was different and if it's a weakness then so be it. I'll admit with all the others that I am not the world's greatest. I've never claimed to be! I'm not like all the other individuals that claim superiority! I am not going to be like those who have fought for the Extreme Title and failed! I'm not a Xavier Kannon, Dorn Tracer or even a Tyler Nelson who claimed it was there time because they were the better man. Yet I do claim one thing and that is I have what it takes to be the man to knock Stryker of his throne. I have what it takes to be the man who shows this man is not a god! That's what I saw when I looked inside myself. After hearing the supposed experts give their two cents and all of them writing me off as a no hoper in this match, I looked inside of myself to see if this was true. I've never been looked up on as such an underdog! Perhaps there really was no chance for me, no hope! However what I saw showed me that the doubt that I felt for a few seconds before was indeed misplaced. What I saw was an image of myself!Standing tall and overcoming the odds that so many others failed to get past.I saw a man that was proven worthy of being a champion.I saw myself in the past!Back then I was the future!I've progressed far from what I was back then. I've shown that to you all. Whether you dispute it or not the evidence is there for you all to see! I see how I've moved on and I smiled.
