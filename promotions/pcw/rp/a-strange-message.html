A Strange Message...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Recently... [[The scene begins to fade in on a lengthy corridor. The winding halls lead this way and that, and as the cameras pan out, the crimson-carpeted floor below the cream-coloured ceiling becomes viewable. Suddenly, and quite shockingly, the crisp sound of continuous buzzing grows audible. It is of course the sound of the air ventilation in this hotel lobby, and almost as quickly as we hear this sound, the noise of distant voices speedily fills the airwaves.]] “I don’t even know who the Duat my partner IS!” [[This voice is familiar, and though secluded, is clearly male.]] “Don’t worry about it! Trust me, everything’ll be just fine.” [[Like the familiar male tones of the first voice, this one too is quite recognisable, and obviously female. The sound of light footsteps upon the red carpet slowly consumes the air also. There are of course, two sets of footsteps, but as they grow closer, it seems that more fill the corridor, and then more, and then more; as though a regiment of marching soldiers tread upon this particular hallway.]] “DON’T WORRY?! That damned Skylar Green has done this, I know it! He thinks this is all just a GAME.” “And it isn’t? It seems to me that you two are just indulging in a friendly rivalry.” [[The man can be heard sighing.]] “It ‘appears’ that way, doesn’t it? And by the Gods, that’s just how I’D like it, but Green. Hmm. I don’t trust him anymore. He’s planning something, I know it by Horus.” [[The tread of feet sounds louder now. And quite unexpectedly a large entourage of heavily armoured men, lead by a male and a female Egyptian round the same corner the cameras have remained upon for the past few minutes.]] The Pharaoh: “What you must realize, my love, is that Skylar WAS our friend. Now, I’m not so sure. He seems to have grown quite hostile about my presence lately!” Isiy: “That’s just your mind, Titu! Why, I’ve watched Green at home on the TV and he seems to be just messing around with you – a friendly rivalry thing…like I already said.” [[Titu scowls.]] The Pharaoh: “And that’s another thing! Why the Duat you had to purchase that wretched thing is beyond even me! We need not watch that corrupted thing! Why, it’s because of them that I unleashed that harsh month-long beating on Chet King!” [[Isiy shrugs.]] Isiy: “Does it really matter? We’re living in the past!” [[Titu frowns, remembering the words of Skylar Green from this past Turmoil.]] The Pharaoh, murmuring: “…The past...” [[Isiy turns to her deluded husband and cocks an eyebrow.]] Isiy: “Sorry?” [[Titu slides back out of his daydream state.]] The Pharaoh: “Huh? Oh, nothing…nothing.” [[Isiy shrugs once more as the marching troops behind them continue to walk in unison, never missing a beat, and their halberds held high. They move onward for a while and eventually round another corner into a well-decorated area of the hotel, a lift situated at the opposite end of the hall on which they now walk.]] Isiy: “I can’t believe you brought this garrison of soldiers with us.” [[Titu heaves a heavy sigh as though he’s growing tired of his wife’s complaints.]] The Pharaoh: “They are NEEDED! Without them I’m NOTHING!” Isiy: “Titu, you’re a W-R-E-S-T-L-E-R, you hardly need fifteen armoured idiots following us around all day to ‘protect’ us.” [[Some of the guards share a painful glance, a little embarrassed by the Egyptian beauty’s words.]] Isiy: “If we were outside and it looked as though we were about to be mugged or something, then fair enough, but we’re in the middle of a frickin’ H-O-T-E-L!” [[Titu shrugs off the comments, waving a hand dismissively as he usually does when confronted by his wife’s doubts.]] The Pharaoh: “Look, do I complain about your foolish fancies? TV; Radio…modern clothing…Urgghh…” [[Xansiv shakes his head, disappointed.]] Isiy: “Unlike YOU, maybe I just don’t enjoy living in ANCIENT times.” [[Hearing this, a few guards giggle. Titu blasts them a hateful stare and immediately the laughs stop. With a hushed voice, Titu turns to Isiy and scowls.]] The Pharaoh: “Halt your observations, fair lady! For, you’re making me appear stupendous around my troops!” [[Unfazed by his comments, Isiy laughs a little, quickly shaking off his seriousness with a cynical dismissal.]] Isiy: “Oh, geez. Look, I think you have MORE to think about that these guys, right? I mean, you have Green this coming Fusion, and then again on Turmoil in that tag match—” The Pharaoh: “And as I’ve said, I have no idea who the Duat my partner will be!” Isiy: “Which is why you need to R-E-L-A-X, look at the options and consider every possibility.” [[Pharaoh nods, loosening up his shoulders.]] The Pharaoh: “Right. Yeah.” [[Titu reaches into his loincloth and brings forth a small, golden key. He soon comes to a halt outside a tall, well-polished door branded with the numbers ‘31’ and pushes the key into the lock. Unlocking the entrance, the Egyptian Madman moves inside and slumps into a conveniently placed chair nearby.]] The Pharaoh: “What I fail to understand is his motives behind this new interest in me.” [[Isiy takes her place on the corner of the room’s bed with a huff. Titu sighs once more, rubbing his forehead with one hand whilst beckoning for someone to come forth from the crowd of guards piling into the room.]] The Pharaoh: “Come forth, Azrateph, my royal priest.” [[The crowd of troops part way as a small, thin man adorned in silken robes steps forward.]] Azrateph, falling to one knee before Xansiv: “What is you bidding, milord?” [[Titu looks down at the Egyptian cleric and summons him to rise.]] The Pharaoh: “Tell me what the Gods have said. You have had your vision, yes?” [[The small, dark-skinned man nods, closing his eyes with deep thought.]] Azrateph: “Of course.” The Pharaoh: “Then indulge me with their wise words.” [[Azrateph opens his cold, brown eyes once more and looks up into those of The Pharaoh; his expression is that of seriousness: it even seems to take Titu by surprise.]] Azrateph: “They warn of a new enemy, my liege. You must watch yourself in battle, and trust no one.” [[Titu’s brow furrows with confusion.]] The Pharaoh: “A NEW enemy? What of Dorn and Bruce?” Azrateph, quickly: “NO ONE, milord.” [[Rubbing his chin thoughtfully, the now bewildered Titu Xansiv can only consider this strange message with an open mind. He is silent for some time until the Egyptian priest breaks the metaphorical ice.]] Azrateph: “Do you require my services further, my pharaoh?” [[Titu snaps out of his state and waves a hand for Azrateph to leave.]] The Pharaoh: “No, no. Feel free to go back to your room, my friend. Take some rest before the show.” [[The holy man nods before hastily taking his leave.]] The Pharaoh: “A new enemy? What could this mean?” Isiy: “Skylar is yet to be your real enemy. Perhaps the Gods speak of Green?” [[Titu considers this for a moment and then shakes his head, doubtful.]] The Pharaoh: “No…that seems unnecessary. Surely my Gods wouldn’t speak of someone I ALREADY distrust.” [[Isiy throws herself back on the bed, looking up at the ceiling.]] Isiy: “Then I guess you’ll just have to wait and see…” [[And with that, the scene fades to black on the anxious face of The Pharaoh.]]
