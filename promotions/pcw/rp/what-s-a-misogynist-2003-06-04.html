What's a misogynist?

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-4-03 Adam walks through a hallway in his home. He’s dressed in a pair of black slacks with a gray Polo shirt neatly tucked into his pants. His hair has been recently trimmed, Adam pulls his hand and runs his hand through his hair and groans. He walks into a room and notices Jaslene sitting on the fair side of the room in front of a computer. Her back is to him. He crosses the room. 'Hey Jas, do you think I’m going bald?' Adam calls out feeling the crown of his head. Jaslene doesn’t answer him. She just keeps staring at the computer. Adam stands behind her and leans over her. 'Adam…' she says softly, 'Do you know what a misogynist is?' Adam stands back upright and thinks for a moment, still rubbing his head. 'I can’t say that I do Jas, why?' he replies. 'A misogynist is someone who hates women Adam.' Jaslene says sternly in an almost disappointed tone. 'What you mean like a homosexual?' Adam says finally pulling his hand from his head and placing it on her shoulder. She shrugs it off and stands up quickly. 'That’s not funny Adam.' Jaslene says walking across the room. 'So what does this have to do with me?' Adam asks. 'You’re wrestling one on Fusion.' Jaslene responds, keeping her serious look and matching it with an equally serious tone. 'And open misogynist at that…Stone Burns.' 'Ok so he’s some loser who couldn’t hack it with the ladies and now hates them. Or maybe his mommy didn’t give him enough love as a kid.' Adam says in an obvious attempt to lighten the mood. 'It’s not funny Adam!' Jaslene exclaims. Adam finally realizes the severity of the situation by examining her face. He slowly crosses the room and stands in front of Jaslene. She turns her face to the side as he tries to look her in the eyes. 'What is it?' Adam asks softly reaching out and taking her face into his hands. 'What’s wrong?' Jaslene doesn’t answer him but doesn’t look at him either. Adam bends down and kisses her on the forehead. 'It’s ok Jas,' Adam says. 'It’s just that…that…' Jaslene stutters as tears begin to well up in the corners of her eyes. 'Yes?' Adam says continually staring into Jaslene’s eyes. '…Gina’s father was a misogynist…' Jaslene responds trailing off. She puts her face into his chest to muffle her sobs. Adam brings his arm around to her back and strokes her head with his free hand. She sobs for another few minutes before finally pulling away from him. 'He would get the worst when he would drink,' Jaslene says through a sob, 'He…he…would continually accuse me and Gina of being lesbians and whores and just about every other name you can think of.' 'Christ…' Adam slowly responds. Jaslene pulls away from him completely and heads back to the computer. Adam hesitates for a moment as Jaslene sits at the computer. The clicking of the keyboard sounds behind Adam as he still stands in the middle of the room. Adam turns his head slightly. 'What are you doing?' he asks. 'What else do you think I’m doing?' Jaslene responds wiping some tears from her cheek. 'I’m researching Stone Burns.' 'What for?' Adam asks turning all the way around to face her back. 'To help you kick the living shit out of him of course.' Jaslene fires back, 'I don’t like him plain and simple.' 'Have you ever even met the man Jas?' Adam asks in response, 'How can you not like someone you haven’t even met?' 'HE’S A MISOGYNIST!' Jaslene screams slamming her fist down onto the desk. The force from the impact rattles the keyboard and shakes the monitor. Adam walks over and slowly places his hands on the back of her neck. He begins to rub her neck. 'I don’t care who you are but that is one thing I will not tolerate in people.' Jaslene says rolling her neck around. 'I will take him down if needs be but that little asshole won’t be walking away from Fusion that much I can guarantee.' 'Please Jas, you insult me…' Adam says with a grin. 'You won’t have to worry about him making it through Fusion, I’m going to beat his ass from pillar to post.' Jaslene grins as she pulls up Burns’ bio from the PCW main site. 'Well let’s see he’s only six foot three, you have him in regards to that.' Jaslene says looking up to Adam. He’s staring intently at the screen. Taking in every bit of information that the bio can give him. 'He’s only two hundred and fifty eight pounds?' Adam says with a snort, 'This is going to be a joke. I’ve got height, weight and most likely skill over this loser. What does he have?' Jaslene shrugs her shoulders. 'Well it should be a good way to break your losing streak anyway.' Jaslene says with a chuckle. 'Come on Jas, you know I don’t like to talk about that kind of stuff. Wins and losses don’t mean anything to me anymore.' Adam says returning the chuckle. 'Anymore? When did it ever mean anything to you?' Jaslene says raising her brow. 'Oh…well…uh…' Adam stutters. He takes a step and brings his arm back to his chin. 'Well?' Jaslene says. 'I’m thinking!' Adam snaps back in a half serious, half joking tone. 'Can’t a man have time to think about something?' Jaslene just laughs at him. 'Goddamit! I hate it when you do this to me Adam.' Jaslene exclaims. 'When I do what?' Adam inquires, 'I’m trust trying to answer your question is all Jas.' 'I just want to be pissed off right now but you can’t let that be can you?' Jaslene answers. 'I can.' Adam answers shaking his head from side to side. 'You are such an ass.' Jaslene says turning her attention back to the computer. 'I know, I know but to answer your question I did care about winning and losing when I was in the AWR but that seems like ages ago so I suppose you are right and that I haven’t cared lately.' Adam answers. 'What else should I know about this moron?' 'He seems to be a brawler for the most part though he does have a couple of submission moves…see?' Jaslene says pointing to the screen. Adam leans in and looks the screen over. He places his hand on Jaslene’s shoulder and she in turn places her hand on his. 'Nothing that seems to difficult to escape or reverse, this match really shouldn’t be too difficult.' Adam says standing back up, 'Still I shouldn’t over look this Burns character.' Adam takes a step back allowing Jaslene to swivel the chair around. She stands up and wraps her arms around him. 'Just promise me one thing Adam?' Jaslene says pressing her cheek to his midsection. 'What’s that?' Adam says looking down at her. 'Win or lose,' she says looking up to him meeting his eyes, 'Beat the shit out of him for me, ok?' 'I’ll do my best Jas.' Adam says bending down and kissing her forehead. She squeezes him tightly before letting go. 'Now what were you saying about going bald?' she says with a giggle. 'Nothing! I am not going bald! I’m only twenty six!' he exclaims. 'Uh honey, you are twenty eight now.' Jaslene says with a grin. 'Shhhh…no one has to know.' Adam says pressing his finger to his lips. Jaslene gives him a look before pushing him away. He turns and walks away, dropping his head in disappointment.
