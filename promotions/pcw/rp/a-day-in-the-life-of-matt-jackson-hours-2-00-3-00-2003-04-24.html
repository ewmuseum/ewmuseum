A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 2:00-3:00

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/03 Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 2:00 am Matt jolts awake, slow to regain his pose. He tries to move his arms and notices that they are bound and then as quickly as he awoke, he starts to remember what happened to him. Looking around, he notices that he is in a rather tiny room; it is to dark for him to totally notice where he is. Then without warning he feels sick in the pit of his stomach and he vomits. Listening he hears it hit the dirt floor with a thud and he continues to look around, trying to figure out what is going on. He notes that his clothes are missing and his body is sore and bruised. All around him is pure darkness, the walls of his prison are only a few feet away, he could reach them if he moved his legs just a little, but due to his hands being bound, he knows that it would be a bad idea. Taking a deep breath, he scans the ropes with his brown eyes and begins to figure that the situation is futile. It is his own fault though, the famed Agent made a mistake when he got to cocky and now the fullness of his mistake is coming into play. I can’t believe I just tried to walk in the front door. What a rookie mistake, but live and learn I guess. There has to be someway out of here, you are the great agent, think! Moving around some more, Matt gives up on loosening the ropes. The sweat is building up and his normally spiky brown hair is now painted to his face by his perspiration. The tiny room is stuffy and that isn’t helping Matt’s condition any. He is all ready starting to become dehydrated and the hotter the room gets, the more he loses. He wants to try to break free of the ropes, but any more movement is going to cause his body to lose more water then he can expend right now. Matt knows all the characteristics of a situation like this; he has been trained for it. Letting his full weight rest on his arms, Matt gives his legs a much-needed break. He doesn’t know how long he was out for or how long he has been standing, but his legs are wearing down on him and they need a break. He is also hoping that his weight on the rope, might just force it to break, but he knows better then to get his hopes up. Unless something some how falls in his favour he knows that this could go on for a while. Whoever is doing this wants something from him, if they didn’t they would have already killed him and gotten it over with. Now he is just going to play the hand that is dealt to him and take everything one minute at a time. His attention is drawn away from keeping himself up right, as a door to the right of him swings open. The flash of light from the outside blinds him and before he knows it he collapses back on his legs. The door is slammed shut and the room goes dark again. Matt’s eyes reopen and adjust back to the darkness. The man in front of him is impossible to make out in the blackness. Moving closer, Matt can feel the man’s heated breath on his bare skin. It collides with his skin, like a pebble hitting a huge rock, just adding to the smell of stale vomit in the room. “Well, Agent Jackson,” the man begins, letting his voice fill the small room. Taking a step closer to Matt, the man sticks his face right up to his. “We have some business to take care of.” The man sticks his stubby finger into Matt’s barrel of a chest and lets out an evil laugh. What the hell is this guy talking about? I better think of something quick to get out of this situation. I am not going to be able to last for long if this keeps up. “Who-who are you?” Matt asks, feeling a rush of pain go through his body. His back is becoming more and more aggravated Not only from the sheer anxiety of standing, but from the rash of sweat that is trickling into the open wounds he received from the taser. He is used to feeling pain, he receives it on a weekly basis every time he steps into a PCW ring, but this is different. He isn’t fresh and his body is still recovering from Spring Fling. “You would love it if I gave you my name wouldn’t you Agent Jackson, but don't worry my name doesn't matter,” the man replies, adding in a stinging back hand to further is point. “I am the one in charge here and I will be the one asking the questions.” Taking a step back from Matt, the man relievers another backhand and a smile comes across his fat face, revealing his chipped yellow teeth. The man runs his hands through his greasy black hair and steps back up to Matt, once again letting his rotten breath touch Matt’s tanned skin. This is going to go on for hours and no one is going to be the wiser of me being here. I might as well just sit in for the ride I guess. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 2:30 am The beating is showing no signs of stopping. One backhand after the next to the face of Matt, adding even more pain to his all ready ravished body. The blood is already trickling from his split lip and the small scratches on his face. The man is relentless showing no signs of stopping, as the on slot continues, blow after blow raining down on the ex-Ranger. I am not going to be able to survive much more of this. I have all ready lost too much and if I just continue to bleed out, I am going to die. “What do you want to know?” Matt chokes out, tasting his own blood even more. A smile parts his angular face at the notion though, his white teeth are caked in the fresh liquid, but it doesn’t matter anymore. He licks his lips getting all the blood off, waiting for the man to respond. “I thought I told you I would be the one asking the questions,” he screams, giving Matt a quick kick to the stomach, forcing his legs to go rubbery and force his weight onto the unforgiving rope, which rips at his wrists, causing the skin to burn and tear even more. Gritting his teeth, he tries to double over to relieve some of the pain, but the rope has no give. Pleased with his work, the man takes a step back and pulls out a pack of cigarettes from the back pocket of his jeans. Taking one from the box, he places it in his mouth and lights it. Casually taking a few puffs and relaxing, his eyes still focused on the half passed out CIA Agent. A small puddle of blood is forming under Matt, as the man watches, some of it being absorbed by the dirt and vomit. Finishing his cigarette, the man drops it to the floor, sending embers everywhere. The man steps back up ready to hash out another beating, only to take a step back as Matt begins to cough up blood. The man backs up, giving the agent sometime to recover. He needs him alive and beating him to death would probably end up getting him killed too. He cracks his knuckles and tries to let the storm pass. “I am going to give you a little time to recover there Agent Jackson. I wouldn’t want you to die on me,” the man says, with a sinister smirk. The only response he gets is Matt, vomiting up blood. The situation is worse then he originally thought. When they were trying to wear him down when he was out, they must have damaged something major. Matt violently coughs one last time and then his head falls still and his body goes limp. Getting worried the man rushes over and checks for a pulse. Matt is still breathing and there is a pulse, he is just out cold once again. The man walks towards the door and kicks a little bit of the dirt up on his way out. Opening the door, the light floods the room again. Letting the broken body of Matt Jackson be exposed to the light. Cuts and bruises lay well exposed all over his 5’8” tanned frame, as he stands in a pool of his own blood and vomit. The man slams the door behind him, letting the room fall dark once again and letting Matt hang there, hanging on by a thread of his life. Am I going to die? I didn’t expect it to end like this, but I guess everything happens for a reason. It has been a nice run in the PCW and I am going to miss it, but most of all I am going to miss Haley. I am going to miss life and I am going to miss nailing the sleeper cell and protecting this country. 3:00 AM
