They came like the Black Plague (Part 1 of ?)

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-3-02 Everyone who is everyone that has got married knows that there is a couple days in the year that you would wish that could you just be in a deep coma and that day is when the in-laws come over. My Wife, Stephanie is always running around cleaning the house putting pictures of my in-laws in specific place while my eleven-month year old son Toby and I just sit on the couch watching cartoons. She runs around like a raccoon with rabies trying to make everything perfect and is always in a frustrated mood. Stephanie: Jeff why can’t you get up from the couch and help me? You told me not to help you out remember hunny? Stephanie: Well I need your help! Ok calm down, what do you want me to do? Stephanie: I don’t know yet! Calm down Steph, I’ll get Toby cleaned up while you take care of whatever you got to do. Stephanie: Thank you Jeff. No problem babe, it will be all right. So while Steph is running around doing whatever she has to do, I decided to clean up Toby before his grandparents come. He lies in his crib as I get his clothes out just smiling and moving his legs around wanted to run around the house. All right Toby, my boy let’s look good for grandma and grandpa. I take out this stupid looking puppy shirt my mother in law got him for Christmas. My wife wants him to be wearing all the stuff my in-laws got him. So I got put these matching Puppy pants on him and these socks that look like they came out of Richard Simmons closet. Dam I hate that dam shirt it makes him look like he wants to watch an exercise video of Richard Simmons. Shit what time is it? Holy crap they are going to be here any second and I got my PCW shirt on, I better get dressed! I put some “Peter, Paul and Mary” on the CD player to keep Toby awake and then I headed down to my bedroom. I put on this t-shirt that my in-laws got me that look like it came from an outlet store. It says something in Spanish but I don’t know what it is. It reads: “Yo con estupido!” They always laugh when I wear it, but I don’t know why it’s so funny. The doorbell rings Oh shit there here! Better get Toby! I run out of our bedroom and run into Toby’s room. He’s half asleep and looks at me half smiling like he just took a drug of some sort. Dam Peter, Paul and Mary! I need to get him some new CD’s. That shit puts him asleep. Stephanie: Jeff, my parents are here! Coming hunny! I walk down the stairs with Toby in my arms and there they stand, “The Black Plague” smiling so happily, but oh so evil. They begin to smile as I walk down the stairs with Toby in my arms and they both put their arms out like they want to give me a hug. What the hell are they smoking? They want to give me a hug. Mother In-Law: Come here, how are you? I’m doing just…… The next thing I know they grab Toby out of my arms and start hugging him, smothering him with kisses. Those bastards. They then turn around no longer facing me like I’m invisible. Then my father in law turns around and notices that I’m also exist in the same room they do. Father In-Law: Well hello Jeff, how are you? I’m doing great sir, how have you been? Father In-Law: I’m doing just fine. Hey Honey? Mother In-Law: Yes? Father In-Law: Look Jeff is wearing the t-shirt we got him from Mexico. Mother In-Law: (Laughs) Oh yes. That is a great shirt. Stephanie: Mom what does that shirt actually say? Mother In-Law: It says, “I went to Mexico” in Spanish. My Mother In-Law then whispers something into my Father In-Law’s ear that I can hear very clearly. Mother In-Law: (Whispering) You know what it says right? Father In-Law: (Whispering) Yeah, “I’m with stupid.” They both laugh out loud. I on the other hand am not laughing because I can hear them clear as day. Those bastards got me this shirt for their own pleasure. Stephanie: What is so funny Mom? Mother In-Law: We forgot something. Stephanie: What did you forget? Mother In-Law: We are staying here for the next week! SURPRISE! Oh No! This can’t be happening Stephanie: Why didn’t you tell me before I could have cleaned everything up for you in the spare bedroom? Father In-Law: Oh it’s all right dear. We wanted to come see our Grandson, Daughter and her husband. Wow, what a pleasant surprise it is. Mother In-Law: Well let’s go into the kitchen and get something to eat. Um Jeff? Yes? Mother In-Law: Our bags are in the trunk; you can put them in the room. Sure Mother In-Law: Thank you dear. Those bastards! I hate In-laws. I walk outside to their car and pop the trunk where an overload of suitcases pops out landing on me. Oh no this week is going to be hell….
