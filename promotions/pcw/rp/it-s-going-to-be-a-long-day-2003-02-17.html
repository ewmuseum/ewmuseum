It's Going To Be A Long Day

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/03 As the sun crept through the blinds and shone into the bedroom, the blue eyes across from me shot open as I looked over and noticed that Chris was awake and ready to go. He sat up in bed as I rolled over onto my side and pulled the sheets over my head, trying to hide from the morning and reappear in a world of sleep. It was not that I did not want to see Chris, I just was not feeling the way I was used to feeling. The room was spinning in circles and my eyes could not manage to open anymore than half-way. I was a total mess. 'Tessa Bear you have to get up!' Chris' voice rang the usual morning tune that would take several times of repeating before I would even begin to stir. But this morning it seemed absolutely ineffective. I did not even want to roll over to look at Chris. I just wanted to stay in bed, I was far too exhausted to get up. 'I don't want to get up.' I whined as the pressure to my head increased and I felt like I would blackout at any moment. Instead of turning to face Chris though I buried my head further into the pillow. 'I know you're tired, but you have to get up!' Chris said as he sat down beside me on the bed and pulled the sheets off from and started to run his cold hands up and down my back. In response to the cold my body shivered and Chris looked over at me on the bed wondering what triggered such a response. 'GAH! Chris your hands are cold!' I shouted with the little energy I had left inside me as I pushed his hand away. Instead of putting it on my back now he moved it up to my forehead and looked just as shocked at the temperature as I did when his cold hand came in contact with my skin once more. 'Oh my god Tessa Bear, you're burning up!' Was the response given as he pulled his hand back quickly before I could swat him with a hand of my own. 'Oh my god Chris, your hands are cold. Keep them away!' I shouted as I buried my body under the blanket once more to avoid any further contact with Chris' icy hands. As he walked away from the bed into the bathroom that was built into the bedroom I closed my eyes and started to fade back into another peaceful sleep, but before I could get too comfortable I was awakened by the stampeding of three children all after one thing, my attention. 'Tessaaaaa!' The chorus of three whining children still in their pajama's rang out as they gathered around my bed. 'Go bug Chris!' I muttered into the pillow which turned my words into nothing but a mumble, but the kids heard enough to understand what they were to do and off they marched over to the door of the bathroom on it. 'Chrisssss!' They sung out now to Chris who was inside the shower and more than likely going to tell them to go see me for help with whatever the problem was. 'I'll be out in a minute!' Chris shouted from inside the shower as the sound of the nozzle could be heard as he shut it off and more than likely grabbed a towel to dry himself off. 'Tessssaaaa' They cried out once more as they turned back in my direction and then climbed up onto the bed. Alex sitting by my head, Evan by my feet, and Christa in the middle of the two. Getting only a groan for a response Alex decided to find out just what was wrong with me and reached out for my forehead with his cold morning hands. 'GAH! I thought I told you...' I brought my head off the pillow and looked over noticing that it was only Alex, but still not wanting to feel anymore cold hands I gently pushed him away before the dizziness overcame me once more and brought my head back onto the pillow. 'Tessa are you okay?' Asked the concerned Evan as he crawled up beside Alex and looked down at me as I closed my eyes and attempted to go back to sleep once more, trying to block the children out of my mind hoping that Chris would be getting out of the bathroom sometime soon and take care of them for me. 'She's sick! Of course she's not okay stupid!' Alex yelled at Evan as Evan squeezed in between Alex and Christa. 'She's sick?' The two younger ones questioned with confusion in their eyes. 'Yes, feel her head if you don't believe me.' Alex used in defense for his words and proof that he was older and wiser than the other two. 'Poor Tessa.' Christa said as she ran her tiny hands up and down the back of my shirt as if rubbing my back in a motherly manner. 'Is she going to have to go to the hospital?' Young Evan asked, too young to understand that just a touch of a illness of this sort was nothing major or deadly. 'Of course not, idiot.' Alex retorted, shoving Evan off the edge of the bed, causing him to tumble off the bed backwards and land on the ground two feet below. 'Tessssaaaaaa!' Evan shrieked from his new seat on the floor. He stood up and dusted himself up, then jumped back on the bed and sure enough went after Alex for revenge. Before I knew it I was caught in the middle of a brawl between the two boys. 'Evan! Alex! Stop fighting, Tessa's trying to sleep!' Christa shouted like a mother who is disgusted with her child's action above the boys who were wrestling each other on top of the bed, each of them trying to gain the upper hand on the other to prove their point. 'Make me.' Alex said as he turned his head over in Christa's direction sticking out his tongue and then focusing his attention back in on Evan who was not defeating Alex with strength, but outsmarting him by sending his foot forward into Alex's crotch. 'AHHHH! YOU JERK!' Alex screamed falling down onto the bed, his eyes starting to water in pain. Evan merely responded with a smirk as he jumped off the bed knowing that he would be in trouble if I could get the strength to shout, or if Chris finished with his shower. 'Alex don't yell! Tessa needs rest so she can beat El Romantico on Tuesday!' Christa lectured to her brother who now sat up and looked like he might strangle Evan the moment he laid arms on him. 'Then tell that little brat not to touch me, little miss perfect.' Alex snarled at Christa, sick of hearing her lectures and not paying much attention to her words since he felt he was older and much more mature than she could ever be. 'I'm not a brat!' Evan squealed, lunging forward at Alex once again as another brawl between the two began just as Chris walked out of the shower. 'KAMI! What is going on out here!' Chris exclaimed in total shock. On one side of the bed there was Alex and Evan rolling around trying to beat th hell into each other and then on the other side there was Christa sitting beside me as I watched on helplessly, unable to do much more than watch and hope that they would end this senseless fighting. 'He started it!' Alex and Evan said in unison, each of them pointing at the other as they turned around and faced Chris who looked like he was ready to scream, realizing that it was going to indeed be a long day with me fighting some illness and him being stranded with three very grumpy children. 'Yeah well I'm ending it. Get off the bed and go get changed, we got a long day ahead of us.' Chris said as he scooped Christa into his arms and headed towards the door as Alex and Evan jumped off the bed and ran ahead of Chris into the hallway towards their bedroom knowing that Chris meant business. 'Chris I can help you if you need it.' I said as I sat up in bed as the dizziness attacked once more, rendering my rosy red cheeks into a snow white tint. 'No Tessa Bear I can handle it, you just rest.' He said, walking over to my side of the bed and leaning over to kiss me on the cheek before walking off into the hall to began what would be a long day for both him and I. My only hope would be that Chris could handle the three rascals and I could somehow overcome this sickness before Tuesday rolled around.
