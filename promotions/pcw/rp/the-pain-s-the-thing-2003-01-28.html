The pain's the thing.

28 January 2003

Title: The pain's the thing. Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Jan. 28, 2003 The battle...lost. Romantico had gotten hold of the Cruiserweight Title..Not from any measure of outwrestling me, at least. The training had paid off, as I kept him on the defensive for the best of the match.....She picked a bad time to try to help, but she meant well enough...Only for that damn Tomas to take advantage of the opportune chance to get one over on her challenger...That decision being cause to distract me from the task at hand. She was sticking her nose into MY business, not for the first time and certainly not the last...The mental analysis was enough to keep me from distraction from myself, but she had deliberately interfered with my work. She didn't deserve to hold the TV Title in the first place, winning by virtue of her position while unconscious and riding off the Rednecks from that point on...I had already seen the Trapezoid cheat his way to gold, and we'd taken it from him, so why should this have been anything different now? But no...She somehow did it again, on her own in spite of the beating she took....But there was too much need for retribution now. She robbed us once more, and I wasn't in the mood to have her cheating keep getting in our way, just like the tag title match at Alliances, just like the TV Title match the HIT had tapped out in, and now like that which was just a little earlier in the night... I wanted it over. I wanted HER over. And I got what I wanted for now... ....Now, I had Chet King to deal with. It had been so long since he'd lucked into the Cruiserweight Title against me and the Mexican 15 months previously, it wasn't worth my notice to think of..This was going to be a battle between two men with something to prove for now. He got cheated, but on the other hand the crowds rallied behind him. He no longer had to deal with the aggravation of an unappreciative audience who cared little for my tribulations, who would ignore the fact of who the better man is for their selfish desires....This night, there would be no need for doubt anyway. Chet was good, but the majority of his big wins came with the aid of others for most of his time in PCW, and now he would be the one endangered by that possiblity, if it was even needed.. His charisma was bigger than his skill, and I didn't care for charisma at this point, I just wanted to beat the hell out of the guy to prove I could fight for something that HADN'T been held by the PURE send-offs and Cyclonica..But why? Why did I need to prove myself again?? I'd showed the people what I could do....Maybe they just needed reminding. They saw what I did to Romantico, but nobody buys him as a real threat in the art of grappling anyway..It wasn't all that much of a step upwards to deal with Chet from a wrestling perspective, but the fans, they wouldn't notice that...They'd just notice that their favorite former Continental Champion was getting beaten up, and they would boo, and I would revel in it. I didn't see any reason why not to.. It's not like they were going to stop any time soon. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
