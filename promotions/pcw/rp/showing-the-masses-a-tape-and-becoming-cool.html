Showing the masses a tape... and becoming Cool??

undated

Date: 19th June? A win...A beatdown...... topped off with a couple of fans who just don't fit the bill of being 13yr old chicks. The beginning of the week for the 'Pretty Cool Guys' has been something filled with many ups... and many downs. A wise man could compare this to being something of a rollercoaster... but of course, we all know how stupid that would really be... since Rollercoasters always stop after going around the track twice.. and there are kiddie rollercoasters that don't go up or down... just play 'Theme Park' God damn it... you'll see that you can create the worlds greatest flat-track rollercoaster in the world.. Erm... yes, that really was something totally off the point there... it'll probably even make a few of you people a little weary of what I should be doing here... talking about the past week of the team... rabbiting on about how comparing it to whatever would be stupid... yeah, I'm just going to get down to the point of it all tonight.... but first, a word from.... Nah-Uh... nobody, but I bet that make you feel like something was going to happen... like a commercial break or something? Whats that... you didn't care? Oh well, theres no need to get all pissy about it... it was only a joke... ONLY A BLOODY JOKE DAMN IT!! ... phew... yes, I have calmed down to the point where I will be able to talk about this next installment of the fantastic show.. so, we see... Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore had apparently gained two new followers in the form of the 'Heartbreakers'.. a Tag Team who wanted to come along and join the Band... getting in on some of the action theirselves...although, our proud heros decided against such an outcome, they left the budding PoP Wannabes alone to linger on the thought of having a chance at getting through... although, nobody knows or understands how big a chance this will truely turn out to be. Following that epic encounter, we saw another squash match from Rich and Ryan as they took Misplaced to the Wood Shed and then some. Many had called for Sherwin and Moore to come through again... and they did so in convincing fashion.. taking their ever expanding record of Tag Team Competition in PCW.. to 3 and 0. Of course, after the match... something bad happenned, but for the emotional safety of all the PCG fans that are watching this video, the following segment of information will be... significantly altered to give forth an decent impression of the actual events... ** The Magical Camera of Fantasia begins to Roll Footage from Turmoil this week. ** The scene slowly begins to open up inside an empty arena, where inside the ring... three midgets are standing still in the middle of it. Two of the midgets, are dressed up like PoP Stars... really taken to the max in all the parts of glamour and glitz... the other guy, well, hes dressed to be the same as Akiyama... but the best thing that the boys could get is a Bill Clinton mask with a Spiderman Costume. This was obviously done to create that skitzo persona everyone around the world just happened to come down and ramble about. All three midgets stood high in the ring as official match commentary came from none other than... Rich Sherwin! 'Hello folks... and welcome to another exciting edition of 'Pretty Cool Turmoil'... I say another because the whole setting would sound... really lame if anyone found out that this was really our first show.... ah, I shouldn't have said that line there... but regardless, is it my problem if it isn't marked out on the que cards my mom wrote? Of course not... of course not! Now... inside the ring we have 'Pretty Cool Guys' members... Ryan Moore... and his talented, sensual, terrific Tag team partner.. Rich Sherwin... standing tall against the dark force of Akiyama...' The view zooms into the 'ringside' action... the midgets of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore getting closer to the mix with every passing second. When the midgets get about twelve paces away from Akiyama, some dramatic music of climatic fashion begins to beam out of the speakers.. once again, those speakers being from The Magical Speakers of Fantasia! Sherwin and Moore stand high and mightly, looking at their fiendish attacker before starting to talk in those annoying midget tones.. 'They say... that you are the evil... demon of hell called... Akiyamahayamama!!... they say... that its demons like you that give people like us... a bad reputation as being nothing more than... wimps.. losers... and like Fred Durst, a microphone abuser!', shouts Sherwin before looking over to his Tag Team Partner in 'brotherhood' fashion. Ryan Moore doesn't say anything for a few moments and seems to be thinking about the lines that he was supposed to be coming out with. Some time passes before he is able to get down and dirty with the lines he was paid to say. 'Oh Yes...', he begins, looking around the arena as if to think that there were thousands of people, each one of the sitting on the edges of their seat. Moore returns his evil eye stare to the monster midget that is Akiyama... a move which does nothing to unease or unbalance the monster of doom. Moore decides that it'll be best to just continue with the talking, getting that dramatic edge and 'Midget Oscar Award' for best Supporting Actor. He continues, '... I see that it is indeed you... and for the actions that you have allowed to occur throughout your life... for the actions that you have allowed to flow freely... we will... take you down to the ends of the EARTH!' Ryan and Rich Sherwin stand high, looking ready to launch into the phase of attack at almost any God given second. They get ready into their specific stances of attack and take one step forth, the distance between the 'NVG' and themselves reducing itself to nine heart filled spaces. Akiyama holds out his hand, forcing the duo to stop in their tracks. He looks around the arena for a few seconds before finally uttering some words his 'mortal' enemies. 'Chi... siete... per dire che cosa basterete a me... Akiyama... il dio dei demons... e del destroyer di voi?!' he bellowed, the visual aid shaking around to try and show the vibrations of the Gods voice. Sherwin and Moore begin to jump around the ring, making the power of Akiyama look a little stronger than it really should be in this day and age. When everyone stops moving around, Sherwin stops for a few seconds and begins to think about what was just said. 'Excuse me... are we even on the same bandwidth here... I could have sworn that I was told... this version of the tape was going to be shot in English... not Italian you freakin 'Neo Void Muppet!', is all that was said from Rich Sherwin before the screen shot faded out into black, a little message coming up explaining that after the words stated by Midget Sherwin, footage was stopped and Akiyama was allowed to do whatever he wanted to the low class impersonators. ** The Magical Camera of Fantasia slowly fades out into the world of nothingness, allowing out doting narrator to cut back in and deal with the matter at hand. ** So yes... as you were able to see there... 'The Neo Void Muppet'... I mean, 'God'... was able to put the beat down on both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore.. an event that was taken out of all proportions by our video-mix, but it was given to all you people in a toned down view... because really, we don't want fans to die... after all, PCG needs you to blow all your money and your parents money on their spankerific Merchandise! With that nice undertone in hand, we now take ourselves to the next scene of the ride that Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore like to take forth around their current locations... and now, we see the most stunning moment of them all... the day that Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... attempt to become dudes... dudes as cool as the Cowabunga Connection!!Yes... now its over to me, Rich Sherwin.. the only worthwhile member of PCG and the only guy who is able to make full recoveries from injuries over the space of 12hrs. Many people like to blame the fact that I have 'Wolverine' like abilites... and... then there are the people who just look at me in a really cold manner and say 'Hey... look at that wimp, hes trying to hide his injuries and make us think he's healed overnight... heh, was a grade A moron!'. Now, instead of having me run into a ramble about the abilities that I do... and do not have, today, like the narrator commented about... is the day that myself... and Ryan Moore... attempt to do something that has never ever been attempted by people of our status before. We will be making a bold move over the lines... into the land of the lost... into the land of the magical lepricons... oh yeah, well be stepping over to the Other Dimension... and No, don't imagine us falling into a Dark Void... because that would just be totally unrealistic of you. Regardless, the day starts off in the cosy confinements of a store... a store where you just happen to buy all that cool dude stuff and what-not. 'You know Ryan.... we should like, totally start to think about speaking in the way of the 'Dude'... because like, dude, we need to be soo far out when it comes to those Mega-Super-Dudes... the Cowabunga Connection.', I said, looking impressed with the words that I'd spoken, gradually getting excited about the way I was being able to tackle this Cool idiolect that both members of CC were able to use at will. Oh, just to make a point, at the moment, we were both wearing our regular PoP outfits. Ryan looked over at me and flashing a grin, amazingly happy at the way I'd been able to throw out that whole line of dialogue. 'Far Out dude... that is so totally fab, its like... we have so totally been able to like, get on with this stuff.... dude, can you just imagine the faces of Cowabunga Connection when they see how we've totally stolen all their individual stuff?', he said, sounding just as cool as I did with the cool way of their idiolect. We look at each other in the totally radical way and began to strum air guitars like those boys from TV... Bill and Ted. We looked around the Totally Cool store for a few minutes or so... searching through everything and anything that we could wear... and still look that shade of Cowabunga Coolness that everyone around that kinda spectrometre was. The scene faded out slowly inside the store... but was quick to reopen on the outside of the location. The view was fixed on the doors, not even attemptint to move from left to right... or anything for that matter. I jumped out of the store first, dressed in a pair of bright orange shorts, some sandels and a white vest... showing the world two very important facts. The first one being that I may not have the greatest sense of style in the world... and the second one being that I knew how to get jiggy on the very grounds of 1990... but I guess thats not all too bad, after all... Cowabunga Connnection can't really be all that hip... all that cool and totally far out like they claim to be... can they?Ryan Moore was the next dude to slide out of the store, he was dressed in a pair of really bright blue shorts, some sandels and a white vest! The color change was there to inform people who was who... and by God, the brightness of the items really did go that damn far to show how with it we liked to think we were! 'Hey dude... I hear there is like, some totally cool thing... going on down at the beach... man!', I said in my totally cool version of a Cool Persons voice, obviously straining myself to get everything right... down to the very final detail of course. Ryan laughed and smiled somewhat, looking happy to be doing something that would incite humor for the both of us... and not just the humor that gets nailed on him for every single tape of the week... and then some. 'You mean like... one of those totally cool beach parties like things?', Ryan asked in his usual excited voice. I smiled and nodded my head in excitement, just waiting for the moment that Moore would agree, allowing me to get that all vital headstart in the race to the party scene. He nodded his head somewhat, comfortable with the fact that we would be acting like dudes for the better part of the day. The scene and the installement ended with Rich taking the lead on the rush down to the beach. Who will get there first? What will happen there? Nobody knows just yet... but you will know... ONLY... if you are smart enough... and gutsy enough... to tune in...... NNNEXT TIME!!!!
