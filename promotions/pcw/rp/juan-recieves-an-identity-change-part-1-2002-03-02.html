Juan Recieves an Identity Change (Part 1)

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/02 Time: 5: 26 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Lisbon Secret Officers Center Part 1 “What’s life worth, if I can’t have myself be known?” The scene opens with Juan Borro entering the secret officers center, which is something like the Secret Service in the United States. Juan was call they’re earlier in the day for unknown reasons. Juan believes it has something to do with his trial. He’s hoping they found him an attorney or something along those lines. Juan arrives in the center, and takes a seat in the waiting area. Juan picks up a news magazine and sees his face in the front page. Juan seems startled as he stares at the picture. It’s a huge picture of Juan holding his head in displeasure. On the top it says in big letters “Our hero might be gone, forever.” Juan flips to the page with the article, and begins to quickly read through it. Juan totally can’t believe what he’s reading, as the newspaper has more insight into the situation that Juan didn’t even know about. Juan read the whole thing, and looks at certain comments made by certain people. They all vary, but Juan reads a few of them to himself. “I have lived a long life, with many ups and downs. I tried to do my best to make a success of myself, but it was hard. Juan Borro is a man who went from the bottom straight to the top. He’s an extraordinary young man that should be praised for his accomplishment and not punished for them. The Chinese government started all of this controversy with a bribe, and a young a naïve Juan Borro was put in the middle. He shouldn’t hold any sorrow for his actions, which were not even sure happened. The kids of this country looked up to this man, and now it all seems shattered.” Comments made by Maria of Benfica. “Juan Borro is an idol to all of us, but the fact of the matter is this trial is in China, and we can really do nothing to help him. We can protest all we want, but China won’t care. They’ll persecute Juan Borro no matter what happens, and that’s how the story will end. I will say that the day he is guilty will be a sad day in Portugal.” Comments made by Martino of Algarve. “They can’t do this. I may not know all the facts, but it’s probably just because the Chinese are jealous. Why would they want to deal with Juan Borro in the first place? It’s all a conspiracy if you ask me, and the only way Juan will go free is if they get to the bottom of it.” Comments made by Alexander of Boavista. Juan decides not to read the rest, as he has seen enough for one day. Juan puts the magazine down and tries to snap back into reality. Juan sees the words on paper, but can’t believe they are talking about him. He can’t believe people are taking the time to try and deal with his personal issues. In a way it’s nice and comforting, but in another it is scary to know, his life plays such an impact on others. A secretary comes walking up to Juan. She taps him on the shoulder to get his attention. Juan turns around and she speaks. Secretary- Hello Mr. Borro, I am the secretary around here, and I’m here to tell you that your appointment is ready. If you would just follow me, I will take you to the room you are supposed to go to. Juan- Okay. Thank you. Juan does as followed and goes off following the secretary. Juan arrives at a rather small look with just a table in the center. Two men are sitting on the table, as Juan slowly and cautiously walk in. Juan- Hi! I was given a call to meet with you guys, but I have absolutely no idea why I’m here I’m guessing it has to do with the trial. Manuel (Officer 1)- In a way it has to do with your trial, and everything going on. You should really take a seat so we can tell you more. Pedro (Officer 2)- Yes. The reason you are here isn’t very complex. What we need you to do is be very cooperative, though. Juan senses whatever is going down seems to be serious. He hesitantly takes a seat, and looks up at the officers to speak. Juan- I don’t want to seem rude or anything, but this awfully looks like an interrogation room, so this better not be any type of trick. Pedro- No! Please we aren’t going to be interrogating you at all today, but we do need to have a very secret conversation that no one else should know about. So this is why we are in such a little room and taking such precautions. Juan- Okay, I can understand that, it just seemed a little odd to begin with. Can I ask what this secret thing your talking about is. Manuel- Yes. You can ask what it is, an we’ll get to that is a few minutes. First, we’d like to introduce ourselves so we all don’t come off as strangers. Juan- Okay. Manuel stands up and approaches Juan. He takes his badge out from his wallet to show Juan his identification. Manuel- My name is Manuel, but you can call me Manny. I work in the field of relocations and new employment. I’m also a counselor is switched identity courses, so I’m highly qualified in the area we will be discussing. Pedro, the second officer, then gets up and approaches Juan, doing the same routine as Manuel did. Pedro- Hello my name is Pedro, and I’m a partner of Manuel’s. I’ve worked here ten years, and have a 75% rate in the field of hiding prisoners, and keeping their files closed. Don’t hesitate to ask me question. Juan listens to their descriptions, and becomes a bit nervous, and is filled with tons of questions. Juan- What is up with the hiding people and switching identities deal, and how does this all have to do with me? Manuel- Well Juan, you see our job is to keep you out of public eye, up until your trial. A lot of people are after you and want you dead, so the best thing to do is help you be safe, and don’t worry a bit, because we do a good job at it. Pedro- Yes. We won’t exactly have to go to the extreme of hiding your identity. No document will have to be changed in the process, but we will have to stretch the truth and configure your appearance to meet our standards. Juan- This all is just seeming way to odd to me. I don’t see why I have to do this all. What’s the reasoning to why I have to do this, can you tell me that? Manuel- It’s a hard thing to grasp, but we’ve found twelve reports of people trying to kill you and people close to you. You may not know of them because the plans for all of them have been found out, but they are there, and the best thing to do, is downplay everything as much as possible. Juan seems a little startled; not knowing so many people would hate him after this event. He then goes on to ask them more questions. Juan- I just took up a position in the Lisbon City Council, how will that be affected. Manuel- Well I’m sorry to say, that will have to wait until after the trial. We can’t take to chance of putting the whole City Council in jeopardy. Juan- Okay, so does that mean I can’t be a wrestler either. I love wrestling in PCW I can’t give that up. Pedro- We realized you’d feel like that, so we came up with the proper security precautions. You also aren’t in much danger when you are around that many people. We have to mostly watch out when you are in your home here in Portugal. Juan- Okay, well that’s some good news, so what is going to happen to me? Pedro grabs a paper, and unrolls it. It has a whole load of tasks, and things that need to be done on it. Manuel reads off the list. Manuel- 1) You need to change your name. You will go by the name Jose Vuergo.2) You will be living in Coimbra. It’s a lovely little beach area, so it should be no discomfort there.3) You will be living in a house with full security, but you will have to record anytime you are expecting visits, so we are ready.4) You will be working as a part time soccer coach for a youth under seventeen soccer clubs. The team is very good ant we thought you’d love the job. Juan- Excuse me, but if they know anything, they’d know it’s me. Manuel- Nope because then comes number 5, which you will be going to in a little while, before you meet your team. You’re going to get a complete makeover, where we are going to completely change your look so no one can tell whom you are. Juan-Oh man. Pedro-Well let’s not waste anytime. Follow us, and we’ll take you through this all, along with extra information. Scene fades to black, with Juan going off. TO BE CONTINUED………………………………..
