Doubts?

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/4/03 “Tuesday” spoke a voice calm and collected, as it’s owner paced slowly around the room. “Yeah, Tuesday against three other men” spoke James his voice clearly showing signs of nerves by the way in which he stuttered slightly trying to comprehend the idea of going head to head with three other men. The thought of facing one man in his debut match was enough to send James into deep thought, searching the back of his mind, wondering and hoping that he wouldn’t choke in the heat of the moment in front of the thousands of spectators, but three men. That was a different kettle of fish all together. James is sat on a small wooden stool in the centre of a relatively large room which at one time or another may have resembled a gym at this point in time however it has been converted into a rather modest observatory with a large telescope sitting predominantly at the far end of the room pointing to the night sky, a small bed next to it and it becomes apparent that for the time being at least this is James’ home or somewhere to sleep would be a more appropriate term. “Your voice is fluctuating surely you can’t be worried about it all, no-one is expecting you to go out and win your first match James, let alone in somewhere like PCW they know this is your first time out and they will try to exploit that. Jackson isn’t a seasoned veteran but don’t let that fool you he’s got a lot more ring action than you have.” James nodded taking in the words of advice from the man whose complexion was pale but not unfriendly, a small face containing a large friendly smile, with a head full of greying hair. “I’m also with some guy called Burns, Stone Burns I don’t know but I’ve heard rumours about him I’m sure it’s just idle chit chat but some people have referred to him as a..” James swallowed deeply his throat drying up as he tries to come to terms with the rumours he had heard hoping, praying that they were not true. “..Knife welding maniac” The greying mans lips curled upwards into a smug grin as he nodded a little too happily for Edwards’ liking before sighing slightly and looking James in the eyes. “Well the rumours aren’t too far from the truth” spoke the man his head hanging slightly after seeing James’ face turn from one of worry to horror “he’s a misogynist or something either way he hates women and likes playing with knives what more can I say” James rose slowly from his chair, running a hand down his face in contemplation stroking his chin, turning away from the man he began to pace up and down in front of him pausing occasionally just to run his hands through his hair, the pressure was clearly getting to him. “When I signed up to this gig it was for one reason Jack. To get money so I can get back into what I really want to do, I want to see the stars Jack it is who I am. Look at me I’m no wrestler do you really expect me to do anything in the ring against them?” James’ words almost pleading to the man who we now know only as Jack. “And just to top things off you’re telling me that there is a risk that one of these people will be bringing a knife into the ring with them, well isn’t that just fantastic” James slunk down onto the stool again burying his head deep within his hands shaking his head. I can’t do this it’s just too hard. There has to be an easier way of making money than fighting…except this isn’t just fighting for some people this is a life, a way in which they have to survive. Do I really have the drive pushing me forward, how do I know that I won’t just become yesterdays news someone that nobody remembers, or if I am remembered for the wrong reason James reached into his back pocket, pulling out a black leather wallet, opening it to reveal a photograph of himself and an attractive female roughly four years younger than James. Her auburn hair stopping just below her ears and emerald eyes which appeared to be staring directly at you. Lifting up the photograph James planted a small kiss on the forehead of the female. “I miss you Emily but you’re living the dream now at least for now, I’m doing this so we can be together again I know you don’t know how proud I am of you but I am I really really am, I just hope you are safe up there” By now Jack has positioned himself behind James and is resting a comforting hand on his shoulder causing James to let out a loud sigh of discontent. “She’s the reason you’re doing this James. You know she would be proud of you no matter what you do, however she also knows and feels bad about taking your dream, don’t you remember the launch day. She didn’t want you to be mad. Deep down on some level I know that you are but your love for her doesn’t allow you to show it. James look in your heart and you know what you are doing is right” Jack said squeezing James’ shoulder lightly before turning away and heading towards the door of the gym/bedroom “Besides if you manage to beat the agent dude and knife welding maniac all you have to worry about is the Russian Sniper and you’re all set” laughed Jack as he left the room leaving James in a daze sat in the chair not fully aware of what was just said and is silent for a few moments before it sets in. “RUSSIAN SNIPER WHAT DO YOU MEAN RUSSIAN SNIPER” shouts James as he bolts off of the stool and dashes towards the exit in order catch up to Jack, the words echoing around the high ceilings of the gym. The room fell silent once more the barrenness of it all with just a single bed, bedside table and a telescope pointing towards the night sky and the stars that encompass it. Things in this life can be scary there is no doubt about that yet there is something that always pulls us through, if we are frightened we still proceed as we know that it will be for the greater good in the end. James was clearly scared of what was to come but he had to proceed, with caution but proceed all the same for he had love driving him on. Oh and the dream you can never forget the dream
