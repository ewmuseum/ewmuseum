The Death Saga: Temple of the Morning Star

3 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-01 For the first time in his life, Darkstar was intimidated by evil. He stood, frozen in his tracks as he stared off, past where this winding road ended, the castle rose high above the ground, the tallest building he has ever seen. The spiers seem to smoke, but it cant be, there is no fire burning in this building. For something so deep in hell, it sure looks cold. He swallowed deeply and began to walk, he did not know why he was worried, he was the Lord of Dreams, the Prince of Nightmares... he was thought and fantasy, he was myth. Yet, faced with having to possibly battle the morning star one on one... that is kind of daunting. Why would he want a fight though, too him DarkStar must be nothing but another lost soul, a fallen deity much like himself. There was more to this that Harle wasnt telling him, he knew it, she wasnt being honest with him at all, why would she be. She must be bitter, she must realize that he is with Talena now, but why didn't she say anything... This just doesn't make any sense... He could ask why for hours and get no answers... I could ask why for hours and get no answers... but I still want to know... Theres only one way Drazail Go In... Go In... My Thoughts exactly... He began to walk, slowly, one foot in front of the other, he could feel the heat radiate from this cold building in the middle of nowherehell. He walked past the drawbridge and the leafless trees and sighed, sucking it up and walking into the main room of the temple. He was in satans castle... Cool However, he didn't go unnoticed, a puff of smoke and a short, fat demon with a book popped up from beside him, he sneered and spoke. Satan's Desk Clerk: And who might you be? DarkStar: ...I have many names. But I am the King of Dreams, of the Nightmare Realms... The Mortal Gavyn Drazail... DarkStar, of the endless... The demon waves his hand. SDC: I get the point, your expected with the big men.. Men? SDC: Go past the bars, and through the big red doors with the severed heads in front of them. His boots clammored against the red and black marble floor, he reached the door and shuddered, surronding the area were the severed heads of those that failed, those that didn't make it out, each one of them was still alive, in horrible pain, and talking... Eternal damnation... DarkStar looked up at the big doors, his thoughts rushing. For the hundredth time since I regained it, I reach into the pouch and I touch the sand. I sift it through my fingers. Feel each grain of it, inexhaustible. Endless. Like myself, like the few others of my kind. Endless. Tonight I feel alone. I have always been solitary, but here on the nightward shores of dream, loneliness washes over me in waves, lapping and pulling at my spirit. I sprinkle sand into the waters of night. The grains burn as they fall, reminding me of another in times long passed away. I watched him even then as he fell, his face undefeated, his eyes still proud. It is time for me to walk the abyss. Time to reclaim my own. I must talk to the Morningstar. ... I do not have high hopes for the meeting He gulped, pushing the doors open with both hands, they swung and crashed on the walls with a loud, metal on metal boom. And he realized, he had walked smack into the middle of some sort of meeting, hundreds of demons, all sitting around the center of the room, a room big enough to house a state, and each one bored their one, two, three, and more eyes into his face, looking him up and down. A shallow clapping was heard, then triplicated, as the rather large and daunting Lucifer walked out from the crowd, flanked by two other demons, much smaller, but not less intimidating, he had a smile on his face that could melt wax as he greeted DarkStar. Lucifer: Drazail! So Happy you could make it, we were hedging our bets on if you would make it through Spirit Road, if you had the courage, apparently you have, I guess Beelzebub here lost a steady amount. DarkStar: Of money? Lucifer laughed loudly, shaking the foundation. Lucifer: Of souls my dear man. DarkStar looked from Lucifer, to the other two demons he was flanked by. Lucifer: You may be wondering about my partners here, you see, you came here and destroyed Hell after defeating me, although it wasnt really me, it was my predecesor, like the endless, we are immortal in name only, not body, but since you have, it is written in legend... 'Some years ago, the Dark, the shadow creature, came forth to challenge hell.' This episode ended in... perhaps a stalemate. But the civil war in hell that ensued tipped the precarious balance of power. We rule in coalition now. Azazel, Beelzebub and I. DarkStar: Hell, a triumvirate? Things change indeed. Lucifer laughs again. Lucifer: If only you knew Beelzebub: You soon will! Azazel: That was cliche. Lucifer looked down and muttered something about children. DarkStar: I hate to change the subject, but I was told that getting here would get me out of hell... Now, I'm here, let me the hell out. Sorry about the pun. He stared down at him. Lucifer: Your half right.. He motions for DarkStar to come closer. Lucifer: You see Drazail... your destiny led you here, every part of it... DarkStar sneers. DarkStar: I'm getting tired of all the talk of my destiny... get to the point, no more cryptic bullshit, tell me when and how im getting out of here... NOW Lucifer growls and kneels down, getting into DarkStars face, looking him eye to eye. Lucifer: You dare boss the triumverate around, tell us what to do, who do you think you are, you have no power here Drazail. What power do dreams have in hell? The hundreds laughed behind him, like good lackeys do, DarkStar just looked knives through his head. DarkStar: You say I have no power? Perhaps you speak truly..., This is your realm after all. Do you forget, however, that I am the keeper of dreams? You say that Dreams have no power here? Tell me, Lucifer Morningstar.. Ask yourselves, all of you... What power would Hell have if those here imprisoned were not able to dream of Heaven? Nothing, noone would worry about it nor would they come. So, let me go Triumverate... His eyes glowed red. DarkStar: Before I show you exactly how little power I have. Silence... Not a demon breathed or moved... DarkStar and Lucifer stood, nose to nose, eye to eye, minutes passed... And Lucifer laughed, patting DarkStar on the shoulder and standing up. Lucifer: This is why I like you Drazail! Yes, your destiny does fall in with me, and it involves your body being reanimated back to life. DarkStar grins. Thank God, I get to see Talena again Lucifers look becomes stern, and the room grows cold. Lucifer: However... you will not be joining it. DarkStar: What do y-...! Suddenly, chains, locks, cages, everything comes out from the wall behind DarkStar, he fights, trying to shed the chains that now bond him, but he can't, they are too strong. They retract, slamming him onto the wall hard. Lucifer walks towards him, putting his hand out and running it across his motionless chin. Lucifer: Your destiny... was to help me rule the world. His nail slashed DarkStars cheek, blood poured out. DarkStar: Wh... How... Lucifer: It was quite simple my good man... DarkStar began to fade... What...? A figure parted the crowd... Lucifer: I had my princess on the inside. The figure... the make up... the clothing... No.... The figure came into focus now... And Harle Quinn stood, arm in arm with the morning star. DarKStar: Har... Le... Harle: Sorry lover, oi guess oi forgot to tell 'ou a few things... Eh. Too late now, no? The pair turned and began to walk away... to go claim what was now theres, the body of the dream king. DarkStar on the other hand, blacked out. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Somewhere on the planet earth, Tyler Nelson made the call to get DarkStar's body dug up from the cold ground, little did he know that he too was a part of Destiny's plan... The Dream Lord would live today... But not as he once was... ------------------------------------------------------------------------ DarkStar hung on that wall, in pain, in agony, he thought and couldnt think of a plan. One thing dominated his mind however... Talena.
