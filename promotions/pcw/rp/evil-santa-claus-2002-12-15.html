Evil Santa Claus

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15, 2002 Santa Claus: Ho Ho Ho! What do you want for Christmas, little girl? [The bodacious looking Santa Claus, equipped with real beard, looks a little girl in the eye. Her little green eyes filled with such joy, the nervousness contorting her face. She really wants to ask for it, but is afraid that this most gnarly Santa Claus will tell her that she can't have it. She has to go for it, no matter what. This is something that she has wanted for so long and SHE MUST HAVE IT! She must know what it is like, the joy of being the only girl on the block with one. All the other little girls will want to be her friend, want to hang out with her, and most of all they will want to be JUST LIKE HER!! An eight year old girl has these thoughts, these passions about her Christmas gift. Santa will say yes, he has to....she's been such a good little girl] Little Girl: I WANT A PONY!! [She blurts it out quickly as the Wal-Mart employees smile at the cute little girl. She seems most afraid of those Wal-Mart people because they are elves, but with Wal-Mart vests on. Couldn't they at least have taken the vests off? Make it more realisitic for the kids? How do you expect any normal kid to believe that there is a Santa Claus? Unless you want them to believe every elf works at Wal-Mart!] Santa: Cindy, the gift you have asked for is quite extravagant.... [Santa looks at the parents as they shake their heads at him, telling him not to promise her the pony. They are just a lower-middle class family trying to get by. They can't afford a freakin' pony, and the hurt on the girls face would be too much for them to handle. Santa is the only salvation for this family, the only link in the chain that can supply the stability] Santa: ...BUT, Santa knows you have been a GOOD GIRL! So, pony it up, little girl. [The girl smiles and gives Santa a kiss on the cheek. The parents look as if they are about to be placed six feet under as they walk past with fire in their eyes. Santa finds a great satisfaction in this. When it's not Christmas he's sitting back in the Automotive section of Wal-Mart trying to show snot nosed teenagers how to change their damn oil. He's waiting on customers who spit in his face any chance that they get. Fellow employees who constantly make those Santa Claus jokes, and now he's sitting in the front of the store wearing a Santa suit. Like some kind of bogus jackass he's granting wishes like he's some kind of God...and it's REALLY GREAT!!] Santa: Bring on the next child! [Santa looks to his right and watches the last family storm out of the store. He smiles wickedly, but is stopped short when a 240lb man hops onto Santa's lap] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: HEY, DUDE!!! [Santa looks apalled] Santa: Aren't you a little old to be sitting on Santa's lap. [Tommy, wearing his Tubular Surfwear sweatshirt and khaki cargo pants, smiles brightly to Santa] TTT: Your never too old to ask Santa for some gnarly gifts.... [Tommy looks to the long line of children with their disgusted parents] TTT: ...RIGHT KIDS?! [The children all applaud loudly as Harry stands at the front of the line, trying to conceal his laughter. You can see his stomach bouncing up and down as if the members of Twisted Sister on his T-shirt are laughing] Santa: Well...all right then. What would you like for Christmas...ummm...little boy? [Tommy smiles and looks back to the crowd] TTT: Santa, I thought you'd never ask that most bodacious question! I just want two things for Christmas...and I believe it's not that much to ask for. First, I'd like that totally blind bogus dude, The End, to face horrible pain and injury against me, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. I want him to BLEED!! I want him to beg for my most gnarly mercy! I want to beat him SO BADLY, SO BODACIOUSLY that he'll be able to see again! That when he's unconcious and down for the count I'll feel this...sort of...radical feeling inside of me. That a small piece of the most righteous puzzle has come together. That I...Tommy...have taken a most heinous celestial and taken him out...FOR GOOD!! [Everyone, including Harry, stares at Tommy as if he were completely psychotic. They have a good right to do that too. Tommy's blue eyes are filled with such anger, such violence. His body is hunched and his chest is heaving up and down. Santa's leg is shaking because of the large amount of weight that is being placed on his bum knee. He's certainly no youngster and with every passing second he isn't getting any younger. But how do you tell a psychotic looking surfer that is two times your size to get off of your leg? Well, without getting your ass handed to you, it looks like Santa is going to have to tough this one out] Santa: Ummmm, well, that sounds like a nice present. I'll see what I can do about that one, what is the other gift that you want for Christmas...little boy? [Tommy seems to be composing himself as the million dollar smile once again is placed across his face] TTT: I'd like nothing more than to walk out of Decemation the PCW Continental Champion. Santa: That is a very great gift, but I think that one is more up to you than anything. [Tommy looks at Santa with crazed eyes] TTT: What did you say? Santa: I said that winning the title would be more on you, than me granting you the gift. Lets face it, your going to have to get through The End. Then it could be anybody...Tim Walden, Rush, The H.I.T...Chet King. That is a lot of work and not even, me, Santa Claus can grant something like that. [Tommy begins looking at Santa differently, as if he's beginning to take some kind of weird transformation. The beard is fading away, the rosy cheeks are gone. Santa is morphing into a mask, with those white eyes...BLIND EYES! The frame of a professional wrestler begins to take shape and the Celestial has now become Santa Claus. Tommy is sitting on The End's lap, asking The End for Christmas wishes again...getting denied...AGAIN!! What does it take?! The End is always there to screw Tommy over! Always there to make him feel like he's nothing, less than him. Not anymore!] TTT: DIE!!!! [Tommy lunges onto the Santa, giving him hard right hands just like he did to the Christmas Tree earlier in the day. The fat, old man tries his best to defend himself but it is absolutely no use. This muscular, strong man has him pinned to the ground with such a vice grip. He's not going anywhere and he's about to face the Grim Reaper face to face in a few moments. If this surfer keeps driving that clubbing right hand in as hard as he is...it will soon be THE END for Santa Claus, for Richard K. Messina of Los Angeles, California. His career in the automotive section of Wal-Mart will be over and Howard, the flaming homo, will have to take his place. This surfer is going to ruin everything is one fail swoop. Somebody must help Santa...ANYBODY!] TTT: You've denied me for the last time, End! SKY! You stupid blind, bogus BASTARD! You are not better than the most tubular dude in PCW!! You will never be on my level! You don't add up to what it takes to be BEAUTIFUL, to be absolutely SEXY!! Your blind, ugly AND STUPID!! I'M NOT GAY!!! DAMN YOU AND YOUR BOCA DUDE!!!! [Tommy pounds as employees and customers pull Tommy off of Santa. There are dozens of people pulling but it just seems like a hard task to stop. This large man is possessed with some kind of bogus force that can't be stopped. His blue eyes seem to be flaming red as he keeps throwing punches around with lightning fast speed. You can literally feel his mind slowly slipping to the frayed ends of sanity. Everything is coming together all at once and driving this bodacious dude to madness. The people are able to stop him as he begins to settle down. Employees check on the Santa, seeing that he's in one piece still. Tommy backs away letting himself calm down, watching The End slowly morph back into Santa. The adrenaline gland has stopped its secretion of fluids and Tommy is slightly sane again] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: THAT WAS FAR OUT, MAN!!! HAHA! [Harry can only laugh at the situation, not able to cope that his brother is losing his mind. The End has gotten into Tommy's head...breaking his spirit. Harry knows he needs to help and it will come in due time, but for now Tommy needs to let some of the aggression out of his far out system] Little boy: Look, Mommy, I got a replica PCW Continental Championship! [Tommy immediately looks to the front aisle of Wal-Mart and sees the little blonde boy dancing with the styrofoam belt] TTT: THAT'S MY BELT, DUDE!!! [Tommy runs at the kid full speed and snatches the belt from his hands. The boy immediately breaks down into tears as Tommy runs down the aisle like a lunatic with the styrofoam belt held in the air] TTT: GNARLY!! CHEER ME!!! I AM YOUR CHAMPION!! YOUR BODACIOUS CONTINENTAL CHAMPION!!! [Tommy kicks over a mannequin in the men's clothing area. He stands over the fallen plastic dummy as if he'd beaten it down for half an hour in a grueling match up] TTT: The End is nothing!! He has fallen at the hands of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy!! [The people in the store are absolutely frightened. The security guards aren't really sure if they should keep watching this maddening display or calm this guy down] TTT: I will beat you all!! I am TUBULAR!!! TUBULAR!!!! TUUUUBBBUUULLLARRRR!!! [Every has their eyes fixed on Tommy in his celebration. Harry walks over to the recovering Santa and jumps onto his lap] Santa: OUCH!! Harry: Hey, man. [The Santa can only grunt in pain as Harry smiles at him with his stoned eyes] Harry: I want a ferrari.... TTT: I AM TUBULAR!! Harry: I want three ounces of weed.... TTT: I AM TUBULAR!! Harry: I want a jacuzzi with massaging jet sprays AND massaging hands... TTT: I AM TUBULAR!! Harry: Twisted Sister to do a tour this summer.... TTT: I AM TUBULAR!! Harry: A new spine... TTT: I AM TUBULAR!! Harry: AND A PONY, MAN!!! [Fade to black]
