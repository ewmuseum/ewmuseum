Gratuitous Action Sequences.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, within the aisles of the local Save-Rite Supermarket. The Grecian demi-god meanders through the produce section, pushing his cart forward while idly poking a various fruits and veggies, when an odd look crosses his face. Grabbing the handle of the half-full cart firmly, he whips it around almost effortlessly, swinging into a high arc into the air. The impact is intense, as a black robbed assassin greets the rusty wire shell full on, sending the figure sprawling wildly out of control, crashing into a lemon stand unconscious before he hit the ground. Steele whirls around, grabbing a nearby watermelon and spins it in front of him, ninja stars imbedding themselves into the rind. Big Al chuckles as he cracks his knuckles, staring down the aisle at the group of ninja, glowering at him behind their dark masks. “By the gods, is that a sword in your pocket, or are you just pleased to meet me?” The first wave of ninja spring forward silently, each unsheathing gleaming blades in fluid motions, poised to strike. Steele makes a mental note: when you ask if that’s a sword in a ninja’s pocket, the humor is lost, because it IS a sword. Steele grunts as takes a step back and kicks to the side, crunching one of the heavy wooden legs of a table bearing the deliciously over-sized watermelons. The wood groans and shudders for but an instant, caving under the immense pressure of the melons and creating an avalanche, so to speak, spilling out over the Oriental warriors. The ninja try to hack their way through, slicing a path, but the onslaught of melons proves to much, burying the assassins under hundreds of pounds of rolling fruit. Big Al begins to guffaw loudly, a laugh that is choked off as, out of the corner of his eye, he notices shades of black slithering over the aisle partitions. He looks into the rafters, to see ninja perched on the naked beams. He looks to the lettuce section to see ninja rise out of the leafy greens. He looks at the stock boy, realizing that the red smock and khaki pants are merely stretched over a black leotard with matching black mask – a ninja. Steele assumes a properly heroic fighting stance and thumbs the side of his nose. “So the game is afoot… then HAVE AT YOU!! Hi-Keeba!!” A rustling wind-like sound catches the ear of Big Alan Steele from behind, and he spins on a heel, narrowly getting his meaty forearm up just in time to fend off a elbow, a killing blow expertly aimed for his temple. Steele then makes yet another mental note: ninja are more than willing to break martial art movie code and attack from other positions besides the front. More ninja leap into the close quarters combat, and Big Al finds himself completely on the defensive. Kicks aimed for the heart designed to stop its beating… an chop directed at the trachea, designed to crush it… a foot sweep narrowly snapping the lower leg in twain… Steele manages to direct attacks to the meaty portions of his body, absorbing the lesser impacts and staving off the more formidable ones. A roundhouse kick is dodged with a slight body bend and tilting of the head as the sharp eyes of Big Al watch it fly by. Our hero spins with the kick, managing to catch it in his grip. The ninja’s eyes widen as the sudden move catches him off guard, and Steele steps into the man, using the combined momentum to lift the warrior off his unstable footing and swing him around, the body acting as a weapon as it clubs in a wide arc, sending the combined ninja flying off into the distance, crashing into various perishables. Steele doesn’t even have time to catch his breath as a bo staff slices through the air, its blunted end jabs at the air his head once occupied. The bamboo pole then whisks back towards him, and Steele drops backwards, hands back to protect his measured fall as he avoids the side impact. As his hands hit the floor, he twists to one side, putting all his weight on the right arm, and driving his leg into the thigh of the attacking ninja. The force knocks the assassin into a kind of sideways spin that makes him awkwardly crash into the tiled linoleum floor, spouting Japanese epithets in agony. Steele is up before the pole hits the ground, catching it in midair. Spinning the bamboo bo around, he then slaps it off the floor, and holds it out in traditional kendo style, the pole vibrating in his clutches. Looking across the aisle, he slaps at a bag of oranges, sending them through the air and landing neatly inside his cart in the distance. The scream of battle startles him, as a pair of nunchaku wielding ninja rush him. Swinging the bo around vertically, he presents the weapon, and shoves it forward, forcing the ninja to block using the chucks. Big Al grins, expecting this, and lets go, grabbing the two heads and clunking them together in a crude, but effective move, knocking the ninja cold. Zip line. The distinctive sound from above alerts Big Al, and he leaps to the side, a dangling ninja slicing a razor sharp katana through the air. Ninja begin to rappel from the rafters, the glint of cold steel reflecting in their hands. The ninja’s swing was a bit of a mistake, as the momentum of the swing sends the ninja slightly into a turn, as he nears the ground. But the moment of respite is all that is needed for our hero, as he swings upward with a devastating uppercut that lands into the midsection of the Oriental, whuffing the air out of him. The impact also has the benefit of swinging the ninja up and over, still attached to the zip line. The breathless form swings wildly, the line forcing him into a wide circle, ensnaring the other ninja. It wraps them all together as his zip line ensnares the other lines, bringing the ninja cursing together in a collective group. Steele reaches into the watermelon pile and plucks a sword from beneath the fruit. He then turns and hurls the sword, spinning towards the entangled zip lines. It cuts neatly through, and the ninja all land atop each other with a sickening, audible crunch. Big Al leans over, and picks up a ripe tomato off the floor. Dusting it off, he examines it looking for bruises. Perfection in a tomato. Steele grins and retrieves his cart, placing the tomato inside. Looking around, he nods in satisfaction at the dark forms littering the produce section. He pushes the tricky carriage forward, grumbling at the one wheel that won’t turn right. He moves towards the end of the aisle, starting to make the turn… But instead he merely shoves the cart forward, the squeaky wheel whining in protest. Streaks of silver flash through the air above the cart handle. The ninja stars stick into the throat of a portrait of the ‘Employee of the Month – Chad Derhammer.’ Steele grabs a smallish display table housing red onions and hurls it around the corner of the aisle. Stars stick into its under surface, and an Oriental exclamation of surprise immediately follows, the sound of wood splintering into unarmored human forms unmistakable. Steele cautiously steps around the corner, tensing for battle. He crouches and slowly moves his way forward, side stepping his way. His vigilance pays off, as a screaming black ninja leaps out at him. Big Al merely ducks and gives him an extra push, the little extra knocking the ninja off balance and exploding him into a freezer door, shattering the glass and wiping out a Swanson Hungry-Man display - ‘the whole pound of food men love. (tm)’ Big Al slowly reaches forward, his eyes darting every which way. He slowly grasps a box of Betty Crocker cake mix… and a black-gloved hand shoots out from the empty shelf space! String hands grip Steele’s wrists, as the big man tugs back, a look of panic crossing his visage. Steele eyes focus forward, into the shelving and sees a cutout through the cheap aluminum, and the angry eyes of a ninja burning into his chest. The list! The ninja have his shopping list!! Two ninjas leap from out of nowhere, wielding huge-assed hammers. They are coming in from Steele’s flanks and swing their mauls with deadly precision, one vertically, one horizontally. “HI-KEEBA!!” Twisting his hand to grip the ninja across the aisle, Steele grabs and pulls back with all of his might. The aluminum gives way, the hapless ninja crashing through the section in a rain of various cake mix flavors. Simultaneously, the mauls find their mark… only Big Al is no longer there. They crunch into the ninja who was bodily hauled through his own trap, the one crashing downwards finding its mark on his back. The other maul clangs off the handle of the other, and Big Al steps forward and sends the ninja flying back with a side kick that reels him backwards and into a Brittany Spears Pepsi display. The other ninja quickly raises the maul of his fallen companion and raises it high, rushing towards Big Al. Steele waits until the huge hammer makes its downwards arc once again, aimed at crushing his skull. But he steps forward, jabbing an upturned palm into the long shaft, changing the fulcrum. The maul spins on its momentum, his palm the axis, and underneath his outstretched arm. It catches the unsuspecting ninja full on the chin, sending him flying horizontally, limp as a wet dish rag. Big Al glowers as he rubs his bruised palm before grabbing a box of double devil’s food cake and flips it into his cart. He strokes his bearded chin, straining at thought. “If one knows himself as well as his enemies, he need not worry the outcome of a thousand battles. Now, if I know ninja…” Big Al shoves his cart through the busted aisle partition, alert as he enters the cereal section. A large display of Cheerios dominates the middle, a long shadow stretching behind it. Big Al roars a war cry and runs forward, shoving the loaded cart towards the shadow… which comes alive! Ninja flip out from behind the bright yellow boxes, only to each be greeted with wicked punches from Big Alan Steele, as he picks them off one by one, sending them skidding across the supermarket landscape. Steele snags a box of Chocolate Frosted Sugar Bombs for the Graces and whistles an aimless tune as he heads for checkout. The checkout girls snaps her gum as her nimble fingers whisk the items across the laser grid with startling accuracy. She grins at Big Al as the items fly across and into the hands of the bagger, who neatly places them into the bags. Steele narrows his eyes, and cracks her across the face with a backhand, sending her crumpling to the floor. The bagger is stunned as Big Al reaches over the checkout counter and grabs her by the hair, lifting her cold body off the ground. The bagger starts to stammer out in protest… as the girls face slides off neatly into Big Al’s hand!! Behind the fleshy mask was another mask – a ninja mask. Steele nods at the bagger knowingly, and places his money into the register, taking exact change with accompanying Green stamps. The bagger nervously places the two bags of groceries into Steele’s waiting arms, just as a sword slices out from the adjacent checkout counter! Steele almost nonchalantly raises a leg and stamps down on the sword, halting its motion. Then, with the other leg, he snaps a quick kick into the register area, and catches a crouching ninja in the chin, tumbling the assassin against the moving conveyer belt… We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele as he enters his home, Stately Steele Manor. The Graces rush up and snatch the bags from our hero, and Big Al walks into the kitchen,. Where trusted manager Sue Saksnussum sits, snarling while smoking a cigarette. At the groceries’ arrival, she leaps from her chair, ripping into the bags. The Graces flee in terror as Sue utters in a guttural manner, clamoring for something specific. Not finding it, she whirls at Steele, eyes blazing menacingly. “You moron!! You forgot it AGAIN!!” Steele looks back at her, an innocent look on his face. “Forgot what?” “The coffee filters! And the creamer!!” Terri, noted cook of the Graces, also whines upon seeing the supermarket haul. “And you forgot my eggs and flour!!” “You are hopeless, you know that?!” Sue growls, wiping drool off her slavering chin, “I ask you to get only a few items from the store, and you can’t even manage that!!” “Perhaps you left it off you list…” “THIS IS THE FOURTH TIME TODAY!!” Sue screams, “NOW GO BACK AND DON’T FORGET ANYTHING THIS TIME!!” A slow grin crosses the face of Big Al, and he grinds a fist into his palm. “Excellent… Hi-Keeba!!”
