Pet Shop Boys

5 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th Sept. 2002 Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell stumble around the Regina night, having spent the greater part of the last couple of days celebrating their new PCW contracts. After a final sendoff party with some of their friends like CHZ’s Matt Hunt and RUSH’s Stimpo, Surge and Campbell continued their bender when they learned of Xavier Kannon’s 40% Front Office tax, this time drowning their sorrows with the cheapest high-alcohol “beer” that they could find - Steel Reserve High Gravity Lager. The tax would of course force Redemption Song to sell their brand new and fully hooked up living quarters on Montreal’s infamous Rue de Mal #13 and return to their studio flats in New York City. But the party was certainly one for the ages. There were women. There was booze. The mudput was put to good use as was the jello and whipped cream chamber. There was loud music. A midget was set on fire. There was a hot dog fight. There were 3 freaky-deaky DJs. There were mounties breaking up the party in the wee hours of the morning. But the party didn’t stop for Jim and Ben. They decided it would be a great idea to catch an overnight flight to Regina, the city hosting the next Fusion, and buy a dog. The biggest dog they could find. Yes, it was pretty random. But they had been drunk for a string of nearly 14 consecutive hours, and these two highly trained athletes rarely drink. If not for their valet Misty acting as an unofficial chaperone, the half-brothers would probably have gotten in a lot of trouble. Fortunately this night, trouble was not going to find them. Ben and Jim walked around the Canadian streets at dawn, their CHZ Tag-Team Championship belts still fastened tightly around their waists. Ben, dressed down in the same Nike track suit that he had been wearing for the last 2 days and Jim in his standard bluejeans, black Stetson hat and “Redemption Song” t-shirt and worn brown leather cowboy boots. And Misty, stunningly gorgeous as always but looking more disgruntled than Miss America at a football match, lets out sigh after disappointed sigh. But in all, the two half-brothers have been in training so long that they’ve rarely had time to bond. Misty couldn’t begrudge them one small party before the grind of Primetime would begin to take it’s toll. Misty guides Jim and Ben into the first pet shop that she can find. Jim strides confidently into the store but Ben trips over the doorway on his way in. In an exaggerated, drunken reaction, the Apache instinctively rolls into a somersault and then springs up to his feet just behind Jim. Jim gives Ben a little elbow as he notices the very cute and freckled brown-haired young lady behind the counter. The Tombstone, Arizona native slams his fist down on the counter and smiles widely at the young girl. Jim (strong western drawl): Listen here now young lady, we want the biggest fucking dog you have in this place! Ben (yelling): YEAH! The girl nervously backs up a couple of steps behind the counter and reaches down for some forms. Cute Girl: Um, okay. We have to ask you a few questions first to see if you are suitable to buy a pet from us. Is that okay? Jim: Questions? We got plenty of money missy now give us the biggest fucking dog you got, hear? Ben: Yeah! We want a giant mofo that barks and crap like that. Cute Girl (to Ben): Are you an.... Ben (interrupting): AN AMERICAN INDIAN!!!!! YES I AM!!!! Now give us a damn dog or else I’ll call the National Association for the Advancement of American Indians on you AND your “pet store.” Misty walks up behind Jim and Ben and puts a hand on each of their shoulders. The girl looks relieved and flashes a quick smile at Misty as a form of thanks for attempting to get her men under control. Misty: Boys, now come on now, this girl is just doing her job. Jim: Misty baby?! How’d you get here?!! Misty: I’ve been with you two ALL NIGHT!! That’s the 20th time you’ve asked me!! This girl is trying to see if you two are suitable for a dog and so far I wouldn’t even sell you one of those die-in-one-day goldlfish. The girl behind the counter smiles and Jim and Ben straighten themselves out. Cute Girl: Okay, question number one is patience and I’m putting you both down as a no. Question two, does your job involve much travel? Jim: Hells yeah! Haven’t you been watching CHZ? Cute Girl: Um, no. Ben (mock-monotone): Well, you don’t know what you’ve been missing then! Jim (laughing): I love when he does that!! But seriously, we do travel a lot, we’re professional wrestlers. The girl shakes her head negatively and scribbles some more notes before continuing. Cute Girl: What sort of percentage of your time do you think you are at home? About 50%? Jim: Naaaaah.... much less than that. Maybe 20%. Cute Girl: Okaaaayyy... have either of you had any pets before? Ben: We had a couple of dogs on the reservation back in Jersey that we’d throw rocks at and shit like that. Cute Girl (rolling eyes): Wonderful. There’s an Indian Reservation in New Jersey? Ben: YES! HELLO!! It’s not like it’s 1850 anymore lady! Misty starts chuckling and Jim smiles widely as if he had just seen Misty for the first time in a long while. Misty puts her finger up to the cowboy’s lips to stop him from asking her how she got there for the 21st time. The cute girl with the freckles puts on a pair of Buddy Holly style glasses and continues. Cute Girl: How many jobs have you had in the last year? Jim: Hell girl, in the last three months alone we’ve been in PWA, CHZ, did this little thing for OSW and now are in PCW. I guess that’s four in the last three months. Now we got a 40% tax or something on our salaries but Ben and I here are half-brothers so we’re going to apply for the Family Discount. Cute Girl: Okaaaayyyy! Do you guys have any other sources of outside income other than your wrestling careers? Ben: Hells yeah bitch! This cowboy mofo here was willed a sports representation agency called the Watkins Group. Cute Girl: You know, I wanted to say this before, you really don’t sound like a Native American. Ben: What the fuck?! Like you’re the expert Miss I Live In Canada And Work In A Fucking Dog Store!! What do you want me to say “how” and offer you my peacepipe. Cute Girl: Jeez, I’m sorry, I didn’t mean for you to take offense. Ben: Well, you could make it up to me. Want to smoke my peacepipe? Misty jerks Ben back by his shoulder as the girl quickly backs away from Redemption Song’s high-flying Apache. Misty (quietly to Ben): This girl is like 16 years old! Ben: So I’ve got plenty of jel... Misty: Shut up! You guys are NOT helping yourselves!! The girl behind the counter moves back in and over to where Surge still stand, leaned over the counter with his hand still in the exact same place where he had slammed it down. Cute Girl (to Jim): Did he say the two of you were half-brothers? Jim: Yeah, Dad was a bit of a player you know what I mean? Liked the redskins I guess. Cute Girl: You know it’s not funny to play on stereotypes. Jim: Fuck you. Cute Girl: Excuse me? Jim: Go on, next question. Cute Girl: I hate to ask you both but have you been drinking? Am I on some sort of hidden camera show? Misty: I’m sorry sweetie, I’ll get these guys out of your hair. Jim: BUT... BUT.... Ben: We didn’t get the fucking dog! Misty: How many times have I told you we have a cocker spaniel back in New York already!!! Jim: Shit, you’re right. Sorry to have taken up your time. Misty opens the door of the pet store and Jim, Ben and Misty walk out back onto the streets, now starting to bustle with morning commuter action and the far-off sounds of honking car horns. Ben: We have to get some sleep man! Misty: Something tells me you two aren’t going to be showing up on Fusion tomorrow. Jim: I think we need another day or two Mist. Without Tom around, we have to get together the three of us for a gameplan against Misplaced for next week’s Turmoil. Misty: Don’t worry boys. You’ve had your fun. Now it’s time to get down to business. You know you’re going to have to send those belts back. Jim and Ben look down at their waists in a moment of pitiful deflated pride before nodding their heads in unison. Jim: Yeah, we kind of thought so. Just wanted to give ‘im one last whirl. Misty: PCW now boys. Let’s get going. We’ve got a hotel room setup for you in Oregon.
