Devils To The Left Of Me, Angels To The Right - Here I am, Stuck On A Log With Ellie~!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/2/02 Xavier Kannon and Seafood don’t mix. After eating some dodgy Prawns back in Kansas City his hallucinational journey had him travel, literally, to the South Pole where in his feverish state he thought a trek across the globe might help his preparations. Using a Snowmobile they managed to get off the continent of Antarctica quickly, paying an Argentinean Captain to take them on his boat, which however was overtaken by Pirates who forced the couple overboard and into the ocean with only a piece of driftwood for company. We open up on a piece of driftwood floating somewhere of the Argentinean coast. Holding on to the buoyant debris are the 1st couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall, having been made to walk the plank by Pirates a few hours earlier when the boat they has paid for a ride on was taken over and the crew bailed. Eleanor : “How many days have we been floating now?” Eleanor is still wearing the Sheepskin coat that was given to her at the guest house she stayed in the night before but it is now soaked all the way through as are the White roll neck jumper she has under it and the pair of Blue, flared sandblasted jeans. He long, usually wavy hair is wet through and matted down over her head. As she asks the question Xavier checks his watch, tilting it to get the water out from behind the screen. Xavier : “About 2 hours.....So about 8% of a day....” Xavier is wrapped in his full length leather coat with a Black v-neck jumper, White t-shirt and Black jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg. As he swims, holding onto the wood it can be seen that he has shed his shoes and socks and is propelling the wood barefooted towards a small dot on the horizon that might just be civilisation. Xavier : “You know Ellie~! if you would have just left me to sleep it off in the 1st place then none of this would be happening, but no, what did you do? You grabbed our passports and booked a Taxi to the airport!” He doesn’t seem very happy that he is doing all the work while his valet pulls herself on top of the wood and checks her nails, even searching the soggy coat pockets for a nail file. Eleanor : “I am SO not the one who wanted to eat at a buffet...Please, could I BE more insulted at having to dine in that place....And anyway, why don’t you just call a Scientologist and get him to come and pick up up.” The Gold Patron Meritorious takes a break from swimming and rests on the log, letting the feeling come back to his legs, while she doesn’t even consider taking over propulsion responsibilities. Xavier : “What do you suggest I do? Use the phone that comes with this fine piece of wood? Maybe fetch my carrier pigeon out of my coat pocket and send him......Or should I send an e-mail?” Eleanor : “E-Mail.” His valet replies straight away and detecting none of the sarcasm in Kannon’s voice. As he sighs she turns to him and realises he was no serious. Xavier : “So how do you suggest I send this e-mail then, I seem to have left my laptop in the same place as everything else I could really do now, in the “Geez, thanks for stopping me Ellie~!” shop!” He shakes his head and starts to paddle again while she rests her head n the driest of her arms that cling to the log and starts to go to sleep much to Xavier’s annoyance. As he mutters at his valet’s apathy 2 bright flashes of light appear, one on either shoulder and a small devil stands on his left shoulder while a little angel flies around his left. Devil : “Go on.....Push her off, she’ll only slow you down! Push her off, maybe she’ll float to somewhere or maybe not, but you’ll be able to make it to March Madness and win the World Titl-” The angel on his opposite shoulder raises it’s hand and the devil loses his voice and stamps his foot as he knows the angle is controlling him. Angel : “Look at her Xavier, you know she wouldn’t make it 10 metres if you throw her off the log now, you are strong enough to take both of you to safety and so what if it takes an extra hour or so? Won’t it be worth it to have Ellie~! with you on Sunday?” Devil : “Meh, heavenly fool....Unless he casts her off into the ocean deep then he won’t even make it to St Louis....She’s asleep Kannon, just prise her hand off the wood and let her float in her own direction.” The Scientologist, understandably confused looks to each shoulder before closing his eyes and dunking his head under the water before shaking it and looking again to see them still on each shoulder, waiting patiently for his answer. Devil : “C’mon Kannon, cast off the dead weight!” Angel : “Leave her be and you shall reap the rewards of the Ellie~! fruit Xavier, each good deed is repaid in kind.” Xavier is now thoroughly confused. Xavier : “Huh?” The angel and the devil forget talking to him and begin to argue with one another, running around the base of his neck as they chase each other around, the devil waving his trident and the angel flying away. Kannon clears his throat rather loudly and both stop and look up to him, awaiting his choice. Xavier : “Um you 2.....I appreciate you coming down here an all, I guess you must have pretty busy schedules so I appreciate this and everything but I already follow the teachings of L Ron Hubbard so I know how to lead a good life....” With those words he flicks the angel off his shoulder and it goes flying in an arc before splashing into the sea, sending a little bit of water flying up. The devil then starts to do his victory dance, body popping away to self-made beatbox sounds. Devil : “I said who’s house? Say what say what? Lucifer’s house! Who’s house? Lucifer’s house! Who’s house? Say what say what? Lucif-.....What?” The devil turns and looks up to Kannon’s bright Green eyes and starts to get nervous, this is increased as Xavier raises a hand up. Devil : “Hey! Pick on someone your own size!” Xavier : “I also do not believe in the concept of good and evil so I’m afraid you Mr Lucifer, unless you are willing to give up your hard-line evil stance and move to the middle ground....Are also gone!” He slaps his hand backwards and sends the devil flying off behind him and splashing into the sea, shooting off cuss after cuss before trying to convince the angel to pull him out of the water. On the log Ellie~! starts to stir again and narrows her eyes as she turns to Xavier. Eleanor : “Wow, you must have SO been at sea too long....You are creating little voices to play with, and unless you do it with a hand puppet....That’s never good!” The Scientologist starts to swim harder, building up good speed and even Ellie~! positions her body as to create less drag to battle against him and they make good progress towards the island that is looking on the horizon. Eleanor : “So what do you think is on that island?” Xavier : “With any luck an American Embassy, a phonecall and a plane.....But I’d just be happy with fresh water and fruit right now.” Thinking about the island and the bounty it might hold actually compels Eleanor to start paddling, and very slowly she actually begins to move her legs up and down to aid Kannon. Eleanor : “I’m hoping there is a cocktail bar right next to a beauty parlour because this salt water is doing nothing for my hair and this wood is murdering the ends of my nails....Oh, and someone to do my washing....Then maybe they have a wrestling ring that you can practice in, maybe by fighting Monkeys or something.....Or murderous natives that want to cook us in a big pot....But what are the chances of that ha-” Xavier : “Don’t say it!” The Gold Patron Meritorious leaps in to cut her off much to her annoyance and the look she gives him all but asks “What was that for?” as she stops paddling and again becomes dead-weight just as the beach begins to appear in sight. Xavier : “Every time we say something it seems that it either happens or the total opposite happens, there must be some divine force that guide us, watches over us and unfolds reality before us in accordance to out thoughts, hopes and fears, creating a tailor made existence for us....That would explain the weird stuff that happens.” Eleanor : “Ya wha?” She doesn’t seem to follow the Scientologists philosophising and is confused by the unfolding reality concept. Kannon tries to think of a way to simplify it. Xavier : “Like a video game.....You do something and life reacts to it!” Eleanor : “Like when you buy a great pair of shoes and then you see the best ever dress to go with them so you buy it....Wow, that is SO great, I just thought I was a compulsive shopper but it was really fate guiding me....Cool!” The Gold Patron meritorious considers correcting her, but with the islands tree line now taking shape before them he decides to put his energy into getting on solid ground. Eleanor : “I hope that there is...Like, NOTHING on the island, because then there will totally be stuff, because that is how the fate thing you said works! I hope there are no people, and no technology...So now there will be!” As the prep speaks, Kannon notices something on the beach ahead of them, movement and objects pointing to one explanation. Eleanor : “Wow! It worked! There are people on the beach and they are cooking food and waving too us.....Wait....They have TV Cameras and sound mics and everything! You know what this means? WE ARE GOING TO REALITY TV ISLAND!” The camera pans to show a crowd of show-off, similar 10-24 year olds all waving and jumping about like idiots trying to get on camera for something as trivial as seeing something in front of them. Back on the driftwood Ellie~! is grinning broadly as the shot starts to fade out. Eleanor : “Wow, we are going to be on TV even more! We can go in the little hut thing and ask for a plane ride as well as converse with some of the SO totally un-annoying and totally normal acting people that go in for the shows like these!” The Scientologist seems less impressed by the prospect and mutters under his breath as we hit Black. Xavier : “Sigh.....I should of drowned myself!”
