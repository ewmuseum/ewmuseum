An Adventure Soon Forgotten

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-12-02 Skitzo opened his eyes and screamed. Skitzo: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!! Skitzo: FUCK ME WHAT IS IT?!?!?!? Skitzo: I fell asleep at the wheel!!! Skitzo looks around frantically, suddenly realising the need for sleep resulted in the manifestation of a dream, including Bobby Stormbringer and some destruction of property. Shaking the tiredness from himself, Skitzo opens them eyes wide and looks around, to find he is driving along a busy highway along the hard shoulder towards Sacramento, California! As he drives along, the beast in the cage begins roaring out, drawing the attention of passing cars and motorcyclists… Skitzo: Mind yer own fuckin’ business!!! Skitzo: I’m really starting to get hungry after all this travelling… Skitzo: Well when you’re contract is worth like $500, you do start lacking in various physiological needs… Up ahead, a building appears in the distance, somewhat small, a fine whitewashed building with gold-bordered stained-glass windows. As Skitzo draws closer, the building becomes much more clearer…and the realisation of who occupies it strikes him. Skitzo: A NUNNERY?!??! Skitzo: By the highway??? Skitzo: ………………………………JACKPOTTO!!!!!!!!!!!! Skitzo: Time for a feed… Skitzo stops the buggy carrying the cage and slows to a stop in the verge, out of sight of the nunnery up ahead. Getting out, Skitzo walks along the roadside, stopping and crouching behind some bushes and looking at one through one of the larger windows. Inside he notices three nuns conversing, and a fourth carrying what appear to be cups of tea through to the room the other three are in. Skitzo: Tea! And doth mine eyes deceive me or doth I see cakes! Skitzo: Ok here’s the plan… Skitzo begins talking to himself through a plan to get a free feed while the nuns go about their business, unaware of what is about to happen to them… AAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!!! HEEEEEELP MEEEEE!!!!!!!! The screams ring out through the area, and the nuns jump up from their seats in horror at the howling from outside. The lead nun takes a few cautious steps towards the front door, and gently pulls it open, peeking outside. As she scans the area for the source of the scream, her eyes meet the motionless body of a man lying at the front of the house. His clothes tattered and his hair messed, the nun rushes out and looks over the man before declaring to her sisters in the house. Nun #1: Sisters help me! This man has been struck down…let us heal him! Lying on the ground thinking to himself, Skitzo manages a small smile through his homeless appearance. Skitzo: This is easier than I thought! The nuns spill out of the house and take hold of Skitzo, doing the Good Samaritan thing and taking him into their care. As they prop him down on a sofa, and discuss how to help this mysteriously fallen victim, Skitzo opens his eyes slightly to the sudden realisation he is surrounded by… Skitzo: TITTYBOOBS!!!!!! LET ME AT ‘EM!!! The nuns of course burst out into an ungodly screaming frenzy and promptly threw Skitzo back out of the house. Standing up and dusting himself off, Skitzo yells at the door. Skitzo: I JUST WANTED SOME DAMNED CAKE AND TEA!!!!!!!!! The nuns are still screaming in the house, while Skitzo looks up at the chimney. Skitzo: New idea! Skitzo: WE WOULDN’T NEED A NEW IDEA IF YOU COULD KEEP YOUR FUCKING HORMONES AT BAY!!!!! Skitzo: At least I’m not a redhead… Skitzo: True…tee hee… Skitzo begins his climb up the roof, scaling a drainpipe to get up onto the tiled roof of the nunnery. Looking at the highway across the way from him, he grunts and turns to the chimney, and stopping next to it, does what any good character does and pulls a full length fishing rod out of his pants, and reels the string and hook down the chimney…meanwhile in the house. Nun #1: Who WAS that ghastly creature? Nun #4: I never knew evil like that existed… Nun #2: Let us pray for our souls sisters…From the fireplace, a little hook suddenly appears in the empty space, and actually begins looking around the room as though it had a life of its own. Snaking its way to the floor, hidden as the monofilament blends with the carpet, the hook sneaks around the back of a very nunnish chair, and around the back of the sofa, underneath it…and between the feet of the nuns before snaking up the leg of a coffee table and hooking onto something…Skitzo pulls the rod back in time for a loud ripping sound to echo out, the force of the tugging sending him reeling back and slipping off the roof, crashing into a bush before one of the windows. Immediately getting up, Skitzo looks into the room in time to see one of the more nubile nuns running around in the nude, her clothes obviously…oh wait…there they are floating up from the roof, sifting down and landing on Skitzo’s head…covered in soot and the smell of a virgin wench. Skitzo: It’s times like these I wish I was gay… Skitzo: I am! Skitzo & Skitzo: YOU ARE?! Skitzo: Nope, but you shoulda seen the look on your faces!!! HAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!! As Skitzo laughs out, the nude nun suddenly turns and glares at him, her eyes almost as red as her rather cold nips. Skitzo barely has time to get out of the way as the coffee table comes crashing through the window…though inches from his face he suddenly shudders. HOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK!!! HONK! HONK! HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOONK!!! Skitzo opens his eyes again and shakes his head to find he is travelling along the roadside of the very same highway as before. Looking around frantically, he sees the cage and notices the cars that pass by are the same as before. Skitzo: What the fuck was that? Skitzo: A déjà vu dream? Skitzo: What about-… Up ahead, Skitzo suddenly notices the white golden building from before, and his eyes widen in horror. Skitzo: UHHUHHHUHH! OHHH!!! GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY WAAAAAY!!!!!!!!!!!! With that, Skitzo puts the foot to the floor and speeds past the nunnery, the sun drawing overhead and glinting off of the overhead sign reading “Sacramento – 10 miles”… Skitzo: Just so this wasn’t a wasted experience for everyone…Nerezza is a dirty vampire pigeon, and Gaz can’t crack a decent fart, never mind a decent prank! HA! SKITZO IS GONNA BEAT YOU TO FUUUUUCK!!!! Skitzo speeded off into the distance as the generic camera suddenly goes to static. The End
