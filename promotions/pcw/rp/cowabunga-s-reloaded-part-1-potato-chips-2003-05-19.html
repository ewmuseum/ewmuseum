Date: May 19, 2003 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!! What in the name of Bruce Dickinson are you doing?! [The PCW World Champion walks out into his backyard to see one of the most disgusting displays he's ever seen before. His baby blue eyes are wide open in absolute horror and how another man can subject himself to such harsh conditions] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I'm hungry, man. [The manager of the PCW World Champion quickly retorts as he stuffs another potato chip into his mouth with utter satisfaction. Lay's potato chips have begun a new campaign by putting out different flavors of potato chips for every state in America. Harry is having the super cheese chips that are supposed to be from Wisconsin, the cheese state. As the potato chip is done being chewed to perfection, Harry swallows the mushy chips into his digestive system with a moan of pleasure. There is nothing better than to eat a whole bag of potato chips while watching really hot chicks sun bath on the beach] TTT: I don't care if your hungry! I can totally whip up one of those gnarly energy drinks that I bought from GNC! THIS! This is totally bogus, dude! You shouldn't be eating potato chips at a time like this! Harry: What time is it, man? TTT: It's WORKOUT TIME!! [Harry looks into the bag of potato chips and notices that only half of the bag is gone. He then looks out to the beach and notices that many hot chicks are just lying down for a sun bathing session. It's quite obvious to even the stoned Harry that his lounging has just begun. Unfortunately, it's not obvious to his big brother that it's begun] Harry: I was thinking that it was more around one, man. [Tommy looks at his bodacious new watch which has an electronic chinese dragon in the background that dances around. It cost him two hundred dollars at the mall, but it was worth the purchase. Only thing that is heinous about the watch is that Tommy can't tell time with it, he's used to his digital watch. For the past few days he's only been guessing the time] Tommy: It's around that time, dude. But you need to get up and you need to get in that gym with me! [Harry tosses the bag of potato chips onto the ground. The ants will slowly rejoice as they have hit the mother load of food. Harry will not rejoice as he will be forced to do power squats and pushups for hours] Harry: I didn't ask for this, man. Tommy: Neither did I, dude. Harry: My back is broken, man! Tommy: That is NO EXCUSE, compadre! [Harry turns back toward the beach, his eyes searching for the hottest chick. He easily finds one that is belly down with her bikini top lying next to her. His mind is now free to think through the haze of his stoned condition. It was in November, or one of those months where it was cold, when he was struck by a not so far out spinning back kick that broke his back. It was the kick that ended the Cowabunga Connection's reign as most righteous tag team of all time. It was the moment that spiraled the Tubular One into his successful singles career. Now the PCW officials want Harry to enter the ring one more time to face off against a chick. They know he has a broken back because he GAVE THEM his retirement slip. Or was that just some homeless guy on the corner of Rodeo? Who knows. Either way it's no secret that Harry is hurt badly] Harry: What if I die, man? Tommy: I won't let you die. Harry: What if I get paralyzed, man? Tommy: Then I'll get you the most bodacious wheel chair that money can buy. Harry: With rocket boosters, man? [Tommy smiles and nods his head] Tommy: With rocket boosters. [Harry turns his attention back to the beautiful woman bathing in the sun as he rubs his chin in deep thought. Really it's just a front to throw off his brother so that he can kill time. The longer that Tommy stands there the more he'll see all of the hot chicks on the beach, which is his own backyard. Harry is happy teaming with his brother once again to form one of the most unstoppable teams in the history of wrestling, but his health is not what it once was. The Connection will not be 100 percent and certainly the rust will show. It's quite obvious that they're going to claim victory against the heinous Pharaoh and his freakin' hot Egyptian chick, but it won't be a walk in the park] Tommy: Those babes are looking extra bodacious today, Har. [SUCCESS! Harry knew that it was only a matter of time before Tommy finally gave in to the babes] Tommy: Now lets go workout. [Now that sucks. Tommy must have had a great night last night to pass up beautiful ass like that to get Harry to workout. Maybe getting a victory over Pharaoh and his Egyptian honey is more important than landing some bodacious babes. Harry knows that he must stall some more] Harry: Do you really think that you can beat Skylar Green and The Pharaoh at the same time? Tommy: Without a doubt. Now lets go to the gym. Harry: I'm just saying...what if they are in cahoots together just to dethrone you of the title? What if this whole Cowabunga Connection reunion is about crippling me and softening you up, man? Tommy: I never really thought about that before, dude. If it's like that then their plan will fail. Harry: Why's that, man? Tommy: Because Skylar is blind and Pharaoh is TOTALLY BOGUS! Harry: Yeah, man! Tommy: Check out that bodacious blonde babe. [Harry looks out and notices that the entire beach is filled with hot blonde chicks] Harry: Ummm, which one? Tommy: The one on your far left, next to the red head and only a few inches from the OTHER bodacious blonde babe! [Harry takes a second glance and, sure enough, he sees the babe that Tommy is talking about. Her pink two piece is barely covering her private parts and it's probably against the law of physics how her swimsuit is staying on her body. She's very tall and her hips just come out of absolutely nowhere, which is something that drives Tommy wild. Her skin is a very dark shade of tan and it's almost making her seem as if she's Puerto Rican but with her extremely bright blonde hair it's obvious that she's a native of California. Every movement that she makes and every smile that she makes just sends shivers down a man's spine. It's beauty in motion.] Harry: She's far out, man. You should go talk to her! Tommy: You think? Harry: WITHOUT A DOUBT! You should walk out onto that beach with your best pimp face on and invite that girl over here! GO WILD! You deserve it. You know why you deserve it? Tommy: I'm PCW World Champion? Harry: No. You deserve it because your ALL THAT AND A BAG OF POTATO CHIPS, MAN!! Tommy: TOTALLY! Harry: TOTALLY! Tommy: Without a doubt! Harry: THERE'S NO DOUBT ABOUT IT, MAN! [Tommy flips on his far out Oakley's and walks out onto the beach like a man on a mission. Harry smiles brightly at his unbelievable victory and picks up the bag of chips that he threw down in anger. The ants will now hold their tiny heads in disappointment as their food supply for the millenium has been taken away from them by an evil force] Harry: You all that and a bag of potato chips, and now I can EAT MY POTATO CHIPS! [Harry tosses a handful of chips into his mouth and holds his head back in absolute ecstasy] Harry: Wisconsin is far out, man! Fade To Black
