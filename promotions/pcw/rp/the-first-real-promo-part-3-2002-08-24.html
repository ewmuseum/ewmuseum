The First REAL Promo(Part 3)

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 [Fast forward a hour. Not much change, except our villain, Eddie Mandrake, is standing in front of a neon green screen. Still sporting that Jacky cut from an hour ago, he is standing there now…wearing a sequin, silk robe. Everyone is scrambling to get everything ready. Eddie is standing there with a script. He’s reading it intently, as everyone is getting the equipment set up. The Director, who looks like Marlon Brando combined with Fat Bastard, wearing a Purple Muumuu, has a seat in his reinforced Director’s chair. The producer is sitting in the darkness with the monitors.] Director: All right Eddie, this is going to be very simple. All I want you to do, is read the teleprompter, and make sure you get it right. Eddie: …No. Director: Come on now, don’t be a wuss. Just read it, and we can go home. Eddie: …No [BEEP]ing way am I going to read this tripe. Director: Do you want a re-write? Eddie: I want a total re-work, Damn it! The only thing that I’ll cut wearing this get up, is a long piece of bologna. Get me out of this crap. [Eddie walks off the set, rather angry.] Director: Producer, you better talk to him, and GET ME A DAMN HOAGIE! [We see Eddie entering his trailer and slamming the door shut. The Producer follows, and knocks on the door.] Producer: Eddie, look, we won’t make you do the Ric Flair thing. We do have other ideas on the docket. Eddie: You better, damn it. Re-work the [BEEP]in’ thing. Producer: Okay, we’ll let you know when you can re-enter wardrobe. Eddie: Right-O. In the meantime, I’ll raid the mini-bar. A few hours later [We return to the scene and we see the director, who has a drumstick in one hand, and the rest of a chicken in the other. The producer once again, sits in the shadows. The director is shaking his head, as he takes a bite out of the drumstick.] Director: Alright, I hope things are more to your liking now. Eddie: They are…they are much better now. Director: Okay, now, roll camera, roll sound…and…ACTION! [We now turn to the green screen, but we are looking through the television, where we see Eddie, standing in front of his logo, kind of like the very old school promos that you would see on old WWF Slam-Jams. The purple’s and black hues and lights are rather prevalent. Eddie is standing there with his hands on his hips, and owning a rather nasty leer.] Eddie: Well, Well. Pay-Per-View time. Over the last few weeks, everyone has witnessed my fury, my power… [A psychotic twitch on the right side of Eddie’s face begins.] Eddie: Although, my rather psychotic side has not been revealed yet. That is coming…even though some people may think that stringing up a pig’s intestines like crown moulding, using it’s blood as paint, and sticking it’s head on a letter opener psychotic. Well…guess what? I’m Psychotic! Scorpion, Semore Smoke, and Blayze will feel my psychosis on Sunday. Let’s start with Blayze shall we? Blayze had no business entering the match that I had with Scorpion on Tuesday. You come out, and you attack without provocation, you dare…attack me? I won’t even start on you, He will fall. Then there is Semore Smoke. I’m surprised this guy can even stand, let alone wrestle. [Another psychotic twitch occurs.] Eddie: Smoke, you want to mess with me? Do you realize what you are getting yourself into? Do you even know? I guess not. Just try me at the Pay-Per-View. You’ll know too late, what it’s like to experience the pain I have in store. Then there’s Scorpion. You are my Example, my toy, the thing that I like to play with. One of a kind, if you happen to die, I can always find another “plaything.” However, I intend to use you to the fullest, you will be my focus. You WILL KNOW WHAT IT MEANS TO FEEL ABSOLUTE PAIN! [Yet another psychotic twitch occurs. This time, Eddie has to disappear from sight briefly to try to regain his composure. When he returns, he is right in front of the camera with a very sick grin on his face.] Eddie: Boys, you all better pray to whomever you may worship in the next life. Pray that I don’t take you alive. Scorpion, you better hope that you do not wake up with me in your face. I will not become simply your worst nightmare…I will become your EVERY nightmare!! [Eddie’s eyes are glowing with anger and rage, his heart racing, and his breathing rate heavy.] Director: And……Cut. [A round of applause can be heard from all the crew in the area, as the lights return to normal. Eddie looks around, and he returns to his trailer rather quickly. The director takes a chomp out of the breast of the chicken.] Director (Mouth full): ALRIGHT! LET’S PACK UP! [James walks into Eddie’s trailer with a massive smile on his face. Eddie is motionless in his comfortable laz-E-boy.] James: Absolutely Wonderful! They are gonna love that [BEEP] I’ll tell ya. Eddie: You don’t understand at all, do you? James: What? What’s wrong? Eddie: I’ve never lost control like that before…especially in public. In private it’s completely different… James: I understand completely. If you do not want to do another take, I’ll understand. Maybe head away from here and find some way to unwind before your big PPV Match. Eddie: Maybe I will do that. [Eddie now gets up out of the chair, and slowly walks away from the sets, which are now being demolished. He heads back outside and enters his car. He inserts the key into the ignition, but pauses, briefly.] Eddie (Thinking): There is something missing…but what? [Eddie starts the car, and drives away.]
