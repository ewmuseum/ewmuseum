The Intruder Part 2

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-11-03 RP 2 Turmoil 15th Shards of glass remain scattered on the hardwood kitchen floor. The reporter for the Nashville Times sits in a chair, blood trickling down his face and his hands tied behind his back by a rope. Never before has he felt such fear for his life. All he knows is that he was sent here for a huge assignment in hopes to get front page and a promotion. If he knew yesterday that he would risk his own life to get a few answers from a professional wrestler, perhaps he’d think twice. “Please Miss, I have two small children and a beautiful wife. This is insane.” The cold ends of the double barrels press further into the man’s temple. Teresa is sitting in a chair next to him. She holds a bottle of Bud Ice. Sitting back, she places her right ankle on her left leg and shakes her head. After a long swig of her beer, she sets the bottle on the table and exhales deeply. “You damn right this is insane. I’ll tell you what else is insane. Coming inside my private home with your little notebook and expecting me to give you all these answers to your stupid little question just so you can get the credit for grabbing the scoop on Teresa Jane Tomas. Now give me one good reason I shouldn’t leave your brains scattered on my kitchen floor beside my favorite mug you broke.” Bobbie, the reporter stares to Teresa. His eyes fill with tears and leave trails down his already blood-streaked face. Thoughts of a major lawsuit come to mind if he lives to tell about it. He then gets a little brave. “Because Teresa, you’re not a killer. Sure you’ll kill innocent creatures but you won’t kill a human. I’ll make you a deal. Let me go and I won’t ask you a single question. We’ll forget I even came here.” Teresa laughs. This man obviously doesn’t know what kind of a woman she really is. True, she hasn’t ever killed a human being but she too is human and humans do change as does the seasons. She sets the gun on the kitchen table beside them and stands with a new bottle of Bud Ice in her hand. She then takes a step to him and brings a full bottle of Bud Ice to his lips. The aroma of the beer enters his nostrils. “Here, take a drink and I’ll think about it.” Stubbornly Bobbie keeps his mouth shut. For a short skinny man, he is very brave or hopelessly stupid. With her thumb and forefinger, Teresa grabs his bottom lip and pulls down. His mouth opens and she does just as she said she would. He coughs as the cold beer is poured down his throat. Teresa doesn’t stop till he chokes on the alcoholic beverage and the bottle is empty. She then places the empty bottle back on the table and sits down back in her seat. Once again the shotgun is aimed toward his head. This time, instead of his temple, the double barrels are pressed against the spot directly in between his eyes. “No, I’ll tell YOU what will happen. If I change my mind and decide to spare your sorry life, you will go home, kiss your kids, make love to your wife and if I see anything in ANY paper or magazine concerning today, I will hunt you down and when I’m done, you’d wish you had drunk that beer.” Bobbies makes a failed attempt at freeing himself from the chair. Teresa shakes her head and runs her finger over the trigger. She lowers the gun toward his crotch. Bobbie’s eyes widen. “You crazy bitch! When I make it out of here, I’m gonna pull the biggest freaking lawsuit on your psychotic ass so hard, PCW will fire you and the next job you’ll be having is roadside trash duty at the state pen.” Teresa yawns and finishes her beer. “If you make it out of here, and if you do make it home, who would believe you?” BOOM!!!
