A Year In The Making (Part1)

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 January A large spacious room serves as our opening backdrop. A murmur of activity floods the once silent room as various individuals from the Wrestle Tattler community waltz in through the double door entry-way, one superstar after another as the venue begins to reach its full capacity. The oval tables that litter the room have been tarted with claret red table cloths and name plates indicating the seating arrangement, let's be honest it wouldn't be sensible to sit both Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams at the same table, certainly with 'Shanghaied' looming. A refreshment stand sits against the western wall, littered with various delights. To the right of the food stall sits a beverage stand with a large punch bowl serving as its focal point. However the room’s main focal point is the built-up pedestal that has been kitted with various sound equipment and plays the part of the altar on this occasion. 'YAY... We're furniture shopping!' murmurs the sweet and innocent voice of PCW's Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall as she walks hand in hand with devoted husband, CEO Xavier Kannon. The great Scientologist sighs at the statement of his ditzy wife, an expression that this may very well be the beginning of a long night as he ushers her over to the seating arrangement, reserved for the party of The Front Office. Gesturing Xavier Kannon showcased his manners by drawing out the seat for Ellie~! But this rarity of 'Human' affection is suddenly interrupted upon by the voice of fellow stable mate Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'Mr. Kannon, always a pleasure....' The former PCW World Champion turns to greet his subject with his cold featureless face he stares on that of Gaz Green's, however the Emerald Menace retorts with a subtle nod to the Scientologists wife who lowers her body mass down on the missing chair. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' With picture perfect execution Xavier Kannon timely slid the chair beneath his wife, saving her blushes and further embarrassment of Hubbardville's first couple. Taking lead Xavier Kannon sat offering the onlookers nothing but stern glares, his actions prompting Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa to join them. 'Skanky whore....' snarled Ellie~! Under her breath, however her rude statement was overheard by the Soviet Scarlet who prepares to retort when suddenly as her jaw lowers, Gaz Green counters with a steamy yet unprovoked kiss on the married wife of Alexander Miyagawa. Following suit Xavier Kannon draped his arm over the slender shoulders of his beloved and tilting his head he whispers what may have been sweet nothings, may have being past tense as the camera over hears. 'Ellie~! My little Poon Puppy.... In the name of Hubbard would you simply sit there and be quiet!' Standing at the doorway like the silhouette of Satan himself is the goliath of Old School Wrestling Hessian who adorns his long flowing Norseman's cloak and skull fronted facial mask. Strolling in causing an immediate silence he is closely followed by the buzzing of a motor, the monotonous sound is soon unveiled as the electric wheel chair that Minion The Imp currently resides in. With OSW in attendance RUSH is soon represented by their beer swilling brawler Brock Newbludd who seems happy enough to take advantage of the free bar as his aftershave substitutes to the smell of freshly pulled ale. With first inspection it looks as though the seating arrangement reflects current feeling within the community as the sparse PURE table looks more an embarrassment than an asset with Senji Yulav conversing with the Spandex crime-fighter Rent-A-Hero. Hessian sits alongside ever present Minion and those associated with the Academy at present in Head Mistress Tempest and her own minions so to speak in both Magnum Wolf and Skylar Green who toys worryingly with a lighter. 'Must burn something green, I must...' yelps Skylar. Both Tempest and Magnum Wolf look at their colleague star struck as the blind performer continues to toy with the lighters naked flame. A few adjustments and the glowing yellow flame roars larger as the lighter masquerades for a potential flamethrower. 'Whoa... Psycho arsonist...' injected PWC's recently acquired Magnum Wolf. 'HOW DARE YOU!!!' snarled Skylar Green as the comment fuelled his existing rage, something obviously bothering the man once known as The End. - Meanwhile - Returning to the wooden entranceway we capture further variety in the Wrestle Tattler community as Dante Jackson makes an appearance, a former student himself he is closely followed by former Civil Disobedience member Trevor Hawke who was a product of the Boda era. Like a runaway locomotive Pete! Materialises from a cloud of smoke at the refreshment stand, disjointing his jaw, similar to that of a snake his mouth appeared as a bottomless pit as he ploughed through the food on offer. 'FOOD - FOOD - FOOD - FOOD - FOOD....' cries the jovial Pete! Devouring a silver-plated tray of sandwiches in the process. Almost at full capacity the graduates have already taken up residence with Angelo Deville sitting like a loner in a tailored business suit, sitting opposite to the Living Nightmare of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who contently shelters in the shadows. Exchanging nothing but wary glares the former classmates sit in silence as they await the unveiling, they await the arrival of law personified 'The Phantom Booker'. With all promotions in attendance, a real rarity occurs, long since forgotten, the retired Semore Smoke rolled in on his personal chariot of a wheel chair as those present and correct stare in awe. One stare in particular was that of Minion The Imp who twitched his purple nimble fingers before applying pressure to his wheel chair controls... Suddenly under the foul smell of burnt tires and a low pitched screech Minion raced forward on a collision course with former PCW performer Semore Smoke who freezes from the shock. Hitting just under five miles per hour Minion braces himself for impact when suddenly his efforts are thwarted by the dumbfounded Hessian who wrenches back on the vehicle. 'Stay away from the jobber!' hissed the satanic Old School performer. With the murmur of conversation filtering the air, with the abundance of talent on display the place appears on a 'High' when without warning the lights dim and all eyes sway to the raised pedestal expecting the biggest name in professional wrestling... Not Avalanche... The Phantom Booker. The seconds idly pass-by when on queue Hessian raises from his seated position and with a sway of his fur cloak the behemoth heads to the microphone, accompanied as always by the purple-headed warrior, Minion The Imp who has the luxury of using his wheel chair. 'What the hell....' was the general murmur from the onlookers as Hessian clears his throat in preparation for his big announcement. 'ZIEG HEIL! Now hear this, due to the fact the Phantom Booker hasn't shown his face since Jane's cunt impressions were found on Toomes' face, he isn't in attendance tonight. As you all know, the fact remains that these graduates before us tonight were under my rule, the era of Hessian in PWA, which saw many fail and few shine as brightly as the talents here tonight... Therefore it is my duty to be spokesperson... According to Gaz. In any case Angelo Deville, Nerezza, Gaz Green and Aimz - Not a member of my class but nevertheless - Are here tonight as a year in waiting finally comes to an abrupt end and I see my creations finally recognised for their efforts! You have all risen to great heights and will continue to do so, and you all have my utmost respect. I may no longer be a Headmaster in the PWA, I may no longer rule PWA with an iron fist and promotion is a common occurrence but graduation hasn't taken place since the rising of Xavier Kannon a year ago, thus to reach this level pedestal those graduating would need to be special... And indeed all of you are, ranging from 3-time PCW Tag Team Champions in under a year, to the Universal Champion to one of the most respected females in the business. Tonight is your night. You may have won many awards and titles and so forth, and PWA graduation is the icing on the cake. It was a pleasure to have taught you, and I wish you luck in all your future endeavours. A collection of oddities, each unique from the next, a mobster who has gone on to fight alongside a great in Tyler Nelson, a collective formed around a Jester and Vampire who for six months dominated the PCW Tag Team division and a woman who showed she's more than just firesnatch and giggles... Speaking of which... I will now hand you over to Amy 'Aimz' Campbell...' Before Hessian steps down in place of Amy Campbell the congregation look upon him as soft... 'Oh... And all go fucking rot in the dankest pits of hell!' A cheer from certain circles erupts as the spotlight falls on the former OSW performer, lighting her path to the platform she takes control of the microphone. - To be continued -
