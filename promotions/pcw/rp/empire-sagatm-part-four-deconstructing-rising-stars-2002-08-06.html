Empire Saga™ Part Four: Deconstructing Rising Stars…

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/02 Empire Saga™ Part Four: Deconstructing Rising Stars… [{The camera opens within the sanctity of John Crichton’s base of operations…Pastelli’s Warehouse…the perfect cover for any Criminal Organisation. The camera pans through the façade of the packaging warehouse, through to the ‘secret’ mass meeting hall and finally delving into John Crichton’s own personal office…which as the word ‘Foreman’ printed in big, black, bold letters onto the pane of glass at the upper half of the door. The camera cuts to the inside of the room, as we see John Crichton sat upon his lavish, black leather chair, with his feet up, on his equally lavish mahogany table… In the background we can hear the faint dialogue of a man speaking…as the shot zooms outwards…we can see John Crichton is watching the television…we can also see his the two familiar henchmen, George and Reginald, standing next to the door, still wearing their black three piece suits, white shirts and black ties…Our attention is drawn back to Crichton as begins to laugh slightly and shake his head in disgust…}] Henchman George: What is it, sir? Crichton: [{Looking away from the TV in the direction of George}] Henchman George! Come over here and look at this muppet on the TV… [{Henchman George walks over, standing behind Crichton’s chair.}] Henchman George: What is it, sir? Crichton: This guy on the TV, you know, that fellow I face on Tuesday Turmoil…You know Lord King Venneman Pendragon, the crusader of truth… Henchman George: What about him, sir? Crichton: Well, he rides a horse, he wears chain mail, he carries a sword and shield around with him, don’t you see what this means? Henchman George: That he has taken a liking to fancy dress parties? Crichton: No, it doesn’t mean he likes fancy dress parties…it means that he’s a PSYCHO! Henchman George: How come your happy about defending your Television Title against a psycho, sir? Crichton: [{Sighing slightly}] Because…he’ll be busy dressing up in his mother’s clothes to notice me kicking his ass! Henchman George: Sir, I don’t… Crichton: I’ve done my homework Henchman George…I’ve seen all four Psycho films…I KNOW what makes Psycho’s tick… Henchman George: I don’t think that all Psycho’s dress up in their mothers clothes, sir. Crichton: [{Looking up at Henchman George}] I don’t pay you to think! Besides he HAS to be a psycho! Henchman George: Why is that, sir? Crichton: Because he thinks I’m in league with the US Military…[{Beginning to smirk a little}] He thinks it’s a nation-wide conspiracy just because a couple of security guards [{Breaking into a laugh}] don’t know where I am…[{Putting his hands around his mouth, in an attempt to amplify his voice}] THAT’S BECAUSE I’VE SPENT THE LAST EIGHT MONTHS TRYING ESCAPING THEIR TYRANNICAL GRASP…WHAT A DUMBASS! Henchman George: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA Henchman Reginald: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA Crichton: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA Henchman George: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA Henchman Reginald: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA Crichton: MWHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA…[{Putting his head in his hands}] I’m gonna get my ass copy-righted for this… [{All three men instantly stop the copy right infringement laugh and remain silent for a couple of seconds, before Crichton’s attention is drawn back to the TV as Pendragon’s promo continues…}] Crichton: [{Starting to giggle again}] Did you just hear what he said? Henchman George: [{Almost breaking into a full-blown laugh}] Yeah… Crichton: He said…[{Breaking into a drawn out laugh}]…He said that I was from a fantasy land… Henchman George: I know… Crichton: But he did get one thing right… Henchman George: Really? What’s that? Crichton: I am a kind of superman! Henchman Reginald: [{Muttering}] That’s debatable… Crichton: [{Fixing Henchman Reginald with a VERY disgusted look}] What was that, Henchman Reginald? Henchman Reginald: Nothing, Sir…[{A smile developing upon his face}] ************ Still he remained undetected…however, he still had yet to make a move on the villain known as John Crichton…he found himself hesitating to make his move, especially after seeing that little demonstration he performed the previous night… His plans had to be changed…for he hadn’t previously catered for John Crichton’s abnormal strength…the last time the two of them met Crichton didn’t have chance to show off the strength, for the man who ‘controlled the squirrels and various other furry animals’ sent his swarm of furry animals upon him…not for the first time his plans had been cancelled by outside oppression… …This time he would not fail…his new plan would assure this…he wasn’t going to confront the man face to face…he was going to bring this criminal organisation down around his ears…allowing the proper authorities to realise where he is hiding… …Normally he would like to confront the villain face-to-face, but he had to back down…for he wasn’t sure whether he could take Crichton one on one and his mission to take down John Crichton must not fail…that was the most important factor in the whole equation…
