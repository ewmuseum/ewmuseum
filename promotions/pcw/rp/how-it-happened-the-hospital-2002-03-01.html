How It Happened: The Hospital

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st March 2002 (The Author Fully acknowledges that although this is set mostly in 1997 and that MWF didn’t exist then. However, it’s nicer than talking about the abysmal 1997 year of WWF or WCW which we didn’t get in England at the time.) August 27th, 1997Anaheim Central HospitalCasualty Department His eyes open, adjusting to the light that shines so deeply into them. He tries to sit up, but his head feels so heavy, so he lets it lie there on the pillow, unsure of who or what he is. The neon bulbs shine into his brain and for a second he gets a slight tick in his eye, but that stops quickly. The camera zooms out from his eyes and shows that it is Miyagawa... looking rather younger than he does now... his hairline having moved back down towards his eyebrows by a good three inches and having no beard. Or well... a better way to put it is that the beard is yet to be grown. He finally struggles up onto his elbows and as the camera pans out some more, it reveals that he is in a green hospital gown and lying on a casualty department trolley in one of the little cubicals. A grey haired doctor wanders into shot wearing his typical white lab coat and of course, carrying a clipboard. Doctor: Hi there, I’m Doctor Mark Sloan... and you are? Miyagawa sits there on the bed a little dumb founded, the look on his face indicates that he really is trying to think of something but just manages to fall short of the target that he is aiming for. Doctor: Don’t worry if you can’t think of your name, it’s perfectly normal to be suffering from mild amnesia after what you just went through. Miyagawa: Went through? What did I just go through? The Doctor looks at Miyagawa a little worried and quickly writes something down on the clipboard with a pen quickly. He looks up at Miyagawa slightly and then writes something further down. Miyagawa: Doc, be straight with me, what happened to me? Doctor: You drove a car off a bridge onto a railway line. Luckily it wasn’t one that was in use at the time, but your car is a wreck. Miyagawa: They say the car makes the man... what car did I have? Doctor: Let me just check the notes... **checks information on clipboard** apparently you had a Skoda. White; you owned a white Skoda. Miyagawa looks completely shattered, as if his whole world just collapsed. Miyagawa: I might not be able to remember who I am, but I owned a white Skoda. I must be a complete loser. But at least you can trace the car to find out who exactly I am, right? Doctor: Not exactly. After they pulled you out of the car, the petrol tank ignited and the car exploded over a five hundred meter area. They just don’t make under priced exploding cars like the used to. They couldn’t retrieve the number plate nor the number etched into the engine. I’m sorry. And you didn’t have any ID on you, so that doesn’t show us who you are... however, you left your driving licence in the car, and we managed to retrieve a small burned portion of it which luckily has your name on it. Unfortunately we searched the local database and we couldn’t find you. Miyagawa: So what is my name then? Doctor: Apparently... **checks information again** Your name is “Isawa Miyagawa”. The front part of the box was burned off, but it centres it when they print it, so it would have only had something there if it was really long. There is a slight chance it’s incorrect, but all evidence leads us to presume that your name is indeed “Isawa Miyagawa”. Miyagawa scratches his chin in expectancy that the mention of his name should open up some sort of memory door in his mind, but alas it does not. He shrugs back at Doctor Sloan. Miyagawa: Sounds kinda Japanese. **Pauses** I’m Japanese, aren’t I? Doctor: Well strictly speaking, because you’re pretty much a John Doe and found in the US living as far as we can tell as an American Citizen you qualify fully as a fully fledged American Citizen. Although I suppose you could call yourself a Japo-American. Miyagawa: Japo-American. I like that. Maybe I could issue my own passports or something. **Grins for a second** Anything else you know about me? Doctor: I’m afraid not, that’s the limit of everything we have on you. There wasn’t much recoverable from the accident. ******************* March 1st, 200215 Harlington StreetSuburbs of Anaheim, California Miyagawa: And that’s how it all happened... well, that’s what happened at the hospital anyway. A fifty year old or so woman passes over a plate of cookies and Miyagawa takes one from the plate and dunks it in his cup of tea before taking a bite. Miyagawa: Thanks... Mom. I’ll get that belt for you on Sunday for you. In fact, I’m pretty sure I can get you ringside tickets if you like... I’ll ask Duke.
