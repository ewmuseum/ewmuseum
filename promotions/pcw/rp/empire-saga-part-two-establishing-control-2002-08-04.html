Empire Saga Part Two: Establishing Control...

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/02 The Empire Saga Part Two: Establishing Control… It had been almost a week since his inter-gender tag match on Tuesday Turmoil, in which he and Jaden were pitted against, the satanic duo of Clyde and Bethany…he was still in pain now. Going into the match he was fully aware of Clyde’s demonic nature…his brutality…and his totally sadistic fighting style…however Crichton still questioned result. Yes…okay Clyde did kick his ass from pillar to post…but he got some good shots of his own in…Crichton never doubted his ability to take Clyde out in a fair fight…but every match with Clyde was never…EVER fair. Crichton had to admire his innovative ways to break the rules…his innovative way to knock people out… ‘Where the fuck did he get the golf buggy from anyway?’ he silently mused, holding his ribs in pain. …Then the icing on the cake came when one of Shabazz’ former thugs ‘The Rush’ Rush Mason came down to the ring to attack Crichton…he got his ass kicked as well…and then sent to hospital to boot…served him right for getting involved where he shouldn’t have… ‘Did Rush actually believe he was at the same level as John Crichton?’ He silently mused, shifting uncomfortably in his ‘throne’. The question was debatable at best, considering Rush was only the PCW Extreme Champion last week…he wasn’t good enough to face Crichton…he was good enough to be the TV Champion…although some in the PCW locker room no longer believed that Crichton deserved to TV Champ anymore… ‘What did he have to do to prove himself to these people?’…He beat the living crap out of Jordan Shabazz three separate times…twice out numbered by Shabazz and his group of thugs…in fact Crichton firmly believe that HE was the one who drove Shabazz out of PCW…he figured that Shabazz couldn’t take it that ‘a white boy’ was SO much better than him. His problem ended with Shabazz as soon as he resigned from the PCW…Crichton never liked to dwell in the past…he preferred focusing upon the things in hand…like his inevitable battle with current Rising Star champion Pendragon…Crichton had never actually seen the kid wrestler…but he brought A LOT of reputation with him into the match…perhaps the PCW match bookers had finally found a legitimate challenger for his much loved Television Title? Crichton doubted it greatly, but it was never out of the question. ************ After his last match, Crichton had headed straight back to his newly ‘acquired’… ‘business’, located in his hometown of Chicago, Illinois…more specifically, in Pastelli’s Warehouse…he sat on his luxurious leather chair, behind his luxurious mahogany table. He had overthrown Freeze from his tyrannical empire…and taken it for himself…for himself to use and abuse. All that was left to do, was to convince the rest of Freeze’s empire to look upon him, John Crichton, as their new lord and master. Crichton had looked in Freezes’ ‘appointment book’…and found that all of his ‘employees’ would come to the warehouse that very night…to update him on their progress in their lives or crime…update him on all of the mayhem they’ve cause and how much money they have made over the past week…then Freeze would extract his percentage and send his troops back out, into the public once more. Crichton didn’t want to disrupt the smoothness of the system…so he decided that he would adopt this system in his tenure as the Tyrant of the Empire… It was simple really…he would wait, until all of his new ‘workers’ came back to the warehouse…where, instead of being confronted by Mr. Freeze, they would be confronted by their new lord and master…John Crichton. At first he would talk to his new troops, try to persuade them to accept him as their new leader…if this tactic failed, he would try to gain their confidence and their ‘services’ by using the age-old tactic of power and force…he would show them their industrious leader, Mr. Freeze, he would show them what his new role in the company would be…Crichton’s personal waiter. THAT should gain their attention…then he would draw them into his little scheme by appealing to their weak spot…all criminal weak spots…their greed. He would persuade them of the wealth that would come, if they just followed him…it would work…it had to… ************ Crichton stood upon the stage that elevated high above his future employees…he was stood behind a wooden stand, which housed his various papers…notes of guidelines he should follow…appearance was EVERYTHING and he appeared to be a consummate professional. Granted, he wasn’t sure how many members of the audience would appreciate this…but it was an essential image to present to his future work force. The seats placed before him were now almost full…it was almost time to begin…two massive henchmen, Crichton had already convinced to join him, opened the door for another set of his workforce…upon seeing the last few groups enter HIS establishment, he wasn’t filled with much confidence. His professional look just wouldn’t cut it, with this ‘group of people’. He used the word ‘people’ sparingly…a more appropriate word would be ‘apes’, but he had to make do with what he had. His trusty henchmen at the door nodded to Crichton…a signal that everyone who was expecting to come to this ‘meeting’ were here…so it was time for Crichton to begin… Crichton: I know what you’re thinking…[pause] you’re thinking ‘who the hell is that guy?’ [a lot of people in the crowd nodding in confirmation of Crichton’s suspicions] Well…I am your new boss…I now own this crime empire now…and Mr. Freeze will no longer be a part of this businesses operations from now on…[a lot of murmuring from Crichton’s audience as the information slowly began to sink in] …Yeah, you heard right, I have taken control of all business ventures that ‘Patelli’s Warehouse’ is involved in…to put it bluntly…I am the MAN now…and if you want to continue ton work for this business you WILL accept me as your new leader…you’re new boss…[even more murmurs from the crowd, this time a little louder than before] …Mr. Freeze was a deficient leader…he was stupid and useless [more murmurs] …but with me this WILL all change…this organisation WILL prosper…and we WILL take dominance within the ranks of all criminal organisations IN THE WORLD…let alone in the United States of America…have no doubt about it gentlemen…we WILL cause havoc…and we WILL become billionaires in the process…[an equal mixture of approval and scepticism abundant] …What you don’t believe me? You don’t believe that I can do better at this than Mr. Freeze? You don’t believe that I’ve seized control of this organisation from the illustrious Mr. Freeze?…[A smile developing on his face, as the crowd gasp in awe] …Well let me prove it to you! Send him in guys… The two hefty henchmen at the back of the room nod in understanding as they open the door once. This time we see Mr. Freeze…wearing a white suit, with a platter of drinks in his right hand. Crichton: [A bigger smile developing upon his face] There he is! There is your former boss! Now merely my personal waiter…I seized control of this organisation, not be holding a gun to his head…but in a fight…a fair fight…that was over in a matter of seconds rather than minutes… …Some of you may watch the news, it’s doubtful, but if you have you will know that I, John Crichton, have fought four members of the Avengers to a stand still…after the fight I wasn’t put into prison or killed…I escaped to fight another day…believe me when I tell you that I am not the one with whom to fuck…and if you work with me, rather than against me, we will make this organisation succeed…beyond all expectations…until we ARE the most powerful criminal organisation on the planet… …Bigger than the Mafia…bigger than H.Y.D.R.A…hell even bigger than Starbucks…[a mixture of applause and laughter from the crowd] …So what do you say, eh? You can either walk out that front door and go back to being NOTHING…or you can stick with me and I’ll make you rich…and POWERFUL…[The audience explode in approval] ************ He had done it. He had successfully ensured the man-power needed to make this organisation work…make this organisation profitable…to create the kind of safety he needed to keep himself intact from all of the enemies he had made over the last couple of months…at last he could rest easy…safe in the knowledge that nobody had the balls OR the ability to hurt him in any way shape or form… ************ He had gained entry into the ‘meeting’ remarkably easy…he heard everything Crichton had to say and was silently impressed by the speech…from what he could gather, so was everyone else in attendance…but that made it easier form him. Crichton would think he had a sweet little deal going on here…more importantly he would think that he was safe…he was wrong…
