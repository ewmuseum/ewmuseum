The Skin Doesn't Lie

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Skin Doesn't Lie Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 08/03/03 “Ugh, I was hoping that one would of faded by now.” The body of Xavier Kannon is the human equivalent of a Magic Eye puzzle. To the casual glance, or viewed from a distance, his toned body and chiselled face are blemish free... Still the image of the pretty-boy Xavier of old, before the scowl replaced the smirk. But when you look closer, when you focus, and hunt, his body shows his story. “Sure brings back some memories.” Looking closer is exactly what Xavier is doing how, standing in front of a full length mirror, looking himself up and down. His skin a timeline of his wrestling career... The attacks, bumps, chairshots... All leaving their reminder. His voice has an aura of fondness, each scar and blemish having made him the man he is today... And nobody loves the man looking in the mirror like the man looking back at him. Xavier : “Halloween 2001, I remember freaking out at tasting my own blood.” The tip of his right index finger slides up just above his hairline, the nerves sending back a slight difference in texture where a scar lurks... Where once was a rip in the skin formed from raking against a cage, only a memory and slight touch sensation remain. Xavier : “Until I learned you have to spill red to gain gold.” Lips breaking into a smile, in his mind he’s standing on the top level of a Triple Cage set-up back in NTICW. No longer a PCW superstar, he’s a hungry rookie again. No longer gaining from his reputation, he’s still building one... And in his hand, clenched in fingers caked in dry blood is the NTICW World Title. Slowly, his eyes follow his finger as it moves down his torso, drifting to the left side of his body where a dark line remains... A calling card from the scalpel that cut open his side to remove his spleen. Xavier : “But I never realised wrestling could cost an organ.” There’s an obvious reason why Xavier is less fond of this memory... He lost his spleen but gained a legion of fans. That night, he turned into their hero, NTICW’s biggest Asshole became their hopeful saviour by default. Give him the choice, no matter how redundant it is, he’d of kept the spleen. Xavier : “And then I got THIS... For HIM.” Disgust echoes off Kannon’s vocal chords, if the memory of the spleen scar brought thoughts of disdain, the small line running through his left eyebrow, where hair no longer grows, angers him more than any other. A scar gained from valour became a reminder of what he now despises. Xavier : “And I still never got a thank you.” He can still remember it being stitched up, blood from un-stitched cuts needing to be wiped away every now and then. That night Xavier was a hero, he didn’t win a match... Or a Title... He didn’t even land a punch... But in trying to rescue Jeremy Howard’s brother Justin, Xavier was beaten within an inch of consciousness. Bloody hand-prints marked where he crawled, trying to find someone to tell, hoping it wasn’t too late for someone else to rescue Justin. But the biggest scar from that night was emotional, as the bond between mentor and apprentice was weakened... And Kannon would never sacrifice himself for anyone ever again. Xavier : “But I got my revenge...” His finger moves up, softly following the slope of his forehead until it hits a groove, not even noticeable up close, it can only be felt. Xavier : “I took what he loved, I took his belt... And all he could give me was this.” Just an inch above that scar he got from serving Jeremy Howard, is the one he received fighting him. Angered, furious, and disappointed by the actions of a man he considered his friend, Jeremy Howard put Xavier’s body through more brutality than anyone. No Cage drops, Powerbombs, anything Akiyama could do... Nothing... Could equal the ferocity of the attacks Kannon was subjected to at the hand of The Krazed Lunatic. For Xavier didn’t just hit a nerve, he whacked the entire nervous system. One scar on his forehead, probably 5 years off his career. Xavier : “I really need to book some laser treatments soon....” It doesn’t look as if Xavier has ever really noticed them before, his neck cranes forward to observe their reflection in the mirror, pupils dilated. Until now he hadn’t weighed up the cost of this damage against all he’d gained in wrestling. His body may ache, his joints may be stiff, but at least he had his appearance... But that too has been tainted. Xavier : “...Can’t have PCW’s main attraction looking anything less than perfect.” But as he looks at the reminders of the damage he’s taken, in his mind the Scientologist knows at March Madness he’s only opening himself up to even more. He could of retired after Shanghaied, but just one week in a hospital bed was too much, and the last 6 weeks on the sidelines have had him itching to return. Despite all he’s achieved, something keeps bringing him back... There’s something he’s yet to do in PCW, that one thing stopping him from being complete. Does he even know what it is? He could of booked himself in countless matches to become a 2-time World Champion, if he wanted the Title Sweep he wouldn’t of brought back the Rising Star Champion. Like Salmon drawn upstream, some latent sense in Xavier’s mind keeps bringing him back to the ring, until whatever it is happens. Xavier : “Well, you’ll probably all have some more company soon.” Until he can finally walk away, until he feels he’s ‘complete’, Kannon knows his body will be cut, battered and bruised. Out of the 29 others he could be in the ring with next Sunday, so many will have reasons to go for Kannon... Akiyama - If he even returns. Rent-a-Hero - The one man who may of suffered the most at the hands of Xavier. Duke - If tossing Xavier around were an Olympic sport, Duke would have a Gold medal to show off. ...More matches equal more hits equal more scars equal more memories. Xavier : “And until... Well, who knows?” One day he might find out why he does it... Why he walks into arenas full of 1000s who hate him... Why he’ll throw his body around like it’s un-breakable... It isn’t for the money or he’d of sold PCW for millions and retired. It isn’t for fame as he despises most of the world’s populous. It isn’t even for Ellie~!, as most things are. Even with a man in the spotlight as much as Kannon, there are still mysteries. Turning away from the mirror, he turns away from his memories... From the past. In his future now is March Madness, a chance to emulate the likes of Tyler Nelson and Jeremy Howard by becoming 2-time PCW Champion, and the chance to show that no matter how many time you put him down, he’ll get back up, he’ll keep returning to the ring... ...And maybe one day we’ll know why. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
