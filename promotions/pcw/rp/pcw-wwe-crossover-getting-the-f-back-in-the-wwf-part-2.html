Title: PCW/WWE Crossover-Getting The 'F' Back In The WWF Part 2 Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-31-00 ..::/::..Our story begins during a lovely afternoon in the city, hundreds of normal citizens like you and I walking the streets in efforts to get to their respective places, not a cloud in the sky as the sun makes it's presence known to all those below. But the main focus within the city is none other than the city mall, where hundreds of citizens go to satisfy their shopping urges. Within it's walls is three floors worth of popular stores, restaurants, and novelty shops including Wendy's, Abercrombie & Fitch, Manhattan, Babbages, and Hot Topic. Black, white, blonde, tall, old, young, and everything in between hustle and bustle through the mall, filling the floors with action. On the second floor, we find none other than everyone's favorite superhero, Rent-A-Hero, donning a full body Venom replica suit complete with thin white spider insignia on the chest, shouting and hollering merrily while playing a spirited game of Marvel vs. Capcom 2 with WWE's The Hurricane, a slight sweat breaking from his green and black hair and running down his mask as he recieves a digitized beating from everyone's favorite superhero within the mall's 'Tilt' arcade, jam-packed with lights and hot music as games like Dance Dance Revolution and Tekken 4 rest within it's walls. As the two rage on in digital battle, the screen flashing with action, our hero exclaims..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Submit now Hurricane, there is no way you can best my button mashing powers friend!! NOW TELL ME....WHO'S YOUR HERO!!'The Hurricane:'DEFINITELY NOT YOU!! WAIT TILL I CAN BUILD ENOUGH POWER IN MY COMBO BAR YOU FIENDISH GAME PLAYER YOU!'..::/::..They both mash the buttons and jerk the control stick on their respective sides of the arcade machine, both men intensely serious about beating the other within this arcade battle. As the Hurricane takes a slight glance behind him, his eyes bug out in shock as he sees two redheads leaning against the silver rail outside 'Tilt'. Given a closer look, Hurricane identifies the female to and male to be none other than OSW's Reno Davis AND AMY CAMPBELL....the Lois Lane of our currently arcade engaged hero. The two seem to be enjoying eahc other's company, Reno playfully tapping the soft shoulders of Aimz as she throws back her hair in an almost flirtatious manner towards the fellow OSW star. Hurricane can't take his eyes of this suspicious sight, hurricane exclaiming..::/::..The Hurricane:'GREAT JERRY SPRINGER RENT-A-HERO, TURN AROUND IMMEDIATELY, YOU MUST BE MADE AWARE OF THIS SITUATION!!' Rent-A-Hero:'Nice try 'Cane, I'm not losing this match...next thing, you'll tell me that babies are born from girls and not in magic cabbage fields...psssh.'The Hurricane: *Turning to Rent-A-Hero with utter seriousness* 'RENT-A-HERO, I'M AS SERIOUS AS THE WWF'S RATINGS DROP, YOU'VE GOT TO TURN AROUND!!' Rent-A-Hero:'Your trickery is quite pathetic, but I shall indulge myself to look for your sake.'..::/::..Both our heroes turn about, the couple nowhere to be seen, suprising Hurricane while Rent-A-Hero gives a hearty laugh of disbelief as he turns back to the arcade machine to finish off Hurricane as 'KO' appears widely across the screen..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'See, even when you try as you might with your efforts to cheat....my Rent-A-Powers always help me prevail in the end. You were acting like you saw my beloved, sweet sweet Amy cheating on me or something.' The Hurricane:'Bu...'Rent-A-Hero:'NO BACKING OUT NOW HURRICANE, you lost so you are paying for our afternoon consumption partner. Come, let us traverse to the food court shall we?' The Hurricane:'Yea...I guess...partner.'..::/::..Hurricane reluctantly walks aside Rent-A-Hero, still adamant about what he saw, but having no proof to back it up as the two leave Tilt and walk the second floor of the mall, discussing things along their way..::/::..The Hurricane:'So, your facing Jason Snow on Fusion huh my spandex enwrapped partner of saving stuff.'Rent-A-Hero:'Wow, did you Hurri-Senses tell you that?'The Hurricane:'No...but that big billboard up there sure did help.'..::/::..The Hurricane points his taped hand upward to the billboards hanging against the third floor rails, Rent-A-Hero looking up to find a billboard with his face upon it and Snow across from him, with the other information on the match upon it..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Oh yeah right...' *laughs with a hint of stupidty in his voice* 'Well, Snow is a heinous villian within the roster of the PCW and I am the chosen one to bring him down my emerald green compatriot. He struts around PCW thinking he can commit any crime he feels like and that he is worth all the hype he continually pumps his infalted ego with. Well 'Cane, I'm the pin that's gonna burst his bubble, for my KAMEHAMEHA powers are going to do the one thing so many others have failed to do...END THE REIGN OF EVIL THAT IS SNOW. It's time someone put him in his place and if not now, then when fellow hero? This is why I'm willing to risk my life, my career, and ympowers to bring him down once and for all so he can feel the full pain that justice will bring upon him. He has less credibility than Britney Spears trying to be a gangster rapper, and it will all be proven when I out quaff him in the ring with these KRYPTONIAN POWERS! If it's a fight he wants, then it's a fight he shall have when he and I square off to find out what force truly does prevail in the end...good or evil. It migth be an uphill battle...but no matter the cost, no matter the pain I must put myself through....GOOD WILL PREVAIL, you can bet your sweet spandex on it Hurricane.' The Hurricane:'I believe you partner. I'm sure you will have all the powers to succeed in defeating this evil Snow-man. Now let me talk about ym match with Tajiri next Smack...'Rent-A-Hero:'NOW HALT ONE DARN TOOTIN MINUTE HURRICANE....I don't know how thigns work in the WWE, but in a RENT-A-HERO RP, I'M the only one who gets to have long-winded speeches. AFFIRMATIVE?!'The Hurricane:'Oh fine...see if I ever let you get a long-winded speech when I invite YOU for a crossover. Anyways that's not important, I was told by an informant, well actually a fortune cookie...might I add it was quite scrumptious and the fortune read 'You will find an 'F' stealer in the city mall'...'Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY MOO-SHOO AND EGGROLLS...those fortunes sure do get detailed.' The Hurricane:'Tell me about it...but whoever has their hands on that 'F' is somewhere within this mall and it's our job to find...'..::/::..Before The Hurricane can even finish his sentence, he turns his eyes and finds them locked upon none other than two of the WWE's most gorgeous females....Stacy Kiebler and Torrie Wilson, standing in front of a Victoria's Secret shop no doubt.. The two voluptuous blondes smile sweetly at the two heroes, quietly gesturing them over as they lure them in with their sweet looks. Stacy lightly tugs at her illegally short black skirt as it does a terrible job of covering her 41 and a half inch legs and Torrie strikes her all too familiar 'hands on hips' pose as they both obviously want the heroes just a little closer. The Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero looks at the beauty that calls them forward, then each other, then the females again, their mouths hanging wide open as Rent-A-Hero says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY.....BOOTY...SHORTS....HURRICANE....' The Hurricane:'In.....deed....my friend....'Rent-A-Hero: *coming to senses* 'WAIT WAIT WAIT, first of all this is wrong, I have a Lois Lane....second of all, how do we both kow this isn't some intricate trap set up by a higher power to lure us within that store so we can be jumped by some unknown evil?!'The Hurricane:'Now Rent-A-Hero...let's be serious....like we're not going to go in there and fall for whatever trap is set for us.' Rent-A-Hero:'Why...cause it's just waht we heroes do?'The Hurricane:'NOOOO....I read it in the script.' ..::/::..The Hurricane whips out a thick white script reading 'WWE/PCW Crossover Part 2' from out of his emerald green cape. Rent-A-Hero shakes his head in disbelief, snatching the script and flipping through it before exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Holy Low Life Losers....someone DOES actually plan this crap. Well, with the said...let us fall into the trap then..' The Hurricane:'And let us pray we get a slight peek of an undergarment...if the Power Of Greyskull is kind...'..::/::..Both the Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero sigh with laxed hope as they both succumb to the charms of the WWE Divas without anymore thought, the two gently flaunting their bodies to get the two heroes within the Victoria's Secret shop with little to no real effort. Upon getting the two within the shop, littered with assorted types of designer bra's, panties, and other feminie 'feel good clothing', the two WWE Diva's whisper in each other's ear quietly, both giggling as our hero and the Hurricane stand confused and in a daze..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'They're whispering...this can't be good...' The Hurricane:'Rent-A-Hero...something's tingling...and it ain't my HURRI-SENSES!!'..::/::..Our hero sighs in shame of the ease of how The Hurricane has forsaken his morals, the two girls finally ending their whispering as they both eye our heroes, Stacy saying to them both in that silky seductive voice of hers..::/::..Stacy:'So boys...what do you think we should do with you?'The Hurricane:'TELL US HOW YOUR INVOLVED IN THE PLOT OF TAKING THE 'F' FROM THE WWF...or simply use as sexual objects, either way...I go home one happy hero.'Rent-A-Hero:'Darn WWE superheroes...'..::/::..The two girls once again giggle, Torrie this time taking her turn to speak as she says..::/::..Torrie:'Well as much as we'd love to have a little fun with you boys...there's someone else who's already voulnteered for the job...'..::/::..Suddenly, the huge 'SALE' pile of panties flies up in the air and emerging from within it is non other than....THE UDNERSELLER...I MEAN UNDERTAKER. He strikes a mean face, removing his sunglasses and placing them in his faded black jeans as he cracks his knuckles and says..::/::..Undertaker:'I dropped the WWF's ratings...NOW I'M GONNA DROP YOU...boy. Wait...that didn't sound good hold on, let's do that again. Can ya'll put the panties back on me again?'..::/::..Stacy, Torrie, Rent-A-Hero, and The Hurricane stand collectively confused by the Undertaker as he just stands there, knowing he made himself look like a complete idiot before saying..::/::..Undertaker:'Oh fine fine fine...how bout this one....I'M GONNA CHEW YOU HEROES UP LIKE I CHEW MY TA-BACCA...boy!' *utter silence* 'Screw it, I'm just kickin' yer asses....boy.'The Hurricane:'OH NO, HOW CAN WE EXPECT TO BEAT HIS NO-SELLING POWERS?!'Rent-A-Hero:'NOT TO MENTION HE WON'T GIVE US ANY HEAT WHEN WE TRY TO MAKE A COMEBACK!! BUT WE MUST STAND TALL HURRICANE.....NEXT RP!! NARRATOR MAN, FADE OUT PLEASE, HURRY!!'..::/::..I don't get paid enough for this....and with that, The Hurricane and Rent-A-Hero are left face to face with the WWE's most dangerous backstage force, THE UDNERTAKER.
