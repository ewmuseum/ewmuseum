I Wanna Be A Contenda!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/02 Time: 1: 16 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Gym “I Wanna Be A Contenda.” The scene opens with Juan Borro at the local gym in Lisbon, Portugal. Juan felt in order to have a good showing in the Pay-per View he’d get himself a personal trainer. Juan isn’t sure about the idea of a personal trainer, but he knows if he wants to be in shape and have a better chance of winning at the Pay-Per View, getting a personal trainer is a good choice. Juan is just entering the gym and is signing a few papers for membership. Juan looks around to see if he sees the person who is training him anywhere. He sees loads of trainers, but they all pass him by. Juan then sees somebody he is certain his trainer will be. A tall and skinny woman with a perfect shaped body, and long legs, is seen asking around for the name Juan Borro. Juan notices that she is. He checks her out and is left in complete awe. He goes up to her, and tries to put on a little Portuguese charm as he approaches her. Juan- So I see you’ve been looking for me. I’m guessing your either my trainer or just another adoring fan. Women- Oh please, don’t be offended, but my husband is a bodybuilder. I’ve always thought soccer was a little bit feminine, but that’s just my opinion. And I’m not your trainer, but I will take you to him. Juan- Hey! Soccer is not feminine. I mean I’m a wrestler also. You know one of those big bad wrester’s, who beat up other men all of the time. I’d call that pretty manly if you ask me. Women- Well that’s kind of odd also. The whole idea of men grappling with one another is something I’ve never found a sport in. Juan becomes offended, and shows his feeling through the way he responds to the rather sexy women. Juan-You know what, I don’t care what you think. Both of those sports are very manly, and people love the men who do them. Women- If you say so. Juan- Yes! I do say so. And wait did you say my trainer was going to be a “he” as in a person who is male. Women- Yes, you’re trainer will be Mr. Bentley. He is a very accomplished wrestling instructor and will do a great job. Juan- I remember specifically asking for a female trainer. You know somebody I would get along with a little bit better. Through my years in sports, I’ve found out nobody else is more comforting then a woman. Women- I’m sure that’s true, but when the secretary saw the survey you took a while back, it said you had a girlfriend, and you know we wouldn’t want to cause any conflict between the two of you. Juan responds a bit aggravated, but still with a smile on his face. Juan- Of course you wouldn’t want to do that, now would you? (speaks under his breath) Then again, nothing bad has ever came from having more than one woman separately or all at once. The woman hears the comment makes, as she smirks and responds. Women- I’m sure you wouldn’t mind that happening, but by the looks of you I doubt it would ever happen. Juan- Hey! You sure are quite insulting. And by the way I get so many woman you wouldn’t be able to count them. Everyone wants a piece of the Golden Heart himself, and I mean everyone. Women- Before I comment on that nickname of yours I think I’ll stop while I’m ahead. Were running late for your appointment with Mr. Bentley anyway. Juan- I was going to say the same thing, but you just kept trying to bring up more conversations with me. The woman nods her head, and goes off toward Mr. Bentley’s office, which is at the far end of the gym. Women- Follow me and try not to talk. I have a very low tolerance for men who are full of them selves. Juan doesn’t respond, as the women turns around to see if Juan is following her. When she turns around, she notices Juan’s eyes are glued to her ass, as he looks away the second she turns back. Then when she turns to the front he looks again. Juan- Wait did you say something. I was just looking at the exciting gym equipment they have here. Women- I bet that wasn’t all you were looking at. Juan shrugs, as he had no idea what she’s talking about. As they keep walking, Juan continues with a conversation. Juan- what kind of name is Mr. Bentley anyway? Am I going to get a car out of this deal or something? The woman smirks as she gives Juan a look of idiocy. Women- Yes, you’re going to be getting a car. Juan- Cool. So what nationality is this guy? I mean I’ve never really seen a Portuguese man with the last name Bentley. Women- He’s English, but speaks perfect Portuguese, so don’t worry. Juan- I won’t have to. I’m a very talented man you know, I speak the English language as well as Portuguese. Women- I’m sure you do. Juan- Ah….well don’t believe me, my girl back home is ten times better than you anyway, so ha! Women- And you think I care. A Chinese man, who is punching a punching bag, taps Juan on the shoulder. Juan turns around, and acknowledges the man. Juan- Umm….did you just tap me. Chinese Man- You dirty man who kill hero of mine. I make sure you pay for what you did, right now. The man takes out a knife, but Juan reacts quickly and kicks it out of the man’s hand. The man then sacks Juan down to the ground and grabs the knife. The women calls for security, and approaches the Chinese man. She kicks the knife out of his hand once again, and then wraps her leg around the man’s neck, choking him until the security has come. Juan- Hey, I had him handled perfectly, all by my self you know. You didn’t have to come and help me. Juan responds, while a little out of breathes. Women- Yea, but I felt I’d help out, because I wouldn’t want to see such a handsome man as yourself get hurt. Juan- Oh I see. Women- So who was that man anyone, and why was he talking about how you killed his idle or something. Juan- Long story. It ends with pretty much all-Chinese people not liking me. You see I was accused for a murder I never did. Women- Okay, that’s all I have to know. They continue walking until they finally arrive in the room of Mr. Bentley, Juan’s new trainer. Women- Well there is the door. You two should hit it off pretty quickly, so I shouldn’t have to introduce you. Juan- (responds under his breath) I’d rather be hitting it off with you. DOH! Think of Juliet, think of Juliet. Women- I heard that. Juan- Oh….. Juan then quickly enters the room, and knocks twice on the door. The door creeks open, and Juan walks right in. Juan sees a small old man, and guesses he is a secretary or something, so he goes up to his desk and talks to him. Juan- Hello! Is Mr. Bentley here? Old Man- Yes! He is. Juan- Okay, well where is he? Can you point me to him or something? Or is he already out in the gym. Old Man- Sorry to disappoint you, but I am Mr. Bentley. Juan- Oh sorry, I just didn’t expect you to be so……. Mr. Bentley- Yea, so old, small, and wrinkly, I already know that. I’ve been told it a bunch of times. Juan- Oh. Okay. Mr. Bentley- let’s get straight down to business, and hit the gym. I’m not here to talk to you, I’m here to pump you up for you match. Juan- Okay, sounds good. The two men go off, as Juan lifts some waits. Juan is given a new nutritional diet for the next week, and a group of exercises to do every day. They also go over a few new wrestling moves, for Juan to use for the Pay-Per View. The guys go on for a while, until Juan finally feels tired. The Scene Fades to black with Juan going to get a drink. But don’t be deprived, Juan will bulk up and find himself a win at the PPV.
