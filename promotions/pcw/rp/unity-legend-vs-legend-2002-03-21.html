Unity...Legend vs Legend

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-2002 “Only in the darkest of times, can one find the light. I never realized how true that could be, until I was forced into battle with mine ancestor. The most bloody day of mine life….” The scene slowly brightens and we are met with a visage of medieval type warfare on a fairly large scale. Men in black cloaks litter a green field, and a small group of horsemen ride through the carnage, a large man in golden armor at their head. In the immediate background, the massive stones of Stonehenge loom in their ancient unknown glory… Pendragon: Fan out!!! We have to reach the magician! ‘Tis he who be the cause of this unholy mess!! The horsemen fan out in a line, and they mow through a group of footmen, blades flashing in the light. With each strike, a man merely disappears, sent back to death in the time from whence he came. It is unnerving for the true men of this fight to see their vanquished foes disappear before them, but they tarry on. Pendragon races ahead of the group, in hot pursuit of the Ancient, who rides a huge warhorse amidst a group of Knights, one of which wears golden armor much like Pendragon’s… “Arthur. Why he was brought forth to face mine wrath, I have no idea. Merlin…The magician…The Ancient. ‘Twas his fault and with him I have laid my blame. But the day bore many costs…” A lone knight backs up against one of the stones, as thirty men close in on his position. He hacks this way and that, and limbs fly everywhere. The numbers though. The numbers are too much for almost any man. Richard…The Lionheart, begins to succumb. A blade pierces his thigh, and he lets out a scream… CHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGGGGGGGGEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The battle cry calls out and Pendragon, mounted upon Xerxes, crashes into the group of men surrounding the archaic king like a ton of bricks. Bodies fly and disappear as Pendragon hacks his way to the old King, but when he reaches him, he finds him sitting down, his back against the rock. Pendragon leaps from his horse and kneels beside the noble King… Richard: To die another death, is not such a thing, young Lord… Pendragon grasps the man’s gauntleted hand with his own, and a tear slides down his soiled cheek… Richard: Weep not, fair Pendragon. Thy life is just begun… Pendragon: But thee could have shown me so much. And now, I have lost thee… Richard: I am in thee, young Pendragon. Always remember that. I am in thee, and I am proud of thee…Noble King… Pendragon’s eyes widen as Richard’s body fades to nothing before his eyes. He rises, lowers his head and places his right fist to his heart in a warriors salute… Pendragon: I will avenge thee, good Richard…. “Good friends are often the cost of war, and just because this one was mine own did not make it an exception. ‘Tis a shame too…Good friends have always been hard for one such as myself to come by. The day would drag on though, and the horror was far from over…” The shot cuts to show a group of infantry bearing down upon a lone man in a golden wizards cloak. He faces them without fear, and merely points his hand, palm out, at the men. The hand glows blue and what looks like a fireball flies from the hand. The ball moves swiftly towards the now petrified men, and meets them with a huge explosion. Bodies fly this way and that, landing hard upon the reddening ground. The footmen lie lifeless, and the Wizard; Merlin, turns his back and walks slowly from them, to join the fight elsewhere… “To fight a Wizard is truly a different type of battle. The Nobles were ill prepared for the task, for the most part. They, however, did bring magic men of their own. These men were skilled, but Merlin was Ancient beyond all terms of the word…” Two men stand on a hill. Merlin in gold, a Noble in his black cloak. Though the stand twenty feet apart, their faces contort as if they are locked in combat… Noble: Vile Merlin…I’ve trained my whole life for this day… Merlin: AYE! Are thee truly the best that the Nobles have to send to me? Merlin laughs as he places an open hand toward the man. He slowly closes the hand into a fist, and the man suddenly clutches at his chest. A questioning look comes into his eyes as he slowly slumps to the ground… Merlin: Should have trained harder. Thy did have promise… “Death is an odd thing. ‘Tis something I have never feared, for my God has always protected me, and I know that when the day comes, I shall be exalted above all mortal kings. That was the promise. Yet still, when the fighting is at it’s gravest, I still fight for justice, for life. Most people want to go to heaven, but very few wish to die. And so it was when I came face to face with great Arthur. King of Legend…Mine ancient father…” Pendragon, wielding a bloodied sword and covered in blood himself, stalks to the center of Stonehenge, where waits Arthur. Arthur stands, black blade drawn, in ultimate splendor. There is not a mark of combat upon him, and he seems fresh, whereas Venneman Pendragon has fought the whole day. Pendragon stands face to face with the Legend, who merely looks at him… Venneman: Thou were my hero. And now…Thy mind is taken. Thou art not thyself. The evil Merlin controls thee as a puppet. The skill may still be in the shell, but the spirit…The spirit is absent, and thus I shall have no problem running thee through. Merlin has brought back but the evil and greed with thy body. The good has fled to it’s rightful place in Heaven, and what stands before me is but a shell of the man who once was… Arthur: Evil. Perhaps. What great Merlin did was bring back the best parts of me. Goodness has no place in the world; never did. He brought the great Arthur forth to do two things…To kill thee, and to rule beside him over the kingdom of men. And now I shall perform my first duty… With that, Arthur strikes out with his blackened blade in an offensive furry. He slashes and hacks at the younger Pendragon, who is barely able to keep the blade from his throat… Venneman: The stories are true. A master thou were…Yet I am the master now!!! YAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!! With a shout, Pendragon fights back with all his might against the ancient swordsman. He leaps this way and that, slicing and swinging his mighty blade. Arthur does his best to deflect the rain of blows, yet soon he bleeds from a dozen cuts. Excalibur slices through the ill wrought armor like butter, and it is all Arthur can do to keep from receiving the killing strike. Arthur: Thou hast been taught well. Even in my present state, thy makest thy ancestor proud of thee, young Pendragon. But this cannot go on. Something has to give… With that, Merlin appears from thin air behind Arthur, and suddenly Arthur pushes forward with an assault of his own. Thrusting this way and that, Pendragon is forced back, and slices soon appear on the lord king, and his blood blends with that he is already covered in. A look of horror spreads across Arthur’s face… Arthur: Merlin!!!??? What is it that thou art doing? I can use mine own skill against this man! I need no magical assistance! Merlin: BAH! I need to be rid of the young King. It shall be however it shall be, Fool!! Arthur continues his assault, at the same time yelling at the wizard… Arthur: Arthur Pendragon is no puppet! Unleash me from thy strings, sorcerer! Let me finish this mine own way! Merlin: Nay, my son. Thou lacks the strength ad the skill. This will end it. Arthur grits his teeth as he knocks Pendragon to the ground and levels for the killing strike… Pendragon: Good Christ…The candle has burned low indeed… Suddenly, Arthur pauses. The sword lowers and a light of recognition comes into his eyes… Arthur: End it now, young King. Send me back and put a stop to this charade. I am proud of thee, and thee has much work to do. Goodness does have a place in this world, and thou must fight for that place to be safe… Arthur kneels before the fallen Pendragon who rises and levels his own sword… Pendragon: Then let the blade of Excalibur taste thy blood for the last time…I truly wish things were different. Thou would have been a legend to merely know… With that, Arthur bows his head and Pendragon swings the sword. Arthur’s head flies from his shoulders, and Merlin lets out a scream before fading into nothing… Merlin: It isn’t over, Pendragon…. A wind blows through the glen, and through the stones as Arthur’s body slowly disappears. Pendragon’s hair blows wildly in the wind, and suddenly, from each stone, a blue light streaks towards Pendragon. He jolts as if an electric shock has taken him as the lights converge. He rises into the air before the lights, as suddenly as they begun, stop. He drops to the ground and lies motionless save for his deep breathing. The union is completed, and Pendragon has become whole…
