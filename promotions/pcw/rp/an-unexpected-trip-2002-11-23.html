An Unexpected Trip

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-23 A crowd of solemn faced people, dressed in black huddles around a small dirt area. Flowers are brought forward by a few of the people, and are either placed on top of the freshly piled dirt, or in front of a picture of a smiling woman. One of the crowd members walks forward to look at the photograph, but she bursts into tears and has to be taken off to the side by one of the men. A priest stands behind a pedestal and looks out into the group of people, as if searching for someone. A car door slams shut and Nathan emerges, wearing a black suit. Brandi grasps one of his arms loosely and the two hurry towards the group of people. As they get closer, more and more of the people turn around to stare. Brandi looks quite out of place wearing her blue dress. She pretends not to notice the glares coming from all around her, and instead makes her way to the priest. He manages to force himself to smile. Priest- Now, if everyone could just quiet down please. I believe that the deceased’s daughter is here to say a few words about her memories of a wonderful woman. The priest steps away from the pedestal, and everyone turns to Brandi. She clears her throat to quiet down the few who are still chattering amongst themselves. Brandi- First off, I’d like to thank you all for coming here today. I’d also like to apologize for being late. I wouldn’t have been late, had I not been the last person to receive the message, but hell, I’m used to that. I mean, I only found out last night while I was watching a movie. She sure did know how to ruin a person’s plans, didn’t she? The priest walks over to Brandi and whispers something in her ear. She listens quietly and nods her head before turning her attention back to the crowd. Brandi- Now, I was asked to share a few memories of my mother with you all. To be honest, I don’t really have many good memories. I mean she was self-centered and stubborn. She constantly was getting into matters that didn’t concern her. Hell, if she’d had her way, I’d be broke and living on the street somewhere! She never wanted me to succeed in a wrestling career, and then when I did, all she wanted was money. A few groans are heard from the crowd. Nathan takes a couple steps towards Brandi, but stops when she motions for him not to. Instead, he folds his arms across his chest and listens to the hushed whispers coming from the crowd. Brandi- Yes, that’s right. A few people might choose to not remember this since she’s dead and all, but I for one am not going to forget it. I was the one that placed her under psychiatric care because I knew what a threat she was to herself. Did any of you listen to me, or even attempt to help in any way? No, you didn’t. Nathan- Babe, don’t do this. Come on. Let’s just go. You’ve got Alliances to worry about and we have a plane to catch soon. Brandi- Nathan, damn you! I’m not doing anything but telling these people the things that they’ve forgotten. I don’t need to worry about Alliances right now, and you’d better stop calling me babe. Nathan- What do you want me to call you? Bitch?! Brandi- You know what? Screw you! You’re just like those other ingrates. Before you met me, you were nothing! Just some college boy thinking he could make it big one day plying football. We both knew it would never happen, so I gave you a shot to do something else. Nathan- Brandi, hun, you’re making a scene. Brandi- I’m not the one making the scene! Brandi looks around and sees everyone staring at Nathan and her. She blushes. Brandi- I apologize for my…friend’s behavior. A loud ringing noise drifts throughout the crowd. A few people check their purses and pockets, but there cell phone remains quiet. The ringing fills the air once again and Brandi looks around a bit before realizing it’s coming from her handbag. Brandi- Excuse me. Brandi grabs her phone and quickly walks over to a tree before answering it. Nathan watches her closely, and although he is out of hearing distance, the expression on Brandi’s face is clearly visible. He watches as her face goes from a smile, to a look of surprise, and finally to disgust. She nods and hangs up, replacing the phone back in the handbag. Nathan jogs over to her, and when she sees him coming Brandi quickly turns around and starts walking towards her car. Nathan jogs faster and when he catches up to her, her grabs her by the shoulder and turns her around to face him. Brandi- What in the hell do you think you’re doing? Nathan- What’re you talking about? Brandi- You damn well know what I’m talking about! Are you trying to ruin everything that I’ve worked for? Or are you just trying to destroy the friendships that I have because you have some kind of jealous fit? Tell me, Nathan. Which one is it? Nathan just stands there and stares at Brandi as her anger builds. Nathan- I’m sorry. I didn’t mean to do it. It’s just…the more I thought about everything, the more I grew angry. Brandi- So you had to go and attack Tim from behind? You know, you were wrong Nathan. You are one of those pussies that you despise. Brandi turns around and once again starts walking towards her car. Nathan stands there for a few minutes, thinking about something. When Brandi gets to her car, she fumbles with the keys, trying to unlock the door. This gives Nathan time to catch up. He reaches her just as she gets the door open and sits down. Nathan grabs onto the door to prevent her from closing it, and looks down at her. Nathan- I am truly sorry. Brandi- No, Nathan you aren’t. Let go of the door. I have a plane to catch. Nathan- You can’t just leave me out here! Brandi- Watch me. Now let go. Nathan- I have no ride and you have my ticket. I’ll be stuck here. Brandi- I know that, and guess what. It’s not my problem. This is your last chance to let go of the door, Nathan. I’ll tell you what. The whole time I’m on the plane back home, I’ll be thinking of you. Goodbye. Brandi looks over and sees that Nathan still has an iron grip on the door. She shrugs, grabs onto the door handle, and shuts it as hard as she can. Nathan lets out a howl of pain and lets go as the door bounces back open. Now Brandi gently shuts the door and starts the engine. With a smile and a wave, she quickly pulls out of the parking lot and disappears down the street.
