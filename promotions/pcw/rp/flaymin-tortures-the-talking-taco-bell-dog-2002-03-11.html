Flaymin tortures The TALKING Taco Bell Dog!!

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/3/2 When we last left off, Flaymin was coming to grips with having his luscious afro brutally shaved off by a deranged mad man named El Diablo. Thinking quick, he decided to shave Jenna’s pubic hair and glue it to his head to form a Mohawk. What conniving plan does Flaymin have in stall for El Diablo?Read on to find out… [Scene opens in Acapulco, Mexico. Cruising through the streets in a flashy 1984 Mitsubishi rent-a-car are none other then Flaymin Aymin and his buxom, blonde bombshell of a side kick-Porn Queen Jenna Jameson] Jenna: I really think you’ve got your facts all muddled up. Flaymin: For the last time, I know I’m right. I sat awake thinking about it all night long. [Jenna turns to Flaymin with a surprised look on her face] Jenna: Really? Flaymin: Well no…. but it was the most intense two minutes of brainstorming I’ve done in quite a while. Jenna: Tell me what the plan is again? Flaymin: Right. We barrage our way into the Taco Bell Head Office here in Acapulco and take that little mother-f*cking Chihuahua mascot of theirs hostage. Jenna: You think that will work? Flaymin: Sure.That Taco Bell dog is the key to El Diablo.He loves that dog as if it were his own son. Jenna: How do you know? Flaymin: Because he has posters of the damn thing plastered all over his bedroom walls. You should see his shrine dedicated to the dog.He places a burrito in front of this little golden temple shaped like a Chihuahua every morning. [Jenna begins to giggle at the thought of the El Diablo sacrificing a burrito to the Taco Bell dog.] Flaymin: That’s not the half of it.About 6 hours after he ‘sacrifices’ the burrito to the shrine, he comes back to see if the shrine has consumed it. Jenna: But wouldn’t the burrito still be there? Flaymin: Nope.I just pick it up and eat it. He thinks his Babylonian Demonic mental powers which he supposedly uses to channel with the shrine is what makes the burrito disappear. [By this point Jenna is laughing out aloud and banging her fists on the dashboard.] Jenna: That is just too stupid to be true!!! Flaymin: Oh it’s true all right. He used to feed the shrine Taco’s but he stopped soon after I told him that Taco’s would cause the shrine to let out some serious gas. It was a sight to behold.He’d walk around the shrine about fifty times, sniffing and snorting the air and analysing the putrid smell of a dirty fart. Jenna: Well then where was the stench coming from? Flaymin: That was me letting out some steam after I’d eaten the taco!! [The laughing intensifies as Flaymin and Jenna find it hard to control the amusement that is a direct result of El Diablo’s idiocy. Flaymin pulls the car up to the kerb and turns it off] Flaymin: Alright, here we are. Did you bring the weapon of choice? [Jenna reaches into her bag and pulls out a razor] Jenna: Here it is. Flaymin: Brilliant.This plan will work like a charm. [Flaymin suddenly begins to scratch his head quite vigorously. His bald head of course has a thin strip of Jenna’s pubic hair glued across the middle of his scalp.] Jenna: What’s the matter? Flaymin: This Mohawk is giving me a rash. Somehow I don’t think my head, super glue and your pubic hair mix all that well. Jenna: You should have used the blow drier. Flaymin: Nah that would give the hair split ends. Jenna: Well then what are you going to do? Flaymin: Nothing.I’ll have to put up with it until I can grow my real afro back. Nevertheless we have a job to do!! [Flaymin and Jenna get out of the car and storm into Head Office of Taco Bell. They head straight into the elevator and go up to an unspecified level. Five minutes later having realised the building has 47 floors, Flaymin and Jenna make their way back down to the ground floor to ask the receptionist for some directions. They walk over to the receptionist where Flaymin begins to ask about the Taco Bell dog’s whereabouts] Flaymin: Hi, I’m looking for the Taco Bell dog. [The receptionist looks up at Flaymin’s Mohawk and raises an eyebrow.] Receptionist: Uh… the dog is on…um…. I’m sorry your hair is distracting my attention.By the way what is that?? Flaymin: It’s a deluxe version of the Mr T Mohawk. Receptionist: That is soooo 1984! Flaymin: No, actually it was 1986.Now tell me where the damn dog is. Receptionist: He’s on the fifth floor having a bath. [Flaymin and Jenna quickly head for the elevator once again and go up to the fifth floor. Upon exiting the elevator they stare up in awe at the majestic site of the dog’s apparent home] Flaymin: Wow, look at this place. [The entire floor is shaped like a gold-encrusted taco. With flowing fountains on either side of the floor, exotic plants scattered around and a long marble floor leading into a room with a silver door. Flaymin and Jenna walk towards the room and stand outside the door] Flaymin: Ok, hand me the razor. [Jenna hands Flaymin the razor.Flaymin turns it on to make sure it still works.Upon hearing the buzzing sound indicating its operational use, Flaymin turns it back off and slips it into his pocket] Flaymin: It’s showtime!! [Flaymin opens the door and walks in. Inside the room, three maids are busy giving the Taco Bell Chihuahua a bubble bath while a fourth maid stands near the dog with a bowl full of dog biscuits and is ever so slowly hand-feeding the dog some biscuits and rubbing it’s head. Flaymin stands there speechless, amazed at the royal-like treatment of the mutt] Maid: Can I help you? Flaymin: Uh… yeah.I’m here to err…. Massage the dog. [The maids look at each other suspiciously] Maid: But the Chihuahua’s body-rub appointment isn’t ‘til 3pm. Flaymin: There has been a change of plans.So if you’d excuse me, my assistant and I would like to massage the dog. [The maid pulls the dog out of the bath and rubs it dry with a towel. She then hands the dog to Flaymin before all four maids leave the room and close the door behind them. Flaymin looks at the dog holding it up with outstretched arms. The dog swerves it’s head to the side and looks at Flaymin] Taco Bell Dog: Tichero Taco Bell! [Flaymin’s jaw drops and his eyes begin to pop wide open] Flaymin: HOLY SH*T!! Jenna come quick, the dog talked!!! Jenna: No way. [The dog looks at Jenna and once again begins to speak in a fast-paced Mexican accent] Taco Bell Dog: It iz tyme forrr my massaaage.You, woman with beeg boobs- rub my belly! [Jenna looks at the dog in astonishment] Jenna: He… it…….. it spoke! Flaymin: Quick, rub it’s belly while I shave it. [Jenna takes the dog and begins to rub its belly. Meanwhile, Flaymin pulls the razor out of his pocket, turns it on and quietly begins to mutter under his breath] Flaymin: So El Diablo thought he could get away with shaving my head huh? Well now I shave the hair off his beloved Taco Bell dog!!! [With the razor turned on, Flaymin turns back to Jenna and the dog] Flaymin: Hold him still Jenna, hold him still!!! Jenna: Uh Aymin, this isn’t going to work….. Flaymin: What do you mean? [Jenna holds up the dog close to Flaymin] Jenna: It’s a Chihuahua- it’s naturally hairless. You’ve pretty much got nothing to shave off. Flaymin: WHAT????NO…. IT CAN’T BE!!! Jenna: Sorry, but it’s true. [Flaymin turns around and hurls the razor out the window. He furiously kicks the dog’s portable bathtub over and begins to yell some more as the soapy bathtub water spills all over the floor] Jenna: Hey this dog is kinda cute.Why don’t you hold him. Taco Bell Dog: No, keeeep that eeediot away.Heez Mohawk iz scaring me. Flaymin: What’d you say? Come on bitch, you want some of this? Jenna: Aymin it’s only a dog. Flaymin: I don’t give two flying f*cks what it is.The mutt has attitude. Come on princess, lets dance! [The dog begins to look frightened and starts to yelp. Flaymin grabs the dog and heads over for the portable bathtub in an attempt to drown the dog. Jenna: Aymin, that won’t work either. Flaymin: Why not? Jenna: Because you kicked over the bathtub about a minute ago, remember? Flaymin: AAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Scene fades to black] With his devious plans all but over, what will Flaymin do next to torture El Diablo’s prized possession?What will the dog say next?Is it about to meet a grizzly death? Keeping in mind the outrageous events that have just transpired, it is safe to say that anything is possible!! Stay tuned to find out the fate of the Taco Bell Chihuahua!!!!!!
