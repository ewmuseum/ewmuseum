Equal War Chapter One- Part Three

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18-10-02 War’s meaning brings civilians to their knees. When two warriors collide, women shriek, children cry, and grown men fall to their knees. The destruction of two huge and mighty Titan’s can devastate the strongest of our race. But what can stop them… they must fight, fight until there is a winner. Until one titan falls to its death… when there is a loser… and a victor. Until then… destruction will reign on. Throughout time… these titans have fought… many have tried to make them stop, they all have failed… so still, the battle rage’s on… and now it will be decided to it’s peak. People sit in their housing, crouched in the corner, with their heads rested their knees. Tears drop from their eyes and hit the shaking ground beneath them as the war rages on. They say that a hero will save us. Well, where is our hero now? My losing streak continued. Why? I did not know, I had my mind on so much. Fate had abandoned me. Every time I stepped into the ring I did my fancy moves, I pleased the crowd to the extent of my category placing in PCW, yet I could not win. The last person I feel victim to was Semore Smoke. A man with talent, a man that, like myself, had won the NTICW World title. That match was for the PCW Extreme title. Having the title around my waist would have been a big honour. It would have accelerated my career. Yet, I was not the victor. I fell victim to an assault by Smoke, he pummelled me in the Japanese Death match. Then, he pinned me, for the one… two… three. Where had my heart gone…and where did my spirit lie? Jamie Celt’s eyes gleamed and his mouth stretched wide with a huge smile. His hair was down, not spiked up like its usual self. It was gelled neatly and tidily. He was not in his casual, casual attire. Instead he wore a light brown jacket and trousers a navy embroidered tie, and a plain white shirt. He stood in front of a large and rather modernized building. While some may recognize this building purely because of it’s placing, others would not. For it is indeed the same building that Jamie paid for to use as the Celtic Japanese main headquarters. The building had been completely remodelled. It was no black, with a huge ‘C’ logo on it. Jamie looked up at the huge complex. “Man, I’m a genius!” He tells himself. He then made his way up into the building entrance. A security barrier was being made. Though, due to it not being finished, Jamie just walked on through, and went up the long steeps and entered through the large circular doors. Inside it was not so glamorous. Though a lot of improvement had been made on the main foyer, it still had a lot of refurbishing needed. He made his way through the foyer. Oriental men in uniforms were working tirelessly around him to get the building finished before the deadline. Jamie continued to grin heavily. He felt ecstatic. He father would be so proud. He had made his first wise business move. He knew the Japanese Economy and links with the country would boost the Celtic gang dramatically. He felt extremely conceited of himself. Never before had he capitalized on an opportunity such as this. He had always been out on the streets, simply fighting for the Celtic gang. Never did he know, as the Son of the Leader of the Celtics could he make such an impact of the ’executive’ side of things. He continued to stroll down the foyer, looking around him at the indefatigable work going on. He continued to walk with a restrained posture. He then reached the end of the foyer. He looked over at the two newly installed elevators. They were gold plated and looked very ‘La De Da’. He decided to test his new investments. He pushed the small red button on the elevator to his right. He then heard the sound of a bell and almost immediately the elevator open it’s doors. Inside there was only one person. That person was Jamie’s best friend and cousin, Gerald Kelly. He was dressed casually as always with a blue denim jacket and black, baggy trousers. He looked at Jamie and a humorous smile came over his face. “What the hell are you wearing that shit for?” He asked rather amused. Jamie stared at him, seeming rather upset and irritated. “Shut up Gerald! I need to look good for the opening of this place.” Jamie responds irately. “Heh, you didn’t wear a suit to Johnny’s funeral man!” Gerald replies in a negative tone. “Well, that was a long time ago dude, I’ve changed, get used to it.” Jamie says in an agitated voice. Gerald continues to stare at the suit. “Yeah… whatever Jay.” He finishes. Jamie gets into the elevator and Gerald gets out, they do not speak as they pass each other. Jamie simply presses down the button with the number 3 on it and the doors close as Jamie sees Gerald walk out of the building. Jamie did not understand what was wrong with his friends lately. They always seemed upset with him because he was ‘growing up’ so to speak. They were annoyed that he was no longer a rough bearing street kid, who liked to pup a bullet in someone’s kneecap for fun. He knew he was right, though he did not understand why he felt so bad about it. Jamie heard a bell and the elevator door opened again. He stepped out into a large area with, on the right, a long corridor, and on the left doors with signs over them. Some of these signs said ‘Gym 5’, ‘Café’ and such things. Jamie lazily looked around the area. In front of him was an empty, unfinished reception area. Around Jamie many workers were doing their jobs restlessly. Jamie looked up the long dark corridor. He then slowly made his way up it. The further he went the darker it got. Eventually he came to the end. He made out a man, in a uniform like the rest of the workers. He kept his heads down low and indolently mopped the same spot over and over. Though it was quite obvious that he knew Jamie was there, he did not look up. He just kept mopping over and over. His face was covered a cap which was an optional part of the uniform. Though Jamie could barely see this man, he approached him to see what he was doing. “Hey, is that part of the floor really that dirty?” Jamie asks sarcastically. The man continued to mop, not paying any attention to Jamie whatsoever. Jamie began to feel nervous; he did not know what was going on. He slowly stepped closer to the man. “Hey, dude, what the hell are you doing.” He said apprehensively. He continued to ignore Jamie. Jamie then built up courage, and in turn that courage turned into frustration. He walked closer again until he was beside the man. “Hey! Answer me you frigging moron!” Jamie said, gets extremely agitated. Then, in one fast swoop, one that Jamie barely saw. The man swung his brush swiftly against Jamie’s legs. Jamie feel back. As he fell he saw the man looking down at him. His body then hit the floor with a great bang, head first. The last sight he saw was the dark ceiling, as he drifted off into a deep sleep. *** Jamie’s eyes twitched, he slowly regained consciousness. He looked up at a blinding light, which made him close his eyes again immediately. He covered his eyes with his arms. “Wh-what’s going on.” He mutters. As he begins to get more used to the light he removes his arms from his face. He opens his eyes once again. His head feels like it’s been stabbed several times. A figure comes in front of him. A young, oriental man, with a strong build. “Hello Jamie-san.” He says quietly. Jamie recognizes this man immediately. It’s the same one that had been following him over the last few days. Jamie tries to get up to interrogate this man but his body feels like it ways a tonne. “Who the hell are you!” Jamie says, trying to sounds vindictive, though can’t due to his weakened state. The man continues to stare down at Jamie. He wears a long, black t-shirt and a white karate pants. The oriental man smiles amusingly at Jamie. He is engrossed by the fact that Jamie talks to him in a challenging voice despite his condition. “I am your worst nightmare Jamie-san.” He replies threateningly. Jamie becomes confused. “What?” He asks perplexed. “I am your double Jamie-san. I can perform any move you can, I can think any strategic plan you can and I can fight as well as you can.” The man says. Jamie becomes confused and rather anxious. “What are you talking about?” Jamie continues to ask. The man simply stares at Jamie at shakes his head. “Look at you.” He says in revulsion. “You were once a proud warrior. But you have lost your roots. Now you are wearing fancy suits. You are a gangster! A wrestler! You dedicate no time to the Celtics and no time to PCW. You have a match of Friday, probably the biggest in your career. You go up against the entire PCW roster! Does that mean nothing to you? It’s for the second biggest title in the sport, yet you do not even prepare! Instead you stand around here trying to make ‘business investments’. Jamie Celt… you are not yet worthy to face me. You need to go back to your roots. Then return. In that time I will be waiting.” The Japanese man shots these words. He then shone the heavy light on Jamie’s face again. Jamie lay on the ground with his hand over his eyes. When he finally opened them again, the man was gone. He slowly got up and found he was in a small room with one window, which was blinded over. He then realized he was in one of the unfurnished rooms of his building. What he did not realize was who that man was. As vindictive as he seemed, he was right. Jamie had to prepare for his match, to hell with all his business deals. He had more important things to worry about. It was time to get back to his roots.
