Date: 05/23/03 Stained Glass. Grey clouds rolled like loose wheels in to the heavens, as the once pleasant sun bright day was rudely hampered by the surprising and unexpected darkness accompanied by the sudden fall of rain, and whistling of harsh winds from miles away. The urban-like asphalts were unusually empty from the everyday legion of vehicles and the production of car beeps and tunes.. the fiend presence of tandem sycamore trees shadowed the Bainbridge County, Georgia neighborhood. The views all coming from the facade window of building ‘136’ .. In it was the curious misogynist, and behind him was utter darkness.. so dark the front of his face became at bare sight.. the brummagem face features caked upon his face were the usual of a devilish grin, after the pursing of his lips, and then a repetition of a devilish grin. Looking at the curtilage of the building, a groaning murmur kicked in as Stone swung his head around, as light followed him through his path. He leaned to his side, and with an audible click came on three bulbs enough to enshroud the room that they were in, with light.. it was seemingly the 'master bedroom' as a king-sized bed slept in the middle of the room, as to the side came an escritoire littered with books and paper.. to the side of the bed, Stone strutted over in his newly equipped and vestured mackinaw coat. Perched uncomfortably on a single wooden-chair, hands fettered and tethered to her back by thin rope, and cloth stuck in to her mouth, and coiled around to the back of her head. She wriggled to no avail, as she stressed the crimson color of her cheeks and visage even further. The victim's name was as mentioned before.. Anna Marie Torres.. Curled, dark hair, precisely pricking her shoulders.. and her face, although stretched by the cloth, sleek and tan, featured with her soft and plump lips, and light brown eyes. Stone chuckled to his amusement, as her struggle to escape became more and more haphazard.. bending forward he drew his head forward, meanwhile digging in to his side coat pocket and slipping out his polished, metal blade. Anna Marie effortlessly bawled, known to be obstructed by the choking cloth. Bringing the ever SO sharp blade in to appearance and sight.. glowing in it's resplendent ivory shade, Anna Marie cried out so loud, it 'broke through' the cloth. He placed the blade to the middle of her neck, and she quieted.. he milled and circled around her with the blade, as her eyes became lachrymose at the acute stinging of the blade.. Stone languidly started advancing forward with the blade, already feeling the vocal cord... Paralyzed, Anna Marie could not further move nor' talk or the blade would sink in to the depths of her neck. Pleading she kicked her feet, luckily not having the blade driven in to her neck but Stone shook his head mercilessly and began sliding down with the blade towards her chest, then slowly back up towards her neck belligerently.. The misogynist then began to in a flit manner dab at her neck, rendering a cringing wince... Heedlessly, Stone stabbed at her neck and she squalled but the cloth blocked half of the magnitude. The miser sunk the blade bloodcurdlingly in to her neck, cleaving it and rushing out the incarnadine colored blood.. Stone raved inaudibly as he sickeningly plunged it deeper and deeper, finally ending his savage crime.. The landing of a head on the floor came to audio, as the body tied to the chair was headless.. and he leered balefully as he placed his blood imbrued blade in to his coat pocket, and floored the chair with his foot pounding on the head. It looked as if he was in woe but Stone weathered the paunch whirling attack.. he trotted on past the door and in to the darkness of the room but somehow finding his way out to the facade curtilage of the building.. then sneering nonchalantly as the rain pounded down from the wrinkled gray skies. Digging to the other side of his coat pocket, he pulled out a black mobile phone... hastily dialing a few digit numbers and holding it to his ear.. The recipient of the call answered.. 'Victim Number One is dead and off.. now to Victim Number Two.' Stone broached and ended as he went on forward in to the rain, in the process of pulling out a crumbled sheet of paper, reading..
