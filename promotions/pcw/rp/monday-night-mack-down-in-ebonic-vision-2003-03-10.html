MONDAY NIGHT MACK-DOWN!!! IN EBONIC-VISION~!

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MONDAY NIGHT MACK-DOWN!!! IN EBONIC-VISION~! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-10-2003 EBONIC’S CAPTIONED FOR THE EBONICALLY IMPAIRED…OR IF YOUR JUST VEEEERY PALE. :p ..::/::..Our story opens up in the most unusual of ways. Instead of the haven of our hero’s humble apartment or in the middle of a fight with the most evil of villains….we find ourselves immersed within the dark, wild reaches of a local club. The green and red lights shoot all about the packed dance floor, the strobe flickering on and off as “In Da Club” by 50 Cent is invading the ears of all those within. The bar is jam-packed with many seedy locales, the dance floor is crowded with beautiful women and handsome men, and the party doesn’t look to be stopping anytime soon. The camera pans slowly to the right until finally, it locks onto none other then Darrel, Rent-A-Hero’s “player” buddy who was introduced two stories ago. (For those with short-term long-term memories.) Darrel is standing with his arms folded at the entrance, nodding his head to the beat as he flashes a gorgeous grin, which only accentuates his handsome features. Popping the collar on his silk, see-through shirt, he rubs his hands and looks to the entrance before yelling..::/::.. Darrel:”THE PARTY LOOKS JUMPIN’ RENT…COME ON BOI!”..::/::..Although the music is pumping loud and with no end in sight, the heroic voice of one Rent-A-Hero can be heard from behind the black curtain entrance..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”DO I HAVE TO DO THIS?!”Darrel:”YOU WANNA BE A MACK DON’TCHA?!”..::/::..Darrel taps his foot, eager to party as he awaits our hero to join him by his side. After a moment of reluctance, our hero joins Darrel….but his dress sense has been COMPLETELY altered from what we know him for...::/::.. Darrel:”Now that’s what I’m talkin’ bout son, your looking fresh and clean boi.”..::/::..Our hero stands peeved, like a tomboy girl forced to wear a skirt for the first time. He stands in an oversized red FUBU shirt, shimmering blue FUBU jeans, and a pair of blue leather Tim’s.(Timberlands for the ebonically challenged.) He scratches his head, itching under his red hoodie and fixes the heavy platinum chain around his neck, with a iced out(really shiny for you non blingin’ folk out there:p) Superman “S” charm about it. The icing on this proverbial cake is the red and blue FUBU MASK upon his face….our hero having made the transformation to hip hop as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Darrel….seriously…COTTON...AND DENIM….this is against my religion master of mackhood friend.”Darrel:”Just quit complainin’ player and have a good time. Besides, you know how HARD it was for one of my shawdy’s(Young lady’s) You’ve got the dress, I’ve taught you the three main rules….now…you gotta handle ya biz. Go ahead and spit to one of these gurlies and get that algebra eh?”(Talk to a girl, get her liking you, and get the number buuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuddy :) ..::/::..Our hero stands confused, once again scratching his head as he asks..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Ummm…WHY would a girl want me to spit on her, then ask me to help her with a MATH PROBLEM? This mack stuff doesn’t sound to kosher if you ask me.”..::/::..Darrel shakes his head in disdain, raising his open palm up as if to slap our hero, who immediately covers his head out of fear from the previous slap instances. Darrel smiles, realizing he already knows the deal as he puts his hand down..::/::..Darrel:”Look…just watch…and learn eh?”..::/::..Darrel probes the club, his soft brown eyes almost like a radar as he scans for a target if you will. Finally, his eyes lock on a GORGEOUS Latina woman in a pair of tight stone-washed jeans and a red bandanna top, talking to what appears to be one of her girlfriends as she sips on her drink of choice. Darrel licks his lips, fixing the black leather Kangol hat sitting on his head as he rolls(walks up) to her with that same look a tiger would give his eventual prey. Our hero is watching closely as Darrel stands beside her, gently moving her ear to his lips as he whispers inaudibly in her ear. The woman obviously seems to like whatever it is he’s saying, licking her lips and giggling as she takes Darrel by the hand and drags him to the dance floor. Darrel flashes a wink to our hero before being sucked into the sea of dancers on the floor, Rent-A-Hero rubbing his chin before exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”GREAT SIRENS OF CYRANO….Darrel made that looks easy! Alright, it looks as if I shall take my hand at this. Here I go…first, let me use my Rent-A-Sense to find a suitable female.” ..::/::..Our hero looks about, careful in his eye movements as he takes the time to scope out any girl that falls into his viewing range. Finally, his eyes lock on a caramel skinned woman, her hair flipped at the very ends in a neat fashion and wearing a pair of pleather black pants which hug her soft hips. Our hero can’t help but also take notice of the “Wonder Woman” belly shirt she has on, our hero grinning to the camera as he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”I wonder if she digs masks?”..::/::..Our hero rubs his hands together as “Pretty Fly For A White Guy” hits the speakers in full force, fitting the occasion as our hero walks towards the lovely light-skinned princess, moving his arms in an almost crescent motion while walking as if he had a limp in one leg. Looking to the camera, our hero says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:”Oh don’t think I’m doing this on purpose….I just have a lot of change in my pocket.” –winks-..::/::..Our hero grows increasingly closer to the girl, she catches him out the corner of her eye, turning towards him with a finger to her cheek as our hero grows a little nervous, hiding it with his “gimp walk” if you will. Finally, he stands to the side of her, leaning against the bar with his elbows as he flashes a grin at her, the girl initially receptive to his grin as our hero moves towards her ear. Once in her ear our hero whispers in a moment of UTTER, INCREDIBLE…SMOOTH…IDIOCY!!!..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:”Something…something…something…something….shawdy.”(Young lady)The girl looks to our hero, giggling in a cocktail of amusement and delight as she asks..::/::.. Girl:”Umm….what was that supposed to mean?”Rent-A-Hero:”Well…I don’t really know, I just figured I’d say something a lot and end it with “shawdy”…seems to work with most guys. GREAT ELEKTRA’S OF EMBARASSMENT….I’m sorry if I wasted your time.” ..:/::..Our hero looks to back away in failure…but is stopped by the soft touch of her hand over his, surprising our hero as she looks towards him…her soft dragon-kin green eyes meeting his aqua blue pupils. She then says..::/::..Girl:”You know, it’s nice to meet a guy who’s on the real(honest) in his game.(approach to a girl) In your case…well…you ain’t got none, but it’s cute. Plus I think that mask is hot to death!(very nice looking, cool) Rent-A-Hero:”Well….I do be keeping it cri-zunk in da club, nah wha I mean shortbread, WIGGA WHAT!?!?!(WHAT IN THE HI-ZELL IS THAT?! I AIN’T TRANSLATING THAT!)..::/::..The girl merely giggles yet again at our hero’s poor use of ebonics, jerking his arm as she drags him over to the dance floor..::/::.. Girl:”COME ON BOI, SHOW ME WHAT YA WORKING WIT! LET’S DANCE!”Rent-A-Hero:”DANCE?!”..::/::..Our hero looks to the screen, mouthing the words “help” as the scene freezes in place, our hero risking embarrassing himself knowing he is not an ace on the dance floor. WILL OUR HERO BECOME A MASTER MACK?! WILL HE MAKE HIMSELF LOOK STUPID ON THE DANCE FLOOR?! WHAT DO THEY MEAN WHEN THEY SAY “NAW WHA I MEAN?!”FIND OUT SAME WIGGA-TIME…SAME RENT-A-WIGGER-CHANNEL….SHAAAAAAAAAAWD-AY!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
