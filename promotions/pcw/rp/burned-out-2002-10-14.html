Burned Out

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Burned Out Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 14/10/02 Our shot opens up on the snoozing and content face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon as he takes a nap in his hotel room. For once the Scientologist seems at complete ease... His Dianetics practices he went through an hour or so ago removing all the stress from his body... ...He looks completely at ease. *SMACK* That is until a little hand who’s wrist is clad in all varieties of beads and bracelets slaps it. “Like, wake up or something...” The hand with Pink and Silver varnished nails continues to lightly slap the stubbled face of Xavier, who is doing a good job of resisting awakening from his slumber. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!? Like, stop being asleep!” He lets out a groan as Ellie~! continues to whine and just turns his head so as to plant his face in the pillow to shield it from his wife’s hand. Eleanor : “Hey.... No ignoring Ellie~!” The little prep tries to pull the pillow from under his tired head but Kannon drapes an arm over it... Ellie~! pulling back with all her might but the 110 pound frame of hers can’t budge it. Eleanor : “Like.... Get.... UP!” She takes one last tug at it but her grip breaks sending the preppy little Commissioner rolling back off camera. “Ow!” Ignoring his wife flat on her ass, the Gold Patron Meritorious rolls over and pulls the covers tightly around him, encasing himself in snugness. Xavier : “I’m burned out, I need rest!” He takes a few seconds to fidget and get comfortable but finally finds his spot and lets out a sigh as he prepares to return once more to slumber. Xavier : “I deserve a rest after last month...” Walking back into shot comes Ellie~!... Brushing herself down, pulling a strap back up then pulling her denim skirt back down, looking at her man in annoyance. Cue whiniest of whiny voices. Eleanor : “But I want to go to the MALLLLLLLLLL!!!” She hammers her little hands on the side of the bed, the mattress not conducting what little disturbance it makes over to where Kannon is laying. Xavier : “You don’t need to go shopping, if you buy anymore here we’ll have to charter a Jet just to fly it all back...” His words are softened from travelling through the duvet and as soon as they finish, a yawn is heard followed by more fidgeting. Ellie~!: “I don't want to buy anything, it’s called “Rennaicance”... Like, looking at the Mall ahead of the match type thing... See, I’m, like, being a proper manager and everything... YAY ME!“ As she jumps on the spot and applauds herself, Kannon slides under the covers and crawls across to where she is jumping, reaching out both arms to grab her ass and stop her bouncing. Xavier : “It’s called “Reconnaicance”... And Ellie~!, my little Fuck Ferret... All I want is some REST, can I just have some rest?” His bright Green eyes look up to hers, pleading, before he kisses just below her skirt’s belt and slides back under the huge duvet. Eleanor : “Come onnnnnnnnnnnn!!!! There’s, like, finally something I can help you with, THE MALL BRAWL!!!“ Kannon is just heard mumbling under the covers. “Damn fucking burn-out.” His preppy wife continues her unusual display of enthusiasm. Eleanor : “You can, like, BRAWL and stuff..... While Ellie~!, like, SO knows MALLS!” Under the duvet, Xavier curls up into a foetal position and pulls all the pillows under with him.. Anything to barricade himself from Ellie~!’s nagging. Eleanor : “I’ll flash you if you get up!” She waits hopefully but still nothing, just Kannon yawning again. The little prep stomps her foot and crosses her arms in a huff. Eleanor : “You’re no fun when you’re burned out!” *RING RING* *RING RING* *RING RING* *RING RING* A loud groan resounds from under the covers as the phone rings and Kannon’s sleepy head finally emerges, eyes barely open as he looks to Ellie~! Xavier : “Can you get that.. All I want is just a LITTLE bit of peace!” Ellie~! reluctantly obliges, her body language and reaction REALLY putting over the reluctance. Eleanor : “Fine... Good job SOMEONE here isn’t burned out...” The little prep trundles over to the phone and picks it up. Eleanor : “Like..... SO.... Ellie~!” She pauses as a voice speaks through the handset to her, each word seemingly confusing the Commissioner a bit more until she puts the phone down and the weary voice of Kannon filters out through the bedding. Xavier : “Who was that?” The prep looks oddly a the phone before snapping out of it and looking back to her husband and his fort of hotel bedding. Eleanor : “Someone wanting to ask you about a feud or an angle or something, maybe an injury... I dunno...” She shrugs, disinterested. Eleanor : “.... But they bored me so I made it a wrong number and hung-up.” Underneath the covers, Xavier groans again. Eleanor : “So now I’ve answered the phone, can we go to the Mall?” Slowly, Xavier pulls the covers down to show is weary face, looking half asleep as it is. Xavier : “Why don’t you just join me, maybe help get a bit of burn-out out of my system?” He pats the bed next to him and winks to his wife as the phone rings again. *RING RING* *RING RING* *RING RING* Ellie~! immediately protests, crossing her little arms and trying to look as if she is standing firm... Kannon rubbing his eyes to see more clearly. Eleanor : “I’m, like, SO not getting it this time...” She looks up to the ceiling, humming to herself as the machine picks it up. “Please leave your message after the tone....” *BEEP!* “Er.. Hi.... Mr Kannon... So... Sup?” Xavier snarls and grabs the bedside lamp, ripping the cord from the wall and hurling it at the answering machine. *SMASH!* Ellie~! watches the flight path of the lamp and looks on, smiling as it smashes the machine into bits... Making her a little giggly. Eleanor : “Wow, someone is, like, SO stressed!” Kannon rolls onto his back, looking a little more awake. Xavier : “Now, to cope with this burn-out I can either go back to sleep..... Or, you can help me get a bit more of that frustration out of my system...” The Scientologist winks at her invitingly as she looks back at him, purring playfully. Eleanor : “Well.... We, like, SO can’t have you being stressed now can we...” The little prep winks back then leaps onto the bed, on top of the covers and her husband as the shot fades out to Black on Kannon pulling Ellie~! under the duvet. “You can’t get this at the Mall....” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
