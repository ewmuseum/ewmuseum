Performance of First Miracle

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-02 Performance of First Miracle The scene opens in a quaint suburban town where Heaven Rayne lives. Recently she has visited her parents back in her home town of Las Vegas, Nevada, and Heaven is glad to be back in her quiet, current home. It is Wednesday afternoon, and Heaven appears stretched out leisurely across her wooden hanging porch swing, eating a Popsicle and reading the newspaper. The early summer day is hot, so she sports a red halter top with a white stripe transversely on the front and white strings tying casually at the base of Heaven’s neckline, petite white cotton shorts, and no shoes. Heaven’s beautiful golden hair is pinned up in a bun to cool off her neck and shoulders. Heaven’s small, white cat, Cloe, lays beside her in the porch swing. Heaven strokes her lazily with her left hand, while balancing the newspaper on her knees and twisting the orange flavored Popsicle in her mouth with her right hand. Her coordinated red toenails wiggle as her eyes follow the print on the paper. Heaven finishes her Popsicle and lays her newspaper down so that she can go inside to throw away the stick. As she walks into her air-conditioned house the cold air breezes by her face, and she rejoices in the fact that she can spend time outside on such a nice day and then come inside to cool off. She decides that the cold air feels good, and she will go get Cloe and bring her in to take a nap. Moments later, she is pulling down the blue comforter of her bed and is about to climb between the sheets, when suddenly a wave of guilt passes over her. From out of nowhere she is reminded of the family across the street from her parents’ house in Vegas. Before she left Sin City, Heaven had promised herself she would eventually find the husband of the family, find out about his problems, and help fix them to stabilize the household. Being an angel, if necessary she can see places as though she is really there, and she can connect with the people, without physically going there. This is what happened before she left Vegas. She saw into the brown house. She can also make herself appear or disappear to or from anyplace, but most of the time that is unnecessary. Heaven realizes that even though she is enjoying her nice day of relaxation, she really aught to consider her angel duties. After all, it is her calling to help these people. Heaven is still new in responding to her calling, and is unsure of exactly how to get to these people. She just stands there, closes her eyes, her face scrunched up in concentration, and just thinks, “I need to help the family in the brown house….Lord I don’t know how I am supposed to do this, but you said in time, it would come to me. I need it now….” Within seconds the scene changes, showing you only what Heaven sees in her mind. Heaven is still physically in her house, but a door has opened in her mind, showing her what she needs to see. She can help these people from where she is at. The old, brown house comes into focus once again, this time starting with the living room. A rugged man appears on the junky, outdated, orange sofa. As the view becomes nearer, you can see that the man looks to be in his late twenties-early thirties. He is the type of person that appears ugly because of the way they present themselves, which in reality if the person took a shower and put on fresh clothes, they would be very attractive. Although a bit unshaven, his tanned face is pleasant, he has dark blonde hair that may be a lighter shade if it is washed, and sad dull blue eyes that seem to be aged far beyond their years, perhaps due to hardship or sorrow. He is wearing faded Wrangler blue jeans, full of holes and corroded with dark stains, and an old begrimed black t-shirt displaying a popular professional wrestler from the early eighties. The man appears to have fallen asleep, but in his right hand is a lit cigarette. The cigarette falls to the floor which is already in horrible condition because of cigarette burns and stains. The scene moves and towards the front of the room is the little five year old boy Heaven saw the last time her mind and eyes visited this house. There he is, skinny and pale, with dark purple lines still visible under his eyes, and he is still wearing the same dirty shirt that is too small for him, and no pants, only underwear. The little boy has been again entertained only by the cartoons, and is being quiet not to disturb his daddy. The little boy turns around and notices the cigarette that has fallen carelessly onto the floor, and he sees the fire which it has begun to create. Being very smart for his age, especially for a child who is endlessly left unattended to, the boy hops up from the floor, and hurries around the corner, into the kitchen. He stands on a stool, turning the faucet on full blast, filling a Mason jar up quickly and runs to pour the water over the burning cigarette. Relief immediately washes over the small boy’s face once he has put out the fire and he goes back to watching television. The lazy father doesn’t even wake up from his little cat nap, instead he continues to snore away as his child is left unattended, and his wife is in the bedroom hiding more black eyes and another bloody nose. The bedroom is shown, and once again Heaven sees the poor lady sobbing into a stained pillow. Picking up on her supernatural instinct, Heaven knows just what this family’s problems are, without even looking into it any further. The husband is a low life loser, who abuses the wife and is too lazy to work. He is either out drinking, running the streets, and messing around on his wife, or at home being abusive and sleeping. Heaven decides to scare this pathetic excuse of a man. She wants to show him what track he is on, and where he is headed if he doesn’t straighten him up. She implants in his mind a series of nightmares, which will make him think seriously about changing his tune. Again the scene changes, this time you see only what the man sees in his dreams, and you hear his voice, narrating the nightmares, as if he’s telling himself a story. *It was Wednesday morning, and I had been out all night with Stephanie again. We met at Robert’s Bar at 11:00 p.m. like always, and we had our usual drinks. We went back to Stephanie’s and I spent the night; details aside. Anyway, like I said, it was Wednesday morning, and I came home. I knew Becky would be pissed at me for staying out all night, and I knew that she would probably be able to tell, like always, that I had slept with another woman. Stephanie wore a different kind of perfume than Becky, and somehow I never noticed Stephanie’s lipstick stains until after I was home and Becky pointed them out to me. So on this particular morning I took four blue Zanies to calm me down, make me not care about anything, and make it so it wouldn’t hurt me when I saw tears of pain well up in Becky’s eyes again. I came home at 7 a.m. Joshua was still asleep in our bed, but Becky was already in the living room waiting for me to come home. As soon as I came in the door, she began her bitching, but I didn’t want to hear it and yelled at her to shut the hell up. She told me to quiet down because she didn’t want to wake up Josh, and that pissed me off even more, because I didn’t feel like quieting down. She was a bitch, and I was tired of her. She started questioning me on my whereabouts and accused me of having another affair. When I decided that she had said enough, I punched the wall, and when that didn’t serve its purpose as a warning, and she kept talking, it was her turn. I ended up blacking both of her eyes and making her nose bleed. After that I couldn’t stop…I was on a roll. I just kept beating and pounding her face into the ground. All of the sudden I heard, “Mommy?” It was Josh. He was awake and wanted Becky. I expected Becky to try to push me away long enough to get up and answer our son, but to my surprise, Becky didn’t fight to push me away, in fact she didn’t move at all. I got to noticing, and oh my God! She was turning cold and blue! “Becky! Becky, baby, wake up, I’m sorry! I didn’t mean to, baby, I …..Please!” “Mommy?” Oh no, Joshua was still calling for her! What was I going to tell him? He was going to hate me forever. “Becky!!!” My cry went on and on, fading out into a haunting mode. Suddenly all went dark. I couldn’t see anything and all I could hear was my own cry for Becky………* The man flits out of one nightmare and into another, still the focus of the scene is in his dreams, and you hear him narrating this one as well. *I had just drunk a whole six pack, and I reached into the pocket of my jeans for my pack of Marlboro’s. I lit one and sat back feeling relaxed. Joshua was watching cartoons in the same room as me, and I let my eyes follow the animated characters on the screen, not really wasn’t paying any attention. That damn Becky probably hadn’t fed Josh all day. I hadn’t seen her come out of the bedroom since I set her straight that morning in the bedroom. She is such a pussy, I hit her once or twice and she acts like I’ve beaten the shit out of her, anyway, she needs to feed our kid. She bitches because I lost my job, and she says we don’t have any food to fix, but I know she’s got money stored in that jar under our bed. She’s not a good enough mom to use that money to feed our son; she’s saving that money to leave me. She knows if she ever tried to leave, I’d kill her, so I don’t know why she insists on saving that money. She won’t leave me anyway, because she loves me and I love her. I leaned back on the couch and closed my eyes, and before I knew it, I had fallen asleep. I don’t know how long I had been asleep but when I woke up I was standing outside our house in a white robe. I couldn’t figure out what the hell I was doing, or why I was wearing that, when I realized the house was covered in black soot and parts had burnt to the ground. I forgot about the mysterious way I ended up here and my clothes for a second, as I became interested in what had happened to our house. I walked up to it, and everything was tarnished. I stepped over big black objects and into what was left of my home. The air had a heavy stench, unlike anything I had ever experienced, but I imagined it was the smell of burnt skin. The thought horrified me, and I searched frantically for Joshua and Becky, but they were nowhere to be found. Finally I found them, and I wished I didn’t know then what I didn’t know before that moment….my wife and son were dead. They were lying together on the living room floor right beneath the window, as if they were close to escaping the fire, but perished before they had a chance. They were black, and their skin was gone. It was the most horrible, frightening moment of my life. I knelt down over their corpses, and surrendered myself, crying out to God, whom I wasn’t even sure existed. “Oh God! What have I done to deserve this! Please… take my life; I would rather not live without my family!” Then suddenly it hit me, and I looked down at my white robe. “Dear God! I am dead!” The moments before my death flashed before my eyes and I recalled myself asleep with a cigarette in my hand. I didn’t remember anything else. I must have burnt the house down and killed myself and my family. A whole life of regrets was recalled to me in that instant. I cheated on my beautiful, loving wife dozens of times, I talked down to her, and beat her to a pulp numerous times. I messed around and lost my construction job because I stayed out all night drinking and slept all day, not showing up to work. Even though I had promised my wife a gorgeous home when she was pregnant with Josh, and we decided to get married, five years later, we still lived in a dump because I couldn’t keep a job. “Oh God! If only I could somehow live my life over, I would do it so much different. I really did love Becky so much….” * At that Point the scene switches away from the dream and goes back to normal as Heaven decides the man should wake up. Heaven watches as he awakes much to his surprise, and thanks God over and over that he and his family are alive and that he has a chance to start over. The two horrible dreams he had hit home hard! He finally faces up to reality before it is too late. Heaven sees the man run into his bedroom to comfort his wife. He wipes away her tears, kisses her, tells her he’s sorry, and tells her that everything is going to be alright. In that moment Heaven is satisfied that the husband has learned his lesson and will not go back to his old ways. The family is going to be ok after all, and Heaven feels complete, having surrendered to her calling and performed her first miracle. END
