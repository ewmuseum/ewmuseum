The beast with in - It's power shown

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 Introduction: 'What fires burn within my heart and force me to contend with the perils that await me at this tragic journey's end? I have walked the roads that lead to Hell, I have challenged all but fate. I have fought and bled and carried on just to reach this final gate. And now the task before me looms, this dire deed undone; I shall make my stand against the three until the battle's won. What fear or wound could ever still this last defiant cry, As I stand against the shadow 'neath the endless burning sky?' -C. Vincent Metzen (Strong lashes of lighting rippled through the sky, showing it's power like a pissed off bull. The dark hand of night laid across all that could seen by the eye and the dark clouds offered no signs of light as well. Every time a whip of lighting would go off a large mound could be seen, it was that of a mountain. The boulders of the mountain were dark grey, no life appeared to be growing on the mountain nor living on it until a second flash went off and a shadow could be seen climbing. Closer we came to see who would climb in weather like this only to reveal Jay Sylo. He wore his traditional white baggy pants with the blue fire around the legs and a long sleeve shirt. On his feet he wore a pair of hiking boots which were laced up over his ankles.) 'Almost there,' He said to himself. (He dug the metal spikes of the boots into the side of the mountain while reaching up and grabbing a near by rock so that he could pull himself up some more. The lighting became more intense, almost like it was working against him but this didn't stop Jay. He continued to climb pulling himself closer and closer to the top, he knew he was close because the air became thinner and thinner as he went higher and higher. He took a second to look down....by now he was a good two stories in the air. This didn't phase him as he continued to climb. His footing slipped out from under him but quickly he caught it back pulling himself up once more.) 'You can do this,' Jay told himself. (With one last tug he pulled himself up to the side of the cliff. He pushed up to his feet and looked down. The ground seemed to fall away as the lighting continued to crackle now followed by loud pops of thunder. Breathing heavily, Jay watched the night sky as the lighting popped over and over. He closed his eyes, just listening for once but he felt something with-in....something was growing and he had an idea of what.) 'It's not always great at the top,' Jay muttered out. 'And now I fear the worst.....this beast that grows with-in could possibly end the career of a fellow wrestler if not used properly and I fear I cannot control it,' Jay finished letting out a sigh. (The thunder continued to roll but Jay still stood with his eyes closed. A look of pain moved across his face as his fist clenched together. Jay let out a loud yell which echoed through-out the land making birds scatter from their home and flee to higher ground.) 'WHY AM I CURSED! THIS BEAST GROWS AND I KNOW NOT HOW TO CONTROL IT!' He paused a moment. 'I can't loose to this....I must fight Afwa Kilmanjaro and he has done nothing to me...he is an innocent, someone I do not wish to hurt but for his own good he must not push me for I fear it could trigger something with-in,' (He shifted his thoughts, back to only one year ago.) --Flashback-- (This place was a ruff neighborhood, not the kind of place you would want to raise your children. Night hadn't fallen yet but it was close, you could smell it.) 'Why am I always drug to these parts?' A young man whispered out. (He was heading to the night club he worked at. He worked as a bouncer and even though was a big man, knew he couldn't dodge a bullet if he was shot at. So usually he just minded his own business and went on. He had on his work outfit, a skin tight black shirt and a pair of baggy leather pants. His hair was spiky and blue, his goatee was the same to match. Finally he arrived at a large building with neon lights and music pumping from with-in. A long line of men and women stood to the side of the building as two rusty, green looking doors opened and closed at the same time. A large man stood at the door with his arms crossed checking I.D's as people came in. The young man walked over to the other bouncer and tapped him on the shoulder. The large man dressed in the same out-fit turned and let out a sigh of relief.) 'Man thank god your here,' The man informed the younger man. 'What's up Frank?' The young man asked. 'Sylo you wouldn't believe it if I told you. I've had four guys try to pick a fight with me already, I'm ready to go home and go to bed,' Frank replied. 'Well Frank go on I got this,' Sylo replied. 'Thanks man I owe you one,' Frank said as he dashed in the building to inform the owner. (Some timed passed as normal, Sylo would check the I.D's and turn away those who wasn't 18. It ticked a few people off but that's just one of the things that came with the job. About thirty minutes later a loud 'BOOM!' went off. Sylo leaped through the old rusted green doors to see what was going on. On the far end a man with a gun was shooting it into the air as people scattered. A look of fear moved over Sylo's face but he knew something had to be done. He looked and saw that his friend, Frank, had been shot and suddenly the fear changed to rage. Everything seemed to move in slow motion as Sylo charged at the man who didn't even notice. Sylo placed a foot on the rail and leaped out yelling, the beast had been unleashed once more. Sprawling the man to the floor and knocking the gun out of his hand, Sylo then began to pound on him. He picked the guy up and threw him over the bar, making him crash hard into a stand of empty boo's. His eyes glazed over, they were the haunting ice blue all over again as he snarled and leaped over the bar like an animal and began to pound on the man. The club owner had to pull Sylo off as the man laid loosing loads of blood from his mouth and nose.) 'DIE!' Sylo yelled as he went to punch the guy once more. (The club owner grabbed Sylo's arm, it took everything he had to hold him back. The police finally came and arrested the armed gunman...this was the last night Sylo ever worked as a bouncer.) --End Flashback-- (Sylo stared out into the wild. His eyes had a glassy look to them, and the cold blue look faded in slowly.) 'No longer must I hold back my true power,' He boomed out. (He kneeled down clenching some dirt in his hand and stood back up letting it sift it's way back down. His eyes became the icy blue once more as he snarled a small bit.) 'Kilmanjaro, I will finish you fast....do not tempt me or you will be destroyed,' Sylo snarled out. (The beast had shown it's head once more. Sylo clinched his fist and let out another loud, booming yell.) 'I WILL NOT LET THIS RULE ME!' He yelled out into the night sky as the thunder tried to cover up his yells. (slowly his eyes became normal.) 'Afwa, beware and may god have mercy on your soul....' (Just like that Sylo disappeared into a blanket of darkness. He was gone but his memory was left, imbedded inside the mind, left for the world to hear. He was ready for Tuesday and Tuesday was ready for him...but what would happen between times? Only time will tell and for now times up.)
