Unity Trailer for 'The End'

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/15/02 *Cue Music: Drum beat with brass accompaniment. Stock music last used in wrestling as Dean Malenko's WCW entrance theme.* Voice:'Some are fierce.' *A torso shot of Vulcan Flare as mercenary-for-hire Lancaster, dressed in a blue combat suit with rifle rounds strapped in two crossing rows down his chest. He has his hair dyed blue, and a large automatic rifle that looks more like a full machine gun, firing of to the rear left of the camera in a sweeping motion at something with his mouth open indicating screaming.* Voice:“Some are timid.” *A shot on a beach facing the water. It is a bright, sunny, cloudless day. People in the proper swim attire run around, sunbathe, play games, fly kites, and barbeque. The camera is on Jaden’s level, the female PCW star lying on her stomach on a red beach towel, reading a Danielle Steele novel. A slight gust of wind blows her black hair back over her yellow bikini straps. Jaden looks up a little above the camera to the left and smiles at someone.* Voice:“Some are mysterious.” *A white sky in a snowy forest. There are many trees, all of which are leafless but free of snow. The End and Redeemer (played by celebrity actor Michael Jai White) are up in the tree, staring down at a group of five cyborgs. The End hangs upside down on a thick tree branch which supports his weight. Redeemer is perched atop the branch, ready to pounce.* Voice:“Some are one-dimensional.” *A greasy spoon diner with a white suede seating theme shows PWA cadet Charlotte sitting behind the counter wearing a white apron and pink shirt under it that shows enough of her cleavage to give the NTICW’s Scott Taylor (wearing a khaki bubble vest, jeans, and blue flannel) a good reason to gawk. The counter is impeccably clean.* Scott Taylor:“Damn, you’re looking hot. Wanna have sex?” *Charlotte slaps Scott Taylor hard in the face. He recoils and holds his cheek where he was slapped, but turns right back to Charlotte.* Scott Taylor:“Dear God, that turned me on even more.” *The very next scene is Clyde, dressed up in a dirty apron and hat as the diner’s chef, throwing Scott Taylor out of the diner by his vest collar and pants. Clyde claps his hands off and moves back inside the diner. Charlotte sticks her head out of the door.* Charlotte:“Jackass!” Voice:“And then... there’s the monkey.” *Jordan X Shabazz is standing in a white-walled corner, eating an apple like Mr. Peepers from SNL, dressed in a rented monkey suit that looks exactly like the ones that the Bloodhound Gang wore in their most famous video “We Ain’t Nothin’ But Mammals.” The chorus of that song plays while Jordan is on camera.* *Cue Music: “Feel So Numb” Rob Zombie* Voice:“They will all have to put their differences aside if they want to survive.” *A huge battle scene, the epicenter of combat where two armies, human (in combat fatigues) and cyborg, clash. The battle is not up-close, but rather a wide view of the field, with extras playing the soldiers and cyborgs that fight with rifles and hand-to-hand.* Voice:“The cyborgs are bigger.” *Ivan Stanislav holds his arms up to the sky, yelling at the top of his lungs. He towers over the much smaller El Diablo, whom he is chasing with an evil look in his eyes through an underground slum area that almost seems like a sewer. As El Diablo ducks through a narrow alleyway of obstacles, Stanislav barrels through destroying anything and everything in his path with his bulging arms.* Voice:“The cyborgs are stronger.” *Hessian and Ivan Stanislav hold up a massive boulder in a collapsing mineral ore. The boulder must be 15 feet across of solid dense rock and mineral. Close-ups of Hessian and Stanislav’s face are shown. They strain themselves to the limit and together they hurl the boulder away.* Voice:“But what humans lack in size and strength...” *El Diablo and Scott Taylor crawl hastily through a cramped pipe. As they go along, behind them Ivan Stanislav’s fists smash through the pipe, getting closer and closer to them.* Voice:“... They make up for in intelligence...” *Timo and RAH work in a protected lab with coats on (RAH wears his old PCW mask), building devices metallic in base and cylindrical in shape, with timers in the middle of them.* Voice:“... Tenacity...” *A beaten and bloodied Duzza, lying alone in an alleway next to a large dumpster, cyborgs walking aaway from him, slowly rises to his feet leaning against the dumpster for support.* Voice:“... And a monkey.” *Cue Music Teaser: “We Ain’t Nothin’ But Mammals” *Two cyborgs are hit in the head with pineapples. Looking up to a rooftop behind them, the camera zooms in on Jordan X Shabazz doing a hopping monkey dance while eeking and squeaking at them.* *Cue Music: “Feel So Numb” Rob Zombie* Voice:“This summer, join the fight to save the human race.” *Three anti-grav cars speed away from a tall glass-walled building.* Voice:“Charlotte.” *A shot of Charlotte on a gray brick wall, turning around and gasping from alarm; her long blonde hair flicks around from the one side of her hair to the other.* Voice:“Scott Taylor.” *Scott Taylor is scared by the same thing that scared Charlotte, both on camera. Charlotte is startled, but stands her ground. Scott Taylor turns and runs face-first into the wall.* Voice:“Jesse Ramey.” *Carrying the American flag in his left hand and an assault rifle in his right, Ramey leads the human charge into battle against the cyborgs.* Voice:“El Diablo.” *Sitting by a fire in a trash drum, El Diablo laughs.* Voice:“Jordan X Shabazz.” *Cut Music.* *A body close-up of Jordan X Shabazz, flexing while standing beside Jesse Ramey and Duzza in a war room.* Shabazz:“I can take them!” *Close-up face shot of Duzza.* Duzza:“You sure? They got lotsa guns.” *Close-up rear body shot of Shabazz, walking away with a full brown leather suitcase packed.* Shabazz:“I think I’ll sit this one out.” *Cue Music: Dramatic Build-up to Fade Out* Voice:“The End.” *With two swords, The End dives off a 40-foot ledge into a crowd of hostile cyborgs.* Voice:“And many more wrestling stars in... THE END.” *The screen fades to black, then the words “The End” move to the center of the screen from the foreground.* Voice:“An El Diablo Film.”
