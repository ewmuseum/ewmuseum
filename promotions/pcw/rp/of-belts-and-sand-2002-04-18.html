Of Belts and Sand

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 18 We assume that Snow was thrown out of the church before any major damage was done. Now, he must focus on next Friday night on Fusion where he will face a longtime rival of his - Big Alan Steele. The two have battled on two occasions previously and neither time has there been a clear cut winner. Now, however, the stakes are much higher. Next Sunday, Big Alan Steele will battle Jason Snow for the greatest prize in all of wrestling - the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. On the New Breed front, everything looks well - other than the fact that we still have absolutely no idea why Chris Chase handed the PCW Championship over to the Killin’ Villain. What we do know, however, is that both Dustin Downey and Corey Williams are major factors. New Breed looks like one of the most dominant forces in PCW at the moment... Now, as Snow lies comfortably on Daytona beach - sunglasses covering his cold blue eyes, and the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World lying sloppily on his chest. He lies on what appears to be an actual leather beach towel, something that we’ve seen only once before - the last time that Snow was on a beach. Summer is approaching, and the weather is fine on this Monday afternoon. There’s a cool breeze blowing to take at least some of the burning sensation from the hot Florida sun. In the background, we can hear laughing, chatting, and even a few splashes, although the water is quite some distance away... The PCW Champion looks in fine form. His long blond hair is still left untied, as it usually is lately, and is about shoulder length - splattered against the white leather beach towel. Physically, Snow has never looked better - with that in mind, it’s hard to remember a time when Snow looked worse. He’s consistently in top form - never changing. We can’t help but notice the scowl on his face... Snow: “Damn Malloy... he said this would be relaxing! I feel about as relaxed as a frying hotdog, damn it! Last time I ever trust that peasant!” Snow pulls off his sunglasses and quickly shields his blue eyes with his brawny right arm... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS! The sun down here could blind an eagle! What in the hell do all those peasants see in this hell hole, anyway!?” At that very moment, Snow peeks out from behind his arm to see an absolutely gorgeous woman approaching. She’s tall, busty, blond, and wearing next to nothing. Snow can’t help but let his jaw drop. She doesn’t seem to notice, however. When she comes to Snow, she stops, kneels down beside him, and speaks... Woman: “Well hi there, handsome. I couldn’t help but notice that beautiful belt on your chest.” Snow: “Yes, and I can’t help but notice the ten pounds of silicon in yours!” The woman seems startled by Snow’s remark for a moment, but then grins - Obviously, she’s been taken in by Snow’s youthful good looks. His championship is candy for her too. She’s already drawn in too much for insults to repel... Woman: “So what’s a handsome boy like you doing all alone at the beach?” Snow: “Why, basking in the Greatness of my own presence... what else?” The woman is somewhat surprised by the Killin’ Villain’s answer, but she simply takes it as a joke - or hopes that he’s joking. We, the fans, know that he’s not joking, however. Snow tilts the sunglasses back down over his eyes and resumes his former position... Woman: “Ah... well, mind if I bask with you?” Snow’s mouth tightens as he tries to find the meaning in her words. Quickly, he sits straight up and pulls his sunglasses off... Snow: “If I didn’t know better wench, I would say that you were trying to get into my leather pants!” Woman: “Observant, aren’t you?” Snow scowls... Snow: “Damn it, wench! I’ll have you know that I’m a happily married man!” The woman shrugs... Woman: “Well... I don’t see your wife...” A devilish smile appears on her lips as she lightly touches Snow’s right leg with her index finger. As if in protection mode, Snow clutches the PCW Championship title close to his chest and glares at the woman through wide eyes... Snow: “Are you inferring that I - the Killin’ Villain - would engage in adultery with you!? A fowl peasant wench, no less!?” The woman doesn’t know how to respond to this... no one ever does. She stands up, realizing that she has no chance with the Killin’ Villain. Her pride is hurt... Woman: “Well, you didn’t have to be mean about it, you know!” Snow: “I’ll be as mean as I please! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! And I’ll have you know another thing, wench! You had best take your eyes off me, because the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!” Her eyes grow wide in the face of Snow’s disrespect... Woman: “You know, I really don’t like guys like you! You think that just because you have gold, you can treat people however you want!” She points to the championship title that Snow is still clutching to his chest. The tall blond woman has her hands on her hips now, and is glaring down at the Killin’ Villain... Snow: “No, wench! I treat people how I like because I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” Snow slams the PCW Championship down in the sand beside him... Snow: “And furthermore, this championship - although it does say that I am the Greatest and most envied wrestler alive today - is not the root of my Greatess and Superiority! Then again...” Snow sneers... Snow: “You would know nothing of Superiority.” The woman is enraged, which isn’t an uncommon reaction when a person first comes in contact with Snow’s inflated ego. Snow is arguably (though not very arguably) the most egotistical man in the history of PCW. Everything he says and does is completely for his own self-benefit. Still, no one can deny his talent. The beautiful blond woman, however, attempts to do just that... Woman: “You’re the biggest as(CENSORED)le I’ve ever met! You know what!? I can’t WAIT until Big Alan Steele hands you your ass at Fusion! He’ll make a much better champion than you!” She can tell that her verbal blow has landed directly on the genitals of the Killin’ Villain. When she sees the gasp on Snow’s face, she does nothing but smirk. Of course, this reaction enrages the two-time PCW Champion. Quickly, Snow leaps to his feet to retaliate... Snow: “Big Alan Steele!? BIG ALAN STEELE!? Damn it, wench! Have you no sense!? Big Alan Steele wouldn’t know what Greatness was like if it crawled up his toga!” She knows that she’s won - Snow has become extremely defensive. His words are faster than usual... Snow: “And furthermore, wench, I’ll have you know that your beloved...” Snow scowls as he says the name... Snow: “...Big Alan Steele... will NEVER - and I know because I’m Superior - win the PCW Championship! Never! Never! I refuse to let someone that inferior degrade the federation that I and my New Breed BUILT!” Although there’s a deranged quality to his voice, we can’t help but realize that the Killin’ Villain’s words are far more than his usual empty threats... what could he be planning this time? Obviously, New Breed can’t be trusted... The blond woman rolls her eyes, tired of this game, and starts walking away - probably looking for the next pretty face... Snow yells after her... Snow: “Damn it, wench! Didn’t anyone ever tell you to listen when a Superior Individual is speaking! I said that Big Alan Steele will NEVER become c -... Are you listening to me wench! Hello!? I’M SPEWING GREATNESS HERE!” The woman doesn’t pay any attention although she can no doubt hear the Killin’ Villain - everyone within a hundred yards can hear him. Angrily, he picks up his championship belt, without dusting it off, and holds it by the strap as the scene fades to black...
