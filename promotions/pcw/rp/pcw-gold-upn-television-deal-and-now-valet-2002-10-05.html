PCW Gold, UPN Television Deal and now Valet?

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 - 10 - 02 'I'M A TAG TEAM CHAMPION - I'M A TAG TEAM CHAMPION - I'M A TAG-....' sang the jovial Jester, yet to be unveiled before the camera when suddenly the Wrestle Tattler behemoth Nerezza's voice filtered over. 'NO GAZ! WE'RE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS....' The conniving collective who have just came off the 'P.W.A Tonight' set are in high spirits. Early reports from those upstairs have indicated the debut show involving OSW's Amy Campbell had been a ratings spectacle and was the first chink in the CNN armour, as both companies slug it out for ratings supremacy. 'WE'RE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS - WE'RE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS - WE'RE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!! roared the dynamic duo in unison, from full-time wrestlers to television presenters, does a music contract loom in the careers of Pranks With Attitude? The scene finally opened within the confines of the Asylum members private locker-room, furnished with a table and it's draping cloth coverings it had been laid with complementary champagne and a host of snacks. Slouched in a reclined chair was the Emerald Menace himself, Gaz 'The Jester' Green, and his wardrobe still that he had adorned to the masses only minutes earlier. Sitting opposite in a similar manner was Nerezza, casually dressed, long gone is the crimson funeral suit to be replaced by his black Gothic t-shirt and flowing trench coat. The trickster team reminisces over their debut show and smile, basking in their newfound glory, there appeared little else to do but celebrate. Gaz Green firmly grasped at an ominous looking sheet of paper, catching Nerezza by surprise. 'What's that?' hissed the fledgling vampire. 'This my friend is our 'To Do' list and I'm just ticking those appropriate...' retorted the Lord Of Laughter his eyes fixated on the small print of the document. 'Get ready-... Debut without a hitch-... and celebrate the demise of CNN!' continued the PCW comic. 'CHECK, CHECK-.... AND CHECK' bellowed the living Nightmare. 'Since there's nothing else left for us to do today I say we head for home?!?' concluded Nerezza, his cold stare locked on his comrade, patiently waiting an answer. 'We haven't done everything squire!' informed the Jester, his gaping smile broadening further to showcase his pearl white teeth. 'What-....' replied the Angel Of Fear, his speech interrupted upon by the untimely Gaz Green. 'Err... Arr... I have deduced that Pranks With Attitude, even though we are Champions, deserving Champions at that, are the underdogs for Red October!' injected the former NTICW comedian, Nerezza facially expressing confusion towards the latter comments of his colleague. 'Think about it. Every loss we have sustained during our short stint with PCW has been tarnished in controversy, some interfering nobody gets involved and we end up being screwed worse than Ellie....' added the Asylum member as Nerezza intruded upon the conversation with a comment of his own. 'Yeah that girl has had her brains 'FUC-.... OUT!' explains why Kannon keeps her from arms way as a Commissioner because one more head shot, that's banging her head Gaz and not giving head, anyway could leave her Steven Hawking - ised' The room became bathed in laughter as both men broke into hysterics, there were tears forming from the eyes of Gaz Green and an unveiling of Nerezza's rigid teeth, with his two canines on display. Suddenly a thunderous thud descended upon the room from the direction of the door, again another as though somebody were hitting the frame with a clenched fist at force, momentarily cutting the proceedings short. Nerezza stood dumbfounded whilst his smaller co-worker took the initiative to answer.... 'WELL AREN'T YOU GONNA LET ME IN SUCKER!!!' cried a distinctive voice, followed by the distinguished owner, the A' Teams very own B A Baracus, better known as Mr. T. Strutting into the party atmosphere was Mr. T, adorned in cobias amounts of jewellery, draping from his broad neck and his fingers laid siege to rows of rings as though Fort Knox personified. Nothing appeared out of place, his hair still a Mohican and his cargo bottoms still present and correct, accompanied only by a tight fitting chain-mail vest. 'Nerezza this is Mr. T... He's here about the vacant valet opening!' said Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'WHAT VALET OPENING?!?' screamed the Spawn Of Evil, obviously unaware of the events around him. Gaz tilted his head and muttered into the active ear of Nerezza who listens with intrigue at the proposition. 'It's a one night only thing - We'll hire a valet from those wishing to apply and even the sides at Red October, so if Playaz Inc. want Malcolm X in support or Redemption Song want to bring the Washington Red-Skins we'll thwart their efforts by having our very own muscle at ringside!!!' Nerezza reluctantly agreed by nodding his head and the interview was underway. 'HURRY UP FOOL!' yelled the infamous actor. 'Sorry sir, err-... well what qualifications do you have in relation to this job?' questioned the Prince Of Pranks. 'WHAT KINDER DUMB ASS QUESTION'S THAT, HUH?!?' responded the African American with a serious tone, almost snarling. 'Is it true you can turn a fountain pen into a Swiss army knife or baby's push chair into an armoured car?!?' said the inquisitive Jester subtly. 'YOU'SE WANT A KNUCKLE-SANDWICH SUCKER!!!' groaned Mr. T, standing to attention, pacing back and forth for no apparent reason. 'Gaz let me try.... B A Baracus sir, do you have any fears at all?' asked Nerezza, brandishing a white hood and placing it over his head, resembling a member of the defunct Klu Klux Klan. As the former A' Team actor turned he saw the white ghostly feature adorned to the face of Nerezza and screamed in terror before fleeing the scene, crashing through the door like a locomotive. The devious duet looked on at the shenanigans with star struck expressions, trying to fathom the events that have transpired but as they dwell on minutes past the door sounds again, this time a pleasant tap rather than the thunderous crash of wood. 'ENTER STRANGER - Man, I've always wanted to say that!' gloated the King Of Komedy Gaz Green. Again the door sounds, a subtle tap against the wooden structure and again no signs of anyone entering. This episode continues until the behemoth Nerezza patience wears thin and the goliath raised his 300 lbs mass as his talons reached for the door handle, turning the brass knob clockwise. The vampire stood in awe; obviously the person has left an impression on the creature of the night, leaving Gaz a mess of questions. 'Well squire?' Nerezza swung the door ajar and the camera view turned to the dumbfounded gaze of the party host Gaz Green who's jaw dropped and his eyes widened, glassed over in liquid, blinking violently he looks upon the unknown applicant, yet to be unveiled. 'YOU’RE JOKING, RIGHT?' ranted the Velvet Prankster. The camera angle switched to display a ghost of P.W.A's past in the form of super genius Steven Hawking who rolled into the locker-room, assisted by his motorised wheel chair, head tilted his on-board computer flickered into life. 'I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKING~!' - To be continued -
