Feeling Green

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th May 2003 A bitter and eerie breeze stems across the open plain. The sudden gust toys in and out of the assortment of headstones that litter the Gethsemane Cemetery on this overcast day, the sky is as grim as the backdrop we have been subjected too and the ghoulish shadow of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Vampire totally eclipses what little light radiating. The Living Nightmare sits perched to the steeple of the Gothic church and swaying his head in a pendulum motion he witnesses the flood of humanity congregating on the ground below him. The beast snarls knowing their presence is nothing more than a camouflage, another opportunity to project their names through the media and some of the faces in this identity parade could benefit by having their names put in a positive light for once. The trench coat clad Vampire murmurs but the point of interest shifts to that of the two towering metal gates that fortify the burial ground. “Can we get this show on the road, if this takes any longer I’m sure I’ll contract pneumonia and then maybe you could lay my casket alongside so we can have a double funeral!” mutters the distinctive voice of the Jester. It’s common knowledge that when there’s Nerezza, Gaz Green isn’t usually far behind in tow and this event would be no exception. Peering down at his wristwatch the Emerald Menace targets the second hand as it pivots full-circle around the watch face. The green tailored suit looks to have the day-off for once in a showcase of respect for the deceased and its substitute, a little black number of similar fashions. The two metallic gates stood high above those seeking entry, consisting of a series cold steel beams that had rusted over time and now lay home to a network of creepers. Suddenly from the depths of the congregation emerges the behemoth known as Atlanta Johnson, better known as Propaganda and like the sweeping Angel of Death he motions over to the metallic blockade. His presence becomes the focus of attention and all heads strife towards his towering frame, his broad shoulders testing the durability of his fabric white shirt, his black trousers ran down his long legs and his spectacles lay crooked on the bridge of his nose. With a hefty push that would’ve been admired by ‘Samson,’ Propaganda opened the demonic gates and as they swayed ajar to allow entry they screeched whilst dragging along the bare concrete floor of the sidewalk. “And its about time…” sighed the Emerald Menace rolling his eyes. Headed up by Atlanta Johnson the crowd of Wrestle Online superstars followed his lead and one by one they entered the dark, dreary backdrop of the Gethsemane Cemetery. Like the yellow brick road from ‘The Wizard of Oz’ the people from all corners of the community, be it Primetime Championship Wrestling, Old School Entertainment or Primetime Wrestling Circuit kept to its pathway. There was murmur of conversation but overall silence had seeped in and the most visible of sounds was that of the shuffling of feet as Propaganda led his industry co-workers towards the burial site. “I can’t believe there’s no Hessian anymore!” muttered the King Midas of Tag to himself as his eyes roamed his surroundings, the backdrop a litter of turquoise trees randomly inserted onto the landscape but forward he marched, following the lead of one Atlanta Johnson who surprisingly was without Eric Sagar. The chilling breeze of earlier had once again injected itself and motions down the pathway. The burial site becomes visible courtesy of a group of gentlemen standing motionless in black tailored suits and onward the congregation marched in short yet slow strides. Finally the journey ended with all parties standing before the open casket of the Mercenary of Satan and within the beast laid, coated in his in-ring attire of a Norseman’s cloak and glistening facial mask the goliath looked in peace with his arms crossed over his ribcage. The church layers with row upon row of wooden seats that give off a tangible smell courtesy of the excessive use of polish. Statuettes litter the neighbouring and decayed walls and the stain glass windows showcase their assortment of colours as the light penetrates from outside. An unknown man crouches next to the open casket paying his respects, his stance sitting alongside a wreath of black flowers that home a note: “Ready to kick your ass in the afterlife, Dave” The unknown gentleman finishes and is quickly replaced by an oddly looking fellow with a tatty suit; most likely the product of a secondhand shop and strikingly so is that this person resembles our (being Primetime Championship Wrestling) Extreme Champion. The man unveils a replica OSW World title and nestles it over the lifeless corpse of Hessian before being interrupted upon by the bark of PCW referee Dark Gimmick, “YEAH! YOU’RE NOT SINGING ANYMORE!” Another guest silences the skeletal Dark Gimmick and the organ interpretation of ‘Awe of She’ by A.S.H. melodically plays in the background, it’s injection the queue for the grey headed priest attired in long black flowing robes with a purple neck scarf draped over both shoulders to entertain. “I’m Mr. Belfanew and I will be taking this service in remembrance of the former professional wrestling juggernaut, Hessian. We shall start with eulogies from his fellow co-workers. First up is…” bellows the priest before looking down at his notes, “Gaz Green” he concludes. The Lord of Laughter, the product of Hessian’s Headmaster ruling of Primetime Wrestling Academy stands erect from the cluster of the audience and cockily struts over to the podium. “Hello, Hessian masterminded P.W.A so being his student-… Bitch I at-least should pay my respects to the man! So Hessian, if you’re in Heaven or if you’re in Hell, I know your enjoying yourself. Rest In Peace, squire!” confidently says the Prince of Pranks before heading back to his seat under a blanket of applause. The Jester looks back as he hears the muttering of Propaganda, something about the use of ‘Profanity’. “Thank You, Mr. Green” expresses Mr. Belfanew, whilst returning to his pedestal, “Next, is James Edwards!” The astronaut wannabe takes residence on the podium and begins his speech but airtime has elapsed and as the man known as ‘Lunar’ pays his own mark of respect the scene slowly draws to a closure, bringing another Jester moment to an abrupt end.
