In the End, Pass the Tribute... dang, I'm losing my head.

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/17/02 About two hours ago, I was walking down the rarely utilized County Road Seven to poison the food in the restaurant that Jordan X Shabazz was supposed to be going to later in the day. It was windy and stormy, very awkward. It was fair weather not five minutes before that. Something was wrong. I stopped and took my bearings, when there was the smell of smoke and a high-pitched whining sound. Then, from about 3 feet above me, there came a loud, low-pitched voice more animal than human. It said: Demon:“Write for me the best RP in the world, or I'll eat your soul.” Obviously, this thing was full of itself, and I wasn’t in the mood to play. Having no one to look at (not that I could anyway), I just pointed my head up at the source of the sound, and shook my finger as I said: End:“No.” I called the demon’s bluff, and he didn’t expect that. Taken aback by my choice, he tried to recover, and act tough again. Demon:“What? You have to write for me the best RP in the world.” I really didn’t have time to deal with this wuss. My tone was direct and unwaivering. End:“I believe I said no. Good day.” The Demon can’t understand how I can refuse his challenge, or order, whatever. The demon makes the obligatory snarl as he tries to get me to reconsider to save face in from of whatever other demons that’re probably watching him and laughing right now. Demon:“But... I'm going to eat your soul and your flesh.” Fat chance. End:“Fat chance. Who are you, Rob Zombie now?” The demon grows tired of my games. Demon:“I grow tired of your games.” End:“Hey! Only I’m allowed to repeat the first-person narration, got it?” The demon growls. I can smell his breath. It’s like burning sulfur and rotten cabbage. I wish I didn’t have so strong a sense of smell. I might not after this. Demon:“Maybe I should just demonstrate my power.” The demon charges at me. He’s probably thinking he’s going to kill me. But in a show of my own most devestating maneuver, I stomp down hard on his foot. From behind the Demon, I hear Pharell and Mo’nique making sounds like it hurt just watching what I did. Pharell and Mo’nique:“Ooooh... Damn!” The demon hops up and down on his good foot. The ground shakes, he’s so heavy. Demon:“Dammit, that was my pinky toe.” I crack a smile. End:“I’ll do it again.” The voice of Sean Combs rings in my ear. End:“Do it again.” The demon has set his foot back on the ground. I’m sure he’s eyeing me like “Try it, I dare you.” Away behind the demon, Pharell and Mo’nique voice their objections in sympathy for the demon. Pharell and Mo’nique:“No, no no...” I lift my foot high into the air and stomp down again on the demon’s foot. Pharell and Mo’nique:“Oooooh!” After a few moments of hopping around off of his sore foot, the demon sets himself down again, scratching his head, I believe. Demon:“So, it would seem we're at an impass.” I walk around the demon and go on my way. End:“Not at all. I passed you quite easily.” The demon sits down on the ground and snaps his fingers. His voice is pouty now. Demon:“Now what am I going to do?” I call back to the demon as I head onto the Mexican restaurant. End:“Tenacious D should be coming past in a few minutes. Hide and wait for them.” The demon’s voice perks up. Demon:“Really? Thanks!” There’s the same high-pitched whining behind me, and then he must be gone. The wind clears up, and it seems to be fair weather again. End:“Aw, crap.” By my estimate, I won’t get to the restaurant in time to catch Jordan X Shabazz. Luckily, I hear something coming in my direction. I stick out my thumb and hope for the best. DJ Joseph Hahn:“What’s up? I’m late for a gig, so I can’t really pick you up. Sorry.” The Linkin Park band member rides his flying whale off into the sky, in the direction of the giant statue that wakes up and dances every time it rains. So, I’m walking along County Road 7, to the restaurant where Jordan X Shabazz will go to eat. I don’t even know how I know that he’ll be there. He just will. I’ve still got a match for Turmoil, but I won’t go into any details because this RP really isn’t even for that. Then, the thought comes across my mind. End:“Autochicken!” I have absolutely no Idea what I just said or why. Dear Lord, don’t let El Diablo’s insanity be rubbing off on me. I was this close to getting a good run in PCW. The last thing I need is the Mexican Crazy Fever ruining my head. Ugh. And there’s still the movie to do. I never even said yes, I don’t know why I go along with it. Maybe it’s just to humor him. But I’m getting tired of even that. So tired... Hey, is that pizza I smell? Michaelangelo:“Yeah, dude! You want the rest? We’d finish it, but we have to go fight Shredder.” Wait a second... The Ninja Turtles aren’t real. AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!
