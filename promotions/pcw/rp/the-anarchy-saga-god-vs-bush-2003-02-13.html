The Anarchy Saga: God vs Bush

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-03 Since when was Bush Infallible? Apparently America's brilliant government leaders have turned against Christianity. According to the Official theologian of the United States government, the Pope is wrong. Now, I hate religion and think anyone that follows something so blindly is either a moron, a american, a catholic, or a republican. Some people would argue that those are all the same thing. Correct me if i'm wrong, but isn't that silly little religion quite strong in the belief that the Pope is infallible, that he can do nothing wrong, that if he too raped little boys then it would be what God wants everyone to do, then Michael Jackson would be absolved? So shouldn't the government finding some random guy named Michael Novak to go against the Pope's words and essentially say 'look dude, you are so wrong', a guy who is apparently a GOVERNMENTAL theologian, which in itself breaks a thousand rules that have been laid in the Consitution for hundreds of years, shouldn't that piss people off? If this was normal times, maybe, if the people were really worth anything anymore, sure. No, you are all uneducated piles of nothing. Most of you take up space, waiting for the day you can retire from your dead end jobs with less then 10 years left of your life so you can collect Social Security that WON'T EVEN BE THERE when you are old enough to retire, assuming you make it that far. Which currently doesn't seem that likely. Instead these idiots follow their PRESIDENT more then they follow their SPIRITUAL LEADER, does that tell you anything about the way Americans think? Does that convince you to join the cause anymore? It definately should. It shows you that even the close minded religious zealots would rather see Iraq nuked then see their religion defended. You see, this Novak (no relation to good ole Eddy) believes that what Georgie Porgie is doing is a JUST WAR... He is trying to make Bush seem important by saying he is DOING THIS OUT OF THE BIBLE. A JUST WAR. What the hell. While were at it why don't we just say Iraq is Satan, and the United States is nothing but good angelic slice of heaven, mmm Apple Pie and Baseball right? Wrong. America is full of corrupt liberals who know absolutely nothing about what they speak, they ignore the people more then the WWE does, and this is alot more important then Pro-Wrestling. It makes me want to vomit. It should make you want to vomit to. It should make you want to burn the flag and fuck up some Starbucks or something, whatever it takes you to feel better. You see, by claiming this is a Just War and that the Pope is wrong, in theory, they are trying to plant it in your feeble minds that Bush is God, and that us Americans are good and true. Even though 50% of our population isn't american, hell, they aren't even White, OR Native American. They are Rican, or Black, they are minorities who happen to be so uneducated that they breed faster then real americans, then those of us who dedicated themselves to becomming education. The country is being overrun by the future Janitors and Cab Drivers of 'America'. Those that believe Bush is a religion. So tell me, is this YOUR America?
