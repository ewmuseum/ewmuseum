Reliving a nightmare. Part 3

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 I stared out the window trying to see up and down the street. The squad car was still sitting the in the same place it had been for the past few days. The only other signs of life that I could see were one of my neighbors taking out the trash, a young man running down the street, his long blonde hair flying about in the wind, and the mailman placing a bundle of mail into my mailbox on the edge of my lawn. It had been a few days since I told my husband, John, about the nightmares being back. And about what exactly had brought them back. He was caring and thoughtful enough to use his pull to get a squad car to watch the house. He also against my wishes had someone go over to his house and warn him to not come around. I had tried to talk him out of this. But he wouldn’t have anything to do with it. He said that he felt it was a good move. But me, I thought it was a stupid one. He didn’t understand the man. He didn’t know what the man was capable of. He hasn’t seen those eyes. He hasn’t heard that voice. He doesn’t know the Ripper. He doesn’t know the freak. John just placed his arms around me and pulled me in tight. He whispered into my ear telling me that it was going to be alright. That he wouldn’t let anything happen to me. That he wouldn’t let anyone hurt me ever again. I wanted to believe him. I wanted to place my trust in what he said. But how could I? How could I believe that Ripper wouldn’t come around? I begged with him. I pleaded with him. But nothing seemed to work. Nothing seemed to get through to him. I’m not sure anything could have. I’m not sure he understands just what this man is capable of. I don’t think anyone does… except me. Now here I am. Alone, scared, and worried that the Ripper man will come to my house. Squad car or no squad car out front, I don’t think it matters to him. He’s enough of a lunatic to just walk up the sidewalk anyway. He’s just crazy enough to try anything… anything… As I starred out my window the mailman turned and headed up the street. The little red arrow on my mailbox was flipped up. Telling me that I had mail, I had already known that. I had seen the man place it there. But as I sat there looking out at it. I wasn’t sure I could find the courage to step out into the world to retrieve it. I wasn’t convinced that he wasn’t out there. Was he waiting for the right moment, waiting till he spotted me, and waiting to seek his revenge? I pushed away from the couch and the window. I closed the blinds and walked to the center of the room. Unsure of what I should do. Should I go out and get my mail? Should I pretend that this is like no other day? Should I take the chance that he’s not out there? I walked towards the door. Placing my hand on the knob I froze. What will I do if he is out there? What will I do if I spot him? Will I be able to go back inside? Will I be able to move this time? Or will be frozen in time like that awful night? So many questions I can’t answer. So many questions I’m afraid to find the answers too. I slowly twisted the door knob and pulled the door open just a crack. A burst of fresh air him me in the face causing me to close my eyes for a split second. At that instant I had thought I seen someone. I had thought someone was on my porch. I was afraid. Afraid someone was there and even for afraid to open my eyes to find out. I found the strength to slowly open my eyes. As the light began to seep in and the scene before me came into focus I had found my eyes were playing tricks on me. Nobody was on my porch. Nobody was within my range of view. Just before I pulled the door back to open it a thought crossed my mind. What if I had seen somebody? What if that somebody got out of my sight just before I opened my eyes? I shook my head trying to knock the thought out of my head. I would have been impossible for someone to hide within the split second I had my eyes closed. Nobody was there I was just allowing my fear to the better of me… again. I pulled the door back and stepped out onto the porch. The air was refreshing, the sunlight was warm, and the day seemed rather nice. I looked around and surveyed my surroundings. My neighbor to the left of my house was out watering his grass. A neighbor across the street was raking leaves. Other than that I could not see anybody, except for the two gentlemen sitting in an unmarked police car halfway down the block. I began to step down the stairs when I heard a rushing car coming down the block. I stopped and hurried to duck behind a bush that raised up high enough for me to hide behind. As the car raced past my house I realized that it was just some teenage kid. I had jumped at the noise and hide from the driver for nothing. This was really beginning to be annoying to myself and how I was living my life. I came out from behind the bush and looked around one last time. This time not finding or hearing anything out of the ordinary. I began walking towards my mailbox when I glanced at the car sitting down the street. The undercover police officers waved at me and I smiled back at them. As I reached the mailbox I lowered the red arrow and pulled out my mail. As I began flipping through the stack I noticed most if not all of it was bills. As I reached the bottom letter which looked like a card a light drizzle began to fall. I looked up at the sky and found that dark clouds were beginning to roll in. The wind was just beginning to pick up as well. My hair began flying into my face and my neighbors leaves began to blow across his yard. I looked back down at the unaddressed envelope and began to open it. Who could this be from? An old friend I hadn’t seen in years? An ex-boyfriend perhaps wondering what I’m up to now? As I opened the letter I found that it was just a small notebook sheet with just two words on it. Somebody had been watching too many old thriller movies, as the message was created with letters cut out of magazines. I’m watching…I dropped all of my mail except for the letter as I began to scream. I turned violently around looking, searching for him. He had to be around. He had to be. But where was he? Where was he hiding? Where was he watching me from? Could he see me right now? Was he somewhere around here this instant? The two officers in their unmarked car jumped out and began running towards me. One of them fell as he hit the rain drizzled grass of my front yard. The other made it safely looking at me. Most likely wondering what in the world I was screaming about. I couldn’t speak the only thing I could do was hand the officer the envelop and the letter. He read it then began looking around. He pointed at his partner who was now standing. He then pointed towards the house and told him to check it. His partner ran off towards the house as he began to call the incident in on his radio. After a few moments he looked at me and began talking to me. “Emily, are you ok.” I stood there looking at him blankly. He was a friend of John’s. They worked on the force together. They have been for a few years. They even had him and his wife over for dinner a few times. Now here he was seeing her in the most terrifying time of her life since that first awful night that this whole nightmare began on. “Yes, I want my husband.” I was able to stumble out at least that sentence. “He’s already on his way.” He replied. “He’ll be here shortly.” I began looking around trying to see if I could spot anybody. I was looking for somebody that stuck out. For somebody that seemed out of place. For somebody I thought didn’t belong. I was searching every where within sight for the freak, for the Ripper. The rain began to come down in a pour. The officer directed me towards the house. The door swung wide open. The inside’s appeared dark, darker than I remember them being. “Is it safe? Can we go in safely?” I was asking the officer. Begging him for an answer. He took my hand and pulled me towards the entrance of the house. “It’ll be fine.” Are you sure I was thinking. “I promise.” He said in response to my thoughts. As we stepped up onto the porch I could have sworn that my eyes caught something, or rather somebody peering out from behind the blinds. I stopped and yanked my hand away from the officer. I took a step back as he turned to look at me. “What’s wrong?” He said as if he hadn’t noticed the pair of eyes. “I… I… thought…” I wasn’t sure of what was real anymore. Did I truly see someone peering out? Was it real? Was it my imagination? I wasn’t sure. As I stood there the officer called his partner on the radio. “Is the house secure?” We both stood in silence as we awaited the response of his partner. But none came back over the radio. Again he called to his partner. “Is the house secure?” As we stood there still awaiting an answer. An eerie feeling came over me. A feeling that something bad was going to happen. A feeling that someone was in the house, that someone had done something to the other officer, and that I would be the next one that something happened too. The officer put his hand on his issued revolver and turned to me. “Stay here until I come back for you?” I could do nothing but nod as he turned and vanished into the house. As I stood there alone I began to wonder. What if the freak gets him too? Who will protect me from him? Will my husband get here in time? Is this how it’s going to end? The rain was coming down harder now and the thunder was beginning to roll in as well. The lightening flashes lighted up the darkened sky, at least for momentary instances. I began to shiver from the coldness of my wet clothes. The fear dwelling deep within me was growing with each passing second. I knew that this was the end… A noise behind me took my attention away from the house. It was a car pulling up. A car I recognized, John, was pulling up into the drive. As he exited his vehicle I ran out into the pouring rain to meet him. He put his arms around me and hugged me tight. “Are you alright?” He said with real concern in his eyes. I kissed him and tightened my grip on him. “Yes, now that your home I’m ok.” He pulled away and looked into my eyes. “Where are Jim and Frank?” I pointed towards the house. “Their inside.” He pulled me towards his car and opened the door. “Wait in here. Keep the doors locked and don’t get out till I come back.” “But… Joh…” He cut me off. “Don’t move. I’ll be fine just stay in here.” He then turned and ran towards the house. I sat there inside the confines of the vehicle. Waiting for my husband to come back out and tell me that everything was ok. To tell me that whoever was in the house is gone. To tell me that he’ll keep me safe. To tell me that, the freak, won’t come around and bother me. After a few minutes John came back out of the house with one of the officers. He ran down the steps and opened the car door. He grabbed my hand and pulled me out. “Let’s go in.” “Are you sure it’s safe?” I asked even though I knew John wouldn’t take me any place that wasn’t. “Yeah, it’s safe.” “But where’s the other officer?” I was worried for him. I knew he had a family and was a close friend of Johns. “He’s inside. He’s fine. He had just gone out of the back door to look around when a branch from a tree fell and hit him. He’s dazed but he’ll be fine.” We then walked up the steps and went inside the house. John told the officer to take his partner to the emergency room to get checked out. That he would be staying home the rest of the day to take care of his wife. “Thank you.” I said as he smiled at me. “Let’s go get you changed.” He said standing to his feet. I pulled on his arm and made him sit back down beside me. A million questions running though my mind. I looked over to where I had thought I seen somebody looking out at me. Where I had though he, the freak was standing and waiting for me. John must have read my mind as he put his arm around me, as he whispered into my ear. “It’s ok Emily. I’m here and I’ll protect you.” He sounded so confident. I stared deep into his big blue eyes. Eyes that told the story of how much he loves me, how deep his love goes, and to what length we will go to protect me. I was unsure of how to phrase the words. I was unsure of how to say what needed to be said. The only thing I could do was just come out and say it. “Was anybody in the house or am I losing my mind?”
