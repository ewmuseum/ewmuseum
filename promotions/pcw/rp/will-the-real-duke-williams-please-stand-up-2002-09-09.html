Will the Real Duke Williams Please Stand Up?

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th September 2002 The camera opens on a city street, presumably somewhere in Anaheim, California. Along that street walks a man wearing a cowboy hat… and what appears to be a black stick on beard. You don’t recognise him to a moment because of the lack of goggles, but it is indeed none other than Miyagawa. He pulls his hands out of the pockets of the long black trenchcoat that he wears and seems to check the fingertips for some strange reason. In fact, he appears to peel some skin off, lick the underside of the flap and push it firmly back down. He slowly approaches a Police Car and pulls a can of something out of his pocket before spraying it into the air… it’s green silly string. A whole can of it. He stops by the back of the Police Car and slowly sprays onto the rear window “Duke Williams Rules, Hoss” in big bold silly string letters. He finishes and takes a second to admire his handy work. He then realises that no one has tried to arrest him. Duke/Miya: God dammit, what do I have to do to ruin Duke William’s bad name around here? He peers around the side of the car at the two officers sitting in the front and slams his fist down on the rear window, making a loud noise… but the officers don’t do anything. Hell, they don’t even turn around. He walks towards the passenger side window and taps on it with the back of his hand. It slowly gets rolled down, revealing two Californian Police officers driving Starbucks Coffee and eating several donuts. Duke/Miya: I like… just vanilised your car. The blond haired almost surfer like officers look at him for a second as he crouches down to be seen. Police Officer #1: Like narly dude, totally reeks of awesomeness. Those pigs must die! Miyagawa is slightly stunned for a second. Miyagawa: Eh? The cops continue to eat their donuts and drink their coffee. Miyagawa: WHAT IN THE BLUE HELL IS THIS COUNTRY COMING TO? EMPLOYING MORONS AS FEDERAL OFFICERS!! JESUS F’CHRIST! Miyagawa stands up immediately and walks away from the car, before turning and throwing the can back at the car, hitting the windscreen and shattering the glass so that it all frosts over. The Police Officer he spoke to sticks his head and arm out the window and does the surfer hand signal with the little finger and forefinger raised with all the others closed into the palm. Police Officer #2: ROCK ON DUDE! Miyagawa just stands there and shakes his head in annoyance and partial disappointment. Miyagawa: What the hell do I have to do to get Duke Williams arrested in this town? He thinks for a second and then realises it. Miyagawa: I’ll do exactly what George Michael did… I’ll masturbate in a public toilet! --- The camera changes now back to the Asylum gym where JJ is standing on the camera shot, also dressed as Duke Williams, complete with stick on black beard. JJ: No way. Not in this life. Go to hell. The camera pans around to show Miyagawa, still dressed as Duke Williams attempting to plead with him. Miyagawa: Oh come on JJ… it’s just a different place. Then you can let a female Police Officer find you, and it’s all game on from there. I’ll even buy you a few magazines on the way if you need them. JJ seems rather infuriated by this. JJ: NO WAY. Get someone else to do it. Have Anarchy try to rape Byron Calver whilst dressed as Duke or something. I am not tossing off in a public toilet. Get a flaming grip, will you? Miyagawa shakes his head in disappointment. Miyagawa: Ok, ok… I’ll ask someone else. --- The camera changes to the interior of an adult video store. Standing there is none other than PCW Interviewer, Cal Seaver. Looking at some videos that star a woman who looks particulary like Tiffany Jennings… Miyagawa, still dressed as Duke approaches him from the side, startling Cal. Cal: Mister President… I’m… wait… you look a little pale… Miyagawa: Cal, it’s me. Listen, would you be willing to dress up as Duke Williams and masturbate in a public toilet so that a Police Officer can walk in on you and find you? Cal pretends that he didn’t hear that too well. Cal: Come again? Miyagawa: Oh come on. The Blood Brothers found you tossing off in a sex shop six months back, so don’t try to act all innocent with me. I’ll pay you. Cal: How much? Miyagawa: Say ten bucks. Cal frowns at him. Cal: Piss off. Miyagawa shakes his head. Miyagawa: Ok, I can see there won’t be any bargaining with you… I’ll try to find someone else then I guess. Miyagawa walks past Cal while shaking his head and goes to walk out of the shop when he passes a rack of Newspapers and on the front page of Sports Daily is a big picture of Duke Williams… the real Duke Williams. The headline… “Duke Williams Arrested” Miyagawa: What in the blue hell….?
