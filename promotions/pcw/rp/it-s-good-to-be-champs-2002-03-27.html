It's Good To Be Champs

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/02 Time: 3: 22 P.M Location: New MexicoScene: Restaurant “ And I’m proud to be a Tag Champion ” The scene opens with Juan Borro, Kevin Klaymore, Chester Bin Laden, and Fidel Castro, all sitting down around the table, making cheers. The men are all happy about their victory on Sabotage. It’s a spirit filled moment at the restaurant, and everyone is having some fun. Juan: Excuse me guys, but I think I have an announcement to make and Kevin would probably agree with everything I have to say also. (The other men look on as Juan raises his glass and stands on his feet. Kevin decides to stand beside him, and the two men stare over and Chester and Fidel.) Juan: Fellow men, leaders, politicians, and National Icons, I thank you to a large agree for the way you two showed no resilience in stepping into this country and fighting on their soil. Everyone makes comments on how political figures talk a big game but never take part in the action, but you my friends have taken part in the action. In the combat they call wrestling. (The men Hoorah and raise their glasses in the air, stumbling on their seats, a bit drunk from the wine they’ve received.) Juan: You guys fought the United States and you guys one. They may be able to say they beat you, because of bombings and other unfair techniques the Americans use because they’re not real fighters, but you can say you went nose to nose with them, and kicked they’re ASSES! (The men Hoorah once again, as feeling and inspiration enters the veins of Juan Borro, and he continues his rant with love and compassion for his fellow political icons and ring warriors.) Juan: We, meaning Kevin over here…. (Juan taps Kevin on the shoulder and Kevin nods.) Juan: And I. Will not let the two of you down. Come Friday, and come the PCW Pay-Per View, we will prevail. Not only for the gold around our waists, or the satisfaction of being the best, but for the pure pride of our people and our motherland. (Chester and Fidel look on at the young and pride filled Juan rant with the same passion they once had.) Juan: People say “How can you win something for your people, when you’re really just winning it for yourself?” My answer to that is “What do you admire more, a piece of gold to wear, or people calling you their hero and children following in your footsteps. For what good is a piece of gold in there is no significant meaning to it. (The men nod in agreement, as the spotlight goes over to Kevin.) Kevin: I may not be as much of an inspired speaker as Juan is, but at least I’m not drunk so I can make some sense. (The men laugh in their seats as Kevin continues.) Kevin: You see wrestling is something I love to do. It’s something I’ve loved to do ever since the day I saw it on television. I always wished I could be one of those men in the ring. Now when I look back at it I wish I could’ve been watching men like ourselves and the rest of International Incident on my television set, because we’re not fighting just for people to look up to us. We’re fighting for people to look up to us, and follow our thoughts and beliefs. You may not know it now, but people probably look up to us the most, especially kids. I’m sure children back in our countries would rather follow a message us exciting wrestlers are sending rather then a bunch a suit wearing old men like Politically Incorrect, am I right? (Hoorah! Goes the men bearing smiles and good feelings, as the alcohol hits harder in their systems.) Kevin: I used to look at you guys, Chester and Fidel and think, Woah! Those guys must get paid loads of money for doing absolutely nothing. Fidel: That’s not too far from the truth my friend. I mean other than the ladies who work for me. (Fidel smiles, and Kevin blushes, knowing how drunken Fidel is, but still continues his speech.) Kevin: Well now I feel you guys do more than you’re given credit for. All of those people whose lives you’ve affected, and the matters you guys have to put up with every day, is beyond me. (Chester gets up on his booth and starts to do a dance, as Fidel just laughs until he falls on the ground.) Juan: I don’t think they’re paying much attention to us Kevin. Maybe we shouldn’t have bought them they’re own bottles of wine anyway. Have you tasted that stuff, it’s pretty strong. Kevin: Yea, but you drank a bottle also. How come you’re not as out of it as those two are? Juan: I’m immune to it. You seem to forget I was on my own fifteen or so years of my life. What do you think I did in my spare time? (Kevin chuckles and nods, understanding what Juan is getting at.) (Meanwhile, Fidel and Chester are making a huge amount of noise screaming all types of things in different languages. Some of the other people in the restaurant start to hear, and they talk and stare amongst their selves.) Juan: Be quiet and sit down guys, were not supposed to be making a high profile in this place, we don’t want word getting around. Kevin: Woah! You just told Castro and Bin Laden family member to be quiet, that’s pretty damn cool. Juan: If you say so, Kevin. Do me a favor, and put Fidel’s disguise back on before someone sees him. (A customer gets fed up and approaches the table, before Kevin could put Fidel’s disguise on.) Customer: You guys are totally out of order, some families are trying to have a nice peaceful meal, and you drunk are running our nice and pleasant night out with the whole family. (The man then looks down and notices Fidel Castro pouring wine down his throat, right in front of him.) Fidel: You can take your family and tell them to shove it. Customer: You’re……………….You’re…………………………You’re the leader of Cuba……You’re……. Juan: He’s Fidel Castro and I advise you do what he says so why don’t you just get out of our way, before people have to be harmed. (The man stands their in shock not moving, and Kevin takes him picks him up and throws him into a table across the room. Kevin then gets both Chester and Fidel on his shoulders and runs out of the place, with Juan in front.) (Juan notices the employee at the door, and the other people working their screaming “Stop them”. Juan thinks fast and pulls out two-hundred dollars, placing it in the young man’s pocket.) Employee: Cool dudes, have a nice day now. (Juan quickly unlocks the car and Kevin throws both of the men in the back seats, as they speed away into the night.) Kevin: That was a close one. Juan: Well you know there are reasons why we are the PCW tag team champions you know? Kevin: Yea, and come Friday we do not see our selves losing them. And that’s no matter what. Juan: No matter what! (The scene fades out with the camera going to the back of the car where both of the titles are laying. The camera keeps zooming in until the segment ends.)
