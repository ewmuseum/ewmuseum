The Rebellion Saga: 12/25/02

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/25/02 The tree was lit up and the glass glinted and glittered with the color. Greens, Blues, Reds, Whites, all of it came together to create a work of pure beauty in the form of a fir tree. That wasn't the real look of pure beauty however. In a few short hours the presents under this tree would be converged upon, ripped open and devoured mentally, used and reused until they were forgot. The lucky ones would last til the next year, when the cycle all happened again. The unlucky ones wouldn't make it past March without finding the place where used things go to be forgotten. Gifts never last very long. No matter how special. A diamond engagement ring will last until the wearer dies, and if it becomes a family heirloom it will last until the day someone who doesn't care loses it. That happens often. Things with emotional value become worthless when emotion is drained. Emotion which can be a good or bad thing depending on what side of the line you stand on. It can be what gives you the drive to continue, the drive to succeed, or it can be your downfall. The thing that kicks your legs out from under you and sends you sliding down a hill that you will never be able to catch yourself on before you hit the ground. But which is better? To feel everything, to be a emotional pillow, or to be cold and uncaring. To not give a shit about anyone other then yourself and those close to you, if even them. To be emotionally dead. To be the man who butchered Pharaoh and laughed about it, or to be the man that almost slit his wrists on a snow covered Canadian hill side a year ago... Or which is worse, to only be one of those things, or to go from one to the other every single day on varying extremes. Welcome to DarkStar's world. A world where he is forced to live in and breathe in. A world that for 20 years of his life made him into a calculating killer who never cared about anything except for Talena, a man who could kill you and your family and not bat a eye. Then suddenly he was whipped into knowing he was a connection between humans and gods, the son of Destiny and his own mother. The Lord of all Dreams and the Prince of all Nightmares. Now, not only did he feel, but he felt everything. Every emotion from every person he ever contacted. He was everywhere and nowhere all at once. If someone dreamed, there he was, but yet he still stood here talking. If someone had a nightmare, he was there too, but yet he was still here sleeping. He is everything and nothing. If you asked him to explain it... he couldn't. This was thrust upon him before he even could deny it. So on Christmas, he didn't just feel the happiness of seeing his loved one open her presents like a giddy child, he didn't feel the happiness of recieving his own gifts, he felt everything. Every single sadness, pain, heartbreak, and emotion mixed up with his own. He didn't even know what was his anymore. It sickened him. It was time to put it to a end.
