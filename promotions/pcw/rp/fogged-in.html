Fogged In

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov.8th/02 The notorious fog of London was thicker than pea soup as I strolled along the banks of the River Thames. As I exhaled, the frigid November air turned my breath into a fog all it’s own. Through the grey fog I could barely make out tiny white dots floating about, as the snow from the sky. The waves beating against the river banks produced a soothing sound that brought my nerves to ease. As I stopped to take in the sounds of downtown London, my match against BladeD played through my mind over and over again. Ever since last Friday, in Zürich, I’ve been on this incredible high. My first victory, the lethal chair-shot I dealt Mark Amulet . . . it was all a dream come true. I couldn’t have imagined a better night, and I was having doubts that my night at the London Dockland’s Arena would be as productive as my last outing. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As I leaned against the boardwalk’s railing, I began to compare the local weather with my recent ‘attacks’. Like the snow, I would stalk my victims stealthily and silently . . . then boom, with a flurry of action I’d leave them in a fog of confusion . . . they wouldn’t know what hit them. Now I know some would disagree with my choice of comparison, but then again they have never hunted a man down with a steel chair. In the background I could hear street cars buzzing around, and street vendors praising their wares. Peering through the fog I began to feel like I was back in Michigan staring out into the fog which drifted inches over the water of Lake Michigan. If it weren’t for the annoying British accent of a man talking near me, I’d actually believe I was back home . . . but it was not to be. As the man continued to talk, I looked around trying to find a recipient of the man’s prattling . . . but there was no one in sight. I began to think the fog was impeding my sight of a second person, but there was no one there. As the man continued to talk, he pivoted from left to right, and I could see that he wasn’t holding a cellular phone or anything . . . he was just standing there talking to himself. Normally I’d think he was quite odd, but then again, he didn’t make a living wearing spandex and dancing around a ring in front of thousands of people. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I had decided to leave the psycho to ramble on by himself, and made my way down the river. Near the end of the boardwalk, I found myself watching a young couple sitting on a bench. They stared at each other with love in their eyes, and it took back to when I began wrestling. When I had gotten my first taste of wrestling competition I fell in love with the business. There was little I did that didn’t have some connection to wrestling. I ate, breathed, lived wrestling . . . but as time went on, I realized that the sweet taste of success had quickly turned to the bitter tang of boredom. Even now I feel that the PCW is no challenge for me. There is not one man on the roster that could challenge my physical and mental talents. I could not, for the life of me, think of anyone man that I’d prefer entering the ring with. I perceived no one as a threat. With each name that passed through my mind, all I could picture was another rung on the ladder to the top. To me, PCW is nothing more than a game of ‘Snakes and Ladders’ . . . the only difference being, there were no snakes on my game board. Rich Rollins . . . BladeD . . . Mark Amulet . . . they are just the beginning. They are but small, insignificant ladders to boost me to the next level; they’re almost a waste of my time. What I need . . . what I want . . . is a real challenge. I need to face a man that will raise to a level surpassing all other men. The question is, who is dumb enough to step into the ring with Markus Eugene Riley? Who is asinine enough to tangle with Mark Michigan? Perhaps the lack of in-ring invitations is a sign that these PCW chumps aren’t as dumb as they look, sound, and act. Suddenly, the young lady sitting on the bench winded up and slapped the living daylights out of the young man. Aww . . . the cruel mistress known as ‘love’. Many a man has felt love’s departing sting across his cheek. But I am one man who will never feel its full effects, for I plan to slap love right in its savage face. I knew after I had disposed of Mark Amulet during the November 8th episode of Fusion, my sights would be locked on Alliances . . . my first pay per view. That would be my big chance to make a name for myself. As if beating every second person I see with a chair isn’t enough to catch the attention of my peers, I knew if I placed well at Alliances I would bask in the spotlight for sure. However, everything depends on who my teammates are . . . and if they know my golden rule. And what is that golden rule, you ask? Well, it’s quite simply really . . . basically it goes something like this . . . STAY OUT OF MY WAY! ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Realizing that I had speaking out loud for the past few moments, I took a second to look around to see if anyone had noticed. Indeed, there were a few folks glaring at me as if I were crazy. As usual, I just gave them a sneer and walked away. Embarrassing, no? For one to make a fool of himself . . . that is the epitome of embarrassment. Both Rich Rollins and BladeD made the mistake of embarrassing themselves by believing they were better than me; they both learned their lessons the hard way. As for Mark Amulet, he also learned embarrassment when I laid him out on the floor with a steel chair . . . unfortunately, he didn’t learn the lesson. Now I will be forced to lay him out again at Fusion . . . tisk, some people will never learn. ***I would like to apologize for the lateness and lack of length of this rp. Several freak snow storms in Nova Scotia left me without electricity. So I had to do this all last minute. Again, I apologize***
