Date: April 8, 2002 *** *** *** The scene opens in a room that’s thick with heavy grey smoke that’s rolling out of the large man’s cigar. He sits in the middle of the room, which appears to be some sort of office, smoking away in a comfortable looking reclining chair... there’s something familiar about the man - something disturbing as well. His face looks like a web of wrinkles and scars beneath a clean shaven head and above a thick, greying black, beard. He’s no small man, by any means. If he were standing, we would be able to see that he is a pair of inches over six feet in height and weighs a tad over three hundred pounds. And one thing is for sure - no one would call this man handsome. He’s wearing a tight black t-shirt that exposes the better part of both his massive, tattoo covered arms... Large Man: “So you wanna know about Jason Snow, huh?” He smirks... Large Man: “Yeah, I remember Jason Snow... from way back...” The familiar looking man raises his cigar to his lips, inhales, and then blows out a thick grey ring. Through the smoke we can see him smiling, and painted or tattooed in bold red letters across his front teeth are the letters “D - E - A - T - H”. Suddenly, we know who the man is. Death, of the notorious DEATHROW wrestling stable. The man is a wrestling legend in the indies. Near the end of his wrestling career, he spent a couple of weeks in PCW, but he was forty years old then, and his best years were well behind him... Death: “I didn’t think much of the little f(CENSORED)ker when I first saw him.” Above all else, Death has always been known for his fowl mouth and equally fowl personality. He’s gained at least twenty pounds since retiring from the ring over a year ago. His body was never really a flattering one, but now it’s bordering on “grotesque.” In his day, Death was all about raw toughness - not skill. He was notorious for his bar-room-brawl style of wrestling and the fact that he wasn’t interested in winning matches - only hurting people... he was very good at what he did... Death: “He was just like all the other SIW rookies at the time. Had a head full of f(CENSORED)king pipe dreams, you know? They were all like that. Thought they were going to be big f(CENSORED)king Superstars. I don’t think any of them are even wrestling anymore! Little bitches! I cracked the dreams out of their f(CENSORED)king heads! But a little while later...” Death pauses to take another hard drag from his cigar... Death: “I realized this Snow kid... he was different. Sure, he may have looked like just another little sissy. And I still don’t understand why he wears those f(CENSORED)king leather pants. But hey, if he wants to look like a faggot, who am I to stop him?”Death chuckles while some members of PCW management hope that no Gay Rights Activists are tuned in. The large man lets some cigar ashes fall carelessly onto the floor before continuing... Death: “When SIW shut down, he was in rehab or some s(CENSORED)t at the time. Alcoholism or something. Like I give a f(CENSORED)k!” Death shakes his head and scratches his thick beard... Death: “We went to the UWL for a while, and then I headed to PCW for a while. Hell, if I’d been there in my prime, there wouldn’t be a f(CENSORED)king thing left of that place! But I was forty f(CENSORED)king years old when I got there! Anyway, I sent word to Jason to get his ass over to PCW, but he was too busy spending time with his bitch or something - you know, some sissy s(CENSORED)t like that.” Death shakes his head, mocking the thought with his eyes... those eyes... cold blue, like some kind of tinted steel. Those eyes would rival those of Jason Snow’s himself - only their older now. In his day, Death was known as the Blue Eyed Devil. Now, at age forty-one, those eyes are beginning to show the effects of age. The large smoking man scratches his chest before finishing off... Death: “I’ll tell you one thing though, he may not be the biggest bastard ever to step into the ring, but if he can go one on one with the Blue Eyed Devil and live to tell about it - those f(CENSORED)king pansies in PCW are in for some trouble.” Death, who’s now smiling menacingly at us, stands up from his seat, takes one last puff from his cigar and absently discards it on the floor... As he walks out of the small, office-like, interview room, the scene fades to black... *** *** *** April 1, 2002 - 9:45 am She told him, over and over, that he didn’t have to come back so soon... he wouldn’t listen. He couldn’t listen. He couldn’t stay away. Secretly, she wanted him to retire. Secretly, he kind of wanted to retire too. But that was a month ago, and now, after having taken March off in its entirety, the young former PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World, Jason Snow, realizes that his story is not finished yet. He’s not even twenty-four. His prime is still coming, not behind him. But she doesn’t care - she wants him to stay home... to stay with her. Despite her protests, she understands his situation. She knows that if he steps away from the ring now that he will undoubtedly regret it for the rest of his life - he has so much left to give to the sport... He called it a knee injury, and indeed, there was pain in his knee. But he had wrestled successfully through worse pain before. This time however, he opted for quick surgery that would give him a valid excuse to take some time off while he evaluated his future. Recovery was quicker than any of his doctors had expected, and now the Killin’ Villain finds himself healthy and ready to make his return... The only thing standing in his way was her... his wife... ...Women are so much different than men... She told him to stay - for just another month. He couldn’t. She told him to think about his daughter - how they had been having such great times since he returned home and they became a family again. She didn’t have to tell him to think about it, he could never get it out of his head anyway. His daughter was the center of his universe, even when he and his wife were estranged. Now that they were back together, and as happy as ever, he loved being able to see his daughter every day... but that didn’t change anything. Men are so much different than women... She was just getting used to things. Just getting used to waking up every morning with her husband lying in bed next to her, their infant daughter sleeping peacefully in a bedroom down the hall. This is how she had always dreamed things... she didn’t realize all of the work that came along with being the wife of a wrestler. But now, she knows that things are going to get back to normal - back to the way she had long ago accepted her marriage would be. She would wake up, probably five days a week, alone. Her daughter would still be asleep down the hall, but her husband, Jason Snow, would be away doing God knows what with God knows who. She had learned to trust him, but gradually that trust wore down and withered away to nothing. She became paranoid every time her husband came on television - afraid that he would go a little too far with whatever woman happened to be gawking at him on that day... But now, she knows that she will have to trust him again - and this time, she will have to sustain that trust. He told her, and she believes him, that he never touched another woman until long after they became separated. And then, he touched many... ...Women are so much different than men... She forgave him, and has learned to trust him again. He could never do that for her, if the roles were reversed. If she had been the one sleeping around with everything available, he would be in prison now for murdering one of them. But she’s not like that - she can see past it. Snow lives in a world of physical emotions. He loves, he hates. She has many more emotions, though she has learned that she’ll never be able to teach Snow the same ones... He’ll forever be what he is now - but she loves him for it. She loves the way that he would do absolutely anything for her, and sometimes she even loves the way he would do absolutely anything to protect her. But now, on this rainy Monday morning... he’s leaving... She doesn’t know when he’ll be back. He doesn’t know when he’ll be back. The only thing that they are certain of this time, is that he will be back...Cab Driver: “You gettin’ in or what, bub?” Suddenly, the young wrestling star snaps out of his daze and nods to the little cabby, dropping his two Snake Skin suitcases so the little man can load them into the trunk of the cab. Without looking back to his house, Snow gets into the back seat of the cab and waits... the camera man, who guides our view, gets in next... Cab Driver: “Hey! That’s gonna cost extra for the camera man, bub!” Snow nods, his eyes never breaking their absent stare, and the cab driver comes around and gets into the driver’s seat. When he pulls the cab out onto the street, Snow can’t help but look back at his old house. Without ever turning the camera, we know that his wife is standing in the doorway, watching him leave... Cab Driver: “Where to, bub?” Snow: “Airport.” The Cab Driver nods. She said that she couldn’t come with him. It would be too hard at the airport. She would rather say good bye at their own home, and then wait by the phone for his call. He didn’t argue - he didn’t want anyone to see him cry in public anyway. But now, as the cab speeds away from his home, Snow has to fight the urge to tell the driver to turn around. Another month wouldn’t hurt, would it? What harm could it possibly do? Sure, he may not receive a paycheck, but this comeback isn’t about the money! He doesn’t need money! Everyone knows that Snow is set for life with a fortune that is rumoured to be in the range of eighty to a hundred million dollars. So if it’s not about money, what is this about? Why must he leave his wife and family behind for this? He knows as soon as the question enters his mind. He’s coming back to PCW - to wrestling - for one reason and one reason only... the competition. Since he decided to start wrestling at the age of nineteen, everyone has always known that Jason Snow was something special. They probably could never have imagined that he would one day be the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World... that title - the one that he’s coming back for now - is the highest prize in professional wrestling today. So much history behind it... He won it once, and now he craves it again... Cab Driver: “So, bub, ya goin’ on a trip or something?” The Driver’s words, spoken in low, raspy, tones bring Snow’s attention back into reality. He doesn’t want to be here... Sarcastically, he responds... Snow: “A trip? What in the hinges of hell tipped you off? Was it the suitcases and the fact that I’m heading to the airport!?” Snow scowls... Snow: “I swear that you damn peasants get a lower IQ by the minute!” Cab Driver: “Whoa, whoa! Hey, I didn’t mean nothin’ by it, bub! I was just tryin’ to make some conversation!” Snow: “And what, by Greatness, gave you the idea that I, the Killin’ Villain, would fancy a conversation with the likes of you, plebe!?” Cab Driver: “Alright, fine! Have it your way! It’s a long drive to the airport and I was just tryin’ to be polite! Kids! Sheesh!” Snow glares at the Driver’s image in the rear view mirror through squinted eyes... The Cab Driver, an older man with grey hair and a dark, wrinkled face, continues to drive on while shaking his head in disappointment toward Snow’s attitude. As much as we feel for the Driver, we can’t help but be happy to see that very little has changed about the former champion. Even his looks are identical - only more vibrant. Indeed, the month off seems to have served the young superstar well. His eyes, cold blue and seemingly made of steel, have regained their youth. When we last saw Snow, just over a month ago, those eyes seemed tired and worn. But now, after having a month to “recover from surgery”, they seem as youthful as ever. Snow also looks his age for the first time in several months. Usually, when someone came upon Snow for the first time, they would swear that he was a man of at least thirty years of age. But now, as we watch him in the back of the cab, he looks like a man in his early-to-mid twenties again - like when he first entered PWA nearly one year ago. The only real change that Snow has made about his appearance is his hair - it’s a little shorter now. It’s left untied today, and hangs nearly to his shoulders. Other than that, everything seems relatively the same. Snow has obviously been keeping himself in the peak condition that we’re used to seeing him in, though we can only see his bare chest and abs between the open sides of his black leather jacket. And indeed, his attire is unchanging as well. Those familiar jet black leather pants and Snake Skin boots are present... Snow is back... Snow: “Kids? Are you implying that I, the Killin’ Villain, am a mere peasant child!?” It took Snow a moment to think of a response, but now, he seems pleased with his words. In the rearview mirror, we can see the cab driver roll his brown eyes... Cab Driver: “Yeah, you’re just like all the rest of them... ungrateful little bastards.”Snow: “Ungrateful? UNGRATEFUL!? Why, by Greatness, I’ll have you know that I am a very grateful bast - er, I mean, a very grateful Superior Being! Why, one might even say that I am a Greatful Superior Being!” The Cab Driver doesn’t seem to understand... Cab Driver: “Huh?” Snow: “Oh! For the love of Greatness! I should have known that a peasant like you couldn’t comprehend the brilliance that is spewing forth from my mouth!” Cab Driver: “Whoa! Now, wait just a damn minute, bub! What the hell is all this ‘Greatness’ and ‘Peasant’ stuff? Huh? Cuz, for your information, bub, I’m gettin’ a little bit sick of it here!” Snow: “Have you no knowledge of who I am!?” Cab Driver: “As far as I’m concerned, bub, you’re just another number who’s paying a pretty hefty price to be driven for about a hundred miles.” Snow: “I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it!” Snow, as always, is quick to identify himself. It doesn’t work, however, when the person he’s being identified to isn’t a wrestling fan... Cab Driver: “Huh? Is that supposed to mean something to me, bub?” Snow: “Yes, BUB! That should mean everything to you! Why, by Greatness, the mere fact that you didn’t bow to me at first site is an insult to my Greatness and Superiority!” Cab Driver: “Well, I’ll say somethin’ for ya - you’re definitely the most interesting person that I’ve ever driven... that don’t mean I like ya.” Snow: “Liking me is beside the point! You’re supposed to worship me!” The Cab Driver rolls his eyes and repeats an earlier insult... Cab Driver: “Hmph! Kids!” Snow is ready for it this time... Snow: “Hmph! Peasants!” We catch the Cab Driver glaring at Snow in the rearview mirror. Snow, of course, returns the glare. In this tense moment, full of conflict, we can’t help but think how refreshing it is to have the Killin’ Villain back in PCW. Sure, he’ll be insulting everyone and everything, but in the current state of PCW, would that be such a bad thing? As Snow smirks sarcastically at the Driver, the scene fades to black... *** *** *** We find ourselves back in the office-type building where Death had previously been interviewed. Now, however, a man who seems to be Death’s complete opposite. He short, quite chubby, with a balding grey head and he’s wearing a sweater vest over a white, button down, shirt. Nervously, this unknown man repeatedly slides his glasses from the end of his nose up to his eyes, and within seconds, the fall back down. He begins to speak... Man: “Jason Snow? Yeah, sure, I remember that little bastar - err... I mean, err... Yes, Jason Snow was a troubled child.” The man seems to be unable to control his urges as we can see by the way he fidgets in the red leather recliner. After pushing his glasses back into place once more, the man continues... Man: “And he was trouble when I had him at Georgeville High School too! You see, I’m the principal there - Ralph Hatt.” Ralph Hatt, Jason Snow’s old high school principal. We can’t help but wonder if Jason Snow himself is part of the cause for Ralph’s constant fidgetting... Hatt: “He came to me in... oh, what was? 1990? Ninety-one? Something like that. He was twelve or thirteen at the time. I don’t know, what is he now? Twenty-three? Almost twenty-four? Well, that would make him...” The principal is a strange man, to be sure. After trying to count Snow’s age, minus the amount of years that have gone by, Ralph decides to move on with the inquisition... Hatt: “That boy was nothing but trouble from the first time I saw him. And I don’t mean trouble in the traditional sense - pranks and what not. No... not Jason motherf(CENSORED)king Snow!” The principal seems as surprised by his words as we are. He clears his throat before continuing... Hatt: “He was a dangerous kid - had to keep an eye on him constantly. Never know when the little f(CENSORED)k... err... disturbed child... was going to lose it on some poor kid! Or worse yet, a teacher!” Hatt is shaking now, and his middle aged face is growing redder by the second... Hatt: “And it’s not like he was a stupid little f(CENSORED)ker either... err... I mean... err.... not like he was a slow learning child either. He was bright enough - I’ll give that to him. But he always used his intellect for evil - never for good! And the ‘Superior’ this, ‘Greatness’ that talk... it was enough to drive a man INSANE! Do you know, that before I met Jason motherf(CENSORED)king Snow, I had a full head of luscious blond hair, I was six feet tall, and had a cock that could make Madonna drool! Err... I mean...” While the principal tries to find a way around his latest slip of the tongue, the camera man decides that we’ve heard all we need to and the scene slowly fades to black... *** *** *** April 7, 2002 - 5:45 pm Spring Fling is on the horizon - Snow has never worked a Spring Fling before. And when tonight’s all said and done, he will still have never wrestled one. But when we see the look in Jason Snow’s blue eyes while he stares at the arena - we know that he plans to make an impact tonight. ...The Arrowhead Pond... It’s not the first time he’s been here, but it’s more special than before. Everything about wrestling seems to have regained that sweet scent that it had when he had first taken up the sport. Everything is the way it’s supposed to be, he thinks... well, almost... The air in California can be dry at times, and Snow has never been to fond of it. The southern parts of the United States are usually his least favorite to visit, and this day is no exception. As much as he is looking forward to tonight’s event, he can’t help but wish it were held somewhere else... something about the look of the place has always left a bad taste in his mouth. Maybe it’s the way there are so few real trees... just pom trees... he hates pom trees. Someone is coming. Quickly, the Killin’ Villain dips down and out of sight behind a green Mazda in the parking lot. As the steps get closer, we realize that they belong to none other than Mark Malloy! Quickly, Snow jumps up from his hiding place, rushes up to the former PCW commentator, and grabs him by the arm... Snow: “Peasant!” Malloy is shocked and startled beyond his own belief. In a shaking voice he screams... Malloy: “Don’t hurt me!” Almost on instinct, the young PCW employee shields his terrified face with his hands. Snow gives him an odd look... Snow: “What the hell is the matter with you, peasant!?” Malloy is silent for a moment, and then repeats the word... Malloy: “Peasant? ... ... Snow? Is that you?” Snow: “Why in the hinges of hell don’t you take a look for yourself... unless, of course, you’re finally willing to admit that you are in fact a closeted homosexual. In that case, the mere sight of me will PROBABLY cause you to ORGASM! And then, I would be forced to brutally murder for traumatizing me for the rest of what-would-be my slightly less Superior days.” Snow’s words take a moment for Malloy to digest - but that’s just further indication that it truly is the same old Jason Snow. He tends to confuse people at times with his egotistical babbling. Malloy stammers... Malloy: “W-where did you come from? Does management know you’re here?” Snow: “Hell no! Of course not! No one knows I’m here! Why, by Greatness, that would ruin the surprise!” Malloy: “What surprise?” Snow: “You think I’m going to tell you? A mere peasant? You think I’ll tell you all my Superior plans to reclaim my rightful place at the very top of PC... er... forget I said that peasant!” Malloy: “Snow, you’re being ridiculous! Calm down!” Snow: “How can I calm down, peasant? How can I - in all my Superior Greatness - calm down when tonight is the beginning of a new era in PCW!?” Malloy: “Snow, what are you talking about?” From the grin on Snow’s face, we can tell that Malloy will soon get his answer - whether he likes it or not. As the sun goes down in Anaheim, Snow looks up at the arena and the scene fades to black... *** *** *** Mark Malloy sits nervously in the same chair, chewing his fingernails and avoiding eye contact with the camera... Malloy: “No! No way! I’m not saying anything about him! No way, no how! You know that I get in trouble every time I open my mouth about that basta- I mean, that... uh... uh.... OH NO!!!” With that, Mark Malloy bursts out of the room, leaving us staring at the empty chair. It’s been an interesting look at Jason Snow’s return to PCW... but we’re all left with the same question... what’s next?
