Making Love!?!?

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 (The scene opens with Kid Krazie and his inanimate friend Luna Luvedick, the shopping cart, walking into a crummy, run down, awfully cheap one bed one bath Apartment in the Jersey Village area of Houston, Texas. Krazie puts his new key in the lock and turns it until he hears the affirming click that tells him the door is now unlocked and ready to be opened.) Krazie: “Well Lu-Lu, do you want me to carry you across the threshold?” Lu-Lu: “We aren’t married Krazie!” (As Lu-Lu tells Krazie they aren’t married, he picks the cart up and kicks the door open. As the door swings open his eyes go wide in amazement and he walks across the threshold carrying the semi heavy shopping cart.) Lu-Lu: “Ok… You can put me down now!” (Krazie sets the shopping cart down and takes his time basking in the glory of the wonderful apartment.) Krazie: “WOW!” (Krazie looks from the living room to the breakfast nook, from the kitchen to the door that leads to the bedroom. His eye begins to tear as he stands there looking around.) Lu-Lu: “No offense but Kroger Island was WWWAAAYYYYYY better than this!” (Krazie looks to the shopping cart with pain in his eyes and a combination of anger and confusion displayed on his face. His shoulders slump as he just stands there looking from his apartment to the cart.) Krazie: “Well, it’s my first apartment so get off my nuts!” (Krazie storms off to check the place out a bit more closely. Lu-Lu meanwhile rolls her way to the living room noticing a rather large rat scurry across the living room floor to a hole in the far wall. She then rolls her way to the kitchen noticing a small blood stain surrounding the complementary refrigerator. She finally rolls her way to what the complex calls the breakfast nook.) Lu-Lu (To herself) “This is what they call a breakfast nook? All it is is the corner of the living room near the window!” (Lu-Lu now makes her way rolling towards the bedroom. As she rolls by the door pushing it open enough for her to roll on in. If she had eyes they would pop open in shock as she is met with the sight of a naked Kid Krazie laying spread eagle trying to look as sexy as he can with his eyes locking in on the cart.) Krazie: “Come her you sexy little thing you!” (Now I’m going to have to fade this scene out because I am down right disturbed!!! But the good news is the scene is only faded for a few seconds!) Krazie: “Oh yeah!” (As the scene re-opens Krazie is laying back on the bed with a cigarette in his mouth and Lu-Lu laying on her side next to Krazie. They are both under the covers as Krazie enjoys the smoke and Lu-Lu enjoys whatever a shopping cart would enjoy after sex…) Lu-Lu: “…WOW… Simply… WOW…” (Krazie smiles and nods his head in a bit of agreement and a bit of satisfaction.) Krazie: “I’d have to agree…” (Lu-Lu proceeds to do a little rendition of ‘Get Your Freak On’ by Missy Elliott featuring Nelly Furtado. As Lu-Lu sings Krazie begins to do a little dance, he begins bumping and grinding while lying down on the bed.) Lu-Lu: “Getcha, getcha, getcha, freak on!” (Krazie rolls out of bed and begins to put his clothes on as Lu-Lu rolls her happy cart ass off the bed as well. The two make their way to the living room where they decide where the furniture is going to go.) Lu-Lu: “Ok, I could see the couch right here, and the coffee table right over there and maybe those drapes we saw, hang them right there…” (Krazie nods his head and makes his way outside to fetch the furniture. He comes back with a couch full of holes, missing cushions, and the unmistakable odor of urine!) Krazie: “Where did you want this???” (Lu-Lu rolls to the area where she wants the couch and Krazie walks over to meet her and then drops it causing a dust cloud come from the impact. Krazie begins to cough as he goes back out to fetch the rest of the furniture.) ***TWENTY MINUTES LATER*** (Krazie stands in the middle of the room where there is a fire barrel burning. Lu-Lu is looking around checking the room out. There in front of the couch are two plastic crates with wooden planks on top of them, some burnt drapes covering the windows and some broken, chipped and over used dishes in the cabinets.) Lu-Lu: “Welcome to our home!” (Krazie looks around and nods his head. He then walks over to the couch and takes a seat as Lu-Lu rolls over sits next to him on the couch. The shopping cart has some envelopes in it’s front basket as Krazie picks them up and checks them out.) Krazie: “Happy Resident, Whoever it May Concern, You Just Won a Million Dollars, Mr. Krazie, and lastly another Happy Resident.” (Krazie opens the one addressed to Mr. Krazie and as he reads it, his eyes pop wide. After reading the letter he jumps up and begins dancing around the apartment. Lu-Lu meanwhile looks over at the letter that Krazie dropped; she is also surprised and delighted.) Lu-Lu: (To herself) “Dear Mr. Krazie: It is our pleasure to inform you that you will get a shot at the PCW Rising Star championship. Sunday, April 28, 2002, you will take on current PCW Rising Star champion Andy Murray. Good Luck,PCW Management (Out of nowhere Lu-Lu is hoisted up by Krazie and is carried away to the bedroom where the scene fades yet again with disturbing sounds emanating from the room.) ***MEANWHILE*** Voice: “I can’t believe it!” (Tiffany Nicole Thomas, Krazie’s old girlfriend and valet, stands in their beach side home in California on the balcony overlooking the ocean. Her face looks sad as she thinks of Kid Krazie.) Tiffany: “Where could you be…” (Tiffany begins to cry as she realizes Krazie is gone for good!) from Houston Texas, weighing in at 215 pounds..........KID KRAZIE!!!!!!! Tiffany: “What the…” (Tiffany turns around and walks inside and looks to the television. There she sees the love of her life Kid Krazie rolling down to the ring in a shopping cart. She looks confused and a little hurt.) Tiffany: “What the…” (The scene fades as Tiffany sits there watching Krazie get beat by Stryfe and the further beating received by Duzza.) ***TO BE CONTINUED***
