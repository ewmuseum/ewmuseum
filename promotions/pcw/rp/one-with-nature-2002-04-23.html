One with nature

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 23, 2002 “Strange that so few ever come to the woods to see how the pine lives and grows and spires, lifting its evergreen arms to the light, -- to see its perfect success; but most are content to behold it in the shape of many broad boards brought to market, and deem that its true success.” -Henry David Thoreau But the pine is no more lumber than man is, and to be made into boards and houses is no more its true and highest use than the truest use of a man is to be cut down and made into manure. Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart, collectively known as The Heartbreakers are experiencing this dilemma as we speak today. They are at this very beginning stage in the woods, just as Thoreau speaks of. Their careers could go in two different directions, they could be made into the strong boards and houses just as the strong pine was, or they could be cut down into manure, never to be heard from again. There is a higher law affecting our relation to pines as well as to men. A pine cut down, a dead pine, is no more of a pine than a pine in the tree. Both of the pines look the same, act the same, even smell the same, but one of those pines is alive. The career of Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart as a successful tag team is still alive. As long as they stay alive, and fight off the “evil lumberjacks” that are trying to end their reign as supreme beings, they will remain alive. The camera fades in on a large campground where a red and a pink tent sit perfectly side by side, set up without a crease or hitch. Out of the two tents almost simultaneously appears Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart, the Heartbreakers! They both have on brightly colored robes with hearts imposed on both of them. After breakfasting on heart-shaped pancakes, they returned down Pine Stream on their way to Monona Lake, which was about five miles distant. They could see something that caught both of their attentions in Pine Stream when nearly half a mile off. Just below the mouth of this stream were the most considerable rapids between the two bodies of water, called Heartbreaker Falls, where were large flat rocks washed smooth, and at this time you couldeasily wade across above them. “Man oh man that is beautiful,” Jack uttered, admiring the beauty of the water. “You sure are right Jack…” Johnny responded. Jack looked over at Johnny at Johnny was glaring down at a pocket-sized mirror that he had pulled out along the walk to fix his hair. After walking for what seemed to be an eternity, they finally arrived at the stream. Jack ran down alone while Johnny walked over the portage, still glancing at himself in the mirror in a very boyishly humorous manner. Near the Lake were islands, and a low and meadowy shore with scattered trees, birches, white and yellow, slanted over the water, and maples, -- many of the white birches killed apparently by inundations. There was considerable native grass; and even a few butterflies – whose movements we heard, though we did not see them, mistaking them at first for bees -- were flying there. Jack waded around in the water for awhile, flailing his arms as if he were fighting someone… “Come on Hackenmann…let’s go Creeper!” Jack screamed. Johnny just stared over at the seemingly crazy Valentine and then went right back to looking at himself in the mirror. Not before long, the screaming started up again. “Pailthorpe, Crichton…I’ll show you who’s FORGOTTEN!!!” Jack shrieked. “What the hell is the matter with you Jack? We’re supposed to be on vacation. PCW has given us a week off and all you can think about is our matches this week…take your mind off of that and think of something more important,” Johnny responded. On command, Johnny smiled and went right back to parting his seemingly perfect hair. Jack was in the water practicing kicks and punches as if he was in a real match. Soon enough a loud, girlish scream is heard and Johnny jerks his head once again to see what the matter is. “RUN! RUN! It’s a SHARK!!!” Jack helplessly screamed. As Jack ran out of the water and tried sprinting by Johnny, Johnny placed his mirror gently on the ground and grabbed Jack by the throat as he flew by him. “Jack…” Johnny quietly said. A panicked Jack didn’t know what to say, and all he kept screaming was “SHARK! SHARK!” “Jack…why don’t you just calm down…BECAUSE THERE ARE NO SHARKS IN THIS WATER!!!” Johnny screamed. “Oh yeah? Why don’t you get in there then?” Jack sarcastically stated. “Fine, I will,” Johnny replied. Johnny ripped off his clothes and sprinted down to the water and hopped in. A nervous Valentine then ran down after him and tried to pull him out but was unsuccessful. “Would you relax Jack, there is nothing to worry about…you see, sharks have to live in salt water and this…” Johnny splashes some water in Jack’s face. “…THIS ISN’T SALT WATER!” Johnny screamed. Jack jumps back and sprints off of the dock once again. “What, what is it?” Johnny questioned. “It’s back! It’s back! I saw it!” He screeched. Johnny looks around in the water for whatever Jack was talking about. All of the sudden he gets set as if he is going to catch something. “What are you doing? You’re not going to try and catch it…are you?” Johnny nervously inquired. “Shhhh,” Johnny said. Johnny got down in a squat like position and pretended to wrestle around with an imaginary object for awhile. After a few seconds he became bored and reached his giant hand down in the water only to pull out a small, miniscule minnow. “Oh my GOD!” Jack screamed. “Is this what you were crying about Jack?” Johnny interrogated. “Yes, yes…be careful, it’s still moving!” Jack shrieked. “Jack…this isn’t a shark,” Johnny started, “It’s a FRICKIN minnow!” A puzzled look came across the face of Jack Valentine and then he took a big gulp. “Oh…I knew all along, I was just testing you,” Jack said as he tried to cover the situation up. “Uh huh, I hope you’re not this much of a pussy Tuesday night when we face the fear freaks, and then Friday when we face the forgotten ones,” Johnny said. “Pussy? Who are you calling a pussy? You’re the one that had to wrestle around with a minnow for a long time just before you could get it. I could have had that thing in seconds,” Jack mockingly stated. “I was kidding Jack, can’t you take a joke, or is that little heart shaped brain of yours not big enough?” Johnny jokingly stated. “Very funny…very funny. We’ll see who will have the last laugh Tuesday night, after I hit the Heart Attack on Hackenmann and the Mason freak!” Jack said. “You won’t be attacking any hearts until I kick them into a ‘Dis-Heartening’ position,” Johnny replied. The pair argued for hours about who would get the pinfall on Turmoil against Fear Factory. After listing nearly every maneuver each one of them had in their repertoire they became tired and headed back to the campground. As the sunset in the background, the pair finally agreed that they need to work together. They walked with their arms draped across the others shoulders and as the sun faded out, the unlikely city-boys became one with nature…and they were still wearing their robes imprinted with hearts.
