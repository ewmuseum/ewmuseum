Gaz 'Insert Gimmick' Green

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30 March 2003 This next caper involving Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester and Vampire coalition opens up in the bustling City of Las Vegas Nevada. Far from the smog induced streets and hypnotic lights this new chapter is high in the clouds, more specifically, the Jester’s penthouse apartment. The gaping glass-panelled windows invite the radiant sun much to the grunts and groans of the teams colossal behemoth, the ever trench coat clad vampire Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. “You have a nerve Green!” he hissed in aggravation as he adjusted his tainted sunglasses in a fidgeting manner. The tangible smell of polish filtered annoyingly through the team’s base of operations and everything on show glistened with a twinkling effect for whom ever cleaned obviously took great pride in their work. The Jester was nowhere in sight hence the resistance of a retort but the City Of Sin had this week lost a son in Jon Kano who retired thus nestling the weight of its wrestling roots on the shoulders of Gaz Green. “Is there any reason why I should be here?” grunted the goliath. His aggravation was far from subsiding as he grinded his teeth almost to the gum line, his two piercing canines drooping over his lower lip. The vampire’s mountain of flesh remained rooted into the leather reclined chair, the very same throne used by the Emerald Menace. It is from this perch that the mastermind orchestrates his devious doings and in recent memory he has sat there in thought with only two persons on his mind, be it Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. The besotted couple have became the catalyst to Pranks With Attitudes misery inducing antics and have been the butt of their jokes, the darker side of comedy. “So this is married life?” The distinctive voice being none other than the green tailored Jester who waltzes into view with a cocky strut. Rotating the chair the burning red eyes of Nerezza are brandished as he lowers his sunglasses over the bridge of his nose so he may gaze over the source of these murmurs. “And how is the other one taking it?” questioned the inquisitive bloodsucker smugly for his bitter taste towards Natalia Miyagawa had been well documented since her untimely arrival. The Soviet Scarlet use had grown thin in the mind of the Living Nightmare, her presence merely an award for the intervention she played in securing the teams second Tag Team Championship reign. With a smile etched along his angelic face the Jester happily retorted, “As well as can be expected, maybe I should invite them both for a bit of three’s company action!” The pranksters remark light hearted enough to warrant the curl of his associate’s lips but before a smile had became apparent the Angel Of Fear had already quenched his facial expression and inquisitively asked about the reasons behind his presence. “I would appreciate it if you can get right to the point and tell me what is so damn important that it couldn’t wait until tonight?” Gaz rubbed his hands in glee, his smile murmuring a hidden agenda but the suspense he had generated was enough for even he to spill the beans and his mouth soon gained momentum. “It struck me like lightening the other night. We have been in Primetime Championship Wrestling for eight months solid and haven’t once adorned singles gold and I think I know why?” Nerezza merely stared wide-eyed with keen interest as the Motor Mouth Gaz Green continued. “Our gimmicks. I know you have this ‘Grrrrrrrr’ going on but that isn’t what the fans are looking for, believe me. So like a think tank I brain stormed a few ideas and thought I’d run them by you before we unveil them live on Primetime television…” “REALLY?” choked the Spawn Of Evil in a dumbfounded manner. Nerezza put his talons over his face in disgust, shaking his head he appeared like an airline passenger going through an emergency landing procedure, something that his partner caught hold of. Like a child on Christmas day the Jester retraced his steps almost tripping up on the step that leads to the bedroom quarters of the dwelling and vanishing through a door with a deafening thud. Soon following suit the trench coat clad giant strolled up the two stairs towards the last seen whereabouts of his fellow Pranks With Attitude partner and patiently waited for his re-emergence. Suddenly the door swung ajar and standing in the midst of an uncontrollable giggle was the Lord Of Laughter showcasing a long fur Norseman’s cloak and a skull fronted facial mask, similar attire to that of Old School Entertainment’s Hessian. “What do you think?” asked the masquerading Jester. “You look… Like… Like an idiot!!!” replied the Creature Of The Night trying to recompose himself with deep breaths but the giggles continued to seep from the corners of his cracked crimson red lips. In a huff the door slammed shut and the sound of rummaging soon followed. A few minutes elapsed and the silence was soon erupted by the entrance of one masquerading Gaz Green, for now the prankster stood wearing a spandex costume with a Jester insignia and long flowing cape coming off to built up shoulder pads. “Let me guess SUPER JESTER~!” joked the vampire as he enjoyed the catwalk portrayal his partner was putting on. “Actually I was thinking more towards Rent-A-Jester!!!” “I have all the necessary things to make the transition, my Jester sense, my super kick, my electrical touch…” concluded the Prince Of Pranks towards Nerezza who idly stands by shaking his head in a ‘No’ fashion. Again the green tailored comic vanished into his bedroom enclosure only for the sound of rummaging to filter but this time the clattering of metal could be heard as one ting after another echoed. Emerging rather embarrassingly Gaz Green came out to the onward glares of his vampire accomplice wearing pieces of discarded metal over his upper body and atop of his legs. The Living Nightmare hissed a giggle then it transcended to a laugh before escalating into a full-blown howl of hysterics as Gaz Green stood in full portrayal of his new gimmick. “Err… Arr… What’s so funny? I thought Gazimus Prime had real potential” “Wait I have another…” With that said the four-time Tag Team Champion again disappeared into his private suite for a number of minutes. Missing for a length of time the door finally opened and holding a catalogue pose looked to be an extra from a George Romero movie for now the King Of Komedy was clad in cut pieces of dirty fabric and courtesy of make-up his complexion rivalled that of Nerezzas. “THAT’S ORIGINAL~! A down and out, I can see Clay introducing you now… Hailing from ‘The Gutter’ famished and unfed Gaz ‘The Tramp’ Green!” injected the sired vampire triumphantly. A few moments passed before the Jester finally retorted solemnly. “Actually I’m a zombie. I thought it was original and I can’t recall one ever-gracing Wrestle Online and it can’t be too difficult to pull off. And so the era of GARETH~! begins. Like what do you think?” “How does don’t give up your day job sound?” replied the pale complexioned night dweller as Gaz bowed his head in shame only to vanish once more, crashing the wooden door behind him. With the Jester concealed within his bedroom Nerezza used his time wisely and made a quick exit, treading lightly over the laminate flooring and closing the door behind himself the apartment crept into an eerie silence. With a thunderous crash the bedroom door once again swung open unveiling yet another gimmick possibility as the Jester motioned his head; obviously looking for the whereabouts of his business associate who as we know has since departed. “Rezz?!?!?” Looking stupid in his iron pressed Armani business suit, Tommy Gun at hand and cigar limping from his mouth the Jester’s head swayed in a pendulum motion as he inspected his living quarters for his fiendish friend. With the charade done and dusted the scene slowly faded to black ending another tale of the dynamic duo, Pranks With Attitude. - The End -
