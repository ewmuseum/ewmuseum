The idiot's guide to filling in Immigration forms...

Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was coming to grips with the sad realisation that The Spice Girls were no more.Gone…. Finished….. Washed up…. Stranded in ‘Has-Been’ Land. Thus Flaymin is on a mission.Not just any ordinary mission- but a critical assignment necessary for the salvation and longevity of bubblegum pop forevermore!!! In other words- Flaymin is going to London to re-unite The Spice Girls…. [Scene opens at Heathrow Airport in merry ol’ England, a country famous for…. Great weather… nope, that’s not it….Great teeth…. Uh not quite…Excellent sportsmen…. *shakes head* Ah, Merry ol’ England- Home to the finest, drunkest, toothless, mentally challenged Football Hooligans on earth!! Flaymin is shown wheeling his bag off the plane and onto the tarmac below, all the while staring at a map in his hand. He walks over to a flight attendant and taps her on the shoulder] Flaymin: Excuse me miss, but can you tell me which way England is?Because I can’t find this ‘Heathrow’ anywhere on this map. [The Flight Attendant gives Flaymin a strange stare before smiling warmly] Flight Attendant: You must be the drunk in seat 24B? [Flaymin misinterprets this as being a discussion about bra sizes and reacts accordingly] Flaymin: 24B, are you kidding me?36DD is the way its meant to be! [This time the Flight Attendant raises an eyebrow before walking off in disgust. Flaymin merely shrugs his shoulders and continues walking into the Airport terminal, wheeling his bag behind him. Inside the terminal, he walks over to the Passport and Immigration check-in and begins to fill out the generic form required on entry into a foreign country] Flaymin: ‘Name’. Ah, that’s an easy one. ‘Date of Birth’ Geez, this thing is a breeze. ‘Gender’ Done! ‘Country of Origin’ Orange blows, I’ll select lemon. ‘Purpose of visit’ Pop Music Salvation ‘Occupation’ Deep Sea Diver ‘Do you have a criminal record?’ [Flaymin’s frequent stints in prison and his numerous run-ins with the law all begin to flash before his eyes as he grins with pride. Having completed the form, he walks over to the counter and hands it into the passport officer, who is an obese middle-aged woman with an apparent lack of charisma. The passport officer looks over the document and circles the question relating to ‘Gender’] Officer: You filled this in incorrectly. Flaymin: Yeah? Officer: You’re supposed to circle either ‘F’ or ‘M’. Flaymin: What did I put? Officer: You wrote ‘No thanks, I prefer AM Radio’. Flaymin: I wrote that?What on earth was I thinking! Everyone knows AM Radio sucks major monkey balls!! Thanks for the heads up. Officer: No, you don’t get i… ah screw it, I’ll do it for you. Flaymin: Thanks doll face! [The mean looking woman looks up at Flaymin with a blank expression on her face] Flaymin: Smile!You should know that I’m about to save pop music from eternal damnation! [The woman shakes her head in confusion and then moves on to the next question] Officer: Country of origin? Flaymin: No thanks, I prefer Sprite over Fanta. [The frustrated officer spells out the question for Flaymin to understand] Officer: O-R-I-G-I-N!! Flaymin: S-P-R-I-T-E!Do I have to spell out everything for you? Officer: Fine, Spain is close enough to Sprite…. Congratulations you’re now a Spaniard. Flaymin: Cool! Officer: Under ‘Purpose of Visit’, what do you mean by Pop Music Salvation? Flaymin: I have been self-appointed as the saviour of Girl Power! Officer: Who? What? Where? Flaymin: I’ll be resurrecting The Spice Girls and getting them back on their feet again! Officer: Those ho’s are never on their feet- they’re either on all fours or on their back with their legs in the air. [Flaymin looks at the Officer in surprise] Flaymin: Really?Why would they be doing that? Officer: Read in between the lines, honey. [Flaymin looks around in bewilderment] Flaymin: What lines?Can you just stamp my damn passport already! Officer: Easy there sonny, we have a few more questions to sort out. [Flaymin rolls his eyes and sighs] Officer: Are you really a deep-sea diver? Flaymin: Pornstar actually.But I spend so much of my time down below with limited air available, that I like to think of myself as snorkelling through the whole ordeal. Officer: That’s so disgusting, I’m not even going to ask you the final question! Flaymin: You don’t care that I have a criminal record? Officer: Are you kidding?Everyone here has a criminal record.At first we’d just ship them to that shit hole known as Australia, but we can’t do that anymore…. Flaymin: Damn Austrians! [The woman smiles and stamps Flaymin’s passport] Officer: Congratulations, you’re now free to tour England! [Flaymin takes his passport and walks over to the baggage carousel to pick up his bags as the scene fades to black] In the next instalment;The Search for The Spice Girls begins!!
