Final Preparations

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Final Preparations Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 31st Game time. No, it's not just the name of an Evan Levine finisher. It is a period of time, existing just before a big battle, that is used to psych up, in order to fight one's best. Twenty some odd men are battling inside of a school later this evening. For all of them, it is indeed 'Game Time'. In the growing organization that is Politically Incorrect, only one man is getting up for 'Game Time.' Reginald V. Lovecraft is preparing with his heart and soul for what he knows will be his toughest match. With him are William S. Powell, his tag team partner, Katherine Willis, his intern, and Mr. Louis Brown, his newly hired attorney. Awaiting their arrival at the arena is Roger Schultz, William S. Powell's campaign manager. Schultz realizes the opportunity before Mr. Lovecraft is immense. However, he also realizes that should Reginald become PCW Extreme Champion, he WILL have to deal with William S. Powell. While Reginald and William are good friends and tag partners, they are also extremely competitive. The odds of Powell challenging a victorious Lovecraft would be extremely high. Schultz has always had to reign in Powell's renowned temper. Now, with professional jealousy one huge Lovecraft triumph away, Roger walks a slippery slope. Can he keep things calm? Only time shall tell Meanwhile, in the Politically Incorrect limo, the foursome guarentees victory... ::Surrounded in lovely black opulence, the fearsome foursome that is Politically Incorrect rushes to the arena. Technically, they are a fatal fivesome, but Roger Schultz isn't there, hence the fearsome foursome moniker. Lovecraft, Brown, Powell, and Willis all sit in the luxury vehicle's confines, doing their best to prepare Reginald for the war of his life.:: William S. Powell: Are you ready, Reginald? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Absolutely, William! It is my time to shine! William S. Powell: Well, Reginald...I fully expect first crack at that title when you win it. ::William stares intently into his partner's eyes, in essence throwing down a gauntlet.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Be my guest. A little friendly competition never hurt anyone. ::Sensing the tense atmosphere in the limo, Katherine Willis decides to try and lessen the tension.:: Katherine Willis: So...would you like to tell Mr. Powell what happened in the Emperor's Suite? ::She giggles, leans over, and places a sensous kiss on Lovecraft's lips.:: William S. Powell: No need. I already know. ::Both Katherine and Reginald turn to William, glaring daggers.:: William S. Powell: I was in the closet of the Emperor's Suite that night...and I had a video camera set up. I watched the tape. Katherine and Reginald: YOU WHAT?! William S. Powell: I watched the tape. And, I must say, for a virgin, you held your own quite well with Katherine, Mr. Lovecraft.. Katherine Willis: I'll say...I've never had so much fun in a bedroom before! Reginald V. Lovecraft: TELL ME YOU DIDN'T SEND THE TAPE TO FUCKING C-SPAN! William S. Powell: I burned it. I just couldn't send it in. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Thank you...although, we WILL be having a conversation about this later. Mr. Louis Brown: So, THIS is Mr. William S. Powell? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes. William, this is Mr. Louis Brown, our new attorney. ::William and Louis shake hands.:: Mr. Louis Brown: It is an honor to meet you! William S. Powell: Likewise. We shall have much use for you in our battles with the Ultimate Authority...and speaking of that, have the needed calls been made? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes indeed, Mr. Powell! Katherine Willis: What are you two talking about? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Nothing, my dear. Just some plans that will come to fruition this evening at Fusion. Katherine Willis: Will they help my Reginald become PCW Extreme Champion? William S. Powell: No, Katherine, because that is an objective Reginald needs no help for! Look at him! 6'7', 299 lbs. of raw power, muscle, and Southern charm.He is the biggest member of Politically Incorrect, and as such, it will take much more than a few paltry trash can blows to keep Mr. Lovecraft off of his feet! ::Lovecraft smiles, flexing his considerable muscles under his crimson suit.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I am not an easy man to topple. I will battle with everything I have inside of me...all the passion, anger, rage, and emotion this Southern gentleman can muster. In the South, school is indeed out. Now, it is time to show that...as this southerner escapes from old, current, new, whatever school you want to call it, and becomes PCW Extreme Champion! :As he finishes speaking, the limousine pulls into the Teaneck arena. It slowly rolls to a stop, as Roger Schultz throws open the doors, welcoming his comrades to the arena. As he does so, he takes William S. Powell by the cufflinks, and takes him off to the side.:: Roger Schultz: William, if Reginald does win the Extreme title tonight, you have no intention of going after it, correct? ::Powell smirks.:: William S. Powell: On the contrary...I made him promise to give me a shot at it! :He smiles as Roger Schultz shakes his head.:: Roger Schultz: YOU CAN'T! The Round Robin tournament is still going on! You two have to be on the top of your game if you want to win! You saw what miscommunication did when you lost to the Prices! William S. Powell: BUT I WANT A SINGLES TITLE SHOT! REGINALD GOT ONE! ::Schultz is reaching his breaking point.:: Roger Schultz: AND SO WOULD YOU IF YOU WOULD HAVE BEEN AROUND TO SIGN UP, RATHER THAN OGLING WOMEN AT A STRIP BAR AFTER LOSING TO THE PRICES! ::For the first time, Powell realizes the logic in Roger's statements.:: William S. Powell: You know, you're right...I've been acting like an ass. Roger Schultz: There's plenty of time for you to get a singles championship. You're in your twenties. Reginald may not have many more opportunities. William S. Powell: I think I'll go wish him good luck. Roger Schultz: You go do that. ::William runs off to the Politically Incorrect dressing room.:: Roger Schultz: That boy may be a bit odd...but he has a good heart. Reginald, I know you can't hear me, but, good luck. Fade to.. Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect!Lovecraft is READY. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
