Solar League of Celestial Oracles...

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/02 There is no air, yet one can breathe. There is no light, yet one can see. A dead calm, a silent vacuum blacker than empty space. In fact, there is no day, nor no night, just one never-ending absence of light and time. Time stands still, having no meaning and no effect. It is with that brief description that a mind as feeble as that of a human begins to get an elementary understanding the true essence of the Celestial Realm. It is nothing, and yet it is everything. It is but empty chaos on its own. But the beauty of the Celestial Realm is that it shapes itself to the viewer’s perception. But to the three Oracles Kenshiro, Rumiko, and Sho, they all see the same thing, a Middle Eastern palace from the latter half of the first millenium, AD. This is but an arbitrary form, it could be anything. The Oracles are here for a summit, if one could call it that. The oracles are gathering from all around to discuss the issue of The Sleeping Dragon… The End. There are Sho, Kenshiro, and Rumiko as representatives of their territory. Which territory? Earth. Each planet in the solar system, along with three moons, have sent representatives to this summit. Even the oracles agree that they haven’t the slightest idea what to do, they hope to come up with some sort of plan. And following that introduction we enter upon the conversation of the three Earth oracles as they walk (again, the walking path a purely arbitrary device and design). Kenshiro:“So… why are we here?” Rumiko:“A forum of some sort.” Sho:“We are to decide a definitive course of action concerning The End.” The Oracles step through the large golden doors on the front façade of the freshly painted Taj Mahal (which is the final form that the surrounding area of the Celestial Realm has taken to suit the comprehension of the Earth Oracles) into a room that seems infinitely expansive in all directions and are greeted by a large balloon. Jupiter:“Greetings, Earth delegates…” Kenshiro:“That balloon just talked!” Sho, walking in front of the others, turns his head back at the winner for the Least Likely Oracle contest… Sho:“You embarrass us, Kenshiro.” Sho turns back to the Jupiter delegate, a large balloon-like creature with four eyes at ninety degree around itself, giving it 360 binocular vision. It has five grasping suction tentacles, and is 60 feet high and 100 feet in diameter. Floating because of the light gases in its body, the creature is indeed an odd, perhaps frightening creature. But it is harmless, a creature that subsists entirely off of water. Jupiter:“It is quite alright, I understand that some Earth creatures are not as open-minded as others. Please, come and introduce yourselves to the other delegates.” The Jupiter delegate ushers them over to a group of beings that seem to pop out of thin air, the Earth Oracles walking but a short distance in the long empty room. Rumiko bows politely to the delegates. The first to approach her, a blue one-eyed spherical creature 2 feet in diameter rolls up to her and stands on one four-toed appendage, shaking her hand with the other. Pluto:“Is this the Rumiko that Sho brags about?” Rumiko is flattered that Sho would speak about her, and in such praise. Kenshiro steps forward. Kenshiro:“Then obviously you’ve heard of me.” The Venus delegates, a school of ten “jellyfish” creatures in a pyramid formation, floats up to Kenshiro and the creatures circle Kenshiro. They just survey him for a moment before coming back to formation and making a sound quite like human laughter. The clear laughter of the Mars delegate, a 10-foot humanoid with dull red skin and elongated limbs, digits, and neck, makes it clear. Mars:“You’re the one that gave away the secrets of the Celestials to the common ice cream man?” Irked and embarrassed, Kenshiro turns to Sho, who is standing back with Rumiko. Kenshiro:“Did you have to tell EVERYONE?” Sho shrugs and nods. Sho:“Yes. Yes, I did.” Mars brings Kenshiro over to meet Mercury delegate, a being of pure energy taking no other form than that of a bright orange wheel of fire rotating on a vertical axis counter-clockwise. It gives off a very intense heat, which would cause most non-metals or Earth to combust or melt; luckily, this is the Celestial Realm and the heat is non-noticeable. The Mercury delegate speaks psionically, as do all creatures in the summit with the exception of Kenshiro, not yet versed in that skill. He is fortunate enough that Celestial Oracles can understand any and all languages. Mercury:“Do not take offense, we merely make comments and light of the situation in jest. There’s been little to laugh about since The End awakened.” Kenshiro: *Disinterested*“So Rumiko and Sho have been pounding into my head.” The Venus and Mercury and Mercury Delegates surround Kenshiro and orbit him in erratic paths, rapidly changing colors through all the visible human spectrum. Mercury and Venus:“THIS IS NOT A TRIVIAL MATTER, THIS PARTICULAR SOLAR SYSTEM, ALONG WITH ALL LIFE IN THE UNIVERSE, COULD BE ANNIHILATED!” Kenshiro consigns himself to the group and remains mostly silent for the rest of the time. Kenshiro:“Okay, okay. Geez.” The Titan delegate, sent from Saturn’s only life-bearing moon, stalks up to the group. It is purple in color, having three digits on all four limbs. The bottom half of this thing resembles that of Roadrunner on Earth, only larger in size since the legs are easily 4 feet long; the torso of this creature is that of a lean though muscular male gorilla, and the head of the creature is that of what could most likely be considered a lion on Earth, though the teeth are very jagged and in rows, stretching all the way back around the head, and the mouth cannot be closed. Mars:“Titan delegate, it is good to see you.” Titan:“Yes, though I would have preferred a meeting under other, less disastrous circumstances.” A voice comes from nowhere across the infinite reaches of space-time. Saturn:“How could you forget about me?” Kenshiro and Rumiko look around, but don’t see anything. The other delegates are all fine, not perplexed (though emotion is hard to detected in the Mercury, Venus, and Pluto delegates. Sho is standing back merely observing the exchanges in greetings, as the summit cannot begin without all the delegates present, and there are still a number that need to show. Pluto:“We could not forget about the Saturn delegate? No, really, how could we?” Rumiko taps Sho on the shoulder, asking about the Saturn delegate. Sho’s star pupil, there are still many things that are a mystery to her. Rumiko:“Sho, where is the Saturn delegate? Does it not have form?” The delegate from Jupiter interjects. Jupiter:“The Saturn delegate has form.” Pluto:“How could it not have form? My child, even though the Celestial Realm shall give us infinite space to suit our needs, you still don’t know how uncomfortable it was for us all the last time the Saturn delegate was here.” Nodding, Sho answers Rumiko. Sho:“The Saturn delegate, to say this simply, is a 9,000-foot-long lava worm that could swallow the Empire State building whole.” Pluto:“And when you get something that big in a room with all the rest of us; I don’t care how big this realm is, we were packed in shoulder to shoulder.” Mars:“So the Saturn delegate attends psionically.” Kenshiro:“Ah.” The front doors, which exist both lightyears away and right up close at the same time, swing open and in step two lizardman creatures, a grayish-blue tint to their scales. They are five feet tall, but possess scaled wings protruding from their backs that reach 9 feet across, though they are folded at the moment. Another noticeable feature of these beings is that they have no eyes. They creatures take a single step forward that lasts an eternity and bow fist in palm to greet the others. Earth’s Moon:“Greetings. Is everyone here yet?” Jupiter:“No, we tarry for Neptune, Uranus, and Io delegates.” Earth’s Moon:“One would imagine that you would come with your own moon’s delegate, Jupiter.” Jupiter:“We are independent entities in government.” Turning back to his pupils, Sho informs them of the latest arrivals. Sho:“The delegates have just arrived from our own moon.” Kenshiro is taken aback by this statement. Kenshiro:“We have life on the moon? With such a pathetically thin atmospheric barrier? How is that possible? Why hasn’t NASA found it?” Sho:“Humans cannot explore the dark side of the moon, which is where these creatures are from.” The Moon’s delegates come over to Kenshiro. Earth’s Moon:“We are actually subterranean creatures that come out to feed by frozen minerals and nutrients that we dig up from the ground. We can’t breathe above ground, must hold our breathes. And with the half of the moon perpertually dark, we evolved out of the use for our eyes, much like the Jupiter delegate is naturallyh positively charged to repel the lightning strikes that blast around it and its kind every moment.” Kenshiro turns around and stares into a large gaping mouth. Kenshiro:“AHHHHHH!!!” Falling back, Kenshiro hears all the delegates laugh again. The Pluto delegate rolls over and helps him up. Pluto:“Don’t worry, that’s just the Neptune delegate, it likes to scare new faces like that.” The Neptune delegate, a 30-foot-long greenish creature that is a crosses the armor and pincers of a scorpion with the form and head of a shark, floats in front of Kenshiro. Neptune:“A habit of mine, sorry.” Behind the Neptune delegate, the Uranus delegate, an 8-limbed (two legs, six arms arranged in a line along the sides of its body going from head to tail) serpentine creature that is greenish-blue, coils itself up around its own body, the 50 foot long form becoming more compact though not necessarily smaller. Venus:“Is Io here?” The Io delegate, a 3-eyed fishlike creature (with two tentacles coming out of its back to grasp creatures, two claws from its underside to rip and tear at their flesh) that lives on that moon of Jupiter, in the water that exists underneath a solid layer of ice, has yet to arrive. What the other delegates do no know is that the Io delegate has been eaten by its own kind, their primary food source (xacsliars, akin to eels on earth) too low in supply to feed them all. Sho:“Putting off the summit any longer is unadvisable, every moment of time we spend lingering, the end of the universe draws ever closer.” Saturn:“Agreed.” The Oracles from around the solar system, Kenshiro and Rumiko more just observing for future reference, finally begin the summit….
