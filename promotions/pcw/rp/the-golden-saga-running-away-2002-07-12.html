The Golden Saga: Running Away

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-12-02 Run away. Run away from your life. Run away from your responsibilities. Run away from it all. It happens all the time. A kid in Boston, right now, is being abused by his father. Within the week he is going to pack his bags and run away, onto the 'mean' streets of Boston. He will eventually go back home, but only after selling drugs for more then two months and living in run down housing complexes. His father will welcome him back and it will be all good for more then two months until it happens again. This time the kid wont come home. So Why Are You Running Away? Two people fell in love in Washington state, Spokane or something like that. Noone really lives there anyway. They fell in love and eventually one asked the other to marry her. They agreed and for six months... Six whole months... they planned the wedding to the second, it was going to be perfect for both of them The day finally came, and the groom stood on the alter tall and proud. He knew this was the day where his life was gonna go from mediocre to perfect. The wedding started at 12. Then 1 o clock came. 2 o clock. The bride never showed up, she ran away from the responsibility of marriage, got drunk in a local bar, and had threesome with two people she never met before. The groom will recover, but for now he will sit on the alter of the church until his second cousin comes up to him and helps him out. So Why Are You Running Away? Love is the basis for most people running away, you see. In Utah, a major contributer to discrimination because of religion, two A students at Brigham Young University fell in love. Two people at the top of their classes. They couldn't stand to be apart so they started breaking the rules, they went to bars together, started to sleep in the same bed, did sexual things, kissed, everything. BYU does not approve of these actions, and when they were seen holding hands in public they were kicked out of the school. It's not because BYU doesnt approve of relationships in general, its just because they don't really approve of same sex lovers. They were forced out of the school, their families, and their lives. They both moved to Washington DC and became avid gay and lesbian rights activists, eventually they are both going to make major changes in the minds of thousands because of their sad story of being Morman outcasts. It hasn't happened yet, but it will. So Why Are You Running Away? Prom night in Miami. A large regionalized High School is ready to let the seniors into the school for the night of their lives. The time comes and everyone is let in. But, sitting at home is a kid whose date never came. He waited, and waited, and waited. His tux was collecting dog hair and the corsage was beginning to wilt. He couldn't figure out why she would stand him up on the most important day of his life, he left a message on her machine ending the relationship and telling her off like he wanted to at the time. About an hour went by when he got a call from the hospital, his girlfriend was in a car accident after being blindsided by a drunk driver, he should come to the hospital right now. He did and met her there, she was injured badly. She will live though, and those times at the hospital were full of love and devotion. The problem was that he forgot about the answering machine tape, when she gets home in a month or so and listens to it, she is going to end it with him. He's going to graduate alone and mourn the loss of his love for years. He will leave town to escape the memories and wont be seen until his reunion in 25 years. So Why Are You Running Away? Somewhere in eastern San Diego, a 18 year old kid is going to rob a bus driver on the last leg of his ride, he is going to get a massive amount of money from this bus, seeing as it goes up and down most of Southern California. He is going to take the money, buy some drugs for his habit and something nice for his girlfriend. Then the cops are gonna find out it is him. He will run away from them in a stolen car and try to cross a intersection at rush hour. This is when irony (the bitch) will come into play. He will be killed after getting hit in the drivers side by a bus. Hah. So Why Are You Running Away? And in Oxford, Mississippi, six people are going to have a battle of epic proportions, and three of those people have a chance to take that ever important Continental Title away from Legend Inc., however, the opposing team has nothing on the Dark Age. Legend Inc, Duke Williams, and Chris 'The Fallen Messiah' Chase will be forced to lay down and let the new guards come in. Eventually they are going to be beaten so bad that only one question will pass the lips of myself... So Why Are You Running Away?
