Malice the New Testament of PCW

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/20/02 -A man sits in an old Archie Bunker style chair that sits in a small apartment on Chicago’s northwest side. Pictures of Martin Luther King, Rosa Parks, and Jessi Jackson hang with pride on the walls. The record player is on and an old Charlie Rich 45 is pumping out “Behind Closed Doors”. The man in the chair sucks on a cigar as the smoke fills the room like an old honky tonk at three in the morning. The camera zooms in on the face and its none other then Duke Williams.- Williams: You can knock me down but ya can’t knock me out. You can bend me baby but you can’t break me. Use me but don’t abuse me. You can suck me but watch the teeth honey You catch my drift? PCW Malice chapter two begins right now. I whole new round of rowdiness and raunchiness headed up by the Man Of Malice Duke Williams….me. -Duke flicks an ash and turns on the TV using the clicker that’s on the TV stand next to the chair. The first thing that pops on the old boob tube is the Jerry Springer Show.- Williams: Lesbian truck drivers and there secret admirers. Now this is quality television. Damn that woman has more facial hair then I do. I bet she’s got more balls then the whole Ultimate Authority combined. -Duke makes a face like he just sniffed Oprah’s ass after an eating binge at Big Mama’s Soul Food Kitchen.- Williams: Lesbians are hot and kinky these broads are dikes. Big difference, this is false advertisement. -Duke turns off the tube, gets up, and walks out onto the balcony that over looks the city.- Williams: When you have the working man on your side you’re unstoppably. Sure I lost to Kannon at Madness due to that little anal wart know as Ellie but today is a new day. Prior to the firings the Malice Crew was a club of guys who hung out together. After Madness we’re a gang of four who will watch each others back and do what we have to do to ensure victory for the crew as a whole and not just for ourselves or oneself. -Duke spits off the balcony and watches it drop like a young child.- Williams: So that brings us to the new beginning which kicks off this Friday in the great state of New Jersey. A state that embroils the blue-collar attitude that the Malice Crew exemplifies. A state that in size looks likes an underdog compared to it’s neighbor but in spirit and commitment to the betterment of our society it ranks just as high. It’s also home to the first battle of the Crew and the Authority in what’s guaranteed to be a long and illustrious battle were the Malice Crew victorious is the only viable out come. Nelson listen to my words and understand them. WE WILL NOT REST, TILL YOUR CORPORATE CRIMNALS ARE SHUT DOWN. I’ll stalk you, mimic you, anger you, frustrate you, and I’ll hurt you, till you forfeit control of this company. I’ve watched you and your goons form and fall apart month after month. Others have waged war upon you and have come close to destroying you. However once they get you down on your knees, a position your very familiar with. They find compassion and let you skate away defeated but ready to rebuild. -Duke strokes his mountain beard and adjusts his chrome shades.- Williams: Nelson I don’t have that compassion and neither does Kano, Rush, or Shabazz. What we do have, is commitment and a defined goal to shut you down. I have no doubt we will win the overall battle. WHY? Greed. Plain and simple, the thing that drives you will also destroy you as it has done in the past. I’ve watched it as one of your goons, as a commentator, and now a fighter. Greed not only drives you but also Kannon, Waldon, Makonie. You think Kannon is going to stick around as the second man forever? Waldon yes, Kannon not a chance. Do you really need a history lesson? Do you not remember a guy by the name of Jeremy Howard. Kannon is driven by a hell of a lot more greed then Howard ever was. Enjoy it while it lasts kid, cus it won’t last long. -Somebody enters the front door. It’s a young women of about twenty-two here skin is milk chocolate, he breasts are firm and large, he hair is braided. Duke walks up to her and gives her a kiss on her sensuous black lips. The kiss seems to last an eternity as beauty and the beast tongue wrestle. She pulls back with a big smile on her face.- Kesha: Baby what’s going on?? Williams: Nothing my sweet hossette I’m just getting ready for the big match this Friday. Kesha: Good cus I was getting sick of supporting your out of work bum ass. -Duke walks over and smacks her ass- Williams: Well I’ve enjoyed supporting your sweet ass Malice Style. -Kesha laughs- Kesha: I went down to the post office and canceled all those checks you sent to the Scientologists. Crazy muthaf*ckers. Williams: You ain’t lying. Kesha: So when you get your ass back to work are you fighting that crazy ass cracker who hangs out with that big assed hoe? Williams: I have all the members of Ultimate Authority including Kannon. Kesha: A man of mine better kick his cracker ass. If you don’t I will. If that bitch ever thinks about interfering in one of your matches again I’ll beat her with my shoe. Williams: That’s right beat her Malice Style!!! -Duke laughs at her anger.- Kesha: Boy what you laughing at? Williams: Nothing. Kesha: Good cus I’ll beat your ass with a shoe too. -Duke walks over to her takes off her shoe and kiss her feet and works his way up her leg.- Kesha: Damn baby if your gonna do that turn those camera’s off. -Duke stops and gets up.- Williams: Your right we’ll do that later. One year ago I entered the PCW with a new wife and a fresh outlook. I’m not going through my sixth divorce but I don’t give a damn. So let’s run down the competition this Friday. -Duke sits back down in the chair.- Kesha: Start with the racist. Williams: Fair enough, lets start with Makonie. You my friend are a piece of work. A true follower with not mind of your own. If I were you I would rethink entering the ring with two pissed off black guys, a white guy in love with a black women, and Kano. Does my girlfriend make you sick?? Does the thought of eating her pudding make you gag. Good!! I hope you choke on your vomit and die. I’m not against tracking you down boy and dragging you behind a pickup truck. Hoss I’ve spent my whole life defending the south and all she stands for musically and culturally. But its guys like you who make people think of the south as a bunch of inbreed racists. I’m going to make an example of you in a Malice sort of way. Kesha: If you don’t I will. Punk ass bitch think he’s gonna talk that sick jive and get away with it. I’ll beat him ghetto style. Williams: Then there’s the kid who’s spent his whole career as a lacky and a butt toy to the queers in PCW. Timmy Walden the definition of mid-card jobber. How can you go your whole career as nothing more then a third man. Walden lacks the talent to ever be a second man and kids that’s just sad. All his life he’s had problems winning, the worst was as a child at a birthday party when he had to have a Chinese finger trap from Chucky Cheese surgically removed from his finger. In Mexico Walden is billed on the cards as El Puso. Do you think I really fear this guy in your clan Nelson?? Your only as strong as your weakest link and Walden is your weakest link. -Duke lights up another cigar.- Williams: Kannon you beat me at Madness. You didn’t think I would say that did you? Lets not forget the fact that your genital wart infested sex toy had to save your ass from a trip that would have sent you Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. Lets not forget the fact that you’re still a second officer in a corporate army that is losing fast. You’re a criminal hoss and you will get busted. This time around you can’t hide so accept the fact that it Malice Time and Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound is waiting for you. -Kesha hands Duke a glass of whiskey.- Williams: So Nelson that leaves you. I hope you got that aids test cus I don’t want to catch anything from you homeboy. AIDS, GAY, Nelson, Screws farm creatures, likes little boys. You know the drill hoss I won’t bore you. You also know its all true so really it isn’t worth arguing. I said it earlier and I’ll reiterate it again so please make like the deaf gynecologist and read these lips. WE WILL NOT REST, TILL YOU AND THE REST OF THE CORPORATE CRIMINALS ARE DONE. -Duke flips the TV on.- Williams: I think I’m done. Kesha: What about the wild card? Williams: Wild Card? Kesha: Big Alan Steel? -Duke ponders the name for a moment.- Williams: I don’t trust anybody except my brothers in the crew. Big Al has proven to be a stand up guy time and time again. But the lure to enter the UA and to have back up to his title reign is there. So which way does he float?? Al hates Ty but we’ve seem people who hate him join up with him in the past. Does that happen here? Only time will tell. I’ll trust Steele, but at the same time I won’t take my eye off of him. If he swings with the crew or calls it down the middle there is no chance of losing this match against Nelson and Kannon and the two other guys who don’t matter. To get down to brass tacks is to say ITS MALICE TIME!!! -Duke smiles at his lady and winks as they get back to business as the camera fades.-
