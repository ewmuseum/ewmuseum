A Vassal To A Vassal.....The Horror Of It All.....

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 10 of 2003 A.D. This whole ‘ownership’ thing is starting to aggravate me. Damn that wretched Green. I pray his soul someday rots in the darkest void for eternity. I’ve grown sick of his games. They claim I’m lost my mind, reports even make assertions that I’ve become criminally insane. But in fact he is the one who has gone mad. Hell……I’ve had innumerable agents of those ‘other’ faiths try to have me incarcerated back into that repulsive asylum, and it’s started to piss me off. They envy me and all the blessings bestowed upon me, and they obviously envy the depths of my faith. But that is what those peons cannot fathom. I‘m not mad. I merely follow orders, and I do not waver in my purpose. So my methods are a tad intrusive, and wholly unorthodox. And maybe a few have lost their lives, and others their souls, in the process. So what?? Let the hypocrites weep for them, let them pass judgment with forked tongues while they ignorantly brush off the murder of those same innocents on a daily basis. Sacrifices must be made for the greater good. And when Great Cthulhu rises from R’lyeh to return humankind to its primal, savage roots, and eradicate this degenerate fucking species, those same skeptics will grovel before me for mercy as I stand by the court of the masters in those last days. Their judgments will mean nothing at that time. And I will feel nothing but glee as I watch those downtrodden buffoons plead for their redemption. Skylar Green has been a thorn in my side too long. I was instructed to eliminate the bastard, and instead I now find myself a lowly vassal to the Celestial flunky. And I’ve lived in shame for every solitary moment of this damned travesty. Green’s spirit should be adrift in some remote rift in space and time, not controlling my actions like that of some accursed puppet master! Nobody pulls my strings, especially not some blind mongrel spawned from inferior immortals. The irony makes me sick. But then again, the Black Pharaoh works in mysterious ways. Who am I to question His omnipotent wisdom? It’s not my place nor it is yours. Surely you agree on the issue? Ah, good. You’re learning your place rather nicely, child. Someday, when you’ve grown older, I suppose you’d make a rather nice addition to the sect. I have my own ambitions, and I intend on defying that impudent scum. The World title will come into my possession, breaking me free from the power of Skylar Green and his monstrous curse. And it will come to pass at the expense of another. Pendragon, that delusional crusader of hollow dreams……my Azathoth, what a miserable cur. The way he offers false hope to the masses is a sham. Let me ask you, who is the real enemy here? Venneman Pendragon, the one who flaunts his fairy-tale ideals amongst the persecuted who crave such an exodus from society and its horrors? Or me, a man who may embrace unpopular philosophies yet makes no bones about reality and its harsh destinies? Self-proclaimed paladins like him do no favor to the social collective by promoting such filth among those who will never attain it. Then again, such is a consequence of fighting on the side of good. Lies are your scripture and your actions are wholly founded of untruths. Better to give hope to those who will never receive such grace than allow them to wallow in the throes of their own fate. The latter may make them stronger and forge their will to that of iron, hereby giving them the ability to subsist. Trick them into following a path of illusion, crushing their spirit when so they reach the end of the barren trail. A temporary relief, nothing more. And one that will lead to an even slower, more agonizing death for the foolish when time comes. I see another has come to your mind. No, my child, do not cry for Marcus Stryker. He has chosen his path. Cavorting with fate has become his trade of choice. He likes to toy with death, but I fear he’s prodded too far. I’m sure even now the stubborn fool is trying to resist the angel of death, even as he straddles the boundary between Mortality and Eternity…. Still you cry?? I told you to stop worrying. Bear in mind the repercussions for disobeying me. Lord Daoloth would be more than happy to raise the shroud and show you the depths of true madness. Good. Now wipe those tears away, young one. Do not mourn the loss of a false savior. He is no better than Pendragon. His lies only corrupt humanity, pervaded with frivolous promises and sense of false security. He and those like him…..they will blind you only as long as you allow them to. But I can offer you escape. You can take my hand, child, and look into a world where the veil has been lifted and all knowledge is open to your eyes. It is here that the influence of Jehovah God’s puppet show cannot reach and where all vistas of reality, however dark and desolate that they be, are revealed. The true intentions and nature of the universe can be witnessed with one’s own eyes. Alas, I fear I cannot yet show you this world. You are not ready, and the mere glimpse of such blasphemies may drive you into the same environment of padded walls and sedatives that I once dwelt before my salvation. Most mortals simply cannot handle such a sight. Argh….I can feel that fool Celestial probing my mind. It is here that I leave you, child. But if ever you take up the black arts, and feel you are ready to look past the phantom world that poisons your mind, come to me. And I will change all that. Or maybe I’ll simply show them to you anyhow for my own kicks. That’s just the way I am. But hey, at least I’m honest in my intentions.
