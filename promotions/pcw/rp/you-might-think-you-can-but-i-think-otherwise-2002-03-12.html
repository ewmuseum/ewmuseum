You might think you can, but I think otherwise!

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-12-02 To this day, the Krazed Lunatic still can't explain what really happened between Xavier Kannon and himself. Before March Madness, Kannon was the Krayzie One's right hand man in his battle with Drake Maxwell. But now, nine days after the pay per view, these two men will fight in a tables match for the Continental title. After it's all said and done, one man will walk out the Continental Champion while the other man walks out having come up short. All of that pales in comparison though to the loss of the friendship that is now gone. That's a fact the Krazed Lunatic is going to have to live. In the mean time, it appears Kannon isn't the one causing problems in this fued. Because, as the scene begins to fade in, the Krayzie One is seen walking into his attorney's office in Elkhart, Indiana. Jeremy, in brand name blue jeans and a plain white t-shirt, opens the door for the lovely Jessica, whose clad in a black leather skirt and a matching red leather top. Neither of the two appears to be pleased at the moment. Rather, they seem upset. Jessica: I already lost my son once. I'm not going to lose him again! The two lovers begin to make there way toward the receptionist's desk with yesterday's phone call fresh on there young minds. Jeremy: I'm sure Kannon's attempts are meaningless. He's just trying to rile me up before the big match. Jessica: Well I hope you put his head straight through that table. Jeremy's face stays expressionless, but his words tell enough of a story. Jeremy: Oh, I'm not going to be his punching bag this time. I'm not reasong with that guy anymore. He had his chance..... Jessica: .....And blew it..... Just like he does with everything else. Jeremy sighs. Jeremy: I can't wait to hear his excuse on Wednesday. He'll blame the tables, saying they oppressed him. With his new change in attitude, one could actually believe that happening with the Gold Patron Meritorious. Recently, Xavier Kannon has gone so far as to blame everyone else, but himself, for his recent string of losses. And, when Kannon goes through the table on Turmoil, things will still be the same for him. He'll have lossed to a better man, but was unable to face reality. He lossed. Jessica: I wouldn't put that past him. Jeremy stops short of replying to her, as the two reach the receptionist's desk. The lady, in a white blouse, looks up from the stack of papers on her desk. Receptionist: Yes? Jeremy: We're here to see Jacob Frost. The Receptionist knods her head to Jeremy, then looks down toward a planner book. Jessica moves in close on Jeremy, as he places his arm around her, trying to comfort her while she worries about her current plight. Receptionist: Ah, here you are. Mr. Frost will see you in a moment. You can wait over there. Jeremy follows the ladies finger to a set of chairs for waiting. Jeremy: Okay. Jeremy, with Jessica still in his arms, leads the lovely beauty over to the waiting area. Jessica: You know, I still can't believe he thinks I'm your wife. Jeremy takes a seat right next to Jessica in the waiting area. Jeremy: It just goes to show you that he doesn't listen to anyone else. He's too full of himself. Jessica: I don't know about that. If its one thing I feel sorry for him about, its that he has to listen to that b!tch continuously copy every single thing he says. How annoying. A smile crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: If Kannon was smart, he'd figure out the real reason they made duck tape. Jessica turns to Jeremy, looking at him with disdain. Jeremy shrugs. Jeremy: What? If I had a woman like that, I'd tape her mouth shut. Receptionist: Mr. Frost is ready to see you, Mr. Howard. Jeremy stands to his feet, fixing his blue jeans. Jessica also gets to her feet and begins to walk with Jeremy as he starts making his way toward Jacob Frost's office. The two have to follow a hall that curves around before coming to the office of Jeremy's attorney. Standing in the doorway to his office, is Jacob Frost, dressed in a grey business suit. Jacob Frost: Hello Jeremy. Jeremy reaches his hand out, shaking it. Jeremy: Hello. Jacob motions for the two lovers to take a seat in his office, to which Jeremy and Jessica comply. Jacob Frost: What brings you here today? Jessica: This son of a b!tch wants to take my son after I just got him back! Jeremy grabs Jessica, trying to hold her back from snapping. Jeremy continues to hold his lovely woman in his arms, as he takes over control of speaking with the attorney. Jeremy: Of course you know that we got Troy back. Well, this certain individual of Scientology has decided that he's going to try and get Troy taken away from us, just because he's jealous of me. Jacob Frost: Sounds like he's really jealous. Jeremy: I came here to find out how much of a case he actually has. I mean, I already have a meeting with the Welfare Officer of the state of Indiana later this week. Jacob Frost: This is serious. Jeremy holds Jessica tightly in his arms, trying to reassure her without words. Jeremy: I know, thats why I want you to get on this as soon as possible. Jacob reaches for a pen, then begins to take notes on a piece of paper. Jacob Frost: What can you tell me about this man that chooses to try and get your son taken away? Jeremy: His name is Xavier Kannon. He's a Scientologist with no real home thats only claim to fame is jobbing to Drake Maxwell back in NTICW. Jessica: And he has an annoying b!tch of a girlfriend that follows him around like the lost dog that she is. Jacob Frost can be seen taking down all of these notes. Nothing seems out of the ordinary. Jacob Frost: He's a Scientologist you say? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Jeremy: Is that even a religion? Jacob Frost: Not one that I'm aware of. I might need to check into that. Jeremy: What should we do for now? Jessica sits up from Jeremy's arms, awaiting the answer to this question. Jacob Frost: For now, you should go see the Welfare Officer and go from there until I find out about this Xavier Kannon and his Scientology. Jeremy: Alright, I'll do that. Jacob Frost: I'll get back to you toward the end of this week, early next week at the latest. Jeremy reaches his hand out, shaking hands with the attorney. Jeremy: Just see to it that my girlfriend doesn't lose our son again. Jacob Frost: I'll do everything I can. Jessica: Thanks! The two lovers, Jeremy and Jessica, leave the office of the attorney and begin to make there way back toward the blue 2001 Pontiac Aztek they now drive. Before they get there though, a thought crosses the mind of the Krazed Lunatic. Jeremy: I just thought of something. Jessica, while still walking, turns to Jeremy. Jessica: What's that? Jeremy: How do I know that the man that called me yesterday as the Welfare Officer, isn't really an imposter posing as one. I mean, I'm sure Kannon used someone to do this. He used me to get noticed in PCW. Jessica: There's no doubt about that. He used you! Jeremy: Well, tonight, I'm going to use a table.... To put Kannon's head through! Tonight, in front of a jam packed crowd, Xavier Kannon and Jeremy Howard are going to wage battle in a tables match for the Continental title. When its all said and done, one man will have went through a table the harsh way. Whoever the man is that is still standing after that, will be a winner and a loser. For, when two former friends collide, neither actually wins. Both will have lost something already. The scene fades away, as Jeremy and Jessica enter the Pontiac Aztek to begin heading for Turmoil..... to black!
