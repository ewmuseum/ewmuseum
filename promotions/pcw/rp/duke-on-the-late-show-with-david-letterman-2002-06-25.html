Duke on the Late Show with David Letterman

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/25/02 -It’s the Late Show with David Letterman tonight’s guests include John Goodman, and PCW wrestler Duke Williams. Dave runs through his show and finishes up with John Goodman. He then cuts to a commercial and sits back at his desk with a smile.- Dave: My final guest tonight is long time wrestler and PCW commentator who this Sunday at the Gold and Glory will take on three other men in a deadly double cage match. He is “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. -Paul Shaffer starts to pound out a mean instrumental version of Rhinestone Cowboy by Glen Campbell as the Malice Man enters to the screaming cheers of the audience. Duke is wearing a Blue button down shirt, sport coat, blue jeans, boots, black thin glasses, and his black Stetson. Duke pumps up the crowd. Dave stands up and shakes his hand as they head to their respective seats. – Dave: Welcome to the big show you look nice tonight. Williams: You hitting on me Dave? -Dave looks into the camera with his big gap toothed grin.- Dave: No, we’re just not used to seeing you with a sports coat. I’m thanking you for dressing up to be on the show. Williams: What are you saying wrestlers don’t dress nice? -Dave glares at the camear- Dave: Paul can you help me out here. -Paul puts down some sheet music and hops on the mic.- Paul: What he’s saying is your styling man. Duke: Oh, ok thanks Dave. -Dave looks at the camera and just shakes his head.- Dave: I have your profile in front of me here and it says your 6’3” and weigh in at 265? Duke: I’m getting old and they just put a Crispy Cream next to my apartment its more like 275 now. Dave: Born in ahh 1960 that makes you what ahh 41 now? Duke: Forty one and going strong Dave. Dave: You fight a lot of kids don’t you?? You were body slamming opponents while they were playing on the see-saw you know what I mean. Duke: I take a lot of crap back stage for being the old man but lets just say I can out drink’em, out think’em, and out play’em…..Malice Style hoss!! Dave: Hoss? What’s that? IS that some kind of wrestling lingo? Duke: It’s a term of respect. It’s like a Yankee calling another Yankee man. Dave: So its like ahh this. -Dave adjusts his collar.- Dave: How you doing there hoss? Duke: Not bad. -The crowd applauds.- Dave: So are you enjoying your stay here in the Big Apple? Duke: Lets just say its been an interesting time. Dave: Well why what do you mean? Duke: I get to the airport yesterday and after they spend three hours fondling me to make sure I’m not a terrorist….. Dave: This was getting off the ahh plane? Duke: Yea. -Dave starts to sweat- Dave: I don’t think ahh those were ahh airport workers. Duke: So I’m not going to get my wallet back? Dave: Unlikely. Duke: IT just starts their Dave. Dave: Oh really theirs more? Duke: You ain’t lying. I get to the hotel with my kid and some lady comes up to me and wants me to sign an autograph for her. Dave: Well that doesn’t sound too odd. Duke: She wanted me to sign her breast. -Dave chokes on his coffee for comedic effect.- Duke: She had some big one’s too. Dave: So what did you do? Duke: Damn Dave come on I don’t want to anger my fans. Dave: Well I can understand that. So this Sunday you have the big PPV card called Gold and Glory and you’ll be there? Duke: I’ll be the color man on the mic and I have a big continental show down in a double cage. Dave: Oh man that sounds scary. You know the last time I watched wrestling it was growing up in Indiana and it was Bruno Sanmartino vs Chief Jay Strong Bow you remember those guys? Duke: Vaguely but yes. Dave: and then there was my favorite Dick The Bruiser. He would threaten to break your legs. I’m gonna smash you. He’d say and we kids would all go wild. -The crowd laughs at Dave’s impersonation.- Duke: Not much has changed to be honest. Dave: So do you have an Indian or a Bruiser in this match? Duke: I have a little Cherokee running through these veins and I’ll bruise ya! -The crowd goes wild.- Dave: So who are you rassling and tell us a little about their getup? Duke: The first guy is Kannon and he’s a Scientologist. Dave: A Scientologist? Duke: Yup. Dave: We went from Indians to Scientologists. Now Chief Jay Strong Bow used to hit you with his tomahawk chop what does this Kannon fellow hit you with a copy of Dientics and a collection plate? Duke: That’s about right. He comes to the ring with this pig names Ellie… Dave: Now is Ellie a real pig or a person? You never know with rassling. Duke: She’s a real pig. -The audience laughs.- Duke: Then theirs Miya and there really is no story to this guy. Dave: He’s just kind of there? Duke: More or less. Dave: Does he maybe bruise people? Duke: Not really. Dave: Maybe he should become Miya the Bruiser? Duke: That’s an idea if you would manage him? Dave: I can barley manage this little show. Duke: The last guy is legend Inc. Dave: I bet he’s a big guy and wears a boa? Duke: No he’s a young kid who wears a suit. Dave: A suit, oh my. Duke: This guy I don’t get. He cuts promo’s but they make sense. Dave: What’s a promo? Duke: When a wrestler lets you into his life, cuts an interview for PCW television. Dave: And this guy doesn’t do that? Duke: No he does it a lot but nobody knows what the hell he’s talking about. Dave: Does he have incoherent rambling’s….oh man that would be a great name for a wrestler….from parts ahh unknown its Incoherent Ramblings!! Duke: That would be a sweet name. So anyway this guy makes scene its just not relevant. He’s talking about some guy named Chopsticks, Furbies, and Darth Vader. Dave: Maybe his deranged is he from parts unknown? Duke: Nope California. Dave: That explains a lot. Duke: I guess it does!! You know I get criticized for not having the best promos but at least I’m relevant. Dave: I feel for the guy I’ve been doing these shows for over fifteen years and not one of them has been relevant! -Dave stares into the camera like a zombie.- Duke: This Sunday on Pay-Per-View I’m going to knock him into the real world. A very malicious world. Along with the rest of the crooks. Dave: They sound like very weasely guys. Can you bruise them up for me and old times? Duke: I’ll send them all Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound just for you Dave. Dave: This Sunday on Pay-Per-View its Gold and Glory live from Lexington, Kentucky call you local cable operator for details and to order. Now is that the main event your in? Duke: No that’s Big Alan Steele versus Frisco in a Mount Olympus Match for the world title. Dave: That sounds dangerous! Duke: I’m not trying to sell the Pay-Per-View but I think somebody may die in this one. Dave: That’s it I’m ordering right now! Paul some dialing music please! -Paul starts to play the outro music as Dave has the phone in his hand.- Dave: Thank you for tuning in order Gold and Glory and tune in tomorrow for jungle Jack Hanna and Don King. Good night everybody. -Dave waves as the World Wide Pants logo appears and exits.-
