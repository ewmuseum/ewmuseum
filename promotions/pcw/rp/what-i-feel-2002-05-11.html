What I Feel...

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.05.02 Duzza has been around this way for some time. Duzza was around here before PWA was called PWA, Before PTC was called PTC. Duzza has seen a lot of men and women come and go, some made a lasting impression, some were just like a shooting star, a quick flash then fizzled out. Though he may not of spent all this time in PCW, he has been involved in a PCW ran operation nearly every step of the way. When Tyler Nelson regained control of PCW, Duzza saw it happen. He may not have been wrestling in PCW then, but he wasn’t far off. When Stanislav defeated Meanstreak Duzza was around to see it. When EW famously departed from PTC and screwed over everyone who trusted them, Duzza was there. Hell, Duzza was one of the people who got screwed. Duzza often looks back at these times, they seem so distant now, they seem just like stories now, but Duzza was there for all of it and still remembers them like they was yesterday. And when people in the future look back at this time, things they are bound to remember, things like the sure to be match of the year candidate retirement match between Miyagawa and Boda, or the feud between Jordan X Shabazz and Mark Makonie, Duzza will be able to say he was there for all of it. But Duzza remembers a time when him and Da Westsidaz where the only black wrestlers around. Duzza remembers getting heat from everyone and their mother for calling white people “crackers.” Now there are more black wrestlers than Jay-Z has sold albums. Every single one of them calling white people “cracker” and none of them getting any heat for it. Duzza bought this craze of dissin’ white people to this place and what did he get for it? Nothing. No one cares about Duzza no more. He’s just another black thug now. People seem to forget Duzza was the original black thug, people seem to forget Duzza was claiming the ghetto before there was even a PWA. Maybe they haven’t forgotten, maybe they just don’t care. This plays on the thoughts of Duzza every night and day. Duzza wishes he could turn back the hands of time, maybe to the days of NZW, where Duzza repeatedly named the top student, or maybe just back to PCW 2. Where, although he didn’t win much he was well known, people cared about him. Maybe Duzza has lost his touch, what with all these other blacks around now, each of them claiming the hood and each of them hating on whites, perhaps Duzza just doesn’t stand out like he used to. Who knows, who actually cares? Duzza fears that no one cares any more. And he’s getting sick of it all. Duzza has worked hard from day one, only to have others bite his style and steal his lines. For a long time, Duzza hasn’t done anything about it, just tried not to let it bother him and get on with his own career. But he can’t do that anymore. For every Jordan X or Statuz Quo or Drew Hostyle or Rush Mason, people keep forgetting about Duzza a little bit more every time another brother comes along. Now Duzz has love for all the brothers in this business, but he can’t sit back and let everyone else around him take all the fame that he used to have. He’s sick of everyone else running through the gate that he opened and as a man he can only take so much of it. And the time for Duzza to act all cool and laid back has long passed. Now it’s time for him to make a stand and the new Duzza starts making this stand Sunday at Supremacy. For Duzza has a title shot at the Rising Star champion Kid Krayzie. And although Duzza hasn’t done much to prepare for this match, he’s not gonna go down easy against Krayzie. It recently emerged that krayzie’s father was one of the white men that killed Duzza’s father. You can bet that Duzza is going to make Krayzie pay for his fathers sins. The scene opens in Duzza’s Hampton mansion. A massive, white building, with balconies, 3 door garage, swimming pools indoor and outdoors and basketball courts. The scene moves into Duzza’s bedroom, that he shares with his ebony queen. There is a massive red, silk sheets covered bed in the middle of the room. There is a large balcony that looks over the ocean on the left of the room. In the top corner of the room there is a large tv. Duzza and his wife Brittany are sitting on the bed looking at the television. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy white pants, a tight black T-shirt with a big silver “Best of Both Worlds” logo on it. He has his hair cornrowed tightly to his head, running along his head and down the back of his neck. He has one silver bracelet and a thick silver chain with a diamond crucifix. His wife, is wearing a baggy black jumper and some tight denim jeans. She is holding onto his arm and has her hair leaning onto his shoulder. Both Duzza and Brittany are staring at the television, watching it with great interest. On the tv is the promo from Kid Krayzie, where he talked about an event in his past, where his father and a gang of white guys killed a innocent black man infront of his child. That child was Duzza. Duzza knows this, he remembers the day like it was yesterday and will not let Krayzie get away with this. Once the promo finishes, both Brittany and Duzza sit in shock, neither saying a word until Brittany starts speaking. Brittany: “Duzz, honey are you ok?” Duzza: “I’m sick of this shit” Brittany: “How do you mean?” Duzza: “I’m just sick of it all?” Brittany: “What?” Duzza: “Ain’t you got hears? Don’t you understand me? I’m sick of this shit, I’m sick of living this white mans lifestyle we’re living. I’m sick of bustin’ my ass every mother f*cking day in the ring and not getting a ounce of respect” Brittany: “Malek, you get plenty of respect” Duzza: “Whatever, I don’t get shit. I been here before half of these fools, crackers and n*gga’s alike and I ain’t got shit” Brittany: “How can you say that? You’ve got a mansion, you have a Bentley, a Jeep a Merc, you have everything you wanted. We have everything we always spoke about when we was in Harlem” Duzza: “Don’t you think I know that? We would spend every night and day talking about this lifestyle” Brittany: “So why you buggin?” Duzza: “Because, it’s fake. Everything is fake, this isn’t the real me, I’m just a black man living in a white mans world and I’m sick of it” Brittany: “I know your upset because of what Krayzie’s father did to you, but your not making any sense” Duzza: “Oh I’m not clear enough for you? You want clear? Hows this, I hate this place, I wanna pack up and move back to Harlem…hell f*ck Harlem, I wanna go live in Africa” Brittany: “ Malek, get a grip. We’re living your dream, this is your dream. I sacrificed everything so you could follow your dream and I’m not gonna stand here and let you throw everything away, why you acting like this?” Duzza: “Don’t you see? Nobody gives a f*ck about Duzza no more, I used to be the thug everyone knew, now I’m just another thug talking trash and hating white people. I’m just another Negro now” Brittany: “Who cares what anyone else thinks? It’s all about you” Duzza: “I give a f*ck what people think. Where did it all go wrong?” Brittany: “Duzz, your trippin’. People know you, people care about you, who’s filling you with all these damn thoughts?” Duzza: “What does that matter? Here my career is going downhill faster than a white guy in the ghetto and all you wanna know is who is filling my head with thoughts?” Brittany: “No, that ain’t it. But Duzz, if you think it’s going downhill, then do something, don’t holler at me in your East Hampton mansion, go out there and sort it out” Duzza: “Oh, I’ma do something about this, that’s for damn sure” Brittany: “Malek, don’t kill nobody please” Duzza: “I’ma do what ever the f*ck I want to do. I don’t give a f*ck about nothing no more. It’s me against the world, everyone I thought was close to me, f*ck them, everyone who is a enemy of mine, f*ck them” Brittany: “Malek, your scaring me” Duzza: “You ain’t the only one who ‘bout to get scared girl, you ain’t the only one. I’ma be scaring everyone, I’ma have everyone fearing my name, I’m sick of getting no respect, I’m sick of people looking at these other n*gga’s instead of me, I’m sick of it all. See, it’s time for a change, I ain’t gon’ be no push about no more, I’ma take a stand and I’ma start this new me with that punk ass cracker fool Kid ‘mother f*cking’ Krayzie” Brittany: “Malek, as a Christian male, don’t do something you will forget” Duzza: “Theres only one thing I’d regret doing to Kid Krayzie and that’d be letting that fool walk away with out a scratch on him, that my friend is the only thing I would regret. And after Krayzie, I’ma make every one, I don’t give a fuck who they are, black white asian or latino, they all gonna fall before me, I swear every cracker, n*gga whatever, I’ma get them” Brittany: “Why must you always be so nasty man?” Duzza: “Because I’m a nasty mother f*cker” The scene starts to fade as Duzza storms out of the house, his bag with his wrestling gear in it is in his hand as he goes of to start the next phase of his career…
