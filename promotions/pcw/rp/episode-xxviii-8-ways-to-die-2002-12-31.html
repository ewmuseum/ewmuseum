Date: 12/31/02 |--Scene--|Opens up in the Dark Woods. In the woods, Sakura was slowly standing up from the ground. Standing on both of her feet, as she was wincing and glaring at Wei. She had gotten into a slight fighting stances. Wei had stopped walking, as he noticed that she was standing up. But soon she dropped her gaurd, as Wei was looking at her with a curious look on his face. But the look soon changed into an amusing one. |Wei|'Giving up so easily. Fine! Then die!' |--Scene--|Soon watched him firing the orb out of his hand. The orb was soaring across the ground in great speeds. Sakura was just watching the orb approaching her in great speeds. Noticing that her body was glistening from the orb's light. Switches to a close-up of right Sakura's eye, Where you could notice everything turning white. Hearing a loud explosion within the whiteness. When the whiteness vanished, you were able to see part of the woods ablaze in a large mass of inferno flames. Wei having an amusing look on his face. While Kei, Rei, Mayuka, and Akane were having frightened looks on their faces. Josh was having a relaxed look on his face. Akechi being amused about seeing that. As Yukko was keeping a serious look on her face. |Wei|'It seems that your training to this teenage girl was half ass.' |--Scene--|Josh smirked. |Josh Starwind|'If it was half ass. Then how come she's still standing?' |Wei|'What?!' |--Scene--|Through the flames, you were able to see a bright cerulean colored orb pulsing within the flames. Wei's and Akechi's expressions turned into shocked ones. While Kei, Rei, Mayuka, and Akane were having relieved looks on their faces. Within the pulsing orb, you could notice a determined Sakura. On her skin, you were able to see faint onyx colored streaks there. She was pulsing with raw energy. Pointed a finger at the shocked Wei. |Sakura|'It's time for Round 2! And this time I'm going to win!' |--Scene--|Wei was continuing to look shocked while watching the new Sakura. Just then, Sakura quickly moved both of her arms down. When doing that, she caused the cerulean colored inferno of energy to exploded into a large raging inferno. When doing that, he caused the blaze of fire to be put out. Sakura smirked when doing that. |Sakura|'Wow. This power feels great. I wonder if this is how Josh feels.' |--Scene--|Switches back over to Josh and Akane. As Akane was continuing to heal Josh with her powers. |Akane|'She has the same markings as you. Why is that, Lord Starwind? |Josh Starwind|'She just discovered her Neutral Mode with her Spirit Energy. But her markings is different.' |Akane|'Huh?' |Josh Starwind|'Look closer.' |--Scene--|Showing a close-up of Sakura's markings. Could see that her markings weren't faint. |Akane|'Hmm? Why do they look that way?' |Rei|'Because she's only halfway through the transformation, Prissy Priss.' |Josh Starwind|'Yea. And the only question is will this new power will be able to handle Wei.' |--Scene--|Soon showing a both, Sakura and Wei, standing in their fighting stances. As they were going to get ready to fight once more. |Akane|'I truely hope so.' |--Scene--|Showing a close-up of Sakura's face.
