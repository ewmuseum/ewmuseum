Tired and Irritated

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/02 Sometimes you are driven to the point where you don't even know who you are. You are driven to the point where you are like the infamous Jekyl and Hyde constantly switching from one character to the other. It can be caused by stress, anger, or just plain out confusion. Being in this state only adds onto the feelings you currently hold deep within. A fine example of this would be Kramer Wild Wing. One minute he seems like a good guy, and the next he is paying off Ultimate Authority to protect him. To many of PCW followers that was a huge blow. Ultimate Authority are the 'bad guys' of PCW. They are the guys who play dirty, and who find pleasure in upsetting people. What is exactly going on inside the head of the Wild One? Could the months of getting no respect be getting to him? Could the whole fact that he isn't real talented be eating away at him? Or could the explanation just be that he is trying to get attention, even if it is for being a bad guy? The scene opens up inside of a hotel room located in Ann Arbor, Michigan, where the upcoming Sabotage is to be held. The hotel room is like any other, not too big and pretty peaceful with the nicely made bed, dresser, and a nightstand with phone and lamp. Yelling can be heard through the almost paper thin walls of the hotel room. It gets louder and louder giving us the notion that the yeller must be getting closer. Suddenly the doorknob on the door turns and we can see Kramer, red face and all, in a pair of black pants and a plain white shirt that is tucked into his pants. Kramer: Can't anybody do anything right these days!? He shakes his right fist, which is clutching on a piece of cloth. He steps all the way into the room slamming the door sending an ear splitting sound throughout the nearby halls. He holds up the balled up mass in his hands and unravels it. We see a black silk shirt with the picture of a small dragon on the left side with Kramer Wild Wing written over it in a rainbow shape. The only noticeable problem is that it looks ten sizes too small to fit Kramer. Kramer: I tell them that it needs special care, and what do they do? They throw it into the washer and dryer shrinking my three hundred dollar shirt. As he is spurting all of this out he is pacing to and fro still looking upset with a crinkled up forehead. Kramer: I don't make enough money to keep buying new things like this. Still feeling anger he balls the shirt up again and tosses it into a waste bin that is sitting in one of the corners of the room. He then proceeds over to the bed where he falls back onto it looking up to the ceiling trying hard to rest for a few minutes. The dark circles around his eyes give us the indication that he has been up for quite a while and is extremely tired. His eyes slide closed as his arm dangles freely from the side of the bed. Finally some peace and some......BRIIING...BRIIING. Kramer opens his eyes and sits upright as he answers the phone. Kramer: Hello? Voice: Is this Kramer Wild Wing I am speaking to? Kramer: Who wants to know? Voice: I am Leron Marks a reporter for the RAT Wrestling Newsletter. Kramer: I've never heard of it...... What exactly does RAT stand for anyway? Leron: It stands for Ridiculously Addicted To. Along with that it gives the hint that our newsletter is about giving info about wrestlers and what’s happening with them and such. Kramer: Oh really? Then I guess you are calling me to ask a question or something right? Leron: Yes. Kramer: Well I don't have all day so spit it out. Leron: Well...I was inquiring about whether or not you are secretly, behind doors, involved in any way with the Ultimate Authority? Hearing this Kramer stands up from the bed with an angered expression coming back across his face. His tone of voice also has risen as he replies. Kramer: Is that all anyone cares about!? What the hell is wrong with you people? You call me at my house, you track me down and call my hotel number, send me gazillions of letters through the mail, and personally I am getting quite sick of all of this. Leron: With all due respect Mr. Wild Wing, if you would just spill the beans then you wouldn't have this problem. Kramer: Listen Leron, this conversation is now officially over. And I promise that if you call me back again I will personally hunt you down and beat the living crap out of you. Kramer slams the phone down and the pacing starts yet again. Any hopes of relaxation are shot as the adrenaline pumping through his veins at the moment keeps him on his toes. Kramer: I don't know if I can take much more of this. I stay up all night thinking about possible job alternatives if I happen to lose on Sabotage. And I also stay up all night pondering what future PCW holds for me if I do in fact stay. Sunday is the big obstacle I have to defeat. I am going to be pushed to my limits, and unlike any other time, there will be no second chance so I can't afford to lose. The other guys involved for the most part seem to share the same feelings. And if they don't they have one messed up mind frame because PCW is the best federation there is and to not want to stick with it would be absolutely crazy. He stops pacing yet again and walks over to the phone and picks it up, looking like he wants to get something off of his shoulders. He hits the redial button. Leron: Hello? Kramer: You still want that answer? Leron: Are you kidding? Of course I do! Kramer: Well get your pen and paper ready because I want this to be written word for word. Leron: Got it. Kramer: Sure in the past I paid for the protection of LeBlanc and Grant. I can't explain what was going through my mind but I just did it. Then it wasn't a week after that that we had a tag team match together making us seem even more like associates. Don't let those happy smiles fool you. As everyone saw we lost the tag team match because obviously when it comes to playing fair Ultimate Authority don't play out too well. Honestly if it weren’t for me that match would have been nothing. As far as I can see, Ultimate Authority only gain victories when there are more than just one of them involved. So in no way, shape, or form am I involved with those jerk offs. I don't support what they stand for, I don't support their members, and personally I feel that they are breaking up anyway. I mean as you saw they already fired LeBlanc, and after Sunday I doubt Grant will be with them much longer. Oh and by the way screw Scientology!!! Besides I believe that its better to be a loner and to fend off everyone, because alliances almost never work out. Who knows though, one day you might catch the wild one in a pack. After finishing up a smile cracks across Kramer's face as he hangs up the phone. The tension and anger has left so he sprawls out on his bed hoping to get the rest he needs. The scene slowly fades to black.
