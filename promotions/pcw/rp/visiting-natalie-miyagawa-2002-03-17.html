Visiting Natalie Miyagawa

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/17/2002 *The scene is that of a typical hospital. The hospital is Anaheim Memorial, in Anaheim California. But why would Boda be visiting a hospital in Anaheim California. He has no family here. All his family is in Florida and New York. He has no friends in Anaheim, besides from a few former NBA mates. Needless to say, this is getting away from the point of the story. Boda is here for something. He’s dressed in a suit and tie. This of course not being his regular attire. Boda also has in his hand, a single black rose. Looking at the hospital directory, Boda tries to find room 420. Ah, there it is, as Boda points to the map. The World Champ heads to the lobby elevators, and pushes the up button.* DING *The reflective doors open, exposing the blue elevator walls inside. Chris Sloboda steps in, and pushes the button for the fourth floor. As the doors close, they are suddenly stopped, and into the elevator steps a Rent a Cop. The security guard is dressed in black pants and a white dress shirt, with insignias on it representing the hospital. In his left hand, he has a box of Krispy Kreme donuts. The guard says hello to Chris, who in return, nods. The guard hits the button for floor 5, leaving a glazed residue on the button from his donuts when he removes his finger. As the elevator starts to move, the cheesy elevator music plays, thus giving Boda a chance to think* How dare Miya try to get me to give my blood for his ailing wife. After all, it was his fault I had to take her out like that. Chase is lucky he’s to ugly to be married, or else I’d take his old lady out too. But that wouldn’t do any good anyhow, because even if a sod like Chase could get a woman, she’d be so hideous, that she’d scare even me to death. Hell, she’d be so ugly, that she’d make Chase look dashing. But, that’s another story all together. And being I’m a mega-asshole now, I need to focus on my being here in the Anaheim Memorial Hospital, and not going off on a tangent about Chase and his ugly women. Ah, 4th floor. *The elevator comes to a stop. The doors open and Boda steps out of the elevator, leaving the donut eating security guard alone. The doors close behind Boda as he walks down the hallway. As he walks towards Room 420, he is stopped* “Can I help you?” asks the nurse at the nurse’s station. “This is ICU, and only family members are allowed up here to visit.” Boda leans on the counter, and lays down a hundred-dollar bill on the counter. “I’m here to visit a fallen family member. Is that a problem?” The nurse looks around, then slyly takes the hundred-dollar bill and pockets it. “What room sir?” “I’m visiting the young lady in room 420. Thank you” Boda replies with a evil grin. The nurse points in the direction of 420, “It’s down that way. But, it says here she is Russian, and there is no way you are.“ “Actually, I’m of Czechoslovakian heritage. We are cousins. Oh, and I was never here, okay?” Boda replies as he gracefully slaps another $100.00 on the counter, before he walks that way. I hate the smell of hospitals. The cleanliness. The prestine look. This seems like a busy hospital too. All I’ve seen the past 20 minutes is doctors and nurses running back and forth. Orderlies changing bed linens, and in one room, I saw some drugged up punk masturbating and throwing his seamen in the nurses hair. Yeah, this is a sick place. Now, where the hell is room 420. “I would have expected her to be under police protection. Especially since her husband is a coward.” Boda talks to himself as he stands in front of Room 420. *Boda opens the door slowly, as we are greeted by the beeping and buzzing of medical equipment used to keep patients alive. It is dark in the room, except for the little light over the bed that nurses use to check patient vitals in the nighttime. Chris walks in, and over towards the bed. He stops in front of the sink, and looks at his reflection in the mirror. An evil, sinister person looks back at him. Boda lays down the single black rose, and turns on the faucet. He starts to wash his hands* Chase and Williams, The Controversial Messiahs. Two thorns in my side since PCW2. Both claim to be better than me, yet, I’m the one wearing the gold. Miya, Natalie’s husband, foe, former champion.... my partner this week. Over my..... no, over Natalie’s dead body that is. How dare Duke book Miya and me as a team. Hell, that little man will probably attack me, instead of fight with me. I don’t blame young Alexander though. After all, he blames me for putting his wife through all this. But, we all know it was his fault. He brought her into PCW. He made her a fixture here, and as a manager/valet, she is as capable to being injured as everyone else. But look at her over there, she looks all peaceful. Almost angelic like. *Boda grabs a paper towel, and wipes his hands dry. He turns, wads the towel up, and shoots it into the waste receptacle. The Big Bad Boda Daddy then walks to the patient’s bed, and reads the chart.* Patient: Natalie MiyagawaD.O.B: ******Sex: FemaleSymptoms: Blood loss, severe head trauma, severe internal bleeding.Mechanism of injury: Severe trauma from short fall *Boda stops, and takes out a marker, and scribbles something on the chart* Mechanism of injury: Bounced Czech to the concreteOther precautions: Patient is pregnant, about 4 months. All care is directed to both patient and unborn.Blood Type: DR A Negative Rare Eastern European Blood Type. Only 2 other known matches in World.Prospect: Looks grim, without proper blood transfusion of said blood type. *Boda puts the chart back, as the rest is plain old doctor gibberish. Boda steps to the side, where the beeping and dinging of the equipment gets louder. He looks down at Natalie, who is laying there, with tubes in her mouth to keep her breathing. Boda reaches out and grabs her hand in his.* “Ah, sweet Natalie Miyagawa. Wife, and soon to be mother.” Boda rubs Natalie’s face with his free hand. “Nothing personal dear, it was just business. This would never of happened if the one that you love, Alex, never cheated to beat me for the title. But, it will soon be over, oh yes it will. The pain, the suffering of you having to live with, and be married to Alex will soon be over. Courtesy of Boda. And, as for the child inside your womb.... don’t fret, for you and he will forever be together. As for your husband..... first I must deal with him as we have a common enemy to face. Like it or not, we will be forced to fight. But, Alex better not try any of his slick Japanese shit, and attack me. No.... because tonight, I have a proposition for Alex. And, if he truly loves you, then I think he’ll take the offer. Of course, Nat.... Miya is not my only obstacle. No. Chris Chase is almost as big a threat to me as your husband is. Not for long. No, for after Spring Fling, hell, maybe after Turmoil, I’m sure Chase won’t mind sharing this room with you as he is going to need some serious recovery.” *Chris looks over at the end table across the bed from him. On it, is a picture of Alexander Miyagawa. Boda stares into the pic, and shakes his head, before looking back down at Natalie.* “Here you lie in this cold hospital, while Miya is where?” Boda questions an unresponsive Natalie. “He’s in Salt Lake City Utah ready to wrestle for another Title shot against me. Tell you what, sweet Natalie, if you were my wife, I’d have never put you in danger like Alex did. But, you chose him. Or, as he said, he chose PCW, and PCW found you for him. Well, speaking of choices..... I guess I have a moral dilemma to face. To save your life, or not too. Only time will tell, only time will tell.” *Boda kisses Natalie on the cheeks, then smells her hair. He hears voices coming down the hall, so hurriedly, Boda places the single black rose on her chest, and rushes out of the room, just in time to miss the doctor and nurse coming in to check on her. Boda heads out to the 4th floor elevators, as the scene fades out to black.*
