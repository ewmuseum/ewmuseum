Metal Gear Spoofage (part 4)

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/02 Once more our intrepid hero marches onward. Haven disposed of the threat of Vulcan Meanstreak, he now heads toward his final confrontation.... Snake: (Looking up slowly) Metal Gear. Indeed, before Snake's very eyes stands the machine itself. Towering over him, strangely animalistic....it waits, unmoving. Snake: Uh.... The warrior clutches his earpiece, phoning in. Snake: Colonel, how can I.... Colonel: (In a cheerleader outfit) I'm a little teapot, short and stout, this is my handle, this is my spou..... Snake: (Shaking his head) Bethany. How can I stop.... Bethany: The blood of a thousand virgins shall lead to the dawning of the plague. All life shall die, in these, the darkest day...... Snake: Master, how can I stop this thing? Master: Well, we could have just planted a bomb in his items while he was in the torture room, but nooooo.....you had to play your silly little torture game didn't you Hosse....oh THERE you are Snake. Flustered, Master turns his back and motions an unknown someone away. Snake: ....Master? Master: Yes? Snake: You seem kind of....off. Master: What ever do you mean? Snake: Uh, nothing. Never mind. Cutting off the connection, Snake walks to the leg of Metal Gear....grunts and begins to climb. Hand by hand, foot by foot he scales....finally he tops out, laying flat on his back at the top. Snake: Phew. Sitting up, the legend breathes deeply....then pauses as he notices something in the distance from his new, higher vantage point. Snake: .....Liquid? Indeed, across the massive chamber stands the terrorist ringleader himself. Inside a smaller command booth he confers with Revolver Hosselot, his back turned. Quickly Snake clutches his ear. Snake: Master, come in! On the monitor Master appears. However he quickly ducks out of sight....a rustling sound commences. After a moment he pops back on screen. Master: Uh, yes Snake, what is it? Snake: (Looking over) I see Liquid over there! As Snake looks on, the terrorist appears to duck out of sight quickly as Hosselot visibly sighs. He then pops up again, straightening out his coat. Snake: Master! Master: (Fumbling with his uniform quickly) Just a second! Hosselot: Dear god. Snake: What was that? Master: Uh, nothing. What is it Snake? Snake: (Looking up) I think I might be able to snipe Liquid from right here! In the distant chamber Clyde again throws himself on the ground. Again sighing deeply, Hosselot shakes his head and hunches over to assist. Snake: Master! Master: (Rising with his hair on backwards and his sunglasses upside down) What!? Snake: ..... Shaking his head, Snake continues along the walkway winding around the top of the giant bipedal death robot.....eventually he makes his way to a small staircase. And at the end of this, a command center. Snake: Liquid! Hiding around the corner, the hero eavesdrops with his ear pressed to the wall. Hosselot: Why do you insist on keeping that stupid disguise up anyway? Liquid Clyde: It's for aesthetic value! Don't you understand anything about super villainy? Hosselot: I understand only a complete idiot couldn't tell that you're really Master. Liquid Clyde: Bah! Snake kneels carefully, touches his ear. Snake: Master.... Liquid Clyde Yes....I mean uh...no....I mean..... Hosselot: Don't answer the call you fool! Liquid Clyde: Argh! Hosselot: He's hiding right around the corner, can't you see his foot sticking out!? Liquid Clyde: (Turning around) Why I believe you're right. Hosselot: So move so I can SHOOT HIM! Liquid Clyde: You don't have to yell. Grudgingly, Liquid steps to the side. Hosselot takes aim and fires his pistol, nicking Snake in his exposed toe. Snake: AGH!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! The hero hops up and down in agony, right in front of the doorway where Clyde and Hosselot regard him. Liquid Clyde: Fancy seeing you here, brother. Snake: (Hopping) Did you have to go for the foot? Sick freak! Liquid Clyde: Mwahahaha! Here, catch! Liquid reaches down, scooping up a large book off a nearby table. He then chucks it at Snake, striking him and knocking him back a bit. Snake: Hey! Stop that! Liquid Clyde: You need a little education in your diet. Here, have an ENCYCLOPEDIA! Again a book flies, hitting Solid in the head. Liquid Clyde: And WAR IN PEACE! Snake: AGH! Liquid Clyde: And Stephen King's uncut hardcover version of the STAND! Hosselot: This won't be pretty. Using all of his strength, Liquid hauls up the massive tome and chucks it at his brother. It catches him in the midsection, sending him stumbling back to the guardrail and tumbling straight off. Snake: AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! AGH!!!!! Down, down falls Solid Snake. His last vision, the mad cackling of his brother as he waves. Hosselot: About time you did something right. Liquid Clyde: Shut up. Through the blackness plummets the hero, until at last he painfully crashes off Metal Gear's knee. He then bounces to the floor, landing in a heap. Snake: Someone....take....my mind....off....the pain..... Bethany: Very well. A bolt of agony shoots through Snake's testicles, he screams and hops to his feet. Snake: OKAY, THAT'S ENOUGH!!!!! Bethany: You sure? Snake: YES!!!!! Bethany: Meh. The virus temporarily ceases again.....Snake hobbles to Metal Gear and again begins to slowly climb. Slowly, slowly he makes his way back to the top, eventually topping out again. Snake: Let....me....die.... Again the wave of pain rips through Solid's balls. Whimpering, he rolls onto his face. Snake: I didn't mean that literally....agh..... Bethany: Oops. With tears streaming from his eyes, our legend crawls once more towards the control room. Snake: Will....rip.....gizzard....out.....eat....your....soul..... Muttering endless threats and curses, Snake finally reaches the chamber again. Climbing inside, he pulls himself up to a computer monitor and fumbles in his pockets, producing a key card. Snake: This better work. Shakily, the man places the small card in a slot located at the side of the comp. Gently it slides within....then a series of crunching sounds emanate. Followed by shredding sounds, followed by burning sounds. Snake: That can't be good. After a long moment of silence, the monitor buzzes to life. Voice: PAL code recognized. Snake: Thank god. Voice: Metal Gear activated. Nuclear modules online. Selected target, your current location. Command room sealed, gas activated. Kiss your ass goodbye in t-minus 400 seconds. Have a nice day. Snake: .....oh, crap. Snake bolts for the far door, only for it to slam shut on him. He pounds on it, only for his ear to ring. Master: Thank you Snake. You activated Metal Gear for us. I really must express my gratitude. Snake: Who are you? Colonel: Uh, Snake, I think it's fairly obvious. Master: Mwahahaha. Dear brother, you've helped us greatly! Snake: Who ARE you? Bethany: God, how stupid are you!? Master: ......very well, Snake. Behold! With that Master pulls at his hair, removing it entirely. He then takes off his sunglasses revealing..... Liquid Clyde: Me! Snake: .....oh my God! Bethany & Colonel: (Slap their foreheads) Liquid Clyde: You may die now! Liquid signs off, as the slow hiss of poison gas begins to flood the chamber. Snake: (Coughing) Makes me long for Amy's nice smell.....well, maybe not. Ack..... Solid slowly slinks to the ground, covering his mouth with his sleeve. His codec then beeps again. Byron: Snake, I'm here! Snake: Just because I'm going to be dying soon doesn't mean you can take advantage. Freak. Byron: .....fine, stay there and choke. Snake: Wait, can you get the door open? Byron: Sure, just give me a second. Snake: Don't try anything once it's open, alright? Byron: Try what!? Snake: You know, your 'stuff'. Come on, I've read all about those alternative lifestyles, I'm not dumb. Byron: Ugh, save yourself. I'm leaving. Snake: Wait! Byron: What? Snake: Open the door! The world's counting on me! Byron: Oh very well. A series of blips and bloops ring out, and the door slides open once more. Snake tumbles out, rolling down the stairway and landing painfully at the base. Snake: Argh..... And nearby, a tall man with a burned face watches and sneers. Liquid Clyde: Brother, did you like my sunglasses? Snake: Who are you? Liquid Clyde: I'm you. I'm your shadow. Snake: So, it was YOU who always scared me when I was a little kid. Liquid Clyde: .....right. Anyway Snake, to put a long story short, I am your brother. Snake: (Gasps) Liquid Clyde: That's right, we're twins. But not ordinary twins....we were cloned from our 'father'. The greatest soldier of all time, Dai Lo. Once upon a time. A secret government lab. Men and women in clean, white lab coats surround a young asian fellow. Dai Lo: What you fuckin want, white boys? I know how shoot self up! Man: We need to take a sample of your cells. Dai Lo: Crazy foo! You never take Dai Lo alive! The ultimate soldier whips his fist up, catching the closest scientist in the face. He then backhands the next, springing to his feet. Dai Lo: Come and get some! In a flurry of kung fu moves, Dai battles off the legions of poor workers....till finally one manages to slip behind him, sinking a needle full of sedative into his neck. Dai Lo: Ah....damn it all. Night night. Dai Lo slinks to the table, where the scientists shake their heads..... Liquid looks on wistfully.....then snaps his head down. Liquid Clyde: Wake up! Snake: (Snoring) Huh? Wha? Liquid Clyde: Anyway, we were cloned from Dai, along with six other brothers. They were killed off to encourage strong growth in us. Yes, we were murderers even before we were born, brother. Again Clyde looks down, then does a double take. Liquid Clyde: Will you wake up? This stuff is important you know! Snake: (Snoring) More cookies....habeebebebe..... Liquid Clyde: BAH~! The evil one leaps to his side, into the air and straight into the waiting cockpit of Metal Gear. The beast roars to life, as Liquid laughs. Liquid Clyde: Now I will show you the power of the weapon that will lead us in the 21st and a half century! Snake: NO! In panic Snake leaps off the balcony, all the way to the floor. His legs buckle from the impact, he falls to the floor. Snake: Dear God that hurts. The huge nuclear equipped walking battle tank stomps forward, shaking the very ground. The hero backpeddles, looking to the left and to the right. Snake: How do I stop it!?!? A blast of hot missile death erupts next to Snake. He spirals through the air, landing hard on the ground then rolling to the other side just in time to avoid being ripped to bits by machine gun fire. Liquid Clyde: What's wrong, Snake? Snake: You're trying to kill me! Liquid Clyde: Well, yes, I see your point. Again Clyde fires a missile, narrowly dodged by Snake. Frantically he calls in. Snake: Byron, what do I do? Byron: Uh, um....I know! Try saving your mission! Snake: Try whating my what? Byron: Save your mission! I forgot to tell you, there was frequency set up so you could back up your data. Snake: And that means!? Byron: If you get killed you can start over from the last place you died! Snake: ......cool! Byron: Yeah! Snake: What's the frequency? Byron: Contact 140.92. Snake: Gotcha! Racing behind a nearby box, Snake dials into his codec again. Snake: Uh, hello? Mei Loc: What you fuckin want white boy? Snake: Uh.... Mei Loc: WHAT YOU FUCKIN WANT!? Snake: Wait a second, weren't you Big Boss about three seconds ago? Mei Loc: This low budget spoof, silly white boy! Snake: Oh yeah. Mei Loc: I ask again, WHAT YOU WANT? Snake: .....nothing. Mei Loc: Thank you, come again. Somewhat confuddled, Snake hangs up just in time for a rocket to cause his covering box to explode. He flies back again, slumping against the wall. Liquid Clyde: Now you're MINE! Just as the huge machine bears down on our hero, a lone figure pops out of nowhere. In an amazing display of strength he slips underneath Metal Gear's foot, holding it aloft. Snake: You! Akininja: Us. Snake: Don't start with that again. Akininja: We are prisoners of death. Only you can free us. Snake: Uh, if we don't do something about the kook in the big robot we're all gonna be initmately aquanited with death. Liquid Clyde: Stop blabbering during my big scene. DIE!!!!! Liquid raises the foot of the death machine, shaking it like a wet dog. At the end of its foot the Akininja hangs, gripping his toe for dear life. Akininja: Help....us....twit...... Snake: I'll save you! Searching around, Snake's eyes fall upon a conveniently placed set of Stinger missiles and a launcher. He grabs hold, takes aim and fires. Snake: See you in Hell. The rocket bursts forth.....zooms towards its target. Then, as it reaches Metal Gear's steel casing it veers to the left. Snake: .....uh oh. The heat seeking missile makes its choice, choosing of course the source of heat. Unfortunately this proves to be the Akininja rather than the cold metal of the robot, and blows him into a million pieces. Aki's body sails in all directions, his severed torso landing directly in front of Snake. Akininja: You idiot. Snake: Ulp. Liquid Clyde: Die! Clyde stomps forward again, aiming his massive rail gun at Snake. Then he takes a step, but stops short. Liquid Clyde: Uh? Another step, but Liquid finds himself unable to move. He struggles, struggles....then grunts in frustration. Liquid Clyde: Hosselot, I told you to put gas in this thing after you go out joyriding! NOOOOOO!!!!!!! The massive machine buckles at the knees.....Clyde tries to steady it, but it is to no avail. Metal Gear falls to its knees, a massive explosion ripping forth from its body. The impact manages to take Snake off his feet, slam him into the wall, drop him to the floor again, lift him up AGAIN, flip him all the way over and drop him on his head. Snake: Uggggggh..... Snake lies on his face as the world fades around him. All that is in his vision, the burning wreckage of Metal Gear..... then footsteps footsteps drawing closer a human form Clyde walks from the fire. He walks towards Snake. He fills Snake's vision, even as it fades out..... To Be Continued
