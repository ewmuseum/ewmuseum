The Peril of Night Part 3

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 She froze upon the spot, as that cold voice broke the warmth and silence, plunging her back into the reality that she was insignificant and that this force was impending doom upon her. She could do nothing but watch and tremble in fear, as she stood shaking so violently that the forest floor creaked. The figure simply stood there, as if it was watching her in some sort of amusement, mocking her as she stood there a quivering mess. A brief low-pitched chuckle ebbed from the fiend’s mouth as it stood opposite her. She could make nothing of it, as much as she scrambled her mind, her memories and her thoughts to try and comprehend this being, she could not. Who was it and why was it doing this to her, how did it achieve such awesome powers that even her own were nothing in comparison? The thoughts continued to trickle through her mind, as she wondered so many things. The figure still stood there, as her total shock began to break and her heart rate began to fall to a reasonable pace, although she could not help but breathe heavily still trying to capture the breath that had been whisked away form within her by the coming of this being. It then moved for the first time since it’s sudden appearance from nowhere and it paced two steps closer toward her. She backed away one step to try and slowly edge away from it. She knew she would fail her bid for escape but she knew that she must try, for this situation worried her deeply. Perhaps for the first time she would come out worse and be destroyed by this seemingly awesome power. It was not in her nature to simply wait for destruction she would fight it in any way she could. With these new thoughts in mind she stepped a few feet back, as the figure once more stood, with only it’s neck rotating to view her and observe her, as it seemed. Perhaps this was something more sinister, yet she no longer cared, all she wanted was to escape this thing that had seemingly captured her. The hunter had become the hunted. Her curiosity could well have led her to this impending feeling of fate, but her desire may be able to free her. And so she continued to edge backward, slowly moving away from the being, as it stood there, still as the night itself just glaring at her movements, watching them all so carefully but doing nothing to stop her. The somewhat lacking interest of this powerful being only caused greater intimidation to Anna and so she turned and lifted her feet. She quickly removed her black shoes as she started to run from it. The voice that had haunted her for so long had now become something far darker than she could ever have comprehended and it was following her. She did not want to be caught and so she fled. Tossing her heels into the general direction of the being, they both hit it and seemed to bounce off in opposite directions, due to some sort of force field that surrounded the immense power of this person. Despite the enigma that raged in her mind like a storm, because of the somewhat invincible properties of the being that followed her, she sprung to her feet and fled for her life. The heat of the moment raged, as she ran and ran still further away into the night. Trying to escape destiny and whatever the twisted motives of this figure were, she had longed so badly to contact it, but now she had what she wanted, how could she complain? She had no will to begin considering the depths of the irony, although she recalled as she ran an instance during her childhood where she had a similar scenario on her hands. She stopped for a brief moment to catch her breath. She saw nothing move around her, one again she had reached a silent neck of the woods, not even the crickets in the grass stirred; yet the silence was what she feared most. For several minutes she stood in silence, not even the wind’s bluster would accompany her lost soul, as it trembled with fear, causing her body to quake with anxiety. She sighed with relief, as she sat upon the nearby fallen tree. Despite how uncomfortable she was, she was less than concerned about anything but keeping silent and hiding from whatever was following her. She had a great feeling of ambiguity to whether or not she was still being pursued, or whether she had been pursued at all in the first place, yet she stayed there, feeling that the safest thing she could do was to hide in the underbrush and hope she was not found. Then from nowhere she was tapped upon the shoulder. She leapt from the log upon which she sat, as if something had scolded her. She turned to see what had touched her in the forlorn hope that perhaps she was mistaken and perhaps it was not this looming figure, from which she had fled. However, as she had known all too well it was indeed the figure of such dark nightly powers. She grimaced and shook her head trying to claw her eyes shut, but she could not for it seemed to almost overpower her, as it stood there, simply observing her once again with it’s disturbing and twisted glare. Knowing that running or hiding were no longer any use, she looked up with newfound bravery and courage, which was false, for she looked only because she had no other choice. It glared at her, as if it were surprised by her ability to look upon it. The beast seemed to snarl and grunt at her once, although she managed to keep an emotionless look upon her face, as she returned its glare. The two were locked in a heated staring silence, before this was broken, by Anna’s new approach. “How do you look upon me, Anna?” Enquired the figure in a raspy voice. Anna kept her face entirely straight, not wanting to look afraid and then replied:. “Isn’t this what you wanted. Why else would you have followed me so far?” She answered with a question of her own. The figure’s face almost seemed to fluctuate, as if it were irritated by her comment. “It is you who hunted me.” It replied. Anna knew this was true but still tried to keep a confident approach, despite the fear that welled up inside her. “Enough of this small-talk, why have you followed me?” Demanded Anna in the heat of the moment. As she did this facial expression became a blinding scowl, as the figure readied a reply for her and then spoke. “Because we want you Anna.” Said the voice in such a sinister and blood-curdling tone that the hearts of fiends and devilish creatures would be turned to stone in fear. Anna’s eyes widened and her lips began to move in a state of confusion, before she enquired. “What do you mean ‘we’? I don’t understand, there is only one of you!” she said, now becoming frustrated and a little worried by the sinister riddle left in her mind, which set off a chain reaction causing her true emotions to begin floating to the surface. “We are both many and one Anna. For I am one of many that are like me. You cannot understand for you have been lonely for so long, but we can change all that. We are not like other people Anna, we are like you, we are unique.” Explained the voice in a deep and husky monotonic voice once more. “You are like me?” Replied Anna, in a somewhat intrigued, yet astonished fashion. For she never thought it could be true. When she said this she had pointed to herself to iterate her surprised nature and the cloaked figure simply responded with two nods of its black hood. “I… I… just don’t know…” Said Anna in a tone that showed her as blatantly confused. She looked down to the floor, as if looking for an answer but could not think what she should do, she didn’t know whether or not to go along with this mysterious figure. For she wanted to be with people like herself, yet she also knew little of this character and wished to know more. “We must go, we shall return. Then we will need your answer.” Said the voice as the black figure slowly walked backward and vanished into the shadows of the towering trees from whence it came. Anna simply stood confused, as the anguish, fear and concern that she dwelt upon drew to a sudden halt. She needed to make her decision, but what should she do? As she walked back through the forest, an enigma grew within her mind, with every step questions came to her. Who was the figure? When would it return? What should she do? The questions creating a maze of twisted confusion and a whirling puzzle within her mind, she was tired and weary and simply could not decide what to do or how to answer these questions within her head. Then from nowhere it came to her. Suddenly it became so clear, as if the scissors of enlightenment had cut the knot of puzzling fibers and enigmatic strands of confusion, she knew exactly what she must do. Then, as she reached a clearing in the forest, she looked up toward the moon and raised her hand before her eyes. She then closed her eyes and recited something from within her memory, as if she had read it one million times aloud: “Seta opa kaya…” A fire grew in her hand as she simply stood there laughing insanely, she had made her decision.
