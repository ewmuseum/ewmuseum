Die...For Your Cause...

Date: not entered “All men get out there on the double!!! We need everyone in their riot gear with tear gas, pepper spray, and stun guns!!!” Pandemonium wrecked through the 22nd Kendall Police Department. Everyone was running in one direction or another after the call had come in of rioters wreaking havoc downtown in the business district. The Special Forces has mobilized in the parking lot with their armor on, and were being handed the essential weapons they needed before piling into the SWAT van. Tires burned rubber as they sped out of the underground garage and into the city, sirens wailing the entire way. Inside the van, tension was thick. This was one of those pressurized situations that called for a cool head and a strong body. One slip up, and you either had an incident on your hands, or a thousand hands on your body…beating you to death. In a sense, it was one against thousands…one against many… But in the end…it was always the few in numbers that prevailed… They were more sadistic…more savage…more crafty…and completely and utterly psycho down the wire. One tall officer lifted his visor up and took a huge breath. Letting it out, he noticed a leg jittering over in the other side of the van. He followed the leg up to the man’s face, a young looking guy with sandy brown hair. His visor was down, but he could see through the glass that he was totally nervous. “Hey rookie!” the tall man barked, looking him down in his fear stricken eyes. The boy jumped before looking up in his superior’s face. “Sir?” he whimpered…no way a cop should sound. “Toughen up! This is the real thing! None of that simulation sh!t back at the academy! You go out there with your knees knocking and your teeth chattering and you’ll work those sons of b!tches into a frenzy!” With all he could muster, he gathered himself to a steady composition. The older cop nodded. “Good, kid. That’s how you gotta look out there. You are the law…it’s us against them, and we’re outnumbered at least 9 to 1 usually. If you don’t keep your head sharp out there it’s your life, got that?” “Yes, sir,” He said, a little weak, but a lot more confident than before. “What’s your name, kid?” “Ken…Jones.” The older guy stretched his hand out to him. “Sergeant Lou Stevens, pleasure to meet you.” They shook hands…Lou had a tight grip. “You stick near me when we get out there, I’ll make sure you don’t get lost in the shuffle.” “Right!” a little more enthusiasm came flowing into him. Just then, there was a commotion from the front of the van. A lot of yelling got the attention of the officers in the back of the van. “What do you mean you need backup? We sent in two units as soon as the call came in ten minutes ago!” the officer on the passenger’s side spoke to the dispatcher through the radio like she was crazy. “Is it that many civilians on a rampage that you need THREE more vans?” “GET A CHOPPER OUT THERE TOO!!” She responded, sounding a bit panicked. “I LOST CONTACT WITH ONE OF THE OFFICERS BEFORE HE COULD DESCRIBE THE NATURE OF THE SITUATION.” The bearded man speaking into the radio swore under his breath as he looked to the driver. “Right!! We’ll be upon the scene in less than three minutes! You make sure you get those other two vans and that chopper on the scene ASAP!!!” Lou looked over to Ken and nodded solemnly. “You hear that? It’s getting wild out there. This isn’t the time to get a pink stripe down your back!” That’s what they referred to as going chicken in the force…turning pink…lot better than turning yellow. Ken nodded as the officer up front looked back to the team. “Listen up! We’re deploying you about two blocks away, so that there’s no trouble if the rioters see the van approaching! Once you are dispatched, you are to maintain order and keep the peace as peaceably as possible WITHOUT resorting to drawing your gun. We do not want casualties here! Arrest if you see fit; there will be a fire truck on the way with a hose to keep them back! DO YOU COPY??” “ROGER!!” everyone complied as they prepared themselves for the battle. The gas masks went on, and the guns got loaded. Everyone grew silent as the tires screeched to a halt. Game time… “MOVE! MOVE! MOVE!” Everyone dived out of the van and began scattering in all directions. They were all to converge upon the same point, but the key strategy was to surround the area and swarm in upon the rioters. It wasn’t always an effective plan. But this time…maybe it would work. Lou and Ken ran off with a few other officers into the northeast direction. They slinked through the parking lots and through the back alleys, drawing nearer and nearer to the crisis zone. They could hear the crackle of flames nearby, along with the sound of destruction amidst. Ken thought back to what he was doing before the call came in…watching the last part Tuesday Turmoil back at the precinct. They were in this town, live for the night. He would have gone if not for the fact that he was on duty tonight. The rioters were probably revolting from the return of the Dark Age to PCW by Tyler Nelson. He shrugged it off as a few screams came through the air. “OPEN FIRE!!!” And whoever it was that yelled that did open fire…except it was gunfire. Lou stopped in the middle of the alley they were running down and cursed under his breath. “Son of a b!tch!! There’s always some trigger happy mother f^cker that thinks with bullets before the gas!! Now there’s gonna be an incident with citizen shootings!!!” They walked on a bit further until they found the source of the shooting. Three lone cops were backing off into the end of a closed alley, shooting off their rifles at whatever was on the other side of the alley. Ken watched as the third officer began unloading gas shells upon the convergence closing in on them. Lou glowered at the gun toting men, yelling at them from their blind spot on the left. “STOP SHOOTING, D@MMIT!!” The three of them looked over in their direction…more than enough time for a blocky blue mailbox to come crushing down upon them. Ken’s eyes widened as Lou turned to the other two men with them. “You two!!! Get out there and fire off some gas grenades!! Make sure you slow those rioters down!!” They rushed past Lou and Ken and stepped into the alley. As soon as they raised their guns, a huge dumpster came rolling into them, crushing them against the wall of the alley! Lou was getting pissed. He looked back at Ken and nodded. “Me and you now, boy! Let’s go do it!!” Ken nodded, and the two of them dived out into the alley. In front of them, gas clouded their view like a poison fog. They rushed ahead with their gas guns drawn, ready for anything. Suddenly, they heard a low rolling coming towards them. Ken looked at Lou, who looked back at him after staring ahead for a few minutes. “JUMP!!” Ken and Lou dropped their guns and leaped up. They grabbed the railing of the overhead fire escape just as a large Buick came gunning past the down the alley. The car crashed into the dumpster and burst into flames. Lou dropped back down and grabbed his gun. Looking back, he noticed the slow puddle forming under the car. “RUN, D@MN YOU RUN!!!” Ken wasted no time in dropping down and making a break for it. He and Lou dived out of the alley just as a huge ball of flame came gushing from the alley. Ken pulled off his gas mask and coughed as Lou stretched his back out. “What’s going on here!?” Ken asked Lou. “It’s like a war zone!” “That’s how it is! Can’t turn your back or you’re dead!!” Ken adjusted his glasses before putting them back on. Then when he looked ahead, his eyes almost fell out. They stood in a parking lot, and scattered around the lot were parked cars. They looked completely destroyed, their hoods pounded in, their roofs flattened, some flipped onto their sides or their backs, but all of them were on fire. And that wasn’t the worst part…there were bodies all around the ground…some on fire…some ripped apart by their limbs… Lou stepped ahead and looked over the bodies a little more closely. “That’s the teams we sent out earlier!! What’s the f^ck’s going on here!!” He reached down and pulled up his radio. “This is Sergeant Lou Stevens on riot patrol! We have a lot of casualties here in the parking lot of 9th and 5th! It’s looks like the first two teams sent out…or at least what’s left of them!! We’re going to need more forces out here!! Maybe even the d@mn National Guard!!” “ROGER! HAVE YOU ENGAGED THE RIOTERS YET?” “Negative on visual, but we have been attacked through the gas. I’ll keep in contact!” he put the radio up and looked back to Ken. “Let’s move, kid!” Ken was hunched over splattering his dinner out on the pavement. Lou shook his head and slipped his gas mask back on. “First time you’ve seen a dead body before, son?” Ken nodded slowly, a sick look in his eyes. “I’m all right now…let’s go.” He wiped his mouth and put on his mask again. He was set to run off, but Lou stopped him. ‘Wait…do you hear that?” The rookie listened…nothing but crunching glass, and chaos. “What?” “You hear the rampage being caused…but do you hear any people?” Lou tapped his ears and pointed to his head. “What kind of rioters are silent when they riot?” Just then, a huge roar echoed through the air. Ken and Lou jumped as a few more screams came out into the air. Lou cocked his gas gun and nodded in the direction of the noise. “There’s our answer right there!!” It was only about a half a mile away that they heard the noise. They ran up from around the corner into the middle of Main Street, and they were in for a surprise. All the way down the street…about nine blocks…the riot ensued…the officers tried as best as they could to keep them back…but they proved to be ineffective. Two men sailed through the air, crashing into the display window of an electronics store. Another man went head first into a fire hydrant, his body flipping back from the pressure of the newly created public fountain. The other five officers came rushing back from out of the cloud of gas, shooting shells all the way. Ken and Lou watched silently as the cops fled from whatever was in the cloud… …And then…they saw them… With a tremble, a black silver buckled boot stomped out from the gas, and then another. The boots trailed up a pair of multi-zippered black vinyl pants, and then into the huge chest covered in black mesh material. And then…the image of Akiyama came into the clear…and Ken had nothing else to say but… “OH…MY…GOD.” The nVg let out a huge roar as he went running for the five officers. He speared three of them to the ground, allowing two of them to run off down the street. Just then, a woman in black and ice blue vinyl ran in front of them. She wasted no time in going into a handstand and kicking across both the throats of the cops, a thin line cut through their necks as blood spilled from them, sending them to the ground instantly. She came back onto her feet…and for the first time, Cyclonica’s face was not emotionless. The Divine Prophetess…was pissed off. ‘How DARE those filthy BASTARDS deny US!!!” she growled, walking towards Akiyama slowly. He stood above the three cops he attacked and was kicking the guts out of their chests. “Those bookers…those sand rats…those filthy MORTALS!!! THEY DARED TO THINK THEY COULD SIMPLY REFUSE US OF WHAT RIGHTFULLY BELONGS TO THE GOD?!!” She watched as one of the cops tried crawling away as Akiyama’s attention focused onto crushing the skulls of the other two men into the street with his hands. She watched him like a cat stalking a mouse. Then she grabbed him by the back of his belt, holding him in place. “You bugs will be exterminated from our way!! All of you insects…will DIE!!” Her razor sharp stiletto heel stabbed into the back of his head, killing him instantly. She withdrew her heel and walked off, a crimson dot trailing behind her on the ground. Akiyama left his own mess of the two cops behind, letting out a growl before tearing a light post in half with a lariat. Lou couldn’t believe this….not one bit. “What is this??? There’s only two of them!!! Where’s the rest of them???” Ken watched silently as the Divine Prophetess and the Neo Void God came stalking towards them, their black enraged eyes locked on them. He felt himself stepping back…away from them. “Um…run…” Lou looked at his partner. “What did you say?” “RUN!!” “I’m not running from one man!! Are you insane!!” Pride…gotta love those things about humans that make them completely oblivious to the true fear of god. But Ken knew the nVg…he knew the fear of god…it controlled his life, even when not faced with the chaos of Akiyama. He ran…smartest thing he did all night. But you should know not to turn your back on the god…that’s when the apocalypse is brought upon your absolute destiny. Ken was lucky this time…two swat vans sped into the street, blocking the Celestials from our mortal friends Lou and Ken. They had another chance to live… …For a while… The two vans filed one behind the other, forming a blockade in front of the Celestials. The back of the vans flew open, allowing a fresh swarm of officers to flood out in full battle armor, decked with batons with stun guns on the end…fancy cattle prods. They also had the huge shields that could defend them from attack…mortal-devised attacks, anyway… strapped across their chests was a modified assault rifle, with smoke and tear grenades, along with the usual grenades for those hard to kill moments. Looks like the Calvary has arrived… “Worthless!! You think you can stop us? We will not be disrespected by the lowest of existence! Not tonight…and not next week by a couple of pintsized PEASANTS!!” Cyclonica ran off to the side, while the god ran straight for the men. There were at least 20 of them out there before him, all with their arms raised to strike, and with their stun batons crackling with energy. “HOLD YOUR GROUND, MEN!!!” One of them called out. They all stood fast…watching with wide eyes as the Neo Void God came roaring upon them like a howling beast of the dammed… =©= “What the hell’s wrong with you, boy??? Why’d you take off like that??” Lou made sure that his anger was expressed, yelling his words at the rookie. Ken stood against the wall of a nearby alley catching his breath. “Ak…i….ya…ma…” “What the hell are you talking about?” patience was not an option. “What the hell’s an Akiyomama?” “Akiyama…he’s the Neo Void God.” Ken hissed out through tired breaths. Lou looked at him like he was crazy. “You’re not digging into the stash down in evidence locker, are ya?” “He’s one of the guys from PCW! I saw him on Fusion tonight!!” Lou was not getting the point…he must have fallen into the slow category like the rest of humanity. “You’re telling me that this guy’s some steroid monkey wrestler you saw on TV?” Ken nodded, causing Lou to stare at him for a moment. Then he began walking out towards the part of the alley they came in from. “Where are you going?” “I’m going to stop that whacko freak.” Ken looked at him like he was crazy. “Are you mad?” “You’re the godd@mn mad one here! I’m not scared of some mad @ss Jap in plastic pants running around like he’s Godzilla!!!” “You’ve never seen the stuff he’s capable of!!” Ken was pleading with him now. Lou just wasn’t having it. “I don’t care if he can shoot fire out his @ss!! I’m stopping this guy!” “You can’t! You don’t know who he is?” Lou was drawing the line with him, drawing his pistol and checking for bullets. “Ken…listen to yourself…you’re afraid of one man. ONE MAN!! I’m not afraid of one guy…and neither should you!” Just then, a SWAT van came screeching into the wall next to the alley they hid in. It crashed and burst into flames, but Ken and Lou still heard the screams of their comrades from inside. They backed away from the flames, shielding their eyes. Then from around the corner appeared the god…standing amidst the flames like the villain of lore, birthed by flame, allowing rage to burn him from inside. His face was twisted in a sadistic grimace of insanity as he glared on at the two men. Just before Lou could do something, two officers jumped onto Akiyama’s back. They gripped at his neck and shoulders, trying to pull him backwards onto the ground. The nVg shrugged his shoulders, and shifted so that he tossed the two men into the flaming wreckage. His cold black eyes returned to them, staring them down evilly as their burning screams came forth. “There’s no where for you flat-scans to run…” They both turned, and found Cyclonica standing on the other side of the alley. She was licking her ice blue nails of a crimson fluid, like a cat cleaning her paws. But the Divine Prophetess was nobody’s pussy… “Just like for all those others…just like all those others…you will learn to obey…and honor your god…and if you will not learn…you will simply have your meaningless live exterminated from this world.” Lou was livid…he took his gun and walked right for her. Not a smart thing to do…he didn’t see the @sskicking Powell received. “Listen here, you crazy b!tch. You and that muscle freak behind me are under arrest!! Put your freaky hands where I can see them!!” She took a step forward, throwing a crescent kick up in front of her. Her heel drew sparks from the wall as it scraped the brick before landing in front of her. She stared dead into the eyes of Louis Stevens, her lips slightly puckered open. “…Did you just turn…your back…on your god??” Lou didn’t have the chance to look back. Ken was knocked to the side as Akiyama rushed past him and scooped Lou up onto his shoulders. His gun flew from his hands, going off into the wall as it landed. The older man screamed upon the back of the god, his spine was being bent in half as Akiyama held him there…the Celestial Torture Rack. Then Akiyama bounced him forward and spiked him down on the back of his neck. He laid there broken on the cold concrete, no choice but to stare up at the man who had the power to execute the HAND OF FATE, and bring Absolute Apocalypse to someone’s Destiny. Cyclonica walked over to him and knelt down before him, scratching her nails across his face. “You are to NEVER turn your back on the Neo Void God!! That is nothing but disrespect and an insult to his Celestial presence!! Tonight was a bad night to turn away from HIM…mortal scum. Because now…your stupid little life is nothing more than simply…OVER.” Her nails dug into his throat, clutching the nerves laced in his neck. He could do nothing to stop her, his entire body being paralyzed from the ADA. All he could do…was watch his entire world fade out in a silver haze… Blood ran down his nose as he died…tilting head off to the side, the life faded from him. She left slits of red in his flesh as she stood back up, wiping the blood on the brick wall next to her. Then she noticed something… “Come out, come out, where ever you are…” Akiyama snapped his neck back, glaring at the shadow making off behind them on the wall. Cyclonica gave the god an arched eyebrow before walking away the way she came. Akiyama nodded, and the chase was officially on. Ken was on a break for his life. He fled down those streets like a madman; constantly looking back to see how far the god was from catching him. Just as he turned his head, he saw the nVg come stalking around the corner straight for him. A shriek escaped his lips as he pumped his legs faster. Then in the background he heard the rapid motor of the chopper in the distance, slowly approaching. He ran off further down Main Street, past the carnage that had already unfolded. Soon, the helicopter had converged upon the scene, and had a spotlight cast down on the street. The spotlight was once again on the god, which is never a new thing for him. He looked up straight into the light, hissing at the brightness like a snake. Then he reached down, clutching the edge of the manhole cover below. He pulled the iron disc up from the ground and flipped it into the air. Heads or tails? The huge coin rotated into the air, and it smacked against the rear rotating blade of the chopper. Then gravity took effect as it came back and sunk into the roof of the chopper. Stepping over the open sewer drain, Akiyama continued walking…allowing the chopper to crash down to the street behind him in a burst of fire. Ken was in awe…but even awe didn’t stop him from running on. Everything that he ever lived for ran through his mind at that point…love, pride, and duty…and that point it looked like he would never live for those causes ever again. It was his choice to become an officer…and he should have known that at some point he would have to give his life for this job… …For this cause… But it was a foolish venture…because the cause was as pointless as broken pencil. There is no way possible for law and order to exist…period. Balance of the negative and positive must be equal throughout the universe…to extinguish one would mean utter anarchy. Anarchy…it was something Akiyama and Cyclonica were said to usher in quite well…along with terror… Terrorists were not much the same in their own foolish methods…they were willing and risking to give their lives for their cause upon 9/11, and in the end, their cause turned out to get their entire country’s @ss kicked by the free world. And all for what…ALLAH? Because one country felt that another country was becoming too big for their britches? Well…shows what you bastards know… Ken rushed stumbled into a dark alley and tried as hard as he could to keep from breathing. He stared out of that alley waiting for the god to walk by him, and hopefully not see him at all. Those huge dinner plates never blinked as silence ensued… Those huge dinner plates never blinked as he felt the pain shoot through his back. He staggered forward…back into the light. Following him out of the light was the Divine Prophetess, with an emotionless look back on her face. Her right heel was soaked in the man’s blood. Ken felt himself getting weaker, his eyes filling with tears as he realized he was about to die. Die…for your cause… Soon…more terrorists would have to die for their cause…they would have to sacrifice themselves to a god they refuse to acknowledge. Allah will not have pity on their souls in the next life for them offering themselves to end it all at the hands of the nVg. Terrorists would have to lose themselves for their beliefs…for their titles…which belong to the god of tag teams…it didn’t matter at all that they would give themselves up to be destroyed by the Neo Void God. Suddenly Ken had to deal with the HAND OF FATE that was dealt to him…and he saw that he had no choice but to fold. The fist of the god sent him cracking back into the wall of the building behind him. He felt his entire ribcage shatter at the blow, but his pain was not over yet. Cyclonica stepped towards him and stared him in his dying eyes, her hand holding his chin to make him look into her black eyes. “You…have given your life up for nothing…just like the others have and will do…everything that you fight for is worthless in the eyes of the god. Mortal…your priorities are obsolete. And if you don’t realize that you are nothing in a world that he governs…then you will lose your life.” Ken found both heels spiked into his stomach as she flipped him over onto his back. Then she hopped forward and stabbed them both into his chest, piercing through the heart and lungs before back flipping and landing against the wall he previously stood dying against. There was nothing left for him but pain…and as he lied there, he felt even more pain as he looked into the brooding face of Akiyama. He used his last bit of strength to roll himself onto his chest, so he could stare at the ground and say his final prayer to whatever god he worshipped. As pain wrecked his body, it numbed him to a point…and he never felt the boot of the nVg apply pressure to the back of his neck…not even as it snapped and sent him to death… His back was facing the god… …Never turn your back on the Neo Void God… …It will absolutely…be your end… =©=
