Comin' Home Pt.3:Twist Of Fate

19 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/04 Note:Just to make sure it's clear which rps were my world title rps. 'Comin Home Pt. 2&3' are the World Title rps, and 'Comin' Home Pt.1' is just an intro'. 'One more over here, bro'', I called out over the loud dance music. The bartender replied with a nod, and a few seconds later a shot glass filled with whiskey. I was in a local club in Detroit that was filled with men and women dancing their asses off, and me drinking mine off.. It was only about ten hours until Turmoil, but I was in the mood to mope around with some whiskey flowing through my veins. The night before I'd learned of my only true love's suicide from my mentor, Sully. I sat and drank down shot after shot while thinking about Melissa. We'd been a good couple from the end of high school up until she made me choose between her and wrestling. We did everything together, and had a lotta’ fun. 'Why'd she make me choose?', I questioned silently. I was about to ask for another drink, but dropped my shot glass over the counter. Needless to say I'd had enough, and was going to have one hell of a time getting out of the damn place without breaking a few bones. I began to get up, but felt my legs give out causing me to fall back down onto the stool. I sat and stopped thinking about Melissa long enough to wonder one thing. 'How the hell am I gonna' get out of here!' As I sat and contemplated my escape from the Techno Club O' Doom 2004, a young brunette woman was stalking me from behind as I would soon find out. I felt a pair of arms wrap around me, and heard a purring voice in my ear. 'Wanna' come to my place, stud?' I was amazed that even though I probably smelled like I'd bathed in whiskey, this women wanted to get it on with me none the less. I wasn't in the mood for sex at the moment, as I'd been awake since 7:30 a.m. the day before. But if I wanted to get home I was gonna' have to do something. I whispered into her ear that she should come to my place. I stood up and made sure to wrap my arm around her shoulders for a little bit of support as we started out of the bar. We moved slowly down the steps leading to the spacious dance floor. I began to spit some game to make her giggle as we moved. After dodging dancers on the outskirts of the dance floor, we went through the door and the cold brisk air sobered me up a bit. As we moved towards the car I got a good look at the brunette's face for the first time, and it hit me like a sack of bricks. I stood and stared at her with a look of shock on my face, and she simply giggled and asked a simple question. 'What's wrong, Buzz. You look like you've seen a ghost!' 'Oh shit, you're a ghost!', I yelled and she burst into laughter. 'I'm not a ghost you asshole. I heard you were back in town talking to people that you've been ignoring for years. So I thought I'd go see Sully, and teach you a lesson.' I shook my head, and started laughing. I leaned on my car and looked up at her. I smiled and even though she was angry, she couldn't help but smile. 'You can't do that Buzz. You can't just walk out on me for five years, and expect to charm your way back into my life!' 'I never said anything about your life...I was just trying to get back into your pants.' We both laughed and I motioned for her to get into the car. She shook her head, and walked around to the driver's door. She held her hand out and I gave her a high five. She laughed and punched me in the arm. Everything was happening so fast that I was having a tough time keeping up in my drunken state. 'Ouch, what'd I do?' She looked downwards as if it was obvious what she wanted. 'You wanna fuck? Here? It's kinda' cold...' 'No you ass! I want your car keys so I can drive US home.' 'There's an us again?', I asked curiously? She smiled a bit at me as if to say 'duh'. 'Well, come on woman I'm drunk as hell. I can't read minds!', I jokingly yelled. She grabbed me by the arm, and walked me to the passenger side. She unlocked the door, and sat me down. A few seconds later she was sitting behind the wheel and looked over at me with a sly smile. 'We got a lot of catching up to do.' I smiled back at her, and sat back as she pulled out of the parking lot. I thought about the events from December until now, and could only think of one thing besides Melissa and the World Title. I looked up at the black 'heavens' filled with stars, and though to myself. 'What a fuckin' year, dad...what a year. Hope I'm doin' you proud....even if I am an asshole.' The End
