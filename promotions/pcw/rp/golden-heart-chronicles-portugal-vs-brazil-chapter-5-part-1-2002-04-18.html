Golden Heart Chronicles: Portugal vs. Brazil (Chapter 5 Part 1)

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 5 Part 1: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. In chapter three and it’s following parts will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. In chapter 4 Juan focuses on his International match up for the PTC I-PPV. It is composed of his huge Borro (GOAL~!) Bash. The final chapter of the Golden Heart Chronicles is where Juan just puts his main priorities first and does something he hasn’t done in awhile, have fun. Time: 1:53 P.MLocation: Sao Paulo, BrasiliaScene: Bar (World War III has just begun.) Part 1 The scene opens with Juan Borro in Brazil with a group of his die-hard Portuguese national soccer team fans, to be manly enough to do the unthinkable. The unthinkable being stepping foot in a bar in one of the wildest places on the earth wearing rival colors. You see this isn’t just any man coming in to that bar you see, this is PTC’s best International wrestler doing so. Juan has been feeling quite good lately. He’s won his last few matches in PCW showing how great of an athlete he really is. He threw a bash that will never be forgotten in Portugal. And in a huge cluster of a match Juan eliminated six men to be crowned the best International Wrestler in PTC. Since Juan won this match he felt a need to do his usual gloating. Another thing he wants to gloat about is how his former teammates are going to decimate Brazil in soccer for the whole world to see. Juan is shown coming off of a bus with ten other Portuguese national team fans right by his side. These aren’t the usual fat old fans people are used to though. These are all former bodybuilders, professional arm wrestlers, Portuguese indy wrestlers, and backyard wrestlers Juan brought to get his back here in Brazil. I mean what did you think; Juan was going to try and protect himself all alone. He may be the best International wrestler, but he’s not an army. Juan steps foot off the bus with the men by his side, they spot the usual Brazilian chaos. Mostly police chasing people, kids throwing rocks through windows, and attempted suicides. Of course that is nothing unusual in this city of sin. “I guess these people still haven’t learned how to live.” Says one of Juan’s companions who laugh at the hectic sight. “Well boys what do you expect. I mean this is no Portugal. Must I go over the history lesson of how these people were our slaves?” Juan asks his companions as they all nod with a “no”. Juan continues with his crew as they start walking the long block to the bar. It took the people some time to notice, because the Brazilians just aren’t as quick as us Portuguese, but when they did notice the crew it wasn’t too much of a friendly greeting. Yes, we received some boos and curses in our way, but the Brazilians are too cowardly to do anything about it. The block almost ended when a Brazilian gang right in front of the door of the bar confronted them. “Excuse me fellas, but what do you think you doing with them shirt on round here. I wouldn’t want any body to make the wrong impression.” A gang member says as he circles Juan. A second one of the members then comes and strolls around. “Yea, I mean fans of Portugal around here really have to watch themselves. Somebody might get a little upset. And you know when people get upset they do something a little DRASTIC!” The other member says as he pops our knife and breezes his fingers across the sharp blade. The rest of the big men are ready to fight, but Juan holds them back not wanting any unneeded violence. “Boys, boys, boys, what do you think your doing? We just want to go inside and enjoy what should be a good game. If you won’t let us do that I might have to do something drastic myself.” Juan pulls out his knife and smiles. “Oooh one knife, don’t make me piss in my pants. Where’s the bus, we better get the hell out of here? Please, is that the best you can do?” The gang member asks as he turns to the rest of his boys smirking. “Well now that you ask, no it’s not the best we can do. Raul, Nelson, Manny, show them what we got.” Juan says as he nods over to the men. The gang member turns around to see three gats with silencers pointed straight at his head. The ducks and steps backward as his boys just have measly knives. “How the hell did you get that across the border.” One of the gang members shouts out for Juan to here. “I have my ways. Now let us in.” Juan replies. The men step away and Juan and the rest of his boys walk into the noisy bar. When they enter everyone stops what they’re doing and stares at Juan and his group. Juan tries not to seem fazed as he goes to take a seat. When Juan is about to sit down, one of the men puts his foot over the seat in front of the bar, as do the rest of men sitting near empty seats. Juan smirks and makes one hand movement signaling for his boys to get down to the business. Within a good ten seconds all of the front seats were cleared when the bodybuilders picked up the seats with the men on them and placed it behind them. Juan and his group then took seats, as the game was about to start. “What the hell do you stupid Portuguese think your doing over here. Do you know what happens to Portuguese souls that step in this bar?” One of the whiney drunks in the bar shouts out. “Oh and what’s that?” Juan says fully confident. The man then points over to corner where a shadow hides the mans identity. The large man then gets up and steps forward. The man ends up being none other than the CCW legend Andrew “The Ace” Hawk. Juan can’t help but break out laughing along with the rest of his companions. Juan settles down after a few seconds and speaks. “I’m supposed to be scared of this guy, please. I just beat his ass in the ring a few days ago. Why don’t you just save face and leave.” Juan says mocking Hawk, and breaking out with laughs. “The only one that will be leaving is you. You’re the only little piece of crap I see in this bar.” Hawk says as he goes over and picks up Juan by his collar. Juan’s boys come over to attack Hawk, but Juan waives them off once again. “No need guy, Hawk and I are cool. It’s just too bad he likes such a shitty team. I’m sure he has enough brains to let us stay and witness Portugal get their asses hand to them.” Juan says using reverse psychology on Ace. “ You know that’s the truth. You better just sit down and not speak, because if I hear your voice even once, your ass is going through the window.” Hawk says being completely serious as he cracks his knuckles. “ Sounds good. Let me get a drink and we’ll get this thing on our way.” Juan says as he grabs some brandy and gets in his soccer mood. The game begins and the men start drinking like fishes. The first twenty minutes of the game seems to be all Portugal, and Juan shows his enthusiasm. The Portuguese section starts doing drunken chants and dances as the Brazilians just scream and curse at them. Before Juan and his group could do too much celebrating Brazil comes back taking control of the rest of the first half. This time the Brazilians all over the bar and in the streets start celebrating as if they’ve just won the World Cup or something. Juan is staying pretty silent knowing it won’t last. The first half draws to an end, with everyone in the bar completely drunk. The first half intermission goes by and the bar is a complete mess from all of the celebrating. All of the guys are either lying on the ground or half out of it. The two rivals are now singing and dancing together on top of tables and chairs. “Ya know yesterday I was in this store and got this shirt and a guy told me to buy it.” Juan says as he stumbles to the floor. “That’s crazy, who would do such a think.” Hawk responds with his hands in the air chanting loudly. The two men continue to babble on until they almost make some sense. “You know dude, PCW is really getting corrupt with these Ultimate Authority chicks. They like so won’t give RAH and I a match and were going on strike until we do. Is that crazy or what?” Juan says laughing “They’re chicks I thought they were dudes.” Hawk says as he scratches his head. “What ever.” Juan replies. The guys then pass out on the floor as the second period starts. Of course like you all know the match ended in a 1-1 tie. Of course the moral of story is friends are only friends with beer in the hand. Scene Fades to Black. The Following promo was paid for and publicized by the Golden Heart Chronicles.
