Sexaholics - Part 3 - The Meeting

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 3, 2002 [Holy Trinity is a bodacious church. Tommy isn't religious, and neither is Harry. That's why they are taken back by the large gothic structure that is before them. They've never really seen anything like this so upclose. Church is a boring place, they were told by others, but how can a place with far out stained glass be boring? People just take things for granted too much, man. Alas, PCW's most bodacious tag team isn't in front of the church to attend a religious ceremony but to appear at a group meeting that Tommy's therapist told him all about. It may not help, but it will hopefully show both of these ravaged men a way to cure themselves. Sexual addiction. Not a thing to laugh at or to just shrug at. This could be the very thing that has held back both men to their goal of tag team champions. Ridiculous? No way, dudes! It's not in the least. It's a very good explanation for the shortcomings of the tag team in the recent months. Tag team title shots taken away, lost, and forgotten. They have been totally gnarly mainstays of the PCW tag division for quite some time and they have never truly received the gnarly respect they deserve. Sexual addiction? You bet your condoms, man. But they are going for help, for control. They are going to fight their addictions head on. Tommy will control his will to have sex with random women and Harry will finally forget about Hustler magazine once and for all...well, not for all...that's too long, man. But he has to!! DAMNIT! Harry won't listen probably] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: This place is going to cure us of all the bogus sexual stuff we've been feeling, dude. [Harry wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, looking at Tommy with very hateful eyes] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: No way, man. [Tommy sighs in his PCW T-shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black sandals. Harry needs to understand] TTT: Dude, your going to have to put all this stuff behind you. It's totally bogus the way that you look at Hustler, it's all that you seem to do with your free time and it's only getting more heinous. Harry: Hustler is far out, man. Don't knock it until you've tried it. [Tommy shakes his head and begins making his way into the church. It's a bit bogus at first to be walking into a church, sitting around a group of people who are also sexual addicts. I don't think that gnarly Lord above would really enjoy all these sexual people hanging out in his house, dude. But...it's where they were told to come. The church is most dark as a few stagglers sit in the pews, praying for a better life. Tommy shakes his head finding it bogus that people are always asking God for stuff. It must be bogus sitting in heaven hearing people complain. It's like God is a totally heinous customer service representative who hears all the crap. Poor God] Harry: Am I allowed to have marijuana in here, man? TTT: I guess so, dude. But God has X-Ray vision and might see it. Harry: Not far out, man. TTT: Totally. He might hit you with a lightning bolt, compadre. Harry: Oh well. At least the mary jane will light and I'll get a quick high before I go to see Satan, man. [Tommy shrugs about to enter through the green door that will be their most excellent salvation. It's then that Tommy is taken from behind and pulled into the adjacent room. Harry doesn't notice as he stares at the statue of Jesus Christ really thinking how he looks a lot like Axle Rose from Guns N Roses. So, that's how he got all those chicks, man] Harry: Tommy? Where are you, man? [Harry shrugs and enters the room. It's a very dreary place this room. It's filled with people of all types. Balding business man, prostitute from the corner of Rodeo, the head cheerleader, the guy who bags your groceries, a librarian, and the owner of Starbucks coffee. All of these people have problems with sexual addiction. Harry takes a seat, very timidly. He doesn't want to be here, he shouldn't be here. This is not far out] [In the adjacent room, Tommy is being sexually attacked by a horny woman. Heather wants it. She needs it. She was supposed to be in the meeting, but she finds it so boring. She isn't addicted to sex, she just loves it. She loves having sex with tanned men with giant...] TTT: Chill out, babe. I'm here to try to get rid of this bogus addiction that I have. Heather: Your not addicted. TTT: I'm not? Heather: No. You just love it. Sex is attracted to you. You can't help it that your beautiful, great, and a sex machine. It just comes easily for you. Now get over here and screw me! [Tommy suddenly begins to see the light. Maybe he's not addicted after all. Maybe it's just that he's a magnet for sex and needs to turn down the magnetism. Hmmm, sounds right. So it's time he let this babe Heather in on why he's called 'Totally Tubular'] [In the tiny room just adjacent from where Tommy is adding another name to his list, Harry sits in the semi-circle with other fellow sexual addicts. They have introduced themselves, cried, and admitted that they were addicted to sex. They have broken the barriers that hold them back, except one dude. Yeah, it's Harry. He sits in the uncomfortable wooden chair with crosses carved on the back staring at the people around him with paranoid eyes. He suddenly feels that this REALLLY isn't far out, like at any moment they will bring out the Kool-Aid and tell him that it will save him from totally far out salvation. And where in the name of Twisted Sister is Tommy???] Meeting Leader: Now it will take very careful steps to fully recover from your addictions. You must find something in your life that you enjoy doing, that will become useful. You must become addicted to that. Cancelling out an addiction is such a beautiful process and with the power of the Lord behind you it's POSSIBLE! [The meeting leader looks like he's never had a girlfriend in his life. His fat face, giant hairy moles on his face, and the thickest glasses that have ever been discovered. The guy must be a step ahead of being blind] Harry: When you mean useful do you mean like reading Hustler, man? Meeting Leader: No, that is why your here...is it not? [Harry shrugs with confusion filling his eyes] Harry: Tommy said that if I came here he'd buy me ice cream, man. Meeting Leader: And just who is Tommy? Harry: He's my brother, man. His therapist told him to come here so that he didn't have sex anymore, man. I think that is not far out at all. Meeting Leader: That's funny because I did receive a call this morning that two young men were going to be joining us. I wonder where your brother went to? [Harry shrugs as he looks down to a bookbag that is beside him. It's as if Harry now has X-Ray vision because he can see that far out new issue of Hustler lying in the bag. Harry then looks at the owner of Starbucks coffee. His blank stare of realization has clouded his senses. Harry slowly reaches into the bookbag and has grasped the magazine like a true thief would do. His stoned smile is growing like a blooming flower as he begins to address the group members] Harry: I've gotta go to the bathroom, man. [Harry then jumps up with the magazine shoved under his shirt. He makes it to the old, scratched wooden door and looks back to the group with a large amount of pain on his face] Harry: I've gotta go bad, man. [The door then shuts behind him, to do his duty...whatever that may be] Meeting Leader: You all remember when you first came here. You were afraid, denying that you all were not addicted to sex. [He points to the high school cheerleader, still in her cheerleading outfit, which drives the owner of Starbucks wild...but it's lost it's charm. He is being cured] Meeting Leader: Melissa, you were in strong denial, telling us that you weren't addicted, just a 'magnet'. That the football players were so strongly attracted to you that sex just found you. That the pounds of semen extracted from your stomach were just out of magnetism. But now you've gone clean for six months. You've stayed strong, and now the new members can learn from what you've laid down for them. The Lord will remember what you have done, Melissa. [The cheerleader named Melissa looks quite proud of herself as a smile crosses her face. She looks extremely timid, shy even in the chair still. It's always been that way. Having these people know that she serviced the entire football team, volleyball team, baseball team, and even the chess club...THEY NEEDED IT! They really did!] Voice from other room: YESSSSSS!!!! [Everyone in the semi-circle looks confused, startled by the scream of ecstasy from the other room. They walk to the adjacent room and open the door. All of their faces are filled with shock, and the cheerleader has a look of being turned on. Tommy stands in the center of the room with his PCW T-shirt covering his genitals as Heather lies on the wooden floor with a blanket covering her naked body. Tommy smiles, feeling most embarrassed by this moment. Heather isn't embarrassed though, she loves it. In fact, they can all stay as the audience as her and Tommy go for a second round] Meeting Leader: THIS IS UNCALLED FOR!! You are on sacred ground!!! [Tommy shrugs] TTT: It's just an old wooden floor, dude. [The meeting leader looks like he's about to pass out. This naked man has insulted the house of God, insulted him, and most of all he's insulted this group meeting. DAMN HIM! But he still needs help and he can help this naked man overcome the true demons that pull him under. He'll teach him control] Meeting Leader: Tommy, you should get dressed and come with us to the other room. We can help you overcome this...together. [Tommy looks at the naked Heather, wrapped in the blanket. She looks most sexy and ready to go another round. Then Tommy looks to the fat, mole filled meeting leader with his band of sexual misfits behind him. What should Tommy choose? Another steamy sexual encounter with Heather? Or give up his sexual ways to listen to a fat guy preach about how the Lord can save him from his evil ways?] 5 MINUTES LATER... Meeting Leader: Tommy, you made the right decision. [Tommy now sits in a chair with a large smile on his face, knowing that this is going to be the right decision. It was quite bogus to see Heather try the throw him through the wall, but that cheerleader babe took care of her. That was most gnarly] TTT: Where did Harry go, dudes? [Just then the bathroom door opens and Harry has a satisfied smile on his face. He then takes his seat and slams down the Hustler magazine. The owner of Starbucks leaps up and looks at his magazine with rage] Owner of Starbucks: You stole my magazine!!! Harry: I gave it back, man. [The owner of Starbucks picks up the magazine and flips through it, most of the pages stick together as if they were glued] Owner of Starbucks: The pages are stuck together!!! [Harry shrugs] Harry: It was like that, man. [Tommy laughs to himself] TTT: Your totally bogus, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [The scene then fades to black]
