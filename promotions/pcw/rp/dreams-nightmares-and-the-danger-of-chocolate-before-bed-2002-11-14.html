Dreams/Nightmares and the danger of chocolate before bed

14 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-14-02 RP 2 Fusion 15th Dreams. They’re results of one’s subconscious thoughts. In some cases, dreams are a way to rest the mind when sleep alone won’t do. Nightmares are feed by fear. Different souls have different fears and when these fears become so great, they come to life in one’s sleep. Reminds me of a certain 80’s horror film. To the world, Teresa appears to be the tough chick with a huge attitude and a bigger temper. She separates business from pleasure and does it well. Some may wonder where’s her pleasure. Being busy with training and busting gut to climb the rating ladder and prove to the world a woman can and will make it, pleasure seems to be pushed aside. This explains why she hasn’t allowed herself to become serious with anyone, especially her fellow roster members. Who knows, maybe she will meet someone someday that will fill the gap in her life which she chooses to ignore. Can you actually picture Teresa as an old maid? I didn’t think so. Just the other night she was involved with a so-called date. It wasn’t real. It was an experiment she was told and with Nerezza of all people. She didn’t throw on her charm face and pretend to be the average clueless woman. She was her typical laid back that really didn’t care what others thought of her. If it was real, maybe she would’ve tried harder to impress the poor guy. At either rate, the date was a failure and bottle of champagne she nearly finished didn’t help matters any. You see Teresa has two weaknesses. We all know one being a former love and rising OSW star with the last name Taylor, and the other is alcohol. She knows her limit, which is more than the average woman her size, but sometimes she forgets. Where was I? Oh yes, dreams and fears. What does Teresa fear? It’s not snakes or rats or flying insects with stingers. It’s not the dark or scary movies. Her fear is being alone. Not the loneliness of being without a love companion. This is loneliness in general. No friends, no enemies, and no family. Why do you think she agreed to the so-called experimental date? She wanted company and Nerezza in his own way offered. In all honesty, she wouldn’t mind getting to know the vampire better, but she won’t let it be known. The date was ruined, he seemed so not into her, and besides what if he by accident, bites her? The sky was dark and the stars were bright. Teresa tossed and turned most of the night. With too much chocolate before bed, Teresa dreamed she was dead.What once were curiosity’s temptation, let to an unforgettable sensation. He was tall and had eyes of rubies. When he looked upon her, all he saw was her radiant beauty.The lust of the flesh was no match for the beast, for tonight she became the feast. She resisted with all her might, but his hypmotizing charm caused her to loose the fight.Softly upon her ear, he whispered words, which eased her fear. She closes her eyes when into her ears continue his whispering lies.His cold breath causes her body to shake. In her mind, her heart did ache. Unheard are silent screams and near is the end of this dream.His teeth pierced her skin and she felt life drain from within.
