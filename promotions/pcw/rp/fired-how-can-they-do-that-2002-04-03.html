Fired?! How can they do that?!

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/04/02 Fired?! How can they do that?! ..::The scene opens up on the rolling orange mountains in the backdrop of Cairo, Egypt. We look around the area that we currently find ourselves to be in to discover a long flowing river in front of us. The Nile. On either side of the vast, sparkling water-filled River we can see that long rows of green flourishing trees of tropical origin line the Nile’s banks. The warm sun beats down upon us as we study our surrounding…why are we here? Hopefully theses questions will soon be answered. Around ten metres away from the Nile’s banks rows upon rows of small houses, some built in the image of great buildings of times long ago and some build to look and house not the rich can be found. Outside the house’s doorways we find many people, the majority of which are from Egyptian heritage, sitting out enjoying the warm weather. Suddenly into the scene walks a tall man, well built and of dark skin. He wears a trailing black robe and walks along the Niles banks aided by a tall thin cane, which itself bears a golden snake atop of it. As the cameras pan in on the mans stern face we find him to be in fact our resident Egyptian God, and PCW TV champion, The Pharaoh! In his other hand we find Carmoon “The Cat” been held with ease in the mighty warriors Godly grip. And next to Pharaoh we find, as usual, his great Mentor and fellow Egyptian God, Horus! The God of the Sky wears a long white rope that features many ancient marking upon its material in golden embroidery. In his long, thin arm we find a similar cane to that of Pharaoh’s but Horus’ is thinner and completely black, shimmering in the Egyptian sun, it also bears no snake or any other strange golden symbol atop of it::.. The Pharaoh: They don’t realize what they’ve done, Horus! They are fools for doing such a foolish act! Horus: Yes, they have clearly not thought through their ideas properly… The Pharaoh: MORTAL FOOLS! That’s what they are, Mentor! Idiots for letting me go like that! How exactly is firing their Television champion going to result in being a good thing? ..::Pharaoh sighs::.. The Pharaoh: Duke Williams must be sobbing mournfully for even thinking about putting that foolish mortal, Doctor Rupert Healey in charge of things…the idiot went and fired nearly all the best prospect talents the PCW has! ..::Titu strokes the feline in his hands to which it purrs::.. The Pharaoh: What do you think, dear creature? ..::Titu looks down at the cat as if waiting for an answer. At length the Egyptian God smiles broodingly::.. The Pharaoh: Exactly! They are fools! Every last one of them! Horus: Who are you talking about, Titu? The Pharaoh: All of my foes! Akira Akiyama, Doctor Rupert Healey, even Duke Williams for putting that inferior minded fool in charge of PCW’s finance! ..::Pharaoh pauses again and lets out a deep sigh of pity::.. The Pharaoh: I truly feel for the fans; without me to dazzle them then PCW will seemingly lose many of its viewers! And what about Isiy? Without a PCW contract I won’t get a chance to battle Akiyama at Spring Fling for Isiy’s release… ..::The Jewel scowls::.. The Pharaoh: That imbecile, Rupert Healey, has he no thought for the real life troubles of Gods such as I? I shall not be treated like this! On Turmoil things are going to get sorted out! I promise you that, Horus! No Mortal financial advisor is going to stop me from getting back my wife! Horus: What are you going to do then? The Pharaoh: You’ll see! I promise you that! You’ll see… ..::Pharaoh looks out across the Nile and smiles grimly::.. The Pharaoh: Beautiful is it not? ..::Horus looks across the Nile in the direction Pharaoh is gazing::.. Horus: That it is, my pupil! The Pharaoh: Yes…it is a shame and a blessing that this river floods once each year… Horus: What are you getting at, Titu? The Pharaoh: Every time this amazing river floods it destroys homes, it sometimes kills cattle and if the odd person is unlucky enough to get caught in its waters then it kills them too; yet if the Nile did not flood what would Egypt be like? It wouldn’t have these trees and these plants, or these fields of crops growing upon its land! It takes something as destructive as a flood to produce so much good… ..::Pharaoh pauses and smiles as if remembering something::.. The Pharaoh: To do good I must do bad…to give the fans what they want I must become destructive… To defend MY TV title I must do whatever I can do to destroy my opponent… to defeat Akiyama at Spring Fling I must go out of my way to hurt the Neo Void God… ..::Pharaoh strokes Carmoon “the cat” again and once again the cat purrs in response to the action::.. The Pharaoh: Ah yes…it is clear to me know…coming here was a good idea, Horus…my eyes are now open to the light…I know now what I must do if I am to come out of Spring Fling as the better man… Horus: But what is you aren’t resigned to PCW? ..::Pharaoh gazes down at his Mentor sarcastically::.. The Pharaoh: Horus, by the obviously holy name of this damn cat in my hands, I say to you this, I am the PCW TV champion, PCW cannot strip me of my title, for I have done nothing wrong to deserve it, they cannot NOT resign me for I am too much of an asset to the roster, and they cannot shun me away for once again I have done nothing to deserve such a punishment…I promise you, Horus, I will be signed to PCW again by the end of Tuesday Turmoil tonight… ..::Horus looks at Pharaoh doubtfully::.. Horus: If you say so… ..::Horus suddenly catches the eye of Carmoon “the cat”::.. Horus: And what is with this cat, my pupil? Do you still believe it to be your loyal bodyguard Carmoon reincarnated? The Pharaoh: Yes, yes I do…I mean there is nothing to say that it isn’t Carmoon is there? ..::Horus sighs doubtfully::.. Horus: I suppose not…but…well, it’s just… The Pharaoh: Just what? Horus: Well I have my own reasons why I don’t think its Carmoon reanimated… The Pharaoh: Do you care to share those reasons? ..::Horus stammers and then suddenly he bluntly comes out with his opinion::.. Horus: Well it’s a cat for Imhotep sake! We found it in ‘the palace’ after it killed that rat and I just don’t believe that it is Carmoon…I mean come on haven’t you noticed something about it that kind of says it can’t be Carmoon? ..::Pharaoh gazes down at his mentor, locked in a puzzled expression::.. The Pharaoh: No…what are you talking about, Horus? Horus: Well, Carmoon was male, right? ..::Pharaoh chuckles::.. The Pharaoh: Unless there was something that he wasn’t telling me then yes…he was a male… Carmoon: And seemingly then if he were to be reincarnated then he’d come back as a male too? Agreed? The Pharaoh: Yes… Horus: So why in my name doesn’t that cat have any male genitals?! ..::Pharaoh springs back in confusion::.. The Pharaoh: W-what ever do you mean? Why this cat is a male if I ever did see one! ..::Horus stares up at his pupil sarcastically::.. Horus: Really? The Pharaoh: Yes! ..::Horus grabs the cat away from Pharaoh’s hands and raises it in front of him. At length Titu speaks::.. The Pharaoh: Well…it was a mistake anyone could have made! And I see little reason why this should count out my theory on this cat… Hours: This female cat… ..::Pharaoh scowls at Horus::.. The Pharaoh: (continuing) been Carmoon reincarnated! So what, he’s a female now…he obviously annoyed the Gods at one point or another! And anyway, Horus if I want to believe this cat to be my former bodyguard then that’s my business, not yours… Horus: Very well… The Pharaoh: And the point in coming down here to the Nile’s banks today was for me to think about Spring Fling and how on earth I’m going to win the rights to keep my Godhood, defend my TV title and rescue Isiy all in one match! And with the news of my ‘firing’ coming over from the USA I have more than one single problem on my hands! This cat, Horus, has nothing to do with you…if you wish to question it then feel free to do so…when I’m not around! ..::Pharaoh pauses::.. The Pharaoh: I’m sure you too would be so hot and bothered if it were you whose wife and Job had just been taken from you! But as I said earlier I promise you this, I will be resigned to PCW tonight…and after I’ve rescued Isiy from the clutches of that evil rat Akiyama I shall make my decision whether to sue PCW for my unlawful firing! OSW is only a phone call away but without Isiy I’m moving nowhere…Doctor Rupert Healey! Bah! He’ll be the end of PCW!! ..::The scene fades to black::..
