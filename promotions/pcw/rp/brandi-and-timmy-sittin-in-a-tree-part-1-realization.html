Title: Brandi and Timmy Sittin' In A Tree! Part 1: Realization. Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered 'Susie and Jonny sitting in a tree!' 'K-I-S-S-I-N-G!' Ah, the familiar chants and taunts of youth... It's a rite of passage for the popular, for those who, even at the elementary school age, KNOW they're going to end up getting a whole lot of ass during their days. One's first real girlfriend can he an exhilerating experience. Some meet them in school.....others at work.... Still others meet them at 1:00 AM in a Final Fantasy VII chatroom.....but we're not going there.. Fact is, kissing a girl's fun. Especially when you haven't even remotely THOUGHT about second base yet. And now, in PCW....a schoolboy's crush is manifesting itself. Yes, everyone's favorite poster boy for hysterectomies, Tim Walden, has himself a potential girlfriend. Brandi Wilson. Wait, hold on, rewind.... WALDEN?! GIRLFRIEND?! COULD IT BE?! The HIT's interested in finding out. And, if indeed Mr. Walden has found himself a chica, Trapezoid plans on treating it with the seriousness due the occassion. Absolutely zilch. ::Sitting in his hotel room, apparently having found nothing better to do outside, The HIT is watching the soap opera unfold between Brandi, Walden, and Nathan. After watching, the Trapezoid sits, spellbound. Slowly, tears come to his eyes, moistening his cheeks, as he reacts with the only emotion he can. Pure, utter hysterics.:: The HIT: BWAHAHHAAHAHAHHAHAAHAHAHHAAHAHA! Oh God, please stop! I can't TAKE any more of this damnit! Be merciful, and stop this veritable comedy of errors! This has GOT to be the funniest......::Trapezoid pauses, tears choking him up.....::, funniest damn thing I've ever seen in my life! I mean, WALDEN GOT PUNKED OUT BY THE INTERN! THE PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER GOT BITCHSLAPPED BY A GUY WITH THE OVERALL APPEARANCE OF A LIMP LO MEIN NOODLE! ::Flopping backwards onto the bed, Trapezoid reaches desperately for a can of refreshing Mountain Dew. He opens it, yet his hysterical laughing prevents him from lining up the can right, as he spills a bit of the drink on his shirt, shoulders, and chin before finally getting it into his mouth. He drinks several mouthfuls, before being struck by the thought of Walden with a girlfriend, and promptly spews the Mountain Dew out, misting the television screen:: The HIT: I mean, is it POSSIBLE?! Walden ranks just above Hank~! and the Ultimate Warlord on the overall I JOB TO ALL-O-METER! Brandi's actually experienced SUCCESS here, except for the stint where she was in a band.....that was a stable....that was a band.......GODDAMNIT! THE SHEER MECHANICS AND RESULTS OF THIS HAPPENING ARE GIVING ME A HEADACHE! I mean, it's Walden.....and it's Brandi! But still....somewhere in my mathematically warped and demented mind, it actually makes sense! That's it.....I've finally gone insane! But it makes sense damnit! WHY does it make sense? ::Trapezoid's tears of laughter turn to tears of confusion. The soft drink once again quenches his thirst, as the gears in his mind begin turning....trying to rationalize his statements.:: The HIT: I mean, Walden's a perennial loser. It's in his genetic makeup. Somewhere, on the eighth day, God decreed that one day, a man named Tim Walden would enter a sport called pro wrestling, and suck more than any that has ever sucked before! But Brandi, somehow, she seems to be ATTRACTED to losers! I mean, she hung out with Hank~!! She hung out with the Storms! And she hung out with the stable band's drummer.....who was named.....anyone want a guess? YES! THAT'S IT! DRUMMER! THE STUPIDITY OF THESE PEOPLE BOGGLES MY MIND AT TIMES! ::Trapezoid simply bursts out laughing again, any semblance of talking tough to Walden, Brandi, or any of his opponents completely gone. He buries his hands in his afro, trying desperately to stop. Finally, he manages to gain control of himself, shaking somewhat violently still.:: The HIT: That's it! This proves it! Opposites attract! There is no other possible explanation for a universe in which Walden and Brandi are obviously flirting with each other, unless the earth and the galaxy we live in have all been swallowed by a massive black hole, and rather than dying, we have been transported to Bizzaro-World. But, damnit, I'm going to have to stop laughing....and figure out how I can USE this! Sure, Walden kissing a girl would probably tank the PPV ratings, but that doesn't help ME any! No, I have to figure out how to EXPLOIT this new development to my advantage! If Tim Walden really IS pursuing Brandi, how do I take advantage of this? ::Slowly, a cruel smile plays itself over The HIT's lips. He shudders with the evilness of his thought, throwing it around in the chambers of his mind before nodding in agreement with himself.:: The HIT: Yes......that is EXACTLY what I'm going to do! At Alliances, Tim, I am going to tear you apart....but, until then, I'm going to do the one thing you'd never expect. I'm going to help you, Timothy. After all, Brandi can't expect you to know what to do romantically.....as you've had less sexual experience than I have. And, when you get less sex than a guy who is legally named 'The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid', you DEFINITELY need some help. Never fear Timmy! Dr. Trapezoid is here to the save the day! I'll help you have fun with your girlfriend, chica, or whatever other terms ghetto people call their women......and then, at the Pay-Per-View.....I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS!! :Trapezoid skips.....YES SKIPS out the door, prancing like a little schoolgirl as we fade to chalkboards.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
