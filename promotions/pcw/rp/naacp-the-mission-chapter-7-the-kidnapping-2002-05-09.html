NAACP- The Mission [Chapter 7. The Kidnapping.]

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/02 Damned court cases. They're hard as hell to try. If you've seen any lawyer, when they have a court case with Millions and millions of dollars on the line, they can't afford to make a single mistake. The one mistake, perhaps discrediting your own witness, could not only ruin your entire case, but it could ruin your career as a big time lawyer. If they know that you fail under pressure, the big cases won't just come rolling in. Most all lawyers are in the business for the money...And trying cases with Millions on the line and winning will not only help you with the money, it'll make other people that are suing a company for big time money come to you to be their lawyer. For instance, OJ Simpson's lawyer was Jonnie Cochran... And even though everybody and they momma know that the playa was guilty, he got off. Right? So next time, when Sean 'Puffy' Combs got in trouble with the police, he called on Jonnie Cochran. He won the case and he didn't lose anything... Well I guess he did lose Jennifer Lopez's bootylicious ass. But you're missing the point; point is that the popularity of the case made Jonnie Cochran one of the most known lawyers in the business. He's a rich bastard now who no longer needs to work, he had his own show on BET, he was doing radio spots for cash left and right, everything was golden for him. Jake Jackson, Jordan X Shabazz's friend and lawyer wants to be exactly like Jonnie Cochran. Even if it means playing dirtier than he's played before... The scene opens up in a downtown Montreal eatery. Montreal, Quebec Canada, one of the most visited places in Canada. It's a tourist attraction for all of its fine Wines and fine sights to see. But, it's one of the most despised places in Canada for it's separatist attitude. Jordan sits down eating [What else] greasy chicken, watermelon, a cold Miller Lite, and a pack of Newport’s on the table. Their are also fries. Jordan is just about finished with his food when he spots somebody that he thinks looks familiar. It's a short Asian man who Jordan has seemed to see everywhere he's gone since getting to Montreal... Hell, Jordan thinks that he even saw the man on the same flight from Dallas, to Montreal. The man walks to the counter while looking around the restaurant for somebody, apparently. He quickly orders a Jackweiser [Budweiser] and he sits down at the table across from Jordan. The Asian man flashes Jordan a quick look and he says something into his cellular phone, which he had opened and ready. Jordan gets up and begins to walk out of the restaurant. His hotel isn't more than twenty minutes away from the restaurant so he decided to walk. Jordan walks slowly on his way back to his hotel admiring all the things that make Montreal great. Annoying French people that give you dirty looks and whisper things about your clothing, and ugly ass hobos who ask you for bills instead of spare change. We also see a group of Montreal Canadian fans that are cheering like crazy ass maniacs over some hockey game they won over the Carolina [Big F^cking deal eh?] Hurricanes. Jordan is almost to his hotel when... THAT SNEAKY ASS ASIAN GUY STANDS IN FRONT OF HIM! Jordan looks at the man, and he freezes, it was getting dark, and this man seemed to be following him. The weather was cold, and so was Jordan, attired only in baggy khaki shorts, some white Old School Nikes, a Toronto Raptors baseball cap, and a Phat Farm shirt. The Asian man smiles at Jordan, and then.... SHABAMM~! Lights out playa... ************************ Jake Jackson was stressed. And that wasn't unusual. Being a lawyer he was used to the regular grind of working 18 hours a day and taking no rest except for sleep. He wasn't tired, but reviewing all these short three page biographies on the jurors was hard. He couldn't believe that it was so intense for a case like this, for he had not tried something the magnitude of this case for at least three years. As he reviewed and revised he thought of Jordan. Jordan told Jake that he would call him when he got back to the hotel. They had to go through their mock courtroom pretend Jordan was on trial thing. Jake thought about how the people from the other side played dirty, he'd been in the business for quite a while and threats of physical violence was never below his counterpart, a single man who would defend all three defendants, his name was Jeff Miller. He had a history of excellence. He was the white Jonnie Cochran. He never allowed his opponent to get the upper hand in anything. He argued every point, and made sure that the prosecution was always on it's toes. Bah~! Why not call Jordan on his cell just to make sure he's okay, Jake thought to himself. He picked up the phone and pressed 1 on his speed dial, the number of Jordan's cell phone.... RING! RING! RING! The phone rings...And rings.... And rings... Nobody answers. Bad, bad news. Jake shakes his head and runs his hand through his hair. This is going to be a long ass night... ************************************ Bright, red lights shined down on his face. He was temporarily blinded. His head throbbed with pain. He tried to sit up, but he realized he was strapped into a chair. His arms bound together behind his back. He was hooked up to what appeared to be a Lie Detector Machine, and he had this little electrical wires all over his body. His shirt was off, but thankfully, his pants were on. Don't want any nasty shiznit right? The room was rather cold, and small. It had green walls with peeling paint, and a roof with even more peeling paint. There were two men in the room. One was that sneaky little Asian guy, and the other, was a much larger white guy with blond hair and blue eyes. He stood about 6'6, and weighed at least 330 pounds. He wore a black suit, just like the short, sneaky Asian. They stared at Jordan, then they stared at each other, and they nodded. The torture was about to begin. ************************** Jake Jackson rested from his work for a minute. He looked out the window into the hot Texas night. Jordan was supposed to fly straight back to Dallas after the Pay Per View where Jake Jackson would put him on the stand... A fake stands of course. Preparation for the stand would be key for Jordan; he was most definitely going to be called to the stand by Jeff Miller. Miller knew his stuff like that. As the fog set in, Jake decided to call it a night. He would sleep for a few hours then he'd wake up, and call Jordan. By then Jordan would be in bed, and he would probably HAVE to return his phone call. ****************************************** The pain was unbearable now for Jordan. These two men had been shocking him through the electrodes for about a minute and a half now. His chest burned, and so did his arms. He checked his chest and could find no immediate signs of permanent scaring, the last thing he wanted to end up looking like was Clyde. 'Alright man, I'll answer the father F^cking questions,' Jordan screamed in pain to the two men clad in dark suits. 'Just unhook me to these damned cords.' 'I'm afraid we can't do that,' the small, sneaky Asian guy replied in all seriousness. 'We've got to give you a quick lie detector test so Jeff knows what to question you on when you get on the stand.' MOTHERF^CKER! Jordan had a feeling that Jeff was behind this. But he just didn't put two and two together. His head still throbbed from being hit in the back of the head with a blunt object, then stuffed in the trunk of a two door Honda Civic and being brought to this remote, abandoned room in rural Montreal. Now all he had to do was answer these questions and he would be free right? As the small Asian begins to set up the lie detector machine, Jordan thought about PCW Supremacy. Mark Makonie and him had a feud that was much more physical and violent than this, he had to try and sustain the pain. He had a huge match with Makonie lined up for Sunday, it was his first ever PCW Pay Per View debut, and his only. If he didn't win this match, then it'd be hell for every single other PCW wrestler... And if he did win it, it'd be hell for the Rising Star Champion. So either way, somebody would be in some serious trouble. 'Just relax, and answer the questions truthfully.' The large white man said to Jordan. RELAX? Is this cracka crazy? Jordan thought to himself. But in all actuality, it would be hard relaxing while being strapped to a chair and being forced to answer a series of questions. What did they want to know anyways? 'First question, is the reason you filed this lawsuit because you hate white men and you just want to cause a ruckus in corporate white America?' The Asian man asked. 'No. The reason I filed this lawsuit is because I honestly feel that these three, dirty companies, are racist pigs who deserve to be shot.' Jordan shot back with a hint of fear in his voice. No reaction. The Asian and the white guy just looked at the monitor and nodded. 'Next question, if given the opportunity to lie and cheat your way to victory in this court case, would you take it?' 'Of course.' Again, no reaction. Just the checking of the monitor. 'Final question, what are you thinking of right now?' 'I'm thinking of PCW Supremacy. I'm wondering if I have what it takes to win one for the entire black race, I'm wondering if I have what it takes to win one for my people, I'm wondering if I have the stamina to last in a match even though you guys are doing this s^it to me. I'm wonder...' 'Okay, okay, you made your freakin' point,' the Asian said with anger in his voice. 'Now, if you tell anybody about this, you can bet your ass that some of your friends and family members will have... shall we say 'Accidents.'' The small Asian said. 'Yeah,' Jordan said wincing in minor pain from the shocks that he absorbed. They unstrapped Jordan from the chair and they took all the cords and wires off of him. He was free.... For now.
