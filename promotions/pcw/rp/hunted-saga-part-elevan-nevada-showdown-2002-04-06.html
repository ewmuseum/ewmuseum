Hunted Saga Part Elevan: Nevada Showdown...

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/4/02 The camera opens up in a service station somewhere in the desolate Nevada Dessert. The camera cuts to inside the diner, a couple of minutes after we last left Crichton and Aeryn. Crichton simply nodded in agreement. Crichton picked his burger up and took a bite, as the window, a few booths down, smashed and something hurtled through hitting the back wall. Smoke began emanating from the object, as the camera fades to black. The smoke around them has cleared, and everywhere is silently quiet. The camera pans across to see the owner of the establishment, is draped heftily slumped over the counter. The camera pans further across to see one of the waitresses, seemingly unconscious on the floor, with broken plates and broken glass surrounding her. The camera pans across further to see Aeryn and Crichton huddled up in their booth, both for some unfathomable reason still conscious, when others around them are not. The camera then cuts to outside the diner as the four fully dressed and armed soldiers are standing prepared ready to enter the diner. Fleming is stood a little bit behind them looking intently through his binoculars at the contents of the diner. Fleming shakes his head a little and then looks at the soldier directly in front of him. “It all looks clear from this angle, go in and retrieve the target!” Fleming commands The soldier in front simply nods at him then, with the rest of the soldiers, slowly and cautiously proceed to the diner doors. The camera cuts to back inside the diner, as Crichton, cautiously raises his head a little and ventures a quick peak out of the window. That’s when it hit him like a ton of bricks. The sight of Fleming commanding troops towards his location. All of his questions of the events that had happened mere moments earlier had fit into place. The concussion gas bomb that screamed through the diner window, perfect tactics for army soldiers when securing a building. Of course since he had been in the army he had realised what the projectile was and had managed to cover his mouth and noise with the handkerchief, he had also told Aeryn to do the same. It had appeared to work, because the soldiers were now closing in on their position and they wouldn’t be entering the building yet unless the gas had dissipated. The soldiers enter the diner, covering each other by pausing while the other one gains ground. As soon as they enter the building they split up into two groups, one group begins to check the opposite direction and the final two head Crichton’s way. Crichton briefly risks a look over the top of his cubicle seat, and notices them heading his way. “Aeryn this is the plan. Two are coming this way, pretend to be unconscious, then we attack.” Crichton whispered. Aeryn simply nods in agreement, and drapes herself over the table, pretending to be unconscious. Aeryn felt that familiar sickly feeling of guaranteed danger that was about to be placed upon them. Aeryn realised however, that if they didn’t try to stay free of the armies mighty grip, she would most certainly be killed for knowing too much and Crichton would be experimented upon. They both knew the risks when they set out to follow their dreams within the ranks of the PCW, and now Aeryn had to admit that this was a bad idea. She knew Crichton had to go through with this, but she didn’t HAVE to come with him on this little adventure, but then she realised that she did in fact have to go through with this, because she undoubtedly loved him and she felt that it was simply meant to be. . Crichton feels the blood rush to his head, as the footsteps of the soldier’s get nearer and nearer. Crichton remembered thinking that they took more like an hour than a couple of minutes to reach him. The soldiers draw closer, up to the cubicle in fact. One of them leans forward and checks Crichton’s pulse and within a blink of an eye, Crichton reacts, by grabbing the soldier’s rifle. Crichton took the soldier by surprise and as a result the rifle came out of the soldiers grasp fairly easily. Crichton then grabs the soldier across the neck, forcing him in the firing line of the second soldier. ‘Not that they were going to shoot him anyhow’ Crichton silently mused. They couldn’t he was too ‘valuable’ Crichton suspected that was the reason for the concussion grenade at the start of their onslaught. Crichton realised that the soldiers not being able to kill him was the only upper hand he had in such a situation. Without another thought Crichton simply pushes the soldier currently blocking the firing line forward, then cracking him around the back of the head with the butt of the rifle. The soldier instantly goes south. The other soldier simply looks at him in shock. “Why the hell did you break your cover dumbass? Now I got a clear shot!” The soldier simply states. A cocky smile appears upon Crichton’s face. “You can’t shoot me! Fleming doesn’t want me dead, I have to be alive to be valuable.” Crichton declares confidently. The soldier seems to take a step back and reassess the situation at this point, the expression on his face barely changing. “Yeah, but if I shot you in legs you wouldn’t die and you would be in extreme pain.” The soldier simply replies in a monotonous voice. Crichton hadn’t thought of that and now he was at the soldier’s total mercy. Unless, he could bluff his way out of the situation. Crichton decided that it was at least worth a shot. “You don’t know that! This experiment is very delicate one on a genetic level involving my blood, and if any of it is spilt, that fucks up the entire experiment. Fleming will be on your ass and as for the guy employing him, he will not hesitate in having you killed for it if need be. Crichton explains. The soldier seems to visibly think about the situation for awhile. “There is another reason too.” Crichton begins “My girlfriend is behind you!” With that Aeryn pulls her foot back and delivers a swift kick to the soldier’s gonads. He keels over a little and Crichton finishes off the proceedings with punch across the soldier’s jaw. After the man goes down, he ventures a look in the direction of the other soldiers, more specifically where they went. The camera follows Crichton’s gaze as we see an almost empty diner, well apart from all of the unconscious bodies lining the counter, the floor and various tables. Crichton then grabs one of the unconscious soldiers and begins to pile him in a cubicle. “Here’s the plan of action.” Crichton began whispering. “We go out of the back door and edge around the side of the building taking Fleming off guard. We kick his ass and get in our car, then leave this god-forsaken place forever.” Aeryn nodded. “Agreed!” She stated quietly. After both soldiers bodies were bundled, sloppily into the cubicle, Aeryn and Crichton cautiously make there way to the opening in the counter, but before they make it, the other two soldiers come out from around the corner of the far end of the diner counter. Without thinking bout it, both soldiers began to fire. Crichton reacts quickly tackling Aeryn to the ground behind the diner counter and to the floor as the camera cuts to the outside of the diner. Fleming is looking through his binoculars, to see where the familiar sound of gunfire came from. He sees the two soldiers firing and then visibly fumes at the ears. “Those two imbeciles!” Fleming curses. Fleming then extracts his army issue walky talky from the top pouch of his combat suit and presses the button on the side. “Do not fire upon the target! I Repeat do not fire upon the target!” Fleming exclaims. “He’s killed two of my men sir, and I am going to kill them both!” One of the soldier’s claimed. “Aw…Hell!” Fleming curses once more as he picks up his rifle from the landed helicopter and makes his way toward the diner. The camera cuts to back inside the diner as Crichton warily gets to his hands and knees and clicks the safety off his rifle. One thing was for sure, he sure as hell was gonna go down fightin’ Crichton thought as he briefly pops up from behind the counter to unleash a few shots, forcing the soldiers to dive into two separate cubicles for cover. Crichton then grabs Aeryn by the hand and dragging her into the kitchen with him. The camera follows them as the zigzag through the surprisingly clean metal kitchen to the back door. The soldiers get up from their cover and quickly follow them. Crichton kicks the back door open and pushes Aeryn out ahead of him. Crichton notices all of the gas tanks directly to the left of the door, but it doesn’t register in his head. Crichton gets to about 100 yards away from the diner before the soldiers appear at the doorway and begin to fire. Crichton ducks and rolls, instinctively firing a shot at the soldiers. However, the shot passes the soldiers and impacts upon the gas canisters. The reaction is instantaneous, the gas canisters explode, taking the guards and half of the diner with it. Crichton has a shocked expression upon his face. “Aw…that ain’t good.” Crichton remarks. Aeryn simply looks at him blankly, no realising the full implications of the explosion. “Why? It took out those two soldiers didn’t it?” Aeryn questions. Crichton simply looks at her for a while. “Yeah, but what about the other people inside? The owner and the waitresses?” Crichton points out. The realisation then visibly appears upon Aeryn’s face. “Aw, Hell your right!” Aeryn states. The camera cuts to the front side of the Diner as Crichton and Aeryn look at the damage caused by the gas explosion. The whole front side of the diner has caved in and whoever was on the inside didn’t look like they were getting out in one piece. Crichton and Aeryn had first been wary of coming round the front, but they had found the army helicopter deserted. Meaning that Fleming had gone inside the diner, and Crichton suspected he went in after he heard gun shots. “We can’t do anything for anyone now! Nobody could have survived that!” Crichton states sombrely. Aeryn nods in agreement. “Yeah. Besides we’ve got a Pay Per View to get to!” Aeryn replies. Crichton simply looks at her and then back at the destroyed building. “How come every time I get into trouble, stuff blows up?” Crichton rhetorically questions as the camera fades to black.
