Parties Only Lead To Trouble (part 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Between 5/7/02-5/9/02 The scene opens up in a living room. It has a nice dark red tone that is easy on the eyes. The furnishings are pretty exquisite as well. The beige carpeting supports an entertainment center, which is against the wall in the middle of two entranceways. A big light brown leather sofa with matching love seats takes up space about eight feet from the entertainment center. There are also shelves all over holding pictures, as well as knick-knacks. A coffee table and rack of CDs and tapes finish up the room. Our attention suddenly turns to behind the couch as a pair of black shoes with red shoelaces can be seen poking out from behind. A faint yawning can be heard, so the camera quickly pans to behind the couch giving us a better look of the person. The person sprawled out on the floor is shirtless, in a pair of Hawaiian shorts bearing many different colors. Wearing no shirt giving us the chance to see a big tattoo across his shoulder blades reading 'Wild' in red lettering with a black outline. The man's brown hair is tousled and in his right hand he grips the neck of an empty Budweiser bottle firmly. The figure starts to slowly move. He lets go of the beer bottle and pushes his upper body up from the ground. He rolls over a bit and is now in a sitting position with legs crossed in a horizontal manner. He rubs on both sides of his head probably trying to battle a headache. Another yawn comes and he arches back with his mouth in the widest oval position possible. After he finishes stretching he looks around with an arched brow looking extremely confused. Kramer stands up and scratches his head obviously trying vigorously to remember something. Kramer: That must've been one wild party last night. ((He takes a pause while catching his balance, as he is still slightly tired and wobbly.)) I must've been really plastered if I can't even remember where I am. Wait this can't be the same place. I remember a fire of some sort, and it got on a rug and that charred a good portion of it. Maybe we were in another room? He slowly walks to the entranceway of the room and peaks from left to right down a hallway, yet he sees and hears no one. He takes a few more baby steps and suddenly his eyes bulge and his lips press tightly together as he cups his mouth with his right hand. Trying to keep from spewing, he rushes up and down the hallway feverishly looking for the bathroom. Reaching the end of the hallway he turns the corner and luckily spots his destination. He sprints to the door and races to the toilet, and lifts the lid. On his knees, with his head draped over the sides of the toilet, he lets it all out. Luckily the angle quickly changes to behind him, but we still can hear the gagging sounds. Kramer lifts his head up after one pure minute of letting everything out. His throat is sore and he tries to fight the nasty taste that is left in his mouth. Voice: Need a washcloth? Kramer jumps, startled by the voice. He turns around from the toilet to face the person. It's a man, with thick, wavy brown hair, and mustache. He stands leaning against the door of the bathroom in a red button down silk shirt, and black dress pants with black shoes. The man walks into the room and goes straight towards a closet, opens it up, and pulls out a white washcloth. The whole time the man is moving around, Kramer is watching him like a hawk trying to remember if he knows this guy or not. He tosses the washcloth towards Kramer, who catches it in midair and quickly rubs at his face to get any unnecessary residue off. Finishing, Kramer sets the washcloth aside as he stands to his feet. Kramer: I must have been trashed because I can't exactly remember how I got here, and quite frankly I don't know who you are. Man: Oh I'm sorry. My name is Ralph Zanchez. As far as how you got here, you had asked for a ride home because you had forgotten where your car was so I obliged to drive you. By the time we got in my car however you were already passed out so I had to take you back here. Kramer: Thank you for that. Well I guess it was a good thing that I didn't find my car, because who knows what would have happened. But there is still something that is bothering me. Ralph: And that would be? Kramer: Well when I woke up I had no shirt on, and I was wearing shorts. Ralph continues to look on towards Kramer slightly nibbling on his lower lip. Kramer: Now I know that I wasn't wearing shorts yesterday. Any idea on what happened? Ralph: Well as I was trying to carry you into the house, along the way you bumped a table in the hallway and the vase that was on top of it came down and the water inside got all over you. I felt bad that you would have to sleep in wet clothes so I took off your shirt, and slipped you into some shorts. None of my shirts would fit your broad shoulders so that's why I didn't put a shirt on you. Kramer: Ummm....thank you. Kramer knows why he did it but he still feels very uncomfortable about a male seeing him naked. The awkwardness doesn't leave so Kramer is ready to leave as soon as possible. Kramer: Thank you for all you have done. I really appreciate it. I'm pretty sure I have already caused you enough trouble so I guess I will be going now. Ralph steps out of the doorway and into the hall as Kramer walks through. Kramer takes a few steps before Ralph calls out to him. Ralph: Don't you want your clothes? Kramer: Oh yeah almost forgot. Ralph: Follow me. Ralph steps in front of Kramer, and like he was told, Kramer follows behind. A few minutes pass as the scene changes into a basement. Kramer stands nonchalantly at the bottom of the steps as Ralph walks over to the dryer where a laundry basket is laying. He pulls out a pair of dark blue baggy pants, and a dark gray shirt reading 'The Voices In My Head Tell Me To Burn Things'. Ralph walks back over towards Kramer. Ralph: I took the liberty of drying and ironing your clothes for you. Kramer: Thank you but you really didn't have to. Ralph: It was my pleasure. Kramer: Like I said, thank you for your hospitality and such is there anyway I can repay you? Ralph gets an excited look on his face. Ralph: How so? Kramer: Well since I am a PCW wrestler, maybe I can get you a ticket to the event of your choice? Or maybe I can give you some money or something. Or you can name the price. Ralph: Really? Hmmm.....There is only one thing I really want. Ralph steps closer and closer to Kramer, causing him to back up against the wall. Before Ralph can reply Kramer's mind starts racing. He looks at Ralph's silk red shirt thinking that a straight man would never wear that. Then he thinks about how happy Ralph seemed when he said he had slipped Kramer into some new clothes. There is only one thing this Ralph fellow could possibly want, and that's KRAMER!! Kramer: Listen man I'm not gay so....... Ralph: Who's gay? Kramer: You are! Ralph: I'm not gay!! Kramer: Oh really, than what did you want as a repayment huh? Before Ralph can respond the scene slowly fades to black as this first installment has come to a close. What is it that Ralph wants? Where is Kramer's car? All of these questions will be answered in due time, so stay tuned!!
