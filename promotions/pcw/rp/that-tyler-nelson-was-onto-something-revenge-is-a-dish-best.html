'That Tyler Nelson was onto something...': Revenge is a dish best served with caviar and champagne.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now Our trips to the land of controversy have been far and few between this month, after Corey made a promising return, the only time he has showed on PCW television is to do the bidding of New Breed, and more specifically one Jason Snow. Yes, Corey has made few appearances this month, but the few things he has done have been unforgettably controversial. Making a spirited return and dissing New Breed big time only to rejoin them later that night, joining Stewart Hix and Stryfe as the three most undeserving PCW World Champs of all time in a match that will surely be known as the most controversial of the year..... even if it wasn't quite the 'epic' Chris, Corey and Snow often referred to it as. Corey has battled Big Alan Steele on three occasions, not quite successful on any of them.... but he does not really care about that. For what is Big Alan Steele to Corey other than Snow's enemy? Corey doesn't really like Big Alan Steele, after all, the guy is just one big Greek homo, but he has never really done anything to deserve the wrath of Corey. New Breed have a deal, for although at the beginning they were four (and later three) young guys looking to prove to PCW that there was a new era of PCW about to begin, they achieved that goal a long time ago. Since then, all three men have developed, developed in different directions. Each man has tasted success, each man has been a PCW World Champion, and each has become their own man. Of course, that's saying thing from a business point of view. Behind the scenes New Breed are still as chummy as ever, hell, they even manage to muck around during the shows. The three cocky, young superstars would still rather hang out with each other than anyone else in PCW, they are still superior to the rest of the roster, but with egos that huge it's hard to stay friends with anyone.... particularly when you are a billionaire. So when it gets down to business, New Breed have a deal, an agreement between friends who all secretly believe they are superior to each other. The deal is that each man helps the other man achieve their goals and fight their battles... and do it in a very New Breed fashion. We know that Corey and Chase have been doing things for Snow and the New Breed cause, but what exactly has Snow been doing for Chase and Corey? And more importantly, what has Corey been doing for Corey? ::The scene opens up in a hotel, only this is not just any hotel. New Breed fans and controversy junkies we remember this large and distinguished hotel as the European-styled URO hotel. This was where the original New Breed was born for Corey, there are so many memories. Like the time Corey told Badge that Wild Deuces could never be family like the New Breed because even Badge's REAL family pretended not to know him. Or the time Chris burst in the room carrying a sledgehammer over his head? And who could ever forget, the first and only ever.... IRON MAN ORGASM MATCH! Those were the days when Corey was dirt poor, but New Breed were the hottest thing in professional wrestling. Those were the days before Corey realized that what the people said they wanted to see and what the people REALLY wanted to see were two entirely different things. Of course, the times in this hotel were not always happy, for one cannot live on fame forever. The contract made between Tyler Nelson and a then naive Corey for a ridiculous sum of money has haunted Corey for the majority of his PCW career, being the second least paid wrestler in PCW.... later becoming the very least paid after Jane Reagan signed Hank to a multimillion deal. In fact, the contract continued to make his life hell until just a few weeks ago when he inherited 1.3 billion dollars from his Father. But before then, Corey was thrown out of the hotel for being unable to pay the rent, much to the delight of a stuck up prick by the name of Frange (pronounced 'Franj') who happened to be manager at the time. But here we are just a few months later and so much has changed, Corey is a much bigger person than he already was, something even he thought impossible at the time. A former PCW champion, one of the most hated (and therefore, according to Corey, loved) wrestlers in all of professional wrestling, a member of the single most successful stable of all time. Corey walks onto camera, looking just as cocky as ever but perhaps a little more reassured than ever before. He is of course dressed in his usual attire, a bear fur coat and tight leather pants, no shirt as to both draw attention and show of his masculine physique. He approaches the front desk, walking casually but with such style and grace that it seems he is almost walking in slow motion. He spots a ratty looking man in a tuxedo with gelled hair that parts down the middle and a French moustache, at just about the same time the man spots him. Written on his name tag is on word 'Frange'. Both men instantly recognize each other, for they are wearing the exact same clothes are they did all those months ago (that's the thing about e-wrestling isn't it, no one ever changes their damn clothes).:: Frange (French accent): You! ::Both men share a heated glance which lasts until the moment Corey breaks into a smile. A smile which we know by now is used by Corey to both annoy and disguise his feelings, allowing him to turn from a rambling idiot to a pissed off psychotic.:: Corey: Frangey! How's it going old son? Frange: Look monsieur, I thought I told you never to come back again! Corey: Hey look man, I'm sorry about that whole thing that happened between us a few months back. But if it's alright with you I'd like to keep the past in the past. So, whaddya say... (Corey offers a hand of friendship).... buds? ::We can only just pick up the meaning of what Corey said through the mix clichés and irrelevant catchphrases that Corey speaks in, and Frange obviously picked it up too. His eyes shift down at Corey's hand, and then back up to Corey's annoying smile. Of course, anyone that knew anything about Corey would never accept this kind of handshake, but Frange hasn't seen Corey for a long time. The last time Frange saw Corey he was nothing but the 4th member of New Breed, a stable with no individuality who never backed up what they preached. Constantly claiming to be bad to the bone, claiming to take no shit, but always seeking the fans' approval before doing so. Corey doesn't need the fans', or Snow's for that matter, approval to do anything anymore, he is his own man and is stronger because of it. So as Frange's eyes travel down to Corey's hand once again, perhaps Frange does not understand that he is the lesser of the two evils in this situation. For these two men to have a fair handshake would be for Corey to step down from his evil perch, not Frange. But after making some sort of fuss like Corey gives a damn, Frange grudgingly accepts the handshake. Because despite being portrayed like a dick the first time he was seen in one of Corey's promos, the man is not without class and honor. And the exact second that we realize this, the exact second Frange gets comfortable with this new situation and let's down his guard, that is the exact second that Corey jerks his hand towards himself, pulling Frange with it. And precisely a second after that sudden snap of action, Frange's body flying towards Corey's body connects with Corey's fist flying away from Corey's body. We hear a delightful cracking sound as Frange the Frenchman falls to the ground. Once stationary again, Frange looks up to Corey, rubbing his chin, and shoots him a look that is half surprised and half pissed off. Corey leans over and stares down at Frange, no dumb grin on his face this time, bloodshot eyes and a psychotic mixture between a smile and frown.:: Corey: Well, 'bud', guess what? YOU'RE FUCKING FIRED! ::More left field comments do not come very often. However less than confused, Frange begins to laugh.:: Frange: Fire me? Ah, monsieur, I may not be up to date with American customs, but I'm pretty sure that a customer cannot fire the manager, no? ::Corey mocks Frange's distinctively French laugh before returning back to his psychotic smile/frown.:: Corey: Well laugh at this ya toad eating bastard! SECURITY! ::Suddenly two security guards come onto screen and start dragging Frange towards a nearby door.:: Frange: What are you doing!? Stop! Put me down! ::The security guards do stop and they put Frange back down on his knees.:: Corey: You see CCW Enterprises made a new acquisition earlier this afternoon.... ::Corey slaps Frange in the face twice, once with the forehand once with the backhand. Corey then reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out a piece of paper which he shoves into Frange's face.:: Corey: That's right, read it and weep. I OWN this stinkin' hotel!!! ::Corey instructs the security guards to pick up Frange once again and they drag him to the exit door which they then open. The guards place Frange on all fours so that the upper half of his body is outside whilst the lower half of his body is inside and facing Corey. Corey licks his lips and takes a few steps back before running up and kicking Frange right in his little French ass. Frange goes flying outside and onto the cold hard pavement. Corey sticks his head through the door.:: Corey: And stay out! ::And with that Corey slams the door shut on Frange, never to be seen again in a CCW promo if I can help it. He turns around to face security, dusting his hands.:: Corey: Oh, thanks security.... ::Corey begins to walk off but remembers something at the last minute and turns back to face security.:: Corey: Oh... and you're fucking fired too..... Thank you. ::The two security guards are stunned, but that quickly turns into anger and they begin to advance on Corey. Corey backs up and holds his hands up in defense, but smiling the whole time.:: Corey: Whoah whoah! I'd hate to be getting physical with one of the most powerful men in all of New York. I could sue you out of house and home you know. Of course, if you want you can keep advancing and I'll BEAT you out of house and home, and out of your wits I might add. Don't forget that I'm a former PCW World Champion and rich enough to bribe any cop or judge in this State. ::Corey looks up, and we finally realize why he is so reassured of himself. Although he has been playing second fiddle to Snow over the last few weeks, and though his clothes are old and dirty, and although he looks more like a punk than a business man, we cannot forget that Corey Williams is a BILLIONAIRE. The two security guards slowly back away from Corey and exit through the same door that they recently helped through Frange out of. Corey just simply shrugs and turns, he has business to attend to at HIS hotel. Corey begins to walk to the front desk once again, but much to his annoyance is approached by a clerk.:: Brown-nosing Clerk: Umm, excuse me Mr. Williams sir. ::Corey's eyes shift to the clerk for one second, but he just continues walking on. The clerk laughs nervously and begins to walk with Corey.:: Brown-nosing Clerk: Uhh....Can I get you anything? Corey: No you can't, and you know why? Brown-nosing Clerk: Umm..... why? Corey: COZ YOU'RE FUCKING FIRED! ::That finally stops the clerk in his tracks, Corey keeps walking and finally reaches the front desk.:: Corey: In fact..... ::Corey jumps up onto the desk.:: Corey: Excuse me! Everyone! If I may have you're attention please! ::Everyone in the hotel turns to Corey.:: Corey: Thanks. Look, I'm the new owner of this hotel Corey Williams. And before you all get back to work, I have just one thing to say to you..... YOU'RE ALL FUCKING FIRED! ::Corey smiles to himself, finally satisfied. He climbs off of the front desk and begins to swagger around the lobby.:: Corey: Go on... get out before I call the cops and sue you for trespassing..... ::A nearby hotel employee manages to find the courage to speak.:: Hotel Employee: But sir, this is a hote-- Corey: GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY HOTEL! ::And with that everyone in the hotel gets up and leaves, just like that. A stampede of noise and then.... silence. Corey sighs to himself.:: Corey: ...You know what? That Tyler Nelson was onto something........... ::Corey holds his arms out wide and looks up to the heavens, simultaneously the camera cuts to an overhead shot of Corey.:: Corey: POWER TRIPS FUCKING RULE! ::The camera slowly zooms out further and further as we see that Corey is all alone in the huge lobby of this hotel.... Allowing him to yell at the top of his lungs and pretty much do whatever the hell he wants. The scene fades to plaid.:: !The End!
