Mono:-The First:- Plan

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/04/03 “What in the nine bloody hells does that woman think she is damn well up to?” Lacuna, as eloquent as ever, spat the words out angrily. Her face was screwed up into a frown as she sliced the landscape up with her eyes. She was looking for Jade, and apparently Jade did not want to be found. It had been some time since Jade had even bothered to show her face around the house. The living arrangements were being stretched, as Jade’s boarding was under intense scrutiny, as she had not been seen for quite some time and it was possible that she wasn’t even there. She balled her small fist angrily and closed her eyes, resting the fist against her forehead, tilting her head to the soft flowing grass lapping gently at her feet. To her right was Brook, her head servant and probably her only friend beside Jade. He was definitely the only member of her workforce that she trusted enough to allow freedom of speech. “Have you considered that she may not even be here? She could have gone back to that strip club or something” added Brook with a sly smile spreading across his face. It was common knowledge that Brook did not like Jade, in fact he despised the woman and what she had put him through. She treated him as one of the general servants, and that offended him greatly. “Of course I have!” she snapped back, flinging her eyes open and her hand downwards into her side, clearly frustrated “Last I heard she was going off after her cousin and his cronies that took the club from her. She could be dead!” She began to pace around in a small circle, venting her frustration on a small tuft of grass by kicking wildly at it with a black booted foot, sending little blades of grass flying away like shards of a broken window into the warm and moist air. “God forbid” replied Brook with a little too much sarcasm evident in his voice, for Lacuna obviously picked up on it as she balled both fist and strides towards him, pressing one first into his chubby cheek. Brook’s eyes glowed with fear as Lacuna stood right in his face. “Do you want your testicles taken off via the slamming of two bricks, Brook?” Brook shook his head violently as Lacuna slammed her fists together as if to reinforce quite how serious she was being. Lacuna really did care about Jade not showing up. She was at Turmoil, yet she was so distant it was unbelievable. They had not worked together as a team to much success that night, as neither seemed to trust the intentions of the other much. Jade had her own agenda, and it didn’t include Lacuna. “Well then, if you wish to keep them, you will gentle your tone and be more courteous to your superiors. I do not like the class divide, Brook, but she is an independent woman and you, you are practically my property. You will keep your sarcasm to yourself from now on. You understand, peasant?” she turned away and purposely let her raven black hair whip across Brook’s face as a final insult as his face turned towards the grass. He had mightily offended her as she very rarely brought out the class ideals. Indeed, she was of a higher class than him, but she rarely thought of herself as anything more than an equal. “Yes Ma’am” he muttered, but Lacuna had already started the long walk down the lawns and was out of hearing range for Brook’s embarrassed mumbling. The grass got greener and deeper as she walked. She had left the part of the gardens that were actually tended to, and her walk had led her into a small nature reserve that she kept for such a time. There were many times that she required a quiet place to think, many more when she just wanted to be alone. It was peaceful and unkempt: just how she liked things. It was the total opposite of life in the big houses, Brook always made sure everything was in its correct place, and if anything wasn’t then another was brought in to replace it. Order did upset her, it reminded her of her parents. But she did not want to think about them now, because she had a different subject to think through, she was losing Jade and it felt like there was nothing that she could do about it for the time being. Jade had locked herself up and had gone into her own little worlds. She was so far away in herself, but where was she? Her little touches were everywhere, but she was not around to enjoy them. She had lost her only friend just like she had lost her parents. “NO!” screamed Lacuna loudly “NO! NO NO NO!” Birds flew out of the trees as her words slowly died down, the calm restoring. It was not meant to be like this! Everything she ever wanted found a way of escaping her. She was a loner and it appeared that she was always going to be like this. It was like Jade was always saying. She needed a relationship, although Jade never put it so politely. She smiled at the thought, then frowned as she realised she was thinking in the past tense. She was acting like Jade was dead, and she had only been missing a very small time. She wasn’t dead! She couldn’t be dead. She was around somewhere and Lacuna would make it her duty to find her. Funny really, that she had hated Jade for so long. The first time they met Jade had begun stealing from her. Nothing important, well, she had tried, but only got away with minor things. It bothered her then, but now it felt so trivial. Maybe she was making too big a deal out of this, Jade was a free woman and could come and go when she wished, but it disturbed that she had finally made a friend and then her friend, who had been trying so hard, would completely disappear on her. It was hurting more than she could ever let on. She could never get on with people, she was too self absorbed, but she had liked Jade and Jade appeared to like her too. Why she had chosen to go it alone was beyond Lacuna, but at least it was possible to make it on her own, she had done so for most of her life. People only dragged you back and halted progression. Jade had caused her all these problems. She could be training or maybe doing something work related, but she was held back, thinking about things, and specifically Jade. This had been analysed as one of Lacuna’s major problems, that she would think about everything in far too much detail, causing her to become fixated on one solution that she had convinced herself was the only one suitable. This was no exception. She jumped to her feet, suddenly excited. Things were spelling out now, it all made sense. “Yes! Yes, I will find her. She has to be there! It’s all down to me… My Fault, my problem” she growled the last couple of words as she glanced around suspiciously. She could feel a presence in the overgrown untamed wildness of her minor nature reserve, but all she could imagine was herself, looking down upon her. Just like the dreams. From afar, Brook watched on intensely. His piggish brown eyes followed her every enthusiastic move from his perch towards the top of a small hill overlooking the nature reserve. She was passing around the small pond, its two birds rapidly moving away from the approaching woman. Her hair was whipping around in the growing levels of wind, and she wiped it back with the side of her hand. Her every movement was exaggerated by her apparent excitement. “She has a plan…”he whispered to himself as she reached the outer extremities of the nature reserve. It looked as if she was heading back to the house, but he could not be sure. From here, Brook had the perfect angle from which to follow her movements, and he needed to know what she was up to. “God dammit, I feel dirty. She doesn’t need a stalker, she has issues” he said aloud, but all he could think about was protecting her. Yet again, his thoughts were contradicting his words. It was not only Lacuna that needed to make up her mind. She was passing from his view now, so he stood to his shiny shoed feet and passed uncomfortably across the rutted and scarred landscape of the hill so as to follow her progression. Her smoothed down his black suit and wiped the wisp of thinning brown hair out of his eyes, settling down on a steep step like feature, kicking his legs idly over the side of the step. Lacuna had gotten into a golf buggy that had been left there weeks ago and was now hurtling back towards the house, climbing the steep slope of the gardens and lawns quickly. Soon she would be entirely out of his poor view, as his eyesight wasn’t what it used to be, so he needed to follow. He needed to know what she was doing. “That ruffian wont have my Lacuna hurt, she’ll have me to deal with. Whatever troubles she’s gotten herself into, Lacuna wont be getting her out.”
