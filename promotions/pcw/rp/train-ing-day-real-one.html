Train-Ing Day (Real One)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered In the past couple of weeks, Flaymin’s alliance with the Pharaoh progressed a step further.It was announced, reluctantly we might add, that Flaymin would be taking over as chief trainer of The Pharaoh’s lacking security team.Will the bumbling bodyguards get back on track with the help of the Flaymin? Just wait and see….. [Scene opens on a bright, sunny day in the middle of a field that is surrounded by shrubs and other small plants. Flaymin stands upon an empty tin can and lectures his students a.k.a. The Pharaoh’s Security Squadron] Flaymin: Right, do any of you know why you’re here? [The overweight security guards look at each other in puzzlement and shrug their shoulders.Finally, a rather stocky, middle-aged bodyguard named Horacio steps foward] Flaymin: Yes, you the 40 yearold virgin. Horacio: Sir, we believe we are here to refine our skills. [Flaymin stares at Horacio for a second before raising an eyebrow] Flaymin: What skills?You couldn’t defend a picnic basket from a rampaging pack of bull-ants! Horacio: We get free lunch?Cool! [The inferior troupe of security guards begin high-fiving each other] Flaymin: No, no you idiots.Today is day one of the ‘Flaymin Aymin Intense Exercise Program’. [Another security guard named Phillip steps forward to question Flaymin] Phillip: So then what are we doing in the middle of this field? Flaymin: Ah ha!Allow me to show you all. [Flaymin leads the inept security guards through the field until they arrive at a nearby train track.Flaymin places one foot up on the steel train track, with his hand on his hip] Flaymin: Now, who knows the first lesson of being a good bodyguard? Horacio: Watching that Whitney Houston movie of the same name? Flaymin: I’m sorry I thought this was a Security school.I think you’re looking for ‘Homo Haven’ where you can prance around in tight lycra and sing all the Whitney Houston songs you like! [Horacio looks at Flaymin’s ‘WHAM!’ T shirt and frowns] Horacio: But isn’t WHAM! also gay? [Furious, Flaymin gawks back at Horacio] Flaymin: Don’t you let me catch you badmouthing WHAM! EVER AGAIN!! [Flaymin continues to stare at Horacio with a serious look on his face. A moment later he eases up and returns back to his normal state] Flaymin: Ahem, now the reason we are here today is simply to train you all in techniques that deal with stopping the rush of crazy fans. I’m sure The Pharaoh doesn’t like to be approached by insignificant little fans and its your job to stop these inferior leeches from approaching your boss. Which is exactly why today we will be tackling TRAINS!!! [The group of guards instantly grow silent.Their eyes grow wide and they watch in awe as Flaymin explains his ploy which involves the bodyguards tackling oncoming trains] Flaymin: Now this is by no means an easy job.But if you want to successfully stop the mad rush of moronic fans then you really need to get the practice by unleashing your resistance techniques against a rampaging train! Phillip: You… you mean…. You want us to tackle REAL trains? [Flabbergasted, Flaymin throws his hands up in the air before responding sarcastically] Flaymin: No Alice, I meant Thomas The Tank Engine.He’ll scoot on in and you can all sit down, braid each others hair and have a nice cup of tea….. Horacio: Does Thomas like sugar in his tea? Flaymin: For crying out loud-OF COURSE I F*CKING MEAN REAL TRAINS!!! Look guys, it really isn’t as bad as you all make it sound.Here, let me show you this scar I’ve got. [Flaymin lifts up his shirt, revealing a nasty gash on his left hip] Horacio: You got that while tackling a train? Flaymin: What?Hell no! I’m not that stupid!I got it after a beer keg exploded in close proximity. [The perplexed guards all look at each other in confusion] Phillip: What does that have to do with tackling trains? [Flaymin looks at Phillip before pointing his index finger at himself and nodding his head] Flaymin: Hey, hey, I’ll ask the damn questions here. Phillip: But you brought it up…. Flaymin: Brought what up?Quit straying from the job at hand with your pointless stories! Phillip: But you…. Arrghh never mind. Flaymin: You, come here! [Flaymin yanks out a nervous looking guard named Stanley and pushes him onto the train tracks] Flaymin: Right, now all you need to do is relax.Bend your knees a little and keep a straight back as if you’ve got a carrot up your ass.Now when the train comes, just try and bearhug it.Then you drive it back with the power in your legs. Stanley: Will that stop the train? Flaymin: Uh….Kinda. Stanley: Kinda? Flaymin: Well, with your bloody carcass in the way I’m sure you’ll momentarily slow the train down while its wheels crunch over your bones. [Stanley’s jaw drops open and he stares back at Flaymin with a horrid look on his face. Flaymin reassuringly places his hand on Stanley’s shoulder] Flaymin: Relax, you’ll be fine.You do have life insurance, don’t you? Stanley: No sir, I don’t. Flaymin: Yikes!Then this could be messy! [Suddenly in the distance Flaymin notices some shooting steam in the air.Slowly, the undeniable figure of a train begins to appear….. coming closer towards the group- and more importantly young Stanley who stands on the train tracks, shaking in fear with his knees knocking repeatedly. Flaymin steps off the track and yells out some final words of wisdo… well words of idiocy actually, back at Stanley] Flaymin: Remember kid, just relax and go with the flow. Oh and don’t bend your neck like that or you’ll probably get a scratch on it. [Stanley straightens up his neck to avoid possible ‘scratching’.He then widens his arms into the ‘bearhug’ position and waits for the train. Gradually the train grows closer and closer…. CHUGGA! CHUGGA! CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [Stanley gulps in fear one last time as the train closes to within 20 metres of him….] CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [The chugging sound begins to heighten and the roaring vibrations of the train intensify, as the train is suddenly only a few metres away from Stanley…] CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA!CHUGGA! CHUGGA! [Scene fades to black] What happens next???Does poor little Stanley join that Train Station in the sky?Or will he survive another day? Tune in next time to find out!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
