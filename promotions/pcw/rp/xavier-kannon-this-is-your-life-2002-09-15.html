XAVIER KANNON - THIS IS YOUR LIFE?!?

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 - 09 - 02 The scene opened in the confines of a secluded television studio with a congregation of empty seats and a command tower overlooking the built up podium used for talk shows and live broadcasts. The area has a dense silence as though this segment were after business hours but that tranquility would soon be interrupted upon by two members of the stable known as Asylum, notably the individuals would collectively be Pranks With Attitude. 'Another conniving caper by the green genius....' hissed Nerezza adjusting his crimson red sleeveless trench coat we have became accustomed to. The vampire smiled to his comrade, brandishing his two piercing canines in the process. 'There's very little else we can do to get under the skin of Hubbardville's favourite son!' retorted the emerald menace returning the smile by showcasing his pearly white teeth. The stage had been set-up with a host of comfortable chairs and a large plasma screen television set surrounded by angled cameras, which would capture the show from various different points. 'Again we Main Event - Hopefully we can eradicate our last outing with the World Champion and make Hubbard's little boy cry....' continued the jovial Jester. 'Quoting the commentary team... Sad even for your lowly standards, Gaz!' replied the behemoth as he scouted around for suitable equipment. 'Err... Wha-... Who... Did the commentary team say something similar, I rarely take notice?' murmured Gaz 'The Jester' Green rolling his eyes. Gaz took up his position on the stage under an intense spotlight that showcased his choice of attire. Standing in a smug catalogue pose the Jester allows the camera to roam over his velvet business tuxedo as he swept his hand through his dyed green hair, again held in a fashionable spike. Taking hold of a red book that sat snuggly on one of the fabric chairs, Nerezza peered through the optics of the camera capturing his colleague in all his glory. Nerezza making some last minute adjustments gestured a three second countdown with his nimble fingers, indicating what appears to be the start of this show entitled 'This Is Your Life?' the camera begins to roll. Gaz soaked in one last deep breath in preparation for his big announcement then blurted out his opening line. 'Ladies, Gentlemen and... Lowly Scientologists I welcome you to another edition of 'This Is Your Life?' with myself the stand-in host Gaz Green. I'm sorry that your usual host Michael Aspel was unable to attend but celebrity workloads and the fact that he managed to tie himself up and lock the broom cupboard door could only be matched by David Copperfield' roared the Lord Of Laughter drawing a thumbs up from the obscure Nerezza. 'Today’s show is about wrestling's leading name....' bellowed the PCW comic before an untimely interruption from the Angel Of Fear who continues to operate the camera at distance. 'IVAN STANISLAV!!!' 'HUH?!?' choked the host, taken back by the immediate comment before regaining his flagging composure. 'BIGGER! Today’s leading name has done it all. He is the current PCW World Champion, the present owner of the company and founder of the infamous stable known as the Front Office... XAVIER KANNON~!' The blank screen of the motionless plasma television set flickered into life with a still image of the Scientologist himself after the historic event when he captured the Championship. 'As a boy Hubbardville's favourite son wasn't interested in religion, which as you know has taken a strangle hold over him since his introduction into Scientology. Xavier Kannon was your average youngster growing up in a world with numerous religions, all trying to brainwash and influence until one eventful day changed his life....' cried the Prince Of Pranks quenching his dry throat with a glass of still water. “.... That day saw a Catholic minister make advances on the young Xavier Kannon, offering to take him to a special place. It was either those advances that alerted him or the Gary Glitter hit 'Do You Want To Be In My Gang?' playing softly in the background but that one encounter changed the life of our Champion forever. He then soon sided with the religion known as Scientology and his life took a dramatic turn, because it was this belief that inspired him to the squared circle and try his hand at wrestling. It was during his time in wrestling and a company known as NTICW that the budding Scientologist would meet and marry a dizzy diva called Eleanor Hall' continued the devious prankster Gaz Green holding back his own laughter as Nerezza giggled quietly to himself, most likely over the Gary Glitter charade mentioned earlier. 'AND XAVIER DO YOU RECOGNISE THIS VOICE?' 'Oh, Xavier, I told you never to marry that money grabbing whore...' said a soft sweet voice accompanied by an anonymous woman. As the camera drew closer, the woman could be identified as Anarchy masquerading as the CEO's mother as she adorned a long flowing dress with a flower insignia design splashed over the fabric randomly. 'What a pleasure to be making the acquaintance of the woman who brought forth the Gold Patron Meritorious!!!' grinned the velvet trickster ushering his colleague to the seating arrangement. Both celebrities crush heavily into their respected seats, Gaz crossing his legs to look a little more formal and with a tilted head he addressed the impersonating Anarchy. 'So Mrs. Kannon, how does it feel in being the main person to groom such a successful son within the wrestling industry?' questioned the inquisitive Jester. 'Well my boy has turned his life right around. From a young age he was the centre of bullying and would come home in floods of tears causing him to wet the bed right up until his late teens when he finally discovered wrestling as a form of therapy....' whispered Anarchy in full portrayal of Mrs. Kannon. The unpredictable comedian began to slowly nod off into a dreamlike state as Anarchy ventured around Xavier Kannon's past then suddenly sprung back into life with yet another announcement with regards to a featuring guest. 'Not to cramp your style Old Mother Hubbard but we have another guest....' yelled Gaz Green in bated breath. With a cocky strut Miyagawa stepped hotfooted onto the stage with a knitted jumper and chord trousers, his hair had been sprayed over with a temporary grey colouring. The imitating geriatric took a seat alongside his fellow Asylum members and waited for his queue. 'So Mr. Kannon....' asked the instigator of injury before Alexander Miyagawa cut his progress short. 'My kid could've been anything but he decided to be a bleeding Scientologist. What kind of religion is followed by the likes of Tom Cruise? I mean it's all well and good being some hot-shot wrestler but doesn't he realise that his success is due to marrying some fine ass with a business sixth sense....' injected the Asylum leader with a tone of authority. As the trio lock into what appeared to be a stalemate a huge crashing of thuds could be heard from the direction of the door, followed by a hoard of thunderous bellows. 'SECURITY - IF ANYONE'S IN THERE THEN ITS TRESPASSING!!!' 'I'M GOING TO PHONE THE POLICE!!!' 'OPEN THIS FRIGGIN DOOR....' continued the security guard as he laid into the door with a series of clenched fists. 'Well folks it's been yet another success story and I hope you got to know Xavier Kannon a little better and as a tribute we have this short film!?!' said Gaz Green as the camera peered over to the television set now playing footage of the recent miss-haps involving both Xavier Kannon and Pranks With Attitude as the Asylum crept quietly away. -Footage- Ellie is shown skipping around a corner as the voice of her husband, PCW CEO Xavier Kannon is heard. 'MY LITTLE FUCK FERRET... I'M HOOOOOOME!' The door is heard slowly opening, followed by the sound of an explosion. *KKKKKKKKKABOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMM!!!!!!* 'IN HUBBARD'S NAME!!! THE HARI KRISHNAS ARE TRYING TO BOMB ME!!!' Suddenly as the Asylum vanish from trace, the security guard enters under a rattling of keys and scurried over to the television switching it off as the next segment began rolling. As the irate guard glances back and forth the image draws to black, signalling the end.
