Babysitter Screening For Grimer

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/02 Its been close to a week and Kramer hasn't let Grimer out of his sight. The once very timid mouse has transformed into a more out going type creature, who isn't afraid of everything like he once was. That could be because Grimer is spoiled. He has a huge tunnel that stretches from the corner of Kramer's living room, all the way up to Kramer's room where his cage resides atop Kramer's dresser. Not only that but a car (one with a wheel so when he runs on it the car moves), top of the line food, and his own set of clothing! Clothing I say! Going from the dreary laboratory life, to a fun filled one, Grimer must think he is in heaven. The scene opens up in Kramer's house, about ten feet from the front door to be exact. He sits sternly in a cold steel chair which is set behind a table filled with papers. His dress has a sophisticated look with the white button down shirt and black slacks, which gives us the impression that something important is going on. From the right comes Jason who looks highly relieved in his red button down shirt and tan khakis. Jason: Kramer is this really necessary? Kramer turns to Jason and looks him flat in the eyes. Kramer: Your flies down. Jason's eyes widen as he peers down. Sure enough his zipper is down so he quickly pulls it up a slight embarrassed. Jason: Do you really think its necessary to get a sitter for Grimer? I mean you will only be gone for two days. Kramer: Two days is an awfully long time Jason. Besides I wouldn't be able to live with myself if something happened. Grimer's my new best bud. Jason: Well then do you think its really necessary to screen sitters? All they have to do is feed the little roden....errr Grimer. Kramer: If you haven't realized it yet, their are psychos in the world. If I leave the wrong person with Grimer could end up dead. And he doesn't only need feeding. He's just like a little baby who needs supervised play time, proper grooming, and changing of clothing and diaper ((yes if you remember Grimer has a little bladder problem so he wears a diaper courtesy of Kramer)). Jason walks behind Kramer and takes a seat in the living room laughing to himself. A knocking on the door catches Kramer's attention. He opens it to reveal an elderly woman with the grandma type look. Woman: I'm here for the interview. Kramer: Come on in Ms....... Woman: Ms. Grendel. Kramer escorts her to the chair on the opposite of him. She takes a seat and places her hands on her lap as Kramer gets ready to drill her with various questions. Kramer: Have you ever babysat for anyone before Ms. Grendel? Ms. G: Only my grandchildren. Kramer: So you have never looked after animals? Ms. G: No. I have an allergic reaction to any pet bearing fur. Kramer: Thats all of the questioning I have. I would have loved to hire you but I have a pet mouse that needs to be cared for, and since your allergic, I wouldn't want to put you in any danger. Thanks for coming though. Ms. Grendel stands up and shakes Kramer's hand as she heads for the door. Kramer opens it for her and shuts it as she walks out. He then turns walking back to the table catching Jason staring at him snickering. Kramer: What's so funny? Jason: I still can't get over how pathetic you are. Instead of worrying about your big match on Tuesday, here you are worrying over a stupid mouse. Kramer: First of all I have been thinking about Tuesday, and personally I feel its time for me to get back some gold. The first step for that comes on Tuesday, and when I win I am going after Sunshine. And second Grimer isn't stupid! Jason: Why are you so hung up over the mouse? I just don't get it! The tunnel was alright, but then you bought him clothes? Doesn't that seem a bit weird to you? Kramer: Weird? Look, if you had experienced what I did, I am sure you would be doing the same thing. Before Jason can retort another knocking comes to the door. Kramer walks over to answer it which leaves Jason eager to know the experience that causes him to worry so much over Grimer. At the door, Kramer backs up a bit as a burly man with bushy beard, walks in with his motorcycle look. Kramer takes a seat and so does the man. Kramer: Hello? Man: May names Ginger Bout and I am here for the babysitting interview. Kramer looks at the man up and down trying hard to keep from laughing due to the mans name. Ginger: ((in a booming voice)) Don't let the name fool you. My parents were a tad bit drunk when they named me. I guess thats what happens when you watch Gilligan's Island reruns so much. Kramer: Ummm...ok. Well have a seat Mr. Bout. Both men take a seat and the interview begins. Kramer: Have any experience? Ginger: ((thinking to himself)) I have been a bouncer, stunt man, hitman, and had my own bar......no I don't think I have ever babysat but I am always up for new things. Kramer: Do you like animals? Ginger: What kind? Kramer: Like mice? Ginger: Of course I like mice. I got to feed something to my python don't I? All of the color in Kramer's face drops as he hears Ginger's answer. Ginger: You alright? You look a little pale. Kramer: Oh me? I'm fine. Listen I will call you if you get the job. Ginger stands up and walks out the door. Kramer follows close behind. Ginger turns around remembering something. Ginger: But I never gave you my number. Kramer slams the door in his face and walks over towards Jason who is busy counting the money in his leather wallet. Kramer: That's it. Grimer is coming with me no doubt about it. Jason: Whats the point in that? You are going to be wrestling. Kramer: Oh believe me I will think about something. I mean I can't leave him with a freako like Ginger. Jason: Ginger....that's amusing. Kramer: You better make sure you have a super helping of KFC, because I want to get as powered up as possible. Tuesday is a day of triumph....its a day that I will become the number one contender for a title I have held in the past. Jason: Whatever. The scene slowly fades as Kramer walks upstairs probably to play with Grimer. Jason on the other hand continues to count his money, which shouldn't take too long considering he barely has any. The scene slowly fades to black.
