Don't Break My Heart, My Achey Breaky Heart

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17 [The scene opens on a still picture of one of the most ugly men to every grace the prints of Kodak paper. The man's name is Bubba McGinty, a very popular shock jock in the southern area. Country music is his game, and making fun of rock n roll has gotten him his fame, negative and positive. Bubba's teeth are stained yellow from years of cigar smoking and tobacco chewing and they are crooked because of the handful of times he fell off his horse while riding through the country. His red, round face has a very disturbing Santa Claus image but his yellow eyes totally kill it. He has his black hair, or what's left of it, combed straight back as it shines brighter than the brightest star in the sky. All in all Bubba McGinty is pretty much the biggest hick in the entire world, and he's fine with that] [Bubba sits behind his large microphone, with barrels of hay and posters of half naked women on the walls. The radio studio was once a state of the art, beautifully structured radio station. Unfortunately, the designers of this studio didn't know about Bubba's certain tastes when he's spinning the records on the air. He demands big breasted, busty gals be placed on the walls so that they are always within his viewing pleasure. He wants barrels of hay surrounding him and guests because he enjoys the smell of it. Then there is the hint of motor oil that he demands be spread through the vents at certain times to make it feel like he's back in his garage, working on his pick up truck. The heads of the station have begun to get fed up, but he brings in top notch ratings for this country music station and has attracted great guests to his talk show. Bubba is here to stay, until this day] Bubba McGinty: Ya'll love me!! I know ya'll do. Because I've got a hum dinger of a show for ya'll. Billy Ray Cyrus is gonna be joinin' us and he's gonna break down some lyrics from 'Achey Breaky Heart'. I know you ladies are just DYIN' to hear that. Joinin' Billy will be two wrasslers called Cowabunga Connection. I don't know much about those guys but I know they are from PCW, and that's where Duke Williams is from and Duke Williams is the cat's pajamas. HAHAHA!!! [Bubba's laughter sounds like a mix of Jabba The Hut and Chewbacca. He then begins coughing loudly, the sound of flem rising from his throat into his mouth. He swishes it through his mouth and just spits it on the floor. In the adjoining studio a pimple faced intern, quite like the one in PCW, holds his head low and sheds a tear] Bubba: Yeah! A great show today. I'd like to go on and talk about the escalating situation in Afghanistan. That is just some crazy SHIIIIZNIT! Our troopers are searching high and low for that GOD DAMN Osama bin Laden and we can't find him. So here is what I propose to the heads of the army. You spread some VIAGRA around those caves and mountains and I'll guarantee that that prick will pop RIGHT UP!! HAHAHA!!! [Once again the Jabba/Chewbacca laugh echoes through the studio, most likely making listeners cover their ears because it's so loud and boisterous. Another shot of flem rises and Bubba spits it in a half filled cup of coffee. He then survey's the flem he spit in there, raises his eyebrows and then takes down the rest of the coffee and the flem. The intern then looks grossed out, throwing his face into a garbage can to release his stomach acids into it] Bubba: Seems my intern friend is having a bit of a problem with his stomach...freakin' moron!! But it's time to bring in my guests. First Billy Ray Cyrus!!! [The once big country heartthrob walks into the studio with his half mullet and inbred looking facial hair. He takes a seat with a smile on his face, obviously happy about something. He places a pair of earphones around his mullet of doom] Billy Ray: It's great to be here, thanks. Bubba: Damn, you look like you haven't lost a step, boy. I'd like to take you to the barn, smack your ass, and call you kitty. [Billy Ray seems confused and frightened] Billy Ray: Huh? Bubba: Just a thought, but it's not important. The important thing here is that your going to begin to sing for us. So do what we all want you to do and sing 'Achey Breaky Heart' for us... [Billy Ray smiles, his face showing complete embarrassment] Billy Ray: I'm done with all of that now, Bubba. I've put that all in my past and I'm concentrating on becoming an actor. I was in that movie, Mullholland Drive. I got to play the pool boy who was banging the directors girlfriend. It was a great time and it got me a heck of a lot of exposure. David Lynch is a great director. [Bubba doesn't even pay attention to his first guest, breaking into song after Billy Ray's statement] Bubba: Don't Tear My Heart, My Achey Breaky Heart!!! [Billy Ray can do nothing but laugh and keep a sane face as this ignorant fat, ugly man ruins his one and only country smash hit that he cannot seem to out run for the life of him] Bubba: Come on!! Sing, Billy Ray!! Sing or I'll have to bend you over the desk and SPANK YOU! Billy Ray: Uhm...don't you have other guests? Bubba: HOT DAMN!! Your right! Bring in the Cowabunga Connection, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry. [The studio door opens and Tommy is the first to enter. He wears a Hawaiian dress shirt, with a colorful floral pattern, blue and yellow swim trunks, and a pair of black sandals. His blonde hair is wet down as he wears his million dollar smile, showing off his pearly white teeth. Harry enters immediately behind his best friend and tag team partner. Harry wears his BRAND NEW Pittsburgh Steelers football helment with 'Bongus' printed on the front. He wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Harry seems very upbeat and more stoned than usual] Bubba: God damn, boy!! Why in the HELL are you wearin' that football helmet? You got my big bad baby boy Duke Williams dropping you on your head or somethin'? [Tommy and Harry put on the ear phones, both with stupid far away smiles on their faces] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You sound gay, man. Bubba: I am not gay! How dare you come into my studio with those kinds of accusations! Just because I want to throw Duke Williams over a saddle, paint his butt with vaseline, and ram it home doesn't mean I'm gay!! So get that out of your head. [Tommy and Harry look at each other, then look at Billy Ray who shrugs his shoulders, and then they look at each other once again] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Your totally bogus, dude. Bubba: Yeah...whatever. In speaking of gay...don't you have a match with a couple of gay men this Tuesday? I think their called the Heartbreakers, or so this piece of paper typed up by my pimple faced intern says. TTT: Your totally right, dude. We've been most unsuccessful since the restart of the company, but we're looking to change that with a most bodacious run against the Heartbreakers on Tuesday. Bubba: I've got a picture of these guys that I pulled up from the PCW website and I'd love for them to break my heart....HOT DAMN! Harry: Your gayness is scaring me, man. Bubba: How do you pretty boy surfers expect to beat a team that's all about the PINK like The Heartbreakers? I mean look at these boys!! I'd love to oil them up with Crisco, bust out the spatula, and spank SPANK SPANK!!!! Billy Ray: Do you mind if I leave? Bubba: Your not leavin' BOY!! Billy Ray: Ok. TTT: Dude, it doesn't matter if they are gay. What matters is that the Cowabunga Connection is the most bodacious, most radical tag team that has ever graced the halls of PCW. First Politically Incorrect went about taking bogus actions to make sure we didn't succeed and now it seems that The Heartbreakers are going to take that role. They certainly are bogus in a heinous way. Harry: Yeah, man. Bubba: The Stillers suck! The Titans make them their bitches any day of the week. Harry: What, man? Bubba: The Stillers suck! Harry: What's a Stiller...Ben Stiller, man? He's funny, man. Bubba: The Pittsburgh Stillers. Harry: Ben Stiller lives in Pittsburgh, man? TTT: We were just there and we didnt' see him. Talk about being bogus, dude. I'm going to call Ben up and tell him about his heinousness. Billy Ray: Do you guys smoke crack? TTT: No, why? Billy Ray: Just a thought. Bubba: Listen. There is no way in the name of Duke Williams, my babycakes, that you can defeat The Heartbreakers this Tuesday. Not only that you have to wrassle the Pretty Cool Guys, another top contender for the PCW Tag Titles. TTT: Where do you get that info? Bubba: My pimple faced intern. [Bubba points to the other room where the kid stands waving his hand] Bubba: He's going to get extra vaseline tonight. [The pimple faced kid begins throwing up in the garbage can again] TTT: Pretty Cool Guys are not cool, in fact, they are TOTALLY BOGUS!! Just a duo of ugly dudes who can't sing. Harry: Yeah, man. If they sang Twisted Sister they would rule all, man. TTT: Totally... Billy Ray: Are you sure about that crack thing? TTT: Positive, dude. Billy Ray: Just had to double check. Bubba: Listen, I'll grease you up, smack a half baked turkey on your bum, and then lick off the juices, but your not going to come up with two big victories against these teams. TTT: You know what, dude. This interview is OVER!! [Tommy jumps from his chair and leaves the studio. Harry, always a bit slow, stays in his chair] Bubba: Your not going to storm out of the room, moron? [Harry looks over to Billy Ray and then back to Bubba. Harry leaps over the large radio console and headbutt's Bubba with the Steelers helmet. Bubba is knocked unconscious] Harry: Cowabunga Connection rules, man. [Harry then leaves the room. Billy Ray giggles to himself, going behind the microphone] Billy Ray: Hello, this is good ol' Billy Ray Cyrus comin' at ya. I've got a great body, a great voice, and I'm extremely well endowed so ladies...just as Rod Roddy from the Price is Right says...COME ON DOWN...YEEEE HAW!! [The scene then fades to black]
