The Death Saga: My Carousel

23 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-01 DarkStar continued his travels, slowly revelling in the glory of this 'hell', this hell which is nothing but skyscrapers lifting to the heavens (bad pun) and the cars whirring by on the streets. DarkStar stood on the corner of one of these hell-streets and gazed off down the road, it was bustling. This all made no sense, Hell was no different then anything was on earth, theres no firey pits, no lakes of fire, only run down buildings, vehicles, and materialism... Now I get it... DarkStar thrust his hands deep into his pockets, strolling down the street a bit further untill he reaches a intersection, on the way he passes hundreds of the damned. Bitter and cold irony, in heaven you have nothing, you have your thoughts, hear you have everything, just like on earth... irony indeed. He crosses the street, before he gets halfway he gets cut off, a brand new (does hell have time?) Corvette almost runs him over as it spins into his direction, behind him a old lady yells a series of profanities to get the car moving, of course, DarkStar walks to the window... Driver: Get in. DarkStar stared blankly at the man. Driver: Look, the big man wanted me to show you around town, now get in the car moron. He reluctantly complies, sliding onto the leather seats. DarkStar: ...Nice car. The driver looks over with a patronizing face on before looking back to the rode and speeding off. The pair are going about 70 down this street. Driver: So, has he told you ab-... Wait... You look familiar... DarkStar raises his eyebrow, giving a puzzled look. Driver: Wait a second... You killed me! Ah... that was where I've seen this clown before... I can remember it pretty disctinctly. He was a bully in high school, made my life a living hell and almost killed me on one occasion. Nice to see he wound up here Driver: You faggot Goth Kid! Im tired of you, I have a right mind to-... WHAT ARE YO-... NOOOOOO!!!!!!! DarkStar uses the side of the door as leverage and lifts his foot up, kicking the man across the temple from the other chair, half unconcious the man bleeds into the door. A few seconds later and the windshield shatters, the man goes flying... DarkStar simply gets up. Now where does that poor bastard go... ah, who car-... whats this? Standing in front of him, about two stories tall, is a clothing store, Satan and Taylors. You know, I've been wearing the same thing since my death, maybe I could use a change. He enters the rather lavish store (not realizing he is in the high class section of the underworld) and looks about a bit, before the store clerk notices him. The woman looks him up and down and speaks. Clerk: And WHO are YOU? DarkStar: I have many names. But I am the King of Dreams, of the Nightmare Realms... The Clerk waves ehr hand disrespectfully. Clerk: Whatever, I didn't ask for your life story dirtball, what do you want? DarkStar looks behind her, at a beautiful white and black leather trenchcoat. DarkStar: That trenchcoat, I want it. Clerk: If you have enough money, its yours. DarkStar: Money? Satan makes you use MONEY? The clerk lets a breathe of mocking air out before rolling her eyes. Clerk: Tsk. Satan doesnt make us do anything, we do all this by our own will, he trusts us, without a moron like you working ther-... Her sentance is cut short. Her forhead impaled with the broken off arm of a mannequin in the corner, the blood squirts all about as the index finger jams its way into her brain, cutting off her motor functions and leaving her, dead on the floor........ DarkStar walks out of the store... his white and black trench coat flutters in the breeze, a quick shot of his new clothes shows that he means this eternity business. The trench, a pair of sunglasses, new boots... Robbery at its best... To Be Continued
