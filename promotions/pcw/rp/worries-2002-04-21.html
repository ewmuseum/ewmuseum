Worries

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/02 The scene opens up to a road surrounded on the sides by many bright green trees. Animals are flocking around playfully as the weather is just right for it. A baby squirrel frolics into the middle of the street and stops to scratch the back of his ear. The poor thing never had a chance, because zooming down the street was a blackberry colored truck. Had the guy driving the truck not been late for work, the squirrel would have lived another day. But instead there the squirrel lay, as flat as a pancake with tire tracks along its back. Another car comes into view. It's a Volkswagen Beetle with a white coloring and red and orange flames on the hood of the car, and along the doors. The car, just like the one before rolls right over the dead squirrel, making it unrecognizable. The scene switches inside the car. There we see Kramer who is looking kind of tired at the moment. Kramer today is wearing a black shirt with a neon green skull on the front, along with baggy tan colored cargo pants. Kramer reaches over to the radio and turns it on. The soft soothing sound of a violin can be heard and Kramer quickly flips the channel. Just like that the soft music turns into a blaring of vocals with Disturbed who are belting out 'Voices'. Kramer starts tapping at the steering wheel and bobbing his head to the beat. Kramer: (singing the best he knows how) Someone is gonna die when you listen to me..........Are you breathin no, do the wicked see you? From there he just hums along only knowing certain parts. He continues driving down the long narrow road. Getting quite bored with the current station Kramer starts to flip to other channels, and he stops at the mention of his name. Its a wrestling radio show, (nothing compared to Duke's of course) and Kramer is the subject of the moment. He listens as two guys consistently bash him for hiring Ultimate Authority to watch his back. They also dog on him even more saying that he hasn't been able to win a match by his own, against a decent opponent, for a long time. Kramer of course turns a little red faced as he turns it off. Kramer: Yeah so I'm a yellow bellied coward so what? Sure I may have paid them to help me out but I had good reasoning. I knew interference would be a factor considering Crichton is not an honorable man, so I asked for a little back up. Even I was suprised that Grant and Leblanc offered to help me out. The fine was hefty for their services but it....it was worth it. Kramer pauses as his look changes to worried. Kramer: Maybe it was a bad idea. I mean I know what those guys are like. They are ruthless, and sadistic people. Anyone associated with Nelson is bad news. It'd be my luck if they expect me to use thier services again tonight expecting even more money then before. And if I don't have the dough then I could possibly end up in the hospital. Oh Kramer what have you gotten yourself into!! He slows down at a red light and bangs his head on the steering wheel repeatedly. Kramer: I'm one screwed man. How am I gonna stick up to the likes of them? I'm just one man. I don't have many people to back me up, and personally I would feel even more upset with myself if I bring more people into the mix. Not only might I have to deal with them, which I am hoping is not the case. I also have to worry about the Dark Ages, seeing as how they like to do things in unison. I doubt that I will just be fighting Badge tonight. I need a miracle to get me out of the hole tonight. The light turns green and he slams his foot on the gas pedal causing him to speed up down the road. Kramer: And all of this time I have claimed to be a Duke Williams fan. I have let myself as well as Duke down, and I suppose I deserve what comes my way. I should have looked to Duke for help, because he has always been one to defend me. Yet I didn't and I fear that he has lost all faith in me. Kramer continues to look straight ahead with a sort of emptiness about him. The scene slowly fades.
