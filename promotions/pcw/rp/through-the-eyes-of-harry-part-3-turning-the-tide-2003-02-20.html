Through The Eyes Of Harry - Part 3 - Turning The Tide

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 20, 2003 [Alley Baggett would probably feel absolutely high on air if she knew that her hot selling statue was in 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's living room. She might be a little weirded out at the fact that Tommy's brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, is watching it like some kind of shaggy watch dog. Harry isn't exactly watching it, he's trying to come to a conclusion. He's trying to figure out whether or not Tommy will like what Harry has done. Will this statue be able to replace the space that the broken one had? Harry knows that it won't but it may help ease Tommy's pain. Maybe ease just enough so he can get up and get his head together for his title defense against Venneman Pendragon. It's morning...only a few days before Tommy has to enter the arena for his defense. It'll be just enough time to get him ready for his defense. The loud sound of Tommy's door creeking open echoes throughout the entire beach house. Harry's brown eyes brighten up as he stands up and sort of gets ready to motion toward the statue with it's legs spread wide open] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: TA DA! ['Totally Tubular' Tommy is not a depressed young man. In fact, he's been a shining star amongst a brutal landscape his entire life. The life of the party. the charismatic showman that the ladies flock to. He IS what entertainment is all about. Lately, he hasn't been able to grasp that special quality he possesses because of his broken statue. Rent-A-Hero trashed it during his sit down interview, right in front of his face too. Ever since that day Tommy's life has crashed to a holding pattern. He's gone completely inward and hasn't let anybody inside. He's become a total bore and a crying little girl. This is not who Tommy is, and even before the statue incident happened, Tommy has NOT been the same man] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Been there, done that. [Tommy then disappears from the living room and into the kitchen. Harry is sort of in shock at the response. Tommy didn't even really take a good look at it. Maybe he still needs to wake up, see the statue more clearly, that's it] Harry: Tommy, this is a GREAT statue! It's Alley Bagget. Look at her, she's a bodacious babe..uhh...man. [Tommy has a box of Captain Crunch in his hand as he pours a bunch into his mouth, many of them bouncing off of him and down to the floor. Harry holds the statue up, making sure that his bodacious brother can get a better look at it. He does. Then he turns away and dives back into the Captain Crunch box for some more] Harry: Dex at the Adult Merchandise Mart said that this was a hot selling one. I know it's not even close to what you had, but if you GIVE IT TIME you can really begin to start things over. A new statue. A new attitude. A new outlook on life. A new way to DEFEND YOUR CONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP!! [Tommy munches on the Captain Crunch just staring at his long haired, hippie looking brother with emotionless eyes] TTT: Har, I appreciate the excellent offer... [Tommy swallows down a mouthful of Captain Crunch] TTT: ...but it will never replace the gnarly Budiet statue. [Harry sighs and places the Alley Baggett statue on the breakfast table] Harry: Tommy, you need to understand that it was JUST A STATUE! I know that it meant a lot to you and that you viewed it as some kind of voodoo good luck charm, but shit happens!! People die. Statues get broken! I'm sure that it happens every single day! Death is a part of life, bro. We all have to live with it's consequences...as we both have known from experiences. [Tommy nods a little knowing full well that their father's both died in the ring together] Harry: I know what your feeling and I know that it sucks...but it's not doing anybody ANY good just to sit here like this and mourn. Especially not when you have a big match against Venneman Pendragon! You need to put it aside for just a few days so that we can get through this thing, man.... [Harry stops realizing for a quick moment that he ACTUALLY said 'man' after something and he didn't purposely put it there. That's really weird that he would do something like that...oh well...it doesn't matter. He needs to get his brother back on a somewhat stable track] Harry: Do you realize the CRAP we've been through together, as a unit. Some things are fuzzy but I do remember most of those moments. The Cowabunga Connection battling with Politically Incorrect last year at March Madness, being screwed out of the tag team titles by Xavier Kannon. How we retired team after team, racking up the wins in the column, sending us deeper and deeper into the hall of fame of tag teams. We may have never captured the gold, but there is no doubt about it that we WERE the best! You made up for that with your win of the Continental Title, bro, and it's TIME to show everyone that you may have turned over a new leaf, but you can still get in the ring and get the job done! No matter what...I'm going to be with you EVERY STEP OF THE WAY!! [Tommy just has the suspicious look on his face] TTT: Your sober, aren't you? Harry: Me? Sober? [Tommy looks again with suspicion, and then shrugs] TTT: Guess not, dude. [He then shoves more Captain Crunch into his face as Harry looks down to the statue surprised at how well the detail is done on it] TTT: Besides why would I want a statue of something that I know I can have over here in twenty minutes, dude. You see that breakfast table she sitting on? Harry: Of course. TTT: Well, she was on there a few months ago too. [Harry seems confused by that as Tommy is chewing on some Captain Crunch] TTT: You know what I mean, compadre. The horizontal mambo. Taking the dog for some pink surfing. Harry: Ohhh!! Well, the statue can be sort of a memento or something. TTT: I have her panties. Harry: Oh. [Tommy shakes the box of Captain Crunch and has this look of sadness come over him. It's nothing like the sadness for his statue but it's more like being let down. Because it's obvious that the Captain Crunch is all gone. More than half a box has been consumed in only five minutes. It's another downfall for The Tubular One, especially when he gets down about something, and it makes him eat like he hadn't eaten for days. It's extremely rare to see Tommy get like this too. Harry has probably seen something like this a few times in the entire lifetime that they have known each other] TTT: Did you really mean what you said, dude? That you'd bodaciously stay by my side no matter what? Harry: Tom, come on, I'm your BROTHER! I'm family! You could have arms growing out of your head and I would stay by your side. [Tommy's face sags in disgust] TTT: That would be totally bogus, dude. Harry: Your right, but I wouldn't leave you! TTT: That's very gnarly of you. Harry: Thank you. [Harry can see it just like on those cloudy days. The sun is just waiting behind the heinous clouds and the next thing you know the sun is out brighter than ever. It's almost blinding when it comes through the clouds and that's what Harry can see by the look on Tommy's face. Tommy just needed to be reminded of the special things that he had besides the statue. That Tommy has a family that cares for him and loves him no matter what choices he makes in life. If he wants to donate to a local porn shop instead of Make A Wish Foundation, then that's just something the family is going to have to live with. That's when Tommy slowly walks over and gives Harry a hug. It's not one of those cheap hugs that Tommy does, but it's one of those tight 'I don't know what I would do without you' hugs] TTT: I'm sorry, dude. Harry: It's all right, bro. TTT: It's time we get ready for some bodacious training, how about you? [Harry doesn't even have to send the signals to his brain to smile, it just happens] Harry: I was wondering when you would ask. [Tommy then trots out of the kitchen with a new sense of energy, and a new sense of purpose. Harry shakes his head at the statue not believing that all he had to do was remind Tommy of the important things in life. Even in his heinous super villain state it still sunk into his brain, which still shows that deep down somewhere in The Tubular One still lies the good hearted person he once was. Harry just smiles, enjoying his sober state of mind. It's like he's a totally different person, one who has a very strong grip of reality. It's a nice feeling] *DING DONG* [The doorbell nearly gives Harry a heart attack as he stumbles backward from the shock. He shakes it off and begins making his way through the living room, toward the front door. Since it's winter and no babes are around the beach these days, Tommy has the glass door covered with a sheet. Harry peels the sheet back to see who this unexpected visitor is, and it's a very odd surprise....] Tommy and Harry's Mom: Harry, we need to have ourselves a family discussion....NOW!!! [Fade to black] To Be Continued...
