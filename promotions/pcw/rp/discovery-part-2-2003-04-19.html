Discovery, Part 2

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 19, 2003 Discovery, Part 2 Written: April 19, 2003 [As the doors open a brilliant light spills forth onto the PCW World Champion. He can feel the heat of the sun piercing onto his skin and it's such a beautiful feeling. It's something that he's felt his entire life and he's never taken it for granted, like some people do. His tanned skin becomes golden against the strong rays of the sun and his blue eyes begin to light up into a sky blue. It's as if the sun gives him a power that nobody can explain, a special essence about himself that makes people turn their heads. Whether he's an evil super villain or whether he's a good samaritan who is willing to give, the people will always be taken back by his looks. The doors open wider and his blue satin shirt flows behind him like he's some kind of golden God being released from the gates of Heaven. His blonde locks of hair are spiked up into a frenzy as if he'd been electrocuted but it suits him very well. With his warming smile he completes the puzzle that is 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and stands motionless for a few moments. He looks out to the large crowd that has gathered and he looks into their eyes. The first thing he looks for are the bodacious babes, which there are plenty of. Always bodacious. The next thing that he looks for is the unbelievable sense of inspiration he puts into these people. The little children with their action figures in hand, the smiles on their faces, and the never ending rush of dreams running through their minds. Tommy knows that when these children watch him they hope, to one day, be just like him. They want the entire package deal that comes with being the most bodacious dude on the planet. The look, the skill, and the charm. They want the glory, money, and babes. It's at that moment he looks to the men of the crowd and their eyes tell him something totally different. They are completely, and utterly, jealous of what he is and what he's done. These men can't handle the simple fact that Tommy has gone out, everyday, and worked his hardest. He took the gifts he was blessed with and he ran with them the best he could. Unfortunately for these jealous compadres they will never experience that as most do not want to try and succeed. They only want to sit around and hope that it comes to them. Men like Venneman Pendragon just wish that things like the PCW World Championship will come to him, but it won't. Nothing that great just comes to a person especially when the man holding the title is named 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, an ultimate inspiration] [Tommy slowly walks toward the table that has been set up at the front of the convention center. He takes a seat into the steel chair and looks down at the piles of pictures. He's most upset about the pose he's doing in the pictures. He's looking seductively into the camera with his right index and thumb on his chin. He's beginning to feel that it's some kind of hopeless cliche that he's doing, like some kind of cheesy fashion model would do in a cologne ad. As he thinks about that cologne ad thought he remembers that he did the same exact pose in his Tubular cologne ad. So, technically, he just ripped himself off. Oh well. That still doesn't defeat the simple fact that he is absolutely hating the pose he's doing. He can't go hours of signing a picture that disgusting, these people deserve to see him better than that. He'd be robbing from them if he didn't have a much better pose. Tommy turns to one of the PCW officials, dressed in a shaggy looking suit] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I can't sign these, dude. Official: Why? Tommy: Look at this pose, dude! It's totally heinous. Official: This is the exact pose that you asked for earlier this morning. We just ran this things off at the local Kinko's! Tommy: Well, Kinko's totally ripped you off. You should go back there and get one of those gnarly refunds. [The PCW official can only sigh deeply, trying to figure out what to do. The day before Tommy called from a local Denny's restaurant and demanded the pose for his autograph signing. He knew it would be coming so he was ready for the forceful demand. He ran off extra copies just in case he wasn't satisfied with the number of copies. Now, at the moment of truth, the surfer boy is declining to want to use those photos. This is something that he did not prepare for and he damns himself for not thinking of it in the first place. He looks into those stupid looking blue eyes and he can see the arrogance just pouring out of him, like manure from a cow. It makes him sick, in fact he thinks he'll go to the bathroom in a few minutes to bring back up his McDonald's lunch] Official: There are almost a thousand people here to get your autograph and with Spring Fling only a few days away this is a must. If you would have read your contract in detail you are required to do this, especially as PCW Champion. Tommy: And I'm here and ready to do this thing. It takes two to tango my most balding compadre and if you don't have the bodacious photos I need then I can't sign. Official: We have them right here! These are the photos that you asked for! You were at Denny's when you told us to use that pose! Are all those punches to the head from Pendragon finally catching up to you or is it that your just that stupid?! Tommy: Dude, now your really digging deep with the knife, aren't you? Well, you can insult me but it doesn't change the fact that these pictures are totally bogus and that I'm much bigger than you. Now you either get me the required pictures that I need or I'm getting back into my limo to go back to that bodacious naked babe at my hotel. Official: You don't seem to get it, Tommy. It's in your contract that you have to sit here and sign autographs no matter what the pictures are. You told us to put these stupid looking ones here instead of the regular PCW ones that we have for each wrestler. We would have had those ones as back up but you ordered me to have them burned to a crisp. [Tommy chuckles to himself, going back to the Denny's breakfast that he was enjoying at the time. He was just having a good time at the expense of being the champion, throwing his weight around more than usual. The guy was bugging him about those heinous pictures so he just started spurting things out, he didn't expect the dude to go and actually do it. Well, if a dude is willing to burn some pictures then he's willing to take Tommy getting up and leaving] Tommy: That was most bodacious when I told you that. So, since you burned the back up's like a heinous compadre you can explain to my 'adoring' fans that I'm out of here. [Tommy gets up from the chair and walks back through the double doors which he came from. The crowd is sort of confused at this point as the bald PCW official stands nervously at the table checking out the crowd. Some of these people have waited for hours to get their spot and those will be the ones who rush the table. By the time they rush the table Tommy will be long gone] Official: We seem to be having a slight problem with scheduling as Tommy is supposed to make an appearance some place else right now. Random Fan: WHAT?! That's gay! Official: I'm sorry sir but these things happen from time to time. The scheduling department gets really messed up, especially when titles change hands and everything. Spring Fling is right around the corner and the stress is much highered. Random Fan: It's still gay! Official: We are so sorry for the problem. We will be passing out free Spring Fling T-shirts to all of you. That's the best we can do at the moment, but we do honor your dedication to the company. Random Fan: Tommy is gay!! [The crowd cheers in agreement as the official nods his head] Official: That's right. Tommy is a fruit cake. He could have stayed here to sign your autographs and just showed up late at the other event, but he refused to. I think that he referred to you people as cattle. Just ready to go to the slaughter, especially the women. [The crowd gets angry and most of them begin to run toward the back of the convention center. They know where the limos go during events like these and they are ready to strike. The official looks on with a satisfying smile as the huge crowd works like the cattle that they are and goes toward the real problem. He's proud at what he's done for the moment, because he's sure that he'll hear about this situation later in the day. Probably lose his job and be out on the streets with nothing but the shirt on his back. That doesn't matter now because he wears the smile on his face] Official: Ok, I'm gonna go throw up now. [Tommy sits in the back of the limo with his brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. The two have been bonding big time lately, reliving the past as the Cowabunga Connection. Both men have been discovering the link between their tag team legacy and the large success that Tommy has been coming to now. They may have been best of friends entering PCW, later finding out to have been brothers, but they were not tag team partners. They were thrown together in hopes of reinventing the tag team division of PCW. It worked for a short time but the two men stayed with it during the downtimes and helped roar it into the high times. When Harry was hurt it was like fate came down and said, 'Let there be Tommy'. After that The Tubular One rose to the occassion and captured the PCW Continental Title. It's as if he truly wanted that all along and didn't waste any time showing it. Both men have an understanding that they know the paths that they are known to have. Harry: To support and manage his older brother to the glory that he deserves. Tommy: To become one of the best wrestlers in the world and to totally dominate, eventually retiring as PCW World Champion. The men just only need to look one another into the eyes and realize this understanding, but it's hard to do that at this moment as the limo is rocking back and forth] Tommy: Dude, what's going on? Limo Driver: Well, there are hundreds of angry people trying to tip us over. Tommy: Bogus. Limo Driver: Totally. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [The car begins making its way a bit off of it's wheels. Tommy and Harry and extremely cool in the backseat just watching the people's angry faces. Tommy smiles to himself and seems a bit proud of what's going on. The people are angry because he walked out of the autograph session. Honestly, that is the most large sign of flattery he can have right now. These people boo him out of the building, even as the champion, but when he won't sign their autographs they get angry. So, obviously, they care about him enough to try to kill him. Now that's kind of bogus] Tommy: Dude, step on it! Harry: Yeah, man. Limo Driver: I'll run people over. I'd be committing murder! Tommy; I'll get you a bodacious lawyer. Limo Driver: Well....ok. [The driver slams the gas down and the limo lunges forward. Many people leap out of the way of the on coming car and then just as fast as they started hitting it...the limo is gone. The people are perplexed at what has just happened and they begin to go back about their lives like none of it had happened] Fade to black
