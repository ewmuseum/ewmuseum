Still a Wuss

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/03/02 Love, gold, respect: that's all any man can ever ask for, and it seems reasonable that they would want it. Love gives you a happy life and a reason to get up every waking morning. Gold instills in you the feeling of much power and superiority. Respect let's you know that you have done something right, and that people look up to you in one form or another. Add those together and you are living a fantasy. Just the mere thought of obtaining those three things make you want to work for it. One man is on that journey to happiness. He has a chance at all three but hasn't managed either quite yet. That man is none other than........Kramer Wild Wing! The scene opens up to the soft ballads of a country music star. It plays for several seconds as the man bellows out his sorrows in a smooth, soft tone. That is overtaken however by a bunch of static and finally a blaring of electric guitars and drums blasts into our ears. The noise rises as the guitar rifts get longer and longer. The blackness is slowly removed as light takes it's place. The camera pans around at it's surroundings, to reveal that we are in a living room, Kramer's living room to be exact. As the guitars are taken over by a screaming vocalist, Kramer comes in from the hall sliding along the wooden panel flooring. His socks glide over it with ease causing him to almost crash into the corner of the wall, as he has a bit of problem with stopping. After managing to stop he leaps up in the air and plays on his air guitar strumming along to the music. Kramer makes a sorry attempt to sing, but quickly stops realizing that it's not really doing him justice. He continues on with this unusual way of acting, not stopping the slightest bit. That is until an unexpected visitor comes strolling in through the front door. Kramer doesn't hear this person enter, so he just continues jumping around in the air like a madman. An arm reaches out as he is jumping and touches him on the shoulder which scares the living daylights out of him. Kramer falls to the floor with a look of shock and utter horror on his face as he turns towards the person. Jason: Hey Kramer. Kramer: Jason!!! You scared the crap right out of me! Jason: I honestly couldn't resist. You were just sitting prey and I had to strike. Kramer: Well do it again and I'll strike you hard across the face! Kramer stands to his feet and rubs the side of his leg which is covered up by his black shorts. Jason just stands there and chuckles a bit. Kramer: I haven't seen you for a while you know. Speaking of which, where in the world did you go? It's like you dissapeared off the face of the earth or something. Jason: I took a break. To make a long story short, depression was setting in due to the fact that things were slowing down for us as partners. There was absolutely no point in me going out there root you on when in the end it was for nothing anyway. Kramer: Then why did you come back? Jason: All I needed was a tiny break. Kramer looks at Jason who has a cheesy grin on his face. Kramer: Bull! You only came back so soon because you heard that I have a chance at the TV title on Turmoil. You want to be there in the limelight when I win it. A look of shock crosses over Jason's face. Jason: Kramer Wild Wing believing in himself? That's a first. Kramer: Damn, I forgot to add if I win. Jason: Oh.....Well anyway I am here to give you the extra support you need to beat Syko. I have mobs, gang members, jail birds, and rabid dogs ready to go at him if you want him 'softened' up a bit before your match. Kramer gives Jason the ol' raised eyebrow. Kramer: As tempting as that sounds, I'm gonna pass. Jason: You don't have to say yes now but if you want later..... Kramer: I said no! If I lose I lose. I would rather lose then get an unfair win. Jason: Sometimes I wish I could swish around that messed up brain of yours! When will you learn that nice guys finish last, and good guys always get their asses kicked because the bad guy cheats. Kramer: Your can say whatever you want but I won't sink to your level. Jason: Sooner or later I will drive you to the dark side, muhahahaha!! The scene goes dead silent. So silent in fact, that we can hear a band of crickets in the background. This continues for about a minute before everything picks back up. Kramer: I know you just got in here and all but I have somewhere to be. Jason: No you don't. You just want to kick me out so you can continue to play with yourself, or whatever it was you were doing. Kramer: I wasn't playing with myself!!! I was drumming away on an air guitar. Jason: Drumming on a guitar? It doesn't make much sense does it? Kramer: Not really. Can you guitar on a drum? Jason: You do not realize how stupid you just sounded! Kramer cuts out of deep thought with a look of embarassment on his face. Kramer: Didn't I tell you to go? Jason: I'm going, I'm going. I'll be back in a few hours so we can catch the plane to Turmoil. Jason walks through the door and outside as Kramer walks over towards the phone. He picks it up and then sets it back down after a few moments of contemplation. He repeats this three times, until he finally keeps it in his hands. His big brown eyes peer down at the numbers, and his shaky index finger reaches out and starts dialing. There are two rings until the person on the other end picks up. Woman: Hello? Kramer: I uh...ummm..... Before he ever gets a real word out he quickly hangs up the phone and steps away from it as though it has a disease. He is still shaking, but it has moved throughout his entire body. He pounds himself hard in the head before walking back over towards the radio and turning it back on full blast. The scene slowly fades to black. After watching how hesitant Kramer was, we knew that the person he had attempted to call was Nancy. Ever since he really grasped the fact that he has feelings for her, he can never seem to get in contact with her without freezing up. After hitting her dog a few days ago, she hasn't really been fond of him. I guess maybe it's a bad time for Kramer to realize that she has caller ID? Along with that he has the title match up on Turmoil, and another match on Fusion which may or may not be an important one to win. So with all of this weight on his shoulders, you kind of wonder why he hasn't fallen apart......
