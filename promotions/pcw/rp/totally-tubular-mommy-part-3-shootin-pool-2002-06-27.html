Totally Tubular Mommy - Part 3 - Shootin' Pool

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27 [The scene opens on a billiard ball spinning around. The ball is black except a small white circle in the middle with the number '8' in the center of it. The green felt underneath the ball only fuels the spin that the ball has been cast under, making it so hypnotizing to any nearby eye that happens to be witnessing this. With each passing second the ball begins slowing down it's high rotation and then suddenly another ball, with a yellow stripe across the middle smacks the rotating '8' ball from view] [Mr. Pockets Pool and Pub is located near downtown Los Angeles. Harry always says, 'It's that pool place in the gathering of stores, man' and Tommy will reply while shaking his head with, 'It's what we call a mini-mall, compadre.' Harry will then sit silently for a few moments as if banking it in his memory like some sort of human computer and then he'll giggle like a little school girl. Then two days later this same conversation will commence, which leaves Tommy bewildered each and everytime. BUT!! Mr. Pockets has served the most bodacious tag team in all of wrestling very well. This pool hall/bar consists of well over thirty pool tables for pool playing pleasure, and a full sized bar in the front with every drink imaginable. The atmosphere is set pleasantly with a sort of Hawaiian background with the ocean painted on the walls, Hawaiian girls doing hula dances, and palm trees gently blowing in the wind] [A very handsome, dark skinned young man is leaning over with a bright smile on his face. His dark black hair is spiked up in the front as the rest of his buzz cut-like hair is pushed forward. His large, toned arms bulge through his tight silk tribal symbol shirt. The scene moves down to show Tommy's mom, wearing a very tight pink belly shirt, tight blue jeans to accent her perfectly sculpted gluteus maximus, and black boots. Her breasts hang down as she is leaned over the pool table with the Chippendale-like man leaning against her back, trying to guide her through her next shot] Chippendale Guy: Ok, babe, your really FEELING great with this shot. I can almost feel it COMING!! [Tommy's mom smiles moving the pool cue back and forth toward the ball she is going to hit] Tommy's mom: Hmmm, Bill...you know I love it when you do that. [The scene zooms out to show 'Heavy Metal' Harry and 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, the Cowabunga Connection, sitting on the opposite side of the pool table on bar chairs. Tommy, in his PCW T-shirt, khaki shorts, and black sandals, holds his pool cue as if it was a samurai sword, ready to bring it down on this beefcake guy named Bill. Harry, in his usual Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots, has a blank expression on his face. He holds a cube of that blue cue chalk in between his thumb and index finger, just blankly looking at it. He then looks over at the slowly homoicidal Tommy] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Do you think it's safe to eat the chalk, man? [Tommy doesn't hear Harry's stupid question as he stares angrily at this...'BILL!' that is leaning over his mother like some sort of sexual leech. This bogus situation must be dealt with Tubularly and not end up in a heinous scuffle that gets them kicked out for good, because Mr. Pockets is totally gnarly in a far out way!] Bill: All you have to do is stroke it gently, don't be too rough because you don't want to smack the balls too hard. Gentle stroke. The balls will sway to the hole and then the cue can be sunk in the holster to begin another shot. Tommy's mom: It's so hard!!! Bill: I know, but you have to trust it. It's not going to bite...only you can. Tommy's mom: I suck. Bill: Yes, you do but we're taking care of that. [Tommy's whole body is beginning to go into small spasms as he begins taking this lesson in pool an entirely different way. He sees Bill's hands on his mother's chest, supposedly guiding her arms but obviously he's guiding something else. Bill's other hand 'happens' to be placed on his mother's butt] Harry: Remember that time I ate chalk in freshman year, man? That was far out! [Tommy then leaps up from his bar chair and smashes his pool cue over Bill's head. The beefcake, ladies man collapses the the hard ground like a sack of potatoes. Tommy stands on the otherside of the pool table breathing heavily as his mother looks up with a total look of shock on her face. Both look down to see that Bill lies unconcious like the little punk that Tommy knew he was in the first place] Tommy's mom: Why did you do that?! [Tommy looks up to his mom] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: He was making a most bogus move on you, mom! Tommy's mom: He was just showing me how to play pool!! TTT: With his hand on your breast and ass? [She then is silent and seems a bit uneasy about that, knowing exactly what was going on] Tommy's mom: Well... TTT: DAMNIT, MOM!! YOUR TOTALLY BOGUS! [Tommy's mom slams her fist against the pool table as many of the other pool patrons begin looking on with quite an interest] Tommy's mom: You know what is 'bogus'!! It's the sorry excuse that you call a tag team!! Your a shell of a man now, just like your father was before he died. He was so focused on becoming the champion, so focused on being number one that he forgot what he was truly like! Harry's dad just went along for the ride, taking hits of acid everytime things got crazy! You two are going to find out on Fusion and at Gold and Glory just what I'm talking about. TTT: MOM!! How dare you spread your bogusness on me and Harry like that!! We are the most bodacious team in PCW and we will win the tag titles at Gold and Glory and I will win that four way match on Fusion. Just you watch. Dad might have been focused on the right thing because maybe you were too busy getting most freaky with beefcake compadres!!! [Tommy's mom slaps him across the face] Tommy's mom: You NEVER talk to your mother like that! Harry: Yeah, man!! [Tommy's mom slaps Harry across the face] Tommy's mom: Don't you agree with me!! [Tommy now slams his fist down on the pool table] TTT: This is TOTALLY HEINOUS, dudette!! Your telling me not to talk like that to you?! What about the way you always acted in front of me!! You brought guys like Beefcake Jones around all the time, rubbing up against them like someone rubs a lamp hoping for a most bodacious genie to come out!! You would talk about the most nasty things that mothers shouldn't tell their sons. I don't want to hear about your sex life because I don't tell you about mine! So you don't talk to me like that!! Tommy's mom: I was a young woman and I had needs!! I still have needs now that I'm going into my early forties. I need to make sure that I'm surrounded by men, because at least those men appreciate me, unlike my own son who doesn't give a damn about his mother! [Tommy seems to be getting horribly upset as he looks into his mother's eyes with pure hatred behind his baby blues] TTT: I never gave a DAMN?!?! That's a totally bogus statement if I ever heard one. I always tried to show you how bodacious and gnarly I was with my surfing. You just smiled, waved, and then went back to your men. Your like a man-o-holic or something, it's totally bogus in a most heinous way! Tommy's mom: I never ignored you!! I supported you in everything that you did. TTT: With a wave and a smile?! Tommy's mom: I did more than that!! [Tommy rolls his eyes now] TTT: Yeah, there was one time were you actually clapped your hands! [Tommy's mom screams loudly as tears stream down her face] Tommy's mom: I'M OUT OF HERE!! TTT: GOOD!! [She storms past the growing crowd of on-lookers as Tommy breathes heavily, feeling all those years of anger and hatred slowly excrete from his body. He did the right thing by confronting his mother, telling her how he truly felt about the way he was treated as a child. He didn't have a father figure to help him mold, but only a slutty mother who treated Tommy as if he were some sort of cheap mistake] Harry: I'm eating this chalk, man. There is nothing you can do to stop me. [Harry tosses the cue stick chalk into has mouth and lets it sit in his mouth for a few moments, letting the taste kick in. He immediately spits it out, smacking Bill the beefcake in the eye with it] Bill: OUCH!! Harry: FAR OUT, MAN! [The scene fades to black]
