Don't Mess With The Monkey (part 3)

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 The scene opens up for the last time at Kramer's house. He has just got done packing for the trip to St. Louis, Missouri where March Madness it being held. His two suitcases are sitting by the door ready to be picked up, and Kramer has changed into a nice business suit (which was of course suggested by Jason). Binky is sitting on top of Kramer's shoulder as they both await for the arrival of the limosuine and Jason Alexander. Kramer: Now remember when we get to the airport just act cool, and try not to move around as much. It won't make since if I am claiming you to be my stuffed toy and you start bouncing all over the place right? Binky shakes his head. As he does Kramer sticks his hand up and Binky gives him a high five. Kramer: Now I am not sure how Jason will react but we don't really care. All I have to do is explain the situation. Suddenly the doorbell rings and Kramer walks to answer it. There is Jason, who too is wearing the black business suit just like Kramer. As soon as Jason comes in he looks suprised. Jason: You do have Binky! You weren't lying? Kramer: Huh? Jason: I saw you on the news just a few hours ago. They said you had claimed you saw Binky, and they couldn't find him here so they didn't believe you. But you do have the monkey! Kramer do you realize how much money they are giving for his return? Kramer: Hey ain't nobody taking Binky. Besides he doesn't want to go back there. When that Alfred guy came by Binky hid away and when he came out he seemed scared even though everyone had left. I think there is seriously something wrong with that circus. Jason: Kramer you can't be serious! Do you realize how much trouble you will be in if they find that you have him and aren't willing to give him back? Kramer: Listen, can we not talk about this right now? We really need to get to the airport. It would look really bad if I don't make it to March Madness on time don't you think? Jason: Oh I guess you're right. Kramer grabs his bags and goes to walk out of the door with Binky still on his shoulder. Jason: You are bringing the monkey? Kramer: Yeah, why not? I don't want to leave him here by himself. Jason: Get real for just a moment will you? How are you going to get him on the plane. Kramer: You are looking at my stuffed toy that I just can't part. Kramer flashes a cheesy smile as he walks out to the limo. The limo driver takes his bags and places them in the trunk as he climbs in. Jason follows behind and sits on the opposite side of Kramer and shuts the door. Jason: Did you hear about the two percent rising in the KFC stocks? Kramer: No can't say I did. Jason: My resources say it was due to your new commercial for wild wings. The people really loved the commercial with you fighting the bearded lady in the mud so much that they went out and bought wild wings. Kramer: Oh cool. It was fun making that commercial too, but when she slipped and fell on top of me I thought I was a goner! She weighed like four hundred pounds! Jason watches nervously as Binky jumps over to his side scoping him out. Jason: So are you ready for your big match? (he says while wondering what Binky is doing, or better yet what he is going to do) Kramer: Oh....well I figure its either going to be me winning or hopefully that Anton kid. Jason: Yeah his youth could play as a major factor. Then again Ganker and Red Rock aren't that old either....but Anton looks like he has the most potential. But I don't want to hear you already signalling yourself out like that. Its not good. Kramer: Hey I am just stating that I might not win. Jason: You could if you try hard enough. Besides I will be at ringside with the might bucket of chicken... Kramer: I don't even think the chicken can help me out this time. But I promise I will try my hardest to pull out with the win. It would surely feel good to be that one step closer to a belt again. So yeah I will work my bum to the bone. Besides Binky will be there to cheer me on as well, won't you? Binky nods his head yes as he leaps back over and sits next to Kramer. Jason: You aren't growing attached to that monkey are you? Kramer: So what if I am? Jason: Kramer you aren't going to be able to keep him! Kramer: Sure I can. Instead of arguing Jason just keeps quiet. For the rest of the half hour long trip there is nothing else said. The scene fades again as they finally get to the airport. Like always with Kramer something has got his attention and its not his match. It obviously doesn't bother him that this match is extremely important. It doesn't matter to him that he could make the break right here and move on to some good things. It doesn't matter that he has a chance to impress the millions at home and thousands in attendance. Thats just Kramer for you though. The only thing he has said about his match really is that he hopes either Anton or he wins. So he has ruled out Ganker and Red Rock.
