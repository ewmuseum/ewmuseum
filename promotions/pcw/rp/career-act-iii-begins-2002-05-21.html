CAREER: ACT III BEGINS.

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-21-02 Song Listening Guide: 'Why Go' by Pearl Jam. (Listening to 'Black' while you RP is real good inspiration! ;-P) BOBBY S. CAMPBELL CAREER: ACT III BEGINS. Then.... (4-20-02) 'Um. Bobby? Something wrong?' 'No. Nothing... I was just thinking... thinking that this is the right moment. This is the right day. Not just thinking.. I know- yeah. Yeah, I know it is. I know yer a bit young, but...' 'Hee hee, babe? You aren't makin' much sense, I gotta tel-' Kira's words turn to a gasp as right before her very eyes, Bobby Campbell slips out from under her leg and drops to one knee on the wooden paneled ground. He fishes around in one of his pajama pockets and fishes out a small and plush black box, letting it rest in his open right palm. He holds it out to Kira and smiles very pleasantly, not in the smartass way that Bobby would usually smile. Kira holds both her hands over her mouth in total shock. 'Kira. I've done this once before. With someone pretty famous, actually, but... you're the only natural redhead for me. No really. I mean it!' A couple of teary chuckles peek their way through Kira's defenses as Bobby winks up to his dream girl. They are tears of complete joy. For what could be the final time in PCW history, the camera shot zooms in on the calm and adoring face of Mr. Prime Time. His face with rigid features that still maintain a boyish quality. All of his red hair falling back and away from his eyes since he is looking upward. No shades. Just a goatee and dark blue eyes that speak of only two things every second of every single day. The Perfect Storm lurking inside and a burning love that is so very much easier to see. He opens the case to reveal the diamond and emerald ring inside. There is only one thing left to ask: 'Kira. Simply put, I really do love the hell out of you. Will you do me the honor... of becoming my wife?' And now.... * * * --The third week of May, 2002-- The more things change. The more they stay the same. As of exactly one month ago, things were very different for Robert Stormbringer Campbell than they are right now, at least in most ways. They were much more complex, to be sure. First of all, there was his identical twin brother, Randy. A cold, spiteful, jealous, heartless bastard if there ever was one. Back in early March, exactly two months ago, Randy hijacked the very life of Bobby, 'safely' stashing the once successful Primetime Championship Wrestling superstar in one of his many warehouses in Tampa, Florida! This after knocking him clean out with a rare painting. Bobby was unknowingly hidden hours away from their hometown of Orlando. Eventually Randy set into motion the signing of his brother's out clause in his PCW contract. Randy knew that few things could hurt Bobby more than having his Pro Wrestling dream taken away. What a jackass, eh? However, to the demented brother's credit... Randy fooled you all. He had their newly discovered half-sister, Amy 'Aimz' Campbell convinced. He had all of the wrestling fans convinced. He had Bobby's personal assistant Carl Everstrong convinced. He had PCW management convinced. He had all of Dark Age convinced, even such a longtime rival of Bobby's as The End, Skylar Green. He had OSW Owner Jane Reagan convinced of it. He convinced Bobby's lover and now fiansce', Kira York, that he was his very own twin. The man has no conscience to speak of, or he at least keeps it well hidden. Randy even took over Charlie Loc and Bobby Stormbringer's plans of bringing Mona's Syndicate over to PCW, as well as already being in NTICW. He learned through ransacking Bobby's emails about the plans and just faked his way through conversations with Loc. But he never quite learned why Bobby would side with two people he supposedly despised so much in Charlie Loc and Mona. It could be assumed that Randy tried to psychoanalyze his brother's motives. The only reasonable explanation he could come up with is that Bobby simply wanted revenge on Jeremy Howard, Miyagawa, Darkstar, and Xavier Kannon for the disaster that turned out to be the end of Asylum. Even though it originally wasn't in the plans to hurt anyone as much as Randy did.... The well-concieved and drawn out process would come to a head at PCW Spring Fling, back in April. Virtually destroying ex-Asylum members Darkstar and Xavier Kannon in the process, for an added effect. It is totally unknown why Miya and Krayzie were left out of the ambush. Perhaps even Randy realized that that would be a bit too out of line. Granted, the stable was formed and victims laid at Randy's feet. In his mind? It made for a great Pay-Per-View moment. Mission accomplished! But for once, giddy and arrogant thanks to 'victories' over brother Bobby, Kannon, and also Darkstar, Randy did not meticulously plan out the future like he usually did. It would end up being the biggest mistake Randy Stormbringer has made in nearly a decade. A simple trick just two nights later that Bobby would quite frankly never fall for. Mona's lipstick on a note. Bobby, unlike Randy is oddly faithful to the woman he loves. This is Randy we speak of, however. 'Bobby Stormbringer' made his way to a room, probably expecting to get laid. Not! Instead, Randy was crucified, beaten, bloodied, burned, and virtually left for dead. In the real Bobby's later opinion, Randy deserved this and so much more. Unfortunately for Randy, he was not alone in this. The girlfriend wasn't spared. Trust that Kira has no twin, and the one and only Bobby Stormbringer's girlfriend was bladed and hung. An innocent little lady that simply wanted to come on the road, on the wrestling tour with the man she loved. Randy got what he asked for. Kira, however, did not. Randy may be a sick son of a bitch, but he has one thing going for him. Guts. He still managed to come back. After all the injuries, including his old football shoulder injury. He did manage to wrestle a couple of times more. All he got for the trouble was a nasty, ring-laden bitch slap from Mona and a terribly fractured leg at the hands of Dark Age. He is only human after all. Randy... and therefore, 'Bobby Stormbringer'... hasn't been seen since. At least not on PCW television. Goes to show you that sometimes being rich and powerful won't even help you get out of dangerous situations. Bobby, in a harrowing but untaped experience, freed himself from Randy's bodyguards and cronies in Tampa after four weeks of captivity. He did this only a few days after Randy and Kira were attacked, ironically enough. So after all of Randy's hideous mental and physical attacks against his brother, the most elaborate trick of all finally backfired right in his face. The biggest travesty of all may be the complete loss of Kira's beautiful innocence... but without innocence comes the truth. Without innocence comes a stronger person. It may come in the harshest of ways, true. But without the ashes, how could a phoenix rise from them? Think about it and wait... just wait. Perhaps in several years we will look back on that moment as more than a simple tragedy. Only time will tell. As for the real 'B.S.' himself, he had a chance to save his own flesh and blood on the night of April the nineteenth, or perhaps to do the damage himself. He was halfway to the Fusion arena, ready for the all out war zone that would ensue, when suddenly..! He stopped. He pulled the rent-a-car over and realized in that one moment that whatever was going to happen... would happen. The actual Bobby Stormbringer also realized that the use of his character, that of his PCW persona had gotten out of control. He knew that in that 'Stormbringer versus Darkstar' match Randy would come up on the short end of the stick. Severely. It was then that a wide smirk played across his face. Bobby realized, most importantly, that 'The Wheel Of Fate' was playing against both he and his twin. It was high time just to go with the flow of it all. It didn't much matter to him, nor to Kira, what people thought actually happened to Bobby. They could believe that he was burned... that his leg was broken. That his stable self-destructed. Whatever they wanted was fine with them. When and if Bobby finally retuned, Randy would finally be out of his hair, at least for several months to come. Then all the naysayers could see with their own eyes an unburned man. A completely healed man? A miracle!? Hardly. Instead, Bobby reasoned that it was the perfect time for the more subtle things in life. No less than 'Bobby Stormbringer's' first full day as a wrestling free agent than he asked the light of his life to marry him. No one knew or saw this, but... Kira said yes. Duh! So they ran off to some remote (and rather secret) part of the Carribean and didn't get married. Kira convinced 'The Showstopper' that the right thing to do was to wait until their family and friends could be there to see it. It didn't have to be right away, a slip of paper would never change how the two young lovers felt for one another. No, they ran off just to do that. To run away, to get away. From everyone and everything. To basically celebrate their engagement and just decompress and relax. That was nice... but by the time the first of May rolled around, Bobby became restless. Now legally Bobby Campbell, to share his real father's last name, there was still much to do, even in quiet and peaceful times. Bobby, by the way, kept his last name as his middle name. Stormbringer. After all, if it was anything different than that then how would Bobby sell all those 'B.S.' t-shirts!? Exactly. So for the past three weeks the final student of Triple H has, in a sense, graduated himself to a new level. Now Bobby Campbell is the mentor. And Kira York is the student. 'WHAT!?' you may be asking. Understandable. You'd be a sexist, but nevertheless that gut reaction is understandable. So many questions. Questions that only a select few people truly do know the answers to: How did this 'mentoring' arrangement come to pass and who convinced who? When and where, exactly, will this wedding of theirs take place? It has been revealed that Bobby Stormbringer has been accepted onto the brand new PCW roster. Why did Bobby Campbell go back to PCW when he so loathed the political climate there? Considering how 'overpaid' he was before by Jane Reagan, what are the details of his contract this time? How will B.S. respond to four of the five 'Dark Age Vol. 4' members not being in the new PCW, at least as of right now? Are his motives for returning to PCW to finally gain a freggin' Championship Title or two? Or just revenge? Will Bobby's strange and supernatural power, 'The Perfect Storm' as he calls it, ever rear its ugly head again? Since he is in PCW again, will he ever find out one or both of the remaining mystery men responsible for attacking B.S. with Randy at their side when the year was still new, or is it simply too late? Will Randy ever intrude on his life down the road? Or will Bobby get vengeance on Randy while he is in a weakened state, perhaps? Finally, what about the strange twists and turns in his little sister Amy Campbell's life in the past couple of months? Will Bobby help Aimz like he wishes he could?? Or will the gap between them that PCW and OSW's rivalry has inevitably created keep the long lost siblings at arms reach from each other!? *phew* We can at least answer one of those questions for you, thankfully. Let 'The Perfect Storm Chronicles' begin. No really. I mean it.... THE B.S. IS BACK. THIS HAS BEEN A B.O.T.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
