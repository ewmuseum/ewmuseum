Heed the call.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (BRIIIIIING!) “Hello?” (SMACK! The phone receiver goes flying from Steve’s hand by the force of THE Multiple Man’s blow!) Steve: “What are you doing?!” Tmm: “Saving your life, that’s what I’m doing.” (Click! Tmm places the handset back onto its cradle, severing the connection.) Tmm: “You may not know it, but you were in grave danger just then. If you were lucky, it just might… MIGHT only kill you. More likely, it would puree your soul like it was a cheap milkshake. Don’t EVER answer the phone again, let the machine take it.” Steve: “Phones are NOT dangerous.” Tmm: “Whoever said anything about the phone being dangerous? The phone itself has nothing to do with it. A tool, a medium… nothing more.” (BRIIIING! Steve’s hand snakes out and snags the receiver.) Steve: “Hello? Uh-huh…” (SMACK! Again the receiver clatters to the floor.) Tmm: “Have you been listening to me? NEVER answer the phone! Let the machine take it! How can you be so foolishly cavalier with your life? With ALL of our lives?!” (Steve puts his hands on his hips and glares defiantly back at his employer.) Steve: “The worst PCW can do is fire us. I’m not THAT worried.” (Tmm’s brow furrows in puzzlement.) Tmm: “PCW? That was PCW?” Steve: “Both of them were. Something about a Tuesday match with Violence Jack. There was more, but SOME jackass hung up the phone on me.” (Tmm’s eyes narrow at Steve’s insolent tone.) Tmm: “You expect me to be sorry? Don’t be such a damned fool. Just because it was PCW this time doesn’t mean that it will be them NEXT time. Do NOT answer the phone. Let the machine take it.” Steve: “While we miss important calls. A brilliant plan. Really. It is. Just genius.” Tmm: “Idiot. If it is truly important, they will make sure that we know. There ARE other avenues of communication available.” Steve: “Cats got all of the carrier pigeons.” (Tmm shakes a fist in fury.) Tmm: “Curses! But was it cats… or something darker?” (BRRRIIINNNG!!! Again, Steve’s hand shoots out, but this time Tmm manages to pin it to the table before it is too late.) Tmm: “Will you be a slave to your social conditioning for all of your life?! Resist and live!” (BRRIIIING!!! The phone rings again. Cold sweat breaks out on Steve’s face. His entire body quivers with barely contained reflex.) Tmm: “Steady man! If man you be! Do not succumb!” (BRRRING!!! Steve’s trembling gets even worse. His limbs shake wildly.) Steve: “I CAN’T TAKE IT!!! LET ME ANSWER IT!!! I NEED TO!!!” Tmm: “No you don’t.” (A faint click from across the room.) Tmm: “See? It is done. You made it. The answering machine protects us better than a thousand armed guards!” (His lip curls in scorn.) Tmm: “And you would throw it away at the behest of a small buzzer. How sickening.” (Again, the answering machine clicks, one message fatter than it was before. Steve gasps in the throes of massive emotional release.) Steve: “Bah.” Tmm: “You mock me, because you do not understand. And for that, I forgive you. Come, let us check our newly acquired message.” (Tmm turns and strides across the room. Less than five paces away, the phone rings behind him! BRRIIIING!!!) Steve: “Hello?” (Instantly, Tmm wheels around and dives for the receiver!) Tmm: “NOOOOooooooo!!! (But he is too late. The phone speaks with an unearthly voice dripping with inhuman vileness…) Phone: “Are you satisfied with your current long-distance service?” (Steve’s eyes roll back in his head until only the whites show and he howls like a damned creature as his soul writhes in agony! Thus, another foolish mortal learns to his eternal misery that one must never, under ANY circumstances, EVER answer… the phone call of Cthulu. Camera fades out.)
