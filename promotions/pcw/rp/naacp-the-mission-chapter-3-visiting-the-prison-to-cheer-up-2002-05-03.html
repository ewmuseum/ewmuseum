NAACP- The mission [Chapter 3. Visiting The Prison To Cheer Up The People... Yeah Right.]

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/03/02 His arms tensed, as his hair was blowing in the wind. He wiped a bit of sweat off of his brow. He was dressed according to Jordan’s wishes, in a black suit with a black top hat. He constantly checked his watch as if he was afraid of something. The bags he carried in both of his hands were beginning to get to him. Sweat trickled down his forehead as he went into one of the many bags and pulled out a Discman. His Smashing Pumpkins CD was in there. He turned it on. It didn’t matter to him what song was playing, as long as he didn’t have to stand outside the large building that is the NAACP headquarters and hold those bags like some Taxi Driver. Jordan finally walked down the stairs and he reached Chester Charles, his brand new Lackey. Jordan dressed neatly, nice, baggy khaki shorts, a khaki colored New York Yankees Baseball cap, brown untied Lugz boots, and a large khaki-colored Platinum Fubu t-shirt. Jordan also wears dark sunglasses, which shield his eyes from the glowing sun. Chester begins the long trek towards the Jeep that Jordan owns. Jordan slowly walks towards his car when his cellular phone rings. “Hello?” “Hello, this is Jake Jackson, remember me Jordan?” Jake Jackson was at one time, Jordan’s only white friend in the world. Their professional wrestling business careers, and their personal lives are intertwined forever. About three years ago, Jordan wasn’t exactly wrestling. Though not much of it is mentioned in his official PCW biography, Jordan was involved in a Drive by shooting in downtown Toronto. Jordan wasn’t alone though, a couple of his good College buddies were involved as well, in fact, and they were considered the leaders of the group. It was over a barroom brawl that the men had been involved in earlier, they were all looking at 15- to life in Prison for Attempted Murder and aggravated assault. Jordan was still a student and was working as a busboy at a restaurant; he didn’t [At the time] make enough money to actually pay for a lawyer. Yet, there was a cocky young upstart lawyer that was looking for a case that would hopefully break open the floodgates for his business. He had just started his own firm with a friend of his Jackson and Johnson. And since the case was a big deal in Toronto, Jake decided to take on Jordan’s case… For free. After a two week long trial, Jordan was acquitted of all charges when it was proven that he had no idea that they were going to do a drive by shooting. In all actuality, Jake Jackson, being the sneaky bastard that he is, blackmailed one of Jordan’s friends into telling the judge and the jury that Jordan was an innocent person who just happened to be in the car at the time of the shooting. Needless to say, Jordan was very grateful to the man known as Jake Jackson, and as time progressed, the two men became good buddies. Besides being a lawyer, Jake Jackson had another dream that he usually kept to himself. Jake always wanted to be involved in professional wrestling. By this time, Jordan had just begun the grueling training for professional wrestling. Jake Jackson had a vast experience of watching wrestling tapes, and studying several different wrestling styles. After talking with Jordan for more than two months, it was decided that Jake Jackson would become the professional wrestling advisor to Jordan X Shabazz [Then known as Jordan Crazy]. The two men embarked on a spree of violence and madness that would light up the independent wrestling scene. Tables, ladders, chairs, staple guns, baseball bats, barbwire wrapped boards, they all became weapons at the disposal of Jordan X Shabazz and his then advisor, Jake Jackson. The two men were causing quite a ruckus, which got them banned from performing in several states. Jackson still ran his firm with his buddy David Johnson, but he didn’t go to the office as often as he did before. They both dreamed of the big time… And then finally… They got the call. The call came from the PCW. They weren’t exactly asking Jordan to join the PCW necessarily, but after Jake had sent in a compilation tape which showed Jordan in various brawls and wrestling matches, they wanted him to head to the PWA to refine his professional wrestling skills and prove that he was dedicated to making it in the business of professional wrestling. Jordan did his time in the PWA, and Jake was always right beside him, barking orders at him like Paul Heyman does with Brock Lesnar. When Jordan got promoted to the NTICW, Jake Jackson stuck around for a while, then after realizing that Jake could no longer do what he once could for Jordan, he left. He went back to Toronto, Ontario Canada and his fledging firm, which had grown from two lawyers, to 300 lawyers. Jordan moved on, but he didn’t stay in touch with Jake. The two men drifted apart and the regular Sunday phone calls in which they strategize wrestling styles and talked about sports, had ended. But now, out of the blue, Jake had called Jordan. “Of course I remember you man, we were good buddies for quite a while,” Jordan replied with a smile on his face. “What’s on your mind Jake?” “Good news Jordan,” Jake said breathing heavily through the phone. “I was thinking of returning as your advisor.” As Jake finished his sentence, Jordan’s face immediately lights up like a kid on Christmas morning. “You mean you’re going to come to the PCW and advise me?” Jordan asks while he enters the car. “Indeed that’s what I mean Jordan,” Jackson starts. “I’ll meet you at the Crisler Center in Ann Arbor, isn’t that where Sabotage is being held?” Jake asks. “Yea dog, when you get here holla at me through the phone.” “I will.” “Later.” “Peace.” CLICK! Jordan hangs up the phone and puts on the air conditioning in his car. Chester Charles begins the 3-hour drive to Jordan’s destination… The Tom Howard Correctional Facility in Waco, Texas. ************************************************ The scene reopens on a bus coming from Houston, Texas. The bus was hot and muggy. They had been shackled together by their hands and feet. The cuffs aren’t more than a few feet away from each other so they really can’t do much in forms of weapon making. The men on the bus are some of the most creative men in the history of the Tom Howard Correctional Facility. They use toothbrushes, paper, toilet seats, plastic forks and knives, ANYTHING they can get their hands on to hurt other people. It’s a dangerous place for a white man to be. Black males, 15% by Hispanics, and 10% by Caucasians populates 60% of the prison, and the rest were a mixture of Asians and Native Americans. The white people are the minority. Most of the blacks and Hispanics in the prison stick together, they pretty much run the white people. Whenever the white kids come into the prison, they are given something affectionately known as ‘The Talk’ by the prison veterans. ‘The Talk’ consists of three things, yelling, yelling, and YELLING! The prison veterans tell the batch of young white prisoners stories of how they dominate the white people, they tell them stories about how corrupt the prison guards are, and how white people get beat up. It’s just something they do to scare them into thinking, and in essence, KNOWING, who runs the prison. Normally these prisoners would be angry. They would be mad at the position they are in. But good news has come abound. Usually, whenever the prisoners have a good month, they get a prize. Sometimes they get to go to an empty movie theater to see the latest in movies. Sometimes they get to go bowling. But today is different. Today the prisoners get to have a guest speaker. A man whom they have grown to love thanks to the television screen. Jordan X Shabazz was the guest speaker for today. Little did the prison guards know, that Jordan X Shabazz wasn’t only going to be making a speech? But Jordan X Shabazz would be a part of a crime, that the city of Waco, would never forget… ********************** The scene reopens with Jordan X Shabazz getting out of his Jeep. Chester Charles was told by Jordan to stay in the Jeep and get ready for him to return. Chester, being Jordan’s white slave, agreed. Jordan quickly passed through the inspection at the front gate, and the metal detector. He got into the main prison area where the inmates would watch from their respective cells. Many of the skinheads in the prison decided to put their small televisions with the volume as loud as possible to disrupt Jordan X Shabazz and his speech. It didn’t bother anybody, because though they were subdued, six of the black prisoners were ready and jacked for what was about to happen. The facility was quite nice for the one of its size. It was a nice, cozy, little prison that held 500 inmates. It had televisions in every room, a large recreation area with Nintendo Game cube and X-Box’s, and Playstation2. They also had a DVD player, and a basketball court outside, a football field, a small squash room, a large cafeteria with great food, and a small store that sold candies and sodas to the inmates. The inmates were also being paid $2 a day for their cleaning chores and whatnot. As Jordan made his way to the makeshift podium in the middle of the recreation center he looked for the cell that housed his six buddies from Queens. They had all caught a case for armed robbery. The prison had relaxed rules, so if Jordan permitted, the inmates would be let out. Jordan stood atop the podium created with the cafeteria tables and a left over stand from Goodwill. Jordan brought his mouth close to the microphone. “Please allow the inmates out of their cells and into the recreation center,” Jordan said with confidence in his voice. As the sound of cells unlocking began, their plan kicked into gear. William Johnson, a prison guard at Tom Howard to 14 years, immediately left the area. He went to the outside of the prison where he set up a rented vehicle that the six inmates were to escape in. Jordan had already paid him $1,000 dollars cash for this. He would be the getaway driver for the first group of prison escapees. All the prisoners were out now. Jordan stood at the top of the podium, and used a remote control that he had requested to show the video he had prepared. The video began with a shot of four Los Angeles Police Department officers in their dark suits and ties. It was a shot from the Rodney King trial from ten years ago. As the volume raised, we could hear the jury foreman speaking. “The jury find the defendant, not guilty of the crime of…” The same thing, four times in a row. The six prisoners that were going to escape, Mark Case, Danny Peterson, Christopher Long, Morris Simpson, Jonathan Borders, and Donovan Leonard began to try and get the other prisoners riotous. They whispered words of hate towards the white guards in the prison. A shot flashes the screen of a drunken Rodney King being beaten by the police officers that we just acquitted of any wrongdoing in the case. Some people shout out inaudible words at the screen. The shot on the screen quickly changes to the riots that ensued after the verdicts were handed down. Hostile black folk running around grabbing things, destroying things, throwing bricks at cars driving by. Some of the prisoners cheer while Jonathan Borders, the man who thought of the breakout, begins to tell some people not involved with the breakout how cool it would be if it happened again. Jordan smiles at the screen as his shining jewel flashes across the screen. The four black men who attacked that white trucker. The smile on Jordan’s face is wide; he tries to conceal it just in case somebody was looking at him. They show the poor trucker being beaten, and hurt for no reason other than he was at the wrong place, at the wrong time… And he was white. Jordan looked at Jonathan and he nodded. The plan went into full effect. Jonathan, Donovan, Christopher, Morris, Mark, and Danny all stood up. They picked up the chairs they were sitting in, and they ran for the nearest guards. Soon enough, everybody joined the fray. The video stopped on the screen as Jordan ran towards the exit where he would lead three of the prisoners to his Jeep. The stakes were set high for Jordan; he risked being taken to prison by saving these guys from a long time in prison. But he had to do it, for they were his homies from back in the day. The alarm sounded as the officers began coming in droves. As the other inmates fought with the guards, Jonathan, Morris, Mark, Donovan, Dan, and Chris all slowly made their way to the doors where Jordan stood. As they came, Jordan began to run out and towards his car, as according to the plan. They had to make it look like Jordan was running for his life or else it would be known through the video cameras that Jordan was in on the escape. As Jordan ran towards his car, the six men following him split up. Jordan ran through the gate where there was supposed to be an officer guarding, instead he had went inside the prison to stop the ruckus inside of there. The men were still hot on Jordan’s pursuit, or so it seemed to the dumb, fat, ugly white cops. Three of them went off into the blind spot for the cameras, the place where the cameras couldn’t see, and the place where William Johnson had parked his car and lay in wait for the three prisoners. While Jordan slowed down a bit where the three inmates caught him. They made it look like they attacked Jordan. They shoved him into the backseat of the Jeep, and they shoved Chester Charles out of it. They began to drive with Jordan in their backseat. ***************************************[Hours later] It is now 11 PM. All six of the inmates got away scot-free. But Jordan was downtown with the FBI after being taken in for questioning. His ribs hurt like a bitch. He still experienced the cracked ribs that he had suffered at the hands of the Ultimate Authority on his first ever-wrestling victory win in the PCW. ************The questioning was finished, and Jordan was onboard a late night flight en route to Cleveland, Ohio, from where he would go to Oxford and get ready for the PCW’s Friday Fusion. He was tired as fuck, and glad as fuck too. He was lucky that the police didn’t see through his white lies. [The following is taken from Jordan X Shabazz’s laptop computer journal.] Date: May 2nd, 2002. Time: 11:45 PMLocation: Somewhere over some state. Heh. I’m a common criminal. A man who decided to help six old buddies of his. The planning took months, I had to keep it under wraps from everybody. Nobody knew that this was in planning. Those guys made sure everything went off without a hitch, alls I did was be the thing that got the boys out of their cells, and close to weapons. It was great, my boys are free, but for how long? I don’t know. Alls I know is that I answered those questions quickly, and believably. Now that that crap is out of the way, I can get to Oxford. Its rather late Thursday night, or almost Friday morning. I’m going to be reaching Cleveland in about an hour. I’m very excited, my first ever PCW singles match is against Tim Walden. The bastard who fired the very talented former two time Extreme Champion, Karl Leblanc. Walden may be too preoccupied with RUSH wrestling to see what he’s going up against. I do things to help all the black men in America, case in point, what happened earlier today. But I’ve got to do something on Friday that I’ve never done before. I’ve got to injure a white man, but not for myself, I’ve got to do it for every black man out there. And every Karl Leblanc fan out there. Walden made a mistake in firing him, even though the big dumb confused n***** was in the Ultimate Authority, black wrestlers aren’t something you see everyday, and whenever they get fired, it pisses me off as they diminish something almost diminished to the zenith. It’s time for Timothy Walden to pay for his sins, in front of the God of The Crunk, and in front of all my black brothers. It’s time for Jordan X Shabazz to show these crackas why it’s Ride with the Malice Crew, or Die in front of the Malice Crew…. In other words… RIDE OR DIE MUTHAF***AS. Fade out.
