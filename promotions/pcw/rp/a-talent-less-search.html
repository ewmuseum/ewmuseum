Date: 10.30.02(#4 FF, #1 TT) “Okay people, listen up and this won’t take long.” That’s the voice of Rush Mason, which surprisingly has been heard much more frequently now that his handler decided to stop wasting time and start working harder. However, Rush Mason talking to really anyone sounds like a recipe for trouble, of which there are many examples. “Bon Dieu…will you people just make a line? My eyes are blinded by the lack on intelligence in this room.” The scene begins to fade in from black, and we can see that Rush Mason is standing in front of a large group, wearing an Aéropostale sweatshirt with the sleeves rolled up, and a pair of baggy jeans, complete with the white Air Forces on his feet. He seems to be cursing under his breath, and then turns, sitting down at a table, above which hangs a sign that reads, “Assistant Try-Outs.” Rush: Okay, let’s go, plebian beings. I’d like to get through this as quickly as possible, and then maybe count it as community service for a tax deduction. The first entrant steps up, he looks like a strapping young man, and Rush reads off a paper, to no one in particular. Rush: Your name is….James Gallagher? And you’re currently in college at Oxford? The student nods and Rush rolls his eyes, as the young man takes a step forward, and then takes a step forward. Rush sighs, already feeling fed up with this process, and begins to ask questions off a sheet in a bored-sounding monotone. Rush: Why do you want to be my assistant? What’s your motivating factor? Are your mo… Suddenly, a huge grin comes across Rush’s face, and he releases in one of those evil genius moments that these people have come to answer his questions, and do anything that he requests of them to “test their will” or to just watch people do funny things. “Sorry, what was that question?” Rush rubs his hands together in perfect villain fashion, and then turns back to the man, looking innocent, and putting on a smile that one will only see from Rush when he is with whatever woman will accept his vileness or when he’s being cruel. Rush: Hmm…I was just going to ask whether you would consider wearing a giant monkey suit, following me and pretending that you’re a lively little orangutan? The student chuckles to himself, but then gulps in fear as he realizes that Rush is not joking at all. Rush, somehow, is still keeping a straight face, and the student replies very apprehensively to Rush. “Uhh….I guess I would, although it’s not really-” Rush quiets him as he sweeps his hand out in front of him, and replies happily in a tone so sickly sweet that you can almost see the fun that Rush is having. Rush: Well, do you think you could give me a small demonstration? I mean, there’s lots of people who can jump around like an orangutan, but I need a quality orangutan. The student sighs and then begins to hop around, hunched close to the ground as he makes high-screeching sounds, like “Ooh Ooh” and things of the like. Rush meanwhile, looks on, scratching his chin, until after a minute or so the student stops, his face now red, and panting rapidly. “So…*huff*….have I got….*huff*….the job?” Rush feigns like he is thinking very hard, continues to scratch at the stubble, and says in one of those voices where people tell you that your relatives are dead. The student’s face turns from a mild grin into utter blank emotionless disappointment as he hears Rush’s words. Rush: Well, if you were a good orangutan, you could’ve gone for three or four more minutes non-stop, so I’m going to have to decline. We’re just looking for someone with the right tools. The student is already walking out of the room though, cursing to himself about this taking away his dignity or some banter like that, but of course, what does Rush care? He’s having the time of his life. Rush then addresses the entire line, giving out more stupid instructions. Rush: Plebeians, listen up! This job is going to be harsh, harsher than anything you can imagine. So, basically, I’m going to weed out, to put it kindly….the pussies. Several people in the line chuckle at this, making the same mistake as young James did, and Rush immediately points out the few people who did, and starts off again in a sickly sweet voice. He addresses one particular youngish looking girl. Rush: You….think I was kidding? The girl’s smile fades a little, but she’s still grinning partly as she replies to Rush, who has been waiting for this moment ever since about the seventh grade. “Well…I mean, that’s the most ridiculous thing I’ve ever-” Rush cuts her off sharply with a sweep of his hand, yelling into her face at what seems to her like hundreds of decibels. His voice echoes as it loudly resonates off the walls, and now everyone’s attention is surely on the Voodoo Villain. Rush: BON DIEU, YOU’RE THE DULLEST PIECE OF FILTHY SCUM THAT HAS EVER WALKED THIS EARTH! The woman gulps once, and then, along with a large majority of the crowd, migrates quickly towards and out the large door of the hall as Rush smiles a smile of pure self-achievement. However, he furrows his brow at a masculine-looking man with muscles that are “guns,” so to speak. Rush: You’re still here? What are you, mute or just retarded? Rush, though, partly regrets this choice of words as the man steps over to him, looking very solemn, and quite angry, as well as almost Rush’s size. This time, it’s Rush’s turn to gulp, until he hears the voice of the man. “Ju want-a the assisting? I cou’ gib you lossa fun!” Rush blinks several times, and motions with his hand for the large security guard to carry the gay sounding man out the door, and Rush then smiles as he sees that the room is entirely cleared. Rush: My life hasn’t ever felt so fulfilling…I think I just ruined that days of over 50 people! The only thing better that I can think of is taunting homeless people by dropping food and stepping on it! Truly grotesque, is the ego of this man, but his moment is interrupted by a shrill sound, and Rush reaches into his pocket to pull out a cell phone, answering it in normal tone. Rush: Hello?….Yeah, I did the stupid try-outs….Huh? Need One?….I don’t give a damn about the demographic….*sigh* This idea sucks….Fine, I’ll find some one who isn’t totally sub-par, and maybe I’ll let them stand near me and do my bidding. Rush then slams the phone closed, and turns to the floor where the emptiness is now not looking so good in his new predicament. He mumbles to himself as he walks over to the door, kicking at the floor. Rush: Damn plebian agent says I need a manager because of the demographic’s liking to secondary characters. I swear… However, as Rush walks by the huge security guard once more, an idea goes off in his head, and he takes a step back, stepping back through the doorway, and turning to the security guard, who’s busy devouring a Twinkie. Rush: You! What can you do? The security guard continues to chomp on the Twinkie parts that are currently in his mouth, and then somehow replies through the cream-filled goodness in his mouth. “Nutin, yo. I just pimp hoes, and keep it real.” Rush shakes his head, but then thinks back to the phone cal, and reluctantly says in a drawn out tone to the security guard, who’s still paying more attention to his Hostess snack than to the 6-figure making man in front of him. Rush: I suppose….you might be able to hang out with me for a few days….but we’re not friends, and you’re nothing but muscle. In fact, you don’t even have a name as far as I’m concerned. The security guard once again takes a huge chomp out of the Twinkie, and then decides to reply through another chewing mouth, showing terrible manners. “Yo, mah name Ray Ray. I’m keepin’ it real.” Rush clenches his fist, and then turns to Ray Ray, motioning for him to follow, and the 300+ pounder follows behind, continuing to enjoy the beautiful Twinkie. Rush mutters as he looks back at Ray Ray while the scene fades out. “I can’t believe this shit. I thought there might be one other educated black person out there, but obviously I found one of the millions who happens to be dumb as a rock.” “Yo, let’s make a KFC stop, cuz’ I gotta keep it real.”
