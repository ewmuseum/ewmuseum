My Opponents and what I think of them...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: TODAY (If I made the deadline for Fusion then count this as #6. If not, oh well, it's still for TT.) Driving all the way back to Germany was tiring, believe it or not. After driving all the way to Germany only a week ago to find Ivan Chistov, and failing miserably, I didn’t expect to be heading here again. And yet, I was so close to Chistov in the end. His alias, Terry Gordan was in an unknown location for the past year already and where would be the last place to look? In a church, of course. I ruled out the possibility that Chistov may be in the priest’s house beside the church. That was where his assistant, Clair Rivence was and I don’t think they would be hiding in the same location, waiting for the police to find them. Ivan had to be in the church, in a cellar or something, I didn’t know. As I drove down a road, watching trees and countryside speed past me, I thought of my PCW tenure by going through all of the opponents I was to face in the next week, leading up to the PPV, Supremacy where I hoped to have a match. Mark Makonie: This guy, I don’t know whether to trust or not. On one side, I beat him once in singles competition and beat him again in a tag match. Then, my past two matches, I was actually with him, both in tag matches. Those matches, we lost both. What does this tell me? Any opponent can beat that Makonie easily. I only hope that he pulls in an effort against SOCIETY in our handi-cap match against him. We may have a chance, with us having an advantage. If Makonie stays out of my way and does nothing, it can be a singles match where I think I can beat SOCIETY. SOCIETY: This man is one of my toughest competition in the Rising Star division. Both SOCIETY and me are big men, which gives an advantage to me, considering that most of the guys in the division, SOCIETY can handle easily. I haven’t really faced SOCIETY. We’ve been fighting our own battles, waiting until we get the chance to fight each other. Now, we get the chance. As long as nothing goes wrong, like Mark Makonie screwing things up, I’m confident in winning my match. It’ll be like a good luck charm and a warm up for the gauntlet on Tuesday. I can figure out SOCIETY’S style and use it against him more in the gauntlet match, when we will finally get a chance to face off with each other again. It should be good, him and me in the ring. Sivisian Krikorian: I don’t even know if I can spell this guys first name. Or last name for that matter! I may have faced off against this guy once or twice in tag action but I’m not that worried. The ‘Ukrainian Sniper’? That sounds like a person who hunts geese, not a wrestler. If the guy sounds like he hunts geese, what does he do to real people? I don’t see any threat in this guy, but who knows? Anything could happen and it is a free ball game. This guy could come back from behind and end up winning the whole match. Nah, what am I thinking? This guy stands no chance against me and in the end, I’ll be on top. Dan Osbourne: I don’t like this guy, period. The one match I was with him, in tag competition, was the match that I lost all respect for this guy. This guy can’t wrestle, has no future, and will be in the Rising Star division until somebody fires him. This man was what cost me a crucial win in the division. I couldn’t even run down the ramp to break up the pinfall! That has got to be the record for fastest win, because this guy sucks so badly, it’s not even funny anymore. If Dan and me meet up in the gauntlet, you’ll be sure that’ll I’ll kick his ass, over and over, and over again. I guess him sucking so bad is an advantage in the Rising Star division because he’ll be easy to lost and because of this, I see no real threat in Dan Osbourne. Stone Burns: To be honest, I haven't really watched this guy. When I first saw him in the Rising Star division, I rules out Burns as a threat, simple because of his name. Despite all this, Stone has been the surprise in the division and now he is a threat against me, being right on my tail in the standings. Because of the fact that I didn't check up on Stone, or didn't care to, I have to watch him closely on Tuesday until I come out. I'm pretty sure that I'll beat him though. Stone, like mostly all of my opponents are no match for me. My size will out-wrestle all of them in the end and give me the win. James Edwards: I can't believe this guy! This is an opponent that will challenge me to my core. Edwards, or 'Lunar', as people call him has surprised me and made me raise my eyebrow. This guy isn't big, he smiles like a freak, and wrestles good! I have to defeat this guy to continue on in the round and you can be sure I will. Over the past few days, I've watched this guys style and know what he does and what his weaknesses are. His weaknesses? Do you expect me to tell you all of my secrets? Lunar has raised an eyebrow, but I'll be sure to smack that smile right off his face and pin him, one, two, three. I guarantee it! Shane Wethers: This guy, I know the most about. No, I haven't faced him one on one yet, but in Primetime Wrestling Academy, I heard about people talking about him. Because I knew Shane was soon going to be in the federation, I started watching him intently in PWA and know all of his moves off by heart. Of course, the thing that gives me an advantage over this guy, besides that I know all of his moves, was that he was in PWA. You see, I was so good, that I didn't need to take a trip to the academy. Instead, I moved right along to the big leagues. Wethers may have gotten some training in the academy, but I learned all of my trainings on my own. Wethers will be a swift ride to my last opponent. Matt Jackson: I myself can not even say that I am not scared. In the month before I came to the federation, Jackson and his associate, Haley Hilton, were associates of Xavier Kannon. Yes, THE Xavier Kannon, former PCW champion. Jackson made a lot of people look twice at him in the first month behind Kannon, making him the heavy favourite in the Rising Star division. I know what you are thinking. Just because Jackson was with Kannon, he isn't that good. He could just have been bluffing, right? Wrong. Matt Jackson went by opponent, after opponent and is currently unbeaten in the whole tournament. I have to find a way to overcome the power of Jackson, and I am confident. I have been studying Jackson's stealing style and I figure, if I beat Jackson at his own game, I can beat him in the match. You can be sure I'll give it my all against Jackson. And there are all my opponent that I will have to go through this Tuesday in the gauntlet. You can be sure it isn't easy. I snapped out of my trance and realized where I was. I stared ahead at the sign that I was looking for. I was in the town, ready to find Terry Gordan...
