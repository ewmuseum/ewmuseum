The Fear and Loathing Saga: Higher then a Plane...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/??/02 I figured the smartest thing for us to do was just get onto the plane and fly off without collecting anything from the house, I was getting a bit paranoid for understandable reasons. The drugs we still had with us were enough to keep us in jail for a while... even despite the fact we were both famous and bigger then life. I tried to stuff the blotter acid as far down into the seats of the car as I could, when I felt it hit the metal of the seat I felt a little bit more secure, I also felt a little bit more hungry. The inside of our car was a thick haze of smoke. Talena coughed a little as she rolled down her window. She wasn't a light weight but we did just try to eliminate most of our stash before we tried to get on a airplane. I couldn't help but think how this would look to other people, people who got to see this happen live or whatever. People who saw this while they walked by and looked in on the black sportscar parked on the corner of Market and Cherry. If they looked past the haze they would see that inside sat the two most important people in the sport of wrestling, and it would be two people who aren't generally associated with the drug culture. You might wonder why we actually have tried to distance ourself from the world of drugs while we are in the public eye. Neither I nor Talena concentrate or find that important. We aren't Semore Smoke or Stewart Hix. We aren't the general one dimensional druggie junkie failures. We are a pair of young, attractive, smart, and most importantly, rich people who want to see how high they can get before they are dashed on the rocks. Neither of us feel the undying need to put our crutch in the forfront. Why would we? We do it now because we want PCW to be embarressed, Kannon thought he could fire me because of drug abuse, he fired me because I was scared. It was as simple as that. It was more then a month ago and I still feel bitter resentment towards it, and thats why I tell this story. So you can see exactly what me and Talena tried to do to get our own brand of revenge. It was simple, we took our images and our reputation and the reputation of Primetime Championship Wrestling and its president Xavier Kannon with us wherever we go. So why not try and use this responsibility to our advantage? What could be more embarressing then seeing stories in the tabloids about your former number one contender being seen with acid oozing from his pores screaming about Walruses and Woodchucks naked somewhere in Vegas. It was brilliant, and I knew it all coincided with my attorney and lovers little plan to find the American Dream. It made sense when I saw our terminal zoom up onto us from a short distance away. We both laughed when we realized how funny these moving walkways really were. Alot of this is in a blur to me, from between the car, the valet, and the airplane. But I know that we eventually got on it, and I thought for a moment I realized what the American Dream really was, I thought I knew it before we even set foot in that den of inequity that is Las Vegas, before we even got onto the fucking plane. In between my incessent giggling when I was asked if I needed a second sack of nuts, I thought about it. The American Dream, it was the idea that built the nation, the feeling that made political parties what they were, and the reason me and my lover were on this hurtling chunk of metal. The American Dream... Revenge
