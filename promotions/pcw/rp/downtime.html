Date: not entered BREEEEE! BREEEEEE! A spinning red lantern and shrill, piercing siren splits the air as we rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, within Stately Steele Manor. All those present, trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, The Masochist, Graces, cover their ears, grimacing at the unexpected intrusion of noise pollution. “By the Gods!!” shouts Big Al above the commotion, “What manner of sound be this?! Tis the very call of the harpies that threatens my senses with destruction!!” Sue tries to point in the direction of the glowing red phone and is rather unsuccessful as the noise pains her head, “I dunno, but I think that stupid phone has something to do with it!” Steele look sat the fire engine red phone in puzzlement, it glowing red with each high decibel round. He cautiously makes his way over to the phone. Gingerly he lifts it, the flashing lights and shrieking alarms ceasing, and raises it to his ear. “Y’ello?” “Ah, good! Steele! We need your help!” the pleading voice on the other end says. “Commissioner?! How did yon receive my number? It is thusly unlisted…” “There’s NO TIME!! We’ve gotten reports that a large number of super villains have been seen congregating out on Sandy Beach, on the southern portion of San Jose. Such a thing can’t be good…” Steele furrows his brow in confusion, “From whence did the glowing phone and blood-curdling siren make their way into my humble abode?” “Jeezus Al, are you listening?! We followed you home once and had a blonde let us in to install it, happy? Anyways, we have reports that Dr. Doom, Clayface, Rabid Weasel…” “Why is it that I have become San Jose’s first line of defense? Surely you and your men could check such a situation out to ascertain…” “We need you, Big Alan Steele!” The commish implores, “Remember, its Sandy Beach on the south side. My men will back you up as soon as the situation is under control by you!” “Is that a steel drum I hear in the background, as well as the gentle lapping of a Caribbean shoreline?” “Uh…” CLICK! The line disconnects, leaving a ponderous Big Al to slowly lower the phone to its rightful place. Sue steps forward, rubbing her temples in an attempt to assuage her newfound migraine. “They could have at least TOLD us they were installing a hotline…” she says, scowling. “Though their methods of asking crude and intrusive, once again San Jose teeters on the brink of destruction!!” Big Al postures, “And only WE may save her! Come, Lord of Pain! Let us make haste as on the winged feet of Hermes, to once again battle the forces of ultimate evil!! And by the gods we’ll give them what for!! Hi-Keeba!!” The Masochist shrugs and struggles out of a comfy chair, while Steele points towards the door leading into the garage. “Quickly!! To the Steele-mobile!!” “You mean the Winnebago? Or Sue’s Jetta?” Big Al sneaks a look back at his manager, her eyes boring holes into his chest. “Er… the Winnebago. A chariot fit for Zeus himself!!” “That’s right… last time you borrowed my car, there was a ding in the door, so I ESPECIALLY don’t want you taking her gilded doors out for crime fighting.” “But Ms. Sue!” Steele tries to counter, “Was the malicious and dastardly ‘loose shopping cart OF DEATH’ that compromised your chariots beauty! And we must use a vehicle of a more nimble quality…” Big Al looks soulfully into the steely eyes of Sue, who never flinches her gaze. Steele sighs in defeat. “Come Lord of Pain,” he mutters, “The law of Zeus shall be upheld in a less than able ride.” The two race out of the room to the garage, as Sue calls after them. “And The Masochist drives!” Later, The massive Winnebago pulls into one of the parking lots of Sandy Beach. Big Al bites at his lip as he guides the barge-like vehicle across three parking spaces, a good distance from most of the other cars in the lot. The Masochist looks around, straining at his seat belt. “No, no, no… I know we can find something that is closer than this…” “By the gods!!” exclaims Steele, pointing through the windshield, “Tis worse than we feared!” On the beach, a dazzling horde of angry-looking spandex and armors line the beach up and down. Gone are the frolicking families and the sun worshippers, replaced now by the most infamous of the criminal element. From Joker to Juggernaut, from Two Face to Two Ton, from Magneto to Mysterio… wave after wave of evil stretch out across the sand… catching waves. Big Al growls, his huge muscles tensing up as an angry look befalls his Mighty visage. “Hades spawned demon brood!! How despicable of them to desecrate a place of merriment set aside for the families of San Jose! We MUST put an end to the diabolical schemes, if not for anything else but the children!! Think of the Children, Lord of Pain!!” “Uh, Al… it looks pretty okay out there. I mean, I see picnics and beach volleyball. Look! Green Goblin is building a sand castle and the Penguin is body surfing…” “The fiends!! To battle!! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele leaps from the cab of the Winnebago, sprinting across the hot sand, fists balled and held back, ready to strike. The Masochist sighs and quickly follows, running after his partner, who is building himself up to a berserker frenzy, screaming out Olympic epithets. The Masochist finally catches up with Al and jumps on his back. “No! Stop and actually LOOK for a second!!” Big Alan Steele skids to a halt, the Masochist flipping over his back and landing hard on the beach. Our ever-vigilant demi-god frowns, but places a hand over his eyes to shield them from the sun. Hobgoblin is shielding his little hobgoblins from Big Al’s soon-to-be onslaught. A volleyball game has stopped, the normally vengeful villains taking steps back, hands up for defense. In fact, ALL the super-villains are looking at him, not so much in fear, but in an alarmed manner. Dr. Doom clears his throat and rises from his beach towel, from where he was seeking a tan. He slowly steps forward, a disturbing sight in his usual helmet and cape… and swim trunks showing off his pasty-white man-flesh. The eyes on Doom look back and forth, nervously, as he extends a hand to the simmering Big Al. “Heh, heh, heh… erhem… Big Alan Steele! Nice to see you! Come to join our little water foray, have we?” Steele’s eyes narrow, as he rebuffs the handshake. “Tell me, oh thee of wanton destruction and sadistic mayhem, tell me how is it that I find thee and your villains-in-arms all clustered so, on yon fair beach head?” “Oh, well, being hell-bent on taking over the Earth takes a lot out of those of us keen on our brand of work.” Steele’s eyes go wide in anger, “Takes a lot out of you?!?! Why, by Zeus’ thunder I’ll take a lot out of you right now… !!” Doom hastily holds up his hands, plaintatively, “No! Don’t! It’s just a reunion! A single day off among the many of plotting, planning and purveying of our evil tasks! Surely even you can concede the need for a day or two off here and there, right Big Al?” Big Al hesitates, lowering his clenched fists. “I suppose I do,” Heavily sighs our hero, “Perhaps on this one day we can come to a temporary truce, to replenish our tanks with the fuel of goodness and righteousness.” “Only with evil and malice.” “Oh, of course, of course,” concedes Big Al, “Tis what I implied.” “This is one of the real benefits of being unionized with the Legion of Doom. We get benefits as well as paid holidays, plus once a year we all get together and just hang loose, to get reacquainted, fool around, begin to formulate devious plots combining strengths, barbecuing… thank you Big Al! I’m sure you will see that all will be in rightful order today, though tomorrow I promise nothing.” Steele beams, and extends his hand out, “Agreed!! Today we have a truce! Hi-Keeba!!” A small cheer riffles through the villains, and once again their relaxing activities begin anew. Doom smiles underneath his helm and returns to spot in the sand, while The Masochist pats Steele on the back. “That was real gracious of you, Big guy. And besides… it’s not like they are harming anyone.” Big Al’s eyes are following the restarted volleyball game, dimly aware of what the Masochist has said. “Yes… and perhaps we should stay, maybe, to ensure that their activities don’t range beyond the bounds set forth by good lady Justice…” “Not a bad idea… hey! Where are you going?” But Steele hasn’t heard, walking over and picking up the volleyball that has flown out of bounds and near his feet. The Croc eyes Steele up warily as he approaches. “May we… have our ball back?” “But of course, of course… say! I have an idea! How about allowing the Masochist and I to play as well!! Perhaps we can begin a new era of understanding and…” Lex Luthor makes a mad dash under the v-ball net and swipes the ball out of Big Al’s quickly. “No! The teams are set! We can’t let you guys play! It’s a tourney!” “But the truce… perhaps if we were to…” starts Big Al. “No! No! A thousand times no! We just can’t let you in!” Steele stares down at his empty hands, as Luthor poots the ball back to the other side of the net. A look of sadness becomes etched into Steele’s brow, as he kicks absently at a shell in the sand. A malicious gleam twinkles in Lex Luthor’s eye and the smirk on his face grows until he can’t contain it any longer, bursting out into pure, maniacal laughter. “BWAHAHAHAHA!! At last you have been defeated by exclusion!! We, the villains of the Legion of Doom have forced you to taste the bitter pill that is our glorious, though admittedly petty, victory!!” The entire cadre of villains begin laughing and pointing at Big Alan Steele, the shame clear on his face. He staggers back, eyes wide with the peril of his impending defeat. He grabs at the Masochist, and yells. “Quickly!! Take the Winnebago and get us back-up!! I’ll hold out here…” “Are you sure? I can…” “JUST GO NOW!!!” The Masochist runs off with the keys, leaping into the Bago and zooming off. Meanwhile, the taunts and catcalls continue to rain on our hero’s head. He desperate tries with all his learned techniques to avoid their scorn… ignoring, the sticks and stones principle… all to no avail. Just when it seem like our hero has no dignity left… the sound of the Olympic Theme Song!! Played on a novelty car horn! Steele grins as waves of relief wash over him, the Winnebago returned after 15 long minutes. From out the side door the Graces race onto he sand, their tanned and taut bodies resplendent in their bikinis. Sue follows, in a big sunhat and carrying an umbrella as a smiling Masochist brings up the rear. “By the gods!” exclaims a relieved Steele, “I knew not if I could hold out much longer!! Did you bring the required weaponry?!” “Of course!” The Masochist produces a volleyball net and ball. The Graces squeal in delight, setting up the net and practicing right away. The male villains have all stopped, their eyes molesting the blonde and lovely Graces. Steele grins as his girls (and the Masochist) take their spots and begin to play in earnest. “Who needs suntan oil?” Sue Saksnussum calls out, and the girls all raise their hands. Lex Luthor walks over, as if in a trance. He gently taps Steele on the shoulder, gaining the Greek warrior’s attention, but his eyes never able to actually stray from the jiggle-fest created by the Graces. “Uh… do you mind if we join in?” Big Al tsks heavily, “I am truly sorry, Lord Luthor, but our teams are filled to capacity. And wipe the drool from yon chin…” Lex angrily wipes his chin with the sleeve of his shirt, accosting Big Al. “What do you MEAN the teams are filled? There are only six Graces and you two dunderheads!! I know Sue won’t play… so that makes you 4 people short!!” Steele smiles, shrugging his monstrous traps as if to say ‘sorry.’ Luthor howls in rage, a coven of villains surrounding him, dragging him back to the original game. “Oh, you’ll pay for this, Big Alan Steele!! You may have won THIS time… but next time I will NOT be defeated so easily!! You hear me!! You’ll rue the day you ruined our beach refuge!! Argh!!” The Masochist walks over, a bottle of lotion in his hands. “Wow. That was a close one, huh?” Steele grins and nods in agreement, “Hi-Keeba!!”
