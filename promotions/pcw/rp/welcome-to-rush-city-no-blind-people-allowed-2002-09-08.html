Welcome To Rush City....NO BLIND PEOPLE ALLOWED!

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/8/02 OOC: I swear this is a real place. Seriously. Recently, Rush Mason has been rather detached from PCW affairs, ever since his return from his ‘injury’ at the hands of Clyde during July, he has been seen less on PCW Television. However, his two new protégés, Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson, have been starting to rise through the tag team ranks and Rush Mason is certainly paying attention to PCW and his ultimate goal of becoming PCW Champion. This week….a certain part of that dream can become fulfilled if he wins the #1 contendership to the Continental Title. However, he faces stiff competition from Frisco, El Diablo, and Snow, definitely not a group of men to be messed with. However…Rush is not exactly one to show his fear….even if he feels like a three year old near a group of scary clowns. “I come to you second-rate citizens…err…people with love, or at least not that much hate, in my heart, because you have named this wonderful little quaint hamlet after me. Yep, that’s him. That’s the Voodoo Villain, the man who calls himself the future of PCW. Of course, the scene has not come up yet, but one can only guess that he is continuing his sarcastic asshole streak that has brought him up through the ranks of PCW. “Of course I…well sort of appreciate the notion, but you small-town folk must realize that my true dreams lie elsewhere…somewhere between the sacred poon of J-Lo and throwing The End into busy traffic. My true dream of….one day having an orphanage built in my name. Anyone who knows of Rush, or who has even heard this little schpiel probably would’ve figured that Rush is not exactly the type of guy who wants an orphanage built in his name. Maybe a morgue, but not an orphanage. “But I don’t want any of those nasty kids with fleas and shit. That’s just disgusting. If the kids aren’t totally second-rate, they can come, but none of this street trash is coming into MY orphanage. Ah, yes, that’s much more like our ‘hero.’ As these words are said, the scene finally opens up to show Rush, in somewhat formal clothes, standing in front of a podium, with a small microphone in front of his face. Rush: Yes, it’s truly a pleasure to be here in Rush City, Minnesota….well not really a pleasure but it was required of me since I couldn’t stand that Kannon idiot any longer. As we scan around the scenery, it looks nothing like a city, more like the hamlet that Rush talks of, but one might wonder why exactly a PCW superstar would be visiting a hick town in the middle of Minnesota, when he is supposed to be heading to Portland, Oregon for the biggest match of his life? We certainly don’t know, but even Portland looks pretty appealing compared to Rush City. Rush: Yes this umm….interesting…town has inspired me to become a poet, and write a poem which addresses this town and why I’m so important so that it was named after me. Yes, the modesty of the soon to be #1 contender to the Continental Title is just mind-boggling. We still haven’t seen whom the crowd is that he is addressing, but Rush reads off a paper a small poem. Rush:A city named after meA sub without the parA bumble and a beeBitch….get in the car After me is named the city of rushWhere I can go and see a titty in the brushThat’s where I can go and findAll the hicks that exist in my mind Yeah, that’s the poem and as Rush finishes, we hear….nothing. No clapping or anything of that sort, only Rush standing alone and as the camera pans out, we see a small pig and three toddlers standing in front of Rush, as he pretends there is a large crowd and slowly walks off towards his car, pretending to be walking amongst a huge crowd. Rush: The work of a truly great celebrity is never done. And Rush slides into the car and drives off down the dirt road, leaving his ‘crowd’ behind, as the scene fades out. “Who was dat guy?” “I fink it wuv Wush Mafon!” “No way, vat was Thatuz Quo!” “Oink”
