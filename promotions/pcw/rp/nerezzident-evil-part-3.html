Nerezzident Evil (Part 3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th, May 2003 Pinned to the ground with a white complexion splashed across his once colourful cheeks, the Jester scurries backwards with his eyes fixated on the point of entry. Suddenly the wooden door crashed with such an impact that the hinges rattled and the decayed walls emitted a powered like residue that flutters to the carpeted ground. The door repeatedly jerked as the Jester’s eyes widened with fright, his trembling body continuing to shuffle along the grim corridor and doing so he passes a collection of wooden doors each etched with the familiar symbols of a helmet, shield and body armour. Scrambling to his vertical base the Jester inspected each of the doors frantically, sweat seeping along the brow of his forehead. His nerves apparent as he ventured from one door to another when with a thunderous bellow the entrance to the corridor caved in scattering debris everywhere and forming a murky cloud of dust that scuppers visibility. “One would think that Dracula would take five minutes out of his busy massacre schedule to pluck the cartilage from his teeth but, NO!!!!” cried the Emerald Menace as he cringed his angelic face before tilting it towards the heavens. Not dwelling on the situation the comic surged along the corridor with an adrenaline rush and courtesy of Lady Luck he found a door with a helmet shape etched underneath the lock. Fidgeting he took a firm grasp of his iron key and positioned it into the lock peering back at the rubble a few feet away. The dust had begun to settle and visibility had improved when suddenly a silhouette fizzled in and out of the density of the mist. “Oh, please God let it be another dispensable soldier!” prayed the green tailored Jester with his hands momentarily clenching together. “I’m too young… And good looking to die!” he proclaimed further as his green eyes twitched right to see the leg of a behemoth cut through the smoke. After a few tense seconds of fidgeting the door swung ajar in time for the Jester to make his timely exit just as the Vampire made his presence known. Dried bloodstains had tainted the black trench coat stretching along his broad shoulders and the crimson colour trickling either side of his indented cheeks formed a goatee effect. The pure Vampire within had engulfed the little human presence of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza as his canine teeth protruded into full view and his purple streaked hair netted and tangled at the back of his head whilst a few strands hung loosely over his piercing red eyes. As the Vampire swung his head in a pendulum motion with his eyes inspecting each of the doors the attention reverts back to the frantic Jester who stands cornered in a small-boxed room with a single light hanging overhead. Against the wall is a large grey chest that looks similar to that of a freezer and with curiosity the conceited comic approached it, taking the lid by two hands he lifted. “What have we here?” questioned the trickster to himself as the floorboards creaked from the behemoths weight as he crept closer in pursuit of his next victim. The sound filtered in from the neighbouring corridor outside. The Jester gulped hard as he squinted his eyes to investigate the metallic chest and to his astonishment the assortment of items came across as a huge disappointment, the sigh an indication to this. Picking up a fabric bag that nestles against the container he emptied the content into it. The dirt stained walls gave an eerie awe to the backdrop and its furnishings remained minimum at best for sitting beside the door was a small oval table with a typewriter covered over in cobwebs and a large metallic rack covered in cleaning utensils. “Well at-least I have the opportunity to write my memoirs before I die!” sighed the Jester as he tried miserably in injecting a little comedy. As the prankster gazed over the assortment of cleaning utensils littering the twin tiered racking a misty fog simmered from beneath the door, trickling across the bare concrete floor like the Angel of Death. Suspiciously the Jester tilted his head to the ground and his open mouth blurted nothing but gibberish as he lost all knowledge of the English language. Remembering the last encounter with a fog like substance the former Front Office Entertainment Manager desperately waved his hand over the cleaning devices behind. “Take that…” bellowed our hero as he swung a duster at the cloud of silt for it only to pass through, his frantic waving cutting nothing but thin air. The cloud expanded in volume as it began ascending to head height when with quick improvisation the Jester snatched at a cordless vacuum cleaner. Miming ‘Please God let it work’ as he closed his eye his index finger waved over the trigger and with a subtle click the device sprung into action. With a curl of the corners of his lips a sinister smile materialised across his perked face and seething the moment he sucked up the smoke like fog. “THAT’LL TEACH YOU BITCH~!” triumphantly roared the comedian as he blew the tip of the vacuum cleaner in mock cowboy fashion. Standing smug and happy with his performance the Jester placed the device on top of the metallic chest when without warning a squeaky voice murmured from within, “Let me out, let me out!” Peering with a dumbfounded expression the Jester watched as the plastic cleaner bounced up and down atop of the item chest, occasionally flickering with flashes of yellow sparks. Seizing the moment the Emerald Menace edged out through the door clutching the fabric bag that follows in tow. Vanishing from the crime scene our host passes down a blind right hand turn and directly into another flimsy puzzle, masterminded by the occupier no doubt. Cornered off from the neighbouring corridor was a single door with no keyhole or opening device with nothing but a cryptic clue engraved onto a brass plaque, “When the Centurions return, new journeys will open” Looking towards the two Centurion statuettes the Jester noticed two concrete tablets imbedded either side of the boxed corner; possibly pressured buttons? Shouldering the bronze coloured statue the object scraped forward before settling atop of the trigger and a click sound murmured from the direction of the locked door. Taking heed from the first the Jester began applying the same method of shouldering the opposite statuette to the second concrete tablet, however to no avail as the Centurion simply wouldn’t budge. Scratching his head in thought the sound of footsteps filtered the uneasy silence and continuingly growing in decibels as the assailant drew closer. “Oh shit!” gulped the Lord of Laughter as he stood in the midst of a shadow. Looming over the petit comic stood the goliath Nascosto Ireul Nerezza with saliva seeping from his low hung jaw and his draping arms dragging in tow as he shuffled closer and closer with each intimidating stride. “No, no… No more Jester… Die Jester die…” hissed the satanic Vampire mindlessly. A pause soon followed as both figures stood with their eyes fixated at the other. “Easy tiger! You don’t to drain little old me ‘cause I have green blood, it’s a genetic disorder, I blame the parents!” pleaded the Tag Champion only for his hearted speech to fall on deaf ear. Again the silence swamped the corridor when suddenly a thought emitted from the mind of the Evil Super Genius (Self-Proclaimed mind you…) and for no apparent reason he removed his velvet business jacket as he sat his bag down on the floor. Taking up a matador stance the Jester toyed with the hunger driven Vampire by swaying his jacket back and forth. “TORO... TORO... TORO... Run already stupid!” cried the Original Prankster. - Bang - Charging forward the Jester suckered the Vampire and the Living Nightmare crashed through the blockade like a steam locomotive and in doing so he fell to a heap on the ground within the confines of the next room. Seemingly out cold the cautious Champion shuffled into a room furnished in various oil paintings that align all four walls, pictures of people and distinguished backdrops. With a small hole in the roof a cold, bitter wind circulated through the room and its presence stirs the fallen Vampire who twitches from each passing. “What now?” murmured Gaz Green as he ventured inward. Peering up at the rafters black ravens nest and occasionally pass overhead yet the Jester pays them no mind as he exams the pictures on show. Beneath each is a red button but before the Jester can investigate further his leg gets snagged by the now conscious Angel of Fear who violently claws at his partner as he screams in a blood-curdling manner. “Don’t you ever give up?” Rummaging into his bag of tricks he removes a glistening tin with ‘First Aid’ wrote along the canister and with a press of the nozzle he almost blinds his assailant who squirms about on the ground with his talons scratching at his pale face. Fumbling about further in his bag, Gaz Green pulled out a bag of bird seed as though he were a magician and looking at his aid worryingly he doses the fiend in a sesame seed assortment. In doing so he bides himself a few much-needed minutes as the ravens circulating above move in for the kill; obviously attracted towards the shower of sesame seed that litters both the floor and Nerezza. Still however cornered the Jester went about quickly pressing the hoard of buttons beneath each portrait, failing miserably he clutched at a secured candelabrum in means for a weapon but the brass instrument would only tilt forward and in doing so a click sound triggered and a discoloured wall at the far end of the room elevated to unveil a doorway. “Open Sesame!” smugly retorted the trickster. Racing over to the doorway the Jester peered down at what looked to be a concrete staircase that winds in a circular fashion all the way down past the first floor and into the heart of the mansion. Dimly lit and looking somewhat a scene from a horror flick Gaz Green took his chances, unaware of the plastic sign post fixed to the left-hand wall with the message, “Laboratory…”
