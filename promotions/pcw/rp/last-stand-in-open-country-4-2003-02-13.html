Last Stand In Open Country #4

Title: Last Stand In Open Country #4 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 02/13/03 Sometimes a man knows it’s his time to board that train and move on. Not certain of the trains destination, speed, or purpose, it’s a blind leap of faith. An instinct similar to a bird flying south for the winter to escape the cold. That train is coming and Duke Williams is ready to board it. The Malice Man sits on a corner stool in the Green Frog Bar and Grill in Missoula, Montana. The place is dingy and looks to have a few bar fights. The walls are paneled with wood and the bar has a giant mirror behind it coved with bottles of whiskey. The place is full of power drinkers and down right alcoholics. Duke fits in well wearing nothing but black from boots to hat. A glass of Jack Daniels whisky rests in his hand as he stares into the mirror. It was just this morning he found gray in his hair. “I found comfort and courage from bottles of whiskey. I swear to you friends these old high times sure seem risky. I have backed away gently from those who tried to burn me and blocked up my ears that no one should learn me. I have paid my dues with losses and wins. But in the end it all comes down to one rumble for a piece of gold I covet.” Slurs the Malice Man somewhere between depression and drunkenness. Duke has wanted the World title since he walked into PCW. He’s never been so close in his life and the thought of victory consumes his every thought. But he hears the train’s whistle in the distance just as he spots the shine of the gold. It’s a race against an invisible clock but the finish line is obvious. March Madness only a few weeks away. ”I have flown like a bird from every cage that confined me and broken every one of the ties that bind me. I have danced me around some sad ol' sad ol situations and taken my share of those sweet invitations. I have made friends and foes in my quest for gold. Some angles better left untold and some stories that have never unfolded.” Speaks Duke with conviction and angst. Tuesday Duke fights in yet another Main Event match up. It wasn’t that long ago that he was opening up PCW cards against Hank and JJ. But for the last year or so he’s been no stranger to main event match. For all the main event matches he’s been in you think a title shot would have happened along the way. However that hasn’t been the case and this match is no exception. “I recall a time I set my soul on fire just for show. And all it ever taught me, was the more I learn, the less I seem to know. You think with all that wisdom my win loss record would be more copasetic. But my friends I can tell you that’s not the case. I’ve been lied to, sinned to, and done wrong by just about every promoter and partner I have ever dealt with. I’m still waiting for some checks form that funny talking fellow from New York wearing them high heeled Gucci shoes.” Rants the Malice Man lost in a world of drink. Duke’s an old school man of the world. Tuesday he fights two young guys looking too burry him like a turd in a littler box. What those kids don’t know is Duke isn’t going down that easy. The trains rolling closer hoss, and that makes him a desperate man. Some might even classify him as dangerous like a wounded wolf with nothing to lose. “I’m past the point of fighting for pride my friends. Now I’m just swinging to see how many I can take down with me. To retire with the world strap around my waist would be an honor I can’t even comprehend. Darkstar had his chance at it and blew it. Another lesson lost on youth and inexperience that he probably won’t make again. Then there’s Pharaoh who was pushed up the ladder so fast burn marks our on his hands. Some may say Nero’s age and speed of ascension had something to do with his insanity. I would disagree but I’ve been wrong more times then I’ve been right in my life.” Say’s Duke with a lost twinkle in his eyes. One more month and Duke’s career is over forever. The other two will have long careers that span many more moons. But for the warrior with the malicious attitude this is one of his last battles. His foes in the match don’t appreciate him but the fans do. This win will go out to them and maybe send a message to the roster that he is for real in the rumble. “I hope the boys are ready to be sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound!.” Smile Duke as he finishes up his glass of whiskey. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
