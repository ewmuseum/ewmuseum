The Night After...

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31.08.02 The scene opens outside an average sized suburban home. There is a one car garage attatched to the side of the house and the house is painted white. It is about midday on a sunny Saturday. There isn’t a cloud in the sky and people are walking along the street. There are a lot of cars, parked outside and around the house, none of them parked particularly well. There is everything from low riders and jeeps to pinto’s and ford Escorts parked around. Inside of the car, none of them are particulary clean, but on the outside they are all shiny. This shows that owner of the cars are most probably late teens or in their early 20’s. On the doorstep, there are two people on the doorstep, both are Asian and look completely out of it. The scene moves into the house. Inside there are bodies everywhere, they are not dead, but asleep. There are people asleep all up the stairs, on the couches, leaning against tables, on tables, underneath tables and on the floor. There is alcohol and food everywhere. Big bowls of punch, no doubt containing more vodka than anything else are on the tables, while bottles and cans are all over the floor and half eaten boxes of pizza are also everywhere. This is obviously the aftermath of a big party. Looking around at the people who are passed out, there are blacks, white’s, Asians, everyone. Some people are thugged out, while look more punk rockish. Then, a average height, but muscular white guy starts walking down the stairs.This man is obviously one half of the PCW tag team champions, Mark Makonie. He is dressed in just a baggy wife beater and white boxer shorts. His head is shaven and he has one of his hands his holding his head, he obviously has a hang over. He walks to the bottom of the stares, stepping over a few pissed-out-of-their-head men and he walks across the living room, stepping over more passed out guys before walking into the kitchen. Once, he is there he suddenly stops, looks up and turns around, with a total look of shock on his face. He only just noticed what state the place is in Makonie: “My house! Noo, my beautiful, suburban house!” Makonie looks angry and he walks over to the couch and starts hitting a black guy on the couch, he’s not hitting him hard, but hard enough to wake him up. The man, being hit is none other than Duzza, Makonie’s tag partner. Duzza eventually wakes up Duzza: “What?! What?! What do you want… I was havin’ this fly dream, where I was in a car with Halle Berry and… man you look angry” Makonie: “Look at my house! Look at it!” Duzza, looks around and after his eyes are focused he noticed Makonies living room, filled with trash and drunks. Duzza: “Shittttt son… look at yo house!” Makonie: “It’s ruined!” Duzza: “Naw, it ain’t. Why you be trippin? It’ll be clean in no time, we just gotta get everyone out, me included and then you just run around with a vacuum” Makonie: “You are not leaving me with this mess! It was your idea to throw a party to celebrate are win, your gonna help me clean this shit up” Duzza: “A’ight dawg, a’ight, no need to trip dawg, I’ma help clean this place up” Makonie: “Ok. Good. Well… that was a cool party we threw wasn’t it” Duzza: “Hell yeah! This musta’ been the first party to ever have thugs and neo-nazi’s partyin’ side by side and no one catchin’ a curbin” Makonie: “Yeah. You know Duzz, it’s funny, when everyone is pissed and the music is loud, no one even notices what race the next guy is” Duzza: “For real. Your wastin’ your life wit’ all that ‘hate the blacks and Hispanic’s and Asians” shiznit” Makonie: “Well, I don’t know about that” Duzza: “Aww c’mon, y’know you loved it last night, it was straight thuggin’” Makonie: “Well, it was cool… Never thought I’d have so many black people in my house” Duzza: “Yeah well, you may wanna check the silverware dawg” Makonie: “Oh don’t worry. I will do” Duzza: “Dawg, I was jokin’” Makonie: “Yeah… err… so was I. Look, I’m going to make some breakfast, you want some toast or something?” Duzza: “Damn nazi, you sure in a nice mood this morning…or afternoon… but yeah” Makonie nods, before turning away and walking into the kitchen. Meanwhile, Duzza gets up off the couch and searches for the TV remote, which is probably buried underneath one of the drunks. Just then, Makonie comes storming out of the kitchen, a angry look on his face, he is staring right at Duzza Duzza: “What? I didn’t do it… It wasn’t me, whatever is broken, I didn’t do it” Makonie: “Where is it?” Duzza: Where’s what?” Makonie: “My belt obviously” Duzza: “How was that obvious? And I ain’t got a clue what you on ‘bout dawg” Makonie: “Don’t give me that bullshit. That’s what you said, when we was riding from a show together and the cop pulled us over cause you was speeding” Duzza: “Dawg, for real, I ain’t gots a clue what you talkin’ ‘bout man” Makonie: “My belt! It’s gone” Duzza: “What mother fucking belt?!” Makonie: “the tag belt you fucking idiot” Duzza: “Ooooh” Makonie: “Oooh? What oooh is that? Is that a ‘oooh I thought you was talking about the belt you wear to hold up pants’ or is that a ‘oooooh I sold your belt for liquor money’?” Duzza: “It was a “oooh I thought you meant the little black belt you put around your waist” not the big fuck off gold thing. Maan. Only a damn fool, could lose a belt like that. Ha, your going to go down in PCW history! As the damn cracker who lost his belt!” Makonie: “Shut the fuck up. I didn’t lose it, it was stolen. And you took it didn’t you” Duzza: “Me? Why the fuck would I take my own partners belt? Seriously?” Makonie: “See, I be nice to African Americans, I invite you in my house, and what do I get? I’m stolen from” Duzza: “You need to back the fuck up right now white boy. For real. I didn’t steal yo damn belt, and all my boys are still laying around drunk… same with ma asian homies. If anyone took it, it was one of you skinhead friends” Makonie: “Yeah whatever, one of your lot took it, don’t even deny it” Duzza: “Cracka, how you know it’s even been stolen? How you know it ain’t just been misplaced? Maybe you left it upstairs” Makonie: “I ain’t no fucking idiot. Unlike some people, I actually graduated from high school” Duzza: “Oh you wanna go there?” Makonie: “I’ll go wherever I want” Just then, a large belch is heard from behind the couch, both Duzza and Makonie go over to the couch and look behind it. On the floor behind the couch, is a big, wide white guy with a shaven head. Wearing Makonie’s tag team belt around his waist Makonie: “Errr” Duzza: “Now what’s that? Oh, it’s one of your white boy friends with your belt” Makonie: “Err.. Sorry Duzza” Duzza: “Huh? Can you say that a bit louder? I didn’t quite catch it” Makonie: “I said sorry! Ok… I’m sorry for accusing your, or any one else of taking my belt” Duzza: “Haha, yeah I ‘eard you the first time. Jus’ wanted to hear you say it again. Well, on that note, I’ma get my boys and I’ma bounce. I hope no white boys have stolen my car” The scene starts to fade to black as Duzza goes around waking up his boys and Makonie tries to wrestle his belt of the fat white guy sleeping behind the couch.
