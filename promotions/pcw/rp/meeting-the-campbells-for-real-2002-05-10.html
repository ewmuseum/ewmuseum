Meeting The Campbells...FOR REAL

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Meeting The Campbells...FOR REAL Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-10-2002 Demonic Distorted Voice:'YOU DO NOT BELONG WITH AMY!!! I WILL TEAR YOU AND HER APART!!! YOU WILL NEVER BE WORTHY OF HER!! YOU ARE NOTHING YOU SUITED FREAK!!! WHAT YOU HAVE WILL NEVER LAST!!!' Rent-A-Hero:'NOOOOOO!!! NOOOOO!! STOP STOP!!' ..::/::..Our story begins within the dark confines of the living room of the Campbells, the parents of OSW starlette and love interest of our hero Amy Campbell. The only inclines of light within the room are the pure rays of moonlight seeping in through the blinds of the window to the far left, just enough to illuminate none other than the startled and panic-stricken Rent-A-Hero as he is sitting up from his formerly horizontal base, his eyes wide and opened to the fullest within the darkness. Tiny droplets of sweat escape the eyeholes and the hair hole of his Green Lantern replica mask, our hero running his fingers through his blonde hair as it now begins to slick back due to the sweat saturating within it. He looks at his hands, trembling uneasily after what had to be a terrible nightmare he had the night before, documented in our last Rent-Adventure where he suffered through the torment of Amy's father not approving of him...spelling certain doom for their relationship if that was the case. He buries his face within his hands, trying to regain a sense of calmness within his soul but all the while still shooken up about the nightmare. Shortly, our hero feels the soft caress of two hands against his own...our hero not even instinctively jumping back startled for he already recognizes the satin touch trying to comfort him...he looks up to confirm his suspicion and he is correct...identifying that touch with the lovely Amy Campbell, gazing into his soft blue eyes with concern and love as she keeps a soft grasp on his hands. Amy keeps this grip as she slides onto the couch with Rent-A-Hero, who gladly makes room for her as she leans against him, leaning her forehead against his as the two of them seem to share so much emotionally even though they seem to share so little, Amy finally gaining the nerve to speak after brushing back a loose strand of her flowing red hair and being quiet enough so as not to awake her parents in the nearby room..::/::..Aimz:'Rentie...I knew something was wrong. What's wrong...please tell me. I'm listening.'..::/::..Our hero doesn't want to look Amy in the eye, but a soft push of her index finger against his chin raises his head up effortlessly, our hero unable to resist the silent pleading her eyes relate to him. He breathes in, trying to stay strong and 'heroic' in front of his 'love rabbit' (I can't believe they make me say this stuff) as he answers..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Great Gummi's of Bear Amy...I'm just worried my sweet. I'm scared your male parental unit may not approve of me and want me to stay away from you. It's been on my mind since coming here and it's been eating me up like a Sarlacc chomping on Boba Feet Amy. I want to impress him so badly so I can stay with you...you are one of those things in my life that gives me 'completion' and I feel is 'my own'...losing you would drive me into a frenzy even the Incredible Hulk would be scared of.'Aimz:*sigh* 'Even at night you talk that gibberish.' *Giggles, a rarity with Aimz* 'But seriously...don't worry a lot. Just be yourself and I'm sure he'll like you Rent-A-Hero...'Rent-A-Hero:'Is this the truth my sweet siren of my heart?' Aimz:'Ummm welll....no. That was actually just motivation. He's probably gonna shoot you dirty looks and interrogate you constantly...but you STILL have to be yourself Rentie. You just have to show him the person that I fell in love with and I'm sure it will all work out fine. I love my dad, you know that...but I will not let him or anything else compromise what me and you share. What we have is special and nothing is gonna break that, you understand.'Rent-A-Hero:'I'm quite aware of your feelings on our Union. I feel the same as well my crimson haired crysanthimum...' Aimz:'WHAT?'Rent-A-Hero: *sigh* 'How about I just say I love you and we'll call it even?' *Aimz smiles innocently as she snuggles up against our hero* 'I love you...'Amy:'I love you too.'..::/::..With the understanding reached and the lovely Amy calming the restlessness within the soul of our spandex enwrapped hero, the two kiss quietly in the darkness, their eyes closed tightly and swept up in the passion the two feel for each other...finally breaking their show of affection, Aimz keeps her arms wrapped around the neck of our hero, striking that devilish smirk of hers before asking quietly within the ear of Rent-A-Hero..::/::..Aimz:'Are you wanting what I'm wanting?' Rent-A-Hero:'Aimz...we couldn't possibly...I mean, this is your parents house.'Aimz:'What they don't know want hurt them. Besides, you know you want it...' Rent-A-Hero:'Oh alright Aimz...but we better not get caught.'Aimz:'YES!' ..::/::..Aimz leans her head in for yet another kiss from her heroic significant other, but he rises up abruptly and rushes off into the dark kitchen, Aimz left on the couch with a 'what the he**' look on her face followed by her arms folding in a show of annoyance. Our hero then returns with two cans of Tahitian Treat, the drink he JUST so happens to be sponsoring at PCW Supremacy. He hands one to Amy and pops the top on the other, guzzling it with pleasure as he looks to the camera cheesily and says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Even Aimz is aware of the of fizzy and TAHITIAN-EY taste of TAHITIAN TREAT, the only drink sponsored by PCW's Crusierweight champion, RENT-A-HERO!! Remember everyone, TAHITIAN TREAT, the only drink that'll make you sneak one out of your girlfriend's parents refrigerator!' *Busts a cheesy smile as Amy sighs in the background*Aimz:'We can't even go on vacation without him being cheesy...' Rent-A-Hero:'I hope I didn't confuse my want with your want sweetness..' Aimz: *eyeing soda, then back to Rent-A-Hero* 'Nooo...what could I POSSIBLY have wanted late at night, all alone, with you, in the dark, besides a Tahitian Treat?'Rent-A-Hero:'A late night game of Halo followed by a nightcap of Gummi Bears?' Aimz:'It's official...I'm dating a Barney incarnate...' That Next Morning.... ..::/::..The sun makes it's presence known as it rains down its rays upon the neighborhood streets where we come back to the house of the Campbell's in a perfectly normal Nova Scotian neighborhood. Inside, we focus in on the kitchen where we find a slender middle aged woman, in a light sundress with red flowers strewn all over it. This woman is the bearer of Amy Campbell, the woman who pushed the OSW superstar from her womb some many years ago and that woman is Beverly Campbell. Her beauty is an aged, fine beauty that only comes with years of life on earth...like a fine wine or a rare flower. She hum a heavenly hymn, the only other sound accompanying the sizzling in the kitchen as she merrily prepares breakfast. Batting back her short dark hair, she removes the frying pan from her gas stove and brings it over to the large table with a red and white checkered tablecloth laid over it, sliding off pieces of crisp bacon onto a plate with a paper towel covering it, the grease seeping through as the bacon cools. This accompanies a table with eggs, pancakes, and a tall, frosty glass of orange juice along with a simple white plate at each chair...four in all. Smoothing out her apron and cutting off the stove, she sits the frying pan down on an unheated range and beckons..::/::..Beverly:'BREAKFAST IS READY!!' ..::/::..Shortly, that call is met by the lumbering steps of none other than Amy's father...Forrester Campbell. He walks into the kitchen, a heavy man yet with a slightly muscular build as he fixes his black dress tie to go with his white dress shirt and black slacks, an obvious sign of a true business man. Sitting down at the table, he receives a kiss from his loving wife on the cheek as she fixes his plate with a large portion of each food item, Forrester obviously enjoying what he sees upon his plate as he gets ready to dig in with his knife and fork. But before he can begin to savor the homemade breakfast, he stops and asks inqusitively, fixing his glasses as a stren look overtakes his face towards his wife..::/::..Forrester:'Where's Amy and that Rent-A-Guy or whatever...I already don't like him, what kind of name is 'Rent-A-Whatever'?'Beverly:'Forrester please, you hate EVERY guy our baby tries to bring home. He seems like a nice boy...a little out there, but nice. I'm sure Amy wouldn't be with him without good reason.' Forrester:'Whatever you say dear...but I got my eye on him. Speaking of which, let me go check on my pumpkin.'..::/::..Forrester stands up, finally sinching his tie in tight as he exits the kitchen, turnign down the hall where family various pictures of the Campbell's through the years can be seen in evolving order ranging from the earliest of years to the present. He approaches the second door on the left, knocking on it gently and announcing to Amy in a soft manner..::/::..Forrester:'Amy..it's me honey. I'm coming in.' Aimz: *giggling and light moaning mixed in as she answers* 'Could you...hehe....wait just aaaaaa.....minute dad.' ..::/::..Suddenly, Forrester snaps, noticing the changing tones in Amy's voice which alerts him of foul play within the room. You can see his face construed in anger as he yells..::/::.. Forrester:'HE'S IN THERE ISN'T HE!! LET ME IN, I'M GONNA KILL HIM!!' ..::/::..Forrester focuses all his energy and slams into the door, the door swinging open with force as he rushes in, legs outstreched and arms open as if he was about to tackle someone as he looks about the room, Aimz standing there in a tightly wrapped towel and her hair dripping wet as she is shocked by her father's forceable entry. He looks about to find the room in order and no signs of our spandex enwrapped hero, Aimz standing in the face of her father with a haughty pose as she says..::/::..Aimz:'Looks like your back to treating me like I'm still in high school huh?'Forrester:'Umm...I'm sorry dear...I just thought well you know...'he' was in here.' Aimz:'He' has a NAME you know daddy.'Forrester:*clenches his teeth* 'RENT-A-HERO SWEETIE...' *fakes a smile.* 'Sorry...but you know I got strict rules about you two being alone in a room together...there'll be none of that int his house. Sorry for all that back there. I just wanted to let you know I'm gladd your back and welcome home baby...I missed you.' *Sees the warm smile overcoming his daughter's face, Forrester also returning the smile* 'Well umm...breakfast is ready, you better call 'Rentie' so you two can eat....' *mumbles* 'I hope Beverly left those eggshells in his pancakes like I wanted...'..::/::..Forrester shortly exits, still taking a slight probe of the room as he leaves, the door slowly closing behind him. Aimz takes in a sigh of relief, going over to the cozy little bed in the corner of her room and pulling up the covers, revealing Rent-A-Hero laying as flat as a board so as not to be caught with the full Green Lantern suit on now. Aimz giggles before once again kissing our hero, looking to him and saying..::/::..Aimz:'the one tiem you actually lay on your back when I tell you to...it's cause you've gotta hide, isn't it ironic.'Rent-A-Hero:'I swear...your father guards you better than the WWF guards RVD from breaking that glass ceiling. Does he always hulk up in situations where your with me?' Aimz:'Unfortunately yes...and you better hope we don't get caught or he's gonna have your head for a basketball.'Rent-A-Hero:'Now don't you worry Aimz...you know I wouldn't risk anything to get destroyed before my meeting with the infamous Juan Borro. I've got a PCW Crusierweight title to defend remember? That means I've GOTS to stay 100% so I can battle him for the fullest and show him who the true champion of the Crusierweights is. I've got more moves than Michael Jackson and N'Sync combined and I'm gonna show him as many as it takes in order for me to hold onto what's rightfully mine. Juan might be an ace at soccer...but in the ring, he's no Superman. All I've gotta say is he better bring everything he's got PLSU a reserve supply of Energon, because when it's all said and done and his gravity is defied by yours truly...I shall leave with two things...a victory over the man who has beaten me twice and the most VOLCANIC belt in the PCW right now...The Crusierweight title. Now can you comprehend that Amy?' Aimz:'If I knew what it meant...I sure probably would. Anyways, I gotta get finished drying up so we can eat breakfast...but first...I sure would like to start my day off with a few more of those kisses.' *smiles* Rent-A-Hero:'WOOHOO KISSES!! I'm starting to like these better than clear Gummi Bears.' ..::/::..Our hero leaps out of the bed and wraps Amy in his arms, kissing her softly as they recommence their romantic espionage withing the hosue of the Campbells. Amy leans in lovingly to her hero as their kiss gets deeper, having him backed to the window further into it. SUDDENLY, the door flings open, Aimz hearing it alertly leading to her first instinct so as not to get her lover in trouble...PUSHING HIM OUT THE WINDOW!! She shoves him with all our might, our hero not knowing what hit him as he flops out the window at the split second that Forrester steps in to once again check on Amy..::/::.. !!~GYAAAAAAH~!! !!~THUD~!! !!~OWWWIE~!! Forrester:'What just happened?'Aimz:'Oh nothing...just taking a look outside.' ..::/::..With that said, Aimz abruptly shuts the window, the camera going outside where we find Rent-A-Hero caught up inside a large hedge, dangling from it as he looks to the camera as if he's just been in a terrible fight and weakly beckons..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'That does it...I need to learn how to fly...'..::/::..With that said, our hero limps his head back as the scene begins to fade, not even have the energy to pull himself out as the scene ends.
