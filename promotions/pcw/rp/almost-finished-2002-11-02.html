Date: 11-2-02 An odd smile spreads across Brandi’s face as she sits at her desk. A pile of photographs lay scattered in front of her. She looks down at them, attempting to process the information they contain in with the information stored inside her head. She holds two pictures up, side by side and looks each one over carefully. Brandi is careful not to overlook any detail. Everything must be taken in. One picture is bright and cheerful. It is a large group photo, taken outside on the grass. In the background is a small building. It’s obvious that the building has seen better days, but no one seems to mind. They’re just happy to be there and to have each other. The other picture has an ominous looking building. The three story building blocks most of the sun out of the picture, but is new and clean looking. It is also a group picture. However, instead of the smiling faces of children, this photo features large, scruffy looking men wearing hardhats. The men who created the building. After studying each picture closely, Brandi sets the cheery one with the children back down on her desk and brings the picture featuring the new building close to her face. She holds the photograph at different angles, as if she expects the building to pop out at her. Finally she sets it back down and gives a smile of approval. Brandi- I’ve waited a long time for this. Well, actually I haven’t. But in some ways I have. I mean, watching such a work develop from a shabby building that no one cared about into this massive building that will soon be filled with people. This one building will change the lives of so many for the better. Brandi looks down at the faces of the smiling children. She leans back in her chair and closes her eyes as though trying to remember something she’s seen. Brandi- They tried so hard to keep me from it. It almost worked. I never would have thought that they would all insist on standing in front of that stupid little shack as the wrecking machine started up. Never would have imagined that they would continue to stand there as it roared closer and closer to them. They were determined to go down with their home. Hoping that it would be their one little chance to save that worthless land. Her eyes open and again she looks into the faces of those children, no look of sympathy on her face. Instead, a chuckle wells up deep inside her and slowly makes its way out. Brandi- You ignorant little fools. It didn’t work, did it? I got what I wanted and you got sent back to where you belong. Scrounging around on the streets, looking for a place to stay or perhaps a bite to eat. Her train of thought is interrupted by a knock on the office door. Nathan steps inside carrying a piece of paper in his hand. He stands in front of Brandi’s desk and reads over it carefully, as if not wanting to make any mistakes. Nathan- A man just called. Said his name was Joe and that he did the work for you on the mall. He wanted to know if you got all the pictures you requested and also to let you know that for some reason the check you gave him isn’t going through. Brandi- That’s no surprise. I told Mr. Walden to close that account right after giving the check to Joe. He didn’t work like he said he would. He wasted a lot of my time. Because of him and his lazy crew, the shops in the mall are just now beginning to set up. Nathan- You did it on purpose? Brandi- Of course I did it on purpose! That’s what you get when you hire a bunch of hicks to do a job. Just because I had them working weekends and overtime and everything doesn’t mean they should slack off. Surely the job could have been finished sooner. Nathan- You’re kidding. Brandi- No, I’m not. Now, is that all Nathan? I really do have some work I should be doing. Nathan- What’d you have to do? Brandi- Business stuff. Nathan- You always say business stuff! You never want to tell me anything. I’m not an idiot. I can comprehend things if you would just give me a chance to. Brandi- Calm down, would you? I didn’t mean anything by it. I just have some paperwork I need to go over. A few contracts need looking over. I have a couple of tapes I need to watch for Turmoil. Oh, could you run and get me some paperwork from Mr. Walden? I do believe that he has something for me regarding Tomas appearing on Tuesday. Nathan- Yeah, I’ll go get it right now. Brandi- Thank you. As Nathan leaves the room, Brandi’s attention is diverted towards the far wall. It is decorated with pictures of her with the Cruiserweight and Extreme titles. A few Front Office pictures rest in the center, and a frame off to the side contains a picture of Nathan. She gets up and walks over to them. Brandi- Tag team gold next? Another picture to add to the wall, or perhaps just another thing to conquer. Two great things handed to me at once. A shot at both HIT and Tomas, plus a probable shot at Tag Team gold. Brandi’s eyes focus on the center picture of the Front Office. Brandi- That is of course, if the titles don’t fall into the hands of someone worthy within the next couple days. She pauses for a moment on the picture of Nathan and smiles briefly before turning her attention back to her desk and the piles of paperwork and sighs. Brandi- I swear these stacks never get any smaller. I suppose that they will only get bigger from now on. With the recent injuries and stipulations going on around here, I’m beginning to wonder how I’ll manage to get it all done in time. Brandi gathers up a few loose pieces of paper and shoves them into a folder. She takes the folder under her arm and starts out the door, flicking the light switch off on her way out. Brandi- Well, there’s always tomorrow. With that, she closes the door behind her and lets darkness incase the room. On the desk, the eyes of the children stare out blankly into the empty room.
