Date: Oi. “A toast!” Everyone stands, including the tuxedo-wearing members of the audience and those at the head table. Big Alan Steele beams, also dressed to the nines and raising his glass. The grinning toastmaster with the smooth, bald head looks over at Steele and grins widely, nodding to Big Al in tribute. His eyes lock into Big Al, a slight narrowing of his eyes, almost in… contempt? He whirls back to the audience, and makes a grand gesture. “Yes, a toast to you, Big Alan Steele,” Lex Luthor spits out in malevolent glee, “A toast to your DOOM!! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!” Steele brings the glass to his lips, the words not really sinking in. Soft snicks are heard in the audience… all of whom now leap at Steele, tuxedoed men flying through the air, brandishing katana… We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he rests rather uncomfortably in his bed at Stately Steele Manor. His body is covered in ugly bruises. His face is completely swollen black and blue, while various Graces gently administer ice packs. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum looks on, obvious disgust registering on her face, while The Masochist bolts into the room, breathing heavily. “Omigod!” The Masochist manages to gasp out between breaths, “What happened!” Sue takes a drag on her cigarette, nodding at Steele. “I see they let you keep all your teeth this time. That’s good, because I hated crawling around in the dirt looking for them before.” Big Al beams, then grimaces, the beaming causing him a jolt of pain. “Tis quite simple,” he manages to croak, “Today was Big Alan Steele day!! Hi-Keeba!” He punctuates his ‘Hi-Keeba’ by coughing up a lungful of blood. The Masochist looks back as Sue for confirmation, which she affirms by passing him a formal ‘invitation’ to the ‘gala event.’ This seems to only agitate The Masochist. “I just don’t get it,” he says, shaking his head sadly, “How could you just walk into such an obvious trap then? How could you let them beat on you without even raising a single fist in retaliation or defense? How?!” The Masochist finishes by looking poignantly at Sue, who only looks with rolling eyes towards Big Al, who, by the way, is still smiling ear to ear. “But do you not see, Lord of Pain? Twas not a trap but a reward!! A reward for being as righteous as I can be… and I could not be more pleased!” “Well, okay then. I’ll bite,” spits out a now angry Masochist, “If you knew the whole time and everything, then what is the deal?” “Tis as such… the villains in the city of San Jose, in fact the entire world over, deemed me worthy of the complete and total ire, which, conversely, is their emotive of respect and admiration. You see, young Lord of Pain, the nefarious criminal element knows only a world of hate and pain. Thusly, should they hate someone the most, they respect that person the most!! So, they award me each year of their affections, showering me with fist beatings! A noble gesture by evil, to be sure!! That is why they hold such lavish affairs all in honor of the son of Zeus!!” Sue takes a drag on her cigarette, “So Al, which abandoned warehouse was it this time?” “Warehouse no. 41, on the West Pier.” The Masochist continues his protests, “But how…” Big Al frowns, “Admittedly, they were stealthier than one could imagine…” “Moron!” Sue yells, “They were NINJAS! The whole ‘audience’ you had there were nothing but a blood-thirsty pack of ninjas!” “They were wearing tuxedoes…” Big Al puts forth, meagerly. “I would have thought the facemasks covering their identities would have given them away, tux or no tux.” “Oh, Al…” Tsks the Masochist. Big Al tries to look dignified, despite his obvious momentary shortcomings, “Well, I felt that such an honor befitting a hero such as I deserved my presence! Tis a rare and precious thing, indeed!” “God, your ego may know no bounds,” snarls a livid Sue, “This is the 7th time in two and a half weeks they’ve done this to you! Lex Luthor isn’t trying to honor you… he’s trying to KILL you! And why SHOULD he come up with a better plan? If I were him, I’d go to the well as many times as I could until it was dry too.” Big Al nods, bringing a quick look of pain across his face. “But you do not understand! In their world, you honor a hero by wishing them great amounts of agony… and only I, Big Alan Steele, am given his own day on the Roman calendar! Hi-Keeba!!” Masochist shakes his head. “Unbelievable.” “The trophy… was it still serviceable?” Sue polishes off her smoke. “The doctors managed to recover most of it from where the sun doesn’t shine, but I doubt you’d want to display it anymore. But now onto business, as in are you going to be ready for tomorrow, Al? I don’t want you hindering Duke out there…” “Hinder?! HINDER?!?! By the gods, woman! You do me a great disservice!! I am the fastest of healer and shall be MORE than ready and definitely willing, or I am not the anointed son of Zeus!! Hi-Keeba!!” He thumps his chest for effect, eliciting groans. The Masochist stares into space, a sarcastic look on his face. “Wow… you think that… that maybe someday I could achieve such an honor?” Steele smiles warmly and places a fatherly hand on his shoulder, the sarcasm zipping by his ear. “I know you can… just stand by my side. Hi-Keeba!!”
