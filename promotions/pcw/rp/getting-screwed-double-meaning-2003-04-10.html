Getting screwed..Double meaning

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/04/2003 “Dodge don’t you dare feed me that crap!” Jade spoke bluntly in a harsh tone as her head leant back over the arm of a large black leather recliner. “Shut up!” The man on the other end of the phone is clearly getting on her nerves. “Now that you have managed to keep your filthy mouth shut for one minute, I’ll tell you exactly what I want from you. Unless, you want your nice little taxi firm in pieces dear I suggest you may wanna tell me. Phone number for ‘Books’ now.” Jade picks up a thick Cuban cigar from the ash tray next to her and places it between her plum shiny lips, taking a long sweet puff. “Don’t tell me he does not exist, that is not what I want to hear.” Jade takes a slight pause as the voice on the other end squeaks. “You know you’re right. I would not want to do that to you under normal circumstances but this is desperate. If I lose my standing, my reputation in the underworld I will not have anything left.” She takes another long inhalation of the cigar. “You know I barely have a penny left to my name even after my very healthy PCW contract and well I am sure I could come to some arrangement with ‘Mikey’ The switch, I hear things have been going rough for him too lately and well, I could be just the business he needs to get things flowing again.” Another small pause. “I’ll tell you what, I’ll give you a minute to think things through; this is probably making you think a whole bunch of crazy thoughts. Oh and don’t run away! Nathaniel is right outside with a shooter and one hell of an itchy trigger finger.” Jade giggles to herself as she puts the phone down, giving her time to think about Rojer ‘Dodge’ Vantham. Jade leant against the wall and placed her foot up against it. She had been warned dozens of times before that she should not be caught screwing about with him, he had friends in very high places and well it was best left alone. He was the running boy for some big head in the hierarchy of underground illegalities. Dealt with things like transport for massive scaled heists and bank robberies. He even arranged the transport in things like the first ever successful Vegas robbery. That was a robbery to be proud of. Now Jade was not to be intimidated by power, if she got in a good punch down she could get off on it. Especially the mood she was in, her plans were to just go over her filthy cousin Mani’s head and squash him when she could look down on him. The phone rang. Jade pushed its silver case against her ear and brushed the hair out of her face; she takes one last drag out of the stumpy cigar and squashes the tip between her thumb and forefinger. There is a beep as she answers, “Hello?” Jade grinned as she heard agitated foul speak coming from the other end of the phone. “I see you decided to test my theory on Nathaniel being outside the door huh? Listen to me fucker! You’re lucky he didn’t take out both of your knee caps!” Jade grunted as she sat back down atop a plaid puffy tartan cushion. “Now tell me. Are you going to speak? Well I really hoped that I would not have to do this to you Dodge but you leave me little choice.” Jades grin fades as she seemingly goes red in the face. The mans pitiful blubbering and begging for his life had Jade in an uproar. “You think I am going to waste my precious money getting your places put into a million tiny burning pieces?” Jade hisses. The man on the other end of the phone calms down, he thinks he is safe now and that Jade was bluffing. “No, that would be stupid of me, and well that is exactly what you think I am, isn’t it you rat bastard?!” She gets a reply which makes her even more mad, steam almost pouring from her ears. “No, not at all. I’m just going to get in touch with a friend of mine who has contacts with the Russian mafia. I’m sure that they would love to get their hands on the slime that jacked 10 Mil of their coke, wouldn’t you?” Jade shakes her head as she once again hears the deplorable ramblings of the man on the other end of the phone. “Sure I would, it’d save me money anyways wouldn’t it? That knee capping you just received will be a walk, no limp, in the park for you when they have done” “I don’t have to do anything for you Dodge this is the way the whole thing works. I bribe you to get me something I need, shoot you in the leg when you attempt to run off and then you do it. I am not going to fall into any of your underhanded stipulations to me getting that phone number. No way! No! That’s just disgusting, what on earth makes you think I would stoop so low as to go on a date with Bobby Juno! What do you mean ‘why?’ Eww. No. Well how the fuck would I know somebody who would want to go out on a date with him. They’d have to not even have heard of him. His weight problems are renowned, and his eating habits, it is too gross to even consider! No deal. It is impossible! Nobody I know is stupid enough to go with -him- on a date!” Jade huffed, knowing he would probably now have to be killed. “Is everything OK in there Jade, I’m pretty sure it is smoke!” Lacuna calls, a hopeful look shines into Jades eyes. “Give me his number and I will call you in 30 minutes. Have stitch take care of it. I don’t care how much it’ll cost you! Keep the phone with you I may have someone.” Jade beeps the phone and ends the call before getting up and walking towards the door. Jade grips the brass handle in her hand and yanks the door open. Lacuna falls onto her hands and knees and looks up at Jade. “Something interesting sweetie?” Jade looked slightly bemused as Lacuna looks up at her grinning and shaking her head. “Good, I think your gardeners are burning something out back anyways so…perhaps that’s what you could smell?” Jade offers her hand to Lacuna and yanks her up to eye level. “Oh, and I have a surprise for you sweetie!” Jade enthuses. “I know you’re all frigid and stuff so I don’t expect you to agree to this.” Unbeknownst to Lacuna Jade was manipulating her as the two walked down the corridor leaving from Jades bedroom and into the lobby. Jade grasps Lacuna’s hand and holds it firmly as they walk down the large steps. “I had planned to set you up on a blind date. Now don’t go all flippy on me like I know you are. I am gonna be there the whole time, in a corner of the restaurant so things don’t get too hectic.” Jade sighs, in all falseness. “Of course your frigidity won’t allow you to do that.” “You think I am frigid?” Lacuna looks obviously offended and pulls her hand away from Jades. Jade blushes and lowers her head enough so that Lacuna does not see her smirking slightly at the corners of her mouth. “Jade dear I am sooo not bloody frigid! I’ll prove it to you, infuriating little woman you are sometimes. ” Lacuna huffs back down the corridor. “I’ll bloody show you that I am certainly not frigid, Eugh you are such an ignorant little girl sometimes.” As Lacuna speeds off Jade gently flicks her hair out of her face the palm of her hand and taps her chin with her fingers. Jade leans back against the expensive white washed walls and flips her phone up against her ear. “Yeah. You have yourself a deal. Yeah it was nice doing business with you too. Now give me the number. Yeah right here.” Jade pulls out a pen and paper from the pocket of her black cargos and press it against her knee as she kneels down. “OK got it, next time we speak hopefully it won’t end with you getting shot in the leg sugar. Ha ha! That’s a sure thing.” Jade rose back to her feet and began rushing towards the bedroom door of Lacuna. “I know the perfect outfit for this date honey!” Jade was more excited than Lacuna was, getting this number could really set her on the right path. Financial ecstasy flowed through her veins as she pushed open the door to Lacuna’s bedroom. -[Fade]- Brook busily polished away in the fantastically huge hallway, whistling along to the radio playing that really old pop song, ‘Its raining Men.’ His body shaking as the brush beats away the dust of life which should have long ago rotted into these antique chairs, other maids tend to the flowers, carpets, marble flooring, the Van Gogh’s down the hall in the house. Jade bounces the first lot of the stairs of the split staircase and lands in a crouching position. The entire troop of maids and workers look up to the top of the stairs while Jade holds her toned arms out at her side. “Ladies and Gentlemen! Presenting to you on this marvellous April evening…Lacuna Devries!” Lacuna tugs at the red lined, black corset dress. Her painted fingernails cover her usually displayed torso as she holds her dress about an inch off of the floor. Her raven hair is tied up into neat tresses above her head and her eyelids are gently caressed with red remnants of eye shadow, her lips full of crimson colour as the rich beauty floats down her steps with an unearthly sound of grace. Jade giggles, “The red represents lust, the beauty of black shows that she is not easy, she is a slutty virgin. I think this is one of my better works! She hardly even looks frigid huh Brook?” Brook was fumbling to turn off the Camp track he was bopping to, to pay any attention to Jade at all. He just answers with a non complex answer of; “Indeed.” Lacuna gasps and attempts to pick up speed down the stairs and perhaps blast off one of her mighty shin kicks of death. It was lucky for her that Jade was nimble as she grabbed a hold of the back of Lacunas corset and let out a shocked yet pleased gasp as she looks to the wall which is covered entirely in mirrors. “Wow! Your boobs really look great when there is this much pressure on them, almost like you have some.” As Jade chuckles to herself Lacuna gets a little frustrated and tries to pull herself to her feet. “Stop squirming! Your wonder bra will come out of place. Hey Brook! You know why men call these things wonder bras?” Brook looks up with a curious, slightly worried look as he sees Lacuna dangling. “Cos’ when you open them up you wonder where the hell their tits have gone!” Brook has to bite his bottom lip to stop himself from bursting into a fit of laughter and Jade just chuckles away as she pulls Lacuna back to her elegant stance. Lacuna elbows Jade out of the way and Jade falls with an ‘ungh’ as she does so. “You gonna go into a mood again? Damn you Brits really are stuck up.” Lacuna spins on her heel and points her finger in the face of Jade. “Too far Jade! Little too far, the breasts are bad enough! I don’t want my country mocked, it means too much to me.” Jades face straightens as she walks down the stairs after Lacuna. Her hands go behind her back twiddling in her slightly nicotine stained thumbs a loose piece of her black mesh shirt and licks her own rouge lips. Brook stairs in awe at Lacuna’s radiant beauty and opens the front door for Lacuna after handing her a black silken sash which she wraps around her delicate yet shapely frame and melts into the beautifully woven piece of material. Jade taps Brook’s wrist as Lacuna steps out of the building. Brook hands her a Mercedes remote control ignition and she steps out of the building. “Nothing like a drive to get your senses going wild huh Lacuna?” Lacuna stops in her tracks and looks behind her, Jade flashes the keys at Lacuna and her hairs on the back off her neck instantly stand to attention. Lacuna stops long enough for Jade to catch up and hook her arm as Lacuna carefully grips the bottom of her beautiful dress. “You know I am driving right? I haven’t had a chance to put this baby through its paces yet and well, I am looking forward to it, a whole lot.” Jade throws herself over the driver side door and kicks open the door for Lacuna to get in the passenger seat. Lacuna reluctantly and timidly climbs into the seat and pulls the mirror down from the sun blind, checking her hair is stick perfectly in place. “Um, Jade?” Lacuna has a questioning tone in her voice, something Jade was perfectly prepared for. Jade looks at her as she slams the 4 x 4 car into reverse and the pedal meets the floor, Jade slides a pair of tinted shades up her delicate nose. “How, exactly did you find me this date?” Jade looks out of the rear view mirror to check nothing is coming for a while as she reverses down the huge gravel road which leads to the Mansion. Her hair is manipulated in the wind creating huge whipping brown and white effects in the air. “Don’t pretend you can’t hear me Jade!” Jades facial expression does not change but her attention does, even though she is doing approximately 40 mph, she finds time to look in the glove box and pull out a Mudvayne CD, she flashes it in the face of Lacuna. “This is real music sweetie not that 1994 shite some people like to pass of as it.” Jade grins, pressing the eject button on the CD player and tossing it out of the car. Lacuna stares at Jade utterly repulsed and miffed as the CD slides along the gravel silver face down. “Calm down hunny.” Jade beams as the first track to a Mudvayne mix comes on. Jades head start moshing crazily back and forward, her hair going into even more of a mess. Lacunas hand goes onto the steering wheel as she tries to steady Jade who is still picking up speed in reverse, they get closer to the gates. “Get your hands off of there.” Jade slaps Lacunas hand off of the steering wheel. “You bloody crazy woman focus on your driving!” Lacuna screams. Jade laughs as she runs her hands back through her hair, once in some kind of order she checks the rear view mirror in time to see the gates are open, with a quick inverted spin Jade forces herself straight and onto the main road from Lacunas estate. Lacuna squeals as the Michelin dry tyres rip against the floor, a barrage of smoke following behind them as Jade gains speed again. “And answer my question don’t be all evasive on me!” Lacuna orders, Jade nods her head and looks at her, her focus once again taken from the road. “A dating service.” Jade informs, Lacunas face looks totally perplexed her finger going to her chin as she pondered just how Jade would do that. Coming to no sensible conclusion at all Lacuna raises her brows and looks at Jade. “How the bleeding hell did you manage to do that without me taking any part in it whatsoever.” Jades hand goes from the steering wheel and to the back seat as she fiddles along its leather lavishness, her hand caressing the seat until her fingers manage to find the object of her search. A black wig falls between her finger tips and she pulls it into the front seat. Jade puts it on her head arranging the wig with both of her hands as the steering wheel is kept straight by her knees, their speed going well over the national speed limit. Once the wig is on her head Jade puts on her best English accent. “I’m looking for a date sunshine, blah blah blah.” Lacuna looks at Jade and Jade smiles at Lacuna, though she does not laugh at first in a second they are both laughing like wacky little school children.
