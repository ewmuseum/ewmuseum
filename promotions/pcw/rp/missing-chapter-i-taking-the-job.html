MISSING: Chapter I: Taking The Job?

undated

Date: MisSyNg: Chapter I “You are becoming very good at this!” It was true, I had a knack for teaching people to believe me. I was in the confession booth at the Hare Krishna community centre, like I usually was, in booth #17, talking to the same old missionary as always. I believed every word the man told me and trusted him quite well, without actually knowing his real name or identity. I had grown to know the man so well, that I was finishing off his sentences. I was talking about my match this Friday in a tag bout and I finished off his sentence, giving myself the confidence to win the match. It was a talent I had, and I was a natural. “You are a lot friendlier than some of the people I get come to me.” I didn’t know what he meant. I was ruthless in the ring, wanting nothing more than to make Overweighter punish. I guess niceness wasn’t sometime I had. “You know, ‘SyN’, you used to be an undercover detective, didn’t you?” My thoughts fled back to the time when I was called to be a detective. Called to stop a mysterious culprit set to blow New York a flame. When I finally got to the end to meet this evil man, it turned out to be my very own brother from Germany, who I had left behind on a trip to America when I was younger. I had defeated him then and if needed to, I would again. “Yeah, I used to.” I didn’t want to talk about it and yet, the missionary seemed like talking about it had a purpose or something. “Well, I normally don’t tell people this but one of our Krishna members like you just ran away and quit the club mysteriously. The funny thing was, he broke into the club and stole his personal record.” I was a little confused. “What’s the point?” “Well, the point is, when he broke into the place, he stole over four million dollars that the Krishna had saved up!” I had never known of any money that I had paid to the Krishna for anything so how could this person steal so much money? “Four million dollars! Where’d the Krishna get that money?! The missionary coughed and sighed really loud, as if he were trying to stall or something. After a few seconds of silence, he finally answer my question. “Oh, the Krishna saved that up years ago and put it into the bank.” His answer wasn’t very convincing and I didn’t buy it. “Ok, so why did I need to know this?” “Well, the four million dollars was everything to the Krishna and now the place is bankrupt. You have to find the thief! You’re the only member in our group that knows anything. Please, you have to do us a favour.” I didn’t know what to say. I would love to do the mission but with PCW, I didn’t know how my time schedule would work. “With PCW now, how can I do two jobs?” “Well, you have a lab top so you can have the files on your computer and use the lab top to locate this person first off. Any help would be appreciated.” It all fit together but do ANOTHER mission? I could do it! “Well, I can see what I could do. Do you have any information for me to start with?” The person on the other side of the curtain in the prayer booth laughed. “We have barely some information. Just one name that people around here remember, Ivan Chistov. He is foreign but that is all we have.” It certainly wasn’t much but it would give me some work. The Hare Krishna had given me some confidence and I could do more things now. “I’ll do it and see what I can find.” But what COULD I do?
