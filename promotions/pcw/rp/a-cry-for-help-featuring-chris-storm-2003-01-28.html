A Cry For Help (Featuring Chris Storm)

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 28, 2003 As the sun dipped down behind the pink clouds the commotion inside grew louder and louder as I did my best to try to plan out what I would do with the children once Chris and I went on our vacation to Paris. I felt guilty that I had to leave them behind but there was no other choice. But who could I leave them with? Surely not my mother. She would ruin the happiness of the trip by calling me up each day to rub in how much I shouldn’t have left the children, plus Chris and her were not exactly on the best terms. A nanny was out of the question. There was no way I was letting the children spend even a day with someone I barely knew. I paced back and forth across the living room carpet as it suddenly hit me. I knew exactly who I could call and being the nice guy that he was he’d of course say yes. I’d call Jackson Rust and hope that he would understand. Gah! Ever seen the old western movies where there is a stampede, and the cowboy gets trampled by charging buffalo? I envy him right now. Between Alex's teasing everyone, Christa's not able to pick what to wear, and Evan's running around the house naked, well I wish I could try and face a herd of cattle right about now. Well maybe just P.W.A., same thing. I continued to chase Evan through the house, holding a pair of clothes for him to put on. Meanwhile Christa had pulled out all of her clothes and had them laid all over the floor, trying to decide what to wear. Alex was thankfully dressed by now but was picking on Christa and laughing every time Evan ran by. I stopped when I noticed the mess Christa had made. I was about read to pull my hair out. Well I am a former World champ and Grand Slam champion. A rising star in PCW that is going for the tag team titles in just twenty four short hours. In a situation like this, there is only one thing I can do. 'TEEESSSAAAAA!!!!! HEELLPPP!' I could hear Chris’ voice crying for help upstairs but I figured that achieving as much he has in his life time he would be able to control a couple of kids within a matter of seconds so I lied a slight bit to him by calling out, “I’ll be up there in a second!” Instead of making my way over to the stairs though I walked over to the telephone and began running my finger down a piece of paper with a list of numbers on it, in search on Jackson’s numbers. As I ran my finger down towards the bottom of the list I finally found Jackson’s as a smile came over my face and I began to dial it up as I took a deep breath hoping that Jackson would be there to answer the phone. I had finally caught Evan and managed to throw some clothes on him, Christa had picked out her outfit and changed, and with a good ol' slap, Alex was under control. 'I would make such a good dad.' I laughed to myself. 'Heck I will make an awesome Tag team champion' I laughed. I put both hands on my hips, forgetting Evan was still in my arm. I suddenly felt something pulling on my pants leg. I looked down to see Christa. Her face was rather red. 'What's the matter Christa?' I asked. The little one stayed quiet. She only pointed in a direction. I turned my head to see what she was pointing at. There was a trail of clothes being left behind by Evan, who had someone snuck away from me. I let out a scream and quickly gave chase once more. Evan turned a corner and rolls down the stairs buck naked. Christa and Alex watched from between the bars upstairs, laughing the whole time as I chase Evan around the house. Tessa was sitting on the couch talking on the phone as Evan ran past her flashing her. I chased after him very embarrassed but quick on my heels “Come back here you little mud bug.' I laughed. I sat on the couch totally oblivious to my surroundings as a tuned out the world around me and tuned into the phone and the ringing on the other line. ...RING... ...RING... Evan ran by me naked, but it didn’t effect me until he turned around and started to run the other direction once Chris started to catch up with him. “Oh my god what on earth is going on?” I shouted as both Evan and Chris stopped dead in their tracks and looked up at me with the phone still pressed up against to my ears. Evan and I both point to each other. 'He did it' we repeat. Our heads turn and we look at each other. 'Come back here and put some clothes on you little nudie.' I shouted to him. That was Evan's cue to start running again. I started to give chase but the rug under foot decided it would slide and I fell flat on my face. Tessa just looked down and giggled as she pointed that she was on the phone. I hopped up like I meant to do that and ran after Evan. I could hear Alex and Christa cheering Evan on from upstairs. I cast my head up towards them. 'You two finish getting ready to go! As soon as I catch Evan and slap some clothes on him we are leaving.' I shouted. However that wasn't my smartest decision as looking at them caused my attention to turn away from the kitchen post that was right in front of me. THUD! I hit the ground like a rock. Alex and Christa sarcastically started to clap for me. I merely waved at them. Evan came walking back up to me. He just stood over and looked at me. My eyes focused on Evan. I quickly rolled over, 'Gotcha!'' I shouted as I went to grab him. Evan merely laughed and ran off before I could catch him. I could only watch as Evan ran back to where Tessa was. I got to my feet and ran after him. I dove at him to try and make a catch but Evan jumped into Tessa's lap. 'What the?' Tessa shouts as she looks at the naked Evan sitting in her lap. He lays his head down on her and gives her a hug. As the phone continued to ring I was interrupted by a pair of small arms wrapping themselves around my chest. I looked down to see Evan bare naked clinging onto me for his life. Raising his head quickly to look up at me with puppy eyes to get out of the trouble he knew he would be in his head hit my arm knocking the phone out of my hand and sending it tumbling to the floor. Just as it hit the floor I heard a voice on the other line answer. “Hello?” “Evan quick get up!” I shouted trying to gently shove him to the side so that I could grab the phone but much to my misfortune the commands fell deafly on the child’s ears as he continued to cling to me. Seeing Chris sitting back up just a few feet away on the living room floor I quickly shouted out. “Chris, get the phone quick!” But still being still somewhat out of it after the hard dive to the floor Chris was just as deaf to the shouting coming from my mouth as Evan and the voice called out once more. “Hello?” I pushed a little harder on Evan finally getting him onto the couch as I dived out towards the phone. “Jacks…” CLICK…BEEEEEP “GAH!” I shouted out in frustration as the only answer I got was the dial tone. I was too late and there was not much time left to get out to the restaurant so I couldn’t call back if I wanted to be ready on time. I simply placed the phone beside me on the ground before letting my head fall to the side and rest on the top of the coffee table. I pulled myself off the ground and grabbed Evan. 'Ha, got you know you little rascal.' I told him as a pushed my finger on his nose. I looked down at Tessa. 'Uh you ok Tessa Bear?' I asked. She merely lifted her head up and gave me this look. I gulped, I have a feeling it might become a handicap match against P.W.A. if I didn't shut my mouth up now. I took Evan upstairs to get him dressed. Alex and Christa walk back to their rooms, disappointed Evan was finally caught. I dropped Evan into his room and scooped up the clothes on the floor, handing them to Evan. 'Now be a good boy and get dressed. We are about to leave.' Evan nodded and shut the door. I smiled as I started my way back downstairs. Flopping down next to Tessa, I started to rub her shoulders. 'What's the matter?' I asked. “We are never going to get Paris at this point.” I sighed lifting my head slightly to look Chris in the eye. “Now don’t say that, I’m sure we will find someone to watch the kids for us.” He responded, patting me on the head and then standing up and walking out of the living room to finish getting ready himself. I sighed and forced myself to believe that Chris’ words would be true as I headed up after Chris towards my room to pick out an outfit; my only hope being that once I got home Jackson would still be home as well and have no problem watching a small group of out of control kids.
