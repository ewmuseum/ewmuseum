A long night

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/6/03 What causes a person to not sleep at night, the thoughts in ones head are always there and should not simply be enough to force someone to maintain consciousness. I had no real troubles on my mind, no more than usual. By some fluke and a lot of skill I was still the rising star champion, undefeated in quite a while and ultimately I was enjoying the whole experience of PCW so what was is it that’s keeping me awake, stopping me from getting the precious rest that I need. The sound of footsteps stomping on the metal grating echoed down the corridor and into James Edwards’ bedroom, despite the fact that his door was closed. If James had to find fault with the PCW airship at all it would be the decking, the designers choosing to use a metal grate structure as the platform for people to walk on where as while it was a practical procedure which insured the ship was light yet study, James would have given his right arm just to have a bit of insulated carpet installed, nothing fancy just something to stop the constant echo which seemed to engulf his room. Lying on his back in the darkness, James closed his eyes trying to shut out the noise, block off any outside distractions that may come to the forefront of his mind but to no avail. He stretched his arms high above his head arching his back as he did so a number of bones cracking causing James to wince slightly as he turned over onto his side and pulled the covers over his head once more with three words constantly running through his head for no apparent reason. Who needs sleep. Who needs sleep. Who needs sleep. James: I NEED SLEEP. James bolted upright in his bed, cold beads of sweat dripping down from his forehead shaking somewhat as he had just awoken himself somewhat unusually from his deep slumber. Glancing around the room trying to maintain some form of dignity, trying to rest his pounding heart James let out a loud sigh before pushing himself to his feet assisting the process by placing his hands on his knees as he did so. The soft carpet engulfed James’ toes and immediately began insulating them as he stepped cautiously through the darkness of his room on the airship making his way to the en-suite bathroom, arms outstretched searching for the door handle bending his fingers back slightly on it as he finally reached it. As he opened the door the dull hum of the extractor fan situated within the rest room whirred into life pre-empting the action most commonly associated with bathrooms. Still fumbling around in the darkness James flung his left arm out palming against the wall in a semi-conscious effort to find the light switch before finding it sending a surge of bright yellow neon light through the room causing James’ pupils to dilate to nothing more than pin heads. Stepping back squinting in an attempt to shield his eyes from the torture of un-natural light James waited for a moment trying to allow himself the time to adjust to his new somewhat brighter surroundings. Stepping into the room once more James leant down over the wash basin and turned on the tap, the sound of the water hitting the porcelain drowned out the gentle hum of the extractor as the basin began to fill up slowly with luke warm water, leaving James to simply stare forwards examining himself in the mirror in front of him. There were heavy bags under his eyes, his eyelids visibly drooping from the lack of sleep that he had been experiencing lately, his usually clean shaven face with the customary smile was no longer evident, a different man was staring back at him. A small layer of stubble had begun to encompass James’ face and the smile that he always carried to the ring had been replaced by a worried frown. It wasn’t the fact that he wasn’t sleeping that was getting to him; on the contrary James had been sleeping like a little baby until two weeks into the journey down South America. That was when the nightmares started. They always started the same way, he would be alone in the darkness in his room, lying there perfectly still then he would hear a noise, like the laugh of a child or the footsteps of another person down the corridor, then he would lie awake once more, trying to get to sleep but not being able to, the noise slowly taking over him reaching into the very pits of his soul until he woke up. It was worst when he heard the children, never knowing exactly what they were saying but knowing they were trying to tell him something. Something he couldn’t understand for the life of him. A steady flow of water began to make it’s way over the wash basin onto the tiled floor onto James’ toes dragging him out of the trance like state he appeared to be in. James glanced down at the basin and proceeded to turn off the hot water tap before draining some of the excess water away. Cupping his hands he splashed a relatively large amount of water onto his face causing him to let out a small sigh of contentment as the warm water seemed to albeit briefly take away from the distractions that were warping his mind. A steady rapping at the door grabbed James’ attention as he was drying his hands on a small green towel hung haphazardly on a small prong just beside the wash basin. He proceeded to leave the bathroom turning off the light behind him and shutting the door, the humming of the extractor still just audible but not enough to irritate James as he stepped into the darkness of his room once more, making his way cautiously to the main entrance to the room where the rapping was continuing to echo from. James: Alright Alright I’m coming. As James entered the final approach for the door the rapping stopped, James however continued sensing that whoever had been knocking had heard his call and was now patiently waiting his response however when he finally reached the door and pulled it ajar James was proved to be wrong as there was no-one there at all. He poked his head out into the corridor and looked left and then right not seeing or hearing anyone. Normally he would have stopped there, his natural instinct, his demeanour would have told him to turn around, enter his room, lock the door and attempt to go back to sleep. Instead however James stepped out into the corridor and began to step walk along the cold metal walkway, which dug into his bare feet. James: Hello…is anyone there? Gaz is this one of your stupid pranks? James shook his head, cherry knocking wasn’t Gaz’ style he’d wait until you opened the door and then stab a pencil up your nose or something before bounding away thinking it’s the most humorous thing ever. The rising star champion took a few steps forward down the corridor until it became apparent that someone was trying to follow his footsteps, taking a step as close as possible as to when he took a step, the give away sign was the shoes the clanged on the metal grille as they walked. James froze and tried to hear the sound of footsteps behind him but to no avail. Common sense should have stopped James from turning around to see what had decided to play a game of cat and mouse with him. Common sense should have told him to keep on walking and not to turn back no matter how large the temptation. Yet still James turned around and found himself looking down at a small female child who was looking up at James with large brown eyes and a coy smile on her face. Little Girl: I’ve been sent to get you James. James took a step back looking at the girl inquisitively who just stared blankly up at James, not seemingly responding to his glance. James: Excuse me? Little Girl: I told you, I’ve been sent for you. James stepped forward a few steps and knelt down in front of the little girl her eyes following James as he did so. Little Girl: Emily sent me to tell you that she’s ok. James: What…what are you talking about? Little Girl: The crash James…did no one tell you about the accident. A look of horror took over James’ face as the little girl turned and started to skip down the corridor, James rose from his crouched position and proceeded to follow as the small child disappeared around the corner of the corridor, as James reached and turned it however there was no-one there just a long expanse of corridor no little girl insight. James sighed and hit the wall to his right with a closed fist through frustration before turning back in the corridor and heading towards his own room once more closing the door behind him, locking it as he reached it. Stumbling forward in the dark once more James noticed that the bed had been made and that the humming from the extractor fan inside the bathroom was whirring gently, something it shouldn’t have been doing seeing as it had been turned off when James left the bedroom. Cautiously James moved his hands toward the handle of the bathroom lowering it slowly before pulling the door towards him. Before him stood his sister Emily staring directly into his eyes as James began to feel a lump in his throat grow, he hadn’t seen Emily in so long he had almost forgotten what she had looked like, he took a step forward his arms outstretched as though to give her a cuddle, as he took another step Emily’s mouth opened stopping James in his tracks. Emily: James…I… No sooner were the words out of her mouth Emily’s eyes rolled backwards into her skull as she dropped to her knees then face first onto the tiled floor her skull cracking with a sickening thud a large piece of metal jammed into the back of her neck with blood gushing out over the cream floor. James: Emily…Emily…EMILY! James awoke with a start sitting bolt upright in his bed. His alarm clock was beeping frantically as the red LED’s flashed the time of 10:00 at him. James: What the hell was that about?
