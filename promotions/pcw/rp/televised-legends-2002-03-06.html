Televised Legends...

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 March 2002 'There is no right and wrong...no good and evil...no light and darkness...only neutrality...a common ground...unity...harmony...and the Neo Void God...his job is to be the scale of the universe...balancing them both out.' ~~~Savage Tranquility, page undetermined Useless...it is all useless... We know Akiyama had no chance against double main event title matches against PCW veterans Miyagawa, Legend Inc, and Frisco...those titles were as good as lost... Backstage antics were all the rage lately...veterans of PCW's early days were entering just at the next generation was looking to take claim to the birthright they had fought and bled and sweated for since they even thought of stepping into that squared circle. Then they come back...like old disowned prodigal sons looking to reclaim what they had left... what they believed to be theirs... But just like a rented movie, ownership passes from after a certain time limit... Akiyama was raised to know battle tactics and strategy. So naturally...he could smell victory and defeat as strong as the scent in between Ellie's legs. He smelt it at the Madness...and he smells it approaching again soon. Be it on Fusion-Ha...or on Sabotage...one of those matches would end miserably. One way or another...a belt would be lost from the NVG. Akiyama was right about three being a magic number though... How can one person claim championship over such a vast field as television? There were so many aspects to claim, so many points and ideas to represent. Could Miyagawa really be such a person to embrace all of these media points? Maybe...maybe not...such a task would require definite unity within a being. And the major task given to the Neo Void God has been tailored exactly for this championship... Unity... 'Bring existence towards an collective harmony...' Cyclonica echoed through his head the tasks that Akiyama must deal with. There was a major point in beginning his mission of universal harmony, and that would have him begin in PCW...where there are so many fighters of talent doing battle. There's perfectly nothing wrong with battle, but when it is done in such a way that nothing but chaos is left...then disorder is left in the universe. Disorder brings pandemonium... Pandemonium brings complete annihilation. Nothingness... The same could be said about television. Through this magic box, many messages and ideals can be given and produced into the hearts and minds of society. This device can do it all...make us laugh, make us cry, make us happy, make us sad, stimulate us, and neutralize us...anger us... if you would, please...Imagine yourself...sitting in your cozy little recliner within your residence. In one hand is a nice cold can of beer, in the other, your remote control. You toiled and labored through out the day in anticipation of seeing PCW divas scantily clad and frolicking along the beaches of the Bahamas. Then, when you turn on the television, you find that the show's been cancelled for a newsbreak on the war on Osama Bin Laden. Your first reaction? Pissed... The anger's been instilled... Now you're all upset because you can't see the lovely luscious ladies of PCW all slick and wet in provocative positions in the sand. Your anger's going to spread into the most constructive way possible...domestic abuse...community vandalism...extreme violence...murder... All because Chef's boyfriend had to be an @sshole and cause some trouble...stealing away your entire means of American entertainment... Nippage... You still want someone like Miyagawa in charge of television at a time like this? Of course you do not... What you want is someone who's going to go right over there to Afghanistan and straighten out this entire mess that Bush can't clear up. What you want is the one person who can banish scum like him out of existence and off your TV. What you want... is Akiyama. But of course...you're not the only one... Open upon the park...exact park name disclosed at the time...it is night, and the moon shone bright upon the green trees below. Along the riverbank, shadows emerged to conspire against he who would be your TV savior. 'Are you here?' A woman...the voice familiar... 'Yes...where are your precious Slayers?' A man...also familiar... 'All around you, fool.' The voices hissed out through the brush of the foliage above. The larger of the two shadows on the ground turned his head towards the leaves, having been caught off guard. He looked back to the smaller, more lithe shadow. 'Cute...aren't you sneaky with your squad?' he sneered. 'You're not the only one.' The womanly shadow looked to the waters of the river, where heads began to pierce the water's surface one by one under the natural spotlight of the moon. She nodded slowly. 'At least you're learning something from knowing me, Legend.' His identity was revealed. Legend...underground leader of the mercenaries known as the Legends. It was unfamiliar as to why he and his team were named the Legends...but if they were so legendary, wouldn't their exploits be known? Moving along... 'Spare us with your bland compliments, Mai. Just tell me why you brought us here?' Mai Miyamoto...also called the Slayer in Japan. She headed another merc group called the SlayerS...short for Slayer Squad. In her own context, she was a legend in her field as well, but the reason on why she was is also unknown. Her voice cleared. 'Well...we both know that we've been chasing after the same little prize for the last couple of weeks. Akira Akiyama…the buyer wants this guy in a bad way, and he's proved how far he's willing to go.' 'True. If he went to the lengths of hiring both your team and my team…then he wants him real bad.' Slayer's outlined showed her putting her hands on her hips. 'Well…since we've both been dragged into this mess…might as well make the best of it.' “We can’t get this guy all the way to Japan. It’s almost near impossibe because of his fighting skill, as well as his powers.” Legend replied. “Well since we can’t transport him ourselves...why not have someone else do it?” Legend stared at her hard through the darkness. “What are you saying? We get Moroboshi to handle Akiyama? There’s no way! Moroboshi’s dead!” 'And there is one other way to guarantee a delivery to the buyer.' she announced. “Moroboshi was the only one who could pull this job off! There isn’t anyone else!” They both grew quiet for a second. Then Slayer spoke up in a hushed voice. 'Portalis.' Legend nodded. A sigh escaped her lips. 'If you release one of them, then you release them all, do you realize that?' 'Hey, if it's the only way we can bring this man down, so be it. We're willing to deal with the consequences for the money that's on the line here.' There were murmurs from both the teams hiding in the trees and the waters. The air grew thick with the tension building up on the critical decision about to be made. Then… Legend stretched out her hand… 'For the money…' Slayer took his palm in hers… 'We're going to be legends…' And from that day the preparations were set into place. Both of these two forces were destined to collide against each other…where a worthy prize was deemed for the taking. On one side there was the Legends…and the SlayerS… And on the other…Akiyama… But if we were to put it into bigger conflicts…on one side there was Miyagawa… And on the other…still Akiyama… >>>The scene opened to show Akiyama appearing on the Ananda Lewis show. He walked onto the set and traded smiles, hugs, and kisses with the vivacious host. 'Ananda…the Void Banishment is the thing sweeping the world. It's inching its way into every aspect of society.' Akiyama is shown stepping up to a plate. Before him, Greg Maddox lobbed a scorcher in his direction. Akiyama stepped back and swung on the ball…with the HAND OF FATE!! The ball soared out of the park, drawing whistles from nearby Nomar Garcia-Lopez, Sammy Sosa, and Mark McGuire. 'BOY! That ball really got banished!!' Nomar exclaimed. 'It probably broke the Void barrier!!'…And with that, the MLB logo appears on the screen.Another scene showed Akiyama standing before a payphone. A pedestrian came running up to him begging to use the phone. 'Are you going to dial 1-800-COLLECT?' When the lanky teen shook his head, the god bellowed in disappointment. 'We banish the fool who doesn't dial 1-800-COLLECT! Do you want to be banished alongside that freak of nature Carrot Top?' the teen dropped on his knees, wailing apologies and promises to dial 1-800 COLLECT. Akiyama nodded. 'Very good…now go forth, child. Dial…WITH STYLE!' Alyssa Milano walked up to Aki, and he wrapped an arm around her. 'We're going to fill HER Void.'Akiyama appeared yet again, this time on ElimiDATE. He was shown standing around with three other men, all of suave stature at a movie theater. The hot sexy chick across from them smiled before turning around to grab their popcorn. When she looked back...only three remained. Going back to get the drinks, she found only two left this time. With one final trip, she returned with the candy...and all that was left was Akiyama...sitting alone in the darkness of the theater. She looked around, then at him with a questioning look. The NVG only shrugged before slipping his arm around her and grinning into the camera.Next scene...showed Akiyama socializing and hobnobbing with Hollywood superstars Whoopi Goldberg, Ted Danson, and Tom Selleck. All three of them are legends of TV and film...legends...yet where are they now? Whoopi is starring on a game show, Hollywood Squares...how sad...Danson and Selleck...they’ve dropped off the face of the earth. Banished...if you will...
