Date: Dec.6th/02 Still, I think about it . . . and still I realize that thinking about it won’t help . . . I still try not to think about, and still the more I try, the more I think about it . . . It’s still a vicious cycle that I must break . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ DAMN YOU AMULET! No, I have to stop thinking about that son of a bitch . . . I SHOULD HAVE BEEN THE EXTREME CHAMPION! Gotta stop thinking about the past . . . STUPID AMULET . . .WHY COULDN’T HE BUG SOMEONE ELSE FOR A CHANGE!? Gotta start thinking about the future . . . I’M GONNA PUT AN END TO AMULET’S IDIOCIES! Dammit, I just gotta put up with this shit for one match . . . it can’t be that hard can it? I’ve done it before . . . but we lost . . . THIS HAS GOT TO STOP! But when . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ??? ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Those were the first thoughts that went through my head when I walked out my door. They were just the thoughts that I needed to rid myself of. I had hoped my little stroll through downtown Grand Rapids would help me focus on the task at had . . . and what I had to do to ensure victory over those two nincompoops, Vilas Lavagna and El Romantico. You’d think with names like those, they’d be push overs . . . but if there is one thing I’ve learned, it’s to never underestimate your opponents. I’ve done that too many times, and just look at my win/loss record. Not pretty, eh? Nope, didn’t think so . . . But if there is one other thing that I’ve learned, it’s that what happened in the past is exactly that . . . in the past. I can’t let my past failures jeopardize my future . . . and I can’t let my thoughts for the future jeopardize all I’ve done in the past. It’s all just a lose/lose type of paradox. You can never win if you focus on any other time than the present. Mark Amulet and myself are enemies . . . but that’s in the past, and in the future . . . but for right now we are partners and that is something I’m gonna have to live with for now. DAMN YOU AMULET! He’s ruined everything . . . I just can’t escape that fact . . . and Amulet knows that. He has a distinct advantage over me, knowing that he can keep his cool when you confront each other. Son of a bitch . . . God I hate that man . . . But I gotta get over it . . . cuz the only one I’m hurting with these dispositions is myself . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As I navigated my way around the busy sidewalks, I couldn’t help but realize that Mark Amulet is ruining my life. He is all I can think about . . . I just keep repeating myself over and over and over again . . . the words “I hate Mark Amulet” replay in mind none stop, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As I rounded a corner, I noticed a little commotion in front of a toy store. As soon as I saw the sign in front of the store, I knew what the problem was. - - HARRY POTTER AND THE CHAMBER OF SECRETS! NEW TOYS SOLD HERE! - - That damn kid . . . if there was anyone I hated more than Amulet, it was that damn wizard. Kids shouldn’t be idolizing some make believe movie character . . . they should be idolizing real life athletic heros, like Michael Jordan, Bo Jackson, or Mark Michigan. This presented me with a whole new problem . . . I had a severe problem with egotism. I hated anyone who took the spotlight off me. What’s the problem with wanting to be famous and looked up to? What’s the problem with wanting to be the center of attention? Why can’t little children look up to me as they do Mark Amulet or Jon Kano? What’s wrong with Mark Michigan? Suddenly, two kids broke away from the horde of children . . . both of them had their hands on a Harry Potter doll, trying to rip it away from each other. As they fought, I could see them glaring into each others eyes. Then, something I never expected to happen, did happen. One of the kids, a young boy about the age of twelve, released the doll allowing the other child to run away with it. The young boy then turned away from the store and began walking in my direction. As he stumbled by me, I stopped him and asked him why he had let go of the toy . . . “I guess I didn’t want it as much that other guy” . . . then he walked away with his head down, and his feet scuffing along the pavement. I couldn’t believe my ears! He had just given up . . . just like that . . . didn’t even try . . . but why? Then I remembered his words . . . which started me thinking. The reason why Amulet has gotten the better of me in the past . . . He simply wanted it more than I . . . His fake note from Xavier . . . his stupid best three out of five contest . . . he just wanted to prove how much more he wanted victory! He did what he had to . . . He pulled out all the stops . . . I did nothing . . . I didn’t even try . . . And therein lies my real problem . . . and therein lies my solution . . . Amulet won’t get the best of me anymore . . . He will pay for his wrongdoing . . . Just like Lavagna and Romantico will pay for thinking they could beat us . . . Maybe now, with a glimmer of enthusiasm, a Michigan/Amulet alliance could work . . . Maybe it won’t be so hard to live with for now . . . But not for long . . . for a certain man’s come-up’ins will soon . . . come up!
