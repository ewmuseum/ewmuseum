'Honey, I'm home'!!!!

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/2002 ‘Hey honey... I’m home.’ *Those are the four words that Chris Sloboda thought he would never murmur again. After being imprisoned for something he didn’t do, he almost never got the chance to return to Eustis Florida, to see his daughters and his lovely wife. Life in prison was hell enough. But life without seeing his kids grow up, or spending another anniversary or birthday with his wife Stacey was worse. But, those thoughts perished when Mexi showed up in court, with the evidence to save Boda. Now, after getting screwed at Turmoil, Chris has flown home to Eustis, and in the middle of the night, walked into the bedroom he shares with his wife, and awoken her with a surprise kiss.* Stacey: “Chris?” Boda: “Well, who were you expecting?” *Stacey opens her eyes, and smiles at the site of her husband. She rolls completely over, and touches Chris’ face with her hand.* Stacey: “What are you doing here? It’s the middle of the night. I didn’t think you’d be home so soon.” *Stacey moves over, leaving room for Chris to sit on the bed next to her. Chris plops down, the bed squeaking slightly under the monster size of Boda.* Stacey: “So, are you okay?” *Stacey had little knowledge of the past five days of Chris’ life outside the home. She saw on the nightly news that he was arrested, and detained for attempted murder. But, outside of the one phone call she received from Brad [Chris’ lawyer], she knew very little. Of course, her curiosity is getting the better of her, and she itches to know what exactly went down. Chris lies down, and tells the story, ending it with Mexi saving the day with the tape of Mona setting him up.* Stacey: “That little bitch. Why you? Why screw up your title opportunity?” Boda: “I don’t know. I don’t get it. But, I’m over it Hun. I’ve got only to focus on the goal at hand... and that’s the pay per view on Sunday. Speaking of which.....you and the girls are flying to St. Louis with me, right?” Stacey: “Sure, why would I miss my husband becoming the World Champion.” Boda: “Great.” Stacey: “Maybe Mona will show up, and I’ll teach her about messing with you.” Boda: “Listen Hun.... you handle my light work, like Miyagawa, and Akiyama. Let me handle Mona. She’s a loose end, and I think I’ll tighten her up at NTICW’s PPV next month right here in Orlando.” Stacey: “Okay [yawns]... if you say so. I just don’t like the idea of her messing around, like that, and costing me to lose you for so long.” Boda: “Hey, it’s all right Hun. I’m over it, for now. I want to just think of one thing, and one thing only.... winning the title at March Madness. Nothing else matters at this point. Okay Hun.” *Chris bends over, and kisses Stacey on the forehead. She snuggles in the blanket, and closes her eyes. Chris gets off the bed, and walks to the door, turning off the light. Stacey murmurs out to Chris.* Stacey: “You just got home, where you going?” Boda: “No where, just going to go to the court, and shoot around for a bit. Got some thinking to do. Ok? And, tomorrow, I’m heading over to Ocala, to visit Dory.” Stacey: [yawning] Okay, come to bed soon, please.” Boda: “Sure Hun.” *Boda leaves, closing the door behind him. He walks to his oldest daughter’s bedroom, and opens the door slowly, checking on her. There, sleeping peacefully, is Mackenzie, five years old, and full of the same fighting and athletic spirit her father has. Chris walks into the room, and places a kiss on her cheek. Mackenzie stirs, but stays asleep. Chris pulls the sheets up, covering her young body from the chill of the night air. Chris leaves, and goes to check on Carleigh, his 3-year-old ‘daddy’s girl’. To his girls, Chris is the Supreme Champion of everything. As he looks in on his little sleeping beauty, he can’t help but think, as she lies there peacefully, about the upcoming match, and how he plans on once and for all proving everyone wrong when he wins the big one. Thoughts of throwing Legend Inc. around the ring prance through his mind. Visions of once again pounding the shit out of Snow enlighten Chris. The whole match runs through Boda’s mind as he stands there, watching his youngest, most precious child sleeping. Chris places a loving kiss on Carleigh's forehead, and leaves her to dream her precious dreams. Upon walking down the stairs, to the first floor of his home, he opens the foyer closet, and takes out a basketball.* Boda: “Man, it’s nice to feel you friend.” *All of Chris’ young life, from his early teen years, to about three years ago, revolved around handling the rock. It was what helped Chris get out of the ghetto, and create the life before the wrestling career. For about 14 years, all that mattered was taking that little round ball, and sending it through the nylon rope hanging from a basket 10 feet off the ground. Now, playing ball, or just even shooting some hoops merely helps Boda concentrate on the new life he encases. There is only one thing that could take the place of the feeling that handling the rock gives Chris. That being winning the World Title. Chris takes the ball with him as he walks out of the front door, and straight to the full court caged basketball court in the front yard. Chris flips the master switch for the lights, and the court floods with white playing lights. Boda walks out onto the court, and dribbles the rock. He dribbles up to the foul line, and sets, and shoots the ball. As it swooshes through the nylon, Boda calls out the name of one the unlucky wrestlers in the rumble this coming Sunday* Boda: “Legend, Inc.” *Chris grabs the ball, and dribbles back to the foul line, and shoots again, as he calls out another name* Boda: “Kid Wonder” *Chris repeats this on each and every shot, with the ball hitting nothing but the bottom of the net every time.* Boda: “Akiyama...[shoots ball]...Corey Williams...[shoots]...The End... [shoots]...Miyagawa...[shoots]...Chet King...[shoots]...Bobby Stormbringer...[shoots]...Jon Kano... [shoots]...The Chef...[shoots]...El Diablo... [shoots]...Flaymin Aymin...[shoots]... Darkstar...[shoots]...Xavier Kannon... [shoots]...Chris Chase...[shoots]... Juan Borro...[shoots]...The Rush...[shoots]...Tyler Nelson...[shoots]...Snow...[SLAM-DUNKS THE BALL]. All 19 of you, are on notice now. Boda is going to win that World Title, and not one, or all 19 of you can stop me. I lived my life to play ball, and now that that is over, I live my life for ONE THING.....and that’s to win that TITLE. I consider this match ONE ON NINETEEN. No friends in the match, only enemies.” *Boda looks at the basketball, and rolls it to the fencing around the court. The scene fades out with Chris walking back to the house*
