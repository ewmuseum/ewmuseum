A Weird F'n Night

9 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/04 Part of being a human being is being forced to make decisions on a daily basis. Some are easy to make while others are hard. Some will make you smile, some will make you frown, and some will make you stay up at night and wonder why. For weeks I had searched for an answer. The answer to the question of whether or not I had made the right decisions in life. I found myself at a crossroads, and was not helped any by the constant mindgames of PCW's Continental Champion, Skitzo. Those two problems combined had thrown me off guard. It had made me lose my focus, and lose my grasp on what needed to be done to be successful. I finally realized that I had not climbed the ladder to become the best in the business by doubting myself. I was the best because I believed in myself, and knew that I could beat anyone on their best day. Once I realized that again, I was back on track. There was no way I was going to be stopped by anyone or anything. I awoke from my deep thought to lift the shot glass that was filled with whiskey to my mouth. I drank it down and slammed it on the counter before looking around the smoke-filled bar. The bar was your average bar that you would see in the movies. You had your four sided bar in the center of the place, complete with about 16 stools. All of which were always occupied. The floors were made of grainy wood, that was cracked in certain places, and the walls were just the same. It was not my surroundings that interested me, however. It was not even the pretty women that surrounded me, mostly because I had gotten four numbers earlier in the night. It was the television set in the bar that interested me. On it was PCW's syndicated television show that showcased quick promos, and classic PCW action. The current occupant that was wasting television time was Nathaniel Oscar Seller(aka. No Seller), his promo made me chuckle as I sat and ready his lips. 'What a little asswipe', I thought to myself as I sat and watched him rant about how he was going straight to the top, and running through myself and Boda along the way. The promo ended and No Seller left the screen thankfully. I was set to turn away from the set when the classic match of the night began to appear on screen. It was Skitzo vs Brianna Washington in the original Spider's Net match. I had never seen the matchup, but knew it was the match when Skitzo's 65 day Extreme title reign ended. I watched for a few moments before becoming bored at Skitzo's antics, and turned back towards the bar tender for another drink. I was quite surprised to see him staring at me blankly, but thought nothing of it. 'Yo, bartender another shot o'whiskey over here!' The bartender shuffled over to me with the bottle already in his hand. He looked at me for several seconds with a look of confusion. He eyebrowns were scrunched as if he was thinking deeply. 'Is there a problem, dude?', I asked with an annoyed sense in my voice. I arched my eyebrow before receiving my response. 'You're 'The Buzz' aren't you?', the bartender shook his head confidently at his asumption. A smirk spread across my face, and I motioned for him to pour me my whiskey. He did so and I picked it up and drank it down. I turned and started to watch the television monitor again that hung high in the far right corner. A few seconds later, I felt a tap on my shoulder. I turned to see the bartender tapping my shoulder, and became annoyed. I brushed his hand off, and turned completely around on my stool. 'Yeah I'm 'The Buzz', what's it to you?', I did not like to be bothered by fans when I was trying to relax during my off time. To me fans were a parasite when I wasn't on the job. 'Look I don't get autographs unless it's set up through the company.' He laughed and shook his head while cleaning a glass. 'I don't want your autograph. I don't know who the hell would want an autograph from the biggest asshole in professional wrestling.' I started laughing at the comments made by the bartender, and stood up off the stool and grabbed the bartender by the shirt. A look of fear filled his face as he dropped the glass, and the shards shattered almost in slow motion in front of me. I took my attention off the glass, and refocused on the bartender to see him looking around for the bouncers. 'You want to see how big of an asshole I can be, jackass!?', he shook his head no almost in a violnetly jerking manner as I clenched my free fist. I released my hold of the man, and slapped him playfully on the cheek before sitting back down. 'I just wish I could have seen you fall last week.', the comments enraged me, but I simply sat on the stool. I knew he was trying to get under my skin, and wanted to hear if he had anything other than crap to speak. 'Really and why's that?', I asked in a curious tone before reaching down in front of me to pick up my leather jacket and put it on. 'Because I lost money on your match at Shanghaied.' I started laughing before slapping him on the shoulder, and I reached into my pocket to pull out my wallet. 'Is that all because I mean I'm in a generous mood where I'll give you a nice crisp hundred if you shut up like a good boy.', the bartender's face became flushed with blood and he began to yell. 'NO, THAT'S NOT ALL! DOES THE NAME BRIANNA WASHINGTON SOUND FAMILIAR TO YOU!?' The bartender's lips quivered and his eyes began to fill with tears as he stared at me. I was shocked at what was occuring, and answered as best as I could. 'No, not really.', I answered with a laugh. 'Oh, so it's funny? That's my wife!' I raised my eyebrow in confusion, and scratched my head before answering. 'Buddy, I don't know if you need help or something, but why on god's green earth would I know your wife's name!?' His face looked as if it should be obvious to me, but I still sat dumbfounded. 'I FOUND OUT THAT YOU FUCKED HER LAST WEEK!' A sudden sense of rememberance spread across me, and I remembered meeting a red head at the grocery store when I was buying some groceries for my apartment. She said something about how she was going to divorce her husband, and I took her home. We talked, made out, had sex for hours. You know the usual Buzzoresu Special that the women of the mid-west had grown accustomed to. 'Hey, man I thought you'd be happy since she is divorcing you. I mean maybe she'll go easy on your pockets, since I made her a very happy woman.' I cracked a smile to ease the tension, but soon realized that something was wrong still. He began to look as if someone had shot his dog right in front of him, and soon looked up at me with anger in his eyes once again. 'She's going to divorce me?...I'm GOING TO KILL YOU ..YOU SON OF A BITCH!' The bar tender attempted to leap over the counter at me, but I just stepped to the side, and watched him slide across the slick wooden floor. The entire bar stared at the scene in disbelief, and I saw two portly bouncers waddling over towards the scene. The larger of the two bouncers shoved me as they reached my vicinity. He spoke with a nasty sneer on his face. 'You like picking on guys smaller than you? How bout I toss you out this bar, shrimp!?', he loud voice boomed and spit flew from his mouth as he finished his statement. I brushed the saliva off my jacket and laughed before speaking. 'Holy shit...I never knew they made life size twinkies.' The large man took a wild swing at me, which I easily ducked. My amatuer boxing instincts kicked in, and I delivered three shots to his gut, and followed up with an uppercut that sent him falling backwards onto the bartender who was attempting to stand. A loud groan emerged from both men, and I looked up at the remaining bouncer. I motioned for him to get some. 'Fuck that. They don't pay me enough to fight drunk wrasslers.', He shrugged his shoulders and went to help up the other bouncer. I noticed that the bar was completely silent, and looked around to see everyone staring directly at me. I for once in my life became embarassed, and pointed at the tv screen. 'Don't watch me, watch the clown on tv.' No one listened, but I couldn't care less. I brushed myself and headed around the bouncers and bartender toward the door. On my way out I watched as Brianna Washington was beating Skitzo to take his Extreme Title. It was a sweet sight, and I realized that history would be repeating itself soon. But I would not only take his pride and his title away from him. I had other plans. Plans no one could handle. The End
