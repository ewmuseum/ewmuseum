The Hero Saga: McASS KICKING

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25, 2002 And they say that a heeeeeeeeeroooooooooo will save us! The large glass window shatters, sending pieces all over the place. It glints in the flourecent light of the resturant as its patrons scatter. The screams change to cheers as the caped figure of Darkwing Star can be made out. 'Hello my proud patrons of people personification! It is I! Darkwing Star! I have come to liberate a dozen McBurgers for the McMayor!' Darkwing strikes a pose and smiles broadly. The McWorkers behind the McCounter begin to McPrepare the McBurgers when the McManager makes his way out from the back. He is wearing the typical McUniform except without the McSilly McHat. 'What the hell happened out here!!' The McManager wasn't very happy at Darkwings McDramatic McEntrance. It had apparently McCost a few hundred McDollars. 'Fear not! Kind McCitizan of the McTropolis of McPhiladelphia, for this will all be paid for... with MIND BULLETS!' Darkwing Star squints his eyes and the McManager seemingly forgets everything. 'So superhero man of FEAR, what will you be wanting today?' Darkwing Star laughed loudly, making sure to lean his head back, the rest of the gathered crowd laughed with him. 'I'll take a dozen of your finest McBurgers kind sir.' The manager quickly strolled to the intercom and announced the order, 'A dozen McBurgers, the good ones, not the ones that we picked off of the ground.' Silence. 'I mean... that we give to the regular customers.' Silence. 'I mean... FREE McBURGERS FOR ALL!' The crowd cheered loudly, Darkwing Star had inadvertantly gotten them all free McBurgers. That was a reason to celebrate, as much as a reason as anything else. Darkwing waited patiently for his order, for it was just another morning burger run after smoking a loud of pot with the President of the United States. Or was it? A loud scream pierced the patrons ears as something walked through the dramatic entrance spot of DarkWing Star... A burst of electricity shot out and pinned the hero to the ground, as much as he tried he couldn't get up. The evil laughter of his arch nemisis could be heard... 'No...' Surprised, Darkwing muttered almost under his breath, but still in dramatic Superhero fashion. 'It is... my... arch enemy.....' A large 45 year old man in a beat up cat suit, possibly a mascot of somekinds, walked in with his henchmen (because they all have henchmen somehow)... He laughed, kind of high pitched, but still evil... 'THE PUSSY!!!!' Gasp! HOLY CLIFFHANGER BATMAN
