The Sylencing of Three

3 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-01 The Sylencing of three: The sands of the hour glass had ran out, the last grain was about to fall into the sea of sand laying at the bottom. It was almost time for a night of madness, a night of blood shed, a night of pain, and a night of champions. It was a night where men stood and took their position in the lime light and others fall into the dark abyss of loss. Jay Sylo, The New Age Knight, The Sylencer, The Gladiator, The Celestial Knight was ready. All this time he had trained the beast, teaching it to work with him and lose it's violent seeds. Many thought he was crazy but being crazy is only a state of mind, if you know who you are then your never truly crazy. His rising star title was within reach, he could feel it but first he had to 'take out the trash' so to speak. Steve Johnson, Ripper Ryckman, and the rising star champion Lance Bell. He had no feelings toward any of these men, none concerned him and none scared him. (The water loomed on low like a shadow covering the ground. It's water sparkled and gleamed in the sunlight as air bubbles from fish could be seen. Trees acted as a mother for the lake by providing shade in areas and surrounding it with arms of nature. Animals played all through the woods as Ol' mother nature watched out for them all. An old wooden deck sat into the lake a small bit connecting to the fertile land behind it. The deck was gray and cracked, time had touched it many times and as the gentle wind blew ripples into the lake it rocked the deck making it feel as though it would fall apart. fish made their home under this deck, having their babies, feeding, living, and even dying. It was good coverage for them seeing as large, green weeds shot from the lake's floor up beside the deck. A figure sat dangling his feet in the water and watching the sun gleam down as schools of fish played. He would kick back and forth making light ripples under the water which scared a few of the fish into hiding. It seemed to him everyone seemed to hide behind something but would never admit what. Three of the men in the March Madness match for the rising star title also hid. They hid behind their own identities. Lance Bell walked around acting arrogant because he wasn't sure of his own abilities, he doubted himself and he had all right to. Steve Johnson had to TRY and make fun of others but why? Because he was ignorant? Yes. Because he was insecure? Yes. You see Steve Johnson was angry, angry because no matter what he KNEW he could not win the March Madness match at hand. Finally there was Ripper Ryckman. He hid behind another personality, he acted crazy to try and scare people off but Sylo could look through him and see that deep down inside he was scared out of his mind. Fear is a common thing and something not to be ashamed of but over the years Sylo learned that if you live in fear you die in fear. Your supposed to live your life to the fullest and not live with the burden known as fear because then your soul is never truly free. So he sat, continuing to dangle his feet in the water while listening to a family of blue birds sing. Were they fighting? Who knew either way the sounds were beautiful. One of the many things left in nature or the real world in that fact which still had its beauty.) 'I envy you,' Sylo said looking up to the birds. 'You live your life with no care in the world. All you must do is live and survive. You do not have to put up with the hardships of life, you do not have someone gunning for you every day of your life, you do not have to deal with the ignorance,' (There was a brief pause from the singing, almost as if the birds were listening. He let out a long sigh and looked back down to the water noticing fish were staring up at him.) 'I must face hardship tonight even my friends, tonight I must fight three men in one ring to prove that I truly am better than them, to prove they are nothing more than talk,' Sylo said letting out another deep sigh. 'Why is it they cannot just look inside and see their fear, maybe then it would make my job easier. I intend on winning, yes but I know it will be a fight because ignorance doesn't die easily. I have learned this many times in the past,' (It now seemed as all of nature was listening to Jay's story. He shook his head until he felt something touch his shoulder. He jumped a small bit and turned. Standing behind him was Akiyama and The Pharaoh. Sylo looked in shock not knowing how they found him.) 'We knew you would be hear,' Akiyama said looking to Sylo. 'Yes, we thought you may need some support before tonight,' The Pharaoh replied. 'Thank you guys but I feel like this is something I must do alone so I ask you no matter how bad it gets tonight, you two please stay in the back,' Sylo said with a dead serious look. 'But' Pharaoh started to say but was cut off. 'Please Titu. You know me, you know I am a man who likes to fight my own battles and tonight I shall. Remember I am not alone, I carry the beast of my ancestors within me. Steve Johnson, Lance Bell, and Ripper Ryckman will all fall to me and if anyone tries to come from the back they will all feel the same fate that AFL-CIO did,' He was determined as he said this, you could see it in his eyes. 'What about Leblanc Wednesday night?' Akiyama said crossing his arms. 'Leblanc is nothing more than a mindless slave of the system. Very violent yes but he's not very bright. I can take care of him on my own I do not need any help with him. All I ask is that you two watch for anyone heading toward the curtain at that time,' Sylo said looking back to the water. 'What about that Warden fellow, I don't trust him,' Pharaoh said looking down to Sylo. 'He is nothing to me, If he tries anything then one of you may come and dispose of him if I already haven't,' 'You are like our brother now Sylo, we will not let anyone harm you nor your chances of winning. You saved us once and we are forever grateful for that,' Akiyama replied patting Sylo's shoulder a small bit. (Sylo continued to stare into the lake almost like he was looking for answers to some burning question. Sylo snarled a small bit as his blue started to creep over every crevice of his eyes.) 'Do not worry for tonight I will dispose of all three men in my way and walk out the rising star champion....then I will take care of what is left of Carl Leblanc later. Do not worry for I am on a mission. A mission in which I cannot be stopped,' Sylo said snarling again. 'Good luck our friend,' Akiyama said as he turned. 'Come Pharaoh let us go and leave Sylo to be,' (The Pharaoh nodded as Sylo continued to stare down at the crystal blue water. The two looked back once more and then as soon as they appeared, vanished into nothing almost. Sylo sighed as his eyes turned back to normal.) 'Show time, may god have mercy on their souls,' was all he muttered. (Sylo stood up and stretched as all his muscles popped from his body. He turned and walked back toward the highway getting into his car. He quickly drove off kicking up a trail of dust. He was ready for tonight and Wednesday, but what part would the beast play in all of this? Only time would tell and for now time is up.)
