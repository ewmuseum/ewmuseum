The Chat King Wrestling Show (Special Guest: Dante Jackson)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9 Feb &#039;03 Voice Over Man: Welcome to another episode of The Chat King Wrestling, with the host Chet King ‘The Chat King’! The voice over man introduces us to another episode of The Chat King Wrestling Show as Chet King walks out from the back and makes his way onto the stage where he stands next to his designated seat. The camera does a quick pan over the cheering studio audience before going back to Chet. Chet King: Thanks for tuning in to yet another episode of The Chat King Wrestling Show, we’ve got another quality show lined up for tonight. Although, I’ve got to admit that I’m not too happy with who tonight’s guest is. Sure he’s one of the best in the business today, but he is also an asshole. Our guest tonight is the future of the wrestling business, and I’m sure he’d like to believe he’s also the future of the rap industry. Tonight’s guest is Dante Jackson! ‘Patiently Waiting’ by 50 Cent and featuring Eminem plays as Dante Jackson walks onto the set, from the back, to the jeers of the studio audience. Dante shoots them all some menacing glares as he walks over to Chet. The two men, who up until recently had a heated rivalry, stare at each other for a moment before reluctantly shaking hands with each other. Both Dante and Chet then sit down on their respective chairs while the audience continues to jeer Dante. Chet King: Welcome to the show Dante, this is actually you’re second appearance on the show for those keeping score. Dante Jackson: That’s right. I wasn’t too sure about appearing on your show again, since I had such a bad experience last time. Chet King: Because I punched you? Dante Jackson: No, because this show is shit. Chet King: Well the ratings suggest otherwise. But anyway, for those wondering, the reason I punched Dante last time he appeared was because he decided to get in my face about some stuff and started making accusations about me, so I decided enough was enough and punched him. And that show was what triggered our two month long feud. Dante Jackson: A feud in which I defeated you, two times out of two matches. Chet gets a bit embarrassed about this. Chet King: And both times were cheap losses. Dante Jackson: Take it like a man, King. You lost just like the bitch you are. Chet King: Take it like a man? That’s what Walden said to me when he was on the show last time. Why am I always getting people on the show who I’ve lost to? Dante Jackson: Because there’s no one you can have on the show who you haven’t lost to. You’ve lost to everyone King. Chet King: Shut up Dante. If you’re calling me a loser, then tune into Turmoil and watch me defeat Gaz Green. But we’re not here to discuss me, we’re here to discuss you. Dante Jackson: You just don’t want to talk about yourself, because you lost to me twice. Dante holds up two fingers. Dante Jackson: Count them. Twice! Chet tries to quickly change the subject. Chet King: Dante, can you confirm, for everyone out there, if the rumors that you are going to appear in Black Knight 2 are true? Dante Jackson: Indeed they are. Chet King: Wow! Despite our differences, I’d like to give you major props for getting that part. Dante Jackson: Thanks King, but that still can’t take away the fact that I defeated you twice out of two matches! Chet does not seem happy, but still tries to move on and ignore Dante’s comment. Chet King: When can we expect Black Knight 2 to appear in cinemas? Dante Jackson: At the moment we’re aiming for a March 2004 release, which means we’re going to have to start filming next month. Chet King: I’m sure the big question on everyone’s mind is will you be taking any time off from wrestling to film the movie? Dante Jackson: I’m trying to organize things so that I can film the movie, as well as continue to wrestle, and also try to fit time in for my rapping career. Chet King: You’re going to have an extremely busy schedule to fit all of that into. Dante Jackson: I know, but if anyone can do it, I can. Chet King: I’ve also heard rumors that you’re pushing for Veneman Pendragon to get a part in the movie as well. Any truth behind that? Dante Jackson: Yeah I’m pushing for that cat to get a part, I think he’d fit in really well and the two of us will have great chemistry. Chet King: Do you think Pendragon will get the part? Dante Jackson: I’ve told Universal Studios that I want him to have the part, and I’m sure with my word as well as Pendragon’s talents, he’ll get the part. Chet King: Do you think that this sequel will be able to exceed the first one? Dante Jackson: With myself staring in it, and hopefully Pendragon, I don’t have any doubts that it will exceed the success of the first one. Chet King: Alright, well everyone can expect Black Knight 2 to come out in March 2004. Now Dante, lets just discuss your rapping career for a moment. Have you recovered yet from losing that battle rap contest to me? This doesn’t make Dante happy. Dante Jackson: I didn’t lose to you. That contest was rigged. Those people chose you because they’re idiots. Chet King: But I still beat you, didn’t I? Dante Jackson: But I beat you both times we wrestled, didn’t I? Chet once again decides to quickly change the subject. Chet King: As a fellow member of the music industry, what’s your opinion on what’s been happening with Michael Jackson? Dante Jackson: Trying to change the subject once again? I beat you twice King, twice! Chet King: Alright, alright. Dante Jackson: Just so long as you know that I beat you twice. Now as for my opinion on Jackson, I think that cat used to have flow back when he was with the Jackson Five, but let me tell you that I wouldn’t let him near kids nowadays. Chet King: His revelations were a bit odd, weren’t they? But I do feel sorry for the guy, have the worlds media talking smack about him. Dante Jackson: Yeah I do feel sorry for him, although I don’t understand why he decided to become white. Why’d he turn his back on our people? Aren’t we good enough for him? Chet King: He said he has a condition which causes him to go white. Dante Jackson: He also said many other crazy things, and do you believe them? Chet King: You’ve got a point there Dante. Now before we go, have you got anything to say regarding your March Madness World Title Battle Royal qualification match this Tuesday? Dante Jackson: I hope Alan Ackart’s been paying attention to my matches lately, because if has then he’ll have seen that I’m on fire. No one’s going to stop me. King over here couldn’t, and Ackart won’t be able to either. I’m going to defeat Ackart and move onto the March Madness where I’ll stake my claim as the wrestling worlds top guy by winning the world title. Chet King: That sounds sweet like a fairy tale you tell a little kid, but I’m the one who’s going to win the world championship, Dante. I’m going to go through Gaz on Tuesday and then onto to win the battle royal at March Madness. Dante Jackson: King, while I’m in the ring with you, you won’t stand a chance. After all I did beat you twice! Chet King: Dante, I’m going to make up for those loses by winning the battle royal. Dante Jackson: Just remember that I beat you twice Chet. Twice out of two matches! Chet, obviously realizing that Dante isn’t going to stop rubbing his victories over Chet in his face, decides to end the show. Chet King: It’s time to end the show, and Dante I’m glad that we’ve managed to go through this show without it breaking down like you’re last appearance. Dante Jackson: I think the reason it hasn’t broken down is because you know that if it did then I’d beat you up like I did in both our two matches! Chet sighs. Chet King: Alright, well to close this show, due to popular demand, Dante will be performing his latest hit from his album ‘That Second Coming. Remember to tune in next time, and to all PCW programming this week. Good night everyone. The credits roll as Dante gets up and starts to perform his latest hit.
