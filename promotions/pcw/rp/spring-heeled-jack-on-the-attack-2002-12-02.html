Spring Heeled Jack, on the attack!

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/12/02 ...I awoke to the dawn of a new day. I knew not where I was, nor did I care. The last think I remember was the leering face... or faces... of Skitzo carrying PCW's commissioner away from us and ruining our plans to improve backstage conditions. All I remember was the hideous stench of poisoned air before falling down. Considering that I don't need to breathe, it made one thing clear. Skitzo's farts are killer. I made an effort to sit up in bed after unfolding my wings from around me. They provided suitable extra heat for me, especially with the wafer-thin excuses for blankets provided by this Texan hotel. The walls around me were colourless at first because of the loathsome sunlight attacking my eyes. I held up my right wing in front of me, trying to shield myself from those horrid rays. As my eyesight asserted itself once more, I wish I'd stayed blind. The Texans have little taste for decoration with their mint-green walls and cattle skulls. Thankfully, the hotel itself was not where I'd spend the day. Gaz had suggested a little old-school pranking might relieve some stress. It would be like old times, just me and him, no Natalia. A golden opportunity to catch up on the carefree days. We were due in California this friday to battle Skitzo. Kannon had also thrown a veil of shadow over the match with a mystery partner. So far, Mr. Psycho's insane ramblings might have held a clue as to who it may be, but the lunatic's sentences were as corrupt of sense as Kannon's morals. We would have to be on our toes for this one, but now was not the time. This was to be a small slice of history re-lived. * * * We'd picked our target this time to be a place we visited a long time ago. Just after we had been taken into PCW, in fact. These halls of poor, tortured souls held the insane and half-witted. The Asylum. A purgatory as opposed to euthanasia, where the deluded may only find comfort with their delusions. Trapped forever in their own personal hells... what a travesty! If it weren't for the fact that I would relish much more on their suffering, I would release them all from their mental prisons. But we had another agenda... Despite the security the prison boasted, the prisoners themselves were not always confined to their cells. During the day, as I had watched in preparation, they would occasionally be taken into the garden... if slabs of concrete and one oak tree could be called a garden... and those capable of interacting with the outside world would be given a few minutes of fresh air. It was this window of opportunity that I had to capitalise on. Gaz, in the meantime, was in disguise and infiltrating the Asylum. Dressed in blue overalls and a cap, the Jester had the mission of opening the cells. Rather than unleashing these madness-drowned wretches into the open, we would capture the chaos inside it's own walls. Should one escape, I would pay mankind a small service by cleansing it of this insane filth. With our missions set and our plans made out, it was time to put our little game into action. Ikuze! * * * The darkness was just beginning to coat the sky. Thick strokes of indigo blotted the sun and a few stars tried to avoid drowning in the darkness. But the night had brought with it another form of darkness. A shadow that was scaling up the walls of the monument to insanity. Considering that this was a place of security, the metaphoric bars of the cage opened only inwards. After all, few were so eager to go in as they were to escape. I found my vantage point on one top of one of the slanted rooftops. The circular garden was empty, probably awaiting one more coherent prisoner before it slept. I would have to plan a fast exit and flying was out of the question. I knew only one solution - one legend to mask another. The Asylum was once home to a man known only as Jack, having long since forgotten his name. Apparently, he made some small fame for himself in a local dirt rag when he attempted to mirror the actions of one of England's more... colourful... characters - Spring Heeled Jack. Back around the last turn of the century, horror stories known as 'Penny Dreadfuls' spoke of a man with hands like talons and the ability to breathe flames that petrified London. His activities were not entirely fictional, however. Several people, women in particular, were attacked. Their clothes would be shredded and their bodies and faces wracked with blue flames, before this demonic thing would bound off into the night, leaping over things so tall that only a man with springs in his heels would be able to advance past them. His infamy spread all over early 20th century England and had also poisoned the mind of this fellow. After being rejected by women for the last time, the distraught man fashioned a set of claws from regular kitchen claws and taught himself how to blow fire from his mouth like a circus performer. His downfall was that he didn't quite have springs in his heels... and a dead ended alleyway saw him arrested, sentenced and silenced. He died of a heart attack so many years ago that many had forgotten him. But to whatever poor man was being led out into this garden, 'Spring Heeled' Jack was about to visit his cellmate once more. I recognised the Warden coming out immediately. The cocky walk was attributed to Gaz and the man with him at the time seemed to be timid. Trying to hear his thoughts, I could only make out the repeated mantra of 'Don't hurt me...' what a fine piece of prey! Gaz led him to a bench and ordered him to sit. I could make out the emerald menace's instructions even from up here... 'Sit tight, I need a whizz.' he said, leaving the paranoid man sitting in fear. His hair was white, like some spectral entity himself. How I wished I could have sampled the fear that had made him this way... but nevertheless, Gaz had re-entered the complex and was probably waiting for my part of the show to begin. I removed my glasses and felt my eyes begin to get warm. According to Gaz, everytime I complain of them being hot, they glow a bright red. Without a way of knowing, I checked my outfit over one time before proceeding. I wore a tight black leotard, just like in the ring. Attached to my shoulders was a billowing cape that stroked the back of my knees. All I needed now was a slight... modification... to my hands. In a second, a pair of hooked claws replaced them and the look was complete. Showtime. Bounding off the wall, I began to cackle loudly, drawing the attention of the senile, paranoia-stricken man in a heartbeat. A heartbeat that I could hear slamming into overdrive. 'HEEHEEHEEHEE!' I cackled as I leapt around in front of him, exploiting his fears of the past. 'STILL IN HERE?' 'J-J-Jack?!' he stammered with shock. He was too scared to move from the bench, despite the face of the Spring-Heeled Jack staring right into his. 'Yyyyyyyyyyyyyyyessssss! I've come back for you, heeheehee!'. The high voice I had hurt even my vocal cords, but the results were above satisfactory. I heard dripping and noticed the floor becoming increasingly moist as the man lost control of himself. Time to put the final touches on... I started to rip lightly at his with my claws, not intending to hurt him physically but to just entice him to move from the bench. I slashed the air around him and began to laugh like a mad man myself. With the lunacy of this place, I feared too much longer here would corrupt my own mind. I could hear the thoughts of the prisoners beneath the ground. Such agony and torment - but I knew that this sort of meal was like sugar, sweet to the tongue but overall, it was poisoning me. I had to move now, lest I join these outcasts. 'Let's die together! It's so much fun in the coffin!' I squealed. Finally, the deranged man leapt up from his bench and began running in circles, screaming. I heard the door to my right open up and saw Gaz come out, laying on the ground as though knocked out. With the stage set, all I needed to do was wait for the other cast to arrive, while I made my retreat - exit Nerezza, stage left. Enter the armed guards, stage right. * * * 'So, how does it feel, squire? Any change in the temper?' Gaz said, peeling the fake plastic wound off his head. We were back inside that tasteless room, among the cow skulls and the horrid paint. I remained silent as the television announced our latest performance. '...copycat attacks today after inmates at the Gardner Institute reported seeing one of their cellmates who has been dead for just short of a decade! Some of you may remember the Spring-Heeled Jack attacks of '94 where 3 women were assaulted by a man copying the actions of the mythical monster. Apparently, Jack was spotted inside the compound by James Summers, who is now lying in a state of catatonia. He alleged that Jack's ghost appeared out of thin air, tried to kill him and was coming for the other cellmates. During the frenzy, James knocked out one security guard before being restrained. The officer who preferred to remain anonymous was not seriously injured. Police are warning the public to be vigilant in case a series of copycat attacks resume. In Sports news, it's been a bad season for...' I pressed mute on the remote, smirking. Pranking had become a therapy that I'd cut down on since winning the titles. I felt like I'd released... no, channeled my anger in a different way. It told me one vital thing... Pranks With Attitude needed a little more prank and less attitude. Another therapy session was due soon...
