Mentally & Physically- Pt 1

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-01-02 ::It was claimed official just about a week ago, the official signing of the “Golden One” Shawn Mosely into PCW. James Creon’s goal of about 6 months ago finally came true as he possibly changed Shawn Mosely’s life, or even safed it by making this decision. Finding Shawn Mosely is not the problem now, getting him mentally and physically for the road he will travel in PCW. We now see both James Creon and Shawn Mosely sitting in James old broken down car somewhere in Palm Beach, Florida. Palm Beach, Florida. . .a place where Shawn has lived so much of his life, a place where he escaped his mothers death so-many years ago. It’s now a place where he use to live. Shawn asked James to visit his house after his first match with Mark Estep. Now they are about 20 minutes away from the place Shawn left 6 months ago. Many thoughts are going threw restructured mind. . .his last match versus Estep . .his next match versus Andy Murray . . .what to expect with his old house. A VERY short list of thousands of thoughts going threw this potential Superstars mind. Shawn is sitting there in the passager side looking out his window at the passing houses and people doing various things as he passes them doing about 35 mph. Shawn, not in his usual cocky, arrogant attire, is wearing regular jeans and a white t-shirt with black sunglasses on. James on the other hand is paying close attention to the road as he has never really been around this part of Florida, and basically wants to get in and out as quickly as possible. James ,without warning, cuts out of nowhere the first words:: “Excellent first match Shawn. Plus you were away for so long. I told you, that you can do it” James says with a smile on as he looks straight head at the road. Shawn just sits there with no facial expression at all and refuses to respond to James words. James tries once again to start a conversation. “Uh. . so we’re going to Arizona after this little visit, right?” James says with a louder tone then the last time. “Why are you in a rush James?” Shawn replies as his head leans against his right hand. “No. Just that your next match is held in Arizona, and well, just wanted to make sure that you knew that Shawn. . .that’s it” James says. “You said this whole experience was to get mentally ready for the next week. But now it just seems like your rushing. But rushing to where is what worries me” Shawn replies with his head still leaning on his hand. James doesn’t even respond to Shawn’s last statement, but to the fact that they are approaching Shawn’s old house. “Well, here we go” James says as they pull into the long driveway, which leads right to the huge front door. Shawn’s head finally comes off his hand as he sits straight up in anticipation of what he will see. The car gets closer and closer as the view gets better and better for Shawn. He starts to notice all the regular features of the house as nothing has changed. Hell, It’s only been 7 months right? But as they edge closer to the house, the house begins to look wrecked and mugged. First thing that comes to Shawn’s mind is those extreme over the line wrestling fans. Overall, the dilapidated house did not bother Shawn Mosely at all, the memories inside the house, on the other hand, did. “Is this good” James says as he stops the car 20 feet away from the front door. Shawn says “yes” as he steps out of the car. James looks at Shawn for a moment and then also steps out of the car as he turns the car off. Shawn turns around and glances at James. He then begins to make his way up the stairs and to the door as he doesn’t wait to open it. During this whole process James is right behind him just like he said he would be for the rest of his wrestling career. The inside of the looks like a mess, also. As Shawn walks past each room a special event is going threw his mind with every room he passes. One can’t help but wonder all of these events. They all took place with in the last 3 years, but his attitude since has changed. Could you imagine what the cocky, arrogant, “I am better then anyone” , attitude did here? All that doesn’t really matter now. James has brought Shawn back to his old house because this is part of his training, the mental side of it all. Putting the past behind him and moving on to the future. “A lot of memorable things happened here” Shawn says to James who is right behind him as he makes his way to the back of the house which leads to the beach. “Yeah, I can just imagine. Now it’s time to leave this go Shawn, you have a whole new beginning in PCW. I , along with them, have given you a second chance and you will succeed even further this time.” James replies as he follows Shawn into the huge living room. “I never realized I didn’t have anyone in my life. No One came looking for me, or even knew my house was like this.” Shawn says with a disgruntled look on his face. “ Yes, I know. You have me, and the PCW family, though. All this stuff doesn’t matter. The girls, the money, It’s a part of your past. The PCW World Title is something more to look forward to.” James replies. “Yeah. . I know. I guess I just have to say Goodbye to the state of Florida. Place I grew up in, had wrestling success, a place a loved James. Now, I doubt it. All I have now is the memories.” Shawn says with no facial expressions showing. “And you also have a match with Murray Tuesday, so lets get going.” James cuts in quickly. Shawn takes on more quick look around, and then looks at James. *Sighs* “I guess your right, James. Just have to put this place behind me” Shawn replies. “Shawn, this is all a part of your recovery back to the top. I just want you to know that. This is more important then the physical side of the sport.” James says. “Yeah. Let’s get out of here. We have one more place to visit before we go to Arizona” Shawn says. ::James nods his head and finals Shawn back threw the house. This time Shawn just looks straight ahead and pays no attention to anything. They both make their way outside, back to the car. James gets into the car quickly, but Shawn turns around and gets one more,and possibly his last look at a house that brings back so many overall memories to Shawn Mosely::
