Date: 11/19/02 The End is spotlighted in a dark room, wearing his ring attire of a black body suit and ninja mask. The End:'Okay, because of the small size of this promo and because it has absolutely nothing to do with PCW, it's taking potshots at Ali Khadafi and Darkstar. If this were much longer and better produced, I could even call it being against Vampir Nosferatu, just to make Rossian angry....' A bustling community was Colony Omicron 17. While the humans worked to build up cities in Mars's newly terraformed moon, Phobos, the colony held itself in orbit around Mars, matching the moon's orbit. The colony functioned as a bit of a hotel, a way-station for those who came from Earth and are waiting to be processed. The colony was also home to the best research facilities in the solar system. I'm sorry, did I say bustling? I meant hum-drum. Life on the colony was never too interesting, despite the different people everyday coming in and going out, sports and entertainment becoming the major industries. Fact of the matter was that things just weren't... sociable. People came through all the time, but none stayed long enough to get to know them, creating a feeling of populated isolation. A prison in which one was in solitary confinement in the midst of the general population. You could never connect, could never become 'friends' with a person. One barely had any reason to learn their names. They'd be going off to Phobos or Titan or Europa or any one of the colonies orbiting the outer planets. Mainly, Colony Omicron 17 was used as a refueling station for those needing to stock supplies before making the trip through the asteroid belt. Unlike the trip to Mars, which was made in hyper-sleep since it's a six-month trip, the Asteroid Belt had to be navigated manually, a crew of at least three (for contingency reasons) pilots awake and ready to take the helm at any moment. Now, the events that I shall describe to you are indeed unusual. They may be called extraordinary based on the extreme ordinariness of life on the colony. August 20th:'It was with a good deal of hesitation that Scott Whitaker allowed Nathaniel and Zoral to go out and play out on the grav deck. There had been reports of a strange disease that had struck the colony's rodent and lizard population. Seemed like the plague, they came out to die with blood spurting from their eyes and gurgling from their mouths. Certainly out on the grav deck the vaccum would prevent anything from happening to them, but on the way their suits could be cut or torn and the plague microbes could enter into them that way. 'Scott could not accompany the children today, his week-long break was over and it was time for him to return to the laboratory to join Leo, Edith, and Ho Chien. Willa had her off time this week and agreed to accompany the children, giving Scott a greater level of comfort that Nathaniel could play on the grav deck with his friend.' 'How was your day?' asked the blonde-haired, fair-faced Willa as her tow-headed Adonis of a husband Scott. 'Just fine,' he said, having a good day, just a tiring one. He dragged his feet a little walked into their family's quarters. Sparsely furnished it was, most of their things were locked up in storage to be transported to the surface of Phobos once the terraforming project had been completed. All there was were a few chairs and a table, all made of a recently discovered metal alloy. Sure, there were beds and desks in the rooms, but other than that there wasn't much. It was a temporary situation; or it was supposed to be, but the terraforming was taking longer than it should have. Things were bad, the plague had wiped out the species that the humans tried to introduce. Scott threw his white plastic lab coat on the floor and sat down by Willa on the cushioned metal couch facing the southwest wall that has the entertainment monitor displayed, throwing his arm around her and kissing her lips. Their matching silver plastic jumpsuits squeak as the surfaces of the clothing rub against each other. 'What are you watching?' Scott asked of Willa. 'Old wrestling tapes. Vintage 2002.' Scott stretched out his shoulders and neck in a rolling motion. 'Yeah, ever since the gene-altering wave started in 2045 we just haven't been able to have good sporting events. Everyone's evenly matched and it's always a tie or a draw.' Willa turned up the volume. Dave: THE END HAS DONE IT! THE END WON THE ALAMO MATCH EARLIER TONIGHT AND NOW HE HAS WON ALLIANCES 2K2 FOR THE CELESTIALS! Jeff: Sulfuric Acid endorsement, Fanta Girls, and something like 3 or 4 title shots. Not a bad haul for a night's work. 'Now there was an athlete,' Scott said, sitting still on the couch. 'He was tall, strong, incredibly agile, and he was a successful wrestler despite being blind.' Willa nodded her head. 'Yeah,' she agreed. 'They don't make athletes like they used to...'
