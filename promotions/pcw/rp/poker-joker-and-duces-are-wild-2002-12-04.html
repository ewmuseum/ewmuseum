Poker, Joker and Duces are Wild

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-4-02 RP 1 Adrenaline 8th Dating with Teresa, as we all may know isn’t all it’s cracked up to be. Just ask poor Nerezza. Granted, if their so-called ‘date’ had been for real, perhaps Teresa would’ve taken things a little more seriously. Tonight however is a real date if you want to call it so. It all started with an old friend, bottle of booze, and a game of poker. Teresa is home for Thanksgiving. It’s a Friday night and our down home country girl decides to take a drive to Cookeville, a town known for its steakhouses and dance clubs. True, she could’ve stayed in Nashville, but she’d rather not bother with those crowds. In Cookeville, (pronounced ‘COOK-vull’ by locals) there is a popular hang out called Cotton Eyed Joe’s. It’s most famous for country line dancing and wet tee shirt contests, but after midnight, everything goes. The time is 10pm. The midnight purple Chevy pick up squeezes into an empty parking spot. Moments later Teresa steps out wearing her usual comfortable but stylish Levi’s and a V-neck camouflage shirt which fits her waist nicely. Her sandy blond hair is worn down and a brown leather jacket covers her shoulders. She steps into the front door wanting nothing more than good beer and good conversation with anyone that doesn’t mind her buzzed babble. The bar is naturally packed with college students and a handful that will meet 30 in a few years. For Cookeville being a college town, it’s a pretty good turnout. Most everyone is too involved with their own clique’s to even notice a wrestling star just walked in. In all honesty, Teresa would rather have it this way. She buys a bottle of Bud Ice and luckily finds an empty table near the dance floor. The speakers are bellowing an Allan Jackson tune. Two lines of cow folk dressed in their Wrangler’s and Stetson hats stomp away on the hardwood floor. In the corner of the room, a nine-ball pool tournament is taking place. Finding the pool table more interesting than a bunch of drunken nuts dressed as cowboys and cowgirl stomping around on a wooden floor, Teresa stands and makes her way toward the pool tables. There are already a small crowd watching the tournament so she tries to blend in as best as possible without making a scene, then someone taps her shoulder. Teresa turns her head and her baby blue eyes widen. Standing beside her is Charlie Price or just ‘Chuck’ for short. Chuck is an old friend of Teresa’s from her childhood. They’ve known each other since the 5th grade. He was a jock and for the mere simple fact that she played school sports as well, most put her in the same group. Back then, call her a jock to her face and you didn’t leave without a busted lip. They partied together, got drunk together, got into trouble together, and they always were making bets with each other. Their egotistical pride had gotten in the way of their relationship being more than just friends. Perhaps it was for the best. Together they would’ve made the best Bonnie and Clyde of today. “ Tomas, you’re the last person I expected to see tonight. Good to see you old buddy.” After all these years, that’s all the poor guy could think to say. Teresa shakes her head with a smile and slips her empty beer bottle in a trash can beside her. Chuck wasn’t much the sensitive type and usually short on words with touchy situations. “Good to see you too Chuck. Life been treatin’ ya good? Let’s grab a table. I’ll buy us a bottle of Jack.” A huge smile crosses Chuck’s face. He’s not a big man, but he’s not a stick person either. Standing close to 6 foot and around 180, you can say he’s pretty much average build. What little workout he does is only to keep his beer gut from overlapping his waistline. He has short dark brown hair and his eyes and complexion doesn’t hide his Indian heritage he received from his grandfather. “Now you’re speaking my language. I’ll grab the table and you grab the booze.” Some things never change. Half a bottle of Jack Daniel’s and five poker games later, both Chuck and Teresa were in the clouds with slurred speech, dirty jokes, and drunken laughter. They had just spent the last hour catching up on each other’s lives and the threads of their friendship spun back together as if they never were parted. Chuck had spent his life traveling the US working for a construction company while Teresa pursued her business career and later evolved into the wrestler she is today. Neither had married, though Chuck confessed to three kids and two fiancés. Chuck loves his women, but when it comes to settling down and commitment, he tends to tuck his tail and run. Teresa spoke about the falling out of her and Jack Slayter. Chuck laughed when she told about the incident at McDonald’s. He was bold enough to say he wished he were there to witnessed that. Before he told Teresa he would’ve ripped Jack a new butt hole, Teresa had wasted a shot of whiskey all over his face. He didn’t say much when she told him about Scott. He would hate to see he waste any more whiskey. “ Ya know Tomas, we should’ve got together. We’d be an explosive couple. Just think about it.” Just as she was about to win this hand of poker, he had to spit something like that out of his mouth. Of course Teresa knows he doesn’t mean any word of it. Chuck only said it to distract her concentration. “Don’t even go there Chuck. I know exactly what you are doing.” With a cocky but intoxicated smile, Chuck replies. “What’d I say?” Teresa lays two cards down and draws two from the deck. “You know exactly what you said, now shut the hell up before I black your eye.” Chuck sits back in his chair so far, it falls over and takes him with it. He falls on his back and he stumbles back to his feet picking up his chair. When Teresa looks across the table at him, he is grinning like a sly fox. Got a wager for ya Tomas. Seeing this game is almost over, if I win, you have to agree to one date with me tomorrow night at the Outback. If you win, then you’re off the hook.” Teresa raises an eyebrow to Chuck. A date with him would be hell. He’s fun to hang out with and have a good time, but anything more would be disastrous. “Ok, you’re on. I win, you have to dance with that ugly chick gawking at you from the pool tables and I get pictures.” Sure enough, a girl wearing tight Wranglers and a black cowboy hat is staring at Chuck with hungry eyes. Her dried out hair is in dire need of a cut and she’s missing her two front teeth. Even looking at her from a distance, you can tell she has spent a few too many years on meth, a very dangerous and cheap drug made from household chemicals. “That’s cruel Tomas. It’s a bet.” Teresa gives a confident smile as she lays down three Kings and two Jacks. Chuck shakes his had and lays down Three Ace’s and two Queens. “Looks like we’ll be seeing each other tomorrow night at the Outback. I forgot to mention one thing, you’re buying.” Chuck gathers his playing cards and puts them back in the box. He stuffs the box of cards in his shirt pocket. He then leaves the still sitting and a bit angered Teresa a smart-ass kiss on the cheek before walking off. “See ya at seven. Don’t be late!” “Kiss my ass Chuck!”
