The Bellzier Chronicles: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part Five]

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 27, 2002 DreamLandTime Unknown? Surprise is such an aspiring emotion, isn't it? We could be knee deep in the turmoil of our own lives,yet still in awe over the simplest of things. Such is the way I was feeling, as I stared at the talking,clapping squirrel which had brought me into this god forsaken place. No introductory music, no themes, itwas just plain shock. What a plot twist, huh? I am getting pretty good at this. I was basically standing toe to toe with the pesky critter, who was on his two back legs standing up, afew meters away from me. He still had those beady little eyes that I found myself drowning into. Godforbid that actual people had eyes like that, because if they did, I would never be able to look at someonein the eye again. Most likely, I would have been one of those people that stare at you at the base of yourfeet. You know those folks that never look you directly in the face, but take a gander at your hand,your foot, your ear - hell, anything to avoid the eyes. I wondered if he had some special power thatcauses people to fall apart at his very gaze. Reminds me of a vampire, except squirrels don't drink blood. Doh. Am I forgetting that this is Dream Land again. Bad, bad Tom. The talking squirrel crossed his arms and gave me a tired look. “Well, are you just going to stand there looking stupid, or are you going to do something productive with your time? Perhaps ask me a few pertinent questions about my appearance here? Perhaps inquire about why you are in our dimension? C'mon, you have a brain that’s bigger than mine. Use it.” Damn, this critter really got under my skin. I took a deep breath, trying to calm myself down. “Okay, I'm not going to hurt you. In fact, I will stay calmand ask you very rational and calm questions.” Oh damn, I know I'm not calm right now because I just used the same damn word three times in two seconds. Sherri placed her hand on my shoulder, as if trying to steady me. I suppose I was shaking in anger, fury,and whatever uncontrollable emotions that were poking out of my consciousness. It actually did help, and Ifound myself no longer seeing the squirrel with a bullseye on his chest. “First thing's first: Who are you?” The squirrel laughed, running away from where he was standing and into a nearby tree. He disappeared into the foliage for a moment, but then reappeared a few moments later with an apple in his mouth. He ate itlike a human would. My, that's interesting. The squirrel appeared a few seconds later, chomping on an apple. “Man, I do love apples. They're so sweet and juicy - much better than acorns or any of those nuts. Those shells hurt my teeth too.” “Are you always this evasive, or do you naturally turn and run from confrontations? I asked you a very simple question, and I want a god damn answer now.” I demanded. I'm getting pretty good at playing the tough guy routine as well, it seems. The squirrel laughed. “So, the big, bad wrestler man has finally decided to show his truecolors. It's funny when someone makes an ultimatum, when it's as clear as day that they are in no positionto be making any sort of demand. You are at my whim, Lexian. I am not inclined to answer any of yourquestions.” The Jello warrior chimed in. “But you will, because otherwise, why would you be here? You obviously need something from him, so he isn't entirely at your whim, now is he? The squirrel got an ugly look on his furry little face. The Jello man certainly did have a way withwords. The talking squirrel simply replied with a ‘hmph.’ “Fine. It seems as though you've aligned yourself with a formidable bunch, Lexian. Do you plan to go to battle with them as well? “Battle?” Sheri asked. “I don't go to battle, squirrel face. I'm smart. I deserve better. I should be calculating the mean average of acorns all squirrels in Dream Land eat each year, just because I can.” “And I fight for no one but myself, and for my Jello warriors.” The ongoing fight for Jello rights. It's a formidable battle, to be sure. “No army, no brains...Lexian, how do you ever expect to escape Dream Land alive?” The squirrel said in an obviously sarcastic voice. “You tell me...” I asked. “My name is Nutcracker, head foreign liaison for Maples the Caterpillar, also known as the man who owns you. In other words, he rules over all of Dream Land.” I had to bite my lower lip to stay calm. “Oh yeah, I get it now. You’re one of his cronies, aren’t you? You’re one of the bad guys. Well, that’s just great. This was just a setup from the beginning…” It was becoming as clear as day, at least it was to me anyways, that I was being played like a puppet. Maples was holding my strings, and just pulling them whatever the hell he wanted to. I don’t like being used, not for these purposes. My life was my own, and I would appreciate at least a frickin’ letter explaining why I’m being sucked out of my own dimension. “Oh please, give me a break. Don’t give me this crybaby shit. Life is life, and life screws you over big time. Now, were you brought here against your own will? Of course you were. But it’s not like we took a gun and stuck it to the back of your head. You followed me voluntarily…” “Voluntarily…” I began to say, an incredulous look on my face. “Of course. I just leapt at the chance at following you down a three inch hole.” “Isn’t life grand?” Nutcracker said. “Lameness in our world can be extended to yours, if only for a short while. How else do you think you got such lame appliances like Teflon, playdough, and Tupperware?” “Tell me already….what does Maples want with me?” Nutcracker just stood there, laughing his head off. Gosh, I didn’t know my unhappiness was such a funny concept to him. Maybe in Dream Land, serious conversations were funny and comical situations were serious. It was a plausible hypothesis. “You already know the answer to that, and if you don’t, you’re denser then I first believed. He needs you to hold off the onslaught of King Coverdale, who is trying to take control over Dream Land again. Coverdale wants you to help him regain his old world, so you’re in a virtual tug of war with the lamest men in Dream Land. Don’t you feel special?” Sheri shrugged. “How could he? Either way, he’s somebody’s bitch. Not exactly something to be proud of.” Insulted by a little girl. That wasn’t even lame; it was flat out embarrassing. “I don’t want to help either one of them,” I announced. “You can go tell your lame ass boss that I won’t help him do anything, and he should go slip back into whatever cocoon he came out of.” Nutcracker paused, as if he were pondering what I just said. “Well, two things come to my mind at this very moment. One, he didn’t come out of a cocoon. Caterpillars slip into a cocoon to become a butterfly, not the other way around. And secondly, I wouldn’t insult Maples if I were you. He has a bad temper, and he has an odd way of making people pay for insulting him. The last person to make that mistake ended up…” I tried to finish his sentence. “Ended up what? Dead? Tortured?” “…he was buried alive with overweight slugs. Do you know what kind of torture that is? I have shivering nightmares about that whenever I think about it. Anyways, either way, you’re going to have to help one of them, so you might as well pucker up and decided which ass you’re going to kiss.” The Jello warrior put his hand on my shoulder. “Tom, don’t do it. They’ll torture you no matter how much you help them. It’s better that you resist them, and try to escape back to your own world. I would be willing to help you…” I looked at the Jello warrior with appreciation. He was more of a man than some people in my own world. Gosh, is that a tear in my eye?…nah, it’s dust. Sheri put her hand on my shoulder as well. “I’m here to help too, Tom. I don’t like either one of them, and I just think you’re swell.” “Awww shucks, thanks guys…” Sherri is coming on to me. I hope that isn’t illegal here… Nutcracker just sat there exasperated. “I can’t believe you guys want to help this shmuck. Can’t you see he’s in some deep shit? There will be no mercy for the people that help him…” “I don’t care!” Jello warrior shouted out. “Coverdale is just using me and my people. I will no longer stand idly by, letting him push all men and women of jello kind around. Let my people go!” “But wasn’t it Maples who first gave you creation?” I asked, raising a very stupid point. I mean, he was going to help me. “Don’t you owe him something?” “Dude, do you want me to help you or slobber knock you around?” He asked me. “I can really do either, so if you want to slobber knock, I’m ready to take you down, sucka…” Sucka? I took off my jacket is a mad fit of anger. “Let’s go…c’mon jello man, I want to have dessert right now!” Before anyone can throw a punch, however, Sherri steps in between us and tries to calm us down. Sherri pulled us apart and began to shout. “Stop it, stop it. We’re all on the same side, remember? We should be angry at that damn talking squirrel, not each other. Now, let’s stay civilized.” Sherri was right, and has once again proven her worth as truly an intelligent….little….girl. Uhm, yes. I guess it was pointless to be going head to head with someone who wants to help me. I looked at the Jello man, and gave the guy a hug, trying to tie the bond between us. I think it really helped. After the hug, I felt him spank me on the buttocks. I would have slapped him, but I was too embarrassed to say anything about it. Sexual harassment by a jello figure? “I can’t believe you are all so dense to realize that your lives are now in jeopardy. Don’t you want to live? Don’t you want your quality of life to go up, not straight down into the shitter?” Nutcracker asked. “We’re going to do this with or without you, so you might as well leave or give us some suggestions.” I replied. The squirrel gave me a look, but then sighed. Did we turn him, or is he going to send for reinforcements? This is a good time for a dramatic pause… DRAMATIC PAUSE! Ah, there we go.
