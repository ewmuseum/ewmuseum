A Superhero Is Born Part 1

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 13th, 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen walking down a street. He notices a Spiderman The Movie poster. He laughs and begins to sing a tune.) Mike Frampton: Spiderman, Spiderman, runs around like a spider can. I think that's how it goes, ah hell who cares. Batman is the number one superhero. (A small kid stands up to Mike.) Tod: That's wrong, Spiderman is the ultimate superhero. He swings around like only a spider can. (Mike stares down at the child.) Mike Frampton: Spiderman is only a genetically altered freak! Batman is all human! WOO! He could beat the living webbing out of Spiderman. Tod: Spiderman would just use his webbing to trap Batman. Mike Frampton: Spiderman has to use webbing to win his fights. He's a wuss. Tod: That's dumb. Batman uses his boomerang and that is the equal of webbing. Plus Batman has no super powers. Mike Frampton: Exactly Batman doesn't need super powers. Plus batman has already had a good four movies under his belt. Spiderman has one, and it has friggin Tobey Maguire in it. Tod: You said a naughty word. It doesn't totally matter, Superman would rip batman apart. Mike Frampton: Yeah well... (Big Daveylicious steps in behind Mike. He has been listening to this childish conversation. He is wearing a black jacket and Suit pants.) Mike Frampton: Davey, who would win if Spiderman and Batman fought? Big Daveylicious: Well now Mike. This is the question of the day. Superman or Batman? Mike Frampton: No Spiderman idiot. Big Daveylicious: Shut up Mr. Thong. Tod: Well who? Big Daveylicious: I'm thinking. (Big Daveylicious strokes his beard and decides. He brings his hands down and a smile crosses his face.) Big Daveylicious: Mike the best way to find out is to make it happen. Since that is impossible... Or is it? Mike Frampton: No it is impossible now who would win? Big Daveylicious: Just for that Superman. Tod: Big dummy we said Spiderman and Batman. Mike Frampton: That's Mr. Big Dummy to you. Big Daveylicious: I'll handle this. (Big Daveylicious picks the kid up and throws him into the street. The camera stays on Frampton and Big Daveylicious as burning rubber and tires screeching is heard. Frampton turns to Big Daveylicious with a serious face.) Big Daveylicious: Let's just walk slowly away from here. (The two men walk slowly down the street. Suddenly Mike stops. He begins to shake. He falls down. Big Daveylicious helps him up and Mike stops shaking.) Big Daveylicious: Are you O.K. Mike? Mike Frampton: Mike? I am not Mike. Big Daveylicious: Oh Sh**! Who are you then? Mike Frampton: I'm faster than a speeding bullet. I can leap over tall buildings in a single bound, I'm... Big Daveylicious: Superman? Mike Frampton: SUPERMAN! (Mike runs to a wall and jumps. He slams into the wall and falls back as the scene fades out.)
