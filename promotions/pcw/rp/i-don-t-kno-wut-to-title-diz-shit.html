| I don't kno' wut to title diz shit |

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.9.02 (~It's a beautiful day as the sun elegantly stretches over the horizon. You know its morning as the drug dealers begin to creep back into their holes and the sun begins to spread its neon lights over the tall red brick buildings of the projects. Through one of the apartment windows, sunlight splits through the weed clouded and eases up the wall in lines as a result of the blinds blocking full lighting. An alarm suddenly goes off making that annoying sound continuously over and over waking up next door neighbors. A red indigo light forms; 3:00am but no one is there to notice it. The red see-through cordless phone sitting on the small oak wood dresser begins to ring which echoes through the empty apartment. Finally it stops ringing and just like normal, at this time of hour, gun shots would blast in the air but this time there were no screams. No police sirens getting louder as they come into view, not even the crazy woman that would run into the streets with a shower cap on her head wearing a blue gown screaming, 'Oh lord, not my baby!' Then figuring out it wasn't her child and ripping him off before walking back in the house. Next thing you know, music begins blasting at truck volume level and an exodus of black people walk up rowdy than a bitch. Waking up the elderly, all the youth of the projects through a huge party. Half butt naked strips freak dance with freaky men, gangstas get they thug on, pimps get they pimp on, and low riders get they bounce on. A slight breeze of wind ripples his silk black Fubu shirt as lil' Thug takes his new found glory, which is the Tag team title, and places it on a bench near him. Straighten his platinum chains around his neck, he check the time on his platinum silver watch with diamonds placed along the perimeter of it. 3:43am, lil' Thug looks around the massive crowd for Sheeca and Buuday which are no where in site.~) 'You gon' flow or not?'
