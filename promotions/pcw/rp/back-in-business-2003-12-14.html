Back In Business!

14 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-12-03 Fighting against the cold winter air, the evening sun shines down upon the land producing twinkling diamonds in the waters of the Bayou D’Arbonne. The shimmering light dances in the fairly calm swamp to the gritty sound of machinery off in the distance. The noisy vibrations send tiny ripples along the bayou prompting a couple of alligators to rise up, poking their heads above the glassy surface and examining the commotion on land. Quickly they delve back into the depths out of sight as loud clattering erupts, followed by the diminishing roars of the machines and the rumbling calls of one man to the other. ???: How does that look?! The burly gentleman in the crane motions to the foreman who stands holding a clipboard checking off various points, seemingly ignoring the crane operator. Crane Operator: HEY BOSS! HOW’S IT LOOKIN’??? Sighing, he waits until the last of the machinery have died down to a comfortable silence before calling out once more. Crane Operator: Yo, boss how’s it looking up there?! Once more the foreman ignores the man in the crane, favouring his clipboard until another worker steps forward, tapping the foreman on the shoulder prompting him to turn towards the crane operator. Crane Operator: Well?! Foreman: What? Crane Operator: HOW DOES IT LOOK?! Foreman: WHAT? It is at this point the man inside the crane realises the foreman is wearing soundproof headphones, partially hidden by his long, slicked back hair. Gesturing the removal of the headphones the foreman complies, taking off both the headphones and his hardhat allowing his hair to flop freely into its natural state. Along with the dark bags under his eyes and stubbly goatee, each feature brings into recognition the identity of the foreman, none other than Skitzo. Crane Operator: Finally, geez! How is the sign looking Mr. Williams? Skitzo: WHAT? The crane operator sighs and cusses under his breath, failing to see the humour in Skitzo’s ignorance before pointing to the giant sign before both of them. Skitzo: Ah yeah the sign, she look perfect Roy, great job! Throwing his hands in the air frustratingly the crane operator carefully backs the machine into a suitable parking space before exiting his vehicle and advancing towards Skitzo, who hugs the clipboard closely while grinning back cheekily at the dissatisfied workman. The last couple of workers take off home leaving Skitzo and the crane operator alone to talk. Roy: Ok now that the sign’s in place you’re ready to rumble Mr. Williams! Skitzo extends his hand meeting the workman’s and after a hearty handshake they turn towards the finished product standing mightily on the bayou’s edge. Skitzo: I knew the day PCW closed I had to have something to fall back onto, and now mon frère with your help I’ve got that very thing…just in time for the goddamn PCW to re-open. Roy: Well Mr. Williams our company was more than happy to oblige, lord knows your recommendation would be a great benefit to us! Skitzo: Well when you got a business such as yours with hard working employees, good nature and excellent skills it’s my pleasure to recommend you. Roy: Yeah, oh and if you don’t mind…would an autograph be out of-… Skitzo rolls his eyes and smiles politely at the worker as he scribbles onto more paperwork on the clipboard. Skitzo: Yah-ha, we can get to that as soon as business is finished man. I got the paperwork back from the head office today so everything including the extension and rebuilt portions should be paid and accounted for… Signing his name on a final dotted line Skitzo hands over the clipboard to the worker, who tucks it under his arm and tips his hardhat in thanks. Skitzo: If there are any problems or anything get your office to call me and I’ll deal with whatever’s up, if not then come back for the machines anytime tomorrow! Roy: Sure! Once again, great doing business with you and hey good luck with your wrestling in future Mr. Williams! Skitzo nods then remembering the autograph takes the clipboard back, scribbling his name on a spare sheet of A4 nestled at the back of the important documents. Winking at Roy, Skitzo hands the clipboard back and shakes his hand once more before turning back to his creation. Skitzo: Thanking you. Skitzo waits as the worker heads back to his car and waves goodbye once more as he takes off into the distance. With a sigh of relief Skitzo tucks his hardhat and headphones under his arm, smiling back at the great achievement before him, his eyes welling up proudly. Skitzo: This is fuckin’ amazing………I got my own GATORADE FACTORY!!! WOOHOO! YOU BEAUTY! Dropping to his knees in rejoice, Skitzo throws the hardhat and headphones in the air, embracing his new establishment with open arms…then gasping in horror and looking up one second too late to see the hardhat succumbing to gravity all too quickly. Letting out a shrieking yell is all he can do… CLUNK! The hat hits him directly between the eyes knocking him flat on his ass. Lifting his head slowly up off of the mucky ground he spits out a small clump of grass. Skitzo: …I’ll get you one of these days gravity…your days are numb-… THUNK! …Until the headphones hit him, knocking his face back into the dirt. Lying there for a moment Skitzo composes himself in the mud before suddenly leaping to his feet and punching the air wildly. Skitzo: THIS WON’T RUIN MY DAY! Skitzo: OR MINE! Skitzo: FUCKING BUMPS TO THE HEAD?! Skitzo: This can’t be good for my personalities… Sighing, Skitzo grabs his things and fetches his keys from his pocket, heading to his new and improved home. As the sun sinks further beneath the horizon the lasting light catches the building, illuminating it for the entire bayou to see. Sitting close to the shore is Skitzo’s house, the regular old whitewashed two-storey modified apartment building with balcony. Now however, connected to the house via a little passageway from the back door and facing inland is the newly constructed factory building in all of its glory with the Gatorade logo emblazoned high upon the wall. Behind the surrounding 30ft high steel security fencing topped with the finest razor wire sits the pride and joy of the Mental One. Inside Skitzo’s home however the longest reigning former PCW Extreme Champion is not celebrating, for business is not over just yet. Sitting on a plush leather sofa Mr. Psycho dials up a number on his telephone, waiting eagerly with each passing ring. Finally a voice pips up on the other end of the line much to Skitzo’s satisfaction. Ignoring the gibberish at the beginning of the call mentioning monitored calls and so forth, Skitzo clears his throat as the receptionist on the other end offers their assistance. Skitzo: *Ahem* Hello, my name is Mr. Williams could you put me through to David Lamb please? Receptionist: Certainly Mr. Williams and what is the nature of your call? Skitzo: It’s in regards to the workforce for the new factory in the Bayou D’Arbonne in Louisiana… Receptionist: Ok Mr. Williams I’m putting you through now, please hold… As the cheesy music plays over the line Skitzo covers the receiver and begins twitching. Skitzo: If I have to be polite to one more person today I’m gonna fuckin’ crack! Skitzo: There’s always Visiting Hour at the old folk’s home tomorrow, no fear! Skitzo: Ah the old folks, god bless the incoherent old fuc-… ???: Hello this is David Lamb, is that you Mr. Williams? Skitzo: Errs………sure Mr. Lamb how are you today! Mr. Lamb: Very well sir, I hear the factory is complete that’s great to hear! I suppose you’ll be calling about employees… Skitzo: Certainly sir… Mr. Lamb: Well I’m not the man you should be calling, heh…but…I think I have the memo here…give me a couple of minutes… Skitzo nods then cocks an eyebrow as to why he did so over the phone. He can hear the chairman mumbling to himself and begins rolling his eyes and sighing. Unfortunately the chairman hears a sigh as he picks up the phone again with the desired information. Mr. Lamb: Have some manners Mr. Williams, please. Skitzo: Oh no sorry sir I’m just so relieved this is all finally done y’know! Mr. Lamb: Alright. Ok I have the information here and…we have 4 trainees and 31 people transferring from other branches in California and Illinois. They should be with you next Monday, and you know when the first deliveries are right? Skitzo: Sure do sir! Mr. Lamb: In that case I have a meeting to get to, remember we’ll be keeping tabs on your progress as spokesperson with Primetime Championship Wrestling as well as your managerial position with our new branch. Skitzo: Ok Mr. Lamb I won’t disappoint! Mr. Lamb: Good to hear Mr. Williams, goodbye. The chairman hangs up before Skitzo can bid farewell himself. Hanging up, Skitzo reaches for his nearby celebratory bottle of champagne and with a pop of the cork guzzles the fizzy alcohol down gleefully. Skitzo: How many other wrestlers can say they own their own soft drink factory! Skitzo: Who’d want to? Skitzo: I can’t help but wonder why I invested in this factory as opposed to helping to get PCW back in business… Skitzo: The possibility of another fucking airship is enough to turn anyone away. Skitzo: I LOVED THAT GODDAMN AIRSHIP! Skitzo: I’m partial to Gatorade myself… Skitzo stares out into the bayou, as the golden sky turns darker with time the dancing diamonds of the sun become small twinkling stars. As the scene fades out the Mental One notices alligators begin moving into the bayou, gutturally roaring into the night. Skitzo: Yes…Gatorade…heh…heh… Fin
