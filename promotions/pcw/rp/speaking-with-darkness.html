Speaking with Darkness...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dream-World Darkness…sheer and utter distortion, that’s all you are, that’s all you’ll ever become Akiyama. You can’t get rid of me; you can never run from me. I am your instinct; I am your soul. And the classics, I am the manifestation of your heart. All the hatred you bear, all the sorrow and guilt. You are a sea of Darkness and in the end, I’m proud to call you home. I am your never-ending nightmare. I created it; I live in it. I tell you 'oh yes, you have atoned. But in the end that heinous sin will still be here. For you see, I am what’s left.' Heh, heh… I live off your pain and suffering, and there is nothing you can do about that. Oh sure, you have succeed in controlling me, but you will never get rid of me. You still possess too much harsh emotion. Just…get it off your chest big guy! Hee…heh. Ahh… Your torture is my pleasure, but I think you have already realized this. Ooo… I can hear your heart racing. '…Wake me from this nightmare…please…' Your blood is running cold… 'Darkness, leave me be…let me sleep in peace…' The sweat beads are forming; your temperature is rising. 'Darkness! I will and do control you. Yes, I am your… home, but it is still my soul!' And it shall be mine… Akiyama shot his almond brown eyes open and found… nothing. You see, in this black abyss, you find what? Nothing…? Yes? I am right there. The only things that you can even see are your eyes. 'So there still is light,' Akiyama responded weakly into darkness still panting from the adrenaline. Oh the Irony! What color are they? '…' Ahh… the realization has hit. I must admit Akiyama, they are quite beautiful. That lovely almond brown color. They suit you quite well. Just as your hands… 'I can agree to that,' Akiyama concurred with almost emptiness. 'My hands are just as red as your claws.' But then again, you too have a claw. 'My claw is of flesh, nails, and bone.' You and I both know the truth behind that… Akiyama drew in a small, subtle laugh, and then sighed. 'Yes, we do. Figuratively speaking, of course.' Very good! You and I really aren’t that different. We bot— Akiyama cut Darkness short. 'Don’t take me for such a fool, Darkness. I am willing to admit that you and I are one in the same. We share the same body, the same mind…' He now cut into himself. The same soul? Those three simple words sent Akiyama’s mind into anarchy. He wasn’t willing to admit to something that he himself felt to be true. There was a harsh reality to what Darkness had said, and even subtler than a brick wall itself, there was a reality to what he wanted. Hmmm, I can tell by the silence, you’ve come to some conclusion, that, could it be, you’ve come to the truth Akiyama? 'Darkness, you aren’t going to make me say it. I’m not willing to admit it. Why? you ask. Oh, hell, ask it.' Why, Akiyama? Humor me, please, I beg of you, please… enlighten me. 'He, he…ah, well, if your going to be arrogant about it, then no.' Suddenly a pain shot through Akiyama’s shoulder blade and he let out a groan. Do not toy with me. You and I are the same body. 'Do not underestimate me, Darkness. My Will is stronger than your ‘torture’. And just remember, your torture is my pleasure.' With that said, a wry smile slipped across Akiyama’s face, and he indulged into his nightmarish sleep once more. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- After some silence, Akiyama’s mind felt at ease. 'No one listening, no one watching, the solitude of silence. A solace all its own,' he said in his mind. He opened his mind’s eye to find what he wished be gone… 'That’s one hell of a will ya got there Aki.' With that said, Akiyama unconsciously dropped his mouth. A winged demon of his own height and size flapped and flew in front of him. His massive bat wings never missed a beat, his skin was that of an alligator’s purple scale and his long white fangs flashed with a 200-watt grin that made him seem even more malicious. 'You seem…surprised,' the demon said placing himself delicately on the ground and crossing his arms and wrapping his wings about him. 'It’s like looking into a mirror…' Akiyama mumbled under his breath while putting his head down. 'Yes, it is, isn’t it? Figuratively speaking…of course.' 'Of course.' Akiyama replied slowly bringing his head up as his alter ego walked passed. Akiyama and his companion stood in a black Void with barely enough light to nothing more than the area around. Akiyama was wearing black pants, and a black shirt with no shoes. His arms hung quietly at his sides and his mind raced with a thousand thoughts at once. His long black and brown hair helped him blend into the nothingness around. Darkness stopped, gave Akiyama that once-over look and continued walking passed. As he did, the areas would slowly light up. 'Welcome to my humble abode. This is the place inside of you that happily houses me. Quite eerie, I know. But home nonetheless.' Akiyama got straight to the point, 'There is something more to me that you want, isn’t there?' 'You were right Aki, I shouldn’t take for a fool.' 'And knowing you, you’ll do anything to get to me.' The demon’s face once again lit up with a smile. He opened his wings and unfolded his arms and placed his claws idly at his sides. He turned to the side and kept his head down, but Akiyama knew Darkness saw him, if not only sensed him. Akiyama actually began to study the fiend with almost a sense of pity. In the position he stood, he hid no secrets; portrayed no lies. In a way, Akiyama felt his own eyes on himself, and in truth, that’s exactly what was going on; he was on the outside looking in, and Darkness held the door wide open. After a few minutes Darkness lifted his head out of who knows what kind of thoughts and asked, 'Are you finished?' Akiyama blinked and nodded a few times to answer. 'What did you hope to find? A weakness, a fear? Trust me, my friend, you won’t. Let me ask you, Aki: did you ever really stop to think of what I am?' 'Yes, once.' 'And…' 'And nothing. I guess I felt that you were always here.' 'Which is precisely true.' Darkness let out a small giggle that flashed his fangs. 'I told you, I am the manifestation of your heart Akiyama. I am all your fears, your hatred, your guilt, and anything else along those lines.' He began to walk towards the stolid man and with each step he took, his form began to change, from the feet up. His clawed feet suddenly had black boots; next, his legs were covered with navy blue pants. Akiyama watched Darkness go from demon, to wrestler. The next thing he knew, he saw himself as he is now…black vinyl, black tape…and… 'The PCW World Title...” This Akiyama said directly to Akiyama. 'What do you wish to accomplish with this, Darkness?' 'Well, a reaction would be nice. Always so critical. Never shows emotion, either. That’s how I came about.' Suddenly, Darkness walked a way and Akiyama sighed. 'How about this?' A sweet voice asked from behind and Akiyama immediately knew what form Darkness now tried on. 'Leave her out of this.' 'She is your nightmare, your sin. She is the form I most always take Akiyama. It’s yours in that makes you so vulnerable.' Suddenly two wings ripped out of the poor girl’s back and Darkness reappeared, smiling all the way. Darkness turned towards Akiyama and placed a claw forward to where a small pool had appeared. 'Take a look Aki, and tell me what you see.' Akiyama quickly turned around and retorted, 'I know what I’m going to see.' Darkness raised his brow line as to say What? 'You.' 'So, you are willing to admit that you and I are the same soul?' As if the HAND OF FATE had slapped him, a thought flashed across Akiyama’s almond eyes. He sheepishly cracked a grin across one side of his mouth, slowly, deliberately began to walk towards the demon and spoke. 'No, but I am willing to admit that you and I are the same.' 'What?' Darkness asked puzzled at the look on Akiyama’s face more than at what the man said. 'Despair, hatred, guilt. I am all that, and nothing more. I am you, just in human form, my friend. Hell, I’m even equipped to say that you and I are kindred spirits. But, there is one thing that makes you different from me. The reason that I have my soul.' 'Tell me then.' 'No, not yet. Do remember telling me that you had no weakness or fear?' Akiyama now fully smiled. 'Liar…' That single word drifted in the air and Darkness’s eyes dilated at the sight of Akiyama. 'You haven’t a soul. And I am your prized possession, Darkness. Like a son to a father.' 'So, if you and I are so similar, what makes you possess what I cannot?' Darkness said feebly to Akiyama turning away. But before he knew it, Akiyama was right in front of his face. And the next thing he said, he made sure he articulated every single word and syllable. 'My one… single, shred…of humanity.' -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Akiyama suddenly opened his eyes to find a young woman with (for lack of a better word) shock of spiky blond hair in front of him. He was sitting on a park bench in what seemed to be the middle of the day. Akiyama took in one deep breath and the young woman spoke, 'You were having a nightmare…'
