Praise be to Allah for Morons!!!

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/20/02 The following is a chat transcript from an online interview the Chef gave to Culinary Terrorist Monthly Magazine. The opinions of the Chef are not to be taken seriously, as the whole idea of the promo is to be ridiculously and mind numbingly stupid. ******************* *Cheffry247 has entered the chat room “YoungPolynesianWomenForSale”. Dr.Demento59: A nickel! SunYatSinMojo: No! Fifty Dollar! Dr.Demento59: Listen, I can get a Brazilian young girl for a dime on the streets of Rio, are you going to lower your prices or am I going to have cut off my supply of Quaker Rice Cakes to your brothel? Cheffry247: Hey, are any of you going to interview me for Culinary Terrorist Monthly? SunYatSinMojo: Oh, hey Chef, just let me change into my other screen name… *SunYatSinMojo has logged off. Dr.Demento59: So he wasn’t selling young Asian women for anal sex? Cheffry247: Scram infidel, before I have your radio station nuked! Dr.Demento59: You can’t threaten me; I’m a famous radio personality!!! Cheffry247: That’s strike two you mongrel, don’t pester me, I have dangerous weapons at my command! Dr.Demento59: ***Mounts Cheffry*** I’m the Byron Calver of the radio world!!! Cheffry247: So be it… *Dr. Demento59 has logged off from the chatroom…permanently!!! Cheffry247: Ah, Praise Allah for the miracles of satellite missiles… *Q&Aguy138 has entered the chat room “YoungPolynesianWomenForSale”. Q&Aguy138: Ready to begin? Cheffry247: Ready as ever, Allah willing… Q&Aguy138: How does it feel to be interviewed by the world’s leading Violent Chef themed magazine? Cheffry247: It’s a great thrill to be interviewed, period… That piece of rancid pork the Boobie Boy has yet to interview me, and I’ve cried many nights wondering when he’ll make due on his promises. Q&Aguy138: Do you really cry over stuff like that? Cheffry247: No, not really. The only time I believed I’ve ever cried was when I saw Bertha naked for the first time…what a sight… Q&Aguy138: That horrible? Cheffry247: What are you talking about!?!? I was dramatically enchanted beyond any scope of the human mind. I really think Allah made us for each other… Q&Aguy138: I see… Well, how does it feel to be back in the PCW Tag Team division? Cheffry247: It’s a blast Mr. Interview Guy! I’ve always loved teaming, but I never really had a partner that put much effort into the team. I think Mr. Parshin is going to be a huge exception. Q&Aguy138: What do you think are your chances of becoming a Tag Team Champion for a third time with three different teams? Cheffry247: Well I really wanted to do this just to rub it in Boda’s face, you see. I feel my chances are great. Politically Incorrect is a great team, and possibly a future Hall of Fame team, but I am the greatest Tag Team Wrestler in PCW History. I’m also Allah’s Champion, and with Allah on my side, anything is possible! Q&Aguy138: That’s what I really wanted to talk to you about. You claim that Allah is with you during every match, is that really true or is it all just some cockamamie farce? Cheffry247: Cockamamie yes; a farce it is not! Allah is with me all the time. He holds my hand while I walk to the ring. He helps me back up when I get knocked down. He smites jobbers and infidels who stand in my way. He also tucks me in at night and kisses me on the cheek. Q&Aguy138: So he’s like a mother to you… Cheffry247: A Father! Definitely a father… Q&Aguy138: So Allah is a male? Cheffry247: He’s male all right, what do you think I keep a harem around for? Q&Aguy138: I see, well what do you consider is your greatest accomplishment in PCW? Cheffry247: Winning the World Title at Back to Old School in 2001 is right up there, but I’d have to say my first act in the PCW is still my favorite. Q&Aguy138: Why was that so memorable? Cheffry247: I served the Reverend a sausage, a freakin’ sausage!!! Q&Aguy138: And? Cheffry247: They were really good sausages, fried in truffle oil too! Q&Aguy138: Ah yes, truffle oil… Cheffry247: I’d say truffles are my biggest weakness as an avid food lover, I can be rendered spellbound instantly once I spot one. Other than that, I tend to watch my weight…well I’m a big fan of the Whopper, washed down with an ice-cold Perrier!!! BOO-YAAAHHHH!!! Q&Aguy138: Well, I guess we know what is your favorite food to eat, now what is your favorite food to prepare? Cheffry247: It would have to be my remoulade of radicchio, rutabagas, and raspberries, along with the sautéed @$$ of one of my foes, usually Kano… Q&Aguy138: I’ve often wondered when you talk about sautéing an enemy’s ass if you mean that literally or not? Cheffry247: Well I’m no cannibal, but Bertha even has trouble not eating herself let alone another human… Q&Aguy138: Yikes! So has Bertha ever eaten another human? Cheffry247: I’d wager quite a bit of money on Osama Bin Laden’s Discover saying there are more people in her stomach right now than in the Void. Q&Aguy138: My god… Cheffry247: My “Allah”, please… Q&Aguy138: Well thank you for the interview Mr. Chef, but my mom is calling me for dinner, we are having Stouffer’s Salisbury Steak!!! Chefry247: You’re mother? You’re in a popular magazine and you still live with your parents? Q&Aguy138: Yep…Cheffry247: What is the estimated circulation of the magazine? Q&Aguy138: Three people…possibly! Cheffry247: Three people!?!? More people read Boobie Boy’s column than your magazine, barely… Q&Aguy138: Yep, thanks again… Cheffry247: Yeah, thanks for wasting my time!!! SHALALALALALALALA!!! *Q&Aguy138 has logged off from the chat room…permanently!!! Cheffry247: MUWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! I love pushing buttons… *Dr.Demento59 has entered the chat room “YoungPolynesianWomenForSale”. Cheffry247: You again!?!? I thought I nuked you like the Interview twerp! Dr.Demento59: Yes, you thought you did, but I jumped out of my house before the missile hit and drove to my friend’s house the next town over! Cheffry247: What friend? Dr.Demento59: Jerry Lee Lewis III’s house, why? Cheffry247: Is that 247 or 248 Maple Lane? Dr.Demento59: 248, why you ask? Cheffry247: Oh, nothing… *Dr.Demento59 has logged off from the chat room…permanently!!! Cheffry247: Praise be to Allah for morons… ***End of Transcript***
