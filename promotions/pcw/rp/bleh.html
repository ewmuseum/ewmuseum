Date: Bleh Skylar's last match was on PCW Turmoil one week ago. He'd been teamed with Gaz Green and Tessa Windsor to take on the team of Dorn Tracer, The Pharaoh, and Dante Jackson. Skylar Green would once more be facing the team of the Triumvirate, this time in a match with Brianna Washington on his side. She'd been dealin gwith Dorn, as Sky had with The Pharaoh. There was a difference, though. Dorn hated Brianna, and wanted to crack her spine. Brianna, of course, felt likewise. Skylar didn't hate The Pharaoh. Titu was just another opponent to him. The Pharaoh, however, was consumed with his inability to beat Skylar on his own. It was all that he could think about. Skylar challenge The Pharaoh to multiple contests, both to prove to himself that any win he racked up was not a fluke, and also to give The Pharaoh enough chances at Skylar that his losing streak against the blind one was solely his own fault. Skylar's team had showed impressive unity, despite some early problems (mainly caused by Gaz Green). Skylar and Tessa got along well enough; he even let her carry the PCW World Title. In fact, he let her carry both belts down to the ring. The match was a display of teamwork, though. His partners were former tag team champions, after all. Then again, they were champions with people that they wanted to team with. Still, they were tag team champions, and they managed to work well enough that the team won. Tessa Windsor got her Television Title shot and she won it. Team Green, Too. A name made in jest at the people in the team, but it worked. And they all had their respective championships. Tessa had her TV Title, Gaz had her... his Continental Title, and Skylar with the World Title. Now he was wrestling in a tag match with Brianna Washington. She's a former Extreme Champion. Skylar held that belt, for about the same period as Brianna, actually. He wasn't quite a cruiserweight anymore by the time he got there, but he won it anyway. Brianna hadn't been in many tag matches since joining PCW. She had a decent record though. Her team with Matt Jackson even defeated Violence Jack and SOCIETY in a main event. She would be Skylar's partner. The two never really ran into each other before. Not really shared words or anything. Especially with Brianna moonlighting as the Druid all last month, opportunities to talk were almost non-existent. Skylar, though, had confidence in himself, and in his teammate. He had confidence in their ability to work well as a team. They had never partnered together, but neither had he, Gaz, and Tessa, and look at the result of that match. Brianna Washington hadn't teamed with Tessa or Miss Moonlight when they faced off AJ Trinidad, Adam Stryker, and Manuel Labor, but they won nonetheless. Teams which have no experience together can win. If they have chemistry. Doesn't need to be an instant connection, if they can merely be on the same page in the ring (liking your partner is a bonus but not exactly required) then the team will do well. Experience as a team does not assure victory. That would work against Dorn Tracer and The Pharaoh, who had experience as a team. Further working against that team was the fact that both members had separate agendas. Dorn Tracer would want to defeat Brianna Washington. The Pharaoh would want to defeat Skylar, especially since he would win the PCW World Title. Dorn, however, wouldn't have much interest in helping Pharaoh win the PCW Title, and The Pharaoh likewise wouldn't particularly care if Dorn Tracer wanted to pin Brianna. Skylar and Brianna, however, could work together. Skylar didn't care whom he pinned, or who on his team pinned whom. Together, they would be a good team. They would work toward a common goal. Winning.
