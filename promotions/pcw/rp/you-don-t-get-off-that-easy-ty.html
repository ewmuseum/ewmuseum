Title: You don't get off that easy Ty Featuring: White Mexican Date: Feb. 26/2002 “So how much we get?” Our view comes into focus the image of a Camero Cop car is situated on the shoulder of a highway. The figure talking is hidden inside the car but the cop with the short brush cut who stands outside the passenger door is flipping through a large stack of bills as dust floats clouding our view. “Fifteen… sixteen…” As the officer slowly thumbs through the bills the dust settles we zoom in closer on the cops face as he smiles widely. “What, how much?” Comes the yell of excitement inside the car. The cop chuckles as he pats the top of the car and walks around to the driver’s side of the car. Our view cuts to the inside of the car focused in on the driver side as the door swings open and the officer slides into the seat while whoever is in the passenger seat repeats the question again, “How much man?” The man slams the wad on the dash and looks over, as do we to the familiar face of Mexi with an anxious look on his face. “Twenty-Two grand Mexi” spits out the cop. Mexi laughs, “Awesome, told you he’d make it worth your while.” The cop nods as cranks the starter causing the car to roar into action. “You want me to drop you off where now?” questions the yet unnamed officer. “Who said anything about dropping me off yet? Your not gonna let him get away that easy are you?” Mexi says. The cop chuckles and pulls out onto the highway quickly accelerating. “You want me to pull him over again?” He asks. Mexi nods, “What’s he going to do call the bribe police?” “Your evil.” Mexi shrugs, “It’s not like Nelson doesn’t deserve it. He choose the wrong Mexican to F*CENSORED*K With. There is silence for a couple of minutes as the car races down the highway speeding to catch the speeding CEO of PCW and Greed Inc. Eventually the speeding cop car catches the black limo hummer weaving into on coming traffic and cutting off a red minivan. “Hit the sirens.” Mexi orders and the cop obliges setting the cherries and sirens on. The cop car pulls out and passes the minivan riding up on the bumper of the hummer. We cut to the inside of the hummer and the angry face of Tyler Nelson can be seen and lucky for us we can’t hear what he says over the sirens. Nelson pulls the vehicle off the road and he slams the steering wheel in frustration as we cut back inside cop car. Our officer friend opens the door but before he can exit Mexi grabs his arm. “I got this one.” He says as he pulls out a pair of old school cop glasses that cover half his face and as he steps out the car he slaps a cop hat on his back. We cut to outside and follow Mexi who is hardly recognizable in his police get up. He calmly walks up the driver side door and Nelson already has his hand extended out the window with a large wad of cash in hand. “I don’t want your money!” Mexi yells as he swats the wad of cash out of Tyler’s hand and it skids in front of the hummer. “What do you think your doing that was 20 grand!?!?” Shouts Nelson from inside as he turns his attention to the officer. Mexi stares at him very seriously and crosses his arms. “You giving me a ticket or you just gonna stand there and stare all day?” Questions the angered Nelson. Mexi steps back as he orders Nelson, “Out of the car sir.” “You have got to be…” “I said out of the car!” “Fine!” Nelson yells as he whips open the car and stomps out. “Assume the position…” “What?!?!?” Nelson cuts him off. Mexi grabs Tyler by the right arm and slams him face first into the side of the hummer and holds pins him with his forearm. “You better get used to this position where you’ll be going.” Nelson tries to turn and look at to him the unknown officer but Mexi keeps him pressed firmly against the side and he tries to reply but it comes out barely audible, “Does police brutality ring a bell?” Mexi chuckles as he spins Tyler around and holds his forearm at his throat pinning the man he faces on Friday night against the hummer. With his left hand Mexi reaches up and slowly pulls off the shades staring right into Nelson’s eyes that light up with rage as he recognizes that the cop is Mexi. “It can’t be police brutality if I’m not a cop Ty.” Mexi states pulling back and letting Nelson up. “You son of a bitch! You’ll pay for this.” Nelson yells angrily as he looks ready to attack. Mexi glances over at his shoulder and waves at the real cop who is leaning against the hood of the cop camera, then back at Nelson, “Of course he is.” Nelson grumbles as he climbs back into the hummer and starts up the car. “See ya soon Ty.” Mexi yells as Nelson peels off the shoulder of the highway running over the wad of cash kicking the bills up into the air. Mexi slides the oversized sunglasses on and walks back to the cop car as the bills and bills of Tyler’s crooked money blow in the wind and we fade out. ---- View White Mexican's Biography
