Date: All Saints Day(November 1) [We return to Gladiator Arena after a commercial for Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. We see two large pedestals one coloured red, on blue, and a giant mat surrounding the floor around them.] Mike: Welcome back to American Gladiators, event number four is The Joust. Each contender has thirty seconds to attempt to knock the gladiator off the platform with out giant Q-tips known as pugel sticks. If they knock the gladiator off its worth ten points, a tie is worth five points, and if you get knocked off? You get nothing! Larry: Coming into this event, Eddie Mandrake is on the verge of elimination being five points off from the lead. He needs to defeat the gladiator in the joust in order to advance to the final events. Mike: And that won’t be an easy task as he takes on the awesome Sabre in this event! Larry: Sabre is very difficult to even get a single shot on his, as opposed to actually defeating him. Well, I guess we will see how Eddie does against him, because we are ready to go. Larry #2: ENGARDE! (Whistle Blows!) Mike: Sabre trying the overhand shot, Eddie blocked it! He immediately goes low and trips Sabre with the pugel stick…Wow, Eddie is incredibly quick with the stick. Larry: Indeed, he is very… POMP! Sabre: ARGH!! Mike: Incredible shot by Eddie onto the chest of Sabre! Sabre just rolled off platform and down to the mats. [The crowd cheers for Eddie, but Eddie just doesn’t seem to care. He throws the pugel stick to the mats below, and Eddie simply looks pissed off!] Larry: How can he look that way? He defeated Sabre, he got ten points, and he may have possibly saved himself from certain defeat! Let’s find out why with Mike. [The camera goes to the floor where Mike and Eddie are standing by. Eddie is perspiring lightly.] Mike: Eddie, you have probably survived onto the Eliminator, but it will depend on the performances of Wesley Berry and Rico. How do you see yourself at the end of the event? Eddie: I see myself moving on, and I want Raisin Bran gone! Mike: What the hell does Raisin Bran have to do with anything?! Eddie: Two Scoops…Raisin Bran? Bah you’re too stupid to comprehend it. Mike: You also seemed a little angry on that platform…was that intensity? Eddie: Hell no, I was pissed! I should have beat Sabre in about five seconds less that what I did on the platform! I’m kicking my own ass on that one. Mike: Well, if everything goes to your plans…atlasphere is up next, and Laser is going to take you out personally. Do you have any comments that trash talk? Eddie: Fucking right, Laser…expect me to come heavy and hard asshole! Mike: Back to you Larry. [Back to the action shots, where we now see Wesley Berry getting ready to take on Sabre.] Larry: Up next, Wes Berry going to take on Sabre… Mike: This ought to be good. Larry #2: ENGARDE~! (WHISTLE) Mike: Sabre coming out hard and heavy! POMP! POMP! POMP! POMP! WHISTLE~! Larry: Four solid shots send Two Scoops flying a good two feet in the air before flying off the pedestal. That means…No points for Wesley Berry. Mike: Which means only one thing…Two Scoops may be automatically eliminated from the competition; we’ll have to see what Rico does here against Sabre. Larry: That’s right, both men are ready to go. Larry #2: ENGARDE~! (WHISTLE) Mike: Both men going hard here. Rico delivering and landing his shots…Oh man are they rocking Sabre here…He may lose his balance here! Larry: Can Rico follow it up…NO! He cannot as Sabre regains his balance and now he begins to go onto the attack. Mike: Twenty seconds left and Rico goes for the overhand left…Sabre ducks and Rico now off balance, and Sabre’s trying to close it up! Larry: Can Rico regain his balance? He looks in a back way here… Mike: Ten seconds left, all he needs to do he regain his balance and he’s got five points here! Larry: He’s trying to regain it…Sabre’s not giving him any quarter however! POMP! POMP! POMP! POMP! (WHISTLE!) Mike: Rico survived the onslaught and received five points…So after four events, here’s how it looks…Eddie Mandrake has redeemed himself here and has re-taken the lead with twenty-five points, Rico Costantino is in second place with twenty-three points, and Wesley Berry is eliminated with his previous round leading nineteen points. Larry is on the floor with Two Scoops. [Down to the floor with Wesley Berry and Larry. Wes looks a little dejected, most likely because he knows he’s leaving.] Larry: Wes…I hate to be the bearer of bad news, but with your nineteen points…you have been eliminated. Wesley: Well, I guess the Two Scoops is only at One Scoop right now. Larry: Do you have any parting words? Wesley: Well, I had a lot of fun again with you guys. The competition has stepped incredibly since I was back in the arena here, I want to thank you guys for inviting me, and it was great. Larry: Well, congratulations, and I hope that we will see you again soon. Wesley “Two Scoops” Berry everyone! [The crowd cheers as Wesley Berry waves at the crowd, the camera then pulls over to Rico and Eddie, with Mike standing by.] Mike: Well guys, it is down to you two…Atlasphere is next, Rico what is your strategy? Rico: Well Mike, I’m just going to try to score as much as possible and stay ahead of the Gladiator. With that, I should score a lot. Mike: Eddie, how about you? Eddie: Kill that son of a bitch Laser, and hope to take a few spectators with me. Mike: Uh, yeah…Give it up for our finalists Rico and Eddie. [The crowd seems a little weary because of Eddie’s threats. Only half respond and Rico waves at the crowd, Eddie just walks away. We now go to a red, white, and blue, bordered screen within the screen, we see a Gladiator in a large cage, wearing a football helmet. On his clothing…it says Gemini.] Gemini: RAGE IN THE CAGE, BABY…ATLASPHERE IS NEXT!!! [Fade to commercial]
