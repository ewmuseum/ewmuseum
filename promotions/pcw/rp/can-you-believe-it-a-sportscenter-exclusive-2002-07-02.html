Can you believe it? A Sportscenter Exclusive...

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 2, 2002 NYC, New York 8:25 pm Robert Yarney: [walks into the building and looks around] Wow.... Legend Inc.: Well, it appears that the ESPN company has done quite well for itself over the years. Robert Yarney: I'd say so. Well, what do you expect from a TV station that gets every male from the ages of 15 - 45. That's a broad viewership. Legend Inc.: Don't forget about ESPN the Magazine! Robert Yarney: [nods] That's right! With every subscription you get a free ESPN the Magazine Pullover coat! Legend Inc.: [puts on a ESPN the Magazine Pullover] You mean, like this! Robert Yarney: Tom...where did you get that? Legend Inc.: They were giving them out outside, didn't you see it? Robert Yarney: No I didn't. You know, that looks really lame. I mean, you're just giving ESPN some free publicity. Legend Inc.: So what? It's their television station any way. Besides, I'm sure this will really impress them. ESPN Coordinator: [comes up to Robert and Tom] Are you Tom Lexian? Legend Inc.: That's me. ESPN Coordinator: I'm the coordinator for tonight's Sportscenter. We need you backstage immediately - the interview will occur promptly at 8:38 pm with Stuart Scott. Legend Inc.: [looks at Robert] Booyah! Robert Yarney: Oh stop being lame. Legend Inc.: I'm not being lame. I'm being excited. It’s every guy’s dream to be on Sportscenter. Robert Yarney: You know, a few hours ago you were scared to death to do this interview. Now you can't wait to get grilled by Stuart Scott. What's the deal? Legend Inc.: There's no deal. Robert Yarney: What? Stop bullshitting me around Tom. I can see it in your face. Legend Inc.: Just like any other good actor I thrive on attention. I live off the thrill of being in front of the camera. I can smell it, Robert. It's intoxicating. Robert Yarney: No, I think you're intoxicated. Maybe you're still hung over from those shots you took earlier. Legend Inc.: I'm fine, Robert. Robert Yarney: You look a look little too energetic to me, Tom. Why don't you sit down for a second?... Legend Inc.: [pushes Robert away] I told you I'm fine. Geez. What's your problem? Robert Yarney: [sighs] This is an important interview, Tom. Really important. I don't want you to screw it up for yourself. Legend Inc.: If I screw it up I won't blame you for it. I'm the one that took the shots, remember? Robert Yarney: I don't care if you blame me for it. I care whether or not you get over with the ESPN viewers. I know how bad you want to be respected, not matter how much you push this whole 'Lamest Wrestler In The World' thing. Legend Inc.: Alright, you got me. I do want to be respected. But I want to be respected in the ring. Not out here. I could care less if these viewers saw me as some great actor. I want them to realize that I am a world class athlete. I don't do that during promos. Robert Yarney: Then why don't you tell them what you're really all about. Legend Inc.: [begins to walk away] That's exactly what I intend to do, Robert. Exactly. * * * The scene changes to Tom walking into the backstage area. He looks around for a dressing room and finds one around the corner. He opens the door slowly and puts his bag down. He stretched his arms a bit and rubbed his eyes. Here he was...he was at the big show. Sportscenter and ESPN were two of the biggest names in cable television and sports entertainment. Hopefully Legend Inc. would be the same way. But should he really be serious about this interview? He knew that would cramp his style. Legend Inc. was a happy, go lucky kind of guy in the ring - let's not mention how he is in the ring. To change who he was....would be a definite stretch. Tom tried to forget about all the worries and just do his best. As he looks into the mirror he jumps back startled as he notices someone behind the door. Legend Inc.: [turning around suddenly] What the...you again?! The Joker: [closes the door] That's right. It's me. Big surprise, huh? Legend Inc.: Don't you ever do anything else besides sneak up on people? And how are able to get around without people noticing you? The Joker: Don't worry about that. Legend Inc.: I'm making it a point to worry about it. Now tell me. The Joker: You see these clothes? Legend Inc.: [looks at the Insiders ridiculous outfit] Yeah I see it. The Joker: What are the words that come to your mind when you see it? Legend Inc.: Stupid. The Joker: [rolls his eyes] Did you not take english when you were in grade school? I said 'words,' as in plural. Legend Inc.: Flamboyant, idiotic, ugly...? The Joker: Yeah, that's right. Keep going. Legend Inc.: Mismatching, ridiculous, out right horrible.... The Joker: Alright, that's enough. You see, people take a look at me and all of a sudden they don't want to know me, get it? They see some weirdo and immediately they look the other way. Legend Inc.: Are you trying to tell me that you get around because you are dressed funny? The Joker: Bingo! You are a lot smarter than you think, kiddo. Especially for someone who got a 250 on his SAT I Math. Legend Inc.: Hey, keep your voice down... The Joker: Pssh...like anyone gives a damn. You're a wrestler now, you can kick anyone's ass that tries to make fun of you. Besides, you're not the only one that sucks at math. Legend Inc.: You too? The Joker: [laughs] Are you kidding me? I was awesome at mathematics. The stuff is too simple if you ask me. But I didn't come here to discuss Algebra with you...I came to discuss the interview. Legend Inc.: Look we've already had our daily conversation...I don't need more Insider advice. The Joker: You see, that's where you're wrong. You need all the help you can get. Legend Inc.: Is that right? The Joker: Yeah, that's right. Robert was right...this is a very important interview and you'd better be on your best behavior. Legend Inc.: What? Is Chris Berman going to come out and yell at me if I decide to make some lame jokes? The Joker: I wish he would - maybe you'd stop being so damn cocky. Look at yourself, Lexian, you're representing the PCW and all of wrestling. You act like an idiot out there, you're doing everyone in the wrestling world a disservice. Legend Inc.: [watches the Joker closely] You mean that? The Joker: Damn straight. This is no joke. We've been trying to break back into the wrestling world for years now. We've been yearning to be mainstream for ages and now we're the closest we've ever been in twenty years... Legend Inc.: We're...? The Joker: This can't be the end of it. This could make or break everything. So just don't screw up. [leaves the room] * * * The scene changes to the two anchor men of the night, Dan Patrick and Kenny Mayne. Dan Patrick: Good evening everyone, I'm Dan Patrick... Kenny Mayne: And I'm Kenny Mayne. Dan Patrick: On the other side of the fence, we have the Bronx Bombers going up against the Red Sox. Well, one might say that this is just another match up between two over priced teams, it's not everyday you get Roger Clemens versus Pedro Martinez on the mound. Out to Yankee stadium we go... Top of first, Clemens is in trouble with runners on second and third with Nomar at the plate...Nomar, hot shot up the middle scoring two and giving him two ribbies...Red Sox up by two...Bottom of the Fourth now, Pedro is on a tear....The whif....Paul O'Neill...The whif....Bernie Williams....The whif...Chuck Knoblauch...The, The, The whif. Pedro would strike out 13 in the game total with a complete game shut out. Clemens only allowed two runs in 7 innings pitched but that's all Pedro needed. Red Sox 2 wins over NY 0. Kenny Mayne: Well, of course it's time for that all time special favorite, the Sportscenter Showcase. We tallied your votes from the ESPN website and in a very close voting the winner was...Inc...Legend Inc. [Video montage begins] Voice Over: He is a man possessed. A man with a dream. That dream: to become the World champion in PCW. Who is this man? He is the 'World Renowned' Inc...Legend Inc. [Scene begins with an opening shot of Legend Inc. as a child] Tom Lexian was born in San Francisco, CA. He attended regular schools, a regular post secondary education, but there was something inside of him that told him to ignore the degree he had received at USF for Biochemistry and pursue another dream: wrestling. After wrestling in many smaller promotions across the western area of the United States, Tom finally made it big in the UWF and RWW finally accomplishing the seemingly impossible by becoming a world champion. It was soon time to move on, as the DWF beckoned to him... As his career continued, he would land in other promotions such as IWO, oWa, and FSW. He would land the most fame in DWF, where he would dominate the competition. He took a brief hiatus only to return to PCW where he has held both the World Tag Team championship and recently the Continental title. [Scene begins with a shot of Gold and Glory] Legend Inc.’s PCW career would come to a crossroads at Gold and Glory where he would receive a shot at the PCW Continental title. He would have to climb the ring against three other of PCW’s finest in Xavier Kannon, Miyagawa, and Duke Williams. He would brave that ring and rise to the occasion, dropping Kannon with a huge power bomb he has labeled the Legend Death Drop, and eventually winning the title. Now Legend Inc. has a chance to prove that he is truly a champion. On turmoil, he will face Rent-A-Villain, a man who has not found his true self. Will Legend Inc. be successful? [Video Montage ends] Stuart Scott: I am joined by Tom Lexian, otherwise known as Legend Inc. Tom, I want to thank you for being on the show today. Legend Inc.: My pleasure. Stuart Scott: I first want to ask you about your match at Gold and Glory. What happened? Legend Inc.: Well, I told everyone that I could compete in the PCW. And it's true. I'm not BS'ing anyone when I tell them that I am the best technical, and all around wrestler in the world. But I just got off my game plan that night. Give credit where credit is due, and all three of the other wrestlers put out one heck of a match. But I pulled down deep and I derived some energy from the fans. They really allowed me to win that match. Stuart Scott: A lot of analysts say that you really had no shot against Duke Williams because you do notoriously ineffective matches against hardcore brawlers. Legend Inc.: That's completely unsubstantiated. While it's true that I've never won a hardcore title or an extreme belt, I don't feel the need to do so. If I wanted to, I probably could. But as I've told people before, I'm going for the real gold and that means being a world champion. I can handle myself just fine against brawlers and these so called 'Extreme Wrestlers.' Duke Williams was a good friend of mine, but I really don’t know where his head is at sometimes. Stuart Scott: You take on Rent-A-Villain on Turmoil. What kind of strategy do you have planned? Legend Inc.: This man has a strategy that I have never seen before. He’s trying to confuse me and everyone else by putting on this charade about his character. One day he’s a good guy, the next day he’s the man causing terror and havoc. I said from day one that he wasn’t to be trusted, and I’m sorry to say that I was right. We need more good guys on the team nowadays. Stuart Scott: Can we delve a little bit into Rent-A-Hero? I know we've spoken about him a bit already but... Legend Inc.: I've already said my piece about him. In closing, he's just a tad one dimensional. He can definitely bring the pain but can he finish me off? Or any of the others for example? That's what you have to do in a match, and I’m sorry to say that he will not be able to take me down. He and Snow couldn’t get the job done at Gold and Glory, and something tells me that his head just isn’t in the game. If you lose your mental focus, you’ll lose every time. Stuart Scott: And then there's Duke Williams… Legend Inc.: Ah yes... Stuart Scott: You both have been talking a lot of smack about each other. Tell us why you think he abhors you so much. Legend Inc.: I honestly don't get Williams sometimes. When he was a part of the Malice Crew, I really saw him as a friend and a comrade where we could get things down and clean up PCW. But what has happened? It’s nothing more then him being jealous. I just hope it doesn’t get any worse, and I hope we can settle all of this. The Malice Crew has been a welcome scene since all of this toil and trouble that PCW has been in. I hope Duke doesn’t ruin it all. Stuart Scott: A lot of people have criticized your title run by saying it was a tainted win. Legend Inc.: I think I won that title fair and square. I might have gotten here fast, but I am ready to prove myself. Stuart Scott: Do you think that justifies you winning the title? Legend Inc.: As far as I'm concerned I am the champion. I have confidence that I'll come out the victor in the match and hopefully this will lead to bigger and better things. I can’t look too far into the future, as that would be foolish, but I can say that Rent-A-Villain will be just a memory in everyone’s mind after Turmoil. Stuart Scott: Well I thank you for your time Mr. Lexian and good luck in your match. Legend Inc.: Thank you.
