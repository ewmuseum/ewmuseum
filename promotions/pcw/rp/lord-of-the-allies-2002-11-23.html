Lord of the Allies

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 23, 2002 &#039;Look at them all,&#039; Bellie~! whispers, her voice the epitome of forlorn hopelessness. Spread-eagled on a sprawling mound of pineapples, eyes wild and staring at the azure canvas above, she whines to her husband as she always does. Her clothes now hang in tattered patches from her various bits, ever-threatening to fall away and provide The Virgin Byron Calver with his first uncensored peak at a woman&#039;s naughty business. A sorrowful and pitying moan escapes her lips, and she forces herself to a sitting position so she may point at those she&#039;s directing Xamine Herr Kanyon&#039;s attention to. &#039;They&#039;re... like... so... savage.&#039; She rambles slowly and monotonously, her face taking on a ghastly cast. With each word her eyes seem to retreat further and further down into her core, as if trying to escape whatever madness has consumed the others by hiding in the darkness of her personal abyss. &#039;Like, much worse than they were when we got here. You haven&#039;t, like, noticed?&#039; She asks rhetorically, unknowingly stampeding over Herr Kanyon when he opens his mouth to speak, oblivious to everything but the thoughts raging through her mind. &#039;Look at them all,&#039; she whispers again, dripping with wondering horror. &#039;Running around, like, all haphazard and stuff... doing nothing... giving us nothing...&#039; she trails off slowly, then her face blanches. &#039;Xamine! They&#039;re like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; waiting to die! ...or something. We must stop the madness before it&#039;s too late!&#039; This heartfelt plea rises into the air like a morning dove, spreading its wings and careening off on some profound journey of survival. Herr Kanyon looks down at his wife with an utterly bemused look, one eyebrow cocked in an expression of mild concern. Slowly and patiently he raises his gun and takes aim at the dove, &#039;What the hell are you talking about, Bellie~!?&#039; The words are precise and measured, and he tilts his head to the side as they breach the air. &#039;We haven&#039;t even been here twenty-four hours.&#039; The bird plummets to the barren earth, lifeless, and Bellie~! feels its pain. &#039;Like, so?&#039; She demands in a huff, angry at the belittling of her thoughts. &#039;They&#039;re obviously nuts! Just, like, talk to them and you&#039;ll find out. None of them ever say anything important... And they&#039;re, like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; boring!&#039; She looks around as if about to express something in the strictest confidentiality, then leans closer to Xamine, smiling involuntarily as the spiky fruit probes her. &#039;I don&#039;t think they even, like, know what they&#039;re doing.&#039; She nods sagely after this, but Herr Kanyon just continues staring blankly. &#039;Uh... Bellie~!?&#039; Kanyon prompts, then points at the mound she&#039;s on. &#039;Look at all the pineapples we&#039;ve gotten! Every time I turn around there&#039;s five more!&#039; Herr Kanyon gushes, excitement entering into his face and voice with the feverish haste of Bob into a Girl Guide meeting. &#039;Their workrate is unbelievable! What else is there to know?&#039; Bellie~! picks up one of the pineapples and scrutinises it closely, turning it this way and that, examining it from all angles. &#039;But what does it mean?&#039; She finally begs, then takes a bite and winces. &#039;These pineapples mean, like, absolutely nothing. They&#039;re... &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt;...&#039; She pauses and rolls her eyes back into her head, thinking hard for the term to best describe all of the pineapples thrust forth by the Alliances competitors. &#039;Empty,&#039; she finally sighs, letting the pineapple drop to the ground with a unhealthy squishing sound. Huddled off to the side in a pathetic looking ball, The Virgin Byron Calver stirs from his stupor for a moment, then slumps back into his pitying and unexplained daze without a word. Herr Kanyon throws up his arms and turns away from them both, knowing better than to pursue the situation with his wife any further. Luckily, he is saved from Bellie~!&#039;s insightful ramblings by Spikey Funktacular, who bursts from the forest and comes charging directly over. Panting, he stops and begins curling up his lips in a smile, but Herr Kanyon quickly shields his eyes and holds out an arm, imploring the man not to unveil that heavenly smile. Amused, he acquiesces and gives a mock salute instead, which Kanyon doesn&#039;t return. &#039;Where the hell have you been? I sent you FSW punks out yesterday!&#039; The PCW CEO shouts, balling his hands into fists and pressing them against his sides. He looks around Funktacular, trying to spy his friends, then regards the other with palpable suspicion. &#039;Where the hell is Bologna? And Tyger? And... that other guy?&#039; &#039;Devil and Smells&#039;Em never met me on the other side,&#039; Funktacular responds with a dismissive shrug, then inclines his head towards the pineapple orchard. &#039;Timid&#039;s still over with the pineapples, gorging himself to death. Wouldn&#039;t even speak to me,&#039; Spikey shakes his head, perplexed, and steals a glance at the half-naked Bellie~!, now squirming in delight on her prickly bed, her thoughts of a moment ago forever lost. &#039;What do you mean you never met them?&#039; Kanyon demands impatiently, then perks up as he considers what wasn&#039;t said. &#039;So we &lt;i&gt;are&lt;/i&gt; on an island, then?&#039; Funktacular nods and his breathing returns to normal. &#039;Oh yeah, and not a very big one, either... Maybe a couple of kilometers across,&#039; he guesses, inexplicably holding his arms before him as if to measure the vast distance in a yard or so. &#039;Did you run into any bears or anything?&#039; Kanyon queries strangely, peering very hard at Funktacular. &#039;Uh...&#039; Funktacular looks around for some help, and takes the opportunity to sneak another peak at Bellie~! &#039;No, Herr Kanyon... Just Boris.&#039; A startled look crosses Kanyon&#039;s face at this, and Funktacular shakes his head ruefully. &#039;Out in the middle of the woods, mumbling to himself about you and the crumble of civilization. Seemed pretty irked about the whole thing... Nice guy.&#039; &#039;Hopefully the big ape gets stuck between a couple of trees,&#039; Kanyon mutters to himself, then snaps his glare back to Funktacular. &#039;If you went all the way around the island,&#039; he begins, scrunching up his face as he thinks his way through, &#039;how the hell did you miss the other two?&#039; Funktacular just offers a shrug, then TING~!, the light flies from his mouth like Cowabunga Connection from a straight bar. In a jealous rage, the sun retreats behind some clouds, unable to face the bearer of such a powerful beam for at least a little while. Kanyon rubs his eyes in a daze, silently praying his fate isn&#039;t the same as Hyde&#039;s. Blinking, he looks up at Funktacular and smiles when his beady little eyes come into focus. Then he remembers who each of them are, and the authoritative scowl returns. &#039;Well go find them,&#039; he snaps curtly, waving a hand as if sending back a bowl of soup in a restaurant. &#039;Yeah right,&#039; Spikey chuckles, moving past Kanyon towards Bellie~! &#039;Right now I&#039;m going to taste some of this sweet, sweet fruit,&#039; he moans, reaching down between Bellie~!&#039;s spread legs, where he pauses momentarily, then snatches up a pineapple. He chomps down on the pineapple with starved intensity, then his eyes go startlingly wide and he spits the yellow bits all over the ground. &#039;This tastes like shit!&#039; He shouts, frantically scratching at his tongue to remove the juice -- or at least kill his tastebuds. &#039;Worst pineapple ever,&#039; he pronounces solemnly, casting it aside and grabbing another. He takes a bite from it and the cycle repeats. &#039;Jesus Christ! Are they all this bad?&#039; &#039;They seem to suit the rest of us fine,&#039; Herr Kanyon grumbles, displeased with the accusation that his fruits have gone sour. &#039;Yeah,&#039; Spikey agrees, crossing his arms over his chest, &#039;but you guys never actually taste them.&#039; &#039;We skim!&#039; Kanyon shouts in some strange and indefinable defense, marching away from Funktacular and down off his rock. &#039;Come on... Let&#039;s go find out where your buddies went,&#039; he calls back over his shoulder in a crisp, no-nonsense voice. Funktacular steals one last, longing look at Bellie~!, then scampers off after Kanyon, weaving effortlessly through the endless stream of drones delivering their pineapples to the pagan pile. The two march across the sand sharing an uncomfortable silence, each knowing something is terribly wrong but without any idea how to right it... Or any will to do so. &#039;So...&#039; Spikey offers absently. &#039;How&#039;s PCW treatin&#039; ya?&#039; He asks with marked disinterest, simply trying to pass the time. Kanyon isn&#039;t fooled, and he responds flippantly with, &#039;I own it.&#039; There is another silence, then it&#039;s Herr Kanyon&#039;s turn. &#039;Hey... Something I&#039;ve been wondering,&#039; unlike Funktacular, there is sincerity in his voice. &#039;How the hell are your teeth so white? Aren&#039;t you dating Aimz?&#039; Xamine asks, clearly stumped. &#039;You&#039;d be amazed,&#039; Funktacular laughs, &#039;but the dirtier the things I put against my teeth are, the cleaner they seem to get.&#039; He pauses for a second, considering. &#039;Come to think of it, your teeth should be just about as shiny as mine,&#039; he jests, but Kanyon no-sells it. They enter the orchard and manoeuvre their way between all the foragers, looking around for Cruel and Tyger. Instead, they find Timid Bologna, passed out in the centre of a pile of half-eaten pineapples that dwarfs even Kanyon&#039;s impressive stash. A low moan escapes his mouth, and a unceasing gurgling sound hums up from his distended stomach. The FSW Champion looks more like Pete~! than his ordinary self, and Kanyon and Funktacular quickly move on, trying to purge the image from their memory. &#039;There seems to be a lot less people today than there were yesterday,&#039; Kanyon states in a bewildered tone, scanning his thinning herd. Indeed, between here and the rock the pair has only passed a dozen men. &#039;What the hell is going on around here?&#039; On cue, the sand beneath their feet crumbles inward and collapses, forcing them to pull up short with a pair of muffled curses. The hole that has opened up before them is roughly four feet in diameter, and perched right down in the middle of it is a muscular blonde-haired man in an orange prison jumpsuit. He darts a suspicious glance at the two men staring down at him, then leaps from the hole and hugs them each in turn, shouting about freedom. &#039;Blow?&#039; Herr Kanyon demands incredulously. &#039;How the hell did you get here?&#039; &#039;Dug,&#039; Snow says shortly, nodding a confirmation of the statement, then he repeats himself. &#039;Dug my way out.&#039; He shoots a wondering look all around, admiring the lushness of the earth and its creations. &#039;Uh... But we&#039;re on an island, Blow,&#039; Funktacular stammers, scratching his head. &#039;How do you dig through the ocean? Surely you didn&#039;t go underneath! It&#039;s molten!&#039; &#039;A little perseverance goes a long way, boys,&#039; Blow informs them, pursing his lips and furrowing his brow to underline the importance of the statement. &#039;You&#039;ll do well to remember that.&#039; &#039;Why the hell would you escape now, anyways?&#039; Kanyon asks, awe-struck. &#039;You&#039;re up for parole!&#039; &#039;Screw &#039;em,&#039; Blow responds cheerily, reaching down into his pocket to retrieve something he cradles in the palm of his hand. With great concentration he fiddles with the object, then spreads a small amount of white powder on the skin between his thumb and index finger. In a wink it&#039;s up his nose, and he&#039;s holding it and sniffling for all he&#039;s worth, joyful tears streaming from his eyes. Kanyon and Funktacular stare on, struck dumb. &#039;Funny, the habits you pick up in there,&#039; Blow chuckles with a shake of his head, once he is able to talk again. He returns the object to his pants and jerks his head around in all directions, in short spastic movements. His eyes are wild and savage, surveying everything as if it could be hiding his mortal enemy. &#039;Right,&#039; Herr Kanyon says nervously, taking a wide step around the con. &#039;Well, uh, good to have you back, Blow... Everyone else is over there,&#039; Kanyon says, pointing over his shoulder. &#039;Do me a favour and see if you can cheer up The Virgin Byron Calver.&#039; &#039;Yeah,&#039; Blow says, more to himself than Kanyon. &#039;Blow can cheer anyone up,&#039; he mumbles cryptically, then wanders off towards the beach. Herr Kanyon and Funktacular look sideways at eachother, then continue their trek. After a few minutes they find themselves in constrictingly thick brush, but before very long that opens into a vast clearing and the two stop, staring up at the monolith in its centre as if at hell itself. The building juts out of the ground and looms ominously over the whole of the island, casting a shadow even blacker than Dudza, but only half as likely to steal your purse. Of wrought iron, stone, and blue marble, it is a wonder of modern construction and a testament to man&#039;s dominance -- and fear -- of nature. The air is deadly still, with no insects or animals anywhere near. The only sound is a pulsing buzzing noise, like a bug zapper getting consistent victims, and it coincides with the flashing neon sign draped above the front doorway. The two men stare up at this ludicrous abomination, jaws on the floor as they read the words flashing in bright neon purple: &lt;i&gt;The Punchy Pineapple Casino&lt;/i&gt;! &#039;Oh... my... Hubbard!&#039; Kanyon whispers, unable to tear his gaze away from the bright symbol of crime and carnal pleasures. They stand there for another moment, willing the blood back into their legs, then slowly approach the front door, expecting everything to disappear in a blink, like some glorious mirage. It doesn&#039;t, obviously, and when Funktacular reaches for the door handle and gives it a pull, the heavy steel door opens easily and soundlessly. They are immediately subjected to fluorescent and neon lights, buzzers and sirens, a strange voice singing in another room, and a thick cloud of smoke. Half of the missing wrestlers are here, sipping on strange yellow beverages, eating slices of pineapple, tossing betting chips around, and generally just having an irresponsibly grand time. The fun and games stop when the players lay eyes on the newcomers, however, with some of them even diving beneath tables. Alarmed by this behaviour, Tyger Smells&#039;Em and Cruel Devil search the room with their eyes, and, finding the problem, begin sauntering over to Kanyon, their faces prim and smug. Funktacular wanders away from Kanyon, undoubtedly on a quest to find some showgirls, leaving him alone just as the other two arrive. &#039;May we help you, oh grand imperial leader?&#039; Devil smirks, speaking facetiously and eyeing Kanyon with a great deal of contempt. Tyger nods and grins at his side, enjoying the look of stark shock on Herr Kanyon&#039;s face. &#039;How...? What...?&#039; Kanyon stutters, then collects himself and tries again. &#039;Where the fuck did you worthless peons get a casino?!&#039; The explosion should catch the mobsters off-guard, but they simply exchange a confused look and then regard Herr Kanyon with an appraising eye. &#039;What the hell are you talking about?&#039; Tyger asks, looking around as if staring at the trees and brush of the jungle. &#039;Yeah,&#039; Devil agrees. &#039;What casino?&#039; Kanyon glares from one to the other, then back again, wondering how to approach these obvious madmen. He seems stumped for a moment, then his eyes light up as something else altogether strikes him. &#039;You sons of bitches!&#039; He shouts maniacally, staring around at the pineapples literally spilling off the tables to fall on the floor. &#039;You&#039;re making a mint here! And it&#039;s all tax free! ...or it was until now!&#039; He ends this last with a greedily sinister cackle, throwing his head back to give it more force. &#039;Are you insinuating we&#039;re not on the level, Herr Kanyon?&#039; Tyger demands with a snort, then fails miserably at trying to keep a straight face. &#039;We&#039;re not making anything here, except maybe a grave for you if you don&#039;t piss off,&#039; Devil snarls, closing the gap between he and Herr Kanyon with one menacing step. The latter simply smirks and rolls his eyes, trying not to drool as he thinks of all the potential taxes. &#039;We came here to get away from you and your pathetic reign, pal,&#039; Devil rolls on, speaking quietly through gritted teeth. &#039;So don&#039;t even think about trying to extend it... We won&#039;t run again. &#039;We&#039;re sick of running from you, Kanyon,&#039; Tyger chimes in, still looking much more amused than pissed off. &#039;So if you insist on staying, shut your mouth and pay the cover fee. Otherwise, drag your pretending-CEO ass out of our office.&#039; &#039;Before we throw you out,&#039; Devil finishes, meeting Xamine&#039;s eyes square and flashing a threatening grin. &#039;What about them?&#039; Kanyon demands, pointing to the various heads peeking up over tables to stare at the scene, which quickly disappear at his notice. &#039;They&#039;re coming with me.&#039; &#039;Like hell they are,&#039; Tyger growls, then remembers himself and replaces his smile. &#039;They&#039;re free to go, of course, but...&#039; He trails off, leaving the obvious unspoken. &#039;Your world is coming crumbling down, Kanyon,&#039; Devil chuckles. &#039;And it&#039;ll be Ty and I that pick up the pieces to make something grander than you ever dreamed. The people are here with us because they know what&#039;s good for them. We don&#039;t care what they do, and we don&#039;t try to impose our will unnecessarily. Pretty soon the rest will smarten up and follow,&#039; he pauses for a moment, a devious sheen overtaking his eyes. &#039;Even you,&#039; he whispers. &#039;I&#039;ll never be a part of this peon orchestra,&#039; Kanyon laughs nervously, backing towards the door. &#039;Nor will any of the PCW faithful,&#039; he adds, ignoring the familiar faces peeping at him from their places of false concealment. &#039;Order will prevail!&#039; He pauses for one last glare, then turns and bolts from the acrid room. And so the intangible struggle is underway, with order having taken a heavy blow at the hands of anarchy today. Deep within the island&#039;s core there is laughter, the kind that only comes from purest amusement at the expense of another. The two sides will battle fervently in a fight neither can win, each taking considerable losses -- and perhaps total annihilation -- along the way. Fate has decreed that only one may stand as Lord of the Allies, but, true to form, she&#039;s kept the identity of that champion secret. His only opponent now is the ultimate weapon of both creation and destruction, the universal equalizer... Time.
