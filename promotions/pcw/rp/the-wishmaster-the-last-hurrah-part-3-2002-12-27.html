The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 3

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/02 {From the hands of The End} Thank you, Chet King. Your predictable nature through your ego, the way you constantly need to justify yourself rather than just let your work stand for itself... so you validate me. You wouldn't talk to a nobody. But you have to defend yourself everytime a 10-year-old kid says 'You suck!' instead of just laughing and moving on. Oh, how small you've demonstrated yourself to be. I personally don't care much for what you've done, but at the same time I don't really disrespect you. Would you like to know why the attack? Because, remember that match I showed you last promo? The first one we were in together? You were silent. You disregarded me. And look what happened, I won. Your heart wasn't in the match. You weren't concerned about proving yourself or upstaging me, or Clyde, or Rush Mason. You thought that because you were Chet King that you could just waltz in 3 hours late for role call, learn who your opponents were last-minute, and still win. I didn't want a repeat of last time. I want to give you ample warning that you are indeed in this match. And I wanted you to have something personally vested in this match, ie proving me wrong in the ring. You see, Chet, this will be my last match whether you like it or not. And I don't want my opponents half-assing it in the ring. That doesn't make me look good in winning, you know? Maybe you don't, who knows. I really don't want to here it. Oh, and of course it's not the highlight of my career, I've beaten better people than you. But now moving on to more important people, Tim Walden. That's right, Tim Walden is more important than Chet King. At least to me. Tim, you're coming off the match... Trick or Treat IV. I'd congratulate you for defeating The HIT, but... you know. Not exactly a hard thing to do. He was fortunate enough to get to the finals of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament anyway. Shame many of those matches had you and/or other Front Office members getting involved to the point that a chaos of suck erupted at ringside, and at some point a winner would be arbitrarily decided. Hell, he gave you the win in the Finals by lifting you up to the title himself, he's already proved that math aside, he's got little common sense. So you'll forgive me if I don't cheer you. Once again, you're involved in the Continental Title division by some luck. What an insult it is to us by Kannon that we couldn't be given decent competition. Well, maybe you've improved since giving me my debut win in PCW. You did dominate Cage 1 at this year's Alliances. Even pinned your own teammate, quite an accomplishment. I'll reserve final judgment until I face you in the ring. Concentrate on HIT if you want... but trust me, it's not doing anything for either of your careers. My advice to you would be: keep trying. You'll get to the top if you don't revert to the jobber Walden we all still know is hiding inside of you. Just don't SNAP. You tend to be more a serious idiot than a bumbling idiot then. Next is Dante Jackson. You were a 2-time PCW Tag Team Champion for 9 seconds. Don't limit yourself. You've got undeniable talent. Can be a big player in PCW. Advice, get rid of Quo. You tend to be viewed as a moron by associating with them. In fact, just forget the Playaz, Inc. idea. Work for yourself. Everyone knows you're carrying the tag team, the entire stable. Why put that on yourself? Why have to worry about three other men's careers. Yeah, they're black, but if they can't help themselves then they don't deserve your help. Quo, he's a big guy, if he can't take care of himself then he's in the wrong business. Duzza's only PCW success came as he teamed with a white supremacist. Can't be happy with him about that, but look at him now. He's useless. He lost to Jamie Celt! Khadafi... dang, a cold shiver just ran down my spine. Um... HIT. My former mentor. You really like to refer back to those 2 weeks before I began outclassing even you in wrestling skill. I wanted to get to PCW to face you at some point, after a short detour in EW (which was smart because by going there to that place, I looked even better) I got to PCW in January of this year. But you weren't there. Lucky me, though, we ran into each other in PURE. And I won. You couldn't beat me. Not without help. By yourself, I'm undefeated against you. But you managed to put up a fight each time. Hopefully the loss to... Tim Walden, sorry for the pause I was laughing there... won't get your spirits down too much. Actually, I'm counting on you to be extra fired up following Decimation. Give me a good final match. Though rumor has it that you're going to be taking a hiatus yourself. Possibly an extended one. Then we'll both have to work hard to make this a good send-away match. My advice to you would be to forget about Walden and aim higher. You're better than him. You fail by trying to overwhelmingly prove the obvious. Stop trying so hard. Finally... Rush Mason... Thanks to Chef you managed to win the Continental Title before me. Don't count on winning it again. At least not in the immediate future. I'll win the #1 Contendership at Adrenaline and designate my replacement. And though I don't know who it is just yet, I'll guarantee you this: it won't be you. My advice to you, Rush, is to go back to the 'Greatest Man Alive' gimmick. Sure, you sucked then, but you were infinitely more entertaining. In the Amos & Andy sort of way. Who knows, maybe Xavier Kannon will decide to carry to you success again. Lord knows your achievements are based on what others give you, rather than what you win yourself. NTICW Tag Team Titles -- Carried by Kannon. PCW Extreme Title -- As you were about to lose to Kramer Wild Wing, Jordan X Shabazz and Thug roC distracted him for you to keep you from jobbing. PCW Continental Title -- Handed to you by Chef for a swerve. You see, Rush Mason, you've accompished little on your own. And even the Malice Crew couldn't keep you from being mediocre. Hell, you were Jason Core's bellboy at the time. KARL LEBLANC held the PCW Title before you could win it! While it made you as champion a slight breath of fresh air, it also devalued the title to the point that your winning it was pointless. Jason Core was the last decent Extreme Champ. Yeah, I'm counting myself in that, but I never wanted it in the first place. I tried to lose to Smoke, I tried to lose to the others. Amazing, I couldn't distance myself from it until I rigged a ceiling to fall on me. And you wanted it and fought tooth and nail for it. Pathetic! My advice to you, Rush Mason, is simply this: Be your own man... For once... I've more to say, but no longer to my opponents for Adrenaline. Respond if you must, ignore if you wish. This isn't for a response, this is catharsis. Frankly, you could talk back and I'll walk away because I've said what I had to say and am not going to humor you in conversation. We'll meet in the ring soon enough... *End*
