Welcome Back Manuel!!!

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 The camera fades in… The Chef is shown in his dressing room getting ready for his match against Big Daveylicious on Turmoil. He brushes his dark black fluffy Islamic Militant beard and gives the mirror an angry glance, trying to see how frightening his appearance really is. The Chef: Oh Allah, why have you forsaken me with dominating the PCW MidCard once again!?!? I’m crying out to you!!! The Chef gets on his knees and prostrates towards Mecca. The Chef: SHALALALALALALALA!!! There is no god but Allah, and Allah is his name! SHALALALALALALALA!!! The Chef stands back up, and continues to address his divine imaginary friend. The Chef: Well Allah, I’ve prayed, prostrated, and Allah knows how many times I’ve flagellated myself with the Koran! But still, no title shot, even after I sauté the @$$es of Boda and Chet King! What’s an Islamic Militant to do when you don’t admonish his prayers!?!? The Chef looks up at the ceiling, hoping for some divine inspiration, but nothing comes. The Chef: Well that settles it, tonight; I’m fighting OLD SCHOOL CHEF STYLE!!! The Chef rips off his Islamic Militant clothes as the camera blurs the spots necessary. He reaches into his gym bag and pulls out…A SMALL ASIAN BOY!!! Manuel Hung: I dis many years old!!! He holds up two fingers. The Chef: Manuel!?!? How did you get in there!?!? More importantly, how did you survive in there???? The kid reaches into his pocket and holds up a couple Power Bars and some Mott’s Apple Juice. The Chef: My goodness! You’ve been eating health food!?!? No son of mine eats health food… Manuel Hung: I can see your nipples Cheffy… The Chef blushes and covers up his hairy nippage. The Chef: Well, if you would jump out of my gym bag, I would be able to retrieve my plain old white Chef suit! The kid hops out of the gym bag, and offers the Chef his old white hat. The Chef: Man, this takes me back, Allah knows how long… Manuel, obviously bored after living in the gym bag for probably weeks, decides to kick the Chef in the leg. The Chef: OWWW! Bad Manuel Hung! The little boy begins to cry and crawls under the gym bag, quivering in fear. The Chef: Oh Manuel, I know you have ADHD, I’m sorry for screeching at you. The tyke pops his head back out of the gym bag. Manuel Hung: Weawy? The Chef: Yes, weawy…I mean really! Manuel leaves his hiding spot and gives the Chef a hug. The Chef: AWWW, now don’t we fell much…OWWW!!! Manuel, in another ADHD outburst, kicks the Chef right in the stomach, causing the Culinary Conquistador a great deal of pain. The Chef: Ah damn, there goes my ulcer… The Chef flops over onto the floor and Manuel dances around him. Manuel: I got Cheffy! I got Cheffy! I got Cheffy! The Chef: Call a nurse will ya? Hey…why are you calling me Cheffy? I’m your Daddy! Manuel: No, Yakov is Daddy now… The Chef: But, wasn’t I the one that rescued you from the Guatemalan orphanage!?!? Manuel: No Cheffy, member Bertha found me in the gym bag? The Chef: Geez, I can’t even remember my own storylines, ok you make your point, just help Cheffy back up.Manuel notices that the Chef has gained over 40 pounds since his “Hippie” days. Manuel: You are weawy fat Cheffy! The Chef: Yeah, well the Bertha clone likes the fast food restaurant, and I unfortunately eat there with her, but I’m on Slim Fast! Manuel shakes his head. The Chef: Well actually it’s some Sam’s Club brand weight reducing milkshake…Now can you help Daddy put his gear on? “WHAT THE F*CK IS GOING ON HERE MOTHER F*CKERS!?!? The Chef: Hey Bertha, just hanging with my kid, that’s all… Bertha: Where the f*ck has he been!?!? The Chef: In the gym bag, of course… Bertha gasps. Bertha: I had that f*cking stinky thing washed like two queer as f*ck times the past few mother f*cking @$$ humping b*tch @$$ f*ck @$$ shitty as f*ck days, b*tch ho! Manuel Hung: I rided in da bag for lots of times! The Chef slaps himself in the forehead. The Chef: But of course! That also explains the screaming we heard when we had the bag re-stitched by the tailor! The Chef: (Putting his Chef suit on) Manuel, why did to jump into the bag? Manuel: I wanted to see ya Daddy… A tear drops from the Chef’s eye. It’s one of those precious family moments folks, give the Culinary Conquistador a break… The Chef: Oh Manuel! I’m happy to see you too! Welcome back to the family!!! The Chef bends down to pick up the little tyke and give him some fatherly love, and in the process his shirt rips! SHHRRRRRRRRRIIIIIIPPPPPPPPPP!!! The Chef: Ah nuts!!! This is going to cost Osama Bin Laden a fortune to repair! Bertha: Looks like your f*cking too heavy f*ckface! The Chef: Yeah, looks who’s talking… Bertha lets out a thunderous “HMMMPPPHH!!!” as a sign of disrespect for the Chef’s recent comments. The Chef then realizes the secret behind the ripping of the garment. He jumps down to the ground and begins to prostrate. The Chef: Oh Allah! Thank you for the wonderful sign! I know now never to return to my PCW OLD SCHOOL roots! Bertha, fetch me my back up Islamic Militant gear post haste! Manuel: Bertha, what’s Daddy doing on the floor? Bertha: It’s f*cking religious adultery kid, he’s a queer as f*ck moron… Bertha and Manuel sigh as the Chef praises Allah. Manuel then turns to the camera and shouts… Manuel: ADIOS ENEMIGOS!!! The camera mercifully fades to black.
