Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {8}

Date: December/March “Begin the procedure. There is no time to lose.” Yep. The god was down. Looks like his daughter has done it again. Gotta love family. The Neo Void God was face up on the cold stone ground, his eyes closed shut and twitching rapidly as three people stood over him. One was Cyclonica, the daughter and Divine Prophetess of the god. Her arms were folded across mesh halter pressing into her chest, her eyes glaring down at her father with the same spite and malice that he would show towards Kannon. Father and daughter shared similar traits at certain points…you could see features within each other when they were angry or…whatever other emotions they were capable of past that. The other was Matrix; the computer whiz dressed in all black. He sat on the floor with a laptop in hand, clicking away at the keys like he was an active e-fedder pushing out a few last minute roleplays and segs before the PPV deadline. It was amazing how one’s work ethic could pick up in a second when there was something on the line…and Matrix was one that was known for his unmotivated work ethic. A sandbag worker if he ever was one. He was sitting on the said sandbag at the moment. The other being present in the room at the time was Sonata Kojima; long time friend of Akira Akiyama. She stood away from the god’s body, looking down at him with wide concerned eyes. Concern was a funny thing…no one’s really concerned about the person’s well being before they commit the action that inflicts harm, but afterwards they begin to care. Is it result of not thinking things through? You could say that. Not like Kannon completely thought things through with going against Akiyama. Unless he actually didn’t mind his destruction as an end result. Whatever kinky games he and Ellie like to play, Nurse must be one he enjoys the most “Is he okay?” she whispered, the concern laced through her voice like Clyde’s body laced with scars. “Y…you didn’t hurt him did you?” Matrix shrugged his shoulders as he continued on. “Dunno. I heard off the net that this here sandbag was the official slayer of Neo Void Gods. Gotta give props to my boy Death Chicken on AIM.” “Stop your whimpering.” The order barked from Cyclonica and echoed off the walls. Sonata stepped back a bit, but moved in closer, never taking her eyes off of Akiyama. Cyclonica looked down at Matrix with a stern look. “Progress report.” His brow wrinkled, his eyes glazed as the screen glowed into his face. “Things are…going…according to plan. His neural network is woven complicatedly, but I think I can maneuver through this. If I can beat Giga Extreme Villain on Hyper Fantasy Realm 13, then I can do this.” “Spare me your pathetic digital achievements and get the job done. If you cannot disconnect his neural receiver from his brain and link it to the transmitter before I lose psychic hold, we’re all screwed.” Sonata didn’t like the sound of that…kinda like no one liked to hear the god’s music play when they were in the ring. It was like the sound of doom roaring in the distance, calling them by name. Of course doom would be waiting for them all…they’d just have to step up to it one by one. “Screwed? What do you mean?” Cyclonica rolled her eyes. And she was supposed to be good for something. “Look, you’ve done your job, now stop bothering me. You’re breaking my concentration, and the less concentration I have the quicker daddy dearest here wakes up and rips all of our spines out…then beats us to death with them. That’s what I mean by screwed.” Seems like Ms. Kojima was having second thoughts about the whole deal. She leaned against the stonewall in the corner, her hands to her temples as she thought this deal over: Here’s Sonata Kojima…average Japanese girl, grew up with a black belt in Karate, went to college and became a designer. Alternate life, you say? Ex-anarchist with the group known as the Turbo Brigade. Ex being the key word. You have a tendency to quit after you’re caught by the Japanese government special forces. But she was pardoned only if she’d help the Special Forces with a small favor… Well…Akiyama was by no means a small favor. -=-=-=-=-=-=- ‘Infiltrate the home of Akira Akiyama; gain his trust, and that is all you have to do. The others will do the work from there.’ The brunette in the white skirt paced back and forth, reading over the file in her hand. Sonata sat on the stool in the white sterile office looking up at the woman. ‘That’s it?’ The woman in white nodded. ‘It. And we let you go free. As long as you keep to your part.’ Sonata nodded slowly. ‘Sounds good. Who are the others?’ The woman pressed a button on the computer, and on the sidewall three images came up; one of a light blue haired woman, another with black and light blue hair, and a man with black hair. ‘Their names are Glacia, Cyclonica, and Matrix.’ -=-=-=--=- “The neural pathways are about 47 .8% clear.” Matrix reported, his finger rolling on the track ball quickly and clicking. “Once they’re clear the upload to the transmitter can begin.” “Good.” Cyclonica’s hand went to her head, her eyes closing tightly in frustration. “His mind’s tighter than Ellie’s ass. It’s going to use my entire resources to keep him controlled for the moment.” Another chuckle came out from the Matrix. “I would think Ellie’s ass is kinda loose…they way I’ve seen her walking on TV that is. Plus I have my sources…thanks again Death Chicken.” “Keep quiet! I don’t—“ “Now there’s a queen of extreme!” Matrix looked up at the dark ceiling, a perverted smile on her face. “I’d love to get me a handful of her Hubbards!” “MATRIX!!!” The arm of the god snapped straight up in the air. Everyone jumped as it remained there, rigid and frozen. Cyclonica growled as her brow wrinkled harder. Slowly, his arm began to lower back down to the ground. She exhaled deeply before snapping her glare to Matrix. “When this is all over…I will personally rip out your crotch and leave you a Xenu… without the aid of a sex change.” Matrix looked at her with a dumb look. “Um…what’s that mean?” This was not the time to ask questions. The vein on her forehead was already throbbing. “Xenu is a pussy. Xenu …is a pussy. Got it?” “…I’m sorry, but that has no sexual innuendo whatsoever. I mean when I said Hubbards, people would know I was talking about Ellie ti—“ “RAAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!” That shut him up real quick. Cyclonica turned away from him, sitting against the stone pillar and breathing slowly. Matrix pouted as he sat, clicking on the computer. “Geez…point out a bad joke and she gets all demon goddess on you. Must be the genes.” Sonata didn’t find herself a part of this unusual debate. She was in her own world thinking about what she’s done. This was a man she grew up with. She went to elementary school, high school, college, and graduate school with Akiyama. They trained in Karate together, they slept over at each other’s houses, made sandwiches and camped out together. They were best friends to the end… But then things changed. Akiyama went off, became what he is today, and she became a designer by day, revolutionist by night. How she ever got mixed up in that crowd she’ll never remember. But fate is a funny thing…all in all it leads you right back to where you were. Right back to the people you care for. Her mind was reflecting on everything… -=-=--=-=- A black haired little girl in a yellow jumper and a black shirt sat on the playground drawing a picture on the ground in chalk. Her hair was in pigtails, and she had faint freckles on her face. Then a shadow was cast over her pretty picture of stick figures in dresses…beautiful women in elegant gowns in her mind. “Hey, you’re that Kojima kid aren’t you?” Couple boys stood over her, glaring down at her with their arms folded. It was despondent how young boys were already angry with the world at young ages. “Hai,” she responded. “What do you want?” One of the kids took lead. “I heard your dad’s a karate teacher. Why don’t you give me a lesson?” Kids were always ready to pick a fight…well some of them. Others were ready to end it. “School’s out, buddy.” Their eyes went from the girl on her knees drawing to the tall kid behind her. He was nearly twice their size, wearing blue jeans and a blue tank top. His black hair was tied back in a ponytail, and he stared at them hard. “If you still want lessons though…my school’s always open.” The kids didn’t seem so eager for tutelage now. Cowardice…some people grew out of it, others never at all. Names float to mind…but that’s another time. The girl turned around, a smile on her face. “Hey, I know you, don’t I?” The boy shrugged. “Maybe. I get around.” The girl stood up and bowed politely. “I’m Sonata Kojima, but you can call me Sonata. And you?” The boy looked at her for a second before shrugging, going along with the flow. “My folks call me Akiyama.” …that was the first encounter between Akiyama and Sonata. But the last? Sonata Kojima, now grown up, sat on the edge of the wooden bridge in the park, staring down at the river below. She held a flower in her hand, and was slowly picking at the petals. She brushed a few off of her black skirt and looked up to the sunny sky, a smile on her face. “You really like him, don’t you?” She turned around, finding her sandy haired friend leaning against the railing. She was dressed in a white blouse and blue jeans, a pencil in her hair and a distant look on her face. “It’s not news to you, Yuri.” Sonata said with a smile. “You’re the big reporter that knows everything.” Yuri shook her head with a grin. “Nah, I have a long way to go. I don’t even know what field of reporting I want to go into. National news, entertainment news, there’s so many to choose from.” “Could always go with sports ya know. Wrestling always needs good writers.” Yuri looked at her with a realizing stare. Then she smiled, taking the pen from her hair and jotting something down on the scrap of paper she had. “That’s a great idea there. I can see my name now in the headline: YURI “QUEST” TAKAYAMA, PURORESU #1 NEWS REPORTER AND COLUMNIST!” Sonata giggled at her friend’s prediction. “I just can’t wait to get back to Japan. France is nice and all, but nothing beats home.” “Yeah…” Yuri nodded slowly, watching Sonata pluck the petals from the flower. “By the way, did you hear about your secret love, Akiyama?” That got her attention right away. “What about him? And don’t call him that. You make it sound like I’m obsessed with him.” “You might as well be. You two are always together, you grew up together, you two might as well get married and have kids!” As much as she tried not to, the mere thought of that made her blush. “Nah, I don’t think there’d be too many people happy with that.” Yuri waved her away. “Willow? That’s just a crush. It’ll pass. But you…you’ve got a chance there. I’d say you go for it…if things were different.” “What do you mean if things were different?” “Oh…didn’t you hear? Akiyama left this morning for Japan. Went to look for a job over in Tokyo.” No one ever heard their heart break…it was a physical thing you felt. Like someone just buried your soul under tons of despair and sorrow. Yuri sighed as she leaned off, patting her on the shoulder. “Don’t worry. I know you’ll be able to find him in Tokyo. And when you do…the next time you see him you can tell him how you feel about him.” Sonata sniffed, tears shoved back into her eyes as she smiled at Yuri. “Thanks.” Yuri nodded and walked away, leaving Sonata sitting there on the bridge looking down at the river passing her by. Chance passed her by, coincidentally. Now she may never have another chance to express herself…how much she really cared for him. She raised the flower to eye level, looking at the final petal left. Plucking it off, she pressed it to her lips in a soft kiss before dropping it to the waters below. “…he loves me not…” -=--=-=-- Opportunities come once in a lifetime. She knew it…Cyclonica knew it…Matrix knew it…and Akiyama knew it personally. He knew once he came back to PCW that he’d get a big shot at the big prize…and he’d have to destroy nearly the entire federation just to get what he wants. And he’d have no remorse in doing so… but Sonata…she had remorse in doing what she had to do. “Almost done. Just a few more minutes and we have ourselves a full blown god at our disposal.” Matrix looked up from the laptop with a perplexed look. Cyclonica opened her eyes, looking up at Sonata standing before her with fists balled up. “Let him go.”
