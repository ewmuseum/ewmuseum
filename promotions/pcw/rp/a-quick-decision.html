A Quick Decision...

Date: not entered **Note**The text in Italics is more of a serious narration from Rich Sherwin… in a sense, they are like his thoughts on the matter. It has been days… no, weeks… since we last saw those boyishly wonderful faces of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore. It had seemed like an eternity… since we last heard the fans scream… and shout out their verbal tirades of disgust… at two guys, who like everyone else… were only trying their damnest to make a living. One would wonder why the world hates these characters so? … why they would accuse them of being… “Homosexuals”… or whatever other terms people come up with to accuse one another of bending in a way… that is not seen as being the ‘norm’ of general society. Does this reminisce of ‘hate’ have anything to do with the story you’ll be glancing your eyes upon today? It could do… but then again, it probably won’t… the idealistic nature that follows with you people assuming something will read ‘serious’ because its introduction is ‘serious’ is just… too amusing to brush to the side… or under the carpet. However, there is something of a reason behind this… there really is a reason as to why the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ have decided to finally emerge from the depths of whence they came. This is their story… starting from the beginning? No… it would be too long… but for now, we begin here… we begin today… and so, the book unfolds… and the paths, have yet to be written… Looking back… reflecting on the past is something that I did too much of… it’s something that I probably did the most of in my early years. Doing something… and then looking back at whatever damage that it may have caused… so what if thinking wasn’t the strongest weapon in my arsenal… my old man had said that “Actions” spoke significantly louder than words… it was a phrase that stood in my mind for as long as I could remember… but sometimes, I look back and I think… ‘Was that the right thing to do?’ The scene opens up in the busy café… located somewhere within the United States of America. Not a lot of information given there… but for the time being, information isn’t going to be the real needy basis of your problems. As we finally come to a complete open of the scene, we find ourselves looking at a single table… Rich Sherwin sitting on the left, Ryan Moore sitting opposite him. They appear to be engaging in conversation… drinking coffee while trying to sort out various details of whatever step they would wish to take next in their attempts to finally get two hands on the PCW World Tag Team Championship belts. The start of the day… this was something that I happened to remember clearly… being inside that busy café at such a time. I didn’t think that any form of service was going to come our way… but thankfully, it was something we managed to get… and the wait wasn’t all that long either. The last few weeks had been filled with nothing but disappointment for both myself and Moore… we started off as being good picks to lift the Tag Team Gold at the ‘Wrestle Vessel’ Pay-Per-View… then we were pretty much all but ready to defeat the Cowabunga Connection to earn another shot at Gold… but, something seemed to have stopped itself the weekend before it all… and confidence seemed to simply vanish from the camp. To this day… we’ve not been able to figure out the problem… but it’s something that I’ll hope to be finishing within the near future. Sherwin and Moore continued to talk… their conversation unheard by anyone else… but as the cameras slowly began to drift in toward the duo, their voices were picked up. Starting off as being little whispers… progressing to becoming loud whispers… and then, converting into the average pitched voice. “So… do you really think this decision is going to prove best for both us and our careers?”, Ryan asked… leaving you all in confusion, but Sherwin in the right frame of mind to continue. “I’m sure that he’ll make a good addition to the band… after all, the more… is all the merrier… isn’t it?”, he stated, looking rather surprised that Ryan was asking questions this late into the whole story. For those of you that have not been able to pick up on the general census of things, the boys are thinking about getting somebody else to join their group… somebody that would not only give them a psychological boost… but something that will also ensure that boredom won’t be something that constantly lingers in their lives. This was the first decision I found myself making… with the Partial effort of thought behind it. Like I stated before… thinking and me, we weren’t the perfect couple to be walking around the world… but whenever I bothered to sit down and have a thought about a decision… about an idea, or… anything that really requires some brainpower… I was able to do something. This character wasn’t anybody that I knew personally… but he was somebody that I’d heard about though sources of the ‘Record Company’… whether or not he was any good… would have to be seen later down the line or something. “OK… if that’s really the way that you want to go about this… I guess I’m not in any real position to try and talk you out of things. You do know… that if this happens to fall through… and if we’re unable to really do anything from this newest recruit… our failures will lie in your hands… staying there until the end. Unlike… everything else before this… there won’t be any “Peace” to slide to…”, Moore stated, sounding kinda annoyed before removing himself from his seat at the table and walking towards the exit of the location. Sherwin looked over to his friend as he pulled open the Café door and left… walking past the window, before escaping into the depths of ‘Out of Field Vision’. I tried to think… there was nothing that could have really set Ryan storming off in such a way. Maybe he was worried… about the actual band, especially after we lost a member of ‘PCG’ in the most unfortunate way… only a mere three months ago. I didn’t think that Ryan Moore would allow himself to be open… I didn’t think that he’d allow himself to show any form of emotion when it came to this… but I guess all those years, I’d been wrong about it… and when the time came for me to do something worthwhile for him… I blew it… I did the one thing that I really should have revised before choosing. Sherwin looked down at the table, glancing lifelessly into the empty mug that used to contain his order of Coffee. He didn’t understand everything at the moment… especially with Ryan Moore choosing to storm out of the location and all. Sherwin remained still… vacant… unable to do anything except look in his lifeless mode. The pitter-patter of foot-steps can be heard… making their way over to the table that Sherwin was currently sat down at. The individual dropped a white leaflet down… touching the table with no sound… no noise… no nothing. “Hmm… what’s this all about?”, Sherwin said out aloud… looking down at the white leaflet spread across the table. He picked it up and held it out infront of him… so he could read the note in the easiest of manners. “… Hmm… so this is the place that our new ally has been attempting to try out over these past few days… all the way… in Canada… I guess I can make the trip now… especially since I just dropped the baggage.” Like Ryan before me… I removed myself from the table and began to walk off into the sunset if you will. I began to step off into the future that would eventually come to shape the very thing that’ll become my story… that’ll become my adventure. Canada has always been a place I heard close to heart… it had always had that special place inside of me… and now I was getting the opportunity to go back. It may not have been for my past friends… and it may not have been for my past family… I was going to see the new guy… I was… going to see it all unfold before my eyes.. Rich Sherwin was like none other in the history of his kind… making the kind of unforeseen journey that could make any general member of the public bow down in shame. A lot of people would not go out of their way to make a journey… just to see somebody, who for all they know… could turn out to be nothing more than a waste of space… nothing more than a pathetic piece of trash that’s looking for something to cling upon. We saw Sherwin walk away from the Café… looking rather pleased with himself, taking a seemingly long look into the future… a long look into the stars. The camera fades out… into the depths of darkness… this very short segment in the lives of PCG having little meaning to it… having little point to it… but revealing the very important fact, that somebody… will be joining the ranks of the band… soon.
