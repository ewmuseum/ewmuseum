Jesus smokes Dope with the next World Champion Duke Williams.

27 October 2002

Title: Jesus smokes Dope with the next World Champion Duke Williams. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/27/02 So many people hate me, so many thrive to make me fail, nobody wants to see me the world champion. Do I deserve the gold? I think I do, who’s better then me? Who’s as consistent as me? Who has as much power as I do? If you want to keep your job or get into the stadium at a reasonable price you need to answer me. Ask your self this; are you a Goth God President? Not El Diablo the Mexican fluke. He tries so hard and fights so valiantly yet he never comes home with anything more then a Cracker Jack prize. Do I respect him? Not truly. Do I like him? No. Has he been a pain in my ass on a consistent basis? Yes. For this he must pay. He must learn that I am the next world champion of PCW You can not stop me for I am your God and your president. Then there is my coworker, my subordinate, my Employee, Tim Walden. He will work with me in this match and help me win it. He will if he wants to keep his job. That’s not a threat my friends that is an actuality. My goal of winning the world title is both clear and present and anybody who does not help me in this goal becomes an obstacle. Obstacle will be eradicated as swift and definite as my power allows. Do you really want to challenge the power of your God and President? I think not Timmy. Then there is Jason Snow. From the purity and innocence of his youth thrown into the harsh reality that is life. Oh how the golden boy has greened. Was he just a giant rock of fool’s gold? I doubt it; I think he’s as pure as it gets. But even pure gold is no match for Platinum. The platinum in which your God and President Dukestar The Goblin is carved from. The best of the best and hoss there ain’t no better. Consumed by the social structure and ecosystem that is prison is his mind really in the right place? A world only understood by those with in it. Snows jargon and actions tell me he is a lost soul. Prison has changed him and the world title does not belong around a prisoner’s waist. But a President and a Goth God? What better combo. Not even McDonalds can offer a better combo then that. I’m the most experienced, most powerful, most intelligent, and most Goth. I have the most history, accomplishments, and am the only God. I set my goals on taking the Presidency away from Tyler BEEP and did so. Now I embark on a new goal in which I also will not fail on. That goal is the world title and this is my story. Dateline: Sunday October 27, 2002Place: The dark Evil goth Lair of Dukestar the GoblinMeeting: Conference between Good and Evil Purpose: Domestic relations with the God of Good -If you were in the lair you would first notice how damp and cold it is. Some where around 60degrees with a chilling breeze coming from somewhere you can’t figure out where. The lair is dark with black covering everything. The only light comes from a black light, which lines the ceiling. Sitting at a table constructed by the bones of murdered nuns and saints sits a man clothed in white rob. He is Jesus Christ. On the other end of the table is God dressed in all black with the world Malice in red on his shirt. He is Dukestar the Goblin. They chat getting to know each other over a warm glass of goats blood.- Jesus: So you beat out the Satan by a small margin in the last election. Dukestar: Yea he got caught sleeping with three interns a week before the election. Jesus: Darn. Dukestar: Yea that’s what almost gave him the edge plus he had Floridaville the ninth layer of hell wrapped up. Jesus: Lots of electoral votes in Floridaville. Dukestar: Damn right but some how I won. You know he’s just out dated with the whole red look. Its very 70’s. Goth is the new fashion and the new party I’m happy to represent my Goth constituents. Jesus: I’ve always leaned towards the Goth ideology over the satanic anyways. Dukestar: Even Jesus loves Dukestar!! -The two toast the goats blood as they laugh.- Jesus: You know when I still made appearances as a mortal I chose the profession of carpenter. I see you’re a wrestler. That must be tough. Dukestar: You don’t get much pussy as a carpenter. I wanted a job where I could stick my nail in the right hole hoss. -Jesus does not smile at Duke’s humor.- Dukestar: Oh I bet nails are a sore subject with you after the crucifixion and all. Anywho lets get down to brass tacks….err….ahhh….. Jesus: Its ok that was a long time ago. -Duke smiles.- Dukestar: You want a Klondike bar? What would Jesus do……for a Klondike bar!! Jesus: Huh? Dukestar: It just some mortal humor it’s a Tv commercial and a Jesus saying. -Duke’s evil dark manager and the seductively dressed supportive girlfriend brings out two Klondike bars.- Dukestar: I love eating the chocolate then the ice-cream. It’s a bit messy but you know I am a God. Jesus: It is a very tasty treat. Anyways before we get to business lets talk a little PCW. Some of the angels and myself watch every Tuesday and Friday. -Duke laughs an evil laugh.- Dukestar: Jesus loves PCW!! You watch OSW? Jesus: Darn Tooting!! Dukestar: You watch OSW? Jesus: You know I forgive but even Jesus can’t forgive for that crap. -The two men laugh.- Jesus: So you have this God power now and your that close to winning the world title. Dukestar: Woop woop Jesus: So I assume your going to win that number on contenders match? Dukestar: You want to smoke a bowl?? Jesus: I thought you’d never ask. -Duke pulls out his peace pipe.- Dukestar: Well first off you have this dolt El Diablo. Now Jez you know Satan and I know Satan and this cowboy is no Satan. Have you ever seen a devil wear make up like he does? Jesus: He ripped that off of the Ultimate Warrior who was also a devil. Dukestar: And look how long he lasted. -The two men laugh as they hit the pipe.- Dukestar: This guy thinks he’s Goth. Oh he can deny it all he wants but he does indeed what to be Goth. The name, the face paint, even the way he talks. He’s a Goth wannabe. And I mean who can blame him. Jesus: He is a Goth jock sniffer. Dukestar: You can’t be a champion in this day and age with out being Goth. And he isn’t even a smidgen of a Goth. In the giant cookie of Gothdom he isn’t even a crumb. He’s not even in the cookie shop. He’s in a different state. Jesus: Another world. Dukestar: Damn right he’s a So do you really think he’s going to be a President and a Goth God. Jesus: You can’t reiterate that point enough. Dukestar: Trust me I’m going to run that pony till it dies. You just can’t tell people enough times that a Goth God and President can’t lose. Jesus: Tell me about it. Dukestar: I just did. -The two men laugh it up as they hit the smoke.- Jesus: Dude look. -Jesus freaks out as he stares at his hand which in effect freaks Duke out.- Dukestar: What is it holy hoss? Jesus: My hand…it’s….it’s….it’s…more Goth then El Diablo!! -The two men crack up.- Dukestar: Jesus is more Goth and has a better chance of becoming champion then El D!! Jesus: What about that loser Tim Walden? Dukestar: Did you just call Tim Walden a loser? Jesus: Ahh no I said he’s a boozer. -Duke cracks up.- Dukestar: Jesus called Tim Walden a loser. -Jesus cracks up too.- Jesus: Isn’t he in the Front Office with you? Dukestar: That he is my water walking amigo…that he is. Jesus: So are you two going to tangle? Dukestar: If he headlocks me in a dream he better wakeup and apologize. And being a God you know I can view dreams. Jesus: Better then Must see TV. Dukestar: If Walden doesn’t help me I’ll fire his ass. I like Timmy a lot and I respect him but its my time. Not anybody else’s just my time. When its your time hoss its your time but kiddies its my time. Jesus: After all you are a Goth god. Dukestar: As much as a Goth God as you mother Mary was a virgin. Jesus: Evil dude that’s my mother your talking about. -Duke laughs an evil laugh as Jesus hits the pipe then laughs too.- Jesus: What about Snow. Dukestar: Yea I know the temperatures are dropping and snow is due at the end of the week. -Jesus laughs a high holy laugh.- Jesus: No my Goblin God friend I mean Jason Snow. -Duke gets what Jesus is saying and laughs.- Dukestar: Right…right…right….Jason Snow the prison bitch. Jesus: He will be redeemed for his sins when he goes. Dukestar: Well he may “go” this Tuesday when I kick his ass all over Turmiol. Jesus: I like Snow. Dukestar: That’s your problem Jesus you like the wrong things. Jesus: What? Dukestar: You’ll take Virginity over sex. Wine over whiskey. Your dull man. Jesus: That’s the path to salvation. Dukestar: Dude you’re already the good gods son. Your set no matter what happens. So live a little Jez!! Jesus: Umm sorry. Dukestar: Your dull like Jason Snow is. At least at one time in his life he was a sexual being with a sense of humor. Jesus: I took acid once. Dukestar: Get out. Jesus: Really! Dukestar: Damn dawg!! You rule. Just as I ‘m going to win on Fusion I think our relationship is going to work. Jesus: Good and Evil working together maybe we can finally take down MTV. Dukestar: Lets not get carried away. Jesus: If I can turn water into wine then this Tuesday you can win your match. Dukestar: What are you saying, that it would be a miracle if I won? Jesus: Your taking it the wrong way dude. Dukestar: Get out of here. Jesus: But…. Dukestar: GET OUT!! -Jesus hangs his head as he walks out the back door of the lair.- Dukestar: This Turmoil get ready to be sent Whiskey Bent and EVIL GOTH Hell bound!! That is the Evil Goth God Front Office Truth. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
