How To Destroy Maguai---err..Miyagawa - Part 1 - The Box

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 27 [Gremlins is a totally bodacious movie, dudes. Nothing beats the ever so cute Gizmo. IF your mom or dad brought home that cuddly little compadre, you'd be jumping for joy. Dogs and Cats would be a thing of the past, because your getting a totally gnarly Maguai for Christmas! Though, the movie taught us many gnarly lessons about how you have to take care of things, and have responsilbilities. Like not feeding it after midnight. Don't get it wet. And, of course, never let it get near sunlight. BRIGHT LIGHT!! That is a totally gnarly line] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy in the past six months hasn't been known as a singles wrestler. In fact, the Tubular One has been in arms with his half brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, as the most bodacious tag team in PCW, The Cowabunga Connection. In the ranks of PCW he hasn't been a singles wrestler at all. There have only been a few certain Extreme matches in which he stood in a singles fight, but it was still along side his most bodacious brother, Harry. But now, after being screwed over by the totally bogus P.A.W. at Third Dawning, the Tubular One sets out in his first 'official' singles match against a former PCW Heavyweight Champion, Miyagawa. Not only is he just a a great wrestler, he is the bogus leader of the totally heinous Gaz and Nezerra, who stopped the Connection from achieving their ultimate goal, Tag Team Champions. Are they that jealous of the Connection? Or is it that they fear the greatest tag team to ever step foot in PCW? Either way, these dudes will pay and Tommy sets out to make them pay by going to the source itself, Miyagawa] [Tommy stares up to the beautiful night sky and wonders to himself, 'Why?'. Why did these new PCW wrestlers come in and interfere into their match. Why did they make everything totally bogus and cost the Cowabunga Connection the tag team titles? It doesn't make any sense and it smells of another one of those totally heinous conspiracies. Sitting atop Hotel California, surrounded by the totally bogus generators and air conditioning systems, doesn't really help his atmosphere, but he feels much closer to his father when he's up this high. When the stars shine brightly and the moon beams almost as bright as the sun, it's a perfect night to have a most gnarly conversation with his father, Radical Randy] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dad, I'm just trying to be radical like you told me to be. Harry and I are trying the best that we can, but we face total bogusness everytime we have a championship match! It's starting to eat away at me like a totally heinous cancer, dude. [Tommy leans against the marble edge, which is a good 15 stories from the ground below. Tommy doesn't look down, there is no reason to look down. He only looks up, hoping to get some kind of answer. Be it a totally radical sign in the stars, or a flash of the moon. Something to acknowledge that his dad is listening to him out there] TTT: All the dudes and dudettes on the beach think that what I'm doing is pretty craptacular. That being one of the greatest surfers in the world I've proven to you that I'm radical, but they don't understand what's in my head. When you had that accident, the one that mom caused for you, things we only going BODACIOUS for you! You were on the rise and Harry's dad was taking you there, dude. You were about to achieve what you worked your entire life for, that you almost sacrificed your family for! You were radical, but the world didn't get to know that. But they still have a chance to know how rad you were, by seeing me and Harry perform in that ring night in and night out. [Tommy's eyes have a hint of tears forming. His bright blue eyes becoming magnified by the tears. He runs his hands through his blonde hair, letting out a sigh to pass the nerves] TTT: That night under the stars with the bodacious babe Infinity, I realized what it was I needed to do. I needed to make my father's name be known, through his only son. I'm going to make you proud, dude. Whether you know it or not. [Tommy looks to the sky, watching the stars once again. He doesn't see a sign of anything] TTT: Bogus... [Suddenly the hatch to the roof whips open and a long haired figure rises out of the building like some sort of evil creature. Tommy leans against the edge of the building, looking down now wondering if he should hang over the side to get away from this bogus thing. The figure gets closer and is dragging a box with it. Seems rather odd now, and Tommy is no longer thinking about leaping off the building, but wondering what in the name of Kelly Slater is this thing?!] Figure: DUDE, MAN!! [Tommy lets out a large sigh and rolls his eyes. He knows EXACTLY who it is....] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I've got some news for you, man! [The Heavy Metal One steps into the moonlight, escaping the shadows from the generators atop the hotel roof. His Twisted Sister T-shirt looks a bit soiled, his black leather pants look a bit out worn, and his black cowboy boots seem to be on their last mile. The box behind Harry stays in the shadows as Harry's stoned face is filled with absolute joy] Harry: You've got the match of a lifetime, dude! [Tommy seems confused. He doesn't know anything about an upcoming match. Just the Pay Per View, which is over, and his Third Round GTT match. What has this compadre been smoking?] TTT: Dude, I don't have any upcoming matches. [Harry shakes his head with a smile on his face] Harry: But you do, man. A big one, man. TTT: Who is it with, then? Harry: MAGUAI, MAN!! [Tommy looks unbelievably confused. He knows that a Maguai is that cute little dude from the Gremlins movies, which are totally bodacious in a far out way may he add. But why would he be wrestling a Maguai in an upcoming match? What EXACTLY has Harry been smoking??] TTT: Dude, I don't get you at all. I know that ever since the whole Third Dawning incident you've gone on a bogus binge of hitting your new Jerome Bongus, but this is totally heinous to interrupt me, dude. I asked to have some alone time up here... Harry: No, man. It's offcial! Your going against Maguai, MAN!!! MAGUAI!!! [Tommy just shakes his head and puts his hand in Harry's face to shush his erratic breathing up] TTT: I'm not going against that gnarly Gizmo dude from Gremlins! Harry: Not Gizmo, but it's one in disguise, man!! I know it. Look for yourself, dude. [Tommy takes the piece of paper in Harry's hand to see the official Turmoil lineup. He then realizes what Harry is talking about....] TTT: You mean MIYAGAWA!!! [Harry is now the one confused] Harry: Maguai, man. TTT: Miyagawa, compadre. Harry: Maguai, man!! TTT: IT SAYS MIYAGAWA, DUDE!!! [Harry pauses for a few moments, looking back and forth, then commences with his thought] Harry: Maguai, man.... [Tommy crumbles up the piece of paper in a temporary stint of anger. He then throws the piece of paper in Harry's face. It has absolutely no effect on the stoner] TTT: I am facing, Miyagawa, the former PCW Heavyweight champion, dude. I can't believe this, dude. I'm going to be wrestling one of the most bodacious dudes in PCW in a singles match, and it's the MAIN EVENT!! We've never main evented a show in PCW before, dude. To tell you the truth I'm feeling most nervous now. [Harry smiles devlishly to himself and slowly pulls the box closer to the moonlight] Harry: I've got something for you that will certainly do the trick to get you a win, man. [Harry smiles putting the box under the moonlight as Tommy's eyes widen......] Fade To Black
