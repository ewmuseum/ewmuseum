History: The Pharaoh

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Whatever today is... 5/23/03 Around the time that Skylar Green had left the Primetime Wrestling Academy, a new cadet had come into the institution. He was tanned, fit and trim for his size, and had amazing agility. He said he had traveled from Cairo, Egypt, and that he was over two thousand years old, even though he was obviously incapable of producing any papers which proved it. His name? Titu Xansiv. But no one was 'worthy' of speaking that name, for they were but commoners. He was an Egyptian God in human form. A Pharaoh. That was a lot to live up to, believing oneself to be a god on earth. There was something there that could perhaps make oneself believe so. He could take a good amount of punishment. Wasn't quite 230 pounds but he could heft up opponents 300+ depending on how he lifted them. The real selling-point, though, was his near inhuman agility. His flips, twists, spins; he flew without wings. Something as simple as a body splash he could turn into something truly special. That is really what got him out of the PWA when New True Internet Championship Wrestling opened up for that, the second time in its history. Out of everyone there who even seemed to be a little... 'off', it was The Pharaoh who was the only one that nobody could take seriously. That's saying something. There were a number of rather 'different' people working in NTICW by that time. There was Ken Kaze, a walking accident. He was pretty clumsy, and talked to a trash can; he wasn't supposed to be taken seriously, so he didn't have that working against him. There was Corg, a goth who, although not the largest man on the roster, was just so creepy and sick in his methods that people moved out of the way when he walked past. Hessian was another one of those Satanic-types that the wrestling world filled with during the 1998-2001 'dark gimmicks are awesome' trend. Everyone and their mother was a Satanic person. Hessian, however, carried himself in such a way that whether or not you believed what he did, you were scared of him. You didn't want any part of him. He could snap your neck in a second if he got his hands on you, and he wasn't as slow for his size as other wrestlers so you really didn't want him coming after you. He didn't require that you take him seriously. He was going to do what he was going to do, regardless. And then there was The Pharaoh. He was his own cheering section, though he had Carmoon for a bodyguard and Isiy at his side. The Pharaoh was just so high on himself. He was convinced that he was a god, and he was going to make everyone else see the same. He couldn't be taken seriously, the way he carried himself. He had no sense of humor, no willingness to realize his flaws. He considered himself better than human. The innate problem with this was that there were always people that he could not beat. The Pharaoh managed to stay near the top of the card, but couldn't take that next step, couldn't actually attain a championship, couldn't prove beyond a doubt that he was what he said he was. Then in December 2001, there was a blessing upon him, in the form of a kidnapping. A staged kidnapping. Blain Bishop, tired of running the NTICW product and wanting to get out of the wreslting business, staged a kidnapping of himself by Eternal Wrestling members. He was 'kidnapped' on live television, in a match where Drake Maxwell defeated Xavier Kannon for the NTICW World Title. Shortly thereafter, NTICW closed, as Bishop had intended. The move was done with PCW as a 'shadow partner': the federation would snatch up the open contracts of any wrestlers it was interested in bolstering its lower card with, while disposing of the unneeded talent. The Pharaoh was lucky enough to get into PCW. The federation has always been a haven of rather unrealistic people, and The Pharaoh was no exception. He fit right in. Walking in, he immediately joined with another 'god' of PCW: Akiyama, the Neo Void God. Together the two formed the tag team known as 'The Divinity,' and it was but a short time before they won the PCW Tag Team Titles. They set a record for the length of their title reign, but then again their title reign took place at a time when the whole federation was dealing with the March Madness qualifying bouts for the pay-per-view main event, and thus tag team competition was actually put aside. Their lengthy title reign was a booking fluke in which for a month and a half, Pharaoh and Akiyama had not had to make a single title defense. During this time they had come to acquire other alliances, as they were taken (or they took under themselves, depending on whom is asked) into the stable 'International Incident.' A rather ineffective stable, it was also comprised of Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore -- the tag team known as The Golden Game, Reeco Suave, Anton The Kid... overall a detestable group of riff-raff. Being a part of I2 put a strain on The Divinity, as they weren't ones to identify themselves as citizens of their countries, but the very physical embodiment of the spirit of their respective countries (Egypt and Japan). Even though The Pharaoh took up a United States residence in Anaheim, California, and even though Akiyama had been banished from Japan for losing to Big Alan Steele in an important match, they still felt as though it was their obligation to represent their countries. But as a part of International Incident? As part of a stable of an injured man, a pubescent teen not old enough to drive? Of an Iberian tag team that was fast becoming obsolete? They were not satisfied with their position, and they sought to break their ties, their very association to I2. By the end of March Madness, I2 was all but disbanded. The Pharaoh and Akiyama had gotten free of their I2 connections, but the strain that was put on them by their association to the less-than-memorable stable became a strain upon their own team relationship. Each 'man' in The Divinity considered himself to be a god. And what do gods do in every mythology they are ever present in but clash and conflict with one another? Superiority issues, representation, and the simple want for individual success drove a wedge in The Divinity, and the longest-reigning PCW Tag Team Champions lost their belts in a rather uninspired match against the team of Frisco and Legend, Inc., (a team which would also split shortly thereafter). The driving point for the split would be The Pharaoh's Television Title win, taking the belt for his own. Such a gesture was taken as a subordinating effort on the part of Akiyama, who was not only ranked in the Television Title Division, but was the #1 contender to the belt. By The Pharaoh claiming the belt as his property, and not the joint property of The Divinity (whomever of the two would hold it), he made a bold statement about how 'equal' he felt the two gods to be. Infuriated by this statement, Akiyama severed The Divinity. The Pharaoh became irate, and wanted to go on the attack. The nVg, in turn, started to gather together persons who would knowingly and willingly function as his subordinates. While The Pharaoh fought to surpass Akiyama, Jay Sylo and The End were coming under the Neo Void God's wings. Sylo had been granted Celestial power, making him even more powerful than he was. A man who was 6'8' and close to 280 pounds was thus made a 7'1' 350 mass of muscle who tore through the Rising Star Division with little difficulty. The End was of Celestial origins, and though his blood was tainted by a half-human origin, he refused to be granted any more power, as he felt himself strong enough as it was. The nVg liked that, he liked having someone with clear limitations; someone who didn't want to be a god, didn't want to be as strong or stronger than the Neo Void God himself. With the Celestials (Akiyama's new stable that proved more effective than the other stables he had attempted to start in the past; a testament to the ability of non-Japanese-based groups to work together) at his side, Akiyama would wage psychological warfare against The Pharaoh, attacking his wife, sending his bodyguard into the Void, never to return. The Pharaoh had lost his safety and security, and that of those closest to him. Given that, he really had nothing left that he could lose. Nothing save for two things: His wife, Isiy Xansiv; and something that meant as much, perhaps more to him at the time, than even his wife -- his godhood. As they would reveal, a god can become mortal if they submit themselves to it. Akiyama would thus challenge The Pharaoh to a 2/3 falls match at the pay-per-view Spring Fling. The Pharaoh went in with full intention to win every fall. Akiyama determined the stipulations for each fall, a separate prize on the line each time. In the first fall, the Television Title would be the spoils for the victor. The second fall would determine each 'man's' respective godhood. The loser would be stripped of their godhood, forced to be a commoner; a mortal. In the last fall, The Pharaoh's own wife, Isiy, would be the prize. If Akiyama won, he could do with her as he pleased. In the first fall, The Pharaoh fought hard, though physically outpowered by Akiyama. He managed to get a pin with his finisher, lifting up the nVg (who had over 100 pounds in weight advantage) and spiking him into the floor. The Television Title was safe in his possession. However, within the next minute, Akiyama stole the pin back. There was more fighting, a climactic battle between the two powers. When everything seemed lost and The Pharaoh was about to lose his wife, the referee, Jay Sylo, turned on Akiyama to help Pharaoh get the win. Partly for escaping from Akiyama's control, and partly for the NTICW ties that he and The Pharaoh had, Jay Sylo screwed Akiyama out of the final fall. The damage had been done, however. If nothing else, the core belief of The Pharaoh that had driven him for all the time that he had been known to the PTC and PCW was gone. What had he to live for if he wasn't a god? He needed a purpose now. The Television Title could be that purpose, but titles are fleeting. Indeed, it wasn't very long until The Pharaoh lost the Television Title. Being so distraught, he couldn't wrestle. He had to take a hiatus. Months later, he made a return, taking on Xavier Kannon at the WrestleVessel pay-per-view. He defeated Kannon, but was screwed out of the World Title match that he should have had. Kannon won, and The Pharaoh was again pushed down the card as he decided to take out his frustrations on Frisco, the man who had knocked him out of contention before his World Title match had even started. It was a hard fight between the two forces. The Pharaoh no longer considered himself a god (in fact, he conveniently discovered that he was human all along) but had acquired a fighting force to back him up. Frisco had his own henchmen. Though there were constant battles between the two, it all ended with a fight that The Pharaoh won. Frisco was incarcerated in Argentina. He is on death row, currently. The Pharaoh's first obstacle was out of the way... Frisco was defeated. The only one left was Xavier Kannon. As Fate would have it, however, The Pharaoh would never be able to get to Xavier Kannon. As soon as The Pharaoh would get close to Kannon, something else would get in the way. At the first, The Pharaoh was placed into Continental Title contendership, and thus he would be stuck in a four-way battle for that belt. Xavier Kannon, as the World champion, was thus out of his range. The Pharaoh would not win the Continental Title, but part of him didn't want to because it would mean that he would have to wait even longer to take his revenge on Xavier Kannon. After that, The Pharaoh was taken into the World Title Division, he and 7 other men. There was such a clamor for the World Title that The Pharaoh would have had a hard time anyway trying to get to Kannon. Things were made even worse by a renewed feud with an old enemy. The Neo Void God, Akiyama, had been gaining momentum over the summer by single-handedly winning and defending the PCW Tag Team Titles. And now he was taken into World Title contention along with The Pharaoh, Duke Williams, Miyagawa, Clyde, El Diablo, and Snow. Given their past issues, it didn't take a rocket scientist to understand why Akiyama singled out The Pharaoh for destruction leading into the Red October pay-per-view. The Pharaoh had won a title from Akiyama, pinned him twice in one night; Akiyama, though, had taken the most important thing from The Pharaoh: his 'godhood', his sense of invulnerability. Now that they were both in the World Title rush, Akiyama wanted to both deny The Pharaoh of his World Title goal and win the World Title himself. It led to the Red October pay-per-view where there would be four matches to determine the main event and who would fight for the World Title. El Diablo turned heel to defeat Snow. Duke Williams suffered a broken nose, but outwrestled a brawling Miyagawa to advance. Clyde set Xavier Kannon's hair on fire in an inferno match to win the World Title in the first match of the night. Though Akiyama failed to win the World Title, he did eliminate The Pharaoh (who had already been injured and had to deal with a bad back throughout the match) in their singles match. The Pharaoh put up a valiant fight, but couldn't pull off the win. After Red October, Xavier Kannon was taken out of the equation for a short time. The Pharaoh had nothing that he could do except stay at the top of the ladder and await Kannon's return. An attack by El Diablo would officially put him on the injured list, though, not to return for a few months. During his time away, Xavier Kannon won Alliances for his team by acquiring RUSH and having some of its wrestlers assist him in eliminating Rush Mason, Skylar Green, and Akiyama. El Diablo would lose to Clyde on multiple occasions and walk away from PCW (and therefore take with him any chances of comeback retribution on the part of The Pharaoh). When The Pharaoh returned, he was immediately caught up in a feud with Clyde, as the two got into a feud focusing on Clyde's invoking the plagues of Egypt. The Pharaoh couldn't stop Clyde from releasing all the plagues, only the final one, by winning against Clyde in a brutal battle. The fight took a toll on The Pharaoh mentally, however, as he grew unstable. He temporarily lost his sanity. He turned his back on the fans and became one of the heels, something he hadn't been since his 'Divinity' days. Chet King, the PCW veteran who wasn't on a good streak since returning, stood up for the fans and feuded with The Pharaoh, who returned to win the Cruiserweight Championship. The Pharaoh brutalized Chet King and threw the Cruiserweight Title at Chet King, declaring himself too good to hold the belt. The Pharaoh had always wanted revenge on Akiyama and Xavier Kannon, but they were gone. The only other thing he could do was to win the World Title. And so we come to the present. The Pharaoh must defeat Totally Tubular Tommy and Skylar Green to fulfill the only goal that he has aggressively sought since entering into PCW. Every other battle he has fought has been a defensive battle, a distraction, or a fight for revenge. This will mark the first time that The Pharaoh is the aggressor, the first time that The Pharaoh has set his sights on his opponent before the opponent has targeted him. Skylar Green is another roadblock for him to face, but The Pharaoh considers him merely a harmless nuisance. Totally Tubular Tommy is the World Champion, a man who has gotten by on cheating tactics and/or to win the PCW World Title; a man whom The Pharaoh has targeted since The World Title became the only thing that The Pharaoh could go after. Much like Totally Tubular Tommy, The Pharaoh has developed a focus that he has never had before. His distractions were not for the fun of the business, but for always being trapped in a corner; for never having any choice in what he could do. The Supremacy main event is all about choice. The Pharaoh has taken his fate into his own hands; he is reaching for it and he will not rest until the PCW World Championship is his. Totally Tubular Tommy and Skylar Green each have their own motivations and histories leading up to this one match. These are but one man's reasons.
