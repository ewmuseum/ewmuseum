My own personal prison

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/12/02 The slow dripping of contaminated water from a small rusted tap rang sharply through the hollow dark chamber, echoing through the surroundings. The surrounding was nothing more than a cold damp haunting cave, with minimal essentials such as the water tap located somewhere off in the darkness, with the only source of light originating from a UV Ray torch attacked to one of the cold jagged walls of the chamber, beaming it's mighty light neon blue shade on the body of one man we had slowly become to get familiar with.............. The Sinister Paladin Gideon Poe. Here he lays, in this cold dark chamber, his arms tied by rope to two think long jagged pieces of rock which looked as though they were carved by demons themselves. His long black hair smothered most of his face as his body slouched miserably, the ropes preventing him to fall to his knees. Around him on the cold floor lay a pool of blood and vomit, both seemingly neon blue under the UV ray, even if Gideon did gesture his head up slightly his retinas would have been scarred and frizzled under the sheer force of the ray. Yet slowly his breathing began to pick up, deep and deeper, his arms flexing and wrists tightening, forcing more specs of blood to slide down them past the already crusted crimson fragments. Slowly his breathing spawned into a growl, before he mightily shot his head and body upright into the cold air, letting out a defining yell as he did so while pulling with his arms at the ropes, his eyes red and sore from the UV's rays, and his face drenched in cold sweat. The ropes slowly began giving way, substantial strands tearing and snapping in a falling domino effect..... He slumped back down again! Whatever mighty valiant attempt The Shade had put up to free himself once again had failed. He was weak, plain and simple. 'How are we today my dear boy?' came a familiar croaking voice. Gideon's head didn't budge until the UV Ray was shut off with another echoing 'CLICK'. Then slowly he moved his head from side to side, before gesturing it up to the voice of his unwanted mentor. 'What do you want old man?' he questioned in his deep dark matured voice. The old man came closer to him, even with Gideon nearly at his knees he was still a good foot taller than Jeremiah, who's long white hair and beard remained in perfect shape. 'Why Gideon, I'm here to end your torment my dear boy' 'Good, then let me die' 'Hahaha, my dear boy, you know you don't want that' 'Don't I?! Free me of this world, free me from this scarred earth. I've got nothing....' His words were cut off by him hurling up a sickeningly large combination of crimson blood and vomit. 'Nothing to live for I think you were about to say? When will you learn, that this fight you are battling Xavier Kannon in is hopeless. RUSH is his, move on, if you have nothing left to live for why do you still linger in RUSH defying him' 'I may as well die from this earth soon and go out swinging. Or you can let me die now, either way suits me fine you sick twisted fu...AAAAAAAARRRRGHHHHHHHH!!!!' His screams echoed through the chamber as Jeremiah drove a small needle into the back of his neck. 'GET THAT FUCKING ANTIDOTE OUT OF ME!' 'Nooooooo!' Jeremiah eventually yanked the needle out of Gideon's back, and slowly began cutting the ropes he had tied his student up with a large machete. 'One minute you want to live, the next you want to die. Either way you have no choice' Gideon then hopelessly fell to the cold floor, his body landing in the various pools of vomit and blood. 'You belong to me Gideon, the sooner you accept this, the better.' Gideon merely rolled onto his back, both hands resting on his stomach. 'I've got no life, no soul, nothing that resembles an existence.......I belong to no one, because I have nothing for anyone to own' 'We'll soon see Gideon... we'll soon see' Fin
