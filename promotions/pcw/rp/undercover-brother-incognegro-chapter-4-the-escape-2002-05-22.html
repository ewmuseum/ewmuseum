Undercover Brother, IncogNEGRO [Chapter 4. The Escape.]

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/22/02 If you missed chapter 3 let me fill you in right quick on what happened. Jordan was pulled from the backseat of the Cadillac Deville that he had rented earlier, wearing a mask. The KKK members that pulled him out thought he was one of their redneck friends named Jack. They were going to go on the hunt for some black people to do, God knows what to. The girl working the front office of a piss poor redneck owned motel, was actually a rather intelligent, young, KKK leading female, who had tricked Jordan and Rush Mason into thinking that she was a simple minded young farmer's daughter type. The KKK members, about 30 in total, were ready to attack and maim Rush while he slept. But since there wasn't enough room, about twenty of them had to stay outside. One of the few people that got to go into Rush's motel room was Jordan X Shabazz, under a ski mask, and thought to be another regular KKK member by the rest of the Klan. When the men entered Rush's tiny motel room, they were shocked to find Rush was gone! While some members shouted some racially motivated remarks, Jordan X Shabazz replied in a very black manner by saying: 'Shut the f*ck up!' Which blew his cover. Now with the ten angry KKK members 'Mean-Mugging' Jordan X Shabazz, he heard 'DUCK DARKIE!' from somewhere in the room... Meaning only one thing... Rush Mason. The scene reopens with Jordan X Shabazz dropping to the ground as Rush Mason comes out of the closet holding a big broom. He came out swinging! .... But being the jobberific Rush Mason that he is, he only took out one guy. With Jordan on the floor, Rush screamed. 'Jordan, to the dark mobile!' like an idiot. Jordan got up immediately as he and Rush managed to run out of the motel. With ten angry KKK members behind them. Jordan and Rush saw some of the other KKK members going around the back door of the motel, to see what all the commotion was. So, seizing the opportunity, Jordan and Rush hoped into the Cadillac Deville, and took off.... But some the redneck KKK members brought their trucks to the gathering, and they begin to follow Jordan and Rush. 'Oh damn it, Dark master Flex, what are we going to do?' Rush asked Jordan angrily. He was originally excited to come with Jordan on a supposedly easy mission of sneaking into a KKK funded church, and to break into their vault, and steal their money. But now that he and Jordan were in danger of getting attacked, he didn't want to be involved. Jordan didn't say a word; he kept his eyes on the road. He was a thick patch of road that was made up of dirt, and cobblestone, mainly dirt. As Jordan sped up, the dust flew into the air, disorienting the three trucks that followed them. As Jordan turned sharply onto another stretch of cobble and dirt road, he began to think. 'DAMN IT DOG! We've got a main event match on Friday I can’t risk getting killed. We're going to have to get out of the car and take off on foot...' 'Hell yeah! We're black, fast runners, those slow ass white people aren't going to catch us,' replies Rush with a grin. As Jordan and Rush turn into another town, Moore, Oklahoma, they come across a largely residential area. The houses were your typical white picket fence, rich peopled owned houses. As Jordan slowed down, Rush opened the passenger side door and braced himself for a jump. Just as he was about to leap out, he heard it. POW! POW! POW! The sound of gunshots echoed loud and clearly. These were no ordinary KKK members they were serious! They wanted the heads of two black men who decided to come into their neighborhood. Laughter, hooting, and various other redneck type things were heard from behind Jordan and Rush. 'Well, so much for running, right Jordan?' Rush says, pointing out the blatantly obvious. 'Yeah, but since their stupid as hell, they wouldn't think enough to shoot out our tires!' Jordan says. Well, you know how it goes; when somebody says something like that God decides to make him or her feel the effects of talking trash. POW! Squeak! HISSSSSS! The 'pow' was a gunshot, and the 'squeak' was the sound of the of the tire slowly peeling away. The hiss was the sound of the air as it left the tire. Jordan was now wobbling on the residential roads, so, typically, he sped up. Rush' face had a grimace on it, he wasn't happy at all. But good news was on the way, the three trucks that were chasing them, were now down to just one. Stacy, the KKK leader, drove it and she had two other men in the truck with her. One was trying to act like a sniper by leaning out the window and aiming for the other tires on Jordan's rented Caddy. But before he could shoot, Stacy swerved as she neared the remnants of one of the tires. Causing the redneck KKK member, fake ass sniper, to fall out of the truck window, and hard onto the road. 'AHHHHHHHHHHH!' screamed the redneck as Jordan checked his rearview mirror, and he laughed at the fate of one of the rednecks that were chasing him. Rush mumbled something about Jordan needing to get out of his freakin' car before it exploded and they both died. And Jordan decided to agree. As he turned out of the residential neighborhood, and into the industrialized area of Moore, Oklahoma, he stopped his car completely. As Rush got out of the vehicle and immediately began running. Jordan looked down the road and noticed that Stacy, and her cohort, were at least 25 seconds away. Just enough time for Jordan to head into the backseat and quickly take out the things he needed. Mainly his wrestling attire for Fusion, his laptop computer, and some wrestling boots. He stuffed them quickly into a Nike gym bag that he had brought with him. He slung the bag over his right shoulder and took off running behind Rush. As Stacy and her cohort turned the corner of the street and entered the industrial neighborhood, they met another car... A large van... BAM! BOOM! EXPLOSION! Yeah, it's obvious what happened to them. They got into a vicious collision, while Rush and Jordan ran away, leaving Jordan's rental car sitting in the middle of the road. Rush and Jordan run into a train station, a small one, perhaps the only of it's kind in Moore, Oklahoma. As they got to the bottom of the stairs, Jordan and Rush both leaned over and put their hands on their knees. Gasping for breath. 'That.... *Gasp* Was... *Gasp* Tiring.' Rush says as he chokes out his words. Jordan doesn't reply. He just picks up his stuff that he had dropped on the bottom of the stairs when they reached the bottom. Jordan is careful with his laptop computer, he doesn't want to mess it up in any way shape or form, and it was his only contact, besides his two-way pager, with the NAACP offices in Dallas. As Jordan notices the relative emptiness of the subway, Rush runs up to one of the ticket-selling woman, who stands behind a plastic window that separates her from the rest of the world. She appears to be about at least 50, and she's very ugly. Her uncombed gray hair falls to the side of her head, giving the impression that one side of her head is heavier than the other. She wears thick, horn-rimmed glasses. She looks at Rush with a look of 'I've been here all day, hurry up and get on with it.' Jordan walks over to Rush and he stands behind him, reading what trains take you to where. Jordan finds the train that will take him from where they are now, Moore, to Bayonne, New Jersey. Which isn't too far from Atlantic City, New Jersey, the site of PCW's Friday Fusion show, which is the first one back since the mass firing that occurred. Jordan orders the tickets for both men, he pays quickly, and both men are off on their way. Jordan and Rush walk towards the waiting terminal for their train, it was now 4:52 AM, and the train was set to arrive at 5:00 AM. There were a few other people there, all of them white, they all appeared to be strung out on drugs or drunk out of their mind. Jordan and Rush sit down on a bench as they await their train. Jordan unzips his bag quickly, and he gets his laptop computer. His first order of business is to head to his email, to check and see if the NAACP has sent him anything.... Nothing. Nothing at all. Next on his agenda was to check the PCW website to see whom he was facing for Tuesday. The staff usually tells you who you're facing a good week in advance so you can get your travel stuff together, but with the new roster and all, he had no clue. As Jordan clicked on the link for the early Turmoil card, he was shocked to see that he was facing a wrestler in the Extreme Division, former FSW Intercontinental Champion, Mike Frampton. Well, to Jordan he was just another cracka, on the PCW roster, taking up space. Only there because of his past history in that shithole known as Five-Star-Wrestling. Shabazz closed his laptop quickly as the train approached. Frampton, and the Ultimate Authority, the crackas that were going to get the Black Superman this week... Should be an easy week.
