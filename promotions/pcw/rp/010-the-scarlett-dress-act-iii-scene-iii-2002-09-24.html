010 - The Scarlett Dress; Act III, Scene III

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09 - 24 - 02 District Attorney's OfficeNew Orleans, Louisiana 9:00 am As she steped into a brisk cool space of air she called an office, Kendra James had mixed emotions over the couse of the past two days. While she had happened to be gone, everything had grown bigger. From the paperwork she needed to fill out, to E-mails from certain coworkers and pop up ads warning her of computer porn. She didn't care for that, just wanting a cup of coffee to settle her nerves and to check the morning paper. A secretary propped her head in-between the opening, looking into the room. Her aubrun hair drooping down past her creamy skin. 'Um, the Peterson case...' 'Not wanting it,' Kendra drifted far away from that as she began to lean back in her executive black chair. She played with her hair, running her fingers through it as the secretary moved into the room, taking a seat in a chair adjacent from Kendra's desk. 'Why?' the auburn girl asked her. Kendra took a breath, 'Because, this Jackson case is starting to pick up, finally.' she took a chuckle afterwards. 'Could you fetch me the morning paper?' 'Certainly.' the auburn girl told Kendra cheerfully. Leaning back, Kendra took out a Portable Disc player from her purse and tapped the play button, closing her eyes as she enjoyed the music. Soon a knock came over the door, shifting her view of everything back into where is the auburn girl with her newspaper. Turning her head towards the door, she could spot the girl, standing there holding the paper. 'What happened to you?' Kendra asked. The girl shrugged, 'Had to swipe this from Newberry. The old geezer was lying back at his desk, ogling over the Vollyball games on ESPN 2.' Kendra laughed, 'Just like the old man. Hand that to me.' The Assistant District Attorney outreached her hand and let the paper touch her palms. Flipping it open in front of her, she looked at the headline, before gasping. She tossed the paper down, before getting out of the chair, and hurrying somewhere. 'Where are you going?' the secretary asked her without much calm in her voice. As it was with Kendra, either you caught her before something happened, or she wouldn't speak to you for the rest of the day. The secretary had fallen for the ploy as she looked at the headline herself. There, she began to read it out quietly to herself, '3 Wounded in Shady Hills Massacre.' The shot went back to Kendra, walking down the hall with a step in her walk. She didn't even pay attention while walking in front of certain people. Pushing them out of the way, James even moved her boss, Alan Black as he began his very own way into his office. 'Excuse me,' Black halted the woman dead in her tracks. 'but you're straight in my way to my office. What's the hurry?' 'The hurry?' Black smirked. 'What do you think?' 'Oh that,' Kendra caught herself in mid-bravado. 'Well, I heard something that happens to be something you need to listen to. Its in the paper.' 'What did the press get a hold of now?' 'You know that Jackson case I've been working on?' Black nodded to her, knowing full well that his department of the state happened to hold court over everything put out by the press, the news conferences, everything. The budget happened to be over exceeded thanks to the lavish press backlash from Evans outburst a week ago. It all played out, like a opera, from beginning to end onthing would end until the truth was found. To her boss, Kendra James was more than just a female wanting attention and a spot, she was a trendsetter. In her very own way Kendra James was a rolemodel to many different people, and none of them could change the fact that they honored this woman, to her downed knee. Black sharply dressed in an Armani suit, with a cornflowerblue tie, moved in closer on Kendra, spotting her eyes bask around, looking for a way out. 'That case, yeah. What about it?' 'Well, its about the cases main suspect.' she continued on, before lowering her head. 'He may be dead.' 'Dead?' he questioned, with fear in his voice. 'You can't be serious.' 'I am, its in the paper today. Apparently somebody came into the main hallway of the cubicles, and started shooting holes in the room. We checked the scene out earlier, with Evans of course and he said that it was maybe something to call a fluke.' 'Flukes only occur once, this was no fluke.' he stated coldly. Kendra's eyes widened, 'You mean this was a planned hit?' 'Of course,' he began to note towards her. 'Its been well documented that during the June tournament, Jackson had gotten back into a heated feud with his former Mafioso crime boss, Reno Enders. The two of them happened to involve themselves in a church shootout, and a chase down LAX. Enders had almost every bone in his body broken by Jackson, and wound up spending a good three months up at Cook County Medical.' 'Jesus...' she trailed off, with her eyes beginning to grow heavy. 'Well, where's Enders now?' 'We don't know, after his release, we kinda lost track of him.' 'Dammit!' James cursed as she began to take a few steps back to her office. 'Why couldn't a worser person be shot at on that day?' As she continued to question herself, she only could wonder what in the world was Ender's motive for the killings, if he hadn't a thing to do with Jackson? It was nearly four months ago, how long can a person hold a grudge, Kendra thought to herself. In the true perspective, many people happened to have problems with Jackson, ranging from the press, to even the very own father, for some reason. And she didn't even know the story out of that one. * * * Davidson County HospitalNew Orleans, Louisiana 2:30 pm We rushed her down the white hallway, moving through anybody we felt like, because they were in worse need of help than my sister was at the moment. I didn't care, as the doctor's continued to flourish her with IV into her blood stream, barely keeping her alive. I overheard them saying that they could clog the small hole in her stomach, where the bullet happened to protrude and almost kill her, but I wasn't taking their answers for granted. Seriously, my sister could be dead for all I know. 'Help her now!' I pleaded with the doctor's who were trying to do the best that they could at the moment. The blood happened to be soaking through the towel as they shouted at one another, 'Clog the hole! Clog the hole!' 'Is that all you can say?' I sarcastically shouted towards one doctor, hovering over April as he fooled around, trying to take the bullet out of her stomach. 'Shut up!' he responded quickly, causing me to back away from the questioning. We pushed through another double door, into the emergency room where they told me to back away from the scene. I obliged as I sat down in wait, hoping that some good news would come out of this incident. I wished that I was the one that had gotten shot, not April. She didn't deserve any of it. I just met her and my flesh had been torn. Looking down at my shirt, I could still see her blood against my now drenched gray Akademiks baseball jersey. This was supposed to be my celebration moment, my American Dream. Winning two MTV Video Music Awards is nice, even for a Best New Artist like myself, but when family gets involved, I couldn't let all of that go. Not for fame, not for anything. I pulled myself off of the small blue bench, flanking me happened to be a gray haired man, riped with age and experience in these matters. Sometimes, I found old people to be the only ones that would listen, give up their time to hear a young person's view of life. 'Hey old timer.' 'Hey yourself, youngin'' I smirked, as his scratchy voice was a harmonica in my opinoin. Needed for good conversation. 'I thought y'all stopped calling us that.' The old man chuckled, 'No, we stopped caring about your generation.' 'My generation?' I asked the man, with a bit of observance in my voice. 'Our generation hasn't even started yet -' He cut me off, 'That's not what I'm talking about.' 'So what are we talking about then? I've just seen my sister get shot in the chest old man, don't even try to fool me that you've seen worse.' He turned his head, with a slight grin on his face. 'Afterall, youngin' I've been in World War II, so I've seen much worse than your sister being shot. I've seen men die, smelling their rotting carcasses and having them dragged over for American families to cringe and feel sorry for those who happened to kill them.' 'Damn,' I told him, finally stopping my odd and offbeat pacing. 'That's something serious.' 'Yeah it is.' I finally turned my head to face him, his tired ice blue's looking me dead in the face. In all of the times I hadn't paid attention to many people in my time, this was a person to give some attention towards. 'What's your name?' 'Lieutenant Benjamin Darling, Marine Corps, Second Battalion.' he replied to me, causing me to instantly jerk my hand in his direction. He shook it strong, gripping it before letting go of it. A smile grew on my face, before the doctor broke through the emergency doors, holding out a clipboard with a phone book's worth of documents to sign over. 'Do I have to?' I asked the good doctor meekly. 'No son,' I sighed when he told me that. 'You'll have to pen them.' By that time, I had crashed to the floor with my eyes bulging out of my head. Almost like those Japanese Anime cartoons when somebody says something so stupid, it causes everyone to fall to the ground in shock of his idoicy. 'Documents, ugh.' I sighed to myself, as I wiped off the small patches of dirt clogged onto my shirt and pants. I could only cringe at the mere thought of having my hand crumpled up like a cement mixer thanks to the paper work, but I guess it'll be noteworthy. Something to single my name out. I took a pen out, clicking the top as I continued to press on with my name, and April's as well, showering the documents, until I happened to come midway through. My cellphone began to buzz against my lower thigh, causing me to stop instantly. I changed my intentions away from the stacks upon stacks of paper and a name happened to appear in the call box. Kendra James. 'Hello?' 'Dante, its Kendra.' 'What now James? Haven't you tried to fry me enough?' 'No, that's Evan's job. But what I'm trying to tell you is that someone is trying to kill you, and April.' 'What makes you think I hadn't thought of that?' 'Because Jackson, either you did kill Tyrell and somebody is after your ass because of it.' 'Wait, I didn't kill him.' 'Why should I believe you? You were in the house, had your blood on the mayor, even your prints were on the murder weapon. So tell me a good reason why you can't be turned in and sent away rocking in the slammer for the rest of your life?' 'Because, I...' I didn't have anything to say.
