A New Side.......

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd May 2002 Success....... Success is measured in many ways. Success can be measured by how much money a person has, but would you truly call a Millionaire or Billionaire, successful??? Sure they are successful in business, but are they successful within themselves??? I think not...... Now what does all this talk of success have to do with The Dark Messiahs??? I hear you ask. Well unless you have been under a rock the past couple of days you will have learnt that The Dark Messiahs have arrived in the PCW. Also that the PCW is airing it's first show since the roster cut, Friday Fusion. Now Fusion is set to be an explosive line up with The Dark Messiahs taking part in a PCW Tag Title Tournament...... Now you would think looking at the card that Daniel and Mike have pulled the short straws, because in the opening matches of this tournament they are going up against former Tag Team Champions, The Heartbreakers. However this is not the case for The Dark Messiahs, for you see they are more dangerous than you realises. You see at this moment of time you know nothing about the true Dark Messiahs and what they are all about. All you know is that they are 2 guys who took part in some dark ritual and get severe headaches now and then. However as this promo continues you will realises there is a much more evil and sinister side to Daniel and Mike............ The scene opens back up in the apartment where we last left Steel and Stone. However even though we are in the same apartment, it doesn't look like it is. When we left this apartment the place was pristine, now it looks like a bomb has hit it. The glass coffee table has been smashed into pieces, the sofa has been ripped to shreds, and the paintings have been ripped from the wall and destroyed. Even chunks of the brick wall have been taken out!!! But out of all these things the most worrying thing is the path of blood which runs across the oak floor into the room next door!!! Suddenly we hear Steel's voice coming from the next room....... Daniel Steel's Voice: (Fearful) Jesus Christ!!!! Quickly we follow the path of blood, which leads us into the next room where Steel and Stone stand over Derek's motionless body. Looking around the room it looked like this was a bedroom, the reason I use 'was' is because just like the lounge this room has been decimated. The bed has been over turned, the side lamp has been smashed, the stereo has been torn apart, and the blinds have been ripped down. The light streams through the window, highlighting Steel's and Stone's faces. Both their faces are full of dread and fear, as they stare down at Derek, who isn't moving. Part of Steel's face is covered in blood, and also his arms. Stone is of a similar state, but most of the blood seems to be on his hands. Both men look at each other with their eyes full of fear........ Daniel Steel: (Still Fearful) What the hell happened here??? Mike Stone: (Fearful) I don't know. The last thing I remember is getting that severe headache, then I just blacked out. Daniel Steel: Same here. That pain was immense I’ve never felt anything like it, since.......... Mike Stone: (Interrupting) The forest!! Daniel Steel: Exactly. (Pause) So what are we going to do??? We got to phone the hospital, he needs help. I can't believe some people nowadays, I mean how sick minded do they have to be to do something like this??? Mike Stone: (Agreeing) I know, they must have attacked whilst we were blacked out. Daniel Steel: Yeah. (Sighs) Come on then let's get moving, we got to report to the PCW......... Mike Stone: (Interrupting) What about Derek??? We can't just leave him like this, he is my friend. Daniel Steel: (Thinking) Right, well as I said, we ring the hospital, tell them the address, and let them come tend to him. Mike Stone: (Wondering) Why can't we take him to the hospital??? Daniel Steel: (Sighs) It isn't exactly going to look good if we turn up carrying Derek in the state we are now. We are covered in blood, those bastards obviously wanted to set it up so it was us, who took the blame. Now come on we haven't got time to waste........ With that both men walk out of the bedroom and into the lounge. Stone has pulled out his mobile and is obviously phoning the hospital. As Steel opens the apartment door and they both step out the last words we here from Stone is........ Mike Stone: Yes, I would like to report an accident..... As the door closes we take one last look around the lounge. As we do we notice something flickering on the TV which even though on it's side still has the camcorder attached. As we look closer we can see the picture more clearly. It's Steel and Stone!!! Suddenly the screen flicks into action, and Steel and Stone are tearing apart the lounge!!!! They are screaming in anger!!!! Derek lies on the floor in freight with blood dripping down his face. Steel and Stone have snapped, they have totally changed!!! Steel moves over to the camcorder and now has blocked the rest of the scene. The snarls of anger coming from Steel can be heard as clear as day. He throws the camcorder to the floor as it fills with static the scene fades to black........ PCW Head Offices.......Present Day....... The hustle and bustle of the reception area of the PCW Head Offices fill our ears as the scene opens up. It is obviously a busy day in the PCW, as everyone is preparing for PCW's first show back, Friday Fusion. The reception area is a very well laid out place with high glass windows letting the light stream in. In the left corner is a 'waiting area' with leather seats and a leather couch. Also as in most waiting areas there is a coffee table with a pile of magazines. A beautiful blonde blue eyed receptionist is attending the reception desk itself. As she is sat down the only part of her face we are able to see is her face. To give you the best picture of her face would be to say Trish Stratus. As we take a look over the reception area, Steel and Stone step through the doors. At first they look overwhelmed by the size of the place. They are wearing their usual gear, baggy blue jeans and black T-shirts. They have obviously cleaned up from the earlier fiasco. They quickly move over to the reception desk where the receptionist is busy on the phone........ Mike Stone: (Politely) Erm, excuse me miss??? The receptionist holds up her index finger to signify for them to wait, as she continues on the phone....... Receptionist: (Finishing Call) Okay, yes I will make sure that gets to Mr Booker and Mr Nelson, okay, thank you for calling, goodbye. The receptionist hangs up the phone and shuffles a few papers before looking up at the Dark Messiahs.... Receptionist: (Fake Smile) How may I help you gentlemen??? Daniel Steel: (Smiling) Yes, we are The Dark Messiahs, we're here to finalise some details with Mr Nelson. The receptionist checks her computer then turns back to Steel and Stone with a smile still on her face....... Receptionist: (Still Smiling) Okay, just take a seat in the waiting area and I will tell you when Mr Nelson is ready. Mike Stone: Thank you. Both men move over to the waiting area as the receptionist goes back to the phones. Both Steel and Stone sit down with a sigh in their voices. Stone quickly grabs a magazine and opens it. We see that the magazine is none other than 'Playboy.' Stone turns the magazine round as he looks at the pictures, to Steel's amusement........ Daniel Steel: (Laughs) Hey Mike, you ready for Fusion??? Mike Stone: (From behind the magazine) Hmmm........ Daniel Steel: (Laughs again) Will you put that smutty magazine down and speak to me!!! Stone puts 'Playboy' down on the table, and looks over at Steel with an angry glint to them...... Daniel Steel: (Laughs) Hey don't look at me like that man, I’m just trying to help you get over your porn addiction, it's really harmful to your health you know. Steel looks over to Stone and sees that he is steaming with anger, and quickly decides to change the subject....... Daniel Steel: Anyway back to my original question, you ready for Fusion??? Mike Stone: (Insulted) Of course I am ready, why would you think otherwise??? I've been training just as hard as you have!!! Daniel Steel: (Holding hands up) Okay, okay, chill man, I meant no offence. The reason I was asking was just for conversation purposes. Jesus you are really on edge at the moment, what's up with you??? Mike Stone: (Confused) I honestly don't know. Ever since that incident at Derek's apartment, my insides seem kind of mixed up. I know it sounds weird but........... Daniel Steel: (Amazed) No, no, it doesn't sound weird all, I’ve felt exactly the same. It's like my head is all over the place. Mike Stone: Exactly.......I really hope it passes by Fusion. Daniel Steel: (Hopeful) It will, don't worry. I mean we've been preparing for this moment since we were 14. Sure we may be facing former PCW Tag Team Champions, but come on they were only champions for 1 hour. I mean 1 measly hour as champions doesn't make you the best in tag team in the PCW. Mike Stone: Yeah I know, and I suppose up to now they have had it pretty easy. Sure they have faced teams like Politically Incorrect, but not to sound too full of ourselves but they ain't anything on us. Daniel Steel: The rest of the PCW probably has a view of us, just as some newbies who got lucky. But what these people need to realise is that we were brought in to the Tag Team Division for a reason. Everyone knew that the tag team division was in trouble, so we were brought in to give it that kick, it is as simple as that. Mike Stone: Yeah, and Fusion is where it is all going to start. It may be a struggle but I think we can get through it. So what if we are up against tough competition. The tougher the better I say, because when we do come out on top the more we will have proven our point. We are here in the PCW for a reason, and that is to be come the Tag Team Champions, and no one is going to.............. Before Stone can finish both he and Steel have grabbed their heads yet again and are screaming in pain. The hustle and bustle of the rest of the reception area has stopped as they are all focused on Steel and Stone. Both men are now on the floor screaming in pain. A crowd has no surrounded them as both men begin to shake like they were having seizures. The receptionist quickly pushes past the crowd and kneels down over the two men. As she does Steel and Stone have stopped shaking and seem to lie there for a few seconds......... Receptionist: (Concerned) Sirs are you all right??? As she asks this both men sit up. They look around them with an evil glint in their eyes. They then both look at the receptionist. Something seems to have changed in Steel and Stone, something sinister has flooded over them. Along with the receptionist both men rise to their feet. Steel takes another look at the crowd, he seems to grow angry, as everyone stares at him........ Daniel Steel: (Shouting) What the f**k are you lot looking at?!?!? Back the f**k off before I do something you may regret!!!!! None of the crowd moves they seem shocked by this graphic language coming from Steel's mouth. Stone looks over at Steel, and the anger seems to spread to him as he shoves the receptionist back........ Mike Stone: (Shouting) What the f**k is wrong with you people?!?! You deaf or what?!?! He told you to back off, so back the f**k off!!! Come on.......MOVE IT!!!! The crowd doesn't move a muscle. This just angers both men more as they look at each other in disbelief. Daniel Steel: (Furious) Fine if you aren’t going to back off yourselves we are going have to make you!!! Quickly Steel grabs a suited man from the crowd. The man looks terrified as Steel stares him directly in the face, with the anger raging inside him........ Daniel Steel: (Shouting in the man's face) Now perhaps you can explain to me why you and all your suited pals can't take the hint and back the f**k off!!! Man: (Terrified) I, erm, erm........ Daniel Steel: (Mocking) 'Erm, erm,' wrong f**king answer!!! Now my friend you are going to learn why me and my friend Mike Stone here are the most dangerous tag team in the PCW!!! The atmosphere in the reception area is nothing but tense. Everyone is silenced from these actions from The Dark Messiahs, this is a new side of them we have not seen before. The man is in tears of terror. Man: (Weeping) Please, please!!! Mike Stone: (Shouting) Shut the hell up!!! We don't care what you think. I've had enough of this chitchat, it's time for you to learn the true meaning of pain!!! The man even though silenced, tears still stream down his face as Steel throws him over to Stone who grabs him by the throat. Stone lifts him up as if to chokeslam him whilst Steel has taken a running jump off one of the leather seats and hits the man straight in the face with a missile dropkick, as soon as he has hit Stone brings the man crashing down right through THE COFFEE TABLE!!!!! The man is out cold and glass is shattered all over the place. The crowd has quickly disbursed in terror as Steel and Stone look down at their bloody victim. Steel turns to Stone and smile evilly, and Stone returns the smile......... Daniel Steel: (Evilly) That's what 'The Heartbreakers' have in store for them at Fusion!!!! Mike Stone: (Evilly) There is going to be pain, there is going to blood, but most importantly there is going to be The Dark Messiahs standing tall!!!! With that both men laugh evilly as they turn their backs on the fallen man and walk out through the glass doors, as the scene fades to black.............
