The Assassination Series Part Four: Four Way Dance...

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/5/03 “I…uh…have…uh…to…uh…stop!” Warrior panted, as he grinded to a halt and propped himself up on a nearby wall. “We don’t have time to stop!” Blind Rage replied, extremely animated. “Look, some of us don’t have superhuman stamina like you…” Blind Rage seemed to consider this for a minute. “Yeah, and…” “We’ve been sprinting for about an hour or so…” “I really need you to get to the point.” “Well normal humans just can’t do that…except the really weird ones that do it for fun!” “Ah…I see. You’re telling me you’re out of shape, is that it?” “No. I am very fit, but I do not have superhuman stamina, hence the stopping.” “Come on, don’t give me that…you can pour it on a little more than that!” “Yeah, but what is the point of getting to the Warehouse to rescue Handbag Warrior and Badger Man, if I’m in no fit state to fight!” Blind Rage seemed to consider this option again. “Okay. Five minutes, no more though!” “Uh…uh…you’re one damn slave…” Warrior breaks off as Blind Rage is attacked from behind, by someone. He didn’t recognise the perpetrator, but the blow didn’t do Blind Rage much good. It pissed him off. Warrior has told his ears at the primal scream Blind Rage’s vocal chords manage to emit as the behemoth slowly turns to confront his attacker. It is clear by the dress of the attacker that he appears to be some kind of mercenary/super villain taking the opportunity to try and cash in on the ‘outrageous’ Owl-Man’s bounty. From the mercenary’s fighting stance, it was clear that he knew how to handle himself and he was clearly versed in the world of martial arts. He also had a stereotypical villain moustache right from the 1900’s. He had to be French there was no doubt about it. “I don’t know who you are boy…but you just admitted yourself in Justice Inc’s house of pain…RRRRAAAAHHHHHRRRRR” Blind Rage roared, as he charged towards the mercenary. The mighty blow from Blind Rage missed the extremely athletic mercenary, and indented his fist into the concrete pavement. The mercenary retaliates with a savage kick to the back of Rage’s head, again with little or no effect to the mountain of a man. In fact, it just made him more angry, if that was possible. Quickly, Rage managed to remove his mighty fist from the pavement and charged at the mercenary once more. A brief look of despair developed upon his face, as barely managed to flip over the top of the mighty superhero, causing him to crash through a wall. The mercenary stood with his back to Warrior, stroking his 1900’s moustache in a menacing fashion. “Hey!” Warrior exclaimed, managing to gain the mercenary’s attention. “Who the hell are you?” “Who me? I’ am the mighty Batroc…” Batroc replied, in a thick French accent. “I knew it…a damn Frenchy. You have no friends Frenchy!” “No sheet…I’m here to keel you.” “I don’t know what the hell you’re talkin’ about, but Blind Rage is going belt you.” “Huh?” WHAMMMMMMOOOO. Blind Rage’s uppercut connected and lifted the mercenary clean off his feet and clear across the street, only stopping with the impact of a building. “Shouldn’t have got him angry, Frenchy!” Warrior declared, his attention was quickly taken with another primal scream. “You can stop now he’s seriously gone!” “RRRRRRRAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHRRRRRRR” Warrior turns to see an animal like creature bound toward him, with rapid-like saliva dripping from his mouth. He recognised him instantly as the mutant known as Sabretooth. Warrior barely managed to evade the razor sharp claws of the rabid animal, as he ducked, rolled and smoothly extracted both of his laser axe’s from the side of his leg, all in one effortless motion. He quickly got back to his feet and effortlessly fell into a fighting stance. Sabretooth roared again and lunged forward with another one of his razor sharp claws. Warrior spun out of the way, managing to get behind the monster, he then took the monsters legs out from underneath him with his blunt laser axe frames. Sabretooth quickly got back to his feet and smiled at his opponent. “Nice. This should be fun.” “Yes, it should.” Blind Rage’s voice bellowed from behind the mutant monster. “Huh?” Sabretooth turned to catch another one of Blind Rage’s mighty uppercuts, which even lifted the monster known as Sabretooth clean off his feet, however this time, due to the mammoth’s body weight, the building wall didn’t stop him. “Nice.” Warrior exclaimed, as he struggled to see the mutant’s lifeless body as he soared to new heights. “You ready?” “Can’t we savour the moment first?” “The only time we’ve been attacked, is when we’re stationary…if we’re not stationary, the chances suggest that we won’t get attacked.” “Damn you and your level-headed reasoning!” Blind Rage simply sighs, and begins to job towards the intended target - the warehouse where the Handbag Warrior and Badger Man are being held by the deadly Bullseye - soon they would walk directly into the trap, which had already been set by the super villain. However, some things were unavoidable and were necessary. This was one of those situations.
