Nowhere To Return

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-29-02 I stared there, at the abandoned shop, for what seemed like hours. In reality, it could have been years. Or could have been moments. This was it. My prayers had been answered. The troubles of my life had been cleared, and now, my focus could once again return to my true calling… the teaching of the WITs. Oh, what a splendid joy it was to see how these average men had under my guidance become so much more… and in the future, no barriers remained. I thought about each of these Warriors In Training for a long time. WIT 1, with his fearless attitude, almost as if destined to become a warrior like myself. WIT 2, jumping to the conclusion of any question, some before even asked. He will be a teacher like me some day, I thought. Oh, and of course, there is WIT 3. Perhaps the most controversial of the pupils. The most daring, the most outgoing, the most disobedient. Almost as if he has a thirst for trouble, that is. Almost as if he can’t help talking back to his superiors. Almost as if… he were me… These WITs, they had gone through so much with me. My strenuous training through the PWA… my captivating feud in the XWA… and of course, my abrupt arrival in PCW. Primetime Championship Wrestling. The place where champions are born—and alas, I was too. It took only a few weeks to attain the Rising Star Championship belt. And to this day I held it close to my heart, laying pain and misfortune to those who seeked its golden glory… Golden glory… Gold and glory… Gold & Glory? Ah yes, the enormous event, scheduled to happen in only a few nights. Who was I fighting? Kris Burden, that was it. The young superstar that had made such an impact on the powers that be. Such an impact that he required no training, no long stays in lower federations… immediate arrival to the top. I wasn’t resentful… was I? I wasn’t angry, just because I had so much tedious work to get to where I am, and this… newcomer immediately grabbed the spotlight? No, not jealous… just confused. But I couldn’t worry about Kris Burden right now. I couldn’t worry about PCW. I had to concentrate on the thing that mattered most—rebuilding my life. Almost humorous was it that I had waited an entire week before coming here, to the abandoned shop. That I had not even told them why I had left in the first place. If I so wanted to devote my life to the training of these WITs, why was it that I stayed so distant? In the deepest realm of the warrior, there is some need for companionship. The word companion immediately made me think of another word. Friend. I remembered vividly how I had wanted a friend so much that I had believed in my mortal enemy. So foolish was it how I had stumbled deep into one of his bitter mind tricks, actually conceiving that I, the Infinite Warrior, was in fact the evil one. I thought that Lance Minion had changed. I didn’t think of him as The Insane Clown, PoGo, anymore. So convincing were his lies, that I left my WITs under his supervision, time after time. …So that they grew accustomed to him, as I was busy with my career. So that I was out of the picture. That couldn’t be, could it? No. Of course not. It was ludicrous to think that these WITs, whom I trained myself, would fall under the evil grasp of Minion. In fact, they were probably inside right now, waiting anxiously for my arrival. I swiftly moved towards the door. It couldn’t be, I thought. I burst inside to the main room. Nobody here… that was okay. They were right in the next room, right inside the door across the way. That’s where they were… waiting for me. I crept slowly towards this room… came right up close. I opened the door, closing my eyes. As my feet stepped in… nothing happened. No noise… no movement… Opening my eyes, I looked around. The room was empty. The room that my WITs were sworn to stay within was without a trace of human life. They were gone.Sitting down on one of the beds, a tear dropped from my eye. “'What good is being free,”' I said aloud, “'if there is nowhere to return?”' With that, I lay back, and before I knew it, I was drifting into a deep sleep.
