Battlefield PCW...Part 5...'Shackled'

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-17-2002 The scene opens into a cavernous stone alcove that is illumined by a single torch. In the center of the dimness stands a lone figure. The camera closes in and we are met with the visage of Venneman Pendragon. Stripped to what appears to be a loincloth, he hangs suspended from the ceiling by his arms that are shackled to a chain that runs through a hoop above. His head hangs down and his body is limp. He bares the bruises of what must be a hundred beatings. His hair is matted and he is quite filthy, quite a contrast to the regal force we are accustomed to. Crusted blood decorates his visage in various places… “Two days of hell. But I am strong still. These dullards, they wish to break the Lord King, but ‘tis not in the cards. I have dealt with more than these paltry fools can toss at me in my time. But, I must admit, even I do not enjoy the beatings. If only there were a way out of this mess. I have important work…Work that is pivotal for the redemption of the Land of Gauze, pivotal for PCW. I need to find my way…” His eyes open and he gazes longingly towards a sword that gleams brightly, even in the gloom. His blade, Excalibur, leans against the wall next to the iron door that closes his solitary prison. His eyes spark with the intelligence that has served him so well over the years… “And the bastards taunt me with the sword of Kings that should be by my side. They think they taunt me. What I lack in my own power, gazing upon that most sacred of blades makes up the difference. And yet, shackled as I am, I cannot think of a way from this prison…” Before he can finish his thought, the sound of a key rattles in the iron door. The hinges creak as the door swings inward and in walks Lyco the Joblin. Pendragon raises his head a bit to look at the smallish figure, but drops his head when he sees who it actually is. Dressed in boiled leather armor, the Joblin looks like a child set about playing soldier. The club he holds in his right hand doesn’t look childish though. The club looks downright nasty… Lyco: Ready to bow down and kiss my feet, infidel? Venneman: Must I really answer that question, you foolish bastard? I bow to no man…Or child… Lyco’s face darkens as he approaches Pendragon with the club… Lyco: I had hoped you were not broken as yet. This is far too much fun to cut short, don’t you agree? Pendragon says nothing but tightens his jaw as Lyco begins to swing the club at his midsection. The cavern resounds with a hollow thump as the club bounces off of Venneman’s well defined abdominals. Lyco makes his rounds, beating the front, then the back, then the sides, carefully avoiding the Crusader’s head. Lyco: The pain….It is awakening, yes? We could end this easily, Crusader…Just relent and swear allegiance to the new ruler of Gauze….Cravier Dannon! Pendragon raises his head a bit but it falls against his chest soon after…. Venneman: *whispers* Closer… Lyco’s face lights up as he looks closely into the face of Pendragon…. Lyco: Something to say, infidel? Do you relent? Pendragon’s eyes fly open and he lets fly a large wad of spit that splatters onto the face of the Joblin. He quickly steps back and wipes the spittle from his face and shakes his head… Lyco: You will learn to regret that….Your Majesty… Pendragon merely laughs. He starts slow, but soon the sound builds and Lyco doesn’t like it… Lyco: SHUT UP FOOL!! ***THUD*** Lyco: DAMN YOU! ***THUD*** Lyco: CEASE! ***THUD*** Pendragon keeps laughing even though Lyco whales on him with the club. Lyco’s frustration soon becomes evident though as he steps back, wipes his mouth, and leaves. As soon as the door latches shut, Pendragon stops laughing and frowns at his blade… Venneman: Fear not. One day your edge shall drink of his blood…I swear it. “Alone again, but for how long? ‘Twas the same thing every day. Lyco would come to mine prison, beat me with the club until his sickness was sated, and then he would leave…Frustrated. Is it so hard to understand that I shall not be broken? ‘Tis a battle of the wits, and it was not one I was about to lose. Superiority would overcome. I only hope the same can be said of my men.” The door swings open once more and in walks a man with red hair. Clad in light armor, a sneer dominates his face. He carries no weapon, only himself. As he passes, he grabs up the blade Excalibur and holds it with his right hand. He approaches Pendragon and holds the blade to his face… Man: Oh, how you must yearn to be able to hold this blade once more, do you not Crusader? The perfect balance, the deadly edge; a perfect weapon. Venneman says nothing as the man waits for a response… Man: Do you not wonder who I am, Crusader? Venneman: Why would I wonder? You are nothing to me; merely another who would revel in the torment of his captive better. Neigh, you interest me not, brigand. Man: But I am high amongst your captors, King Pendragon…I am the Lord of Flayme. Pendragon peers at the fellow but still says nothing… Flayme: One day, you will see who the better is… Venneman: What do you want? Surely you have come for a reason! Flayme: I merely wanted a look at the beast we have captured. You do not seem like so much to the likes of me. Venneman says nothing and stays motionless. He knows it is pointless to try and wrestle against the chains that hold him. It only brings pain and takes energy. Energy that he would need later. With a growl, Flayme slaps Pendragon across the face with the back of his left hand before tossing the blade to the floor and leaving the cavern… “Not only am I bound, but I must deal with fools the likes of this? This predicament was becoming worse by the moment.” Before the thought fully leaves, the door opens once more and a strangely dressed fellow enters. He wears a kilt and a bone helmet, along with a stout looking leather mantle. His eyes are distant as he walks up to the bound Pendragon. Pendragon takes a deep breath before turning away in disgust from the newcomer… Venneman: By your reek, I would dub thee the Mad Celt…I am correct, am I not? Celt: Of course…Who else? Venneman: And has the Crazier one sent you here to get me to….Relent? Celt: Nope. Curiosity has led me here. You are an oddity in the land. Nobody stands up to us. I merely wanted to see first hand the epitome of stupidity. You are undone. You are trapped. Defy the odds if you will, simple math says that you are destined to go down to defeat… Venneman: We shall see, Celt. Celt shakes his head as he makes for the door. He pulls it open and nearly runs over a woman who has come to see the oddity that is bound in the cavern. She gives Celt a sly smile as she closes the door behind him. She turns to Pendragon and looks upon him with a mix of both hate and reverence. She wears little, tan short pants and a tan breast wrap make up her ensemble as she goes barefoot. She slides up to Pendragon and circles his chest with a finger…. Woman: So big, so strong…Such a man. Indeed you are strange, Crusader… Venneman looks up at the touch and peers into the woman’s face… Venneman: And thou must be the Wench of Thomas…Your cruelty is spoken of by the oppressed peoples of Gauze… She takes a bow… Wench: I see that we have something in common…Both reputations precede. You and I…We could do great things here in the land. You could rule the people with me by your side. Venneman: HA! You wish a strong puppet who will bow to your feet. A cruel leader to hide behind so that you might elevate yourself beyond where you might rise upon your own… Wench: You would be so trite? Venneman: I would refuse you because it is the right thing to do. Wench: You will regret it, Crusader… Venneman: We shall see who regrets what, wench. In a huff, the Wench of Thomas leaves and Venneman sags once more. He shakes his head and lets out a chuckle… ***FLASH**** The scene abruptly changes to show Pendragon, still battered, still in his loin cloth, back in his bedroom. A note lies upon his chest and he shakes himself to a glimmer of consciousness as he reads the words… Venneman: You will return when you finish…Know this as fact, Crusader… “I was in my own home once more, in time to make it to my Turmoil matchup. I guess there is a higher power working in this after all. Otherwise, I would still be shackled in the lair of Dannon in the land of Gauze. Turmoil shall see me victorious, and after I deal with the fools who would try to take my place in line for MY TITLE I will return to the Land and victory there shall be sweet as well….Celt, Tomas and Flaymin Aymin. Lambs to the slaughter for a man seeking vengeance and satiety…” >>>TO BE CONTINUED
