How long will this act go on? Missing my crunk!

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/02 -Duke Williams is on stage with his lady Bonnie. He’s playing her Malice Style right there on the stage at the Travolta Tavern in Hubbardville, Wisconsin. The crowd is jamming out to the soul filled blues and country beats Duke is picking on Bonnie. He pauses down and takes a sip of Hubbard Band drinking water.- Williams: You know your old Malice friend is taking a lot of heat in this here community perpetuated by a young man who resides in this town. He brings up statements of my past and situations where I was not so friendly toward you great people of Scientology. -There is some talk through out the crowd.- Williams: That’s right I was down right despicable. I was mean, angry, malicious, and hell bent on taking out what I thought was a bunk of a religion. -The grumbles in the crowd get louder.- Williams: People I’m a good man who fights for the blue-collar simple man. So why the hate towards our religion? Why the anger and the frustration? It’s a simple answer, look at the spokesmen you send out to put your name out there in the PCW. Xavior Kannon is nothing more then a whiney, arrogant, self-absorbing brat who uses the name of Scientology to further his career and get his way. This is the image he is circulating to the rest of the world. -The crowd becomes very attentive.- Williams: Think about it for a moment. Those of you who watch wrestling understand what I mean. He gets booed not because he’s a Scientologist like he claims but rather because he’s a first right cum puddle. Pardon the foulness but that’s the cleanest I could get it. -Duke sips down some more water.- Williams: Look at his company. I’m calling out Xavior Kannon to respond to my questions in the Hubbardville Herald. Number one do you or do you not hang out and team up with a man who spawns hatred in the form of oppression to the tenth degree in Mark the Racist Nazi?? Do you not work with a CEO who has been investigated by the federal government for corrupt business opportunities? Do you work with a warden and his most violent criminal? Do you not work with a man who wants to split peoples wigs? Have you really set a good example of what Scientology is all about? -Duke holds up today’s copy of the Hubbardville Herald in which these questions are asked of Kannon.- Williams: Kannon your not mistaken about my past and my anger and oppression I forced upon Scientology. However before the TR training you we’re not a very nice man were you? Were any of you? You see I have changed my life to live the preaching’s of L. Ron Hubbard. I’m a converted man with a new out look on life. A new attitude towards life. A new opportunity to advance my life to the next level. I thank Scientology for that and I will do my best too spread its good word. I will do so in a positive, enlightening way, that will not anger others. You can still love Xavior Kannon as he is still one of our brothers. I love Kannon, but I also have to do what is best for us as a whole and I hope you can understand that. -One clap turns into about five hundred as they cheer on the Malice Man.- Williams: With that lets rock out a bit shall we?? Now I know most of you are my age and not quite as young as our puppy dog friend Kannon. Some times when I wake up and face the day in a young mans business I feel like a dinosaur. Now I don’t know what the hell a Nelly is but I’m sure the kids enjoy her. Unfortunately there are not a lot of kids in scientology so lets do this old style. -Duke tunes up his guitar and starts to pick as the crowd claps along to the tune.- Williams(singing): HEY MAN THEM AIN'T HIGH HEEL SNEAKERS/ AND THEY SURE DON'T LOOK LIKE COWBOY BOOTS / AND THAT AIN'T ROCK & ROLL YOU'RE PLAYING/ AND IT SURE AIN'T COUNTRY OR RYTHM & BLUES/ YOU'RE SINGIN A SONG ABOUT MAKIN LOVE TO YOUR DRUMMER/ WELL GAY GUITAR PICKERS DON'T TURN ME ON/ AND WE DON'T ALL GET IN TO NELLY/ DO YOU HAPPEN TO KNOW ANY OLD BILLY JOEL SONGS/ CAUSE YOU SEE I'M A DI-I-NOSAUR/ I SHOULD'VE DIED OUT A LONG TIME BEFORE/ THERE'S A WHOLE LOTTA DI-I-NOSAURS/ GIVE ME MY HAT/ EXCUSE ME MAN BUT WHERE'S THE DOOR/ IT USED TO BE WE HAD A LOTTA FUN IN THIS OLD HANGOUT/ WE'D GET STONED AT THE JUKEBOX AND STAY OUT OF FIGHTS/ NOW & THEN WE'D HAVE A LITTLE SMOKE IN THE TRUCK OUT BACK/ OH THEN A LITTLE JIM BEAM AND WE'D GET RIGHT/ AND YOU KNOW THESE FLASHING LIGHTS SURE MAKE ME DIZZY/ AND THIS NEW MUSIC IS VERY STRANGE TO MY EARS/ IT LOOKS LIKE THEY'VE TURNED THE LONGHORN INTO A SPACESHIP/ AND I'LL BE LEAVIN JUST AS SOON AS I FINISH THIS BEER/ CAUSE YOU SEE I'M A DI-I-NOSAUR/ I SHOULD'VE DIED OUT A LONG TIME BEFORE/ THERE'S A WHOLE LOTTA DI-I-NOSAURS/ GIVE ME MY HAT/ EXCUSE ME MAN BUT WHERE'S THE DOOR/ GIVE US OUR HATS EXCUSE ME MAN BUT WHERE'S THE DOOR -Duke takes a bow to the juice drinking audience who cheers him on.- Williams: Thank you very much I reckon you’ll know what I mean. That just about does it for me you don’t have to go home but you can’t stay here. Remember to look both ways before crossing the road. Chew with you mouth closed. Always wash behind your ears. And if the good Hubbard’s willing and the creek don’t rise will do this again next week. Free hot dog day at the shop tomorrow you’ll have a good night. -Duke walks back stage where john Travolta tosses him a towel.- Travolta: Nice Job Duke. Williams: Thanks hoss. Travolta: I love when you call me hoss! WEEEEEEEEEEEEe Williams: So what’s going on? Travolta: Not much just getting ready to close down. Williams: We can head over to the Hubbard Red Hots and I can cook up some dinner. Travolta: You have milk shakes there? Williams: A five-dollar milk shake. Travolta: Bad ass. -The scene changes to Duke and John sitting in a booth. Duke is eating some dogs and John is sipping a thick chocolate milk shake.- Williams: So what was in the brief case? Travolta: What ever your mind tells you was in it. Williams: Bullsh*t what do think was in the brief case. Travolta: Enhanced wisdom from L. Ron Hubbard. Williams: Really cool? Travolta: You know I think your right about this whole Kannon situation. He’s poising our image. Williams: He is with out a doubt, a tarnish on the gold values we hold so high. Travolta: You can say that again killer. What’s the deal with him bringing in more celebrities? We already have enough. Our star power doesn’t need to be deluded by bringing in inferior celebrities. Has that other John ever been in Urban Cowboy or Saturday Night Fever? I mean who the hell is he. Williams: That’s what I’m saying!! What the hell is a Nelly? Travolta: I think my daughter listens to Milly, I sure don’t. Williams: That Nilly gives me a headache and gas. We’re just old timers. I guess we just don’t get it. Travolta: What do you mean old timers?? You and I are the epitome of cool!! We set the stage that the kids follow. My dancing and you catch phrases have been curving culture for the last twenty years. I’m not going to be ousted by some one hit wonder movie star. Your not going to be out done by a young arrogant punk wrestler who tarnishes our image. Williams: Damn it your right!! Travolta: You need to get out there and beat this kid down in the name of Hubbard and age. Show the kids why us old timers will always rule. Stick it too him. Williams: Malice Style!! I catch your jive hoss. Travolta: What’s the deal with that money hungry hooker he’s always floating around with? Williams: She’s a leech who is control that man. Travolta: At Supremacy you take care of Kannon I’ll take care of Ellie. -Duke looks at John with an inquisitive look.- Williams: I don’t follow. Travolta: Before the show I’ll do some dance moves and swoop her off her feet. Then Kannon will not have the one thing he needs. His mind. Williams: Damn fine idea but I don’t know if you can pull it off. Travolta: I’m John Travolta I can do anything. Williams: and I’m Duke Williams and Kannon is going to get Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound…..Hubbard Style!! Travolta: Right on! -The Camera fades on the aging icons giving each other five.-
