Part Dos! (Morning Show on Hot 97)

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/02 {::The limo driver pulls up to a reserved spot for visitors. He parks the limo and Lance and the crew step out of the limo. They look around at the building and they see the Hot 97 logo to the right of the brown looking building. They walk in as they are met by the visiting woman. She gets their names down and then, they are searched by security guards and then they are shown to where the morning show is being held. They look up and see the ON AIR logo in red. They walk in as the first one to see them is Miguel, the shows producer. He shows the five men to their seats as Star turns around and chuckles as Buckwild is in the back half asleep and Miss Jones is on her two way talking to some friends. They are on commercial, but a few seconds later they are off of commercial and they are back on the air....::} Star:This is Hot 97, This is the Star & Buckwild Show featuring MS.Jones. We have our guests for the day, PCW's wrestler named Lance Bell and his little hoes (laughter in the studio) Lets get this thing crackin my negro's..... I'm going to love this show today (smiles) {::As the camera pans around looking at the five friends they look a little bit afraid. Not knowing what they have just gotten themselves into. Everyone in the studio begins to laugh as Star seems like he is just warming up.... the scene fades as Lance begins to speak....::} Star:Anyways, I gotta tell you this, date I had last... Yes, I had myself a white bitch please, applaud my black ass, I gots me a white bitch (fake applause's) Anyways, I pick the girl up at her house, yadda yadda yadda.... we had a great dinner.... and after all that bullshit... Buckwild:Did she give it up? Star:Hey, please negro, let me finish the story.... anyways. I was driving her back to my place and she was on her cell phone.... and I over heard her conversation..... she said 'Sure, I'll babysit tomorrow at his house.' HIS, being me.... Know what I did? Ms.Jones:Oh God, I wonder what you did.... Star:Yeah, I took her cell phone threw it out the window, I was whoa, whoa bitch..... anything happen to those kids that's my ass....(laughter in the studio) Ms.Jones:So wait, you threw her cell phone out the window and said that to her? What, are you crazy? Star:(raises voice alittle bit) Hell Yeah, that bitch was going to get my ass in trouble.... the white whore, anyways.... we have Lance Bell and his little hoes today.... JuJu:(laughs nervously) Hehe, we ain't his little hoes.... Star:(stares down JuJu), Ok.... so your his big hoes.... JuJu:(laughter in the studio) Well, we aren't his hoes at all.... Star:Right, anyways we are also here with Lance 'The Sensation' Bell, how you doin my friend? Lance:Doin' real good.... Star:So you big time superstar now, tell me, and don't lie, how many bitches you get a night? Lance:(chuckles) no bitches man... Star:Whoa, Whoa, hold up, you gets not hoes? No prostitutes, and you wrestle in PCW? What the hell is your problem, you gay my friend? Lance:Heh, no....... Well, I just started, I lost to Jon Kano and I had a draw with Bob.... so in the records I'm 0-1-1, but that'll change... I'm too nice for that federation... Kyle:Lance is like so totally rad dude, he's like unstoppable. Ms.Jones:So lance tell me about yourself and explain yourself. Lance:I am the best wrestler out there in PCW. I'll just have to wait and get familiar with PCW to succeed to my highest abilities. Tomorrow night I face Red Rock in my first ever title shot for the Rising Star Title. Ms.Jones:Well, confidence is good.... Star:Yeah, alittle too confident.... So tell me a little bit about your friends here Lance. Lance:Well you got Kyle, James The Third, JuJu & Flava. Star:James The Third... you one of those British cats ain't you? James:(has a british accent) Excuse me? I'm a human being.... not a cat as you referred to me earlier. Star:(looks confused) Hey, shut up you ignorant bastard.... it's slang you British hoe... (laughter in the studio) James:WHY I NEVER! Star:Oh please shut up with that why I never crap... (turns to JuJu as he is laughing) Who the f*ck is you laughin at you sorry excuse for a black man... JuJu:Whoa, Star, why you gettin on me for? Star:Listen, listen, the one thing I hate to get into is arguments (laughter in the studio) but tell me, what you doin followin this white guy around for... are you getting paid? JuJu:Um No. Star:Good god man, you are a disgrace to all black men.... Kyle:Dude, do you know what's so funny, he said that you wouldn't be crackin on him! Star:Oh really, son, just because you black exempts me from crackin on ya' broke ass? You cheap bum... you disgrace me.... Ms.Jones:So Lance what do you think about these two matches that are coming up Sunday and Tuesday? Lance:Let me say it like this, Nobody, and I mean nobody is going to stop me on these two days. On Sunday, I have a very good chance of becoming PCW's Rising Star Champion, and on Tuesday is just a match where I need to prove to myself and to everyone else that I can hang with the big boys and beat up on Jon Kano, Newbludd and Mike Storm. Ms.Jones:Sounds like a plan {::As Lance and Miss Jones continue talking to one another, JuJu at the time is playing around with a bottle cap... He seems very much un-interested in what Lance and Ms.Jones are speaking of. Then he loses control of the cap, it rolls on the floor and next to Star's feet. JuJu bends down to pick it up and....::} Star:WHOA! WHOA! Hold up bitch, what are you doin next to my long john (laughter in the studio) JuJu:Nah man, it's not how it seems... Star:Man I know what it is, you are a broke ass, d*ck suckin hoe.... you she-man, get the f*ck out of the studio. Tryin to give me head during my show, what's wrong with you fool? Get out! (laughter in the studio) {::Smokey bows down his head and gets up from his seat and walks away. James, Kyle & Flava follow him out as Lance stays put....::} Ms.Jones:Well, Lance good luck tomorrow night and on Tuesday, and your future in PCW.. Star:Yeah, and make sure you get some bitches, what is life with out a secured wife, kids, money and a mistress? Just can't happen... Lance:I'll make sure I'll keep that in mind.... {::As Lance gets up from his seat, the scene fades to black::}
