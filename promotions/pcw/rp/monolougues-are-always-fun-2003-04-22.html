Monolougues are always fun!

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 22, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within what seems to be a dark room with only a single light in the room. You hear the sound of a door opening and then closing and then you see Semore Smoke, pulling back a chair, and sitting down, with his face right in front of the camera. He looks straight at the camera and begins to grin as he speaks… Semore Smoke: Hello everyone, it’s been awhile since I addressed the fans directly and to be quite honest, I’m glad I haven’t done it in awhile, because the reactions I’ve been getting are just ridiculous and horrible! I mean, how can you boo Semore Smoke, that’s like the Braves actually winning a World Series… it’s one of those things that aren’t supposed to happen. But anyways, I’m not here to talk about the fans, just to address them. I figured I’d begin using these promos to promote myself instead of what I do and how I make my living. You see many people are scared that I may try to “buy” Primetime Championship Wrestling, well, that’s untrue as of right now, but don’t think I’ve automatically put it out of the question, it is an option since I got so much money to throw around these days. I just felt I should throw that rumor down the hole before it even gets it’s own spot on the PCW website. Now, onto my last match with Adam Stryker, AJ Trinidad, and Jackson Rust just a few nights ago. Jackson Rust is a loser, so he doesn’t even deserve my attention, but since he is my partner this Friday night on Fusion, maybe I should devote a little bit of denotation to him. He cost myself and himself the match, and his ass better wish that I take out my aggression against Trinidad, Tracer, and Dante Jackson tomorrow night, instead on him during our tag match this Friday. Now, onto Adam Stryker and AJ Trinidad, Trinidad is probably the most pissed off wrestler in his day right now, because he still does not have his precious kangaroo friend back yet, Crespo. Well, AJ, I’m afraid I lost Crespo, or did I? If he’s with me on Turmoil, then you’ll get him back, but if he isn’t, then you won’t ever see that damned kangaroo again, and that’s a fucking promise. Adam Stryker may be like one of your running buddies, but you two didn’t seem to have any blood lost against each other Sunday, I did enjoy seeing the two of you fighting it out in that match, even though some dumb ass figured you were a woman, how stupid is that? Adam Stryker himself though won’t be a problem for me to get rid of, he’s only in this because his ass turned against me earlier, and I hope like hell that his ass is taken out of the picture for good after this Friday, and that will happen for sure. Maybe I should address my thoughts on this Wednesday’s Turmoil. My match is high up the card, and why it is up the card, I have no idea, but I’m climbing like I had promised I would and there’s nothing stopping me from gaining the championships that I long to have and soon will have. Trinidad, yet again is standing my way of an opportunity for another win, and he’ll lose for sure, I’ll make sure that I tear his ass a new one in our match and there is no stopping that from happening. His win Sunday will be avenged when it comes to Turmoil and Fusion this week, when I rip everyone’s legs off and stick them up their asses as if they owed me some money. Dorn Tracer however is new to me, I have no major beef with the guy yet, but if he stands in my way this Wednesday night, then I’ll take care of him in one swift night. Trinidad decided to take things a bit personal, and I decided to take that a bit further when I kidnapped his kangaroo friend, Crespo, he’s lucky that I left him another friend, whose name escapes me as of right now, but he’s just lucky that I left her for him to talk to. As for Dante Jackson this Wednesday, he had a grueling match against El Romantico and could surely be in trouble when he faces myself, hell, even Trinidad has a chance to put him away for once in his life time, and that’s a lot to say for AJ. Dante needs to step up his work however, I saw his promo earlier, and to be quite honest, it pissed me off extremely. The fact that he’s gonna just put down my former home fed of New True Internet Championship Wrestling, or better known as NTICW, the crap federation of Wrestling Tattler. But the fact that he’s gonna announce that I was the World Champion and that RUSH was a hell of a lot better then NTICW and that he was in RUSH, Jesus Christ, what the fuck does RUSH and NTICW have to do with anything in PCW? I’ve evolved a lot since my days in NTICW, just like I had evolved a lot from my days in PWA when I was in NTICW, I’ve worked with the best and I hope he realizes that soon enough when I kick his ass on Wednesday night Turmoil. There’s not much more to say, other then to promote my new incorporated business of money loans. Money Inc. is it’s name, and money is it’s game, if you need money, then just call this number, [detail withheld], you may get an answering machine, and if so, then just leave your name, number, address, and what your stake in my loan is. I remind you that you are stated to pay me back an interest rate of twenty-five percent, and if you choose not to, then your ass will be grass and I’ll surely enjoy smoking it. Expect my team of expert repossession men to come to your house on the day that my money is due, and if you don’t pay up, then expect it to come out of your ass, literally. So don’t hesitate to call today, the number is flashing down below, long distance charges will be counted if applied. Thank you. {{{{Smoke stands up as the phone number continues to flash at the bottom of the screen. The scene then fades to black, in the already dark room…}}}}
