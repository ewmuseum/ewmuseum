Return of the Skitzo: Finding Fusion Part II - Riding The Bullet and...Channelling the Quo?

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-10-02 Skitzo found himself in a nice economy class seat on the Bullet Train. After a hard night beating down on a PCW Hall of Famer, one does like to indulge in the culture of one’s surroundings…or book into a sleazy hotel and watch pornography all night. Skitzo was going to opt for the latter, being a man of passion and…“self-catering”. Finding himself in PCW again was one thing, but being booked in a clusterfuck for his first return bout was quite another. Sitting next to a man looking out of the opposite window, Skitzo sighed, thinking to himself. Skitzo: PCW again… Skitzo: This train stinks… Skitzo: There’s something about travelling to a PCW venue a week before it’s due I don’t like… Skitzo: It’s the smell damn you…it’s like…chicken and grease… Skitzo’s nose takes him over to the man looking out of the window at the passing locations. Taking a big whiff, Skitzo recoils and then coughs. The man turns slightly, just enough to catch a glimpse of the man’s face. Skitzo: By Jove it’s the lollygagger… Skitzo: Who? Skitzo: ONE-TIME PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPION, SUCKA!!! The man by the window turns and sees Skitzo, who turns towards him and smiles. For a moment the look of horror on the man’s face is all to be desired, until it turns into a look of confusion. Skitzo: Doth mine eyes deceive me, or do I do declare I do see Statuz Quo sittin' right over herre! Indeed, Skitzo is sitting right next to Jordan X Shabazz V.2.0 and current one half of the Tag Team Champions, Playaz Inc., though Statuz doesn’t seem to look so enthusiastic towards Skitzo. Statuz Quo: Who da fuck are u? Skitzo: Who be me? Skitzo: Who be we? Skitzo: We be ye? Skitzo: Shut up… Unfortunately, Skitzo’s personality disorder is unknown to the God of Ebonics, which Statuz makes clear in his almighty literate reply. Statuz Quo: Shut up? Who da fuck u think u talkin' to? I ain't ya damn momma! Skitzo: I am Skitzo, PCW employee one year in the making...you're Statuz Quo ain'tcha! Skitzo: …My mother was a fiend… Statuz Quo: Oh lord, anotha cracka hired back by PCW...If I wuzn't Statuz Quo, why da fuck would I answer to it bitch? Skitzo: Because you might be R. Kelly, and you know he likes to make up different personas to lure his prey in-...uh...I mean...meet his fans! Statuz takes the remark offensively, and turns so he’s facing Skitzo face-to-face-to-face-to-face. Skitzo however gazes on none-the-wiser. Statuz Quo: Are we tryin' to be smart? Look herre motha fucka, don't make me jump da fuck over this chair and whoop yo motha fuckin' ass… Skitzo: Look at the sign overhead sir, it says 600 seated, no standees! Statuz Quo: It also says handicap seat in the back but I don't see u payin' attention! Skitzo: Why would I? It's not my place to pay attention to your mother while she’s in her seat... Skitzo: BURN! Skitzo: You damn right! Skitzo: SUCKAAAAAAAAAA! Statuz’ face is clearly filled with anger, as he contemplates a hard right off Skitzo’s face, then remembers he is a representative of the good ol’ United States of the Ghetto and stops. Looking ahead, he can see the waitress-type girl bringing a cart full of snacks and drinks down the aisle, but at that moment the miniature bottles of Vodka are replaced with the registering of the ‘Mother’ comment… Statuz Quo: My momz dead u stupid ass motha fucka. So would u really be lookin' back at my momma? Jus' shut da fuck up fo' I kick ya teeth out of ya mouth… Skitzo: Mr. Quo, I did not spend all those Yen to purchase a Train ticket to be seated next to a scallywag like you, we are both employees of PCW, where is the love? Statuz Quo: I have no love fo' faggot ass crackaz…letz get dat str8 now… Statuz Quo takes a deep breath and relishes in the victory over verbally knocking Skitzo da fuck out. Quo stops the waitress and picking out a miniature bottle of Smirnoff…the non-cracka kind. Skitzo however jumps right back into the argument, never a beaten man on the verbal side of things. Skitzo: SU EBONICS ME ASUSTA! Statuz Quo: Wut? A light bulb suddenly shines over Skitzo’s head as he realises what Statuz’ one weakness is-… Statuz Quo: Cracka-bonics? Skitzo: Ich Willensgeschlecht herauf Ihre Mutter… Statuz begins sweating under the collar, as the snack lady heads down the aisle a petite woman in an outfit walks past, barely acknowledging the two PCWers. Statuz Quo: Um assistance woman, I believe ya'll let on a special ed student… Skitzo barely even acknowledges Statuz in his ramblings and suddenly freaks out. Skitzo: Statuz Quo...the tag division would be crappy without you... Skitzo: Otherwise all the contenders would have no material for trash talking! Statuz Quo: They still have no material fo' trash talkin', they jus' get trashed on… Skitzo: I'll bet...say Statuz, how you lookin' forward to that there Mall Brawl on Friday... Statuz Quo: I ain't lookin' forward to dat shit at all. I'ma jus' head to da pot sto' and smoke me a blunt while lettin' ya'll crackaz go at it. I ain't got tyme to waste my talent 'n a cracka/cluster/fest… Skitzo: WELL I AIN’T GOT TIME TO BLEED! Skitzo: If you’re Aimz you got no choice… Skitzo: Sure Statuz, if you had any talent I'm sure you'd wish to conserve it! Statuz Quo: And I'd still have more talent then u… Skitzo: Sto andando ballare sulla vostra tomba! Statuz Quo: Where's dat fuckin' woman at?! I mean, I didn't kno' we were ridin' on da special ed/cracka train! Skitzo: This isn't a train it's a bus… Statuz Quo: Train, bus, wut da fuck, they all go shit… Skitzo: You ride on them! Skitzo: You make them!! Statuz Quo: Ha-ha-ha, shut da fuck up…Diz zoo ass bus, lettin' animals like u on 'n shit… Skitzo: Vous êtes un morceau de bonne journée de merde... Skitzo: AU REVOIR, VOUS CUNT!!! Statuz Quo: I don't even kno' wut da fuck u said, jibberish talkin' bitch Statuz Quo: I outa whoop yo trailer park livin' ass jus' fo' sayin' shit I don't understand! With that Statuz picks up his things and moves to another seat far behind Skitzo. Giggling schizophrenically to himself, Skitzo waves to a gazing onlooker, somewhat distraught by this display of Yankee communication. Skitzo gives a sarcastic shrug towards the little old woman and winks at her, receiving a gesture of the tongue in return. Twitching suddenly, Skitzo’s eyes widen in horror and he turns towards the window Statuz once was staring out of, trying to follow the passing Japanese cities and scapes as his eyes begin to get heavier and heavier… To Be Continued, Mon Cher!
