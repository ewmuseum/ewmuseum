The Sage Of A Champion Series Part 4

Date: 9-2-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion'The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!'Part 4 Chapter 3: The Battle To Destroy BattlePowers BeginsDay 3: Part 2 of the battle against BattlePowers {BattlePowers? BattlePowers? BattlePowers? WTH? I don’t know understand?} {Are these the type of questions you ask of? Well here part one of the stories of BattlePowers} ::Scene:: The year is 2015. Wars in the world become more and more threatening. Everyone is scared that at any moment a war is just going to break out of nowhere. The world is so corrupted by the government and other countries wanting to go to war with each other that living is almost impossible… The stock market has dropped faster than a speeding bolt and the world is slowly going into a great depression. There is just enough of the stock market left for living, but not enough for much extra. The CIA and FBI almost don’t even work together anymore. Most alliances ever formed are all slowly being destroyed. It is basically every man for his own well being at this point in time. The only “alliances” you could say that are really formed are families and the army/navy/air forces. Hopefully that makes sense to you all… Anyway, after “9-11-02” the world had never been the same. Because of that one attack we almost have no world at all. If it isn’t the countries fighting it is civil fights themselves that have destroyed the whole world to begin with…. Well it is trying to at least, yet it don’t sound as bad as it is because people tend to live their own lives, children still go to school, and life for the most part seems normal. In fact, most teenagers don’t even realize all the wars going on. This may not be making any sense… I mean, how is it that wars are going on… including civil wars and the economy is almost dead yet people still live their lives? Well it is hard to explain… Let’s just say the world goes on well civil wars and things of that sort. It is truly not easy to explain. Just take the crime rate and multiply it by two. You will then understand where I am coming from. Out of all this that is happening the NMIAAF (National Military and Infantry Association of Armed Forces) was formed. It used to just be the NMA (National Military Association), but now that the world is in such a bad contention that this NMIAAF was formed. Basically this association controls anything and everything that deals with war. It is a very strong association that controls the army, navy, air forces, ect. You get the picture. Only the NMIAAF has the power to call war and send troops out, things of that nature. The President himself can’t even call a war. Congress at this point only controls residential things. Food, money, ect. Congress no longer has the decision to consider wars and things of that sort. Congress’s only concerns are of civilians, so if something doesn’t concern civilians in anyway, then the NMIAAF is responsible for it. The NMIAAF is the second most powerful organization in the world. Congress still has the most power in the United States. The NMIAAF also controls the FBI and the CIA. This is the only reason why they work together in any way. Other than that they are two completely different organizations that do two completely different things like for the most part, it has always been. Only now that the NMIAAF controls them both the NMIAAF plus control the army, navy, and air forces they have a lot of power in their hands when it comes to war. Charles Banks is the head of the NMIAAF. The man is only 45 years old, but he is a very smart businessman and he has all kinds of degrees and majors in history, politics, and other things relating to war and even business. He is considered one of the smartest men on Earth and that is the main reason why he is lucky one to be the CEO and President of the NMIAAF. This may sound strange, but he was the one they created this organization in the first place. The story behind his journey to get the NMIAAF is a story of it’s own. The NMIAAF is the organization that put together this little war in itself called “BattlePowers.” BattlePowers like said before is a simulation of war in real time. It is designed to help soldiers in war. Basically, when you go through a “battlepower” you go through everything you would normally go through in war. From every bullet hitting you to every guy you kill. “BattlePowers” is located in the outskirts of Arkansas in the lower left hand area, by Texas basically. BattlePowers is a very huge place. It consists of four main environments. There is a BattlePowers – Desert Area, Jungle Area, Street Area, Sea/Ocean Area, Air/Flight Area, and Antarctic Area. Each designed to simulate war in that specific environment. Robots are used as the opponent and their AI is probably better then most human AI, so basically if you complete BattlePowers a regular war will be about two times easier. BattlePowers is also designed to make war two times easier which is why the AI is harder, so that when men go into war they have nothing to fear. They already have the knowledge to do better then their opponent. Other countries have tried to replicate BattlePowers, but have failed at doing so. The only way you can get into BattlePowers is if you are in the US Army, Navy, or Air Force. There are tons of background checks, scanning, and other things of that sort to go through before you can get into the “battling areas.” Other countries have tried to get Americans to become spies for them, so they could find out what BattlePowers looks like and how it works, but no camcorders or cameras are allowed in the facility. Other countries have attempted to destroy the place, but failed because of the high security used by the NMIAAF they are able to stop most attacks and for the most part other countries leave the area alone.The NMIAAF keep most of the information classified, so civilians only know what they are told by news reporters, which when stuff like that happens, we all know it isn’t always the best thing in the world. In fact, normal civilians know nothing about the danger involved with BattlePowers. Since it is only a simulation they think it is very safe and support it a lot. The actual people that do participate in BattlePowers aren’t aloud to speak a word of what that see or do while in BattlePowers and if they do so their punishment will be decided on what was told. The minimum punishment is being expelled from the force they are with, but you could actually be sentenced to death if you told too much, so that isn’t much of a problem. BladeD is taking the biggest risk of his life trying to shutdown BattlePowers because he has to watch what he says to people about BattlePowers. No matter what you do, the NMIAAF will find out if you told someone about anything that deals with BattlePowers…. That is the kind of power NMIAAF has. BladeD is risking death, but it isn’t new to him. He is actually in line to become President of the NMIAAF because of the accomplishments he has had in his lifetime, but right now that is in jeopardy. In fact, that is one thing they are trying to do. They are going to completely make him President, and possible Co-CEO if he will “come to his senses” and forget about trying to shutdown BattlePowers. BladeD would like it a lot to become President and/or Co-CEO of the company, but he wants BattlePowers shutdown and just becoming President or Co-CEO can’t get it shutdown. Something like that has to go through Charles Banks as well, so BladeD can’t become President and/or Co-CEO and shut the thing down. If he could he most definitely would that is for sure. His only hope is that he can find a way to get the place shutdown without losing his life or anyone else’s at the same time.
