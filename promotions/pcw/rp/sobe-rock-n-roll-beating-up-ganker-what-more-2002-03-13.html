Sobe, Rock'n'Roll, Beating up Ganker, what more?

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-13-02 8:00pmSeattle, WashingtonMentioned: MetallyxCharacters Used:Ganker RansolverHank Gunyon~!Janelle StoutAna Lagunez --- The segment begins with Hank and Janelle in the living room of Hank's apartment. Hank is wearing a long black leather trench coat, black jeans, blue scorpion t-shirt, and a multicolored beret. Janelle is wearing a long sleeve light blue t-shirt, dark blue jeans. Hank's arm is around her as they watch the flat screen sitting on Hank's black leather couch. Ganker is asleep on teh love seat of the apartment with a blanket over him and a pillow over his face. Hank and Janelle are watching Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back and nearly laughing their a**es off in the process. Ganker wakes up and yells, 'NO! NOT THE FLYING MONKEYS! KEEP THEM AWAY!' In a terrified tone.>Hank: 'The monkey dream again Gank?'Ganker: 'Yes, it still gives me chills to even think about it. It be so damn terrifying, damn flying monkeys.'Janelle: 'What are you both on about this time?'Hank: 'Simply put Ganker was bit by a rabid monkey, no disease was spread, although I still have my doubts that something didn't affect his brain. Anyway, the monkey bit Ganker and seemed like it flew away and ever since Ganker's been having dreams that a flying monkey biting him over and over. It really has had a toll on his sex life, ask Lindsey Carter or maybe even Ana.'Ganker: (slapping self) 'I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself.'Hank: 'Calm down bro, everyone has a bad dream they keep repeatin'.'Ganker: 'Really?' (Hank nods) 'Even yo man?' (Hank looks away whistling) 'That's what I thought... Damn flying monkeys.' (sighs) 'I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself, I hate myself...' (sighs as he turns to watch Jay slap Silent Bob, he laughs) 'HA, that cheers me up.' (grins) 'Now back to what I was doin' previously.' (sighs) 'I hate myself, I hate myself...'--- Hank gets up and walks into his bedroom and over his cd system as the scene switches to his bedroom and shows him flipping through his cd collection of bands like, Tool, Metallica, Radiohead, Saliva, Hoobastank, Rob Zombie, White Zombie, Ozzy Ozbourne, Black Sabbath. Some of those now no longer exist, but there cds ain't they? Hank puts on Metallica's Kill'em All album and blasts out his speakers as the scene switches back to the living room to where Ganker is slowly rising with a look of possession coming upon his face, you know the whole, wide eyes, no facial expression. Ganker then jumps on the loveseat and starts to air guitar and headbang, he headbangs right over the back of the loveseat. Hank turns down his system as Ganker is shown head butting the floor in anger. Hank then reenters the living room and sits back down by Janelle as she leans her head on his chest and snuggles in close. Hank wraps his arm around her and hits stop on Jay & Silent Bob. Hank glares over at Ganker head butting the floor, then turns toward Janelle and kisses her lightly on the lips and grins.Janelle: 'That was nice.' (smiles)Hank: 'Well let's jus' say I'm in a good mood babe. I mean how could I not be. I'm wit' my girl watchin' the funniest move ever made. Hell, this Friday Fusion I get to even the score wit' my arch nemesis Red Rock and his little, and I do mean little, partner, D-Kay Calyx. All D-Kay is, is a replacement for the man they used to call the other half of the AKR, Duzza. Red Rock needed to fill Duzza's place somehow and I guess D-Kay cuts the mustard, I mean he is in Red's new stable, The Deuces, The Wild Deceuces to be more exact.'Janelle: 'well you have to trust a psychopath for a tag team partner though.' (laughs)Hank: 'Yo obviously have never seen past matches I've been in. I jobbed to myself, I ran at full force into a wall. Ganker and his depressing head buts to the floor are nothin' to half the sh*t I've pulled on myself feelin' sorry. I still beat myself up whenever I am bored or Ganker and I are walking through the park or mall laughin' at the less fortunate.' (pauses) 'That's always enjoyable.' (laughs)Janelle: 'Fine, you win, you're the most psychotic wrestler I've ever met, now get back in your asylum cell.' (grins) 'You bad boy you.'Hank: 'I will later babe, but only for yo.' (smiles)--- Ana Lagunez enters the apartment with two six packs full of Sobe. Ganker jumps up as Ana pops the top to one and the scent catches his nose. Ganker grabs it and devours it down as fast as he can. Ganker then flexes and does his 'Ganker Is God' thumb thing. Ganker then jumps down back on his loveseat with another Sobe in hand. Ana hands Hank one as he pops the top and sips it. Ana lays the cases down and sits down in a black leather reclining chair.Ana: 'What'd you do to him now Hank?' (smiles)Hank: 'The usual, but remember, it's all in my preparation for Fusion, torture Red Rock until he submits.' (laughs diabolically)--- The segment cuts out. ---
