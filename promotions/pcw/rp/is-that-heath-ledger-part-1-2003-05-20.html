Is That Heath Ledger? (Part 1)

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20, 2003 Tyler Preston paces around the movie set of “To the Ring and Back” waiting for his counterpart, John Gargano to show up. John is busy discussing various issues with the direction so that he is well informed lest he score the role in the movie. The scenery is pretty basic, and still in the construction stages. A ring is placed in the center of the building with blue, iron folding chairs surrounding it. This mimics the actual venue or a wrestling arena. The ropes of the ring are a scarlet red. A large, white male with black tank top flexes his muscles as he screws the ropes in to the turnbuckle. There is a loud banging of steel against steel that echoes through the set and provides a shrill sound for anyone present. Tyler walks with a confident smile, on the verge to execute another quality prank. Tyler looks a lot different. He sports hair extensions that hang from his blonde head. He has allowed stubble to gather on his face. He walks with a confident stroll and keeps a solemn face. Tyler Preston is a spitting image of the famous actor, Heath Ledger. For extra measure, he wears a ‘A Knights Tale’ T-shirt to really sell the idea that he is the famous actor that he intends to depict. He can hardly keep a straight face, as he knows that his costume to very realistic. He searches for John to let him know the news on his latest prank. As he searches, a young, “teeny bopper” looking girl approaches him. She wears black, thick framed glasses with a blue tank top and tan Capri pants. She composes herself as she musters up the courage to speak with ‘our super star’. “Oh my gosh, Heath, I am so in love with you. All your movies are like the best thing I have ever seen,” the girl says, trying to get on his good side. Tyler continues to gaze around the set for John. He finally comes to grips and knows he will have to handle this girl. He smiles and nods to her. “Thank you, why don’t you go get something for me to sign. You obviously are one of my biggest fans,” He says in a confident tone of voice, a little lower then Tyler usually speaks. The girl is in awe that a super star would pay that much attention to her, so she bolts through a door to retrieve something for him to find. Now that Tyler has scored some time, he bolts for a corner where he suspects John Gargano will be. Sure enough, John Gargano sits in the corner with his wrestling garb on, looking over a flimsy script. He says lines out loud and acts them out in a professional way. Tyler saunters in that direction, wanting John to spot him. After a few more lines, John looks up from his script and sees Tyler. He bolts over to him, thinking that Tyler is the famous Heath Ledger. “Wait a minute! Are you who I think you are?” John asks with awe in his eyes. He circles around Tyler, shuffling his feet. He examines Tyler to see if his notion is correct. Tyler smiles, and is glad that he has been present with a chance to test how affective his prank will be. “Well, that depends. Who do you think that I am?” Tyler asks, attempting to change his voice as much as he can. He brings it down about an octave so that John doesn’t notice that it is him. Unbelievably, John does not recognize the voice, and Tyler does a silent cheer in his head. John continues in an excited and frantic voice. “You are Heath Ledger aren’t you? Oh my word, what brings you to my set? I just figured that you were getting a little too big for this kind of stuff,” John says, trying to get in good with the thought to be movie star. Tyler just chuckles in another low tone. He continues mimicking the famous actor the best that he can. “I like the small movie roles. I mean, a guy has to stay humble doesn’t he?” Tyler asks as he smacks a stable hand on to John’s back. His hand slaps through John’s thin black T-shirt, causing him a little pain. John opts to act man enough and not show any signs that it stung. “You mean you’re going to be in this movie to? Man, some one must be hiding the memos from me because I never caught word that you were in on this one,” John screams with excitement. In the back of his mind he can’t believe that Mick or any other member of the staff for that member would keep a crucial piece of information like that from him. “Yea, I’m going against you in the ring. My character’s name is Big Bad Bubba. Didn’t you read the script?” Tyler makes it up off the top of his head. John thinks back to the script and can’t recall a Big Bad Bubba. Tyler is on the verge of laughing as John contemplates this thought so intensely but he holds back. “You are Big Bad Bubba huh? I guess I didn’t get the revised copy. The only one that Mick gave me has me going against some Hulk Hogan look-alike,” states John as he continues to think. Tyler just shrugs, pretending that it is not a big deal. “You better get a revise one, the scenes sound pretty intense and you are going to have to be ready. Are you in shape?” Tyler asks with a giggle. He smacks John on the back as hard as he can. John winces in pain but then chuckles thinking that Heath is just toying with him. Tyler smiles and smacks him again on the back. John quickly back tracks with a look of pain on his face. “Heath, what the hell man? That stings!” John yelps as he attempts to rub the top of his back to relieve the pain. He can’t reach the area so he puts his hands on his knees to give his back some rest. “Heath, stop it. I’m not Heath Ledger you tool, it’s Tyler. Now that I know this prank works, its time to bring it to the target,” Tyler says with a smile and a wink. John perks up, not wanting to let Tyler know that he injured him. John instantly has a million questions in his head about Tyler’s intended purpose. “Hold on a second Tyler. What are you possibly going to do walking around looking exactly like a famous actor? You have to fill me in,” John demands, wanting to receive a good laugh at some one else’s expense after he was tricked quite easily. Tyler smiles, ready to explain the entire thing to John. John listens closely, analyzing the plan to make sure there are not chances of it falling apart. “Well John, we both know that Mick has been kind of a burden on us lately. I mean, I know he is your agent and all, but he’s really been busting our chops,” Tyler says. John steps back, not wanting this plan to involve his own agent Mick. He doesn’t want to anger the man that has all the connections to cause his career to soar. Tyler frowns, knowing that Mick is a corrupt man and needs a dose of his own medicine. “Just wait a second Tyler. You can’t do anything to Mick Varelli. He could have me fired if he were to get angry enough. It’s not worth the risk,” John warns, disconnecting himself from being any part of Tyler’s latest scheme. Tyler just beams, knowing that his mind is already made up to go through with it. “John, get off of it. You know that Mick doesn’t have as much power as he makes people think he does. Come on, this will be a funny one. You thought I was Heath Ledger, and I’m sure he will to. He’ll flip,” Tyler entices. John shakes his head in disapproval despite all the coaxing by his counterpart. Tyler shoots a pleading look in his direction, but John is like a stone. “No Tyler. I don’t walk up in to the ‘Laugh Factory’ and try to mess up your career,” states John, trying to make the issue personal so that Tyler will turn back from it. Tyler just laughs, recalling an instant that John indeed did. This idea spreads to John’s head, and he blushes, knowing that he contradicted himself. “Is that right John? I faintly remember a time where you came in and threatened to show what you called ‘intimate’ pictures of Kalli and me. I would call that career threatening,” Tyler says smugly as John avoids eye contact with him. “Ok Tyler, whatever. I just don’t think it is a good idea for you to do this to Mick. He has a short fuse, and anything could trigger it off. Just leave him alone to the shooting of this movie is over. It will be in both of our best interests. “It’s a good thing that I didn’t come here for your approval. Just make sure the cameras are rolling so I can show this one to Gaz,” Tyler says in a cocky tone of voice as he walks off to search for the man himself. John jogs up in front of him and stops him in his tracks. Tyler just smiles, and avoids pushing through him. He figures that could start something hostile. “This won’t prove anything to Gaz and Nerezza. They couldn’t care any less what kind of pranks that we do,” John states. He doesn’t want the influence of Gaz and Nerezza to cloud his judgment and do something that he otherwise wouldn’t do. “You are wrong my friend. This is the week that we have to pull out all the stops. If we are in high enough honors to be in the Prank Pot Luck at Supremacy, then we have to show the PWA that we are indeed the right people for the job,” Tyler says with a smile, allowing his mind to wander to greater things like holding the championship titles in the PCW. He quickly refocuses back to reality and the task at hand. “But Tyler, we’re wrestling in a ring. It’s not a prank contest. Don’t be stupid about this,” John says, hoping that his insults will have more of an affect that his previous arguments. “Hey, if being stupid involves pulling the greatest pranks and winning the Prank Pot Luck, then I guess I am the stupidest man in the room,” Tyler says with a smile as he nudges John out of the way. John finally concedes and moves out of the way. Tyler walks around the set, nodding at various people who peer at him with excited eye, thinking that he is truly the super star. Tyler even waves at a young woman who can’t take her eyes off of him. John watches Tyler go off on his journey to find Mick. He puts his hand over his face and shakes his head in disapproval. Tyler feels emotions quite the opposite. He is excited to execute a prank that will make Mick Varelli red in the face after he finds out that he has just been had. John’s stomach turns as his acting fate rests in the hands of a man that is dressed like an existing actor.
