Eddie Mandrake's Psycho.

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 27, 2002 The following promo is rated TV-MA [Highways. They interconnect a continent. They also have a dangerous side. Especially, when the weather is involved. We look down the road, and we see Eddie Mandrake’s WRX driving just outside of a major metropolitan center. In the car, we have an Alpine Stereo system, in the CD player right now, is a custom burned CD. The previous song just finished and now Metallica’s cover of Bob Seger’s “Turn the Page” begins to play. Eddie’s face remains emotionless as he continues to drive. On a long and lonesome highway east of OmahaYou can listen to the engine moanin' out his one note song… Rain droplets begin to pounce onto the windshield. Eddie’s face turns from no emotion, to pure revulsion in a matter of six seconds. Eddie quickly hits the fast forward button. Nothing happens. He punches it, and it switches to the next song. “Here to Stay” by Korn. Eddie, head bangs while listening to the extremely hard and industrial beats of the song. Then the lyrics kick in… This time taking it away I’ve got a problem With me getting in the way My final sign So I take my face and bash it into a mirrorI won't have to see the pain bleed bleed This state is elevating As the hurt turns into hatingAnticipating all the fucked up feelings again The hurt inside is fading This shits gone way to far All this time I’ve been waiting Now I cannot grieve anymoreFor what’s in side awaking My god I’m not a whoreYou’ve taken everything And now I cannot give anymore… Suddenly, a loud horn is heard…Eddie inadvertently drove into the oncoming lane, and there is a Mack Truck heading toward him. Eddie swerves out of the way back into his lane. Eddie’s eyes show how close he came to meeting the biggest bounty hunter of them all. However, the song is still going, but the rain is coming down in a torrent now. Wet out conditions. Eddie looks down the road and sees a neon sign that’s very well lighted. A beacon in the torrent. The sign says…”Motel.” Eddie pulls into the property, and parks just outside the office. Eddie turns the car off and runs inside to try to avoid the rain. He gets a little wet, but not too much. He is now in the office. In there is a cash register, a desk, a sign-in book, a cork board with twelve keys on it, numerous stuffed birds, and a very drab paint job. Eddie hits the bell, and almost immediately a middle aged stands up from behind the desk. He’s dressed in a burlap coloured cardigan, a white t-shirt, and a pair of grey dress pants.] Man: YES?! [Eddie jumps back slightly not expecting the attendant to jump up like that.] Eddie: Shit! Do you always hide in there? Man: Not all the time, I actually live in the house on the hill with my mother. Eddie: How old are you? Man: About fifty-one. Eddie: Uh, right…Anyway, can I stay here while the storm dissipates? Man: Well, might be a while, I hear that the storm is going to last the night at least. Eddie: You mean the rain is going to last at the same rate for the whole night? Man: Yes actually. I am actually a part-time meteorologist, I know when a bad storm is going to hit. Eddie: I see. Well, can you give me a room? Man: Take your pick. [He points to the array of keys on the pegboard behind him.] Eddie: Hmm…Give me…M6. Man: Well…if you get M1, you’ll be right beside the office…if you need anything, just knock on the wall. Eddie: That’s okay, I’ll take 6. Man: I insist you take M1… Eddie: Dude, I’ve seen a movie where I guy masturbated to a woman who took the room next to the office. Now, if you intend to masturbate in front of me, then you are worse than that faggot Sebastian Toomes. Man: I don’t do that! Eddie: Sure you don’t… [Eddie looks at the name tag that he happens to wear almost all the time.] Eddie: Norman. Now get my keys you sick fuck. [The man grabs the keys for M6 off the board, and hands them to Eddie. Eddie signs the sign-in book, and leaves without saying a word. The man turns and heads back to his house. Eddie heads to his room; he unlocks the door and enters. The décor is pretty much the same as the office, except there is a few paintings on the wall of naked women instead of stuffed birds. Eddie looks about the room, and heads to the bed.] Eddie: That guy is just fucking creepy. [Eddie grabs the remote and turns on the television. It’s on a local news broadcast.] TV: The string of weekly murders at the Bates Motel has continued since July, all of them unsolved. The owner of the Motel, Norman Bates is the prime suspect. No evidence has been found to connect Norman Bates to the murders. A local resident has stated that they wish that Norman Bates gets his in the end, because they all know about it. [Eddie hears some screaming from the hill. He gets out of the bed and heads to the window…most of it is barely audible.] Norman: Mother, the guest said something nasty to me today. Mother: What?! Norman: Don’t worry; I’ll take care of… Mother: NO! I’ll take care of it. Norman: Mother, no. Mother: Are you talking back to me boy? Norman: …No, not at all mother. Mother: That’s what I thought. I’m gonna teach this young man some manners! Eddie: Hmm…I smell set-up. [Eddie picks up a nine millimetre Beretta and hides it from sight. He just heads into the washroom, and closes the door. He cranks the water to a temperature where the steam won’t fill the room. He undresses and enters the shower. He grabs the complimentary soap in the dish and begins to lather up. During this time, the door silently opens, and in comes someone wielding a knife! Eddie has absolutely no clue what is about to happen to him. Eddie begins to hum to himself “Gets me through” by Ozzy Osbourne. The soon-to-be-killer is approaching, Eddie is still lathering up. Cue the Screeching Violins The curtain flies open, showing the visage of Norman Bates’ Mother, however the barrel of a nine millimetre Beretta gets pulled right in front of figure. Record Scratch Eddie turns to the figure now.] Eddie: Hello, Mother. [Eddie pulls the trigger, and the bullet goes right through the eye of “Mother.” Mother drops to the tile floor and blood begin to slowly stream from the head of “Mother.” Eddie steps out of the shower and looks upon the lifeless corpse. He sees that “Mother” is nothing but Norman Bates himself.] Eddie: Well…I knew he was a sick freak. I did the entire world a favour by getting rid of this freak. I might as well leave the cops something to remember me by… Morning [We tune into the local station that Eddie was watching.] TV: Yet another murder at the Bates motel, the most gruesome to date, and it was the motel’s owner, Norman Bates Himself! The police stated that the walls were painted with Bates own blood, his internal organs strewn around the premises, and his bones were left as a calling card to find out where the actual act took place. One example of how brutal the crime was, the victim’s intestines were stapled to the top edge of the wall leading to the ceiling, basically making it act like crown moulding. The Bates motel murders are now even more unsolved than ever…. [Fade to black…The End.]
