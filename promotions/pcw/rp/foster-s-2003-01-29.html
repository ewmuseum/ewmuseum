Foster's.....

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/29/2003 *The scene opens up in a Melbourne, Austrailia, pub (as they call them in any place that the people have an accent). This place looks rough. It is one of those drinkeries that you wouldn't step into unless you knew how to fight. There is a few men playing pool and four men sitting at the bar, all with huge mugs full of beer. The place is damp and dark until the front door opens and in walks the legendary Jon Kano. The Aussie men stare at Kano for a few moments as he walks in. Kano is dressed in a 'KANO ROCKS~!' t-shirt, baggy blue jeans, and Doc Martin boots. Kano pulls up a stool to the bar and has a seat.* Barkeep (in wacky Austrailian accent): Whaddya have, mate? Kano: What do you got? Barkeep: Beer. Kano: What else? Barkeep: Beer. Kano: Alright, I'll take a beer then. *The bartender reaches underneath his bar and pulls out a large can of FOSTER'S (Yes, it is Austrailian for beer, just ask Argyle). He sets it in front of Kano, who cracks it open. Kano takes a drink, then lights one of his trademark Marlboro Menthol Light cigarettes.* Barkeep: Menthol? I only know two types of people who smoke menthols. Women and queers. Kano: You would know wouldn't you? Barkeep: What the hell is that supposed to mean? Kano: Nothing. I am a happily married man. Man sitting to Kano's left: I always thought that was a contradiction in terms, mate. *Everyone in the bar, including Kano, share a laugh.* Man sitting to Kano's left: I can tell by your voice, that you be from America. Lovely land up there. Where abouts are you from, mate. Kano (exhaling smoke as he speaks): Sin City itself, Las Vegas, Nevada. Man sitting to Kano's left: No shit? I was there back in '97. Lost my ass at the Mirage. Kano: That place will definitely do that. Barkeep: So what you doing in my quaint little corner of the land from down under? Kano: I am a professional wrestler. I am in town to do a show. Barkeep: Is that a fact? I have never met a professional wrestler that I couldn't whoop. Kano: You are staring at one. *The barkeep reaches out for Kano, who only shoves back from the bar. Kano grabs the barkeep's head and slams down onto his own bar. Another man comes out of nowhere and wraps his arms around Kano, Kano breaks the hold and decks the man. Kano grabs a pool cue, laying on the floor, and takes on all comers. One man charges, catches it in the gut, goes down. Another, another, another, and with only one man left besides Kano, the man runs straight out the door. Kano drops the pool cue and grabs his beer. Kano slams the rest of his beer and begins to walk out of the pub.* Kano: Charge the drinks and damage to Xavier Kannon. Lets see, I took out five men by myself. Damn, Akiyama better be as good as he was tonight or I might actually take out the Neo Void God himself. I guess I really am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano walks out of the pub with a smug little smile on his face.*
