It's a Match

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 16,2003 Brianna Washington lay on her new bed for the next month, looking up at the ceiling of her room, of flawless white coloring. The camera backs away and we see clutters throughout the room, from the corners filled with books and unknown gadgets, to the desk against the wall filled to the brim with paper and other miscellaneous trash. It’s obvious that she packed more than she had room for, but the look on her makeup painted face shows content with it all. It’s been a little over a week since the ship took off, and Brianna is still having fun, not having had the luxury or time to visit all of the events located on the airship thus far. “It won’t be that bad….it can’t be.” Brianna says to herself still staring relentlessly up at the ceiling, going through her common thought process. “But I can’t stand the man! How does PCW expect us to team together? It’s just asking for trouble! They should just cancel the match because there is no way in hell it’s going to work out. I’m going to wind up knocking Dorn out cold due to his smart mouth and it will be all she wrote.” saying this, a smile etches across her face and a small chuckle comes out from her pursed lips as the thought of knocking Dorn out really hits her. “If he does manage to pull something smart on me, which is highly unlikely, I’ll just get him back on Turmoil after my match with El Diablo. Retaliation is such a sweet thing against such an unkind immoral person like Dorn Tracer.” With that she jumps to her feet in a quick motion, having to extricate the part of her black shorts, which crawled up her rear end as she was sitting down (more affectionately known as a wedgy). In a slow, methodical motion, she rubs her hands together and looks around, trying to think of something to do as boredom has set in. “There’s always the casino….no it’s probably packed at this time of day. Maybe the pool?” She bites the tip of her finger in complete thought, before rushing over to one of her suitcases, which has yet to be unpacked. She rummages through it and pulls out a sleek, formfitting black one-piece suit. “So I did remember to bring it. Off to the pool I go I suppose.” Brianna throws the bathing suit onto her bed and reaches down to pull off her shirt. As she does this, the scene fades to black momentarily. It comes back a few seconds later showing Brianna now sporting the bathing suit. She looks around and picks up a pair of black sunglasses, which she throws on, now finishing her outfit. “An hour or two of hanging by the pool and then I come back here. Oh what fun.” She says with a tone of sarcasm in her voice. With that she opens up the door of her room and steps out, making sure to lock it so there aren’t any unwanted visitors while she’s gone. She walks down the quiet hall, having no problem going through as no one is around. The scene suddenly switches to the pool area where people are happily frolicking in the water enjoying the coolness of it. Suddenly Brianna comes into view and she has a big dark pink beach towel draped across her shoulder. She spots a clear space on the other end of the room and quickly rushes over before someone else stakes it. She sets her beach towel down and takes a seat, instantly enjoying the heat of the sun beating down from the plexiglass windows. Brianna surveys the entire deck spotting J-Lo and Ben Affleck arguing (what a surprise), a small fight in the far corner which is broken up, and a semi empty pool as the majority of people are just walking around. “Hey beautiful.” Brianna looks up and instantly looks away as a bare chested man with pasty skin stares down at her. “So what brings you here to the pool?” Brianna just ignores him hoping that he will get the hint and go away, but he doesn’t, plopping down beside her. “You’re a quiet one huh? I just wanted to say how much I enjoy watching you wrestle. How would you like to put a few of your moves on me in my room later tonight?” he says nonchalantly trying to keep cool. “Just what I wanted to hear.” Brianna says, surprisingly. This shocks the man to no end as his pick up line worked. A huge grin comes across his face as his confidence level grows. “Well come to room 310 aorund 7:00pm.” With that he gets up and as he steps in front of Brianna. She kicks his leg out from under him and he dives head first into the pool. After a few seconds he pulls himself out coughing up water, not expecting that to happen. He then walks away slowly, but manages to maintain a smile. ”Your feisty Ms. Washington!” “I’ll feisty you you sleazebag.” Brianna whispers to herself. The hours pass by and it is now around 6:55. The camera is in the man’s room where he is busy pounding on the cologne and greasing up his hair for the arrival of Brianna. He’s startled by a knock at the door and quickly sticks a piece of gum in his mouth. “Hello…” The man chokes on his gum as Matt Damon is standing there. Matt looks him up and down and smile crosses his face. “What…what are you doing here?” the man asks. “This woman told me that you wanted me to come to your room so here I am.” “Uh ok…” “Don’t worry I’m not gay, just bored. Ben’s out with that good for nothing skank and I wanted someone to play Dungeons and Dragons.” “DUNGEONS AND DRAGONS! Come in come in!” With that the scene fades to black.
