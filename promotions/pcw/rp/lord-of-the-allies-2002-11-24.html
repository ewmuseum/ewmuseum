Lord of the Allies

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 24, 2002 The Punchy Pineapple Casino is now teeming with people, laughing, drinking, gambling, and otherwise being merry. The lights are dim and mellow, make the room glow with a melancholy air of mustiness that belies its young age, as if it has already stood for eternity, and will for another to come. Fruity tasting pineapples change hands with the ease and consistency of STDs in New York, though unlike the Big Apple all of the goods are ending up in one place. Well... Two places. The Tyger and The Devil lord over the proceedings with a steadily increasing sense of power and greed, having amassed relatively more riches than one could in ten lifetimes among society, all at the hands of their own ingenuity and others&#039; lack thereof. Pineapples are packed and stacked everywhere, tumbling from counters and tabletops only to land in another fresh pile being started on the ground. The door opens and closes rhythmically with the steady stream of traffic needing to go and pick more currency to gamble away. There is a glassiness to their eyes, as if they&#039;re no more than china dolls, and the stench of atavism saturates the air. &#039;What a glorious place, this island,&#039; Devil tells Tyger, nodding and emitting a mad cackle. &#039;Overflowing with opportunity, for those willing to go the extra step to get it,&#039; he continues, glancing around the room approvingly, peepers wide and bloodshot with the strain of monitoring them all. With a transparent flash they suddenly harden, having spied a woman trying to cheat the casino of a slice of pineapple, and Devil whistles loudly, alerting their hired muscle. Nerezzula and Grass sweep down upon the offender and sweep her up into their arms, ignoring her violent protest, then run her straight to the back of the casino and through a heavy black door, kicking and screaming the whole way. All business, the two representatives of Mob Rule stalk back towards the room, fists clenched angrily at their sides. Players eye them askance, knowing full well what is going to happen and heartily disapproving, but finding themselves unable to leave or do anything about it. They try to pretend nothing is going on and nonchalantly clear a pathway for the two to pass through. They enter the small room with an inflated air of majesty, glaring down their noses at the man now tied to a wooden table in the centre of the room, the only decoration to speak of. Like some archaic altar it confines the man, ignoring and perhaps even revelling in his struggles, as are P.W.A., looming over him with their arms folded importantly. &#039;Cheating us?&#039; Tyger asks in a scandalised voice, as if accusing the man of raping their very souls. &#039;Please!&#039; The woman screams, her gothic eyes darting about as she struggles wildly against the bindings. &#039;It was only a little bit! Just one slice! We can start over!&#039; She continues writhing madly where she lies, too stupid to realise she&#039;s screwed even if she gets loose. &#039;I&#039;ll do it honestly this time, I swear!&#039; She moans pitifully, then pitches in a last ditch hope of desperation, &#039;I&#039;m a lesbian! I have sex with everyone! You need me! &#039;Bullshit,&#039; Devil snarls, pulling out a large, serrated hunting knife. &#039;Do we fucking look like Argyle? We don&#039;t give a shit if you give hummers more freely than Aimz and swallow every single time.&#039; He advances towards her, brandishing the knife along his fingers with the ease of someone who&#039;s travelled this road many times before. &#039;You raped the cardinal rule, bitch, so now you&#039;re going to pay for it... On our terms.&#039; A sadistic yet justified grin twists Devil&#039;s visage as he runs the blade across the convulsing woman&#039;s neck, leaving a line of spreading red in its wake. Like a chicken hanging by her feet, Callisto Banned shakes and gasps desperately for air that can no longer come. Her big, wet eyes look up at them pleadingly, imploring them to stop this madness while there is still time. &#039;Don&#039;t blame us,&#039; Tyger whispers down to her in her dying moments. &#039;You brought this all down on yourself.&#039; Devil, Nerezzula, and Grass all nod their sombre agreement, then Devil gives a curt nod and the vampire plunges down atop her, eagerly lapping up the blood spilling from her neck as she watches and feels in indescribable horror. The sound of his feeding is loud and obnoxious, and the other three shudder simultaneously at the dreaded slurping, but they persevere just the same. &#039;Damn people wanting more than they deserve,&#039; Tyger mutters, abruptly turning his back on her, but not moving to leave the room. &#039;You know who else is taking more than they deserve, albeit in a different fashion?&#039; Devil turns to face the same way as his partner, cocking an eyebrow as he regards him from the corner of his eye. Tyger reads his mind and grins sagely, then both utter the name at the same time and with like levels of disgust. &#039;Xamine Herr Kanyon.&#039; There is a moment of reflection, then they silently agree to go forth from that room and out of the casino, ignoring all those stealing apprehensive glances as they pass. They don&#039;t concern themselves with the concept of leaving the others unguarded, as even among the savage and uncivilized it is only the absolute bottom of the barrel - the cream of the crap - who would steal from her peers. Nonetheless, the people sense something is happening and rise to join the pair on their journey, abandoning their pineapples on the various tables. The midafternoon sun beats down harshly on them for the first time in days, making them wince and bow their heads to its power. They don&#039;t slow down, however, nor deviate even slightly from their course as they march purposefully across the sandy earth, snaking through the trees like a gentle breeze, a small army of wrestlers at their back. &#039;Who the hell is that?&#039; Devil blurts as they come abreast of a throng of trees, staring appalled and pointing at a lithe blonde man rocking slowly back and forth on the ground, cradling his knees up to his chest and mumbling to himself. Tyger snorts disparagingly. &#039;Ignore him,&#039; he says with a dismissive wave of his hand. &#039;That&#039;s just Kid Blunder... Used to be a major tool in this giant machinery of ours... Now he&#039;s just a plain old tool.&#039; There is a tinge of regret in Tyger&#039;s voice as he says this, but Devil chooses to pay it no mind, just as he does to the incessant rambling about disrespectful youth pouring from Blunder&#039;s mouth. He praises the good old days of yore as if stroking a fond dream... Or a woman he&#039;s trying to bed, exaggerating and praising and flat-out lying to appease their egos. &#039;He and Bologna should definitely get together,&#039; Devil mutters as they continue on into the orchard and through it, stepping over the moaning form of the named man as he tries to recover from consuming too many pineapples. They step onto the beach and spy their target perched atop his rock, surveying the land with a wary eye. At his side is Bellie~!, waving her finger around and yammering on about god knows what. A blonde man sitting on a towel glances up at them and smiles, and both nearly fall over from shock. &#039;Blow?!&#039; Devil asks, wide-eyed, staring down at the apparition and the great mound of white powder beside him. &#039;What the hell are you doing here?&#039; &#039;Well,&#039; Snow begins with a wan smile, &#039;I was just cheering up Byron Calver.&#039; He stretches his arms up above his head and gives a mighty stretch, apparently a little stiff. &#039;Byron Calver?&#039; Devil asks, furrowing his brow. &#039;Don&#039;t you mean &lt;i&gt;The Virgin&lt;/i&gt; Byron Calver?&#039; Tyger blurts, mistaking himself for some Dragnet guys for a moment, just as the whole crowd from the casino finally catches up. Snow glances up at them knowingly and winks. &#039;No bitch can resist Blow,&#039; he informs them with a serene nodding of his head, and they tear off across the sand, horrified, pitched down into the darkest depths of their minds in an attempt to forget what they were told. Fortunately, Herr Kanyon interrupts that vain thought, calling down to them from his rock like a king from his throne. &#039;Ah, look at all of you, coming crawling back now!&#039; Kanyon revels, extending his arms out to his sides to encompass the entire crowd. &#039;Yeah,&#039; Bellie~! pipes up arrogantly at his side. &#039;You&#039;re all like, &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; our bitches!&#039; Her voice grates down to the core of every man, ruthlessly destroying whatever bridge to sanity had remained within them. &#039;We welcome you back, of course,&#039; Kanyon continues, ignoring his self-named fuck ferret, and grinning satisfactorily from ear to ear. &#039;Just so long as you pay tribute to Order... All of you get to work gathering pineapples right away!&#039; He orders, despite the gigantic mound of pineapples still resting behind him, enough to sustain he and his bitches (Calver and Bellie~!) for at least a year. Tyger responds with grand laughter as Devil puts it into words, &#039;we&#039;re not here to pay homage to you, you arrogant son of a bitch!&#039; He shouts, feeding off the growing look of rage on Herr Kanyon&#039;s face. &#039;We&#039;re here to take all those ill-begotten pineapples from you... Just to satisfy our own greed.&#039; He grins madly, and surprisingly, Herr Kanyon does too. He is about to respond when an altogether maniacal &#039;DYAHAHAHAHA!&#039; explodes over the beach and the incomparable Boris Stained&#039;da&#039;Slav thunders out of the forest, painted from head to toe in berry juice yet looking somehow less savage than everybody else. He strides between the two factions, regarding either side with an enveloping and unequivocal sympathy that words could never begin to do justice. In their midst he stops and spins around, eyes pleading desperately for whatever piece of humanity still burns within them. &#039;Don&#039;t you see?&#039; He booms, waving his arms at the island in general. &#039;This means nothing! Why are we bickering over pineapples? Ready to kill over pineapples! Are we not the same?&#039; He demands wearily, slumping down to his knees so he may regard the heavens more readily. Throwing his head back in agonised rage he calls upon god to spare and save them all. &#039;Dah, we are the same,&#039; he continues with finality, lowering his head and staring meaningfully and individually at everyone gathered as if at his own children. &#039;Now we must stop all this fighting and bickering, and enjoy the life that we can only achieve as a harmonious community...&#039; He trails off, pleased with the recognition now flaming in many of their eyes. They begin moving in towards him, gathering like schoolchildren around their favourite teacher... Or the icecream man. &#039;Dah, come to me,&#039; Boris whispers, tearing up at the realisation of his dream. His satisfaction is short-lived, however, as his words fall on deafened and desensitised ears. Enraged to a frenzy, the mob swarms in overtop him, angry and unquestioning as only a mob may be. The rest of the beach is bare of life as they all pour forward, kicking and punching for all they&#039;re worth, mercilessly slaughtering the last sane man among them, doing together what none of them could have done alone. Boris&#039; hurt outweighs his pain as he feels life meaninglessly slipping away at the hands of fear and greed. A last, humourless laugh floats up and over the din of the murder just as the sun takes its place beyond the horizon, plunging their bodies into the darkness their minds know all too well. The laugh seems to condemn them all further with it&#039;s hollow irony. &#039;DYAHAHAHAHA!&#039; it proclaims, then the mighty OSW Champion leaves this world, though his body remains on the beach, being senselessly beaten beyond death by the crazed crowd of wrestlers he failed to save.
