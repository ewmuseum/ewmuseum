Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {2}

Date: December/March [Tuesday, December 11, 2002] “We are gathered here to pay tribute to the passing of Yoshihiko and Yukina Akiyama. World famous martial artists, respected members of the country, and noble parents.” Floating white specks in the air…icy flakes dancing through the sky slowly…gracefully. They seemed to know that sorrow was in the air. The gray sky made things more solemn. Of course solemn was the color this whole affair was painted. Tragedy…pain… Family and friends were gathered, decked in their garbs of mourning. Family, there was cousin Ayu, her parents, Anarchy, Cyclonica. Friends included some people we haven’t seen in a while. Our eyes glanced around as the priest spoke honorable words of our parents. Over on the side was Botan Otonachi, dressed in a black suit with an open black silk blouse and her lavender hair pulled up in a ponytail. Black shades held back her eyes, but the look on her face told us that the bounty hunter was sad for us. Behind her was the rest of the gang; the twins Xemaphiro and Chemaphiro, Iori, Passine, Diametro, and Enzyme. The brunette Xema was in a black skirt with a white blouse with short sleeves at the shoulders and black heels. Her blonde twin Chema wore an alternative black blouse and white skirt of the same fashion with white heels. Iori was in a black suit with an opened collar white shirt underneath, the cuffs and collar of the shirt peeking out from underneath the black jacket. He wore black shades as well, even though the sun wasn’t out on this snowy day. Passine was by his side, in a dark red tight dress with red heeled boots. The pink haired girl was fighting back tears in her eyes as she leaned her head against Iori’s shoulder. Diametro was in a simple black suit with white shirt and black tie. That was his style, definitely. The beast Enzyme, surprisingly, was in a black sweater and black slacks. We never knew he was capable of wearing anything outside of white camouflage. These people used to be our friends…back when we shunned being the nVg. We were all friends back when we had our own low time Yakuza clan. For a short time we were a family…but for some reason we threw that all away, to become what we are today. Last time we saw Botan, Yuu was with us, the woman that was revealed to be nothing more than our imaginary friend. Botan and we had a fight…and we assumingly left her for dead in a motel room. It normally pisses us off when we find people alive when they were supposed to be dead…but seeing Botan here with the gang is comforting in this occasion. “It was indeed a tragic event for us all to hear that they were murdered in cold blood in their own home after patching things up finally with their only son after almost a year of bad blood. We thank the Lord that they had to past away with affairs with their son worked out.” Ayumi Hamasaki…cousin of the god… She was in a black knee length skirt with a black sheer blouse and a black tank underneath, black calf high boots. Her light brown curls were down her back, and her eyes were soaked with tears. Mascara ran down her cheeks as she bawled, but no one held it against her. Ayu was always emotional, and giving this was her aunt and uncle that were dead, she was bound to overflow. She was always like a little sister to us, we always looked out for her in good and in bad. If there were anyone that we genuinely trusted, it was her. Seeing her like this…there was nothing in the ring that could be done to us that could equal the pain of seeing her cry. “Yoshihiko Akiyama’s only living relative, Daigo Akiyama, has been missing for years. Yukina Hamasaki is survived by her brother, Shoichi, his wife Haruko, and her niece Ayumi. Yoshihiko and Yukina Akiyama are survived by their only son, Akira Akiyama, and their two granddaughters, Anarchy and Cyclonica.” Yes…granddaughters… Anarchy was taking things well. There was a stagnant look on her face as she looked on at the twin caskets. Dressed in a black blouse, tight black slacks, and a long black jacket down to her knees. Her ankle was still taped, and she was leaning against Jenna, her human crutch. Jenna was in a slinky black dress with a thick jacket over to keep warm. Jenna looked affected by the deaths, but Ana didn’t. We could understand…Ana had just met them, and she didn’t have time to attach emotionally to them. Cyclonica was another story. Matrix and Glacia were by her side, Matrix in an all black suit, his eyes cast down to the coffins in respect. Glacia wore black leather pants and a blue blouse under her black leather trench coat. Dark blue shades hid her eyes from reading, but the pursing of her blue lips showed that she felt some emotion about the whole event. Cyclonica’s mother didn’t show much care about anyone or anything…we were surprised to see her show up at all. Cyclonica stood between her mother and Matrix, black silver stiletto heels, black stockings, a tight black miniskirt, and a tight black butterfly blouse with folded cuffs. Her blouse cuffs poked out from her waist length black fur coat, the feathery collar blowing around her neck in the wind. She had pulled her hair back in a ponytail, a ice blue stream in the breeze. She stared down at the graves with dead eyes… Cyclonica didn’t get along with our parents when she met them. She was rebellious, independent, her own being. With her grandparents appearing on scene mandating how things should be done, that really disagreed with her nature. We wondered what she felt right now… Did she feel sorry for not having better relationships with her grandparents? Did she feel remorse for her actions against us now…sinning against the family… “Let us all have a moment of silence for the passing, to honor, respect, and remember them as they were.” Everyone’s head bowed…even ours. We all looked at the snow and thought of the better times with the deceased. Sonata Kojima by our side clutched tighter onto my arm, her hands gripping through the fabric of the black trench coat we wore over our black suit. Her black skirt blew a bit in the breeze, but she closed her coat tighter around herself to keep from flapping. Everything was done…they were gone… What good is being a god when you can destroy lives…but not save any? -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Sonata bowed to the last of the guests, thanking them for paying their respects. Finally, the priest came up to us, shook our hand politely before Sonata’s. “My condolences go out to you, my son.” He said, looking up into our eyes. “Losing one parent is hard, but both at the same time is horrendous. I’m just glad you have a lovely and loyal woman by your side that’ll help you through all this.” Sonata looked up at us for a second before smiling politely at the priest. As he left, Sonata and we watched the cars drive away, most of the people either going back to the castle for the reception, or back to their homes. Then, a limousine pulled up to the curb. We watched as Alexander James Maxis, Ryoko Godai, and a young woman climb out of the back, the door held open by their assistant, Mano. The larger bald Utsure stepped out of the passenger’s seat and walked up the path, leading the charge as behind him the Maxis family approached. All of them were garbed in black suits, masculine or feminine appropriate. AJ Maxis held out his hand towards us. “Akira…on behalf of the Maxis family as well as everyone at Maxis Inc, we give our most sincere apologies.” We took his hand and shook it before he reeled us in for an embrace. Godai did the same before ushering in the young lady at her side. “Akiyama, I’m sure you remember our daughter, Hana.” Last time we saw the girl she was about nine…maybe older. She looked up at us, her eyes taking time to recognize our face from so long ago. We’ve probably change…a little more sterner, our eyes no longer almond, but a glazed silver…maybe larger than she last remembered. Hana stared at us for a second, her brown eyes meeting our own silver. Then she leaned in and wrapped her arms around us as much as she could, only spanning to the edge of our back. We knew nothing else to do but hug her back…it was strange…all these years of destroying people…all this time of suffering and pain, and we lose touch with how to respond to emotions and affection. We were only used to being touched in the action of being hurt. It was awkward to feel human warmth against our frame without having to brace for assault, guard against attack…expect pain to surface. She leaned back, and gave a small, polite smile, but it was a friendly smile that was sincere. “I’ve missed you, Aki. I’m so sorry.” Our mouth opened to respond…but there was nothing we could really say to her. We just nodded slowly and ruffled her on top of her light brown haired head, like we used to do when she was younger. Her eyes then shifted from us to something behind us. All eyes turned towards the graves…as well as ours. Standing there was Inphino Blitz and Mina de Margo. His…woman…was in black fishnets, a black skirt, black heeled boots, and a black frilly blouse and feathery coat. He, was in his same attire…black bondage pants, shirt, and trench coat. They both stared down solemnly at the caskets, in the girl’s hands a bouquet of chrysanthemums. Sonata knew what we were going to do…she stepped out in front of us before we got our first step ahead. She glanced back before approaching up to Blitz. They turned towards us and stepped ahead, Mina in front of Blitz as Sonata was with us. The two women our ambassadors…Blitz and we rivaling entities for eternity… Mina nodded with a sad look. “We’re here to pay our respects.” Sonata smiled, taking the flowers and hugging with Blitz’s manager. “Thank you…coming from you that’s wonderful.” Mina looked back at Blitz, who made it a point to keep eye contact away from us. He knew that our position was volatile, even more in this situation. “I know what it is like to lose parents…” he began slowly. “I grew up without mines. The pain subsides…in time. But you’re a strong person, and you’ll make it through this trying time.” He extended his hand towards us, more daring than the time he pointed a Flintlock point blank in our face and threatened to fire. It was probably the most daring thing he’s done against us since his badass days with the Void Elements…when there were no good side and bad side…just a bad side, and we and him stood on different sides of the field. We glared down at his open hand…and slowly took it. The tension was a deep as the snow that afternoon…the Maxis family, Sonata, Mina…reminded us of the tension in combat. The tension held between Kannon and us…though Kannon is by no means a rival. He’s just some mortal that deserves to get his preppy ass kicked in the way he never received in his life. They left…they all left…and all that remained was us…and Sonata. We stood before the grave, staring down at the two caskets in silence. Sonata…we’ve known her since childhood. She’s probably the only solid friend we’ve had in our entire twisted life. Here she stands…by our side…in our darkest hour… “Everything will be fine.” she whispered to us softly, her hand slipping into our own paw. “They are never really gone from us, as they will stay with us forever in our hearts and mind.” She looked up at us before returning her eyes back to the caskets. “They had just come back into your lives…” her voice started to waver, her vocal chords tightening. “They were proud of you, of what you become, despite your persecution from the rest of the world. And after all this…someone takes them away from you…from us all. Your mother, father…your daughters’ grandparents…Ayumi’s aunt and uncle…they were like parents to me, as well during the time I knew them. They treated me like their own daughter…” She sniffled…the tears were imminent. Crying…tears were only pleasant when someone deserved to suffer…but Sonata…she never deserved it. “………Rrrrrr………Rueeeeturiiibyuuuushion……” Her moist eyes stared at us in shock. “What…did you say?” “…Wee…willl….havvvve….ouurrr…..ruueeeturiibyuushion.” “Retribution?” either she was surprised at our words, or she was surprised at our speech altogether. “Retribution…we will find the murderer…and there will be no hell for them.” Definitely the combination of sudden speech and dialogue that shocked her. “The authorities will find out who did this. Leave it in their hands.” That could not be done. Trust not in the untrustworthy…they who cannot back up their own abilities… when you wanted results, you skipped the mortal middleman and go straight to the god. We knew the remedy for all situations. “Zettai…Unmei…Mokushiroku.” Those three words echoed through our mind, a new creed, a fresh motto in a changing pinpoint in our existence. “That, is the answer to everything…Zettai Unmei Mokushiroku…The Absolute…Destiny…Apocalypse.” We walked away from the graves. Standing there anymore would have set us off right there. Sonata watched us leave, we could feel her eyes on our back as we passed the black car and began to walk down the path leading back to the road. Glancing back, we saw her lie down the flowers from Blitz. She pressed her palms together and paid her respects with a small prayer. When she turned around to leave, she found us waiting for her. She smiled when she saw we waited. “Home?” We nodded. People were probably waiting for us back there…best if we showed up. “Hai.” She approached and took our hand again, leading us off towards the black 4 door sedan. A mortal cost us the trust of a daughter…and now our parents were lost due to another. Because we are with tragedy…that doesn’t make us a good person. We still commit the sins we’ve committed before. But now, things are different. This time…we have justification for the pain we cause. We’re not sadistic… …we’re just vindictive…
