The Building of an Empire: Ball Room Desire

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/03 The Building of an Empire: Ball Room Desire A sweet soft melody plays silently through the thin air as Romantico takes a step inside the door of a beautiful ballroom. The second he takes that one step it’s as if he’s taking in the air of a certain event never experienced in his hard lived storybook of a life. Years of living in the streets in dirty and wet mattresses or stacks of cardboard. Now everything had changed. He looked at everyone else as if they were in the same social level as him. He tightens the silk navy tie he has draping down his white ruffled tuxedo with a soft golden cufflink. After experiencing the chaotic nature of the club scene Romantico brings his presence to the majestic and enchanting life of the rich and prestigious. Romantico slowly walks toward the middle of the ballroom floor with eyes wandering all around him. He was a new face to the scene, and like anything new he started off as something unexpected. In the club life the money in your pocket gave you power, here the same money gives you power, but the way you obtain that money is what they really care about. A brutal wrestler with a non-existent family may have had money, but to them he was just any other scum they bump into every day. Romantico never wanted to be friends with these people, but being IN with these people would be important to him. As if it were the cycle of Darwinism, where only the strong survive. It’s a routine you catch onto easily. The first day you meet a well dressed man with an educated laugh and businessman smile. You shake their hands, compliment their attire, and then ask their wife for an innocent dance. You talk to them about what they love most, money. They look at you as if you are one of them, and then you know that you’re in. Next, you meet with them on weekends and play golf. You get yourself a date and have dinner as a couple. You become a good old educated buddy. They trust you and become hasty. They start letting you know things they wouldn’t tell that ordinary stranger. They soon trust everything they have in your hands. They don’t need to know your name. All they care is that you’re one of them. The rest is cake. You go with them to the bank one day after a game of golf or after going to the theatre. You take a look at their credit card number as they take out some money for the next day. You take money out of their account little by little as you urge them to take out more and more every day. You let them lose track of that thing they love so much, and then you’re set. It’s almost too easy. You see money comes in from many places for me. I work so hard and take bruises and headaches to acquire just a percent of what I could by just doing things I like to do. An empire cannot be built in a day. But I’ll try and do it in a week. *The Approach* I walked around a little and blended into the scene. I went back and forth winking at old couple. I knew the old couples were the ones with the most money. You see the more years on the belt, the more money in the bank account. That’s when I saw him… The scene zooms into a wealthy looking Spaniard with jet-black hair and one streak of gray across the middle. Name: Manuel San DominguezLocation: Barcelona, EspaniaOccupation: Stock broker and Financial Entrepreneur. Investments: The owner of Real Betis farm club.Bank Account: 4,610,414,821 Euros The first few seconds I began approaching his I could feel the money rolling through my fingers. A smile stretched across my face as I took a seat next to him and his family. The plan was that I would just wait until he said a certain thing I had a basic knowledge in, and go on from there. I believe it’s a risk I just might take part in handling. Why even take a risk darling, let’s go to an exotic land or a cruise and just relax. We relax all year dear, we need to start thinking about our future and what we plan on leaving for the children and grandchildren. Don’t we have enough? The Spanish premiera league is looking toward more foreign talent to compete, and the league is not giving us more money to handle with. It’s going to have to come from our own pockets. You and that futbol club, when will you just give it up. It always asks too much from you. There was about a five second span of silence, and I knew it was now or never for me. There’s a flock of young talent roaming around the smaller cities of Portugal, you know? Excuse me? To the best of my recollection, Portugal is producing one of the finest young athletic leagues in Europe. If you don’t mind me asking how do you know about this? You see my brother was Juan Borro. Really? What a fine young star. It’s a shame how things turned out. Well the past is behind us, all we can do is recollect and pray. Indeed. The conversation went on for three hours and every second the numbers churned through my head quicker and quicker. A song then came on, and for this night only I put the cherry on the pie. Sir, would you mind if I asked your beautiful young wife to dance? It would be an honor. As I grabbed her hand and walked her to the center of the brilliantly beautiful ballroom, I felt it in m veins. The empire was about to begin.
