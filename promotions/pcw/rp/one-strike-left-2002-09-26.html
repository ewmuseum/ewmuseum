One Strike Left

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th September 2002 My world is in darkness. I can’t feel nothing, I can’t hear a sound and everything around me is covered in black. I try to open my mouth, to make a sound, any sound….nothing. I try to move, but nothing happens. I try to open my eyes, there is no feeling. What the fuck happen to me?! Just as I about to give up all hope of being in the first life, I feel a soft vibration which is soon followed by a loud crunching sound and I feel wind rush up past my sides and then pain shoot down the top of my head as I hit something. Everything come flooding back to me as I hear a familiar sounding voice talking. I don’t know where I am, I don’t know what I doing, but the only thing I could do is listen as my senses all steadily flow back into my body, Someone going to pay when I figure out where I am!! As my sense continue to come back to me, I relax my body…or rather what I can feel in my body and begin to listen to the man who is speaking with the rough voice and Japanese accent. Fool….. As defined by the Oxford dictionary: One who is deficient in Judgment, Sense, or Understanding. For an example of this word…take a look at Jung Khiem Loc, a well paid role-model and spokesperson for this company, yet one of our biggest bane’s. This man has single handedly turned the PURE situation from bad to worse, now we’re going to have to go to great efforts to secure a victory against PCW. Not only has this man managed to cause just as much damage as Inphino Blitz but he has a mouth to boot on him, anything that is said about PURE, Asia as a whole or himself, he shoots straight back at. He is out of control and if he’s not taken care of, he will cost this company more than what he is being paid. Holding one of PURE’s prestigous Title’s and being a member of TokyoPop he is putting our respective company in danger. Nothing more can happen to hinder our “plans”, He must be taken care of…. TOKYOPOP Administrative C.E.OKazuki G. Thomoki There is a long pause before a short chuckle as the voice continues,“It seems you’ve gotten yourself back into trouble master Jung.” Huh? I try to open my eyes and take a look at the person speaking but nothing happens. My senses are still all messed up as I hear the voice cough and continue. As he continues to talk in his put-on “business” accent I think to myself. TokyoPop the fool…they think I not able to handle myself?! “I’d presume that you’d have something to say about my last comment, that is if you’re not too intoxicated and you’re actually listening to what I’ve been saying….” The voice trails off as I try to shake my head in disgust. Once again the vibrations begin to run through me, soft growing hard, I embrace myself for the loud bump but for some reason it doesn’t come. I was just about to drop my mental guard when I feel something cold on what I think is my neck. Fucking Cold! Du Mah Mai! A shiver run down my spine as I feel the icy touch let go of me and the darkness in front of me slowly develops into a soft gray haze. “Is he awake?” I hear a second, less professional and more White Boy sounding voice come from the left of me. I presume that the man who spoke got a response because I hear him mumbling something in Japanese. I not know how to speak that stupid language so I have no idea what he talking about, I think it about me though because he speaking in his own tongue…If he want to talk shit, why he not say it to my face HUH?! Stupid White Boy. My world continues to materialize from the gray haze as sounds begin to become more clear as the two men continue to converse in Japanese next to me. “How long am I going to have to watch over him?” ”Just after PCW Red October…” ”What happens if he doesn’t make it to Red October?” ”It’s your job to take him there….if he doesn’t make it and carry out the plans then he’s as good as dead and your going to be given a knife for Hare Kari.” The more professional man begins to chuckle as the other gulps in the background. I bet he shitting his pants. Heh, wait till I wake up! Then we going to see who going to be laughing and chuckling! I going to prove I can take care of myself… suddenly everything came back to me. Everything that was materializing suddenly formed in front of my eyes as my surroundings all came into view and my eyes grew big like a White Boys as I tried to take it all in. I found myself sitting in a comfortable leather chair of some form of Limousine. Sitting next to me was the familiar fat business man who I had trouble with back at the private airport in Tokyo and opposite him was the very professional…I never remember that name, he just make me hate Japanese more than I already do! I know I in a limousine because everything begin to make sense, the bump was from me hitting my head on the roof, the vibrations were just of the car rolling over the road and the chatting was from the two executives sitting around the car. They not going to be sitting here for much longer!! I chuckled out loud as I thought to myself…maybe a bit too loud as both men looked at me. “Ahhh Jung, you’re finally here with us! It seems you’ve been having a wild time…with the Hennessey and all! We’ve been monitoring your process…” The man was about to finish before I cut him off, “So you the fucking White Boy who giving me shit from the moon?!” “Excuse me….” “You know what I fucking talking about! If it you I giving you five seconds to stop this car and get the fuck out before I chop you up and send you back to Japan in boxes! You hear me?!” I shot out my last words like bullets leaving a gun. All I received was a chuckle as the businessman reached into his coat and tapped something. Everything seemed familiar as one word sprung into my head. The Matrix…wait that two words! “You going to bug me…huh?!”The businessman continued to tap away inside his pocket as I clenched my hands, grabbing onto the arm rests for dear life as the man next to me began to sweat, red faces and all. “Jung maybe you have been hitting the bottle a little too hard! No need to worry, we’ll get you some help once you’ve done everything as planned!” The man trailed off. “What you fucking talking about?!” The man shook his head and took his hand out of his pocket producing a cigar. “You really have drunk a little too much Jung. I’m talking about what you’re supposed to be doing for PURE and most importantly TokyoPop!” I stopped for a moment and thought to myself as the car began to vibrate and bump. Ohh shit, maybe I did drink too much! Stupid Japanese White Boy, he pretend he going to bug me and now he probably reading my mind… As I trailed off I must’ve left a confused and worried look on my face as the businessman laughed yet again before pulling out a lighter and sparking the cigar before continuing to talk. “It would seem you have placed yourself in a dangerous position. Not only did you manage to piss off Xavier Kannon but you also found a place on the PCW team for Red October. How do you plan on completing the task now?” the man puffed out a huge cloud of smoke. I drew in from where I was sitting, I hadn’t had a ciggie for a few days…or was it hours now, “I sorry, I not know what I do to piss him off but I not going to fuck things up for PURE, it o.k” Suddenly the man seemingly choked on a draw of the cigar, “You not getting paid by PURE, you getting paid by TokyoPop! We not care what PURE do, if any member of TokyoPop are injured in that ring, that what we caring about.” I forgot what I even needed to do! Stupid White Boy, confusing me! I looked across to the man sitting next to me and was just about to ask a question when he answered it for me. “Mr. Kazuki ordered this man sitting next to you to “watch” over you so to speak until your job is done and your payment has come through! His name is Udai” the man grins towards me. I took a look to my left and saw the chubby little man behind his glasses and leaned back a little. Pfft, that White Boy look after me?! If he get in my way I break his glasses…then I break his face! Udai shuffled in his seat and managed to bow before speaking quickly in English, “Hello master Jung, do not worry, I’m a fan of your work and i…” “If he talk again I going to fucking hit him….” The man chuckled once more as he took another draw from his cigar, “Do what you like with him but remember that he’s with you until Red October is finished. If you don’t complete your objective you don’t get paid, the company leaves you and Udai will be forced to commit Hare Kari!” Udai gulps once again, Pathetic White Boy “So you not going to put anything into my stomach?” “What are you talking about?” ”How about show me a dark street and telling me to make a decision?” “It’s not even raining outside….” The man wound down the power window with a churn revealing a clear night sky as the wind blew into the limo. I felt the breeze rush through my hair as I shook my head. Did I just make everything up? Stupid alcohol always mess me up! Everything been fucked up since I get kicked out of Vietnam As if he read my mind the man smiled a shiny smile at me and commented, “No you didn’t dream anything Jung, what happened to your body will happen ten times worse if TokyoPop does not succeed in PCW! If you and Udai fail, you will both be dealt with….” What the fuck?! He think he talk to me like that… I pull myself up out of my seat and hunched over ready to knock the business man in the face when the car suddenly lurched backwards, causing me to fall. I stood up again and began to raise my voice at him, “You know I getting sick of this TokyoPop shit…I in this for the money and me! If I not get my money I going to make sure you all die because i….” “Quiet frankly we’re sick of your shit to Jung! Someone up there likes you and this is your last chance to not disgrace TokyoPop! Now get out, we’re at your apartment!” the man grunted towards me. I not believe he talk to me like that. I wipe that fucking smile off of his face when I finish working for TokyoPop, stupid White Boy Udai opened the door next to him as the breeze rushed in and so did the sounds of China Town. “Good night Jung….it’s been a pleasure dealing with you!” The man commented as I left the limo and slammed the door shut behind me. Think he can talk to me like that…I show him! As I stepped onto the street next to Udai the car began to take off and the cigar was flung out the window landing at my feet. Not being one to take shit from any White Boy I lept up and sent my foot crashing through the right tail light as the driver sped up and disappeared around the corner. “You nothing….” I screamed into the night. I looked up and saw Udai near the entrance to my apartment as I shook my head and took a look at the stores and people around me, it may be night but China Town still alive and well! “I finally home….” -=***=- I not care what anyone think…I only in this for me and the money! If a better offer come up, I not care. I happy to snake out whoever I need to too get places….at least I not getting on my knees or having to pay anyone myself! That right, the Vietnamese Viper only work for one man and that Jung…. People think they can treat me like shit. They think they can walk all over me just because they paying me, when I get up to the top I going to make sure that they all pay! No one going to get away for treating me like this, not TokyoPop, not Kannon, not no one…. Soon enough you all going to see….so very soon!!
