TEC: The End & Handler Responds to RP's and Events

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/02 The End and his handler sit in a computer lab. There are twelve computers facing the front wall with a markerboard hanging on it. Both men are at the back left of the lab, three wooden shelves filled with job-seeking books upon them, with an ID maker behind him. Right by the shelves, near the door, is a Hewlett-Packard Laserjet 400 printer. No one else is in the lab. Handler:“Well,” I write, addressing my character The End who is sitting to my right in this RP. “You’ll be happy about this RP.” The End:“Why would that be?” he asks. Handler:I respond by typing, “Because you get to star in this RP.” My character The End gets excited, but he tries to mask it. The End:“Really?” he says, more of a slightly indifferent statement than a question. Handler:“Yes,” I say, “for you see that I’ve got some free time today, while I’m working in the computer lab, so I figured that before I continue with the RP series focusing on the Misfits, I’d do something with you.” The End:“Excellent.” So, before we continue with the RP, The End and I peruse the recent in-card events of the past few PCW shows, as well as reading some of the RPs that people have posted. The End:“Wow,” he utters simply without much feeling. “I feel like I should be addressing all this stuff that’s happening.” Handler:“Well,” I look to him to explain, “technically, you can’t because most of these RPs are ‘off-camera’, meaning that you – first, don’t know this is going on; second, can’t reply to it.” My character scratches his head and rubs his chin with his face pointed down to the floor. The End:“Well,” he starts, “you already broke that format by showing me these RPs, reading them to me. I might as well respond to them.” I write it into the RP that I slap myself in the forehead, not realizing that I had in a way re-invented a way to respond to off-camera RPs. Handler:“We might as well do it just this once,” I say. “But we shouldn’t do it too much or people might get really mad.” The End:“Very well,” I write it in for him to say to me in a plain voice in the RP. So I go through and pick out some RPs that I find interesting. Armed with a bunch of them, I then read through them more carefully, pick out what I think I could write it in for myself and my character to respond to, and get a drink from the soda machine. When I come back, a commuter student’s kid is messing on my computer. Good thing I saved it before I left. Handler:“So, we’re back. What should we respond to first?” The End:“What about Tyler Nelson’s ‘Shred Everything!’ RP?” Handler:“That sounds good. Yeah, the RP is very good. Gets Tyler’s character over very well. That, and it reinforces the little rumor blurb that’s on the PCW site about Tyler Nelson being investigated by the Department of Justice. Anything you’d like to add in-character?” The End:“Just that when I met Tyler Nelson backstage at a PCW event that I went to while I was still in PWA, I told him not to hang around with those Enron people. Guess he didn’t take my advice.” Handler:“Ah ha. Well, next is a man you’ve teamed with before, and now you’re facing him in the second round of the Global Title Tournament, Drake Maxwell. Drake’s got a good style to his RPs, and he does some very interesting and unique things. Especially in the one we’re responding to now, which is ‘Becoming Your Enemies…’.” The End:“I suppose. Though I’ve been around Drake quite a bit lately, so I can tell you with accuracy and certainty that he’s actually a boring guy outside of the ring. And I think he knows that. But if he has to live more interesting lives vicariously through his opponents, that’s not too… healthy, is it?” Handler:“It’s hard to say. But listen, we’ll have to break this up. I have to go to the bathroom right now, so I’ll be right back. In the meantime, I’ll write in something quick for you to do.” The End, with his handler gone to the bathroom, sits still in the computer lab. Black ninjas crash through the windows and challenge The End to a fight. He obliges. Imagine the fighting yourself. I return from the bathroom, and get back to writing. Handler:“Next up is Miyagawa, and we’re following his series in which he finds out that he’s totally a different person than he thought he was. I suppose it’s a way to reinvent or reintroduce a character that’s already established while reaffirming old relationships and whatnot.” The End:“I don’t know. What I can say for sure is: Miya, (I’ll call you that because I don’t care much for your first name), you have weird friends and a weird family life. And really, follow Steve’s advice; the best way to get out of a car is to OPEN THE FRICKIN’ DOOR!” Handler:“And what do you think about this MIYASTREAK thing that’s been going on in the cards lately?” The End:“Personally, I think it’s laughable that in an attempt to regain some heat he once had, he must merge his gimmick with Meanstreak’s (which might’ve worked WHILE MEANSTREAK WASN’T WRESTLING). There is Meanstreak, the original one, in OSW. I believe he can play himself just fine, so Miya doesn’t’ need to do it.” Handler:“Well put.” The End:“Compliment yourself, you wrote for me to say that in-character.” Handler:“Well put.” The End:“Compliment yourself, you wrote for me to say that in-character.” Handler:“Well put.” The End:“Compliment yourself, you wrote for me to say that in-character.” Handler:“Well put.” The End:“Compliment yourself, you wrote for me to say that in-character.” Handler:“This really isn’t getting us anywhere.” The End:“Agreed.” A guy comes into the computer lab, and just stands by my computer and watches me type. I hate that. I ask if he needs anything, and he says no. So I send him away. People can’t mind their own business. Darn them. DARN PEOPLE TO HECK! Wait, I’ve got an RP to write. Handler:“Okay, the next thing we’ll analyze is Xavier Kannon’s failed bid for another Cruiserweight Championship on Tuesday Turmoil. Kannon’s great, and he’s probably one of the greatest cruiserweights that PCW has, but I honestly think that the Cruiserweight Title is a bit of a lower aim than what he’s really capable of. If not in the World Title division, Kannon could easily be in the Continental Division.” The End:“No, he’ll stay down at the cruiserweight level. He’s too busy trying to shill for Snapple (I forbid the handler to tilde the product) and get that woman Ellie to dress up like it. And, just a few days until the March Madness main event, where the man has a chance to wrestle for a shot at the #1 contendership to the PCW World Title, he’s trying to win back the Cruiserweight Title. Buffoon. He’s aiming too low, and concentrating on the wrong things. That’s why he’s starting to lose consistently. The sooner he gets rid of all these distractions, the better he’ll be in the long run.” I stop typing and stretch my body out. A girl comes in and asks for help using the printer. Not that it’s that hard, or that I can’t verbally give her instructions from my seat, but she’s hot, so I have to get up and go over to her to show her how to do it. Then again, I know the girl, and she’s not that bright, so maybe I’d have to show her anyway. Handler:“I think Jeremy Howard would be a good next choice. He’s up to usual form, quality RPs and the same winning workrate that we’re used to. His RPs are more of trials and tribulations rather than the aloof champion above all concerns type of RPs I saw when he was PCW World Champion.” The End:“Okay, JKH, my handler read me your RP ‘The next time I see you, death might be your best option!’ It starts off with, ‘It doesn't matter what kind of roadblock you put in the way of Jeremy Howard. He will overcome it.’ Well, I have to agree with that; Jeremy Howard triumphs over all. Except, of course, for losing the World Title to Ivan Stanislav. And Snow. And failing repeatedly to get your brother back. But other than that, yes, you are definitely a man that overcomes all difficulties.” Handler:“Ouch, that was a low blow.” The End:“I don’t really like the guy.” Handler:“Point taken.” I stop typing and check my e-mail. I hate it when, as a guy, I keep getting all these advertisements telling me that I can increase my bust size. I DON’T WANT TO! In contrast, my friend Cindy always seems to get mail in which they can tell her that she can increase her penis size. Weird. The End:“Speaking of that… why don’t we cover Snow’s ‘On the Cover of the Rolling Stone’? Snow’s having some issues he needs to deal with. And he’s gotta be a strong man to pass up what regular men would kill for in Tammy Hart. But that’s another story. Why’s he on Rolling Stone? I thought being featured on the cover was just for important people? Rolling Stone must be lowering their standards.” Handler:“Um… to Snow’s standards, he’s got great quality and workrate, and his RPs just keep getting better. Now onto Akiyama and Jay Sylo. They’re working together now on an angle, and their RPs coincide. That goes well to show the coherence of the group.” The End:“Akiyama, there’s ways to go about ‘Enlightening’ someone, but I’m certain that you can ASK to do so, without the whole kidnapping thing. Heck, I even helped you test The Rush to be the God of Crunk, but that’s something he willingly went through. Jay Sylo’s not so angry about things now, but I’m sure he wasn’t too thrilled being taken to some place in a burlap sack. Oh, yeah, Sylo, um… you were already pretty big and strong. So, you got taller, stronger, and faster… YOU WERE A METAHUMAN AS IT WAS! Now you’re just getting a little greedy for power. But it’s your choice whatever you do. Ho, and Aki, you can start marketing those Void Baptisms; they work better than anything GNC has on store shelves at the moment.” Handler:“Hehe.” Wait a minute. I’ve suddenly got the urge to herbal. {Commercial: This RP is brought to you by Herbal Essences. Why? Because. Don’t ask questions.} Handler:“Next up is Legend, Inc. I don’t know who he is, but he’s been in PCW before it looks like. His workrate is great, and his RPs are the same. There’s a poll I saw in which there’s speculation of whether or not he can keep this up, but I’m just focusing on the here and now, and Legend is definitely doing very well in the RP battle. The End:“Right. Legend, Inc. He was retired, but came back for ANOTHER beginning. Now he’s currently living in a dream world. Man, I don’t like people with Chronic Indecision Syndrome. Are you retired or not? Are you in the real world or not? Geez, is ANYONE in their right mind?” Handler:“Awfully harsh. But I guess I’m making you that way. The next guy to analyze is… Juan Borro. He’s a good writer, and his workrate is very good. I’m not sure if he’s as ready for the World Title level as some may think, but he’d be good for the upper Television Title/lower Continental Title division.” The End:“‘Celebrating the Escape…’ Juan’s happy to be out and about, escaping the Chinese prison he was being held in. He does have a tendency to jump whenever he spots a Chinese guy in a uniform, however, and this includes US police officers, professional athletes, even the guys that work down at the Manchu Wok. I think all we have to do to throw Juan off his game is have the entire front row be nothing but Chinese people. He won’t even come out.” Handler:“The new Rising Star Champion Lance Bell is missing it seems. He works well enough in PCW. A good first win, I’d say.” The End:“Lance Bell… missing…. Well, looks like those people I hired did their job.” Handler:“I probably should have read more than this one RP he did. He’s already got rights to the name Juju. Well, fine, I’ll change the name of “Juju the Albino Iguana,” which I came up with independently, to Bobo the Albino Iguana. Just to be fair to Lance. But back to The End’s trash talking.” The End:“With any luck, Lance’s friends will realize that he’s not worth saving. In the meantime, there’s some dead weight knocked off the PPV.” Handler:“I don’t have much of an impression of Mike Storm as a handler. What do you think about him, End?” The End:“Mike Storm… I can’t seem to place the name. Wait a minute, is it the little guy that’s only fighting for a Television Title shot at the PPV? Ohhh… nope, doesn’t ring a bell.” Handler:“Quit playing around, End.” Handler:“Unfortunately, Mike Storm seems to think that anyone cares about a little guy like him. And to think that he actually feels that he deserves to be at the Television Title level… heh. He’s just there because all the good people are either held below the TV title, or ranked higher than that. But, while he’s fighting for the #1 contendership to some garbage title that was held by TIM WALDEN, I’ll be wrestling in the MAIN EVENT for a #1 contender’s shot to the PCW World Title! Forgive me if I find whatever he’s doing a little bit… trivial, in comparison to my match.” Handler:“Well, there’s two more people we’ll talk about, but first, let’s fill this thing up with some blank space, shall we? The End:“Yes, blank space is good.” The RP Rules of Etiquette smack the handler in the face. He grabs the book and reads it. Handler:“Oh, yeah, blank space isn’t a good idea.” The End:“Well, we need a break from talking.” Handler:“How about a preview from the next Misfits RP where they get to the Land of the Lepers?” The End:“That can work.” Richard and Drek awake locked inside of a cage made of extremely hard, dense wood. They are inside a small hut with a contained fire burning in the corner to provide light. Drek is sitting Indian-style on the floor by the time Richard gets up. He rubs his head from the beating he received earlier. “Richard can still feel the hits,” he says in a little painful manner. “Yeah,” Drek affirms without looking back at Richard. “They can throw punches with the best of ‘em.” Richard glances around the hut. “They did not get Slade?” he asks. “Nah,” Drek says, “it doesn’t look like it. Everything is alright, though.” “If by that, you mean that it was our intention to come and get captured by a band of rogues and put in a cage so we can’t get Lamar’s medicine, then Richard agrees, everything is just peachy.” Drek turns back to Richard, a little agitated by the negative tone taken by the scruffy-bearded Misfit. “Slade’s still out there,” says Drek. “He’ll get us out.” Richard remarks snidely, “Wasn’t Slade the one that yelled, ‘GET THEM!’ rather out of the blue?” “I’m sure there was a reason for that. Slade had some plan. I hope.” “You are not filling Richard with confidence. Handler:“It’s time to discuss the last two people in this RP. Let’s get back to it. Boda’s “The Truth Revealed: Did or Didn’t Boda Do It?” I admit the whole set-up thing with Boda had me thinking it’d be a quick resolution to the problem. And indeed there was. But I never suspected that Mona would be the one to try to setup Boda. It happened, though. Boda likes to put those kind of things into his RPs, and that’s one of the reasons why he’s at the top right now.” The End:“Well, looks like you’re in the clear after a moment where it looked like you might not be able to make it to the pay-per-view. Then again, Juan Borro’s escaped from Chinese prison, which is like the unescapable Alcatraz of our day, so you’d be able to get out of processing jail to come to March Madness. It’s a good thing you didn’t mention my name as a possible suspect, because it would have been a clear lie. I wouldn’t want you out of the pay-per-view. In fact, what better way to make an impression on the Rumble than to eliminate the ‘Ameri-Czech Badass’ himself? You’re not as feared as you think, Boda. Don’t be so high on yourself. If need be, I’m sure ten men in the ring will team together just to get you out of the ring.” Handler:“Last is Bobby Stormbringer. He’s up to usual standards. He kinda waltzed into the PPV in his mini-bracket, but I feel he should be in the Rumble anyway. End?” The End:“Hey, Stormbringer. About this RP, “The Chases,” I’d just like to say thanks for all the free press. You’re doing wonders for my career, I mean, losing to me and THEN mentioning me and using my handler’s most recent RP style in one of your promos? Man, I’m laughing all the way to the bank on that one. But enough of that. You’ve got four Asylum members in the ring at the Rumble. And out of all of them, you’re the one with the least accomplishments behind your name. And DON’T talk about all your meaningless titles in lower feds. That would be like going up to Sammy Sosa and saying, ‘Well, since I won more little league trophies than you’ve won Major League Baseball awards, I’m better than you.’ It’s just not the same. So, you’ve done the least in PCW. So, when it comes down to it, you think any one of your teammates are going to let you win if they can pull off the victory? You do? Heh, funny. But at least you’re one of the few people smart enough to realize that I am indeed a threat in this main event. Good for you.” Handler:“Well, I’d say that’s pretty much it for this RP. I’ve gotta get ready to go home for Spring Break. I would’ve liked to put up more RPs for the Rumble, and maybe luck will be in my favor to post some more. Otherwise, see you at the PPV. This is Skylar Green, signing off.” Fade to Black
