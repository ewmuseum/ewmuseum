Rebirth (FF #1)

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/15/02 The time comes in a mans life where he must change everything and start on an adventure that will change his life forever. It could change for the better, or in the worst case scenario it could change for the worse. In my case it changed for the worse, but my case is different, I was given a second chance, I WAS GIVEN A REBIRTH. The scene opens it is in what seems to be in some sort of dark alley location. A man is seen in a far off distance walking towards the camera. He walks very close to the camera. And there is a sudden flash of light. It was a door opening. Out walks Carl Cash holding onto a soda. A Tab soda to be precise. He walks through a kitchen and into his front room. In his front room there is a big TV, a wooden block television. There is a Nintendo Entertainment System hooked up to the back. There is also a very big Book box sitting on the table in front of him with a lot of tape cassettes. Carl Cash- Finally, once again I am going to get my shot. I get my shot to redeem myself. I finally get the chance to prove the first time around was a fluke. That I really am a great wrestler, that I can compete with some of the top names in the game. This Friday I have my chance for redemption. If I have ever gotten a second chance, I have taken advantage of it. I always will come through at the end of the second chance. Carl Cash gets up from his comfortable spot on his couch and walks down the hallway. He opens a door to his right and walks down a flight of stairs. When he gets to the bottom of the stairs the camera shows what is on the bottom level of his house. He has a great workout area. He has free weights, he has weight machines, he has treadmills, and Stairmaster. There is roughly about a million-dollar in exercise area and equipment. Carl Cash- This is why I will not allow myself to flop, I got in with the wrong crowd the last time around, and this time around I am going to do so many different things. Every day Ill be training down here or at some gym at whatever city we are in. I mean this Friday; I make my debut no further then an hour away from my home. I will be happy and very relaxed going into the match. I will be able just to leave from my house to get to the arena. That is the best thing about my first match back, I can be completely and totally ready for it. Nothing will get in my way, nothing will stop me. Cash walks back up the stairs and walks further down the hallway. He now steps outside of his house and is on a big deck. He sits on this long lawn chair and takes of his shirt to get a tan. He pops off his Sunglasses and sets them on a table that is sitting next to him. Carl Cash- This is a great way to Relax, this is a great way to get ready for matches. Do not think about them, because if you end up thinking and worrying about it too much if you lose. It becomes that much harder on yourself, and I found when I concentrate on the match a lot, I usually end up losing and I end up doing a lot of things I wouldn’t have done. I get myself in a lot of trouble, and it ends up ruining my life for that short period of time. So now, after months of therapy and talking to other wrestlers, I know what I have to do before every match. I need to treat every match the same, I cant give one more attention than another one, just because there might be a title shot involved, or some great wrestler involved, I have to treat them all the same. All of a sudden there is a ringing sound, and Carl Cash reaches underneath his lawn chair and pulls out a huge cell phone. He holds it up to his ear and begins to talk. Carl Cash- Hello, how in the hell did you get this number, no I don’t need any credit cards, don’t you know who I am, obviously not, I don’t have the time for this. Click, Cash hangs up the phone. He looks toward the camera and starts to talk again. Carl Cash- I am so surprised how all these stupid people getting my number, especially my cell phone number. I don’t have it any phone book, and I only give it out to people I like, well there was that one time. I gave it to that shopping channel, when I bought all those Nintendo games. I bet you they sold my number to stupid companies. God Damnit, they’re going to get a piece of my mind. But that will have to wait until after today, because today I am a busy man. Busy with what? That is none of your business, I have got places to go, I have got a lot of people to see. None that are of any importance to you, so I don’t really need to let you in on them Carl Cash gets up off his lawn chair, and he goes over and jumps in his pool. He starts swimming laps. He begins talking why he is swimming laps. Carl Cash- That is what is so great about me, you never knows what is going to happen next. I am the most spontaneous person you will ever meet. I do whatever I want to do, whenever I want to do it. Like now I just jumped into my pool when I was fully clothed, I didn’t care at all, I just wanted to go for a swim. So I went for a swim. If I want to hit someone with my cellular phone, I am going to hit someone with my cellular phone. If I want to drink a few beers, I am going to drink a few beers. You get the picture now don’t you? Carl Cash gets out of the pool and looks at the camera and begins to talk one more time. Carl Cash- So this Friday at Fusion, I make my rebirth into PCW, this is where I let the past be the past, and look at nothing but the future. I know there will be people that will look at my past, and judge me about my past. I’m not all about that, Ill do anything to prove them wrong, and I mean anything.
