Missing: Chapter VII: Chistov's Fifth Alias

undated

Date: Chapter 12 I could not believe it. There it was. The name I had been looking for all along was on the sheet I was looking at. Are you clueless? Then you haven’t been following along. You see, I got the job of finding a mysterious criminal who stole four million dollars from the Hare Krishna, the pride and joy of my life, and don’t listen to those no good losers, it isn’t a cult. Anyway, the man who stole all the cash’s name was Ivan Chistov. My database record showed that Chistov was an evil man, but didn’t really tell me anything else. The one thing it did tell me was that Chistov had five aliases in total and I figured that I would need to find all five of them to crack and the case and figure out why Chistov needed all that money. So, through various leads that I had gotten, I had figured out four of the five names that Chistov went under. Ivan Chistov. Fred Mystique. Fredrick Thomas Terry Gordan I only needed one more name. To obtain that name, I went to a small priest’s house where Chistov was said to be seen last. There, I heard a conversation between Chistov’s partner Clair Rivence and the boss of it all, Henry Claval. The conversation told me that Chistov had stolen the money for a mission called Project 84. Henry Claval had obtained five of the very first Playstation 2’s to be released and Japan and used the chip inside to make a deadly bomb, that would cause destruction of DOUBLE the bomb dropped on Hiroshima. While Claval and Rivence were in the house, taking a break, I quickly went downstairs to the room with all the information and grabbed a stack of papers that gave me information on what the mission was to do. I was sure that Claval had extra copies so I knew that the mission was still on. Anyway, the papers, which are currently in front of me, had the information I wanted, and the fifth alias of Ivan Chistov. To say the least, I was totally shocked. Of all the people in the world that this man could be, it had to be the last person I wanted, my brother. Sven Neirlinger. The same brother who tried to kill me last time around we met and I foiled his plan. Sven, the same brother who spend years in slavery and taking care of my mom while I lived a free life in America. The only thing was, would Sven expect me to be the one to stop him again. This was just a very bad dream. I read over the papers in front of me again at the mission plan. I needed to read it over again to make sure I knew when to strike and to think of how I would strike. Project 84 Assailant’s: Henry ClavalClair RivenceSven Neirlinger Missing Date: TBA Mission Briefing: At approximately 4:00 on a to be announced date, Project 84 is lifted in the air by a small helicopter. At exactly 4:21, the plane flies over the New York City Power Plant (NYCPP) and at that time, the small, five feet bomb is released from the helicopter. Upon impact, one thousand feet below where the plane flew, the small Project 84 bomb explodes. The power from the electrical plant adds even more power to the already deadly bomb, killing thousands instantly and leaving many other wounded. Projected Deaths: One million people. Projected Injuries: Two million people. Meanwhile, halfway across the world, Claval, Rivence, and Neirlinger sit and enjoy a cup of coffee in Germany with a job well done. I had read it, now I had to stop it. The only thing I now needed to know was the date on which this was to happen. How could I find that out? I needed some thinking time. Of course, that would be hard as my very first PCW PPV was approaching fast on me. Tomorrow night, I would have to fight in two matches. One match, beating Siviskian Krikorian to advance to the Rising Star title match, and the other to actually win the title that was meant to be mine. The Ukrainian Sniper, as people called Krikorian, I wasn’t worried about. My theory is that if you can’t pronounce his name, he can’t be that dangerous so I see no reason to be scared. I think Krikorian will be a swift match before my big match to accomplish the one goal I came to PCW to achieve. Win the PCW Rising Star title.
