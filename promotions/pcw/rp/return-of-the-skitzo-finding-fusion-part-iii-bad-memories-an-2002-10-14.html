Return of the Skitzo: Finding Fusion Part III - Bad Memories and Gator Huntin' Relived!

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-10-02 He squinted as the sun shone brightly in his eyes. Rather than shying away from it altogether, he felt himself moving closer to it, his arms reached out trying to grasp at the golden warmth. Suddenly the sun shining before him disappeared, and Skitzo found himself standing by a luscious swamp. The atmosphere was calming, clean, as Skitzo looked up high into the cloudless sky and took in a breath of the fresh and refreshing Louisiana air. The cool, crisp air invigorated his lungs, and as he looked around the Bayou, taking in the pristine scenery of the grasping florae at peace with the Louisianan faunae. Skitzo’s eyes welled up and only one word came to mind from the beauty before him. Home… Walking along the bank of a relatively large swamp, taking care not to slip into the murky depths, Skitzo smiled, as just beneath the glassy surface of the water, occasionally breaking through, was the telltale scales and markings of a good ol’ Alligator. A mischievous grin graced Skitzo’s face as he suddenly realised the possibilities of being back home and in the presence of Alligators – Gator Hunting! Skitzo looked off into the distance, and past a big old tree hanging over the edge of the banking, complete with homemade tyre swing, a little old shack rested beneath the baking sun overhead, shadowed slightly beneath a large canopy of lanky branches. Skitzo glanced occasionally to that sun; it shone brightly in his face, drawing him in. Skitzo hopped and skipped along the edge of the swamp like a giddy little child, pleased to be back home in his natural surroundings. Skipping over some large clumps of grass, Skitzo froze suddenly, and took a mere few nanoseconds to assess the situation before jumping back as fast as he could. Stopping back outside of the long grass, Skitzo looked over to what had made him jump and sighed a great sigh of relief – for an Alligator, 20ft long, basking in the sun had stopped him in his tracks. Skitzo, being the man he is, knew of course an Alligator is a cold blooded reptile, and if its basking in the sun that means its readying itself to hunt which means pissing one off results in you being hunted. With that in mind, Skitzo was ready for a day at the Gator Hunting. However, before he could take to the swamps, he had to visit the little shack…why? He really didn’t know. Taking care of where he stepped, Skitzo danced across the ground towards the shack, and stopped, pressing his back against the cold wood of the wall and listening for any signs of life within. The skin of his bare back picked up moss and mildew from the wall, evidence the place was not kept in the best of care. Clad in only his denim shorts and black army boots, Skitzo sidled around the shack and stopped before a window, crafted poorly with one single sheet of glass wobbling where it had been placed. Skitzo, or William Williams to only he, wiped the glass as clean as it could be and peered inside. The telltale signs of age were evident in every sloppy spider web and mould-covered morsel of food left to rot on the floor, if it could be called that. Everything within the confines of this dank room could be identified as items Skitzo grew up with, and he took no time to think about entry. Bursting through the makeshift door Skitzo found himself in the first ‘room’ in the house – the den. Skitzo didn’t even have to fight the tears that didn’t well up in his eyes as he gazed upon the charred remnants of memories long since gone. Where a weak old table and two rickety chairs used to be, there now lies a pile of broken splinters, on top of which are the burnt remains of what seem like many possessions crammed into a crispy black pile of ash. The man they once knew as William Williams sighs and shakes his head, knowing not of how all this devastation came about. Moving into the only other room in the house, Skitzo’s jaw dropped. Finally it all fell into place. Hanging by the ceiling, gagged with cloth and bound with much rope were the gently swinging corpses of his parents. Cletus and Marjory Williams hung there before him, their bodies barely decomposed. A chunk of flesh oozed off of Marjory’s leg and plopped down onto the ground, immediately surrounded by rats, more shock to Skitzo. His heart was racing but he showed no emotion, only silence. Bowing his head, Skitzo turned and left the house. Skitzo himself had burned down the house, he knew that…but he had not killed his parents, nor ransacked the house…had he? It only hit Skitzo as he exited the house what had happened. Now he was alone. What family he had he had just found dead in his own home growing up, and now there was nothing. Through all of this though Skitzo never once felt sympathy of pain for his deceased parents, for their neglect had driven him to the brink of sanity, and he had himself willingly pushed himself over into the abyss. A year in the Silenced Bayous Insane Asylum had perfected his madness, and now rather than a simple gimmick, Skitzo had been transformed into the disease itself, though he made it look good. Walking away from the house, Skitzo sighed and skipped off across the land, heading for the nearest road, which would take him to Bernice, where he could rest for the night. Jogging along one of the many unnamed streets off the main roads, Skitzo skidded to a halt on the hot tar of the road, noticing a familiar building hidden in the trees about 20ft up the road. Taking off, Skitzo ran along the road, cursing and gesturing to the cars and trucks that passed, honking their obnoxious horns at him. As he got closer, the building gradually came into view, and Skitzo grinned as he realised what he had found… “Uncle Joe’s Chicken Farm” Walking the last few feet to the farm, Skitzo jumped over the little rotted picket fence and stopped at the front door, old and worn just like everything else around him. The flowers and lawn ornaments, which graced the garden before, had been reduced to rubble and weeds, though Skitzo had no time to check out all the details as an old man finally answered his call. Skitzo: You be Uncle Joe I presume? Uncle Joe: Dat I be son, who be ye and what ye want? Skitzo: I was wonderin’ if y’all had some chickens left here, sir… Uncle Joe: HEHAA!! You be in just the right amount of luck boy, I say I said you be in luck! Come on in I get ye a drink! Skitzo: I’m afraid I must decline sir; I’m only here for the-… Uncle Joe: Chickens, I gotcha! Come on in! Skitzo sighed, contemplating a chuckle but rather just walking on into the farm. Closing the door behind him, Skitzo was impressed with what he saw before him. While the exterior of the property was abysmal, the interior of the house was quite well kept. Albeit the colour scheme was bland, the general cleanliness of the house was enough incentive to keep Skitzo going on through the house, past the stairs and out of the kitchen into the back yard…where the chickens were brooding. Uncle Joe was rather enthusiastic to show off his produce. Uncle Joe: Now you be my first customer this week, you can choose any one of them chicks you want! Skitzo looks around. Chicken bones, bloodied feathers and broken beaks lay strewn around the barren yard. The chicken coop itself has a harsh atmosphere about it, and then Skitzo fazes out of his daydream and looks to Uncle Joe. Skitzo: First customer this week? When was it your last customer done bought him a chicken? Uncle Joe: That’ll be four decades tomorrow! Skitzo’s eyes widen and he shakes off the idea of someone staying in business without customers for 40 years, and heads for the coop. Lifting up the roof, Skitzo peers in to find six chickens resting on the seed-laden floor of the coop, gently pecking at individual seeds and swallowing them up. Skitzo eyes up four of the chickens, and as Uncle Joe hobbles along next to them, points to his favourites. Skitzo: I’ll take the one with the large comb…the one with the eye patch, the one with fangs and-…wait a minute! FUCK-A-DOODLE-MOOOO!!! Skitzo pulls his hand out of the coop as the chicken with the fangs suddenly dives for it. Backing off, Skitzo glances into the coop as the chickens with the eye patch and the fangs suddenly jump the scrawniest of the bunch, a chicken with only one wing nestled in the corner. The two chickens jump on it, clawing at its back and neck and ripping at its throat. The chicken with the fangs clamps down on the one-winged chicken’s throat and twists, ripping off a chunk of flesh. The chicken hits the floor and begins convulsing, as both chickens proceed to dig into its back, tearing at the spine and pecking at it. Uncle Joe: That one’s called ‘Lucky’! Skitzo: You don’t say…ok look sir, just gimme fangs, eye patch and the two other ones! Uncle Joe: I say that’ll be $34.99 plus postage and packaging! Skitzo: I’m taking them out of here myself, no need to post them… Uncle Joe: Aight… Skitzo fumbles around in his pocket and pulls out some cash. Fiddling out the correct change, he hands the money to Joe, who disappears inside the house for a moment, then returns with a basket in his hand. Opening the coop, Joe scoops up Fang, Eye-Patch and the two other chickens into the basket as quickly as he can, handing it to Skitzo and pointing to the door. Uncle Joe: Good doin’ business with ya son, now you be going now, hya! Skitzo is pushed back through the house and out the door. He finds himself standing by the road, having been forced out of the house almost as quickly as he entered. Skitzo looks at the basket, then at the house, then down the road. Skitzo: What the hell… Skitzo looks in the basket to see the chickens sitting quietly, and then takes off back in the same general direction as his parent’s house in the bayou. Running along the road and turning into the unnamed street, Skitzo takes care not to bash the chickens around so much, hopping over the clumps of grass towards the house. As he nears it though, Skitzo suddenly takes a sharp right through a small path in the woods and pushes past all the overgrown shrubs and weeds, through to the other side…and to a small jetty. Skitzo: Ahhh…memories… Skitzo: Painful memories… Skitzo runs a finger along the scar on his torso. The Alligators’ teeth marks are as evident as they were 11 years ago when the Gator attack occurred. Putting the basket down on the jetty, Skitzo reaches down into the murky water, clotted with weeds and gunk and mud. Feeling around, Skitzo pulls his hand back out, this time with a rope in his grip. Pulling on that, Skitzo grins as a small boat floats out from underneath the jetty, looking as new and as fresh as the last time Skitzo hid it under there. Skitzo: My boat! Skitzo: IT’S ALIVE! ALIVE! Skitzo: It’s inanimate goodness! Skitzo immediately grabs the basket and hops into the boat, grabbing his little self-made oar and paddling out from the jetty…until he realises he forgot to untie it and is stopped in his tracks. Doing as he should and untying the boat, Skitzo paddles out into the middle of the murky waters a little, then stops…taking in the choking density of the wilderness around him. When once before he could clearly see the bright shining sun, he is now trapped beneath a thick canopy of exfoliating trees, old and withered with age. Sitting calmly and quietly in the boat, Skitzo opens the basket to pull out a chicken…and pull out a chicken he does – one without a face or limbs. Skitzo: GODDAMN YOU FANG AND EYE-PATCH!!! Skitzo: Stupid goddamn birds! Skitzo: If I were Jordan X Shabazz, by god you’d be in trouble right now… Fang and Eye-Patch cluck in the basket regardless, but as Skitzo looks along the banks for any other signs of laughs, the sound of sinister giggling seems to arise from the basket… Skitzo: No way… Skitzo: They are devil birds… Skitzo: Devilled birds go nice with devilled eggs… The giggling stops. Skitzo look to the shore and sees a large Alligator staring right back at him, through its strangely hypnotic yet demonic eyes. Skitzo: Holy sheeiiite! That Gator would pick up a pretty penny at the Bernice market! Skitzo quickly grabs the last remaining sane chicken and dips it in the blood of the killed one, and proceeds to hang it over the edge. Skitzo: Come and get it you big ol’ bastard… As though obeying him, the big Alligator, all 25ft of it slides into the water, and disappears. Skitzo’s eyes widen as he realises he hasn’t been Gator hunting in a number of years, yet continues to bob the chicken in and out of the water. Hearing something behind him, Skitzo turns around, feels something jerk in his hand, then turns back around to find nothing but a few feathers left in his hand. Skitzo: DAMMIT! Grabbing Eye-Patch out of the basket, Skitzo dips it in the blood and bobs it in and out of the water, as the Alligator beneath the water circles back around for another pass. Skitzo stares hard below the surface and makes out the form of the Gator speeding towards the boat…and lifts the chicken out, too slowly though as the Gator rises up out of the water and snaps at the chicken, taking off its legs and a slice of its belly! Skitzo: DOUBLE DAMMIT! Skitzo: Plan B… Skitzo holds the chicken underwater, and as the Alligator passes again, releases the chicken and dives into the water, grabbing hold of………a rock! Struggling to get out of the water quickly, Skitzo feels the rush of water pass him where the Gator swims by. Hopping into the boat, a frustrated PCWer reaches into the basket and pulls out the last chicken – Fang. Skitzo: Ok Fang…I’m pissed off already with you lot…before it only took half a chicken to get one of these big bastards landed, I wasted $35 on you…now… Skitzo unzips his shorts and pulls them down, revealing himself to nature. Grabbing hold of Fang by the neck, Skitzo grits his teeth, turns around…and proceeds to shove the Chicken they called Fang up into his ass! The clucking and involuntary farting makes Skitzo groan and coo as he sits his ass overboard and clenches tight, cutting off the air supply to Fang. Dipping the chicken underwater, Skitzo waits and listens carefully as the chicken tries to escape from his anal lock, flailing around wildly as in the distance, through the mud, the great beast swims gracefully towards its prey. Skitzo clenches harder, causing Fang to go all out…and start biting. Skitzo howls frantically, trying to hold his position as Fang bites down into the supple flesh within…failing to notice the Alligator speeding towards him. Closing in, the gator’s jaws part, revealing the chicken-stained teeth and the massive mouth…inching towards the large, angry chicken until… SNAP! Skitzo: GOTCHA MUTHAFUCKA!!! As the gator pulls the chicken, it also pulls Skitzo with it, and as he is pulled underwater, Skitzo unclenches and grabs hold of the gator. Thrashing at its face and eyes, Skitzo beats down on it as Fang disappears into the gator’s gut. Wrapping his arms around its neck, Skitzo tightens the hold, trying to choke the gator as it swims rapidly around the swamp. The fight continues for some time as Skitzo tries to stop the alligator while clenching again…for obvious reasons. Speeding through the murky depths, Skitzo pulls an arm around and jams a thumb into the gator’s eye. Though underwater, Skitzo grimaces, as the gator seems to squeal in pain. Jamming his other thumb into the gator’s other eye, Skitzo roars out as the blinded gator starts speeding in odd directions…snapping out and trying to rip Skitzo to shreds. The Gator and Skitzo flail around in the water for moments on end, until the alligator changes position and swims straight…straight towards a pile of large rocks and trees lying at the bottom of the swamp. Skitzo lets go of the gator, and then howls out as it suddenly grabs hold of his arm and pulls him down, racing to the bottom of the swamp. Skitzo screams out, but only the oxygen bubbles make it to the surface. As they speed towards impending doom, Skitzo looks up into the sky, to see that big, bright shining sun shining brightly down on him, filling his eyes with light and hope…until everything goes black and Skitzo’s body is filled with a vague trembling. -------- Skitzo opened his eyes to find himself staring out of that same old window on the Bullet Train. Looking around, everyone was leaving, and so he undid his seatbelt and left with them. Standing out on the platform, Skitzo stretched and rubbed his eyes, looking to the signs around……………however not one of them read Tokyo……… Skitzo: Ohhhhhh SHIT! To Be Continued, Y’hyear!
