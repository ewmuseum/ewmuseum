Homes Don't Always Bring Happiness

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 11th, 2003 Ding The cheery elevator door chimes as its doors open, allowing me and a young child to step in. I still do not know the child’s name. All I know is that I saved this child’s friend and for that he is eternally grateful. The child looks up at me with a smile that reminds me of the little boy that I took care of. Seeing his smile I can’t help but to feel pride run through my blood. Even if I was not going to be able to defeat Teresa Tomas this Tuesday at least I had saved a life and earned the respect of a family and two caring friends and knowing that I had just done that it had totally dissolved away any panic I had about Chris walking out on me. Once he had found out what I had done he would be so proud of me and would take me back for sure. Ding The elevator chimed once more as it’s door opened, bringing both the child and I to the floor which Chris Storm dwelled on. He pulled me by the arm as if he had known the way but just as he remembered that he did not know where he was going he fell behind me following my lead to Chris’ apartment. Once I reached the door that brought us to Chris’ apartment I reached out banging my hands against the door making a knocking noise. Instead of “Come In” I heard shouts over who would answer it. I listened closely to the shouting hoping to match once of the voices with Chris’ but before I could succeed the door swung open and I was greeted with two simultaneous hugs, one being from Alex, the other coming Christa. “Eww you are wet!” Shrieked Christa, as she quickly pulled herself away from my body which was still soaked from falling into the lake at Central Park. “Where have you been?” Alex asked as he noticed that his clothes were slightly wet now as well from coming in contact with mine. “I went for a walk.” I said as I walked into the apartment. “More like a swim.” Alex snapped back with wit combined with laughter. “Hey who is that?” Christa asked as she pointed a finger towards the child who had followed me in through the door and now stood shyly behind me. “This is…” I began before it occurred to me that I still did not know the child’s name. “Evan.” The little boy finished my sentence for me in a quiet voice. He looked up at Alex and Christa who looked tall compared to him. He must have been younger than I thought because both Christa and Alex were small for their age as it was. “Evan.” I repeated for myself and the children aloud. “Where is the phone?” He asked quietly as Christa and Alex stood a few feet away still examining the child trying to figure out why he was here invading their turf. “In the living room.” I answered as the child made his way to the room following my words. “Is he Chris’ son?” Questioned Christa from out of the blue. “Chris’ son?” I laughed. “No, Chris doesn’t have any kids, silly. Speaking of Chris, where is he Christa?” “He left.” Alex answered for her, as Christa walked off in the living room to see what else she could find out about Evan. “What do you mean he left?” I snapped, not wanting that as an answer. “He said he had to go out and think about some things and left.” Alex answered in a smaller voice to show that he too was confused as to why Chris left. “Gah!” I said to myself in frustration. But before I could become too upset my thoughts were cut short by a cry coming in the living room. Right away I began to think that Christa had began to feel threatened by Evan and attacked him for no reason, but when I had walked in the room I could see Evan crying on Christa’s shoulders. It was a cute but saddening sight. The first thought that came to my mind is that his friend had not made it. I sat down beside the child rubbing his back in attempt to comfort him. “What’s wrong Evan dear?” I said to him expressing my concern. “My…dad…” Was all I could get out of him before he chocked on more tears. “Your dad what?” I whispered in a gentle voice to him. “He …shot….my….mom” He said, choking tears back between each word. “Oh my…” I could not even begin to imagine the grief this child was going through at the moment. Sure I had lost my own brother not to long ago, but it wasn’t at the hands of someone I loved. I simply sat there in a loss of words as the child continued to wail. “The guy…he answered….he was a policeman…he found her….bleeding…and dead...” He continued on, chocking even me up with tears. “But where will you stay?” Christa questioned young Evan. “They said they are going to find me a new family because daddy’s going to jail.” He said softly too himself, obviously very shaken up from this harsh turn of events. All he wanted to do is call his parents to go home, but yet he ends up finding out that one of his parents are dead at the other parents hands. I couldn’t take anymore. After nearly witnessing the death of a friend and not having to deal with the death of this mom I couldn’t allow this child to go through anymore stress. “I’ll talk to the policemen. You can stay with me.” I said, doing my best to comfort the child. “But…” “Don’t worry about it Evan. I may have just met you today but I will see to it that you have a happy life and if that means adopting you myself then so be it.” I said doing my best to assure the child that even though he had gone through more emotional trauma in a day than a normal person goes through in a lifetime. Sure I had my own problems to deal with, such as getting Chris to forgive me for walking out that one day, and beating Teresa Tomas on Tuesday, but Chris was away at the moment and Tuesday was still two day away so why worry? I just hoped that offering a child a place in my family would not raise fireworks that would rip the family further apart. That was the last thing I needed but if it was the way it had to be I would live with it.
