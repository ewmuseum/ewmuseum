Date: 4-26-02 Awake in the darkness… spinning out of control… The fun never stops… Come one, come all… See the living nightmare… … Your soul is all it costs. * He had bought his ticket a long time ago. Too far back to even remember when. So many years have passed, and the ride still hasn’t stopped. He wanted to finally get off, he was sick of it. He was no longer amused anymore. His cries of glee slowly turned to screams of agony. But escape was impossible. His feet were chained together, his arms stuck in the machines, his mouth gagged shut. What was the fool to do? It only went faster and faster and faster… He woke up that morning and he was ready to crank up the speed on the machine yet again. * ‘Round and ‘round and ‘round it still goes. Where shall it stop? Not even he knows… *Who mans these death machines? There are so many out there that others are riding on, sucked into the same fate as Jeremy. Would it make sense to say the same person whose riding them. They controlled their own destiny; they controlled their own life…. … They controlled their own death.* In the bathroom lied a small needle, just a small object to prick flesh with, right? Who would have thought that this simple device controls that very machine that holds the rider’s life in his very own hands? He looked in the mirror. He was scaring himself yet again. His needle was the only thing that could make him and his world beautiful again, dizzy to society. * *Into the vein the precious life fluid crawled, flowing through the maze that provides the user with his very life. His heroin was his only life now. * *The ride was going faster now than ever before. Spiraling down and out of control. There was no use in even trying to stop it now. The incredible gyrations provided the illusions he craved so. Some frightening, others comforting, but all were glimpses of the horrors to come. * *He woke up a large room. It had a beautiful “grandeur” to it. Whoever constructed a place like this must have been very rich. It was like a elegant ballroom of some sort. And what purpose do people have in a place like this? * DANCE!! *Shards of glass from the chandelier above showered from the ceiling, impaling unto the floor below, ripping wounds in it. Stryfe tried to find a way to avoid the onslaught with his so-called “fancy feet”, but soon the holes formed into one large one, and he fell in.* *He plunged into the sea below. The undertow sucked him down to the depths, deeper into the black… then back into the rich blue and to the surface below. Through a blood red sky, he fell into the sand.* *He was now in the desert. The sand at his feet was rapidly changing colors like some sort of psychedelic experience. He was way in over his head now. What would his destination be during his travels through his mind?* *All he could do is wander now. Travel onward through the sand, or perhaps the gray matter in his head, depending on the way one wants to perceive it. It seemed like days had passed with the rapid pace of the sun rising and setting. He finally reached a large pyramid and began to climb. It only grew more and more with each step he climbed: Perhaps an omen, perhaps nothing more than a fancy illusion. Everything suddenly disappeared around him, and now he was left plummeting through space. Voices kept echoing throughout his trip.* “You have no chance.” “Give up now.” “Save your own life.” *Within a flash, he now found himself strapped down on a table, unable to break free. Then he saw a horrific sight: The large metal blade swinging down towards him. He tried everything he could to free himself, but to no avail. Closer and closer it came. The steel started grazing his skin. The hot blade of death was burning him internally despite external damages taking place.* *Soon the table started melting, taking Stryfe down with him. After everything that went on so far, what could he be in for next on this roller coaster ride from hell’s veins?* *He reformed in what appeared to him a large cave. It was dark inside with no light whatsoever and he had to grope around to find his way. Before he could even catch his breath though, the entire place began to rumble. There was no way out. He would for sure be crushed by the rubble. The ceiling then caved in. At least some light peaked through now. The sun was shining bright as Stryfe began to climb out. But what’s this? It began to laugh at him. There, in the sun was a reflection of Miyagawa’s face.* “There is no escape!” The voice from the sun erupted. *Larger and larger it grew. It soon became the only thing in Jeremy’s sight. Once high in the sky, it now was within arms reach of him. A white, yet very unheavenly light flashed from the sky, blinding Stryfe from seeing the sun finally reach its breaking point and explode.* *It seemed it was all over for him… until he opened his eyes to find nothing but a tiny flame burning in front of him, which he extinguished with his foot. Before he knew what hit him, the flame rose up and encircled him until the only thing in his eyesight was a crimson, orange and yellow hell. *The flame flickered out seconds later and Stryfe now found himself a beautiful forest. It seemed a place so peaceful would be secluded from the dangers that lied everywhere else. He was wrong again…* Run, run. The fun’s not done. *A loud buzzing echoed through the woods. What could it possibly be now? A large chainsaw?! It was heading straight for him at blinding speed. The large word “Syko” was most notably painted on it, even in green and black: Goblin colors. As fast as Jeremy could run, it only gained on him more and more. He could feel the presence of the blade on his back as he finally saw an exit in the distance. He give it everything he had and with all of his strength, leaped out of the forest, leaving the giant chainsaw behind.* *He wasn’t safe yet though. He was now standing in the freezing cold of the frozen tundra. The sharp winds pierced at him. The cold was next to unbearable. Those winds only grew stronger as Stryfe pressed on. A large shard of ice from a glacier above cracked off and fell into the thin ice below, opening up a large, near frozen sea at his feet. He tried to step around it, but the strong winds blew him in.* *It was bitterly cold as he instantly went dumb. Then began to feel warmer…… And warmer And now felt comfortable as he began as he regained feeling in his body. Then hotter The burning. He opened his eyes to find himself now swimming in an ocean of fire. Further and further down he went and eventually his feet touched ground. E was now where many men feared most: The depths, the infernos of hell itself.* “Is this my time?!” He shouted in desperation, searching for an answer. *He got his answer. The walls started closing in on him. Spikes began to protrude from the rock. He was trapped once and for all in his own misfortunte. Closer the steel drew, just like so many times before. He was sweating in fear, lubricating the metal and in poked at him. Deeper it went into the fresh wounds. Blood seeped out of the shell… … Then he woke up. He was back on earth now, and his mind cleared for the challenges lying ahead. The whole experience was a simple trip through the users mind.* Needless to say, the mind can be quite a scary thing after all…
