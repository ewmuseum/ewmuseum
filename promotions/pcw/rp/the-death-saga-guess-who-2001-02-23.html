The Death Saga: 'Guess Who?'

23 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-01 His eyes were opened even wider as he looked around at the small, but still dramatic, carnage that he has layed out here, in the underworld. The realm of the damned. The wrecked car, the dead shopkeep. Even he can be more evil then the inhabitants of hell. Walking down the street, he stood in front of a small eatery, Hell's Bagels and thought to himself. I wish I brought money with me, I sure could use something to eat Almost on cue, a portly man, maybe about 40, wearing all white like he is a tourist in Florida approached the Dream Lord. He seemingly read his mind. Man: Excuse me, but could you use something to eat? You seem a bit confused. DarkStar turned, eyeing the man up and down before nodding hesitantly. DarkStar: ...Yes, thank you. I'm famished. The man opens the door to the Bagelry for DarkStar, they both walk in and get a small table, near the counter. The older gentleman goes up and orders to plain bagels with cream cheese. Retrieving them from the worker, he returned to the table, handing DarkStar his and sitting down. DarkStar: This may seem rude, but you really dont seem like you should be here. Your nice and caring, I can tell you want to help people. Hell, you bought me a bagel. What are you doing here? The man smirks, spreading cream cheese on his bagel. Man: Well, it was more of a clerical error up in heaven, they told me they would get me up there the moment it was fixed, that was a little while ago, it doesn't really bother me much. Patience is a virtue as they say. But the only-... Damn... Excuse me a moment. He stood, pushing his chair out. He walked to the counter and placed the empty cream cheese container on the counter. Man: Excuse me, I was only given enough Cream Cheese to cover one half of my bagel, could you please give me a second? The pimple faced worker looks up at him from his magazine. Worker: That will be 50 cents extra, jagoff. Twitch... Twitch... *Snap* Man: WHY! WHY ME! WHY AM I STILL HERE IN THIS HELL! NOOOOOO!!!!!!! The pure anger and grief propelled him into the center of the main road, off to the right of Hell's Bagels. He fell to his knee's in the street, looking into the sky. Man: God! Why Have You Forsaken Meeeeee!!!!!!!!!! Also in his grief, the large truck was not seen, nor was the splat it made as it hit him, leaving nothing more then a spot on the ground. DarkStar took the oppurtunity to stand from his table, finishing his bagel calmly and whiping his hands on his new pants, walking towards the door, a small bell tings as he exits, walking down the street once again. Nowhere to go, and nowhere to turn. A tap on his back, he goes to turn around, but before he can a pair of hands reach across his eyes, covering them up. 'Guess who?' The voice... so familiar... but he cant quite put his finger on it. The light, squeaky voice... slight accent. Suddenly it hit him. It couldnt be... Not her... Here? He pulled her small hands off of his face and turned around, it was true. She came down here, to the realm, and the next words out of her mouth were one of the bigger surprises. Harle Quinn: Lucifer wanted moi to tell 'ou some informacion, yes? He had no thought, as he passed out and hit the ground with a small thud.
