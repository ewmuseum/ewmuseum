Will Scientology and Preppyness Invade Argentina?

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/3/02 After a trip to the South Pole, a hitched ride on an Argentinean ship, being taken over by Pirates, being washed up on a Reality TV Island, surviving an attack by island natives and being closer than most would like to get to a British Navy Destroyer’s gun volley, Xavier and Ellie~! are finally making their way back to America, and more importantly St Louis where Xavier is in the World Title Battle Royal. Presently he and his valet are on the HMS Halesworth, a rogue British Navy vessel off the Argentine coast who still believe that the Falklands War is going on and that they have been on deep patrol for over 20 years. They have agreed to take the Scientologist back to America under the illusion that he is a secret agent for the American Army who needs to get back to the USA to get the support of his country for the war that the crew believe is still going on. What will happen when Xavier gets back to the US? Will his lies catch up with him? Will he avoid eating Prawns at a hotel or not? Or will he even get to America let alone in time for March Mayhem? We open up in Xavier and Ellie’s guest quarters aboard the Navy Destroyer, a sterile Grey cramped room with bunk beds fitting against the back wall and no creature comforts. Eleanor is lying on the bottom bed, now minus the bikini and dressed in a Navy issue t-shirt and ill fitting Blue jeans instead while on the top bunk Xavier sits with his legs dangling over wearing the same style uniform issue t-shirt along with his Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery. The door to the cabin vibrates with the sound of knocking and Xavier drops down from the top and sits on the bottom bunk with Ellie~! laying around him. Xavier : “Yep....” The door opens and one of the ships crew walks in and salutes, still under the illusion that Xavier is a special forces guy for the US Army. Eleanor : “Whoa, you haven’t had a sailor show up at your door since that time you go pissed with the Rush and those guys were on sure leave at the bar you we-...I’ll stop talking now...” Kannon stares holes right through her before sailor clears his throat to get there attention, standing to attention himself. Sailor : “If you would like to come with me sir the Captain has requested to see you.” The Scientologist goes to get up before dropping back down on the bunk, crossing his legs on the mattress. Xavier : “Okay, but can I get some shoes? These things you jokingly refer to as floors really aren’t that warm....Can I ask what it is about?” The crew members remains staring at a spot on the wall somewhere just to the left of Xavier’s head and seemingly hasn’t blinked since he got in the room. Behind him there is a lot of movement on the corridor which gets Ellie~!’s attention but still, the sailors eyes don’t move as he answers back directly. Sailor : “I’m afraid I can not divulge that information sir.” Ellie~! pulls herself from around Xavier and leans up against the back wall, looking inquisitively at the sailor. Eleanor : “Animal, Vegetable or Mineral?” He question is met swiftly with the same statement given to Xavier. Sailor : “I’m afraid I cannot divulge that information ,ma’am.” Xavier : “Is it bigger that a bread box?” Sailor : “I’m afraid I cannot divulge that information sir.” The Scientologist leans back and assesses the situation, reminding him of something he heard in a lecture he bought on tape that was given by L Ron Hubbard himself. Xavier : “Oh, is it like that Philosophical conundrum where you know the only bit of information that can save the world but if you tell anyone it will end all existence so you must was the world in a constant equilibrium of thought?” Sailor : “I’m afraid I cannot divu-” Ellie~! cuts off his automated reply with another question, rubbing her tummy under her t-shirt. Eleanor : “Or is it time for lunch because I am like, SO hungry here?” The crew member simply carries on from where he was cut off from, still focusing on the one spot with his Brown eyes. Sailor : “-lge that information sir. I’m afraid I cannot divulge that information ma’am.” Ellie~! gets up, hopping on the floor at it’s coldness, looking the crew member up and down. Eleanor : “Are you of those British guys that aren’t allowed to move even if I like kick you really hard in the shin or something?” Sailor : “No, those are beefeaters ma’am.” The Gold Patron meritorious nearly breaks out into laughter. Xavier : “Haha, Beefeaters, Snow would have a field day with that one!” Ellie~! is now walking around him but still his eyes don’t move. Eleanor : “Can I kick you in the shin anyway?” Sailor : “No you my not ma’am.” Kannon then gets up, also troubled by the cold of the floor as shivers and shakes his toes. Xavier : “Okay, I suppose we’d better go with you, seeing as we are at a time of war and all.....That and after all the surprises that have happened to us over the last few days what can he possibly say that will effect us!” The sailor does an about turn and leads them out of the cabin and down the corridor of the Destroyer towards the Captain’s private room. Sailor : “I’m afraid I can not divulge that information sir.” The Scientologist sighs as he walks alongside his valet, a few steps behind the sailor, still being troubled by the cold temperature of the floor. Xavier : “That was a rhetorical question.” His valet sneers at the back of the sailor and repeats his last words. Eleanor : “A.......Rhetorical.......Question!” Sailor : “I’m afraid I cannot divulge whether or not I knew it what rhetorical sir.” The Scientologist shakes his head at the mans lack of a sense of humour as they arrive at their destination and the sailor backs on the door solidly 3 times and waits for the word to enter. Captain : “ENTER!” The opens the door and stands guard outside it, nodding to Xavier and Ellie~! to enter the room which they do nervously. Inside is the Captain’s tactical room with maps of the area and units as well as various monitors showing video reels of the surroundings. Xavier : “Er.....Hi, how can we help?” The 1st couple of Scientology look around the room and Kannon lays eyes on a screen which supposedly puts them very close to the Argentine coast and a fair distance away from Florida which was his agreed drop-off point. Captain : “The time to act is now......And you two will be the spark that lights the fuse, our eyes and ears.....The pair who will win the war!” Kannon and Ellie~! turn to eachother and gulp, not liking the sound of the news. Eleanor : “Will you just tell him you are a wrestler!” Ellie~! elbows him on the arm and whispers. Xavier clears his throat and stares at the floor for a few seconds before looking up to the captain with a furrowed brow. Xavier : “Er.....How do you mean? I thought you were going to drop us off quickly so we can get US support for the war?” The captain un-curls a map on the table in front of him and points at the Argentinean coast. Captain : “Well, I suppose as you are military I can let you in on this.....We have been out of contact with the rest of our fleet since we got back off deep patrol so we can only assume that the Argies have killed them all...So we are going to invade them!” Xavier : “The Falklands?” Captain : “No......Argentina.....” Funnily enough this news troubles Xavier as he realises that he will be involved in the upcoming conflict on the much outnumbered side. Captain : “We will give you and Miss hall a lunch ship and you will do reconnaissance at this city here in the coast.....San Lorenzo.....Scout around and look at Naval strength, and if they have any Barracks there as this will be our insurrection point.” Xavier walks around the table to the map and takes a good look at where they are going to land, checking out what lies around it. Xavier : “Okay, give us a ship, some clothes and some food and we’ll scout around for 2 hours and come back to you and tell you what to expect....Then, we will invade Argentina!” Captain : “Good to hear it Soldier, meet me back here at 1400 hours and I’ll brief you.” They both salute each other as Ellie~! gives the laziest salute possible, barely managing to get her hand up to eye level before turning and walking out, followed by Xavier and waiting for him to come out onto the corridor where she punches him on the arm and whispers at him harshly as they walk away from the guard at the door. Eleanor : “What are you doing? You need to get to St Louis and I just want a bath! So why are going on a suicide mission with this bunch of crackpots!” Xavier puts his hand on her shoulder and whispers into her ear as they walk along, pulling her long wavy hair out of the way. Xavier : “Relax.....We are getting a boat, clothes, food and probably money! All we have to do is get there, get to the airport and catch a flight to St Louis! Then let this lot do their little invasion while I go and win me a PCW Title!” Eleanor grins in agreement as the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “So you like it?” Eleanor : “I..........Like........It!”
