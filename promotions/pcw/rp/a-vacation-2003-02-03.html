A Vacation

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 3rd, 2003 A vacation. It was something I had never gotten to taken with someone I loved until today. After all the stress that had built up upon Chris and me in the last few weeks with the arguments between Jackson and him, and then the pranks being pulled on us behalf of P.W.A., we deserved one and thanks to a late Christmas present that is exactly what we got. As the light from the sun grew dimmer and dimmer, the time I had to get myself looking perfect for Chris counted down. Five minutes left and I still had not picked out an outfit, what on earth was I supposed to wear? I forgot to bring along a dressy outfit in case of a moment like this. I figured that most of our time would be spent just sight seeing or maybe hanging out with each other . I searched my bags franticly in search of anything decent enough to wear to a restaurant but all I could find was clothes that you would wear if you planned to be hanging around the house for the day. I felt like a total idiot. I ran to the closet, hoping that maybe I would find a bag that I had forgotten that I brought along. How lucky was I, I didn't find a bag but what I did find was a black dress hanging on a coat hanger. Whoever was here last must have forgotten it. I measured it up against me and noticed that it was a tad bit small, but that never killed anyone. Besides do you actually think Chris Storm would mind, him being a former porn star and all. I quickly changed into the dress that I had found. It fit my slender figure perfect, except for the fact it was a little small at the bottom. It barely covered my backside so I would have to try my best not to keep a straight figure and not bend in the least bit unless I wanted to expose my ass to the world. I hoped Chris would not mind too much, next time I went on a trip of any kind I promised myself that I would never forget to pack something dressy again unless I wanted to wear something similar to what I was wearing right now, looking like one of those girls trying to showcase their body to the world as if saying come and get me, I dare you. Noticing that time was wearing down and Chris was expecting me in two short minutes I ran to the mirror in the bathroom just to make sure I looked as great as I possibly could. I took the brush and ran it through my hair once more making sure that it was a smooth as it could possibly be. Placing the brush on the top of the sink where it was before I noticed that it was time to go meet Chris, so I rushed to the door and hurried down the hall anticipating the moment Chris and I would walk out of the hotel room and into a dream. It was finally the day. I have waited for this day for a long time now. I never thought it would ever happen. Here I am waiting in the lobby of my hotel room in Paris, waiting to go out on the date with Tessa Windsor. It was more than a date though. It was going to be a magical night. I am so excited. But I am also worried. Looking at myself in the mirror in the lobby, I made sure everything was just right. My black and silver dress suit, one of my favorites. My hair is slick and spiky. 'I hope she likes it. I have to look my best tonight.' I told myself as I fixed my shirt. Every button perfect. The clothes had to be perfectly clean. The night was going to be perfect. I just hoped the butterflies in my stomach would quit dancing. I was so nervous I didn't even notice I bumped into the flower pot. 'Ah man!' I shouted as I got dirt all over myself. I started to panic. Tell me this didn't just happen? I brushed off all the dirt and ran to the desk. I grabbed some napkins and wiped it off. Most of the dirt went on my crouch. I started to wipe it off when I heard footsteps coming. I turned around to see Tessa. I walked up to Chris and greeted him with a kiss on the cheek. He responded by wrapping his arms around my waist and pulling me close to him, embracing me fondly and then took my hands and led me out the hotel doors to a limo that was the color of the nighttime sky. 'Oh Chris, this is way too much. You didn't have to get a limo, your car would have been fine.' I said feeling rather guilty that Chris felt he had to do all this for me. It might have been out of love, but I hated for Chris to spend his money like this, even if he did have a lot of it. 'Tessa Bear, don't worry about it. I'd give you everything in the world if I could.' He said, opening the back door of the limo helping me into it. He climbed in after me and sat down as close to me as he possibly could. 'You think the kids will be okay with Jackson?' I asked, still a bit nervous about having them with a man that had never watched them before. I felt guilty leaving them behind but Chris and I had not been able to go out on a big date like this in awhile and how could I say no to Chris, I loved him to death. 'They will be, If not Jackson will regret it.' Was the response I got from him as he wrapped his arms around my shoulders pulling me closer to him so that my head was now leaning on his shoulder. Tessa's head was on my shoulder. I looked down at her as the limo drove off. She had a point about Jackson. He was a friend of ours and all but I have never seen him with kids before. He might not be able to handle them. After all with three little ones they can be a handful. 'Tessa is that a new dress?' I had just noticed her dress. It looked great on her. But she didn't look too comfortable in it. Maybe I sprung all of this on her too quickly. 'Uh. .yeah I just bought it. How do you like it?' she said. There was a tone in her voice I couldn't place. She must have been worried I didn't like it. I rubbed her shoulders. Whispering into her ear I said, 'It looks great on you. Anything would.' The limo suddenly stopped. I rolled down the window a little to see what it was. The night light of Paris stood out as the lights reflected off the window of the limo. I smiled as I noticed we reached our destination. I was one step closer. But I wasn't home free yet. I exited out of the limo and quickly hurried to Tessa's side and opened her door for her. She stepped out and smiled. 'Chris dear, I don't see a restaurant anywheres.' she asked a little confused. I simply smiled and kissed her. As we kissed I turned my face so to point her face in a certain direction. I broke the kiss. 'We are eating there.' came my reply. Tessa's eyes lit up as she stared at the Eiffel Tower. It was all lit up I took her by the hand and led her toward it. This was greater than anything I could possibly imagine. I followed Chris as he led me closer to the tower. I could feel butterflies hatching in my stomach. I never thought of it but what if he was doing to cheer me up so he wouldn't feel bad about breaking up with me. What if he was sick of me and my clinginess and wanted to keep me from getting upset. I knew Chris would never do such a thing, but I loved him so much that I had to worry about small things such as that. 'Look at the tower, it's so beautiful.' I exclaimed, looking up at it as my blue eyes lit up in joy. 'It may be beautiful but it's nothing compared to you.' Chris said with the same voice that I had fallen in love with long before we were together. I never admitted it to anyone but even when Chris wanted to bash my head in with a steel chair or just plain see me in pain I still had a soft spot for him, which is why I didn't bother to fight back. How could I hurt someone I loved so much? We made our way to the Observation deck where the table was set. A candle in the middle. The plates set out. I pulled Tessa's chair out for her I sat down in my own and looked across at her. I just stared at her blue eyes. 'Chris is there something on my face?' she asked me. I didn't realize I had stared at her for so long. I just shook my head and laughed. 'No I just got lost in your eyes. I could stare in them forever.' I told her. However my stomach let out a mighty grumbled. Tessa looked at me with a smirk. 'Forever huh? Looks like you could stare at a nice plate of food right about now.' she laughed. My stomach gave me away. I stood up and walked out of her field of vision. I came back with a tray and a bucket. In the bucket was a bottle of the most expensive wine I could buy. The tray had our dinner on it. From the best restaurant in Paris. I set her plate in front of her and set mine in front of me. I removed the lid and took in the smell of the food. I looked over at Tessa hoping she was enjoying her meal. I reached into my pocket to make sure it was still there. Sure enough, it was. I felt the limp in my throat when my figured touched it. What would she say when I showed it to her? She must have noticed my expression as she got a worried look on her face. I looked over at Chris and although I loved him I could not meet my eyes with his. I felt my head continuing to sink down. My mind was screaming a million different thoughts but the one screaming the loudest was the one that said that something might go wrong. You went and did it Tessa. You pinned him down with too many responsibilities and now he's finally speaking his mind. It's all over now and it's your fault for not trying hard enough. Why else would he suddenly get along withJackson out of nowhere? He's trying to hook you up with another man! He wants you to have Jackson Rust so that you will forget about the man that means the most to you. Chris Storm. He might not be saying it to your face, but that's only because he has feelings with you, just not the ones you want him to have. Brace yourself Tessa, things are only going to get worse from here. The butterflies in my stomach were multiplying and I felt like I was going to breakdown right here in front of Chris at any moment, but I would not allow myself cry. I refused to make Chris feel responsible and like he had to stick with me to the end. Commitment was a big word, but it was a much bigger responsibility and maybe Chris was just not ready for that. I took a deep breath. This was it. I had to do it. I couldn't let her drag on any longer. I grabbed the item in my pocket and pulled it out. 'Tessa. . .' I felt my mind ready to explode like a ticking time bomb whose time had run out. This was it. It was over now and within moment I would be swimming in my own tears once the words dropped from Chris' mouth that were like a ton of bricks falling down on top of me, Tears were tingling in my eyes. Tears that I was trying with all my might to hold back and keep from exposing my weakness for Chris, perhaps the only weakness I had. I saw the tears in her eyes. Could she know what I was about to do? I hope not. I wanted it to be a huge surprise. Although it would change my life forever. I knew it. But I didn't care. My life needed a change. I took another deep breath and opened the box. I stood up from my chair and kneeled down in front of her. With one final breath I asked her the question. . . '. . .will you marry me?' I felt like a fool. I should have known he would never do anything that would scar me emotionally or physically. The tears that I had been holding back finally emerged in full force. I'm sure inside he was thinking I was a fool and he was just hiding it, but the tears would not give up so I had to let them win. My face was red as a cherry and the tears were flowing like water from a river. I had no clue what to say. My mind went blanker than white sheet of paper yet I felt my heart take control and led my mouth along the path of life. 'Chris, you know I love you…..but really….how could I ever…. say no to you? Of course I do.' I said while chocking back the tears between every few words. I simply fell down beside him and cried his shoulder to let him know all the love I truly had for him and that this was indeed the greatest moment of my life. I couldn't believe it. She said yes. She said YES! This was the greatest day of my life. I just let Tessa cry on my shoulder as it all sank into me. We are engaged. That means soon we will be married. Then we will have a family. I would be Alex, Evan, and Christa's dad. It was all so sudden yet wonderful. Finally it happened. The union of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. It all started off as arguing in PTC to now a marriage. Who would have thought? Certainly not me.
