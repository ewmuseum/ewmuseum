How The Young Become Old: Tales from Hell (Letter 1)

Date: July 4th, 1986 July 4th, 1986 Dear Mom and Dad, You know I’ve been here at camp for a while now and I’m not seeing anything great in it like you had promised me. I may only seem to be ten years old, but this place is a dump. I miss being home and not having to do anything like I do here. On top of having to go to regular classes they’ve assigned me to a drill instructor and he puts me through these trainings every day for hours and hours. I don’t know how much longer I can hold out doing this stuff. I know I was an awful child when I was home, but I’m sorry for all of that. I’ve told you so many times in the past that I could change, but I guess I just pushed you to the limits so much that you and the teachers at school couldn’t handle me any longer. Today is sadder than normal considering it is the Fourth of July and we don’t get to take a break or anything for it. Normal routine is what Mr. Alexander tells us. Hopefully you’re going to be able to come and see me on the next visit day. I miss you both so much and I love you more than you know. Your son, Jesse ----------------------------------------------------------- The day had been exceptionally long, hot, and rainy. I had found myself trailing off from studies throughout the day and the teacher had caught me doing it several time; I was severely disciplined for each time. I don’t know what was the cause for this drifting, but maybe it was the fact that this was going to be my first Fourth without any kind of celebration. I was truly a miserable child, and the day was only going to get worse. Suddenly, the horn sounded signaling for classes to be over and hell was about to begin, or at least that’s what I liked to refer to it as and I had only been here for two days. I had already begun to make my way down the hallway of the study building without even knowing it. I had already fallen into the routine of things; you have to in this environment or you’ll be devoured by not only your drill instructor or the other kids in the camp, the older kids that is. The other kids, there weren’t many of them around my age at this camp, I was a horrible person for the things I had done to deserve my spot in this camp and once the older kids had caught drift of those reasons they made it their main priority to break me in, only I didn’t know of these plans yet; that all comes a little later on in this story. My drill instructor, Major Charles Alexander III; but to us, the students, he was known only as Major or Sir and you addressed him just as if you were serving in the actual military. This place was made for two reasons, to both break the will of a child and ruin his life or to mold them into the future generation of leaders in the country. This was what Major Alexander told the new recruits on our first day; we were later told that one of those things would happen to us, by our peers or in my case just the other students. The major had put out some of the best soldiers America had seen and then again he had also been the reason so many were confined to mental institutions. Being a child of only ten I wasn’t going to let this break my spirit; I was still learning and was forced to push myself to believing that I would be the one pupil that would not only surpass all of Major’s standards, but break that bar and also break his spirit in the process. This was the evil I had inside of me since the time of my birth, and the very thing that had me. Placing my boot out of the study building and onto the soggy ground wasn’t a very delightful thing as the sound of major’s voice began to ring through my ears like shattering glass, “Students! Fall in line!” The hussle was about to be broken out, as I didn’t want to be the last in line today. Just as I hard learned never to wonder off from my studies I had also learned never to be last in line when major began to scream. My eyes grew large after that and I began to run to that line with nothing else on my mind, and being the smallest didn’t make things any easier. Every second I was being passed by several of the older kids not even giving me a second glance as they did. Then it hit me and the fear in my eyes grew even more, I was counting the number of people in line. It was everyone, with the exclusion of one person, and the person was me! I slid into the very last spot at the end of the line and lowered my head as far down as I could bring it, the last thing I wanted was to have to look major in the eyes, that’s where he always got you. “Cadet Ramey!” The sound of major’s voice almost echoed even in the outside environment. My blood went cold at the sound of just that, his voice, speaking my name and I could barely mutter the words that came next, “Yes, major?” “Ramey! Do you realize who was last in line today?!” I knew what was coming next and he was only playing this out as much as he could, trying to break me more and more. “Sir, yes, sir.” Only it didn’t come out the way you would expect it to, my nerves were still broken and my voice was as well. The tears were climbing in my eyes and even though my head was lowered he could smell them on me. The sound of his feet coming slowly towards me, another echoing sound adding to the madness, “You do realize after your little instance of being the last in line for the past two days what will happen to you now?! Do you cadet?!” I began to shake now, I could barely control my breathing as my heart began to race, and the sound of major coming closer and closer worsened it all, “Sir, yes, sir!” “Then until you can get yourself to the standard of the rest of your peers it’s to the hole with you!” The echoing sounds all stopped and began to surround upon two words that took more meaning in my mind. The Hole!
