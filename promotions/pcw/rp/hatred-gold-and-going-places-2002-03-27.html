Hatred, Gold, and going places...

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/03/02 The quest for Gold has always been a major factor in the lives of great men. Ever since such vast pursuits for “gold” have taken place, men have always been in search of some great reward at the end of an immense undertaking. Many great a man has fallen victim to war, and many have lived through to see their ultimate reward at the end of such times. This reward is usually something of great importance, and for some strange reason we usually refer to it as the metaphorical “Gold” of a hard labour. But the quests for Gold can, and have had some devastating effects on the parties taking part in them. As has already mentioned, many men have died in the attempt to salvage the symbolic Gold of their toil, and it is no different wherever you go, be it war, or be it simple hard work. Strange events can occur on these pursuits and many things can come to an abrupt end. This face is no different when we look at the grand quest of one Titu Xansiv, better known to PCW fans around the world as The Pharaoh. In his quest to claim the Television title, or should I say “Akivision” Title, from the Neo Void God Akira Akiyama, Pharaoh has lost a lot of things. Nothing really major happened in his fight against the new evil of Primetime Championship Wrestling until the fateful night of the 24th of March 2002 when two singular events would disrupt his life and threaten all he lived for. Last Sunday on the PCW’s televised primetime show PCW Sunday Sabotage Pharaoh had something taken from him, well to be more precise he had two things taken from him. They weren’t minor things… they weren’t even his property, they were in fact his best friend Carmoon, and his wife Isiy. After a brutal assault on the massive Carmoon Akiyama went as far as to banish the colossal beast into the Void, a dark place of despair and death is imminent to those who enter into its darkness. But that was not enough to satisfy the Voltage fighters hunger for despair and dismay. After the destruction of Carmoon, Akiyama went on to kidnap the Pharaoh’s beautiful wife Isiy Xansiv, transporting her to an unknown destination, most likely of terrifying surroundings. Now Pharaoh has more than the Akivision title to fight for at Spring Fling; for Akiyama proposed a match of dreadful significance. Not only will the TV title be on the line at the PPV, not only will the Godhood of PCW be on the line at PCW’s April Pay Per View, and not only will pure dignity be on the line, but Isiy Xansiv, the most delicate and beautiful of all Immortals beings will be on the line! If Pharaoh cannot defeat the Voltage fighter at spring fling in the Trinity falls match then Isiy will be no more…this is the word of Akira, the neo void God and current Television champion of Primetime Championship Wrestling… Hate has been said to drive people into madness. It is a thing of unimaginable power and it is truly something that people take for granted. Some would go to the lengths of saying Hate drives our world, that hate is the wheel that constantly spins our simple minded planet into what it is today. It has been said that hate for a person is wrong and bad things can happen if one person hates another. They never said that a God couldn’t hate another God…they never said that was wrong… [The scene opens up in what can only be described as a bedroom of some sort, a well furnished place, amazingly decorated and well kept to say the very least. A large, comfy looking, white quilt-covered double bed sits stationary in the centre of the room surrounded by a set of mahogany table-draws combos. Two vast cupboards stand to the far right-hand side of the massive room, and a set of double doors are to be found next to them. The room is in complete darkness. Only a glimmer of light shines in through a crack in the curtains that blank out the sun light from entering the bedroom. Yet none of these items are what we currently look upon, in fact what we gaze upon is not an item of furniture at all. Currently we gaze upon a picture sitting in the middle of the huge bed. Its glass cracked and its picture obscured slightly. But the face that we can see in the silver, shimmering frame is quite easily visible to us as Isiy Xansiv, the wife of The Pharaoh. Suddenly an eerie silence envelops the room …it feels as though we are not alone…and at length our assumption is backed up, for as we stand here alone, as it would seem, a familiar voice breaks the dull silence.] “Gone…everything I had, all gone…” Says the voice slowly and wearily. “But I will have my revenge…” [Suddenly the curtains over the bedrooms window are drawn apart, and the voices face is revealed to us as that of The Pharaoh…Titu Xansiv…] The Pharaoh: Akiyama probably thinks that by kidnapping my wife and putting her freedom on the line at Spring Fling that that is going to dismay me in some way. That this fact is going to put me off of my intentions to become the PCW television champion…well he is sadly mistaken… [Pharaoh pauses.] The Pharaoh: Akira couldn’t be much more wrong, for you see my dear, [Pharaoh turns to the broken picture lying on the bed; shards of sharp glass surround it before Titu brushes them away so that he can pick up the Picture and its shattered frame.] The Pharaoh: I will fight to get you back until my very soul is put to the test of how far it can possibly go! I will not back down from one such as Akira Akiyama. The PCW’s Television champion shall pay for the acts of evil that he has cast upon me, and you… [Once again Titu pauses.] The Pharaoh: Trust me, my love; you shall be free soon enough… [Pharaoh tosses the picture back down onto the bed. Small fragments of glass are thrown aside as the picture hits the duvets surface. Pharaoh sighs and shakes his head before walking towards the opposite side of the room. He gently walks across the floor, not making a sound, and before long the Egyptian God reaches the bedrooms double doors. Slowly he opens them and carefully slides out of the room. We now find ourselves in a long corridor. Many doors are found on the sides of the passages walls, and at the very distant end of the extensive hall a set of steps can be found seemingly leading off downstairs. Pharaoh speaks as he walks towards them.] The Pharaoh: Akiyama shall not be victorious at Spring Fling, I will not hear of it! He and his inferior team of “Celestials” shan’t keep The Pharaoh from rescuing his wife from imminent dismay! It shall not be! I will make sure of it! If Akira thinks that he, and the likes of Jay Sylo can put an end to me, then they are mightily mistaken; I shall rise from the metaphorical ashes and triumph! [Pharaoh sighs.] The Pharaoh: She is the only thing that keeps me going…sometimes I think I could not go on without her, and even though sometimes we have minor arguments it is still she who is and will always be my first and one true love. Some say I am a romancer, yet I would never look upon another woman while my only hope from madness is there to pull me aside from the temptations of this Mortal world! [Pharaoh scowls.] The Pharaoh: Akiyama shall pay for the travesty he has brought upon my head! He will pay dearly! His only chance at playing a mind game with me was to do some sort of evil act upon my wife, and without my knowing he did. I was not about to see the events live that took place on Sabotage on Sunday, if I had have been then none of these sadistic mind games would have taken place… [Pharaoh sighs and clenches his fists.] The Pharaoh: I simply wish I had been there! I should have been there! This is all my damn fault! But Isiy, my one true love, fear not, for I am coming to free you, and I will not rest until I have you in my Godly grasp once again! [Pharaoh finally reaches the top of the staircase and looks down. Before him is a winding mental frame of strong iron, cast in black. He gently makes his way down the stairs until he reaches the bottom to find his mentor Horus waiting for him.] Horus: Are you ready, Titu? The Pharaoh: As ready as I’ll ever be… Horus: Excellent! [Horus motions for Pharaoh to follow him, and our resident Egyptian God does so…] Horus: Our destination shall be kept secret for the time being, Titu…when we get there I’m sure you’ll thank me…on the way we may meet some strange folk that I believe I need to introduce you to, old friend! [Pharaoh looks at Horus with a confused expression.] The Pharaoh: What do mean by that? Horus: You shall see! It is a secret for now! [Pharaoh nods before Horus begins to speak again.] Horus: How are you feeling now? The Pharaoh: Useless! I wish that I could have been there! Horus: But Isiy and Carmoon never told you that they had intentions of going to Sabotage, this is not your fault, believe me! [Pharaoh sighs.] The Pharaoh: I shall have my revenge, for Carmoon soon enough, but I still fear for Isiy’s well-being… Horus: If Akira was to hurt Isiy then the council of the Gods would surly punish the NVG. Don’t worry about her for now…you must concentrate on training yourself to fight the Neo Void God, if you don’t and aren’t prepared for him then you will never set Isiy free from his evil grasp! [Pharaoh nods his head.] The Pharaoh: Then I know what to do…I must defeat Akiyama at his own game. That of the mind! Akira holds both Isiy and his precious Akivision Title at the moment, but on Friday Fusion this week I have my sights set on his title. If I can win that then it means that Akira has something I want, in the form of Isiy and I have something he wants, in the form of the TV title… Horus: I see where you are going with this, my pupil; you always were a wise thinker… [The Pharaoh grins.] The Pharaoh: And I still am… [Horus laughs…] Horus: Yes, yes, now come, we have very little time to spare, Titu…. [The scene begins to fade as Pharaoh and Horus leave the room headed for the Palace’s front doors. But the questions still remains, where has Horus got in mind to take Titu, and what lies ahead for the self proclaimed Diamond King? Soon all the answers will be revealed…]
