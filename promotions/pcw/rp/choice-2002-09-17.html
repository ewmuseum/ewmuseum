Choice

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/17/02 There are paths you choose in life. Sometimes you choose the right path sometimes you chose the wrong one. Each decision you make it alters the next decision you make. If you decide to speed on a rainy day, that could cause an accident. There are so many things in like that you have to worry about. Right now I am in the middle of making a decision like that. The scene opens with Carl Cash driving in an automobile. The PCW camera is on the inside of the car so it can not really tell what kind of car it is. Carl Cash is currently talking on his cellular phone. We pick up only his part of the conversation because you can’t hear the person on the other end of the line. Carl Cash- Where the hell do you want me to meet you? Well first of all what exactly am I getting here, I want to know that first. This better be some good stuff. How much is this going to cost me. Three Hundred dollars, well I am going to have to stop at the bank to pick that up, ill be there in about a half-hour. Carl Cash hangs up the cell phone and continues driving. His car starts to slow because of an oncoming red light. He puts his right turn signal on. He drives no more then three more blocks and he takes a left turn into a parking lot of a bank. He steps outside of his car and walks inside of the bank. He walks up to the front counter and starts to talk to the lady. Bank Woman- Can I help you? Carl Cash- Yes, actually I need some withdrawal slips, can I get them from you? Bank Woman- You sure can get them from me. The bank teller hands Carl Cash a withdrawal slip, Carl Cash walks away from the teller to a big table sitting in the middle of the room with other people writing out things to the bank. Carl Cash rights out a slip so he can withdraw three hundred dollars from his Savings account. Carl Cash walks back up to the bank tellers’ window and hands her the Withdrawal slip. Carl Cash- Here we go. Bank Woman- Will that be all today? Carl Cash- Yes, that’s all I need today. Bank Woman- Large or Small bills? Carl Cash- I want three one hundreds. Bank Woman- Here is one, two and three. Thank you and have a good day. Carl Cash- You too. Carl Cash walks out of the bank and gets back into his car which we now see is a 1983 Ford Thunderbird. He starts the car up and drives out of the bank parking lot. He takes a left and returns to the original street he was on. He once again picks up his Cellular phone. He dials a number and starts to talk. Carl Cash- Hey man, I just got out of the bank and I am on my way. Where do you want me too meet you? There is a pause. Then Cash starts to talk again. Carl Cash- All right Ill meet you at the McDonalds on Regent Street. All right Ill be there in like ten minutes man, see ya then. Later Carl Cash hangs up the phone. He continues driving and all of a sudden a person cuts him off. Cash rolls down the window and starts to yell and he throws an obscene gesture out the window. Carl Cash- You idiot, where did you learn how to drive. I bet you got your licensee from a cracker jack box. All of a sudden there are flashing lights on behind him, and sirens starts to go off. Carl Cash pulls his car over to the side of the road. He quickly grabs his license, registration, and insurance card. The cop is making his way over to the car. Carl Cash’s window is already down. Police Officer- Hello Sir, do you have any idea why I am pulling you over right now? Carl Cash- I believe so, I just gave that person up there the bird, and was yelling at them through my window. Police Officer- Yes, that’s it. Can I see your License, registration, and Insurance card please. Carl Cash hands the officer all three things. The officer walks back to his car. Carl Cash- This is just great, if I get a ticket because of this I am going to be extremely pissed. For real, that guy pulled out in front of me, its not like I was going to speed up follow him and hit him in the face or anything. I would never do anything like that. God, this is so lame. If I get a ticket I am so fighting this. This is not good at all I am supposed to fight BladeD this Friday in Milwaukee. I can’t be getting in trouble. Carl Cash looks at his rear view mirror and sees the police officer walking back toward the car. The Police Officer hands Carl Cash back the three items. Police Officer- Why did you do that anyway? Carl Cash- The guy cut me off, and I know don’t I suppose I over reacted and just let my emotions get in control of me. Police Officer- You have a clean driving record and everything, so this time I will give you a warning, but if I or any of my other co-workers will be forced to give you a ticket, you understand? Carl Cash- Yes I do officer, and thank you very much. It will never happen again. Carl Cash rolls up his window as the Police Officer is walking away. He starts up his car and looks for an entryway to get back on the road. He finds one and enters into traffic again. Carl Cash- Now I am going to be late getting to the McDonalds, and he is going to be very pissed at me. But oh well I have a reasonable excuse, I mean I got pulled over for giving someone the finger, and screaming at them out my window. I am willing to bet that the person didn’t even hear or see me. Carl Cash takes a right and you can see a McDonalds about three blocks ahead. He drives to the McDonalds. He parks his car and notices his friend on the inside. He steps out of the car. Carl Cash- Um, just stay here all right. I will be out in like five minutes. The PCW cameraman listens to Carl Cash. Carl Cash walks into the McDonalds and walks over to where his friend is sitting. Carl Cash hands the man the three hundred dollars and the man gives Carl Cash a big brown McDonalds bag. Carl Cash looks inside the bag and walks back out to the car. Carl Cash- Now this is some sweet stuff, I can’t believe it only cost me three hundred dollars. I mean this is classic. I have to come to this guy more often. Carl Cash has just bought something for three hundred dollars. It is inside a big McDonalds bag, we have no idea what he just bought but stayed tuned tomorrow. We will find out the answer.
