The Clone Wars Part 3: More Stuff Happens

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/02 The camera fades in… A still image of the previous promo is shown, with the Chef crawling up the stairs to safety; or so he thought! Narrator: Hello folks and welcome back to another mind-numbingly stupid Chef promo! When we last left the Chef, he was in a whole heap of trouble, let’s take a watch just to enhance the visual quantity of the promo…wait, I wasn’t supposed to say that, now I’m going to get my handler into some deep shit trouble!!! The scene of the Chef’s imminent capture plays once again… “Hello f*cko!” POW!!! Big B socks a surprised Chef right in the kisser!!! He tumbles down the steps all the way back down to the basement and lands there with a sickening thud… “F*cking sweet as f*ck!” As the whale-like woman retrieves the Chef from the ground, a familiar villain in the Chef promo series casually walks down the stairs to meet his foe. Emeril: Well well well Mr. Chef, it seems we’ve met again, and once again I’ve stolen your woman! BAM!!! Bertha holds the Chef back up. The Culinary Conquistador, bruised and barely conscious, replies… The Chef: Kiss my grits you piece of rancid pork! SMACK!!! Bertha wallops the Chef in the back of the head with a thunderous slap! Emeril: I think you’d better shut that yap of yours or my new LOVER is going to make a pulled meat sandwich out of you…BAM!!! The Chef: MMMMM…pulled pork…does it come with a poppy seed bun? SMACK!!! Emeril: Take him to the kitchen…OF DEATH!!! BAM!!! Bertha drags the Chef away as Emeril laughs maniacally…The scene pauses into a still picture. Narrator: Wow, I’ve still got a job, and we’ve still got a promo to show, on with the next part in our Star Wars title rip-off spectacular!!! The image fades out and fades back into into a moving scene. “BAM!!!” The camera shows Emeril and Bertha dragging a now unconscious Chef into the Kitchen OF DOOM!!! Which is basically just Emeril’s kitchen…WITH AN OVEN OF DOOM!!! It’s actually a normal Frigidaire oven…OF DOOM!!! Narrator: Wait a second; wasn’t this called the Kitchen of DEATH in the last promo?!?! Awww hell, that little remark probably just got me sacked… Awww shit, here comes my pink slip…no wait, it’s yellow! It’s a memo!!!…that says I’m fired…Awww nuts… Back to the promo! The Chef is thrown onto a giant cutting board which lays on a long counter stretching across the Post War Era-styled kitchen with the contemporary black cat wandering eye clock with tail, and the “Green just like my shit after I had a few bowls of Boo Berry Cereal” colored cushions on the chairs… Emeril: Lay him out, BAM!!! It’s time for ol’ Emeril to make a stew…of THE CHEF!!! The culinary evil genius grabs a chef’s knife and begins mincing and cutting carrots, potatoes, celery, and other vegetables found in the basic stew… The Chef: You forgot the bouillon cubes… Emeril: Ah yes, I did…wait a second, you’re supposed to be… TALIBANG!!! The Chef pops Emeril right in the face with a sold right hand. He hops down from the counter and faces his treacherous clone girlfriend… The Chef: Why Bertha!?!? Why!?!? Bertha: Why f*cking what you piece of sh*t @$$ f*cking loser!?!? The Chef: Why did you leave me for him!?!? Bertha: I f*cking never… Before Bertha can complete her sentence though, a black-eyed Emeril leaps over and covers her mouth, shushing her on the spot. Emeril: She left you because I’m got everything a woman wants… The Chef: And what’s that!?!? Emeril: Food… The Chef: Yeah, well I’ve got food! Emeril: Money… The Chef: Yeah, well I’ve got money! Emeril: Do you have IT!?!? The Chef: Are you crazy? Only Ed has IT!!! Emeril: Ok, so maybe we are equal, but I’ve still got your sweetie pie, BAM!!! The glass window of the kitchen shatters, a hippopotamus-like figure flies through the window and lands awkwardly on the floor, causing minor earthquakes to occur in the area. “It’s f*cking go time!!!” The Chef: Bertha!?!? But you’re already…huh!?!? Emeril: HeHeHe…well it looks like I can take out two of my hated rivals now! Even though I secretly still long for the companionship of Clone #1, I’ll just have to make due with #2!!! The Chef: Are you saying you managed to create another clone of Bertha Stewart to serve you!?!? Emeril: Yes…BAM!!! The Chef: And you not only used her to attack me but confuse me into thinking that MY Bertha was the one responsible…? Emeril: Yep, that was the plan…BAM!!! The Chef: You culinary school dropout!!! The Chef charges at Emeril and SPEARS HIM DOWN TO THE KITCHEN FLOOR!!! Bertha Clone #2, defending her master, grabs the Chef by his hair, lifts him up effortlessly and tosses him FACE FIRST INTO THE WALL!!! SMACK!!! Bertha Clone #1: That’s gotta f*cking hurt… #1 engages #2. Bertha Clone #1: You are gonna f*cking pay for that, motha f*cka!!! Bertha Clone #2: You f*cking think so? We’ll f*cking bring that nasty sh*t here f*ckface, let’s f*cking have at it!!! #1 and #2 charge at each other and slam into each other like a couple sumo wrestlers! Both stagger back a charge again, this time #2 leaps up into the air and drops #1 with a LOU THESZ PRESS!!! She hammers on #1 and both begin shouting out various profanities… “F*cking @$$ humper!” “Cock @$$ed b*tch!” “Monkey nuggeting f*ck ho!” “Pansy @$$ed sh*t monger!” “F*cking piece of sh*t @$$ed skank!” The Chef refrains from whaling on Emeril to speak to the feuding Berthas. The Chef: Please guys; could you try and stop the constant cussing, it’s really getting annoying? Emeril: Yeah…BAM!!! Bertha Clone #1: F*cking make me b*tch, the f*cking audience still f*cking love it to sh*tty @$$ mother f*cking death!!! The Chef: (To Emeril) Well I tried… Emeril: It was a good effort…BAM!!! The Chef: Now where was I? Emeril: Um, I believe I was on top of you smacking you in the face… The Chef: Oh, ok…wait a second! …. Emeril: What? The Chef: (Stupidly) Weren’t you holding that rolling pin over there too? Emeril: Yes, yes I was… CRACK!!! The Chef slams the rolling pin into the face of Emeril, causing the wooden kitchen utensil to break in half! The Chef: No soldier of Allah is THAT DUMB!!! The Chef turns around and is startled to see #2 on #1 holding a chef’s knife over her head, ready to drive it into her blubber filled body!!! The Chef: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The scene becomes a still image. Narrator: Ha! I’m still here! You though this would be the final episode? Well wrong! We are going to milk this thing as much as we can! Well, let us see, the Chef is in another pickle right now, he’s probably going to get out, but if you want to find out how…TUNE IN NEXT TIME!!!…SAME CHEF TIME…SAME CHEF CHANNEL!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! The still image fades to black.
