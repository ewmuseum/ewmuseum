Impressions

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Impressions Featuring: Tim Walden Date: September 20, 2002 ...So here I was, back again...almost exactly where I had left off in fact, but though the elements of the game remained the same, the players had all switched positions themselves.... ...Well, better make the most of it. The scene opens on a nondescript hallway, almost like the sort one would see in a hotel, except without the overt glamour, the lavish sort of carpeting replaced by a mostly tiled floor and the walls not as extravagant in their decoration and choice of lighting, almost like a cheap motel with an expensive cleaning staff if anything...The end of the corridor, not too distant but still a good few doors down, is a window to the outside, ground level evident through the clear glass to an late morning sky, the slivers of blue squeaking between the doorway and the buildings nearby as if to exist as a curtain rod, which one could just pull the shutters of the rest of the day across with one quick motion, while the odd passerby was disrupting the view on the outside, it suddenly sank out entirely but to peek around the frame that had passed into the hall... Hmm, seems like a pretty sharp place, hope their reporting is up to snuff....Let's see...Third door on the right....1....2....Here we are...WHAT IN THE BLUE HELL?!!? Tim looks into the room, dumbfounded as the camera shifts around him to show the contents of the room..It is an office, rather much like his own in fact, outside of the....er....breathtaking interior decoration, the purplish hues visible on the walls and the green-and-white checkerboard floor scheme just vibrating with energy, desk covered with a mix of stacked papers and goofy ornaments in the 'executive toy' range, tipping back and forth, creating cute little patterns in some cases...Disturbingly perky, to say the least..This effect is further compounded and possibly confounded by the items around the desk..A maple cabinet behind and to the left of the desk sits open, holding..what appear to be videotapes..And most unnerving of all, the purple walls are difficult to notice..Due to the sheer surface area taken up around the room by posters, a wide variety of shapes and sizes of various stars of the wrestling world as he looks across...Jeremy Howard...Amy Campbell...Brock Newbludd...Mike Marvelous...Anarchy...Timo Bolamba...Frisco...Brandi...Howard Porter...Xavier Kannon and Ellie~!...Tempest...Alexander Miyagawa...Anarchy again...Amy Campbell again...Meanstreak...An autographed picture of Romana Dumetruscu...Anarchy for a third time...JJ......Wait a second...JJ? All right, the guy working here is just plumb loco. This is freakin' creepy...I've heard of the sort of oddballs who work here, but I never thought they were this far out of whack...I feel like I stepped into an insane basement shrine thing her-- Voice from behind: Heyzzies! Tim: AAAAHH!! Tim hops slightly and abruptly, jolted out of his wits by the peppy greeting as he whirls around in self-defense, only to see the man before him...Surprisingly, the first notable thing is that the man is actually a little taller than Walden, though his wearing a bright red T-shirt and baggy black shorts, hair mildly disheveled and a goofy grin on his face.. Tim: So...you would be... Guy: Byron Calver, Wrestling Tattler reporter! You may remember me from a couple of the PCW shows just before you got back.. I so cannot believe this....How the hell did this guy get out of the cellar and land a job like this?! Tim: Yeah, yeah, seen it, now let's just start with the interview... Byron: All righty! Now, let me get the tape recorder running, and we'll start...Hiya, wrestling fans, this is Byron Calver for the Wrestling Tattler, and I have a real surprise for you this week as I've managed to get an interview with one of the hottest comeback stories in the business right now, none other than one-time part-owner of PCW who has come back strongly to join up with Xavier Kannon and his evil Front Office group after a several-month hiatus from the federation he has called home for the better part of a year, Tim Walden! Tim: Gee, that has to be the nicest introduction I've gotten in a while.. Byron: Hahaha, but seriously, Tim, how does it feel to be back in the ring again? How do you THINK it feels to be back from that hellhole Negative-Five Star Wrestling?! I worked my ass off only to get shafted by that little jerk Orion and his idiotic wasting of my talent with worthless fights with Carl Blair, then being told to let Meanstreak walk all over me entirely YET AGAIN or be sued over breach of contract?! I came there in hopes they'd have the sense to let me have my spotlight, to get my deserved fame, and look what they gave me! Tim: Well, generally I guess I just wasn't enjoying FSW, and it was time for a change, and when Xavier asked me if I could come up to PCW, I came to Paul, I told him 'This isn't working out, I don't enjoy it', and he said 'Meh, you weren't of much value to us anyway.' Byron: Well, THAT was brilliant. What about the Front Office thing here, how does it feel to be the financial arm of the group? Tim: Well, I'd have to say this, it's good to see that the owner trusts me enough to get the job done that he has me in such a valuable position, and I'm feeling good about how it's gone so far..Well, except for that cigarette thing at Turmoil, but I know well enough that the smoking tax'll hurt that little Southern belle more than any mere foot pain can get at me. Byron: Any thoughts about the match you have tonight with Kramer Wild Wing, your first since coming back to PCW not counting that little fake-out World Title thing? Any thoughts on whether I can beat a guy who lumbers about with a bucket of chicken?! You'd think all the grease in that stuff would've gotten to him by now, 'And your winner by heart attack...' Tim: It's rather simple, really..Kramer represents a lot of the past of PCW, he used to be big news when he won the TV Title from the HIT, the Cruiserweight Title from the HIT....Did he ever actually win anything from anyone besides the HIT?! I mean, besides the power of the ten-piece bucket, what has he got? Just an average bit of wrestling talent, and it isn't enough to even avoid tapping out to the aforementioned woman..Besides, that chicken's gotta have clogged his arteries with grease, it's obviously affecting his wrestling. Byron: You've really been coming out there and boosting the Front Office domination of PCW lately, and it was no less evident than Turmoil, where you not only instituted new taxes but also helped Duke Williams send Miyagawa to what very well may have been his death..Any guilt about that? *sigh*...Why does he have to play the guilt card?! Does he not have any respect for my conscience to go and pressure me to feel any worse than I already would for killing the guy??? Well, I think it's about time that we kick some of that emotional dirt back.. Tim: You know what? Honestly, I used to idolize Miyagawa as a youngster, he was one of my influences back when I was going into PWA 17 months ago, but I've outgrown it..He was getting in our way, and we had to dispatch of him somehow, wish it wasn't this way, but maybe if we're lucky he'll have only been severely injured by that compactor and unable to wrestle again..I really haven't heard anything much now, but I think that should kill all of the useless Asylum's momentum. Byron: Speaking of the 'useless' Asylum as you described it, what exactly is your impression of them as a group? And it's time to go in..... Tim: I know where you're coming from, Calver...You're still annoyed that I'm going against them, those guys whose disturbed prank antics somehow got you and that anorexic Dumbatrescu together, and I think you're pissed off at me here...You're pissed off because I ran in and took out Anarchy not once but twice, totally ruined your pathetic happiness during that Cruiserweight Title match. You think I can't tell from the posters you've got hanging all over here that you're too blinded by your own infatuations with the females to see things impartially! ...For the kill! HA! Take THAT, you little basement-dwelling JJ fanatic! Byron: HEYZZIES! THAT'S JUST PLAIN WRONGZZIES, AND BESIDES JUST NOT TRUE!! I'll have you know that there are posters of every Front Office member in this office as well! Tim: Oh, there aaaare? And where's mine, then?! Byron: ..Well, I think I put it away in the desk a few weeks after you got fired. Tim: Suuuuuuuuure... Byron: Really, we don't have to continue this interview if you don't want to stop making up things about me. Ugh, stupid little brat, whoever said the media was honest? Tim: Okay, okay, have it your way, I'll back off.....zzies. Tim bursts into a bit of a chuckle, which quickly intensifies to a guffaw... Byron: All right, that's it! I didn't want to have to do this, but I'll just have to cut the interview short...No hard feelings, all rightzzies? Oh God, this is too much...The kid is absolutely insane, but it's freakin' hilarious to listen to... Tim: O-o...Okay, I'll g-*giggle*-get out of here, you JJ-happy freak! Byron: NOW!! Tim: All right already! Walden walks back out of the room, shutting the door behind him, as he continues down the hallway Tim: Thank God...I can't believe I got stuck getting interviewed by that nutcase, I swear that boy needs to go to a nice psychiatrist before he ends up with restraining orders out the wazoo.... Tim walks down the corridor, and soon disappears back around the corner from which he came.. ...Meanwhile, back in the office, Byron pushes himself back from the desk while pressing a button.. Byron: Phew...That was so aggravating.... Byron looks up at the now-closed door, and the two objects upon it...Firstly, a flat-screen television showing a wrestling match, notably Flaymin Aymin versus El Diablo...And secondly, a Tim Walden poster. Byron: Sorry Timmy ol' pal, but you got in the way of my entertainment...Couldn't just tell Tim Walden I was too busy watching a Flaymin Aymin match to talk to him, goodness knows he'd've kicked my ass....Better he think I'm some nutcase with a JJ fanaticism and a hard-on for women wrestlers than to go getting into some stupid fight and killing my credibility. The scene fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
