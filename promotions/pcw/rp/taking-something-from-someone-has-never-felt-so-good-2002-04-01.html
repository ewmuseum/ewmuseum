Taking something from someone has never felt so good!

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/04/02 Taking something from someone has never felt so good! [[PCW Friday Fusion, the 30th of March, the day when things changed in the life of Titu Xansiv forever…that fateful night The Pharaoh was set to take on Badge, Akiyama and Clyde in a four-way dance for the PCW Television Title. After a savage beating at the hands of DA MAN and his good friend, Badge, Pharaoh and Akiyama were left on their own to fight it out for the ‘Akivision’ title. A long and strenuous battle took place giving the fans a glimpse at what they can expect to see in the Trinity match at Spring Fling. But by surprise it wasn’t Akiyama who came out of Fusion with the TV championship as it had originally begun…oh no! The 30th of March was The Pharaoh’s night and just like the Neo void God took Titu’s wife Isiy, Pharaoh took Akiyama’s TV title! Now The Pharaoh goes in with an edge over his nemesis, Pharaoh possesses the TV title and Akiyama bears Isiy in a temporary void. Who will win what at Spring Fling? Only time will tell us the answers to these illusive questions!]] ..::The scene opens up in what appears to be a restaurant. Not your typical grimy and plainly unhygienic restaurant, but one fit for the higher classed people in the world. A place to eat fresh food of great excellence and a restaurant fit for the greatest of kings::.. …::The distinct sound of champagne glasses been clattered together producing an amazing musical noise of brilliance rings out in the air followed closely by the sound of divine laughter::.. ..::The restaurant is pretty much packed and as we look around to see many rich men and woman enjoying their seemingly expensive meals two men stand out to us in particular. As we close in we find one of them to be PCW’s very own TV champion, The Pharaoh. Titu wears simplistic attire composed of a long white loincloth, apparently made of the finest silk and a large selection of gleaming bangles and rows of expensive jewellery::.. ..::The shot closes in on the face of Titu Xansiv and quickly the second man with him, or should I say second god with him, is revealed as his mentor, Horus. Pharaoh sits behind a vast, well-polished table with his newly won PCW television Title hanging loosely over his right shoulder. Pharaoh grins smugly as a waiter wearing a red and black tuxedo rears up to the table::.. Waiter: Hello sirs, can I please to your orders? The Pharaoh: Well to be perfectly honest, my friend and I haven’t truly looked over the possible foods your restaurant has to offer yet…so please, reframe yourself from asking such stupid questions like that when you know damn well we have just arrived! ..::Horus gives Pharaoh a quick glance and smiles. The waiter seems taken back and splutters slightly as Pharaoh sits back smugly in his seat::.. The Pharaoh: You may leave now! ..::The waiter hangs his head in shame and slowly slumps away from the godly pair. Horus chuckles to himself as Pharaoh smiles proudly::.. Horus: Well…you certainly showed him! The Pharaoh: Yes…yes I did! Just like Akiyama, I gave him an ass whipping that he’ll never forget! Only his was verbal, Akira received a physical ass kicking from everyone’s favourite Egyptian God this past Friday on PCW fusion!! ..::Pharaoh laughs before reaching down onto the table’s surface, picking up a red leather-clad menu::.. The Pharaoh: Now, let us see what this marvellous place has to offer in the form of food to us divine beings! ..::Horus nods before picking up a menu for himself. The two look through the thin books pages and at length Horus speaks::.. Horus: Have you begun to contemplate your training for Spring Fling? …::Slowly Titu looks up from the menu::.. The Pharaoh: I see no great reason to do so; after all I have already defeated the inferior rat, Akira Akiyama! Training won’t be necessary in my opinion, yet if you think I would benefit from working on my move set a little then fair enough. Horus: You have a good point, Titu. Yes, you have defeated Akira, and in doing so you have taken what is most valuable from his side- ..::Pharaoh grins and pats the TV title gently::.. Horus (continuing): -but by taking a small amount of time to train I think you will help you to up your performance at Spring Fling! The Pharaoh: That I guess is true, and if I am to reclaim my wife I shall need to be at my best…but the psychology for our match is already drawn at a half! Horus: What do you mean? The Pharaoh: Think about it, Horus! Now that I hold the TV title I have something that Akira wants…he holds Isiy, and of course I want her. The mind games are drawn at one a piece and Akiyama cannot possibly take anything else from me that I wish more than Isiy! ..::Horus looks on, clearly intrigued by his pupil’s words::.. Horus: You have brought this theory up once before, Titu, when you didn’t claim the TV title as your own. It is a good philosophy I will admit. In simplistic terms you mean that now it isn’t as though Akiyama has everything you want; now both of you have something that each other yearns! The Pharaoh: Exactly! Yet there is something else on the line in our match… ..::Pharaoh stares darkly at Horus and grimly the Egyptian God of the sky glares back::.. Horus: Your godhood… ..::Pharaoh nods in agreement::.. The Pharaoh: If I were to lose that fall then I would be stripped of the name “God”, no longer would I be able to call myself an immortal… ..::Horus cuts Pharaoh off in mid sentence::.. Horus: Not true, my friend! The Council of the Gods, led of course by Zeus, only have the power to take away your Godhood, not the fact that you are immortal; you have escaped death, Titu, you are and always will be immortal! ..::Pharaoh grins::.. The Pharaoh: Are you sure? Horus: As sure as Snow is white, my friend! The Pharaoh: This is good news indeed! Yet my Godly powers would be taken from me; no longer a God… ..::Pharaoh sighs while contemplating the loss of such a importance as his godhood::.. Horus: But you will not necessarily lose that fall, Titu. The Pharaoh: Yes, but what if I do…I hate to think of losing my godhood, how embarrassing that would truly be! Horus: I suggest that you reframe from thinking like that; it will only divert your true aim for Spring Fling…rescuing Isiy… The Pharaoh: Yes, you are more than right, Horus… ..::Horus turns back to his menu and looks it over again::.. Horus: I think I’ll order some caviar to start off, and then the peppered chicken and salad for seconds… The Pharaoh: A good choice…I think I’ll have the same… ..::Pharaoh raises his hand in the air and clicks his fingers summoning the waiter from earlier on::.. Waiter: Are you two ready to order? ..::Pharaoh sighs::.. The Pharaoh: You aren’t very intelligent are you? ..::The young waiter, somewhere in his early twenties takes a step back in shock from the sarcastic Pharaoh::.. The Pharaoh: Now I wouldn’t call you over if my friend and I weren’t ready to order would I? ..::Pharaoh, ignoring the waiter, turns to Horus::.. The Pharaoh: Damn Mortals! ..::Pharaoh shakes his head in disbelief::.. The Pharaoh: Sometimes they remind me of Akiyama; so stupendous! Akiyama knows little of where he’s coming or where’s he’s going. I sometimes wonder why I even have to feud with him over MY television title. He simply isn’t worth it. The idiot thinks that by having Jay Sylo as the special guest referee in our match is going to faze me…how un-intellectual does he think I am? Horus: Very, obviously… The Pharaoh: Well he couldn’t be more wrong! I took his television title for a reason, not to spite him! With this title by my side I have a weapon against him in our battles! The fans now see me as the dominant man in our warfare! On Fusion I beat him fair and square in the ring…and I’ll give him his respect; he put up a good fight, but in the end the better God won! The only thought I have on my mind now is Isiy! She’s all that matters now! I would even willingly give my godhood to have her back… ..::Pharaoh is suddenly cut off by the waiter::.. Waiter: I’m sorry, Sir, but could you please give me your orders so that I can go and tell the chefs to prepare your food… ..::Obviously agitated by the waiter’s interruption, Titu glares up at the young man angrily::.. The Pharaoh: How dare you interrupt me, especially when I was talking about my abducted wife! I’ll tell you our orders, but only so that it will speed up the time in which you take your leave our company! Two dishes of caviar for starters…and to finish off we’ll have two peppered chickens and two portions of salad siding…now get out of here ya’ mug! Waiter: Before I go, will you be drinking anything? The Pharaoh: Ah yes! A good point! A bottle of your best champagne! ..::The waiter thanks the two gods and walks away::.. The Pharaoh: Isiy shall be set free and that I promise! Akiyama has held my wife long enough in his void prison…at Spring Fling Akiyama shall be defeated three times, first for the rights to be called a God, secondly to retain MY freshly won TV title, and thirdly for my wife…Isiy… ..::Pharaoh looks down at the red leather-clad menu in his hands. Horus looks on and sees that the tension is building in him; slowly his grip on the menu begins to increase::.. Horus: On a lighter not, Titu, congratulations on winning the TV title, old friend! Even though you say that you won it for a reason I think you still deserve the congratulations that you are yet to receive! So well done! ..::Pharaoh looks up from the menu and forces a grim smile::.. The Pharaoh: Yes, thank you… ..:: Pharaoh pauses::.. The Pharaoh: And you can congratulate me when I get Isiy back too… ..::The scene fades to black::..
