A Warrior

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-20-02 Atop the cheap mattress and the rusty bed of a hotel room, a warrior sat, contemplating his life. In his hands he clutched a calendar. Nothing special about this… or was there? Yes, indeed there was, as this calendar marked the two-month anniversary since this warrior’s legend began to grow. Two months since his life was changed forever. He remembered the night clearly, as if it had happened only hours ago. The meeting. His archenemy had come to the alley, just as the warrior had asked, and soon they decided to put aside their differences to concentrate on the task at hand. Three young wrestlers… that was all he needed. Three unsuspecting men that would be taken under the guidance of the two former foes. “They are nothing. No better than any other fool. But these wrestlers have one advantage: training by us. They are nothing now, but that simply means we can have our way with them; mold them into the perfect warriors.” The warrior remembered his exact words. He remembered how he had come up with the idea, and why he had set the plan into action. Trigger had everything; or so it seemed from a glance. A wonderful career, all the money he needed, and a name that was synonymous with power. He was a warrior… but that was not enough. He knew that despite what others said, legends do die, and soon his would as well. And so this warrior took action. Three young men would soon be molded into three wise warriors. They would carry on his legend… they would carry on his life. At first things started off just as Trigger had hoped. Strict training and fierce punishments for insubordinance started to turn the souls of these Warriors In Training into rock. Then something unexpected happened. Trigger began to feel for his students, seeing them more as children. He was the past and they were the future, a tie that bound them as family. It seemed even as if Lance Minion, better known as The Insane One, PoGo, and Trigger’s bitter enemy of many years, was starting to bring kindness to his normally bitter heart. Things had been going great… but that is not always a good thing. In all of Trigger’s life, things never came easy to him. It was always through pain, agony, and suffering that he achieved success. Burrowed deep down inside him was knowledge of misfortune to come. And come this misfortune did. No more than a week ago, an event from the warrior’s recent past caught up with him, dragging he and his good times down into the gutter. When he had committed the crime, the American legal system was not a worry of Trigger’s, knowing that the evil deed was only a punishment to straiten up a WIT (Warrior In Training). But whether it be life reeking vengeance, or simply luck at it’s downside, Trigger was in trouble, and nothing he could do would save him now. Even worse in the mind of the Infinite Warrior was the fact that he had yet to tell Minion and the WITs of his arrest. Week-long disappearances were common from Trigger, but wait much longer and he knew they would suspect the worse. It was time to tell them. It was time for Trigger to face the truth. Rounding the corner of the block at nearly 10:00 at night, Trigger saw that the abandoned shop stood the same as it had always been. He walked, looking down, with the increase of debris guiding him nearer to the destination headed for. Tilting his head up, the Infinite Warrior saw that even in the dark, the shop’s grotesqueness was recognizable. Paint peeled down in layers, revealing the rotting wood beneath. Thick graffiti defaced the entire front wall, rendering each symbol meaningless. The only thing worse than the site was the repulsive odor, attacking the nostrils and stunning the senses. Trigger was not concentrating on its hideousness this evening, however, as he was there for a different purpose. The Insane One’s car was not parked outside. That was a good thing, for Trigger did not know if he had the courage to relay his tragedy in front of another warrior. Chills ran down his spine as the Infinite Warrior found himself face to face with the door of the abandoned shop, and his throat closed up as he turned the knob and entered. In any other situation, panic would be found nowhere in Trigger’s appearance, but this was different. He had shamed in the past, but this was the present. There were three men inside this shop that looked up to the Infinite Warrior. And now he had to let them down. He walked across the empty main room, eyeing nothing suspicious. It was the door on the far wall that would lead to more nervousness. That door led to the WITs’ Quarters. Standing outside the entrance, Trigger took a moment of hesitation to regain his composer; back to the feelingless individual these trainees had come to respect. In one motion he found his courage and entered the room with utter swiftness, where he found WITs 1, 2, and 3 staring back at him, wide-eyed. “They know something,” thought Trigger to himself, “I can see it in their eyes! Nevertheless, I must tell them now. Backing out is not an option on the road of life, and nor is it an option here.” He cleared his throat, and began; the WITs hanging on to his every word as they always had done before. “WITs… at times I have been exceedingly stern, even harsh on you. But all in all, the one attribute you can always find in me is a brutal honesty. It is now that I must call upon this attribute, as I have something to tell you.” The WITs stared blankly back at him. There was a long silence, before finally the quick and energetic WIT spoke up. “We already know, Trigger, sir. Minion told us this morning.” Trigger was flabbergasted. It was a shock alone that The Insane One knew, but even more than he had already told the WITs. Although his character was hard to determine, it didn’t seem like something Minion would do without first consulting the Infinite Warrior. Perhaps the news the WITs knew was not the news Trigger was prepared to tell. “What…What did he tell you?” A gleam came across the face of WIT 2, the same one that had answered previously. “That you got hired to PCW, of course!” The Infinite Warrior’s jaw seemed to drop three feet. PCW? Primetime Championship Wrestling? The same Primetime Championship Wrestling that Trigger had been training to get into for months? This certainly was a change from the grim reply he was fearfully awaiting! Quite suddenly, Trigger became aware of the eyes glued upon him. The third WIT sensed something was not right. “What’s wrong? You don’t seem too happy.” “Oh… I am… but there is something else I need to tell you all.” The Infinite Warrior gulped, feeling the panic sweep over him, and finally began. “More than a month ago, I committed an act that although intended as a punishment, was perceived as criminal behavior. WIT 3 knows all too well how I locked him into the trunk of a car, and drove him off the side of a bridge. It was a cruel method to get my point across, but I cannot change the past, and so I must live with the fact that I did something wrong. Now, it is time that I receive my punishment. Last week I was arrested on accounts of first-degree assault, and attempted murder. This I no doubt deserved, but even so has been very hard on me. Soon I will be put on trial, where I must answer for what I did. If I am proven guilty, I could spend a long time in prison…” Trigger droned off, unable to finish. The excitement in the WITs’ faces drooped to heavy frowns. Finally Trigger had accomplished his career goal, and another roadblock stood in the way. WIT 3 felt especially bad, seeing as it was his own talking back that had led to the punishment, which had now led to this. But no matter who was to blame, this was a terrible occurrence to befall the Infinite Warrior. Trigger saw the hurt in the eyes of the WITs. Although he had done something wrong, and was not proud of his actions, he knew that he had to stand tall for his pupils. He would not let them make the same mistakes he did. “Do not fret, my Warriors In Training. It is only I who committed the crime, and therefore only I who shall pay. You have nothing to be afraid of, and should continue your lives without hesitation. I have things much rougher, as of late. Things may not work out in my favor, and there is nothing I can do to prevent that. But even knowing all of this, I am going to continue my life as I have always lived it, understanding that each day could be my last. I have not been on trial yet; I have not been convicted... I have not yet been doomed. Miracles do happen, and although I mustn’t count on one to save me, I can rest assured that my soul will not parish. This is life. It is not simple, it is not easy. If you want the benefits it offers, you must put as much effort into it as you can possibly muster. I have been giving life all of my effort since I became a warrior, and you three must as well. I am not dead; nor are any of you, but tomorrow could be different. Live life to the fullest, my WITs, and the fullest will come back to you in return.” With that, Trigger grabbed a chair from against the wall, and proceeded to sit. Here he stay, unmoving, for a long, long time. Unmoving as well were the WITs, who through all of their trauma and fear knew that they had been taught a valuable lesson, and could only wish the best for their beloved mentor.
