Dude I so rule!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6--19-03 ~ The scene opens up to Ganker Ransolver two days after his second PCW victory. Ganker is shown in the local bar drinking at the counter a bottle of Corona. Soon enough Ganker stands up and smacks the bottle off the counter. As the bottle makes contact with the dirty floor it shatters in millions upon millions of pieces. Ganker grabs his leather jacket from the counter and slips into it as he then leaves the bar. Ganker turns to his left and begins to walk down the sidewalk, placing his hands into the pockets of his leather jacket. Ganker comes to another bar, but just stands outside in front of it as he takes his hands out of his pockets and runs them through his long blonde hair. Ganker smiles as he pulls out a wad of cash form his pocket. Ganker enters the bar and walks over to a pool table as he grabs a stick on the way. Ganker sits in a darkened corner with the pool stick in his left hand. Ganker stares around the bar to notice it is mainly occupied by the same gang, those who aren’t in the gang are beaten until they ‘decide’ to leave. Soon enough the leader of this biker gang approaches Ganker and Ganker just simply picks some dirt out of his finger nail and flicks it on the man.”May I help you gentlemen?”~ The biker looks at Ganker and then cracks all the knuckles on both of his hands.”Wow, you can crack your damn knuckles!” (sarcastically) “Should I be impressed?””No son, you should be filled with fear! You have just entered the Eagle’s Nest!””Cool, maybe I can cut off a talon and sell it on E-Bay and make a few bucks.””I see you’re a smart ass.””I’ve never considered my ass smart dude, but then again if you’re comparing it to you and your friends then I guess it is.””You have a death wish or something kid?””Maybe if I was going to die, but honestly I know I’m not. You see dude I wrestle for PCW and if you so much as lay a finger on me I will show you why I’m 2-0, so how about we play a little game of pool instead.”(laughs) “You and me kid? You honestly think you can out shoot me?””I’ve shot a little stick back in Seattle, I’m pretty sure I can beat you, but incase by some damn miracle I do lose to your pea brain, I’ll leave your little damn bar. However if I win then you’ll fork over every damn cent your gang has on them.””How about just $20.00 a ball?”~ Ganker nods as he walks over and points to the bartender to rack up the balls and get ready the best table.”Since I’m going to beat you, I’ll let the loser go first.”(laughing subtly) “It’s so kind that you let the damn winner go first.”~ Ganker goes first and knocks the 6 balls in the six holes.”I guess you owe me boy.”(surprised) “How’d you do that? You cheated didn’t you?””If you call cheating pure skill with the stick, then yea.”~ Ganker collects his money and exits the bar. Ganker continues to walk down the street with his hands in his leather jacket. Ganker eventually comes to his hotel and enters. However instead of going up to his room he stops at the lobby and sits on a black couch, watching guests come and go as they please. Ganker is soon enough approached by some PCW jobber who looks a bit angry at the sight of seeing Ganker Ransolver. Ganker looks over at him smiling a cocky smile at him.”How can an asshole like you be doing better than me! I worked forever in wrestling school to get to where I am and here you are just coming off of your friend’s contract he handed to you!””Dude, it’s a little thing called talent. You know dude I think I do recall watching a match of yours and you really don’t have any ‘talent’ dude. So you wanna know why an asshole like me makes it? Cause I have more skill in my damn pinky then you ever could imagine of having! Sure you worked your ass off, but that’s your problem not mine! Now listen boy, get the heck out of my sight, your smell is disgusting me.”~ The jobber raises his first to Ganker.”Yeah dude, go ahead and hit me, I dare you! However you best hope it knocks me out so I don’t get back up and kick the holy heck out of you!”“I would hit you, I really would, but you’re not worth my time or effort.””Yeah right dude, get the heck away now, you disgust me.”~ The jobber lowers his fist as he turns and walks away from Ganker Ransolver. Ganker eventually stands and when he does he is spotted by a PCW cameraman. Ganker signals for him to come over as he sits back down. The PCW cameraman rushes over and sits down a reasonable distance and turns on his camera aimed at Ganker.”Listen, I’m doing you a favor by allowing you to record me, it’s not everyday yours truly speaks his mind to a damn television camera. Now listen up slappy, I have a #1 contendership to the cruiserweight championship. This is something I’m not going to let slip through my fingers. This is one thing that Hank couldn’t get through his head, something that cost him each and every time under his PCW contract, but unlike Hank, I will not let any opportunity slip through my fingers. I have this shot to be something not even being here for two weeks, jus’ barely in fact. However all I have to do is go through Menolly Xenos, Manuel Labor, and Matt Jackson. All I have to do is pin one of the four of those participants, which should be easy enough seeing as how I am Ganker Ransolver and each one of them is not! That and the fact Menolly has already felt what it’s like to step into the ring with Ganker Ransolver! Menolly has already faced what the ‘Vengeance’ is all about! I’ve proven myself time and time again and I feel I am more ready than ever to go into Friday Fusion and walk out the number one contender to the PCW Cruiserweight Champion! I’m a former RSE Cruiserweight Champion! However that’s beside the point, what is the point is my match tomorrow on Friday Fusion where I will be the victor. Then again you have people like Manuel Labor who say they are the superior man of the match, but what does he know? He’s a freaking loser! Sure he’s the Extreme Champion, but heck how hard is that dude? Heck my tag team partner Hank~! Won that title! So as much as Manuel Labor wishes to believe he’s something special, he’s really not. Sure he’s had his victories in the past, but hell he’s never faced me! He’s never stepped into the ring wit’ the man who lays down the ‘Vengeance’ upon each and every single opponent! Ganker Ransolver has Manuel Labor’s number dude, and in the words of my former tag team partner, That’s A DAMN Fact! Now dude, get the hell out of my damn space, you’re sitting too close as it is!”~ The cameraman gets up to leave, but Ganker pulls him back down and stands up.”No dude, you’re so pathetic you’re going to stay here while I get up and leave you.” (laughs) “Sucker!”~ Ganker heads up to his hotel room where his girlfriend waits for his return as the cameraman sits back and places his camera next to him.”What an asshole.”~ The scene cuts off to black.
