NedRap: Writing

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Durrr [Our scene slowly fades in inside the now familiar apartment of Andy Murray and Rab McLean, PCW’s resident Neds. In the lounge area, Mr. Murray sits on one of the brand-new grey leather chairs recently purchased from a pawn shop, you know those ones with the flip-out footrests? Them. Andy sits hunched over a piece of paper resting on his knee, he appears to be writing something. He is today wearing a visually stimulating yellow & white Adidas tracksuit/shell suit, and as-per-usual, his black hair is buried under a thick layer of greasy hair gel. Rab sits on the other chair, flipping impatiently through all 5 of the channels on the television several times, in true Ned style. Rab is wearing a black & white Kappa shell suit, his brown greasy hair is today without gel.] Rab: Pish … Pish … Boggin’ … Crap … Pish … FITBA’! [Indeed, Rab has found a game of football to watch. It may be Hereford Town against Accrington Stanley, but it’s fitba’ all the same.] Rab: Eh? [Watching 22 unknown players playing in a 500 capacity stadium that is not even half-full seems to have shell-shocked Rab a little, as he scratches his head in confusion. Of course, our Nedly heroes are used to watching the likes of David Beckham and Henrik Larsson, not Phil, James, Jack and Jack’s mate from the Bookie’s. Rab starts to flip through the channels again, looking for something else to watch. Andy raises his head from his writing.] Andy Murray: Ho min, what ye doin’ ya dobber? Rab: Lookin’ for sumthin’ tae watch on this TV pish min. Andy Murray: How are ye nae watchin’ the fitba’? Rab: Cos it’s nae Rangers min. Andy Murray: Ah. That’s pure boggin’ by the way. Rab: Aye, it’s some chbues fae Englund or somethin’. Andy Murray: Pish. [Andy lowers his head to continue writing, but is quickly interrupted by Rab.] Rab: What ye up tae? [Andy looks up.] Andy Murray: Ah’m writin’ this NedRap min, but it’s pure hard, I huv nae written much. Rab: How don’t ye get one of they Uprising wogs tae write it? They’re rap-chubes, aren’t they? Andy Murray: Aye min, but they’re a bunch ‘o dobbers min. Jordan X Shaboom looks like that Boxin’ fella, Don King an awl, which is real pish. Rush Mason is a ugly bampot who looks like a Hamburger, an’ Ah’m gonnae huv tae pure hammer this Tug Boat dobber on Fusion. Rab: Tug Boat? Ye mean Thug Boat? Andy Murray: Tug Boat, Thug Boat, Thug roC, same thing, ken min? Rab: Aye. Andy Murray: And they pure speak stupit an’ awl, cannae understand a word they say, stupit chubes. [Rab nods, and returns to his TV browsing, as Andy starts writing once more. After about 7 seconds, Rab gets bored, and hits the “Standby” button on the remote control, the television flashes off. Rab rises from his chair.] Rab: This is pure borin’ min, Ah’m off tae see Barry, wan’ tae come wi’ me? [Andy pauses for a moment, to decide.] Andy Murray: In’t it a bit far away? Rab: Aye, but he can help ye wi’ the NedRap min. Andy Murray: Briwliant. [Andy rises to his feet, stuffing his pen and paper into his pocket, and walks with Rab across to the door leading out of the apartment. Rab clasps the door handle and turns it, however to door does not open. Rab shakes the door a few times, still it wont open.] Rab: Pish min, the door is’nae openin’. Andy Murray: Gonnae gee’sa shot min. [Rab moves over, Andy grabs the handle, pulls, still it wont open. Andy bangs on the door a few times, before he accidentally catches the latch with his hand, the door swings open.] Andy Murray: Hoho. Rab: Briwliant. [And so, closing the door behind them, Andy and Rab set off down the corridor, doors at either side leading into different apartments, funnily enough. After passing three doors at either side, Andy and Rab stop at one of the many doors, concluding their “long” journey. Rab rests his hand against the wall, catching his breath.] Andy Murray: That walk’s pure long and awl. Rab: **Wheeze** … Aye min. Andy Murray: We need tae get a Corsa for this pish … [Rab removes his hand from the wall, breath caught, as Andy knocks on the door twice. After a short delay, a Nedly voice can be heard from beyond the door. It is of course, Andy & Rab’s pal, Barry.] Barry: WHO ARE YE?! Andy Murray: Ho min, it’s me an’ Rab. Barry: Oh, pure, mad, mental min! [Barry can be heard trying to open the door, obviously having the same trouble as Andy and Rab were having. Barry slams on the door a couple of times, still doesn’t work.] Barry: The door’s nae openin’. Andy Murray: Punch ‘at metal thing ‘en. Barry: Awright muckah. [BANG!] Barry: AYYAA! That’s pure sore min … [Slowly the door slides open, as Barry, another Ned, stands clasping his fist from where he punched the latch. Beyond him, we can see an apartment which is nearly identical to Andy & Rab’s, minus the new grey chairs, and replace them with horrible brown couches.] Rab: Awright muckah? Andy Murray: Wha gwan? Barry: Just bangin’ min … Come in by the way. Andy Murray: Cheers. [Andy and Rab step through the door, and into the apartment of Barry. Rab looks around in utter amazement, even though the place is just like his own apartment. Barry, wearing traditional Ned attire of a blue & white Nike shell suit, closes the door, as Andy walks across the room, taking a seat on one of the horrible brown couches.] Rab: Hoho min … This place is pure class by the way. Barry: Aye min … Take a seat. [Still looking around in amazement, Rab walks across towards the couches, only to trip & stumble over a small coffee table … That’s what you get when you don’t look where you’re going. Rab sits next to Andy, who pulls out the lyrics from his pocket, the paper is now all crunched and crumpled. Barry takes a seat on the other sofa.] Barry: So, wut can I do fer you’se? Andy Murray: A’right, know this NedRap thing we’re doin’? Barry: Aye. Rab: Well Andy’s writin’ it, and we need yer help min. Barry: Hmmm … A’right, wut ye got so far? [Andy flattens the paper a little on his knees, making it look a little more presentable, before clearing his throat, and reading the lyrics so far.] Andy Murray: Ahem … Comin’ fae the hard knock life o’ Glesga, Ah’m a f*ckin’ Ned livin’ on the Nappers Brae, Sprayin’ on the wa's and kickin’ in yer baws, Gee’s any cheek and I’ll mak ye shit in yer breeks … Barry: That awl min? Andy Murray: Aye. [Barry pauses to think for awhile.] Barry: How ‘bout this … “Ah f*cked ye maw, I battered yer paw, goin’ down for a chippae ‘cos Ah’m needin’ feedin’, Ah’m gonnae hammer you if ye dinna like me, An’ Ah’m gonnae tell The Big Man if ye pish me off” … Rab: That’s pure briwliant min. Andy Murray: Aye min, that’s class. [Andy pulls the pen from his pocket, and quickly scribbles down Barry’s lyrics.] Andy Murray: Any more? Barry: Aye … “I like fitba’, birds, Bucky an’ Fags, If ye huv’nae got me fags & booze, gawn f*ck off befre I chib ye …” [Again, Andy jots down the lyrics. Our scene slowly fades out, leaving the lyrical genius’s at work. NedRap, it’s the future you know? But when exactly will it hit the charts? Very, very soon.]
