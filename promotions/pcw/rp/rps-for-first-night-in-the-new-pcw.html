Date: Last Week 7:30 AM It's a breezy, brisk 70 degree morning. The End wakes up, and after a shower and continental breakfast, leaves the Best Western he spent the night in and calls a taxi. A short wait out in front of the beige-colored four-story building with Georgian style pillared facade, and The End's taxi takes him away. 8:02 AM The End stops at a 7-Eleven to get a slurpee. He moves through the door to the rear wall that has the slurpee machine. Not really caring what flavor he gets, The End grabs a cup from the left of the machine and places it under a spiggot. As he pours it, a man in his thirties, with a his tanktop and jeans and a dirty blonde ponytail, walks up and slaps the cup out of The End's hand. Rob Van Dam:'Wrong cup, dude. You don't want a Hardy Boyz cup, you want a Rob Van Dam cup!' The End thrusts his cane into RVD's throat and drives his head through the glass door to the milk refrigerator. RVD collapses on the floor in a pool of his own blood. The End steps over the body of the WWE 'wrestler' and opens the soft drink fridge, removing and drinking a gulp of Fanta. Yes, Fanta; if Chef has a problem with that he call the Law Offices of LikeIGiveADamn. As The End goes up to the counter to pay for his soda, a robber, his entire body covered in black with a fashionable ski mask runs in, pulling out a gun, and holds up the store. Robber:'Hands up! Gimme the cash! And a Penthouse!' End:'Ugh. Mongrel.' As the Indian clerk, whose facial features, green jacket, black shirt, and white pants make look like the person upon which Apu from The Simpsons was based, a full-grown man wearing a pinkstrap over his head and dressed like Speedball of the New Warriors (anyone remember him?) charges into the convenience store, hopping to a stop, and placing his hands on his hips. He turns to the robber and addresses him. Rent-A-Zero!:'Halt evildoer! Were you not told as a child that crime does not pay? Sure, you may get some cash from it, but in the long run, it will hurt you far more than your human mind will comprehend. I, Rent-A-Zero assure you that perpetrating this act will get you nowhere, except put in jail by your friendly neighborhood superhero--' The End interrupts the 'superhero.' End:'You realize that the robber already got the cash and walked out, right?' Rent-A-Zero:'What? Dangit, that's the fifth time this week!' Rent-A-Zero runs to catch the thief, the automatic doors not opening in time; the hero runs straight into the glass and falls back on his behind. Getting up, his ego more hurt than anything else, he runs out the store, the doors behind him left open, so that The End and the coutner clerk can hear Rent-A-Zero say, Rent-A-Zero:'Hey! Get back here, I wasn't doen with my heroic monologue!' An awkward moment of silence follows, The End and the clerk just standing there. After a few seconds, Rent-A-Zero walks back into the store, dragging the robber, behind him, holding his gun and the bag of money and the Penthouse magazine. Rent-A-Zero stands the robber back at the counter, hands him his gun, and gives the bag and magazine back to the clerk. Rent-A-Zero:'Now, I'm want you to wait until I fly back in here, and then start from there, okay? And this time, don't go anywhere or do anything until I say so!' Rent-A-Zero stomps out of the convenience store. Everyone looks at each other in confusion, while Rob Van Dam continues to bleed on the floor by the slurpee machine. The End takes another drink of his soda. As he goes to get another, Rent-A-Zero runs back into the store. Rent-A-Zero:'Halt evildoer! Were you not-- hey! I said don't do anything until I say so!' Robber, Clerk, End:'Just get on with it!' Rent-A-Zero:'Fine.' The hero takes another moment to get back into character. Finally, he's ready to begin. Rent-A-Zero:'Halt evildoer! Were you not told as a child that crime does not pay? Sure, you may get some cash from it, but in the long run, it will hurt you far more than your human mind will comprehend. I, Rent-A-Zero assure you that perpetrating this act will get you nowhere, except put in jail by your friendly neighborhood superhero; for if no one has let you in on this before, this establishment is under the supervision and protection of yours truly, Rent-A-Zero! Now, if you would be so kind as to put your gun away and come with me.' Everyone justs stands where they are. Rent-A-Zero whispers to the robber. Rent-A-Zero:'Okay, now do something.' The robber opens up the Penthouse magazine to the centerfold section and flashes the naked woman at Rent-A-Zero. He covers his eyes, falls to the floor, and goes into a screaming fit. Rent-A-Zero:'Holy Nizbittlesham! My eyes! My Krypto-Saiyan eyes! You are truly evil, you ruined my virgin eyes! You must have discovered that my weakness is sexy naked women. I have one in particular that I have my eye on, but all women intimidate and scare me. You must have figured it out. Was it Dr. Dastardly who told you? Professor Perfecto? It doesn't matter, once I deem it safe to open my eyes again, I will use my mastery of Kaio-Ken and unleash Spirit Bomb, Koosh-Ball of Absolutely No Pain, and my most devestating maneuver, the Bizzatch Slap of Misfortune!' The robber kicks Rent-A-Zero in the stomach a few times to knock the wind out of him so he won't keep talking, then mounts him and punches him in the face as The End and the clerk stand back, not bothering to help the hero. Rent-A-Zero:'Ow! Stop punching me! It really hurts! I'm allergic to beatings!' The robber takes the cash and magazine and runs out of the store. Rent-A-Zero kicks around like a burned dog for a little while more. The End pays for his sodas and walks out of the store. The clerk presses the microphone button and speaks into his intercom. Clerk:'Clean-up at the front counter. Sanjay, we have another pummeled superhero down on the floor in front of the counter.' On the television behind the counter, running silent, the PCW channel plays its Turmoil match analyses and predictions. The End is the favorite to beat Rent-A-Hero 99-1 by popular street poll. In retrospect, we never should have let Gregory Shane Helms vote. 8:47 AM The End, in his standard PCW ring attire that he seems to wear everywhere, arrives by taxi at the Make-A-Wish Foundation 's Headquarters. Handing the fare to the Irish driver, The End steps out of the car, where he is met by a young man just barely twenty. He wears a white shirt, long black tie, and black slacks and leather shoes. The hair cut is cropped in the back, low-cut all around for a conservative look. His pale white face has red pimples and freckles all mixed together. With his crackling, squeaky voice, the man greets The End with a weak shiver of a handshake. Intern:'Hello, Mr. The End, and welcome to the Make-A-Wish Foundation. Thank you for taking time out of your busy schedule to do this.' The End tightly grasps the intern's right hand with his and shakes it violently, just to show dominance. End:'I don't work until Tuesday, so I'm willing to give up a day of my time. Where is he?' Intern:'Jimmy? Oh, he's right over here. Come here, Jimmy!' The intern calls over to the door of the lavishly decorated building that is constructed like the Taj Mahal. Not that the design serves a purpose, the Make-A-Wish Foundation just has that much money. 'Jimmy' rides over in an electric wheelchair. Jimmy:'Hello.' The End feels Jimmy's hand. Finding it odd, he runs his hand up Jimmy's right arm, and then searches his face with his hands. The End picks up Jimmy and holds him up, with Jimmy kicking and thrashing his arms, then tosses him back down in his seat. Jimmy:'Hey, watch it! What the hell kind of big brother are you?' The End pulls the intern over ten feet away. Jimmy is left to watch them at a distance. End:'Um... when I came in today, it was under the impression -- that your people gave me-- that I would be a big brother for a day to a 9-year-old boy named Jimmy from Ohio that suffers from cerebral palsy.' Intern:'And there he is.' The intern looks over to Jimmy and waves. Jimmy returns the intern's cheesy smile and waves. End:'That is not Jimmy, and he does not suffer from cerebral palsy.' The intern steps back, shocked at The End. Intern:'How dare you attempt to trivialize young Timmy--' End:'Jimmy.' Intern:'Jimmy's plight! He has a terrible terminal illness!' The End points back over to Jimmy. End:'Sir, 'Jimmy' is five feet, ten inches tall; weighs about 178 pounds at my estimate; has a razor-sharp five o'clock shadow, and long, tangled hair running down his arms to the back of his palms.' The intern shrugs, looking back over to Jimmy. Intern:'Well, it's my last day here, and I couldn't care less who you take out. If he says he's Jimmy, he's Jimmy. Just take him out for the day.' The intern walks back inside the grand Make-A-Wish Foundation building. The End walks over to Jimmy, who is none too pleased that he has The End for a big brother. Jimmy:'Hey! I asked for Big Alan Steele! Why'd I get you?' End:'Because you're wicked. But I guess I'm stuck with you, so let's go.' The End walks over to the taxi, with Jimmy following in his electric wheelchair, when Rent-A-Zero runs up and stops him from entering the taxi. RAZ knocks on the front passenger window to get the driver's attention. Rent-A-Zero:'Nevermind, Citizen. Go on your way before I report you.' The driver shrugs and drives off. The End grabs Rent-A-Zero by the throat and lifts him into the air. End:'I assume you have a good reason for doing that?' Rent-A-Zero answers, as The End is not choking him, just suspending him above the ground. Rent-A-Zero:'I saved you, good citizen, from the outrageously high fare that that cabbie would have no doubt charged you for taking you to where you need to go. Taxi rivers are a mischievous and devious breed, even among Earth humans, and are not to be trusted by anyone.' End:'I prepaid the fare, you twit.' Rent-A-Zero looks back at the taxi, which is speeding off to the west. Rent-A-Zero:'Oh.' The End tosses Rent-A-Zero away and makes Jimmy walk to the bus stop with him. They aren't left alone for long, Rent-A-Zero follows them. The End turns around, drawing his sword, and puts it to Rent-A-Zero's throat. End:'You have ten seconds to convince me to let you live.' Rent-A-Zero:'Wait! Wait. Look, I'll level with you.' End:'Why are you following me around?' RAZ lets out a deep sigh, and scratches his head. Rent-A-Zero:'I need attention. I am so desperately lonely.' End:'That explains it.' The End tosses RAZ back to the ground. RAZ gets up and dusts himself off. Rent-A-Zero:'Besides, you're warm, and I'm ice cold. I'd like to borrow some of your heat.' End:'Is that so?' Rent-A-Zero:'So... can I hang out with you?' The End shoves Rent-A-Zero back onto the ground. End:'Forget it. I can't stand you as it is, and you already remind me of someone I'll have to deal with this Tuesday. Stay away from me, or I will kill you.' The End, with Jimmy following, walks away from the depressed Rent-A-Zero. RAZ turns his back and drags his feet as he goes back to his Zero-Rent-Paid Cave. 9:16 AM As Rent-A-Zero sulks in his sparcely-furnished (nothing, really) Zero-Rent-Paid Cave, he remembers the early morning's events. The End rejecting an offer of friendship, the clerk having him tossed out of the store, the uncooperative robber who kept ruining his attempts to be a hero; nothing worked out for him. And the more he thinks about it, the more he realizes it. Rent-A-Zero:'I tried to play nice. I tried to do the right thing. But the world doesn't want heroes. It doesn't want nice guys. It wants people who are out for themselves. People who are... evil. And as much as it pains my Krypto-Saiyan heart to say so, I will have to do the unthinkable. I shall become Evil Zero! I shall use my powers for evil, instead of good!' RAZ's old country black butler, Manfred, quips while doing whatever he is in the dark shadows behind RAZ. He can't afford the lighting. Manfred:'What powers?' Rent-A-Zero:'Quiet, you!' 9:37 AM The End sits down on the bench in the park. Jimmy is by him, pacing back in forth with his electric wheelchair folded up on the ground. Jimmy is smoking a cigarette. End:'So, what's your angle?' Jimmy:'What?' End:'Who are you really?' Jimmy takes a deep breath in of his cigarette, and slowly releases the smoke through his nose. Teens from the Truth campaign are walking by, shaking their heads. Jimmy flips them off. Turning his attention back to The End, he reluctantly answers. Jimmy:'You gonna tell anyone?' A snicker comes from The End. End:'Does it matter at this point?' Instead of lungs-full of smoke, Jimmy lets out a sigh. Jimmy:'My name is Jhurgis Patlova.' The End ponders the name in his head. End:'Jhurgis... you're Lithuanian?' Jhurgis:'Yes. I was a big soccer star in my home country.' Ironically, Olympic Heights High School children playing on the grass nearby have started up a game of soccer. Jhurgis watches them, reminiscing about his own past with the sport. Jhurgis:'I was the best player on the Lithuanian National Team. So good, in fact, that I rarely even passed the ball to my teammates. I thought I was too good for that. And eventually I got to thinking that I was too good for the Lithuanian team, so I came to America to join the national team.' The End is not sympathetic at all to Jhurgis and confronts him straightly. End:'And you never realized that your country is ranked 300th in the world in soccer?' Jhurgis turns his full attention back to The End: Jhurgis:'That doesn't sound so bad.' End:'There are less than 250 countries in the world.' Jhurgis:'Damn.' The End does back off a little, and allows Jhurgis to go back to his sotry. End:'So, what did you do when you came here?' Turning back to the children playing soccer, Jhurgis continues. Jhurgis:'No one wanted me, and I wasn't really good at anything. I tried to get a job wrestling, I even won a few championships against REALLY bad people, but I could never really defend myself. Then there cam ean offer from LIthuania -- they wanted me to coach the national team, paying me $6 million Lithuanian dollars.' End:'Which comes out to $53.70 here.' Jhurgis:'Having no family here, and nothing to do, I pretended to be terminally ill. Those Make-A-Wish people pay for everything you could ask for. I'm not a bad person, I just have no marketable talents, and no one would care about me otherwise. I'm kind of like that guy... Juan Borro. He's one step away from where I am.' 10:14 AM Out in a JC Penny's parking lot, two ponytailed and goateed Italian men in dark sunglasses and black suits run away from a red Ford Explorer. The men hurry over to their black 1998 Buick LeSabre and speed off, skidding their tires as they weave around the other cars in the crowded parking lot. Memorial Day Sale. In a short time, a middle-aged man with a plain face and receding hairline wearing a bland gray three-piece suit and black Bruno Malley shoes walks out of the store and approaches the car. As he opens the door with the remote locking device, Evil Zero runs up and tackles the man to the ground. He takes the keys and tosses them into the nearby aesthetically-deplorable pond. Then, Evil Zero talks loudly to the man, even though he is not paying attention. Evil Zero:'Ha ha! Now that I've thrown your keys into the water, you most certainly will not be able to take home the merchandise that you have purchased on this day, the day that you have purchased the items that were on sale because as we all know -- Our veterans gave their lives so that you could get a day off from work!' Evil Zero gets in the man's face and does a ridiculous 'evil dance.' Evil Zero:'See how I used my mastery of Kaio-Ken and unleash Spirit Bomb, Koosh-Ball of Absolutely No Pain, and my most devestating maneuver, the Bizzatch Slap of Misfortune, to prevent you from fully enjoying your Memorial Day! I am truly evil!' As the man runs after his keys, the Explorer explodes into many pieces. A fireball erupts into the sky. By this time, a crowd has formed. The bystanders talk amongst themselves. Young Woman:'Did you see that? He kept the guy from getting blown up!' Boy:'He's cool!' Old Man:'He's a hero!' The wreck's owner approaches Evil Zero. Man:'You saved my life! Thank you.' Evil Zero stands there in awkward silence, with the crowd of people applauding him. Evil Zero:'Uh...' Rather than try to salvage the situation, Evil Zero runs away. 11:26 PM A house party is being held at a Boston Market in Lake Worth, FL. Seinfeld actor Wayne Knight joins Jason Scott Lee (whose long hair and loincloth give the impression that he's reliving his days on the Jungle Book set). Many extras are on hand for the event. The only person of any noteworthy importance is Anna Keyson,in her usual black leather suit, with purple make-up all over. With everyone eating rotisserie chicken and drinking Fanta (bring it, Chef), Lee -- impersonating Kramer Wildwing tonight, calls Wayne Knight (his penguin suit having a few spills of buffalo wing barbecue sauce on left breat pocket, and more spills on the shirt lapel) outside in the cool, moist night air. Knight:'What's up? You seem down.' Lee paces back and forth on the sidewalk with his head at the ground and his hands in his pockets. Seeing a stone by his foot, he kicks it. Lee:'I just wish I had people care enough to come to these parties that I throw.' Lee gets a pat on the shoulder from his short, chubby sidekick. Knight:'What are you talking about? There are a ton of people in there! It's a packed house!' Lee looks at Knight with a serious face. Lee:'I mean, I wish people would come to parties and I don't have to hire them to fill the room.' Knight:'Oh. That. You know, you really shouldn't worry about those things. No one's ever liked you, and it's hard to believe that's going to change anytime soon. Just live your sham of a life while you still have a job to support it.' Knight coaxes a smile out of Lee. Lee:'Yeah, you're right.' Knight and Lee pull on the door handle to rejoin the party, but it won't budge. Lee:'They locked us out!' Wayne shrugs, as if he's not surprised. Knight:'People were ignoring us all night, and it's not like you hired them to spend to with you, just to come to your parties.' Knight and Lee sit down on the sidewalk, putting their hands under the chins. There they sit, silent, not doing anything. After a while, Lee pulls some jacks and a superball out of his pocket, and starts playing with them. Lee:'Wanna try?' Knight says nothing, only slaps Lee on the back of the head. 11:20 AM: The Blank (black hat, slacks, and trenchcoat, with a white shirt AND ABSOLUTELY NO FACIAL FEATURES!!!) of Dick Tracy fame sits alone inside his super-secret-mansion that everybody knows about. With him are his Hispanic stunt-double, Julio, white cameraman Will, silent black sidekick, Henry, bodyguard Anasha, and scientist Melody. The Blank is punching a red punching bag suspended from the ceiling by a chain, held steady by Henry. The bag is Julio. Julio: *In pain, broken speech*'{Something in Spanish?}' Blank:'You're the stunt-double, so you're paid to take this kind of abuse!' Julio:'{Something in Spanish!}' Will:'He's got a point there. You don't pay him to take abuse at your hands.' Blank:'Very well, let's take a break.' Henry unwraps Julio, who falls to the floor clutching his ribs. Before Blank can walk off, Henry taps him on the shoulder, and then stares at him without saying anything. Henry:'....' Blank:'Well, if you're bored, go watch TV.' Henry goes over to the red 'Friends' sofa that sits in front of the 27-inch Daewoo TV. Picking up the remote, he turns it on and goes channel surfing. Blank goes into the kitchen and fixes himself a marshmallow and chocolate chip sandwich, with honey buns substituting for bread. Anasha comments on his choice of food. Anasha:'Given that your career doesn't ever get any further than the Light-Heavyweight Championship, you might want to take a little more pride in getting a shot at it.' Blank:'I pay you to protect and support me, not offer those negative opinions. If you're disagreeable one more time, I'll dock your pay.' Anasha:'You haven't paid us in months.' Blank:'Good point.' Henry comes running in the room, screaming at the top of his lungs. Henry:'AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRGGGGHHH!' Henry collapses onto the ground and curls into the fetal position, sucking his thumb. Anasha kicks Henry while he's down. Anasha:'Melody, how many times do I have to tell you -- If you want to offer a man sex, use the vaginal soap I gave you first!' Melody looks up from the machine she's tinkering with. Melody:'Screw you!' Then, she withdraws and talks quietly to herself. Melody:'Wait, maybe I did. There was that one New Year's Eve that got me wanted in 47 states.' Blank sits down to watch the TV that Henry was watching. Blank:'So, Will, you're sure that my last flash against that Indian --' Will:'Native American. That's what you have to call him.' Blank:'Crap! Why didn't you correct me last time?' Will:'You got all into it, with the Pilgrim and cowboy outfits. I didn't want to ruin your fun.' Blank:'But I could have all twenty Native Americans in the US angry at me!' Will:'They wouldn't do anything to you, they'd risk losing all their government money.' A sigh comes from Blank. His body language suggests a deep breath, but he has no nose or mouth. Blank:'But if I walk down the street and get scalped by Indians, I'll count it as your fault.' Blank pushes his sandwich against his face, but having no mouth, he can't eat it. So, it smushes against his face, and he pulls it back down. Melody:'You know, I could make you that prosthetic mouth so you could actually eat the things you're trying to kill yourself with. You haven't eaten anything in so long.' Blank:'I've only got to gain 180 pounds before I'm over the Light-Heavyweight limit of 230 lb. This sandwich is fine. There's got to be nearly a million calories in this one snack.' Anasha:'Especially when you added the lard to it.' Jhurgis:'You should eat healthier.' Everyone stops and looks at Jhurgis in a mean stare. He tried to get someone to care about what he said and it failed. So he drags his feet as he leaves the super-secret-mansion hideout. Blank:'Julio, I keep telling you to lock the door!' Julio: *Weakly*'{Something... in... Spanish.}' Blank:'He's got a point there; I did tie him up and bring him down here before he could do his check of the doors and windows. Anyway, what was Henry watching?' Blank turns the channel. It's a new commercial featuring Bob Dole in a speedo, talking about his increased sexual energy thanks to Viagra. Everyone's face goes pale, and fear comes across it. 'FEAR' is written on Blank's face, to show it. Blank:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Will:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Anasha:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Melody:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Julio:'AAAAARRRRGGGHH!' Everyone runs into the kitchen with Henry and falls into the fetal position, rocking side-to-side and sucking on their thumbs. 2:55 AM Lee:'Wow, they're making this an all-nighter.' Knight:'You think we should leave?' A pause. Lee:'... No.' The End:'This is the point where there'd be some character and situation making fun of Charlie Loc, but since he's been gone for so long, I forgot what he's like. Oh well.' Anyway, that's enough out of me. I've actually got some training to do for Turmoil. See you.
