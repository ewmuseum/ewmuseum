The Duck 4

Date: ... Night time. Dock 46… A pair of webbed-feet trot briskly down the length of the dock, each step causing a creak in the old planks of wood, like strokes upon the keys of an out-of-tune piano. The composition, that of a doomed end… Stopping at the end of the dock, it lets the bag slung over its shoulder sag to the wood surface. Pulling it open, and digging in, it pulls out the gold plated strap that is the PCW TV Title. It sinks quickly. One down…---“VAT DO YOU VANT WITH ME?!” Snapping, the silhouette halted the old man’s blubbering with a shushing gesture. The old master of diabolical machinery, devious devices, and sinister solutions winced behind his half inch thick lenses. LAB: “(Whispering.) But… I already told you zat I don’t even understand you…” “QUACK?!?!?” LAB: “No, of course I didn’t mean to imply zat you vere crazy! I just can’t understand ze Duck dialect! Err.. I mean… can’t understand ‘VELL’.” The Mallard scoffed, stepping out of the shadows. Syko’s 2nd in command, and personal assistant struggled against the ropes that held him tightly to a chair. The bruises, and breaks on his worn skin would indicate harsh punishment endured. The Duck stops, at the feet of LAB, pulling out a snap shot photo of Syko parading about with Bertha. It’s beady little eyes lock in total solidity on the old man. The old man’s heart rate skipped a beat, then sped up… The Duck could hear it. LAB: “…Heh… You see… heh… Ve humans like… to share feeling for von anoth---” Duck: “QUACK?!” LAB: “AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH~!”---No sleep. Hours upon hours of planning. Love can not wait. Must make Pendragon die… In the name of love… Love that has a lust for blood… And sexual intercourse. Sketching arduously, white sloppily thrown onto a blue canvas, the Goblin concentrates hard on the blueprints before him, trying to make sure it comes out just right. It seems to be a trap of some sort… The PCW TV title planted, Syko would need to rig the arena so Pendragon would never make it to the belt, and thus Bertha as well. Stick Pendragon enters the arena, and makes his way to the locker room. But, the locker room is rigged with a…scribble scribble… Scratch that… Pendragon is making his entrance down the ramp, when suddenly a trap… scribble scribble… Venneman walks in, and triggers a trip wire that tugs on a hammer handle that strikes a glass jar of pickles. The pickles fall into an empty pale hanging at the end of a 2X4 that flips backwards just caressing the edge of shiny black bowling ball… enough push to get it rolling. … As Pendragon makes his way down the arena corridors, the bowling ball races along a pipe line… EVERYTHING SHATTERS, THE ARENA IS CRUSHED INTO OBLIVION!!! STG: “DAMMIT!! NOT NEARLY COMPLICATED ENOUGH TO OBLITERATE THE LIKES OF PENDRAGON!” The small Goblin violently crushes the large sheet of paper, crumpling it into a ball, packing it tightly, and finally hurling it as far his arm will send it. STG: “(Grudgingly.) Stupid Pendragon…” Satisfied by the ball of paper hitting his war room wall, the Goblin yanks another from his desk, and prepares to spread it out on the table… “Quack” Startled, Syko looks down, and scowls at the sight of that obnoxious Duck smugly looking up at him from atop his desk. STG: “GET OUTA THE WAY, STUPID DUCK!” Syko shoves the Duck aside, while spreading the blue sheet of paper out, which he almost immediately begins scribbling on. STG: “(Hissing under his breath.) …think Goblin, think!… …Cranium work… …Must devise devious plan to annihilate Pendragon… …To get Dearest Bertha …” Careful not to step onto its ‘friend’s’ battle plans, the Duck returns, looming over the messy diagram to study it, as well. Syko abruptly shifts the blue print over, leaning over it to block from the Mallard’s view. The Duck waddles to the other side. Again, Syko pulls it away… The Duck follows. Trembling with anger, the Goblin leers at the Duck. It looks at him. It merely licks his nose, and returns to studying the blueprint. STG: “Grrrrrrrr…” As he quakes with rage, the Goblin leans back from the desk, rubbing his temples. STG: “Mustn’t let mallard invoke rage. Fighting in name of love. This kind of vengeance is honorable. Must focus…” He feels his composure return just long enough for him to look at the Duck again. Only now his vision is blurred, and he sees two Ducks. Duck: “Quack?” STG: “Must kill.” The disgruntled Goblin ups himself from the desk, and mindlessly wanders from the room, his blood shot eyes refusing to shut. The Duck watches with a grin as the paranoid Goblin exits the war room. With the Goblin gone, the Duck’s eye’s shift evilly… Scribble Scribble Scribble---Having solved his urge to go on a killing spree/tangent, the Goblin returns, refreshed and rejuvenated, his nerves cooled by some unknown, magical force(Daiquiri machine). STG: “At least I still have one joy in… MY GOD!!” Startled, the small master of evil shrieks!! His war room… Scoured with blue prints… The walls also are scribbled upon… Wading his way into the mess, Syko picks one of the blue prints up, and studies it for a moment. STG: “Bertha… plus Knife… Equal sign…HAPPY FACE?!?! Impossible!” He picks up another. STG: “A cow being shot? (*GASP*) THAT’S NO COW! Whoever is foolish enough to take responsibility for this is a TERRIBLE ARTIST!” Fuming, he shreds the sheet to pieces, and discards it like confetti over his shoulder. Now his bulging orbs scan the room, looking for the culprit who’d desecrate the visage of his Bertha… STG: “Yoooooou! I should have known!” The Goblin leers at the unconscious form of Dr. LA Borodinsky, bruised and battered horribly, tied to his chair. He doesn’t answer… Being unconscious, and all. Syko immediately pulls out the closet firearm at hand, a .45, and takes aim… STG: “NO EXCUSES!!!!!!” BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM The old man stirs, awakened by the Goblin’s bad aim. LAB: “Oh god… Vere am I? Vat’s zat noise?” STG: “Don’t worry, I’m here to save you now!” Syko hides the pistol behind his back. Rushing to the old man, Syko kneels to work at the ropes that bind his wrists to the arms of the chair. STG: “Who did this to you?” LAB: “Ze… Ze… Zire…” STG: “WHERE DID THEY GO?!” LAB: “It vas…” STG: “WHAT ARE THEY HERE FOR?!?!” LAB: “Ze… D-… D-…Da…Du-c-” STG: “THE DAIQUIRI MACHINE?!?!?! OH GOD NO!!!” And like that, Syko bolts from the room, leaving his faithful assistant to writhe in the chair… Bolting through the corridors of his secret HQ, the Goblin makes lightning haste to his vault, huffing the whole way. STG: “There are three things I love in this world… 1. Bertha… 2. The TV Title… and 3… My Daiquiri mixers… AND GOD HELP ME IF THAT IS GONE…” Syko runs down the last stretch of the hall that leads to the high security vault where he keeps his greatest, most prized items. But, as soon as he turns the corner, his heart stops, seeing the massive, 1 foot thick titanium door lying on the floor, having been ripped from it’s hinges… Only one creature could be responsible for this. He arrives at the vault, but what is waiting there for him brings him to his knees. The Daiquiri Mixer hangs from the ceiling in a noose… A noose made out of it’s own Plug-in cord… That has been severed from its body. The Goblin looks to the sky, rips out wads of hair, and shrieks to the heavens. Bitter agony. There’s a soggy sounding succession of footsteps that catches the Goblin’s attention. He turns to catch the culprit. The Duck. STG: “You…STOP! HALT NOW!” The Duck stops dead in its tiptoeing tracks, looking back as if though confused. Duck: “(Innocently.) Quack?” STG: “Leave my domicile.” The Duck pleads with a helpless gaze, trying to fool the Goblin. STG: “…GO! AND NEVER RETURN! YOU MONSTER!” Duck: “QUACK! QUACK!!!” STG: “No ‘buts.’” The Duck stays, its eyes fixated on the ground. But not for long. The Goblin bounds forward punts the Duck with a hard kick, that sends him tumbling backwards for a go several feet. Before the feathered fiend can get up again, it falls victim to another kick… STG: “OUT!!!!!!” And with that… The Duck goes.--- So… When I won the TV Title, many things came with it. Fame… Fortune(Enough to buy a Daiquiri Machine(RIP)) And women… But now, look at me. The TV Title has caused me to lose all three. And now all I have is the TV Title… What? WHAT?!?! THE TV TITLE IS GONE TOO?!?
