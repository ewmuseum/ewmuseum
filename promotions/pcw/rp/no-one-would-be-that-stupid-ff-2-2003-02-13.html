No one would be that stupid. FF#2

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-03 Adam and Jaslene are seen walking hand in hand in the middle of the afternoon. Adam dressed a bit more casual than normally, in a pair of blue jeans with black boots and a black AWR shirt on. Jaslene is in a pair of black pants with a midnight blue top on. The two laugh and giggle as they continue to walk down a street. Adam stops and flipping up his sunglasses reads, “Rohan’s Rassilin’ World…this is the place right Jas?” She replies in a playful tone, ”You betcha’!” Adam smiles as he pulls Jaslene into him. They stare into each other’s eyes before Adam bends down and kisses her. ”Well I guess we’d better head on in…” he says pulling away from her. He turns and heads toward the school. She lets out a groan before trying to pull him back, “Ooooooh…we can be a bit late right?” He stops. As he turns a smile comes to his face, ”’fraid not Jas. I’m new to the PCW and I need to make a good impression. Had a certain someone, who shall remain nameless, not agreed to this for me in the first place…” he trails off. ”Ok look that was in the past! Can’t we forget about it and move on already?” she asks with a look of almost desperation on her face. Adam scoffs, ”The past? It was only a couple of days ago!” Jaslene gives him a shrug of her shoulders. He turns and continues toward the school. ”But didn’t you make a good enough impression in your match with Skylar Green? Which might I add came to a shock of most of the people there? You put on one hell of a show when not too many people expected it.” she calls out. Adam again stops allowing Jaslene times to catch up with him. ”Yeah but that impression was on the fans not on the higher ups of the PCW. Don’t worry this will probably be quick anyway.” They walk together once again hand in hand. They come to the door of the building and Adam it for Jaslene. ”Thank you.” she says walking through the door. Adam nods as he goes through the doorway. They are in what appears to be an empty lobby. Jaslene walks over to a small table and swipes a magazine off of it. She examines the cover of it carefully. ”October two thousand?!” she says aloud before tossing the magazine back onto the table, ”There’s no one here, this must have been some kind of joke. You know? Like haze the new guy type thing.” She turns and heads for the door but Adam stops her. ”Just because the magazines aren’t recent doesn’t mean there is no one here,” he replies calmly adding, ”When was the last time you went to a doctor and there were up on the latest editions?” ”Fair enough but it could still be someone just messing with you Adam.” she answers. ”No one would be that stupid.” he says slyly. ”Oh…” Jaslene thinks to herself for a moment. Suddenly a door bursts open. Jaslene jumps into Adam, ”AHHHHH!” she screams. Adam laughs as Jaslene returns to the floors. She slaps him in the chest, ”It’s not nice to laugh at other people Adam!” ”But you laugh at me all the time!” he exclaims. ”Yeah but that’s different, I’m not laughing at you…I’m laughing with you.” she responds. Before Adam can say anything an older man comes walking through the door. More like hobbling. He’s got a full head of white hair. Dressed in sweats he approaches Adam, ”Who are you? Hm? A new student perhaps?” the man croaks as he looks Adam up and down. ”Me?” Stryker answers looking at Jaslene, ”I’m Adam Stryker. I’m here from the PCW. I assume you’re Rohan?” ”Eh? What did you say?” the old man now turns his attention to Jaslene, ”I’m a little hard of hearing, you’ll have to speak up.” Jaslene sighs. Adam drops his head and shakes it from side to side. A silence comes over them as the old man continues to ogle Jaslene. ”WHAT I SAID WAS, ‘I ASSUME YOU’RE ROHAN’, AM I CORRECT? Adam yells. ”There’s no need to yell young man.” the old man answers. Adam slaps his forehead, ”But you…I just…AW forget it! Come on Jas let’s just get out of here.” Jaslene stops Adam from leaving, ”Excuse me sir but we are looking for a man named Rohan, he runs this school. Adam here is coming to address the class.” ”Aye, I’m Rohan…” Rohan trails off a bit before his face lights up, ”Oh! Oh! You must be Adam Stryker from the PCW come to speak to my class. My sincerest apologies for not realizing sooner. Come!” Before Adam can say anything Rohan heads back through the door he came in. Adam follows with Jaslene bringing up the rear. Faint grunts and groans can be heard. ”This place doesn’t look half bad,” Jaslene says catching up with Adam, ”Though you’d never know it from the outside.” ”True.” is Adam’s response. The grunts and groans grow louder and louder until they finally emerge in what appears to be the main gym. A group of students are running through some drills inside the ring set up in the middle of the gym. As Rohan blows his whistle, everyone stops. A couple of the students look over as shock comes to their faces. Murmuring can be heard coming from the group. ”Class! Today we have a very special guest. I’m sure some of you know who he is and to those of you who don’t, listen and listen well. He’s been through a lot in the business and has a lot to teach.” Rohan pauses to take in a breath, ”Let me introduce to you…PCW’s very own Adam Stryker!” Adam steps in front of Rohan to a warm reception from the class. ”Thanks, thank you Rohan for that introduction,” Adam says, ”Well I’m sure a lot of you have questions so lets get right to it.” Jaslene wanders off as the students start asking Adam questions. She seems distracted, lost in thought. ”Well he doesn’t seem too upset about this anymore.” She looks over to see him laughing, ”At least he’s laughing. Something he probably won’t be doing that tomorrow.” Jaslene sits down on a weight bench and just watches Adam field questions. A young woman stands up. ”Mr. Stryker, where did you train? Was it at a school like this or did you just learn on the fly?” she inquires. Adam thinks for a moment, ”Well for sure there is no way to learn on the fly so get that out of your heads now. But I’m sure some of you have had a background in wrestling prior to this be it amateur at the High School level or in your backyard. I started as a mat technician in High School. I won four consecutive state championships and lost only two matches in four years.” Jaslene giggles as a collective, ”Ooooh…” comes up from the class. ”So right there I knew I was good. And yes I did train in a school but it was much, much different than this. I was lucky enough to be accepted into an elite Japanese school that taught mainly submissions. I trained for nearly two years before my first real match. I’ve spent about a total of five years just training in Japan and even with that I didn’t finish. Jaslene’s eyes open wide, ”Five years just training? Damn no wonder he carries himself like he does,” she thinks to herself, ”Well now his confidence doesn’t seem that bad.” Another young woman stands up, ”Your feud with AJ Trinidad was simply mind boggling, does it upset you that it only yielded one legally sanctioned match?” ”You’re damn right it does,” Adam replies, ”AJ and I helped to build the DWO up. Had I not gotten injured our feud would have been one for the ages. But that’s life and at least we remained friends in the end.” ”Yeah that did have one hell of a feud, mostly mind games but oh well, the DWO just didn’t give them many chances to get into the ring across from each other. Everyone wanted, they both wanted it and especially the fans but oh well.” Rohan steps in front of the class. ”I have a question,” he proclaims, ”What is the best advice you could give these promising young up and comers?” Adam pauses for a moment to think, ”Just to not give up. This business is a killer. More people will try and knock you down than well try and help you. My advice is not so much not trust anyone but to be careful about those you do trust. I can tell you that people will latch onto you and ride you for as long as they can…don’t let that happen.” ”Adam’s dead on there, trust is a fleeting feeling that can burn you more than it can help you.” Jaslene shifts her weight and leans forward on the bench, ”I’m worried about this tag match on Friday. I know for a fact that Adam doesn’t trust either of his opponents but the rage he feels toward Storm will force him to even if just for a night.” ”Ok class let’s thank Mr. Stryker for coming out and visiting us today,” Rohan says as he starts to clap. The rest of the class stands and claps with Rohan until he suddenly blows the whistle, ”NOW GET OUT THERE AND RUN SOME LAPS!” Adam laughs as the class files out of the gym. He shakes Rohan’s hand one last time before walking over toward Jaslene. ”How did I do?” he asks. ”Hm? Oh I wouldn’t know, I wasn’t really paying any attention. she guilefully responds. ”Uh huh, come on Jas you ready to go?” he asks. ”Of course, I’m ready to go eat,” she says as she starts to snicker, ”How about…haha…Chucky-E-Cheese’s?” ”Don’t start with that!” he says as they continue to head out of the gym.
