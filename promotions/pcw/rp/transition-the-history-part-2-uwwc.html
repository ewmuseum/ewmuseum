Transition -- The History (Part 2: UWWC)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered *Three weeks ago, while in FBI custody while being investigated for tax evasion; narrated to Sandy Wong over a tape recorder.* Onto the Ultimate World Wrestling Championships, a larger eastern regional federation based in Virginia. My first big feud there was with Mike Sanders, Triple H, and Billy Gunn. Yeah, a stable (called the Backyard Gang for a reason they never really explained) against myself. But they all had the same problem as the Undertaker, and because I didn't pull my kicks, I wiped the floor with them all. An entire stable taken out by one guy. Pathetic. They tried to attack me backstage, tried to stop me from threatening their titles (all of which I won from them in the span of a couple of weeks). They failed miserably, and have gone back to the fake version of professional wrestling, the version that treats this as a show, instead of a legit sport like it should be. I remember, they challenged me to a survival gauntlet match. That's a match where I fought all three of them, and even if I lost a fall, I'd still have to fight the others. First out was Triple H. He cheated quite a bit, but I grabbed his foot when he tried a groin kick, spun him into a Stretch Muffler, and from there went into the Reverse Boston Crab for the win. He screamed like a little girl and tapped out in seconds. Next was Billy Gunn. He came in with a moonsault off the top rope. I grabbed him and spiked him into the mat with a Tenzan Tombstone Driver. Quick pinfall. Finally, Mike Sanders walked out. He hyped himself up on the mic for 5 minutes before the match, but was quick to fall to an STO that set-up a 450 Splash. And it was all over. Backyard Gang, defeated. They dissolved after that. Next was Beast and Samurai. I'd already won pretty much every title in the fed except for the tag team titles (because I was a nice guy and everyone hates them). They wanted to steal some of my thunder, try to take me on together. Beast was bigger than myself, but less talented. Samurai was a small fry. I think it took about 3 weeks to finish them off, then again I was nursing a self-incurred injury at the time. I remember them, they were a lot like John Crichton was in the PWA. Their catchphrase was 'Whatcha gonna do about that, bitch?' They couldn't ever back up their words, it was sad, really. Just like the Backyard Gang, Beast and Samurai had just tried to attack me. They were so predictable about it, it wasn't much to beat their asses down. And in no time, I was back to the main event where I should've been. Handicap match, quick thrust to Samurai's throat leaving him on the mat, a Tornado DDT put out Beast. That guy was like 7'3' and 450 pounds, and couldn't take a hit. Sad. Powerbombed Samurai onto Beast, did a top rope moonsault stomp onto both of them. Pinned Beast for the win just to make a point. Coffin Mom... he was next. The only other person in that federation with some small measure of skill. He was another person who wanted to get at me. I was the best there, he felt he had something to prove. He apparently called himself Coffin Mom because it had something to do with his dead mother and... I forget. He never told the same story twice. He never talked about himself, he was too busy trying to pick out flaws in other people. Somehow he got a match with me at the UWWC final show. The fed was going under due to lack of finances and fan interest. He botched his own finisher and killed me. I came back to life 36 hours later. (No, morons, all that stuff you 'learned' about me last year wasn't a gimmick, it's just stuff that I've grown past. Yes, I'm half-human, half-grigori. Yes, I still possess Celestial abilities. Yes, I'll probably never really die unless someone cuts off my head or removes my heart. Does that mean I can't choose to be a regular guy? Jackasses.) The UWWC stayed open for about 7 months (5 1/2 longer than HWF, but even 2 months alive for the HWF was surprising looking back), during which it grew from a subregional indy to a regional holding shows all within the state of New York, though the fed itself was based in Virginia. I was only there for the last 4. I won the Hardcore title on my first show, my first match there, by defeating Beast (hehe). We had both drifted over from the HWF, Beast was there as well, but not with Samurai. The very next show, I won the European Title (that place was a WWF/E clone; the high number of WWE wrestlers) from *gasp!* Mike Sanders. I held the Hardcore Title for a little while, beating back all challengers in the 24/7 rule for it. I made a name for myself by refusing to use weapons while defending the belt. To win, I'd lock opponents in some submission and they'd tap out in order to not be crippled; they'd try again later. After that I won the Light Heavyweight title (I was a light heavyweight then) from Billy Gunn. He wasn't a Light Heavyweight himself, but no one was willing to classify themselves as such. He was just given the belt. I won it from him with ease. After that, I vacated the European Title, simply because no one wanted to challenge me for it. I believe Test won the European Title in a 4-way match that I mandated (himself, Cactus Jack, Matt Hardy, and Samurai) and feuded with Raven after that. While holding the Light Heavyweight Belt, Kurt Angle put out an open challenge for the Intercontinental Title he held. I took up the challenge and beat him. There I was again, holding two titles. I'd been in the federation for a month, no lie, and I was already a three-time Double-Champion. Triple H was getting worried about my quick rise to the top, and Mike Sanders and Billy Gunn were vengeful over losing their titles to me, so they formed a stable to take me out. That's right, an entire stable dedicated to me. After I destroyed the stable, I still had the Intercontinental and Light Heavyweight Titles. There were like two light-heavyweights left in the fed anyway, and neither wanted anything to do with me. That, and the President had to go on vacation right before a pay-per-view, and as the only trustworthy guy, he left me in charge. There were Tag Team Titles that no one was doing anything with, a Light Heavyweight belt out of contention, and the IC title's contenders were starting to wear thing. Deciding to do what was best for the federation, I vacated the Light Heavyweight Title and opened it up to any rookie wrestler or just anyone who was working in an even smaller independent fed to come to the federation and compete in a massive battle royale for it as a way to get noticed. The WWE's Christian ended up winning it. The Tag Team Titles were put on the line in a tournament, which Edge and Christian won. And the #1 contendership to the IC title was won by Test. Test lost the European Title to Edge. Test wanted my IC title badly. He did all that he could to win it. He failed, of course, but when Kurt Angle asked for challengers to his World Title, I just gave Test the belt (having held it for two months) and challenged Kurt Angle for his title. It was a quick match. He came out throwing a bunch of right hands to my face. I just stood there letting him get off about five. Blocked the sixth, turned into a Cobra Clutch, and suplexed him out of his boots. The match was over. He may have been a great amateur wrestler, but in professional wrestling, he was still trying to get the hang of it. So now I had the World Title. And everybody wanted it. So I defended it. A rematch with Kurt Angle was botched, purposefully messed up to get the title off of me. We were wrestling each other in a Submission Match. Coffin Mom came out and knocked us both out with a chair. He put Angle on top of me and the referee counted a pin, awarding the World Title to Angle. Angle was upset afterward and declared a tournament to crown a new champion, declaring his win null and void. I didn't participate, Angle said I would face the winner anyway. So that freed up my time to terrorize Coffin Mom. He lost our matches, but always kept trying to one-up me. Something about nice-guys who won their matches that he couldn't deal with. I won the World Title back once everything was over. And then came the final PPV, the final show. Every title was on the line. Edge beat Ace Spade for the European Title. Scorpio won the Light Heavyweight Title. Kurt Angle and I won the Unified Tag Team Championships, making me the only undisputed Omnichampion (I coined it myself; the tag team titles were at one point split into two entities -- the Tag Team Titles and the Hardcore Team Titles. I won the Tag Team Titles with Krazy Krash, who made a temporary appearance in the UWWC before suffering an injury. I shortly thereafter vacated the title belts as I was unable to find a partner) in the history of the federation. Julio Garcia won the Intercontinental Title for Rhythm & Rhyme (a tag team consisting of a small Hispanic rapper and a large black rapper). And I defended the World Title at the final show, successfully, I might add. *Next session: PWA*
