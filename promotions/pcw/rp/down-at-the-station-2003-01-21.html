Down At the Station

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-21-03 Chris paced back and forth. He was a nervous wreck. Sweat was pouring down. His heart was racing. He couldn't stay still. All around him police officers walks around, going about their daily business. There sitting calmly near him was Tessa Windsor. Alex and Christa occupied the seats next to her. However Chris couldn't sit down. His merely paced back and forth biting his bottom lip. One of the officers that was at his apartment walks into the small waiting room. Chris stands up when he spots him. The two shake hands. Chris looks back to Tessa and gives her a nod. He follows the officer to a small cubical. The officer points to a chair offering it to Chris. He nods and takes a seat. The cop takes his seat behind his desk. He looks at some paperwork, keeping his eyes away from Chris. The clock ticks on. The only other sound is the echos of the feet and papers shuffling about. Chris starts to fidget as he gets more and more nervous. The officer finally puts down the papers and looks directly at Chris. Finally he had enough. Chris couldn't take it anymore. He had to ask. 'What happened to Evan? Where is he?' Chris was starting to speak fast. The cop leans across the desk and places his hand on Chris' shoulder. 'He is alright. He is currently placed in a foster shelter. But don't worry, he is being taken care of. Now I understand you and your wife want to adopt Evan?' the cop informs him. Chris gets a little paler. He looks around and back down the hall where the waiting room is. He can see Tessa and the kids through the window walls. They are watching a tv placed in there. Chris smiles and turns back to the officer. He rubs his chin and lets out a nervous laugh. 'Uh you see. Tessa and I are not married. We are merely living together and dating. The kids are her brothers. He died and left the children to her. Now we raise them together in my New York apartment.' Chris bites his lip. He knew that wasn't want the cop was expecting. The cop starts to rub his face. He looked down at the desk at the papers. Chris let out a sigh. He knew this meant trouble. The cop had a bad look on his face. There was something in his eyes that showed he was looking for something to help the situation. Chris started to get worried now. 'What if they don't let us adopt Evan? What if they think we are unfit? It might be my fault. They know I don't even know who my own parents are. That is why they won't give us Evan.' Chris started to think to himself. Chris had to get a grip on himself. He was stressing out about this too much. His mind was racing in a million directions and he couldn't figure out why. Chris digs into his pocket and pulls out his wallet. He looks at his sheepishly then back down the hall to see Tessa. She appears worried. She looks at her watch then over at Chris. He forces a smile and waves to her. Tessa brightens up and smiles back. Suddenly Alex and Christa start bickering and Tessa's attention is drawn from Chris. Chris slumps into the chair and looks back at the officer. He is now going through his filing cabinet. Chris looks at his wallet again. He produces a small picture of a women from it. It has a slit down the face of it. The picture appears to be old. Chris smiles and rubs his index figure across the women's face. He whispers to himself, 'mom..' A sudden chill hits Chris. Everything freezes. Chris looks around confused. He can see everything doing exactly what they was the moment everything froze. A female officer is bring a cup of coffee to her cubical. A man in handcuffs is being pulled into the station, putting up a struggle. Chris looks across him to the cop. He is pulling out a file. Chris starts to get freaked out now. He jumps to his feet. Everyone, everthing. Froozen in time. 'Tessa' he shouts. He hits a quick 180 and looks at Tessa. Her, Alex, and Christa are all froozen. Tessa is trying to break up Alex and Christa, who are arguing with each other. Chris' heart stops. He rushes to the door. He pulls on the handle however the door is locked. Chris starts to slam the door. He yells out, 'Tessa! Tessa!' yet nothing. She remains like a staute before him, motionless. The sounds of feet scrapping the floor echo out. It gets louder and louder. It almost sounds like it can break the glass in the room. Chris turns and looks but before he knows it he's slammed up against the glass, a hand around his throat. 'Remember me! Long time no see Storm. I was starting to miss you.' the sick voice rang out. Chris stared into the eyes of his enemy. The man looked exactly like Chris. Only he had scars covering his face. The other Storm was dressed in camo gear. He looked like a highly trained solider of some kind. His grip was vice like. Chris would feel his throat being crushed. The glass behind him started to crack under the pressure. 'You're getting weak Storm. Very weak. You think you can defeat Clyde and Darkstar? Ha think again. You are too soft. They will rip you to pieces. And Jackson Rust? What are you doing tagging with him. He is the enemy. You are a disgrace. That women has made you pathetic. I mean look at you, getting so worked up just about a child. A true warrior doesn't need these things. He only needs combat and blood on his hands.' The man laughed. He released the grip on Chris' throat. Chris fell to one knee and gasped for air. The man looks down at Chris and spits next to him. Chris looks up at him. The man keeps laughing at Chris. 'You are a joke. Look at you on the ground. No wonder you can't get laid. If you can't stand here, then how can you stand in bed? I bet Jackson doesn't have that problem.' the other Storm gave a full hearted laugh. The room started to get darker. From the back of the room shines a weird light. The fake Storm doesn't even seem to notice it. Chris rises to his feet. He stares at the light intensely. It appears to be coming from a doorway. A figure is standing within the light. Chris focuses on it and he can make out that it's a female. She starts to walk forward. Chris steps back. The other Storm has still yet to notice her. She is wearing all white. It almost appears she is a ghost. Her face is pale and she looks weak. Her steps are unsure. One hand stands over her stomach. Chris finally realises who it is. The women from the picture. She sees Chris staring at her and smiles. She holds out her arms to him. Where her hand was, the dress is now red. Blood starts to pour from her stomach. Chris jumps back scared. The other Storm finally turns around to see what is going on. He sees the women and his jaw hits the ground. The man starts to look worried. 'Mom.' the other Storm says. Chris turns and looks at him shocked. Did he just call her mom? What is going on here? The other Storm rushes to the women's side. He grabs her in his arms. 'Mom, you shouldn't be up in your condition. Go back and lay down mom. You are so cold. You should try and stay warm.' the Storm told her. Chris was shocked. The man that was just crushing his throat was now holding his mother in his arms, taking care of her. The other Storm picks up his mother and carries her through the light which she came from. The light gets brighter and sudddenly flashes throughout the room. Chris shields his eyes from it. When he finally opens his eyes again he's back at the officer's desk. He looks around confused. Everyone is moving about now. Chris looks back at Tessa. She has finally calmed the kids down. The window Chris was slammed against is no longer cracked. Chris turns and faces the officer again. He has the file out and a smile on his face. Chris looks at him with a queer look. 'Mr. Storm you are in luck. I have found a way for you and your girlfriend to hold temporary custody of Evan. Until you are able to fully adopt him that is. But it will mean you will need to make some changes.' he said with a stern voice. Chris just nodding in agreement. He was still trying to shake off the effects of what just happened. He noticed he still had the picture out. There was a drop of blood on it. Chris starts to get scared. His hand starts to shake. He puts away the picture and looks back at Tessa. She waves to him and gives him a warm smile. Chris smiles back and gives a thumbs up. Tessa's eyes light up and she claps. He laughs slightly to himself and turns back to the officer. 'What changes?' Chris ask. The cop looked back over at the papers. He gets them in order and places them on the desk. Using his palm, he turns the papers around and slides them to Chris. He picks them up and looks at them. While Chris' eyes scan over the papers, reading each line, the officer fills him in on what needs to be done to be granted the temporary custody. 'For starters you need a larger living space. The apartment is not acceptable. It is only suppose to house three people.' Chris nods as the cop speaks. He is still looking at the papers. Chris thought about it and smiled. 'It shouldn't be a problem. Tessa has a two story home in Massachusetts we can stay in.' The cop smiles warmly at the news. However he turns Chris' attention to the second page. His has a more serious expression now. 'Second, Tessa and you will need to commit to a more serious relationship. Not marriage, but something more serious then simply dating. Temporary custody is rarely given to couples that are merely dating. If they break up it's hard on the child and the situation is harder to deal with.' Chris was taken back. More serious? He didn't know what to think. He looked back at Tessa. She was watching the tv with Alex and Christa. He smiled and turns back to the officer. 'Where do I sign?'
