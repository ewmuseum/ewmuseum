Making Amends

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/02/03 It is really unpleasant to have someone you know, especially someone who is extremely close, not support you and your decisions. It is really hard to take on a new venture, and succeed when you have no backing. Why you ask? You are vulnerable due to the fact that you feel as though no one cares, and you want so badly to prove everyone wrong so you can rub it in their faces. Brianna Washington is in this state of emotion. She is living out a dream that she has had for a while, and there is no one there to back her up and give her encouragement. Her best friend is too worried about her getting hurt, that he doesn't even take into account that she has the experience required to tackle such a dangerous sport as is wrestling. Now with that weight on her shoulders, her debut match is going to be one tough obstacle in her road to fame and acceptance. The sound of a blender shredding something to pieces can be heard loud and clear. When the scene opens up, we see Brianna pounding on the buttons to the blender. She pulls her finger off of the button, and the swirling motion stops altogether. She pulls the lid off and stops at that moment noticing Milo walking into the kitchen in his flamboyant colored shirt and dark gray pants. Milo catches her glance and takes a glance at the red open neck shirt, and black pants that Brianna is sporting. There eyes connect and this lasts very briefly as they carry on with the activities. 'Hey I have been meaning to ask, have you seen my...' Milo throws out hoping to start a conversation. He stops however because Brianna has migrated into the other room. Milo storms out of the kitchen and into the living room watching Brianna as she sips from her smoothie while staring intently at the television, offering no words or much expression at all. Milo stomps over the television and stands in front of it with his hands crossed over his chest. Brianna looks up giving him an arched brow. 'Do you mind?' she spurts while trying to look past him. 'Bri this is going to end now! I am so sick of you ignoring me all because we had one disagreement.' 'Disagreement? That's what you think? Milo it was more than a disagreement! You basically told me that since I am not built like a brick house, that I have no chance in hell making it in wrestling.' 'That's not what I was saying and you know it.' Milo throws out. 'Then what were you saying huh?' 'Never once did I say you couldn't do it. I was just trying to make sure that you realized that this isn't a game. I wanted you to realize that this isn't like your inventions where you can make a mistake and then start all over. One mistake in wrestling can end your career faster than a heartbeat.' his voice gets low and sincere as he takes a seat next to Brianna who is taking in his every word. 'Yes I realize that. But I think I can handle it, I mean I put up with you don't I?' She replies laughing a bit. 'You know, I have this thing covered. I may not win on Sunday, or Tuesday for that matter, but I will still make an impact on the whole of PCW. I will show them that I am not just another female athlete, but damn good one as well.' Brianna belts out as she stands taking her empty cup into the kitchen. 'Girl on Sunday you are going to kick some ass, and then on Tuesday you are going to kick some more ass!' Milo chimes in flashing a smile at Brianna. “Well I’m off.” Milo stands and starts walking towards the front door. ”Where are you going?” Brianna asks. “Don’t you remember? I have that hot date tonight.” Judging by the huge ear-to-ear grin on his face, he really likes this guy. “Oh you mean that firefighter you met the other day?” “Yeah. I hope he plans on bringing his hose because things are going to get hot!” He licks his finger and touches himself making a sizzle noise. He walks to the front door and exits. Brianna walks back into the kitchen and she starts slaving away on the dishes scrubbing off all of the impurities while humming to herself. Nearly a half hour later all of the dishes are washed, and it shows as Brianna’s fingers are wrinkled up due to the water. She walks upstairs to her room and as soon as she looks over to the mirror she stops. She has a big water spot on the bottom of her shirt so she quickly throws it off revealing her white bra and slender waist. She goes to her dresser and instantly pulls out a light pink shirt and throws it on. Just as she is about to leave her room, her eyes rest onto a letter sitting on her desk. Brianna walks over, sits down in her black rolling chair, and begins to scan the letter. The camera zooms in and we can see that it was her PCW acceptance letter outlining what’s to be expected, her pay, and all of that other good stuff. Am I really ready for this? I have put on this front that I am ready and rearing to go, yet negative thoughts still poison my mind. What if I have as rocky a start as when I decided to go on my own as an inventor? I don’t know if I can handle that pain and rejection again. I guess when I come down to it, my love for the sport will keep me focused…just like my love for inventing. There will be rough times no doubt, but in the end I am going to be out on top. It would be amazing if I could somehow combine my love for both things…..WAIT! Brianna takes off out of her room and runs down the hall and into her invention room. We recognize it right off the bat with the big table in the center of the room, but this time we also see all of the shelves stocked full of weird looking gadgets and gizmos. “I love both worlds, so why can’t I combine them together? I’ll get to show off my gadgets on national television, while giving me a bit of an edge in my matches! This is the boost I needed to bring up my confidence level!” Brianna rushes over to the shelves and starts looking around trying to find the perfect thing. After a few seconds her eyes rest on a small bulky watch. She pulls it off of the shelf and grasps it firmly into her hands, before placing it around her wrist. Brianna presses the large button centered in the middle of it, and a small rope shoots out and it hooks onto an opposite shelf. She presses a button right below it and the rope and hook retract, springing back into the body of the watch. “I’m going to give Jackson Rust and Mark Michigan a run for their money.” It is obvious that Brianna is starting to feel less and less belittled as she is finding ways to demolish her fears of messing up and not succeeding. The only question is, will it all matter in the long run? The scene fades to black.
