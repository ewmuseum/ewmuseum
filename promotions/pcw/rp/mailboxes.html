Date: now Ah, a clear sunny day in central park. What could be any better than a nice grassy knoll for a change of pace from all those towering buildings? Well, unfortunately, if you looking for some relaxation under a tree, or stroll under the sun, perhaps you should return on another day. Take an example from the city folk trying to find safety from anywhere but the recreational landscape. Many against one. It must be the worst pain ever. Several deep gashes in his sides, probably causing severe internal bleeding, mark where that savage Canuck made his move. His flesh, in at least four locations, resembles a smoking crater, all of which he has four eyes to thank. Lodged into his back, there are multiple ninja stars. His hair stands at its ends, the result of a great deal of voltage surging through his innards. Can things really get any worse? Bolting out of nowhere in the fashion of a torpedo, the busty red head of the team catches the outnumbered foe off guard, letting her fist guide the way straight into his spine. His vertebrae shattered by the sneak attack from behind, the unfortunate fellow folds in half over the tip of her outstretched arm, becoming the passenger on a ride heading straight for a side of a near by building. A pint of blood forces its way up his throat, and gets into the eyes of his transportation. Leaving her pulverized victim to continue on a collision course with a hard place by himself, the toned red head stops mid-flight to wipe the gooey blood and spit mixture from her eyes, as well as spit in disgust. But, someone’s immediately there to pick up where she left off… Perhaps a fate worse than smashing into a sturdy structure at well over 100 miles per hour, the human punching bag collides with a blast powerful enough to substitute napalm. Catching a glimpse of the perpetrator before the searing of his flesh, he saw a swindler pumping a deck of playing cards with surging energy, to the point of a purple glow. Dispatched towards the heavens after an explosive game of 52-card pickup, a broken man soars; longing for the moment he should touch solid ground again. A small gash here followed by swift blow there. Easily more irritating than an annoying swarm of bloodthirsty horse flies, a single blue demon plays games with his airborne prey juggling his limp body to prolong its journey. Armed only with a rapier, the smallest of his multitude of foes poses to be the hardest to avoid. Blipping from destination to destination in a flash, this one confuses him. Suddenly, he feels the force of gravity clutching tightly around him once again, pulling him towards the ground. Unable to move suddenly, he toys around with the idea that perhaps rigor mortis has already set in, not that he could do much at this point anyway. Stabbing in without warning, he finally feels the chill that has surrounded him. Encased in a block of ice from head to toe, the sensation of his own body freezing into dead matter… …Is quickly changed to a new sensation: A soft touch. The solidified prisoner is lower to the ground by slow working, and controlled force that is unseen by the human eye. “Scott, your optics!” Reluctantly, but complying, the leader of the team releases a beam of red energy through narrow ruby visor. Nailing his mark, the ice begins to evaporate, unable maintain it’s hard form under such extreme heat. Soaked with water, a bleeding, burnt, battered, and bruised mess of a man falls into the grass, coughing. Barely clinging on to the edge of reality, he uses the last bit of strength left in his conscious state to look at the eyes glaring down upon him. This is, or whatever is left of, Juan Borro. Gathering around the fallen target that lies on his back, men and women of a different breed surround him. Slightly confused by the order, they just glare down upon him. Looking up from the center of attention, Scott Summers, AKA Cyclops brings his shielded eyes to meet those of the most educated looking one of the lot. Cyclops: “Jean, what’s the deal? This guy is serious trouble… We can’t help him!” Meeting his accusing glare, she frowns. Jean Grey: “Um, Scott… Aren’t you forgetting something?” A blood-curdling ring, like several swords unsheathed at once, breaks the conversation. Short, hairy, and vengeful at heart, the adimantium boned Wolverine readies to make history out of the Portugal soccer punk. Wolverine: “Less talkin’, more gutting…” Jean: “Logan, wait!” The rabid Canuck steps back to pull the cigar from his grit teeth, and give Jean a suspicious look. In fact, there isn’t one in the entire bunch of them that doesn’t raise an eyebrow, curiously. Jean: “Guys, what’s wrong with all of you? HAVE YOU COMPLETELY LOST YOU’RE MINDS?!?!” “EARTH! WIND! RAIN! LIGHTNING!” Before anyone can tell Jean she’s the one in the shallow end, a powerful gust of wind rushes the team from all sides. Unable to maintain themselves on their own, they grasp one another, attempting to anchor down against the ferociously forming whirlwind! Blanketing the skies above, black clouds shed darkness across the city, and bolts of lightning crack the atmosphere like whip lashings. Only one being on Earth has the power to become the eye of such a storm, the weather witch, or more commonly addressed as Storm. Storm: “ELEMENTS, GIVE ME THE STRENGTH TO END THIS UNWORTHY, MALICIOUS, FOOL’S MISERABLE EXISTANCE!!” The Goddess has gone into a stage of destructive weather control that her allies have never witnessed. Her appearance is quite un-nerving as her eyes now take the form of raw energy, and her hair erupts ablaze. “Wait a moment, Storm!” Storms fury that fuels a gushing rainfall dilutes to merely a trickling drizzle. Her concentration a blur, she isn’t as quick as some to realize upon her shoulders sits the mischievous master of teleportation, Night Crawler. Night Crawler: “Storm, Jean said we couldn’t kill Juan. We all think she might be crazy, but…” Storm: “Get off me rat! Thou can’t fly with such a mongrel upon my person!” Down below, where everyone is scatter about, thoroughly drenched. The storm has ceased they observe, pulling themselves off the ground, and at least they can be thankful for that. Landing with a gooey splash, Storm and Night Crawler become victims of the gravitational pull, and immediately are unconscious. Rouge: “JEAN, SUG’R, I BETTER HAVE NOT GOTTEN MAH HAIR WET FOR NOTHING!” Gambit: “Relax, Cher’.” Shadow Cat: “Like, she better!” Iceman: “Whoa, chill out for a moment! I’m sure if we gave her the chance to speak, she’d explain her plan… TO GET THIS BUM WET FIRST, THEN LET ME FREEZE HIM SO HE CATCHES A COLD!” Why not just take this golden-toed coward out? It would be so easily, and quite frankly, exhilarating! The thought of it alone is delicious, but, desire to do so will never fill their appetite. An appetite for pure, uncorrupted, foul intended, and brutal revenge! They must devour, because deep in the depths of their hearts they are starving. “DANGER ROOM SESSION NOW COMPLETE.” A simultaneous reaction, specifically the heart stopping abruptly to skip a few beats, they now realize in horror what they have done. The pale, ghost white faces, frozen in shock seem to say it all. The original scene dissolves; however, those within it do not. There never was a park, merely a hologram created by this strange empty facility. The walls have a slight glimmer to them, and are blue, but other than that, they are just walls. “I believe you may have gone a little too far with that, my X-men.” Breaking the ice in the freeze frame, Professor Xavier swiftly glides into the scene in his hovering wheelchair. Accessing the situation the way only a powerful telepathic could, he reaches out to all their minds, a small brow rising almost seconds later. Professor X.: “I can sense your hatred for this man…” Meaning the bleeding Juan Borro on the shiny floor, at their feet. Professor X: “…Which is not a bad thing. But, unfortunately this was a training session, and this man is not your enemy, but rather, a friend.” Cyclops is the first to fall to “Juan’s” side, clasping him by the shoulders, with an attempt to revive him. Cyclops: “MORPH! ANSWER ME! YOU CAN CHANGE BACK NOW!” Wolverine: “BLAST THAT BORRO! MY HATRED FOR HIM MADE ME BLIND TO THE FACT I WAS KILLING MY OWN FRIEND!” Rouge: “Oh, mah God!” Gambit: “(Some Cajun rhetoric…)” Iceman: “It was only a training session! How could we let it go so far?” Wolverine: “The punk just looked so real, bub! We can’t blame ourselves!” Cyclops: “IF ONLY JUAN HADN’T DEFILED OUR HOME!” Iceman: “Remember guys… WHEN WE GET OUR HANDS ON THAT BASTARD, WE GIVE HIM AN EVEN WORSE BEATING THAN WE GAVE MORPH!” Rouge: “YEAH! DO IT FOR MORPH!” Convulsing blood from the mouth, Morph coughs… Cyclops: “Holy crap! He’s still alive?” “TO THE INFIRMARY!” Elsewhere… “Holy defiled property, Batman! This thing reeks of urine!” Right outside of Wayan manor, the caped crusading Dynamic Duo ponders over what resembles a mailbox completely in shambles. Toilet paper, brown nuggets, a yellow tint, not to mention the fact some one knocked it over! Batman: “Right, Robin… But, who could have been dastardly enough to do something like this?” Reaching down, Robin picks up a small slip of paper. It reads… “Juan Wuz Here!” …And in small print… “I live on (Censored)” Batman: “TO THE BATMOBILE!” Leaving a cloud of dust in their wake, the team of crime fighters race off to obtain justice. Emerging from some bushes nearby appears the devious, pint-sized villain himself, Syko the Goblin! Syko the Goblin: “Another happy customer… MUWAHAHAH!” Scampering away, the Goblin drops something: small slip of paper with the name Juan on it. fin
