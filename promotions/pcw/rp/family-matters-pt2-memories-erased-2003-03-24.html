Family Matters pt2- Memories Erased

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th March El Diablo is clearly in Turmoil, he has a sister he never knew he had. But How? Why? What? Where? When? Who?- There are so many questions El Diablo needs answering, and such little time in which to find those answers. While researching his family tree, Diablo found a startling discovery- or at least Patty did. Who is Patty? And what is that discovery? The statement to answer both those questions is this: Patty is El Diablo's sister. The Mexican Devil only ever thought he had a brother, Pedro and he passed on in that fateful car accident nearly nine years ago. The worst part about this shocking discovery is that El Diablo was hitting on Patty, without knowing she was his sister, and that made him sick to the stomach, so sick in fact that he spent the night in a bath tub cleansing himself from the utter filth of that moment. Where did this Patty Diaz come from? Is she for real? El Diablo is now with his sister Patty inside a Doctor's room. The reason why they're there is simple, a blood test to find out if she really is his sister. The Doctor's room is quite large, a neat white tiled floor encompasses the room, white wallpaper is up and the room has a window on its right, with a not so pretty view of the car park. The room is set out with the Doctor's desk in the top left of the room with two seats directly in front of it, a medical bed at the bottom right, where the tests will be done, several cupboards and drawers in the bottom left of the room and then wash basins and sinks all along the top right of the room. The room is indeed very neat, except for Dr. Freeman's desk, which is full of bundles of paperwork all around a nice computer. And on the wall behind him and his desk are all his credentials to prove he is who he says he is. Despite all this security, El Diablo doesn't feel the slightest bit secure, and that is completely understandable as his whole life has been turned upside down in only a matter of hours. Imagine in, twenty-eight years thinking you had one brother and that your life was meaningless and empty besides wrestling and now it is revealed you have a sister who at one point you were attracted to, and you simply do not know how to react. It's quite a disturbing dilemma to be in. 'So, how was your day?' Patty asks. The tension could literally be cut with a knife. Diablo looks up at her, and not a very happy look either. 'It was fine until all of this was revealed,' he snaps back at her, 'How I'm supposed to concentrate on wrestling after all this news is beyond me, but I know you're not bothered,' he finishes very bitterly. The beautiful Patty looks down at the floor, not in shame but by the fact she does not know how to act around her new brother either. Luckily for the both of them, Dr. Freeman walks into the room. Dr. Freeman is a young Doctor, in his early thirties and has short black hair, wears glasses and is about 6'0' in height. Dr. Freeman is wearing a blue and white striped shirt with a matching blue tie and black pants with black shoes. He is looking quite the part today. Dr. Freeman takes a seat behind his desk and looks at the open files he has. 'I understand you'd like to have a blood test?' he asks them both to make sure. They both nod instead of speaking, opting to be silent rather than suffer the tension that occurred earlier. El Diablo's main problem with this is that he was hitting on her and it really freaked him out. Diablo is quick to put his wedding ring back on now. 'OK, well who's going first?' he asks in his thick Californian accent. Patty stands up. 'If you'd like to follow me over to this medical bed please,' the Doctor requests. Dr. Freeman stands up, walks around his desk and leads the way to the medical bed at the bottom right of the room. Patty hops up onto it, the Doctor then opens one of the cupboards and pulls out a needle, he tears it out of the packaging and prepares to inject it into Patty to get a blood sample. The Mexican Devil watches, cringing just waiting for the needle to go in. 'So who is your father?' he asks suddenly and sharply to Patty. Patty looks across at Diablo rather than at the Doctor or the needle. 'Raul,' she replies sternly. Raul is El Diablo's father. It would seem they are most definitely related, and that these tests are just here to prove it. Diablo however does look a little bit taken back by her response. 'Are you certain?' he asks to double check. Patty nods, 'I'm positive,' Diablo hangs his head, knowing that his father had hidden from him that he has a sister. 'How old are you?' Diablo promptly asks right after. Patty smiles now that he is talking to her. 'Twenty-four,' El Diablo realises that for twenty-four years his father had lied to him. The very dame father that blamed him for Pedro's death had hidden something as magnitudal as a sister from him. The emotion is clearly hitting Diablo as tears begin to fill his eyes, but he quickly blinks to erase them. Because they were talking, Patty didn't even feel the needle go into her, so now she has sat back down and El Diablo heads towards the medical bed. He jumps up onto it and Dr. Freeman takes another needle out of it's packaging. 'I have pictures of our father,' Patty states. This prompts Diablo to look at Patty rather than the needle. El Diablo after all, hates needles. Despite all the pain he'll go through in wrestling, he just cannot stand needles. Go figure. 'Yeah me too,' he promptly answers back, 'he was an asshole,' Patty looks shocked, as she felt her father was a great Dad to her, and maybe he was, El Diablo doesn't know. Soon enough Diablo's sample of blood had been taken too, and so he leaps off the bed and heads towards the door with Patty trying to follow him. 'You can get your results in a week,' Dr. Freeman informs them. Diablo starts walking away fast to avoid being with Patty. 'DID PEDRO NOT MENTION ME?' she shouts to Raul, to make sure he heard. This bombshell causes The Mexican Devil to stop instantly in his footsteps, turn around and face Patty. 'Pedro knew you?' he asks in shock. Patty nods, locking her beautiful brown eyes with her brother's eyes. El Diablo's world had been rocked further, he and Pedro were so close and for him to have not told El Diablo was betrayal in his eyes. The brother he had worshipped was now little more than a memory, anger and betrayal now fill the void Pedro left when he died.
