Legacy of Hessain: Part 1

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/12/02 Welcome to Nosgoth, curious little soul. Welcome to a land where magic is as real as you believe it is, where technology has hardly touched the fingers of man... and where man himself has not yet become the sole source of darkness in the world. Our story begins inside a tavern in the village of Ziegsturhl. It's almost time to shut up shop, but a nobleman is in need of rest and refreshment. To think that one decision would have so much impact... The tavern, like the rest of this world, is medieval in design. Simple wooden beams and supports hold up a flimsy roof. Sitting with a mane of messy dark hair covering his face is the nobleman in question. He flops over to the bar, exhausted and weak. His pleas are met with indiscriminate rejection from the fat barman. 'The Tavern's closing. Best be on your way, stranger.' he spat. 'What? No mug of ale for a weary traveler from distant Coorhagen? I can reward you well, for I am of noble blood!' begged the nobleman. 'I stay open for no man in these dark times. Things come with the night that no sane man would welcome.' came the merciless reply. And so, he left. - cold of heart and soul. Forced to the road, and the long, bitter night. As the door locked behind him, though, misfortune shot her arrow once more. Standing in front of the nobleman was a mob of brigands and thugs, all bearing clubs and sticks like a pack of wolves. 'THAT'S HIM!' yelled one of them, charging. The nobleman drew his sword, but a smashing blow from the club of another thug cracked the bones of his hand. Defenseless, he could do nothing as the beatings broke his body up into pieces, other than yelp in pain. The assault continued for an eternity until someone must have seen a tear fall... or perhaps it was saliva from a sliced mouth. 'End it - NOW!' came the command. Walking towards the battered victim was a man in an executioner's mask. This whole situation had been planned with even the need to mask the brute that would commit the murder. He bore a sword that showed the tell-tale signs of the Serioli - Nosgoth's master craftsmen. At least, death would come with a certain shade of ironic glamour. As the sword slid comfortably between the discs of my spine and neatly cleaved the cord, I couldn't even hear my own death cry... And then... darkness. Pain. Torment. I awoke in a place that was not Heaven... rather, I was bound to a pair of poles by my wrists, with a sword still in my back. My face was no longer mine - it belonged to a hell spawn, bloodied and twisted by wooden strikes and bruises that robbed my looks. And here I was. Hades. Purgatory. Hell. Acheron. No matter it's name, I WAS DOOMED. At least, until I felt another nearby. 'Vae victus' has been a motto of mine. 'Suffering to the conquered'. And now, as I was conquered, I was suffering. I saw their faces, laughing and enjoying the slaughter. Not even the pillars of flame in front of me were burning enough to compare with my burning desire for revenge... and then a physical burning as the Serioli Iron sword was pulled from my back. My bonds released me and I turned to see a vision of Hell... 'How we diddling, Squire?' came the cheerful, yet still somehow grim greeting. Stood before me in robes of the darkest emerald green was man with a composure as deathly as mine. He announced himself as Morgazius, a Necroprankster. Master of fallen souls and the darker side of comedy. And he gave me an offer that I simply couldn't refuse. He took the sword by the blade and offered the hilt to me... and as I took it, my fate was sealed. Waves of electrical and magical energy flowed from his hand, coursing throughout my body and veins, altering my very being. My face was hidden by a skull mask, my hair burning red with my rage and my body had increased to match. I WAS REBORN. But at what cost? I was now undead... and to learn that nothing is for free. Not even revenge. 'You shall have the blood you hunger for. Arise... HESSAIN.' Morgazius' words echoed in my skull as my eyes opened. I saw pitch black and felt... enclosed. After a little thinking, I realized I was in a stone coffin. Shoving the lid off, I sat up, hungering... And I came to the conclusion. Life... would never be the same.
