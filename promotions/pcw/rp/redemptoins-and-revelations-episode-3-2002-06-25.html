Redemptoins and Revelations: Episode 3

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/25 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) Why is it that, in the Bible, whenever God seeks vengeance or reprisal, or seeks to set an example…..he sends an angel? Is that what lifelong Earthly servitude reaps? Millions of mortal men and women dedicate their lives to their faith, only to be elevated to the status of divine hitman to a hypocritical deity? It is questions like that which have plagued my thoughts and attacked my ever-corroding sanity in recent days following that debacle that claimed the lives of three US soldiers and led to a half dozen more being committed to Deerfield State Sanitarium. One of the deaths was caused by a heart attack near the onset of the Dreamland skirmish, the others are yet to be determined. Insanity, of course, is the terrestrial alternative to ‘death’ in the Dreamlands realm those loathsome deities inhabit. Whether it was sheerly their demise in those death-tinged depths of the Leng valleys or a glimpse of the nearing presence myself and Delta Fox merely felt just before we were pulled from that hellish plane. Only moments before we were returned to Earthly consciousness I recall that look in the eyes of Sergeant Maddox…..my God I’ll never forget the stark terror in his eyes as they lifted toward the swirling color in that perpetually unnatural sky, that demented gleam that radiated from within their sockets before being replaced by an icy glaze that still sends chills down my spine. What was being carried on those sinister winds as they wailed above the canopy I believe we will mercifully never know. That’s a secret contained in the mind of Maddox, and unless the day comes where he stops babbling in monosyllables and snaps from that eerie unblinking trance, it’s one he alone will take to his grave. And that, I believe, is the most merciful thing that fate can grant on him. Craven and McCarver have assured me time and again the Blackhelm brotherhood exists only because God has decreed it unto them to lead such a society. But if He so desires for this organization to raze those cosmic forces now inhabiting our world, why does He allow for such setbacks? Can He not grant some sort of divine protection for His servitors to prevent tragedies and tactical disasters of this scale? Whenever I plumb the depths of my knowledge of Christianity and God’s will, I only dredge up the repugnant vision of that amorphous blasphemy as he churns and bubbles at the center of all chaos to the eternal and monotonous tune of those accursed piping flutes, the abhorrent chaos incarnate which is Azathoth . I see the Crawling Chaos in his many forms, that of the gliding shadow devil which haunts the dreams of many an asylum detainee on a nightly basis and that of his Earthly pharaoh with the steepled pshent adorning his head and the blood-red eyes that throb with the fathomless knowledge of antediluvian existence. I see the tentacle-faced monstrosity Cthulhu as he slumbers the aeons away in ancient R’lyeh beneath the waves of the Pacific. But as much as I fight to envision the celestial in which I claim to serve, I see nothing but those abominations I wage war with. The contradictions, the inconsistencies…..there’s no escaping the truth any longer. For a while I trusted that I had finally found inner peace in my ever-so-subtle faith in Randall’s beliefs. Now, again, I fid myself questioning the legitimacy of both the mission and the purpose the mission serves. Still….with the dark cloud that was the Leng excursion finally gone, I can regain focus on my usual job in PCW. In just a few hours I climb in the ring with The Multiple Man, Kramer Wild Wing and Mike Frampton. Victory ensures me an opportunity to challenge that insipid Chrichton (or Jordan Shabazz, either/or suits me) following Gold & Glory on the 30th for the Television title. And even then, I plan on walking out of that particular event with Extreme championship gold wrapped firmly around my waist. As for the circumstances surrounding my possibly victory in both instances I’m not sure….assuming I eradicate those bargain-bin caliber ingrates at the Pay Per View and proceed to thrash either Chrichton or Shabazz shortly afterward, would I potentially become the only dual champion in this organization? Or would the council heading PCW get antsy about the prospect of a white-trash, bitter, vendetta-driven thug from the cesspool area of Boston becoming a chief representative of the company and conveniently retract their proclamation? I’m going to be blunt: I have no worry for that maggot Frampton, and even Kramer Wild Wing poses a rather minute threat in comparison to my opposition tonight and at Gold and Glory. THE Multiple Man. The enigma, the mystery, the lunatic, whatever he is. Hell, I’m still trying to figure that puzzle out. Time and again I’ve been sat back and watched those acid-induced trips in proverbial Wonderland, and I find myself simply entranced by the absurdity of it all. What I did to that inept Saotome was meant to serve as an example to all the detractors and the ignorant competition….but I don’t know much of an effect it has on a damned maniac living in a perpetual haze who has deranged conversations with blue tentacled blobs. Yet that basket case is all that stands between me and ten pounds of gold, not only tonight but at Gold & Glory. I have seen the unbridled vengeance of the nethermost abyss and vivid revelations of the prophesized Cleansing, and rekindled the childhood fears of demons lurking the shadows. Fears that were extinguished with age, but have been reawakened with the disclosure that their nonexistence couldn’t be further from the truth. And after exploring the subterranean labyrinths of the Leng valleys and standing before irreverent Azathoth himself, I firmly believe both my sanity is at its breaking point and my resilience is at an all-time peak. Someone must suffer for all my pain and pay the penalty for my uncertainty. This ignorance and desensitization has gone on too long. There is no God for me, and there is no Messiah. I am alone in a cold and bleak world, with no hope for redemption. And for TMM, Kramer, Lovecraft and the others…..it only ups the proverbial ante. It’s like a shooting gallery really, and God only knows I’ve pulled the trigger far too many times already back home. The time has come to turn away from those petty falsehoods, unlearn the lies forced down my throat and return to how things were. This is dedicated to the declining cancer of a species that is mankind. I’m through defending what doesn’t deserve defending, and I sure as hell am done with being the maintenance man for the ignorance that infests our nature. And I wholeheartedly apologize, on behalf of all human kind, to Christ for veritably spitting in his face for his sacrifice. But then again, I also think if Jesus would have known the human race would have ended up this decrepit and depraved, he would have instead voluntarily nailed himself to the cross....
