Until Death Do Us Part, Part I: So close...

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-16-2002 *It’s been hard for him to believe exactly how long it’s been since the girl he loved, perhaps the very essence of his soul had left him. 6 months already. A half a year of loveless torment had fallen over him. But Stryfe was a loner; he was accustomed to the pain, right? Not at all. Sierra had weaned him from the throes of isolation and Jeremy had broken out of his box, secluded and locked away from a world that can be so cruel. He had overcame the odds and had begun to start over from scratch. Once again opposition was ahead of him, more specifically in the forms of Red Rock and Ben J. Palithorpe. Sure, he was aware of it, but it’s far from the only thing he had mind. He simply lied in bed and drifted into his ever-buzzing mind.* “I miss her.” He unusually calmly said to himself, showing his broken heart through his words, “I wonder what she’s up to now and doing with her life. I suppose she’s found a new game to play.” *It wasn’t often Stryfe had displayed any human emotion that set him apart from many others people of his type, but for the time being his image meant nothing at all. *But come to think about it, what has Sierra been up to anyway? Surely, she had no intention of possibly thinking the man she left would come around to try and win her back, or in Jeremy’s case, forcefully steal her back. But sure enough, she lost faith in him. That was her mistake…* “Wherever you go I will find you.” *Connors wasn’t the only one who started over. Sierra had done the same. She was barely 18 and she no longer had any guidance in her life. She no longer had the gentle touch of his guiding hand to hold her up for all the world to see. She was alone… … Or was she? Actually, she had met a man to give her a guiding light. Matthew Howe was a young man, an old high school sweetheart as a matter of fact. For being an exchange student from Great Britain he had adapted quite well to the American direction of life. He was quite a wealthy man as well, inheriting from a rich, near royal family. He had bought his sweetheart a new car, a house, as well as many other things. Such things her old boyfriend could never provide despite his coveted PCW contract since all he ever did with the money was spend it on alcohol, cocaine and heroin. He never believed in living the life of an average rich man anyway. It seemed she had it made. Watching TV in the living that same night, she hears a fist coming in contact with the door. Someone was knocking.* She yelled out, “Who is it?” “It’s just me”, exclaimed the voice from the other side of the wood. *The door was locked, so she got up to open it to reveal Matt standing with a lovely gift; A bouquet of roses for his love.* “How sweet of you” Matt exclaimed to his love, “Anything for you my dearest Connie.” *Connie? Everyone had been exposed to her as the gothic wonder ‘Sierra’. Had she been reborn herself? It doesn’t even seem she herself knew. Either way, young Matthew had still been showing his affection no different than he was five long months ago: As if they had just meet. It was love at first glance. Little did he know those eyes would soon be blinded though.* Connie was still overwhelmed by the beautiful flowers he had brought her. She could only reply, “Oh, you’re too much.” Matt got down onto one knee to display his feelings, “No, you’re too much for me. I don’t deserve you.” He confessed. *Connie could only giggle with glee, still caught in the throes of ecstasy. She took his coat and the two sat down to continue watching their programming. Boston Public was on at this time. They watched knowing fully well that many of the overblown plotlines in no way reflected upon high school life as many had suggested.* One of the math teachers had walked up to the principal, their leader with a shocking revelation. He told him, “We have reason to believe Jimmy has a nuclear bomb in his garage, and Jessica is an alien from Mars.” Matt could only watch in disbelief, muttering, “Such compelling drama, don’t you think?” “Yes this show is something else. That reminds me that I should go back to finish high school.” Connie replied. “Yes. Education is of the utmost importance.” She then announced to him, “I guess so. If I’m going back to school tomorrow, I should probably get to bed early. I’ll meet you upstairs in a little bit.” “Alright then. Sweet dreams.” He wished upon his love. *She gave Matthew a kiss on the lips to wish the same as she walked up the staircase to the second floor of the house. She crept into bed, and closed her eyes to travel into an angelic slumber that would make the hearts of even the most hardened men melt, including the pair of glowing eyes shining through the window.* “Yes. Sweet dreams my dearest.” And if I die before I wake, I pray to lord my soul to take… *Connie woke up the next morning with the vision of her future brighter than ever before. She know the only way to climb life’s ladder was up and she was already ahead of pace. Soon though someone would be there not to keep the light on and hold the ladder for her, but to smash the bulb and burn the wood… slowly. Matt dropped her off at school. He didn’t go in himself since he had already graduated. She had much to catch up on since her escapades with the old trash she threw out long ago.* A voice boomed out from the front of the classroom. “Students please welcome back Connie Wizzman.” She exclaimed. *Silence. No surprises. Not often would you hear empathy from fellow students. They had many other things to worry about than just one person they have never met before. Most notably themselves. It was supposedly a rather routine day at school. Well, almost. It seemed she had been doing everything the right way for the day so far. To apparently one person though, that was not the case.* “You forgot me. You left me behind. You’ve become the hypocrite you feared.” *She kept hearing these strange voice throughout the day. She tried her hardest to ignore those voices though. She figured her imagination had gotten out of hand again. That was nothing new. Isn’t that what anyone would think?* *Staring at pictures lifelessly of Red Rock and BJP with his own blood splattered on them, Stryfe had felt a presence nearby. It seemed to tell him to take a look out the nearby window since that was what he did. What’s this? Without any effort, he had found his obsession walking out of school. As far as he was concerned, it was coming back for him and now the rest was up to him.* “Peek-a-boo, I see you…” *She had arrived home about 6 minutes later. When passing a mirror in her house, she noticed her appearance. It was disgusting to her to say the least. She ended up with the nickname “goth slut” from nearly everyone in the school. She didn’t want to be an outcast anymore. The hideous sight brought tears to her eyes as she rushed into the bathroom to rid herself of the crazy makeup and clothes. It took her almost a half an hour to get the look she wanted, but eventually she did. She had rid herself of her wild appearance. She rid her self of the scars from a paranoid sadist. She was just another girl.* “You can’t shake me off. You can’t shed your skin to escape me. I will always be with you.” *She could no longer ignore those voices. Something was opposing her every move and thought. Of course, she only knew one person who was able to play little games with another person’s mind.* “And he was incapacitated, he never would again do such a thing.” She thought. “I can’t keep thinking of him like that.” She then realized. “I have to move on.” Matt soon hollered up the stairs to Connie, still locked in the bathroom, “You almost done in there? I can’t stay away from your beautiful face any longer.” “I’ll be down in a minute.” She yelled back. *She finally stepped back out into the world to reveal someone completely new. She was gorgeous. She crept down the stairs ever so quietly as not to surprise her boyfriend who was sitting at the television set again. There she was for young Matthew and soon the whole world to see.* He could only ask in disbelief, “Is that really you?” “Indeed it is.” She replied. He then told her, “You look amazing.” She asked, “You really think so?” “Darling, you know I would never lie to you.” *She sat down on the couch next to him as they shared a hug, which became a kiss, which led to touching, then to grabbing and within seconds, hormonal bliss. With one strong pelvic thrust, “Sierra” had been stabbed straight through the heart. Stryfe couldn’t actually see this, but he knew very well what was taking place. He soon felt the rusty old knife in his heart bury deeper and deeper with each passing second, and it ached like never before. Connie was his sickness and he had to find a cure.* *Night soon came. That was when the nocturnal minds started to work. He had no map of the large town, no way of following her, but the one called Stryfe could sense her from all the way across the globe. He would go that far to find her, not that he had to though. She was only blocks away from her ex yet she had not a clue. Not yet at least. The hunt was on and he was out not kill, but to revive his love.* *At home the young couple shared a beautifully romantic candlelight dinner of chicken. Something bound to make anyone gain a few pounds. Either way, it was time for her nightly exercise. Since she moved she had begun a ritual of taking a walk around her neighborhood a few times. She had run into a few people here and there, but never anyone too far out of the ordinary. That would soon change. Sure, to he didn’t seem like such a strange being, but anyone would think that after staying by his side for so long. She still never felt any remorse for leaving him alone to die, but those feeling would catch up to her with interest.* “Hello Connie.” Erupted a voice from close behind her, “Or shall I say Sierra.” Acting out confusion, she simply stuttered to the figure behind her, which she could not make in the near pure darkness, “I-I don’t know what you’re talking about out, sir.” “Oh, I think you do.” *Almost like some atheist miracle of his own doing, lightning began to flash nearby to reveal the one she left behind to her. She could only gasp in horror, as she couldn’t believe her eyes. Was this really her old boyfriend who not long ago was a throwaway to society? She parted his familiar long black hair, which was almost completely covering up his face to see the eyes of death itself staring wide open at her.* “My god it really is you!” She cried, “What do you want from me?!” Stryfe replied, “Please, please. I’m not out to hurt you. I think you can find me to be a very sensible man, Connie.” He paused for a second, then continued, “I simply am here to take you… home.” *What was Connie to say? It’s not like she had grown to hate him. She still admired him for his spirit, but her mind was set. She found happiness with Matthew.* “I’m sorry Jeremy.” She soberly told him, “But I found someone new for me.” “Don’t you EVER call me that again!” Stryfe snapped back at her, “I am Stryfe to you, and nothing more.” *Another flash of lightning zipped by, and when the smoke cleared, Connors had disappeared from sight, but certainly not from Connie’s life forever. He would be back soon enough. In terror, all she could do was run back home crying. The minute the door slammed shut, Matt knew there was something wrong.* “What’s wrong dear?” He asked, concerned obviously. She just screamed back, “Please! Just leave me alone!” *She sprinted upstairs to not drown her sorrows, instead to drown herself in them. She now knew the error of her ways, and now she knew she made another one by not going back to the one who stitched her into his being. The tears soaked her pillow as she cried herself to sleep She had just committed another sin and she had forgotten to get on her knees and pray.* “Yes, Sierra. Cry to your hearts content. When your tears dry up, I will be there waiting.” Will you love her, cherish her and keep her until death do you part? I do…..
