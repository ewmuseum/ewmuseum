The Ghetto Prep

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Ghetto Prep Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 04/11/02 “Ahhh, another day, another 100,000 dollars!” Our shot opens up outside the PCW International Offices in London Docklands where the confident and content figure that is Xavier Kannon strides through the front doors of the majestic building, loosening his tie and day dreaming of his bank balance. “Like.... SO shut yo mouth cracka!” Kannon stops in his tracks and narrows his eyes, his head slowly turning to see his wife, Ellie~! leaning against the wall of the building.... Trying to look menacing. Xavier : “Um.... Ellie~!?” He walks slowly to her, casting a look of confusion upon his wife who is dressed a little more slutilly than usual. Eleanor : “What you, like, want cracka?” His face puzzled, the Scientologist tries to figure out what she is saying while she pretends to chew on fictional gum and sticks out her “Ghetto Booty”. Xavier : “Do I want a cracker?.” As his wife adopts a “You dissin’ me?” pose, he notices the Burberry head scarf she’s wearing and gestures to it. Xavier : “That new?... Looks good.” Annoyed, the Ghetto Prep points to the scarf and rants. Eleanor : “You, like, SO dissin’ my colors, bitch?” The usually giggly and playful Commissioner walks at him, gesturing wildly and trying to act tough. Eleanor : “Theez be, like, tha colors of da PREPS. Da damn preppiest gang on da west, like, side!” Finally it twigs in Kannon’s mind... The hours stuck watching MTV at the hotel while he works has finally seeped into the little prep’s mind like things tend to do given how impressionable she is. Xavier : “Are you being ghetto?” Eleanor : “Fo, like SO sho!” He sighs and shakes his head as the prep keeps up her “Acting Tough” stance, staring evilly at all the White people walking past and muttering... “Like, White people...” ... Kannon puts his arm around his wife and guides her. Xavier : “I think it’s time I took you home...” Ellie~! nods. Eleanor : “Like, yeah... Back to my, like, homies in ATL, playa, so we can SO stomp us some crackas, bitch!” The Scientologist rolls his eyes as he walks her through the Docklands.... *THUD* *THUD* *THUD* *THUD* *THUD* *THUD* They turn onto the main street where a Black sports car drives past with it’s tinted windows down and NWA blaring on the stereo. The little prep nods her head to the beat and sings along. Eleanor : “Like.. SO fuck da po-leez... Fuck, like, fuck da po-leez!” With Ellie~! still singing along, the car speeds off into the distance with a screech. Eleanor : “Like, SO fuck ‘em, cracka!” Kannon looks embarrassed as all the passers by look at his wife and shake their heads at her language... She just looks right back at them with her usually innocent pale Blue eyes and retorts. Eleanor : “Cracka.” Another gaze, another retort. Eleanor : “Cracka.” Xavier : “You’re range of insults seems rather limited.” She snaps at him. Eleanor : “Shut yo mouth, snowflake!” Coming the opposite way is a man walking his Black Labrador, the little prep nods as she looks at it pass. Eleanor : “S’up, dawg?” *WOOF!* Eleanor : “Fo sho! Keep, like, rep’n ATL and stuff!” Then a woman walking her White poodle walks past. Eleanor : “Damn crackaz wit’ cracka dawgs!” Their stroll takes them past a Dixons store where in the window, rows of TV’s are on show, all showing the sports news and the football highlights. Eleanor : “Like... Sup, playaz?” A goal is scored and 5 players... 5 White players are shown hugging in celebration. Eleanor : “Like, SO get dem gay assed crackas off my TV!” Not really sure what to do about his wife’s sudden turn to the cult of Ghetto, he decides to change the subject and hope she follows suit. Xavier : “So, what do you want to do for dinner? I was this great Italian place just around the cor-” Eleanor : “Shut yo whiny cracka mouth, foo!” Cut off in mid-sentence, Kannon’s eyes follow where Ellie~! is gesturing. Eleanor : “I ain’t eatin’ no cracka food, bitch!” The little prep is standing outside a KFC.. Savouring the smell of Fried Chicken while eyeing herself over in her reflection from the window, turning to check out her behind. Eleanor : “Yo, snowflake.. My ass look big in this?” Trying to be diplomatic, he looks at her ass which looks lie she’s shoved a couple of pillows down her pants. Xavier : “Um... Kinda...” Eleanor : “Datz good.. Don’t be wantin’ no cracka gilrz ass, I gotz me a ghetto booty goin’ on!” She pushes the doors open and storms past the elderly couples trying to leave. Eleanor : “Getz outa my way, damn White peepz...” Xavier following behind and still looking embarrassed, Ellie~! storms towards the counter and marches to the front of the queue. “Hey!” “There’s a line here, you know!” She just shrugs the protests off. Eleanor : “Shut yo cracka mouth before, I, like, SO pop a cap in yo skull!” “Er.... Can I help you?” The counter worker nervously starts to serve her as she snaps back. Eleanor : “Shut yo cracka mouth, bitch!” Taken aback, the minimum wage worker cowers while the Ghetto prep looks at the menu, not seeing what she wants. Eleanor : “Gimme sum collared greens, grits, sum fried chicken wit da grease stuck to it an’ some watermelon.. You get dat, cracka?” “Er... We don’t ha-” Eleanor : “What? yo don’ got that for a, like, nigga? You only SO given’ dat to yo fellow crackas? Now, like, SO get me ma damn food, snowflake!” Finally, Kannon walks up to his wife who’s still muttering “Cracka this” and “Cracka that” under her breath and cautiously speaks to her. Xavier : “Um.. Ellie~!... You're white!” Eleanor : “I.. Like.. Am?” Kannon nods. Eleanor : “Wow, that is, like, SO embarrassing and stuff!” The shot fades to Black on Ellie~!’s blushing face. “Hey, it’s, like, hard to remember what color you are sometimes!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
