Evil dreams.

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th April One man can be pushed, buckled by huge amounts of pressure. Just because you’ve been through a strict communist army, seen your parents die at their hands, and now wish to get revenge, doesn’t mean you’re not susceptible to being able to be bent by your own brain. As someone once said, Sivasian Krikorian should be near unbeatable, 260 pounds of solid muscle, fast, strong, has a huge pain threshold, the only way people can win is if Sivasian Krikorian BEATS Sivasian Krikorian. However, many of people were breaking from the pressure, as was Sivasian. The pressure was huge. James Edwards, making his debut in the three way match as well, was cracking just as much. His mental state could be questioned, as probably could every man in the four way match. Sivasian sunk his head into the pillow. It dropped down and absorbed his heads weight. Sivasian, clad in a purple silk pajama pair of tee shirt and shorts, huddled himself inside the bed. It was cold outside, and since his training in the Ukraine, in the exposed mountains outside of Kiev, he had hated the cold. He pulled up the covers, and rolled over to kiss his wife good night. As he snuggled back into a sleeping position and slowly fell into unconsciousness , the worriers of the world were removed from his shoulders. Sivasian loved sleeping, you never died when you were sleeping, you never really got hurt when you were sleeping, and hell, if things REALLY got back, you could wake up. In the ring a fist from SOCIETY and then a dropkick creates pain, but you can’t wake from that ‘dream’, oh no, he was in PCW for the long run. Sivasian awake in his homeland. He instantly recognised the bright yellow corn fields from the flatlands of the Ukraine. Some had said that it shone as though it were the sun in the middle of summer. Alas though, it was not summer, and the yellow looked duller than normal to him. He searched round, his hometown, his birthplace, the place he grew up, met his best friend Brian (the reporter in earlier rps). He walked forward, his feet touching the soft grass. He looked over to the horizon and knew that the horizon would be nothing but thick bushes and trees, flanked with two roads. He knew this from heart. Suddenly, in front of him he saw a sniper rifle. The thing which he had cherised throughout his life. The thing which had earnt him his fame, and somewhat of a fortune. He lifted the gun up. This had saved his life many times, many many times this had got him the shot and kill one second, one millisecond maybe, before the other sniper had seen him. BANG! He knew what it would feel like to shoot. This comforted him though, for he liked to be here, and peace was with him here. He’d never had to shoot someone in his hometown, and so this had been his place to associate with peace. Somewhere where he knew he wouldn’t have to kill people. However, today felt different to him. The gun felt heavier, and his senses, something not quite as ridiculous as the superheroes have, but a general sense there was somewhere out there, maybe in the cornfield, maybe in the woods. BANG! A blistering shot ripped through his head, he felt the blood spurt through his eye, and flap down across his body. He shook his head, he wasn’t dead, he felt nothing. Getting back into his army role of some 5 years previous, Sivasian turned and withdrew his pistol, something with which he was assustomed to fire without using a scope. He span and dropped to one knee, offering better stability and more accuracy. He didn’t need to think, it came naturally to him. His eyes, now somehow fully recovered from a bullet through the head, scanned the landscape up ahead thoroughly, nothing. Another bullet whistles past his head, he can hear it, he can feel the push of air through the sides of his hair. He breathes heavily and drops down to a more natural sniping position, and scans the horizon again. He pulls round his sniping gun, his favourite too, and peers through the scope. He sees nothing for a fraction of a second before something darts between trees ahead. Smirking to himself, he mutters. ‘Got ya now, American.’ He stops for a second, takes a deep breath and lines up, rustling his feet for a second in the grass. The target moves again. Sivasian rests the buck of the gun on his shoulder, and focuses harder. He places his gloves hands over the trigger, as sweat beads form on his fore-head. He takes one more breath, and squeezes lightly as the target once again appears in his crosshairs. He pulls harder and a bang goes off. The figure ahead drops down, as Sivasian feels the recoil of the gun into his shoulder. He smirks to himself, before almost instantly reloading another bullet into his gun. ‘Rusty, but good….’ He mutters as he stands up again, only to see Stone Burns running towards him as fast as possible. Almost a knife wielding maniac you may say. Burns has a red blood head and gritted teeth. He jumps as he nears Sivasian, and sweeps the knife right to left furiously. Sivasian back tracks, and falls. Burns jumps onto him, but Sivasian grabs his arms, and rolls him over. Both men appear eye to eye for a second, before Sivasian twists the hand of Stone Burns and plunges the knife deep inside his foe. He sees the eye of Burns lock straight and blood rush from his mouth, as the knife pushes through human skin and muscle. Sivasian, confused, looks ahead. ‘That building wasn’t there….’ Says the sniper, as he wipes his brow, hot in the midday sun, and picks up his fallen gun once more. Sivasian peers back to make sure Burns is dead, only to find his body has disappeared. Sivasian shakes his head, and carries on, wondering what’s going on. Believing this is actually happening to himself, he cannot believe that such surreal events have occured. His troops towards the building, scoping the front door and the open top roof it has. He slings the gun on his back, and redraws his pistol. Suddenly, some weight appears to have been taken from him. He feels, and to his surprise he finds the gun he has cherished and loved for so long gone, his sniper rifle, it’s scope and it’s ability to kill a man upto 1500 metres away, all gone. He sighed as he clocked back his pistol and raised it with two hands. He pushed through the door and scanned the room, nothing but a plain metallic looking floor and a small table in the corner. Sivasian looked around, aware that only Matt Jackson was left of the four men he had to face on Tuesday, and being a secret agent meant he was doubly dangerous, and knew how to handle a fire arm. Sivasian slipped back outside, immediatley feeling the midday sun on his neck. He saw a vertical ladder leading to the roof, and climbed it slowly, each step seeming to tire him out more. A letter and a gun lay ahead of him, the same gun which had mysteriously disappeared earlier once again layed, this time with just one bullet in. The letter read along the lines of; ‘You’ve got one shot to kill me sniper, can you get the Agent?’ Confused, Sivasian lifted the gun, and peered out. Suddenly another figure appeared, this time in the complete open. Sivasian laughed again, and peered through the scope. He took no time at all squeezing the trigger, followed by the usual jolt. In the distance the figure fell. Sivasian smirked to himself, before exiting down the ladder again. He walked through the field to the figure, now some 50 metres in the distance. He smirked once more, happy his job had been done. Walking upto the figure led to a huge confused look on his face, shock you may say. He spoke quitely and slowly as Jackson’s shot burst through the air. ‘Mom….?’
