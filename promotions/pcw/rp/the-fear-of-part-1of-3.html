Date: Dec.11/02 The air was bitterly cold, and reeked of rotting flesh. As I walked down the pitch black hallway, the screams of the other small children sent shivers coursing through my body. I had no idea what the next few meters had in store for me, which was why I walked right into a wall. After I shook it off and caught my bearings again, I continued on my way . . . Suddenly a large, fire breathing clown jumped out in front of me . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ With a scream, I jumped out of bed and froze in my tracks. I swung around wildly, searching my room wildly for the fire breathing clown; I was alone in my room. That same nightmare has plagued me for years on end . . . Ever since, I have never set foot inside a Haunted House . . . I was always more of a roller-coaster type of guy . . . Haunted Houses were just not my thing . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As a child I had always been frightened easily. Horror movies, the Boogie Man . . . yes, even circus clowns . . . you name it, I was scared of it. However, as the years went on, I grew out of most of my childhood phobias . . . all, except the Haunted House. And if it weren’t for the Extreme Title being on the line, I probably would have withdrawn from the “House on Haunted Hill Match” already . . . Knowing that my career in wrestling is currently on the rocks, I knew that I had to tough it out, and fight not only twelve other men, but my greatest fear as well . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Instead of worrying about my ordeal, I finally decided to face my fears so I wouldn’t be at such a disadvantage during my match at Fusion. It was three in the morning, and after that awful nightmare, I knew I wasn’t gonna go back to sleep. I figured I’d make the most of the time I had, so I flicked on my computer and headed to the PCW Homepage to check the latest news. Everything was pretty much the same, so I looked to see if there were any changes to Fusion’s card . . . and right there . . . at the top of the page . . . the words “House on Haunted Hill” drilled a hole of fear right through my eyes and into my brain. It was as if the words were looking into my soul, and awakening all the fears that I had tried to hide for all my life. I knew that I had to put an end to those fears . . . I had to . . . I just had to . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I began searching the internet for articles on Haunted Houses, and which were the scariest. I knew the only way I could conquer my fears was to boldly go where I had never gone in years. I found a website that gave a listing of all the Haunted House attractions in Michigan, however, they were all closed by the end of October. This would present a problem for any ordinary joe, but I’m a wealthy pro wrestler . . . so I called up Michigan’s number one Haunted House, The Haunting, and booked a time for the next day . . . for a price of course. If any Haunted House attraction could scare my fears away, it was this place . . . It was voted the number one in all of Michigan for the third straight year . . . 20,000 square feet of haunting . . . 2 houses of hair raising, nail bitting, goose bumping terror . . . That place sounded intense . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I had finally fallen asleep, resting my head on the keyboard. By the time the sun came up, I had managed to type roughly forty pages of “tvAgfrMb55U45ftLhbraEq5rhyTbzae4' as I tossed and turned my head over the keys. The nightmare of the clown had not returned, and for that I was thankful. I had already lost enough sleep for one night . . . and I still wasn’t as refreshed as I had wanted to be. I only had a few hours to get ready and drive over the fair grounds where the Haunted House was set up. I managed to take a shower, eat breakfast, and shave at the same time . . . then rushed out of my apartment building, only to find a parking ticket on my windshield. Just great . . . that’s all I needed . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Would I get to the Haunted House on time? Would I face my fears? Would I be ready for the House on Haunted Hill? Find out next time . . . same Michigan time . . . same Michigan channel . . . BOO ~ !
