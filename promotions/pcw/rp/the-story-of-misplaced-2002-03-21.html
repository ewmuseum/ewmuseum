The story of Misplaced

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/21/02 Journal Entry: ………………. The scene opens with a pudgy man standing in the middle of the New Zealand streets. A man who has nothing better to do that just stand around and ponder over things. Or is he? It turns out to be Carey J. Nowski, the newly signed PCW Wrestler. Should he have been signed? Who knows, but he is there. Carey, not a very talented wrestler, is just standing in the middle of a cobblestone road in the middle of a New Zealand city. The streets are stifling with business, and marketers are scrapping for business. Carey continues through his journey of this unknown land to him. Strangers fight for a look at this stranger walking down the street. Carey seems to have no exact destination, but he is rather roaming. In his ventures, Carey proceeds to a small arena. Out of curiosity, he wanders over to the arena. People are ragging the stranger in favor of money, business, or supplies. A rush of people are entering this arena, and a curious stranger wanders over to see what the hoopla is. It appears to be a cricket match, a very popular Middle Eastern sport. Carey doesn’t know whether he should try and get in, but at the first glance of the rush he decides he’d rather not. A bit frightened, Carey pulls out his Fox Mulder doll to comfort him. Through the crowd, a rowdy fan confronts Carey. His name appears to be Stephen Cairns, from his name tag. He’s wearing a silver Polo Shirt with white stripes down the side, and silver pants with white stripes all around. It appears that he is a cricket player, but he is most certainly not. He has a ticket in his hand to get into the arena, and he notices that Carey does not. Stephen Cairns: You…You over there! The weird guy with the doll! Carey turns to find the odd stranger holding a ticket in his face. Stephen Cairns: What do you say to a cricket show? I have an extra ticket here. Carey appears not to know what to say. He quietly approaches the man, and he grabs the ticket out of his grasp. He studies it reluctantly for a moment, but he then hands it back to Stephen Cairns. Stephen Cairns: Come on man! Join me! It’ll be something new…from the appearance of you. Carey looks at the man with a look of….something. Carey: What’s that supposed to mean? Stephen Cairns: Nothin’…I mean, something! I mean…just come to the game with me! It’ll be a blast. It’s all on me. Reluctantly, Carey nods his head in agreement. He yanks the ticket, and they silently enter the long line of rowdy cricket fans trying to enter the game. They enter the small stadium, and see the two teams on the field. Carey looks as if this game is insane, and it is to him. He looks is awe at the drunk fans scattering throughout the arena. They search the crowd for there seats, and they finally come across them. They take there seats and sit silent, staring at each other for a moment. Stephen finally makes an action by pulling out a replica cricket paddle and waving it up in the air. Carey just looks in amazement at the excitement a grown man could have over such a silly sport. Finally, Carey breaks the ice and says some things… Carey: So wait, what’s the name of this sport again? Stephen: …….You MUST be kidding! Carey nods his head no with a serious look on his face. Stephen: How do you not know about cricket? It’s only about the greatest sport there is! Carey: Really? Stephen: Really. Carey: Is that so? Stephen: God d*mn, Yes it is! Easy with the annoying questions! Carey: Why? Stephen looks at him with a look of anger. Carey quickly grins, but he straightens up his face soon. Carey: So what is the objective to this game? I mean…I see so many people here to watch it, but I’ve never even heard of it. Stephen cannot believe the words coming out of this mans mouth. He just chuckles, and proceeds to explain the game. Stephen: Amazing, just amazing! I didn’t know people didn’t know cricket! Carey: Well, we don’t have cricket back in the states. Stephen: Ah…an American! I see. You guys have your “hockey”, and rip-off “football”, but we have good old cricket! Lets just start by telling you that right now New Zealand and England are squaring off right now! New Zealand is in the black, and that’s who we’re cheering for! Got that, b*tch? Carey: Right…so how do you play this game? Stephen: For that, you shall have to wait! This amazing game can only be described through the actions of the play! Carey keeps a straight face, but slips a chuckle at the strange speech patterns of this man Carey: Is that thing that you have on a cricket uniform? Stephen: Yes, of course. This is an official New Zealand Cricket uniform! I even have the pads! Carey: They wear pads? Stephen: Of course! They even wear a mask, which I have right here! Stephen pulls out a black mask with a white face guard. He slips it over his head, continuing to confuse Carey. Carey: Alright, what are the rules of this game? You don’t just whack each other with those sticks, do you? Stephen: Ah…it involves nothing like that! Cricket is a team sport for two teams of eleven players each. A formal game of cricket can last anything from an afternoon to several days. Although the game play and rules are very different, the basic concept of cricket is similar to that of baseball. Teams bat in successive innings and attempt to score runs, while the opposing team fields and attempts to bring an end to the batting team's innings. After each team has batted an equal number of innings the team with the most runs wins. Got that so far? Carey: That’s the most intelligent thing you’ve said tonight! To bad I don’t understand a thing you just said! Stephen: Well that’s just too bad, because you’re going to hear the rest anyway! Now ask me more questions! Carey thinks for a moment, but he can’t come up with anything. Carey: It sounds like baseball. Stephen: No! It’s so much different, and better! Carey: Alright…how is it played? Stephen: I told you the basics! It’s to much to describe to you. You’ll just have to watch and see. Here we go! New Zealand bats first! Carey watches on as the odd game gets under way. He notices the style, and is convinced that it is baseball. Carey: You’re filthy government didn’t steal this from baseball, did they? Stephen: How dare you! Of course not! Cricket is so much better than baseball! Carey: Are you any good? Stephen tries to ignore the question. Carey: Are YOU any good? Despite the anger in the shout, Stephen still ignores Carey. Finally, Carey smacks Stephen upside the head to get his attention. Stephen: What? Carey: Are you any good? Stephen: Any good at what? Carey: Dancing and eating cake! What do you think? Cricket! Are you any good at…*snort*…cricket? Stephen: What was that snort all about? I’ve never seen someone snort like… Carey: God d*mn you! It’s a problem! Will you just answer my question? Are you any good at cricket? Stephen finally shrugs his shoulders and answers. Stephen: I’m ok…ah, I’m great! I’m going to be a cricket star. You just wait and see! A drunk fan from behind overhears the comment. Drunk Fan: Ha! Nice try with that one, mate! Stephen: God d*mn you Aussies! Why don’t you stay in your own filthy dump of a country! Drunk Fan: Shows how much you know! Australia is a continent! Stephen laughs at the moronic comment, and continues to bicker with the Aussie. Meanwhile, Carey stares at the cricket game in awe, still convinced that the European government stole the sport from baseball. He’s finally redirected to the real world again when some shouting breaks out! Stephen: AUSTRALIA IS A COUNTRY AND A CONTINENT! You stupid….stupid…Aussie! Drunk Fan: Way to go lad, want a cookie? I’d suggest you shut your trap before you get your arse beat! Stephen gets a look of rage on his face. Finally, he exits his seat and gets in the mans face. Stephen: Nobody talks to me like that! Nobody! Especially a pitiful Aussie! Drunk Fan: How dare you! You should have respect for such an elder! I know more about cricket than you and your friend combined! I can take you any day! Stephen: Was that a threat? A threat? To me? You better say that wasn’t a threat! Carey sits quietly awaiting the mans response. Stephen and the Aussie start to draw other attention, including security. Drunk Fan: As a matter of fact, that was a threat! What are you going to do bout’ it? With no more words said, Stephen takes a big swing at the Aussie. The Aussie grips his lip, but he quickly throws punches back. Drunk Fan: You……shouldn’t….have……done….tha… Stephen blocks the punch, grabs the mans arm, and flips him over his back and onto the concrete. Carey’s jaw drops at the sight of the great move! Carey: Where’d you learn to fight like that? Stephen: No time to talk now kid, duck! Carey: Duck? Confused, Carey quickly figures out what Stephen meant as he feels a beer bottle shatter over his head. Carey quickly falls to the ground and grasps his head. A pissed off Stephen charges over to the beer bottle man and tackles him to the ground. He grabs a beer bottle and gives the man a taste of his own medicine by continuously beating him with the shattered parts of the beer bottle, giving him major cuts on the forehead. A man jumps Stephen from behind and starts to thrown punches, but Stephen rolls off and gives the man a shot to the privates. The man crouches, and Stephen then delivers a huge kick to the nose of the man. A full riot has broke out through the crowd. Stephen runs over to check on Carey, and notices that he is back in his seat, watching the game. Stephen manages to rush out a question, or an attempted one. Stephen: How can you just….sit….there through….all of this? Join in on the fun! Carey: I find this sport to be rather interesting. I’m just enjoying the game. Stephen: Don’t come crying to me when… Stephen feels a tap on his shoulder, and immediately wings a big fist to the man behind him. He then realizes that it was a security guard. He just shrugs his shoulders and jumps back into the fight. Chairs are being ripped off and used as weapons, and every given object is flying through the air. So far Stephen has taken out a security guard, two fans, and he’s now going after a police officer! Stephen charges the police officer who is armed with a club, but the officer whacks him immediately with the club. Stephen wriggles in pain on the ground, but he manages to trip the police officer as he tries to leave. With great stamina, Stephen makes his way back up. He climbs on to a seat and jumps into the police officer’s arms! The officer is stunned for a moment. Stephen: Hey b**ch! Police Officer: Screw you! You have to right to say that stuff to me! Stephen: Screw me? Did you just tell me to screw me? Well, that must be what you meant, because you’d need to find your d*ck to be able to screw me! The police officer looks at Stephen as if he’s insane to say that to a man of his authority. While Stephen is still in the mans arms, he starts to punch the officer quickly! He jumps out of his arms and kicks him. The officers stumbles back and bangs his head off a seat, knocking him out cold. Stephen: Oops…that wasn’t supposed to happen! Stephen quickly rushes out, but he is surrounded by a swarm of security guards. They all bombard him with whacks of clubs, knocking Stephen to the ground. They cuff him and carry his bloody, unconscious stump out of the arena. Carey catches the action and rushes after the guards. Carey: Hey…hey you! The security guard with Stephen quickly turns around, but makes a sigh of relief at the sight of Carey.Security Guard: Yes? What do you want?Carey: Where are you taking him?Security Guard: Oh, this guy? I’m taking him in to the station. We’ll see what happens with him from there. The security guard turns and leaves Carey standing there, but Carey makes one last shout. Carey: What station? He regains the security guards attention, and the guard responds once again. Security Guard: He’ll be going to New Zealand Police Patrol. Don’t worry, he’ll be alright, just a few nights in the cell for him. The security guard notices Stephen flinch, but he quickly kicks him in his head to knock him unconscious again. He continues to drag him, beating his head many times off of the concrete steps. Carey just follows the man down the steps, but he keeps his distance to not make it seem so obvious. Hours Later We pick back up with Carey in a police station, confronting the head chief. Carey: I just want to see the man! Why can’t I do that? Chief: Because he is not allowed visitors right now. I’m sorry, but that’s the way it has to be! You’ll have to wait until I approve him of visitors! Carey: We’ll you’ll have to approve him now! I really need to see him! It’s an emergency. Chief: Are you hear to bail him out, or are you family? Carey: No… Chief: Then I’m afraid you can’t see him. Please get out and take your business elsewhere. Carey frowns and turns his back to the chief. After some quick thought, he rushes back to the chief. Carey: Is there an ATM Machine here anywhere? Chief: Go down the hall, and it’s next to the restrooms. Carey hustles down the hall, and finally spots the raggedy ATM machine. He withdraws $1000.00! He folds the money into a nice packet, and returns to the counter with the chief. Chief: Can I help…Oh, it’s you again! Carey: Yes, it’s me again. How much is it to post bail? Chief: For Stephen Cairns, bail is set at $750.00! I’d imagine that you don’t have that kind of money on you, so I’d be on your… The chief is interrupted by Carey pulling out the enormous wad of money. He shifts out $800.00, and puts it in the chiefs grasp. Carey: Keep the change! Now, can I see Stephen? Chief: Just…yeah…whatever! The chief points the way to Stephens cell, and Carey rushes back to meet him. He sees a bruised Stephen lying on a bed asleep. The cell is very small, with a toilet, bed, and a sink. The walls are chipped all around, and the ceiling is falling apart. Carey knocks on the bars twice, and finally just screams Stephen’s name. Stephen jumps up, and is extremely surprised to see Carey. Stephen: What in the blue h*ll, what are you doing here? Carey: You’re free, I posted bail and you can go on their word of release. Stephen: Why’d you do that? Thanks man, but I don’t need your help. Carey pauses for a moment, and thinks to himself. Finally, he brings himself to speak to Stephen. Carey: No, I actually need your help! Stephen: What? What do you need my help for? Carey: You see, I’m not just a tourist. I’m a professional wrestler. I’m part of the PCW, Primetime Champion….. Stephen: Yeah, I’ve heard of it. Wrestling is all fake anyways. Carey: That’s what I believed at first also. Carey pauses for a good moment. Stephen gets impatient and starts things back up. Stephen: Well? Get on with your point. Carey: You see, I’m a tag wrestler. Only, I don’t have a tag partner. Back in NTICW, I had a partner by the name of Riot, but he left me to wrestle singles. He saw better career opportunities, and a bunch of other crap. Stephen: If you’re asking me to wrestle, the answer is most definitely no! Carey gets a sad look on his face, but the determination still shows in his eyes. He paces around for a moment to regain his composure, and finally returns to the cell. Carey: Look, nothing is going for you here! I’m giving you an opportunity to come back and get a real job! You’ll be loved! I was watching you fight at the cricket match earlier, and you can really deliver some blows! You’d make a great wrestler, and I’d love to have you as a part of my team! What do you say? It is the least you could do! Stephen: No! No way! I’m a cricket player, and I’m not giving that up for wrestling! I don’t even know a thing about wrestling! And, I’d have you as a partner! No offense, but I know you can’t fight. You’re to, um, you’re to much like you! If I’m going to abandon my career, I at least want to do something where I’ll be successful. Carey frowns again, but looks like he won’t give up on getting Stephen to wrestle. Carey: How much do you make from playing cricket? Stephen: Well, to be honest, I don’t exactly play, yet!! I will though! I can make you sure of that! Carey: How? How do you know that you’ll get a career in cricket? Meanwhile, I’m giving you a chance to take part in a career that you know will get you a job. It’s already set up. Think of it, you’d be the star fighter of the team! You could control the team! Stephen gets a more re-assuring look, and continues to think about it. Stephen: Lead the team? You mean I’d get to control the league? Carey: Well, no, but you’d lead this team! You said it yourself that I’m no good! Stephen grins, and finally nods his head! Stephen: What the he*l! I’ll do it! You suck, and I rock! That’ll make a great team! Carey: I detect sarcasm? Stephen chuckles, and gets up off the bed. Stephen: Yeah, sarcasm, but I’m willing to do it. I’m still going to be a cricket player though, ok? You got that? Carey: Yeah, that’s fine. In fact, I want to learn a little more about this base…er…cricket game anyways! Maybe I can even play a little! Stephen: That’s a laugh! I mean…yeah! We’ll give it a go! Now, can I get out of here? Carey goes and gets the chief to unlock the cell, and they finally walk out of the station together. The streets are cold, dark, and empty. It doesn’t seem to affect the two as they strut down the street together. Two days later We see the two again entering Carey’s conspiracy building in Seattle, Washington. Stephen looks at Carey as if he’s insane, which he practically is. They enter his office first, and then they go up to there apartment. Stephen: This is our place? This small apartment? Carey: Yeah, it’s not that great, but it has everything you need. Don’t worry, we’ll be traveling a lot anyways. We’ll barely every be here. Stephen: Good… Stephen tosses his belongings on the floor and starts to look around. Carey: By the way, we have a team name? Stephen: What’s that? Carey: The name for out team. Tag teams in wrestling usually have catchy names. We’re now officially “Misplaced”. Stephen: Oh, I get it! A talented jock like me, and a nerdy wrestler like you! It’s brilliance! It sounds like something I’d think of! Carey chuckles, and he also tosses his bags on the floor. He clicks on the computer, and accessed the official PCW Homepage. He checks the card, and to his shock he finds that Misplaced is booked. He checks last weeks card, and he also finds that they were booked then. Carey: Oops… Stephen: What? What did you do now? Carey: Nothing, but somehow we had a match last Sunday. Stephen: A match? We didn’t even know each other then. Carey: I know, that’s very odd. Carey continues to ponder over this. He shakes his head, and pulls his Mulder doll out of his pocket. Carey: Nah…it couldn’t be government backed, right? I mean, Stephen, you’re not a government man, are you? Stephen: What? Have you lost your blasted mind? Carey: Yeah, I must have. By the way, we have a match on Sunday. It’s our first match…that we know of! We need to be in Albequerque, New Mexico by Sunday morning. I’ll book us a flight. Stephen: Don’t worry about that now, lets go out. What are some good places around here to party for the night? Carey thinks for a moment. Finally, he comes up with his favorite place. Carey: Ah yes, I like to hang out down at the milk bar with Mulder! Stephen: Oh man, you need some help! We’ll take a ride downtown and see what we can find! Go get dressed! Stephen slips on a tight black t-shirt, and a pair of black jeans. He spikes his very high spiky blond hair, and sprays on a bundle of cologne. He actually looks half civilized, until he is seen next to Carey. They exit the building and enter the car to find a place to “hang”. That’s all we see of them as they round the corner in the bright green Volkswagen.
