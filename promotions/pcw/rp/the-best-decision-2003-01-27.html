The Best Decision

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 27, 2003 DING DONG DING DONG The clock inside the house strikes Noon signifying that there are only twelve hours left in the day and that the other twelve hours have already passed. Although it was nice to know what time it was without even having to ask it just added more stress onto the bedroom situation. If we wanted to get the kids in bed and asleep on time then that would leave us nine more hours to get everything unpacked and to talk over the bedroom situation with everyone but as I looked over and noticed that there was only one box left to unpack. Breathing a sigh of relief I made my way towards the kitchen where Chris was preparing a lunch for the children and I. I sat down at the kitchen table laying my head on the cold wood of the warm cherry colored table and closing my eyes letting my mind drift. But it wasn’t long before Chris cam over to keep me company as I felt the warmth of his body heat wrap around me and he sat down in the chair beside me and rested his chin on my shoulder. “Tessa Bear, is something wrong? You look all upset.” He whispered into my ears while brushing a strand of my light brown hair out of my face, behind my ear. “No it’s nothing important. Don’t worry I’ll get it handled eventually.” I said while strumming my fingers against the polished warm cherry table top. “Don’t lie to me, I can tell something is wrong with you.” He said, grabbing onto my nervous hand and holding it inside his hand that is closest to the table. “Do you really want to know?” I said, turning around to face him, giving him my full attention. “Of course I do.” He answered in a warm tone, looking me eye to eye to show that he does not want me to hold back anything from him, no matter what emotions it may spring upon him or I. “I don’t know what to do with this whole bedroom situation.” I answered with a lower head, almost ashamed that I couldn’t solve a problem as simple as this. If I could not even manage to solve a childish problem like this how did I expect to help Chris win the Tag Team Titles this Wednesday against Nerezza and Gaz Green? “Don’t worry about it Tessa. I’m sure no matter what choice you make it will be the right now.” He said, placing his hand under my chin and lifting my face up from its downward tilt and then kissing me on the cheek in attempts to cheer me up. “Well do you think Alex is right with what he said?” I asked as I smiled slightly to let him know that I was alright. “Well he is right about one thing. I love you to death Tessa Bear. But as far as sharing a room goes it’s up to you. I have no problem with sharing a bed but I don’t want you to think I’m rushing things or in this relationship for the wrong purpose.” He looked me sincerely in the eyes using his body language to explain that I could trust him that I would have his support no matter what I decided. I simply nodded back to him before my mind wandered off topic and something else of a different matter came to my mind. “Hey Chris I noticed something. You left that box of uhhh …well… you know what behind at the apartment didn’t you?” I started to say in a softer voice than before. I hated to bring it up knowing that it might stir up a problem but at the same time I knew that even though Chris was no longer into that type of lifestyle he might want to still have that box just for memorabilia. “No I didn’t leave it behind. I threw it out.” He answered looking over at me wondering why I would ask such a question considering the contents of that box had caused much turmoil within our relationship not too long ago. “You did what?” The shock nearly knocked me out of the seat. Was I hearing the words I thought I would never hear? Maybe the whole box had something to do with his sudden change of heart about Jackson or maybe he just got sick of being looked down upon because of his actions in the past. “You heard me, I threw it out. After a bit of thinking, I realized why you got upset. After all I got upset over a little hug between you and Jackson, but after seeing me with another woman like that you must have been a lot more upset than I was about Jackson. Besides, those pictures mean nothing to me now that I’ve found you.” He flashed me an affectionate smile as his blue eyes locked in with mine and I could feel the great amount of love he had for me. “Has anyone ever told you how great you are?” I asked standing up from my seat, walking over towards him. “All the time.” He teasingly responded as he too stood up from his seat. “But you know what would make me even greater?” “What’s that?” I asked curious as too what he thought could possibly make him any greater of a person than he already was. “Becoming one half of the PCW Tag Team Champions with you.” He said with a wink before walking off into the hallway to finish unpacking the final box.
