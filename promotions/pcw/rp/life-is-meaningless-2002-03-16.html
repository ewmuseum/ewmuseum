Life... is... Meaningless

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th March 2002 Here I am once again, going round circles inside my own head. I try to find the doorway out of my mind but as always it eludes me, it having been lost a very long time ago whilst I was still young an innocent. Innocence lost... What a foretelling that must be, to loose one’s innocent while still at just such a young age. Anyway, back to the matter at hand. Chris Chase. What an ass. God, no I just can’t think about this damned match, there is just too much on my mind. He talks about me as if I’m some great big threat. I’m not a threat, I’m just a man and I don’t pretend to be anything different and from that I gain strength. He talks as if he is of some sort of weird consequence to me at all... but he means absolutely nothing to me at all. I’ve been booked to fight him, therefore I fight. I don’t particularly want to get into the ring specifically to kick his ass from pillar to post, but if there are staff members out there that feel he needs help in getting placed into a main event, then therefore I must help him in that small way. I am simply a man who fights and nothing else, so why on earth should I pretend to be anything different? I am not specifically special in any manner; I simply know what I want. And all I want for Christmas is my two front teeth. If only that was the truth. I wish it was with every bone of my body. If I could I’d ask the doctors to suck my dead corpse clear of that life giving blood to save Natalia... but I am of no consequence... I can be no help... I am meaningless. And so I do not stay, there is no reason for me to stay... her father is flying over on Sunday and I have no idea what I will say to him. What should I say to him? “Hello there Natalia’s Dad, I’m sorry I can’t remember your name but I think it might very well be Boris or maybe Sergei... shame I can’t really ask her because she’s in a Coma from an attack by a Czechoslovakian ass who I can’t even get revenge against... oh and while he was attacking her, I was in the ring talking my mouth off. So Sorry once again.” Somehow I am of the feeling that he might very well be quite annoyed. So annoyed that I expect the guy who looks like Ivan’s twin to attempt to rip my skull out of my body. Maybe then they can at least use my festering organs to save my wife’s life. Jeremy Howard walks into the room... he’s wearing one of those Spring Fling t-shirts with Boda’s face all over it. They handed them out earlier... I take a glance across the locker room at mine... sitting in the trash upon several sheets of screwed up poetry and a copy of the “Little Book of Calm”. He carries in his hand an envelope and holds it out to me... I take it quickly and place it on the dressing table next to me, not really taking much notice of it. Jeremy: I know your down, but it will get better. I glance up at him for a second, my mind wanting to believe my friend but my heart and soul just keeps telling me that I failed... no, worse... I failed HER. I made vows to love and protect her always and I failed HER. I promised I would always be there for her to protect her and the baby... and even worse... I failed both of THEM. Jeremy: I used those when I was down over my brother and it helped a lot. Jessica said it might be a nice gesture to pass them on. When you, Natalia and Mini-Miya are all back up on your feet find someone who feels like life as pissed on them and pass them on. I don’t even speak to him, I just look at the image of Boda emblazed on Howard’s chest, slightly deformed because the Krayzie one is obviously wearing a shirt a couple sizes too small. He follows my eyes and looks down at the t-shirt. Jeremy: I’m about to get a quick workout in... I didn’t want to sweat in a decent one. A smirk appears on my lips for a few seconds as a muscle reaction to the joke, but my eyes tell a different story because I’m in no mood for comedy of any type. Ben Stiller stabbing himself in the eye with a pitchfork would probably get a laugh, but I doubt I would actually mean anything by it. Jeremy: Listen, you be good, alright? I nod at the Krayzie one who shuts the door on the way out. I glance over towards the envelope and eventually reach over and grab it. I look down at my watch... the show hasn’t started yet... it’s far too early... I thought being here might take my mind off my problems, but instead of sitting in a hospital corridor on my own, I’m sitting in a dressing room on my own. Am I even in my ring gear yet? Have I taken my shower? I sniff the air... that’s a negative. Screw it, I’ll have a shower later, Chris Chase can smell my sweaty armpits for all I give a damn about the New Breed lackey. At least Snow had balls. I open the envelope... it wasn’t sealed properly, in fact it looks rather well used. It’s probably the original envelope. I unhook the flap and open the envelope out, not exactly knowing what to expect... inside are some little white cards... I pull them out and put the empty envelope back on the table. They’re blank on one side each, so I flip them over. They appear to have writing on them. Miyagawa: Better to light a single candle than to curse the darkness. I quizzically place that particular white card onto the dressing table before looking at the next one which is apparently a quote by a person called “Booker T. Washington” I blink a couple of times but the name doesn’t change... I disregard the possible joke and read the quote out loud. Miyagawa: I will permit no man to narrow and degrade my soul by making me hate him. I stop for a second and read that quote, just to myself, inside my head once again. And then once again out loud just to confirm it. Miyagawa: I will permit no man to narrow and degrade my soul by making me hate him. I slowly think about that some more. Boda is trying to get me to hate him, but if I do, will my soul be forever etched in hatred. Just because his heart is black doesn’t mean I should turn my heart to black just to defeat him. I place that card to the side of the other one for a second... before picking it back up and placing it in my jacket pocket... the jacket obviously being hung off the back of the chair I sit upon. I look down at the next card and read it too out aloud. Miyagawa: Darkness cannot drive out darkness; only light can do that. Hate cannot drive out hate; only love can do that. I glance down at the card again. Great; Krayzie gave me Psychic cue cards. That’s just fantastic. But Martin Luther King didn’t answer one riddle... what can drive out greed? I walk across the room to my duffle bag and open it up and pull out my ring pants and as I do so a small piece of paper drops to the floor. I dump the pants on top of the duffle bag and pick the piece of folded paper up and unravel it revealing a poem. “Life is a great sunrise. I do not see why death should not be an even greater one.” - Vladimir Nobokov I look down at the piece of paper written in Natalia’s own script for a few seconds. When did she write this? Did she know was Boda was planning? Why didn’t she tell me? Why? Dammit, why?! A splash of water hits the paper and causes the edges of the “v” in Nobokov to run slightly. Only do I ten realise that the splash was in fact a tear of my own making. I fold piece of paper up and pull my wallet out of my trousers, placing the piece of paper inside. And then shoving it back into the pocket of my trousers on the right side. Chase... Chef... Steele. Meaningless to me. All of them. Nothing has any meaning anymore. Miyagawa: I will permit no man to narrow and degrade my soul by making me hate him.
