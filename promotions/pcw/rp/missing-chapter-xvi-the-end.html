Missing: Chapter XVI: The End...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: FINALE!! While Ross (Edwards) is sleeping, time to pound his ass with some rps! Here I was. Sven was only a merely twenty feet from me. The last time I had seen him was over a year ago when I stopped his last devious plan. Now I was here to stop his current plan and I wouldn't leave this place without ccomplishing that. Sven: Well, there you are my brother, Stephen. What is up? His tone was sarcastic, much like it had been when we were children. Sven liked to joke around a lot and came off less serious than he was at most times. I was Sven's finger move very slowly to the trigger on the rifle he was holding. I could grab the pistol that I had tucked into my inside jacket pocket but that would take to long and by that time, I could be dead. Instead, I quickly pushed a small red alarm in my pocket. I acted like I was scratching my knee and triggered the silent alarm. Police knew where I was and would be here to help in a few minutes. I had to stall. I broke out of my dream and realized that Sven had his finger around the trigger of the rifle. He was slowly raising it up to get me. I had no chance but to run! I dashed towards where Clair Rivence and Henry Claval were silently filling a container with gas. Shots rang out through the area. I was being shot at! I tried to run in zig-zags to avoid Sven's shots. As I got closer to the seen where Henry and Clair were, I realized something. Beside the container where they were filling gas, was a toy car, a regular kids one. I had read in the Project 84 booklet that I had stolen that the bomb would be disguised in a toy car. If the toy car and gas were here, than I guessed that the battery pack was to. The battery was a 200 volt that didn't look like it had much power. Believe me, it had power. I guessed that today was the day when Project 84 would go off. In a field! The placement where the bomb was wouldn't matter because the impact would instantly anybody within 500 miles! While being shot at from my brother, I noticed that about fifty metres from the big PCW ariplane, was a smaller jet. One Henry, Clair, and Sven were in the air high enough, the bomb would go off and the three would be safe. This was it. A rifle shot created smoke right near was I was! I quickly grabbed the gun out of my jacket pocket and aimed ahead. I tried not to hit the PCW airplane but I'm pretty sure that a shot hit the wing, no damage. ~~BANG~~ For a moment, I thought that I was shot and it was all over. Waking up myself, I realized that I had hit Clair! Her female body dropped to the ground. I sort of felt guilty for shooting Clair, being a girl and all, but she was in this just as much and it was only business. Henry Claval looked at Clair on the ground and noticed me shooting at him. Leaving the scene, he fled. Trying to take the Project 84 bomb with him, I fired and hit the box with the toy car! A small explosion rang out. Henry dropped the bomb and dashed for the jet. I shot at Henry the best I could. The shooting lessons I had taken helped but I couldn't hit him. I arrived near the airplane and stared down at Clair. Blood was vividly pouring out of her mouth, she was dead. I scanned the area nearby: X- SyN X- Project 84 bomb X- Small Jet I stared at the small jet where Henry was already climbing into. The familiar face of Sven poked his head out of the jet. I dashed at the bomb to save it! Sven: This is not over Stephen! You do not know what we have planned! The jet's propellars starting spinning around and the jet lifted off of the ground. Henry was driving and Sven had his head out of the jet. I was right near the bomb. Sven: Die!! I knelt down to pick up the bomb. A shot landed right near me. It was Sven and he was shooting at me and the bomb! If Sven managed to hit the bomb, it wouldn't cause that much damage, just an explosion. It would only hurt anything really close to the bomb, ME! As the jet became louder and lifted off into the air, Sven shot a solid bullet. It landed in the middle of the car box, sending a ringing through my ears! My mind went black and I fell to the ground, losing track of everything that happened next. I had stopped Sven's plan again, but failed to capture Sven himself! ~~FADE TO BLACK~~
