The Flashbacks [Chapter Five] - Revelations I

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd of July, 2002 The day began with a warm breeze flowing through the air of Memphis. A car, driven by a hurrying Egyptian, rushed past as Tom and The Pharaoh hasted onwards back towards Titu’s home in California. Isiy had been given quite a shock, apparently. Peter StCross, whomever he may be, had continued to ring the house asking for a man named ‘Charles’. Titu had a feeling that he knew the name… ‘Charles’, he was sure he’d heard it somewhere…and no he was sure it wasn’t just out and about on the street. The mystery caller wasn’t the only thing that The Pharaoh had on his mind either. You see this coming Wednesday he had to once again step into the squared circle– this time with Corey Williams. Corey was an egotistical asshole with a big problem, which was his arrogance, actually, but he had a bigger problem, he wasn’t well liked within the confines of the PCW locker room. People didn’t like the fact that he thought he was controversial when in actual fact, and to be frank, he was about as controversial as a toilet roll tube. Xansiv and Tom rushed down the highways and byways until finally they were greeted by a large sign reading ‘Welcome to California’. Pharaoh sighed a breath of relief, as did Tom; after-all the entire journey from Memphis had been long and strenuous; and managing to get from there to California in a day and a half was simply amazing. Pharaoh drove a few miles along a winding road until he came to a grinding halt outside a huge mansion. Pharaoh explained that this was his home to Tom, who immediately congratulated Pharaoh on owning a brilliant place such as this. Pharaoh sighed in disappointment… he had wished for a temple style home, but thanked Tom for the compliment anyway. Pharaoh walked steadily up to the house’s front door and knocked. Moments later the huge well crafted entrance swung open and Isiy rushed out, embraced Pharaoh passionately and kissed him for a time that, if it had been deducted from the life of a mayfly would have surely killed the tiny creature, or shortened its life span significantly. “Isiy, my love” Pharaoh began, “I came as quickly as I could…are you alright?” Isiy’s brow wrinkled slightly. “I think I’ll be fine now that you’re home.” she sighed, “But I’ve been so scared…someone’s been ringing the house none stop for a day now…it’s been awful!” Isiy grasped her husband’s arm in distress before noticing the presence of Tom. “I see that you’ve met Tom, Titu?” she asked. “Yes,” Pharaoh smiled, “…I have…” his gave slowly fixed upon his personal assistant. Tom waved to them both, in a way that someone who didn’t quite know how to react to this kind of situation would… “Now, come! It’s time I sorted out this problem!” Pharaoh made his way through the front door into the house; Isiy and Tom followed him abruptly. He edged carefully towards the telephone and immediately noticed that the answer machine was full… “Hmm…” he huffed, thoughtfully examining the machine. Pharaoh cautiously pressed a large green button upon the answering machine’s shell and listened to three of the countless messages, all of them were practically the same. It was fairly spooky, he thought, the guy seemed so intent on finding this Charles guy, and why had he continued to ring this house out of the thousands of others in California? Was he THAT sure that he’d found the right place? Questions…Pharaoh hated them… He turned the machine off after the third message ended. “Tom, go into the lounge, please. I need to talk to Isiy for a moment…alone.” “Yes, sir.” said Tom, as he calmly walked out of the hallway. Isiy stared up at Titu, her face a portrait of confusion. “Isiy, when this man rings again, I shall answer the phone…everything’ll be fine, I promise.” Isiy tried for a smile, but could only settle at a half grin. “…I was really scared, Titu…I didn’t think he’d stop!” Pharaoh hugged her; a tear was in her eye… “Now, you must be hungry?” she said, sniffling. Pharaoh grinned, his pearly white teeth glittering the light from the Californian sun outside. “Actually, I am!” Isiy nodded and left the hallway, seemingly headed for the kitchen to prepare the hungry Egyptian warrior a meal fit for a ‘former’ king! Pharaoh walked away from the hall and entered into the lounge; Tom sat on one of the couches, a smile splashed on his wearily little face, flicking through the channels on Titu’s TV. “What a day,” began Pharaoh as he slumped himself down on a couch next to the one Tom sat upon, “I still wanted to go to Gold and Glory! I needed to scout out Corey Williams and what better way to do so than by watching his match with Jon Kano?” Tom nodded, “Don’t worry, Mr Xansiv, you’ll be able to beat that punk!” Titu looked at Tom doubtfully, “Tom…you do know who Corey Williams is, right?” Tom opened his mouth but Pharaoh cut him off before the words formed. “He’s a damned former World Heavyweight Champion!” Pharaoh stated, “…no matter how he won the title…” he added, swiftly. “But, sir…you are far more talented!” Pharaoh smiled, pleasantly surprised at Toms confidence in the Egyptian. “Well, thank you, Tom. At least someone had faith in my skills!” Tom nodded thoughtfully, “But anyway, sir…what’s goin’ on with this mystery caller?” Pharaoh sagged and rubbed his eyes wearily. “The Gods know, its probably some crazy fan trying to get through to the house with an alibi, which, if that’ IS the case, is a very poor alibi…” he paused, “Charles? Ha!” Tom’s face curiously tightened. “But, sir, I thought you said you’d only just moved in? You said nobody else had your new number other than PCW headquarters? Pharaoh thought about this, it was absolutely true… “Yes,” he began, “you’re right…I don’t know, then…maybe they got the new number from the telephone directory?” This was also true, it could have come from there; the Xansiv’s number was most certainly listed, Isiy had told Titu a few days ago… “When that maniac rings again, I’ll make sure to give him a piece of my mind, though!” said Pharaoh, an expression half between anger and satisfaction knifed upon his face. Pharaoh waited, and waited…and waited some more, and still the mystery man failed to call; which was a damned first. Things remained like this for forty-five minutes and then, to Pharaoh’s delight, the phone rang. Titu leapt to his feet, like some sort of predatory frog, rushed to the phone, and got there just as the answering machine kicked in… Hi, it’s Peter StCross again, Charles ring me--… Pharaoh picked up the receiver and cut the man off in mid sentence… “Hello? This is Titu Xansiv, I don’t know who you’re--…” “Xansiv? You mean Charles Xansiv DOES live here? Are you Charles’ son?” Pharaoh didn’t quite know how to react. “Erm…no…this is Titu Xansiv, the owner of this house, now I don’t know who--…” “I’m searching for a Charles Xansiv…he was an archaeologist who went missing years ago on a construction site…” At these words an image flashed across Pharaoh’s eyes. It showed a man walking across the desert towards a temple. He wore the sort of clothing an archaeologist would perhaps wear. The vision faded away and Titu was left with the sound of the man’s voice in his ear. “Hello? Mr Xansiv?” asked StCross “Yes, yes, I’m still here…” “So do you know of him? It’s remarkable, though! You sound just like Charles, too…” Titu’s brow wrinkled… “That cannot be true! I am six thousand years old!” “Excuse me?” the man said, bewilderment in his voice “You heard me………didn’t…you?” For the first time in years, Titu realized how stupid that sounded…six thousand years old? That was impossible…wasn’t it? “You’re six thousand years old?” asked the curiously baffled StCross. “Well, err…no… of course not! I meant twenty…six…seven, even! Twenty Seven…and my father died many years ago…so no, this is not…Charles Xansiv’s son…” There was silence for a moment. “Then it is you! At last, for so long I have tried to track you down, and I find you in California, Charles!” “I have already told you! I am NOT Charles Xansiv! Now stop ringing this house--…” “The diamond…do you still have it?” said the man, abruptly. Titu’s face was a portrait of confusion. “Diamond? I own no--…” Then he himself remembered the tiny lucky charm he had owned for many countless years. “Shaped…as a…heart?” asked The Pharaoh, with a gulp. “Yes.” Said StCross, to Pharaoh’s horror. And suddenly it came to Titu. He was Charles; there was no mistaking that his voice, his last name, his diamond…they all pointed to the inevitable truth. He was the ‘Charles’ StCross was searching for. Pharaoh gasped…his face pale as a sheet…he didn’t know how to react. “But…but…how do you know me?” he asked, a stutter of distress in his voice. “I worked with you many years ago…we were archaeologists, we worked on the same site in Egypt together.” Pharaoh’s face was paler than it had previously been just a moment ago, he could feel memories coming back, but no! They were not true, they couldn’t be! He knew his past! He knew what his life had been like…then he tried to remember his days as The Pharaoh of Egypt, six thousand years ago…and he couldn’t …now in his mind he could only picture things that didn’t make any sense to him…not from the way he was thinking, anyway…he saw men digging and landscaping, he saw the same two people, the man and the woman, from his dreams…he saw a temple, ruined and shattered from sand storms, weathered away so that no detail could truly be made out. “You lie! I have never met…a Peter StCross before in my LIFE!” he wailed, frantically. He didn’t want to remember the truth…he truly didn’t but it was all coming back to him now…but no! He thought, they are lies…all of them…the concussion must of ruptured them to seem like the truth. Yes…that was right…the concussions were to blame… “Please, Charles, you have to believe me…what happened to you out there? On that fateful day? What have you become? Can’t you even remember the good old time? Me and you would go out and get drunk after a hard days work out in Egypt…We’d go out on the pull…that’s where you met Isabelle…remember?” Isiy? Was this guy telling a lie? Or was he telling a truth? Pharaoh couldn’t make up his mind. He remembered something…something about that voice…he knew it from somewhere, but he didn’t want to know where from, yet he knew it was from where StCross had said…back when they had been friends…years ago… But Pharaoh didn’t want to know…he ignored it. “Now, Mr StCross…I don’t even know you, so, so--…” He paused…he knew he couldn’t say it…so much had been lost…so much needed to be gained. “You won’t put down the phone, Charles…for you know, deep down in your heart that what I say is the truth…” Pharaoh shook his head violently. “No! You LIE!” “I don’t…and you know it…” Pharaoh was silent…then he spoke… “And what proof do you have that I am this man? …This Charles?” If Pharaoh could have see Peter StCross at that moment he would have seen the man smile in a thankful sort of way, as though he had wanted Pharaoh to ask this question. “I have enough, my friend.” Pharaoh contemplated this. He needed to prove this guy wrong…he WANTED to prove this guy wrong. For what he said was obviously untrue. Pharaoh shook off any thoughts that he could possibly be this Charles fellow and recaptured his confidence… “Then I will meet you, Peter StCross…and you can prove that I am who you believe me to be!” “Settled…meet me in the town square under the clock at three O’clock tomorrow…and be there, for it’s time that you knew of the truth…” StCross put down the phone leaving Pharaoh standing, bewilderment flowing through him. He snapped out off in a sudden rush, which led him up to his room. There he searched through his bags, through his draws, through his clothes. The diamond, he though, where is it? “Titu? Titu?” Isiy’s voice rang out through the house, like a hot knife through butter. “Yes, my love?” answered her husband, wearily. “Your dinner is ready…” she shouted…then he heard her gently say “Will you be joining us for dinner, too, Tom?” He lay back, looking up at the white ceiling. Pharaoh wasn’t a man to be outdone…he hated that…he would meet StCross tomorrow and confirm the man to be mistaken! Xansiv smiled. But first he’d have to have something to eat…he was starved! After that he’d have to find the diamond… He left the bedroom and set off downstairs… Tom still sat upon the couch tucking into the food Isiy had prepared. Pharaoh sat on the opposite couch from Tom next to Isiy who had left Pharaoh’s meal on the small glass table. It rested upon a green mat as not to scratch the glass’s delicate surface…Pharaoh picked it up, sat back in the chair and began to eat. “So, what did you say to that man?” Isiy asked at length. Pharaoh wondered what he was going to say…he couldn’t tell Isiy about meeting StCross and he couldn’t tell her about his fear of the truth. “Oh, nothing…I don’t think he’ll be ringing again.” Said Pharaoh, before shovelling a forkful of food into his mouth. “Oh, I’m glad…that man was beginning to scare me!” Pharaoh shovelled in another mouthful of food. Isiy was a good cook and he had always loved her meals. From the looks of things Tom was enjoying the pasta she’d prepared too. “So when are we setting off for Mobile, Alabama, sir?” asked Tom, wiping his mouth on a small white tissue, which he’d produced from the mysterious recesses of his greasy grey suit. “Very soon, Tom. It’ll be an interesting night, for Corey Williams is a class wrestler within the ranks of PCW, even if nobody likes him. He has held the world--…” “But, he never won it fairly. The only title Corey Williams has ever won is the Rising Star! And come on, Mr Xansiv, let’s face facts here, Williams of no standards in comparison to you.” Pharaoh grinned. “You may have a point, Tom. But let us see how the Gods determine my fate, shall we?” Pharaoh put his now empty plate down upon the glass table and looked over at Isiy. “My Love, I must depart once again…” Isiy looked upset. “Again? But you only just got here--…” “I’m afraid I must, dearest…” Pharaoh motioned for Tom to leave the room. He put down his plate on the floor next to the couch and slowly left. Pharaoh and Isiy embraced one another and kissed for a time that if it had been deducted from the life of a may--…wait…you already know THAT bit… “I will be back in due time, my dearest…I promise you…” Isiy tried for a smile. It seemed to her that Pharaoh made a lot of promises…thankfully he always lived up to them. “Ok…” She murmured, sadness clearly evident in her sweet voice. Pharaoh lost his grip on her hand and left the room. His things for wrestling were still in the car. He had known this would only be a short trip, but he had never known that the events which had taken place here today would have ever ended as they had. He got in the car, followed by Tom as Isiy stood on the doorstep waving them goodbye. They set off back on the road, this time they were headed for Mobile, Alabama, where, in the Civic Centre Pharaoh would step into the ring to wrestle Corey Williams. “We aren’t leaving town until late tomorrow, Tom…” said Pharaoh, breaking the silence that had enveloped the car “Sir, but why? What do you have to do in California? We--…” “Don’t question me…I have business to sort out…” “Oh I see…but why don’t we stay at your house? Wouldn’t that be a lot easier than booking into a hotel?” Pharaoh concentrated on the road ahead. “I don’t want Isiy to know about this…arrangement.” Tom fell silent. He simply nodded his head as Pharaoh suddenly diverted the car to cut a corner, which led into a posh-looking street. “And now, I have some unfinished business…” Pharaoh felt peaceful now…he lay upon the red leather bench looking up to the ceiling. The Doctor sat, legs cross upon a tall black chair, a notebook in his left hand, a ballpoint pen in his right… “Tell me about your childhood…” Pharaoh thought and nothing arose… “I-I…” He gulped. “I can’t…” The Doctor looked up over the top of his glasses. “That bad?” Pharaoh sighed. “No…I just can’t…I can’t remember it…” The Doctor calmly put down his book and looked down at the distressed Pharaoh. “Mr Xansiv…these visions…do you not think that they could be…your childhood? You say that you can’t remember much of your life; perhaps these visions are…your life?” Pharaoh immediately sat up, a look of anger about his handsome visage. “No! I refuse to believe that!” The doctor sighed. “It is almost impossible that you can be six thousand years old, Mr Xansiv…can I call you Titu?” ‘The Jewel’ nodded. “Perhaps you want to believe the impossible? Believe what is not true? You want to block out reality?” Pharaoh winced. “No…no…” he moaned. The Doctor stared at his patient… “Titu…go home and think about what is going on in your life…think about what is true to you…think about your past…your real past…” Pharaoh stood up…a look of sheer anger painted upon it. “I know my real history, ‘Doc’!” The Psychiatrist seemed not to be fazed by Pharaoh’s outburst. “Perhaps you should think about THAT past and then think about your REAL past…” The Pharaoh got up off the bench, muttering. “I won’t have this! First StCross and now YOU?” The Doctor, for the first time in the session, looked puzzled. But he reframed from asking any questions… “In the name of Horus, I’m damned well leaving!” Pharaoh walked towards the door but stopped when he heard the Psychiatrist speak. “Pay at the desk, please, Mr Xansiv…” Pharaoh left the room with a slam of the door, which echoed throughout the establishment. “How did it go, sir?” asked Tom as Pharaoh got back in the car. “Not so good, I’m afraid…” Tom sagged. “Never mind, Mr Xansiv!” Pharaoh didn’t say a word; instead he started up the car and drove off down the lengthy highway as the sun began to settle over the horizon. They found a place to rest and quickly fell into deep sleeps. Even the monstrous thing that had previously been sat at the back of the car rested well… Tom and the beast slept soundly, Pharaoh didn’t… Pharaoh dreamt a twisted dream. The day had been tiring on our Egyptian hero and all he needed right now was a damned vision. The Doctor had said that perhaps they were due to the fact that he was remembering his past. But Pharaoh didn’t believe him, after-all none of the dreams had truly proven to show anything of The Pharaoh, like his face and such…and obviously, if you thought about it, if it was Titu’s past, then one would presume he’d be involved in it. So far he hadn’t even seen anyone who looked like him…so what were they? He saw a tall young man in his early twenties briskly walking down towards a pit…this place was defiantly Egypt…Pharaoh could practically smell the sand in the air. He’d become extremely accustomed to the smell and knew it as soon as it hit his nose… but this was a dream for the love of Seth! How could he possibly smell anything? The man walked ever more towards the pit. Slowly but surly he grew upon it and leisurely the pit was revealed to hold the ruins of a huge building…Egyptian markings carved all over its outside…its pillars made from the greatest slabs of multicoloured marble Pharaoh had ever seen. Pharaoh stirred, for in his dream like state Titu saw the boy slowly begin to fall into the crater…he was there and then he wasn’t; sucked into the sand around the old palace like a worm down the throat of a baby crow. The boy screamed…his hand reached out of the sand and then he was gone. And like the final grain of sand in an hourglass his hand disappeared… Pharaoh awoke, very suddenly, “The Forth!” he said…
