Aftermath

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/05/03 ‘Well that could have gone better’ thought James to himself as he stared blankly at the wall in front of him running over the events from turmoil in his mind over and over again. Whilst not depressed about the loss James felt a little disappointed with his own performance you could tell by the expression on his face unchanging his green eyes burning a hole through the wall, things could have been different if he did this instead of that, if he had paid attention after hitting Krikorian with the supernova then Jackson wouldn’t have been able to hit the bourne ultimatum. James slowly runs his fingers over his face, which had, began to bruise shortly after being planted face first into the mat. It was one of the problems he had he bruised like a peach. “I suppose I did all I could, I never really thought I could win. Compared to Krikorian, Burns and myself Jackson is a seasoned veteran and it showed. He just waited until it was time to strike his ring presence was all there, he knew that the Supernova takes a lot out of me and that I wouldn’t be able to defend myself that’s what you get with experience I suppose” James’ words not baring any malice or anger but a feeling of wonder “The feeling though…well before my face got smashed in, was like nothing I’ve ever experienced before, I want more…I want to feel that way again” James’ face changing for the first time a broad smile appears on his face showing off a set of pure white teeth, which stand out even more with the bruising around his jaw and cheeks. “I want more” He had somehow got into the moment of the bout, the adrenaline was coursing through his veins something which he had never felt before, even when he bared witness to his sister completing the dream, the emotion was never like that before it was like a drug and James’ expression showed just how badly he wanted this now, the smile the hunger in the eyes. Despite being beaten James had already been grabbed by the bug, the crowd, the noise, the cheering after an impressive move, he now understood how so many people fell in love with this sport the passion that flows through every roster members body is immense. James raised slowly to his feet and walked across the small dressing room that he had been assigned a few empty bottles of water cluttered the floor and his sports bag was thrown haphazardly next to a small chair with barely enough room for him, let alone a bigger superstar. James turned on the tap that was situated below a small mirror with barely enough glass to show all of James’ feature and began to cup the free flowing clear liquid into his hands before splashing it lightly on his face, some of the water hitting his bare chest and cascading down it. A few small beads dripped off his clean-shaven chin before he looked up at his reflection in the mirror. Looking at the reflection in the mirror James no longer saw the man he knew, James Edwards was dead, James ‘lunar’ Edwards had been born and taken the place of the man whose one dream was his main focus in his existence, this was a man of more experience a new thirst and thrive for life, things were changing for Edwards and his life was about to get a whole lot more exciting. Turning slowly James exited the room and headed into the corridor complete with pastel shades, the door slamming back against the frame as he passed through it. His boots making a clunking noise which echoed around the now deserted arena. It had just passed midnight but Edwards had not yet left after the events of turmoil, the adrenaline was still pumping around his body, his senses heightened to the extent that he felt as though he could hear the air. James continued his trek through the arena, taking in everything. The sights, the smells he could almost taste the residual energy in the building and continued to until he reached the top of the entrance ramp where he had been just a few hours previously. James closed his eyes and recalled the memories, which were still fresh in his mind. When he heard his music hit and the lights hit him…the noise of the crowd. It was by no means the largest cheer of the night, he hadn’t expected that. If the truth were told he hadn’t expected a cheer at all. James walked down the entrance ramp his boots rapping lightly on the metal, glancing around the stadium he couldn’t believe how quite it was, how life was just taken away from the building, which had almost seemed alive when he was in the ring. By now James had reached the bottom of the ramp and approached the empty space where the ring had previously been, the ring crew must have cleared it away straight after the crowd had dispersed. Standing in the gap all James could think of was turmoil next week when he would have the opportunity to fight again but this time to win. Sivasian Krikorian, Shane Wethers and Society three men who stood between James and my first victory. He already knew what Krikorian was capable of and he had a brief knowledge on Wethers however once again their was a potent threat in the bout. A man who had not only impressed James but to some extent scared him. A man who had demolished two men. The man who was known as Society. There was one problem though. James was not ready to conform just yet.
