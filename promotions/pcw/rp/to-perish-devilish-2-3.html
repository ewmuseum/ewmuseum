Date: No date...JUST READ! He keeps going and going and going… No, not Duke William’s sex drive…though that’s a lie straight from the Void. The reference is referred to his ignorant statements. It’s like every time something’s said towards him, he comes back just as stupid. It’s like he has a vocal reflex of stupidity. But that’s just fine and dandy…Akiyama’s got himself a reflex as well. Duke talks…the nVg destroys… Fair exchange…wouldn’t you agree?? =©= The scene opened upon the foyer of a well furnished home. There was a pale yellow light bulb in the glass ceiling fixture above, shining down on all of the framed pieces of art. Suddenly, the doorbell rang. A loud barking came from deep within the house. Footsteps began to follow. =©= Male Voice: All right, Snowy! Calm down! It’s just the door. =©= A man wearing a brown sweater vest, tan khakis, and a white shirt came walking to the door. He ruffled his brown hair with his hands before opening the door…and he literally screamed like a girly man. =©= Man: What took you so long to get the champagne, Cindy? I was getting worri---OH MY GOD!!! =©= And it was God of course…the Neo Void God…. Towering against the frame of the door stood Akiyama. He glared down at the man with cold eyes…neither wavering nor moving. The man shivered slowly in fear at the presence. Then a sound came up from behind him. =©= =©=Heaving. =©= =©=The man slowly managed to peer past the god to find the Divine Prophetess hurling off to the side on the rose bushes. =©= Cyclonica: Ugh…Duke…and I???? How DARE he even utter those words that he said about me??? I wouldn’t touch that filthy mortal with a hot cattle prod!! The mere thought of his calloused hands touching my perfect skin makes my stomach bubble…OH GEEZ!!! =©=The man winced as she began throwing up again. =©= Man: Hey!! That’s a prize bush!! =©=Cyclonica snapped her dark eyes towards him angrily. She wiped her mouth as Akiyama growled at the man lowly. =©= Cyclonica: What…the FREAK…did you just say? =©=The man began stuttering as he backed into his home. He suddenly swung the door closed, flipping the locks over it and pressed his back against it while whimpering. =©= Man: Please!!! Go away!!! Leave me alone!! Cyclonica: You had better had been referring to this rose bush I just threw up on…because if you weren’t, you’re going to be in so much pain… Man: What did I ever do to you!?! Cyclonica: We have business with you human!! Open this door and permit us to enter!! Man: NO!!! I CANT!!! Cyclonica: Why? Are you as feeble as Duke Williams?? Open this instant!! Man: NOOO!!!! NOT AGAIN!!! PLEASE NOT AGAIN!!! =©= Cyclonica looked up to the god with a blank face. =©= Cyclonica: Akiyama…give this man a…HAND. =©=The man leaned against the door breathing heavily, his heart racing and his adrenaline pumping. Then he paused for a second…listening slowly to what could be happening outside. Suddenly…a huge black taped fist came crashing through the oak wood door. The man stumbled down on the ground and watched in terror as the huge arm reached down and unlocked the door. Then a book kicked the entire door off the hinges. Akiyama walked into the house with Cyclonica following close behind. =©= Cyclonica: Hello, Seibzhen. It’s been a long time since you’ve been employed by the Neo Void God. =©= The man she called Seibzhen cowered under the gaze of the god. =©= Seibzhen: PLEASE!!! I don’t want to go back!! Cyclonica: Shut up! Don’t act like being a servant for the Celestial ONE is a bad thing! At least you’d be higher up on the evolution scale than Neanderthal Duke…but technically at this point in his life mountain gorillas are a few notches above him…no real offer on the table there. =©= Just then, a white dog came running up to them on a barking spree. Akiyama gave the mutt one look and it stopped right in its tracks. Cyclonica booted the dog in it’s muzzle as she looked back to Akiyama.=©= Cyclonica: Duke has truly crossed the line with his heinous and lewd remarks towards me. He even had the audacity to say that you and I have been intimate! That is nothing more than preposterous!! His eyes gawking over my perfect frame is more than nauseating! It’s revolting and sickening!! And who knows what kind of venereal diseases the man has running through his mortal body! Duke will never get his grubby sweaty paws on this! No FRICKIN way! If I ever have to look upon his beard littered with potato chip crumbs and his cowboy/beer hat, then I’m going to vomit all over again! =©= At that point, she looked up on the wall. Her face twitched a bit as she looked away…and vomited all over the dog. =©= Seibzhen: SNOWY!! =©= =©=Opening back upon the home, we find the man called Seibzhen slouching in a sofa as Akiyama and Cyclonica walk around the living room looking around. The entire room is decorated nicely, with a few pieces of artwork hanging on the walls. Down at the feet of Seibzhen was Snowy…his snow white fur tinted a bit tan. =©= Seibzhen: Poor Snowy… Cyclonica: Poor Snowy? Screw that mutt! If you’re having concern for anyone it should be me! Duke Williams wants to sex me up! He wants to make me one of the whores on his list! And you’re worrying about some puissant mongrel…I see why you humans are flawed…your priorities are all screwed up! Seibzhen: What do you want from me anyway? Cyclonica: First thing’s first…why do you have a pictures of Duke Williams on your wall? Seibzhen: Who’s Duke Williams? =©=She pointed up at a picture frame on the wall sharply. =©= Cyclonica: This guy…that’s Duke Williams! Seibzhen: No! That’s Captain Haddock from The Adventures of TinTin!! =©= Cyclonica reached into her pocket and handed a picture to Seibzhen. =©= Cyclonica: Tell me this picture doesn’t look like the ones on your wall… =©= Seibzhen took the pictures and examined them: Seibzhen: Vulgarians!!…a dead ringer! Billions of blazing bilious blue blistering barnacles!!! Cyclonica: Your speech…stop that! =©=Yeah…Seibzhen was a Tin Tin mark…he had a thing for the comic strip worse than lonely e-fed fanboys have a thing for the Aimz. =©= Seibzhen: I’ve got to meet this man!! Cyclonica: No…you don’t. This man is vulgar, blasphemous, and disgusting, not to mention a womanizer, drug user, alcoholic, and a huge stereotype for rednecks and mortals. Seibzhen: (Whimpering) That doesn’t sound like Captain Haddock… =©=Akiyama glared over at Seibzhen, which shut up his sniveling instantly. Cyclonica took her pictures back quickly. =©= Cyclonica: I need these photos for our plan tonight…which involves you. Seibzhen: No…I’ve retired from the whole Void business. After Akiyama turned me into Rent A Villain…I settled down and made myself a life worth living. I have a dog, a fiancée, a nice house, and a great Tin Tin reading group. I have it all! =©=Cyclonica stared at Seibzhen for a second blankly. =©= Cyclonica: Yeah…Sure…Whatever… =©=Akiyama palmed the skull of Seibzhen and dragged him screaming towards the door. Cyclonica stepped to follow, but noticed the dog in front of her. She picked him up by his collar and looked at it closely. =©= Cyclonica: Yes…you can help too. Once this plan is finished, it’ll be the end of the Malice Man and all of his radical ways!! SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS. =©= She sniffed at the dog closely. The dog quickly licked at her nose, causing her to retract her head, with nose wrinkled. =©= Cyclonica: But first…a bath for you, beast. =©=
