Shred Everything!!

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/26/02 The picture forms as the black Hummer limo comes.....humming.....into the parking lot of Greed, INC in Dallas, Texas. It motors down the drive and whips around in front of the building, coming to a screeching halt at the entrance. Almost before it stops moving the front driver’s side door is flung open and the CEO of PCW and Greed, INC comes darting out of the seat. He scampers around the front of the vehicle and in through the revolving doors to the lobby of the grand structure. The scene fades out. Opening back up inside of the headquarters we are welcomed by the sound of paper shredders working double time. We are inside of an office area, full of cubicles divided by four foot partitions. Men in white button-up shirts and ties scurry about along with women in business attire, gathering up documents and ferrying them to the shredders on the far side of the room. Manning the shredders are several other men, these gentlemen are wearing suits, however. Off to the side of them stand another pair of men, one of them is easily recognizable. “I want everything shredded!!” shouts the CEO of Greed, INC Tyler Nelson. “Don’t leave anything behind for those stiffs in the Department of Justice to get their grubby little hands on.” “Yessir!” says one of the shredders, a young looking executive type man. “Did anyone ask you to speak, junior?” questions the CEO. “N-No sir,” the young man stammers. “Then shut the fuck up,” Tyler says matter-of-factly. The young executive simply nods his head up and down while continuing to shove the reams of documents down into the shredder. Standing next to Tyler is one of the upper level executives at Greed, INC, Rick Keene. He is a rather shady looking fellow with a pitted face and slicked back black hair. His suit is top of the line, but the powder blue color negates the quality. “They totally caught us off guard, Mr. Nelson,” explains Keene in a somewhat nasally and definitely annoying voice. “No one ever saw this coming.” “It’s all that burrito boy’s fault,” says Tyler angrily. “This is all because of that stupid lawsuit he filed against me for wrongful termination. And what the hell is up with that? Shouldn’t I be able to fire anyone I want anytime I want for any reason I want?” Keene emphatically nods his head in agreement. “I am the CEO after all. Who can challenge my power?” Tyler looks over at Keene for some sort of response. He gets nothing. “Helloooo-o?” “Oh,” pipes Keene. “No one can challenge you Mr. Nelson! Who does that guy think he is?” “I dunno, but after Fusion he’s gonna be about as healthy as Mexican water. I’m gonna hurt that cocksucker real good. No one messes with the greediest player in the game!” says Tyler indignantly. The CEO looks out over the office, scanning the workers. “C’mon! Let’s get busy out there!” The workers are being run ragged by the CEO and Keene, transporting stack after stack of documents from the cubicles to the shredders. Bags of shredded paper are being taken out of the office almost constantly, on their way to the incinerator deep within the bowels underneath Greed, INC headquarters. A slight smile crosses the lips of the CEO. “Pretty soon all of the evidence will be gone and those pricks won’t have a leg to stand on,” snarls Tyler. There is a slight pause before Keene cautiously speaks. “Ummm......I did tell you that they downloaded some stuff from the mainframe, right?” “Yeah.....so?” Tyler questions without much concern. “Welll......,” Keene starts hesitantly. “They got some data files for the stuff that we are shredding.” “THEY WHAT?!” Tyler exclaims. Keene immediately starts to try and calm the CEO. “Now...I don’t think that they got anything concrete to work with....” “You don’t THINK?! What in the HELL does that mean?” demands Tyler. “What are you trying to tell me, Keene?” Keene stands there for a moment, wondering why he even opened his mouth. He swallows hard before he speaks. “What I’m trying to say is that I don’t know what they downloaded. No one was allowed in the room when they were in there. It could be anything.” Tyler grimaces as if he was kicked in the nuts with a steel-toed boot. “You have GOT to be KIDDING me! This is not the kind of information I wanted to hear right now!” The CEO starts to pace around the room while Keene does his best to blend into the surroundings. He has no luck, however, because of that God awful powder blue suit. After several laps around the office, complete with hair pulling and kicking of cubicles, Tyler walks back over to where Keene is cowering by the shredders. “I want you to finish up shredding all of this shit,” says Tyler rather defeatedly. “Then I want you to have all of these people sign a non-disclosure form. Then I want you to fire them.” “Sure thing Mr. Nelson,” replies Keene. Tyler glances blankly out across the room again, then turns back to Keene. “Start deleting all of the hard drives on every computer and wipe out the network memory. Back everything up to disks and bring them up to me. No copies.” “Yessir.” “I want all of this done right away. I don’t care how long you have to stay but you do not leave until it’s finished,” orders Nelson. “Do you understand that?” A simple nod from Keene serves as the expected response to Tyler’s question. “I’m going upstairs to take care of some things. Do not disturb me until you have everything finished that I have asked. If you do, you will vanish off the face of the planet never to be heard from again.” Tyler gives a cold glare to Keene that pierces through him. A shudder runs up and down Keene’s spine as Tyler pivots on his heel and heads out of the office. Keene watches the CEO exit the office and then wipes his brow of the forming beads of sweat. We fade out. We open up once again in the office of the CEO of Greed, INC. The splendid room is one decorated with no expense spared. The black marble floor shines without a single blemish to mar it. The white walls are covered with various high dollar paintings, as well as poster size photographs of Tyler holding the PCW Heavyweight Championship, Tyler with some of his Media compatriots, and Tyler with various dignitaries from around the world. The CEO walks around the spacious office, perusing the photos on the wall. He stops in front of the poster of him with the PCW Championship belt. “I was robbed,” Tyler says disgustedly. “That is the greatest moment in my wrestling career, more so than any CANCELLATION or any other victory that I ever had. That was what I wanted when I decided to enter the sport. I won it fair and square, just like I’ve done everything in my life, and it was taken from me by that crooked son of a bitch Porter. Now I have these ruthless bastards from the Department of Justice trying to take the one thing that is truly mine. The one thing that I alone am responsible for building.....Greed, INC. And it’s all because of that ungrateful asshole, Mexi.” A look of utter contempt comes over the already scowling face of the CEO of Greed, INC. He turns away from the poster and makes his way over to his huge oak desk. He moves around behind it and pulls the tall, black leather high back chair out from underneath it. He sits down in the comfortable chair and rolls himself up to his desk. He turns the desktop computer on, then pulls open the top desk drawer on the right hand side. While the computer beeps and hums as it boots up, Tyler reaches into the drawer and pulls out a shiny .38 snubnose revolver. The lights from the office gleam off the untarnished gun. Tyler lifts the gun to eye level and stares at it longingly. “They won’t take my Greed away from me,” says Tyler as a strange smile comes across the lips of the CEO of Greed, INC and PCW. Then suddenly Tyler bursts into a fit of maniacal laughter. He throws his head back and laughs, the gun held tightly in his right hand. The scene fades to black.
