Date: 12/22/04 The wooden bench would make his back ache night after night. Every morning he would feel like during his sleep he was bent in every which way. Well, the sleep was over and it was time to wake up, that was one of the better parts of being homeless, no responsibilities, he had all the time in the world. Maybe not ALL the time in the world, after this short trip he had to get on a plane as quick as he can and get to China for Shanghaied. Shaun Styles stretched as he squirmed around in his old heavy black jacket with a fake fur interior. His face seemed to get more rugged as some traces of facial hair began to appear. He lets out a breath of icy cold air as he sits up watching the busy people pass to go to work. Shaun Styles: Fools! I only work one day a week and I'm doing just fine! He let out a hacking cough and rubbed his hands together to warm himself from the New York winter. The snows turned to ice since its below freezing point making Shaun Styles shiver with every step he took. It was February 20th, and that day only meant one thing for Styles...Payday. He made his way down the street and took a left, finally standing in front of a bad excuse for a hotel. Shaun Styles enters and ends up in front of the...front desk. Unfortunatly there is no one behind it. After looking around for a little bit he finds one of those little bell things. Ding! Ding! DI...ng! DING!!!! DING!!!! DIIIII---MUTHAFUCKING---NNNGGG!!!! A kid no older than 20 comes out of a 'Staff Only' door and walks up behind the front desk. He's wearing a red staff jacket and some tight Diesel jeans as he stares at Styles blankly. Shaun Styles: Took you long eno--- Before he could finish his statement the hotel empoyee takes the bell and puts it away before walking back into the staff room. Shaun Styles: ...That little bitch. Styles jumps over the front desk in a very James Bondish manner and kicks the staff door open. That same 20 year old kid is seen making out with a depressed, fat girl as they are interrupted by the red, green, and blue haired PCW superstar. Shaun comes up to him, grabs him by the collar and pushes him against the tiled wall. Shaun Styles: I'd like to know if any mail came for Shaun Styles lately...Please. Hotel employee: Ugh...Sure, I'll check right away. He quickly looks at the girl before walking out of the room followed by Shaun Styles. Without hesitating he opens up a drawer from the desk and pulls out a letter. He hands it to Styles who's eyes light up and he gets a big smile on his face. He opens the envelope and reads just the beginning... 'Pay to the order of Shaun Styles...'
