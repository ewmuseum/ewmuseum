Oh my greatness! (part 2) When will it end? *sigh*

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/02 What a joke. What a goddamn joke. How can this happen? How can a man of pride and dignity allow himself to be screwed over by an ingrate like Duke Williams? How can a man of zero accomplishments in 800 years of life screw over a man the stature of Kid Wonder? After everything Kid Wonder has done for Duke. He gave Duke an identity. When nobody gave a damn about Duke or his phony run at President, Kid Wonder stood by his side and made people take notice. And this is how Duke pays K-Dub back. What an ass. Duke Williams, in a fit of rage and jealousy, rigged the battle royal drawing. He rigged the drawing in an attempt to tarnish Kid Wonder's name. In an attempt to tarnish Kid Wonder's greatness. In an attempt to tarnish Kid Wonder's legacy. And for what? To help out a man who is only a legend in his own mind. Despite popular belief, Duke made it so that a newbie jobber by the name of Tom Lexian would enter first. Then he made it so Kid Wonder, a real legend, would have to enter next. This man, Tom Lexian, is a legend in his own mind. He only believes that he's a legend. Kid Wonder, on the other hand, is a real legend. He's a living legend. He made the word legend popular. So how does Duke get his newbie jobber named Tom Lexian to become a legend in his own right? He puts him in the biggest match of his life against the actual legend. But Duke and Thomas won't win that easily. For Kid Wonder, unlike this Lexian character, is a real legend. He's proven his legacy time and time again. And eliminating the Lexian boy will just prove it again. But enough prelude. Let's go out to Elmwood Park in Omaha, Nebraska. In an attempt to further tarnish Kid Wonder's great name, the people of Elmwood wouldn't let him hold his 'Greatness Convention' indoors. Is Doug Troy still upset at being 0-for-life against Kid Wonder to make him stand out in the cold? What a dicksmoke. No worries though. Doug Twat will be handled by Kid Wonder's personal birth, Meanstreak, at OSW's minor league show soon enough. Now out to peaceful Elmwood Park in Omaha. It's a clear, yet cold Wednesday morning in Omaha. We're just hours removed from that emotional Turmoil in which Duke Williams screwed over Kid Wonder. But we can't talk about that yet. Wonder has business to take care of. This is Day 2 of the 'Greatness Convention'. It has now moved from Portland to Omaha. It's gone from indoors to outdoors. But the same cast of characters as before sans Dwayne Johnson. They're just sitting on a park bench instead of in chairs. At one end of the bench sits the oh so lovely Amanda. She's wearing a winter hat and a fur coat to stay warm. After all, nothing that beautiful deserves to be cold. Amanda's cheeks are a bit of a rose red color from the slight wind. But besides that, she looks like her usual beautiful self. Next to Amanda, sadly, is R&B singer, and child lover extraordinaire, R. Kelly. He too wears a fur coat, but only because he thinks it's stylish for a fruit like himself to wear stuff like that. Next to Kelly is the greatest hockey player of all-time, or so people say. He also just led Team Canada to a gold medal at the Salt Lake City olympics without Russian protest. The man holds so many hockey records it's sickening. The man, clad in black Armani suit, is Wayne Gretzky. He seems immune to the cold weather. Whether it be because he's Canadian or he's a hockey player, we'll never know. Finally, next to Gretzky, shaking visibally due to his poor health is the greatest boxer of all time, Mr. Muhammad Ali. The 2nd greatest Muslim to ever grace anything PCW related sits at the other end of the park bench in grey sweats and a black top. He's still shaking, but can't control it. He's still a sad sight to see remembering what he once was. We shift from the people who were invited to the 'Greatness Convention' to the person behind this great event. That man, of course, being one of only two men who can claim to be a PCW legend, (the other being Tyler Nelson), the one, the only, the great Kid Wonder. K-Dub is wearing a Roberto Luongo hockey sweater and his trademark red, baggy, Vanilla Ice genie-esque pants. In the background from where KW is standing, we can see what appears to be the home of Kurt Ragnarok. We also see a middle-aged man walk up to said K-Rock home, toss a can into it, and walk away. Wonder, not worrying about what's happening behind him, just stands and looks at the four people on the bench. 'Goddess and gentlemen,' Wonder says greeting the people who have come out, 'I apologize for the inconvenience. Never in my life did I feel a man of my greatness would be subject to such inferior conditions.' Amanda, at the end of the bench, nods her head. None of the others follow suit. 'I am not just a PCW legend,' continues Kid Wonder, 'but I'm a legend in pop culture. I don't just make things popular. I make things extraordinarily popular. I'm a trend setter.' Kid Wonder looks around. He appears to be looking for something. He looks down at the ground and smirks. 'Take ice for example,' says K-Dub as he raises his head back up. Wonder looks directly at Wayne Gretzky. 'Wayne?' Gretzky looks at Wonder with a somewhat confused look on his face. 'Yes?' 'Who made ice popular,' asks the teen idol to the hockey great. Wayne looks dumbfounded. 'Canadians,' he answers in that 'I dunno' kind of voice. Kid Wonder sighs. 'No,' he tells Gretzky with disgust. 'It was me! I made ice popular!' Wayne Gretzky, sitting on the bench, is really confused now. 'What?' 'What? WHAT!?!' Kid Wonder is really disgusted with Gretzky right now. He can't believe one man can be so ignorant, even if he is Canadian. 'What do you mean 'What?'?' Kid Wonder starts to get into the face of Wayne Gretzky. 'When I was five, my parents took me to the Rockies. And while up there, I slipped on some ice and fell,' Wonder screams at Wayne. The story of falling gets a laugh or two from R. Kelly. Wonder glares over at Kelly. 'Do you find something funny?' 'You fell,' Kelly says with a snicker. Wonder moves over to where R. Kelly is sitting. He now stares at Kelly and yells, 'Yeah!' Wonder stares a hole into the singer. 'So what? I fell! I was a little kid! I didn't have ice skates!' Wonder cools off a little. He takes a deep breath. 'But I got back up,' KW tells the singer as he slowly backs up. 'I always get back up. Whenever someone knocks me down, I rise again and become even stronger than before.' The people on the bench start to pay attention now. For now, it seems like Wonder might actually be trying to say something. As for Wonder, he stops backing away and stands in front of the four on the bench. 'Last night,' Wonder continues on, 'a jealous ingrate by the name of Duke Williams knocked me down. He and his new friend, a guy by the name of Tom Lexian, screwed me over. For, you see, this Lexian guy considers himself a legend. But the fact is, Tom is only a legend in his own mind. I, on the other hand, am the real deal. I am a legend. I am Kid Wonder.' Amanda, sitting at the end of the bench, claps. She does it in that heelish valet manner. Wonder grins. 'Thank you,' he tells his girlfriend. 'But what I speak is the truth. I mean, what has Tom Lexian done,' asks Wonder. 'Been a star in the DWF? Been a star in FSW?' Wonder pauses for a moment. 'Big deal,' he says with conviction. 'How many times have guys like him died on my turf? How many times have guys from this inferior places come to my home and tried taking my spot? Too many to count! But do they ever succeed?' 'No,' shouts out Amanda. 'That's right,' Wonder says. 'They always fail. And it's because while they believe they're great...I am great. And Tom Lexian will be no different,' Wonder says with a smirk. 'If there were a such thing as Legend Inc., Tyler Nelson would own it and I'd be the franchise of it. And Tom Lexian,' Wonder pauses for a moment. 'Well, Tom Lexian wouldn't even be allowed within a 100 mile radius of it.' 'Yeah,' shouts out Wonder's supportive girlfriend. Wonder just grins. 'So what does he and Duke do,' asks K-Dub. 'What do they do? They try screwing me over. They try knocking me down. But you know what? I won't let it happen! I'll fight back like I've never fought back before! I'll take their best shots, stand tall, and then kill them! That's what I'll do!' 'Yeah,' shouts out Amanda. 'You'll enter 2nd, eliminate every single self-centered wannabe in that damn match, and become the champ!' 'No,' Wonder tells Amanda. 'I'm gonna do like every should-be champion out there and protest. That's what I'm gonna do.' Kid Wonder gets an evil grin on his face. 'And when I'm done with those that try screwing me, I can finally complete my legacy. My real legacy. I will be the PCW Heavyweight champion.' The guys on the bench all seem uninterested in what Wonder just had to say. Amanda, on the other hand, paid attention to every word and applauds Wonder's great speech. Wonder stands in front of everyone, standing as tall as his 5'9 inch body allows him. 'Now,' Wonder tells them, 'if you'd excuse me, a man of my stature can no longer 'mingle' with any of you right now. I've got business to take care of. This meeting is adjourned until I find the need or want to grace any of you with my presence again.' Wonder signals to Amanda. 'Let's go, babe,' he tells the lovely lady. Amanda gets up from where she was sitting. She latches onto K-Dub's arm. The King & Queen of PCW walk off. Meanwhile, the men are left there on the bench not knowing and/or caring as to what just happened. They just sit there as we get out of there.
