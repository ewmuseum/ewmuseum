I see undead people...

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/12/02 Chirp, chirp. Tick, tock. Inhale, exhale. Normally sounds you don't hear at all. Each of them rhythmic drones, following a set pattern. But the crickets outside, the grandfather clock inside, Gaz and Natalia's breathing were combining to form a sound all of their own. The sound of impatience. Nerezza had invited his friends... well, his friend and his friend's current closest thing to a steady girlfriend... to his home in Akihabara, Japan. It was an eclectic place to say the least, as though several cultures had clashed inside. One wall had what appeared to be a fireplace like in some great English mansion, complete with Nerezza's original scythe suspended by a pair of hooks on the ceiling. Opposite that was a skip West, where posters advertising famous vampire movies, namely Interview With The Vampire, Blade, The Lost Boys and Bram Stoker's Dracula were framed with their own lighting sources each. In between two of those was an immense clock with a pendulum, ticking away. A small stack of shelves displayed a set of ornaments that looked like dragons guarding crystals. A vase filled with violets sat at the top, providing a sweet smell to the room. To the right of that was a door that had a 'No Entry' road sign hung on it. To the left, Japan itself lay. Bamboo wall-scrolls with geisha designs and awesome dragons hung to the side of a window that offered a glimpse of 'The Electronic City', Akihabara. Sheets of glass and neon sprung into the sky and sparked across the city, showing where the inspiration for Blade Runner came from. Across the oily barricade of the slick black sky, the lighting was an artificial aurora, showing that man could replicate nature easily. Back inside, the final wall was Nerezza's childish side. It was a set of three shelves, all running parallel to each other and filled with statues, action figures and dolls of anime and videogame characters. Below those shelves was a television set on some kind of stand, while a red velvet cloth covered all below it. In the middle of the quaint room was a table, cut and shaped like a coffin. Five opened cans of Rezz Bull were places on top, all crushed once the user was done with them. Surrounding this table was a couch and three black leather armchairs. On the couch, Nerezza lay sprawled in a state of relaxation. The tranquilizers that The End had given him as a goodwill gesture were clearly working, as he lay with his eyes half open and a smile crept from one corner of his mouth. His companions, however, were less than amused. Gaz and Natalia sat in separate chairs, neither of them sporting a smile. The plane ride was absolute hell, delayed by an age and finding Nerezza's home in Japan was a challenge in itself, especially since neither the russian beauty nor The Jester could navigate in Japanese. On top of this, the trio were expected due back in Huntsville, Alabama on Tuesday for a match with an opponent that boasted even stranger qualities than Nerezza himself. 'So... how we diddling, Squire?' Gaz said, shattering the silence. Natalia herself jumped a little in her chair after being so used to the quiet. Nerezza rolled his head across to his partner, still showing his tired expression. 'Diddling like a diddle-dee-dee...' he replied, sounding as though he'd just woken up. 'What do you think of my humble little home? 'Little being the operative word... even I've bumped my head in places!' Natalia squeaked. 'It's Japan.You learn to duck after the first three or so concussions.' the vampire chuckled. 'So, Rezz, we need to talk strategy. How do we beat an undead guy? You should know...' Gaz pleaded, running a hand over the tips of his spiked hair. Nerezza put down the sixth and final can of Rezz Bull onto the wooden floor and grinned. 'Did I ever ask you for tips on how to beat living people? It's as simple as hitting someone until they lie down for three seconds. I don't care of he's a demon, an incubus, a kappa or Spring Heeled Jack. Smack my head hard enough and I go down. It's happened to me before and it'll happen again to him. Specifically on Tuesday.' the living nightmare methodically explained. 'Isn't there some kind of vampire magic you could use? Some undead power?' Natalia asked in a naive manner. Her life had been lived mainly around a goggle-wearing JapoAmerican and vampires and such weren't as commonplace as they were as in Gaz's life. Nerezza slowly placed his palms by his sides and lifted his mass from the sofa, almost tripping over himself as he stumbled away and out of the door. A series of knocks, rummaging and crashes followed, including the scream of 'BAKA NEKO!' and the wail of a cat. After a minute or so of the commotion, Nerezza walked back into the room, clutching a flat piece of wood underneath his arm and a glass in one hand. 'Let me see if I can get some of the undead razzamatazz going here... whoooooo's up for a Ouija board?' the vampire said in a whining grimace. He dropped the board onto the coffin shaped table and dragged the sofa closer. 'Ouija board? Rezz, I don't know...' Gaz stammered, going pale. 'I agree, this is taking things a bit too seriously.' Natalia joined in, also looking less than enthusiastic about the presence of the item. 'Relax, people... you're in the room with a vampire. If the supernatural forces of evil were to come by, I'd ram their heads into the ethereal walls.' said the vampire. 'Now let's channel someone!' The trio placed their hands on the upturned glass and closes their eyes. Gaz and Natalia both had lumps in their throats the size of bowling balls, but the tranquilizers in Nerezza's bloodstream kept him calm. Suddenly, the collective yanked their hands from the glass as though it had become electrocuted. The lights in the room flickered madly and a chilling wind crept through around the back of everyone's necks. Suddenly, smoke began to rise from beneath the table and engulfed the room. The three darted from their chairs as it rose into the air and covered the ceiling. Gaz crawled over to the window, coughing and spluttering to release the grey cloud. As he did so, the plume rushed out through the window and into the night sky. Slowly, Gaz, Nerezza and Natalia stood up from the cover of furniture and peered down at the board. All seemed normal... if what happened a second ago could be called normal... until... THUD!!! A corpse collapsed on top of the board, cracking the coffin-table's leg and causing the arrangement to collapse. The body was ice-blue with shades of rotting hair that was graying. Emerging from the kidney area on the left hand side of the face-down corpse was a gear stick from a car, bloodied and stained. Jammed around the head was a steering wheel that crushed the skull and had embedded itself deep inside the cranium. 'AAAAAAAAAAAAWWWWWWW, CRAP!' Nerezza screeched in frustration. 'What's wrong?' came the reply from Gaz and Natalia replied in synchronization. 'WE SUMMONED AYRTON SENNA!!!' The vampire screamed in anger. Natalia and Gaz stared at the body for a second, before Natalia calmly shoved the cadaver to one side with her foot, then picked up the glass, placed it on Nerezza's head and slammed the board down onto it as hard as she could, shattering the glass on impact. Nerezza keeled over as a combination of supreme impact and the tranquilizers put him out. 'Govniuk!' Natalia exclaimed. '...well...' Gaz muttered. 'At least we know how to take care of undead people...'
