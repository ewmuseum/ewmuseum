Headin' Down The Highway With Pookie

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.08.02 “I Got Five On It” by the Luniz blasts out the car stereo as it speeds down the busy road. The car speeding down the road is a big black BMW X5, about $42,000 worth of metal. Obviously the driver of the car is PCW superstar Duzza. The camera moves into the car. In the driving seat is Duzza, dressed in a pair of baggy white pants and a black wife beater, which fits tightly to his muscular physique. He has a black du-rag tied around his head and his hair set back in cornrows. On his left shoulder is a crucifix tattooed on his shoulder. He has a large platinum chain with a diamond studded “D” at the end of the chain. On one wrist he has a silver Rolex that he bought south of the border down in Mexico, it doesn’t actually work and it’s not actually made out of silver, but it looks good… from a distance. On the other wrist is a silver bracelet with his name engraved onto the name tag. He also has quite a few rings on his fingers. In each ear lobe he has two silver hoops. Sitting next to Duzza, in the passengers seat, is his faithful homie Pookie from Harlem. The man who used to be a bum, until buying a lottery ticket, became a multi-millionaire when he won a mega-roll over. When Duzza got out of the hood, he bought a mansion in a mainly white area, to get away from people like Pookie, so imagine his shock when Pookie moved him and half of Harlem into the mansion across the road. Duzza is speeding down a highway which is leading to the state of Texas. The actual reason why Duzza is speeding is currently unknown Pookie: “DUZZ! Slow down” Duzza: “Can’t slow down, got places to be” Pookie: “Wha!? Where do you have to be?” Duzza: “Nigga… where am I usually on Tuesdays?” Pookie: “Errr… waiting in line for welfare?” Duzza: “You’re so funny. No, I gotta get to Turmoil” Pookie: “That’s nice… but why the fuck am I coming?” Duzza: “Y’know, I don’t actually know” Pookie: “I don’t belive it. You got me driving with you all over the country again” Duzza: “I know, it’s actually quite shocking you still fall for my tricks” Pookie:” Actually quite shocking? Nigga, you sound more white every day” Duzza: “Shut the fuck up!” Pookie: “I guess that’s what becoming best buddies with a nazi will do to ya” Duzza: “What did you just say” Pookie: “You ‘eard. You and that cracker done become best buddies” Duzza: “Nigga, I ain’t best buddies with that cracker, man I hate that fool and he hates me” Pookie: “Yeah on camera, but off, we all know you two done become bestest pals” Duzza: “You need a slap for real dawg, I hate that white boy. If I wasn’t forced into teaming with him, I wouldn’t, but what can I do?” Pookie: “Your like a slave. You always do what the white boys tell you to do, just like a slave” Duzza: “Where you come up wit’ this shit dawg? I’m my own man, I just need to what I need to do” Pookie: “What ever” Duzza: “Man, you a lucky nigga, you won the lottery, I meanwhile have to provide for my family, I gots bills to pay man” Pookie: “Working with a nazi ain’t the right way bro” Duzza: “It’s the only way dawg, belive that” Pookie: “What ever, if I was in your shoes I wouldn’t be teaming with no damn nazi and I’d be more focused on yo match with Flaymin Aymin and Violent Jack, didn’t he already kick yo ass once already anyway?” Duzza: “Ya know nig, you really starting to piss me off. You think you sooo good, but you ain’t shit man. You just another nigga, just like me man, you ain’t nothing” Pookie: “Yeah well, at least I ain’t no sell out” Duzza: “Sell out!? How did I sell out!” Pookie: “When you turn your back on your old hood to get rich working with a nazi that’s selling out!” Duzza: “What! I’ve been rich for years dawg, I’ve been working with a nazi for two weeks, you best remember that, mother fucker, before I kick you out of the car” Pookie: “Aww, you ain’t gonna do shit, your all talk” Duzza: “Yeah… that’s what I want you to think” Pookie: “That’s it. Let me out of the car, I can’t stand being near your sell out ass no more” Duzza puts on the breaks and the car screeches to a halt. He waits for Pookie to get out. Pookie: “Err… just kidding. Love ya really Duzz” Duzza: “Man, you lucky I don’t kick your ass worse than Aymin and Jack gon’ get come Turmoil” Duzza puts the car back into gear and speeds of down the highway, heading for Lubbock, TX, with thoughts of hurting Flaymin Aymin, Violent Jack and Mark Makonie playing on his mind.
