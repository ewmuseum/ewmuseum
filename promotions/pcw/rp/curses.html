Curses!

Date: not entered (We see the inside of THE Multiple Man’s secret lair. Tmm lounges on the couch, idly watching the television.) “Shh! Real quiet now. Don’t startle him. No sudden movements…” (Creeping up ever so silently behind him are Raul and Steve, a net stretched taught between them.) Steve: “NOW!!!” (They leap! After a brief struggle, Tmm is thoroughly enmeshed.) Steve: (panting) “Ha! Got you now, my fine friend! Now you’re going to TRAIN for that Lightweight Contendership match… or else! Mwahahahahahaha!” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Steve: “What do you mean?” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!” Steve: “A decoy?” (Hurriedly, he checks the Tmm in the net. No pulse, no breathing, wooden.) Steve: “Well, I’ll be hornswoggled.” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]?” Steve: “Why not? It’s a perfectly cromulent word.” (Appreciative clapping from the other side of the room. A man stands there. He looks remarkably like THE Multiple Man, but not quite. Perhaps it’s the dark sunglasses, goatee and cape.) Tmm?: “A VERY good attempt indeed, but all for naught. You see, THE Multiple Man is not here.” Steve: “Oh really? Then who are you?” Tmm?: “I? I am…” (Whoosh! Dramatic swirl of the cape along with action guitar riff from offstage!) Tmm?: “THE Multiple Man’s long lost evil twin brother, Least Common Denominator Man!” Steve: “No you’re not.” Tmm?: “Yes I am!” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.].” Tmm?:” Curses! You’ve penetrated my secret identity! It is true, I am in reality NOT Least Common Denominator Man, but in actuality, I am really…” (Whoosh! Dramatic swirl of the cape along with action guitar riff from offstage!) Tmm: “THE Multiple Man, posing as his long lost evil twin brother!” Steve: “Good, now start training for your match with Juan! You’ve slacked off enough this week!” Tmm: “Don’t think you’ve won yet! I still have one last secret in store for you!” (He claps loudly. On cue, over a dozen goons pour in from various doors.) Tmm: “Like most villains, I am able to disguise my lack of ability and talent by attacking you with goons! Go get them, my evil minions! Crush them all!!!” (The goons mill around uncomfortably.) Goon1: “Umm… you do understand that we’re just temps, right? Tmm: “Yes! Slay them both, my faithful minions! Why do you delay? I did not see this treasonous pause when I unleashed you upon my filing cabinets! Your ruthlessness astounded even me!” Goon1: “Well, that’s what you hired us for.” Tmm: “Indeed! Now kill them!” Goon1: “You really can’t expect us to perform nefarious deeds for only seven bucks an hour.” Goon2: “I would!” Goon1: “I mean, you really can’t expect MOST of us to perform nefarious deeds for only seven bucks an hour. And anyway, our contract states that we get a break around now. So, we’ll see you in fifteen.” (The temps tramp off to the break room.) Tmm: ”Curses! Foiled again! But you’ve not heard the end of me! Yoinks!” (Tmm turns on his heel and runs from the room. Steve looks at Raul in disgust.) Steve: “I’ve never, ever seen anyone go through such effort to avoid an hour or two of exercise before.” (Raul grunts and they begin gathering up the net as the camera fades out.)
