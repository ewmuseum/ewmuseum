Dante Jackson Tries Once More, To Make Jordan X Shabazz 'Black' Again [Finale?]

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.11.02 When we last left Jordan X Shabazz he was tied up in lighted room and he was watching rap videos. Dante Jackson, the man who tied Jordan up, thought that this would bring the ‘ghetto’ out in Jordan. He wanted Jordan to return to his old ways. Has it worked? We’ll see…. The scene opens up in the lighted room. The sun has now risen and the sun shines brilliantly into the room. The room is very small, only the television and the chair are in there, there are no other signs of furniture. The floor is made of pure marble. Jordan X Shabazz had woken up hours earlier in the middle of ’Back That Thang Up’ By Juvenile. Since then, he’d been through countless rap videos, gun toting, Ebonics spewing, booty shaking, videos. He was tired, and wondering when the hell Dante Jackson would get back and take him out of this house. He had know idea where they were, but he knew it was somewhere in the Hollywood Hills, as contradictory as that sounds. Suddenly, the door opened. In walked a smiling Dante Jackson, he wore the same clothing that he wore the night before, his clothing was ruffled, and his shirt was on backwards, leading Jordan to believe that he had engaged in some serious sexual activity the night before. ‘U Understand’ by Juvenile is now playing on the screen. The videos just kept coming at Jordan X Shabazz. Dante neared him and studied Jordan’s face. He was looking for any telltale signs of attitude or disrespect, that the media has installed into the youth of Black America. “Jordan, how do you feel?” “Angry.” “That’s a good start, black people are supposed to be angry. Angry for virtually no reason too.” Dante replies with positive reinforcement. Dante Jackson unties Jordan from the wooden chair that he sits in. Shabazz stands up and stretches, it had been hours since he had been standing upright, and his back ached from the wooden chair. His head was pounding, not only from the non-stop music videos, but from hangover that he got drinking what he thought was Kool Aid, when in fact he was drinking Kool Aid topped with Jack Daniels. Jordan walked outside the door of the small and secluded room and walking into what was like a landfill of trash. Soda cans, beer bottles, popcorn, rice, all of those things littered the floor from the party the night before. A few men were passed out on the dance floor. Shabazz headed to the rest room, and he vomited. When he came out he was shocked to see Dante Jackson waiting with a huge grin on his face. “You heard about the concert tonight dog?” “Dog? What the hell are you calling me a dog for?” Jordan asks angrily. “Do I ever purposely insult you? Why do you feel the need to insult me?” Dante Jackson sighs heavily. “Damn it Jordan, something’s wrong with you. But there’s another possibility in making you black again…” Jordan sighs. “I’m black enough, I represent black people in a positive way, there’s no way I’m changing.” “Heh, we’ll see…” ****************************8 hours later. It turns out that Dante Jackson’s huge surprise, the thing that is supposed to bring Jordan back from the brink of whiteness, a rap concert. That’s right, watching the music videos didn’t do it, but to Dante Jackson, nothing brings the black out in people more than a rap concert. The concert is a joint one being held in Inglewood, California by The Cash Money Billionaires and Nappy Roots. Dante Jackson knows that nothing angers Jordan more nowadays then rappers. The place where the concert is taking place doesn’t seat more than 11,000 people, and more than 7,000 weren’t going to be there. Dante used his connections to get Jordan and him backstage, Dante and Jordan had been watching the show from backstage for about an hour now. The Nappy Roots just went out and warmed up the crowd with their raw mix of insane beats and crazy lyrics (Big diff eh?). ******************* Two hours later. The Cash Money Billionaires have performed all of their hits, except one, it’s called ‘Still Fly.’ As they are in the middle of performing this hit, Jordan X Shabazz just can’t control himself any further. Shabazz picked up a microphone from backstage, with Dante trying to hold him back, Jordan makes his way to the stage. Security allows him on the stage because it is the last song of the concert and they assume that Jordan could perhaps be a part of the performance. Jordan walks out onto the stage as the 7,000 at the Grand Olympic Auditorium cheer loudly for the Big Tymers, and now, Jordan X Shabazz. “… ain’t got no job, but I’m still fly.” the group of men sing. Jordan walks directly towards the loud speakers, and he unplugs them. The crowd looks on in silence. Some fans are angry, but since some are wrestling fans, they decide to allow Jordan to go ahead and speak. “Ahem. Sorry to interrupt the program here, but I can’t help myself anymore. I sat through one hour of complete disrespect by that despicable group of men known as the Nappy Roots.” The crowd doesn’t know how to react. Jordan is a member of the Malice Crew in the PCW, which is perhaps the top baby face stable. The wrestling fans remain quiet so Jordan can continue, but the non-wrestling fans, who do not know Jordan as a ‘good guy’ began to jeer the Civil Rights Activist. “These men have an album out there now called ‘Watermelon, Chicken, and Gritz.’” Jordan states as the crowd cheers, some of the fans in attendance have bought or somehow own or have heard the album and obviously they enjoy it. “Now, I’m a fan of watermelon’s, greasy chicken, and grits, but, I do not approve of them exploiting southern African Americans like that. They’re playing up the stereotype.” Some of the fans who know Jordan from his work as a Civil Rights Activist begin to cheer the man. Jordan nods and holds his hand up in the air as a sign of asking them to be quiet. “Now, after hearing that dreadful performance, I thought that the bleeding of my ears, and the raping of my mind, but it continues here with these so called ‘Cash Money Billionaires’ or the ‘Big Tymers.’ Now, first off there is no ‘Y’ in timers, and second off, why are you going to sing about how rich you are when most of your fans are dirt poor?” Jordan asks angrily as some fans in the crowd jeer Jordan, thinking that he was insulting them. The Big Tymers were now getting uncomfortable, Jordan X Shabazz was attempting to make a fool of them at their own show. And he was a guest of theirs to boot! This was the ultimate in disrespect. The Nappy Roots had made their way from the locker room and now were on the stage. The entire concert had been stopped by the angry Civil Rights Activist. Sweat drenched Jordan Shabazz’s Duke Williams ‘Malice Style’ t-shirt, and he didn’t care. He was angry damn it, black people were being made fools of by these nasty and disgusting men that claim to be musicians. “And another thing! White people will always bitch and moan about how their tax dollars pays for our freakin’ welfare, but it doesn’t help when you are bragging about having no job, yet driving around in a fucking jeep with 23 inch rims, a DVD player in there, PlayStation 2, and a television with a satellite in there.” Jordan screams at the Big Tymers. “So, in closing, you and the Nappy Roots can kiss my black ass, for attempting to ruin the morale of the black man, trying to discredit my cause, and trying to dis…” Jordan is grabbed from behind by two security guards and Dante Jackson. The Big Tymers are in shock that anybody, let alone one of their guests, would actually interrupt their show. That had been going so well. Shabazz is dragged straight to the exit of the Grand Olympic Auditorium. He is thrown out of the building. Dante Jackson follows the man that he considers a sort of mentor. He looks at Jordan. “Man, you’re turning whiter than John Crichton after realizing that he can’t handle all of Jaden’s fine booty,” Dante says with a look that is a mixture of laughter and anger. “Concentrate on your damned wrestling match for goodness sakes. Stop trying to stop black people from doing what they do for a living.” “But it’s morally wrong. I represent the Malice Crew as an intelligent, respectful, skinhead hating bastard. And I do NOT approve of white men trying to steal Hispanic and or black women, like John Crichton is doing. I’ve got anger towards him, and you can bet your ass that I’ll take it out on him on Turmoil.” Dante Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz begin walking towards Jordan’s rented Cadillac Deville. Before they enter the car, Dante says something to Jordan. “Say nigga, you heard what Duzza was saying about you?” “Nigga? Is that what I heard you just call me?” “Yeah, that’s what I said.” Dante replies. Jordan X Shabazz gets in the car and starts it up. Dante tries to get in the passenger seat, and he finds the door locked. He bangs on the window with his fists twice. “Hey, let me in.” “No. You called me a ‘Nigga.’ Have a nice day.” Jordan zooms off into the night as Dante Jackson is left stranded because he called Jordan a term that Jordan no longer likes to be used. The scene fades with Dante heading back into the Grand Olympic Auditorium. Could this be the last time Dante Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz interact? Or could this be a prelude to something much bigger... We'll have to wait and see...
