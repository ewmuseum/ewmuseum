We hire a buncha idiots

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4th Brandi sits behind her large oak desk, head buried in her hands. In front of her are piles of paperwork, organized neatly into sections and sub-sections. She sighs and spins her chair around, hoping the view from her rather large window behind her will soothe her stress away. She rubs her temples and stares out at the wonderful view. She leans forward quickly, her face twisted into a look of shock. She jumps up out of her chair and quickly smashes buttons on her phone, sitting on the desk. A woman’s annoyed voice crackles through the speaker. Woman- Yes? Brandi holds down the button with an urgent look on her face. She glances back over her shoulder and out the window once again. Brandi- Get…in…here…now! Woman- (Sighs) Again? Brandi- Now! Brandi runs back towards the window and presses her face up all the way against the glass. She turns her head as if to get a better like, and her cheek slams into the cold glass, but she doesn’t notice. The door opens a crack and a young, woman with curly orange hair timidly peaks her head in. Woman- Brandi? Brandi quickly turns away from the window, leaving a slight smudge of makeup where her cheek was. She doesn’t seem to notice as she rushes across the room. The woman nervously takes a step back, but Brandi swings open the door and pulls her inside the office by her arm. She drags the poor woman over to the window and quickly points outside, her arm coming within inches of the woman’s face. Brandi- Look! Do you see that!? Woman- Um…Brandi? What am I supposed to be seeing? Brandi throws her arms down like a child when they get frustrated with an adult for not understanding. Brandi- You don’t see it? The woman shakes her head. Brandi stands behind her and pushes her face up to the glass. She jabs her nail against it, filling the office with an annoying tapping sound. Brandi- It’s right there! Woman- That’s what you drug me in here for? Brandi- Yeah! Go get rid of it. The woman starts to laugh, but Brandi’s look proves that she’s dead serious, so the woman quickly shuts her mouth. Woman- I’m your secretary. That isn’t my kind of work. Brandi- Well then, either make it your work or find me someone who will. Now! Brandi turns her attention back to the window as her secretary is glad to scoot back out of the room fairly quickly. Brandi- I can’t believe this. I mean, I’m always so careful. I go through so much trouble to try and prevent something like this from happening. But does it do me any good? No! Of course not. Why should things go easy for once?…Well, things do seem to go rather smoothly most of the time. I mean, Tim is doing a pretty good job with the taxes. Managed to fix things up pretty nicely around here…That’s beside the point! The intercom beeps and she spins around quickly, waiting for some kind of good word. Secretary- I’ve informed someone of the situation and they said that they would take care of it immediately. Brandi- It’s about time! The secretary mutters something under her breath, but Brandi’s attention is back at the window and she doesn’t hear her. She sits back down in her chair and tries to focus on the paperwork once again. She manages to read through a couple forms and rearrange her piles a few times before turning the chair around once again. She watches closely as security guards walk out of the PCW Corporate office building, talking and laughing. Brandi watches them suspiciously, following their every movement as they walk towards the back of the parking lot and get closer to her Camaro. As they approach the car, one peers inside while the other walks around it. Brandi unlocks her window and yanks it open before sticking her head out. Brandi- Hey! Both men turn around and look up into the many rows of windows. They see Brandi towards the top of the building, and wave up to her. She doesn’t return the gesture, but instead offers them a quizzical look. One makes a telephone with his fingers and holds it up to his ear. Suddenly it clicks that these must be the men that her secretary called. She nods and points to something down below, causing the men to once again search around the vicinity of the car. Brandi- Right there! One man lays down on his stomach and looks underneath the car. The other man motions to Brandi that he can’t hear her. She sighs and cups her hands around her mouth. Brandi- Over there! Behind you! He just stands there, staring up at Brandi so she points behind him, making him turn around. He nudges his friend with his foot, and the two of them give chase throughout the parking lot. Suddenly the first security guard trips over something, and falls flat on his face. The other tries to jump over the fallen body, but his foot catches on his shoulder and he goes down also. Brandi- Damn it. Now I have to go take care of things myself. Brandi storms out of her office and quickly walks past the desk outside her office. The secretary shrinks back for a second, but calms down when Brandi walks past her without acknowledging her existence. Instead, she walks down the long corridor and pushes the button for the elevator. When the doors open, she hurries inside. A man in a suit and tie is already standing inside. Man- What floor? Brandi- Ground. The man pushes a button and Brandi leans against the back railing, impatiently. Man- How are you today? Brandi- Fine. Man- You like the new office? The elevator stops on the ground floor, and the doors open with a loud chime. Brandi nods quickly, and walks out leaving the man talking to himself. Brandi continues her journey past the front desk, ignoring the greetings from many of the workers. The front doors slide open, and her eyes squint in an attempt to adjust to the sudden burst of light. She walks down the blacktop, eyes focused on her objective. The two security guards are just managing to dust themselves off as she approaches them. Security Guard- Miss Wilson… Brandi- Shut up! You’re uglier than a baboon’s ass. Now, I sent you out here with one little job, and you can’t even accomplish that. Is there something wrong with you? I mean besides the obvious, being your face. I mean like, emotionally. You a little slow, or just stupid? The guard pauses for a second, while the other quickly raises his hand. Brandi lowers her head and sighs. Brandi- It was a rhetorical question! Guard- Rhetorical? Brandi- Nevermind. Guard- Are we in trouble? Brandi- Well, you couldn’t accomplish a simple task. You failed miserably, and made total fools of yourself in the parking lot. You’re an embarrassment to have working at a PCW building, and I’m disappointed in you. Someone from PURE could have done better! So, yeah I’d say you’re in a bit of trouble. Guard- We’re sorry. Brandi- You’re sorry?! Because of your incompetence, I had to leave my paperwork up in my office, and come down here to waste my time on a job that I figured the stupidest employees in our building could do. Well, congratulations. You proved me wrong. Now, go upstairs and outside my office while I try to figure out a job that the two of you won’t even be able to mess up. The two security guards quickly scamper off. Brandi, glad to have them gone, relaxes a bit. Brandi- Now, to take care of some business. Brandi circles around the car, and spots her concern. A few rows away, a stray dog runs along, stopping periodically to sniff the tires of a car. Brandi sneaks around behind her car, crouches down, and waits. The dog eventually makes its way to the row, and sniffs her tire. Brandi waits nervously until the dog turns around and starts to lift up its leg. Then, she jumps out from behind the car, raises up her arms, and screams. Brandi- Get away! The dog runs away, startled. Brandi realizes that he could come back, and quickly takes after him, high heels and all. She somehow manages to gain on the animal, and reaches down, grabbing him by the scruff of the neck. The dog turns it neck as if to snap at her, but she promptly smacks him on the tip of the nose and starts back towards the office, dog in tow. When she walks back through the automatic doors, the scruffy looking animal causes some odd looks in Brandi’s direction. Brandi notices that the snobby woman at the desk is glaring at her, so she walks over in her direction. Brandi- Hey Tina. I have a job for you. Tina rolls her eyes, but stands up and takes her headset off. Tina- What now? Brandi lets the animal go, and he quickly runs off towards the back of the building, barking and causing women to run through the halls screaming. Tina looks at Brandi, horrified, but Brandi just smiles, waves, and heads towards the elevator.
