A Simple Walk From The Parking Lot....

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/04/02 “Wow! I mean that was, like, SO AMAZING!!!” As we see only Black, the all too familiar voice of Xavier Kannon’s valet is heard screeching her delight at recent events. “He was all...’Well...It’ll be a LADDER CAGE MATCH!’ and you were all...’Like...SO?’...Then he was all...’I’ll beat you’....’Then you were like...’Um..No way!’...Then you SO pinned him! Fridays rule!” As the shot fades in we see Xavier Kannon and his valet Eleanor hall getting out of Ellie~!’s Jeep Cherokee outside the Arena that will house the monumentous Spring Fling PPV where Xavier will fight former friend and now sworn enemy Jeremy Howard in a Ladder match with a twist...It’s in a Cage. Both of them have broad grins of confidence across their faces after they walked triumphant from Fusion, Xavier pinning Jeremy to win his team, comprised of Boda, the Chef and Frisco, the Main Event. Xavier : “Fusion was the turning point my little Poon Puppy, up until then it was Jeremy who had been the oppressor, always being one step ahead....But he made a fatal..Well, hopefully it will be fatal, error...” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a Black hooded sweatshirt with a Green and Purple blazing “XK” on the front. With it h wears a pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Green and Purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. Xavier : “I think I actually did a good job of PRETENDING to be scared of the match! Like I, Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon from the Church of *Religious Reference Deleted* would be worried about being locked in a cage with Howard!” As he spouts off about Fusion Ellie~! nods along, watching her man grab the bags from the back of the Jeep before setting off for the wrestlers entrance. Eleanor : “Cage......With.......Howard!” His preppy valet is wearing a skinny fit Pink t-shirt with Snapple~! in glittery Silver across hr 32B chest. With it she wears a pair of faded Blue hipster jeans with a rainbow coloured fabric belt. Her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is loose and bounces with natural volume whenever she takes a step. Xavier : “In fact...I still think it is a bit unfair that Howard got the match he wanted, forcing me into ladder and Cage match...I mean, I won’t be able to pin him two matches straight this way! The Krazed Lunatic, the man who is Krayzie ‘til he dies, the Krayzie One....Pinned by ME in what should surely go down as the greatest moment in Fusion history!” They continue walking towards the exit, smug at the situation they have found themselves in. Xavier : “If that is the best Duke man do then his quest to own PCW will end in tears. How can he own a whole company when he can’t even deal with poor oppressed me? Well, it doesn’t matter now, Duke will be reduced to nothing more than 3rd Chair come Monday, and hopefully a new era, one where religious minorities such as *Religious Reference Deleted* can flourish.” They pass the ranks of expensive cars belong to PCW’s front office and marketing personal, and past the rows of rental cars the early arriving wrestlers have travelled to the show in. Xavier, who’s Auburn hair is tightly tied back, is carrying all of the bags, Ellie~! is carrying none. Eleanor : “Okay...So Duke and Howard are ticked off the list as no threat...Um...Who was that other guy?” Fresh from his own belief that the threat of Duke Williams and Jeremy Howard has been neutered, he seems hocked, and almost worried about the addition of a new foe. Xavier : “What do you mean other guy? There is Duke, the washed up, alcoholic, bigoted oppressor of *Religious Reference Deleted*.....He can do nothing to me tonight. Then there was Jeremy, who along with his bitch wife, ugly kid and downright backwards brother cannot stop me taking up my rightful position as Continental Champion....So who else is there?” Ellie~! seems hard in thought trying to remember the person’s name, only vaguely remembering something insignificant at the back of her mind. Xavier : “Is he in PCW?” Ellie~! nods, piecing together the attributes of this mystery threat whilst trying to work out his identity. Eleanor : “Yeah....He..Um..Had funny hair.....Didn’t really talk about a great deal of useful things, like his match....Er...His sister is that ‘popular’ one in OSW...’Popular’ as in a school slut, football team kind of way.....Er....he was in Asylum...” The mention of Asylum, the stable that Xavier was inducted into at Shang-Haied triggers his thoughts and narrows the man’s identity down some....Now he just has to work out which member of the group he destroyed is his threat. Xavier : “Er... Darkstar?..... Miyagawa?..... Gaijin?.... Trashcan Man.... Ed.... Joker?” As Xavier reels off the names his valet shakes her head after each name, muttering he comments. Eleanor : “No, wasn’t a Goth...Didn’t have a mail order bride..... No make up.... Who?... Wha?..... Huh?” The rejections of all his suggestions leaves the Scientologist confused, wondering who on earth it was that Ellie~! saw mention Xavier at Turmoil. His eyes suddenly narrow as he thinks of someone it could be. Xavier : “Wait a minute......It wasn’t one of the commentators was it? They are meant to mention my name!” His preppy valet then excitedly shouts out, now knowing who it was. Eleanor: B.S!!!” Xavier: “No, they are actually meant to say my name...They get paid for it...” Xavi’s response wipes the smile off his happy valet’s face as she again tries to tell him who it was. Eleanor : “No...B.S...Stormbringer, you know? He as in Asylum?” The look on Kannon’s face is so blank that isn’t even blank due to it’s blankness. He looks back at Ellie~! as if it is was something ludicrous she just made up out of nowhere. Xavier : “Who?” Eleanor : “Stormbringer...Like, the guy...Aimz is his sister for some reason....He fights End on Sunday, at least I think as he isn’t really mentioning much....He was in Asylum, then it broke up....He was the person that helped Howard win at March Madness? Now he hates you, Miya, Howard, Darkstar and apparently Nelson?” Even as she explains it to Xavier the Gold Patron Meritorious’s face stays blank, making Ellie~! pause for a second and think. Eleanor : “Or I could have been someone I invented...” Xavier : “I think that may be it my little Shag Squirrel! Why would I know or be involved with anyone like that...I actually HAVE a feud, and so far it’s been a good one...Even though it has involved me being Superkicked, hit with Chars and Powerbombed through Tables!” They are now in sight of the wrestlers entrance, the door through which Xavier will enter as a challenger, and hopefully leave as Continental Champion if he can do what very few if any can claim to have done...Beat Jeremy Howard in back to back matches. Xavier : “So that just leaves Duke and Jeremy now we have ascertained that B.S either doesn’t exist or he might as well not do....” They get to the entrance and Xavier fumbles about in his pocket for his identity which he shows the man in the booth only for the door not to be unlocked. Xavier : “Hmmm, the locking mechanism must be broken...” The man in the booth speaks into his radio and as Xavier paces around outside it along with his confused valet, 3 extra security men appear and open the gate from the inside. Xavier assumes it is being opened for him, but he is wrong. Xavier : : “Thanks...Huh?...WHAT?...GET OFF!!!!” The 3 security men wrestle him to the ground, pull his arm behind his back, and drag his bag away from him before emptying the contents put over the ground as they search it. Eleanor : “HEY! YOU CAN’T DO THAT DO HIM!!! LISTEN TO ME...YOU...AND YOU...STOP IGNORING ME!!!” Kannon yells out in pain as his struggling only leads to his arm being pulled up harder and the security man is now straddling him to subdue his attempts of escape with the added annoyance of Ellie~! pulling at one of his arms in a vain effort to break his grip. Eleanor : “Get.......Off.........Him!” The man finishes looking through the first bag, the contents off which are now strewn all around the scene. Rather than leaving it at that he grabs to next bag, unzips it and holds it upside down to shake out the contents. Xavier : “WHAT ARE YOU DOING!?!?! THIS HAS TO BE ILLEGAL!!!” The security guard looks down at Kannon with contempt. Security : “We got a message from PCW management who said a locker room source gave your name as a steroid dealer...” At hearing this Xavier struggles even more and is pounced upon by the second guard to help subdue him. Xavier : “Management......Source.....WILLIAMS!!!! THIS IS YOUR WORK ISN’T IT!?!?!?!?! YOU CAN’T PROTECT YOUR BOY THIS WAY DUKE! I’LL GET HIM....WILLIAMS?!?!?!? As Xavier yells out the name of his oppressors the scene fades as more security come rushing along to carry out the search.
