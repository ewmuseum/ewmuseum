Date: Drawing toward the epic conclusion of the Tank ﻿*********The Tank __39—********* Of Mice and Rats The lights are bringing me from the darkness now,but my eyes don’t work so well anyway ~Leigh Donovan Wednesday, November 20 - Day 139 Leigh Donovan lies motionless in the infirmary as he has since the chaos that went down in UnitB yesterday. The injuries aren’t series, the doctors said, though they may prove disfiguring - it’stoo early to tell how many of the scars will heal. He’ll need dentures, but that will likely have to wait until his release from the Tank. As of now,he cares little about his appearance. His face is a mask of bruises, scars, and dried blood - hecan still taste it. When he’s released from the Tank, he knows that he’ll care about hisappearance. His appearance will be part of his livelihood. Of course, he’ll still be able to affordany cosmetic surgery that he may need, being one of the richest young rockstars on the planet. How he’ll avoid the cameras and publicity upon his release for a long enough time to get thesurgery is another question. But none of this matters to him now. All that matters is that he failed in his task to destroy J-Thug. Not only did he fail, but he was then humiliated by the beast by receiving a viciousthrashing, unaided by any of the guards - they were all busy elsewhere. Most humiliating of all,he was saved by his cellmate, Jason Snow. Why couldn’t he have just let Leigh take the beatinglike a man? Even if it resulted in death, to Leigh, it would be much better than having each ofthose prisoners remember that Leigh needed Snow in his moment of conflict. He’s not sure who he hates more now - Snow, the man who saved him from his beating, or J-Thug, the man who issued it. The sound of a mop breaks the silence of the room and soon enough, Leigh realizes that it’sHarvey Blake on his work detail - he was transferred from the kitchen to hospital maintenancewhen the Warden felt tension was getting a little too high between the whites and the blacks inthe cafeteria. “Har-” Leigh calls, still unable to speak very well - the prison doctor warned him against it, infact. Even if she hadn’t, the pain that came with each word would keep his verbalizing to aminimum. The left side of his face is completely purple, bruised from his broken jaw. “You’re a pathetic sight, you know that?” smiles Harvey as he speaks in a playful voice. He’sstanding straight with his mop at the foot of Leigh’s bed. “Jesus. Look at you. Fuckinggruesome, man. Ain’t a pretty kid no more, that’s for damn sure.” Leigh tries to return Harvey’s warm smile, but his broken jaw has completely immobilized thelower portion of his purple face. Harvey understands. “You know something though, kid,” Harvey says, sitting down beside Leigh on the bed, “I can’tsay that I’m not disappointed that you didn’t get that big motherfucker in a body bag. Cameclose enough, I’ll admit. Another second and he’d have been bleeding his fucking brains. Butstill...” The light drains from Leigh’s eyes. “But don’t worry about that now,” Harvey, the one eyed Nazi, says in a soothing voice. “Youproved your loyalty to me, and I guess you can say what came of the situation might end upbetter than we ever imagined. Then again, it could turn completely sour.” Leigh doesn’tunderstand what Harvey’s talking about now - he’s not exactly up on current events having spentthe last night and day in the infirmary. “Still, son,” Leigh’s voice grows narrow. “You disappointed me, and that’s something I don’tjust forget about. You had your shot at that nigger and you blew it. I’ll give you a few dayswhile I decide how I want you to pay the debt.” And then, quite suddenly, Harvey’s rock hardface turns warm. He pats Leigh painfully on the cheek, grabs his mop, and exits the room. Wednesday, November 20 - Day 139 Nothing had changed when Jason Snow came back into the Tank from Turmoil. When hestepped into Unit B, all eyes were still on him - the man who broke down the mountain they allknew as J-Thug. Now, lying peacefully on his bunk with the cell all to himself, he’s unfortunately disturbed byhis partner in crime, Tim McDonald. “There’s slugger,” says Tim playfully, slapping Snow onthe leg. “You’d better not fuck up tomorrow, you know. Odds are three to one against you, andwe’ve got a shitload of guys betting on J-Thug.” Tim McDonald and his Italians run thegambling circuit in the Tank - it’s part of how they make their riches, despite being incarcerated. “Fucking bets coming in from all over the Tank - not just Unit B.” Snow’s curiosity is sparked and he can’t help but think whatever McDonald is talking aboutcan’t be good. “What the hell are you talking about?” Snow says, sitting up in his bunk. McDonald looks stunned. “What am I talking about?” McDonald repeats. “You playing dumpon me?” “I have no fucking clue what you’re talking about, you fucking weasel!” Snow and McDonaldhave been at a truce for quite some time, but from time to time their true feelings about eachother surface. Still, they’re forced into a faction by the thread of a common enemy who seeks todestroy them both - Vincent Giovanni, the man who found out the real reasons for DougHattoni’s death. “Jesus,” McDonald says, “have you been under a fucking rock?” “Tuesday,” Snow says in point form. “Turmoil.” “Shit, that’s right!” McDonald laughs. “You just got back.” “Exactly,” Snow says, his cold eyes locking dangerously on McDonald. “Now... what thefuck is going on!?” “You and J-Thug,” McDonald says, unable to keep a straight face. “In the gym. Tomorrow.” Snow silently glares at McDonald for a moment. “In here,” McDonald says, “This is just as big as Ali versus Frasier. Snow versus J-Thug.” Snow leaps down from his bunk, enraged that something like this has been organized without hispermission. “How the hell is that possible?” he says through clenched teeth. “I’m up forfucking parole at the end of the week, you little cocksucker! This is going to fuckeverything up! The guards will have me in the hole before either of us break a fucking sweat!” “Calm down!” McDonald says, backing away, though his smile is unwavering. “Half of thefucking action toward this thing is coming from the guards! Without Geoffery Rankin around tofuck this up, no one’s going to say a God damn word. We’re all looking forward to this Snow,and everyone’s got something riding on it. Just relax and get yourself ready for the fight.” “McDonald,” Snow says, grabbing the spiky haired young man by the collar, “if you fuck up myparole, I swear to God that you’ll be the first person I’ve ever murdered.” Tim has heard threatslike this many times before, but never combined with such cold and serious eyes. “I’ll eat yourheart with your motherfucking bones, McDonald. Don’t fucking cross me on this.” “Everything will be fine,” Tim says calmly as he removes Snow’s firm hands from his collar. “Just be ready for the fight. If you lose, I’ve got a lot of people to pay.” With that, McDonaldwhirls to his left and makes his way out of Snow’s cell. With a sigh, Snow steps to the doorway of his tiny cell and looks across the balcony. Immediately, he notices that he’s under the heavy gaze of J-Thug on the other side of thebalcony, his left eye moderately swollen from the blows that Snow gave him yesterday morning. It takes nothing from the menacing nature, or the intentions, of his steady glare. Wednesday, November 20 - Day 139 Tim McDonald, after having waited nearly a half hour to use the telephone, quickly dials thedigits that have long been imprinted in his brain. Finally, the game of chess between himselfand Vincent Giovanni will end - fatally. He can barely hold the telephone thanks to his sweaty palms - this call, of course, could turnagainst him, and not Giovanni, in an instant. “Hello?” a gravelly voice on the other end answers. It’s Mario Quintanelli, the highest rankingperson in the Hattoni family mafia that Tim knows well enough to comfortably call. “Mario? That you?” Tim says, almost in a whisper. “Maybe,” the man on the other end says, “depends who the fuck I’m talking to.” “An old friend,” Tim says in a low voice - he’s pulling out all of his thespian skill now. “TimMcDonald.” “No fucking way!” the man on the other end says warmly. “Did they finally let you out of thatcage? I thought you were penned up for at least a year.” “Probably less,” McDonald answers, “but I’m still here. This call is about... business.” “Business?” Mario says, startled. “Jesus, Tim. Things have changed. You know, I’ve got...problems with the phone. You know what I mean? I think the connections bad.” Tim knowsexactly what Mario means - the wire’s tapped by the FBI. “We’ll work around it,” says Tim. “Tread lightly, my friend,” Mario warns. “This is the telephone that my wife and my children use. I don’t want to...” he hesitates. “You get the idea. Now... lets get down to it.” “I’ve got bad news, Mario,” Tim says even quieter. “Really fucking bad.” “Is this something you want me to handle?” “No offense man,” Tim says. “I know you’re moving up quick, but this shit is above you - allthe way to the fucking top. I need you to take it to the proper authorities on my behalf.” “What the fuck are we talking about here, Tim?” Tim thinks of the best way to word his lies. “It’s about the recent bad news that a few of ourfriends got,” he says, referring to the recent incriminating evidence that surfaced, making itlikely that several high ranking mobsters will end up in prison. “You know something about that?” Mario sounds eager. “That’s big shit out here, Timmy. Ifyou’ve got some information, this could fucking make both of our careers.” Tim loveshearing those words. However, he knows that should Justin Hattoni, Doug’s heir, not buy thelies he’s about to spew, it will break everything he’s worked so hard for - not to mentionthe fact that Tim and his entire family will be dead by the new year. “Yeah,” says Tim. “We’ve got fucking pest problems here in prison. God damn rodents all overthe place.” The word Tim is eluding, for the fact that the phone line is tapped, is: rat. “You’re fucking kidding!” Mario is upset. “We fucking knew it! I was talking to...” he nearlyslips. “Fuck!” he exlaims. “You’re not going to believe who it is, Mario,” Tim says, his voice starting to shake. “Tell me,” Mario insists, much to Tim’s relief. “Who is this prick?” “A friend of some high-ups,” McDonald answers. “He likes to Seek things... then he likes toDestroy them.” He’s referring to Vincent Giovanni, known throughout mafia circles as Mr.Search and Destroy. Of course, Tim is lying - he was actually the one who anonymouslyhanded over the evidence to the authorities, anonymously. This whole time, he has been plottingto frame Vincent Giovanni with his own people. “Jesus fucking Christ!” exclaims Mario. “Are you sure.” “They have him for murdering a C.O in here,” Tim says. “Caught him red-fucking handed. Nohigh-priced lawyer was going to get him out of this one. So he rolled the fuck over and landed aton of his pals in prison. In return, he’s not going to serve an extra fucking day for the murder.” “I never trusted that motherfucker!” says Mario. “Listen,” Tim says, getting to the real point. “What the fuck do you want me to do about this? Imean, S and D is a pretty high up guy, you know. Do you want me to wait for you to get wordback to me?” “Fuck that!” Mario says quickly. “I’ll vouch for you. Besides, the Don himself sent a shitload ofletters from prison saying that you’re the new man in our business. You’re word is golden withthis family now, kid. Every since you shared a bunk with Hattoni, himself, the whole fuckingfamily has been waiting for you to get out.” “So,” Tim says, trying to get back to the point, “about this... rodent... problem.” “I’ll tell you what to do,” Mario says coldly. “Find the biggest fucking mouse trap you can, andsnap the motherfucker’s neck.”
