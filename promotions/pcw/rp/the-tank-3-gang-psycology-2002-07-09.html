The Tank 3 - Gang Psycology

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9, 2002 Prisoner Profile File Photo: Coming Soon...Name: Douglas James HattoniPrisoner Number: 488856Nickname(s): The Doug, The HattBorn: 02-08-1950Height: 5'9Weight: 315Hair: Greying, thick, kept neatEyes: BrownFacial Hair: Thin grey mustacheTattoo(s): Children’s names (left arm), Wife’s name (right arm)Build: FatFrame: MediumIdentifying Characteristics: Thick grey hair always kept neat, mafia appearance.Current Sentence(s): 1994 - Drug Trafficking (8 years), Murder (20 years)Previous Convictions: 1977 - Manslaughter (served 8 years)Family: Wife, 2 sons From the Office of the Warden “The most powerful man in the entire prison is without a doubt Doug Hattoni.” says Warden Gavin Miller as he rubs his aging bald head. He’s leaning back in his seat with his feet resting on his desk. “Hattoni came to the Tank back in ‘77 on a manslaughter conviction - should have been murder one, but it’s so damn hard to touch these mafia bastards. In the end, the D.A took what he could get and Doug served eight years of a twelve year sentence. By 1985, he was back on the streets... or in his case, back in the penthouse.” “Doug Hattoni didn’t get powerful in prison,” continues the Warden, “he stayed powerful. He was a notorious mob leader on the outside, and of course, when he came to the Tank the first time, the Italians used him to build a foundation. For eight years, they ran the Tank. When Hattoni was released, they fell apart.” “In 1994, Hattoni came back to the Tank,” the Warden sighs, “A lot older, and a hell of a lot wiser. Drugs were coming in by the boatloads and we couldn’t find a damn trace of evidence that would lead back to him. The Italians were once again, very quietly, the number one gang in the Tank.” The Warden scratches his clean shaven chin, “You see, the Italians aren’t like the other gangs in the Tank. Where the Thugs - the blacks - and the Aryans - the whites - would beat the hell out of their grandmother for fun, the Italians do everything for business purposes. And the business manager of the whole operation... ...is Doug Hattoni.” ***Monday, July 8 - Day 3 Doug Hattoni sits quietly on the bed of his cell, the barred gate left wide open - the prisoners are free to roam around the unit for another few hours. Doug has never been one to flee his cell at every opportunity like most of the prisoners in the Tank. For years, he’s quietly lead the Italian group, which has never been completely exclusive to Italians. And in his time in the Tank, no one has imported drugs without Doug knowing and approving - at least, not without being punished. “You wanted to see me?” a shaky voice asks from just outside Doug’s cell. Without looking, Doug gestures the speaker to enter. “I’m looking for information,” Doug says in his raspy voice. “Why else do people ask for me?” The second man in Hattoni’s cell is a young, freckle faced man with bushy red hair and a wirey build. At only a few inches over five feet and just nineteen years of age, no one would consider this young man a physical threat. However, since proving his worth to the old Italian - as well as other gang leaders - as an information man, he’s been able to walk the halls of Unit B without fear. To his fellow inmates, he’s known simply as The Rat. “A new Prisoner came into Unit B a couple of days ago,” Doug begins, still without looking at the Rat. “That would be Jason Snow,” answers the impish red-head. “As you may have heard, my boys are losing clout around here, these days,” Doug says without emotion, leaning back on the concrete wall of his cell. “Oh really?” The Rat says with wide eyes, “I hadn’t noticed.” He’s trying to play dumb, but Doug is too smart for his games. “Don’t play fucking dumb with me, kid!” Hattoni sneers, “It’s all over the Tank. My hold on this Unit is slowly eroding away. I do not enjoy losing control of things.” The Rat nods, not knowing what he should say in response - if he should say anything at all. Doug scratches his fat stomach with his hairy left hand and then runs his fingers through a thick head of gray hair that no middle-aged man has a right to. “And sadly, I believe I know the cause of this, though I do not like to admit to it.” The Rat raises an eyebrow - he’s a sucker for juicy information. Little does Doug Hattoni know, the Rat has also been dealing information to both the Thugs and the Nazis over the past few months. “The cause?” Why Doug Hattoni trusts the Rat is beyond the other high ranking Italians in Unit B. “Bruno has been my muscle for eight years now,” Doug sighs, confiding in the untrustworthy red-head, “Hell, Bruno was my muscle back in the seventies when I first came to the Tank.” “There’s a problem with Bruno?” “I’m fifty-one years old, kid,” Doug says, circling the question, “Do you know what that makes me around here? Physically? I dinosaur. Bruno’s five years older than me. Everyone in the Tank knows that the main muscle of my gang is growing old and weak. And because of that, no one fears us - no one fears me.” The Rat raises an eyebrow, “So what do you want me to do about this?” Doug, in his usual way, refuses to directly answer the question, “This new inmate - this Jason Snow... I have seen him a few times here and there. He looks like a lion. He could bring the intimidation back to my Stallions. He could make me a feared man in this prison once again.” “You want me to bring Jason Snow to you?” “Tell me, Rat - what do you know about this... Jason Snow?” Doug asks, finally turning his head to the young inmate who he’s been conversing with all this time. A smile comes over the Rat’s freckled young face as he steps deeper inside Hattoni’s cell. *** From the Office of the Warden “Humans, by nature, are pack animals,” begins the Warden as he browses through a bookshelf to the left of his desk, “We eat in packs, we laugh in packs, and we hunt in packs. In the Tank, I get a chance to see human beings in their most primitive of forms - ruthless, greedy, self-centered... everyone just wants to do what’s best for themselves. And what’s best for themselves, often, is to join up with a group... a gang... a pack.” The Warden turns away from his bookshelf and leans back on his desk, “In the Tank, not everyone joins a pack. But the more you have to offer, the harder it is to stay away. Quite possibly the most important asset a man can have in the Tank is the ability to fight. And I’m not talking about some emotional mumbo-jumbo that a psychiatrist might give you - I’m talking about the physical ability to fight.” “That’s what everything comes down to, isn’t it?” he continues, “I mean, if the president gets pissed off at another country, does he sit down and talk it over with them. Maybe for a minute or two, but in the end, he opens up a can of whop-ass on them and then goes to an elementary school and preaches how violence isn’t the answer. In the Tank, violence is the answer. And the better you are at dealing violence, the more sought after you are by the higher ups in the Tank... it’s also you’re only chance to stay away from them...” *** Tuesday, July 9 - Day 4 Miggy has been trying to find a way to approach the subject since he was threatened by Harvey Blake and his Nazi companions. He wasn’t able to sleep last night. How can he try to persuade Jason Snow to sign on with Harvey Blake’s Nazi crew, even after Snow knows how much he hates the group of skinheads. He’ll have to sort all of that out later. For now, his only chance to keep his throat uncut is to find a way to put Snow in with the Nazis. Besides, maybe with Snow - who he believes is a friend - in the Nazis, the Nazis won’t hassle poor Miggy so much. “Jason,” Miggy addresses his lion-like companion as they take a seat beside each other in the noisy cafateria, “How are you settling in?” Snow grins, “The people are pricks, the food sucks, and my roomate keeps talking about fucking me in the ass.” He leans back in his seat and looks around the cafeteria. Each of the gangs - the Italians, the Aryans, the Thugs, the Latinos, and even a small group of Jews, separate themselves from the other inmates. Snow notices that there are a large number of prisoners that don’t seem to hold rank in any of the gangs, but he also notices that none of these prisoners look like a dangerous sort. “Well, it doesn’t have to be that way,” Miggy says quietly, looking his young companion directly in the eye. He hates doing this, but he knows Harvey Blake - the monster - will have him killed by the end of the week if Jason Snow doesn’t join the Aryans. “Sure it doesn’t,” Snow shrugs. “I could ignore the people, go on a hunger strike, and slit my cellmates throat... but then I’d be stuck in here for another fifteen to twenty years. I can’t wait to get away to Turmoil tonight, Mig. I don’t know how you’ve been here so long without going crazy.” Miggy nods, trying to bring the subject up gently, “Well, there are people here who would help you - stick up for you. You have a lot to offer, you know.” “Like who?” Snow sneers. “Well,” Miggy sighs and then looks over his shoulder at the Aryans sitting a few tables back. Snow takes the hint. “The Nazis!?” “Look, Jason,” Miggy says in serious tones, hating himself for his own words, “You have to face reality. This is prison - things are different. In here, if you get an opportunity to be protected, you take it.” “What the fuck has gotten into you, Mig?” Snow asks, his brow wrinkling, “You hate those Nazi fucks as much as I do!” Snow is standing now, and points at the Aryans without concern for their notice. His temper is beginning to boil - he can’t control himself when he gets like this. Miggy looks up in fear, “Jason, I’m just trying to give you advice here. If the Hombres gave me a chance to join up with them, I’d do it in a second. But I can’t - I’m old, I have no value to them. You have value.” Snow’s eyes blaze as he glares down at his little friend, Miggy. Suddenly, a Correctional Officer approaches from behind and grabs Snow by the arm. “Snow! Come on, you’re flight’s ready, you lucky bastard. You’re going to have to tell us how the fuck you managed to persuade a judge to let your ass out of here two days a week.” As the C.O. tugs Snow toward the exit, the young lion’s anger for Miguel Sanchez - Miggy - is still evident. “You know what,” he nearly shouts, “Fuck you, old man! I thought you weren’t like the rest of these fucks! But now I see what you really are! Just another greedy little prick!” “Come on Snow!” the C.O. tugs Snow hard, quieting the young lion, and the pair leave the cafeteria. Miggy watches his friend escorted out of the cafeteria, on his way to a wrestling show where he will entertain thousands of fans live and millions on television - he’s so lucky, and he doesn’t even know it, thinks Miggy. The little Latino then looks over his shoulder and finds himself burning under the lone fiery eye of Harvey Blake. *** From the Office of the Warden “Balance.” The Warden begins while sitting behind his desk, toying with a teetering scale. “Everything has to find a balance - a happy medium. I don’t deny that I hate Harvey Blake and everything the sick bastard stands for. But I do have to acknowledge that without Harvey Blake, or someone like him, there wouldn’t be a white man in the Tank who wasn’t forced on his knees every night after lights out - and I’m not talking about praying.” The leaves his scale for a moment and opens a drawer of his desk. He pulls out a small stone and places it in one cup of the scale, dropping that cup to the scale’s base. “Even though the situation’s not so drastic anymore, in the Tank, whites are the minority. Whether that’s a problem with the judicial system or the economical state of America, I don’t know. What I do know is that next to the Jews, whites are the most scarce group in the Tank. That’s not to say there’s not a fair number of them - and that’s not to say they’re not feared. They’re feared because of Harvey Blake. Without that fear factor, they’re nothing. So you take the good - the fact that whites are able to walk through the Tank without fear - and the bad - the fact that they’re lead by a the meanest one-eyed Nazi ever to slice a swastika into himself... and that brings us back to what I mentioned a moment ago...” “A happy medium,” the Warden pulls a tiny stone carving of a swastika out of the drawer and places it in the other cup of the teetering scale. Within moments, the weight of the swastika and the stone balance. “Or maybe, the medium’s not so happy afterall...” *** Snow has long-since left for his appearance on PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil, and Miggy hasn’t spoken to another person all day. Now, at nearly six-thirty, he’s near tears as he rushes his little cart of books toward the library. Will the Aryan’s be waiting for him in the gym? He’s almost sure they will be after the scene in the cafeteria today. As soon as he enters the gym, his nightmares become reality - he finds himself staring into that devilish one blue eye of Harvey Blake. “Hi there, spick,” Blake says with a smile, his shirt removed, exposing his forty-five year old tattoo covered upper body. Miggy wants to turn back, but the books must go to the library. His heart sinks as he looks at the far end of the gym where two of Blake’s Nazis are watching the door to make sure no C.O’s are nearby. The little Latino understands his fate. “You see, spick,” Harvey says in a much more serious tone as he slowly swaggers toward his prey, “There’s a time-proven way to run a gang in the Tank. If a blue chip comes in - recruit him. That’s what I tried to do with Jason Snow, spick - but you failed me.” Blake begins circling Miggy who stands still with tears welling up in his terrified eyes. “Failure can never,” Harvey says shaking his head, “never... go unpunished.” With that, he delivers a hard right hand to the little Latino’s right cheek. Miggy collapses from the blow and then finds himself swarmed by a pack of angry Nazis, all kicking and punching away at his old and time-weathered body. “Get the fuck out!” screams one of the skinheads watching the far door before sprinting across the gym toward the main floor of Unit B. The pack of violent Nazi’s immediately follow him. By the time the C.O struts into the gym, holding a cup of coffee in his right hand, all the Nazis have successfully escaped the scene. “Oh shit!” he gasps, dropping the coffee - all he finds is Miggy’s motionless body lying in a pool of ever-deepening blood.
