Tonights the night for retribution (part 1)

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-11-02 Bob Vurdo sits on the seat in his office awaiting the arrival of Grimgor who has been with the black gang all day. Bob is waiting for the right moment to pounce on the black men's families. It will be a heartless massacre but Bob doesn't care about that, all Bob cares about is himself. If an innocent person has to die just to prove a point Bob Vurdo has no problem with that. In the mind of Bob Vurdo people are seen as objects that can either be used or disposed of to get to the top. Grimgor knocks on Bob Vurdo's office door waiting to be welcomed in. 'Come in,' Bob yells in a rather unenthusiastic voice. Grimgor turns the door handle and walks into Bobs office. The room is rather empty only being furnished by Bob's business desk complete with computer and two filing cabinets leaning against the back wall. Grimgor slowly walks over to the chair facing Bob on the other side of the desk. Bob looks up from his computer screen with bloodshot eyes and a plain look on his face. 'What happened today?' Bob doesn't look like he's in a very god mood. 'Tonight would be the night to strike.... G-Dawg is having a party at his house.... It will be the last party before they attack us.' Bob looks at Grimgor and smiles nervously. 'Tonight is the night.... Tonight is our last chance to win this battle.' Bob gets up from his seat and walks toward the blue cabinets at the back of the dark cold room. 'Anything the matter?' Grimgor knows Bob isn't in a great mood. 'Yes.' Bob snaps and turns around to face Grimgor with a red face with an expression of rage on it. 'If we don't do this properly tonight I am a dead man! Your a dead man!' Bob turns back around and opens up a draw from the filing cabinet. Bob turns around with a gun in his hand which he stares down at. 'This here is the tool I used to get where I am and this here is what I'm going to use to stay at the top.... You haven't met the real Bob 'Fucking' Vurdo, the meanest fucker in the world! I'm back and I'm ready to do business like I used to!' Grimgor gulps nervously as he knows his life is on the line in more than one way now. 'Life has fucked me over Grimgor! Black people fucked me over and took my woman! I'm going to do that to them right now tonight! Tonight is the night for retribution!' Bob opens up his black business vest and places his gun into the inside pocket. 'I'm not going to be on the front line but I will be there to give the orders.... I need this for protection.' Grimgor nods 'I'm going to go to bed.' Bob looks down at Grimgor and smiles. 'It's five fucking thirty and your going to bed.... That is the biggest load of crap I ever heard you fucking pussy.... Your going to that party tonight so don't even try to get out of it.' This is exactly what Grimgor didn't want, the less time he spends with the black men the better his chance of survival. 'Alright,' Grimgor sighs before getting up from his seat and walking toward the door. 'Thank you Grimgor.... Thank you.' Grimgor opens up the door and walks out shutting it quietly behind him. Bob sits back down in the seat for his desk and picks up the phone. He punches in Tony's number so he can discuss the plan of attack. 'Hello.' Tony answers the phone in his usual high pitched cold hearted tone. 'Yeah Tony its Bob.' 'Heya Bob.' 'Tonight's the night we will strike all the black men are going to a party.' 'Are you sure about this?' Tony knows what a cold heartless thing Bob is doing. He doesn't want Bob to regret anything this important. 'Positive.' To be the boss of any Mafia family you have to be cold hearted. To be the boss of the Vurdo family is certainly no exception. 'Well I'll get the men ready.... Meet me down the casino in half an hour.' Tony doesn't agree with the needless killing of the black woman and children but he doesn't care enough to oppose it. He is actually quite happy to see Bob Vurdo back to his usual Ruthless self. 'Alright.' Bob hangs up the phone and stands up from his seat to get ready for the night. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Joey Cuppi and Pietro Darillo are new comers in the Vurdo family waiting for their chance to rise in the ranks. They have been called to a meeting by Tony and are very excited little do they know about the night that lays ahead. They sits down at the long table waiting for Tony to enter the room so they can all commence the meeting. There are four other young up and comers in the Vurdo family sitting around waiting for their orders. Bob Vurdo sits at the head of the table silent waiting for Tony. Tony opens up the door at the back of the bare room and enters. The room stays silent as he makes his way over to a chair waiting for him under the table next to Bob. Tony sits down and looks around at the six young men he is about to give the orders to. 'Tonight is the night which could make or brake you all.' Tony looks around seriously at the young men who are listening intensely. 'I want you to split up into three groups of two men and go to three separate houses each.' Tony pauses and lets the information sink into the men who are looking nervously on at Tony. 'When you get into the houses I want you to kill everyone in them.... Woman.... Children..... Anyone.' The men are looking quite shocked and nervous about this arrangement. 'I have three envelops in my pocket which gives you the addresses I want you to go to.... You guys do this and your made men.... This is a big time operation.' Tony pauses and spends a few seconds in eye contact with each of the six men. 'Are you in or are you out.... This is your last chance to get out.' Tony gives them a few seconds to think it over. He looks around at the men and can tell each one of them will be in on it. He hands out the envelopes to each second man. 'When your finished come meet up with me at the docks and we'll get you all cleared.' Bob and Tony each get up from their seats and walk out the door into the empty dark casino. 'You think they'll do good?' Tony asks. 'So long as we slow down those nigger fuckers I'm happy.'
