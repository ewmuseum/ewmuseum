The Jobfather - - Part III

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 March 2003 The hunter has become the hunted or for a better analogy, the prankster is being the recipient of his own level of abuse. The Front Office Jester for a number of days now has been living a nightmare as an unknown entity continues to orchestrate his master plan by using the Emerald Menace as his own personal pawn. From the vague telephone conversations we can deduce that this person has singled out Gaz Green not for a personal vendetta but for his ties with The Front Office. The actual stable has been circulating for many months now and in that time they have asserted their authority right across the spectrum, from bullying the lower card to strangling the upper. In that time they have crushed the competition, be it ‘Asylum’ ironically a stable that once homed the collective of Pranks With Attitude and have ridden the company of Primetime Championship Wrestling of it’s cobias deadweight. With so much happening in the life of Gaz Green at the moment this extra baggage is quickly becoming a distraction. A distraction that may filter over and into his feud with current Tag Team Champions Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor who continue to thwart his efforts of capturing that elusive fifth title reign. A distraction in his quest for one benchmark, worryingly more so is that it could also jeopardise his first clear-cut chance of becoming the PCW World Heavyweight Champion as he headlines the March Madness Pay-Per-View. This next instalment opens on the barren land of concrete of a restaurant car park. Various vessels litter the parking bays, some tatty motors and some upmarket classics, which glisten from the over hanging street lighting. One of these cars a real prize, a Shelby GT 500 with a license plate that reads ‘F.O Jester’. This alone begs the question, ‘Why is the Jester eating at some average Italian Restaurant?’ Usually a man of taste, a man who likes to throw his money about, so to see his automobile parked at some average restaurant then something simply doesn’t add up. Entering the establishment the place was a heap of chaotic human activity as waitresses bustled about laden in plates of food and customers sat in conversation. Sitting uncomfortably in the corner, next to a gaping glass window is the Conniving Collective of both Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green and his faithful valet of Natalia Miyagawa who finger through their menus. “They could’ve at-least picked a classier joint to conduct business!” murmured Wrestle Online’s only prankster. “I don’t see why I had to come?” retorted the Soviet Scarlet in a sulky manner. “With you here I can guarantee no-more surprise’s!” sighed the Lord Of Laughter as the camera dropped to ground level to show his black shoe dragging a briefcase back into the darkness of the underside of the table. As usual nothing appeared out of the ordinary. The Front Office Entertainment Manager was dressed to impress with his forest green business suit whilst his representative sat wearing a glistening evening dress, it’s array of colours twinkling from the restaurants lighting. “So what will it be?” roared some female voice. Peering atop of his menu, his hair and eyes just coming into view, Gaz Green inspected the assailant. A large woman holding a pen and notepad stood motionless, her hair draping scruffily over her broad shoulders she was clad in a red and white apron that was randomly stained giving it an atlas effect. A real ogre that could’ve made a cameo appearance in Lord Of The Rings or even rivalled PCW’s very own Bertha in the looks department. “When you’ve done staring what will it be?” hissed the hired help again. “Err… Arr… I’ll have what ever your having Gaz?!?!” said Natalia with a half hearted smile as she placed the burden of ordering on the shoulders of her client. “Well….” Injected the beast once more. “Tell you what, we’ll have a fourteen inch ham and pineapple with a dish of fries and two clean glasses of your finest bubbly… Squire… I mean Squiress!” replied the former four times Tag Team Champion, asserting a sarcastic tone in the latter half of his order. On queue the beast of Bognor vanished into the density of the crowd as she went to fulfil the order of the Primetime couple. Breathing a sigh of relief the duo was again left to themselves. As Gaz lowered his jaw to speak a blanket of darkness filtered over as two behemoth individuals loomed over menacingly, emitting an awe of intimidation. “Your sister has just left squire… She’s getting us our food and believe me I use the word food loosely…” “HUH?!?!” both men retorted in a synchronised fashion. The bulky henchmen stood almost identical wearing long flowing black trench coats, vacantly staring down at the devious duo of Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa who stare back worryingly. A few minutes of silence wears on when suddenly one of these geezers dropped his heap of flesh into the chair whilst holding his hand firmly into his jacket pocket. Creating a gun effect it became apparent that the meeting is well in session. “The boss would like to see you!” grunted the man. “Whoa, I thought he would be here…” answered the worried PCW superstar. “We have strict orders to deliver you to the boss, he would like to make your acqintance… Err… Arr…” “Acquaintance you mean! What about Natalia, she has nothing to do with this?” asked the inquisitive Jester. Before any retort the goliath of waitress appeared, dropping the pizza into the table centre with a thud and disappearing for a second time, momentarily disrupting the meeting. Using his initiative Gaz lent over the dish sniffing at the aroma and tipped it’s content into the lap of the thug who bellowed in agony allowing both Gaz and Natalia to side step the second. Making their escape the Jester parried the case into the face of the advancing second thug the duo ventured into the car park where a black limousine stood coughing smoke into the atmosphere. Panicking Gaz sacrificed himself as his valet made her getaway, surrounded the sides certainly favoured the fiends when without warning a thud sounded as a blunt object crashed against the Jester’s cranium. Dropping to a heap with the briefcase handcuffed to his forearm his last image was that of a pair of black shoes standing before him and a car screeching from the crime scene. “You don’t look so tough now without your Front Office friends now, huh?!?” murmured a voice from within the limousine as a hand protruded from atop of the ajar window to drop a cigarette to the ground. With that said the tailored Jester was carted across the car park in what looked like a kidnapping scene and thrown into the vehicle unconscious begging the questions, ‘Who is the man in the limousine?’ ‘And what have they installed for the Pranks With Attitude member?’ - To be continued -
