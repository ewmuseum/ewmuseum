Be the Man.

Date: not entered SAN JOSE TRIBUNEApril 18, 2002 LOCAL WRESTLER PLANS TO MAKE GOOD, EAT OPPONENT “Yon title match shall be most deliciously satisfying!” Local product Big Alan Steele boasts. AP Newswire – Seattle, Wash. Posing for a throng of fans and media on the steps of the Key Arena in Seattle, Wash., wrestler Big Alan Steele offered bold statements of victory on his upcoming match, and praise of his opponent as surely being ‘Tender and tasty.’ “We have come to blows many times in the past, this Overlord of Cold named Snow, and tomorrow I shall complete his mortal cycle once and for all, praise be to the gods of Olympia! I also believe that I shall also find this Hades-spawned Snow to be a man of taste, his well-maintained and lean physique to mesh nicely with a nice bottle of red! Hi-Keeba!” BAS stated, stunning the onlookers in attendance. “I hear the brains are the sweetest,” Added Mr. Steele. When further asked to expound upon his bizarre quotes, the capricious big man merely grinned ferally, flexing a powerful bicep much to the delight of various ladies in attendance. “The time is now upon us, where I, Big Alan Steele, shall make that final ascension to the summit of Mount Olympus, taking my anointed place with the gods on high, and snatching the belt from the hands of the vile and contemptuous Snow, no matter how succulent and juicy his calf and thigh muscles shall become upon a thorough basting in the oven, or possibly rotisserie cooked.” When informed that everyone knew of his intentions to win the title and needed clarification on his proposal to consume his opponent, Jason Snow of Backwater, Neb., a confused Mr. Steele again addressed the reporters at hand. “Why, tis merely a common aspect of wrestling, as taught to me by my trusted manager, none other than Sue Saksnussum! To properly attain PCW immortality as its World Champion, I must devour this man whole, totally digesting him en route to complete victory! Though we have feuded before and have had venomous words for each other in recent weeks, Lord of Cold shall almost certainly be well-marbled, with a kind of piquant aftertaste. Hi-Keeba!” When again confronted with reporter testimony that cannibalism is not a usual aspect to professional wrestling, the San Jose resident, dressed in a sterling white toga, seemed to grow agitated towards the crowd. “Your thickness of mind is stunning even to me, Big Alan Steele! Tis has all been explained to me quite perfectly by the maiden Sue, and tis a common vernacular speech! In order to BE the man, thou must EAT the man! I am only following the edicts of those who hath tread before me! Yet you question my motives! Begone, for there is much training to be done! The villainy that has pervaded PCW shall no longer reign supreme, and I, Big Alan Steele, rightly ordained by the heavens, shall strike thee hence, sending him back unto Hades bosom, from whence he hath come! No longer shall PCW live under a champion who has soiled the glean of the gold! For now, The son of Zeus’ time is at hand! Plus, I must prepare the ingredients for his cooking… Hi-Keeba!” Drawing strained laughter from the crowd, as many did not want to further anger the once genial giant, it was posed what would he do should Jason Snow turn out to be a tougher opponent, and more stringy, than anticipated? “I do not know,” shrugged Big Alan Steele, “But with enough ketchup you can eat ANYTHING!” It was then that a reporter asked him if he had a recipe ready for Jason Snow. A beaming Steele replied, “I do indeed!” Below is a transcript of that recipe: Marinated Jason Snow w/ bell peppers (under glass) 1 (232 pounds) Jason Snow, cut into 6 pieces4 cup red wine, mixed with 1 cup water24 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil8 large onions, halved and thinly sliced2 cups dry white wine10 to 16 bell peppers, green and red, trimmed, cored, and cut into eighthsSalt and pepper to taste Place the Snow pieces in a bowl with the wine-water mixture and marinate for 1 hour. Then drain and dry thoroughly with paper towels. In a pan large enough to hold all the ingredients, heat 10 tablespoons of the olive oil over medium-high heat…We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he sits dejectedly in front of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, who flings the daily edition of his hometown newspaper into his face. “In order to BE the man, you have to BEAT the man! BEAT THE MAN!! BEAT THE MAN!!” A seething Sue roars in the face of Steele, covering him in spittle. “You can’t do anything right, can you? Goddamn… one night before our biggest match… a mere 12 hours to go… and you go and pull this PR blunder!! How do I spin this, Al, huh? How do I spin you wanting to eat Jason Snow?! Dammit, you are about as sharp as a sack of wet kittens!!” “I merely…” “You merely what?! You merely made us look like idiots, that all!! We are the laughingstocks of PCW!! Eat the man… what were you thinking?” “Well, tis simply a mistake in semantics. I could have sworn upon Hera’s statue that you said ‘eat.’ Tis an honest mistake.” BAS tries to soothe. “All shall be righted once the title is exchanged into my own hands, once again bringing glory and honor to Mount Olympus! Hi-Keeba!” Sue is seething, but at least her face color has changed from purple to a simple scarlet. “Well, I guess I’d better get on the phones… I have a lot of PR work to do because of this eating business. YOU just start packing… is that a spatula you have there?” Big Al looks around nervously, hiding something behind his back. “…….. No.”
