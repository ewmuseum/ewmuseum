Juan Recieves an Identity Change (Part 3)

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 Time: 7:29 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: Soccer Fields Part 3 “ It ain’t worth winning if you can’t win big.” The scene opens with Juan Borro along with Pedro and Manuel who are escorting him through the different phases of his new identity. Juan is seen in Pedro’s car taking a deep breath before he leaves and meets his new team. In a way Juan is excited, but he is also a bit nervous. People usually have a lack of respect for him, but he does truly love helping young lives. What Juan does hate, is cranky, rich, snobby little kids. And it seems as if some of the kids on the team, might turn out that way. Juan-So you guys have talked to these kids before, right? Do you think they all seem like nice young men? Pedro- Yea, of course they are. Do you possibly think we’d want to set you up with a bunch of bratty terrors? What do you take us for? Cops. Oh wait that’s exactly what you take us for. Pedro pushes Juan onto the field, and Manuel does a sadistic laugh, as he takes his walk toward the group of children. Manuel- Have Fun! Oh and one more thing you should remember. Make sure you hold onto your wallet. Juan- (speaks under his breath) Make sure I hold onto my wallet? Why would I have to hang on to my wallet? Juan then looks around a bit and the children still don’t notice him. He looks up at a building, and in big bright letters it says “ School of Juveniles”. Juan then becomes very nervous as his hands shake and he is reminded of the horror he went through when he did Scared Straight. Juan looks back at the secret officers, who are speeding of in there car. Juan stares at them and attempts chasing the car, but they speed away, and wave goodbye to Juan, laughing at the same time. One of the children notices Juan in a distant, whistles over, and then calls his name out loud. Team player- Coach Vuergo, is that you? Juan looks over at the kid wondering who he’s talking to. Juan was about to say “No I;m Mr. Borro”, but when he looked down at the paper in his hand it said how his new name was Jose Vuergo. Juan took the paper, stuffed it in his pocket, and approached the boy with a response. Juan- Yes! I am Jose Vuergo, you guys can just call me coach. Where is the rest of the team, I’d like to meet them. Team Player- Not sure, they’re probably smoking or getting high in the back of the building. Let me introduce myself, I’m Sam, the guys around here call me Squealer, but I don’t mind, there a bunch of incompetent idiots anyway. Juan- Wow…..well you seem like a pretty good kid. Why are you in such a hell whole like this one? Sam- Well I was put here because I kept trying to escape my orphanage. They would always catch me and not know what to do with me, so they sent me here. It’s not that bad, once you learn to play the cards. Juan- I see. Well I was in an orphanage too, so I can see why you wanted to get out so badly. Why don’t we go and try to find the rest of the team? Sam- Okay. The two set off and begin looking for the rest of the team. They check all the sides of the prison for about thirty minutes, and it starts getting dark out. Sam finally finds them all and calls over to Juan. Sam- I found them coach, come over here. Juan quickly rushes over, and sees thirteen kids, smoking and gambling behind a bunch of trees. Juan quickly becomes enraged and starts screaming. Juan- What the hell do you kids think your doing? Do you want me to get one of the officers and have him eat the living shit out of all of you. Juan couldn’t believe the words coming out of his mouth, because he sounded like someone from the Shanghaied prison, but he knew it would be for the bets to show the kids who is the boss. Team Player- And who the hell do you think you are. You better get out of here, and let us be if you know what’s best for you? Juan- Who am I, who am I……… Juan was about to say how he is Juan Borro the world class soccer star, but he remembers Juan Borro is gone for now, and he can’t go around saying it. Juan- I’m Coach Vuergo. I’ll be helping your soccer team win that championship I’ve heard so much about. So if you want to be on this team, I suggest you throw out that cigarette and get the hell on the field. Team Player- I’ll get on the field, but it’s not for you it’s for the game. As for this cigarette, I can name a few places you can stick it. The other kids laugh at their teammates joke, but Juan just smirks and goes eye to eye with the juvenile. Juan- What’s you name smart ass? Team Player- It’s smart ass, you just said it. Juan- Don’t try and be funny, what’s really your name? Team Player- My name is Jimmy, but you can just refer to me as “Your worst nightmare”, because that’s what I’m going to be. Juan- All right “You’re worst nightmare” why don’t you tell us all a few places where I can stick this cigarette. Jimmy laughs and then responds. Jimmy- Are you serious? Juan- I’m serious as anything. I want you to name the place where I should stick it. What are you afraid? Jimmy- I’m not afraid of anything. You can take this cigarette and stick it up your rich Portuguese ass, for all I care. Juan- You’re not afraid of anything? That’s a laugh. You should watch what you say because in a few years you’ll be in a real prison, and I can name a few more things you can have stuck up your ass. And I don’t think you’ll like it, unless that’s your thing, I’m not sure. Jimmy- Shut up. Juan- Oh…. Big come back from a little man. Why don’t you pieces of shit get on the field so I can teach you soccer, because I doubt any of you really know how to play. Let’s go. Juan gets on the field and does a little display with the soccer ball, doing all types of moves, and other things. Jimmy watches as the other kids look on in awe at Juan’s dribbling and ball skills. He quickly becomes disgusted and lets it be heard. Jimmy- Wow…he can dribble a soccer ball, big deal. It’s not like he’s a pro soccer player or anything, he has to resort to teaching a bunch of kids, what a loser? Juan laughs at the stupidity of the kid speaking. He then grabs the ball and throws it at Jimmy’s feet. Juan- Why don’t we see what you can do. Jimmy starts dribbling the ball, and drops it every few times. He then tries a move, but trips over the ball. Everyone begins to laugh at him, as Juan comes over, grabs the ball and does the move with complete ease. Juan- You’re real good! Juan says sarcastically, but the kid doesn’t find it funny. And continues along the practice pissed off and displeased. Juan- For today, I’m going to be nice and let you guys play around. If I ever see you smoking or doing crap like that again, I’m out of here and there will be no soccer season. It’s as easy as that, so you guys better watch what you do. The kids listen to Juan and kind of believe what he’s saying, Juan grabs the ball throws it in the air, and whistles for the game to begin. He then notices the secret officers car up ahead and goes running up to it, leaving the practice. They open the door and speak to Juan. Pedro- How did the practice with the Juveniles go? Has your hair fallen out yet, or is that saved for practice number two. Juan- It went fine for your information. I’m a pretty powerful guy when I can be one, so why don’t you just shut up and drive me to the next phase. Manuel- Wow! Someone has a little attitude I see. Juan- Just Drive. The men drive off, as the next phase they will be going to is Juan’s new house, and living arrangements. Scene Fades to Black. TO BE CONTINUED……………………
