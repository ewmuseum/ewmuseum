Get Out of MY City.

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/07/02 There are very few things in the World that Xavier Kannon cares about... First and foremost there is himself, then you have his wife, Ellie~!... Possibly the only women who can even get near him as far as pure spite goes.... Then there is Scientology, his faith and the religion he tries to spread everytime he is on TV, then there comes money and finally, Hubbardville... The purpose built city in Wisconsin solely for Scientologists who want to escape the oppression of the outside world. His passion for this small but superior city was well showcased when PCW President Duke Williams subscribed to Scientology in an attempt to get under Kannon’s skin... It worked. Protest after protest was held Outside Duke’s business and Xavier went to any length to drive Duke from the city he loves so much. Kannon may have hated Duke, but at least the PCW President has money.... It’s when someone without that invades Hubbardville’s city limits that we really see Kannon enraged... And with only 4 strikes left for the rest of his life, we see the Gold Patron Meritorious try a new approach for him... .... Diplomacy. “DAMMIT! GET OUT OF MY CITY OR I’LL HAVE YOU KILLED!!!!” With the bellowing voice of Xavier Kannon heard, we fade into the fine city of Hubbardville, or rather a small alley just off Wehatenicolekidman Avenue, which contains the dumpsters used by the Hubbard Burger franchise . In this space we see the irate Scientologist bellowing at what appears to be a large pile of Black refuse sacks beside a large Green dumpster.... His loud rantings are attracting the attention of several passers by who stop at the entrance to the alley where Kannon’s wife Ellie~! stands.. Watching her husband with inquisitive eyes. Xavier : “Okay... I’ll give you a count of three... That fair?” The Gold Patron Meritorious turns to face the opposite wall and covers his eyes as if playing hide and seek. Xavier : “1..................2...................3.....Hey, that sounded like one of my matches against the Pharaoh!” At the entrance to the alley, Eleanor nods in agreement at her man’s statement and turns to all the people around her with pride. Eleanor : “Against....... The........ Pharaoh!” Kannon removes his hands form his eyes and turns around only for his then calm face to contort with rage once more as stamps his feet on the concrete in frustration. Xavier : “What... Why... WHY ARE YOU STILL HERE?!?!?!?!??!?” His bright Green eyes are wide open and his head gestures towards the pile of Black sacks, a vein throbbing on his forehead becoming more noticeable on his Red face. “I like it here...” A gruff, mumbling voice is heard from the sacks and as the camera moves a little closer to Kannon it is seen that beside the dumpster, huddled under a pile of garbage sacks sits an old, dirty looking man with a tatty tartan blanket draped over his legs. “What is he shouting at?” The fellow Hubbardville residents that have gathered on Wehatenicolekidman Avenue speculate as to what and why Kannon is yelling, Ellie forming her own idea in her Preppy little mind as to why her husband is so irate. Eleanor : “I hope it’s Oscar the Grouch.... He was like, all Green and fluffy and lived in a bin right?” The Scientologists look to the Prep with disbelief for a few seconds as she wonders what she said before they get back to their speculation. “I heard there was a homeless guy living around here...” “Maybe he’s finally kicking Ribisi out?” “Oscar the Grouch? Could be him right?” Ellie~!’s hopefully eyes are shown before we cut back to Kannon and the homeless man... he Scientologist now pacing around wondering what he has to do. Xavier : “Okay... I am not an unreasonable man so I’m sure we can work this out without causing a scene.... Or letting any outsiders know we had a disease ridden, waste of space, lice infested, dirty, smelly, urine soaked walking bag of scum like you in our fine city.... No offence...” Bum: None taken....” The Scientologist pulls his wallet out from the pocket of his Black leather trousers and opens it up, pulling out a handful of notes which he holds out... The homeless man craning his neck to get a look of the currency in Xavier’s hand from his little hole in the garbage. Xavier : “So here... $654 for you to leave.. What do you say?” “Can’t be Oscar.. Oscar needs no money... because he lives on, like, Sesame Street...” Still the Scientologists look at Ellie~! with bizarre expressions before we cut back to the homeless man’s face, he is now shaking his head. Bum : “Nope...... Don’t need no money...” Kannon’s eyes roll back into his head as he starts to pace back and forth in front of the homeless man who quite simply doesn’t see the fuss in his own little world where he is quite content. The Scientologist stops pacing and takes a deep breath before holding a finger up. Xavier : “Okay... ONE, that was a DOUBLE NEGATIVE!” He then holds a second finger up. Xavier : “TWO, of course you need money, say to BUY A FUCKING HOUSE SO YOU AREN’T SLEEPING IN AN ALLEY IN MY CITY!!!!” Despite the rantings of the temperamental Gold Patron Meritorious the bum looks quite content in his little spot, bordered on one side by the dumpster and barricaded in with full refuse sacks. Bum : “All I need is my blanket..” He looks down to the dirty, tatty, hole ridden Red, Blue and Green tartan blanked he has over his lap with fond eyes... Reminiscent of Linus from Peanuts and his security blanket. With these words an evil smirk crosses Xavier’s face as he takes 2 wary steps forwards, trying to disguise his intent by apparent defeat. Xavier : “Okay, okay.. You win.... I see you are a man who sticks to his guns...” Kannon is now just a few feet away from the smelly old man and the pong is already getting to him as he is evidently breathing through his mouth. His hands are held up as if in surrender, but there is a sparkle on his bright Green eyes that gives something away. Xavier : “BUT WHAT IF I STEAL YOUR BLANKET!?!?!?!?” He quickly lungs forwards and grabs the corner of the man’s blanket, trying to pull it away quickly but the homeless man holds onto it tightly and as Kannon tries to rip it away the old man’s teeth sink into the Scientologist’s bare forearm. Xavier : “OOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!!!” Kannon’s quickly recoils and holds his arm, staring a it with fear filled wide eyes, locking onto the imprint of the man’s rotten teeth in his flesh. Xavier : “ARGHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!” At the entrance to the alley the crowd of Scientologists look on in shock, concerned at what they are seeing. Eleanor : “Do Grouches, like, bite?” Cutting back to the alley, Kannon is still staring at his arm in disbelief as the bum mutters to himself, hugging his blanket tightly. Bum : “Nobody takes MY blanket...” The Gold patron Meritorious looks at him with disbelief in his eyes. Xavier : “YOU BIT ME!??!!??!?!!? YOU ACTUALLY BIT ME?!?!?!?!?” His eyes are now darting around in all directions as he holds his arm, not knowing what to do. Xavier : “MY HUBBARD!!! I can feel your diseases swimming into my Scientological blood as I speak.... Oh Zenu, I COULD HAVE RABIES... I don’t want to be known as the RABID SCIENTOLOGIST.... WHY DID YOU BITE ME?!?!?” The homeless man just mumbles t himself as he pulls his refuse sacks closer around him to built a fort. Bum : “Tried to take my blanket...” By this point, the temperamental Scientologist is having a full blown panic attack.... He is close to hyper ventilating and his eyes are not blinking as he Green eyes still stare at the bite mark. Xavier : “Oh no... I’m breaking out in a cold sweat, your disease filled saliva is eating away at my Scientological flesh.... SOMEONE GET ME SOME BUM ANTI-VENOM!!!!” The 15 or so people at the entrance of the alley are still looking on bemused as Kannon starts to stumble around, close to collapsing and his voice is heard yelling out. Xavier : “IF I DIE I AM MAKING SURE THAT GOES DOWN AS A STRIKE FOR YOU!!!!” Eleanor : “You, like, SO can’t give Oscar the Grouch a strike!” Our shot starts to fade out on the other Scientologists shaking their heads at the prep as Kannon, running on pure adrenaline, sprints off in the direction of Hubbardville’s L Ron Hubbard Memorial Hospital and the bystanders walk off with the exception of Ellie~! who’s pale Blue eyes are looking thoroughly bemused as we hit Black. ”Maybe it wasn’t Oscar... Could have just been a gross disgusting homeless man... Like, Ewwwwwwwwwzies!”
