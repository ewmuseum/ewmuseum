Battlefield PCW....Part 4.

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-15-2002 'The battle had been like nothing I had ever seen thus far. The enemy seemed to have a limitless amount of troops in reserve. Each time we would decimate one wave, or beat back another, ANOTHER wave would assail us. 'Twas not what I had expected. If the first attack was any indication, we were the better trained army, but numbers can play a part. Ahh yesterday...' The scene opens to show a group of perhaps fifty mounted knights riding hard though and open plain. At their head rides Venneman Pendragon, golden armor gleaming in the early morning sunlight. He holds his sword in his right hand as he reins the horse with his left. He seems completely in his element. Riding at the lead of his own heavy cavalry. The men behind ride hard for their king, though none are truly sure what is in store for them. The formation tops a rise and comes face to face with a rather large enemy infantry contingent. Pendragon smiles as he lowers his head and heels Xerxes into the fray. His men do not hesitate as they follow. Faster and already armed, the cavalry chews through the infantry with a ferocity that has never before been seen in the land of Gauze. Soon, bloodied bodies litter the tranquil plain and Pendragon turns his horse and leads his men from the heart of the enemy. Said enemy has found their wits and now are a tad more prepared as they give chase to the faster cavalry. Pendragon looks back and smiles as he leads the enemy back to the trap. The horses race over the plain and into the 'U'. Pendragon stops and watches as each horseman scampers up the ridge and into safety before he himself seeks the safe side of the trap. The enemy infantrymen rush full force into the valley and soon many of them are pin-cushioned with arrows. in confusion, the ranks break and men run in all directions. Some try to backtrack but they are met with a ferocious cavalry charge that cuts all in its path; Richard Smith had done his duty. Others try to climb the ridge, only to find that with this choice death cam via a sharpened pike. The rest are slaughtered by arrows from the dead on archers. Finally, every enemy soldier lies dead upon the field. The men take up a cheer as Pendragon rides into the valley, his sword held high.... Men: HURRAY! LONG LIVE THE KING!! HAIL THE KING!!! Venneman looks at his men, and his fondness is evident. He looks at the men who litter the field in disdain and begins to speak to his men in a confident voice... Venneman: My sons! Behold! Today was a great victory! Over superior numbers we have triumphed, and we have taken no casualties. From the distance, a loud rumble can be heard. Pendragon turns around and sees a huge contingency of cavalry and infantry bearing down upon his position.... Venneman: Good Christ.... He races his horse up the ridge and leads the mainstay of his cavalry to the east as the army draws near. Soon, the enemy cavalry is in the midst of the U and arrows fly. Some fall, others make for the ridge. Some are smitten by the lance, but their ranks hold as they are drawn from their horses and into hand to hand combat with Pendragon's infantry. From the east Pendragon now comes rushing in with his cavalry for an attack to the rear. Charging hard, his sword held high, his unit is suddenly attacked from below! Venneman: What treachery is this? The horses all rear in fear as a camouflaged unit seems to rise up from the very ground. At it’s head stands none other than Lyco the Joblin. He smiles menacingly as he and his men advance quickly upon Pendragon’s men, carrying long pikes themselves. Some men are thrown to the ground, others ride around in confusion. Pendragon leaps from his horse and takes out three men, err, Joblins with a mighty swing of his sword as he lands lightly on his feet. A look to the west shows Pendragon’s small contingency of men being overpowered by sheer numbers. Venneman: Good god…What have I led the lads in to? Lyco: Their doom, of course….Did you think you had seen the last of me, Crusader? Venneman: Actually, I had planned upon it! With that, Pendragon races into close quarters with Lyco, his blade a twirling blur. It is everything Lyco can do to keep the blade from taking off his head. Lyco: Wait, wait WAIT!!! Pendragon stops suddenly, his sword and inch from the Joblin’s throat…. Lyco: If you come with me to the bosses, I will spare your paltry forces certain death…. Venneman: You would spare their lives for mine? Lyco: Of COURSE!! You are the threat to the powers here, not them. It is you who rally the land to the cause of the good…. Venneman: Of the righteous…. With a look to his troops, Pendragon lowers his sword, places it in its sheath, and raises his arms. Lyco: GOOD! The Lord of Flame, The Wench of Thomas and the Mad Celt will be overjoyed to see you….. “Though I had planned for this, I had not planned thoroughly enough it would seem. Lyco surprised me and now had to face three who represented the scourge of the land…I cared not what foul intentions they had for me, I would rise above. It is my nature to over come. Mine own enemies seemed to be growing daily, however. One day I would get to the cancer, and like the surgeon with the bone saw, I would cut it from the breast of the Land. In so doing, Gauze, AND PCW would be free from the oppressors who would dictate to them how to live….How to be. And if somehow my King’s Gold made it back to me, then that would make my victory all the more sweet. Here, I deal with four crazy villains….In PCW, I deal with Syko, Aymin, Tomas the Wench and a face new to me, Jamie Celt. I cared not who I faced, only that I would be able to wreak havoc among those who were un-pure...Those touched by the taint of Kannon….” Lyco rubs his hands together as his men bind Pendragon’s arms behind his back. Pendragon snarls as Excalibur is stripped from his waist and tossed to the Joblin… Lyco: If you are lucky, you may die quickly by your own blade…. Venneman: Keep thy dirty hands from mine blade, vermin! I shall come for it, and with it, I shall smite thee with all my vengeance. Lyco merely laughs and with a wave of his hand, his men drag Pendragon in the direction of the enemy camp. ***FLASH*** The shot switches to show the “U” next, and the enemy retreats from that death trap as well. Pendragon’s men look at each other, each man’s face asking the same question…”Huh?” Richard strides from the ridge into the clearing and sees the colossal form of Pendragon being led away from the battle ground in shackles… Richard: It cannot be! He would give himself so that we might carry on! SOLDIERS!! RALLY TO ME!!! THE KING IS TAKEN!! Instantly, each man runs to the clearing, and into formation. Eager, the men listen as Richard lays out the situation….Meanwhile… ***FLASH*** A bloodied Venneman Pendragon is kicked along by different men on horseback as the army approaches a fortified mountain. The gates open wide and the army enters amidst the cheers from those inside. As Pendragon comes into their view, the cheers are cut off by awed silence. He is led past the staring multitudes of both commoners and soldiers, and his visage becomes one of ferocious insanity… Venneman: What then, ye vile dogs! Think not that ye have collared this beast. My bite is neither stopped, nor shall thou be protected from my wrath and vengeance. A boot to the head draws a deadly glance from Pendragon but the horsed fool pays him no heed, just stares defiantly into the eyes of insanity…. Venneman: Ye shall de for that transgression, worm. I will personally see to it. The man finally turns from Pendragon’s gaze, a bit of fear evident upon his face. Pendragon stops in the middle of the road and looks around at his captors, his tormentors. Suddenly, he laughs. In the midst of the enemy, he laughs. The people shudder at the sound and turn away, mothers hiding the eyes of their small children. His eyes burn with both insanity and rage, and finally, the people can take it no more. A large stone hurtles through the air and strikes Pendragon in the head, dropping him to the ground in a heap. Four men pick him up and carry him off, much to the crowds pleasure… >>>TO BE CONTINUED
