Searching For Statuz Quo

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.12.02 The scene opens in a large recording studio in uptown New York City. The room, is not large. Only about 15 square foot, and then about a 6 square foot recording booth. On the far left of the studio is a large window. From the window you can see a lot. You can see the projects of Queens to the far left, and amazingly to the far right you can see the lights of Broadway. It’s hard to believe how close the hard life, the poverty of the hood is to the fame and fortune of Broadway. Duzza owns this recording studio, he owns his own record company, which is a smaller branch of the Arista Corporation known as ‘Into The Street Records’ On the right side of the room, stands all the recording equipment. It is there that three guys are sitting, one of them is Duzza. The other two are fiddling with different buttons, while another guy is in the booth. Duzza is dressed in a pair of very baggy black leather pants, a white sleeveless T-shirt and a black jumper tied around his waist. He is sitting on a big black swirly leather chair and talking to the rapper inside of the booth Duzza:“ Yo, dawg, cut it from the second verse, one mo’ time” At this time Duzza’s cell phone, a Nokia 3330, starts ringing, with the ring tone as ‘Hard Knock Life’. Duzza looks at the phone, and realizes it’s the wife. His half Jamaican half Asian queen, the only woman who has stuck by him during all the court cases, shoot outs, jobbing and thug activites. He tells them to turn down the beats and answers Duzza:“ Hey babe, how you doin’?” Brittany:“ Don’t you ‘hey babe’ me” Duzza:“ What? What I done wrong now baby girl?” Brittany:“ Where was you at 12?” Duzza:“ Err…at the studio…where was I supposed to be?” Brittany:“ Eating lunch with me, downtown…” Duzza:“ Oh damn, I knew I was supposed to be somewhere, err sorry Britt” Brittany:“ Yeah, you do that again and you’ll be sleeping in the spare room, ya hear?” Duzza:“ Yes, I hear you. Sorry” Brittany:“ Now, about tonight, I want…” Duzza (interrupting):“ Sorry baby girl, I got someone else on the other line, yo hold on” Brittany:“ But but…” Duzza:“ Yeah yeah, who this?” The voice on the other end, is quiet, and also sounds quite freaky“ Duzza, Duzza, Duzza, it’s your dawg…” Duzza:“ Err…hi…what’s your name again, I gots a bad memory” Voice:“ Duzza, it’s me… lil Thug” Duzza:“ Dawg!! Shit, sorry dawg, didn’t recognize you over tha phone man, damn you sound diff’rent, how can I help you man?” Statuz Quo:“ We need to meet” Duzza:“ A’ight dawg, I can do that, what ya want to meet?” Statuz Quo:“ Now” Duzza:“ Now?! Damn dawg, I’m in the middle of somethin’, how ‘bout later?” Statuz Quo:“ We need to speak now Duzza, it’s important” Duzza:“ Aww man. Ok, well I’m in NYC dawg, where you at?” Statuz Quo:“ 52nd street” Duzza:“ Man, 52nd street?! That street goes on for like..ever man, you got a address or something?” Statuz Quo:“ Duzz, you’ll see me, don’t worry” Duzza:“ A’ight dawg, I’ma leave now” Duzza hangs up the phone, completely forgetting about his wife and explains to his people that he’s gotta go. They decide that they’ll stay on. Duzza puts his phone in his pocket, puts on a baggy leather FUBU jacket on and leaves the studio. He walks out onto the street. The street is pretty busy, not with cars, but with people. People are walking up and down the street, people of every race, black, white, Asian, Latino, Hispanic, many. Duzza puts on a pair of sunglasses and a black wooly hat, to minimize the chance of him being recognized. The day has turned cold, the wind is strong, Duzza quickly zips up his coat and walks over to his car. He has a 2001 BMW 4x4. It is painted black, with tinted windows in the back. The car is the size of a average jeep, but looks much more streamline. Duzza gets into the car and starts the engine, he puts on the CD player, which is playing R. Kelly’s ‘TP-2.com’ album. He pulls away and heads for 52ND street. It does not take long for him to get there, no more than 15 minutes. He rolls down the windows on the both his side and the passenger side of the car. He drives slowly down 52nd street, looking for Statuz Quo. End Of Part 1
