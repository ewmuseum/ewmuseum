Sandy Wong and The Legends

Date: Present “Did you remember, Akiyama? I hope you didn’t forget about me...because I certainly haven’t forgotten about you. None of us have...and we’re all gunning for you...because you’re a very wanted man. You may think you can succeed, but you’re wrong. The time has finally come for you where you shall be just another notch of defeat on someone’s belt...just another statistic in the records. You should remember now that Legends never give up...” “Legends never die...” One would think that Akira Akiyama had finally ditched all the madness that inhabited his life once he was exiled from Japan. But that was soon to be a false theory by the end of this day. Akiyama woke up that morning with his head banging like a drum. Turns out the real banging was coming from the door, and it only looked to intensify his headache. He kicked the sheets off of him and staggered over to the door to answer it. Opening it, he found PCW’s lead reporter, Sandy Wong. She was dressed in a yellow V-Neck halter-top along with tight blue jeans and white and yellow sneakers. Akiyama scratched the back of his head groggily and groaned. “Aw...what do you want, Wong? We have a GODLY headache after last night’s Celestial meeting.” ~~~The scene shifted to the night before, where Jay Sylo, Pharaoh, Carmoon, Isiy, Yuu, and Akiyama sat around a round card table. Each of them held a single card in their hand, and in the center of the table was a tall familiar frosted bottle with Japanese writing on the label and a clear red liquid inside. Next to it was a small shot glass. Each of them stared at each other warily before they started to toss their cards down on the table. “King.” Pharaoh declared. “Just so fitting for me, is it not?” Isiy nodded with a smile. “Queen...how ironic!” “Jack” Carmoon announced. “How about you Sylo?” He tossed his card out on the table silently, revealing it to be an ace. The Egyptian trio all groaned, and Yuu stared out at the cards with a sad expression. “Gee...looks like I lose. All I have is this two of spades.” she placed it down, and everyone stared at her with a dumbfounded expression. “Yuu,” Pharaoh began. “Deuces are wild in this game.” “I thought deuces were losers?” she protested. Jay shrugged his shoulders with a slight smirk. “Only in PCW, sweetheart.” the entire table exploded in laughter. Yuu slowly caught on and started to laugh with them. Then they all noticed someone didn’t join them in their joke. They all looked to the far end of the table, where Akiyama stared down at his card. “Aki...it’s you’re turn.” Yuu told him. He looked up with a weak smile and nodded. “Um...sure..we have an ace too.” “Akiyama...” Pharaoh began. “We can see the vein throbbing on your temple. We know you’re lying.” Akiyama reached for his head and felt the vein protruding from underneath his skin. “D@mn!” he reluctantly reached for the bottle and began pouring shots. ~~~ “You think we could get a little rest because of our bad luck!” he griped. Sandy Wong just stared hard at him, her dark eyes almost bulging out from their sockets. The color rose to her cheeks as she spoke. “Um...Akira?” “Yes, what is it, Sandy?” “Are you aware that you’re standing there naked?” A sudden wave of alertness swept over him. He looked down and saw that she spoke the truth...he was pretty darn nude. He stared back up at her, finding her eyes tracing over each and every muscle along his frame. In some sense of regaining his dignity, he placed his hands on his hips and nodded. “Well...of course we knew that.” he said. “Do you think we would have done so otherwise unconsciously?” “Oh...of course not.” she muttered, her eyes locked in on something down below. Akiyama snapped his fingers, and brought her attention back up to his face. “Hey...you focus on the NVG...” he pointed to his face... “...Not the NVP” ...before pointing down below to his crotch. Her eyes followed his hand, but Akiyama snapped again, and caught her attention. Then suddenly, he heard voices coming from around the corner. He grabbed Wong quickly and propped her right up in front of him, so that she was blocking all vision of his pelvic area. From around the corner came Pharaoh along with Sylo, Isiy, and Carmoon. The Egyptian trio was decked out in their usual golden attire, while Jay Sylo walked around shirtless in his white and blue pants and boots. They all stopped when they found Akiyama and Sandy standing there staring in their direction. “Akiyama...” Pharaoh greeted. “...and Sandy Wong?” “Um...” the NVG stammered. “Well...” Time for some quick thinking. “I came here to get an interview from Akiyama before March Madness. I’m interviewing all the participants in the brawl.” They all nodded, understanding it all. Pharaoh and his entourage walked on, but Sylo stopped for a second. “Put some underwear on.” he muttered deeply. “And make it brief.” “Ha, ha, ha!” Akiyama replied bitterly as Jay snickered down the hallway. “You’re lucky we don’t banish you for that little joke!!” Suddenly Sandy wrapped her arms around the back of Akiyama’s neck. “From what I’m feeling, either there’s a microphone in my back pocket, or there certainly isn’t anything LITTLE about it.” “Well...” Aki grinned, slipping his arms around her waist. “Let’s just go and have a mike check.” he pulled her inside his room and slammed it shut behind him. Later... “Sorry.” “Bah...” “I’m really sorry.” “We repeat: BAH!” “I forgot all about it!” The scene showed Akiyama and Sandy Wong standing in the elevator alone, side-by-side. The NVG was dressed in his usual attire of all black vinyl, PVC, leather, and tape, with his black and silver hair tied up in a ponytail. A frown of disappointment was on his face as Sandy pleaded with him... a microphone in her hands. “I forgot I had that in my pocket! I must have slipped it in before I left the studio!” she explained. “Can we go back upstairs?” “No.” he replied hurt. “You get NONE...of the NVP.” “Aw!” she stomped her foot in a small tantrum. “We do not need any of this nonsense coming up. With this big main event coming, we have to work extra hard to catch up with the likes of Miyagawa, Legend Inc, Juan Borro, and Darkstar. We’re a little behind.” he peered back behind Sandy and shook his head. “And we would say you know about being little behind.” She glared up at the god, her dark eyes piercing blades into his face. “I wish I could say the same for you about being extra hard.” Akiyama turned to face her with an annoyed look on his face. “YOU DARE TO BELITTLE THE NVP???” Wong wasn’t one to back off from a fight. “You’re the only one who can belittle the NVP!!” On the ground level, hotel patrons flocked to the door of the elevator, awaiting it’s arrival. Just as the numbers beeped to arrival, the doors slid open and the NVG and Wong came tumbling out onto the ground. Sandy’s legs were wrapped around him like a snake, and the both of them rolled about on the carpet in a fury of passion. They stopped in mid grope, and stood to their feet, straightening out their clothes and smiling sheepishly. “Nothing to see here, folks.” Sandy announced embarrassed. “Just your Neo Void God communing with you mortals, so that we can know how to serve you better.” Akiyama turned around to find a insanely obese woman eying him sensually. “I’d like to be served!” she blubbered. Aki took a few steps back and grimaced. “Sorry...we’re a god...not a buffet table.” he dashed out of the hotel lobby with Sandy Wong following him all the way. Akiyama and Sandy Wong were shown walking along the city streets that morning. Amidst the other people walking along trying to get here and there, they didn’t seem to blend in. Well...Akiyama didn’t seem to blend in given his vinyl trench coat and such. He walked with his arms folded over his chest, while Sandy kept her hands in her pockets. “Well, wasn’t that embarrassing?” Akiyama asked. “Mmm-hmmm.” she moaned. “Anyway, we guess there are some things that we should explain to you.” “Mmm-hmmm.” “First off, the first order of business for the NVG to take care of in this March Madness event is to handle the loose ends like Chef, Juan Borro, El Diablo, and Flaymin Aymin. We wouldn’t want these International Imbeciles trying to seek out redemption for practically dooming their alliance to failure because of Pharaoh and us leaving.” “Mmm-hmmm.” “Then from there we look to take care of the jobbers we personally do not hold grudges against. That includes Xavier “Crapple” Kannon, In-Breed, Bobby Stormbringer, Chet King, Kano, and Ty Nelson. And then that’s when the real work begins.” “Mmm-hmmm.” Akiyama folded his hands and placed them behind his head. “We mean, Miyagawa’s taken a beating from us before, but Darkstar, Boda, The End, The Rush, and Legend Inc...they’re virgins towards the onslaught of violence that we bring to the table. But unlike virgins, we’re not planning on being gentle with them. By no means...we’re going to try and be as rough with them as possible!” “Mmm-hmmm.” Akiyama stopped and stared at her. “What are you doing?” She looked at him confused. “Huh?” He looked down at her hands and sighed. “Get your hands out of your pockets while we’re talking to you.” “Why?” she protested. “What am I doing?” “You know what you’re doing!” he growled annoyed. “You got your hands down there playing Pocket Poker!!”She glared shocked at him, drawing her hands out of her pockets. “How dare you!!” “How dare us?? How dare YOU to do that in public, let alone while we’re talking to you!!” he retorted. “All we’re trying to do is have a nice conversation with you about the March Madness, and you’re over there pressing your button!” Sandy placed her hands on her hips, looking jauntily at Aki. “At least I know where the button is.” “Quit squawking, Wong.” he muttered. Just as he was about to shoot out another sexual insult, a girl came running up to him. She looked to be about thirteen, and was dressed in a pair of dark blue hip-hugger jeans, blue sneakers, and a white short-sleeved tee shirt. Her hair was about shoulder length, and brown with streaks of red in it. “Oh snap!!” she exclaimed. “You’re Akiyama, aren’t you??” “Yeah, that’s us.” his arms stretched out with his fingers ready to point out the next phrase. “Aki-frickin-yama.” “Oh snap! I can’t believe this! You’re like the coolest guy in PCW!” she said in a marked-out voice. “Can I have your autograph?” Akiyama shrugged. “Sure thing, kid.” he took a pen and a slip of paper from her. Then he scribbled a bit on the paper. “Hey...your pen’s not working.” “That’s not the only thing we have to get working again,” she mumbled softly. “If you smell what the Wong is cooking.” Akiyama stared slowly up at her. “We wouldn’t be able to smell what you’re cooking if you closed your legs once in a while.” Her glare came back onto her face. “Wh-wh-why do you have to say things like that in front of people?” “Because you started it.” the NVG handed the girl her paperback and shook his head. “What’s your name, kid?” “Kat.” “Well Kat, you’re going to be at the Madness, right?” “Right.” “Well, you catch me before the battle royal, and we’ll make sure you get an autograph. Is that cool with you?” She gave Akiyama a thumbs up. “Disco! Later!!” she scampered off down the street. Akiyama smiled at Wong. “You hear that? She said it was disco! That chica’s destined to be enlightened when she grows a bit older.” “What’s stopping you now?” “There’s a line drawn between DA GOD, and DA MAN...and we’re no where near crossing that line yet.” he stepped ahead of her and continued walking. She exhaled deeply and followed. “Well anyway, what’s the use of you even trying to win the World title?” she began. “Look at the competition lined up for this battle. Snow, Legend Inc, Darkstar, Boda, Miyagawa...the list goes on and on.” The god shook his head. “Listen...we’ve been trying not to think about the belt at the end of this match. There’s something I’ve learned about championships in PCW.” “And that would be?” “You get the ones you never wanted and you don’t get the ones you stress out on. Sure, determination is good, but if you’re going around with a hard on whenever you see a gold belt buckle like half of these guys are doing, then you get really pissed off in the end when you don’t win that bastard.” Sandy chuckled a bit, coming to some conclusion about the topic. “So you’re saying you’re not worried about the belt?” “Not in the least.” Aki slit across his throat with his finger. “We’re more worried about clearing the ring of most of that riff-raff so that the real children of battle can shine.” Cue yet another interruption. “Hey mack, you wanna dog?” Akiyama and Wong turned to their left, where a man in a white apron, black jeans, and a white tank top stood behind his hot dog cart. His hair was tied back in a short black ponytail, and his face was slightly masked by the hat he wore low. “Sure...I could go for one.” Sandy complied. Akiyama looked at her before turning to the vendor. “Fine. Let’s get one dog. You have anything to drink there?” The man shook his head as he fixed her hot dog. “Only Snapple.” Akiyama’s face twisted in disgust. “Snapple? Freak, man, don’t you know that stuff will make you more impotent than Snow before his morning Viagra shots?” Sandy stared at him strangely. “Where the heck did you hear that?” “Heard it from Timo before he jumped ship.” he grinned. “Anyway, we’d rather drink your @ss sweat than Snapple, Wong.” “Akiyama!!” “Bah, it’s the frickin truth!!” he took her hot dog and handed him a few bills before walking off. He passed Sandy her dog and moved on with their conversation. “ Have you looked at the names listed in this Brawl?” “Sure.” she mumbled through chewing. “Lot’s of big names.” “Be a lady, Wong. Don’t speak with your mouth open.” she threw him a glare before he replied. “ No, we’re not talking about the names, we’re talking about the NAMES!” She shot him another look, this one more confused than menacing. Akiyama shook his head, signaling an explanation. “You see, you have The Rush, The End, The Chef, The One who Rocks, The Boss, The Messiah~” “That’s about it, isn’t it?” Now Akiyama threw her a glare. “Can we finish?” he asked. “The Snow Queen, The Chat King, The Aussie, The Mourning Star, The Mexican Devil, The Goggled Freak, The Controversial One, The Golden Toe with lead for brains, the B!tch Bad Boda Daddy, The Crapple King, and The Legend.” they stopped when they found their path blocked by construction work. A detour sign pointed them to their left into an alley. “With all those THEs in the line up, where can the NVG fit in?” “There isn’t just THE Legend!!” someone yelled out. Akiyama and Sandy looked around surprised. “There’s THE LEGENDS!!” With a flash of speed, someone ran through them and knocked Sandy on the ground. The NVG looked ahead of them to find a familiar man in black corduroys and leather loafers and a Hawaiian shirt. “You look familiar, boyo.” he muttered through his teeth. “What’s your name?” The man looked at him surprised. “What’s MY name? What’s MY name??” laughter rang out all over the alley, indicating more voices and other people. “You should know my face very well! I’m the one that allowed you to gain back your strength so that you could go on and defeat Chet King for his Continental belt!!” Akiyama didn’t like him already. “We never fought that match, you bastard!! Now you’ve angered the Neo Void God!!” “I am Jubei Hasegawa! And we...are The Legends!!” that last phase acted like a signal, for when Akiyama looked up, he saw someone crash into him from above with a senton splash. He crashed into the ground, rolling around to catch a view of his attacker. It was the girl from before...Kat. She turned back with a grin and kicked him straight in his face. This chick had strength...more than she looked for a twelve year old. It was almost superhuman of her to possess that power. Akiyama rolled over onto his back, looking up to find someone else quite familiar.was the vendor guy from before... He held his hot dog cart high above his head along with the help of another. She was a black haired woman in a green sports bra and black sweats. Together, the two dropped the cart right above Akiyama’s body. It was his time to strike. He rolled backwards over his head and to his feet, barely escaping the crushing metal stand. Using his peripheral vision, he could tell there was four of them, two on his left, one near his front right, and one on the far right. Sandy was crouched behind him watching the entire event. The god thought hard about what was about to happen. Either an ambush or one at a time... and given how things looked, one at a time wasn’t going to cut it. Time for the NVG to practice for the Madness. He lunged out and grabbed the girl Kat by her throat. She tried to scramble, but his lock was too tight on her. Then as the other three moved in, he swung the girl like a club at them, keeping them at bay. The one called Jubei came in too close, and Akiyama tossed the girl right into his face before he walked towards the last two. Both of them looked ripped and toned from combat. They must have been professionals in order to concoct a plan like this. The vendor moved in first, followed by the woman. With fists flying they pounced upon Akiyama, striking and jabbing with straight accuracy. Akiyama decided he was through with these fools for the time being. He roared up to his feet and grabbed both of them by their throats. Then he picked them up and crashed them into the wall before turning to their other approaching allies and tossing them at them. They all crashed into each other on the ground, piled and tangled in between each other. They all regained their bearings and turned back to Akiyama. The Neo Void God was hunched over with his arms dropped by his sides. Then he threw his arms out in a huge roar, and behind him it looked as if everything behind him was rippling like waves on a pond. A huge black opening appeared behind him, with lightning crackling around him. Jubei Hasegawa stared in shock at the event; it must have been his first time seeing the Void in person. “Legends!! Fall back!!” they all backed away quickly down the other end of the alley. “We’ll be back, Akiyama!! Don’t think you can hide from us!!” The NVG’s rage subsided, and he drifted back into a more civilized tone. Sandy Wong stared dead at him with her two dark orbs hollow and unbelieving. Akiyama gazed down at her, his almond eyes glazed from the sudden anger burst. “Legends?” he huffed in disbelief. “If that’s what we’re to expect from legends...then you should get ready to see a legendary @sskicking dished from your Neo Void God...WITH STYLE!” “Er...I have to go...do stuff.” she whimpered. Then with a stutter step, she raced off down the alley. Akiyama shrugged as he watched her leave. “Heh...maybe she just can’t handle the NVP like she thought she could.” And with that said... Akira Akiyama cracked the joints in his neck and walked off towards the sidewalk again. ~~~To Be Continued~~~
