Last Stand In Open Country #5

26 February 2003

Title: Last Stand In Open Country #5 Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 02/26/03 There are three things that you don’t mess with in Duke’s life and they are Whiskey, family, and America. A true patriot the Malice Man is as pure, plumb, perfect when it comes to saluting that ragged old flag. Although he’s the first to admit America is by no means perfect she’s still far above the rest and there is no alternative. It’s the Green Frog Café in Missoula, Montana and sitting around the bar are some of America’s proudest assembly of men. In the black hat and “give war a chance” t-shirt sits Duke Williams. Next to him in a VFW hat is Jim Miller a man who served his country for over thirty years. The bartender Willie servers up the drinks as Johnny Paycheck play’s in the background from the old Wurlitzer jukebox. “You see that there rock musician spew his liberal propaganda on that awards show?” Rambles Jim in a slow southern accent. The Malice Man looks at Willie as he downs a glass of Jim Beam whiskey. Nothing gets the Green Frog Café more fired up then a bunch of simple-minded liberals or hippies as there know as in the shadows of Ajax mountain in God’s country. “Nothing’s worse then a greasy rock singer talking politics. Here’s an idea, shut the fuck up and sing. That’s what they’re paid to do. But the only thing worse then a rock star flapping his fucking jaw like an old lady in a sewing circle is a wrestler shooting him mouth off.” Retorts the Malice Man with a fire in his voice. Jungle Jim Miller has a slight smirk on his face, as he down’s some Blue Ribbon beer. Willie refills Duke’s glass and waits for some more round table commentary. “The only wrestler who has a right to say bull is you Duke. I mean you know what the hell your talking about while the rest of those mental midgets ain’t got a clue. I heard one of them shiny faced fuckers yapping a bunch of commie talk on your TV show.” Willie says as he chews down some pickled egg. Duke smiles at the complement as he reaches over and grabs an egg. “You most be talking about that loud mouth hippie Darkstar. What a sick motherfucker putting down the land of the free. I’m going to put him down a patriot missile crashing into a sand jockey’s base. Ain’t not motherfucker going to talk about my country like that hoss.” Duke thunders as he too eats an egg. “You know them things will give you gas.” Stammers Jim Miller with a serious expression on his face. “You bet your ass he gives me gas. Just looking at his dirty looks give me the toots then when he opens his mouth I’m a shit wind laying hoss, hoss.” Responds Duke with an angry look in his eye. The bartender smiles at Willie who cracks up uncontrollably. The two man laugh at Duke who looks lost out in left field. They try to tell him what they’re laughing at but can’t seem to catch there breath as they crack up. Finally Jim is able to clue Duke in. “Hoss I wasn’t talking about Darkstar I was talking about them damned pickled eggs. They make you shit like a sow in heat.” Jim explains to Duke while laughing. The Malice Man smiles as he takes another sip of booze. “Eggs or Darkstar they both give me gas but Friday on Fusion I’ll correct that. I’ll make sure he pays for what he’s said about old glory. I’m going to do it for all the veterans and war heroes of this great country. This one isn’t for me it’s for America. Let he freedom ring as I send him whiskey bent and hell bound.” Duke says with his chest out bold. Let freedom ring again. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
