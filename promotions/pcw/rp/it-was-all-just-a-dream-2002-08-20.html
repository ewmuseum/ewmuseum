Date: 8-20-02 ::Introduction:: How can I overcome this? Losing in the GTT to a very good PTCer just shoots all the shit. How can I overcome not being successful in PCW? I have to work harder so that I can actually win some gold. Right now the team of myself and Duzza seem to produce some chemistry, but we will find that out in Fusion and Turmoil this week. We team together to take on Misplaced on Fusion and Crichton and Murray on Turmoil. We have to win this match if we are going to win gold. This will show us where we are at in PCW against other competitors. This will put the mood for the pay per view. We have a chance to win some gold on Sunday, but right now all I am convinced in is to win on Turmoil and Fusion, and worry about the pay per view when it comes. How can we make sure that we will have definite wins? ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to an all African American town where people are carrying around boom boxes with Tupac blaring through the thin lining over the speakers. People are lying around in the sidewalk playing jacks and putting up graffiti up on the walls with gang signs and love. “Yo whats crackin my nigga?” Asks an African American male with his hair corned rosed and he is wearing baggy jeans with some brown Timberlands. He has on a fat Albert shit that is red and he has some bling up in his teeth. “Wat up my nizzle?” Says the man back who the camera recognizes to be Mark Makonie. Has Mark Makonie actually turned black on us? Has he come to his senses to believe that African Americans are better than the whites because he sure looks like he is. Mark is dressed in some Phat Farm blue jeans with some black Air Jordans on his feet that are not even tied. The shoe strings are hanging down the sides of the shoes and he has on a white, silk, button down Pelle Pelle shirt. “Where are you bouncin to today?” asks the young man who we find out is Tyrone Beazly. “I gotta go on a green run since I gots to makes some money.” Mark says as he is taking a brown pic out of his pocket. He starts to then pick out his hair to make his afro bigger. “Who alls goin wit ya?” asks Tyrone as he puts his spray paint bottle down on the sidewalk. “No ones since I didn’t ask anyone.” Mark says as he continues to pick out his hair as some ladies in short skirts walk by. “Hello ladies.” Tyrone says as that is followed by a whistle. Tyrone licks his lips as the ladies turn around and smile their big white smiles at him. Their tops are just halter tops as their juicy boobs are about ready to fall out of both’s shirts. Mark just stands there, continuing to pick out his hair so that he can make it much bigger. Tyrone starts to walk to the girls as Mark grabs his bicep. “Where do ya think you are goin cuz?” asks Mark as he puts his pick away in his right front pocket of his denim jeans. “I was just goin to get some pussy like we had back in da day.” Tyrone says as he starts to walk back to where Mark is standing. “Cuz, you think this wash is all soap and suds?” Mark asks as he starts to head down an alley that is filled with trash and graffiti on the walls. “What?” Asks Tyrone as the two African Americans stop at the front of a fire escape. “I needs ya help on this run yo.” Mark says as he has a serious look on his face. “Why mine?” Tyrone asks as he starts to pound a fist right into his other hand. “Cause ya are tha only person that I cans trust on this job.” Mark starts to say as they continue to walk down the alley. “I knows that ya will have my back if I run into trouble. I know no one else out there would want to run wit me since they are afraid of getting caught. Cuz this is the only way I live.” Tyrone shakes his head as the two starts to head out of the alley and to a park that is nearby. There are rusted teeter tots there with a multi colored mary go round that doesn’t even go around any more. They go past them as on the side of the curb as they go up to an old Caddie that has twenty inch rims on it. It is painted a white with a bronze interior that has been redone. “Damn cuz, when did you get dis all chromed out?” asks Tyrone as he opens up the passenger’s door. “I just got it back from tha shop last week.” Mark says as he opens up the driver’s side and gets in. The two are in and settled in the nice Caddie as Mark starts up the old beast. The hip hop music in the car starts to blare as Mark starts to shake his head up and down to get in rhythm with the beat. The back seats of the car as bumping as there are some subs in the back hooked up to an amp. Mark puts the car in drive as the two starts to head across town. An Hour Later The Caddie pulls up to a house that has the siding coming off of it. The front two windows have been taped up with duct tape with a little gap so the fresh air can come in. The black shingles on the top of the house are coming off as one slides down and hits the brown, dry lawn and breaks into many pieces. Mark stops the Caddie right on the curb next to the house. “Who lives here?” Tyrone asks as he looks at the house. “A new customer that needed the green fast and I mean quick bro.” Mark says as he puts the car in park and he shuts off the car. “Damn Mark, you knows dem people that have been new before.” Tyrone says as he starts to roll his window up in the Caddie. “I know, but this times I have a different feeling.” Mark says as he opens up the door to the Caddie and he gets out. “All I says is to have my back.” With that said, Mark starts to head up to the house after he closes the door to the Caddie. Mark heads up the dead grass as he goes up the two cement steps that lead up to the door way. He knocks on the door as a voice comes out from behind. “Hold on my brotha.” Says the voice inside as it is not far heard from the door. Mark waits on the outside as he takes the green out of his pocket in the little sandwich bag. The guy inside opens up the door as he is dressed in some ripped jeans and a fat Albert shit that is yellow. “Fifty dollas my man.” Mark says as he shows the bag of green to the man. “What in tha hell do I look like cuz?” The man asks as he is taking out some cash rolled up out of his pocket. “A bank?” “Hey cuz these are some Laurie buds and they are the best around town right now.” Mark says as he takes the fifty dollars from the man. Mark starts to see two people having sex on the kitchen floor as he starts to go inside to the people. “Where in tha hell do you think you are goin?” The man asks as he steps in front of Mark. “I just want to see some action.” Mark says as he stops in his tracks as he doesn’t go any farther. “This is no free porno movie son, this is my fucking house.” The man says as he slams the door in Mark’s face. Mark starts to head back to the Caddie as a car pulls up with some hydraulics on it, bouncing up and down, going down the street. The guy in the passenger’s seat pulls out a nine and he starts to bust caps on the house. Mark starts to cover his head and roll in the grass, but that doesn’t stop from the guy’s shooting. Tyrone starts to get out of the Caddie as Mark stops rolling on the grass. The guys go away as they start to play another song in the car. Tyrone starts to run to Mark who has been left unmovable in the grass. “Mark get up bro.” Tyrone says as he starts to hit him in the ribs and shaking him. Mark does not respond to anything as Tyrone puts his hand over the heart of the shirt. He sees that his hand in covered in a red blood and that it isn’t his. He looks down at Mark and he sees that Mark has been shot in the heart and he looks up to the sky as he screams. Mark sits up in his bed as he is in a cold sweat. Sweat is going down his face as he is breathing heavily. He jumps out from under the covers as he goes to hit the light switch on to his room. He then goes to the bathroom that is connected to his room. He flips that light switch on as he goes to the mirror. He starts to touch his face to make sure that he is still alive. He then looks and sees that his skin is still white as he strokes his goatee down. Mark then goes out of the bathroom and he heads back to his bed. He flips the light off in both rooms as he goes back to get under the covers. He sits up a bit and starts to think. “It was all just a bad dream.” Mark says as he lies down to catch some more rest for the night.
