50 Pharaohs & a Big Stack of Chairs.

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/07/02 Tuesday Turmoil was the home of 2 career altering points in the life of Xavier Kannon... After the events of Gold & Glory the Scientologist walked into the Mobile Centre with a new attitude, he was finally going to use his “Stroke”... No more wrestlers attacking him, no more interruptions while he was cutting an interview. The night started off with him beating the Pimply Faced Intern Kid to a pulp for daring to ask him a question, an act carried out to test PCW Security who he ordered to stand back... Flexing the power his 20% ownership gave him. But it was the next act the truly shocked the wrestling world... When the Scientologist and Frisco turned their backs on Tyler Nelson, deciding to join up with the Phantom Booker after pointing out Nelson’s shortcomings over the last few weeks, an act that only solidified Kannon’s power as he joined with TPB to make 50%... And with that new rush of Authority going to his head, he continued to drop lower. So far even Kannon has not attacked a woman, but on Tuesday, Isiy, the wife of the Pharaoh was the victim of an X-Bomb shortly followed by her furious husband who took a beating from the cocky Hubbard follower.... But it was still 30 minutes before the act that will be remembered more. After the Gold Patron Meritorious decided he would replace Corey Williams, he was in a match with the Pharaoh, and with the no DQ rule he implemented he tied the Pharaoh in the ropes and with a chair handed to him by Ellie~!.... “IT” happened.... With the crowd silent he swung the chair at the Pharaoh’s head so hard the base went flying off into the crowd and the echo was heard for a good few seconds above the gasps from the fans... Then after the academic pin, Kannon would not leave the Pharaoh, recalling events from when the pair clashed in NTICW he revealed how himself never pinning the Egyptian has been eating away at him.. Until then... But with the act done... That would not be it for the Scientologist and the Egyptian... .... As Kannon put it himself... “To the beginning..... Of a beautiful hatred...” “Now... I think it was more like THAT!!!” *WHACK* “DAMMIT... Still didn’t break....” Our shot opens up in the huge Mannequin and Egyptian clothing warehouse just off Juliet Lewis Lane in the Industrial quarter of Hubbardville where we join the first couple of PCW and Scientology who are standing in front of rows and rows of mannequins.. Dressed to resemble someone very familiar to the Gold Patron Meritorious. Xavier : “Maybe my swing is off.... I’ll try coming in slightly from the left...” Behind the couple is a large stack of foldable steel chairs, similar to those used for ringside staff at PCW events... Kannon takes one off the top and taps it on the cold concrete floor. *Chink* *Chink* Beside him, a rather enthusiastic Ellie~! stands awaiting his forthcoming action with glee, still pumped from the acts of brutal carnage her man perpetrated at Turmoil. Eleanor : “From...... The....... Left!” In a split second the Scientologist pulls the chair back over his head and swings it with all his Scientological might at the next mannequin in line that has been decked out in full Egyptian clothing similar to those that the Pharaoh Titu Xansiv wears. Xavier : “GHAR!!!” The base of the steel chair smashes into the mannequin’s head but rather than mimicking Tuesday night when the base of the chair was send flying off into the crowd by the impact... It is the head that takes the brunt of it and while the chair dents a little the smashed up mannequin drops back onto the concrete floor. Beside the Scientologist Ellie~! looks at it, disappointedly. Eleanor : “Like...... Didn’t..... Break!” She almost looks upset by the fact as Kannon throws the chair across the warehouse, skidding it along the floor until it hits the side wall and flips up to a halt. *CRASH* Xavier : “Yes, I can see that my little Poon Puppy... This just isn’t doing it for me....” He takes a step back and gestures to the 5 or so rows of 10 mannequins, all dressed identically with the clothing from the bizarre double purpose warehouse. Xavier : “These aren’t the Pharaoh, they don’t bleed or look up to me with pleading eyes as the steel swings towards their fragile heads...” His frustrated valet walks over to, and entwines herself with him, looking with her Bambi-like Blue eyes into his piercing Green pair, running her finger across his lips and talking softly. Eleanor : “It may not be doing anything for you.... But Turmoil did it for me, so if you want normal marital services to resume... Smash that chair over that Egyptiany thing’s head!” The Scientologist sighs as his sultry Wife unwraps herself from him and takes a few steps back, a teasing smile on her face. Xavier : “For you my little Fuck Ferret.... I’ll swing until ne-.. Well, until I break the chair..” He again grabs the top chair and tosses it up into the air, spinning it almost perfectly along it’s vertical axis before leaping up, plucking it out of the air, and bringing it crashing down upon the next mannequin’s head dress wearing head. *WHACK* With the swing he let go of the chair just before it connected and the steel chair goes spinning off from the collision and crashes onto the floor some 10 metres away. *CRASH* But as he looks over to it he sees that it is dented and twisted but still in one piece... Similar to the mannequin who’s head is severely dented.... Kannon frustratedly kicks the figure sending it flying before stamping his feet on the cold concrete. Xavier : “In Xenu’s name why won’t it just BREAK!!!” Back over by the chairs his valet and wife is looking frustrated. Eleanor : “Maybe if you loosen the base and I’ll pretend I didn’t know....” Behind her Blue eyes she thinks back to Tuesday night where the bloodied Pharaoh was tied in the ropes, helpless, as Kannon swung wildly at him and connected with a chair shot so hard it sent the base flying off into the crowd and an echo all around the arena... An act only her Preppy mind could have been turned on by. Xavier : “Argh.... I need to do something REALLY malicious, hitting dummies just doesn’t make me feel like enough of a cold heartless piece of shit....” He walks back over to Eleanor, kicking the pile of chairs over on the way before kissing her on the lips, and at that point his eyes come to life with another evil idea. Xavier : “As always you inspire me my little Shag Squirrel..” As he breaks off Ellie~! does a little teasing growl while the Scientologist pulls out his cellphone and hits a speed dial, pacing around impatiently as he waits for an answer. “Hello...” Xavier : “It’s Kannon, we got any Egyptians here?” “I’ll run a check...... Nope...” Xavier : “Anyone who has been to Egypt this year?” “One... Mi-” Xavier : “Change that.” The Gold Patron Meritorious hangs up and drops the phone back into his pocket as Eleanor smiles back at him, knowing that he has just ruined another person’s life.. Something the Preppy valet sees as a good quality in her man. *Knock* *Knock* *Knock* Our scene switches from the warehouse to that of a bright, sunny street in northern Hubbardville where 5 members of Hubbardville’s Police service stand at a White door, night sticks tapping on palms. “Coming...” From through the glass panel we see a man approaching and as he opens the door the first officer grabs him and forces him down to the floor. Man : “HEY! WHAT DID I DO?!?!?” They handcuff his hands behind his back and 2 officers pull him up onto his knees so the other 3 can take kicks at his ribs. Man : “UGH...” Officer #4 : “Mike Warren... You are under arrest for visiting Egypt when Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon doesn’t like Egyptians...” Mike : “Wha-” Before he can even reply a night stick connects with the side of his neck, dropping him down to the floor so more blows can be rained in on the soon to be ex-Scientologist who has no way of defending himself. Officer #2 : “Come on, let’s carry out the Gold Patron Meritorious’ orders.” 2 of the officers push the door open to show that the man has brought back some Egyptian artefacts and put them on display. The 2 men then shake their heads before one pulls out a small canister of petrol and flings it down the hallway while the other strikes a match and throws it onto the trail of liquid, immediately bursting the hall into flames that start to lick at the souvenirs. Officer #1 : “Good Scientological riddance..” As they then walk down the path we see the other 3 officers carrying the man down the street until they reach the River Hubbard where a big signpost with OUT written on points left. The 5 join together once more and lift the man above their heads before throwing him into the river and after he emerges back on the surface the currents take the unfortunate Scientologist away. “Okay.... I was saving this but here is your surprise...” We cut back to the Mannequins and Egyptian costumes warehouse where Ellie~! stands enthusiastically in front of a bemused Xavier and gestures to the 5th row, farthest to the right mannequin who takes a step forward to reveal himself as none other than famous Scientologist Giovanni Ribisi in full Pharaoh dress. The Scientologist looks at the goof then to the chairs before looking to his Wife with loving eyes. Eleanor : “I thought you’d like! YAY ME!!!” As Giovanni trundles over to them Kannon grabs one of the chairs and with all his might swings it at the bewildered fellow Scientologist... The star of the Mod Squad a mere rabbit in the headlights as the weapon accelerates in at his skull. Eleanor : “Oh....” The chair then smashes across Ribisi’s head with massive impact and momentum. *WHACK!!!* Eleanor : “......YES!!!” The base of the chair goes flying off across the warehouse as Giovanni Ribisi, wearing the rest of the chair around his neck falls backwards onto the cold concrete floor and doesn’t move. Xavier : “My Hubbard that felt great!” He turns around to see his valet leaping up frantically on the spot and applauding, nearly fainting through excitement before running over and leaping at her husband, grabbing him and kissing him all over his face. Eleanor : “That, like, SO RULED!!! You just totally hit him over the head and the thing, like, flew off.. Just like Tuesday!!!” She again excitedly starts to kiss him before Kannon breaks off and turns to us. Xavier : “I got a huge stack of chairs and one of your head... Bye..” With those final words we see the couple drop to the floor before cutting to static.
