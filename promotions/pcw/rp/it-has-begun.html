It Has Begun

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/04 One final blow to the head, and the man fell to the mat. A man of great stature stalked over the fallen man's body as he tried to get to his feet. Even with head gear the fallen had been knocked silly by Ron Morrison. He nudged the man with his foot, and the man refused to stand.'Get up you incompetent little shit!', his voice boomed through the surrounding gym. Ron reached down and pulled the man to his feet by the tank top and shorts, and then shoved him backwards into the corner of the ring they stood in. He gave an evil glare that caused the man to shake with fear. Ron's hands grasped on both ropes, to almost block the man from moving. 'I pay you good money to spar with me. I do not pay you for fear!', his voice was stern. He moved backwards and stopped in the center of the ring. He looked around and pointed to the enormous gym that surrounded them. Weights, cardio stations, televisions, 3 rings, and anything else you could imagine in a gymnasium filled the enormous room. He looked back at the man.'You get 10,000 dollars a month, and free access to all of this. Now tell me...are you really going to pass up that because you're afraid to get knocked down?'The man reluctantly shook his head no, and beads of sweat flew from his hair that stuck out of the sides of the headgear. The man was not a small guy, and look as though he was more than capable in the ring. However, he moved slowly out of the corner and was about to lock up with Morrison, when a voice called out.'Mr. Morrison, I have big news on the PCW deal!', called a voice enthusiastically from a cracked door on the opposite side of the gymnasium. Ron turns away from the man, and starts to leave the ring. The man lets out a huge sigh, and sits onto the mat while sweat pours from his body. Ron steps down from the apron, and his landing on the slick floor causes a thud to echo through the gym. He turns back at the ring, and glares in at the man.'I'll be back for another match in about 10 minutes.', he smiles slightly and the man shakes his head and wimpers before laying backwards onto the mat. He removes his shirts on the way across the gym. After he wipes the sweat off of his brow, he tosses it to the floor, and enters the open door to be met with a cool crisp air coming from a portable fan. The room was a small office with a wooden desk sitting in the center, and shevles filled with trophies and belts lining the walls. Ron walks towards the desk and stood before it. Behind the desk sat an older balding man who sported gray hair and a moustache. He leaned back and and put the paper he was reading down.'What's the big news, Dave? I was in the middle of a shoot spar when you interrupted me. This better be good.', Ron stated in a somewhat annoyed voice. He folded his arms and stared down at the paper which sported a PCW logo at the top. His eyebrow raises as he awaited an answer.'Well, I have good news and bad news, Mr. Morrison.', he looked up with uneasy eyes.'Good news first.....''You're challenge into the PCW Face to Face tournament has been accepted by PCW, and you get to face a man named...hmm..Skitzo.', the man looks unsure as he looks up at Ron, who smiles and looks off into space.'Excellleennttt!! Skitzo, will be a great first victory for me. I'll beat Skitzo, Roberts, Boda, Blitz, Kano, whoever it takes to win the PCW title. There will be no stopping me in pro-wrestling just like my illustrious career in MMA.', As Morrison rants, Dave begins to look uneasy as he looks back down at the paper again.'Sir, you're forgetting the bad news...'Morrison shakes his head and looks down at Dave. His smile fades somewhat as he awaits the bad news.'They have also said that they will not sign you to a full time contract. Atleast not until they see your behaviour. The Takayama fight didn't help your reputation around promoters.--------------------------------------------------The crowd was wild as blows were being exchanged by both of the monstrously sized fighters. Which each blow, sweat and blood flew from almost all directions. Keiji Takayama began to gain the advantage as he caught a wild left hand from Ron Morrison, and flipped both men onto their backs. He wrapped his legs arounds the arm of Ron, and began to try to pull it down to hyperextend it from its joint. Ron would not falter, however, and held his mighty arm up while gripping onto his other hand. He began to roll towards to Takayama with both arms, and stood on his feet while Takayama kept his legs wrapped around the arm of Morrison. Takayama attempted to pull Morrison back onto his back, but 'The Demon's' monstrous tree trunk legs kept him stable. Ron released hold of his captured arm and began to deliver blows to the unblocked face of Takayama. With no other choice than to let go or lose, Takayama released the arm and delivered a kick to the gut of Morrison. After rocking Morrison, Takayama stood, and took a swing that would become the final concious act he ever committed. Ron ducks the punch and rears back, only to deliver a haymaker that rocks Takayam's head side to side; snapping the neck in the process. With little emotion in his eyes, Morrison stands and breathes heavily as the referee instantly rings the bell. The match is over due to a knockout. Morrison raises his arms victoriously as trainers and medics spill in the ring. One group to congratulate Morrison, the other to check on Takayama. A man walks to Morrison with a microphone for a post-fight interview. He snatched the microphone and walked towards the body of Takayama.'I told everyone in the world of Mixed Martial Arts to fear me, and to respect me. I have knocked out the only obstacle between me and the Pride Championship later tonight. Let his blood be a sign of things to come!', he dropped the microphone, and the crowd booed in dismay at the lack of respect shown by Morrison to his fellow fighter. Soon the celebration turned sour as medics began to wear worried expressions on their faces, and soon a stretcher was brought to the ring. Ron walked up to the body of Takayama as he was being put on the stretcher, and instead of any sign of respect. He merely showed a look of disgust and left the ring.--------------------------------------------------'Mr. Morrison! ...RON! Are you listening?, with that said Ron snaps out of his flashback and returns to the present moment. He places both hands on the desk and looks Dave in the eye.'What do you mean they won't sign me full time?''Just what I said. They want to see how you react to management, and if you keep the fights in the ring. They think you are a loose cannon to be blunt.', after he finished his statement, Dave found himself being slammed against the wall with Ron holding onto his shirt. An angry look spread over his face, and he raised a fist.'Are you trying to say something because I can sure as hell replace you, and get someone who gets the job done!?', Dave looked on with fear, but reached up and pulled at Ron's hand. Ron releases Dave and turned away. He turned back with a look of sorrow, and begins to speak.'Look I'm sorry about that, but I really need a new venue of competition. From all I've ever heard, Pro-Wrestlers are the toughest, baddest guys around. Well, I know I'm the baddest mother f-cker on the planet, so I have to prove it.', Ron moves back towards the table as Dave sits back in his seat, and straightens his shirt. He looks up at Ron with a questioning look.'I mean no offense by this, but you have an uncontrollable temper. You're never going to get signed if you can't control yourself outside...or even in the ring.'Ron shook his head to agree with Dave, and sighed before speaking any further.'When I step in the ring something happens, Dave.', Morrison walks away from the desk and paces in front of his trophies and title belts. He stops in front of the Pride Championship tournament trophy he won on the night he beat Keiji Takayama.--------------------------------------------------After defeating his opponent in the finals, Ron Morrison was the Pride Grand Prix champion. He sat in his dressing room and looked at his trophy as trainers, friends, and family celebrated around him. Ron did nothing but sit and stare blanky at the trophy. A door opened but was barely audible due to the celebration. An official walked in with a solemn look on his face and approached Ron. He kneeled down to speak with Ron who did nothing, but continue to stare at his trophy.'What is it?', Ron questioned.'Keiji Takayama....' the official trailed off.'What about him?', asked Morrison with a stern voice.'He suffered a broken neck during your fight, and well....they don't think he's going to make it through the night......you....killed him. I'm afraid that we can't let you compete for, or reign as champion in Pride.', the official reached down from the trophy. However, his hand was grasped by Ron, and the bones could be broken if he squeezed any harder. Ron looked up at the fearful eyes of the official with a but angry stare. He sneered and moved the official's hand away from the trophy, as the celebration died down in the background. All eyes were on them and Ron begain to speak softly and sternly.'I earned that trophy and you or the army of Japan are not going to take it. You don't want me to compete for your bushleague fighting promotion because a fighter who had no business fighting me, died? Well good riddons I say. Good riddons to Pride FC, and good riddons to Keiji Takayama. I hope that as he lays in his bed, and can do nothing, but stare incoherently as his wife and kids crying around him. I hope while he's doing that he realizes the mistake he made when he signed on to fight me. I hope he realizes that he chose death over life. It is not my fault as his incompetence. Now get out of my room before another man goes to the hospital tonight!', Ron released the mans hand, and the man scurried out of the room. No one in the room said a word, and simply stood staring at either their feet or Ron as he brewed in his seat.'What the hell are you standing around for!', his voice startled many in the room. 'We got a celebration to get on with..WE'RE THE CHAMPION whether they say so or not!', many false smiles filled the faces in the room as Ron stood for the pending celebration.--------------------------------------------------'What do you mean something happens?', questioned Dave.'I don't know what happens exactly. It's like it's me that steps into the ring, but something takes over once the fight begins, or even after it ends. I'm not angry because I have no reason to be angry. I have everything in the world going for me. Wife, money, pride...But when I have a fight, it goes black and something takes over like I said.''The Demon, ey?', Dave laughed slightly.'I don't believe in demons or whatever you want to bring up. Theres life and theres death, leave it at that, Dave. The only demon that exists is me when I'm fighting. I can't even remember one of my fights. I always have to watch tapes afterwards to remember anything that happened. How am I supposed to control something I can't remember happening?', Ron said as he turned away from the trophies. Dave stood up and moved towards Ron with the paper and a pen. He handed them both to Ron, and smiled slightly.'I don't know, but you better figure something out. Because if I know you...you're going to be the champion of PCW very soon.', Ron took the paper and put it on an empty space on the shelf. He uncapped the pen and signed the contract. He then tossed the pen at the table, and handed the contract back.'Fax it over...now if you'll excuse me I have a spar to finish.', Dave nods and walks towards a door behind the desk, and leaves with the paper in his hand. Ron turns and walks back out into the warm gymnasium to see the man leaning against a corner of the ring. Ron walks back towards the ring, and sees a look of frustration fall over the face of the man. Ron pulls himself up onto the ring, and smiles when entering the ring.'I'm backkk! Give me all you got, boy. I have a big match soon.'-END-
