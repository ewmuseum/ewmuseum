Feel The RUSH

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 23 - 03 ::The scene opens with the complete visage of a colossal tower made of glass, steel, and concrete. Rising towards the heavens like a stalwart guardian of the Gods, the mighty structure looks down upon the beautiful surrounding landscape that lies before it. Lush, green trees, neatly trimmed bushes, and beautiful marble statues compliment the Atlas-like structure. Ionic-style columns hold up the overhang high upon their shoulders at the front door, casting a beckoning shadow onto the concrete arc that acts as the front door parking area. But just what is this place? What company could commission such a modern architectural marvel? Well, the sign says it all…:: RUSH Inc. ::The mighty corporate tower of glass and steel belongs to the resurrecting RUSH wrestling federation. Beautiful and awe-inspiring, one has to wonder how a company that was owned by PCW only a month or so before could still have such a structure in their possession. But, taking in consideration that it might not even be a functional place of business, but merely a landmark could also be possible. It’s a mystery, but is it worth pursuing?Dorn Tracer thinks so.:: Tracer, staring up: Marvelous. Simply marvelous. I can’t believe such a backwater piece of shit could own something so nice. Howard must have dug up quite a pretty penny. ::RUSH’s former boss, however, had disappeared some time after RUSH’s first resurrection. If the contract to the symbol of RUSH Wrestling was under his name, the government or some major corporation would have confiscated it. Xavier Kannon, PCW’s former owner, is also another possibility as the owner of the tower. It’s an enigma, and Dorn has found it fit to find out.:: Tracer: RUSH can’t truly still own this place, so I better find who does. I can’t just waltz in and start grabbing up files and documents if the owner if some putz in North Carolina… ::The Astronomer leaves the crescent black top and walks up to the revolving glass doors. He looks past his translucent reflection and gazes at the beautiful white tile floor within. The sparking UV rays of the Great Star beam down upon them and cast a golden glow across the floor. Dorn just stands in silent, subtle awe of the tower lobby.:: Tracer, awe-struck: Not bad at all… this place could contend with the Lunar Citadel. Definitely not owned by anyone heralding a RUSH banner. But, I’ll give them the benefit of the doubt… ::The Shooting Star Champion pushes his way through the revolving glass doors and steps into the huge, two-floor lobby area. A receptionist desk sits in the middle of the room, while three stunted corridors of elevators lie beyond. To the right, the corridors end and the glass wall begins. Two the left, there’s an employee cafeteria area and a set of escalators that lead to the second floor. There doesn’t seem to be anyone around except the pleasantly plump-looking woman behind the receptionist desk. She seems to be happily munching on a box of donuts and humming a happy-go-luck tune while tilting her head from side to side. Figuring that this rotund female can be the beginning point of his brief journey, he walks up to the receptionist desk and plants his hands upon the marble top. The tilting of her head stops, and so does the humming, but she continues to consume glazed donuts like they’re going out of style.:: Receptionist: Hullo! Can I help you, sah? ::She speaks with a quaint British accent, heavy with zest. Though Dorn doesn’t particularly like it, he decides not to let something so petty bother the Shooting Star Champion and just cracks a false, optimistic smile at the robust receptionist.:: Tracer: Well I’m not sure, ma’am. Depends on what I want! Receptionist: Tehe! Well sah, I can try! Tracer: Alright, can you tell me where I might find the owner of this fine establishment? Receptionist: Why, sure! Very top floor, lad! ::Tracer nods in acknowledgement and strides past the shady marble receptionist desk, leaving the cheery lady to her donuts and the voices in her head. He approaches the first stunted corridor of elevators and stops upon entering it. At the end of the small hallway is a large golden statue of an indistinguishable figure. A jumbled mess of pointed geometric shapes, the golden figure of puzzlement has to be one of those ‘it’s what you perceive it as’ statues that only hippies who are completely stoned can figure out. But that doesn’t seem to interest Dorn; what really peaks his curiosity is that the statue is made of pure gold.:: Tracer, to himself: Who in the name of Sheor the Plague could purchase a golden amalgam of geometric figures… Tracer: Might as well find out. Hopefully he’ll still have those fucking tapes. ::He turns to the chrome-colored elevator doors nearest to him and presses the small green triangle that points up adjacent to it. The little button lights up to a cheerful neon green and a subtle, mechanical hum emits from somewhere above Dorn. He just sort of stares at the double set of chrome doors, tapping his foot as the perpetual humming noise comes closer and closer. When it sounds like it’s right in front of him, a loud DING resonates from the doors and they slide open, revealing a small mahogany elevator room. The Astronomer steps into pleasant smelling room and turns towards the small control panel adjacent to the door. The circular depressed buttons count from up, starting from the letter ‘L’ and ending with the number ‘20’. Since the pleasantly plump receptionist told him the big guy would be on the top floor, Dorn presses the white ’20’ button, causing it to glow green. The elevator shudders and creaks, then Dorn can feel a force pulling his body down as the elevator ascends to the top.:: Tracer: The only two people I can think of that have the money to purchase a place like this are Tyler Nelson and The Phantom Booker… but I doubt either one of them would want ANYTHING to do with RUSH. Maybe Howard repurchased it… Tracer: Nevertheless on who it is, I MUST have those tapes. Viewing the ultimate failure known as Jonathan Drachen will definitely assist my ‘Get rid of RUSH ass-thorn’ quest. ::Dorn’s lips curl into a sinister little grin and he lets out a small, throaty laugh. The prospect of getting rid of Jonathan Drachen is like finding pure gold in your backyard. It’s just too good to pass up. The pest decided to attack the Shooting Star Champion, so now that said champion is going to make Drachen’s life ANOTHER living hell, seeing as how it already was at one point. It will surely crack and shatter Drachen’s already fragile mind and sending him swirling into an uncharted, chaotic void of madness and depression. A very tasty proposition.:: DING! ::The strong hand of gravity lets loose of Dorn’s body as the elevator comes to a screeching, yet steady halt. A bell hidden somewhere in the elevator gives another mechanical chime and the chrome doors slide upon. The bright golden rays of the sun pour into the elevator from the surrounding glass walls and cause Dorn to shield his eyes with his coat. He looks up at the floor display screen and notices it says… 20 Tracer: I’m here! Time to feel…the…RUSH! ::The Shooting Star Champion cracks another grin and steps out of the elevator. The doors close behind him and the elevator gives a familiar creak before heading back down. A long corridor stretches off into the distance before Dorn, accompanied by a red rug. The walls are made of beautiful polished oak and are adorned with classical works of art and beautiful busts of famous figures, figures of power.:: Tracer: Guy must think highly of himself to have such things. This man better be God himself or I’ll be severely disappointed… ::Tracer starts off down the hallway and can see two huge wooden doors at the end of the corridor. Both are ajar slightly and a thin beam of light creeps out from within the room, almost like a beacon to bring the wary in. Dorn reaches the door rather quickly and just stands before them, letting the tension and anticipation build within his body. It all begins to tingle and he can bear it to no more. With an outstretched hand, he bursts through the doors and finds himself enveloped by a wave of sunlight. Dorn yanks his shades out of his pocket and puts them on to refract the damaging sunlight. He walks into the room and finds himself in a breath-taking, elaborate domain of marble, polished wood, beautiful paintings, expensive technology, and a tall, throne-like chair of red cushion and furnished wood frame.:: Tracer, in awe: My stars… what a place! ::He approaches the huge, crescent-shaped desk that sits in front of a glass window backdrop and stares at the back of the large, elaborate seat. It rocks back and forth slightly with a subtle creaking noise, leading Dorn to believe someone is beyond the back of that chair.:: Tracer: Hello? Show yourself, please. Voice: Welcome to my domain, visitor. May I help you? ::The voice is strangely familiar to Dorn, but the Astronomer shrugs off his feeling of déjà vu and continues.:: Tracer: Are you the owner of this establishment? Voice: I am. Why have you come? Tracer: I’d like to access your archive of Adrenaline and PPV tapes. Anything concerning Jonathan Drachen. Voice: HA! That bastard? Why would you deal with an underling such as he? Tracer: He’s become a thorn in my side. A maniacal one, at that. I must research his past days in order to know how to break him. What’s it matter to you? I’m prepared to pay for the tapes. Voice: I despise the man as well! He caused me nothing but bitter agony… ::Tracer’s face twists into puzzlement and he tilts his head, trying to circulate the strange words through his mind.:: Tracer: You know him as well? Voice: Of course I do, you fool! Tracer: Who are you?? Voice: You really wish to know? HA! Fool! I shall show you, but prepare to be AMAZED!! ::Suddenly, the chair whips around and all is revealed. Dorn gasps and jumps back, completely shocked at who the owner of the tower is. It takes him a couple of seconds to regain his composure, and when he does, his jaw drops and he just stares at the mysterious owner.:: Tracer: It’s… YOU?? .......Milkman:: YES! ‘TIS ME! ::Steve ‘Milkman’ fuckin’ Carnage!! The former janitor of RUSH is now the owner of the huge, elaborate RUSH tower!! Dorn just stares for a moment… then bursts into knee-slapping laughter!! The defiant look on the Carnage’s face melts into confusion, then into irritation as he watches the Astronomer laugh it up at him.:: Milkman: What’s so funny, cretin?! Tracer: YOU…HAHAHAHA…ARE THE OWNER OF THIS PLACE?! Milkman:: YES! Quit your laughing! NOW! Please? ::Dorn doesn’t stop, however. He just merely slows down so it comes easier for him to talk. Carnage’s face has longed since turned red from embarrassment and anger, trying to control the rage building with his body.:: Tracer: That’s preposterous! You’re just some jobbing fuck who was cleaning toilets day in and day out. No way you own this place. Let me speak to the REAL owner! Milkman:: I AM the real owner! I rose from the ashes of my shit-shoveling ways, got a COLLEGE EDUCATION, and bought this place with the money I saved! AHHAHAHAH! ::Carnage suddenly leaps off out of the chair and ascends to the top of the expensive desk, with a maniacal look foaming from his face. Dorn backs up, an amused look on his face, and folds his arms across his chest. Carnage’s voice rises to a rather squeaky roar as he tries to sound intimidating.:: Milkman:: And now that I am on top, things will start to change! It’s STEVE CARNAGE’S TIME NOW!! I’m sick of being oppressed! I’m sick of being brushed aside! I’m sick of being insulted… now it’s going to be MY WAY! THE MILKY WAY~! Tracer: Oh my stars… Milkman: I R MILKMAN!!!!! ::The deranged former janitor raises his arms to the sky and lets out a half-hearted roar. Tracer just slaps himself in the forehead and shakes his head in shame, embarrassed of the fact that he knows this man. Dorn just throws his hands up in defeat and turns away from the raving lunatic.:: Tracer: Whatever, Carnage. I’ll just go to the local video stores for the tapes. Thanks anyway… Milkman:: You’re… leaving? Tracer: Yes, Steve. I’m a little frightened at your insanity and wish to have no part in it whatsoever. Thanks for aiding me in my quest though… jerk off. ::The Shooting Star Champion turns towards the doors and heads out, despite the pleas of a desperate milk delivery man. The huge doors slam shut behind Dorn and he starts off back towards the elevator. Suddenly, a man in an Alumni suit bursts forth from the elevator and accidentally runs into Dorn!:: Tracer: Watch it, infidel! ::The man just continues on, however, breezing right past Dorn. Dorn shoots him a dirty look, but decides not to bother with it and gets on the elevator. Just before the door closes, Dorn hears an enraged voice sound from within the elaborate office of Steve Carnage.:: Man: What the fuck, Carnage?! GET OUT OF MY OFFICE! NOW! GET BACK TO CLEANING THE TOILETS! Milkman:: Yes sir! Sorry sir! ::Dorn, meanwhile, just shakes his head as the elevator doors close.:: [End.]
