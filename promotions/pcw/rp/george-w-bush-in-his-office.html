Date: Young Tiger doesn&#039;t appear, I&#039;m just bored George W. Bush was in his office, coloring his Spiderman book. He lifted a pink crayon and colored Spiderman pink. Suddenly, Dick Cheney walked in... 'W...' George looked up. 'Master!' Cheney looked down at the art of Bush... 'Erm, George, Spiderman's red...' 'But I want him pink!!!' 'Whatever...' Cheney sat down at his desk and looked at all the papers in front of him while George Bush still colored. 'So W...After Iraq, who else?' Bush thought. 'Let's bomb Canada!' Cheney looked confused. 'Why?' 'I don't know.' George W continued coloring when Colin Powell walked into the office. 'Hello' Cheney looked up. 'I'm having a heart attack!', said Cheney falling from his chair. Colin Powell smiled. 'YES! One down!' (OOC: I give that copyright to Skylar Green) Colin Powell loaded a shot gun (OOC: Also to Skylar Green) Bush looked at the gun. 'That gun is unblimamamamamable!' '...What? Nevermind, I'm going to kill you Bush!' Bush held up his Spiderman coloring book. 'Not if Spiderman protects me!' Colin shot and the hole went through Spiderman's crotch and flew over Bush's head and broke a window. Bush cried. 'You killed Spiderman! You will pay for this!', said Bush. 'Now I'll kill you!' Bush cried. 'Mommy! I want my mommy!' Colin Powell looked sad for Bush and tried to comfort him. 'There there Bush...' 'Mommy!' Colin Powell, then lifted a pistol for his back pocket and lifted in the to Bush's head. 'Don't cry Bush...' Then Powell felt really sad he was doing this and dropped the gun. Then both Bush and Powell cried. 'Hey! I'm missing Yu-Gi-Oh!' Powell looked confused at Bush's stateful. 'You...Gi...Oh?' 'Yeah, it's a show.' Bush then left the office and Powell was alone in the office. Then the scene cuts to Eddy who was sitting on a couch. *End* (I said it would suck...)
