Title: Bashing Head On Wall = Bad Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered Failure. The HIT is not accustomed to losing. But the fact remains, Tim Walden defeated him TWICE at Alliances. In the ladder match, due to his own fault, on an Times Table bulldog that he never should have tried. In Alliances, though...Walden beat him on his own, with a triple powerbomb combination ending in Vampir Nosferatu's Cuban Uprising via pinfall, despite Trapezoid's shoulders never being pinned to the mat. Still, Trapezoid'll call a spade a spade. Walden won. But now, the pathway of vengance begins. HIT WILL exact vengeance on all in his path until he gets to Walden. And the new owner of PCW's soda contract, Skitzo, stands in his way. Beware, Skitzo.... For none of your personalities can save you from a very ticked off Trapezoid.. ::A steady pounding can be heard, occuring approximately every five seconds. The thuds increase in intensity, until their source is discovered. The source is The HIT, and his head repeatedly bouncing off a wall. The regular intervals only serve to make the ritual look more painful, before he ceases. Head bruised, as well as his ego, he looks up, dazed..:: The HIT: Walden beat me.....son of a gun, I don't believe it..but he did. Twice.. :Trapezoid slurs the next few words due to the head bashing. His head spins a bit, as he attempts to rise, but falls back to the ground.: The HIT: There has to be SOME way to forget this aberration! I mean, I LOST TO TIM WALDEN! But, he seemed invincible. It was amazing...he threw me around unlike anything I've ever seem from Timothy.....maybe Walden really DID finally turn the corner! Maybe now, he is a worthy foe..and if he is, then...he's ready and worth my time for...well, you'll see! ::Trapezoid's babbling seems to be pain induced, but, is he serious? Time shall tell....:: The HIT: But first, I've got other matters to deal with. In the form of a multiple personaltied nutso Gatorade sponsor named Skitzo. Skitzo.....one of the few human beings I've ever met who might just be more odd than I am....oh, man.... :Trapezoid attempts to get to his feet again, and using his arms to keep balance, succeeds. Then of course, he promptly falls back down again, with more incoherently slurred words.:: The HIT: SKITZO! NO ONE LIKES GATORADE! GATORADE SUCKS! WATER IS MUCH BETTER! GATORADE.....NO! WATER! WAAAAAATTTTTER SUCKS! IT REALLY REALLY SUCKS! AHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! ::Trapezoid's pathetic imitation of The Waterboy falls apart, as he falls down, seemingly in complete and utter dazed state. He lies unconscious on the ground for several minutes, before our intrepid announcer's voice returns.:: Does he seem like a dangerous man now? Not really..more like a stupid man. But still, he is ready for Skitzo this week. If he wakes up, that is. Some people are really quite dumb. HIT is one of them. Skitzo is too. And so, I have but one more thing to say. HIT likes Spam. ::Fade to the inner depths of the human mind, to try and explain this atrocity.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
