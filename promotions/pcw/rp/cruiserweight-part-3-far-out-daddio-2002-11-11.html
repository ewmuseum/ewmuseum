Cruiserweight?! - Part 3 - Far out, Daddio

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 11 [His body is in absolute agony. Every bone, muscle, and tendon aches. What in the name of Judas Priest was Tommy thinking taking him to that place? Yeah, he's going to be a cruiserweight. That doesn't mean you have some crazy guy stretch him like he's one of those far out Gumby dolls. The only thing that is keeping 'Heavy Metal' Harry from going insane is the sweet thought of his true love, Avril Lavigne. The sexy Canadian rock princess who has stolen his heart. The girl who can rock as hard as anyone in Twisted Sister and keep the same attitude. The girl who will one day walk that far out aisle to marry The Heavy Metal One. The girl who is the sister to his opponent, Vilas Lavigna. Talk about not being far out. Some punk like that being a brother to something so beautiful and special as Avril. This world doesn't make any sense. One child can come out to be such a freakin' prick and yet the other comes out to be a far out kind of person. That didn't happen with Harry and Tommy. Both of them came out perfectly fine. Not one flaw...well, except for that whole sexual addiction thing, but that has been taken care of] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: My darling love, man. [Harry lies in the sand of Tommy's private beach. His Twisted Sister T-shirt taking a much needed break in the house as his pale gut extends over his leather swim trunks. The brown haired man, with the shaggy goatee, holds a clipped out magazine picture of Avril Lavigne in front of him. Her pale skin glowing like one of those far out glow worms, man. Her eyes so full of happiness. She's one bitchin' chick, man] Harry: Where are you my love? [The sun isn't as warm as it should be. There are one of those bogus cold fronts moving through and that whole winter thing, that usually puts a damper on the beach. The temperatures will dip into the low sixties, making the ocean feel most cold when trying to hit some waves. Hell, even if you just want to take a quick dip into the ocean it's not even possible with those totally heinous temperatures. Under the sun Harry feels fine, not that he'd even notice anyway. His mind is on two things: Avril Lavigne and becoming PCW Cruiserweight Champion. He never really concentrated that much on wrestling, he usually let Tommy take care of that department. He is the dude who has the true passion to become one of the best. All Harry ever wanted to be was to be a great manager, and that he was. In NTICW, Harry led Tommy to a far out career until the jump to PCW where they asked him to become a wrestler because of his size. That was not far out, man. Not far out at all. He never wanted to get in the ring to do battle, just to be at his brothers side. But now, with the help of Tommy, he feels that he can win this thing. He can go through the competition in the tournament to capture singles gold. He knows it's selfish and not far out to think like that, but Tommy is all behind him in the quest. He wants the tag team titles, for both of them to share the glory, but that's not going to happen just yet. In a way, if he does win then they will both feel that victory, that glory if you will. Because the roles have been reversed now. Harry as the wrestler and Tommy as the manager. Talk about far out, man] Voice: Hey, man. [Harry sits up to see where the voice is coming from, but he immediately throws himself back down from his sore muscles. He uses his eyes to look around and there is not one single soul in sight. Tommy is gone. The beach goers aren't allowed on this portion of the beach. Nobody should be here] Voice: Hey, dumbass...I'm over here. [Harry pulls through the pain and sits up, with a grimace on his face. He looks to the ocean to see a drunken figure walking along the water. The dude looks kind of familiar, like he's seen him before from somewhere. Too bad it isn't Avril, because that would be far out, man] Harry: Who's there, man? Drunken Man: HEEEEYYYY!!!! [The drawn out word almost makes the man sound like The Fonz from Happy Days. Except the voice is much too slurred to even come close to The Fonz. Harry limps his way down the beach getting closer to the man near the water. The man wears a tied dye T-shirt with the Grateful Dead logo on the back, you know those dancing teddy bears. The shirt is accomppanied by a pair of bellbottoms. The guy looks like he fell out of the late sixties, early seventies. Kind of far out, man] Harry: How'd you get on the beach, man? Drunken Man: I have my... *hiccup* ... ways, man. [Harry is kind of weirded out as to why the man has his back turned. The way he dresses kind of reminds him of someone he saw in a very old wrestling magazine once. He remembers the tied dye the most, that's what sticks out in his mind. Why would some old wrestling guy come to visit him? Give him advice? A lecture? Or maybe Harry could be dreaming all of this. He pinches himself in the arm and nothing happens] Harry: So, you wanna smoke a bowl or something? [The man turns around and looks like he came straight out of a ZZ Top video. His long beard hangs down to his chest, His long hair is bushed outward with a balding spot in the front. He wears very large sunglasses like in that Hawaii 5-0 television show. Harry looks like he has just seen a ghost, well, because he is] Harry: DAD!!!!! 'Far Out' Frank (Harry's Dad): Yeah, man. Harry: I thought you were dead, man. Frank: I am, man. Harry: But your here, so that means your not dead anymore, man. Frank: I'm just visiting, but I'm still dead, man. *hiccup* Harry: Your breath smells kind of like Old Granddad whiskey, man. Frank: Heaven has a far out bar, man. Harry: Far out, man. Frank: Yeah, man. [There is an odd moment of silence between father and son. Harry can't believe that his father is right before him. He looks bitchin'. Well, of course he looks bitchin' because look at Harry, he's the spitting image of his father. Two far out dudes] Frank: So, how about sparking that bowl, man? Harry: OH YEAH! I almost forgot, man. [Harry pulls out his far out peace pipe full of sweet weed. Harry hands it to the man of honor to spark up the first hit. Frank quickly takes the bowl and lights up. It's been such a long time since he's had one of those, man. Oh yeah!! It just hits the spot. He's been waiting for 17 years to have another taste of the sweet weed. He's taking it from his son, man. That's freakin' far out, man!! The kid looks bitchin'...as he should...look who his father is! Frank then hands the bowl over to his pride and joy] Harry: So, what's the visit for, man? [Frank pauses for a few moments, goes to open his mouth about ready to explain, then closes it. He goes back into deep thought, but he's really come up with a whole bunch of...nothing] Frank: I forgot, man. [Harry laughs] Harry: That's far out, daddio! Frank: Yeah, man. Harry: I do have this killer match coming up this Tuesday against some dude named Vilas Lavigna, man. Frank: Is that the same as Avril? Harry: YEAH, MAN!! How'd you know about that babe? Frank: I may be dead but I keep my eye on the things that are important, man. Harry: FAR OUT! Yeah, that's this dudes sister and I want to marry her, man. Frank: Really, man? Harry: Yeah, man. Frank: That's far out, man. Harry: I know, man. [Harry passes the bowl back to his dad, who takes it like a champion. Nobody can take a bowl hit like 'Far Out' Frank, no question. Just the way he takes the hit makes it seem like he's that dude who came up with the invention of the bowl. He could have, just can't remember it] Harry: But this match is going to be far out, dad. It's in the cruiserweight division. [Frank looks sort of confused, then looks at the bowl of weed in his hand] Frank: Cruiserweight? Harry: It's what they call the lightweights now, man. Frank: Lightweight? Harry: Yeah, man. [Frank points at Harry's large belly and pokes him like he's the Pillsbury Dough Boy. Harry giggles too] Frank: CRUISERWEIGHT?! That's far out, man. Harry: I know, man. Frank: So, are you going to have pre-marital sex with Avril or wait until after the wedding, man? [Harry thinks about it for a few moments and then flashes his father a smile] Harry: I was thinking I'd wait until after the wedding, man. It'll make our night of sex mean more than if we just jump into it, man. Frank: THAT'S MY BOY, MAN!! Always thinking about the future. Harry: Did you know that you had sex with Tommy's mom? Frank: I did?! Harry: Yeah, man. Frank: Sweet lord! How'd that happen?! Harry: I was hoping that you would tell me that, man. Because she's my biological mom. Tommy and I are brothers now, man. Frank: Whoa...I must have totally missed out on that, man. Was I any good? Harry: You had to be to get me out of it, man. Frank: You know...I was wondered how she got fat again after she had Tommy. Weird, man. Harry: Yeah, man. Frank: Son, I'm sorry that I wasn't able to be there for you when you needed me the most, man. Harry: It's not your fault, dad. You couldn't help that you died. Frank: I guess so, but I still feel absolutely horrible that I wasn't able to be there for you, man. I wasn't there to see you ride your bike the first time, talk to you about the birds and the bees, or to watch you graduate high school. Most of all, I'm sorry that I'm not there to see you be a professional wrestler, man. I always wanted to step in the ring to help out my best buddy, 'Radical' Randy, but I just never got to it. I was too damn lazy to even do it really. But you stood up and you got in there. I'm proud of you, man. Really damn proud of you. Harry: Thanks, dad. Frank: Whether you win this 'Cruiserweight' thing or not, your still my son. You went out there and gave it your best shot. Even though both me and you KNOW your going to win. [Harry smiles] Harry: Yeah, man. Frank: I love you, son. Always have. [Harry would cry but his eyes are too damn bloodshot to even do that] Harry: I love you too, daddio. [The two men embrace, Harry feeling like a part of him has been put back together once again. That missing piece of the puzzle has been found, and it is finally complete after all of those not so far out years. It's over. Harry can carry on] Harry: WHA?!?! [Harry pops up from the sand, alone] Harry: What the hell happened, man? [He looks into his right hand to see the peace pipe...the contents inside burnt out. It seems Harry took a couple hits and then fell asleep. But it was just a dream] Harry: That was some damn good sh*t, man. [Harry then rises to his feet, his muscles sore and bones aching. Crazy Gerhardt could have been a little more gently. The bastard] Frank: Later, man. [In the distance the father of 'Heavy Metal' Harry walks off to his home. Ready to conquer a new journey, a new portion of his life. It wasn't a dream] Fade To Black
