Ellie~! Issues a Challenge...To Xavier

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/02/02 It hasn’t been a good weekend for far for Xavier Kannon which is not a good portent for his upcoming matches, probably the biggest 3 in his still young career. On Saturday he suffered a new indignity, ranking somewhere alongside losing to the Pharaoh’s bodyguard back in NTICW. In the 1st round of the GTT, the tournament he palmed off his Snapple~! shilling duties to concentrate on, he was knocked out in the 1st round by OSW’s Seth Masters, a rookie, a nobody, and a man who has lacks the illustrious Title history wrestling’s only Scientologist has. So with the GTT out of the way, and after the inevitable rant about being robbed, he then has the biggest week in his wrestling life. At Sabotage he teams with the newest member of Asylum Bobby Stormbringer to face Drake Maxwell and the End, then on Tuesday he gets a rematch for his Cruiserweight Title against a man he has never beaten, “The Messiah” Chris Chase, the man who beat him for it at Shanghaied, then the next Sunday is March Madness, his 2nd PCW PPV where he will be in the Main Event Battle Royal for the PCW World Title. But with all this ahead of him, as well as taking back his Snapple~! spokemanship, can the Gold Patron Meritorious put the events of Saturday out of his mind? Can he defeat some of PCW’s best talent when he crashes out in the 1st round of the GTT? And will his reaction effect the rest of Asylum? “I DON’T BELIEVE IT!” comes the familiar holler of Snapple~!‘s spokesperson Xavier Kannon, “Seth Masters....I AM BETTER THAN SETH MASTERS! SETH MASTERS ISN’T A SOON TO BE 2 TIME PCW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION! SETH MASTERS ISN’T THE OFFICIAL SNAPPLE~! SHILLER! SETH MASTERS WAS NOT THE MVP OF SHANGHAIED!” as Kannon vents his frustration we hear bangs and crashes. The camera is walking along the hallway of Xavier’s Greenwich Village apartment, just a few hours before he flies to Calgary for Sabotage. We pass the bedroom door, we pass the batch room door and then we get to a closed door with BREAKING STUFF AFTER A LOSS ROOM adorned upon it with a plaque, and by the sounds of it, the Scientologist is using it to great effect. “THIS IS ALL BECAUSE I AM A SCIENTOLOGIST! THAT’S RIGHT!” he claims, still yelling wildly as the crashes continue, becoming more frequent, “IT’S DISCRIMINATION! IF I WERE A CHRISTIAN THEY WOULD NOT HAVE TREATED ME THIS WAY! IF I WERE JEWISH THEY WOULD NOT HAVE DARED PULLING THIS! EVERYONE KNOWS IT...ME AND CHAZZY 2 COOL WERE ROBBED!! XAVIER KANNON DID NOT SCREW XAVIER KANNON....THEY SCREWED XAVIER KANNON!” The sound of breaking glass is then head and the bangs come to an end. “OWWWWWWWWW!” says Xavier in the kind of voice reserved for someone who has just punched right through a window, “OOOOWWWWW! That’s it.....I think it’s out of my system, that and I’m a face so I’d better show some sportsmanship....” The door opens and out steps a rough looking Xavier Kannon wearing his trademark long, Brown leather coat. Under it he has a Black, un-done Black silk shirt with Purple lightening patterns, where it falls open a KANNON AUDITS! t-shirt is revealed in Black with Green lettering. Along with these he has a pair of his exclusive Black boot cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple intricate embroidery down the left leg to where a pair of Purple, Black and Grey Skechers adorn his feet. His long, Auburn hair is everywhere, adding to his dishevelled look and he is holding his bleeding right fist in a White towel.” “Oh.....” he says, surprised to see the camera there as he opens the door, “You caught that?” Anyways, I’ll be with you in a minute, go and talk to Ellie~! or something, she usually has something to say. The camera walks further down the hall and the sound of Ellie~! screeching in her high pitched whiny voice is heard. “I CAN’T BELIEVE HE LOST!” the Preppy valet bellows, “ALL HE HAD TO DO WAS WIN THE THING SO I COULD SHILL SNAPPLE~! SOME MORE! I WAS REALLY GOOD AT IT! IT MADE ME FEEL CLEVERER AND INSPIRED ME TO MY BEST WORK! The plaque on the door reads ELLIE~!’S PREPPY BITCHING ROOM and similar smashing sounds are heard as if Eleanor is throwing vase after vase into the walls. “I WAS, LIKE, SO CLOSE TO INVENTING A MACHINE THAT WOULD TURN DRAKE MAXWELL’S VOCAL CHORDS TO JELLY SO TAT HE WOULDN’T POLLUTE THE ATMOSPHERE WITH HIS INCESSANT RAMBLING! A WAS MAYBE TWO DAYS MAX AWAY FROM MAKING THE END SEE AGAIN SO HE COULD LAUGH AT JUST HOW STUPID HE LOOKS IN THE FRUITY GAY PORNO IN MASKS GET UP!” The door then opens and Ellie~! appears in the door way, also looking surprised to see the camera. She is wearing a cropped and sleeveless Purple Snapple~! top with Silver lettering that shows off her flat stomach and navel piercing. With his she has a pair of faded Purple denim jeans with Gold stars down each leg. Her long, wavy, Brown hair with subtle Blond highlights is loose and falling down her back and as usual, her wrists are adorned with a chunky watch complimented by numerous bands and bracelets. “You think I did a good job shilling Snapple~! don’t you?” she asks the camera, tilting her head and fluttering the eyelids of her pale Blue eyes to entice sympathy. “What did Xavier do for it? Fair enough he made it the official soft drink of Scientology...But how many people actually follow that? And he may have kept it as the official soft drink of PCW after Alan Kreigman won that honour....But did he inherit Super Genius powers? No, I don’t think he did! I am, like, SO the better Snapple~! shiller....And you know what I am going to challenge him to?” She stands, hands on hips staring at the camera, waiting for an answer and after about 10 seconds she sneers at the lack of any feedback and carries on. “I am going to challenge him to a SHILL-OFF! After Sabotage....Me, him, Snapple~! people in suits and 1 bottle of Strawberry Lime Snapple~! and we will see who gets the license to shill!” she grins evilly at the prospect, inheriting the evilness of Alan Kreigman to make it thoroughly convincing, “After all, he has disgraced the Snapple~! name....Has Ellie~! lost to Seth Masters? No, I don’t believe I have! Has Ellie~! lost 2 straight to Chris Chase...No, I have SO not! I am the best Snapple~! shiller and when I get back to my secret lab I shall build a special machine that transmits holographic images to every living room in the world to Shill even to those who can’t usually be reached to shill...Like Canadians, who all live in wooden huts with no power.” A door down the hallway then opens and Xavier emerges, his hair now tied back neatly and looking a little less wild than he did before. “What are you talking about my little Poon Puppy?” he asks as he walked into the living room and collapses on the sofa and open up a bottle of Snapple~! Apple. Ellie follows him in and looks at the Gold Patron Meritorious drinking the product she is so fond of shilling. “Not much...” Ellie~! replies innocently, “You know, Xavi, maybe if you spent less time DRINKING Snapple~! and actually talking about it then you might not be so far behind me in shilling ability!” she smirks. “Huh?” replies Kannon, wondering what she is on about, “It’s called product placement and it’s a very subtle yet effective way of marketing a product. We familiarise the viewer with Snapple~! Watch it in it’s natural environment, we don’t shove it down their throats, they just see it being drunk and think Hey, I wouldn’t mind getting some of that Snapple~!!” Ellie~! looks unimpressed at Xavier’s methods of selling PCW’s official soft drink, still believing that she should remain the spokesperson. “So you are saying that you just drinking it does more to sell it than me inventing an airborne virus that alters peoples DNA so that only Snapple~! contains the vital Amino Acids needed to build new proteins and therefore live?” she asks, thinking that her point has it won. “No, I’m saying mine won’t get me put in prison or killed by the C.I.A!” he jibes back at her, “Look Ellie~!, you knew the score when you signed the contract, you would shill Snapple~! until I was no longer in the GTT and no-” he sees Ellie about to speak and quickly counters to cut her off, “And no....You can’t build that time machine you keep talking about and have me win my match, I told you not to mess with the space time continuum unless you forget to write down where you’ve been!” Ellie~! crosses her arms in a strop. “Okay then....” she demands, “I challenge you to a SHILL OFF! Me, You, some people and some Snapple~! Whoever can sell the most Snapple~! wins the license to shill!” “Or...” ponders Xavier, trying to think of a way to appease her, “How about this.....Tuesday Turmoil, Cruiserweight Title match.....Xavier Kannon versus Chris Chase, if I win, I go back to shilling Snapple~! in my non-Evil Super Genius way, if I fail to get my Title back, they....” The Scientologist sighs, “You get to carry on shilling Snapple~! with the spirit of Alan Kreigman that you inherited.....Those my terms.” Ellie~! grins and jumps up on the spot, clapping her hands energetically. “YAY ME! she exclaims, “I hope Chase SO kicks your ass!” she adds before realising what she has said, “I mean....Um, not on Tuesday, good luck in winning your Cruiserweight Title back, I mean in the March madness Battle Royal!” again she thinks after she speaks, “Er....I mean sometime else...Anyway, I’m going to prepare for the Shill Off....If it ever happens, not that you’ll lose...I think I might go now...” Ellie~! says, deciding to call it quits while she’s behind/ “Yah....You do that!” Xavier says, shaking his head and sighing at his valet as the shot fades out.
