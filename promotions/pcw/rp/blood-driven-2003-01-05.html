Blood Driven

5 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/05/03 'Urgh.'In a darkened alleyway, the burned man known as Clyde stands over the torn apart body of a young woman. Her throat lies slashed, as his long and sharp blade drips with her blood....he breathes heavily. Clyde: It's not enough. Somewhat annoyed, the demon kicks the corpse. A small bit of blood escapes her, as he grunts. Clyde: Not enough! Bah! In irritation our monster kneels over the girl, slashing her in all directions in a mad fury. Clyde: NEED MORE BLOOD! BLEED DAMN YOU BLEED! However the dead woman simply refuses to cooperate. Clyde: Bah. Footsteps clack through the darkness. Clyde spins around, his eyes blazing their eternal dark fire. Clyde: (Raising his knife) Who's there? Closer, closer still the steps clicker, as the demon waits. He hunches, his blade glimmering in the faint light of the alley....then a shadow emerges, covering the entire area. Clyde: Eh? The massive, dark form engulfs him....then, it gradually grows smaller as it gets closer. Finally a little girl steps forth, prancing and smiling wickedly. Bethany: Lalala. Clyde raises an eyebrow as he looks at her, drenched head to toe in crimson. Clyde: What the hell? Bethany: What? Clyde: How in the name of Baal did you manage such bloodshed? How!? The demon's eight year old concubine grins again, literally dripping blood as she observes Clyde standing next to his victim. Bethany: Oh what's the matter, little Clyde can't get blood to save his rotten second life? Clyde: Shut up. Bethany: It seems in your old age you've just lost it. Clyde: Shut up. Bethany: While I, I can cover myself entirely in the blood of the sheep, while you, you struggle to get blood out of the proverbial dead blonde teenage turnip. Clyde: Shut up. I think she had some sort of clotting disease. Bethany: Yes, the rare and terrible disease called 'Clyde sucks'. Clyde: Bah indeed! I can gain more blood than you in 80 days, I'd bet my collection of bootleg Vic Morrow decapitation videos on it! Bethany: Then I propose a race arond the world. The queen herself shall drop the checkered flag. A raised eyebrow greets the child, courtesy of the burned man. Clyde: What? Bethany: Uh, I mean I propose a race around the block. No checkered flags, just first person to cause more bloodshed wins. Clyde: You're on. The little girl begins to say something else, but is cut off as her monsterous lover bowls over her, charging into the black. Bethany: Oof! SON OF A BITCH!!!!! Painfully, Bethany scrapes herself up and gives chase. A short distance away, Clyde jogs along enjoying his lead. Clyde: Now let's see....ah I've got it, the homeless! Making a sharp left, he sprints towards one of his favorite stomping grounds. A tiny alcove where a flock of homeless people have settled....no matter how many he kills off, they always seem to respawn. Clyde: Heh. Negotiating the ever familiar passages, Clyde hops into the alcove with arms spread in dramatic fashion. Clyde: Honey, I'm home.... His look of evil mirth quickly fades into one of confusion, then anger as he gazes upon row after row of dead men. Their blood coats the ground, running and pooling. Clyde: .....what the HELL!? The killer runs up, sifting through the bodies. He runs his fingers through the rapidly congealing blood, then glances up as mad laughter rings forth from the blackness. Bethany: Mwahaha! Clyde: Bethany! I'll get you for this! Bethany: Maybe, but the race is mine! Clyde: Bah, I still have the old folks home! Bethany: Been there already, dear! Clyde: The schoolyard! Bethany: Dead dead dead! Clyde: The docks? Bethany: Won't be sailing any time soon. Clyde: The used car lot!? Bethany: Bennie's sold his last Yugo. Clyde: BAH~! Bethany: Mwahahaha! Ta ta! The child's voice fades, as Clyde holds his chin. Clyde: How in the hell did she move so fast? Thinking, thinking, the demon attempts to come up with a plan. Clyde: I'm too far behind....can't catch up. Unless.... His eyes light up, as an idea flits through his mind. He chuckles darkly..... A bit away, a line of men and women wait. At the front of it, a middle aged woman stands beneath a familiar sign, manning a cart. Red Cross Aide: Every little bit helps, step right up. With a prick of a needle, the aide takes life from the closest man. Man: (Clutching his arm) Ow! Red Cross Aide: There you go, that wasn't so.... She stops though, as a shadow falls over her. Slowly, she looks up. Red Cross Aide: .....bad. A shriek escapes her lips, as she dives out of the way just in time to avoid being crushed as a huge form falls from above. Splinters fly in every direction as the blood cart crumbles beneath the feet of Clyde, who snatches up a pouch of blood and laughs. Clyde: Mwahahahaha! Red Cross Aide: Aiyeeeeeeeee! Sounding like the Devil himself, the monster cackles as he empties the pouch over his own head. Then another, then another, then another, until the blood covers him entirely. Clyde: Mwahaha....a winner is me. From around the corner stomps Bethany furiously, glaring straight ahead at her mad lover as he chugs blood like they were non-alcoholic beers in the hands of a bald redneck wifebeater. Clyde: Mwahaha.....eh? AGH!!!!! A blade flashes out, sticking Clyde right in the neck. He falls backwards, stumbling and pumping blood into the air as Bethany watches. Bethany: A loser is you. Bitch. The demon's life essence spurts out, falling like rain over the terrified spectators and one very angry little girl. Clyde wanders back towards her, smiling while trying to hold his neck together, and takes a wild swipe at her but misses by a mile. Clyde: Ack. More, more blood escapes the demon, covering our view entirely and causing us to fade to red.....
