First Ever Episode of Why I'm Better Than you are

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-5-04 The camera cuts into a place that looks exactly looks like a setup for a late night show. A oak wooden desk sets angled in the middle of the stage. Behind it sits backdrop for what would seem to be windows set in front of a faux city. From your view to the left of the desk sits three chairs. All of a sudden the camera turns around and pans a live sitting of soldiers packed into about twenty-five rows. All of a sudden the audience begins to cheer as the announcer cuts in. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the first ever edition of… A logo flashed across the screen as the people yell along with the announcer. Announcer and people: Why I’m better than you are! All of a sudden a man in a black suit steps out onto the stage. The man is none other than PCW superstar Kris Kanton. He waves to the crowd as he makes his way over to the desk. Kanton relaxes comfortably in the chair. Kanton: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to our inaugural edition of… Why I’m better than you are! The crowd cheers loudly. Kanton: Our first and only guest of the night is the one and only expert on greatness… Dr. Gustov Van Lickasnatch! The crowd cheers, hoots, and howls loudly as a man in a white labcoat walks onto the stage. He is about average height and small of small build. Obviously he is a weakling. He was raggedy brown hair and wears a large pair of glasses. He smiles as he sits next to Kanton. Kanton: Welcome Dr. Lickasnatch, shall we get started? Lickasnatch: Certainly… Kanton: Lights please! The lights dim as a large white screen drops down behind Kanton and the good doctor. A picture of Kanton posing in wrestling attire can be seen projected onto the screen. The good doctor and Kanton turn around as the doctor points with a laser pointer. Lickasnatch: (pointing the laser around Kanton’s upper body) As we can see here, Kanton is a very large and built man. He can easily intimidate anyone he comes within sight of. His muscularity is almost unreal. It is obvious he spends hours every day perfecting his already perfect physique. Kanton: I am pretty good aren’t I? Lickasnatch: Also he carries himself quite well. He seems to carry an air of self confidence unseen by any man in existence. It’s is amazing. Kanton: Lights please! The lights come back on as the projection turn off and the screen raises back into the ceiling. Kanton: So doctor… Are you saying I’m better than everybody in the world? Lickasnatch: Yes, I am. It is not possible for anybody to be as good looking, as strong, or as smart as you are. Kanton: You heard it yourself folks. It came straight from the doctor’s mouth. A jazz solo cuts in as Kanton is handed an envelope. Kanton: Ladies and gentlemen… THE TOP FIVE REASONS WHY I’M BETTER THAN YOU ARE! The crowd erupts into cheers. Kanton slowly opens the envelope and begins to read it. Kanton: Number five… I have more money than you! Kanton pauses. Kanton: Number four… I’m a better wrestler than you! Again pauses. Kanton: Number three… Women think I’m sexier than you! Pause… Kanton: Number two… I’m a smarter than you! Pause… Kanton: And the number one reason of why I’m better than you are… Drum roll… Kanton: I’m just that damn good! The crowd erupts into another round of cheers. Kanton: Thank you ladies and gentlemen! Once again remember… The crowd yells along with Kanton as he speaks. Kanton and crowd: WHY I’M BETTER THAN YOU ARE! Kanton: Good night everybody! A jazz solo cuts in as Kanton and the good doctor shakes hands. Kanton takes the card and tosses it to the sound of a plane flying through. The camera then cuts out as Kanton looks on smiling.
