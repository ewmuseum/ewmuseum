A History of Revenge

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6th, 2002 Revenge has always been the blood-ridden tactic to payback the enemy for his wrongdoing towards one another. Whether it was a simple practical joke done by a comrade or the beating received by a hated fellow and his gang, revenge suddenly begins to erupt in the mind of the victim. Ever since Blayze was a youngsta back wheelin’ and dealin’ in the hood of Compton, he has managed to get back at all the individuals who either mugged him, tortured him, pranked on him, beat him up etc..Sadly, most of the times, those people either never saw the light or day or had to eat out of a straw for the rest of their pitiful lives. Sometimes payback can get a little out of hand but don’t you feel so refreshed after you know you got your piece of revenge? As a member (and still partly is) of the Crips, Blayze was forced into many different life-threatening situations. Trespassing another gang’s territory meant for warfare and a usual assaulting of one another. Blayze put himself in a predicament that he wishes he never got himself into in the first place, it led to him being stabbed twice on the left shoulder and have a deep wound in the back of his head due to a brass knuckle punch. His gang brothers begged him to let them “cap some asses” but Blayze said, once his wounds healed, he would do the dirty work himself. Only a week later, still feeling the painful effects out the assault, Blayze went on a mission, and within no time, his mission was accomplished. Two members of the “Latin Kings” gang were hospitalized, one with a puncture lung, the other having his middle finger cut off and a stab wound to the forehead. The head leader of the “Latin Kings”, Ilio Corrado Menendez was left laying in a pool of his own blood after receiving 4 inch deep wounds on his back due to machete strikes. Blayze was successful and was never brought to justice due to his gang’s connections. I never regretted my actions towards the “Latin Kings”. Frankly, I wish they all were buried alive instead His real world problems were easily solved with either a bullet to the back or a knife strike to the skull but once he entered the confines of the CCW ring where he furthered his wrestling career, all the haters followed in and out of the ring…. Date: December 2001 2 months after Blayze’s entrance into CCW and the problems had already arisen. Titled as the “stereotype black man”(Hell, he IS Black for Christ’s Sakes), Blayze had a hard time adjusting to the backstage politics and in ring work. Some wrestlers would work a bit stiffer with him then others, he would be the constant topic of gossip because of his past life. Things were not going the way Blayze thought they would be Heh, I got my fair share of revenge on those punks. Some physically, the other, mentally, it taught them a valuable lesson It was evident, Blayze had sort of began to grow an ego or was it just an imaginary piece of fiction created by the backstage gatherers? Everyone saw it with his or her very own two eyes, the one who step foot into CCW as Jerome “Blayze” Jordan was climbing his way, rung by rung, up the ladder of success. Those bullshitters suddenly wanted to gain my respect. I gave them each the one finger salute and never looked back Date: March 2001 It was the dawning of CCW’s soon-to-be famous stable known as the New Strangers and it gave Blayze instant exposure and a shot at glory. Due to his break into the spotlight with the N.S. , Blayze began his tag title quest with later on put him in the record books towards the end of CCW. It was the beginning of a road that would lead Blayze to his destination, his dream, his true astonishing accomplishment.. I always shrugged off the haters and paid them back, not only with my fists but also with my achievements. Those punk ass bastards wished they were in my shoes, too fuckin bad they weren’t and never will be Date: August 2001, Gold & Glory PPV Blayze’s shining moment, his destiny has failed made its arrival. The night of the PPV, pumped up and yes, still surrounded by controversy, Blayze had his mind set on the World Title. After a crucial and tiring fatal four-way, Blayze some how managed to climb the last rung of the ladder and snatched the World Title. The faces of many dropped backstage, there was an air of silence and a bit of nervousness on how this African-American would lead CCW as World Champion. Blayze new his job was done but there was still a bit more of revenge that needed to be accomplished. I thought everything was going the way I wanted it to but dammit, someone just had to try and fuck it up Date: January 2002 – April 2002 It was a journey through the depths of hell for Blayze. A man who had somehow risen from the shadows and beat Blayze for the World Title, a man who later became owner of CCW Enterprises and CCW President, and that man…was Spider. He tried to block all the paths of for Blayze, he shoved him in matches that almost send him to the Hospital, hell, he even fought Blayze for control of the company and Blayze fell short of the victory. The pain and mind games were slowly getting to Blayze, and it was evident Yea, I got my head toyed with and it sucked. Well, you all know the term, “what comes around goes around?”, the tables surely did turn on Spider’s punk ass. Spider took his “presidential powers” to new heights and shoved Blayze into multiple matches, pushing him farther away from a Second World Title. Spider thought he had everything going smoothly for trying to fuck up Blayze’s career and reputation…too bad he did not know he was in for a taste of his own medicine How sweet it was to see the look on his face.. Things suddenly went downhill for Spider. His office? Burnt to crisps by Blayze. His reputation? Ruined by Blayze. His career? Put to a sudden halt by Blayze. Payback was a bitch and so was Spider. It showed to everyone that one-day, the victim will get the last laugh and as Spider looked up at Blayze, Blayze looked down at him from the top of the ladder and laughed in the face of adversity, knowing he conquered it. I thought things were going perfectly..until I signed with PCW As Blayze made an impromptu appearance at Wrestle Vessel, crippling Thug RoC and screwing up Shabazz’s hopes, he knew he had returned to his roots. With Rush on his side, the thuggish duo was ready to dominate as the future of PCW, until Clyde decided to have his own say in all this. The heartless son of a bitch put himself in a predicament where now, he cannot get his rotten carcass out of. On last Thursday’s Turmoil, Rush inserted himself in the middle of a match which feature Clyde and sadly, felt the after affects of the Asphyxiator. Blayze was not there by his buddy’s side as Clyde feasted on the unconscious body of Rush, but on this week’s Turmoil, revenge will be bittersweet. I do not plan on going soft on some darkish lifestyle living imbecile and his follower bitch Darkstar We seem to have a clear indication on how Blayze handles his assailants and no regrets have ever been made. On Turmoil, Blayze has his quest set to send two dark demons to the morgue, revenge will be worse then seen in the past and just maybe Clyde and Darkstar will learn the real meaning of the word “die” A legend in the making? No no, Darkstar’s lucky spring lately will be laid to rest and so will his body and soul. As for Clyde, it seems he is sick of the real world and the ground he walks on, no problem, because I heard the underground does not mind making room for him. The coffins will be set, and I can really care less what Charlie Loc has in his mind for our match but I know, PCW’s darkish “fan favorite” duo might just breathe their last breath, not only in the PCW ring, but on our Earth! Come Tuesday, two men will realise why you do not fuck with a Thug, and that those two men will be..Restin’ With The Angels… Amen..
