Metal Gear Spoofage (part 5)

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/04/02 Sleeping late as usual, eh Snake? Liquid? How the hell are you.... I'm doing just fine, thank you. ...... ....oh, you mean how am I still here. Well, I won't die as long as you live. Solid Snake blinks his eyes slowly, fresh waves of pain rocking his mind. He shakes off the cobwebs to find himself tied before his brother, who stands with arms folded. Snake: Well, looks like your little plan failed. .....and just what the hell were you planning anyway? I never quite caught it. Liquid Clyde: .....must you question everything? What ever happened to spontaneity? Snake: You don't even know, do you. Liquid Clyde: Shut up. I've brought you up here for a reason, dear brother. Snake: To talk me into a coma? Liquid Clyde: No. I want to share something with you. Snake: You and Byron both. Can't you just leave me be and live your own queer lives? Liquid Clyde: .....what? Snake: Uh, nothing. Liquid Clyde: You always were an odd one. Anyway....allow me to tell you about my dream. Liquid looks to the sky slowly, sighing. Liquid Clyde: Back when Dai Lo was alive, warriors such as us were desired. We were needed. But things....ugh, are different now. Shaking his head sadly, Liquid breathes out....then turns slowly, his gaze hardening. Liquid Clyde: WAKE THE HELL UP!!!!! Snake: Huh? Wha? Liquid Clyde: Bah. Can't a man deliver a poignant speech nowadays atop a downed nuclear equipped walking battletank to his hogtied brother without the audience dozing off? Snake: Nope. Liquid Clyde: You make me sick. Snake: I try. Clyde stomps over to his sibling, lifting him off the ground and shaking him. Liquid Clyde: Do you even know why you're here? You bombastic simpleton! Why are you still breathing? Why is the world cursed with your wretched existence!? Snake: (Shaking) Just....my.....dumb....luck....agh....agh....agh.... Liquid Clyde: BAH! Angrily, Liquid chucks Snake facefirst to the cold steel. He skids to a halt, ending up right in front of a familiar looking form. Snake: Amy? Struggling against his tied arms, Solid works himself to a position where he can see Liquid. Snake: Is she dead? Liquid Clyde: How should I know? It's really hard to tell with her, you know, the damned odor doesn't give it away. Snake: (Nodding) I know. Liquid Clyde: Doesn't matter anyway. We shall now battle to the death. Snake: But brother, I don't want to fight you. Clyde looks blankly down at his sib. Liquid Clyde: What do you mean you don't want to fight me? Snake: I mean we're brothers and stuff. I'm not going to fight you and that's that. Liquid Clyde: .....you can't do that! I've waited 30 long years for this moment! Put up your dukes! Snake: No. Liquid Clyde: ....what do you mean 'no'!? Fight me! Or the girl will die! Snake: I have a feeling she's dead already. Liquid Clyde: How do you know this? Snake: She has a large hole in her head. Liquid Clyde: But I didn't do that. Snake looks around shiftily, squirming a little. Snake: No comment. Liquid Clyde: .....why that was rather bastardly of you. But it matters not, you still shall die here. Snake: The Colonel! He'll know what to do! Our hero tries to reach his codec, but cannot make any headway on the ropes. Finally he resorts to throwing himself on his side, mashing his ear against the ground. Snake: Colonel, come in! Colonel: No, not my catholic schoolgirl outfits! You can't! No!!!!! Another voice abruptly cuts off the cross dressing commander. Houseman: Roy Campbell's days of playing dress up are over. This is the Secretary of Defense Ron Houseman. Snake: What have you done with the Colonel? Houseman: He's been arrested for high treason. Snake: Since when is cross dressing high treason? Houseman: Since I said so. Now then, you went and destroyed Metal Gear. That really was the entire reason for sending you in, so that we could retrieve it once you took care of all the assorted goons.....but you couldn't even do that right. You're fired. Snake: No, how will I feed my family? From somewhere behind Ron, Campbell's voice yells out again. Campbell: Don't listen to him! He can't fire you, you're a hundred miles away and don't even work for him to begin with! Snake: Oh yeah! You can't fire me, I'm a hundred miles away and don't even work with you to begin with! Houseman: Damn you Campbell! Well, no matter. I've sent a lovely platoon of bombers to reduce the base to rubble. I'd say you have around two minutes to catch up with your brother. Make it good. And have a pleasant day. With that Ron Houseman signs off....Liquid smirks as he walks over to Snake and removes his ropes. Liquid Clyde: Well my brother, we are the utter definition of the word 'boned'. Snake: Seems that way. So I guess our death fight thing is off. Liquid Clyde: You wish. Snake: Doh. Snake shakily stands up, as his brother cracks his knuckles and paces away from him. Liquid Clyde: You stole everything from me. And now I shall have my revenge. The evil man turns, assuming a fighting stance. Liquid Clyde: Have at you, Snake! Snake: Huh? Liquid Clyde: (Sighing) It means fight me you dolt! Snake: Oh. Liquid Clyde: Gruaaaaaargh! With a shout, Liquid charges at Snake. He throws a punch, which the hero ducks but a swift knee from Clyde knocks him for a loop. Snake: Ow my nose! Liquid: Hahaha! As Snake clutches his face, Liquid Clyde dances around him throwing punches. Liquid: (Punching) I....will....kill....you....dead! Snake: That hurts! In desperation Snake grabs his brother's fist and twists, flipping him over and to the ground. Clyde however sticks his foot out, kicking Solid in the balls then reaches up and slaps him quickly across the face two times. Snake: Ow ow! Liquid Clyde: (Kipping up) Now we'll end this! Snake: What do I do? Searching around, Snake tries to find some measure of fighting back....and finds nothing. He backpeddles as Liquid closes in on him. Liquid Clyde: DIE! Clyde leaps forward with a punch. Before it connects though Snake stumbles backwards, his right foot catching on his left. This causes him to fall, and ironically enough forces Clyde's blow to miss entirely. Liquid Clyde: Whu? The burned man flies through the air from the force of his own whiffed punch, sailing right over the outstretched Snake. Over the side of Metal Gear he goes, shouting a final time. Liquid Clyde: SNAAAAAAAAAKE!!!!!! Snake: WHAAAAAAAAAAT? Liquid Clyde: ......you aren't supposed to answer me you dolt A loud CRUNCH cuts off the terrorist leader. Panting heavily, Snake glances over the side, then lays on his back. Snake: It's finally over. Almost as if in answer to the legend, a series of explosions crack through the ceiling of the structure. Debris plummets towards him, crashing all about in a plethora of carnage. Snake: Crap! Ducking and dodging, Solid Snake hops over to the carcass of Amy who he kneels beside. Snake: Amy, are you gone? Byron: She's gone, Snake. Materializing out of nowhere, the creator of Metal Gear places a hand on Snake's shoulder. Snake: Just because my girl's dead doesn't mean you can move in, big fellow. Byron: .....I was just trying to console you. Snake: Yeah yeah, and the next thing you know you'll be waiting for me to drop the soap. I'm way ahead of you. Byron: That's disgusting! Snake: You're telling me! How could anyone be into stuff like that. Byron: I guess it's their choice.... Snake: I know it's your choice. But mine is mine too, and I wish you'd respect that. Byron: I do respect that! I mean no, I don't....I mean.... Snake stands, putting his own hand upon Byron's shoulder. Snake: I realize you're confused. But most people like you are at first. Byron: What do you mean like me!? Snake: You know, come see come sa. Byron: Huh? Snake: It's alright. We'd better get out of here though, this place is going down. Byron: Yeah. There's a garage near the door, let's head down there. Snake: Just don't try to get me in the backseat of the jeep. I'll be watching. Byron: (Stares) Snake: Stop checking me out and let's go! Solid Snake leaps ahead, behind him Byron sighs and follows. And soon the pair reach the garage, where indeed a jeep awaits. Byron: (Climbing in) Hurry! Snake jumps into the back of the vehicle, Byron revs the motor and the pair blast through a nearby gate. Faster, faster they rush as the legend tries not to be thrown off. Snake: You're gonna get us killed! Byron: We'll be killed anyway if we stop! Just lay down.... Snake: No! Byron: WILL YOU SHUT UP ABOUT MY BEING GAY!?!? Byron blinks....then shakes his head angrily. Byron: I mean I'm not gay! But still shut up about my being gay! I mean....argh!!!!! The tunnel races by, accelerating greatly....then Snake's eyes widen as a second car speeds up behind them. Liquid Clyde: Not yet, Snake....it's not over yet! Byron: AAAAAAH CRAP! HANG ON!!!! The scientist stomps on the gas pedal, sending the jeep screaming forward. However Liquid follows just behind, aiming a rifle at Snake's head. Liquid Clyde: Now youAGH! Before the man can hit the trigger his head bounces off a low overhang in the tunnel. His jeep spins madly out of control, he barely keeps from wrecking it as Snake looks on. Snake: He's coming again! Liquid Clyde: (Driving up beside Snake) You'll pay for thatOOF!!!!! Clyde winces and doubles over in agony as his jeep runs over a bump in the road and sends him bouncing crotch first into the gear shift. Snake: Ooh. Liquid Clyde: (Speeding up) YOU'RE FINISHED!!!!! Byron: LOOK OUT!!!!! Liquid swerves his jeep directly in front of his brother's, causing it to crash into his with a horrible screech of metal. All three men fly through the air, out of the entrance to the tunnel and into the all consuming snow. Shaking off the cobwebs, Solid Snake looks about to find himself pinned face first underneath his jeep. Beside him Byron shares a similar fate. Solid: Byron, you okay? Byron: Yeah, I think so..... Solid: Stop touching my ass. Byron: I am not touching your.... Snake: Uh oh. Solid looks up in shock as a bloody figure stalks towards him. From the wreckage of his jeep, shirtless, wounded but stalking on like a revenge crazed zombie, Liquid Clyde marches towards him with his rifle still firmly clutched in his hand. Liquid Clyde: (Moaning) Snake.....Snake..... Snake: Uh.....dammit I can't move! Byron: Me either. The terrorist at last reaches his brother....takes aim with the gun. Liquid Clyde: (Moaning) Snake..... Snake: Don't worry, he'll be dropping dead of a heart attack any secon..... A loud BLAM cuts off the warrior, as Byron's head disintegrates from a rifle shot. Solid Snake looks to him blankly. Snake: Well, I'm sure the deux ex machina won't allow me, the HERO to die.....right? Right? RIGHT Another BLAM, and the legendary Solid Snake's brains splatter over the side of the jeep. He falls limply to the snow, his eyes eternally open in a stupid dazed expression. Liquid Snake looks at him for a second....then limps beside him. He sits in the snow.....prods his brother's head. Liquid Snake: I never liked you anyway. And as a slow, evil laugh rises from the terrorist, we mercifully fade to black...... The End Hosselot: Yes sir, the entire unit was wiped out. Yes sir, in the end, Liquid proved to be superior. Yes, I am in shock as well. Yes, he had very little in the way of competition, I recognize this. No, no one knows who I really am. Yes, Asa Fountain knew who I was, but he got six bullets in the lung for his troubles. No, no one knows you are the third brother. Yes sir. Thank you, goodbye. Mr President. The image fades.....then a controller flies through the air, crashing through a television set. We pan back to see an irate man snarling from his position on the couch. Clyde: You mean there'll be a SEQUEL to his idiotic story? And as we look upon his hate filled face, we mercifully fade to black......
