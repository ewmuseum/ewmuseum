Youngsters and the music they play theese days

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-4-02 RP 2 Turmoil 5th RP 1 Fusion 8th It’s Friday night. It’s cold, raining and flat out boring. Teresa left the arena an hour ago in hopes to celebrate her big win against Brandi. Front Office Princess, what does she do anyway? Teresa crosses the city street at the crosswalk. She half chuckles thinking to herself she was once associated with the office personnel, but that was Indy days and she still had a lot to learn about this dog eat dog business. There’s not much to say about those times except all se really done was paperwork and wore the name ‘Commish’ on her office door. Naturally the city streets are busy on Friday nights. The late night rain didn’t hold anyone back it seemed. You may be thinking it is dangerous for a woman to be walking city streets after dark. Teresa handles men twice the average man’s size most every week. She’s sure she can handle the Friday night mugger with a pervert mind and a psychotic mind. As said before, her hopes was to celebrate her upsetting win, well upsetting to Front Office, but what do they care? Just one tiny match which will be nothing compared to what’s at stake Tuesday. Either Walden will become Teresa’s personal valet for a month or she’ll be typing paperwork and escorting Walden to the ring. This should be quite a challenge with Teresa and HIT’s determination against Brandi and Walden’s ability to cheat. Teresa now finds herself passing several clubs with huge neon signs and band posters taped to the windows in attempt to lure customers. As of yet Teresa hasn’t found anything of interest. She isn’t looking very hard either as her mind continues to drift into millions of thoughts. Then something happens. AHHHH!!! The scream causes Teresa to jump back almost bumping into someone. A heavy bass riff follows the scream with guitar sounds backing it up. She finds herself nodding to the beat and she forgets everything she was thinking about. A young man’s voice is heard singing and she allows the music to lead her inside the club and she doesn’t stop walking until she is leaning against the wall near the stage. The band consists of the young man singing lead. He wears baggy black jeans and a long sleeved black shirt that has silver ninja straps running down the sleeves. Over his shoulder is a black and silver Les Paul Gibson. A drummer with slicked back blonde hair sits in the back. A short and slim female with shoulder length brown hair plays a red 'Flying V' five string, and a normal height guy with spiky brown hair plays lead guitar. Patiently waiting by the phoneHoping that you will call me homeThe pain inside my love deniedHopes and dreams swallowed by prideEverything I need it lies in you ‘Cause I’m broken I know I need you now‘Cause deep inside I'm broken You see the way I liveI know I know your heart is brokenWhen I turn awayI need to be brokenTake the pain away The lead singer catches Teresa staring at him as he performs and sings directly into her eyes getting the crowd cheering louder. I question why you chose to dieWhen you knew your truth I would denyYou look at meThe tears begin to fallAnd all in all faith is blindBut I fail time after timeDaily in my sin I take your life The young man turns away from Teresa’s direction and pauses allowing the rest of the band have their moment while he entertains with his own guitar. It seems as though his whole body is becoming one with the music. He then returns to the microphone singing the last verse very slow. All the hate deep insideSlowly covering my eyesAll these things I hideAway from you againAll this fear holding meMy heart is cold and I believeNothing’s gonna changeUntil I'm broken The song ends with the words ‘Until I’m broken screamed loudly several times. Before the song is over, the club crowd is on their feet so to speak and screaming the roof off. The band seems pleased for doing such a good show. All but the young man step behind stage. He actually jumps off stage and approaches Teresa. I never would’ve thought I’d see the day I would be standing in front of Teresa Tomas. I couldn’t help but notice how much you liked our stuff. Can I buy you a drink? Teresa looks the young man up and down then laughs softly. No offence kid, but aren’t you a bit young to even be in a club like this? Yeah, I did your shit. I hope to hear more of you in the future. The young man pulls out his ID and walks to a bar. He comes back with two beers handing one to Teresa. What was that about my age? I’m 22 thank you and I hope to see more of you amongst our live audiences in the future. The two find an empty table and sit across from each other. Teresa can’t get over how young the guy looks. Looks can be deceiving she supposes. Mind if I take a look at your ID? I want to see how old you really are. The young man shrugs and pulls his ID out of his wallet and places it on the table. It reads his name as Adam McAllister and it states he is 22 years old. Teresa examines the laminated card another minute and grins. She hands the card back and shakes her head before taking a swig of her beer. She can feel the cold sensation travel down her throat. I know a fake ID when I see one Adam. How long have you been going along with this? Adam’s face blushes a little and he places the card back in his wallet. Teresa notices he hasn’t touched his beer. Don’t tell anyone please? We’ve been doing this for a few months now, with the fake ID and all. I’m just 16. Suddenly, Teresa starts laughing holding her stomach. She pauses, closes her eyes and takes a deep breath. When she opens her eyes, she sees Adam looking non-to happy. I’m sorry kid. Didn’t mean to offend you. I just find it humorous that so many bar owners can’t catch such a phony ID. But hey, you’re entertainment for the night and your band is what brings in customers, I guess they don’t care. Adam combs his fingers through his chin length hair. Don’t worry about offending me Teresa. I thought you were laughing at my age. I get a lot of that you know. Anyway, I’ve been wanting to know something lately. I’ve been keeping up with you ever since your early days in NTICW. Been a huge fan of yours and Scott Taylor. I just want to know how you two are doing now? Teresa stares blankly at Adam. She places her half-empty bottle on the table. She hasn’t even thought about Scott in a month or two and she is sure he hasn’t thought about her. I guess you can say they have gone their separate ways. Sure she misses him, but a woman has to do what a woman has to do. To her, Scott made his decision when he signed himself to that Reagan woman in OSW. She says nothing for what seems the longest time and finally shakes her head. Are you going to even drink your beer? Adam shakes his head worried he said something wrong and slides his unopened beer to Teresa. Teresa pops the top, takes a long swig and leaves the half-empty bottle beside her own half-empty bottle and stands. She slides a few large card-like papers out of her inside jacket pocket and a pen. After jotting something on the back of one, she hands them to Adam and leaves without a word. Adam sees Teresa has handed her ringside tickets to Turmoil and on the back reads: Enjoyed our chat. Loved your music. Now I hope you enjoy my show.
