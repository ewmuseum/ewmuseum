Legacy of Hessain Part 2 - Redemption

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/12/02 Recap: Having been murdered for seemingly no reason, a nobleman from the town of Coorhagen was been given a second shot at life by the Necroprankster, Morgazius. Resurrecting him as Hessain, a vampire, revenge is the only thought on his mind as he awakes inside a stone coffin... Legacy Of Hessain: Part 2 - Redemption I sat up in my coffin. I went to make the gasp of air that any human would have clawed their way through stone for, but no breath was to be drawn. I was no longer human... just a creation of Hell, the Necroprankster's masterpiece. Regardless, I hungered. I hungered for blood, and for revenge. But one at a time... I leapt out of my coffin with strength that no man could have known in life. My body was not only rejuvenated, but enhanced! I stood at a height that normal men could not fathom and my boots ground of the stone floor like a potent mill. The armour that Morgazius had given me was forged of hellfire and iron, as was the sword that was once driven into my back that now clung to my hand as my hand clung to it. Passing a pool of water, I soon realized that I was not completely restored. As the lightning outside flashed, the ghoul appeared and reflected... my skin had been displaced from the greater part of my face, leaving a tatter around the eyes and chin. My boney mask, teeth and all, was my price. But I would extract an even greater price soon! As I emerged from the Mausoleum, the night's touch on my skin cooled but not calmed me. Indeed, the lunar glow that illuminated the gravestones around me was empowering my being. I was reborn, stronger and with the moon's kiss, I was at full strength. And then... the time had come. I trudged down the gravel path, out of that crypt, that charnel-house... that womb. I could smell the scent of wine in the air and heard voices to the north. I instantly recognized the voices as those of my killers. My red hair and beard began to burn brightly with contempt and rage - I knew it was those who had sealed my fate and now, those who's fate I would seal! Hunger and weakness are no bar to vengeance's call. I needed blood and knew precisely where to find it... I would find my slayers, and send them back. - whence I came! I marched around the path and next to a campfire, those brigands were celebrating... these scoundrels could not afford wine of that magnitude - had my death been pre-arranged and paid for? Were these good for nothings rewarded for my demise?! Such skullduggery! In an instant, I charged at them, my blade in my hand and their death in my eyes! The resulting frenzy would haunt be throughout time... 'What trickery is this!?' 'There he is!''I thought we killed you once, bastard!''Lousy cur... if we put you down once, we can do it again!' I had crossed death for this moment. My mind was empty, save for one thought - I WOULD KILL! I swung my sword, eviscerating the first of these fools. His innards dropping to the ground shook their stomachs, showing that this was no illusion. The second lost his head in the blink of an eye. The third turned to run, but his drunken stupor brought him to a rock, crashing his chin on the ground. The final one, I strangled to death with my bare hands, crushing the dog's windpipe like a blade of grass. Then, as the drunken fool tried to stand, I took my time, slashing his body open in four strokes. 'VAE VICTUS!' I yelled, once more demanding suffering to the conquered. I gestured a hand towards him and like magic, the blood flowed from his body into my mouth like a manipulated fountain.There is no greater release than that from vengeance sated. With my assassins dead, my quest was over. Or so I though. The chirping of another force boomed into my skull... 'Easy, Tiger! These nobodies were just the instruments of destruction, not the cause. Look to their main-event masters. Look to the Pillars, and gain way to the Fortress Of The Mind...' I knew not what the Necroprankster spoke of... was a higher power involved? Would this tangled web be consuming me further? I pressed onwards, through a woodland area. An ordinary man would have feared the darkness, or whatever lurked in it. Be it owl, wolf or monster. As I was who I was, I knew that the food chain in this forest had a new master. The walk would have also seemed to be long, but time passes quickly for those who have eternity. I neared the first sign of civilization... which left a bitter taste in my mouth, even after the blood had taken it's grip on my tongue. It was a wooden sign post, painted with an arrow and the word 'Ziegsturhl'. This is where the bloody deed of my murder had taken place. Ziegsturhl was a place I would have to pass through on my way to The Pillars, but first, I needed to take a little detour... * * * Knock knock knock. 'We're closed for the night, stranger. I open for nobody.' Suddenly, the barman's face widened and his ruddy cheeks opened to display the shock as his bar was broken by the door. Ripped from it's hinges, I advanced and slung it into the counter, watching as the iron splintered the wood and sending ale across the floorboards. And standing petrified in one corner, was the man who turned me away. A heartless man who would not spare one mug of ale. With one swish of my sword, I sent the remaining ale across the floor, ruining his business. Then, like those oak casks, I set to destroy him. With one punch, I ploughed my fist into his ribs, then, with an almighty heave, made him literally heartless. With my known wrongdoers suitably dispatched, I settled myself in the tavern, as I should have so many nights ago... and had a quiet, peaceful drink. There is no rest for a vampire who needs no sleep... but reclining for a moment had not been forbidden by Morgazius. I placed my feet on the bar and enjoyed a little red tipple. After all, it's commonplace to find people almost drinking to live inside a tavern these days.
