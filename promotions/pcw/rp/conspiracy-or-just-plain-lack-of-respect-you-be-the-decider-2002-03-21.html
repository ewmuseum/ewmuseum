Conspiracy, or just plain lack of respect... You be the decider...

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-21-02 --- Hank apartment is quiet in the first time in years. Hank is lounging out on the couch, head slumped into his chest. Hank is wearing baggy black JNCO jeans, white wife beater, long black leather trench coat, and his hair a mess all over his black leather couch. Janelle enters and Hank looks over with a slight smile. 'Hey babe, how's it goin'?' In a depressed tone. Janelle smiles at him and walks into the back bedroom she shares with Hank and closes the door. Hank then searches around him for the remote. He leans forward and pries the remote from under him right as Ganker enters with a big grin on his face.--- 'Hey man!' Ganker shouts out in excitment. Stroking his chin. 'I've been better.' 'What's the deal wit' yo Mr. Bummer?' taking a seat in a black leather recliner, flipping on the flat screen. 'Ah man, I don't know it's just the fact that PCW uses me as their lap jobber. I am jobbed to the weakest opponents, I have only dragged yo down man.' sighs and slouches deeper into the couch. 'I can't believe I was so blind not to see this sooner. I am such a dummy.' Places his hands over his face and sighs. Ganker laughs as he flips the channels a little bit and comes to some SoBe commercial that Hank did awhile back, two weeks ago to be exact. 'Yo have commercials and fame, that's why yo in PCW ain't it?' gets up and walks into the kitchen for a soda. Hank sits up a little bit more than he was and looks at the television to a showing of MTV cribs he did. 'No man, I'm in PCW for the competition, but I obviously am not respected throughout the backstage. I am an original to the PCW and have been wit' it through thick and thin. They treat me like sh*t man.' sighs. Sips his soda. 'Yeah man, they treat me like sh*t jus' for bein' yo friend.' Walks back and sits in his black leather seat as Janelle walks out in a pair of dark blue jeans and a black hoody. Hank looks up and over at Janelle and grins and straightens up. 'Hey baby, I missed yo today.' Pats his leg and grins at Janelle. 'Why don't yo come over here and sit on my knee and tell me all bout yo day.' Walking over and sitting on Hank's leg and laying against his chest. 'I went to the mall and looked around, but you know I didn't have enough for the top I wanted.' Sighs and smiles as she rubs her head over Hank's chest. Looking like he's going to be sick, Ganker turns his head. 'Can yo both please not do that in my pressence?' Laughing, Hank looks over at Ganker. 'No, jus' cause it makes yo sick.' Continues to laugh as he then kisses Janelle to make Ganker even more nauticious. 'Sucka dog!' Getting up and walking back into the kitchen for a bag of potato chips. 'I hate yo guts Hank!' Sighing Hank responds. 'I know boyo, but who doesn't?' Laughing. 'Good point.' Grinning and laughing. 'Like I always say, or pretty much jus' came up wit' in like a split second. I'm a jackass, I'm an a**hole, but everybody loves me jus' the same.' Walking back into the living area and sitting back down with a bag of chips and a new soda. 'Don't we have a match against John Crichton and Andy Murray for Fusion?' Questioning tag team partner. 'Yeah, we do, but we probably be paid to job the match like usual. I feel horrible for acceptin' the money, but I only want the best for my girl Janelle here.' (smiles at Janelle) 'As long as you're happy is good enough for me baby.' Janelle replies. Ganker adds, 'What about Sabotage?' Annoyed Hank responds to Ganker's petty question. 'Don't yo do anything but ask stupid questions?' Ganker shrugs as Hank sighs. 'Well yeah dumbsh*t we do have a match for Sabotage too.' 'Cool!' Ganker replies. Directed at Hank, 'Actually you are not booked for Sabotage babe.' Janelle replies. 'I feel so used by PCW, I haven't even been given a rematch against Jason Core for my PCW Extreme Championship. The title I should have never lost in the first place.' Hank sighs. 'I think I should be next in line for a shot at that PCW Extreme Championship man.' Ganker replies. Janelle adds, 'I'm sure whoever gets that Extreme Title shot between the two of you will make Jobbers Inc proud by recapturing the title.' Janelle smiles at Hank. Ganker is about to puke, 'Yo two make me sick.' 'I know, and I love it!' Hank replies in a laughing tone. --- The segment cuts out to black. ---
