'Dear Diary'

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 November With the tangible smell of polished oak, we open within the base of operations of Primetime Championship Wrestling's dynamic duo, the collective known throughout Wrestle Tattler as Pranks With Attitude. The residence we've seen a thousand times before, it's owner, one Gaz 'The Jester' Green, its location, his native Las Vegas Nevada or, as he prefers, The City Of Sin. The living quarters sat in an eerie silence, desolate and uninhabited with no signs of life. Everything appeared in place yet there was no Gaz Green, no Noscosto Ireul Nerezza nor Natalia Miyagawa, a peculiar backdrop for an opening scene. With the Tag Team Champions somewhat engaged, missing, vanished, no-where too be found the camera roamed the luxurious suite, capturing the Jester's personal furnishings, showing a man of taste, a man who appreciates the finer things in life. By-passing an electrical utopia, the camera wandered over to a desk where a book lay open, it's pages scribbled with wet ink, notably the handwriting of Gaz Green. 'What's the significance of this book?' 'Has the Jester become an author?' 'Or does this span a little deeper, is this the collective thoughts of Gaz Green, a diary of events?' Either way it is rude to pry into something that doesn't concern you, but with Alliances done and dusted, revelations revealed and a three hour event of corruption to digest, maybe some light would be shed with nothing more than a peep. Maybe the word 'Shed' was a little vague looking back over the fateful night of November 24th. With so much happening over the course of just three hours, we could simply turn our backs and wait for the rumour mill to flood or unearth the very truth from the Jester's standpoint. 'What does Pranks With Attitude think of the re-shuffling of the roster?' 'How will this influx of talent influence P.W.A's Championship tenure?' 'What will happen to the bond of Team Bastardos?' With more questions than answers, the camera zoomed in with curiosity showcasing the lettering to a scale that allowed those watching to read and so began the innermost thoughts of one PCW comic-.... Dear Diary, It has been twenty-four hours since Alliances and after a gruelling night that has seen every human emotion physically possible displayed on the faces of those who made the event simply eye-candy. What a way to end the year, huh?!? My life has always been a roller coaster and this week saw me hit its very depth after losing to a loser. Who would have thought that I, Gaz 'The Jester' Green would bow out of the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament in only the second round, humiliated at the hands of-... Heavy Metal Harry. I mean, I'm not a sour loser, well, maybe I am but beaten by a greasy haired, gibberish talking hippy-.... Hmmm, take a deep breath or better still count to ten. Where was I? Oh, anyway that was Tuesday Turmoil, what a start to the week. After taking a few steps backwards in our preparation for the Fatal Four-Way Elimination Match, Nerezza thought that it would be a good idea to show our faces-... No-... Make an impact at Friday Night Fusion. There's me thinking we made an impact? That was merely a bubble in this volcanic lava pit as the real eruption was saved for the Pay-Per-View but I'll get to that in due course. So with a little bargaining power, well our tax paid up front, those above allowed for us to commentate during the Main Event where all four members of Playaz Inc. squared off against one-another. That's right - Statuz Quo vs. Dante Jackson vs. Ali Khadafi vs. Duzza It was like the squabbling of Ja Rule fans, fighting aimlessly, proving nothing more than their incompetence but it did however make for interesting television going into Alliances. The bout became a little more interesting when four combatants became six, hell Nerezza and myself where twitching to give the people a sample of what to expect during the Tag Team Championship match and we never disappoint. SMACK~! BANG~! CRASH~! Well you get the message-... So I got a little carried away-... So I got carried away with a steel chair but its been well documented that there has been no love loss and just blood loss between Pranks With Attitude and Playaz Inc. Well that was Friday, forty-eight hours before Alliances and we made a statement-.... So we pushed our weight around, big deal, it was nothing more than what Xavier Kannon does week in and week out but unbeknownst to us, well, everybody was that our very own CEO had gone far further than any of his predecessors. Enough of Front Office banter for a moment because what is important is that P.W.A done a repeat of the November 15th episode of Friday Night Fusion. November 15th? The No Ladder Match! The very same contest where we retained our Championships against the best in the business, this time however we did it during a much larger occasion, Alliances 2002. All right they were two different situations but a successful title defence meant we, as in Pranks With Attitude are still on course to be beating the longest reign set by Politically Incorrect. So we won, or did we? I think there was only one winner last night. He may have lost in his bid to win the Universal Championship from Angelo Deville, but no one can deny that Xavier Kannon rocked the very foundations of Wrestle Tattler, and on a side note Alexander Miyagawa, WHAT?!? I took away his health, well Nerezza did, but I masterminded the moment, I took away his career and Natalia all in one fateful night, only for him to return with Team OSW (Ohmygodthatfederationreally Sucksat Wrestling). From one surprise to another, if the returning Miyagawa wasn't enough nor almost scoring a pin fall over Tyler Nelson then Xavier Kannon's purchasing of RUSH must surely send pulses racing. YES, XAVIER KANNON, PCW CEO AND MY BOSS NOW OWNS RUSH Gulp springs to mind. I can't believe that I'm writing twenty-four hours after running a Pay-Per-View gauntlet about whether or not I have a job and where will I fit in. Who would've thought that the PCW Tag Team Champion would be worried about his job, worried that some RUSH superstar hasn't had the go ahead to fill my boots, or that I'm being forced out by this new crop of talent. Chris Mathews Stimpo Black Dragons The new guys in the back, the new guys on the pay roll. Well with so much happening I'm going to conclude, probably best since I'll have loads to rant and write about after Wednesday's Turmoil. Anyway this game is all about risks and playing the right hand, well it just happens I have a trump card yet to play and maybe, just maybe it's time to, 'Dance with the Devil' Or perhaps, 'Deal with the Devil' As the camera sways over the final sentence, a difficult sentence to digest it hints towards an area of Primetime Championship Wrestling that Pranks With Attitude have yet to venture, dealing with the devil incarnate, CEO Xavier Kannon. Focusing on the word 'Devil' in the latter sentence, the camera zoomed out, blurring the words that make up this diary allowing the scene itself to draw to a close. - The End -
