It's Sunday, Father's Day, and here I am in Japan trying to wrestle for PURE. And El Diablo STILL won't leave me alone!

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/15/02 Taking the taxi to Ikusa, The End is already in his ring attire, his Junior Heavyweight Belt slung over his shoulder. In America, there might be some worry about someone trying to steal a champion's belt if he'd walk around with it in public, but here in Japan, the public respects its wrestlers enough to give them their space, and certainly enough that they wouldn't try to steal and pawn a championship title. Along with The End is his film producer, El Diablo of PCW. El Diablo is following The End to make sure that he doesn't get held up in Japan to return to the states to work on El Diablo's movie, and to make sure that The End doesn't miss his PCW tag match with El Diablo against PCW Hall of Famer The Chef and PCW rookie Alexis Parshin (who as intimidating as he may be physically will never get very far in life if he retains his feminine name). El Diablo:'So, why are you in Japan again?' The End doesn't respond, just holds his Pure Junior Heavyweight Title in El Diablo's face. El Diablo:'Nice shiny belt. Where'd you get it?' The End sighs. End:'Ugh. It's from PURE. That's why we're in Japan. I've got a title defense tonight.' El Diablo smells the belt. El Diablo:'Seems to be real gold. They might be an actual federation. Who's running it?' End:'Miyagawa.' El Diablo scratches his head. El Diablo:'Who?' The End shakes his head in disappointment that his producer's mental state is so degraded. End:'Remember that guy that Kid Wonder killed and then gave the title to?' El Diablo:'No...' The End thinks for a second. End:'Oh yeah, he jobbed to Bad Battio.' El Diablo:'Yes! Now I remember him! No one could forget that debacle. So, he's running his own federation, is he? No wonder he's not seen so much in PCW. You think that's why he jobbed the PCW Extreme Title to Ellie?' The End shrugs and points to the camera in the front passenger seat. End:'Perhaps, but let's not worry too much about trivial matters such as that. Are you sure you're up to wrestling men like Chef and Alexis Parshin on Turmoil?' El Diablo double-takes, looking wide-eyed at The End and snapping his fingers. El Diablo:'Chef! I thought we were wrestling Ultimate Warlord and Boca Del Inferno! Darnit! I have to fire that personal assistant.' The End smirks and taps the back of his fingers against the closed taxi window. End:'That wasn't your personal assistant, that was Ricky Thunder.' El Diablo:'I don't know any Ricky Thunder.' End:'You do, you just never remember.' El Diablo:'I'm a busy man! I can't remember everyone who comes up to me. But onto this matter with Chef and... who?' End:'Alexis Parshin.' El Diablo:'I'm fighting ANOTHER woman? I just got finished beating Heaven Rayne.' The End cracks his neck and knuckles, eagerly anticipating his upcoming PURE match. He still has to deal with El Diablo until he gets to the arena, where he's notified the security not to let the Mexican Devil into the building. End:'Alexis is a large man from Uzbekhistan.' El Diablo:'Is that near Roscoe's Chicken and Waffles?' End:'... Yes.' El Diablo:'Oh, Alexis! I wouldn't say large. He's just fat. About 350 pounds of fat. But somehow, a fat drag queen prostitute with a red wig gets a lot of business these days.' The End and Derek eye El Diablo suspiciously. El Diablo:'What? I was lost! I needed directions!' El Diablo changes the subject back to his original point. El Diablo:'Anyway, I think it would be best if you softened them up for me. You're bigger and stronger than me, so I'll let you beat them up first, and then you can tag me in and I'll finish up.' The End adopts a tone that matches is words exactly. End:'Oh, really?' El Diablo turns to Derek, The End's cameraman for PURE. El Diablo:'Where were you for work last Wednesday? We were trying to shoot RAH and Aimz's sex scene, and you weren't around to film anything!' End:'As I recall, there was Nerezza running away from Jaden for running into the women's locker room without pants on. I don't know what she was going to do to him.' El Diablo:'Yes... well, we still didn't have a cameraman.' Derek:'What are you talking about? I've never met you before in my life!' El Diablo:'No, I know who you are. You're Ricky Thunder!' The End pats El Diablo on the shoulder to calm him down. End:'You can never remember Ricky Thunder when you see him, yet everyone else you meet you call them Ricky Thunder?' El Diablo:'... Well, we were still trying to film the sex scene.' End:'What are you talking about? Nerezza ran away from Jaden until she attacked Heaven, and the only thing that you were involved in with RAH and Aimz was that you followed them after they left the arena from that awful meeting that you call a rehearsal.' El Diablo waves off the comment. El Diablo:'Whatever. So, what're we doing in Japan again?' The End leans back in his seat and folds his arms. End:'... I'm here to officiate a Japanese Sushi Pie-Eating Contest.' El Diablo stops for a second, and then answers. El Diablo:'Well, why didn't you say so? Who are your opponents?' End:'Takumi Umeshita, Charlie Loc, and The HIT. They'll be on the other side of the ring on the same team. Whomever pins me wins the Light-Heavyweight Title.' El Diablo:'Will you have partners?' End:'In a way. Jordan X Shabazz and Brian Simon.' El Diablo:'You mean Diddy Kong and Greg Carmichael?' The End shakes his head in the negative. End:'No, Greg Carmichael is Syn.' El Diablo:'Then who's Brian Simon?' End:'No one.' El Diablo:'Okay then.' El Diablo also leans back into this seat. The taxi weaves through traffic as it makes its way to the arena where The End shall defend his Junior Heavyweight Title yet again, and hopefully, come back home with it. After about twenty seconds, El Diablo leans forward again, excited. El Diablo:'Hey! Want to see the new trailer for the movie?' End:'How can you already have a trailer for a film you haven't even starting shooting?' El Diablo holds his hands up to his face and then moves them apart in a rainbow motion. El Diablo:'Magic. So, want to watch it?' The End takes off his mask, showing El Diablo his blank eyes. El Diablo:'Right, that whole blindness thing. Keep forgetting that.' El Diablo turns to the camera held by Derek. El Diablo:'Tell you what, just roll the footage anyway.' Derek shakes the camera left and right in the negative. Derek:'What footage? This is just a digital camera, it's not hooked up to anything.' El Diablo stops again. El Diablo:'Want to be in my movie?' Derek: 'No!' El Diablo:'Fine, just be sure to show up when we start filming once we get back to the states.' Derek keeps the camera close on El Diablo, but turns to The End and whispers, 'What?' to him. The End, his head turned away from everyone out of the window, just shrugs and mouths the words, 'Just humor him.' El Diablo taps the driver on his shoulder. El Diablo:'Can you go any faster? The End and I are very busy men and we need to get to the airport as quickly as possible.' End:'I haven't wrestled at PURE yet!' El Diablo:'PURE, what's that?'
