Semore checking into rehab!

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 3, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a sun filled day with little to no clouds out and about. The sun is high in the sky, hinting that the time of day is somewhere near noon. Semore Smoke enters the scene wearing a white dress shirt, and white jeans. He's wearing his normal Timberland Boots, and is wiping sweat from his forehead as he enters the scene. The scene, which had been a downtown street in Semore's hometown of Wilson, North Carolina, shifts and shows Semore standing in front of a building marked with a sign that says, 'Rehab Facility'. Semore doesn't look happy to be standing in front of the rehab facility and he turns to his left and yells out at someone who we can't see… [Semore Smoke]Are you sure that's what will impress the judge!? {{{{A man walking down the sidewalk past several people who are walking down as well going somewhere. The man looks at Semore and you can see his face, it's none other then Semore's old friend, and current NTICW President, Mick Palmer. Mick looks both ways and crosses over towards the side of the road where Semore is. Three cars whiz by after he crosses the road and then the traffic seems to stop around that area, but you can still hear the sounds of cars and people yelling that you would hear in any city or town. Mick looks at Semore and then up at the building and says…}}}} [Mick Palmer]I would think it's the best way for you to get over with the judge. It always looks good to see someone who's gone to rehab and has tried to change his ways. But, I just need to ask you one question, you're not lying to the rest of the PCW, WrestlingTattler.com world, and I when you say that you weren't the one smuggling in drugs, but you were framed? {{{{Semore looks at Mick with a scolding look on his face. He then looks at the building and says…}}}} [Semore Smoke]Look, Mick, you know me better then just about anyone. Yes, I may be a pothead, but I wouldn't be dumb enough to try and smuggle weed into the country from Jamaica. I know for a fact that the airports have extra security set up that would pick something out like that easily. {{{{Mick looks at Smoke for a second with a puzzled look on his face and then says…}}}} [Mick Palmer]Well if you weren't smuggling in anything, then why did news reports say you were acting suspicious when you were pulled aside? {{{{Smoke answers with a hint of anger in his tone…}}}} [Semore Smoke]Look man, I thought you'd believe me out of all people in the world. I didn't do it, ok. I wasn't acting 'suspicious' as the report says. That's just a half-truth or lie that the reporters love to throw out to make the reports sound and look real and legit. Look, let's just go inside, sign me up and get today's session over with, ok? [Mick Palmer]All right. {{{{The two men walk towards the two story building, which sits in between a convenient store and a bank. Smoke walks up to the front door and pulls it open. He then walks into the lobby room and sees several people seated in the waiting room, and a few more signing up for the session today. Smoke looks around the room, which is painted a bright blue with clouds and birds to signify harmony. Smoke walks into the room and feels chills run down his spine as he feels the vibes of the room, people trying to quit their addictions by meeting each other. Smoke had been to similar functions in the past, but none of them had ever helped him with anything, in fact the stories that he had heard at the time gave him more ideas, and also made him want the marijuana, which he smoked, more. Mick, who will be his witness for the sessions walks in behind Smoke and seems to feel the same vibes and chills that Smoke felt. He shrugs them off easily, considering he had a similar obsession with marijuana, but had gotten over it as a kid, for Semore, it was too late for that. It had become a part of his life, but this session wasn't to break his obsession, but to make the judge sympathetic and feel as if he was actually trying to break it by going to sessions. He would quit right after the trial the following week. But before that day he had to go through four sessions of listening to 'horror' stories as some of them called it, and he knew he would leave with an obsession for his pipe when he got home. Smoke stands in the line for sign ups, which only has two more people to go before him. The person signing up now is wearing a business type suit, so he doesn't look like someone with an obsession, but he looks like someone who might be a lawyer or a banker. The man finishes signing up and walks into the waiting room with the rest of the helpless addicts. A woman, who is right in front of Smoke is next, she signs the piece of paper and answers a few questions like the other man did. She has long flowing blonde hair, a purple blouse, with a pair of Lee blue jeans. Smoke looks at her as she walks away and notices that she has piercing blue eyes, and a beautiful smile. Smoke is next, the secretary behind the desk hands him the clipboard and asks…}}}} [Secretary]So you're here for to sign up for today's session, do you plan on joining us for any other sessions this coming week? {{{{Smoke signs the paper on the clipboard and then looks up to answer the woman's question as he hands her back the clipboard…}}}} [Semore Smoke]Yes, I'll be back next Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday. {{{{The secretary marks off a check on the clipboard…}}}} [Secretary]What substances are you hear for council on? [Semore Smoke]Just Marijuana. {{{{Another check on the clipboard…}}}} [Secretary]Ok Mr. Smoke. Just go have a seat in the waiting room until the current session is over and they'll call you in when they're ready. {{{{Smoke nods and walks into the waiting room with Mick Palmer in suit behind him. They both sit down in the waiting room where conversation is buzzing around everywhere, some of the people are talking about past sessions together, and others are asking as first timers how it goes. Smoke just sits quietly with Palmer as they both wait for the session to start…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED!!
