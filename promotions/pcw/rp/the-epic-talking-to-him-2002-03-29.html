The Epic-'Talking To Him'

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 ::It’s around 1:33 p.m. the day after Scott Nolan and James Creon finally found Shawn Mosely, the man James has been searching for ever since he left the wrestling world. Now we find Scott Nolan and James Creon sitting in the small Kitchen of one of the room’s in the “Jaxx Inn” where Shawn Mosely has been staying ever since he came back from South America. Both of the men look exhausted from the past so-many days as they have been searching for Shawn. Now they sit in his kitchen as Shawn is in the bathroom taking a shower after the 13 hour nap he took starting last night when James and Scott found him. Both men wait patiently to talk to him, James seems the most nervous of the two as he is shaking his foot repeatively to the annoyance of Scott. Scott on the other hand, is biting his hands as he is still on the edge knowing nothing what to expect from the usual cocky, arrogant Shawn Mosely. Scott, once again, is the first one to brake the silence between the two as the shower can be heard in the backround of the two voices. “I am so tired” Scott says to James. James foot stops moving quickly for about 3 seconds to glance at Scott. Then, of course, he starts moving it again in anticipation of the Golden One to come out of the bathroom. “What are you going to ask him?” Scott ask as he looks at him wanting an answer this time. James foot stops one more time. This time it is long enough for him to talk. “I don’t know man. Just want him to rethink is position is as far as his wrestling career is concerned. *pauses* I even have a contract offer by a top promotion” James replies. “Really? Who?” Scott says quickly. But before James can answer the question, both of them realize that the shower has stop. They both look down the hall in which the bathroom is located, and then they look at each other. They can hear slight movement in the bathroom as James starts talking again. “Um. Uh. . Scott?” James says softly. “What?” Scott replies. “I . . I think it would be better if it was just me talking one on one with Shawn” James says. “But . .but why?” Scott says “I thought I was a part of this thing to.” “Yeah. But trust me. I think it is the better thing to do. Just do me a favor and go to the bar, or somewhere else and let me talk to the man” James replies. Scott just gives a great big sigh of disappointment and then replies. “Alright. I’m doing this for Shawn, and his career though” Scott says. Scott takes one last glance at James and proceeds to walk outside. James just gives him a nod of respect for what he is doing for him, and Shawn Mosely. Right after Scott closes the door, the bathroom door to Shawn Moselys room opens. James gives the hallway a quick look and begins to wait for Shawn. A few seconds past before Shawn actually leaves the bathroom and begins to walk to the kitchen. He has a cleanly shaved face, his usual long dirty blonde hair dripping wet, and something new James sees, a tattoo of a cross on his right upper arm that has the word “mom” in it. Shawn is just wearing shorts with no shirt on. James notices that he actually seems in somewhat good shape, of course, not what he use to be. Shawn takes a glance at James, and then at the other chair next to the table. Shawn takes a slow motion movement to the chair as he sits down. Not one word is spoken for about 1 or 2 minutes until Shawn breaks the ice and speaks. “So what do you want with me” Shawn says kind of quietly. James takes a few seconds to gather up what he wants to say to Shawn, and then speaks. “Shawn, I just want to be straight up with you, and tell you the truth” James replies. James takes another few to gather up his thoughts again. Shawn just sits in the chair with his head down ready to listen to what James has to say. “I’m here to help you Shawn. Nothing hurts me more to see a man as young as you are with so much potential to be great. When I heard you left the wrestling world for good I could have broke down right there on the spot, but I decided that I wouldn’t let something as bad as that happen, and that’s why I’m here Shawn. To help you.” James says in a slight demanding way. “So what are you saying? Just because you want me to wrestle, I have to?” Shawn replies with some anger behind his words. James eyes light up with a slight fear of actually losing him and having no chance to get him back into the ring. “No. . no. . no” James says quickly “ I just don’t want you to walk away from something you love for the wrong reasons. And that’s why I want to be straight up with you and lay down an offer” Shawn just gives a great big sight not knowing what to say. “Ever since you left I have been looking for you, the fact that you left hurt me since I knew you way back when, and now you were a big time established wrestler. I have been in contacts with several big wrestling promotions. But in the end, PCW layed down the best offer. I mean, you follow me Shawn, you’ll be wrestling in a week for one of the best promotions in the world.” James says. Another slight moment of silence. This time Shawn replies to James previous statement. “Huh. I’m sort of shocked” Shawn replies. “Don’t be shocked. Your one of the best young talented Free Agents out there. And you have another chance. Which most people don’t get” James says strongly. Shawn gives another big sigh and begins to think what he should do. “One thing is for sure Shawn, you can’t go on like you are. You’ve probably been going out every night drinking your young life away” James says. “Wrestling screwed me James. I don’t know if I want to go back to a sport I loved, getting backstabbed again” Shawn replies, once again with slight anger. “That is not true. The only thing that will get screwed here is yourself if you don’t comeback.” James replies. James sits their looking at Shawn. Shawn takes a glance at James and then down at the floor where he continues to think on what way he will go. “And say if I do comeback, and accept the PCW contract. What do you get, 25-50%? There has to be a catch” Shawn says with a sarcastic tone. “No Shawn. That’s what I have been trying to get across to you this entire time. I think you can become big. Real superstar in PCW. It might take a while, But you have the potential to be holding a title around waist with in the first 2 months of your stay. I have everything planned down for you. I just need to bring that cocky, arrogant, old Shawn Mosely out of you with training, and more training. After that, PCW is yours. Just have to want it like you used to. After your mom died.” James says from his heart. “James. I know you really think you know me. And I’ll give you credit for knowing my wrestling career as you have trained me when I was younger. But, please, if you want me to comeback. . .don’t mention my mom.” Shawn says with watery eyes. Both men take a quick glance at each other again. “It’s all up to you Shawn. What do you say?” James says. Shawn looks hesitant , but finally answers the question James waited so long to ask. “Yes. I’ll comeback. I think I forgot that wrestling was the only thing I had when my mom died” Shawn says. James just gives Shawn a great big smile. “Good. No turning back now , Shawn. Are you sure. Because I’m a few seconds from making this call to confirm your signing with PCW.” James says. “Yes. I am. I need this.” Shawn says with, finally, a little bit of a smile. James just nods his head with a smirk with it. He reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out a black cell phone. He glances at Shawn and then at the phone. “I’m going to finish getting ready” Shawn says as he gets up and goes back into the bathroom. James proceeds to dial a number. He puts the phone up to his right ear and waits for someone to pick up. Finally, after a few rings, the other line picks up and James starts to converse. “Yeah. Hello. . this is James Creon” James says. “O yes. James. How you doing?” The person replies. “I’m fine. I’m doing better now though. I finally got Shawn Mosely” James says. “What do you mean. Got him. .as . . into PCW?” The other person asks. “Yes, of course.” James replies quickly with a smile of joy on his face. “Alright. We’ll get all the paper work done for him. We want him to debut on Friday Fusion” The other person says. “What? Next Friday, right?” James replies. “No. We want him right away. We have a match setup for a Shawn Mosely, Mark Estep match.” The other person says. “Firstoff, I’ll get the news to Shawn. Secondly, who the hell is Mark Estep. I don’t want Shawn facing jobbers every week.” James replies. “Whoa. .whoa. .whoa. Mr. Creon. Who do you think you are. From the tone of your voice I would think your doing all of this for yourself. But, I’ll tell you what, you get Shawn Mosely here, and he won’t have to know that your just a greedy scumbag” The other person says. “Yeah. Whatever. Anything else?” James replies. “One more thing. He will also take on Andy Murray the following Tuesday. We really want to get him going” The other person says. “Alright. I’ll talk to you later” James replies has he turns his cell phone off and puts it back in his jacket pocket. James sits there looking at the floor day dreaming on what the possibilities are if he could get control of the Golden One. The sky is the limit for the young superstar, and James knows it. He will succeed big time in PCW, and James knows that also. The real question now is, is it just for the money. ::Shawn is officially back in the world of wrestling. He faces Mark Estep in his debut this Friday. That match is followed by an Andy Murray matchup, which is then followed by a battle royal at Spring Fling that will pit many young PCW stars. . for the Rising Star Title. Can Shawn Mosely actually come back strong, win all of those matches, and next Sunday night be named Rising Star Champion in front of all his doubters? Only time will tell::
