Date: READ OR PERISH The phone rings. Again. Finally, in the middle of a third ring, the fool picks up. What a pitiful dolt. Such a slow minded simpleton. “Hello?” he asks, initiating his own doom. “Um, err…” The assailant rattles out in a gargle, clearing his throat of its manly charm. “Hello, is this Duckstar… Crap, I mean Darkstar?” Curious of the female tone, but not suspicious of its gruffness, Darkstar contemplates the “Possibilities.” Darkstar: “Why yes, I do go by that…” ???: “You stink, fatso! I’m a girl, not a man, and I could beat you up. And I could kill you.” Darkstar: “…” ???: “This is Bethany.” Darkstar: “Oh… Right.” Bethany?: “Do you know what?” Darkstar: “What? (Grimacing behind the veil of telecommunication.)” Bethany?: “Clyde isn’t home, because I killed him.” Darkstar: “Well, if that’s the case, he should be back pretty soon…” Bethany?: “And I also sent him out for groceries, you butt smeller! That means I’m all alone, and nubile. And I want you to come to my house because I want to have sex, because I’m only five, and don’t know how to dress myself.” Darkstar: “Da hell?! Now I’m kinda confused… What in the world is going on?” Bethany?: “Make sure you don’t bring anything, and especially don’t wear a helmet.” Darkstar: “(Sighing deeply.) If Clyde really needed a baby-sitter for his Goddamned girlfriend, he should have just asked.” Bethany?: “Errm… Yeah, a baby-sitter. That’s just what I meant. Now hurry, because for every second you are late, I will eat you!” Nice weather today, as we are greeted by the sight of a peaceful park place. Kids frolicking on the jungle gym, while parents relax close by on benches. Not too far off, a lunatic resides within a payphone booth, giggling at the squeakiest, most feminine extent of his voice. ???: “Bye-bye, LOSERSTAR! HEE HEE HEE HEE!” Slamming the receiver down hard, this mysterious man continues to giggle maniacally. He is draped from head to toe with apparel meant to hide ones image: A trench coat, shades, and a wide brimmed hat. Mysterious Man: “Hee hee! … err … (Realizing he is still giggling like a school girl, he returns to his manly tone.) … I mean, MUWAHAHA! What a blind ignoramus! He is stepping right into my trap, and now nothing can stop me! Nothing!” Trying to get out of the phone booth sure seems to pop the tire, though. The door seems stuck, and immediately the odd assailant falls into panic, hammering the door with fists. Meanwhile It’s early morning here at the “Clyde residence.” Bethany, the young 8-year-old, axe murdering, foul-mouthed valet of the burned man is working enthusiastically in the kitchen. Standing before the stove, a grin is slashed from ear to ear across her face as she flips a pair of sizzling, filth-ridden rats in a pan over a blazing flame. Hearing her lover come bounding down the stairs, awakened by either the smell of breakfast or the sound of rats being cooked alive, the sadistic youth quickly adds a few dabs of cyanide. Clyde: “Oh Bethaaaany…” Stepping into the threshold of the kitchen, Clyde is greeted “Hotly.” Bethany: “EAT THIS!” Luckily the burned man ducks in time, the fried rat along with the pan flying over his head. Bethany: “What the fuck happened to you?” Clyde approaches Bethany, drenched in water, all of his clothes tattered more than usual. Clyde: “Dear, did you happen to install a trapdoor with a plunge into a pit of gators without telling me? Hey, put that knife down, and answer me!” Bethany drops the knife, crosses her arms, and pouts. Bethany: “We can’t fucking afford that, and it’s too fucking hard to steal an alli-fucking-gator! What the fuck do you think, dumbshit?!” (*DING*) A quick moment of confusion. Bethany: “GODDAMN MICROWAVE!! I didn’t put anything in…” Indeed, the small box of artificial heat production has announced it is done with its duty, despite supposedly being empty. So, Bethany moves to it, and hits the open button… (*BOING-BAMN-WHOOSH-CRASH!*) Suddenly, boxing glove on a spring erupts from within the microwave, and nails Bethany square in the chin, launching the shrieking girl out the kitchen window! Clyde merely rubs his chin in wonder, and frowns. Although out of place, in this house there is no end to the insanity. Clyde: “Well, that’s odd…” (*RING-RING*) Clyde: “Now what?” The phone. Clyde moves to it cautiously, not because he’s nervous, but from basic instinct gained by spending time with Bethany. Clyde: “1… 2…” Abruptly yanking the phone off the hook, Clyde puts it to his ear. No explosion, or booby trap of any sort. His wrinkled expression of anticipation slides from his face, and he opens his mouth to speak… Hissing voice on the other end: “SLEEEEEEEEEEEEEP!” Confused, but deprived of time to react, Clyde’s face is blasted with a purple, gaseous cloud that immediately knocks him out. Clyde: “(Eyes rolling back.) Sounds gooooo…” Like clockwork, Clyde falls limply to the floor just as… (*Knock-Knock*) Darkstar, hands in pockets, looks around curiously as he waits at the front door, rocking a bit on his heels. Receiving no acknowledgement, he reels back a balled fist right before catching a glimpse of an untied shoe. Darkstar: “Uh-oh…” Bending over, the blue haired punk reaches down to tie his shoe. Just as he does so, a pair of arrows whiz overhead, and pierce the hard wood door, sinking firmly into it. Both meant for the skull of Darkstar, as he realizes, startled back up and looking wide eyed at them. Darkstar: “If this is somebodies idea of a sick joke… (Grins.) I’M IN!” The Goth excitedly turns the knob of the door, and slips one foot in before… *THHHHWAAAACK!* …Collapsing with a thud in the doorway. His fallen body is dragged in the rest of the way, and the door shut firmly behind him. *** “So, he finally decides to wake up… Good, now I have both of you at my will.” Eyes drowned in darkness slowly feel the burning light. Although very dim light, it is enough to slightly sting the retina as one emerges from sleep. “Yes, it is you two who sit within the palm of my hand!” Darkstar groggily gains his senses back, and takes a first horrible glance at the image of one… Syko the Goblin! Syko the Goblin: “And in my palm, you are UTTERLY HELPLESS… SRUGGLING…” Clyde: “WE GET THE POINT!!” Darkstar: “Huh…?” Understandably, Darkstar takes in the scene with confusion. He is clamped to a stone brick wall by his ankles and wrists, upside down. Clyde is chained to a wooden, high-backed chair of some sort. And Syko, looming with the loom-esqe looming ability loomed by only the looming legends of villainy, looms maniacally over his prey whilst wielding a .45 caliber. Syko the Goblin: “Point? I believe it is I who shall be getting to the point, Clyde…” Darkstar: “(Yawning.) What’s going on?” Clyde: “Eh, Syko trapped us both in MY dungeon to…” The pistol clicks, the echo bouncing though out the dungeon walls. Syko the Goblin: “NO! I WANNA TELL HIM! Errm… How good of you to ask, old chum. You see, though clever devices and a well thought out plot, I, Syko the Goblin, have captured you both. Now, you fate lies solely in the palm…” Darkstar: “Hands, yeah I heard that. But, why?” Syko the Goblin: “Why? Why? Why, for the continental title of course. How can I chance letting you fools take the belt from me? With you out of the way, how can I not win the title? My logic is flawless!” Syko grins, beaming proudly over two blank faces, waiting for some sort of horrified reaction. Clyde: “How the hell are you going to win the belt…” The guns aim is snapped at Clyde. Syko the Goblin: “MINE!!” Clyde: “…fighting three people by yourself?” Syko the Goblin: “THERE IS NO TIME TO THINK, I HAVE TO PREPARE FOR MY MATCH, YOU INCONSIDERATE…” “DIEDIEDIEDIEDIE!!” 5 minutes later. Well, the dismembered body of Syko lies twitching on the dungeon floor as the three battered victims stand around him, slightly confused. There’s no blood, and something about the twitching just doesn’t seem… human. Darkstar rubs his sore wrists, and shakes his head. Darkstar: “I can’t believe he didn’t tell us he’s a robot.” Bethany: “Was…(Shrugging.)” In perfect unison, they throw their heads back in hysterical laughter. (*Clap…clap… clap…*) Abruptly their laughter halts as they all turn around only to be startled by the sight of Syko! This time his image on a television screen lowered from the ceiling, an emotionless look on his face as he claps slowly. Syko the Goblin: “Bravo on defeating my double. Pity, though… You still wont be getting to my title anytime soon. You see, you sit now within the palm…” (*CRASH!*) Shattering the monitor, and imbedding itself into the innards of the set, a butcher knife hurled by the ruthless hand of Bethany demolishes the TV. Clyde: “Well, that’s enough of that.” Once again with the laughter. (*CLICK*) Suddenly the lights goes out, and the area becomes pitch black. Darkstar: “Ohhh… I’m so scared of the dark!” More laughter… And then the clatter made by a ferocious pack of dogs fills the air. No more laughter. Fin.
