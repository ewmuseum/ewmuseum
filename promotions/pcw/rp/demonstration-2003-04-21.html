Demonstration

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 21, 2003 Begin Transmission: (Around a circular table sit four men. Two of them are unfamiliar, but two are already all too familiar. Michael L. Chang, the President of the Order of Society and Logic, and SOCIETY. The other two appear to be ranking officials in the Order, and are suitably pleased with the work of Mr. Chang.) “Excellent, Mr. Chang,” the first official called out. “SOCIETY is truly ready to begin his mission.” “Of course he is,” Chang retorted, angrily. “He is the perfect weapon. Tell them, SOCIETY! Tell the world who and what you are! Speak!” (The immense man growls angrily at first, but begins to speak in a careful, measured tone, that contrasts deeply with his appearance.) “Greetings, world. I am what you most fear. Come on. Finally, you can admit it to yourself. No matter how normal you may believe yourself to be, I assure you, YOU ARE NOT. Inside each of you exists a quirk. No matter how big or how small, that quirk is what keeps you from the perfection of normalcy.” (The two officials applaud at SOCIETY’s eloquence.) The second official was quietly approving. “You’ve done much work with him to prepare him for this campaign. We have no doubts he is mentally ready. But, is he physically ready?” Chang was dumbfounded. “How could you POSSIBLY ask me that? Look at the man! He stands 6’10”! He weighs 357 pounds! He IS the ultimate warrior!” While Chang was in shock, the first official had a readied explanation. “Perhaps, but remember, size is not everything in professional wrestling. He must have skill.” (At this, SOCIETY rose from his chair..) “You DARE mock my skill, gentlemen?!” SOCIETY was livid, as the two officials looked on with shock. “He’s losing control, Chang!” the first called. “Do something!” the second screamed. Instead, Chang stood there, smiling. “Gentlemen…you wanted to see what SOCIETY was capable of. And now, you shall..first hand. Destroy them.” (Without so much as a glance to his creator and colleague, SOCIETY leaps into action. The first official is horrified, as SOCIETY reaches out, wrapping one of his mammoth hands around the official’s throat, choking the man to near unconsciousness. Chang looks on approvingly, and raises his arm skyward.) “Up!” Chang called. (And SOCIETY complied, hoisting the man up high into the air, and then, with no further prompting, driving him down viciously onto the circular table, toppling it over with the force of the blow. The second official can do nothing but stare, horrified.) “He’s…he’s a monster, Chang!” The terrified official lifted up the chair that he had been seated on, holding it out at arm’s length. “But, I thought that’s what you wanted,” Chang said, grinning. “You told me you wanted a soldier who could destroy all our opposition! You told me you wanted the ultimate operative! And now, he is here before you, and you quake! Why?” “Chang! You know damn well he was supposed to be OUR operative! Of the Order! But you take him for yourself!” The second official was terrified, yet still in enough control of his faculties to realize what was going on. (SOCIETY stepped towards the second man. With no recourse left, the man reared back with the wooden seating device, and cracked SOCIETY across the brow with it. He did not go down, but rather, roared loudly. Another shot had the same result, with a droplet of blood now trickling down SOCIETY’s forehead. Finally, the official reached back with all he had, and broke the chair over SOCIETY’s head…but in the end, SOCIETY remained standing.) “Ah, now you see why he IS the true Divine soldier of the Order of Strength and Logic! Your paltry blows could not even move SOCIETY! Even as he bleeds, he is stronger than any other! Now, SOCIETY, show him how you will finish your opponents off in the ring!” (SOCIETY moves quickly, hoisting the official up on his shoulders, in perfect power slam position. The Beacon of Normalcy drops to his knees, driving the official’s ribcage into his shoulder. The man howls in pain, clutching his ribs as he falls to the floor.) “That is Equalization! SOCIETY’s prelude to the end! Now, show him the true meaning of CONFORMITY!” (SOCIETY wraps his arms around the man’s midsection, and lifts him upside down, squeezing the man’s ribs. The official’s breathing grows short, as he fades into unconsciousness. Chang moves over, and taps SOCIETY on the shoulder, causing the hold to be broken.) “Excellent, SOCIETY! That will serve as an excellent warning to those who oppose us.” (SOCIETY surveys himself, taking a look at the damage. With the exception of the trickle of blood running down his forehead, he is barely even breathing hard after destroying the two officials.) “We don’t want them dead,” said Chang. “We just needed to demonstrate your abilities to them, as you will demonstrate your abilities to the world on Friday!” (SOCIETY stoically looked down at the two men..the first two he had taken out. He knew these were the first, and were by no means the last. Still, there was a touch of regret in what he had done..but his conditioning quickly shoved this aside.) “I am SOCIETY. And, Athumoth, and the Ukranian, on Friday, you WILL conform…to SOCIETY.” “Come, SOCIETY. Let’s leave this place.” (And, as on Turmoil, Chang and SOCIETY leave together.) End Transmission
