Wake Up Sleepy Head (Featuring Chris Storm)

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/13/03 As the sun seeps it's way through the cracks in the blinds a new day begins. Normally I'd be in a rush to get up and prepare myself for yet another typical day of waking the children up and teaching them some sort of lesson since they were never in area for too long to go to any school in particular, but today I figured they needed a holiday and so did I after the horrible streak of luck I had on Friday which started off with an ass kicking on behalf of El Romantico, Erik Erratic, and Adam Stryker, and ended with me being left in Awe of He before Nerezza decided to take a taste of my blood and make an example out of Chris and I. Yes Friday did not turn out well especially considering Chris and I had lost our tag team titles to none other than P.W.A. but things would change and a new streak of luck would come, or so I hoped as I rolled over in my bed and rested my head on Chris' chest, causing him to wake up as well. 'Good morning Tessa Bear.' He said with a yawn as his eyes slightly, but only to check the time to make sure that we were running on our usual schedule. 'More like good night.' I replied as my heavy eyes shut under the weight of fatigue and I began to drift back off to sleep, paying no attention to the world around me. 'Tessa Bear it's 7:00 you have to get up!' Chris said as he sat up in bed, causing my head to slide off his chest into the sheets that were covering his lap. 'Can't we just take one day off to rest?' I whined like a child not wanting to get up for school as I buried my head further into the sheets to express how I was feeling about getting up this fine Wednesday morning. >
