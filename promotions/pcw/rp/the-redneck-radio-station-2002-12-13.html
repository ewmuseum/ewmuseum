The Redneck radio station

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/13/2002 ~Old School. Have two words ever described Jon Kano more aptly? I think not. But what is it to be Old School? Is it just being around for a long time? Is it being a person who just doesn't know how to quit? Or is it something else? To Jon Kano being Old School is being the man who reminds everyone of what PCW is all about. No one is more qualified to be the constant reminder of PCW's heritage, tradition, and honor. He was here when the names of the greats were here in PCW and not with the competition, people like Doug Troy, Meanstreak, Ivan Stanislav, Ed, and Da Westsidaz. He was here when the Blood Brothers were formed. He was here when 'The Reverend' Jon Hull was on his run as the greatest PCW Heavyweight Champion of all time. He was here when MEDIA formed. He was part of MEDIA. He formed the Outcasts. Bottom line, Jon Kano is the essence of Old School and the essence of PCW.~ *The scene opens up inside of an Alabama radio station. The host, an overweight man wearing jeans, a flannel t-shirt, and a John Deere hat, is sitting behind his control desk and with the earphones on, all ready to go.* Host (in a thick Southern drawl): Alright, we are back and ladies and gentlemen, we have a special guest in the studio today, he is in town for a professional wrestling show on Friday. He is a multi-time champion and a world class athlete.....Jon Kano. *Jon Kano enters the studio. He is dressed in a PCW t-shirt and jeans. His long blond hair is tied back into a pony tail. He shakes the host's hand, sits down, and puts the earphones on.* Host: Welcome to Alabama, Mr. Kano. Kano: Thanks, can't wait to get the hell out. Host: What is that supposed to mean? Kano: If you have to ask me what that means, then you are truly a simpleton. Host: I'll tell ya what, my family has been in the great state of Alabama since before the Civil War. Kano: I wouldn't be proud of that. Host: Now wait a minute, Mr. Kano, Alabama.... Kano: ....is a shithole. I hate this state. Trailer parks, inbreeding, all of this shit just makes this what could be a great state a shithole. Host: Well I am not gonna try and argue with you, sir. Lets take a call. Line one, we have Marty from South Bumfuck. Marty, you there? Marty: Yeah, how are you doing today? Mr. Kano, you are truly a wrestling great. Kano: Thank you, lovely weather you guys have in South Bumfuck. Marty: My question is why Mr. Kano must always make fun of our home state of Alabama? Kano: My answer is that Alabama blows. Next call. Host: We have on Line two Al from Greensboro. Al, ya there, buddy? Al: Ya know it. I got a question for Mr. Kano. Now Jon, what would happen if Brandi would beat you in your match tonight on Fusion? Kano: You know, Al. It might happen. I am a marked man, no one wants to help this legend. Brandi has the whole damn Front Office in her corner. I got nobody. So it is possible that she might technically get the win, but I would like to just say one thing, I do have a little surprise for her. She better make sure the whole damn Front Office with her tonight, and then some to beat me because I am not some new blood who is straight out of wrestling school. I am a living legend of wrestling. I am twenty-six and have done more than most of the wrestlers twice my age. And tonight I will show any one in the arena, all the fans watching at home, and Brandi herself why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF, especially when I take her God damn Television title. *The scene fades to black as Kano gets up, newly motivated for his match on Fusion, throws the headset down and leaves the studio to a confused host.*
