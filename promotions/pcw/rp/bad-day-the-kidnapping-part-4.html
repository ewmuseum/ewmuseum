Bad Day. The Kidnapping Part 4

Date: Sucks! Eddy’s headache was a killer. He woke up his forehead hurting from the headache, almost a migraine and he tried to go back to sleep to forget it was there however it was and he couldn’t bear the feeling much longer. He jumped out of bed and stumbled into the bathroom, however it didn’t have anything for Eddy’s use (Mostly his sister’s weight loss pills and other medicines he didn’t need for a headache). Eddy then jumped into casual clothing and out of his boxers and walked outside to the cool air before approaching his car with a pair of keys ready to start the ignition. He opened the door and began to drive off. The headache however was killing him even more now and it hindered him yet he continued driving to the drug store. It was covered in ice outside in Texas, a white blanket of ice, which is very rare for Texas. Eddy flipped on the radio and these were the first words. 'Dallas/Fort Worth residents, do not drive UNLESS it is importantly needed, and even then it’s not exactly right to drive. The roads are icy and a car accident is bound to happen.' Eddy forgot about that. He even barely noticed the ice, and he felt truly stupid for not. Yet, he was on his way to the drugstore and decided not to stop… A few minutes later… 'Argh! Closed!' Eddy, with his headache in true pain was in rage that the store wasn’t open. He tried beating on the door out of anger but then stopped and sigh. The roads were pretty empty because people were smart enough to not drive. Eddy had planned to search for his sister today but yet again couldn’t do it and had to postpone it to another day. Then he looked across the street and saw something: The Gym! Maybe he could blow off time there and get ready for his match again El Romantico for the Cruiserweight title, which would be Eddy’s first title in the PCW. Eddy ambled across the icy street and onto the parking lot, before entering… CLOSED 'WHY?' said Eddy in anger. 'I just want to do something so I don’t have the drive on the streets!'. Eddy began walking across the street before he slipped and fell on his ass. 'Darn it…' Eddy tried climbing up again yet he slipped and fell on his ass yet again. In anger, and slowly walked across the ice. In about 5 minutes, he made it across and was safe in the parking lot of the drug store…Yet bored. He got in his truck and decided to listen to music or the news to kill time. Yet once he turned on the radio all that came in was static. 'It was working just a second ago..' Eddy tried turning it up but the static noises hurt his ears and it didn’t help the radio work. He tried changing channels, but all that could come up was cheesy N Sync and nothing else. Eddy tried listening to the boy bands, but couldn’t bear it. Before long he punched off the radio and laid back. 'Maybe I can call someone?' He picked up the cell phone, but it wasn’t working. Then he realized he was late on paying the bill and his phone was turned off… 'God…' Eddy decided to fuck the roads and tried starting up the engine, and he got it started and began driving carefully down the roads, slowly and steadily…A very mundane, long drive. He tried the radio again, and 'Bad Day' by Fuel played. I’ve had a bad day again… 'Heck yes I have….', said Eddy driving ever so slowly down the road. Then the radio went into static again and Eddy sighed, and drove home… Later… 'Man! I put the dead bolt lock on!' Eddy tried opening the door but it wasn’t helping. The dead bolt was on and Eddy has no way of getting in…except the window. Eddy tried tugging open the window closest to the door but it was locked down. After about 2 windows Eddy found the 3rd to work well and opened, as Eddy tried climbing in…. Yet he slipped on the ice on the window sill and fell down, breaking 2 vases, a couple of pictures and hitting his head on the table. Eddy rubbed his forehead, and slipped on a piece of glass as he climbed up and fell backwards, cutting his back with a long scratch from another piece of glass. Eddy had never been so clumsy in all his life. Eddy felt his back hurt as his rubbed his forehead again and eventually was safe from slipping on the glass. He went to go get a broom and a dustpan but he couldn’t find any. So Eddy had to pick up the glass by hand or not at all. So he decided not at all and to watch TV. Of course by his luck today the TV wasn’t working because THAT was turned off to, mostly because his kidnapped sister was late on paying it. Eddy still had his headache, had a stomach ache, a scratch on his back, but this was the perfect scenario to fight in. If he was in this pain when fighting El Romantico then he should fight his way out of it, and he decided to go to the punching bag the far back room. Eddy punched it, kicked it, and fought the punching bag his hardest and never felt better because he WAS very ready for the match. It was the first good thing that happened all day… …Except the punching bag DID fall off it’s chain and hit Eddy in the head, but Eddy was still ready… …And Eddy never did lose his headache, or his 1 foot long scratch on his back didn’t feel better… …And-, well, I’m being very pessimistic now… …Can we fade from this promo?………Thank you.
