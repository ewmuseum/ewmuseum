When in disgrace with fortune and men's eyeswe all alone beweep our outcast state,And trouble deaf heaven with our bootless cries,And look upon myself, and curse our fate;Wishing us like to one more rich in hope,Featured like him, like him with friends possest,Desiring this man's art, and that man's scope,With what we most enjoy contented least; Yet in these thoughts myself almost depressing,Haply we think on thee and then our state,Like to the lark at break of day arisingFrom sullen earth, sings hymms at heaven's gate;For thy sweet love remembered, such wealth bringsThat then we scorn to change our state with kings. 'Fortune and Men's Eyes' -by William Shakespeare. Dear Yuu, The days narrow in on the event called Madness. Here we sit, working and toiling away, trying to train our mind and body to be stronger, more disciplined, and better than theirs. And yet, no one will recognize our greatness. Today, we went hunting. We found a large deer in the woods, and managed to kill it with a fist straight to the chest. In the legends and stories about us, they say that we are inseparable with our skills, which it is our only friend and only weapon, but they are so wrong. Although the technique adapts to our opponents, we still utilize the same lethalness that it was created to exhibit. There is nothing more absurd than trying to hit squirrels with the Danse Macabre kick. We made a composite Kendo stick with some wood and intestine in our excursions in the woods. It is not a very powerful weapon, but it certainly can assist to kill large game, but rarely on the first shot. We are good with our hands to finish off a suffering animal, however, and they are always dead within ten seconds after we hit them. The stories about us being a cold being, with no emotion. We are not cold. We are only prudent. The humans of the city are worse than we are. They set out traps; try to kill beavers and wolves for their fur, with no regard for the life they are slaying. We slay to survive, just as the animals slay each other to survive. Hunting is a pleasure that we can admit to. The other one is training. We sit outside our cave, meditating, letting thoughts come to us, concentrating our Celestial energy and balancing both the Void and the Aura within us so our actions will be more powerful. As always, it is in preparation for our quest, the quest for us to not be afraid of the world. The more we see them, the more the humans of the city disgust us. Yet, watching them leaves us with some sort of longing, emptiness inside us that hunting and training cannot fill. The voice inside us tells us that she can fill that emptiness, but we don't believe her. There is no one else around here. When we saw the other hunters, they are talking with their brethren peacefully, sitting for a well-earned snack. One of them had a girl at his side, and although she appeared to be a frail creature at the side of the man, she was very capable of defending herself and making her own decisions. They all talked as if they were family. We believe that, in fact, they were family, two families camping together. The family we had never let us experience things like this. Many people envy us. They want to feel the experiences of war, of heroism, of battle, of being one of the most powerful swordsmen and martial artists on the earth. However, as glorious as those things may be, they will never fill the emptiness inside us. We worked hard for our reputation, our title of godhood, but in the end, it gave us nothing. We wish we had a friend. However, the humans that we know, they are all shallow and weak. They think of nothing except for themselves. They think not of the animals they kill, the environment they decay, the people they hurt. They only think of themselves. Us, we think of the animals, and give thanks to this global realm when we manage to bring a deer down. When we gather berries and leaves, we thank the realm for providing us with such a rich bounty. When we train, the sun shines on us, on our bareback. The wind tosses our hair around gently, playing with it as if we were a friend. As we hunt, though, we realize one thing. The forest and the planets and the animals, all of them are getting weaker, more tired, less muscular. The plant wilt, the animals are slow and cumbersome. We look around, and realize that the ground is starting to get brown and hard, dry and cracked. The sunlight in the sky, even, is starting to dim in the sky around us. The energy of the earth is being absorbed, the truth of the existence is being taken away. It is all thanks to men like Tyler Nelson. We must remember the quest that we train hard for every day. We do not wish vengeance on him. We wish to be disciplined enough to face the world, and then strong enough to make a change. Humans like the CEO are not fit to live. The selfish purposes, the people who harbor them, and the truth they concoct do not deserve to live. We are not the only one who has been betrayed by him. My hand tires, but our left arm itches to practice with the sword. The Saishukessen Ougii is sheathed, placed at our side. We do not draw it unless we intend to draw blood. we reach forward, past the Saishukessen Ougii, to grip the wooden practice sword we use when we work on our technique. We end our writing here. We will train now. The purpose, we will remember. We want to be strong so we will not be afraid.
