Memories of Life Past

21 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/21/02 LostAlwaysSilent Alone on the steeple of a church he perched in the darkness of Tokyo, Japan's outskirts. His mind like a hurricane. Shin Yagyuu, Shinsetsu Yagami.... He could no longer reflect on the beauty of the lotus by his home because his home was burned to the ground nearly five hundred years ago. -=- Wind blows through the cherry blossoms swiftly as the man with black and red eyes stares at a beautiful woman playing in the water with a child. Child: Father, come play! The man smiles lightly and walks down to the water. Woman: Shigei, go play father and I need to discuss something.. The small boy runs off to a tree and hacks away at it with a play sword. Woman: Shin, what is it that troubles you my love... I hear you wake up every night in a cold sweat. I see the concern on your face. I'm not a fool my love... Shinsetsu: Oda.... His armies sweep closer to us every day. I will not let him take all we have worked for... Kitsune... My life may be trivial to history but seeing your's and Shigei's smiles makes that ok... The woman cocks her head slightly.. Kitsune: You are not making much sense my love... The lone samurai looks down in frustration... Shinsetsu: I go to battle Oda... tomorrow... A look of fear crosses the woman's face as tears begin to roll down her face... Kitsune: Who will go with you... Shinsetsu glares down at the dirt in anger. Shinsetsu: No one... They are all too cowardly to stand up to him... So I go alone.. They refuse to fight at my side after all I have done for them... And if I can not stop him Oda will come and kill them.. That's why I want you and Shigei to leave with what is left of our money... Kitsune wraps her arms tightly around her husband... -=- Shin suddenly shakes off the memory as the voice reaches out to him again.. Yagyuu.... your thought's are of your past life... If I show you the events that transpired after your death will it put you at peace... Will it calm your weary soul to know Oda, Nobunaga murdered and raped your wife only days after you were dead... Shin's eyes snap open in rage... Would it help to know your son grew up to serve Oda... That's right... Oda raised your son Shinsetsu.... He made your blood his lap dog... Shin's eyes closed as he stared at the blackness of the void... w... why... why do you torture me... So that you will hate them... They fear and despise the celestials. They will never accept you as a leader like they did back in the 1500s... You were one of the first Celestials to walk the world as a man... Only because you knew no better... Shin's eyes slowly slide open as he begins to glow bright blue... Yes.. Purge the weakness of a life you led by mistake... Purge it from your perfect soul.. The dark figure flips forward off the steeple and hits the sidewalk hard as people on the street back away from him. Man: Is that a.... Woman: It's a celestial RUN!! The woman takes two steps in the opposite direction before Shin's straightened hand rips through her stomach..... As fast as the rest the people can see the after effect another man lies broken on the sidewalk a stop sign forced through his stomach. He fights like a mad man... Amazing that he is older than both of us combined... The being has no control... Cyclonica looks to Akiyama as the two watch the carnage on the street... The void led him into this battle to purge him but should we stop him before he goes to far? Akiyama simply stares down as Shin uproots a yeild sign and slams it hard into the concrete. The man next to Shin only feels the next and last few moments of his life.. He does not see the kick that hit him in the back of the knee. He does not see the hand that grasps his throat and he does not see the pole of the yeild sign until it is forced firmly through his upper torso... Kill them all Celestial.... Shin drops to one knee suddenly as the end of the street begins to swirl in fury as a tornado... If they wish to fear me then I will give them a reason to... I will let them see what I have seen for FIVE HUNDRED YEARS! Suddenly at the end of the street a void rift rips open. The few who escaped Shin's fist are now pulled into the void by immense force. Shin stands staring at the rift as it slowly closes.. Shin turns his head slightly... To look at Cyclonica and Akiyama. The two leap from their perch on the roof and land next to him... Yagyuu... You need dicipline... You need to learn your abilities better... and you.. Shin raises a hand and begins to turn around. I was doing what had to be done... Cyclonica's eyes widen a bit in shock... I can speak.. I choose not to... now if you will excuse me... Where are you going? Shin simply turns and walks away as Cyclonica stares after him. Akiyama no longer next to her. Fade
