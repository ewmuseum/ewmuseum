Is Kano an alcoholic?

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/19/2003 'Jon, you are drunk,' observes senior PCW correspondant, Cal Seaver, as he walks into Jon Kano's locker room. In the process of walking into Kano's locker room, Cal Seaver had to step over several empty Icehouse bottles and a half empty bottle of Jose' Quervo. 'I'll have you know that that is almost a lie,' Kano responds with a slight slur in his speech. Kano, slumped in the corner, is half way dressed in his wrestling attire for his tag team match in a few hours. 'Jesus, Jon, you are in no condition to wrestle tonight. I am gonna go talk to Kannon,' warns Seaver. This last comment brings Kano to his feet and Cal Seaver tries to hightail it out of Kano's locker room. This strategy does not work well for Sir Calvin of the Seavers, as Kano grabs him by the back of the head, and slams the door shut. 'Now, Cal, if you would do that then it would have reprecussions for me. And that is something I simply cannot allow.' Kano says in a sly, calm voice. 'Listen, Jon, I know you wanted that kid, but you can't just keep doing this to yourself.' This does nothing but anger Kano, who takes his right hand and places it around Cal Seaver's throat. Kano simply lifts the frail Seaver up the nearest wall and just holds him there. 'What did you say, you twinkle-toed little cocksucker? Did you just refer to my personal life? Do you know how sick and tired I am of people like you prying into the most intimate details of my life? No you don't. No one knows how I feel right now, and God damnit, no one can tell me what to do with my life.' Kano lets Seaver down. Cal is clutching his throat and gasping for air, anyway he can get it. Kano grabs Cal by his shirt, opens the door, and chucks him out into the hall way like a sack of garbage. 'And stay out, Slappy.' Kano walks back into his locker room and completely loses his temper. He grabs many of the empty bottles and throws them, one by one against the far wall, shattered glass now lays on the floor. He then grabs a small table and bashes another wall with it. Finally, Kano looks relieved and stops the onslaught of mad rage. 'Damn, I think I am sober now. I got a little pissed, but that is all right. I am a better wrestler and entertainer when I am pissed. Tonight will be no different. I step into the ring with three other wrestlers, all three new blood. But my tag team partner tonight is different. He has potential, and I almost like him. A month or two from now, I may actually like him. He has respect for history and tradition. But Bob Vurdo still has a lot to work on, but he can make something of himself. The other two are simply flashes in the pan. Kannon got a hard on to have as many women as possible wrestling and now he has an epidemic of women trying to hang in a man's industry. Tonight will be a lesson for the ages as I show everyone once again why I am what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!' Kano exclaims as he straightens himself out and begins to look a little respectable before his match tonight as the scene fades to black.
