Hair Dryers are wonderful things

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-26-02 RP 1Fusion27 Rain, rain, have you ever seen so much rain? I’m driving down the road, wipers on full blast and it’s raining so hard, I can’t see the lines on the road. Hell, I don’t even know where I’m going. Let’s just put it this way, it all started this morning… I woke up around 8am like I always do in the small hotel bed. Yeah, I practically live in hotels. Hey, clean room, free ice, and if you’re lucky and get a good one, you’ll have complimentary breakfast and an indoor gym and swimming pool. But of course, those ain’t cheep. With all this tax crap going around with the Front office, I’m left to give Mama what I give her every week, spending money, and food. I have just enough afterwards for a Hotel 6 for a week. Did I mention I love my job? As I was saying, I woke up feeling like shit. Last night I got my ass kicked by Anarchy and Cyclonica. Yeah I expected it. Them two are the toughest women in PCW. Well, there’s Miss Brandi, but I haven’t really been fortunate enough to bump into her. If you know me, you know when I do ‘bump’ into Miss Front Office, and she lights my fuse, don’t you think twice I wouldn’t let her know where I stand. I ain’t scared of no one. Period. First thing I do when I crawled out of bed is peep behind my closed curtains. I saw a beautiful sunny day. Perfect for sight seeing and checking out this place called Ames, Iowa. I didn’t have to make an appearance again till Friday and I desperately needed to get away and gather my thoughts. I have this huge match for the contendership spot for the Cruiserweight title I want so badly. I’ve been told Jamie Celt wants that belt just as much as I do if not more. Bah! Once I do win that belt. I’m gonna give him a real belt whoopin’ with it. I told the runt that when I beat him with my own leather belt, it wasn’t personal, but no, he has to go take it personal and well, I’ll have to just keep beating him till he learns don’t mess with me. Eventually, I make to the shower. I spend 15 minutes digging this hot steaming shower. I even sang a little while I was washing my hair, but you didn’t hear it from me. Eager to take my Jeep for a joy ride, I shut off the water and reach for my white towel that supposed to be in every bathroom. Believe it or not, I couldn’t find it anywhere. Thank goodness for hair dryers. Now is the time to picture Teresa Tomas wearing her birthday suit and drying herself with a hair dryer. Amusing yes? I get dressed and French braid my hair. I would like to smack out beloved commentator, Jeff for his lame ass comments about my hair. Obviously he doesn’t see very well. Because if he paid the least bit attention, I do have hair. It trails down my back to my waistline as it always has. My hair is also blonde and even has black streaks in it. Now how can you get buzzed hair and ‘looks like a guy’ from that description? Ya know, one of these day, he’ll say the wrong thing and I’ll bust a tooth or two out for him. The weather was still as nice as it was when I woke up by the time I hoped in my Jeep. I took coverings off and left it open thinking this is going to be a wonderful day to relax and gather my thoughts. My back still hurt from Cyclonica’s razor sharp nails, not to mention the pounding headache from Anarchy continuously bashing my head against something. I nearly had that match won. So close, but yet so far away. I didn’t make it a mile and out of the blue, here comes the clouds. I’m thinking. “What in the world?” I didn’t even have time to finish putting the cover back on my jeep when the rain it. When I finally made it inside the Jeep, I was drenched from head to toe. (Wicked witch voice) I’m melting! That’s when my day starting making a turn from good to bad. I attempt to drive just a little ways further down the road. The attempt is successful for a couple more miles and I have to stop. There’s no way I’m going to wreck this beautiful Jeep Wrangler. If anything were to happen to it, there will be hell to pay. I do not lie. So, where this story going? It’s going no where. Just like I’m going no where cause I ended up pulling to the side of the freaking road because it began to rain so hard I couldn’t hear myself thing better less, drive in between the unseen lines. The moral of this story is, never forget your hair dryer.
