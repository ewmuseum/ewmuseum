Hard Work

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/04/03 Don’t you just hate it when you try to put something down in words, but all that comes out is a jumbled mess that doesn’t even make any sense? That’s exactly how I feel right now. In fact, this just so happens to be my third attempt at trying to write something down that doesn’t sound like corny, crappy bull-shit that people will just scoff at. You see, my world has been falling in on me. I know it sounds pathetic, but dammit, we’re all pathetic every once in a while during our lives. It’s no secret that I went to hell and back, but if it wasn’t for the image of my father, I would have never even made it back. You see, he helped me back then, and now I want to do the same for him. I want to return the favour, not because I feel like I have to, but because I want to. God only knows we all go through tough times in our lives, but those times are made easier when you have people around you to give you support. However, I can’t offer him anything. I can’t do anything to help my father,not because he won’t let me… because he is dead. Yeah, it sucks. But what can I do? What would the original Lucha Legend want me to do? Sure, it’s not for the Cruiserweight Title, but do I care? No. I want to win. I want to be considered a winner. I want to achieve great things! But I also want to honour my father in every way I can…. Y’see? Every time I try to focus, it always goes back to the same thing…. My Father. Maybe this’ll give the rest of them the advantage he needs to beat me. Maybe, the idea of going back to the floors, becoming nobody, fading back into the shadows will be enough for them to crush me like the bug they all think I am. Maybe, Maybe, Maybe. All I know for sure is that I’m not giving up. I’m not throwing in the towel. I will do what I can to be a great PCW Cruiserweight Champion and to be a great son to my father, who I am sure is looking down on me from up above. And above all else, I will do what I can to be myself as well. The fight won’t be easy, but then, when have you ever known a fight to be like that? The point is, I’m gonna make sure things turn out the way I want them to, the way that they deserve to turn out…. And I won’t let anything stop me from doing that.! Hell I live for the hard work!
