The Crusader Chronicles...Part 1.

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-4-2002 'Sometimes, when you are up against something you do not fully understand, you have to go to the beginning. That was where I was headed. I needed to find out what made the Super soldier, John Crichton, tick. I wanted to know the man for what and who he clamed to be. I needed to look at the world through his eyes. To do that, I figured the best I could do was get to the nearest army base and talk to the original Super soldier...Captain America!!' (The scene opens onto a hot desert landscape, in front of a heavily guarded gate to a military installation. The guards have quizzical looks on their faces. Some of them point and stare, others just stare. Finally, we see the object of their interest. Venneman Pendragon, PCW Rising Star Champion, enters the shot from the left. He wears the belt and is dressed in his shirt of golden chain mail armor. He has Excalibur sheathed at his left hip, and although it appears to be quite hot, he doesn't sweat. He calmly rides up to the gates and stops. He waits a moment before becoming a little impatient...) Venneman: You there...You can open the door now. I am quite finished waiting... (The soldier who mans the gatehouse looks at Pendragon as if he just got off the boat. Some of the other guards unclip their sidearm holsters and stand ready to strike...) Gate Soldier: Now, how can I do that? You want in there, you have to have proper clearance. I just can't let anyone in here. I need to have orders, papers, and credentials...Got it? (Pendragon climbs down from his horse. As he does, a few of the soldiers' hands drift down and come to rest on their weapons. Pendragon walks over to the gatehouse, looks in, and smiles....) Venneman: Why then, you are in luck. You are hereby ordered by the Lord King Venneman Pendragon, Crusader of Truth, to open the gate. Now, you are authorized. (The gate soldier looks to his comrades and instantly, all are laughing madly. Pendragon looks a little confused, and finally the gate man calms enough to give him an answer...) Gate Soldier: Although we do appreciate your authority....*snicker....LORD King Pendragon....*snicker...We are under the strictest of orders not to let anyone in this facility. I wish we could help you, but right now, we cannot. What is it that you need inside the facility, anyway? Venneman: I am looking for Captain America. He is the original recipient of a Super Soldier serum, and I have to do battle with someone else who has had it. I need to get some pointers. I am only a man, after all. (Once again, the soldiers bust up. Pendragon is starting to appear annoyed, and he loosens his own weapon in its scabbard. The soldiers keep laughing...) Gate Soldier: Hmm...Yes. Captain America. Let me see. I don't believe he is here. You are in the wrong neck of the woods if you are looking for him. He stays away from the desert. At this installation, we deal with 'other' things. Venneman: Well then why don't you get on your little radio gadget and call me someone who might know a little more than a disrespectful whelp whose sole purpose in life is to press a button to open a gate in the middle of the goddamned desert. This tires me and my time is vastly more important than yours. (The soldier seems to have had enough of Pendragon, and he refuses to play along with the insane fellow...) Gate Soldier: If you must know, Captain America is a comic book character. He was also the subject of a movie and various cartoons. He was never a REAL super soldier. Those don't exist. You need to have you head examined, my lord...I fear there is something quite wrong with you....Captain America...Don't you ever watch TV??? (Pendragon shakes his head, trying to take in all of the information. He looks around at the grinning faces and realizes that perhaps the joke is indeed on him. But then he shakes his head once more, and heads back to his horse, his confident smile portrayed once more upon his noble visage...) Venneman: Oh well. If you people refuse to help a Crusader locate the only person who can help with a very serious problem that may have global repercussions, so be it. But it is upon your conscience, not mine... (Pendragon hops back into the saddle and takes off at a canter...) 'Hmm...Very uncooperative. And to try and tell me that Super Soldiers do not exist. Perhaps Crichton had already gotten to them. These military types do like to stick together. I wonder where I need to go next...To think that these fellows tried to pawn the idea that a Super Soldier is merely a figure from a comic book. Absurd. How else would John Crichton, my opponent on Tuesday, have received the treatment? He does seem to destroy his opponents as one who is inhuman might. And if these people do not really exist, why is it that a group of superheroes hunted him down, and another rescued him, albeit too late to truly help him? Troubling questions, and I think I know where to go now...' *****FLASH****** (The shot is now outside of the Pentagon in Washington DC...How do we know? Cause right there is the Pentagon, stupid...Damn. It looks like a tour group is gathered out front. They appear to be a mix of people, students and the elderly, foreigners and domestic people. One man stands out. Pendragon is there, and he still wears the Rising Star Title and his golden shirt of armor. He stands and listens as the tour guide speaks...) Tour Guide: The Pentagon houses the think tank of American Military might. All of the innovations and advances that the US Military has made over the past few decades have been come up with in some room of this great building. Both military personnel and civilians work here, striving together to defend America in the most safe and efficient way possible...Umm yes, you there...In the..Armor?? (Pendragon has raised his hand, and interrupts the speaker...) Venneman: So you fellows basically come up with better ways to kill greater amounts of people? Speaker: Umm...I guess you could put it that bluntly. We prefer to think that we are making the world safe for democracy. Venneman: Hey. I am not knocking you. Any great leader knows that in war, the faster you take out your opponent, the stronger you stay and become. Force is a necessary device in maintaining order and expanding one's realm. Speaker: Who are you? Venneman: I am Lord King Venneman Pendragon, Crusader of Truth, and I come here seeking answers. If what you say is true, then I came to the right place. I am looking for Captain America. I need to learn about the physical strengths of a John Crichton... (The speaker begins to laugh, but abruptly halts upon the mention of Crichton. He looks around, a tad nervous. The crowd laughs, however, at the Captain America comment...) Speaker: I do not know what you are talking about. Venneman: I have to do battle with the man, and I need to know if he is for real. I am merely a man, and I don't really feel like facing a real life superman straight from fantasy land. I need to know. The world may depend on it man! (The speaker pulls a hand held radio from his pocket and speaks into it. He smiles at Pendragon as four men in black suits come from the Pentagon. They surround Pendragon.) Speaker: These fine gentlemen will escort you inside. There are some people who I believe can help you. I hope... (The voiceover starts again as Pendragon shakes the speaker's hand and walks with the four men into the Pentagon...) 'Finally, I was in a position to get a few answers. I knew that I had him when he stopped at the mention of John Crichton. Perhaps it was some kind of a huge governmental conspiracy. Seems the modern day realms are often good at that. I was never good at hiding my actions from my people. That though, is not the way of a hero. And it seemed that to be a hero to the land of Gauze, and to the world in general, I would have to start with matters in the PCW. Skyler told me that everything happens for a reason, and that Gauze is relative to the happenings in PCW. It was not the most easily digested information, but I found myself believing in it more and more. I couldn't get the place from my mind. I wrote everything down after I found myself blood covered on my couch, and I have gone over the events hundreds of times. I needed to believe it, to keep from being crazy. A king does not have the luxury of insanity. John Crichton...More destined gold for the King. At least now I was getting a little bit of deserved respect. And maybe, just maybe, these people might be able to give me some insight into what to expect from a Super Soldier. Every advantage would help. His title would be mine no matter what. I was divinely ordained for it...' >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
