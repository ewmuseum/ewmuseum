Nerezza Also Endorses...

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/11/02 The scene opens up inside an elegant victorian mansion. Illustrious paintings hand from the walls, depicting great moments in history. Turn of the century items lay around on exquisite tables, giving a warm yet sophisticated atmosphere. A shadow looms over the corridor as someone approaches... a quick pivot reveals a young, beautiful woman in a flowing, satin evening gown that reaches to her ankles. She wears silky gloves on each arm that go past her elbows and occasionally, a little peek of her matching shoes slips from underneath the lacy skirt. Her fingers, wrists, ears and neck are highlighted with exotic jewelry, sparkling sapphires and elegant emeralds. She sways down the corridor with grace and form, her luscious brown hair bouncing delicately and her piercing blue eyes searching curiously around. She seems distracted by a light emanating from one of the great oak doors. The lady grasps the door handle and pushes down, swinging the door open to reveal a feast fit for a king before her. Dishes of Royal standard are set before her, with highly polished silverware making up the cutlery and domes covering the platters on which they rest. The aroma tantalizes her until she can no longer resist. Gasping, she wanders around the table, looking at the lobster, chicken, vegetables, all the culinary delights. She seats herself down, tucking herself into the wondrous table before placing her dainty little fingers on the ring at the top of one of the domes and lifting it off. The silver is placed down besides the dish and awaiting to tease her glossy, pink lips is a block of the tasty Walls' Viennetta Ice-Cream. She picks up one of the spoons next to it and presses down on the layer of chocolate, cracking it and diving into the section of delicious ice-cream. Cutting off a chunk, she scoops with up gently and with all the table manners of an Etiquette teacher, places it onto her tongue. Suddenly, a giant crash sends her into a panic as the window in front of her is shattered by something huge flying through it. When she looks up, standing amongst the shattered glass is Nerezza, dressed in his two-tone red and black tuxedo, white gloves and red spectacles. He looks at the girl and begins to make his way towards her. She leaps from her seat as the aggressor turns the table over. The camera zooms in on the delicacies spilling onto the floor and going to waste, their succulent smell serving no purpose. Further smashes and crashes slam through the air as the girl makes her getaway with haste, picking up her skirts and running as the beast pursues her. Nerezza's path of destruction continues as the girl flees. He rams his fist through the paintings, ripping apart the head of Napoleon Bonaparte and smashing a bust of Socrates to the ground where it splits into a hundred irreparable pieces. The camera cuts back to the girl, who is now trapped at the end of the hall with nowhere to turn. Nerezza closes in and grips the skull of the girl with his giant gloved hand, then rams her face repeatedly into the wall. The view turns to a close up o the back of Nerezza's hand doing the unspeakable deed until after around ten smashes, he releases her and watches her drop to the floor, unconscious. Her face shows no sign of bruising but her hair and makeup are ruined, leaving her in no state to attend anything. Nerezza glances through his red spectacles into the face of the camera, then adjusts his black bow tie. He smiles, showing a gleaming fang, then delivers his punch line in a calm manner. 'When you want ice-cream with a powerful hitting flavour... NEVER ignore Walls.'
