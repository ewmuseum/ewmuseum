Date: 3-27-2002 ..::/::..Our story recommences from the 'Piledriver' nightclub, the wild and rowdy site where the crem de' la crem of the PTC world are partying non-stop in honor of the returning PCW superhero and all around good guys...RENT-A-HERO. Even with all the action and smiling faces swarming his party, Rent-A-Hero can't take notice and even FAKE a smile at this moment...for his heart is too broken and contorted to even fathom his usual happy thoughts. 'Rapture' by Iio overtakes the club as Rent-A-Hero looks down from the booth overtop the dance floor, watching on as various PTC personalities shake what their proverbial momma's gave them and having the best time doing so. Stars like Andy Murray, Corg, Syn, and even Darkstar are just an afterthought to him as his eyes stay locked upon Amy with deep want in his blue eyes, his hand against the plexi glass as if he were reaching out to her from above. He fixes his red and yellow Flash mask with his free hand and continues to stare, watching her dancing closely against Jason with that devilish smile of hers....a smile he used to be treated to regularly...never truly appreciating it when it was his for the taking. Jason, wiping the sweat from his lightly tanned brow, holds Amy by the waist and rocks with her to the beat as Rent-A-Hero turns away from this saddening sight, not wishing to torture himself anymore by looking. He pops the collar on his open up DBZ shirt and tucks his hands in his blue JnCO jeans, releasing a hard-earned sigh as he says aloud, in the privacy of the lush booth with all kinds of comfortable and exotic furniture around..::/::..
