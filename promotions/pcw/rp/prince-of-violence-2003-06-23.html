Prince of Violence

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/06/03 Success is not a destination it's a journey. And in the long journey, to that very destination is my entering upon the Cloud Nine PPV. From this day till' next, you can call upon me as the PCW Prince. It's just a matter of time before the King sadly takes that unexpected turn, and has to hand down the throne to a newer, tougher, and more reliable man. So the prince is fine, but the King-to-be is all the better! [I had drifted off. Knowing I was up in the morning, I was meeting with a local friend of mines. An old buddy. Him and I, go way back… I was eagerly looking forward to meeting up with him once more. He's a cool guy, I'm sure you'll all love him! OK, so maybe not love, but you'll like him as much as you like me. What do you mean you don't like me? Be quiet!] She stood tall. Her slim figure, enlighten in the heaven like background. She wore a dress, knee length. She looked dazzling to say the least. Her sent of perfume was so attractable it was unbelievable. Her blonde hair, shoulder length, was visible from all angles. Who? You ask. I haven't a fuckin' clue! [Wakening, I looked around the room. Left in wonder as to whom the women of my dreams was I got up out of bed. Looking around my bedroom, I felt quite good. My head was clear, and I was decent enough to deal with my Turmoil match. I was really looking forward to next weeks show, but that's not important. I felt fresh. New. Awake and ready to go. Which I did.] [.Fade.] This is a café I've been coming too since I came on the ship[. It is quiet, secluded and I dont get bothered. [In the corner I sat waiting on my 'date.' I drank away at my cup of coffee, triple sugar in order to keep my excited. I was pumped about meeting up with Jon; I hadn't seen him in like … 2 years! He read the Michigan Express, reading the weekly wrestling section. Not much about much to be honest, the usual rating figures and rumor rundown. The waitress approached, tall, slim and blonde. I paused for a second, before chuckling at myself.] 'Shut up Manuel!' [It couldn't really be the same dream chick, could it? Her badge read 'Becky,' with a cheesy quote in size 2 under her name… 'Happy 2 Help.' Mixing numbers with letters isn't cool, on name badges! She approached all smiles, as I titled my sunglasses down to the tip of my nose.] Becky: 'Hi, Mr. Labor… this is compliments of the man, at the juke box.' [As she handed over a pint of what appeared to be Miller™. I smiled, before rolling my eyes over at the Juke Box, at that precise moment, 'Suspicious Minds,' by Elvis began to play as Jon, strolled over, he was always a big fan of Elvis, and even done the wrestling gimmick more than once. I stood to show my appreciation. Embracing with long time friend, Jon Fuller. Him and I, used to wrestle back in HWF, he was under the name Jontastic back in the day. He's been one of my good, possibly best friends since I was 19 years old.] Manuel Labor: 'How's it going man?' I asked, eagerly. Fuller: 'Not too shabby my friend, I see your doing well.' He told me, sitting in the booth. [We both sat in the corner table, which was somewhat converted into a booth. We had so much to catch up on. He unzipped his jacket and stuffed it aside his lap. I was all smiles at seeing one of my old time buddies.] Manuel Labor: 'Yeah. Well, I'm doing bad, that's for sure. How have you been man? I haven't seen you in ages! What brings you to America anyway?' I asked him in wonder. Fuller: 'Well, my Mother hasn't been to well as of late, she's been under the weather. So I had to get a flight over from the old sunny United Kingdom, over here to see Mum. But… because I'm in the states I couldn't help but leave you the message on your answer machine. Glad you came, I was relying on a text message on getting myself here.' Manuel Labor: 'Hah, well. I wouldn't miss it man! Sorry to hear about your Mum. Be sure to tell her I was asking for her.' I told him, saddened by what I had heard. Fuller: 'Will do. So how are things? PCW Property now, huh? I saw you on TV. We don't get much PCW on direct TV in the UK; we see enough of it though. I saw you face Flaymin Aymin a week or two back, and saw you fight some Jackson fella. What's the deal with you losing man? You really need me to tone you up again? He said smiling. [I laughed.] Manuel Labor: 'No senior, I'm just getting started, PCW hasn't seen anything yet. I'm much more focused, and I'm in better shape than I ever have been. My intelligence level is increasing rapidly. And for a wrestling veteran I'm still learning a hell of a lot, as the week's progress. Day by day I find myself in the same predicament, wondering whether or not I should reveal myself and true talent to PCW , as of yet… I've came to the conclusion that, those matches you had talked about… [I took a slight pause.] They were nothing! They were a tool scraping my ring rust! Now that I'm all polished up and as good as new, I'm ready to go… and Cloud nine is just a stepping stone in getting to where I want to be in this business! [Jon looked overwhelmed. He taught me a lot, not it all, but what was needed, when it was needed. Therefore I give him all the credit in the world. He looked at me, head to toe and back again.] Fuller: 'Well, kid. You do look better it has to be said. Your upper body, has gained a few inches since I last seen you. Shame little Manuel hadn't done the same! [I laughed at his remark, as did he. He was always a practical joker, even bigger than myself! He signaled for the waitress, no not Becky, Becky's colleague. Melissa, also 'Happy 2 Help.'] Fuller: 'Hi, could I have a café latte please. Nice and thick, if you could.' He smiled, charmingly. Melissa: 'Sure no problem, I'll bring it right over sir.' She said recording the order in her notepad. Manuel: 'It's good to see you Senor. You need to come to a PCW show! I'll get you a VIP pass and backstage since thats where I work as well and ever- [He cut me off by raising his hands, in somewhat of a pleading manner he smiled.] Fuller: 'Look man, with all do respect I have no intentions of coming to a PCW show. It's a good business and a great company, but it's just not my style to be involved in. I appreciate the offer I really do, and I wish you all the luck with your PCW career, I wish you all the best. As far as I go? Wrestling is the least of my worries right now my Mother needs me, she needs me bad. I'm only over this side of the Atlantic to see her, and you for today.' [Melissa came back with the latte, placing it on a coaster in front of Jon. I was a little gob smacked as to what he had just said, but I could understand where he was coming from. The reasons being left to themselves. He's never been a fan of PCW to say the least. I stared down at the pint he had bought me, temptation is a great thing, depicts you from whether or not to do it. So ... as I drank the pint ever so smoothly, he took a sip or two of his café latte.] Manuel: “Ah, nothing better, huh?” I told him putting the pint tumbler on the table. Fuller: “Nothing. So, what’s the deal with you and PCW? Long term? I hear your the Extreme Champion?” Manuel: “Well, I’ll be there as long as it takes and yeah, I dealt with that waster Stryker and a few others after him.” Fuller: “Yeah?” He asked frowning his eyebrows. Labor: “Yup. I’m really looking forward to it though. It’s my chance, win or not, to prove myself in this company. Show everyone I’m not just another signing, I am the real fuckin’ deal. To show them that I’m not just any side show mid carder. I am the future of this business like it or not, anyone who has a problem with it, can take it up with me at Cloud nine. I don’t care if you’re nice, cool or as good as a gum tree!” [Manuel took another gulp at his drink. Fuller smiled.] Fuller: “Not lost anything as far as confidence is concerned now have we Manny?” Labor: “Uh uh.” I sounded, still drinking my drink. [Slamming the glass tumbler onto the table in front of him, Manuel wiped his mouth giving a nice ‘Ahh…’ in appreciation to the pint.] [Fuller smiles, rolling his eyes.] Fuller: “Yeah well just make sure you can back all this upman! Look I got to get heading, I don’t want to be late.” He told me, standing from the booth. Manuel Labor: “Not a problem dude.” [I stood also …] ”You take care of yourself, I appreciate you coming all the way out here, I’ll give you a call through the week.” [Manuel shook the hand of long-time friend Jon.] Fuller: “You too buddy, I’ll talk to you soon.” He said as he left. [Closing the door over behind him, Fuller had left. Manuel Labor turned staring into the distance. We kept our camera’s rolling as Manuel began talking, muttering a few words.] Manuel Labor: I will surpass the rest of my opposition. I’ll overcome, see further and overlook all the odds, I may be a rookie in the eyes of most, a veteran known to others. But as of this day until I am pronounced PCW World Champion. The name Prince Of Violence will do me fine. [.Fade.]
