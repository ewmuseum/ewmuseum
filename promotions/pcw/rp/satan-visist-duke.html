Date: 7/11...thank you come again! -Duke Williams is reading the latest in the series of O’Reilly books. No spin in this promo. He sits on the coach when a knock is heard at the door of his Missoula Mansion. He grabs a shot gun and answers the door.- Williams: Who the hell is it? Door: Its Osama Bin Ladden I was wondering if I could hide out here? The caves were ruff on my back. -A familiar yet annoying voice is heard.- Williams: Your about three words away from getting shot. -Duke cocks the gun then swings the door open. Standing before him is Gilbert Godfrey and an MTV camera crew.- Williams: What the hell? Godfrey: I’m I going to stand out here all day and get bitten by these annoying misquotes or are you going to let me in? Williams: What is this all about? Godfrey: About five minutes if your talking segment wise. Williams: What are you doing here? Godfrey: Selling girlscout cookies. What do you think I’m doing here? -Duke slams the door in Gilbert’s face.- Godfrey: What an asshole!! -An MTV producer steps up and knocks on the door. Duke doesn’t answer it he just yells through the door.- Williams: I’m loading my gun. Producer: Mr. Williams if you would please open the door I’m with MTV. Williams: What the hell is an MTV? -Godfrey looks at the producer and shakes his head.- Godfrey: He lives in Montana what do you want? Producer: Its like CMT only with out the country. -Still through the door- Williams: Now that doesn’t make any damn sense. Ok hold on. -The door flies open.- Producer: Duke my name is Justin Case producer at MTV. We have a new show where people write into us with names of celebrities they would like to see interviewed by other celebrities. A fan from Tulsa, Oklahoma wanted to see Gilbert Godfrey interview Duke Williams. Would you do it for the fan? Williams: This fan has a sick sense of humor. Ok come on in. Gilbert: Your not kidding.. -The two men sit down at a kitchen table. Duke lights up a cigar.- Gilbert: So do you enjoy rolling around in the ring with other men in tights? Williams: Do you value your life? Gilbert: Freemarket or other? Williams: Keep joking boy. Gilbert: I intend too! So your electrocuting women is that a thrill to you? Williams: I did what I have to do to win. I don’t to play game and I go down to the level or my opponent no matter how sick or twisted that needs to be. Gilbert: What if your opponent was a flaming homosexual would you stoop down to his ahhhh level? Williams: Keep pushing it hoss and you may find out when I make you my prison bitch. Gilbert: Ok lets move on then. What’s your favorite color. Williams: Black and yellow. Gilbert: If you could be on animal what would it be? Williams: A panther. Gilbert: I hear the black attack is recruiting. Williams: I hear an ambulance coming for you. Gilbert: Gotcha. It says here I have to hype up a match with you Chris Chase, and Legend INC versus Clyde, Dark Star, and some other guy who’s name is smudged from the jelly from my donut. Who’s the mystery guy? Williams: Some goblin or some other stupid sh*t. Gilbert: So how tough can a guy who’s named Clyde be? Wait no I change my mind I think big bad Clyde is going to win. Williams: I think you’re a fucking moron. Clyde is a tough son of a bitch but he’s also a silent son of a bitch. I wish I could buy him for what he’s worth and sell him for what he think he is. I’d be a damn millionaire. Gilbert: Whatever., -Duke grabs Gilbert by his ear and throws him out of his house.- =w=
