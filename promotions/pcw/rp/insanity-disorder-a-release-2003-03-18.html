Insanity Disorder: A release

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-18-03 'I'm unemployed in the mind but my body is a store mannequin 40 hours a weekWithout this job my week would be an empty glasswith one annoying crack that needs to be filled with somethingSo I might as well rise with the sunand pretend we all have a purpose to live out' -unknown- I manage to recycle what I’ve lost, over and over, and over again!Never realizing what I’ve truly lost comes from within!I stagger to my obstacles, hurdling over with ease,Forgetting what makes life work, resting one what makes it cease!Relaxing my fucking brain hurts, knowing more than it ever wanted,Seeking more than it ever wanted, having more, than it could ever handle!In seeing this I discover scandle.Approximately moronic devices get thrown into the path, now halted,Torn down by those whose past has faulted,All my fucking wounds burn, heavily salted!And my head, my fucking head hurts listening to the majority shriek out!Yelling in pain, voicing their thoughts in uneducated words and cloutWho the hall are you I ask!While I keep cheating on myself,Having affairs with attractive thoughts in persuasive clothing.Moving without moving, the song remains the same, again.Knowing without knowing.My heart beats through my brain, again!Louder, harder, than any one of my repressed dreamsAnd visions lash out, silencing my screams,Through all the loss familiar means,I’m back again, to inbetweens. The time in life when majority rules,Disturbing the fools I stand alone!Burn every bridge over the troubled waters.Now stranded on an island full of fermented disorders.You could never know me like I know me!Don’t pretend to understand the rage that I am holding!I’m angry at the universe, for the way she treats me now.Always trying to hold me down!Under a palm of submissive bars,Unable to break free and get very farBefore ending up right back in my cell!Or as I term it now, my personal hell! The fact remains the same, any step at this point is one in the wrong directionSo let me fester amongst the dying and dead upon selection.See, I contemplate madness in it’s favorite little corner.Jailed by my ultimate insanity disorder!Even shadows have shadows in the blackness of my mind.Even harder yet, is what you truly want to find.Another failed case of the blind leading the blind.My sickness remains, evermore, confined! I give up soon, or my ego does at least.Getting trumped on every move in the belly of the beast!Finding myself playing your game, your rules, on your board of insanity.But insanity to you is just a second home to me!I hide my dark intentions behind a well misplaced smileAll the time you think that things are sober for a while.Until I drug you with pain in which I find true pleasure!And twist and dig in deeper, holding tighter at my leisure.You’re screams I hear already, and inside you do as well!I know you, I was you, now all that’s left is an unused shell.From out of which rose a being, made by a mixture of revenge and hateCount your days, live them out, don’t you dare fucking hesitate! The reaper, he will find you, your bereavement he will sew.Not a threat, not a warning, just thought you’d like to know. You’re ass is mine. Taking a deep breath A.J. Trinidad steps off the stage, out of the flooded light and into the quieted audience. Eyes of the thirty plus people in attendance at Long Beach’s Studio Resolve beam through A.J. as he calmly pushes aside a chair and makes his way to the door. One lone clap breaks the eery silence. A flood of applause echoes and vibrates afterwards as the acoustic building praises one man’s voice on a night of Spoken Word, when no other seem to have stewed up from quite as deep inside a soul. Trinidad offers not a look back over his shoulder as he pushes the door open and exits the club to find a cab waiting for him outside. “Long Beach Arena” Trinidad orders to the driver, “I’ve got some business to attend to.” The cab door shuts and the taxi pulls away into traffic disappearing in the sea of alike yellow colored cars.
