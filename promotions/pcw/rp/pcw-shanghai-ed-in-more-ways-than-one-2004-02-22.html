PCW: Shanghai'ed (in more ways than one)

22 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/04 Baggage claim seems to have gotten slower. Even China has beefed up security and screenings after 9/11. We should really get that phrase put into the dictionary: 'after 9/11.' So much comes from it. Anyway, the customs and border officials usually just speed people through if they have American passports. This time was no exception. I had the girls' passports ordered in time for the February 2nd schedule for Shanghaied. We're behind twenty days, but all the better; the passports arrived a week late. It wasn't the passports that weren't in order, really. It was our work visas. Rent-A-Hero and I were at adjacent counters at the same time. 'What do you mean there's a problem with my work visa? It's through PCW, and the federation is still up and running. They cleared all the paperwork before giving us our tickets.' The border official obviously couldn't have been of much help. He didn't know why the visas weren't in order, just that they weren't. Of course, that didn't mean that we'd be detained at the airport. All of our U.S. Passports were still good, and those gave us access to the country regardless. As it was, we just wouldn't be able to wrestle with the work visas we acquired through PCW. Not until this little issue of why they weren't being accepted through the government database was cleared up. Zoober and James were there with us, having been on the plane with the rest of us. I think the only ones who weren't on the plane were Mike Wade, Brian Arson, Shaun Styles, Donovan Emerson, Naomi, MSA, and Blitz. The former four were on a different plane, Naomi and MSA had a layover in Japan, and I didn't even know when and how Inphino was getting into China. Rent-A-Hero wanted to go to the hotel and get his things unpacked. I let the girls go with them, but I stayed behind with some of the other guys. We were going to stay put until someone gave us an answer about our visas. Having a physical presence there is always better than just leaving people to think that they can slack off and not do their jobs while you're gone. PCW Shanghai'ed. Live from Shanghai, China. It's one of those traditions that we weren't giving up. We'd already thrashed the March Madness tradition with the late pay-per-view in the new year, but no getting around it I suppose. The opening was a bit rocky. There couldn't have been a December pay-per-view even if we did start on time. The lateness of the first show only pushed things back further; in fact, it wasn't until two weeks ago that James and Zoober decided to bother with the Shanghaied pay-per-view. Boda and I thought that that was the problem, that our work visas might have been cleared for early February instead of late February. But such a problem is easily corrected, and really, James and Zoober should have handled it by the time of our arrival. We were still held up for a while as it was. Some of the other wrestlers were also gathering. Everyone wanted an answer. It was Saturday, after 5pm, and we were supposed to be wrestling the following night as far as we knew. This sort of thing was something that we wanted to get in check as soon as possible. Finally, Zoober and James told us that the problem was through internal affairs. Luckily, the government doesn't shut down on the weekends like in the U.S., so they'd try to get it cleared up before the show tomorrow. In the meantime, they suggested that we just go on to the hotel and get checked in. A bus was waiting for us (longer than the driver thought that he'd have to) to take us to the hotel. Feeling a little uneasy (since we know that those two are as incompetent as can be when it comes to something important to us as wrestlers) about the whole thing, there was still nothing much we could do aside from checking in. We crammed into the small bus and went on our way.
