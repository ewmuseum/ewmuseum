Ragamuffins Revenge - Part 3 - JELLO!

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 21, 2002 [What can a man really do when he's pushed the the brink of madness? Ridiculed by everyone and everything that he comes across, only known as a name that he despises. He never made the name up, it was that dumbass who made it up. In a feeble attempt to be funny, to get a rise out of his clan of followers, the man called him the name...this dumbass spoke the word that would haunt forever. The man known as 'Totally Tubular' Tommy spoke the word 'Ragamuffin'. How do you avoid the name when everybody Tommy knows, is pretty much everybody in Los Angeles? The entire city was uttering the name Ragamuffin as if it were a common thing. They pointed, laughed, and screamed with hilarity. These people didn't know what was good for them, they were just following a stupid trend that would make them cool. They were calling him Ragamuffin simply because they wanted to have the life of Tommy, they wanted the fame and fortune. If they just called a poor, ugly man Ragamuffin then all would be well in their lives. The only thing that it did was drive the man closer to madness. So now he strives to find the man who started it all to make sure he no longer destroys lives. Sandy Wong is in grave danger and the man who can save her was beaten horribly by that dumbass surfer. He also is feeling a hint of madness now, realizing that he cannot face up to being denied. It's eating away at his soul, just like Ragamuffin. They now are sharing the same kind of bond with one another, a bond of madness. God save them all] Old Man: JELLO!! [The senior citizen slams his cane down onto the floor with little force. At one time the man would have snapped the cane in two, but now he can barely keep a hold of it. He never thought himself to be in a place like he is in now. A retirement home, living with people just like him. Dying people, sick people, forgotten people. Sleep, Talk, Medication, and Jello. These are the things that are everyday life for the people of Greensboro Retirement Home. Their children don't want to take care of them so they let someone else do it for them. A visit every now and then with the grandchildren makes them feel better about themselves. That if they visit their forgotten relative somehow it makes it all the better. That they did the right thing for their father or mother] Old Man: Give me my damn jello, boy! [The thin, dark face of Ragamuffin comes into view as he frantically searches for a bowl of jello. The room is tiny and smells like crap. Dressed in green scrubs, Ragamuffin tries to fit into this place. He needs sanctuary from the police who are just two block away. They spotted him going into Canada and the chase was on every since. Now he's stuck in some old folks home trying to figure out how to get out of the state of North Dakota. He needs to know where Tommy has gone after Fusion, after that horrible attack on The End. The hurting has already begun and Ragamuffin was not there to stop it. He failed in one battle, but he will not fail again] Ragamuffin: Sir, I'm trying to find your jello but I have no idea where it went. [The old man whacks Ragamuffin in the shoulder with his cane. It's not hard, but enough to sting his arm] Old Man: DAMNIT! Get my jello. [Randy topples over pictures of grandchildren, war photos, and medals of honor. In search for the man's missing jello. He needs to find it and he needs to do it fast. The longer it takes the louder the old man will yell, which will bring the other workers into the room. They'll easily recognize him as the escaped lunatic and surely the police will arrive in seconds. He can't let a senile old man destroy his quest to stop 'Totally Tubular' Tommy] Ragamuffin: Just calm down, sir. I'm going to have it for you in a second. Old Man: One... THAT'S IT! IT'S NOT HERE! YOU LIAR!! [The old man slams Ragamuffin in the ass with his cane. The surprise shot sends Ragamuffin through the flimsy wall into another room. There, in front of him, sits a large bowl of green Jello that would make Bill Cosby cream in his pants. The old woman is out cold and didn't even stir when Ragamuffin smashed through the wall. Ragamuffin grabs the bowl with a smile, the first time he's smiled in a very long time. He turns to the old man with the jello] Old Man: MY JELLO! [Ragamuffin places it on the man's tray and hands him the plastic spoon. The old man begins digging into the jello as if he hadn't eaten for days] Ragamuffin: Eat up, sir. Old Man: Yum...mmmmm...Jello. [Ragamuffin looks outside of the window, which has a great view of the small town outside. The police still surround most of the town and search into barber shops, grocery stores, and antique shops. They are questioning pedistrians who might have caught a glimpse of the ugly man who is known as Ragamuffin. He feels trapped in a corner now. He thinks about giving it up that maybe Tommy isn't worth all of this trouble. That he should go back to that institution and get a bowl of jello as well. It wasn't so bad there really. NO! What else does he have to lose? Nothing. Absolutely nothing] Old Man: Son... [Ragamuffin turns from the window] Ragamuffin: Yeah? Old Man: You smell like shit. Ragamuffin: I was stuck on a truck with pigs, sir. Old Man: I feel sorry for the pigs then. SMELLY BASTARD! [Ragamuffin smells his armpits and gags at the smell] Old Man: Well, I thank you for getting me that jello, boy. Everybody in this place seems to ignore me, forget that I exist. Just because I ain't what I used to be doesn't mean I'm still not here. My own family doesn't even care. Yesterday was my birthday and not one person even came in here to wish me a Happy Birthday. They had important things to do like hiking in the mountains, enjoying life. I don't think it would have hurt them to visit for five minutes with the man who raised them. The man who gave them life! Ragamuffin: I'm sorry to hear that, sir. Happy Birthday, by the way...even though it's a day late. Old Man: You don't work here. You know, I may be old but I'm not stupid. Those cops outside must be looking for you. [Ragamuffin looks a little shocked] Old Man: Son, it doesn't matter what you did. Your obviously running because you didn't really do anything. That's why most people run in the first place. Always being wrongfully accused, always the brunt of the joke. What'd you do? Ragamuffin: I didn't do a thing, sir. I was pushed by this dumb surfer guy to the brink of my sanity. It's ridiculous that this started over a nickname, a little insult. Then it spread like a disease and now nobody even knows my real name. Not like anybody would care anyway. I broke out of the mental institution to save a woman from getting hurt by the same man. Everbody thinks this guy is so great and that he's a heck of a nice guy, but I see right through him. He's evil, greedy, and will take anything he gets his hands on even if he can't have it. I'm the only one that knows his true nature and I'm going to finally put an end to it and show the world that I AM NOT RAGAMUFFIN! [A nurse walks in and sees the escaped man sitting next to Mr. Donofrio. She runs out of the room and to the nurses station. Randy and the Old Man, Mr. Donofrio, share a few moments of silence which is stopped by the sound of flat feet running toward the room] Police: FREEZE!! [Ragamuffin knows only one thing to do, and that's leap toward the window. He dives two stories down into thorn bushes. The cold air rips into his cuts as he screams in agony. The police outside are stunned by the man's fall and don't chase him. The police inside are bewildered of how fast this psycho could move] Cop: Damn that Ragamuffin... [Mr. Donofrio smashes the cop in the face with the cane] Mr. Donofrio: HE IS NOT RAGAMUFFIN!! [A man in a trenchcoat, surrounded by little children enters the room] Man: Dad? What's going on here? [Mr. Donofrio pulls back his cane with force and whacks his son in the testicles] Man: ARGH!!! Mr. Donofrio: THAT'S FOR NOT WISHING ME A HAPPY BIRTHDAY, BITCH! [The scene fades and reopens on a private jet heading from Canada to California. On the side ia a blue and yellow surfboard that reads 'Tubular' underneath it. The plane can only be one man's 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, who sits on the blue and yellow leather couch with a martini in his hand. He wears a blue and yellow suit and tie, and a million dollar smile on his face. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, brother and now manager of Tommy, sits across from him in his black leather vibrating chair. He's decked out in his Twisted Sister gear with a stoned smile on his face] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It was most bodacious the way I took out The End, dude. I feel so alive right now. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: That was the first step in the right direction for us, compadre. We showed everybody in that locker room that nobody messes with The Tubular One like that. Nobody says no to me or else you'll feel the gnarly wrath of a steel chair against your bogus skull. Now all we do is relax, drink a few more excellent martini's and go to Decimation to become the Continental Champion. Harry: ...and to get Sandy Wong, man. TTT: Of course! [A beautiful stewardess comes into view. She wears a blue blouse with a yellow skirt that just comes to the bottom of her butt. It's Tommy's dress code for bodacious stewardess babes on his plane. With her large breasts swelled up in the tight blouse she sits down another martini beside Tommy. Her blonde hair is up in a bun as her red lips part into a beautiful white smile] Stewardess: The news just said that Ragamuffin was being pursued by the police in North Dakota, Tommy. It's only a matter of time before they catch him. TTT: GNARLY! That will be one less bogus compadre that we'll have to worry about. Harry: Yeah, man. Stewardess: Will you be needing anything else, Tommy? [The bodacious dude, and soon to be PCW Continental Champion, looks the beautiful woman up and down] TTT: I believe there is one other bodacious request that I have, babe. [She smiles wickedly and begins making her way toward the bathroom. Tommy gives Harry a thumbs up and then heads to the bathroom. Harry doesn't notice and turns up the massaging fingers in his leather chair] Harry: That won't do, man. [Harry turns it to full max and the chair shoots him forward and through the small table beside Tommy's leather couch] Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! LETS DO THAT AGAIN! [Fade to black]
