The Dating Game Part 1

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/5/03 It was a beautiful day in Atlanta Georgia the sun was beaming down a slight breeze was there to take away the humidity and people were walking around without a care in the world, the same could be said a few thousand miles away in Sydney Australia although it was fast approaching winter, children were still playing on the streets with Frisbee’s and balls just watching the hours tick by until their loving parents called them in for some Kangaroo burgers and fries. In fact it was as though the whole world was a beautiful place, with no war and no famine just beautiful weather and fun…except for in Berlin where James Edwards was stood, on the site of the torn down Berlin Wall the rain thrashing his hair and his body, a small grimace on his face as he stares blankly in front of him. James: Bloody Weatherman he goes and says, leave your jackets at home today as it’s going to be sun sun sun in sunny Berlin I step out of my hotel suite and bamn it’s raining…it’s a conspiracy a bloody conspiracy I tell you. James stared down at the floor before raising his head slightly glancing left and right trying to imagine what it was like to have been there that fateful night when the wall had fallen, he could remember sitting at home watching the events unfold on the television but he could not fathom the sense of freedom the people must have experienced, the feeling of liberation the feeling of wonder the feeling of James: Oh come on! James’ wondering mind had been disrupted as a low flying bird had decided that James’ hand would be the perfect spot for it to deposit it’s waste in the form of a large cream coloured piece of faeces landed with a disconcerting splat. Reaching into his trouser pocket with his untarnished hand he fished out a piece of tissue paper and began to wipe away the bird excrement before disposing of the wet, gooey paper in the nearest trash can. James had taken it upon himself to fly out to Berlin early, having not been booked for Fusion, spend a few days training, taking in the sites as Turmoil was not going to be a cake walk. A gauntlet match the memo had said, leaving James in a state as he did not know exactly what a gauntlet match was, all he knew was that he would go out there and fight like he always did and go for the win, something he had been doing quite a lot recently. The relative silence which was only not total silence due to the rain fall that the meteorologists had failed to notice was broken by the horrible bleeping sound, which was supposed to represent ride of the valkyries coming from James’ mobile telephone, James looked dazed for a moment looking around wondering where that infernal beeping was coming from before realising that it was his own phone, picking it out of his pocket he pushed the receive call button and raised the phone to his ear. James: Hi there your speaking to James Edwards how can I help you? Female voice: James sweetie how are you doing this fine day, it’s Sharon here I’ve got a proposition for you. James glanced at the grey sky for a moment, the rain trickling down his chin to his neck before speaking into the phone once more. James: Sharon…I don’t actually know a Sharon maybe you’ve got the wrong number. Sharon: Sweetie darling, I’m your agent, you know sort out your public appearances, get you more money then take a nice big wedge of it. That sort of thing. A puzzled look took over James’ face, his normal ever present smile had faded somewhat as he was trying to comprehend exactly what was being said by a woman he only knew as Sharon saying that he now had an agent. James: I don’t have an agent, I just wrestle you know I didn’t realise I needed an agent for that. Sharon: Of course you do sweetie, without me well you would be lost without me, I’ve been sorting out your plane tickets and accommodation ever since you joined PCW, did you never think how the tickets were just arriving on your doorstep? PCW assigns all new wrestlers agents so that they don’t have to worry about this sort of thing. James: …ok Sharon: Anyway sweetie, here’s the deal, I’ve got a bit of television work lined up for you. James: No… no way I’m a wrestler I can’t go on television and not wrestle I don’t want to be one of these sell-out people. Sharon: Nonsense sweetie, you’re a celebrity now and that’s exactly what you are wanted to appear on. They are currently filming a celebrity edition of the dating game, basically if you win you’ll have to take out some normal girl out to dinner wine and dine her pretend you had a good time then tell the about it the next show. James: Celebrity? James’ voice was full of shock and to some extent awe, he had not fathomed the idea that he would or even could be considered a celebrity, sure he wanted to remain focused as a wrestler but if he could get more people to pay attention to him, he had to exploit the chance. Sharon: What? James: Celebrity, you said a celebrity edition of the dating game…I’m considered a celebrity now? Sharon: Sweetie! How naïve you are, the Germans love wrestling they eat it up like a frankenfurter. James: You mean Frankfurter, Frankenfurter was the transvestite off the rocky horror show. There is silence for a moment a slight mumbling can be heard through James’ earpiece, which resembles many words that could not be printed here for legal reasons until Sharon breaks the silence once more. Sharon: Sweetie, that’s not important let’s just say they want you do you want to be involved or not? I know you are an attractive lad a very attractive lad with muscles and…anyway even if you don’t win it’s a bit of exposure isn’t it. What do you say? James pondered for a moment he really didn’t want to do this, but he knew deep down he’d have to, after all this was all for one thing in the end he had to do anything and everything to achieve his dream, no matter how humiliating the actions…or consequences. James: Ok…it’s a deal. Sharon: Oh this is great sweetie I just know you’re going to do so well, anyway sweetie be at the studios tomorrow about seven in the morning, I’ll send you the address later. James: Sharon. Sharon: Yes sweetie? James: Please stop calling me sweetie it’s just so…annoying Sharon: Ok swe…James. The line goes dead leaving James standing in the rain in a dazed like state, similar to how he had been for the duration of the phone call. In just a few minutes the hoard of information that had been delivered to James was slowly taking effect, seeping into the walls of his brain, which a lot of the time were a lot thicker than they should be. James: I’m a celebrity…hmm I can deal with that.
