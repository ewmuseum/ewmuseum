Humanity Pollution

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 26th, 2002 November 26th, 2002Journal Entry – Thailand Airline8:17pm Dear log, My journey to the United States was one that I soon wished I would forget, and have it placed into the back of my mind never to be seen again. The constant lack of intelligence here is more and more of a distraction to me then these people know. The only thing that would have made the plain ride good was if it crashed into the ground, desinigrating into millions of pieces without hesitation. I couldn’t count how many problems these people went through, just to take a simple lift to the States. “I order a window seat no an alley seat!” I had to listen to this lower class sloth rant about his position on the plane. There is nothing to see out in the sky that you can’t see on the ground. But yet his mind told him he was right and for countless minutes, this being made a fool of him self. Was his window seat worth his dignity or didn’t he mind trading in what little respect he may have had in for a chance to see clouds float by. How I wish I could have just thrown him outside and shown him something more glamorous then a window seat. A first class ticket to the ground below. And if that wasn’t enough, I had the enjoyment of being seated in first class. What a joyous occasion, considering the greed of these people. To imagine that one of the passengers wanted the curtain closed so the lower class citizens didn’t see him. In fact the curtain would be an improvement, since I don’t believe they wanted to see him either. It was a shook to me, to see these people with the diamonds and fancy clothing, sit here and waste their time on a public flight. I thought they had enough money to afford a private jet, but it just higher my suspicion that they wanted to save a few cents. “I would like a Sirloin steak, medium rare with all the fizens please.” I hope they enjoy stuffing their face and enjoying them self. The moment they crash down to reality with by a moment I will cherish for life. I can picture them, or more importantly Romantico, bowing to his knees in forgiveness, wanting something for nothing. Typical being, always wanting a handout, but it pains me to believe even he of all people would stoop to that level. But this is Romantico we are talking about here. He can only get easy handouts considering no human would dare associate with him. But besides his constant attempts to throw me off my game, these people aboard this flight have polluted the world with their dribble of comments and thoughts. The question remains is why didn’t I take a private jet? Maybe I wanted to get back in with society and see what actually makes these people so ignorant to each other. Or maybe it was to prove my point that I am the more enlightened one. But whatever reason it was a reason in it’s self. “I don’t want to hear another word out of you for the rest of this trip.” Such a loving family isn’t it. A mother you can be proud of, yelling at her child who barley made a peep. If anyone shouldn’t speak it’s her. I have got to say this trip proves that this world is a pollution and the people involved are like a never ended usage of dirt and grim. It sickens me to think I have to live with these people, while they waste my air, my water and my life. Cowards are they all. They should all be dealt with quickly and painfully. November 27th, 2002Hotel Suite - Amarillo, Texas6:52 a.m. He stood alone, awaiting the rise of the sun. Normally Vilas would get those extra minutes available of sleep, but after the torture of the plain ride and the re-occurring dream of his failure at Alliances, sleeping was no longer an option. He just focused his eyes out on the city landscape of this foreign land and dreamed of the possible endings that could occur. I never did look at the bright side of things. The worst case scenario was the best case to me. Who really wants to play over in their mind how they are going to defeat their rival? You don’t learn anything for thinking the best possible endings. You have to play the worst endings then you can avoid them at all cost. Vilas closed his curtain and returned to his bed, crossing his legs and closing his eyes. He placed his fingers together almost in prayer, but in a different form of meditation. He controlled his thoughts and played the images of his defeat over in his mind. His body would jolt at the sign of an impact, but it wasn’t serious. His silence was broken by a knock at the door. His shot up from his position and raced towards the door, swinging it open with great force. Emptiness…Some kind of joke… Vilas shook his head, and in the corner of his eye a small faint figure could be made out. A flower, more importantly a rose. Red this time and not white like all the rest. Vilas grasped the flower and returned to his roof, locking the door behind him. He walked back to the window, sliding the curtain away and stepping outside. He raised the rose into the sunlight of the morning day. He twisted the rose around in his fingers and carefully studied the flower. This represents the death of humanity. Such a beautiful delight that is used for negative purpose. Each pedal tells a different story, and of course it shares hidden information. Unlike Romantico, I don’t hide behind my own mask. Sure no one ever sees my true face, but at least I am not hidden behind a world of lies. One sickens joke and one gentle game later and people suggest you to a special doctor. But who really needs the help. I am the one who held my team alive in those cells. I kept them alive for as long as I could and they were ungrateful to me. Should I repay them in anyway? Well maybe sometime down the road but for now it’s not any concern. Why dwell on the past of my failures. That was before, and this is now and a whole new time for me to strive forward. It starts today and starts when the day falls short. Vilas gently takes the rose and picks off each pedal into his hand. He discards the steam and rubs the pedals in his fist. He takes s strong breathe in and soaks up the smell of the sign. He closes his eyes and dreams of the negative energy that surrounded his face. His positive energy would turn them blind, and the each thought would add a different aspect to his pain and furry. Romantico’s wish will be my pleasure to proceed. He has gotten the best of me before, but now there is no where to hide his face. A mask can’t control his fate. I will decide whether he moves on to not, and until I am ready to continue, he will remain a lowly creature of my imagination. Vilas takes the rose pedals and throw them into the air, watching as they float down to the ground slowly, getting lifted by the stiff breeze. Vilas smiles as closes his eyes one last time. “You took a wrong turn. There is no way back.”
