Voices in the Dark....Inner Demons

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-3-03 The scene opens into a screen of pitch black. What sounds like running footsteps can be heard in the darkness, and as the footfalls grow louder, we can also hear the muffled sound of exerted breathing. Suddenly, the sound of steel on steel rings out, and in the spark that erupts from the meeting of two blades, we can make out the forms of two large men. Flowing hair accents them both, and they appear to be deadlocked. From above, a white light shines down to illumine the two combatants. One man wears shining golden chain mail armor. His sword glows with an inner light, and his blue eyes hold a vast look of knowledge, mixed with an age that doesn’t seem to match his body’s years. His opponent is a darker version of himself. He stands darkly in black armor, wielding a sword that glows with an inner darkness, seeming the epitome of evil. It is the same sword that the white fighter wields, yet twisted to darkness…TWO PENDRAGON’S!!!! Evil Pendragon: Thou should have stayed where thy had been hiding these last months, Pendragon…. Pure Pendragon: Thou speakest words that thou will yet fail to back up, vermin! Pendragon lashes out with a flurry of offense, and the light bounces off his sword as the two men almost seem to dance…A deadly death indeed. The Dark Pendragon backs until finally he braces himself and deflects a blow and launches into an attack of his own…. Evil Pendragon: I own thee….Thou just does not see it as truth yet. The insane desire thou hast for conquest….That is I! The love of the battle….It comes from within, does it not? Pure Pendragon grits his teeth into a humorless grin as he braces against the onslaught…. Evil Pendragon: When thou lookest inside thy ownself; who does thy see? Pure Pendragon drops his sword and backs up a couple of steps, a horrible light in his eyes. The darker version merely stands grinning, as he lowers his own sword. Evil Pendragon: I grow stronger each day….Thou should have stayed in hiding…. Pure Pendragon bows his head and peers up at the dark visage through disheveled locks of hair, and he begins to smile…. Pure Pendragon: Never….. The Dark Pendragon leans in a little closer, trying to discern the words…. Evil Pendragon: What hast thou proclaimed? Pure Pendragon: NEVER!!!!! With that he twirls his sword high overhead with both hands and aims a slash at the neck of the unbelieving Evil Pendragon. Just as the sword strikes home, the sound of thunder breaks the silence after the scream, and a lightning bolt streaks for the tip of the sword, and the scene shifts….. ****FLASH**** The scene is now a semi illuminated bedroom. A sleeping Venneman Pendragon jumps awake in the middle of his large bed…. Pendragon: Good Christ! The dreams grow ever darker…. From out of nowhere floats a disembodied voice…. Voice: THOU SHOULD HAVE STAYED GONE!!!!!! Pendragon wraps his massive arms around himself and shudders. He rolls to the edge of the bed and stands up. Clad only in a pair of breeches, sweat glistens off of his body as he walks to a window with a wonderful view of the full moon. His golden hair falls back from his head, longer than we remember it, and his eyes hold that look that has instilled fear in so many…Knowledge with a hint of insanity. What holds the man together? We know it not…He begins to speak as he peers out at the moonlight… Pendragon: What have I done to deserve these demons? What must I do to exorcise them? He bows his head in a small prayer, before continuing…. Pendragon: And now I toss my blade back into the fold…The life I left behind has called me back. The people I turned my back on in favor of locating mine own sanity have beckoned mine return. But what is it I return to? The PCW landscape has dreadfully changed since I last donned the cloak of competition… He shakes his head and lets out a small chuckle…. Pendragon: The more things change…The more things stay the same…Once more, my influence holds in a big way in PCW. Friday, I made my return. To watch the cowardly group of feeble half men battle for a prize I once sought, it made mine own stomach turn. A champion who would rather be…Dare I say it? IN the Ocean rather than defending his title…And a man whose very music makes my head ache; Dante Jackson…The Lyrical Messiah. I prefer to do my rapping on the heads of blowhards such as this fellow, but I digress. Twas the fans call that turned me around…Brought me back… The scene cuts to show Fusion…IN particular the matchup of Tommy, Dante and Skylar Green….. Before the Hero can get back to his feet, a sickening crunch is heard as Jack’s knee connects with the side of Rent-a-Hero’s head. As the Masked Hero drops to the floor, Jack and Totally Tubular Tommy stomp away at him as Dante sits back up, holding his mouth. Jeff: The Masked Moron takes a beating, part two! Duke: It ain’t been a good night for Renty so far! The beatdown continues as Jack breaks off and puts the boots down into Sky while Dante resumes attacking Rent-a-Hero when suddenly, “The Small Hours” by Metallica starts up.... Jeff: Huh? Duke: Crazy Sword Guy!!! ...AND DOWN THE AISLE RIDES VENNEMAN PENDRAGON ON HIS TRUSTY STEED, XERXES!!! Dave: VENNEMAN PENDRAGON IS BACK!!! The Knight leaps off his horse and onto the apron where he blocks a shot by Dante AND HOTSHOTS HIM ON THE TOP ROPE!!! Pendragon then quickly slides into the ring as Totally Tubular Tommy charges him but Pendragon dodges him, bouncing off the ropes AND HITTING THE CONTINENTAL CHAMPION WITH THE SWORD IN THE STONE!!! Dave: PENDRAGON IS CLEARING HOUSE!!! Tommy grabs his spine and rolls to the outside where Heavy Metal Harry supports his weight and helps him up the aisle, Dante Jackson rolling out to follow them as Violence Jack sees Venneman charge him and rolls out to, satisfied his work has been done. Jeff: DAMMIT! Who let him back?!?! Harry, Tommy, Jack and Dante head back up the aisle, as in the ring, Pendragon paces around to a huge cheer while Rent-a-Hero and Skylar Green climb to their feet and watch the heels head up the ramp. ****FLASH**** Back in his bedroom, Pendragon smiles as he looks out onto the night… Pendragon: And mine fans went wild…The bliss that was mine that night brought everything washing back…The victories, the ominous defeats…Everything. And now, mine mission is clear to me once more…Continental Gold…King’s Gold…And anyone in mine way should prepare…. For the pain…. A lightning bolt crashes outside, and Pendragon is engulfed in the white. With his image emblazoned on the screen, the shot finally fades out, but not before someone else has their two cents… Voice: You should have never returned………..
