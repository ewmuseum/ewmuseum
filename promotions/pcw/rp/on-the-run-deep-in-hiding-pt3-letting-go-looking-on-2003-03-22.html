On The Run, Deep In Hiding pt3- Letting Go, Looking On

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd March To create a future, the past must evolve from within you. A beautifully lit red sky in Mexico sets the scene. It is early morning, round about 5am, birds are out flying and chirping, shop owners are preparing to open their stores and El Diablo is standing atop a cliff nearby to a lovely scenic lake, which is still pure blue with no pollution at all in it. Diablo is stood on top of some sandy rocks staring out into the early morning breeze, that gently blows his short red hair back slightly. He clenches his deep brown eyes closed and lets out a loud sigh into the wind. The Mexican Devil wears a thin white vest, blue cut-off jean shorts and brown sandals- in other words, he is dressed like a stereotypical Mexican without the dirt and sweat that is 'supposed' to cover him from head to toe. By Diablo's feet is a large silver pot, which he is being extra careful not to knock over. 'Pedro, it all went wrong when you left us. Without you my life went under, every time I got near a success a series of failures followed me. If you were still around, you'd be able to talk me around like you always used to, you always made me smile, you always made me see the good in life and people then when you were so harshly taken away, everything was bad, and all I could see was the bad, all I still see is the bad and the hurting,' Diablo speaks to Pedro calmly, like he was there. Pedro of course was El Diablo's brother whom died nearly nine years ago. Pedro and Raul were always close, so when he was taken away it really hurt him, and caused his change into El Diablo. Diablo has had such a hard time coping with the death of his brother, as his mother and father blamed him for Pedro's death and that hit him hard- knowing he wasn't loved and that the one person who did love him was gone. Raul slowly kneels down and then sits down with his legs dangling from the edge of the small cliff he is sitting on. He looks next to him at the silver pot and smiles innocently. 'If only you were here now, you'd bring me peace, I know you would. I can just imagine what you'd say right now, 'Cheer up, smile and go to confession, it will really clear your mind,' maybe I will Pedro, it's been so long since I have. Do you remember when we were five and we'd play-fight? You'd say that I was going to be a wrestler, and when you said that it really touched me, you really believed in me, it was your words that helped inspire me into a career of wrestling. I know you never saw me get into PCW, or were here to help me along physically, but spiritually you have been my motivator, I just know you've been watching down on me- no matter who or what I've become. And it really helps me just knowing that,' he continues to speak to his dead brother to find peace within himself. Pedro really was Raul's motivator and that is why even after his brother's death he continued to train to wrestle, to make it big one day. Diablo lived through many dark days after Pedro's premature death, the only light to guide him was the memory of Pedro and that is what spurred him on. Diablo touches the silver pot as another gentle breeze blows his hair back, and the red sky begins to pass by slowly. 'If you were still here I'd probably be different, after all I'd have you to help me through all my dark days. I missed not having you here when I suffered such dark and harsh days after your death. You really affected me, no I don't blame you, I of course and rightfully so blame Flaymin Aymin, your murderer. I know, I know, you'd say I'm holding an unjust grudge, but to me it is very justified and I cannot help that feeling Pedro, I really can't. I feel so empty without you and I feel such anger at Flaymin. You were and still are my guiding light Pedro, but soon that must change, I cannot keep living in the past. I love you Pedro, just know that,' he says while tears fill his eyes. While Diablo was suffering a horrible loss of not just Pedro, but his mother and father the driver of the vehicle that killed Pedro, Flaymin Aymin was enjoying a life style of porn films and beer. When Diablo found out that it was Flaymin that killed Pedro, just thinking about the contrasting times they both had really made The Mexican Devil go crazy. How could Flaymin do it? How could Flaymin pretend to be his friend? Diablo still missed his brother and Aymin brought about his anger and rage. El Diablo promptly blinks to wash away the tears that were stemming. He sniffs and again stares out, but instead looking at the lake. 'Don't get me wrong Pedro, I still hate Flaymin for killing you, but I hate him for something more as well. He ruined my heritage by making me lose my mask, and I know that is something you cherished when I wrestled while you were still alive, I can never forgive him for any of those things. I know that you'd say life is too short not to forgive, and of course you'd know the literal meaning of that, but it is something I cannot do, just as I cannot forgive mother and father for blaming your death on me. They made out like the blood was on my hands, I was so hurt, it made me insane with anger and jealousy because of their love for you, but no I didn't hate you, because it was like you passed their love onto me, and that made me feel good. However Pedro, I can never forgive them for what they did to me. But I just hope you can forgive me for wanting to move forward, wanting to take a grasp on my life once more,' Diablo talks maniacally to the silver urn that holds the ashes of Pedro. Knowing the truth, Diablo's suffering and hurting continued, it was like the truth has opened new channels of hurt, pain and anger that filled El Diablo, it brought him to new lows and a new need for sickness that was evident through Aymin/Diablo I. The Mexican Devil slowly stands and carries the urn up with him as he does. 'Forgive me Pedro, for this needs to be done. Nine years I have kept you here in this urn, and I feel I never released your soul. I kept that urn and your ashes for me, and that was selfish, but I really needed you then Pedro. I need you now, but it is unfair of me to hold on to this, not only for you but also for me- I need to release you to move forward in my life, I need a future, and you need to be free at last. This is the lake we swam in when we went on vacation when we were young, I know you'd like to rest here. I love you Pedro, please forgive me,' Raul says, choking back the tears that are filling him. Diablo swiftly opens the top of the urn and gently scatters the nine-year-old ashes over the edge of the cliff. The ashes float down slowly, until they hit the water, Diablo sighs loudly and closes his eyes. 'Now we can both move forward in our different worlds Pedro. We can both think about each other and be happy knowing that we are brothers. So now I can concentrate on my future, I can be fully mentally and physically involved for Turmoil where I can capture the elusive PCW World Title. Now that we are both set free Pedro, I have a new will and desire to be champion, I am reborn,' Diablo says whilst opening his eyes and smiling at the thought. El Diablo screws the lid of the urn back on and slowly walks away from the cliff as a beautiful blue sky replaces the disappearing red one.
