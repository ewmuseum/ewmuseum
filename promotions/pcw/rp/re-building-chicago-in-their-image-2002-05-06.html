Re-Building Chicago In Their Image.

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 Xavier is far from a happy Scientologist. Ever since he arrived in PCW he has been preaching that he is wrestling’s ONLY Scientologist using that fact to justify his claims of oppression. Being the only Scientologist suited him, it was both something he could proudly claim and use to his advantage, and add to that the TV time he received each week he was the “Golden Boy” of Dianetics despite his apparent lack of scruples. Now things have changed. There is another. Normally Xavier might have just accepted the fact, took the wrestler under his wing and used their alliance to push Scientology forward....But I don’t think ANYONE would predict that happening as the new Scientologist on the block is none other than PCW President and the perpetual thorn in the Commissioner’s side....Duke Williams, the man he must face at Supremacy. Kannon and Duke have hated each other for over nearly 2 months now, ever since Xavier turned his back on Duke’s friend Jeremy Howard the “Malice Man” has had only bad things to say and he was able to control Kannon....That changed at Spring Fling and after the Ultimate Authority fired Howard, Duke knew words were not enough and came out of retirement for the sole purpose of extracting pain, and the Continental Title from the Commish....And now he has taken away Xavier’s exclusivity....And Xavier isn’t too happy. “Is it ready?” We open up in a secret laboratory in the Hubbardville Institute for Scientific Research in the secret, hidden section of Hubbardville. We are focusing in on a chair which has a crown shaped device attaches to the head rest and shackles on both arms and on the footrest. “Yes Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon, we have been installing the software all morning and now we have a perfect 3-Dimensional representation for you to explore....It was a tough task but as you said, a man of your stature couldn’t go there in person...” We pan left away from the chair-like contraption to show the Commissioner of PCW, Xavier Kannon talking to another man who is dressed in a White coat. Xavier : “You are damn right I am too good to go there....It is home to a bunch of savages.... Neanderthals...... Un-Cultured morons whose language is based upon yelling “DA BEARS” and eating greasy processed cheap meat in a bun....It makes me want to vomit by Scientological stomach’s contents up just thinking about it.” The Continental Champion is wearing a Black silk shirt, un-buttoned to show a Black “ULTIMATE AUTHORITY” t-shirt with White lettering. Along with these he has his usual pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to where a pair of Black, Purple and Grey Skechers adorn his feet and his long, Auburn hair is neatly tied back out oh his face. Scientist : “Right....The virtual reality City browser 5000 model is on-line....” With this statement he looks away from Xavier and raises his voice. Scientist : “Computer, initiate program......Chicago.” With this voice command all the computers in the room come to life and whirring sound fills the room as the fans kick in to deal with the heat produced from the super powerful computers that Hubbardville’s premiere research institute houses. Xavier : “Now.....I just want to make sure of something, none of the people ‘inside’ that thing can touch me right? Give me a computer virus or something? Because we all know that every native of Chicago accumulates so many diseases and bacteria inside their body they turn the Monkey from Outbreak Green with envy....As opposed to any disease which might have turned it Green...” The Scientist gestures to the chair and reassures the Commish. Scientist : “No, fear not Gold Patron Meritorious....Nothing in there can hurt you, even the food....” Xavier takes a seat and the shackles automatically snap over, holding his hands and feet in place as the Scientist straps him in around the waist before lowering the virtual reality helmet down, 4 points of it touching his temples and forehead with a final fifth one resting on the base of his skull at the back. Kannon closes his eyes and starts to regulate his breathing, calming himself down. Xavier : “Duke has invaded Hubbardville, he has seen the fine City that we...Scientologists, and we do NOT include him, built....So now it is time for me to see where Duke calls home....And seeing as how actually going there would most likely kill me....This will be my way....” As the Scientologist readies himself for the experience, the Scientist walks back over to the main computer terminal and starts to type in commands at an exceptionally fast rate...Each touch of the “Return” key causing a chain of lights to light up on the console. Xavier’s face is pale in anticipation as the Scientist’s voice is heard instructing him. “Now....1st thing that will happen if you will feel a bit disorientated, but the computer has a built in guide to show you around who knows everything in the program to make sure you don’t get lost....Ready?” Xavier : “Ready...” *FLASH* In a blink of an eye we are transported from the sterile surrounding’s of the lab to the hustle and bustle of a crowded street with lanes of pedestrians criss-crossing each other as they go about there business. As the camera moves through the crowd we see Xavier, holding his head and shaking it in his hands as if he is trying to clear his head of something. After a few seconds his lifts his head up, using his right hand as a visor due to his eyes eye’s sensitivity to the bright sunlight. “Wow......So this is, like, where they chose to start the thing? Well, I suppose there are SO many places that suck more...” We look over Xavier’s shoulder to see none other than Eleanor Hall walking out through the doors of the Drake Hotel, revealing that we are on Michigan Avenue in Chicago, Illinois. So close to Hubbardville, Wisconsin in geographical terms but so far away in other things....Like class, breeding and civility. As soon as the voice is heard Kannon’s eyes widen, recognising the voice. Xavier : “Ellie~!?” Behind him the computerised version of PCW’s Commissioneress approaches. She is wearing a Chicago Bear’s over-sized jacket with a Blackhawk’s t-shirt underneath coupled with a pair of ill-fitting Blue jogging bottoms. On her head she has a Chicago Cub’s baseball cap and her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is pulled through the back forming a ponytail. As the Gold Patron Meritorious turns around his jaw drops as a shocked expression masks his face, Eleanor wondering what he is looking at her like that for. Eleanor : “Yup, that’s right......I am, like SO your guide to this cesspool of a City....Lucky us pixalated types have no sense of smell...Anyway, they thought it would be good to have someone recognisable and someone said the original idea of L Ron Hubbard was a bit tacky so you get me!....YAY COMPUTERISED ME!” Despite her explanation Kannon still can’t take his eyes off how his usually fashionably dressed valet is presenting herself. Given that she isn’t the real Ellie~!, she manages to pick up on the hint and looks down to what she is wearing. Eleanor : “Yeah....Like, SO don’t get me started on this! I look as if I am from Chicago, wait, I think that might be the point...Anyway, you want to see Chicago right?” Xavier : “Well I can’t say I exactly WANT to see it, but it would be good to see why that retarded inbred hick is so proud of this big ball of smog and poverty!” The 1st couple of Scientology then begin to walk through the crowds of people who are making no effort to move out of their way and arms are brushes against and shoulders barged, much to virtual Ellie~!’s disgust. Eleanor : “Okay, that is...Like, SO enough of that!” The computerised valet clicks her fingers and most of the people around them disappear, leaving less than a quarter of the inhabitants. Ellie~! grins smugly as does the Scientologist as the rest of the people don’t even notice the sudden neutralisation of their fellow inhabitants. Xavier : “Ah....Now this is much better....A lot less rude and ignorant people wanting to barge into us because of our beliefs, my Scientology and your Preppyness. If only we could do this in real life Chicago wouldn’t be the armpit of the nation we all know it is....Kind of fitting that Duke Williams resides he you could say.” Eleanor : “You......Could........Say!” The couple now have no obstructions as they walk down Michigan Avenue, but the peace of their stroll does not last long before their disgusted faces return to their faces. “HOT DOGS! HOT DOGS! GOOD OLD CHICAGO STYLE HOT-DOGS!” They look to their right to see a crude stall where an overweight man, dripping with sweat as he stands over a grill is shouting out to the passers by, one of whom walks over and converses with the vendor. Eleanor : “They....Like, actually allow things like here. Supposedly the residents find it one of Chicago’s “touches”.....Obviously followed closely by food poisoning and bowel disease.” The man comes away from the stall with a greasy hot-dog, pilled high with onions and dripping in ketchup, looking as if it could well be named The Coronary Special....However, it is not the food that now causes the pair to becomes disgusted....It is the cardboard cup bearing the logo of Pepsi that has turned their face sour. Xavier : “Have these people not heard of Snapple~!? Surely they have been watching PCW to see me fight off the oppression of their insult to athletes everywhere Duke Williams? How can they not embrace the sweet and fruity elixir that is Snapple~!? Hubbard, I had no idea this city was so BACKWARDS!” Eleanor : “Was........So.......Backwards!” As Xavier shakes his head, Ellie~! is distracted by something else.....At the edge of the street where the drain is, a cloud is rising up through it, obscuring the view behind it. Eleanor : “Oh yeah, and the stuff underground is, like, on fire....Or boiling...Or whatever it is. Anyway, the stuff down there gets to up here...” The Scientologist looks at not just the vendor, the Pepsi and the drains but everything around him, looking as if he is having trouble taking it all in. Xavier : “You know the thing you did with the people? Can you do that with everything?” Being the first time the program has ever been used, computerised guide Ellie~! doesn’t look sure until she points to a fire hydron and clicks her fingers, turning it into a Llama much to Xavier surprise. Xavier : “A Llama?” Looking pleased with herself for managing to fulfil the feat and realising she is essentially the God of this realm, a preppy one at that, she just shrugs. Eleanor : “I, like, wanted to see what one looked like...” Xavier : “Right.....With your powers and my much enlightened state of mind from being a TRUE Scientologist, unlike Duke....I think we should mould this waste of building materials into something more fitting, something in our image!” Both of them nod at the prospect of this, their eyes glowing as they do every time they have a shred of power or influence over something come to them. Xavier : “Okay.....The Drake Hotel there, it has to go, lets make if the L Ron Hubbard Hotel...The inhabitants could do with remembering his name. That coffee shop there, I don’t like the look of the people in it...Make it only sell Snapple~!....Oh, and put some more desirable people in it too.” At Xavier’s requests Ellie~! clicks her fingers and in the blink of an eye they change into Xavier’s visions. The Drake hotel becomes a towering mirrored glass structure bearing the name of Scientology’s founder and the coffee shops subtle shop-face is replaced by the gaudy Orange and Yellow check pattern synonymous with Snapple~! Eleanor : “Wow.....Like, being virtual SO rules.....Ooooh, that one over there can become a Prep School! Chicago women really are...You know...Dirty, icky, smelly, unfashionable and all round skanky...” She gestures with vigour at a building across the street and it transforms into a school with the stereotypical steps at the front and as a bell rings a crowd of preppy girls, all dressed in the newest season’s fashions and the latest hair ideas from Vogue comes running out. The Scientologist then turns to his valet and points out what she is wearing. Xavier : “I think you can change out of the Chicago sports franchises clothes now....The NEW Chicago is a lot more fashionable than that.” She nods in agreement and clicks her fingers at herself but nothing seems to be happening. Eleanor : “Huh?” She tries it again and once more there is no flash of light and no change in her attire. At failing twice she goes into a sulk and starts to stamp her feet, clicking her fingers over and over again. Eleanor : “Like.....DAMN IT! SO..... Get...... Me.......OUT OF THESE HORRIBLE CLOTHES!!!” Kannon puts his arm on her shoulder to calm here down. Xavier : “Relax my little Shag Squirrel! Why stay here and remould Chicago when we have Hubbardville already built the way we like it?” With this knowledge she stops her strop and Xavier punches the inside of his left palm 3 times and disappears in a flash of light. Ellie~! tries the same but with no effect and the realisation hits her. Eleanor : “Wait, I’m.......Like.....SO built into the place....” The camera zooms in on her face as she realises she is forever enslaved to this computer program and she hurriedly starts snapping her fingers at herself again to try and get out of the Chicago attire as we hit Black. “DAMMIT! GET ME OUT OF HERE! OUT....... OF......... HERE! I HATE CHICAGO....” Now only the sounds of her virtual sobbing and whining voice fading out can be heard. “I.......Hate.......Chicago......”
