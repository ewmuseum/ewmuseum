The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part III]

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4, 2002 Jack Valentine arrived back at PINK! headquarters after being doused in the purple ooze, he looked around, with a somber look on his face. Jack Valentine: 'Anything important happen while I was away?' Johnny Heart was still wiping himself off from the heinous attack he took from purple lovers, the Village People, yesterday, and traces of the color that he hated so much remained as specs all over his body. Johnny Heart: 'Only two, call Elton, the big boys are waiting for a message of some kind, and the Liberace wants to see you.' It was never good when the executives fighting with and for the PINK! wanted to see you. Usually they had something bad to say about you, or bad news to get you. They always seemed to look down on everyone who was fighting for the PINK!, thinking they were the best themselves. Jack Valentine: 'What the hell do they want?' Johnny Heart: 'Who knows...they probably want a new boy toy to play with.' Jack just looked at Johnny like he was sick or something. Jack Valentine: 'I don't know, but it doesn't sound good. I can't be having all of this stress before our match tomorrow night. We've got to focus on what really matters, and PINK! must come second. Johnny stepped back and gasped for air like he was going into shock. Johnny Heart: 'How could you say such a thing? It is the PINK! that brought us the titles in the first place, we must not turn our backs on it.' Jack Valentine: 'The PINK!...The PINK! wasn't there when I was attacked two days ago by those idiot Village People. I'm starting to think that they PINK! is a big fraud.' Valentine sighed as this was his last day of work for the PINK! for awhile, and he had hoped to be out of the building by lunchtime, the PINK! he could put off, but not the #1 Contenders Match, nor Elton and Liberace. Liberace had the best room in the building. It was not the biggest, but it was certainly the best. It had a fine view of the harbor and was high enough up that you could open a window and still be able to use the phone. Liberace: 'Thanks for coming up to see me, I realize that you are going to be away for a while, but perhaps you can help me.' Valentine sighed inwardly but leant forward with a pretence of interest and enthusiasm, it was this blend of duplicity and servility that had made his relationship with Liberace so successful. In a way it was a compliment that Liberace was so keen to retain him in his office, but obviously he wanted something. Valentine and Heart were the biggest guys fighting for the PINK! and they were needed in this crucial time in the war against purple. Liberace: 'In fact the matter of you and Johnny going on leave for awhile may be of some use in this matter. It's only a small thing, if you can contribute it will be much appreciated, if not then it is no loss. You should not worry yourself unduly about it.' Liberace fell silent for a moment, as if he was an actor that had forgotten his lines, and suddenly the whole pretence was about to fall apart. But he started again... Liberace: 'Here is a file, I want you to find this line of clothing. In this envelope you will find all that you need. If you find them phone me on the number given. Day or night you will get an answer. If you do not find them, then it is our secret...don't even tell Johnny about this. This mission is top secret Jack, and will be most successful for the PINK! if you can pull it off.' He pushed the file and the sealed envelope over towards Valentine, then he brushed the feather boa that he had around his neck over his shoulder. Valentine leafed through the file. Jack Valentine: 'This photo is not wonderful, how can I be sure that I will recognize the clothing if I find them?' It seemed an intelligent remark, unusual for Valentine. But then again, no one new about the secret PINK! vs. Purple war that was going on, and no one new what kind of special traits Valentine possessed. Liberace: 'Oh don't worry, there will be no doubt whatsoever if you find them. These purple clothing are one of a kind.' The Director seemed almost to admire the purple. When he was back at his desk Valentine opened the sealed envelope, trying to hide it from the rainbow colored gay squad and Johnny Heart. As promised there was a phone number, there was also a plastic card that could be inserted into a cash dispenser to obtain money. On the back was written the PIN number. Valentine was later to find out that within the limits of daily withdrawals it offered the possibility of near limitless funds. He was beginning to realize that this was not just a routine job. Kevin Ya Ya Jelly, or K.Y. to his friends, was a Private Investigator, was past imperfect. He had lost his license after a prison sentence. He was an alcoholic, past imperfect, now his liver was shot and he was on orange juice. He was waiting for Jack Valentine to meet him. They had been friends a long time ago, before Johnny. It was one of those friendships that was close enough that either of them could call in a favor when it was needed (No, not that kind of favor, SICKO!). He sat in the bar, the stained glass behind him, and a glass of orange liquid in front of him. There had been a time when he had surrounded himself with music and noise, the flamboyant lifestyle that all of the PINK! men, including Valentine & Heart live, but now just the silence seemed noise enough. His mind ranged restlessly, his eyes flickered across the room. At last Staten arrived. Jack Valentine: 'Hello K.Y., can I get you a drink?' Valentine made to go to the bar. K.Y. Jelly: 'No, I'm sure that it is my round.' K.Y. went to the bar to order, leaving his half empty glass on the copper table. Valentine was considering tasting it to see if it really was orange juice, when K.Y. came back and picked it up. All these lies and deceits were like some kind of disease. Jack could not even trust his friends now, his work in the PINK! had infected so much. That's why he came to see K.Y., to see if Liberace was telling the truth. Jack didn't even confide in Johnny for this one...this matter was like none other Jack has ever seen. Jelly's face betrayed nothing as he looked at the file, it was only a few pages, it was not clear but it seemed likely that it was a summary of a PINK! employee, perhaps it was someone that had gone rogue, over to the purple side. However the photos were all operational and material, not the usual catalog clothing shots. There were a handful of addresses, bank accounts and hobbies and the like, all having to do with purple. It was still very much the process of an editing operation, Liberace was not giving them the whole truth. Maybe he was not giving them any of the truth. K.Y. Jelly: 'So the deal is you want to find this clothing?' Jelly looked at Valentine, who was covering his mouth before he spoke. Jack Valentine: 'It's no big deal, just a little favor, I know that you are so good at these things.' Now Valentine was not telling the whole truth. K.Y. Jelly looked at him, he could kill him with a single blow, he knew how to do it. He could hand back the file and tell him what the target really was, that might shock Valentine even more. Instead his face betrayed nothing as he promised to do what he could. Later, Valentine & Jelly met at the edge of one of those huge forests that fill up the gaps on the maps. Valentine had found his purple clothing, or so he claimed. Valentine was not convinced of this, but he had the money that he had promised. Jelly counted it before explaining. K.Y. Jelly: 'The deal was that I get you to the target. I can't guarantee that it's here but if you head north from here you'll probably find it.' Jack Valentine: 'How will I find it?' Valentine was unconvinced. K.Y. Jelly: 'Oh don't worry if it's here, it'll jump out at you. I've no doubt of it.' Valentine began to feel like a fish that was getting reeled in, on the drive out he had felt like the fisherman. It was a sobering feeling. Similar to the match on Friday, The Heartbreakers feel like they are getting reeled into a trap, or a screw-job...earlier in the week, they felt like they were the ones that were going to be screwing...no pun intended. Jack Valentine: 'How do I know which way is north, is it something to do with the moss on the trees or pointing my beautiful PINK! watch at the sun?' The sun was thin and watery anyway. K.Y. Jelly: 'Oh you can try that if you want, but there's a compass here if you want it.' Valentine cursed as the mud filled his shoes and the thorns tore his PINK! suit. If there is anyone who doesn't like to get dirty, it's Jack Valentine. There were a few dismal tracks through these woods that led him to believe the clothing was back through them. As he walked, thinking about all of the problems...Liberace, Pretty Cool Guys, Cowabunga Connection, whether or not he was doing a mission for Liberace and the PINK! or a rogue mission, and being set up. He kept walking for miles and found nothing...not even a trace of clothing back in the woods, it seemed as if he was misled. A twig snapped, and Jack turned round, but instead of turning round he was falling, hands held his head and feet kicked at his legs, he was twisted and falling. He felt the wet ground underneath him, he was on his back. A rough hand was across his face and his eyes were held shut by fingers that seemed like pokers. A knife was across his throat, but to be honest it seemed like overkill. Jack could make out the man, holding several boxes in his hands...it had to be the clothing he was looking for. Mysterious Man: 'And why don't you just start to tell me who you are ?' Valentine thought to himself, well Liberace did say that he would recognize the clothing when he found it. -TO BE CONTINUED-
