Missing Persons Report

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-6-03 A two thousand three Harley Davidson Road King pullsup to a police station. The person on the bike turnsoff the ignition as they sit back. Reaching up andremoving the helmet, blonde locks shine in the sun.It’s Jaslene Myst. She locks the ignition of the bikeand places her helmet on the backrest of the backseatbefore stepping off of the bike. She smirks as a young adolescent runs into a streetlight pole at the sight of Jaslene in leather. Tossingthe keys up into the air, she catches them and stuffsthem into the pocket of her leather jacket. She headstoward the station. Jaslene bounces up the stairs andinto the station as a uniformed cop holds open thedoor for her. An elder woman sits behind the desk at the front ofthe station. Jaslene walks up to her. “Can I help you?” the elder woman asks. Jaslene looks down at the nametag sitting on the desk,“Yes you can…Miss Farnsworth…I need to file a missingpersons report.” “Oh!” Miss Farnsworth’s face lights up, “I can’t helpyou with that but Detective Dorston can help youthough. He’s down the hall, third door on the left.” “Ok thank you.” Jaslene says with a smile beforeheading down the hallway. “No problem dear!” Miss Farnsworth calls out, “It’s myjob!” Jaslene lets out a nervous chuckle, “Weirdo…let’s see,down the hall…third door on the left…” Jaslene stops as she comes to a door labeled DetectiveDorston. She reaches out and takes the doorknob intoher hand but stops for a moment. “What am I doing here?” she thinks, “Adam is probablyjust out with AJ or something. Maybe this is amistake. Though it has been a couple of days since Ihave heard from him, maybe something is wrong. Aw whatthe hell.” She turns the knob and steps into the office. Shelooks around but notices that there is no one in theroom. “What the fuck!” Jaslene says under her breath, “Thatfat bitch up there lied to me!” She turns around and bumps into someone. She isknocked back and falls into a chair. The chair flipsback and Jaslene hits her head off of the desk in themiddle of the room. “OUCH!” she cries out. “What the fuck!” A man crosses the room and places a cup of coffee downon the desk before kneeling next to her. Jaslene holdsher head in pain. The man sits the chair back uprightand kneels down in front of her. “Are you ok?” he asks reaching up to her face. She slaps his hand away, “I’ll be fine. Who are you?” The man stands back up, “I’m Detective Dorston.Farnsworth told me that there was an attractive youngwoman waiting for me in my office.” Jaslene opens her eyes and tries to focus, “Yeahthat’s me. I need to file a missing persons report.” Dorston drops his head for a moment before walkingaround the desk and taking a seat. He opens a drawerand pulls out a notebook. He leans back in his seatand pulls a pen from his shirt pocket. “Now tell me Miss…” he starts clicking on the end ofthe pen. “Myst, Jaslene Myst.” Jaslene replies. “Miss Myst…” Dorston says writing on his notebook.“What can you tell me about your missing person? Lastknown whereabouts? Who he or she was with?” “It was a he.” Jaslene says crossing her right legover her left. “Damn…” Dorston coughs. He reaches out and takes hiscup of coffee. Pulling it to his mouth, he takes asip. “Ahhh…good stuff. Would you care for some coffeeMiss Myst?” “Uh no thanks.” Jaslene says looking at the Detectivewith a look of disbelief. “And please just call meJaslene.” “Ok Jaslene it is.” Dorston says with a laugh. “Wherewas he last seen?” “I’m going to have to say Tempe?” Jaslene saysscratching her head. “You don’t know?” Dorston asks, “What makes you thinkTempe? Out of all of the places around and that isnowhere near Los Angeles.” “It’s where his last show was.” She answers trying tothink. “At least I think that’s where it was. I don’tknow for sure since I wasn’t able to make it. I was abit under the weather so I told him to go on withoutme.” “Show?” Dorston asks, scribbling something down on thepaper. “What does this person do?” “He’s a professional wrestler with the PCW.” Jaslenesays as she pulls out a piece of paper from thejacket. She unfolds it and nods, “Yes he wasdefinitely in Tempe last. At least that’s what theschedule says.” “PCW huh? No shit.” Dorston says as he sits up in hischair. “One of my favorite wrestlers just returned toPCW a couple of weeks back.” “Oh really? Who is that?” Jaslene asks with a smile. “AJ Trinidad.” Dorston says with a sneer. “AdamStryker screwed him though.” “What? How did Stryker screw Trinidad?” Jaslene sayssitting up in her chair. Detective Dorston raises his brow, “I’ll tell you how,ever since the DWO Adam has been hanging off of AJ’scoattails and that is a proven fact!” Jaslene stands up, “If I weren’t such a lady, I’dreach over this table and slap you silly flatfoot!” Droston gets up and leans over the table, the two ofthem are looking eye to eye, “Did you just call meflatfoot?” “You’re goddamn right I did!” Jaslene says throwingher finger up into Dorston’s face, “How can you thinkfor a moment that it was Adam hanging onto AJ’scoattails? Clearly Adam was the better man. Clearly itwas Adam who carried the feud and made it as big as itwas!” “What are you saying?” Dorston say throwing his fingerup into Jaslene’s face. “Are you the new head of AsSFaCe now or something?” “As it so happens to be,” Jaslene says realizing howstupid they were acting, “The man I am looking forjust happens to be Adam Stryker!” Dorston looks shocked and takes a step back. “You’rethat Jaslene Myst?!” She smacks herself in the forehead, “How many JasleneMysts do you think there are in LA?” “Oh I don’t know.” Dorston replies, “How am I supposedto keep up with everyone in the Los Angeles area?” “God are you always this stupid?” Jaslene says turningher back to him. “Well if you are going to insult me than I don’t thinkI’m going to help you Miss Myst. Good day!” Dorstonsays turning his back to Jaslene. “You don’t get to pick and choose who you help!” sheexclaims turning back around to face him. “It’s yourjob to help those in need!” “I said, ‘Good Day!’ Miss Myst.” He says throwing hishand up and motioning her to leave. Jaslene scoffs and walks off. She storms through thehallway and pushes past a couple of uniformedofficers. Miss Farnsworth goes to say something butJaslene turns and gives her the finger before walkingthrough the door to the outside. “Damn idiots.” She mumbles to herself as she comes tothe bike. She pulls the helmet back on and unlocks the ignition.She slides the key in and brings the bike to life.Jaslene takes one last look back at the police stationbefore taking off on the bike.
