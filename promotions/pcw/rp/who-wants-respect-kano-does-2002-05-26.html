Who wants respect? Kano does.

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/26/2002 ~Respect. Aretha Franklin wrote a great American classic song about it. Many men have prided themselves on receiving it. Jon Kano only expects it. When you disrespect anything about PCW or PCW's history, you disrespect Jon Kano. Kano has been a part of the juggernaut known as Primetime Championship Wrestling since October of 2000. He was a cornerstone in the construction of PCW. He is the past, the present, and the future of PCW. Corey Williams is the essence of the problem of the 'New Breed' mentality. THe lack of respect shown towards established superstars and the tradition of PCW is something that Kano cannot stand by and watch while Corey Williams does this to PCW. On Turmoil, Kano gets to show Corey Williams why Jon Kano is the essence of PCW and why he has been here for so damn long.~ *The scene opens up on the beach of the lovely city of Virginia Beach, Virginia. It is a cold, blustery day, so the beach is deserted, except for one man. This man is, of course, the legendary Jon Kano. He is dressed in baggy tan khakis pants with a plain white t-shirt. Kano just looks off into the ocean, as the sun sets behind him.* Kano: I just don't get it. I really don't. Corey Williams actually has the balls to question my status as a legend? I was wrestling when he was sitting in Biology class learning about the difference between men and women. I was headlining cards when he was learning the difference between a wristlock and a wristwatch. He says that I have been here too long and I just don't have the guts to retire. F*ck him. I have been here damn near since the beginning. Maybe I have been here too long, but I have my reasons. Its because I refuse to let people like Corey Williams forget about the roots of PCW. You have to remember the few of us that laid the PCW foundation of which the empire was built. I am talking about the legends of PCW history. The sad thing is that many of these legends are retired or in OSW because of that son of a bitch Nelson. I am talking about Jon Hull, Kremmen, myself, Doug Troy, Miyagawa, Kid Wonder, The Chef, and countless others. If it wasn't for us, there would be NO PCW!!! Why am I a legend? I had a hundred thousand people who didn't even speak English chanting my name when Kriegman, Blood, and myself wrestled in the Shanghai, China for my Continental Title at Shanghaied '01. *Kano reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pack of cigarettes and begins to pack them.* Kano: Corey loves to think he is controversial. When I listen to him speak, I don't think it is controversial. I am the king of controversy. I was the one who kidnapped PHD when he was running the whole damn show. I spent months at TPB's neck after I kidnapped PHD, only to join forces with TPB. Then when Nelson bought PCW from TPB, I formed the Outcasts. I was the leader except no one wanted to follow my lead. They all wanted to do their own things, so I joined Media. I was Nelson's chief enforcer. I was the man who took care of any problems Media had, and then I got tired of taking Nelson's impossible orders that only served his own selfish interests. At the time, being in Media was a great decision for me because I had to make sure the future of PCW had some impact from me. I have spent the last year kicking the hell out of anyone and everyone Tyler has thrown at me, including Tyler himself. And on Friday Fusion, I beat the hell out of Tim Walden so badly, I embarassed him so horribly, that Nelson fired his ass. I took a man that I made, and I destroyed him. Tim Walden is no more. *Kano reaches in the cigarette pack and pulls out a cigarette. He lights it and deeply inhales the cancerous, deadly smoke.* Kano: Corey keeps following the New Breed mentality, which will ultimately destroy him. He has no respect for tradition. He and his little bitch friends in the New Breed passed the World Title around themselves a few weeks back like a blunt. That title deserves respect. When you disrespect that sacred, prestigious title, you disrespect PCW. When you disrespect PCW, you disrespect me. And you will RESPECT ME. I don't ask for many things. I don't ask to be liked and I don't ask to be your buddy. All I ask is for simple respect, I ask for nothing more, and I WILL NOT ACCEPT ANYTHING LESS. I started this legend sh*t, and I am truly the essence of what a legend is. And on Turmoil, Corey will learn to respect me and will find out just like Tim Walden did, why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *Kano flicks his cigarette butt off into the sands of the beach.* Kano: Heh, somewhere right now that damn Indian is crying. *The scene fades to black as Kano continues to stare off into the oblivion known as the Atlantic Ocean.* ~R-E-S-P-E-C-T. On Turmoil, Corey Williams will find out what it means to Jon Kano.~
