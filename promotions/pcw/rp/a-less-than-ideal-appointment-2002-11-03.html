A Less Than Ideal Appointment

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Less Than Ideal Appointment Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 03/11/02 “Ahhh, with Ellie~! finally off job hunting, my days are stress-free once more...” Our shot opens up on the face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon, looking far more relaxed that normal. Xavier : “No more phoning me in business meetings to ask if I like that way she’s done her hair...” Kannon is dressed for business in a navy Blue Armani suit, something obviously influenced by his old mentor Tyler Nelson, and his long mane of Strawberry Blonde hair is neatly tied back... Add to this the well trimmed Goatee he sports, he looks the part. Xavier : “No more coming back home worrying how much she spent to pass the time...” Walking through the reception area of the new PCW International offices in London, everything is going well for the PCW CEO. Xavier : “.... Thank you, Hubbard.... Thank you.” A smile on his face, the Scientologist strolls through the marvellous architecture of the offices, housed in London’s Docklands, admiring the decor and angles of the interior. Xavier : “I just feel like nothing could ruin this day.” As he mutters those most dangerous words, he casually strolls up to the reception desk, confidence oozing from every pore while his eyes glance at one of the mirrors built into the interior to check out how he looks in the beautifully tailored suit. Xavier : “The Head of European Marketing is expecting me...” In his clear, concise tone he addresses the receptionist, standing tall over her as she relays the message through the intercom and gestures Xavier, her boos, through. “She’s ready for you now, Mr. Kannon.” Kannon thinks nothing of the Marketing Head’s sex. With a nod of the head, and even a trace of a smile the CEO acknowledges and wanders into the offices... A hive of activity that controls all of PCW’s business outside North America. Xavier : “My, what a marvellous painting...” He quips in a jovial tone while looking at a large print that hangs on the wall depicting himself holding up the PCW World Title. All around the offices are images of the Front Office, their stamp well and truly marking PCW business now. Xavier : “Now, time to see how much the rest of the World is making me...” A confident smile on his face and a spring in his step, the CEO strides down one of the aisles between the desks, casting an eye over some of the work being done as he gets closer to the door marked “Head of European Marketing” which is bordered by 2 giant PCW logos. *KNOCK* *KNOCK* With his hands, still sporting some cuts from Fusion he taps the door and waits for a response. “Like.... Come in... And stuff!” Suddenly his eyes narrow, he recognises the voice. The sound of the voice that comes though the door makes him double take, shaking his head and trying to shrug it off. Xavier : “No.... It couldn’t be.” Deciding in his mind that it couldn’t possibly be the person he believes it to be, the CEO opens the door and strides in. Xavier : “Hello, sorry I’m a bi-” Something that doesn’t happen often.... Happens. The Scientologist stops dead in his tracks. Eyes widening. Jaw dropping. Disbelief on his face. “You’re, like, SO late! Is it too much to ask for you to, like, be on time?” The camera pans from Xavier’s face to the person behind the desk, Ellie~! Wearing a smart skirt suit and her long, wavy light Brown hair with Blonde highlights tied up in a bun, the little prep looks back at him through her plain lens glasses. Xavier : “Y-... Y-... Ellie~!?” Hearing her name, the ditzy PCW Commissioner just smiles and waves. Eleanor : “Hi!” Slowly, Kannon makes his way towards the desk, still waiting for someone to reveal this as a joke.... Ellie~! on the other hand just sits there smiling at him. Xavier : “You’re the Head of European Marketing?” Not picking up on the fact that it was a question, she just sits there fidgeting with excitement and brimming with chirpyness. Eleanor : “I, like, SO know! YAY ME!“ Almost cautiously, the CEO takes a seat across the desk from Ellie~!, still not believing it as he looks around his wife’s lavish office. Xavier : “But you’re.... You know?.... You’re...” The giggly Commissioner finishes his sentence for him. Eleanor : “ELLIE~! I know! I was here using the Sauna and they were about to, like, adver-.. Er... Advrota-.... ASK for someone to do it and I was all, yeah, I wanna do that so I, like, SO said I was your wife and they WAY gave me the job!” Kannon is silent is disbelief. Eleanor : “YAY ME! Trying to compose himself, Kannon finally gets some words out. Xavier : “Um.... So how are the European Marketing figures?” With pure hope in his bright Green eyes, Kannon looks right into Ellie~!’s pale Blue pair and the prep’s face just goes blank. Eleanor : “Tha wha?” She tilts her head to the side, looking right back at him for any clue as to what he meant. Xavier : “You know? You’re Head of European marketing so I’m wondering what the marketing figures are like?” Eleanor : “Ohhhhhhhhh, you mean the FIGURES!” She buys herself a few seconds while “Um”ing and “Ah”ing as Kannon just looks at her, wondering how much she’s cost the company already. Eleanor : “Like, the toys?” Kannon sighs and shakes his head. Xavier : “No, not the ACTION figures... The FIGURES.... Numbers... Money!” The look on Ellie~!’s face is total confusion as she just aims her Bambi-like eyes at Xavier then suddenly thinks of something and reaches for her computer monitor. Eleanor : “Look!” She struggles to turn the monitor around while Kannon hopes a spreadsheet with this quarter’s figures will await him. Ha! Eleanor : “Like, how cool!” On the monitor is a scrolling screen saver, Silver letters on a Pink background. ELLIE~! RULES! YAY ME....AND STUFF! Xavier’s head drops into his hands as Ellie~! looks proud of herself, craning her neck around to see it too while looking upon it with pride. Eleanor : “That’s my name and stuff!” The pained and already weary face of the CEO looks up to his wife who looks back, wanting praise. Xavier : “Come here, my little Fuck Ferret...” Kannon pats his thigh and Ellie~! scurries around the desk and parks her pert ass on his leg, putting an arm around him as Kannon tries to be diplomatic with his words. Xavier : “Sometimes, when someone has a job.... They fancy a change....” Ellie~! nods. Xavier : “And to let the change jobs, some employers will fire them so they can go and do another job... And earn more money... And have more fun...” Before he can either finish, Ellie~!’s face springs to life and she holds a finger up. Eleanor : “Oooh! I like, SO wanna do that! Fire me!” Xavier shrugs. Xavier : “Okay, you’re fired!” The little prep leaps off his leg and hops up and down, applauding herself. Eleanor : “Yeah! I’m, like, SO fired.. YAY ME!“ A huge sigh of relief is let out by Kannon who can once again think a thought that doesn’t involve millions of dollars being haemorrhaged. A sly grin then crosses his face. Xavier : “It’s also customary for the fired person to have sex with her old boss on her desk....” Ellie~! shrugs. Eleanor : “Okay!” Xavier growls as he leaps out of the chair and into the clutches of his wife who hops up onto the desk and the pair entwine while we fade out to Black as their bodies slide across the desk, sending the clutter dropping to the floor. “Mmmmmmmm... being fired SO rules!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
