Date: 756976 BC And on the 8th day, God created Avalanche. Avalanche: Huh? On the 10th day, Avalanche stopped watching TV and got out of bed. Avalanche: *Yawn* Then there was some Biblical flood, so Avalanche became a Pirate and took over a huge boat, tossed a guy called Noah overboard and got himself a well-trained army of Beasts... Including a new weapon, the DOVE GRENADE~! Pull the Olive Branch out of their mouths, lob them, then duck! Sign of Peace, my arse. Avalanche: You know I'm better than you, right? Avalanche grew weary of God's rule, and put him on a warning. God made Wales, that didn't do much to get him back in Avalanche's good books. Avalanche: Okay, why does everything suck? Why is there no electricity? Why doesn't anyone wear shoes? Why doesn't Bethlehem have cable? The world was growing too slowly for Avalanche, so he began to plot his take-over... ...But Satan beat him to it. Avalanche: Pfft, loser. God banished Satan to hell, meaning Pink had to move out and allowing her to start making music... That was the last straw as far as Avalanche was concerned. Avalanche: Okay, this world blows, I'm taking over. To combat Avalanche and spread his word, God hit on some chick called Mary, spiked her drink, took her back to his and knocked her up... And act he jokingly called the... Er... Date Rape. Mary: But I'm a Virgin! Yeah, right. So Jesus was born, grew a beard, and began to spread the word of God. Avalanchious Caesar: Nail him to a cross. But Avalanche controlled the Roman Legions, so killed Jesus... Well, there was no TV, he had to do something to pass the time. God went mad, and decided to smite Avalanche, to drive him insane in the only way God knew how. He created the Welsh. Avalanche: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! God laughed, then created Wales for the Welsh to live in. Avalanche: YOU BASTARD! I'LL GET YOU! Avalanche kicked God in the nuts... ...And so began the War of the Heavens... ...Or something.
