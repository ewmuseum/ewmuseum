Making Sure of things.

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15-12-02 Bob Vurdo is sitting in the passenger seat of Tony's car as they drive through the city. Tony is driving the white Mercedes calmly through the city on his way to the election meeting. Bob is wearing his usual sharp black suit and Tony is wearing a dull brown version of the same suit. As they look out the windscreen they are forced to squint due to the glare coming from the sun and the other cars metallic pieces. 'Fuck I hate the day.' Bob obviously is fairly grumpy with having to wake up at 8 AM to get ready for a stupid meeting. 'You will get used to it.' Tony smiles as he takes a left hand turn. 'Fuck that I quit.... Can I go back to bed now?' Tony smiles as he shakes his head and pulls over to the place they are scheduled to meet. 'You ready?' Bob looks down at himself to make sure he looks good before opening the door and getting out of the car. The two men walk through the large car park to the steps of Amber Hill Council's building. Bob stops and looks through the large glass rotating doors and into the lobby where various people stand. Bob enters through the doors with Tony behind him and enter the lobby. Every person in the lobby turn and stare at the two men who just entered. Bob and Tony both look around before looking down and walking toward the front desk where a middle aged lady sits staring at the two. The room is quiet but Bob and Tony don't realize that word has got out about a mobster running for mayor. 'Hello I am Mr...' The lady interrupts Bob 'Mr. Vurdo go down the hall on my left and to the second door on the left.' The lady points in the direction she is taking about as she looks over Bob. Bob and Tony walk through the hallway and to the door to the room where the conference is being held. Bob opens up the door and enters the room where about twelve upper class looking men are sitting around the table waiting for the conference to start in a few minutes. A few people look up before looking back across the table at the people they are talking to. Bob decides to take a seat by himself a few seats to the side of an old gray haired man. Tony sits next to Bob feeling outcast. 'I want to get out of here this place is strange.' As Tony is about to answer a lady walks through the room with her briefcase in her hand and sits down at the head of the table. The attention of all the men turns to her as she prepares to begin the conference. 'Hello and welcome to all of you who are running for mayor this year. I am Mrs. Campbell the official spokeswoman for Amber Hill. I have a sperate roll to the mayor however. I look over what the mayor is doing to make sure everything is in order.' The lady pauses and clears her throat ready to continue. 'I have a few things to hand out before you can leave.' The lady pulls a load of papers and places them on the desk. She licks her finger and begins counting through the papers. She counts to twelve and creates a pile before repeating the same thing four more times. 'Could you please pass these around the table. The men sitting next to Mrs. Campbell grab the papers before taking one and passing it on to the next man in line. Once the papers are fully handed out the lady begins speaking again. 'All right now just sign this form and we're done for the day.' The lady pulls out a clip board with a piece of paper stuck to it and passes it around before standing up and walking out of the room. 'Find out where she lives,' whispers Bob to Tony. 'Already have.' It become obvious that Tony has thought this through fully. A man passes the form to Bob before getting up and leaving. Bob takes a look around and realizes he is the only one left in the room. 'Take down the names.' Tony pulls out a pen and notepad from the pocket on his right breast. He jots down the names quickly as Bob signs the form and lays it on the table. 'All right I'll go and organize tonight now.' Bob gets up form his seat and begins walking the room before turning around and watching Tony also get up. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Peter and Diaz both sit in the black car outside the council building waiting for their moment to strike. The two thugs have been sent by Bob to let Mrs. Campbell know who is going to be the next mayor and to make sure it happens. 'This is easy as shit and we get good recognition for this.' Peter has been the new up and comer in the Vurdo family for a while. The two then notice Mrs. Campbell walking through the car park toward her car. Diaz then turns the car on and prepares to follow her to the place where they can strike. Mrs. Campbell gets in the car and begins driving through the car park toward the main road. Diaz turns onto the main road and drops down in speed to make space in between their car and the one in front so Mrs. Campbell can get in front of them. She does just as planned as the two begin following her on her way home. 'Do you think it's a good idea for Bob to run for Mayor?' 'It's fucking brilliant.... Tony and Bob have done so many new things with the Vurdo family that has put them a notch above the rest. We are the most unique family in the whole of America and it works perfectly.' Diaz nods in approval of what Tony is saying as he continues to follow Mrs. Campbell's red sedan through the city. Diaz is a new comer to the Vurdo family and he sits right at the bottom of the list. Peter is a fair way above him as a mid ranged thug which makes Diaz respect him a lot more. 'How long did it take you to get where you are?' 'A few months but from here its a lot harder to work up.' Mrs. Campbell makes a turn off the main road and onto a quieter suburban road before driving up the road and turning into a driveway. Diaz speeds up and pulls over on the side of her road next to her house. Mrs. Campbell then looks out at the car in a scarred way. She panics and runs to the door of her house as Diaz and Peter get out of the car and run up to the door step. Mrs. Campbell jiggles with the lock a bit before finally opening up the thick white front door but it is too late. Diaz grabs Mrs. Campbell from behind and throws her into the wall holding her by her jacket. 'You know the election?!' Peter stares coldly at Mrs. Campbell making him look a lot scarier than he did before. 'Bob Vurdo is going to win that election and your going to make sure of it OK?' Mrs. Campbell nods her head in fear. 'We will fucking kill you and your family if you breath a word of this to anyone!' Peter turns around and begins walking away as Diaz lets go. 'Bob Vurdo for mayor okay?' Mrs. Campbell rolls herself up in a little ball sitting beside the wall crying as Peter and Diaz get in their car and drive off.
