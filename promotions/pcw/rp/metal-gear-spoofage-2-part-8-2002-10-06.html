Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 8)

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/6/02 On a bridge high above the New York harbor, a young man cowers before a fighter jet. Kriegidus: Mwahahahaha! Raiden: Why me? A rocket launches from the Harrier, zooming straight at Jack. He dives out of the way, however the missile takes out a chunk of the bridge. Kriegidus: Hmm. For a brief second the terrorist leader ducks out of sight....when he pops back up, he is wearing what seems to be an old World War 1 Red Baron type hat. Kriegidus: Now where did I leave that tape of Ride of the Valkyries? Argy: Can we not listen to some Aqua instead? Kriegidus: Aqua!? How are we to conduct evil with Aqua playing? Argy: You'd be shocked, why just the other day I had this Greek boy, and...... Kriegidus: (Holding his ears) I don't hear you, lalala! The plane speeds off into the distance, as on the ground Jack tries to catch his breath. Snake: Raiden, catch! From the helicopter high above, Solid Snake throws a box down. Raiden: (Looking up) Huh? The crate lands directly on the commando's head, knocking him flat. Snake: I told you to catch! Raiden: The birdies are pretty floating around my head. The roaring of the fighter jet awakens Raiden from his daze. He scrambles up to see the plane lowering itself straight down on top of him, flames of exhaust shooting down. Kriegidus: BURN BABY!!!!!! Argy: Burn baby burn....disco inferno, churn baby churn..... Krieidus: Will you shut up!? The odd transexual brings the plane closer to the bridge, the hot exhaust scorching Raiden's hair. Raiden: My hair, NO!!!!! Frantically Jack grabs the crate, pries it open and yanks out a Stinger missile launcher. In desperation he points it up, blasting nearly point blank at the Harrier. Argy: That's cheating. After the initial shot Argy pulls the plane back up, then whips it around and speeds out of sight. Raiden: I want to go home! Snake: Raiden this is no time for that! Raiden: This is a perfect time for that! Snake: DUCK!!!!! Jack looks about in confusion, then throws himself to the floor just as the plane zips by deadly close to his head. Kriegidus: You should have beheaded him there! Argy: I cannot help it, I can't concentrate without my musica. Kriegidus: Oh very well, put the damned thing on! Clapping his hands girlishly, Argy reaches into the front of his shirt and produces a tape. On the bridge, Raiden looks on as Barbie Girl by Aqua begins blaring out of the airplane. Raiden: What the HELL? I'm a Barbie Girl, in a Barbie World! I'm made of plastic....it's fantastic! The Harrier charges in again, floating at the side of the bridge and launching a group of bombs as Argy sings along. Argy: You can brush my hair, undress me everywhere! Kriegidus: (Holding his ears) World domination isn't NEARLY worth this. Raiden ducks and dodges, trying his hardest to stay alive but the sheer number of explosives proves too much. A blast knocks him off the bridge.....Kriegidus laughs evilly. Kriegidus: Mwahahaha! We got him! Argy: I'm a Barbie Girl..... Kriegidus: We got him already, turn the damned thing off! Argy: Party pooper. Kriegidus: And take us over there! Begrudgingly Argy does as asked....flicking off the radio, then slowly taking the Harrier to the spot Raiden fell. Kriegidus: Well, let's..... Kriegidus stops short as he looks down and sees Raiden hanging by one hand from the railing. Kriegidus: Oh, dammit! Argy: What now, boss? Kriegidus: What do you mean what now? ARM THE GUNS SO I CAN SHOOT HIM WITH THE GUNS!!!!! Argy: Very well. With a few flips of switches, Argy prepares the machine guns of the fighter jet. Kriegidus then begins spraying them slowly at Jack, who kicks his feet frantically. Raiden: NO!!!!! Just before the bullets reach their intended target, Raiden kicks himself back up onto the bridge underneath the hail of gunfire. Argy: What the. Holding a stitch in his side, Raiden begins running circles around the remains of the walkway. Kriegidus: What the hell is he doing? Argy: He appears to be running around. Kriegidus: I know that Argy! Argy: Then why did you ask? Kriegidus: It was a rhetorical question! Argy: I don't believe you. Kriegidus: BAH! In frustration Kriegidus whips his guns around, trying to cut Jack into pieces but the commando proves too fast. Kriegidus: Take us higher so I can get a clear shot! Argy: Uh, boss.... Kriegidus: Don't question me! Just do it! Argy: Fine. Shaking his head, Argy lifts the plane again.....as soon as he does though a massive blast nails the underside of the plane. Then another, then another. Kriegidus: What the hell is that!? Argy: He's blasting us with surface to air missiles, sir. Kriegidus: Why didn't you tell me!? Argy: You said go up, we go up. Kriegidus: But we're going to be killed! Argy: I'm only following orders. And frankly you were being quite rude. Kriegidus: AGH!!!! More explosions strike the underside of the vessel......it begins to spin like a carnival ride gone out of control. Kriegidus: Can't you keep us steady? Argy: Well I probably could if he wasn't pelting us with missiles. Kriegidus: That's all your fault! Argy: No, it is yours. Kriegidus: Yours! Argy: Yours! Kriegidus: Yours! Argy: Yours! Kriegidus: Yours! Argy: Mine! Kriegidus: Mine! Argy: Yours! Kriegidus: Mine! Argy: Agreed. Kriegidus: ARGH!!!!! Another missile nails the Harrier, this one hard enough to cause the battered plane to spin straight off the edge of the bridge and towards the helicopter carrying Snake. Snake: Bobby, pull us up! Nothing but silence greets his words. Snake: Bobby, I said..... Snake turns, then frowns in disgust. Snake: Pull your pants up and get us the hell out of the way! Bobby: Sorry. Just in the nick of time the fat man does yank on the lever (er, the other lever) and the copter rises out of the way of the burning plane. The Harrier sinks out of sight, the screams of its passengers filling the air. Raiden: We got him! Almost as if in response to Raiden, a strangely whiny shriek fills the air. Snake: Uh oh. A huge jet of water erupts as the robot known as Metal Gear Kreller leaps from the sea, holding the remains of the Harrier in his teeth. Inside the cockpit of the plane, Kriegidus blinks.....then slowly raises his hand to his eye. Kriegidus: Uh..... The evil leader lowers his hand again, then shakes furiously at the sight of blood. Kriegidus: They got my eye!!!!! Rage builds in Kriegidus, he snarls and shouts. Kriegidus: Argy, go! Argy: Aye aye sir! Kriegidus: ..... Argy: I think I will go see if I can get a closer look at the eyesore! Kriegidus: Ooooooooh, shut up! Argy: Aye aye! Kriegidus: ...... With that the cockpit opens again, Argy leaps out. Spreading his arms, he slowly, dramatically falls to the ocean.....then sinks beneath the depths. Kriegidus: ...... Snake: ...... Raiden: ...... Kriegidus: ...... Snake: ....... Raiden: ....... Finally the odd man pops up again sputtering. Argy: Need more practice with that trick. Pulling himself up to the surface, Argy stands on it carefully. He then begins to sprint, faster, faster across the waves. Snake: It's running on water! Argy: (Tongue hanging out) Huff....huff..... Finally the vampire leaps, striking an Air Jordan pose. Up, up he leaps as cameras flash out of nowhere.....reaching the top of the strut and darting away. Meanwhile Metal Gear Kreller dives again, pulling the plane down with it. Kriegidus: Wait I didn't close the hatch againgurglegurglegurgle! Finally all is silent on the smoking, nearly destroyed bridge.....Raiden stands. He shakes his head......takes two steps, then falls flat on his face out cold. Snake: ...... To Be Continued
