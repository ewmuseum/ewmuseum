Fishing Trip Trio (part 1)

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/03 The sky was painted blue, and the beautiful smells of spring lingered in the air, as the squirrels and birds frolicked around in the trees enjoying the weather. They weren’t the only ones enjoying the weather however, also out were Brianna, Milo, and Kramer who had found time in their busy schedules to plan a fishing trip. “I don’t like the idea of messing with such slimy, disgusting creatures.” Milo protested as Brianna planted a fishing hat filled with hooks, onto his well groomed hair. “Stop being such a baby. This’ll be fun trust me.” “Fun? This is far from fun Bri. Fun is going to the pool for a swim. Fun is a Spring Break party with a bunch of hot guys and all the beer you can drink. Fun is watching Martha Stewart show you twenty different ways to plant the same flower.” Brianna gives Milo a raised eyebrow at his last statement, but shrugs it off, putting her own fishing hat on top of her curly reddish brown hair. Neither her nor Milo looks right in the hats. The light tan colored hat doesn’t go with the bright blue silk shirt and black pants Milo dons. And Brianna’s white button up short sleeve shirt and tight dark red shorts don’t go well either. “So where is Kramer at? Isn’t it about time for us to go on our torturous adventure to waters?” Milo asks checking himself in a hand held mirror he pulled from his pocket, looking highly displeased at his appearance. “He had to rent the boat. He should be calling soon.” Brianna replies looking through her rented tackle box, checking out the hooks springing an idea in her head. Milo takes a moment away from looking at himself in the mirror, and peers over at Brianna catching her expression. “Oh I know that look! You’re not planning on bringing one of your gadgets along are you? Because if you do, I feel less safe than I did before.” “Thanks for boosting my self esteem there Milo.” “Oh come on. Your gadgets are good and all, but sometimes they whack out and either blow up or cause serious damage. I don’t really want to deal with that today.” Brianna closes the tackle box and clutches a hook in her right hand keeping it out of sight. She then starts walking out of the living room and heads for the stairs leading upstairs. “Where are you going?” Milo asks quizzically taking a seat on the couch, lounging about before it’s time to head out. “I forgot something. I’ll be down in a minute.” Brianna races up the steps attacking them two at a time, before she makes it to the top. Instead of going straight to her room, she turns right, which leads to her lab. She opens the door and slides in quickly shutting it lightly. Her eyes travel around the room as she sets the hook on the table centered directly in the middle of the room. She grabs a couple of things off of the shelves, and pulls out a toolbox from under the table. Just as she is starting to screw odds and ends together, the scene switches downstairs where Milo is busy dozing off on the coach. A sharp, consistent rapping comes from the door but Milo doesn’t budge as he has slipped off into sleep. “Anyone here?” A voice shouts from the door. Another loud knocking can be heard and finally Milo comes to live shaking his head as he sits up on the couch. “Hold on a sec.” He calls, while standing on his feet pulling off the dreadful hat as he pulls open the door. “It’s about time. I thought you guys decided to go without me or something.” A very agitated Kramer says, while stepping into the house. “No such luck. Brianna’s been upstairs for the past fifteen minutes and I think she’s concocting some sort of gadget.” Milo says while making his way back to the couch. “You say it like your worried. What’s so bad about her bringing one of her gadgets along? Who knows it might help us catch some big succulent fish.” Milo shudders at the descriptions used to describe the fish. “Do you know if she’ll be any longer? I could only rent the boat for three hours, and I want to use up the time for all of the money I spent on it.” Kramer says looking at the unenthused Milo, realizing who could care less. Just as Kramer is going to call for Brianna, he sees her skipping down the steps with a fishing rod in her hand. “Milo that’s not so bad.” Kramer says, while following Brianna to the door. “Just you wait and see Kramer. It may not look deadly, but I bet you it is.” “Still talking about me?” Brianna asks Milo while giving him a friendly push out of the door and onto the porch. All three walk to Kramer’s Omni, wearing matching hats, and looking quite the opposite of any real fisherman. They all take their seats, and within seconds the car revs up and takes off down the street. “So I heard you have another big match on Turmoil…as well as Fusion.” Kramer says as the car comes to a halt at a red light. “I would rather not talk about work right now. I just wanna have fun and talking about that just brings stress.” Brianna replies crossly, which causes Kramer to try to figure out what he said that offended her so much. Milo catches Kramer’s worried look from the mirror and laughs a bit. “Don’t take her reply to heart Kramer. It’s her time of the month.” “Oh.” Kramer says uneasily. “MILO!” Brianna booms, highly embarrassed by his announcement. “Hey you make it so obvious.” The red light switches to green and the car continues on its path down the street. The sound in the car has dropped to pure silence as no one can come up with a conversation starter. They don’t have to worry about that anymore as they pull up to the dock, their destination. All three of them pile out of the car and take a few steps out near the edge of the dock. Milo turns pale because one of the planks he’s stepping on, sags under his weight. He quickly steps off of it and rushes back on to solid ground, not willing to take any chance. “Stay right here while I go get our boat.” Kramer says jogging towards a fat, gray haired man with a bushy beard. “So you still actually think this is going to be fun Bri?” Milo asks to pass time. “Certainly.” She replies placing her hat back on top of her head. She turns to Milo and pulls his hat out of his clutches and slams it on top of his head to his dismay. Brianna stands looking out of the water, and enjoying the glistening spots, as Kramer pulls up to the edge of the dock in a small motorboat. Brianna wastes no time, and rushes towards it tackle box and self-made fishing rod in hand. Milo follows reluctantly with his head down. As he steps in the boat his face instantly contorts into that of disgust. “This is dirty! Look there’s filth everywhere!” he pouts. “A little dirt never hurt anyone.” Brianna replies, pulling on his shoulder causing him to sit next to her. “Let’s let the torture begin shall we?” Kramer says in Milo’s direction as he pulls the chord on the motor. The boat starts shaking and then pulls away from the dock, on a direct course to the center of the water. The scene slowly fades to black. TO BE CONTINUED!
