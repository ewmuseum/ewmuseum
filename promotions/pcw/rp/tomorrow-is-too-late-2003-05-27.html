Tomorrow is too late

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/05/03 .... Silence.... .... Who was it that once said 'Silence is golden'....? .... Whoever it was, they were a fool.... .... Silence isn't golden.... .... In fact, it has nothing to do with gold what-so-ever.... .... Silence is just a sign of the weak.... .... And so, it comes as little surprise that Manuel Labor has once again appeared on our television screens.... .... No doubt he'll have some harsh words to give to Adam Stryker and Kael Law.... .... After all, it is those two who he has most of his dealings with this past week.... .... It was Law who interfered in my celebration, giving aid where aid was not needed.... .... It was Kael Law who nailed Manuel Labor with a vile chair shot that took him down and most definitely out.... .... It is Kael Law who for whatever reason has set out to make himself look good at the Lucha Legends expense.... .... Well, one thing's for sure.... .... Whoever came up with 'Silence is golden' did not come up with 'What goes around, comes around'.... .... On the contrary, where as one means jack shit, the other is a picture perfect outlook on life.... .... Kael Law may believe he's got Fuller on the run.... .... But hell.... .... Just wait until Wednesday Turmoil.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- .... One month.... .... It can make a helluva difference.... .... After a month in the PCW, both Adam Stryker and Manuel Labor's careers were in different places.... .... Becoming a true superstar doesn't happen over night.... .... Hell, it doesn't even always take a year.... .... But, when the timing's right, you'll know it.... .... When Manuel Labor reaches his peak and proves what he is worth, you'll see him with that strap around his waist and the biggest smile he's ever had wrenched across his face.... .... It doesn't matter how long it takes to get to the top just so long as you do get there, and trust me fans.... .... Manuel Labor will get there.... .... Some people got it.... .... Some people don't.... .... But just what this fabled 'it' is however, is anyone's guess.... .... Does having 'it' mean that you can pull off win after win and prove yourself to be the greatest thing since sliced bread....? .... Or could having 'it' simply mean that you have the ability to entertain the fans, regardless of the result of the match.... .... I'd like to believe the latter.... .... Granted, mostly because it makes Manuel Labor look a little better in his current light, but hey....? .... Why can't I be biased....? .... The fact of the matter is, Labor deserves it.... .... He's given up a lot to be where he is today, made great sacrifices and gave everything his all.... .... Plus, he's one of the few performers in this weird and wacky business that has the ability to entertain the fans.... .... Screw the fact he spends more time on his back than with his arms raised in the air.... .... Screw the fact that when push comes to shove, he's the guy left lying.... .... Screw the fact that when we look back at the record book, his name won't be remembered amongst the brightest of stars.... .... The truth is, it's not how many titles you earn, nor how big your win/loss tally looks.... .... What makes a man in this business is entertaining the people who watch it.... .... Long ago did actual wrestling pass through the window.... .... No one gives a damn really.... .... What matters is that they can go out there and have such an effect on that audience that when they're getting in their car, getting set to leave the arena, or when they're lying in bed that same night, they can think back to themselves.... .... Shit.... That guy was pretty cool.... .... Maybe I'm wrong about this.... .... Maybe I'm too new-aged in my thinking to be speaking sense.... .... Or maybe, I'm right and times have changed to such an extent that the guidelines of old, and the way this business once worked doesn't apply anymore.... .... But whatever way you look at it, whichever path you chose to take, I'll be sticking by my guns.... .... Manuel Labor may have been knocked down once again, but he's not out.... .... Not by a long shot.... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
