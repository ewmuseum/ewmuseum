Intrigue, Betrayal & Deceit

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Intrigue, Betrayal & Deceit Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 22/11/02 ‘What was with Kannon and Akiyama?’ they asked, given that the much accepted opinion was that the nVg detested the arrogant CEO, seeing him as cowardly. But all of a sudden, there seemed to be some common ground between the two very different personalities. Nobody was under any illusions that Akiyama was doing it out of respect for Xavier’s authority. Nor that Kannon didn’t fear any kind of confrontation with the giant Japanese exile. “As you’ve seen, and will see.... I can be very persuasive.” The night DarkStar returned to PCW, brought out by Rent-a-Hero, PCW went through changes. First, people power. In something never seen before, the entire Korean crowd stood at Renty’s call and started heading to the exits, the scenes being sent live to every PCW follower around the world. Xavier had been backed into a corner for the first time ever, and the Mourning Star was back. But from potential embarrassment, came utter shock. “Maybe he just decided it was better to be with me than against me?” Nobody outside the Front Office has ever wanted to ‘align’ themselves, as it were, with Kannon since he became CEO. They knew any benefits to them would be outweighed by his inevitable betrayal, after all, he’d done it to everyone else. Then again, who’d want to get in the giant Akiyama’s bad books. “Someone should have measured how high that Goth bounced off the stage, might be a record.” Just as the Spandex-clad PCW Hero and the Mourning Star were rejoicing their live defeat of Kannon’s will, the curtains flew open behind them and the cheering crowd suddenly gasped, knowing what would happen before the unfortunate faces. Kick. Crunch. Hallmark.Lift. Awe. Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. Using the distraction, the CEO could throw Rent-a-Hero down headfirst between his legs, sending his already weakened neck compacting under his weight as his head connected with a dull thud onto the metal ramp. “Everyone has to have something in common, right?” How could Kannon and Aki get along? They were everything each other hated. The nVg had no time for Xavier’s sort, the God standing silent while the Scientologist always seemed to have some words leaving his mouth, Akiyama getting by on raw power and agility when Xavier survived on a staple diet of illegal moves and run-ins. But after DarkStar’s body bounced up into the air after being drilled, and Rent-a-Hero laid motionless... The 2 men just looked straight into each others eyes and the world could see what had been done. “What was done, was what had to be done.” No matter how much power and sway Xavier had, he was one attack from Akiyama away from a hospital bed, while at the same time, the nVg knew that Kannon has no qualms about firing or suspending anyone at the drop of a hat. So they just looked at one another as the crowd booed. A slight nod from each man acting as a suitable exclamation mark for what just went before it as “Rock is Dead” played, the Scientologist not taking his eyes off the giant until the curtain closed behind his giant frame... Then he knew he was safe. “And it worked, for as long as I wanted it to.” In the weeks running up to the Mall Brawl, there wasn’t a bad word or action between the Front Office and the Celestials. Both sides happy to let the other exist and do their thing. Despite their differences, they still had one thing in common, they were hated. Maybe that was another attraction to the arrogant Scientologist, a man who seems to find great amusement by getting a rise out of crowds. There was talk of the Celestials and the Office merging, surely brining un-rivalled power upon PCW. “I had all the power I needed.” Indeed he did. Throughout the whole thing, Akiyama probably knew it was coming, after all, Xavier Kannon isn’t someone you turn your back on for a seconds, let alone weeks. And just after the Mall Brawl, Xavier Kannon and Akiyama were due to be partners, both men in the same corner... Surely the Scientologist would make his move then, wouldn’t he? It didn’t even take that long. “Like you didn’t see it coming.” In the Japanese Mall, Akiyama looked unstoppable. Body after body was broken and eliminated before him, and the giant looked to be making a victorious return to the country that exiled him. Only one man stood in his path, Inphino Blitz. But that one was soon to double. “If I didn’t do it first, he would of.” True. So as Akiyama and Inphino Blitz battled, a Hand of Fate did indeed seal Blitz’s... Driving the Black Dragon Master through a fire door to elimination. Then, a thudding clink of pressurised metal on skull, a dull pain resonating throughout his head and a stumble forward. The eyes were rolling back into the head of the nVg so as not to see the smirking figure of Xavier Kannon stood behind him, Fire Extinguisher in hand and a smugness causing a warm glow though his body. “It didn’t go exactly to plan though.” Maybe Xavier should learn from what he does, for the same fate befelled him and shortly afterwards he and Rent-a-Hero went through the same door to elimination... The same door where Akiyama was waiting, enraged. It all began with Kannon and the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse onto metal, and that’s where Akiyama must have though it ended. Dragging the dazed body of Xavier up, the nVg knew exactly what he was going to do, and did it with more conviction than ever... Lifting the Scientologist up above the detached metal firedoor and implanting his body down onto it, the impact knocking Xavier out cold before he even knew he was off his feet. “But I always have the final say.” If Akiyama made the deal to stop Xavier interfering in his business, it worked, but as soon as Xavier regained consciousness everything Aki tried to prevent happened. Using every bit of power he, Ellie~! and the Office have, Xavier has set upon stopping the Celestials winning Alliances. Last Friday he made the Main Event at Fusion a battle of Allies, The End v Akiyama in a Last man Standing Match. “Don’t forget... You know?” But no matter how much the nVg and the Void Ninja beat one another, no matter now much punishment was inflicted, it couldn’t hurt their chances more that the master stroke. The 4th member of the Celestials, Boca Del Inferno. The clamp to their wheel, the shackles to their legs, the Albatross around their neck... ... The Reamer in their team. “I was simply making up numbers.” At Alliances will we see Xavier Kannon with his back turned for just a second fall foul of Akiyama? In the Main Event, so many things from Kannon’s past will have the chance to get revenge, question is will Akiyama even find a way into the queue? Or simply fight his was to the front of it? He was cost a win by Kannon in Japan, but in San Antonio, he won’t make the same mistake again. “Different mistake, same result... He won’t win.” He played Akiyama before, he suckered him in, he betrayed him and he eliminated him. Now he just has to beat him. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
