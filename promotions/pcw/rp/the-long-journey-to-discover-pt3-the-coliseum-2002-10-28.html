The Long Journey to Discover....pt3: The Coliseum

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th Oct When last seen El Diablo had just burned t-shirts of his rivals Duke Williams, Jason Snow and Tim Walden in an Italian shopping mall in Rome. Why? To burn away their hopes of victory in their four-way winner goes on to face the PCW Champion at Alliances match, but would this really work? However, that wasn't the only reason why El Diablo burned the shirts, another reason was to send a message to Clyde. A message to show that he will do anything to get the title that Clyde holds, a message to show that he will be just as sick and twisted as Clyde is and a message to show that his days of holding the PCW Title are slowly burning away until the 24th of November when El Diablo will capture that title and be the pinnacle of PCW. It's simply a dream of El Diablo's to become PCW Champion and he is desperate to make that into a reality, just like any man would. Only now, it is an actual possibility of becoming a reality. El Diablo's sudden turn and change of heart at Red October showed exactly what he will do to become PCW's Champion, and to what extent of brutality he will stoop to just to get a shot at that title. Is El Diablo a desperate man? Or just a mastermind in progress?------------- In view is one of the most beautiful sights ever seen, a place where many battles raged on, a place where wars were a daily ritual, a place where thousands would come to watch pure brutality- THE COLLOSEUM in Rome, or at least the ruins left from it. PCW is nothing more than a modern day Coliseum, battles rage on in the ring and wars go on between the mighty, and just like in Ancient Roman times thousands attend the events to watch the sheer brutality and art of the fighting. The only difference is, PCW is televised and gets a worldwide audience. What a shame that the gladiators of those times never got such exposure, they must have been truly artful and true warriors in their sport. And now PCW comes to Rome, how can it match up to such legendary fighting that must have occurred here back in the day? The ruins stretch on for what seems like an eternity, and how disappointing it is that such a marvel was destroyed and no longer stands to support anymore, great battles. El Diablo is standing in awe of what once was a great structure, and wondering what it must have felt like to fight there and be in the spotlight. To El Diablo, The Coliseum is a legendary battleground, where heroes like himself reigned and where villains like Clyde were slain. It's almost like a dream to be fighting in Rome, but to be fighting inside The Coliseum would be truly magical to him, and there he would show himself to be the best fighter in the world, and how true it could be. El Diablo is with a tour group to look around those ancient ruins of The Coliseum, and for once El Diablo truly looks to be enjoying himself. With him, of course is his wife Angela and his 24/7 security men, Kurt and Leroy. They are both deliberately dressed much like the President of the United States security are, shirt and tie, long pants and polished shoes, long jacket and dark sunglasses. The way they are dressed and standing around El Diablo, it shows him to be an important person, when in reality he is no more important than say, a cop. Yes El Diablo is important in his own mind. The tour group is a group of about fifteen people, including El Diablo and cronies, and of course the sixteenth person there is the well-tanned, dark haired beauty at the front, called 'Natasha' El Diablo knows her name due to the simple fact that she is wearing a name-tag for all to see. Angela does not feel threatened at all though. 'Now, this area here is where the Emperor would be seated to watch the battles. It is a modern day owners box,' Natasha says sternly as she directs the tour group to wander over to another area. The tour group of course walks steadily over to where Natasha is showing them. 'It's a great tour group, considering that she's doing it in English,' whispers Kurt slowly and softly. The Mexican Devil nods and for one moment takes his eyes off what was a hero's lair, and looks back at Kurt. 'It's because we all speak English here. But yes, impressive,' confirms El Diablo before turning back to look once again awestruck at the impressive sight. Behind El Diablo though there is some pushing and shoving between some people. It soon becomes apparent that it's Kurt and Leroy pushing people away from being in the same vicinity as The Lord of Mexico, El Diablo. Meanwhile, El Diablo is oblivious to what is occurring as he is so mesmerised by what was The Coliseum. 'Move away,' Kurt says as he shoves a man down to the ground and begins kicking dirt on him. 'Just step back ten metres!' warns Leroy to a large man, 'No one is allowed near El Diablo, he is THE important person here,' he finishes. The large man shakes his head in disgust and just walks back, of course by now the tour guide Natasha has noticed what is going on. 'Right, you four go! You're banned from being here,' Natasha speaks loudly, aiming her objections at Kurt, Leroy, Angela and El Diablo. Kurt and Leroy look annoyed as they drag El Diablo away from The Coliseum. El Diablo is still in awe at the famous structure and has yet to acknowledge that they were sent away. Angela follows closely behind them. As they walk away, the rest of the tour group just applaud and cheer at their ejection. When El Diablo realises what has happened he is obviously angry and upset, as he believes he was receiving some sort of power from just being where so many great athletes and fighters have stood before. Where blood has been spilt and where bones have been broken, and just being there and looking at the size of The Coliseum it has shown El Diablo just how small he really is in relation to those surroundings. Although small, he's not obsolete and he purely intends to show and prove that. 'WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT?' shouts El Diablo in annoyance of being sent away from what he considers to be a magnificent structure. The four of them are now seated on the edge of a water piece outside of The Coliseum, and El Diablo is seething with anger. 'We were keeping those vermin away from you,' Kurt reassures his boss and friend. Diablo smiles and looks down at the dusty ground. 'Well, that's great, but now because of the vermin I cannot be in The Coliseum, and that could surely cost me my match on Tuesday,' he says softly with a hint of anger in his tone. Angela wraps her well-tanned arms around her husband and kisses him on the cheek. 'Why not sneak back in?' Angela says with an evil smirk on her face. Could they? Security must be tight at this famous sight, and then there is that tour group who know that they were sent away and out of there. Is it really a possibility that they could sneak back in? And for what reason would they want to sneak back into The Coliseum for? 'Lets,' smiles El Diablo calmly. ****NIGHT APPROACHES**** Several hours have now passed by, and the time is now nearing midnight and The Coliseum's tours are now well over, but security is still strong around the area. 'Now is the time,' whispers El Diablo to everybody. They all nod and slowly start to crawl across the ground as they begin to approach an entrance to it. As they see the entrance they notice that the guard is fast asleep and that they are crawling for no reason. 'Stupid,' Diablo whispers again. They all stand up and wipe the dirt off of their clothes and all go inside the area that once was the magnificent structure labelled The Coliseum. 'Just imagine it,' says The Lord of Mexico, 'I am standing right now where many great and ancient fighters must have stood, and stood victorious of course,' he says with a mighty roar of passion. Angela tries to shush him, so they aren't seen sneaking in. 'Surely if I was born a few centuries earlier, I would have been headlining The Coliseum and defeating the world's greatest fighters here. And if I could that, then I will have no problem with Duke, Snow and Walden! THEY ARE NOT AS TALENTED AS I AM. THEY ARE NOT AS GOOD AS I AM. THEY ARE ALL WORTHLESS VERMIN,' El Diablo yells into the night air. Diablo breathes heavily and looks around the broken Coliseum, only he can see it standing in his eyes and he is the centrepiece of it, he is the warrior preparing for battle in the middle of it all, and all the fans are roaring him on. Diablo rips his shirt off in the heat of the moment, and remains standing with his muscular physique showing. 'No one can beat me. I will be INVINCIBLE!' he again yells into the cool night. At the end of his loud yell, several flashlights single in on them all, and by then they all know what to do- RUN. And run they do, trying to leave behind any clue that will show that they were there. A security guard walks to where they were and find El Diablo's torn shirt. 'Que?'
