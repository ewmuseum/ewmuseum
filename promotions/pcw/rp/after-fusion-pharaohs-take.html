After Fusion (Pharaoh’s take)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today “Now you’ve gone and done it, you stupid son of a bitch!” I hissed, rolling in agony upon the white-quilted bed in the medical section of the James H. Hilton Coliseum. “What the fuck did you have to go and do that for? Are you really that dumb?!” Shouting really was no good. I’d have to personally see to it that Clyde got what he and Bethany deserved for trying to crush one such as I with a frickin’ lighting-rig. The fools…did they really think they could eliminate me from the equation so easily? “Someone, Godsdamn help me over here!” A balding EMT rushed towards my presence, his brow furrowed with concern. They were trying to help me, I’d give them that, but unfortunately for my aching body nothing seemed to ease the pain I was suffering. “Are you alright, sir?” “Answer me this…do I look alright?!” I laughed, painfully. My entire body was covered with cuts, burns and gashes and by Horus did it hurt! The EMTs had attempted to cool the wounds with ice-cold water – it was something I wished they hadn’t attempted. It seemed to only make the slashes, bruises, and other bodily wounds I had tolerated hours ago feel worse. I rolled there, in utter pain, as more men and women came to aid me. “We’ll try the water--…” “No, you fuckin’ won’t!” I snapped. “But, sir--…” “There are no ‘buts’, man!” I bellowed, “Just give me some pain-killers or something to ease my aching frame!” How the Duat I’d survived I’d never know for certain. But I vaguely remembered Akiyama switching on his void armour before the thing had a chance of destroying the both of us. Perhaps the armour protected me as well as the nVg? Perhaps it didn’t? Who knew for definite? All that I knew right now was that I’d survived…the Gods had given me a second chance…and this time I wasn’t going to fuck it up. For the wounds I’d suffered here tonight I’d beat Akiyama and take the World Title as repayment! But there I am speaking of the future again. First I’d actually have to make sure Akiyama was defeated. And that, in its own right, would be something that’d take up all of my heart, energy, and faith. I’d beaten the nVg before, but lately he’d transformed from a focused being of ultimate power to a motivated, monstrous God of ultimate destruction. The Neo Void God had changed drastically and it would take all of my ability to stop him in his path of gaining the World Title. “Here you are, Mr. Xansiv.” Said an EMT holding a glass of water and two small white tablets within the safe confines of his palm. “Thank you…” I snatched the items from his hands and gulped down the fluid after I’d inserted the tablets carefully into my mouth. I could only hope that this would suffice my consistent agony. My entire body winced as I drank…was it even possible for your throat to feel like it’d been crushed when in actual fact it hadn’t? My eyes wandered about the hospital-like room… What had happened to the God, Akiyama? I hoped the son of a bitch had died in the accident (and I call it an accident in the most limited of meanings). But deep down I knew he wasn’t dead. How the Duat could you kill someone--something like him--like it. It was practically impossible. Earlier on in Fusion I had attempted to blow the bastard the Duat up, and even then it was the one who was left without hardly a scratch on its massive frame. Right now I feared not to address the beast as a ‘him’, for it certainly wasn’t human enough to be classed as that. Akiyama; the nVg; the monster of PCW, had been banned from Japan months ago, a time I’ve tried to forget on many an occasion, and yet it defies its limits in the world to gain a chance of beating the living crap out of me and a shot at the World Heavyweight Title. And now I look about the medical ward in this dreary arena and find that the monster has gone. It’s nowhere to be seen, and one would of thought that it would have been brought here after the lighting-rig fell from the heavens. The nVg truly was a personified equivalent of defiance. And this coming Sunday I’d have to battle that beastly creature until I’d pinned its shoulders to the mat for three seconds. Three Godsdamn seconds to beat a God at its own game – it truly wasn’t enough… “It should kick in soon, Mr. Xansiv.” Said an EMT, breaking my thoughts. “Yes, thanks…” I murmured absent-mindedly. Then it struck me… Where was Isiy?! I vaguely remembered something happening…something bad happening. Akiyama had done something to her…NO! Cyclonia had done something to her…but what? Damn it! My frickin aching head was so pummelled after the shots Akiyama had put down upon me that I couldn’t remember… Then it hit me…like a fist from the God wearing his void gauntlets. Cyclonia had wickedly beaten Isiy down to a bloody pulp after she’d nobly come to my aid. How I wished she’d never got involved. How I wished I’d never got my wife occupied in my wrestling life. It had all begun with Xavier Kannon many, many months ago back in NTICW. And now, at present, it was Akiyama and Cyclonia’s evil that had wrecked my wife’s stability again… How could I live with this any longer? I’d have to do something…something to all those who had disgraced Isiy and I. Clyde and Bethany, for trying to kill me, would pay dearly sooner or later. Akiyama would pay for its part in Cyclonia’s beating of Isiy at Red October, and perhaps I’d even manage to gain some sort of revenge on the Celestial this Friday at Turmoil, too. But, no…I couldn’t worry about those matters at present…I had to find out where my wife was. I knew Isiy was scared of the Divine Prophetess…it was obvious from the way she acted in the arena and at home when I mentioned Akiyama’s managers’ name, but I wasn’t afraid. I’d avenge her…I’d get Cyclonia back somehow. Nobody, after all, touches my one true love and gets the fuck away with it! I tried to force my pain-flooded body from the bed, so was my need to see her beautiful face again. But it was in vain as I fell back on the grubby mattress, out of breath. “Medic!” I cried. A man, short and stubby, rushed to my side. “Yes, sir?” “I want to see my wife! Where is she?!” I was frantic now…my worrying had enveloped me. The man’s brow furrowed as he contemplated my question, and at length he spoke, sympathy oozing off of every syllable uttered from his mouth. “Mr. Xansiv, your wife has already left the arena - she’s been taken to the Ames state hospital for a thorough check over.” He informed. “How is she? Is she alright?!” I questioned, a little uneasy. “She looked alright, sir. She was bleeding from the mouth…” I groaned in sorrow as his words processed through my skull. “But she seemed fine…a bit dizzy… but Ok, nonetheless.” I thought about his words for a moment and then looked him straight in the eye. “I want to go and see her now.” I said, attempting to get up again. The man looked about worryingly at his colleagues gathered in the room. “I don’t think that’ll be possible tonight, sir.” One said sharply. “After all, you’re not at all well enough to leave, just yet.” Another continued. Bah! To Duat with all their opinions on my medical state! I’d be fine…absolutely fine! And besides they couldn’t keep me here if I didn’t want to stay. My bruised legs swung from the bed’s side and touched the icy cold floor below. For one reason or another I was suddenly aware that my ripped loincloth was still wrapped tightly around my waist as I attempted to stand upon the ground. My vertical base was quickly taken from beneath me as I fell over, face first, and landed, thankfully, on the palms of my hands. “For fuck sake!” I snapped as EMTs tried to aid me. To their surprise I pushed them off forcefully, and crawled to the bed’s legs on my own accord. I scrapped myself back onto it and sighed, my expression hate-filled and angry beyond normality. Damn that Clyde and his schoolgirl lover Bethany! Because of their stupidity I couldn’t get the Duat out of this stupid arena until I was fully fit to see Isiy! Now I really was pissed off. At Red October I was going to unleash all my anger and rage on the nVg. Fuck his ability to kick my ass whenever he wanted. Fuck his Godliness. Fuck Cyclonia. And Fuck the World Title! It was about nobility now, not some glittering belt! It was about me and who the Duat I was! Akiyama was the one who started this whole thing off and unfortunately for him, I’d be the one finishing it! He obviously wanted a piece of me! So come and get it, Akira! Come and fuckin’ have a go and see what you end up with! In my opinion, you’ve bitten off more than you can chew, asshole, and at Red October we’ll see who remains the better being! You made a mistake when you came looking for trouble in my neighbourhood again, you fucked up piece of shit! Monster, they say? Ha! We’ll see who the dominant ‘monster’ is in our match, Akira! We’ll see who walks away without a few broken bones, nVg. We’ll see who beats the shit out of whom! And you, Cyclonia, you dare to beat down my wife? Well, let’s see how you like it when I beat you down! And do you know who the Duat you can blame for all the pain I’m going to put you through this Sunday, Akiyama? Clyde, Neo Void T H I N G…Clyde… For now, however, I’m stuck here, in this frickin’ arena, and because of that I can’t do anything to piss you off. So I guess I’ll have to wait until Friday on that special edition of Turmoil, won’t I? Yeah…you and Colt, huh? What a team… I chuckled silently to myself as the EMTs glared in shock. “What?!” And then they continued about their work, or did a pretty good job at looking like they did. I still hurt. I still could hardly move. But rest assure I’ll be ready for Friday, and more importantly, Sunday. You really think you’re going to beat me, nVg? Then think again, because for what you’ve done, you’ll pay with your pride. I know I can’t take your life…but I can take your shot at the World Title. So I guess I’ll see you in Duat, Akiyama. I’ll see you there and know damn well that it was I who beat you on two separate PPVs and cost you what you wanted the most. Akiyama?… nVg?… Neo Void God?… …pfft…
