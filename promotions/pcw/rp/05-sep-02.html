Date: not entered You know, grenades don't explode into a shower of flames. When you throw a grenade, they explode with a loud, yet dull, thump. Like artillery shells. There's no Hollywood-sih explosion, and people don't fly all over the place, thrown by the concussion. The same way people don't fly ten feet in the air after being shot with a bullet. You see, what a grenade does is this - when you pull the pin and the spoon (the handle on the side of the grenade) flies off, propelled by a spring, you are completing a ciruit of sorts, igniting a timed fuse, which takes about 3-5 seconds to detonate, depending on the age and model of the grenade, information that can be easily discerned from the lot number, if you're fortunate enough to get ahold of the shipping crate it comes in. Now, when you throw it, and the fuse ignites the explosive charge, the explosive within blows apart the steel shell of the grenade. This does cause an explosion, but no flames. What happens is a dark cloud of smoke and a dull, loud thump is heard. Metal shrapnel, caused by the shell being torn apart from the explosion, flies everywhere, shredding flesh, kevlar, you name it. There is signifigent kinetic energy, and yes, it does blow things apart, but the major aim of a grenade is to utilize those expanding gases created by the explosion to propel those fragments into someone's flesh, riddling them. The blast radius of your run-of-the-mill frag grenade is about 5 meters, roughly 15 feet. The danger area, where you might get shredded by shrapnel, is about 15 meters, roughly 45 feet or so - its been awhile since I was at the grenade range. Inside the blast radius, you're toast, literally blown apart. If you're in the danger area, you're probably going to be the neew, proud owner of a piece or twelve of metal shrapnel, puncturing your body at sub-sonic speeds, hurtling through your body and tearing you apart. And the point is? When I see a piece of shit like 'Flaymin Aymin', I wish I had a standard issue M-67 fragmentation grenade. It would be easy. I could open his locker, and gently pull the pin, weighing down the spoon of the grenade with, say, a boot or something. The moment he carelessly opens his locker and lifts up his boot, the grenade will probably roll out of the locker, hitting the ceramic tile floor with a metallic clink, as his brain tries to register what the fuck is going on, and just about the time his addled brain realizes it, the grenade will detonate, utterly destroying him. Not that I need a grenade. I could easily come up behind him with a length of steel pipe, and precisely slam it at the base of his neck, shattering some vertebre, paralyzing him for life, a life that would be tragically cut short by the pipe caving in his skull. Or, using the same length of pipe, I could devise a pipe-bomb, or nail bomb. Materials easily pilfered from the contruction crew that PCW has, and a trip to the local drug store. A simple hotel room can become a primitive munitions factory. Flaymin Aymin. That cocky piece of shit. No regard for the sanctity of private property, one of the hallmarks of capitalism and the Enlightenment philosophers who inspired Thomas Jefferson, James Madison, John Jay, Benjamin Franklin, and the others. But unlike certain people in PCW, I won't kill Aymin. Nor will I resort to illegal methods. I merely have to destroy him in the ring. Take him apart, methodically, carefully. No, I am no petty lawbreaker. Not like Seymour Smoke. He is a weed-smoking burn-out loser. A hippy who thinks he has the right to flaunt the anti-drug laws of our country, the same country that gave birth to him, and quite incidentally, gives him the right to be a hippy, drug-addled, chronically unemployed pussy piece of shit. I will deal with him as well. I might like to break his kneecaps if he ever smokes that shit in the locker room before or during the time I am in there. See him writhing on the tile floor in pain as I bludgeon him repeatedly. I would like that. I would like that a lot. I, myself, am like a grenade. Tomorrow night, on Fusion, during my match, I will be the grenade, the bookers pulling my pin, my spoon flying off from the crowd, and throwing myself right into Aymin's midst. I will explode on him, I will shred his body like shrapnel. I will blow up, apart, on 'Flaymin Aymin'. And if anyone else annoys me with thier Communist ways, I will keep exploding, until this federation is free of law-breakers, drug addicts, and criminals. Violence in the workplace? Yeah, its here. And I'm not going anywhere. I'm the violence in these people's workplace. Tomorrow, I take the Extreme Title. And Flaymin Aymin is scraped up with a dustpan.
