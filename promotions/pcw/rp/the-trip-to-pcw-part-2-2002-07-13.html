The trip to PCW (Part 2)

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-13-02 (The scene opens up to Scorpion driving his 1996 Blue Chevy Cavalier into the parking lot of LaGuardia Airport in Flushing Meadows, New York. Police security is visible in every corridor of the airport. Men in black borates and in army wear walking bomb-sniffing dogs are walking around in the parking lot where cars stay over a period of time as they travel to curtain area. Scorpion approaches a booth where he takes a ticket and enters the lot where he will keep his car for the next couple of days.) This is it, this is the journey, this is where it all begins, this is the beginning of a new day for Scorpion. The PCW era begins now. (Scorpion parks his car in front of the fence barricading people from enter the facility. He takes the key out of the ignition and gets out of the driver side door.) Dam it’s hot out, this heat wave is killing me. (Scorpion takes a small hanky out of his pocket and wipes his forehead. Scorpion then puts the hanky back into his pocket and opens the back door so he can grab his baggage. Scorpion shuts the door and proceeds down to the sidewalk that takes you into the American Airlines area of the airport.) Whew, I need to calm down, I’m not even at the event yet and I’m already nervous. I need to calm down everything will be fine. (Scorpion enters the airport and goes directly to the security area where he places his bag on the track for them to be checked. He goes through the security machine and the machine begins to beep.) Oh no, could it get any worse? Security Guard: Sir please would you mind coming with me? Scorpion: Sure. Security Guard: All right then, right this way. (Scorpion walks to a corner of the area as the security guard uses a device that checks if he has anything on him.) Oh man, I don’t have anything, why are the doing this? Security Guard: All right sir….. Why is he pausing? Security Guard: Your all cleared to go. Scorpion: Thank you very much. Security Guard: Sorry for the incovience. Scorpion: It’s all right. (Scorpion grabs his page and heads down to the walkway to gates 18-31. Scorpion walks down to gate 23 and sits in a chair looking out into a window watching the planes land.) The PCW is a whole different atmosphere then CCW. I only know just a couple of guys there, how will I handle the pressure of performing my absolute best every day? I know I can do this, everyone is depending on me, I have to do this for my family because this is the big time now, and this is where everyone wants to be in PCW. (A lady comes over the intercom system.) Attendance lady: We will boarding the play from New York to Mississippi in just a few moments, so we will be begin boarding, any children flying alone, senior citizens, handicapped or families with a infant can begin boarding the plane at this time, thank you. (Scorpion looks at his ticket.) Row seven, seat E. I hope that it’s a good seat; it doesn’t seem that is a full house. Did I bring my Discman? Yeah I know I did, I could have never forgot that, that’s the only thing that keeps me occupied. I’m so nervous; I just wish that it would just happen now. I wish I were there now. Attendance lady: All right ladies and gentleman, we will begin boarding at this time. Sections 15-21 can begin boarding at this time, thank you. (Eight people walk up to the check in counter to board the plane. Scorpion begins to grow over anxious and gets in line trying to get in the plane.) They don’t check tickets sections, do they? Geez I hope they don’t cause I want to get on that plane now. Here we go it’s my turn. Attendance lady: Excuse me sir this is for section seven, we just called sections 15-21, I’m sorry but you have to sit down until your row is called, thanks. Scorpion: All right sorry. (Scorpion sits back down.) Dam it! They do check where you sit; all this tight security stuff is what has really changed the way we fly. Dam those terrorists! (The attendance lady walks back up to the counter.) Attendance lady: All right ladies and gentlemen we will begin calling rows 5-14 up to th counter. (Scorpion runs up to the counter) Attendance lady: All right sir, now it’s your turn. You bitch…. Attendance lady: All right sir, have a good flight and thanks for flying with American Airlines. Scorpion: Ok thank you. I only do this for the frequent flyer miles, if it wasn’t for that I’d be using Jet Blue, they got television! (Scorpion walks down the board ramp and onto the airplane. He shakes the pilot’s hand and waves to a sturdiest. He walks down the aisle, takes his bags and puts them over head and takes his seat, right next to the window.) I love window seats it’s like being a kid all over again. (The plane continues to slowly board as the sturdiest closes the overhead doors on the plane.) Pilot: All right ladies and gentlemen, we will begin to take off in the next couple of moments. The plane is about a quarter full so have a very comfortable ride. The time here in New York is 12:27 and we are scheduled to take off at 12:30, so sit back and relax as we make the trip to Mississippi, thank you for flying American Airlines and were glad to have you aboard. (The plane begins to leave the gate area. Backing up very slowly. As Scorpion looks out the window you can see the flight directors backing the plane up.) Wow that is a hard job. To be the eyes of the plane and all the pressure, wow that’s what I’m feeling right now, going into PCW. It’s like I’m blindfolded and trying to find the right path into PCW. (The plane begins to move onto the runway.) This is awesome, nobody is sitting next to me. Here we go, I better put on the headphones. (Scorpion grabs his Discman and puts in his mix CD. The first that plays is “Click, click, boom” from Salvia.) Click…click…Boom. Put down it down on the radio. Click…click…boom! (Scorpion begins to doze off.) Dam I’m so tired. Better get some rest before I head to PCW. (Yawns) (The scene closes with Scorpion falling asleep on the airplane.)
