Revitilization-Part 1

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-01-02 Soul-searching, something we all do at times. Something a lot of us need to do, for answers. But what if it isn’t us doing the searching, what if someone else is searching our souls. That can be a life-crippler but if we stand strong we can overcome anything, no matter how powerful. Our hearts, or brains and our souls are more powerful than most may think, united they can overcome any and all odds, no matter how difficult or unfeasible. But sometimes, if we cannot bring up the courage, the strength or the desire to trounce our problem then some may seek help, together they can and will overcome, and they must overcome. Sometimes, I feel like doing something adventurous, something, that on a usual day, I would never do. Though, ever since my horrific encounter with one ‘Jamie Lee’ in Tokyo, I feel I have no choice but to find my roots. Though I will not yet go back to Boston to look for them, or for that matter even the United States, first, I must clear my mind of the oppressive thoughts that have infested it. And, to clear my mind I must be alone, alone on top of the world. Jamie Celt could not yet return to Boston, first he had to finish the PCW World Tour, and he could not wait until it ended. Though in the mean time, his mind and soul needed cleansing, and what better place to do it than the Himalayan Mountains. He was in the heart of Switzerland, where the next PCW event was being held. It was Friday Fusion, and Jamie had a chance to break his losing streak by taking on the talented Gladiator, Eddie Mandrake, who was current PCW Cruiserweight Champion, in a non-title match. He felt this was his chance; he was having such a hard time getting into his matches in PCW, mentally that is. Though now that was trying to cleanse his mind and soul, he felt that could all end. He would finally have his full mind in his matches, and show the form that won him the NTICW World Title. Though, Mandrake would be no easy opponent to defeat. He was a true Gladiator, a titan in the ring. His skill was incredible and he was on top of his form as of late. Though, Jamie knew he could defeat him, if he could just have the right temperament towards the match, he felt his chances were good. Soft trickles of snow feel on Jamie’s wet hair. He dug his stick into the cold, snowy ground and pulled himself upwards. He breathed heavily with every step he took. The snow fell rapidly onto him. Though his heavy jacket and pants prevented the snow soaking into his skin. He barely felt the cold. In fact, he quite liked the cold, as he always seemed to be feeling overly hot as of late. He continued to look up the mountain. It was mainly a blur due to the heavy snowfall. Though he could see was near a landing. He carried a tick in his right hand. It was basically a piece of wood that had a sharp edge in order to stick into the snow. Jamie panted heavily. He was leaving forward, due to the heavy bag he carried on his back. He feared of he stood straight he would fall down the mountain. Though all the trials the he was facing, he loved every second of it. It was the most enjoyable experience he had had in months, and it was just what he had needed. In reality, he felt slightly out of place in PCW, but this, this would surely set everything into perspective for him. He made his final trudges towards the landing. Then finally he was on straight ground. He threw down and stick and turned round. He was at least five thousand feet up in the air. The cool wind blew into Jamie’s face. It was beautiful. He was cool, calm and hand preserved what he would have once considered an impossible task. While many would think you would be shattered after such a thing, Jamie felt completely different. He felt 100% revitalized. He sat down in the icy snow and breathed heavily, with a grin on his face. He then lay down in the snow, determined to get even cooler. He loved the cold conditions. Though, the snow seemed to be getting overly heavy and the wind started blowing extremely hard. Jamie feared that it would blow him down the mountain, which could indeed kill him. He got up from the snow and searched around for shelter. He looked all across the landing for a cave off some sort. It seemed hopeless; everything appeared to be closed over due to the heavy snow. Then finally, Jamie sat a small gap in the vertically onwards mountain. He crouched down on his knees and crawled into the tunnel. Inside it was not nearly as cool. He began to warm up again, which he did not really want, though still, he was glad to be away from the almost deadly wind. The cave seemed to be overly light. So, out of curiosity, Jamie continued down the tunnel, which got vertically wider as he went on. Then, there was a small corner in the cave. Jamie heard breathing, and the sound of a strong fire. He slowly continued down, and looked across the corner, and there he saw a man, a small man. He seemed to sense something was wrong and he looked over in Jamie’s direction, scared and bewildered. Jamie’s heart skipped a beat. The two men were staring at each other, with their eyes wide open. The small man’s fear then turned to anger. Jamie feared he would not even make it to his match with Eddie Mandrake, who was also one of his partners at PCW next Pay Per View, Alliances. The little man stood up, still looking at Jamie. “What the feck are ya doin’ there ya fecker!”
