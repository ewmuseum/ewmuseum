Fluff and stuff

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/01/03 'I don't remember when we exactly met. It was a short while ago. There was a faint smell of blood in the air. For a moment, I thougt it was a coin or something, that coppery smell. But it got stronger as I wandered downtown. Perhaps someone had been shot and I would be allowed to feast on the remains of the massacre. The shootings around here are rarely answered by the understaffed police force. So I followed my nose to what seemed to be a dead end. I should have known better - a butcher's. I was getting hungry anyway, so after taking a look around, I jammed a claw into the lock and jiggled until I heard that satisfying CLICK! and let myself in quietly. ' 'Fluffy not from here. Fluffy sleeping when things on two legs put big pile of sticks round him. Fluffy wake up and bug fly into Fluffy. Bug bite Fluffy and Fluffy go sleep again. Then Fluffy wake up later in big sticks and Fluffy see bright light. Fluffy scared and start banging sticks. Two leg things shout at Fluffy, so Fluffy make noise back. Two leg things go to sleep and then the sky begin to fall. Two leg things still don't wake up. Fluffy try to eat two leg things but they no taste nice. Fluffy shake because sticks jump around. Then sticks break and Fluffy come out. Two leg things still not wake up. Fluffy think they sleep all the time when they on fire. Two leg things sleep too much. Fluffy hungry.' 'At first, I thought I was going to find another vampire. My claws were ready for a fight. I needed to take some anger out on people anyway, the damn Front Office was busy maing my unlife miserable. The smell of blood was now near intoxicating. Corpses of animals hung on hooks all over the place and I imagined that Gaz Green would eventually be hanging from one of those hooks. There were slurping noises coming around from the corner and I wondered what lowly vampire would be so desperate to raid a butcher and suck up animal blood. And then, I saw him.' 'Fluffy see lot of two leg things. None of them sleepy. Fluffy smell food but Fluffy not want two leg thing to see Fluffy. Fluffy hide in dark. Lots of big rocks here to made shadows. Big rocks all look the same. Fluffy smell food and fly towards smell. Fluffy see Fluffy in water that doesn't stay on ground. Other Fluffy do exact same thing as Fluffy. When Fluffy float in through hole in water, other Fluffy run away. Fluffy find food but Fluffy find strange skin on food. Fluffy rip it off and rip food open. Fluffy food smell good. Fluffy eat.' 'The strangest little thing. It was as if someone had taken various parts of animals and formed a chimera of them. It had a tongue like a chameleon's, long and almost tubular. It was slightly transparent and I saw blood ascending it like a straw. And ingenious tool that had been neglected of my evolution. The next thing I noticed was the head, you couldn't help but notice it. It was huge, egg shaped and bulbous. Like those aliens... the ones you always see these days. His eyes are almond shaped, black and highly reflective. The rest of his body reminded me somewhat of a kangaroo. It's front paws were held downwards. Almost like a Tyrannosaurus Rex, except these hands could grip things, they were prehensile. The legs were huge, way out of proportion with the body. As I said, very much like a kangaroo. Starting at the top of it's head and running all the way down to the rear was a row of spines. They looked floppy and rubbery in texture. His entire body was grey and smooth, very shiny. And of course, it's mouth was wide open, revealing a very sharp set of teeth. I was amused by the fangs that resembled my own. And then he looked at me...' 'Fluffy eating and eating. Fluffy still hungry then see someone with two legs. He not have sticks like others. He look more like Fluffy but he have different colours. He had teeth like Fluffy. Fluffy think he copy Fluffy. He stand there looking at Fluffy with big paws.Fluffy get scared and two leg thing walk away. Fluffy think he scared too. Fluffy like him.' 'The most curious thing happened. The spines on his back shot up, rigid. He looked like a buzz-saw. The tongue shot back into his head as he lowered it. At first, I thought it was Gaz and one of his pranks, but then it cocked it's head to one side playfully and hopped forwards. The spines on his body went floppy again and he hopped forwards. On the off chance that he spoke, I did something that both humans, vampires and probably even this thing's species would consider stupid... and said hello.' 'Two leg thing make funny noise. Fluffy make same noise back. Fluffy good at that, because Fluffy do it to food and food come closer and Fluffy eat it. Two leg thing put out big paw. Fluffy like him, so Fluffy put out paw too. Two leg thing point at two leg thing and make another noise. Fluffy point at him and make noise too. Fluffy think he called Rezz from sound. Fluffy think that all the Rezz are funny but this Rezz different. This Rezz remind Fluffy of Fluffy.' 'Well, at least he responded with Hello too. I went for two in a row and offered my hand for him to shake. he then just stuck one of his hands out. Well, I don't think he's much of a social thing so I doubt there's any point teaching him about handshakes. I thought I may as well introduce myself to him, but at the same time, my face must have looked an absolute picture. Everytime you see a movie where a kid finds an alien, or a dinosaur, or something like that, they always point to themselves and say their name. Well, call me out of ideas but I pointed to myself and called my name out. Well, Rezz, anyway. Most humans can't even say my name. He seemed to understand, anyway. His eyes opened wide as he pointed a claw at me and repeated my name. Now all he needed was a name of his own.' 'Rezz point at Fluffy. Fluffy not called Fluffy yet. Fluffy not called anything yet. Fluffy point at Fluffy and Rezz not say anything. Then more food come in and another Rezz. Other Rezz say Fluffy. Fluffy eat food. Rezz eat other Rezz. Fluffy must be called Fluffy.' 'And what happened next was just one of those odd situations. The thing pointed to itself, expecting a name. I was at a loss for words. I'd just met... something... and it begged a name of me. The situation went from bad to worse when we heard two sets of footsteps. One of them belonged to a small french poodle, who must have wandered in after smelling the meat that Fluffy had unwrapped, releasing the aroma through the open window. The dog was pink and looked more like candy floss. It was shaved in certain areas too and looked so tasteless that I felt like putting it out of it's misery. Then a voice came... and you can guess what this voice was calling for. Before anything else could happen, the creature pounced on this poodle and his tongue shot out like a javelin into the middle of the head. I knew this wouldn't sit well with the owner. And it didn't. A fat, aged woman in a disgusting dress that matched the dog's coat in colour waddled in, then saw her beloved pet laying in the floor being drained of it's blood by.... whatever it was. It was lucky that I had the urge to silence her before her scream could bring any unwanted attention, although the initial shock of seeing her dog was quite a tasty mouthful. After the two of us had eaten together, I picked up the poodle's collar from it's broken neck and looked at the name tag. I would have put it on the creature, but... that's just not my style.' 'Rezz point at Fluffy and call him Fluffy. So Fluffy is Fluffy. Rezz take Fluffy with him to big rock. Rezz have strange thing and Rezz say Fluffy is a Chupacabra. Fluffy think Rezz is silly. Rezz teaching Fluffy to speak and learn new words. Fluffy ate something that looked like food skin, but it taste bad. Fluffy think Rezz angry because he wanted to eat instead. Fluffy not told Rezz he ate it yet. Fluffy not going to tell.' 'Well, I managed to smuggle the little thing through customs. It was hell keeping him quiet on the plane. Once we got back to Akihabara, I decided to do a little research. Based on as much information as I could scrape together, he sounds like the mythical Chupacabra - a legendary bloodsucking animal that inhabits Puerto Rico and attacks livestock. Aside from the usual sort of people who take The X-Files a little too seriously, I found many pictures that looked absolutely nothing like him. Including some of his corpse. It's amazing, the lengths some people will go to just to get their 5 minutes of fame. Although Fluffy did jump up and down at one particular picture that resembled him the most. I spent the next few days trying to get him to talk English. He's still at the level of a child, but aside from his limited vocabulary, he's extremely intelligent. The only problem is that in my excitement, I seem to have misplaced my novel that I was working on, Legacy Of Hessain. I'll kick myself when I actually find the damn thing.'
