Things To Do In Denver When Your Dead, More Like Things To Do In Las Vegas When Your Not A Champion!

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08 - 10 - 02 The Munny Maker, a move in the arsenal of Gaz 'The Jester' Green but it has been commonly known as Shattered Dreams and coincidently that is feeling within the Pranks With Attitude camp. The move itself is a kick to the groin, a low blow, again something both Nerezza and Gaz Green can relate to as politics dictated the outcome of their Championship bout at Red October when the team dropped the ball to Playaz Inc. 'Pull your self together!' hissed the distinctive voice of Noscosta Ireul Nerezza, the vampire harshening his tone towards its recipient. The scene eventually opened on the Las Vegas apartment, the dwelling if you would, related to former Tag Team Champions Pranks With Attitude. The atmosphere had an awe of disappointment as the camera basked in the beautiful furnishings on display.... 'Gaz, we aren't to blame, again we were robbed....' roared the Living Nightmare again, yet to be unveiled. Sitting slouched into a reclined leather chair was the shell of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Still adorning his wrestling tights, smeared in sweat and blood from the contest sanctioned at Red October he raises his arm, brandishing a bottle of whiskey. Wetting his whiskers with the bottles content he remains dazed, his baby-like face now camouflaged in thick bristled brown hair, the Jester's natural colour. Suddenly an empty bottle hurtled across the room on a collision course with the cranium of the PCW comic, ducking for safety the glass shattered against the wall, fragments showering the crimson carpet. 'WHAT THE F....' bellowed the Lord Of Laughter, his eyes swaying back and forth. 'Now I have your attention, I guess its time to lay my cards on the table?' said Nerezza drawing a glance of confusion from his comrade. 'This past Sunday was a minor set-back, none of us were to know that Playaz Inc would have the backing of Commissioner Ellie and that The Uprising had signed the loser Duzza!' continued the Wrestle Tattler behemoth. 'The Yank Duzza went to town on a great white pony, Stuck his hand up its arse, And pulled out Mark Makonie!' sang the former NTICW comedian, breaking into a mass of hysterics as the alcohols after-effects took their strangle hold. Laughing and giggling like a small child, Gaz Green couldn't imagine the hole he had dug and with every minute that strolls by that hole was growing deeper. Without warning the monotone blare of the telephone rang 'Ring - Ring' momentarily saving the intoxicated Jester as the titanic Nerezza grasped the receiver firmly with an open palm talon. 'This had better be good....' groaned the goliath. The caller kept the line busy as Nerezza listened on, nodding his head with agreement before replying and concluding the conversation. 'Right - Thanks and good-bye!!!' With each minute that past Nerezza's patience wore a little thinner as he tried desperately in salvaging his partner until the PCW vampire erupted with an explosion. 'LISTEN UP - THAT WAS....' 'Bobby Weathermonger thanking us for the Ellie coupon of lust!' interrupted the Prince Of Pranks, his eyes glazed over and concluding his sentence with a hiccup. 'Err-... Arr-... So that's what happened to that coupon, not saying I was going to use it... I mean, it entitled you to a free night of lust from Eleanor Kannon-Hall and I think she's done enough screwing with regards to Pranks With Attitude!' retorted the Angel Of Fear momentarily sidetracked before reverting back to the initial conversation. 'Anyway, that was the office and not BBC 2's comedy show before your smart mouth tries to distract me. Christmas has came early and we haven't even have to thank a paedophile with a white beard, more like a egotistical, self centred, manipulative CEO named Xavier Kannon for giving us a title shot during this weeks Friday Night Fusion!!!' 'Sorry to burst your bubble squire but you kinder forgot to mention the fact that he's a washed up former Heavyweight Champion....' injected the Conniving Comic. 'CLYDE - CLYDE - CLYDE - CLYDE!' cried the Trickster Team in unison, mocking the scientologist as a cloak of their own disappointment and adding a little moral to the P.W.A camp. The jovial Jester had resurfaced, trying to sober up; he is still in the dark about the whole contest involving Playaz Inc. The bout may not be Main Event, but it was indeed a return match with the present Champions and most importantly it was for the ownership of the gold, which rightfully belongs to the Asylum collective. 'Hmm-... Jester... One more thing. The match is a Steel Cage, which means brutality at its finest, no rules or repercussions and a chance to feast from the beaten bodies of Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo!' grinned the Spawn Of Evil as he brandished his frightening canines that oozed cobias amounts of saliva, which he licked away with his forked tongue. 'EEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWZZZZZZZZIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEESSSSSS' proclaimed the King Of Komedy as he watched the events unfold in his mind. 'I mean Playaz Inc. are a nasty piece of work. You have a convention of idiots with Statuz Quo, a graduate of the 'Evan Levine School Of Dyslexia' whilst his run-in buddy, the grafted Dante's Peak and Samuel L. Jackson....' continued the Emerald Menace standing erect, his balance a little wayward. 'Huh?!? Explain the Dante Jackson thing 'cause I'm sure Dante's Peak was a movie about a volcano?' questioned the towering vampire, clad in a draping trench coat and your typical Goth's wardrobe of blacks bomber boots and dark clothing. 'Well, he's a little hot headed and as frightening as Samuel L. Jackson swinging a light saber!' answered the Instigator Of Injury Gaz Green rolling his eyes. 'RIGHT....' responded his colleague. Trying to fathom the B/S of his co-worker Nerezza hits the Jester with an idea of his own. 'Gaz I have booked an appointment with a Psychiatrist. You took a nasty chair shot from the loser Duzza over in the land of the rising sun and I'm concerned about your state of mind!' informed the Creature Of The Night with a solemn stare. 'Aye - Sounds fun~!' screamed the devious Gaz Green in glee. The room went into a dense silence as Gaz slouched back into the reclined chair, grasping a remote in which he turned on the television set that filtered over the sound of nothingness. Nerezza meanwhile paced frantically his hands hidden in the pockets of his attire, when suddenly he removed a small piece of paper, browsing over the small print; eyes swaying to the sentences bold lettering. 'Err... Arr-... Must dash... Things to do, people to eat...' said the bloodsucker vanishing through the glass panel doors of the balcony and into the nights sky with haste. 'Was that our PCW complementary coupon for Ellie's Night Of Lust?' murmured the Jester looking a little dumbfounded, a star struck expression glazed over his face. 'Well if I'm visiting a shrink I'd best shave...' concluded the former Tag Champion as he rubbed his bristled cheeks, eyes fixated on the open balcony as the gentle breeze seeped in. - Scene suddenly closing -
