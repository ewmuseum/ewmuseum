Hospitals make the money world go round....

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 19, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within what seems to be a hospital room. The room walls are painted a pure white color. In the room, there is a nurse tending to someone on the hospital bed, which is colored in a silver lining of metal and has yellow sheets on it. The nurse moves out of the way and you see Semore Smoke lying in the bed, looking up at the TV screen as he watches the highlights on a PCW news show on the weekend. Currently rolling on the TV is the scenes of Smoke being attacked by Adam Stryker from behind when he was in the ring on Fusion in a heated conversation with AJ Trinidad. Stryker takes him down hard with a chair to the skull and then continues the assault with the chair by hitting Smoke in his already surgically repaired back and ribs. The scene then cuts to another scene of Smoke being loaded up onto a stretcher and into an Ambulance in the parking lot. Smoke cuts the TV off by hitting the off button for it, which is on the bed control panel right beside him. Smoke turns to face the nurse who is bringing in his food now… Semore Smoke: Hey, nurse. Mind if I ask you when the doctor will have my X-Rays analyzed and everything so I can see how bad of shape my back is in? {{{{The nurse pulls the food cart right beside Smoke’s bed and puts the tray in front of Smoke with the function that allows the top part of the cart to come off the top of it and move out so the tray can be set over the bed. Smoke sits up slowly in the bed, holding his lower back as he does sit up. He grabs a fork and begins to eat the Spaghetti that is on the tray, as soon as he puts the fork in his mouth, he spits the food back out and pushes the tray away…}}}} Semore Smoke: Nasty ass hospital food! So, did you hear what I asked? {{{{The nurse turns to Smoke and nods her head as to say “yes”…}}}} Nurse: Yes, I heard you Mr. Smoke. The doctor is currently still analyzing the X-Rays, I’ll be sure to come in here and tell you when he is done and he’s got the information for you. {{{{As soon as she finishes her sentence, the doctor walks in, in his white over coat and with a stethoscope around his neck. The doctor has in his hand a manila envelope and the doctor looks over at Smoke with a smile, which makes Smoke smile as well…}}}} Semore Smoke: Speak of the Devil and look who arrives. Dr. Andrews, have you got the results finally? {{{{The doctor nods his head and cuts on a wall viewer for the X-Rays that’s in the room and reaches over for the light switch in the room and cuts off the lights. He opens up the manila envelope and pulls out a few green sheets that are the X-Rays. He puts the first X-Ray on the viewer, then the second right beside it, and the third and last beside that one. There are circles on the pictures pointing out several different things in the picture and the doctor points at the first one on the left with the pin in his hand…}}}} Dr. Andrews: I’m not gonna leave you in suspense with the results, your back is just fine, it’ll be soar for a week or so, but other then that, you’ll be fine. And here’s why your back is fine. This right here, looks like a contusion of one of the rib, but it’s actually the fused vertebrae, that one vertebrae right there saved your back and possibly your life. The last doctor who did that surgery on your back was a genius. He placed the vertebrae in a position to where it would absorb all punishment to your back! It’s quite the technique that saved you from all possible problems with your back. {{{{Smoke looks shocked that his back could practically be indestructible. He then looks at the doctor with a look of curiosity on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: So all and all, I can be out of here tomorrow and wrestle tomorrow night? {{{{The doctor nods with a smile on his face…}}}} Dr. Andrews: Yes sir, that’s exactly what can happen. Your back will be soar, but you’ll be ok from action tomorrow, I’ll prescribe you some painkillers for you to take tomorrow morning and they should help you from all pain during the day. You’ll be fully recovered within a week though, so that’s all. {{{{Smoke’s grin on his face becomes even wider as he thinks of the possibilities of his return to the ring on Sunday night. But he tones down his smile a bit when he feels his ribs…}}}} Semore Smoke: Doctor… what about my ribs, they’re a bit soar as well. {{{{The doctor nods his head…}}}} Dr. Andrews: Yes, your ribs, well, that’s a different story all together, they are however injured a bit, but only bruised. But bruised ribs can hurt like hell, I know that myself from experience, so that’s why I’m gonna wrap them up for you tonight and you keep them wrapped up for a week, the pain medication that you’ll be taking for your back will also dull the pain in your ribs extensively and they should be healed within two weeks at the most. So you’re not really hurt at all. {{{{Smoke chuckles as he sits back in his bed again…}}}} Semore Smoke: Then AJ Trinidad and Adam Stryker’s attempt to put me out of action failed, ha! This is great, an indestructible back, I’m almost invincible. Dr. Andrews: Yes, almost invincible, you can still break your back if you take a huge fall, but the simple chair to the back will not phase it much, just make it a bit soar. Even an up to fifteen foot fall onto a ladder won’t hurt it, but if you fall more then twenty feet, whether you land on a bed or whatever, your back will suffer from the G-Force effects. {{{{Smoke nods his head as the doctor cuts the lights back on and begins to take the X-Ray sheets off the viewer…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, no sick bumps then, I’ve gotcha. Well, I guess I’d like to be alone now to contemplate tomorrow. Pay Per Views always get to me, and I like keeping up an undefeated singles record at Pay Per Views.… In fact, now that I think of it, the only matches I’ve lost Pay Per Views are impossible clusterfucks anyways, hopefully I can keep that streak alive tomorrow night. {{{{The doctor puts the sheets back into the manila envelope and sets the envelope at the foot of Smoke’s bed and leaves the room, as he leaves he tells Smoke…}}}} Dr. Andrews: Yes, well, good luck tomorrow night, I wish all the people that visit here the best of luck in their jobs, no matter how unique they are. {{{{The nurse follows the doctor out the door and they close the door behind them. Smoke looks up at the ceiling, and thinks about tomorrow, but in the serene silence of the room, and the concentration of the times. Smoke slips into sleep, a sleep that is the ultimate of relaxation, one in which borderlines the line of life and death…}}}}
