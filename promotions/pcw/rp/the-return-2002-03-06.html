The Return.

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/6/02 The city is at war. A rough shove sends a man in a suit hand against a dirty concrete wall. “Give me your money and valuables or be killed and I take them from you!” The suit hurriedly passes over his wallet and jewelry, blubbering for mercy. Satisfied, the thief swings a backhand and pistol whips the suits, who crumbles into the filthy floor of the alleyway. The vandal grins and turns to make his escape… but comes face to face with the twin barrels of a sawed-off shotgun! A sinister voice from a man in a mask… “Hand over the goods, bro. Finders keepers.” Lawlessness runs rampant across the streets of San Jose, where ordinary citizens walk a dangerous line between impoverished survival and the cold embrace of death. A round table of the most evil criminal minds of all time. Lex Luthor stands at the head and holds aloft a snifter of fine imported brandy. Luthor: “Gentlemen! To evil!” A city no longer lingers on the edge of insanity, but fully cast into the fiery embrace of Hell. The streets are no longer alive with citizens, but teem with villainous scum so malevolent, so dastardly, it goes beyond the scope of human recognition. And where are the police in these dark times? The police station facade is riddled with holes. Inside, tables are overturned and officers crouch behind them, shotguns in hand. Sweat glistens on their foreheads. “GET DOWN!!” Machine gun fire rips through the station as maniacal laughter emanates from the outside. The cops just stare vacantly towards the front, numb to the scene. A rather rotund officer belly-crawls to a table and utters words that hang in the air like the sword of Damocles… “We’re out of doughnuts!” Years of reliance on outside forces of justice have caused their ranks to be quickly scattered to the four winds. Once the presence of good was lost in San Jose, the criminal underworld quickly converged to gain a piece of the action, like so many cockroaches drawn to a fetid carcass. An armored truck rumbles down the street, two nervous security guards at its wheel. Suddenly, the steel walls a foot thick begin vibrating noisily. The truck engine continues to moan, but the vehicle stops moving. Panicked, the guards instantly bail just as the car begins flying up into the sky! Overhead, the grating laughter of Magneto plays across the rooftops. With a simple motion, the reinforced steel rips apart like so much wet paper, the sacks of cash spilling out into a nebulous cloud which slowly moves it’s way towards Magneto… until a screaming rush and a plume of smoke… and it’s gone! Magneto roars in rage! Magneto: “DAMN YOU HOBGOBLIN!!” The cackling green man flies off, spewing a trail of smoke from his ride… which suddenly explodes! Hobgoblin screams as he plummets to the Earth! The shrieking and familiar laughter of the Joker rises over the streets. Joker: “Hubba hubba hubba money money money! Who do you love!? Just like I drew it up on the chalkboard! HAHAHAHAHAHA!!” The sacks of loot fall into his brightly colored ice cream wagon and jets off, cackling. But like with all vermin… what happens when the lights turn on? “WHAT IS THIS?!” “Uh sir?” “I REQUIRE MY ICE CREAM IMMEDIATLEY FOR DOOM HAS COMMANDED IT!!” The poor waiter cowers in fealty. “Y-y-y-y-es sir! B-b-b-ut you just arrived…” Dr. Doom: “Such insolence!! Another such outburst shall surely cost you your pitiful lives!” Waiter: (weeping in fear) “Yes sir! It is a pleasure to serve you, Lord! I shall bring forth your…” Dr. Doom: “DOOM commands you to utilize the magical intonations!” Waiter: “P-p-p-p-lease may I serve your…” Doom: “Fudge Brownie Sundae with extra sprinkles.” Waiter: (gulp!) “May I serve your Fudge brownie sundae with extra sprinkles, O Lord!” Doom: (waives an imperious hand) “You may serve us. Go now!” The waiter rushes off, thankful to be alive, leaving the imposing Dr. Doom to casually seat himself at the outdoor café table. Throwing his green cape behind him, he lounges back, watching a carjacking out in the street. The normally stoic Doom allows himself a small smile at the scene and takes in a deep breath of the salty California air, drenched with the screams of the innocents. Ah, paradise. Suddenly, a ninja, looking backwards, sprints by the café. While normally in san Jose, this has become a rather ordinary sight, the observant Doom noticed something odd in the ninja’s eyes. Real fear. A jack-booted thug in full Nazi regalia also runs screaming by. Dr. Doom tenses up in his seat, unsure of what is going on. Craning his neck down towards the street, he looks from where the running men came from. A shocked Doom sees a street filled with pandemonium! Villains of all shapes and sizes are now running down the street like rats leaving a ship. Dr. Doom raises a manacled hand and rubs his metallic chin… Waiter: “Your fudge brownie sundae, your Lordship.” Dr. Doom: (exploding in anger) “DO NOT INTERRUPT OUR REVERIE!! IMPUDENT FOOL!!” The waiter dives under a table for cover, but Doom has already forgotten him, as the steady stream of running baddies has reached the sidewalk café. Fire burns in Doom’s eyes as he stands, surveying the improbable scene as the scum literally push each other aside in a desperate race to… to what? Doom reaches into the crowd and grabs a spandexed villain, green in color with question marks adorning the outfit. With fantastic strength, he lifts the man by the collar into the air. The Riddler still pumps his legs, trying to run away through the air. Doom: “What is the meaning of this?! Tell us at once or face the wrath of DOOM! Can this be another riot, such as in 96?” The Riddler: (breathless) “What comes with the force of the ancients, blessed by the forgotten gods of a lost civilization with innocent yet deadly intent?! Lemme go! PLEASE!!” Doom: “BAH! You’re riddles bore me!” With a powerful swing, Doom tosses the Riddler aside, who hits the ground running and continues flailing his arms wildly in his spastic attempt to escape. Doom again reaches into the crowd and grabs a automaton. Doom: “You! You are one of the minions of Master Computer! You have no knowledge fear, yet you run along with this horde of cowardly henchmen! What say you!?” Master Computer Robot: “Affirmative. Master Computer knows no fear. However, Master Computer is programmed for self-preservation. Master Computer has already relocated to an off-shore out of country site. All minions are instructed to leave these United States immediately or face self-destruction.” With that, boosters fire from the back of the humanoid robot, forcing Doom to release or be dragged into the upper stratosphere, where the automaton now speeds away for safety. The crowd in the street has now grown more sparse, the lion’s share of the evil men already gone. Doom crosses his arm in confusion and slowly sinks back into his seat. Turning his back to the street, he grabs his spoon and prepares to dine. Doom: “I, Dr. Doom, shall fear no such enemy!” Slowly, a rumble can be felt. The sound of a large vehicle sounds from down the street. Doom ignores it, raising a spoonful of ice cream and brownie to his mask-mouth opening. But a custom car horn blares out the first bar of the Olympic Theme, causing Doom’s eyes to go wide behind his horrid metal visage. The careful observer could even notice sweat forming around the eyes… Doom slowly turns back to the street, where a Winnebago has passed. Doom breathes a sigh of relief… until the Bago suddenly stops. A beeping indicates it’s backing up. Doom gulps hard and looks at the license plate of the backing Winnebago: HIKEEBA. We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he rolls down the passenger side window and grins out towards the gaping super-villain. Steele rests a huge arm on the window frame and waves towards the now shaking Dr. Doom. Big Alan Steele: (booming voice) “Ha ha ha! Greetings, Lord Doom! Tis I, Big Alan Steele, once again within my chosen home of San Jose! You and all others shall be pleased to note that I again have taken up permanent residence here, and shall require MUCH training to knock the proverbial rust from my ample carriage! I see you and the others have made San Jose a veritable din of evil in my absence… well done!! It seem you should provide me with the necessary training I so desire to battle the nefarious villainy of PCW, starting with the wicked Chef and his various curs! Hi-Keeba!!” Inside the cab of the Bago, trusted manager Sue Saksnussum at the wheel, grows impatient. Sue: “Dammit Al, stop mingling with the help! We’ve got training to do!” BAS: (to no one in particular) “Indeed!! Much time must be made up for! Let us begin at once!! Hi-Keeba!” Sue guns the Winnebago’s engine and rumbles off down the street. Those lovely and irrepressible dance troupe members, the Graces, all wave at Doom through the back window, blowing kisses. Panicked eyes behind the mask of Doom dart to and fro as the Bago disappears into the horizon. He starts to run, but stops, looks at his ice cream, turns to run, stops, etc. Finally, he sits and quickly shovels the frozen treat into his maw, getting ice cream all over his mask. Finished in seconds, he stands and prepares to run… Doom: “ARRRRRGGGGHHHHH!!!!!” Clutching at his head, Doom falls to his knees, a victim of brain freeze. The warrior comes…AGAIN!
