Baked Beans. The weapon of champions!

Date: not entered When we last left off Flaymin had just discovered the injustice served to him in PCW Magazine.His reign as co-longest TV Champ had been denied! What will Flaymin do to get even with Tyler Nelson?? Wait and see.... [Scene opens in uptown New Jersey on a nice, sunny day. Cruising through the smog filled, hobo-infested, crap hole of a city is Flaymin Aymin and his Porn Queen assistant, Jenna Jameson. Flaymin drives his crappy 1972 Datsun 180B through the streets, as the car constantly stops and starts along the way due to exhaust problems among other things. Smoke begins to puff out of the car and loud ‘boom’ sounds continually re-occur] Jenna: Why did we have to come here in this bomb? Flaymin: This is New Jersey.If you bring a new car to this joint, it’ll be ripped apart within five minutes flat. Jenna: I don’t think there are that many thieves on the streets. Flaymin: Woman, I have hard-proof evidence. Jenna: Such as? Flaymin: C.O.P.S [Jenna shakes her head in irritation. Meanwhile, Flaymin pulls the car over to the kerb] Flaymin: Here, hold the steering wheel while I go and find a rock or something to stop the car from rolling backwards. Jenna: Where are the brakes? Flaymin: Pfff.Brakes! That’s a good one. [Flaymin jumps out of the car and looks for some solid objects.Upon finding two garbage cans, he places one behind each rear wheel of the car] Flaymin: That should do it. [Jenna gets out of the car and walks over to Flaymin] Jenna: So is this PCW Towers? Flaymin: Sure is.Lets go pay Mr Nelson a visit. [Flaymin and Jenna walk into a high-rise building and go up the elevator to the 8th floor. When they exit the elevator, Flaymin gazes at the surroundings in interest] Flaymin: Hey, they’ve refurbished the joint.It looks totally different. Jenna: It was about time that cheap bastard renovated the building. [Flaymin and Jenna walk through the hallway until they arrive at the office of Tyler Nelson, which is apparently being heavily guarded by six armed bodyguards. Flaymin tries to casually walk past the bodyguards, but is brought to an abrupt halt when one of the guards grabs Flaymin by the collar and pushes him back] Flaymin: Hey, hey, easy on the shirt.K Mart doesn’t sell these things cheap, you know! Guard: State your official business here. Flaymin: I need to speak with the boss. Guard: Do you have a scheduled appointment? Flaymin: What for?He’s probably biting off his toenails. Guard: He may do what he wishes.You have no right to question his antics. [Bemused, Flaymin blankly stares back at the guard] Flaymin: Are you gonna get out of my way or do I have to start a brouhaha? [Upon hearing Flaymin’s threat, all six bodyguards surround Flaymin, staring down at him furiously. Flaymin’s eyes boggle open and he instantly retreats backwards and begins to walk away] Flaymin: Yeah you’re lucky!Lucky…. That I have to go to the toilet… and stuff….. yeah!I’ll be back when I finish my business! Jenna: What happened? Flaymin: It’s a no-go zone. Jenna: Why didn’t you fight through them? Flaymin: You want me to fight through six bodyguards??? Jenna: I’ve done it before. Flaymin: NO, you’ve f*cked 6 men, not fought them. Jenna: Oh yeah… silly me! [Flaymin and Jenna turn back and head for the elevator.They get into the elevator and emerge a moment later on the ground floor of the building] Jenna: So what do we do now? Flaymin: Protest, I guess. [Flaymin and Jenna walk out of the building and start pointing up to Tyler Nelson’s office up on the 8th floor. Flaymin begins to yell at the top of his voice] Flaymin: Come out, you pimple-assed little monkey!! [The window in Tyler’s office remains shut] Flaymin: Hey you little bitch; we’ve got business to discuss!!! [Still no reaction from the office of Tyler] Jenna: This isn’t going to work. What if we got cars to honk their horns? Flaymin: And how would we do that? Jenna: I could parade around topless. Flaymin: Nah, you’ll catch a cold. [Jenna sits on a nearby bench and slumps backwards] Jenna: We came all the way to this shit-hole for nothing. [In the corner of his eye, Flaymin spots a sleeping homeless man. Quietly he creeps over to the hobo and begins to rifle through his personal belongings] Jenna: Are you stealing from the homeless again!?!?! Flaymin: Shhhh!!You’ll wake him up. [Out of the hobo’s personal bag, Flaymin pulls out a few cans of baked beans. With the cans in his hands, he walks back over to Jenna] Flaymin: Baked Beans…. Breakfast of Champions!! Jenna: Let me guess, you’re going to fart your way to the top? Flaymin: Nope.I’m gonna hurl these cans at Tyler’s window. [Flaymin puts the cans of baked beans on the floor.He starts rolling his arms forwards and backwards (Every athlete needs to warm up!)] Flaymin: Alright, hand me a can. [Jenna picks up a can of beans and hands it to Flaymin. Flaymin kisses the top of the can and then aims it directly at the window of Tyler Nelson] Flaymin: This’ll teach that punk! [Flaymin hurls the can high into the air….. closer….. and closer to Tyler’s window…..Unfortunately it falls short by around a metre and splatters on the wall beneath Tyler’s window] Flaymin: Damn!! Jenna: Let me have a go. Flaymin: Um…. No.I’m trying to hit the window…. Not the sidewalk. Jenna: I can throw! Flaymin: You can swallow too, but that isn’t going to help either. [Flaymin picks up another can of baked beans.Once again he kisses the can and then aims it at the window of Tyler Nelson’s office. He launches the can into the air…. Up….. up…. Aaaaaand….. BANG!!!!! The can hits the window directly, but doesn’t smash it.Instead it just rebounds off the window and comes crashing down to the ground splattering all over the sidewalk] Flaymin: What the hell? Jenna: Ha!You hit the sidewalk too. Flaymin: Shut up woman and hand me another can. Jenna: I think the window is bullet proof. Flaymin: Jenna, my dear slutty assistant.Bullet Proof is one thing…. Baked Beans proof is another thing. [Jenna shrugs her shoulders and hands Flaymin another can of baked beans. Flaymin kisses the can and then quietly begins to chant to himself] Flaymin: That’s one for the money…Two for the show….Three to get ready…Four to hit Tyler’s window! [Flaymin throws the can with all his might.The cans sails high into the air, directly towards Tyler’s window. The can lands squarely on Tyler’s window, causing it to shatter into a thousand pieces] Flaymin: Wooooo!!!Did you see that!!! [Flaymin looks up at the window and begins to yell once again] Flaymin: Get out here you little son of a female dog!! Jenna: Bitch. Flaymin: Yeah, that too!! [Suddenly a figure sticks his head out of the broken window and looks down at Flaymin] Jenna: Is that him? Flaymin: I don’t know, it’s too high up for me to tell. [The figure sticking his head out the window begins to point down at Flaymin] Flaymin: He’s pointing at me! [Flaymin continues to stare up at the broken window and the mysterious figure.Suddenly, the main doors of the building burst open and the six bodyguards that were guarding the office, run out of the building, charging towards Flaymin. Flaymin watches in shock as the six burly men stampede towards him and Jenna] Flaymin: Uh oh… Jenna: What should we do? Flaymin: RUN, woman, RUN!!!!! [Flaymin begins to dash down the street, but realises a second later that Jenna isn’t behind him. Instead, she’s back at the scene of the crime collecting the cans of baked beans] Flaymin: Forget about the freaking cans!!!!! Jenna: We could use these!! Flaymin: JUST RUN DAMMIT!!!! [Jenna drops the cans and runs towards Flaymin, narrowly escaping the heated chase by the mysterious bodyguards] Jenna: Where did we park the car??? Flaymin: Forget about that piece of junk, I can crawl faster than the damn thing. [Flaymin and Jenna continue to run down the street with the bodyguards in hot pursuit. They approach a busy road and dodge through the traffic, safely making it to the other side. The bodyguards remain on the opposite side of the road and decide not to hurdle through the thick traffic. They stare at Flaymin and Jenna before walking back to the building. Meanwhile, having realised the chase is over, Flaymin and Jenna lean back against the wall to get their breath back. Jenna looks up at the building directly in front of her and begins to rub her eyes] Flaymin: What’s the matter? Jenna: The building in front of us.It’s PCW Towers. [Flaymin looks up at the building and raises an eyebrow] Flaymin: Hold on a sec…Then whose window were we throwing baked beans at? Jenna: Beats the hell out of me. Flaymin: Whoops, I guess we went into the wrong building after all…… [Scene fades to black]
