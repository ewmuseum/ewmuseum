'Is The End Nigh?'

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 - 11 - 02 'CRASH~!' We bare witness to Gaz 'The Jester' Green implanting the forehead of Heavy Metal Harry into the canvas with authority and looking to seal his place in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament's Semi-Final booth. In desperation the Jester crawled with exhaustion towards his incapacitated opponent and slung over his limp arm in an attempted cover as the commentary crew argued over the origin of his finisher, 'The Last Laugh'. 'THE DROP OUT FROM GREEN! THAT PRANKSTER STOLE KANNON'S MOVE!' roared former Primetime performer Jeff Marx. 'No Jeff, that's the Last Laugh! The moves are the same!' argued his commentary partner Dave. 'But Kannon was around first!' retaliated Jeff, trying to rally in favour of his beloved Front Office and more importantly gain some browning points from one CEO Xavier Kannon. Meanwhile back in the ring the referee beats his open palm against the canvas, making the count in favour of Gaz Green. 1-.... 2-.... Suddenly, clawing back from staring defeat in the eye, Heavy Metal Harry raised his shoulder. The official signaled a mere two count and this spurned a rage-fuelled disagreement from the Emerald Menace as he argued his case, allowing the devious tactics of his opponent to seep through and snatch victory from the jaws of defeat. Grasping the tights of the PCW Tag Team Champion, Heavy Metal Harry rolled up his opponent into a schoolboy, and the rest as they say is history, the inevitable three count followed by the victors arm being raised. The victory however was short lived as a brawl mounted in the ring with the combatants of the fatal four-way elimination match giving the audience a taster of what can be expected Sunday at Alliances2K2. The long term rivalry between Playaz Inc and Pranks With Attitude further escaladed whilst Front Office representatives Tim Walden and Brandi made the a physical acquaintance of Teresa Tomas and The H.I.T. With five teams, ten individuals and what looks to be a Harlem turf war in process, PCW officials race in to stop the fight. After several minutes of physical negotiations, the squared circle desolated with only the beaten collective of Pranks With Attitude remaining, trying desperately to fathom the events that have unfolded. 'Tell me I never just lost to Heavy Metal Harry?' said Gaz 'The Jester' Green shaking his head in disbelief. Taking the bout and more importantly the outcome badly, Gaz looked towards Noscosto Ireul Nerezza for comfort as the camera picked up on his watery eyes, 'Was the Jester going to cry?' 'No-...' murmured the behemoth vampire with consolation. 'You lost to a greasy haired, metal moshing hippy called Heavy Metal Harry!' concluded his supposed supportive shoulder with a smirk. The duo ascended the ramp way united, still PCW Tag Team Champions, nothing would change that, well nothing until the Pay-Per-View when they run the gauntlet. As they walked what could be regarded as their personal 'Green Mile' Gaz peered into the crowd who with jovial faces mocked and belittled him with laughter and artistic expression. Banners aligning the ramp way read, 'Gaz Green loses to Heavy Metal Harry - Who next, BladeD?' 'Former PCW Tag Team Champions P.W.A!!!' 'NEREZZA SEEKS TAG PARTNER (Gaz Green need not apply!)' Adding insult to injury the crowd rally a 'You Suck!' chant as the duo hit the apex of the ramp way, vanishing through the curtains of the Jumbotron and into the backstage area. Breathing a sigh of relief Gaz shrugs off the bout as being an upset and heads for his locker-room when suddenly a congregation of the roster formed before him. Standing star struck and dumbfounded the masses gathered, everybody, no matter of their seeding within the company they stood screaming insults and abuse. 'No, this can't be happening?!?' 'WHY ME!?!' 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' roared the Lord Of Laughter with a thunderous bellow, exerting his lungs capacity. Suddenly the scene changed within the familiar confines of the Jester's Las Vegas apartment. From the living quarters we have become accustomed to as a base of operations, we open in the bedroom of the gold laden Jester as he sits up in bed. Mouth ajar, pale in complexion and sweating from the forehead profusely it looks as though the whole episode had been a dream, or quite possibly a premonition to future events. Wiping the sweat from his brow and rubbing his eyes in an attempt to beat his tiresome feeling, Gaz threw back the blankets and walked barefooted across his laminate flooring. 'Arr-... Err-... Cold?!?' murmured the Velvet Trickster. Re-enacting a tribal initiation as he hopped and skipped about, it looked as though he were walking across hot ashes and not cold flooring but after several hilarious minutes of battling his interior elements he finally made it to his personal gym equipment. 'That dream felt almost real-....' 'Was it the foretelling of the future-....' 'Was it a disguised message that, Pranks With Attitude will lose the PCW Tag Team Championships-....' These question niggled the Jester, what if it were true, what if he is going to lose his pride and joy at the Pay-Per-View (again). Gaz could only cast his mind towards his robbery at Red October, almost four weeks to the day; P.W.A lost the Championships to evil incarnate, no not Xavier Kannon but Playaz Inc. 'I CAN'T ALLOW THIS TO HAPPEN!!!!' hissed the once jovial Jester, grinding his teeth and dropping under his lifting bar, Gaz pumped iron with authority. In his dimly lit room he continued to lift the cast iron bar as he showcased his Cruiserweight physique and like an animal possessed he tore his muscles, testing their boundaries, challenging himself to the point of injury. 1 2 3 4 'I WILL BE VICTORIOUS AT ALLIANCES! I WILL BE VICTORIOUS AT ALLIANCES-....' groaned the Prince Of Pranks before an untimely interruption from the distinctive voice of Nerezza who sat in the shadows, camouflaged, his eyes seeped through the darkness. Stepping into the light, pushing aside his long flowing trench coat, Nerezza made his presence known, startling his incoherent partner who almost dropped the weight across his chest. 'Glad to hear it!' 'HUH?!?' retorted Gaz Green, a little taken back. 'I'm glad to hear that we shall prevail at the Pay-Per-View, confidence is a virtue my human friend!' hissed the Living Nightmare, congratulating his partners confidence. 'No-... I had a nightmare-... I lost and became the laughing stock of the locker-room-... I'm worried-... Worried that I put the Cruiserweight Championship before OUR own success and now I'm going to pay the penalty!' said Gaz Green, open and honest. 'A premonition if you would!?!' replied the Spawn Of Evil, Nerezza. Gaz simply nodded, approving his partner’s statement and displaying facial expressions of worry. Nerezza however shrugged off such nonsense and dipped his talons into his pockets, removing a pack of cards, notably a pack of tarot cards that may hold more answers than questions, setting the Jester's mind at rest. 'Do you believe?' asked the Angel Of Fear, waving the cards before Gaz Green. 'Was that your attempts at an Agent Fox Mulder impression? Hey, what have I got to lose, let's do this!' answered the PCW comic with a grin, his face returning to it's more familiar bright and perky state. The duo headed into the living quarters of the Las Vegas pad, allowing the scene to slowly draw to a close. As the screen turned black, ending another segment with the devious duo, P.W.A, Nerezza's voice filtered over the silence. 'It wasn't that bad for a first attempt at doing Fox Mulder!'
