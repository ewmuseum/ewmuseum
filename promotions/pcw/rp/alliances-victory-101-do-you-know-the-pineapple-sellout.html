Alliances Victory 101: Do You Know The Pineapple Sellout?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.21.02(#5 PPV, Team #8) Do you know the Pineapple Sellout?The Pineapple Sellout?The Pineapple Sellout. Yes, I know the Pineapple Sellout.Who sells himself like a cheap whore? Ahh…Timo, you’re back amongst your first family, buddy. You’re back in the place that spawned your talentless carcass into something of a star. Before, you were jobbing to the likes of Akiyama and Boda….then it was Meanstreak and Toomes…now it’s Frampton and Marvelous? Pathetic. “Alliances….the return of everyone’s favorite Polynesian sellout.” Harsh words, but does this man deserve such a labeling? I mean, he only turned his back on PCW, then on OSW, and now he’s rotting away in FSW. People have done worse. But you decided to come back at Alliances, Timo. The show where people prove their loyalty to one another. How very cliché of you, Bolamba. “PCW welcomes Team FSW, made up of Tyler Nelson…” Sellout teaming with sellout. Doesn’t surprise me one bit. The only difference is that we care a little bit more about the greasebag who at least got fired then we do about a fucking turncoat. Fuck what the fans think. Everyone in the locker room knows exactly what you did. You think having 56 enemies out there matters if you’ve got the ‘Greediest Player In The Game’ on your team? “Mike Marvelous…” Please. What a joke. PCW reject. OSW reject. Oh, what a surprise, another two-time failure making it big in FSW. They’ve got a stange knack for picking up the trash of others, don’t they now? Trash like you and Marvel boy. “Angello Deville…” Universal Title? Bah…when he comes into my fed and pins me, that’s when he’s the goddamn Universal champ. Ask Kannon and Clyde what they think, but I know. No one rules the roost around here except me. You think I lie? Watch me carry my joke of a team to victory, spitting in the faces of Duke, Kannon, Brandi, Waldnen, all of them. “and….the FSW World Champion….Timo Bolamba!” And the crowd cheered as they heard the name of the FSW hero. Fantastic for you, hero. You know what heroes get for their work? Look at Rento and his little retarded team. That’s how we treat you do-gooder types. Do-gooder. See: Rush Mason’s last promo. You, your teammates from last year. I spoofed them all, but I saved the venom spitting for you, Island Boy. “The Samoan Silencer.” Silence? Silence? What would you know about that? All you ever hear is the adoring fans cheering for you. Egging you on. ‘We love you, Timo!’ Fuck you. Fuck them. No difference. Silence….no, better would be the unfailing screech from you heart monitor after Alliances. “Prepare to be silenced? No, I’ll prepare for nothing but war and victory.” That’s right. Blood. Guts. I spill it all, whether you detest me or not. But I hope you have some sort of deep liking towards me. I hope you come up to me before the actual match and try to shake my hand. I’ll spit in your face. I’ll piss on everything you represent. That title too. You hear my entrance, you best run. Before I walk over Kannon’s body, pass by Clyde’s wrecked carcass, and skip away from the unconscious Void God to my victory? Know what I’ll do? I’ll come right for you. You….the only man of last year’s team competing this year. Unfortunately, you’re competing in true sellout fashion. For the other team. So don’t come to my fed and say it’s good to be back. Don’t you, the only returning Alliances winning team competitor say how good it is to be back in the fed that spawned you. Because I don’t care. You turned your back. Sell….out. Maybe I’ll spray that on your chest in spray paint after I bloody you to a bulb and stain the FSW belt crimson?…I’m not a walking cliché like you. Crucify you?…I’m just better than that. You know what I’ll really do? I’ll finish you and throw you to the dogs. In fact, I hope Deville tries to get along with you so I can end that team’s hopes. And you’ll be to blame. You’ll be all to blame, Timo. You’ll be the scapegoat. The champ in FSW, but nothing more than a chump back in PCW. PCW, OSW, FSW. So the cycle continues….what’s next? RUSH? PURE? PWA? Turn your back some more, Bolamba. But this time, someone’ll put a knife in there, right where it belongs. Bring all you’ve got champ. This Sunday, you enter the world where your pathetic games won’t work. No Mob Rule to fight. You walk into here nothing more than another victim. Except for that special allure you have, turncoat. Come on, traitor. I dare you.
