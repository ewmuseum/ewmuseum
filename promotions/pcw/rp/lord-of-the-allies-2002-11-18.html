Lord of the Allies

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 18, 2002 The mighty metal bird pitches and swoops in the foul night air, mercilessly and unceremoniously cutting through clouds like Aimz through sexual taboo. Streams of electrons rage and explode with unmitigated ferocity, the electrical storm flinging bolts of lightning in every direction with neither care or conscience. Over the brief but frequent crackling of ionisation, the only sound is from the plane&#039;s many engines, whining defiantly in their battle against gravity. The air surrounding the plane is aglow with static and charge, and far below the Atlantic Ocean opens its hungry, omniscient eyes, roiling and glossy with the anticipation of the inevitable. The scent of the beast is thick on it, and time seems to be the last pitiful defence barring utter atavism. Similar diabolic consideration is being given within the flying bus as sixty of the world&#039;s premier wrestling competitors eye one another with palpable distrust. Split into fifteen uneasy groups of four, the ring titans whisper uneasily over mahogany tables set into the expansive fiberglass floor. Suspicious eyes dart back and forth with the haunting rhythm of pendulums, and invisible and farcical rays of hate and death beam outward. An alien being with more refined vision would see enough force flying to shame even the most potent electrical storm. Men of all shapes and sizes, of all different backgrounds, ideologies, and morals, shift uneasily under the pressure of their charge... Under the pressure of that universal attraction... That irresistable lure within us all. &#039;Fuck &#039;em, Tyger,&#039; a refined voice snarls from one of the tables near the back of the plane, defying the unspoken rule of condemning only those outside your four. The speaker&#039;s vast blue eyes shift from Tyger Smells&#039;Em to the objects of his curse, the threads of his white Armani suit seeming to follow the rest of his body in stiffening in challenge. His wild blonde hair is contained and mollified by generous amounts of gel, stretching it straight back and flat against his head in a paradigm of order, but there is no such sensei for his mind... Or his tongue. &#039;We need the help of these clowns like we need to follow the language doctrine of Homeless Inc.&#039; &#039;Hey now,&#039; the angry black man sitting across from him growls, his voluminous auburn ponytail shaking with escaping rage. Eyes blue enough to shame the most startling oasis take the aggressor in and immediately dismiss the threat, flashing with a warning even the densest PCW newbie would heed. Dangling before his chest between either side of his stylish Hawaiian tee-shirt is a chain riddled with shark&#039;s teeth, a blunt reminder of his role in the wrestling ring. &#039;Funktacular and I may not be your favourite choices for teammates here, Devil, but we&#039;re not exactly thrilled with the situation ourselves. We all have to live with it, though, and persevere, to uphold the glowing name of FSW.&#039; He pauses for a moment, letting the reminder of their purpose hang over the table ominously for several seconds before continuing. &#039;I vow to do my Samoan best to co-exist with you and Tyger, but so help me...&#039; Timo&#039;s eyes glaze over and he tosses a glance over his shoulder towards the middle of the room. When his face returns, it is contorted by profoundly pitying disgust. His ordinarily rich voice is almost ethereal with it&#039;s fervor. &#039;...If you ever draw similarities between us and Homeless Inc. again, my pride will crush you before my mind can dismiss you as harmless.&#039; Devil&#039;s mouth twists into a smirk as he begins to respond, but Tyger raises an admonishing hand and cuts the air with his rumbling voice. &#039;As much as it pains me to admit it, Bologna here is right.&#039; The hand shakes furiously before him to quell protests from both Devil and Funktacular. His black suit has a deceptively slimming effect on his frame, luring opponents into a false sense of comfort that is quickly shattered as soon as he moves or speaks. &#039;There are fourteen other teams here, not one of them screwed in the fashion we are. They cooperate the way we fight. It comes natural to them. We will need to work together if we are to overcome this greatest of obstacles.&#039; &#039;Pfft,&#039; Functakular interjects intelligently, and the other three men shade their eyes in case he gets any funny ideas about flashing a smile. His plain white tee-shirt is a perfect representation of how seriously he takes these matters, let alone anything else. &#039;We don&#039;t need the help of these dumbass mobsters to defeat the likes of Ali $%#@dafi, Rent-a-Hummer, and GradeF. What possible help can these fools offer us, Timid?&#039; Timid Bologna sighs as Devil pipes in again. &#039;Put the pipe down, Spikey... Without us you don&#039;t even stand a chance against Jamie Whiny Whelp.&#039; He snickers loudly and turns his attention to Timo, lifting his voice enough to be heard by all. Most of the assembled workers get quiet, turning their heads to witness what promises to be a fight. &#039;And fuck yourself, Timid... I&#039;ll draw whatever comparisons I want. If I say you&#039;re stoned and retarded like Homeless Inc., you just take it dry like that bitch Boris Stained&#039;da&#039;Slav, you hea-&#039; &#039;WHAT?!&#039; The savage Russian roar threatens to crack the walls and bring the whole lot of them crashing down. The bearded giant upends his table as he clambers to his feet and begins stomping across the room to deal with this blasphemous accusation. Anarchy begins to take hold as people scatter out of his path, but the four members of Homeless Inc. try to steal the show. &#039;Watch yo&#039; mout&#039;, cracka!&#039; An angry match-stick look-alike shouts as he climbs to his feet. Dudza looks at his friends to make sure of their support, then parrots the most vocal of their bunch. &#039;Goddamn crackaz! Quit yer hatin&#039;!&#039; &#039;Shut up, oldin&#039;!&#039; A booming voice mocks from somewhere else in the room, and the green-suited Grass &#039;The Burning&#039; Green rises to his feet with a smirk. At his side, the vampiric beast that is Nerezzula grasps his belly and laughs at the heavens. Everywhere people are standing upright, the tension and foreboding manifesting itself in taunts and challenges. The room crawls with angry and hasty figures, like an ant-hill under the first drops of a new rain. &#039;Why don&#039;t you sit down, Boris?&#039; A jeering voice calls, its owner fondling a little blonde girl as it does. She finishes the thought for him, &#039;Yeah, like, Boris, you ugly peon! Sit down before you, like, get &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; stuck... and stuff!&#039; The grating voice rips down every PCW spine in attendance, and OSW&#039;s mighty champion throws his head back to bellow a brutal curse. &#039;Great juicy pineapples!&#039; Timo shouts, his wild eyes darting around the room before resting accusingly on Devil. &#039;Look what you&#039;ve started!&#039; The mobster only grins as he leaps up from his chair and moves into the throng of angry bodies. His FSW associates have no choice but to follow. &#039;Chill out, dudes!&#039; An esquirish voice calls out as if shouting over a wave, &#039;this is totally heinous!&#039; No one pays much attention to the objections raised by Bogus Bisexual Billy, but that doesn&#039;t mar his spirit in the least. Minor brawls jump up like bush fires all over the plane, and for one hectic moment it appears everything may end in a glorious battle. Then an overwhelming buzzing steals every other sound out of the air, and all of the lights vanish, deserting the wrestlers in complete darkness. There is a few muffled curses and grunts as people get up, and a couple of shocked cries as others take cheapshots. Then a voice comes out of the black like a beacon of things to come. &#039;Now look what you&#039;ve done,&#039; it rasps in a scolding tone, and follows itself up almost immediately with a high-pitched and scandalised voice. &#039;Leave him alone!&#039; &#039;Shut up,&#039; a third chimes in. &#039;Fuck yourself.&#039; &#039;All four of you shut up!&#039; &#039;And who the hell are you to tell them anything, hotshot?&#039; &#039;Exactly... Listen to him, he knows what he&#039;s talking about!&#039; &#039;Of course he does!&#039; &#039;I do?&#039; &#039;Who?&#039; &#039;You!&#039; &#039;Me?&#039; &#039;Fuck you!&#039; The madness continues as Shitzo argues with only himself, and the others begin to move about their business of getting to the cockpit and finding out what&#039;s going on. The sound of flint rubbing steel is heard, then several lighters cast small orbs of light - enough to see the angry faces of their bearers and nothing more. &#039;I don&#039;t have to put up with this,&#039; a voice booms as a swirling ball of fire suddenly comes to life, floating impossibly between the massive hands of the giant Celestial Apinata. There are gasps from those who have never witnessed such magics at his hands, and even from those who have but still can&#039;t get over it. With a triumphant grin he splashes the fire on the ground and a shimmering doorway opens... A portal to some other place - and perhaps some other time. The wrestlers eye eachother warily as Apinata beckons Bicyclonica through and snaps his fingers for La Fin to navigate. The blind ninja rises pompously to his feet, then tip-toes carefully and arrogantly across the floor to the portal. All of them have the air of superior begins, but it&#039;s focussed in him most of all. Which is why there is great laughter when Vile Lasagna drops one foot into La Fin&#039;s path, spilling him to the floor in a tremendous crash of pride. Every member of the Bitchos team cackles madly at this display of cunning, but the revelry is quickly cut off as Apinata points an admonishing finger at Lasagna and the man disappears from sight. There is no sound to warn of his going, no brief moment of transparency, just complete and instant banishment. Someone clears their throat uneasily as La Fin climbs to his feet and shuffles through the gateway, followed closely by the last member of their team. Apinata pauses before going through, lording his superiority over everyone else. &#039;Wait! Wait!&#039; A whiny chipmunkish voice cries out from the dark, literally sobbing from fright. &#039;I&#039;m afraid of the dark! Please! Take me with you!&#039; The young Whiny Whelp charges into the glow of Apinata&#039;s portal, and the immortal stares down at him as Pete does a cheeseburger. &#039;Come with me, child, and you shall never return,&#039; Apinata booms menacingly, but the true threat of it is lost on the dense child, and he nods eagerly before snuffling and pitching himself through headfirst. The Celestial gives his head a weary but amused shake, then stalks through the portal himself, pulling it closed behind him. &#039;What an asshole,&#039; snorts G.O.O.F., one of RUSH&#039;s contenders, then the air rips and he is gone, banished to the same place as Lasagne. For a while no one dares to say anything, all hoping silently for the lights - and the sanity - to return. &#039;Screw this,&#039; Devil&#039;s voice suddenly booms from a patch of pitch-dark, carrying through the expanse like the plague. &#039;Most of the teams are just PCW jobbers, right?&#039; There is some bickering, mainly from the jobbers, but eventually they quiet down when told and there is a consensus. &#039;So couldn&#039;t we just sort of pile their bodies up in the centre and have a fire? That&#039;d give plenty of light, and it&#039;s not like we&#039;re losing anything.&#039; A few scattered cheers go up at this suggestion, but they are quickly torn, twisted, and converted to screams as the plane, having heard enough, begins looping and swooning aggressively, nose-diving towards the water. Bodies fling around madly, crashing into each other, the walls, and the tables without prejudice. They grab at one another and posture for the top, but all hit rock-bottom as the plane smacks the unforgiving blue monster and snaps in half. Dozens of bodies drift aimlessly out from the wreckage, fewer conscious than not, pulled and tossed by stone-faced waves. The cold waters sting those unlucky enough to be aware of their position, sapping any strength they have to resist. They quickly succumb to the numbness and join their peers in unwary sleep, unsure whether they&#039;ll ever breathe the sweet salty air again. In the distance a looming shape waits patiently for their arrival, grinning in the darkness of its solitude. It is their only chance, and this it knows well, but only a fool would consider it to be their saviour. It will keep them alive a little longer, sure, but at what price? What will the desolation and darkness of that island demand in return for sustenance...? What will it rob from their souls in order that they should fight and conquer one another, even for just a brief time...? None of them know, and fewer still can begin to imagine, the trials about to be thrust upon them. The truth of their plight must manifest itself from within, yet cannot be uncovered alone. Alliances are designed, formed, and broken in the blink of a cosmic eye... Where trust was yesterday is now a boiling cauldron of hate and deceit, and trust - even within oneself - is a contradiction of itself. The law states that only one shall emerge from this darkness... But what that one possesses that is foreign to the others is unknown. The means to repel the beast. It is the answer that burns all minds, but cannot be found without knowing the proper question. It is for this question we must seek, not the answer, if we are to remain sane. ...But who says that&#039;s a priority?
