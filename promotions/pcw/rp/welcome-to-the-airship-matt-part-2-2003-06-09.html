Welcome To The Airship...Matt Part 2

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/09/03 With his mind crumbling into a thick dismay, Matt Jackson glides smoothly over the wooden floor, passing the large refined oak dresser, with the brass handles…past the suitcases, most black canvas, with the lone brown one, which are still strewn across the room, needing to be unpacked, but left for careless ventures of love and lust. He stops at the black leather recliner and sets himself down, coming to a rest…letting the light shine in from the window directly behind him. Crossing his arms over his barrel of a chest, the red trench coat, wrinkles a bit, but still covers the black body suit below. He leans the seat back and kicks his black combat boots over the lip and affixes his jade orbs onto the soft white wall in front of him, knowing full well that in just under a few days time, he will be standing in a PCW ring…on this very airship, facing off against three other men. Three men, who believe that for some reason they can actually break away from mediocrity and step up to the level of Matt Jackson. Breaking free of the nothingness that has encompassed them, since the dawning of their lives. Each one doing it differently, Tyler Preston failing as a comedian, hoping that he could some how make something of himself, as a wrestler…this times going to be no different from his other failures. Rolling his eyes, Jackson lets a smile come across his angular face, as he puts his arms behind his head…looking up at the ceiling, his thoughts swirl together, with the Fusion match, skipping through, like a pebble moving over a pond, causing small ripples, but nothing more then that, not the shock wave of anything major, but Matt doesn’t expect anything more from three people, who quite simply just aren’t up to par…no one over the past month is up to par though, with the exception of Edward’s fluke win. Jackson has stepped over everyone far surpassing them in every sense of the word, this time isn’t going to be any different, just different faces and names…in the end it doesn’t matter though, because the result will be the same…the result Silvio doesn’t want to face. I saw your press conference, his mind sneers, showing his unadulterated scorn for the man, the scorn that’s been building up inside of his five foot eight inch body, ever since Silvio made his first move at Supremacy. He’s been spending time, learning about his current arch-nemesis, but from his current biddings, you can tell how serious he really is taking the man. You think you belong Silvio? I brought you into PCW, because you made me sick…sick of your voice, your look…sick of you. That’s why you are here Silvio, because of me and you better remember that. He flicks a piece of his tall spiky blonde hair out of his face, continuing to relax in the recliner. His every emotion calm and cool, his thoughts cold and calculating, matching his pose, the look of fearlessness and power, the same look he would have at Fusion. Everything is going in perfect order, just as he planned it. Silvio believing that he can stand toe to toe with him, because he bought his way into PCW shows, because he got in the face of Matt…it makes him just another, another person who is hoping to ride on the coat tails of the agent. You think that due to your success in the PWA, you are going to have the same domination here? He thinks, chuckling to himself, as the words form in his head. You think you fit in next to names like Jason Snow, Pharaoh, and Xavier Kannon? All men who came from the PWA, he questions his own thoughts, pausing…waiting, almost like Silvio is going to respond himself, hoping that he would see the error in his ways. You think you can stand with me, because you won Tempest’s made-up little title? Not likely Silvio, you were in a class of underachievers, people who were just like you, the only difference is you became their leader, he barks out, wishing everyone could hear him, but knowing it’s better to hold his tongue. I would much rather be on the graduation list, cemented in stone next to Totakoxe Salvador…for all to see, for all to remember. He knows he’s correct in these thoughts, because in a few weeks, Silvio’s “Coming Storm” Title reign is going to go to the waste side, burning out of people’s memory, the ashes flying away faster then Silvio’s soon to be short stint in the PCW, a place by all description, he’s not cut out for. A place he only happens to be, because he got lucky and forced Matt to get him hired…letting Jackson seek redemption on him for his mistakes, protecting the innocent that Rochelli hopes to prey on, not knowing how dangerous of a game cat and mouse is, when the mouse is trained to knock-off people like the cat. A sudden, but forceful knock comes on the door of the room, making Jackson jump from the seat…from his throne. He swiftly takes the few steps in between him and the gateway to the rest of the airship. Not wasting a second, he twists the brass knob and jerks the door open, peering outside to see who this unwanted disturbance is going to be. It’s no one, not a soul is standing in the well-lit hallway. His cherry mood disappears and is replaced, with disgruntled anger for the person who just made him stir for no good reason. He grabs the thick wood door, ready to slam it shut, but as he does, his head tilts forward…his eyes lock onto a small white envelope on the threshold of his residence. Reaching down, he scoops it up and studies the bulky paper for a moment. Softly shutting the door, he walks back into the room, his mind a frenzy at what could be the contents of this package. He’s never received something like this, but even if he had, he would still be weary of the situation. He takes his perch on the enormous king-sized bed, with the ornately carved out headboard, with the strange design that goes all the way down to the wooden frame. He’s careful not to awaken the woman who is quietly napping on the left side of him, close to the wall. ~>…
