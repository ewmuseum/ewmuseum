Death to Cheffy: Part 1

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/10/02 The camera fades in… We see the office of a television executive for the Kid Net production company. The office is full of vibrant colors. The walls are decorated in different shades of yellow, with pictures of past Kid Net stars hanging all around. There’s Rainbow Randolph, Wally the Whale, Mr. Fluff Fluff, Jeffrey Dahmer, Neon Nick, A bowl of potato salad, and more. A row of shelves stretches across the opposite side of the room, packed with various awards, certificates, plaques, and a piece of lingerie autographed by Farrah Fawcett. Behind the giant glass desk there is a bright orange colored leather chair, turned towards the window, which gives us a majestic view of Times Square. KNOCK! KNOCK! Person in the chair: Come in… The door opens and in comes the Culinary Conquistador! The Chef moseys on into the office and sits down on one of the smaller orange leather chairs reserved for guests or applicants. Person in the chair: Do you know why you are here? The Chef: I’m perplexed to tell you the truth. I am a chef after all, and when I got a letter from Kid Net productions, I thought it was for my adopted son! Person in the chair: Well it definitely was for you… The Chef: Well yes, I noticed that after I checked the address on the envelope… The chair quickly swings around, revealing an old, crotchety mill…er…billionaire dressed in a fancy Italian suit and rolling two Chinese health balls around one hand. Person in the chair: You are a very prized commodity to us now, Mr. Alouette! The Chef: Why so? Person in the chair: Watch… The man picks up a remote control and presses a button. The giant television, hanging from the ceiling on the opposite side of the room begins to show a taped presentation of Jingle Jackson Show. … The entire set looks like a carbon copy of Santa’s Village in the North Pole. Wee men and women are dressed as elves, and two men dress up as a reindeer. Kids gather around a man dressed in the Holy Trinity of Christmas Colors, with bells placed all other his suit. His long ears are elflike, but his normal size distinguishes him from the midgets. He skin color though is eerily similar to the Jack the Pumpkin King’s from “The Nightmare Before Christmas”. The Chef: Don’t I know that guy from somewhere? Jackson: Hey kids…HE HE HE! (Grabs his crotch) HE HE HE! Who wants to dance the Jingle Mingle Ding Dong with me!?!? All the kids scream an enthusiastic “YES!” Jackson: Well then, let me pick out a sex toy…I mean…child…HE HE HE! Jackson walks through the crowd of kids, grabbing the boys on the ass, and gives the girls a nice pat on the crotch. He smacks the ass of one boy and picks up the youngster. Jackson: You will dance the Jingle Mingle Ding Dong! Kid: I want my mommy! Jackson: Your mommy isn’t here, but I’ll be your DADDY!!! Now let’s sing! Kid: Ok… Jackon: Ex-IT LIGHT!!! Kid: … Jackson: En-TER NI-IGHT!!! Kid: What? Jackson: TAKE MY HAND!!! Jackson grabs the kid by the hand, and pulls him closer. Kid: These aren’t the words… Jackson: OFF TO NEVER-NEVER LAND!!! HE HE HE!!! Jackson looks around the set very cautiously, AND THEN TAKES OFF WITH THE CHILD!!! All of the stagehands rush after the bolting pedophile. The cameraman runs after Jackson as well, letting go of the camera and spinning it around into the direction of the confused kids. Girl #1: Jingle Jackson touched my in my private zone… Boy #2: My mom calls it the “Don’t Touch Me Place” Boy #3: My dad told me girls will like to jump up and down on my winkle. Girl #2: Can I jump up and down on it? Boy #3: Sure… FLICK!!! The television executive turns off the monitor and looks back at the Chef. Man in the chair: Because of Jingle, AKA Michael Jackson… The Chef: (Interrupting) I knew it!!! Man in the chair: Ahem…as I was saying, because of Michael Jackson’s antics, we have one young boy with a Grammy up his ass. The Chef: That’s some sick foreplay… Man in the chair: …AND! While left alone, all the children in the audience engaged in some kiddy orgy!!! Jackson has put the future of our network in jeopardy… The Chef: Hopefully he gets the chair, or at least the guillotine! Man in the chair: (Nods) Chef, I am Monty Richards, the president of CEO of Kid Net Production Studios, and we want you to bring this Network out from the hole that Jackson has buried it in… The Chef: One question: why me? Richards: After going through the list of potential candidates, we were ready to give up and go back to Rainbow Randolph… The Chef: Ok. Richards: But Jackson, whether he knew it or not, contributed in our decision. The Chef: How so? Richards: While sodomizing that child, he was heard to have said, “I am the Queen of PCW!'... Well one of our executive board members happens to be a PCW fan, and immediately thought of…Bertha Stewart! The Chef: (Rolls eyes) Of course, she’s always thought of before me… Richards: But her constant cussing, less than adequate physique, and smell of rancid sausage probably wouldn’t go well with a group of children. You sir, on the other hand, seem to be perfect for this job. The Chef: Just in case you didn’t know, my track record isn’t sparkling either. I’ve assaulted the former Vice President and current President with a lobster, I defected to an Islamic Terrorist Organization, I blew up a Toys R’ Us, and have been stalking Grizzly Adams for god knows how many years. Richards: Well compared to the rest of the candidates, your track record IS SPARKLING, and we want you to be the SAVIOR OF THIS NETWORK!!! Think about it: you get full control of the show as executive producer, the Kid Net penthouse is all yours, Bertha gets a buffet to cater to her needs 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and most importantly… The Chef: You mean I don’t have to cook for her anymore!?!? I’M IN!!! The Chef shakes Richards’ hand vigorously. Richards: But don’t you want to know what else you get!?!? The Chef: (Totally ignoring Richards at this point) I can’t believe it! I will never have to cook for Bertha again… The Chef sighs with satisfaction. Richards: Chef, you’ve made the best decision of your life! You will not regret this! The Chef: I can’t wait to sing, dance, and cook for those little bastards…er…American swine dogs…er…children! Richards nods approvingly. Richards: Let me show you around the studio, we’ll take you over to props and costumes and get you fitted for your new uniform! The Chef: (Feeling woozy) All this attention…all these gifts…too much to take…at one time…going to…go…night night…now… The Chef instantly faints, keeling over face first onto the ground. Richards smiles and laughs heartily/ Richard: Great acting Alouette! I almost thought you did…(Realizes the Chef really did faint) faint…SECRETARY!!! As the secretary runs into the room with a cup of water to revive the Chef, the camera peers over to the window overlooking Time Square. The camera then zooms through the city at a fantastic speed towards the Penitentiary. The camera then goes through the front door of the jailhouse and through the various rows of cells until it stops at one cell. A figure, cloaked in black from the lack of light, watches at small black and white television. The television shows footage of the Kid Net/Chef deal with images of the Chef shaking hands with Richards, and fainting yet again after receiving his first weeks pay through a check. Bertha luckily has a wheelbarrow set up behind him, which catches him as he falls backwards and she rolls him back out of the sight of the camera. Figure: Grrr… The Chef will get his just desserts when I get out of this hellhole… Guard: Dinner’s Here! A security guard walks by, handing the inmate his dinner for the evening, which the dark figure snatches greedily from his hand. Figure: OOOOH, steamed broccoli!!! HE HE HE!!! The dark figure jumps up and grabs his crotch as the camera mysteriously fades to black.
