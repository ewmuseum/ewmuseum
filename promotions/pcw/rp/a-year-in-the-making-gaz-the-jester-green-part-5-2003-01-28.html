A Year In The Making - Gaz 'The Jester' Green (Part 5)

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 January 2003 Recap: Being the last graduate Xavier Kannon took pride in this well-documented fact, the man branded 'The Next Big Thing' and there wouldn't be one person to challenge such a remark... Well for a year anyhow... A long year has passed since the Scientologist had his name etched alongside the greats of the wrestling industry, men like Jason Snow, Ivan Stanislav, Bobby Stormbringer and Avalanche... Maybe the latter two should never be sentenced alongside a word such as great but nonetheless, the Academy had produced the talent of tomorrow. It is four of these talents we have followed during their graduation ceremony, a bunch of oddities, each unique in their persona, be it a Jester, Vampire, Mobster and Red-Headed Female. Gaz 'The Jester' Green Nascosto Ireul Nerezza Angelo Deville Amy 'Aimz' Campbell Three quarters of this contingent have stepped up to the batting plate so to speak and have since disappeared through various circumstances, events that would defy odds, who would've predicted that Wrestle Tattler’s resident stalker Bobby Stormbringer had replaced Amy Campbell’s speech... That one is actually believable... However who would've foreseen Buffy The Vampire Slayer's very own blonde bombshell Sarah Michelle Gellar making an unexpected appearance during a Nerezza shoot and Angelo Deville playing directly into the hands of the New York Police Department? With the end of this ceremony drawing nearer and the crowds increasingly growing restless, a state personified by Hessian, the Titanic Satanic Insaniac bellows down the sound equipment one final time as he introduces the Emerald Menace, PCW's resident prankster Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'THAT'S IT... Fucking Jester... Office Jester, Corporate Jester, Child Molester, get your green ass up here and let's get this show done and dusted before Bobby Stormbringer invades with the new Phantom Phive!' The piercing white light perched to the rafters again shines down over its victim, like a police helicopter, no more like an alien craft, minus the array of different colours and the overall lighting spectacle commonly associated with abduction. Point being the light beams directly over the Jester and his Front Office colleagues who watch idly by showing their support; a final mutter of conversation and Gaz Green looks pumped, as he prepares to be thrust into the limelight. 'Let's hope we aren't visited by the Pranking Police, huh?!?' sarcastically murmured the tailored green Jester to his stable mate Xavier Kannon who uses his option of a retort. 'Somehow I can't see that happening... Squire...' The squire comment draws a smile from its recipient as his punch line hits home, and with a final dust down of his business jacket the arms and legs of Gaz Green move in tandem as he cockily struts over to the pedestal where an inpatient behemoth lays in wait. 'Like... I sooooo thought those lights were Outer Limits material, NO... Twilight Zone!' said the ditzy diva Ellie~! Towards her suffering husband who can only sympathetically smile before patting his head in utter disbelief, more so when the former cheerleader decides to whistle her own remix of the shows classic opening melody. 'Hubbard why do you continue to torment me?' cries the former World Champion, looking to the heavens. '... But Xavier... I don't want a mint?' answers back his loyal valet. One could only imagine the visions flooding the dear CEO's mind. Was he throttling her with his tight grasp or was Xavier Kannon setting his beloved up for the Hallmark, possibly a position the entangled lovers had been in before, one could only imagine as the Scientologist sat motionless, almost in a dream-like state, a false smile etched across his face. The congregation sat watching the Jester making his way to the altar where they had already witnessed the demise of three former colleagues, a subtle cough to clear his throat and his nimble fingers vanished beneath his business jacket, retrieving a piece of folded white paper. Unravelling the article, Gaz perched the document against the angled altar and finally soaked in his final breath of preparation before beginning, much to The Front Office’s delight. “Where to begin?” opened the Lord Of Laughter. “Eh, like the beginning and stuff!” voiced Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Her untimely interruption causing her husband to contract red blushes of embarrassment as he wishes for the ground to open up and swallow him, thus saving the Scientologist from further humiliation at the hand of his beloved. “The beginning… There wasn’t much of a beginning… The Academy saved me from the cesspool of Indy wrestling by offering me the opportunity to showcase my talent to a much wider audience, those writing for the papers, emphasise the word talent, maybe bold lettering and under-lined…” self promoted the Front Office Entertainment Manager. “Anyway… I was put into a class under Cassius Deamonte, which brings me to my first thank you! Cassius Deamonte, thanks for nothing, and believe me when I say that comment came directly from the heart. Please… In my opening match I lost to Charlotte… This is where you people justify this act of oppression with cries of ‘WHO?’ However lucky for me, Cassius Deamonte’s tuition was like his Wrestler Tattler tenure, simply short, not short enough though huh?!? His departure paved the way for Gaz Green to join the cream of the crop and with a little haggling from the talent spotting Hessian I was aloud to jump ship so to speak…” continued the arrogant trickster as he gazed back miming a genuine ‘Thank You’ to his mentor, Old School Wrestling’s Hessian. “So I thank El Diablo for his bolstering support, his personal chats meant as much to me as the tuition I received from Cassius Deamonte, one would say ‘What chats?’ Then there’s the man who made this happen… The Phantom Menace… Oh, The Phantom Booker… He noticed the Jester and promoted, no, he tossed Gaz Green into the pitiful promotion of NTICW!” further insulted Gaz Green, shuddering at the very mention of New True Internet Championship Wrestling. As Gaz stood re-composing himself another voice filtered through the dim lighting of this dwelling. “Are you going to actually thank someone or wage war with words?” Squinting with his piercing green eyes, the culprit remained concealed as Gaz Green concluded his speech with a hoard of genuine ‘Thank You’ comments, short and sweet he at-least paid thanks to some of the people who opened the doors of his six month career. “So I pay my respect to Angelo Deville for setting the standards and challenging the ‘Powers’ into graduating the four of us. I also would like to thank Nascosto Ireul Nerezza for being such a dedicated partner and playing just as big a part in Pranks With Attitude’s success as I did… However… No amount of respect will save you from the spillage of claret come Shanghaied and lastly to Xavier Kannon for saving me from NTICW and giving me the burning desire to prove the doubters wrong!” With a few wayward roams of his green eyes, Gaz looked spooked as he quivered into the microphone one final time. “WHAT THE F….” Causing the flock to turn in unison at whatever had spooked the Jester, the assailant, the being, the… It could’ve been anything however the congregation turned to look directly at the door, nothing of real importance. “Just kidding….” Joked the jovial Jester as the PA system hit with a bombardment of music coaxing those in attendance to flaunt themselves via the dance-floor and prompting the scene to draw to a close, ending another segment involving the shenanigans of Gaz Green. - The End -
