Ten Ways To Hurt Tim Walden With A Melon Baller (Part 2)

undated in the fed's own posting (Cooking Utensils Match build, autumn 2002)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ten Ways To Hurt Tim Walden With A Melon Baller (Part 2) Featuring: The H.I.T. Melon baller. Skewer. Rolling pin. Chopping block. Meat Grinder. Steel Chair. Which of these things is not like the others? Well, unless the Palace of Versailles has dignataries sitting on chairs of steel, the last one. The rest are cooking utensils. Prime weaponry in a Cooking Utensils match. But, rather than prime cuts of meat, a crisp summer squash, peaches, or even cake batter, the utensils will be tenderizing, chopping, slicing, and mixing flesh, bone, and blood. France, home of Julia Child, shall see a fervent fricasee the likes of which have not been seen since the Ohta Faction battled Iron Chef Japanese Masaharu Morimoto in Iron Chef. CARNAGE! RUIN! IT COMMENCES! The school kitchen has been abandoned for a new kitchen, in the grand city of Paris. The somewhat sliced up Walden likeness is along for the ride, remaining on the counter of the kitchen. Trapezoid smiles, a full array of dangerous looking Cooking Utensils laying on the side. The HIT: Bonjour. Welcome to Paris, France. Capital of the oft-occupied nation in European conflicts. Also known for a lot of other things, like Jerry Lewis. Most of them suck, but one thing that will decidedly not suck is the Cooking Utensils Chef Memorial Match for the PCW Continental Title, live and in color from the Palace of Versailles. The Rush, Rush Mason. The End, Skylar Green. Totally Tubular, Tommy. The Taxman, Tim Walden. And The HIT, well, I don't have a real name. But you get the idea! The five top contenders for the Continental Title, battling it out for supremacy. Simply put.....the Continental Champion is the number two man in PCW. And that's a position I would very much like to fill. Trapezoid grins, the melon baller coming back into his hand. Feeling a surge of power running through him, he takes the baller, and jams it, handle first, into the Walden melon's gut, causing a large indentation. The HIT: Method #6...jam the handle into Timothy's abdomen, causing a nice wound to appear. Man, it's fun inventing ways to injure people with a seemingly innocuous object! But then, that's what this match is about. Turning things people don't usually think of as hardcore into hardcore. I mean, the average person doesn't see , say, a potholder, as an especially dangerous weapon! But, in the right hands..IT IS LETHAL! Potholders can be used as choking devices. It's all about thinking outside the box. The lethal Trapezoid raises the baller, shoving it metal first into the melon's mouth, knocking out several teeth. The HIT: Number seven.....the plain and simple ol' Chicago style dentist job. It's amazing what happens to a man when he sees his teeth lying besides him on the floor. He starts to panic...like you are while watching this! It's time for you to panic Tim, as it is time for everyone else to panic. Facing me now is a damn good cause for panic, because you face a man well-versed in the enviroment we are entering. I know of pain. I know of torture, and I damn well know of panic. True panic is lying on the ground, with a man standing over you, 200 pound tombstone in hand, ready to erase you from existence! THAT IS PANIC! And so, I can sympathize with those who lie below me, as I prepare to strike the deathblow. Sympathize, yet that shall not cease my assault! A vicious uppercut follows with the baller, landing to the chin of Walden melon. The HIT: Method #8! Scraping the chin....EVILY! You know, I would have thought I would have had a lot more evil ways to hurt Walden than this. I'm almost dissapointed at my own lack of creativity right now. But, I'll have more than one measly melon baller to use. I'll have literally HUNDREDS of demented weapons to use! Cooking utensils of every shape, size and function! Just think of it man! I can be melon balling Walden, while Tommy and End are locked in a rolling pin death duel! I thank the Chef for making such a spectacle. I'm truly looking forward to competing. Despite my earlier exchange with former student The End, I'm ready. Sure, he probably got the better of me in the written word, but I shall best him where it matters most: in the Palace! Yea, though I walk through the Palace in the shadow of pain, I shall fear no Ending, rather, The End himself will be ended! Rather than a melon baller, it is a large skewer The HIT hefts this time. It appears to be a foot long, as the Trapezoid raises it, getting ready to drive. The HIT: Rush Mason wants to mock my way of life? He wants to poke fun at something? Well, let's see how HE likes it when I poke fun at HIM! Or, more precisely, at Walden! :Trapezoid drives the skewer down hard into Walden's head. It stays there, until HIT removes it. Taking the melon baller, he scoops some of the melon out from the hole.:: The HIT: Number nine...using it to pull out injured tissue after I drive a skewer into Walden's skull. But, all of those pale in comparison to #10. Ten is the most evil thing I could possibly think of. The one thing that will hurt him most. And that of course, is as I drop the melon baller on him after the match, as I heft my Continental Title HIGH! THE END IS NIGH WALDEN! I WILL BE THE VICTOR!! Fade to.....pencils.
