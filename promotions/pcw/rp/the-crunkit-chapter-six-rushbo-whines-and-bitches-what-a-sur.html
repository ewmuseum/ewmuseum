The Crunkit Chapter Six: Rushbo Whines and Bitches, What a Surprise

Date: While You Were Sleeping Rushbo had indeed escaped from the goblins, but this was countered by the fact that he had no clue where he was. He had lost his pony, food, his wizard, his white people, and the buttons on his fly new jean jacket. He was obviously not a happy little Crunkit. So, he wandered aimlessly in the area, never really moving anywhere, going in the general vicinity of where he was supposed to be going, but never really sure which way that was. Soon, the sun began to sink, strangely, the sun sank, and the world lived in blackness! HAHAHA! Sorry about that, I work double time as a depressing story narrator as well. However, the sun began to sink, now this is a monumental thing,behind the mountains. Well, it may not have been that important, but it was important to the little Crunkit, it meant that at least they would say he had completed some of his journey when they found his tiny corpse. Rushbo: Well what in the name of Rush Mason? I guess I just conquered the Misty Mountains! Great, now I only have to find the rest of the group, put up with white people for who the hell knows how long, climb some more mountains, and steal some shit from a dragon. Damn, how the hell did I get into this? Realizing that there was no one to hear him, Rushbo decided he better save his beautiful voice for when ears would actually hear it. Yet, he still wandered around, wondering at the same time whether he would ever see Juandalf, Sethin, Trevin, or Coren ever again. Soon, he even was out of the small high valley. However, now a horrible thought came across his Crunkit mind. He wondered if, with his magic bling bling, should he go back and look for his companions, amongst the tons and tons of white people in the cave? Rushbo: C’mon, they’re white people too. Don’t they have some sort of pale people bond? They all speak Caucasian right? Unfotunately, Rushbo had come to the conclusion that he was going to go back and help his friends, and was already starting to feel terrible for himself, when he heard voices behind him and his train of thought changed. He stopped everything he was doing and listened, and it didn’t sound like the creatures were speaking Caucasian, so he crept closer, being more careful than ever. He crept down the path where ditches on two sides of the road lay. He heard the voices were coming from the left ditch. He saw the head of Sethin the dwarf, but luckily he was still undetected. Rushbo: I’m gonna scare the white out of those guys. Juandalf was arguing with the dwarves at the edge of the ditch. They were trying to figure out exactly what had happened in the tunnels. The dwarves were just mumbling amongst themselves. Juandalf: We can’t leave Rushbo here. Sure, he’s annoying sometimes, but he is your only burgular, and he’d probably say you guys were trying to pull a Rodney King on him if you DID try to leave. Sethin: Oh right, the wonderful racial comments are exactly why we don’t want that dude here anymore. Juandalf: He’s just used to his own world. Besides, he’s not a bad guy, and I feel responsible for his safety out here. I wish that you morons hadn’t lost him in the first place. You can’t believe the Malcom X and Martin Luther King speeches we’re gonna here when Rushbo comes back. Trevin: Why’d we even bring that dude? From what I can tell, he’s not exactly grade A-thievish material man. Juandalf: I chose the best man I could find. Perhaps my choices were a bit limited, but Rushbo has got a big heart, and he can do what it takes. Trust me, he goes absolutely crazy for many anyway. Stupid kid’ll do anything… Sethin: Sure, he’s got heart, but will heart get our totally radical treasure back from Smaug? Juandalf: Listen, I brought him along, and I don’t bring along useless stuff, I didn’t bring my Russian girlfriend, now did I? Now she would be useless on the trip, but boy is she a good coc- Coren: We are getting far off topic? Do you have a particular reason to want to bring the burgular back with you? Juandalf: Of course, he is my good acquintince and I mean to bring him back in one piece. Now, either you three help me look for him, or I can go look by myself, and you three dwarves can see how far you make it without me or him. By the way, you didn’t say why you dropped him. Sethin: The dude is heavy for a little dude, besides all those pasty white dudes came and tripped me from behind. There was nothing I could do! Coren: He is not lying, Juandalf. Everyone was fighting, we all nearly got our heads cut off. Then you made a giant flash and told us all to run. There was no time to see if the little burgular was there. Now here we are, without the dumbass. Damn that little bastard and his spaceshot mind. Juandalf ignored the excuses and was just walking off to look for Rushbo, when out of literally nowhere stepped the grinning black face, followed by Rushbo’s body. Rushbo: Here’s your burgular! Yeah, I love you too Coren. I always knew you white people were trying to leave me behind. The four men jumped so high that they got nose bleed. Well not really, but it sounded good in a Ludacris song. Juandalf, surprisingly, was just as astonished as the dwarves to see him, but he was more pleased than the others. Juandalf yelled at Trevin about being a terrible look-out man, but the fact is that this incident helped Rushbo’s reputation more than it hurt anyone’s. Rushbo: What? I’m a master burgular, didn’t I tell you? I can appear from virtually anywhere. And none of them did doubt that the little man was a master burgular after that. Trevin was the most confused of them, wondering how Rushbo had come up to the camp right under his nose, though he had never seen the man. Rushbo was very pleased with his efforts, and he just sniggered to himself and didn’t bother to mention the ring and when asked how he accomplished this feat he replied: Rushbo: I’ve been fooling white people since the day I left the crib, Casper! However, none of them took notice to Rushbo’s jokes any more, because they knew that they had a great burgular, so what was a few white jokes here and there? Trevin surprised Rushbo very much with his next comment. Trevin: Dude, I don’t know how you got past my eye, but no one’s ever done that before, so I take off my fake do-rag to you. Trevin, at your service. Rushbo: That’s right whitey, bow down to repay the sins of the white man! I mean, your servant Mr. Baggins. Then everyone wanted to know about the adventures of Rushbo after he had been separated from the party. He told it to them, with some stretching of the truth. Rushbo: Yea, there were about a thousand white guys, but I kung fu’d all of them and the last one I hit a running reverse Death Valley Driver on, it was awesome. Coren: What’s a Death Valley Driver? Rushbo: Haven’t you been to one of those CWF event? Crunkit Wrestling Federation? Man, I gotta take you sometime, it’s the shit. Anyways, Rushbo exaggerated his tale, so he ended up as the “hero” of the day, even though all he had mainly done was stolen a ring and almost get himself killed. But of course they needed to continue their quest. Juandalf of course told them that they needed to, but even he had to agree that it was time for some rest at the moment. So they rested that night, but in the morning, they continued their long quest towards the Lonely Mountain… *** *** *** *** Soon, however the trip was back somewhat to normal, as Rushbo was complaining, much like he had before they had arrived at the Misty Mountains. Rushbo: Uggh, I’m so damn hungry. I could eat a whole white person right now. Which one of you is the smallest? Once again becoming annoyed with Rushbo, none of the dwarves replied, but Juandalf did, but he wasn’t very pleaseant with his reply. Juandalf: Unfortunately, there’s not much I can do, unless you’d like to go back to the goblins and ask very nicely if they can pretty please give your luggage back. Rushbo: No thanks, I’ve seen quite enough of those pasty white folks, thanks very much. Juandalf: Well then, we gotta just pull out pants up, not count our chickens before they hatch, a penny saved is a penny earned, GOAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLL~! As they continued on, Rushbo looked everywhere for something to eat. The blackberries were still just flowers, and of course there weren’t any nuts. He drank once from a small mountain stream that came across the path, but the water tasted strangely like old people, so he didn’t try it again. He had three wild strawberries that he found near the stream, but they actually looked like old people, so those were not edible. They still walked on and one. The rough path disappeared even, so they had no guide as to where they were going. The grasses and plants vanished, and they found themselves on top of a wide steep slope of rocks. When they began to descend this, small pebbles rolled from their feet and soon larger pieces of stone were clattering everywhere around them. Before you knew what was going on, the entire slope seemed to be moving faster than white people when they hear about a Michael Bolton and Phil Collins duet CD. Finally, they reached the bottom. Sweating and panting, Juandalf panted out to the rest of the group something strange. Juandalf: That sure got us a little behind, but goblins will have trouble tracking us through that, besides, white people have bad ankles. Juandalf seemed almost cheery, even after that expierience. Sethin: Yea, maybe so dude, but they won’t find it difficult to roll some boulder down here to squash us. The dwarves and Rushbo felt far from cheery, and were rubbing their legs and feet, both in pain from the rocks and rubbish that was flying around. Juandalf: Don’t be so down in the dumps. We’re gonna turn now and be quick! Look, the sun is going down behind the mountains! Indeed it was, the shadows were already being shortened but getting blacker with the dark sun. They limped along at a slow pace, but as fast as they could go in their condition, down the gentle slope of a forest on a slanting path that didn’t help the leg and foot injuries at all. They pushed through what seemed like a never-ending path full of branches and other hazards. There was no wind, almost no sound, except for the occasional crunch of a pine cone underfoot, but most of the night was passed trodding on pine cones quietly, but all of them were hurting… Rushbo: Do we really have to keep going tonight? Rushbo resumed his whining once it became so dark that it was no longer possible to see anything but the one meter directly in front of you. Rushbo: My toes hurt, my feet hurt, my legs hurt, my balls hurt, need I go on? Juandalf, however insisted. Juandalf: Let us go a bit further. Rushbo sighed a deep sigh as the group trudged onwards, despite the constant sighs, not only from Rushbo but from every member of the group. Finally, after what seemed like hours and hours, they came to a clearing, where no trees stood. The full moon watched over them, and they could tell that this place was not a good place to be at this time of night. All of a sudden, their fears were confirmed as they heard a long howl from down under the hill. The howl made them shudder where they stood. This howl was answered by one that seemed even close than the first one. It was wolves that were howling at the full moon. Rushbo was the most afraid, for everyone knew that wolves didn’t want that nasty white people meat. He had never encountered any near Crunk End, but the blaring rap music that always came from his hobbit hole would’ve scared them away anyway. To hear this howl was the exact last thing, well second to last thing he wanted to hear on this night. The first one would’ve been hearing that he would be fighting a Nazi in the ring, which would be condoned by Ultimate Authority, probably on Fusion, HEY!!! Rushbo: Oh Rush Mason, god of Crunk, don’t let the wolves get me. Let them have one of those fake religions like Scientology, Islam, or White People. Trevin: Dude, white people isn’t a religion. Rushbo: Well it’s a group of annoying people. I see no difference between that and a religion anyway. We just escaped white people, and now those damn wolves are gonna pick their teeth with my bones. Juandalf: Get up into the trees quickly! Juandalf quickly led the group to look for trees on the edge of the clearing, any that had branches low enough for everyone to reach them. They found the trees as quickly as possible, because the wolves were evoking an amazing amount of velocity out of the group. They climbed as high as they could at that speed. Sethin and Trevin sat atop a tall larch that looked like a giant Christmas tree and the two dwarves looking like ornaments. Coren sat alone in a pine, as Juandalf sat alone in another pine, a huge one that only he had been able to climb. And you wonder where Rushbo was? Well, in the hurry, no one had bothered to help the small Crunkit, so he ran around from tree trunk to tree trunk looking for one to climb. Sethin: Dude, you left the Crunkit behind again! Trevin: Dude, I can’t always carry that little dude on my back down tunnels and up in trees! What do you think I am, a camel? Coren cried out from across the clearing at the two, whose bickering could be heard all over. Coren: If you don’t give him some help, he’ll be eaten, we’ll have no more of Juandalf’s help, and we won’t get our treasure back. You better get your dwarf asses in gear and save him! Sethin, give the Crunkit a hand up. Sethin grumbled, but he slid down the tree and reached Rushbo just in time to be fully scolded by Rushbo. Rushbo: That’s right, Casper, you get your dwarvish ass down here right now. Sure, you guys thought you’d just let the black guy die? Now there is NOT gonna be any negligential racism in here, I’ll tell you that! Sethin: Dude, shut up, I’m saving your life here. Sethin made Rushbo climb onto his back and then scrambled back up into his tree just as the first of the wolves trotted right into the clearing where the entire group was. This brought silence to anything that Rushbo might’ve said. The wolves snapped at Sethin as he climbed, but they couldn’t reach him. Within a minute though, the entire pack of wolves was around the tree where Sethin, Trevin, and Rushbo sat, tongues hanging out and eyes fiery. As evil as their intentions were, the wolves, which had the evil influence of darkness in them, they couldn’t climb trees. So, for the mean time, the group was partly safe from any harm in the trees. However, their chances of getting out of this situation alive were becoming slimmer and slimmer. This was evidently the wolves meeting place, as more and more people poured into the area. They were intelligent enough to have guards that stayed at the base of the tree, watching and drooling over those in the trees. One wolf even snarled up at Juandalf in the language of the wolves. He told him that the wolves and goblins usually stay close to their mountains, unless they are driven out or looking for new homes, or their wolf wife has just gotten pretty fed up with them cheating and they’ve decided to set their wolf house on fire. They said that they had come with the goblins on a great raid, obviously a result of the murder of the Great Goblin, which the wolves did not know was perpetrated by this very group. The wolves told them of their terrible plans to attack the villages of men during the night, leaving alive only those strong enough to be used as slaves. This was horrible to listen to, because Juandalf hated listening to the wolves bitching and because he realized the immense danger that he and his friends had gotten in to. The wolves were angry and surprised by finding the company in this place, and they thought that this group might be in cahoots, which sounds like something that white people would do, doesn’t it? Like, “Myself and Vice President Schlongenheimer will be in cahoots for the rest of the day.” Anyway, the wolves thought that the group might be in cahoots with the men of the wood, and coming to spy on them. They thought that because of Juandalf and company telling the wolves and goblins’ plan to the men, they would have to fight a terrible war. Now, just to interject another narrational bit of wisdom here, doesn’t it seem like the all might evil wolves would WANT to go to war with the men? Wouldn’t that be the heelish thing to do? Or maybe these wolves are tweeners, or they’re just confused as to what they are, kinda like Flaymin Aymin. However, the wolves had no intention of letting the party out of this clearing alive. They also mentioned a helpful bit of information that goblin warriors would be arriving very soon, so we’re talking boatloads of fun here. Now even Juandalf, one of the most powerful wizards ever known to Middle Earth, hearing this, was terribly afraid that he and his comrades might never make it out of this situation. He was at least going to do the valiant thing, which apparently is Juandalf’s thing now, even though he’s supposed to be a heel, which by the way is another very confusing story, but that’s not the point here. He grabbed all the pinecones in the tree around him, and lit one in blue fire and flung it down, where it struck one of the wolves, and then a green fire, and then a red one. The dwarves cheered and even little Rushbo showed signs of being content at this. Fire always scared wolves, much like black people always scared those old white people who wear their pants up to their necks, but this fire was not comparable to any other. It was like those old white people seeing Dennis Rodman, and then peeing their adult diapers all the way back to the nursing home. Very soon, there were bunches of wolves rolling around to put out the fires on their coats all over the clearing. “Biatch, what the fuck is goin’ on in mah mother-fuckin’ forest?” A thundering voice could be heard and a large eagle, known to many as the Lord of the Eagles was shown. He sat, his large black body, on top of a lonely pinnacle of rock on the edge of the mountains. Eagle Lord: I hear those muthafuckin’ wolves! Are those bitches being pussies again and ackin’ all crazy cuz their bitches dumped them? Or is those crazyass cracka goblins out in mah woods tonight? He flew up into the air, as swift as a do rag onto a bald head, or maybe that’s not a good analogy, but this whole story is kinda fucked up anyway, so you shouldn’t be looking for good analogies. Two of his guards from either side flew up to follow him, and they all looked down onto the ring of the wolves, which was a tiny spot in their eagles eyes. The Lord of the Eagles had very keen eyes, and he could not see the people in the trees, but he could see the tiny flashes that were the fire, and he could hear the pained cries of the wolves beneath him. Eagles are not kind at all, some are cowards and some are cruel and punishing. However, the ancient race from the north was crunk as hell, and when we say crunk as hell we mean pimped out up and down all around the town jump jive and wail if ya hear me. The goblins hated and feared the crunk eagles, but could never reach their nests high up, or drive them from the mountains. The Lord of the Eagles was curious, so he flew off with some of his guards towards the large clearing where Rushbo and the dwarves and Juandalf lay, awaiting some horrible fate that the goblins had for them. Back down at the clearing, the wolves were definitely hurting from the pine cone assault of Juandalf. But still the wolf-guards didn’t leave the trees. They snapped and jumped even higher now, maddened with rage, but Juandalf seemed hopeful that they could escape. Suddenly though, that thought left as goblins came to the clearing, running and screaming. They soon learned what was going on and they laughed at the group. Goblins aren’t afraid of fire, and soon they had a plan to get rid of the group once and for all. They put out all the fires that were in the middle of the clearing, but they didn’t put out the ones nearest to the trees. Soon there was a ring of smoke and fire all around the company, and it was slowly capturing them, closing in. Smoke was in Rushbo’s eyes, he could feel the flames licking at the tree, and he could see white people dancing, or doing something that would be dancing if they were black, but they were doing a cross between the Macarena and terrible break-dancing, it was horrible indeed. And in an instant, the flames were upon Juandalf’s tree. The lower branches began to crack. So, he climbed to the very top of the tree. One of the flashes came from his wand like a streak of lightning and he got ready to spring down upon the goblins. That might’ve been his death, but at that very second, the Lord of the Eagles swooped down upon him and seized him in his talons, and was gone. The servants of the Lord of the Eagles tore through the goblins’ ranks and some grabbed the dwarves from the tree-tops. In fact, Rushbo was almost left behind for the second time of the night. He had to grab hold of Sethin’s legs as he was borne off and Rushbo swung through the air while they traveled back to the Lord of the Eagle’s hold. The last thing Rushbo could remember was hearing the goblin screams and yells, but then he looked down, and never quite having a stomach for heights, threw up for a long time, and even after he stopped, he felt very ill until they touched land again. The group was brought before the Lord of the Eagles by his servants once they arrived at his hold. The Lord was already speaking to Juandalf when they arrived. Rushbo was quite relieved to find out that he wasn’t going to be eaten by the giant ghetto eagle. He caught the last of the conversation between the two. Juandalf: Yes we are very hungry, and if you would take us as far as you can, that would be terrific. Lord Eagle: Biatch, you should know fool that I got the muthafuckin fizzle to feed your grizzle. Rushbo: Good, because I could eat a whole fried eagle. Speaking of which, you have any old eagles that you’re kinda sick of, because I haven’t eaten a fried bird in days and I think I’m turning whiter every day I don’t have it. Lord Eagle: Biatch, I’ll sizzle what I can dizzle if you’ll chizzle for a mizzle. Rushbo: Sounds good to me. Soon, Rushbo’s stomach was full with roast pigeon, and he was happy once again, probably not for long because soon he would start bitching about something, probably that all the white people were going to kill him. But for now, he had no complaints at all. He curled up on a rock that felt better than any feather bed he’d ever slept on. All night however, he dreamt of Crunk End, and how much poon he was going to get when the bitches back there heard about this…
