The Golden Saga: When the Fondest Feeling Dies

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 16th, 2002 Love. Everyone feels Love. Love for a person, Love for an object, Love for a family member, Love for a feeling, Love for a TV show, Love for a life, Love for something that happened. No matter how much you Love however, it eventually dissapears into nothingness. Love dies. Love always dies. It is a cycle that can never be stopped no matter what happens. People change. Feelings change. Love changes. People die. Feelings die. Love dies. The cycle. Life. Love. Death. Life. It's how it works. Nothing can stop it. But then why do people love? Some say that it is a chemical situation that exists in the endorphins of the brain. Endorphins are peptide hormones that bind to opiate receptors and are found mainly in the brain. Endorphins reduce the sensation of pain and affect emotions. Endorphins are the bodies natural morphine. A drug that releases into youre head and begins to change your emotions just because of its existence. You see, endorphins are said to cause love, but endophins also cause pain. Life has pain. Pain releases endorphins. Endorphins cause love. Love is pain. Pain is life. Which means, in its basic essence that Life, with Pain, is Love, with Pain. Is it chemical? I don't think so. Is it emotional? Is it spiritual? I truely don't know. It could be any one of those things that equate to the feeling of seeing a loved one. When your own heart betrays you and begins to beat faster and the endorphines enter your brain or the spirit feels itself become enlightened through emotional ways or when you get plugged in the ass by that homosexual Cupid and his arrow of love, whatever it is. It happens. Everyone falls in love with something. What does that mean though? Its natural to love. Its natural to die. Since Life is pain is love is pain then is life death and is love life? Or is death love and life is just itself. Natural and beautiful. Is love natural and beautiful? Love is always painful. Life is love is pain? What does it all mean. I sit here up at night and think about it. How can I not have the answers. I am a deity and I sit on the throne of the gods, I speak on a first name basis to Christ and his father. But yet noone gave me a handbook or told me what I was supposed to know. I was thrust into a position that I didn't even need, all because of the fact that my bloodline suggested it, because Destiny handpicked me to be the fucking filter for all your pissy little dreams and nightmares. I know everything about you, well I could know everything about you, but I choose not to remember it. Why would I wan't to. I know your definition of love. I know why you think it by just looking at you. Its completely wrong. In my humble opinion of love at least. You may be wondering what that is now. Maybe I will tell you, well... it would be easier if I showed you. Look next to me, laying down in the same bed as I. It is my angel, the light that guides me in the darkness, mon amour, Talena. This is love. This is why I do it. Yes, love dies. Feelings die, people die, deitys die, I die. You will die. Animals die. TV Shows die. Things die. It all dies. The moment though. The moment of when you look next to you and see the beauty of life, the thing life created that you saw and triggered the endorphins in your head to make your emotions point to her and go 'fuck, that is someone i love', that moment, and the moments that follow when you are with her, that is why I do it. That is why I love. She is my definition of love. She is its existence and proof that it happens to everyone. Love... ... Love is Life.
