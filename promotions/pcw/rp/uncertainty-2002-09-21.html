Title: Uncertainty Featuring: Tim Walden Date: September 21, 2002 (OOC: Yeah, I know this RP's past the deadline and all, so it really doesn't count..I just had an inspiration this morning and felt that I should build up a little more thought-wise to the match, so this is rather a between-shows RP OOC, but IC it is set pre-Fusion.) What to do, what to do...There has to be some way to get to Kramer...So happy-go-lucky with his little vermin Grimer, he probably doesn't care if he wins or loses...But I've gotta win this one...So I'd better strike early and often. I sit here in the back of my limousine looking forward beyond my driver to the afternoon sky, on the way to my first match in a while...I had best get there early to meet with the rest of the Office and get ready for the match, so this would probably be my last real opportunity to relax... Leaning back and sipping a Vanilla Coke, the sweet tingles of vanilla distracting from my thoughts...Distraction that I know I shouldn't be letting get to me, but that I cannot resist...I cannot let myself fall to this scum, it would destroy my momentum, but I know that barring some sort of disaster, I can beat him..Some sort of disaster indeed... Flashback..late 2001, Thugs 4 Hire runs in on a Kramer match for a beatdown...Then...it all came unglued. We had him broken and beaten, but suddenly, he came into contact with the power...The power of the ten-piece bucket...He went on a rampage, took out the whole group at once, it was just plain ridiculous, but it was as if the bucket's power had taken hold of the whole momentum of the match...Ferris Wheel Of Destruction brought down both my hopes and the entire group's dignity..I had to avoid something like this happening again, I could not let Kramer make me look like a fool again in my first match back.. There had to be something I could do to ensure this didn't happen..I knew Duke could beat him, as he had done so many times before, but I had to watch my back too...When you try to send a man to his death, don't think that at the very least, his friends will be utterly pissed-off, and this would just compound their frustrations from Tuesday..There was something just not right at the moment, some supernatural aura that clouded my mind...It wasn't a deep foreboding, but only a mild tinge of worry...I tried to shake it off by taking another sip, but it only worsened... Wait...it dawned on me..Too much Vanilla Coke at once...Good thing, I was starting to overanalyze things and this couldn't be healthy for my wrestling condition...I pushed down on the power window switch and as the tint dropped, I leaned my head out the window, if only slightly for fear of road signs cutting short my night before it even started..Scanning the buildings along the commercial strip, a Walmart with a McDonalds in the parking lot, a local golf store, a Jeep dealership...I leaned back in as the limo pulled to a stop, but my glance was caught on something just beyond the trucks, a glimmer of red and white that brought back fond Canadian memories, at least outside of the whole CJS obsession....The limousine started moving again, and I looked forth to whatever it was.. It was just what I needed to see, in the sarcastic manner at first, a Kentucky Fried Chicken franchise, complete with special offers on Wildwings guaranteed to 'Piledrive your tastebuds'..But a thought struck my mind...'Jeeves?' 'Yes, sir?' The voice rang back obediently, it was nice to have a chauffeur of one's own as opposed to having to share one with the rest of the upper-classes... 'Pull over, I've got a hunger for some chicken right about now.' What to do indeed... ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
