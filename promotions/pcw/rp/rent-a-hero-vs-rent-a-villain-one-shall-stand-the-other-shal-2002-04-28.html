Rent-A-Hero vs. Rent-A-Villain...One Shall Stand...The Other Shall Fall

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Rent-A-Hero vs. Rent-A-Villain...One Shall Stand...The Other Shall Fall Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 4-28-2002 ..::/::..The suprises never cease in ym life do they...if you told me today would be the day I'd have to fight the greatest evil in the world...that being myself....I'd call you a LEX LUTHARIAN LIAR CITIZEN. But as it turns out...I've found out a lot of things aren't what I thought them to be a to first. Not only am I the first in a series of supposed 'killing machines'...but I'm also replicated in the form of Dr.Harkins dead son. Dr.Harkins is a mean ol' man obsessed with revenge at the world for the death of his son, and he took it that I'd be the weapon to make that happen...I was the failure...yet once he recaptured me through this henious trick that involved my love Katie, he looked over my genetic code yet again and created the CORRECT models of what I should have been...and I stare one right in the face as we speak. I look into his light crimson eyes and I see everything I was fortunate enough not to have been...bloodthirsty, enraged, and having no earthly feeling towards others. I stare into his eyes and I realize one thing...there can be only one. The time has come, Rent-A-Hero versus Rent-A-Villain...good versus evil...Joker versus Batman...a handful of Gummi Bears versus a handful of thumbtacks. I can see in his eyes that he has every intention to snuff me out of the big picture...it will NEVER happen. The same way The Chef and his rotund clone wife, Bertha, can never hope to snuff out the allied forces of Juan Borro and Rent-A-Hero. Yes he is a legend and yes he is one of the hardest men to tackle in the PCW, but Juan and I are the new generation...a new breed of wrestlers looking for our time in the PCW and if it means going through Chef to prove that point, so be it. His evils and injustices will all be repaid in one grueling match where there will be no winners....just survivors...and Juan and I shall stand tall, tall enough to battle at the pay-per-view to find out who truly is better and who is the king of the Crusierweights...I can't wait for it, but right now, Chef and his overweight clone wife are the priorities and must be beaten. Need I nto forget the epic battle I will be thrut in as well when myself, Jason Core, El Diablo, and Miyagawa face off against the evils of The Pharoah, Juan Borro, Flaymin Aymin, and Boda! That match is gonna have more action than a John Woo film but there will be only one set of winners....and TEAM JUSTICE WILL PREVAIL. Not only will we combined our total SMACKAGE powers against the four losers who stand across from us, but we will bring them to their knees and have them BEGGING for the painful justice to end. But before I can even worry about those battles ahead...I MUST DESTROY RENT-A-VILLAIN! We cannot co-exist and the only way to settle my past and my soon to be future is to find out who shall live in this battle...may the fight begin..::/::..!!!!!WHAP!!!! ..::/::..The scene unpauses, the fist of the evil Rent-A-Villain smashing across the face of Rent-A-Hero as he goes flying into some of the computer consoles in the background, sparks and small electrical explosions crackling and starting everywhere. Clarise, the lovely vision of purity who aided Rent-A-Hero during his capture, screams in fear as she crawls by one of the tubes with the other copies of Rent-A-Hero within, scared out of her mind of the terror that is Rent-A-Villain. He stands tall, hsi dim red eyes probing the area as he cracks his knuckles yet again...gritting his teeth and aching to cause destruction. His full body black spandex suit squeezes against his tight, muscular body as he stalks the area, the elderly yet sinister Dr.Harkins laughing as he watches his creation prepare to do what it does best...destroy..::/::..Clarise:'MAKE HIM STOP DR.HARKINS!!' Dr.Harkins:'That is not an option my dear...just stay still and say your prayers for now...my Rent-A-Villain will deal with you in due time my sweet. But for now...watch as he decimates this utter FAILURE!!'..::/::..Rent-A-Villain slowly steps over to the wreckage of the computer consoles...sliding away wrecked parts with his foot as he searches for the body of Rent-A-Hero, the thirst in his eyes to destroy the hero we've grown to love so much. As he begins to move another console out the way using his foot, some of the rubble flys back, Rent-A-Hero emerging from it as he peppers Rent-A-Villain with flurries of lefts and rights, the fists on target as they repeatedly hit his face, sending Rent-A-Villain back as he holds his face in pain. He touches his lip, seeing the blood on the tips of his finger and lickign the blood from the wound with his tongue as he looks at Rent-A-Hero, standing tall and brandishing a few cuts on his exposed body. Seemingly ENJOYING the pain, Rent-A-Villain say..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'Is that the best you got Rent-Boy? If you think that pansy a** offense is gonna keep me from grinding your a** into the ground...your mistaken.'Rent-A-Hero:'Enough trash talk Rent-A-Villain...let's finish this!'..::/::..The two Rent-A-Brothers begin to trade monstrous blows with each other, punches and stiff kicks going back and forth between the two as they try their hardest to stand up to each other's offense. Dr.Harkins heads up the steel steps again, Clarise seeing this and giving chase to the demented old man as the two 'brothers' continue their brutal battle, Rent-A-Villain lifting our hero up and hurling him into a set of lockers nearby, our hero screaming out in utter pain as he leaves deep dents in the lockers. As Rent-A-Villain tries to get in a few kicks, our hero throws a savate kick upwards, landing in the his chest before our hero follows with a stiff clothesline down to the hard metal floor, the battle still ensuing as both men are on the ground. Both men begin to choke at each other, rolling about on the floor until finally, Rent-A-Villain gains the upper hand, landing insane amounts of blows to the face of our hero before he grabd him by his limp neck, blood trickling from Rent-A-Hero's face as Rent-A-Villain says quietly..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Oh brother...why don't you get it, I'm superior...I'm simply BETTER then you. I am the weapon of everlasting destruction you NEVER came to be and now because you can't embrace what you should have been...I have to finish you off for the good of Dr.Harkin's cause. Maybe I'll have some fun with that little girlfriend of yours...Amy is it?' ..::/::..Rent-A-Hero's eyes grow with intensity at the mention of her name from Rent-A-Villains lips as he continues to mentally intimidate him, running his fingers across his blood soaked face as he whispers in his ear..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Maybe I'll have a little fun with her...BEFORE I end her life as well. How woudl you like that, lover boy?'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero fights to get words out, his body writhing in pain and incredibly weak as he tries to form words. While trying to speak to his look-alike arch-rival, he notices a loose sparking wire near him, our hero trying to speak Rent-A-Villain as he reaches out for the wire..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Usually I don't say things like this...bu...bu..but...when you scr....ew wit...h what I l..l...love...I WILL END YOU!!'..::/::..At that instant, our hero snatches the fizzling wire in his hand and jams it into the chest of Rent-A-Villain, the electricity surging through his body as he lets out a horrid scream of pain that fills the whole laboratory, his body lighting up like a human lightbulb. Our hero then follows by kicking thrust kicking him off, Rent-A-Villain sailing into one of the clone tubes and shattering it upon impact, water flowing onto him as he lays limp in the wreckage, the Rent-A-Hero clone within the tube dormant and lifeless. Light smoke begins to emit form the charred body of Rent-A-Villain as Rent-A-Hero fights his way up to his feet, lookign about in disgust at the various clones of him as he wipes the blood from his mouth and nose, his hands strewn with dry blood..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'None of you deserve to be among the world of the living...because sadly, Dr. Harkins has led you to believe it's only worth destroying. If that's your belief for this world...you have no right in it.' ..::/::..Our hero rummages into one of the lockers he smashed against, which opened it forcibly from the impact, and finds a plastic red gas can housing a full tank of fuel. Our hero immediately removes the cap and begins to walk over tot he cloning tubes, dousing the tubes and everything else in the near vicinity in the flammable liquid as he seems to have no pity on the evil remakes of himself. Once he FINALLY uses the whole can of fuel, he grabs the wire he used to fry Rent-A-Villain and steps back...eyeing his 'brothers' for the last time before tossing it into the gas...the flames wasting no time in engulfing all showered within the liquid as the many clones of Rent-A-Hero are lead down the path of destruction....dead before they can even taste the breath of life. As the flame burns, our hero turns his attention upstairs as he says to himself, the crimson flame burning bright in the background..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Now for Dr.Harkins...' ..::/::..Before our hero can even begin to traverse up the steps, he is met right at the top by the wily doctor...who also happens to have the lovely Clarise Honeypanties hostage, easy to see why as he holds a Wahlter PPK revolver to her head of logn blonde hair. Dr.Harkins sees the wreckage before him, the destruction of his greatest achievement at the hands of our hero, his face construing into a maniacal look that begs for revenge...and that revenge is against out hero. He slowly makes his way down the steps, our hero backing up so as not to give him reason to hurt the lovely Clarise, who tries to stay calm while the cold barrel of the reveolver presses against her head..::/::.. Dr.Harkins:'I...I...I can't believe you destroyed my children...ironic that my FIRST BORN...my failure, destroys all of my perfected creations. Rent-A-Hero...were you jealous that maybe, just maybe, they were better then you...stronger then you? Don't you see? YOU AN INCOMPLETE DOLT WHO SOMEHOW MANAGED TO MAKE A LIFE FOR HIMSELF!! That's okay though...because I can still make more, I can still create more of these wonderful creations. Consider your life spared Rent-A-Hero, but the girl won't be so fortunate, I'm keeping her until I escape safe and sound and I'm out of your reach...then I'll consider whether I make her an experiment subject or just kill her. A shame isn't it though Rent-A-Hero, she is a pretty girl, and her life is now lost because YOU couldn't contain your jealousy...and to think I based you on MY son.'..::/::..Dr.Harkins still keeps Clarise in a tight grasp, the mad doctor making it down the stairs and nearing himself towards a electronic door that's across a catwalk, below that catwalk being a river of some unknown red liquid substance, flowing freely below it. Our hero looks to Clarise, who also locks eyes with our hero as he begins to calmly speak to Dr.Harkins, something not completey right about this situation..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'That's your downfall doctor...your too busy trying to play God to realize that sometimes...you HAVE to let things go. Your obession with the 'perfect weapons' and 'revenge' has made you a broken and bitter old man. I may be a failure to you Dr.Harkins, but the experiences and things I've felt and known have all been worth it and in reality...I owe you so much...but then again, I HATE you for putting me through so much...making me believe things that aren't real...and even NOW, my questions are left unfulfilled and unanswered. Dr.Harkins, you have a choice now....end this madness, or let this madness bring you to your death.' ..::/::..Dr.Harkins lcoks eyes with the bload soaked Rent-A-Hero, both equaling in intensity as Dr.Harkins dosen't even take a mere second to reply..::/::..Dr.Harkins:'NOTHING...will stop my revenge.' Rent-A-Hero:'Have it your way...' ..::/::..AT THAT INSTANT, Clarise firecely kicks Dr.Harkins in his leg, the doctor loosing hsi grip as Clarise runs out of harms way, Rent-A-Hero dashing towards the sadistic doctor. Shortly after regaining his bearings, Dr.Harkins fires a shot, ripping through the shoulder of Rent-A-Hero as blood begisn to spout from the wound...yet STILL not enough to stop the advancing of Rent-A-Hero as he throws a fierce punch, catching Dr.Harkins in the face as he goes sailing over the catwalk, barely gripping to the rail as he tries his hardest to pull himself up. Our hero holds his shoulder, trying to hold back the blood flow as he looks to the struggling Dr.Harkins, our hero knowing he could help him back up to safety if he wanted to but just watching as he uses all his strength until finally...his grip loosens, the doctor falling back as his scream rips through the air...shortly silenced as he falls within the liquid below....his screams forever muffled by the mysterious liquid..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Goodbye...father...' ..::/::.. At that instant, an explosion rips throuh the laboratory, setting off a chain reaction of devestating explosions as Clarise helps our hero, walking with him towards the door that Dr.Harkins was about to use as his exit. Clarise opens the door, helping our hero through as it leads to some steps to the outside world...but before he can walk out, our hero looks back at the exploding laboratory, his eyes scrunched in a mix of pain and sadness as he quietly says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Brothers...I'm sorry you had to miss the beautiful thing they call 'life'.' Clarise:'Come on Rent...we must escape.' ..::/::..With that said, our hero and the lovely Miss Honeypanties make their way up the steps, closing the door behind them as the explosions begin to rip through the laboratory.....the remenants of Rent-A-Hero's origin soon to be buried in rubble or scorched by endless crimson flames....but in the midst of all this, a hand suddenly shoot through the rubble, an all black hand fighting for life in the midst of all this destruction as the scene begins to fade...that sinister voice of Rent-A-Villain chanting in the midst of all the destruction..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'Th...is...is not....the...end....br....br....brother....this...is....not...the...end...bro....brother.' ..::/::..Those words lead into the slow fade, a new beginning for one man, and a continuing fight for the other. As sure as the words were spoken from Rent-A-Villain's mouth...this, along with other things...is not the end, just a prelude to more things yet unseen. WHAT STRUGGLE IS NEXT FOR OUR HERO?! WILL RENT-A-VILLAIN SURVIVE THE DESTRUCTION?! WHAT MORE DOES OUR HERO NOT YET KNOW ABOUT HIMSELF?! FIND OUT SAME RENT-A-TIME...SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
