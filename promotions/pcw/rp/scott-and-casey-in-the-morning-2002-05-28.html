Scott and Casey In The Morning

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-28-02 -It’s 6:30am in the lovely state of New Jersey, a small state with a big New York attitude. You know the time because the promo opens up on a clock radio which just went off. On the radio is Scott and Casey from New Jersey 101.5.- Scott: So the PCW is going back to it’s roots right here in New Jersey and this morning PCW superstar Duke Williams will join us. Casey: He’s washed up couldn’t we get Kannon or Nelson? Scott: I’d watch it he’s heading to the studio right now. Casey: I have a protection order from his boss Tyler Nelson saying he can’t touch me. What’s he going to do beat up on a poor DJ? Scott: Good keep running your mouth. Six-thirty six on New Jersey 101.5 this is the Scott and Casey morning show. -The show stops down for a four minute stop set.- Scott: We’re back on New Jersey 101.5 joining us in the studio is former PCW continental Champion and host of PTC Malice and color man for PCW Turmoil and Fusion he is “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. Duke thanks for joining us! Williams: Anytime Scott. Casey: Lets cut the sucking up and get to the questions. What did it feel like when you lost to Xavior Kannon at Supremacy? Williams: Scott, who’s this hump? Casey: Didn’t you read the show info it’s the Scott and Casey show. I would be Casey hence one half of the show. Williams: I thought they fired you? Casey: Funny I thought they did the same too you. Scott: Boys please, I don’t want a blood bath in here. Casey: Yea you better hold me back. Williams: This boy’s hankering for an ass whooping. Scott: So PCW invades all of New Jersey this weekend as both Turmoil and Fusion broadcast from the garden state. Casey: I noticed they left you off of the cards Duke. Nelson is finally getting the act together. Williams: Really dipBEEP cus if I’m not mistaken I’ll be at both shows with a headset calling the action. Scott: That’s a good point. Casey: That where you should stay!! Your too old to wrestle. Williams: hey hoss, I may be too old to cut the mustard but I can damn sure lick the jar out baby. Bring your wife on and she’ll tell the world all about it. -Scott laughs- Casey: Yea real funny!! Williams: Thank you!! Maybe you should listen to a good show like PTC Malice on fancast.com. Go ahead and take notes you may learn something about the business. Casey: Your yet to answer my question, who did it feel to lose to Xavior Kannon at the Pay-Per-View. Williams: It felt embarrassing and I let down my fans. But I’m not gonna come on here and cry about it. I’m not gonna come on here and bitch and whine. I’m not going to give him the satisfaction. Yes he beat me, was it one on one? NO. Scott: Is there talk of a rematch in the future? Williams: If I have to beat him down then so be it. Casey: Why don’t we cut to the chase Duke, why are you avoiding him. Scott: What are you talking about Casey? Casey: I’m talking about dookie running like a little girl from Kannon. Williams: Where do you come up with this crap? Casey: Oh is it crap Duke?? Why aren’t you in the brawl that’s on Fusion?? Kannon’s in it. Williams: It would be better served having a third man call such a large match. Casey: So your giving up a chance at Kannon?? Sounds like your scared to me. Williams: It sounds like you’re an asshole. Your not baiting me into that match. Scotty: We have Tommy from Trenton Park on the line. Tom… Tom: What’s up boys!! You know, Duke used to be quite the hard core wrestler back in the days. Williams: It was never my strong suit but thank you. Casey: Suck up. Tom: Now he won’t even fight?? Seems to me the Malice Man is turning into the Mediocre Man. Williams: Nothing but morons call this show!!! I would be better suited in the broadcast booth. Scotty: We have another caller on the line with us. Dave: What up Scott, Casey, Duke? Scotty: Not much, you’re on with Duke Williams. Dave: Duke you got a hairy ass hillbilly listening here! Williams: Hell yes!! Casey: What a dolt. Haven’t you ever heard of Tyler Nelson? Dave: IS that the loser in the white shirt with aids? Williams: Right on!! Casey: What do you think of your loser friend not joining the rumble? Dave: You know Shabazz and Rush are in it!! You should enter it just to kick the hell out of Kannon. Williams: That’s an idea. Casey: Oh so if Kannon’s outnumbered then your willing? Williams: Damn right hoss!! That’s Malice Style! Casey: Pussy Style!! Williams: How about I kick you ass right here? Dave: Send him Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound! Williams: What a pud! Scotty: We have an other caller. Kevin: Duke did you hear what Corey Williams had to say? Williams: Corey Williams is about as controversial as your six o’clock news! The guy is white, plain and simple. He don’t impress me much. There’s a difference between controversy and idiocy. Rosa Parks was controversial. Tom Green and guys like him and Corey Williams are idiots. There complete boobs and not funny at all!! Kevin: I agree with you Duke. Casey: What a suck up. Williams: Corey Williams is going to get rocked this Tuesday. Casey: Sure he is. Williams: I’ve had about enough of you. Casey: You touch me I’ll call me lawyer. Williams: That’s a chance I’ll just have to take. -Casey is heard darting out of the studio as Duke follows him out.- Scotty: Somebody is going to die!! Lets go to a break. -End Transmission-
