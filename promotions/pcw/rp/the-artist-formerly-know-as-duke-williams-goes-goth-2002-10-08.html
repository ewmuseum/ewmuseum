Title: The Artist Formerly Know As Duke Williams goes Goth!! Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/8/02 -Duke Williams is on an image changing trip in Chicago, IL. His first stop is the Alley store on Belmont. This is the top goth/leather clothing store in the city. Duke is dressed in his ostrich skin boots, black wranglers, a PCW polo shirt, and black Stetson hat. He walks in and meets Spike.- Williams: You Spike? -The man points to his extra long and spiky mo hawk.- Williams: Right. Spike: You must be Duke Williams?? Williams: Yea that’s me hoss. Spike: What can we do for you bro? -Duke bites his tongue not to make fun of this freak.- Williams: The bookers in PCW seem to be booking more towards Goth’s and freaks so I want a make over so I can fit into their plans. Spike: Its not a look rube it’s a way of life. Williams: Yea what ever. -Duke pulls out a wad of cash from his pocket. The kid grabs the money and smiles.- Spike: Lets get started Mr. Williams. -Spike looks over Duke and just shakes his head.- Spike: This is going to take a lot of work. Strip to your boxers. Williams: Don’t you think you should by me a drink first? Williams: What the hell is wrong with Duke Williams? Spike: Sounds all like John Wayne and shit. -Duke makes a fist.- Williams: What’s wrong with John Wayne? Spike: When I think John Wayne I think apple pie and apple pie isn’t Goth. Williams: What about Malice? Spike: Naw that’s my girlfriends name. Malice is feminine. -Duke turns red as an apple with anger.- Spike: What about chains? Williams: You have to be kidding me. TAFKADW: Ozzie is that funny British guy on the MTV? Spike: And musical icon. TAFKADW: No kidding he’s a musician? Huh. -Spike just shakes his head.- TAFKADW: Ok I’ll give it a shot. -Spike hands the artist formerly know as Duke Williams a pair of leather pants. It takes a little effort but Duke squeeze into them. He stands there bare foot in leather pants.- Spike: Perfect! TAFKADW: They ain’t wrangler’s hoss. Spike: I know they are much better. -Name Pending just glares at Spike.- Spike: Now you need a belt. TAFKADW: I know that’s why I’m going through this change. Spike: In time bra….in time. I’m talking about a belt for them pants. -The two men head over to the belt rack which is filled with the ugliest belts a conservative republican has ever seen in his life.- Spike: how about this one? -Spike grabs a bright orange belt with silver dice as the buckle.- TAFKADW: What about this one? -Duke grabs a black belt with red flames going around it. The silver buckle says fuck in big block letters.- Spike: No, it won’t work well with the shirt lets try this one. -Spike grabs a black and bright orange belt with a chrome set of boobs as the buckle. Duke just smiles and nods as he straps that bad boy in the leather pants.- TAFKADW: KICK ASS!! Duke puts it on as it fits tight to his fit body.- Spike: We’ll get you a pair of Doc Martins and you’ll be all set. Now the true make over. -Spike leads Duke into the back to a barbers chair where Duke sits down. Standing in front of him is a tattooed chick named Malice and a faggy guy name Trey.- Trey: I bet he would look great with no shirt and a nipple ring. TAFKADW: I don not do piercing! Malice: Come on name pending you can do it. How about a tongue ring to pleasure her better with. TAFKADW: Only if I can try it out on you. -Malice looks at her boy friend with a frightened look on her face.- Malice: Lets move on. Trey: We need to do something with that hair. -Malice roughly pushes Duke back and tugs on his hair as she washes it. She then starts to dye it a dark black. An hour later out walks the Malice man dressed in all black, with leather pants, a spike chain around his neck, and jet black hair in pig tails.- TAFKADW: Are you sure it looks ok? Trey: Look a doll baby. -Name pending shoots the homo a nasty look.- Malice: Hun you look like a million dollars. A little make-up and your done. TAFKADW: Make up? Malice: Some black nail polish and black mascara and your goth baby. TAFKADW: Well baby if you dig it and it will get me gold why not. -Name Pending sits back down in the chair as Malice does his nails and make up. Trey walks back with some black fish net gloves that Duke slips on. He is all dressed up and looking goth.- Spike: Damn you look bad ass. I through some extra cloths and make up in this bag for you to continue the look. Come back in a month to redo your hair.
