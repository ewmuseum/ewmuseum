Tykes Revenge

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 4, 2003 [Somewhere a retard is crying. Mentally handicapped left with nothing once again. It was ashame because they used to have absolutely nothing before, but that was before the 'Tubular Man' came in and helped them. They were picked on by all the ignorant who didn't understand and only knew how to reject something that they didn't understand. Many children have been left emotionally scarred from such encounters by 'The Real Retards'. Tommy stepped up to the plate and he used his large sums of money to fund 'Tubular Tykes' centers. There were two of them when it started out and then they just multiplied like cells. There ended up being over twenty centers in the state of California and thousands of mentally handicapped children were learning that they have the right to be just like everybody else. Tommy himself would even visit the centers and give the children pep talks to up the morale of their depressed states. Many of the teachers and therapists really respect The Tubular One for the sacrifices he was making to make sure the children had everything that regular children had. Tommy would even have parties for the exceptional children at his very large beach house. Cookouts, games, and beach fun filled the day and it turned out to be one of the greatest days some of the children would ever have. Some of the children still don't understand how their facilities were taken away from them. Many of them blame the 'stupid, poopface popiticans'. While many of the strong willed children know exactly what happened: Tommy pushed them back out to the places that drove them absolutely mad. They ask themselves: Why did he do it? What possessed him to even think about it? How can he sleep at night knowing what he's done? Those questions have been answered by two simple words: Greed and Power] [William Hedgerow was at the Los Angeles Tubular Tyke Center ever since it started over two years ago. He remembers not wanting it at first and rebeling against it. He used to act up in the classes and throw things during the activities. Then he met Tommy one day and his attitude completely changed. He began to pay attention in class and respect the teachers that were only trying to help him excel. He remembers Tommy personally coming to his home and giving him a special award in academics because he had scored the highest in all of the center. Tommy spent the entire day with him. It was sunny, not a cloud in the sky, and the air had that country smell going on. They went to the beach and scoped out the beautiful women that were walking around. Tommy even went out of his way to get William a number from one of the ladies and even got one of them to kiss him...ON THE LIPS!! Then he took William to an ice cream parlor and gave him all of the ice cream that he could handle. William ate a little too much and almost upchucked everything back up but he held the chunks back not to spoil the perfect day that he was having. Tommy then gave William tickets to an NTICW show that he was going to be attending and they were FRONT ROW SEATS!! Tommy came out to his really cool song and gave William a special high five that they only knew about. For about 48 hours William felt extrodinarily special and it was something that he would never give back in a million years. In fact, he wouldn't even trade in his Downs Syndrome to erase that day...that's how much it meant to him] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDE!! This traffic is totally bogus! 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy, in his hawaiian dress shirt and spandex shorts, sits impatiently at the wheel. His blue shaded sunglasses glint in the sunlight somehow making his eyes somewhat visible. 'Heavy Metal' Harry, on the other hand, is in all black without sunglasses. His eyes squint as the sunlight beats onto his pale skin and the sweat pours down his face as if he had just stepped out of the shower. The traffic jam on the freeway is extremely backed up, much more than the usual. On the radio they mention something about a stupid truck overturning. Tommy wonders to himself, 'Why can't they just put the truck back up and move the traffic faster'. He doesn't really understand the whole impact of the situation. He just wants to get home so that he can invite some bodacious babes over to his house. He's in a playful mood today, more than usual] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Hey...how come that weird looking kid is staring at us, man? TTT: Because we're totally famous, that's why. Harry: Well, he doesn't seem happy about it, man. [Tommy looks over to the mini-school bus that is beside them and notices the odd looking child looking down on them. It's obvious the child recognizes them, but he doesn't seem all too thrilled with the fact. Tommy figures he must be so overcome with excitement that his only reaction is to look totally sad] Harry: Doesn't that kid have some kind of disease, man? TTT: I think so. Harry: Well, this traffic better move because I don't want to catch it, man. [Tommy looks back up at the bus and notices something about the way the child looks. He has Downs Syndrome! Tommy is proud of himself for knowing that. By surrounding himself with all of those retards he learned a lot about handicaps and all that totally bogus stuff. It just made Tommy feel so much better about himself that he didn't have to battle mental illness, or does he? As Tommy looks closer there is something so familiar about this child, as if he's seen him someplace before. He then comes to the quick realization that dudes and dudettes with that Syndrome all seem to look the same, so that would explain a lot to him] TTT: There is something totally heinous about that kid. It's starting to get kind of creepy. Scream at him or something. Harry: I'm not screaming at him, man. TTT: Don't be afraid. Harry: He might infect me, man. TTT: He doesn't have projectile Syndrome spray or anything, he can't really harm you from here. [Harry nods and then looks up to the child who still has that glazed stare in his eyes. Harry then looks back at Tommy with a bit of apprehension on his face. Tommy waves his hands at Harry motioning for him to go ahead while sort of being supportive. Harry then looks back up and takes a long sigh] Harry: STOP LOOKING AT US, MAN!! [The scolding words have absolutely no effect] TTT: Scream louder. Harry: STOP!! LOOKING!! AT! US! MAN!!!! [The child then opens the window on the bus, hanging his head out of the window] Harry: Uh oh, he's gonna infect me, man! [Harry goes for cover and Tommy just tries to stay cool, even though he's not really feeling too cool at the moment. The child is hanging halfway out of the window with a bit of anger on his face. He's never really seen an angry retard before and is quite scared. It's like one of those heinous Fox shows, When Retards Attack. Whoever has a video camera at this point is going to be in for making some bodacious bucks] Child: How could you do that to us, Tommy? How could you just throw us out in the cold like that? I thought that you really liked us and were trying to make a difference! It just seems to me your just like all those other stupid rich investors who are trying to make a good image for themselves! TTT: Dude, what in the name of totally bodacious super villains are you talking about? I've done a lot of totally bogus things so your going to have to point it out for me. [William Hedgerow sighs loudly, feeling that he's much more brilliant than Tommy will ever be in his entire life] William: Tubular Tykes! Remember all those kids? Yeah, well now we're back out into the system and it's ten times worse than ever. You plucked us up...told us that we were extra special and exceptional and then you threw us back out like yesterdays trash! Does that ring any bells? [Tommy looks closer at the Tubular Tyke and his memory begins to go into overdrive. He remembers this dude, and it would seem he is FAMILIAR! Tommy hung out with the little dude on the beach over a year ago or something like that. He remembers using the kid to get a ton of babes numbers and get that totally bodacious sympathy vibe with the retard. It worked out perfectly and got more numbers on the beach that day then he's EVER gotten in a month! It's a record that stands even to this day] TTT: Your BILLY! I remember you now! You know I'm really sorry about that, but business is business. One day when your all grown up and stuff, working your ass off as a janitor at some department store you'll TRULY understand the bodaciousness of what I did. I wasn't just doing it to build these gnarly water parks...I did it because I cared about you radical retards!! William: Yeah, it was so kind of you. Everytime I get mashed potatoes thrown at my head I'll always smile brightly knowing I have a lifetime free pass to your STUPID WATER PARK!! TTT: EXCELLENT!! Now your getting the hang of it, dude! [Harry still has himself rolled in a ball, trying to hide himself from the evil retard bile that will rain upon him. William has absolute fire in his eyes, feeling the hatred of the past few months boiling up into him. He's wanted to confront Tommy so badly and it's such a twist of fate that he gets the chance] William: I really believed in you, Tommy. Even though I've been hating you for the past few months now I did feel good when you won the World Title. That's because I knew you had the heart in you to be the best in PCW. You may be one of the best wrestlers your a really CRAPPY human being! TTT: I'm a bodacious human being. William: Your an arrogant, greedy asshole! TTT: HEY! Don't you go calling me names! William: Why don't you get out of that expensive Mustang and get over here and stop me! TTT: That's just totally bogus to do something like that, dude. William: Well, it's bogus to just up and cut off people that your supposed to be helping. You know...I should have seen it coming really because how can some surfer guy who can't even help himself help us kids? TTT: I help myself all the time, dude. I get manicures, back rubs, and totally bodacious...well, I think that some else should teach you about the birds and the bees. William: You'll never get it, Tommy. I hope that one day you do understand what your supposed to do, but it will probably be too late to do anything once you truly realize it. TTT: DUDE! Your talking like one of those heinous therapist dudes. William: Yeah, well being with one all the time for the last month would tend to do that to me! [Tommy pauses and looks forward, noticing that there is starting to become a break in the traffic. It seems that they are going to actually move now. Bodacious] William: Tommy...I wish you luck. Because at the rate your going with your life your going to need all the luck in the world. [Tommy just ignores the statement and puts the pedal to the metal. His mustang flies through the freeway now, enjoying the open road once again. Harry lifts his head up with fear in his eyes. Tommy looks over and decides to have a little fun with his stoned brother] TTT: Dude...you looks weird now. Harry: OH NO, MAN!! He infected me! TTT: Dude, your face is starting to deform!! HEINOUS!! Harry: CRAP, MAN!! [Harry looks into the mirror and notices the same face that he always sees in the mirror. A flow of relief flushes over him as he turns to Tommy with a bit of anger in his eyes, but mostly embarrassment] TTT: Your totally easy to get, dude. Harry: I'll stop smoking weed again, man. TTT: Ok...I'll never mess with you again. Harry: Far out, man. Fade to black
