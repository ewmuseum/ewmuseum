On Top Of The World

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th October 2002 They not have any decent music here…stupid Japanese!! I began to flip through the list of songs on the jukebox while twiddling with a pool cue in my other hand, What with this?! “Oi, Fat Man…Where the Gangster beat?” I asked over my shoulder as I continued to flip through the songs and fiddle with my pool cue, I couldn’t his face but I presumed he looked puzzled as Udai relpied, “What do you mean by ‘Gangster Beats’ Master Loc?” How can someone be so educated and not know about good music?! I began to spin the cue stick around in my hand violently trying to overcome his stupidity and control myself from snapping it over his head. As I skimmed down the list of tracks on the Jukebox my eyes lit up as I came to the last song on the list, “This a Gangster Beat….” I smiled and pushed play on the touch-screen of the Jukebox and turned around to my jacket sitting on a bench and reached in for my packet of cigarettes. Producing one I put it in my mouth and lit it, Should I offer him one? I thought to myself, Nah he not deserve it! Chuckling to myself I lit my cigarette with my gas lighter and placed it back in my jacket pocket before adjusting my tank top and black suit pants and taking a draw. “I don’t hear anything Master Loc…” Udai looked up and commented as he finished setting up the pool table, pacing the 8 ball in the middle of the triangle and the white ball on a black dot up the top end of the White pool table. Most people would’ve been amaze at the white velvet covering the pool table and the glass rims, but not me. I couldn’t really care less… I exhaled the smoke and took another draw before sending my boot into the Jukebox, “Work Fuckin’ Du Mah!” The Jukebox then began to make whirring sounds and began to flash as Udai looked up with a worried expression on his face, What afraid you going to have to pay for it Fat Man? Once again I chuckled to myself and turned to break the pack, bending down over the table and lining up the White Ball with the top of the triangle, rearing back and letting my pool cue slap the White Ball into it…splitting them up with a crack. “Nice break Master Loc!” Udai smiled and tried to act friendly, Just as I opened my mouth to comment, the Jukebox began to blare out the first beats to the song I’d selected, “Now this what I fuckin’ talkin’ about!” Udai adjusted the glasses on his nose and looked at me nervously as “Lookin' At You” by Warren G began to pump through the two sub woofers on the Jukebox as I nodded my head in time to the beat, taking a draw from my cigarette and enjoying every second of it. I took a look at the pool table as I exhaled another cloud of thick smoke, Fuck I set him up! I noticed both a striped and a solid ball were hovering near the two corner pockets near the break as Udai leant over to take his shot. Should I kick him? I smirked as I thought to myself, Nah, I going to beat him anyway so I play fair! Udai let out a smart ass remark before taking his shot and pocketing the striped ball and setting up another, “Now I’m going to make magic Master Loc!” “What you say Fat Man?” I tilted my head onto the side, Udai took yet another shot and pocketed another striped ball as I chuckled out loud, He pretty good for a Japanese Fat Man! I let him say what he wants…he beat me I break his face! I couldn’t help smiling again as Udai slapped the White Ball setting up one of his striped and leaving me with a difficult shot to pot a solid. “Nothing Master Loc, I just feel I’m a worthy opponent for you at pool!” Udai chuckled as his stomach wobbled, “Pfft, watch this….” The smoke drifted in front of my eyes as I reared the pool cue back and slapped the White Ball pocketing a solid and taking a step back from the table to plan some strategy. From a few feet away from the table on the opposite side to me, balancing the cue stick on his fat stomach Udai nodded his plump head and clapped. “Impressive Master Loc” Udai commented, You not seen nothing yet… I reared back the pool cue and slapped the White Ball again pocketing two balls in the same pocket, both solids as I stepped back from the table with a smile on my face. I took a draw from my cigarette and leant down over the table to take another shot as I bumped my head in time to the music, sending smoke rings up in front of my face. Managing to knock two solids into comfortable positions it didn’t bother me too much that it was Udai’s turn. The Fat Man not going to win anyway…. Udai moved over to the table and leant down to play his shot as I stood back from the table leaning against a bench, smoking the rest of my cigarette and ‘appreciating’ the music as I looked over at him. He took his shot and pocketed yet another striped as I asked him a question, “Where you learn how to play pool Fat Man?” “What do you think us Business Executives do in our spare time Master Loc?” Udai answered without even looking up from the table. He starting to get smart…I teach him a lesson soon, I took a draw from my cigarette and scanned the table looking at possible shots as I asked him another question, “I thought you sit and play with yourselves or hire cheap Bangkok pussy…” Udai chuckled but didn’t look up, “Very funny Master Loc, may I point out we’re in Tokyo, Japan not Bangkok, China” He starting to shit me off again… I clenched the pool cue in my fist and squashed my cigarette in my hand as I gritted my teeth. “All the same, I thought you just hire whores when you off of work” I snapped back at him in a smart ass tone, Udai took another shot and pocketed another striped ball as he stood up and pushed his glasses up his nose, “Master Loc, we don’t hire whores for pleasure….the company does for us!” I took a step back, the bench digging into my back. What the fuck… I was surprised he just stated that so matter of factly , Maybe he not so Japanese as I thought he was!! “So what are you going to do at Red October?” Udai finished his shot and waited for me to take my turn, I ashed out my cigarette and then leant over and took my shot, pocketing another solid. “I going to get paid…that what I going to do!” “But Master Loc, aren’t you worried about the pride of PURE and TokyoPop?” Udai looked a little stunned that I was putting myself before the company. Pfft… I took another shot pocketing yet another ball and only leaving the red solid and the 8 ball left for me to go. “Let get this straight…I not in this for PURE, I not in this for PCW, I not in this for TokyoPop! I work for them, I get paid…I in this for me!” I took another shot and sent the White Ball crashing into the solid and knocking the 8 Ball near a pocket, It teetered over as Udai leant over to play his shot and I scanned the table, seeing I had an easy run if I managed to pocket the last remaining solid. “So you don’t care what you have to do…as long as you get paid?” Udai took a shot and pocketed another solid as the song came to a close. That right muh-fucker!! I leant back on the bench and fiddled with my cue stick as I nodded my head towards Udai who looked disappointed and shook his head before taking another shot. He pocketed a striped ball and stepped away from the table looking at me, “Sorry Master Loc but I think you should be putting the company at the same level as your own Pride and self….” I glared at him and gritted my teeth once more as he lined up his shot and took careful aim, hesitating to shoot because he knew I would be able to capitalize from his mistake. “Why you say that?” I asked him, Udai looked up and paused, “I’m only looking out for you Master Loc….” “Why you fuckin’ say that? HUH?!” I glared at him again, Udai took his shot and sent the White Ball careening around the table just tapping one of his striped balls and coming to a stop right in front of my solid ball and in line with a pocket as I forgot about my anger and smiled, making my way to the table to take my shot. “No worry Fat Man…” I leant over and smacked the White Ball forgetting about what he said, Stupid fuckin’ White Ball… Instead of pocketing my remaining solid ball I knocked it near to the pocket and on an angle sending the White Ball into the pocket as Udai smiled and picked it up preparing to take two shots. I backed away from the table and glared at him as he smugged sheepishly, Fuckin’ think he can win it HUH?! He win it we see how he do in a fight… He took his shot and grimaced as he smacked the 8 Ball into the pocket, handing me the game as I felt a sinister grin creep over my face. “Good game Master Loc, hopefully our victory over PCW will be much easier!” Udai commented and chuckled forgetting about his loss. I smirked, “Thanks Fat Man…you just make my day!” -=***=- I not believe this…. I endure all the shit that get thrown my way and finally everything is paying off, this really not making any sense but I not care because I the one who winning the game….. No one can stop me now, I not going to be stopped! I can’t be stopped….. Not by PCW, not by PURE, not by TokyoPop…. I…am…unstoppable
