Settling In

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-03 One problem had been solved, a long lost gift had been found, and another problem was created. The room sat with silence. Something very unusual when you have three little kids in the room with you. Yet Alex, Christa, and Evan’s eyes remained on Tessa and Chris. The two adults seemed to be lost for words at the moment. Christa’s question still hung in the air. The little one stomped her foot down again and asked once more. “What about us?” Chris looked at Tessa and motioned for her to get up, the two rise to their feet. Chris pushes the boxes to the side and stands to the side, away from everyone. Tessa bit her bottom lip, trying to think of a way to fix everything. She looked back down at her tickets, hoping to find some way to make everything alright. Chris just rubbed the back of his neck. He didn’t think of the kids when he bought the tickets. He had screwed up again. How can he expect to help Tessa win the tag titles if he can’t even buy her a simple gift without causing problems. “Why don’t you three come with us?” Tessa said with a half hearted smile on her face. She looked over at Chris to see his reaction. Chris just looked at her with pleading eyes. He walked back over to her and gave her a small hug, moving his lips near her ear. Chris whispers in her ear so that the kids won’t hear him. “But Tessa, I was planning this to be a vacation for us.” He pulled his head back and looked at her in the face. They both had disappointed looks on their face now. Tessa looked past Chris at the kids. They stood their waiting for an answer. “We will talk about this later. You all still need to unpack and get ready for bed. We can talk about this in the morning.” Tessa finally said. To add to her point, she started to unpack the boxes in her room. The kids looked at each other confused. The littlest one had to speak up finally. “But what about me? Where am I going to sleep?” His voice was weak and hardly audible. Chris stood forward and walks up to Evan. He kneeled down in front of him and put his hand on his head. “You remember the room I was going to stay in?” Evan merely nodded in agreement. “Well that will be your room from now on. I’ll help you pack your stuff away in there in just a moment.” Little Christa responded, shocked that Chris was giving up his room. “But where will you stay Chris? Are you moving out?” Alex nearly fell over laughing. Surprised at his sister’s innocents. Chris smiled and Tessa frowned at Alex’s reaction. Alex answered Christa’s question between fits of laughter. “No dummy, Chris is going to be staying in Tessa’s room. They are going to be sharing a beeeedddd.” he said as he laughed and pointed at them. Tessa snapped at Alex for the rude comment but Chris only laughed. “Look you all need to stay at unpack. If you all get it finished soon I promise to take you all out to eat tonight. You all can pick the place.” Chris added. Hoping to fix everything and get them moving on packing away everything finally. The kids all shouted gleefully at the chance of getting to go out to eat tonight. They quickly scampered out of the room and upstairs to start unpacking. Chris turned back to Tessa and let out a sigh. She wrapped her arms around his neck and just stared at him, smiling. “Thank you” she told him. “For what” he asked. She just put her finger on his cheek and traced along the lines of his face. “For getting the kids to finally go unpack. And for the tickets and bear. They are wonderful.” she kissed him softly on the lips after her last word. Chris merely smiled to her. Glad she was happy. He gave her a light squeeze. Chris hung his head in shame a little. “I’m sorry though. I meant to give you that at Christmas. I packed it away to hide it from you and well I guess like they say, ‘out of sight, out of mind’” he told her. Tessa scrolled her finger down and under his chin. She lifts his head up a little so she can look at him in the eyes. “Chris there is no need to be sorry. So you forgot? You had a lot on your mind. Right now the only thing you need to worry about is beating P.W.A. and winning those tag titles.” Chris laughed and kissed her on the lips. “You are right. Come on, we have unpacking of our own to do.” he said. Tessa releases Chris and the two go about, starting to unpack their stuff. A smile was across his face as he thought about it. Things were starting to look up for him. Who knows what the future holds for him? Maybe he might add a little gold to his life? Then again, he was already planning for that. A devious smile crosses his face as he thinks about it. Chris shakes his head and starts to remove the rest of his stuff from the boxes. His new home, his new family, his new life.
