Introduction to Smokie Tang(RP 1 of many)

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 28, 2002 (OOC Note: This isn't my best, but it's an introduction to what I have in store, hope you enjoy) {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up in a dark ally way where you see a bunch of old Vanilla records thrown about in it. It looks to be late in the day and Semore Smoke steps around a corner and steps into this ally. The ally was abandoned years ago, and the remains of that time still wait for any visitor in it. Along the ally way you see two doorways, one on each building, which makes the walls of the ally-way. At the end of the ally is a tall wall that no man could jump, even if they tried. Semore walks into the ally kicking around a few old albums that lay out on the asphalt. He walks around an old dumpster, which has rotted over the years and holds his nose as he does. After stepping around the dumpster he sees an old jacket. On the jacket is a cane. Semore picks up the cane and the jacket, which it rests on. He pulls a picture out of the jacket, as if he knew it were in there and looks at it. It’s a picture of a young man wearing that jacket, and in his hand is the cane that he holds in his other hand. Semore thinks back to the old days and then the flashback happens….}}}} Narrator: Well, this is the story of a man named Smokie Tang. I would know who Smokie is, since I was his best Damian, Dante Jackson. But everyone calls me Shrecky, so why not you? Lets go back to where it all began. The year was 1976, Smokie Tang had just been born in Johnston Memorial and boy were doctors puzzled. You see Smokie was born with some special brain or something like that. You see, Smokie could talk right after being born, he was telling people to fetch different stuff for him, he even told the doctor “Hello” after he popped out. Another thing I should tell you they found out, was that Smokie Tang couldn’t live without reefer. Yep you heard me, he couldn’t live without marijuana in his system. It seems that he got hooked on is in his mother’s womb when she did drugs and such as a crack whore. Smokie would never be like any other child, since he would always be high. Smokie’s father was a pimp here in Wilson, North Carolina and he pimped his wife, Mamma Rhett, on plenty of occasions. They used drugs heavily and so did Smokie. But time to move onto when we were eight years old. You see Smokie had this gift with woman, well mainly a curse. Where no woman could stand him, they would run away at the site of him. It seems that Smokie would be destined to live without a lover, he couldn’t even get a whore to do him for the right price. His father always kept Smokie on a strict leash, Pappa Shabazz would always hit Smokie with his pimp cane to keep him in line to always be bad and on the bad side of the law. And boy did Pappa Shabazz seem to be everywhere. I mean there was this one time when we saw this robbery in progress. I told Smokie, “Hey let’s go stop those robbers.” Smokie nodded and we started to run off, but then out of nowhere came Pappa’s cane and we stopped in our tracks. Smokie’s mom died when he was only nine years old, and that left him and Pappa Shabazz lonely. {{{{The scene, which had shown everything that he would say as he would say it, changes to Smokie and Pappa Shabazz sitting down at a dinner table eating soup. Across from Smokie is an empty seat where Momma Rhett would usually sit. He looks at Pappa Shabazz and Pappa just pats Smokie on his back…}}}} Pappa Shabazz: Yeah I miss her too. But we all have to leave this world sometime. {{{{Smokie slurps on his soup and then looks at Pappa Shabazz and says…}}}} Smokie: I’m sorry Pappa Shabazz. {{{{Pappa slaps Smokie with his cane and then says with a scorn look on his face…}}}} Pappa Shabazz: Don’t you ever tell me that you’re sorry. You don’t apologize, that’s not the Tang way! Smokie: Ok Pappa Shabazz… Pappa Shabazz: And? Smokie: I’m your bitch until you die. {{{{The scene fades to Pappa Shabazz lying in a bed and he looks to be dying. Next to the bed is Smokie on one knee keeping his father company…}}}} Narrator: Well as if by some fate, the next day Pappa Shabazz was on his deathbed. He had a bad run-in with a rival pimp and had been shot and stabbed several times for it. Smokie fights to hold back the tears as his father looks at Smokie and says to him… Pappa Shabazz: Look Smokie, you better not cry for me. You have to promise me that you’ll never be on the good side, always stay on the bad side of the tracks. That’s where your true roots are, and you need to take over my pimp service. Smokie: Yes Pappa Shabazz. Pappa Shabazz: And? Smokie: And I’m your bitch until you die. Pappa Shabazz: Thank you. Oh and Smokie, take this. It’s my cane. I know I brought you up hard, but it was the only way I could set you on the right path. You need to promise me that you’ll always let the whores watch after it, but never let a Virgin take it. {{{{Shabazz pulls a cane out from under the covers of the bed and hands it to Smokie. Smokie sets it on the floor beside him and then looks at Pappa and says…}}}} Smokie: Yes Pappa Shabazz. Pappa Shabazz: And? {{{{Smokie smiles this time as he says it…}}}} Smokie: And I’m your bitch until you die. Pappa Shabazz: Thank…. {{{{After saying that one word Pappa Shabazz chokes and then looks off into space. The covers stop moving up and down from his every breath and he lets out a long breath and never takes another one. Smokie just hangs his head down low as he gets up. He then just walks out the room as his father’s people prep Pappa’s body for disposal out at the shipping yard….}}}} Narrator: Well, now with Smokie Tang no longer Pappa Shabazz’s bitch. He was free to do what he wanted to do, and he chose to stick with his already chosen lifestyle. So with a joint in his mouth, and the cane in his hand, by the time he was seventeen, he was the King of Pimps around Wilson. But this is a story on a little trouble he ran into, that could have stopped that and could have changed Smokie Tang forever…. TO BE CONTINUED…
