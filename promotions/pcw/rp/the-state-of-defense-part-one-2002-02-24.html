The State of Defense: Part One

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/24/02 The State of Defense… RP#1 for Turmoil and March Madness Location: Former High Impact Apartment, Now Temporary Base of Operations: Calgary, Alberta, Canada…Prior to Sunday Sabotage Chapter One: How on earth does a Cocky, Outrageously Talented Canadian Saviour take on a Scientologist? “*BLEEP* THE CHASETRON 2002 AUTOMATED DEFENSE SYSTEM IS NOW ONLINE…*BLEEP*…INITIAL OBJECTIVES: NUMBER ONE: KEEP CALGARY CLEAN OF SCIENTOLOGY…*BLEEP* NUMBER TWO: ORDER DUSTIN A PIZZA…*BLEEP*” “Wow…this is like the best thing since the time that we trained a South American Tree Frog to be our Butler…” “Yeah…but then you had to go and tread on it, my dumb and excitable friend. Look, all things considered, wouldn’t you think that I’D rather clear Calgary of all Scientological activity?” “Oh sure…and you let a Multi-Thousand dollar piece of machinery go to waste?” ::. ~(Voice of Chris Chase)~Glancing out into the snow-wrapped street ahead of me, I smile. I mean, after all, wouldn’t you just simply smile at the wintery diorama ahead of you? Of course not, for once, I actually agree with all you unentertaining, drab cretins. So, of course, the question remains…why do I smile in such a way in the face of a snow-covered street, on a cold February’s day? Why, you really don’t know me that well now, do you? In all actuality, it’s the victory on this past Tuesday that I managed to pull off over Timo Bolamba, who subsequently defected to the OSW after failing to save face after such a loss. To defeat a man who was a PCW World Heavyweight Title contender is, of course, a tremendous achievement, and it impresses the right people. Heck, I really didn’t even need the win to impress anyway, but alongside the long list of people that have fallen to the righteous hand of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, it seems the inevitable conclusion to the disastrous affair between New Breed and Timo Bolamba… Inevitable? Sure, it may seem cocky, arrogant and certainly conceited to say this, but heck…Timo was sure to lose to a better man, an indomitable, spectacular man who lights up the PCW like an over-intensified neon light. Sure, you’ll thank me for bringing THAT up later. Anyhow, with light-hearted blatant self-promoting analogies aside for the time being, there’s more pressing matters afoot, demanding my attention like life-sucking whiners such as Tim Walden…of note, the defense of MY PCW Cruiserweight Title Belt. Defending it from what, you ask? I’m defending it from the situations it could face if it was sucked right back down into the jobberific life of the Scientologist reducing it to the level as such loser titles like that of the Extreme title, in other words, if Xavier Kannon held it again. After nearly a month, prior to the March Madness Pay-Per-View, Xavier finally get his return shot at the title…his only shot at winning this thing back. After defeating the likes of Xavier himself, Timo, El Diablo and other random jobbers…it’s hard for me not to say that I’m overwhelmingly confident about the whole thing…I’m on a hot streak, and I know it… There is a tap on my shoulder, nothing strange there, I’m used to being tapped on the shoulder by a multitude of random people, be it ‘Controversial’ Corey Williams, be it ‘Deadly’ Dustin Downey, be it Bozo the Clown. That’s not new to me. However, as I turn around from the window over-looking the street below, the apartment formerly of High Impact’s residence, most notably the change in interior design is extremely fresh on my young eyes. Standing behind me is Dustin Downey, natch, but behind him, is an almost strange, NASA type control center of minor proportions. A radar screen can be seen on the wall where Dustin’s cockroach farm used to be, and a map of Canada can be seen where there was once a replica of the PCW2 Continental Title. Folks, remind me never to lease the apartment out again, especially to the likes of Dustin and Rob Mannesmann. Buffoonery on a grand scale, by any means, is represented here…in this place that I USED to be able to call my home… Now however, times have changed. And with his arms wide open, Dustin introduces me to my supposed replacement….:: Dustin: Look Chris, with the ChaseTron 2002, you’ll no longer have to do any work at all…with concerns to ridding the world of idiot Scientologists. ChaseTron 2002: ~(Repeating itself)~ ORDER…DUSTIN…A…PIZZA!! ::. Dustin chuckles at his own slow-witted quips…pulling a small instruction leaflet from his pocket…reading: “The ChaseTron 2002 – The Way to a Friendly Future…for Blatant Self-Promotion”….:: Dustin: HA! Tom Cruise won’t see this one coming…now, let me see here… ::. Folding my arms sharply, I snort at the sheer prospect of being replaced by a no-good machine, a lousy work of engineering designed to simulate the likes of an inimitable Champion in myself. Hammering at the console of the machine, Corey Williams slowly rises from the seat…kicking the machine with his foot….:: Corey: Now…why couldn’t they put in a CONTROVERSIAL mode? Jeez…it just makes the whole thing pointless… Dustin: Don’t you go spreading around awful rumors about this awesome machine…Corey, look at it, isn’t it just the most beautiful piece of machinery that you have ever seen in your life? ::. Of course, as Dustin says this, Corey and I begin to pay a little closer attention to the Super Computer-type machine, until a panel falls off the side….:: Corey: It looks a little tacky to me, are you sure that you spent twenty thousand dollars on this piece of junk? Ah forget it, I’m going to kick back on watch some good Sunday afternoon television… ::. Passing beside me, I watch as Corey picks up the television remote and leaps over the couch, which had once been the centerpiece of the room. Now, with the hulking super-computer in the way, it has been relegated to a desolate corner where the likes of Dustin’s weird and wonderful pets used to reside. Taking a sneaky peek out of the window, I notice a long trail of animals crossing the busy, wintery road below the apartment…wincing as an armadillo is sent rolling down the street by an eighteen-wheel truck – what a tragedy. With a sharp questioning look, Corey looks up at me, as if waiting for a signal so that he could turn the television on with a simple flick of a button. Then, of course, Dustin comes crashing into the scene, tripping up on a wire, and landing on top of Corey on the couch….:: Dustin: ~(Muffled)~ Okay, that wasn’t meant to happen…ouch… ::. Corey shoves Dustin off of him, which is easier said than done, especially with Dustin’s head firmly trapped in between the cushions of the sofa. He struggles to get himself free, reaching down with his free hands to pull his head out of the sofa. When he finally does manage to free himself, he sends himself, one cushion, various coins and a wad of gum flying across the room…landing on the floor with a resounding crash. Picking himself up, and dusting himself down, Dustin begins to search for his instruction leaflet once more…frantically delving into his over-sized pockets. Meanwhile, whilst I sigh in sheer exasperation at it all…Corey places the couch cushion back into place, before turning on the television set that is literally a matter of a few feet away from the bright yellow coloured and stained couch that he’s placed himself upon….:: Dustin: ~(Pulling out the Instruction leaflet)~ AHA! Here it is…the ChaseTron 2002 instruction manual – the key to unlocking the vast potential of this potentially awesome device. Do you know exactly how important this is, Chris? ::. I frown cynically at Dustin, who makes his way over to the console of the Super Computer, gleaming in childlike exuberance. He halts in front of the machine, gawking at it in wonder as I reply…typically with an analogy of some form….:: Chris: It’s probably about as important as an Xavier Kannon seminar on the wonders of Scientology. Give me a break, Dustin…to think that a machine could replace yours truly in eradicating Scientology is completely beyond comprehension. Especially as far as you’re concerned… Dustin: Don’t be silly Chris…this thing could never truly replace you…and besides, this Bad Boy’s going to save you an awful lot of work in the near future, don’t you think? ::. To this slightly moronic question, I shrug…thinking to myself of the implications that could be imposed if this machine did actually take over half of what I do from day to day, in that ever-constantly altering routine of mine. With my work ethic, it’s easily understandable that I’d simply sit on the fence when faced with a moral dilemma of these proportions, whether or not to let a computer do my work. And then, I think to myself, what the hell am I thinking? For the sake of my pride, there is only one thing to do…and that’s to do it all by myself, if not beside New Breed. So, I reply to Dustin, in a somewhat thoughtful way, a cryptic way, as I spiral into the deep depths of thought….:: Chris: You don’t know the half of it, Dustin…now then, as far as this Cruiserweight Title defense is concerned… Dustin: ~(Interrupting)~ Ah…you needn’t worry about that Chris; the ChaseTron has it ALL under control. With the simple touch of a button, you’ll be locked into a world where active “Chase-bots” will take care of all your battles from now on… ChaseTron 2002: *BLEEP* CHASETRON 2002 IS ONLINE…NOW EXECUTING A VECTOR SEARCH: RANGE 20 KILOMETERS…*BLEEP* ::. Sighing at the very thought of this highly lame idea as the Super Computer whirrs and bleeps, I shake my head…smirking cockily at the very thought of proving myself against the machine, proving myself to be better than a collection of algorithms and A.I. interfaces, a Super Computer by any means. The fluorescent lights of the apartment begin to flicker slightly, due to the power consumption of the colossal machine and that of the television set. Looking over my shoulder, I notice Corey watching some re-run of Melrose place, or a drama of a similar magnitude, before once again turning my attention to the excitable Dustin Downey, who, seems more excited than usual. (Sigh) I’m willing to bet Xavier’s career on the fact that it’s all to do with aforementioned Super Console….:: Corey: Does that thing have a volume control button? If it does, would you turn the DAMN thing down…I’m trying to watch a CONTROVERSIAL television show, DAMNIT! Thoughts of Chris: Whenever did Melrose place become Controversial? Chris: For MY sake Dustin, you’re talking as if I can’t exactly handle the battles into which I’m thrown into, into which I usually leave as the victor. Are you a complete imbecile, or are you currently on Kannon’s Snapple funded payroll? ::. Ignoring my protest against the machine, Dustin begins to flick a series of buttons on the “Idiot-Friendly” console. The reflection of his blissfully ignorant expression on the console screen glares at me, as I lean against the windowsill, crossing my arms in the most arrogant way possible….:: Dustin: ~(Reading the Instruction manual)~ Hmmm…press button beta, alpha and zulu…before activating the Justified matrix. Now then, click on the Start Menu button on the bottom left hand corner of the screen, scroll up…and click the “Destroy Microsoft” button… ::. Pressing that final button on the screen, there is a loud roar from the top of the apartment, a roar sounding not unlike that of a space or ballistic missile. Turning to face out of the window, I frown in curiosity at the sight of a mid-range ballistic missile flying across the clouded sky…the contrails of which tearing a hole in the blanket of snow pouring down on the fair city of Calgary….:: Dustin: Oh…so THAT’s what that button does! ::. At this act of blatant idiocy…I smack myself in the forehead…not once, not twice…but three times…THREE TIMES DAMNIT! I tell you, this Dustin fellow is going to drive me off the edge one of these days….:: Dustin: What do you think so far Chris? Eh? Eh? Isn’t this thing awesome? I mean, look at what it can do…I tell you, if I were at Microsoft right now, I’d be running for dear life right now… Chris: ~(sighs)~ Oh brother…sure, it can destroy Microsoft, that’s a soft target if I ever knew one…but can it imitate yours truly, can it wrestle to the full extent of my talents? Is it blessed with an awesome set of skills that it can utilize to the nth degree? IS IT CHRIS CHASE? NOTHING can replace ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, nothing can imitate or copy me…I’m a unique, entertaining young man, and if that thing is supposed to be ENTERTAINING…then Xavier’s got a worthy shot at the PCW Cruiserweight Title. I tell you Dustin; it just isn’t going to happen… ::. Slamming my fist down upon the windowsill, I breathe heavily after that quick fire burst, that leaves my former Tag partner more than just a little confused and astounded. He continues to tap buttons on the console, in a random order…as if he had given up all hope. This is before I watch him throw himself onto the computer, banging his head on the buttons….:: Dustin: Ouch…Ouch…Damnit…ouch… ChaseTron 2002: *BLEEP* THAT DOES NOT COMPUTE!! THAT DOES NOT COMPUTE!! … *BLEEP* NOW LOADING ADOBE PHOTOSHOP VERSION 19.0…NOW DISCONNECTING SERIAL BUS…NOW EXECUTING MOCHA.EXE. ::. Out of the side of the super computer, a small dispenser slides out, above another sliding panel with a coffee cup on top of it. The dispenser pours out steaming hot mocha, straight into the cup. Eventually, it over-pours, sending steaming hot mocha all over the carpet below, and causing the computer to bleep a few more times, as Dustin continues to mash his head on the console buttons….:: ChaseTron 2002: *BLEEP* ERROR 404…MOCHA MUG NOT FOUND…PRESS F5 TO REFRESH. *BLEEP* NOW LOADING NEWBREEDTHEME.MP3…PLEASE INSERT TOAST INTO THE CD-ROM DRIVE… *BLEEP* ::. And with that, ‘Bulls on Parade’ begins to play through the Super Computer’s speakers, as it whirrs and bleeps constantly, as I look on with an ever increasing smirk on my face. Suddenly, there is a slight yell from behind me, followed by a flying object of some sort that smacks Dustin in the back of the head….:: Corey: For CRYING OUT LOUD!! STOP IT!! Dustin: Huh? ::. With one final smack against the buttons of the console, Dustin pulls himself up…and rubs his forehead wonderingly. Swiveling in his seat, he looks at me with that same confused, dumb-assed look of his, which also comes as standard with your usual moron…befuddled at all the hubbub going on around him, as if he couldn’t explain it for himself. Looking at the room, he spots the mocha stain, along with a long trail of thrown objects, notably paper and other forms of pulp-based type materials. I sigh to myself, for the most part at the sheer volatile nature of this computed system, this useless, inane thing that sits right before my very eyes. How on earth this could ever match up to my potential…I don’t know. However, as I manage to catch a flying mocha cup, and take a sip…maybe there is some use for this piece of junk after all….:: ChaseTron 2002: *BLEEPS* CHASETRON 2002 IS NOW PREPARING FOR STANDBY…IT IS NOW SAFE TO TURN OFF YOUR CHASETRON…*BLEEP*…WELL…DON’T JUST STAND THERE, TURN IT OFF DUSTIN YOU MORON! *BLEEP* ::. Dustin shakes the cobwebs out, then reaches for a switch beside him…turning off the machine without second thought, if that could ever be applied to Dustin…to whom ‘Second Thoughts’ are things that only happen in Romantic movies and in the land of Oz. He rises from the seat, with a blank expression on his face, before falling to the floor…onto his knees….:: Dustin: Damnit…what a damn waste. And to think that this was a newly refurbished apartment…and I got rid of the hamsters…all those darn hamsters…~(Rising to his feet)~ Oh well…say, Chris? ::. In these promotions of mine, this is usually the point when Dustin comes up with some hare-brained scheme, either to get noticed, or to scare the pants off the down and homely folk…or to annoy innocent aristocrats. Dustin, after beating himself around the head for a while, now looks bright, and enthusiastic again…an expected phenomenon since Dustin has the attention span of an 8-year-old child. Once again, I’m tapped on the shoulder…this time from Corey, who had subsequently turned the television set off…and was awaiting something to fill up his free time that involves gambling, drinks and women. Of course, in a former High Impact apartment, that isn’t exactly possible….:: Chris: Please tell me that you don’t have another hare-brained scheme again Dustin, my head’s still numb from the last one…or was that from the last time that Xavier Kannon showed his ugly mug on Primetime Television…either way…ugh… Dustin: No, No, No Chris…I’ve something far more lucrative AND entertaining in mind…actually, not in mind…I sort of had it written down. ::. Dustin fumbles around in his pocket for a second, whilst Corey and I look on, puzzled in most ways at the very thought of what Dustin could possibly have planned for Corey, myself and possibly PCW World Heavyweight Champion, Jason Snow…in lament’s terms, the whole of the New Breed. With enough brain power devoted to that, I also begin to consider tactics for Xavier’s latest comeuppance…like there was anything else better to consider…other than the PCW World Heavyweight Championship match at March Madness, which is little more than a week away now….:: Downey: ~(Pulling out and reading from a slip of paper)~ Well…guys…what do you say to a once in a lifetime opportunity? Thanks to the ChaseTron computer system…it’s figured out the perfect destination for us…now…before I tell you…are there any questions? Chris and Corey: WHERE THE F*CK WAS SNOW? ::. Fading away briefly from this scene, the camera travels roughly a mile away, to another apartment building in Calgary. There, standing amongst a crowd of others, is Jason Snow, a rather confused Killin’ Villain at that…looking at the faces around him, he suddenly exclaims his controversial catchphrase, in sheer shock at the sight before him…I mean, heck, I would too be shocked if I saw this sight….:: Snow: OH MY GREATNESS! WHO THE F*CK CLONED CHASE? ::. Now, it suddenly dawns on you fans watching at home…that a whole host of Chase-bots surround Snow, all staring at him with that same old arrogant smirk of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…and as the scene finally fades out…they have just one thing to say….:: Chase-bots: *BLEEP* AND YOU CAN JUSTIFY…THAT! *BLEEP* ::. And closer to home….:: Chris: ~(sighs)~ And you can Justify…that! ::. Fade to White!.::
