The Robot of Fairness Lives!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #3 TT, #1 PPV Last time we encountered Rush Mason, he was part arguing with his friend Jordan X Shabazz and part tinkering with some unknown object. The object is yet to be revealed, though it may have something to do with Rush’s refereeing and wrestling objectives for Tuesday Turmoil and the up-coming PCW Pay Per View, Wrestle Vessel. “What the hell is it already?!” Yes, those are the wonderful greeting words that are first heard as our scene opens up. Rush is still tinkering with his mound of metal that is supposedly his new invention, while Jordan X Shabazz is tapping his foot against the floor impatiently, waiting for Rush’s unveiling. Jordan: George Washington Carver wouldn’t have taken this much time to make a sandwich. Rush goes through his memory, scratching at his slightly stubble-filled chin, trying to remember who George Washington Carver is. The knowledge is probably somewhere in the hours of lectures and scolding from Jordan on the importance of black history. With a snap of his fingers, Rush has remembered and quickly responds, as though he had the knowledge throughout. Rush: Well that’s because George Washington Carver made things out of peanuts. His idea of a sandwich was probably a peanut in between two peanuts. This, not surprisingly, just brings a shake of the head from Jordan, who has almost given up trying to teach Rush anything, no less black history. Rush looks as though he thinks himself rather clever and goes back to his tinkering, with a Phillips screwdriver in hand. Jordan: Rush, do I really have to stay here and see this thing? I was really just about ready to watch the Malcolm X special on A&E. Rush puts a finger up, to signal that his friend only need wait a little bit longer for the invention to be ready. While Rush fiddles with his contraption, he speaks to Jordan, though not directly facing the man. Rush: Jordan, you ever think about possibly getting another role model? I mean, Malcolm X is great and all…but he’s dead… At this suggestion, Jordan’s eyes light up with almost a fire inside, and Jordan begins to shout, his voice rising, with passion in his voice. Jordan: The reason that the black hero is dead is because of the white man! Rush turns around for a moment, but sees the determined look on Jordan’s face and turns back around, not wanting to incur any more anger from Jordan. Picking up a wrench, Rush tightens a loose piece on his object, and then steps back from it. Rush: Good graces of Bon Dieu… It’s…beautiful. Well, beautiful might not be the word chosen by the normal bystander, or by Jordan, or by anyone other than Rush and Papa Shango. It looks a little more like a structured figure than its former mound-like phase. As Jordan’s eyes cross it, the look measures somewhere between stupefied disbelief and incredible confusion. Jordan: Well it’s certainly…uhh…what is it? Rush looks at Jordan blankly, while Jordan tries to avoid Rush’s gaze now, sure that Rush sees something out of this piece of metal. Rush shakes his head as he points towards his creation. Rush: It’s a robot, you moron! Well, of course that is one interpretation of the metal object that sits in front of the two men. Jordan nods his head in understanding, though he still doesn’t see in what way it resembles any form of a robot. Jordan: Well Rush that’s really a great invention, but I gotta get running so- Of course Jordan is cut off before he can finish the sentence, by a wave of the hand by Rush, dismissing any thoughts of Jordan leaving before the amazing robot can be shown off. Rush: No way Jordan! You gotta see this thing, it’ll blow your mind. Out of Rush’s view and hearing, Jordan throws his head back and groans, while Rush works on making the robot operational. He fiddles with some buttons, and the robot begins to make strange squeaking noises. Jordan: This better not be another “Rush Mason Clonemaster 5000.” Shabazz refers to some incident of Rush’s doing involving inventions, but that’s an entirely different story, and Rush just nods his head, as Jordan breathes a sigh of relief. Rush: Hey! That was a good idea…I just mixed a few things up. It’s now Jordan’s turn to look back at his friend flatly and blankly, as Rush tries to look innocent, shrugging his shoulders and looking back towards the robot, which is supposedly starting to boot up. Rush: It was! Jordan obviously disagrees, but Rush’s thoughts quickly turn back to the robot, which is now blinking suspiciously and is in an upright position, its “face” becoming lit. Jordan: And you’re sure it won’t go ballistic? Rush gives a look that is supposed to instill trust in Jordan, but all that it accomplishes is to make Jordan fear the robot even more. As both men turn towards the machine, it blinks its robotic eyes and… Robot: Why hello Mr. Mason, Mr. Shabazz, I’m the Robot of Fairness. Rush pumps his fist in excitement, while Jordan just looks on, slightly wide-eyed, and Rush tries to start a conversation with his new toy. Rush: Hey Robot, why were you made? The Voodoo Villain can hardly contain his excitement and glee. Although it’s very strange to see a grown man jumping around like a little kid on a brand new trampoline. The robot’s mouth moves synonymously with his words. Robot: I was made to simulate the persona and judgment of you, Mr. Mason. Rush starts to dance around the room, ecstatic at his own amazing work. Jordan walks over to the robot to inspect though, looking over every part of it. To Jordan’s surprise, the robot reacts quickly. Robot: Mr. Shabazz, it’s lovely to see you. Jordan isn’t impressed at this though, and he decides to start testing the invention of its worthiness. He steps back and looks over the robot once again, before asking the robot a question. Jordan: Robot, do you like white people? The robot blinks the robotic eyes once again, processing Jordan’s words while Rush continues to dance around the room, looking more and more like an idiot. The robotic voice finally beeps back in a flat tone. Robot: I am fair to all people. At this, Jordan’s eyes goes on fire with fury again, and Rush stops his dance as Jordan starts to punch at the robot, while shouting loudly. Jordan:This robot loves white people! Taking a break from his celebratory dancing, Rush dashes over and pulls Jordan away, but then instead of trying to help his friend, he goes back over to the robot. Rush: Robot, are you okay? I swear to Bon Dieu that I won’t let anyone else hurt you, okay? Once again, Rush appears foolish as he tries to comfort the robot, something that doesn’t have a heart, while Jordan, a living breathing human, stands fuming on the other side of the room as Rush walks over. Rush: Okay Jordan…we’ve got a new rule around here. We don’t beat up the robot, oookay? Jordan spits on the robot as he walks out of the room and Rush follows behind, trying to calm his friend down while we hear only a robotic voice as we fade to black… “Yeah, that’s right, run. You don’t want a piece of this, buddy...'
