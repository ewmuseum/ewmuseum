The Cloning Of A Lost Love!

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27th, 2002 (Mike sits at a table staring deep into a picture which is covered by his body. Candles are lit around it. He sighs and looks at it. A violin begins to play in the background.) Mike Frampton: Nobody could match up to you. *Sigh* Mike Frampton: Why were we split apart? *Sniffle* Mike Frampton: Isn't there anyway we can be together? (Mike turns away and the camera reveals the picture of Michael Graves! Mike flips on the television. Mike puts it on the news and a woman is reporting. Her name is Sandy Long.) Sandy Long: We repeat the first human has been cloned! This is a medical marvel as the clone is identical to the human in age and height. Usually the clone is a baby but for some reason the technique used sped up the aging process. This is Sandy Long reporting. (A grin crosses Mike's face as he turns off the television.) Mike Frampton: That's it! (Mike rushes out the door as the scene fades to black. The scene fades back in as Mike re-enters his apartment. He leads in the clone of Michael Graves!) Mike Frampton: w00t! Graves it's great to have you back. Michael Graves: It's great to be back. Mike Frampton: Now are you the clone or the real deal? Michael Graves: Umm, I don't know! Mike Frampton: Judging by that answer I would say you are the real deal! Michael Graves: WOO! Mike Frampton: Indeed! Michael Graves: I have something to tell you. Mike Frampton: As do I. Michael Graves: I am not gay! Mike Frampton: I was gonna tell you the same thing. Michael Graves: That's a Nelson thing. Mike Frampton: ZING! Michael Graves: So like what's going on? Mike Frampton: Well I got my job back with the PCW. Michael Graves: Great! Mike Frampton: Yeah so I want you to watch my matches. Michael Graves: Money? Mike Frampton: For? Michael Graves: Watching the match. Mike Frampton: Well. Michael Graves: PLEASE! Mike Frampton: Alright. Michael Graves: WOO! Now when is your first match? Mike Frampton: Tomorrow! Michael Graves: Yeash! Any chances? Mike Frampton: No biggy. You know me, cool and easy and nice and breezy! Michael Graves: Eh? Mike Frampton: I am the comic relief! People need that. I am the Jerry Seinfeld of the PCW. Michael Graves: Seinfeld? Mike Frampton: Alright I am the George but unlike George I win many times! Michael Graves: George? Mike Frampton: Oi! (Fade to black.)
