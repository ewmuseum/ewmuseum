Demon Chair

8 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th April 2004 A bare church hall, very plain, very boring. But it’s a bit busy; in the middle an oval of thirty or so chairs is formed. Every seat full except for two at the head of the egg shaped circle. A man near the top seems to take charge. He has curly brown hair surrounding a great big shiny bald patch. He has thick glasses frames and is wearing a blue and white polka dot cardigan. “Hi folks.” Everyone listens as he speaks, as if he was the leader – he probably is. “Hi Dave.” The whole room respond harmoniously. “Now today at the Saint Charlotte Church of Christ we have two very special guests to attend our bible reading club this week.” He slowly says in his wispy southern voice. There’s a short hustling and bustling from the audience in query of who it is. “Now I bet you’re all excited but there is no need to anger God by talking when someone else is speaking is there?” “Sorry Dave.” The whole room apologises in unison. “You are forgiven, children.” He stands up and motions his scrawny little finger to the door “now children, you may recognise them off of PCW wrestling. They are Eric Sagar and Propaganda.” There is an applause from everyone in the room as the monstrous Propaganda strides out of a swinging wooden door behind him he is followed by the short and plump preacher that is Eric Sagar. Propaganda steps to the side and lets Sagar walk ahead of him. He waddles in front eyes up everyone in the room. Then takes a seat, Propaganda takes a seat next to him displaying no emotion at all. “Hello Mr Sa-” Dave starts to speak “BROTHERS AND SISTERS! WE HAVE ARRIVED!” Eric Sagar roars triumphantly. “Now, the Lord in the heavens tells me that none of you have ever had the privilege of meeting someone quite like me. Is that right brothers and sisters? GIMME AN A-MEN!” “A-men” everyone in the room hollers. “Uh, actually.” A short man with tanned skin and long, straight ginger hair wearing a discreetly grey suit, trousers and tie, with a white shirt, stands up slowly. “I’ve met Bruce Willis.” “Sit down, shut up and hope the lord doesn’t strike you down RIGHT NOW!” Propaganda is about to rise to his feet in attack, Eric Sagar commands him down by pressing his arm against the seven footer’s chest. Sagar glares a burning hole through the man who sits down with his head hung in shame. “Now as you have never met anyone quite like us I am sure you will have all have questions. So go ahead ask away.” One black woman raises her hand. Sagar had not noticed her until now – as soon as he spots her his eyes widen in shock. He didn’t expect any blacks to be here. “Ummm, miss are you lost?” Sagar tries to be polite. “No, sir.” The woman responds. “Oh so you did want to be here?” A black Christian? This went against Eric’s stereotypes, to him blacks were drug taking nymphomaniacs. This was stuck in his head. He decided to just ignore it and answer the question. “Yes.” “What’s your question?” “How do you feel that the political organisation you were associated with, the R.I.S.P. has been dubbed nothing more than upper class skin heads.” ”I feel hungry. Next question?” He completely blanks the black woman making her feel unequal. Which is exactly as Sagar sees her. A middle aged white woman dressed very formally with blonde hair raises her hand. “Yes, you madam.” Sagar points at her. “Don’t you feel that the woman before me had a good point? Not only are you promoting Christianity but you are promoting racism and aligning them with each other giving religion a bad name-” “What’s your name?” Sagar asks. “Harmony.” “IT DOESN’T MATTER W-” Sagar suddenly stops his The Rock style shout. “Harmony. Were your parents hippies?” He scoffs. “Yes.” “WELL THAT EXPLAINS IT! NO WONDER YOU’VE TURNED OUT HOW YOU ARE WHEN YOUR MOTHER WAS PREGNANT SHE WAS SMOKING THE WACKY-BACKY! AND I BET SHE GAVE YOU SOME! I BET YOU HAVE SOME! POLICE! CALL THE POLICE! DRUG ADDICT! MAKE SURE SHE DOESN’T GET YOU WITH A NEEDLE!” Sagar shouts at the top of his voice. He pulls out his cellular phone. “I would leave now before I get a drugs raid.” “I’ve never been so offended in my life.” Harmony huffs as she strides out. “Take your friend with you.” He commands pointing at the black woman. Equally as offended the black woman strides out after her. The rest of the people in attendance are astonished at the audacity of this man who just strides into their church and offends some of their loyal members. Sagar puts his phone away and glares at Dave. “Mr. Sagar calm down.” “No! YOU INVITED ME TO THIS HUMBLE CHURCH I EXPECT A PURE GOD WORSHIPPING CROWD! INSTEAD YOU OFFER ME BLACKS AND HIPPIES! I DON’T KNOW WHO YOU THINK I AM BUT I WILL NOT BE MOCKED BY THE EVIL THAT INFESTS THIS WORLD! I AM NO LONGER GOING TO STAY HER AND BE EMBARASSED!” As if by command Sagar jumps to his feet and a nail catches a chord on the seat of his trousers. Riiiiipppppppp! Eric Sagar’s flabby backside is hanging out of his trousers and is covered in spotty red silk boxer shorts. His face was red with rage now it’s beetroot with embarrassment. There is a slight giggle from the audience. He just glares. “W- WHAT THE HELL IS THIS!?” “Pardon, Mr. Sagar?” Dave asks trying to hold back a chuckle. “A DEMON CHAIR! YOU SAY WE ARE IN A HOUSE OF THE LORD! BUT YOU DECORATE IT WITH GODLESS FURNITURE! WHAT ARE YOU TRYING TO DO? CURSE US!? I KNEW THERE WAS SOMETHING WRONG WHEN I WALKED IN! IT’S THE DEMON CHAIRS! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN THE LORD COMMANDS YOU TO DESTROY THE DEMON CHAIRS YOU SIT ON!” Propaganda stands up, on command, grabs his chair and hurls it through a stain glass window. Coloured glass shatters everywhere like a bomb has gone off and the window cries in pain. Sagar gets out a lighter from his pocket and starts to torch this chair that has just ripped his trousers “No! Stop this!” Dave says as the everyone else starts destroying their chairs one way or another. “You’re ruining the church! Stop it!” Sagar takes a break from his arsonist tendencies to shout at Dave. “ARE YOU TRYING TO SUPPORT THE DEVIL, DAVE?” “No.” “THEN DESTROY A CHAIR!” “No.” “Brother Propaganda, eject this Satanist from a house of God.” “Certainly.” Propaganda says as he grabs Dave and like the chair, he hurls him out of the window. Sagar and the rest of his audience continue to destroy their chairs while singing “Kum-by-a”. So in conclusion, a quiet Church located in the middle of obscurity, which had a weekly bible reading has turned into a racially divided community with no furniture. Eric Sagar has left his mark somewhere else.
