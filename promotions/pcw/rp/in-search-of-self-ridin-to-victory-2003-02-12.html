In Search Of Self: Ridin' To Victory

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-02-03 Leaving the Lloyd Center Skitzo felt like a million bucks. He had just beaten, nay, DESTROYED the Totally Tubular One with his trademark 20” black rubber dildo, and ensured a place in the March Madness World Title Battle Royal match. This could be the closest he ever gets to a World Title shot in his whole wrestling career, and all with the swing of a dildo! With his trusty kitbag slung over his shoulder and a twinkle in his eye, Skitzo hopped into his rental car. The silver Audi TT Coupe was to take Mr. Psycho from Oregon to Boise, Idaho in three days. On Tuesday he started on his journey, leaving Portland and making the 6hr drive across Oregon, stopping on the way to pick up a large coffee and a Playboy…the essentials for a long car journey. Again, no PCWers vouched to travel with Skitzo and he was alone on the wide-open road. He didn’t mind though, there were plenty of people in his head to keep him company. Coming off this “big win” on Turmoil he was hyper, but took great care not to let the adrenaline go to his head and into the gas pedal. Two people now had succumbed to the rubbery goodness of Skitzo’s 20” friend. Statuz Quo and Totally Tubular Tommy, not bad considering a 20” black rubber dildo has a menagerie of other possibilities. In any case Skitzo was in the World Title match and nothing else mattered…except the Fusion clusterfuck. He had beaten TTT, THE PCW CONTINENTAL CHAMPION…nothing could stop him now. The world was his oxter! He looked out to the left, eyeing up Baker City Municipal. He was close to the border with Idaho, a thought entered his mind of border patrol, but then he remembered this wasn’t the America/Canada and he wasn’t a black man in love. The Audi was coping well with the road, after a near smash yesterday which almost saw a squirrel snatched into the black void of death after leaping into the cab of an 18-wheeler and trying to reciprocate with the driver’s ear. As the truck swerved, Greyhound coaches, sports cars, family saloons and many more vehicles were forced into the verge as the truck finally came to a stop…after hitting a children’s hospital. Needless to say the load of sulphuric acid didn’t hurt anyone…other than the 54 terminal children, but Skitzo saw that as more a release than anything else. Route 84 had been kind to him otherwise. The radio was blaring, Playboy was blowing in the wind coming through the window, flipping past buxom blondes and busty brunettes, and Skitzo’s coffee cup lay empty on the floor by the passenger seat. Skitzo was hyper, semi-erect, and tapping the wheel to the sound of Kinky Friedman talking about the woes of being in a situation with no toilet paper and finding a picture of Jesus on the floor of the stall…Skitzo was having fun by himself just enjoying the moment, there wasn’t anything that could affect his mood or ruin or day or…anything! That was until of course he passed the turn-off to State Highway 86 at 70mph, and noticed a small, scrawny man holding a sign. “Lake Owyhee State Park” Skitzo: HITCHHIKER!!! Jamming the brakes on, the Pontiac behind him was lucky enough to miss causing a pile-up as it skidded around Skitzo, flipping him off as it passed. Reversing smoothly back up to the man, Skitzo stopped beside the man, opening the door and nodding at him. The man simply stood there, his long, greasy black hair blowing in his face, his beard untamed, his face rife with wisps of age and wrinkles telling the story of years past. Skitzo cocked his head for a moment, and finally motioned for the man to get into his car. Walking forward a couple of steps, Skitzo realises he isn’t wearing any footwear, only a variety of smock, bland and brown. Finally getting into the car, the man sits down, buckles up and shuts the door. Skitzo: Hey buddy, the name’s William, what are ya headed to the State Park for? The man doesn’t speak, only looking ahead at the busy road. Skitzo shrugs his shoulders at the lack of reply, and revs the car up, checking the road for a space to drive in. Seconds turn to minutes, as the road remains busy, and Skitzo finds himself watching the mirrors like a hawk, meanwhile the man beside him sighs, looking at the road. Skitzo: So, you got a name? Or do I gotta call you Buddy? Man: Buddy will suffice… Skitzo: Ok…just give me some time this road is hellish to-… Buddy: You must grasp at an opportunity when it arises, William. Life is full of opportunity just waiting to be taken. You have to find an opening, to finally go somewhere new and be something else. Otherwise you are left waiting, waiting, waiting for something that isn’t coming. Are you going to take the opportunity awaiting you now? Looking into the busy stream of traffic, while wondering what the hell possessed him to say such a thing, a smile emblazoned itself across Skitzo’s face, and as a Toyota passed, Mr. Psycho slammed on the gas and roared into the busy lane, barely missing another Greyhound coach packed mostly with OAPs, who barely noticed the fact they almost met their maker. Cruising down the road now, Skitzo turned the radio up, glancing at the Playboy magazine flipping in the back seat now, while ‘Buddy’ sat watching the road. Being the conversational masterpiece as always, Skitzo goes for the icebreaker… Skitzo: So…do you like vaginas? Buddy: I am not a particular ‘fan’; I appreciate the female form as a whole-… Skitzo: Doesn’t every red-blooded male? Buddy: I see a woman for who she is, not what she has… Stunned silence. Skitzo didn’t know what to think, was Buddy gay, or effeminate, or Canadian. The day continued on its way, and Skitzo was enlightened as to the joys of turning a simple pornographic magazine…into litter! Buddy was also kind enough to urinate in the coffee cup and toss it out of the Audi, watching as it landed in a soft-top, splashing lovingly over a newly-married couple on their way to Vegas…or was it somewhere no one really cared about except the husband’s sperm? Another mystery woven into time no doubt. The day went on and Skitzo was finally within reach of the border to Idaho. Buddy had taken a nap for an hour or two, and was waking up. Yawning, he smiled at Skitzo, mumbling some kind of prayer under his breath and reaching his arms out, almost as though hugging the setting sun. Skitzo wondered where the hippy came from, but decided to just launch into another conversation, hoping it would lead to finding out more about Buddy. Skitzo: I once threatened a Native American with the foreskin of a sailor...got me whiplash and a fine for the theft of the skin... Buddy: What was troubling you so that you left you had to threaten one of our native brethren? Skitzo: ………I think you missed the point… Buddy: You are a very unique individual William, and again I thank you for taking me where I must go. I stood for forty minutes and forty seconds with no help from anyone. You are my Samaritan…thank you! Skitzo: As long as you keep your smock below knee-level, thank me all you want! Buddy: You are an entertainer? Skitzo looked at Buddy quizzically. They had never met before, and Buddy seemed to think Skitzo entertained people, how did he know? Skitzo: Oh, so you’ve seen me wrestle? Buddy: A Wrestler? You bring enjoyment through violence, that is wrong, William. Can you find no peaceful path in your heart to bring enlightenment to others? I myself am a wanderer, spreading the good word of-… Skitzo: Lesbian bees? Buddy: No-… Skitzo: Slightly anaemic, well-dressed Country nigga? Buddy: No-… Skitzo: President Bush’s vas deferens? Buddy: No, the good word of-… Skitzo: I can make my Willy wink… Buddy: Just drive my Brother…just drive… And so on the way to Buddy’s destination, and Idaho for Skitzo’s big clusterfuck, Buddy learned just whom he had come across. Fate had brought them together, and now Buddy was along for the ride, spouting wise words only to be criticised and shot down by Skitzo, who seemed more intent on finding a constellation in the stars reminiscent of a clitoris than discussing the afterlife and homosexuality with Buddy. TTT had been defeated, and now Skitzo was on the road to extreme something-or-other with Buddy…a mysterious man with more on his mind than a schizophrenic convention. Skitzo in any case had gone the distance and beaten the PCW Continental Champion. With his trusty 20” Black Rubber Dildo, there was nothing Skitzo couldn’t do…in PCW anyway… To Be Continued
