Me and Kano never got along

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/02 -A middle aged and crazy man sits in a rocking chair on a front porch of a colonel mansion out looking the Montana skyline. He drinks down his coffee and ponders life, love, and the pursuit of happiness. In his hand is a silver pocketwatch that he swings around as he talks into the camera.- Williams: I’ve rode side by side with a lot of cowboys in my day. Rough and rowdy was the path to success back then. Trust, a man’s word, and violence, was the law of the land. Things have changed now; no longer am I an outlaw fighting the law. I am the sheriff. The governor. The President. I have matured and grown. I take the side of what’s better for the whole and not the few. Unlike the late great Miyagawa I’m a true man of the people. So when one of my people are attacked viscously and unprovoked, I must act. Trust was broken. I thought I could trust my old friend Kano to watch and protect the sweet Ellie with some help by some kid. They failed. -Duke spits into the wind blowing due west.- Williams: They not only failed but also abandoned a helpless woman. I women of innocents, reputation and importance. Was a cabal the intention from the get go? They assassinated her by leaving her to the dogs. Now they must pay. They must pay a swift and malicious punishment for their actions. We in the Front Office must send a message to the rest of the terrorists that they will pay for their actions. -Duke swings the pocketwatch around and around as he talks.- Williams: I should just fire Blade’s little ass right now but then I could no longer make his life hell and what fun would that be? Plus he doesn’t know any better. Look at his ramblings called promos. He’s crazy. I can handle him with my pinky finger. I can cut his throat before he can even looks at me. I’m faster then a glace and more damaging then a tornado. This kid is a walking dead man. -Duke lights up a cigar.- Williams: Kano on the other hand knows better. He knows not to f*ck around. HE was a man I thought I could trust. That trust has been betrayed. He was a cowboy I rode side by side with. He reminds me of story from my youth between me and Billy the Kid. Kano acts just like Billy did, he is almost a mirror image. You see me and Billy the Kid never got along. He thought he was a legend too, plus I didn't like the way he cocked his hat and he wore his gun all wrong. -Duke lets out a puff of smoke from his cigar.- Williams: Billy and I we had the same girlfriend and he never forgot it. That little lady had a cute little Chihuahua until one day he up and shot it. The hoss had a silver pocketwatch that he never did wind. He was a mean son of a bitch who crippled the piano player for playin' his favorite song. When you get right to it, me and Billy the Kid never got along. Just like Kano when he rode with the Malice Crew what did he contribute? -Duke stares off into the twilight for a moment.- Williams: Me and Billy the Kid never got along and that is a fact. Now understand I didn't like the way he parted his hair and he wore his gun all wrong. You ever get that with somebody? Something so unimportant that it just drives you crazy. The way they eat, dress, breath, little quirks that drive you nuts. Billy was like me he was bad to the bone, all hopped up on speed. I would've left him alone if it wasn't for that Senorita. Just like you and I Kano a women gets in the way. That’s the way it always works. Ellie is Kannon’s lady but I respect them both. Now with me and Billy he gave her cash and he paid her hotel bill took care of her and all her needs. But hoss it was me she loved and she said she always will. I'd always go and see her when Billy was gone, yeah, me and Billy the Kid never got along. -Duke smile a sadistic grin.- The kid bugged me I didn't like the way he tied his shoes and he wore his gun all wrong. One day I said to Billy 'I've got this foolproof scheme' We'll rob the First Bank and Trust 'It's bustin at the seams.'. -Duke laughs out loud this time.- HA HA HA I admit that I framed him but I don't feel bad. Cause the way I was livin' was drivin' me mad!! Billy reached for his gun but his gun was on wrong. Bang hoss Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound yeah, Me and Billy the Kid never got along -Duke makes a gun with his finger and pulls the trigger.- Williams: I may not of liked anything about the bastard except I did like the way he swayed in the wind while I played him his favorite song. That little lady sang harmony with me To 'La Cucaracha'She wound up that silver pocket watch and loved her new Chihuahua. I moved in to the Hotel and got a room with a shower. She became my first wife many years back and I still remember when we would lay and listen to that watch tick hour after hour. Right now I hear the wind blowin' o so strong and it reminds me that me Billy the Kid never got along. -Duke swings a silver pocketwatch in his hand.- Williams: Kano and Blade I’m going to do what I have to do to make you pay. Life’s a dance boys and I’m the balladeer. But I don’t have time to teach you’ll the new moves hoss. I’ll stick with the old ones thank you. Tradition will be taught in the form of Malice on Turmoil. Blade what are you going to do? A loser from the Bronx, big deal. F*ck the Bronx and f*ck you. You’re no Billy Blanca for the Bronx. Your more like a nothing. You’re a nothing in the PCW, the world, or life in general. A bottom feeder, man. Part one thorough Ten I still don’t care. Your nothing but a twenty-two year old punk. I have titles older then you. -Duke keeps swaying the pocketwatch.- Williams: Oh Kano…he rocks….at least that’s what they tell me. Still haven’t seen. A little less talk and a lot more action huh hoss? A living legend? Me yes, you hardly. Who is going to remember Kano in five years from now? Not a damn soul hoss. So bring your game and do what you do best. Lay on your back and take the job. You can teach the kid Blade a lesson huh? Oh yea in the name of the Front Office you kids are going on a vacation. Whiskey bent and Hell Bound…Yeah, Me and Kano never got along. =w=
