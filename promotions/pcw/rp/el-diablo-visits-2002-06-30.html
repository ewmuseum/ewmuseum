El Diablo visits?...............

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June At Gold and Glory, El Diablo goes head to head against The End in another streetfight. So far in streetfights he reigns supreme in that classic victory over Flaymin Aymin last month. But now he faces a man that can use his senses to his advantage. The End may not be able to see, but in every other aspect he can certainly put in 110%. So what does El Diablo have in store for him? A building is in clear sight, on a beautiful blue-skied morning. The building looks kind of old, due to the dark gray and black bricks that were used to construct it. On the outside of the buildings are some small green bushes and an oak tree on a mini field, which has daisy flowers surrounding it. And on the outside of the building in small gold lettering above an arched doorway reads 'The Marchant School For The Blind' Inside the building resides one classroom where children of all ages are taught, the age ranges from four to seventeen, and some of their conditions range from born blind to recently blind. A sad state really, but good in how they can still be taught the basics of the school subjects. There are fifteen pupils in the classroom, all behind their own personal wooden desks, and standing at the front of the classroom is their teacher Miss Devon: yes she can see. On the desks in front of the children, are their brail alphabets. Some of the older children have books in brail in front of them so that they can learn to read some classics, such as 'Hard Times' by Charles Dickens and plays like 'Romeo and Juliet' by William Shakespeare. Magnificent how it is done. Miss Devon bangs on the desk in front of her with a wooden stick to get silence from all the children that were talking before class would start. 'Children, children,' she yells to gain their attention, 'Today I have a special surprise for you,' A child seated in the front row raises his left hand. 'Yes, Billy,' responds Miss Devon. 'Is the surprise that we'll have a school play again?' asks Billy politely. Miss Devon shakes her head, even though she knows that they can't see her do that. 'No, no Billy, this surprise is much better,' Miss Devon responds eagerly. The entire class let out a cry of 'awwwww' in obvious hope of wanting a school play. 'Don't worry children, we'll have a school play in a few months,' she reassures them. 'Yayyyy' is the response from the class. She taps the desk again with a wooden stick, to quieten down the pupils. 'But today, we have a special guest in class. He is a professional wrestler,' Miss Devon says hoping for a good response from the children. But the class stay near enough silent, until a child seated near the back of the classroom raises her hand. 'Jane?' asks Miss Devon, anticipating her question. 'Would it be PCW wrestler, The End?' she asks in hope for it to be so. 'No,' answers Miss Devon hastily, 'He's too busy to even consider coming here,' Jane sighs in disappointment, 'So who is it then Miss?' she asks again with less enthusiasm. Miss Devon walks over to the classroom door and opens it. 'Today's guest is PCW wrestler El Diablo,' Miss Devon says to the children, hoping for at least some enthusiasm. Instead the children boo at him. El Diablo walks in casually, wearing his 'Mexican Devil' T-shirt, black denim jeans and black sneakers. He obviously has his face paint on. 'The End?' asks El Diablo, 'What about The End?' Diablo walks to the front of the class while Miss Devon closes the classroom door. 'He's going to kick your ass,' replies the blonde haired, sixteen year old Tommy Martin. El Diablo just smirks. 'The End couldn't kick my ass off even if my ass was already on his boot,' jokes Diablo, 'But seriously The End has a 'me' complex going around, and at Gold and Glory, I'll show him why they call me the Director of Dictation,' Diablo wipes the sweat from his forehead and awaits something from these children. 'They call you that because The End has dictated his hatred towards you and you were too blind to see it,' says Tommy once more. 'Funny you should mention being blind,' says Diablo seething at the prior comments. Miss Devon is standing against the door watching in shock, and being fifty-seven years old, she feels she can't do anything about it to stop it. Tommy refrains from doing anything as he knows El Diablo will be able to take him out, if he tried anything. 'That was a low blow,' says Jane from the back row of the class. El Diablo pouts mocking the girl. 'Do you even know what a low blow looks like?' taunts Diablo to the child, 'Now that was a low blow,' he jokes. The children in the classroom start to boo him as Miss Devon gasps in horror of what is going on. 'Children, calm down, I'm sure he is just joking around,' Miss Devon calmly says to ease the children down. She motions for El Diablo to approach her, and he does so quite promptly. 'What are you doing?' she whispers to him. 'I'm messing around with them,' he whispers back. The teacher nervously nods and allows for El Diablo to continue. 'So just how good are your senses?' asks Diablo to the children. Tommy stands up to answer, 'Better than yours,' The class respond with an 'oooooh' Diablo nods with a grin on his face. 'So what did I have for breakfast?' ponders Diablo for an answer that he is certain the child won't know. Tommy starts to sniff in the direction of the Mexican Devil and waits several seconds before answering. 'Chilli cheese burritos,' he answers with a confident smile on his face. Diablo look shocked, so he walks up the kid and pushes on his chest, knocking him down into his chair once more. 'Lucky guess,' says Diablo, as he fumbles around in the pockets of his jeans. 'Hardly,' quips Tommy, 'And that is why End will beat you on Sunday. Senses,' states the child. Diablo steps backwards so that he is immediately in front of the entire class again. 'Oh yeah?' questions Diablo. 'YEAH!' the whole class shout. Miss Devon now has her head in her hands, regretting ever making El Diablo a guest speaker at her school. Diablo looks a little nervous now and off guard. 'Yeah, yeah, yeah, well, at least....at least I CAN SEE!' shouts El Diablo at the children. Miss Devon can't believe what is going on and is still watching in shock, not knowing what to do. Suddenly all the children stand up. 'Lets get him,' leads Tommy. Diablo looks nervous and starts jumping around the classroom as all these blind children attempt to grab him and punch him and kick him, but all so far are failing, and this brings a smile to the face of El Diablo. 'Oooh you almost got me,' El Diablo taunts the children. Miss Devon opens the classroom door and just leaves to stand on the outside of the classroom, obviously not believing what she is seeing. 'Come on, grab me,' teases El Diablo while holding his shirt out to be held. El Diablo then laughs and heads back to the front of the classroom. 'And that furthermore, is why I will beat The End,' he comments as the kids still search to hit him, 'Thank you and goodbye,' Diablo then calmly walks out of the classroom, but stops as he sees the teacher. 'That was fun,' he says to her, before heading out of the building.
