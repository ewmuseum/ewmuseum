Celebrate Good Times...

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th November Time 0632Date November 26th There it laid, the letter he had waited a month for, and the letter that he hoped was the bearer of good news. Kai stared at it, not sure whether he should attempt to open it or leave it, for all he knew the academy had released him… The typeface was ornately designed with large blue letters reading ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling.’ The envelope itself was a creamy shade of white with a rough sandy texture. Kai looked over the envelope, trying to picture glory, but he feared rejection. In ancient China, Kai would have been a lord less warrior, but in this quaint New York office, the master of Taijiquan sat motionless. The letter had traumatised him; it meant more then a contract or a letter of shattering proportions. No. It was much more then that. The saying, ‘A picture is worth a thousand words’ was meaningless; those few words printed within the envelope, sitting arms length away, were the equivalent of life over death. Kai removed his blue and white Walé Adiyemi beanie from his shaven head, and ran his hands over the contours of his skull. He stretched his hands towards the ceiling of the office and placed them behind his head. Contemplation. Not Kai’s best strength, he was often impulsive and lacked clear concentrated processes of thought, he could only obtain this state through deep spiritual meditation. This…this was hardly the environment. In front…a large oak, possibly teak desk, solidly built with the usual accessories for any lawyers office. A small, sleek personal computer stood on the right of the desk along with stationary items, which included a chewed black biro. A comfortable, relaxing chair completed the layers office ensemble. However, this was far from the spiritual area in which Kai needed to meditate. He was tempted to reach over to the letter and open it, but had previously been instructed to, “TO NOT TOUCH ANYTHING!” his eyes rolled, then his despair turned to a slight smirk. The secretary looked like a bulldog…she was small and well bulldoggish. Her manner was dogged and lacking of any etiquette whatsoever. Probably due to her fear of Chinese people, Kai began to laugh to himself. As he readjusted his manner, a slightly built male walked into the room dressed in smart attire, consisting of a black pinstriped suit, with white shirt and a black tie with a silver fern on. Kai acknowledged the Maori descent of the man and continued to examine his counterpart. The man approached his desk and looked towards Kai’s general direction, he raised an eyebrow, obviously expecting Kai to stand, or at least acknowledge him in someway. Kai sat there keeping himself to himself and glaring at the letter. A cough, Kai’s gaze transcended upon the lawyer, as their eyes met the lawyer began to sit down, either intimidated or just because his feet are tiring. Kai stares aimlessly into the New Zealanders eyes looking for some indication of whether the letter holds well or bad news. The lawyer, seemingly nerved by Kai’s permeable glare shakes his head and focuses down on the sheet before him. “So?” Kai asks the lawyer. His posture, seemingly more relaxed knowing that the lawyer is perhaps intimidated. “{coughing} I am acting on the behalf of Primetime…” “I gathered that idiot. Even with a degree in law…” “AS I was saying… I am acting on the behalf of Primetime Championship Wrestling, operating in accordance with the words within this letter.” Kai notices a name written on the pen in the upper pocket of the lawyers suit. The name reads, ‘Vaikona Reihana’. Another roll of the eyes from Kai insinuates his displeasure of the lawyer’s actions. Mr. Reihana reaches forward and picks the envelope up in his right hand. A small bead of perspiration drops down Reihana’s head. “Is it hot in here or is it me?” “Its you…” Reihana manages a smile, although he wishes to punch Kai in the nose. Reihana opens the envelope and removes the letter from within: Reihana Reads: Dear Kai Xao Kua, We are delighted to inform you that you have been accepted into our organisation, Primetime Championship Wrestling. You will be required to sign this letter and return it via the present employee. You shall be having an initiation match on Friday against a man known as Arden. Thank You. The letter is whipped from the hands of Vaikona Reihana as Kai rereads the information. He beams a grin, and looks towards the lawyer, who is sitting amazed at Kai’s decorum. Kai grabs the personalised pen from upon the desk and signs his name. Slamming the paper down onto the desk, he quickly runs towards the large double doors. The drone of, “PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA-PCDUBYA” can be heard down the hallway as the door swings close. //end prologue//
