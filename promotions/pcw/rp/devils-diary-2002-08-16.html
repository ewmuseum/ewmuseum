Devils Diary?

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th August (OOC: I was trying something different for this rp! So it's like a diary entry of El Diablo's thoughts. I hope it works well to get into one aspect of El Diablo's mind) Devils LogHell Date: 666.6 Friday Fusion soon approaches, a match of a lifetime awaits me. Today is the day I team up with Akiyama to face Xavier Kannon and Tyler Nelson. I look at it like this, I am teaming up with the #1 Contender for the PCW Title, against a former PCW Champion and the current PCW Champion, what more could I ask for? A win of course, but I know that there is more to life than winning, and that is to gain the upperhand, and somehow I plan to do this. But what can a 5'9', 216lb Mexican Devil do to gain an advantage over my own tag team partner- that overgrown behemoth known as Akiyama, as well as gaining the advantage on two of the very best PCW has? It will be hard, this I know, but when have I ever given up on anything? How can I even team with a man I don't trust?- Yes Akiyama is only a man, no God! Especially after his dastardly deeds done to me on Turmoil! Luckily enough I am fine, and I am fine enough to compete, just a few bruised ribs and some stitches at the back of my head! I know I have to team with that big freak, and co-exist with him in that ring, just like Xavier Kannon and Tyler Nelson must aim to co-exist, but I promise myself that I will gain retribution at his expense and show him why the Devil is always superior to the God. My head is spinning at where my career is heading, only three months ago I was lucky to be given a match, and now I'm headlining an event with three of the very best! Not to mention being able to face a legend in Duke Williams, a spandex wearing weirdo in Rent-A-Villain and two former World Champions in Chris Chase and Jason Snow- hell I'm going places and fast, and to prove it's no mistake, I will have to destroy the nVg Akiyama! Can I do this? Will I do this? YES! YES! I promise myself I must. As I write this, all that is on my mind is that match, and how I can try and find each one of their weaknesses! I know Akiyama is my partner, but I won't be surprised if he turns on me at any point during the match, and I must be prepared for any kind of assault from ANY of them. Akiyama He is a beast, but is very arrogant. I know he underestimates me, and I can only assume he under estimates Kannon and Nelson too. All are mistakes on his behalf, just like his belief that he is a God! Sure he is a great wrestler, very powerful, but I despise him like I despise Flaymin Aymin! There is so much I could say about him here it's unbelievable, but I'm sure I'd just start to repeat myself. El Diablo and Akiyama will make a great team, but sooner rather than later he will be rattled by me. His weakness is arrogance, and I won't take anymore of his crap. Xavier Kannon Surely I'm better than him! Maybe that's why he's never been able to fight me before now! I lasted longer than him in that March Madness Main Event, but then again so did everybody else! And what surprises me most is that he's managed to make a career out of that pathetic showing. Nevertheless, Kannon has proved himself to be one of the best, but I'd never let him hear me say that. What he's doing with this World Championship is a joke, he is making the whole company out to be a joke, and that does not make me happy! When I finally start to make my name in PCW, all he does is degrade it, and while he is degrading PCW he is degrading El Diablo, and I won't tolerate that. Kannon's weakness is his ego, and I'll be sure to show him a thing or two in the ring, that will make him question his ability. Tyler Nelson He's hired me and he's fired me! He's fired everybody and then some! And now he is making himself out to be some sort of saviour? Fine, whatever, I really don't care, but what I do care about is showing him up in that ring tonight! Is it possible? Anything is, and I'll be sure to prove that I am the right man to main event now. Nelson is skilled and very calculating, he's a living legend around PCW and has had many a PCW World Title reign. Only one thing is his weakness though is title, so somehow I can't see myself getting to him! Yes, I am in a huge match, and yes I am Main Eventing one more time, will I let this get to my head? Of course! Why shouldn't I? But I'm well aware of the bigger picture, and the bigger picture to me right now is getting my rightful shot as #1 Contender at that Continental Championship! End Devils Log!
