The Death Of Rent-A-Herp Part 3-Murderous Thoughts & An Appointment With Death

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Death Of Rent-A-Herp Part 3-Murderous Thoughts & An Appointment With Death Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 6-28-2002 Well your back for the second serving of torture are we.... Good, feast your eyes and senses on what you helped destroy... In fact, let me tell you what happened after the woman who supposedly 'loved' him left him high and dry, no explanation or goodbye given... Rent-A-Hero may have no said a word about how she left, but that dosen't mean it didn't hurt all the same you idiots. The moment he realized and came to grips with the fact that his 'redhead princess' had left him, it felt like someone to the sharpest blade possible and RAMMED it through his very heart. From that moment on...his death was inevitable. They say the most brutal death is a scorned man alone with his own thoughts...and to think it could have all been turned around with some compassion...a little love and help from a fellow man...but it didn't turn out that way did it. How about we get a look behind the Rent-A-Senses eh? Will that make you feel better...I hope it dosen't, cause right now...you deserve to feel like utter sh** for your part in murdering something righteous in this world... So your Rent-A-Hero...Aimz has just left you with no goodbye and has left you all alone...practically not even acknowledging the fact you even worked up the nerve and courage to PROPOSE TO HER. In so many words, what did she say... Hell no. I would never be with you. You are forced, no matter how hard it sounds, to come to grips with the fact that that woman never wants to spend the rest of her life with you...and she would rather leave you and never contact you again then be 'Mrs.A-Hero'. You've had some bad things happen to you in your life... The constant teasing from the kids because you wore a mask since second grade... Getting turned away at your Junior prom for the simple fact that you thought 'formal wear' was adding a bow tie to a full Green Lantern costume... Finding out you were never even the seed of a man and a woman...but some test tube freak failure... But NEVER...EVER have you had to deal with something that hurt you this much. Hurt you so much that you would forsake superheroing...forsake this semi-charmed thing you call 'life'....just to make this suffering stop. You don't have your strength anymore...your power has been snatched... You feel like Superman chained to Kryptonite... The Green Lantern without his ring... Superman devoid of Lois Lane... You look about at your room...and even though it's so full...it's so empty without her. The way she would walk into a room and amke it a party...the way she'd cuddle against you when you've had a hard day...the way her quiet whsiper would bend you to whatever her command would be....all of this is missing and without it...your just not you...your not Rent-A-Hero...your just a grown man in a mask. You are what you fear of being the most besides being completely normal... Being a complete FREAK... That's right, no need hiding it...Amy must have snapped into her right mind and realized she's been wasting her time with a FREAK like you. That's right 'superhero'...admit it...you have and always will be a FREAK. You didn't even get the dignity of being produced the proper way...you were just spurned up in a test tube and sent out in the world...and since then, you have been just a FREAK trying to blend in with normal people. The truth is...you never were a complete fool for even thinking you could walk alongside 'Jane and Dick' everyday in society. The stares, the gawks, and even the quiet snickers...you dealed with them for all these years....and all of that was woth putting up with... When you found her... Aimz was so special for a reason even unknown to her...a reason she NEVER seemed to figure out, but she did it everyday and it made you love her more...even though your not sure she felt the same....She accepted you.... She accepted you for being Rent-A-Hero. She 'loved' you, or at least feigned enough sincerity to seem as if she loved you, for you supposedly. When you were with her...you didn't feel like a FREAK... You felt like an equal... And in one fell swoop...after a night of torrid and extreme passion... She left you laying alone and cold in the world...back to where you started... FREAK to the entire world...As you lay on the bed with no will to move....no will to speak....no will to feel...you can't help but think how much easier you would have been able to deal with this revelation if it weren't for that fact that it took a woman that you cared so strongly about... To have to LEAVE you for you to figure this out. It would have hurt so much less if LOVE didn't drag itself into this whole thing... Is a pay-per-view even coming up this Sunday? Do I even have a match? You don't and can't remember...cause the one all-consuming thing to you at this very moment... Is why Amy...WHY COULDN'T YOU SAY GOODBYE DA** IT!?!!?! It would have given you so much more dignity...so much more pride...if she just had the decency to look into your eyes with those dark jewels of hers and mouth 'Goodbye' to you. Maybe then...you could take this so much better...but she didn't. And to think, that one word could change the entire way you feel right now. You might ACTUALLY have some confidence in your matches...you might actually have confidence in YOURSELF...but it's not there all because she couldn't look you face to face and tell you to just 'screw off' with some dignity. You want to hate her...but you never will...you love her too much...no matter how much she dosen't love you back. Where's that Rent-A-Strength now? It faded when she faded. Now you know why Batman NEVER got himself attached to a woman...Because as soon as she scorns you...you are nothing. Tag titles...they mean nothing. Wins and losses, screw that. Putting on an incredible match, none of your concern. Your only concern is why she would do this and why she would destroy something this beautiful. As you lay there....you'd imagine how nice it'd be to just be able to accept being 'normal'. Yup... No masks No dumb catchphrases No 'powers' Just a normal nobody that the world would gladly accept and love.... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
