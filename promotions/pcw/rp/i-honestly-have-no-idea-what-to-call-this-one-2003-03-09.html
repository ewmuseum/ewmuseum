I Honestly Have NO Idea What To Call This One

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: I Honestly Have NO Idea What To Call This One Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 09/03/03 “Soooooooo... You’re Bi-Polar then?” Our shot opens up on the bored face of PCW’s Xavier Kannon as he waits in Ellie~!’s newly acquired Gym for her, standing beside the OSW ring she’ll train for Queen of Extreme II in. Trying to make conversation with the Bi-Polar squared circle, he finds it hard going. Ring : “Fuck talking. Fuck conversation. Fuck dialogues. Fuck chatting. Fuck having a good ol’ natter. Fuck exchanging words of a similar language.” Seeming confused, Kannon questions the ring, although trying not to anger the 99% of it’s psyche that will just want to kill or rape him. Xavier : “So what’s left after that?” Somehow, although in a way that can’t really be described in text form, so you’ll just have to trust me, the ring shrugs. Ring : “I dunno, I’m just here to say “Fuck” and try to kill things.” Raising his head and “Ahhhhhhhh”ing as if to say “I see”, Kannon checks his watch. His eyes roll as he sighs, wondering where his wife could be... Realising that could be anywhere throughout the universe during any time period, he stops that fast. *CLUNK! CLUNK! CLUNK! CLUNK!* Kannon’s eyes narrow as he tries to work out where the rhythmic thumping is coming from, each thud followed by a shake of metal. Xavier : “Please say she isn’t trying to enter Robot Wars as herself wrapped in Tin Foil again...” Fearing the worst, Kannon’s green eyes don’t remove themselves from the doorway where the door shakes on it’s hinges with each thud. Ring : “Fuck thuds. Fuck bangs. Fuck bumps. Fuck smashes. Fuck cras-” Snapping around at the ring, Kannon growls. Xavier : “I GET THE PICTURE!” Offended and enraged, the ring scowls.... Well, it doesn’t technically move, but once again, trust me here... There’s a definite sense of a scowl. Ring : “BLEAUGH!!!”(Translation : I’m Bi-Polar and I’m going to stab and rape you.) The thuds get more intense, shaking the very foundations of the house.... Xavier : “Now I know how the neighbours must feel twice nightly and once in the morning.” ....Before the doorway is sent flying, smashed brick being hurled in every direction as a giant metallic figure in a tusked mask crashes through... Pure evil intentions emanating from it. It must surely be about to kill Kannon, seeing as it’s tall... And big.... And has horns... And stuff. Xavier : “Ellie~!?” Okay, I lied... It’s Ellie~! in a suit of armour, a tea tray breast plate and an Elephant mask, complete with 6” rubber tusks. Observing his wife in her ‘suit’, Kannon tilts his head to the side. Xavier : “Dare I ask?” Through the eye holes in the Elephant mask, Ellie~!’s pale blue eyes transmit the beaming smile she has under the rubber. Making her most threatening pose, Ellie~! then replies in the gruffest voice she can... Still a little more whiny than anyone else’s. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!? Hell to Xavier, come in?!??!?” She motions a telephone call as Xavier just puts his hands on his hips, waiting for an answer. An answer that Ellie~!’s body language and breathless moan are making seem the most obvious thing ever. Eleanor : “I’m, like, SO Suicidal, Homicidal and Genocidal!” Trying not to disappoint her, Kannon lets her down gently. Xavier : “Er... My little Poon Puppy, not saying you’re wrong or anything... Even though you are, that’s.. Um.... That’s Sabu.” Taken aback, Ellie~! questions him with her head cocked. Eleanor : “It is?” With her man nodding, Ellie~! thinks about it for a second then shoots her index finger up into the air as a light bulb flashes up above her head... Well, except for the light bulb bit, and anyway, in Ellie~!’s case it would probably be more of a spent match. Eleanor : “Ooooh, yeah... I mean, I’m Titanic, Satanic, Insanic!” With an “Of course you are, my dear” look on his face, Kannon nods. Xavier : “Oooookay.” The ring then decides it’s time to butt in with it’s oh so variable wisdom. Ring : “Fuck Titanic. Fuck Satanic. Fuck Insanic.” With a grumpy look under her Elephant mask, Ellie~! plants her hands on her hips, not seeming happy with the squared circle. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?! Titanic, SO ruled! With the boat, and the sea, and the DiCaprio... It was WAY cool, could of almost, like, really happened and stuff!” Not sharing the same movie taste as the little Prep, the ring decides to give it’s long awaited movie critique of Titanic with such effluent and dynamic use of the wonderful English only it can give. Ring : “Fuck DiCaprio. Fuck Winslett. Fuck Zane.” Tired of the ring’s act.... Understandably... Ellie~! puts her foot down. Eleanor : “Like, shut up and stuff or I’ll reveal that all the molecules of Nitrogen and Oxygen in the air were SO really Goths like me in disguise and they’ll all kill you and stuff as part of my WAY master plan!” The canvas sags in the middle of the ring then shoots right up to symbolise a gulp. Eleanor : “Now, say you’re, like, SO sorry!” But the ring is Bi-Polar so can’t be sorry, obviously... DUH! Ring : “Fuck sorry. Fuck apologies. Fuck regret.” Screeching through her Elephant masks and banging the tea tray hanging from her neck with her fists, Ellie~! calls for her master plan to come into fruition. Eleanor : “GO! GO! GOTHS! YAY YOU!” At the command of their GothPrep leader, from thin air a hoard of Goths... Well, about 100 people who all dress and black and look like they haven’t smiled since, well, ever... Appear and start to descent upon the ring. Ring : “Fuck this!” It’s too late however as the Goths attack, acting as a kind of depressed ring crew to dismantle the Bi-Polar construction as Ellie~! pulls her Elephant mask off and grins and waves to him. Eleanor : “BYE!!!....Polar! Get it, Bye Polar? YAY ME!” As she jumps up and down on the spot to applaud herself, Kannon watches Ellie with a look of pure bemusement on his face. Noticing his look, Ellie~! ceases then skips over to him, happy look still on her face as she flashes him an innocent smile. Eleanor : “Okay, you can teach me to wrestle now! If I‘m SO going to be the OeQ woman I’ll need to, like, do moves and all!” Trying not to be distracted by the sound of 100 Goths taking a ring apart using only their bare depression, Kannon looks surprised. Xavier : “I’d almost forgotten I hadn’t actually taught you any wrestling yet.” Chirpy grin on her face, Ellie~! nods. Eleanor : “Yeah, we kinda dragged it out well... OH! I know, you should, like, SO reach me to wrestle in the next promo and stuff!” Weighing it up, Kannon likes the idea. Xavier : “Fine by me.” Taking his wife’s arm, the happy couple walk out of the Gym, leaving only the moaning and whining of the Goth ringcrew and the eloquently put protests of the ring... Ring : “Fuck dismantling. Fuck being taken apart.” ....As we fade to black. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
