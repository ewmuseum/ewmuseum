Where do all the jobbers come from? Find out!

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March something 2003... A film projector clicks as it flashes up on the wall of some old rusty, dusty garage. It was an old fashioned black and white movie with the '10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5 ETC.' coming up. The black and white letters ticker to number 2 as the screen flashed up. A 60’s black and white picture of a young boy on a unicycle flashed. 'Wrong video, dumbass!', said a deep voice from behind. The film changed, still old fashioned and old, and the voice of the man talking on the screen was an old fashioned bad audio version of a 60’s camcorder movie. Welcome viewer. You will get a tour of the beloved 'Jobber Factory', on screen! Utility belts came up with metal heads on them rolling down as people crowded around the, adding the traits to the face. This the design room. People get sorts of body parts and add things to them. Everything is a robot, and will learn to move, walk, talk….and job. An image or Young Tiger came up. Stars, or jobbers, like Young Tiger… Jan Kano came up. Jon Kano… Tim Walden came up. Tim Walden… HANK~! Came up. HANK~!… An image of Pete came up. And Pete all originated from The Jobber Factory. We hand selected each piece perfectly to get the perfect jobber to join PCW and job. The beloved jobber entertained fans, wrestlers, and people world wide with losing the matches they were in. The jobber is a strong profession, loved by many. Many feel sorry for the jobbers, don’t though. They’re robots. A metal head was getting add-ons like hair and eyes. Our busy staff designs each head and it’s traits. The eyes, mouth, hair, legs, chest, stomach and genitals are carefully designed. A man in a white robe was designing a wrestler’s genitals, which were very small. People design to genitals to make it seem like the person is real…Not! We design the genitals small and puny to make sure that jobber doesn’t get too busy, you know? A woman with safety goggles was connecting the pieces together. Our staff connects the pieces together to make a perfect jobber, a work of art The screen show a few crying men and women. A perfect jobber is a work of art…The beauty of seeing a person lose every match, and never win brings a tear to every staff members eyes. The art of making a perfect jobber is astounding, true, and heartfelt. How could real people win if there wasn’t the jobbers? The screen showed Young Tiger getting pinned…1..2…3. When fans crowd around the screen, and see people like this lose…Well, they probably laugh, make insults, and point their fingers and scream 'He sucks!'. To entertain fans around the world is a dream our staff wants to accomplish, and they have done it went the jobbers. The screen showed a robot, or a jobber, falling apart into bits. Please, for jobbing to continue, we NEED your support to make our jobbers more valuable, made of better material, and so we can continue to make more. However, with dying funds, we can’t do it! Please, select a jobber from our list and choose to support that jobber. 5 cents a day. 5 cents! Less then your cup of coffee, a much as a gumball from a vending machine. That gumball could entertain fans around the world, and prove the jobbing profession is a great profession. So, dial 1-800-JOBBERS and choose a jobber. Young Tiger has nobody supporting him. He needs your help! Tim Walden is falling apart, and so is Jon Kano! Your part is just 5 cents, that’s it. A smiling jobber came up. 5 cents…Could make a jobber, or future jobbers, more proud of themselves…Sure, they don’t have true feelings, but pretend like it. They could job forever! So, fill out this form and send to '[detail withheld]' or IM 'Pined Away 9' on AIM. Your Name____________ Jobber Name___________ How did you find us?___________ Would you like to sign with our newsletter? _____________ Now send…Don’t be afraid. Send your jobber form and support a jobber today! A cheesy parade was going on. If you sign up, you’ll also receive a FREE 'I Helped Jobbers' T-Shirt, free of charge except for Shipping and Handling. Let the world KNOW you supported a jobber, and support jobbers around the world, today! The projector flickered to a halt where a black wall stood. 'Hey! What was that?', said a voice. The camera turned where Young Tiger stood, long black hair wavy and eyes confused. The cameraman mumbled something. 'Uh…I was…Just looking through the tapes…' 'Oh….NOBODY needs to know I’m a machine though…If your camera on?!!' 'Not that I know…is it?' Eddy looked at it. 'The lights on!' 'But it’s off…' Eddy looked confused. 'Well…Ok…But tell nobody about this, OK?' 'OK…' The camera finally did get turned off…But that’s your evidence folks!
