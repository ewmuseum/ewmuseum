Scattered Life (part2)

2 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-2-03 There Brianna was, still standing blankly out into space after hearing that her new boyfriend, the man that she thought was what she needed to make life better, had a son that he not only didn't tell her about, but never took care of in the first place! As it all runs deep into her mind Brianna goes ballistic, running into the house, slamming the door and making many grunting sounds as she vents her frustration. Several hours later we see the lights turned out and she is sitting on the chair in the living room, Indian style staring at the door waiting for it to open. Finally it does and Raphael comes in, turns on the lamp near where Brianna is sitting, and is shocked to see her staring up at him. 'Geesh Brianna! You scared me girl! Why were the lights off?' he waits for a response but doesn't get one. He bends down to give her a peck on the cheek but she pulls away. Brianna looks deep into his eyes and then turns shaking her head looking down to the brown carpeting. Feeling concerned, Raphael drops to her side and turns her face towards him. 'Baby what's wrong?' 'You tell me. I mean one minute I think you are being a hundred percent honest with me, and the next I find out you're not. So you tell me why you didn't feel like mentioning your son was very important.' Raphael's face scrunches up as he stands back up looking all around trying to find the right words to say. 'Samantha was over here I take it?' 'Unless there is another girl you refuse to tell me about. From what I hear you haven't seen your son and stuck her raising the poor kid all by herself. When...if when...were you planning on telling me? Or were you just going to keep it a secret?' she asks sincerely. 'To be honest...never.' Brianna's eyes bulge as she shakes her head, stands up and heads for the door. 'Brianna, it was just a one time thing. She is just saying I am her baby's father because she sees that I am stable and happy. She wants to take that away from me. She wants to drive you away from me thinking she still has a chance which she doesn't. Brianna you have to believe me!' Brianna reaches for the handle of the door and pulls it open. She takes a step and feels Raphael's eyes focused on her wanting her to stay. She tilts her head a bit and then turns sideways facing Raphael once more. 'I don't know what to believe. All I know is that this is too much for me to worry about right now.' She leaves out the door and Raphael follows her but stops on the porch as he watches Brianna sprint down the street. He hangs his head in shame before shutting the door. Yes my plan worked! Now I swoop in to make the save and it all works in my favor. Finally Brianna will be mine like she was supposed to be, and she will see that the first time she made a mistake by turning me down. Brianna continues to walk down the dimly lit vacated street with her hands in her pant pockets trying to keep warm. A silver car pulls up by her and honks catching her attention for a mere second. 'Oh god not him, not now.' She walks faster and the car follows her, going very slowly. Finally the car stops all together and a figure gets out. Thomas walks towards Brianna and and stays by her side as she continues to walk trying to lose him along the way. 'Brianna what’s wrong? You seem upset.' He asks with much emotion. 'I don’t want to talk about it.' 'Was it Raphael? Did he do something to you? Tell me Bri and I’ll set him straight.' He pulls Brianna near him and for a brief second Brianna is happy to have Thomas there...that is until they are approached by a woman. 'I told the chick that I had a son and it was that guys now where's my money? You said fifty so cough it up.' The woman is the same one that Brianna had encountered earlier that day. Brianna looks on in disgust as Thomas pushes the woman out of the way to run to Brianna's side. Brianna looks at him and then slaps him in the face. 'You set me up you son of a bitch! Well I guess you did throw away fifty for nothing, because I found out that Raphael really does have a son, so to hell with both of you.' She pulls out some change from her pocket and boards the bus that stops there. Thomas follows the bus with his eyes as he realizes what just happened. The lady continues asking for the money, before Thomas digs into his wallet and tosses is at her getting back into his car and driving off. The scene switches to inside of the bus that Brianna had jumped on. She sits in the front and it’s very peaceful as there is only a few other people there. The bus stops and a tall rough looking man climbs in wearing a gray shirt and black jeans. His face, looks worn and tired, and his short brown hair looks scruffy. He sits a few seats down from Brianna and opens up the book bag he was carrying. The man catches Brianna’s eye, because she has seen him from somewhere before, but can’t quite put her finger on it. Not wanting to just sit there and stare, she decides to try to start a conversation with him. 'I don't mean to be rude, but do I know you? You look so familiar.' she says in his direction. 'You might. I know you though.' 'How?' 'You're a PCW wrestler. I have been a big fan of PCW for quite some time and follow it constantly.' he replies not looking up, but inside of his bookbag. 'So have I. That's why I decided to join in the first place.' Brianna continues to stare the man down, and is shocked to see something jump out of his bookbag. Before we can see what it is the scene fades. TO BE CONTINUED YET AGAIN!
