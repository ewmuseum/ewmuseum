The Material Saga: New Model

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 6th, 2002 DarkStar drummed his fingers on the long conference table. He knew this office well enough, the divisional leader of Hot Topics multimilliondollar corporation resided here. The place drew a thick feeling of bile from deep within his throat. The taste of utter sickness. Months had passed since the last time the company tried to recruit him in the name of consumer fashion. They wanted him to be a complete jockey for the clothing you see every single 'goth' kid wearing when you go into the more remote portions of the mall. Yeah, near fucking Macys. Thats supposed to be goth. When in the name of gods did the nook wedged between the Sports Authority and Macys that noone ever entered become goth. The entire premise of Hot Topic made DarkStar gag his entire meal up, no matter how good the meal was. This morning was a real good meal as a matter of fact, he scoffed when he remembered it wasnt even needed to be paid for... the diner figured they would just draw publicity off of him as he sat in his little vinyl seat. Thats what he was to people now, publicity. At least they were worshiping the right guy. DarkStar didn't even bother feigning humbleness anymore, both he and Talena knew that they were the reason PCW was still afloat, along with the rest of the Dark Age of course. The crowds cheered for him like the deity he was, he revelled in it. It went straight to his head like cheap liquor, and twice as intoxicating. Talena began to get the same way, not noticing DarkStar slowly slip from his roots, slip from the underground gothic harem that he owed his lifestyle to. It wasn't big changes at first, he started to pay more on clothes, talk to more higher up on the goth ladder, Trent Reznor came over to dinner the other night, and Robert Smith and him were supposed to play a round of drunken mini-golf after Talena left town. He was the uppercrust of lifestyle, he bent and molded it to his own fashion. This trend was fashioned by him, his fashion wasnt made by the trend. He has been like this for years upon years, it just got marketable all of a sudden. The older woman entered the room, a smile taking up most of her face, DarkStar stifled a laugh. He could see right through her. This woman went to a Ivy League school and needed a job, she graduated in the dead center of her class, so the best jobs werent available to her. Instead of wasting her education she started dressing goth around her 29th birthday, she got a job at Hot Topic at 32, and now is a Regional head. Fucking poser. She was going to preach to. 'Good evening Gavyn! Talena! How are we doing on this fine, fine night!' DarkStar made a mental note of the word night being emphasized, man was she trying hard. 'I figured I would finally grace your presence tonight Vivian' She also liked to brag about her name, how it had the roman numeral for 666 in it. It didn't. She was missing a V, she didn't know that though, and noone really thought about telling her either. So it was all for the better. DarkStar sighed and realized that he wasn't wearing a spot of Hot Topic clothing, neither was Talena, he was greatly amused by that. 'Well, I'm glad you did Gavyn, as you may know... um... alot of our spokespeople dropped out...' It was a trend really. DarkStar did this whole media darling thing a few months ago, Hot Topic was right there to clothe and pay him, he wasn't nearly as big then as he is now though. He is fucking Wheaties box big now. 'Yeah, that tends to happen when you see thirteen year olds wearing what you are.' DarkStar nearly chuckled at his own hypocriticalness, alot of his fans have started to get younger and younger. Even he catered towards this new trend of black clothing and lipstick on guys. Vivian was not impressed. 'Yes, I guess it does.' DarkStar gave a cocky smile, 'We both know why you want me here,' he leaned forward, making sure that is impecable taste in clothing was better visable, 'You need a big bad gothy person who is in the public eye to shill this.... clothing for you, right?' She made a face and nodded. 'I guess you could say that.' It was Talenas turn to talk now, 'So, what do me and DarkStar get out of this.' Vivian grinned and began to recite like she memorized this (and to make sure she made this sound like a great deal), 'Exposure and standard modeling checks, for the both of you.' The look DarkStar gave froze her like she had fallen into a polar ice cap for the last millenia or two. 'Yeah, its definately time to lea-...' DarkStar began to get up but was stopped by Talenas arm, she caught Vivians gaze and started to sway back and forth, so did Vivian, DarkStar slowly sat back down. 'Dear, sweet Viv, I am sure you can give us a better deal then that...' The eyes were locked, DarkStar loved when she did this, 'You are important to us here... what would you like?' Talena shrugged and looked up at her lover. 'What do we want?' He didn't want this ending up like last time. Being nothing but a whore for the merchandise with no input and barely any royalties. This time was different though, he was the biggest and best goth star in this world today, they should be throwing money at him and hoping it sticks. 'Free creative control, our own line of clothes, and.... Seventy Percent of the royalities.' He scratched his chin. 'Yeah that sounds fine.' Talena smiled and looked back to Vivian, 'You heard him, you are going to agree of course, right?' Vivian nodded and stood up to shake hands with the two. Talena surpressed a giggle, in the mind of Viv, this would be what she wanted to give them, and she barganed them down from what it was... then hopefully she would get fired, but either way its fine. 'Pleasure doing business with you again.' DarkStar took Talenas hand and walked out of the office. 'You know, I can't help but feel like a Corporate Whore.' Talena stopped and pulled DarkStar to her, 'Why? You are this good and deserving of all of this, its not your fault that you became popular, you just know how to milk it for all its worth.' DarkStar smiled. She was right. 'I really am this good.'
