Date: not entered The sun is at its highest peak as the noon hour strikes and the traffic picks up. Unfortunately for me this business was all picking up while I tight squeeze for time. The clock would strike three in just a few hours and by then I would have to be back to watch the kids since Chris wanted to go plan some things for the wedding. The date still had yet to be determined and yet as each day went by I became more and more nervous. So today I decided to take Christa and get out of the house. I still needed to find a wedding dress along with a dress for Christa so what time would be better than a peaceful Sunday afternoon?.....ANYTIME! Pulling into the parking lot of the bridal shop I found out that people no longer used Sunday as a day of rest, unless their idea of rest was sitting in traffic for hours and only moving several hundred feet. Instead it seemed that Sunday had turned into the busiest day of the week, but that didn’t bother the happy mood I was in any. I was going to look at wedding dresses and keep any sort of aggravating thought from my head, even if the thought dealt with the match I would have with Gaz Green on Friday, or the Battle Royal that would take place on Sunday that would place me against twenty-nine others. “Yay we are finally here!” Young Christa cheered from her passenger side seat. Upon seeing the shop she immediately began to unfasten her seatbelt and nearly jumped out of her seat. It seemed that to her this was the biggest event of her eight years she had lived, yet I could only imagine years from now how excited she would be once it was finally her own wedding that she was shopping for. “Slow down kiddo; let me park the car before you run off without me.” I said as I pulled into the space closest to the shop. Boy was I ever lucky; perhaps as lucky I was to survive the prankster’s paradise match with nothing more than a cut up forehead. “Then hurry up slow poke!” She shouted in a teasing manner as the car finally came to a complete stop and I pulled my keys out of the ignition, but not before Christa opened the door and rushed over the my side of the car, waiting eagerly outside the driver seat door. “I’m hurrying! Relax the stores not going anywhere.” I answered in the same teasing manner as I shut the car door and then began to follow Christa who was leading far ahead of me into the bridal shop. “Good afternoon young lady.” A masculine voice chimed from the entrance of the shop. I looked up at the entranceway and noticed a rather well built blonde standing there with the door open, allowing Christa to step inside the shop. He then looked over at me and winked, leaving the door open for me as well. Instead of returning the affectionate wink though I simply thanked him for holding the door and followed Christa over the bridal section where she was admiring dresses that were much too large for her. “When I grow up do you think I’ll find someone as great as Chris to marry?” Christa asked as she turned her head away from the dress and looked up at me with her little blue eyes widening with curiosity. “Of course Christa.” I answered as I patted her head causing her to smile and her mind to wander off into the future. “Are you ladies finding everything all right?” A voice questioned from behind Christa and I. I turned around to notice that the same blonde was now peering over our shoulders watching us like the way a hawk watches its prey. “We sure are.” Christa answered for the two of us with her usual gleeful smile, but something about this man caught her attention and suddenly made her stop in her tracks and stare up at him for a moment as she studied the man carefully as if she had seen him somewhere before. “Hey Christa, look at this dress.” I called over to the young girl in my best attempts to snag her attention as I pointed over to a smaller dress intended not for the bride, but for the position Christa would serve at the wedding, the flower girl. Instead of looking over in our direction though the girl was much more focused on the man that stood before her. Finally she managed to reply but it wasn’t to me, it was to the man competing for her attention by simply standing there and staring. “Chris? What are you doing here?” The girl asked seeming confused. In her mind she remembered that Chris was at home with Evan and Alex, yet there before her eyes stood a man with the same exact physical attributes but before she could say another word I knew just who it was. “Ein.” I whispered to myself with a growl before reaching out to Christa and pulling towards me for her individual safety. Much to my surprise even my most inaudible words could be detected by his ears as he came up with the witty response, “Did someone say my name?” followed by a grin as if to say guess who. “Chris!” Christa joyfully explained as she pulled herself away from my grasp and jumped into the arms of the man, embracing him tightly. “Christa, what are you doing?” I screamed out before jumping towards the man myself not to embrace him, but to take back the child whose safety and well keeping I was in charge of. “Chris?” The man questioned. “Oh dear Christa I think you have me confused, you see I am much better than Chris.” “Chris’ brother?” The girl asked, understandably confused as to what was going on considering I had not known myself still where this man came from and why he looked so much like Chris. “Don’t ever try to associate me with that weakling!” He snapped at the girl with narrowed blue eyes. He looked down at her in disgust and then looked over at me who was standing beside her. His sudden look of disgust turned into one of lust as his face suddenly lit up with a smile and he started to walk towards me, looking me up and down studying every inch of my body. “Come on Christa, maybe we can come another day.” I said quietly to her before taking her by the hand and turning around to face away from Ein and walk away as quickly as I could to avoid yet another confrontation with the Chris look alike. “But you can’t leave yet, I haven’t gotten your measurements.” Before I could manage to make it even half way to the door I found Ein in front of me. He smirked as reached out towards my hands and grabbed onto my wrist pulling me towards him. Locking his eyes with mine a sick smirk crossed his face and he pulled my body up against his. I could feel his warm breath making contact with my neck as my mind immediately began to panic and deliver thousands of messages to my conscious all at once. …Think quick Tessa! You can’t let him use you like this, not in front of Christa… ...But you don’t want to lash out on him. He might go for the easier target which would obviously be Christa… …But if you acted fast enough you could easily get away with Christa and get back home… …But why let this impersonator ruin your afternoon…. …You’re in trouble now Tessa… “OUCH!” The scream of Ein broke my thoughts as the tight grip on my wrist was suddenly released and I was free to run away, but not before wondering what had caused the sudden shriek of Ein. Looking down I could see Christa standing proudly beside the downed Ein and realized that the little girl who was practically a clone of myself when I was younger had saved me. “No one touches my aunt Tessa like that!” the little girl yelled at him before walking over to me proudly and taking my hand to lead me out the door as if the roles were reversed and she were the mother and I were the child.Leaving me simply astounded I began to search for the words to say but found myself only able to say, “Oh my god Christa you…” “Deserve an ice cream.” She cut in with a giggle knowing very well that after a courageous move like that she was one hundred percent right that she did deserve it. “Exactly what I was thinking.” I finished her thought as I came to a realization of my own that if little Christa could take down Ein like that with a simple kick, just imagine what I could do to the two hundred twenty six pound Gaz Green if I really tried.
