An Emotional Rollercoster

17 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/17/02 The scene fades to a graveyard some where. It's fogy and rainy day. Very depressing. We move down a small, winding road, past gnarled trees, looking twisted and bare. Off a little ways from either side of the road are grassy fields, barren aside from the occasional slabs of rock poking up from the earth. There is a black limo parked on the right side of the road. The back passenger door has been left hanging wide open and it almost points us in the direction of its occupants, for off to the right we see two figures. As we move closer, we can see Michael Graves and Big Daveylicious. Big D is wearing a black Armani suit, black trench coat, and Oakleys in his front pocket. He holds a black umbrella over his head as he stares at a headstone upset. The pain in his eyes are like somebody killed his best friend.) Big Daveylicious: Why? Why'd you have to go? (Michael pats Big D's back.) Michael Graves:It's okay, Big D. It's not your fault. Big Daveylicious: It's not okay! My life is ruined. Michael Graves: Let it go, Big D. Let it go! Big Daveylicious: How could you be so inhuman?! They gave me their all. Michael Graves: What are you talking about? The PCW Womans division didn't give you anything. (We suddenly get a good shot of the grave stone. On the stone their are Sassy, Brandi, and Kya all in reveling outfits. Below them are the inscribing: 'Wherever they're careers go, or becoming nobody hoes, nobody knows and nobody cares.') Big Daveylicious: But they were great to letting my hero rise! They were good. Real good. But now their PCW careers are dead! Michael Graves: Why do you care? You delivered your point and it was proven. Women don't belong in a wrestling ring. (A tear runs across Big D's cheek. He wipes it away.) Big Daveylicious: But they were such an important part in my career. I mean they had boobs. Michael Graves: If making you horny was important for your career than you gotta be kidding me. Big Daveylicious: But I loved them all! Michael Graves: Just think of the good times. (We get a close up shot of Big D's face with a smile. As we fade to a Tuesday Turmoil during the towards the end of the Evening gown match in Ed's Topless bar. Brandi releases her nut grip off of the thong wearing Big Daveylicious and slams his head into Sassy's crotch and both lay unmoving as Brandi celebrates and the scene keeps repeating itself.) Michael Graves:{Voice-over} Just think of the good times. (We than fade back the grave yard with Big D smiling with one tear shed across his cheek.) Michael Graves: Well, aren't you atleast gonna say 'Hi' to the camera? (Big D wipes his tear off. His eyes blood shot red as he stares at the camera man holding the camera.) Big Daveylicious: Get the hell outta here! (The camera man continues his job and doesn't move a budge. Big D starts walking up towards the camera man.) Big Daveylicious: What are you inhuman?! I have feelings damn it! Stop filming me! (Big D pushes the camera man down and causing the camera man to drop the camera on the lens side. Michael looks at Big D oddly. Big D shrugs.) Big Daveylicious: What? He was filming me without my permission. Michael Graves: He came over to find out thoughts on your opponents. You know DarkStar and... Big Daveylicious: What am I to you people?! A puppet? A man to dance around on strings with your command! Get outta hear! (Big D lays by the PCW's Women Division Grave as Graves walks away as the scene fades to black.)
