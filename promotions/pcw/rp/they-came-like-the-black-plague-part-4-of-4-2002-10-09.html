They came like the black plague (Part 4 of 4)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-9-02 Scorpion’s mother in law, Mrs. Waffle is shown sitting on the couch sitting by alone. With her hands on her forehead just lying there very upset. Mother in-law: I don’t believe this. I voice my opinion on the way I think my daughter should live and I get yelled at for being to harsh to her. Mrs. Waffle then picks her head up glaring at all of the family photos of Scorpion and his wife with his son Toby. Mother in-law: They look so happy. I should be happy that she is happy. They have such a lovely son and I love my grandson to death. I guess it doesn’t matter what my son in-law does for a living as long as she is happy. That’s all that matters. Mrs. Waffle gets up and heads outside where her husband is sitting having a cigarette. Mother in-law: Your absolutely right honey about the way I treated our daughter. I need to apologize to her. Father in-law: I’m glad you finally got around. It’s about damn time. Mother in-law: Let’s go inside. They head inside and begin to walk up the stairs into there daughter’s room where they are all taking a nap. Scorpion, Stephanie and there son Toby. Mother in-law: They look so happy. Father in-law: I told you she was happy. Mrs. Waffle walks up to Stephanie and taps her shoulder. Mother in-law: (whispering) Honey wake up. Stephanie: Ma… Mother in-law: I’m sorry; I treated you the way I did. Stephanie: It’s all right I just want you to realize that I’m happy with what I’m doing, I wouldn’t want it anyway. Mother in-law: All right dear, we are going to head downstairs. Stephanie: All right we will be down in a minute. Mother in-law: Ok. Mr. and Mrs. Waffle head down stairs and sit on the couch. Father in-law: You did the right thing. Mother in-law: I don’t know what got into me, I guess I just thought the way she was living was not up to my “standards” and I shouldn’t be like that. Father in-law: It’s all right, I’m just glad you came around or this would be a very tense household. Mother in-law: That it would be, that it would… Stephanie and Scorpion walk down the stairs and into the living room. “Hello everyone.” Mother in-law: Jeff, I’d like to apologize for my actions they were out of line and very bad on my part. You do a tremendous job of taking care of your family and you have one of the funniest jobs in the world. I’m very sorry for what I said. “It’s alright, nothing to worry about you just got me very upset.” Mother in-law: I’m very sorry. I said some pretty dumb things. “Oh it’s all right.” Mother in-law: And to my lovely daughter, I’m very sorry the way I have treated you. It’s very dumb on my part to question the way you live. I’m very sorry. Stephanie: Oh Mother it’s all right. Father in-law: Thank god. Mother in-law: Let’s make the rest of our time here the very best it could be. I’m very sorry to anyone I have offended today and I hope that we can continue having the best families in the world. Father in-law: I seconded to that. Stephanie: I as well agree. “Hell, I don’t just say that stuff without some alcohol!” Scorpion grabs a bottle of wine. “Cheers!”
