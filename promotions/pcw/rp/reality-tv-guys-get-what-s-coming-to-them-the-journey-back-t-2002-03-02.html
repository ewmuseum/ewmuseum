Reality TV Guys Get What's Coming To Them (The Journey Back To St Louis Continues)

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/3/02 There is no doubt that the worst invention of the 20th century was the atomic bomb. Never before had something so devastating been invented with the sole purpose of destruction, plunging the World into a huge stand off, each hiding behind their weapons of mass destruction, all the countries stocking up to outnumber the other and plunging the world into fear. The contest for 2nd place though is more of a contest, but if you look back on the last year of the 20th century, the year 2000, it will become clear what it is.....Reality TV, someone had the great idea that bunging normal people in front of a camera and hoping they act naturally that it will make great TV, how wrong they were. Never before had so many annoying people been thrust into the public eye, all acting up their various personalities in their hunt for attention while the audience watches at home praying that the worst invention drops in to wipe out it’s nearest challenger. Being juxtaposed into one of these shows is something that you wouldn’t wish on any person, let alone 2 of them, but on Xavier Kannon’s trek back to America to make it to St Louis on time for March Madness, he and Ellie~! are now clinging to a piece of driftwood and riding the tide to a small island just off the Argentinean coast...Which houses around 10 people, 5 cameras and enough egos to turn half the viewers homicidal....And Eleanor hasn’t even set foot on it yet. We open up on the horrified face of Xavier Kannon, staring at the crowd n the beach who have gathered to shout and scream in front of the cameras to mark the arrival of the 2 strangers. Xavier : “Come on, we can turn around! I have enough energy in me to get us somewhere else, anywhere but here. I’d rather be stuck on the Pirate ship than on an island with 10 complete imbeciles!” Xavier goes to turn the logo around but Ellie~! grabs his arm and stops him, pulling him back so he swims towards the island once more. Eleanor : “No way! Those shows are like, SO great! They like, do stuff, and vote on stuff...Fair enough it isn’t as good as the Real World, but hey....We are SO going to be on TV!” The Scientologist sighs and grits his teeth and he speeds up and within 2 minutes his feet touch the seabed and he pushes the driftwood aside, joining Ellie~! in walking through the surf towards the beach where 10 people in their early 20’s try to out do each other in welcoming them. The camera zooms in on the crowd to show a generic mix of similar looking men and women, none seeming to stand out from the other, we then zoom over their shoulder and into the trees behind them. Voice : “They say only 10 and camera men......They no tell truth......Think we stupid!” We move into the woods to see a pack of 5 warriors dressed in simple brown fabric loin clothes. All of them have long, wild Black hair and tribal tattoos, some matching to show family lineage. One of the men is holding a crudely made Bow with a quiver of Arrows while the other 4 have sharp, vicious looking swords, cerated along the blade. Leader : “They no respect customs of the ancients. They ruin home.” We move back to the beach where Ellie~! is greeting the group of 5 girls on the island who are dressed in bikinis, some with headscarves and sunglasses, but none of hem having any features that make them stand out from the others. Girl : “Wow, are you, like, the new girl on the island?” Eleanor : “Er......Like.....Yah!....I guess so...“ They take her by her arms and escort her up the beach to their hut. Girl : “We’ll get you some dry clothes....” Eleanor : “Okay......Like cool, is that your hut?” Girl : “Er.....Like, totally.” As Eleanor is taken into the girls hut to change Xavier finally makes it to the beach, walking with slouches shoulders and a bored expression on his face, trundling through the sand as the sea water runs off his coat. Man : “Hey! Welcome to the island! It’s so good to have a new person here after 2 weeks!” The Scientologist looks at the meagre resources on the island. Simple wooden huts, hammocks and clothes drying on long vines and sighs, before putting on his false sincerity voice. Xavier : “Um.....Yeah, I’m really pleased to be here....Say, you don’t happen to have a boat or a plane or a helicopter or something because I need to get to St Louis....Oh, and a map would be useful too...” The crowd of 5 men looks at him inquizically for a few seconds before one laughs, triggering the other 4 to do so and one nudges Xavier’s upper arm in jest, causing the Gold Patron meritorious to look down at his sleeve, an agitated expression on his face. Man : “Haha, I see you’re a joker!” Xavier : “Er.....Yeah...Joke, funny.....So was that a no?” Man : “Haha!......What a joker!” Suddenly banging and rhythmic rustling is heard from the trees behind them before a war cry erupts and the 5 warriors run down the beach, now all brandishing crudely forged swords. Xavier : “Hey...I’m still new, you 5 wanna show me how you deal with this?” The wily Scientologist takes a few steps back into the surf as the 5 men turn and look in wide eyed horror at the approaching assault. Xavier : “So in case of native assault we just stand still? Yeah, I think I can deal with that. Personally I’d run, but hey, I don’t want to impose my customs on your little community.” He stands back and watches as the 5 warriors tear a swathe through the 5 men who make no effort to defend themselves and instead just run around in confusion trying to call a meeting to see which way to run as they are being hacked at with cerated swords. Warrior : “You no respect custom so you die!” Man : “ARGH!” The Gold Patron Meritorious looks down at the water he is standing in which is becoming tinged with Red from the flow of blood off the beach so he shrugs and side-steps to the left and out of the Crimson liquid. Xavier : “I’m clear of the blood now.....Carry on, but try to keep it to that side if you can?” He points to his right to where the blood is running and the 5 continue their brutal attack on the 5 island members who are now lying in many parts o the beach with the 5 natives walking over the corpses and towards Xavier. Xavier : “Oh....Hey, you guys heard of Scientology? We have been thinking of spreading down to these parts and that swordsmanship showed great enlightenment!” Leader : “Huh?” Kannon takes a few steps towards the band of warriors, putting his hand on the leaders shoulder and gesturing with his other hand, the leader watching bemused by the words of the stranger. Xavier : “Okay....In the 50’s there was this guy called L Ron Hubbard who was all ‘Hey, I should make up my own religion!’ so he went and did that and then the World got Scientology, we are a bit like Buddhism but better, anyway, we are looking for new members all the time, and this island would be a very good base for us....You know, because if you are Scientologists you will be less prone to sticking those swords into me!” The 5 men still stand befuddled in front of him. Xavier them moves suddenly and the 5 spring into an aggressive stance, bending their knees and holding their weapons back, ready to strike. Kannon stutters, worried about what to say when you have small band of natives who have just slaughtered 5 men before your very eyes. Xavier : “Okay, memo to self - No sudden movements!” The leader of the band holds an open palm to the other men, getting them to hang back as he steps forward to address Xavier. Leader : “Too many......People come to island, disrespect custom......We say 10, they now bring 12....We see you and woman come.....Get to island by sea...” Xavier looks puzzled and points to his chest with both hands, smirking. Xavier : “You think I Want to be here? I am meant to be in America....Am-Er-Ri-Ca! But instead I found myself at the South Pole...An-Tarc-T-Ca! Then I was on a boat and was made to walk the plank by Pirates.....You know....Pi-Rats! Then I floated here and saw those people on the beach, I didn’t want to be here, let alone break some customs thing...What is that? Do I have to declare my items because most countries have Customs Officers....Of-Fi-Cers to do check and that way people don’t get hacked to pieces!” He them waits, hopeful that he will be spared from the same fate the other males on the island. Leader : “We say 10, you be 12!” The Scientologist points down to the various body parts and grabs a severed head, holding it up by the hair, it’s lifeless eyes looking at the warriors who twitch uneasily. Xavier : “Well there were 12....Now there ate seven....Se-Ven! So there is no problem, a whole other 3 people can come here before customs intervene....In fact, you could have spared 3 of these guys!” He drops the head onto his foot and gives it a gentle kick so it rolls along the damp sand to join the rest of the corpses. Leader : “You 7......7 stay.....You no have 8.......7!” The leader then waves to his man and walks back towards the trees, followed by the rest of his warrior band. Kannon waves to them before looking down at the mass of bloody body parts by his feet to which he turns his nose up. Xavier : “Um....Bye guys....I’ll explain this to the others then? Okay...I will.” He walks around the bodies and starts to walk up to the Girl’s hut when the 6 females all pour out and walk down the beach, Ellie~! now wearing a shiny dark Blue bikini and see through wrap, suddenly they stop and see the butchered remains of their island-mates. Eleanor : “Whoa, weren’t they like, alive before?” Girl : “Um....Like, they were, he has SO killed them!” The Snapple~! shiller looks confused then turns around to see that he is standing directly in front of the hacked up remains, with some of their blood on his feet. Xavier : “Damn, this doesn’t look good!” He sighs and starts to run through what happened. Xavier : “Well there were these warriors in the trees and they were upset about you lot mucking up their island so they decided they would star to kill you all....Starting with those guys, but then I talked to them and they said they’d stop butchering you all....Which is good news really isn’t it?” The 5 women stare at him with horrified faces as Eleanor walks to stand beside her man, whispering into his ear. Eleanor : “Are you sure you didn’t just kill them and don’t want to admit it?” He puts on his best sarcastic voice to answer her. Xavier : “Yes my little Poon Puppy, I sliced them up using only my stubble and overgrown fingernails! NO I DIDN’T KILL THEM!” The 5 girls stare at each other then turn to Xavier and all pull their diving knives out of their bikini bottoms, brandishing the blades at the Scientologist. Girl : “Well we haven’t seen anyone else in the woods and we have been here for 2 weeks, so isn’t it funny that they show up, just as you and 5 dead bodies do?” The 1st couple of Scientology look to each other and gulp as they start to walk backwards towards the surf, walking around the pile of body parts. In front of them the 5 armed girls stalk them, ready to extract some justice as the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “Um....This is sure going to take some explaining!” Ellie~! huddles up to him as they are forced back into the water and we hit Black. Eleanor : “Take.......Some.......Explaining!”
