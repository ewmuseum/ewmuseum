The Past, The Present, and Fidel (Part 2)

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/13/02 Time: 2: 19 P.M Location: CubaScene: Jet “ The Past, The Present, and Fidel” Part 2 The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore on an official International Incident jet, on their way to Cuba where they’ll be meeting Fidel Castro. Juan is listening to his walk men, while Kevin is watching “Fast and The Furious” on his portable DVD player. Juan’s music track skips, when Kevin jumps up when the race in the beginning of the move starts. Juan: Woah Kevin! Watch it their buddy, what’s going on, and why are you acting like a ten-year old at a Harry Potter movie. Kevin: What are you talking about, how do you expect me to not get decided when Ja Rule comes out. Juan: Kevin, please, his only line is MONAJEE! Kevin: Say it with passion Juan, it’s not MONAJEE, it’s MMMMMMMOOOOOOOONNNNNAAAAAAJJJJJJEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Juan: Same thing, how many times do I have to tell you that no Americans have any skills what so ever? Kevin: I don’t think he has skill, but I do find humor in these Americans, so why not use it to my advantage. Juan: What ever you say Kevin. The two men go back to what they were previously doing, when the plane starts making some odd loud noises. Juan: What the hell just happened? Kevin: I don’t know, but I think we might have slightly hit something. I’m going to go check on the pilot. Kevin goes to check on the pilot, and Juan looks out the window to see what’s going on, but can’t see beyond the shadow of clouds. Kevin: Juan we have a problem. Juan: What’s going on? Kevin: You should really come over here, and take a look at this yourself. It’s a bit odd, and I don’t know what to do. Juan goes over to see what’s going on and notices that the pilot is lying down on the ground, with three empty vodka bottles by his side. Juan: Holy Sh*t, did he just drink three bottles of Vodka. That’s impossible. Aren’t there laws against drinking and flying? The plane makes a real loud noise once again and shakes, sending both Kevin and Juan, flying into the other side. Kevin: We have to do something, and we have to do it quick. What should we do with the plane and the pilot? Juan: Hmmm……Is your cell phone on? Kevin tries dialing his cell phone for help, but there is no signal because they are out of the calling range. Juan then notices a walkie talkie by the steering wheel and quickly picks it up. Juan: Yes! We’ll just call for some help. Press the button over there so I can tune in to the station. Juan points to the button real quickly and Kevin is misguided and clicks the wrong button. Juan: Hey Kevin, why is the plane going down. Kevin turns around and looks at the button he pushed, and notices it says “Landing” on the top of it. Kevin: Umm… Juan, I think I might have pushed the wrong button. Juan turns around and quickly grabs the steering wheel, trying to steer the plane in another direction. Kevin looks out the window and notices how they are rapidly approaching the ground. He then runs over to the wheel and both him and Juan try to make it slow down so the landing isn’t too hard. The men hit the ground and land, as they go flying from the after affect. The two get up, with only a few bruises, and they quickly shake it all off. Kevin: Woah! I can’t believe we didn’t get ourselves killed. Juan: Me neither, let’s get out of this thing. The two men leave the plane, and start to travel all throughout the wooded area. They notice a group of people up ahead. Juan: Look over there, I see a group of people. Let’s go ask for help. HEY OVER HERE, HELP US! Juan smiles, as a haunted expression comes upon Kevin’s face. Juan: What’s wrong. Kevin: Those guys don’t seem to friendly seeing as they’re screaming, and pointing rifles at us. Juan: Holy SH*T, RUN! The two guys start running and dodging bullets, as the wild men star gaining on them. Juan turns around and sees a bullet flying at his head, but suddenly him and Kevin are pushed right out of the way. Juan: What was that? Juan turns around and sees a naked woman, pointing at the two men, and telling them to follow her. Juan: God has come in the form of a naked woman to guide us to our destination I say we follow it. Kevin grabs Juan’s arm as he starts running after her. Kevin: Watch out Juan. I saw this move where this tribe of naked woman took these guys and kept them in cages where they prepared to eat them. Juan: Kevin, how many times do I have to tell you to stop looking at those stupid American movies? Juan and Kevin run after the naked woman, until there is nowhere else to go. The woman doesn’t say a word but point to a curtain, telling Juan and Kevin to go through the curtain. Kevin and Juan do what she says, and after they open it they see nothing but a huge collage of beautiful naked woman. Juan: I’ve arrived in heaven. Juan goes running after the naked ladies, as they all quickly attract to Juan and Kevin. Juan takes all the ladies with open arms, but Kevin seems hesitant, because he wants to be faithful to Mandy. Kevin: Juan have you already forgotten about Juliet? Juan’s in a euphoric trance and has oddest look on his face. Juan: No way Kevin, I love Kelly, I’d never forget about her. Kevin: Kelly? Juan shakes out of it, and attempts to walk away from the naked ladies. Kevin helps push Juan along, until they reach the woman who is the head of the tribe of naked woman. Kevin: Excuse me, but we got lost here, is there anyway we can get to Castro’s mansion from here. Naked Woman: We could take you to the main square, which is by there. Juan: Great, when will we leave? Naked Woman: Not so fast, first you have to do something for us. The woman smirks as Juan and Kevin look at each other shrugging. The next time we see Juan and Kevin, they’re doing the N’Sync song “Bye, Bye Bye” completely naked. The women are enjoying it all, as Kevin and Juan are completely blushed. The show goes on and Kevin and Juan finally finish. The scene fades to black, with the women guiding the Golden Game to their destination. Both Juan and Kevin miss the friendly tribe of naked ladies, but business is business and it has to be done. TO BE CONTINUED……………………………
