Date: not entered Shadows dance across the expansive lawn of Stately Steele Manor. The witching hour has come and gone, the crescent moon close to completing its journey across the evening sky. There is a tranquil silence to tonight, the affluent suburban neighborhood nestled into restful slumber… A shadow flits over the wall surrounding the Manor. Desperate eyes scan the darkened home of our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele. Not a single light shines at this late hour. Making a break of the entrance, the man dashes to the porch, feet squishing into the soon-to-be morning dew. The head furtively looks around, scanning not only the surroundings, but the façade of the house as well. Nervous fingers fumble in pockets, and the slightest sound of rustling keys carries in the cool, evening breeze. The correct key is inserted and twisted, allowing the front door to crack open ever so little, with nary a sound. The mysterious figure slides inside the home and gingerly shuts the door behind. Inside, the figure tip-toes through the foyer, careful to avoid any disturbance witch could wake the peaceful inhabitants. Hugging the wall, the man expertly weaves around the décor while providing himself a quiet and hidden path. Leaving the carpet for the hardwood of the kitchen entrance, the dark form removes his shoes, and glides slowly into the kitchen… FLIK! The Masochist cries out, startled by the sudden and painful intrusion of the overhead light fixture, tripping over his own feet and crashing to the floor. “AH! What gives?!” The Masochist rubs his eyes and looks up from the hardwood… and his jaw drops in shock. We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he stares down coldly at the Masochist. He is dressed in a toga-robe, and his face wears a dour expression, indeed. Big Al drums his fingers expectantly on the table, as his eyes bore holes into the Masochist, who begins to shrink from the icy glare. “L-L-L-ook, Big Guy,” the Masochist finally manages to stammer out, “it’s not what it seems!!” Big Alan Steele’s eyes only narrow at the Masochist’s words. The scared man gulps hard, to try and clear the lump from his throat. “I was… out… uh… late at the… err…” “STOP your childish whimpering!!” Big Al makes a stopping motion with his outstretched hand, “For it shall do you no good, for I, Big Alan Steele, know now to be true, what I have suspected for so long. Your late entrance to my abode this evening only confirms it.” “Whoa there, Al,” the Masochist cautions his partner in crime-fighting, “Let’s not jump to hasty conclusions here! I was just out late picking up some salve…” “What is that on your face?! Come! Move yon self better into the path of the light so I may inspect the strangeness on your visage…” The Masochist stands, and turns away from the light as much as possible. “Ah, that’s not necessary, Big guy. I was just…” Steele snarls and stabs out with his hand with lightning quickness, grabbing a hold of The Masochist’s chin. He then proceeds to forcibly turn his face into the light… Big Al’s eyes go wide. “SO! How did this come to be?! What shall be your excuse for this contusion surrounding yon eye orbit?!” “Oh, that?” The Masochist meekly counters. “I just… errr… fell and hit myself with the doorknob on the front door, that’s all. Nothing major. Just a black-eye…” Big Al’s face turns crimson as he lets go, the Masochist shrinking away from BAS’s rising rage. But, just as suddenly, the anger crests, and Steele slumps his shoulders, turning away from the masochist, facing the far window of the far wall of the kitchen. His voice breaking with emotion, Big Alan Steele fights back bitter tears. “I have provided you with food, shelter, and rendings into formless masses of unconscious pulp!! What more can you ask of me?!?! Why must thou hurt me thusly? Oh, what manner of sin must a man commit in a single lifetime to deserve such a fate as mine?!” The Masochist chokes. “What do you mean, man? I haven’t done anything wrong?” Steele turns, fire burning in his reddened eyes. He points an accusing finger at his longtime partner. “WHY MUST YOU TURN THIS KITCHEN INTO A HOUSE OF LIES?! I know of your deceit… your treachery!!” “What are you talking about?” “Judas!!” Big Al booms, the sound of thunder crashing in the background. “Thou hast been seeing another superhero!!” “No!” The Masochist cries out. “It’s not true! You are the only one…” “Lie to me no longer, Lord of Pain! The evidence of your deception hath been mounting for many weeks!! And now, whilst I am on the threshold of my greatest triumph in PCW, with my greatest gladiatorial combat in years measured in hours away, I find my sparring companion to be missing for an entire fortnight, only come already come home bruised and battered!! So spare me your lies, Lord of Pain, for they only entrench you deeper into the pits of Hades!! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele is now standing heaving as he shouts down at his companion of the last year or so. The Masochist cowers from the onslaught, turning away in shame and disgust. Tears stream unchecked down his cheeks, as he recognizes his guilt. “I never meant for you to get hurt… only me…” Big Alan Steele’s voice softens, his tone almost pleading. “What did I do wrong, my friend? Were the beatings not sufficient? Should I have broken more of your bones? Would it have been more appropriate to render you vegetative more often? Tell me, Pain Master!! I must have answers!” “Oh, no no no. It was NEVER that,” The Masochist begins, his voice becoming far away, “no one ever made me feel as pain as you did…” Despite the situation, Big Al manages a slight grin. “… and while you will ALWAYS be my favorite, Big Al, you can’t offer me everything. So, I went to fight clubs, boxing gyms, pee wee soccer games, Oakland Raider home games… wherever people fight. And that’s when I met her…” Big Al growls at the mention of ‘her.’ The Masochist, turns to BAS and continues. “Oh no. If you would meet her, you would like her. It’s funny…” The Masochist heaves a slight chuckle, “She’s a lot like you. She brings me into worlds of pain I’ve never known…” The masochist walks across the room to the other side of the kitchen table. Big Al follows suit, taking an opposite seat. Big Al clears his throat and speaks. “And what shall I call this woman.” “Oh, I don’t know,” The Masochist sighs dreamily, “I never got her name. She only calls herself the Sadist.” Big Al grunts in derision. “I, Big Alan Steele, severely doubt she has the power of Olympia behind her capable of separating you from this mortal coil, as I.” “And you are right, Al,” says the Masochist, putting up his hands protectively, “Like I said before, NO ONE can hurt me like you…” Steele slams both fists hard into the table. “Then what, by the gods, has she that I lack! I must know!!” “Well,” the Masochist squirms, “You do it better than anyone else, but SHE does IT, if you know what I mean.” BAS pauses, thinking hard. “It?” “No, not just it… IT!” Nudge, nudge, wink, wink. “Say no more!!” exclaims Big Al, relieved. “Then I, too, shall use weapons!!” “NO,” The Masochist sighs deeply in exasperation, “Not weapons… she does IT!!” Extra added emphasis on the ‘IT.’ “Hmmmm…” Steele muses, “Ed has ‘it.’ Perhaps I can borrow some…” “Trust me, you don’t want IT from Ed. But I digress. Now, how can I explain this…” “She gives him sex, Al.” “AH!” The two men are startled by the sudden sound of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum’s voice, who trudges into the kitchen, looking very much like Death, only warmed over. Her face seems to be sliding off her face somehow… “The Masochist goes off and gets beaten by his sadist, who then has sex with his bloody, broken body,” Sue deadpans. “The kind of woman I can respect.” Big Al crinkles his face at the thought. “BY THE GODS!! Then I shant compete with her for control of your nightly beatings!!” “But that’s just it, Al! I don’t WANT you to compete with her!! I’ll say it again… NO ONE can hurt me like you do, and I don’t want to give that up. But I NEED what she can give me that you can’t. I want this to work between us… but I need you to let me go a little. I need some space.” “But… the upcoming match is soooo important, not only for me but for the gods of Olympus! How can I adequately prepare when most other training partners run for their lives should I call them forth?” Big Al’s voice rising an octave. “I need your inhuman ability to absorb obscene amounts of punishment to finely hone my skills!!” “I’m sorry, Al,” apologizes the Masochist. “I guess I got caught up in the newness of the relationship. Of course you can beat me, beat me all you want! I admit I was a bit selfish and I promise I won’t desert you again. Are we still partners?” Big Al looks down into the table, trying to take it all in. Slowly, he looks up, and the two long time friends lock eyes. Steele’s face breaks into a gushy smile and he stabs a hand out across the table. “But of course!! Hi-Keeba!!” The Masochist grins and reaches out, grabbing the hand in friendship. The two shake, Steele’s hand grinding up the Masochist’s hand with audible crunching noises. The Masochist merely flinches at the pain. “Excelsior!! A friend hath returned to the fold!! Surely this shall please the gods, especially when I offer up your broken bones in sacrifice to appease the father Zeus! Hi-Keeba!” “I look forward to it, Big guy!” Gushes the Masochist. Sue leans back against the linoleum countertop, clapping derisively at the two fast friends. “What a moving spectacle,” intones Sue dryly. “and to think I almost missed it.” “Yeah,” says the Masochist, raising a questioning eyebrow, “What are you doing up anyways?” “Well,” Sue replies, “My body and caffeine have a sort of symbiotic relationship – I give caffeine a host, while it supplies me with the needed energy to make it through the day without killing anyone. As it is, I need to maintain a certain blood level of caffeine to function normally, even during slumber.” Sue reaches into the coffee jar and grabs a handful of un-ground beans, tossing a bunch into her mouth. She munches on them, her face sliding back into normal position, color returning to her face. “Ah, Gevalia… sweet, sweet nectar. Now maybe I can get some sleep. Plus, take note, boys… by eating this, I have just saved your lives. You owe me.” The Masochist squirms, running his fingers across the table in nervousness, trying to get out a question. “Umm… are we going to be sparring tomorrow night? You see, the Sadist is going to take me to a driver’s ed class, where she volunteered me to be a cone for the students…” Big Al’s eyes narrow, but then go soft. After a moment, he smiles. “No, you go and have many good times with yon lady-friend.” “And have much sex.” Sue chimes in, with a wink. “For but a single eve,” Big Al continues, “a man of a lesser pain tolerance shall suffice.” “Who can we call, Al?” says Sue. “You know they all unplug their phones and leave town for two weeks when we try and get them.” BAS growls. “You are the manager… line up an unquestioning lump of flesh at once!!” Sue salutes, grinning. “Shawn Prodigy coming up, Big Guy!” Big Al pumps a fist high into the air. “Hi-Keeba!!”
