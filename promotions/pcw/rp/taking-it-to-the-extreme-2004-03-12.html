Taking it to the EXTREME

12 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/04 Some measure of fame had been reached in the past week. The name of Shaun Styles is putting its mark on the world of wrestling by capturing his first ever title in PCW...and it's not just any other title...It's the...EXTREME TITLE!!! *Cue the heavy, angry rock music* The scene fades in on a particularly cloudy day in the city of New York. The grace of the sun shines on the metropolitan area at its most busiest hour. The streets are filled with shoving and pushing as people rush to and fro. Emerging out of the darkness of an underground subway is Shaun Styles looking to be in the highest of spirits. A grin that's all too hard to hide brights up his face as he's sporting a BRAND NEW white shirt and the same old ripped jeans. Can't have everything now can we? He looks around with wandering eyes for a little bit acting like he hasn't lived here for his entire life. Quickly he makes his way down the crowded street whilst looking at all the sky-scrapers casting their giant shadows over him. Turning a corner he finds himself away from all the rush of the business world. Now he's entered the world of the punks, goths, and other black shirt wearing fellas...The Village. Walking by a few men with red mohawks taller than him and army boots that shake the ground with every step, he appears to have reached his destination. Pulling his frizzy locks of orange hair out of his face he looks at the store in front of him. 'Uncle Jimmy's Tattoo and Piercing Parlor' Shaun Styles: My, that looks...unhealthy. A short brick building stands in front of him guarded by a few goth kids as the foundation of the cheap estate crumbles straight to hell...That's where all you damn sinners are going!! Repent and live a moral life filled with clothes that god forbid aren't black!! You're not even individuals anymore, every other kid is just like you or tries to be!! What the hell is wrong with you hippies?!?! *Narrarator clears throat* Sorry about that, I might have been rough on the poor little children...Anywho... Shaun Styles makes his way towards the entrance as the two goth kids each take a step next to the door and end up blocking it. Shaun gives them a bewildered look as they stare back at him with no expression. Shaun Styles: I like your lipstick. Black's a real good color on you. The two tall teenagers look at each other until turning back around to the Extreme champion. Goth #1: Leave our sanctuary at once. Goth #2: I can't believe he noticed my lipstick! It's so great to finally be noticed...and complimented as well. Oh, how I love life! The first goth kid gives him a cold, hard stare as he snaps back into his trance. Goth #2: Umm...I meant...You can't come here...You know nothing of the life of sorrow we live. Leave at once. Shaun Styles: I don't think you understand the situation I'm in. Goth #1: Humor me, mortal. Shaun Styles: Hey! Does that mean YOU'RE immortal??? Goth #1: ...Uh...I guess...I'm...not...Kinda takes away the point of calling others mortals doesn't it? Shaun Styles: Yeah, I guess it does...Anyways, the reason I'm here is that last week, in a very hardfought victory might I add, I, Shaun Styles, won the extreme brawl and have captured the EXTREME TITLE. Goth #2: What's that have to do with us? Shaun Styles: Quite the impatient one, aren't we? The goth starts to blush as he turns away to fix his make up making him seem pale. Shaun Styles: I'm here to get extreme, you know? Get a tattoo or two, maybe a piercing. I hear the belly button one is pretty popular nowadays. Goth #2: Get out. Shaun Styles: Fair enough... Shaun Styles turns around and leaves the secluded area of the village filled with dissapointment. Alas, Shaun hasn't found extremity here but rest assured there will be plenty of oppurtunities for that in the weeks to come.
