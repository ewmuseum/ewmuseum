Hunted Saga Part 14: Unwanted Pressure...

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/4/02 Previously… Crichton takes his pistol retrieved by soldiers a while ago, out of the back of his pants and aims it at the entranceway of the door. He was warned by Aeryn, who was acting almost as a look out that the man was heading their way. Luckily the reception area was deserted when they got there, otherwise this whole thing coulda got messy, messier Crichton corrected himself. Crichton saw the big figure, almost Zombie like, make his way up the road and toward the reception. Crichton read the mans eyes and found nothing more than hate, a need for revenge. With Crichton’s current problem, the inner battle, he could identify with the man, but there was something about him that seemed familiar. As if by cue, Aeryn gasps. Crichton looks at her, prompting her to explain. “Crichton, that’s the diner owner!” Aeryn stated. “I thought he was dead, with the rest of them.” Crichton replies. “Me too.” Aeryn simply declares. Crichton felt a sigh of relief, relief that people may have survived the accident, but the realisation suddenly hit him. The vengeful look in his eye meant that somebody close to him had died and Crichton was willing to bet that they died in the explosion that he was part responsible for. He could do nothing about that now, all that he could do was keep himself and Aeryn alive and that meant threatening him and persuading him to leave them alone. The diner owner edged his way through the door. Crichton decided that this was going to get very messy indeed. And Now… (ooc note – the italics represent past sequences) The new dawn sun arose, the light hitting Crichton by surprise forcing him to extract his sunglasses from the dashboard of his car and carefully placing them over his eyes, whilst he tries to steer the car with one hand. A lot of issues had been playing upon Crichton’s mind for a while now and the incident last night with the Diner Owner who calls himself Dan Lyons didn’t make his situation any better. As a resultant from this confrontation the inner battle, which was currently raging within him had took a turn for the worst. The inner battle was created by the incident at the diner and it was basically Crichton’s conscious advocating the good part of him, pitted against Crichton’s subconscious, which basically wanted his dark impulses to thrive. The incident with the diner owner reopened wounds as it were and having to deal with the situation temporarily placed his dark impulses firmly in charge. Both Crichton and Aeryn looked on in shock as the man they both though was deceased strolls through the Reception door, heavily padded with a navy blue bullet proof vest, nicely accompanied by a rather mean looking shotgun. Crichton gulped a little at the incredibly intimidating sight of the ‘dead man’, Crichton also thought that he detected a little homicidal glint in his eye as Lyons steps closer to his prey. “So are you proud of yourself?” Lyons questions moving ever closer to Crichton. “Um…uh…what do you mean?” Crichton replies with a hint of innocence masked within his voice.Lyons grins a little. “You know full well what I mean! Are you proud that you managed to kill my wife and my daughter, by destroying my diner!” Lyons shouts with nothing but anger in his voice.Crichton suspected as much as soon as he saw Lyons with the shotgun and the homicidal glint in his eyes. He was out for revenge and to be truthful Crichton could sympathize with him on this account, all of his life had been destroyed, because of Crichton’s carelessness. “No I am not proud or in fact happy about that! The fact is, the whole incident was an accident and if you want to reap revenge on somebody my first suggestion is the guy was in charge of soldiers that stormed your diner, Mr. Fleming!” Crichton explained, also with a hint of anger within his voice.Lyons grin turns swiftly into full blown laughter as he takes another step towards Crichton and then without warning slams the blunt end of his shotgun right across Crichton’s jaw. Crichton slumps to the ground, holding his jaw with agony. “You don’t have to lie to me you piece of shit, I was informed about the whole situation by the other survivor of the demolition job you reap upon my wonderful diner.” Lyons stating menacingly. “And who was that might I ask?” Crichton questions just about making it back to his feet. “You know him very well indeed Crichton, his name is Fleming, Mr. Fleming.” Lyons answers. “I might of damn well known, he was the one who put you up to this in another one of his attempts to capture me and he was the one supplied you with such weaponry. It’s all starting to fit into place now.” Crichton thought out loud. “Very observant Crichton, I can now see why Fleming decided that you were a problem.” Lyons commented. “If your working for Fleming that means you’ve come to capture me and I’m telling ya I ain’t gonna be taken easily.” Crichton states stepping forward menacingly.In a blink of an eye the blunt end of the shotgun connects with Crichton’s jaw once more and he finds himself of the floor holding his jaw once again. “Fleming may have given me the means to get to you, it doesn’t mean I share his ideologies and his goals. I simply used him and his ambitions to simply get to you, so that I might get a chance to reap my vengeance.” Lyons replies with relish in his voice. Crichton hears the words exiting Lyons mouth, but he does not listen. The last blow to his jaw, finally rocked the boat and his subconscious was beginning to take control, he felt his dark urges come forward and he no longer felt guilt for his actions in destroying the diner, he now felt pleasure. This sickened Crichton’s conscious, but this was being slowly pushed away, birthing his dark impulses. Crichton slowly raises his head looking directly at Lyons with anger in his eyes. Lyons takes a step back; “Stay back, or I will shoot!” Lyons demands aiming the shotgun directly at Crichton’s face. “If you were going to shoot me you’d have done it already.” Crichton begins “No, your hesitating, thinking what your life will be like if you take my life in cold blood.” Before Lyons could even reply or take action to this, a very loud scream is heard, coming from the reception entrance. Lyons turns to see a woman running away, back to her car, but realises there and then that he has made a school boy error. He turns back just in time to see Crichton pouncing at him arms stretching, aimed at his gun. He grabs a hold of the shotgun and forces the nozzle toward the reception wall. The noise of the blast was almost deafening as the array of pellets blow the wall wide open. Lyons stumbles backwards, from the sheer momentum and speed of Crichton’s pounce and all that was going through Lyon’s head as he and Crichton crashed through the reception window, was that he had failed. He had failed his loved ones, but he vowed that if he ever lived through this, he would be back one day. A day in which Crichton will not be as lucky as this day. Crichton and Lyons fall to the cold, grey, concrete pavement and Crichton easily releases Lyons grasp of the shotgun, then smacks him across the jaw. He then lifts the shotgun from the pavement and aims directly between Lyons’ eyes, whilst slowly rising. “Now, give me one good reason why I shouldn’t kill you right here, right now!” Crichton demanded. “Crichton!” Aeryn’s voice shrills from the reception. “What?” He questions, turning to face Aeryn. “You can’t kill him!” She states. “And why is that exactly is that?” Crichton asks in a slightly raised voice. “Because we’ve destroyed the majority of his life already and we shouldn’t go as far as taking his life.” Aeryn answers. “Maybe your right but…” He trails off as he turns back to where Lyons was, to find nothing at all. However, he sees a figure running off into the night and without a further thought releases a couple of shots. That was merely the primary factor, which was playing upon his mind at the moment and was doing his damned best to forget all about this. This was mainly because it was the first time the subconscious dark impulses took control of his body, he consciously knew what was happening, but couldn’t do a thing to stop it. So he decided to think about something else, also from the night before. His match with the Pharaoh; Pharaoh beats Crichton silly against the canvas............drags him up and dumps him from the ring. He follows him out again, kicking him in the ribcage over and over. Inside the referee counts.............one.............two..............three.......... Jeff: Better hurry. Pharaoh looks up at the ref, but this proves enough of a distraction for Crichton to strike back with a kick of his own. The ref continues to count...........four............five..............six..............seven............. Duke: Hurry! Pharaoh finally fights back, with a yell knocking Crichton off of him. He lunges for the ring but Crichton grabs his foot..........just as the champ kicks him off the ref counts. Eight...............nine.............right before Pharaoh slides back in the ring ten!How could this happen, he had been more prepared than ever in his life, so why wasn’t he currently the PCW Television champion? The answer was simply, very simply, it was his life outside of the PCW. All the events leading up to the titanic struggle The Pharaoh and himself wasn’t exactly the best training methods imaginable, he had to cool down his from the adrenaline rush of danger and he also simmer down from the dark impulses. Still he was making no excuses for his loss, The Pharaoh wasn’t the Television champion for nothing and the match was a draw, so technically he didn’t lose to him at all. Crichton did not take much pleasure from this, but decided that it was indeed a good point. Anyhow, he had the perfect chance to vent his anger somewhat in his match tonight against Kramer Wild Wing, which Crichton was particularly looking forward to. This was mainly to keep his dark impulses at bay, with violence and Crichton vowed that in the Friday Fusion arena in Seattle, Washington that night, there would be a definition of violence.
