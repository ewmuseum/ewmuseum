A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 7:00-8:00

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/8/03 Everything has been happening so quickly…Jason Hilton is trying to take the one thing I care about away from me. That bastard Makonie thinks he can hurt Haley like that? Believing that he just tie her up and force himself on her…the whole notion makes me sick. Why would a man do this to his daughter…leave her with a skinhead. There isn’t any honour or justice in it, just pure hatred. He hates me and it’s causing the suffering of everyone around me…everyone. Makonie is my main concern right now…not Jason Hilton. Hilton maybe the brainchild of this little plot, but Makonie took the one thing that is valuable to me. The one woman I love and now he is torturing her and expects me not to do anything about it…to stand idly by and let him do as he pleases? I might if it wasn’t someone else…if it wasn’t Haley. He will be stopped…I will stop him. It’s going to have to wait though, seeing as every time I get my hands on him someone has to stop it. The captors mean nothing though; they can’t always be around…the backstage area is to large to permit it and that’s when I will strike…taking my revenge. The only problem is I have other things to take care of…PCWers. If I don’t continue doing what I am doing Haley is liable to end up dead…dead at the hands of a nazi. All of this caused by her father…he put me in the position to revert back to Christian MacManus…to call upon the only man who knows how to survive in a situation like this. I am going to have to use him on Turmoil…I must survive in perhaps my biggest match to date. This isn’t going to be another Stone Burns…a match where I can just pretend like I know what’s going on and come out with the win. This is a match with a real PCW veteran…Violence Jack. A man that brings out the exact feelings in me Stone Burns brought out…that Makonie brings out. His past leaves nothing that I can’t handle…he doesn’t scare me. I understand a man like Violence Jack…a cultist. In his purest form he is nothing more then a fraud…a weak leader who wants to have support of people. It’s been done before and this is nothing-new…Heaven’s Gate. Look at what happens to them all in the end though, they crash and burn…leaving their leaders toppled and beaten, just like Jack is going to be on Turmoil. He isn’t alone though…someone is along side him. A man who is going to destroy everything and take over, a man who isn’t to far off from myself. When I first came to the PCW I was going to purge it and save Mr. Kannon, but now my motives have changed…I found out the path wasn’t the one I should be going down…I found something new to live for, someone to live for…Haley. -Christian Michael MacManus ***Location: UnknownDate: May 7, 2003Time: 7:00 am Shaking his head, Matt sighs as he feels the ropes cut into his naked skin. Leaning down some his ab muscles ripple exposing the cuts on them. Thing’s hadn’t exactly gone the way he planned on Turmoil and now he was reaping the benefits as he sits in the plywood chair…feeling the punishment jolt through his body, causing him to squeeze his eyes…feeling the sweat trickle into his open wounds, sending the blood down his back like tiny rain drops. Testing the ropes, he kicks his feet a bit…splashing the water around in the tub, noting that it gives him no leeway. Standing in front of him in the tiny room is one of his captors…wearing jeans and a white tee shirt; the man looks like nothing more then a thug in the darkness of the room. His vial breath almost cuts through the mixed smell of dried vomit and blood, causing the operative to gag, but he controls it well…not letting the small food they have given him topple to the floor. Readjusting his brown eyes, Jackson lets them scan the man he calls…Nameless. He is the one who is letting that bastard Makonie hold his princess captive. Everything he is doing still isn’t enough. Matt knows that he needs to up the bar and make Makonie fear him…hate him. Putting him through the same hell that Matt is going through. Taking a step forward…Nameless matches up his face with the young agents, breathing his rancid breath on him. Letting it cut through Matt’s skin like a scalpel, causing him to wince and moan…only bringing a smile to Nameless’s face, revealing his chipped yellow teeth. It doesn’t affect Jackson…he has seen worse and this isn’t hard to block out. Shaking his head Nameless stares at Matt, through cold black eyes. “Have you changed your mind yet Matt?” Throwing his brown hair back, Jackson smiles and ignores the pain coming from his split and cracked lips. “No, I have told you a million times you prick. You are going to have to kill me if you want your desired result.” “You really like to make this though, don’t you…you son of a bitch,” Nameless says, pacing back and forth on the dirt floor in front of Matt. “We are making this easy on you Jackson. She isn’t worth all this pain and torment.” Maybe he is right…is Haley really worth all of this? Of course she is…I can’t let thoughts like that get to me. I am not going to lose to a man like Jason Hilton, when the prize in this game is so special…so precious. “My decision is final…if you want to kill me do it, but I am not just going to turn my back on Haley. You think that some hired thug like Makonie is going to faze me? The only thing he does is drive me harder to want to kill him,” Matt barks out, smiling all the while…tasting the blood that is pouring into his mouth from his lips. “We can make this highly easy Matt…you are in the Main Event on Turmoil. I checked and to say the least it looks like a few tough cookies are going to be in there with you,” Nameless tells him, letting a smile pass…removing his look of anger. “So?” Matt inquiries. “Well Matt, Violence Jack isn’t small by any means and he is by far a huge star in PCW. Which leaves me wondering what happens if you step into that ring at less then full. You can’t be feeling good right now…just look at your body, it’s a battle field of bruises and cuts.” He is right, the past few weeks I have just been getting by, but Violence Jack isn’t just someone I can get by on. He is the Continental Champion…a veteran and a damn good one at that. Normally I wouldn’t stand a chance, but now I have even less of one. On top of it all…his partner is just a monster. Doesn’t matter though, I won’t give up until I get her back. Letting a sick psychotic laugh filter the room…Nameless turns to Jackson and hits him with a striking backhand. “I see you know I am right Jackson…going into the ring with those two monsters you want to be on your game.” “I know what are you trying to do and it’s not going to work…none of this bothers me. I have been trained to look past all the bullshit,” Matt shutters, feeling the pain surge through his body again. He might be trained for this, but it still affects him the same way he would anyone else…in the psychical sense at the least. Rubbing his chin…Nameless looks on. “I am going to put this into the most simple terms I can…even you will understand these Matt. You have a good thing going for you in PCW. I am not a talent scout, but you are getting the job done. So, just let Haley be and focus on it and let it just happen. You don’t need her you are going to be a huge star on your own,” Nameless quickly lies. “Fuck you,” Matt screams, letting the spit fly from his mouth onto the thug in front of him. Taking a step back…Nameless wipes the spit off of his face and throws his hands down in a rage. He reaches into his back pocket, pulling out the small black taser. Turning it on, he watches the small bit of electricity move between the bars and smiles once again, as he takes a step forward. “I am growing tried of doing this Jackson…but you leave me no choice,” Nameless hisses…sticking the taser into Matt’s barrel of a chest, causing him to grip the flimsy arms of the chair. Within a matter of seconds, his body falls limp…forcing Nameless back. “Matt you need to make the right choice and this will all be over.” It won’t be over until I get my hands on that Nazi bastard…he has thrown me more off path in my quest then anyone. Causing me more pain then anyone and taking what I hold most dear. You will pay Makonie…you will pay with your life. 8:00 AM
