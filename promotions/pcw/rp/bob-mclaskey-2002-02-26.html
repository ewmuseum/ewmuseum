Date: 2-26-02 OOC Note - AIM me at [detail withheld] and MSN me at [detail withheld]. Hello PCW, im back and better than ever. Im going to show you what my 1 posted rp in PWA has taught me... Absolutely nothing. But im getting back into the swing of things and im actually starting to like rping again. But instead of my medium sized crap rps, with no workrate, ive decided to try something different...So, here ya go... Hope you enjoy.... Oh, and due to the graphic nature of this Promo... it has been cut short due to current time constraints... Bob Mclaskey? I was standing there with my jaw dangling as the phone rings again. I look over at the phone and then back at the truck and to the phone again. Trying to get a grasp of everything that is going on, i walk over to the phone and answer it and am relieved to hear the voice of my agent, Jay 'Flash' Richards, on the other side of the reciever... Mark Estep:Hello? Flash:Mark.. where ya been man? Mark Estep:Oh, ive been in a lonely place called PWA.... because my lazy ass agent couldn't get me back in PCW fast enough.... Flash:Hey man, it wasn't my fault. I was talking to Tyler Nelson for the last 2 weeks about it and he just now decided you were ready. How do you feel? Mark Estep:Im fine i guess, but i havent had any physical competition in almost a month... I have gained a little bit of weight over here... Flash:I understand, and thats good because tonight you got a little bit of running to do... Mark Estep:What ya' mean? Flash: I mean, first you have to stop by the PCW board meeting and talk to the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, he has some things to say to you. And you have to sign your contract to make your return final. Mark Estep:Heres a question, does that guy have a real name? Flash:Haha, i don't know... nor do i care... Mark Estep:Haha....And second? Flash:What? Mark Estep:You said first, so i just assumed that there was a second... Flash:Oh yeah, well, you have a commercial that i set up for you tonight... Mark Estep:Let me guess... for Gatorade right? Flash:Hah! Not... Mark Estep:Well, im not doing a commercial for Snapple... i have better things to do than mark for that terrible drink.... Flash:I know, i think it's nasty too.... But no, you have a 'Got Milk' commercial. I tried to get them to come up to Chicago, but i couldn't. Mark Estep:Well, where do i have to go for this?!? Flash:Not too far, just to Pawnee, Illinois... Mark Estep:Where's that at!?! Flash:It's just a few miles south of Springfield. Mark Estep:SPRINGFIELD!?!? Flash:Thats what i said... Mark Estep:Do you know how far away that is? Flash:Far enough i guess.... With that being said, Flash hangs up on his #1 prospect. I hang up slowly and remember the long road trip i have ahead of me before Friday Fusion anyway. So, i put everything behind me, and i pick the phone back up and dial D-Kay Calyx's number again. After 4 rings, D-Kay picks the phone up.... D-Kay Calyx:Yo... Mark Estep:D-Kay, i got us a vehicle... D-Kay Calyx:Can it hold 6 people? I lean over from the kitchen counter top and look out the front door, over Amber's shoulder i catch a glimpse of the truck. Mark Estep:Uhh... I'd think so. D-Kay Calyx:Good... Alright, i have to get ready to go.... Mark Estep:Alright man, ill pick you up in about an hour... D-Kay Calyx:Cool, later dude... Me and D-Kay both hang up and i walk over towards Amber who is still staring in amazement at the huge truck sitting in the driveway. I walk out to the huge truck and step up on the first step and open the door. There are keys sitting on the drivers seat in the front. I sit down on the seat and put the keys in the ignition and turn the keys. The truck starts right up and i sit back in awe at my spectacular feat. I get out of the truck after shutting it back off and i walk back into the house to get a phone book. Amber:What are you looking for? Mark Estep:Well, Bob McClaskey... Amber:Whos that? Mark Estep:Well, in CCW there is a guy called Jason Macallister, and he is also a truck driver. And Jason is currently away on injured leave, so i figured i would call a guy that was in a union with him... Amber:And that is Bob McClaskey right? Mark Estep:Um.. good guess.... She gives me a look that could burn a whole through a truck... a semi truck if you will. As i look through the phone book, i find nothing. Then, i realize that the phone book doesn't have numbers for out of the Chi-town area. I then look up and grab the phone off the counter top and dial 411 Mark Estep:Damn phone book!! The phone rings once and a computer voice comes on. Voice:If you would like to find a number out of the state... press 1 now... Mark Estep:Well, thats me.... I push one... Voice:Please state who you are looking for... Mark Estep:Bob Mclaskey... Voice:Please hold... Moments pass on the phone before the voice finally comes back on... Voice:The number is.... [detail withheld]. Mark Estep:Thanks... The scene fades to black as i write the number down on a piece of paper....
