Reconstruction

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20, 2002 OOC Note: For those not familiar with my work, I usually start out series’ with an Introduction...and that’s what it is. Do not expect the drawn out dialogue between Jack and Johnny that you have come to know and love ; ). Just imagine a highlight reel of The Heartbreakers playing with a deep, narrator’s voice blaring in the background... And so the highlight reel plays, with shots of Ultimate Authority reigning supreme and then The Heartbreakers capturing the gold in a huge upset at Supremacy. “Pink” by Aerosmith blares in the background as the narrator’s voice drowns out the music and begins describing a tale ever so familiar in PCW, and even in the “Political” system that we live in today. The assembling of the newest roster to hit Primetime Championship Wrestling may very properly be made the occasion of a few earnest words on the already much-worn topic of reconstruction. Seldom has any front office in PCW, let alone any leaders at all, been the subject of solicitude more intense, or of aspirations more sincere and ardent. There are the best of reasons for this profound interest. Questions of vast moment, left undecided by the last roster in PCW, must be manfully grappled with by this. No “Political” skirmishing will avail. The occasion demands leadership. Whether the tremendous war at Supremacy, so heroically fought and so victoriously ended, shall pass into history a great success for The Heartbreakers, barren of permanent results, -- a scandalous and shocking waste of blood and treasure, -- a strife for empire, as one may characterize it, of no value to liberty or no value to go down as great ones, -- an attempt to re-establish a new team by force, which must be the merest mockery of a tournament, -- an effort to bring under new tag teams into which will try to destroy the newfound domination brought upon by the PINK!, and to bring men into the tag team circuit who claim to be greats in their own minds and wrestle with “revolvers,” and who do not even conceal their deadly hate of the tag team that conquered all; the tag team which put an end to the “Politically Incorrect” reign of terror the struck PCW for the longest tag team title reign ever...no respect has been given to The Heartbreakers. This tag team tournament really will do nothing that can be considered final as to these questions. The Heartbreakers, Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart, have proven their greatness. They destroyed possibly the greatest tag team in PCW history in a mockery of a match at the pay-per-view at which both teams were fired. They were then bluntly mocked and stripped of their titles, only to have to be shoved to the bottom rung of the ladder once again to prove their worth. The PINK! is not afraid though, the PINK! will not die. Jack and Johnny have every right to be angry at Tyler Nelson and the Ultimate Authority, for it was Politically Incorrect who reigned within the ranks of Ultimate Authority, and it was they who probably had a say as to what titles would be vacated. The new laws and the tournament and the proposed amendments within PCW do not scare The Heartbreakers. It is The Heartbreakers that are leading the way in the tournament, it is the Heartbreakers who the fans will pay money for once they return to the PCW Pavilion...the home of Jack Valentine’s first ever match in PCW. While there remains such an idea as the right of Ultimate Authority to control all title belts within PCW, -- an idea, by the way, more deeply rooted in the minds of men of all sections of PCW than perhaps any one other idea, -- no general assertion of human rights can be of any practical value. Human rights, sportsmanship...they will play no factor in a tournament that will decide who the men are, and who will run off like little boys with their hands down their pants. To change the character of champions at this point is neither possible nor desirable. All that is necessary to be done is to make the consistent with themselves, and render the rights as champions to control all within PCW...The Heartbreakers plan to do this come Friday night. The arm of the Ultimate Authority is long, but it is far too short to control Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart. They must have the power to protect themselves, or they will go unprotected, spite of all that they have placed upon others here in PCW. There is no doubt that they will be trying to get The Heartbreakers out of contention early, starting this Friday against the so-called Dark Messiahs. This control that Ultimate Authority will try to gain early on within the ranks of PCW, like all other great systems of wrong, founded in the depths of human selfishness, and existing for ages, has not neglected its own conservation. It has steadily exerted an influence upon all around it favorable to its own continuance. And to day it is so strong that it could exist, not only without law, but even against law. Custom, manners, morals, religion, are all on its side everywhere in PCW; and when you add the ignorance and servility of the good PCWers to the intelligence and accustomed authority of the Ultimate Authority, you have the conditions, not out of which their reign will again grow, but under which it is impossible for Primetime Championship Wrestling to wholly destroy it, unless the PCW be armed with despotic power, to blot out Ultimate authority, and to join all wrestlers together in a fight against their evil terror. This, of course, cannot be done, and ought not even if it could. The true way and the easiest way is to make PCW entirely consistent with itself, and give to every loyal wrestler the elective franchise, -- a right and power which will be ever present, and will form a wall of fire for his protection. Selfishness will get in the way though. The Heartbreakers really could give a damn if PCW is strong enough to end Ultimate Authority...hell, as much as they hate Ultimate Authority, they wouldn’t mind being a part of it if it allowed them to wear the tag team straps around their waists to that ring. One of the invaluable compensations of the late rebellion is the highly instructive disclosure it made of the true source of danger to PCW. Whatever may be tolerated in monarchical and despotic federations, no federation is safe that tolerates a privileged class, or denies to any of its citizens equal rights and equal means to maintain them. Ultimate Authority will try to take over this federation and it is virtually impossible to stop them. The Heartbreakers must leave the task of destroying Ultimate Authority this Friday to face the team of Mike Stone and Daniel Steel. A team comprised of two wrestlers straight out of PWA...the same place that Jack Valentine wrestled in and Johnny Heart trained wrestlers in. The same place that Jack and Johnny each got their start in. It would be a true test of determination come Fusion, however like Jack and Johnny were upon their arrival in PCW, the Dark Messiahs are inexperienced. Jack and Johnny have developed themselves into THE top tag team in PCW, and they’ll be damned if ANYONE tries to get in the way of the PINK! The highlights pause on two pictures of Jack and Johnny and “A Heartbreakers Production” flashes across the screen in PINK! text...
