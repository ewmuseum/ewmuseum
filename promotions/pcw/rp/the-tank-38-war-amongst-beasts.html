Date: One of the final few RP&#039;s of the Tank Series ﻿*********The Tank __38—********* War Amongst Beasts Collisions of planets occur in varying degrees. War is nature and nature is war. Hating life stemsfrom loving to live. But some of us beastsfight to die. ~Leigh Donovan Tuesday, November 19 - Day 138 Tuesday morning rolls around and the prisoners make their way from their cells, like always, tostart their daily routines. Like many of his fellow inmates, Tim McDonald makes his way to thetelevision where some of the older prisoners have already tuned into the morning news. A total of sixteen bodies, none of which were very recently deceased, were discovered by thepolice late Monday night, and now it looks like at least four very high ranking mobsters will bespending the rest of their lives in prison. And all of this is thanks to Tim McDonald, the sly young non-Italian leader of the Tank’s Italianfaction. He was the one who gave the police the information they needed, anonymously throughthe Warden, and as he stares at the television on the main floor of the Unit, he’s happy to seethat his plan is going as he’s been hoping it would. Now there’s just a matter of a phone call to the right people. But that will come tomorrow, hethinks as he turns to look at Vincent Giovanni over his shoulder. Giovanni is pale and his jaw iswide open as he stares motionlessly at the television. Little does he know that in this deadlygame of chess that he’s been playing with Tim McDonald is about to come to an fatal halt - he’swatching Tim’s move to checkmate on the television screen, and he doesn’t even know it... Tuesday, November 19 - Day 138 With a few more hours to spare before he’s roughly escorted to PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil, JasonSnow peers down over the main floor of Unit B from his perch on the cell balcony. Earlier thismorning, when Snow saw his cellmate, Leigh Donovan, take his coveted blade out of the cell,Snow knew that today was the day that Leigh meant to finally take his shot at J-Thug. And there, sitting with his loafered feet atop one of the checkerboard tables on the main floor, isJ-Thug - as imposing as ever. With massive arms folded across his bare chest, the leader of theTank’s most prominent black gang, the Thugs, watches the television from afar. He doesn’trealize that he’s under the heavy eye of Jason Snow. Snow has never quite known what to think of J-Thug. They’ve had very few run-ins, thoughnone of them have been pleasant. When Snow came to the Tank, over four months ago, J-THugwas the single most dominant beast in the jungle. Now, with Snow’s presence, that’s debatable.At six-feet-one, and a bulky two hundred and fifty pounds, Snow’s not quite so large as J-Thug,who stands six and a half feet tall and weighs nearly three hundred pounds in muscle. Both menare physical specimens in their own ways - Snow, for his unparalleled level of fitness. Eachmuscle rips from another and can draw attention all on it’s own. J-Thug, for his unrivalled sheermass. He’s a man who has never blended into a crowd in his entire life. The giant black beast is wearing a stocking hat that nearly covers his eyes, though from hisperch, Snow can see that they are ever-alert. And that’s what scares Snow about what’s about tohappen. When Leigh Donovan steps in to make the death strike, he won’t stand a chance - he’snot suited for the goal that he has set for himself. J-Thug’s murder is far beyond his reach. Oddly enough, Snow can’t fight the urge to protect Leigh, his cellmate, despite the fact that thetwo have never really got along. When Leigh came to the Tank, soon after Snow did, he wascompletely unprepared, both mentally and physically, for the horrors that were waiting for him. Nearly two months ago, the young man finally snapped. Snow knows that Leigh has been tryingto mold himself into something that can survive in the Tank, but to Snow, the efforts arehopeless. Leigh is confused, and in his confusion, grasping for any lifeline he can in this sea ofhorror - when he grabbed Harvey Blake’s line, however, he was grabbing on to barbed wire. Snow spots Leigh at the far end of the main floor, having just come back from the library. Leightightly clutches a book to his chest as he walks from the heavy steel door toward the middle ofthe room where J-Thug is lounging... Tuesday, November 19 - Day 138 Clutching the Iliad, the largest book he could find in the Unit B library, close to his chest, LeighDonovan has his sights set keenly on J-Thug. The monster of a man is sitting alone in themiddle of the main floor, fortunately, though a few of his henchmen are no doubt lurking nearby. J-Thug has been the primary cause of all Leigh’s pain since coming to the Tank. It was by J-Thug’s heavy hand that Leigh became a ‘prag’ and was forced into doing all sorts of sexualfavours for J-Thug and many other members of the Thugs, the Tank’s most prominent andexclusive black gang. But Leigh fought back. He even went so far once as to bite J-Thug’s penis in protest of one ofthe monster’s grotesque demands. Since then, Leigh knows that his life has been in jeopardy. That’s half the reason why J-Thug must die. The other half of the reason is the fact that Harvey Blake requested J-Thug’s death. HarveyBlake is not a man to disappoint. As he closes in on J-Thug, he can feel the hairs on the back of his neck tickling him, aching to bescratched - but he can’t take a hand off the book that he’s clutching to his chest. When he’s within range, he cautiously slips the blade out of from the middle of the book andforcefully pushes it toward the back of J-Thug’s head - he hopes the massive man will diesilently so he can get out of range by the time the commotion starts. But J-Thug doesn’t die at all. The moment he feels the pointed end of the steel touch thestocking cap on the back of his cranium, he whirls around with the reflexes of a mammoth catand pounces on his would-be attacker with a sloppy combination of looping right and lefthanded punches. Before he knows what’s happening, Leigh Donovan gets cracked with a right hand on the chinand finds himself unable to keep his feet beneath him - the mighty blow sends a shock clear tohis knees, and soon enough, he topples over. From every corner of the Unit, Thug members attempt to aid their leader in his beating of theyoung Australian, and it’s all the C.O’s of the Unit can do to keep an all-out riot from ensuing. Harvey Blake’s Nazis have taken the opportunity to start smaller scuffles with members of J-Thug’s gang members, something that also draws the attention of the C.O’s on the floor. And in the middle of it all, Leigh Donovan is left to fend for himself against the black monsterknown as J-Thug. Be it the fact that their attention is needed in other areas, or the fact that noneof them fancy dealing with the ogre, each and every C.O on the floor finds another area to usehis skills, and Leigh Donovan remains unaided in the middle of the main floor. “You think your man enough to snuff me out now, prag?” J-Thug fires simultaneously with athunderous right hand that breaks through Leigh’s wiry defense and smashes into his left cheek. “Look what you got yo-self into, motherfucker!” He fires another booming right that jarsLeigh’s entire body. “You should have stayed in your place as a bitch! My bitch! Now you gotto motherfucking pay, prag! You fucking stepped up, now it’s time to get your punk assknocked down!” J-Thug fires yet another right while holding his struggling victim down with his mighty left. From the floor, Leigh manages to see through layers of blood and tears well enough to reach hisown wiry hand around the incoming bombs and scratches wildly at J-Thug’s face. “Nigger!” hescreams in a loud shrilly voice that can be heard above all the commotion. His fingernails,which he’s been growing for the past few weeks for just this type of situation, slice deep into J-Thug’s face and he begins tearing skin away, bit by bit. J-Thug lets out a mighty howl while he sloppily grabs at Leigh’s free and torturing right hand,taking the opportunity to land another pair of crushing blows. With every smash, Leigh Donovan can feel a little more of his consciousness slipping from hisgrasp, though most of the punches aren’t landing clean enough to pose a threat of knocking himfully out with a single landing. Fights are breaking out all over the Unit. On the cell balcony, Harvey Blake and Jake Hooperare savagely beating a young black man and are trying to toss him over the edge. Near the heavydoors that lead to the gym and the library beyond, two Hispanic men have come to blows with aC.O that is trying to hold them away from one of Harvey Blake’s Aryan’s. Tim McDonald is inhis cell, ironically with Vincent Giovanni, being guarded by the three largest members of hisItalian mob in Unit B. Leigh’s blood splashes on the floor as J-Thug lands a blow perfectly clean, and the youngAustralian’s hand falls limply to his side. Despite everything in his mind, heart, and soul,commanding his body to attack, his body is unresponsive - all lines of communication betweenhis brain and his muscles have been shut down. Another blow lands and Leigh notices that hecan no longer open his left eye - he only hopes that he still has one. Another blow lands and hisvision is replaced by a prolonged white flash... ...And then it stops... No more thunderous shots, just the pain that remains. For a moment, Leigh thinks he’s dead - orat the very least, so close to death that his soul has detached itself from his body. But there’sevidence to the opposite - he can still hear the commotion of the chaos, which by now, cannearly be classified as a full scale riot. He can still taste the blood that coats his mouth and isrunning down his throat. A moment later, his vision restores itself, in the eye that’s still useful,and he finds a brawl under way just a few feet away from his face. Through his clouded vision, Leigh can easily tell that the mammoth black figure is J-Thug. Tohis surprise, however, it’s the moderately smaller white figure that’s dealing out the majority ofthe punishment. J-Thug stumbles back, getting hit with the fourth consecutive blow - he can’t help but feelsurprised that this one nearly crumbles him. He’s never been handled like this before, andthrough the rapid fire of arms and fists, he still has yet to be able to identify his attacker - theman who pulled him away from Leigh Donovan a mere moment before he delivered what he wascertain would be the deathblow. A fifth and sixth shot slam into J-Thug’s face, through his barrier of massive black arms. Aseventh. The eighth shot is finally enough to send the beast to one knee, where he feels the rock-hard heel of a boot collide with the open-side of his scratched face. The mammoth struggles to regain his feet, but the surprising power of his attacker isoverwhelming. With what logic he’s able to muster in a situation such as this, he realizes thatthere’s only one man in the Unit, maybe the entire Tank, with the power and ability to match hisown. From Leigh’s perspective, things are beginning to get more clear. Clearly, he sees J-Thugstruggling to hold his balance on one knee while the attacker attacks with blow after blow like alumberjack cutting down an impossible tree one swing at a time. Leigh struggles to smile, though his teeth have nearly all been shattered and his mouth is amodern masterpiece of blood and bruise. It must be Harvey Blake, he thinks - the one-eyedAryan leader must have come to his rescue, and just like he did twenty years ago, is beating theleader of the Thugs at his own game. Leigh’s illusion of his savior is shattered when his good eye recovers enough to see the flowinglocks of blond hair that whip and bounce with every motion. His yellow beard frames grittedteeth and his eyes are as cold and blue a frozen ocean. Finally, he fires a rising knee into thecranium of J-Thug, and the black beast topples to his side and covers his bloody head with hisarms. When the dust settles, the rhino is lying on the floor, no longer able to continue, while the lionstands heaving over his fallen prey. With gritted teeth and a heavy chest, Jason Snow peers down at J-Thug; the ultimate beast -conquered. He looks even larger on the floor, thinks Snow when the adrenalin finally begins todrain and his reason breaks free of distortion. His motionless body covers so much space. Leigh Donovan rolls onto his left side and vomits a mixture of blood and food, and then liesthere in it, unable to find the strength or desperation to roll away. “I cou-” he tries to saysomething to Snow, but finds his fractured jaw and lack of teeth too much to overcome. “I di-”he tries again, but pushing the words are too painful. Snow knows what Leigh’s trying to saythough. He didn’t need any help. Ironically, despite the state of the once-handsome youngrockstar’s face, Snow can still see the anger being conveyed. “Sure you didn’t,” Snow says to him. The site of J-Thug’s limp body attracts the attention of the every prisoner and C.O in the entireUnit. A message has been sent, and it’s words are loud and clear. Like a wave, the fightingstops. Tim McDonald stands in front of his three Italian body guards, looking down at the rubble. Hecan’t believe his eyes. Neither can Vincent Giovanni, who’s been plotting both McDonaldand Snow’s death for quite some time - he never dreamed his opponent this formidable. Benjamin Graham, head C.O of the Tank, turned away from the brawl he was attempted to breakup before it was even over. The lack of his attention broke the fight up, and all of their eyes arenow fixed firmly on the scene in the middle of the Unit. Jake Hooper takes a step back from the balcony, releasing his grip on the young black man’sthroat - he and Harvey Blake were still struggling to toss the powerful young man over the edgewhen the scene caught their attention. There, larger than life in the middle of the Unit, J-Thughad been crumbled and is on display for every prisoner in the large room. With bloody knuckles and a fire glowing blue in his eyes, Snow steps over the injured beast andwalks across the floor to the stairs that lead up to the cell balcony. He’s never experienced suchsilence in prison as the prisoners are giving him now. Every footstep echoes like an example ofunwavering power. With every eye, even the lone one of Harvey Blake, Snow makes his way to his cell to preparefor Turmoil.
