The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 6

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-17-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 6” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: BladeD is going to be taking on the greatest task known to man. Metal Armor is a machine built only to destroy. It isn’t a weapon of defense at all. BladeD is about to find out the mission and the president is going to be the one to tell him, but will his parents allow it? ... ... Wait a second… That just sounds weird, (but remember that the time is 1994, so he is only 14 years old at this time), but anyway. How will BladeD react? :: :: Scene - Office of the president. :: :: knock, knock, knock :: Mr. President: Come in! Commander Fuller: Hello president. General Powers: Hello sir. Mr. President: Hello Commander Fuller… and General Powers. What brings you to my office? Commander Fuller: Well sir. We were given a letter from an unknown person at the moment. He has requested that no one reads the full letter and we haven’t even finished reading the letter yet ourselves because it is so long, but we found a part in it that you might want to know. Mr. President: Go on. :: At this point Commander Fuller begins telling the story about the letter. He is telling the president about Metal Armor, the threats, and the money issues. Basically everything you have read so far is what Commander Fuller is telling the president, and then Commander Fuller allows the president to see the part of the letter that he is allowed to see. :: Mr. President: Well, this is all very interesting and so unexpected, but we are going to have to get BladeD’s parents on the phone and have them bring BladeD down here. I still don’t think it would be the best idea for BladeD to do this job. General Powers: I am glad you agree with me sir because I was telling the commander here the same exact thing. Commander Fuller: Well, I have seen what my men can do and cannot do. I have seen what BladeD can and cannot do. I would rather risk me life with BladeD even at 14, then with a 30-year-old experienced person. It sounds insane and I know it does, but you have to realize that it is the best situation at the moment. Mr. President: If that is what you believe, then that is what I am going to go for because I know that you are going to make the best decision for the world. I need to keep this part of the letter if you don’t mind, just in case we need to go into any type of war, I will have the proof I need to send out troops or use nuclear weapons. Once you find out whom this guy is I want you to inform for. Now let me get on the phone and tell the secretary to get BladeD in here. :: Mr. President phones the secretary letting the secretary know that this is urgent and that a limo will arrive at BladeD house to pick him up, along with his parents. :: Commander Fuller: Well, what you have to understand is that we were told in another part of the letter that once Metal Armor is launched there is no turning back, and it would take more than one nuclear missile to destroy it. Mr. President: WHAT!!!!! Commander Fuller: I know, but that is what we were told by this. Mr. President: That just doesn’t make any sense. We have the strongest nuclear weapons in the world. General Powers: But we need to trust the person that sent this letter because he has given us more information in this letter that we haven’t even finished yet, than we could have gotten in a month’s worth of work, so we need to trust the guy for the information he has freely given us. I mean he is hoping that we at least trust him on his every word. Mr. President: But we have no clue who this guy is or where he is from. We don’t even know whom he works for. Commander Fuller: But with information this valuable we need to listen to his every word of advice sir, so don’t worry about nuking anyone because it isn’t going to be enough to do much. All it is going to do is make this situation worse. If congress or any people bring up that situation of nuking like that it is totally out of the question by MY ORDERS!Mr. President: Well, you are the head of all this, so I guess is there isn’t much more I can do, but listen to you. Commander Fuller: Well, I hope you can try and understand where I am coming from on this matter because I would really hate for you feel bad through all this. My people are stressful enough as it is and we don’t need an even more stressful president. I have been doing this sort of thing for 15 years. I have a handle on what to do and what not to do. :: While talking there is a knock on the door. :: Commander Fuller and Mr. President (Simultaneously): Come in! BladeD’s Dad: Hello Commander Fuller, General Powers, and Mr. President. BladeD’s Mom: Hello everyone. BladeD: Sup yo? Mr. President: Welcome everyone. :: quick pause :: Here, :: points at a chair. :: sit down. We have a lot to talk about. BladeD: Mr. President. What brings us here? Commander Fuller: Mr. President. Let me answer for you… BladeD… We need. BladeD: What do you need me for? Do you want me to go through training some kind of typing or something? Commander Fuller: Not exactly. :: Commander Fuller begins to explain the story from the letter (that he hasn’t finished reading yet!!!) Basically just letting BladeD and his parents in on the situation at hand. BladeD parent’s faces change through out the story being told. You can see everything from looks of shock to looks of fear, angry, and sadness, but when Commander Fuller explains to them that BladeD is the one that is chosen for the mission at hand is when the real emotions are let out because BladeD’s mother begins to cry. She doesn’t want to have her “baby boy” at risk of death even though she knows that the U.S. could lose their lives over this. BladeD seems clam and collected. He hasn’t changed an emotion through out the whole thing and the scene goes there. :: Mr. President: BladeD… How do you feel? I mean you haven’t changed the expression on your face since we began. BladeD: Two words… ………… I’m ready. BladeD’s Mother: :: in a crying voice :: No BladeD! No!BladeD: Mother. Listen to me on this one. I may only be 14-years-old, and I am probably still just a baby to you, but I have matured faster than most 14-year-olds and you must understand that what I am about to do isn’t for me, or for the Commander, or even the president. The mission I am about to take part in is for the safety of the United States of America. Mr. President: Wow BladeD. You are an amazing kid. :: The scene is going to cut here. We just got into a very emotional situation and that is what’s making this so great. Don’t you hate it when the story is stopped right in the middle of emotional parts like this because I really enjoy stopping at points like these. Nothing else is going to be revealed yet, so all you can do is just stay tuned! ::
