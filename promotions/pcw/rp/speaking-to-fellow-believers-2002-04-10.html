Speaking To Fellow Believers...

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/04/02 Ellie~!’s Prologue : Okay, so it’s like Tuesday now, and normally we would normally be at Turmoil, but because of that thing on Sunday, a Per per View I think it was called, but I can’t work out why as they let us in for free...Anyway, Xavier has taken this night off to go and do one of his Scientology things so I guess I’ll go with him, it’ll give me a chance to sleep and him a chance to talk about himself...Two things we both like doing. Anyways, that is about it, unless you can think of anything I’ve missed...Did I mention he won the Continental Title?..... Our shot opens up outside the San Diego Convention Centre, just as shot walk away from the arena where the post-Spring Sling Turmoil will take place, pitting the new Continental Champion Xavier Kannon against an old adversary in the Pharaoh. “So...Like, all you do is talk to them and they listen? I dunno...Sounds kinda boring...” Right on cue, the whiny lack of enthusiasm that is the voice of Eleanor hall, valet of Xavier Kannon is heard of shot, voicing her disapproval that being here as the camera pans around to show a large sign upon which is written CALIFORNAIN CONVENTION OF SCIENTOLOGISTS. “Relax my little Poon Puppy, Scientology is a Religion of order and respect. They listen to me out of admiration because unlike other MAJOR Religions we aren’t famed for Child Abuse or Terrorism.....So basically they do what we say...” The sound of two pairs of footsteps is heard getting nearer as the voice of wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon is heard replying to that of his valet. He speaks in his usual, relaxed and bordering on cocky tone in contrast to his valet. “Would they do anything I I wanted seeing as I’m the valet of a Scientologist? Will they? Huh....Xavi?” The camera scrolls down the sign advertising the Convention and to the smaller lettering below it where it reads Guest Speakers : Xavier Kannon, John Travolta & Isaac Hayes. After a pause Xavier replies to her, this time his voice having a certain sigh to it, as if he is appeasing her with it. “Okay...If you tell them you are with me my little Shag Squirrel...Then they will do what you say. If they don’t, get their name and they will be dragged outside and kil-...I mean, e-mailed a reminder to be polite....” Just following his slip of the tongue that nearly admitted some of the practices Scientology are accused of, the 1st couple of the said Religion appear in shot, walking to the foot of the wide pal Grey concrete steps towards the 5 sets of Glass double doors that stand at the top of them. .....Yay, a room full of Scientologists, you can imagine how thrilled I was about that...That was sarcasm by the way...But that last bit wasn’t..I’m confused...Anyways, it bets being surrounded by the likes of Jeremy Howard and Duke Williams so I headed on in.......... Xavier, with his PCW Continental Title proudly slung over his shoulder, is wearing a navy Blue Versace suit with a Silvery-Grey shirt and contrasting tie underneath, part of his neat new Corporate image his is inheriting through Tyler Nelson. His long, Auburn hair seems to have Been shortened a bit and is tightly tied back, again, looking smart and tidy. Xavier : “Anyway, I shouldn’t be here too long, these Californians crave stars so I’ll just show up, wave a bit, maybe even crack a smile, talk about whatever I want no matter how far off topic a-” As he runs down his plans and explains to his preppy valet what they are here for her attentions seems to dwindle, not being focused anywhere else but instead, in the space of 2 seconds she drifts from interest to abject boredom. Eleanor : “Okay, okay...I get it! You turn up...Blah blah....What were the last two again?” His valet is wearing a Pink, skinny fit sleeveless top with Snapple~! written in sparkly Silver across the chest. With it she wears a Brown suede knee length skirt and matching boots. Her long, wavy brown hair with Blond highlights is neatly tied back with just a little left loose to fall down the sides of her face. ......What? I have a short attention span, it’s not my fault. I just spent the last month listening to Jeremy Howard and Bobby Stormbringer ramble on and you expect me to keep interest in stuff...Well, at least Jeremy’s were relevant...But he called me a bitch and said he’d break my face so I don’t like him..... The Scientologist sighs at his valet’s annoying character traits as they walk through the set of Silver Steel framed Glass double doors farthest to the left and as they walk through a dark Red rope is set up to separate the path this door leads to from the main foyer. “Well if it isn’t out little Gold patron meritorious....” As Xavier checks a map of the nearest auditorium, Ellie~! looking over his shoulder but not showing much interest, a loud, deep, booming voice is heard from off shot, one that Xavier recognises but Ellie~! just looks blank face at. The corner of Xavier’s mouth breaks into a smirk as he chuckles to himself and looks up. Xavier : “Well if it isn’t our biggest, baddest Patron with Honours!” The 1st couple of Scientology turn to be faced with the site of 70’s soul singer, composer of the theme from Shaft and the voice of South park’s chef...As well as being a fine upstanding Scientologist...Isaac Hayes. The singer is dressed in a smart Black suit and wears his pair of trademark dark glasses. Xavier : “How in the name of Xenu are you?” The pair of Scientologists walk towards each other and shake hands before hugging in a gesture that sends a message to solidify the strength of Scientology against all those who try to oppress it. Eleanor stands a few feet behind Xavier, on hand on her hips and the other twiddling with her hair and a look of boredom already crossing her face. Isaac : “I’m good...I should be speaking after you...But as usual, I bet that half the people will leave seeing as they only turn up to be witness to your wise words, not dissimilar to the Sermon on the Mount that...Other guy...Supposedly gave!” The Gold Patron Meritorious nods and grins smugly at the compliment that Isaac gave him. He then pats the big man on the shoulder, trying to reassure him. Xavier : “I’ll try keep a few of them back for you...I’ll say we are throwing some Hari Krishnas in stocks and pelting them with rotten tomatoes for 10 minutes...Anyways, I heard a rumour you had defected to Crunk?” At the mention of this, Isaac’s eyes suddenly look from left to right very quickly, looking almost uncomfortable at the accusation of treachery before Xavier’s solemn face breaks into laughter, after a second Isaac catches on to the joke. Xavier : “I’m just kidding you big man...I know a man as high up in Scientology as you would never join another Minority Reli-...Wait, make that a THIRD RATE Religion...Like Crunk!” A look of disgust at the mention of Crunk, PCW’s official Religion for 5 days before Spring Fling, only overturned after Xavier and the rest of the Ultimate Authority won the bidding war for the ownership of the Fed. Beside them, Eleanor is looking blankly into space, humming to herself. ........So they were, like, talking and stuff and I was all, like YAWN! But anyway, I think that is Barry White, you know the guy the weirdo sees in Ally McBeal...I love that show.... Isaac : “Yeah, I nearly broke out into laughter when I was asked to join. Told that it was the Religion of my people, that I was the Crunkest singer in all Crunkdom thus I should join....But CRUNK? Look at who they had supporting them? Some washed up hick and a failed wrestler. You wouldn’t catch someone just inventing Scientology on the spot!” Their discussion of the Scientology versus Crunk debate is broken off as a voice over the public announce system in the auditorium next to them is heard and a man in a Grey suit steps out through the deep Red curtains to hold them open. “Now, welcoming our first very special guest...he is the PCW Continental Champion, overcoming oppression both from the President and from other wrestlers...he was born into poverty in Hell’s Kitchen but unlike all the moaners and complainers that are the cancer of out nation he bettered himself...he is GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS XAVIER KANNON!!!” The man in the grey suit holds his ear, receiving a message through his ear piece before he gestures for Xavier and Ellie~! to head onto the stage. Xavier says his farewells to Isaac who heads off to take his seat in the crowd. YEEEEEEAAAAAAAHHHH!!! As the firs couple of Scientology walk out onto the stage they receive a standing ovation from the Scientologists from all around the world who have travelled for the rare chance to hear the words of Scientologists such as Xavier, John and Isaac. Ellie~! seems particularly pleased with the reception. Eleanor : “Wow...They like SO love me...YAY ME!!! Hi Ellie~! fans!” She bounces on the spot and waves to the fans before taking one of the seats at the back of the stage. Xavier walks to the podium as the rows of monitors over his head show his approach from all angles. He gets to the lectern and looks out into the sea of Scientologists before him, their applause dying down. He then looks to his Continental Title that is draped over his shoulder and pats it three times before twitching his left shoulder to drop it down onto his hand before he holds it aloft to another rousing ovation and barrage of camera flashes. In her chair at the back Ellie~! mutters to herself as she sees the Gold Patron Meritorious basking n the adulation. Eleanor : “Damn show off...Stealing my applause....Like, SO unfair...” Kannon lowers the Continental Title and drapes it over the lectern as he adjusts the mic to his liking. he then clears his throat and looks out into the auditorium, almost showing nerves. Xavier : “Hello my fellow Scientologists...I was wondering all the way here...On the drive from Anaheim, the scene of my glorious achievements on Sunday...I could talk to you about the foundings of Scientology, how Hubbard first pulled together the proof to show it is the way.....I could talk to you about the endless oppression Scientologists suffer, but I am no complainer, I am no whiner...Instead, I will talk to you about....ME and all that I have...And will achieve!” At first the audience seem a little stunned by his topic matter but then slowly start to applaud his upcoming talk as Xavier grins smugly at the chance to talk about himself as he adjusts his tie. Xavier : “Now, as you would have seen on Sunday, when you paid your $40 to the then corrupt PCW to watch one of the greatest moments in the history of Scientology. When I climbed that ladder to grab the Continental Title, just like Hubbard did himself, reaching up to garb the knowledge out faith was founded on. After 3 months of being looked down upon, being held back, and having to accept treatment as a second class citizen...I OVERCAME it all to win...This...” He pats his Title, sat proudly before him as the video highlights of his match with Jeremy Howard roll to a close and the images return to the live feed of him at the lectern. Xavier : “But unlike this GOD the Christians talk of, I didn’t just rest on that, I didn’t take the proverbial 7th day off to rest...no, I decided that NO MORE would any wrestler in PCW have to be subjected to the intimidation I was...So I took decisive action, and using the budget from Battlefield Earth II, a movie that narrow-minded critics were just lining up to slate...And I gave it to the one man who treated me fairly...Tyler Nelson!” The mentioning of the former-Triple Crown Executive gets an enormous round of applause for his support of wrestling’s only Scientologist. DA BOSS~!, as he is known, having a found place in the hearts of the nation’s Dianetics followers. Xavier : “Now, I, a man born a poor child in Hell’s Kitchen is now 20% owner of Primetime Championship Wrestling. How did I get there you ask? Was it through hard work? Well, only to overcome the barriers other people put in my way. Was it though bribery? Well, those accusations have NO LEGAL FOUNDING! No, I got it through SCIENTOLOGY! Were it not for Hubbard I would be living in the gutter, COSTING you money, being a burden on society.....SCIENTOLOGY saved me, just like it saved you, and I cane save your friends and family!” His passionate words stir up the crowd who again spring to their feet and applaud him, cheering his words and support for the faith they follow. As the crowd settle down, Xavi takes a second to compose himself, loosening his shoulders. ........I’m glad to see that SOMEONE his having fun up there...Sitting at the back is boring and this chair is uncomfortable so I can’t sleep. I wish Preps has Conventions. They would rule..... Xavier : “This week coming up will be the most important for Scientology since the 50’s, the week where Hubbard finished his draft and first showed it to those others open minded enough to accept the truth. For this week, even though I won a part of PCW and don’t even have to, I am fighting for YOU three times! I will step into that ring for all who follow Scientology, and I will win....FOR YOU!” As they slowly stand to applaud him he steps back from the lectern and holds his Title aloft to a giant roar which finally spurs the crowd fully to heir feet for a final standing ovation as the Scientologist takes a bow to his peers. Xavier : “Thank you...Thank you...Your adulation of me is much appreciated, and mush deserved...” He finally drags himself away from the jubilation in his name and Ellie~! rises from her sea, stretching before joining him by his side as they walk towards the curtain. Xavier : “Even if I do say so myself I think that went brilliantly...Don’t you?” The first couple of Scientology are ushered through the curtain as the shot fades to Black and Ellie~! replies unenthusiastically in a tired voice, stretching once again. Eleanor : “Er...Like...BORING!!!!!!!“ The sound of the Gold Patron Meritorious sighing is just heard above the applause as the shot fades out to Black. “Thanks Ellie~!, your enthusiasm in my work is what drives me...But something tells me at Turmoil you’ll be pleased....”
