Meeting with a 'friend'?

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-30-02 Joey: Yo Buddy wake up. We're here. Ricky awakens to a startling shaking that he is receiving from the young shipmate. He holds his head slightly and sits up a bit. He looks around blankly for a few seconds before remembering the boat trip to Haiti. He gets to his feet still a bit wobbly, but bounces between the walls going up the small flight of stairs. He gets to the deck and looks around his vision still a bit blurry. He heads over to where the crew is leaving the ship down the large flight of stairs and stumbles down those as well. He gets to the bottom, and shields his eyes from the sun, the glare so intense he can barely see. He makes his way to the street where there is a man waiting standing and holding a sign, that reads 'Ricky Thunder'. he is standing next to a short black limo, and is wearing a chauffeurs outfit. Ricky head over to the car and greets the guy. Ricky: Hey, I'm Ricky T, what can I do for ya? Who sent you? Nobody knew I was catching this ship. Driver: Bonjour, Ricky Thunder. Je parle' Anglais, mais ne tres pas bien. Parlez' vous francais? Ricky: What?! NO! No habla frenchy....or whatever.... The driver chuckles a bit as he opens the door for Ricky. Ricky slides into the lush backseat still wondering where the car came from. The driver heads around and gets in and they are on their way. The car travels just a few blocks before the clicking of the locks can be heard, as they pull up to a stop light. Ricky looks around as suddenly juveniles ranging in age from about 8-14 start pounding against the windows, many of them carrying assault rifles. They continue to test the handles and beat on the windows, but none of them draw fire at the car. Ricky bewildered by all of this, slumps down in his seat a bit, when the locks again click, and the back door opens. A vaguely familiar looking man slides in and Ricky stares at him for a second. Flashbacks run through Ricky's head..... *The Blazer pulls into a parking lot for a Denny's. They pull to the backside of the lot, and exit out. There are no other cars in this particular area, and directly across the street is a few young thugs eyeballing the duo carefully. Tony glances back at them and shoots a quick grin, the two young thugs leave the area and disappear back inside the old abandoned building. Ricky and his uncle head inside the restaurant where a jukebox sits right inside the door and booths line the wall with a few available seats at the bar. They make their way to the corner booth and have a seat as their waitress comes over to take their order* Ricky: Just a glass of water for now thank you. Tony: I'll have the #4 platter with a diet coke. *The waitress jots down the order and makes her way to the back, as Ricky and Tony stare silently across the table at one another. Tony's face plagued with pain, the pain one goes through when a close friend or relative is suffering. Ricky's face spattered in flashes of hate, violence, and fear. Ricky buries his head in his hands as his hair falls through his fingers.* Ricky: I just don't' know what to do Tony. I mean, I know I have a chance for a new start here, but what everyone else needs to know, is my past. They need to respect me for what I have done, they need to respect me for who I am. Not for my brother's accomplishments. Tony: Ricky, how about this? You start over. Clean slate. No more past, no more future, just right now! You need to pick a place to begin and right now it seems you can do that. With your fresh start in PCW it gives you the opportunity to divulge in greater things. Look at what you accomplished in CCW in your short tenure. You now have the chance to prove yourself in a new fed. I mean come on Ricky, you are the 'King of Extreme'. Ricky: Speaking of which. I signed up for an Extreme Title match next week. It is a school grounds match. First man off the grounds wins. There will be legends and former champions in this match. I am risking a lot here. I could prove once again why I was the King of Extreme or I can be embarrassed, and again falter where I should flourish. Tony: Don't worry about it Ricky. You have what it takes. You have always had it in you, that competitive spirit. That drive of carnal anger. You can be the one to lead PCW into it's next era, you know it as well as I do Ricky. The Phantom Booker does not sign just any ordinary, no talent, slacker. If he signed you he sees your potential. Now it is time to grab that bull by the horns and take charge. This is your time Ricky. You are now PRIMETIME! *The waitress arrives with Tony's food and their drinks, as Tony begins to chow down on his meal, Ricky leans back against the bench watching the traffic shoot by on the Avenue. The scene fades out as Ricky gets up and exits the restaurant with his uncles eye's following him.* Ricky: Tony. I just wanted to say I had been thinking. While you were eating I decided something. Tony: And what was that you decided Ricky? Ricky: Well thought about what you said and I decided you were right. There is no need no mull over the past. What is done is done. Shane was a great athlete and was respected for it. Now it is my turn to shine as I will be leading the new wave of talent in the PCW. I mean hell, The Phantom Booker personally selected me to be in his new Phantom Phive. The PCW is where I let go of Shane's legacy and start leaving my own. Friday Fusion, I am going to show up for that Schools Out Match, and the only thing that will be out, is me. Out of the building and off the grounds that is. Tony: Ahh, that a boy Ricky. I knew you would come to. Hey I gotta get back to work, let's go. *The duo steps around the side of the building and notices the two thugs that were eyeballing them when they arrived, rummaging through the truck. Tony shouts out at them as one of them takes off running while the other stands there frozen in fear. Ricky and Tony rush to the Blazer as the young man stands there silently. Ricky lunges at the guy as Tony intercepts him, and asks him to wait by the building. Ricky growls at the guy as he takes a step backwards before retreating to the building. Tony reaches out and places his hands on the guys shoulders. As he begins speaking to him the kid remains motionless and silent, only nodding occasionally to acknowledge what is being said. The young mans face is paralyzed in fear, his eyes locked on the man in front of him. Suddenly he jerks backwards and steps away from Tony, there is a quick flash of steel and a loud bang as Tony hits the ground. Ricky's eyes widen as the guy bolts from the scene scaling the chain link fence, and disappearing into the night. Ricky rushes to his uncles side who is lying on the ground clutching his stomach. The red blood is flowing through his fingers as Ricky kneels beside his uncle* Ricky: I'm sorry Tony, you shoulda let me kill him when I started too. Tony: I'll be ok. It's just a flesh wound. Besides, if this had been you laying here, how would you win that match on Fusion? Ricky: This is no time for jokes Tony. I'll be right back, I am going to run inside and call an ambulance. You going to be ok, long enough for me to? Tony: Well, I am going to lie out here and bleed to death if you don't, so I guess I will have to be. *A small smile comes across the face of Tony as Ricky jumps to his feet and rushes inside.* Ricky: Hey Lady!!! I need to use your phone!! My uncles been shot! Waitress: Oh my! Is he ok? Ricky: The phone!!! *The waitress hands him a portable phone, and stands near as he makes the call to 911* Ricky: Yeah, uhmm, My uncle's just been shot...we are at Denny's, on Haddon Avenue. He is around back..hurry up! I don't knowe what the guy looked like..and he had better be damn glad! Now, please hurry up! *Ricky tosses the phone back to the waitress and rushes back out the doors. As he rounds the backside of the building he notices the first guy that had fled the scene had returned. He was crouched down near his uncle, but suddenly stands straight up. Ricky can see that he is holding his wallet in his hand, the guy looks down at Tony then fires 3 more shots into him. He drops the pistol on him and says* Man: I guess you were right Tony, all bets were off. Looks like you lose this time. *Ricky screams out as he rushes towards the guy who then follows his friends lead and disappears over the fence and into the night* Scene cuts away to the police station later that night Officer: By the way, my name is Officer O'Malley. The reason I asked you to come down here, was well, to clarify some things. Your uncle Tony, wasn't everything you thought him to be. Ricky: What are you saying? O'Malley: Your uncle's job, his persona, everything. It was a cover up. We have been tracking one, Tony Pricana, for about 2 years now. He has had strong lines linked to the underground mafia he in New Jersey. The DEA, FBI, local boys, everyone. We have all been watching him. I am surprised he didn't know. The truth of the matter is... Ricky: Wait a minute. You're telling me my uncle was a mob boss? get off of it. Tony was no mobster, he was the nicest guy you could ever meet. He didn't deserve what happened to him tonight. Nobody deserves that. It is just bullshit that it went down that way. The guys who did this will pay for it, I will assure you that. O'Malley: Let me show you some video footage we had confiscated from surveillance. *O'Malley gets up and goes over to the small tv/vcr combo sitting on top of the steel file cabinet. He hits play on the panel and the tape jerks to life. A soundless black and white video begins to play as it shows Tony, socializing with a group of well known local gangsters. They stand about talking for a bit when the door busts in. There is a large guy standing there holding a slim man, wearing a pinstripe suit and glasses, forcing him into the room. A few guys run up and take their punches. Then they all step back and one guys approaches Tony, speaking to him. He then points to the guy as Tony shakes his head, but the man initiates another conversation and hands Tony a .45 automatic. Tony puts the gun to the small, professional looking man's temple. He wiggles his fingers a bit a takes a deep breath as suddenly, the big guy, pulls a gun out and blasts the business man in the back of the head.* O'Malley: You see. That man that was killed. Gregory A. Studegar. He was a well known bookie. He had no job, no family to speak of, and absolutely no background. We tried for a long time to bring him in, but we had nothing on him, however, his obvious ties with the mob finally did him in. He took one bet too many. Did you happen to see the guys who were responsible for his shooting? Ricky: Only ...for a second... *Ricky's voice trails off as he is still staring at the blank tv screen. He doesn't know what to make of what he just saw. He glances around the room then back at the officer* Ricky: I think I need to go. O'Malley: Well I want to show you a few pictures real quick. See if this is one of the guys who were responsible. *The officer shows him a few different pictures before Ricky pinpoints one* Ricky: That's him. I'm sure of it. Who is he? O'Malley: Reggie K. Griffen. Hired hitman for the mob. I wouldn't take any chances, I would get out here as soon as possible Ricky. These aren't the kind of guys you play around with. Ricky: I believe you. I got to get to the airport anyhow, I have to get ready to head out for fusion. About Tony... O'Malley: You know as well as I do.... * A small tear drops from the eye of Ricky Thunder as he gets up and makes his way from the office and back down the corridor leading outside. He exits the police station and hails a cab, as he instructs him to the airport.* The scene shifts back to modern time as Ricky shakes the cobwebs from his clouded head. He stare wide eyed at the man sitting next to him. Ricky: Griffen! What the fuck.... Reggie: That's right Ricky boy. Ya see, I heard a rumor that you pointed me out at the police station that night, with the incident, with your uncle. What you didn't expect was...well, the fact that O'Malley works for us as well. He decided to pretend to care, he decided to pretend to be a cop for the night, and well you bought it. You skipped town, there was no trial, there was nothing, ESPECIALLY NO WITNESSES. However, with all this now resurfacing and me hearing you had some...well....business here in Haiti, I figured what a 'PRIME TIME' to fix up loose ends. Ya know? It's only business. Ricky: What the fuck do you want from me? I mean as far as I knew, O'Malley was legit, I wasn't goin anywhere or saying anything. All I know, Is that you killed my uncle. Ricky lunges across the seat but Reggie grabs him by his neck and pins him back. He pulls out a .45 and sticks it right at the tip of Ricky's nose. As Ricky glares down the barrel of the gun, into the eyes of a mad man, he shows no fear. No quit, and absolutely, no give. He sneers a bit and Reggie cocks the gun. Suddenly Ricky spits right in the face of Reggie, and as Reggie stares in disbelief, Ricky sits back in the seat and grabs the gun. He places the barrel right at his forehead and demands Reggie to pull the trigger. Reggie smiles a sadistic smile and looks at Ricky before lowering the gun. Reggie: You got heart kid. You could be of use to us after all. I guess he was always right. Ricky: I ain't helping you. No fucking way. Reggie: I got something you may be interested in. By the way, we heard about the car, I can get you another one in a few hours. You want the same kind? Ricky: What the hell is going on? One minute you want to kill me the next you are giving me my preference of cars? MY FUCKIN HEAD IS KILLIN ME!!!!!! Reggie: Soon Ricky. Sit back and relax. Everything will be clear soon.
