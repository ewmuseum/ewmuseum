I got your message Kannon, now I've answered it.

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-16-02 A champion making the challenge? To some, this might be an odd occurence. Then again, this is Jeremy Howard. There is no telling what this man will do next. One minute, he's knocking over a Snapple machine in a display of disgust for his former friend, Xavier Kannon. The next, he's challenging Tim Walden to a match for his Continental title. One can only wonder, what's next from the Krazed Lunatic? Normally, one might expect for the scene to be opening up inside of the luxurious home of Jeremy Howard. Not this time. Because of a certain enemy of the Krazed Lunatics, the scene fades into a wooden door. Across the upper center piece, a sign reads: Bo Davis- Child Welfare Officer of Indiana. Suddenly, the camera begins to fade back, thus showing the Krayzie One and his lovely girlfriend, Jessica standing before the door. Jessica: I can't believe Kannon had the nerve to visit a welfare officer. Jessica, wearing regular jeans and a tight white t-shirt that exploits her ample chest, stands to the side of the door, trembling in fear. It's not everyday a parent has to visit a welfare officer. And, when they do, bad things tend to come of it. Jeremy: Don't worry about. I'll handle this. Jeremy, also in jeans and a Colorado Avalanche hockey jersey, steps to the door, letting out a few knocks. Knock. Knock. Its only takes a few moments, but a man whose obviously the welfare officer, standing a few feet shorter than Jeremy's five eleven, opens the door. Bo Davis: Can I help you? Jeremy: Yes. I'm Jeremy Howard and this is Jessica. Jeremy, with his palm facing up on his hand, points toward Jessica. Bo Davis: I've been waiting on you. Come on in. We have a complaint to discuss. Jeremy leads the way, as Jessica follows behind him to a set of two chairs that sit side by side in Bo Davis' office. Bo takes a seat behind his desk, turning his chair toward where the Krayzie One and Jessica sit along a wall. Jeremy, never the comdian in a serious situation dealing with his son, keeps a poker-like look on his face. Jessica, on the other hand, comtinues to tremble in fear. Bo Davis: Recently, I had a gentlemen walk in here and make some allegations of negligient parental practices by yourself and your girlfriend, Mr. Howard. The Krayzie One keeps his eyes on this Bo Davis, watching his every move. Jeremy sees the welfare officer pick up a stack of papers, perusing the top sheet. Jeremy: How can I be a negligient parent when I just got my son back seventeen days ago? Bo Davis: May I ask, just how did you get your son back? Jessica reaches over and grabs Jeremy's arm, probably feeling comfort in his touch. Jeremy adjusts himself in his seat, before going forward with his reply. Jeremy: Your governer had my son placed in a family that took part in an occult. I went at got him back from them. Bo Davis: Was there any violence involved? Jeremy: Only as much as was needed. Bo Davis: I see. Bo picks up a pen from his desk, then scribbles something onto the top sheet of paper. Jeremy keeps his eyes glued to Davis and his pen. Bo Davis: What is your profession Mr. Howard? Jeremy: I'm a professional wrestler. Bo smirks, sending a feeling of anger through the Krayzie One. If it weren't for Xavier Kannon, the Krazed Lunatic and his girlfriend wouldn't be in this situation right now. But no, the Gold Patron Meritorious as he likes to call himself, decided to take things one step too far and include Jeremy's son in his plot to piss the Krayzie One. If its a pissed of Krayzie he wants, this might've been the way to go about it. Bo Davis: As a professional wrestler, have you ever done something gratuitously violent? Jeremy sits forward in his seat, seemingly ready to jump out at this welfare officer in anger. Jeremy: Just last Tuesday, I put a man through a table. The direct seriousness in Jeremy's voice fills the air more with the tension that has enveloped the room. Bo Davis: I think I have this covered. I just have to ask you one more question. Jeremy knods his head, okaying it. In his eyes though, one can see his patience beginning to wear thin. Add to that, the lovely Jessica squeezing his arm harder and harder out of fear that her son could be taken away again by this man that sits before her, and a recipe for a Krayzie explosion has been started. Jeremy: And that is? Bo Davis stands to his feet, almost provoking the Krazed Lunatic with that move. Bo Davis: Have you ever struck your son? Jeremy: Have you ever played Russian roulette with all six chambers loaded? Jeremy stands to his feet, clenching his fists. Jessica gets to her feet also, but she smartly chooses to move away from her increasingly hostile boyfriend. Bo Davis: Mr. Howard, I'll warn you. This is not a good idea. Jeremy: Are we scared? Jessica: Jeremy! Don't get Troy taken away. Bo Davis: I've already got your violent history noted in my files. You're not of sane mind to raise a child. I can have Troy ripped away from you right now if you lay a hand on me! If its one thing that this man should have been told before his meeting with the Krazed Lunatic, its that threatening Jeremy Howard is like putting meat in front of a rabid dog. He'll attack it with a fury. With that in mind, Jeremy reaches out and grabs Bo Davis by his shirt, jerking him forward harshly. Jeremy: Don't ever threaten me. Before Bo Davis can make any sort of defense, Jeremy locks on his cobra clutch submission manuever while shoving the welfare officer's face into his very own desk. Jeremy: Now, lets get something straight right here and now. I don't care who it was that came here and spewed this crock of bullsh*t, but I'm not having it. Jeremy's muscle can be seen fully flexed in his shirt, showing just how hard he's pushing down on Bo Davis' head into his desk. Jeremy: I went through Stan Hinkle to get my brother back. I went through Richard O'Bannon to get my son back. Don't think I won't go through you! If you'll excuse me, I'll be leaving now. I better not recieve another phone call from you, or else bad things will happen. Jeremy releases the painful hold on the welfare officer. Davis gets back to a standing position, wobbly from the lack of oxygen going to his head previously. Bo Davis: I'll be sending some officers to your house to pick up Troy. You're not fit parents! Crack! Bo Davis just committed a crime. He committed the same crime that Xavier Kannon has already committed. He committed the same crime that Tim Walden committed to bring about the challenge. Bo Davis just crossed Jeremy Howard and the Krayzie One nailed him with a superkick to the jaw for it. The welfare officer's jaw snaps, laying awkward on the carpet where the rest of Bo Davis' body lays. Jeremy: D#mn, I broke your jaw. I'd care, if you would have said something I wanted to hear. Jeremy drops down to his knees and hands, kind of in push up style, right beside where Bo Davis lays on the carpet bleeding from his mouth. Jeremy: If I see so much as one police officer show up at my door, just as Stan Hinkle what happened to his wife and daughter when crossed me..... I do remember Mrs. Hinkle threatening Stan with divorce out of fear. Bo Davis' eyes close. Jeremy gets back to his feet, staring down at his work. Jeremy: You should have never taken the money from Kannon. He might be rich, but he's not the best option. Always bet on Krayzie. Jeremy turns away from the welfare officer laying on the floor, toward Jessica. A look of shock plasters her face. She doesn't appear to have a clue as to what to think. Jeremy: If Kannon thinks challenging Walden was unbelievable, wait until he gets a load of his puppet here. Jeremy makes his way over to where Jessica stands, putting his arm around here as the two begin to leave the office of Bo Davis. Bo Davis continues to lay on the ground, unwilling to move out of fear. Fans worldwide love this man for what he stands for, but he won't hesistate to strike another soul if they compromise the safety and sanctity of his home. This man is Krayzie. The scene fades away to Jeremy and Jessica walking away from the welfare officer while others rush into it.... to black!
