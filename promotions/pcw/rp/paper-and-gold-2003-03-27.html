Paper and Gold

27 March 2003

Title: Paper and Gold Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 27, 2003 Embittered.... I looked deeply into the monitor, the tax form alterations for the next year just having begun anew. It was good work for the moment, a chance to funnel my angers into the anguish that would be upon those who would undoubtedly become embittered themselves toward me over the course of the year..It was nice to have a jump on them, to be able to subject them to the bitterness of taxation, the objects of embitterment such simple things...Papers, deceptively mind-boggling to complete, merely appearing as a mild irritant, but signalling something greater, the taxes that took tens of thousands of dollars from those who had to take time out of their lives to fill those forms...How it would make one bitter. Just so was it now, with the gold around my waist, the Television Title, something so seemingly harmless....how it had shifted in my eyes. Once, just mere weeks ago, it was my goal, the chance to get back to the gold I'd been so sorely lacking, to spite Tomas and finally grasp the technical superiority, the victory I so sorely yearned for...And the title in the process. I had had such a lackluster run with the belt previously, back when I had been unable to even prevent Flaymin Aymin from keeping it away from me...Now, I was enabled to make the best of my second chance to show what I could do with the TV Title.....Now, I could find redemption.... ....Now, I was just being made out to be a bigger joke than back then. I would've liked to have been able to blame Howard..Even he couldn't keep from holding a grudge over the RUSH days, helping start the first challenge to his authority, but for whatever reason it wasn't showing...Nonetheless, I was accomplishing the inverse of what I set out for now, and the title was at the root of this extended ordeal.. Friday Fusion. Two days before March Madness, coming off a big disqualification victory over the man who would be World Champion and making the world know my determination and spirit...I thought I couldn't be taken down a peg, not in any fashion without that little bitch's involvement, I could beat someone as untalented, as utterly arrogant and useless as Kano....I was wrong. I knew something was up, the counts weren't quite as slow normally as they were when I had his shoulders to the mat, and the loss I took was a foregone conclusion given the double-time count I was put under..All I could hear, though, looking back on it was Dave and Duke's adherence to the belief it was fair...honest.... Bull. I would have had him beat in any fair situation, but his need for one-and-a-half counts proved an undoing compared with the four or five I'd have required..Nevertheless, that's still where things began. The start of a series of events that were causing me more harm than good....From there, came the title shot, one which the idiots believed I didn't even earn at that point, that Kano would have been more deserving of the opportunity than a man who couldn't win over the other contenders in his division or even beat a Schmoopy...I couldn't have fought better, and at the beginning of things, I honestly felt I had it in the bag... Then came the frustrations, again..Saddle hardly called things down the line, much as I tried to even out the feminine advantage with the corner-intermission. It wasn't so bad, that my first success in the match had been not even acknowledged by her...She made me tap out, it was true, but at least I could hold firm with the knowledge that it wouldn't have mattered if the match had been called by a professional. This was just as well a match as I could manage in the situation, even if I let myself become distracted by the antics surrounding it...But soon enough, the antics overtook me. It didn't matter to the fans that I had her in an inescapable position, that she was doomed to either give up or fall to injury..All they heard was 'She never even quit, Sally's accepting Rent-A-Hero's towel throw!', and 'Walden has Bushed the title..' It didn't matter that I'd technically had the match won previously. It didn't matter to the fans that their hero had been left there..All thanks to Kern and Duke, I was no more valid a champion than a man with the second-lowest vote tally was a democratically elected official in people's eyes. Ever since, it was just one damn moron writing an editorial on my weak championship after another..They didn't care about having a hard-fought match on each of the four shows prior when the last energies of my body weren't enough to beat Chris Storm in his rage, they just pointed at him as yet another man whom the so-called 'champion' could not defeat. My championship reign was just what I gave the federation at this point...Paper, and bitterness. I would never gain the respect I deserved at this point, forget fairly beating Duke and kicking out of that powerslam through the announce table, forget that any reasonably fair-minded man could see me beat Kano and Teresa, to them, I was just a loser, someone even Semore Smoke thought himself big enough to make punchlines of at my expense... ....Someone who would beat him to within an inch of his life if he'd ever get his chance to fight for my 'paper gold'. As long as I held the title, it wasn't over yet though. I could turn a successful reign from this yet, not to go down in the ranks of Mr. Showtime and Mike Marvelous as merely men who couldn't handle the challenges thrown at them...But every time it happened again, I would be deeper in my difficulty, not looking to gain the unworthy affections of the madding crowd who would never appreciate my efforts. To beat Trinidad would be, if nothing, a start. He had respect to a point, much as the DWO had become merely a negative footnote in the minds of the many, being World Champ there hadn't been devalued all so much to the average man. He had concerns of his own for this match that would make him hungry for a win, and I could almost have sympathized for him, but I had concerns of my own that were more important than any measure of pointless sympathy... I had my reputation. Money was here to stay, there was no need in wasting my time on any woman who wasn't interested in me for myself or pleasing the masses by reviling that which had brought me my successes, but the skill I brought to the ring had to be defended by somebody, and Dave was certainly not helping matters...It was a shame that I would have to crush the man's dreams, but by daring to buy into the mess that was 'my Five Star career', better-known as 'me no-showing the events because they wanted me to lay down for Big D and do humiliating stunts which they ended up hiring a look-alike for', he had already brought more shame to my repute than I would care to place in the value of his friend's safety... Perhaps it's heartless and selfish to value myself over the life of an innocent. Perhaps I would be nothing more than a shallow, arrogant being to force him to put every fragment of his being into this bout... Perhaps I was already there... Perhaps I was just looking out for number one. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
