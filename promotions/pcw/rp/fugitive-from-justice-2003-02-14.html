Fugitive from Justice

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/14/03 Vision clouds, as cold and damp death envelopes the senses. The lungs struggle for air, as the mind slowly shuts off.....without it&#039;s fuel, it&#039;s ever greedy demand of oxygen remaining unrequited, the strongest muscle in the body quickly abandons ship. Yes, drowning is a very fascinating thing. A man finds himself in this state, sinking into endless waters. The fall he took to the hard surface of this manmade lake would have killed an ordinary man.....he, however, is far from ordinary. Clyde: Blurb. Bubbles rise from the demon&#039;s mouth, as his hair rises in stringy clumps. He waves his arms frantically, clawing at nothing solid at all. Finally in complete desperation, he kicks himself to the surface and gasps for air. Clyde: (Hocking) Acccccck....phew.....argh! With seeming death averted, the burned man shifts his attention all around. Glancing down, he spies another form floating next to him, and with a scowl yanks it up. Clyde: Hurry up Bethany, we have to get out of here..... Tommy Lee Jones: I didn&#039;t know you cared. For a brief moment Clyde simply looks at the old man in his grasp. He then dumps him back into the water and scrambles in the other direction, swimming for all he is worth. Clyde: Bloody hell! Argh!!!! Tommy Lee Jones: Get back here sport, we aren&#039;t finished yet. Clyde: Oh yes we are. Tommy Lee Jones: No we aren&#039;t. Clyde: Yes we are. Tommy Lee Jones: No we aren&#039;t. Clyde: Yes we are. Tommy Lee Jones: Yes we are. Clyde: No we aren&#039;t. Tommy Lee Jones: I agree Bugs, now come over here so I can shoot you. Clyde: BAH~! Angrily, Clyde turns and lobs a floating chunk of debris at Jones. The marshal ducks.....Clyde throws another. Then another, then another....then his hand becomes caught. Clyde: (Pulling) Ugh, yeargh! Again, again the monster tries to yank the trash free, until at last he turns and yells at it. Clyde: STUPID FLOTSAM! I DAMN YOU TO THE NINTH CIRCLE OF SEAWEED HELL! Bethany: OH DIE ALREADY!!!!! The burned man looks on in stupid awe at the eight year old in his grasp. She wheels back a foot and kicks him squarely in the nose. Clyde: OW!!!!! Bethany: Now can we get the hell out of here!? OW!!!!! A gunshot cracks out, a bullet scraping by Bethany&#039;s cheek. Tommy Lee Jones: Kids do you have any clue how hard it is to try to get a bead on someone while swimming for your life? Bethany: (Lining up a knife) I wouldn&#039;t know. With a shriek the little girl hurls a blade, glistening as it spins through the air. Bethany: CATCH!!!!! DIE!!!!! CATCH!!!!! Around the halfway point Tommy squeezes off another shot though, plinking the knife straight out of the air and into the deep. Clyde: .....nice shot. Tommy Lee Jones: Thanks. Now die, cowboy. Clyde: WhaARGH!!!!! Another shot and this one finds the mark, striking Clyde right in the forehead. He falls back into the water, vanishing from sight. Bethany: (Shrieking) CLYYYYYYYYDE!!!!!! DON&#039;T LEAVE ME ALONE WITH THIS B-GRADE HASBEEN OF AN ACTOR!!!!!! Tommy Lee Jones: (Treading water and reloading) Hey I resent that, porkchop. Bethany: I swear I&#039;m gonnaACK!!!!! Abruptly something tugs at Bethany&#039;s foot, pulling her under. She flaps her arms madly, then squints to find Clyde pulling on her with all his might. Bethany: Blubububububub! Clyde: (Shrugging) Blububububububububub! Bethany: Blubububub! Clyde: (Scowling) Blubububub! Tommy Lee Jones: Blububub, blublub. Clyde: (Slapping his forehead) Blublublub! Cursing in his own underwater way, the demon swims for all he is worth while tugging on Bethany&#039;s foot. Deeper, deeper the pair descends, until reaching a large metal pipe. Clyde: Blub. Not hesitating at all, our killer pushes himself into the hole with Bethany in his grasp. A pull of water quickly catches them both, sending the pair hurrying onward......faster, faster, faster they move. And at last they are chucked out of the opposite end, coming to a very rough landing on a beach. Clyde: Mmmmmmmmph. Resembling nothing more or less than a large demonic ostrich, Clyde struggles to yank his head out of the sand. At last he does, tumbling backwards over and over until at last coming to a rest at the feet of a very perturbed Bethany. Bethany: (Kicking) ASSHOLE!!!!! Clyde: (Grabbing his head) Ow, I was just SHOT there.....! Bethany: (Kicking) I DON&#039;T CARE!!!!! Clyde: OW!!!!! Tommy Lee Jones: Will you lovebirds knock it off? Our pair freezes, then sighs simultaniously and turns to find a dripping wet Tommy Lee Jones standing with his gun drawn. Clyde: Okay I&#039;ll bite, how did you manage to follow us? Tommy Lee Jones: It&#039;s in my contract, I never die in these movies. Clyde: Oh, that explains it. Bethany: RUN YOU FOOL!!!!!! This time it is Bethany who yanks Clyde away, dragging him by the hair in the direction of a nearby set of woods. With Jones in hot pursuit, they struggle through thick branches desperately.....until running smack dab into a man. Bethany: .....Harrison Ford? Harrison Ford: Uh, hi. Tommy Lee Jones: (In the distance) Will you two slow down? I&#039;m old. Bethany: LET&#039;S GO!!!!! Again the girl pulls Clyde onward, smacking aside shrubbery furiously. She pushes onward, only to again run into a man. Bethany: ......Wesley Snipes? Wesley Snipes: Yo. Tommy Lee Jones: (In the distance) You can&#039;t just make this easy, can you kids. Bethany: URGH!!!!! And once again the child leads Clyde onward. Further, further they press into the woods, until yet again smacking into a man. Bethany: .....William Freidkin? William Freidkin: Hi. Tommy Lee Jones: (In the distance) I swear you&#039;re irritating me, sports. And yet AGAIN Bethany drags her evil lover onward, trudging for all she is worth. Abruptly the path ends though, and the pair finds themselves in a massive clearing. Bethany: ......well now. Tommy Lee Jones: Freeze. Sighing deeply, the little girl raises her hands. She then turns her head....and does a double take as she finds the marshal&#039;s gun is not trained on only her and Clyde, but on three others as well. Jones alternates his sight, trying to keep everyone in his vision. Harrison Ford: I didn&#039;t kill my wife! Wesley Snipes: I didn&#039;t kill those agents! William Freidkin: I didn&#039;t kill this franchise by directing a third unnessesary sequel! Clyde: I don&#039;t care! Tommy glares at Clyde. Tommy Lee Jones: Don&#039;t steal my HEY!!!!! Suddenly all three men bolt in opposite directions. Tommy Lee Jones looks one way, then another, then another....then stomps his foot and pulls out a walkie talkie. Tommy Lee Jones: Put out an ATB, I want a search of every henhouse, outhouse, whorehouse, crackhouse, doghouse, cathouse, frathouse, firehouse, flophouse, warehouse, farmhouse..... Bethany: Quick, let&#039;s go before he finishes babbling! Clyde: Argh! Grunting and holding his gunshot head in pain, Clyde sneaks into the woods as Tommy Lee Jones continues rattling off various houses, and we mercifully fade to black......
