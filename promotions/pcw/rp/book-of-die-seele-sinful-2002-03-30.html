Book of Die Seele Sinful

30 March 2002

Date: 3-30-02 (-----'s mean it's just a break in the chapter so you know) Book of Die Seele Sinful Chapter 1: Others pay for one's sin It was so long ago when that lonesome stranger wondered into town. He didn't seem like any of the commoners and for some reason he had a feeling to him, not one of those good feelings that make you want to welcome him but a feeling of death. That was three years ago and since that time the town had felt a great plague of disease wiping out half the population. War also came in these three years and it always seemed to come when the stranger would show his face. Almost seemed as if he was paying for his sins by unleashing them upon the town. It wasn't fair and all the town's folk wanted him gone but every time they approached whoever it was ended up dead. Many tried to threaten the stranger but all ended up either brain dead or gone. It was weird he never said much and when he did people listened. He wasn't a big man, he wasn't a brilliant man but something about him condemned these people to a living hell. They lived this way but one day set everything apart, one day alone made this place into a living, breathing hell. It all started out on a normal day. Most of the towns folks had went about their daily activities as usual but the stranger had descended from his mountain home. His hair was a mess and his eyes were blood shot; you could tell he was in a bad mood. Many times he had told the town's people to refer to him as Lord Crichton; Those who didn't wound up dead. Today he made his way into town, took what he wanted, and started to head back when a small child stopped him. He looked down at the child who stared wide eyed with innocence washed across his tiny face. You could see in his wide blue eyes he was full of questions and this small child had more bravery than over half of the town combined. 'Why are you always grumpy?' The small child asked in a voice which could barley be distinguished between a whisper. Lord Crichton's face showed anger as he went to back hand the small child. The child's frail body slammed into a cart full of fresh vegetables which scattered like cockroaches in the light. The sky went from a peaceful blue to a deadly red with in seconds. Silver lighting whipped through the blood making mass confusion as town folks rushed for their houses. Fire reigned from the sky like rain on a hot summer day and the earth began to shift causing jagged boulders to protrude from the ground. Screams filled the deadly sky as Lord Crichton's eyes began to glow. He growled, angry, but no one knew why, where had this man come from and why did he insist on causing havoc here? The boulders began to surround the town like a cheap prison cell. The fires of hell literally rose from the cracks in the ground causing an even deadlier barrier in which no one would dare try to cross. This was the dance of the demons fire, this was hell condemned to one spot but why? 'YOU! ALL OF YOU WILL LIVE IN MY HELL! YOU WILL ALL PAY FOR MY PAIN! IF YOU TRY TO LEAVE THIS VILLAGE YOU WILL DIE! DO NOT CROSS ME FOR I AM LORD CRICHTON!!!' His voice was dark as midnight as he boomed this across the villagers ears. They huddled in their homes, scared to death of what Lord Crichton would do next. All their exports were blocked; they would die either way. So now they were faced with a choice....to stay and die a slow and painful death or to attempt to escape and die a fiery death by the flames of hell. They needed a savior, they needed a hero but no one could enter and no one could leave. This town was paying for the sins of one man and for what? So he could be refreshed and live again? Eternal life is what he sought after but would that be what he got? Or was their someone who could possibly save this town before every one began to die. Chapter 2: The rise of a new stranger No one can remember where he came from but one day a man stepped through the fires of hell with determination in his eyes. They welcomed him but feared him at the same time. He revealed he would be their savior and make Crichton pay for his own sins. The town folk praised this man, treated him like a god almost but he would not accept their gifts. All he asked for was shelter to stay and food to eat until he could build up enough strength to battle this evil force of sin. He would feast and stay in secrete, Crichton never knew of him, never saw him, and figured the town folks were doomed. A few had went crazy and rushed into the fires of hell, after watching the horrible death of their friends at the hands of demons the town folks knew they could not give their soul away that easy. But this stranger, he promised so much and asked for so little. His purpose was unclear to many and to many he was just crazy but all in all he had walked through the fires of hell, he had made his way into this supposedly condemned city and they knew if anyone could save them it would be him. So they put their fate into this man they knew nothing of because all knew it WAS their only hope. ---- The day he decided to finally end this evil the town folks celebrated. It had been three months since his arrival and now all the talk would turn to action. He geared up in a type of battle armor many had never seen before, his eyes stayed a white color with beautiful blue mixed in, what they didn't know was of the power this man held. Stepping into the town square he looked up at the mountain. The sky hadn't been blue since the day Lord Crichton went mad; that's probably why the town folks loved this man's eyes so much. Weapons were offered to the warrior but he did nothing more than turn them down. He spoke of not needing any more weapons then his hands; they now knew that he surly was crazy. Slowly he began to climb the mountain, his black boots stepping through the ashes of the fire rain. It didn't seem to faze him even when the blaring heat from top the mountain blasted him square in the face; he only pushed on. It was a two day hike to the top and day one was over for now as he camped out under the sky which would show no stars for now. As he sat under the sky with only a blanket to sleep on he thought about why he did this, why he was the slayer of sin, and why he had been chosen. Picking up rocks and tossing them down the side of the mountain provided entertainment for the young man as he waited for the perfect time to rest so he would awaken at the perfect time to begin climbing again. As he sat he talked to himself or did he? It seemed he talked to a higher force. 'Why you chose me I will never understand,' His voice boomed through the darkness but into a whisper it stayed. 'I am only a man and I promised these people so much, help me slay this hidden sin so that their souls shall not be damned,' Skipping his last rock he laid his head down on a small pillow. He slept the whole night and dreamed of nothing. The next morning he awoke and without one work stood, packed his things and started his climb. As he got higher the air became more damp and moist, it became hotter, and most men would have had heat strokes or passed out from dehydration. Not this man, he was on a goal and he would beat this demon no matter what it took.----- Finally he reached the top where the cave of Lord Crichton sat. As he came closer he could hear the swears of Crichton exit the cave and escape into the demon red sky. He stood outside the cave and braced himself and stared straight into the depths of the cave. 'Lord Crichton, show yourself for I, a slayer of sin, call upon you to testify or drown a painful death in a pool of your own sins,' His voice boomed into the dark cave. Lord Crichton exited and growled. He called upon more fire from the sky's but nothing came, his power did not seem to work in the presence of this man. Lord Crichton's face turned a bright red as he became angry. His eyes began to glow once more as the young man only looked on. 'Do you accept my challenge?' 'NO! NOW PREPARE TO DIE!' Lord Crichton yelled with a dark passion. 'You leave me no choice,' The young man gripped his hands like he was holding a precious item. He snarled as his eyes turned completely ice blue. Lord Crichton charged and tried to hit the young man but he only moved back as the ground shook. The town folks stood at the bottom watching as the two men collided with fists and feet of fury. 'You can't defeat me Lord Crichton' The young man's voice snarled. 'AHHHHHHH DIE!' Lord Crichton slammed a knee into the gut of the young man and shoved him back nailing him with a few rights and lefts. He jumped on him pounding down but with one mighty roar the young man broke free and shoved Crichton forward. He began to bombard him with various punches and kicks. Lord Crichton reeled back as blood began to drip from his mouth and nose. 'Confess your sins or die by the flames of hell!' 'Never!' Lord Crichton yelled. He picked up a jagged rock and charged forward trying to stab the young man but with one mighty blow he sent Lord Crichton falling backwards. Then with one last snarl the young man charged forward and knocked Lord Crichton screaming off the top of the mountain and landing on a jagged rock killing him with all his sin. Blood ran from his body as the boulders went back into the ground followed by the fires of hell.------- That night the towns folk through a great celebration. The sky was clear and the stars could be seen once more. He was a hero to them but in his mind he was no hero; he was only doing his job. The town celebrated far into the night but once it was over he was gone and all that remained was his memory and as quick as he came he was no where to be found.
