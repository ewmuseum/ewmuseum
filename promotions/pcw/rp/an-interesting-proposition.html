Date: not entered [Scene opens in a busy airport- just another hectic day in the life of a professional wrestler. Flaymin casually strolls through the airport, past all the different Departure Gates.Dressed in a sharp… err. Singlet and matching shorts, Flaymin wanders along barefoot as curious wrestling fans gaze at him in a star-struck manner. Flaymin reaches his Departure Gate and looks up at the Television Screen above to find out when his plane departs.He then looks down at his watch to check the time] Flaymin: Shoot!What am I going to do for 45 damn minutes…. [Flaymin turns around and takes a seat on a nearby bench and begins to run his hand through his afro aimlessly. A few minutes pass before a man with spiky hair and arms plastered with tattoos, takes a seat next to Flaymin. He looks at Flaymin and smiles] Man: Do I know you? [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Maybe. Man: I’ve seen you somewhere! Flaymin: The Emergency Ward in Texas? Man: That’s the place!You’re that freak with one testicle! Flaymin: Yep, that I am! Man: I’m Johnny Noxville…. From Jackass. Flaymin: Never heard of it. Johnny: Its great.I get paid to make an ass of myself. Flaymin: Me too! [Johnny reaches into his bag and pulls out a pamphlet and hands it to Flaymin. Flaymin takes the pamphlet and reads the title on the front] Flaymin: ‘Pamplona’. Johnny: Yep, Running of the Bulls, Baby!!We’re filming an episode there and you’re more than welcome to join us as a guest on the show. [Flaymin considers the invitation] Flaymin: Sounds interesting…. Johnny: It’ll be great.Me and the guys are gonna get naked and rub ourselves with red bodypaint. Flaymin: When you say ‘bulls’…. Do you actually mean those big, crazy things that are in those Looney Tunes cartoons? Johnny: That’s them! Flaymin: Hmmm….Would dodging these bulls while butt-naked be considered a dangerous activity? Johnny: No- just a stupid one. Flaymin: Ah!I excel at stupidity. Johnny: You’ll have a blast!We’ll find ourselves some nice Spanish hookers and then they can ride our bulls! [Flaymin’s eyes light right up] Flaymin: We get our own bulls?Cool bananas!! [Johnny frowns at Flaymin momentarily before breaking into laughter] Johnny: Ha ha, I almost fell for that! Flaymin: Fell for what? [An awkward silence ensures before Johnny finally breaks the calm] Johnny: Soooo, you in? [Flaymin looks at the brochure once again.He flicks through it and grins at all the gruesome photos of idiots being rammed by bulls] Flaymin: Gee, I really would love my own bull….And I sure would like to ride one…. Johnny: What kind of bull are you talking about here? Flaymin: Big ones! Johnny: You really don’t have the slightest clue, do you? [Flaymin shakes his head from side to side in confusion. Johnny puts his arm around Flaymin and smiles] Johnny: In that case, you’re going to fit right in with the guys. [Suddenly over the loud speaker, the final boarding notice for the flight to Spain resonates around the airport] Johnny: Well, that’s my flight.You in? Flaymin: I don’t think so. Johnny: Think about it.You know you want it. [Flaymin pauses.He takes one last look at the Pamplona pamphlet and strokes his chin] Johnny: Final call Mr Flaymin!Are you in…. or are you out? [Flaymin looks up at Johnny] Flaymin: I’m in. [Scene fades to black] In the next instalment…..Flaymin guest stars on Jackass!!!
