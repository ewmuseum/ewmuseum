The Most Gnarly Death Of N'Sync

3 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3 [The scene opens up on a close up picture of the members of N'Sync. Their faces look so happy in the poster, knowing that they have totally cornered every preteen girl into buying their albums. They've made a lot of money from girls just like that, and the smiles on their faces certainly seem proud of that fact. While they lounge on private airplanes to some beautiful island for time in the sun their little preteen fans suffer back in the United States with their crumby lives with nothing to listen to but the horrible pop music by just another manufactured boy band] [Flames begin to rise toward the young men, posing to be straight and sing as if they know all about love. Their smiling images are clouded by smoke and the bright orange flames that suck them into nothing but burnt, crispy paper. The scene zooms out showing the poster being burnt on a clothesline in the backyard of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The pieces of burnt poster fall to the beautifully cut green grass that Tommy tries to make his gardner, Frank Fusselman, to care of. The flames that engulf the poster blend in so beautifully with the sunset on the ocean's surface. It's truly a magnificent sight] [The sounds of 'We're Not Gonna Take It Anymore' by Twisted Sister can be heard playing in the background. Dee Snider blares with his screeching vocals hoping that every misguided teen can listen to his song, buy the album, and then revolt against authority. The wonderful hair metal band that looked more like clowns than a band, but to 'Heavy Metal' Harry who listens to them religiously, it's a big deal to him. The stoner, wrestler, Harry, wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt proudly, which is accompanied by black leather pants and black cowboy boots. He watches the N'Sync poster go up in the flames with a satisfied smile on his face. A few days frustration has been taken out in just a couple flicks of the lighter. Actually it wasn't a couple, it was more like twenty or thirty because of the damned wind. Harry now has a blister forming on his right thumb from trying to get his Twisted Sister zippo to light. It was all worth it though, to watch those manufactured pop singers go up in flames. They will never...EVER...compare to the pure greatness of the 80's hair metal movement] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Burn baby, burn...man. [Harry feels like he's having sex right about now. Watching all those closet gay men get burnt up. Well...maybe he shouldn't think about sex while looking at those guys] [Behind our heavy metal loving stoner comes the other half of the Cowabunga Connection and Harry's half brother, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. Tommy wears a blue T-shirt with yellow writing that reads 'Tommy is the Name, Being Tubular Is My Game', blue and yellow swim shorts, and a pair of black sandals. His bright blonde hair is soaking wet as he has just come out of the pool, finishing up some most bodacious laps. There is nothing better then doing the butterfly stroke, it's a stroke that Tommy knows well these days] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, what in the name of Tubular are you doing? [Harry turns to his brother and smiles brightly] Harry: I'm burning them. [All those Tommy sees is a burning piece of paper on the clothesline. Obviously there was something on that poster, or maybe Harry is so high that he burnt a blank piece of paper thinking that it was ET again. How Harry HATES ET] TTT: Did you burn another ET movie poster, dude? [Harry shakes his head with absolute fear in his eyes. The small mention of ET, the cute little alien, just sends shivers up his spine. Cute to others, but to him ET is a disgusting creature that is one of the sickest creations in movie history] Harry: No way, man. I was burning an N'Sync poster. [Tommy nods with a smile, now understanding the logic in that. Those bogus N'Sync wanna be's cost them the tag titles. Talk about yet another super bogus act against The Cowabunga Connection] TTT: Those dudes totally screwed us at Gold and Glory. My beef really is with those totally bogus dudes, The Heartbreakers. [Harry seems confused] Harry: Why, man? TTT: Lets face it, dude. They were already eliminated from the match and came back down. They distracted you and you weren't able to get back in the ring. So if it wasn't for those bogus Pink dudes we'd have those tag titles around our waists instead of those Allah worshipping compadres. [Harry sort of has a befuddled look on his face. He's obviously thinking deeply, which is not his strong suit. He then looks at Tommy with that usual confused look] Harry: What is it with that Pink, man? [Tommy shrugs] TTT: I don't know, dude, but it freaks me out. [Harry then watches the flames die out slowly on the poster as it's pretty much just nothing now but vapor floating through the air. Harry seems happy with himself. Not only has he done his brother and him a big favor but he's done the world a favor] Harry: N'Sync really does suck, man. [Tommy is kind of hesitant to answer and then nods his head sort of agreeing but finding an arguement to it] TTT: They are pretty bogus in a most heinous way, but you have to admit when you play those dudes the most bodacious babes come out of the wood work to shake their groove thing. Harry: You can play anything and they'll do that, man. TTT: They won't do that to Twisted Sister, man. [Harry seems horribly offended] Harry: How dare you say that about the Gods of Heavy Metal, man!!! Babes love them! TTT: In the 80's, compadre. [Harry just ignores it, letting the hard rocking sounds of Twisted Sister take him away. How can that stupid N'Sync stuff even compare to the thrashing sounds of heavy metal? It's impossible, man. Harry then walks over to the waist high wooden fence that was constructed by the ever so popular Frank Fusselman. Harry is checking out the beach in front of him. The far out babes walking along the beach, bathing in the sun, and enjoying the beautiful California ocean. He really needs a babe of his own...maybe tie down and get serious. Well...first he just needs to get a babe to even talk to him. You need a first step to everything] [The Tubular One now walks over next to his brother, somehow feeling that he is troubled] TTT: We've got a pretty big match this Friday, dude. The Pretty Cool Guys and The Heartbreakers. It's every team for itself and this is for the Number One contendership for the titles, dude! We can totally redeem ourselves and once again grow totally bodacious in the eyes of the fans, and then take out those Allah loving compadres. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: I know that your down about not taking the titles at the PPV and us totally reigning as the most bodacious brothers of all time, but sometimes fate deals us a most bogus hand. They certainly did when Pretty Cool Guys held your foot from entering that ring. We've been given another gnarly chance and we have to take it like the most radical dudes we are. [Harry smiles] Harry: Yeah, man. [The two brothers look out to the beach for a few moments as Harry then turns back to Tommy] Harry: I want to f**k the girl in the yellow, man. [Tommy looks at her and shrugs] TTT: Gnarly.... [The scene then fades to black]
