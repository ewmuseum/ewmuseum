Date: Short finale RP for the series The bumbling noises of a crowd was around as a short break was going on for Rammstein, that were almost through with the concert but weren’t done yet, and because of their live performance. Kirk and Bradley had made this break useful by going up for food. Kirk dumped relish, onions, pickles, ketchup and mustard as his hot dog, while Bradley found a hamburger stand and gobbled his cheeseburger down. They quickly rushed back and met each towards the front of the pit, ready to listen to Rammstein again. Kirk and Bradley had no interest in Rammstein until they came. They made the way up, until the wild members of Rammstein came back up ready for another song. The opener of 'Tier' started as the crowd cheered and guitar’s became heavier as the riff exploded and went through the area like a bomb would dropped. Bradley and Kirk headbanged, and look liked idiots doing it, but that didn’t matter. 'Was macht ein Mann …was macht ein Mann ', sang Till. Meanwhile, Eddy was at a laundry… Eddy was in the middle of folding a towel at the laundry mat with Mr. T next to him, doing nothing. His cell phone rang and cause’s a vibrating sounds as it rumbled along the top of the wash mahine. He answered. 'Hello?' 'Dude, you HAVE to get to New Zealand', Skylar Green said. 'Why?' 'That’s where Fusion is taking place.' 'All the way down there? In Auckland I guess?' 'Yeah.' 'Damn. Well, I’ll have to get to the air port then…Long way…' 'Yup. I’ll see you there.' 'Bye.' 'Bye.' Eddy hung up and tossed his blue towel into a green laundry basket along with other clothes and started getting out of the laundry mat. 'HEY EDDY! Where ya goin’?', Mr. T shouted. 'New Zealand.' 'You mean that place below Antartica?' '….No. Australia.' 'Oh.' Eddy went to his truck and threw his basket in the back seat. Mr. T followed. 'Man! Don’t go!' 'I have to.' 'Why?' 'I have a match. Listen, I have no time.' Eddy started up the ignition and was off down the road, leaving Mr. T behind. Eddy started toward the airport. At the concert, 'Tier' was ending. Kirk and Bradley cheered. 'Thank you!', shouted Till. The band then quickly unleashed into 'Adios'. The song began slow, but when the heavy part came, fires arose and the singer threw plastic penises into the crowd. Kirk reaches up and caught one. 'I caught it', said Kirk with a voice he blew out. 'WHAT?!', screamed Bradley. So he didn’t have to repeat, he showed him the plastic penis. '…DAMN THAT’S BIG.' At the airport, Eddy was inside with the bags on his shoulders, ready for New Zealand. He walked up to look at the screen, and you could guess what it said. Delayed Eddy sighed and perched on a bench. After 'Bestrafe Mich', Rammstein stopped for a second. It was time for the last song. 'Feuer Frei' started playing as the crowd erupted. Nothing erupted at Eddy’s airport though.
