Meeting Manger's Crew...The Hunt For Makonie Is On-2.

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/03 Choices are something everyone has to make at some point in their lives…each one affecting you in one way or another, helping you to learn from your mistakes and make sure you are more apt to handle the situation the next time it occurs. Letting you gain experience no matter if it’s right or wrong. The thing with choices is though, there isn’t a different down the line answer. It’s not set-up as a right or wrong all the time, there is always that little thing that makes it, so that you might have made a choice that in some eyes in wrong and in other’s eyes it’s right. That is why there isn’t a clear cut reason…an answer to the question, is my choice right or wrong? Some many people fret over making a bad decision when there is no one to say if their decision was right or wrong. People need to start looking at things in terms of…fate. Everything in life happens for a reason, you can’t choose what’s going to happen to you…it’s impossible. The only thing you can do is take things in stride and deal with them, as they come at you…doing the only things you know how to do. That’s why I made the choice to join the skinheads…to revert back to the Chameleon. It’s the only way I know how to survive…to get Haley back. I wasn’t given a choice; everything that’s happened so far is nothing, but fate. This was going to happen, no matter what choice I made. She was going to be tried to forced away from me, but I had a choice. To sit back and except fate, or to change it…to make my own destination. Christian MacManus…the Chameleon wasn’t made to think or chose his own fate. By design the government molded me into a killing machine to hunt down the assassin of legend, but then I met Haley…she made Matt Jackson into more then just another disguise made by the Chameleon, she gave me feelings and emotions…made me into a human and not a killing machine. She made me promise that I would never revert back to the Chameleon, but now it’s to late…the clock is ticking on her life…our life together. My back is against the wall and I have no other choice other then to hunt down a man who made his own choices…Mark Makonie. In his eyes, he believes that his choices are right and he is doing what he should be…following the path that leads to his fate, but he is to blind to see that it’s not the path in front of you, but the path behind you. *** Quietly walking around the gigantic circus tent, the young operative jams his hands into the pockets of his long brown trench coat and lowers his head…hiding the look of disgust that is painted on his angular face. His disgust stems from his involvement in the nazi concert…Norticfest. It’s the two-day event that Shane Weather’s a young nazi rocker game up with, to get the movements message across. Matt has been here for a little more then three hours now and he already wishes that he could kill everyone in sight. Matt continues his trek through the tent, holding back his emotion every time some skinhead bumps into him, as he walks through the tent. His brown eyes casually scan his surrounding looking for Mark Makonie…hoping that by some stroke of luck, his enemy might just be there. It’s almost impossible to tell in the masses because each of the men looks the exact same…wearing white tee shirts and black jeans. Jackson is the only one that stands out, with his black dress pants and white dress shirt. He is also the only one without a shaved head, but no one can tell…the flesh coloured swim cap covers his spiky brown hair perfectly, not letting anyone be the wiser to the Chameleon’s undercover work. Just the way the operative wants it…the way he planned it, taking a certain amount of pride into the way he is pulling Simon off. Looking at all the nazi’s around him in the hot tent, Matt does everything in his power not to let himself gag. All around him are men who believe that they are the master race, but in reality they are just a bunch of scared kids…standing in one area, sweating their asses off for the cause. A cause, which is utter bullshit in Jackson’s mind, but something that he is pretending, he believes in. Standing in the venders’ tent, Matt sees the square of tables in the center…each one touching the other. Giving all the men an equal chance to sell their propaganda merchandise, hoping that maybe they can just get one person to conform to them…Jackson won’t conform to them, he won’t conform to anything…including Society who is going to make him try to at Turmoil. Society doesn’t scare him; he knows the gauntlet match is as good as his. Another man runs by Matt, bumping into the Chameleon’s shoulder almost sending him straight down to the brown grass ground. Keeping his balance Jackson spins around to face the man, holding his shoulder, which is still sore from this past Turmoil. His body is a complete mess from the massive amount of ring time he has allowed over the past month, but it doesn’t matter because at Supremacy it’s all going to pay off when he wins the Raising Star title. The man quickly turns, facing the agent, his eyes locked with Matt’s, a smile crosses his worn face, as he takes a step forward and reaches a hand out to Jackson’s. “I am sorry about that man, with all the excitement of everything I guess I just wasn’t paying attention,” the man offers, Matt nodding and shaking his hand. “It’s all right, I completely understand what you mean man. This is one hell of an event and I think it’s really going to do a lot for us. I am Simon by the way…just joined the movement a few days ago,” the Chameleon lies…letting a smile reveal his perfect white teeth and pulling his hand away from the sweaty nazi’s. “It’s good to see that we are getting some new members. I noticed you were dressed a little out of the ordinary, but it’s cool…just as long as you support the cause. My name is Sivalia, it’s a pleasure to meet you Simon,” Sivalia says, his voice a little worn from all the excitement. “So, what got you into the movement?” Matt asks, trying to seem friendly…hoping that the man won’t see through his hatred of him. Sivalia stands for a moment, watching Matt as he crosses his arms over his barrel of a chest. The skinhead makes a similar movement, trying to cover the fat that is hanging off of his chest. “Well I was in the army a while back…sniper in fact, but one day I got into an argument with some Ukrainians and I ended up killing one of them. It was him or me in a sniper duel though and I just happened to come out a head. They dishonourable discharged me and I had nowhere else to turn…that’s when I met Manger.” A Ukrainian sniper? It sounds like Sivasian Krikorian…except Krikorian is still alive and trying to hunt me… to snipe my perfect record, it’s to bad he is just going to come up a little short in the end. He isn’t hardened like Christian MacManus; he hasn’t done the things I have. His record keels in comparison to me and there isn’t going to be any rabbit pulled from his hat on Turmoil. “Interesting…I know a Ukrainian sniper. He’s in the PCW with me,” Matt blurs out…catching himself too late. “Oh yeah is that some sort of secret organization of the government or something?” Sivalia asks, with a look of pure confusion on his face at the words the man he knows as Simon just spit out. “No, I am a wrestler there…part of the reason I joined the movement. I was sick of them goddamn minorities holding me down. In fact I might just get a chance to face this famed sniper at Turmoil…that is saying he is good enough to get through about eight other people,” Matt adds in, letting his confidence fill his voice, as he tries to cover up for his earlier mistake. “That’s great man…our message can get even more people with you on national television like that. I am sure that fucking communist bastard will fall to the white power…they always do.” He is going to fall, but not to white power…to a minority just like he is, because that’s what I am. You people and your stupid message…thinking I am going to waste my time telling people about it. Who do you think I am? Mark Makonie…maybe, except the fact I am not a failure in the ring. “Well I have to be going…I want to get a good seat with Weather’s starts to play. His shit is going to rock, probably the best white power music we have.” Matt nods to the man and quickly walks past him, letting his black dress shoes trample the dead grass more, taking in the smell of the rotten foliage beneath his feet. Stopping for a moment, he stretches his 5’8” frame to his full height and lets his falling arm brush past his 9MM Beretta, wishing he could just pull it out and kill the man he just met. Showing him that his beliefs are biased and in the end, he’s going to end up paying for his crimes against society…the true Society. This whole thing just makes me sick…a bunch of teenagers who believe they have the answers to the problems that infect society. I can’t believe that I risked my life time and time again to make sure these people were safe…making sure that people like Stone Burns were safe. Killing people like Sivasian Krikorian because they were liable to hurt my country…Society thinks just like these skinheads, thinking that by brute force he can change things, when it reality it doesn’t work like that. Reaching the other side of the large tent, Matt casually walks out one of the open sides and looks around at the mass groups of skinheads. None any different from the one’s he has been seeing all day with the exception of height and weight, which doesn’t even show in his cold eyes. He just shrugs it off and steps out into the hot summer Los Angeles sun…making him smile a bit because of how much Haley enjoyed the time she lived her. The Chameleon keeps his eyes peel, as he continues his trek around the grounds, seeing the enormous stage off to his left…the glazed black wood conforming to the large speakers that are placed around it, but at the same time, it shows no signs of give, holding strong against the conforming. Then out of no where Matt sees a skinhead that looks different from the others, he is standing off in a corner of the stage, shirtless…so he can show off the swastika tattooed over his heart. Jackson turns his full attention towards the man, to notice he is wearing urban camo pants. It’s Makonie…the gamble paid off and now it’s time to put all my chips on the line…to finish the bet that I came here and made. Reaching inside his trench coat, Matt places his hand on the pistol and breaks into a jog, following the direction that the nazi is standing in. He is about 25 feet away and the Chameleon is closing the distance quickly, ignoring the sharp pain in his ribs from the kicks he received from Makonie at Turmoil. Before, he can get to the skinhead…a man steps in front of him, forcing him to stop his jog…it’s Manger. “Hey Simon, I have some more people I would like you to meet,” the older skinhead says, motioning for Jackson to follow him. Damnit Manger not now! Manger grabs Matt’s arm and pulls him along, as Matt continues to stare at the man he believes his Makonie. Reluctantly the agent follows…taking his hand off of the pistol and jamming them back in his pockets. I know you are here Makonie…your time is limited.
