Madam Zell

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-7-02 Brandi sits in the hard wooden chair in front of the fold-up table. Lindsey sits on the other side of her, in a similar chair. Other than the more comfortable looking chair on the other side of the table, the room is empty. Brandi looks around the small room, and suddenly feels very uncomfortable. If someone had to stay here for awhile, it might seem as though the walls were closing in on them. Now Brandi can understand how people can go crazy when they’re trapped in small spaces. Brandi turns her glance over to Lindsey, who looks quite comfortable in the strange room. There is only one small light bulb hanging above them on the ceiling, casting it’s eerie light to those waiting underneath. There is a large looking window in the back, but a black sheet is thrown over it, allowing no light to seep through. Brandi almost walks over and pulls the sheet off. She just wants to see the bright sunlight fill the room. If only it did, maybe it wouldn’t be so creepy in here. She decides against her urge, and instead sits patiently in her chair. The only exit in the room is the same door in which they came. That has to be a fire hazard of some kind. Brandi gives the room another quick glance and decides that there’s more wrong with this place than a fire hazard. A fresh coat of paint would do quite well. Get some new furniture, and add some more light and the place might not be half bad. Brandi- I can’t believe that I let you talk me into this. I mean, look at this place! It looks like it could fall over at any minute. There are probably rats crawling around somewhere. I swear I just felt something brush up against my leg. Oh, and if that light bulb goes out, we’ll be stuck in here in the dark. As if on cue, the light bulb starts flickering on and off. The two women look up and watch it closely. Finally it decides to stay on, for the time being at least. Behind the closed door comes the sound of a creaky floor, and muffled voices. Lindsey- Look, just trust me on this one. Give her a chance, would you? Brandi- Well, I don’t have much of a choice now. You already got me in here. She already took my money. I’m not leaving until I hear what she has to say, no matter how much of a crack job she is. Lindsey- She’s not a crack job! I’m telling you that she knows what she’s talking about. Brandi- Ok, ok. I’ll believe it when I see it. What’s taking her so long, anyway? Lindsey- She’ll be in here in a few minutes. The light bulb flickers once again, but this time it decides to go out leaving the two of them in complete darkness. Brandi looks around the room, but it’s no good. Her eyes aren’t adjusting and she can’t see a thing. As suddenly as it flickered off, the light turns back on. Now, in the seat across from them, a woman is sitting. She is wearing a turban wrapped around her head, a long black dress, and lots of jewelry. Her eyes are closed, but Brandi gets the feeling that she can still see them. Brandi- Ms. Cleo! Brandi laughs and Lindsey gives her a dirty look. The woman nods her head in Brandi’s direction, without opening her eyes. Brandi looks around the room, unable to find how the woman got in. Surely she would have heard the door open. It is right behind them. There are no other ways to enter the room, however. Brandi decides that she must have been too worried about sitting there in the dark to be listening for the door. Woman- My name is Madam Zell. You have come to me searching for answers, yet you doubt my powers. I have appeared here before you, yet you still search for my point of entry. I assure you that you will find none. However, if you continue your search for answers, you will find many. Brandi rolls her eyes at the woman. It’s hard to take anyone dressed that way, in a poorly lit room seriously. Madam Zell- I am not playing a joke on you. If you would like, I will give you your money back, and you can be on your way. If not, I suggest you just sit back, and let me work. Brandi starts to stand up and ask for her money, but Lindsey makes her sit back down. So, Brandi sits there, staring at the strange woman with her eyes closed. Madam Zell- Your friend is wise. She has made a good choice. Now, I will tell you what I see. The woman leans backwards and appears to be having convulsions. Brandi looks at the woman strangely, then over at Lindsey. All Lindsey can do is shrug, and watch the strange woman’s movements. Finally she sits upright once again, and her eyes pop open for the first time. They pierce holes in Brandi’s forehead before they snap shut again. Madam Zell- You will be tested soon. At the end of the week a great challenge awaits you. You will be pushed more than you ever have been before. Lindsey leans over towards Brandi, and whispers in her ear. Lindsey- She must be talking about Fusion. Brandi motions for Lindsey to be quiet and sit back, which she does. The woman’s head falls down against her chest for a few minutes. Brandi looks bored, sure that the woman is just putting on an act for the two of them. Surely it can’t be hard to do. She just feels sorry for the people that really do believe it and give the woman all their money to hear the same things she tells everyone else. Again the woman’s head snaps back into an upright position and her eyes open. Weren’t her eyes brown a few minutes ago? Yeah, they were. Now they’re green. The same color as Brandi’s. A sharp pain shoots through Brandi’s head. She closes her eyes and rubs her temples. When she opens them again, the women’s have closed. Lindsey looks worried. Brandi motions that she’s fine, and the two watch the woman attentively again. Madam Zell- You aren’t sure how you will fair with this new challenge. People are doubting you. That is causing you to doubt yourself and your own abilities. Forget about them. Forget about their thoughts, their predictions, if you will. Just clear your mind of it all. Focus on what you want to do. What you know you can do. Focus on that, and you will be fine. Lindsey- So you think she’ll be able to overcome this new…challenge? Madam Zell- Oh, I have no doubt. It is up to her, however. If she chooses to worry, she chooses to fail. A little worry is fine. It just can’t consume you. If it does, you’ll run into problems. Brandi leans back in her chair, waiting for the pain in her head to go away. Madam Zell- You are in a great deal of pain, are you not? Brandi looks shocked. The woman’s eyes are still closed, and Brandi has been hiding the pain well, so she isn’t sure how the woman could know this. Brandi- No, I’m not. Madam Zell- Ahhh…you don’t want them to know. I see. You are afraid of many things. You’re afraid that your career will interfere with your relationship. You’re afraid that the pain will not go away. I assure you that it is only temporary. It will soon leave you in peace. Brandi jumps up from her seat and stares at the woman. The woman is becoming more and more strange as time goes on. She seems to know things that Brandi hasn’t told her, hasn’t even told anyone. Of course, she couldn’t know about that, could she? Of course not! She has no way of knowing. Madam Zell- Do not worry. I am frightening you, yet I have done little. I have only taken a glimpse into your thoughts and your future. Lindsey stands up, and looks towards the door. Madam Zell- Ahh…you want to leave. My abilities are making you uncomfortable. For this, I apologize. That was not my intent. However, if you would like to know more I will reveal all that I can. Brandi and Lindsey look at each other and shake their heads. Madam Zell- Very well. The woman opens her eyes and stands up. She has to be barely over 5 foot. The dress and all the jewelry make her look smaller than she is. Madam Zell- You would like to know more. You are just afraid to ask. Afraid to know what I know. I assure you that there is no need to be afraid. Lindsey- Uh…I think we should be going. Madam Zell- Ahh…but you have no place you need to be going to. You just want to get far away from this place to think my words over. Very well. I understand. I will leave you now, but know that I will be here waiting for your return. The light bulb flickers off once more. When it comes back on, the woman has disappeared the same way she arrived. Into thin air. Brandi shudders and opens the door behind them. It squeaks loudly, and Brandi knows she would have heard that if the woman had used the door. There would be no way to miss that sound. It’s so loud, and the door was so close to them. The next room, filled with dusty shelves and books, shows no sign of the woman. Brandi- Where’d she go? Lindsey- I have no idea. The two look around the room a little more, but still find no sign of the mysterious woman so they leave the shop. Brandi- That was creepy. Lindsey- Yeah, it was. I didn’t think it would be like that. What did she mean about you being in pain? Are you ok? Brandi looks down the street, trying to avoid Lindsey’s eyes. She hates lying to her friend. Brandi- Yeah, I’m fine. I don’t know what she meant by that. Lindsey- You sure? Brandi- Yep. They give the strange shop one last glance before disappearing into the ever present crowd on Los Angeles’ streets.
