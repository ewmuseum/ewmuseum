Believe In Yourself, Man - Part 2 - Fat + Hat = Puke

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 17, 2002 [That was a horrible thing to do, man. Our Heavy Metal rocker is one let down dude. Promised something and it wasn't there. A dirty and most not far out trick. It was played by his brother and tag team partner, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. He said that Avril was there, spread eagle, on the black satin sheets. She wanted his BUDDY! She wanted the buddy, she wanted it all, man. When Harry ran down the off-white, hard plastic steps of Hotel California he was on his way to a totally not bitchin' disappointment, man. He made his way down the golden hallway, with the red carpet that has the golden designs of palm trees. It was like the way had been paved for him, another destiny about to come true. Through the oak, handcrafted door Harry could smell her, he knew that it was her smell. All of the people inside Room 578 were ignored because Harry was on his way to a totally far out destiny. Avril, spread eagle, wanting the buddy. The bodacious buddy as Tommy put it. Harry was going to have pre-marital sex, but that was all right. If Avril wanted it, then that's what Harry wanted. He stopped at the black doorway with the gold plate that reads 'Harry's Heavy Metal Domain'. He wonders if Avril is impressed with what he did with his hotel room. She'll probably jump his bones the minute he walks through the door. For some reason, as he grips the golden knob...and no not HIS knob, he feels nervous. His love, his true love is just behind the black door. It's just like in that far out game show, Lets Make A Deal, and Harry knows that he made the correct choice in the door...well, because it's his door and Tommy said that's where she is. So, it's not EXACTLY like that bitchin' game show, but it's similar. Ok, Harry's still pretty stoned, cut him some slack. He opens the door and slowly makes his way in. He has his eyes closed tightly, wanting this moment to feel extra special. He opens his eyes to one of the most disgusting sights that he will probably ever witness in his life] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: GAH!!!! Not far out, man!!! [Standing on his black satin sheets is one of the fattest, ugliest women that he's ever laid eyes upon. She has three chins, man, THREE CHINS! Is it even possible to have three chins? Wouldn't you just try and lose some weight after the second chin came in? His jelly rolls hang over each other like layers on a cake. Stretch marks and dimples are all over her body. Everything is jiggling around like in a big bowl of jello. Harry has found his form of Kryptonite because he has pinned himself against the wall, gagging like crazy. He can't move from the wall, it's like some kind of fat chick force has pinned him there. Harry takes a second look. Come on, when something disgusting comes across you have to take that second curious look at it. Harry is in the process of taking in his second look. Another quick glance at one of the ugliest female creatures to grace the planet earth] Harry: OH NO, MAN!!! [It's actually even worse than the last time. Because now she's on all fours with her gigantic pimpled filled, fat ass perched into the air. The smile on her chubby face just gave away the thoughts that were plagued into her mind. Harry takes control of himself and slowly begins sliding toward the doorway that he was nervous about coming into earlier, and now all he can think of is getting out of there. Now why would he go and do something like that? Talk about a totally not far out trick, man...not far out AT ALL!!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: HAHA!! Totally righteous, compadre!! [Standing at the outside of the doorway is the man who set this totally bogus thing up in the first place. His brother. His tag team partner. His best friend since birth. The surfer dude stands there in his Hawaiian button down shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black sandals. A very devious smile crosses his tanned face, his perfect smile evident to all at the party who are laughing. Harry stumbles through the doorway, holding his stomach as if he's about to spew. Harry collapses in the middle of the living area. A large group of partyer's have gathered around to see what Harry's reaction will be. Will he spew? Or will he not. To spew, or not to spew...that is the question] TTT: I've got ten bucks in that my bro spews totally heinous chunks on the floor! [The crowded room begins chanting and screaming for bets. A chant of 'Spew' and 'Don't Spew' have filled the room. Harry just notices that everything is spinning now. He looks at a red head girl with large breasts and an extremely thin waist. She has on this cute little purple latex shirt that looks like she is naked, which she is minus the latex, and Harry suddenly begins focusing on that babe. She's spinning pretty fast now. You'd figure since she was spinning so fast and going upside down that some of those clothes would fall off, but they don't to Harry's disappointment. The only remedy for the sickness that builds inside his stomach is fresh air. Because looking at a totally hot chick just isn't helping matters right now. Air is good] TTT: Oh no, dude! SPEW!! You don't want to go outside, compadre. Stay here and let it loose bodaciously! [Harry is already out of the door and running down the hallway toward the elevator. He presses the down button viciously and frequently. He figures that the more he hits it the faster it will come, that's what they always do in the movies. Well, this isn't a movie and Harry ends up waiting in the golden hallway, holding his gut for a few minutes before the elevator arrives to him. He jumps onto the large elevator which has a padded bench in the back. A man in a red uniform with one of those strange oval hats stands on the elevator with a pasted on smile. Harry looks up at him, holding his stomach and breathing heavily] Harry: Get this thing down to the ground floor, man! [The elevator operator smiles and nods. He has acne all over his face and his greasy black hair hangs out from underneath his oval red hat with the gold lining at the top. The sight of this dudes acne isn't really helping Harry's spewing chances here. The white heads are large and ready to pop with nasty, white pimple juice. Harry can't help but look at them. They are freakin' huge, how can you not look at them. The operator stares back with that same pasted smile on his pimple filled face. His skinny body straightens up as he seems to look a little uncomfortable with some long haired headbanger staring at him, holding his stomach] Elevator Operator: Is there something the matter, sir? [Harry would try to talk, but he can feel that roast beef and peanut butter coming back up from earlier in the evening. Room service does rock around here, man] Elevator Operator: Well, if your going to spew, spew into this. [The operator takes off his ridiculous looking red hat and hands it to Harry. The headbanging Cruiserweight looks at the hat wondering if it really is a hat. It could be some kind of transmitter that hails an alien craft to take him to the distant far off planet Hiccup, where the blue aliens with weiners on their head's do nothing but hiccup, hence the name Hiccup for the planet. Harry then quickly comes to the realization that he's look at a stupid hat, and not a transmitter. Remember, Harry is still stoned out of his mind] Elevator Operator: Hey...your 'Heavy Metal' Harry from PCW. I thought that you look familiar. [Harry throws up into the oval hat, and the elevator operator doesn't even notice this. He goes on like Harry is just standing there interested in what he has to say] Elevator Operator: This is pretty weird that I've run into you. Because I was out back earlier on break when this pigeon came up to me, started talking to me about a girl pigeon named Lila and how 'Heavy Metal' Harry was going to win the PCW Cruiserweight Championship. I quickly replied with 'Cruiserweight?!' because you look a little big in the stomach...you know...no offense. [Harry still pukes out his roast beef and peanut butter, looking up at the pimple faced elevator operator with a bit of anger] Elevator Operator: Then the pigeon and I started rapping about different life issues. I really have gotten depressed about my spreading acne. I've had it all the way through high school and it's become quite an annoyance. I can't even get a date with gigantic fat women with my face looking like this... [Harry suddenly is taken back to the three chinned Jabba The Hut that was doggie style on his black, satin sheets. His spewing increases to a projectile like spew, and suddenly the hat is filling up rather quickly. The elevator operator still pays no attention to the spewing Harry] Elevator Operator: The pigeon was giving me a lot of insight on how I can fight acne. There are great topical creams that can fight acne big time. I've looked into it already and I'm going to pursue it. Then there is Acutane which has every single side effect on it, including death. I just might jump right in on it anyway, because I'd rather be dead than have my face look like a pineapple filled pizza. [Harry looks back up at the guy's face, finally stopping his spewing. He can breathe once again as the acids in his mouth sort of burn his tongue. He needs a tic tac, man. For some reason the guys face does remind Harry of a pineapple pizza, but with gigantic white heads that pour pimple juice. Harry resumes his spewing] Elevator Operator: The pigeon kicks ass though. He told me that all I need to do is to believe in myself. That all I have to do is not give up on my hopes and dreams. If I lose track of them then I'll just be stuck in this elevator with my face full of pimples. He told me just when I want to give up, just when I feel it's time to throw in the towel, that's when things will work out. My dreams will come true, my hopes will be raised beyond any limits that I've ever imagined. All because I never gave up, and because I believed in myself. The pigeon told me that your the one who said that to him, and how it really helped him out with Lila. Harry, that was really insightful and you didn't seem like the type to do that...no offense once again...you just don't seem like the kind of guy who comes up with intelligent stuff like that. No offense, of course. [Harry breathes in and out as the spewing has stopped. The hat is filled to the brim with puke. Chunks of roast beef stick out as Harry looks at it with curiousity. He then looks up at pimple puss and hands him back his hat, which has a lot more weight to it now. The elevator operator smiles at Harry as they have finally reached the ground floor of Hotel California. Harry gets up from the bench and walks out into the main lobby, looking back at the elevator operator with confusion] Harry: Was it me or was that one of the longest elevator rides I've ever been in, man? Elevator operator: I don't know, Harry, I've stopped paying attention after all these years. I wish you good luck in your second round match. I don't know who your opponent is but I have this strange feeling that your going to make another huge win to move on, like the true champ that you are. Good Luck, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. Harry: Thanks, man. [As the door closes the operator puts the hat on his head and Harry's large amount of spew covers the man's head. Harry gags again....] TTT: DUDE!!!! [Harry jumps forward from the generator atop Hotel California. Another dream?! Another bowl, another dream. It seemed so real, like it truly happened. The throwing up, the pimple face, and...ugh...that FAT WOMAN! Sweet Jesus, man] TTT: Avril Lavigne is in your bedroom, spread eagle, compadre! Lets go! [Harry looks back at Tommy with a sly smile on his face. He's been here before. This is exactly what happened in his dream before. He was tricked, it's that simple. He's not going down to his bedroom because he knows exactly what kind of fate is waiting for him] Harry: I know about the fat chick you have waiting for me, man. [Tommy's face looks bamboozled. It's like someone just sucker punched him] TTT: Dude...no....FAT CHICK?!?! HAHA! No way, compadre. Lets go, to your room, and go get Avril. Give her one bodaciously from me. Harry: No way, man. There is a fat chick down in my room, I'm going to throw up in some elevator guys hat, and then he's going to pour it on his head, man. [Tommy stands there silent for a few moments, not really sure how he should respond back to something like that. Tommy just shrugs and heads back toward the doorway from the roof] TTT: Well, I'll just have to entertain Avril myself, dude. You can sit up here and play with the totally heinous pigeons. Harry: I'll do that, man. [Tommy exits through the door as Harry looks up to the sky with a smile. Victory is finally his] Harry: All because I believe in myself, man!! [Fade to black]
