Jealousy and Anger

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 We are on the day of PCW's March pay per view, March Madness. It takes place in St. Louis. This was the first pay per view of 'Amazing' Mike Storm just a year ago. It was here that we saw him take on Miyagawa, Pheonix, Jake Keeton, and JJ in a cage for the PCW Cruiserweight Title. It was in this match that we saw Jake Keeton keep the Cruiserweight TItle by cheating. This night saw five debuts in this match alone. First we had AJ and Jay Storm come out on the side of 'Amazing' Mike Storm. On the other hand we had four men come out for Jake Keeton. He had his tag team partner, Slade, and then three other men new to PCW, and there these five man formed the Five Star Five. They saved Jake Keeton's Title from Mike Storm's grasp. Storm will not allow somethin glike that to happen this year. This year he goes for the Number One Contendership to the PCW Television Title, and he will not allow himself to be cheated two years in a row at this pay per view. He will not allow a less qualified, and less worthy opponent to walk out victorious over him again. Brock Newbludd plays that role this year, but this year it will not be the same. Now we join Mike Storm in St. Louis. Scene: Camera opens to Missouri's famous giant Arch. It shows it at a distance, but standing under it we can see the silhouette of a persom. undure of who it is though. So the camera moves closer in, it gets to where the man can be clearly made out to be masked PCW star, 'Amazing' Mike Storm. He stands there wearing a pair of gray sweat pants, and a gray hooded seat shirt. The hood hangs back and you can see the back of his mask, this is how we know it is Mike Storm. He stands there, looking up at the arch. Then the camera pans around, to show him looking up at it from the front. He talks as he continues to look up at it. 'Amazing' Mike Storm: Well this may be on odd place for you to find me, but I just have never seen this thing, and wanted to see what it was all about I guess. Scene: Storm shrugs his shoulders and looks from the arch to the camera. Mike Storm: Tonight, tonight is very important. Tonight is when I finally move on to get a match with the Television Champion what I will not be screwed out of victory by some immigrant group. Flaymin Aymin will not be involved for once. His group of people who need deportation will not be around. I will get into the ring with the Television Champion, Miyagawa. You know what though, I can not get ahead of myself now. Scene: Mike Storm begins walking away from the arch and stalks as he goes. Mike Storm: Before I get to go against Miyagawa, I must first take care of Brock Newbludd. Me and Brock will go at it in a ladder match. After what has happened between us recently, this match will be violent. This match will have blood, this match will not be pretty, but most off all, this match will have a clear cut winner. This match will not have anything dumb finish with a disqualification, or anything like that, this match will have one victor, who will win. I mena let us face it, this whole thing between me and Brock is all because of one small thing, Brocks jealousy. That is the only way to explain it. He is jealous of me, he is mad at me for showing that I am better than him. I mena let us look at it. Brock Newbludd return to PCW, in his first match he faces me, and guess what? I pinned Brock Newbludd, fair, right in the center of the ring. Now he is apparently a man who hold a grudge. He is a man that can not let go, but he is not mad only because I beat him, oh no, there is more. Mike Storm: I mean he is not just mad because I fairly beat him. No there is more. IT goes all the way back to past PCW run for both of us. He is mad because he chased after the Cruiserweight Title for so long, he was mad that I was immediately a top contender for the belt. I was already ranked ahead of you there. Upon my debut I was marked as a future Cruiserweight Champion from the start, and not only that, but I won the belt, and you were in a few matches where you tried to take it off of me, and you Brock, you could not succeed. You could not beat me. You still can not beat me. You will not beat me tonight. You will never ever beat me, one on one. You will never ever beat me fairly. You can not do it, and that kills you inside, because you know it. Inside you know it. You feel it, you can not beat me. You cost me a chance at the World Title, because you could not make it, and for you, knowing that I would be there, in a match contending for the World Title, when you would not be, it would be that final nail, the final straw, it would be that last thing to push you over the edge. You would not allow it. You know what though, I am actually kind of happy you did that. Scene: Storm pauses and smirks a little bit. He let's that sink in a little bit, before explaining what he means. Mike Storm: You see Newbludd, you sure as hell have pissed me off. You have greatly angered me, and I look foward to hurting you. I look foward to taking you, and making you feel true and unbearable pain. To make you cry out in agony, and to be honest, I will more than enjoy it. I will be more then happy to inflict pain on you, beyond what you thought was possible, beyond anythnig you have imagined. By you eliminating me from the tournament, you ahve given me the desire, the motivation, the mind state, and they way to hurt you. You see if I was in the main event, I would not have my ladder match with you. A ladder match, a match where DQ is not possible, a match where you are subject to what ever I wish to do to you. Scene: Storm laugh a little at that. Mike Storm: It is a scary thought, and yet I enjoy the idea. You will be at mercy tonight Brock. You see, I was telling you I was driving you over the edge, but you have done so to me now. First you try to cost me my first round match with out success. hen you cost me my second round match. Then you cost me a four may match, because I was to usy beating on you backstage to get out to the ring to pin Jon Kano or Lance Bell. I mean I enjoyed getting my hands on you and all, but I do not enjoy missing what could have been an easy win. That is okay though, because you will pay. Mike Storm: Brock usually I am not an angry person, usually I try to keep my cool, I try to provide PCW fans with a decent role model to fool, but tonight you can throw all of that out the window. What I do to you, it will not be moral. It will not be ethical. It will not be something that people at home should mimic. It will also be something never forgotten by all of those who witness it. Everyone who sees it will be amazed by what I am capable of. They will be in shock that a good man like myself can go and completely go crazy on someone like I will. That I can do things that will harm myself just to hurt you that little bit more. I mean not look it right now, but Brock wiat until we step into that ring. Wait until we get in, and you look acrss the ring into my eyes. You will see intensity that will scare you. Wait until you feel the cold metal of that ladder crashing into your skull, as long as your still conscious you will wonder why you ever messed with Mike Storm. Then when you make up the next day in the hospital, when you regain your memory of what happened to you, you will then know that I, Mike Storm, truly am, simply AMAZING! Scene: Camera fades out as he continues on walking.
