NAACP Move Over, N.I.G.G.A Is Coming Through...

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/26/02 PCW is back in business. After coming off an OSW-like entertaining Fusion, the entire PCW roster was in shambles. The Supreme Machine and Ricky Thunder from the CCW had been signed. Blood Angel and Hiro Saotome both signed contracts, and most importantly, The Phantom Booker was at the helm of what appeared to be a brand new stable. Jordan and his fellow Malice Crew members watched in awe, as Tyler Nelson, got the beating that he is righteously and rightfully deserved. But where does this leave the Malice Crew? Is it possible that The Phantom Booker, and the four men that were with him, are going to be working side by side with the Malice Crew? Or is it just another stable to watch out for? ***************** The scene opens up in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, Duke Williams, and Jon Kano, had all just engaged in one of the most brutal, intense, and testosterone packed main events the PCW had seen in a long time. They were all tired. Each one of them had gone their separate ways. Atlantic City is known for it’s incredible nightlife. Casinos, strip clubs, hookers, easy scores on drugs. All of that. It was dark now, the moon shined down on the streets. The lights from various hotels, bars, and strip clubs illuminate off the sidewalks and car windows. The streets are packed with homeless men, hookers, and drug dealers. The air blows some trash off the dirty streets. We see current PCW and Malice Crew wrestler, Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan is wearing some dark colored Jeans, which we cannot see clearly due to the lights flashing everywhere. He wears a heavy, dark colored jacket, and a white shirt. He has brown Lugz boots, and his trademark Toronto Raptors basketball cap on. He has a bottle of Pepsi in his right hand. He turns into a dark alley, walking slowly and taking in the scenery. There were puddles of water and mud every few feet. The place smelled rank. There was a bunch of overflowing trashcans, garbage on the floor. A few homeless men trying to catch some shuteye. Every step Jordan takes there’s a crunching nose made, the pitter-patter of rain begins to start. Jordan throws the hood on his jacket up as he checks his watch. 10:13 PM, where the fuck was the guy? He was supposed to meet Jordan there at 10:10. Hell, Jordan didn’t even know what the guy looked like. Jordan checked the sign on the street, the corner of Ross/Johnson, he checked his two-way pager again, he read the text message what seemed to be like a hundred times. He couldn’t stand the pressure of waiting, it was too intense, it was freakin’ mind-boggling. The text message said specifically, ‘Meet in the alley on the corner Ross/Johnson, I’ll send somebody.’ Debbie Barker, Jordan’s superior worker at the NAACP had given him this secret mission. He was sent to do a series of things, stealing funds from a church, bounty hunting. Things like that. This was a huge mission, which Jordan had been doing for the NAACP. He was trying to improve his ranking, socially in the NAACP. He was a lowly spokesperson, or so he thought. He was nothing more than a man who did countless hours of paper work, and was always writing speeches for the public. On top of that he was a professional wrestler who had to split his time between Japan and America for PURE and PCW, respectively. Wind blew into Jordan’s face as he appeared. The man. Jordan could tell it was he; he was a scraggly old black man. He appeared as if he hadn’t shaved in days. He wore tattered jeans and a torn Los Angeles Dodgers baseball style shirt, which was unbuttoned, showcasing the man’s hairy beer belly. The man stumbled forward, obviously in a drunken stupor, perhaps Debbie had promised him cash in exchange for giving Jordan his instructions. It was just a mere coincidence that he was in New Jersey. The man looked at Jordan, he nodded. Jordan nodded back. The man slapped the envelop in Jordan’s hand. He turned around and walked away slowly. Jordan made sure that nobody in the alley was watching him, and then he quickly opened the envelope. He read the contents as quickly as possible. Mr. Copeland, we have been very disappointed in your performance as of late in the NAACP. Over in Japan, The Reverend Jesse Kintei was brutally attacked at the hands of one of your opponents. This angered is greatly. I am sorry to inform you, that you have now been terminated from your NAACP contract. It was a nice relationship, but it’s got to end now. Good luck in the future, Sincerely, Debbie Barker. Jordan couldn’t believe what he read. Just to make sure, he read it over a few times. He…He had been… Been… FIRED! Jordan leaned his back against the slimy and wet wall. His life was in shambles, in the PCW, he no longer had direction. Once he was having an excellent feud with Mark Makonie, the Internet ‘Smarks’ were saying that perhaps this feud could resurrect the dying Rising Star Championship, but now it appears as if Ricky Thunder was taking Jordan’s place. His job at the NAACP had been terminated. What was he to do now? *Light bulb flashes* Only one thing could get Jordan back to his old Militant ways… ****************************************************** The next morning. Jordan was dressed in his finest clothing. He had just slept in his small and cramped hotel room in Atlantic City. The night before, he had called everybody and they momma about this. It was a resurrection of sorts. The news media was out in full force; CBS, ABC, NBC, and a bunch of wrestling publications were there. Jordan has decided to hold a press conference, in Seaside Heights, New Jersey. A hotbed for teens, it’s where they spend their summers. Getting drunk and having sex. It’s like Spring Break, but in the summer. The scene opens up with a fairly large podium on a larger stage. Behind the podium stands a large poster, with Jordan X Shabazz’s face on it. It’s obviously old and been used, because the name on the marquee still says ‘Jordan Crazy’ Jordan X Shabazz’s old wrestling name, the one he had forsaken. There are a bunch of white, wooden chairs, lining the stage. The seats are mainly filled with numerous famous African Americans. The Reverend Jesse Jackson, The reverend Al Sharpton, hell, even OJ Simpson is seated on the stage. Jordan X Shabazz stands at the top of podium. He’s angry, obviously. The crowd of about… 300, mostly African American, stand at attention as Jordan pulls the microphone close to his lips. Jordan X Shabazz: “You all know me from the Primetime Championship Wrestling organization, and you all know me from the NAACP… But I’ve resigned my post from the NAACP.” As Jordan finishes his sentence, the crowd, who didn’t know that Jordan was fired the night before, begins to gasp in awe. Jordan nods solemnly as he ruffles his blue suit. He clears his throat. Jordan X Shabazz: “Also, in the PCW, I don’t know what is going on with me, it appears as if the white man has struck again. They have decided that I am not worthy of the Rising Star Championship anymore, considering they have not booked me in the Rising Star four-way title match.” Jordan X Shabazz hangs his head in shame and anger. The 300 or so in attendance begins to boo greatly. Jordan actually did care about the Rising Star Championship, but apparently, it wasn’t in the cards for him. Jordan continues on. Jordan X Shabazz: “Even worse, somehow I managed to piss off one of the match makers, and now I’m facing… Mike… Frampton… On Turmoil.” The wrestling fans in the crowd do a collective groan. Many of them begin to get very angry, and began shouting obscenities about the matchmakers. Jordan X Shabazz: “But fret not my loyal Negroes and Negrettes, I’ve found myself once again. Getting into the PCW has sidetracked me from my ultimate goal, the ultimate goal of total domination of the United States. It’s time us Negroes rise up, it’s time we rise up and… FIGHT THE POWER!” Jordan screams and throws his right hand in the air to add enthusiasm to his statement. Some people in the crowd are generally angered by the fact that the white man is still running America. Some of them begin screaming loudly. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now, I ask you all, to support me in this time of need…” The people begin to cheer for Jordan. Jordan X Shabazz: “I’ve brought a few people here with me.” Jordan says while gesturing towards Jesse Jackson, Al Sharpton, and OJ Simpson. Jordan X Shabazz: “These people… Are helping me start up a NEW group of black men, women, and children, that will help African American’s all throughout America. It will be called Negroes International Growing Group Association… Yes I know, the name isn’t too great, but you can call it by the acronym of N.I.G.G.A! The crowd is now into Jordan’s speech. The three men sitting on the white chairs behind Jordan X Shabazz, Jesse Jackson, Al Sharpton, and OJ Simpson all stand up, and they pull on a string that reveals a large poster showcasing the acronym N.I.G.G.A, and having a picture of Jordan X Shabazz standing up with a military like weapon in his hands. Jordan X Shabazz: “Ladies and gentlemen, it’s a new era, and a new day, JORDAN X SHABAZZ IS BACK TO HIS OLD WAYS!” The scene fades with Jordan X Shabazz, Jesse Jackson, Al Sharpton, OJ Simpson, and a bunch of angry, black crowd members, throwing their right hands in the air.
