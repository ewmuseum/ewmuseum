A Philosopher Part 2

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8th, 2002 *Monologue* Mark Malone, humphy, a little nazi. A LITTLE NAZI! You see I have somewhat a problem with Nazis. I'm an anti-nazi, someone who will shoot a nazi. I don't take their crap and I damn sure will not take yours. I will be at Fusion and I will stomp a hole straight in your ass. No nazi could ever take me down. You hail Hitler all you want but I shit on your flag. Malone, I am going to kick your ass from here all the way back to 1939 Germany. I am going to win Mark, I am going to kill you. *End Of Monolgoue* 'To fight is simple. To not fight is to be strong.' said an old thoughtful man 'To fight is to be me. To be me is to fight.' replied Frampton 'To say such things is to be out of touch with the real world.' said the old thoughtful man 'My world has carnage and fighting. This world is my world.' replied Frampton 'To see through eyes of yours is to see through the eys of satan.' replied the old thoughtful man 'My eyes are the eyes of a man trapped. A man trapped in a world of hate, violence, and brutality.' replied Frampton 'Tell me young sir, what do you do for a living?' asked the thoughtful old man 'I fight for money. Money for I fight.' said Frampton 'I can see you love your work.' said the thoughtful old man 'That I do.' said Frampton 'How can a young man like you love to fight?' asked the thoughtful old man 'I love taking others to my world, this world.' finished Frampton *Fin*
