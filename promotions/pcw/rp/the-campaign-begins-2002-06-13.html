The Campaign Begins..........

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th June 2002 Gold........ Gold is one of the things in this world that men grave. Gold is what drives men of this day and age. I bet if you were to pass a man in the street and ask him what was on his mind right now, apart from the obvious, he would probably say money. Money and gold makes this world go round. He who has the gold, has the power. He who is without the gold, does not have the power. You see gold divides the rich from the poor, the powerful from the weak, but most importantly the good from the evil........... All this talk of gold is for a reason. You see even though the Dark Messiahs have been away from the PCW for a while, they still have that thirst. They still have that graving for gold. However the gold The Dark Messiahs grave is not gold that you can buy something with. Oh no, the gold they seek is the gold of prestige, the gold that you wear around your waist to show the rest of the world that you are the best in your field. The gold I talk about of course is none other than PCW Tag Team gold. It is obvious that every tag team in the PCW grave the same thing The Dark Messiahs do, so what, I hear you ask, makes The Dark Messiahs so sure that they are going to get a shot at the titles??? All will come clear in a matter of moments as we join The Dark Messiahs.........in a mall?!?!? The scene opens up in the centre of the mall. As like most days the mall is buzzing with shopaholics. Mums dragging their tired crying children from one shop to the next, ignoring their whines because of the importance of getting that one dress. Men in suits also walk briskly with a hot Starbucks Cappuccino in their right hand and a mobile in their left pressed close to their ear. Old couples slowly make they’re way through the crowds with the people behind them getting irritated about how slow they are walking. Yes indeed it is another happy day in the mall (not). In the middle of this chaos, is a stall, a bright red stall. There is a curved sign connected to 2 wooden poles on the stall, and on the curved sign it reads, 'Just Give Us A Chance!!!' On the desk of the stall are a few fliers, what they say on them we do not know. Also along side them is a megaphone. Sat down on a steel chair behind the stall with his feet on the stall and arms crossed is Mike Stone. He watches with interest the hustle and bustle of the mall, he like many others wonders why so many people are so addicted to shopping??? As Stone wonders on, Daniel Steel comes into view carrying 2 Starbucks Coffees. He has to squeeze passed a few people before he eventually arrives at the stall. Stone stands up and is handed his coffee. Both men take sip of their coffee before placing them on the stall desk. Steel takes in the mall atmosphere before turning to Stone........ Daniel Steel: (Amazed) Sure is busy today. Mike Stone: (Sighs) It's always busy in a mall. It is amazing the amount of people who are obsessed with shopping. I mean what do they get out of it, apart from a huge credit card bill every month. Daniel Steel: Does it really matter??? I mean look at it this way, the bigger the crowd of people, the better, because more people will hear about our campaign. Mike Stone: (Anxious) I'm not sure about this whole campaign thing. Are you sure it is going to work??? Daniel Steel: (Confident) Of course it is going to work, trust me. Stone still looks apprehensive as Steel takes a last sip of coffee before picking up the megaphone and moving to the front of the stall facing the crowd........ Daniel Steel: (Through Megaphone) Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, may I please have your attention. Almost instantly the crowd stops. There is absolute silence in the mall now. The whining children have stopped crying, and all attention is now on Steel. Steel smiles and speaks again.......... Daniel Steel: Thank you. Now let me start off by asking you a question. How many of you watch PCW??? There is a little discussion in the crowd, until they fall silent.............. Daniel Steel: Could I see a show of hands please??? Another little discussion in the crowd before about 25 hands shoot up........ Daniel Steel: (Smiles) Okay thank you again. Now for my second question, what do you people think about the state of the PCW Tag Team Division at the moment??? A man's hand at the front shoots up......... Daniel Steel: (Pointing to the man) Yes, you sir...... Man: (Nervous) Well.....erm..... I think that at the moment it kind of stinks, nothing much is really happening, it's a bit boring. Daniel Steel: (Agreeing) Sir, you just took the words right out of my mouth. Ladies and gentlemen you heard what that man said, the PCW Tag Team division at this moment in time stinks!!! And you know why it stinks??? It's because at this moment in time 2 tag teams, The Heartbreakers and the Pretty Cool Guys are being hailed as the best Tag Teams in the PCW today. Now call me jealous if you want, but have any of you actually seen these 2 teams in action??? Their wrestling ability is none existent. I mean sure I’m not afraid to admit that we got beat by The Heartbreakers but that was just pure luck on their part. Anyway getting back to our point, not only to they both lack wrestling skill but they don't even seem to care about wrestling. I mean on one side you have got a team who are obsessed with the colour pink, and on the other a team who are more concerned with their music careers than actually training for their next match. There is another discussion in the crowd before some speaks up from the back........... Person in the crowd: Get to the point??? Steel looks to see who shouted that, whilst he does Stone sighs with impatience and grabs the megaphone........ Mike Stone: (Through Megaphone) Our point is quite simple ladies and gentlemen. Just like you we do not want to see these 2 talentless teams getting a shot at the tag team titles before we do. That is why we are here to day ladies and gentlemen to campaign for a shot at the PCW tag team titles. (Points to sign on stall) All we ask is for you to 'Just Give Us A Chance.' There is a slight chuckle from the crowd, Steel looks a bit hurt by this chuckle. But Stone looks at him and shakes his head, before turning back to the crowd.......... Mike Stone: (Slightly Angry) Yes go ahead and laugh if you want, but this is a serious matter and if action is not taken immediately then you people could have one of those talentless teams as your PCW Tag Team champions. Is that what you want??? Do you want to see the PCW Tag Team Division return to the state it was a month ago. We certainly don't, and if you people called yourself PCW fans, you wouldn't either. So what I want you to do, if you are true PCW fans is to come up to this stall and sign our petition, so that the PCW Tag Team division actually has a future. If you peop........... Before Stone can finish both he and Steel drop down to their knees grabbing their heads screaming in pain. We've seen them act like this before, and know it is bad news, unfortunately the people in the crowd don't and draw closer. The crowd has now formed a circle around the men. They seem amazed by what is going on. Suddenly both Steel and Stone stop screaming and get back up to their feet. They dust themselves off then snap their heads around to face the ground. Just by the look in their eyes you can tell something has changed............ Daniel Steel: (Shouting) What the f**k are you lot looking at, haven’t you seen another person before?!?! Man: (Confused) But, you were just on the floor screaming in pain........ Mike Stone: (Angry) Don't be so stupid. Man: (Still confused) But you were just........ Before the man can finish Steel grabs him by the collar and pulls him close so they are nose to nose. Steel's facial expression is nothing but anger, as he stares the man in the eyes....... Daniel Steel: (Anger) Hey, you need to learn when to shut your mouth!!! (Turning to Mike) What you say Mike??? You think we should teach this piece of s**t a lesson??? Stone nods with an evil grin on his face. Steel quickly throws the man over and Stone grabs his throat. Steel points over to the stall, and Stone nods again. The crowd makes a path towards the stall, they all seem captivated yet again (people nowadays, eh?). Steel grabs a chair from behind the stall and places it behind Stone and the man. Stone looks behind him to check Steel is there, he then lifts the man up as if to chokeslam him, as he does Steel takes a running jump from the steel chair and hits the man in the face. Stone quickly brings the man down right through the wooden table!!!! The women in the crowd scream in terror. Steel quickly snaps round to face the crowd............ Daniel Steel: (Shouting) You see, that is why we deserve the damn title shot!!! And that is why we are the best damn tag team in the PCW today!!! Stone then snaps round........ Mike Stone: (Shouting) We are suicidal, we are genocidal, and we are homicidal!!!! And we are getting our title shot, no matter what anyone else says. Be prepared because there is going to be pain!!!! After Stone has said this the crowd frantically scatter and the scene fades to black..........
