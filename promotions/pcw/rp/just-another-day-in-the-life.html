Date: 7/8/04 7:30 A.M Each digit on the clock had a deep crimson ambience surrouding it. A flip of a switch and I turned the alarm on the clock off before the woman who lay beside me would wake up. Her name was long since forgotten after a night full of Jim Beam and wild sex. Just another day in the life of Buzz, right? This is what I did....wrestle, party, screw, run away while they slept, and then start back at step one. The cycle had grown own to me, however. I was only two years away from thirty years old, and as much as I hated to admit it....It was becoming time to settle down a bit. The truth was I didn't know how. She stirred around a bit and I froze. She stopped and I slowly crept off of the bed. I gathered up my clothes and after a few minutes I left behind a memory that would most likely be forgotten come tomorrow. As I drove down the highway, I thought about my time in PCW so far, and what was next. The only thing in my sight was winning the World title, but not just from anyone. From Skylar Green, himself. Since I had stepped foot in PCW that had been my goal. Many did not understand why I would focus solely on the title, and why I would have the guts to do so, early in my PCW career. The fact was I had spent seven years on the wrestling circuit, and felt that it was time to finally get my due in the big time. Nothing more nothing less. RING RING Who could be calling at 7:30, I thought to myself. I fumbled through my bag on the passenger seat and pulled out my cellphone. 'Hello', I answered without hesitation. 'Hello sexy...do you know who this is?', purred a voice. 'Uh......', I had no clue. The voice was familiar, but a name wasn't. 'Monica! From the show last night....' she responded with vibrant enthusiasm. 'OH THE RAT!...Shit...', I caught my mistake, but it was too late. 'The WHAT! What the fuck did you just call me! I am not a ring rat, asshole. I can't believe I slept with a jerk last night..I thought you said I was special! Ughh...', she was obviously not happy with the comment. I began to explain myself but found myself talking to dial tone. It didn't really matter though. I'd always have more women, but the World Title gold I was going after wouldn't always be there. Now was the time to grab it. Let's just hope it doesn't hang up on me when I come calling. Heh...Just another day in the life of Buzz, right?
