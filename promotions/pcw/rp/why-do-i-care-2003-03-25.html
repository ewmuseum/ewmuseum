Why Do I Care?

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Why Do I Care? Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-25-2003 I never asked to be who I am….it just kinda happened. To be perfectly honest, I don’t get where I came from or how I came to be who I am. It’s like everyone has some big “origin” or purpose as to how they came to be or why they d the things they do. Everyone, from Dorn Tracer to Akiyama, has some BIG huge history as to why they act the way they act…fight who they fight….and say what they say. Except me.How’d this come to my Rent-A-Senses, simple. I looked at the MOTIVATIONS behind EACH AND EVERY one of my opponents this Tuesday, and I must say, it has some relation at least to their origin.Akiyama needs that title to prove his dominance as the true force in PCW….Xavier wants it because he’s just a greedy, conniving fiend who feels that if it has a place on Earth, he’s entitled to it AKA the “Lex Luthor” complex. ”Totally Tubular” Tommy is forever trying to hammer in the point that he’s a big time player, he’s been trying to prove that ever since breaking free of being one-half of the Cowabunga Connection and trying to prove he’s not just some joke….. I could go on and on about the motivations and backgrounds as to what drive the way we as people….in my case “Kryptonian”, do things. Except my own. All I can remember is just waking up with a costume on and getting into one dumb situation after the next. And I’m not talking ONCE IN AWHILE….I’m talking ALL the time Rent-Heads.From battles with intergalactic herpe’s infested ho’s to being thrown from buildings to see if I can “fly”, I’d find myself getting pushed in directions that I could NEVER explain myself going into. It’s as if some force I don’t know about just scripts what happens to me next….and it happens OVER and OVER and OVER again. WHY DO I LIKE GUMMI BEARS?! WHY DO I WEAR THESE TIGHT SPANDEX COSTUMES DAY IN AND DA OUT?! WHY IS MY MIND SET ON FIGHTING TRUTH AND JUSTICE?! WHY AM I ALWAYS FIGHTING?! Xavier can blame his greed on oppression…. Aki can blame his lust for strength and power on his eternal God-hood… FOR GOD’S SAKE, BLADED could blame his obsessions with convoluted promo’s and apple’s on his superhuman soldier upbringing! Yet when I wake up everyday and fight a clan of superhuman samurai pizza cats or fight a gang of street ruffians….or even battle the villains of PCW, I can never explain my actions. I can never figure out the higher purpose or the reason of why I’m so obsessed with truth and justice and WHY I’m so willing to fight day in day out.Now I’m going for the PCW World Championship….if you’re a wrestler, you WANT this championship, whether you admit it or not. The fact is…..if I won it, why did I do it? Big Alan Steele did it to stop the tarnishment of the title by the New Breed…. Tyler Nelson won it so it could be made his HIGHEST trophy in his quest for utter power…Even Corey Williams won the title so he could keep Big Alan Steel from getting it, being the prick he was.Duke Wilaims won it to prove he had it in him that “one last time” before bowing out…. And here I am, Rent-A-Hero….a 22 year-old man living in an apartment in a city with no name, getting into constant mishaps and adventures that men with FULL lives could never hope to experience. And I don’t even know how I got there or why do it.I thought my biggest challenge would be finding some way, some how, to defeat 9 other men in hopes of claiming the richest prize in our sport. Now I’m stuck here, mere hours from the start of Turmoil….and I don’t even know why I want it. It feels like chasing a girl, but not knowing why you want her so bad. I can’t name a SINGLE self-motivation as to why I want that title and I can’t help but think that it feels like my life is not my own…. Everyday, I’m walking a path I’m not sure I would have taken.Everyday, I’m fighting a foe I’m not sure I need to beat. Everyday, it feels like everything is happening because someone else deems it so. I don’t know if this is truly the case…but whoever you are….whoever places me in these stupid scenarios…Whoever it is who thinks it’s Fun to put me in illogical battle after battle.YOU ARE MY ENEMY! YOU ARE THE PERSON I WANT TO BRING TO JUSTICE! YOU are the reason why I can’t seem to make decisions for myself, do what I want, or be the person I wan to be. I HAVE THE BIGGEST OPPOURTUNITY IN THE WORLD IN FRONT OF ME….and it’s because of YOU that I can’t figure out why it’s so big to me. Do you think it’s funny? Is it funny to control my life? It must be great knowing YOU have the power to make me or break me day in or day out, whoever or whatever it is you are. If you an hear me, heed my warning citizen….STAY OUT OF MY LIFE! Tonight is my night to MAKE a reason why I should have that title around my waist.Tonight is the night to go down in the record books with a purpose….and for once in my spandex enwrapped life….I’ll have say as to how I do it. I am not a pawn or a character you use to escape your life….I am a person too, and because of you, I’m not sure what it is that defines me as a person.By tonight, I’ll have my purpose….I’ll know WHY I want that World title that bad… And hopefully, I’ll know who you are. Because when I find out, you will NEVER return…Same Rent-A-Time… Same Rent-A-Channel. Finding you may not be the BEST purpose…but it’s a start. ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
