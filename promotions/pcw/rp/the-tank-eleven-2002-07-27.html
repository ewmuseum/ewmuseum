THE TANK __11__ Rules of War CrashCollideDestroyLike butterflies pulled by the tides of wind When the winds calmCattapillers will sift the ashes ~Leigh Donovan Friday, July 26 - Day 22 On Friday evenings, Miggy is always sure to take his books back to the library as early as he can, without risking punishment. He knows that Snow will be wrestling in some other part of the country, and won’t be able to protect him from the Nazis. He’ll still have to cross back in front of Harvey Blake and his gang, but Miggy knows that even though Jason Snow has left the prison, he hasn’t left the Nazis’ minds. “This is horseshit!” says Harvey Blake as he leans on the pool table, watching the old Latino pushing his little cart of books toward the gym, where he’ll cross to the library. “Fucking Italians keeping my hands tied. What the fuck do they care if I give the fucking spick a little scare?” “I don’t think it’s the Italians who care,” says Derek, a young skinhead who’s long been a one of Blake’s most trusted Nazi’s. “I’m guessing the real reason why your hands are tied is because of a certain Irish cunt.” Blake turns to Derek as the young skinhead leans in over the pool table to take a shot and nods to him. Derek is right, after all, Jason Snow has been a thorn in their sides since coming to the Tank some three weeks ago. “Whatever the case,” Blake says, turning his lone eye back in Miggy’s direction, “Hattoni wants me dead. That’s no secret. Worse yet, he doesn’t only want me dead for Snow’s sake - that little prick has wanted me dead from the minute he got here. But after what I did to his fucking buttbuddy, Bruno, Doug’s going to make this shit personal.” Derek carelessly drops his pool stick on the green tabletop after sinking the eight ball. “To be honest Harv, I don’t know why Hattoni hasn’t moved yet. He and Bruno were tight.” “He’s biding his time,” says Blake as Miggy disappears into the gym. “Trying to make me sweat. But the old bastard should know by now - I don’t sweat. He can play this waiting game all he wants, but that’s not how I operate.” Blake stands up straight and straightens his plaid shirt while staring at the heavy steel door of the gym through his one blue eye. “Boys,” he says, “it’s time to fucking move.” *** From the Office of the Warden “We’ve already established that prison has politics,” says the Warden as he stares out his window over the prison grounds. “And with politics, the end result is, and always has been, war... Prison wars are much the same as national wars.” The Warden turns to his desk and leans on it. “You have one group fighting against another, strategically placing their attacks, trying to be sure not to piss anyone else off in the process. More politics. But with any war, if you look deep enough, you’re going to find that what it all comes down to... is two men. Two men with armies willing to fight and die for their beliefs.” The Warden pulls two photos out from his desk - Harvey Blake and Doug Hattoni. *** “Someone get a fucking doctor!” screams a Correctional Officer as he rushes out of the gym, his uniform covered in someone else’s blood. Immediately, the C.O’s split into two groups - one group rushes into the gym to help with, and investigate, the situation. The other group draw their batons as they prepare to send the prisoners back to their cells. “Lockdown!” shouts another of the C.O’s as he and his fellow Officers begin to herd the confused prisoners to their respective cells. “Everybody get in your fucking cell! Now!” Within minutes, the job is done. The prisoners, curious and confused, go back into their respective cells without putting up an argument, eagerly watching the gym door. Among these prisoners is Leigh Donovan. From his cell, he has a straight-across view of the gym door. He knows someone is hurt, and can’t help but hope that someone is J-Thug who he knows went into the gym less than an hour ago. “Come on,” he mutters to himself as he watches through heavily bloodshot eyes. Earlier in the day, he took Doug Hattoni’s advice and visited one of Doug’s employees with a pocket full of cash that he stole from J-Thug. Now, under the heavy influence of heroine, he stumbles back away from the bars and lies on the lower bunk of the cell, feeling nothing but pure satisfaction. Finally, he’s feeling the powerful effects of the drug. It runs through his veins like salvation - not even J-Thug can hurt him now. The large man could try, but until the drug wears off, Leigh wouldn’t feel a thing. He hopes not, at least, because if J-Thug finds out Leigh stole his money, they will both likely find out just how little pain Leigh can feel in his state of bliss. “Make room!” one of the C.O’s from the main floor bellows a moment after Leigh hears the heavy steel door open with a crash. Leigh stands back up, the powerful drug playing with all of his senses. When he gets to the bars, he spots two of the C.O’s rushing a stretcher out of the gym and toward the main gate of the Unit. Lying on that stretcher is the motionless, bloody, and dying body of Miguel Sanchez - Miggy. A moment later, three more C.O’s emerge from the room, pushing Harvey Blake’s right hand skinhead, Derek, in front of them. The young Van Dyke-sporting Nazi has his hands cuffed behind his back and is being pushed forward and beaten at the same time by the C.O’s. He’s a murderer again now. He took it upon himself to be the main man in Harvey Blake’s plan - he held the knife that slit Miggy’s throat. ***From the Office of the Warden The Warden sits in his chair behind his large oak desk staring at a chess board in front of him. He notes the shape and structure of each piece, lightly gliding his hand over the board. “Every war comes down to two men,” he begins. “We’ve already established that.” “But that doesn’t mean we can forget about the other men,” says the Warden, picking up the white bishop. “The soldiers. The men who martyr themselves for their cause - for their owner. Derek Johnson is such a man - martyring himself for Harvey Blake. His crime, which he made no attempt to hide, will no doubt leave him in solitary confinement for the better part of his natural life. And why did he do it?” The Warden picks up the white king and smiles menacingly. “He did it for Harvey Blake.” “But remember,” says the Warden as he puts the chess piece back in its place, “just because a war between two nations is actually between two people, that doesn’t mean there’s not room for a third nation - a third person.” The Warden reaches across to the other side of the chess board and picks up the black king. *** Saturday, July 27 - Day 23 Jeremy Doris, better known as J-Thug, sits up in his upper bunk a few minutes before the wake up buzzer sounds. He knows his cellmate has been stoned all night, but he decided not to do anything about it when the C.O’s finally let him return to his cell last night because he wanted to wait until the young Australian rocker sobered up. He’d feel a beating much better then. “Get up,” J-Thug says in his low tones, reaching under his pillow and pulling out his stocking hat. He puts the black hat on, covering his clean-shaven head, and pulls it down into place just above his eyes. The hat really adds a lot to J-Thug’s intimidating physical presence. Without it, he’s almost handsome. “Yeah,” groans Leigh, not quite awake enough to realize what’s going on. He rolls over on his side and slips back into dream. “I said get the fuck up!” J-Thug says a little louder, but not loud enough to alarm the C.O’s. He pulls the blankets off his waking heroine-addict cellmate. “Now!” J-Thug grabs the young Australian by the arm and effortlessly pulls him out of bed. “Crikey!” says Leigh, shaken from his slumber - he hates himself for saying that. “Where the fuck did you get it, prag?” asks J-Thug before pushing Leigh hard against the bars. “I know you got fucking high last night, and now I want to know how you managed it! I sure as hell didn’t give you shit to put in your fucking nose. How the fuck did you get it, prag!?” Leigh stammers, “I-I don’t know what you’re talking about, mate!” Enraged, J-Thug pulls his massive black hand back, about to strike the young rockstar. A moment before he unleashes his wrath, however, he hesitates. “I know where you got it, prag,” he says to himself, remembering Leigh speaking to Snow yesterday. “Where else? You went straight to the motherfucking lifeline. That fat fucker Italian!” *** From the Office of the Warden “In every war, you need supplies,” says the Warden as he sits back in his seat. “Weapons, food, water... well, in the Tank, food and water are a given, and weapons are a rarity. So the only supplies important in these walls, in most cases, are manpower and drugs.” *** As soon as roll call ends, Snow quickly heads toward Doug Hattoni’s cell, having yet to shower or do anything to make ready for the new day. Not twelve hours ago he was in an arena taking care of his PCW business. Lately, however, PCW business and prison business have begun to blend together - ever since Doug Hattoni lectured him on the power of appearances. Snow finds Hattoni in rare form - standing in front of his sink, brushing his teeth. Hattoni acknowledges Snow’s presence with a hand gesture, and then motions for his young guest to take a seat. After spitting a mouthful of toothpaste into the sink, Hattoni says to Snow, “Very nice job last night on Fusion. Very nice indeed.” “You saw?” Snow asks curiously with an eyebrow raised - he didn’t appear on Fusion until well after Unit B’s lights were cut last night. “A couple of C.O’s are good friends of mine. I make sure their families are taken care of on the outside, and they’re sure to convey whatever news I need.” Hattoni, dressed in his usual grey pants and a tight white tank top, reaches for a familiar blue plaid button-up shirt. “I know everything that went on.” “Glad you liked it.” “I’m not the one that matters,” says Hattoni. “Doesn’t matter if I think you’re a pussy. All that matters is those fucking monkeys in here think you’re the toughest bastard alive. You’ve got to be what Bruno was, and you ain’t as big as Bruno, kid.” Doug chuckles a bit lightheartedly to let Snow know that he realizes the young lion could probably take the late Bruno when the Italian was in his prime. “You’re doing very well in that department, kid. But for now, we have some business to discuss.” “Business?” Snow perks an eyebrow. “Bruno Gasperoni was my best friend in life,” Doug says, characteristically avoiding his point until the last possible moment. “He was taken away from me by those fucking Nazis. It’s true, I sent Bruno to kill Harvey Blake, but Blake knew by killing Bruno, he was starting a war with me.” “You’re letting me go after Blake?” Snow asks - he’s used to Hattoni’s guessing games now. Hattoni extends his hands, a silent gesture that requests Snow’s patience. In truth, Hattoni doesn’t know how to break the news to the young lion. “I don’t know what you saw in that Miguel Sanchez character,” Doug says, raising Snow’s curiosity by his use of the past tense. “Last night, one of Blake’s men murdered him.” “Murdered?” Snow gulps, not quite believing his ears. Even he doesn’t understand why he liked little Miggy so much. Maybe it was his helplessness. Snow has always yearned to protect - it’s the reason why he’s in prison. Now, the old helpless man who he tried to protect has been killed. “Kid,” Doug assures Snow, “rest-assured, Blake will pay. He’s taking on a war he can’t handle. I run this fucking prison, Jason - me! The killing of Miguel Sanchez was a statement, Jason. He knows I was letting you protect the old man. It’s an insult!” Snow doesn’t think of it as an insult to Hattoni - he’s not that hardened yet. He thinks of it as the loss of one of the few men he considers a friend in this hell hole. Before he can think any more on the subject, he spots the enormous figure of J-Thug in the open doorway of the cell. “Yo Hattoni,” the enormous Thug leader begins, “we need to talk.” “Not now,” Hattoni answers, waving J-Thug away. “I ain’t fucking kidding with you, Hatt!” J-Thug’s eyes are intense, letting Hattoni know that there is a serious issue at hand. “We need to talk and we need to talk now!” “Would you listen to that tone?” Doug says quietly to Snow, shaking his head. Jason Snow, leaning on Hattoni’s bunk, prepares himself to fight. “I think he’s unhappy about something. Well, Doris, what the hell is all this about?” J-Thug steps inside the cell, sure to keep Snow under watch though his eyes are fixed on Hattoni. “My fucking bitch up in my cell!” J-Thug spits. “You knew I was keeping him under tap, and you set the motherfucker up with a treat. You know better than to fuck with me, Hattoni! You’ve got enough problems with the motherfucking Nazis! You don’t want the niggers on your ass too - believe that, motherfucker!” “Hey!” Hattoni says sharply, turning toward J-Thug, “Watch your fucking language! The kid came to me looking for a fix. I told him if he had the cash, I’d set him up. That’s the way it works in here, Doris, since your memory seems to be out of town. And as long as you’re here, Doris, I should tell you that I don’t like your fucking tone! Don’t forget, Doris - don’t forget who you’re talking to!” “Who I’m talking to!?!” J-Thug explodes, stepping toward Hattoni. “Listen you fat old fuck, you know who I am? I’m J-motherfucking-Thug! You acting all fucking tough now that you got your fucking lion here,” J-Thug points to Snow, “but I don’t give a fuck about him! We’ll see how tough you talk when I have him on his knees sucking on my dick, Hattoni!” “That has nothing to do with it,” Hattoni fires back, surprising Snow by taking a step toward the massive J-Thug. “You want to talk about politics? Fine! We’ll talk about politics. Doris, I own your ass! You know why? Because you may be ‘J-motherfucking-Thug, but I’m Doug motherfucking Hattoni! Don’t forget how you keep your control over your precious Thugs, Doris. You wouldn’t be shit in here without me! If I didn’t supply you with drugs to feed out to your gang, do you know what you’d be?” J-Thug is silent - he realizes that he’s been defeated. Doug doesn’t stop, however - he answers his own question harshly. “Without me, Doris, you’re nothing but a big nigger on a big power trip. How do you think your loyal followers are going to react to you then?” J-Thug is silent - he knows Doug’s right. Size, strength, and brains will bring a man to power in the Tank, but without drugs, he’s all too vulnerable. “Get the fuck out of here,” Doug says, nodding toward the open doorway of the cell. In hopeless rage, J-Thug does as he’s told and exits Hattoni’s cell. As he walks toward his own cell, his eyes burn with rage. He’s never dealt well with authority, especially when the figure with authority sleeps at night in a cell no larger than his own.
