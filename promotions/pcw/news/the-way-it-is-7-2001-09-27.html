The Way It Is #7 Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Sep 2001 ---- DISCLAIMER: THE FOLLOWING COLUMN IS WRITTEN BY ONE JONATHAN KANO, AN EMPLOYEE OF PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING (PCW). MR. KANO IS A WELL-RESPECTED MEMBER OF THE PCW FAMILY, BUT IN NO WAY DOES HIS VIEWS REFLECT THOSE OF PCW OR ITS ASSOCIATES. IF YOU ARE OFFENDED BY STRONG OPINIONS OR OBJECTIONABLE LANGUAGE, THEN DO NOT CONTINUE. IF YOU CHOOSE TO CONTINUE, YOU HEREBY RELEASE PCW AND JON KANO FROM ANY LEGAL ACTION. Well, Ladies and Gentlemen, life is a bitch. Lets backtrack to last Saturday night, I was listening to PCW Malice, and I said, "Oh what the hell, I'll call in," I mostly thought that because I couldn't take the Red Army propaganda anymore. To make a long story short, my cell phone went to hell and my reception was bad, so I got cut off. Then I continued to listen only to hear my good old buddy, The Phantom Booker, make several jokes about me, and to make some comments about me that I didn't appreciate. I had a nice long conversation with him that night. We got everything straightened out. Damn, breaks your heart that a friendship can actually last in the world of professional wrestling, huh? TRAINING I hate that damn tattler site. I basically killed myself to help my tag partner get in shape for the Lethal Lottery, and suddenly I become gay? Why? Because I sat on his back to add my 300 pounds to his body weight and made him do push ups. Just to toughen him up. But hey, if this little rumor makes my merchandise sales go up, and in turn puts more money in my pocket, fine. You can say I worship Liberace for all I care. But I will say one thing that needs to be said, I AM NOT NOW, NOR HA VE I EVER BEEN A HOMOSEXUAL. I love women, I love how they smell, and how they can pleasure me. I don't get the whole gay thing. I do not find myself or any other man attractive. Men, as a whole, are ugly creatures. (Note from Steven Smith: Denial just ain't a river in Egypt!) Women are designed to be beautiful. And I love beauty. But anyway, I will say one thing about Tim Walden, that son of a bitch, is a lot tougher and stronger than I thought. He actually gained my respect, when he trained that damn hard and never once bitched about it. Why? Because he knew that what I was preparing him for was his ticket to super-stardom. The Lethal Lottery Well, I am going out on a limb here and say that there is no way in hell that Stanislav is going to make it to the finals. Meanstreak will not co-exist with this Commie bastard. Wow, this is gonna be like Godzilla vs. King Kong. The only question is who is who? I think Tim Walden and me stand a decent chance at winning, he and I have the most respect for each other right now. We are beginning to be comfortable with each other, and once you do that you begin to develop chemistry. Chemistry between tag partners is probably the greatest single ingredient to victory. Brock Newbludd and Stephen Blood are prime examples of this. Neither one were the greatest single wrestlers ever, but they had chemistry and that is what made them great. I just want one thing out of this Lethal Lottery, I want to see Boda and Ivan butt heads in this thing. I personally think Boda is better than Ivan, and hardly anyone knows Chris Sloboda better than me. Things I Wonder About? Who cares if Kaiser is missing? Who else thinks DT got screwed out of the HoF poll? Am I Christ-like or what? Isn't it nice having Kremmen as CEO? Am I the only one who is starting to like Badge? Am I the only one who thinks Boda could hand Ivan's ass to him? Am I the only one who would pay good money to see a three way dance between Meanstreak, Ivan, and Boda? Did anyone miss this column? What is Shoot King going to do with out Guy Leslie here? Anyone else sick of the coverage of NYC? Couldn't we just turn the Middle East into a parking lot and save everyone a lot of trouble? (Not from the Phantom Booker: A forest always grows back stronger after burned to the ground. See also the Phantom Booker's political policies on Puerto Rico, Guam and the Phillipines.) Was what I just said totally fucked or what? Aren't you glad you read this column, did it not make you just a little smarter? Thats all, JK
