Storms in Stormville? Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Sep 2001 ---- Recent reports of Jay Storm thinking of himself as "majorly ugly" have still come to light. Apparently this is all because of a women.. or so his brother AJ, explains: "We were in this club right, and Jay tried hitting on a hot babe. She told him to sod off guvnor as he don't like people who look like they've been run over by a truck.". We here at PCW extend our condolences to our fans who have to look at Jay's hidious face during shows. We are currently running an appeal to fun Jay to wear a paper bag over his head during events, but so far we've only generated a mere two Canadian Dollars. 0800-JAYSTORMISANUGLYMOFO... please donate generously, and perhaps we can pay "Doctor" Ed to bash some beauty into Jay's face with Bob, his trusty sidekick.
