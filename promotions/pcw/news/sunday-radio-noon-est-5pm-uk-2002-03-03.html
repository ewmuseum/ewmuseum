Sunday Radio: Noon EST/5pm UK

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday Radio: Noon EST/5pm UK Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2002 ---- Primetime Central Radio with The Phantom Booker and Miyagawa, will its usual Sunday edition at Noon EST/5pm UK time. The Bookerman and Miya will host the second edition of PTC Radio's Dating Game, with NTICW's Aimz' being the lucky bachelorette, choosing from three mystery bachelors from the wacky world of wrestling. Additionally, Booker and Miya will give away free poser images, preview PCW's March Madness and OSW's Crowning Glory Pay Per Views, play a bit of ewrestling trivia, discuss the happenings from OSW's Slam, second round Global Title Tourney discussions and third round predictions, and of course, take calls from wrestlers and fans alike! Be there for all the fun! Primetime Central Fancast
