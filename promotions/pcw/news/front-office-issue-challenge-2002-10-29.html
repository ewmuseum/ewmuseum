Front Office Issue Challenge

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Front Office Issue Challenge Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Oct 2002 ---- This morning, from his hotel in Rome where he is currently preparing for PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil... The owner and CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling, Xavier Kannon released a statement aimed at the big names in the Wrestling Tattler family. “On Sunday, the 24th November,” the release read, “PCW presents Alliances 2002 live from the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, Texas. While this event will be the usual showcase of PCW’s superior talent that the Front Office has assembled, we feel like a challenge. Thus, PCW’s Front Office are issuing a challenge to OSW, FSW and RUSH. A spot in the Alliances match will be available for ONE team of 4 from each Federation. Four OSW members, four RUSH members and four FSW members to fight against or alongside the PCW roster.” This announcement sent rumours buzzing around the Wrestling Tattler organisation and finally Kannon emerged to shed some more light on the situation. “Wasn’t the statement written in ‘Peon’ for you to understand?” he snarled, “I know PCW is the best Federation around, you know it’s the top of the ladder... And come Alliances we’re going to prove it against these other so-called wrestling promotions.” When asked why the Japanese promotion affiliated with WT, PURE was excluded the CEO was quick to reply. “We showed them at Red October that not only can we beat them... But we can strip their roster bare.” As the announcement was only made today, no response has been given from RUSH, Old School Wrestling or Five Star Wrestling... But with the intertwined histories of many on all 4 rosters, plenty of talent will be eager to throw their name into the hat... ...But only if the challenge is accepted.
