Meanstreak To Turmoil? Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Jan 2004 ---- Atlantic City, NJ- Hot off the presses from the PCW public relations department it was announced earlier today that this Tuesday will not only mark the return of Turmoil but also the return of Meanstreak! Could he have been the monster who destroyed the PCW hall of fame? Find out as PCW returns to its regular time slot this Tuesday at 9CST! In another development, last night Ron Starr’s lawyer Dick James used his legal knowledge to book PCW world champion Skyler Green in a handicap match heavily favoring Ron Starr’s pick for Champion Sebastian Toomes. Mixed feelings are floating around the locker room on why Skyler Green was not stripped of his title and why Toomes is booked to take it from him. Congratulations to Avery Vance for being the first wrestler promoted to PCW in its new carnation. He will debut against Terlayne Sterling in what should be an exciting match. With all these developments and after coming off of a Turmoil described as a “dud” is Duke Williams sinking wrestling’s flag ship promotion? How much control does Duke have with Ron Starr’s lackeys still lurking around? What impact will Meanstreak bring? Find out this Turmoil for it could be the last.
