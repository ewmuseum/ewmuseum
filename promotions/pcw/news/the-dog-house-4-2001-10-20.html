The Dog House #4

The Dog House #4 Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Oct 2001 ---- Hello ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the special Red October edition of the Doghouse. The Lethal Lottery tournament has boiled down to the final eight men, only one of whom can walk away from Red October the PCW Champion. I’m going to attack this match at every angle... Lets take a look at our contenders in Random order. #1 Rent-a-Hero: The child has come a long way, hasn’t he? And in such a short period of time. His workrate hasn’t been up there with the main players (As of Oct. 19, 6:34 am), so that’s going to hurt him. Talent-wise, RAH has it, no questions asked. Even if he did have the workrate, it’s not his time... yet. #2 Jeremy Howard: In my opinion, no one deserves the belt more than Jeremy Howard. He wasn’t given a chance to shine in his first reign, and he’s proven that he would have been a better champion than any of our latest ones. His workrate has been excellent, and he’s going all out for this title. Too bad his partner is still too green. #3 Snow: See Rent-a-Hero. He’s got the talent, and his workrate has been right up there with the real contenders. It’s too early for Snow. In December, or maybe even November, it may be a different story. But right now, he should be focussing on the contenders’ match. #4 Kid Wonder: Kid Wonder has made some great strides to get back into his old form. He’s getting closer, but this is one of the most competitive title hunts in recent memory. With Streak going all out, Ivan Stanislav looking good, and Jeremy Howard willing to sell his mama for the title, I have a hard time calling Kid Wonder a true contender here. If his workrate was up with those guys, then there would be no doubt. But still, Snow is his partner, and Snow’s not ready to main event a pay per view. #5 Tyler Nelson: Nelson’s coming on strong as the event draws closer while Kremmen slows down. Initially, I was writing Nelson off as not even a contender for the belt. Now, my tune has changed and I think the quality of his work has more than topped Kremmen’s. #6 Kremmen: Kremmen looks sharp as ever. Initially, I though Nelson was going to hold Kremmen down, but lately, Nelson is putting on a strong showing. Probably stronger than Kremmen’s. Still, Kremmen is the number two man in probability, just behind Streak. #7 Meanstreak: I have to give credit where credit is due. I’ve gone public in the past, bashing Streak for his disappearances but I’m really impressed this time. Streak has stuck around and put together one of the top workrates for the Main Event. He adds a lot to the strength of the roster, and brings enormous hype to the Main Event. Has Streak been the best out of the eight in this match? That’s debatable. Some of Streak’s work has been excellent, yet other examples have been poor(and yes, I did just say that). #8 Ivan Stanislav: Going home to Russia migh not have been such a pleasurable experience for Ivan, because Meanstreak had thoroughly dwarfed him in the early going. That all has changed, and now, Ivan looks like the stronger of the two going into the PPV. The team of Ivan and Streak is the only one without a weak link due to talent, workrate, or prematurity. It’s going down to the wire between these two, but Ivan is pulling ahead. PPV PREDICTIONS!! Lets get right to them... Meanstreak and Ivan vs. Snow and Kid Wonder Everyone in this match is working hard for it. In the end, there’s no way Snow and Kid Wonder will be walking out victorious. Ivan and Streak are just too strong. Initially, I thought this would be a squash, but given how hard both Snow and Wonder are working, I think it may be a better match than I first thought. Winners: Streak and Ivan Kremmen and Tyler Nelson vs. Jeremy Howard and Rent-a-Hero Jeremy Howard’s been giving his all, and like I said before, is probably the most deserving man for the title. It all really comes down to Rent-a-Hero. Kremmen and Nelson are beatable, but only if RAH gets on the ball. In the end, I have to go with Kremmen and Nelson, just because I can’t picture either of them in a #1 Contenders match for the Continental title. Winners: Kremmen and Nelson Continental Title match Badge is here for filler. I can’t see Duke Williams retaining, simply because I think Boda’s got his number this time. All in all though, if Vampir Nosferatu puts on a good show, the title is his for the taking. How can I go against Vampir? Winner: Vampir Scaffolding Match; Extreme Title I mark out for these things. Have you seen Miyagawa lately? Akiyama’s decent, as are Ed and Clyde. But Miyagawa’s finally come out of the woods and is treating the Extreme title like the World Championship. Winner: Miyagawa #1 for Extreme How does a guy as talented as Dan Hollywood get into a match like this? I don’t know, but maybe a go with Two ton will knock some sense into him. Two Ton’s a legend, and the only other real threat that I see is Darkstar. Still Two Ton should be able to take this one. Winner: Two Ton Meanstreak and Ivan vs. Kremmen and Tyler Nelson After all the hype, it’s looking like Kremmen and Nelson may actually end up the worst team of the bunch. The match probably won’t be a squash, but Streak and Ivan, undoubtedly, want it more. Winners: Streak and Ivan Rising Star Title Bolamba and Corey Williams are both talented. Williams, however, is still improving all the time. At the same time as being a good thing, that leaves him a notch below the rest. In the end, I don’t see anyone taking out Unstoppable Eugene. Winner: Unstoppable Eugene #1 for Rising Star Watch out for Syko in this match; he’s not just a jobber anymore. Other than him, all fingers and odds are on Big D. Winner: Big D TV Title Match What has happened to the TV division? Sure, the HIT’s fairly good, but just a couple of weeks ago, we had Kid Wonder, Snow, and Big Alan Steele feuding over that title. The HIT and Kramer Wild Wing? Seems a tad weak to me. Still, I’m going with the HIT to retain. Winner: The HIT Cruiserweight Title Match Everything in me wants to say Tim Walden, who is a great guy in the locker room. In the ring, however, Walden lacks championship form. So the match is between Mexi and Chet King. I’m going to go with Mexi. Winner: Mexi Tag Title Match So far, everything RAH is involved with seems to be going bad. Big Alan Steele is quite possibly the best in all of PCW, but unless Rent-a-Hero regains his phenom form, I’m taking the Westsiders. Look for a fallout between the super faces when the match is over. Winners: Westsiders #1 for Continental Title Jeremy Howard, Snow, Rent-a-Hero, and Kid Wonder. Immediately, you can cross Jeremy Howard out. Not because he can’t beat the rest, we all know he can. Cross him out because the continental title is below him. Of the people left, Snow looks like the best candidate. Winner: Snow #1 for World Title Kremmen vs. Tyler Nelson. Earlier in the week, I’d have said Kremmen. Tyler Nelson, however, is coming on stronger as of late, and probably looks a little better than Kremmen right now. I think Kremmen’s still the more likely of the two to come out on top, but still, I think Tyler Nelson’s been the better of the two. That said, I predict Kremmen to win. Winner: Kremmen Ivan Stanislav vs. Meanstreak Fans have been drooling over this for months. With the team going steady as ever right now, I don’t see how the fans won’t get a match between Streak and Ivan. Both are looking strong, with Ivan pulling ahead a tad in the late-going. Ivan will have to bury Streak to get the win. The match will be an iron man match, which obviously favours Streak to win. So, I’ll have to go with the monster for the title. Winner: Meanstreak Overall, this PPV looks amazing. A great card, top to bottom. The only thing that could have made it better would be if there were a couple of better contenders fighting for the TV title, and maybe a singles match for BAS. Still, this PPV should blow the recent ones out of the water! ~SHOOT~ Ok, here’s the deal. This is a section that’ll be appearing in the column every so often. I’ll be doing shoots, taking a page out of Shooter’s book. When I shoot, it’ll be the only time that you see words like “RP”. Got it? Now, what I want to do is look at the possible main events for Red October, and see which ones offer the most heat. By doing this, we can see which are the most likely main events. Rent-a-Hero vs. Jeremy Howard: Not going to happen, folks. Both of these guys have the talent, but Rent-a-Hero doesn’t have the heat. And he’s not putting up the RP’s he needs to in order to get the heat. Howard’s got it. Howard deserves it. But RAH vs. Howard headlining Red October? No way. RAH can’t headline... yet. What he should be doing, is using this as a golden opportunity. All eyes in PCW are on eight men right now, and he’s one of them. He should be trying to get everyone he can to notice that he’s a big deal in this business. He hasn’t done it yet. If by some chance these two make the finals, expect Howard to take the belt. Ivan Stanislav vs. Meanstreak: This is the most probable situation, since both guys are working for it. Meanstreak’s got the edge here, even though Stanislav probably is more deserving of the title. These two would make a wonderful Main Event, as they’re probably the two biggest monsters in PCW history. Look for Meanstreak to win, unless Ivan finds a way to bury him. Snow vs. Kid Wonder: A little more probable than Howard and Rent-a-Hero, but still, this match falls under the same category. Like Rent-a-Hero, Snow is simply still too green. And at the same time, Kid Wonder might not be capable of carrying a strong main event anymore unlike Jeremy Howard. Don’t expect this match to happen, but if it does, Snow will walk out champion. Tyler Nelson vs. Kremmen: The battle of the CEO’s. Quantity-wise, Kremmen’s got it. But personally, I feel Kremmen’s RP’s have been lacking the quality. Tyler Nelson’s are not. As much as I’d love to say Tyler Nelson would walk out on top of this match, all fingers point to Kremmen, mostly because of his push. Ok, so in this Pay Per View, who stands to gain and lose the most? Lets take a look... ~Who stands to gain the most in Red October?~ Meanstreak - Streak can prove to the world that he’s back and on top of his game. Kremmen - See Streak. Vampir Nosferatu - Can Vampir finally take the next step? Snow - Snow can’t go wrong with the good showing he’s had. Rent-a-Hero - In the same position as Snow, but hasn’t proven himself. ~Who stands to lose the most?~ Kid Wonder - A bad showing will take him out of the shady area of “Main Event” and drop him squarely in mid-card land. Rent-a-Hero - With the bad showing he’s had, it could be a long time before RAH gets another opportunity like this. He dropped the ball. Ivan - If Ivan loses, putting him back in a world title shot would be a mistake. We’re all getting the hint that Ivan can’t win a world title. Well, that’s it for another edition. I hope you all enjoyed it... Here’s the parting shot... Unsung Hero Jeremy Howard. There’s absolutely no doubt about it. The man is always the best when given a chance to main event, but never gets the opportunity to truly shine. He probably won’t take the belt again at Red October, but he’s been the best in the tournament, hands down. This is the only worthy champion PCW has had in months, yet still, he’s held down. His title reign only lasted a single day, and opportunity after opportunity has been taken from him since. Remember the #1 Contenders match he was suppose to get at the world title? It didn’t happen. He won CEO-ship, and it was taken away from him. He never disappoints, yet never gets the opportunity to be the main man. Instead, people who do disappoint get that opportunity. Trashcan Man? Chef? Those are our two champions after Jeremy Howard. Kreigman? Stryfe? Those are the two before Howard. And in the middle, there was one day where PCW had a real champion. In the Doghouse Rent-a-Hero, partner of the Unsung Hero. Rent-a-Hero was given the chance to shine, and really dropped the ball. He was over-booked for the card, to be sure. But still, as of right now (Oct. 20, 2001), he’s put up one RP for the PPV. What’s Howard up to? Eight? Nine? Rent-a-Hero hasn’t brought up the rear, and will be the main reason why Jeremy Howard doesn’t win a second championship. If Rent-a-Hero doesn’t quickly redeem himself, then it may be a long time before he sees another opportunity like this. Till next time; Woof Woof, baby. -The Hound
