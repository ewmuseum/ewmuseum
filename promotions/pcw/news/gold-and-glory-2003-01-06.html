Gold and Glory Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jan 2003 ---- Hershey, Pennsylvania- With three PCW Title Matches and A RUSH World Title Number One Contenders Match, the night was going to be packed with drama, elation and broken dreams. Just who would walk away with gold tonight? And who would remain staring up at the ceiling wallowing in defeat? Also on Adrenaline, Xavier Kannon sent a message to RUSH liberator El Spiffy, but what could it be? Commissioner Ellie~! gets a headache from the strangest of things and just what is going on between Brock Newbludd and Cassius Deamont?
