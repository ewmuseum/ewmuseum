The Tattler Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Mar 2002 ---- (Bill Madison is a junior reporter for wrestlingtattler.com. The views and expressions are those of Bill Madison and do not represent wrestlingtattler.com or any of its other columnists.) Hello, PCW fans and followers. Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Bill Madison. I'm a 29 year-old man from Terre Haute, Indiana. I graduated (with honors) in 1995 from Morehead State University with a degree in broadcast jounalism. I've been a life long wrestling fan, so getting involved in the business was a no-brainer. And after covering indies for local television outlets for three years, I was brought to wrestlingtattler.com to cover independents. So what brings me to PCW? During a convention in Febuary that the majority of wrestling columnists went to, (a convention where one Robert Decker was absent), I met a man by the name of Shooter Kingsley. After getting the chance to talk to the man, he brought up a replacement to his throne as PCW's lead columnist. With Duke Williams in charge, he said he'd rather quit then let Duke have the pleasure of firing him. So after a little talking to Shooter, here I am. He wishes for me to tell you that any complaints you have about his "forced resignation" can be directed directly to Duke Williams. He also wishes for me to tell one Robert Decker to have the best of luck in OSW. Now that he's out of Shooter's shadow, Rob can be the number one columnist he was bred to be. And now that I've got all that out of the way, I will no longer waste all of this space and get to the column. Just Like Old Times, PCW Will Survive The calendar may read March 2002, but it might as well be January 2001. Shady storylines. Financial difficulties. Breaking in new main eventers. Yeah, it seems like PCW is going back instead of going foward. Instead of making new history, PCW is reliving it. The roster might be bigger now, but all of the thing PCW is going through are things PCW has gone through before. So for those having their doubts about PCW, there's no need. Everything will play itself out. If you don't believe it, take the following things into account. Then: With a few exceptions, there were no real angles and feuds going on. Especially at the top of the card. Now: Except for a few feuds near the top of the card, there are no real angles going on. And the majority of the stories going on are shady at best. Then: A new crop of main eventers were brought up. Lackostress, Kid Wonder, and Trashcan Man were all pushed to the main event. The Reverend solidified his spot in the main event. And Doug Troy kept his spot in the main event. Now: A new crop of main eventers are in the midst of coming up. Corey Williams, Xavier Kannon, and Chris Chase are all being pushed to the top. Miyagawa is proving himself to be a solid main eventer. And Boda is keeping his spot at the top while other big names are dropping. Then: After Flaymin Aymin ran people over with a tractor, The Phantom Booker was sued. The company suffered serious financial problems and eventually went under new ownership. Now: Tyler Nelson undergoes serious legal problems. Everything is taken from him, including PCW. The company is now looking for an owner to heal its financial troubles. So you might as well call March 2002 a continuation of January 2001. The current times are looking like the times of old. And six months from now, when we look back on this time period, it will have the same outcome as the January 2001 period. It's the morning of April 8th. A big Pay-Per-View has just concluded hours earlier. The fighters are all healing their wounds. The winners are basking in the glory. The losers are feeling down after the tough loss. But in the end, like so many times before, the man standing tallest will be Tyler Nelson. Just like January 2001, Tyler Nelson will be the man on top after things shape themselves out. Just watch. - Bill Madison: wrestlingtattler.com junior columnist
