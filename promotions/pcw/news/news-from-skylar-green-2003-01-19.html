News From Skylar Green

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News From Skylar Green Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Jan 2003 ---- In a press statement was issued by PCW's resident blind man, Skylar Green, earlier today: "Just a few things to get said and done. "First things first, Rush Mason; I know he'll be at Turmoil and I invite him to a little verbal sparring if he so chooses. But it will have to be before my match, I'm leaving the arena right afterwards. "Right, my match. Who's my opponent? Oh, yeah, the OTHER Green in this federation. Well, I'm going to claim myself as giving more prestige to the surname of Green, and I believe that his former tag team partner, Nacosto Nerezza, will back me up on this. "Ah, Nerezza. There's a class act. He didn't mind me taking some technique advice from me. He even dedicated a move to me from a house show 6-man tag. "So, in honor of the 3-time PCW Tag Team Vampire... Champion, I'm declaring this upcoming Turmoil on the 21st to be the official NEREZZA APPRECIATION NIGHT." That was the end of the press release, save for an included CD of all of Skylar Green's former entrance music. 14 different tracks spanning rock, rap, acid jazz, techno, and more. Nice CD, but the point behind it is as obscure as Pete's waistline. More news as it comes in. From the PCW newsroom, I'm Cal Seaver, I'm very attractive, and I'm DESPERATELY trying to justify my payroll listing. Good day and God... Er... Hubbard Bless.
