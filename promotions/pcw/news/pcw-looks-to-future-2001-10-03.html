PCW Looks to Future

3 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Looks to Future Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Oct 2001 ---- PCW officials are not happy with the work rates of several of its long time superstars. "How in the world can we continue to justify giving airtime and paying out huge salaries to guys like Doug Troy and The Reverend, when young hungry talents like Totally Tubular Tom and Xavier Kannon are working their asses off in the PWA," said PCW Personnel Director Jane Reagan, "Come next week, some of the so called 'old guard' are going to find themselves unemployed." Reagan made the comments at a recent charity benefit to help raise money to support deceased PCW superstar Judas Steele's six illegitimate children. No word from Troy or The Reverend on Reagan's comments. We'll bring you more on this as it becomes available.
