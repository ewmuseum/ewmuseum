Akiyama Update

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Akiyama Update Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Sep 2002 ---- After the Hit-And-Run incident on the Neo Void God concluding Tuesday Turmoil, EMTS rushed him to a local facility to check on his health. After a full examination, Akiyama was reported to have a major amount of blood loss from the accident. Doctors has a hard time handling the abnormal condition of Akiyama's blood, as told by medical expert, Mikado Kisaragi, Ph.d. "His blood is black." she reports to PCW reporter Tiffany Jennings. "I've consulted all my associates, and no one else has ever seen it or heard of a situation like this in medical history. It's a medical anomale, to say the least." When asked on Akiyama's condition, the doctor shrugged her shoulders. "Akiyama lost at least 4.5 quarts of blood, which is half the normal human's amount. Akiyama, however, makes 9 quarts of his unique black blood. Any other person would be in intensive care awaiting blood donors, but Akiyama is surprisingly moving about on his own and functioning moderately. The only difference mentioned by his manager, Cyclonica, is that he seems to be acting distant, empty, and has been brooding heavily.From what I've seen, he's been sulking around in the hospital room staring at the floor silently for the past half hour. He's been released, but I have to make check ups on him every few days to see any change in his condition." Tiffany Jennings waited until Akiyama and Cyclonica came out of the hospital to talk with them. She reports that the Neo Void God looks paler than usual, and had stiffer movements apart from his normal graceful movements. Cyclonica was said to look paler herself. When asked about what happened, she responded: "The god and I have discussed that he not be so active in the ring because of this condition until otherwise dealt with. If there is any activity, it will be dealt with low tolerance. That...will be very soon though, as we have plans set in action. As far as Asylum is concerned... they might be missing a member after I am finished."
