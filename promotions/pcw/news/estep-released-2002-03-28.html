Estep Released

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Estep Released Posted by Sandy Wong on 28 Mar 2002 ---- The contract of Mark Estep was terminated today by PCW. When asked about this sudden termination, PCW spokesman Pimply Faced Intern Kid had this to say: "Who?" I guess that about sums up his career in PCW. The guys in the mail room wish Estep the best of luck in his future endeavors.
