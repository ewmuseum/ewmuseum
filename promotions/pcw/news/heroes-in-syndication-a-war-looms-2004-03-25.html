Heroes in Syndication: A War Looms

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heroes in Syndication: A War Looms Posted by Dream Syndicate on 25 Mar 2004 ---- In what should already be an incredible night of action in Raleigh, North Carolina, an epic tag team showdown is set to occur that could quite possibly shake the organization to the core. And, oddly enough, it's not just E2 vs. MSA for the Tag Titles that has the ability to rock the wrestling world. Olf Magnus Svensson, Zhou, Dom DeMerius, and Ethan Richards are preparing to wage a bloody, brutal war on the next episode of Turmoil. After several weeks of confrontations in and out of PCW shows, the two teams look to clash in a match that will surely have epic effects on the PCW Tag Team Championship chase! Be sure to tune into PCW Turmoil, live Saturday night, only on FOX!
