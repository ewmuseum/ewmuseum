Semore Smoke Arrested? Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Aug 2002 ---- As the title of the report says, Semore Smoke was arrested after trying to smuggle drugs into the United States after his week off from PCW. It seems that PCW stayed in Jamaica after the Wrestle Vessel Pay Per View with a weeks leave from PCW. He stayed there to "enjoy" himself as Semore put it. When coming back into the country for his match against Jaden this week at Tuesday Turmoil, custom agents reported him as "acting suspicious". They then pulled him aside and searched his bags and himself; they ripped open an air pocket in Semore's luggage and found a kilo of marijuana hidden in it. They immediately placed him under arrest with attempts to smuggle illegal substances into the United States. He spent last night in jail and took his one phone call, to call fellow PCW superstar, "Controversial" Corey Williams to come bail him out. His bail of $100,000, was posted by CCW and Smoke vowed to pay him back, when PCW heard of this, reporters flocked to him for comment, here is what Semore Smoke had to say: "I did not try to smuggle illegal drugs into the USA. I was pulled aside, but they grabbed the wrong baggage as mine was taken away from me. Without proof as the luggage being mine, they were forced to book a hearing, which I will go to this next Thursday here in Atlanta, Georgia. I will present my case then, and I'll be sentenced in the same day. There is no proof that the luggage is mine, and in fact the tag on the luggage isn't even my name. My bag was carried away by someone who obviously was slick, but I will find who framed me and forced me to spend the night in jail." When asked about why CCW was the one to bail him out, he had this to say: "After Wrestle Vessel we started to form a friendship so to speak, it's just professional, and knowing that he'd be the only person in PCW who would bail me out of jail." We will bring to you the hearing results when they're available next week. But until then we'll keep you posted more if more is available on the charges and how serious they may be.
