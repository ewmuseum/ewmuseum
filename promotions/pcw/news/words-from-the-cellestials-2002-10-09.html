Words From The Cellestials

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Words From The Cellestials Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Oct 2002 ---- Yuri “Quest” Takayama, freelance reporter with the Tokyo Wrestling News, sat down recently with the Celestial family, Akiyama, his two daughters, Cyclonica and Anarchy, and Glacia, Cyclonica’s mother. The interview went off without a hitch, surprising to say that Yuri is a close friend of Inphino Blitz, Akiyama’s prime enemy. “What has life been like for all of you since finding out that you’re family?” Yuri asked Cyclonica, the spokeswoman for the Celestials. “It’s been a new experience for us all, I can say,” she replied. “ I knew Anarchy had Celestial within her since I saw her, but to find out that we’re half sisters was something even I did not see coming. Nor did I know that the doctor that treated the god would in doubt be my mother.” Yuri turns to Glacia next. “How did you come about finding Cyclonica?” “I had heard in Japan that a blue and black haired woman had taken charge of Akiyama’s affairs. After going through my sources, I suspected that she was indeed my daughter, especially after seeing her face to face a while ago around Wrestle Vessel. She was a spitting image of me at her age, so I took on the lead of Dr Mikado Kusanagi, and clarified my suspicions. Of course I had no idea that Anarchy was also Akiyama’s daughter.” “But how did you become separated from Cyclonica in the first place?” Glacia shakes her head slowly. “That is something I cannot discuss right now.” “Anarchy,” Yuri says, turning to her. “How do you feel now knowing that your father is the Neo Void God?” Anarchy shrugs her shoulders. “It’s a little strange, but when I think about it…something about the whole thing just seems to fit in my life. I knew there had to be something going on, because when he and Cyclonica thought that I had ran him over, he didn’t come after me directly. Anyone else would have been dead a long time ago. But I managed to survive…to this day…and even though I lost the belt, I still gained something more.” “Well it’s nice to see family within the business. Akiyama with his two daughters, and Cyclonica with her mother…it’s touching.” Yuri said with a sappy smile. All four of them gave her a curt glare. Yuri then cleared her throat and got back to the interview. “Anyway, Cyclonica, I know you’re aware of the countless harassment and persecution that the people of Japan have been placing upon you and Akiyama since you arrived in Japan. There are countless people protesting in front of every arena you go to. What do you think about that?” “The mortals have a right to be angry. I mean, as soon as me and father arrived, we started a rampage in downtown Tokyo. Lot of humans got sacrificed that afternoon…yes, indeed.” Akiyama nods slowly, thinking back to that day as he rubbed his chin. Cyclonica pulled the Cruiserweight title up on her shoulder and continued. “But they also shun us for something that happened last year…and since then we have accomplished so much more to outshine that dishonor. But they’re stubborn people…they cannot see that change is not only upon them…but also upon PCW as well as the rest of the world. The time draws nearer where Akiyama will lead the Celestial collective up and proclaim his throne above all who date to oppose him. Clyde had better sleep with both eyes open…because when he closes them…that’s when the Darkness…will overtake him.” “Speaking of Celestial Collective, are you aware of the cult CODE, the Celestial Order of Divine Existence, that has been known for terrorist attacks throughout Japan in the name of the Neo Void God?” Cycloncia nods. “We’ve heard about it recently, and though we’re not involved with it first hand, we plan on seeing what they’re all about. If it’s just a bunch of crazy nVg marked out efedding computer jockeys, then I smell banishment in their future. But if it is an affiliation of followers with a sincere dedication to serve Akiyama, then we will have to decide on what we do with them. But don’t think that this will turn into some religion for Akiyama to compete with that cheesy Scientology crap Xavier Kannon spouts out. If we pick this up, it’ll be the real thing, all the way through.” “Thank you all.” Yuri says, standing up and bowing. Cyclonica waves her hand at her, nodding her head. With all that said, we can only expect a lot out of the family ties between Akiyama, Cylonica, and Anarchy when Turmoil and Fusion come around.
