Sylo Firing All Missiles?

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sylo Firing All Missiles? Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 May 2002 ---- Today in a local air port in Germany, a camera crew was filming a commercial for the air line. They were surpised however when a very large man, identified as Sylo of the PCW flew into a rage. He broke windows, seats, and flipped shuttle cars. Reports are that damage is estimated well into the thousands. But in light of all of this he did manage to save a small child who came close to being crushed to death. As he was leaving a small boy cried out for help. The massive Barbarian known as Sylo, not known for kind deeds, moved over to the backage car he was trapped under. Video tape shows Sylo lifting the car off of the child saving him. Medical officals say if the boy had stayed half an hour longer he would have died. German's now consider Sylo a local hero and are trying thier best to get him back to the states for his match against Frisco on next week's Fusion and for the Up coming Pay-Per-View match against Akira Akiyama. More on this situation will be brought to you as it comes to our attention.
