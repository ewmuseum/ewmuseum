Jaden/Crichton News

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jaden/Crichton News Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Aug 2002 ---- News has it that for the past couple of days Jaden has been carrying out massive number of bags from the "Be Like Osoma Memorabilia Store." Day in and day out she has been there. A few night around 11:00 PM she has been seen standing outside of her old residence, John's Crichton's hotel room with some sort of piece of paper. Police have asked her to move a couple of times for loitering. "Yea, she has been acting kind of crazy lately," the policeman says, scratching the dandruff out of his head. "I heard some loud crashes a few nights ago around 10:45 PM. I thought that Jaden and John were at each others throats again! I don't know what happened up there." "If I was John I would have kicked her ass...Oh sorry can I say ass?" The other policeman asks "Yes, you can say ass," Tiffany, the reporter says, trying to get the inside scoop on Jaden. Moving the microphone closer to the officers mouth. "...kicked her ass out along time ago! We will do our best to protect Mr. Crichton; however, we know we will not always be there to save him. But, I do believe we will be getting another call saying we have to come back to Crichton residence either to (A) Pick up somebody's body (B) They are arguing again and it's getting out of hand or (C) Someone is leaving with us in handcuffs!" "That's some interesting thoughts there; well thank you!" Tiffany says.
