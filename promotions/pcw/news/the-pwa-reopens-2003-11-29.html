The PWA Reopens

29 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We're pleased to announce that the PWA has reopened its doors for business, effective immediately," said Primetime Championship Wrestling spokesperson, Mark Malloy. "The Academy has a lot of history, and is forever linked to PCW." The PWA, has bred a long list of wrestlers, who went on to win various accolades upon their graduation. Graduates include former PCW World Heavyweight Champions Xavier Kannon, Jason Snow, The End and Corey Williams, current OSF champion Inphino Blitz, former OSW World Champions Tom Walczak and Six Finger Cisco, and former NTICW Champion, Semore Smoke. Other prominent graduates include fWo superstar Tim Walden, The White Mexican, tA's Sylo, Hessian, RPW owner Rush Mason, and Rent-A-Hero. The academy has also seen top flight tag teams, such as, Civil Disobedience, the Golden Game and Pranks with Attitudes graduate from its program. Wrestlers looking to break into the idustry, as well as experienced wrestlers looking to hone their inring or mic skills, tweek their gimmicks are encouraged to stop by the PWA and inquire about enrolling.
