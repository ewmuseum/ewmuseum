The Dog House

13 September 2001

The Dog House Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Sep 2001 ---- Hello, and welcome to the Dog House, brought to you by the one and only Prince of the PCW, the Hound. I’ll be sniffing through all the worthwhile PCW gossip and giving you all something to read that’s actually interesting. There’s a lot going on in PCW right now, with the Global Tournament, tension building between the big stables, and Back to the Old School right around the corner. Not to mention, the coming of age of a lot of younger talent, and the way the veterans are sort of at a crossroads in their PCW careers. Lets get going. Back to the Old School, Previews and Predictions Just my opinions. If you have your own, keep them. *Rising Star Title Match* This one should be interesting, as I see a few of these guys could real prospects for PCW. Rent-a-Hero made a lot of waves upon coming into PCW. Everyone’s giving him props. And then there’s Tim Walden, who I must say is THE MAN. He stuck it out in PWA forever and is now finally getting his shot. But my pick for this match is none other than Mr. UK. I got a chance to take a look at his recent work, and I was impressed. He’s got talent, and a solid workrate to go along with it. Pick of the Litter: Mr. UK *Miyagawa vs. Suzahara* I’ve heard a lot of talk about Suzahara being over rated. At the same time, I hear a lot about him being under rated. Personally, I lean toward under rated. But keep in mind, he’s not exactly in the PCW penthouse. Miyagawa on the other hand, looks rejuvinated. I used to read Miyagawa a lot, and when he’s on, he’s a personal favorite of mine. Pick of the Litter: Bring on the goggles! Miyagawa *Cruiserweight Title Match* Can we please stop putting Kid Wonder into these things? He’s won the belt four times now, it’s time to move on. Jake Keeton’s making his return, but from what I’ve been hearing around the locker room, that’s not exactly going to shake the ground of PCW. Dustin Downey has enough talent to be a major player in PCW (Continental contender for sure. World’s possible, but a stretch), but his work rate holds him down. It’ll hold him down in this match as well. White Mexican can be strong at times, and appears to be going hard for this match . The decision’s tough. It came down to three people. The HIT, Chet King, and Kid Wonder. If Wonder gets motivated, he’ll win. But my gut’s telling me to go with Chet King here. Pick of the Litter: Chet King *Extreme Title Match* Lets just look at the major players in this match. Jack Valentine’s been getting a lot of credit lately, but I don’t think he’s got the workrate to take this thing. Morozov’s got the workrate, but to be honest, I don’t think it would hurt him to slow the workrate a bit to raise the quality level. Workrate’s going to get used a lot here. If Badge can get his up, then he’ll have a really good shot. Genetic Perfection is over rated. Who does that leave? Ed. He should have won last time, and he’ll win this time. Pick of the Litter: Ed *Porter vs. Hollywood* I like Hollywood, but when Porter shows up there may not be anyone in PCW who can match his quality. Then again, who’s to say Porter’s going to show up? I’m going with the safe bet here. Pick of the Litter: Dan Hollywood *TV Pinata Match* Another match that comes down to workrate. Stryfe’s got a good workrate. Whether his work is quality or not seems to be a split decision in the locker room. In my view, Stryfe’s shown flashes of greatness, but still hasn’t put it together yet. The Multiple Man has a ton of talent (especially in humour), but he doesn’t show up enough for it to make a difference. Chris Chase is a solid pick in any match. He was the man in PCW2, but seems to have fallen through the cracks in PCW. Overall, it’s hard to bet against Snow with the roll he’s on. Good quality and a renewed work ethic make him the safest pick. Pick of the Litter: Snow *Tag Title Match* Simple rule. Don’t bet against Da Westsidaz in tag. Pick of the Litter: Da Westsidaz *Stairway to Hell* Why’s Jupiter there? The entire wrestling world wants to see Kreigman and Vampir finally go at it, one on one. But no, we have to let Jupiter water things down. Well, Vampir would beat Kreigman one on one, and he’s going to beat him with Jupiter there. Vampir’s arguably the best of the new batch of superstars. Look for him to capture the world championship folks, he’s just that good. Pick of the Litter: Vampir Nosferatu *TV Title Match* Who doesn’t mark out for Darkstar? Everyone loves to see this guy get success, and we all watched his TV title reign with glee. Unfortunately, it’s coming to an end. Big Alan Steele’s a big star in wrestling, and it won’t be long before he’s a big star in PCW. I don’t think Steele’s the best of the up and comers, but he could be sometime soon. You never know in PCW. Pick of the Litter: Big Alan Steele *Continental Title Match* Reverend’s great. PCW’s a better place now that he’s back. Kruger and Williams are both ok. But the choice here, for me at least, is clear. Boda. Boda is finally getting the recognition he deserves, but he’s still underrated! This is the man who stepped into the limelight in PCW2 and gave them a true Main Eventer. As good as Chris Chase is, everyone knows that Boda was the only man in PCW2 capable of carrying the Main Event scene. Boda’s going to keep his belt. Pick of the Litter: Boda *Jeremy Howard vs. Tyler Nelson* This should be the Main Event. The tension between these two is high (although it could have been higher), but I’m just looking forward to the war they wage in the ring. We know what to expect from Jeremy Howard. He’s going to take it to Nelson and give everything he has. And believe me, Jeremy Howard has a lot to give. He’s arguably the biggest name in wrestling right now, and deservedly so. Tyler Nelson has a lot to prove. But does he have the motivation to prove it? If Nelson were at the top of this game, this match would be a classic from build up to pin. But I’m worried Nelson will be a no-show. Pick of the Litter: Jeremy Howard *Main Event* Chef is a superstar, no questions asked. In fact, there may only be one man in all of PCW (two, if you count Jeremy Howard) who deserves the PCW World Title more than he does. But that man is also in the ring. And his name is Ivan Stanislav. How many times now has Ivan been just inches away from winning the title? Not this time. I can’t see anything stopping the big Russian. TCM won’t even be a factor. Pick of the Litter: Ivan Stanislav Ok, I know that was a test of endurance, but lets continue on with some brief words on The Global Championship Tournament You know, I once thought this tournament was going to flop. But once again, the big boys at PCW have surprised me with just how big they can make their events. I’m glad to see some DWF guys sign up, especially Doozer. I was worried that DWF was going to exclude themselves from PTC activities, but I’m happy with the DWF turn out. I’ve heard things about Doozer for a long time now. Many people have said he’s the best in all of wrestling. *Who I’m Glad is in the Tournament* 1.) Ivan Stanislav - Probably the biggest name in PCW. But that’s not why I have him at #1. The reason is simple. Until Ivan Stanislav signed up, you could count the big names on one hand. Once Ivan signed up, everyone and their mother wanted in. Well, maybe it was just Rev and Porter, but those were other guys I wanted to see in this. 2.) Howard Porter - Maybe the best promo man in wrestling today. With a three promo limit, Porter could kill in this thing. 3.)Doozer - Maybe he should be higher. He’s got such a reputation! I can’t wait to see him dismantle people! *Who I wish were there* 1.) TCM - He’s the PCW champ. Sure, he won’t be after the PPV, but he should still be there. 2.) Jeremy Howard - He’s one of the best in the world today. If he’d joined this tournament, I would have admitted that whoever does win it is the best wrestler in all PTC. Now, there’s a bit of a doubt. *Picks to Click* (Sleepers who’ll make a name for themselves) 1.) Vampir Nosferatu - If he puts his best three up, they can rival anyone elses. 2.) Snow - See Vampir Nosferatu. 3.) TEE Kay - I checked out some of his stuff, and the guy really does have some talent. If he steps it up for the increased level of competition, watch out! 4.) Mr. UK - This guy can cut a good promo. I think he’s a little underrated, but here’s a chance to show off. *Picks to Suck* 1.) Duzza - Poor Duzza will lose all the credability that he’d just gained by losing out to the first real competition he faces. 2.) Anyone outside of PCW and DWF who have to take on Ivan or Porter. Before I go, I have two weekly awards to give out. Unsung Hero Boda. Plain and simple. Sure, he’s getting some credit. I mean, he is the continental champ after all. But that seems like a consolation prize for not getting a single fair shot at the world title after winning a match declaring himself the number one contender for it. Boda’s got all the talent in the world, and hopefully it will continue to be recognized more and more. In the Doghouse Trash Can Man. He won the title, and he disappeared. How many times have we seen this in PCW champions? Does the name Alan Kreigman ring a bell? Our answer to him was Stryfe. Luckily, Jeremy Howard gave us a brief glimpse of what a champ should be. Our first in a long time. But TCM’s the current champ. That should come with the obligation of setting the pace for the other wrestlers. Well, thanks for reading! - The Hound
