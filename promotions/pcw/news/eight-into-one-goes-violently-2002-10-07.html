Eight Into One Goes Violently

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eight Into One Goes Violently Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Oct 2002 ---- Tokyo, Japan- 4 weeks ago when PCW Champion Xavier Kannon declared that he would fight twice in one night, just like he did when he won the belt, the Scientologist couldn’t have thought for a second what brutality was in store for not only himself, but the seven other men vying for his Title. All eight gave it there all and the four initial winners needed every second they had to recover before the Main Event when the last four where whittled down to one... The PCW World Champion. As well as the World Title hunt, Rent-a-Hero put his Continental Title on the line after a tribute to a friend but ended up facing the same fate that overcame him... Pranks With Attitude find themselves at the mercy of a spiteful Ellie~!, Anarchy and Cyclonica fight for the Cruiserweight belt but even this great prize paled into insignificance after the events post-match. As if all that wasn’t enough, Syko the Goblin still had both his TV Title and the fair Bertha to defend ahead of his surprise, and how would Red, White and Blue help or hinder Bernard Colt? Then there was the small matter of PCW’s pride as they set foot onto PURE’s turf to for battle in a 10 man tag where Kannon revealed the surprise up his sleeve... Something equally matched by PURE. To see how all this went down and even more matches to boot inside the giant Tokyo Dome, head to the drop-down menu and chose Pay Per View.
