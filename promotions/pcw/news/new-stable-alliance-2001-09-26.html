New Stable Alliance?

26 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Stable Alliance? Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Sep 2001 ---- Could Dark Ages and The Red Army be joining forces? According to Gus Thomas, a vagrant living on the streets of Hell's Kitchen in New York, apparently so. "I saw that burned up lookin' guy and that evil little girl talking with that Russian spy guy from that there wrestling show I see on the TV in the shop window across from my cardboard box," said Thomas. "They talked for five minutes or so, then the little Russian guy handed the girl some kind of briefcase." While most observers dismissed Thomas' comments as just rants from a homeless drunk, one wrestling insider commented it is not that far fetched. "Considering Vampir Nosferatu is a Cuban national, and Ivan has shown interest in the Vamp's background in the past, I think its a feasible situation," said our source, who asked to remain anonymous. We'll bring you more on this story, should there prove to be any substance to it.
