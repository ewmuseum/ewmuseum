It's The Holiday Season

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's The Holiday Season Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Dec 2002 ---- Jefferson, New Hampshire-- When Gaz Green and Nerezza pranked PCW Owner Xavier Kannon a couple of Fusion's ago, not a single person expected that they were going to have their Tag Team titles stripped off of them, exactly on the date in which they tied the PCW record. Of course, being the known con artist and smart alecks the Front Office happens to be, they devised the perfect plan for the titles to be held up. A Carnival of Carnage match on Christmas Eve. Although the broadcast might be two days late thanks to a snowstorm rolling through the Northeast United States, be well warned that the match does not dissappoint for there were deaths, there were Dark Gimmick appearences and murders to go along with him, along with the ever now complimented destruction and Train Ride of Death - "Harcore at 90 mph" to lead in which two new champions, although not either one exactly knew what the other was thinking. Not to mention, one man actually becoming a two time champion under the Lord of The Rings spell, and only holding his "precious" for nine seconds.
