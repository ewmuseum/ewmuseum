Klaymore Injury Update

4 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Late Sunday night Kevin Klaymore returned to the doctor due to some swelling in his knee. The swelling was due to pressure buildup and strain. Dr. Rahim Ackbar Mohammad sent Kevin in to drain the knee. However something went drastically wrong when his his leg muscles became too tight and were as hard as a rock. "Nothing will stop his rise and I believe that this accident will aid him in future matches," said Mohammed. "Also I would like to comment on Kevin's medical condition. I cleared him early Sunday morning when his heart and determination which, in my eyes, led him to an unbelievable speedy recovery with little to no pain. I feel that Kevin will be a great asset in the future. I have never seen any superstar yet to have his 'Lionheart' and determination." Officials at PCW have agreed to let Kevin Klaymore compete however if he should have any serious injury they feel that he should take a vacation where he can heal. If any new information arises we will inform you as soon as possible.
