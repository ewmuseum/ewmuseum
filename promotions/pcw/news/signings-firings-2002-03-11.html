Signings & Firings

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Signings & Firings Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Mar 2002 ---- PCW spokesman Pimply Faced Intern Kid made the following announcements Monday morning. - Damion Xaviers and Ripper Ryckman have both asked for and recieved their releases. PCW wishes both men luck in future endeavors. - Afwa Kilmanjaro has been released from hi contract due to what was termed as a "pisspoor workrate". "If Afwa can get his stuff together, we'll welcome him back," stated PFIK. "He's got talent. He just doesn't have his head on straight." - PFIK has indeed confirmed that former Cruiserweight champion. Kid Wonder has left the company. "I guess the pressure finally got to him," bluntly said Pimply Faced Intern Kid. "The pressure finally became too much and he snapped." When asked if they felt he'd surface up in a rival promotion, PFIK said, "I doubt it. From the way he sounded, he sounded like he was tired of the whole industry. The chances of him popping up elsewhere are slim. And even then it'd be a while before he did." - On a positive note, PCW officials confirmed the signings of four tag teams from NTICW. Misplaced, Cowabunga, Fear Factory, and Samburu. "They are all very taented teams," said PFIK. "They should be more than enough to help revive our tag division." - PCW officials are currently in talks with former GWF superstar Ben J. Pailthorpe, but that's about it.
