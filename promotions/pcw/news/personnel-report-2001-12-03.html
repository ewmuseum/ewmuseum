Personnel Report

3 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Personnel Director, Jane Reagan, held a press conference Monday morning to give the media a run down of the full PCW's personnel moves. -Reagan confirmed both Jay Phoenix and Phelen Kell had been released, calling the releases both "shocking and disturbing. -She said the future of Kory Storm is in doubt. -Reagan confirmed the company had indeed resigned Kya Brady and Brandi Wilson, as well as signing Sassy. She indicated that the attitude of Courtney Z had prompted the company not to offer her a long term deal. -Reagan confirmed reports PCW is close to signing a couple of "big name" free agents. -She indicated the company would be looking "6-8 bodies" over the next few days, to cover holiday vacations for other wrestlers. Reagan promised another report later in the week. We will bring you more news on the comings and goings of PCW superstars as it becomes availability.
