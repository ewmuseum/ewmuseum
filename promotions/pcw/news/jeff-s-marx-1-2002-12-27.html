Jeff's Marx #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Dec 2002 ---- Ghar, I thought my job was just to rant a lot, bitchslap Dave Kern into place and solidify the face/heel boundaries with bias commentating... But, turns out I’m actually meant to do stuff behind the scenes other than 4 hours of yelling and Brandi-marking each week so have to do this stupid thing. Who’s idea was it to have a commentator do a web column anyway? BAH~! So anyway, as I don’t exactly want Duke to slash my wages I shall RELUCTANTLY do this shit, so I hereby introduce you to the 1st Edition of JEFF’S MARX!!! Which is meant to be a funny pun of Jeff’s Marks which still doesn’t make sense as it could be people who mark for me, or people I mark for... And the latter is only Brandi so that’s a short column. Enough of that then.... Here we go. The following are not the opinions of Jeff Marx, as I am simply a puppet reading out the opinions of Duke Williams and the Front Office, with all the swearing and arguing deleted out and edited by officials... Wait, that’s all the fun stuff... GHAR! PCW officials are pleased with Clyde’s run as Champion. After seemingly winning the Title from nowhere at Red October, his two straight PPV wins against El Diablo has solidified his reign and made him only the second World Champion this year, following Xavier Kannon to successfully defend at a PPV. With DarkStar now back and by his side, Clyde looks like he can challenge Big Alan Steele’s 85 day Title reign. The huge Adrenaline Main Event on Sunday where the #1 Contender for Clyde’s belt will be named is expected to shape the Shanghaied 3 Main Event, with all 3 of the combatants having their own reasons to want the shot, especially Snow given he won the belt for the 1st time from Jeremy Howard at Shanghaied 2. After losing to Clyde again, El Diablo is reported to be considering his options, and is not happy about once again losing a match in controversial fashion. He is not yet being booked, and it has been rumoured he’s contemplating management and leading some new ‘un-corrupt’ talent into PCW. Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams haven’t talked out of PCW shows for about a month now, and it can only be a matter of time before their rivalry begins to effect PCW business, with zero communication between it’s two leading men. On the same topic, due to meddling by Duke Williams, the PCW Hubbardville Offices have been put on a permanent hold, reportedly costing the company close to the region of $9 Million, officials are very concerned at the high stakes the duo seem to be gambling with. On a similar topic, discrepancies have been found between the PCW accounts and the figures given for Front Office taxation. Tim Walden s claming it as an accounting error, but if not a large sum seems to be unaccounted for. Totally Tubular Tommy has impressed greatly since his singles run, and his Continental Title win was well received. The entire division as a whole has been boosted in the last month, with the 6 who fought at Decimation all being lined up for future pushes. The TV Title looks to be losing none of the prestige that Venneman Pendragon and Syko brought to it. After the disastrous John Crichton reign, a string of strong Champions has brought the belt back up, and 2 female Champions in the last 2 weeks is also a good indicator of the talent PCW’s females have. The prospect of a Brandi v Tomas feud has many excited, and could be a rivalry to inspire more females into PCW. After a seemingly strong division just a month or so ago, showcased in the giant “No Ladder” match, the Tag Team division has been given a serious face lift. With the new Champions crowned at the Carnival of Carnage, a division made up of singles alliances and run within the PCW solo scene looks to be the right path, meaning to prospect of singles feuds being fought for both single and tag titles is likely. Both of Pranks With Attitude are expected to make good progress following their break-up last Friday. With Gaz having the Front Office behind him, and Nerezza likely to gain the backing of Duke, the pair look set to rocket up the card while they battle each other. The Extreme Title may be in need of a shake-up much like the Tag Titles, with the possibility of it being a Title to encompass all divisions, with bi-weekly clusters to decide the Champion with the likes of TTT and Chet King fighting Pete! and Dark Gimmick. A similar idea may be incorporated for the Cruiserweight Title, where those higher in the card such as El Diablo, Xavier Kannon and Rent-a-Hero may be fighting for the strap alongside it’s current challengers. Of the newcomers, PCW staff are said to be pleased with the effort off camera that Josh Starwind is putting in, and hope when matched with participation in the shows and a good feud, that he can start to work his way up the PCW ladder and capitalise on his Tag Title win. Although some are concerned with the likeness of his character to several fictional ones, as his character develops these are expected to be ironed out. With the arrival of Jackson Rust, a natural trio of Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor and Rust himself is expected to develop, with all 3 being looked on as solid investments worth any push given to them. After their loans to PURE, the Black Dragons, Eddie Mandrake and BladeD are expected to be monitored closely, with any improvement being looked on favourably. Talent looked on as wandering at the moment consists of Jamie Celt and Bob Vurdo. Both are seen as talented singles performers, but so far have yet to grab a foothold in PCW to build from, which officials will hope to see in the future. Kai has been causing concern with rumours abound that some of his material seems to be taken from other sources without permission, a decision on this will be taken soon. Although the roster is at it’s largest for a while, scout’s eyes are always open for interesting characters, especially females that PCW hopes can follow in the footsteps of Tomas, Brandi and Tessa and find a place in the PCW mainstream. So far, PCW’s official singer/songwriter Heather Nova has been happy to attend PCW fan conventions to both play and sign autographs, however is worried about rumours that Flaymin Aymin, on the rebound from the late Michelle Branch may be after her with romantic intentions. Decimation turned out to be a great PPV, with match quality bettering the year’s previous efforts which is certainly saying something. With a chain of great PPV’s from Wrestle Vessel to Decimation having formed since the much maligned roster firing, a lot of hype is already beginning for Shanghaied, where if the feuds running currently are to be paid-of, could easily match the previous two. Well, that’s it from me.... Jeff Marx has finally been reduced to a generic sell-out reading from cards, now I know how Clay feels. Anyway, I’m off to see if anyone topped my bid for half naked Ellie~! pics of e-bay. Die in your sleeps. - Jeff (Still not getting the column’s name.... And why can’t Dave to this?).
