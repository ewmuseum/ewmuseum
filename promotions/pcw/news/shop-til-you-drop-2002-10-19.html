Shop 'Til You Drop

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shop 'Til You Drop Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Oct 2002 ---- Grandberry Mall, Tokyo, Japan- In PCW, tradition tends to go hand in hand with violence and the Mall Brawl is no exception. Postponed from Black Friday so PCW could bring this annual event to the land of the rising sun, the wait was more than worth it as over 50 PCW stars... And a few names from the past did battle for Duke Williams’... Or Dukestar the Goblin as he is now calling himself.... Continental Title. A stipulation originally set by CEO Xavier Kannon to get the Title off Rent-a-Hero, it is now his own stablemate in the Front Office who must fight through the whole fed to retain his belt.... What does Kannon have in store to help the PCW President? And who will he side with or hide behind to do it? Also in the match, 2 PCW stars get a Hummer, and not in the good way, and Rush Mason shows he’s proud to be a well built Black man who still plays with dolls. While Ibe Black shows that Japanese music is truly HELL!!! To see this all unfold and more, head down to the Fusion banner and click “Results”.
