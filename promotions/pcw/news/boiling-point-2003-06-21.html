Boiling Point

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boiling Point Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Jun 2003 ---- Bogota, Colombia- The night's events came to a fitting conclusion in the main event. Most of the vendettas that have been boiling, with no escape inside the airship, boiled over at the end of the night, leaving two opponents standing in the ring, with everyone else left to their own violent devices around the ring. The strange thing was that the match began as a straight up singles experience, but evolved quickly from there. In addition, a coalition made a plan to retrieve two very important children, Dorn flaunted his new status, Abner got a lot closer with his mystery assailant, and Stone Burns continued to be peeved with one Ms. Debris.
