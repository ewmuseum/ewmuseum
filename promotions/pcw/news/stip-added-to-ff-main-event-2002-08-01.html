Stip Added to FF Main Event. Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Aug 2002 ---- With Xavier Kannon threatening to tarnish the PCW World Title on OSW TV this weekend if CEO Tyler Nelson didn’t give into his demands which included immunity for him and his entourage, and that his wife become Commissioner, Da Boss~! was in a sticky situation. But after warning Kannon that he would only be protected for the next 2 days, he would have to fight for the latter... Thus the match between the 2 PCW megapowers on Fusion will be for the Commissionership of PCW. If Xavier Kannon wins, the position will go to Ellie, and if Tyler wins he is free to do as he sees with the front office job. But with Kannon’s immunity running out after the match, there is no telling what the Scientologist will do to receive preferential booking in the coming weeks.
