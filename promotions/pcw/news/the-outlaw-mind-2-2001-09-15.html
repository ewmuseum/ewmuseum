The Outlaw Mind #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Sep 2001 ---- Ah, another edition of the Outlaw Mind. I'd put up a disclaimer, but if you don't like what I have to say, then who cares. It's my opinion and thats what matters. If you have a problem with that, then feel free to post on any of the boards, earning you a spot in my next column riddled with insults. Now, with that stuff out of the way. Has any noticed a few big names have yet to really show up lately. I for one am a fan of Trashcan Man, but he's bailed on us as Champ. Hell, I'll go out on a limb, and say that most of the members of the Old School have bailed on us. Some are sticking around, and showing effort, but some of the others really are putting a black eye on the group. Another guy I miss being around, is Howard Porter. Howard tried so hard for the World title, but fell short. Since then, Porter has been missing. Sad, the man has a true talent that I don't want to see wasted. I think if he'd just some effort, he'd go places here. Nevertheless, he's MIA. One of my questions has been answered. Brandi and Oracle will fight at next Tuesday's Turmoil. I, for one, can't wait. I really want to know who has what it takes. My prediction: Neither, they'll be caught in bed with me AT THE SAME TIME! Now, onto the Back to Old School PPV. I have some predictions for this PPV, and I'd like to see if they come true. So, here goes........ PCW Extreme Title Match Escape from Old School Match Winner must escape an abandoned high school to secure victory>/b> Ed vs Jack Valentine vs Flaymin Aymin vs Guy Leslie vs Clyde vs Tom Walczak vs Nikita Morozov vs Jon Kano vs Kramer Wildwing vs Jeremy Johnson vs Hank vs Badge vs Genetic Perfection Johnny Smith I think this match comes down to only five men, and thats pushing it. Ed, Clyde, Tom Walczak, Nikita Morozov and Badge. Other than that, no one else is even worth mentioning. I'd have to say Morozov. If he keeps up his workrate, the could be going higher. PCW Rising Star Title Match Old Fashioned, Over the Top Battle Royal Entrance order determined at random Tony Rolo vs Rent A Hero vs Sam Potright vs Nuke vs Tim Walden vs Mr. UK vs Jason McAllister vs Alexander Popov vs The Green Mofo vs Akiyama vs Brandi Aren't most of these morons fired? Thought so. With that said, Rent A Hero should have no problems hanging onto the title here. Either that, or he's moving up, and they want a someone to hold that prestigious title belt. Triple Threat Stairway to Hell Match Winner is name #1 Contender for the PCW Continental Title John Jupiter vs Alan Kriegman vs Vampir Nosferatu Kriegman is no showing so far. Jupiter is no showing so far. Vampir is blazing away. Need I say more? Nosferatu should get the elevation he needs with a big win here over two no shows! Grudge Match Gimmick to be announced on Turmoil Miyagawa vs Kensuke Suzahara Kensuke who? I think the goggled eye supremo will take this grudge! PCW Cruiserweight Title Match Table Match White Mexican vs Deadly Dustin Downey vs Chet King vs Kid Wonder vs The HIT vs Jake Keeton This should be a good one. Can Kid Wonder win it for the fifth time? Maybe so, if he shows up for this match and doesn't choke. Returning Chet King and Jake Keeton are looking to make impacts. Mexican, Downey and HIT are looking for some recognition. All six of these men could take it. I think HIT has this one. He's been on a tear lately, it won't stop at Back to Old School. #1 Contender's Match for the PCW TV Title TV Piñata Match Stryfe vs Chris Chase vs THE Multiple Man vs Snow Snow. Nuff said. Stryfe if falling off. He had it for a bit, but he's just flapping in the wind. TMM is too unpredictable. One match he's all over it. The next, he's dead. Chase is an up and comer, but no match for Snow. PCW Television Title Match Briar Patch Match Big Alan Steele vs Darkstar BAS. Darkstar has too many demons in him right now to really focus. Commissionership Match Loser must work as winner's personal assistant for a month "The Scholar" Howard Porter vs Dan Hollywood With Greed Inc. on Dan's side, and no one on Porter's, the Commissionship is going Hollywood. Let's just hope that Porter shows up, so he's not jobbed, then fired. PCW Tag Team Titles Da Westsidaz vs Hans Kruger and Mr. UK Da Westsidaz. Boda added life to a team on life support. The plug is pulled in this match. PCW Continental Title Match Stretcher Elimination Match Boda vs The Reverend Jon Hull vs Hans Kruger vs Duke Williams This is a tough one. Boda has life right now. About the only one on the Euro Union. Kruger really has no chance. Reverend might, if he shows up. This match comes down to Duke Williams and Boda, I think. In the end, I think Boda might squeak out with the strap here. Submission Match Winner is named #1 Contender for the PCW World Title and CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson vs Jeremy Howard If Tyler shows up, this one will be great. If not, Howard is taking Tyler's life away from him. Main Event PCW World Heavyweight Title Match Triple Threat Lumberjack Match< w/Old School, The Red Army and Euro Union as Lumberjacks Trashcan Man vs Ivan Stanislav vs The Chef And this one, TCM has no showed so far, so his reign as Champ is fading fast. Chef and Ivan have been going neck and neck here. I see the sentimental favorite Chef walking out, due to an interference on Ivan, somehow. Those are my predictions, like them, live them, love them. That's all this time. Tune in next time to THE OUTLAW MIND!
