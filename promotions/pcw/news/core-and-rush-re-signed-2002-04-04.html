Core and Rush re-signed

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Core and Rush re-signed Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Apr 2002 ---- As you witnessed on the latest edition of Turmoil, Jason Core and 'The Rush' Rush Mason have both been re-signed to PCW by Doctor Rupert Healey. Duke Williams held the doctor at gunpoint and demanded the signings, which the good Doctor had no choice to agree to. The terms of the deals were not disclosed, but Doctor Healey did release this brief statement: "Yes, it is true that Jason Core and 'The Rush' have been re-signed to PCW contracts. Fortunately for PCW I was able to negotiate the terms of the deals to greatly benefit the financial stability of this company." As for the other member of The Malice Crew, Legend INC, he remains out of a job do to his loss on Turmoil this past week. Legend INC could not be reached for comment, as he seems to have been unavailable for comment for about 2 weeks now. We will keep you posted on this continually developing story.
