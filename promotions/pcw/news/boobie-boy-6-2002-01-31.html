Boobie Boy #6 Posted by Sandy Wong on 31 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Issue # 6 Featuring THE PHANTOM BOOKER~! Welcome everyone to issue number six of 20 Questions. I went into this interview prepared at all angles, but nothing could have prepared me for The Phantom Booker. Despite his power and busy schedule he managed to take some time and answer my questions about PTC, PCW, and some select superstars who he feels will benefit greatly. So scroll down and get reading! Oh yeah, on a side note vote for my column 20 Questions with Boobie Boy as the Official Pre-Malice Magazine 1) PTC seems to be back up and running. What are your comments on that? Favorites, Dislikes? TPB: I agree. The site traffic is through the roof, participation is high, and the fed heads are posting plenty of news. The dislikes are the site could use a bit more content on the resource side, but that is easy to fix. Who is/was/were your favorite all time wrestler(s) of PCW? TPB: Ivan Stanislav, Clyde or Doug Troy. Whenever somebody attempts to destroy PCW you always seem to manage to crush him or her. Most recently with Jax and well you know. What are your comments on that situation? TPB: I have no comments on that situation. Matt: Not even trash talk? TPB: I don’t want Fedwars mentioned in this interview… Who do you feel was the biggest disappointment to ever work for PCW? TPB: Johnny Raindance, Judas Steele and Mike Marvelous Nelson is no longer president. His reign of terror is possibly on the way out. How do you think Duke Williams will do as President? TPB: Nelson is still the CEO, so his reign of terror is still very much intact. Duke will do his best to counterweight it, but in the end, can it be enough? Plus, who knows what Jane Reagan has done to the company...? Speaking of Jane Reagan, your wife, what do you feel on her situation of stealing PCW stars to move to OSW? TPB: Nelson deserves it, so more power to her. What do you do in your free time, watch movies, video games, dance? TPB: This is what I do with my free time mostly. What do you feel will be the most successful PTC fed? (not counting the big two) TPB: GWF right now, by a mile. Talent is good, active and enthusiastic. Those are the keys to running a good E-fed. The second Global Title Tourney is going to take place in a few weeks. Who do you believe should be watched closely? Any one that could give the Vamp a run for his money? TPB: I like Corg from OSW a lot. Kensuke Suzahara is always dangerous. Miyagawa is in there. Shawn Ryan is NTICW is a name to watch. WWA's Angie has great talent, and Kid Krazie from BMW is a worker. What were you like when you were younger? Anything you wish you did but didn’t? TPB: No, I have no regrets. I've had a good life. Being the Phantom Booker, you must get meaning less harassment from random people all day. What is one story of a guy who got underneath your skin? TPB: There was this annoying kid, who couldn't spell and possessed terrible grammar who kept bugging be about posting columns for him.... Shall I continue? Matt: Not necessary… At Shang-hied, Snow pulled a huge upset by defeating JKH. Despite Snow only having the title for 5 days, who do you believe will upend Snow for the title? If not, how long do you think he will hold the title for? TPB: I don't think it was a huge upset. Infact, I had little doubt Snow would defeat Jeremy for the title. It's Snow's time right now. I think we will see the return of a great PCW champion, who will take the title from Snow. Did you ever imagine from day one what PCW would grow into? TPB: I knew we would succeed but not this level... If you were on a desert island and could only bring one thing, what would it be? TPB: A boat Matt: Why? TPB: So I could leave the island, dumbass. What are your favorites? (Movies, food, sports teams, etc) TPB: Lonesome Dove, Brazilian BBQ, and any team from Philadelphia 16) Moving up the ranks. Who, right now, is in the low card but will some day (if they keep up their work) make it to the world title ranks? TPB: Young Tiger, Xavier Kannon, and Six-Finger Cisco What do you believe is the weakest division in PCW, and why? TPB: The Tag Team division is wasteland right now because there is no talent there. Matt: Only cause I am not there. TPB: You suck, so it would just further lend to the lowliness of the division Matt: Ouch What’s the deal with Hank? 2 million on a contract deal and winning the Hardcore title! TPB: He's a monster Would you care to mention anything about roster cuts or hiring or anything else that might catch the average person’s eye? Like perhaps who might be going to OSW? TPB: No, I would not. You will have to tune into Slam! like everyone else! Any chance of me getting hired as reporter or a referee or something in any of the PTC feds? PTC: We could work out you helping with PTC.... Just tell your mom to stop calling me and begging for it, OK? Matt: Deal! And before I ask the last question…its time for WORD ASSOCIATION. PCW World Title TPB: Meanstreak Duke Williams TPB: Makes PCW special PCW Columnists TPB: Robbie Decker PTC TPB: Smokin'! Rising Star Title TPB: Tyler Nelson And lastly do you have anything to conclude this interview? Comments about the column, PCW/PTC, or myself that was not covered. TPB: Sucks, sucks and you didn't ask enough about OSW, which simply shows how badly you suck. That wraps up another column with myself Boobie boy. Despite the verbal bashing given to me I would still like to thank The Phantom Booker for giving the time and effort to answer my 20 questions. I once again welcome all to contact me on AIM or email to setup and appointment with me. [detail withheld] and [detail withheld]. Coming up in my next issues will be Miya and Mexi. I PROMISE THIS TIME! Until then, I will leave on this note…I GOT A JOB AGAIN…well kinda! PEACE!
