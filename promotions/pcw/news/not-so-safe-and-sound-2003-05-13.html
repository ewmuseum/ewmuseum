Not So Safe And Sound

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not So Safe And Sound Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 May 2003 ---- After one bomb threat was made on the National Stadium early Monday morning, it was evacuated, but apparently, now stadium officials are being very cautious with whether they let the show proceed as planned or not. Al-Musaf Jahmed, some guy with a long beard and a turban who looked like he might know what he was talking about, had the following to say: "I'm not sure who's making these bomb threats or what they have to do with the PCW, but I'm sure there are people who remember the Russia/Afghanistan conflict. The fine American people helped us then and will bring joy to us now, so I can't say I agree with those crazy ass bitches." We didn't know he was so vulgar either, but hey, we're not too picky. VP-W, who wasn't on site in Kabul, but was able to contact us live via videophone, said that, "The show will most certainly go on, although security will be tighter than VP-S's mom's beautiful booty." As fears all around the world escalate, ring crew and security were beefed up on by PCW officials who were wary of any sort of small explosive being planted anywhere in the National Stadium. The origin of the bomb threat is also being looked into, as the hoax organizers are searched for.
