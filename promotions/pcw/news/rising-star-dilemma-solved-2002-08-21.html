Rising Star Dilemma Solved

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rising Star Dilemma Solved Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Aug 2002 ---- After the confusion created by the end of the 1st Rising Star tournament match, PCW Commissioner Eleanor Hall was forced into a quick makeshift press conference to solve the situation. “Uh..... Um... Er.... How many are there?” she asked before being told 4, “So... Um... Why don’t they just, like, fight each other?” With that comment, the match originally scheduled to be a singles match to the Rising Star Title is now a 4 Way Match between Scorpion, Eddie Mandrake, Semore Smoke and Blayze.
