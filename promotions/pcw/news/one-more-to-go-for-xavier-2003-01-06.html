One More To Go For Xavier

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One More To Go For Xavier Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jan 2003 ---- After winning the Tag Team Titles with his newest Front Office ally, Head of PCW Entertainment Gaz Green, PCW CEO Xavier Kannon now only needs the Television Title to complete his sweep of PCW belts. Xavier captured the Cruiserweight Title in only his 4th match against Juan “The Golden Toe” Borro, won the Continental Title from former mentor Jeremy Howard at Spring Fling, was handed the Extreme Title by Ellie~! who wanted to avoid fighting Reginald Lovecraft, defeated Frisco for the World Title and now has won a Tag strap in the Gimmick Potluck match. With the Rising Star Title now retired, only the TV Title awaits him. “You really think mere metal and leather means anything to a man of such enlightenment as I?” questioned the CEO. “That belt has Brandi’s name on it, and at Shanghaied she will finally win it back.” At the moment, Kannon seems un-interested in the sweep, but as time goes by will we see the Scientologist lay claims on the TV Title? We’ll have to wait and see.
