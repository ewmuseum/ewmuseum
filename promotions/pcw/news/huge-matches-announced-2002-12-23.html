Huge Matches Announced

23 December 2002

Huge Matches Announced Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Dec 2002 ---- Coming off the literally huge PPV that was Decimation, PCW appears to be keeping it’s momentum with some huge matches being announced for the coming week. On Tuesday there will be the 3rd Annual Carnival of Carnage, where the entire PCW and RUSH rosters who are cleared to fight, will battle for the PCW Tag Team Titles. Rumours are also flying around that some PCW stars not at the PPV will be returning, as well as a much talked about new signing. Fusion will see Brandi Wilson start her quest to regain the PCW TV Title as she fights Jon Kano, Flaymin Aymin and Mani Shigeru for the chance at a Title shot against the new Champion, Teresa Tomas. Also, the new Continental Champion, Totally Tubular Tommy will take on Chet King, the man who helped him at the end of the Treble Jeopardy match. Then, in the Main Event, it will be a GIANT 12 Man Tag Team Match as Xavier Kannon leads his PCW and RUSH team, against that of President Duke Williams. Adrenaline then gives us a follow-up match from Decimation where the Pharaoh will meet DarkStar for a shot at Clyde, only Snow has been added to the mix to make what is sure to be a classic. Also on the card, the #1 Contender for TTT’s Continental Title will be named as 6 challengers fight for the shot, with the Tubular One himself acting as referee. So, following the huge PPV, it seems PCW will ride the waves created for the coming week.
