Rising Star Title Situation Determined

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rising Star Title Situation Determined Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Apr 2003 ---- VP-S had this to say about the Rising Star Title situation following Dorn Tracer's vacating the title after crippling Jonathan Drachen. "We need to determine a champion for the division, but rather than just throw everyone at it right away, we've come up with a way to use the Rising Star division as a way to gauge people's work, make their time there mean something, but at the same time make it clear to them that they're not supposed to stay there." The Rising Star Division will be run as a Round Robin Tournament from now until Supremacy. In addition to bookings against some higher-ranked PCW stars, all of the Rising Star division wrestlers will be booked against each other. At the end of the 5-week period leading up to PCW Supremacy, the top four wrestlers in the Rising Star division will then face each other in a 4-way elimination match. The runner-up will get the Rising Star Title. The winner will get an automatic shot at the Extreme or Cruiserweight Title, whichever they choose (but not both). "To give extra incentive for doing well, the best performer walking into the Supremacy 4-way elimination match will be granted two eliminations. "From there, the Rising Star championship will be up for contention by order of an wrestler's win-loss record. Every wrestler still in the division will get a shot at the title. "As for the matter of how everyone else gets out of the division, that's simple. Either win 10 matches, or win the Rising Star Title and successfully defend it 5 times, whichever comes first. When that happens, they will be taken out of the Rising Star Division immediately and placed into the Extreme or Cruiserweight Divisions, whichever they prefer. However, automatic title shots will no longer be guaranteed upon internal promotion. "Well, that's how it will work, now I will list the wrestlers that are currently ranked in the new Rising Star Division." 1) Mark Makonie 2) The Overweighter 3) Young Tiger 4) Matt Jackson 5) Shane Wethers 6) Rich Cannon 7) Stone Burns 8) Sivasian Krikorian 9) SyN 10) Hanami "The Ahtumoth" Aneyuge 11) SOCIETY "All of these men are booked, either on Turmoil or Fusion, for this week. They are all advised to put out their best effort. PCW booking-staff will be watching closely."
