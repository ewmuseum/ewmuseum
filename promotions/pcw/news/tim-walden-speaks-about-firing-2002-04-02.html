Tim Walden Speaks About Firing

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tim Walden Speaks About Firing Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Apr 2002 ---- Ever since the firings that took place on PCW Sunday Sabotage, a buzz has been circulating throughout the wrestling world on the future status of those whose contracts were terminated. However, the buzz might not be any greater than that surrounding the future of Tim Walden. Wrestlingherald.com has recently come into information from a source close to Tim Walden, who has agreed to talk to us, under the strict confidence that he would remain anonymous and be bought a sandwich. Our source stated that Walden has not signed, but in fact shredded, burned, and then dissolved the contract offered him, and in fact is giving preferential treatment to offers made by several other federations. These offers reportedly include OSW, who are renowned for their signing away of key PCW talent before and would not skip a chance to gain an established star of PCW, NTICW, most likely due to Tim's past relationship with their owner and mutual disrespect for The White Mexican, and the thought-to-be-defunct RUSH Wrestling, for no apparent reason. All of these federations have something to be gained from the hiring of somebody with victories over main-event talent and as much recent television exposure from PCW's weekly shows as Walden has. However, a newly published interview with Tim by prowrestlingtoday.com touched upon the recent roster exodus, and through our in-depth reporting, we have gained a copy of the transcript of these questions, though heavily edited for public viewing. Bill Myers: Tim, the question that obviously looms over this whole interview is what your stance is regarding your recent firing. Tim Walden: (lengthy stream of profanities). I gave my heart and (expletive) soul to (expletive) working in PC-(expletive)-W and this (expletive) is how they (expletive) repay me? By taking my ALREADY (expletive) small contract and TRYING TO SHRINK IT (expletive) FURTHER?!? Well, you know what? (expletive) THIS (expletive) CONTRACT, (expletive) THAT (multiple expletives) DOCTOR (expletive) HEALEY, AND FINALLY, (loud expletive) PCW, BECAUSE I DON'T NEED THAT (expletive)! Bill: So you aren't going to resign with PCW for the lower contract offered? Tim: ARE YOU (expletive) KIDDING ME?!?!? I'M NOT SIGNING THEIR (expletive) CONTRACT FOR ANY LESS THAN A FIFTY-(expletive)-SEVEN PERCENT PAY RAISE! THOSE (multiple expletives) WEREN'T PAYING ME NEARLY ENOUGH FOR MY (expletive) WRESTLING SKILL AND MY CULT FOLLOWING AMONG THE (expletive)PCW FANS! FOR (multiple expletives), THIS NEW CONTRACT'S LESS THAN WHAT BIG-(expletive)-(excrement expletive adjective)-LICIOUS MAKES! AND IF YOU'RE LISTENING, (expletive) "DOCTOR" HEALEY, WHO'S (expletive) LUCKY THAT I DIDN'T (expletive) SNAP ALL OVER HIS (multiple expletives), I'M ALREADY LOOKING AT CONTRACT OFFERS FROM HERE TO (small stream of expletives) JAPAN!! NOW, CAN WE PLEASE CHANGE (expletive) TOPICS HERE BEFORE I GO (expletive) TAKE OUT MY RAGE ON SOME-(expletive)-THING? (lengthy stream of muttered profanities) Bill (uncomfortable): Err...all right, then..How's the Waldenbooks endorsement negotiations coming along? Obviously, Tim Walden's "SNAPPAGE", or uncontrollable fits of rage, have gotten the better of him in this situation, as it looks that unless the PCW bidding war is decided in time, Tim will likely be headed to a rival federation.. We advise you to stay tuned for more updates in the days to come.
