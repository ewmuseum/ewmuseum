The PFIK Meets #1: Darkstar

19 September 2001

The PFIK Meets #1: Darkstar Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Sep 2001 ---- PFIK Meets #1: Darkstar Greetings fans and welcome to a new series of interview columns, cut wittedly by your very own PFIK. Remember kiddies, get your PFIK dolls and t-shirts from PCW Shopzone at the usual places. (remember, cos I get a cut). Today as my guest I had the master of darkness, the lord of the undead... no that's Vampir Nosferatu. Oh yeah, I had the guy who jobbed to Big Alan Steele; Deathstar. Sorry, Darkstar. PFIK: What do you feel is your best achievement since joining PCW? DarkStar: Scaring the hell out of countless children in the crowd... well that and winning the Television title... but thats a gimmie isnt it? I also think its an accomplishment to never have lost to Meanstreak, Alan Kriegman, or Kid Wonder. PFIK: #2: What has been the low point of your PCW wrestling career? DarkStar: Jobbing to Revenant and having to wrestle Extremist constantly. Seriously, someone of my stature and ability should never have to wrestle someone who thinks 'poop' is the funniest word in the english language. And Revenant... well... is a explanation really needed friend? PFIK: Well it is of the opinion of PCW that Extremist sucks harder than PCW #1 Fan's Mom does at a Dave Kern lovefest. DarkStar: *darkstar shudders* I dont even want to think about that, let alone the word 'poop' used on PCW promo channels PFIK: #3: What title do you hold in highest esteem in PCW? DarkStar: ...you arent the brightest are you, I hold the World Title above all others, then the Television title, for I graced it with the prescence of the Dream Lord, only to have a bastard child of a god snatch it away from me... PFIK: Who, Hercules? I thought he was still dating Warlord? DarkStar: No, Big Alan Steele, and hes probably dating Cerebus, those three heads can come to some good use sometimes... PFIK: Moving on from Cerebus...#4: Who would you most like to fued with, and why? DarkStar: My ideal feud was Meanstreak, but now that hes dead... erm in a coma, it would have to be Ivan Stanislav, he is the mecca of unbeatabletality right now, after him I guess it would be Clyde, we know eachother so well it would be a brutal and disturbing feud PFIK: Leading on... #5: Who intimidates you in PCW? DarkStar: The Jerry Maguire Kid... have you ever seen him? The spikey blonde hair, the power of the word fuck... my god, he could be PCW Champion within a month if only he wasnt eight. PFIK: Kid Wonder is only twice his age plus a bit. DarkStar: Well Kid Wonder is also a high profile jobber, but you dont see me mocking him... oh wait, I just did. PFIK: #6: If life was like a box of chocolates, which one would you be? DarkStar: The one in the middle that always gets eaten last, although it doesnt taste any different. Either that or the coconut ones that noone likes, it really doesnt matter though, although Life... is like a box of chocolates. A cheap, thoughtless, perfunctoral gift that no one ever asks for. Unreturnable because all you get back is another box of chocolates. So, you're stuck with mostly undefinable whipped mint crap, mindlessly wolfed down when there's nothing else to eat while you're watching the game. Sure, once is a while you get a peanut butter cup or an English toffee but it's gone too fast and the taste is fleeting. In the end, you are left with nothing but broken bits filled with hardened jelly and teeth-shattering nuts, which, if you are desperate enough to eat, leaves nothing but an empty box of useless brown paper. PFIK: Damn... what film is that from? DarkStar: X-files I believe, either that or I just made it up, you never really can tell PFIK: This one might be obvious... but these are generic questions: #7: Which of the PCW ladies most suits your tastes and why? DarkStar: Well my lover Harle Quinn of course, sociopathic, nymphomaniac, female jester, shes beautiful, kinky, and incredibly insane... which also makes her dangerous... and she wears latex... cant beat latex PFIK: #8: Whom would you most love to tag with in PCW? DarkStar: I like going it alone, but if I was to choose, I guess it would have to be Reverand, assuming hes still around... not because hes good or anything, but because the conflicts would be comediacly unmatchable PFIK: #9: What is your favourite PCW match of all time? DarkStar: Well if its one I participated in, then definately the ladder match for the soda contract, it was brutal, I used Mike Marvelous as a projectile and I beat Trashcan Man... well technically... and I beat Kid Wonder, that was all the people that didnt suck though, it was still fun. PFIK: #10: If you were to be in a movie, what would you be and what movie? DarkStar: The Crow, of course, and I would be the lead, because everyone knows how well I fit into that part... I would be unstoppable, plus im pretty much invincable in real lfie anyway PFIK: Doesn't that mean you would do good and then die? DarkStar: .... shut up PFIK: #11: Who would you just like to kick in the nuts? DarkStar: Anyone not from PCW basicly, especially the heathens on the message boards and newsgroups across the internet, they all deserve a good swift kick in the testesis PFIK: Final question... #12: If anything could be named after you… what would it be? DarkStar: A skateboard... oh wait... thats been done, a religion... because that would just be fun as hell... or heaven... or Baal... whatever it would be at that point PFIK: Baal? DarkStar: Havent you ever played Diablo? PFIK: Diablo 1 DarkStar: You are the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, I figured Diablo had to be in your job description PFIK: Anyway, thank you Mr. Darkstar this has been most... enlightening. Do you have anything further to add? DarkStar: Yes, you smell funny, go away Well thank you Deathstar, the man who wants to do good and then die after being left alone in a box. This is the PFIK, remember kids, Pimply Ain't Easy. PFIK
