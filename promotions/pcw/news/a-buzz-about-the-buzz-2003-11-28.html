A Buzz About The Buzz

28 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With PCW trying to rebuild after a six month layoff, which forced many of the promotion's longtime superstars to seek employment elsewhere, PCW officials have been scouring the earth looking for fresh new talent to lead the company into the future. One such individual is "The Buzz" Cal Roberts. "Buzz has all of the tool to be cornerstone of a promotion," said PCW spokesperson, Mark Malloy. "He's charasmatic, tough, talented, and perhaps most importantly, driven to constantly improve himself and succeed in this industry." Roberts, 28, is well known to fans of the independent scene in central US and Canada, but this shot is his first in a major, televised promotion. Is he ready to handle the pressure? "The question is, is Primetime ready to handle The Buzz," said Roberts, with a confident smile on his face. "I've got the tools in the ring, I've got the skills on the stick and I've always had the look. Now its just a question of who I defeat and when for the PCW World Heavyweight Title." It would appear that PCW officials would reluctantly agree with Robert's assessment. "We would be remiss to discount him from World Title contention sometime in the future," said Mark Malloy. "But, this is PCW afterall. Nothing is given to those who don't earn it." Roberts, who describes his wrestling style as "Buzzoresu", is a powerfully built competitor who uses a combo of high impact manuevers and submission holds to overcome his opponents. He is also not afraid to brawl with a foe, as he was raised by a pro boxer. Will the Buzz live up to his nickname, or will he have to change it to "The Fizzle?" Stay tuned to find out!
