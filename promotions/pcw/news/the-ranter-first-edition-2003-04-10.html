The Ranter - First Edition Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Apr 2003 ---- "Thank you for trusting us with our news. Stay tuned for more ass chewing and checking by our very own BILL....O...RILEY! (Hulk Mode)." - Sheppard Smith, Fox News Analysts For all the columnist trying to bite people, or even eat their asses for doing such a good job, I'm giving you a fair warning. When you chew somebody out, watch how O'Riley does it, then try it yourselves. Opening Words Now, forget the introductions as you can see my name right there in the Column title, so get that point straight. When you look at PCW right now, you barely see anybody try to tell it straight, no its rather ass kiss, smooch, smooch. And that my friends is bullshit writing. I mean, where's your sense of humor? Where's the constant ripping into people for being complete assholes and curtain jerkers that the fans, (THE SMARKS~!) can't see it? Bah, who am I kidding, we've got a horse for a columnist here... Thoughts On the Current Scene Now, checking once you always thought was a good roster, and truthfully its STACKED in the mid-card, lower mid-card ranks, the exact point is that you need one man from the mid card to make the jump. Dante Jackson couldn't do it, and Totally Tubular Tommy made probably the biggest freaking leap from obscurity to sheer brillance over the past six months, its hard to wonder who will come up to join him. Oh lord, I'm hearing that Mark Henry theme playing in my head. You know, the new one from Smackdown: SYM. Oh well. Its gotta be three tough men to pick from - The Pharaoh: (Solid Cruiserweight reign, beat both Jackson and Green in his only SOLID defense yet). Since the year 2001, The Pharaoh has been the mystery man of the WO(WT) community, first backing with his feud with Xavier Kannon that instantly gained him credibility. Before long, he landed in PCW, had a feud with Akiyama and then took sick days for the latter part of 2002. Soon as 2003 hits, BOOM, he's off like a rocket, quickly picking up the pace and taking RAH off yet another Cruiserweight title. The thing about him that is a mystery is his availability. If he didn't have to leave so much, he'd be in the World title picture right here, right now. Remember, Kannon made him bigger not once, but TWICE and now the head honcho of PCW is locked in a feud with Howard, the savior of the 2nd Age. So where does Pharaoh scale on this? He's got a good shot at it, a very valuable shot at getting there. All he has to do is not turn Roberto Clemente on us. Upper Midcard Ranking: 8 Gaz Green: Well, Gaz has been waning off of the huge shit lately, but a Continental tournament victory along with Nerezza (as if they weren't the favorites anyway.) has done him some good. It gave him (sigh) yet ANOTHER chance to break Da Westsidaz record as champs with Nez, because the feud between the both of them really didn't turn out the way I quietly expected it to become, but it was soild. Gave them both character, and even made Green a household name when he started tagging with Kannon. The point is, the only thing holding him back, is the superiorly lax Tag scene which is looking much like OSW's World title scene right now (equivalent of 1993 WCW, by the way.) If Gaz Green stuck to singles competiton, locked himself in a pretty hot feud, it would jump start his rise towards the top. The only remaining clue here is...will Nerezza join him? Upper Midcard Ranking: 7 Dante Jackson: Remember when he tagged with Statuz Quo? When they held the Tag titles, gave P.W.A a run for their money and then he went solo? Yep, Dante Jackson has been almost everywhere, including a few title wins, but when he dropped the Cont. strap, that made everybody put a microscope under him. He was once one of the top men to be a candidate for Gimmick use, but being a rapper, even a good one is completely one dimension. The only thing that would break in two more dimensions would be him losing all his money, his wife leaving him, and taking the baby with him. But this current feud with El Romantico was supposed to be Poetry/Rap, but it slowly became dull and another mid-card showing where only guys like El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin should stick to being. If Jackson actually got a point into matching his good promo skills with card work, then you'd be giving him the Continental title right back instead of just having the tournament period. But, it didn't make sense for the ending of the tournament ANYWAY. Upper Midcard Ranking: 6.5 Shake Shit Up Now, the current state of PCW is mellow, you have a surfer as a champion, a stableboy as his opponent and then you have the saviors of the past two ages trying to KILL one another, so its all good isn't it? Nope. No Tag Division, the Schmoopies and P.W.A keep switching the title around one another, Skitzo wears the Extreme title by beating CHICKS, and the Rising Star title has a better image than both of those titles COMBINED, so yes, the lower, mid cards are a real fucked up joint. But that's for another rant. Ah, can you smell that air right there? That's the new breed of being a columnist over here in PCW, because in a serious tone of voice - none of them actually spill more shit, diss out more odd ball sayings than me - The Ranter. But why are you not reading Mr. Ed? The column who bashes nobody EXCEPT Dante Jackson? Yeah, Enigma got off lightly, and I do mean LIGHTLY, but you steadily have to repeat yourselves - Enigma, Enigma - and you get no answer. The guy who spent nearly a quarter of five minutes hyping himself throughout March, leaving space for many feuds to atleast gain some position recognition and does nothing when he comes. I call man the Shockmaster 2003 just for making me have to even sit there and WATCH his ass work, I mean the Steele/Streak angle was okay, but failed on all portions, and then well RAH drops to CW title, a title I'd love to be placed without many restrictions, so yeah his original heat when he faced Kannon died down after a bit and Walden hasn't been in good shape SINCE his Snappage period almost a year ago. Step it up boys, just step it up. Final Rants... So, yeah PCW is quietly moving along as we get to Supremacy - 1 year after the mass firings. And then, the question is, where the fuck do we go from there? A boom period hopefully. And that's all I've got to say about that.
