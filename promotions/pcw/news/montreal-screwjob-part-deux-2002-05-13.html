Montreal Screwjob Part Deux? Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 May 2002 ---- Montreal, Quebec, Canada- The city of Montreal is infamous in wrestling history. About five years ago, in a promotion that will go unnamed, a wrestler who Jeremy Howard prided himself after was screwed over by another wrestler and his boss for the Heavyweight title. Five years later, PCW comes strolling into Montreal on the lowest of lows after the cancelation of Turmoil and Fusion. What PCW needed was a great show. And, as always PCW delievered. And then......it happened. In the main event, as everyone projected, Big Alan Steele captured that PCW Heavyweight title. But that wasn't the bigest story coming from the match. After the match, a part of Tyler Nelson's past paid PCW a visit. And he did so in a way that NOBODY would expect. How would the champ be affected? What about Tyler Nelson and his group? What about Duke Williams? What about the rest of the roster? You'll have to see to find out. Also at Supremacy: El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin bring their street fight indoors. A PCW legend fights his last PCW fight. And who was that woman with Rolf? To see all this and much, much, MUCH more, click the Pay-Per-View link found in the dropdown menu.
