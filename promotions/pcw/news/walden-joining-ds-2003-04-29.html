Walden Joining DS? Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Apr 2003 ---- Amongst the rumors that are flying, after DarkStar's unprecedented apperance at OSW's Head On Collision, that Tim Walden may have followed DarkStar to OSW...to form a mega-stable, the likes of which not seen since...well..ever. Rumors were flying within OSW that DarkStar was recruiting "brooding personalities" to join a coalition. While Walden isn't exactly "brooding," he is pretty pissed off, so that WOULD make sense. We'll have to keep monitoring OSW (as much as it will pain us) to see whether this rumor will be verified or proven utterly false.
