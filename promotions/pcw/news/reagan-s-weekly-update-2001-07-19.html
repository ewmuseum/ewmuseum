Reagan's Weekly Update

19 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The big news from PCW Personnel Direct Jane Reagan's press conference this week is that she has confirmed that PCW is currently negotiating with a former World Champion from Five Star Wrestling, though Reagan declined to comment on exactly WHICH former champion. Reagan also announced that Ryan Knox has been suspended without pay pending the outcome of the drug investigation being carried out by the Boston police department. Reagan confirmed the company is happy to have resigned Duke Williams, Kramer Wildwing, Carl Cash, Wes Outlett and newcomer "The Hula Boy." Reagan made a special note that the company is currently in negotiations with indy superstar "Five Star" Lance Perfection. Reagan indicated the company may be interested in bringing back Drummer and is considering asking Red Rock to come in for a workout. When asked about the status of the rumored NWC vs PCW Pay Per View, Reagan said "I am not in a position to comment on that at this time." Reagan indicated that the company will be docking the wages of Flaymin Aymin to help pay for the $400,000+ worth of damages the Aussie hardcore legend caused at the Sydney airport. Reagan confirmed the company has entered an agreement to help the beleaguered NTICW, by sending them talent from the PWA. "NTICW is going through a rough spot, so we are helping them out by lending them some talent. To cut short any rumors, we are not entering any kind of partnership agreement, or merger with NTICW," said Reagan. When asked about rumors of a possible UFW night, Reagan laughed and said "Ed shouldn't have quit his day job."
