Akiyama Leaving

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Akiyama Leaving Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Apr 2003 ---- The Neo Void God, after his abrupt victory over Tim Walden, made an announcement about his future to Tiffany Jennings the day after his match via telephone. “We’re leaving…” was the first declaration. After Tiffany asked if he was quitting PCW or retiring completely, Aki clarified. “We just need to take a break…clear our head of some sh-t. We came back kinda abruptly in January because we were pissed with everything going on. Kannon was strutting around like a bitch, Cyclonica had pissed us off to the extreme with her actions outside PCW… and we needed a method of legal anger management. But now… we see that we’re not ready to return to PCW both physically and mentally. That’s obvious when you see us facing Tim Walden in a PPV match…instead of chasing after chump champs like Triple T who don’t deserve it…or beating Kannon within an inch of his jackass life until he faces us one on one like he should’ve at March Madness.” When asked how long he would be out, Akiyama replied with: “Who knows? To be honest, it’ll take a while before we manage to fix ourselves up. We’ve been f-cked in the head for a long time…and we have some physical defects with our body that we need looking at. We warn PCW now though…when we return, it’ll be a new Akiyama and a new wave of pain sweeping across the company. No matter how victorious things look for any of the gimmicky f-cks… they’re still only one step away from their Absolute Destiny Apocalypse.” With that said, we can only hope if the future of PCW will see the nVg once more…
