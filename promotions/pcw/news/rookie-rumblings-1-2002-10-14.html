Rookie Rumblings #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Oct 2002 ---- Well, hello. I thought you'd never see a black man such as myself writing one of these column things, but I guess its that way now. If you don't know who I am, well I'm one half of Playaz Incorperated resident Primetime Championship Wrestling Tag Team Champions. Or you can just call me Dante Jackson. Now, for some part of this way, Xavier Kannon happened to give me this shot, saying that the Shoot King and maybe other passe` writers aren't what the Third Age truly derserves to forsee its future. I'm here not to give my daily dose of things, but well to give my aspect on daily PCW life. Yeah, I'm a rookie, and many rookies aren't given the shot just to give their voice. They're basically the men and women whom are only silent and work their butts off in sight of the fans, but overall, you don't see their expressions, you only see that shell of a person hanging around and showing themselves for how the public perseves them. Well, time to get rid of the introductions and get to what this column is actually about right? A sort of rundown for Turmoil last week, just about how the comments from The Reviewer from RUSH shows that appeared on the Wrestling Tattler site. Well, lets get Turmoil out of the way first. Remember, its on the Scott Keith's Bastardized Review Grading System. Tuesday Turmoil from Sapporo Dome in Sapporo, Japan Your announcers are Jeff Marx, Dave Kern, and Duke Williams. At first, when I look back on normal after Turmoil events following a large Pay Per View, like Red October was, then I know there will be some consequences and some major reprocussions. Did Turmoil bring that? First it was hard to even see with the satelite fucking up here and there so I'll call it how I saw it. Segment #1: We open up with a little quaint segment on Asylum, some of the member still buzzing about Red October. Nerezza and Gaz Green are obviously pissed because myself and Statuz Quo took their tag team titles, but they'll live with it. Miya wants his stable to be better, and his lone female is half human/half celestial = Akiyama Sex Child. Anyway, Chapman, Web Editor, and the guy who makes me look good in pictures comes and disrupts everything. Usual Turmoil Intro before we get into our first match. Match 1: Singles Match Blade D vs. Andy Murray Murray is a lifeless man right now, where's Ben J. Palinthorpe to replace this guy? Anyway, Blade D comes out and basically mops the floor with Andy, sending him to the hospital and right out of PCW in the process. Funny thing about it is, there was no wrestling and the real move was from THE REF! Ref with a chairshot and Blade D pinned him for the three. He gets a win, but leaves a sour taste in my mouth. DUD Segment #2: El Diablo cuts a promo, is it just me or is this guy seriously in need of a title push? Segment #3: Then Chef and Bertha come in and mess around with the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, funny shit in my opinoin, before we cut to match two. Match 2: #1 Contender To Extreme Title The End vs. Scorpion vs. Jon Kano vs. Carl Cash End goes to the outside and begins fight #1829485431 at the asileway with Scorpion while a rematch of Cash and Kano resumes in the ring. Dave and Jeff begin their own conversation as the wrestlers battle, a DDT from End cuts Scoprion down, as he climbs the ropes once more and Springboard Hurricarana's Scorpion to OH MY GOD status from Kern. Scorpion and Cash switch partners and then Scorpion comes close to putting Kano away with a Royal Flush DDT for two. Cash broke it up, before End comes around and DOUBLE ONE LAST TIMES the two of them. Kano breaks the try to pin, chokeslamming End, and then Kanoplexing Cash in a no-selling fashion. Scorpion breaks up the pinfall with an ELBOW DROP O DOOM, before being caught in a Never Again from End. This one's finished 1..2..3. I'll dish out ** stars for End's trying to make this look good. Segment #4: Andy Murray comes out again to get hit by the YELLOW HUMMER!!!! Oh wait, its gold...nevermind. Segment #5: Natalie Miyagawa comes in looking for her husband, but the duo of P.W.A tell her where the snack machine happens to be. Nerezza makes a wise crack about her ass, before Gaz cuts him off. Match 3: Extreme Title Match Semore Smoke(c) vs. Jamie Celt Is it just me or is Celt getting more title shots than anyone else in PCW History, Sunday he had the Crusierweight title, and then the Extreme title Tuesday. Anyway, Smoke starts out on the offensive, with the TECHNICAL WRESTLING START TO A MATCH PERIOD, before the match picks up with a diabolical gass table throw from Smoke to Celt. Smoke turns over for the cover, but only gets two. Man, I hope Smoke gives the Extreme title that Clyde feel again. Colt gave it that sick porno feel, whatever he said had nothing to do with America, just him and his penis having a good time. Smoke pulls out the bag Celt brought to the ring and then grabs a table. Lord, he's about to get medevil on Celt's ass. Smoke sets up the table with THUMBTACKS AND BARBED WIRE! YAY! Smoke takes Celt up top for a STUPENDOUS Smoke Bomb for the three count. *** stars, basically one sided, but Smoke kept it down with that Smoke Bomb from the top. Segment #6: El Diablo and The Pharaoh get into it, I smell a feud brewing between the both of them. Keyword: "You fucking fool Xansiv! You really thought you can start with me?! Nobody starts with me!!" Flaymin Aymin and Snow certainly know about that. Match 4: Triple Threat Match for #1 Contendership to Crusierweight Title Eddie Mandrake vs. Charlie/Jung/Whatever his name will be this week Loc vs. Kramer Wild Wing. Fast paced action from top to bottom, basically all of it surrounding these three feudless guys. Was it me, or did Charlie Loc killing Juan Borro at Third Dawning cement him as a credible threat to the Extreme title? What happened with that? Mandrake and Loc keep each other up, pushing each other to the limit while Kramer gives the dumb fans to cheer about. Mandrake gets the duke, with a submission to face the Dark Cyclonia for the title next week. ***1/2 stars, fast paced, yet for some reason a Mandrake victory left me, unsatisfied. Segment #7: Xaiver Kannon, boy this should be good. He cuts a mean promo on everybody who screwed him out of HIS title at Red October. Anyway, Rent A Hero comes out, without neckbrace! I think I may need one of those Kryptonian things next time after a match, boy did his neck heal. He puts Kannon in a loop hole and basically gives the fans what they want with DARK-FUCKING-STAR coming back and calling revenge out on Kannon. Darkie gets re-instated after the people threatened to walk out on PCW. Darkstar cuts on Kannon, before the TRUE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION AKI-"BABY DADDY"-YAMA comes out and pratically gets in a war with the Mourning Star. Kannon jumps in with Renty and its all BOO KOO SHOO KOO war until Kannnon and Aki get the high hand and send both men to the hospital - again. Match 5: Crusierweight Title Match Cyclonia (c) vs. Teresa Tomas I knew Tomas would be dead by the end of this thing before the bell rang. Tomas rolls up Cyclonia for only two, before the Evil Man's Evil Daughter comes out firing, nearly getting the job done. Teresa seems to come back with a nice sitout brainbuster, before only getting two. Teresa climbs up to the top, catching the Divine Prophetess with a crossbody, but Aki's daughter catches her foe, before going off in a MOVE LIST DECAPATATION of Teresa Tomas and finishes her off with Silence. Even Clay Claybourne can't speak as Cyco just kicks his ass as well. Damn that's one odd powerful family. ***1/4 Segment #8: Aki and Cyclonia pratically fuck Leo Micelli up for life after what he did Sunday to Aki during the Four Way match, this is followed up by Cyclonia wanting Anarchy to leave Asylum. Match 6: Non Title Match Veneneman Pendragon(t) vs. Rent-A-Hero(c) Solid, soild match for both men as Pendragon currently is a shoe in for rook of the year, but if it were for an entire year, I'd give to to Xavier Kannon undoubtably. Winning four titles in a year is something, almost legendary. With that, it gets down to the nitty gritty as a solid twelve minute match comes down to the final seconds. Pendragon does the deed as he pins Renty with a Last Crusade from Pendragon. *** stars, more if it went on a bit longer. Segment #9: Anarchy comes to Miyagawa and tells him about Aki wanting her in CODE, which Miya agrees with, being the goody too shoes face that he is. Anarchy leaves Miyagawa with a kiss, IN FRONT OF HIS OWN WIFE. Damn family soaps, they always give us ideas for sick revenge. Match 7: Singles Match Xavier Kannon w/Eleanor Hall-Kannon vs. The Pharaoh w/ Isiy Xansiv Good lord, Kannon v. Pharoah verison 17, as Kannon gets the upper hand, before getting rocked back with a few of Pharaoh's moves, only getting two on any of the big time moves. Ellie tosses a chair into the ring, meaning only that Kannon's up to something DASTARDLY! Pharoah walks right into A SICKENING HALLMARK ONTO THE CHAIR for the three. Good ass match, which ends the show with El Diablo kicking the shit out of Pharaoh after the match. Always like an Egyptian to get his ass kicked by a Mexican after a match. **** END SHOW That ends it for Turmoil, good show all over and Aki's family tree is growing steadfast. I think that he'll be the dominant player in PCW, soon as somebody gets Clyde for the belt. But me, I have to safely say that with the Neo Void God around, nobody is safe. The PCW Tag Team divison is something to be reckoned with, especially with a bitter feud between Playaz Inc. and P.W.A. Its nice to hold a piece of gold for the first time in your life, but well it means it even the better when some of the teams that are gunning for you are equally talented. The Heartbreakers, Redemption Song, and Cowabunga Connection are just a few teams out there on the market to challenge for our titles, but we need a tag team influx badly. A female influx was good two months ago, now its time for the tag team divison to get a face lift. Singles wise, we have enough people for every divison and Mall Brawl this Friday night could elevate some of the lesser known stars into the fold and thickness of some title races. If it wasn't for the wise idea of Kannon, the Continental title wouldn't be on the line. Last year it was the process of being #1 Contender to the WORLD TITLE, but Kannon's un-smart move could have gotten him the belt back quicker. All he needed to do was beat the entire frickin roster to prove that he was the greatest EVER, but oh well I guess I'll have to do it. Now, being a rookie isn't all its cracked up to be even if you're a 9x platinum selling rap artist like myself. My second album, Tha 2nd Comin' will be in stores sometime next year, just trying to make the DOUBLE DISC CD something to bang heads once more. I'll be back with more, including an interview with Xavier Kannon if I can get his phone number, but until then..I holla. - Dante Jackson
