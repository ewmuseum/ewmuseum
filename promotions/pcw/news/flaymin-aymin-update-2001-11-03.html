Flaymin Aymin Update

3 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flaymin Aymin Update Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Nov 2001 ---- Having now being locked up in excess of two months without even a trial, PCW Management recently inquired about the status of Flaymin and the possibilities of him recieving the death penalty or possibly deportation to Finland. The response they recieved via telephone, however was quite a rude shock..... "Yeah, I've had more then two months to consider my heinous crimes to society and I think I've really learnt about the meaning of life while sitting in this rat-infested hole..... Jail just plain ol' rocks!! Matter o' fact, I'm appealing to the warden that I recieve a life sentence. Lets face it, Jail has unlimited benefits: -I don't have to pay any rent. -I get meals delivered to my cell daily- 3 times a day. -I don't have to go to work. -The toilet is only a metre away from my bed which means I can take a leak from my bunk bed. -There's always a porno playing in the video room. -In PCW we spend endless days travelling around the country, in the slammer the furthest I have to walk is to the toilet and back. -And finally, I don't have to pay no stinkin' tax- instead you all pay tax to support my criminal ways!! So the closest any of you will ever come to me is via a copy of my mugshot which i'll gladly autograph for a small fee." Despite these unforseen comments, Flaymin's attornies continue to push for his freedom, encouraged by the latest lawyer to jump aboard Team Flaymin: Morrocan born legal whizz kid 'Achmed Machmoud-Achmed'. In his time in the Morrocan deserts, Machmoud-Achmed built up a respectable reputation with succesful law suits against nomads stealing water, peasants stealing sheep sh*t and selling it as manure and not to mention his landmark victory againt homeless people stealing cable TV. Machmoud-Achmed, the son of a wealthy camel herder, should really bolster Team Flaymin and ensure he walks out of jail a free man- whether he likes it or not.
