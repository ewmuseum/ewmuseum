DKay Hey Kay?

13 January 2002

DKay Hey Kay? Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Jan 2002 ---- His recent actions include agreeing to take Boda's pet alligator, Powder Puff, for a walk; begging on various occasions; throwing a brick through D-Kay's window to break and in and wait for him; and generally pestering them in them hopes of the Deuces cracking. We managed to catch up with Red Rock's right hand man Boolie, who had this to say: "Whats a Deuce? ...Oh yeah, he keeps talking about getting in, i think he's gone mental or somthing." We also managed to catch up with the one man connecting D-Kay and Red Rock, Duzza, who teamed with them both in PCW2. He had this to say: "The who? Man why my man Rock be wanting to join a bunch of damn cracker fools? Unbelivable." We also told Duzza that another former partner, D-Kay was there, and he replied with the following: " D-Kay done sold out. Hanging around them white boys. He thinks he's too good to be hanging around brothers, I got love for my man and all, but the brother got a beating coming, no doubt." Red Rock was unavailable for comment, as were the Wild Deuces. All we know is, if they want a sacrificial lamb...they have found one. We'll try and bring you more on this story as it unfolds.
