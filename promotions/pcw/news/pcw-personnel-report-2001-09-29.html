PCW Personnel Report

29 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Sep 2001 ---- PCW Personnel Director Jane Reagan held her weekly press conference early Saturday morning, and made the following statements/announcements. -PCW has signed three new wrestlers, El Magnifco Busmuchacho de Mysterio, Unstoppable Eugene and Edgecrusher. She gave little information on El Magnifco Busmuchacho de Mysterio saying only "he is an exciting lucha libre worker PCW fans will love." According to Reagan, Unstoppable Eugene was "hired directly by Tyler Nelson" so she knows little about him. Edgecrusher is the former NTICW North American Champion and a former PCW minor leaguer. -Kaiser has been found and is apparently working closely with Vampir Nosferatu. Little is know beyond that. -Skitzo has been formally released by the company. -PCW officials are not happy with the work rates of AJ Storm, The Reverend Jon Hull and Doug Troy. "Changes may occur" said Reagan. -PCW officials are looking at signing Sassy and Totally Tubular Tom out of the PWA. -Brock Newbludd and Stephen Blood could be returning to action soon. -PCW officials denied rumors they were considering bringing back former workers Tony Stecchino or Johnny Raindance. Reagan promised her next personnel update would come after Turmoil.
