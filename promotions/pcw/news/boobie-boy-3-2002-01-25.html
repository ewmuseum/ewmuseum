Boobie Boy #3 Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy #3 This issue: Juan Borro Date: January 24, 2002 This is our third issue of 20 Questions and it kicks a major GOOOOOOOOOOAL! That’s right, this issue features one half of the former PCW Tag Team Champions Golden Game as well as a former Cruiserweight Champion…Juan Borro. Juan has taken some time from playing soccer and wrestling to answer some questions. 1) Where did your wrestling career start including how The Golden Game was formed? Juan: Kevin and I were in PWA together, but didn't meet until we were both put in EW (Eternal Wrestling). We must have been there just two weeks, but we seemed to work well together as a team. From there we went on to NTICW, and after it closed we got our shot into PCW. It was a pretty quick process actually. 2) So lately it seems that both you and Kevin have not been working together as a tag team…you most recently fighting for the cruiser title. Is this foreshadowing things to come or just a little change to spice up life? Juan: Well.. We are trying to get back in the tag scene, but would like to prove ourselves as single wrestlers also. I'm not the biggest man in this federation, but certainly one of the quickest and best athletes. The cruiserweight division was made for a man like myself, and I jumped at the chance. Kevin and I will win our tag team straps back again one day, but for now my focus is on the cruiserweight title. 3) Speaking of the Cruiserweight title you take on the current champ Xavier Kannon as well as Chris Chase at the PPV Shang-hied …as a follow up to question #2 do you have anything you would like to say about that? Juan: Well I'm sure it will be a great match, but every great match with me in it ends the same way, and that's me being victorious. I've fought against both men in the ring, but have never really had a clean match with either. I am actually willing to keep my stablemates away from interfering if Chase agrees to do the same. I know how much damage both men can do, but I will become 2 time Cruiserweight Champion any way I can. Should be a great match though. 4) Yes it should be… so everyone knows about Kevin’s supposed marriage that has been put off or whatever the deal was with that…what’s the lady life looking like for you? Juan: Well I am currently in a relationship with my lovely girlfriend Juliet. Juliet promises to be by my side for the Pay-Per View so I am glad about that. No signs of marriage or anything big yet, I still need to play the field with some other women who throw themselves at me. Us good looking bachelor's need to fight off the ladies, ya know. Matt: Yeah, unfortunately for me my mom fights them off for me… Juan: Hey a boy’s best friend should always be his mother. 5) So your whole gimmick revolves around soccer…do you still play it or is it just a past vision now? Juan: I still play, but nothing on a world-class level. I'm devoted to wrestling, and I've already hung up the golden cleats. But believe me, I'll be there rooting on my fellow Portuguese National team players at the World Cup. Matt: That’s if Italy doesn’t beat them first! Juan: AH! I hear they still have Baggio on the team, when will he get the hint and just retire. Portugal will prevail, I guarantee it. 6) Being a product of the PWA what are your thoughts on the new talent that are being produced…anybody you like? Juan: PWA is great, and I know how hard my fellow stablemate El Diablo works on it. Their is alot of talent there, too many to name. With the stable occupying my time I haven't had much time to check it out lately. I do remember all of the other great's they've recently produced. Paddy O’Shea is going to make PWA proud one day, I guarantee it. It might not seem like the greatest place for learning, but it does help. Sometimes all you need is a little constructive criticism to help your career. 7) Who do you travel with on the road between shows? Juan: I usually travel with Kevin and Mandy, nowadays Juliet comes along. During our international campaigns I travel with the whole International Incident. Some of the other guys can't stand traveling with Kevin because of all the singing he does on the plane. 8) Do you have any superstitions before a match? Juan: Well I must wear my golden cleats. Not the same ones I use for soccer, because PCW doesn't allow spikes on the bottom, go figure. I pray to my homeland of Portugal and that's about it. 9) You joined PCW with the first wave of NTICW wrestlers such as Syko, Skitzo, and myself. What do you think of the second wave of NTICW wrestlers? Juan: Well I must say some are mighty impressive. When we first came up we were still young and I for one was new to the wrestling scene. I depended on guys like you and the others to teach me the basics. The guys today have a lot of talent, but just have to use more of it. Once they realize to treat this career like a hobby and not a job, they'll do just fine. 10) What was the greatest moment in your wrestling career? Juan: 11) What was the most embarrassing moment of your career? Juan: It is probably when I won the cruiserweight title for the first time. I loved winning the PCW tag titles, and especially loved the glory of winning the NTICW tag titles right before it closed, but the Cruiserweight Tile gave me a feeling like none other. I did something big on my own, and it made my country and me very proud. It was a day to remember. 12) I wasn’t going to bring this up now but since you mentioned it…everyone knows that myself and my brother were the greatest tag team in NTICW history…you having the belts that we wore for over 4 months is preposterous! So, how much will they cost me? Juan: Hey we earned them with our whole one match in NTICW, so we deserved it. But now that you made the offer it's on Ebay. Asking price is four hundred dollars, but Kannon is supposedly going to bid six hundred. Not sure, just a rumor. When will he learn that he and his partner were never real NTICW tag champs? Matt: Money isn’t an option, I got $2000 on them if ya want. Juan: Sold! Matt: Score! Juan: Talk to Kevin for the other… 13) What was your most embarrassing moment in your wrestling career (not including selling your NTICW Tag Belt to me just now)? Juan: I think it was getting beaten up by TIMMY~! (**Note** Timmy~! Is Tim Walden) in the Mall Brawl. I felt used and abused, it's like a girl beating you in arm wrestling. 14) List some of your favorites…(TV show, Food, Soda, Movie) Juan: My favorite TV shows are JACKASS and Tuesday Turmoil. Of course I can't help but love watching myself, and those dumb Americans on JACKASS crack me up. My favorite food is chorizos, and lobster. I did sponsor BRISK, so I'd have to say that's my favorite soda. My favorite Liquor is Port Red Whine. My favorite movie is the Breakfast Club… don't ask. Matt: Don’t tell… 15) The big thing being talked about is the battle of the presidency between Tyler Nelson and Duke Williams. Who are you supporting in this controversial fight? Juan: I wouldn't want to get on Tyler Nelson's bad side, but I have to go for Duke. Nothing is funnier than an all American hick screwing up the PCW presidency. But what the hell, ho does say one hell of a GOOOOOAAAAAAAALLLLL! 16) If you were on a desert island and could bring one thing what would you bring? Juan: Everyone is expecting me to say soccer ball, but hell no. I think I'd bring Juliet. A man needs to get some or else he tends to go crazy. 17) Are you a tits kinda guy or an ass guy? Juan: I need the full package, but nice ass makes me crazy. It must be a Portuguese thing what can I say, how about you? Matt: Definitely a tits guy…an ass is nice but you can’t suck on an ass without getting sick or something… Juan: True 18) Where do you see yourself in one year? Juan: Hopefully, I'll still be wrestling but I have to see how things turn out. I really see myself trying to bulk up and make a run at the continental division. Things happen so fast nowadays, you never know what'll happen. 19) Who do you think will be the first person to defeat Jeremy Howard for the World Title? Juan: I see a man like Akiyama or Vampir beating him. The other guys in the division have skill, but you need to outsmart Jeremy and out power Jeremy in order to beat him. But for now, I see him holding the title for another month or so. 20) Thanks for taking the time to sit down with me today, good luck on Sunday and lastly any comments on myself, the column or the PPV? Juan: It's been nice talking to you, hopefully I can get your ass back in the ring one of these days for a TLC tag match. The column is looking good, and your amount of readers have just hit the roof now that you've interviewed me. I'll let you be the first to know that International Incident has some big plans for Shanghaied, so buckle up. So until next column, I am BOOBIE BOY Matt Elia getting the latest on your favorite and least favorite superstars. Look for Volume #4 hitting shelves soon featuring the current (as of Thursday) Cruiserweight Champion XAVIER “Who Better than?” KANNON! What will the superstar say about his first PCW PPV? Maybe some follow up to what Juan says? It’s all inside issue 4!
