Eating their words?

9 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eating their words? Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Jan 2002 ---- wrestlingtattler.com has reported on a speculation of a Restaurant in PCW, below is the article that can be found on the central website. "It has been spoken of, no whispered of, no speculated that an elite group of Primetime Championship Wrestling, and Primetime Wrestling Academy members have come together to form a business, a Restaurant to be exact. A source deep within PCW has speculated that PCW Wrestler The Josh is playing a large part in this, something about him not making enough money in PCW as a low card worker and needing the money from a restaurant. When Joshua Kosidlo (known to the wrestling world as The Josh), was questioned about his involvement in this restaurant all he could say about it was, 'If God so wills me to do so, then I will, however if he does not then I shallt not.' After thinking about what he said for a decent amount of time, we came to the conclusion we do not care what he says. After poking around some more in the locker room we ran into Zurick. We asked Zurick his thoughts on this restaurant and he answered kind of oddly, 'I thought of a restaurant, it was gonna be called Slit your wrist, but then I was informed I could no longer help my friend due to a passion for scaring away customers, whatever that means.' Again walking away scared, we bumped into Hessian and Minion the Imp. We asked their thoughts, and both had rather different comments. All Hessian had to say, after a long pause of course was 'Infidel'. Minion of course just laughed quite uncontrollably. Finally we gave up the interviewing and left the locker rooms, on the way out however we did see The Josh and Angel talking to PCW's Jobber king HANK~! Could this mean that HANK~! is involved in this too? - Ivanna Johnson (wrestlingtattler.com reporter.)" With all that Ivanna had to say, and the fellow PCWers had to say it does seem like an interesting possibility, that and... well I think PCW needs to offer mental care for their employees.
