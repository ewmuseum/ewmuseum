The Untouchable Hot Shot Posted by Shane Crockett on 7 Apr 2004 ---- AP WIRE- Memphis, Tennessee Fresh off a two year 'leave of absence', the cocky but battle-proven 25 year old phenom, Shane Crockett has arrived in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Following a brief retirement from the sport to heal nagging injuries, the seven year pro out of Laguna Beach, California is full of surefire attitude that's likely to irritate even the most chiseled of veterans. Crockett, however, doesn't seem to mind. 'I've been around the sport for a long, long time. A lot longer than my seven years of experience would have you believe. I might write checks with my mouth that my body can't cash. That's why you've got to talk a big game and then cheap shot everyone before they can get you.' Crockett got his start in California when he was 18 years of age. His uncle is the former NWA Kingpin Jim Crockett Jr., who ran the Mid Atlantic Territory for many years out of Charlotte, North Carolina. Crockett is no stranger to the Carolinas, Georgia, and Tennessee, having traveled around the territory with his uncle. Crockett, though, is ready to make his own legacy in PCW. 'I've had to live in the shadow of my namesake for quite a while now, but it's about damn time that I stood up and started doing things on my own for a change.' Shane, who was on the fast track to stardom before a broken neck put an abrupt end to his career two years ago, is an accomplished wrestler throughout the United States and Canada. Crockett is scheduled to face Jimmy Diablo at PCW Turmoil on Saturday, April 10th, 2004 here at The Pyramid in Memphis, Tennessee. -Associated Press
