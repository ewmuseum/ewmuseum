Heroes of Wrestling PPV? Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Oct 2001 ---- Rumours coming from wrestlingtattler.com are abound with rumours of a heroes of wrestling pay per view from Primetime Championship Wrestling. A rumoured card would involve the following: MWF Rising Star Title Layeth the Smackdown vs Ming(c) Tag Team Match Trashcan Man and The Joker vs Ryan Knox and Derrick Diamond PCW Europian Title Match The Chef(c) vs Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth vs Hans Kruger vs Bad Battio Ladder Match LACKoSTRESS vs Brian Hanson vs Troy Brown Hell in the Cell Grudge Match Ricky Nakagawa vs PGod A PCW spokesman stated clearly on this basis that there is no basis for that basis.
