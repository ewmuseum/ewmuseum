Reaching Out to Youths

10 October 2001

Reaching Out to Youths Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Oct 2001 ---- It may soon be D.A.W.Ning on kids around the country exactly how important reading is. DarkStar, Akiyama, Tim Walden, and Ed have teamed up to for DAWN, a group that visits schools around the country and teach them about the joys of reading. "Even though English is not my first language, I still enjoy reading," said Akiyama. Ed also talked about his love for reading during a recent DAWN visit, reminding children that "Playboy magazine also has intriguing and informative articles." Sponsored by Waldenbooks, the eduational athletes will be helping inner city youth to improve their reading skills. So far, the first stop has been successful, except for several complaints from teachers and parents about the reading materials their children have been bringing home.
