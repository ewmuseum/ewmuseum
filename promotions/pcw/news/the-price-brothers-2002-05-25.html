The Price Brothers Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 May 2002 ---- (Note: This column was written prior to the start of the tag team tournament) There are not many people who can say they came from Primetime's Academy, were signed by one of the biggest federations in todays world and immediatly be placed into a tag team championship tournament. However, there are a few. What makes a perfect tag team? Well there is no straight answer. Some of the two most different people have formed the greatest teams. Some teams are created upon character-likeness, friendship or in some cases, family. Yet again; what makes the perfect tag team? Hard work, commitment and the ability to co-operative with one another. A not-so perfect example of this would be one of the recently signed tag teams to the orginasation of Primetime Championship Wrestling; The Price Brothers. They are both hard working, both are commited to wrestling but seem to lack co-operation. More specifically, the two are the total opposite of each other. Chet is a more relaxed and laid back character. He let's life's little problems just glide by. He lives every day as if there were no tommorow. He hasn't a care in the world with the exception of pro wrestling. Michael on the other hand is more uptight. He dresses like a prop from a box of Harvard collectables. His hair is always neatly combed and he refuses to get any dirt on him or his clothes. Michael likes to get everything done right and early of the dealines. He's a basic dork. It certainly is a wonder how these two young brothers came to reinforce a tag team. They never agree with what the others says. Every day the two are at each others throats about anything. The two constantly debate who is the more popular with the fans. Even the smallest thing like picking a shirt colour can set off World War III with the brothers. No matter what one says, the other always has to disagree. It doesn't make a difference who is right or wrong, as long as the two can get easy pops at each other. I leave you with a question. Should The Price Brothers bite the bullet and realise that the tag team they have is based upon crumbled rocks and split? Or should the two work out there mountains of differences and threaten the world of Primetime Wrestling with a destructive sibling force?
