Personnel Report

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Jun 2003 ---- Dan Osbourne asked for, and been given, his release. The shame of losing to HANK~! was just too much to bear. HANK~! is retired from PCW, and assures us that he is not returning. El Diablo has been granted a vacation to mourn the loss of his wife Angela, who died on Tuesday Turmoil. Violence Jack is still recovering from a concussion received from Dante Jackson at last week's Fusion main event. He shouldn't be out too long, but he is still under observation. We checked. Two Ton is still dead. El Romantico is still healing from March Madness. He hasn't a date to return yet. Clyde still haunts us, but has yet to make a return. If anyone owns the horse parked in space G-36, it's about to be towed and sent to the scrapyard. If you own a large horse with a saddle and Claymore sword with the word "Excalibur" engraved upon it, please come claim your property. That is all.
