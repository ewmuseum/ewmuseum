Pharaoh Cleared

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pharaoh Cleared Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Mar 2003 ---- Followed by a huge sigh of relief from PCW officials, Titu Xansiv, known to most simply as Pharaoh, was deemed psychologically and physically fit to return to action, after a one-week hiatus following a BRUTAL Pay Per View match against the conniving Clyde. The Demon's slaughter of innocents was the catalyst, many felt, for what was at first seen as a total nervous breakdown by Pharaoh. However, PCW medics and psychological analysts have determined that Titu is 'fit' to wrestle. However, under somewhat shady politics, the doctors would not give their estimation of a numerical percent of where Pharaoh is at in his recovery. One PCW worker who witnessed the medical testing was quoted as saying, "He may be fit by those quacks standards, but he's not a hundred percent, no friggin' way." So, it seems as though, while he has been cleared, the situation revolving around Pharaoh's road to recovery from this trauma and shock may wind longer.
