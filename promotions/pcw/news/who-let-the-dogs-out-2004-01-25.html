Who Let The Dogs Out?

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who Let The Dogs Out? Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jan 2004 ---- Update on Duke Williams Arrest...... As Turmoil went of the air this past week, we all laid witness to Duke Williams being handcuffed, and hauled off to jail. Supposedly, the Hossman was found to be in possession a rather large amount of marijuana. Also, supposedly, this came from an 'anonymous tip to local authorities. The only known facts are that there was a rather large, blonde haired muscular man seen leaving Duke's office earlier on the show. That same individual was seen standing with Zoober and James as Williams was put into a police car. Now, while his face was never seen, there is speculated that it was none other than Former 2 Time PCW World Champion and Former OSW World Champion Christopher 'Boda' Sloboda. As of this press release, Duke was able to be released on bail. The amount and the bailer unknown at this time. Attempts to contact Chris Sloboda have gone unfruitful at this time. What does this mean for Duke Williams this week on Turmoil. Have Zoober and James finally nailed the coffin shut for Starr and Nelson? And was that indeed Boda we all saw last week? One thing is for sure, this week.... If Duke has his way.... All hell will break loose on Turmoil.
