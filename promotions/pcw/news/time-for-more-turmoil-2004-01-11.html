Time For More Turmoil! Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Jan 2004 ---- PCW is in the swing of things again, and with the show getting back on track, will new faces (and well as old) step it and carry the ball, or will Ron Starr's dastardly deeds pay off with the company going bankrupt while the pockets of himself and Nelson bulge? Well, if PCW president, raging redneck, and HOFer Duke Williams has any say in it, it'll be the former. But with Dick and Zoober, the later is true for them. Fresh off the set from one of the highest rated Turmoil's in months, PCW's locker, for the most part up and coming, seems ready to meet the challenge, but is the burden simply too much? We hope not. To start things off they'll be a singles match between newcomer Aver Vance, who proved he could hang with the big boys last week on Turmoil, and Brian Arson, a much hated cheat who can't seems to keep his paws off anything, but nonetheless has prospered thus far in PCW. Terlayne Sterling, PCW rookie, who, contrary to a falsified report, is not a newcomer from the PWA, proved his worth last week, but will he meet the challenge of "The Buzz" Cal Roberts, one of the most talked about wrestlers today, who's making his wretsling debut on this week's Turmoil? Here's an odd one: A gay, child molesting Brit in a teddy bear suite versus a Native American. Jay is fresh off his second straight victory over Awfa last week, but "straight" is not a word in Bungles vocabulary, who ended the night with a hug with his opponent this week. But they'll be no hugs and kisses this week as these two beasts battle it out to show who's who in the upper ranks of the PCW roster. With HOFer Jon Kano acting as special referee, fellow HOFer, and acting president, the Malice Man Duke Williams will turn back to the ring to take on the beast of Propoganda, who made his shoking debut in the new PCW last week in what was part of a series of intriguing events to end last Turmoil. At first glance, Kano, a respected and admired wrestler, would seem to be a nuetral party, but last week was the wicked reach of Starr able to turn the HOFer against the boss? We'll just have to see... And for the main event, the first ever title of the NEW PCW will be decided as the colorful Shaun Styles takes on PCW's resident redneck Abner to decide the Extreme Championship in a no-holds-barred, all out brawl. But what do theire foes, Brian Arson and Micheal Jacques, respectively have to say about this? Both feel taht they've accomplished just as much and deserve the same opportunities as their counterparts. Do they? Who knows. But what remains certain is that both Abner and Styles are skilled competitors, and both would like to win the title proving that they are the newbies to step up and take the burden in the new PCW.
