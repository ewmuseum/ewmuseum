Shooting Bullets #23 Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Dec 2001 ---- All expressions and opinions in this article are solely those of The Shoot King, a ham sandwich, and actor/comedian Nipsy Russell. Anything written can not be used without the consent of The Shoot King. Any duplication of this article will result in your ass on a platter. All hate mail and such can be sent to [detail withheld] where it can and will be used in a future article. (This bona fide PCW shoot is brought to you by slashwrestling.com. If you want a real wrestling recap, this is the place to go. Never, NEVER read the recaps of that Shoot King wannabe known as Scott Keith. He blows more than Brandi. The fine wrestlers of NTI....oh, wait, they're CLOSED! And by the BCPD. If you smell bacon and grease, it must be the Baltimore County Police.) Back again for my "MJ" column. Column number 23. And all legit. Not like Decker's. A guy who skips numbers by the handful to make it look like he's somebody. Why is this the "MJ" column? I dunno. Jordan is #23 and plays for the, heh, Wizards. I live in Baltimore. Unfortunately, that makes the Wizards the home NBA team. And you wonder why I hate the NBA. Personally, I hate Jordan. He's just an egotistical bastard who everyone knows is getting paid by the league to return because all these drug dealers called players can't bring in ratings for nothing. This will be the last time you hear me talking about the NBA in a column. They're just not worth it. Contract the whole league. Nobody cares. Now that I've gotten that off my chest (finally), I guess I should get to matters equally as pointless. Jacob Yoder Fired Why me? Why must every person I mark out for go? Judas Steele gets fired for turning into a bitch. Putter Mayhem gets fired for not doing shit. Well, unless there was a phantom e-mail that I never saw. The Chef didn't get fired. He retired. He still works behind the scenes though (his handler does). And now Jacob Yoder spazes out and gets himself fired. Why do I bother marking out for anyone? When I do, they always end up going away. Maybe that's a reason for me to start marking out for Kano. Anything to get him out. But what I don't understand about it is why he did it? What could've pissed Yoder off that much for him to freak like that? I don't know what was said on PCW Cowardice since I'm boycotting it, so I can't go off of that. Like my desperate for attention co-worker Scoops said, Yoder is one of the best out of character people in PCW. Now he's just out of PCW. But I'm not gonna complain. Yoder was fired for a reason, so there is no need to. I'm extremely disappointed in him. But you know what ther worst thing about this is? Now we have no place to send our garbage wrestlers when they become useless (i.e Jay Vyper). Back to Business? Robbie Duker, and that's what I'm gonna call him until he comes to his senses and realizes that Duke is a school for of spoiled preppy bitches and gets on the UM bandwagon, has returned. Oh joy. Oh bliss. What a great day for us all. (sense the sarcasm) Robbie, you are old news. You are nothing but a desperate little pissant begging for attention. Hating you all? You don't hate anybody, Duker. It is so obvious that your heat seeking is done in desperation. Anyone who can't see this is blind. You try way too hard to try and hate. For me, it just comes naturally. I have pissed off every single person on the roster at one point or another without even trying. You, Duker, try to generate heat more than you try to get out of the 3rd grade. Don't worry you'll do it one day. Robbie, your desperate pleas for attention are laughable. You are pissing off nobody. Are you all doing is dragging your already tarnished image farther and farther into the mud. Pretty soon, you'll be back in your offices down in Jersey. But, hey, if you're having fun, all the power to ya. Just makes me look all the better. It's a shame The Hound has decided to leave. Now I'm stuck having to put up with you as my lackey again. Depressing. PPV Predictions How many times must I say this? Do not use a column to make stupid Pay-Per-View predictions. That is what the boards were made for. Our latest violater: Scoops Staley. Scoops? Is he related to Luke or Duce? I hope not. Both Duce and Luke are fine athletes. One plays in the NFL, the other will play there unless he's forced to retire before the draft. We all know how old thoe BYU players are. But Scoops is probably some 400 pounds freak with a dented skull and no bladder control. If he's reated to either of these guys in any way, I send my sympathies out to them. Now, as I was saying, don't make petty PPV predictions here. Talking about the PPV is one thing. But putting up match by match predictions just so you have an excuse to bore us with your drivel is something I can't stand. Now for my PPV predictions. As always, I won't do the match by match thing. That's why boards were made. I'll just take this time to try and guess the monthly surprise. What is the monthly surprise? It's that thing, usually near the end of the PPV, that happens that we all moan about the next day. People quit over it. Flame wars start. So on and so forth. A recap of the monthly surprises thus far and you can see what I mean. January: Media forms with Nelson, Dan Hollywood, Cht King, and Mr. Showtime. Announce purchase of PCW due to lawsuit against PCW after Flaymin Aymin tractor incident a couple weeks before. Two thumbs up....except for Blowtime who, well, blows. Febuary: No PPV (thank God) March: Frisco wins title (a surprise in itself). Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth makes debut. Biohazard made a return for what that's worth. April: Dream Team arrives. They consist of the talented Legend Inc. (too bad he didn't stay). Two of the most overrated sacks of shit ever in PCW, Caged Explosion and Mike Extreme. Oh, and some guy named Jeremy Howard who didn't get noticed until it was found out he lied about his win streak and was uncovered for the liar he is. Jeremy's a liar. LIAR!! YOU'RE A LIAR, HOWARD!! Thing as a whole blew. May: Ryan Knox unveiled as "The Phantom Booker" after beating Nelson (again). Safe to say this blew chunks. June: Stryfe from PCW2 defeats Alan Kriegman for the PCW title in his first official PCW match. Only person to ever do so in PCW history. Stryfe's title reign ended up being lame however. July: No PPV (thanks Homestead) August: Inphino Blitz in the main event. Surprise. Trashcan Man wins title. Surprise. Old School, if I'm not mistaken, was formed at this PPV. BUST!! September: No real surprises. The only surprise would be Chef winning the title for the late Bertha Stewart. Then he'd go on to retire and cause yet another tournament. October: Six other guys are allowed to compete with Streak and Ivan as headliners. Streak wins the belt only to suffer a horrible, and totally uncreative beating from Dark Age. Streak and Brandi get together, both later fired. Steele returns and claims to be the father of Brandi's baby. He's fired. Whole thing totally got slaughtered. Probably the bigest buildup to anything and had the absolute worst outcome. Can't get any worse than that. November: Darkstar returns after being fired. Costs Clyde the belt (thankfully). Wild Deuces form. Safe to say that'll suck in the end. Yoder's gone. New Breed formed. Reverend is....uh...was he ever here? Now for this month's surprise. And look no further than the crowning of a new CEO. Kremmen is gone. We have no CEO. Seems appropriate. So I'm gonna take a guess at who it is. And, no, it's not Nipsey Russell. The CEO will be......... Kid Wonder!?! After reading the radio recap (since I'm boycotting PCW Cowardice until Duke can grow a set), The Phantom Booker said that Tyler Nelson and Kid Wonder would be in control. Tyler Nelson is the president, so of course I understand that. But what ranking does Kid Wonder hold? Sounds like someone slipped up. Of course, if that was a totally OOC statement, and it coulda been since, like I said, I wasn't listening, then I retract my entire paragraph and proclaim myself as new CEO. Just look for this big surprise during either the title match or the #1 contender's match (that Tyler Nelson is in). The Silver Bullets This is my next to last column. In my last column, I will award, for the first time, the Silver Bullets. They will go to people who excell in certain categories that I deem worthy of a Silver Bullet. I won't give any hints as to who will win what. But you can rest assure that they won't nearly be as crooked as the PCW Year End Awards. Seriously. Big Alan Steele as face of the year? What has he done? It's made for IC following. Not OOC following. If that's the case, why isn't guys like Clyde and Ivan, people who have strong OOC followings like Steele's, up for the award? Jon Kano Bash of the Moment You can't imagine the look on my face when I looked at the fan board and saw Jon Kano run his mouth. He called his work quality. He said it was good. He cried about losing to Chuck Manson. NOBODY CARES!!! Your work is terrible. It's always been terrible. It was terrible against Manson and you deserved to lose to him. It's called he's getting a push and you're not. And I'm sure you said something about Stewart Hix. So, in a rare defense of Hix, let me say this. Stewart Hix was great TV and Continental title material. Was he World title material? Of course not. But he was great for the mid-card because he had the workrate. Perhaps you're jealous of Hix because he got a World title push and all you'll ever get is a joke match for the World title. What I was mad at Hix about was that he moonlighted in another federation about being PCW champ and based his roleplays around it. That's just cheap and shows him as the whore he is. Thank God you'll never get that same chance. Quote of the Week "He who laughs last, laughs loudest." -Vince McMahon Why this? Well, after being called crazy for the last six weeks or so because of my "insane" predictions, it turned out that I was right after all. Colorado won the Big 12. nebraska snuck their way into the title game. And, yes, the Browns are the Brownies again and losing...losing...losing. And all of this is stuff I said would happen. Who's laughing now? Eternally Cool Quotes "Do you have any idea how hard it is to get a title off a guy from Calgary? "While the City Sleeps" by MC 900 Ft. Jesus hits as the crowd is still going nuts from the FINGER POKE OF DEATH~! As Trashcan Man makes his way out, Frisco covers Hix inside the ring. Referee Buzz Meacham counts, 1......2.......3! STEWART HIX HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Hix is tossed from the ring by Frisco as TCM gets to ringside. TCM grabs Hix and hits him with the PYRO DRIVER ON THE CONCRETE compressing Hix's spine and putting a small crack in the concrete. TCM goes into the ring as medics attend to Hix who is lying in a pool of his own blood. -Dream Card...June 29, 2001 Oh, and in the event you misunderstood what I was saying, you can pull the guns away from your heads.
