March Madness Matches Announced

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March Madness Matches Announced Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2003 ---- This morning, PCW’s preppy Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall announced the provisional card for PCW’s March Madness PPV, live from the Staples Center in Los Angeles on March 16th. In singles action, Teresa Tomas will defend her Television Title against PCW Financial Reclaimations Executive Tim Walden is a special 2/3 Falls Submission match. Teresa has one up on Tim after making him tap in a Southern Comfort v No Evasion Submission match, can she do it again to continue her mammoth run? Totally Tubular Tommy’s Continental Title will be on the line in a Four Corner’s match that will not only pit him against his nemesis and PCW’s Cruiserweight Champion, Rent-a-Hero, but also the duelling duo of Dante Jackson and Venneman Pendragon. Having held the strap since Decimation, is TTT about to bite off more than he can chew? Weeks of torment for the Pharaoh will also come to an end as he gets to face the evil Demon known as Clyde. Since Shanghaied, Clyde has recreated the Plagues of Egypt to drive Pharaoh to the brink of insanity, and after numerous scuffles they’ll finally get to meet one on one. Will Pharaoh avenge the Demon’s acts? Or does Clyde have an extra special trick up his sleeve... The next match could go down as one of PCW’s most brutal, visceral and hard fought matches ever. It isn’t about egos, Titles, mis-trust... It’s about pure, red blooded revenge. With Sandy Wong dead, all Skylar Green has is the chance to hurt the man we all know was responsible for her death, Violence Jack. With the emotions running rampant, expect both men to try and modify the stipulation, but one thing will stay the same... That this match will truly be something special. Then... The Main Event. In the days following Shanghaied, Xavier Kannon announced that himself, Akiyama and PCW World Champion Snow would be automatic entrants. 17 men and women then battled through a gruelling qualifying match the earn their spot... Only for Ellie~! to interject herself and make the match a 30 Man Battle Royal. Today she announced the additions of Tim Walden, Teresa Tomas and Dorn Tracer into the mix to make the current confirmed field.... Snow, DarkStar, Akiyama, Xavier Kannon, The Pharaoh, Clyde, “The Malice Man” Duke Williams, Totally Tubular Tommy, Rent-a-Hero, Violence Jack, Skylar Green, Tim Walden, Teresa Tomas, Venneman Pendragon, Dante Jackson, Gaz “The Jester” Green, Nerezza, Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor, El Romantico, Brianna Washington, Skitzo, Chet King, Mark Makonie, Dorn Tracer ...25 wrestlers. Who will the other 5 men be? Are they new signings or returning stars? It looks like we won’t find out until the match itself. It has also been rumoured that a Tag Title and Rising Star (Or, as Dorn Tracer has renamed it, “Shooting Star”) Title match may still be added, but as for now, that’s how the card for March Madness stands.
