Transactions Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2002 ---- A couple signings and releases to point out. Da Guvna and William S. Powell, collectively known as Politically Incorrect, have been signed to PCW from NTICW. "They are an extremely talented tag team," says spokesman Pimply Faced Intern Kid. "They could be just what we need to save the disaster that is the tag team division." The duo will make their PCW debut next Friday night against Asylum members Darkstar and Bobby Stormbringer. Also, PCW officials have announced that they have released both Jerry Campbell and Bob from their contracts. If any more moves are made, we'll let you know.
