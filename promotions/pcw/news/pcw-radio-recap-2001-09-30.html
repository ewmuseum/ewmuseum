PCW Radio Recap

30 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Radio Recap Posted by Sandy Wong on 30 Sep 2001 ---- Malice was sub-par on September 29, but we all owe a lot to Duke for keeping the show together and getting the job done despite circumstances beyond his control. -At the top of the show, Duke reviewed all of the recent results in the Lethal Lottery tournament. - He highlighted many current events in PCW, such as the return of Prisoner 61185. -Duke runs down upcoming LL matches for Sabotage. -An unknown caller called to talk about boners. -Another caller said nothing of importance. BODA CALLED!! -Duke says Boda deserves to be WORLD champion! -Boda speaks about the possibility of leaving the Red Army. -Lots of “Ivan Fears Boda”. -Boda talks about Ivan spending time with his family. -Duke says Ivan is losing his hold on the Red Army. END OF BODA CALL -Duke runs down a few TT matches. ORACLE CALLED!! -Oracle talked about being in the RS title match, and then about Brandi. END OF ORACLE CALL -Boda called again, but they didn’t talk about anything important. MEXI CALLED!! -Mexi and Duke talked about Mexi’s name change. -They argued over if Greed Inc was fair. Duke said he couldn’t recall a single instance where Greed Inc has broken the rules. END OF MEXI CALL -Duke was pretty much waiting for the minutes to tick off, so he could get off the air. He brought us out with some country music...Joy! PCW Malice will return next Saturday night at 11:59 pm EST, so be there!
