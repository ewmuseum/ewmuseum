PCW End of Year Awards Results 2002 Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Dec 2002 ---- Greetings friends, let's get to the results without futher delay... Manager/Valet of the Year: #1: Ellie #2: The Void Eating Duck #3: Cyclonica Feud of the Year: #1: Xavier Kannon vs Tyler Nelson #2: El Diablo vs Flaymin Aymin #3: Xavier Kannon vs Jeremy Howard PCW Stable of the Year: #1: Front Office #2: The Ultimate Authority #3: Asylum PCW Moment of the Year: #1: Jeremy Howard's Retirement Return #2: Nelson/KW forming alliance Turmoil after Shanghai'ed #3: Kannon/Ellie Wedding and Reception PCW Tag Team of the Year: #1: P.W.A. #2: Politically Incorrect #3: Cowabunga Connection, Duzza and Makonie, The Golden Game Most Improved Wrestler of the Year 2002: #1: Tim Walden #2: Syko the Goblin and El Diablo PCW Match of the Year 2002: #1: Alliances Main Event #2: Pleasentville Deathmatch - Dillan Meyers v Alan Kreigman #3: Salvation in the Cell - Xavier Kannon v Tyler Nelson Face of the Year 2002: #1: Big Alan Steele #2: Duke Williams and Jeremy Howard Heel of the Year 2002: #1: Xavier Kannon #2: Tyler Nelson and Akiyama Biggest Dissapointment of the Year 2002: #1: "Controversial" Corey Williams as World Champion #2: KW, Howard, Mexi, Boda, Nelson all leaving the fed within three months of each other #3: The New Phantom Phive Greatest Tag Team Ever 2002: #1: P.W.A. #2: The Blood Brothers #3: Goovil Greatest Face Ever 2002: #1: Jeremy Howard #2: Kid Wonder #3: Big Alan Steele and Alexander Miyagawa Greatest Heel Ever 2002: #1: Tyler Nelson #2: Ivan Stanislav #3: Xavier Kannon PCW Wrestler of the Year 2002: #1: Xavier Kannon #2: Big Alan Steele #3: Clyde and Akiyama PCW Greatest Wrestler Ever 2002: #1: Ivan Stanislav #2: Tyler Nelson #3: Jeremy Howard
