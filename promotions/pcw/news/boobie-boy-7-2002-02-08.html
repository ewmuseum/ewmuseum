Boobie Boy #7

Boobie Boy #7 Posted by Sandy Wong on 8 Feb 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy #7 Featuring: MIYAGAWA! Hello everyone and welcome to my 7th issue. As I promised this issue will feature Miyagawa. I have been told this should be the best interview yet so scroll down and enjoy the Japo-American wrestler known as Miyagawa. 1) From what I am told you have a very large success story in PCW. A veteran to say the least tells us how you have managed to succeed where others have failed? Miya: Sucking up to the boss never hurts. Just kidding :-) Basically I had a very good feud at the start with Clyde, back in December 2000 to January 2000 finishing in the Miyagawa vs. Clyde vs. Bad Battio match at the first Shanghaied. That helped tremendously, then I managed to defeat Reverend back in his heyday with a record breaking length (at the time) rp of 24 pages in the second world title tournament. I might have gone on to loose to Meanstreak in the following round, but that was what made me in PCW. I lost against Frisco in a title shot for the world title about six weeks later, I had some personal problems at the time and that rather sent me flying out of it. So basically to finally answer your question, I took the chances and rolled the dice, and this is where I am now. I haven’t exactly been what anyone would call a main eventer for a long time like I used to be, but there is a lot more competition now than there has ever been. 2) You are part of the group Asylum with members Howard, and Kannon, and before his death Darkstar. Can you comment on each of these superstars? Miya: Well apparently I'm supposed to be the "leader" of Asylum because I'm the only original member (or close to it, as Asylum was originally a Tag Team, I'm an original Asylum Stable member) I've made Howard the actual leader because he's a lot more forward in shows. I've never really had the ability to go out and do a lot of original in card stuff. But Jeremy is great, we talk a lot about ideas on AIM and so forth, and we've got a big thing coming up for Asylum that will probably mean more people know about the stable than the fed in PTC at least anyway. When the Dream Team first came around I thought "Christ, here comes a waste of space" and well they were... except for Jeremy Howard. I never really paid much attention to him until about his sixth defense of the Rising Star belt, and only then did we become good friends. I trust Howard more than anyone, although it has got me into trouble before, I still do it today. Kannon I don't know much about, and I still don't. It was Howard's suggestion to bring him in, and I said yes because I trust him. His rp's are great and his NTICW record stands for itself, so he's good. Better than most of the crap we've had in the stable anyway. Darkstar... well... I've got to say something nice, right? Well Darkstar and I talk a lot on AIM again, and he fits in the stable better than Dan Hollywood did anyway, so that's a good thing. For some reason since TCM left PCW the last time it seems to have become a case where if you talk to me a lot over AIM, you're in with a shot. BUT THAT DOES NOT MEAN I AM GIVING ZURICK OR RICH CANNON A PLACE. I doubt Kreller would thank me if I did that. 3) Currently Jason Snow is the World Champion. It seems where ever you wander someone else has a comment on him. What are yours? Who do you believe will beat him? Miya: He's an alright chap out of character, I've always like him, and I've always preferred the heel Jason Snow. In fact even when he claimed he was face, he was still the heel Jason Snow. Howard vs Snow was face vs. heel, not face vs. lesser face like New Breed tried to claim at the time. Snow has never been a face, he can't be a face because an egotistical man would have to loose the ego in order to go face, and that removes the entire character. Everyone said how much of an impact Snow was in become Champion because it's like a "new wave" champion. To me, Howard was always the first new wave champion because he joined properly only in March 2001. Snow wasn't too long after that. The only big thing about Snow is that he's the first champion to have come through the PWA, and that is a big thing, that's such a big thing that it is what should be publicized.**Editors note-IT IS NOW** Going through the PWA does work, and it does help to take the raw talent and make it good. In fact, with Snow, Ron was annoyed when he sent him to PCW2 instead of PCW at the time after reading his first rp there. As for who will beat him... well, with 20 men in it, he's going to loose at March Madness, although he's going to hammer Timo into the ice-age in his first defense. Timo complained when he didn't get a shot against Howard's title reign; so at least he gets a shot. Hopefully I'll get past Clyde and get into the Madness Brawl for it All, but I expect to come third, because that is one time I am talented at (coming third in everything). I don't know who will beat Snow at Madness, but I think it will happen. Just don't expect him to take it lying down, because I expect Snow to get even better before the end of the year. 4) Tyler Nelson despite loosing the Presidency to Duke Williams still has a firm grip in PCW. It is obvious that he has few fans but he does have power. What do you think should be done about this? Miya: I should be Commish. Commish Miyagawa would be great for the fans... this is what I would do: #1: As with Ice Hockey games, I’d give out fridge magnets. #2: Between matches have little games in the rings featuring fans in order to make it a cheery family day out. #3: I'd sign JJ back as Assistant Commish. #4: Fire t-shirts into the crowds with mini-bazookas like they do at the Ave's games. #5: And install good cheer by running occasional fancy dress competitions where the fans can dress up as their favorite wrestlers, with the prizes being that those wrestlers will come down to the ring and have their photos taken with the winners. 5) There seems to be an over abundance of new guys always coming in from either PWA, or NTICW, or other places. Who do you believe is the most valuable rookie, and has shown their worth so far? Miya: I don't think there is one. There are so many new people coming in at the moment that none of the new guys are getting any sort of chance to shine through. PCW needs breathing room, at least two weeks of it where NO ONE is signed. Not even if Goldberg wanted to rp as himself should he be signed. Basically let PCW settle for a couple of weeks and find out who is doing what and just stop signing people just for the sake of it. 6) What is the deal with your new haircut? Midlife crisis or something? Miya: I just tried to get it like my own. Especially the receding hairline, that's very accurate (even considering that Miyagawa is five years older than I am). Basically it was just because a new more accurate hairstyle was released, so I switched to it. 7) What was your greatest moment as a wrestler? Miya: Now that's a hard one. I'd probably say becoming #1 Contender to the world title by beating Meanstreak, Slade and Howard Porter. I screwed up the actual shot at the thing, so that was a big thing. Either that or winning the Cruiserweight title for the first time, mainly because I managed to successfully defend it, something I've never managed to do since. 8) The big topic around the locker room lately has been OSW… what are your thoughts on that and any chance of Miyagawa gracing the screen of OSW anytime soon? If not who do you believe will be going? Miya: Well unfortunately I know exactly who is going. And I can tell you that there are people who are still yet to go who are still on the PCW roster. It's a bit hard to speculate when you know exactly what's happening :-) I can also tell you that the original plan was to have everyone go at the Shanghaied PPV, which was why I never planned anyway to continue the Miya-Ivan feud... and led to me being quite confused afterwards. As for Miyagawa going there... well I'm rather disappointed that I've never even been asked to go. Actually, no, that's a lie. I at one point was going to go to NTICW when it reopened under Yoder, Ron even said yes for 24 hours, but he changed his mind because he felt that PCW wouldn't be PCW without (he listed five people or so) and one of them he said was me, so that was probably why he didn't ask me if I wanted to go. 9) There are so many feds in PTC now I can’t even keep track. What are you favorite feds and who are some wrestlers from those feds that impress you? Miya: Oh, and just to clarify that on #8. If you haven't been asked to go, you are not going. So people, stop aiming me asking if you're being sent there. To be honest, I haven’t followed PTC almost at all. I know vaguely about GWF, but only because I speak with the wrestlers there. I hear that Stewart Hix is in one of the feds and I found the "Canuck Heat Zone" to be probably the most unusual name for an efed in world history. But that's about as up to speed as I am. I wasn't up to speed with Fed wars either, so there's no real change. 10) What are some of your favorites? (Movies, food, Books, TV Show, Comic Book Hero, car ETC) Miya: Trying to read Lord of the Rings at the moment, but I've only read the third chapter of the first book. As for movies, I went to see Black Hawk Down last Friday night, and I'm off to see From Hell this Saturday. I'm a huge Science Fiction freak; I've even been to Star Trek conventions where I met Avery Brooks (who incidentally, is quite, quite crazy... I mean really very very mad.) And a Babylon 5 convention where I met Claudia Christian (prior to her playboy spred which somehow aged her by 25 years in the photos). I've a huge FarScape mark, and am pleased as punch that the Sci-Fi channel have confirmed another three seasons of it. As for other stuff... well Red Dwarf will be excellent (the new series/movie that is), Smallville gets better each week and the new series of Frasier is great. 11) What has been your most embarrassing moment in your wrestling career? Miya: Loosing to Bad Battio. No, loosing to Stewart Hix is probably worse. 12) As many know you are married to the lovely Natalie Miyagawa. Any chance of a little Isawa running around the house soon? Miya: She's pregnant at the moment in fact. She's coming up to 12 weeks now, so the first medical exam is quickly approaching. I guess the writer had better get some research done :-) Matt: Congrats Miya: It hasn't been brought up much, because until the 12 week mark they don't check the kid because of the probability of miscarriage. 13) On the road between events who do you usually travel with? Miya: My long time traveling partner & personal jobber Steve Jabowski (although insiders will note that because of his marriage to one Stephanie Austin, it actually makes him Steve Austin-Jabowski). The history goes that Steve and Miya used to be in the same Indy fed together, and were actual rivals, but after suffering major memory loss at the hands of Miyagawa, they became friends, and when his memory returned, they stayed friends. He's a 60 year old fool who thinks he still looks good in a pair of those wrestling underpants... he's basically a piss take of some well known old wrestlers in the wrestling circuit. Has only once appeared in a PCW Card when Miyagawa, Bang Davenport and Trashcan Man were fighting Clyde, Ed "The Mack" and the Lightning Kid. He brought Bob the steel chair (The Mack used a different one) down to the ring, and TCM smacked Ed in the head with it, enabling Bang to hit his super kick finisher on him and roll him up for the win. 14) Everyone knows that behind the scenes alliances are different than what occurs in the ring…anyone that you feud with in the ring are close with you out? Miya: Ivan and I are buddies. In fact if you feud with someone, it's harder to make a decent feud if you don't get on well backstage, simply because there's no interaction between the two parties. I anticipate a Kid Wonder vs. Miyagawa feud within the next couple of months now that he's gone heel... heck in character, they've been at each other's throats even while both faces, just now it'll make a better feud. Tyler I speak to all the time, mainly because I have to in order to keep him informed about the site, and because he's a good bloke too. Because I'm staff I tend to speak to most of the roster most of the time... although Hank keeps messaging me cryptic things about "MIYAGAWA RULES~!" and things like that... I think he's trying to get into Asylum on the basis that if JJ and Syko can, he can. Just to think that I was thinking about tagging with him until he won the Extreme Title and gained credibility. ;-) Miya: I own a PS2, and I tend to play that a fair bit. For some reason I just got into Championship Manager 01/02 again too, which I hadn't played since October. Tony Hawks 3 is the latest one on the PS2, and I've already got Metal Gear Solid 2 preordered for when it comes out here on the eighth. Matt: MGS2 is a great game…and might I recommend Final Fantasy 10. Miya: I played 7 through 9, just 10 hasn't come out here yet either. 15) Do you have any regrets in your life? If yes what are they? Miya: Regrets? Well I went to a particularly crappy college where I got bad grades in the first year which meant that even though in the second year I got qualifying grades, I had to go to a rather naff University that takes me an hour and a half to travel to instead of one which is one of the top Unis in the country and takes ten minutes to get to. I partially regret doing Computer after Secondary School at all, and wish I had done history instead... then again had that happened I probably would never have become involved in PCW at all (or efeds for that matter). 16) If you were on a deserted island and could bring one thing what would it be? Miya: Everyone says a book, or a person. I say a frickin motorboat so I could leave the desert island. The only person who ever wanted to spend time on one was Tom Hanks, and that was because he was paid to. I'm a fat man, and I've seen Lord of the Flies... I don't want to be no Piggy. Matt: Actually Ron said that same answer in my last interview. Miya: We must think alike... probably both plotting world domination through specially trained ninja mice too. 17) When should we expect you to be holding up the PCW World Title? Miya: It depends if I flunk this year's World Title shot or not. If nothing comes up in the week before March Madness, then bet on me... if the usual stuff happens, don't. 18) You wrestled in GWF a bit, what was it like to wrestle in a place other than PCW? Miya: Well I wrestled in EW too, just I found out after it happened. It's interesting, because it's completely fresh. The general rping isn't as good in GWF is as it is in PCW, but there are people like Crichton (who I did pick to win anyway) who would make it in PCW anyway. Crichton's rps for the Streetfight were just as good as mine, except he did four and I only got two in before the deadline because I came down with the flu and just stayed in bed all day. As I see it, GWF is PCW quality around the time that I joined, in other words, give them six months and they'll be big. Not just big because they have lots of "quality" names that some feds seem to tout that aren’t worth a shit, but because they will make their own quality. 19) What’s the deal with your PTC column “The Bastard Speaks”? Miya: Fed wars had Ron portray a guy called the Ump who was eternally hated. I'm just doing the same thing for PTC that's all. Everyone needs a columnist who marks for the only person everyone hates, and then insults everyone else... besides, it's fun to write. 20) Ok now for Word Association: The Bastard Speaks: Fucking "A" New Breed: HEELS! STOP SAYING THEY'RE FACES! World Title: I'll probably come third at Madness. Old School Wrestling: Nice they stole the name from a stable I came up with :-) Being a Daddy: Great unless we do a typical wrestling thing and it end's up not being true And finally do you have any thing to follow up with? Comments quotes, questions? Miya: Yeah... there are several things that are certain in life... Miya will always come third... Dan is a Douche and THE NEW BREED ARE HEELS~! Boobie Boy would like to thank Miyagawa for living up to all expectations and being a down right nice guy. In other news yours truly would like to remind all to contact me with interest for an interview as well as news for PTC. Next Column will feature Zurrick as well as a few guests. Till then, keep on ROCKIN!
