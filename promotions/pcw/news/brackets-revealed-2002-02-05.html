5 February 2002

Brackets Revealed Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Feb 2002 ---- For those of you who thought these were the PTC brackets...SHAME ON YOU!! Tuesday afternoon, PCW officials released the brackets for the upcoming tournament. The tournament, unlike any other in PCW history, will not determine a champion of some sort. Instead, 16 survivors from the tournament will go on to compete in a 20 man battle royal at March Madness for the World title. They will join Snow, Tyler Nelson, and two as of yet unannounced participants. Also, those that were to recieve byes before will no longer have them. "I believe we found more than suitable opponents for people like Miyajobber," said PCW CEO Tyler Nelson. "And we didn't even have to go into the extreme jobbers like Duke Williams and Jeremy Howard." Nelson continued on. "Notice how both Howard and Duke were left out," bragged Nelson. "Duke can't pick both of them. And I sure as hell ain't picking either of them. So it appears that he has a little situation on his hands." Duke Williams couldn't be reached for comment. Below are the mini-brackets for the tournament. The winners of the 16 mini-brackets advance to March Madness. PCW wishes al participants the best of luck. Second round matches will take place on the 19th and 22nd. Ten, on Febuary 26th, a live drawing will be held on Turmoil to determine the order of entrance for the battle royal on March 3rd. If a wrestler is unable to compete due to injury, their opponent will recieve a bye into te next round. If a person does a no-show without inorming a staff member, they will suffer serious consequences.
