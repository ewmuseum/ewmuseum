Soaring Towards A Lawsuit

24 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On the first Tuesday Turmoil since PCW reopened, fans bore witness to the debut of the Mega Soaring Arrows, Japan’s premier tag team. The pair of blue chippers, the blue-eyed Ukyo Matsuura and the silent but deadly Basho Amuro, won fans over with their unique style of puroresu with their own innovative maneuvers. The response they received during their match with Products of the System did not go unheard, and members of the company are pleased with the product they bring to the ring. Expect to see more of them… except in the recent shows to come. After their appearance on PCW, they were asked to return to Japan to clear up a number of legal matters. When wrestling in Japan, Matsuura and Amuro were backed by numerous sponsors, but since their departure from Japan they’ve made legal troubles for the pair that could cost them grave funds. Also, the recording label representing the band Baroque, is making trouble for the MSA, saying that their use of Baroque’s song “Uta” was an unregistered use of the recording. Ukyo and Basho were caught at the airport before their flight by Sandy Wong, and were willing to answer questions. “This is BULLSH-T!” Ukyo exclaimed loudly in the airport, drawing looks from security and passengers. “We’re good friends with Baroque, and they said it’s fine to use “Uta”! Now bastard suits wanna be bitches about it cause we left Japan for America. We’re not the corporate puppets we used to be, so now they’re gonna screw us outta success here! We won’t stand for injustice!” While mentioning their legal troubles, Wong also brought up the assault and battery charges held against them now being brought to the open by two Japanese members of the press. At hearing this, Ukyo clutched his head in frustration as his partner Basho growled lowly. “This… this is nothing but bullsh-t. You hear me? BULLSH-T!!” After that outburst, a security officer asked him to calm down. He eased himself and continued. “Japan is being a sore loser because they lost us, the future of wrestling they proclaimed us to be. Now after seeing how good we did here, they’re gonna piss us off by any means! Just because we’re Tom Arnolds!” Wong thought about that statement for a moment, and corrected Ukyo, saying the name was BENEDICT… not Tom. Ukyo looked at Basho before shrugging his shoulders, pouting his pierced lip. “Whatever.” MSA said they’d be gone for the second Turmoil, and most likely the third, but promised the fans that they’d return for the fourth show ready for a fight. Details on the Mega Soaring Arrows’ legal matters will be posted on the PCW website as they occur.
