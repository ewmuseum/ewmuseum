Abner Fired/Hired

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Abner Fired/Hired Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Dec 2002 ---- After Adrenaline, the jousting between Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams continued when the CEO set about weakening Duke’s new redneck alliance immediately. “It’s my pleasure to announce that Abner’s contract with PCW has been terminated,” boasted Kannon, “He broke clause 45F, subsection E in his contract when he sold one of his t-shirts to a fan thus contravening the PCW marketing and likeness rights agreement.”. However, Duke was quick to counter. “It’s MY pleasure to announce that I have hired Abner as my ‘Director of Redneck Affairs’, and since he’s on my personal staff, that fag can’t fire him again!” retorted the President, “And lets just say this hoss is givin’ him a good deal right outa the PCW account!” With the match between the pair about to be announced, the Abner incident should be just another catalyst for what is sure to be a brutal encounter.
