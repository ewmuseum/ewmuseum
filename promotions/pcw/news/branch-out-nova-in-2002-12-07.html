Branch Out, Nova In

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Branch Out, Nova In Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Dec 2002 ---- It was confirmed late last night that Michelle Branch has been stripped of her role as PCW’s official singer/song writer... The main reason being given for the move is because she’s dead. After the attack by PCW ref and Prep hater, the Dark Gimmick, Miss Branch was rushed to hospital where Doctors were unable to saves the 1 arm, half a leg and 3 organs that arrived in the Ambulance after the dark referee’s attack. This morning the strange events continued, when it was revealed that Eleanor Hall had PCW’s Personnel Director, Brandi Wilson, sign Dark Gimmick to a wrestler’s contract so she can, quote, “Like, SO kick his bony ass and stuff... PREP STYLE!” So the wrestler v referee match scheduled for Tuesday between Ellie~!’s husband Xavier, and the Gimmick is now an official wrestler v wrestler match. PCW officials wasted no time in naming Michelle’s successor, in the form of Bermudan songstress Heather Nova, singer of such works as “I Have The Touch” from the Craft soundtrack, as well as “Walk This World”, “London Rain” and “Throwing Fire At The Sun”. Miss Nova is expected to be at Adrenaline where she will be introduced to the wrestlers. There is also talk that a new version of Heather’s “I’m The Girl” will be recorded to replace Ellie~!’s current theme, “Bang!” by the Yeah Yeah Yeahs. PCW officials gave their sympathies to Miss Branch’s family, shortly before finding a legal loophole through which they couldn’t be prosecuted.
