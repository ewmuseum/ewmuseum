A Statement by Chris Chase

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Statement by Chris Chase Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Mar 2002 ---- Coming off the back of Fusion with his back in tatters, 'The Messiah' Chris Chase, known by other nicknames such as 'The Celestial Cowboy' and 'That Arrogant Chase', is as expected...peeved at the annoyance caused to him by long-time enemy...Chris Sloboda, the PCW World Heavyweight Champion. "The Bounced Czech? What kind of moronic and deranged moniker is that?" said Chase in response to the question asked on the move applied on him in the latter moments of last night's Friday Fusion. "Boda-Boy is, well, making up more than just a little for his obvious shortcomings by attempting to eliminate the man that gifted him with that title in the first place, the man that beat him way back in PCW2. He hasn't changed one bit since I beat him the last time, he's making the same idle threats, he's making the same bonehead mistakes that got him ridiculed when he turned Communist. How could I have even of fallen for that same old Bounced Czech move? I'll tell you 'how'...some damn cretin shone a light in my eye...or was that the awe-inspiring aura that I tend to give off?" Author's Note - Sure...yes, rant all you want Chris Chase...(sigh). Chase grabs his ribs as he feels a twinge, and then smacks the nearest object, which happens to be a water cooler, with a hard right-hander. "Do you folks realise just what a mess this imbecile is causing? Everything has gone down the pan since Boda became World Champion. Buy rates dropped...and people were begging Dale Earnhardt to run the bastard over. And now, if there's only one person who can bring an end to all of this...it just has to be...well, me. I can bring an end to the unstoppable swirling vortex of boredom...known as Boda-Boy. That self-righteous jackass..." Mumbling other stuff along those lines, a rather angry Chris Chase walks off...leaving this sorry interviewer in a rather shaken state of affairs. Before we leave...we managed to get word from Edward the Strolling Minstrel, currently providing musical accompaniment to Chase's travels... "Boda...thou art a Charlatan, a pretender...twixt reality and a dream. Boda thou will jobbeth to the Messiah."
