Mexi's Moment #2

Mexi's Moment #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Jan 2002 ---- Now once again it appears the columnists have disappeared so I your friendly Mexican is here to step it up with yet another crappy column but hey, at least I’m writing one. Shooter, I’m gonna do what you hate the most and give my picks on Shanghaied here but I don’t care what you think. If you want to ream me out in your next column then good cuz it will mean you actually wrote another one. Now before I get to the main content of this column will inDEED be PPV picks, I fell like I need to shoot on a few things, the first being the state of PCW. Now PCW ever since I arrived in the summer has been going strong, with a solid main event scene, solid mid card and lots of new talent scrapping it out in the low card to prove they could hack it. Now however with OSW starting up PCW will have an even more depleted Main event scene. I know things most of you don’t know when it comes to OSW, one of the perks of doing the poser there, and let me just state that PCW will lose talent. Talent in my opinion it can’t really afford to lose right now. I have never been a selfish person, I welcomed back the return of Meanstreak when he came back and helped out the main event for a while despite the belief otherwise of the #1 fan who has disappeared recently. I do not think that the stars that are leaving PCW for OSW will be a good thing for PCW, it will be a good thing for me personally but not for PCW. Why you ask am I being so critical about myself as a possible main event candidate? Pretty simple really. It’s not my time and I’m not yet believable. The keys to having a solid main event is to have guys there that are not just good Rper’s and hard workers but have incredible heat. If your main event guys get no reaction from the crowd then it’s not a main event. Yes I have heat but it’s not a definite face/heel heat, it’s that type of heat you get after doing some really bad things and apologizing for it. I will welcome the challenge to main event if that’s the case and it looks as if it’s only a matter of time but I strongly say that with the guys up there leaving a huge hole will be left, one that will be a task to fill. Now getting off of PCW for a second let me talk about OSW, which is looking great. As I said I know all the hidden names, and think they will be great for OSW. The site looks great, I’d say the posers look great:) and most importantly Ron is heading it up. Unlike NTICW when he gave it to Yoder and it did alright till it’s abrupt end Ron isn’t take that chance again with OSW. He’s leaving PCW in Kreller and Nelson’s capable hands and heads up OSW himself. I have known Ron for a while and yet only recently started to have actual conversations with him, about the same time I started on as staff and let me say to those of you that haven’t had the chance he’s a great guy, and knows his stuff. Simply seeing that he was heading up OSW was enough for me to know it would become a success, seeing the roster just made it a no doubter. Now on to NTICW, which I will be heading up with Xavier Kannon and Timo Bolambo. The idea behind this is solid, not just another fed but a finishing school for students that need to tweak there gimmicks and learn how to work angles in the big feds. The way I understand RPing will be a focus but not the primary focus as the majority of wrestlers here will have the skills for one of the big two feds, but just don’t have that final aspect which they will get in NTICW. I have helped many a person with angles or gimmicks, just look at Tim Walden, and I look forward to the task at hand and I know Timo and Kannon are two. NTICW will produce future stars in PCW and OSW and is a great idea to help those that would not get into PCW or OSW right now and have learnt as much as possible from PWA. Enough about that, I’ve gotten my talk about OSW and NTICW out of my system and now it’s time to get back to PCW business. Now I do plan to run down the roster soon, but I’ll wait till OSW gets running before I go through ten or more people that won’t be in PCW a couple weeks from now. What I will say is it’s time for my ppv run down of what looks like one of the better PPV’s in a long time. Shanghaied Pot O’ Gold Match There is a lot of young talent in this match and also a lot of names just thrown in for bodies. All and all this match comes down to Paddy, Davis and perhaps Asa. A few of these guys are already in a higher division so it wouldn’t make sense for them to win this, although with the winner having to defend the title later in the net I could be completely off. In the end it won’t matter because who ever wins this will lose it later. Rising Star Title match So much can happen hear because who knows who the third man or woman in this will be. Red Rock and D-Kay are in but does it matter who they face? No it doesn’t and it’s only a matter of time before D-Kay has the strap on him. Red Rock would make a good champ but D-Kay would be better. The tag titles, Man vs Machine Well Nelson’s revealed that AFL-CIO was in his pocket, he may think it was a swerve but it was one of the more predictable ones coming. I see another one coming and it involves Nelson but not this match so I’ll just keep that to myself. However I see the bots getting turned away from the titles again, and probably fired by Nelson. There has to be something in AFL-CIO for siding with him and considering this is a LAST TIME match, it tells me Twisted Metal is out of PCW. Four Corners Extreme Match Thank God I aborted an angle that was planned for this division, for once Walden was right and I’d like to publicly thank him for waking me up. I won’t go into detail but let me just say that Brandi has been one of the best things for the extreme division since Ed. The belt has a problem of jumping ship and losing value. Yes it’s a low card belt and it gives people hope if they can think they could win it. It was brought up on the boards what the most competitive division in PCW aside from the World title is and even with the World Title included I would say the Extreme is by far the most competitive. Let alone it has the most people wanting to hold it because of the packed lower card but with Brandi holding the belt and everyone with a penis trying to get it off her it raises the title to a level it hasn’t been seen at. Brandi has had a good run and I hope that her dropping the title at Shanghaied doesn’t discourage her. Now Corey Williams has the most RPing skill in this match but is inconsistent. Big D and HANK~! Generally put out a good amount of RP’s yet focus too much on sex and excessive tilde use. I think everyone would love to see HANK~! Rewarded with a title for all his months of jobbing but if that happens, would the already announced match on Turmoil become a title shot? I could see that happening and it would be an easy way to get the next big PCW monster a belt in a division that would suit a crazed inmate. For that reason alone I look to see HANK~! Win and then job the title to Matt Leblanc on Turmoil. Cruiserweight Title Kannon = The Future and no that’s not a reference to Ryan Knox or Jake Keeton, but a compliment. The guy has undeniable talent and had NTICW closed earlier when he was the champion and received the same type of push Maxwell had, he would have stuck in the main event scene. That wasn’t to be though and he’s now on his way to a lengthy cruiserweight reign. Borro, and Chase are both solid Rper’s but it doesn’t match up to Kannon. Kannon will restore the CW title to the level it once was at when Kid Wonder, Frisco and Miya all fought for that belt but it won’t be just because he’s so DAMN good, it will be because his competition is too. This is only the start of things to come from three top cruiserweights. Television Title Match Aymin vs Walden, at first it looks like a bit of a yawner but both are capable of putting out good work, at times great. Walden is a work rate away from great things and seems destined to stay in the TV division if he doesn’t. Even with Walden’s work rate is quality is good enough to compete and this will likely end in either a International Incident Screw Job or a foiled one. The Pleasantville Death Match I could piss Dan off by not talking about this match like everyone else but I’m a man of the people and Dan’s a person too so I will talk about it. Dan’s retirement segment was one to be remembered there’s not doubt about that but I feel it was done early and faded into a solid PPV card. There could have been an occasional segment here and there to hype the match a bit but there was nothing. The match itself will be good, but the lead up could have been much better. To me this seems like a send off match that should have been on the show following Kriegman’s Retirement party but then there is still a couple shows where either man could remind us of what they have done together or the reason for this match aside from an official send off. Playboy bunny match for the Continental Title Well this feud has been going on forever and both men have fed off each other and raised the level. It started at “Back to Old School” over the Cruiserweight title and has escalated to the Continental Title. In the past it was Mexi holding the title and losing it to Chet at the PPV, and then moving up in the card. This time it’s Chet’s turn to drop the belt and move up the card filling in for a depleted main event. The more interesting story here will be does Bill Clinton make an appearance in the match? First Death Match The end of another long feud? Perhaps not. Both these men hate each other and are capable of many sick and twisted things. I see both dying and then PCW’s first ever ghost feud begins but as to who dies first, I think Clyde gets the nod to proceed directly to Hell, does not collect $200 dollars for passing go. If you’re squeamish don’t read this match. Drop from Heavens Match An error in booking and thus won’t get much talk, all I can say is it’s BAS vs Aki again on a PPV, this time the Continental Title isn’t on the line. Will this finally put an end to this lengthy feud or will it continue on? I for one hope it dies because BAS’s and Aki’s careers are going nowhere with this and both are capable of more. National Pride Match This match popped up because neither man had anything going for the PPV but it has gained momentum fast. With out a doubt both men are capable of excellent work and both vets of PCW. This will likely be the last match of night where it’s a clean one on one fight and I expect a good match. Ivan is well Ivan and is the PCW heel contingency right now, we’ll see how long that lasts as I’m willing to bet whatever money I have that Nelson has something evil planned Shanghaied, but I’ll get to that later. Miya is a man in need of a feud and proving that he can handle the biggest monster in PCW is a great start, but I don’t see anything long term happening after Miya pulls the huge upset and keeps the monster down. World Title Match Teacher vs Student could be a more appropriate name for this match as Snow has finally worked his way into the main event scene. Part of this is his undeniable talent but part is the depleted main event scene. Snow is the logical choice to succeed Howard as champ but it’s still to soon and although one day Snow will become World Champ, it’s not Jan. 27th. Howard is the best man in PCW right now and there is only one man, maybe two that can compete with him right now and neither are in the World Title hunt right now. Ivan we all know he can compete, Timo when he’s on his top has beaten the champ on the boards but it wasn’t his time and isn’t, yet. Right now it’s Howard’s time and it will be for a while to come. He is the hardest working champion in PCW that I have seen. Albeit I’ve only been here since the first time he won the belt it’s still an accomplishment to out work Ivan. PCW looks solid with him as our champion, and it would be a mistake to take it off him this early in his reign. Snow will have other chances. PCW President Match Duke will become president, there is no doubt about that but will it be like we all hope or will it be another Nelson power play. What everyone seems to forget when PCW reopened Duke was one of Nelson’s men in Greed Inc. Nelson has never been a man with out a plan, he tricked everyone with the Phantom Booker being Knox, he masterminded Media Inc. and is the most powerful man in PCW. There is no way Nelson doesn’t have a plan to keep the power in PCW. I smell the end of Nelson/O.G/Awfa and the dawn of Nelson and Duke and a few other surprises. I don’t want to give away too much because I know for a fact that PCW won’t be the same after this night. Last Thoughts on Shanghaied What only one clusterfuck? What is the booking staff thinking? Obviously they are because it was getting annoying. Yes everyone wants to be in the PPV but a PPV should be a reward to those that have worked good feuds and built heat and worked hard, not just because your name is on the roster. Shanghaied was the start of the Media era last year and I expect nothing less then a new era to start on this night. In all likelihood it will be a chance for the new talent to shine and for several guys below the main event level to take the first step into leading PCW. Once it’s revealed of just who is jumping ship it will become apparent that a big gaping hole is left in PCW’s main event scene and there is going to be a lot of chances for guys to shine. Those that make the best of it will be the names that we fear losing. People come and go, and Ron knew this that’s why he created the PWA to raise talent instead of turning it away. On Jan.27 despite what I think Snow could become the first man to complete the circuit, starting out in the PWA in March 2001, for brief stint and then off to PCW2 several days later. He moved up to PCW in July and now Jan.27 he has a chance to win the World title. As I said it’s only a matter of time, but I think it will be a bit more time till he becomes the first PWA grad to win the World title. Mexi’s Mark outs Just in case I make this a regular thing I might as well make it look like a column. In this section here I will give props to one unsung hero. This column the distinction goes to one of the unappreciated champions PCW has ever had. Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard is arguably the best champion ever in PCW and his reign is only a month old yet already he has defended the belt against more people then the previous champs. He has put a RP up everyday since he won the title and continues to produce quality work. There hasn’t been a more deserving man to hold the belt and I’m not sure there will be again. Krayzie should be the man everyone tries to be not the guy to take cheap shots at because he has the World Title. He has worked to get that title and anyone that says he doesn’t deserve it has their head about six feet up their own ass. Congrats Jeremy, you are Mexi’s Mark Out until I write another column, which could be a long while. Mexi’s Advice I don’t like to bitch and complain and say I hate so and so like all these other columnists. That’s fine if they like to but I choose to help by giving advice. This column my advice goes to Six Finger Cisco. Lay off the Simpson quotes in your RP’s. After I read a couple of yours for your match with Walden and Klaymore I felt like I had just watched two bad episodes of the Simpson’s. Maybe it’s just me, I can only speak for myself but when I say that it was a force to read, I’m not joking. Don’t take it to personally as this isn’t a place to rip someone apart it’s a place to give advice so my advice to you Cisco is an occasional reference to a Simpson’s joke is fine, but fill a RP up and it becomes a cheesy rip off. Change or don’t it’s no skin off my back, it’s just some friendly advice from your friendly Mexican. Final Thoughts Ron aka The Phantom Booker, love him or hate him you have to respect him. He has taken a fed with the help of an excellent staff and turned it into one of the top feds on the net. Now he is starting a new fed in OSW and I’ve never been worked so hard in my life putting together a site but that just shows how dedicated he is to all of us. He has given up countless hours so that strangers, people he doesn’t even know can come and compete in a friendly environment. I’ve seen countless people bash him because he is strict and harsh but he does it for everyone of you reading this column assuming that you’re in PTC. You may think this is ass kissing and quite frankly I don’t care what you think. I’ve learnt that speaking your mind is a gift and if Ron wasn’t deserving of this praise he wouldn’t get it. Thanks for reading and I hope I don’t have to write another column but in all likelihood I will and if I do it will be a run down of the roster and how people stack up and can improve. Mexi
