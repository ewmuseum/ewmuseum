P.W.A Further Punished

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
P.W.A Further Punished Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Dec 2002 ---- Pranks With Attitude have once again been punished. After P.W.A left the arena for last Fusion, a cameraman was found lying bleeding and unconcious with his camera stuck in a very uncomfortable place. Footage reveals that the lone reporter, who wishes to remain anonymous and will be referred to as "Colin", had asked P.W.A their opinions on being stripped of their titles. At this point, P.W.A turned and Gaz gave a 240-volt punch to the camera, frying most of the electronics while Nerezza looked the strap around the neck of "Colin" and used it to ram the camera into a wall, lens-first. The result was an audio-only camera, which recorded a lot of screaming and violent noise, then suddenly went silent after one final yell of pain and a small giggling noise. After the camera had been cleaned off, the footage was recovered. A Front Office statement has been issued concerning the situation. "They just don't know when to quit! This is costing PCW money for resources and an attack of this sick, malevolent magnitude on an expensive camera is just testing our patience further." When asked about "Colin" being injured, the spokesman replied: "Who? A camera costs up to ten thousand, a person does not!"
