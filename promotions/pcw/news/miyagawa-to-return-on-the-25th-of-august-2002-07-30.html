Miyagawa to Return on the 25th of August

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Miyagawa to Return on the 25th of August Posted by Sandy Wong on 30 Jul 2002 ---- The following is an excerpt from the PCW Wrestle Vessel which will best explain it... "Loc seems happy with his Junior Heavyweight Title as he holds it aloft as he departs the ring, The End meanwhile gets to his feet as Miyagawa climbs down. The End goes to depart but Miyagawa puts his hand to End’s chest to stop him. Alex takes the microphone from Clay and begins to speak. Jeff: Oh please. Miyagawa: I’ve been thinking long and hard about this match and what I would do if I actually won it. And what with the baby being due next month, I came to a decision… The good President Mr. Williams knows what I’m about to do since he ok’ed it a few hours ago. I’m leaving PCW. Dave: What? The crowd grow silent. Jeff: YES! YES! YES! FINALLY!!! Miyagawa: But only for a month. Jeff: NO! NO! NO! NOT LONG ENOUGH! Miyagawa: I just need to clear up the problems that an old friend has caused and also to spend more time with Natalia… as I’m sure you all know, she’s due in a couple of weeks and I don’t want to miss that for all of the tea in China… or all of the rum in Jamaica, I guess I should say. A few light chuckles go throughout the crowd. Miyagawa: So this is what I’m planning to do… you see, I’ve now won the PCW Cruiserweight title a record equalling four times. For my first win I won it by winning the Kid Wonder rumble… and Kid Wonder gave me the belt.. well… hit me with it anyway. Miyagawa pauses for a few seconds and holds out the belt towards the End. Miyagawa: Skylar, when Kid Wonder gave me the belt at the end of the Kid Wonder rumble he signalled that I had become worthy finally of being a Champion, and sure enough, victories began falling at my feet. This is all I can do for you too, Skylar… PCW Cruiserweight Champion. If it’s possibly to look surprised through a black mask, then The End is sure doing it as Miyagawa places the belt over The End’s shoulder and signals that “One Step Closer” should stop being played and instead that 'Move B*tch' by Ludacris should be played instead as the duo leave with Miyagawa saying one thing as he departs… Miyagawa: See you guys when I return at Dawning on the 25th of August! Jeff: Bah, this is sickening. Dave: Well, let’s get to a word from our sponsors."
