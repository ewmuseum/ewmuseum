The Bastard Speaks #4 Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Apr 2002 ---- The Bastard Speaks 4. ~It’s about focking time. It’s been a while. I’ve been in a good mood. Bad moods are what make this column; therefore I was waiting for the right one. Expect this column to cover nothing that you particularly want and everything that comes to mind at any one point. I was sent a submission for this column, and amazingly... it’s actually pretty good. That pisses me off because I can’t call this person a slacker or a Anton or something like that... oh and Anton is the new “hip” word for when someone does good and then complains and claims he’s not until he does so little that we all have to agree with him. Anyway, without further a-due, fuck I’m speaking like a frog now... he’s the person you will know simply as... -------------------------------- The Bitch Speaks.... Well, there is a new bit to the column and if you don’t like it, the bastard will tell you to Fuck Off, and then so will I...So if you have any objections...Fuck Off and Fuck Off. First off, a topic that is so annoying it deserves being the first thing mentioned by me...Look to the start of my sentences, what do you see? That’s right, it’s called a CAPITAL LETTER! Sentences begin with them, as do months, days of the week too.....AND NAMES! So why when I look down PTC do I people signing in as ‘dave’ or ‘bob’ or ‘what does that caps lock button and the one with the arrow pointing up do’ DA FOCK? You people can come out with 250 lines...What? More Feds?...150 line....Huh? Some more? 50 line RP’s....YET YOU CAN’T PUT A CAPITAL AT THE START OF YOUR OWN NAMES! I hereby suggest that all PCW writers, bookers and staff call our “Swamp Dawg” leblanc IN EVERYTHING from now on, if that is what he calls himself...Let that be what he is called! Now, I believe that one thing combined with the above makes every RP reader want to put a cap in their head and have Goldust fuck the bullet hole and that’s COLOURED BACKGROUNDS! NO NEEEEEEED! You have one of the finest set of forums in E-Fedding yet you persist in making it look like a 4 year olds first go on a PC! “Look Mummy, I made it go Blue!”, “Wow my little 4 year old, that looks childish for even you!” DA FOCK? ( <--- Think that’ll catch on?) DA FOCK? ( <--- Familiarity making it rub off on you?) Oh, have to go to the roster page to see if we have actually gone that final step and signed “Generic Eric”.....Bye.....What? Why am I being nice to you....DA FOCK? ------------------------- “Anyway, I would like to thank my mother and father for choosing the cheaper brand of condoms all those years ago... and of course I would like to thank the academy for giving me this statuette that when I’m poor and broke in five years time and can’t get a movie roll even for the X Men’s ninth sequel or by flashing my tits yet again, I can pawn it for ten bucks, go buy myself a gun and cap myself in the head.” – What Halle should have said at the Oscars. It’s awards time again in movie land and so it is in PCW... you see the Hound has his little polls back again which means we might actually get them pure and untainted... and maybe even on time. “Best Dark Roleplay” what sort of fucking question was that.... “Darkstar/Clyde/Akiyama... pick one” would have been a better question... at least it prevented stupid people from picking whoever posted black text onto a black background in the rp board a month back. Anyway, it is PPV time again, and since this is a column, I feel I must do a run down. For what reason? To fucking take the piss you dumb son of a bitch. And fucking yes, I am going for more swear words than either South Park: Bigger Longer and Un Cut or Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back. Personally I’m waiting for the Return of the Munches (J&SB 2). Old Fashioned Over the Top Rope Battle Royal PCW Rising Star Title Jay Sylo(c) vs Mike Frampton vs Ben J. Pailthorpe vs John Crichton vs Andy Murray vs Shawn Mosely vs Jack Valentine vs Duzza I pity the fool who has to write this. Not only must they write the most boring type of match to write, but they also have to read Mike Frampton’s roleplays. I’m glad it’s not me, because you wouldn’t see The Bastard Speaks #5 simply because my brain would have turned to soup and poured out of my ear. Incidently, I must say well done to Mexi for writing the over the top rope main event at March Madness. You wrote it better than the WWF did to the Royal Rumble. Anyway, I say Jay Sylo to retain. And yes, I do know he’s not going to cut a promo for it... but quality over quantity wins these days, and there sure ain’t no friggin quality anywhere else in the ring. Mike... well... he sucks... Big D mostly. Ben looks like Token Weed with less greasy hair, Crichton is stuck on the Sci Fi channel, Murray is Scottish, and therefore will wear a skirt and will be laughed out of the arena... Mosely is... who the fuck is Shawn Mosely? Valentine is two months late and Duzza... Actually Duzza will win it because Duzza rocks. Hair vs Mask Match Loser must lose their hair or mask forever Flaymin Aymin vs El Diablo I’ve paid the Italian Hitmen and they can now do their job. It cost me twelve million dollars (Canadian for some reason... maybe Murray tight fisted Scottishness is rubbing off on me) but I’m going to rid PCW of both of these... except for El Diablo’s PWA running ability... I can’t deny how great that is. Miyagawa has to update Aymin’s poser anyway because it’s out of date and looks like a retard... oh... maybe he shouldn’t then... Six-Pack Challenge PCW Extreme Title Jason Core vs The Rush vs Karl LeBlanc(c) vs Orpheus Grant vs Big Daveylicious vs Badge Gah. Do I have to talk about this? I’m writing this column for free so strictly speaking I don’t have an actual contract as such, so I don’t have to write about this if I don’t want to. Jason Core is dull. The Rush looks like he has a tampon hanging from his hair. Karl LeBlanc is a French black man in a jail... aka he bends over in the showers very often. Orpheus Grant is... Karl LeBlanc non French and out of jail. He probably visits gay bars. Big Davelicious... should be shot. Either that or he and Frampton should get a Tag name that isn’t a joke. Badge... if he turns up he should have his Extreme title back without much of a problem Ladder Match PCW Cruiserweight Title Anton the Kid(c) vs THE Multiple Man vs Rent-a-Hero Rent a Hero is back. Anton has dropped into a pit of “I suck” and Tmm has equal quality to RaH but a lesser workrate. Tmm and RaH tagging would be a scary thing indeed. RaH to win and anyone who says otherwise is a moron. Standard Match Bobby Stormbringer vs The End A blind man wrestling... and the reason why someone hasn’t attempted to deafen him yet is? Just obviously only Ricky Martin could have done the job. Stormbringer to win even if Bobby dies between now and then. Standard Match Chet King vs Darkstar You know Darkstar. I know Darkstar.
