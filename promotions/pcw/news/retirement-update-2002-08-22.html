Retirement Update

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Retirement Update Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 Aug 2002 ---- On Tuesday Turmoil Jon Kano forced Corey Williams to sign a document. A clearly determined Kano revealed the contents of this document to Cal Seaver just after the show. "Basically what this here piece of paper says is that at Third Dawning Williams and I are gonna settle this once and for all. No more screw jobs, swerves, turns or comebacks, one man goes and one man stays" said Jon Kano, patting the document proudly. Seaver then went to the other side of the arena to hear words from an extremely hot-headed Williams. "This is an absolute outrage," exclaimed Williams as he threw a nearby stool at a mirror, shaterring it into several pieces. "There's no way I will stand for this utter crap, let Jon Kano know that I'll be contacting my lawyer. This match will NOT happen!" "Aww, he's just got his knickers in a twist because I created more of a controversy with this signing that he ever could," replied Kano with a fond smile when Seaver caught up with him the next day. "I will win this match and reclaim my career, coz that's what REAL legends are made of!" Right now the retirement match for Sunday is still on, but that could very well change with the comings of Friday Fusion, which is your last chance to get all the late developments on this match and the many others scheduled for Third Dawning, only on Pay Per View.
