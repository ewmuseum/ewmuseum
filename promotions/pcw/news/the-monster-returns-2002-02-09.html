The Monster Returns? Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Feb 2002 ---- After bumping into Duke Williams, and buying him a scotch and soda in order to get something coherient out of him, the Pimply Faced Intern Kid got this information. "The Monster is back. And I didn't even need to sign him, because Tyler renewed his contract without even realising it." We tracked down a man called "Mo" who has the ability to call upon Meanstreak in times of need, much in the same way that Clark Kent calls upon Superman, he had this to say. "Meanstreak is not coming back to PCW. And you can quote me on that." Although dissapointed at him not saying "boyo" we brought the story to you right here. If Meanstreak isn't returning to PCW, then who is this Monster that Duke Williams speaks of? Is it simply a change in attitude of someone already on the roster? Boda for instance called himself the biggest player now that Ivan had left, so perhaps it's the former Ass-Ass-In wrestler who feels he has to step up to the plate. Only time will tell who will fit the role of The Monster Known as... ?
