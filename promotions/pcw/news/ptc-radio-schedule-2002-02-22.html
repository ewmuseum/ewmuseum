PTC Radio Schedule

22 February 2002

PTC Radio Schedule Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 Feb 2002 ---- Primetime Central Radio will air this Saturday at 11 pm EST, then again at Sunday 12 noon EST/5 pm UK. On Saturday night, The Phantom Booker and Ed will be filling in for Duke Williams this week, who will be on special assignment. On the program, the Bookerman and the Mack will be announcing the first round winners of the 128 man PTC Global Title Tourney, chatting with members of CAL in regard to the new PTC/CAL alliance and introducing their organization, getting a "love connection" update from fWo's Tempest and OSW's Ivan Stanislav and taking calls from fans and wrestlers alike. The Booker and Ed will also introduce a new weekly segment to PTC radio that no one should miss out on, give away a poser image package from head PTC poser artist, Miyagawa, and introduce a new bachlorette for the second round of the eWrestling Dating Game. Other guests include Mr. Bartee, the owner of the World eWrestling Alliance, DWF legend and new PCW signee, 'Legend Inc.' Tom Lexian, and the usual array of PTC superstars, fans and etc. You never know who will call in...maybe even YOU!!! On Sunday's edition of PTC Radio, the Booker will chat with Aidan Campbell of WWR and Ringcrew, The Dark Liege of OSW, Rush CEO Day Johnson, "Singing" Xavier Kannon, and Diamond in the Rough tourney winner, Paddy O'Shea. The Phantom Booker will be chatting about his experiences in fWo Survivor with other members of Survivor and dish some backstage dirt, host another game of "What will you do for a Free Poser Package?," and as always take plenty of calls from wrestlers and fans alike. So don't miss either edition of PTC Radio! Listen or let others listen to you! 11 pm EST Saturday 12 Noon EST Sunday/5pm UK Go to the home of PTC Radio! Source: Primetime Central
