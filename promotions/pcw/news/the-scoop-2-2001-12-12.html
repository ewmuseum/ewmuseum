The Scoop #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Dec 2001 ---- Hello everyone, I’m Scoops Staley and this is the PCW Scoop. I know I promised a Sunday column, but this is what you get. A lot of things are going on in PCW right now, with Decimation on the horizon. The card looks like a good one, and it will hopefully inject some life into PCW after the turmoil(no pun intended) we’ve been going through. I don’t think I can write a column without addressing the Jacob Yoder situation. I’m not going to get into who said what and what happened and all the little details that the majority of us know nothing about. All I ask is that you don’t judge Jacob Yoder on these actions and on what you may hear about him. Jacob Yoder is one of the greatest out of character people in PCW. I can’t say that I agree with his statement, and I can’t say that he doesn’t deserve his punishment. What I’m saying is that Jacob Yoder is a good man, and I hope that you don’t remember him only for this incident. Anyway, on with other things. Has anyone noticed how none of the guys who were once tagged PCW’s “future” have been up to their regular standards lately? Rent-a-Hero has fallen off the planet. Big Alan Steele has not been anywhere near up to his old standards lately. Vampir Nosferatu and Snow both need to get back to what got them to PCW. Vampir has been writing short stories that have little when anything to do with his character. Ever since Snow joined New Breed, his RP’s have sorely missed his old wit. Each of these guys has what it takes to Main Event. It’s funny how they’ve all fallen off the rocket at around the same time. Is it just me or has Frisco disappeared? Couple that with the barely existent Ivan Stanislav, and I think the sky is looking pretty bright for Jeremy Howard. On that note, lets run down the entire Decimation card! Intergenter Tag Match Both Chet King and Mexi have been getting some much deserved recognition lately. However, I think this match will come down to the females involved. I don’t think there’s any question that Mona is the most over female in PCW. And yes, that includes Brandi. Despite her return, she’s going to have to work hard to regain her earlier PCW status, which she didn’t work to keep. Winner: Mexi and Mona Labour Relations Match Anything with Jon Kano is bound to fail in PCW these days. ‘Nuff said. Winners: AFL - CIO and TBA Goovil vs. Knox and Diamond It’s hard to bet against the legendary Goovil, even if I’m not sure Alan Kreigman has what it takes in PCW these days. Winners: Goovil Lumberjack Match: Downey vs. Chase I don’t think I’ve ever looked forward to a mid-card match like I’m looking forward to this one. When these guys bring their A games, there are very few, if any, people in PCW who are better. I’m hoping they both go at each other down the stretch, since this match has the heat and a wonderful history that could make a classic. Right now, Downey looks like he wants it more, but neither of them are squeezing the awesome potential out of this match. I wish more matches like this would happen at, or would be saved for, Pay Per Views. Winner: Downey Family Name Match: Storm vs. Storm This is the reason why PCW should have dark matches, or go back to putting on Saturday Night House Party. Nobody cares about this match, and all it does is water down the event. If I must flip a coin to pick a winner... Winner: Mike Storm Handcuffs Match: CCW vs. Badge What’s gotten into Corey Williams lately? New Breed has been a blessing for him. Not only has his workrate increased by leaps and bounds, but his efforts have also been rewarded by the PCW Extreme Championship; a belt that is slowly regaining a touch of value. As much as I like CCW, a lot of his work tends to look “sloppy” to me. A little more attention spent on the look and over all feel of his work would make put his stock through the roof. Badge, on the other hand, is a guy who can put quality out, but he’s not exactly working rapid fire. CCW’s going to out hustle him. Winner: CCW Samoan Deathmatch Timo Bolamba is at the end of a streak that saw him go from low carder to semi Main Eventer in the span of about a month. Afwa is PCW’s newest monster. The match looks like it will be good, although I’m not sure if Afwa has the workrate to keep up with Timo. Even though Timo’s been slacking of late, he should put in a solid effort for the PPV. With that said, I don’t see PCW letting Afwa lose all credibility this early. Winner: No Contest Continental Title Match Akiyama replaces Jacob Yoder in this match. If it were Yoder, I’d be picking a new champion. However, I don’t see Akiyama being a step in the right direction for the PCW Continental Championship. Big Alan Steele is a future Main Eventer. Akiyama’s future status is unknown at the moment. BAS’s work of late has not been up to his usual standards, but that’s probably just a passing phase, and the champ will ride his reputation to victory. BAS will hang on to the title for a few more weeks before becoming a permanent fixture in the Main Event scene. If the match were decided only on the work being done for it, Akiyama would win. Winner: BAS Bed of Nails The dark gimmicks will all get together in what will no doubt be among the disgusting matches in PCW history. Clyde and Darkstar are both currently on the upswing, while Vampir Nosferatu looks like a shadow of his former self. As stated earlier, Vamp needs to do what got him here. Right now, he’s writing short stories filled with symbolism that definitely are not going over with his peers. Clyde is finally getting his respect, and after Alliances, Darkstar’s stock has never been higher. With that said, none of them are hitting us with much these days. Winner: Clyde Winner Chooses #1 Contender I have to ask, will any of these wrestlers choose anyone but themselves as the #1 contender? Looking a the contenders, I think this match could steal the show. Everyone in the match is working hard for this match. Boda looks pretty good right now, even with rumours of his retirement running rampant through PCW. Personally, I think Boda should and will hang on for a while yet. He’s better now than he’s ever been in his career. Like I said earlier, Snow has to get back to his roots. The character modification that came with New Breed seems to have sucked out the lively humour that his promos were known for. Miyagawa looks like he wants this match. Winning could make things awkward, especially if Jeremy Howard walks away with the Main Event. Finally, Tyler Nelson has returned to his stellar form. His three PPV promos are deceiving of his workrate, which has been among the best lately. I’m betting on Nelson to really kick up some dust with his workrate, put in his usual quality, and take this one home. Winner: Tyler Nelson Main Event Jeremy Howard has a reputation for coming close, but at Decimation, it seems that there won’t even be an obstacle in his way. Frisco intrigued all of PCW with his recent streak, but it seems that’s over now. Frisco has disappeared. And what about Ivan Stanislav? Well, the PCW championship is officially a workrate disease now, because Ivan hasn’t looked anything like his usual PPV form. Winner: Jeremy Howard The PPV line up looks great, but there’s something missing: Title matches. To my knowledge, there are only two title matches on the night. No rising star championship, no TV title, no cruiserweight title. I’m not sure if the tag titles or the Extreme title will be on the line. But this is a PPV, everyone should be defending their championships. Something I plan to do in each column is a list of Who’s Hot, and Who’s Not. Who’s Hot? Jeremy Howard - Howard smells gold. We’ve all heard that before, but this time I don’t see anyway PCW can keep that championship off him. There may not be a harder worker in PCW, and he deserves every ounce of credit that he gets. Gaijin - Ten RP’s for TT and not bad quality in them. I don’t really think Gaijin has what it takes to make it past the PCW midcard (I consider him low card right now) but an effort like this could soon prove me wrong. Like Duke Williams, I’m a fan of the blue collar guys who give it everything they’ve got every night. That’s not to say putting up ten RP’s in every match is a good thing. Once your quantity starts effecting your quality, it’s time to slow down. But Gaijin seemed to pull of these ten promos rather well. Tyler Nelson - Maybe the hottest of all. Nelson’s a master of saying what has to be said, and saying it well. A three page promo from Tyler Nelson blows most people’s ten pages of filler out of the water. Tyler Nelson is an example of how one doesn’t have to out quantity his opponent to out quality his opponent. And what’s best about Tyler Nelson, is his character and his promos revolve around wrestling. Nelson has climbed back onto the face of the planet and is easily on of the top five or six guys in the federation at the moment. Mexi - The Playboy angle was just what Mexi needed to finally get him “over”in PCW. He’s now a solid midcarder, something that he couldn’t boast just a few weeks ago. I don’t know if Mexi will ever Main Event, but there’s a good chance he’ll become a PCW legend anyway. Kind of like what Jake “the Snake” was to the WWF. A guy who may never win the championship, but will always get respect for what he can do. After the Playboy Angle, however, there’s a chance for Mexi to someday be in that Main Event spotlight. You just never know in PCW. Six months ago, how many people thought Ivan Stanislav would be PCW champion? Who’s Not? Frisco - Starting out the larger of the two lists this time around is Frisco. He worked hard, got himself a title shot and has now disappeared. Now, in hindsight, it’s hard not to ask if giving Frisco the title shot was a wise decision, considering that he only put in two or three good weeks to get it. However, before he disappeared, I didn’t question it. I guess hindsight is 20/20. Rent-a-Hero - No one in PCW is colder than RAH right now. Once a week we have to see him pop up on the fan board asking for some sort of feedback, making himself look nothing more than ridiculous. RAH should relax, sit back, and put his nose to the grindstone instead of constantly looking around and trying to pin point what’s wrong. Things fell apart for RAH at Red October, and he’s yet to put the pieces back together. Vampir Nosferatu - No one in PCW writes better than the Vampir, but right now, everyone’s putting up better promos. Why? Because a short story is not a promo. Realistically, who wants to sit down and read something that has little when anything to do with PCW and current storylines? Symbolism is good, but not when does nothing to develop characters or feuds. How can someone retaliate against one of Vampir’s stories? I want to Vampir get back to his promos, where he excelled. Snow - As chilly as his name right now. Sure, he’s working harder than maybe anyone in PCW right now, but his quality has been cut in half since joining New Breed. His promos have lost their sense of humor, or at least they make a poor attempt at it. Like Vampir, Snow has to get back to what brought him to PCW in the first place. Big Alan Steele - Not long ago, BAS was putting out the most entertaining promos in PCW. Lately, he’s caught a bad case of the blands. It seems like he’s trying to branch his humor out into new areas, but I don’t think it’s working. In fact, I think it’s failing miserably. His workrate’s nothing to write home about either. Big Alan Steele may well be PCW’s top talent, but he’s not showing it right now. Ivan Stanislav - I’m sure no one can believe this, but it’s true. We’re going into Decimation and Ivan Stanislav looks like a shell of the commie son of a bitch who brought his A game to every PPV he’s ever been involved in. Ivan’s a guy who’s known for stepping things up at PPV time. But that hasn’t happened yet. El Diablo - For his filler RP. Not only should he be jobbed, he should be demoted. One of the worst parts about our game is the fact that from time to time match judges look at quantity only when deciding an outcome. Most people attempt to put quality up at every turn so this flaw doesn’t get out of hand. We sort of govern ourselves. The majority of the time, if two wrestlers of Equal talent are in a match, if one wrestler puts up more RP’s than the other, he’ll also have put up better quality all in all. El Diablo’s Filler RP will forever put him on my bad side, because he didn’t even try to do something quality. If I were TPB, he’d be suspended for that. I’m unaware of anything Chet King has done in the past, by the way. Well guys, that’s if for this one. I hope you enjoyed. Scoops Staley
