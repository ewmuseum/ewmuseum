Sandy's Turmoil Preview #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Aug 2002 ---- The camera fades in. Sandy Wong is sitting at a roundtable with numerous seats. She's wearing her hair up these days. Her gray business suit is neatly presses. She smiles and looks at to the door. Andy Murray has entered the room. Clyde has entered the room. Sandy Wong: Okay, thank you for coming. We'll start now, and Duzza can join us as soon as he stops stealing the food from outside. Andy Murray: *Sticks out tongue* Sandy Wong: Now, today we'll be giving a little pre-Turmoil discussion on everything that's going down on the card. Clyde, you're not booked, but I thank you for joining us anyway. Clyde: Pleasure is all yours. Andy Murray: Pure briwliant min. Sandy Wong: Okay, let's start off with the first big thing that's going down. Venneman Pendragon won the Television Title from John Crichton, and now there's a RS Title Tourney. Sandy Wong: Andy Murray, you're a participant. Thoughts? Andy Murray: Aye min, Ah'm pure gonnae win this title thing min, cos then Ah'll be a two time champ an' awl ... An' everybody else in it is a chube ... Sandy Wong: Clyde? Duzza has entered the room. Sandy Wong: Oh, look whos's joined us. There better be all the silverware left when I check it. Sandy Wong: *Waits* Sandy Wong: *Checks Watch* Sandy Wong: *Yawns* Five Minutes Later... Duzza: Well, now that I know everyone be bashin' the RS title, winning it, which y'know I'm gon do, doesn't seem like such an accomplishment any more. But ya know, no one would turn down the belt if someone gave it to them. Sandy Wong: That took a while, this whole "thinking" thing is hard on you, isn't it? Duzza has left the room. Sandy Wong: Aw, I upset him. Sandy Wong: Anyway, let's just get down to match analysis. Sandy Wong: First match on the card is Scorpion versus Jaden. Duzza has entered the room. Scorpion will hurt her. Sandy Wong: So that's one Scorpion pick to win. Andy Murray: Jaden will pure win min, Scorpion's pish, he should gawn back to McDonalds or somethin'. Clyde: If I hit myself in the head with a hammer will you begin to make more sense, Andy? Andy Murray: Wit ye sayin'? Sandy Wong: So Scorpion-1, Jaden-1. Clyde will probably pick Bethany to kill Jaden and give Scorpion the win by default, am I right? Clyde: Sadly, Jaden's rotting corpse still will possess more talent than Scorpion, I'll go with a no contest. Sandy Wong: What about the post-Fusion add-on match of Scorpion versus Duke Williams? Think he'll be looking past Jaden to that? Duzza: If scorpion loses to Jaden, y'know he'll get his ass kicked by Duke. He may as well not show up, nawmean? Clyde: If Scorpion can't manage the effort to crawl on top of Duke's passed-out-from-uncle-Jed's-leftover-moonshine carcass he deserves a thousand Saotome operations. Sandy Wong: At this point, I too think it's just a toss-up. Sandy Wong: *Flips coin* Sandy Wong: Fine, Scorpion to win it. Sandy Wong: Okay, let's get off of THAT abysmal match and on to the next one, Andy Murray versus Eddie Mandrake. Duzza: Murray will win, Andy'll try talk to Eddie before the match and his ignorant Scottish accent will fry Mandrakes brain, for real... Andy Murray has left the room. Sandy Wong: Mandrake is looking impressive coming in, and he's named after a mythological creature that's half-man, half-plant. I pick him to win. Clyde: I have no clue who the hell Mandrake is supposed to be, but I doubt Murray can manage to turn himself towards the exit of the bathroom before the match, thus losing by a countout and sparing us all a great deal of agony. Andy Murray has entered the room. Andy Murray: Wha gawn? Clyde: *Pulls out a hammer* It can't make you LESS sensible, can it? Sandy Wong: Clyde and I picked you to lose to Mandrake. Andy Murray: Haw! Ya chube! AH'M GONNAE PURE HAMMER THAT BAWBAG! The Pharaoh has entered the room. Sandy Wong: It's Pharaoh! Sandy Wong: Nice of you to join us! Andy Murray: Egypt's pure pish min. The Pharaoh: Hello, Sandy. Clyde: Hammer Pharaoh's bawbag instead, I hear the egyptians are into that sort of thing... The Pharaoh: Then you'd be wrong, Clyde... The Pharaoh: I hear you demons hate the Egyptian gods just about as much as i hate you. Sandy Wong: Pharaoh, we were choosing the winner of the Andy Murray/Eddie Mandrake match for Turmoil. Who do you think would pull off the 'W'? The Pharaoh: Andy is too good these days for that title... The Pharaoh: So i'll have to go with Mandrake Sandy Wong: I think you're the first person (including Murray) to say that. The Pharaoh: Murray should win it, my friend, but you know the bookers....they're constantly putting over these new talents... The Pharaoh: I'm afraid I'll have to go with Mandrake... At this point, Akiyama's old nemesis, The Golden Kayfabe Ape, walks through the room and shoots a hostile glance at Pharaoh. It holds a banana up to its throat and swipes it across like a knife, then holds it in Pharaoh's direction for a second. As quickly as the Golden Kayfabe Ape entered the room, it exits... Andy Murray: Pure pish min Clyde: *Cracks himself in the head with the hammer* Clyde: ..... Now speak Andy Murray: Haw? Clyde: Dammit! *Throws hammer down* The Pharaoh: Anyone coming down the sphinx's head for a pint after this, then? Andy Murray: That a pub min? Sandy Wong: I've got other work to attend to, no thank you. But tell Isiy I said hi. Duzza: Hey Pharoh...whatever happened to the Sphinx's nose? Clyde: I refuse to drink at the shrine of any who was defaced by Napoleon. The Pharaoh: Yes, andy, that's a pub. Andy Murray: Pure briwliant, Aye, Ah'll be there min. The Pharaoh: I'm not too sure, duzza... Sandy Wong: I thought passing European soldiers destroyed it with cannon fire? The Pharaoh: Oh, Clyde, I'm sure we can patch things up, i hear you're a good guy now-a-days... The Pharaoh: Just come down for a pint, or five... Sandy Wong: *Remembers the purpose for the roundtable* Wait, we've got to get on with the card-calling! The Pharaoh: I mean, what could possibly happen when you're drunk? Clyde: Either that or the masses have gotten much less intelligent, I'll leave you to decide... The Pharaoh: Yes, Sandy has a valid point. Sandy Wong: Back to Turmoil, Duzza versus Jerome "Blayze" Jordan. Clyde: A hole opens in time-space due to the impossible amount of suck in the ring at one time, causing the arena to be ripped apart. Andy Murray: Blayze is a poof min, Ah pure chibbed him by the way, an' Duzza is pish. This match sucks, how come they both wogs min? Clyde: No contest... Duzza: Blayze is just another black dude who will be gone in a week, just like that thug roC. he ain't as pretty as me neither. i ain't gonna lose to that duzz-wannabe. The Pharaoh: Clyde, I'm intrigued by your view... The Pharaoh: Could you have some sort of effect on this match? The Pharaoh: I'm not sure if you consider them both sinners... Clyde: Everyone has their sin, kiddo... Clyde: Including you. Sandy Wong: I don't know, Duzza, you just haven't been able to pull of a big singles win. I think your biggest was an exhibition against *snickers* Mike Frampton. Duzza: Hey... I beat Loc 'n Makonie, too! ... I think.... Clyde: And then you woke up. The Pharaoh has left the room. Sandy Wong: So, given no other options, Blayze to win? *Looks around the table for agreement.* The Pharaoh has entered the room. Andy Murray: Aye Sandy Wong: Quick bathroom break, Titu? The Pharaoh: Sorry about that, Isiy called... *Rolls eyes* I might not be able to come down the sphinx's head. Clyde: Need you to replace her fishnet stockings? They tear so easily while sorking the streets... working, even. The Pharaoh: How dare you insult my wife! You, you....you evil cretin! Clyde: Complements won't save you... Andy Murray: Shut up ye couple o' chubes! Sandy Wong: Do I need to get those episodes of the Snorks sent through here? The Pharaoh: *Narrows eyes* Right, you've asked for it....you little angel of heaven! Duzza: You know, for an African, you sure do act white, Pharaoh. The Pharaoh: It's my royal up bringing, Duzza... Sandy Wong: That's it! *Plays Snorks on the room's TVs at 1500 decibels* Andy Murray has left the room. Clyde: Did you and Andy share the same bucket of paint chips during your childhood, Sandy? Andy Murray has entered the room. Andy Murray: Haw, sorry min, had tae go down the chippae Sandy Wong: I was attempting to scare you into submission with pathetic children's cartoons from the 80's. Clyde: If I hit YOU with the hammer will you make more sense? The Pharaoh: Me? Andy Murray: Ye gonnae hit me wi' a hammer? Ye do that an' Ah'll chib ye. The Pharaoh: Or him? *points at clyde* Clyde: Hitting myself is futile, sadly... Clyde: The simpering of this group remains unchanged. El Diablo has entered the room. Sandy Wong: El Diablo joins us. Welcome! The Pharaoh: Ah, El diablo... Duzza: Futile? Simpering? Where you gettin' these words? Duke Williams has entered the room. Clyde: From an odd little organ called the "brain" which you were born lacking... The Pharaoh: I believe its referred to as "school." Sandy Wong: Onto the next match, Mark Makonie versus Semore Smoke. The Pharaoh: Ah duke, gracing us with your presence, I see... Clyde: Die. Duke Williams: Yes, sir. Andy Murray: Smoke will win! He likes smokin', which is pure briwliant. Clyde: *Throws hammer in Duke's direction* The Pharaoh: *Laughs* Oh, Clyde, you want everyone to die... Sandy Wong: Hello, Mr. Williams. Poked your head in for a quick spell? Maybe you could help us. Who do you think will win between Mark Makonie and Semore Smoke? Duke Williams: *Catches it and pounds some loose nails down* El Diablo: Ah, The Duke has retired from losing! Duke Williams: The bald guy. Duzza: One of them is high on white supremacy, the other is high of da reefa... Sandy Wong: Makonie will win. Smoke's alliance with Hardly Controversial Corey Williams means that he's cursed to job forever during his PCW time. Duke Williams: Amen. The Pharaoh: My thougths exactly! Sandy Wong: *Smiles* I got one right! The Pharaoh: Duzza, when are you hiring that cameraman, Fred, I believe his name is, to shoot your next promo? Clyde: The Black Hole of Sucks makes a second appearance ridding us all of them both. Sandy Wong: *Giggles* Next match is John Crichton versus Corey Williams... Sandy Wong: I think we can skip that one, agreed? Duzza: As soon as the Pope tells the world about the garden of Eden being in Africa. Duke Williams: Skip. Andy Murray: Skip min, that's pish. The Pharaoh: I'm afraid I'll have to go with Williams, he's got a bit more skill in the ring that John. Clyde: Dark Gimmick to kill them all and eat their flesh. El Diablo has left the room. Sandy Wong: Okay then, the sub main event is a match you're involved in, Mr. Williams. You take on Scorpion in his second match of the night. Duke Williams: I'll kill him. The Pharaoh: I'll go with Duke. Duke Williams: ... Malice Style! Andy Murray: Haw! Scorpion loses twice in one night? Briwliant! Sandy Wong: And it's a Steel Cage Match? Duke Williams: I retired from losing....by fighting second rate talent! *Laughs* The Pharaoh: I tried that with Duzza. Clyde: Duke will overcome an extremely painful case of the gout to stumble to the ring, only to trip over his own shoelaces and give up the match. The Pharaoh: It worked too. Sandy Wong: The only question is, will you beat him fast, or punish him painfully slow? Duke Williams: Pain... Andy Murray: Gonnae kill Scorpion min... The Pharaoh: Yes... Duzza: Play him some of that country western music Duke... punish the cracker. Duke Williams: *Laughs out Loud* Andy Murray: Cracker min? Cream Crackers? They're pure hummin'. Sandy Wong: So that's another free roster spot after Turmoil. Who should the talent scouts bring in to replace Scorpion? Clyde: Vegita... Andy Murray: Rab! Sandy Wong: I'd like to see Dan Hollywood back in PCW... The Pharaoh: I cannot believe i'm not booked this week. The Pharaoh: My eye's feeling fine now... Sandy Wong: Better be, The End's got the blindness schtick down, you don't want to be teamed with him... Duke Williams: At least Chris Chase didn't beat you last week! Clyde: *Jabs Pharaoh in the eye with a fork* The Pharaoh: Oww!!!!! Clyde: Solves one problem... The Pharaoh: *Scowls at Clyde in disgruntled manner* Clyde... Sandy Wong: *Looks over at the dining table* We didn't have any forks over there... The Pharaoh: *Holds up crucifix to clyde* Clyde: Well, we did before Duzza came along... The Pharaoh: Take that, foul demon! Duzza: I didn't take the forks! They always blame the brotha... Sandy Wong: *Whispers to Pharaoh* Psst! You're holding it upside down. The Pharaoh: *Cowers* Andy Murray: Yer awl a bunch o' bawbags... The Pharaoh: Noooooooo! Duzza: Good Lord! Clyde: *Puts picture away* Carry on... Sandy Wong: *Recovers* ANYWAY, the main event is Xavier Kannon putting his PCW World Title on the line against Trigger. Akiyama is the #1 contender, but he's not the one wrestling Kannon! Ellie made an announcement on last week's Turmoil that Trigger, who many say had a disappointing reign as the Rising Star champion. Anyone else see a repeat of what the New Breed did with Big Alan Steele? The Pharaoh: Why, what happened? Was I out with that knee injury, or concusion or something? Yer pure stupit Sandy Wong: The franchise is named after him. Sandy Wong: But don't fret, they've got a franchise named after a stupid cartoon sailor.
