Doghouse #3 Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Oct 2001 ---- Hey guys, and welcome back to PCW’s most regular column, the Dog House. PCW is really looking great lately, especially with the majority of returnees looking like they’re going to stick with PCW. Even Meanstreak. Was I wrong in my comments about the monster? Well, the jury’s still out. Well now, lets get on with the column. I think I have a few extra things in there that should be enjoyable for you all to read. I think PCW management made a big mistake by bringing back Prisoner 61185. The man is a beast, but why don’t we look at his impact on PCW2 a few months back. That’s right. What Impact? And Jeremy’s even worse. They’ll be here for about a month, and probably cut two promos in that time. I know Products of the System are “names” around here, but they’re occupying precious roster space that about ten guys could easily fill better, and make PCW more competitive. How long has Dkay Calyx been waiting in PWA now? Tick tock... Tick tock... It’s official folks, Tyler Nelson is back. You think he never left? Look at Back to the Old School. If Tyler Nelson had put up a fight, his match against Jeremy Nelson could have been a classic. So what’s got Tyler’s engine running? Probably the PCW Championship. Same thing that almost gave life to Jon Kano’s dead, but ROCKING, career. Jacob Yoder is at it again, in classic Amish style. The man is like the silent assassin of PCW. He quietly goes about his business, and then kicks your ass. Yoder’s got as much talent as anyone in PCW. He’s been going unnoticed since his return, but he’s as brilliant as ever. Watch him closely. I think the Contintental Championship could be well within reach. He’s unranked now, but that will change. I think Jacob Yoder is the only man who doesn’t have a bad word against him backstage. You’ll never hear anyone say this man is overrated. What have I been saying about Stryfe? He’s to me what Guy Leslie was to Shooter. The whipping boy. Have you seen the latest rankings? This former world champion is ranked fifth for the Television belt. Just a couple of months ago, this guy was main eventing. And so, we enter into Lethal Lottery grounds. PCW’s latest World Championship Tournament may not carry as much hype as the previous Sixty-Four man tournament, but it’s about as interesting. First of all, it’s obvious that someone had their sneaky little hands in on the pairings. You can tell right off the bat who’s capable of carrying a solid Main Event. Immediately, you strike the names of anyone who’s been in PWA within the last month. Will Ivan and Streak walk through the tournament? The jury’s out. I’ve got word that there’s so much tension between these two right now, that there’s no way they’ll last the whole way to the finals. And that’s good news for Badge and Yoder who have the task of facing the monsters in the next round. I thinkboth guys are immense talents. But it might be too soon right now for either to capture the world title.. And lets not forget their classic PCW2 feud. Could the spark fire be set to rekindle? I think it’s a good possibility. Badge had been on cruise control until the BPA thing. Yoder needs a high profile feud. When these two butt heads, it’s always explosive. And I don’t think either have forgotten their past. The second bracket’s a walk-through for Snow and Kid Wonder. Everywhere you see a decent wrestler in the bracket, he’s paired with a sub-par partner. Whether Snow and Wonder can co-exist is the real question. If they can, they’ll breeze through their bracket and then be fresh for the real competition. The third bracket boils down to two teams, one of which is a major favorite. Kremmen and Tyler Nelson are my picks to win the tournament. Not only am I picking them because of who they are, but their recent work as been top notch. I really think Kremmen’s going to walk away with the gold here. But Nelson will be back. The other team is Boda and Duke. These two both are hard workers, and if they can put aside their differences, the watch for them to do big things in this tournament. Boda should be wrestling for the world championship anyway. He’s as talented as any Main Eventer in PCW. The fourth bracket is by far and away the toughest to predict. First of all, I have to give props to the pairing of Doug Troy and Hank. That was a classic PCW moment. I don’t think they stand a chance in the tournament, but I’ll bet my dog house that their match will be the highest rated of the early rounds. I hope they fight it out at Red October. That’s right, I said “match”. And I said that, because Frisco and Dan Hollywood are real threats in this tournament. Hollywood is way underrated. I think part of the reason is because he keeps stepping into people’s shadows. Well, here’s his time to shine. Frisco has the heat to carry a Main Event, and I have no doubt that Hollywood has the talent. Chet King and Two Ton aren’t real threats in the tournament, but they deserve to be mentioned. The Deuce rocks. Literally. The man shakes concrete when he walks. I don’t think these guys will make it to the Main Event, but don’t be surprised if they make it a long way, clearing out some of the favorites. Vampir Nosferatuis a super talent. He needs to get back to his roots. Back to what got him out of PCW2. John Jupiter is a waste of space. He’ll hold them out of the finals. Hull and Miya have a legitimate shot if Rev returns to form. I don’t see that happening. Miyagawa’s great when the workrate is there. It’s hard to say what’s going to happen with this team. Jeremy Howard and RAH have amazing talent. Do they have the heat to carry a Main Event? Howard does. But it’s still too soon for Rent-a-Hero. A few months from now, both he and BAS (who’s unfortunately found Stryfe as his partner, otherwise would be a serious threat to win the title) are candidates to be top, and yes I do mean top, faces in PCW. Jeremy Howard, on the other hand, should have been given the reigns of top heel long before now. Every time something’s given to this guy, he doesn’t get the opportunity to prove that he can handle the increased responsibility. These two could be the dark horse. They definitely have the talent. So there’s a run down of the teams. Now lets run through the Hound’s odds for the winning team of the Lethal Lottery tournament, and who would win in a final match between them... Keep in mind, this is assuming everyone RP’s. 4:1 - Streak and Ivan (Toss up) 4:1 - Kremmen and Nelson (Leaning toward Kremmen 10:1 - Frisco and Dan Hollywood (Frisco) 10:1 - Jeremy Howard and Rent-a-Hero (Jeremy Howard) 10:1 - Kid Wonder and Snow (Toss up) 20:1 - Reverend and Miya (Reverend) 20:1 - Badge and Yoder (Yoder... I’m a mark) 20:1 - Boda and Duke Williams (Boda) 50:1 - Vampir and Jupiter (Gee, I wonder... Vampir.) 50:1 - Chet King and Two Ton (TWO TON!!!!) 100:1 - Hank and Doug Troy (Hank! Hank I say! ... well, not really, but I can dream) And guess what? The column’s not over. As a special treat, I thought I’d list my own opinion on where everyone in PCW stands at the moment. There are certain signs that you’ll need to know to make sense of this, so I have a Legend for you all. These are in random order, by the way. Here it goes... Unsung Hero Jacob Yoder. I’d love to see Yoder get the chance to operate at the Main Event level, because I don’t think he would disappoint. The best part about Jacob Yoder, is he’s a quality over quantity guy. Why is he unsung? Well, he’s not ranked for a single title. He’s better than 95% of our federation. He may be the best. That’s one Amish mad man you want to stay on the good side of, because he can tear you to shreds. In The Dog House Revered. He comes out on Malice, telling us he’s going to return to form, and then we get nothing from him. It’s over for Reverend. He should go back into retirement and save face.
