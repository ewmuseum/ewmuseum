Enigma Speaks Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2003 ---- Over the last week videos promoting the apparent debut of a wrestler called Enigma have been aired. No one knows anything about this wrestler, other than that he will debuting in PCW soon and that he is an old friend of Flaymin Aymin's. PCW Online recently caught up with the Sacramento native. Reporter: Hello and welcome to PCW. Enigma: Thank you, it truly is an honor to be here and I'm sure it's just as much of an honor for PCW to have my talents. Reporter: To be blunt, who are you and why are you here? Afterall nobody here in PCW or the fans know anything about you. Enigma chuckles. Enigma: Simple, I'm here to compete with the best. You've seen my videos right? The reporter nods. Enigma: Then you'll know just how talented I am. "I...So...Rule" isn't just a catchphrase, it's the truth, and in PCW I know I will be able to go up against the best. There is nowhere else where the talent is so abundant, and that peak has just increased with me, Enigma. Reporter: OK, but who are you? Enigma: That's easy; I'm just a wrestler looking for a break. I've spent the better part of five years working in companies along the West Coast, and not to be vain, but hell I was the cream of the crop and needed to move on to better things. Maybe you and all PCW fans will think I'm pathetic, but wrestling is my life, when I'm not wrestling I'm training, and if I'm not training I must have something serious on my mind. Reporter: Interesting. One thing I have seen recently on PCW television is that you've been stating that you are an old friend of Flaymin Aymin's, can you elaborate on that? Enigma: He used to wrestle on the West Coast for a time, that's all I can say. Reporter: So will we be seeing you debut on PCW soon? Enigma: In due time, for now I'm just happy to do my own thing and cause some hype to walk into, and believe me there will be a lot of hype. Reporter: Anything you want to add? Enigma: Just be prepared for the unexpected whenever you see me. Reporter: Thanks for your time. Even after this interview it was still cloudy as to where Enigma has come from and what his motives are, but from the look of his wrestling skills on the videos we've seen, he looks a very good prospect and is definitely confident in his abilities.
