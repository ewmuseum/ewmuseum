Post PPV Personnel Report

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post PPV Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Aug 2002 ---- As seen at Third Dawning, PCW has signed 5 new superstars. Those being the tag team of “Pranksters With Attitude” comprised of Gaz “The Jester” Green and Nerezza who joined the new version of Asylum along with fellow new recruit Anarchy. Also signed were RUSH superstars Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson who make up “Playaz Inc.” and have aligned themselves with Rush Mason. “Too long PCW has been oppressing the minorities,” said new CEO Xavier Kannon when asked about the new arrivals “so the signings of Quo and Dante are just a sign of things to come under the Kannon administration... As for the others... Well, anything that comes from running Jeremy Howard’s name into the ground is okay by me.” Then, as shown at the end of Third Dawning, Tyler Nelson and the Phantom Booker have been fired by the company. “Nelson’s dismissal is just a natural progression along the PCW timeline. With all the new signings, the old must move on!” Kannon explained, “As for the Phantom Booker... After I got what I wanted, he really served no use to PCW that would justify his wages.”
