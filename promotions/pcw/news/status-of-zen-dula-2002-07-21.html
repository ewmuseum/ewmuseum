Status of Zen Dula

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Status of Zen Dula Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Jul 2002 ---- As sson as officials could get to his aid on Friday evening, the Bodyguard of the Pharaoh, Zen Dula was immediatly rushed to the hospital where he is currently on life support from the blow given to his head and the effects of the electrocution he sustained at the hands of Xavier Kannon. Both Titu Xansiv, and his wife Isiy are at the giant's bedside and were not available for comment, however the perpetrator, Xavier Kannon was more than willing. "I see it as a POSITIVE thing..." he boasted, "It's a message to all the managers, valets and whatever that it's best to stay out of the action and leave it to us wrestlers.... Zen having oxygen pumped into his lungs for the rest of his Peonic life if the perfct example of that." When asked about any action being taken against himself, the Scientologist just shrugged any thought of it off. "Well, maybe if someone HADN'T made a slight alteration to the Pharaohs contract making HIM liable for any injury sustained by his entourage.. I may be worried!" he said before giving us his closing words, "I hope you enjoyed the show Titu...". More on Zen Dula's status as we recieve it.
