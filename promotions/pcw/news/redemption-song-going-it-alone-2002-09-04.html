Redemption Song Going It Alone

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Redemption Song Going It Alone Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Sep 2002 ---- New PCW signings Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell are set to embark on their PCW careers without the manager that got them there. 38-year old Tom Core, who had been acting as Redemption Song's manager and lawyer since their days in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, left the team mysteriously just before Redemption Song's crucial final of OSW's MET competition and was not present for his team's final match in CHZ. Core, a former champion in now defunct 2XW, cleaned out his room at Redemption Song's Montreal headquarters and hasn't been heard from since. "We can't sit here and dwell on it," said Jim Surge via conference call. "The past is the past. He's gone and as far as we know, nothing's going to bring him back. As of right now, Misty will remain with us and serve as temporary manager in addition to her valet duties." When asked if Redemption Song would be seeking to join up with an already established PCW manager, Surge apologized to the media and abruptly ended the phone call. More on this story as it develops.
