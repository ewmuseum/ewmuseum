P.W.A.M.T?

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
P.W.A.M.T? Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Nov 2002 ---- Pranks with anger managment therapy? Visitors to a certain clinic have reported on several occasions that PCW Tag Team Champion, Nascosto Nerezza has been seen making visits to Dr. Sodoffyabich for help containing his rage. Apparently, Nerezza's multiple and frequent transformations have been the result of a build-up of frustrations and may lead to a permanent transformation. "Ze patient has much pent-up emotional anger." the doctor confirms. "If ze effects continue, ze anger vill consume him to ze point dat the man ve call Nerezza may disappear and be replaced by the monster zat he calls Ireul. Zis must be stopped at all costs." Apparently, Nerezza has been placed on medication to suppress his anger but he refuses to give up wrestling. "I'm a tag team champ and no matter if I'm put in no-ladder matches, 60-team events or the kind of match that the Ixtreem era of NTICW would have booked, I'm not taking any time out just because someone tries to rub me the wrong way, you mook." Nerezza replied when asked about some time off. "The answer's bloody simple. Don't annoy me and I won't send your head into concrete. The next person to try it is going to need srugery just like Miyaga... GAH... RAAAARRGH!!!" At that point, we were unable to make out what he said as he stormed off. If he calms down, we'll try and get an interview with him. In the meantime, we can only pray for the safety of Gaz Green and Natalia, as well as his opponents.
