Holla Back Hosses Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Mar 2003 ---- Los Angeles, California- He made no secret of his intentions, there were no cryptic clues, no veiled hint... No speculation... March Madness would be the last time “The Malice Man” Duke Williams would set foot competitively in a PCW ring. February was the Month of Malice, the beginning of his “Last Stand In Open Country”, and it all came to an end in Los Angeles tonight... But would he fulfil his dream of bowing out with the Gold? Or would the man who drew him out of retirement the 1st time be on hand to deny Duke his one wish? Also at Madness... An old face returned and left his former rival in no doubt that he was back, new arrivals sign in and get tossed out, Television and Continental Gold are on the line, our Shooting Star puts on a spectacle, a Legend departs but not before Cal Seaver has a surprise for him, and the Schmoopies walk down the aisle in both senses.
