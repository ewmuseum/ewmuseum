Unholy Alliance? Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Dec 2001 ---- Minneapolis, Minnesota- Back when the Wild Deuces formed at Alliances, "The Reverend" Jon Hull was the leader and the mastermind behind the entire formation. But not long after they were formed, he went away never to be seen......until recently. Why has he decided to return now? And why is he specifically helping Vampir Nosferatu? Also on Fusion, the first round of the New Blood tournament went down with the usual upsets. Alan Kriegman reminds Stephen Blood why he is the evil super genius. And to what lengths will Tyler Nelson go to destroy the Union? To find out all this and more, tune in to Fusion found under the Recent Results link.
