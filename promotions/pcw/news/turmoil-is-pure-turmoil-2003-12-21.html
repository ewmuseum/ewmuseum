Turmoil is pure Turmoil!

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turmoil is pure Turmoil! Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Dec 2003 ---- After nearly six months off the air, PCW came back with a bang! And a little bit more... Starting off with the shocking announcement that currently, two of the company's executives, Ron Starr and HOFer, Tyler Nelson, have been incarcerated on charges related to tax evasion and other monetary fraud, the show picked up pace with the return of a whiskey-guzzlin' legend... HOSS! Forced by FOX to act fast, because another delay would lead to immediate cancellation, the production team responded fantastically, and the show went on with relatively few problems. New faces were seen, as well as old. The return of our last Heavyweight Champion brought a ripple of displeasure from some of the rookies, but a smile to the faces of the fans. Also, PCW's superhero is back, odd as ever, as well as the ongoing legacy of the company's schizophrenic oddball, who managed to stir up some trouble with the staff. There was a whole lot that happened, ending with...well, a surprise. To find out who came back, and who made out big, tune in for the replay of PCW's Turmoil, here!
