Personnel Report

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Mar 2003 ---- Monday morning, PCW officials confirmed the signings of A.J. Trinidad & The Overweighter, as well as the re-signing of Semore Smoke. Smoke and Trinidad will debut/re-debut on Turmoil with a TV Title shot on the line. The Overweighter is expected to make his 1st in-ring appearance next Tuesday. The Mexican Devil, El Diablo has also returned to the company and has been restored to his previous contract. Diablo will contest the Cruiserweight Title against Rent-a-Hero on Wednesday. As seen at the PPV, Jon Kano has retired from PCW and been inducted into the Hall of Fame. Snow, the PCW World Champion heading into March Madness has contacted PCW officials and his contract has been put on hold, whether the 4-time Champion will return to the company is still to be seen. Eric Wolfson has requested a contract annulment and it has been accepted. PCW has been in contact with some more developmental talent, some of whom are rumoured to be arriving this week.
