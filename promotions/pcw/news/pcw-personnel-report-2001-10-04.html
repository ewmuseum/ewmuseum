PCW Personnel Report

4 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Oct 2001 ---- PCW Personnel Director Jane Reagan held her weekly news conference Wednesday night and revealed several shocking developments on the PCW personnel front. -Reagan announced that the company had released Deadly Dustin Downey, Kris Kanton, Jay Storm and Doug Troy for what she termed as "piss poor dedication and work rates." -Reagan said that El Magnifico Busmuchacho del Mysterio will replace Doug Troy as Hank's tag team partner for the Lethal Lottery Tourney on Turmoil. -Reagan mentioned the company is not pleased with the work rates of AJ Storm, Brandi, John Jupiter and The Elia Brothers and said the company will make changes "sooner rather than later" if things do not improve. -Reagan cofirmed the retirement of The Reverend Jon Hull. -She announced the company has signed long time PWA worker and instructor, El Diablo to a PCW contract. -The sultry PCW administrator said D-Kay Calyx, Xavier Kannon, Totally Tubular Tom, Ketsueki Karasu and Ritchie Syxx are on a short list of PWA wrestlers PCW is considering signing. -Darkstar and Stryfe are both nursing injuries that could keep them out for up to a month. -Brock Newbludd is almost ready to return to PCW. Stephen Blood is still a few weeks away. Reagan promised another personnel report over the weekend. We, of course, will bring you all the details of her report just after it takes place.
