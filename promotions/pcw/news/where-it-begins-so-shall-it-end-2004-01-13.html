PCW — Column

“Where it begins… so shall it end…”

13 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First and foremost… my apologies to all. I cannot imagine how I can repay everyone who has been affected by the sins that I have committed, in the past of both PCW as well as the recent activities I participated in while working for Old School Fighting. In OSF, I committed numerous crimes against humanity under the guise of Elegance, or Miyabi, and with this cult following I ruined countess lives, both here and abroad in Japan. There is no possible way I can regain honor from that travesty… but I will strive for it, even if it takes my last dying breath. But as for PCW, what have I done in PCW that I need ask forgiveness for? I let the fans down… and for that I humbly ask for forgiveness. When I had first entered PCW a few years ago, I rose from being a newcomer to the company to the top, scrapping in dark matches at the start of the month, and at the end battling for the world title at the pay per view against the company’s Hall of Fame stars. But I was not able to complete the quest… I did not give my chance to prove PCW my worth 100%. Instead, my mind was distracted… my focus taken away by personal matters back at home in Japan. After the night I blew my opportunity to be PCW world champion, I left the company, dishonored by my own hand. But now… the prodigal son has returned. My stay here in PCW will be one of repentance. I have so many things to repent for… so many sins… and the one that takes high priority is righting the wrong done those few years ago. I want to regain the opportunity to earn the PCW world title again, just as I did back then… and from there I vow that I will not let the people that supported me down… not this time. I only wish to gain back both the respect and honor from the fans by becoming the PCW world champion… only after earning my place back at the top of the company. However… that leaves me to cross paths with an old friend… Skylar Green… we both met in PURE, and it was there that we formed a formidable friendship and respect for each other. We fought alongside each other, fought against each other, traveled together, ate meals together, and so on… all out of the act of friendship and respect. When PURE closed we went our separate ways, he already in PCW, and I to OSE. While I was becoming Old School Fighting Champion and bringing shame and dishonor upon my name… my good friend Skylar Green was earning the honor and respect that I could not, by becoming PCW world champion. And while our time was limited in OSF, here we are yet again… I have nothing but respect for Skylar Green, as a person as well as the PCW champion, and I hope despite my actions in OSF that he will have the same for me as well. But as Propaganda, Sebastian Toomes, and whoever else sees fit, I am a competitor for Green’s title. However, I will not demand that I be granted an opportunity, not because of who I am, who I was, or who I think I am. Only when one earns what he keeps through hard work, while earning the respect of both the people and his peers… then, will an honor such as becoming PCW champion truly be grand. I thank you all for this opportunity to prove myself to you all again, both those working within PCW, as well as the fans reading this now and cheering me on at home or at the arena. With your support, I hope to make myself worthy of your praise and respect. With that said and done… the Black Dragon Master has spoken…
