Loc Warned Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Sep 2002 ---- After recent comments made by PCW star Jung Loc in support of Japanese Promotion, PURE... PCW CEO Xavier Kannon has issued a warning to the Vietnamese star. “I couldn’t give a fuck what worthless piece of shit Title that peon holds in PURE, I have put him on the PCW team to test him,” he divulged, “If he isn’t putting his very, albeit worthless, soul into each punch then the story of his PCW career will be best be titled ‘Wow, I’m Jung Loc and I want to Die’.” Loc, who has recently been out of action with a foot re-attachment, will make his return to PCW action on Fusion and looks like he’ll need to shake off the ring rust fast ahead of Red October.
