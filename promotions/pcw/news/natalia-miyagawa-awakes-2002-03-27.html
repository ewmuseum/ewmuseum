Natalia Miyagawa Awakes

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Natalia Miyagawa Awakes Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Mar 2002 ---- After her traumatic attack at the hands of Chris Sloboda, aka "Boda" the current PCW World Champion a couple of weeks ago on Turmoil just prior to Boda regaining that said belt we here at the head offices thought that Natalia was a gonner. Hell, even Miyagawa himself did. But she's awake and talking to people now and seems well on the road to recovery... that is as long as Boda keeps his half of the bargain. Boda stated to Miyagawa that he will "think" about donating Natalia some of the rare Eastern Europian blood group that both he and Natalia share so that she may have a life saving operation on her performed the Monday after Spring Fling. There was two things that Miyagawa must not do however to Boda until then... he must not attack Boda, and he must not touch Boda's family. We asked Miyagawa what he thought about this. "My wife is a survivor, much like myself. I promised Boda that I won't attack him or his wife until Spring Fling. And I won't... until Spring Fling. It's what the man asked for, and it's what he shall recieve." We asked Miyagawa further about his match against Corey Williams at Spring Fling and this is what he had to say on the subject. "Corey Williams won't make it to Spring Fling. I'm facing him and Chris Chase on Fusion, and the only way I'm going to be able to win that is if I take Corey out... perminently. He won't be getting into the ring at Spring Fling... in fact he won't even be getting into the ring on Fusion... but he will be arriving at the stadium before leaving with flashing sirens two minutes later. And then I'll beat his wife Chris Chase to face Mrs Chase-Williams and Mr Sloboda in a match made in hell at Spring Fling."
