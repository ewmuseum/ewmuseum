Life after PCW #2

3 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life after PCW #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Nov 2001 ---- Our undercover reporter again cornered former PCW Extreme, Television and Continental Titleholder and member of the Alliances #1 PPV Main Event winning team, Scott "The Hott One" Naket, this time in a Tweek's coffee outlet. The mere act of offering Mr. Naket a cup of coffee and a chat was enough to bring forth this tirade: "Now, what I just did in there, was the same way PCW treated me most of my time there. Like a pile of crap. My time there started off fine, and I wasn't complaining. All the time they said, just wait, you'll get your main event matches again soon enough. So I waited. They fed me a mid card title to keep me busy, and told me to work with this while they made a place for me. Well, I did, and when I finished, they decided to make a place for The Preacher instead. What did they do? They gave me a higher up title and told me to just wait, we promise you'll see the main event action soon after you get rid of this. Well, I was going in a positive direction right? Wrong. I lose that title, and what happens? It seems that they gave that spot to Tyler Nelson instead. Tyler Nelson! Now you can see why they said I was a bitch. Was I a bitch? Yes I was, but wouldn't you be one too if you were made a promise three times, and three times in a row they backed out on you for someone else? I would think so. All the time, "we have big plans for you" and "you'll be the star soon enough". It never happened. And there's another example of PCW's political game. Now, everyone in PCW was told to hate Stewert Hix. You know what, I don't really know why. I know he jumped to JeW, but does that make him a bad guy? No, he did what he thought was best for his career, and he probably did it for the same reasons I did. But you know what, this isn't PCW, so let's put PCW in the past, in the little dark room in the corner of the house. It's now a memory. It's now time to take the next step, look forward, and focus on JeW." Our undercover reporter was on the verge of asking Naket probing questions regarding his knowledge and opinion of Stewart Hix appearing on rival federation television with the PCW Heavyweight Title belt, while he was champion, but burst into uncontrollable laughter when Naket closed out by mentioning looking forward and focussing on JeW as a positive career move. Naket again made a hasty exit, not before snatching the cup of coffee. We'll bring you more on the pathetic fall from grace of former PCW wrestlers as it becomes available.
