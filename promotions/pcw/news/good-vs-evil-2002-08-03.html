Good vs Evil

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Good vs Evil Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Aug 2002 ---- Dallas, Texas- No one has ever called Tyler Nelson a saint, but his recent change in direction has people wondering about a rebirth of sorts. The CEO and his Dark Knights have chosen to cleanse PCW of all sinners, with Nelson's sights set on perhaps the biggest sinner of them all.....Xavier Kannon. The CEO and the newly crowned PCW World Champion have been playing mental games thus far, but those games turned physical in the Fusion main event. Who would come out on top, the savior or the sinner? Also on Fusion, Jason Snow goes one on two with Rent A Villain and Brandi and a special guest as well. Akiyama and El Diablo go through a brutal battle for the Continental Title #1 contendership in which one man would win the match but the other would have the last word. To see this action and much more just head over to the Fusion results NOW!
