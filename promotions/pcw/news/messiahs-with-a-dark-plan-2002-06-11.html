Messiahs with a Dark Plan? Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Jun 2002 ---- There is a rumour circulating the PCW Offices that the returning tag team, The Dark Messiahs are planning something big in hopes of getting a shot at the tag team titles. When approached Mike Stone of the Dark Messiahs had this to say, "We are going to do everything in our power to get a shot at our Tag Team Gold." What can the Dark Messiahs be planning??? A Sneak Attack??? An Inteference??? Or just making false threats??? Who knows but stay clued to PCW to find out.
