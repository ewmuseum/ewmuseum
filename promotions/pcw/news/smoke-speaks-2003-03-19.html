Smoke Speaks

19 March 2003

Smoke Speaks Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Mar 2003 ---- Sunday night, PCW was able to do a sit down interview with one of the returning stars from the past of PCW, Semore Smoke. Pimply Faced Intern Kid has this following exclusive on Smoke’s condition and what seems to be, his new life from what was seen on his most recent promo in PCW. Here is the interview... ----------------------------------------- PFIK: Hello Semore, it’s been awhile since I’ve seen you. Semore Smoke: Hey, the same here. In fact, It’s been about six months. PFIK: So, first off, the question that is on everyone’s mind, what in the world happened to you when you were out, I mean we see you now as the leader of this major mob organization in New Jersey! Semore Smoke: Hey, I don’t know nothing about no mob, ok. But I knew I would be asked this sooner or later, so I’ll give it to you straight forward. After I left PCW, I didn’t plan on returning, in fact, I planned on flat out retirement. But then my back began to seriously bother me outside the ring, and I was forced to get the surgery that was needed. I got the surgery a few months ago, and I’ve been in rehab the following four or so months. But... what really surprised the hell out of me was the phone call I got on February 28 of this year. A strange man I had never met, had contacted me from New Jersey, a town I know very well already since a few buddies of mine live there, Jay and Silent Bob. But I left and headed up north from North Carolina in speculation of what it was about. The man had told me that he was my Uncle, and that my father had been murdered in New Jersey, and I was to go and take over our family business as his only son. I never knew my dad, so I thought it was just some crazed fan hoping to get Smoke to do a personal visit. But with curiosity, I pressed onward. I met the man I now know to be my Uncle, Uncle John is his name, John Bordon. My father’s name was Andrew Bordon and he held a business that was one of those lending companies, if you know what I mean. So to cut it short, I now run the business and live in New Jersey, which got me a few offers from old friends who are in PWC and a possible contract with me to wrestle there, but I had already contacted Ellie here at PCW about a contract renewal and extension to PCW. But that’s pretty much how my months out of action went, now let’s get to the future... PFIK: Yes, first off, what are your thoughts on the current situation of PCW? Semore Smoke: What do you mean by current situation? PFIK: I mean, with Syko The Goblin as owner, and your return in the March Madness Rumble and how you were eliminated from the rumble. Semore Smoke: Ah... I understand. Well, I’ve never been a huge fan of either Xaiver Kannon or Syko The Goblin, but I feel that it’s the companies best interest to be back in the hands of Xavier Kannon. I mean... Xavier Kannon was a bit better then Tyler Nelson, am I right? Yeah, I know I’m right. But about the rumble that’s kind of touchy. I wasn’t about to allow a rookie to this federation eliminate me, if he was going to eliminate me then I was going to take him along with me! And that’s what I did when I suplexed... what’s his name again? PFIK: AJ Trinidad. Semore Smoke: Ah, yes. That’s why I suplexed Trinidad out of the ring eliminating myself as well. AJ Trinidad may seem to have impressive numbers with the DWO World Championship, but don’t forget that I’m a former NTICW World Champion, and that title means more to the fans then a simple DWO title! PFIK: Wow, never knew you had this heated side to you before... maybe I should just end this with one last question. What are your thoughts on your Turmoil match against AJ Trinidad? Semore Smoke: Ah, it’ll be a piece of cake. I’ll go in and walk all over the rookie and then walk all over the TV Champ, Tim Walden, next week. It’s that simple. PFIK: Ok, well it was great for you to let me interview you. Semore Smoke: It’s no problem, I kind of wanted to get some of that off my chest. ----------------------------------------- The interview ends there, it tells us a lot about Semore Smoke, but how will he do in the ring? Is his back still injured? No one knows, Pimply Faced Intern Kid was too scared to ask, so we’ll just have to wait for his match against AJ Trinidad to take place before we know...
