Fired Wrestlers React After Sabotage

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fired Wrestlers React After Sabotage Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Apr 2002 ---- After the latest firing of many of PCW's finest wrestlers, questions have come up regarding what will happen to these superstars now. Many wrestlers however, are still just shocked over their sudden firing brought on by the inspections of Rupert Healey. "I have no idea. I think Duke will do the best he can, but I'm going to continue to look in other places. Frankly, I'm not sure if the PCW ring will ever be graced by Crunk again. I know that RUSH is opening and NTICW is looking like a good place to go, it's on the uprise. It's possible I've had my last match, but PCW has definitely not seen the end of the Malice Crew just yet." Those were the words of The Rush, who seemed very layed back about his firing. The Pharaoh had a different approach to the situation. " A champion AND a god fired from PCW: This is preposterous. I will be signed back and for more than the salary that I was getting before. Doctor Ruper Healey will not stay in my way of destroying Akira Akiyama. Jason Core, another Malice Crew member had something to say as well, "I've been in wrestling a long time, and I've had a history of injury, but I'm not ready to quit just yet. My contract was unguaranteed anyway. I know that myself and Rush (Mason) will sit down and chat about our futures and we'll talk to Duke and Tom(Lexian) as well." Lastly, Bobby Stormbringer commented on his abrupt firing. "Well I assume this is all about me being one of the up and coming new guys. Healey is clouding Duke's mind so that he's even allowing the firing of the Perfect Storm. I'll be back soon, and baby that's no B.S." While Legend Inc, Big Alan Steele, and THE Multiple Man couldn't be reached for comment, it was reported that Tim Walden was looking into other feds where he could continue to SNAP~! OSW Owner Jane Reagan has already annouced that any of the fired wrestlers that wish to sign with a "Superior Federation" should come knock at her door.
