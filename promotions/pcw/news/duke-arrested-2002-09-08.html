Duke Arrested! Posted by Sandy Wong on 8 Sep 2002 ---- We have recieved word that late Sunday night, Duke Williams, the President of Primetime Championship Wrestling and member of the "Front Office" stable has been arrested by the officers of the Californian Police Force. The charge? Spraying "Duke Williams Rules, Hoss" on the front of a Police Car with silly string. He has been released on a $1000 bail and has been ordered not to leave the country during the course of the investigation. Eye Witness, Gareth Green had this to say... "It was horrible... green silly string... the pain... the agony... he was like a redneck possessed, but without a wife to beat... oh the horror." The case is expected to go to court within the next two weeks.
