Damn You Nebraska

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Damn You Nebraska Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Jan 2003 ---- Omaha, Nebraska-From a standpoint only known from everybody watching, Turmoil was set to go of without a hitch from Omaha, but instead you have a hick run the operations and get the show held back on everybody else's television for TWO days. That is until we hired a smarty to fix it and thus Turmoil was up, putting the show in showstopping, Heather Nova found it a pleasant suprise to meet Flaymin Aymin, Delores Marx found "Monkeys" a bit bigger than she last thought, Brock Newbluud plays Sign On The Dotted line with Cassius Deamont, and two heated rivals rake to the microphone to show who is the better man. For this and even two kick ass matches, head on down to the Turmoil banner and click results.
