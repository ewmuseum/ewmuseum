Happy B-Day Streak

28 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Happy B-Day Streak Posted by Sandy Wong on 28 Oct 2001 ---- A mysterious phone call came into our newsroom this morning in regard to the life and time of Ethan Giroux aka Meanstreak. "The 28th of October is Ethan Gabriel Giroux's birthday," said the anonymous caller, as if reading a preprepared statement. "He went into the Shady Hills Sanitarium at the age of 16. Only three weeks after that, he was sent into the operating room for the legendary lobotomy. After the lobotomy, the first year was a complete mess for him. He spent 3/4 of his time in solitary, mumbling aloud to himself. Soon following, Ethan shaped up out of nowhere and began spending that time he did in solitary, in the weight room of the sanitarium. He was there three more years after that...and then PCW found him just two years after, scouting out the current pit fighting ground he was competing in. So, if you add a year to how long he's been with PCW and peg his birthday on the 28th of October, that would make Ethan 23 years old." Our own PCW representative scratched his head at the anonymous tipster's math. A simple "Sure." was all he responded with. When asked where this anonymous source got all of this information, the source cackled and said, "I just stumbled onto the documents back at his old home in Lanore, Louisiana. It's been condemned for years, but his father apparently left behind some of the copies of the old medical records. Quite careless if you ask me." As always, we'll keep you updated on this story.
