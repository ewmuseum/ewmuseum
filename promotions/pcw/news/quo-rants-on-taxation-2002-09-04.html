Quo Rants On Taxation

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Quo Rants On Taxation Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Sep 2002 ---- Spreading quickly thoughout PCW, the news of "Taxing” wrestlers has become a big issue. Several wrestlers had commented on it and when it caught up to Statuz Quo, all hell broke loose. "Wut?! Tax fo' motha fuckin' wut?! Shit, I worked harder than Chris Matthews suckin' dick fo' da trailer park, Aimz workin' on a friday at da street corner, El Spiffy! crossin' da river tryin' to get into da U.S., and every other cracka 'n WT all together fuckin' each otha at they annual cracka-fest! Wait wait, let me correct myself, NO ONE works as hard as Aimz!" From there Statuz Quo bursted out laughing before getting a phone call and talking for the majority of the interview. When he came back off the phone, he smoked a blunt, drunk a forty, bitch slapped the reporter for being white then continued... "Ain't no motha fucka takin' tax out my damn check cuz I'ma act my color 'n a minute up 'n diz bitch! They don't kno' me, I will fuck a cracka up, fuckin' around wit me! Ya'll gon' have to call da police up 'n diz piece cuz if I come up short of sum damn money, I'ma busts sum caps 'n diz asses around herre!" he ranted before finishing with, "White people... Holla back youngin'".
