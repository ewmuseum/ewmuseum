Post-PPV Personnel Report

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-PPV Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Jan 2003 ---- After recovering from the events art the end of PCW’s Shanghaied 3 Pay-Per-View, PCW Personnel Director announced that PCW had made several new signings, many of which showed up at the huge event. Alan Ackart and Dorn Tracer both signed PCW contracts last week, and already seem to have had some disagreements, especially if it was Dorn behind the torching of Alan’s locker room. The pair will make their debuts on Wednesday in a Four-Way match also involving Mark Michigan and Brianna Washington. Former PCW Tag Team Champion Mark Makonie has been re-signed after referee Florien Liebig recommended him to PCW’s CEO, Xavier Kannon. Mark will make his return match on Friday Fusion against none other than Young Tiger, who’s re-signing was also announced. Brandi was also pleased to announce the signing of PTC top talent, Erik Erratic who made an immediate impact by attacking Jackson Rust at Shanghaied following his Rising Star Title match. PCW has high hopes for Erik and are expecting him to slot in well following the good progress made by the likes of Storm, Rust and Windsor. All new signings have a lot of expectation on their shoulders, while those re-signed to PCW will be looking to prove themselves all over again in the build up to March Madness.
