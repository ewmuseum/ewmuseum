Mark Makonie News

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Makonie News Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Sep 2002 ---- After the events that happened on this weeks Fusion we found Mark Makonie checking out of a local hospital. After he got outside of the hospital this is what he had to say. "This is the last string that all of them niggers had to pull for me to be one enranged man. Everyone in PCW just better wait after this week because everyone is going to be seeing a new Mark Makonie around here." We asked Mark Makonie about his rapping and this is what he had to say. "That fucking coon has me rappin and says I can make money off of that. Well I ain't going to do it anymore. I don't know what that coon was thinking when he asked me to go into the rap business. Rap is just some monkey music."
