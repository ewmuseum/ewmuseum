Not Quite a Malice Night

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not Quite a Malice Night Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Mar 2002 ---- Alberquerque, New Mexico- The Malice Crew was formed a few weeks ago to act as a "Posse" of sorts for the President of PCW, Duke Williams. Their job was to make sure that people like Tyler Nelson, The Chef, and Frisco stayed in line and didn't cause trouble. The Crew had been somewhat successful in this endeavor thus far.....until Sabotage rolled around. What happened? Who got beat down? Who is missing? You'll have to read to find out. Also on Sabotage, Miyagawa makes a statement to the World Champion Boda as well as a challenge to both Chris Chase and Corey Williams. We also are introduced to the newly hired financial advisor that is supposed to save PCW from it's downward spiral. All this and a whole bunch more so click on the menu bar and scroll down to recent results, then hit the Sabotage banner to see all the action!
