Aymin For More Trouble

16 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After last week's trashing of Sydney Airport by Flaymin, PCW officials have finally recieved a bill for damages totalling a hefty $480,000. Recent meetings between Flaymin and Jane Reagan were uneventful and no real progress was made in respect to who was responsible for paying the mammoth bill. "Flaymin was careless, thus we believe he should pay the bills for the damages," was the only comment made by Jane Reagan. Aymin also issued a single statement, and single statment only. "I ain't paying sh*t, I'm not 'legally obligateded'. This means PCW has to pay this bill whether they like it or not- They shouldn't expect any 'goodwill' from me". Flaymin Aymin is best known for accidentally driving a tractor into a crowd of spectators at a PCW show, injuring 11 people. The lawsuits associated with the incident eventually lead to former PCW owner The Phantom Booker selling PCW to Tyler Nelson of Greed Inc. More on this saga will be revealed in due time, but it seems PCW may have no other option but to pay the $480,000 fine.
