Outlett Recharged

16 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More rumblings of a Wesley Outlett return to PCW as Wes wrestled for the first time since his leg injury at the hands of the Media. Wes who got a pinfall decision agianst Akiru Keyosha at Fridays Power Japan Pro Wrestling event in Tokyo Japan was said to have worn heavy tape on his right hand which he had broken shortly after coming to PCW and which was blamed by many to be the reason Wes preformed well below the expectations of many wrestling experts while with the fed. In a post match press conference with members of the Japanese press, Samantha Sparks responded to questions about Wes's possible PCW return by saying."I have in fact approached Jane Reagan with interest in bringing Mr.Outlett back to PCW but I have yet to hear back from her." If Wes was to return one thing most wrestling insiders agree on is it will not be on his old high dollar bonus laiden contract he signed when entering PCW but instead on a much lower pay deal. More on this as it breaks.
