Signings and Returns

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Signings and Returns Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Feb 2002 ---- Former five-time PCW Tag Team champion "The Innovator" Brock Newbludd has returned from a minor neck injury he suffered a couple of months back. However, without Stephen Blood, Brock will be forced to show his true talent as a singles wrestler. He comes back Friday night against "Amazing" Mike Storm. Asa "Fro" Fountain has been re-signed to PCW after his brief retirement. The members of The Ream Team have been signed to short term contracts. Ultimate Warlord, Boca del Inferno, Shadowman, and "Precious" Peter Love will see some action in the ring over the next few weeks. Former PCW standouts Banshee and Prophesy have also been re-signed to short terms deals. Former DWF wrestler Lance Bell has been signed by PCW. He will make his PCW debut next Tuesday night, Febuary 12th, against Jon Kano. Former PCW Television and Cruiserweight champion Kramer Wild Wing has been re-signed by the company. Kramer will make his return next Sunday night against potential #1 contender Timo Bolamba. Finally, and most shockingly, Token Weed has been signed to PCW. The move was done by Reagan in the 11th hour just before she announced her ownership of OSW. Although Token had a rough go-around the last time, he is expected to contribute this time around. He will face Xavier Kannon next Sunday on Sabotage. More announcements when they happen.
