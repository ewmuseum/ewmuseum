One in the Same?

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One in the Same? Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Feb 2002 ---- New Zealand- Rumors are always circulating. Whether they be in the locker room or on the internet, there are always rumors about certain people going around. A couple of those rumors going around involve people being the same person. Ones most noticable are Hank and Ultimate Warlord being the same guy and Clyde and Boca del Inferno being the same guy. So, to find out, PCW officials had the two pairs meet in first round tournament action. What happened? Would there even be a match? You'll just have to see for yourself to find out. Also on Sabotage: Akiyama makes waves both in and out of the ring. Eight men move one step closer to a World title shot at March Madness. And who will be the next PCW star to go to OSW? To find out all this and more, check out Sabotage found under the Recent Results link.
