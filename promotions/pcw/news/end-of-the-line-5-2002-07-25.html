End of the Line #5

End of the Line #5 Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jul 2002 ---- Well, it's about time for someone to post their views on our pay-per-view, and I figured I'd do it. I'll be doing a PPV recap once the show is up, but in the meantime, here's my preliminary thoughts on this. I submit this as myself. I handle The End, but I'm just doing this special edition of End of the Line out-of-character. That way I can do a better job of analysis. I suppose the usual thing to do is hide your identity, or write in an assumed character. but I don't have the time or care for it right now, so *sticks out tongue* tough noogies to that. Ah, yes, PCW's first Wrestle Vessel pay-per-view EVER, the first in a series of PPV's that will be an entire Caribbean tour! We've got a full card (as always) and all the titles are being defended. Most of the roster is scheduled to compete, as the bookers don't want too many people resting on their laurels. It is a solid card from top to bottom, but I still feel like there are some things that I can talk about. This won't be a repeat of the "Wrestle Vessel Picks" thread that Xavier Kannon created on the WT Boards, no I'll give a little rundown of each match, give my view of what ideally should happen and give what may be a slightly different prediction of what I expect to happen. So, let's get started. King of the Sand Castle Match PCW Rising Star Title Referee: Leo Micelli Venneman Pendragon (c) vs Trigger vs Kris Burden vs Jaden vs Syko the Goblin vs Scorpion vs Ricky Thunder The first match of the night has virtually NO buildup whatsoever. Clusterf*ck numero uno. Let's break it down. First, I'm going to go back to the match in which the Rising Star Title changed hands. I think it was a Turmoil, but I remember the match clearly because I wrote and judged it. So, let's look at that as a standard for judging this match. Venneman Pendragon won the match on the boards. His RPs were good and consistent. Closest behind him was Scorpion, who I think put out more RPs, but the overall quality was only slightly lower than Pendragon. Burden was third in line for performance, doing well in his early RPs, but then trailing off toward the end. And Trigger, the only other person to put up a decent showing on the RP boards, suffered in my judgment because all of his RPs were just Trigger talking to himself with no action, no interaction, no ambiance. As for Heaven Rayne and Jaden, frankly they disappointed me on the boards. Yes, the RP deadline isn't until the day of the show, but it does look better to put up some RPs before then. So, what does it look like now? There are two new faces in this match with Syko the Goblin and Ricky Thunder. Syko is always entertaining to read, and his character is such a blast to pay attention to. There are your standard big men and then there are the slightly smaller guys. Syko is fun because he's weird and evil, but of such diminuitive stature that you just have to laugh and enjoy whatever he does. Ricky Thunder is the man who came into PCW after the mass-firing and quickly broke in with a fantastic showing in the huge Extreme Title match that earned Miyagawa the championship. He looked even better in follow-up matches, and the Ricky Thunder momentum train seemed unstoppable until he had to leave on injury. He's back now, and if we see a full return to pre-injury form, Ricky Thunder will win this title, and only drop it because it's time to move up to a higher division. Any advice going into this match? Pendragon, you're the champ, and you'll do just fine if you keep up the work that you put into winning the title. There's a bit of a curse that most of us who've been paying attention to PCW for a long while tend to believe in (in recent history, the curse tends to take affect more often than not; I'm REALLY hoping that won't be the case here): the curse that when you win the Rising Star Title, nothing happens. Either by lack of a solid division, or lack of a feud, the Rising Star Title changes hands for a brief moment of notice and then goes back into obscurity, dragging the titleholder with it. That shouldn't have to be the case. What would be ideal is if there was a Rising Star division of 4-6 people. There seems to be that, since Pendragon, Scorpion, and Thunder are all relatively new, and Trigger, Burden, and Jaden, sad to say, haven't really proven to me that they could move up any more than to the Extreme Division. But what also needs to happen is that at least two of you get together and FEUD! Nothing brings more positive attention to the Rising Star Division than two people who work a good feud together to get us interested in it. At this point, while there could be very good RPs all around coming out o fhtis match, the fact that the match was thrown together to get these people on the card will make it suffer a bit in the overall "importance factor." Well, advice... Pendragon, you're fine. Just make sure that in some way, shape, or form you try to do your best to relate all your RPs to your match (if it takes, at the very least, one line saying "This reminds me of my match...") Scorpion, just make sure that you keep up your quality in all the RPs you do. Thunder, welcome back; big things are expected of you on your return, don't let us down. Trigger, try to vary your RPs; all of your RPs for your RS title defense were the same dialogue between you and you. That's okay once or so in a match, but the reading gets repetitive if that's all you do. Other than that, your quality would be just fine. Burden, just put out a better showing overall. And Jaden, this is your BIGGEST chance to redeem yourself. Syko, just do what you normally do, and if you have to win the title, so be it. So, what needs to happen in this match? There need to be some lines drawn. Syko is here for star power, but he doesn't deserve to be this low on the ladder. He'd serve better credible purpose in the Cruiserweight Division that Brian, Oakley, and I are trying to help rebuild after last month's Cruiserweight feud between Sunshine, Rayne, and Loc. So, eliminate Syko, and you've got Burden, Trigger, Jaden, Scorpion, Thunder, and Pendragon. Pendragon already seems ready to move out of this division, and a feud with Syko could be good for both of them. So, that leaves Scorpion, Thunder, Burden, Trigger, and Jaden. Scorpion and Thunder look good. Maybe they can get a feud working. These two can RP, and I'd like to see some Rising Star in-cards to remind me that the division does indeed exist. Burden and Trigger somewhat feuded for this title, but the feud was disappointing overall, and while it feels like they're established enough not to need this title, they still need to be in this division to get down the feuding side of things. Jaden is just kinda there. Heaven Rayne's not in the match, and that seems to be the only person Jaden has tried to feud with since entering PCW. What's my pick to win the match? A tie between Scorpion and Thunder, hopefully leading into a feud. Pendragon can retain, but drop it soon to move into a feud with Syko outside of the RS division. Fatal Four Way PCW Extreme Title Referee: Dark Gimmick Rush Mason vs Kramer Wild Wing vs Violence Jack vs Flaymin Aymin Realistically, this is a match between Rush & KWW, and VJ & Aymin. Ah, yes... the Extreme Title being defended in what is actually two matches condensed into one. Going to the RPs, I can't see that KWW will concentrate on much besides Rush, and likewise Aymin with VJ and vice-versa. What should happen? The match should go to Rush to help stabilize the Extreme Title and have he and KWW continue in a feud that got started mid-month. That way, they can use the extra time to finish it. The feud is good for both of them (they need it), and although there's been not as many in-cards as they could have fit to help things, there's still been something. Rush and KWW have worked with this to make it something that you can expect to see. The Uprising members are to have something of their own individual feuds to help them get over as a stable, and Rush has embraced the feud with KWW. The Violence Jack/Aymin feud, however, is something I feel that the bookers and matchwriters are holding together. There's not much chemistry, I feel there. We all know Aymin can hold his own in a feud (and anyone who thinks otherwise wasn't paying attention to Aymin/El Diablo). VJ I'm not too sure of. He's never really worked a feud since being here. And this feud doesn't seem to be much besides senseless post-match attacks. So, by this, I wouldn't put the title on Aymin or VJ because I expect them to continue the feud for the time being, and given the choices, Aymin/VJ for the Extreme Title doesn't seem that compelling. Violence Jack is a great gimmick for the Extreme Title. I don't know how much work he's doing to put himself over outside of his RPs, but it doesn't seem like much. What will happen, I belive, is that the match will break into the two matches I talked about, and the person who dominates their respective feuding opponent the most will get the win. Triple Threat Strap Match Referee: Jeremy Johnson "Controversial" Corey Williams vs Jon Kano vs Semore Smoke The continuation of the CCW/Kano feud, with Smoke coming in just to fill space and put him over. So, there's supposed to be someone coming in to replace TMM? What are my bets they get the usual duty of beating the hell out of CCW in a run-in on his match? CCW/Kano in-cards are getting to be repetitive. I need to see something exciting from them. At this rate, they'll never get out of a feud with each other. If it was more interesting, that's one thing, but now it's just where they are stuck in a feud because there's nowhere else to put them. Semore Smoke is in this match because he needed a feud. He's been put in here, but I don't know if perhaps he's been saving his full skill for some other feud that he REALLY wants. Now, if he can finish up with Smokie Tang... There's not much I'm expecting out of this match, except for CCW/Kano to continue their feud, and Smoke to get something helpful and productive going for him by moving to the Television Division. Gauntlet Match Referee: Bob Charlie The Chef vs The Refugees and Alexis Parshin The Refugees... they don't have many RPs on the board. You wouldn't believe how far I had to scroll down to find something. As for Parshin, I'm disappointed that someone hailed as the future of PCW coming in has yet to really live up to the hype. He's a good RPer, just needs to RP on a more regular basis. This match has to come down to some sort of angle, because The Chef as an RPer will overpower the opposing team. What should happen, Chef wins. What will happen, if Chef doesn't win, it won't be a clean job. PLC Match Glass Panels, Ladders and Chairs PURE Junior Heavyweight Title PCW Cruiserweight Title Referee: Dustin Downey The End(c)(c) vs. Charlie Loc vs. Alexander Miyagawa Yes, that's right. I'm coming in as a DOUBLE CHAMPION, BABY! Sorry, had to get that off my chest. Charlie Loc has up some good RPs on the board already, don't know about Miya yet. I should have anywhere from 1-3 up by Friday, with more hopefully on the way very soon after. This match has some buildup, but I'll be the first to admit that it's not as much as I'd have hoped for. Still, I think it'll be pretty good overall. This match will involve a lot of high spots and broken glass (better damn well be). The match will definitely not be for the weak of heart. So, what should happen? I plan to stay in the Cruiserweight division, though Miya really doesn't need to be this low. He should be up in the TV or Continental division. As for Loc, maybe he'll stay here, but he can realistically go to higher places. So what should happen? I'd like to retain both titles. What will happen? We'll just see about that. Four Corners Elimination Match PCW Tag Team Titles Referee: Sally Saddle The Heartbreakers vs Pretty Cool Guys vs Politically Incorrect vs Akiyama Heartbreakers and PCG have a pretty good feud coming into this. Although I don't think they could match the Recording Contract Match from last week's Fusion. As for PI, they have some heat with Akiyama, but more with the Afghans, who aren't in this match yet. Akiyama should win this match to keep up the interest in the tag division, and also because the singles field needs to thin out at the top before he can jump over there. Akiyama brings more stability and credibility to the PCW Tag Titles, and PCG and Heartbreakers should take this time to finish their feud so one team for sure can move up to challenge the tag titles. PI is not as dominant as they were when they first came in, but remain a strong favorite in the division. The most solid match so far on the card. Last Chance Match If Shabazz Loses He Will Never Again Get a Shot at the TV Title PCW Television Title Referee: Florien Liebig John Crichton vs Jordan X. Shabazz Crichton himself admits he really didn't deserve the title, and though I think we all expected the feud to continue, it just kinda dropped off without rhyme or reason. This match didn't get the buildup it should have. Realistically, the first match between them will probably be the one that people pay attention to more when remembering the overall Crichton/Shabazz feud, and in my opinion, it'll be up to last-minute in-cards by these two, and especially the matchwriter assigned this match, to put these two over, as they have actually dropped in stature from last month. A vacation by Crichton, and a stable by Shabazz have gotten them to this place now. What needs to happen? Shabazz needs to win the title, and have a feud with Miyagawa or Semore Smoke over the title. Crichton maybe can stay in the division if he gets his old workrate back, otherwise there's always space in the Extreme Division. What will happen? I'm betting on Shabazz winning the title. Jamaican Jail Cell Match Takes Place in a Jamaican Jail Cell Referee: Ibe Black Jason Snow vs Rent A Villain Ibe Black? I wrote him in the Uprising v. Makonie/Duzza match as Ibe Dark! Crap! Anyway, this match will be close. Rent-A-Villain needs to kick it up a notch, because following the runner-up position in RPG3 and coming in with a great workrate and RP quality, Snow is on fire right now. At this point, they both seem to be trying to get over as evil, but evil is cute in RAV's case. Snow won't be going for cute, and has the more diabolical edge in this match. Between the two, Snow has had more success, but coming back into PCW, Rent-A-Villain (as RAH) beat Snow. Will the rematch be different? Perhaps, we'll have to see the match that comes out of this. What needs to happen? RAV needs a win more to establish himself in some division. Snow will go on to the highest heights of PCW anyway. Though win you look at it like that, Snow's winning will only serve to drive the point home. Depths of Hell Match Match to Take Place in a Pit Dug in the Beach of Montego Bay Referee: Jeremy Johnson Clyde vs 'Saint' Chris Chase Um... Right. Both men are excellent RPers, and they can put out good in-cards. Still, I'll have to go back and read the past shows carefully (if they're up in teh archives) because I for the life of me can't figure out where the hell this match came from. Had to be something to lead to it, Clyde and Chase are too good and important to "just be booked." What shoulw happen? This match can go eeither way, I'd like a long feud between them if possible. Three Way Dance Continental Title Match Referee: Bob Charlie Darkstar(c) vs. Legend Inc. Tom Lexian vs. Duke Williams Darkstar defends his title against two men who arguably have more heat between each other than with Darkstar. A favorable position for him, he can stand back and destroy the one who falls closest to him. Darkstar can make the Continental Title look SO good if he keeps it and enters a singles feud (since he's got a workrate back), but Lexian and Duke can be just as good as champions. This could stay a three-way feud for a while, this is where these three men need to be: helping to re-establish the Continental Title Division after Xavier Kannon's reign (which may not be matched for a while to come). What needs to happen? This is another match that can go either way, but I'd like a singles match between Lexian and Duke. Still, all men are great and can be wonderful champions. Sarcaphagus Match Referee: Dustin Downey Xavier Kannon vs The Pharaoh Pharaoh went over on Turmoil. Kudos to him. Can he pull out a win over Kannon? This feud has been going on since their days together in NTICW, so there's DEFINITELY history there. Kannon does better in singles matches between the two, but let's not forget that Carmoon and Zen Dula, Pharaoh's bodyguards, both beat Kannon. He's 0-2 against Pharaoh-lackeys. So maybe this shouldn't have been a Sarcophagus match. Maybe it needed to be a Xavier Kannon against The Pharaoh's Legions of Followers match. Besides, I'm not an expert at Ancient Egyptian ANYTHING, but aren't there multiple layers to a sarcophagus? Do you have to put them into one, or two, or three? Maybe there could be something where you entomb them in the process that turned Darkman/Arnold Vosloo into The Mummy. Oh well, that's something for a Clyde-written match. What needs to happen? Kannon needs to win this. Kannon needs to be in a singles feud with BAS. The only way he can do that is to go over here. What will happen? Pharaoh has a chance, but from his own admission, outside IRL factors are getting to be a pain for him. Pharaoh has to find some way to push everything aside and buckle down and out-RP Kannon. He can go over in this match, but even then he wouldn't win the title because he needs more grooming for the division first. What will happen? It will be Xavier Kannon by the dirty win. Argentine Death Match PCW World Heavyweight Title Referee: Buzz Meacham Frisco vs Big Alan Steele Everyone's counting out Frisco here. And so am I. Just kidding. Frisco will make this a close match, probably even closer than Pharaoh/Kannon. Frisco could possibly even beat BAS in wrestling, only to lose by DQing himself or something like that (though it's probably not an option). BAS has been the ultimate face dominant demi-god champion, but this match should be the time where BAS's HUMANITY should show, thus building up the Pharaoh/Kannon winner's confidence in going into the match to win it. What needs to happen? This is probably still too early to take the belt off of BAS. This match is definitely too early to take the belt off of BAS. He has to win to go on to the wildly anticipated Kannon/BAS match. Main Event Thunderdome Island Match Hell in a Cell Afloat in Montego Bay PCW World Heavyweight Title Referee: Leo Micelli Winner of Kannon/Pharaoh v Winner of BAS/Frisco BAS/Kannon is what I'm expecting. What nearly everyone is expecting. THIS FEUD NEEDS TO HAPPEN!!! Both men can pull it off very well. A BAS/Kannon singles feud will redefine the division, and could even bring up PCW as a whole. It coule easily be at the top of not only WT, but places like The News. Let's see it happen! There's my analysis on the PPV. The recap will follow up once it hits the airwaves. As a conclusion, this sucks, but oh well. See ya. This is the End of the Line.
