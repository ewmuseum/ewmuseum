The Chef Comments on Rehab and Groin Problems

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Chef Comments on Rehab and Groin Problems Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Sep 2002 ---- The Chef was tracked down by moronic press members with nothing better to do at a Rehab Center in Vermont. PCW's very own Culinary Conquistador gave some comments about recent events in PCW and in his pathetic life. When asked about Kannon's Front Office Tax, the Chef replied, "I'm a liberal, so I don't mind the taxes, what I do mind though is that the money is probably going straight to Ellie's Shampoo and Conditioner in One Fund". When asked about Syko's apparent interest in Bertha the Chef scoffed at the idea of a Syko/Bertha relationship. "That little runt can flirt all he wants to. Bertha isn't going to fall for his charms, and even if she did I can always make another clone..." When asked about his addiction to Pepto Bismol, the Chef had this to say: "There's a bunch of addictive stuff out there. I mean that Campbell's Chunky Soup has got to have Nicotine in it or something. What I'm really concerned about though is the episode with the pink foam coming out of my mouth. In France having an addiction is considered the "in" thing to do. Now I am a trendy fellow, but there are celebrities out there with far more embarrassing addictions than mine, so go harass them already!" When asked what he would be getting back into the ring soon the Sultan of the Saute Pan said, "I imagine I'll be back soon. My handler's hands are still cramped from all that match writing. I tell ya Kannon is quite a slave driver! But seriously, I'll be back, and I'm looking to get into the World Title picture, and possibly retire abruptly again after winning it!" The Chef went on, "My groin is doing a lot better know, but if I want to return soon I'll have to wear a specially designed harness to make sure there are no more tears down there. A cup with the French Flag emblem would be a good idea too, got that Miya?" The Chef then knocked his Clafouti off the table shouting, "Ronald McDonald could make a better Clafouti than this wretched cafeteria! I bet they only put 1 1/2 tablespoons of amaretto in here, and there is no confectioner's sugar to garnish it! Rat Bastards!!!" He was promptly taken back into the center for "treatment".
