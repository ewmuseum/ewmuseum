Personnel & Injury Report

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Personnel & Injury Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Dec 2002 ---- Following the events of Decimation, Vilas Lavagna asked for his release from PCW and it was granted. After his hard fought match against El Romantico in which he was defeated by the Spaniard, Lavagna thought it was the honourable thing to do to offer his resignation. PCW eyes will be watching him, and he’s said to be on good terms with both the Fed and the man who defeated him, El Romantico. PCW Personnel Director Brandi Wilson has finally been officially cleared by Dr. Rick Niviera following her rib injury. She isn’t thought to be at 100%, as shown after she took a shot to the stomach at the PPV, but after threatening Rick, she gained clearance and will compete on Friday Fusion to earn a shot at the new TV Champion. As seen in the Main Event, both the Pharaoh and DarkStar have been cleared to return, however following the brutal assault by the dark allies, the Pharaoh was taken straight to the local hospital for stitches to his wounds. Vanilla Skillz was checked out post-PPV after the attack by Chris Matthews and isn’t expected to miss his AD announcing gig this coming Sunday. Both Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams were treated for cuts to all over their bodies following their Cage match, as well as the CEO consulting with the PCW dentist following the Stunner he received onto the Cage from Brock Newbludd. Gideon Poe, along with Mordred were both taken to the local hospital to be checked up on following their altercation, but neither should miss any TV time. Due to the sheer length and brutality of their matches, Dante Jackson, Rush Mason, Totally Tubular Tommy, El Spiffy!, Marvin Harris, Stimpo, Ebola II, Marvin Harris and Joe Bananas were all said to be exhausted following the event, all were checked by PCW physicians and all are clear to continue with PCW’s schedule.
