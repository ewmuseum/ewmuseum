PCW Radio Recap

2 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Duke Williams hosted an excellent edition of Malice on Saturday night. - He began the show by running down the Sabotage card. Cyborg called in - Cyborg, who was probably from another federation, said that PCW sucks, and recieved a verbal beat down from the Malice Man. The Phantom Booker called - TPB said that it was rumoured that Ivan Stanislav would meet Jeremy Howard for the PCW Championship at Decimation. - TPB talked about New Breed and said that he thinks they are for real. - Duke and TPB talked about the PCW Pavilion and how it had been quite some time since PCW had been back to its base. They went on to run down the card. - They joked about the Two Ton/Kramer Wild Wing matchup for Friday and compared their appetites for KFC’s ten piece bucket. - TPB talked about Boda’s improvement, noting that quite some time ago he was considered a joke in PCW. TPB reminded the world that Wil Perdue once punked out Boda. - DITR was brought up. Duke said that he liked Red Rock to win. TPB said that Duke has no taste. TPB went on to run through the brackets, saying that he liked all the remaining wrestlers. - TPB talked about how when IWO talent comes to PCW, they do nothing but disappoint. He trashed Kent Anthanson for his recent comments against PCW, reminding Kent he went only 1-7-1 during his tenure with PCW. - They talked PHD’s career, and whether he will end up in FWO or PCW. TPB said that it is rumoured that Asylum have a great interest in PHD and that several other offers had come in for his services. - When asked about big disappointments, TPB noted that Johnny Raindance has been a huge disappointment. He also said that Lack O Stress has a spot waiting for him in PCW, but only if LoS was ready to perform. Next to call in was Ed. - Ed spent most of his time talking about his injury and geeking out female listeners. Ivan Stanislav called. *Ivan was inaudible* - Duke asks Ivan when he will defend the title. - Duke claims that the only way Ivan was able to get the World Championship was by joining forces with Management. - Duke says that Jeremy Howard will take Ivan’s title. Jay Phoenix called in. - Jay Phoenix talked about Vampir Nosferatu and how he is happy to be in PCW. Jeremy Howard called in next. - Howard talked about Ivan Stanislav and Duke supported the notion that JKH could take Ivan’s title. - Jeremy Howard said that he can take Snow. Ivan Stanislav called back. - Duke claimed that Ivan’s values were flawed, saying that a communist had no place in Management. - Duke talked about how long it took Ivan to win the Championship, insulting how many times he has defended it since. Ivan claimed that he would defend the title against anyone, anytime. Snow was next to call in. - Duke asked who in New Breed would be the man to step up and take a World title shot. Snow answered that everyone in New Breed would answer themselves. Duke said that last week Timo said Snow was the guy. - Duke asked Snow if he felt that he could take Jeremy Howard. Snow answered that he could, and went on to insult Asylum. Ed called back. - Ed said that New Breed will blow over. - He says that their egos will tear them apart, noting Snow’s arrogance. - Duke says that he feels the numbers will work against Asylum. Red Rock called in. -Red Rock promised victory in the Diamond in the Rough Tourney. The Phantom Booker called in to finish the night off. - TPB talked about Ivan’s character and how deep it was. He shot down any comparison between Ivan Stanislav in the Bulshoviks of the WWF. - When asked who TPB felt could come up and possibly someday win the World Championship, Big Alan Steele, Jacob Yoder, Chris Chase, and Jay Vyper were among those mentioned. - TPB said that there were many great columnists in PCW right now. - TPB mentioned that Ed’s stock has fallen. Duke closed the show and began the drunken post show.
