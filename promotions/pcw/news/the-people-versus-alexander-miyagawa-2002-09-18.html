The People Versus Alexander Miyagawa

18 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The People Versus Alexander Miyagawa Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Sep 2002 ---- Milwaukee, Wisconsin-- On October the 6th eight men have a chance to become the PCW World Champion by being the only one of those eight to win two matches in one night. However, on Turmoil two of those men, Duke Williams and Alexander Miyagawa faced off on no less than three occasions during different matches; however on the final occasion... it could be called somewhat perminent. Also on Turmoil, Akiyama and the Pharaoh had several runins leading up and after their tag team match against each other. Cyclonica prooved a repeat offender as she targeted Pharaoh's wife just as she targetted Anarchy this time last week. Speaking of Anarchy, she and Brandi face off together to determine who the Cruiserweight and Rising Star Champion will be... with the Rising Star Title being retired after the match... and still speaking of Anarchy, what sort of chaos reigned in during the tag team match between Redemption Song and Duzza and Makonie? All this and more on Turmoil this week, avaliable on the dropdown at the top or on the image map at the bottom.
