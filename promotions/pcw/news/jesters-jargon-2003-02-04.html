Jester’s Jargon

Jester’s Jargon Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Feb 2003 ---- Since the columns area has been dead for a number of weeks and it’s last article was about as informative as a Japanese bus depot time table I have decided too reflect over the current state of PCW >=) With the January polls looming, this month has been open house in terms of votes as a hoard of people stepped up to the batting plate. We have seen Big Alan Steele’s record eclipsed by ‘Now’ former Champion Clyde; we have seen the re-emergence of the Rising Star Championship and Xavier Kannon pretty much etched his name into the Hall Of Fame. The Poll An impressive Xavier Kannon has dictated the last couple of polls and that trend may continue, as he has been the center of controversy. Buying, then later abolishing the RUSH franchise he managed to tie this angle into his existing feud with the insolent Duke William’s creating two great Pay-Per-View matches back to back. Winning the Tag Team titles, Xavier Kannon may wrap up the January award but not before many of the roster can put forward their respective claims. As mentioned earlier, Clyde wrote his name into the record books with a title reign that stretched over a hundred days, thus making the demon a worthy candidate. Then there’s the man who de-thrones him, Jason Snow who comes off the back of this victory as a four time World Heavyweight Champion, unrivalled by any other person gracing the Wrestle Online community. Not bad for a man who up until recently had slipped down the ladder and was competing with men affiliated with the Continental division. Talking of which, the Continental Championship has been red hot with Totally Tubular Tommy losing his tag label and making the transition appear easy as he strangles his grasp of the title in some impressive defenses against some of the divisions biggest assets in Skylar Green, Dante Jackson and the now departed Rush Mason. Speaking of green, both Nerezza and Gaz Green have both made the transition from tag to singles in an entertaining manner with their feud, something that has escaladed since pre-Decimation. With the Rising Star Championship back amongst the prestige of PCW, Akiyama making an immediate impact on his return and the new influx of talent clawing their way into the spotlight, February’s Poll will be very interesting…. Shanghaied Simply an awesome event with some feuds drawing to a closure and new ones emerging from the aftermath, it was another great show made possible by those who have made the effort, be it you the role-players and those behind the scenes (The staff). With the loss of certain talent Shanghaied showcased another crop to watch, no, mark out for in Dorn Tracer, Violence Jack and Erik Erratic. With these onboard and the injury list seizing up and news of the returns of Akiyama and Vennenan Pendragon then March Madness may eclipse the standards set by Shanghaied. Final Thoughts All in all it has been a great start to the year by PCW and with other feds nipping at our heels let’s hope we can maintain our already high standards. No real concern but lets hope we can get the tag division moving again, with that rolling as it were mid-2002 then the best WO fed will just get better…. Watch out for the January Polls!!!! (Those who haven’t voted do so) - The handler of Gaz Green >=)
