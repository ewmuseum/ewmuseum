'Valet-rama' Coming Soon

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Valet-rama' Coming Soon Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2003 ---- After heeding stern warnings to raise his level of activity or face the boot, Flaymin Aymin has decided to take the next step in reality television by launching a contest called 'Valet-rama'. "I'm putting together a worldwide search for a new valet" said Flaymin. "I'm taking the moral highground and so I won't be discriminating at all; this competiton is open to everyone.... Unless of course if you're fat, ugly, don't have a decent rack and prefer to spit instead of swallow- if you fall into this category, then go join a fenimisninsm...ism cult or something" said Flaymin in a statement issued earlier today on the back of an empty carton of Fosters Beer. Flaymin was also quick to add that "Not performing sexual favours will be frowned upon and you will face the consequences of being labelled a poofy dyke". "This will be nothing like those reality shows on tv, because my competition is going to be really.... real" Valet-rama: The Ultimate in Reality Television. !Coming Soon!
