Wrestler Spotlight : Syko the Goblin

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler Spotlight : Syko the Goblin Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Aug 2002 ---- Backstage at Turmoil, PCW reporter Tiffany Jennings caught up with PCW’s very own power hungry Goblin and Dark Age member, Syko Tiffany: Hello Syko, or would you rather be called Mr Goblin? Syko the Goblin: *Sweats, looking around nervously* What... what do you want with me? Tiffany: I'd like to ask a few questions, if I may? Syko the Goblin: Questions?!? Tiffany: Such as, how does the Dark Age manage to co-exist with Tyler Nelson so well? Syko the Goblin: *In a state of panic whips out a laser and takes aim at Tiffany's head* AH-HA! Tiffany stares at the plastic weapon in bemusement. Syko the Goblin: Now I'm in control, woman! Tiffany: Anyway, what are your thoughts about Pendragon this close to your match? Syko the Goblin: And I believe it is I who will be asking the qu... BAH! Tiffany: Um.. Syko, that's a toy. Syko the Goblin: Okay... you win this round... Pendragon, everything I despise in a man. Tiffany: Is it because he's bigger, stronger, naturally more athletic, not to mention more appealing to women than you? She pushes the over friendly Goblin away. Tiffany: And stop touching my hair. He then decides to clamp onto her leg, much to Tiffany’s annoyance. Syko the Goblin: BUT I'M BETTER THAN HIM BABY!!! Tiffany: Don't call me baby. He looks up from her leg, suspiciously. Syko the Goblin: Wait, I understand now. You are tool of the MEDIEVAL EMPIRE!!! Tiffany: Um.. Okay, seeing as you won't stop hugging my leg do you have any final comments? Syko the Goblin: *He lets go then runs off, chasing someone* MY WALLET!!! Jennings watching the little Goblin run away and licks her lips. Tiffany: Hmm, he is STRANGELY alluring...
