The Doghouse #6

The Doghouse #6 Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Nov 2001 ---- Hello folks and welcome to this, the sixth edition of PCW’s favorite column, The Doghouse. How do I know it’s the favorite? Simple. It’s the only column where the first piece of crap that comes to mind isn’t put down and posted. But that’s enough of that. Unlike some columnists, I’ll let my work speak for itself. First of all, I’d like to apologize to NTICW for not getting their polls up. Unfortunately, the Fan Board has been cleared, so now the polls have been erased. I don’t think I can put in the time or effort to do another one (counting those votes takes a lot), but if someone from NTICW would like to do it, I’d be happy to post the results here in the Doghouse. What a month for PCW. From Red October till now has been non-stop excitement. Meanstreak, Darkstar, Brandi are all gone. Ivan Stanislav has joined up with Management and won the PCW World title. And now, Alliances is on the horizon. I think the hype for Alliances has been sub par, but the event will be just as good as Red October, which despite predictability was one of PCW’s recent bests. Meanstreak has left the building. What’s new? I’ve publically hassled the monster for his disappearances and lack of workrate in the past. Now, just as I was starting to like what he was bringing to PCW, he leaves. I’m not going to stick personal opinions of Meanstreak in here, because I only talked to him once and I’m not qualified to give an opinion of him. But I do know what he’s brought to PCW lately. In my own opinion, he’s been weighing the federation down. Every time Meanstreak made a comeback, PCW would put half their total efforts into putting the monster over. And we all know what happened then. He disappeared. So you’ve got a bunch of guys who were made look bad by a guy who’s not even around. That was the way it was in PCW. I find it funny that only now that he’s gone, people finally give their real opinions on what he brought to the table. He was great, there’s no doubt about that. But did he help, or hurt, the federation as a whole? He was never here, so I don’t think he helped. Ivan Stanislav is a different story. This man worked his ass off card after card, always held down by the PCW “glass ceiling.” I think his win over Meanstreak was one of the most significant moments in PCW history. It was symbolic of the “glass ceiling” shattering. Ivan was the man who broke the wall down for the younger wrestlers to step up and have a fair shot. It feels like PCW went through a hurricane, and now we find ourselves on the other side. Ivan Stanislav is the man who led us here. For the first time in months, PCW has a worthy champion. I had hopes for Meanstreak, but like the rest of the “old school” wrestlers, he failed. Ivan Stanislav has been a breath of fresh air, although I don’t think anyone in the federation has quite come to grips with this “new era.” Alliances looks pretty good, with the two major factors being Asylum and Management. I’m looking for big performances out of guys like Timo Bolamba and Big Alan Steele for Alliances. By the way, is anyone else beaming about BAS winning the Continental Championship? BAS and Vampir Nosferatu, the two most recent champs, are a couple of my very favorite PCW’ers. Everyone can expect the second installment of my official polls right after Alliances. I want to see everyone come out and vote so they can be as successful as last time. Has anyone noticed that older wrestlers seem to do better? I think it’s because their handlers are mature enough to throw a couple of extra years onto their wrestler. I read a while back that Meanstreak was supposed to by 23. I had always pictured Streak in his early to mid thirties. Hearing that he was supposed to be 23 kind of turned me off to the “monster” persona. Twenty-three year olds are just kids. Look at the WWF, and the ages of the wrestlers there. HBK won his first World Title at 30. HHH was 31, I believe. Austin was 33 or 34, as was Bret Hart. The Rock, the youngest champ in WWF history didn’t even start wrestling until he was 24, I believe, and that’s older than most of the PCW roster. I think the problem is when people create a character, they want him to be fresh, young and new. They think they’ll age him as they e-fed. The problem is, the life of your average character is about a year or two, and then they come back with another youngster. Ivan is a good example of what age can do for a character. Ivan’s 39. If he were younger, I think it would take away from the character. So next time your making up a character, tack on a couple of years to make him more credible. I’ve been looking around PTC lately, and we have a couple of sleeper feds here. DWF deserves to be BITCHSLAPped. CCW is on and off. One fed that I’ve been really impressed with is HIW. I think everyone should go take a look. GWF is good, but the results are confusing as hell to read. EW is great, but they don’t focus enough on actual matches in their results. NTICW has a bit of the same problem. Getting back to PCW, Alliances is upon us once again, and it looks like a decent card. I can’t say it looks as good as the Red October card, but it looks like it will hold it’s own. Lets go down through the matches and predictions... Rising Star Championship Match Unstoppable Eugene, when on, is pretty much Unstoppable. At least in the lower card. The most Special man/kid in PCW is on his way to the top, if he lasts through the “paying dues” period. Eugene’s going to take it, and probably challenge RAH’s record. Pick of the Litter: Unstoppable Eugene Stairway to Hell Match Syko deserves a BITCHSLAP for his work lately. His promos make me feel like I’m in a cheap cartoon that wants to attract attention for being “wacky.” Wacky, Shmacky, two pages of a throw together promo get you no where in PCW. Especially when Timo Bolamba’s in the match. Bolamba’s destined for great things, and I think they’ll start at Alliances with the TV title. Chet King’s probably going to pull the CW title down. Picks of the Litter: Timo (TV) and Chet King (CW) Tag team Title Match I see that Frisco is being punished. Only thing I can think of to explain being teamed with Jon Kano, the man who called Jacob Yoder a jobber. I think the Blood Brothers are going to take this one home, adding to their legacy. Pick of the Litter: Blood Brothers Tower match Two Ton and Dan Hollywood... I hate to say it, but it seems like the battle of the underachievers. Both have a lot of potential, but neither have fulfilled it yet. I think this will be Dan Hollywood’s night. Pick of the Litter: Dan Hollywood Continental Title Match Akiyama’s ok and all, but we’re talking about Big Alan Steele here, who I mark out for totally. BAS, in my opinion, is the best in PCW right now. A few people are nipping at his heels, but BAS is the man. Pick of the Litter: BAS Soda Endorsement Match Finally! I can stop drinking Goat’s Blood! And with Darkstar out of the picture, it doesn’t look like I’ll be drinking it again. I’m hoping that someone breaks Syko’s neck in this match, but that’s just wishful thinking. Maybe he’ll slip on some cream soda though. I think it comes down to Ed and Kid Wonder. Damn, I never liked Dr. Pepper. Pick of the Litter: Ed Kremmen vs. Howard Jeremy Howard’s got this one bagged. Howard should be CEO right now, but Kremmen owns those rights. Jeremy Howard will win this match, and then turn his attention to the World title. Pick of the Litter: Jeremy Howard World Title Match I’m cheering hard for Boda. I’ll be in the crowd with my autographed basketball and Slaboda jersey, but no one’s going to get that belt off Ivan yet. Ivan’s going to take this match, and move on to a high profile feud. My guess is it will be with Jeremy Howard. Pick of the Litter: Ivan Main Event Badge, Big D, Walczak, Walden - Yeah, right. I mark for Walden, and Badge rocks, but neither are exactly tip-top guys when it comes to workrates. Oh, and Walczak’s on the team, so they’re officially doomed to fail. Akiyama, Gaijin, Itagaki Freeze, Kenji Hirose - No chance, really. Akiyama’s the most notable name on the list, and that should tell you something. John Jupiter, Big Alan Steele, THE Multiple Man, Rent A Hero - This is like the team of the guys who are still too green to make it all the way. And the Multiple Man isn’t worth his hype. He’s got a lot of talent, but he doesn’t do what it takes to take a step forward, unlike BAS and RAH. RAH dropped the ball last time, I think he’ll be looking to make an improvement. BAS is the best. Period. Kramer Wildwing, Jason Alexander, Colonel Sanders, Mrs. Sanders - Team KFC? A humoris touch, but that’s it. Kremmen, Tyler Nelson, Ivan Stanislav, Edgecrusher/Unstoppable Eugene - Adding Unstoppable Eugene will probably be enough to win it for Management. With kremmen and Tyler riding Ivan’s heat, I think Management’s going to be the team to beat at Alliances. Chris Chase, The White Mexican, Kevin Klaymore, Juan Borro - The underachieving team will continue the trend. Jeremy Krayzie Howard, Miyagawa, Ed, Timo Bolamba - Managements only real competition. Timo’s excellent, but green. Over all, this team is probably more solid then management, but my gut tells me they’re not going to take it. Frisco, Snow, Brock Newbludd, Stephen Blood - A distant third. You’ve got Frisco from the past, Snow from the future, and the Blood Brothers from everywhere. They’ll put on a show, but they don’t have what it takes to win it. Kid Wonder, Ned, Big Gooey Sac, Gus the Janitor - Heh? Scott Naket, Kris Kanton, Revenant, The Preacher - Is this a joke? Of course it’s a joke. I smell BITCHSLAP!!!! Expect someone, probably Clyde, to rip Scott Naket’s heart out. I don’t think Kanton will be killed off, since he thinks he’s Hulk Hogan in GWF. Speaking of which, I want everyone to head on over to GWF and pick a card, any card. On that card, you will find Kris Kanton coming down to the ring to “Real American” and making a complete ass of himself. Pathetic, pathetic, pathetic... Alan Kriegman, Barbara Bush-Kriegman, Bethany, Clyde - Evil may rule, but Management always wins. Pick of the Litter: Management Unsung Hero Tim Walden carries home the honors this time around. Walden has helped me out a great deal with my column, and is a wealth of PCW knowledge. He’s also one of the most loyal people in all of PTC, sticking it out for months in PWA before finally getting the call up. Walden knows everything about PCW, and I think that should count for a lot. When people have questions, they go to Walden. I do, anway. In The Doghouse Well, I’m glad PCW management decided to bring in the official BITCHSLAP boys of PCW, because it gives me a chance to put Kris Kanton in the Doghouse. Real American? Real lame. I’m glad to see him making an ass out of himself, and I’ll continue to rip him until the day he goes commie. Then, I guess I’ll have to give him some credit. Well, that’s it folks. Look for polls to come out after Alliances, and results soon after! Woof woof, baby! ~The Hound
