Injury Report

12 April 2002

Injury Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Apr 2002 ---- The following is an injury report on various PCW stars. Bobby Stormbringer suffered second degree burns over 75% of his body as a result of being lit on fire by Stryfe and the rest of the Dark Age. He's been advised to take some time off, but whether he does or not is his own decision. THE Multiple Man suffered second and third degree burns on his face as a result of being the recipient of the Firefly by Flaymin Aymin. "Luckily his face was blurred," said one medic. "Once the fire was put out, it wasn't a pretty sight." THE Multiple Man will still be expected to compete on Friday night. He has been advised by doctors to wear a mask until his face heals. Both Kira York and Reeco Suave suffered some damage to their windpipes after both were hung on Turmoil. Kira has been given permission to take as much time as she needs off. Reeco, on the other hand, has been advised to work on Tuesday or be fired. Corey Williams is recovering well from the knee injury he suffered a few weeks ago. He is expected to make his return within the next week or two. We'll update any more injuries involving PCW superstars when they happen.
