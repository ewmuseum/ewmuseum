Kannon Under Fire

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kannon Under Fire Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Oct 2002 ---- After his sickening act at the end of Turmoil, PCW CEO Xavier Kannon has been fending off more adverse publicity and hate-fuelled threats than even he has to usually put up with. “One day someone is finally going to put their foot right through his smug face!” ranted one fan outside the Turmoil arena before Security could get to him and batter him to a pulp. Children’s Charities were also outraged at what they saw, when Xavier delivered his patented “Hallmark” Cradle Piledriver to the orphan girl befriended by Rent-a-Hero, Amy. “His act was disgusting, if the network had any morals they would pull his sick show in protest at his attack!” In reply, the Network stated they had no morals. Xavier left with the Front Office without making any comments, but when quizzed on the matter today he just shrugged it off. “My show,” he stated calmly, “I can do what I want.” Also, no word has been received by Rent-a-Hero who has not left Amy’s bedside, but PCW fans can be sure the Hero will be out for revenge on Fusion when he teams with the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid to take on Kannon and Tim Walden.
