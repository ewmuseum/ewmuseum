Cruiserweight Facelift Thanks To... Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Mar 2003 ---- Ever since Rent-A-Hero won the Cruiserweight belt off of El Romantico, the title seems to have been header for bigger and better things. Not surprisingly, Jeremy Howard, one half of PCW's new "ruling class," has been one of the leading advocates for this new re-birth of competitive Cruiserweight compeitition. Men such as El Diablo, and now Pharaoh, who are both amongst PCW's top workers, are receiving shots at this title, in what many are calling solely Howard's work to try and ressurect the doings of Syko (and to a lesser extent, Xavier Kannon). Howard has also hinted that more of the same is to come, with rumored CW title shots in the works for Gaz Green, Dante Jackson, and many others. More to come on this and other improvements around PCW as Jeremy Howard begins to reverse the negative trend of ownership in PCW.
