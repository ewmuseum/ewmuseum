11 September 2002

Team International/Think Tank... Media/Outcasts... it's now Front Office/Asylum Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Sep 2002 ---- Portland, Oregon- After Third Dawning two major events happened. The formation of the Front Office, and the formation of the latest incarnation of Asylum. With Brandi getting involved last week in Anarchy's match, and Miyagawa and Duke heading more and more head on, it could only be a matter of time before they meet. And on Turmoil they did, with the stipulation that if Xavier Kannon was pinned, then the World Title would change hands. Who would walk out of the match victorious? Who would walk out champion? And who would walk out as the number one contender? Also on Turmoil, Snow shows El Diablo what it's like to be living like an animal in a cage, Semore Smoke meets the biggest Willie in the Entertainment business, Kannon gets his own personal revenge on Darkstar... thus causing someone else to get their own personal revenge on him. To see all of this transpire and more, head down to the Turmoil banner and click Results.
