The Flame extinguished?

19 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Flame extinguished? Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Jan 2004 ---- As Turmoil went of the air after it’s third and most successful show since PCW made it’s return, not everyone was celebrating. In fact in what could have been a triumphant celebration for his third consecutive win in three weeks turned into a living nightmare for the man known as the ‘Eternal Flame’, Jay Phoenix. When it was discovered that Phoenix’s opponent that night, Bungle, had been attacked backstage and left lying injured and unconscious, Phoenix himself went backstage to offer whatever aid he could … and was met with a sickening attack by the same man that had put Bungle out of action … Afwa, the near 500lb Samoan monster! Trying to gain revenge for two losses at the hands of Phoenix, Afwa unleashed a horrific attack that saw the Native American warrior slammed into the hard, unforgiving steel of the ramp with a Samoan Drop from Afwa … his whole body weight crushing Phoenix to the ground. This was followed by vicious headshot after vicious headshot to the floor, leaving Phoenix bleeding and semi-conscious as EMTs rushed to his aid … but Afwa was not finished just yet! Unseen by the EMTs, Afwa climbed over six feet up the JumboTron, and with a battle-cry that shook the arena, the man known as Kilmanjaro … the man mountain … shocked the fans in attendance by crushing Phoenix with a Banzai Splash to the unforgiving metal once more. Leaving a fallen Phoenix visibly fighting for his very life, Afwa lumbered to the back and hasn’t been heard from since. Phoenix himself was taken immediately to the local hospital where word of his condition has still not been released, but one thing is for certain, it doesn’t look good for the so called Heat of the PCW.
