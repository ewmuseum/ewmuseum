The Doghouse #15 Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Nov 2002 ---- Greetings! It is I, the Hound, back from my regular prolonged absence to give you the Poll results for another month. And what a month it was! Clyde finally got to the top of PCW, Xavier Kannon continues to dominate the upper card, and a lot of hard working wrestlers are earning higher spots in the cards, and more respect from their peers. I usually like to spout off about this or that, but I'm a little pressed for time this month, so I'll just get to the polls. I used a new scoring system this month, with first place getting 12 points and tenth place getting 3 points. This makes a spot, any spot, on a person's ballet more valuable. Xavier Kannon 134 TOP FIFTEEN IN PCW FOR THE MONTH OF OCTOBER, 2002 (as voted by peers) 15. Duke Williams (26/18/17) 61 Top 15: 2 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: 15 Peak: 15 Duke makes just his second appearance in the polls, though he deserves to have been here plenty of times by now. Duke is, in my opinion, the most overlooked man in PCW. Everyone seems to be a Duke fan, but when poll time rolls around, he's left out. Duke still owns the rest of you in card status though, so I guess he gets the last laugh. 14. The Chef (60/0/9) 69 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Previous: X Peak: 5 The Chef returned to PCW with a vengeance, but he returns to the polls with little more than a whisper, finishing in fourteenth, though he's obviously a top then, or even top five, talent in PCW. I don't understand why Chef didn't get more character votes, but the peers are the judges. I look for Chef to finish well within the top ten next month; If, of course, he doesn't go on injured reserve after Alliances. 13. Darkstar (65/0/31) 96 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Previous: 8 Peak: 3 October was a month where Darkstar was getting back on his feet and into our minds. With that considered, thirteenth really isn't a bad finish. Darkstar has finished all over the polls since their beginning an entire year ago. He's finished everywhere from fifteenth to third. 12. Players Inc. (42/51/12) 105 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: X Peak: 12 Very impressive finish for Players Inc. It's rare to see a tag team make the polls, and Players Inc must be doing a great job to finish twelfth. They brought home a lot of points in the workrate category, where they astonishingly finished second only to Xavier Kannon, the king of carrying a workload. 11. Skitzo (39/0/67) 106 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: X Peak: 11 Skitzo finishes just outside the top ten in his poll debut. Skitzo's finish took me by surprise, but I'll be sure to keep an eye on him from now on. He made an excellent showing in character points, with 67, finishing ninth in the category. 10. Rent-a-Hero (60/0/63) 123 Top 15: 5 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: 6 Peak: 6 Rent-a-Hero's strange finishes in the polls continue with a tenth place finish. Whenever I talk to people, RAH seems to be either their favorite or a close second, yet, in the polls, he hasn't been able to break the top five. Don't get me wrong, the top five isn't an easy thing to crack. But in a year's worth of polls, I expected one top five finish for RAH. 9. El Diablo (45/2/80) 127 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: 10 Peak: 9 El Diablo always seems to end up right on the edge of the top ten, but take a look at those eighty character points, up from just fifty last month. As always, Diablo's workrate points remain low, which may be what's keeping him from a top five finish, now that he's solidified in the upper card. 8. P.W.A (60/48/51) 159 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Previous: X Peak: 8 Astonishing. That's all I can say about a tag team finishing within the top ten. These guys are really doing a lot of things right, as you can see by how balanced their production in each category is. Honestly, when I first saw that P.W.A had formed, I thought it was doomed. I couldn't picture either wrestler in a tag team. Well, a few months in, and it's starting to look like they might make a challenge to be one of the best tag teams of all time. I know we all have nostalgic memories of Da Westsidas, but looking objectively, I wouldn't be surprised if a year from now, we consider P.W.A the greatest tag team in PCW history. If they stay together, that is. 7. Jason Snow (101/0/68) 169 Top 15: 8 Top 10: 7 Top 5: 6 #1: 1 Previous: 3 Peak: 1 Snow slips out of the top five after a very poor month. Not a single point in the workrate category reflects just how bad things went for Snow. Still, he managed to rack up over a hundred points in the RP category, and after a very strong finish in November, he should move back to the top five where he's been hovering for a year. 6. Vennemon Pendragon (97/16/72) 185 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Previous: 5 Peak: 5 Pendragon slips a spot, mostly because his workrate slipped a bit. But that's nothing to worry about, especially when finishing strong in both RP's and Character. Pendragon's had a very strong start to his PCW career. Now that he's breaking out of the "newbie" phase, it'll be interesting to see how far he can go. 5. The End (98/10/89) 197 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Previous: 11 Peak: 5 Probably the second biggest surprise this month was the End's top five finish, ahead of established PCW stars and greats alike. People really reacted to the End in October, as he had excellent feedback in both RP's and Character. It'll be interesting to see how he does in the November polls. Can he sustain this high spot? 4. Syko (84/10/112) 206 Top 15: 2 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Previous: 12 Peak: 4 This is my pick as the top surprise. I expected Syko to improve by a few spots and probably make his way into the top ten. But a fourth place finish!? Wow! Syko has really been able to build on hot in-card material to support his wacky RP's. But, with his recent firing, we won't get a chance to see if he could hang onto this spot or not. 3. Clyde (134/38/89) 261 Top 15: 6 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 2 #1: 0 Previous: 14 Peak: 3 Last time I did these polls, I said that Clyde's poll performance seemed to depend on his card status more than anyone else in PCW. I think this proves me right. Clyde + High Card Status = High Poll finish. He still has the title, so by that theory, he shouldn't have a problem finishing around here again. 2. Akiyama (132/40/120) 292 Top 15: 9 Top 10: 9 Top 5: 5 #1: 0 Previous: 2 Peak: 2 Three months, three straight second place finishes for the most consistent man in the history of the polls. Akiyama has nine poll finishes, all within the top ten. He's finished five times in the top five, and now, three straight times at number two. TRIPLE CROWN WINNER 1. Xavier Kannon** (162/75/134) 371 Top 15: 6 Top 10: 6 Top 5: 6 #1: 3 Previous: 1 Peak: 1 If Akiyama's the most consistent man in the polls, then Xavier Kannon is the most dominant. Not only does he finish at number one, for the third straight month, but he also wins his second triple crown, the first person ever to do so (Triple Crown is Awarded for finsishing 1st in all three categories). After winning Alliances, Xavier will threaten to win a third in November. Congratulations to Kannon! Well, that's it for me. Look for me soon with the November Polls and look for them to be a lot more in depth, as in previous months. Till next time... Woof woof baby, The Hound
