Poe Speaks Out On DDT

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Poe Speaks Out On DDT Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Dec 2002 ---- Earlier this weekend we had the chance to catch up with Gideon Poe and Jeremiah O'Barr backstage at Fusion and get their thoughts on this weeks Turmoil, where Gideon Poe will be the special guest referee for the Network Title match between Ariz Ona, and Poe's new found rival, Matt Hunt. =========== Jennings: "So Gideon, what are your thought's the match in which you will be the guest referee for this Tuesday on Turmoil? Poe: Well... Jeremiah: "Gideon is of course looking forward to the match, a chance at officiating a match of this standard is an extreme honour to him" Poe: "You wish too much old man" Jennings: "Uhhh, so, can you guarantee a fair contest in the match,? it's no secret that you and Matt Hunt are developing quite the rivalry, and the two of you will meet at Decimation for his Network title. Poe: "That German bas..." Jeremiah: "....Of course the match will be a fair contest, my protégé Gideon is an elite fighter and a noble, albeit depressed young man. Nonetheless he is fair and impartial, and will bear no favouritism or biasness in the match in which he will officiate." Once Jeremiah was then forced to leave after numerous threats by Gideon involving the old mans limbs being removed with a spoon, we finally got the chance to ask Gideon a few questions uninterrupted. Poe: "I don't care about that Network title. I don't care about winning and I don't care about officiating the match this Tuesday. What I care about is giving Matt Hunt a glimpse of the hell in which I live with everyday. Come Decimation, Matt Hunt's blood will flow and his tears will stain the canvas, and he will then realise.... Victims..aren't we all?”
