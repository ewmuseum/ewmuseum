Borro Jailed

29 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Borro Jailed Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Jan 2002 ---- News is that Juan Borro is being held under custody in a Shanghaeid prison. When PCW officials hit the scene, a police officer gave them the details. "It seems Mr. Borro was arrested last night after allegedly shooting China's Entertainment Executive, who was found dead last night. If convicted, he may face life in prison." PCW's news team work hard to put two and two together. Juan Borro was supposedly black mailed my the Chinese Government. It is said if he did not bring the Cruiserweight Title belt to the International world, that the Chinese government would hunt down and ruin his fathers business. When PCW's news team questioned Juan Borro, about the murder, he answered: "It wasn't me, it just seems to be a coincidence. I am an innocent man, and I will prove it in court." When Portugal's government found out about this they quickly had a huge affect. The people of Portugal are protesting in the streets to let Juan Borro free. People are saying the trial will be Portugal vs. China. Juan will still be able to compete in PCW, as he was paid bail by his girlfriend Juliet. The law is Juan must stay in China, but that was nothing a large amount of money couldn't change. The court date is unknown, but will be coming up in the next month. If convicted PCW may never see Juan again.
