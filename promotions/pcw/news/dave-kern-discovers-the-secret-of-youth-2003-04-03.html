Dave Kern discovers the secret of youth? Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Apr 2003 ---- As witnessed on Tuesday Turmoil for April 1st, Dave Kern let his mouth get the better of him in earshot of Pranks With Attitude. Following this, Nerezza issued an ultimatum on Gaz's behalf to Dave. We finally managed to catch up with the 4-time Tag Team Champions to discuss the matter. "Quite simply, Dave Kern's comments were short of nothing but childish hatred towards us." Nerezza said. "So if he wants to act like a child, he shall be treated as one. It is with personal amusement that I publicly announce Dave's punishment. For Spring Fling, he can sit in a High-Chair, dressed in only a diaper and a baby bonnet, with a rattle and call every single match in that manner. Any attempt by Mr. Kern to wriggle out of this situation until the last light has gone off in the building will result in... ahem... alternative actions." Gaz Green had this to offer: "We figured the easiest way to shut Dave up was to shove a great big pacifier into his mouth. He's used to sucking anyway, so now the dummy can have a dummy for the PPV. We understood Jeff Marx was used to sitting at the desk with two dummies... well, it'll be like old times, but only one can smack him now. He also has the right to give the baby a spanking if he misbehaves. I think it'll be a refreshing experience for Mr. Kern." Needless to say, fans are debating whether it's safer for Dave to do as Nerezza says, or risk whatever else P.W.A has in mind. We'll see exactly what Dave chooses to do at Spring Fling...
