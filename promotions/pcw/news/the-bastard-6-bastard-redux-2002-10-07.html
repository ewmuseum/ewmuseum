The Bastard #6: Bastard Redux

7 October 2002

The Bastard #6: Bastard Redux Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Oct 2002 ---- HOLY MOTHER FUCKER!!!! THAT FUCKER IS BACK!!!!! Yes my friends, I am talking about me, myself and I. The one. The only. The Bastard. I said I would never return, not after I bitched about Karl LeBlanc for three pages which made the guy leave. I promise to never bitch about that much in one person. I shall stick to constructive criticism from now on. Except when talking about other federations, where I will reign fire and brimstone down upon their crappy hides. Batty Batee is reopening Fedwars.org. It seems that after he quit PTC because Ron wouldn’t get his way he went to Fedwars.com. Then he quit because Dave wouldn’t let him get his way. The question is this… how quickly will he get bored when he finds out he can’t quit, and how quickly will the fans get bored when they find out he’s only interested in pushing his fed into as many I-PPV’s as possible. Speaking of I-PPV’s… when is WT gonna have one? Can we get like a New Years Special or something? Can we get Tim Walden versus Kid Wonder? No particular reason, I just think it would be hilarious to see Kid Wonder job to Tim Walden. I’ve reviewed the latest round of PCW apps. And well. How can I put this. We saw some people in the PWA before the “Direct Applications Open” thing and well… only those guys stood out from all the applications. Sure there were good solid apps there… but nothing that said “hire me!”. Probably if we were down around the 20 spaces area it would have been a different story and we would have had to hire some people, but let’s just hope it never ever gets there. Isn’t our female wrestling talent just shit hot right now? Go read a promo by Teresa Tomas or Anarchy, I promise you they are destined for greatness. **Taps on screen** Paging Andy Murray. Wake the hell up. You know I just decided to sit down and write this column. Not even sure why. I guess I saw the column page there untouched… Boobie Boy does RUSH now and Decker/Dicker does OSW (one of the many that jumped ship… and then the ship stopped sailing quite so smoothly) and the Shoot King just isn’t about anymore. We need more columnists. So start writing the sodding things! OSW had a ten meg Slam! Result. That’s ten megs. That’s TEN Turmoil or Fusion results. What did they stick in there… a life size Jpeg of Tempest naked? Did you read Red October? I thought with all those matches it would be a bit much, but holy hell was that great. Managed to beat Third Dawning somehow… and of course now with Alliances coming up you have to try to guess at the teams… if I was a guessing man, this is how I would put together the PCW roster into groups of four… Xavier Kannon Duke Williams Tim Walden Brandi Miyagawa Anarchy Gaz Green Nerezza Clyde Scourge Violator Syko the Goblin Rush Mason Ali Khadaffi Statuz Quo Dante Jackson Ok, now that’s the obvious ones out the way, let’s have a look at the less obvious ones… Duzza Teresa Tomas Kramer Wild Wing Redemption Song Rent-a-Hero Venneman Pendragon Cowabunga Connection Carl Cash The Pharaoh El Diablo The Heartbreakers Frisco Snow Charlie Loc Jamie Celt Jon Kano Eddie Mandrake Scorpion Semore Smoke Akiyama Violence Jack Bernard Colt BladeD Actually I think that puts them together quite nicely. Not say this is how it would go, but if I was going to force people to go together, this is how it would happen. Of course the Ream Team would also get an entry into the affair but does anyone expect them to ever win a match? Even if the opponent no-showed? Red October was great. Alliances looks to be even better, with it being only five weeks away. What feuds ended at Red October… well I guess that RaH-Frisco defiantly ended, and I suppose Clyde-Kannon but Kannon is going to want that belt back again. Duke-Miya defiantly hasn’t ended, and since there is going to be only like four non-main event matches at Alliances, it could very well be that they won’t get to face off in a proper final match until Decimation in December. A three month feud in PCW… let’s hope they can keep it fresh. Anyway, I’ve run out of things to complain about. This is the lesser Bastard, signing off. -The Bastard.
