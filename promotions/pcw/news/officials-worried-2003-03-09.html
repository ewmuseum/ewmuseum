Officials Worried

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Officials Worried Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Mar 2003 ---- PCW staff can now confirm that they too are now fully confused and mystified as to where Akiyama could be. After being "ridden" from PCW by Xavier Kannon, the monster has dissapeared from hospitals and from off the radar screen. Many workers were afraid to release this information, in fear of losing PPV buyrate, but decided to reveal their fears in case of a no-show by the Neo Void God for just that reason, to alert fans that there is a slight possibility Aki will not show up. "Of course he won't show. Coming only to be thrown out ten seconds later by me wouldn't be too much of a Pay Per View for him, would it?" That was the response from former CEO and one of the favorites to win March Madness, Xavier Kannon. However, the response from another PCW worker differed. "I dunno where the hoss is, but I'm sure as hell that he won't miss a shot to beat on that long-haired pussy if he's got one." Of course, the most famous mouth in the business, Duke Williams, was sure Akiyama would make an appearance. "I'm a hundred percent certain that ol' Aki wouldn't miss my celebration of the Month of Malice and even though he'll succumb to the Malice Man, watchin' him throw Kannon like a rag doll will be entertainment enough for a long time." So, differing points of view from two of March Madness's favorites as to whether the monster will make an appearance, but one thing is for certain: If Akiyama does show up, there will be hell to pay for Kannon and hell for anyone else who tries to get in the way of the nVg.
