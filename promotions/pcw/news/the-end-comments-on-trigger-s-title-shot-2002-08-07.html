The End Comments on Trigger's Title Shot

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The End Comments on Trigger's Title Shot Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Aug 2002 ---- *The following is an interview taken by Cal Seaver shortly after Tuesday Turmoil* Seaver: So, End, what do you think about the Commissioner's announcement for the World Title? End: Which announcement would that be? Seaver: The one in which Trigger is given a World Title shot next Tuesday on Turmoil. End: Who? Seaver: Trigger. End: I'm sorry? Seaver: The former Rising Star champion before Venneman Pendragon. End: I don't follow. Seaver: You interviewed him a little while back for your own column! End: Interviewed who? Seaver: Trigger! End: No, I'm The End. Seaver: No, Trigger is getting the World Title shot! End: Since when? Seaver: Ellie announced it tonight! End: Announced what tonight? Seaver: That Trigger would receive a World Title shot! End: Who's Trigger? Seaver: A wrestler in the company! End: Are you sure? Seaver: Yes. End: Positive? Seaver: Yeah. End: Absolutely certain. Seaver: Yes, I'm sure! Trigger's getting the shot! End: Who? Seaver: Trigger! End: What about him? Seaver: Is getting the shot! End: At what? Seaver: ARGH! Despite Seaver's frustration, this continued for another five minutes until he finally realized that The End didn't really care one way or another...
