Chef Suffers Another Groin Injury Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Sep 2002 ---- Yesterday at the Center for Loony People that Froth from Their Mouth and Wet Their Bed Daily, the Chef suffered another stupefying and tenderizing groin injury. It seems that while flirting with a nurse at the Center, the Chef was kicked in the groin by his girlfriend and cloned life mate Bertha Stewart Clone #1. The Clone’s legal team released this statement from Bertha, which has been toned down a bit for the public audience: “If the mother f*cker can’t f*cking perform f*cking sh*tty @$$ f*cking tonight, I’ll f*cking find some other f*cking mother f*cker to f*cking f*ck!!!” When asked why he flirted with the nurse, a groin-grabbing Culinary Conquistador replied, “I lost a lot of money this weekend thanks to that helmet-throwing Dwayne Rudd. I had to whore myself out to make some quick dough.” Rudd’s early celebration cost the Cleveland Browns their opening game and the Chef over five grand in “vanilla” crème-filled pastries. “In France, we pay with pastries,” the Chef informed the crowd. When asked what exactly is the filling in those crème puffs, the Chef told the crowd to watch the film “Van Wilder” to find their answer. Leave it to the French to f*ck up a good desert idea… The Estate of Syko the Goblin had no comment about the situation, but did inform Bertha's legal team that Syko has been practicing and has moved his insertion to climax time up to about 12 seconds. Of course the time could fluctuate with a real partner.
