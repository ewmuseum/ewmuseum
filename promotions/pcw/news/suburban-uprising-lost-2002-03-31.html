Suburban Uprising Lost

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Suburban Uprising Lost Posted by Sandy Wong on 31 Mar 2002 ---- Pimply Faced Intern Kid here, Earlier today I was outside Duke Williams' Office, but coming out of Duke's office there was a familiar face from an old NTICW era, and the last NTICW Television Champion....Christopher V Knight. He seemed to be with someone who I didn't recognize and the no one else did either, when catching up with Chris and his friend asking him why he was there he literally bit my head off. "Watchathink yourdoin' foo', huhuhuh speakwiditbitch" he shouted, as I took a few steps back. His friend was able to speak, holding Chris back. "Sorry about that" he said. "I'm Teddy Knight, Christopher's younger, more handsome, better skateboarding, producing and pimpin' brother". As i looked at what pairing they were i could only help but ask one more question and that was what were they doing here. Teddy explained and Christopher took himself away walking down a near buy hall. "We just got signed up to PCW. It was all Chris though he's always been a lucky bastard, he couldn't even get into McDonalds as an employee i only got us in, because we decided that we shouldn't ask for ten acres of land like we wanted before" that's all Teddy had to see. After Teddy was about to leave we both sighed as we heard something from the halls. "I'M LOST, TED!" screamed Christopher. Teddy just walked away with a wave, as i looked down the hall, Christopher had just walked into a dead end. What type of pairing do we have now here in PCW?
