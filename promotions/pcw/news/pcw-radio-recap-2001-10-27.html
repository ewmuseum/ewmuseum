PCW Radio Recap

27 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Radio Recap Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Oct 2001 ---- Tonight's PCW Malice marked the end of Duke Williams' in-ring career. It was a touching (at least as touching as Duke can get) show from our host, and was a solid way to hang up the boots. - The show started with the usual awesome opening promo. - Duke greets us, and talks about the upcoming Friday Fusion card. - Someone calls in with a question about sHs (Scotty's High Society). Ed calls in to say that sHs was a stable headed by Scott Naket, which refused to die until the as-yet-undisclosed "incident" that led to Naket's firing. (Editors' Note: Naket was fired for breaking into the PCW booking board, deleting bookings, then trying to leave a trail of clues to implicate Pirana for the crime.) - Timo Bolamba calls in with several things to say. - Duke insults soccer; Timo gets pissed. - Our host goes over the Sabotage card. - Duke talks about his career. Says he's been wrestling as this character for four years, and that his career has hit a low point. Gives a heart-felt speech about his life in PCW. - Ed calls in, rants about Judas Steele, talks about his match with Duke on Sabotage, urges people to vote for him in the Hound's Fan Board poll, and closes by saying that not only does he have "it", Duke Williams has "it", baby! - Someone suggests that Duke adds some "spice" to the show -- in other words, get the booze. - Our beloved host talks about football, especially Da Bears. - Once again, Duke issues a challenge to Shoot King. Once again, Shoot King isn't listening because he fears his angry Grandfather. - Duke talks about the new PCW President, Trashcan Man and insults Old School. - A joke about a male sex organ stuck in a pickle slicer. - Says Snow vs. Kid Wonder will be match of the week. - Lacking inspiration to continue the PCW-related show, Duke puts on some country and heads for the beer cooler. Post-show begins. Duke will be back on the air next Saturday night at 11:59 pm EST for another edition of PCW Malice.
