PCW — Column

Words of Wisdom Version 2.0

6 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the second edition of "Words of Wisdom" by the one and only TRUE wise ass, Josh Wilkes. I know it's been about three months since I last wrote this column, but it's been a hectic time since PCW has reopened. That and I've been recovering from a trip to a local bar with Duke Williams and some of the boys. You haven't had a hangover until you've drunk with the Malice Man. Speaking of Duke Williams, I had a chance to interview him back in late December for this very column. However, the interview will never see the light of day as I can't find my tape recorder . . . To recap however Duke is going to do a good job of running PCW. Now that all the bullshit is out of the way let's get my opinion on the top five wrestlers running for the World title in PCW. In no particular order here we go . . . Skylar Green-He's the World champion. What else can I say? He's on the top of the mountain right now, but with the current contenders for that title. He's going to have a tough time not falling off. "The Buzz" Cal Roberts- What can I say about him that HE hasn't said about himself. He's already a legend on the indys and Japan. Now he has to prove himself in the big leagues. He's got the charisma, and the skills as we saw against Propaganda last week. However . . . he also has one of the downfalls of some past great wrestlers. An over abundant ego. I predict the Buzz will rise to the occasion many times in 2004. However, depending on whether or not he lets his ego run wild will determine whether it is for the ring rats, or for World title gold. Inphino Blitz- How can you beat a man when you know nothing about him? Inphino Blitz has been overshadowed recently by the other top contenders for the World title, Buzz and Propaganda, but I peg him for a sleeper in the running for the World title. If Blitz can utilize his mind games, then the enigmatic one has what it takes to reign supreme in 2004. Propaganda- The previous three men are not small guys or slouches when it comes to talent. However, they aren't monsters in the ring either. Propaganda is, however. Then again we now know Propaganda isn't totally unstoppable. Buzz and Boda showed that last week respectively. This man can be hurt, but that's easier said then done. Propaganda is another guy who has the tools to reign in 2004. Boda- He's been champion before why not again? He is the Big Bad Boda Daddy after all. There's not much to say about Boda other than to expect big things in 2004 for the hall of famer. The big question, a lot of bored teenage boys reading this column are asking, who would win if all these guys locked it up? Hmm . . . good question. Before I cut to a close let me just say regardless of what the skeptics say. I've been thoroughly entertained with PCW since week one. We have a lot of elite talent working for us. The talent is there, but in some cases the work ethic isn't. Until next time . . . It's been YOUR pleasure, -"Wise Ass" Josh Wilkes
