Rising Star 4-Way Participants Named

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rising Star 4-Way Participants Named Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 May 2003 ---- VP-S has released the Top 4 wrestlers for the Rising Star Tournament. Matt Jackson is the top performer, going undefeated for 6 wins and no losses. James Edwards, following his two pins last night, has 5 wins and 1 loss. SOCIETY has 5 wins and 2 losses. Shane Wethers has 4 wins and 3 losses. Stone Burns has 3 wins and 3 losses. SyN and Sivasian Krikorian have 3 wins and 4 losses each. Dan Osbourne and Mark Makonie both end the month with one win and four losses to their names. "The final participants for the Rising Star Championship Match are as follows," VP-S read during a post-Turmoil press conference. 1) Matt Jackson 2) James Edwards 3) Stone Burns 4) SyN/Sivasian Krikorian When questioned about the notable absence of the names Shane Wethers and SOCIETY, both of whom did better the overall in the Rising Star standings than the last three names, VP-S had this to say. "Shane Wethers has become lazy as of late, showing up late to the venues, neglecting his press conferences and autograph signings, and overall just getting a bad workrate in the ring. His last two performances have bordered on not even trying. If he thinks that he can win early, and then half-ass his way into the final match, he is mistaken. He's being left out as a reminder to himself and a message to anyone else: if you're going to work, good; but keep the work constant. "On the matter of SOCIETY, his manager Michael Chang (no, not the tennis player) approached me after the Rising Star match and asked for SOCIETY to be left out of that match. I don't know his reasons, ask him about that." But two people are tied for fourth place, making a list of five names for the Rising Star 4-way match. Would there be a five-way match in pay-per-view, making that the only match that the Rising Star wrestlers haven't participated in during their PCW careers? "No," said VP-S. "SyN and Krikorian are tied for fourth place. Therefore, they will have a match to determine the fourth man in that Rising Star championship bout." With that, all the matches for PCW Supremacy are onw announced and finalized. It is a short card at 8 matches now, but it's still promising.
