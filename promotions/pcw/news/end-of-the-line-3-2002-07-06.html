End of the Line #3

6 July 2002

End of the Line #3 Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jul 2002 ---- Meh. It's time for me to do another one of these weekly columns. That's right, seems the PCW advert-heads want to use my column to promote the wrestlers so it won't have to work as hard at it. Ugh. Used again. Well, they want it that way? Fine. Every week, I'll be featuring a PCW star in this column, doing whatever I so choose. But I reserve the right to verbally (or otherwise) beat the crap out of them if they annoy me. And, as always, anyone talking to me is subject to be made to look like an idiot. Like Trigger last week. Or this week, when I feature who of all people but Andy Murray. Believe me, I didn't want to have anything to do with this man. Misfortune, however, played a part in this, with him sitting next to me on a flight. He started talking, and within 10 seconds I wanted to cut his tongue out. It took superhuman patience and willpower to withstand the urge to make him mute. But I just put on some headphones and listened to the White Stripes, trying to ignore him. He kept on talking, and while I had no real interest in what he was saying, I decided to kill two birds with one stone. Taking out a tape recorder, I gave it to Murray and told him to speak into it about exactly what he would do if he ran PCW. The first two hours were relavitely silent, save for a few expletives as he had trouble finding the button marked 'RECORD' in big indent-textured letters. And then what amounts to about five minutes of speech took an hour as he spent forever trying to gather his thoughts. Even talking about him makes me want to kill something. Let's just move on to his excruciatingly painful monologue. Wha’ gwan? Welcome to mah pure brilliant part of this column min, ah-m Andy Murray by the way, pure famous wrestler and all round top muckah. Awright, so I’ve been told by this End bampot, that I’ve got tae pure speak about what I’d do if I owned PCW an’ all that? That’s pure brilliant by the way, because I’d huv quite a lot tae do. First, I’d pure clear out the roster, because at the moment, it’s full of dobbers min. I’d huv tae keep Rush Mason though, cuz us Glesga folk need someone tae hammer wekk-in, week-out, know wut I mean? Anyway, these are the baw bags that ah’d huv tae fire ... - Jordan X Shabazz: He’s got a pure stupit name, and his name is like one of they Chinky restaurant places. - Clyde: I ken he’s just back an’ awl, but he’s a pure baw bag min. He’s a bit like one of they Vampire things, and they’re pure hummin’ min. - Flaymin Aymin: He’s got pure crap hair min, and he’s fae that Antarctica place an’ awl, and that’s crap by the way. - Pretty Cool Guys: Their songs are pish min, an’ NedRap is more pure brilliant. - The Heartbreakers: I think they’re poofs, an’ bein’ a poof must be a load of old pish min. I pure hate these guys, cuz I don’t think they like birds min. And ah’m no talkin’ about pigeons an’ sparrows ab’ awl that either. - Heaven Rayne: She’s a pure minger min. - Rent-A-Villain: Rent-A-Villin’ is a dobber of the first class min, he pure sucks by the way. How can anybody be some sort o’ superhero when we all know that they’re pure fake min. He’s pure stupit if he thinks they’re real ... Course there’d be some more dobbers who I’d fire an’ awl, but there’s too many tae list. Here’s the pure briwliant muckah’s that I’d hire ... - Rab C. Nesbitt: Rab’s a pure top geezer, an’ he’s mah idol an’ awl. He’d pureshow you baw bags a thing or two. - Rowdy Roddy Piper: He don’t take no crap from awl these American bampots, an’ he’d pure kick awl your backsides. Rowdy’s a pure briwliant wrestler an’ awl. - William Regal: Even though he’s one o’ they guffs, I’d pure hire him so I could chap a brick off his noggin’. - The Proclaimers: They may look like a couple ‘o glaikit chubes, but they make pure bangin’ tunes min. They’d show Pretty Cool Guys how tae sing an’ that. That’s ‘bout awl who I’d hire. Ah’d pure keep guys like John Crichton on the roster, cos they’re top muckah’s an’ awl. But they Ultimate Authority chubes pure huv to go, they’re pure pish min. Here’s wut I’d do wi’ all the titles that PCW has ... - World Title: This pure briwliant title would be mine, because ah’m pure famous an’ awl, by the way. I would’nae give any o’ ya chubes a shot, cos it’d be mah title, an’ naebody else’s. - Continental Title: This title’s got a pure pish name min, so I’d huv taechange it to the Glesga Title, cos that’s a pure briwliant name. After that. Ah’d give it tae ma’ pal Rab, cos he’s a pure top bloke an’ awl, so he deserves a title. Plus he’s fae Glesga, min. - Television Title: This title is pure pish, but ah think ah’d give it tae John Crichton, cos I canna think of anythin’ else to do with it. - Cruiserweight Title: This is sh!te min, ah hate this title, it’s pish. Ah’d burn it. - Extreme Title: Ah’d give this to mah muckah Rab C Nesbitt, an’ I don’t need to give ye an explanation, cos Rab is the man. - Rising Title: Pish min. Burn it. Burn it awl. - Tag Team Titles: No question, this wid huv to go to mysel’ an’ Rab, and that’s no Rab C Nesbitt, by the way. An’ that’s that as far as awl they titles go, but I’d make mah own title as well, it’d be cawled the "Ah’m A Big Chube Title", and it’d be for awl they chubes out there, like Flaymin Aymin. Cos, If ah’d give them that title, then everybody would know that they’re pure pish an’ awl. Ah’d also call the company ‘Glesga Championship Wrestlin’’ cos Primetime is a pure pish name an’ awl. Ah’d fire awl they gay referees an’ that, and hire some new ones, fae Glesga, of course. An’ ah’d also have big screens all around the arenas, so that ah could watch the fitba’ whenever I want. Well, that’s about it, ah’m gonnae go watch the tele. Awright muckah? Now, if anyone can tell me what the hell this thing said, I'll give you a toffee or something. In retrospect, he's too much of an idiot to make him look any worse. My job was easy this time. Anyway, that's the first and last time I speak to that man. That's it. This is the End of the Line. Go home.
