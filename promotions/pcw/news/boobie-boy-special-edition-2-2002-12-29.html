Boobie Boy: Special Edition 2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Dec 2002 ---- Hello fellow RUSH fans and I would like to welcome you back to my special edition column full of my rantings. Well as you have all seen it has been one full month since Xavier Kannon’s take over of RUSH Wrestling. Well, first off many wrestlers in PCW have not been all but too friendly to us RUSHer’s. Myself along with Marvin, Gideon, and Clem have found few friends who have helped to join our cause. Many fear the oppression of Kannon and few have openly spoken their mouths. But those who have, one of them being Brock Newbludd have been very supportive to our cause. These people understand our goals, understand our needs and desires and understand that RUSH will SURVIVE. Alas though, this month has been a hard one for us. Xavier Kannon has brought much hardship upon me on my friends. Forced to stay in the basement areas of the arenas before our matches and receiving little welcome to events has made travel tough for us. But for the love of RUSH and wrestling we all trudge on. Now, Tyler Valentine was a friend of mine for as long as I had been in RUSH. He knew of my past and still thought nothing to befriend me. But like many men before him Tyler fell victim to a power that is greater than all others. More powerful than Tyler Nelson’s money more powerful than THE PHANTOM BOOKER’S power and even more powerful than Ellie’s monstrous ass. Tyler Valentine was corrupted by fame. Tyler found himself so engrossed by all of the fans, the same fans who I fight for night after night, and realized that fighting for them wasn’t worth it anymore. Everything that the Resistance was fighting for meant nothing to him once he one the RUSH WORLD TITLE and that is why Tyler…you are an asshole! Reasons why Tyler is an asshole according to The Masked Mullet: 5: He smells bad. 4: He ain't no better then Chris Matthews. 3: He's sneaky and slowdown. 2: He's a cheat, n' a liar! 1: He's a low down dirty varmint. Reasons why Tyler is an asshole according to Marvin Harris : 5) Tyler Valentine is an asshole because his hair looks more like a bad wig than Matthews's, and believe me that’s no easy task. 4) Tyler Valentine is an asshole, because well, he's a vessel by which shit is vacated from the body. What else do you need? 3) Tyler Valentine is a HUGE asshole because we all know he had collagen injections that gave him his award-wining NTICW smile, and lost them only recent because of 'overuse of his jaw and lower gums' since he's been a member of the RUSH defectors. Goes to show what kind of things you have to do to be in Kannon's good graces. 2) Tyler Valentine is an asshole because he was a more likeable person when he thought he was a walking, talking, wrestling Peanut Butter & Jelly sandwich. 1.) Tyler Valentine is an asshole because he's forsaken all the ideals and principles that made him worth a shit and thrown his lot behind a man who dyes his hair in orange SLICE. Reasons why Tyler is an asshole according to Gideon Poe: 5) He thought he was a peanut butter and jelly sandwich..A SANDWICH! COME ON! 4) Chris Matthews had to train him..MATTHEWS I SAY~! 3) He was born on Valentines day, as are many other famous homosexuals that will not be named for legal reason 2) Valentine has lost to Caleb McGlocklan, and let’s face it, WE'VE ALL BEATEN HIM! 1) And the biggest reason Tyler Valentine is an asshole is because he is going to loose his title to Marvin faster than Matthews lost to Brock! Reasons why Tyler is and asshole according to Boobie Boy~! 5) He has gayer hair then Xavier Kannon. 4) Not once have I seen him with a woman, I think it is safe to assume he is a flamer. 3) He is a coward and turned his back on his true friends. 2) He is going down the path Chris Matthews, making him just another joke. 1) He is so far up Kannon’s ass now that together they form the world’s largest anus. There you have it folks…just some ways to show you that if your friends with Kannon you aint friends with us. One Love, One Blood, ONE RUSH~! This is Boobie Boy~! and the RUSH RESISTANCE signing out.
