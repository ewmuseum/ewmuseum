Stanisalv Going to War? Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Sep 2001 ---- Ivan Stanislav has fought a proverbial war since he arrived within the PCW months ago, but now he has come closer to fighting in Afghanistan once again, than ever imagined. In the wake of the tragedy that struck the United States of America on September 11th, President Vladimir Putin of the Russian Federation has been very vocal about the support that any anti-terrorist action would receive from Russia. While the Russian Federation will not allow American planes to use their airspace, they are prepared to offer other forms of support. Russia will give information and intelligence that they have about the Taliban, as well as allow “humanitarian” aid planes to fly past Russia. Most ground shaking, however, is the fact that Putin has agreed to give military aid to any attack against the Taliban in the form of not only weapons, but manpower as well. Rumor has it that Stanislav was contacted last night in the middle of the night and was told that Putin himself is considering moving Stanislav back to Moscow, and then decide where he should be transferred. “As a warrant officer, Ivan Stanislav can bolster morale of virtually any Russian soldier,” says Cal Seaver, who keeps a very close eye on The Russian Bear. “However, the chances of Stanislav actually fighting on the battlefield are very slim, at best. In a few months, Stanislav will celebrate his 40th birthday, and while his age is not a hindrance by any means, he is legally beyond the age to fight in the Russian Army.” As a warrant officer, however, Stanislav can command, and while he is focused on foreign affairs, he could be transferred or promoted to a higher rank, in which he would command troops. “Recently, an Mi-8 helicopter was shot down by Chechnya rebels. The chopper held a major Russian General and eight chiefs of staff, which means that those positions must be filled and free’s up mobility to move higher in the ranks,” said Seaver. “While Ivan Sergeiovich is a great fighter and leader, he is better to be left within the USA, where he can raise morale more publicly for our boys, who may risk fighting soon,” said Alexei Ruslan. “I will be meeting with Comrade Putin soon, and we will know more about this matter after that meeting.” Ruslan didn't stop there. “Whatever happens, whether it is staying in the PCW, or battling in Afghanistan once again, Stanislav will be fighting, and that is what he does best," screamed Ruslan. We will bring you more on this as it becomes available.
