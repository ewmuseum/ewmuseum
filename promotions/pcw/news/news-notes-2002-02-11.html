News & Notes Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Feb 2002 ---- Until someone can be found to replace Jane Reagan, there will be no PCW personnel reports. Just little tidbits of news and notes. El Diablo will recieve a bye into the next round. Big Alan Steele will not be ready to return by the 15th, so this was the only alternative. El Diablo will face the winner of the Vampir Nosferatu vs Anton the Kid match on Febuary 22nd for a spot in the March Madness battle royal. PCW officials have been unimpressed with the work, or lack there of by Avalanche and Token Weed. "If I don't see them lift one finger by the end of Turmoil, they'll be sent packing," said CEO Tyler Nelson. "And if you don't believe me, ask Sassy. That's if you can find what corner she's working." Former NZW talent Ganker has been signed to a contract. However, a time for his debut has yet to be determined. We'll bring you more news and notes when we get them.
