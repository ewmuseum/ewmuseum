Malice Back At The Desk

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Malice Back At The Desk Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Jan 2003 ---- With Eleanor Kannon-Hall now at Xavier’s bedside and expected to take the role of his nurse in the coming weeks, she has resigned from her role as Fusion’s 3rd Chair. On the same note, with the CEO now out of action, the Pimply Faced Intern Kid has been given more Front Office duties so will be standing down from his Turmoil announcing role. Before the Front office had a chance to announce their replacements, Duke stepped in and signed himself to an unbreakable announcing deal with PCW, covering both Turmoil and Fusion. With the RUSH trio of Argyle, Kris Raves and Vanilla Skillz all released last night, the Sunday Adrenaline show has been dropped and it’s time-slot sold back to the network by the Front Office. So with the return to 2 shows a week, and Duke returning to the desk, it seems Primetime has been quick to revert to old times following the events regarding RUSH at Shanghaied.
