After Fusion Went Off the Air

13 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After Fusion Went Off the Air Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Oct 2001 ---- Immediately following the termination of Friday Fusion's broadcast, The Dark Age emerged and taunted Ivan Stanislav, then summoned a hoarde of penguins, which they claimed were genetically engineered in a laboratory from the DNA of the giant penguin that pinned Ivan at the Seaworld Brawl, and sent them into overwhelm the Russian Bear. Meanstreak went to attack Dark Age, but was interrupted by the appearance of Team CEO on the Tron. Kremmen and Tyler Nelson taunted both Meanstreak and Ivan, and claimed responsibility for financing Dark Age's cerebral attack on Stanislav, and sending them out to attack them, along with saying that T4H had earned a night off - with full pay. Team CEO closed out with "Oh, and we'll see you two kids on Sunday, when we use you as stepping stones into the finals of the lethal lottery tournament at Red October, where we will again use you as stepping stones on our way to the Heavyweight Title. Until then boys, adios!" Big Alan Steele then emerged, steel chair in hand, to assist Meanstreak as he went at it with Dark Age, while Ivan Stanislav battled with the hoarde of red-eyed penguins. Source: PCW Production Team
