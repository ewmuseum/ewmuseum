Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 3

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 3 Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Apr 2003 ---- PFIK: The semi-finals are underway. Teresa Tomas and Tessa Windsor are starting in the ring. Teresa starts off with a kick to the gut. Punches backing Windsor to the ropes. A whip away, Tessa comes back and hits a shoulder block. Windsor goes off her feet! Tomas ordering Tessa to try again. Tessa runs to the ropes, another shoulder block. Neither goes down. Tomas points to the ropes again. Windsor turns to the ropes BACK KICK! Tiffany: Tomas doubled over from the kick to the stomach. Bulldog! Tomas shoved her to the ropes. In with a kidney shot. Another. Tomas with a backdrop suplex. Tessa flips out of it and lands behind for a School Boy Rollup. 1.... 2... kickout at two. PFIK: Tomas back up to her feet, she catches Tessa with a clothesline. Tomas pulling Tessa to her feet. Scoop slam. Fist Drop. A pin of her own. Two count. Tomas pulls Tessa back up. Suplex, Tessa headbutts Tomas, escapes. Tags in Chris Storm! Storm comes in with a springboard dropkick. Tomas sidesteps! Tag to Akiyama! Tiffany: The two males are in the ring now. Chris Storm reluctant to run right at him. Storm is calling for a test of strength? PFIK: Akiyama quick to take the offer. Storm is putting up his right hand, Akiyama locking it with his left. Quick kick, Akiyama grabs his foot. Backflip kick from Storm! Storm free, he charges Akiyama with a spear. Akiyama doesn't budge! Double-axhandle to the small of Storm's back. Storm falling to the mat in pain from the crushing blow of the nVg. Tiffany: Akiyama brings Storm back up. Powerbomb! There's a pin. 1... 2... kickout. Pretty loose cover by Aki. Akiyama stands there, breathing scarily heavy and such. Storm brought to his feet, whipped off the ropes. Judgment Blade by Aki CHRIS STORM DROPKICKS THE PLANTED KNEE! PFIK: Akiyama fell into a split. Chris Storm runs off the ropes behind Akiyama. Flipover Neck Whip! Aki falls down onto his back. Storm with the standing moonsault. He tucked in the knees for impact! Cover.... 1... 2.... Kickout! Akiyama still has a bit of power to spare. Tiffany: Chris Storm grabbing the leg. Stomping in and kicking the back of the knee. He's dragging Akiyama over to his corner, albeit with a lot of effort. Tag to Tessa? Chris, Akiyama's still dangerous! PFIK: Slingshot Somersault Body Splash, Tessa landed on the propped leg of Akiyama. Back in the corner, she jumps off the second rope, stomping the leg. Tag back in to Chris Storm. Split-legged moonsault onto the leg resting on the bottom rope. They're isolating the bigger one and attacking a specific area, good tag team strategy. Tiffany: Chris Storm trying to turn Akiyama over. Tessa on the apron pushing Akiyama to help. Aki in a single crab! Chris Storm has the submission locked in, standing with it. Akiyama using his arm strength to drag himself and Storm away from the corner. Tessa reaches over the top rope and tags herself in. Akiyama to the middle of the ring. Slingshot leg drop by Tessa Windsor! PFIK: Chris Storm with the single crab still locked in; Tessa Windsor runs to the ropes. Dropkick to the locked knee! Aki pulls his leg in, flipping Storm off. Tessa running in at Aki. Hard Spike! Tessa Tiffany: Tessa speared out of her boots. Storm drops the elbow on the back of Akiyama's head. Storm grabbing Akiyama's leg again. Akiyama pushing up to a stance. Reverse Enziguri! Akiyama rolls over to his corner. Tag to Teresa Tomas. Tomas in the ring with a missile dropkick to Chris Storm! A pin; two count! PFIK: Tomas lets Chris up, charge into the corner with a Tiger Wall Flip; dropkick sends Storm back into the corner. Tomas up to the second rope. Ten punches. Storm out of the corner with a weak powerbomb to Tomas. Judgment Blade! Tiffany: Storm sent for a loop with that attack. Tessa Windsor with a dropkick to the back of the leg, taking Akiyama down. Teresa Tomas from behind with a rear waistlock. Tessa runs to the ropes, rebounding Tomas away. Tomas rolls to her feet and charges. Tessa with a back body drop over the ropes. Tomas lands on the apron. Elbow to the back of the head; Tessa turned around, grabbed by the head... SUPLEX TO THE OUTSIDE BY TERESA TOMAS! PFIK: Akiyama rolls onto his stomach and pushes himself up to his feet. He grabs Chris Storm and brings him onto his shoulders. Akiyama's leg gives out, he drops to a knee. The nVg can't lift Storm for the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse! Tiffany: Wait, there's Teresa Tomas. She's behind Akiyama, standing back to back. She's helping him lift Chris Storm! Akiyama back up to his feet with Tomas's help... ADA! PFIK: Tessa Windsor is on the floor, Teresa Tomas pis Chris Storm. 1..... 2..... 3! They did it! The tandem of Teresa Tomas and Akiyama are moving to the finals! Tiffany: Impressive. PFIK: Indeed. The next match is P.W.A. against the SNC. Will there be another upset? We'll see once the ring is cleared. Tiffany: Akiyama is rolling out of the ring, Tomas behind him. They're not congratulating each other or anything, just leaving. Storm and Windsor are pretty much knocked out. PFIK: They'll come to soon. _______________________________________ PFIK: Oh, the Pranksters are here! Tiffany: I heard one of Dante Jackson's friends are out here in the sea, but oh well. Naru and Gaz are starting this match off...Naru shot off into the ropes, back with a clothsline by Gaz! Gaz picks up the smaller woman and lunges her into the ropes. A karate kick, a second, and a third! PFIK: Mocking her now, Gaz goes from opposite corner, BRUCE LEE KICK! Tiffany: Naru is down. Gaz covers 1..2..Kickout! PFIK: Gaz walks over and tags Nez as he hops in, stomping away at Naru. He picks her up by the head and drops her back with a Suplex! Naru clutches her back, but Nez doesn't care as he drops a leg drop! Tiffany: What kind of leg drop? Hogan or regular? PFIK: Does it matter? Tiffany: It might! PFIK: Oh well, Nez locks in a cross arm scissors, pulling back on that arm as Naru looks to grab the ropes right now. Amazon climbs in the ring, stomping the hell out of him! Tiffany: I would so think that would be out of character because no 6'8" Asian woman exists! And her boobs look like a fat ice pack! PFIK: There's some 7-foot-tall Chinese woman that we're trying to court for basketball. Tiffany: You mean Yao Ming? PFIK: No, someone different. Tiffany: Hmph. PFIK: Um, right, well Jack forces Amazon out of the ring as Nez lifts Naru up in the air, BRAINBUSTER! Cover! 1... 2.. another kickout! Tiffany: Nez beginning to get frustrated right now as he spins Naru around, HALF NELSON RELEASE SUPLEX! Nez tags in Gaz as he climbs the top rope... PFIK: FROG SPLASH! Tiffany: 1...2...KICKOUT! PFIK: Gaz and Nez are just awe struck right now! They both lift Naru up...LOW BLOW BY NARU! DOUBLE LOW BLOW! Tiffany: What a slut! PFIK: TAG TO AMAZON! Tiffany: Amazon gives Gaz a big boot and knocks him down. PFIK: Naru spinning heel kicks Nez back down, but he pops back up. Tiffany: Right, they're in the ring, and P.W.A have been dominating the SNC for most of the match. PFIK: We both know that a second wind can change the whole complexion of a match. Tiffany: Amazon and Naru team up on Nerezza and whip him to the ropes. Nerezza on the return hoisted into the air with the flapjack by Amazon; jawbreaker from Naru! Amazon kicks Nerezza to the outside and Naru goes back to her corner. PFIK: Amazon picks up Gaz Green; whips him to the ropes. Gaz comes back with a cross body block. Amazon caught him! Rib breaker, falling powerslam. Fallaway slam! Tiffany: Nice chain of moves by Amazon, the largest competitor in this match. PFIK: She reminds me of Callisto Bane with her size and fighting. Tiffany: *Audible shudders* PFIK: What? Tiffany: Amazon runs off the ropes. HOGAN STYLE LEG DROP! There's a cover! 1.... 2.... Kickout! PFIK: Gaz whipped to his corner, Amazon is pointing to Nerezza! Nerezza into the ring, he's stalking up to the larger Amazon. There's a tieup. Amazon is forcing Nerezza back into the neutral corner! Tiffany: Jack making them separate. Amazon backs away. Violet Violence! Amazon must be blinded! Nerezza with a standing clothesline. Now up to the second rope. The Fang! Amazon rolls over to her stomach. Nerezza grabbing the legs. Breaking The Law! PFIK: BLAM! Chair shot to Nez from Naru, she turns....BLAM! Chairshot to Gaz! And another! A third! Fourth! Jack is calling for the bell; P.W.A are going to be advancing here to the finals against the pair of Akiyama and Teresa Tomas, but at what price? ______________________________ That's it for the semi-finals of the tournament. The finals of the tournament, featuring Teresa Tomas and Akiyama against Pranksters With Attitude will be rebroadcast LIVE on Tuesday Turmoil. We filmed it, so you'll see every move, counter, and reversal; rather than the written recaps that you've seen so far. See you then!
