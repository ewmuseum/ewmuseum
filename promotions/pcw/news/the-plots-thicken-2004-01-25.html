The plots thicken...

25 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The plots thicken... Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jan 2004 ---- As PCW airs for its 5th time since reopening in January, and the card is stacked with great matches from top to bottom. What should have been a tag match between the top four of the World Title division, ended up being a catalyst for controversy.(Green/Blitz vs Propaganda/Buzz) This occured when Buzz, enraged with jealousy, caused his team to be disqualified after he attacked Green and Blitz with Green's World title belt. However, the true surprise occured when The Buzz nailed Eric Sagar, and knocked the big man out of the ring. A challenge was laid down and this week Propaganda and The Buzz will lock up in a no disqualification match! Rent-A-Hero and Robert Agg tried to to change Skitzo in various ways, but managed to fail each time. Skitzo would end up attacking both men, and costing RAH a match against Titan later in the night. Now PCW's favorite motivational hero will get a rematch against Titan to avenge his loss. Skitzo will go against the wannabe pop icon Alexander Pryce in singles action. Will RAH attempt to gain revenge against Skitzo for last week, or will Skitzo continue his crazy ways? For weeks Shaun Styles and Brian Arson have been bitter enemies. After two matches they have had a no contest and Arson has pulled out a victory. Can this be the night Shaun Styles evens the score, or will Brian Arson show the world that Shaun Styles is out of his league. Afwa and Avery Vance are set to lock up in singles action. Avery Vance is 1-1-1 in his PCW tenure thus far. Can the "smooth criminal" gain a victory over the Samoan monster? One has to think the odds are against him, as Afwa is almost unstoppable as of late. Not to mention he put a great superstar like Jay Pheonix on the shelf temporarily. On top of that, Avery Vance will have to keep his eyes open for his fellow PWA graduate turned rival, Donovan Emerson. After a no contest last week, DIME is surely going to look for a way to make Vance's life miserable. Also, Inphino Blitz, Darkstar, Skylar Green are all sure to be on the show. Will we get an update on Duke Williams arrest, and find out possibly who the blonde man was from last week. Tune in for all this and more Tuesday on Fox!
