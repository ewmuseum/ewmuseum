Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 1

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Apr 2003 ---- Tiffany Jennings, here. I, along with the Pimply-Faced Intern Kid, called the matches as they happened. There was a little digital recorder there, it just caught our voices. We called the action for every match as it happened on a huge superyacht off the coast of the Pacific ocean at 3 AM Sunday morning. Everyone was up bright and early (actually, the bright part was disputable) and they managed not to kill each other in their small quarters before their matches. The matches were refereed by Violence Jack himself. Without further ado, I'll just play the tape. ---- PFIK: *Yawns* So... I guess we're about to start. Tiffany: Yep. And the first match is Dante Jackson and El Romantico against the Ghosts of Avalanche and Hiro Saotome. PFIK: But we've been waiting for 20 minutes. They're no showing up. Tiffany: Frankly, I'm surprised that we waited this long. PFIK: The match is being awarded to Dante and Romantico by default, and we're slowly regaining our sanity. Tiffany: Finally. _____________________________________________ Tiffany: Okay, the next match is Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor, the Schmoopies, against AJ Trinidad and Semore Smoke. PFIK: Tessa and Semore are starting off together. Smoke calls for a test of strength. Tessa kicks him in the stomach and lays him out with a DDT. A standing leg drop to the back of the head. Tiffany: Quick tag to Chris Storm. Storm comes in and scoop slams Smoke, followed by an elbow drop and a two-count pin. PFIK: Storm brings Smoke to his feet and whips him to the corner, followed by a clothesline and another tag. Snapmare out of the corner, and a slingshot leg drop by Tessa Windsor for another two count. Tiffany: Windsor kicks Smoke in the head; Smoke stands up. Windsor goes to whip Smoke, but he reverses it and pulls her into a knee to the midsection. He whips her over to his corner. AJ Trinidad reaches his leg through the ropes and hooks his foot around, trapping her in the corner. Smoke runs in with a clothesline and kicks her in the midsection. PFIK: A tag into AJ Trinidad. AJ comes into the ring. They take her out to the middle of the ropes and whip her to the opposite ropes. AJ.... scoops Smoke onto his shoulders and hits The Migraine! Tiffany: Tessa comes back into a high vertical body press and crashes onto Smoke. AJ is walking away! PFIK: 1.... 2.... 3! Tiffany: The Schmoopies advance in the tournament and they'll face Dante Jackson and El Romantico in the next round. PFIK: AJ Trinidad actually gave up a chance at winning the Continental Title just to get one over on Semore Smoke! Tiffany: I guess he figures there'll be other chances in the future. _________________________________ PFIK: The next match involves the Cruiserweight Champion, The Pharaoh, and his partner, the 2-Time former Television Champion, Tim Walden, going against Lacuna Debris and Jade, The Significant Others. Tiffany: They couldn't have picked a better name? PFIK: I guess they're going for that "Outsiders" thing. Tiffany: Walden starts off with Lacuna. Walden moves out and locks up with Lacuna, and gets a headlock. Lacuna elbows Walden in the groin, and Violence Jack allows it. Lacuna pokes Walden in the eyes and kicks him in the groin again. PFIK: She rolls up Walden in a Small Package. 1.... 2.... 3! Tiffany: Wow, Pharaoh and Jade didn't even get in the ring and it's already over. PFIK: The Pharaoh is getting in the ring now. He's stomped on Walden! Tiffany: Picking him up... there's the Jewel of the Nile! PFIK: He's angry about Walden ruining this for him! Tiffany: Either way, Lacuna Debris and Jade have moved on to the next match. __________________________________ PFIK: The next match has already started. Teresa Tomas and Matt Jackson are on the outside, fighting each other. Tiffany: Haley Hilton is in the ring with the monster Akiyama. PFIK: She's jumping up for a hurricanrana. Trine! The triple crossed-arms jacknife bomb! Tiffany: Akiyama's holding on for the pin, and Matt Jackson is sliding in the ring. PFIK: Can't get in on time, though. Just as fast as everything got started with that pre-bell attack by Akiyama and Tomas, the match is over. Tiffany: Akiyama just gave Matt Jackson a hard spear! PFIK: Tomas is walking away. Good move, Akiyama would probably attack anyone who got to close to him right now. Tiffany: After these two are removed from the ring, the next match will begin. ___________________________ Tiffany: Our second all-female team of the night, the Suburban Ninja Clan's slave geisha Amazon and Naru are out here and waiting for their opponents. PFIK: Here come Cal Seaver and Boca Del Inferno. Tiffany: He likes to be called calVen now, for some reason. PFIK: Boca's stopped... Tiffany: He's running away! PFIK: It must be the large, scantily-clad Amazon that's scaring him off. Tiffany: Well, calVen's out here on his own. He's running to the ring. Double-clothesline. PFIK: He bounced off! They pick him up. Powerbomb/Neckbreaker combo. Tiffany: 1.... 2.... 3. Okay, that's over. Another quick match. PFIK: The Suburban Ninja Clan advances. __________________________________ Tiffany: Young Tiger and Banshee are starting off in the ring. PFIK: Banshee is biting Young Tiger on the leg. He's getting his teeth deep into the skin. Tiffany: Young Tiger drags his leg over to his corner. Darkstar is just sitting on the apron ignoring him! PFIK: He's already the Television Champion. He may not care about going for the Continental Title. Tiffany: Young Tiger kicks Banshee against the bottom turnbuckle, but he can't get it off. Young Tiger turns and runs to the center of the ring. Chokeslam! PFIK: Young Tiger got wasted. Dark Gimmick really knocked the sense out of him! Tiffany: And there's the three count. The disinterested Darkstar is leaving the ring and Dark Gimmick and Banshee are advancing! ___________________________________ PFIK: Where are Rent-A-Hero and Clyde? Tiffany: Wait, they're fighting over there! PFIK: That's not the ring... Tiffany: They're over at the stern of the yacht, trading punches. PFIK: Rent-A-Hero does a superkick. Clyde ducks and scoops RAH onto his shoulders for a Samoan Drop. Tiffany: Clyde brings RAH back up and whips him, then pulls him into a shortarm clothesline. Duck under. Crossed-arms Reverse Knee Smash! PFIK: Clyde's stumbling over to the edge. We're 30 feet above the water. RAH runs over, mule kick. Tiffany: He's kicked back. Clyde charges him and hits a spear. Some mounted punches. RAH with the palm thrust to hit the nose. PFIK: Clyde stands up and stomps. RAH catches the foot, leg trip. RAH drags Clyde into position near the edge-rail. Catapult. Clyde gets caught on the rail. Tiffany: RAH moves in to push him over. Back elbow by Clyde. Clyde hits RAH's head against the rail. A second time, RAH's head again hits the railing. PFIK: Clyde backing up, he goes in with a clothesline. Back Body Drop! RAH sent Clyde off into the water! Tiffany: Clyde fell 30 feet into the water! PFIK: Wait, RAH's jumped onto the rail... HE DID A SHOOTING STAR PRESS AFTER CLYDE! Tiffany: P.W.A. are falling over themselves laughing at this. PFIK: And since RAH and Clyde are going to be thrashing about in shark-infested waters, I'd say that Gaz Green and Nerezza are the winners by default. Tiffany Jennings: Apparently the Overweighter and Ellie are also in the second round. PFIK: What? Tiffany Jennings: Dorn Tracer refused to tag with Drachen at the last minute, and they've been removed. Overweighter and Ellie get a bye to the second round. PFIK: Alright then. That's round one.
