Sandy Wong Dead

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sandy Wong Dead Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2003 ---- Following the unspeakably brutal attack that Sandy Wong suffered at the hands of Violence Jack last Friday at Great Falls, Montana (which caused Skylar Green to skip his scheduled Turmoil appearance), Sandy Wong was taken to the local hospital in Great Falls. Doctors worked around the clock to stabilize her, but their best attempts were in vain. "By the end of the night," says a hospital spokesperson, "they knew they couldn't do anything else for her and just gave her some painkillers. Her injuries were too severe and too many for our surgeons to treat. She was expected to die within the next few days." Skylar Green accompanied Sandy Wong to the hospital and would not leave her side. He caused a commotion, apparently punching a hole through one of the walls. Once she was given a room, he sat at her bedside until she died. The funeral will not be announced to the public, and only family and close friend shall be invited by phone to attend. Skylar Green was not available for comment. Great Falls law enforcement do not intend to pursue charges against Violence Jack, who orchestrated the attack and lent more than his share of blows. Great Falls police refused to comment as to why such a horrible act is going unpunished by law. Our hearts and prayers go out to the family of Sandy Wong, and we'll report any other nwes as it comes our way.
