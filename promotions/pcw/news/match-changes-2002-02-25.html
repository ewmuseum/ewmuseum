Match Changes

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Match Changes Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Feb 2002 ---- A few changes to announce for upcoming cards. - With Timo Bolamba's sudden departure to OSW, he has been removed from the main event on Turmoil. The match will now be a handicap match with Boda taking on both Sow and Corey Williams. - Also, for Fusion, with Timo's exit, Afwa Kilmanjaro will take on both Chris Chase and Corey Williams in a handicap match. - The match scheduled between The End and Asa Fountain for Fusion has been cancelled after Asa Fountain announced his retirement again. If any other match changes occur, we'll let you know.
