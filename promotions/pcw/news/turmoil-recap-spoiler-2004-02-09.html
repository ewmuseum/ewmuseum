Turmoil Recap (spoiler)

9 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turmoil Recap (spoiler) Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Feb 2004 ---- The show starts with Duke Williams and Jerry Jeff giving PCW’s resident lawyers Dick James and Alan Zoober a hard time back stage. Which leads to a shot of PCW’s own head of security Titan standing in front of Dick and Zoobers door only to be knocked down by Jon Kano. Kano marched into the office only to find out he’ll be facing Propaganda later on in the show. Outside Boda’s black H2 hummer pulls up only to be greeted by Cal Roberts. The two exchange words with Boda getting the best of “The Buzz”. The first match of the night was Jerry Jeff Walker vs. Mike Wade. In the end Wade got the best of Jerry Jeff and came away with the 1 - 2 - 3. Next we flash back to last weeks Turmoil where The Mega Soaring Eagles broke down into an intense brawl with Koji Machida leading Basho in the attack up until an unknown Japanese girl appears out of no where and attacks Koji. In the end, The MSA and the girl stand above Koji in unity. In the back we see Abners red truck pull up only to be greeted by The PFIK, The kid informs Abner he has a match with Alexander Pryce later on in the show. Then we lead to Boda stopping Skylar Green. Both men talk for a bit then leave in a show of respect. Next up its Jon Kano vs. Propaganda, in the end Kano pulls it out with the help of Duke Williams and Boda. In the back we see Donovan Emerson talking to Avery Vance. With Donovan having a legitimate beef with Vance. Backstage Eric Sagar and Propaganda appall the crowd While in the ring Jay Phoenix pulls one out on Avery Vance, after the match Phoenix announces he is going after Cruiserweight gold. In The ring The MSA are interrupted by Dick and Zoober wondering who the girl who helped them last week was. We then find out it is none other than MISS MOONLIGHT!! Former Cruiserweight champion and she wants her belt back. Dick and Zoober make the match Basho vs. Titan later on with Koji as the special referee. In the back Rent-A-Hero and Skitzo have heated conversation. Next up its Alexander Pryce vs. Abner, Abner gets the win with the help of Darkstar. In the back we find Cal Roberts talking to a girl only to be interrupted by Skylar Green. In the Ring Titan takes on Basho Amuro, Basho almost kills Titan which leads Koji to ring the bell and award the match to Basho via knockout. Backstage Brian Arson and Shaun Styles get into a scuffle. Alexander Pryce and his mother, Marie march down to the ring to have a couple words with Darkstar, only to have Talena come from behind and nail Marie in the head. Next is Skitzo vs. RAH, Skitzo gets the win in extremely weird fashion In the back Ukyo meets Koji’s daughter Myoko Machida while Propaganda and Skylar Green have words. Next up is Cal Roberts vs. Inphino Blitz, In this match both men give it there all hitting all of there finishing maneuvers with Skylar Green and Propaganda coming out and making it an all out brawl. Backstage sparks fly between Myoko Machida and Ukyo And in the Main Event Propaganda and Afwa pull it out over Duke and Boda.
