New Roster Announced

19 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Roster Announced Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 May 2002 ---- Over the weekend, the much anticipated announcement was made by The Phantom Booker revealing the new Primetime Championship Wrestling roster. Here is the list in it's entirety: Singles Jon Kano The Chef Duke Williams Big Daveylicious Anna Keyson Jaden Heaven Jordan X Shabazz Mark Makonie Xavier Kannon Corey Williams The Pharoah Flaymin Aymin The End Snow Rent A Hero Akiyama Violence Jack Charlie Loc Duzza Andy Murray John Chrichton Juan Borro Kid Krazie Kramer Wildwing Mike Frampton Trigger Tim Walden The Rush Ryan Stephens El Diablo Miyagawa THE Multiple Man Bobby Stormbringer Tag Teams Pretty Cool Guys The Heartbreakers Politically Incorrect Cowabunga Connection The Price Brothers Dark Messiahs Misplaced The New Iconz These names, of course, are in addition to Big Alan Steele, the PCW World Champion, and the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson. The first show will take place this Friday and originate from the birthplace of Primetime, the PCW Pavillion in Atlantic City.
