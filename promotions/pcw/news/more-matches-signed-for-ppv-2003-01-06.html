More Matches Signed for PPV

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More Matches Signed for PPV Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jan 2003 ---- After his shocking return on Fusion, Skylar Green retained his position of #1 Contender for the Continental Title, embarrassing Rush Mason in the process. With Rush’s close ties to Xavier Kannon stretching back to their NTICW days, the CEO was quick to grant Mason a match at Shanghaied where they will fight for the Continental Title shot. Also signed was the Title match itself where Totally Tubular Tommy will defend against Rent-a-Hero, who will be looking to avenge recent attacks by TTT since his return. With the winners of those 2 matches meeting, the eyes of Dante Jackson and Chet King will be on all 4 as they will also meet in Shanghai in a match that may finally settle their rivalry that has been heated ever since Chet made his return to PCW under the guise of Avalanche. As well as those 3 matches and the previous 3 already signed, 2 rematches from Decimation are expected to be signed where PCW’s Tag Champions Xavier Kannon and Gaz Green will face Duke Williams and Nerezza respectively. With tension building between El Romantico and Tim Walden, many are also expecting a Cruiserweight Title match between the pair to be added, as well as a match to hopefully solve the problems Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor & Jackson Rust are having. Shanghaied 3 will air, as is tradition, from the Shanghai Gymnasium on January 26th.
