Pharaoh Under Wraps? Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Jan 2002 ---- This was posted on WrestlingTattler.com earlier this morning: Titu Xansiv, otherwise known as PCW's the Pharaoh has been called over by Miami police to help with the mysterious murder of Xansiv's uncle, Bernie Xansiv. Police chief Harry Stooge has this statement to say... "My Xansiv has been called in, not by me, but my fellow workmate Detective Cory Malone to help in the murder of Xansiv's uncle." said Stooge when questioned about the situation. "But between you and me, things are looking very strange at the moment, i mean this guy, Pharaoh, he we have no records or police files on him, damn it it's like the guy doesn't even exist!" concluded Stooge. Detecive Cory Malone released this statement to the press. "Mr Xansiv, is currently helping us in the death of his great, great, great, great, great, great, oh damn i dunno how many more greats there are, but anyway, he's helping us with the murder of his uncle. Thank you... Police are still baffled on how Xansiv could possibly have an uncle so old, but when the body was examined police found that Bernie was only 45 years old! This adds even more mystery on the situation and it's quite obvious that police officials are baffled... Titu Xansiv has always held true to the belief that he is an Ancient Egyptian Pharaoh, and up until now people have simply called him mad. But with the new discovery that Titu has no known documents on him, perhaps we will have more light shed on the truth behind Xansiv. Pharaoh could not be reached for comment... We'll bring you more on the situation as it becomes available.
