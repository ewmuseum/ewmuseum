Playaz Inc. Pissed Off

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Playaz Inc. Pissed Off Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Sep 2002 ---- After the #1 Contenders match to the PCW Tag Team championship match on Fusion two weeks ago, Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo; collectively known as Playaz Inc. have been rather not given their tag team title shot. Afterall they were the victors in that match. "Its bullshit! They just don't want another black tag team championship reign!" Jackson lamented after leaving Fusion once again victorious. "It saddens me that we'll have to take such rigorious precautions just to get that shot back from those whitey, pasty, and in your cracka sense "lame" bitches getting the shots on Turmoil." Statuz Quo, busy trying to fight in a Gang War with the one man Wisconsin Crips Gang, had this disturbing and rather hard to figure out comment towards P.W.A. "Dem punk bitches don't kno who de fuck they be fucken wit do they? I'ma str8 knock one of dem fuckas da fuck out if dey try an' fuck up dis muhfucken program. Think they hard n shit, tryna pimp out me and Dante fo dem Tag titles. Naw, FUCK DAT! They ain't handlin' shit, and I'ma personally see to dat shit." With Jackson and Quo on the manhunt, it seems like they aren't wanting to stop, after winning three matches in a row - can they go for four in a row? We'll see.
