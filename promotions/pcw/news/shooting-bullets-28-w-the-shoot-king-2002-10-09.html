Shooting Bullets #28 w/The Shoot King Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Oct 2002 ---- The following are the views and expressions of The Shoot King, a ham sandwich, and actor/comedian Nipsey Russell. In no way do these views and expressions reflect the opinions of those in PCW or its affiliates. And unauthorized duplication of this article will result in your ass on a platter courtesy of The Shoot King. Parental discretion is advised The Bastard asked where I went. Just so he knows, I took the summer off from writing, the came back to blast the former OSW champ and his cronies as they walked out on the company. My last column, entitled Shooting Bullets #27, can be found by clicking the link. As for where I should be; I should be at Wrestling Tattler, but the place seems to be down for the time being. So, every now and again, I write a column exclusive for a fed. Today, I’ve decided to write this exclusive to PCW. Now I’m no stranger to PCW. I’ve been PCW’s top columnist, even in absence, for close to two years. That’s not saying that I’m the greatest thing since sliced bread, although I probably am. It’s just saying how pathetic every new wave of columnists has been. Enough of introducing myself. If you don’t know who I am by now, then you are of no importance. You are in the extreme minority that I couldn’t give two shits about. You, moron, are a Brownie. Today, I talk about “Total Selection Friday”. But before I do, allow me to congratulate a man I have true respect for. A guy who was nearly sent packing more than once. A guy who worked his ass off back in the days of the Primetime Indy Circuit to now become the biggest player in the game. Congratulations to Clyde on his victories at Red October. Seriously, very few deserve it as much as this guy. From slave monkey jobbing to Scourge to slave monkey with the big belt, nobody has made more strides in PCW history than Clyde. And, yes, that includes Boda. So, Clyde, on behalf of the people that matter, I salute you. Now, as far as “Total Selection Friday” goes, do we really need it? Seriously, is it really needed? Should the “fans” of PCW really be booking the show? Absolutely not! Yeah, fans might matter and all, but they are fans for a reason. They shouldn’t be booking the shows. When they do, look at the shit that happens. Clyde, El Diablo, Akiyama, Duke Williams, and Miyagawa have all been double-booked. Of course, one of the matches has Miya’s handler, but it’s still Miyagawa being double-booked. The first four guys I mentioned were already double-booked at Red October. Shit, Clyde and Akiyama are fighting one another TWICE. Semore Smoke, Frisco, Teresa Tomas, Playaz Inc., Jung Loc, Benard Colt… where are these guys? Xavier Kannon is booked, but it’s for his handler and not him. Can’t people like this get a little love? Not when the “fans” are booking the show. You’re going to get the same people working and some of the best talent, the talent that needs more exposure than the main eventers, are left high and dry. I sure as hell hope “Total Selection Friday” isn’t repeated anytime soon. Never would be much better. To let the “fans” book a show less than a week after a huge PPV is absurd. The fans might be important, but they’re not that important. They’re not important enough to throw everything away for. This thing is a joke and I’d bet good money to say it comes back and blows up in PCW’s face. And if for some reason this stunt is repeated, please change the process to which matches are chosen. Only let people that actually have some significance choose what should be there. That way you don’t risk burning out your entire upper card in the span of a couple weeks. Then, and only then, will this “Total Selection Friday” nonsense even stand a chance at working. Truthfully Yours, Shooter
