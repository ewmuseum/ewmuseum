Brock's Punishment Official

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brock's Punishment Official Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Jan 2003 ---- In return for the severe beatdown that he issued on Cassius Deamont on this past Turmoil and Cassius' demand that action be taken against the Inno-F'n-Vator, Brock Newbludd has been placed under a strict 'probation' of sorts. He may not touch Cassius Deamont outside of the ring, for risk of suspension, by order of Xavier Kannon himself. With Brock unable to touch him outside the ring, it's probable that Kannon will not book Deamont into matches with Brock, keeping Newbludd away from his new ally. But, one must wonder, how long can Brock stand to watch Deamont's trick without taking action and how long can Cassius himself hide from the former RUSH World Champ?
