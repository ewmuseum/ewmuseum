Aymin's Career Over Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 May 2002 ---- Doctors at the 'Hinkleberry Hospital' have strongly advised Flaymin to end his career.... His porno career that is. After suffering repeated blows to the testicles courtesy of his EX-Valet Jenna, Flaymin's left testicle was ripped out of his 'nut sack' during his match at the Pay Per View. Doctors were working through the night attempting to re-insert the 'free' testicle but were doubtful that the procedure would be a success. Doctor Hector Bonston released the following statement: "There was blood everywhere, he has a hole in his nut sack the size of a golf ball. Actually, he's lucky he didn't lose both testicles. We're trying to re-insert his damaged testicle but eventually we might have to opt for a prosthetic one... or possibly no testicle at all. Nevertheless, we have repeatedly told him to end his porno career but he remains adamant that 'one ball is better than no balls'" When asked about how long Flaymin will be out of action, Dr. Bonston said that Flaymin won't be wrestling for a while- "The 45 stitches in his nut sack will keep him out of the ring for an extended period of time". PCW Management were unable to contact Flaymin as he was busy shoving small sphere-shaped objects into his nut sack to make up for the lost ball. Stay tuned for the very special 'Testicular Incision Operation' LIVE on PCW TV in the near future!!
