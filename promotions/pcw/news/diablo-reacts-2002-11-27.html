Diablo Reacts

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Diablo Reacts Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Nov 2002 ---- After two defeats at Alliances, PCW caught up with The Mexican Devil, El Diablo just minutes after he was eliminated from the Main Event. PFIK: So how do you feel Alliances went? El Diablo: Are you kidding? Alliances was a *expletive* mess. Alliances should be stricken from the record in my case. PFIK: So what are your thoughts on Alliances? El Diablo: Sunday was a horrible night, and it shall be avenged somehow. Diablo then proceeded to kick the wall for a few moments. El Diablo: I'll express my thoughts in detail come Turmoil and I will have a lot to say that may just shock everyone, but Alliances is *expletive* corrupt and should not count as a real event, that is my next aim, to get rid of this damn corrupted event. Alliances hates El Diablo. PFIK: So what now for El Diablo? El Diablo: Now, El Diablo prepares to walk into Texas, humble and tell all my fans what they need to know. PFIK: Any last words? El Diablo: That PCW Title will be mine some day, and I guarantee it. The Lord of Mexico then walked away in a lot of vent up anger that is just waiting to explode.
