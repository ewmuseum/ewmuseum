Wrestlers upset over Afwa

29 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has emerged that certain wrestlers in the PCW are unhappy about their latest signing, a 468lbs Savage from Samoa, known only as Afwa. "The guy is not only dangerous, but he is wreckless as well," said one PCW superstar, who chose not to be identified. "In this business, you have to trust the guy you are wrestling against to do his best to protect you from injury, and frankly, this guy can not be trusted to do that... AT ALL." Afwa a former PCW superstar has made his return, though it is rumoured that he has only returned due to having his contract torn up due to his killer instinct. When Afwa left the PCW he went to Mexico where he chose to pick on poor defenceless luchadores and in one wrestling match, due to him wishing to administer as much of his authority as possible and show why he is The Samoan King, Afwa totally destroyed the luchadore piledriving him two times onto the concrete floor and literally putting the luchadores life in danger. "It was terrible," said El Flojo, a wrestler in Mexico who witnessed the piledriving incident. "That guy don't belong in the ring." Other wrestlers in Mexico began to hear of Afwa's tyrannical actions and they did whatever they could to try and get Afwa forced out of all Mexican wrestling promotions. It was only though when he had a meeting with the president of the Mexican Wrestling Association that Afwa ended up taking his anger out on the said man, eventually causing the man so much damage that he had to be air lifted to the nearest hospital. After the incidents in Mexico, Afwa left the country in disgrace, but soon after he arrived on the Japanese wrestling circuit and once again the savage in Afwa came to the forefront of his very being. After taking part in a few Japanese Death Matches, Afwa took the matches to their actual meaning and nearly killed two Japanese wrestlers who were thrown into a handicap match with Afwa. Afwa ended up nearly decapitating the two Japanese supserstars and once again left them battered, bruised and with two broken necks. Afwa shows no remorse for his actions, he is a born prince of a Samoan King and uses and abuses all who he believes will stand in his way. Afwa cares for nobody and why on earth the PCW have re-signed this monster is anybody's guess. Rest assured, now that the Savage who has grown meaner then ever before has been unleashed upon the PCW, it's not only the wrestlers who should be upset, for nobody is safe, for the Beast has been Unleashed.
