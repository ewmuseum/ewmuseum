The Column With 'It!'

18 October 2001

The Column With 'It!' Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Oct 2001 ---- Hey-lo, PCW! This is Ed with his first (published) column. Due to the fact that no one else is writing any, I've decided that this would be a great chance to express my opinions in column format, and give the usual pre-PPV predictions. So, without further ado... THE HUNT FOR RED OCTOBER Well, each team gave it a good shot, but the tournament turned out to be as predictable as I thought. I expect Kremmen and Nelson to take the main event, mainly because the Phantom Booker likes to swerve both Ivan and Meanstreak at pay per views. Anyway, here's a few match-by-match predictions. Meanstreak & Ivan Stanislav vs Snow & Kid Wonder This one's more transparent than the Shoot King's urine-soaked bed sheets. KW and Snow gel really well as a team, but with Streak leading the charge, there's no doubt (in my mind) who will win. Kremmen & Tyler Nelson vs Jeremy Howard & Rent A Hero Even easier than the last one. Jeremy's trying hard, but Rent A Hero's simply dead weight. Kremmen and Tyler should take this one easily. PCW Continental Title Match Duke Williams vs Badge vs Boda vs Vampir Nosferatu Boda should win the strap this time around. On the other hand, there's been some pretty blatant pushing for Vampir (see the Newsire articles about grave digging, among others), so don't be too suprised to see him win. Extreme Title Match Ed vs Clyde vs Akiyama vs Miyagawa Clyde and Akiyama like to work their asses off, and Miya always puts out some high-quality stuff, but I think my multi-person RP will send me on to my fourth title reign. If not, look for Akiyama to win. USA Kid vs Edgecrusher vs Dan Hollywood vs Doug Troy vs Two Ton vs John Jupiter vs Darkstar Dan Hollywood should take this win. If not, DarkStar. Everyone else either lacks a workrate or really isn't that good, though I feel kinda bad for saying that. Streak & Ivan vs. Nelson & Kremmen Here's where the SWERVE~! comes in. Look for Meanstreak and Stanislav to dominate, and then get screwed out of the win by some external interference. Tyler Nelson and Kremmen Von Smackdown advance to the main event. Rising Star Title Match Timo Bolamba vs Corey Williams vs Unstoppable Eugene vs El Magnifco Busmuchacho de Mysterio TIMO~! Timo kicks sheer and utter ass, and there's no doubt in my mind that he'll win. If, by some freak accident, he loses, I imagine Unstoppable Eugene will win. ---> Off Topic Rant: The handler of Unstoppable Eugene needs to be both congratulated for his unique gimmick and punched in the face for using it. I have never seen a more offensive and disturbing character. Which, mind you, is a good thing. Big Daveylicious vs El Diablo vs Poppa Plump vs Juan Borro vs Matt Elia vs Ketsueki Karasu vs Syko vs Hank Juan's out for an injury, Matt's an Elia, and everyone else is nowhere to be seen. Since there really isn't any clear winner for this one, I'll pick the man, the myth, the legend...the HANK~! PCW Television Title Match The HIT vs Kramer Wildwing HIT's sort of disappeared, but then again, so has Kramer. This is another hard call. I'll pick the Hardcore Isotrap whatever to win. PCW Cruiserweight Title Mexi(c) vs Chet King vs Tim Walden Make the non-promo-cutter matches stop! Please! Due to lack of competition, I'll go with Mexi, because tequila rules. PCW Tag Team Title Match Da Westsidaz vs Big Alan Steele & Rent A Hero HI-KEEBA! Jeremy Howard vs. Rent A Hero vs. Snow vs. Kid Wonder Snow will win this one, due to the fact that everyone else will have to leave the ring to find Kleenex and get counted out. Meanstreak vs. Ivan Stanislav This will be a Lesser of Two Swerves match. I expect Streak, with his better workrate and overall quality, will end up getting the better swerve -- in other words, he'll lose. World Title Match Kremmen vs. Tyler Nelson Seems kind of anti-climactic to have all those stipulations in a match between two partners who really don't mind eachother. Expect MASSIVE nterference in this, and the usual end-PPV "suprise return(~!)" The Suprise I have no idea. Usually, the staffers at least give me a hint about what's going to happen, but not this time. Maybe the suprise will be that there IS no suprise. Who knows? OTHER THOUGHTS - Duke Williams isn't the world's greatest promo cutter, but his radio show rules all. - No one hates Akiyama, although he really wants us to. - Boda will win the World Title the day that the Phantom Booker is replaced by Jason McAllister. - Big Alan Steele will win the World Title the day he ditches Rent A Hero and goes into singles wrestling. - Pamela Paulshock is hotter than Mandy Moore. - Timo Bolamba's career is looking better and better. His multi-person promos are excellent, and he really seems to keep everyone in character. - If Alan Steele had made it to the finals and won, would he have had to fight himself in the main event? The two Tylenol PMs I took an hour ago are starting to kick in, so I'd better wrap this up. Thanks for reading the column, and remember...I've got "it", baby!
