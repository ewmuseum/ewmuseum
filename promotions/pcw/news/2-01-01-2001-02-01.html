PCW — News archive

2.01.01

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We are having some slight problems with out RP board, but nothing that will prohibit you from posting. Turmoil is up and in the books. Should be added to the archives later on. 64 man world title tourney to begin Friday!!! Second quarter of the first round draw is already posted. Next PPV is March Madness, March 4th, 2001. Please check the RP board and vote for your choice for our new banner design. If you are just checking into the site, we are still accepting applications. Sample roleplays should be longer than 50 lines to warrant consideration for PCW. If you are looking to improve your roleplaying or learn how to participate in an efed, you should join the Primetime Indy Circuit. Hired: Justin Sane Fired: OPF Injured: Johnny Raindance, Genetic Peferction, Johnny G Howard Porter and Polar Christoffersen, LeShawn Spade Resigned: Tiny, Ned and Ian Preston
