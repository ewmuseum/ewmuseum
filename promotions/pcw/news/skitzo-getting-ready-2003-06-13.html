Skitzo Getting Ready

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Skitzo Getting Ready Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Jun 2003 ---- PCW's resident psycho and promoter (yeah right) of PCW's official Soda, Gatorade issued a statement today regarding his Soda Contract Match to take place at Cloud 9. "Being that things have been really slow lately moreso than prior to my active state, I have taken it upon myself to ensure that Cloud 9 will see a resurgence in the promotion of Gatorade. Not only that but I will also ensure that Gatorade continues to be the premier Soda the fans want and love. An interesting thought has also recently come to mind, and so should I prevail at Cloud 9 in the Endorsement Match, I will make an announcement the next week at Turmoil regarding the future of Gatorade and my position as promoter of this fine refreshing alternative to everyday normal bland sodas. Thank you, and remember: Gatorade - PCW's Official Soda: Simply The Best, 'cos Skitzo said so!" More on this if and when it developments.
