Day of the Dark

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Day of the Dark Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Jul 2002 ---- Oxford, Mississippi- Since before Gold and Glory when Legend Inc. Tom Lexian won the PCW Continental Title, he and PCW President Duke Williams have had issues. But on Turmoil they must try to surpress those issues in order to team with Chris Chase to take on the team of Darkstar, Clyde and in his first sanctioned match back in PCW, Syko the Goblin... and all this with Lexian's Continental Title on the line if one of the Corporate Dark Ages members manages to pin him. Was The Chef just doing a confusing swerve last Fusion or had he really abandoned Islam for good? Well he teams with the Refugees and Alexis Parshin against Politically Incorrect and Akiyama in a four on three tag match on Turmoil, so that should give us some indication. Natalia Miyagawa is at Turmoil for the first time in a couple of months, and within ten seconds of entering the building she and Alexander are confronted by one of his opponent's tonight, Charlie Loc. What did Loc say and how did the match end up? All this and Rush Mason makes his first title defense, a new Rising Star Champion is crowned in his first PCW Match and Heaven Rayne is brutally assaulted.
