Post-Supremacy Personnel Report

26 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-Supremacy Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 May 2003 ---- Direct from the desk of the three VP's came this notice today, regarding hirings, firings, and more: Signings -Kael Law, as earlier noted, was signed before Fusion and debuted there. -Menolly Xenos, a fairly unknown female who impressed PCW scouts with her Ju-Jitsu fighting, has been signed and will debut this week on PCW TV. -Sergio Bullent, a former bullfighter, was signed to a PCW contract as well, after convincing PCW officials that his small size would not hold him back. Departures -Jade, who fought in the Prankster Potluck Challenge at the Pay Per View, has asked for and been granted release. PCW wishes her the best of luck. -Mark Makonie also asked for release after months of work in PCW. Again, fair fortune is wished upon him as well. -Cozette du Pres, after doctors revealed it was unlikely she would ever compete again in a wrestling ring due to past injuries, was given release -Chet King, longtime PCW loyalist, was finally let go earlier today. After repeated shots to redeem himself, he was lackadaisical and nonchalant about working, so he was said good riddance to and given release. -Semore Smoke, another old PCW face, left again yesterday, after claiming that he needed to spend quality time with his bong. We hope he doesn't light his face on fire. -The Overweighter, who has left PCW officials severely unimpressed over the past weeks, was told to report to PWA yesterday in a demotion that shocked [name withheld]. He was said to have been entirely dismissing PCW and for that, he has been given the ultimatum of PWA or release. Injury Report -Totally Tubular Tommy sustained a strained neck yesterday but was said to be able for wrestling action by Wednesday, if not at 100%. -Shane Wethers has a pulled muscle in his right leg that has severely limited his ring work. He will be day-to-day, but probably will be given a few weeks off to cope with the injury.
