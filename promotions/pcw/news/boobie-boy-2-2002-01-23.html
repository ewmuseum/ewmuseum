Boobie Boy #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Issue #2 Featuring: Boda I hope everyone enjoyed my first column if not then…. You’re going to get more crap shoved down your throats. I thank everyone for the nice things they said about my first column…(nobody has said anything yet) This column is featuring Chris Sloboda known as Boda to the rest of us. Boda has been one of the best wrestlers in PCW and has climbed the ladder to success only has yet to claim the title that proves that. Despite all that Boda has been kind enough to sit down with me at his Florida house and discuss 20 questions ranging from his injury to his greatest moments…but enough BS…on to Boda. 1) What is life like on the injured reserve? Boda: Pretty good. The time spent with the family, is, well, much needed. I can't wait to get back in the ring though. 2) Since being able to only watch PCW from afar does anybody impress you on TV? Disappointing you? Pissing you off? Boda: I'm impressed by Zurick, Xavier Kannon, of course The Wild Deuces, and..... Duke Williams is doing a hell of a job as a commentator. Disappointing.... well... Dustin Downey did disappoint me... till we sent him packing. As far as pissing me off.... well..... PCW will soon find out exactly who Boda's pissed at... 3) What was Valley Central High School like back when you went to it? Boda: Valley Central...... The Vikings.... man, back in day, it was a great place to be..... The football teams couldn't win more than 2-3 games a season. Hell, my senior year, we won the Homecoming game by forfeit. Whashingtonville had to forfeit their entire season due to academic ineligibility of over half the team. Our school wrestling team was the best not only in Orange County, but also in the state. The academics were tough, some of the teachers,. Well, at my time, should of retired ten-twenty years earlier... but, that's another story. All in all, VC was a great school, and, I plan on visiting this year. I did have the honor of playing ball with Matt Morris, the now starting pitcher for the St. Louis Cardinals. 4) What are your thoughts/ opinions on the PWA? Boda: The PWA is a great stepping stone for the careers of young, inexperienced e-wrestlers. I wish I'd been in PWA when I started, but got thrown right into PCW, because, well, TPB didn't create PWA then. A lot of talent has emerged from PWA, and today, there is plenty of untapped potential there. 5) What is your greatest achievement in PCW? Boda: To date.... I'd say wining the Continental Title. Seeing as I've fallen short a few times to win the World Belt. I also think, the formation of The Wild Deuces has to be right there, at the top. 6) Any plans for a post-PCW time? This injury probably has you thinking about how fragile you can be. Boda: After PCW? I have thought of opening my own fed. Or, maybe go into coaching... I hear the Bulls are gonna need a coach after this season. As far as the injury goes.... well, not really, it was a bad break....[literally].... but, in this business, you see injuries all the time. Had it not been for the need to have it surgically repaired, I'd have not missed anytime. 7) Describe your perfect woman…is that description anywhere near your wife? Boda: Stacy Sloboda! Nuff said! Boobie Boy: A description please… Boda: 5'1" 110 pounds, long, brown hair... beautiful brown eyes.......nice figure, loves her man, no matter what he does to make it in PCW [ala bounce czeching her when I was in Red Army], lips that can suck a...... well, that's enough description. 8) What was the biggest regret of your career? Boda: To be honest... I haven't regretted anything I've done, willingly 9) What is your favorite food? Boda: Chinese and most definitely sushi. 10) Many wrestlers (not Nelson) have superstitions. What are your pre-match superstitions? Boda: I like to do some pre match push-ups..... back in the locker room of course. I also like to call and talk to my two girls on then phone... it kinda gives me piece. Just, sitting, alone in my locker room, especially before a big match... that's my biggest pre-match ritual. Those last few moments of complete, utter silence, gets me in the zone to do what I have to do.... be who I have to be. 11) Who are you favorite people backstage and off camera? Do you travel with anyone on the road? Boda: Backstage...... Vampire, Kid Wonder, Jeremy Howard, and yes, even Ivan are some of my favorites off camera. Some of the newbies, from NTICW are okay too... Zurick, Xavier Kannon for example… I tend to travel alone, but when I do travel with others, it's usually with whomever my tag partner at the time is, or as of late... Wild Deuces itself.... we try to stay together. 12) Speaking of Kid Wonder…what do you think of his recent demotion at the hands of Tyler Nelson? Boda: Nelson's an asshole... and you can quote that verbatim. Wonder is one of the greatest wrestlers in PCW, and just because he won't stroke Nelson's overly large ego, he gets sent to PWA. Bullshit. And, if I know Kid as well as I do.... Nelson's ass will soon be handed to him. 13) What was your most embarrassing moment? Hide no details! Boda: Losing to Shaq… 14) What do you do in your free time at home, since you have a lot of it these days. Boda: Spend it with the kids, play PS2. I also run a class in PWA might I add. Some of my favorite PS2 games are Madden 2002, Metal Gear Solid 2, Grand Theft Auto 3, and Smackdown: Just Bring It. Boobie Boy: On a side note have you played Final Fantasy 10? Best game I have ever played. Boda: Not yet, I will get it soon enough. 15) Throughout PCW history we have always seen Boda in a stable of some sort, either with the Media, The Red Army, and the Wild Deuces. Boda: Well, Media, and The Red Army, were used to advance my career.... I was asked to join those stables. The Deuces, on the other hand... well, I wanted to get the former PCW2 stars back together, and create a stable, where, we can actually showcase our talent. I mean, come on.... Walden was jobbing to Violator before he became a Deuce. Now, he's held the TV title. It's not a numbers thing... it's a respect thing more than anything. 16) If you were on a deserted island what one thing would you bring and why? Boda: Hmm.... tough one. A survival Knife…it’s got all I need to survive. 17) Who do you believe will be the first to be able to dethrone Jeremy Howard? Boda: ME 18) When should be expecting you back then? Boda: In action... as soon as I get booked... the cast came off this morning, and I am cleared to wrestle. Boobie Boy: Really? That’s wonderful to here! Boda: And, I think I might have a special interest in the Ivan/Jeremy main event on Turmoil. 19) Any suggestions for my column? Boda: Nope, the column is fine thus far… 20) If you had to describe me in your own words what would they be? Boda: Let's see.... Talented lacks motivation and direction.... Could be a threat in any fed, if you'd stay focused. And finally expect BIGGER and BETTER things from Boda and the Deuces in 2002. Boobie Boy would like to thank Boda for his time and effort in making this issue better than the first one. Look for our next issue to feature Tim Walden or someone else who is better than Walden…. (Hoping for TPB)! Feel free to contact me at [detail withheld] on Aim to set up an interview. Until next time, life is like a pair of tits, hard and full of curves.
