Boobie Boy #9 Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Feb 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Issue #9 Featuring Mexi Ok by now my schedule is up so everyone who wanted an interview is booked. Sorry to those who have not yet been booked by April is a good month too. Now before I go on schedule I am going to do my last unexpected interview with Mexi. I know this breaks the rules I just made but sometimes fed heads have the greater leverage. And besides I need to figure out how to get my banner to stay up. So scroll down and enjoy! 1) Currently you are not hired by PCW, and within the course of the first show of NTICW lost the Owner ship to your former lover Mona. What are you comments on that situation and are you at liberty to say if you are the commish or not? Mexi: I have agreed not to comment on the outcome of that match on Mayhem. However it's safe to say that Mona like Nelson are scum that will get there’s in the end 2) Mona seems to continue to gain the upper hand with you and make you miserable. What are your plans of stopping her? Mexi: Well I threw the bitch off a twenty foot ledge and people hated me for it. I'm just going to become as successful as possible on my own and prove to the world that Mona is just an evil demonic skank that is just in it for her self and the money. 3) Before your firing you had an outstanding feud with Chet King, the current continental champion. What do you have to say about that feud? Mexi: I'm glad you asked that question Boobie Boy. I have the utmost respect for Chet as was evident by the end of the feud. It was a rollercoaster ride but look where it took us. Cruiserweight Champions a Continental Title feud and had Tyler not crapped his pants and fired me all the way up to World Title contenders over the period of one feud. I don't think anyone would argue that it was the best thing for my career. 4) Everyone knows about your past and being brought up in Mexico…I am sure at one time you were indeed in love with Mona. How did you guys meet, was it love at first sight? And what do you think drove her to a break up? Mexi: Well yes I did love her with all my heart when I met her. As for just how I met her well I was shipped around to shelter after shelter when one day I slipped and fell, opening a nasty gash on the side of my face. With no where to go I just got up and did the one thing that came to mind, I ran. I just kept running and screaming as blood gushed from the gash until I couldn’t run any further and as luck would have it I stopped running at the right time. For a small girl no more then three happened to be standing outside the house I collapsed in front of. The little girl was the daughter of Yoshihiro Asai, who would later be known to the world simply as Mona. Mona helped me into the house where her mother helped clean and dress the wound although it would leave the nasty scar on my face it was the first time we met and for me it was the start of something truly special. 5) Being the former owner of NTICW you know most of the talent there. Who are some of the stand out players that you believe will be in PCW/OSW and climbing the ladder? Mexi: With out jeopardizing the surprise of Showtime let me just say that Charlie Loc impressed me, even under the circumstances. 6) On the road, all of the wrestlers have to do a lot of traveling. Who do you usually travel with? Mexi: Well of course when I was still employed in PCW it was the Deuces, especially Boda, as we would figure out where the Deuces would strike next. Now however, I'm on my own. 7) A lot of obstacles have stood in your way and changed the course of your life for you to get where you are today. What if you could go back and turn down a different road and change something. What would you change and what do you think would be the outcome? Mexi: Not a thing, you can't live life with regrets. I don't like all the things that have happened in my life but I guarantee that every time something bad happened it did provide a good result in the end. 8) Well, it could be said that you are PTC’s most eligible bachelor, are ya looking out for any ladies yet or just playin low until that lady comes for you? Mexi: I’m focused on my career right now. Mona got in the way of that before and until I reach the level of success I desire to be at, I’m not looking. 9) What has been your greatest career moment? Mexi: It’s yet to come. But it will be when I raise my hand with the PCW World Title held high. 10) What was the worst moment of your career? Mexi: Tuesday Night was not a night I care to remember. 11) Any ideas for getting revenge on Nelson? What about an anti-Nelson Stable? I would be sure to be the official spokesperson! Mexi: As I told Boda, I have things under control for revenge on Nelson. Mona may have taken away my leverage point to get back at him but don’t think that I haven’t already made other arrangements to hit him where it hurts. 12) Who’s column is better, mine or Robbie Decker's? Give your reasons? Mexi: Decker said Chet and me sucked so you win by default. 13) What do you think of Jane Reagan and sending PCW talent to OSW? Where you ever approached about going? Would you consider going? Mexi: I can’t blame her for doing it as Nelson really left her no choice. I do however have to question PCW talent leaving the fed that made them what they were. As for if I would consider going? Only if all other options had run out. I may have been fired from PCW but I’m still loyal to them. 14) If you were stranded on a desert island and could bring one thing (not a boat) what would it be? Mexi: TEQUILA~! No actually I would bring a city with me, a city is one thing. 15) Before I began writing my column, you yourself had a column you wrote. Do you plan on continuing it now that you may have more free time? Mexi: HA, free time what is that? I’ll write one here and there just to give an honest opinion of how I feel PCW is going. 16) What are some of your favorites? (Music, food, things to do, sports teams, ETC) Mexi: My favorite thing to do is Walden’s mom. *Nods* 17) Are you a tits or ass kinda guy? Mexi: Gotta be the tits, although there’s more to a woman then just tits and ass and I tend to like that more;) 18) Snow is the current world champion, who do you think will be the person to shift his moment him and take away the title that he currently holds? Mexi: One word, Boda. 19) Do you think you will be rehired by PCW eventually? Do you think you would have to take drastic measures to get rehired for say Kid Wonder? Mexi: I think PCW will be given no other choice but to rehire me. I was and still am the hottest thing in PCW and their future money man and we all know Nelson loves money. 20) And finally, word association! Mona: SUCKS~! Chet King: ROCKS~! NTICW: FUTURE~! Canada: Why the Hell would you say Canada??? The locker room: Smelly. And finally, do you have anything to comment in, mention, allude to or finish up to end this interview? Mexi: Never fear PCW, I will be back… And WHY? …Because… …Mexi… …Can… Well, that’s where I finished but instead of being able to thank Zurick a flash of light over took me and I woke up in my car outside then mansion. Being very curious and lacking my notes and recorded tapes I began to leave to go up to door again, but suddenly the tapes appeared next to me. Disturbed I left the area, but anyway…A big thank you to Zurick for sitting down with me, even though according to him he has all the time in an eternity. Next week should be featuring the NEW PCW President The Malice Man Duke Williams! And lastly feel free to contact me on the PTC Boards, by email, or on AIM under [detail withheld]. Setup an interview and and you might find yourself the latest victim of the 20 Questions. So till next week this is everyones favorite pervert signing off! That wraps up another column with myself Boobie boy. I would like to thank The White Mexican for giving the time and effort to answer my 20 questions. I once again welcome all to contact me on AIM or email to setup and appointment with me. [detail withheld] and [detail withheld]. I will leave on this note…I GOT A JOB AGAIN…well kinda! PEACE!
