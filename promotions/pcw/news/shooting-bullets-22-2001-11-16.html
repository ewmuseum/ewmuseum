Shooting Bullets #22 Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Nov 2001 ---- All expressions and opinions in this article are solely those of The Shoot King, a ham sandwich, and actor/comedian Nipsy Russell. Anything written can not be used without the consent of The Shoot King. Any duplication of this article will result in your ass on a platter. All hate mail and such can be sent to [detail withheld] where it can and will be used in a future article. (This bona fide PCW shoot is brought to you by the fine people at Fancast. Fancast: Proving that there are people better than Duke Williams. Jim Rome. He's so good, people steal from the people who steal from him. And by the BCPD. If you smell bacon and grease, it must be the Baltimore County Police.) I feel the need to say I've got an apology to make. Yes, an apology. You heard me right. We all make mistakes. I, sadly, have made one. And, no, I'm not talking about giving Jon Kano access to a computer. But I'll get to that later in this article. Also, before I get to the meat of the column, which is anything below my opening rant, duh, I wanna add that it's nice to hear from Chef again. He has always been one of my favorite PCW stars. It's a shame he left when he did or else we wouldn't of had the Meanstreak shit that we had (most likely, not a guarentee). No, I didn't jump on any bandwagon. I had been marking out for Chef before 80% of the roster came to PCW. I felt he could've been that stable champ that we needed in PCW. But I have no problems with Ivan being champ. And if anyone does, name me one person who deserves it more than he does. Now that I've gotten that out of the way, I can get to what's important. The Hound Congratulations! You get to have your own section in my column even if it's for one time. I know you like to ask questions, but allow me to ask you one. Why do you find the need to waste valuable space on my page? First, you start by attempting to take a shot at me. And I say attempt because that's all it was. Hound, do you know who Robbie Decker is? He was just like you. PCW's #2 columnist. Thought it'd be fun to try and poke at me in his little columns. Of course, it sucked, but was still better than what you tried. Do you know where Robbie is now? Come on, do ya? Neither do I. Probably off knob-bobbing for food in some third-World country. And you're following in his path. Great job! Also, as I've pointed out in past columns, this is no place for silly PPV predictions. The only purpose they serve is to make your column look longer as if you've got something important to say when you really don't. But that stuff isn't what's bothering me. What really got to me was how you totally contradicted yourself. "I had hopes for Meanstreak, but like the rest of the “old school” wrestlers, he failed." All the "old school" wrestlers fail yet most of your PPV picks have "old school" wrestlers winning. Chet King's old school. Blood Brothers are old school. Dan Hollywood is old school. Ed is old school. Kremmen's old school. Tyler Nelson's old school. And both Boda and Kid Wonder, both of which you say will do well in their respective matches (not counting the main event), are as old school as you get in PCW. So Hound, before you go throwing together another pointless column filled with drivel and what not, think about what you're actually gonna say before you say it. Damn. All that for a 2nd stringer. *sigh* Alliances No, I'm not gonna go into any detailed predictions here. Like I said, this isn't the place for them. Actually, I lied, I will make one prediction. Unlike what The Hound thinks, there will be an Extreme champion at the end of the night. As for the PPV in itself, it has a definite MotY candidate with the main event. But besides that, is anything really jumping out at me? The main event might be good. The soda endorsement match is always good. But since it has gotten away from its traditional ldder match style, will it remain good? But besides that, absolutely nothing sticks out at me telling me to watch. Of course, I'll see it because I have to. But if it's not a grade-A PPV, I won't be surprised. Diamond in the Rough I'm a judge for this, so of course I've been paying attention. Now, without going into names, I'm just gonna say that some of these guys are just brutal. They wouldn't even make it in ECW. But there is more good than there is bad. And if that good is let into PCW, there are gonna be some PCW guys out of a job. I Hear Things I heard from this thing called logic that PCW could be gone by the end of the year. How? Like I'd say. All I'll say is for all of you that bash Ron, you'll find out first hand that he deserves a lot more credit than you're giving him. PCW wouldn't survive without him. He makes PCW tick. And if you haven't seen that by now, you will. Akiyama I said I was gonna apologize, and here it is. Akiyama, I'm sorry for all the abuse I've given you lately. After carefully thinking, it was wrong for me to bash you without knowing who you are or what you've done. And for this, I'm sorry. I will make it up to you by marking out for you until I find a permanent replacement for the douche Juas Steele, the long-gone Putter Mayhem, and the NTICW's Jacob Yoder. Why the sudden change of heart? Only one man can say that they ended Guy Leslie's miserable career. Look in the mirror, Akiyama, and you will see that man. You did PCW a great thing by letting Bethany hit him with that fireball. And for that, I shall never bash you again. Well, at least until you do something dumb. Jon Kano Bash of the Moment I don't even think I can add anything. Being insulted by a 2nd string columnist is pretty bad. But, hey, I will give ya credit on one thing. At least you managed to get on the Alliances card. Yeah, remember Red October. EVERYONE was on the card except for one person. Who was that one person? Jon Kano. But as Hound said, you're only there because Frisco is being punished. And I can't think of any worse punishment than teaming with you. Well, jobbing to you would be worse, but nobody is that cold-hearted. Quote of the Week "My point is that I survived using the same bathroom with Bertha for over nine years, and I think I can survive any harmful gases that may come my way." -The Chef He does have a point. Eternally Cool Quotes "Do you have any idea how hard it is to get a title off a guy from Calgary? Ask Vince McMahon." -Lance Storm "If dynamite were dangerous, do you think they'd sell it to an idiot like me?" -Al Bundy "Scribble.....Scribble.....Delete" -Scribble Moment of the Week When the game ended on Monday night. Yes, football reigns supreme again in this spot. What was so good about it? The referees tried desperately to screw the Ravens over at the end of the game, but Baltimore STILL won. HA! Take that! Cleveland is this week. So they beat us one time. ONE TIME! We've beaten them all the other times, and most of them by a whole lot. Ravens get revenge and BLOW OUT the Browns. WHOOOOO! Eternally Cool Moment Inside the ring, Hix immediately goes after Frisco. Hix swings wildly at Frisco, hitting him with a bunch of rights and lefts, but Hix's overall weakness does nothing to hurt the multi-time PCW title holder. Then, with just his pinky, Frisco nudges Hix and knoks him down and out. Dave: FINGER POKE OF DEATH! FINGER POKE OF DEATH! Jeff: Yeah! We haven't seen that one since Hogan vs. Nash! "While the City Sleeps" by MC 900 Ft. Jesus hits as the crowd is still going nuts from the FINGER POKE OF DEATH~! As Trashcan Man makes his way out, Frisco covers Hix inside the ring. Referee Buzz Meacham counts, 1......2.......3! STEWART HIX HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Hix is tossed from the ring by Frisco as TCM gets to ringside. TCM grabs Hix and hits him with the PYRO DRIVER ON THE CONCRETE compressing Hix's spine and putting a small crack in the concrete. TCM goes into the ring as medics attend to Hix who is lying in a pool of his own blood. -Dream Card...June 29, 2001 There. I did everything I said I'd do. I said I'd make an apology and did just that. I said I'd do another column before Alliances and I did. Now that you're all done reading in awe, especially you Hound, go out and try to make something of yourselves. LATER!
