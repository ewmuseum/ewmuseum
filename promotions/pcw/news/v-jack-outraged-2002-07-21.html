V-Jack Outraged

21 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
V-Jack Outraged Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Jul 2002 ---- Following the release of the preliminary lineup for Tuesday Turmoil on July 23rd, PCW competitor Violence Jack reportedly unleashed a massive tirade in the backstage area. VJ was not upset at his own matchup, a hardcore match with THE Multiple Man, but supposedly irate as he saw the 'other' hardcore match listed on the card: a match for the Extreme title's #1 contendership featuring Thug RoC and Kramer Wild Wing. Violence Jack, named the current #1 contender following his victory over Flaymin Aymin a few weeks ago, was harsh and candid about his feelings on, as he worded it, "being f*cked over by cowardly executives". "It's a crime, a f*ckin' travesty," spat the bitter Boston native when reached for comment. "I come into this cesspool of wannabe extreme wrestlers to finally represent all that the division is meant to be. Brutality, bloodshed, and violence, with no compromises and no apologies needed. Instead what do I get?! I get thrust into a barrage of sorry-ass gimmick matches that mock all I have ever worked for, and have to watch as flavor-of-the-week pretty boys like Wild Wing and stuffed-shirt corporate tools like Reginald f*ckin' Lovecraft get jammed down everyone's throats. Just another case of those conservative front office puppets tryin' to hold me back. So until this bullsh*t is resolved and I get repaid for what I rightfully earned in the first place, I'm servin' notice that all who consider themselves 'hardcore' better grow a third eye in the back of their head. I didn't earn the moniker 'God of Extreme' by fighting in trailers and airports with an occasional stiff chair shot thrown, and PCW is about to learn that the hard f*ckin' way! Whether it means takin' out the rejects of this company, crushing that punk-ass Mason or having to take out f*ckin' Steele himself, I WILL get what I deserve!" At that point Jack stomped off, still noticeably furious as he knocked over a water cooler and hurled objects off tables lining the hallway.
