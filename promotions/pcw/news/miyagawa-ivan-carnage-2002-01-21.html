Miyagawa + Ivan = Carnage

21 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Miyagawa + Ivan = Carnage Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Jan 2002 ---- Bogota, Columbia- The first Sabotage of the Southern Hemisphere kicked off with a bang as the Ivan Stanislav-Isawa Miyagawa fued heated up to boiling point with Natalia Miyagawa getting involved. The pair are due to meet in the main event as a warm up to a last man standing national pride match at the PPV, but will anything prevent one from killing the other before then? And will Jeff Marx ever forgive Miyagawa for the Steven Smith's pants incident? Also on Sabotage, Brandi defended the Extreme Title once again, this time to one of her PPV advisarys, Corey Williams. Jeremy Howard and Snow were forced to work together against Vampir Nosferatu and Akiyama, but who's alliance will falter quicker? And Stewart Hix was refused admission by several midgets. In other words, Tyler Nelson had a very good day... or did Kid Wonder stop that too?
