Stryker Done?

18 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stryker Done? Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Jun 2003 ---- Is Adam Stryker done? Well...no but many of you may be wondering why he has been MIA for the past two weeks or so. It was made public today that Adam was admitted, against his will, to an undisclosed rehab clinic in Los Angeles. He is being treated for a dependancy on pain killers. Jaslene Myst, his manager, made a public statement saying that, "while Adam goes through this tough time in his professional life we ask that all of his fans pray for a quick recovery so he can get back to doing what he loves most, entertaining the fans." When asked about how long he would be out, Jaslene had this to say, "At most another eight to ten weeks." She declined to comment on how the treatment was going so far. So with this news it seems like the earliest we could expect Adam Stryker back in anykind of ring would be late August.
