Green Facing Investigation

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Green Facing Investigation Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Feb 2003 ---- Skylar Green was able to make his scheduled Turmoil appearance this past week, defeating Adam Stryker to advance to the March Madness main event, a 20-wrestler battle royale for the PCW World Title. According to his own words: "I've been released pending an investigation into the reported tax evasion. Trumped up charges, shouldn't hold water. The problem is, because of this, Miami Police have decided to do their own separate investigation about insurance fraud. They assume that since I'm working for free in PCW, I need to get money from somewhere. What's worse, they've got child endangerment charges against me, as well. Yeah, I've got kids. Three girls: 13, 7, and 3. They're in state custody right now. Now, until this all blows over, I'm not allowed to spend more than $100 a day. Subtract $70 right off the top for a hotel because I HAVE NO HOME and you can see the problem this puts me in, between gas and food and such." Surely this is a troubling time for Skylar Green and the PCW Press Office wishes him good fortune in the getting past this.
