Skitzo's Soda Success

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Skitzo's Soda Success Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Nov 2002 ---- It was at Alliances 2K2 this past Sunday that Mr. Psycho, Skitzo won the PCW Soda Endorsement Alamo Match, snatching the contract from Dark Gimmick at the last second. Immediately following the broadcast, Skitzo was seen dousing himself in the refreshing soft drink and celebrating with himself, albeit he was eliminated a record 12 times from the Alliances Main Event. The following day reports from PCW's headquarters reported fifty crates of Gatorade had been shipped to Africa as a good will gesture from Primetime Championship Wrestling and Skitzo himself. The Mental One was approached on his travels and questioned as to his new endorsement contract... Skitzo: Well I brought my A-Game and came out a winner, now the World can feast its eyes as Gatorade, the one TRUE Official Drink of PCW, solves the world's problems, from quenching thirst in third world countries, to being thrown at homeless people on the street to even...........actually being consumed by the populous. Skitzo: WHATCHA GONNA DO BROTHER?!?!?! WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN SKITZOPHRENIA RUNS WILD ON YOU???!?!?! Ya know sumptin' brutha! The Mental One says his obscenities, eats his pussy and drinks his Gatorade for one reason and one reason only and that is to be THE #1 role model for all the little Skitzophreniacs out there watchin' me whoop booty week in and week out! The reason I won that Alamo Match little Skitzters was because of the fact I am the man, and to beat the man you gotta smack him upside the fucking face with a STEEL CHAIR?!!!?!? Skitzo: You grabbed a piece of paper from a skeleton...you're hardly a God... Skitzo: IT DOESN'T MATTER WHAT I DID, all that matters is Gatorade is the official drink of PCW and there ain't a damn thing anyone can do about it......except drink up the Gatory goodness!!! Skitzo: Ug! Ug! Ug! Ug! My foes are all a-fraid, cos I drinks m'Gatorade, I'm Skitzo the Mental One...TOOT! TOOT! Skitzo: Be warned...the Fourth Reich is upon us...........ZIEGHEIL GATORADE!!!!!!!!! With that, Skitzo left singing to himself, while PCW looks forward to the world of Gatorade descending upon it... This News Bulletin brought to you by GATORADE! The Offiskal Drink of PCW!
