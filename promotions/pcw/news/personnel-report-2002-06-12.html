Personnel Report

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Jun 2002 ---- CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson released a memo today with some personnel moves and information on the heels of Turmoil. -Goeren has suffered a serious, and possibly career threatening, knee injury. The knee was too swollen to have an immediate diagnosis, but the doctors all concurred that it "didn't look good". We will keep you posted on the status of Goeren as more information becomes available. -Anna Keyson was released immediately following Turmoil for what the CEO termed "a complete and utter disrespect for PCW, it's wrestlers, and staff. Her dedication to the PCW organization was less than adequate and totally unacceptable". -Also finding himself out of a job after Turmoil was former Rising Star Champion Kid Krazie. "We haven't been pleased with Krazie's work ethic recently and to tell you the truth, it's been nonexistent," stated the CEO. "We aren't in the business of keeping dead weight around here anymore." -The memo went on to state that the CEO is severely displeased with the work of Hiro Saotome and that he could find himself out of a job if his current rate of performance continues. We will bring you any other personnel news as it becomes available.
