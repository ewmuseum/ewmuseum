Masked German to Reveal Himself at Turmoil? Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Jun 2003 ---- This weeks Tuesday Turmoil will be coming to you live, June 24th, from Quito, Ecuador, and besides for being the much anticipated event where the roster booked the card, there are, supposedly, some other interesting happenings to look forward to as well. In the gossip and rumors section-meaing mainly, the Juice Bar on PCW's Airship-the biggest piece of news floating around is the assumption that on this week's turmoil, The Masked German-an assailant of PCW's resident redneck, Abner,-will indeed unveil himself to the world. "I've bet twenty chips on the bastard" a local gambler said, refering to a pool held in the ship's casino, "and by-Jesus, he's better pull through." He, along with many others, are counting on this, placing their chips at stake for the event. However, the real question is, how will the chips play out for the Masked German himself, or for Abner?
