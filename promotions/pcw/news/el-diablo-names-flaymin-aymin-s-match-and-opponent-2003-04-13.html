El Diablo Names Flaymin Aymin's Match and Opponent

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
El Diablo Names Flaymin Aymin's Match and Opponent Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Apr 2003 ---- Spring Fling is fast approaching and now that El Diablo has beaten Tim Walden to gain the right to name Flaymin Aymin's match and opponent at Spring Fling, Diablo has spoken out about what the match will be and whom he will be against. El Diablo: I knew I'd win, I knew it. Diablo speaks calmly. El Diablo: Now I get to continue ruining Aymin's life just the way I want to. You see Flaymin, everyone thinks I'll name myself in a match that you can't win, and I'll be honest that thought did cross my mind but no, it's not how I do things. Everyone thinks I'm that crazy and insane to do it, yes I am a bit crazy but Aymin made me that way but I will not name some ridiculously biased match, oh no. The Mexican Devil then smirks. El Diablo: But I will name a PCW first, and Aymin you will get to be a part of this first, only it will be your last, you see Aymin you will be competing in a "Flaming Tables" Match. Diablo licks his lips and smiles an insane smile. El Diablo: The rules of the match are like this, the first person to be put through a fire-lit table loses, and the rest of the rules are that there are no rules, anything goes; I'm sure you'll enjoy that "Mr Extreme." The Mexican Devil runs his right hand through his dyed red hair, as Diablo looks puzzled. El Diablo: But there is one small, minor problem that bothers me Aymin, that little friend of yours, Enigma. I know that in an anything goes type of match he will interfere to try and help you win, like the good little friend he is. So Aymin I had to come up with a contingency plan, and you know what Aymin a great plan hit me. Imagine it, Sunday 20th April Spring Fling in a PCW first, Flaming Tables Match, Flaymin Aymin vs Enigma! Diablo smiles a sick smile. El Diablo: Then we'll see if you remain friends after that. Trust me Aymin, I have your number and I cannot wait to see my creation unfold. Good luck. Diablo then walked away. So now that the match is named, just what will happen at Spring Fling between El Diablo and Flaymin Aymin? And how will the friendly relationship between Enigma and Flaymin survive such a brutal match? Tune in to Spring Fling to find out.
