Whereabouts of Violence Jack? Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 Sep 2002 ---- During a news conference earlier in the day, one reporter probed PCW spokesman the Pimply Faced Intern Kid as to the status of ex-PCW Extreme champion, Violence Jack. Jack hasn’t been seen on PCW television since the tag team match-up pitting he and Flaymin Aymin against Clyde and Charlie ‘Jung’ Loc, the first match following his loss of the title to Aymin. “Eh, that loon huh?” mumbled Pimply Faced Intern Kid at the question. “Well as some of you know, Violence Jack essentially snapped at Third Dawning by jamming a razorwire crown onto the skull of Aymin, who he had tied in the ropes, then attempted to destroy Aymin once and for all before our crack security team subdued him during a promotional segment.” At this point another reporter pointed out that 2/3 of that ‘crack’ security team was laid to waste off-camera by the rampaging Jack before he was finally brought down with the use of tazers and then handcuffed. Pimply Faced Intern Kid scoffed and pretended he didn’t hear the statement. “After losing his belt to Aymin a couple nights later, Jack went on a tirade in the backstage area and cost the company a good deal of money in damages. And after the events of the tag team match only a week later, VJ was roaming the backstage area in some whacked-out trance and mumbling in some weird language no one could make a damn syllable out of. We figured the poor boy had lost it and sent him to the company psychiatrist for an evaluation. After some rather bizarre findings they decided to throw the poor maniac in an asylum for a couple days to ‘cool off’.” PFIK was then asked about Jack’s progress during the stay in the asylum. Pimply Faced Intern Kid: “Well those couple days turned into a few weeks, and now we think it’s finally safe to let him out again among the fairly-stable PCW roster. We’ve let that psychopath loose on the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament in the meantime as a reward for ‘good behavior’ in hopes he’ll take out some of his excess rage on the bottom-feeders….er, others from around the fine PTC and WT organizations, and now we think he’s ready to return to full-time competition in PCW. Expect to see Violence Jack on television again starting very soon, though I can’t guarantee he’ll be exactly the same.” At this point, PFIK requested the questions return to people who are ‘not jobbers’ and the matter was closed. V-Jack’s return date is set for this Tuesday Turmoil in one-on-one competition against Eddie Mandrake.
