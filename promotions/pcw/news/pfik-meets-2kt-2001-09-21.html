PFIK Meets: 2KT

21 September 2001

PFIK Meets: 2KT Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Sep 2001 ---- Today on PFIK meets, we're gonna have tonnes of fun and a couple belly fulls of laughs. I have been advised by my doctor to not do any more jokes than that in my intros. Apparently people might beat me up even more often than I did. Anyway, people have asked me recently why I'm still an intern now that the academic year has started again. Ok, so they havn't but I'm gonna tell you anyway; PCW has offered me a full time job... as an intern. Since I was only doing Commercial Maintenence at college, I figured I would jack that in and join PCW. Ok, so they don't pay me, but where else would I get free subway sandwiches and free tickets for my girlfriend to the events. If I get a girlfriend that is. Well back to today's show, we are meeting the man of the hour, you can't dust him in flour, Two Ton. He's known to have the biggest personality in PCW ever... but the biggest heart too? I met up with Two Ton at the PCW Offices... on the ground floor after the lift gave way when he got inside. PFIK: Starting off; what do you feel is your best achiement since joining PCW? Two Ton: Well, I been the 3 time croozerwayt champ, and THE ONLY spokesperson of Old Cuntree Buffay! Not like some other companies... PFIK: Three time Cruiserweight champ? Two Ton: Yup. All times by way of theft... but I think my akshins speek fer themself PFIK: So you didn't actually become champ by standard victory methods then? Two Ton: Well, only if yew count skwishin a method... PFIK: #2: What has been the low point of your PCW wrestling career? Two Ton: Dammit, the whole run as 2T:2K1 That don't need an explanation PFIK: So it wasn't dropping the donut when the Hummer hit you? I would say ran you over, but I'll stick with just "hit you". Two Ton: Hey, it was worth it ta get me dat boston creme donut... Mmmmmmm Two Ton: By the way, you got anything ta eat on ya? PFIK: Luckily no. I'm expecting a dodgy answer here... #3: What title do you hold in highest esteem in PCW? Two Ton: Well, that'd be either the croozerwayt belt or the conteenintal belt... bein the only croozer who's big as a conteenint makes my loyalty divided PFIK: Going off topic; why do you like the Cruiserweight belt so much? Two Ton: I knew dat kwestion was cummin... Have yew ever felt a littal croozer gettin skwished and knew it was cuz of yew? PFIK: I have to admit, I know about swishing some things, but never cruiserweights. Two Ton: I didn't think so.. It's almost religious.... almost as good as eetin! PFIK: The next question is "Who would you most like to fued with, and why?" but you can answer Who would you most like to squish if you would prefer. Two Ton: Well, I done skwished about all the peepal I know of who desirv skwishin, so fer now, I'm gunna let my buddy Syko have a run at the belt... But of course if sometime Kid Wonder gets himself flattind, I might just be there fer that.... PFIK: What is so special about Syko then? Two Ton: See, while most of dem croozers go high flyin and lucha libby whateva, Syko just like me.. He likes ta create C.H.A.O.S.! PFIK: What does that stand for? Two Ton: I dunno.. a talkin potato made it up in my dream... means sumthin though... What do you think it mite mean? PFIK: "Creating Havoc And Other Stuff"? Two Ton: I don't know how to say this PFIK, but that idea sucks PFIK: Is it true that you were going to call the group H.A.V.O.C. but then found out that was already copyrighted by the guys who did GI Joe? Two Ton: no, you got it wrong... it was gunna be COBRA PFIK: Who intimidates you in PCW? Two Ton: Now whut the hell kinda loadid kwestion is dat? Do I look intimeedaytid? Two Ton: Tha only thing that intimeedayts me is a narrow alley PFIK: What about Ivan Stanislav? You've got to be worried about a big guy who only eats from either Chinese Takeaways or Spud-u-Like? Two Ton: Why shuld I be cuncirned abowt him? That damn commie done pist of tha wrong man when he starting talkin sh!t bout Two Ton. If he wants me, he knows rite where ta find me! PFIK: Would that be in Taco Bell or McDonalds? Two Ton: *face squinches up in disgust* First off, thanks ta Miyagawa, I still ain't allowed in Taco Bell! He screwd me outta that place a long time ago! McDonald's is okay, but nobuddy has the satisfying goodness you can find at Old Cuntree Buffay! They serve a wonderful varietee of delishis foods at a very valuable price. *Grins as a passing OCB employee hands him a wad of cash* PFIK: If life was like a box of chocolates, which one would you be? Two Ton: *Two Ton spaces out for quite some time at the mention of a box of chocolates, drool flowing from his lips like water from a hose* Chocolate.. Mmmmmm PFIK: Ok... erm... another badly worded question: Which of the PCW ladies most suits your tastes and why? Two Ton: Well, if she had some plastik surgree, I'd say Jeremy Howard. That bitch is uglee, but I think she's kinky... I know Howard's a man, you dumbass! PFIK: Any that are currently female? Two Ton: I'd say Amanda, if she ever ditches that scumbag Blunder PFIK: Whom would you most love to tag with in PCW? Two Ton: Alan Kreigman. I talked ta him backstayg and we gots good kemistree. He a funny guy. PFIK: What is your favourite PCW match of all time? Two Ton: If I said anything but tha Carnival of Chaos, I'd be stupid like Ivan Stanislov is. PFIK: If you were to be in a movie, what would you be and what movie? Two Ton: well, my brother Marvin if they make a new war movie, I culd wear camuflag and be the background... PFIK: #11: Who would you just like to kick in the nuts? Two Ton: Anybody who offers I guess. Hell, just line em up in front of me, and I'll just go kick everybuddy in the nuts PFIK: Final question... #12: If anything could be named after you… what would it be? Two Ton: Well i always wanted my own tital divshin so I wuldn't hafta fite croozers so much... The Phantom Booker never liked the idea of a megaweight belt.. Do you like the idea of a Two Ton Tital? PFIK: Well if you get Big Poppa Plump to put the weight back on, you might even get to have a rival in that division. Two Ton: Big who?? PFIK: the last NTICW World Champ Two Ton: NTI what'sit? you mean a nobody? PFIK: Something like that... well that was the last question Mr Ton, do you have any final words to wrap this interview up with? Two Ton: Yeah... I was told there would be caterin service... where's the free food? PFIK: It's in Taco Bell's down the road... oh. Two Ton: Dammit! ---PFIK. ---Pimplin' Ain't Easy.
