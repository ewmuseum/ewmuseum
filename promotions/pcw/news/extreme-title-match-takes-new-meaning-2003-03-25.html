Extreme Title Match Takes New Meaning

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme Title Match Takes New Meaning Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Mar 2003 ---- After a very...unique title reign holding the Extreme belt, Skitzo is fast approaching Dan Hollywood's record for holding said title. In fact, the master of the MindFuck will put his claim and his spot in PCW's history books on the line on Fusion when he takes on Young Tiger. If he wins, Skitzo is guaranteed at least 46 days as champion, thus breaking Dan Hollywood's mark of 42. However, there is the chance that Young Tiger could pull the upset, which would leave Skitzo at 42. Skitzo is now bound for the history books, at least for the moment, but in what context? Many schitzophrenics, Latinos, and extreme match fans will be watching with baited breath on Fusion...well, maybe not, but we might just say that to make our ratings higher anyway.
