Tyler Signs One More

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tyler Signs One More Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 Aug 2002 ---- As we well know recently, somewhat of a "signing fevor" has been made with several signings being made both by the Phantom Booker and Xavier Kannon, and Tyler Nelson. Both parties, obviously getting thier signings through prior to the PPV when everything is on the line just in case of the worst. Tyler Nelson in this case has made another signing, this time it's Cecilia Verucana, formerly of SAWF, UCW and HATE. This brings the women's division in PCW up to an alltime high, with Brandi, Jaden, Cecilia, Charlotte, Teresa and Romana making it a whole six, which according to our records is one more than the previous maximum. We spoke to PCW Commisioner Elenor Hall about this development... "Well there's only been two female PCW Champions in History. Myself, and Brandi. And everyone knows that I'm better than Brandi, right?"
