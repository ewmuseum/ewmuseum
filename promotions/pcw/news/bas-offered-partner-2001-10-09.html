BAS Offered Partner

9 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BAS Offered Partner Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Oct 2001 ---- In a press release today from the office of PCW's CEO Kremmen, the CEO has magnanimously granted Big Alan Steele permission to select a partner to replace Stryfe in his third Lethal Lottery Round matchup against Tyler Nelson and Kremmen himself. "In the interest of maintaining a fair and just Lethal Lottery Tournament, I propose Big Alan Steele be able to select from any of the boys who are under developmental deals, that haven't already competed in the tournament." Steele, who is wrestling solo, after his partner Stryfe was injured in a mysterious car accident, is allowed to chose one wrestler from a list of eligible names, also forwarded by the CEO, consisting of the following names: The Ultimate Warlord, Shadowman and Infest. "If Steele can use any of these young and talented guys to help him out, I am happy to allow them to compete with him - in fact, I insist upon it," said Kremmen, in closing. No comment from Big Alan Steele was available at press time. We will bring you more on this story as it becomes available.
