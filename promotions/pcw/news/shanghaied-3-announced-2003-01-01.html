Shanghaied 3 Announced

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanghaied 3 Announced Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Jan 2003 ---- January 26th is the date that PCW will present Shanghaied 3, regarded as the 2 year anniversary of PCW’s real ‘birth’. As always it will air from the Shanghai Gymnasium and 3 matches have already been announced for what is being talked about as the biggest PPV of PCW’s 3rd Age. Already signed is a TV Title match where Brandi will challenge Teresa Tomas, however with Tomas still to fulfil a contractual Title defence against Flaymin Aymin, Brandi will have to sit tight and hope for her rival to come though with the belt. Also announced is the Pharaoh against DarkStar in a Last Man Standing match that looks sure to continue the pattern of violence and blood the pair have followed since Decimation. Then, in the scheduled Main Event, 1 year after capturing his 1st PCW Title, Snow will challenge Clyde for the biggest prize in PCW, but in the 1st ever Hell In The Hell In The Cell Match. Details are sketchy, but given Clyde’s acts over the past 2 years, this can be easily summed up as ‘His kind of match’. More matches will be announced over the coming weeks so stay tuned.
