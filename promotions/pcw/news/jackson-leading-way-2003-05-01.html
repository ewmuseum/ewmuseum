Jackson Leading Way

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jackson Leading Way Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 May 2003 ---- With the new Rising Star division plan in place, the one that organizes the division as a sort-of Round Robin tournament, things look to be running smoothly. Competition though the first three shows is fierce and looks to be very much the same for the upcoming Friday Fusion show. Matt Jackson, with two victories, is leading the way for the four Pay Per View spots that will be available to do battle for the RS title (awarded to the top 4 Win/Loss records). Jackson has impressed PCW writers in this, his second month with the company. SOCIETY and Shane Wethers, with one victory apiece, also look to be favorites for the Pay Per View match, but of course this is only in the early going. We'll see much more Rising Star action before everything's over.
