The Chooch #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Mar 2002 ---- (Note: This column was written on the date mentioned at the top of the column, but due to technical problems, namely the forms sucking ass, it was posted late. So just pretend that it's 3/15 and be happy.) March 15th 2002 Subject: The direction of PCW Hello and welcome all to the second edition of The Chooch’s Take. I hope you all enjoyed my first column. If not, to bad, you got no taste. If ya did, then you obviously know what’s good. Well, needless to say, the Chooch has been sitting on pins and needles, at the end of his seat lately. Why? Glad you asked. PCW is hotter and more exciting than ever before. Hell, not since the days of Media, have I been as interested in the going ons. I await each event with a great fever. Hell, I even called in sick to work to watch this weeks past Turmoil. So, enough babbling, this isn’t The Business. Let’s get down to the meat of the matter. I want to review the PCW PPV March Madness, especially comparing my picks to the actual winners. Tell you all what, The Chooch would of done well in Vegas placing the picks he chose. So, with out further ado.... THE CHOOCH REVIEWS MARCH MADNESS Like I said last column, the opening match was a sleeper. Hell, even a monkey that was blind could of seen that Ganker would of interfered on behalf of his illegitimate brother. Estep looked good, but Team Nirvana 2k2 was too much. Chooch’s pick: Hank PPV winner: Hank Real Winner: The fans needing to get beer, food or a piss break. The next match was a hell of a match. Extreme seemed to be taken to a new level, with Jason Core providing the entertainment. To me, anytime Karl LeBlanc gets his uneducated, untalented ass handed to him is PURE GOLD ENTERTAINMENT. The Warden needs to take some advise, and drop LeBlanc into solitaire, and get himself some one better to fight it out in PCW. Chooch’s pick: Jason Core PPV winner: Jason Core Real Winner: The Chooch, because like I said, LeBlanc sucks, and he lost. Here comes the first bad pick on my half. And, it saddened me because the man I wanted to see as Rising Star Champion leaving March Madness didn’t win. Sylo, even though he has since ponied up, and won the title, made a good showing, but it was a really fresh rising star walking out the champion. I only wish Sylo would see the light, and unattach himself with Akiyama. Chooch’s pick: Jay FRIGGIN Sylo PPV winner: Rip Ryckman Real Winner: Rip.... wins PCW gold in second match in PCW. The second match in a row where The Chooch picked a winner, and the PPV disappointed by letting the wrong wrestler win. A hard fought table match, seeing many high spots, and of course, the debut of a NTICW star into PCW. Chooch’s pick: Ganker PPV winner: Anton the Kid Real Winner: Charlie Loc..... for getting brought into PCW A ladder match to name the number one contender to the TV title was next on the bill. Another high impact match as two veterans went toe to toe. Ladder matches have always been my favorite, and this one made me giddy with joy. What made it even better was watching Mike Storm lose yet again. Chooch’s pick: Brock Newbludd PPV winner: Brock Newbludd Real Winner: Hopefully this will be the catalyst to get Brock’s career moving again. The second sleeper of the night happened next. A six pack elimination match for ABSOLUTELY NOTHING AT ALL. I can only guess PCW needed to fill a quick 20 minutes in between matches. Needless to say, I chose the wrong winner. As my pick faltered in the match, another favorite of The Chooch’s won the match. Chooch’s pick: Tim Walden PPV winner: D-Kay Calyx Real Winner: The fans needing a beer, food and piss break I went ape shit over the next match. Two men, at each other’s throats for so long, finally got it on, one on one. This time, the stakes were raised by putting family on the line. All I can say about this match was WOW. It was almost as good as the main event, and it could of easily been any other PPV main event. Once again, I picked the winner. Chooch’s pick: Jeremy Howard PPV winner: Jeremy Howard Real Winner: KRAYZIE KRAYZIE KRAYZIE, and the whole f’n arena for getting to see David Draiman live. Now for the coup de grau. 20 men battling for the one thing everyone wants, but only one would get. The World Title. And, needless for The Chooch to tell ya, this was the most exciting match of the night, next to the Jeremy Howard/Maxwell match. Of course, this match boiled down to two things. Miya and Boda. And the biggest factor was the numbers game. Miya’s stable eliminated. Boda and Mexi in the ring. And to everyone’s surprise.... well, we all know what happened. Chooch’s pick: Boda PPV winner: Boda Real Winner: Boda. Finally after 18 months, and 5 World Title shots, he wins the big one. All in all, this was an all right PPV in The Chooch’s eyes. I’ve watched better, and to be honest, I’ve seen alot worst. But, as well as the PPV was, compared lightly to the events that have perspired the last two weeks. So, now I’ll give my views on the direction of PCW. First though..... I went 5-3 for picks this PPV. Not bad for a first time. Okay, we all witnessed what happened at the Turmoil right after the PPV. It also, in my opinion, has helped resurrect PCW. For too long, PCW has been sliding down the rankings since OSW has opened and quite frankly, OVERSHADOWED PCW. What perspired on the Turmoil after the PPV will forever go down as the catalyst to save PCW. New feuds were exploding all over, and that was just amongst stable members. Of course, what will forever go down as the shortest title reign happened at Turmoil also, as Boda lost after getting knocked the fuck out by Darkstar, and thus allowing Miya some sort of revenge for the elimination at March Madness. Xavier Kannon and Jeremy Howard have been at each other’s throats all week. Mexi lost to Tim Walden and is now his LACKEY for a month. And of course, Boda won the title back from Miyagawa. But, it’s the events of him winning, and the way he did that have even me wondering and unfortunately waiting to see what this monster will do next. For those who live under a rock, and missed it, Boda basically kidnapped Natalie Miyagawa, beat her and sent her to the hospital, and as Miya tried to leave to be with his fallen wife and unborn child, Boda spewed a few Stewart Hix related PCW by-laws, and that forced Miya to stay and fight. But, even bigger than that, if there could be something bigger than Boda beating a pregnant woman, was the Chase/Boda deal that came to light at the end of Turmoil. Obviously, Boda is either desperate, or very smart. I’m not going to chose either option, because if he’ll take out a pregnant woman, he’ll definitely beat on a lowly reporter. But, will the deal Boda sealed with Chase prove costly for the Champ? That’s the next question on my mind. But, I’ve gotten off on a tangent a little. So, before I end this, let’s get back on the direction of PCW. I think it is a step in the right direction. Phantom Booker is back, and he wants his company back. I feel that if TPB gets control again, it will move PCW right back up there with the likes of OSW. But, rumors are abound that others have and will be throwing their names in the hat for ownership. Boda becoming a monster is a step in the right direction. For to long he has been a face, trying to play to the crowd. But, he may of gone to far with the attack on Natalie Miyagawa. Kid Wonder supposedly quit PCW. This is a work, I swear it is a work. No way, no how is the Kid Blunder going to leave the only home that will have him. The tag division has heated up, and it’s about time. I bet 50 that soon Da Westsidaz show up, and try to become 6 time, 6 time, 6 time, 6 time, 6 time, 6 time Tag Team Champs. And, if there is one thing that will keep PCW up top, and from falling, it is this.... SHUT THE DOORS. Do not let any more ‘superstars’ from other feds in, because 90 percent of them take up space, while only a few actually do any good. Well, that’s all I have to say about PCW right now. But to end this Pulitzer prize winning column on a high note, I’ll spend the next 2 minutes writing my thoughts, what pops in my mind about some of PCW’s elite. The Phantom Booker: Good to see the pantyhose freak back Boda: Wondering if he can do anything more evil Miyagawa: What will he do to get to Boda Chris Chase: Is the ‘deal’ his time? Kid Wonder: WORK Mexi: LACKEY Walden: Snap, Crackle and POP Big Daveylicious: A tag partner to save his career Corey Williams: WHO? Snow: Well, the snow does melt in springtime Jeremy Howard: Family ties, or family lawyer? Akiyama: Neo Voids SUCK Anton the Kid: Enjoy the title for now. Loc is coming for it. Jay Sylo: RULES Jason Core: Extreme old timer Well, that’s about covers all the champions, and a few newsmakers this week. Tune in soon, as I will probably write #3 in a few weeks. For now, this is The Chooch reminding you that ‘When push comes to shove, kick the son of a bitch in the balls.’ The Chooch
