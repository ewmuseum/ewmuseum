Akiyama Plane Crash

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Akiyama Plane Crash Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Feb 2002 ---- Incoming reports have informed PCW that Akira Akiyama, the Neo Void God, was involved in a plane crash on his way to Los Angeles after Tuesday night's Turmoil show. A representative of US Airlines reports the following: “The pilots of flight 309 departing from Pearl Harbor, Hawaii radioed to the air control station at Connelly Airport upon its arrival at Los Angeles. He reported of technical troubles with one of the engines, and that they were having trouble maintaining altitude. The pilots then made notice that their navigational controls were malfunctioning…before we received static on the radio. We watched on radar as their plane made a drastic curve off course…where their beacon was lost. We’re tracing this malfunction to a possible freak storm erupting upon their course.” When asked of survivors, the representative replied, “The survival rate is slim…if none. We have the Coast Guard skimming the area where we last located them as we speak.” This leaves a onslaught of questions burdening the staff of PCW. Did Akiyama survive? If so, where is he? Has this event occurred because of the research uncovered by the Tukashimi Corporation? Stay tuned to find out more details as the story unfolds.
