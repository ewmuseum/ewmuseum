The Chooch #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Mar 2002 ---- From the Desk of The Chooch March 3rd, 2002 Subject: PCW’s Pay Per View March Madness Hello all. My name is The Chooch, and after being a PCW fan for almost it’s entirety, I decided to sit here, at my desk, and pen [actually type] some sort of column. Being my first column, I decided to tackle an easy subject, and that being March Madness. PCW has been in the forefront of e-feds for some time now, almost a year and a half. And, from what I’ve seen, when it comes to PPVs, the bar is always raised a step or two more. What am I trying to say, let me make it simple for you simpletons...... PCW is the SHIT, the rest of you feds can only wallow in its droppings. That being said, let me run down my thoughts, and picks on the huge Pay Per View card. First off, the choice to hold the PPV in St. Louis is perfect. Seeing as the city needs some form of highly rated, highly entertaining sport. And because with it’s lack-luster pro-teams performances this year [see The Cardinals *choke*, The Rams *choke*, and the Blues *suck*], the PCW decided to show this city what champions really are. Secondly, this is the smallest amount of cards ever on a PCW PPV, but it is highlighted by the 20 Man Madness Brawl For It All to crown the PCW World Champion. Third, this looks to be a break out PPV for many talented wrestlers. And fourthly [don’t think that’s a word, oh well], as always, we can, and I think we will see the usual PPV surprise. In the past, it’s either been a bunch of ship jumping pirates from other feds, who merely come over, and occupy valuable space, or the return of PCW stars either on the shelf, or coming BACK out of retirement. Tonight, will be no different. The card looks promising. Like I said only 8 matches in total, with the possibility of the main event lasting well over an hour. The opening match, is nothing more than a filler match. The fans arriving late won’t miss anything special. The ones trying to wait in line to buy that $5.00 beer, won’t miss anything spectacular. Mark Estep vs. Hank Gunyon Like I said.... this is a mere filler. Hank is a former Extreme Champion. Mark Estep, had the opportunity to be big in PCW, but vanished for a bit, and now is back. But, that doesn’t mean he’s better. I think this match will end with the usual PCW interference as Hank’s longtime tag partner, and from what I hear, ‘life partner’ Ganker runs in, and the two dispose of a possible Estep victory. And of course, this will go undetected by the ref, and Hank will get his hand raised in victory. Like I said.... this is a Sunday Sabotage match, not a PPV match. Nothing to miss, nothing special to witness. WINNER: Hank Gunyon Now, the next match should have been the opening match. At least it will be more exciting. And, it’s going to be filled with more violence than the aftermath of the England vs. Germany soccer match during Euro 2000. My only dislike of this match is the champion defending his title. No Holds Barred Match PCW Extreme Title Karl LeBlanc (c) vs. Jason Core Let me state this right here, right now. I am unimpressed with Karl LeBlanc thus far. I’ve heard he was suppose to be the baddest son of a bitch in his cellblock. From what I‘ve seen, that only means he liked to take it in the ass with no lube. LeBlanc promos are as short and as impressive as Tyler Nelson’s two World Title reigns. Jason Core is young, fresh, and shows a promising violent side. I hope that the Warden gets his block knocked off, and Karl LeBlanc finally realizes that after years of butt pirating in the Amaranth Penitentiary, that he truly belongs among the Ream Team. WINNER: Jason Core Next on the docket is the Rising Star match. This is an always pleasant to watch division because the competitors are trying to prove their worth in PCW. Four Corners Elimination Match PCW Rising Star Title Lance Bell (c) vs. Ripper Ryckman vs. Steve Johnson vs. Jay Sylo Lots of young talent. Fresh faces. This should be a fast paced, action packed match. Bell, the current champion is full of fury, wanting to defend his newly won crown. Ripper and Johnson are tough as nails competitors, who some day may make waves here in PCW, but my money is on the man with no monkey to spank right now [because he’s on vacation], Jay Sylo. I’m a Sylo mark. This man is stupendous, and one of the major future players of PCW. Come the last man in the ring, it will be the 7 foot giant, and his name is Jay Friggin Sylo. WINNER: Jay Sylo Two matches down [remember, I didn’t count the first one as a real PPV match], and two new champions. The next match up, is a great gimmick match. Nothing better than getting thrown through some wood to make the fans happy. And that of course makes The Chooch happy. Table Match #1 Contender to the Cruiserweight Title Ganker vs. Anton the Kid vs. Red Rock vs. Kramer Wild Wing We have Ganker, the sidekick of Hank. Anton, the youngest wrestler in PCW. Red Rock, a up and coming Wild Deuce member and The KFC spokesman, Kramer Wild Wing. I predict a four-table set up, with some unlucky bastard getting power bombed through them. Ganker is focused, Anton is going through puberty, Red Rock seems to have vanished, and Kramer is busy eating the all you can eat buffet with Jason Alexander. I’m going with Hank’s boyfriend to take this one, sending the pre-pubescent Anton through those tables. WINNER: Ganker The next match is a barnburner. A beer drinking, bar fighting, chain smoking former tag team champion against a man who will never amount to anything till he disassociates himself from the Storm family name. Ladder Match #1 Contender to the Television Title Brock Newbludd vs. "Amazing" Mike Storm The best thing about Ladder Matches is some one is bound to get hurt. And I hope its Mike Storm. He is a cancer in PCW. The whole Storm name is synonymous with losers and untalented wrestlers. Brock on the other hand is a proven champion, in the tag division. He has come back with a vengeance, but seems to lack the one thing he really needs to be great again. Stephen Blood. All in all, I want to see Brock climb the ladder, and win the match. Then jump from the ladder and decapitate Mike Storm. Then I’d have a really great day. WINNER: Brock Newbludd The next match is another filler match. Perhaps it serves some unseen purpose, but I fail to see it. Why not have this match lead off the night, and fill this spot with The Tag Champion defending their title finally. But, alas, I will talk briefly about it, because as a columnist, I must. Six-Pack Elimination Match Tim Walden vs. THE Multiple Man vs. Impact vs. D-Kay Calyx vs. Big Daveylicious vs. Afwa Kilmanjaro Like I said previously, I’d have preferred this as the opening match, and the Tag Champs made to defend their straps in this spot. But alas, some one in the front office of PCW wasn’t thinking here. So, let’s see.... Mr. Heart and Soul Walden, Multiple Personality Man, No-Impact, The DJ, The Woman Jobber, and the Samoan Bigman. In a match named after my favorite beverage purchase choice[a six-pack]. Being this is of little factor on any storylines going on right now, I’ll do the old school boy method of choosing the winner. Eenie, Meenie, Minnie, Mo, Catch a Jackass and Beat him Fro. My mommy told me to beat your ass, and that one is you. WINNER: Tim Walden Now, down to the pre-main event match, and this one is going to be violent, deadly and down right sick. Two individuals at each other’s throats, and it’s all about family. Fuck the McCoys and Hatfields. This one is going down in PCW History. Free Justin Howard Hell in a Cell Match The two men will start inside the cell. Hanging over the cell, in a cage of his very own, is Jeremy's brother, Justin. The first man to escape the cell, climb to the top, and free/grab Justin from his cage is declared the winner. PCW Continental Title Jeremy Howard (c) vs. Drake Maxwell So, not only is the brother of Jeremy Howard on the line. The Continental title is also. And Jeremy, who just won the title, has alot to lose in this one. First, his brother, but, almost as important, the newly won Continental Strap. Jeremy, in my opinion, is the epitome of PCW. Drake Maxwell, well, is talented, but not on the level that he is pushed. Howard on the other hand, is focused on winning the remaining two titles he has yet to win in PCW. So, will that be the leveler in this contest? No way. Jeremy is not one to let other visions cloud his judgment, and thus, he will let his brother free, and retain the title. WINNER: Jeremy Howard And then there was one. The largest main event, outside of Alliances. But unlike Alliances, this one is for the World Title. And, if my memory serves me right, this is the largest match for the World Title ever. And it features some of the most prominent wrestlers in PCW now. Two former Champions are in the match, as well as the current Television Champion, 1/2 of the current Tag Champion and the current Cruiserweight Champion. The rest of the field, are former champions in one division or another, and only two men to never hold a PCW title, yet. Main Event 20 Man Madness Brawl for it All PCW Heavyweight Title Order of Entrance Legend Inc. Kid Wonder Akiyama Corey Williams The End Miyagawa Chet King Bobby Stormbringer Jon Kano The Chef El Diablo Flaymin Aymin Boda Darkstar Xavier Kannon Chris Chase Juan Borro The Rush Tyler Nelson Snow Now I’m not going to get into the who eliminates who, or what’s going to happen to who. I will make a few points about this match. If history of other type rumbles repeats itself, then Snow, the last one to enter, will not win the match. Also, this match is full of stable mates. New Breed, with all it’s members representing in the match. International Incident has The Chef, Akiyama, Juan Boro, Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo in this match. Management has produced its only two remaining members in this one. And Asylum is well represented by Miyagawa, Kannon, Stormbringer and Darkstar. That leaves Legend Inc, John Kano, The Rush, The End and Boda all with out stable mates. Now, normally, one would say this would be a downfall for five. But not The Chooch. See, I believe that the stables will eventually get at each other, because like Tyler Nelson says ... ‘GREED IS GOOD’, and everyone will do what ever it takes to win this match. So, if Chase has to throw Williams or Snow out of the ring to win, you better be damn skippy he will. So, that alone gives the other five some sort of edge. Now, to come right out an pick a winner, will be a hard choice. So many in this match are worthy to be champion. A few know what it takes to win the big one, and a few have been screwed many times when it comes to winning the World Title. So to make this short and sweet, I’ll just list my top six choices to win the title at March Madness. #6: Kid Wonder- The Teeny Bopper is no longer a mere Cruiserweight. No, he has found that edge that has alluded him his entire PCW career... Tyler Nelson. #5: Xavier Kannon- Does Scientology really exist? Kannon believes so, and it was that belief that helped him win the NTICW World Title, and it might just take him there again. #4: Chris Chase- tired of being held down, he is going to make an impact in this match, but will it be enough to regain his former PCW2 self? Tonight, we find out. #3: Legend Inc.- Can Duke’s choice upset everyone, and win on his first PCW PPV? He’s not called Legend for nothing. #2: Miyagawa- I’m a mark for Miyagawa. He’s a strong contender for the World Title. He’s the current TV Champion too. Does he have enough to muster the victory tonight? and, #1: Boda- What can I say. He’s been on a streak lately, beating Snow at every turn. The fans want to see him finally win the big one. He’s a major threat in this type of match, being the largest man in the match. And, he’s more focused this week then ever before. Will Boda win it, or will the Madness be too much for him, again? WINNER: Boda All in all, this has the makings of a great PCW PPV. I’ve never been disappointed before, and it doesn’t look to be that way this time again. A good way to spend $39.95. Well, PCW fans, I am signing off for now. Look for another column to follow after the PPV, sometime this week. This is The Chooch saying ‘When Push Comes To Shove, Just Kick The Bastards Ass.’ The Chooch.
