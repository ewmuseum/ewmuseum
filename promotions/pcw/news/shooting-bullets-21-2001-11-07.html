Shooting Bullets #21 Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Nov 2001 ---- All expressions and opinions in this article are solely those of The Shoot King, a ham sandwich, and actor/comedian Nipsy Russell. Anything written can not be used without the consent of The Shoot King. Any duplication of this article will result in your ass on a platter. All hate mail and such can be sent to [detail withheld] where it can and will be used in a future article. (This bona fide PCW shoot is brought to you by the archives. The archives: Wasting my time almost as much as Duke Williams. Water: The most overrated substance in the World. And by the BCPD. If you smell bacon and grease, it must be the Baltimore County Police.) Yes, just for the majority of you undeserving ingrates, I have decided to write this column. After having to sit through the filth and drivel of Ed and the often pointless thoughts of The Hound, a savior was needed. Insert myself. You needed a good read, and I'm hear to provide it. Although I do reccomend checking out The Hound's interview with Jacob Yoder. Not because Hound does it, or because it's well written, but because Yoder ROCKS! And Jacob Yoder is the sole reason why NTICW stands a chance at passing PCW for the top spot in the PTC. So after you get done reading another Pulitzer Prize winning column by myself, feel free to check it out. Now that I've finished marking out for Jacob Yoder again, I'm glad to see that the World is back to normal. I'm writing columns. The United States are killing innocent people. And the Cleveland Browns are blowing 14 point leads with 30 seconds left. Everything seems to be OK. The Yankees lost, which is good. But why does Jon Kano still have a job? I know we're down to under 50 people, but are we THAT desperate? Eh. Who gives a flying fuck about Kano right now. His name shant be mentioned at the top of my column again. Alliances Alliances, the annual November PPV, is in some 10 days and there has been no hype for it. THANK YOU LORD! Hopefully it remains like this. That way we don't set some insanely high standard and totally blow the PPV. Perfect examples of this are Supremacy and Red October. Supremacy: All the hype was surrounding the TPB/Nelson match. What happened? TPB turned out to be Ryan Knox. Nelson lost, but remained CEO. The angle totally died. Knox sucks. How could this happen? All that hype and it turned out to be total shit. Red October: Was a solid show, I'll say that. But it was all way too predictable. And the finish turned out to be a total bomb. Yes, I was stoked to see Judas Steele come back and punk out that bitch. Then Judas went and became a bitch, and the angle was totally slauhtered. That cost Judas Steele my respect. And as far as Streak goes....let him leave. He's been gone more than he has been here, so if he wants to go, I, for one, won't miss him. So what I'm saying is hype isn't always a good thing. And with two weeks to go until the PPV, and little hype so far, it all brings a smile to my face. Next week we'll work on clean finishes. More Ranting on Streak and Steele With the exception of Media, this was perhaps the biggest angle in PCW history. Tons of build up for it. Tons of people siting their fat asses on the edge of their partially broken chairs looking at their cheap monitors to find out who the father is and what not. And what happens? Meanstreak and Judas Steele totally fuck it up. SHIT! Why not just give it to Syko? He might not be that good, but at least he'll work. What bullshit! Judas Steele, I once respected you, and I led the charge for you to get into the Hall of Fame, but then you go and do this shit. I don't wanna hear any excuses about not being able to see the results becaue of your cheap computer in your cheap Section 8 house. Clyde used a fucking DREAMCAST for shit's sake, but still stayed informed. All you had to do was ask for someone to give you the results and I'm sure they would've done so. It's not that hard. But instead you go looking for some lame ass excuse on why you don't wanna do at least one damn roleplay. ONE ROLEPLAY! You couldn't even do that. You make me sick. Go back to being dead. You were better off that way. And Meanstreak, I, for one, am sick and tired of hearing you bitch and moan over every little thing. We may talk shit about guys like Chuck Manson and Scott Kilborn having huge egos, but nobody has a bigger ego than you, Streak. Shit! Would it kill you to work with someone thrown at you just once without throwing a temper tantrum? Christ! You're supposed to be the big star in PCW. You are far from it. Are you good? Yes. If I were owner of an upstart e-fed and was looking for a big star, would I try getting you to join? Not a chance in hell. I know some people won't like me saying this, but your a backstage cancer. Not as big of a cancer as someone like Scott Naket, but a cancer no less. And I definitely wouldn't want someone with your pisspoor attitude to worry about. You've gotten your way numerous times. You don't have anything to complain about. Roulette of Death Being the guy who thought this up, (with the help of what will remain an anonymous source), I shall not go into the details of this unique event. But right now wouldn't be a good time to use it. Maybe after the Diamond in the Rough tournament I'll pitch it again. But the roster is too depleted with people being fired and people being injured to bust this out. But when I do, I'll be rooting for Kano all the way. Who to Mark Out For With Judas Steele being a bitch, Putter Mayhem gone, and Jacob Yoder running NTICW, I am at a loss of someone to mark out for. I will be conducting some sort of search to figure out who deserves this spot. But until I find that person, I'll just throw Lady Cat here. Why? One: because she's hot. And two: Because I had to say her name somewhere in here. CCW's loss. Who to Hate After careful consideration for over 40 people, I have finally decided who will replace Guy Leslie in the Guy Leslie Bash of the Moment section of my columns. Nikita Morozov looked to have the advantage, but got fired. THE Multiple Man was close, but he's just confused. So who have I chosen? None other than the most overrated PCW person, (can't even call him a star), the man who rocks about as much as Lip-Sync, JON KANO! Jon Kano Bash of the Moment Congratulations Kano, you've finally accomplished something you can brag about. I know you'll try to bring up the fact that you've held a couple titles. Big whoop! So has Vegita, The Violater, Revenant, Scott Naket, Ryan Knox, and so on and so forth. Your talent level ranks somewhere in between Johnny Mayhem, the guy that stole stuff in FSW, and your father's giant shit. Oh, wait, that would be you. You can't be ranked in between an e-thief and yourself, now can ya? I know, you've won PCW titles. Will that be your only defense? If so, that's pathetic. But just for you, allow me to share with everyone your greatest moment in PCW. It was the day you won the Continental title. What a day. That day still brings a smile to my face. You remember that day, don't ya? I'm sure ya do, so I don't need to add anything more here. I'll just let everyone do what I've been forced to do, go to the archives, and check it out for themself. Oh, and a note to Boda. Dump Kano right away or else you'll never get another sniff of the title.
