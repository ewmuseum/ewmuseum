Post-PPV Injury Report

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-PPV Injury Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Oct 2002 ---- Following the events of Red October on Sunday, PCW’s doctor Rick Niviera gave his injury report to the wrestling media... “HELLO EVERYBODY!” he exclaimed before waiting 10 minutes with no reply before continuing with his report that contained the following. The Pharaoh was sent for a back x-ray immediately after his match with Akiyama and the results seem promising with mostly bruising and nothing that should keep him from bookings this week. Snow was checked straight into a Tokyo hospital after his match suffering what were believed to be minor internal injuries after the El Diablo orchestrated attack on him, and update is expected before Turmoil. Mark Makonie suffered a severe strain of his jaw ligaments as well as 12 lost teeth and substantial nerve damage following the assault by Duzza.. He has been taken off the road and may be missing for some time. Duke Williams’ nose is said to be a clean break and was remoulded immediately following the PPV. Rent-a-Hero has had an x-ray of his neck and is awaiting diagnosis following two Hallmarks by Xavier Kannon. The rest of the roster escaped with only cuts and bruises and should all be in top condition for the coming week.
