And Injustice For All

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And Injustice For All Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Jan 2003 ---- Atlanta, Georgia-- On Friday night, El Spiffy! got a chance to win to face Tyler Valentine on Adrenaline for the RUSH World Title, having gotten Xavier Kannon himself to sign the match with Joe Bananas. The catch? Spiffy!'s career in RUSH would be over if the lucha were to lose this match. Could Spiffy! overcome the Front Office's will to become yet ANOTHER #1 Contender to the belt and strike another blow for the Resistance, or did Xavier Kannon have something in store? Our semi-main event tells all. In addition, Clyde took on Pharaoh in a match that can be described only as weird, Marvin Harris met a new foe, and four top PCW contenders faced off in a match with huge implications for the future. For all this and more, head on over to the "Recent Results" and hit the Fusion link.
