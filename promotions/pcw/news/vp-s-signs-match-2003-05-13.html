VP-S Signs Match

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VP-S Signs Match Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 May 2003 ---- After PCW Tuesday Turmoil, VP-S signed The Pharaoh and Isiy Xansiv to face Totally Tubular Tommy and Heavy Metal Harry in a tag match on the May 20th edition of Turmoil. "If the managers want to get so involved in the wrestling, they can be booked to wrestle," is all VP-S had to say in the matter. Heavy Metal Harry has been retired since he suffered a career-ending concussion at the hands of the Celestials in last year's PCW Alliances, while Isiy Xansiv isn't exactly built for giving or taking punishment. While some old Cowabunga Connection fans will be excited to see the tag team back together again, Heavy Metal Harry's relative health for competing is still up in the air. PCW physician Nick Riviera has given HMH a clean bill of health, but everyone knows that he's a quack anyway. And Isiy could suffer terrible injuries from this match. When questioned about that, VP-S had this to say. "I'd take out Heavy Metal Harry and Isiy Xansiv if either Tommy Gordo or Pharaoh could get partners to replace them. Since neither man has any friends in PCW, however, there's no reason to cancel the tag match. So Pharaoh and Wife versus Tommy and Brother is still on." The advantage in this match should go to Cowabunga Connection, a team that has experience wrestling with each other. And Heavy Metal Harry showed on last week's Fusion that he's still able to wrestle if need be. How will things play out? We'll have to see.
