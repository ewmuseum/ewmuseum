Stephens Injured

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stephens Injured Posted by Sandy Wong on 30 Jun 2002 ---- The PCW Pulse reports have confirmed that "Scandelous" Ryan Stephens has sustained two hair line fractured ribs on his right side. Stephens received the fractured ribs courtesy of Mark Makonie and his Nazi followers outside the arena on Friday Night Fusion. Rumor has it that Stephens will still fight, but will tape up the ribs and also wear a leather pad that will provide protection for the ribs. The PCW Pulse was able to get a word with Stephens who seemed rather calm: "It's not a big deal (the cracked ribs). They will affect my ability to toss from that side, and will be mildy uncomfortable, but the pad will provide sufficient protection from further injury. This injury will not hold me down! I will perservere and prevail, because quite frankly, I want it more! The ribs shouldn't be an issue!"
