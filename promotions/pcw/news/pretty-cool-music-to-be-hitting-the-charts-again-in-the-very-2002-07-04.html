Pretty Cool Music to be hitting the charts again in the very near future? Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Jul 2002 ---- Thats certainly the question on the lips of all the 13, 14 and 15yr old Girls that follow the rollercoaster careers of the "PoP Icon" team that just can't decide where they should be and what they should be doing in this business. If any of you care to remember, when the "Pretty Cool Guys" were on the payroll of "New True Internet Championship Wrestling", Sherwin and Moore (alongside former member Sean Smith) had released their first tune to the public, a track that was apparently liked by the majority of "PCG" fans. Upon their signing with "Primetime Championship Wrestling", it had been rumored by none other than Sean Smith himself, that his former friends were working on a complete album full of tracks, some of them even written in dedication to people whom they know. Smith claims that one of the dedicated tracks will be to a certain lady, but nothing cleared on that for the time being. All that can be said for now, is that you stay tuned... and keep an eye out on the shelves at your local branches of MVC, HMV and Virgin.
