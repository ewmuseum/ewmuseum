Wanted Dead or Alive

18 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wanted Dead or Alive Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Feb 2002 ---- Sacramento, California- At Shanghaied a few weeks back, PCW favorite Darkstar was supposedly killed at the hands of Clyde in their First Death Match. But despite being dead, Darkstar had been booked in matches. Probably because he is on the payroll and Tyler Nelson, like the prick he can be, wants the most for his money. So despite the tragedy in Shanghai a few weeks ago, Darkstar was scheduled to compete in the tournament. And PCW Management, namely Tyler Nelson and Kid Wonder, would make sure that happened. What would happen? How would Jason Core react to this being his debut? And what role would Clyde play in the match? There's only one way to find out. Also on Sabotage: International Incident has a little incident of their own. Jeremy Howard tries to overcome to devil worshippers on his quest to a title sweep. And where on Earth is Justin Howard? To find out all this and more, check out Sunday Sabotage found under the Recent Results link. And while you're there, check out the great action that occured on Friday Fusion.
