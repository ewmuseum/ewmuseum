Remembering Old Times

20 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Remembering Old Times Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Feb 2002 ---- Portland, Oregon- During most of their PCW tenure, Miyagawa and Clyde have been feuding in some sort of way. Whether it be directly with each other or as part of different stables, Miyagawa and Clyde have hated each other. But as of late, things have cooled off between the two. That was until Friday when Clyde brought up a forgetful part of Miyagawa's PCW past. Of course, that would be him jobbing to BAD BATTIO!! Now with things heated up, and a shot at the Heavyweight title on the line, how far would these men go to kill the other? And furthermore, how long would it last? Also on Turmoil: How many times does Boda have to beat the champ to finally become the champ himself? Matches are called off. And Jeremy Howard and Kid Wonder use new methods to attack their enemies. But what methods could they be? To see all this and more, check out the Turmoil results found under the link at the bottom of the page.
