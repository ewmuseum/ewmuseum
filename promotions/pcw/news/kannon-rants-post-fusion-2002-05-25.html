Kannon Rants Post-Fusion

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kannon Rants Post-Fusion Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 May 2002 ---- After the ending to Friday Fusion, Xavier Kannon, who appeared to settle his dispute with Tyler Nelson in time for their match looked to all who were around him to vent his steam upon... "WHAT IN HUBBARD'S NAME WAS THAT?!?!? WHO ARE THOSE PEOPLE... GIVE ME THEIR NAMES SO I CAN SUE THEM... BOOKER HASN'T EVEN BEEN BACK TWO WEEKS AND WE HAVE FANS JUMPING THE RAILS TO ATTACK STARS!!!" he ranted before being informed that the Ultimate Authority's assailants do indeed have PCW contacts. Upon hearing this, it only insensed him more. "WHAT? WHO THE HELL ARE THEY? DID WE LET THE FIRST FIVE GUYS INTO THE BUILDING GET PCW CONTRACTS AND THE HOBOS OUTSIDE SNUCK IN EARLY?!?!?" When asked on the condiditon of his stable-mates he had the following to add. "SPEAKING OF MY SUPPOSED FUCKING "BUDDIES"... WHERE IN THE NAME OF TRAVOLTA WERE POLITICALLY INCORRECT? AND AS FOR MAKONIE... GREAT JOB PROTECTING THE IMPORTANT PEOPLE! That last bit was sarcasm by the way... AS FOR WALDEN? STOP GETING BEATEN UP BY KANO! DA BOSS~! COULD HAVE BEEN SERIOUSLY HURT!!!" After Ellie~! dumped her bottle of Evian in his face he appeared to calm down a little. "I shall be looking into this matter, the Ultimate Authority just got a bitch slap and while DA BOSS~! is icing his jaw to make sure that PEON doesn't bruise him I will see to the matter and it will be properly investigated." After the events of Fusion how will the Ultimate Authority react? And could we see more brute force be added to stop any such incident happening again?
