Badge Owed Title Shot?

5 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Badge Owed Title Shot? Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Oct 2001 ---- According to a document obtained by popular online wrestling sheet, Wrestlingtattler.com, PCW Continental Champion Duke Williams owes the leader of Thug 4 Hire a title shot, in return for services rendered. "According to the copy of the facsimile we received from our source deep within PCW, Section 24, Paragraph 6E, it seems clear that Badge is owed a title shot by Williams," said Tattler.com reporter Art Ovmastabashun. According to the section of the contract listed on the Tattler site as Section 24, Paragraph 6 "Under the agreement for the services of Thugs 4 Hire, Duke Williams agrees to give Badge a title shot at winning Badge's belt back from Mr Williams if Badge helps him eliminate one Chris Sloboda. Payment to be recieved no later than 30 days. Failing to do so will result in a severe beating much like a certain black man recieved at the hands on the LAPD some years ago." No word from Williams on the validity of this document, though Badge has been quoted as saying the document is legitimate. We will bring you more on this story as it becomes available.
