RUSH Contract Problems

25 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RUSH Contract Problems Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Nov 2002 ---- It’s been rumoured Xavier Kannon has been locked in talks with his legal team all day over a problem with the purchase of RUSH regarding the contracts of it’s wrestlers. All the contracts, including those of top stars El Spiffy!, Chris Matthews, Marvin Harris, Tyler Valentine and McGlocklan have been purchased by Xavier and are now owned by PCW, but the contracts themselves have not been ‘bought out’ thus still belong to RUSH, whoever may own it. So as for now it seems that the CEO can not do as many thought he would and switch the RUSH roster over to PCW to destroy the cult Federation, now it appears he must find a different course of action that may be hard with only a handful of contracts having buy-out clauses in them. “He’s a bit... Er... Um....” nervously muttered one of Xavier’s Lawyers, “.. What’s the word for someone who says he’ll feed your liver to a tramp?” The contract situation will hopefully be settled by Wednesday when Xavier will make a RUSH announcement at the commencement of the post-PPV Turmoil, where all RUSH wrestlers are expected to be in attendance.
