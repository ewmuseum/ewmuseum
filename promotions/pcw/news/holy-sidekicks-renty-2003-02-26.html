HOLY SIDEKICKS, RENTY!!! Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Feb 2003 ---- Cedar Rapids, Iowa- Few have had it harder in the last 6 months than Rent-a-Hero. From the moment his sidekick and friend Brandi, aka The Black Kitten, was turned by Xavier Kannon upon the formation of the Front Office, PCW’s Hero has been on a downward slope. Struggling for form and focus, Renty has never managed to fill the spot Brandi vacated, and even Amy, the orphan girl took under his wing cannot fill the void... So on Turmoil, Renty finally took that big step and decided to find himself a new sidekick. Who would fill Brandi’s shoes? What would Totally Tubular Tommy say? And could Rent-a-Hero even find anyone? All was revealed. Also on Turmoil, Dorn Tracer ‘Shoots’ on the Rising Star Title, Gaz Green shows how to lose hundreds of pounds in a single night, Violence Jack crosses the line to get to Skylar Green, and the Main Event invades another match to give us a show stealer. To see all this and more, head down to the Turmoil banner and click “Results”.
