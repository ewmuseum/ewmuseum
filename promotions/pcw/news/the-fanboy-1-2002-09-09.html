PCW — Column

The Fanboy #1

9 September 2002

The Fanboy #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Sep 2002 ---- Well, a little introduction may be needed. Why am I writing this column you may ask? Well, PCW’s success as of late is why I’m writing this column. The ratings show it. The wrestler rankings show it. General feeling shows it. PCW is back. We have a tight roster, we have two quality shows again. But, the columnists have gone. The End has gone, That Column Guy and the Slacker seem to have stopped at one, same with the Rush... So I decided I’d try and fill the void. That and get the fucking Price Brother’s “Column” off the site. So, onto the Fed we all love, and where better to start than the newbies. In recent weeks PCW has taken on two of, in my opinion, the best tag teams in Wrestling Tattler right now, Pranks With Attitude and Playaz Inc. Right now, P.W.A are on top form, their RP’s are brightening up the RP Board and their work for the TV shows has been hilarious from the Misplaced impersonations, to the Ninja Turtles, to the Ellie harassing. Both teams have the advantage of two handlers working them, and when P.I get their in-card work and a good feud sorted (Am I the only one who sees gold in P.I v Duzza/Makonie?) they will be strong challengers for the straps. From OSW’s Most Extreme Team, PCW has taken Total Violation and Redemption Song. The latter I see slowly working their way up to the tag titles, where Total Violation will be a great team for pure comedy and violence. As far as singles go, PCW has taken back Jamie Celt (Anton the Kid) and D-Kay Calyx... Both of whom will be eager to make up for their last runs where Anton bombed out the Cruiserweight Title and D-Kay stable hopped like a mad man before never returning from his injury. As for fresh faces, PTC star Hardcore Hoefel has arrived and should fit well into the Extreme division while BladeD... Well, he bought his way in for $25 and I don’t think we need say more than that. The curse of the females may have also struck again. Charlotte is on a break, Romana seems to have gone and Cecilia has hardly been noticed. Looks like it’s time to call them Cruiserweights rather than woman again, with Anarchy and Teresa Tomas looking like they both have good careers ahead of them in PCW. Back to the tag title scene for a minute. In my view, Duzza and Makonie are good champions... That should be a positive thing, right? No. Because they could be GREAT champions. The team itself is a stroke of genius, and Duzza’s RP’ing has been top notch, but they’ve been too shy in the shows. Maybe Duzza should be the teams RP’er and Makonie do their segments, because right now they may struggle to get by Playaz Inc. or P.W.A right now. Currently, with the Cowabungas on form, Redemption Song looking to prove themselves, Andy Murray trying his hand at tag work and the Heartbreakers looking to come back soon... The tag scene has suffered a rather satisfying revival without having to sign the utter crap we had last time it was stocked. Although not newbies, Scorpion, Eddie Mandrake and Trigger are still yet to make their marks on the company. Be it lack of a feud, character, or in Scorpion’s case, a name that is holding them back, all 3 need to pull something out of the bag before they vanish into Extreme Division obscurity... Something that has also happened to Violence Jack. Third Dawning looked like it would be Jack’s break through show, he used his character to it’s real potential, then... Nothing. Jack has possibly what it takes to be the new Clyde, the only thing stopping him is himself. Although it’s just begun, Smoke v Colt could be a great little PPV sleeper match. Good to see a feud with a real reason brought about by gimmick clashes... Colt’s new position as Extreme Champion should bring about some violent ways to teach Smoke how an American should act. Speaking of Extreme, feet being cut off by Clyde seemed a little unrealistic.. Then again, Loc would no-show his own no-showing so I marked. On the subject of no-showing.. Or rather a lack of RP’s, I’d like to bring up something I’ve been pondering about priorities. Right now Wrestling Tattler is a big place and many PCW workers are manning other Feds or helping out with Feds like PURE. Is that a good thing for PCW? No. As much as I hate to say it, things like this could hurt Primetime. With guys like Rush Mason and Rent-a-Villain just begging for a big push, any extra work they have on will only delay what every fan wants to see... Same with writing matches for other Feds, get their roster (As in those just in PURE or CHZ) to do it, after all, PCW doesn’t ask other Feds to write their matches, so why any different for the developmentals... In a way it may sound selfish to expect people to only concentrate on PCW, but time has shown developmentals come and go, PCW doesn’t. With CHZ now in limbo, I expect to see El Diablo back on form and prove my point by continuing his rise up the card. Ahhh, now something I can praise. THE TV TITLE IS ALIIIIIIIIVE!!! ALIVE!!!! A huge thumbs up for the work of Syko the Goblin and Venneman Pendragon (Who seems to have left the rest of the CCW guys in his horse’s hoof dust). The two of them always seem to bring out something new and entertaining, and now the pair have Bertha involved, you can’t help but feel hilarity will ensure. With the Continental Title continuing the string of strong champs, it can almost be classed as main event, something also shown with Fusion’s main event... Which also makes one less sections of the card for me over. Which rules. Currently the main event scene seems paired off nicely. El Diablo and Snow began at Third Dawning, and although it’s gone off the boil lately there are still 8 shows to get some heat into it with Back to Old School being cancelled. Duke and Miyagawa has begun in what could and should be a great verbal feud, both known for great dialogue in shows. With DarkStar going, Kannon is a bit up in the air as where to go... Maybe he will just sit back on his in-cards and wait for the 24/7 thing to play out, that or elevate someone else that is just about ready. Time will tell. Another option could be Akiyama going after the champion, but all signs are pointing to another feud with he and the Pharaoh, this time in it’s proper place of the main event scene. And speaking on main events, Fusion’s sees Rent-a-Villain taking on Frisco, another feud that looks set to explode with RAV seemingly out to punish Frisco for his part in Big Alan Steele’s departure. The feud has a good basis, and this match should help it along nicely. Running along with the main event is the stable scene right now. The Front Office forming came as one of the biggest shocks in recent months and they seem to have good chemistry as well as immediate heat for the way they formed and their actions. Hopefully we’ll see more introductions like the 40% tax. The antidote to them is seemingly Asylum, another heel team who are using their gimmicks brilliantly to play off the Office’s power. With Duke and Miya feuding, the stables can expect to meet a few more times after Turmoil. With the Dark Age seemingly disbanded, that now just leaves the Uprising, who need Rush Mason back on form immediately. Blayze’s character being held over as manager seems to be working well to get them over as cheating heels, but right now the newbie team are showing Rush how it’s none, not the way it should be. September looks like it will be for PCW what March was... Luckily without a roster firing sneaking up around the corner to send the Fed back to the drawing board and hire a set of unreliable and un-entertaining.. Well, as I haven’t said it yet, JOBBERS. With the momentum seemingly set to build, Primetime should hold it’s #1 position in Wrestling Tattler. The ratings. The rankings. The roleplays. That is what PCW has going for it right now... That and no Corey Williams. Until next time I have time, material and the will to type. The Fanboy.
