Boda Seriously Injured

4 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boda Seriously Injured Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Oct 2001 ---- Due to the brutal assault at the hands of The Red Army, and then the attempt by Kremmen to soften up Boda for the Lethal Lottery second round matchup between Boda/Duke and Kremmen/Nelson, PCW superstar, Boda, had been rushed to a local hospital in Birmingham, England. "Mr. Sloboda suffered bruised kidneys, a hairline fracture in his right forearm, and a lacerated liver. He will have to wear a cast for 4-6 weeks for the fracture," said Dr. Malcolm Chapman of the Our Lady of Badteeth and Limey Gits Medical Center. "As for the Kidneys, they will heal naturally. The bad news for the PCW's Boda is that he will need surgery to repair the laceration of his liver. The problem is Mr. Sloboda checked himself out of the hospital, AMA, or, against medical advice. So, if you know where Mr. Sloboda is.please get him to a hospital. His life depends on it." PCW officials were quick to counter the news dispensed by Chapman. "If Boda can move, he will be in the ring," said PCW spokesman Cal Seaver. "He is truly PCW's version of Lou Gehrig." When informed Lou Gehrig died young, Seaver shrugged his shoulders. We'll bring you more on this story as it becomes available.
