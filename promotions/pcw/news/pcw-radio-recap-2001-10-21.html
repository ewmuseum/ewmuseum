PCW Radio Recap

21 October 2001

PCW Radio Recap Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Oct 2001 ---- Welcome to the Recap of PCW’s Malice. Technical difficulties made it hard to hear the show, so recapping it will be difficult. There were two of us working on the recap, and both had troubles hearing. In fact, it seemed everyone did. Still, I think these are the major points. The show opens with Free Bird by Lynard Skynard Duke opens the show by hyping the PPV. He did a run down through the match, which is always enjoyable. The first caller was Alexei Ruslan - Duke made a crack about the Space Race. - Ruslan agreed with Duke that Rec October is a “Now or Never” situation for Ivan. TPB called in next - TPB and Duke talked about the LL tournament and all the matches. - They talked about Kramer Wild Wing for quite some time. TPB said that Wild Wing is a good wrestler but “His workrate SUCKS.” - TPB plugged the column “The Doghouse” calling it on e of the best columns in PCW. This prompted Duke to rant on Shootking; something he would do throughout the night. -TPB's wife got on the line, wishing everyone well for the PPV. There were a few calls after TPB but most were either impossible to hear, or had absolutely no point. Among the callers were Ed and Unstoppable Eugene. Former Wrestling Superstar, current unemployed King of Madness, RANDY SAVAGE visited the studio. It seems Randy is currently looking for a job in PCW, despite repeated rejections by CEO Kremmen due to his advanced age and annoying tendencies. - Randy announced that he was going to give out some PPV predictions. - Randy goes against the grain, saying Kid Wonder and Snow will defeat Meanstreak and Ivan. - Randy says that he feels Team CEO will overcome Jeremy Howard and rent-a-Hero. -Randy says that Vampir Nosferatu will win Duke’s Continental title, prompting Duke to throw Randy out of the studio. Duke asks for Rent-a-Hero to call, and goes on to rip him. Duke takes a moment to rip the Shootking. Duke plugs the Doghouse, and goes through the PPV-related parts of the column which can be found on the PCW site, under Columns (The Doghouse #4). Duke ends the show.
