Aymin Kidnapping

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aymin Kidnapping Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Mar 2002 ---- Enraged by the shaving of his afro, Flaymin Aymin last night took a big step in gaining retaliation by kidnapping....err...'dognapping' the world famous Taco Bell Dog. Early police reports indicate that 'yelping' sounds were overheard from the Chihuahua's palace late last night. When contacted on his cell-phone by PCW Officials, Flaymin had the following to say: "El Diablo might have shaved my afro, but i now have his beloved, prized possession- The Taco Bell Dog!! Shortly, I will be releasing ransom details and if El Diablo doesn't fully comply then I will flush the mutt down the toilet...." Currently, police are in the midst of a nation wide hunt for Flaymin and the dog but are confident that he won't get past the Mexican border with the dog still in his possession. The Mexican police are also led to believe that Flaymin may be in cahoots with 'The Burrito Boys' who attempted to kidnap the pint-sized talking dog late last year. For the full story tune in this Wednesday night for the TV special titled 'When idiots kidnap celebrities'.
