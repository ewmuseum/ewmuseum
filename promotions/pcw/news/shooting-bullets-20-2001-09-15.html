Shooting Bullets #20 Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Sep 2001 ---- All expressions and opinions in this article are solely those of The Shoot King, a ham sandwich, and actor/comedian Nipsy Russell. Anything written can not be used without the consent of The Shoot King. Any duplication of this article will result in your ass on a platter. All hate mail and such can be sent to [detail withheld] where it can and will be used in a future article. (This bonafide column is dedicated in the honor of all those who lost their lives in the horrific tragedy of Tuesday morning.) I'd be remiss if I didn't talk a little about what went down Tuesday morning. What can I say? It was one of the most amazing things I've ever seen. And I don't mean "amazing" in a good sense either. And seeing it all unfold live on TV was creepy. Watching as the first tower burned, then the plane comes and hits the 2nd tower. And all the events that unfolded after that was just a site to see. I was glued in front of my TV while not being able to get on-line (stupid dial-up connections). I'm just now starting to get back to normal life. But, hell, I gotta give the terorists, and especially Osama bin Laden credit. No, I don't condone their actions by any means at all. But I gotta give credit where credit is due. These guys successfully pissed off the United States. It takes a lot to do that and they did it. Too bad they won't get to feel the joys of getting credit from Shooter since they'll all be dead in due time. USA! USA! USA! My 20th Column Yes, this is Shooter's 20th column. The longest reigning PCW columnist, and all around lovable guy, is doing is 20th column today for all of you. And, no, this is not like a Robbie Decker 20th column where he skips 15-19. 20 legit columns done by myself. Too bad I didn't keep them all. Anyways, I'd say it's an honor, but writing for a roster filled with many jobbers isn't very fun. So you all should be honored that I'm doing this. Back to Old School Doesn't this have the makings of the worst PPV in PCW history? A main event few actually care about. Matches that make no sense. And, of course, predictability down the line. As for predictions, you can find them here. And the build-up for the PPV was just terrible. This has the makings of a giant crapfest. Way to enter PCW's 2nd year. Soy Boy's Death Why did Soy Boy have to die? It's depressing to see such a great talent be killed by such crap. And, no, Snow didn't kill Soy Boy because Snow's not crap. Let's all have a moment of silence for Soy Boy. Who Do I Mark Out For? With Judas Steele dead and Putter Mayhem gone, I need someone to mark out for. Quite frankly, I don't see anyone worthy of me marking out for. Jeremy Howard was mean to me recently, so he gets nothing. And others who I'd mark out for seem to be on their way out. So, people, who do I mark out for? Any nominations for yourself are acceptable. Nominations for other people are acceptable, too. Whoever I feel is worthy of me marking out for will get my love. Who to Hate Since Guy Leslie is leaving, and I don't need cheap shots to knock him out, I will no longer have the Guy Leslie Bash of the Moment section. However, with Guy's depature that leaves an opening. I will be watching very closely at all your suckiness to determine who gets to replace Guy Leslie as my bitch. Right now, the leader is Nikita Morozov, but the race is far from over. Whoever I deem worthy of this great honor will get it. The winner will be announced in my next column rather harshly. I wish you all the best of luck. I Get Mail As you might know, MTV has this community service thing set up. With this system you can make a user profile. Now, guys, I'm sure you've seen the kind of crap MTV shows on TV. MTV seems to have no guidelines at all. So, when I got this, you can understand why I laughed. Dear (Shooter), Thanks for creating a profile on MTV.com. A moderator has reviewed it, and found that either the "Scoop" or "Keywords" section is not in line with MTV.com's Community Guildelines. Until you edit the non-compliant sections(s), that part of your profile will be hidden from other site visitors. WHAT THE FUCK!?! What did I say to get this e-mail? All i talked about was your momma, sex, and other good things that MTV ain't afraid to talk about. But, for some reason, I don't meet their guidelines. Well, FUCK MTV! They don't even play good videos much. 5 straight days of music videos and it has been the same stuff over and over. If I have to see the new U2 video one more time I'm gonna kill someone! That is all Quote of the Week Badge had a good one, but it got lost when all the posts got deleted on the board. So I had to go to the DWF for this. "Oh well, I can always play with myself." -[name withheld] Paul, there is nothing wrong with playing with yourself. Just ask THE Multiple Man. Eternally Cool Quotes "Do you have any idea how hard it is to get a title off a guy from Calgary? Ask Vince McMahon." -Lance Storm "If dynamite were dangerous, do you think they'd sell it to an idiot like me?" -Al Bundy "Scribble.....Scribble.....Delete" -Scribble Moment of the Week The moment of the week isn't a good one. Of course, it was the tragic events of Tuesday, mainly watching the WTC towers fall. I think that will be a moment nobody will ever forget. Eternally Cool Moment Inside the ring, Hix immediately goes after Frisco. Hix swings wildly at Frisco, hitting him with a bunch of rights and lefts, but Hix's overall weakness does nothing to hurt the multi-time PCW title holder. Then, with just his pinky, Frisco nudges Hix and knoks him down and out. Dave: FINGER POKE OF DEATH! FINGER POKE OF DEATH! Jeff: Yeah! We haven't seen that one since Hogan vs. Nash! "While the City Sleeps" by MC 900 Ft. Jesus hits as the crowd is still going nuts from the FINGER POKE OF DEATH~! As Trashcan Man makes his way out, Frisco covers Hix inside the ring. Referee Buzz Meacham counts, 1......2.......3! STEWART HIX HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Hix is tossed from the ring by Frisco as TCM gets to ringside. TCM grabs Hix and hits him with the PYRO DRIVER ON THE CONCRETE compressing Hix's spine and putting a small crack in the concrete. TCM goes into the ring as medics attend to Hix who is lying in a pool of his own blood. -Dream Card...June 29, 2001 That's all from me for now. On a final note, Mr. Outlaw lacks HTML knowledge. Just look at his column and you can see that. LATER!
