Loc as "Bright" as Sunshine?

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Loc as "Bright" as Sunshine? Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Jun 2002 ---- After the outburst from Loc ridiculing the supposed "Washed-Up" Cruiserweight Champion, "Sunshine" Juan Borro, it would seem that he himself has a little explaining to do! Doctor Derek Ramerez who was a viewer of the show on cable saw this and rang up the PCW Hotline leaving these comments: "Not only have i treated Charlie Loc for Drug Abuse in the past but he assured me that he was clean and he would try help his fellow superstars who may be in the same situation as him...this being part of an agreement to go on with his wrestling career without letting his history of narcotics getting in the way! It would seem Charlie has just blamed everything that he had done onto Juan or "Sunshine" as he is referred to which basically put both men into the spotlight! Not only has Loc attracted more attention to himself but this is prompting me to ask PCW Representatives for private talks with both men reguarding the serious issues of drugs!" Indeed the Doctor is right, young children watch these superstars and mimmick them, Drugs are not something children should be seeing! Loc was not able to be caught during his "escapade" last night but maybe after hearing this will think about the reprocussions of his actions!
