Enigma Speaks on Aymin's Lack of Focus

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enigma Speaks on Aymin's Lack of Focus Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Apr 2003 ---- Shortly after Friday Fusion had aired, PCW officials were approached by Enigma looking to speak his mind on come issues. Enigma: You all saw it on Fusion, Flaymin has no focus. All he is focused on is getting women naked when he should be focused on that weasel, El Diablo. The gold-masked Enigma was looking very angered as he spoke to the officials. Enigma: See, Flaymin needs to focus on El Diablo because if he doesn't it could be his downfall. El Diablo is out there somewhere plotting away while Flaymin is hosting parties, he has no focus. I've tried on numerous occasions to get him focused, but to no avail, so that is why I am going publicly on the record to tell my friend Aymin that he needs to be focused on the matter, especially after El Diablo will now get to name the stipulation of his choice for their inevitable match at Spring Fling. Enigma then walked away very frustrated. Does Flaymin have no focus? Will El Diablo name himself to fight Flaymin Aymin in a match of his choice at Spring Fling? More on this as it becomes available to us.
