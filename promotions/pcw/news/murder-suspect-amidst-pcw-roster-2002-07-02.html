Murder Suspect Amidst PCW Roster

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Murder Suspect Amidst PCW Roster Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Jul 2002 ---- As people across the World would've seen at Gold & Glory, Juan "Sunshine" Borro not only lost his Title....but ended or rather had his career ended! As the lights cut a shot was heard and Juan was later found dead in the seat of his car whilst one person who he had argued with moments ealier stood out like a sore thumb....that man was Charlie Loc! Surprisingly enough, Charlie was not the one taken away in cuffs, it was none other than Juan Borro's only brother...El Romantico! According to Police Officials a Murder Trial will begin but will be kept secret until enough solid evidence is given to convict Borro's Murderer! Whatever the case, Charlie Loc is definately someway involved and is needed in this trial! More when available....
