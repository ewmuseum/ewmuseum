White Mexican Re-signed

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
White Mexican Re-signed Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Feb 2002 ---- As we all saw at the end of Friday Fusion, the White Mexican returned to PCW in shocking fashion by attacking Tyler Nelson and ramming him head first into the Hummer limo. But, wasn't he fired? As it turns out, Mexi was the one who filed the wrongful termination lawsuit against the CEO of PCW and won. As part of the judgement handed down by Circuit Court Judge Evenhand, PCW was ordered to hire Mexi back immediately and pay him for the time he was unemployed. Mexi was also awarded other compensatory damages as a result of the verdict, most of which are unknown at this time. PCW President Duke Williams reinstated Mexi's contract just prior to Fusion, and we all saw the result as he took out some of his anger on Nelson as the CEO was leaving the arena. An irate CEO/Commissioner promptly booked a match for next weeks Fusion pitting himself against the newly re-hired White Mexican in a No DQ match-up. Mexi has been unavailable for comment, but the CEO had plenty to say. "That f*cking cockroach thinks he can just waltz back in here like he's hot sh*t? He wants to attack me from behind like the coward he is? Well, next Friday I'm going to kick his burrito eating ass all over the Goddamn arena. Then as soon as my lawyers get this mess straightened out I'm gonna fire his worthless ass all over again!" This situation has reached a boiling point in a hurry and we'll be keeping a close eye on it as it continues to rage.
