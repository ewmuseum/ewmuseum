World Title News

20 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
World Title News Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Mar 2003 ---- Last night, after the airing of Wednesday Turmoil, next week's card was announced and, contrary to what many expected, there will be no tournament to crown a new champion. However, Jeremy Howard did book one hell of a main event for Turmoil, one that features 10 of PCW's best and WILL crown the new champion. Clyde, Rent-A-Hero, Totally Tubular Tommy, Venneman Pendragon, Xavier Kannon, Akiyama, Chet King, El Diablo, Skylar Green, and Violence Jack will take place in a match with, literally, no rules. The first man to win a fall thusly becomes the PCW World Champion. Not only does this match feature 2 former champs in Clyde and XK, but it was signed by new co-owner Jeremy Howard, a former PCW champ in his own right. We also have seen the problems Kannon and JKH still have with each other. Throw in the blood feud between Skylar and Jack, mix it with the brewing vendetta between Tommy and Pendragon, throw in some insane action with Clyde, add your monster in the form of the nVg...and you've got one of the craziest matches in PCW history to crown the 33rd champion. Tune in this week for more updates on this situation.
