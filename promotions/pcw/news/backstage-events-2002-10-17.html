17 October 2002

Backstage Events Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Oct 2002 ---- Back in the office of PCW’s Commissioneress, Brandi Wilson is seated, filling out some paperwork. The clock ticks by slowly, as Brandi looks up, spotting Nathan entering the room… Brandi Wilson: Hi sweetie! How are things! Nathan does not answer, a grave look on his face. He merely gestures to the clock, and falls to the plush carpeting face-first, as she lets out a shriek. Brandi Wilson: NATHAN! She immediately moves around her desk to his side, kneeling, looking for what caused his collapse. She finds it, in a piece of thin wood, wrapped in barbed wire. She pulls it out of his neck, looking quizzically at the weapon… Brandi Wilson: When I find out who did this….I’m going to have Mr. Walden tax him to the cleaners! Suddenly remembering Nathan’s final gesture, she looks up at the clock….as a feeling of dread permeates her every pore… Brandi Wilson: 10.……18.… The attack is blindingly fast, as the black robed attacker comes from around the corner, smashing the remaining piece of the barbed wire wrapped wooden object into Miss Wilson’s forehead. She collapses, but being a rather tough person, climbs back to her feet, and drills the attacker in the face with a right hand. He staggers back, and drops his weapon. Brandi steps back to charge, as her own blood begins to drip into her mouth.. Brandi Wilson: You BASTARD! You’re going to pay for this! Wilson charges for a Brandispear, but the attacker nimbly dodges, sending Brandi crashing into a potted plant. The robed man stands behind her, waiting for her to rise. As she does so, he turns her around, catching her in a picture perfect reverse full nelson, turning the move into a suplex. Brandi goes flying over head, bouncing off her desk, and hitting her already-bleeding forehead on the wall. The attacker approaches, his steps seemingly taking minutes, as Brandi raises her hands, as if to stop him. He goes into his pocket, and removes…… Two pieces of paper. He lays each on the desk…and their headings are quite evident. One reads “PCW Contract of Employment”, the other, “Contract for Participation in Mall Brawl.” He beckons Brandi closer, placing a pen next to the contracts, and with his other hand, brandishes a lead pipe.. Brandi Wilson: I……I……….I have no choice…. The battered commissioneress of PCW stands weakly, moving over to her chair, and takes the pen. She signs her name on both contracts, and looks up at the man bitterly. Brandi Wilson: Are you happy now? The man shakes his head no, and promptly lunges into a super kick, toppling Brandi’s chair, and sending her to the ground once again. This time, the man produces an empty Elmer’s Glue container, and kneels by Brandi, scooping some of her blood into the container. From his pocket he pulls a crayon, and affixes his name to both contracts…..eight straight lines to form the letters of his name. He now turns his attention to the glue container, and with one gulp, drinks the blood held within. On the desk, he scrawls a message, and then, making sure that Brandi is still incapacitated, turns to leave the room, contracts in hand. As he turns left out of the office, Tim Walden is making his way down the hall…to speak with Brandi Tim Walden: So, Brandi, I was thinking, next we could institute a “Breathing” tax…and…Brandi? Brandi? BRANDI!!!!! Entering, Walden observes the scene of the crime.. He goes to aid her, but is stopped by the crayoned message on the desk…one that fills him with dread…. “10/18. Time wears thin. Your taxing days draw to a close. Hugs! ??????”
