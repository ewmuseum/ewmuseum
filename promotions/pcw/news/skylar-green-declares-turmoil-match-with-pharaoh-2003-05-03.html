Skylar Green Declares Turmoil Match with Pharaoh

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Skylar Green Declares Turmoil Match with Pharaoh Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 May 2003 ---- Skylar Green indicated after Friday Fusion ended that Tuesday Turmoil would see a tag team match between Skylar and Pharaoh, with each man tagging with a partner of their choice. Skylar announced James Edwards as his partner, siting the potential talent of the Rising Star, and his good nature. Pharaoh responded by decreeing that Sivasian Krikorian was his tag team partner, saying that having a rookie as his partner means that he [Pharaoh] can dictate the match at all times. Someone later contacted Krikorian and told him about it. Krikorian is reported to have just shook his head and called Pharaoh a twit. So the match for Turmoil is a tag match: Skylar Green & James Edwards against The Pharaoh and Sivasian Krikorian. Both Skylar Green and The Pharaoh are unsatisfied with their Fusion match ending, with Totally Tubular Tommy ruining the match by hitting both men with a steel chair. They promise that there will be another match between the two in singles competition to make up for it. Next Friday Fusion will feature Skylar and The Pharaoh in a Submission match, but in a booking snafu, Totally Tubular Tommy has been booked as the guest referee. We can only guess how this will turn out.
