Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mexi's Final Moment Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 May 2002 ---- Well MexiMarks and AntiMexiMarks, this column is a very special one for me. I wanted this to go out on the one-year anniversary of my debut in PWA but alas I was unable to get it done so here it is two days late. Now the main reason for writing this column as is, is not to tribute my career in PCW but to show everyone that PWA works and I’m living proof. I came to PWA on May 10, 2001 and had very little RP experience at all. I had just gotten my acceptance into PWA which seeing PCW on the top of flwnet I thought I had some how been accepted to PCW. At that time I had no clue what I was getting myself into. All I wanted when I signed up for PCW was to get my own poser. I won’t lie when I started I was in it for the picture and that was it. Ironically I would never get a poser made for me that I didn’t make but that’s how it all started out for me. So May 10, 2001 my first PWA RP… The camera fades in to see a couple walk into the PWA around 5 am, a janitor is cleaning up in off to the side. The man is impressive looking, standing 6’2 and quite defined. His skin is a very pale white and his hair is a very light blonde. He is wearing black sweat pants, and a black shirt, and he has a gym bag hung of his shoulder. She is a couple inches shorter then him and quite skinny. She’s wearing a pair of black sunglasses and has shoulder length reddish brown hair. She two is dressed in all black. The two of them walk to the back and enter the change rooms. The camera follows the man in side and as he sits down, we can see a scar across his right eye, from his eyebrow to his upper lip. The man is tying up his boots when he notices the camera. White Mexican: “What do you want? You want to know who I am and what I’m doing here right?” The camera moves up and down. White Mexican: “Well, since it’s my first day and you caught me out of character I won’t give you my normal treatment. Your lucky, you do this again and you might get hurt. Today however I will tell you who I am, where I came from and why I’m here…” He leans back and rubs his chin as he thinks back and prepares to tell his story White Mexican: “My parents were both Canadians, they grew up in Calgary, Alberta. Sweethearts in high school and soon after graduation they got married, they were both 20 years old when they took there honeymoon to Tijuana, Mexico and I was about nine months and ready to come out. So while they were honeymooning, I spoiled the party and my mother gave birth to me on Feb.23, 1978. They decided it would be in my best interest to stay in Tijuana for a year, rather then fly back to Canada. Unfortunately that never happened on my first birthday…” He stops and pauses for a second trying to hold back tears. White Mexican: “Sorry, on my first birthday they were going out to get me a gift and that was the last I ever saw of them. They disappeared and I was on my own, somewhat. I had a friend and her name was Mona, she is still my best friend to this day. She was the one I walked in with. Well anyway her father just happened to be the legendary Ultimo Dragon, and he was kind enough to take me in and raise me as my own. For some strange reason though, instead of giving me water to drink he gave me tequila. It was just natural by the time I was five to drink tequila, it was my water.” He reaches down into his gym bag, pulls out a tequila bottle and has a drink, and then continues. White Mexican: “So as I grew up Mona and I became closer and the Ultimo Dragon taught me how to wrestle. At first the very basics, but by the time I was 13 I already knew how to perform some lucha libra moves. I loved the wrestling but the pain that it caused, made me hate it. I’ll admit it I was a p*ssy. Along with wrestling he taught me to speak English. So time moved on and I got older and I had a pretty good childhood, all things considered. One day however I received a letter telling me that my parents were in Calgary, Alberta. It also told me the cemetery that they had been buried in for the past 17 years too. I felt that I owed it to them to visit there grave, so for the next year and a bit I did all I could to get money. I stole from tourists, I worked in the street, any place I could get a peso I would be. Finally I had enough money to get me there so I hitch hiked my way North and made it to Calgary a month later. I was able to pay my respects and get on with my life. I figured I would try to get a job in Calgary for a while, but it wasn’t easy. I finally had found a job cleaning toilets for an elementary school when something went wrong.” The man takes a deep breath and then continues on. White Mexican: “I woke up one mourning and my body was numb, the same feeling you get in your legs when you sit on the toilet too long, except my whole body. I was told by my boss to go see the school nurse and she immediately sent me to go see a doctor. The doctor ran a number of tests on me and by the end of them I didn’t even feel the numbness anymore. I felt nothing, no feeling at all. I can’t explain how weird it is to touch something with your hand and know it’s in your palm, and yet not feel it. The doctor broke the news to me that my nervous system was destroyed. He figured it was a direct result from drinking too much tequila. At first I was depressed and confused, but in time I learnt how to deal with it, and I realized how lucky I was. From the day I met the Ultimo Dragon I had wanted to be a wrestler, but I couldn’t take the pain. Now because of what happened, I didn’t have to worry about pain anymore. Furthermore I was living in Calgary, the home of Stu Hart and the dungeon. So I went off and was able to convince him to train me. After several years of training in the dungeon, I felt I was ready.” He cracks his neck side to side. White Mexican: “At the age of 22 I joined a hardcore federation, and using my technical ability, combined with the lucha libra training I had, and of course the lack of feeling I dominated, and rose through the ranks quickly. I enjoyed being on top, but it wasn’t good enough. It wasn’t were I wanted to be so I went back to Mexico and talked Mona into coming with me, and we decided to start over. To start back at the beginning with the best federation out there. That’s how we wound up here at the Academy. The reason we’re here so early, well that’s an easy one. To get a jump on the competition. To make sure we are in the top of the class. This is my dream, and I am living it, and if anyone tries to take this away from me. Well you’ll just have to wait and see if someone does.” He finishes tying up his boots, and then looks back at the camera. White Mexican: “Oh right I totally forgot, you can call me Mexi, but my wrestling name is The White Mexican, and so you are warned I am a heel, and if you think we will ever have a nice little sit down chat again you are mistaken. Now get out of here, go talk to Mona or something.” The cameraman leaves the room to see Mona warming up, she is now in her wrestling attire. She has a red halter top on with a leather jacket. Her pants are black with the word tequila in blood red on the legs. The White Mexican then walks out in his attire to. He has the same style of pants, but he has a red sleeveless shirt on with a black mesh tank over top. We can also now see a tattoo on his upper right arm of blood red barbwire. The two go into the ring and start practicing moves on each other, they take a break and Mexi shouts down to us. White Mexican: “Someday everyone will fear me, but for now I will warn you.” Mona: “Fear the Mexican, cuz he does not know fear himself.” White Mexican: “Tequila!” The camera fades out.
