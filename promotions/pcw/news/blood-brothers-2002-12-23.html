Blood Brothers

23 December 2002

Blood Brothers Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Dec 2002 ---- Toronto, Canada- Many saw Decimation as El Diablo’s last throw of the dice as far as the PCW World Title went. After failing to defeat the demon known only as Clyde at Alliances, the Mexican Devil was given a 2nd chance at Decimation, with many others snapping at Clyde’s heels ready to take the next shot if he would fail... But there were other eyes on Clyde in the Skydome, eyes that had both shed blood alongside him, and fought to the death against him, could Diablo pull out the win, or would it once again be the demon walking out with Gold around his waist? Or would anyone even be walking out? Also at Decimation, the RUSH World Title was decided and the camp it will sit in chosen, Xavier Kannon and Duke Williams take each other to the boundaries of their very physical being while their allies try to sway the outcome, the PCW Continental and RUSH Syndicate Titles are decided in Jeopardy matches that threatened to steal the show, while Dark Gimmick tries to start his PPV kill count.
