News Tidbits

7 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News Tidbits Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Jan 2002 ---- Jane Regan just released this report a few moments ago from her office: This week is an interesting one, not only because during this week we will see the PCW return of Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman who recently spent three weeks in Shanghai, China hyping up Shang-haied on the 27th of this month; but because on Fusion Kid Wonder, Duke Williams and The Reverend all make their PCW in-ring returns. On a slightly drier note, PCW2 Veteran Dustin Downey has been released due to his poor workrate recently. On an additional note, PWA Star Kid Wonder has not been signed by PCW, but instead Duke Williams has been added to the PWA as a wrestler by CEO Tyler Nelson. Therefore the Hell in a Cell match for this Fusion is in fact a PWA match scheduled on a PCW card. This is the first time this has happened in history, but Tyler stated that it might be a growing trend to see "who cuts the mustard in the academy". More updates to follow.
