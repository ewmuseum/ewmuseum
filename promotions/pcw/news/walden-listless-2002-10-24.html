Walden Listless

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Walden Listless Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Oct 2002 ---- Reports from backstage sources indicate that recently, PCW Financial Reclamation Executive Tim Walden has grown rather listless at the offices of the federation in recent days. This is thought to be connected with Walden's series of recent failures to retaliate successfully against the efforts of the returning Hardcore Isoceles Trapezoid, as well as the events of Tuesday Turmoil 9 days ago when he was stripped to his underwear and tied to his chair by Teresa Tomas. When asked to elaborate, our source, who for anonymity's sake we'll refer to as "Not Tim's secretary" said: "It's just awful to see him in the office..He's always laying in his chair with this horrible glazed disinterest on his face, and if he has something to do, he just doesn't have the gusto that he had a couple days ago..He's always muttering something about "that damn Trapezoid", if anything beyond what the business requires, and it's really rather depressing to see, like his life's enthusiasm was sucked out or something." We may bring you more on this story as new information comes in.
