The Scoop #3 Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Jan 2002 ---- Welcome back to the PCW Scoop, the place where you know that whether you like the opinion or not, it is honest. I am sorry that it has been so long since the last time I put up a column. Work has been very time consuming of late. There is a lot to talk about this time around with the Pay Per View on the horizon, several rookies to keep track of, along with the usual events in the world of e-wrestling. But before I start, I feel that I must address the Vampir Nosferatu situation. If this were retaliation, I would have put it on the board. The situation was misunderstood. People thought I was attacking things that I was not attacking. The only thing that I attacked was the fact that Vampir Nosferatu was writing stories that did not involve his character and had a loose relation to PCW and its storylines at best. But what I feel I must address here are the people who went off on me for stating an opinion (and yes, 90% of those people did not even know what the argument was about) and saying that I should not have said it. If you do not want to read opinions, then do not read columns. Isn’t that why you read these columns? If I were to just praise every wrestler in PCW, then how could a wrestler actually take that praise seriously? I will continue to criticize people, even when the majority of PCW can not stray from their dicks long enough to take an objective view. What they do with that criticism is up to them. This is only a column. There is a simple point, I will put my honest opinion in these columns, and quite frankly, I don’t give a fuck about your feelings. Now that that business is out of the way, why don’t we move on to something a little more pleasant? How about some rookie talk? Here are who I see as the top three rookies (two months of PCW experience or less) in PCW right now... Top Rookies 1. Paddy Irishman 2. Bobby Stormbringer 3. Xavier Kannon Why do I order them so? Xavier Kannon: Xavier might yet prove himself to be the most talented of the three, as shown through little ingeniously comedic comments like, “Poon Puppy” and “Shag Squirrel.” Overall, however, I do not think that he has matched the two I put above him thus far. Even so, Kannon has shown flashes of brilliance, like his “Columbian Passtimes” RP, which was one of a rare few that have made him look like the future World Champion that he is billed to be. I think Kannon is a victim of the workrate disease. It seems that certain fads come into PCW. When Kid Wonder was everyone’s favorite wrestler, we saw RP length skyrocket. Then, when Jeremy Howard and Ivan Stanislav began posting unreal numbers for every match, everyone in PCW was trying to boost their workrate. Well, I hate to be the bearer of bad news, but not everyone can post one or two RP’s a day and keep their quality at a top notch level. With a little experience, and a shot of motivation, it gets easier to post one or more RP’s a day. But until a wrestler can do that without seeing their quality slip, they should not “force” themself to RP just to get a higher count. A high RP count will get you the win in a match, but high quality will make a career for you. Xavier Kannon’s workrate skyrocketed a few weeks back, and at the same time, the quality of his work was taxed. I am still not sold on Xavier Kannon. At times, he displays championship quality talent. But are these flashes just that? Flashes? Or is it just a somewhat rocky start to what will be the great career of Xavier Kannon (mind you, it has not been rocky in the cards)? Either way, no one could live up to the hype he was packing. Strengths: Great writer, good comedy Weaknesses: RP’s can be dry and tedious Bobby Stormbringer: I really think that Bobby Stormbringer is the best of the three at this point. Why isn’t he at number one? Take a look at his “Driven Part I” RP. It is nothing more than a bean (bean for the count, get it?). If it was not a bean, posted for no other reason than to be added to his post count, then why didn’t he just combine the first and second parts? I refresher course, as he put it, is fine if it is combined with a real RP. If it is nothing more than a refresher course, then there is a place for it, in his bio. It does not belong on the RP board. Other than that nasty little incident, B.S has been the most impressive newbie in PCW. His old school RP style is refreshing, and his comedic wit can be something to behold at times. “FRAIL OLD MAN: Wha? Son, I don't care if your name is wicka, wicka, Slim Shady.” (Excerpt from a B.S RP). It has been a long time since someone has come up with something that clever. Even with his colorfully clever RP’s, B.S does have a slight problem with dialogue. He often puts in things that the reader really does not care about, and his character seems like a fourteen year old punk at times. Lately, B.S has taken a turn for the worse. Maybe it’s because he’s trying to keep up an unreal workrate? Maybe it’s because he was a flash in the pan. Who knows? But for now, lets assume this is just a small slump. With experience at this level, those problems will soon fade. They’re hardly noticeable now, thanks to the superb appearance of his RP’s. It is not the black background, for those of you hoping for a quick fix to sloppy RP’s, but it is the ease in which they are read, and how well they flow. If B.S works out his minor problems, then he will one day be the PCW Champ. No question in my mind. Strengths: RP appearance, good comedy Weaknesses: Poorly written dialogue at times, notion that useless RP’s will help him Paddy O’shea: The sentimental favorite, Paddy O’shea has the character to take him a long way. Mix that with excellent, yet still raw, RP skill, and we have ourselves a blue chipper. I really think B.S is a little better over all, but Paddy has been the man when it comes to PCW newcomers so far. He plays his character as well as Ivan plays his. Using the word “feck” as his version of a catch phrase gives him a hysterically funny edge. From the first time I read one of Paddy’s RP’s, when he was still in PWA, I was hooked. You could tell, even back then, that this man could well be a future PCW World Champ. As I said earlier, Paddy’s strength lies in his hilarious character; a smallish, near middle aged, Irishman with a temper. The best part is, his RP’s even smell Irish. The speech, the use of green and gold... And who could forget the loveable little bastard’s pot of gold? Paddy’s only weakness is that his skills are still raw. His dialogue often seems to be written in short chops, which take a bit of color from an RP that is otherwise exploding with it. He needs to give his characters more to say, without letting them dry up the RP. He could learn a lot from a guy like Jeremy Howard, whose RP’s are loaded with dialogue, yet it’s all important and related to the storyline. It is a fault that Paddy will soon jump like a hurdle, and when he does, PCW’s main eventers had better keep an eye on him. In fact, Paddy’s latest RP’s are already showing major improvement although I think he would be better off toning down the workrate and focussing on pure quality. Strengths: Excellent handling of a gimmick, excellent gimmick Weaknesses: Choppy one line dialogue Shanghaied 2 PCW has done a wonderful job getting Shanghaied 2 off the ground. It has a wonderful mixture of freshness and tradition and a talent packed card that looks like it will impress. The thing that makes this card not only stand out, but also makes it better than recent PCW cards, is the fact that it is nice and neat. There is lots of one on one action, and management didn’t feel obligated to putting jobbers in just for the sake of the compiled jobber ego in PCW. One match that I wish was added to Shanghaied 2 is Timo vs. Boda. The match went down on Fusion, why not postpone it until the PPV? I guess it is too late now. Rising Star Title I won’t go through all of the matches or all of the contenders. All I will say is that Paddy O’Shea will win. He’s just too good. There are others in the match, like B.S, who show promise, but lately, even B.S’s flare seems to be wearing out. Half of the people in this match aren’t even in PCW anymore. So that narrows down the choices. All in all, I like the way this one is shaping up. Some of the new faces are really trying to make a name for themselves. I can not say that I am impressed with Red Rock or Young Tiger, but I am impressed by how they have gone all out since coming to PCW. And when you want to be in PCW that bad, great things can happen for you. It is always refreshing to see people with the desire to do well, and there are a lot of PCW rookies with that desire right now. Winner: Paddy Irishman Man vs. Machine I can not say that I am a fan of this match and I have followed it loosely at best. Neither team has really gone all out for the match, so I am not too sure that it has sparked motivation in them either. Twisted Metal write solid RP’s, although a touch more description would probably help the reader along. Winners: Twisted Metal Extreme Title Match I would not call Brandi a great Extreme Champion, but she exceeded my expectations by miles. Too bad she’s disappearing right when it counts. Brandi will lose the Extreme title. There is a case to be made for Hank~! who put in a wonderful effort like we haven’t seen from PCW’s resident jobber in months. If Hank~! keeps this up, we may someday look at him differently, and am serious about that. Hank~! deserves credit for his performance. Though his RP’s have not been excellent, he has definitely exceeded all expectations and made a good run for it. I don’t think he will be able to keep the title away from Corey Williams though. CCW has looked drained of late, and I think he is still getting used to his new revamped workrate. Still, he has without a doubt been the best in this match, and continues to turn heads. Winner: CCW CW Title It has been a long time since someone has brought as much credibility to the CW title as Xavier Kannon has in recent weeks. And things in that division are really heated now, with Chris Chase on the scene. I think these guys will soon be moved to the TV division to help beef it up. Xavier Kannon has been very good, but Chris Chase has shown that he has what it takes to someday hold the PCW World Title. Chase’s work, for the most part, has been excellent, and his ten RP’s have shown that he can put up a solid number without having the quality sucked out. Chase’s “Vomiting” RP was great (although I think vomiting at the sound of Tim Walden’s name may have been pushing too much) and his latest RP’s have been somewhere between good and very good. Xavier Kannon has been victimized a tad by his workrate, in my opinion. Popular opinion says that Xavier will soon be a star in PCW. I don’t doubt that, but it doesn’t mean Xavier shouldn’t be looking to improve either. I can not help but think that Mr. Kannon’s performance would have been much more impressive had he compacted his work to a few less RP’s. TV Title One minute, Tim Walden looks like he is really going to make something of his career, and the next, it appears that he is stuck in neutral again. When Tim Walden RP’s hard, he can be a force. But Flaymin’ Aymin has been the only force in this match. Winner: Aymin Pleasantville Deathmatch Neither Kriegman or Meyers seem to want this to win this match and/or they don’t have time for it. Whatever the case, I can’t judge anything that I haven’t read. Playboy Bunny Lumberjack Match With Mexican working on other feds, and Chet King showing that he’s a good person by not burying Mexican, this feud hasn’t been exciting as it could have been. Still, there’s no doubt that both of these wrestlers have a very promising future in PCW. I’m going to take the Mexican in this match, just because I think having him as the Continental Champ would be a good step for the division. First Death Match This one promises to be brutal, and I am looking forward to it. Although neither man has put out a large quantity of RP’s, both men know how to write quality. Darkstar’s RP’s are kind of like searching a gothic soul. I am becoming more and more a Darkstar fan with each passing RP. I wish he would write more of them. Clyde is the king of the dark gimmick in PCW but to be honest, I don’t remember a single RP from him for Shanghaied 2. Winner: Darkstar National Pride Match Ivan Stanislav wrote two of the worst he has ever written in the last week or so. There are people who can’t get into PCW who have written better RP’s than those, especially the first. And then, the Russian comes back and follows them up with two examples of his best work. The part about being unable to get through a metal detetor because of suspenders, in “Fickle Americans”, was both clever and humorous. Miyagawa has only two RP’s up as the time dwindles away, but they’ve both been strong. Winner: Ivan World Title Snow and Jeremy Howard have taken a face vs. face feud and made it as interesting as any face vs. heel feud. Of course the feud isn’t without its faults, but for the most part, it’s been strong. And the RP’s for the match have been great as well. Both men have put up a stellar amount of RP’s without losing much to quality. At the moment, they each have eleven solid RP’s with a handful from each man qualifying as great. Jeremy Howard one of, if not the, greatest PCW champ of all time. Snow will have other chances, but at Shanghaied 2, Jeremy Howard will retain. Winner: Jeremy Howard Duke Williams vs. Tyler Nelson If you think RP’s matter here, you’re kidding yourself. This one is about the angle, and about the surprise. Plus, the two characters match each other perfectly for a feud. I really don’t know what is going to happen here, although I suspect that something unexpected will come up. Winner: No Contest Well, that is it for this time folks. Scoops Staley
