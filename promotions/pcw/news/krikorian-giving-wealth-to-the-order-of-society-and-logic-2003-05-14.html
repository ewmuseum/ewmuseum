Krikorian Giving Wealth to the Order of Society and Logic? Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 May 2003 ---- It has been reported that Sivasian Krikorian, the Ukrainian born sniper who has teamed up with SOCIETY lately, has given what he had of his wealth to the cult. "Yes, I gave away some money to them. I had planned to use it to help my search for my parents killers, but as Michael Chang informed me of a lack of wealth at the moment within the order, I have decided there is a far greater and needier cause," said Krikorian. Ask about the fact he had joined for financial help in finding his parents, Krikorian shrugged. "It's only a short term investment, when the shares for Squirrel racing and rat meat increased, the Order shall be rich!" More to come if we get it.
