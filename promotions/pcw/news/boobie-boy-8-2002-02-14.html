Boobie Boy #8 Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Feb 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Issue#8 Featuring: Zurick Ok and welcome back to my 8th issue of Boobie Boy. On my way to the interview I was told that I was going to be sitting down with Bloodlust. For those of you who don’t know, Bloodlust is the newest PCW tag team with Zurick and The Vamphaery Ben. I was told to head to Wayne Manor. Now when called to attend this place I continued to ask myself on the car ride over if this was the name of the place that Bruce Wayne A.K.A. Batman owned. Well when I arrived at the gate I realized that my hunch was no coincidence. It really was Batman’s place. So inside I went and found out that T.V.B was unavailable, despite this Zurick still decided to sit down and take some time out and become the 8th victim of 20 Questions. So scroll down and take a look at this surprising and unique interview with Zurick. 1) Unfortunately your partner couldn’t be here tonight. Despite all that it seems that you know him more than anyone else. Who is this new guy that has been recently signed to PCW? Zurick: Well Mat, Ben is an odd to start with. I've known him for about a little over three years now. When you think you know him, he turns around and does something more than before. He's got amazing talent; people are counting him out of his bracket in the Tourney coming up due to Boda, who has been on a killing spree being in the same bracket. I'm actually going to say Vamphaery over Boda, and that's an unbiased opinion. Matt: It’s two T’s. And those seem pretty biased words to me. Boda is perhaps the heavy favorite going in! You think he can beat the current number 1 contender!? Zurick: Takes a sip from his Nestea Cool* See, People aren't really sure what to expect right now in PCW with Ben, but I know him and I know what can do. See, Boda has great talent. I am not putting him down or anything, but I have seen Ben in action, he may be all shits and giggles backstage, but when he's in the ring he's a machine. 2) In the old NTICW you had much success as a World Champion. Then later on you became some what of a fed head, leaving your position as a wrestler. Now though….you are back wrestling. What has changed the most between the NTICW ring to the PCW ring? Zurick: Many things are different. First and most important is competition. You take guys like Mexi, Boda, Jeremy Howard, Snow, Vampir, amongst many others, and you will find a whole new pool of opponents that can not only give you a run for your money, but can probably beat you as well, and this isn't even counting former NTICW wrestlers such as Xavier Kannon. The attitude is a lot different. I was very fond of the NTICW friendliness, but the longer you're in PCW; the friendlier the people are to you. That’s to be expected anywhere though. You can't walk into any ring, claim you're GoD, and expect people to flock to you. Trust me, I know. 3) Speaking of the PCW ring, you seem to be lacking the same amount as success as you have previously. What is the deal with that? Zurick: Two words there, Bad ideas. My first thing in PCW was a tag match with Ken Kaze. We thought it sounded like a cool idea, and flat out it sucked. Another bad idea was joining Clyde's stable and becoming another character I'm really not. That was my fault there. Another thing was I wasn’t going after anything. I was expecting things to come to me, still am in a way. I have been keeping myself quite busy these last few days though, and I hope to stay this way as long as I can. I've had a really bad start; I am really trying to forget it, pick myself up, and move along. I want to rejoin the land of the living. 4) And also about that GoD thing. I am atheist and don’t believe in a god. So technically you don’t exist to me. Where do you get off thinking that you are GoD? Zurick: With questions like those, you're really going to piss me off. Atheist are you?! Atheist only exists because they could not see my divinity. NOW I am here in the flesh, you so called Atheistism, logically can not exist anymore. You people are nothing but god damned ingrates. I bring woman to the world, yet you scorn me. I ask you to follow 10 simple rules, and I feel like half of you people are the dumbest people I've ever met. I wonder why I even made you in my image. I could have made you look like a dog and sniff each other's asses all day. Don't make me sink America like I sunk the world! 5) Tyler Nelson has been in strong power for almost a year and even though he has lost his presidency to the rouge cowboy Duke Williams how does Nelson’s large control of power affect you? Zurick: Oh Man, You hit a nice little topic here. PCW's inside has been screwed up for so long. Not from just Tyler, we can't blame Tyler, though we love to, for some of the actions of Jane Reagan. Look at what she did to the roster? She tried to cripple PCW from the inside. She Re-Upped Hank's salary to $2.4 MILLION. HANK~! I SAY! Money Like that could be used for things such as funding my churches. Buying the homeless food. Hank~! I SAY! Take a man like Duke Williams, president of PCW. He gives Jane's actions a smile just because he's president. What's Duke going to do when PCW goes broke and can't pay HANK~! How much is he going to owe him? Well, I guess he smiled about the roster switches to balance the money problem out! .......What about the children? What about the churches? Hold on a second..... Stop rolling cameras. *Zurick reaches from his pocket and pulls out a small bottle with liquid, he puts it up to his eye then puts it back in his pocket* Ok, I’m good. On 3..... 1.....2......3 ........What about the children? What about the churches? *A tear in Zurick's eye begins to run down his cheek, and his face shows a sad look* Damn DUKE! Damn Jane! Damn Tyler! 6) Jason Snow is the current PCW World Champion, who do you believe will be the one to defeat him? Give your reasons. Zurick: So many decisions, so many decisions. As GoD's own rule for this one moment, I did know what you were going to say, I have decided not to put myself or Vamphaery over. From looking at the talent in the tournament. I'm going with a long shot here. I'm going to say..... Hulk Hogan 7) You mentioned how you’re past tag teams have failed. What makes you think that this one will be so wonderful? Obviously your partner isn’t as punctual as he should be! Zurick: Yea, I know. Well you may not realize this, but he's busier than you would know. I was on the phone with him about a minute ago, he can't get online due to some net connections, but he is working on our next promo, sprucing it up. He's also working on lyrics to our song. Bloodlust does indeed write its own songs. 8) Being that you believe you and your partner will achieve gold, what do you have to say to the current PCW tag team Champions, Akiyama and The Pharaoh? Zurick: Nothing. Not a thing. Zero, Zip Zilch.................. Ok, I'm lying. I probably got a lot to say...... give GoD a second to open his mind **a moment passes** Zurrick: I just decided to plan the future. Well, First I will say, I destroyed Egypt, and Pharaoh is living in the past. Like, Helllllo. I get rid of things for a reason, just like the 70's. WAKE UP PHAROAH! Then we move along to AKiyama. "Neo Void GoD" doesn't he remind you of an old toy you use to have as a child? Me? I never played with toys. But I know you have Matt. Matt: What’s that supposed to mean? Zurick: We're rising to the astral planes with their gold. That basically sums my divine thoughts on that up. Next? 9) You claim to have a record of 999-0. Despite all that I remember you loosing quite a few matches. What ever happened to those? Zurick: What are you saying? I never lost a thing. I am Nine Hundred Ninety Nine Wins, ZERO losses, and ZERO draws. I've got a streak going on baby. When you're this good, you must be Godly, and I am God.....ly. And No, the Beatles have never been bigger than me.... Son of a bitch, look where you're at now Lennon! I mean..... It's a shame they went the way they did. Ya see Matt, just because you can't see it, doesn't mean it's not true. I am getting ready to be Nine Hundred Ninety Nine One Wins. 10) Who do you travel with on the road, and what vehicle do you travel in? Zurick: Ya know. I'm pretty sure I travel with Ben now, but I don't know how we get where we are going. One minute I wish it, and the next we are there. Just one of those things that GoD can do. I'm pretty sure I'll figure it out fully one of these days. *takes another sip of Nestea Cool* This is truly the drink of GoD 11) If you are god than, why don’t you “wish” yourself the World Title ehh? Zurick: I'm a team player. I'm trying to set an example. How would it look if GoD just wished himself the world title? Duh! We'd have a lot of new Satanist's wouldn't we? Think a little before you ask me stupid questions. I gave ya a brain, use it. 12) What has been your greatest moment in your career? Zurick : Getting the hell out of NTICW finally. Matt: Don’t bash NTICW! Zurick: I know, I'm just joking here. Seriously, winning the NTICW title. THEN getting the hell out of NTICW 13) What has been your worst moment in your career? Zurick: got a few. Anything to do with Ken Kaze, the night I woke up next to the ugliest woman alive, and realized it was George~! (Godly Insert: Don't ask) Losing to Young Tiger, that was another Ken Kaze moment though. Loosing to Sexual Harassment Panda..... Ya know, I have to add another career highlight real quick. One of my best moments, Firing Big Poppa Plump. That's it 14) Some guys hate you and some guys love you. Ok, I lied most guys just don’t really care, but in all honesty a former PCW wrestler contacted me with a question for you. Are you gay with T.V.B.? Zurick: Tell Xavier Kannon, No I am not. Want to hear something funny? Matt: No Zurrick: Oh well. Kannon has had sex with T.V.B, No Lie. I saw T.V.B earlier today, went to introduce him to Kannon, apparently they have been to FRANCE together. Like I said earlier, when you think you know Ben, he just shocks you more. T.V.B comes in the locker rooms earlier today, he says, you’re a good one or something similar. Kannon pops for him. Then later is when Ben brings up there night in France. I tell ya, weird stuff. Ellie is going to be upset that Kannon's been fooling around on her with Ben. Matt: Actually it’s not Kannon though… Zurick: DON'T EVEN LIE TO ME! I can see these things.....next question please.... Matt: But…. Zurick: Next Question! **Editors Note** It Was Hessian of OSW** 15) What is the deal with you and your tournament match for a chance in the March Madness Royal Rumble? Zurick: I know it still has to be around, but staff seems to have, "misplaced it" or decided to move it back to Tuesday. Hopefully it will make Friday's card, but I was supposed to be facing Prophecy. Like you can't see who is going to win that match. The duel of fates I am looking forward to though, happens to be myself and John Borro. Hopefully they will hear what I am saying, and remember my match. Matt: It’s Juan Zurick: I know but GoD prefers to say John. 16) Who are the top five guys from the locker room that just plain old piss you off? Zurick: No one. I am a patient person, I realize no one is perfect and everyone screws up. I like everyone backstage. 17) What are some of your favorites? (Movies, food, places to go, cars, music, ETC) Zurick: Movies, Lord of the Ring, Brave heart, Terminator 2, Gladiator, almost any Dark Ages story like Legend. Foods, when I can allow to gain a pain or two, cheese steak, pizza, focca bread, and when I can't, Greek salad. Places to go: Mars, Heaven, Cloud 9, Malls. Cars, Dodge Ram fully loaded. Music: Metal, Punk, 70's and 80's rock. Favorite band is Alice Cooper. I love video games. I am currently playing Ghost Recon, Medal of Honor Allied Assault, and Ultima Online. 18) Being that you seem utterly obsessed with being “GoD” why doesn’t “GoD” have millions of beautiful women around him? Or does this go back to the gay thing? Zurick: GoD has one woman in his life right now. He had one before, but didn't work out so well. GoD is the loving father of a 2yr old daughter whom he only gets to see once a month on a weekend unfortunately. 19) You said that you wrote your own music….what are those fancy lyrics you came up with? Zurick: A few people have heard the song itself, but hold on.... *ahem ahem* you’re lucky I just happened to bring a cassette of the instrumental..... Pops tape in a "suddenly there" Cassette player. This is the way that you walk to the ring when you're God and you know that you're God because you're God, I'm your Goooooooooood (GOD) I'm your Gooooooooood(God) cause The whole world loves you when you're a god (g-god g-g-g-god) yeah the whole world loves you when you're a god (g-god g-g-g-god) In every church and mosque I be struttin my stuff Because of my wrath the pharos turned to dust Jobbers and punks their moves may abound but in the blink of my eyes the whole world spins around unlimited well scripted, saw your bird when you flipped it your first kiss your first fuck, when you speed in your truck You avoided the cops, habits, I helped you kick it when your me, you get praise, almost e-ver-y day 'cause The whole world loves you when you're a god (g-god g-g-g-god) yeah the whole world loves you when you're a god (g-god g-g-g-god) unscrupulous doofulous, leave you IN tails OF dust, with undeterminable speed from the moves which I bust incomprehensibly vast, there's no mountain too high, and no ocean to deep, to hide from MY eyes the drive by that claimed you, the bullets that framed you, when you had that bad cough, was 'cause YOU PISSED ME OFF, and and and and the whole world loves it when you're a God (g-god g-g-g-god) said the whole world loves you when you're a god (g-god g-g-g-god) I'm...... Your....... GoD *takes a bow as the music stops playing* es, I know, Holy creation. Almost as good as when I did the ten commandments Did you know I originally intended them as song lyrics? Moses took it the wrong way so I just let it flow. Matt: That means your done right? Zurick: I have been done since music stopped 20) And before the last question WORD ASSOCIATION! Jesus Christ: Disowned Buddha: A cheat at Poker Akiyama and the Pharaoh: Expired Visa Old School Wrestling: Day's best work Multicolored Colored Hair: Groovy And finally anything you would like to say...? Questions, comments, smack talk? Zurick: GoD didn't want men to wear a bra. That’s just gross. END! Well, that’s where I finished but instead of being able to thank Zurick a flash of light over took me and I woke up in my car outside then mansion. Being very curious and lacking my notes and recorded tapes I began to leave to go up to door again, but suddenly the tapes appeared next to me. Disturbed I left the area, but anyway…A big thank you to Zurick for sitting down with me, even though according to him he has all the time in an eternity.
