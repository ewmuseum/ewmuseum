Without Corey

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Without Corey Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jun 2002 ---- As reported earlier and displayed on Tuesday Turmoil, Ultimate Authority member Corey Williams has been having problems with their entrance music. "Goddamnit, I would have expected Nelson to already know that niggers are only good for two things, slavery and rape," said a pissed-off Corey Williams directly after Turmoil. However, it seems that the controversial young billion has used his money and connections to fellow "scholars of controversy" to solve the problem himself. "Although I'll still be coming out to that God-forsaken trash that the Lox calls rap, I'll only do it for the sake of the Ultimate Authority." And the new music? "Well it had to be someone controversial and who ROCKed more ass than a certain washed-up has-been. And with Marilyn (Manson) constantly proclaiming that 'rock is deader than dead' it seemed only logical to go to Marshall." That's right, Marshall Mathers, Eminem, Slim Shady. The controversial rapper will be recording a special "controversy remix" of his latest hit "Without Me" that will be used for all of Williams' singles matches. "Hell, maybe I'll get him to come round and perform it to the losers in the crowd. After all, controversy is what the people wanna see"
