Pharaoh Losing It

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pharaoh Losing It Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Mar 2003 ---- In PCW news, it has been reported that following Clyde's trickery over Titu Xansiv at this week's past Tuesday Turmoil, The Pharaoh has not been acting himself. Sources backstage say that the Egyptian wrestler, following his victory over Violence Jack, refused to cooperate with reporters over his thoughts on his match against Clyde at up-and-coming Pay Per View March Madness. This, of course, is not like the Titu Xansiv we have all come to know... 'Mr. Xansiv HAS spoken to us, but only to express his, or 'Ra's' displeasure towards us actually asking him any questions,' says PCW's number one reporter Cal Seaver. 'And the way he went about it was VERY odd indeed... even for someone with as LITTLE a grasp on reality as him!' What this could mean for The Pharaoh is currently unknown, but sources are reporting that Pharaoh has finally been tipped over the edge... 'Well we always knew he had a bit of "problem" up THERE, you know? But we NEVER thought it would get so bad that he'd completely self-destruct,' exclaimed PCW commentator Dave Kern. 'I don't believe the rumours and I hope the fans don't eitherâ€¦' When asked for a comment on the situation, PCW's resident Australian Flaymin Aymin had this to say, 'Ah! Get those cameras outta here! I swear I didn't steal it! ARGHH!!' Aymin then proceeded to speed off down the hall, arms flailing. With Clyde's vice-like grip on Titu's mind ever crushing the Egyptian's mentality, could The Demon have actually succeeded in obliterating Pharaoh's grasp on all things real? As always, PCW will keep you posted as news comes in!
