Wether-Fest Announced

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wether-Fest Announced Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Apr 2003 ---- With only weeks until the release of the third album from the band 'Ice Age', front man Shane Wethers has put together a festival to rock the world. Wethers has brought together bands such as the Foo Fighters, Less than Jake, Tenacious D and Puddle of Mud to perform at the prestigious concert. Last week Shane put the first step into forming his security team at his infamous Rock Festival in June, known as the E-Fest. Shane has been recruiting eleven members to a security team known as 'Eternals Eleven'. These eleven men or women will play a huge part in keeping things in order at the event, which will have tickets on sale this Friday. Wethers has a lot of things on his mind with the stakes in the rising star division getting even more intense, but with the help of a loyal agent and long time friend, Mikey Fishmore is going to help Shane find the Eleven men. A fat leprechaun in a cat suit was quoted earlier 'This is going to be bigger than Woodstock' whilst drinking a pint of tequila.
