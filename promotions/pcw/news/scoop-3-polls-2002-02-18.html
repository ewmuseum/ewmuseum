Scoop #3(Polls!) Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Feb 2002 ---- Scoops Staley here and I brought the January Polls with me. I know they have been a long time coming. Board problems, slow voting, and my own work schedule slowed them way down, but they are here now. Lets get going! I’ll start off with the questions. Instead of just naming the winner, I have the top three finishers and how many votes they received. Whose stock rose the most in January? 1. Hank 9 2. Xavier 6 3. Chet King 3 Whose stock fell the most in January? 1. BAS 11 (A lot of people didn’t agree since BAS is on vacation, but I have to agree because when he comes back, it’ll take him some time to regain his old heat.) 2. Ivan Stanislav 4 3. Tyler Nelson, Vampir Nosferatu 2 Who writes the best dramatic RP’s in PCW? 1. Vampir Nosferatu 12 2. Akiyama 5 3. Jeremy Howard, Snow, Darkstar, Drake Maxwell 1 Who writes the best comedic RP’s in PCW? 1. Timo Bolamba 10 2. Miyagawa 7 3. Xavier Kannon, Snow 2 Who writes the best dark RP’s? 1. Clyde 14 T2. Vampir Nosferatu 3 T2. Darkstar 3 Who’s the most under rated man in PCW? (Close one) 1. Chris Chase 4 T2. White Mexican 3 T2. Corey Williams 3 Now for the real polls. Gimmick, Workrate, and RP’s. In the event of a tie, RP totals will be used to break them. If two wrestlers tie in RP’s, total vote count will break the tie. Top Ten Gimmicks in PCW for January, 2002 (8)10. Akiyama 44 (6)9. Tyler Nelson 53 (7)8. Miyagawa 53 (9)7. Jeremy Howard 61 (-)6. Xavier Kannon 72 (3)5. Clyde 73 (2)4. Vampir Nosferatu 86 (-)3. Paddy Irishman 119 (5)2. Snow 131 (1)1. Ivan Stanislav 134 (Fourth month in a row!) Can you believe Paddy Irishman? He’s the new Unstoppable Eugene. Ivan Stanislav wins the poll for the fourth straight month. The amazing part about that is Ivan left PCW half way through the polls, and he still pulled out the win. The field was a lot more open with many people, like BAS and RAH, not playing a factor. Next, we have the... Top Ten Workrates in PCW for January, 2002 (-)10. Red Rock 41 (-)9. Hank 42 (-)8. Paddy Irishman 66 (-)7. Juan Borro 77 (10)6. Chet King 91 (-)5. Chris Chase 92 (8)4. White Mexican 96 (-)3. Xavier Kannon 115 (2)2. Snow 168 (1)1. Jeremy Howard 210 (Two months in a row!) Jeremy Howard gets a much deserved win here. Surprisingly, both Hank~! and Chris Chase make the list, which is drastically different from December’s list. Only four people on this list were on last month’s list. Top Ten Roleplayers for January, 2002 (-)10. White Mexican 45 (10)9. Boda 56 (5)8. Vampir Nosferatu 56 (-)7. Chris Chase 65 (3)6. Ivan Stanislav 74 (7)5. Akiyama 83 (9)4. Timo Bolamba 92 (-)3. Xavier Kannon 92 (1)2. Jeremy Howard 176 (2)1. Snow 190 Snow takes his first poll win, but the real surprises were the fall of Vampir Nosferatu and the rise of Xavier Kannon. Everything else was pretty predictable. I really thought Tyler Nelson deserved to be on here, but amazingly, he failed to get a single vote. Ok, now for the real list. Since this is my first time doing the polls, I thought I would add my own little accent to them and turn it into the top twenty of PCW, instead of the top fifteen. Note that points are listed in the following order: RP/Workrate/Gimmick = Total Top Twenty PCW Wrestlers for January, 2002 (11)20. Tyler Nelson 0/0/53 = 53 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 10 That’s not a typo. Tyler Nelson failed to get votes in either roleplaying or workrate. Now, Tyler doesn’t deserve workrate votes, but his roleplays are top notch for the most part. I expected a low workrate to reflect on his RP count, but I thought one or two of the old timers would give him the nod. Wait a minute, I overlooked him too! *lowers head in shame* (-)19. Pharaoh 17/9/27 = 53 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 0 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 19 Pharaoh squeaks into the top twenty by something I like to call the newbie factor. New people seemed to band together and give each other votes in these polls. That’s not to say Pharaoh doesn’t deserve to be here. Quite the opposite. The newbie factor offsets the fact that many of the old timers over look newcomers to PCW. (-)18. Hank~! 4/42/11 = 57 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 0 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 18 Oh my God. The first month that I run the polls and Hank~! makes it. Hank~! I say, Hank~! Well, when you think about it, Hank~! deserves to be here. He got most of those 57 votes from workrate, because he really did put in a good effort at the Pay Per View. Tyler Nelson must be shaking his head though. (-)17. Red Rock 6/41/21 = 68 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 0 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 17 I haven’t been overly impressed with the man with steel balls, but I can’t say he doesn’t roleplay like a madman. He gets his votes from both workrate and gimmick, both of which I will admit are great. I like Red Rock though. He worked hard to get back to the PCW level and he’s making the best of his opportunity. I hope he keeps it up and makes a fan of me. (-)16. Darkstar 40/0/30 = 70 Top 20: 2 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 15 I think Darkstar may well be the most under rated man in PCW, even though Chris Chase won it. I must admit, I did vote for Chase on that question, but Darkstar is a close second. Very close. What I like most about Darkstar is the fact that he doesn’t feel the need to write ten pages. He gets his point across in RP’s that are quite short, but much easier to read. Imagine what would happen if Darkstar had a better workrate. You can bet that his RP score would nearly double. (-)15. Boda 56/13/11 = 80 Top 20: 3 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 9 There’s a flaw in these polls. If I had asked everyone their top ten in PCW, Boda would make nearly everyone’s list. But when you go by RP’s, Workrate, and Gimmick, everyone overlooks him. Sure, his gimmick is iffy at best, but Boda writes solid roleplays and a good amount of them. He’s been a main eventer forever, but has not finished in the top five yet. This will forever be known as the Boda phenomenon. (7)14. Miyagawa 33/18/53 = 104 Top 20: 3 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 7 Nasty drop for Miyagawa, who finished seventh in December. He had a good feud with Ivan in January, but neither of them really took much to the other on the boards. You can bet that Miya was doing a lot of staff work lately, resulting in his drop. When Miya can roleplay, there aren’t many people in PCW who are better than him. He’s my favorite to read, bar none. (-)13. Juan Borro 10/77/23 = 110 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 13 I was quite shocked by this placing. I must admit, I usually skim Borro’s roleplays without really reading them in depth, but Borro is slowly getting the respect of his peers. And as that happens more and more people, including me, will begin to read his RP’s in greater detail. His seventy-seven workrate points are especially impressive. (8)12. Clyde 39/1/73 = 113 Top 20: 3 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 2 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Peak: 4 Clyde continues to drop since his great performance in November. He dropped out of the top ten here, which has to hurt. Clyde has been a regular in the polls, with three top twenty finishes. Clyde has been all over the polls since they began. He didn’t place in October, and then went all the way up to fourth in November. Since, he’s dropped steadily. (15)11. Chet King 36/91/0 = 127 Top 20: 3 Top 15: 3 Top 10: 0 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 11 His feud with Mexi and the Continental title got Chet King a lot of overdue respect. Since November, he’s finished consistently in the 10-15 range, but this is Chet’s top finish to date. I can’t help but worry about that big fat goose egg in his gimmick column. (4)10. Vampir Nosferatu 56/7/86 = 149 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Peak: 4 After finally breaking the top five in December, Vampir Nosferatu fell all the way back to tenth. I think the main reason is a drop in workrate, so it’s not a serious problem. And Vampir’s major strength, his gimmick, remained in the top five of that category. I think that there may be a general decline in dark gimmicks around PCW. If that’s the case, then things aren’t so bad at all, because he finished above every other dark gimmick in PCW, and it’s only a matter of time before dark gimmicks return in full force. (6)9. Akiyama 83/22/44 = 149 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 6 Akiyama fell a little bit in January and finished lower than he ever has. But when your lowest finish is ninth, then things aren’t too bad at all. Amazingly, Akiyama didn’t get here on the power of his workrate. In the previous three months, Akiyama’s highest points came from his workrate, but in January, he only received twenty-two (votes were down, remember)! I don’t think that’s a bad thing for Akiyama. In fact, I think it’s good. Akiyama got here because of his Rp’s and his gimmick, something he couldn’t boast before. He may have slipped three spots, but I’m inclined to think this is his best finish yet. (10)8. Timo Bolamba 92/21/35 = 158 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 3 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 7 Timo slipped a bit in December, but he got right back on track in January with an eighth place finish and an impressive fourth place finish in terms of roleplays. Timo dropped out of the top ten in workrate this month, which is concerning, but lately Timo looks like he’ll get back in whenever the next polls are held. (14)7. Chris Chase 65/92/12 = 169 Top 20: 2 Top 15: 2 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 7 Chris Chase inside the top ten? Two months ago you would have laughed at anyone who said this would happen, but not now. Chris Chase has come to life! Even though he’s been in PCW for a long, long, time I can’t help but group him with the rookies, because he’s such a promising up-and-comer. And Chris living up to his potential couldn’t have come at a better time, seeing how PCW’s talent level isn’t quite where it was a few months ago (a few retirements, disappearances, no Ivan, etc...). People like Chase give us hope that there will soon be as much PCW talent as ever, because it’s only a matter of time before the new kids make their own legacies. (12)6. White Mexican 45/96/38 = 179 Top 20: 2 Top 15: 2 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 0 #1: 0 Peak: 6 Mexiacan appeared last month in the twelfth spot, and most people thought that he would hover around there. Mexi proved us all wrong. He comes up with 96 points in workrate to finish just outside the top five. With him currently not wrestling in PCW, I think some of the late votes may have gone elsewhere. (-)5. Paddy Irishman 23/66/119 = 208 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Peak: 5 Impressed? Looks like you are. Paddy O’Shea made us forget about Unstoppable Eugene with his hilarious gimmick. O’Shea’s a better roleplayer too! And 66 workrate votes show that he’s not lazy. The newbie factor (see Pharaoh’s write up) had a lot to do with this placing, but Paddy is showing everyone how great it is to have a gimmick. (2)4. Ivan Stanislav 74/32/134 = 240 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 4 #1: 2 Peak: 1 You can bet that the jump to OSW hurt Ivan in the late running, but he wasn’t doing all that well before the jump either. Still, Ivan has left marks on this poll that perhaps will never be touched with four consecutive wins in the Gimmick category. He also had two straight number one finishes, which is something that I think will be tough for anyone to challenge. But the biggest mark Ivan leaves on these polls is that he’s the first, and at this point only, Triple Crown winner (November, 2001, Ivan won in all three major categories). It’s rare for that to even be challenged for. (-)3. Xavier Kannon 92/115/72 = 299 Top 20: 1 Top 15: 1 Top 10: 1 Top 5: 1 #1: 0 Peak: 3 It’s a good time to be a fresh young blue chipper in PCW. With the constant changing of the upper card lately, newcomers are getting their rewards much quicker. That will probably stop a few months down the line when our current top newcomers wedge themselves into their rightful position, be it high or low card, and it gets tougher for future rookies to make their way up. No one has benefited more from the current situation than Xavier Kannon, and he deserves every bit of this recognition. One of only five men to score 200 points (nearly breaking three hundred), and a serious threat to break a hundred points in all three categories in a month where votes were down, don’t expect Xavier to be going anywhere. He was also only one Gimmick point away from finishing top five in every category! (1)Jeremy Howard 176/210/61 = 447 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 4 #1: 1 Peak: 1 Even though he lost his title, Jeremy Howard came close to winning the number one spot for a second straight month. That’s a sign of true talent, and not a championship pushing your point count up. Howard still topped the workrate poll, finishing a whopping forty-two points ahead of his closest competition. Howard also finished just fourteen points behind the leader in roleplays! I wouldn’t be surprised if Jeremy Howard got back on top of these polls next month, title or no title. (3)1. Snow 190/168/131 = 489 Top 20: 4 Top 15: 4 Top 10: 4 Top 5: 4 #1: 1 Peak: 1 It was a great month for Snow and the points show it. He was the only man to finish with over a hundred points in every category, and made a slight run at becoming the Polls second ever Triple Crown winner. When it was all over, Snow finished first in RP’s and second in both Workrate and Gimmick, making his Triple Crown attempt seem more serious than slight. You can bet that winning the world title added some votes to that total, but it’s debatable how many, since Snow was finishing in the top five when he was still a mid-carder. Well guys, I hope you enjoyed them and I apologize that they were so long coming. I’m not sure right now if there will be February Polls or not, since it’s so late in the month already and there’s no Pay Per View this month. See ya soon Scoops Staley
