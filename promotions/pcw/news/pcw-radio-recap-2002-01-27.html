PCW Radio Recap

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Radio Recap Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Jan 2002 ---- The January 26 PCW Malice Show lived up to the expectations of a pre-PPV show. To start off the show, there is an audio Shanghaied 2 promotion that runs through the matches. When Duke hits the air, he announces that he’s back and ready to talk about the Pay Per View, PTC, and all of the news surrounding PCW right now. Before the Phantom Booker calls, Duke says that Ed’s microphone would not be broken if he had kept it out of certain body cavities. The first real caller of the night is the Phantom Booker. -TPB says that there is a variety of news to talk about, with PTC reopening with a number of new federations. He stresses that PTC is a way for smaller federations to promote themselves. When talking about how popular PTC is, The Phantom Booker said that approximately 3000 old and new applications were responded to Friday night. -TPB brings up the new GTT tournament and encourages more people to enter. -They begin talking about Shanghaied 2 and how the card promises one of the best Pay Per Views in PCW history. When talking about the Pot O’ Gold match, Duke picks Paddy O’Shea while TPB picks Six Fingers Cisco. TPB says that Frank E. Bones’ contract with PCW expires just after the PPV, and instead of resigning with PCW, Bones has decided to sign with OSW. Of course, PCW wishes only the best for Frank E. Bones. -When the Extreme title is brought up, Duke rants about how a woman has no business as Extreme Champ. He says that the other participants in the match should throw their morales out the window and get the Extreme title away from Brandi. -TPB says that he believes the Cruiserweight title match may involve the three ugliest people in the history of PCW. After the joking subsides, TPB says that he likes Chris Chase in that match. Duke is split between Borro and Kannon. -Talking about the TV title match, TPB says that Flaymin’ Aymin brings credibility to the TV title division. Duke and TPB talk about how the TV title division is weak. -The Phantom Booker announces that there is a possibility that star indy fed wrestler, ICE, may work as the special referee in the Pleasantville Death Match. TPB says that he has heard that ICE has problems with Alan Kriegman. -Both Duke and TPB like the idea of the Playboy Bunny Lumberjack match. They joke about how since it will be a PPV, the bunnies should be naked. When they talk about the competitors, TPB has nothing but good to say about either of them. He says that they are “both on the way up.” He says that sometime in the future he would like to see Chet King feud with JKH while White Mexican feuds with Snow. -TPB talks about how gruesome the first death match will be, and both TPB and Duke pick Clyde to come out the winner. -TPB says that the Drop from Heavens match may not happen, and Duke says that indeed, the card si subject to change. -Both men are looking forward to the National Pride match. TPB announces that Miyagawa has no chance, and says that Ivan Stanislav will crush him. -In the world title match, TPB says that this match will be different from past PCW title matches, because the participants are capable of giving a little bit of everything. Both Duke and TPB pick Jeremy Howard to win the match, and TPB says that Jeremy Howard has earned a spot among the very best of PCW Champions. -In the main event, TPB says that Duke has no chance. Duke, however, thinks that we should not underestimate a middle aged, overweight, redneck. The Phantom Booker signs off and Duke takes a call from female FWO wrestler, Tempest. -They talk about current FWO storylines involving Tempest. Duke talks about how Tempest should maybe be doing dishes, although, Tempest says that she can do a little work on the plumbing as well (joke made by Duke earlier in the night). -Tempest says that her gimmick is all about having fun in the ring, and Duke says that his gimmick was the same. Ruslan is the next caller. -Ruslan says that he doesn’t like waiting while women speak and announces that Russian chicks dig Ivan. -Ruslan says that Tyler Nelson and Management are over! He also says that he has no respect for Duke, only for his fellow communists. -Duke talks about how Ivan cheated his way to the top. Next, Kid Wonder calls to finally announce the PCW awards. For complete results, see the PCW main page under the “news” section. Xavier Kannon is the next caller. -Xavier says that he is confident about going into his first PCW Pay Per View. He says that he has already defeated Borro, and Chase’s victory over him was a cheap DQ. -When Duke asked if Xavier could explain Scientology, Xavier tells Duke to pay his money like everyone else. Duke doesn’t like the idea of giving money away for any reason. -Duke mocked Xavier’s accent, so Kannon made fun of the Bears. Ivan calls in to accept his award for wrestler of the year. -During Ivan’s call, a crazy lady calls in. Some hilarious moments occur as Duke asks the crazy lady, who truly was crazy, what she thinks of Ivan. Her only audible words are “Lock him up,” although there’s a good chance that Ivan’s execution was talked about. -Next, in a Malice first, Boda calls the show long distance so he can verbally go head to head with Ivan. Boda and Ivan argue, with Duke taking Boda’s side for the most part. -Boda says that he has back up. -Boda challenges Ivan, saying that Ivan “knows where my locker room is.” -Boda vows that Jeremy Howard will walk out the PCW Champion at Redemption. Sean, from WWA is the next caller. -Sean claims that WWA will defeat PCW in the ratings. -He says that in the next WWA main event, Jack Storm will wrestler. Duke makes cracks about the Storm “family”. In a hilarious moment, the new (official?) Crazy Lady of PCW calls back. Duke asks her if she knows who Jack Storm is. The Crazy Lady thinks it over, and is cut off. In the final meaningful call of the night, The Phantom Booker comes back. -TPB says that NTICW looks very good, crediting White Mexican, Timo Bolamba, and Xavier Kannon. -TPB says that PWA needs revamping. -Someone calls in talking saying that PCW fears fedwars. TPB says that fedwars is a dump, and PCW is more than willing to take them on and crush them. -TPB says that the TV Title division needs improvement. He says that if Big Davilicious can move up the ranks while doing very little work, then there is a problem with the division. -TPB speaks about how PCW is in a period where there are a lot of new faces coming in, and it could take some time for everyone and everything to adjust. He said that some PCW talent will be moving to OSW, and that should help speed the adjustment process along. -TPB talks a little about the firing of Shane Wethers. -The Crazy Lady made another appearance, warning Duke not to put her on hold. Duke tells TPB the story behind this Crazy Lady, who it seems is stalking the Malice Man. -In the final noteworthy moment of the night, TPB and Duke organize a “Tempest Dating Game” to take place next week on Malice. Tempest is willing to participate, and the contestants will be Ivan Stanislav, Xavier Kannon, and Duke Williams. A few more people called, but nothing of note really took place. Be sure to tune in to Malice next week as three lonely bachelors will fight to win Tempest’s heart.
