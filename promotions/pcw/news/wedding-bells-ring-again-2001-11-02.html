Wedding Bells Ring Again

2 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wedding Bells Ring Again Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Nov 2001 ---- After being absent since Red October, Miyagawa finally arrived at the PCW head office today wearing a shiny new wedding band on his finger. He had this to say to Cal Seaver about the situation: "Well Cal, I've been busy getting married and stuff. It's been all fun so far. Her name is Natalia Miyagawa, he's a very classy Russian lady who DOES NOT BELIEVE IN COMMUNISM... sorry had to clear that one up right away." Cal went on to ask Miyagawa about his "retirement". "I was thinking about it for a while. The thought had even come to do it after Red October or after Alliances, as Alliances marks my full one year in PCW... well a year and a bit but I like to think of it as a year. With Meanstreak, Darkstar, Brandi and Alan Kriegman all gone while I've been away, I think it is safe to say that Old School is dead, but I'm not. I can't leave when there is such a big roster gap left... let's face it, I like being the center of attention, so I want at least a three month gap between my retirement and anyone else's." Cal went on to ask about his activities since the PPV. "We went on honeymoon. In Colorado... beautiful place... spent most of the time in a little town called Golden... where Corrs comes from. I love that town, but I couldn't live there... the smell of the brewery gets to you after four or five days... but then again we didn't venture out that often... you know." In passing a few PCW stars had this to say about Miyagawa's happy day. "He could have got married on television the selfish assclown." - Stephen Blood "NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! Oh... at least I will always have JJ" - Stephen Smith "Obviously Miyagawa wants Russian nationality... I can understand why... Da!" - Ivan Stanislav Miyagawa stated that he will appear on PCW television prior to his first booting and introduce his new wife to the world. He also explained that she would not wear anything as revealing as Buffy Vegas, who's man-nipples put him off his food.
