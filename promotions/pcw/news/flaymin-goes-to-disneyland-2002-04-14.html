Flaymin Goes to Disneyland

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flaymin Goes to Disneyland Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Apr 2002 ---- Reports released today from the Official Spokesman of Disneyland, Walt Disney IV, state the intention of the World Famous Theme Park to join forces with PCW's resident whackjob, Flaymin Aymin. Walt Disney IV, released the following statement on the Disney website: "Disneyland is proud to announce an upcoming feature ride that is currently being built. Extreme Hardcore Actions go hand in hand with Flaymin Aymin like Beer and Orange Juice, and we at Disneyland believe it's time we took the term 'Near-Death Experience' to a whole new level. 'The Flaymin House of Torture' will set the standard for death-defying rides all over the world. It will basically be in the form of a Ghost Train with a twist- rather then have cheap polyester ghosts, the ride will feature numerous incidents that Flaymin has encountered in his PCW tenure. You can expect to be run over by a tractor, Officially Endorsed by Flaymin himself! How to survive? Jump out of the damn carriage and run for dear life! Thrill seekers will also be victim to sudden gushes of flames that pop up out of nowhere and burn your ass to the ground like Flaymin's infamous 'Firefly'. Another feature will be the 'Staple Gun Gauntlet' where ride-goers will have to duck, spin, twist and jump to avoid a barrage of staples aimed directly at their heads in an attemt to staple their ears to their respective seats. Riders are also adivised to wear raincoats if they wish to remain dry from the occasional exploding beer keg. Fire, Beer, Explosions, Tractors, Staple Guns and much more: all in the 3 minute ride of your life!! Commit Suicide in style by entering 'The Flaymin House of Torture'!!!" When contacted by PCW Officials, Flaymin was speechless, unable to complete a sentence.... 5 minutes later we realised he was drunk and had just passed out. Legal Note: Disneyland will not be held responsible for any deaths, injuries, decapitations or compensation claims that result from entering the 'Flaymin House of Torture'. Elderly People wishing to indirectly undergo Euthenasia on the ride will be pleased to know that for an extra $10 Disneyland will throw in a Mickey Mouse Bodybag complete with the infamaous Mickey ears which double as a storage compartment for decapitated body parts!!
