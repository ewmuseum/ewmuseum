A Tricky Punchline

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Tricky Punchline Posted by Sandy Wong on 8 Feb 2003 ---- Seattle, Washington- On Turmoil, we saw the very surprising choice that Xavier Kannon made for the new owner of PCW. That man's influence was on Fusion, but more apparent was the new attitude of the Office. On Fusion, they continued to add to their ever-growing numbers. One familiar member came to the Front Office, but it was shocking to see who and exactly how they turned. The main event saw only six people at ringside by its end, Kano and Aymin tried to pull upsets to make it to the March Madness Brawl For It All, and a familiar PTC face threw himself into the craziness that is Storm/Windsor/Rust/Erratic/Romantico. To see all this and more, head to the drop-down menu for "Recent Results" and check out everything that happened on Fusion
