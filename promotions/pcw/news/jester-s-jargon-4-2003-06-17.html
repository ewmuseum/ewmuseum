Jester's Jargon #4

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jester's Jargon #4 Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Jun 2003 ---- Hopefully the first column wetted your appetites and before I get to the announcing of the results I would like to thank those who made the effort to answer the thread and for those who skipped it's presence to make an effort in next months when it gets published. Firstly I noticed a mistake in my own poll, the results should coincide with our last Pay-Per-View 'Supremacy' and not 'Spring Fling' as instructed, this mistake a lapse of the mind. Secondly, some people were a little vague in their answers. Please in future don't just write Jackson because surprisingly we have two in Dante and Matt and during the question stage I took peoples first answers when more than one name cropped up, really this was nothing more than teething trouble but overall everything went according to plan. During the question stage I awarded one point for numbers one and three, them being 'Whose stock in your opinion rose the most during April/ May?' and 'Who in your opinion is the most underrated person on the roster and is deserving of a push?' whilst I deducted a point for question number two, that being 'Whose stock in your opinion dropped during the month's of April/ May?' Hopefully that was easy to follow, so without further ado the results: 10. Entertainment Extreme (E2) Narrowly creeping into the number ten booth ahead of James Edwards is Entertainment Extreme who came into the company with a decently worked angle alongside Pranks With Attitude and as their understudies took over the reigns of the tag division. It's nice to see a tag team break into the top ten and courtesy of their latest feud with Justice Inc, next month both may feature providing they can apply their work of the role-play board across into segment form. Credit to both Tyler and John here because for a longtime the RS division looked to overshadow much of the rookie entrants but E2 have applied their trade and the people have noticed. 9. Brianna Washington I find this a surprise because Brianna has delivered hugely in her last two feuds and continues to work both in cards and on the role-play front. Her role as the Druid was a much talked about angle and rightfully so and if Dorn Tracer were here writing this poll then I know he would be heaping praise upon her. Washington has one of the better gimmicks circulating Primetime Championship Wrestling and a work ethic alongside it, I predict a top five finish possibly next month providing the conclusion of her Dorn Tracer feud delivers as expected. 8. Miss Moonlight During a prediction thread I voiced that putting the vacant Cruiserweight Championship would be a continuation in a long line of mistakes with the title but that was before Miss Moonlight took control of the strap. A credible Champion that's worked and her stint in this division may catch the eye of the bookers and propel her next month to possibly Television but in saying that I can't see anyone dethroning Miss Moonlight as yet. Hopefully people have paid attention to her SOCIETY feud and come next poll Miss Moonlight may have moved up the card and up in the rankings, possibly pressing for a top five finish with Brianna Washington. 7. Tessa Windsor-Green Lacks a real killer gimmick but has one of the best work ethics on the roster, delivers weekly with role-plays and I know first hand that she contributes towards feud, over in the tag scene I will go on record and say that Chris Storm/ Tessa Windsor versus Pranks With Attitude is still one of the highlights in my short career. A feud that still has ties and hopefully we can bring it to a head in the next few weeks and now Television Champion she has really pressed this month and is deserving of her number seven poll status. 6. The Pharaoh Being in a World Title feud and being part of a stable (The Triumvirate) one only bettered by the Front Office in recent memory I would have at-least expected a top five, if not, top three finish from the Pharaoh. Here we have one of the best writers on the roster and for me this is a little disappointing because the stable has produced some memorable material over the course of the month but really this standing shouldn't reflect badly until we see the positions of both Dorn Tracer and Violence Jack. A top three finish looms providing he can up his game and beat our current Champion at 'Cloud Nine' and with the quality Pharaoh produces that verdict wont come as a surprise. 5. Matt Jackson Talking about surprises. Matt Jackson makes the top for the April/ May poll and this status is much deserved after pulling out all the stops via the role-play board to win the vacant Rising Star Championship. His role-play work has been both quality and quantity so no argument there but the question begging is whether he can take this into a credible feud, last month's Makonie war was decent enough but now working alongside the eager Rochelli he may have the right partner to win over the critics. The polls biggest surprise thus far but it's fair to say that Matt Jackson has worked for it. 4. Totally Tubular Tommy The man with a mammoth Continental reign then goes onto World success so no surprise to see Primetime Championship Wrestling's Universal representative cracking the top five booth in this poll. Lost a few points due to the second question concerning the loss of stock but overall number four seems fair enough and if he can squeeze back into the World scene alongside Pharaoh and Green then the fight for a top three finish only intensifies. After his Supremacy loss Tommy looks to be feud-less and hopefully he can rectify that come next month, maybe hop between World and Continental but either way I expect his name to feature heavily in next month's poll. 3. Skylar Green No shock here, a World Title victory and a PCW figurehead. Still writes quality role-plays and still produces a great in-card presence so his top three status is much deserved alongside other names that have featured beforehand. Now working a good feud alongside Pharaoh so everything looks promising in the Green camp, all Green's that is and next month our resident blind superstar may even press for the top spot that is yet to feature. 2. Gaz "The Jester" Green Big shock here for me. Granted I was already established in the tag scene and left after a decently worked angle but to get a top three spot only a few short weeks into my first singles feud then I feel rather honoured. Hopefully people have enjoyed the Jester and Dante Jackson feud and feel I've done enough to be considered a credible Continental Champion but to have earned a second place finish alongside Skylar Green comes as a surprise. The big question now is, 'Who made that number one spot?' 1. Dorn Tracer For me the hardest workingman in Primetime Championship Wrestling gets commended by stars, staff and followers alike. Put recognition back into the Rising Star Championship and then masterminded the Triumvirate alongside already established names like Violence Jack and Pharaoh but accomplishing all this so very soon into his career. Has worked the Television Title scene was well enough and is currently in one of the closest followed feuds within the fed itself courtesy of the chemistry between himself and Brianna Washington. Everything Dorn has received he has earned, no argument there so for the months of April/ May, here stands our deserved poll winner. Hopefully for those who failed to feature, your absence will strive to make you do better next month as this was closely contested with people like James Edwards, Justice Incorporated and SOCIETY falling short by a few wayward points. Anyway hope you've enjoyed seeing a column or two and found them entertaining reads for the few short minutes they last and feel free to leave feedback on the PCW OOC board. - The Handler of the Jester.
