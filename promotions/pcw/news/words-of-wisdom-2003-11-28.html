PCW — Column

Words of Wisdom

28 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the Words of Wisdom from yours truly, "Wise Ass" Josh Wilkes! Some of you may remember me from various failed upstart promotions around the United States. But most importantly I am now a member of the greatest promotion in the world of wrestling...Primetime Championship Wrestling! I'll give you the quick story of how I broke into the business, and how I wound up in PCW. It was a cold and windy day back in 1985 and both me and my girlfriend were ready to fu.....wait that's another story for another day. Ahh...here we go. I was always a fan of wrestling growing up. Watching all of the legendary greats and newcomers who would go onto be some of the current day PCW Hall of Famers. So it was only natural that the moment I graduated high school I stepped foot into a nearby wrestling school only to have the living hell kicked out of me. Needless to say I decided being a wrestler wasn't for me. So I ended up going to wrestling show after show, and doing my best to earn respect from the wrestlers. This was all because I knew I could be helpful to wrestling even if it wasn't in the ring. Needless to say I made it. I went onto be a number of things for a number of promotions. Head booker, color commentator, ring announcer, road agent, etc. You name it I did it. However, for the past 2 years I was in semi retirement from wrestling at the age of 29. I was enjoying life along with my fiancé` and another former wrestling personality, Jessica Connely. When the day came where I received a call from officials at PCW. My hopes instantly shot up as the Pimply Faced Intern Kid told me that PCW wanted me to come aboard for the reopening after a six month hiatus. Those hopes were instantly crushed as I was told I'd only be writing a column for the official website.......so here we are today. Now that your history lesson is over I'm sure all of you that will be reading this would like to know just what the hell you can expect from this column. I will try to keep things fresh meaning one week I could give you my opinions on the matches of the most recent events, interview a member of the PCW family, discuss interesting rumors, or any other of a number of things. Either way you can expect my words of wisdom. Before I go however I'd like to plug the return show of PCW on December 9th, live from Atlantic City, NJ. I know a lot of the guys in the back, new or old. Let me tell you this...PCW looks more ready for Primetime then ever before. Until next time, it's been your pleasure. -"Wise Ass" Josh Wilkes
