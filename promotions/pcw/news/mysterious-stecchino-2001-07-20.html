Mysterious Stecchino

20 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been documented over the past few weeks, that Stecchino has been seen in deep conversation with a few men backstage. Namely... Revenant, Inphino Blitz, Jason Folt and for some reason or another Badge. Tony Stecchino has also been seen hanging out with a tag team down in PWA called the West Coast Connection. While going through Stecchino's trash there has been numerous long distance calls to Jacob Yoder and Tyler Nelson. But What can we make of this? Stecchino talking with members of Greed Inc. When talking to Stecchino's wife Melissa, she has said that "Nelson calls Tony and then he tells me to leave the room. It gets all quiet and then there is a lot of yelling and screaming. Then Tony throws the phone. I hate it when Tyler calls." It also has been reported that Stecchino could possibly grooming the PWA tag team, West Coast Connection for something big in PCW. When a PCW worker passed Stecchino dressing room at Turmoil, Badge and Revenant were seen leaving Stecchino's locker room hours before the event happened. Then minutes later. Inphino Blitz and Ming entered and were in there for over an hour. Then just before his Extreme Contenders match, Stecchino was overheard talking to some one named Jacob on his cell phone. Then Jeremy Howard came up to Tony just before he and Storm got into it and told Tony something about getting with him later tonight to go over some business. This absolutely is making no sense. What is going to become of this in the coming weeks. Only time will tell. For now, this is the backstage informer.
