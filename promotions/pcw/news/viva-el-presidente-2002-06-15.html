Viva El Presidente!! Posted by Sandy Wong on 15 Jun 2002 ---- Towson, Maryland- Some shocking things have happened in PCW in the past, but none may be more shocking than what happened on Fusion this week. The CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson, the self proclaimed 'Greediest Player in the Game' did what many thought was unthinkable for him.....he passed on his chance at the PCW World Title. He let us all in on the reason at the top of the show, but made us wait to find out who his replacement would be in the main event World Title match against Big Alan Steele. And what a replacement it was. To find out who could possibly fill the CEO's shoes, check out the Fusion results. Also on Fusion, Politically Incorrect battled for the Extreme Title....or did they? We also saw the return of Akiyama as he took on 'The Malice Man' Duke Williams, but what was Akiyama doing at the end of the Chef/Parshin vs Misplaced match? To find the answers to all these questions and see a whole lot more action, follow the drop down menu to results and click on the Fusion banner.
