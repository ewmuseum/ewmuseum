The Dog House #2

23 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Dog House #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Sep 2001 ---- Well, the Pay Per View went down as one of the low points in recent PCW history. I won’t mention the MATCH(es) or SEGMENT that pulled the show into the basement, because I think everyone is clear on that. It started with the sickness of Greed Inc showing through and ended with the triumph of Chef. I went eight for twelve with my predictions, which is somewhere between Ms. Cleo and Latoya Jackson. What do I think about what happened? As expected, Rent-a-Hero held the Rising Star Championship. I chose Mr. UK, because I think he deserves a lot of props for his hard work. I think, and hope, he has a bright future in PCW. But Rent-a-Hero’s a big deal in this business, and he’s showing that to everyone. A feud between Vampir Nosferatu and Boda could be great, as Vampir took the rights to a shot at Boda’s Continental title home. I think Vamp is a future world champion, although lately, it seems he’s a bit off. Chainsaw Massacre promo’s are a fun idea, but they really don’t move anything along. If Vampir brings his A-game, watch for he and Boda to tear arenas down. I think Vampir will take Boda’s title somewhere down the line. Maybe then Boda can take a look at the World Championship. We’re all going to be treated to a wonderful feud, or at least a match, between Big Alan Steele and Snow. Both of them are seen as future gems. But what really gets me excited about this is BAS is the ultimate face while Snow is the ultimate heel. It’s going to be fun to watch. So Dan Hollywood is the PCW’s new commissioner. What’s going to happen to PCW??? Jeremy Howard is CEO, or at least, might be CEO, and Dan Hollywood is commish... expect Duke Williams and Snow to reap the benefits of their stablemate’s victories. PCW looks very Greedy at the moment. But with those wins, where does that leave Tyler Nelson and Howard Porter? Did we literally see a changing of the guard at Back to the Old School? Jeremy Howard is our era’s Tyler Nelson, no doubt about it. I don’t know if Dan Hollywood is our era’s Howard Porter, but Porter and Nelson just jumped into the same boat. Former Superstars who can’t seem to cut it these days. Has-beens? I can’t go so far to call a two time PCW Champion a has been. And Howard Porter is Howard Porter. Chef is/was our new champion. I can’t say he doesn’t deserve it, with the work he’s put in over the last year, but where does Ivan go from here? No, not back to mother Russia. Ivan’s caught in a groove right now. It’s a good groove, but it falls short of the PCW title by about an inch. What should Ivan do? Personally, I think he should fall out of the main event scene for a few minutes; have a few feuds that actually don’t involve the world title. Something to rebuild his character and confidence at the monster that we know he can be. But, in comes Streak, and a low key Stanislav is out of the question. Here’s a little something I find amusing. Want to know what it’s like to be an inactive PCW newcomer? Ask Tony Rolo, Sam Potright, or Jake Keeton. Want to know what it’s like to be an inactive established PCW star? Ask Trashcan Man or Howard Porter. I thought Popov might have been given an easier ride. So TCM’s been hitting the bottle a little too much lately? Maybe his promos were too slurred to air. Who knows? Whatever the case, he goes down as one of the worst champions in PCW history. Lets hope he doesn’t end his legacy on such a bad note. And does anyone understand why every single major federation import turn up being a bust? Why not bring up talent from inside PTC? Isn’t that what PTC’s designed for? And then there was Turmoil, which was possibly the best non-PPV in PCW history. Action from start to finish. Just one thing though, I thought a lethal lottery was drawn at random? Streak and Ivan together? So Meanstreak is back. For how long? How long before the monster slips back into some form of absence and leaves the wrestling world resting on HIS laurels? Personally, I have to come right out and say that even though it looks like Streak’s the best thing for this business, he’s also the riskiest. What happens if Meanstreak disappears yet again? It just leaves us with this monster who we keep hyping, yet have seen about 4 promos and 6 matches from in the last five months. If he sticks around this time, great! He’s probably the best in PCW history, and nothing but good can come of it. If he stays for about four days again, like last time... well, then we’re all left hanging. To PCW newcomers, Meanstreak is a faceless hype. An unbeatable faceless hype, yes, but still just as faceless. The real monster of our age is Ivan Stanislav. I think Streak and Ivan are going to march to the finals, and then Ivan will beat Streak all the way back to the motherland. Well, more realistically, a team of mental institution workers will rush down to the ring and drag the monster off to the nut house. Then we’ll be reading reports about how there’s been no change in Meanstreak’s mental state for the next three months. What’s the one knock on PCW right now? How long has it been since we’ve had a worthwhile champion? I’ll tell you. It’s been over a third of a year! Jeremy Howard doesn’t really count, because the title was stripped before he got a chance to be a worthy champion. There was hope for Chef, but now it seems we’ll be going another month without a champion. Here are the weekly awards... Unsung Hero First of all, imagine what it must be like being D-Kay Calyx? I just saw that he’s been shipped from federation to federation and is now back in PWA. I swear, if a bus had room for 5, D-Kay would be holding number six. Seriously, with all the busts that we get from other federations, why not turn just one of them down and give their spot to D-Kay Calyx? Personally, I don’t really know how much talent he has. All I know is that he’s stuck with PTC all this time and has been mentioned repeatedly as on the verge of coming to PCW. So he must have some talent. And his loyalty has already been put to the test, and he passed with flying colors. I see that PCW is looking for a spot for him, and I hope they find one. Or even DWF. I don’t care where he goes, but for goodness sakes, get the guy out of PWA! In the Doghouse Lucky for Tyler Nelson, he’s showed up lately and taken himself out of eligibility for this award. So my doghouse award goes to none other than Kaiser. Usually, I’d like to reserve this award for slacking PCW talent, and I know that this guy has none. But still, when you’re that bad, people have to give you some verbal smack. Till next time, I’m the only columnist with the gonads to tell it like it is. -The Hound PS - if parts of this column seem a bit odd, it's because it was written before Turmoil, and edited after.
