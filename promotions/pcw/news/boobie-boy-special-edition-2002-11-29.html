Boobie Boy : Special Edition Posted by Sandy Wong on 29 Nov 2002 ---- 20 Reasons why Xavier Kannon is an Asshole Volume #1 Inaugural Edition Well Hello ladies and gentleman my name Is Matt Elia otherwise known as Boobie Boy~! As many of you now know PCW has bought out my new home RUSH wrestling and many of us RUSH wrestlers are extremely unhappy about this. So with the help of my fellow RUSH Resistance activist we have come up with our very own column called WHY XAVIER KANNON IS AN ASSHOLE. So Scroll down and you will understand why Xavier Kannon is an asshole. 20) Well first off Xavier Kannon is very egotistical. Most people who are in fact that into themselves are hiding something. In Kannon’s case probably a strong insecurity of his appearance or a neglected home life. Miss your Mommy Kannon? 19) If Kannon is insecure about his looks it is obvious. I mean scruffy beard long golden locks he obviously thinks that we are still living in the 80s. 18) His booking policies, take a look at this weeks card. Do you see a match for him anywhere? Hmm…. 17) He may own the best federation in pro-wrestling today, but can he go home to his house and say that people actually like him? I think it is safe to say that Kannon is the most hated man in all of wrestling today as well. But don’t worry Kannon at least you have a wife that loves you… 16) Ellie~! Does she even know what she talks about sometimes…only an asshole would date a dumb blonde? It is obvious that Kannon is with her to make himself feel better about his own short comings. 15) He hired Players Inc. Who ever hires Dante Jackson, and Status Quo must in fact be an asshole. 14) Look who he surrounds himself with…Brandi and Tim Walden. To hang out with those two you must be an asshole. 13) He jobbed to Angelo Deville at Alliances. Angelo Deville is an asshole so the fact that Kannon lost to him makes him an even bigger asshole. 12) He bought the rights to Battle Field Earth 2, the sequel to the worst movie ever…who ever would actually watch that movie is an asshole. 11) He lives in the past…when was the last time you heard Kannon talk about how he won the NTICW world title? How about 15 minuets ago in the locker room right? What an asshole. 10) He was sponsored by Snapple~! Who ever drinks Snapple is beyond an asshole. They are a bad tasted asshole. 09) Xavier Kannon worships a frozen head. Wow…isn’t that just a little weird? I mean that is beyond asshole that is borderline prickish. 08) He doesn’t get clues. As Kannon reads this column he probably doesn’t get that this is all about him and why he is an asshole. Just another reason why he is, in fact an asshole. 07) He named his finisher after his wife. AFTER HIS WIFE…COME ON… 06) His theme song is by Marylyn Manson who is perhaps the biggest asshole in the entire music world. 05) He lives in a town called Hubbardville which is named after that frozen head in which he worships. And get this, that frozen head created a religion that well, resolves in chat rooms across the world. Good thing he keeps a lap top with him to pray…asshole. 04) He cheated to win his own PPV. With out the help of his new sellout RUSH buddies he would have lost. This just shows that he is….well you know. 03) He bought out RUSH. He stuck his nose in business that he shouldn’t have even bothered to. This makes him a humongous asshole. 02) He will always live in Jeremy Howard’s shadow. And until he realizes this…He will always be an asshole. 01) He picked a fight with Marvin Harris, The Masked Mullet, and I and we won’t stop this until we get what we want. And that is RUSH freedom and the admittance that Xavier Kannon is an ASSHOLE. Till next time, Boobie Boy~!
