Magical Misery Tour...

26 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Magical Misery Tour... Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Jan 2002 ---- Bangkok, Thailand- One year ago Tyler Nelson and Media took control of PCW from The Phantom Booker. Ever since then Kid Wonder has hated the man that has stood against everything he has believed in. On this weeks edition of Friday Fusion, Kid Wonder sought to exact some revenge for every dirty thing that Tyler Nelson has done over the last year. What did the Quintessential Teen Idol have in store? Would the Triple Crown Executive make it through the night? Also on Fusion, the upcoming battle for the PCW Heavyweight Championship started a few days early. How far would Jeremy Howard and Jason Snow go to get at each other? All this plus several battles for number one contenderships, as well as a public hanging? Click the Television link and follow it through to the Friday Fusion banner. GO NOW!!
