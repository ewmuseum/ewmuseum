Chris Chase to be sponsored by Wal-Mart? Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Jul 2002 ---- Sources close to that of recently-returned ‘Saint’ Chris Chase have revealed that the former PCW World Champion is close to penning a sponsorship deal with Wal-Mart, the World’s largest Retailer. Raking in Worldwide Sales in excess of $100 Billion dollars per annum, the considerable ‘Image Improving’ weight that could be thrown behind the Canadian wrestler would be in some unimaginable region. Some question, however, lies on the reasons for Chris Chase’s connection with the brand, which has only come to fruition recently with Dustin Downey’s bizarre behavior at one of their outlets of recent days. A Wal-Mart spokesman had this to say: “In this modern day and age, a Company must adjust to the trends to see it’s profits remain at an acceptable level. For this reason, given the increase in attention that the PCW has been receiving recently, we at Wal-Mart see it fit to back one of the more lucrative performers in his efforts to conquer the federation’s highest climes. He’s one of PCW’s younger, but ultimately more experienced grapplers, who serves as the perfect role model for today’s American – no matter how…Canadian…he may be. Look for this deal to be finished by the end of this week” So far…neither Chris Chase nor Dustin Downey have been available for a comment, following the incident on Monday.
