Column of Crunk #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 31 Mar 2002 ---- Column of Crunk#1 By Will Otto Here it is: The first Column of Crunk. I haven’t figured out what exactly will be the main point of this column, but who says I can’t keep writing anyway? The first thing I’d like to address is the situation with the titleholders. Now, we may have the strongest top 3 title holders (Boda, Krayzie, Aki) that we’ve had in a long time, as the television title was revived by Miyagawa and Akiyama. We’ve also got one of the better Extreme Title holders we’ve seen recently in Jason Core. However, the other three titles seem to just float around. Now let me say something about the tag team division. I think it’s great to actually have one. I think it’s fantastic that PCW is giving these new guys a shot to prove their tag team prowess. I also think that Juan Borro is a deserving ½ champion of the tag team belts. But where the hell is Kevin Klaymore? He hasn’t had a singles match in about a month and a half and he’s come back to PCW about three times during that stretch. Juan is a deserving champ, but he can’t do it alone I don’t think. Within the next two weeks, we will have new tag champions. What about the cruiserweight championship? At Shang-Haied 2, this was the 2nd most sought after belt between Juan Borro, Xavier Kannon, and Chris Chase. Now our champion is thinking about whether he should give up his gimmick? Much of the same thing is happening with the Rising Star Title. Our champion has decided to take a break onto the injured list, which is fine, but it just doesn’t seem like the belt means anything anymore. At Shang-Haied 2, there were about a dozen people who all put out 6+ RPs and now we see champions no-show on weekly shows. Well that’s the end of my title rant. Next up is the feuds. This is what people say have been missing from PCW. The high-power feuds. We’ve just been having matchups at PPV’s made but the only ones that really develop are the top two on the card. For the last two PPV’s I would say this is pretty much true. March Madness had two big matches: Maxwell v. Krayzie and the Brawl for It All. But can you name another match on the card that people were talking about? The only exception I can think of again was the Kannon, Chase, and Borro match at SH2. However, the heat was mainly between Kannon and Chase and culminated really into a push for both men. Now, to Spring Fling. I think that PCW has realized their mistake, and some people have stepped up to try and make those mid-card or lower-card feuds that much better. First off, we’ve got Boda and Chris Chase. I don’t quite understand this feud, because there is no clear cut face, but that also makes it possible for the return of a face, the SWERVE~! of a heel, or interference by a face, setting up a feud for the May PPV. Without a heel though, this feud mainly revolves around Miyagawa trying to get his wife back from Boda, and Chase acting as an attacker. I honestly thought that Miya would get his title shot back in a three-way dance because he would run the gauntlet and win, but I’m not quite sure what happened there. However, this should nonetheless be a great match for the PPV, as both can put out lots of quality RP’s. I’m not going to make any predictions on anything until the card is finalized though. Next we’ve got Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon. For me, this is a match made in heaven. Kannon has unlimited potential as a heel, while Krayzie is my pick for destructive babyface(since Streak has gone heel). In my opinion, this is the most important match on the card. For the second time in as many PPV’s, the career of an NTICW can be helped greatly. We’ll see what happens but the RP’s for this should be fantabulous. Now there’s Corey Williams and Miyagawa. I’m not quite sure how the hell Williams got this match, but Miya can’t be too happy about not getting that title shot. I think this feud may evolve a bit over the next week, but it’s not going to be anything spectacular. However, this match will not be the highlight of the night for either man, because we will see both involved in the World Title Match. As a side-note, I think Corey Williams will win the TV Title in the next two months, he is perfect for that division. Miyagawa on the other hand, will probably feud with Boda and then Xavier Kannon. Frisco and Legend Inc. is a highly touted matchup but so far has boiled down into two brawls last week. However, this week we will probably see some stuff from these guys, which will make their match even more highly anticipated. Frisco has a Grand Slam already, but Legend needs to prove himself in PCW like he has done in other feds. Look for a smaller RP total, but a lot of quality from these guys, who have very different RP styles. We’ve also got on the card another important match, Big Allan Steele v Chef. Here is another feud that has been going well but we should see some extra effort in this last haul before the PPV. With BAS concocting some weirdo stipulation, have no doubt that Chef will have his say about it and probably will do something about it. A lot of humourous RP’s will come from these two PPV time, and I expect that this will mean the REAL return of BAS, because after this we will be seeing the BAS that we saw last year around the time of the Lethal Lottery tournament. HI-KEEBA~! Futher down on the card, we’ve got two feuds that could boil up to great matches at the PPV. For the TV title, there’s a match that could very well be for the Continental Title: Akiyama and the Pharaoh. These former tag partners are fighting for much more than just the TV title though. Because of the three fall situation we can speculate that the person who keeps the TV title will NOT keep their “GOD” moniker. This match is probably the one I’m looking forward to most at the PPV. This feud has been pretty highly tauted for a while, ever since the Divinity broke up. Pharaoh has deserved a good singles feud like this for a long time, while Akiyama will help himself no matter what the outcome. These two also have possibly the most innovative movesets in the entire federation, and it’s always fun to see high spots. Lastly, I’ll talk a little about the feud for the Extreme Title. I’m not going to try to use my medium to promote myself heavily, but I’m going to say that some people have underrated the ongoing feud between OG, LeBlanc, J-Core, and Rush. I don’t see Badge, though his expierience and humor writing are always a contender, or Big D, whose career seems to be stuck in neutral, playing big parts. I think both are better off staying with their tag team partners. So, the feud between the four has been going on throughout the middle card for the last week and a half and I think, and hope, that people have noticed the work that has been put in to try and make this a quality feud. This should be a sleeper match as well, with lots of extreme stunts, high spots, maybe if we’re lucky we can slip in some NIPPAGE~! Those are basically the only really feuds I see on the card YET. I say YET because Darkstar and Chet King, as well as Stormbringer vs End, will probably become better feuds over the next week, and the two final teams for the tag team match will be announced. The last thing I’ll do in my first column is just to do an interesting thing showing where those guys who first came in from Yoder’s NTICW are right now and grade their performance. Xavier Kannon- The highly tauted Scientological prospect has so far lived up to all the hype about him but Spring Fling may prove whether he’s really ready to step up into a main event, upper card, player. I personally think that Kannon has been, and is ready for this job. He is probably one of the best PWA students ever and I think everyone in PCW is glad to have him around. Grade: A Drake Maxwell- I’m not going to say much on this matter, only that Drake made a mistake and he realizes it. Starting over is the best thing for him, and he has handled the situation well from what I’ve seen. The Pharaoh- Pharaoh was one of the main players on the World Title scene in NTICW, but seemed to come into PCW without any major glamour about him. However, he quickly aligned himself with the powerful Akira Akiyama and instantly started to make an impact. Since then, he has proven that he is a future Continental Title contender. His amazing gimmick and good roleplaying skills are a good showing of the type of product PWA can produce. Grade: A- Frankie Bones Left for OSW. Asa Fountain-Retired, came back, retired again. Mark Estep- Came, went, came again, went again Jay Sylo- Came in and has so far had a good career but at the moment is experiencing some writer’s block. Jay is one of the best newcomers in PCW, and I hope that he can find his new writing niche because he will be a valuable part of this fed in the months to come. Anton the Kid Like many others, Anton has had problems with gimmick/writing, but he is the Cruiserweight champion. He has not made that much of an impact however, winning the title on a no-show by TMM. While he still has much to prove, he can do it if he regains his form from main eventing in NTICW. Grade: B Rush Mason One of only four characters active at the moment from Yoder’s NTICW. So far Rush’s biggest accomplishments are being #6 in the March Madness Main Event and being a part of the Malice Crew. Rush is just making it onto the big scene right now, but things with the Malice Crew may heat up in the next few months. Grade: B- There we go. That’s all of the OLD NTICW guys who came in and my assessment of them. Next column, I’ll profile NEW NTICW guys and EW guys who came into PCW, along with PPV Predictions. In memory of our fallen wrestler, one last time, I shall utter the famous words. “DOUG TROY JOBBED TO HANK~! HANK~! I SAY!”
