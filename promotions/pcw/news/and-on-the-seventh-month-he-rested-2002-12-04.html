And on the Seventh Month He Rested

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
And on the Seventh Month He Rested Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Dec 2002 ---- Everyone bore witness to a shocking event this past Turmoil as Akiyama's own daughter Cyclonica knocked him out cold with repeated shots to the skull with a sledgehammer...allowing Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Kannon-Hall to escape from the Neo Void God's clutches. The question is...WHY?? Tiffany Jennings tried to coax come word out of her regarding her actions, but Cyclonica only had the following to say as she had EMTs strap Akiyama down to a stretcher, while security shackled him and muzzle the god. Cyclonica: Due to the intense schedule that Akiyama and I have been under since our return to PCW several months ago, we are taking a well overdue vacation... ALL EXPENSES PAID... of course. Akiyama has earned what he got...So says the Divine Prophetess... Tiffany failed to get any word on why she stood in Akiyama's way, but what was understood was that Cyclonica and Akiyama would take a sabbatical from PCW until his "chronic" skull trauma healed...however long that may take...
