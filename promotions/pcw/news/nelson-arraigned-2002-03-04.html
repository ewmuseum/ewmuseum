Nelson Arraigned

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nelson Arraigned Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Mar 2002 ---- Tyler Nelson was arraigned in Federal Court early Monday morning on numerous counts of bribery, blackmail, extortion, and various other charges. Since Nelson recently fired his attorneys, and all his assets were frozen by the Department of Justice, he was forced to accept a public defender as his council. The CEO of PCW entered the courtroom locked in shackles, dressed in his prison issue orange jumpsuit and a pair of orange rubber flip flops, looking disgusted the entire way. At the outset of the arraignment, Nelson was beligerent and abusive toward the prosecutor, His Honor Judge George Jefferson, and his own defence attorney, at one point calling him a "back alley ambulance chaser". After several threats of being held in contempt Nelson finally settled down and the proceedings continued. After only a few minutes Nelson stood up, waived his rights to a jury trial and asked for a bench trial. A shocked Judge Jefferson granted the motion and adjourned until Wednesday morning. This interesting turn of events leaves a lot open to speculation, as rumors of jury tampering were already running rampant. Without the jury that leaves only Judge Jefferson to decide the fate of one of the most hated men in the world, Tyler Nelson. Nelson will continue to be held without bail as Judge Jefferson deemed the CEO a "flight risk". We will continue to keep you up to date as things unfold in the next few days, with the future of PCW hanging in the balance.
