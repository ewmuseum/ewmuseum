Aimz-RAH on the Rocks? Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 May 2002 ---- As of late their has been many reports as of late that Amy "Aimz" Campbell, OSW superstar and WT female talent has been out and about at various places and hotels with fellow OSW star, Reno Davis. Now this may not sound too out of sorts with you, but the reason this is is because of Amy Campbell's romantic relationship with PCW's Rent-A-Hero. When asked about what he thought about the recent reports that Amy Campbell might be cheating on him, PCW's superhero replied, "Nonsense, why would my red-headed angel ever think to cheat on her spandex love rabbit. I'm sure all her and Reno like to do is play patty cake and such, I trust her with all my Kryptonian heart. Now if you'll excuse me, I need to find the nearest spandex dealer good citizen." As the comment implied, Rent-A-Hero seems pretty trusting of her girlfriend and does not seem to be effected by this news. More to come on this story as we find it.
