Phoenix Rising

6 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News of the PCW reopening, after a layoff of just under six months, shocked the wrestling industry. Immediately questions were asked about why Tyler Nelson had reopened one of the most influential and well known federations in the business … who had signed up for his company … what had happened to the title holders when the PCW closed it’s doors? So many questions, and so few answers coming forth. … one voice however could be heard among the noise, and one statement made that added to the questions. Whose voice? The voice of Jay Phoenix. Speaking from his sprawling home in Flagstaff, Arizona, Phoenix was asked if the news of PCW’s resurrection had any impact on his own state of semi-retirement. Phoenix … who had short runs in both the PCW and PWC under Ron Starr’s tenure … had only one thing to say. “ PCW is a place that has a legendary status, and to see it rise like this from the ashes of it’s own destruction is something that I can empathise with. I joined the PCW originally to get some payback against Vampir Nosferatu, but thanks to the machinations of Ron Starr … the Phantom Booker … I was kept away from ‘his’ boy, and I left the federation unfulfilled. I then joined the PWC … another Starr venture … while recuperating from a knee injury, an injury that Starr exploited as he sent another of his favourites, Romulus, after me. I don’t know what I did to earn the animosity of Starr, except for perhaps be a success in this industry and turn down his offer of making me a legend … I didn’t need his help and told him so. While it is truthful to say that while HIS PCW and PWC didn’t survive, just proving that Starr himself was nothing, it is also fair to say that my time in those ederations is the only things I look back on and wish I could do differently. … I don’t like leaving places without fulfilling my goals, and I was not able to do so in either place. That is the past though, and I am now contemplating my future, and whether that involves the PCW at this time is something that I am not prepared to discuss. … I will say though that when the time comes for me to return to this industry, I can promise you all one thing … I WILL be “ready” " With that remark, Phoenix gave his trademark smile and finished the interview, leaving the new question to be added to the list. … ready for what?
