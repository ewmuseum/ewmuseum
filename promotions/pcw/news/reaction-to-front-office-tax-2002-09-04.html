Reaction To Front Office Tax

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reaction To Front Office Tax Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Sep 2002 ---- After the announcement that the Front Office have introduced a 40% Tax on wrestler’s salaries to cover what they call “Overheads”, the reaction in the back was hostile to say the least. "The tax? If he's not trying to put down the black man in it, then I'm all for it. But if he is...I'M STRAIGHT JACKIN FOR THE CONSTITUTION!" ranted Dante Jackson. When a reporter asked Dante just exactly what his previous statement meant, Dante replied with the following, "Err...I meant that I'm taking the Constitution and fitting it to my ways, can't you understand me speaking your cracka language?!" “It's a damn conspiracy to keep the black man down. It ain't like I ain't already got enough stuff to pay off, and now I’ve lost 40% of ma cash? Now, it'll take fo'eva to pay off all this jewellery I be buying. First I get teamed with a nazi so I can no longer walk through Harlem, without bodyguards and now y'all taking ma cash... it ain't right!” was Duzza’s comments. Miyagawa, who will face Xavier Kannon this coming Tuesday added the following, "I now have you hand over cash to Xavier Kannon and his cronies whenever I get paid? Well this is simple maths for the Front Office. Whatever is taken from Asylum will simply end up being spent on repairs to Arena's damaged by Asylum." While current checks are ongoing from the Wrestler’s Union as to whether the Tax is legal, it seems that the PCW roster will have to accept losing 40% of their earnings.
