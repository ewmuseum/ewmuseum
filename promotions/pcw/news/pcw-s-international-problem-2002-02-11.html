PCW's International Problem

11 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW's International Problem Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Feb 2002 ---- Finally, after extensive research, Japanese officials have figured out if PCW is a 'problem.' The Tukashimi Corporation, the head of this research, and their top researcher, Mushomita Tawakai, had a press conference. Mushomita Tawakai stated, "PCW has not been the problem, but a certain individual within has. We do not who yet however." Following those comments, Mushomita Tawakai brought out a graph showing the recent similarites in all of the sites that hosted PCW. "As you can see, Singapore, Tokyo and Shanghai have been hit hard with recent car accidents that have crippled the insurance bureau's there. Koala Lumpur has been expecially been devastated by reckless driving, shootings, and suicides. We hope to find out what is causing all this." said Mushomita Tawakai. Heads of PCW stated that they will see to this problem as they are now going under an extensive re-evaluation of many of their superstars. "One explanation is that there are certain individuals in PCW that have been traumatized in recent weeks from illness and deaths. We suspect that some of thses people may have been lashing out at others because of their size, strength and fame. We will investigate those men and women," stated an anonomous PCW official, "but their are certain people in PCW who have similar gimmicks like these, and may be going a little too far out of line." When asked if they have any suspects of the cause of horrible accidents, they replied, "Well, it just might be a coincidence... but we do have a man that seems to cause trouble around the people he's around." We will bring more news to you when we find more to give to you.
