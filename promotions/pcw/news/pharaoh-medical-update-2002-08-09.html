9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pharaoh Medical Update Posted by Sandy Wong on 9 Aug 2002 ---- After the devastating attack upon 'The Pharaoh' Titu Xansiv at this past Tuesday's edition of Turmoil, Pharaoh was hastily escorted backstage where he was checked over by paramedics for the hideous burn he received around his eye courtesy of one Francisco Cassius Valerius. Pharaoh, who after the attack ran a Landrover into the side of Frisco's get-away limo as a form of revenge, was given good news that the injury would not blind the Egyptian whatsoever. Paramedics at the scene of the carnage, and later Doctors, report that Pharaoh shouldn't miss any action, but will need to keep his wound dressed with a crude bandage for a few weeks until the burn heels. "Well, Mr Xansiv'll need all the protection he can get now, won't he?" reported one of Pharaoh's supposed 'troops' when asked about the condition of him commander. Keep it 'hush-hush, mind you." With those words the man slowly walked away keeping check that nobody saw him speaking with us, especially The Pharaoh we would imagine. As usual, however, we'll keep you posted as more information becomes available.
