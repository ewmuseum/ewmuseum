Klaymore Chomping At Bit

23 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Klaymore Chomping At Bit Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Oct 2001 ---- Darlier this week we caught up with "The Bomb" Kevin Klaymore who was hard at work during rehabilitation workouts. Although his knee is still heavily taped up he cannot wait to get back in the ring. "I swear man I can't wait to get back in the ring and do what I do best," said Klaymore. "Riptah I know you took me out cause you were scared that I would be better but I'm not the 'Hater type'. The Circle of Justice will bring you to justice and the Golden Game has just begun man. The game has just begun." Kevin continued while working heavily on his injured knee trying to get it ready for his return in a week. "This is my life man and I live, eat, breath for wrestling and I can only say when I come back I will be, much like Special K cereal, new and improved," spouted Klaymore. "The Circle of Justice will also get back on track and become one of the biggest stables in PCW. We will give everybody an equal chance in a match. Even if it is Two Ton versus a luchadore. It will still be one on one the way a fight is intended." Kevin is a little anxious when he returns but will ring rust show or will the new and improved Kevin Klaymore show? Only time will tell. We will keep you informed when new information arises.
