Media vs TPB v2.0?

17 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Media vs TPB v2.0? Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 May 2002 ---- There have been numerous reports of meetings between former Media members Tyler Nelson, Dan Hollywood, and Chet King. These witnesses have reported seeing these three men, along with Buffy Vegas and Cindy Bi, out to dinner on several occasions since the recent return of The Phantom Booker and subsequent firing of the PCW roster. A few people who were able to overhear the former stablemates have stated that they were discussing a possible reformation of Media to restore law and order in PCW after it introduces it's new roster next week. It's well known that these three men despise the returning Phantom Booker and could be looking to counter-balance his influence and the seemingly sympathetic swell of support he seems to be getting due to the actions of his estranged wife, the owner of PCW's main competition OSW, Jane Reagan. Could we be on the verge of another epic battle between Media and The Phantom Booker? Only time will tell.
