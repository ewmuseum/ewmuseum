El Diablo Livid

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
El Diablo Livid Posted by Sandy Wong on 30 Nov 2002 ---- Immediately after Friday Fusion, El Diablo was found to be absolutely livid. PCW caught up with him as he was headed to Xavier Kannon's office. El Diablo: Fusion was a f*cking joke! It was four on one against me, and I never signed up for a damned handicap match. The Mexican Devil then picked up a trash can and threw it against the wall. El Diablo: Clyde is a f*cking corrupt asshole who for one is not fit to be an official, isn't fit to be a wrestler and in no way should still be the PCW Champion. I HAD HIM BEAT AT ALLIANCES, BUT CORRUPTION RULED THAT DAY. El D then kicks the walls several times in frustration and anger. El Diablo: That reminds me, I'm on my way to Kannon's office to get my corruption free Title shot and to have Alliances stricken from the record regarding me. But Kannon really did bug me on Fusion, when he referred to Duke being the "THIRD" chair, I know it was really a subliminal message aimed at me, for being eliminated third at Alliances. Angela, El Diablo's wife then walked out and joined Diablo. Angela: Come on dear, let’s go see Xavier Kannon about all this. As he walks away, he is again heard muttering under his breath. El Diablo: Bah, I hate this damned corrupt machine. They then walked off in the direction of DA BOSS' office.
