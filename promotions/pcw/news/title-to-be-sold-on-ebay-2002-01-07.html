Title to be Sold on Ebay?

7 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title to be Sold on Ebay? Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Jan 2002 ---- Last night while Jeremy Howard was out looking for his son, Mexi managed to break into his house and steal the World title. Information of the sale came from an anonymous source that is famous for jobbing to a PCW referee. "I tried to talk him out of it but he has become so obsessed with money since Mona threatend to take him to the cleaners. He says he's going to auction the belt off after having some fun with it." Mexi later contacted PCW representatives and stated that the bidding will open at 10am tommorow morning and close at 1pm. Bidders can place there bids on EBAY. Lets just hope that someone will bring the title back to PCW and on a side note let's hope that greedy bitch Mona is happy for making her husband resort to such a ploy for money. Although on a rather sad note... no one appeared to tell Mexi that they keep spare belts at PCW Headoffice, so a new one was simply Fed-EX'ed to Jeremy's House for the following morning.
