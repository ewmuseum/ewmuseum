Newbludd's Reaction to the Rednecks

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Newbludd's Reaction to the Rednecks Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Dec 2002 ---- We recently caught up with former PCW star and current Rush roster member, "The Innovator" Brock Newbludd for a quick interview. He was found drunk at a local bar in Milwaukee, or "training the mental aspects of his wrestling", as he called it. When told about the stipulation of free alcohol put on by the Front Office if Duke Williams wins the main event at Decimation. Newbludd raised his head off the bar and was quoted saying this in a slurred voice... "No sh*t?" Well, I'll be a redneck on that day then. Go Malice Man! Hey, didn't he have a radio show or something...erm..well...I'll make sure to stay sober all day for the free after party...Hey did you know that I can write my name in the snow?" We followed Newbludd as he stumbled/crawled out of the bar, after writing "Bro" in the snow, he tripped over his unzipped pants and fell into the snowbank. After regaining his composure, he said... "Dick will win on Ssssunday, ya know why? Cause Kannon's a p-p-pussy and he stole Rrrush! Free beer!" Newbludd passed out in the show immeiaditaly after that...
