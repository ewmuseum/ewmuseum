German Reaction

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
German Reaction Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Nov 2002 ---- Upon the recent news that PCW has "purchased" RUSH, one of the latter's roster members and resident German, Matt Hunt had a rather laid back response. "Pfft, als wenn ich ein gebe, verdammt" When asked to repeat the comment but in English, Hunt seemed happy to oblige "I said, I don't really care. The major point of my concern at this time is knocking that mooch GOD off his high pedistal." He continued, in his near perfect English tone "As long I have a contract and I'm being paid for my serivces - I don't care" After the selfish comments, the German also made an announcement: "Seeing as I've been recently forced into a becoming a twisted indivdual, I'm decided to re-invent myself with a slighted altered nickname. You knew me as Das Technik...but now, and here comes the good bit...mein neues name ist...Das Dangerous Techink. Spot the abbrevation, mooch?" We're soon to hear from Hunt....err...DDT in the coming weeks as this intriguing storyline continues.
