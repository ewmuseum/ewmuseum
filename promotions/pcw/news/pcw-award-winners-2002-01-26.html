PCW Award Winners

26 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW Award Winners Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Jan 2002 ---- The ballots are in. The final numbers for PCW's 12 awards for the year 2001. The big winner, to almost nobody's surprise, was Tyler Nelson as he claimed a share in four awards. Chet King and Ivan Stanislav, out of current wrestlers, claimed three a piece. And The Phantom Booker, to almost nobody's surprise, won the first ever Soy Boy Memorial Award for PCW contribution. Below is the list of award winners and the top two runners-up. Wrestler of the Year 1st: Ivan Stanislav 2nd: Jeremy Howard 3rd: Frisco Tag Team of the Year 1st: Da Westsidaz 2nd: 18 Hole Assault 3rd: Blood Brothers Match of the Year 1st: Meanstreak vs. Ivan Stanislav at Red October in an Iron Man match 2nd: Alliances Main Event 3rd: Carnival of Carnage II Moment of the Year 1st: Media takeover at Shanghai'ed 2nd: Tim Walden jobbing to Leo Micelli 3rd: Finger Poke of Death Feud of the Year 1st: Media vs. PCW 2nd: Clyde vs. Trashcan Man 3rd: Ivan Stanislav vs. Meanstreak Most Improved Wrestler 1st: Boda 2nd: Darkstar 3rd: Jeremy Howard Biggest Disappointment 1st: Meanstreak vs. Judas Steele 2nd: Judas Steele 3rd: Reverend's 3rd PCW stint Stable of the Year 1st: Media 2nd (tie): Red Army 2nd (tie): Dark Age Manager/Valet of the Year 1st: Alexi Ruslan 2nd: Amanda 3rd: Buffy Vegas Face of the Year 1st (tie): Frisco 1st (tie): Kid Wonder 3rd: Jeremy Howard Heel of the Year 1st: Tyler Nelson 2nd: Ivan Stanislav 3rd: Clyde Soy Boy Memorial Award 1st: The Phantom Booker 2nd: Kid Wonder 3rd (tie): Miyagawa 3rd (tie): Duke Williams Congratulations to all of the winners.
