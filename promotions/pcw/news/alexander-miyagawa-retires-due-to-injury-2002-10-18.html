Alexander Miyagawa Retires Due to Injury

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alexander Miyagawa Retires Due to Injury Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Oct 2002 ---- A press release straight from Anaheim Central Hospital where PCW Wrestler Alexander Miyagawa is staying after the brutal attack by his former stable mate, Nerezza was released earlier today. "Dear Fans and Friends. I have a fractured skull. This may not seem like a bad thing to you and I should be fine pretty soon. That would be the case if it was a minor fracture. Unfortuantly, it isn't, it's several major ones. Nerezza slam my skull into that wall so hard that my skull is rather like eggshell now. Apparently I'm lucky not to have brain damage. I've got several months of major surgery as they attempt to replace almost my entire skull with solid metal. Then they have to reinforce my spine in order to prevent my neck from breaking as I nod my head. Then I have to find my wife and inform her that I was not kissing Anarchy, merly hugging her goodbye. Maybe and only maybe then will I return when my life is fully in check once again and I can concentrate on wrestling again. I would have hoped to have gone out in a blaze of glory, but it was not to be. Maybe I'll get a second chance to do that. Maybe not. Yours sincerly, Alexander Miyagawa."
