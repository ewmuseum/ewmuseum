PCW For Sale

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW For Sale Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Jan 2003 ---- As announced rather casually on Fusion, PCW owner Xavier Kannon has put PCW up for sale, although ‘Sale’ might not be the operative word. It is rumoured that Kannon is not looking for the highest bidder, nor the best person for the job... Rather someone to “Inflict” on Primetime to spite Duke, who’s boycott tactics forced him into jettisoning the company. The usual names such as Tyler Nelson and Jane Reagan have been floating about, but the only person known to have met with Xavier so far is former PCW star Avalanche who had the following to say. “I MADE PCW! I WAS IT’S BIGGEST STAR WITHOUT EVEN DOING ANYTHING! LOOK WHO THEY’VE GOT NOW, PEOPLE WHO’S GIMMICKS ACTUALLY MATCH THEIR NAME... WHAT’S THAT ALL ABOUT!?!?” Needless to say, not many are expecting the no-showing loser to be PCW owner anytime soon, but you never know.
