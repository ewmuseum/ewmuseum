"Mystery" Revealed

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Mystery" Revealed Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jun 2003 ---- As of the ending of Turmoil, the "Mystery Opponent" set to face Skylar Green for the PCW World Title was no longer so mysterious. Dorn Tracer, the #1 contender by...well...not much more then his own say and the will to back it up, says that he will come collect before Pharaoh can "win" the PCW title in a tainted match on June 29th's Tuesday Turmoil. With the two vice presidents seeming more and more reluctant to butt heads with anyone, they filled the spot with Dorn, rather then giving the non-title shot requested by Pharaoh. With the two girls being hopefully returned to Sky by Fusion, will that distract him in his defense, or will he have a clear head going into the match? Surely Tracer knows of the situation and will try to prey on Sky's mind with games, but only time will tell whether it will be effective. Also in attendance should be Pharaoh, who will certainly have something to say about his former stablemate getting a World Title shot just days before he's set to become PCW's Champion. Tune in to Fusion to see what happens in this complicated and tangled situation.
