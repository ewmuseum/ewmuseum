Kano on CCW and the Malice Crew

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kano on CCW and the Malice Crew Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 Jun 2002 ---- Recently, Cal Seaver caught up with the legendary former Continental, Television, and Rising Star Champion, Jon Kano while traveling to the site for Tuesday Turmoil. This coversation was supposed to be off the record, but as we all know, Cal is never off the record in his questions. Here is a great portion of their conversation as relayed by Cal Seaver over the phone. Cal: I asked Jon what he thought about Corey Williams after their recent battles with each other. Kano: I hope that little c*cksucker learned his lesson on Fusion. He goes off shooting his mouth like he is some big shot, like he is a legend. He keeps talking about me as a legend like its a bad thing. He gives that impression that I am some over the hill 50 year old premadonna who is going through a mid life crisis. I am twenty six. I began my career when I was twenty, and by the time I was 22, I was a legend, I had the game transformed. He calls me out, talking about how he is going to put me in a wheelchair, but when all was said and done, I was standing tall and proud, while he groggily made his way back after getting the Kanoplex from hell. He reminds me a lot of another little bastard that I handed his ass to, Tim Walden. The only difference is that Tim Walden had a shred of talent in his being, CCW has none. Cal: So is this the end of your short feud with CCW? Kano: Hell no. I won't let his comments about me or what I have done for this company go without giving him a Kanoplex or spear or a dozen for his troubles. Cal: Now level with me, Jon, how do you really feel about the Malice Crew? Kano: Do you remember when I was in MEDIA? Cal: Yes. Kano: Do you remember when I said that was the greatest collection of talent PCW had ever seen? Cal: Yes. Kano: I would be a liar if I said that I still feel that way. The Malice Crew is the greatest collection of talent, yesterday, today, and tommorrow. Shabazz maybe the best pound for pound wrestler in PCW. Rush has a following of believers in Crunk and wrestling ability that is nearly flawless. Duke has been around the block a few times. He has seen everything and knows what to do in every situation. I maybe the best big man in PCW right now. I bring the Malice Crew full circle. I am the glue that bonds. And we aren't done yet with our membership. Cal: Really? Who is joining? Kano: Cal, I know you have a tape recorder in your pocket that is recording every damn word I say, so you can kiss my ass for the rest of this conversation to try and get the names of the two new members coming into the Malice Crew. But I'll tell you one thing, you will never guess who it is. Cal: That is all of the relevant parts of our conversation. This is Cal Seaver, over and out. So in conclusion, CCW and Kano are still going at it even after Kano handed CCW's ass to him on Fusion, Kano loves the Malice Crew to the core, and two members will be joining the Malice Crew in the future. So the question is, when and who? Tune into PCW television this week and you may find out.
