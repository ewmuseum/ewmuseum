Did I Miss Something?

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Did I Miss Something? Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Mar 2002 ---- Prairie View, Texas- March Madness was a great night for the Wild Deuces and Asylum. For the Deuces, Boda did what every PCW wrestler wants to do by capturing the PCW Heavyweight title. For Asylum, Jeremy Howard finally got his brother back and, in the process, retained his Continental title against Drake Maxwell. And with the experienced leader of the Wild Deuces, Boda, taking on the emotional leader of Asylum, Miyagawa, with the PCW title on the line, things could only go up for both beloved groups. But by time the night was over, the two groups weren't only fighting each other, but they were fighting with each other. What happened? You'll just have to see to find out. Also on this special Wednesday Turmoil: Tyler Nelson's fate becomes clear. The Chef brings back a part of his past. And THE Multiple Man does something he had yet to do since leaving CCW for PCW. To see all this and a whole lot more, check out Tuesday Turmoil found at the bottom of the page.
