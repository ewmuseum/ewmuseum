Boobie Boy Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy OUR FIRST ISSUE! Featuring: Tyler Nelson First off for those of you who don’t know me I am Matt Elia, the boobie boy as known as by PCW staff and wrestlers. The story behind that name is actually quite entertaining…well only to friends of Tim Walden. See I was detained from wrestling because my mom caught me in my room with my girlfriend’s shirt off. And when I was able to return to wrestling, I had found out that Miyagawa had told the entire PCW Roster about my little extravaganza. So 3 months later I find myself with out a job or a girlfriend for that matter and I am back to living at home with my mother a broken and miserable man. Hoping to make ends meet I have decided to attempt to write a Weekly Column in hopes of extra cash. So every column I am going to interview a different PCW Superstar. I would like to open my column up with a bang by sitting down for 20 questions with the Triple Crown executive himself Tyler Nelson. Nelson was kind enough to take a few minuets away from his extremely busy schedule to talk to me and answer my questions. So with out further adieu….20 Questions with Boobie Boy! 1) First off where does your opinion lie in the debate about the new NTICW & EW Wrestlers joining the likes of veteran’s like yourself? Tyler: Anybody stand out? Are they part of the new ring crew? 2) If you could change one thing in your life what would it be? Why would I change anything? Tyler: I’m the greatest man in PCW history, I’m the Triple Crown Executive, and I got tons of money. Life is great. 3) Who is the most versatile threat right now to your power? What do you plan on doing to prevent a loss of that power? Tyler: I feel threatened by no one. There isn’t a person or group of people that can take what I have. I will continue to do what I do, and that is what I do. 4) Past love lives? Did she have big B**BS? Bigger than Tim Walden’s? Tyler: I’ve had more women than you could ever dream of in nine lives. Why are you looking at Walden’s tits? 5) Greatest Accomplishment in PCW? Tyler: Being the Two Time-Two Time PCW World Champion. That, and putting Kid Wonder in PWA. 6) Who is your most hated enemy? And if he is still around what do you plan on doing to him? Tyler: I can’t stand that little faggot Kid Wonder. I’m gonna bury his scrawny ass for good. That’s a promise. 7) Build your perfect girl…Hair, Eyes, Chest, Legs, Height, Weight. Tyler: I don’t need to build one. They flock to me like bees to honey. 8) Many sports stars have superstitions…do you have any before entering the ring? Tyler: What would they be? People with superstitions are freaks like all those Goths. 9) What was the witty bitty Tyler Nelson like? Tyler: The big bad Tyler Nelson. 10) What is your most embarrassing moment…we all know mine is the boob incident so confess! Tyler: Losing the World Title to Hix. 11) Wrestler that you feel won’t last the rest of the month? Tyler: Anyone who gets in my way. 11) Feelings on the PWA? Tyler: Perfect for Kid Wonder. 12) Feelings on annoying gay porn sent by Mexi and Clyde? Tyler: They must only send it to you because I have never gotten any. You enjoy that stuff? 13) Favorite sports other than wrestling including your favorite team. Tyler: How bout them Cowboy’s? 14) Plans for retirement? Tyler: Do what I want, when I want, cause I can. 15) If you were stranded on a desert island and had to bring one thing in the world what would it be and why? Tyler: My money, cause I could buy my way off of it. 16) Your favorite sexual position (if you have had sex cause I doubt some wrestlers have had it…AKA: Twisted Metal or AFL-CIO)? Tyler: You some kind of homo? First you ask about gay porn, then about my favorite sexual position? 17) Favorite on the road food? Tyler: Prime Rib and Lobster. 18) What do you think of the idea of this column? Tyler: Is they’re one? 19) If you had to describe me in your own words what would they be? Tyler: Do I know you? What the hell am I doing talking to you? Boobie boy would like to thank Nelson for his time and cooperation in letting the first issue is so successful. Also quick note on next week’s column. It will feature injured superstar and hometown friend….BODA. Until next week this is Boobie Boy saying have an extremely large day.
