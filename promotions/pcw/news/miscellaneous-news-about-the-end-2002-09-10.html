Miscellaneous News About The End

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Miscellaneous News About The End Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Sep 2002 ---- Cal Seaver checked in with this information. 1) First and foremost: The End is one of number of PCW wrestlers (Violence Jack and Totally Tubular Tommy also remain) that have managed to reach the fifth round of the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Only 16 wrestlers remain. The End has beaten Chris Bagwell and Jackson Rust, two men who knocked out John Crichton and The Pharaoh, thus keeping PCW in Bracket 7 of the tournament. Now The End shall wrestle "The Renegade" Rich Rollins of some fed... GCW, whatever. 2) Next, The End has commented on the 40% tax on salaries. He says, "It's okay. As of this moment, I'm waiving my $200,000 a year (The End makes more than enough money off of product endorsements and royalties that have nothing to do with PCW; he specifically requested low pay to free up his funds to hire and maintain other PCW stars) salary. That's right, I'm working for free." So now the Front Office got an extra $200,000 to throw around. As for product royalties from PCW merchandise bearing his name, the Front Office may still take 40% out of that, but the remaining 60% is untouchable and will go to charities ranging from cancer research to multiple schlerosis to the United Negro College Fund. 3) Sandy Wong, The End's manager/valet, has admitted to carrying a bit of a crush on him. She thinks the feelings are mutual but neither seems to say anything about it to each other. 4) The End has stated that he will not pursue a third Cruiserweight Title reign. He acknowledges that there's nothing really more that he can do with the belt, that he had a good reign, and that it's about time that he gain some weight and move up in the ranks. 5) Yes, The End is blind. The End is tired of the confusion about whether or not it is a PCW-appointed gimmick. It has been cleared through Standards and Practices that he shall wrestle without the goggles he wears over his mask to cover his eyes. I've seen those eyes, and I can tell you firsthand that they are as empty and white as a glass of fresh milk. It's really creepy. 6) The End has promised to reveal more about himself as this month progresses. That's all he'll say. Keep tuned to the news for more news about The End, and the other PCW superstars.
