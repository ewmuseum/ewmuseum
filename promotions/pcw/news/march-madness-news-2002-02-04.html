March Madness News

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March Madness News Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Feb 2002 ---- PCW officials announced over the weekend that a 20 man battle royal for the PCW Heavyeight Title will headline the March Madness Pay-Per-View on March 3rd. "We wanted to do something we've never done before," said the Pimply Faced Intern Kid. "A battle royal for the belt would be just that." As for who will be in the battle royal, a small tournament will be held starting next Sunday on Sabotage. From the tournament, 16 men will advance to compete in the battle royal. Unlike last year's tournament, there is not a full 64 man field. So, because of that, seven wrestlers recieved byes into the 2nd round barring injuries or extra additions. Those seven men are Miyagawa, The Chef, Tim Walden, Drake Maxwell, Boda, THE Multiple Man, The Pharaoh, and Chet King. With 16 people figured out, that leaves four more to be entered. The current PCW champion, Snow, is automatically in the battle royal unless he were to lose the title between now and March Madness. And to be fair, the champion gets to enter last. If anyone in the battle royal were to win the title from Snow, they would enter last and Snow would take their spot. The final three spots will be determined by the three heads of PCW. The president, commissioner, and CEO all get to pick one man to skip the tournament and be directly put in the battle royal. "I know that gives Nelson two picks," stated PFIK. "But that kinda happens when you hold most of the power and sign the checks." Tyler Nelson has already named himself as one of the men in the battle royal and is expected to name his new partner, Kid Wonder, as the other man. Although, with Nelson, you never know what he'll do. As for who Duke will pick, sources deep within PCW believe he'll select Jeremy Howard; however, wrestlingtattler.com speculates that the PCW president will select himself and "screw Howard big time." We'll bring you more information when it becomes available.
