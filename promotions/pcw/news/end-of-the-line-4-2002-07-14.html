End of the Line #4

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
End of the Line #4 Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Jul 2002 ---- Yes, it's time for another column. This one I recorded while eating at Roscoe's Chicken and Waffles. Unfortunately, they were standing in line behind me when I went to order. They saw each other and started arguing. Dear God, how I wanted to kill them right then and there. But instead, I turned on my tape recorder and just let it record them talking. ---- Cashier: Next? Shabazz: Duzza, have the police come to your house yet? Duzza: No, why? Customer: Let me get a cheeseburger with everything on it, some fries, and a large Coke. Shabazz: To arrest you for all that child porn that's on your computer. They haven't came? That's weird. I called them a while back. Duzza: Snitch... Man, I ain't got no child porn anyway. Shabazz: Then why did you say 'Snitch'? YOU PERVERT! You're following in R. Kelly's footsteps. Hook me up with the tape, I'll sell it to all the boys backstage. Ambient Voices: What are they talking about? I dunno. Duzza: Ahh, why don't you just go participate in a 3-on-1 beating of a innocent negro on the corner? Shabazz: Negro? You're no Negro. You're a pervert. Period. Duzza: Man, I ain't the one with all the lil' half caste babies running around. You a fake ass negro. I looked out for you from day one and you repay me by spitting on me. Shabazz: I spat on you because you're a disgrace to my race. How much longer before PCW officials stop employing you? This is enough! You're nothing but a veteran who has hung on to the sport too long. LEAVE ALREADY! Go make some porn videos... with five-year-olds. Duzza: Nigga, I'ma have to beat some respect into your white lovin' ass. Your nothing but a flash in the pan, give it two months and you'll be back on welfare in Queens. Shabazz: NO THE FUCK YOU DIDN'T! Face it Duzza, you're weak. Rush, myself, and Thug roC are the only black men on the roster. You want to talk about my family? How about I talk about yours, you rich son of a bitch. You haven't done ANYTHING to earn your money. I run N.I.G.G.A for the people. You run your private parts in little girls. Cashier: Duzza: Bitch please, I paved the way for your ugly ass. Let's look, Rush thinks he's a voodoo queen, Thug roC will be gone in two weeks and it's only a matter of time before the LAPD shoot yo ass... just give up while you're ahead nig'. Shabazz: Quit while I'm ahead? I'm climbing the ranks of the PCW faster than you in the pants of an 11-year-old. I'm almost at the top, all I need is some Television gold around my slim, trim, sexy waist. And then I'm on top. like Malcolm X did, he worked. End: I'll just have a chili cheese dog and a medium Sprite. Duzza: Is that all you got against me? All you got is false allegations of child porn. Get some original shit fool. You're a back stabbing, fake-ass negro who would turn on his own brother for money. You shouldn't even say Malcom's name. Shabazz: How DARE you disrespect me like this? I'm fighting for lazy ass black people like YOU, who are a shame to the race. I'm going to make it safe for black people to walk in LA. AND I'm going to make it 'cool' for black people to be successful in life. Like me with N.I.G.G.A and me in PCW. Duzza: I don't care what the fuck you think you're gon' do. You're nothin but a dreamer, do you think anyone cares about you dawg? Your a disgrace, your all talk. You brainwash people into followin' you, like the voodoo queen, but it's only a matter of time before the realest and truest nig' in PCW reigns supreme, just like R. Kelly will, I will get to the top player... ---- By that point, the owner pulled out a shotgun and convinced both men to take their argument elsewhere. They seem to have some issues that I suggest they shut the hell up about and settle in the ring, because no one cares about their problems to have to hear it on our leisure time.
