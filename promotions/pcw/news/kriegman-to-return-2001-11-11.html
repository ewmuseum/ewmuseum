Kriegman to Return? Posted by Sandy Wong on 11 Nov 2001 ---- Alan Kriegman will be returning from the eye injury he sustained at the hands of THE Multiple Man last month, a week from Sunday at the Alliances PPV, according to backstage sources with PCW. The Evil Supergenius will be working double time, not only representing Snapple in the Soft Drink Sponsorship match, but he and his wife Barbara Bush-Kriegman are said to be making the main event of Alliances a "double date", so to speak, as the First Couple of PCW will be teaming with none other than Clyde and Bethany in the gigantic stables match. "If he comes within three inches of either myself or Barbara, I will vaporize him with my new and improved Palmpilot...OF DEATH," commented Alan Kriegman when reached for a statement by PCW's own Pimply Faced Intern Kid. Clyde was also reached for comment, who had little more to say than "Eatyoursoulkillthesinnersbatheinthebloodoftheinnocentanddootherverybadthings..."
