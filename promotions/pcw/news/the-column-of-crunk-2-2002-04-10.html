The Column of Crunk #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Apr 2002 ---- Column of Crunk#2 By [name withheld] Now aren’t all of you glad that you began to endorse Crunk even before it became the *OFFICIAL* religion of PCW. Okay, well since I wrote my last column, a lot has happened. We have new tag champs, a new Extreme Champ, and a new TV Champ. In addition to that, there was a challenger and a stip added to the Main Event, while we earned another Main Event. Before I get into predictions, I’d just like to say that Spring Fling is going to be the best PCW PPV in a long time, if not ever. Forget Red October for a moment, and try to think of a PPV that has had so many quality matches onto one card. Add the annoncement of the bidding winner for PCW just adds a special mystery angle. With that said, let’s go to my predictions. Old Fashioned Over the Top Rope Battle Royal PCW Rising Star Title Jay Sylo(c) vs Mike Frampton vs Charlie Loc vs Reeco Suave vs Ben J. Pailthorpe vs John Crichton vs Andy Murray vs Shawn Mosely vs Jack Valentine vs Duzza This looks like a good match, much better than the Pot of Gold match at Shang-Haied 2. Justin(Jay Sylo’s handler) has been having some writer’s block so he most likely will not do much for the PPV. However, Jay has been putting out some great RP’s since his arrival, and when he gets his writing flair back, it will be great to have him back. Since the champion most likely will not walk out with the belt, that means it’s up for grabs to anyone who can puts out lots of RPs. I don’t like the fact, but the win in this type of match usually goes to a character who RP’s a lot, because match writers don’t want to read through 50 plus RPs. My pick would be Charlie Loc is he were here this week, but I’ll have to go with John Crichton. Pick: John Crichton Hair vs Mask Match Loser must lose their hair or mask forever Flaymin Aymin vs El Diablo As I was so nicely informed, I forget this match in last week’s rundown of feuds. As feuds for this PPV go, it’s moving along very nicely. With the momentum going back and forth each show, it gives the feud a very good feeling of being equal. Though I can’t say I like El Diablo in a feud with Aymin, it’s not a bad way to move both men up on the card, where Diablo should already be. In a match like this, with hair on the line, the face doesn’t often lose. I also think that Aymin’s fro is more important to his character than El Diablo’s mask is to him. However, there is really no way I can see Diablo losing the match. Winner: El Diablo Six-Pack Challenge PCW Extreme Title Jason Core vs The Rush vs Karl LeBlanc(c) vs Orpheus Grant vs Big Daveylicious vs Badge This match, as I said last week, is mostly about Rush vs OG and Core vs LeBlanc. It’s tough to pick a winner between any of these six men. I think Big D just doesn’t fit in the feud right so he won’t come out as the winner. Badge may fit in and RP well, but he is still a little rusty, so once again it comes down to the Malice Crew against Nelson’s punks. Here’s how I see the match ending: Core is somehow eliminated, leaving Rush alone with the two monsters. Somehow, Rush eliminates Swamp Dawg, leaving Rush and OG in the match, with the winner continuing to feud with the other. However, this situation could easily be repeated with any of the 6 men, though I think that the angle between the Malice Crew, OG, and LeBlanc will give us our last two compeititors. My pick is the one that is simply based on the facts that Core is ready to move up, Badge and Big D both have tag partners, OG is very inconsistent, and Rush & LeBlanc are a feud waiting to happen. Winner: LeBlanc Ladder Match PCW Cruiserweight Title Anton the Kid(c) vs THE Multiple Man vs Rent-a-Hero This is not a match that has been brewing for a long time, and no real feud surrounds it. RAH needs to feud with Borro, TMM is a very inconsistent RPer, and Anton is fed fishing, instead of focusing on PCW. This match may very well be good, but I see it as the worst match on the card, which is disappointing for RAH, who should be middle-card, but he will have to ease his way back in. The fact that it’s a ladder match spices it up a bit, but RAH is already focusing on Borro for post-PPV. However, Anton doesn’t care and TMM is good enough, but it’s always a guess as to whether he shows up or not. RAH has nothing to lose by feuding with Borro for the Cruiserweight title, so RAH wins cleanly and easily, barring a TMM miracle. Winner: RAH Standard Match Bobby Stormbringer vs The End What we have here is a good match that waited too long to be booked. If these two had started a real feud, we could’ve seen this on the upper-middle card, but unfortuneatly, it’s stuck on the lower-middle card. Another problem here is that both are faces. End has a great character, but he needs to find a way to get some heat. B.S is a good compeititor but some of his last few RPs have been about nothing at all basically. However, both are some of the best “new” guys out there and should still have a good match. Picking a winner is tough but I’ll go with End on the fact that he was looking for a push LAST PPV when he Swerved Maxwell. Winner: The End Standard Match Chet King vs Darkstar Same thing with the last feud here. This match was made last minute to get both men on the card. Unfortuneatly, Darkstar has really fallen since he was picked by many to win the Brawl for It All. I think Darkstar is good, but he’ll have to work his way back up to the upper card. Chet King has staff duties, but has really been off in his RPing lately. Neither really has shown anything special this month, but Darkstar needs a PPV win more right now. Winner: Darkstar Three Falls Match Fall One: PCW Television Title Fall Two: Loser drops "GOD" moniker Fall Three: Pharaoh's wife Special Referee: Jay Sylo Akiyama vs The Pharaoh(c) IMO, this match could be the 4th match down on the card, because it is a great feud, and a lot is riding on it. Both Aki and Pharaoh have done a lot for promoting this feud ever since they started it about three weeks ago. This will be a great way to hopefully end the feud with a bang, because no one likes angles that stretch themselves so far that they just become boring. Pharaoh keeps the TV Title and his wife, but the Egyptian god will be no more. 1st Fall:Pharaoh 2nd Fall:Akiyama 3rd Fall: Pharaoh Four Corners Elimination Match PCW Tag Team Titles Golden Game vs Politically Incorrect(c) vs Cowabunga Connection vs Fear Factory Wow, PCW actually has a tag division. For the first time in many months, there are actual tag team contenders who have been left off of the card. Well, finally Klaymore is gone. It destroys Golden Game, but that was only Juan anyway. This is the best thing for Juan at this point. Politically Incorrect is, IMO, the best new team from NTICW that PCW has. It’s a great gimmick and the RPing from PI is great. The Cowabunga Connection is another awesome new team. I think that CC and PI will go at it for the next PPV. Fear Factory seems like the sleeper team in both this match and PCW. They aren’t mentioned much but they will be doing some re-tooling and they will hold the Tag Titles soon enough. However, for this PPV, Politically Incorrect retains and heads off into a feud w/ the Cowabunga Connection. Winner: Politically Incorrect Battle of the Legends Standard Match #1 Contender to the PCW Continental Title Frisco vs Legend Inc. Well, I’ll say it. So far, this feud has disappointed me. Both men have put out two RP’s, neither are at their best. It just seems like this feud could’ve been very memorable but, after starting with a bang, this feud has kind of defused. We may see more, but unfortunately this turns out not to be a battle of the legends, just an uppercard filler match. Winner: Frisco Special Match Stipulation to be determined by Big Alan Steele #1 Contender to the PCW Heavyweight Title Big Alan Steele vs The Chef Well, these are two of the funniest guys in PCW. I wish we could see a little bit more on the RPing front, but workrate isn’t everything. With the stipulation still to be named, we should see something inventive that’s not already on the card, because we’ve got two ladder matches already and a first blood match. I’m predicting a scaffold match or maybe even a Hell in the Cell. Either way, this feud is good, but also could’ve been better. The firing of BAS seemed to just confuse, not add anything to the angle. Winner:Chef Standard Match PCW Continental Title Jeremy Howard(c) vs Xavier Kannon This is the best pure face/heel feud on the card. Ever since right after Madness, these two have been at each other’s throats even before Kannon “killed Asylum” In actuality, this match may have much bigger implications than the Continental Title, which almost everyone expects Kannon to win. The winner of this goes into the World Title division, almost definitely, unless we see a rematch. Since Snow reigned for a brief month, we haven’t seen a World title seeking Krayzie One. Kannon hasn’t yet risen to World Title status, like he has been put up to be ever since his PWA and NTICW days. Everyone else says Kannon, but I say Krayzie. Kannon moves up into the elite world title division. Winner: Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard Tyler Nelson vs Mexi Something got fucked up on my Wordpad so I couldn’t write the Loser Quits PCW stip up by the title. Well, we’ve seen this coming ever since Duke was crowned president. This is a tough one to call, because none of us want to see either man leave. Nelson is the best heel PCW has ever seen, anyone who argues otherwise is dumb or an Ivan Stanislav mark. Ivan’s great, but Tyler’s better. Mexi is the fan favorite, which leads me to believe that he may be the one going. Either way, I’m gonna be sad to read this match if it truly means the end of a PCW career for one of them, but most likely this is either because staff duties are too tough to do right now or just because there is not enough time to RP. This is the kind of match that is not about RPing, it’s just about a good angle. This angle will come off well either way, but I see Nelson winning in the end, if just so he can claim his possible ownership of PCW. Winner: Tyler Nelson Second Main Event First Blood Match PCW Heavyweight Title Boda(c) vs Chris Chase vs Alexander Miyagawa We’ve got Boda as a heel, Miya as a face, and then Chase is very much a tweener, though more leaning towards heel because Miya punks him all the time. Boda has been pretty impressive as a champ, despite having no real gimmick. Miya was a good champ for his three days, and Chase is heading towards his first run as champ, which will come soon enough, even if not at the Fling. Knowing that we just saw a huge heel win(by my predictions) we will be seeing a new champ in either a tweener Chase or a face Miya. The problem with Chase being champ at this point is just that: he’s a tweener. However, he will make some heel moves during this match and become a full-blown heel by the end of the night. Boda will be eliminated first and then I really can’t pick a winner so I’ll go with Chase to get his first run as champ as the last match on a GREAT card. No, but that’s not the end of it. We’ve also got the matter of the new CEO. Autmatically I rule out Boda and Duke. Boda, without the title, doesn’t have enough influence to get it and Duke, already having a presidency on his belt, will not pull a Nelson and become CEO too. That leaves TPB, RAH, and Tyler Nelson. I’m afraid we won’t see Renty as the CEO, so sadly Rush will never become Crunkerweight champion. That leaves Nelson and TPB, possibly the two men who hate each other most in the history of PCW. Nelson took TPB’s job and company last time, now the Booker comes back with a vengeance. We’ve got a Phantom Booker owned PCW. Well that’s my run-down of the show, and unfortunately, I can’t do the wrestler spotlights on new guys like I did last week due to time constraints. SO, next week I will try to do two columns(unless I can fit both into one column). I will do a post-PPV run-down and I will do a column spotlights on EW guys and NEW NTICW. Thanks for the time and keep it crunk while you’re at it.
