Movie Producer coming to Turmoil

25 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Movie Producer coming to Turmoil Posted by E2 on 25 Mar 2004 ---- As mentioned by Mick Verelli, John Gargano's Hollywood agent on last weeks Turmoil. The producer for the movie 'To the ring and back' is set to be in the arena for the highly anticipated E2 v.s. MSA match for the Tag Team Championship, mainly to see John in action. Well known to many is that John Gargano was highly favored for the lead in the movie up until E2's title loss last month. Will E2 regain the Tag Titles? Will this be John's opportunity to impress the producer? Will he finally get the role that he has prepared for, for over a year? Tune into Turmoil when these questions and more are answered when Entertainment Extreme takes on The Mega Soaring Arrows for the PCW Tag Team Championship.
