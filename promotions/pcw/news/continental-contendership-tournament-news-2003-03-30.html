Continental Contendership Tournament News

30 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continental Contendership Tournament News Posted by Sandy Wong on 30 Mar 2003 ---- The signups for the tournament are closed. Anyone in PCW was welcome to apply to be in the tournament, so long as they had a tag partner... any tag partner. The teams reported to Skylar Green, who had this to say: "Some of the teams were to be expected, other teams involve enemies who wish to win the Continental Title to have another reason to bash each other's heads in. Others just want the title because they won't be getting a shot at it for a while (or ever) otherwise." Here are the teams that are signed up to compete in the tag tournament, which shall take place on a special weekend event this coming weekend of April 6th. 1) Pranksters With Attitude, Gaz Green and Nerezza 2) The Schmoopies, Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor 3) Significant Others, Jade and Lacuna Debris 4) The Pharaoh and Tim Walden 5) Dante Jackson and El Romantico 6) Dorn Tracer and Jonathan Drachen 7) AJ Trinidad and Semore Smoke 8) Young Tiger and Darkstar 9) Rent-A-Hero and Clyde 10) Akiyama and Teresa Tomas 11) Matt Jackson and Haley Hilton 12) The Overweighter and Mark Makonie 13) calVen (Cal Seaver) and Boca Del Inferno 14) The Suburban Ninja Clan 15) Dark Gimmick and Banshee 16) Avalanche and the Ghost of Hiro Saotome When questioned about why the Suburban Ninja Clan, PURE: World roster members, are featured in this tournament, Skylar Green had only this to say. "Oh, right. I'm a freelance member of the group. I carry their name as well, it's on my business card. Anywhere I work, they're eligible to receive booking on a volunteer basis until they sign a full contract. So the PURE Trinity Tag Team Champions shall be competing in this tag team tournament. We asked Skylar for some hints as to which ninja might be sent to compete. Skylar Green brushed off the question by saying, "The Clan has no use wasting ninjas to be used in this tournament. They wouldn't want to walk in and outclass everyone." I suppose that means that there won't be any active ninja being sent to compete. In that case, who, if anyone, could the Suburban Ninja Clan actually send on its behalf? Skylar Green swears that he'll stay out of physical competition in the tournament... There are some interesting teams in this tournament, tag team favorites and terrifying tandems alike. All of them could have an equal opportunity of winning this tournament. The teams are listed in the order in which they signed up, and this list is separate from the actual tournament pairings, which shall be announced live on this week's Tuesday Turmoil.
