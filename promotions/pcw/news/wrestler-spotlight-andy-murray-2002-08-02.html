Wrestler Spotlight : Andy Murray. Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Aug 2002 ---- Recently PCW interviewer Tiffany Jennings had the chance to meet with PCW’s resident Ned, and only Scottish superstar Andy Murray to talk about his feelings on PCW and his daily routine. Tiffany: So Andy, how are you this evening? Andy Murray: Oh, Ah'm pure, mad, mental min Tiffany: Can I get a translator? Um.. So.. Er, yeah. How does it feel to be the only Scot in PCW? Andy Murray: Ho! Bawbag! Ah'm no the only Scot min, Rab's here an awl. But I wouldnae want any other Scottish muckah's in PCW, they'd pure steal mah spotlight Tiffany: Was that a good or bad? *Pause* Tiffany: So Andy.. Many of the PCW wrestlers watch their diets. What is your usual nutritional routine? Andy Murray: Diet? Like, Diet Coke an awl? I don't drink that pish min. But, I dinna eat anythin' but battered Mars Bars, Chips, and more pure briwliant stuff fae down the chippae. Vegetables are for chubes min. Tiffany: And you can wrestle WITHOUT having a heart attack? Andy Murray: What ye talkin' aboot? Tiffany: You must have a very tough exercise regime to burn off that fat. Andy Murray: Are ye callin' me fat? Ah'm no fat, ya chube. I dinnae exercise either, that's pure pish min. But I think mah pal Barry has a thing fer that Mr Motivator chube, Ah'm no sure though. Tiffany: Er..Ooooookay. What other sports do you enjoy outside the wrestling business? Andy Murray: FITBA! Fitba's pure bangin' min, Rangers are pure briwliant, an' Celtic are just pish! Ah go tae all the Rangers games, which is pure class min. One day, Ah'm gonnae pure hammer some Celtic fans wi' Rab. Tiffany: Fitba? Is that Indian or something? Andy Murray: Fitba is Scottish min. Are ye stupit or somethin'? Tiffany: No.. I'm American. Anyway, you talk a lot about Rab, what is your relationship with him? Andy Murray: Rab's mah pal. Me an' Rab huv been pals since that school pish, he's mah pure briwliant manager an' awl. Tiffany: Is Rab friends with Ned? Andy Murray: Who's Ned? Tifanny: Ned.. You keep mentioning him. Is he a horse? Andy Murray: Ah've never said a word about no chube called Ned! And horses are fer Toffs min! Tiffany: So what is a Ned? Andy Murray: Ah'm a Ned, ya chube ... Did ye no watch me last promo thing? It was awl about Neds min, go watch it, it's pure briwliant. Tiffany: I was waiting for the subtitles on your promo but they never arrived. Have PCW spoken to you about taking English lessons? Andy Murray: England is full 'o toffs ye stupit chube, what wid I wanna go there fer? Tiffany: Um.. Okay, I have no idea what you are saying. But for those who do, do you have any final comments for them? Andy Murray: Aye min ... Blayze an' Rush Mason, ya couple o' wogs, see the next time I see you chubes. Ah'm gonnae pure hammer ye! And if ye dinna stop annoyin' me ye couple o' bampots, Ah'm gonnae chib a brick off yer noggin'! YE GOT ME!?! Tiffany: No, not really. Anyway, thank you Andy. Andy Murray: Go ram a donkey min.
