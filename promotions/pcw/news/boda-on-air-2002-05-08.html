Boda on Air

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boda on Air Posted by Sandy Wong on 8 May 2002 ---- On a local Orlando radio station, Boda making a guess appearance demanded his match with Alexander Miyagawa add a stipulation. [recorded earlier] Russ Rollins: So, this match with Miyagawa is a simple you pin me or I pin you match? Boda: Yeah, a standard match. So? Dirty Jim: I mean come on B. You are a monster, he's nothing compared to you. Why not make it interesting. Boda: One of us being kicked out of the world of wrestling aint interesting enough? Daniel: I think they want a special match. Russ Rollins: Yeah. I mean, we are huge fans of wrestling, hell, even I wrestled on WcW shows a few times. Jeff Howell: Don't remind us. Boda: Listen guys.... I see what you are saying. So, what if I demand a stip where this is a 'Submision Finisher Only, Loser leaves PCW' match? [canned applause] [end tape] So, as you can see. Boda wants a stip in the PPV match. Will Miya answer the call? He better.
