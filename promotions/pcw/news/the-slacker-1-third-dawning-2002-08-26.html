The Slacker #1: Third Dawning

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Slacker #1: Third Dawning Posted by Sandy Wong on 26 Aug 2002 ---- Hello, wrestling fans, and welcome to a new, probably rather dull column, From The Slacker's Keyboard..I am of course the Slacker, and I'll be guiding you through some quick commentary on the general state of PCW as well as a match-by-match preview of their explosive Pay-Per-View, PCW Third Dawning. You know, a lot of people have been slagging on PCW lately, saying that they're slipping in the ranks, pointing at the latest ratings and how they've fallen behind FSW in addition to OSW, well I've gotta say this: The people don't know what they're talking about. They were all too happy to declare PCW dying and in this declaration content to just skim through it if they bothered to read at all, but I'd have to say that it's been functioning quite well in the past couple months, so much so that I would go far enough to say it's the best federation in all of WT right now. They're finally dumping the dead weight, the workers have paired off pretty evenly for some solid feuds, and the lineup for the PPV is just solid..And speaking of the PPV lineup, I'm too lazy to make a proper transition to it, so here's the rundown: 4-Way match: Rising Star Title: Scorpion v. Eddie Mandrake v. Blayze v. Semore Smoke: This match is perhaps the sleeper of the card, but it's very odd to note how the competitors stack up....Semore Smoke, who can quickly be taken out of contention since he's been already pushed as a plain-old low-carder by firstly being part of the epic, and that term being used in its broadest sense, Kano v. CCW feud, then by actually lowering his heat further by taking the previously-thought-to-not-exist position of "Second banana to Corey Williams". However, he's still probably seen as above the title with his past credentials. This leaves three men..Scorpion and Eddie Mandrake have an interesting, if not very motivated feud going on, but at this point the real story is Blayze..He appeared briefly for the pseudo-CCW invasion, but hadn't been seen since until he returned to join up with Rush Mason and the Militants of the Month, and the assigning of Ibe Black to referee the match makes one wonder if the Militants aren't going to grow one stronger here...I pick Blayze to get the win and the title, since he's not seen as above it but he's seen as reliable. Retirement Match: Corey Williams v. Jon Kano...This match is a GODSEND, or rather a HUBBARDSEND, as these two men are probably my least favourite wrestlers..The man who thinks that he can be controversial by being rather uninteresting, being given the PCW World Title for no good reason but that his backstage friends had it, and paying Eminem to pretend his moves hurt, and the living legend-ary ego, who hasn't changed a bit in two years and if anything looks to have become the man soon to be voted "Most likely to hold down young talent", though against CCW that may be a positive thing...This match is so difficult to call, Corey Williams's head, or lack thereof, against Jon Kano's heart, which is probably gasping and choking with cholesterol...Which will fail first? Corey Williams himself referred to the two men as opposites, which is like saying that Bobby Stormbringer would make a good father...Opposites, outside of the fact that they are both big overhyped steaming piles of suck. And sheesh, Kano, RETIRE ALREADY. I mean, you've teased getting fired or retiring with big overblown segments at least 5 times now in your 2-year career, we're sick of you pulling that schtick, nobody believes your retirement speeches anymore, so nobody ever believed that the CCW attacks'd put you out anyway, severely impacting the reaction to the angle..Corey, Kano, Kano, Corey, whoever loses, the fans are the winners, unless Kano somehow manages to fake his way out of the stips after losing, which wouldn't shock me...I, along with most of the viewers, am praying for a double-countout. 4 Way for Tag Titles: Heartbreakers, Pretty Cool Guys, Cowabunga Connection, Duzza/Makonie: Well, now I'm rushed, so let's be quick. honestly, at the moment I'm doubting that PCG's got the heat to go all the way here, HB's had the belts long enough as is, Duzza & Makonie are just too obvious...CC HAVE to get the belts sometime. Ladder Match: Venneman Pendragon v. Syko The Goblin: Well, these two have really kicked up a storm work-wise, and Venneman is the next big thing, which everyone else has already said anyway...look for either a continuation of the feud with a VP win, or Syko getting the belt and Pendragon moving on up again. Singles Match: Tomb Match: Pharaoh v. Frisco: Well, I'm stumped...The feud has escalated from burning eyes to epic military struggles, and I can't honestly pick a winner here..Pharaoh could use the heat of beating the guy he would've faced for the World Title at Wrestle Vessel, but Frisco could also use a nice boost after losing at WV, though he did get the win over BAS there, but Pharaoh hasn't taken a PPV loss in a while..I am stump-ed. No DQ Match: Continental Title: DarkStar v. Snow v. Rent-A-Villain: Well, Snow's Tank series is pretty darn spectacular, but it doesn't come out very often at all...RAV has seemed to slip a little in his efforts as well, but DarkStar just keeps on pushing through..I predict an unprecedented retention of the belt by the Lord of Dreams, unless they want to put him in the main-event scene or just have RAV finally get one back on them, seeing that he's played the victim for a while in this angle. Gotta watch out for Dark Gimmick though. For Akiyama's World Title Shot: Akiyama v. El Diablo: I've gotta say Aki for this one, simply because having El D win would basically render the whole #1 Contender's match a few weeks ago pointless...However, I doubt that Aki WILL actually win, because he's gotten the drop on El D in the past few weeks brilliantly, and it's time for justice to be served. Main Event: Salvation in a Cell: PCW World Title: 100% Ownership of PCW: PCW CEO Position: PCW Commisioner Position: Xavier Kannon v. Tyler Nelson: Well, this is...interesting. Honestly, I'm actually rather sick of the dull management struggles of the two men, both holding half of the power, yada, yada, could somebody for once EXPLAIN exactly how much can be done with 50% of the company? Oh well, it's rather irrelevant, because we've got the two evils here fighting for the power, nobody one really can cheer for, because we've seen Kannon with power, and we've seen a lot of Nelson with power. Tyler tries to pretend to be a good guy, but COME THE HECK ON! We all know how a typical Nelson ownership goes, and aligning himself with the sWo (Satanic World Order: Nash=Clyde, Hall=DS, Hogan/X-Pac=Syko) just isn't going to make us think he's a good person..You can't go having your "top faces" stabbing the crap out of things without much charisma, and nobody believes it. PCW DESPERATELY needs faces in the top of the card, so come to think of it, I'll pick Pharaoh above just to push him, and I'll desperately plea for Akiyama to just GIVE IN TO YOUR FACE HEAT ALREADY! I mean, this El Diablo feud is counteracting the anti-Kannon face heat and the natural likability of the NVG and the Divine Prophetess..But anyway, back to the match: We've seen enough of Tyler Nelson running the company, and Kannon's just getting started..It'd be a shame to deny Ellie her chance to shine as commish... The groan of the month: Well, look to the PPV match listing....We gave Kano and Corey a spot for those two to bore the crowd into submission, but there's no Cruiserweight Title match? I mean, last month's was the big hit of the show, and The End has been getting a good challenge from some of his recent opponents..Could we not have removed Jaden and Makonie from Tuesday's lineup, thrown in Chris Chase and a ladder, built it up a little, shunted it back a couple days and had a barn-burner? The Brandi markout of the month: Gee, guess which OOC management member came up with this idea..Well, I'm always up for sucking up to management in hopes that I'll get hired, and besides, I'm a big fan o' hers too, so let's see...I'd have to say the match she had with The End was a big markout, even if it was a little bit of a letdown ending, with honorable mentions going to the handicap match with RAV against Snow and the fact that she got through a segment with Bethany without an onscreen stabbing. I just hope she continues to be used as a believable singles competitor with the men, even through the bringing in of all the women which appears to be another attempt at a women's division...Allowing me to segue into: The women's division, mark 4: In the beginning...There was Brandi. She was so-so, but she improved over time...Then, there was Oracle. She just plain sucked, but they had her kill Brandi's heat repeatedly for the sake of a female-on-female wrestling angle. Thankfully, it ended, but only with firing both of them...Then, they tried to put together a division with a couple people who left before they started, LadyCat, Sassy, Kya "Oracle trying to hide her suckiness" Brady, and Brandi again...This would've worked, if not for that LadyCat didn't want to RP, Sassy didn't do much of it herself, and Kya....Continued sucking and only stayed because she was screwing half the locker room. It got so bad, even though Brandi started working her ass off for the Extreme title, that I kid you not, there was actually backstage discussion of having Talena run in for no apparent reason and cheat her out of the Evening Gown match entirely to give...ugh..Mona the belt. Thankfully, unlike a certain five-initialed fed, PCW suddenly came to its senses and remembered, "Hey! Mona's a bad wrestler!"...Then again, could she have been any worse than HANK~! and Karl LeBlanc twice? But that's besides the point..They fired all of them for not working eventually, even Brandi in spite of that she actually did work, and they just tried to avoid having a women's division for a while after that..However, after the events of Supremacy, it became clear that they might try to give it a go again, hiring Jaden, Anna Keyson, and Heaven Rayne...But Keyson got fired quickly, and Rayne got injured, so it wasn't meant to be..Ironically, only now as they try to put together a new one does Jaden go the way of the other two, but this one is definitely the most stable women's division in PCW history...Brandi came back, and has kicked it back into gear, we've got that Cecilia girl, though I don't know what to expect from her much, Romana's style of wrestling is certainly unique, and I get the feeling that Banshee probably could've done better if he'd tried it on for his size, and PCW has also had the sense to sign two of the most promising free agents on the market in Teresa Tomas and Charlotte, a pair of greatly talented workers who've been pushing up through the developmental system for a while now. I see this as a can't miss, unlike the other divisions, and though I want to see them compete with the men a good deal, I'm more concerned about them being not just left to fight each other constantly, but also to do the Jaden job and lay down for the main-eventers' girlfriends...But I think it'll turn out all right. All right, well now, that's enough, I'm tired and I've got more important things to do, like fall asleep on the couch. Signing off, this is the Slacker!
