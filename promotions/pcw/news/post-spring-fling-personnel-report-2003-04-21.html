Post-Spring Fling Personnel Report

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Post-Spring Fling Personnel Report Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Apr 2003 ---- VP-S, one third of the new Personnel Department, had this to say. "We have signed some new and old talent, and they'll all start working in the Rising Star Division. "In the matter of PTC/WT/WO old-timers, we have hired Rich Cannon, as well as Shane Wethers, who will make a return to PCW after being here roughly a week in 2002. They are both booked this week in Rising Star matches. "From the Primetime Wrestling Academy, PCW has promoted Stone Burns and the tag team E2. They will begin work very soon. "New hirees that were picked up from free agency include a Ukranian wrestler named Sivasian Krikorian; SyN, the former NTICW wrestler; a strange man known only as SOCIETY (yes, that's exactly how he spells it); a female wrestler calling herself Miss Moonlight; and someone whom we can only call 'Mr. Surprise'. "As for tag teams, PCW has resigned the Boy Band singing sensations Pretty Cool Guys. In addition, PURE's closing has brought PCW an opportunity to further diversify its tag team ranks, and the federation took the opportunity to sign PURE and LOW workers Justice Inc." That brings the number of PCW signings to 14. "On the opposite end of things, both Teresa Tomas and Darkstar lost respective matches which basically resulted in the termination of their contracts. PCW wishes them both well in future endeavors. Teresa Tomas, looking ahead to the possibility of losing, signed a commentary contract before her match with Rent-A-Hero at Spring Fling. While she is not going to wrestle for PCW anymore, she'll be taking up third chair, which hasn't been filled since Duke Williams left PCW. "Since Enigma was revealed as El Diablo, no sense in keeping a roster spot for Enigma. That character is gone. "Both Jeremy Howard and Xavier Kannon have left PCW. Not much more needs to be said, we all saw what happened. "Brandi has left PCW since Xavier Kannon has left. She was on injury, anyway. "Due to injuries suffered at the pay-per-view, both Rent-A-Hero, Jonathan Drachen, and Chris Storm have asked for releases from their PCW contracts, which they have been granted. "Duke Williams, though long retired, will finally be taken off of the roster. "Cozette Du Pres, coming to a milkbox carton near you. In the meantime, she's inactive and will be placed on the injured roster until we can find her. "At the last minute, Haley Hilton decided before her match to become a manager, and thus the Significant Others won their PPV match by default. "Akiyama is leaving PCW for an unspecified amount of time. "El Romantico, Venneman Pendragon, and Clyde are all on the injured reserve following grueling pay-per-view matches. They shall remain until medically cleared to return. Actually, in Clyde's case, he'll probably just show up again." That is all to announce, though it is quite a lot. That's nearly 30 people coming and going in PCW. There will be more personnel updates as they happen.
