Blade Dilemma

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blade Dilemma Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 May 2003 ---- Story: Reported weeks ago, as you know fellow PCW member Stone Burns was brutally and inadvertantly beaten in the hands of unnamed security guards at the Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport. Those men had been charged and immediately fired from their jobs.. or that's what 'we' were told. But irrelevant to the subject, Stone Burns has yet to receive his blade from the Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport security, and now seeing that PCW has intervened they will now bring this to court where Stone will have to testify further on this.. PCW has mustered up highly ranked attorneys to fight this rather minor case. Stone has been complaining at shows for it and now holds an 'impostor' as he calls it.. this is causing several morale problems hence having PCW step in to this case further. Why they would fight for his blade and not just fire him? Who knows.. but further off this specific topic we'd contacted Desmond Pfeipher (over the phone), PCW's and Stone's appointed lawyer stated this.. "This world tour is far from over.. and is still in activity for another few weeks so we've decided we can further this case and surely win it with ease.. For the past years PCW has been running we'd started and commended no hate crimes, and wrongdoings but we still have many people that show hostility.. and now they have to pay for their doings." Ending that statement, we'd asked him to comment further for the being of Stone's 'blade' and what not they were 'suing' quote unquote for but he hung up on us.. We tried to contact Stone Burns but we were told he doesn't have a designated telephone number and we were recommending Verizon. We will keep you updated on this case as it develops.
