Boda: Good Samaritan

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boda: Good Samaritan Posted by Sandy Wong on 18 Mar 2002 ---- Salt Lake City, Utah- At March Madness, Boda finally won the PCW Heavyweight title. Three days later, Boda lost the title to Miyagawa in a match marred with controversy. He ended up regaining the belt, but on his way to getting it back, Boda did something inexcusable. He did something that nobody with a heat could condone. He hit a pregnant woman. And not just any pregnant woman, but Miyagawa's pregnant wife, Natalie. Knowing what he had done, Boda went to Anaheim to make things right again and try to patch up his friendship with the goggled one. Or did he? Also on Sabotage: The Malice Crew suffers a blow as International Incident gets one stronger. The new and inproved tag team division is on sample. And how would Paddy O'Shea be spending his St. Patrick's Day? To find out all this and more, check out Sunday Sabotage by clicking the link at the bottom of the page.
