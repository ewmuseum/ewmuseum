Indepedence Day

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Indepedence Day Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Jul 2002 ---- The Fourth of July. It's Turmoil. But will there be more than the usual fireworks? Our sources say so. Tyler Nelson has hinted at big names "returning" on Turmoil, but with the closure of the fWo and CCW in trouble, will there be even more? There is already a petition on the internet to get former fWo Star and OSW Star Tempest to sign with PCW, and considering that signing a former OSW person would be a first for PCW, The Phantom Booker and Tyler Nelson would be more than happy to do so... but will it happen? Only Turmoil can tell.
