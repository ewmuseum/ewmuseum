Christmas Coming Early?

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christmas Coming Early? Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Nov 2002 ---- Zurich, Switzerland- PCW fans will probably never like Xavier Kannon, and they will definitely never trust him... So when the CEO puts on a show to open Fusion and promises PCW that he has gotten them an early Christmas Present, they can be forgiven for being more worried than expecting. But Kannon would have to put thoughts of gifts out of his mind for the Main Event where he as his Front Office ally, Duke Williams would face both Akiyama and Clyde in a 4 Way match that was busting at the seams with PCW talent. Between those two happenings, the Chef starts to think Syko isn’t such a bad Goblin after all, Rich Rollins makes in impression with the PCW fans, Skitzo... Well, acts like Skitzo, and can the Void survive the awesome power of PCW waddling destroyer? Add 10 other matches to the Main Event including Teresa Tomas combating Brandi’s “Textbook” style, and Playaz Inc. showing a new meaning of ‘Teamwork’ and what you have is a huge and explosive Fusion that will leave Switzerland less than neutral. To see all this go down, head to the Fusion banner and click ‘Results’.
