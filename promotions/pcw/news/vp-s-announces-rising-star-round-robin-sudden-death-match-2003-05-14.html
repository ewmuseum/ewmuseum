VP-S Announces Rising Star Round Robin Sudden Death Match!

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VP-S Announces Rising Star Round Robin Sudden Death Match! Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 May 2003 ---- VP-S had this to say on the matter. "Turmoil will have fewer matches in order to make time for this special match. Between certain things like scheduling conflicts and wanting to rest our workers, not everyone will have a chance to face everyone by the time that the tournament comes around. Not only that, but to the people who haven't had many chances to prove themselves in singles competition, many losses to their name isn't exactly a completely fair end of the stick for them. "So what I'm doing is booking a Sudden Death Match for the Rising Star Division. It is going to be a Singles Elimination Gauntlet. The way it works will be as follows: "Two wrestlers will start in the ring. They'll wrestle until one of them is pinned or is made to submit. In other words, it'll be a singles match. When one person loses, they'll be sent away and the next wrestler will come out, they'll wrestle... repeat ad nauseum until one person wins the entire thing." The match is designed to give the worst performers of this month one last shot to get some wins to their names, as all pins and submissions they get in this one match will go to their overall win count. As a reward, and because they have left to prove, the top performers will come out last. So the one with the fewest wins will get the first chance to change things around. Matt Jackson and Shane Wethers are all but guaranteed to be the last ones out. There will only be 9 persons in the match, as injuries and a firing have whittled down a beginning field of 14. Rich Cannon is gone from the federation, and Young Tiger, The Overweighter, Shane Robertson, and "The Ahtumoth" Hanami Aneyuge are all injured. Their participation at the Supremacy pay-per-view is not expected. The projected match structure (the order of entry) looks like this: 1) Mark Makonie 2) Dan Osbourne 3) Sivasian Krikorian 4) Stone Burns 5) SyN 6) SOCIETY 7) James Edwards 8) Shane Wethers 9) Matt Jackson This looks to be an outrageous match, and given the "last chance" nature, the first few participants will be expected to push their game to the next level in order to impress.
