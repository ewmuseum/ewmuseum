The Chef Letters

The Chef Letters Posted by Sandy Wong on 7 Nov 2001 ---- Dear PCWers, Hey all, it's your favorite gallant gastronomer! I'm here in some country with the suffix "stan" trying to sort things out in my military career. I got in a big predicament yesterday when we ran out of Spam in the granary and I couldn't whip up my world famous Spam Surprise with a touch of rosemary. Things have been going quite well out here in this undisclosed location. I recently wowed our colonel with my chicken lemonata, although he did mention afterwards I was a little heavy on the lemon, but I'm not changing my recipe because I love citrus fruits! I asked our colonel if I could go along with the special forces troops and provide them with sumptuous meals, but he declined. My point is that I survived using the same bathroom with Bertha for over nine years, and I think I can survive any harmful gases that may come my way. This really isn't going to be a column analyzing anything in the PCW, although I catch a little PCW action on Al-Jazeera television. Arabian cable ain't that bad folks, they've got their own version of "Press Your Luck", although they've replaced the Whammies with Acts of Allah and every contestant's name is Mohammed, man or woman. Sorry about that, I think that joke would have even made Yakov Jakov cringe. Another one of my favorites is the "Al Queda's Most Funniest F*ck Ups". Dick Clark is the host, which confirms my belief he does have a pact with Satan, and we watch Terrorism bloopers and blunders. By the way Yakov, I'd like to thank you for taking care of my kids while I'm out there. I just hope he hasn't bought Manuel that Home Vodka Maker Kit that the scamp has been eyeing. I've heard alot of rumors regarding Operation Enduring Freedom, and I've taken the liberty to address a few of them with some points. 1)No, Napoleon is not alive and leading us against the Taliban. Last I heard, Napoleon and his nemesis Beethoven got into a scuffle over the title of one of his symphonies... 2)Yes, Kano is gay, was there any doubt? 3)No, Osama Bin Laden is not hiding in Bertha's coffin, I don't know where that one started. 4)No, I am not planning a return to the PCW, although never say never. 5)Yes, I think Colin Powell is cute too... 6)No, Ross Perot has not financed any mercenary operation, but he's got 4 billion dollars in the bank so he might... 7)No, I do not have a recipe for Anthrax Pie! So quit asking! I'd also like to give a big shout out to my arch nemesis Emeril over the recent cancellation of his tevelsion show... NANNY NANNY BOO BOO!!! YOU CAN'T ACT!!! Thank you for reading my rantings and remember guys, it doesn't matter how you shake and dance, the last drop is always in your pants... Your Culinary Conquistador, The Chef
