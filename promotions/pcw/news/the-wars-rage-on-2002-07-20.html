The Wars Rage On

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Wars Rage On Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Jul 2002 ---- Miami, Florida- Things are heating up in PCW as of late, with some vicious and unthinkable things happening. That didn't stop on the latest edition of Fusion as the action was non stop from start to end. Pride, championships, and record contracts were on the line as the stars of PCW took to the ring. The Cruiserweight Championship match had a 'mysterious' guest who decided the outcome, and two tag teams waged an epic war in a ladder match for a music recording contract. In the main event, would The Pharaoh break the jinx and finally get a pinfall win over Xavier Kannon, or would PCW's only scientologist gain revenge for an attack on him from Turmoil? Oh, and there's these other two guys involved in the main event named Frisco and Big Alan Steele. Would BAS even make it to the main event? Not if Frisco has anything to say about it.
