Rewriting History

28 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rewriting History Posted by Sandy Wong on 28 Jan 2002 ---- Shanghai, China- One year ago today, Tyler Nelson formed perhaps the greatest stable in PCW history: Media. One year ago today, Tyler Nelson took control of the juggernaut that is Primetime Championship Wrestling. One year ago, Tyler Nelson canned The Phantom Booker and made everyone's lives a living hell. But that was a year ago. This is now. Would Tyler Nelson reign supreme two years in a row? Who could Nelson purchase this time around? Or would a certain someone come down and rain on Tyler's parade? Ruin everything that Tyler has worked to build. Also at Shanghaied: The leader of PCW's future is decided as Jeremy Howard and Snow meet for wrestling's top prize. Alan Kriegman retirement saw the return of Streak, and ended with a huge BANG. And how many faces of Hank Gunyon can there possibly be? To see all the action from this year's Shanghaied, go to the Pay-Per-View link and check it out for yourself.
