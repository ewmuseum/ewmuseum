THAT COLUMN THING #1 Posted by Sandy Wong on 28 Jun 2002 ---- THAT COLUMN THING #1 One day the Hound met the Bastard, and following an illegal act of bestiality.... This was born.... It is I, it is I... It is “That Column Guy”! And yes, my name can be taken as “I couldn’t think of a good name”.... That and if I choose to do what every other PCW columnist does and disappear then it’ll be a case of “Where’s that column guy?” “Who?”.... Bases covered!!! Now, why did I choose now to make my debut column? Easy, it’s PPV time so I can do a PPV prediction column which are easy! Well, that was the plan then I actually thought about it and that’s where all great plans go wrong.... With Gold & Glory nearly upon us, PCW actually has some feuds and angles going on... I know, I had to pinch myself too. But luckily there are enough “Nothing Matches” for me to pick at to make it worth writing. On that topic, just so this column isn’t a copy from the prediction thread, here’s some other stuff to that makes it “Columny”. Feuds: Call me an idealist but how hard are these? Unless you don’t actually know the meaning of the word “Feud” they are pretty damn easy... You go to a “BIO” look up someone’s “IM INFO” and you “IM THEM” possibly using the words “WANT TO FEUD?” and WHAM! you have a feud on your hands... Why do you do this? BECAUSE IT BENEFITS YOU!!! Look what it did for El Diablo and Flaymin’ Aymin, for Xavier Kannon and Chris Chase... It took them from “Some guy on the PCW roster” to “They deserve a push”... And if you do this, please actually DO something with the feud, NOBODY and I’m pretty sure I mean NOBODY wants to se you and your feudee doing post match attacks 2 times a week all the way to the PPV. This leads me to the second part... Segments. As stated before, who the hell reads every RP? Who is going to know or care about your feud unless they see it on TV. In the WWE does a feud get over more by airtime on RAW or by a few spoken words on Access? (Answer is RAW by the way). Segments are there to get your feuds over, people read the show, they’ll read your segment and they’ll see what you are putting over. They should be used to put FEUDS over by the way, as using them to get YOURSELF over can be tiring and do not benefit your feud... So if you are going to do an in-ring PLEASE don’t just let it be you talking!!! “Music hits, enters, speaks, music hits, leave” = “Snore”. So to those languishing in PCW, to those struggling to get a win or people to care about them... It’s easy... Pair off, work together on segments, have some REASON for the feud, even if it is purely over a Title.... Do that and I’ll care, as I feel will others. Now, with that little rant about people not doing something so easy it hurts that will aid them immensely in PCW over, I will get to this Pay Per View... Kris Burden vs Trigger: I should care about this why again? I stopped caring about Trigger at the start of his aborted Goeren feud when the Ultimate Authority game him a mesmorisigly methodical beatdown only for his next RP to carry on with the highly unentertaining WIT saga... Missed opportunity = Stopped caring. As for his gimmick, not sold... “Warrior” as a gimmick cuts it about as much as “Wrestler”... Maybe a few segments explaining exactly what he is would have helped *Looks around* There were none? Oh well, that explains it. As for Burden, something about his character screams “tight”... Even the name ties it up and he just seems a great package... But he too has fallen into the “No Seg” trap so what we have here is a feudless, heatless opener which Burden will win due to the fact his character doesn’t suck. Winner: Kris Burden Ryan Stephens vs Mark Makonie: “Mr 2 Page In-Ring” against “Mr Weren’t You Meant To Do Something” is 2nd on the bill in a booking led feud which few probably knew existed. Maybe if Streak incorporated Makonie into his Segs rather than Streak chants (Yeah, because you’re THAT over!) then this feud could be something... Then again if Makonie did something people might care as well. I have to pick a winner? Okay, as there seems to be no angle whatsoever it will go down to RP quality and that means Makonie... On a side note, if he does win, that’s 2 strait PPV wins, if he doesn’t use that as a springboard he should job to Frampton. Winner: Mark Makonie Andy Murray vs “The Rush” Rush Mason: Quote of the week, made by Xavier Kannon - Rush needs to find a feud rather than writing his own crowd pops off Ellie and Xavier digs - Sums up my opinion here, Murray can also be blamed for his complete lack of in-show material surrounding this... The Rush has been stuck in the Extreme division for near 7 months now, if he had a good feud (In this I DON’T count O.G) then he would be higher... He needs to ditch the Malice Crew and get himself heel to finally do something, as for Murray, he needs to be face.... Hopefully a double turn in this one leading to an Andy Murray DQ win. Winner: Andy Murray Sunshine vs Loc v Heaven: I hate being taken by surprise, it means I was stupid and didn’t see it... If only PCW’s resident girly sport player did this a few months ago he would be retiring in a higher division... As for the people he’s dropping it to.... I’d rather have seen El Diablo against the End for the belt, Loc will win it technically by default but a least it was off the back of a good feud and angle which eases my apathy... Heaven did the role of the Ninja some cred providing the shock value but will she win? No... And with Juan gone will this mean anything? I doubt it. Winner:Charlie Loc Reginald P. Lovecraft (c) vs Violence Jack vs BloodAngel vs Duzza vs THE Multiple Man vs Kramer Wild Wing: The “Let’s give them a match” Gimmick match. Lovecraft as Champion? I preferred Miyagawa, Eleanor Hall or Xavier Kannon... All 3 actually made the Title interesting, as people actually KNEW who had it then... As for the challengers? It’s sad to see Tmm down this low, Duzza’s 1 RP per show is only a challenge to no-showers, Violence Jack has done a nothing so big and fat it’s clinically obese, Blood Angel has signed, taken a break, and nobody knows when he came back because he did nothing before it and Kramer... Well, Kramer RP’s which is something. I also think I am right in saying that nobody in this match has had any incard material since the formation of the Political Machine.. That REALLY helps my not caring. Winner: Kramer Wild Wing El Diablo v the End: YES! A match with heat... I love you guys! Few feud better than El Diablo and although this has lacked the sheer mayhem of El D/Aymin it has progressed nicely, both men looking like they could be awesome TV Champs, great CW Champs or genuine contenders for the Continental.... The End has really stepped it up as of late, after being stuck in such things as the Justin Howard affair and an awful Stormbringer feud... As for a winner, well, I think the End may just out RP him... But I believe El Diablo DESERVES the win, and I’d love to see him pull this one out of the bag. Winner: El Diablo John Crichton vs Jordan X. Shabazz: So the TV Title keeps up it’s record of feudless, heatless nothing matches... I think the only recent TV Title match that actually mattered was Akiyama v the Pharaoh... Why is this match here again? Crichton was the Champion because Jason Core got sucked back to the vacuum that is CCW and as for Shabazz being the next big thing? So he RP’s a lot... So did Frampton, be it JXS’s are infinitely better but the fact he is off TV for nearly all but his matches is NOT big thing material... And a cameo in Rush’s segments won’t help much. As for a winner, Shabazz due to the fact he’ll mention the match... As for the aftermatch Shabazz needs a feud over the title and some in-card work to make the next step because anyone saying he should get to the Cont. division on the basis of an RP a day is dreaming. Winner: Jordan X Shabazz The Dark Messiahs vs The Heartbreakers vs Pretty Cool Guys vs Cowabunga Connection vs Misplaced vs Suicidal Tendencies vs The Chef and Alexis Parshin vs Politically Incorrect vs Rent-A-Hero and Snow vs Akiyama Dark Messiahs, Misplaced & Suicidal Tendencies stand no chance. The Cowabungas finally seem to have heard the wake up call, P.I are no longer the best and have all but faded away, Parshin seems to be awol which leaves us with 4.... Either Heartbreakers or PCG’s get it to heat up their feud, Akiyama pulls It off, or RAH & Snow are given it... I feel PCG v Heartbreakers doesn’t need the Belt, let them run the feud a bit then maybe throw the belt in. The Akiyama thing is great, but I think it has played out before the PPV and if he wins it’s just going to be more of the same... So I’m picking the latter as I have no idea where it is headed... I don’t know if they’ll split, if so who’ll be heel... I just hope being in the Malice Crew won’t heed them too much, but, lets give them the belts and let them carry their angle out. Winners: Snow & Rent-a-Hero Jon Kano vs 'Controversial' Corey Williams I suppose on the back of Corey’s Eminem segment I care about this a little.... Kano will probably do an RP swearing at Nelson due to CCW’s UA connection and after 3 RP’s max between them this will be easily forgotten, with Corey probably getting the win. Winner: Corey Williams Xavier Kannon vs Alexander Miyagawa vs 'Legend Inc.' Tom Lexian vs 'The Malice Man' Duke Williams Some say match of the night, and I hope the writer does it justice... Legend Inc. seems to be finally realising PCW RP’s usually have something to do with PCW, Kannon/Miya is going along steadily, but I think with the Bachelor Party, Wedding and Reception to come we have only seen a taster and Duke seems to be languishing after his Kannon angle which is a shame because his RP style is perfectly suited to a feud, always willing to focus on his opponent and is probably PCW’s best “Give and Take” role-player, and I prefer a good RP’er to a great writer. So who will win? Well, Kannon seems to be the big underdog as most are ruling him out by default, assuming he will be sucked into the main event vacuum to fill in for Nelson. A LOT are picking Legend Inc, but with his latest RP’s I can’t say I’m convinced, he’ll have the numbers but he needs to find a balance between his writing and relevance.. He is probably in PCW’s top 3 writers, and when he manages to fit this around his storylines tightly the he will be a real force. Then there is Miyagawa who’s feud with Kannon looks set to explode right before this match, but this could hurt him in the match with Kannon probably wanting him to lose even if it means he does too... Which leaves my pick, Duke. He was unlucky not to win it against Kannon, but Xavier wasn’t ready to move up so kept it, now is Duke’s perfect moment to grab the Title, and as soon as he gets it a feud with nearly anyone in the upper-midcard and the President would equal ratings as long as they are willing to partake in Duke’s style. Winner: Duke Williams. Mount Olympus Match Frisco vs Big Alan Steele(c) This seems pretty predictable, but it will make a nice change to see a Champion retain at a PPV something not done since Ivan at Alliances... I’m assuming this was scheduled to be Nelson v BAS, which I could have probably bought a Title change on, but with this match.. Steele all the way. Credit to Frisco though, he has come in and worked extremely well with BAS, their Seg work has been awesome, and Turmoil’s work among the finest I have seen in a PCW show. This could carry on after the PPV for another month easily, but if the do move Kannon up to face Steele, a Frisco/Miya feud would be an exciting prospect to any PCW fan. Winner: Big Alan Steele. Well, that’s a PPV picked, so now time for a little closing rant which basically came from a chat room debate I saw and thought about.. The topic was relevance of RP’s. Now, looking at the “On Air” section of the PCW site it’s pretty clear that RP’s translate to on air promos, be them not on Turmoil or Fusion... So why would a promo that has nothing to do with a Feud/Angle/Match be aired? Simple, it wouldn’t. Now naturally, character development is an important part of RP’ing and I’m accepting that sometimes an RP will be segregated by current events and branches off. But what is the point of constantly developing character all RP every RP? Why is the character being developed if he is never going to turn that developed character to PCW events in RP’s? Also, correct me if I am wrong, but in an RP Fed you RP for matches, that’s why we have 2 shows a week, that’s why writers write matches and editors edit cards... Segments alone shouldn’t be used to focus on current events, segments are simply RP’s set at the TV show. If you just want to write about your guy detached from PCW doing something that doesn’t relate in one bit to PCW then join an on-line writing circle or something! If you’re going to be in PCW and have PCW staff make your posers, write your matches and edit your shows together then at least RP about PCW!!! I’ll leave that rant at that for a while, and move on to the “closing” section of the column which will generally be random... And if I do indeed never do another one, it won’t be... See, bases covered! Memo to Mike Frampton: You posted the poll, the people spoke... So if you are going to go, GO!!! Why would bookers give you a match if you have already said you are quitting? Why would writers give you a win knowing you going over will be for nothing. You did he right thing in leaving so do it the right way.
