Red Army To Pay

28 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Red Army To Pay Posted by Sandy Wong on 28 Sep 2001 ---- The CEO of PCW, Kremmen, has announced that the Red Army will be financially responsible for the damages incurred during Ivan Stanislav's rampage. Kremmen, on a shopping spree in Bloomingdale's with the PCW credit card, said "PCW is not in any kind of financial position to cover the costs of repairing the damage from that big goofs roid rage fit." Upon hearing this news, Ivan simply scoffed and walked off.
