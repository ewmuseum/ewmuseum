Borro Goes Global

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Borro Goes Global Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 May 2002 ---- Tuesday night Juan Borro held a press conference concerning FIFA in Lisbon, Portugal. To much of the suprise to Portuguese soccer fans and soccer fans all over the world, Juan announced that he has accepted a contract to be part of the coaching/training team for the Portuguese national team during the World Cup. Juan is being offered 6 million dollars for the role of just working over the course of a month or two with the team. It's also rumoured that through advertising and sponsorship including his role in the movie "Not without my Right Foot" Juan will be taking in a large 13.5 million dollars. When reporters talked to Juan about the subject, he had this to say. "I'm just happy to be back with the Portuguese National Team. I'll make sure they make quick due with teams like Poland, US, and Japan." Juan answered. When asked about all of the money he was recieving Juan had a different approach to the answer. "Yea it sure is alot of money. I don't even know where to spend this 13.5 million dollars not to mention to 152 million I have in the bank. Just yesterday I came to a conclusion on how to spend it. Any PCW wrestler who is upset or feels like they are being paid less then they deserve by the PCW should contact me ASAP. If I fix your financial status, let's just say you owe me one. The idea sounds kind of fishy to PCW officials, but not much is known yet. We'll keep you updated on the story.
