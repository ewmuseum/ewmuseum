Praise Hubbar-....WHO?!?!?! Posted by Sandy Wong on 14 Sep 2002 ---- Hubbardville, Wisconsin- Friday Fusion saw PCW land in Hubbardville, both the spiritual and physical home of CEO and Champion, Xavier Kannon. In a packed L. Ron Hubbard Memorial Arena, surrounded by over 25,000 of his fellow Scientologists he set about making sure the show ran smoothly as not to ruin his address at the end of the show. Both Clyde and the jovial thorns in his side, Pranks With Attitude were dealt with... But not even the works of Hubbard himself could have prophesied what went down at the end of a monumental show. Also on Fusion, a weakened Akiyama has the Pharaoh on his case while his Divine Prophetess keeps up the torture and torment of Anarchy. Syko and Bertha share an intimate moment in the closet as they are hunted down by Venneman Pendragon. Ellie~! shows why she shouldn’t be left alone with a match contract and a pen. Duzza falls out with family and wrestlers make their return to PCW TV... Some more appreciated than others. Amidst the Kannon friendly crowd, which wrestlers would return? Which would rediscover their old ways? And which friendships would be reunited? To see all that happened on this gigantic night in PCW, head down to the Fusion Banner and click “Results”.
