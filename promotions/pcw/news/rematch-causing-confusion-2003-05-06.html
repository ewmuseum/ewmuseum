Rematch Causing Confusion

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rematch Causing Confusion Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 May 2003 ---- As has been reported, Friday Fusion's main event is once again, featuring Skylar Green and Pharaoh. Totally Tubular Tommy will also be a part, but the fans seemed not to have noticed. They care only that they're getting what seems to be a total repeat of last week's Fusion ME. "Dude, I watched Fusion last week, and I SO know that this week, the same thing's gonna happen. I'm all...psychotic, like Miss Cleo," said a young Greek pothead near the arena. (we don't know why he was there 3 days earlier either...probably the pot). Another Greek man, who looked remarkably like one of those people in "My Big Fat Greek Wedding," said that this main event is "stupid, seeing as we just saw these two last week. DOWN WITH ROMULUS!" Of course, he failed to notice that Romulus is no longer a PCW employee, but we forgave him because we're kind people. However, the fans have spoken and they, perhaps, are hoping for something new. Will anything be changed or will the main event go on as scheduled? Does TTT have MORE mayhem planned? Can we FINALLY have a winner? All will be revealed LIVE from Athens, on Friday Fusion.
