Think Red #2

Think Red #2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Sep 2001 ---- Greetings Comrades, It is I, Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan with a much awaited and anticipated edition of “Think Red!” Allow me to be the first to apologize for my lack of writing, though I am sure you know that I am quite the busy Russian, trying to handle all the international strains that come with being a member of the intelligentsia, as well as take care of things on the “PCW front,” if you will. First and foremost, I would like to say that my thoughts and prayers go out to those that were affected by the tragedy that struck the United States last Tuesday. I assure you that while the USA is spearheading the “war” against terrorism, you are not fighting alone, for the Russian Federation is there to back you up in any way that we can! The CEO? Or nyet? That is the question that many wrestlers are contemplating as of late. Who exactly is the CEO of the PCW if it is not Nelson, nor is it Jeremy Howard? The Red Army shared a collective cheer as we saw Jeremy Howard’s jaw drop when the news was released that he indeed was not the Chief Executive of the PCW. But that merely opens another question: Who is? As I look around at those near me, I question if someone knows more than I do. But then again, that is impossible, as I am not only the most intelligent, but the most informed man within the PCW! Whoever is the CEO, it would be best that he keep himself away from the public. Instantly he will be branded a target by virtually every wrestler within the PCW, including Ivan Stanislav and The Red Army. I would not be surprised if it was someone within Greed Incorporated, who is merely holding a sharp knife and ready to stab it in the back of Howard. But that is merely my opinion….who knows if it is true, yes? Injuries caught Red Handed With the new resurgence of opposition against The Red Army comes a newfound form of brutality implemented by her members. Take Amanda, Kid Wonder’s girlfriend for example. It was truly a sight to see her fly through the air with such ease, probably because of the lack of anything in her skull, and hit the side of that van only to lay in a heap beside her boyfriend! Hospital visits are becoming commonplace between those that dare to stand up against us. Just ask Hans Kruger…. After all, he was always a stoic challenger to our communist ways, and now..what is it, four months? Six months? Might as well go for a year out of action, because he’ll never dare to get in our way again! And then of course, who can forget Brandi, who did her best impression of a vampire throwing up on the latest edition of Turmoil. Some call it brutal and uncalled for to inflict such pain and punishment against those that are smaller than us, but I call it necessity! The PCW now realizes that we are still the power within the federation, and no one can stop us! No CEO, no European Union, no veteran, and no Kid Wonder will ever slow our thunderous approach! Who is the next person to be sent to the hospital via ambulance? Only time will tell my friends, but you should not have to wait long…. How long can we weight? No my friends, I did not use the wrong word in the last heading, I did spell the word as it is! Two Ton, the obese creature that has returned to the PCW, recently had words about Ivan Stanislav that we did not find particularly…amusing. While it could be said that he outweighs Ivan Sergeiovich, what does it matter? Rumor has it that it is virtually impossible to heft the weight of this man….but Ivan is not a normal human being! The time will come when Ivan and Two Ton will clash, and when that time comes his tubby lard bottom will be thrown into oblivion! Lethal Lottery The Lethal Lottery for the main event at the October Pay Per View: Red October, which is taking place in the Motherland, mind you, is already being considered and speculated upon. Later rumors that the contestants would be teamed up were later confirmed, which means that someone will be having the pleasure of teaming with Ivan Stanislav. This does not bode well for whoever his opponents are, but one must wonder how Ivan will function with an outside fighter. This however, is for certain: Whoever it is, do realize that it is Ivan who is the commander of the attack, and you are merely backup to make sure no one acts out of line. Failure to understand the rank and file of our system will result in much deserved pain at your own partner, Ivan’s, hands! Closing remarks What else can be said that hasn’t been proven to the PCW in the recent days? The Red Army is stronger than ever, and her enemies lay scattered in her wake. And while Ivan did NOT acquire the PCW World Title, we are patient men, and we can wait until next time. Those of communist heritage or those that are interested in the ways of communism are urged to contact Praporschik Stanislav to discuss conscription into our army, as able bodied wrestlers are always welcome into the fold! Long Live Communism! Long Live the glorious Red Army! The Former Soviet Union lives within all of us! But of course…always….ALWAYS remember to THINK RED! Alexei Ruslan Director of FSB Red Army Intelligence
