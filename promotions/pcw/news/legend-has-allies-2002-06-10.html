Legend has Allies?

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Legend has Allies? Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Jun 2002 ---- Following "Legend Inc." Tom Lexian's return to PCW, he has recently hinted at the formation of a new group in PCW. "Look, don't get me wrong," Lexian said to a reporter hassling him at the local pub. "I love the good guys at PCW, but there's got to be some law and order. What about good over evil? The good guy always comes out on top? We need to bring that back and I intend to do so." Reports say that Legend Inc. has recruited two or three other members for his group that are not known to PCW or to the WT/PTC family and are collectively known as "CoL." "CoL, CoaL, CooL," Lexian said, bewildered. "All I can say is don't worry about it. You'll find out everything at Turmoil." Legend Inc. returns in his first match at Tuesday Night Turmoil against Bobby Stormbringer.
