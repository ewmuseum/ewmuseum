Miyagawa Seeking to Leave PCW? Posted by Sandy Wong on 12 Apr 2002 ---- Recent reports, including one press statement that was aired and afterwards removed indicate that former PCW Champion and eighteen month veteran of PCW, Alexander Miyagawa is seeking to leave PCW. The Pimply Faced Intern Kid managed to track him down to get a few comments. PFIK: So what's the story, bro? Miyagawa: Bro? PFIK: Sorry. Miyagawa: I'm just thinking of my long term prospects at the moment. I've been wrestling in PCW for eighteen months with a week off of injury in May because of a combined attack by Montrose and Phoenix, and a week off for my honeymoon. PFIK: So are you leaving? Miyagawa: I've asked for my release from Tyler Nelson, but he is yet to reply. I've got a family now and they deserve my time. I'm sure that I will get bored incredibly quickly and end up getting back in the ring just to get away from the screaming kid, but hey, that's obvious already to even myself. PFIK: I thought that Tyler Nelson couldn't fire you? Miyagawa: That is quite correct... which is why I might change the option to a leave of absence... because if I quit then my current contract will be voided and if I did come back I'm sure Tyler wouldn't put the same clause in the contract as the Phantom Booker did when he hired me. PFIK: The Phantom Booker? Miyagawa: Tyler has asked to speak to me on Sabotage, so I'll tell him about the little clause then. Anyway, gotta go... Natalia is expecting me at the hospital with some blood test results. PFIK: How is she? Miyagawa: Stable for the moment, but we still need Boda's blood. I'm going to ask him politely, brother to brother on Fusion to see if he'll be generous enough. PFIK: Good luck. Miyagawa: I think I'll need it.
