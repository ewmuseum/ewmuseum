Tomas News

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tomas News Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Oct 2002 ---- For the past three days, PCW’s own Teresa Tomas has been in the backcountry of Alabama guest staring in video. The people of the Real Tree Outdoors/Monster Buck series has offered her a whopping five grand to spend three days in the middle of nowhere hunting deer. When asked for comments Teresa had this to say. It was the thrill of it all that lured me. When asked about the huge sum she will receive shortly, she basically turned her back and walked away. In other news, Tomas is also settling a private and tedious court battle against former lover, partner, driver and friend, Jack Slayter. After leaving Teresa tied and exposed in a McDonald’s men’s restroom, the large redneck stole Teresa’s Chevrolet step side pick-up and headed for Texas. According to our sources, Teresa sued for theft, assault, physical and emotional damage. Jack countersued Teresa for the numerous times she attacked him due to her uncontrollable violent temper. In the end, the judge awarded Teresa her truck, plus three thousand dollars. As for Jack, Teresa was ordered to seek counseling for her small problem and the two are forever banned from each other. Neither parties were available for comments.
