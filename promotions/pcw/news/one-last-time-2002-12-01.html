One Last Time... Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Dec 2002 ---- Waco, Texas- On the last Adrenaline before PCW matches make their way onto the card, it was fitting that the show opened with the return of the last RUSH Champion to announce he’s arrived back, and is ready to fight Kannon for it’s freedom. However, Kannon and his Front Office were not deterred and gave Chris Matthews and Mordred a gift each, Brad Lamen his marching orders and Walden returned to RUSH with Taxation on his mind. Also on Adrenaline, PCW stars try to get Xavier’s attention by recreating something from Waco’s past, El Spiffy! gets a taste of PCW’s official soft drink and the Syndicate Title was on the line in the Main Event. To see all this go down and more, head to the Adrenaline banner and click “Results”.
