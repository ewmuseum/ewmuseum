P.W.A. At Turmoil

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
P.W.A. At Turmoil Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Sep 2002 ---- Rumour has it that Turmoil will once again lay victim to another invasion by the recent tag acquisition of 'Pranks With Attitude'. Last week during Fusion the team decided to steal air time by masquerading as their opponents Misplaced with Nerezza taking the microphone as Carey J. Nowski and his partner in crime Gaz "The Jester" Green portraying Stephen Cairns. Catching up with the dynamic duo, they could only respond with this brief statement - "Last week we thought our stunt was the sole reason for Fusion's flagging ratings and have decided to set aside future segments...." roared the jovial Jester with a grin. "With Anarchy and Miyagawa in competition it wouldn't feel right with only half the Asylum present so we may show for morale support!" bellowed Nerezza. We can only sit back and watch Turmoil to see whether any truth unfolds.
