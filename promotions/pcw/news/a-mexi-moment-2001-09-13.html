A Mexi Moment

A Mexi Moment Posted by Sandy Wong on 13 Sep 2001 ---- This column is brought to you from the Canadian JOBbers Fund. Please make a donation and stop excessive JOBbing today. Well do to the lack of columns recently in PCW; I felt it was my duty as your Cruiserweight Champ to write one. First let me say this will likely not be worth your time and I have include an unedited rant just to piss Frisco off:) Rant Ok, what is going on in PCW of late? We have a lot of new talent being thrown into PCW as of late. I went to check out all the new posers that Miya has been producing and I notice about a dozen names that need to be talked about. Starting in reverse alphabetical order. Sorry if I leave anyone out, if I do I’m human, if not I’m God. Two Ton, The Storms, Skitzo, Syko, Agent Orange, Oracle, Jake Keeton, Kevin Klaymore, Big Poppa Plump and Juan “The Golden Toe” Borro are all recent additions to PCW, my question is where does this leave NTICW? Who is left there? Revenant? But who really cares about NTICW except for VP Mike, let’s move on to how this will affect PCW. As any loyal PCWite will tell you the Cruiserweight Title will not be safe with Two Ton back in the fold. The Storms vs Da Westsidaz, let’s get the two on two match done so we won’t have to hear any more excuses about the Storms loosing in Clusterf*cks. Freaks by Choice or part of them are now in PCW, no Pirana though I guess PB hasn’t lost his mind just yet. Hmm, Agent Orange seven feet tall, perhaps the Brandi Stalker? No, I think we all know who it is by know. Oracle is here of course now we will get to see Brandi and Oracle get it on, yippee! Then you have Kevin Klaymore, Big Poppa Plump and Juan Borro, I would look for them to make a splash in PCW. Lastly the returning Jake Keeton, could his return have anything to do with Two Ton, probably not but I have nothing to say about him really. Now I have done the mandatory overview of new talent I just want to say a few about the vets. First off it’s nice to see Reverend back, I don’t think I’ll find many to argue that. Miya looks good of late and seems to be a little rejuvenated. Kid Wonder is back in the Cruiserweight division, and with the additions made I would think the Cruiserweight division will get quite packed and highly entertaining in the near future. Lastly I want to say can anyone stop Ivan? That’s it for this incoherent rant, my apologies to Frisco for the many grammatical errors. Back to Old School Rising Star Title Lot of good young talent here but I think in the end it will come down to Tim Walden and Mr. UK. It’s a shame Rent-A-Hero won’t be able to give it his all for this match. When it comes down to it all, Tim will likely wind up doing the Canadian JOB but there will be many more opportunities for him. Mr. UK Miya vs Kensuke I just can’t see the goggles losing this one, I just can’t. Miya Cruiserweight Title Match Well what can I say here, it would be easy to say that the White Mexican wins but that likely just won’t happen. I mean if I still hold the belt by the pay per view I will be on day 42 of my reign so as hard as I RP and as good of quality I put out I will expect to do the Canadian thing in the end. Who will win it you are still asking and I wouldn’t be surprised to see the HIT hold the title when it’s all said and done. He has an excellent work rate and is making me earn the victory on Turmoil. I have a new found respect for him but I nor the other four men in this match will just lay down. The HIT Extreme Title Match I’m just going to say Hank won’t win, JJ won’t win, and Ed will also go the Canadian way as to will Valentine. The only non-Canadian in this match will be the Russian. Nikita Morozov Commish Match I haven’t seen Porter lately. Hollywood TV #1 Contender It’s getting close to the time of year when you wake up, look outside and everything is white. Snow Tag Team Titles Da Westsidaz vs ???, hmm does it matter who? No it doesn’t. Da Westsidaz Stairway to Hell Vamp has a 150+ page RP!!! That’s unheard of. I would see that and immediately scream, run and hide for fear of reading it, but I can almost guarantee that Vampy will be one of the few that can pull off a RP of that size that doesn’t put you to sleep. After that I’m left saying Jupiter is Canadian so you all know what that means and Kriegy will be an honorary Canadian on Sunday. Vampir TV Match I would love to see Darkstar hang on to it, but I think Big Alan will steal this one. (No pun intended.) Big Alan Steele Continental Match Boda is a member of the Red Army, he will still be a member of it after the PPV. If he turns Canadian here, he will leave the Army and go after Ivan. Boda CEO Match Well both are great and this should be one Hell of a match. I think Nelson will win with some back up. A man with his track record will not go into a match like this with out getting some help. He’s to smart to assume Howard won’t have Greed Inc and or the Red Army help. I trust Nelson will out think the exKrayzie one. Tyler Nelson World Title Match I just see Trashy lying down on the mat in true Canadian fashion here and letting the big bad Russian win. Chef has the best chance to stop him but Chef took the Continental title from Ivan last ppv. Do you really think Ivan will lose to him twice? Ivan Mark Outs If your name is listed below, I’m marking out for you, if not then you haven’t caught my attention yet. The HIT Nikita Morozov Da Westsidaz High Impact Tim Walden Mr. UK Rent-A-Hero Ivan Boda Vampir Snow Darkstar Wrap Up All I have to say is all you regular columnists please return soon so I can stop boring people with my useless opinions. This is your Cruiserweight Champ signing off. Call 1-800-JOB-bers with your donations today.
