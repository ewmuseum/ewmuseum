That Sly Devil

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That Sly Devil Posted by Sandy Wong on 19 Mar 2003 ---- As was seen at March Madness, The Mexican Devil has returned to PCW. This was quite a shocker considering many people in the industry thought he may never return to PCW or would appear elsewhere, but this was not the case. Apparently, El Diablo called up PCW officials to set the story straight about his return. "Damned right I was going to return to PCW," said El Diablo arrogantly, "There was never any doubts I'd be back." While PCW officials tried to get a word in, El Diablo just wouldn't stop talking, he never gave them a chance to get a word in. "This corrupted hellhole needed me back here, and I needed to be back for retribution obviously. If you haven't seen my hype video's then I suggest you do so, the stories of retribution are there," Diablo boasted, "I'm back for Flaymin Aymin, and then who knows what after that, but the key thing is El Diablo is back in PCW." He then hung up on the officials, not leaving them much for a story. But as history as shown us, when Flaymin Aymin and El Diablo cross paths it will not be a pretty sight.
