Slay Your Thirst

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Slay Your Thirst Posted by Sandy Wong on 23 Aug 2002 ---- For those of you who missed it, this is the commercial that has caused even more controversy between Venneman Pendragon and Syko the Goblin. Syko contends that the party of Sprite and Pendragon are guilty of breach of contract, while Sprite and Pendragon claim that Syko is guilty of bad acting. Who will win? We will have to wait to find out... -- Spot opens to the interior of what would appear to be the great hall of a castle. Banners hang from the walls, and the place is illumined by torchlight. A massive table seems to be set up for a feast, and a Sprite, or Goblin if you will, sits in the middle of the table, sampling the different foods. Sprite: My...All this food and not a thing to drink! Wait...What's that? My! It is a cool and refreshing beverage, yes? The Sprite hops from the table and walks to a corner of the hall. There sits a pedestal that holds a bottle of Sprite. Illumined under a spotlight, the Sprite takes center stage. The Goblin takes the bottle and twists off the cap.... (Clip-clop, clip clop) Sprite: What was that? No matter! I now have the most refreshing drink... (Clip-clop, clip-clop...) The hoof-beats get closer as the Sprite takes a long draw from the bottle of Sprite. A huge refreshed smile covers the Goblin's face as a powerful voice booms out.... "HALT YE VILE CREATURE!! YOU WOULD DARE BESMIRCH THE KING'S FEAST??? RELEASE THY HOLD ON YON REFRESHING SOFTDRINK! The Goblin freezes and looks turns around. There, Venneman Pendragon sits atop his white steed in full armor. He has Excalibur drawn and he points it at the terrified Goblin. His visage is stern.... "STUPID GOBLINS! ALWAYS PARTAKING OF WHAT BELONGS NOT TO YE!! STUPID GOBLINS SHOULD NOT DRINK SPRITE!" Pendragon rears back with his sword-arm and takes a mighty swing at the Goblin. The Goblin lets out a scream that is silenced as the sword severs his head from his body. Green "blood" pours from the wound and as the Goblin falls, Venneman reaches down and snatches the bottle of Sprite. He takes a long drink and a smile replaces the stern look.... "Ah....Truly a beverage fit for a KING! Image is everything....SLAY YOUR THIRST!" Pendragon tips the bottle to the camera and rests his sword on his shoulder. He winks, and the scene fades to black....
