The Plot Thickens

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Plot Thickens Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Mar 2002 ---- Provo, Utah- Over the past few weeks, there has been speculation that PCW has been losing some serious cash. But it was only speculation by people who don't like the PCW President or people who have nothing better to do. But on Tuesday, we finally heard the financial state from the man himself, PCW President Duke Williams. What did he have to say? How did he feel? Did he even care? How much longer can PCW last under the control of the "Malice Man"? Only time and Turmoil can tell. Also on Turmoil: The new GWF guys go after another former PWAer. A PCW hero makes his on-screen return. And how long would Boda and Miyagawa be able to co-exist against the Controversial Messiahs? To find out all this and more, check out Tuesday Turmoil by clicking on the link at the bottom of the page.
