The Forging of Alliances

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Forging of Alliances Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Oct 2002 ---- Sapporo, Japan- Xavier Kannon has had run ins with many men over the year here's been here. But little have had as much pull with the fans as Rent-a-Hero. On Turmoil the R-A-H brings back one of Kannon's former enemies and forces him to resign him to a fixed contract. But who was it? Blood is thicker than water. That is what Alexander Miyagawa said on Turmoil. But what did he mean by it, and who saw him say it and who did he say it to? El Diablo had something of a turn at Red October. Tonight he declared he should be champion and that he would put a stop to anyone in his way. But who did he declare was in his way? And don't forget both the Cruiserweight and Extreme titles were on the line, an annoucement was made about the Black Friday Mall Brawl and also one word that might seem familiar to some. Hummer.
