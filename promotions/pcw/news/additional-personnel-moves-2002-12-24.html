Additional Personnel Moves

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Additional Personnel Moves Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Dec 2002 ---- Not mentioned in the first personnel report were a few sidenotes from the RUSH side of things. -Stephanie O' Connor has been formally let go, after weeks of not showing up for work -Jerome "Blayze" Jordan is ready, once again, to work, and has been released from the short "warning suspension" placed upon him by Xavier Kannon a month or so ago -Mr. UK and Livewire, RUSH's two nametag Brits, have both been placed on the Injured List. LW suffered a severe pull of the muscles in his back and the moment is in a wheelchair. Mr. UK has had a bad knee for over a year, and will need to get it checked out by PCW's arthriscopic surgeons to analyze what should be done next. -Duke Williams, in an effort to thwart Xavier Kannon, is said to be looking at some former RUSH stars to return and one is said to be coming back this Fusion.
