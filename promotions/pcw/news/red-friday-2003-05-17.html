Red Friday

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Red Friday Posted by Sandy Wong on 17 May 2003 ---- Kabul, Afghanistan- There's not much we can say here without spoiling the GIANT surprise of Fusion. Well, actually, there was more than one surprise, but suffice to say, one certainly stood out amongst the rest. However, whether it was the giant return, the man who took a stand against the champion, or the two matches that were announced for the Pay Per View, a lot on this Friday's show was shocking. In addition to shocking, Tyler Preston added confusing, SyN gave us some controlling, SOCIETY did some demolishing, and a married couple did some serious arguing. For all this and more, check out the Fusion banner at the bottom of the page and click 'Results.'
