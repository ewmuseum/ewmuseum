Kannon Comments on U.A. Rift

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kannon Comments on U.A. Rift Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 May 2002 ---- With rumours spreading that he is leaving the Ultimate Authority, wrestling's only Scientologist was hunted down for comment. "Why, there are no problems within the U.A" he said in a sarcastic tone, "I mean, why should there be, he only FIRED ME after I spilt my Scientological blood to keep my Title!" After making a few unmentionable comments about Tyler's mother and what Hubbard would do to her in the bedroom he continued... "I even booked him into that damn match at Supremacy... Which he lost by the way, and then he fired me, the heir to his throne, out of frustration... I suppose he just realised I am the future and he is a remnant of the past!" In closing, the signs do not look good for Friday with comments like these... "If he tries to tag me I can think of somewhere that hand can be shoved that only Rupert Healey has seen before... And don't even get me on the sock headed jackass... I suppose it is there for protection when he shoves it up Nelson's AIDS ridden ass!" So it appears the rumours of U.A troubles are true, the question is can DA BOSS~! do what Jeremy Howard has previously failed to do and control Xavier Kannon
