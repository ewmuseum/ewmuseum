Snow's Legal Troubles Deepen

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Snow's Legal Troubles Deepen Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Nov 2002 ---- According to sources, three-time PCW Champion Jason Snow's legal troubles are far from being over, despite the fact that his parole hearing is scheduled to be held on Saturday, November 23. Snow is reportedly being sued by each of the three men who Snow has been convicted of attacking over the last five years, including the latest, which is the one that landed Snow in Peter J. Tankerton Maximum Security Prison. Ronald Elseworth, the latest in Snow's long line of defense attorneys, had this to say: "Our backs are really against the wall in this one. The fact that my client has been convicted of all three charges is going to hurt us in civil court. There's no doubt that Mr. Snow will have to pay out a substantial amount of money, the only question is how large the total sum will be." Snow is an extremely wealthy men, of course, reportedly being worth between 70-80 million dollars. Elseworth went on to say that he hoped that each of the cases could be settled out of court, otherwise Snow will face the real possibility of going bankrupt. Though he hopes three settlements can be reached, Elseworth finished by saying that the reality of the situation is that all three plaintiff's agreeing to settle out of court is "unlikely."
