No Respect for Honor and Tradition

20 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Respect for Honor and Tradition Posted by Sandy Wong on 20 Apr 2002 ---- -Seattle, Washington Jason Snow, Chris Chase, Corey Williams, and Dustin Downey.....aka the New Breed. A group of cocky and self absorbed athletes with a plethora of talent and the credentials to match. They pulled off a SWERVE~! when Chase laid down for Jason Snow a few short days ago, smacking every former PCW World Champion right across the face. On Fusion, the new champion was slated to face Big Alan Steele for the title. Who would leave Fusion with the belt? Also on Fusion, Duke Williams returns to ring action, Bobby Stormbringer suffers an injury at the hands of the Dark Age, and Chet King makes a premature return to the wrestling ring. To see all this action and a lot more just use the drop down menu to go to Recent Results, then click on the Fusion banner.
