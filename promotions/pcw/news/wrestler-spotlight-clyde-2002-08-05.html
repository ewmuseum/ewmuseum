Wrestler Spotlight : Clyde

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestler Spotlight : Clyde Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Aug 2002 ---- Recently PCW reporter Tiffany Jennings was lucky enough to catch up with PCW’s own Demon.. The Hardcore sadist known only as Clyde. Tiffany: So Clyde, the sun is shining, the birds are singing, summer is upon us and the hills are alive with the sound of music.. How are you on this glorious day? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Um.. Okay... So, this week your teaming with DarkStar again to take on Xavier Kannon and Snow.. Do you think that with your history as both an ally and enemy of DarkStar’s that you’ll have the natural chemistry to give you an advantage over your opponents? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Right... Anyway. Would you equate Tyler Nelson’s feud with Xavier Kannon to the ongoing conflict in the Middle East where each punishment attack acts as an event to be avenged by the opposite side thus leaving the conflict with no natural end? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: What about yourself and your seemingly new enemy the Rush? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Why did the Chicken cross the road? Clyde: To die. Tiffany: Cheery. Anyway, you are also famous for your relationship with Bethany, who many feel is a “TAD” young for you... In a few.. Or a lot... Of years when it would be legal, do you two plan to wed? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: You seem to dress very “Un-tidily”.. You obviously earn quite a lot from your PCW Contract so where does the money go? Are you one of these wrestlers who scrimps and saves for his life after wrestling? Clyde: Life? Die. Tiffany: You’re a stable-mate of Syko the Goblin.. Did you know that Sprite, which is a type of Goblin is also a refreshing soft drink? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Sprites are also partial to a good Western, but can’t take their Chilli. Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Um.. Recently you’ve become someone of a “Fan Favourite” in PCW... Do you have any words for your new legions of fans? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Ooooooookayyyy... Any final words? Some poetry? Trash Talking? A quick song? Clyde: Die. Tiffany: Thank you. Clyde: Die.
