Skylar's Opponent To Be ... More Than One? Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Jun 2003 ---- With Skylar Green being the World Champion, he's bound to make enemies in those that wish to knock him off the mountain he so rightfully climbed. And made enemies he has. Violence Jack, The Pharaoh, Dorn Tracer, Totally Tubular Tommy, and El Diablo are all vying for the chance to bring the King of the Mountain down off of his throne. And it just so happens that Skylar has a match on Friday against a 'Mystery Opponent.' Rumor has it that it may be more than one person. It could be a combination of any of the above, or someone who wants to step up and try his luck. Whatever the case, Skylar had better watch himself if someone decides to play the numbers game. Evidence of this has been brought up by PFIK, who claims to have seen various stars discussing the match and even joking about running in... then there's some that WEREN'T joking. Will Skylar end up having to bite off more than he can chew? Only time will tell.
