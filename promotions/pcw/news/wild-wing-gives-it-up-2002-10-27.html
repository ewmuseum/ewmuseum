Wild Wing Gives It Up

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wild Wing Gives It Up Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Oct 2002 ---- The past few weeks we have seen very little of Kramer Wild Wing. On Turmoil last week, Kramer was reportedly seen leaving the building several minutes after his match, which he never got a chance to compete in. Just yesterday Wild Wing gave the startling news that he was giving it all up. Below is the transcript. "Life just isn't working for me anymore. My family life has been ripped from the seams, and I don't think it can ever be mended." "What do you mean by that Mr. Wing?" "My father....." Kramer's mouth trembles as the words are hard to announce. "My father has taken ill and it's expected that he has a very short time to live. With this going on I have little passion anymore for wrestling as there is no longer a reason to do it. My father was always there to push me along, and since he won't be here anymore, I officially resign from PCW. Besides what in the hell do you people care? You never gave me the credit I deserved, and you always doubted my abilities." After giving that last sentence he stormed out of the room with a hot temper and a tear in his eye. So it is true folks. The Wild one has hung up his wrestling boots, and it is very unlikely that he will ever be returning. The era of crispy chicken seems to be over.
