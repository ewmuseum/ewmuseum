Kano Possibly Done

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kano Possibly Done Posted by Sandy Wong on 2 Aug 2002 ---- After the brutal attack by Corey Williams on Turmoil and the resulting fifteen foot fall to a concrete floor has more than likely ended Jon Kano's illustrious career in the professional wrestling world. Jon Kano was hospitalized following his fall and diagnosed with a major concussion, several broken ribs, multiple contusions and abrasions, and a small bruise has formed on Kano's brain. Jon Kano's attending physician, Dr. Nick Golden, had this to say, "Mr. Kano has been floating in and out of conscience since Wednesday night, often asking for Duke Williams or Kano's wife, Kristin. In my best medical opinion, Mr. Kano will never be able to wrestle again, and if he would, never at the level he was at before. Also, if Mr. Kano decides to make a return, he will almost certainly risk permanent injury. He has had too many injuries in his career, and now they are cashing in their debts with these new ones, so to speak. This last series of injuries has left some permanent brain damage in Mr. Kano, and I have told both him and his wife, that he needs to retire." What does this mean? Well Jon Kano is probably done in PCW in the wrestling sense. We will keep you informed as more information is released.
