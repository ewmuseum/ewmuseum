April 4th Fusion Main Event

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
April 4th Fusion Main Event Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Apr 2003 ---- As you saw on Fusion, Flaymin Aymin came out with a huge combine tractor. He would've destroyed the entire set if not for the quick-thinking Jeff Marx, who lobbied for an EMP device to be placed under the ring for just such occasions. It ended the show early, but luckily Tiffany Jennings was on hand and wrote up a recap of the events after that, as the match still went on as planned. ---- Dave: We've had a wild night, and now it's time to bring it all to a climactic close with the main event! Jeff: This one will feature Chet King getting his ass kicked by three other men! Dave: The three against him aren't exactly friends, either. It'll be fair. "Walk Away" by Epidemic plays. Clay: Ladies and gentlemen, the tonight's main event is a Four-Corners match scheduled for one fall! Though Totally Tubular Tommy is involved, it is a non-title matchup. Coming to the ring first, hailing from Puebla, Mexico; weighing 223 pounds... El Diablo! Boos for the former World Title contender, and Clyde's bitch from October 2002 to January 2003. Dave: El Diablo is back in PCW now that Clyde is no longer the PCW World Champion. Jeff: Great to have him back. He's the only Mexican I can stand. "Call of Ktulu (unnamed remix)" by Metallica rocks the speakers with Violence Jack walking out to the ring. Dave: Here is the Co-Continental Champion! Clay: From Boston, MA; weighing 233 pounds... Violence Jack! Jack walks down the ramp and steps into the ring. The next music that plays is "Back in Black" by AC/DC. Dave: Oh no! Jeff: Oh no! Rolling down the ramp in a super-combine tractor capable of destroying the entire set is Flaymin Aymin! Dave: He and El Diablo have already destroyed the Sabotage set! Jeff: But this time we're prepared! Jeff presses a button on his desk. Sadly, the show must come to an early end. The screen fades to black with the © PCW 2003 logo on the corner as the EMP radiates out from the ring, disabling the combine, but also the cameras. ___________________________________________________________________ Tiffany Jennings here, they sent me out to record what happened. Flaymin Aymin came out in a tractor and was going to destroy the set, but they set off an electromagnetic pulse that disabled the combine and all of our cameras. The match still went on as planned, let me catch you up. The tractor broke down and Aymin jumped out to attack El Diablo and Violence Jack. They were going to fight each other in the ring first, but having both had their problems with him in the past, they set that aside for a moment and double-teamed Aymin. He suffered a beatdown in the corner and a double-team chokeslam before they kicked him out of the ring. The tractor was left in neutral and I count maybe 50 ringcrew workers who had to tie ropes around the tractor and pull it back up the ramp and out of the ringside area (luckily Aymin had put it in neutral before ditching it). After that, they continued with the match. Chet King walked out without his music (the sound system was also rendered inoperative; in fact, if not for the gracious fans repeatedly snapping their cameras' flashbulbs, we wouldn't have been able to see a thing) and he got the biggest and only cheers in the main event. Diablo and Jack were already in the ring trading punches. Jack is more built for that kind of attack and got the better of him. After some general nonsense on Jeff's part, Dave said, "I guess we're going on with the match without light. The Co-Continental champion is taking advantage of the early slugfest." Chet King got to the ring and took the far corner from the ramp, waiting for a tag. Jack backed Diablo to a neutral corner and beat him across the head, climbing to the second rope for the ever-memorable yet ultimately useless 10-punch combination. I don't think anyone's ever won a match off of that. Ever. On Earth. El Diablo took Jack on his shoulders and stepped away from the corner, executing a Powerbomb and rolling over with a Reverse Sunset Flip. Jack kicked out at two. Jeff Marx marked for the Wild Bomb, saying that he liked both men but if he had to pick one guy to win between the two of them, it would be Boston's Bastard Son. "You're too biased," Dave responded. "You're stoopid," was Jeff's answer. I'm sure he would've spelled it like that. Diablo pulled Jack back up to his feet and backed him to the ropes, whipping him away. Diablo hit the ropes on that side. Chet King gave himself a blind tag. El Diablo turned his head for a moment to say something to Chet. He turned around into a Lou Thesz Press by Violence Jack, who mounted him for more punches to the head afterward. A loud chorus of boos notified us that Totally Tubular Tommy was casually, extremely slowly sauntering down to the ring. Everyone else just came down to the ring, really. Had little other choice. He was going to let the three tear into each other for a while. Chet King took to the middle of the apron and grasped the ropes in his hands. Pulling himself up with the jump, he springboarded into the ring for a Reverse Flipover Neck Whip to Violence Jack. Jack's neck whiplashed back, his head crashing down into El Diablo's head with great speed. Jack rolled off of Diablo and Chet King went for the pin. 1..... 2.... Kickout after two. Florien Liebig ushered El Diablo out of the ring (actually, he pushed him out). Diablo had to take a corner. Totally Tubular Tommy finally got to the ring and took another corner. All four men were in the match now. Chet King stood up and executed a Standing Moonsault for another two-count. Given Chet's weight and the height of the move, his Moonsault had almost no impact; he might've done it from the top rope but he probably didn't have time for it. Kneeling at Jack's side, Chet punched him in the face. Jack poked Chet in the eyes, getting him to stop his attack. Chet covered his eyes and walked over to the corner... Totally Tubular Tommy's corner. Tommy backed away a few steps as Chet turned around in the corner. Chet uncovered his eyes and Tommy ran across the apron to hit him with a Clothesline. The recoil sent Chet's body up into the air. As his feet hit the ground again, Chet got nailed in the midsection with a Corner Spear from Violence Jack. Jack Snapmared Chet out of the corner and gave Tommy a Shoulder Block through the ropes, hitting him in the midsection. Tommy was then yanked over the ropes, landing onto Chet in a crude Somersault Senton. Florien Liebig counted it as a pin attempt. 1.... 2.... Violence Jack stomped on Tommy's chest, breaking the pin. Rolling Tommy away from Chet, Jack went to the outside. Both men were down. Liebig didn't bother with a 10-count, he'd let the match go anyway. Totally Tubular Tommy and Chet King came up at the same time, Tommy near the neutral corner (Jack had taken his position farther away). Tommy charged Chet King with another Clothesline, which Chet ducked under and turned around to Tommy's head in an Upside-Down Facelock. Chet had Tommy in position for the Chat Back, but Tommy kicked off of the turnbuckle and flipped behind Chet, coming to his own Upside-Down Facelock on Chet King. Chet, now himself near the corner, grabbed the ropes and pulled himself in while dropping to his knees. Tommy was pulled forward and his chest hit the top turnbuckle. Tommy stumbled back out of the corner and Chet King stood up with a blind Back Kick, nailing Tommy in the chin. After that, Chet King went for the pin. 1.... 2.... Chet King rolled away from Totally Tubular Tommy, as Violence Jack went to the top rope and jumped off with a Body Splash. The attack hit Tommy. Chet caught Jack in the side of the head with a knee lift as Jack tried to rise; bringing Jack back to his feet, Chet took him out with a chain of two Snap Suplexes. Chet then went for another pin on Totally Tubular Tommy for another close two count. Dave Kern spoke favorably about Chet King's performance so far. "Despite being outnumbered, Chet King is managing himself well." "He manages himself before and after every match," Jeff added, most likely with some sarcastic remark following. "His locker room never stops smelling like jizz." Chet kicked Violence Jack out of the ring and brought Tommy up to his feet, whipping him over to El Diablo's corner. Chet followed in with a Jumping Heel Kick. As he fell to the ground, El Diablo tagged himself in by slapping Tommy's shoulder. Florien Liebig saw it and allowed it. Chet hit Tommy with some midsection Shoulder Blocks. Diablo slung himself over the ropes with a Slingshot Sunset Flip. Florien Liebig counted the pin. 1... 2... Tommy broke the pin with a Flipover Neck Whip. He landed over Chet King and turned around, putting him into a Bow Pin. Liebig informed Tommy that Diablo tagged in on him, and Tommy was no longer the legal man. Totally Tubular Tommy kicked El Diablo out of the ring and turned around, catching a Diving Clothesline from Chet King. Chet stood up and brought Tommy to his feet, whipping him to the ropes. Chet followed in and jumped up for a Dropkick. Tommy grabbed onto the ropes and Chet dropped to the mat. Grabbing Chet by the legs, Tommy catapulted him to the ropes. A pair of hands dragged Chet's throat over the top rope. It was Pharaoh! Chet King grabbed his throat and turned back to the center of the ring. Totally Tubular Tommy blasted him with a strong Superkick, making Chet fall out of the ring and right at Pharaoh's feet. Pharaoh grinned as he stood over the fallen Chet King. He said something about enjoying what he was about to do. Pharaoh brought Chet King up and pulled him over to the guardwall. He slung Chet at it; Chet reversed! Pharaoh hit the guardwall and his momentum carried him over. Chet King took a few steps back, and as Pharaoh came up to his feet, Chet charged and jumped clear over the wall to hit Pharaoh with a Cross Body Block! Pharaoh rolled through and mounted Chet King, punching him in the head. Chet poked Pharaoh in the eyes. Pharaoh got off and backed up. Chet followed him through the crowd. At that point, Chet King and Pharaoh brawled up through the crowd and out of sight, and Chet King never came back to the ringside area. Tommy was left in the ring, and El Diablo came in after him. Totally Tubular Tommy and Diablo stumbled forward, hitting each other haphazardly. Venneman Pendragon and Flaymin Aymin hit the ring! Bouncing back off of each other, Diablo and Tommy moved back into range of their respective rivals. Totally Tubular Tommy got dropped on his neck with a very impressive German Suplex courtesy of Pendragon. Tommy rolled out of the ring and Pendragon followed him. Aymin simply tripped Diablo with a Front Russian Legsweep and bashed his face into the mat repeatedly. Violence Jack, meanwhile, was about to climb into the ring when he was hit in the side of the head with the Continental Title by Skylar Green! Skylar pulled Violence Jack back up with little effort and dropped him to the floor with a Powerbomb. Aymin pulled Diablo out of the ring to the floor and pulled out a torch from underneath the ring. He set it to El Diablo's boots and they caught fire! Diablo, bleeding from the nose, struggled to get his boots off. Aymin responded by setting his ring-tights ablaze! Diablo rolled around to put out the flames. On the other side of the ring, Venneman Pendragon stood Tommy up against the ringpost, backed away a few steps, and hit him with the Sword in the Stone! Dave said, "What a devestating Spear to the back!" "Venneman likes going in the back," Jeff snidely remarked. Skylar Green, meanwhile, threw Violence Jack into the ring and told Florien Liebig to begin a ring-out count! The referee did so. 1.... 2.... 3.... Pendragon pulled Tommy up onto his shoulders and dropped Tommy to the floor with a Reverse Death Valley Driver. Tommy didn't even give much of a response to the move that impacted his neck on the concrete. 4.... 5.... Aymin had stopped trying to burn Diablo's pants and settled for hitting him with a chair. 6.... 7.... 8.... Dave Kern mentioned that, "It's a shame that a match involving such good wrestlers has to turn out like this." 9.... Jeff said, "At least one of the good guys won!" 10.... The winner by countout in 6 minutes was Violence Jack. Liebig rose his arms as he started to come to. On the outside, Totally Tubular Tommy and El Diablo were still getting kicked around by their rivals; I could imagine the same for Chet King. Skylar Green had walked away as soon as he told the referee to count the ring-out. The crowd was booing about Violence Jack winning, but cheering for the faces taking out the heels. Pendragon and Aymin walked away, having made their point, and the ringcrew swept up the mess. when the show was over. It was a good main event, though rampant interference did make it shorter than it could have been.
