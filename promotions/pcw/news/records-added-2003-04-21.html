Records Added Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Apr 2003 ---- There've been some new additions to the PCW record books as they relate to some of our titles. Skitzo's Extreme Title reign is officially 65 days, making him the longest-reigning champion. Dorn Tracer has held the Rising Star/Shooting Star Title for 58 days, making him the longest-reigning champion of that division. Chet King has entered the record books by being only the third man to become a four-time PCW Cruiserweight Champion. This happened in a questionable fashion after Pharaoh disqualified himself to switch the title's owner, but nonetheless congratulations to Chet King. Pranksters With Attitude, Gaz Green and Nascosto Nerezza, have become the third team in PCW history, and the first and only team in PCW's Third Age, to win the Tag Team Titles five separate times. Spring Fling marks the end of PCW's first Continental Co-Championship, as Skylar Green just relinquished his half of the championship to Violence Jack. Also, this post-pay-per-view period marks the most turnover in the history of the PCW personnel department, as close to 30 wrestlers have been coming and going. This excludes the mass-firings that we'd all rather forget. That is all.
