Trigger Happy

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trigger Happy Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Jun 2002 ---- PCW wrestler Trigger faced jury today in a case that could have locked him away for many years. The charges, which were for assault and attempted murder, sprung from a mysterious incident in which he locked a passenger inside the trunk of a car, and proceeded to drive the car off a bridge. The victim, a male in his mid twenties, had only minor injuries. It was only after this witness's testimony, in which he claimed it was all a misunderstanding, that the Rising Star Champion was acquitted. When approached for comments, Trigger had this to say: "I feel very relieved that this is done and over with. I didn't feel comfortable saying it at the time, but the victim is in fact one of my Warriors In Training, and the incident was just a punishment gone wrong. Nevertheless, it is great that I may now focus on the next challenge ahead of me, which is defending my Rising Star Championship against Goeren next Tuesday on PCW Turmoil." It seems uncertain that Trigger can triumph in the mist of this chaos, but that is a question that can only be answered if you check out PCW Turmoil this Tuesday.
