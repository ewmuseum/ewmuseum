TCM Elected

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TCM Elected Posted by Sandy Wong on 1 Apr 2002 ---- After a couple of close, yet failed attempts, Trashcan Man has been inducted into the Primetime Championship Wrestling Hall of Fame. He now joins such famous names as Meanstreak, Kremmen, and Frisco in the Hall. Trashcan Man, who was last seen working in PCW's arch-rival Old School Wrestling, could not be reached for comment. But his first tag team partner, The Joker, was. "If not for me, Trashy would've never been the star he was," said The Joker while searching through a dumpster. "I guided him in the right direction by starting Asylum. His success is my success. I'm glad to accept the award on our behalf." Congratulations to Trashcan Man on this great honor.
