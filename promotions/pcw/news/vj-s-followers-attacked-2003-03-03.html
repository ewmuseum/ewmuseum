VJ's Followers Attacked

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VJ's Followers Attacked Posted by Sandy Wong on 3 Mar 2003 ---- **The following footage is taken from this past Friday Fusion. It occured during a commercial break and production couldn't find the time to insert it into the program.** Violence Jack, with his entourage in tow, turns a corner to enter the hallway leading into his locker room. He pauses a moment and looks down the corridor where a small crowd has formed outside his locker room. VJ beckons his men to follow and walks up behind a smaller star of the federation who stands on his tiptoes to look past those in front. Jack clears his throat. VJ: Excuse me, child….. Young Tiger turns and looks at VJ then glances back at the open locker room door. He jerks a finger in that direction. YT: Sorry about that, Jack. VJ cocks his head in confusion. VJ: About what? I had almost ten of my disciples in there…. YT: Well, don’t worry, they’re still there. But not by much….. Jack storms past Young Tiger and several more PCW workers and staff to stand in the doorway. He gasps at the carnage within. Though his various devotion relics and shrines are untouched, all of his men have been laid out throughout the room and most are unconscious. All appear to suffer from broken arms and/or legs, those conscious moaning in pain. Jack curses and backs into his second-in-command, Brother Asenath. Asenath: Dammit…..who could have done it, m’lord? VJ glares dumbfounded at his subordinate. VJ: What a ridiculous question. Narrow your options down, brother. There’s not many that stand out. V-Jack, filled with rage, leaves the doorway and walks down the hall out of sight. Another of the cultists walk up to the stunned Asenath as he surveys the aftermath of the unseen attack. Cultist: This probably isn’t good is it? Asenath: No. Whoever did it was good, it’s not a normal hit and run attack. This wasn’t random. They had a motive, and the skill to pull it off. He points into the room, passing his finger over all of the victims who were also Jack’s room guards Asenath: Look....all of them, broken limbs. This wasn’t a standard beating by any stretch. Cultist: Did High Priest Shanahan look...I don’t know...worried when he took off? Asenath only shrugs as the camera pulls away, panning over the room of battered bodies.
