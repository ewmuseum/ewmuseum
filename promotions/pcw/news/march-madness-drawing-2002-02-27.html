March Madness Drawing

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March Madness Drawing Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Feb 2002 ---- Due to Kid Wonder's outburst at the end of Turmoil, the live drawing had to be abandoned after just three people. So, after PCW President Duke Williams bought a new machine, the rest of the drawing was held early Wednesday morning. Snow automatically enters last due to the fact he's the defending champion. And the three men who already drew, Legend Inc., Tyler Nelson, and Kid Wonder keep their spots. Drawing Results Legend Inc. Kid Wonder Akiyama Corey Williams The End Miyagawa Chet King Bobby Stormbringer Jon Kano The Chef El Diablo Flaymin Aymin Boda Darkstar Xavier Kannon Chris Chase Juan Borro The Rush Tyler Nelson Snow March Madness takes place live from the Savvis Center on March 3rd. You can see it live and only on Pay-Per-View.
