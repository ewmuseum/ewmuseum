Boca Del Inferno... GOING STRAIGHT?! Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Nov 2002 ---- I know, we got just as many laughs reading that as you did, but a press release from The Divine Prophetess Cyclonica of the Celestials had this to say. "Suffice it to say that the Pimply-Faced Intern Kid shall die very soon. But in the meantime, the Celestials are stuck teaming with The Walking STD Boca Del Inferno. Not that I feel sorry for those ignorant flatscans who were hoping for a Ream Team appearance at this year's Alliances but I can honestly say I would prefer nothing greater than that they could get their wish; that way the Celestials wouldn't be carrying dead weight into the next pay-per-view. "This is all an attempt by Xavier Kannon to undermine his enemies. He's been dodging and trying to hold down Akiyama since he was in a position to do so. And he's trying the same with the Celestials. But we refuse to, as Playaz Inc. would put it, Go out like that. "We'll take Boca Del Inferno as our teammate for Alliances and HE WILL BE A WRESTLING THREAT. Who better than to coach on proper fighting technique than beings that are better than human? As for his choice of lifestyle, his attraction to the opposing teams will be an undoubted liability left unchecked. With that, I shall declare that by the time Alliances has come and gone, though Boca Del Inferno shall indeed be dead, HE WILL BE A STRAIGHT WINNER OF ALLIANCES 2K2. "Interview over." We just stopped laughing over that headline... Wait, someone read it out loud again. BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! We'll try to keep some composure long enough to do our jobs and bring you more information as it comes to us.
