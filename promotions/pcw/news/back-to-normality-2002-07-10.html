Back To Normality? Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Jul 2002 ---- DeRidder, Louisiana- Last Friday on Fusion, the recently returned Dark Age hijacked the show and left Tyler Nelson’s foes out cold in their own blood... But on Turmoil would they continue their reign of darkness or would a group rally together to fight them off? In a bloody and brutal main event all was revealed, acts of violence were perpetrated and bones were broken.... Also on Turmoil... The Uprising speak out against Black on Black violence, a Messiah passes away, KFC takes on Voodoo as Kramer Wild Wing and the Rush wage war for the PCW Extreme Title, Allah has his hands full healing his troops, the Cruiserweight battle continues and Xavier Kannon finds a very expensive way of hurting the Pharaoh. To see all that and more, head down to the Turmoil banner and click on Results.
