Boobie Boy #4 Posted by Sandy Wong on 25 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Volume #4 Featuring: Cruiserweight Champion of PCW XAVIER KANNON! Welcome to my 4th issue of 20 Questions. This week the deep painstaking thinking question is answered. What question is that are you asking? Why WHO BETTA THAN KANNON? Obviously its me…ok ok, all kidding aside Boobie Boy sat down with the former NTICWer to talk about the new fed, the title, as well as the ladies. Take a look, you won’t be disappointed! 1) Juan Borro said some rough things about you. He claims that he will win the cruiserweight title back on Sunday…what are your comments in return? Kannon: Well, I think Juan needs to check the archives and look who took that belt off him in the first place. Then maybe on the back of his Portugal shirt he can have the number...0, and above that, where his name usually goes, he can have "Has beaten Kannon this many times"! 2) So if you don’t loose to Juan or “The Messiah” Chris Chase who do you eventually plan on loosing the title to? Kannon: Plan on losing it? The only was this Title is coming off me is if I do an Elvis escapade and go over the 230 limit. 3) So, what exactly is your “official” position at the new NTICW? Kannon: My position is part of a Trio with Timo and Mexi, we take the raw recruits (Or Lemmings as I call them) and tweak their gimmicks and names, give them angles and get them used to a Fed environment. I'll be working with the Mid-Card to get them ready for an NTICW push before they can prepare for a place in the big 2. 4) Are there any stand outs that you see? Kannon: Well, it's still hard to say but personally I am a Tony Tudeski fan. He was in the PWA when I first arrived here and if he didn't take a break I would have pushed for him to get a place in the old NTICW. But now he has finally left the PWA, so it's time see how he copes in a PTC Fed. I'm also a Charlie Loc fan, and am looking at Damian Bekkett to surprise some people with his new gimmick. 5) You, as the same with many of the newer PCWers wrestlers, are a product of PWA…what are your comments on that? Kannon: PWA is the best thing there is for anyone wanting to improve. I am NOT a fan of the Diamond in the Rough, if people really want to make PCW they can go to the Academy (Which El Diablo, Hessian and Boda do an AMAZING job of running). I came into PTC in an Indy fed with an "Angel" type crusader gimmick. Then I hit PWA and became the Scientologist, PWA got my ambiance and storylines sorted. Then NTICW put the finishing touches on me got me used to writing angles and feuds, all of which have helped me in PCW. I think I am one of the best examples of the PTC system in that respect. 6) Who do you feel is your most heated rival? Kannon: No doubt about it, "The Messiah" Chris Chase. Our feud is heading the Cruiserweight Division at the moment, and hopefully we can pull it up above the lack luster TV division with the addition of some other talented Cruisers. But a little warning to them and something Juan will learn, don't get in between Chris Chase and me.... Because all we are going to do is make you unable to worry us as we fight it out for the CW Title. 7) Any plans of forming a stable or joining back up with your own partner THE RUSH…or is Kannon going to show he has balls and stay solo? Kannon: How am I meant to carry around my CW Title AND The Rush at the same time? He could have become a PCW Tag Champion but no, he had to go with his Black Attack buddies and where is he now? To tell you the truth, he's buried so deep in the undercard I can't see him either. As for Stables? Well, too early to tell, if Chase gets the New Breed involved, I may just have to call in some old favors some ex-NTICW guys to even the odds 8) What’s the situation with your relationships? Girlfriend…wife…significant other? (The Name Eleanor is ringing bells in my head) Kannon: Ah, Eleanor Hall, Ellie, my little Poo Puppy. As always she will be by my side, and nothing looks hotter than Ellie only wearing my Cruiserweight Title...Hmmm....In fact losing the Title might be a good idea... *POON! 9) What is your biggest achievement in your wrestling career so far? Kannon: Ah, there are SO many to chose from. It could be wining a Double Jeopardy match for my 1st PTC Indy World Title, or getting a 97 for my first PWA promo, or being Piranha’s class Champion, or being PWA top student 2 weeks running, or winning the NTICW Title in the Tower of Doom, or pinning Drake Maxwell for the NTICW Tag Titles at Redemption...Or, and I will probably go with this, beating Juan for my 1st PCW Gold in my 4th match.... Soon to be replaced with going 1 from 1 and PCW PPV's on Sunday 10) What has been your most embarrassing moment? Kannon: Well, that one would be before Ellie came along, when Iziy, the Pharaoh's wife was my piece of desired ass, and she kinda distracted me...making me lose to the Pharaoh's man servant. However, I did break up the Pharaoh's marriage for a while, so at least something good came out of it. 11) Who do you see beating Jeremy Howard for the World Title and snapping his streak of never losing that title, I say it will be Kid Wonder to do the honors. Kannon: I thought he lost a Title to Tim Walden... that has to show for something. Ah, whatever. To be honest, I don't really care, Timo, Snow and Boda can have their shots, but be it months or even a year, I'll be up there in the PCW Title scene...Then I'll care. 12) What are some of your favorites? (TV Shows, food, music) Kannon: Favorite TV is the Simpson’s, Sopranos, Seinfeld, Sex and the City and of course...Soccer. Food well, I'm a vegetarian, I love Italian, but my favorite food is Cheese and Tomato filled, Onion flavored Bagels. Music, I like Smashing Pumpkins, Placebo, Heather Nova, Ben Fold Five, Jeff Buckley, Moby, Ash, Nelly Furtado, PJ Harvey, Offspring, Beta Band, Mercury Rev...Anything I hear that I like really. 13) If you had a million dollars would you buy me a house? If not what would you do with it? Kannon: I'd blow it all on a flat in the London Docklands and a house back home in the Suffolk Countryside purely for the nighttime views. Burning skied sunsets in Suffolk and towering beautifully lit towers in the Docklands (Looks like a level on Sonic, it's spectacular!) 14) What exactly is scientology? Kannon: Scientology is a belief system founded by Science Fiction writer L Ron Hubbard and most similar to the philosophies of some forms of Buddhism. It is more a set of values on which to structure your life than a religion, more a philosophy on how to make the world better. 15) Any plans of bringing any other scientologists to PCW? Kannon: I am Wrestling's ONLY Scientologist, and I plan to stay that way, I even had it confirmed from my Spiritual heads that there, just like the Highlander, can be only 1. Matt: But haven’t you seen the latest highlander? There are two of them! Kannon: The Highlander "There can be only one" rule should have applied to movies too. 16) Who do you think will win on Sunday, Duke or Tyler, give your reason! Kannon: Duke says he will support all minority religions in PCW so the Church of Scientology has given him their full support...So he will win. 17) If you where stuck on a deserted island and could only bring one thing…what would it be? Kannon: My Cat (Furbinho) or my Disk man (Preferably with CD's!) 18) What do you believe will be the most successful “rookie” federation at PTC? Kannon: Well, I think the Fed that I help run, NTICW, will challenge GWF below PCW and OSW. We know we will lose our best guys to the big 2, but we will also be getting some of PWA's best, and look who has come from there. We have a large bunch of match writers, a team exclusively fro gimmick consulting, and a young hungry roster. In a way, we are the ECW below WWF and WCW, so we'll just have to pull out a few party pieces to get ourselves noticed 19) Why are you so damn ugly? Kannon: Why are you so damn unemployed? Matt: Point Taken 20) And finally, any last comments on myself, the column, or your match Sunday night? Kannon: On the column, I hope this makes up for all Juan's drivvel, on you....Well, Tyler said it all....On Sunday, well, XK's PPV winning streak is set to begin. Oh, I forgot to add that I don't watch RAW, Smackdown, HEAT, Excess or Metal. Only the odd PPV. That’s all for this issue folks! NEXT ISSUE FEATURES PWA’S TEEN IDOL KID WONDER~! Until next time folks…I am Boobie Boy asking you… Can you answer my 20 Questions?
