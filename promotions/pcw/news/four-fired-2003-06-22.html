Four Fired

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Four Fired Posted by Sandy Wong on 22 Jun 2003 ---- PCW, in an effort to increase the effiency of the roster, has made some roster moves, effective as of the airing of Friday Fusion. Neither of the company's vice presidents were available for comment, but a press release was faxed to major dirtsheet sites. -Sergio Bullent has been released, due to lack of overall effort. Officials said that he's too small to be a real force without serious effort and that the drive just wasn't there. -Menolly Xenos, the Brazillian jiu-jitsu master, was released as well. Citing the same problems that plagued Bullent, officials said a lack of effort was apparent. -A.J Trinidad was released after PCW officials failed to come to an agreement on his contract. He had been signed to only a very short agreement, one that ran out this past week and one that PCW chose not to re-new, wanting to lower his wages. They wish him the best of luck in trying to find a job elsewhere. -Shane Robertson, after lackluster "trial" period with PCW, was let go, as PCW wished him best luck elsewhere, as well. In addition to these firings, Shane Wethers was formally placed on the injured roster, after having been hampered and kept out of ring action by a neck injury for almost a month now.
