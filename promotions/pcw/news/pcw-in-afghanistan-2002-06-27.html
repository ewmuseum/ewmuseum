PCW in Afghanistan

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PCW in Afghanistan Posted by Sandy Wong on 27 Jun 2002 ---- As the sun rose this morning in Kabul,Afghanistan word spread that hometown heroes,The Refugee's had been signed by Tyler Nelson and were now part of PCW.At a press conference later that day Hamid Karzai announced that the rumor was indeed true.A national holiday was planned in honor of The Refugee's and Red Cross workers have said that they haven't seen the Afghans happier since the Taliban took control. The Refugee's themselves had nothing to say but many of their fans did. "Ali and Qaboos surely must have been blessed by Allah.Their fathers were great leaders and they received a strict upbringing.They are finally being rewarded and what could be a better way than getting into PCW?They have brought much joy to our people,that joy was taken from us.We were blind but we now see again.Praise be to Allah for this day and we now only pray that they do not fail us," said one fan to our reporters. Afghanistan has had many problems since the demise of the Taliban.Hopefully The Refugee's will bring some happiness back to the country that was once nothing more than a living hell.
