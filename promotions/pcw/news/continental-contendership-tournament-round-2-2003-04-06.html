Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 2

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continental Contendership Tournament... Round 2 Posted by Sandy Wong on 6 Apr 2003 ---- PFIK: Well, El Romantico and Dante Jackson are now going against The Schmoopies. Dante and Chris Storm are starting out. Tiffany: Tieup, Dante gets a headlock. Storm whips him off. Dante with a shoulder block. Back off the ropes, Storm under Dante. Leap frog by Storm. Hiptoss. PFIK: Blocked hiptoss. Chris Storm hits Dante in the midsection, Dante doubled-over. Leg over the head, a backflip. Backdrop suplex. Storm with a leg drop and there's a tag to Tessa Windsor. Tiffany: Tessa comes in with a quick leg drop and a pin. One count. Sleeper Hold from Tessa. Dante stands up and grabs her in the hold for a sideslam. Dante with a senton splash. PFIK: Romantico brought in, the two with a double-team suplex to Tessa Windsor. A pin gets a two count. Romantico brings Tessa to the neutral corner and kicks her in the midsection. A whip out of the corner, Tessa slides to a stop on the mat. Romantico follows in with spinning wheel kick. Duck under. Hurricanrana! 1... 2... Kickout. Tiffany: Tessa with kicks to Romantico's head. A whip is reversed, Tessa sent to the ropes. A back body drop telegraphed. Tessa with the Sunset Flip. Two count. Romantico rolls through and rolls over with a Reverse Sunset Flip. 1... 2... kickout. Romantico gets up and gets forearms in the face. Another whip, Tessa gets reversed and whipped away. Romantico pulls back into a shortarm back elbow. PFIK: Elbow drop onto Tessa, and another two count pin. Romantico tags in Dante Jackson and they get a double spinebuster. A close two count, Chris Storm breaks up the pin. Storm out of the ring, Dante brings Tessa back up for a military press slam. Standing moonsault for a two count. Tiffany: Dante with Tessa back to her feet; in the corner he's just slapping her. Romantico and Storm not taking much liking to that, nor am I. Whipped to the other corner, Tessa follwed by Dante and hits a mule kick. She grabs Dante's head, on the second rope... Diamond Dust! Dante held onto the ropes. Flipover Neck Whip on the seated Tessa. PFIK: Dante pulls Tessa out of the corner. Powerbomb and a pin. 1... 2.... Dante lets up. Dante pulls back up for a piledriver. Another pin. 1.... 2... Dante pulls Tessa back up again. Dante brings Tessa up and lifts her into a high vertical suplex position. Chop block by Chris Storm! Tessa falls onto Dante. 1.... 2.... Kickout! Tiffany: Romantico in the ring now, Chris Storm knocks him to the ropes with a clothesline. Dante with Tessa back up hooked for another suplex. Romantico snap kicks Storm away. Tessa blocks the suplex and knees Dante in the groin! Romantico runs in and executes the Heart Break, but Storm rolls out of the way. Tessa rolls behind Dante. Heart Break to Dante Jackson! Tessa gets Dante over in a School Boy Rollup. Romantico... he's not helping! PFIK: 1.... 2..... 3! Tiffany: Tessa did it! They won! PFIK: The Schmoopies advance in the tournament with a win over Dante Jackson and El Romantico! Tiffany: Dante won't be happy with Romantico. _________________________________ Tiffany: Teresa Tomas and Jade are in the ring, starting off with a test of strength. Tomas gets the better of it, bridges Jade back. Jade comes back up and kicks Tomas's right arm away. Run to the corner, Jade springs off with an arm drag. Tomas rolls to her feet and charges into a drop toehold. Jade brings Tomas to her feet. A whip, reversed. Tomas follows in with a diving shoulder block. Tomas brings Jade to her feet and goes for a Urinage. Elbow to escape, Jade hits a DDT! PFIK: Jade pulls Tomas to her corner quickly and tags in Lacuna Debris for a slingshot leg drop. Pulled up, Tomas gets a double-team single arm DDT. A pin, two count. Lacuna pulls Tomas up to her feet. Snap suplex. Tomas in a rear chinlock. Fighting to her feet. Two back elbows, Tomas pushes Lacuna away. Tomas follows in and leans over for a back body drop. Lacuna kicks her in the shoulder and lunges with a superkick. Tomas ducks and Lacuna turns around into a spinning wheel kick. Tomas takes a moment to rest. Tiffany: Lacuna comes up first, holding her jaw. Tomas brought to her feet, Tomas slaps Lacuna away and unloads with forearm shots. A whip away, Lacuna reverses. Roundhouse kick over the ropes by Jade, hitting Teresa Tomas! Tomas stumbles out into clothesline from Lacuna Debris. Three elbow drops, there's a pin. 1.... 2.... Kickout. PFIK: Lacuna drags Tomas over to her corner and tags in Jade. The two connect with a double-team standing sidekick in the corner. Jade turns over Tomas. 1.... 2.... Kickout. Tag back to Lacuna, Teresa Tomas is pushed into the corner. Up to the second rope. Double-team Superplex! Tiffany: Both women up. Akiyama grabs them by the head; illegally in the ring. PFIK: He bashes their heads together! Tiffany: Again... again... again... PFIK: Grabs them by the throat now. DOUBLE CHOKESLAM! Tiffany: Akiyama's growling. He's pulling Tomas on top of Lacuna Debris. PFIK: Violence Jack is counting it as a pin. Tiffany: 1.... 2.... 3! PFIK: Teresa Tomas and Akiyama are going on in the tournament. Tiffany: Are Rent-A-Hero and Clyde still down in the water? PFIK: I dunno. ________________________________________________________ Tiffany: Why's Dark Gimmick not out here? PFIK: The writer remembered that Mike Kreller retired, so is making proper tribute by not having Dark Gimmick actively involved in the loss. Tiffany: Loss? PFIK: There's another Powerbomb/Neckbreaker combo by Amazon and Naru, folding Banshee into an even smaller two. Tiffany: Ah. PFIK: 1.... 2.... 3. Tiffany: So, those two Slave Geisha are going on in the match. PFIK: At this rate, we can just bring out the next contestants. P.W.A. can come out. Here they are. Tiffany: And coming out riht behind them are The Overweighter and Ellie. PFIK: And as the smarter thing to do for their team, Overweighter is starting in the ring. Gaz Green is in the ring with him. Tiffany: Gaz Green with a palm thrust to the heart, and Overweighter goes reeling back. Gaz does more thrusts to the stomach, and Overweighter seems in pain. PFIK: Odd, given his stomach has its own congressman. Tiffany: Overweighter is stunned, but still on his feet. Gaz runs off of the ropes and charges Overweighter with a... two-handed shove. PFIK: Overweighter's still on his feet. Not sure what he's trying to accomplish. Tiffany: Gaz is calling Nerezza into the ring. Violence Jack is just letting Nerezza walk right in and help Gaz push Overweighter back into the corner. PFIK: But 'Rezz is going back to his corner, so I guess it's alright given the circumstances. Tiffany: Gaz is climbing to the second rope. He's at face-to-chest level with The Overweighter, who stands 7'2" tall and weighs well over 500 pounds. PFIK: Hence the name "Overweighter." Tiffany: There's some ear claps. Why's Overweighter shaking like that? PFIK: The flab is probably still quivering from the walk out here. Tiffany: Gaz has stopped clapping the ears and now he's just holding Overweighter's head in his hands. PFIK: Is this a move? An attack? Tiffany: I don't know. PFIK: Gaz drops off, and he has Nerezza help pull him out of the corner. It's taking effort. He's out, Overweighter is out of the corner and on the ground. Tiffany: There's a pin. PFIK: 1..... 2...... 3! Gaz Green has pinned The Overweighter and knocked he and Ellie out of the tournament. Tiffany: What's Gaz doing? He's throwing something away. PFIK: Those are handbuzzers! Tiffany: Gaz is saying they're shorted out. He's surprised it took do long to bring Overweighter down. PFIK: Now Nerezza is running around the other side of the yacht. Where is he going? Tiffany: I don't know. But P.W.A. continue on in the tournament. What's tha-- the forklift? What're they doing with that? PFIK: Gaz is parting the ropes... they're bringing Overweighter out of the ring! Pretty considerate, moving that weight without help would've been almost impossible. Tiffany: So Overweighter is out of the ring and they're taking him-- to the edge? PFIK: NEREZZA DUMPED OVERWEIGHTER OVER THE SIDE! Tiffany: I can't believe it! Someone get him out of there! PFIK: A sad way to end the second round. All we can do is hope someone can get to the Overweighter in time... hope that he floats........
