A Boobie Girl?

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Boobie Girl? Posted by Sandy Wong on 5 Dec 2002 ---- After that shocking accusation by Mr.UK on last Sunday's Adrenaline, Matt Elia has been in nothing less than a uproar. Although not commenting on it directly he has been surrounding himself by more and more women then ever. In fact, there are even talks that he is considering to bring in a female valet to show everyone that he is not a homosexual. Who and when this Boobie Girl~! would show up are still just rumors but Elia's partner and friend Marvin Harris has been qouted in saying that a female manager for Elia is not out of the question. Marvin: We could use a secretary to help out with all that mail we are getting from all the Kannon-Haters out there. Boobie Boy~! was unable to be reached for comment. Hoewever, PCW is an Equal Opportunity federation and has no problem hiring and serving all forms of interest including Blacks, Hispanics, other forms of ethnicity as well as Homosexuals.
