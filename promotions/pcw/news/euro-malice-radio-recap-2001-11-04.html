Euro Malice Radio Recap

4 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Euro Malice Radio Recap Posted by Sandy Wong on 4 Nov 2001 ---- The debut of Euro Malice, a European version of PCW Radio hosted by Duke Williams, aired for 90 minutes from noon to 1:30 pm London time and was a huge success. Duke opened the show talking about the upcoming Sabotage card, and talked a little about the Boda/ Stanislav match. He then discussed the upcoming shows on Turmoil and Fusion. -The Phantom Booker was the first, and longest caller, as he started off by explaining the concept of the main event at Alliances, then moved to the Diamond in the Rough Tournament. -TPB then gave his thoughts as to who possibly stabbed Judas Steele, and mentioned GREED INC maybe falling apart. -He said that Ivan Stanislav is the right man for the PCW title, and that he could be a champ for a long time. He finished his first call by getting into a discussion with Duke about NYPD Blue, Hill Street Blues, and Dennis Franz. TPB then gave way to the second caller of the night, Badge. -Badge started off by discussing his business, and Duke asked if they were going bankrupt from lack of business. Badge then commented on his relationship with "Management" stating that is was purely business. -Badge then talked about his upcoming match with Kramer Wildwing, and Duke and Badge discussed whether KFC chicken or Cream Soda was better. -Badge said that him leaving GREED INC was the reason for it’s downfall, but Duke disagreed, defending his former stable, and it’s success without Badge. -He then talked about Alliances and gave his opinion as to who he thought stabbed Judas Steele. -Badge finished by saying that as long as Ivan Stanislav and Team CEO keeps paying Thugs For Hire to do their dirty work, Ivan will remain champ. -Boda was the next caller and he and Duke discussed his upcoming match with Ivan Stanislav. Boda, said that he would defeat Ivan even with Team CEO and Thugs 4 Hire watching Ivan’s back. -Boda then discussed Alliances and was surprised to find out that Doug Troy was injured and would not be competing. -Boda mentioned that he was looking for two more people to team with he and Kano at Alliances. -PWA Vice Principal, Young Tiger, then called in and he and Duke discussed the PWA. Young Tiger said that the Paddy Irishman was the best student in the PWA. Duke then asked if Young Tiger was Bubble Boy as suggested by The Phantom Booker. -The next caller was PWA student, The Paddy Irishman. Duke asked Paddy about graduating from the PWA, then busted out in laughter at the Irishman’s thunderous voice. The Phantom Booker told Duke not to laugh because Paddy would lay "Magically Delicious" down on his ass. The Paddy Irishman then closed his comments by saying that he will take a spot in the PCW soon. -The Phantom Booker then called in and continued his shoot on Meanstreak, Boda, and Frisco, as well as Trashcan Man. TPB then commented on NTICW and president Yoder, calling him the right man for the job. -The final caller was "The Messiah" Chris Chase and he and Duke discussed Chase’s nickname “The Messiah” as well as talking about Alliances, and other PCW subjects. -Duke then wrapped up the show thanking those that called in, before ending with a sound byte of Winston Churchill. Tune next Sunday at noon UK time for the second edition of PCW's Euro Malice. Source: Visit the home of PCW Radio!!
