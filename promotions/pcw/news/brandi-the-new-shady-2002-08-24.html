Brandi... The New Shady? Posted by Sandy Wong on 24 Aug 2002 ---- Recently, PCW superstar Brandi Wilson has been working closely with Shady Acres, a foster home agency, to help Mentor children that are underprivileged, or neglected. In Brandi's case, she has been mentoring Amy Reiner, a girl who was neglected by her parents. “Amy Reiner, who is 6, has been a huge fan of Ms. Wilson,” said Ms. Davis. "We feel this would be an excellent way to show her the good that can come out of rolemodels and hard workers.” Brandi has reportedly worked out a deal with PCW which will allow her to take Amy on tour with her for the coming week. "Amy was so excited to see me the first time I met her," said Brandi, "that I thought it would be nice for her to see some of my friends backstage, and give her a time of her life she will always remember. She’s been through so much bad, that it would be great to show her that there are still some good people left in the world." Brandi reportedly picked up Amy at her foster home after Friday Fusion, and has made arrangements for her to watch Turmoil and next weeks Fusion. More news as it becomes available.
