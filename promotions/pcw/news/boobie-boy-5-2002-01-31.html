Boobie Boy #5 Posted by Sandy Wong on 31 Jan 2002 ---- 20 Questions with Boobie Boy Issue#5 Featuring Kid Wonder! Welcome to Issue number five. THIS IS MY FIRST ISSUE UNDER RULE BY DUKE WILLIAMS! This issue also features PCW Teen Idol KID WONDER! He has taken the time to join me and comment on a variety of things such as his PWA demotion, as well as Howard's loss to Snow. So scroll down and check out this issue! 1.. What did you think of Shanghied? I have strange feeling your mighty glad that Tyler is out of power. Wonder: I wish that were the case. While he lost the presidency, he's still commissioner and CEO. But I'm working on ways to get him to lose that, too. 2.. We saw you kick Nelson's Ass on Friday Fusion last week. Should we be expecting more? Wonder: True, I'm not one to give away secrets, let alone my own. Let's just say that if you liked Fusion...you'll LOVE Turmoil Matt: Let's hope this gets posted before that then. 3.. Can you describe your experience in PWA so far? Wonder: It sucks to have to listen to people such as El Diablo and Boda. People that I'm better then. I mean, what am I gonna learn down there? The people in the PWA go there in hopes of becoming the next Kid Wonder or something like that. That might be great for them. But for me, it's just a waste of time. 4.. Being that you're a free agent any chance we might the see the Wonderful one's face on OSW TV or even my former home NTICW? Wonder: NTICW is out of the question. That's no better than the PWA. As for OSW...it's a possibility. It all depends on how much Reagan wants to piss Nelson off. 5.. So, at Shanghied, Jason Snow pulled a major upset over Jeremey Howard. How good of champion do you think Snow will be? Wonder: About as good as Howard was during his first reign. 6.. So last night Jane Regan announced that many PCW superstars will be travelling to OSW. Who do you believe will be going to the new federation? Wonder: I have no idea. Maybe Mexi since he was quick to deny it. And, hey, you never know...I might go over there. 7.. PCW recently has signed an over abundance of superstars to contracts. What do you think of these new guys? Any standouts? Wonder: Stormbringer sticks out because he is quick to kiss my ass. Besides him, not a single one of them stick out. I'm sure that LeBlanc guy will be billed as the 20th coming of Meanstreak or something like that. It's quite sad. When will these newbies get an identity of their own? None of them will ever rise above mediocrity until the new glass ceiling breaks. 8.. Being that you are Kid Wonder, you must be like any kid. What are some video games that you play? Wonder: Anything good. Right now I've been playing a lot of NHL Faceoff on Playstation. 9.. What was your most embarssing momment in your wrestling career? Wonder: Being forced to feud with Johnny Smith. I know it was totally one-sided in my favor, but being forced to do anything with a prima donna douchebag is depressing. 10.. What was your most fufilling or enjoyable moment? Wonder: Kidnapping Tyler Nelson. Besides that, my best PCW moment is yet to come. That'll be when I win the PCW title. 11.. How's Amanda doing? Any wedding bells ringing in the future? Wonder: Amanda's doing fine. We're as happy as ever. As far as getting married goes...that's not gonna happen any time soon. We're both still real young. And being in the wrestling industry, they'll try to turn it into some stupid angle. And I just don't want that. So when we get married, it'll be on our own terms. 12.. I was orignally pushing for you to be the first person to defeat Howard for the World Title. Unfortantely, Snow beat you to it. Who do you believe will be the person to beat Snow? Wonder: Boda. Unless some inpromptu match occurs on Turmoil for the belt, Boda will take it. 13.. What are the odds of Kid Wonder forming a stable with other Tyler elson haters? Wonder: A stable? I could form an entire federation filled with Nelson aters. But I really don't see me being in any stable. Every time I'm in a table, I'm still treated like an outsider. Outcasts. DAMN. I was never accepted by those guys. They just brought me in because I was a name. When Old School formed, my phone never rang once. So I know I'm not accepted by the others in PCW. And with that being the case, I'm not in any hurry to team with them. Matt: Well if ya ever plan on it Boobie Boy will glady be your spokesperson. Wonder: Thanks 14.. It might be early, but any plans for retierment? Wonder: I'm not even gonna answer that. 15.. On the road as you travel, who do you usually travel with? Wonder: I used to travel with Boda every now and again. But these days I don't know if I can trust him. Two knives lodged in my back is enough. I just travel with Amanda. She's great company. 16.. What are some of your favorites? (Food, Music, things to do) Wonder: Favorite food would have to be anything sweet. I've got a mad sweet tooth. And Polly-O string cheese. String cheese and Dr. Pepper. I could live off of that. Have that while listening to some good ol' rock and roll. Some of the newer bands like Tantric and Puddle of Mudd are OK. But I'm more of an old school guy. Pink Floyd. Aerosmith. And almost anything from the 80's and early 90's. And, of course, Sugary Ray. They do give me my music, hehehe. 17.. What is your favorite sport other than wrestling and what is your favorite team? Wonder: Used to be baseball, but that went to hell. So I gotta go with football. And whatever team Trent "All I Do Is Win Football Games" Dilfer is on is the team I root for. 18.. Who are some of your favorite guys back stage? Wonder: I don't talk to many guys in the back. I kinda keep to myself. Keep to my little clique. I could tell you more people that I don't like than I do like. 19.. Who do you believe is the greatest wrestler to ever grace PCW? (Can not include yourself) Wonder: If we're going by raw wrestling talent, it'd be Howard Porter. But if we're going by power, talent, and all the other intangibles, it'd have to be Meanstreak. To be honest, I've never had the real chance to face any of the real "greats" in PCW. But, yeah, I'd say Meanstreak. And since I'm 1-0 against Streak, what's that say about me? 20.. Before I go to the final question, I want to start a new part to my column. I am going to name five different people or places or things(a noun) and I want you to tell me the first thing that pops into your head. WORD ASSOCIATION Cruiser Weight Title: Wonder: Me Tyler Nelson: Wonder: My bitch The Phantom Booker: Wonder: Stocking stuffer NTICW: Wonder: Cure for insomnia Boobie Boy: Wonder: Lucky that mommy didn't catch him with a guy instead of a girl. Now for the final question. Do you have anything further to say about the PWA, Tyler Nelson, Shanghied, my column or myself? Wonder: The PWA sucks. It makes your questions seem good. Nelson's rants are tiresome and have no substance behind them. I want a thank you card for saving this column from cancellation. And make sure you tune in for Turmoil this WEDNESDAY for it will be a night that Tyler Nelson, as well as anyone who watches, will never forget. That is all for this week. Contact me if you are interested in an interview. Starting FEBUARY 1st I am making the column open to anyone in PTC. Remember I am [detail withheld] on AIM or email me at [detail withheld]. Special thanks to Kid Wonder for taking time out of his not so busy PWA schedule to cut an interview with me. On deck for this week is the mighty Miyagawa and Mexi. If neither of them will show than I have back up with Chris Chase. So until than remember wrestling is only a game until Hank starts to get paid 2.4 million.
