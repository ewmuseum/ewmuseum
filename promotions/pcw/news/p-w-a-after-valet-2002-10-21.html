P.W.A. After Valet? Posted by Sandy Wong on 21 Oct 2002 ---- Recently we have seen Pranks With Attitude beaten from one arena to the next by present Tag Team Champions Playaz Inc and in their quest to overcome this stalemate they have even deliberated the idea of taking on a valet. Well catching up with the devious duo they appeared jovial and even hinting towards another chapter in their PCW era. "After I retired-..." said the Jester before a Nerezza interruption. "Don't you mean 'We' retired-..." "Anyway as you saw on Turmoil, we retired a legend in Alexander Miyagawa. Many would think we were stupid to perform such a heinous attack but little did people realise Pranks With Attitude have another agenda installed and the wheels have fate have already began to turn!" continued the Emerald Menace, trying to contain his excitement. "Who's too say that we haven't already aligned ourselves. Who's to say we haven't brought in somebody deserving or from our past, but I'll be damned if Miyagawa takes the credit for the fall of Asylum...." roared the vampire with a hiss. "It's simple Asylum dies when P.W.A say so and not when some has-been can't take the heat of the kitchen!" concluded the Jester as they refused to comment further. So the question is 'What has P.W.A added to their arsenal?' With the team scheduled in a number 1 contenders bout at Tuesday Turmoil, will this weapon in their quest for Tag Team gold make an appearance or is this a swerve to rattle the current champions.
