Semore Smoke Guilty or Innocent?

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Semore Smoke Guilty or Innocent? Posted by Sandy Wong on 10 Aug 2002 ---- Well you heard it here folks, the results from Semore Smoke's trial are in and the judge ruled in favor of Smoke. It seems that the lawyer that PCW appointed for Smoke had everything planned out from the start. The prosecution had such little to go on to prove it's case, and the defendant did, it was pretty much a landslide in Semore Smoke's advantage. When reached earlier today about the case for the first time after the court battle, Semore Smoke had this to say: "I couldn't have done it without the help from my friend Mick Palmer, and my great lawyer, James Macklin. I thought I was dead going in, with just Mick there to show that I had taken steps for rehab, but James pulled through with several pieces of evidence that showed my innocence. I hope they caught that guy who framed me, he's lucky I didn't get to him, if I had he wouldn't be standing right now for sure." Semore Smoke left, as he has to get to the site of PCW Tuesday Turmoil where he'll be facing former NTICW mate, Mark Makonie, in the first round of the PCW Rising Star Title tournament.
