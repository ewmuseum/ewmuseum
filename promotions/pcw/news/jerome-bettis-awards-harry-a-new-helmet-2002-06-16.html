Jerome Bettis awards Harry a new "Helmet"

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jerome Bettis awards Harry a new "Helmet" Posted by Sandy Wong on 16 Jun 2002 ---- When PCW stopped in Pittsburgh for a great live event, Pittsburgh Steeler Jerome Bettis was on hand for this grand event. Backstage Bettis gave "Heavy Metal" Harry a brand new shiny Steeler's Helmet with "Bogus" engraved on the front. Jerome had this to say, "PCW is truly a bad ass federation and Cowabunga Connection is just MAD funny. I had to give my boy Harry his new helmet." Harry was on hand for comment and had this to say, "If I were a woman I'd have mad monkey sex with him, man." In further Football news, the Seattle Seahawks still havn't worked out what shade of blue that their new uniforms are meant to be. And neither have the merchandising people either.
