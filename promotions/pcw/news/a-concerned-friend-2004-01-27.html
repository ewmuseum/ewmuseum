A Concerned Friend

27 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After his match this past Turmoil, Ukyo Matsuura was shook as Koji Machida made a statement to Basho Amuro about an alleged meeting the two of them had backstage before their match, which Koji had officiated. Koji then went on, saying even though Ukyo has no future in Japan and in puroresu, Basho still had a place there, and that plane arrangements would be arranged for Basho Amuro to return home to Japan. This of course left tastes of conspiracy and mistrust in both the fans’ mouths, and most definitely in Ukyo Matsuura’s. Sandy Wong caught up with Ukyo Matsuura at the exit as he started to leave after Turmoil. Basho Amuro was NOT with him. Sandy: Ukyo! Can I have a word? Matsuura sighed, leaning against the wall and giving her a small nod. Sandy: I just gotta ask you what’s going through your mind after what Koji Machida said to your best friend and partner, Basho Amuro at the end of your match! Ukyo: I dunno, Sandy… I know ‘Sho’s homesick. He told me himself. But there’s somethin’ about it that’s just… it not settin’ right with me. If he wanted to leave, I’d try and talk him out of course, but I know for a fact that once ‘Sho sets his mind to somethin’, he’s unstoppable. I seen the guy do it… ran gauntlets back in Japan without sayin’ a word, or losin’ a beat. Why you think he called “Silent Killer”, huh? But if he’s set on going home, I can’t stop him. Sandy: But he’s your best friend, and tag team partner. What will you do then? Ukyo: What can I do, huh? Don’t matter what I do, Japan don’t want me back. But Basho has family over there, a mother and father, and he had a girl he was involved with. I never knew who, but he told me she was just like him, strong, focused, and humble. He raised his eyebrow, his lower lip perking as he thought for a second. Ukyo: A girl Basho… ewww…. He shook it off and continued. Ukyo: Anyways, ‘Sho has people who care about him over there. Me, I just got a mom and older brother who kicked me out the family after leaving puroresu and disrespecting my father’s legacy. He can crawl back to Japan and apologize and all that bullshit… but America… this my home now. The “Hyper-MAX Superstar” represents that USA, doll. Love it, or lick it. He flips his middle finger down to his crotch and smirks, pushing back on the door as he left the building, and left Sandy Wong with a raised brow and slightly agape mouth.
