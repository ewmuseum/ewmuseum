The Butlers (c) v. Detroit Street Thugs

8 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Butlers (c) v. Detroit Street Thugs, FoB Tag Championships Joe — Posted: Oct 8 2005, 10:38 AM From The Igloo in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania: FoB Tag Championships: The Butlers © v. Detroit Street Thugs The Detroit Street Thugs won a #1 contenders match over Future Perfect at Rebirth. Will they be able to keep their undefeated streak this week when they face the current Tag Team Champions? (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) Joe — Posted: Oct 12 2005, 08:09 PM DETROIT STREET THUGS T-BONE AND MOUSE PROMO NUMBA 9: YOU CAN’T SEE T, HIS TIME IS NOW (Our setting is the outside of the Igloo in Pittsburgh Pennsylvania. T-Bone and Mouse sit outside the arena. Both are leaning against the wall, arms crossed, deep in thought about their match tonight.) T-Bone: Ready? (Neither move their head.) Mouse: Yea. You? (T-Bone nods.) T-Bone: Yea. I’m more than ready. (Cut. Inside the arena now. We see the Detroit Street Thugs in a locker room. T-Bone is sitting on a bench, head in hands. Mouse is in the bathroom. T-Bone perks up.) T-Bone: At Sacrifice this week, a destiny will be fulfilled and a road to perfection will be lengthened. My younger brother and I will obtain the Foundation of Brutality Tag Team Championships and only further solidify ourselves as deadly killing machines. We shall continue to become a true threat to the evil force which is our malevolent step-father. A gain of gold will only boost our confidence and motivate us to push and push and push until we can’t move, until we can go no more. And that, that is when the true test begins. Upon completion of our training, we shall move on to the ultimate goal. The destruction of our step-father. Nothing has stopped us on our path yet. And I don’t see anything of significance in our way. Yes, that is a shot at the Butlers. Desmond and Jeffrey. A team we have already crushed once before. A JOKE as the Foundation of Brutality Tag Team Champions. You bring dishonor and disgrace upon those belts. You should be ashamed of yourselves. Come Sacrifice, the Detroit Street Thugs will show you what a true tag team looks like. Two true wrestling forces, coming together as one to obtain one common goal. Victory. Something the Butlers will not have the pleasure of experiencing this Thursday night. (Enter Mouse. He nods at T-Bone, a serious expression on his face. He takes a seat.) T-Bone: Tonight, Butlers, you shall taste my blade. Mouse: That sounds so wrong. T-Bone: What? Mouse: “Taste my blade.” Nigga, please. T-Bone: What do you use that word? You do know that our opponents this week are African-Americans, right? Mouse: You think that changes anything? Nigga, PLEASE. T-Bone: Fine. Say what you will. You’d just better perform when the time comes, and back up those words, or they’ll make you eat them. Mouse: That expression sucks, too. How in the FUCK are they gunna make me eat my words? Hella weak sex. T-Bone: Weak sex? Mouse: It’s my invention. I took gay sex and weak sauce and put them together. It fuckin’ pwns. (T-Bone is left speechless.) T-Bone: … (A pause.) Mouse: Since my bro’ is left speechless. Allow me… (Ahem.) And now I'm feelin' better, 'Cause I found out for sure. She took me to her doctor. And he told me of a cure. (Mouse begins to dance like a maniac.) He said that any love is good love. So I took what I could get! Oooh, oooh she looked at me with big brown eyes. And said… (Mouse turns to the camera and gives it a dark stare.) You ain't seen nothin' yet. (Fade.) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image)
