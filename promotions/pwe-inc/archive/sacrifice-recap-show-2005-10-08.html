Sacrifice Recap Show

Sacrifice Recap Show AlphaGammaSigma — Posted: Oct 8 2005, 03:34 AM Dark Match: Drowneb vs. Novak Before Sacrifice went on the air, Drowneb and Novak battled to a no-contest, when the fight took to the outside of the ring, where both men were counted out. F.O.B. SACRIFICE 10/6/05 Recap Segment ONE - Upon entering the building for Sacrifice, the Marcello family was greeted by the angry stares of the rest of the lockeroom. They nervously, but calmy ventured past the gauntlet of superstars--OOPS, RISK OF LAWSUIT--super-PEOPLE, but were stopped at the end of it, by an angry Headmaster, who insisted that the bandleader, Uncle Shaun follow him. Match ONE: Kid Kash and Frankie Kazarian w/CM Punk vs. Odes and Skitz w/Drowneb After last week's backstage confrontation, both teams were determined to embarrass the other; their captains more than any other member. Unfortunately for the Sonic Death Monkeys, Odes began to complain of embarrassing stomach-cramping before the match actually got underway, so Drowneb was forced to step in, and perform double-duty in order to circumvent a forfeit. Despite this being his second match within a twenty-minute time limit, however, Drowneb fought like a man with a grudge, using his power advantage to keep Kash and Kazarian's momentum down, while Skitz gave him time to recooperate. Skitz finally made the tag to Drowneb, who took control of the match, knocking and keeping the members of Future Perfect down with a barrage of strikes. That is, until "Coach" CM Punk got involved. His distraction allowed the teamwork of Kash and Kazarian to regain the upper-hand from the Sonic Death Monkeys. But it also provoked a response from the "illness-stricken" captain of SDM, who performed a classic leg-trip manuever on Kash, as he was running the ropes. Needless to say, this incited Punk. The remainder of the match was mostly Punk and Odes, trying to outdo each other, without actually doing it in the ring. Repeated incidents of cheating not only confused the ref, and the match participants, but it also caused the tempers of Punk, and Odes to flare. Finally, they had had enough of each other. Both men climbed into the ring, and began to throw down, right in the middle of the match! This led to a four-on-three brawl, when Colt Cabana came running down to the ring. What began as a competitive match, ended in a brouhaha. The ref had tno choice but to throw the match out. Winners: Neither team. Segment TWO - Back in the Headmaster's office, the Headmaster brought up the kidnapping of Desmond Butler, and his brother's beatdown, caliming he knew the Marcellos were responsible. A claim that Uncle Shawn coyly denied. Al promised to find out the truth, and threatened to fire the guilty party, plus have them arrested, and charged for both felonies, of course, assuming that the culprits were right under his nose. Shaun was then let go. Match TWO: Immortal Outlaws, Gifted and Shadow vs. The New Age Outlaws, Roag Dogg and Billy Gunn The New Age Outlaws, Road Dogg and Billy Gunn looked embarrassed themselves, as they were joined by their seemingly hung-over teammates in X-Pac, and Chyna, who repeatedly got in their way during their match with newcomers, The Immortal Outlaws. The veterans, Gunn and Road Dogg were in control, exposing the Immortal Outlaws as a green team. Jesse, and Mr.Ass were all set to put The IMMO in the bunkhouse, when a fight broke out outside the ring, between bitter former lovers, Joanie Laurer, and Shawn Waltman. The Roag Dogg could be seen mouthing "Oh, man. Not again," right before being rolled up for the 1, 2, 3, as Billy was tossed on top of Waltman, who threw a tantrum because Gunn landed on him (due to his drunken stupor, he took it personally). After the match, Waltman had a good cry about it, and they resolved their differences with a brotherly hug. Winners: The Immortal Outlaws Segment THREE - Next, Al called in Anthony Marcello. Like his uncle before him, Anthony denied his family had any involvement in the kidnapping, or the hit. He also denied having any information in regards to the incidents. He was let go. Match THREE: Trey Deep and Randy Orton vs. T-Bone and Mouse. During his ring entrance, Trey Deep looked conflicted, perhaps over whether or not he should even go through with this match, seeing as how he had already been promoted to Underground, earlier on. Somewhere in his deliberation, however, Trey gave in to his ego, and decided to be a good sport, and grace the F.O.B. with one, last "Trey Deep original," as "The Coach" called it. Randy Orton on the other hand, was less than enthusiastic about teaming with a guy who was having doubts. To Trey's dismay, Orton chose to remain at bay, allowing the number-one contenders, The Detroit Street Thugz, to have their way with his tag team partner. Halfway through the match, the crowd began to chant for Orton, as Deep was reaching out to him for a tag. Finally, Orton ran down to the ring to fulfill his obligation. Orton got to the ring just in time to receive the tag from Trey, then poceeding to take back the match, all on his own. Orton was fired up, as was the crowd. He reached back to slap Trey five, only for the Artist Formerly Known as "Carnage" to say his final goodbyes, before walking out on the F.O.B., AND Orton, leaving him to the wolves, just as Orton had done to him in the beginning. WHen Orton turned around, he was T-Boned by T-Bone, who tagged out, allowing his sibling to get the fall with his patented Shooting Star Press. Winners: The Number-one contenders, The Detroit Street Thugz Segment FOUR - After the commercial break, the prodigal son, Angelo Marcello entered the Headmaster's office, accompanied by his father Vicenzo. Al protested Vicenzo's presence, insisting that he get to talk to Angelo alone. Fearing Angelo would walk into a mental trap set by the Headmaster, Vicenzo argued that Angelo needed a translator to get his "sometimes convoluted" thoughts across. Angelo, of course, took offense. Not much, though. The Headmaster wouldn't stand for it, however, and Vicenzo was forced to leave. Match FOUR: Marshall Bourne and Kronik vs. Body Breaker and Mr.Australia In their second outing as a team, the team of Body Breaker and Mr.Australia were NOT as successful, the second time around. Mr.Australia, the man who won the Newcomer Showdown battle royal at "Rebirth," and who returned later that night to nearly win the "Rebirth" match, and the F.O.B. championship, just didn't seem interested. In fact, he looked depressed. He incessantly refused tags, merely by slouching on the top rope. Throughout the match, he appeared to be in some sort of trance. He was of no use to Breaker, who was forced to take on the challenge of the aggression of two men. Not just any two men, but Bourne and Kronik. Breaker held his own against the merciless duo, even achieving a few close calls, or near falls, only for Bourne, or Kronik to interfere on his partner's behalf each time. Still no movement from Australia. In fact, he didn't even appear to be watching the match. His attention didn't seem to be anywhere. Breaker went on without him. After knocking Bourne off of the apron, he gained a short window, and attemtped to finish off Bourne's partner, Kronik with a jacknife, but kronik countered with a backdrop, followed by a spinebuster. Bourne was tagged in, nailing Breaker with his version of the Stunner, "Marshall Law" for the fall. After the match, Body Breaker got in his partner's grill, even going as far as to lay him flat with the powerbomb he failed to achieve on Kronik. Winners: Bourne and Kronik Segment FIVE- Finally alone with Angelo, the Headmaster hit him with question after question, but the dimmest of the Marcello men held firm, denying Al the answers he so desperately sought. In was then that Al realized that--just like his counterparts--Angelo, too, would be a tough nut to crack. MAIN EVENT: FOB Championship: Daniels vs. Garrett After the upset from last week, the F.O.B. champion was determined to prove that that was an accident. That he merely slipped, and nothing more. Indeed, he brought the fight to the challenger, Mark Garrett, who was a little ticked that his opponent, whom he'd beaten only a week earlier, got his name wrong in a promo. But Garrett fought back, with the determination to prove that last week was more than a fling. That he could, in fact, beat Daniels twice. Once for the FOB championship. It was a series of near falls, in a fast-paced, and suspenseful match. But Daniels scored the last run, nailing Garrett with the Angel's Wings, Segment SIX - Angelo returned to his family in their lockeroom. The door was closed behind him. When asked what happened between he, and the Headmaster, Angelo proudly replied "Nothing" with a big smile, to which they angrily replied, "What the Hell did you tell him". Angelo denied telling the Headmaster where Desmond was, or even that they were involved, or that it was Trinity that put the hit out on The Butlers. Seeing the emotion in Angelo's response, the other members of the Marcello clan sighed with relief. They gathered their things, and opened the door to leave, only to find the Headmaster standing in fron of the doorway, much to their surprise. Al smiled at the Marcello foursome, and thanked Angelo for his testimony, just before the show ended.
