But it was The Regulator who was pinned in the Triple Threat Main Event at Retribution. How will Shelton manage when he takes on Homicide once again, with a shot at the gold on the line? Oct. 11, 2004. Fade in on a wide-eyed Brock Lesnar in sweat, and gym clothes. His attention seems to be focused on something ahead of him. Whatever it is, it seems to have Brock in state of amazement. While he stares in silence, we hear the voices of Revenge announcers: Tony Schiavone, and Tazz. TAZZ: There is it! The flying lariat Homicide promised to beat Viscera with! TONY: Did you see that lariat, Tazz? I think Homicide killed him!! It's Revenge. Specifically, the confrontation between PWE Television champion, Homicide, and Viscera. Brock continues to look on in a stupor, as the diminutive TV champ seems to have his way with the MASSIVE "Love Machine," Viscera. Lesnar's mouth--which had just been hanging open a second ago--begins to move. Brock Lesnar - Now is it me, or did Viscera just turn into am overwhelmingly big SISSY? The camera pans over to a wide-eyed Shelton Benjamin, who is easily equally, if not MORE perplexed. Shelton Benjamin - Trust me. It isn't you. The duo watch on in FURTHER disbelief, as the lighter Homicide carries Viscera ON HIS BACK, before heading to the top rope. Brock Lesnar - And I thought MY power was freakish... But how the FUCK did that midget get Viscera to even budge for 'im? We watched those ROH tapes together. Homicide could barely lift Samoa Joe for a fucking suplex, yet, he can carry VISCERA--ON HIS BACK?! It just doesn't add up. I mean, it'd be one thing if he was hitting the gym as hard as me, with the physique and strength to show for it. But he doesn't have the physique to show for it, so I can't believe that--just by looking at him--he trains AT ALL, let alone, as hard as I do. So, what gives? Did the runt get lucky, or is he just on some kind of bogus prescription? Are we in the Bizarro world, not? If I try to pick up one of these dumbbells, am I going to break an arm or something? What the fu-- Shelton Benjamin - I don't know what's up with that, but ENOUGH marveling over the "Notorious 1-8-7" for one day. Correction, for one YEAR. Brock Lesnar - What's the matter with you? Shelton Benjamin - Nothing... except ever since Homicide pinned Reggie for that title at "Retribution," that's all I ever hear about, is Homicide. He's the talk of the motherfuckin' town. From the moment the match was over, it was all-eyes-on-him. It wasn't enough to congratulate him, no. But now, everbody's praising him for being the one to unseat The Regulator, like he did it all on his own. If that weren't bad enough, now he's on the cover of magazines! They're talking about having him do TV appearances, now. And dare I saym there are even rumors that he has movie deals! Him, and his Prodigal-douche bag brothers. They're hogging all the spotlight from the rest of us, but that's not my immediate concern. Just ONCE, I would like to hear someone, anyone praise me for stepping up, and taking back my career. Just once, it'd be nice to hear someone compliment me on going toe-to toe-to toe with the former, and current World Television champions, at the same time. Shelton Benjamin came back from obscurity, to headline a pay-per-view, and came this close to being the one to unseat The Regulator himself! But you don't hear ANYBODY talking about that, do you? No, after that match was said and done, I didn't get one mention. Not a SINGLE mention. Brock Lesnar - And, I take it that pisses you off? Shelton Benjamin - More than that little cock-strut of his (Pointing his chin at the television)! Well... maybe not more, but close. Brock Lesnar - So, that man is stealing your livelihood. What are you gonna do about it? You gonna sit here, and cry all week? Shelton Benjamin - No. I'm going to do what I should have done the first time we met. Brock Lesnar - And what's that? Shelton Benjamin - WIN! I have another title shot lined up if I win this thing. NOTHING's gonna take my mind off of beating Homicide, this week. Not--a--thing. Suddenly, a distraught Trinity enters the weight room. With tears in her eyes, she runs right to Shelton, who braces himself for an immediate collision, and some sudden consoling. Trinity - Shelton, Shelton, it's Chris! Shelton Benjamin - What, what about Chris? Trinity - (Mumbling) IhawChriswithano'erwmn. Shelton Benjamin - Wait a minute, you WHAT?! Speak up. Trinity - I said... I SAW CHRIS WITH ANOTHER WOMAN! Brock and Shelton exchange looks. In Shelton's, there is actual concern. Cut. Later. "Okay, round three. Here goes nothing." Shelton picks up a sheet of paper; a self-prepared summary of their detailed history, and begins to read. Shelton Benjamin - September 17th, 2005. Homicide promises to make an example out of Shelton Benjamin, to his fearless opponent, The Regulator, claiming that he was going to put the hurt on Benjamin, to give The Regulator a taste of what he would be getting at "Retribution." Only, Shelton proved to be a bigger nuisance to the cool-headed title contender's plans, than even he anticipated, by going blow-for-blow with his diminutive foe, and striking back at Homicide with equal, or greater vigor. Realizing that he had his hands full, and that the likelihood of him injuring Benjamin seriously was becoming less, and less likely, Homicide flash-pinned Benjamin, choosing to abort his mission, and escape unharmed, rather than risk getting hurt, or worn out before his title match, by trying to prove something to his adversary indirectly. September 25th, 2005. PWE Revenge Presents: "Retribution". Despite the setback, Homicide goes on to defeat The Regulator, AND Benjamin at "Retribution," and subjugate The Regulator's claims--that a man like Homicide could never, WOULD never defeat a man like The Regulator for the World Television title. In beating The Regulator, however, not only did Homicide end his monumental title reign, and not only did he acquire the World Television championship for himself, but he also painted a bullseye on his chest. A bullseye that men like Marty Jannetty, and Sting were the first to take shots at. October 15th, 2005. A day shy of a full month since their first encounter, Shelton Benjamin takes on his rival in a "Quest for Gold" match, with his second World Television title opportunity hanging in the balance. Will Shelton finally make good on his promises, and seize this opportunity, the way he's sworn to do to others in the past? Or will Homicide not only fail to keep the promise he made to Benjamin, but suffer his first defeat since becoming World Television champion? The answer to both questions, is yes. Yes, I will make good on my word, and beat you cleanly in the middle of the ring, this Saturday, Homicide. And yes, you will fail to meet that standard. You will fail to beat me decisively, by suffering your first defeat since becoming World Television champion, to me. Ever since that faithful night in Philly, I've been looking forward to paying you back for that first loss, Homicide. But now, after watching you bask in the glory, and the hysteria that used to belong to The Regulator, it's not enough to merely flash-pin you for a victory, the way you did me. Hell, after nearly getting my neck broken at "Retribution"--first in the triple-threat match, when Reggie "folded me like an accordion"--then, after the match, when Sting--my formal idol--added insult to injury, by giving me the Stinger Death Drop--it's not enough to merely beat my opponents anymore. I worked hard to bring recognition back to my career, but once "Retribution" was over, it was back to square. It was as if my efforts, or the fact that I nearly got my neck broken, never even happened. And I owe it all to almost winning that triple-threat match. If it's one word I'm tired of hearing, it's "Almost". "Almost" has become the bane of Alpha Gamma Sigma. Well, allow me to be the first to say that the Alpha curse is over. Better yet, allow me to be the one to lift the curse from our heads. This Saturday, it's time Shelton Benjamin got back to what Shelton Benjamin does best, and that's wearing motherfuckers out. Congratulations, "D". No, not for becoming World Television champion, or even for being the one to end The Regulator's notorious "Title reign of DOOM". But congratulations in advance, for being the first person that I stretch. Back in the gym, Shelton removes Trinity's tear-soaked face from his tear-soaked tank top, holding her by the arms out in front of him. Shelton Benjamin Hey, hey. Stop your crying, alright? I'll talk to Chris, first thing tomorrow. The three of us... and Brock, will work this out then, alright? Trinity - (Voice breaking) Al-ri-ight. Shelton hands her a fresh towel. He, and Brock escort her off of the premises.
