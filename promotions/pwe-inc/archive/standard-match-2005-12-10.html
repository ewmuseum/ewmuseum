Standard Match, John Bradshaw Layfield vs Steve Austin Mikel — Posted: Dec 10 2005, 10:58 PM 'End of An Era III' Standard Match John Bradshaw Layfield vs Steve Austin History: Going Cold Turkey has been rough on Steve Austin, even more so now that the holiday season is upon us. John Bradshaw Layfield seems set and determined to "stuff" this turkey and lay claim to being one of the top contenders for the returning 'TPG Heavyweight Championship', however Steve Austin may have a few things to say about that matter, as he too wouldn't mind a shot at the gold. (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) Jacobs V1 — Posted: Dec 11 2005, 04:48 PM A Wrestling God Production Chapter 1: Steve Austin's last match ------------------------------------------- (Fade in) (The camera opens up to a man sitting on a couch talking into his phone. The man is John Bradshaw Layfield. Layfield has on regular jeans and a sweater with “New York” written on it.) JBL: What do you mean they backed out? (JBL shakes his head.) JBL: That's a pile of horse shit. Did you tell them I'll sue? (JBL has a confused look on his face.) JBL: Well, call my lawyer, they can't do this for me. (JBL nods his head,) JBL: Call me back once you get my lawyer and or an update. (JBL hangs up the phone, sighs, and looks into the camera.) JBL: Some people don't have any clue when it comes to business, do they? I think you know why I wanted this camera here. I think you know what I need to address. Last night, from the Sweeten Dome, live in front of millions of JBL fans, I competed in a wrestling competition. But, I didn't just have any opponent. I had 'The Animal' Batista, one of the strongest and dominating wrestlers in the world. And I pinned him one, two, three. It was a thing of beauty. The whole match I was letting him have control to make him think he had control. And when he thought he was going to end it, the Clothesline from Hell picked up the easy win. That is just one of the examples of in-ring smarts I have, but yet, the fans booed me after the match. I did nothing to insult them, I just went out and beat the hell out of that pee-brained gorilla, and they cheered for HIM? I'm beginning to think that The Proving Ground's fans are as dumb as those WWE fans. They root for the underdog I guess. Because they rooted for Batista, but I toned that out. Just wait and see, pretty soon I am going to be the one they all cheer for. It's just the matter of when the PWE fans are going to begin to cheer for a Wrestling God! (JBL leans back against his couch for a moment, and gets straight again, becoming more serious then before.) JBL: And now, they are putting Steve Austin in the ring with me? Are you joking? Listen, Steve. I know we have never been friends, you are more of a redneck drunk, excuse me, former drunk. I am a smooth business man, so why fight? How about you just don't show up for the show? It will save YOU a lot of embarrassment and it will save ME my energy for when I have to get into the ring with an actual challenge. Steve, I'm going to say the same thing I said to Dave Batista. You are not in my league. Maybe ten years ago you would fit into that category. I don't care if you've stopped drinking beer. I don't care if you re-named the Stunner. I don't care if you're friends with that redneck fat-ass JR. You are stepping into the ring with the BEST wrestler in the world today. You are stepping into the ring with John Bradshaw Layfield. (JBL lets out a small laugh and looks around the room, and back into the camera.) JBL: We're not so different I guess. We both know politics, well, I guess I do. We're both good in the ring, well, I'm good in the ring. I guess we're not the same. We both we're born in Texas, but here's the difference: I changed. I am now a wealthy, classy, business man who can still take names and kick ass in that ring. You are still a Texas...turkey eating....redneck moron. I'm going to expose you. Go back to drinking beer Steve. (JBL stands up, and has a look of disgust and anger.) JBL: Go back to drinking beer Steve. I'm going to destroy your dreams of being champion. North American Champion, International Champion, and especially The Proving Ground's Heavyweight Championship. I am going to take you, and beat any talent you still have out of you. And when the bell rings, and my hand is raised in victory, you will know that it's time for you to retire. The people are going to ask 'Steve, why did you retire?'. And you can say that your career was ended................ ......by A WRESTLING GOD! (JBL walks away as the camera fades.)
