To Be a Champion, What price is really worth it? reasenash — Posted: Oct 11 2005, 04:30 PM I wanted to write something different. What I wanted to do today was sit here and gather my information to finish up the next installment of "The Making of a Legend." But the truth is that I just can't do that right now. At least for today, my heart is not in that. Ever since I returned home Sunday evening after a weekend away, something has been bothering me. I tried to let it go. I tried not to be upset about it. But in the end, the truth is that is still just makes me angry whenever I think about it. So here we are. If it is okay with everyone, I am going to write this Column as Rease the person, not as Rease the Underground Owner. I also want it to be known that what I am writing here is of my own accord. Jeff, Tea, or no one else has any clue about the things I have to say here. Like I have warned you before, what I have to say is merely my opinion. You can love it or hate it, that is up to you. I told everyone a while ago that I was going to call 'em like I see 'em, and that is what this is all about. If anyone is offended by what I have to say, then I offer my apology. That is unless you take offense because you know what I am saying is true. If that is the case, then maybe you need to be a little more worried about your actions than you are about how I feel about them. Sunday evening when I returned home I got online to take a look at Underground. That is my job now, and I take it seriously. When I opened one RP thread in particular, I noticed something that seemed a bit odd to me. Jeff had been out numbered in RPs. Not to say that people have not tried to flood Jeff before, but it is not like him to allow them to get away with it. So just to satisfy my curiosity, I IMed Jeff to ask him what happened. When I heard (or read, as the case may be) his answer, I saw stars. I could not believe what I had just learned. Dozer had reached a loose RP agreement with Jeff, and then went back on it. Now let's get one thing straight right now. Jeff knows that Agreement do not exits on Underground. He is okay with that, as am I, But that is not what this is all about. You see, it was not all that long ago that Jeff was diagnosed with Cancer, and he has been undergoing treatment for it. That is why his RPs haven't been as frequent, and that is why he asked Tea to make a special provision for him this week. It was not just to sit back and hav an easy week. It was because he knew that he had Chemo at the beginning of the week, and then was going to have to face it again on Saturday. What he was asking for was not so much an agreement, but a courtesy between friends. OK, I know that according to the rules of Underground, RPing agreements do not have to be honored. I understand that. But this was so much more than just breaking an agreement. This was flat out taking advantage of someone's situation. This was hitting below the belt. And this all came from who? From someone who Jeff considered to be his friend. Lets face it, under any other circumstances, I would not care about an RP agreement being broken on Underground. Like I said, we don't honor them. But this is different. This someone opening up to another human and say I" physically will not be able to RP at the end of the week. I have to undergo treatments for a very real and serious medical condition. Could we keep it on a level field this week since I have to do all of that." His friend then replies to him "We can do two a piece this week." Jeff lets it go, knowing that his "friend" would never take advantage of his medical issue. But he does. He waits until he knows that Jeff cannot respond, and he takes full advantage. To me, at that point it is more than breaking an agreement. It is stabbing a friend in the back. And no matter what excuse you offer, no matter how you slice it, it is wrong. It is a chicken shit thing to do. If I were Jeff, it is the fact that this came from a friend that would upset me the most. If it was someone I did not have a history with it would be no big deal. But that just isn't the case. But I am not Jeff. I am just me. I am an Underground owner, and what made it worse for me was knowing in my heart that thanks to the no agreement rule, If I wanted to remain impartial and fair as an owner, I had no choice but to cast my vote for Dozer to win the match. I had no choice but to help hand him the Underground world title. The truth of the matter is that he won it. No one can deny that. But the issue of HOW he won it will forever stick out in my mind as a glaring example of bad sportsmanship. The truth is that Dozer is the champion. I will always treat his character with fairness and dignity. I may not like how he does things, but I can't change them, and as long as he is technically within the rules of Underground, I won't let my view of his methods influence how I vote on his matches. In my heart I just wish for a Champion that conducts himself with honor, dignity, and sportsmanship. Champions like Dylann with Blue Lightning and Honest with Shawn Michaels. Even Jeff with Chaos. Those are men that know how to conduct themselves as a Champion. Those are men who knew that it really meant to be the flag bearer for a company. They knew that when you hold a belt, your job is not simply to defend it, your job is to set the example for every other role player in the fed. They walked with their heads high because they deserved to. Love them or hate them, they were champions that in all honesty every other role player could look up to. In all reality, Tea could one day become that kind of champion with Dozer, but the truth is that the way he won might very well always overshadow anything else he ever does as a champion, at least in my eyes. But that is up to him. That is up to how he wants to be remembered. Does he want to be remembered as the man who stabbed a friend in the back to get ahead, or does he want to be remembered as a true Dignified, honorable champion. Now is when we all get to find out, It is all up to you now, Tea. You can either stand up and win back the respect that you lost form a lot of other people with your actions this week, or you can keep doing what you have to in order to get ahead no matter who you have to step on along the way. I am not saying that you have to honor agreements. Like I said, we have no rule for that. All I am saying is that you can break all the agreements you want to, but when you take advantage of someone that has called you a friend for so long, you take it a step too far. And you have to ask yourself, Is that title really worth losing a friendship over. Is it really worth the respect that you have lost from fellow RPers. If it is, then so be it. But, if its not, then how are you going to win that respect and friendship back? First of all, Rease, I respect the fact that you said something about what happened, and without completely insulting somebody about it. I have a pretty mixed reaction on this whole thing. But first off, I just have to say...I've said it from the very beginning, Underground should have the same RPing agreement rule as Revenge did, plain and simple. This is an e-fed, but it's different from a lot of others...I think a lot of the people on here are a lot closer, so it shouldn't be geared more towards "you're here to win, and that's that", you know? If an RPing agreement is MADE, it should be kept, and if it isn't, then the person who broke it faces the consequences. To some people (myself included), friendship sometimes is just as important as winning a match. So, with that said, I don't think it's really fair to get mad at TEA!! for what he did (in the sense of breaking the agreement, NOT in the circumstances it happened in...but just him breaking the agreement), although I'm against that. So yeah, in a way, I'm against what TEA!! did, because I feel RPing agreements are one of the best things an E-fed can have, and if I were to break an agreement just to win a match, I wouldn't feel like I had earned it as much...because to me there's no point in winning by quantity, I like to judge my skill with somebody else's on quality. That was the main reason I was IN PWE, was to see how good my writing skills were, and to help improve them, not by overflowing my opponent with them. As much as I disagree with what TEA!! did (especially because of the whole thing with Jeff)...in a sense, you almost can't blame him. Hear me out on this. Jeff, you're obviously a great RPer, don't get me wrong...but I read the RPs with your match with Tim the other week, and I felt Tim did better (not by a LOT, but enough that I definitely think he should've won). Again, this isn't an insult toward you, we all have our good and bad weeks, and I think Tim was really on his game that week, and did some of the best RPs I've seen from him. And I'm not trying to make this about me, but I've felt like I've been screwed a few times in my PWE career, which is part of the reason I'm no longer part of it (RPing, at least). As much as it's "just an E-fed", when you put a lot of time into something, and you don't succeed, when you KNOW you should've...it just isn't right. If you do better, you should win, and that's all there is to it. Storylines shouldn't be taken into consideration, it should be 100% based on who did better. And when Tim lost, I really felt bad for him, because I knew he was trying to do something a little different, and he had a shot at something big. Not only do I think HE thought he did better, but I think a lot of others thought he did too, and to lose in a situation like that really sucks. The point I'm getting at here touches on what TEA!! said in the RPing thread with Jeff, that Tim couldn't beat Jeff on even RPs, when he should've. TEA!! was put in an extremely tough situation. He could either stick with his agreement, "be a good friend", and basically GUARANTEE that he'd lose a WORLD TITLE SHOT...or he could go back on the agreement and prevent that from happening. I'd hate to have been in the situation he was put in, because when you have a shot that big, you obviously don't wanna lose. Let's face it, if they stuck to the agreement (from what it seems), Jeff would've almost 100% guaranteed, retained his title. So, TEA!! picked one and went with it...and frankly, the one he picked was probably the harder one to do. As wrong as some of you may think it was, I bet there's a few people on here who would've done the same. Like I said, I don't AGREE with TEA!!'s decision, but because of what happened with Tim, I completely understand why he did it, and I respect the fact that he had the balls to do it and not give a shit what anybody thought. In an E-fed with such tight friendships and people who definitely wouldn't think very highly of what he did, he swallowed it and did what he thought was the right thing to do. The end result...well, I don't think either TEA!! or Jeff should be very mad about it. I think there should be a re-match between the two to decide the way it really should end up, and knowing the situation both Jeff and TEA!! were in, I guess try and forget about this whole thing...because they were both in very difficult situations. I also don't think either of you 2 (or Rease for voicing his opinion on this) should be looked at as less of a person for this whole thing.
