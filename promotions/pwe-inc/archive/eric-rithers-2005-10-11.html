A few sets of pyros erupt as he walks down the ramp, slapping hands with his fans. He hops up to the center of the apron. He hops over the top rope and goes to a turnbuckle. He poses similar to Christian. Rithers backflips off and then does the same routine on the opposite turnbuckle. Rithers Rack (Torture Rack) 2. Electricity Wave (Springboard Tornado DDT) Background: Eric Rithers has the name “Electifying” for a reason. He is known for pulling off jaw dropping moves and making the crowd want more. He has a devastating finisher titled the “Electrical Shock”, a devistating flip stunner. And for the high risk fans, he uses a corkscrew moonsault he names “High Voltage”. The world better get ready for Eric Rithers because he truly is Electifying.
