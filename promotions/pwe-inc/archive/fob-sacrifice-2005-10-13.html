Match Two – Newcomer Showdown: Chris Jericho v. Randy Orton In a very fast paced match, both Jericho and Orton went back and forth with a wide variety of high impact moves. Orton continuously went for the RKO, which was reversed every time he tried to execute the move. Jericho was able to repeatedly push Orton off before he could land the RKO. Eventually, Jericho was able to hit the lionsault for a 2 count. He picked up Orton and went for the Bulldog, but Orton pushes Jericho off of him and into the ropes. Jericho came back into the RKO position, but he was then able to push Orton into the ropes. Orton came back into an ensiguri from Jericho, which was followed by the Walls of Jericho, which resulted in the tapping of Randy Orton. Coach: And there it is, Joey. Chris Jericho has lived up to his word here tonight. What an amazing display of skill from the FoB newcomer. This guy is going places, Joey. Mark my words. Styles: A great debut here from Chris Jericho, Coach. No doubt about it. (We see Chris Jericho climb the turnbuckle and raise his arms into the air in victory as the crowd continues to boo him.
