A Belated 'Sacrifice'

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Belated 'Sacrifice', Part One AlphaGammaSigma — Posted: Aug 17 2005, 02:04 AM F.O.B. Sacrifice From The Pyramid, in Memphis Tennessee Opening Segment - Sonic Death Monkeys, IN DA' HOUSE! Fade in. Headmaster Al, and the Sonic Death Monkeys are spotted by an exit, backstage. Headmaster Al - You boys don't worry about The Acolytes. You can accompany J.P. to his match tonight, and then, you have the rest of the night off. You boys have earned one. Odes - Thank you for coming around, oh, honorable Headmaster. Headmaster Al - No problem. Just make sure you're out of here a.s.a.p.. Suddenly... "THERE THEY ARE. LET'S GET 'EM" Ron Simmons and Bradshaw attack from across the hall, but Al is quick to push the Monkeys out the door. By the time The Acolytes arrive on the scene, it's too late for a reprisal... against the Monkeys, anyway. Bradshaw grabs the Headmaster. Bradshaw - What in the Hell do you think you are doing?! Simmons - Bradshaw, let 'im go! Headmaster Al - For your sake, you'd better listen to your partner! Simmons - Bradshaw, put the man down! Bradshaw releases the Headmaster with a sneer. Simmons - For cryin' out loud! Simmons distances Bradshaw from the Headmaster... then, grabs Al HIMSELF! Simmons - What's the big idea, helping those fools beat us in the six-man tag match, last week? Have you lost your damn mind?! Bradshaw taps Ron on the shoulder, and Simmons turns his head. Simmons - What? Bradshaw shrugs, with a scowl on. Simmons - Oh, right. Simmons releases the Headmaster. Headmaster Al - (readjusting his clothes) Thank you, Bradshaw. Bradshaw bucks in Al's face. Bradshaw - You, shut up! You've been playing with us! Headmaster Al - It's not what you think. Brashaw bucks at Al, again. Bradshaw - Bullshit! You're helping those punks. We're living proof! Al bucks back this time. Headmaster Al - It's NOT... WHAT... YOU... THINK! Now, back off! Simmons - Okay, okay, okay, okay. Now, hold it, you two. Now, it's obvious that there's something seriously foul going on here, "Headmaster". What we want to know is, who's REALLY in control of your domain? Is it you? Or is it those damn Monkeys? Al takes a second to catch his breathe. Headmaster Al - It's me. It's me, and it has been all along. Bradshaw - If you say so... Headmaster Al - Keep it up, Mister. You are THIS CLOSE to taking the night off yourself! Bradshaw - Bring it on, Headmaster Chimp! Simmons - Now, hold it, hold it, hold it, hold it, hold it. (To Bradshaw) Cool it, cat. I want answers, not to wrestle by myself tonight. Simmons returns his attention to the front, and center. Simmons - Now, what the Hell is going on here? "Step Aside" By The Hiss Real love... Cannot be mea-sured... By the sceptors... or the crowns of the kings of men. Real love... and not the song of the black-bird... Flying backwards... from the window of your neighbor's house. THE BLOOD, AND TEARS I'VE CRIED... COULD FILL THE OCEAN TWIIIIIIIIIIICE! OR, STANDING ALL ALONE... WHILE THE TIDE IS GET-TING HIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGH! Well, if your heart's not in it... ...YOU NEED TO STEP ASIIIIIIIDE!! (Melody) THE BLOOD, AND TEARS I'VE CRIED... COULD FILL THE OCEAN TWIIIIIIIIIIICE! STANDING ALL ALONE... WHILE THE TIDE IS GET-TING HIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGH! Cause if your heart's not in it... (Beat solo) Cause if your heart's not in it... ...YOU NEED TO STEP ASIIIIIIIDE!! The song winds down, and plays itself out. Segment Two - Family Business Close-up of a door-mounted placard. The name on the card, in the placeholder, is "Marcello," and underneath it is a big, red star. Inside the lockeroom, Angelo Marcello prepares for his first match ever in Pro Wrestling Entertainment, throwing a few bones into the air, as his family--present, of course--offer not so much "advice," as they do pressure. Vicenzo Marcello - Now, remember Angelo. The family reputation is riding on you tonight, and our reputation means everything. To the family... to the business. Angelo continues "throwing bones" as he follows along. Anthony Marcello - Yeah, with that said... don't f*** up, tonight! The boyish Angelo just shrugs, and smiles at his cousin, and his disdain. Perhaps, not even realizing that he's been disrespected? Angelo Marcello - C'moooon. When have I ever failed you guys before, eh? The room becomes mournfully quiet, with Shaun, Vicenzo, and Anthony all trading glances. Angelo searches each of their faces for a single objection. There are none. Angelo Marcello - Yeah, see? I told ya' so! Angelo grabs his hat, and heads for the ring. Commercial. Match One - Angelo Marcello vs. J.P. Ruthless Coach: Well, this next match pits the lovable "Sonic Death Monkey In Training" J.P. Ruthless against the Debuting Angelo Marcello, one of the men responsible for the asbolute obliteration of the Bodacious Boys. Joey: Oh How I miss Nikolai. Coach: I'd say it's about time to find out what these boys can do when they're not ganging up on people wouldn't you say? [The Only by Static X busts out over the PA as J.P. Ruthless makes his way out with Drowneb, and Skitz, who are looking over their shoulders. The Crowd gives a mixed reaction as J.P. salutes them, the mob realizing that the Monkeys are the lesser of two evils in this contest.] Coach: And there they are folks, the most dominant force in F.O.B. today, THE SONIC DEATH MONKEYS! WOOO! Joey: Yippee. Excitement about. Coach: There is no need to be sarcastic. Joey: Well I just don't know what you see in these guys. [Just then a chorus of boo's erupts as the Marcello's make their way down to the ring, Angelo leading the pack in his wrestling attire with Vincenzo, Anthony and Uncle Shaun look well dressed as always. Angelo steps into the ring staring down J.P. with a sadistic grin on his face...when suddenly...] Joey: OH MY GOD! [We cut back to the outside where Anthony and Shaun have seemingly leveled Drowneb and Skitz with some sort of foreign object.] Coach: Well it seems we won't have to worry about outside interference on the part of the Monkeys. [This leaves J.P. distracted allowing Angelo to rush in with a running knee to abdomen, knocking Ruthless back into the corner] Joey: And this match is on the way! [Angelo lifts Ruthless up by the hair, landing blistering rights to the face of the young man, he postures to the crowd as they boo him before landing another right, followed by another, before throwing him out of the corner and face first onto the mat.] Coach: An impressive showing by Angelo as he leaves his Monkey face down in the ring, while his family takes care of the rest on the outside! [Angelo stalks Ruthless as he gets to his knees before running up behind him and driving his face into the mat repeatedly] Joey: Well ladies and gentlemen, this is NOT going to be Hart v. Smith in '92 that's for sure. Marcello is seemingly beating the life out of Ruthless without a measure of wrestling. Coach: Don't fix what isn't broken. [Ruthless has now been split open, the assault on his head too much for the skin to handle, blood pours from what seems to be a cut on the forehead and a busted lip.] Joey: What exactly is it going to take to STOP these guys?! [Angelo finally lifts Ruthless to his feet and delivers a VICIOUS powerbomb...but the grin on his face tells us he's not done just yet, he delivers another...and another...and another, before FINALLY covering the broken man in the ring.] Joey: This one has just gotten out of hand. [The Referee gets in postion and makes the count 1 2 3] Coach: And there's a win for the Marcello family in their debut, good lord, I don't even want to see what's left of my poor Monkeys. Joey: Well an impressive win to say the least, I'm sure that Headmaster Al has to be pretty pleased with his new charges. [The crowd boo's heavily as Angelo raises his hands in the air, smearing J.P.'s blood across his chest as well. As the Marcello's leave, Drowneb and Skitz rush to retrieve J.P., and get out. As they help him to his feet, the crowd begins to roar, as Simmons and Bradshaw make a mad-dash for the ring. Drowneb and Skitz drop J.P., and split up, exiting the ring on opposite sides.] Joey: The Monkeys have left J.P. Ruthless, and are hightailing it! [Bradshaw points to Drowneb, with J.P.'s head tucked between his legs. Bradshaw lifts J.P. up the same way Angelo Marcello did, just before he leveled him with a... ] Joey: POWERBOMB! Coach: Poor, J.P.. That's two for him in one night! Joey: And the Monkeys are powerless to do anything about it. Segment Three - "I hate to say I told ya' so, but..." Close-up of the giant smile on the face of Angelo Marcello. Angelo Marcello - I TOLD YA' SO! Uncle Shaun pats his jubilant nephew on the back, walking on ahead. Angelo Marcello - Pa? Vicenzo thinks about it first, but eventually caves in, giving his boy a pat on the back, along with his due. Vicenzo Marcello - Good job, son. Vicenzo moves ahead, smiling. That leaves one. The doubtful Anthony. Angelo Marcello - Cousin Anthony? Anthony also hesitates, before giving Angelo a pat on the back. Anthony Marcello - Good job, Angelo. Angelo Marcello - Thank you, cuz. Angelo joins the others, leaving Anthony behind. With Cousin Angelo gone, Cousin Anthony can show his true colors. He shakes his head, and scows, while Joey Styles comments. Joey Styles - Looks like there's some jealousy there in the Marcello family, and it's coming from Cousin Anthony. Segment Four - Hollywood is coming to US! A white limo pulls up outside of the Pyramid in Memphis. New sensation, Trent Hawk emerges in a white, fur coat. He rips open up his coat, revealing the UCW championship, and smiles. Suddenly, camera flashes begin to go off. Hawk poses for a few them, before turning around, and reaching into the limo to help out his lady. Together, they walk the red carpet that has been layed out. Walk to the entrance of the building, where Headmaster Al is applauding, and awaiting. Headmaster Al - Mr.Hawk! I've heard some good, as well as some bad things about you. For one, I understand you have a very violent temper. Let's try and keep that in check while you're here. Second, I hear you're very proud of that UCW title that you're carrying. Be that as it may, I can't let you enter the building with it. Trent Hawk - What?! Why? Headmaster Al - Didn't you see the sign? Trent Hawk - What sign? Headmaster Al - The one that said, "This isn't the UCW. This is the Foundation of Brutality. No UCW titles allowed". Trent Hawk - If I can't bring it in the building, then what am I supposed to do with it? Headmaster Al - I don't know. Leave it in your limo? Trent Hawk - Leave it in my limo? For one of these Tennessee hicks to steal it, and pawn it for some overalls? Headmaster Al - Hey, "To each his own," right? Trent Hawk - But this title is a part of me! I can't just LEAVE it! Headmaster Al - You will, or you don't wrestle here--EVER! Hawk sighs, shakes his head, and begins to unfasten the strap. He takes it off, and hands it away to his valet. Trent Hawk - Wait for me out here. Girl- What?! But is SMELLS out here! Trent Hawk - It'll only take a second for me to filet this punk, baby. Then, I'll be right out. It's me, remember? He gives her a reassuring smile, and a tickling scratch under the chin. Trent Hawk - Once I finish with him, we're going to get the Hell out of here. Girl - Oh, alright. But make it fast! Trent Hawk - Atta girl! She rolls her eyes, and heads back to the limo. Hawk, then, turns to Headmaster with a frown on. Trent Hawk - Man... you're ALREADY starting to piss me off! Al salutes him as he enters the building. Headmaster Al - Welcome to the F.O.B., son! Al enters the building, and shuts the door behind him. Commercial.
