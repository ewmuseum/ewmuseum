Good luck. (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) The Groovy Dude — Posted: Oct 11 2005, 03:00 PM The Groovy Dude is standing in a dimly lit basement. It is furnished with furniture that had to have come from the early eighties, and is a horrible color of yellow-orange, with embroidered plant life over the cushions. In front of the couch and recliner, is a low wooden coffee table, absolutely covered in bent Pepsi cans and every magazine from the Nickelodeon Magazine to Pudding Weekly. Above the couch, on the wall, are shelves, each holding two replica championship belts that The Groovy Dude has won over his career. He keeps them as a momento of past successes, but he also knows that they have no bearing on the present, but they do serve as a reminder of the good ol’ days. On the adjacent wall are framed pictures, moving in chronological order from left to right, as Groovy has put up Bruno Sammartino, Pat Patterson, The Fabulous Moolah, Ric Flair, Hulk Hogan, The Ultimate Warrior, Doink The Clown, Duke “The Dumpster” Drosse, and finally, The Hurricane. The Groovy Dude himself is standing, looking into a fully body mirror, adjusting the collar on his Hawaiian shirt, which covers his white tank top, both of which hang just below the waistband of his orange shorts. Groovy shakes his head, in which his unkept, matted hair just lays in another awkward position. He takes a breath, and stretches out his hand, smiling widely. The Groovy Dude: Dude! This Groovy Dude is The Groovy Dude, dude! What’s happenin’?! Groovy lowers his hand, scrunches his face and shakes his head. He stretches his arm again. He speaks slowly and quietly. The Groovy Dude: Yo. This Groovy Dude is The Groovy Dude... Ick. Groovy tries again. The Groovy Dude: Salutations, Groovy Dude, here, pleased to make your acquaintance. Just wanted to wish luck to you chaps in our eight-man tag tonight. Groovy shakes his head one last time and goes to the couch and plops onto it. He leans backward, letting his head rest fully on the back. The Groovy Dude: Dude... Why can’t this Groovy Dude get a good greeting down? Maybe he should just wear a sign, that way there won’t be any need for talking, this Groovy Dude can just walk in, point to his badge, and that’ll be that. All there will be to do next is to wrestle and impress those who needs to be impressed, and entertain those who need to be entertained, dude. Groovy stands up, body language screaming determination. He walks right up to the mirror, looks it straight in the eye, extends his hand, smiles. The Groovy Dude: Hi. A look of surprise and satisfaction comes across Groovy’s face. He leaps in the air, pumping his fist high. The Groovy Dude: Yabba dabba do, dude! Groovy struts back to the couch, whistling the Flintstones theme. The Groovy Dude: So, this Groovy Dude will be starting with an eight man tag team match. Not a bad thing, dude, not at all. From the looks of things, this Groovy Dude thinks that Gideon is going to be his favorite on the team, dude, this Groovy Dude means, c’mon! How much cooler can you get than being an Elder that worked directly with the Charmed ones! Oh, that Piper, Phoebe, and Paige, kicking demon butt week after week on the WB! Steps are heard walking down the stairs to the basement. Groovy looks up expectantly, in walks his mother with a white medicine bottle in her hand. Groovy’s Mom: Honey, did I hear the Flintstones theme? Is it time for your vitamins already? She hands Groovy a Wilma. The Groovy Dude: Oh.... dude, this Groovy Dude wanted Mr. Slate. Groovy’s Mom puts her arm around her sons shoulders. She guides him to the couch and the two sit. Groovy’s Mom: Well, dear, there is something that we need to talk about. I know that you thought different for many years... but... There is no Mr. Slate in the Flintstones Vitamin bottle. Groovy sits horror stricken. The Groovy Dude: But— But— He’s so important, dude! Groovy’s Mom: I know that, darling... And it is a terrible atrocity, but the manufacturers think that he and Fred have too similar of a shape, and those who take these vitamins are interested in different colors and shapes... I’ve written countless letters, but I get the same response every time, “too similar...” I’m so sorry. Groovy’s Mom wipes a tear for her eye. Groovy looks away, he chokes back the tears. The Groovy Dude: This Groovy Dude understands. Groovy’s Mom: How about we go to Chuck E. Cheese’s? Have some Pizza, play some games? The Groovy Dude: No, thanks, Ma... This Groovy Dude just wants to be alone. Groovy’s Mom: Alright. Groovy’s Mom stands, straightens her apron, and walks back upstairs. Groovy lays on his couch and puts a pillow over his face. His muffled voice comes through. The Groovy Dude: With great disappointment, comes great strength. Though the tears of heartbreak, comes the stronghold of determination. This Groovy Dude will show the people at Flintstones Vitamins that Mr. Slate is as important as Dino or Bam Bam... This Groovy Dude will seal the deal with a victory on Uncensored! Groovy stands and thunders up the stairs.
