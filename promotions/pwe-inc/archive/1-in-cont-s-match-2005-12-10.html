I’m not a fan of the game myself, but still, it’s a connection between our countries. Just like this week, there is a connection between myself and David. David and I both want to win. But, like Australia when it comes to cricket, I am confident we’ll beat the Indians – who are in this case David – and remain the best. You see David, whether you realize it or not, I am the best. I’m the top dawg in this place mate, and it will only be a matter of time before you realize that. Wake up from your fantasy, your world where you are God and we all must bow down to you! The real world is far different. In the real world, you must bow down to others. You need to be skilful, but smart. And you David? You haven’t made a smart choice by stepping into the ring with me this week. That will not make you a smart or skilful man. And trust me, the only scum around here right now is you. You’re the one who keeps trying to insult me, trying to belittle me, but to no avail. You’re the one who doesn’t even care about what you’re fighting for. You’re the one who thinks that wrestling is not a life. The only man out of the two of us who doesn’t deserve to be in this match is the scum that you are. I do get annoyed with people like you. You make insinuations and suggestions with no evidence, and you don’t even give a damn about what you fight for. I have a right to be angry with that, and I am not one to just keep it all bottled in. If I’m angry, I’ll let you know David, and yes, I was angry. You don’t like my advice? Then don’t use it. If you don’t like what I have to say, don’t listen, because that wouldn’t change much. You want to be a rebel? How about instead of being a rebel, be a man. Actually face me, not just shy away and tell me you don’t care about what I say. David, the fact is, I am better than you. And even though you haven’t listened to me, you’ll be listening to every word I say after our match, because I’ll be walking the walk like I always do, and beating you in the ring. And there’s nothing you do about it! Alright mate, let’s head off. Lieutenant: Uh, sir, you have to pay? Harrier: Oh yes, of course. Silly me. I get so caught up in this David I sometimes forget… you know how it is. Lieutenant: Yes, yes. Hit the Bombs Away and get it all done with? Harrier: You betcha. Duncan pays for the food and walks out to the taxi with the Lieutenant behind him, as the camera fades to The Proving Grounds logo, before slowly fading to black… (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image) (Posted Image)
