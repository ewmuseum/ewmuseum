Deadly Games 2005, PWE Championship. Doc — Posted: Nov 28 2005, 11:54 PM PWE DEADLY GAME 2005 November 27, 2005 Madison Square Garden New York City M9 – PWE Championship: Champion, Ryan Styles v Nicholas Pryde (The arena seems to die just then. Lights dim and the crowd disappears into a complete haze of black. It interrupts any thoughts the fans might've had, and a hush floods them all into eerie silence. The tron flickers to life again, black alive with a sudden beating of white letters. Pryde. The mere sight of the word brings a loud chorus of boos, a hot uproar from the crowd who knows without a doubt who is coming through those curtains. 'Professor' Nicholas Pryde strolls through the curtains, looking like a million dollars. He spins in a circle and stops, arms spread wide to embrace the roar of boos that assault him. He drinks the sound in like fine wine.. relishing the noise, the heat, the very energy that crowd rises with. His arms remain open, stretched in the air as his business consultant, Cassandra Lawrence, pouring with class and beauty strolls through the curtains in her stylish business attire. She stops at his side, taking his arm as he draws her close. The crowd erupts, taunting the Professor’s slow walk down the aisle with loud 'Wooo's. A sign of the past he came from, and the mere sound brings a smile to his face with each step to the ring he makes. A kiss is received from Cassandra Lawrence, on his cheek, and he draws away from her to climb the steps and along the apron, stopping to hang on the ropes. Eyes stare out at the booing crowd, a hint of screams and cheers blending in with the heated hatred most relish in throwing at him. He gestures to himself as the robe slips open to taunt them all with the hint of his sculpted body, and he finally ducks the ropes and enters the ring. The referee is forced, by demanding word of mouth, to walk over and unrobe him as the Professor stands with open arms, embracing the spotlight like fine wine pouring upon everyone’s gaze.) TONY: This man… this cocky, poised individual, has been waiting for his title shot for two months. Ever since he defeated Deuce Jackson at Last Man Standing he’s known he had this match ahead of him. And now… it’s finally here. (The arena goes to black and the sounds of a piano and whispering voices fill the arena. The screen pops on looking like a movie trailer, just black with white letters. The piano pulses harder with each frame. "If you've ever felt........rejected" "Alone" "Lost" "Confused" "Wronged" "Get ready for revenge. Get ready for salvation." "Tonight, comes beautiful redemption" The music hits with the screaming vocals HALLELUJAH! LOCK AND LOAD! "Thank You For the Venom" by My Chemical Romance hits and a red light shines from the entrance way. Into the light steps Ryan Styles. Dressed in his black and red rind attire. Styles walks onto the stage, and drops to one knee as if in prayer, there's an explosion of red pyro and Styles comes to his feet raising his casted arm into the air. Styles walks forward loosening up his arms and neck, before entering the ring. Styles crouches a bit, and raises up, arms in the air as more pyro's go off. Styles then flashes a coy smile before returning to his very serious look, leaning into the corner, and preparing for his match.) TAZZ: Here’s your champion, folks! The ever ready Ryan Styles. Call him “Too Cool”, call him “The Stylish One,” or call him “Dangerous R.” Just don’t stand across from him when that title is on the line, because thus far… his record is spotless in this situation. (Both men circle each other to start. Styles flashes with a high kick that misses. Pryde tries a low leg kick that Ryan evades simply by taking a step back. Finally they tie up, and eventually Styles pushes Nicholas into the ropes, and we get a clean break. They tie-up again, and this time Nicholas pushes Styles into the ropes. And this time Nicholas breaks clean. The two go to tie-up again, and this time Styles manages a drop toehold on Pryde and applies a chin lock. Nicholas tries to escape, but Styles holds on with a waist lock as Pryde tries to scramble away.) TONY: What we’re seeing here is more reminiscent of an amateur wrestling match than catch as catch can. TAZZ: Well, I think that Nicholas Pryde has made it clear he thinks he’s a class apart on the mat, and Ryan’s just trying to put an end to that assumption. (Finally Styles just dead lifts Nicholas and drops him stomach first, then applies a body scissors. Nicholas manages to roll onto his back and tries to grab one of Styles's legs. Styles shifts position instantly and tries to apply a cross arm breaker, but Nicholas locks his hands together to block the move. Nicholas finally rolls so Styles is on his back, then hits a spinning knee crunch to Styles's left leg. Nicholas with a little grinding and hits another knee crunch. Pryde then applies a kneeling figure four, and slaps on a front facelock for good measure!) TONY: This guy truly is a wizard on the mat. Whether or not he’s the best in PWE is yet to be determined, but he’s got the champion locked up pretty good there.) (Styles escapes by punching Nicholas in the face, then applies a hammerlock while Pryde is still on top of him.) TAZZ: Didn’t think I’d see that so early. Ryan knew he was in trouble and he resorted to brawling to get out of the hold. (Styles shifts position so he's on top of Nicholas. Pryde and Styles battle to grab the other's arm finally, Styles again tries to apply a cross arm breaker, but it's again blocked by Pryde. Styles tries to break Pryde's locked hands by chopping at them, but no go. Pryde rolls Styles into a pinning position!) TAZZ: Cradle! ONE! TWO!! (Styles rolls to avoid being pinned again, and Pryde takes the opportunity to sink in an ankle lock on the distracted champion.) TONY: Tazz, wouldn’t you say that Pryde’s going in with just about the best game plan possible, trying to take out Ryan Styles’s legs? TAZZ: Absolutely. That’s the most viable aspect of Ryan’s offense. (Nicholas transitions into an STF, but Styles drags Pryde and eventually does make it to the ropes.) TAZZ: This has been a mat wrestling exhibition so far, Tony. But from the gleam in Pryde’s eyes, I think it’s about to degenrate a little bit. (Nicholas releases the hold and lays some stomps onto Styles. Pryde tries a snapmare, Styles blocks it for a few seconds, but eventually gets taken down. Pryde picks up Styles into a sitting position and punts him in the back, then rubs his boot in Ryan's face. Nicholas then bounces off the ropes and hits a sliding dropkick right to Styles's face. Pryde offers a little bow to Cassandra at ringside, then casually kicks Styles in the back.) TONY: This is hardly the time to be posing and preening at ringside! (Nicholas applies a standing double arm submission, really torquing the heck out of Ryan’s shoulders. Styles finally makes use of his size advantage and just muscles Pryde to the mat, and slaps on a leg lock. Nicholas tries to escape, but can't, so he turns on his stomach and crawls to the ropes.) TAZZ: Ryan’s got half a foot and about sixty pounds on Mr. Pryde, and I was wondering if he was ever gonna take advantage of it. (Styles relinquishes the hold and kicks Pryde in the back. Ryan slams Pryde, then bounces off the ropes and hits a big time guillotine leg drop to the back of Nicholas's head. Styles gets on the challenger's back and hooks a chin lock, then segues into an arm wringer, but Nicholas gets his foot on the bottom rope and Styles breaks the hold.) TONY: I think Ryan was setting up the Stretch Plum there, and we all know how quickly that can change the complexion of a match. Pryde was quick on the draw and made the ropes in a hurry. (Styles grabs Nicholas and hits a brutal sit out power bomb! Styles covers!) ONE! TWO!! (Two count, but as Nicholas kicks out Styles immediately applies a leg lock. Pryde tries to break the hold by punching Styles, but no go. He rolls over and reverses into a leg lock of his own. Styles makes a long crawl, lunges, and grabs the ropes. Pryde releases the hold but lays in some head butts on Styles's leg. He drags Styles to the middle of the ring, lays in some kicks to the leg, and follows with another spinning knee crunch. Pryde applies a chin lock, then rolls Styles into a pinning position!) TAZZ: Cradled again! ONE! TWO!! (Styles kicks out, maneuvers on top of Nicholas, and slaps on a camel clutch. Nicholas won’t quit, so Styles tries the cross arm breaker again, and it's again blocked by Pryde. Finally Pryde manages to get his bottom foot on the ropes. Styles lets go, lays in some stomps, and then slaps on an abdominal stretch. Nicholas tries to escape but can't, and realizes he has to make it to the ropes. So he begins his long crawl, and after about 30 seconds finally grabs the bottom ropes. Styles lets go of the hold and both men slump to the mat.) TONY: The leg has got to be bothering Ryan Styles at this point. There’s no way he’s as worn out physically as Pryde. (Styles gets up and drags Nicholas to the center of the ring, and this time slaps on THE STRETCH PLUM!) TAZZ: Ryan’s got him, Tony! (Pryde kicks his feet towards the ropes, but he’s a million miles away.) TONY: I don’t know how long Nicholas Pryde can withstand this move. It’s to his benefit that we’re not much later in the match, but even if he doesn’t submit, he’s going to be feeling this move right up until the final bell. TAZZ: I can’t see him giving it up. These title shots don’t come along every day. (Styles, realizing that Pryde isn‘t ready to give it up quite yet, lets go of the hold.) TONY: Wow. Madison Square Garden has some truly great fans this night, Tazz. From the moment that Styles’s music hit and the chants of "RY-AN, RY-AN" started, there hasn’t been a point where there wasn’t some significant amount of noise in the building. TAZZ: Well, you really get the feeling going into this match that Ryan has a lot of sympathy from the fans. His partner just passed away, and this guy is coming right for him. TONY: Well, Nicholas Pryde is to his feet again, and these two look ready to re-engage. (Pryde and Styles start out exchanging chops mid-ring, with Nicholas getting the better of it. Irish whip on Ryan, Pryde goes for a chop, but Styles holds the ropes. He comes in for a running high kick but Pryde backs away.) TAZZ: I can’t say as I’m surprised by that. These two know each other. How could they not at this point? Styles has known ever since Last Man Standing that he had this match coming up. (Irish whip, Styles ducks a spin kick and comes back with a running high kick followed up with a roundhouse kick. He goes for a snap vertical suplex, but Pryde slips out and hits a gorgeous backdrop suplex.) TONY: I think that this match has officially been kicked into “high gear.” (Back to a vertical base as Styles gets in a wristlock, Pryde kips out and hits an elbow strike followed with a dropkick. Styles to the outside and Pryde comes off the apron but Styles catches him with an elbow strike in mid air. Pryde manages to recover, and send Styles crashing into the guardrail but he has none of it and comes right back with a BIG TIME lariat.) TAZZ: And this is exactly how Ryan Styles needs to be wrestling this match. Aggressively. He’s the champion, he’s the big man. (They return to the ring and Styles’s contempt is unleashed. He lays in lots of stiff chops and nasty kicks. He even slaps on a half crab, takes it very high and stands on Pryde’s head.) TONY: I don’t even know if there is a desire to win at this point. This is about letting out some frustration. Think of all Ryan Styles has been through emotionally these past few weeks. The feud with Eddy Guerrero, then the partnership. Then, just as they had reconciled, Eddy passes away. I can’t imagine what’s going through Ryan’s mind. (Styles hits a few more kicks including a brutal one right to Pryde’s mouth.) TAZZ: No holding back here. And I’m glad to see it. A Heavyweight Title match at an event as big as Deadly Game isn’t the place for holding back. (Finally Pryde has it together enough to begin firing back, and lays in some stiff kicks to Styles’s leg.) TONY: And finally Nicholas Pryde gets the big payoff from his earlier leg work! (Pryde slaps on a half crab but Styles’s flexibility proves helpful as he manages to kick Pryde in the mouth from the prone position.) TAZZ: That’s the second time Nicholas has been kicked flush in the mouth and he doesn’t look happy about it. (Pryde works over the leg with a grapevine submission. He lets Styles up on a couple of occasions only to kick him in the leg and watch him tumble in pain.) TONY: And I think that’s the old adage of respect flying out the window in the face of anger coming forth. Just a cruel, cruel display from Nicholas Pryde. But if that’s what it takes, then so be it. TAZZ: You can hear the “RY-AN” chants gettin’ louder, too. These fans are totally with Styles tonight. (Irish whip and Pryde hits a spin kick. He goes for THE LEGACY but Styles forces him into the corner. Pryde comes right back with a couple of chops. Irish whip and Pryde goes for a dropkick but Styles holds the ropes and hits another spot on running high kick. Ryan waits for Pryde, and… CHAOS THEORY!) TONY: The Chaos Theory! Ryan Styles’s jumping head kick! He just wiped Nicholas Pryde out! COVER! ONE! TWO!! (Two and a half! Styles goes for the STYLISH EDGE but gets back dropped. Styles comes right back with a dropkick to the head. Styles comes off the second turnbuckle on the inside with a knee drop to the back of the head. The pain from Pryde’s earlier leg work comes through as Styles limps away from his prone victim.) TAZZ: Ryan’s gotta block that pain out here. I think he’s getting pretty close to putting the challenger away. (Styles hits four consecutive sets of mini chops to the neck and covers again!) TONY: Pryde might be paralyzed from the neck down after that! ONE! TWO!! (Two and half again. Back to a vertical base, Pryde goes for a roundhouse kick but gets caught. However, Pryde recovers with an enzugiri.) TAZZ: Get a look at the blood dribbling out of Pryde’s left ear, Tony. You think that’s the result of one of Ryan’s high kicks? TONY: No doubt about it. (Pryde again goes for the THE LEGACY but Styles back drops him. Pryde lands on his feet and goes for an inverted DDT, but gets hip tossed. Styles goes for the CHAOS THEORY but it’s blocked and Pryde hits a dropkick to the face. Now Pryde hits THE LEGACY!) TONY: NO! ONE! TWO!! TAZZ: Ryan got the shoulder up! (The crowd comes unglued as Styles rolls his shoulder to escape the pinfall. Pryde bends over and rakes the champ’s face in anger.) TONY: Nicholas Pryde… going up top? That’s not like him. TAZZ: When your finishing move doesn’t put somebody away, you can start getting desperate, Tony. (Pryde stands perched on the top turnbuckle. He waits for Styles to get up to his feet, and lets fly with a slightly awkward, but nonetheless effective cross body press!) ONE! TWO!! (Two and three quarters! Pryde handles his anger better this time, pulling Styles straight back up to his feet and ascending the turnbuckle again. MISSILE DROPKICK! Styles hits a jumping high kick in mid air to cut him down! SYYLISH EDGE! But it’s blocked! They go back and forth with elbows until Styles hits a high kick and then a LARIAT! Styles hits a HIGH ANGLE BACKDROP SUPLEX followed up with THE STYLISH EDGE!) TAZZ: You’ve got it, Ry! ONE! TWO!! TONY: PRYDE KICKED OUT! TAZZ: That puts him in select company, to be sure. Unreal! TONY: And I think the decibel level in this building just went up another notch, if that’s even possible! (Styles hits two consecutive jumping head kicks followed up with a NASTY released German suplex. Styles cinches Pryde up and hits ANOTHER STYLISH EDGE!) ONE! TWO!! TAZZ: Pryde grabs the bottom rope! TONY: I can’t believe this! Nicholas Pryde is a… I don’t even know what he is! This is insane! (Referee Tim White is doing his best to explain what happened to Styles, who is in disbelief.) TAZZ: I don’t blame ya, Ryan! I can’t figure it out either! (One more time! STYLISH EDGE!!!!!! But Pryde hooks his left leg in the ropes to block the move this time.. Ever determined, Styles pulls Pryde into the middle of the ring and slaps on THE STRETCH PLUM.) TONY: I think we’ve reached the end, Tazz. Nicholas Pryde has felt the brunt of TWO Stylish Edges and is still competing, but now he’s in this torture hold. TAZZ: I don’t know, Tony. Like you said, he’s been through so much. I don’t know if he’ll let it end here. (Battered and beaten, Pryde finds the resolve to slowly make the ropes. Styles will not be denied his victory as he again pulls Pryde away from salvation into the middle of the ring.) TONY: He has to give it up. His body will never be the same if this continues, Tazz. (Finally, Styles lets go, frustrated by the resolve of the challenger. But he covers all the same!) ONE! TWO!! TAZZ: And Pryde barely gets a shoulder up there. He’s really out of it, Tony. He’s running on fumes. (Pryde is picked up and returns to his primary weapon, the chop. He follows up with a spinning clothesline. Pryde goes for a German suplex but it’s blocked. Not to be denied, Pryde hits a flurry of chops to the back of Styles’s and then hits a released German suplex!) TONY: I don’t know how Nicholas Pryde is standing, let alone throwing suplexes at this point, but if anyone had any doubts regarding his qualifications for challenging Ryan Styles, I think they’ve been silenced tonight! (Pryde hits a Dragon Suplex, dropping Ryan on his neck. He goes for another German suplex but it’s blocked. Styles hits a chop and then fires off a rolling kick. They go back and forth with chops and Pryde is finally sent reeling into the corner. Styles brings the pain with multiple kicks to the head. Pryde screams in defiance and fires right back with a couple of vicious chops and finishing with a huge rotation spine buster!) TAZZ: The champ’s in trouble, Tony! (Styles gives one last ditch effort as he lets loose with his trusty kicks.) TONY: The will is there, but I don’t think the body has anything more to give, Tazz! The kicks aren’t hurting Pryde enough to stop him! (Ryan fires off a middle kick but Pryde catches the leg. Ryan throws an overhead enzuigiri! Pryde ducks it! With Styles down on his stomach, Pryde sinks in THE PRYDE LOCK!) TAZZ: There it is, Tony! That reverse Figure Four Leg Lock, and Nicholas Pryde has laid plenty of groundwork for it here tonight! TONY: And even with those long limbs that he could use to punch or chop his way out of the hold in a standard Figure Four, Ryan has no leverage on his stomach. The champion’s reign is in serious, serious trouble! (Styles uses his aforementioned long arms to reach, to stretch out in search of the ropes. Pryde is satisfied to take a rest, secure in the hold. Ryan tries to drag himself across the ring by his own hands, but Pryde’s two-hundred and sixty pound bulk is too much to carry this late.) TAZZ: I think Ryan is cooked, Schiavone. TONY: Well, Pryde’s assault on Styles’s leg showed that the challenger does not, and never will miss an opening. TAZZ: That after the nasty assault by Styles to humiliate Pryde instead of defeating him, had to be brought out by the frustration of this past month. TONY: Ryan Styles was determined to have this victory. And it became especially evident in the way he would keep pulling Pryde to the middle of the ring. And now, that same tactic is being used against him as he languishes in the clutches of the Pryde Lock. (Ryan, the crowd cheering him on louder and louder, begins rocking back and forth.) TAZZ: What’s he trying to do? Flip over? That won’t reverse the pressure. (Styles uses his momentum to flip himself over, forcing Pryde down, face-first. Nicholas tenaciously maintains the Pryde Lock.) TONY: I think he thought he had a better chance of scooting on his back. (Ryan, sweat pouring down his face, clenches his gut and sits up in the hold.) TAZZ: He’s gonna try to punch his way out after all! (Ryan begins firing rights and lefts into Pryde’s midsection, but the Professor won’t relinquish the hold. Styles brings a double ax handle down hard on Pryde’s back, and Nicholas jolts upright. Styles leans forward and hooks his head!) TONY: STRETCH PLUM! TAZZ: But Pryde’s still got him locked up in the Pryde Lock! (Now it’s Pryde who reaches out for the ropes, but Ryan quickly pulls the offending arm back, the two wrapped up in an indescribable amalgamation of body parts.) TONY: I’m not even sure what to call at this point, Tazz! TAZZ: I think somebody just gave up, Tony! TONY: WHO!? TAZZ: I don’t know, but Tim White is separating the two of ‘em and they’re nowhere near the ropes! (Mass confusion sets in, inside the ring. Styles and Pryde lay side by side, now disengaged, breathing heavily, sweating, bleeding, barely able to move. Medics are being flagged down to ringside with oxygen and ice for both men. Tim White leaves their sides to have a word with the ring announcer. After a brief powwow, the announcer nods, hands the Heavyweight Title to Tim White, and puts the microphone to his lips.) RING ANNOUNCER: Here is your winner… by SUBMISSION… and… NEW… (the crowd erupts in a cacophony of boos at the utterance of this word) PWE HEAVWEIGHT CHAMPION… THE PROFESSOR… NICHOLAS… PRYDE!!!! TONY: Styles submitted! Nicholas Pryde held on through the Stretch Plum and forced his opponent to submit to the Pryde Lock! TAZZ: What a match, what an effort! Nicholas Pryde unleashed all of his weapons, only to have Styles keep coming back. By the end, Styles was truly spent. TONY: That’s right. His formidable kicks had nothing left. The match was seemingly over, and it looked like Ryan Styles had snatched victory from the jaws of defeat. Unbelievable story here tonight, Tazz. There’s a new man in town! It’s Nicholas Pryde, King of Revenge! And thank you fans, Underground and Revenge alike, for joining us tonight. It’s been a very special evening indeed. Revenge will be preempted next week, but make sure to tune into the usual Underground timeslot for a one time only free broadcast from both brands next Saturday, for a most important event! You Revenge fans, we’ll see you in TWO weeks! (The camera cuts to the ring, where Tim White drapes the title onto the prone, gasping form of the NEW Heavyweight Champion, Nicholas Pryde. Ryan Styles is now slumped in the corner, staring at the mat, inconsolable, sweat burning his eyes. The ride... is over.)
