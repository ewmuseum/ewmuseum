TPG: End of an Era III

17 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TPG: End of an Era III, Hr 3:Diva, Cont, World Title Mikel — Posted: Dec 17 2005, 10:12 PM Match#8 Diva Championship:SoCalVal vs © Trish Stratus [The match starts off with the champion with the momentum. She muscles So Cal Val around the ring with some punches and a shove into the ropes, proceeded by a Irish whip into a stiff elbow. Stratus can not enjoy the momentum long as Val takes control of the match when she reverses and hip toss, landing the move on her feet, then executing a Superkick that connects with the chin of the champion.] Heyman: The Diva Champ could be in trouble. Piper: I doubt it. That title has been around Stratus waist for as long as thee title has been created. No knock against Val, but it will take more then a Superkick to derail Stratus. [So Cal Val dominates the next few moments of the match as she unleashes the variety of offense she poses and shows off her strength. She connects with a few body slams, a dropkick, and finally a double arm DDT. The champ finally does make it to her feet, but So Cal Val takes her down with a tackle.] Heyman: I guess when she told Stratus that they weren’t friend she meant it. [So Cal Val pulls the Champ up to her feet then locks her in a suplex position before dropping her very quickly. She makes the cover. 1-2-KICKOUT! The challenger can’t believe the kick out. She grabs the champion in frustration and pulls her to her feet. She attempts a second suplex, but Stratus wiggles out of the back, then connect with a CHICK KICK! Both divas fall to the canvas.] Piper: There is what I was talking about. [Both So Cal Val and Stratus make their way to their feet at the same time. Stratus lunges first, attempting for another kick, but Val is able to duck out the way. So Cal Val catches the champ with a FLAPJACK SLAM!!!! 1-2-3!!!] Announcer: Here is your winner…AND NEWWWW DIVA CHAMPION…SO CAL VAL!!! (VIA Pinfall in 3:57) Piper: Wow! So Cal Val did it. She knocked out Trish Stratus. I guess it really is the “End of An Era”. Heyman: It is in the Women’s division as So Cal Val is the newest Diva Champion! [The ref hands Val the championship belt, as she sits in the middle of the ring almost in a state of shock at what just happened. The crowd gives her a standing ovation, just for the accomplishment. It stays on this for a moment, then fades backstage where Maria is standing next to Kris Adams.] Maria: With me at this time is none other then… [Maria has the microphone jerked out from grasp by Rachel Taylor. Taylor pushes Maria out of the view, which earns some boos from the crowd, however those boos quickly turn to cheers when Kris Adams comes into view. Taylor hands her man the mic.] Kris Adams: Madison Square Garden will once again see history. It saw the beginning and the end of the WWE. He has seen everyone from Elton John to Puff Daddy, and tonight it will see the crowning of a new TPG World Champion. Rachel Taylor: You know Kris, I have heard some of the guys around the locker room complaining about that you haven’t “paid your dues” enough to be a champion, let a lone the World Champion. Kris Adams: You know what Rachel, I have been called a lot of things in my time. I have been called “old school”. I have been called “green”. Hell I was even called Chuck earlier. No matter what you call me now though, after tonight, you will all call me by the same name…CHAMP! [Pop. Adams walks off as the scene focuses on the ‘End of An Era’ backdrop for a moment, before fading back into the arena.] Match#8 International #1 Contender: Michael Duncan vs David D [The match starts off wildly as the two men begin to exchange punches in the middle of the ring. They both stand toe to toe for a few minutes, but David D slowly begins to win the battle of slugs. Duncan realizes this and connects with a knee to the gut. Duncan quickly moves in for a side Russian leg sweep, but David S shows his power and reverses the move into DDT! David then begins to display his power some more during the match as he dominates for the next few minutes.] Heyman: An interesting fact I found out this week Pipe. The last two International Champions were both crowned by tournaments…and both had to go through David D to do so. [David D attempts to end it. He scoops up Duncan and begins to lift him over his head. Duncan begins to wiggle around and is able to fall out the backside. David turns around slowly and this allows Duncan to connect on a knee to the gut, then a pump handle slam! 1-2-KICKOUT!] Heyman: We just about had a winner right there! [Duncan dominates the next few minutes, but it really isn’t domination as both men are clearly exhausted from this long and gruel match. Duncan drops David with a snap suplex, then slowly brings the big man back up to his feet. Duncan throws David into the ropes, but David reverses and tosses Duncan into the ropes. SPINEBUSTER! Duncan is sent crashing to the canvas with this powerful move. Duncan is slow to get to his feet and David takes advantage of this connecting with a CHOKESLAM! 1-2-KICKOUT!] Heyman: Oh my! I thought it was over for sure right there! [David goes berserk on the ref screaming at him about a slow count. The ref holds up two fingers signaling only a two count, but David D swears it was a three. David is really screaming at the ref and this allows Duncan to sneak up behind David and applies his trademark Sleeper!] Heyman: This could be all fans! [David struggles to get to the ropes, but is losing conscientious too quickly. He throws his arms wildly in the air, but begins to slow quickly. In a last ditch effort David gives a back kick that connects with a low blow to Michael Duncan! Duncan breaks the hold as he slumps over. David drops to his knees as well, trying to regain conscientious. David climbs back to his feet and charges at Duncan. David attempts a big boot to the face of Duncan who ducks at the very last moment. David spins around slowly but Duncan is able to lift him up on his shoulders, connecting with the BOMBS AWAY! 1-2-3!] Announcer: Here is your winner…MICHAEL DUNCAN!!!! (Via Pinfall in 9:59) [Michael Duncan rolls out of the ring as the ref rushes out to raise his hand in victory. Duncan back tracks up the entrance ramp as he keeps his hand raised in victory.] Heyman: Michael Duncan has done it. He has earned a shot at the International Championship! [Duncan keeps his hand raise for a moment, then walks through the curtains. As soon as he does this, his music cuts off as the old “TPG” theme picks up. Teddy Graham steps out through the curtains and makes his way down to the ring.] Heyman: MY LORD! It is the founder of this company, Teddy Graham! Piper: What the frick he is doing here? [Graham walks down to the ring, Madison Square Garden is stunned at this turn of events.] Graham: Let me introduce my self, I am Teddy Graham. I am the founder of this company. While I would like to say that I was invite to be on this program…I guess my invitation got lost in the mail. However seeing how I founded this place, I figure I don’t need a reason to show up, and since the motto of this show has been “the reintroduction to old school’’ I feel that I should make my presence felt. [Pop.] Graham: Now let me get one thing straight. This is nothing against any of the superstars of today, but these guys…are not what you call “old school” superstars. These guys here, they couldn’t hold the jock strap of guys like Sting, Sabu, Raven, hell even guys like Chaos who weren’t on TPG air are better then any of these punks today. [Boo.] Graham: And I have another beef with this new ownership. What is the deal with saying you are going back to “Old School” when you have the “International Championship” belt being paraded around here? With a belt like the Pride Title sitting on the sidelines? Huh? Explain that to me. From where I am standing this program is nothing more then a self promotion of a guy who just couldn’t ever hack it. Do me a favor guys. Don’t call this Old School when… “Looking Back… I See… That I Never Really Got This Right” [“Cold” starts up as Mikel Sweeten explodes through the curtains, with the World Championship Belt over his shoulder. He stomps towards the ring as Madison Square Garden explodes.] Heyman; My Lord, I’ve seen that walk before. Sweets ain’t happy at all. [Sweeten rolls into the ring and gets right in the face of Teddy Graham. He two begin to jaw back and forth as the arena rises to their feet, expecting this to be a “classic” moment.] Sweeten: Let me get one thing very straight with you. You may have founded this place, but when time got rough you left. I was brought in resurrect this company because it has never been the same since you left. So… Graham: You’re damn right. It has never been the same since I left, nor will it be the same until someone like me returns. Sweeten: Look down on me all you want. Fact of the matter is I could give a rats ass what you or any of your elitist crew think about me. But the next time you publicly roast one of my members again, I will cut off your testicles and cram them straight down you throat. You got me? Graham: Chill Sweets. This ain’t the UCW. That crap may have worked there, but in case you haven’t heard, that UCW crap, ain’t cutting it here. This is the PWE. Sweeten: Funny, I guess you want me to come out here and exchange funny one liners with you. Maybe we could begin to get this thing so heated that some of your old timers will come in and give me a good old fashion “flaming”. Graham: Believe me, I have plenty of guys who feel like I do. The Proving Grounds doesn’t belong in the hands of a UCW LOSER like you. Sweeten: Funny…well you see I’m all about competition. So how about this. You go find yourself three old timers, and I will build a team of three current TPG superstars. Then on January 21st, you come back to MSG and we will settle this…. IN A HELL ON EARTH MATCH!! [Graham looks stunned for a moment.] Graham: I’m sure you would love nothing more then to finally have three superstars on your show that is worth while. But what is in it for us? Sweeten: Well seeing how all you have an erection for the International Championship, we’ll put that on the line. If you boys win, we’ll drop that title and bring back the Pride Title? Graham: And if we lose? Not that it will happen… Sweeten: I will burn that motherfucker right in front of you. Heyman: WHAT! Graham: Deal. [MSG explodes as the two go toe to toe, face to face, once again jawing back and forth. The scene focuses on this for a moment, then fades to a promo. ] 'The Return of Hell on Earth!' HellBound2 January 21st, 2006 Match#9 TPG World Title Match: JC Sinn vs Adams [Sinn is in the ring as “Won’t Back Down” picks up. Adams rushes down to the ring and takes control of the match up very early as he unloads on Sinn with rights and lefts. ] Heyman: This isn’t scientific wrestling, but it is effective. [Adams controls the early part of the match up, as he begins to slow the pace down from being an all out brawl into being a more technical based match up. He is able to hit a few moves, including a belly to belly suplex that dropped Sinn right on the back of his neck. Sinn was a little slow getting up and Adams made him pay with a hard clothesline that sent the International Champion down. ] Piper: Kris Adams is looking impressive in the early going. [Adams controls the next few minutes of the match, however Sinn is able to inject himself into the match when his partner Mathew Brock interferes, distracting Adams for just long enough to execute a jaw breaker. Adams is out on the canvas, for Sinn climbs to the top rope and pulls off a SWANTON BOMB! 1-2-KICKOUT!] Heyman: How did he kick out of that! Huh! Explain that to me! Piper: You have to do more the just high risk moves. [Sinn complains the ref for the longest time. He finally turns around, but Kris Adams is able to drop him with a SPINEBUSTER! The crowd explodes as Adams climbs up to his feet. Mathew Brock slides into the ring. Adams pushes the ref aside and gives Brock a kick to the gut…EVENFLOW!] Heyman: The interference attempted FAILED! [Adams climbs back up to his feet, as Sinn goes for a sneak attack, but Adams scoops him up on his shoulders and executes a SAMOAN DROP!!! Adams pauses for a moment then looks up at the entrance ramp, where the “Guerrero” banner is still hanging. Adams steps out onto the apron and climbs to the top rope.] Heyman: You don’t think? Piper: If he does, this will be the biggest tribute maybe of all that we have seen… [FROGSPLASH OFF THE TOP ROPE! Kris Adams connects Gurrero’s famous finisher as a tribute. 1-2-3!!!] Announcer: Here is your winner…and NEWW TPG WORLD CHAMPION…KRIS ADAMS!!! (Via Pinfall 9:56) [‘Won’t Back Down’ starts up as Adams is handed the World Championship Belt. Adams embraces the title for a moment as Rachel Taylor rushes into the ring to celebrate with her man. The two embrace as the crowd explodes.] Heyman: We have seen the return of the World Championship Belt to The Proving Grounds, and you know Pipe, with a guy like this who represents “Old School”, I have to think the past champions are looking down on this match and are pleased that a man who represents all that they worked for is being held by a man who has so much respect for what they have accomplished. Piper: I will give him this much. He is a “throwback”. He has the chance to be a really solid heavyweight champion. Heyman: For Roddy Piper, I am Paul Heyman saying that you for joining us tonight for ‘End of An Era III”. Good Night Folks! Copyright © The Proving Grounds, 2005. All Rights Reserved.
