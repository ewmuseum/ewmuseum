FoB: Rebirth

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FoB: Rebirth, [Hour 3] Mikel — Posted: Sep 20 2005, 09:36 PM [FADE IN.] [The third hour opens back up as the fans are still a buzz over the tag team out come that has just occurred. The ring announcer steps forward.] Triple Threat Grudge Match Christopher Daniels vs Sean Morley vs The Body Breaker Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is now time for our first of two main events! The next match is set for one fall, and is a triple threat match! Coming to the ring at this time…THE BODY BREAKER!!! [The Game and R Kelly-Dreams/Playas Only Remix hits the titan tron and PA System, the crowd cheers and roars to approval as we see many various fans from the crowd stand to their feet in excitement and out walks The Body Breaker. He walks across each side of the stage, acknowledging all of the fans. He slowly walks down the ramp, slapping the hands of the outreached audience members. He slides into the ring. He climbs to the top turnbuckle and taunts the crowd. He steps down and runs over to another one and taunts the crowd again. He steps down and waits in the center of the ring looking extremely focused.] Coach: I really like this new guy. Announcer: And his opponent… SEAN MORLEY! [“Last Resort” picks up as Sean Morley steps out onto the entrance ramp. After a short pause a top the entrance ramp, he slowly makes his way down to the ring.] Announcer: And their opponent…CHRISTOPHER DANIELS!!! [Daniels steps through the curtains, but before his music can cue up, Breaker explodes onto the scene and drills Daniels with a right hand. Daniels is totally caught off guard by this, and Breaker takes advantage of this by grabbing the man and hurling him into the “FoB” logo set up on the side of the stage. The FoB logo falls to the ground as Daniels crashes on top of the foundation to the logo.] Coach: Breaker taking it to the favorite here in the early going. [Breaker chargers and flies into the air, dropping the elbow to the downed Daniels. Morley climbs to the middle rope and looks on from the ring, with a huge smirk on his face.] Styles: Doesn’t look like Morley will be going any where near this action. Coach: Why should he? He is where he should be. In the ring. [Breaker grabs Daniels by the back of the neck and pulls him to his feet….DDT ON THE STEEL ENTRANCE RAMP! Daniels is DDT’d off the foundation and right into the steel entrance ramp. Daniels rolls over, and Breaker kicks him, forcing Daniels to fall helplessly down the entrance ramp, stumbling to the very bottom. Breaker makes his way down the ramp and picks up the dazed Daniels. MORLEY WITH A SUICIDE DIVE THROUGH THE MIDDLE ROPE ONTO BOTH BREAKER AND DANIELS!!! Coach: Holy Hell! [All three men crash to the floor as Morley makes his presence felt in the match. Morley gets to his feet and lifts Daniels into the ring. Morley then turns around, but is met with a stiff right hand from Breaker. With Morley Dazed, Breaker sends Morley into the steel steps…CRASH! ] Coach: Ouch! That was a sick bump. Morley could be hurt. [Breaker rolls into the ring, but as soon as he reaches his feet, Daniels explodes with a clothesline sending Breaker falling over the top rope and crashing onto the floor below. Daniels climbs to the top rope as Breaker fights to get to his feet…. MOONSAULT FROM DANIELS ONTO BREAKER ON THE FLOOR! [Crowd begins to chant… FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! Styles: My lord…all of these men are pulling out all the stops to win this match. [Daniels pulls Breaker to his feet, then gives him a stiff upper cut. Breaker is dazed by this so Daniels whips his opponent full steam ahead right into the ring post! PING! Breaker bounces off the ring post and falls to the floor. Daniels lifts Breaker to his feet, then hooks him up….SUPLEX ONTO THE FLOOR!] Coach: The ref seems to be very liberal with this count. Styles: This is the main event…it won’t end on a count out. [Daniels springs to his feet, pulling his opponent up to his feet as well. Daniels rolls Breaker into the ring, then climbs up on the apron. He seems to be waiting for Breaker to reach his feet. All of a sudden Morley comes into the picture from behind….POWERBOMBING Daniels off the apron and onto the floor below!] Styles: My lord….a SICK bump as Daniels has absolutely no protection between him and that floor below. Coach: I have to tell you Joey, whomever wins this match, is going to be at a LARGE disadvantage in the Rebirth Match. Styles: Good Point, but I doubt any of these three men are worrying about that at this moment. [Morley climbs to his feet and slides into the ring. Breaker is making his way to his feet, but is VERY dazed….MORLEY DELIVERS A BLUE THUNDER BOMB! Breaker bounces off the canvas. Morley springs to his feet, then goes to climb to the rope. He pauses a top the top ropes for a moment, before leaping off and connecting with the…. REALITY SHOT!!!! Morley makes the cover… 1 2 [Daniels reaches under the bottom rope and pulls Morley’s leg. He jerks Morley out of the ring, then once out of the ring, ramps him into the steel post. Morley staggers forward….BLUE THUNDER BOMB ON THE OUTSIDE! ] Coach: We were a matter of mere moments away from this match being over. Styles: Again, I’m not too sure Daniels might not have been better served letting that one go and saving his energy for the Rebirth Match. [Morley appears to have injured his back on the move. Daniels takes no risk. He grabs Morley and pulls him to his feet, before throwing him over the guard rail. Daniels turns his attention back to the ring, and slides in. Breaker appears to be fighting to get to his feet. Daniels waits and charges…..BIG BOOT THE FACE OF DANIELS!] Styles: I believe that is a tide turner… Coach: That may be a match changer… 1 2 KICKOUT! [The resilient Daniels kicks out after a long two count, much to the disbelief of Breaker. Breaker can not believe it. He pulls Daniels to his feet…SMALL PACKAGE BY DANIELS!!!! 1 2 KICKOUT! [Breaker forces the kick out after two. Daniels rolls to his feet, as Breaker fights to get to his feet. Daniels leaps into the air and connects with a drop kick to the face of breaker. Breaker crashes to the canvas.] Coach: Daniels appears to be gaining somewhat of a momentum. [Daniels grabs Breaker and pulls him to his feet. Daniels whips Breaker into the ropes. Daniels drops to the canvas, as Breaker steps over him and bounces off the second ropes. Daniels scoops up Breaker and drops him with a picture perfect Samoan Drop! Breaker you can tell is totally caught off guard by this. Daniels climbs to his feet, then being the very aggressive wrestler he is, pulls Breaker to his feet… THE LAST RITES!!!! Daniels makes the cover…. 1 2 KICKOUT! Styles: Somehow Breaker finds a way to kick out! Coach: The resiliency of these two men is just outstanding. What is it going to take to put these men away? [Daniels looks on in total disbelief. He questions the ref’s count, but the ref reassures him that it was only a two count. Daniels grabs Breaker and pulls him to his… LOW BLOW BY BREAKER! Daniels slumps over to his knees.] Styles: Ouch…that’ll affect Daniel’s social life. [Breaker realizes this is probably one of his final chances to win this match, so the England Native musters all of his energy and throws Daniels between his legs…. THE BODY BREAKER BOMB!!!! Coach: This one is over! Style: Yup. I do ….what the hell. [Breaker doesn’t go for the cover, but rather rolls his way onto the apron. Breaker climbs to his feet, then begins to climb to the top rope.] Coach: What is he thinking? [Breaker taunts the crowd for a moment, but all of a sudden out of no where Morley leaps up onto the apron and proceeds to knock the feet out from under Breaker. Breaker crashes a top the turnbuckle. Styles: OUCH. [Morley climbs to the top rope, but from the outside. He begins to unload on Breaker with rights and lefts. Out of no where, Daniels rolls to his feet and charges the corner… DROPKICK TO MORLEY! MORLEY IS SENT FLYING OFF THE TOP ROPE AND CRASHING ONTO THE ENTRANCE RAMP!!!! [Crowd begins to chant… FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! FoB!! Styles: My lord…I think he may be dead. [Daniels climbs to the top rope. He delivers a shot right across the face of Breaker. Daniels hooks his legs around the top rope. Daniels delivers another shot to the face of Breaker, before hooking him in a suplex position. Daniels executes a SUPERPLEX off the top rope, however he is forced to release the hold, mid air, as his legs are tied onto the top turnbuckle. Breaker crashes to the canvas. Daniels lifts himself back onto the top turnbuckle. Daniels climbs to his feet, with his back facing the ring…… THE BEST MOONSAULT EVER!!!! 1 2 3!!!! [Daniels rolls over after the bell sounds.] Announcer: Here is your winner… CHRISTOPHER DANIELS!!!! [Now flat on the canvas, the ref rolls into position and raises the arm of Daniels in victory.] Styles: What a win by Daniels! Coach: But does this victory cost him a shot in the Rebirth match? Styles: Good Point. [Daniels leaves his hand up in the air for a few moments then rolls out of the ring and slowly makes his way up the entrance ramp.] Coach: Folks, all night we have been showing you clips and pieces from the FoB’s rich and historic past. Next we want to show you what may be the most memorable night in the past FoB’s era. We go back to last year’s “Rebirth”, and the World Title match between Carnage, Chaos and Robbie Crockett. This is viewed by many as the single greatest match ever seen in the FoB. **FLASHBACK** [FoB Rebirth 2004: World Title Match] **We Join in Progress Chaos picks up Robbie and delivers a backbreaker! He turns to Carnage and lifts The Innovator of Violence high into the air and into a powerslam! Chaos turns back to Crockett who is dragging his feet behind him, and sends him into the ropes, and delivers a HUGE spinebuster!!!) STYLES: Why doesn’t Chaos end this! KING: Because he can end it whenever he wants, he’s the champ! (Chaos lifts Carnage over his shoulders and locks in the TORTURE RACK!!) STYLES: FINALLY! But Carnage is out! (The ref realizes that carnage is out cold) KING: Meh, Chaos is my boy, and he is going to walk out champion. (The ref raises the hand of Carnage as Chaos continues to apply the pressure! Carnage’s hand falls to his side) 1!! (The ref lifts his hand into the air once more, and it falls) 2!! (The ref lifts Carnage’s hand into the air for the third and final time, Crockett is trying to get to his feet, but Chaos doesn’t let the hand fall, he dumps Carnage to the outside, as the ref looks on in shock, and the fans boo.) STYLES: WHAT THE HELL!? He had it! KING: AHAHAHA, he’s the champ what else can I say!? (Chaos turns around and approaches Crockett, but is caught off guard a high knee from CROCKETT!!) STYLES: He’s ALIVE! (HUGE CROWD POP) KING: HOW!? NO! (Crockett lifts Chaos over his shoulders and spins him around and plants Chaos!) STYLES: SHADOW IN THE DARK!! KING: NO! STYLES: COME ON ROBBIE! (Chaos lies out and Crockett lays motionless, and Carnage lays unconscious on the floor! The ref begins his count once more) 1!! 2!! 3!! 4!! (NO MOVEMENT) 5!! 6!! 7!! 8!! (MOVEMENT) 9!! 9.9!!! (Crockett is too his feet!) STYLES: YES! (HUGE POP) KING: Joey, you’re not supposed to pick favorites, we’re supposed to be professionals here! STYLES: YOU DO, NOW SHUT IT!! (Crockett lays a few punches to a recovering Chaos, and then sends the big man flying with a T-Bone Suplex!!) STYLES: WHERE DOES HE GET THIS ENERGY FROM! KING: I don’t know but Chaos is going to cut his supply, and SIT DOWN! (Crockett lays a few more boots to Chaos, before standing the big man to his feet, and setting him up in powerbomb position, Crockett lifts the big man up and breaks off at a run, and the plants Chaos with the Running LYGER BOMB!!!) STYLES: THIS IS IT! KING: NO!! (Crockett stays on top of the champion, the ref drops down and makes the count) 1!! 2!!!!! 2.9!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! STYLES: NEW CHAMP!!?? 0 (CARNAGE! DROP KICKS CROCKETT! Sending him backwards, Carnage then squashes Robbie with a big splash!) 1!! 1.9!! 2!! 2.9!! NOO!!!!! (Robbie somehow kicks out!) STYLES: AMAZING! KING: WHAT A MATCH! (HUGE POP FOR CROCKETT) KING: COME ON CHAOS! (Carnage picks Crockett and lifts him high over his shoulders, but Crockett slides off Carnage’s shoulders and rolls him up!) KING: SCHOOL BOY! (The ref drops down and makes the count) 1!! 2!! 2.9!!!! CHAOS BREAKS IT UP KING: YES! (CROWD BOOS) STYLES: DAMNIT! (Chaos sends Carnage over the top rope, Carnages back bounces off the corner of the ring as he hits the concrete) STYLES: Ouch! (Chaos turns to Robbie as grin crosses his face the blood still dripping down his face from the chair shot earlier. Crockett has been busted open as well, the two come face to face with each other, the exchange some words and Crockett slaps Chaos! Crockett wastes no time, he sends Chaos railing with a few right hands and then a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP!) STYLES: Crockett wants this!! (HUGE POP ONCE MORE FOR ROBBIE) KING: This crowd doesn’t know real talent! (Robbie goes for the cover) 1!! 2!! Nope! (Chaos powers out. Meanwhile on the outside Carnage had brought a table out from under the ring, he perches one end up on the ring apron, and the other on the railing.) STYLES: What the!? (Carnage reaches under the ring and pulls out a bag, he unties the bag and a sadistic grin crosses his face, he dumps the containments from the bag onto the table, which are revealed to be THUMBTACKS!) STYLES: JEEZUS! (HUGE POP) KING: AHAHAHA! Someone is going to get branded! (In the ring Robbie has been working on Chaos’ arms. Crockett has an arm bar locked in, Robbie lifts Chaos off the ground and sends him into the turnbuckle, Carnage hesitates getting back in the ring. Crockett then drops Chaos with a large DDT as he stumbles from the corner! Carnage rushes into the and spears Crockett out of his boots, Carnage begins laying the fist to him, as Crockett huddles trying to block the fists, Carnage lifts him up and drops him on his head with a PILEDRVIER! Carnage begins to ascend the top rope!) STYLES: COULD IT BE!? (Carnage poses to the crowd as Chaos is on his feet and knocks Carnage off the rope, Chaos steps up on the second rope and positions Carnage under his arm, he then leaps into the air nailing the GAME OVER FROM THE MIDDLE ROPE!!!) STYLES: MY GOD!! KING: THAT’S IT!! STYLES: IT HAS TO BE!! (Chaos rolls Carnage over and hooks the leg, the referee drops into position, hopefully for to make the final count) .5!! 1!! 1.5!!! 1.9!!!! 2!!!! 2.5!!! 2.9!!!!!!!! STYLES: STILL CHAMP!? KING: YES! BROKEN UP!!! (HUGE POP FOR ROBBIE CROCKETT WHO DOVE THREE QUARTERS ACROSS THE RING TO MAKE THE SAVE) STYLES: MY GOD! KING: NO DAMNIT! NO! (Carnage and Crockett lie motionless as Chaos is struggling to get to his feet.) 1!! 2!! 3!! (CHAOS IS ALMOST UP) 4!! 5!! 6!! (Chaos makes it up and grabs Carnage who is nearly to his feet, Carnage blocks a few punches, and the ref is suddenly beheaded by Carnage!) STYLES: CHAOS DUCKED THE SUPERKICK AND THE REF GOT IT! KING: HAHAHAHA! (Chaos spins Carnage around and gives him a kick to the gut!) STYLES: Kick to the gut! KING: GO CHAOS… STYLES: … KING: WOOOOOO!!! (Chaos lifts Carnage high above his head and SENDS HIM CRASHING THROUGH THE TABLE WITH THE TACKS ON IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!) STYLES: HE’S DEAD!!! KING: AHAHAHAHA!! CHAOS IS VICTORIOUS!!
