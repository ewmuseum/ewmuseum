PWE Revenge: Open Door Night from Boise, Idaho

4 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PWE Revenge: Open Door Night from Boise, Idaho, Hr 3: Sons/bWo & Styles/MaVen Dylann — Posted: Sep 4 2005, 11:40 PM - Commercial Ends – Segment Eight - The Prodigal Sons Unite ("Did My Time" by KoRn plays over the PA system, and out from the back slowly trots out Shane O'Mac in a nice suit and sunglasses. He stops at the top of the ramp, and throws out his arms. After he does, out from the back comes Robbie Crockett & Samoa Joe. They each walk to a side of Shane, and all three stand at the top of the ramp in their glory...with both Crockett and Joe, both holding their half of the PWE Undisputed Tag Team Championships, looking like they want to kill someone. Accompanying them to the ring is Homicide. McMahon flips off his sunglasses and goes to clean them with his shirt, when... BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Black and red pyros shoot out of the stage to the thundering power chords during the song. After that, all four men slowly walk toward the ring, and take their time getting in.) Tony: There they are! Tazz: The PWE UNDISPUTED Tag Team Champions of the WOOOOOOORLD! Tony: “Mr. Intensity” Robbie Crockett! Tazz: And his partner, “The Samoan Submission Machine” Samoa Joe! Tony: Along with “The Notorious 1-8-7” Homicide. Tazz: And their manager, good ole Shane O’ Mac! (Shane motions for a mic from the time keeper. He quickly doing Shane’s bidding. Shane gives a cocky smirk before bring the mic to his lips.) Shane: Well now. At Last Man Standing 2005, we all witnessed what we KNEW we would see. Underground’s Patriots, Joey Legend and Daniel Justice were caught between a rock and a hard place. They just couldn’t keep up with the greatest tag team to ever grace this business. They couldn’t handle the ferocity that is Robbie Crockett and Samoa Joe. And it cost them two very pretty pieces of gold. (We see Crockett and Joe acknowledge this by shifting their titles around for the crowd to see. Shane decides he is done with the mic and passes it off. Crockett takes the mic in hand.) Crockett: Joey Legend talked it up. He mentioned his win over Chaos and various other singles accomplishments. He then went on to knock me for a couple of my singles losses. Did this phase me? Not in the least. But, just as I’ve said before. At Last Man Standing…it was not a singles match. It was one of the greatest displays of tag team competition that PWE has ever seen. And who came out on top? Well, as you can see by these glimmering belts… (The sound is cut off on Crockett’s mic. He looks at Joe and Homicide and both shrug, not knowing what’s going on. Shane immediately snaps his fingers for another mic. One is tossed to Shane, who hands it over to Samoa Joe. Joe brings the mic to his lips.) Joe: … (Still, no sound from the mics in the ring.) Tony: What the hell… (Before we hear anything from Tazz, the lights in the arena go out. But the JumboTron stays on. It isn’t long before something begins to play. We hear a very deep voice.) Good things come in small packages… …a farce. Napoleon Bonaparte was only five and a half feet tall. Yet he was able to dominate Europe. David paled in comparison to Goliath Yet he was able to conquer the giant. We are not Europe. And although just as big…we are not Goliath, either. (We finally are able to see something. We see the ground. Two enormous shadows on the ground. One man taller than the other. One man wider than his tall partner.) It’s called super sizing for a reason. (The shadows disappear. We cut to wrestling clips.) Some say that size does not matter. (We see a rolling wheel kick to the face of Rene Dupree.) Those people... (We see a hand on Rene’s chest, and a 3 count follows.) ...are the small ones. (We see a very large hand make contact to the chest of Sylvan Greiner.) Who are they trying to kid? (We see the fatter of the two men humping a fallen Dupree.) We all know the truth in this large world of ours. (We see Sylvan receiving a choke slam to the outside of a wrestling ring.) SIZE (We see a shot of the Big Show looking deep into the camera.) DOES (We see a shot of Viscera with a creepy smile on his face.) MATTER! (A final shot with both men standing back to back, grins plastered across both their faces.) (Flash. The lights in the arena come back on. The JumboTron goes to black. And we look back into the ring at a very angry group of Prodigal Sons. Shane is screaming at the announcers as Homicide and Samoa Joe are on the ropes, yelling in the direction of the JumboTron. In the middle is Robbie Crockett, deep in thought.) Match Five – The Prodigal Sons v. bWo (bWo suddenly charges down the ring as if trying to gain some advantage. They don’t ambush, but just start the match immediately, catching the Sons off guard.) TONY: And here we go! (Stevie Richards and Joe start in the ring. The two trash talk, and then Stevie slaps on a headlock. Joe counters by whipping Stevie into the ropes, but Stevie hits a shoulderblock and covers for a 1 count. Stevie bounces off the ropes, and Joe drops down, but Stevie slams on the brakes after a Joe leapfrog and rolls him up for another 1 count. Stevie bounces off the ropes again, but this time gets nailed with an armdrag, followed by a hiptoss. Stevie stumbles to the corner, where Joe peppers him with kicks, followed by some bootscrapes and a running dropkick to the kisser. Joe whips Stevie into the opposite corner and charges, but Stevie manages to drop toehold him into the bottom turnbuckle. Stevie follows that up with a Cactus clothesline that sends both men to the outside.) TONY: I’m a little surprised in the early going here, as Stevie Richards is really taking it to the big man. TAZZ: People like to think that the BWO are just a bunch of pranksters, but that’s not the case, Tony. These guys can all wrestle, most of all Stevie Richards. (Now Crockett and Nova come in. Nova sends Robbie into the ropes, and Robbie tries a shoulderblock to no effect. Crockett tries again, and with the same result. Robbie runs off the ropes again, but Nova ducks down and leapfrogs. Robbie tries a kick, but Nova catches the foot and throws it up, but Robbie manages to backflip and land on his feet. Robbie runs off the ropes again and hits a nasty satellite headscissors that sends Nova to the outside. Robbie follows it up with a clothesline from the apron that sends Nova flying into the barricade. Robbie gets back in the ring and tags in Homicide.) TONY: And here comes the Notorious 1-8-7, here to make suckas recognize. TAZZ: ‘Scuse? Meanie gets in the ring, and is immediately hiptossed. Meanie charges, but Homicide leapfrogs him and hits another hiptoss. Homicide charges this time, but Meanie backdrops him. However, Homicide rebounds off the ropes and armdrag the hefty highflyer. Stevie comes in and grabs Homicide, but we get some miscommunication as Meanie whacks Stevie by mistake, sending Meanie outside. Homicide takes the opportunity to tag in Crockett.) (Nova comes in and acts like he wants a test of strength, but instead kicks Robbie right in the gut. Nova with some more kicks, then a whip to the ropes followed by a back elbow. With Robbie on all fours, Nova takes the opportunity to dropkick him right in the head. Nova with a scoop and a slam, and then hits a baseball slide dropkick right to Robbie’s head. Nova drags Crockett over to his corner and tags in Stevie Richards, who comes in and gets some chops in, followed by a whip to the ropes and a leaping leg lariat. Stevie taunts Homicide and Joe, and then covers for a 2 count. Meanie comes in and they whip Robbie into the ropes, with Stevie hitting a drop toehold and Meanie following up with a FAT senton. Da Blue Guy covers, only to have it broken up by Joe at 2. Nova comes in and kicks the bejeezus out of him, and they take over as Meanie dumps Robbie out of the ring.) (Nova whips Joe into the ropes, but puts his head down and gets a kick for his troubles. Joe follows up with a DDT, and then a snap suplex for a 2 count. But when Nova kicks out, Joe holds onto his arm and immediately turns it into a cross armbreaker. Joe tags Homicide back in, and he applies a front chancery, but Nova manages to back him into the BWO corner, where everyone takes their shots. Stevie Richards tags in and hits some chops, followed by a slam. Meanie then hits a somersault senton, then Nova hits his own insane version, and Stevie follows up with an Arabian Press for a 2 count.) TAZZ: Stevie taking a page out of his old colleague Sabu’s playbook there. (Stevie then slaps on an abdominal stretch and reaches his hand out, which Nova promptly pulls.) TONY: Are we in Memphis? We’re not in Memphis! (Stevie releases the hold and tags in Nova, who applies his own abdominal stretch while Stevie pulls on his free hand. The BWO all come in, Homicide gets whipped into the ropes and drop toeholded by Meanie, then gets met with double sliding kicks to the head from Stevie and Nova. Stevie and Nova then hold Homicide down while Meanie hits another FAT senton. Meanie covers for a 2 count while Robbie comes into the ring. He and Nova go at it and take over, as Homicide gets deposited to the outside. Nova whips Robbie into the ropes and flapjacks him, hitting a facebuster on the way down. It gets a 2 count.) TAZZ: Nova invented the facebuster, you know. (Meanie and Richards come in and Nova hits a Lionsault, followed by a Lionsault from Stevie, and lastly a FAT Lionsault from Meanie that gets another 2 count. The BWO whip Robbie into the turnbuckles, and Meanie lies down on all fours like they’re going to bust some mad phat triple teamery. But Robbie runs, leaps off of Meanie, lands on Stevie Richards’s shoulders, then jumps and hits a ‘rana on Nova. Tony Schicavone reacts in true PWE announcer fashion.) TONY: Christ on a fuck stick! (Meanie grabs Robbie, and Stevie goes for a clothesline, but nails his partner instead, allowing Robbie to make the tag to Homicide.) TAZZ: Cide’s gotta get it together here, Tony. He hasn’t been too effective in this one. (Homicide comes in a house o’ fire, hitting tilt-a-whirl backbreakers on Nova and Stevie Richards before getting hit with a dropkick from Meanie, who in turn eats a blind lariat from Joe that knocks him to the outside. Homicide runs like he’s going to do a suicide dive, but grabs onto the ropes and instead gives Stevie (who was standing behind him) a flying headscissors that sends him to the outside. Nova tries to give Joe his super facebuster, but Joe dropkicks him in mid air, sending the BWO member tumbling outside.) TONY: ALL ABOARD! IT’S TIME FOR A TRAIN WRECK! (Joe starts off first with a tope suicida that sends Nova into the crowd. Homicide then follows up by hitting a running no hands Swanton Bomb over the top rope and onto the prone Meanie. Then, if that weren’t enough, one second later Robbie hits a shooting star press from the top rope onto a standing Stevie Richards on the floor! TAZZ: LUCHA CROCKETT! (Homicide and Meanie get back in the ring. Meanie reverses an Irish whip and ducks a clothesline, then hits a backdrop suplex for a 2 count. Meanie slams Homicide, goes up top, and hits THE MEANIESAULT for another 2 count. Meanie goes up again, but misses a second Meaniesault, and Homicide slaps on a Majistral cradle for 2. Homicide bounces off the ropes and levels Meanie with a running forearm. He can’t hook the leg and only gets a 2 count. Homicide whips Meanie into the corner and follows up with a clothesline, then puts the big man on top and comes off with a ‘rana. He goes for the sure pin, only to have it broken up by Nova. Now Crockett is in the ring again, and those two go at it.) TONY: No shortage of exchanges between Nova and Crockett tonight. TAZZ: Nova got the better of Robbie early, and I think he took it personally. (Robbie goes for a pinning combination and Nova tries to reverse, but Robbie turns it into a mid-air bulldogs. Robbie goes up top and jumps off trying, a flying press, but Nova blocks and hoists Robbie back up. Joe comes in and fired a kick to Nova’s back, knocking him over the top and out to the floor with a sickening splat.) TONY: I think this may have just become a handicap match. (Joe whips Nova to the corner, and Nova tries to sling over his opponent, but Joe grabs his legs in mid-air. Joe jerks Nova’s hands away from the ropes and tosses him into the air. Joe turns and catches Nova over his shoulder and in one quick motion, brings him down with the ISLAND DRIVER! A quick three count later, and the Prodigal Sons are victorious.) Segment Nine – Strong Pairing (We cut backstage and find Ryan Styles near the curtains for his upcoming Heavyweight Title match. We get a glimpse of Tom Carter, formerly Reckless Youth, walk by and immediately Ryan Styles calls over to him, gesturing him over. Carter comes over cautiously and a bit confused.) CARTER: What is it? STYLES: Listen, I just wanted to tell you that I thought you did great at Last Man Standing. I thought you had it. CARTER: Oh…well thanks. STYLES: I mean, beating Triple H, Robbie Crockett and Shawn Michaels consecutively is NO easy task. It takes a great wrestler to do that. CARTER: Well like I’ve been saying…the King of PWE is Tom Carter now. STYLES: Well we’ll see…I believe the king needs his gold, am I right? CARTER: In due time, in due time. STYLES: Well either way, I just wanted to let you know that I hope we can meet each other in the ring one day for the Heavyweight Title. And until then, I got your back if you ever need it. CARTER: Thanks, Ryan. Means a lot and rest assured, I’ve got yours as well. (The two shake hands and Carter begins to walk off.) CARTER: Good luck out there. (Ryan nods back to Carter and we cut from the scene rather quickly.) Match Six – Main Event – PWE Heavyweight Title – Ryan Styles © v. MaVen (The music hits and Maven prances his way onto the stage. The crowd, almost in unison, begins booing heavily.) Tony: Well…I want a hot-dog. Tazz: Me too…let’s go get one. RA: On his way to the ring….MAVEN! (Maven hits the ring and slides in, awaiting his opponent.) Tony: Man…that’s a good hot dog right there. Tazz: Yeah…oh look, there’s someone in the ring. Tony: That’s…Maven…which means… (The Lights go out…almost in a wave, the crowd returns to their seats.) "If you've ever felt........rejected" "Alone" "Lost" "Confused" "Wronged" "Get ready for revenge. Get ready for salvation." "Tonight, comes beautiful redemption" The music hits with the screaming vocals HALLELUJAH! LOCK AND LOAD! RA: The following contest, is scheduled for ONE FALL. Approaching the ring, the only person in this match that matters…weighing in at three hundred twenty pounds...the PWE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAYN SSSSSSSSSTYYYYYYYYYYYYLESAH! ("Thank You For the Venom" by My Chemical Romance hits and a red light shines from the entrance way. Into the light steps Ryan Styles. Dressed in his black and red rind attire. Styles walks onto the stage, and drops to one knee as if in prayer, there's an explosion of red pyro and Styles comes to his feet raising his casted arm into the air. Styles walks forward loosening up his arms and neck, before entering the ring. Styles crouches a bit, and raises up, arms in the air as more pyro's go off. Styles then flashes a coy smile before returning to his very serious look, leaning into the corner…staring at Maven. The crowd has been going insane for Styles this entire time.) Tony: The crowd loving the champ here. Tazz: And they can’t wait to see him slaughter the, “Chump,” Maven. Tony: Indeed, Tazz…indeed. (The bell rings and Styles walks to the center of the ring. Maven, wanting to prove he’s worth his salt, gets in Styles’ face…but Styles doesn’t let him do any talking before he ROCKS Maven with a right hand. Maven hits the ground hard, clutching his jaw. Styles chuckles at him as the crowd cheers him on.) Tony: WHAT A RIGHT HAND BY STYLES! Tazz: That was disgustingly awesome. (Styles picks Maven off the ground and locks in a front chancery. He smiles and HOISTS Maven over his head, vertically, with one arm. Styles holds him there for one…two…three…and he begins walking around in a circle…four…five…six…seven…eight…nine…ten…eleven…twelve…thirteen…BAM! He falls backwards SLAMMING Maven’s back against the canvas with a vertical suplex. The crowd roars as Styles dominates Maven, which is what they love to see.) Tony: That one move could finish it here. Tazz: It could…but it won’t. Styles loves playing to the crowd. (Indeed he does, and indeed he will continue to. Styles picks Maven off the ground, pushes him against the ropes, and whips him across the ring. On the bounce-back, Styles delivers a HUGE standing roundhouse kick to the face, knocking Maven flat on his back. Styles runs to the far rope, bounces off, and delivers a jumping leg-drop to the chest, knocking the Wind out of Maven. Maven rolls around on his stomach, but Styles hops up and grabs the back of his neck in the, “daddy grip,” causing Maven to scream in pain.) Tony; Maven tried to get away, but Ryan Styles isn’t done. Tazz: This is just hilarious. Tony: People are Stupid, Tazz...we all make mistakes. Tazz: Yeah…and Maven was one of WWE’s. (Styles lifts Maven up so he’s facing away from him, and then locks in a rear face-lock…before SNAPPING Back with a SICK Snap DDT. Maven’s head bounces off the mat, and he clutches his neck. Styles hops up and raises his right hand, signaling for the end.) Tony: DON’T LOOK! Tazz: And that’s going to set up the finish. (Styles lifts Maven to his feet, and then locks in a standing headscissors. The crowd goes Crazy as Styles lifts Maven up in the Crucifix position…and brings him down HARD on his head and neck with the STYLISH EDGE! He stands up and plants a foot into Maven’s midsection.) Tony: STYLISH EDGE! Tazz: It’s OVAH! 1… 2… 3!!! RA: HERE IS YOUR WINNER…RRRRRRRRRRRYAN SSSSSSSSSTYYYYYYYYYYYLESAH! Segment Ten - Why Wait? (The referee raises Styles hand in the air as the crowd cheers his victory over Maven. He gets his Heavyweight title and raises it in the air..suddenly…two figures emerge from the crowd….It’s Harvard and Pryde…! They slide in and Harvard attacks Styles from behind sending him down.) TAZZ: No, this is just not right! TONY: For some reason, I knew this would happen. (Both men begin stomping him back and forth. The bell keeps ringing but the men won’t stop with the assault. The ref tries to break them away from them but Pryde toe kicks him and does the legacy! They continue the assault) TONY: This is already out of hand. TAZZ: This has to come to a stop. (Harvard picks him up by his arms and Pryde begins punching his chest until Ryan falls to the mat. Harvard grabs a mic) Harvard: You know what!?! I don’t care about you being in top shape anymore! As a matter of fact, I never actually did! (Pryde continues with the assault while Harvard speaks) Harvard: You see because if you remember, Evan Chyldes attacked you…and even though I pretended to fend him off…….I WAS THE ONE WHO SENT HIM! (The crowd boos as Harvard turns back to Styles. He rolls out of the ring and gets a chair. He puts it under the neck of Styles…and jumps on the turnbuckle… TAZZ: WHAT?! TONY: NO! This will practically kill the man… (Suddenly there’s an uproar in the crowd as Tom Carter runs down the ramp into the ring and begins fighting both men as Pryde rolls out. Carter continues with rights and lefts to Harvard and throws him to the outside with Pryde) TONY: Thank God for Carter coming to the rescue TAZZ: Or else, we would’ve had a former champion (Carter removes the chair from his neck and helps Ryan to his feet as his music begins to play. They both stare at Pryde and Harvard as they walk backwards up the ramp staring down at them also Ryan points at Pryde…He seems to be saying under his breath.. “The Legacy will die” TAZZ: This has been a Revenge production. TONY: And we have to go folks. We’ll see you next week!
