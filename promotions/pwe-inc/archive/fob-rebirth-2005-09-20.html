FoB: Rebirth

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FoB: Rebirth, [Hour 1] Mikel — Posted: Sep 20 2005, 09:31 PM FoB Presents: Rebirth September 20th, 2005 From Austin, Texas [FADE IN.] [Before the “supershow” begins, it is opened with a theme from the original “Rebirth”. ] Yeah.............. The FoB Logo is shown I won't back down Daniels is show attacking Morley I will not bow T-Bone is shown clothes lining Angelo Marcello (I've come to bring you hell) Uncle Shaun is shown staring down The Detroit Street Thugz For all the scars that never heal Various clips of the Rookies are shown All the wounds that will not seal A clip is shown of Chaos holding up the FoB Championship I've come to bring you hell... 30 MEN ONE DESTINY ONE WILL ONE GOAL (Posted Image) [The FoB logo flashes by.] [The video fades as the feed opens up in Austin Texas, as “Across the Nation” picks up. The place goes berserk as the pyros explodes from the stage. We join the Coach and Joey Styles at ringside.] Coach: Welcome everyone to the reintroduction to the FoB Classic… REBIRTH! Styles: I don’t like Sweets, but he got this one right. Rebirth was always my favorite pay per view. Coach: And tonight should live up to the original as we have SEVEN great matches in store for you. And lets not keep you waiting any longer… [u]The 2nd Annual Rookie Invitational Battle Royal Participants: Carlito Caribbean Cool vs. Mark Garrett vs. P.J. (J.P.) Ruthless vs. "The Innovator" Steve Mitchell vs. Bubba Ray Dudley vs. Novak vs. Mr. Australia [All seven men are in the ring as the announcer steps into the center of the ring.] Announcer: Our opening match for Rebirth is set for a over the top battle royal match, in which you may be eliminated by either pin fall, submission, or being thrown over the top rope, and having both of your feet hit the floor below. In the ring are our seven competitors! [The bell sounds as all seven men erupt into the center of the ring forming extreme chaos. ] Coach: Joey…you want to call this? Style: Yeah…one word…Mayhem. [The camera zooms all over the ring. You see Novak delivers chops to Ruthless in the corner for the ring. Steve Mitchell and Carlito are double-teaming Bubba Ray Dudley, and Australia and Garrett are exchanging punches in the middle of the ring. The camera continues to focus on these three battles.] Coach: Of course Styles as you may remember at last year’s “Rebirth” SBM won the rookie invitational and he went on to have a very successful career in the PWE. [Carlito and Steve Mitchell continue to work on Dudley on the canvas. Both men stomp away at the eldest Dudley who fights to get to his feet. Once he reaches his feet he gives Carlito a stiff upper cut shot to the jaw, as Mitchell gives Dudley a thumb to the eye. Dudley slumps over from the shot as Carlito fires an elbow to the top of the head in frustration. Dudley falls into the ropes. Mitchell and Carlito lift Dudley and begin to force him over the top. Dudley is a load, but finally they force him over the top rope and he crashes onto the floor below.] Bubba Ray Dudley has been eliminated! [Carlito and Mitchell celebrate. The two men embrace as Mitchell holds Carlito’s arm up in the air. Mitchell drops his hand and asks Carlito to do the same. Carlito acts like he is reaching for his hand, but at the last moment explodes with a clothesline that sends Mitchell flying over the top rope and crashing onto the mat right next to Dudley.] Steve Mitchell has been eliminated! [Carlito stands there with a cocky smirk on his face, tapping the side of his head.] Coach: Ha! Carlito out smarted the rookie there. [The Camera switches over to show Mr. Australia and Garrett battling it out in the corner. Australia backs outward then explodes with an elbow shot that connects with the temple to Matt Garrett. ] Coach: This Mr. Australia guy looks impressive. [Garrett stumbles forward, in a stack of daze. Australia scoops him up and drops him with a Manhattan Drop! Garrett staggers backwards in total pain. Australia explodes with a clothesline sending Garrett over the top rope.] Matt Garrett has been eliminated! Coach: Styles they are falling off quickly. Styles: Yeah…the way I like it. I don’t get paid by the hour. [Australia throws his hands up in the air as Garrett looks on with a dazed look, knowing he was over powered on this occasion.] Coach: Damn, even I’m intimidated by that. [The camera finally turns over to Novak and Ruthless. Novak and Ruth are exchanging rights and lefts in the middle of the ring. Novak is the larger of the two men, and soon wins this battle. He tosses Ruthless into the ropes. Ruthless springs off the ropes and connects with a flying forearm shot.] Coach: Man, I thought that was SBM for a moment. Styles: You are pretty dumb, you know that? [Ruthless springs up to his feet and muscles the larger man to his knees. Novak lets loose with a shot below the belt, which sends Ruthless over to his knees.] Coach: Ouch. [Novak lifts Ruthless onto his shoulders then stands up. The crowd rises to their feet as Novak lifts Ruthless above his head and hurls him over the top rope! Ruthless comes crashing down on top of the guard rail.] JP Ruthless has been eliminated! Coach: HOLY HELL! [The crowd goes crazy, then breaks out into a chant of… “F-O-B! F-O-B! F-O-B! F-O-B! F-O-B! F-O-B! F-O-B!” Coach: That was amazing! [Novak stands back proud of himself. All of a sudden Carlito charges from behind and sends the large man flying into the ropes. Due to his weight, and momentum the large man is sent tumbling over the ropes, but luckily for him, he lands on the apron.] Styles: OH! I thought Novak was a goner. Coach: You can’t let your guard down for one moment. [Carlito believes Novak is gone, so he celebrates. However Australia takes advantage of this, grabbing Carlito by the hair and tossing him over the top rope! Carlito is very athletic and lands on the apron. Novak rolls into the ring and charges, however Australia senses this and dives out of the way at the last moment. Novak charges right into Carlito sending him crashing off the apron.] Carlito has been eliminated! [Novak is stunned from his collision, and this gives Australia the opening his needs. He grabs Novak and tosses him over the top rope.] Novak has been eliminated! Announcer: Here is your winner…MR. AUSTRALIA!!!! Coach: I don’t believe it! The Mate from Down Under Came Out on Top! Styles: You are SOOOO dumb. [The scene focuses in on the celebration of Australia for a moment. Then the scene fades backstage where Mikel Sweeten is standing in front of a FoB set up along side Al Snow.] Snow: You know Sweeten, tonight is a very special night in deed for the FoB. Tonight we will crown not only our new tag team champions, but also the FoB Championship. Sweeten: The FoB Championship….held by some of the biggest names in all of the bussiness: Chaos, Crockett, Lennex, Nob… well three of the biggest names in the PWE’s history. Snow: Correct. The FoB has a rich and storied history, so tonight we will show you a video package of every single major moment the FoB Championship has had. Let us begin with the FoB’s second Heavyweight Champion, he made his mark by opening up the SWF…frankly the reason we are all here. He is widely considered to be the “Godfather”, and that isn’t because he is apart of the Marcellos clan. Without his dream, desire, this group of guys may have never been able to come together. Sweeten: When Lennex won the FoB Title, it was not only his first independent championship, but he was the first respectable champion the FoB had hold its richest championship belt. Many consider him to be the person to jump started the FoB’s rich history, and so now join us as we watch the “Godfather”, John Lennex capture his first FoB Championship. [The feed fades from our two Co Owners, and fades into a video feed featuring John Lennex.] (Lennex is very slow to get to his feet and ONS starts to club at his back. ONS stops and points down to the ring.) Styles: I think it might be time for Lennex to take the fall. ONS seems to think so anyway. (ONS picks up Lennex, but Lennex shoves him down hard, at the very edge of the scaffolding.) King: Wow that was close. (Lennex mounts ONS and punches him in the face repeatedly. Blood immediately pours from ONS’ nose.) Styles: That’s not a pretty picture. (ONS gets a shot in and rolls Lennex off of him, right over the edge of the scaffolding.) Styles: Oh my god! (Lennex hangs on with both hands and attempts to pull himself up.) Styles: This is getting pretty dangerous. Lennex is hanging at 50 feet in the air. (ONS kicks Lennex in the head, trying to make him fall. Lennex swings but hangs on. ONS stomps on the hand of Lennex, but he still hangs. ONS crouches down and punches Lennex in the forehead. Lennex grabs ONS with one hand and pulls him down. Now both men are hanging.) King: I don’t think I can take anymore of this. (Lennex and ONS sway towards each other and start to trade kicks. They move away from each other and slowly start to climb back up. One back up, they go at each other again.) Styles: Remember folks, the only way there will be a winner of this match, is for someone to fall off the scaffolding. (Lennex picks up ONS with a gorilla press, and hoists him over the edge.) Styles: Oh my god, this is it. (ONS falls back onto the scaffolding, and dropkicks Lennex. Lennex wobbles a bit. ONS tries for another one, but Lennex bats it down and regains his balance. ONS is slow to get up and Lennex gets behind him, hitting a low blow!) Styles: NO! WHAT A CHEAP SHOT! (Lennex turns ONS towards the edge and lifts him up high. ONS gets viciously power bombed all the way down to the ring below!!!!!) Styles: Jackknife, oh my god. Did you see you that?!?! King: That was brutal! Lennex has retained the title, but after that, I don’t think he’ll be able to defend it again. [The scene focuses on Lennex as he has his arms raised in victory. This scene focuses in on the man for a moment, then fades out.] #1 Contender’s Match for the Tag Team Championships The Detroit Street Thugz vs CM Punk and Colt Cabana. -Bone and Mouse explode from behind the curtain. Mouse goes crazy with head banging and arm flailing. T-Bone immediately begins to show off his skills. He twists and turns his sword as it slices through the air like a hot knife through butter. The two then exchance eye contact and then run down to the ring. T-Bone grabs a set of steel steps and moves them to right in front of the ring. He then points to Mouse and Mouse runs up the stairs and leaps over the top rope. T-Bone then follows his brother into the ring, moving up the same steps. The two exchange in a high five and then turn to the tron, awaiting their opponents. All of a sudden pyro shoots off as Cabana and CM Punk enter through the stage. The fans boo and cheer. They run down the entrance ramp and roll into the ring. Tbone walks out of the ring and Cabana rolls out aswell. Mouse and Punk face off. They lock up and Punk pushes Mouse down to the mat and charges at the ropes. Mouse ducks and catches him with a spinning heel kick! He then throws him into the turnbuckle and gives him some knife edged chops! Crowd: WOOO!!!! Mouse puts the armbar on Punk and tags in his partner Tbone! Tbone contiues the punishment as he holds on to the arm of Punk. Tbone does an Irish whip to the corner and takes down Punk with an arm drag takedown! Again Tbone goes for the armbar, but Punk takes him down with a leg sweep and tags in Cabana. He leaps into the ring and goes up against the stronger Tbone. Tbone pushes him down and taunts him. Cabana leaps onto him trying to knock him down but Tbone stays put. Cabana tries it again but again Tbone doesn't fall. He tries it for a third time but Tbone catches him and ddt’s him. Then he follows up with the Michinkou driver and tags in Mouse...... Coach: Good chemistry so far between the two. Mouse comes and and quickly gives Cabana a back suplex! He picks him up and body slams him and is about to go on the top rope and give him a 450 splash! But Punk pulls Cabana out of the way and Mouse misses!!! Tbone comes out to agrue with the ref, and as the ref's back is turned Punk takes off the turnbuckles and exposes the bare steel! He picks up a dazed Mouse and throws him face first into the steel! As both Punk and Tbone run back to their respective corners, Cabana goes for the cover on Mouse 1...2....no!! He kicks out!! Cabana is in shock as he angrily tags in Punk as he goes to finish the job. He throws Mouse into the ropes, and BOOOMM!!! Hits him with the big boot! Punk signals to the crowd and hits him with a leg drop!! 1......2......... NO!!!! Coach: I don’t know why they keep covering, they can’t win unless they hit the other team’s finishing move. Styles: Their wearing down The Detroit Street Thugs! Tbone breaks up the count! The ref's try and stop him as both Punk and Cabana double team Mouse! Punk tags in Cabana, they Irish whip him into the corner and Cabana lifts him up while Punk jumps up and hits with a hard spinebuster!!!! Styles: Mouse needs to make a tag right now!!! Cabana then goes to the top and hits with a moonsault!! 1......2......... but again Mouse kicks out!! Cabana then ers a hard scoop slam and again goes for the cover but again Mouse kicks out! Now Cabana snaps and starts to stomp on Mouse to the point where the ref has to break it up. Cabana again tags in Punk who then sidewalk slams Mouse and puts him in the single leg crab submission!! Mouse tries to drag himself into the ropes but Punk drags him back into the middle of the ring! After deciding that he had enough, Punk releases the hold, sets up Mouse and OH MY GOD!!! It's him with a devasting Clotheline from H*ll! 1.....2... but again Tbone breaks the count!!! Again the ref is distracted as Cabana comes from behind and WHAM!!! Takes out Tbone with a chair!! It's 2 on 1 now!! The ref tells Cabana to get back to his corner and Punk decides to finish the job once and for all. He picks up Mouse and hits with a devastating PowerBomb!! He's about to pin but when he sees Cabana begging for the tag, he tags him in. Cabana again goes to the top and is about to attempt the Mouse Trap but misses as Mouse moves out of the way! Punk now comes in and is fighting for all that he's worth!!! Left's and right's by Mouse and tries to throw a haymacker but is blocked by Punk who gives him a devasting belly to belly suplex! Cabana and Punk are about to finish the match by using a double superkick when Mouse ducks and Cabana and Punk ly knock each other out!! Mouse slams Punk into the bare turnbuckle knocking him out. Mouse falls to the ground panting for breath. Tbone is back up on the apron as Mouse tries to tag him in. Punk tags in Cabana as Cabana tries to stop Mouse from reaching the refreashed Tbone. Cabana grabs a hold of Mouse’s leg, but Mouse hits him with a spinning kick. Mouse jumps and tags in Tbone. Coach: The Big man is in!! Tbone clotheslines Cabana, and Punk who has now entered the ring. Tbone taunts and picks up Cabana. He sets him up for his finishing move, the Colt 45. Tbone is about to hit the move when Punk reaches his feet and spears Tbone to the mat. Punk starts punching out Tbone as the two roll out of the ring. Cabana is still down as Mouse gets to his feet and DDT’s Punk to the mat. Mouse stands back up and smiles at the fans. Mouse grabs the head of Punk and pulls him out of the ring. Mouse jumps over the turnbuckle and punches Punk in the face as he drags him to the back. Coach: Where is he going? Mouse continues bouncing Punk’s head against the wall as he walks to the back of the arena. The camera follows them into the back. Mouse gets back by a dumpster in the back. Mouse pulls Punk up onto the trash can he pushes Punk under his legs and looks like he is setting him up for the Pepsi Plunge. Mouse pulls one arm up and tries for the other one. Punk starts to punch at Mouse’s legs. Mouse almost falls off the garbage can as he tips. Punk realizes he can’t tip over Mouse, so he hits Mouse with a low blow. Mouse grabs at his package as Punk gets out from under Mouse’s legs and sets Mouse up for the Tbone from the trash can. Punk tries to lift Mouse but his back goes out on him. Punk lifts again and this time get’s Mouse in the air. Punk is about to hit the Tbone on Mouse off of the Trash can but Mouse falls back down on the can. Mouse punches at Punk’s face as they both are tipping off the can. Mouse finnally pulls Punk under his legs and jumps off the can. Punk’s face hits the concrete as Mouse falls over him. They both are unconscious as the ref makes the count. Ref: 1… 2… Cabana comes running through the back rooms as he almost reaches the pin. But, from behind Tbone runs up and hits Cabana’s head with a double axe handle. Cabana falls to the ground. Ref: 3!!! Tbone walks over to Mouse and helps him up. Tbone lifts Mouse’s hand In victory. Tbone helps Mouse away as they both smile and celebrate from their hard fought win.
