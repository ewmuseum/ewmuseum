ROLEPLAYING/MATCHWRITING AWARDS Superstar of the Year Best Roleplayer Most Deserving Of A Promotion Best Gimmick Worst Gimmick Best Rookie (This includes anyone who has never been in PWE before, and has debuted on a main brand between Saga III and now. Best World Champion Best Silver Crown Division RPer Best Television Champion Best Dream Champion Best Tag Team Best PWE Legend (Retired Superstar. This category is all time.) Best Storyline of the Year Worst Storyline of the Year Most Improved Superstar Best PWE Show of the Year Most Missed RPer Most Hated RPer Most Loved RPer Best Written Match Biggest Impact (NOT the same as Best Rookie. For example, Postal Maniac CAN be nominated in this category.) Most Influentical RPer Most Underrated RPer Most Entertaining RPer Matchwriter of the Year SOCIAL AWARDS(These are all time) Most Loved Most Hated Best PWE Moment Worst PWE Moment Biggest PWE Impact Moment (For example, Joe leaving owning Revenge to help all the brands.) Most Missed Member Column of the Year Funniest Most Serious Meanest Biggest Asshole(Something tells me Jake Jones won't win this.)
