TPG Uncensored Results

TPG Uncensored Results, 10/9/05 Clarke — Posted: Oct 10 2005, 08:24 PM SEGMENT #1 - (We cold open in the center of the ring, the sirens from Scott Steiner’s entrance music winding down to the point that they are finally drowned out by boos for this man, being handed so much so quickly. Steiner, live mic in hand, smiles and shakes his head, his sunglasses unable to conceal his conceit.) Scott Steiner: I can hear the boos… (Steiner pauses for a moment and takes a step back, the catcalls growing ever louder. He soldiers on.) Scott Steiner: But you people hating me doesn’t change the opportunity I have in front of me. Not one little bit! I’m still going into “Golden Opportunity” with just that… a golden opportunity. And I’m still walking out the Proving Ground’s International Champion. I’m gonna be keeping’ my eye on this tournament, and I strongly suggest each and every person in it keeps their eyes on me. Because I’M the man to watch around here, as Steve Hart is gonna find out later tonight. Now, HOLLA IF YA HEAR ME, LOVELAND! (Uncensored goes to it’s opening credits as the crowd boos a smiling Scotty Steiner.) MATCH #1 – Johnny Gladiator, Ring Riot and Cryss Mystic def. Chill Factor (Heat, Ice, and EvE) SEGMENT #2 - (Backstage, McCoy leans against the wall of a narrow hallway, stretching out his back.) Clarke Andrews: Matt McCoy, well I’ll be. (The Australian Gator straightens up and spits out a piece of chewing gum, just missing Clarke’s brand new shoes.) Matt McCoy: Hey there, Mr. Andrews. What can I do for yeh? Clarke Andrews: Oh, not a lot. I just saw you doing some preparation for the big match tonight. I mean, Eternal. That’s gonna be good. Matt McCoy: Yeah, I suppose. Clarke Andrews: Well, I’ve got places to be, people to talk to. But before I go… good luck. (Andrews extends his hand. McCoy considers for a moment and then takes it. He begins to walk away, but Clarke pulls him back in. The two men stand almost eye to eye.) Clarke Andrews: And Matt… if you DON’T win tonight… you’re fired. (The crowd, watching on a monitor, heavily boos the administrative ultimatum. Andrews smiles and casually walks away, leaving the Gator with an extended hand and a troubled look on his face.) MATCH #2 – Junior vs. E-Rock (We head over to the team of “Mean” Gene Okerland and Jesse “The Body” Ventura.) Gene: Well, this next match will be a great one. Jesse: Damn right. We have Junior, the— (Ventura is cut off when “Outta Control (Remix)” by 50 Cent featuring Mobb Deep rocks the PA system. Junior emerges to a great pop and slowly walks down to the ring, slapping hands with his loyal fans.) Jesse: Listen to this. The fans are showing love for the high flyin style of Junior. His opponent will be tough though. Ring Announcer: The following contest is scheduled for a one fall, in the ring, from Los Angeles California, weight in at one-hundred and ninety five pounds……JUNIOR!! (Suddenly, “Bring Me To Life” by Evanesence hits and E-Rock emerges to a shower of mixed reactions. Some fans cheer while some boo.) Jesse: Here is the more powerful man, whose moves are more damaging, E-Rock. Ring Announcer: And introducing his opponent, from Toronto Ontario Canada, weight in at two hundred and fifteen pounds……E…..ROCK!!!!! (The ring announcer just got out of the ring when E-Rock charges Junior and the bell rings.) E-Rock is chargin but Junior hits a drop toe hold that sends E-Rock’s neck into the bottom rope. Gene: Smart move there, Junior using his in-ring style to his advantage. Junior tosses E-Rock into the ropes and hits a big dropkick that sends E-Rock to the outside. He begins to throw a fit and isn’t pay attention to Junior who runs into the ropes and flies with a swanton plancha that has the fans chanting “Holy Shit!” “Holy Shit!” “Holy Shit!” Jesse: What a move from Junior. Obviously these fans agree. Junior rolles E-Rock into the ring and makes a quick cover. 1…………………… kickout. Junior lifts up his opponent and hits a few chops. Junior then smacks a few left hands and another toss into the ropes. E-Rock comes back with a big clothesline that turns his opponent inside out. E-Rock stomps away at Junior and lifts him up, and drops him with a ddt. Hooks the leg. 1……………………… 2………………………….. Kickout. Gene: Near fall there. E-Rock HAS to slow down Junior if he wants to win. E-Rock connects with a snap suplex and then applies a sleeper hold to Junior. Junior slowly gets up and begins to elbow out. He runs into the ropes and lands a big handstand elbow to the face of E-Rock. Both men are down and the ref begins his count. 1……………… 2……………… 3………………. 4……………….. 5……………….. Both men slowly get up and exchange left hands. Junior blocks a few and hits a few straight right hands. Another toss into the ropes and a quick headscissors that sends E-Rock into the corner. Gene: Junior gaining momentum, let’s see what he does with it. Junior charges E-Rock and connects with a monkey flip. Junior climbs up to the top turnbuckle and hops off, trying to land a cross body block. E-Rock, sits on on knee, causing Junior’s stomach to land on the knee. Jesse: WHAT A COUNTER. Big change in momentum there. E-Rock begins to taunt Junior and laces his opponent with some chops followed by a scoop slam. E-Rock again, stomps away at Junior. E-Rock lifts up Junior for a brainbuster and has him stalled up there. Junior wriggles out and lands a neckbreaker and again both men are down. 1…………… 2…………… 3…………… 4……………. 5……………. 6…………….. Both men slowly get up and again, exchange punches. Junior managers to block a few and rolls up into a small package pin. 1……………………….. 2………………………. Kickout. Gene: Another near fall there. E-Rock has to be careful. E-Rock gets out and goes for a scoop slam but Junior rolls out and lands a huge inverted ddt. Junior hops off the 2nd rope and lands a big moonsault into a pin. 1……………………………………… 2………………………………………….. Kickout. Gene: Not again, this is rediculous. E-Rock slowly gets up and Junior kicks him right in the gut. E-Rock doubles over and Junior is able to land the Pedigree! Gene: It’s over. Junior is advancing. Junior smiles at the crowd and goes to the middle of the apron. Jesse: Junior is gonna win with style right here. Junior springs up to the top rope and lands a big Swanton Bomb. He hooks the legs. 1………………………………….. 2……………………………………… 3………………………………………. Gene: He did it! Junior advances. Great match there and E-Rock is yet to win. Jesse: He overcame the size deficit and hits the Pedigree and won it with the Springboard Swanton. Ring Announcer: Here is your winner………JUNIOR!!!! (Junior celebrates the win on the turnbuckles as we roll out of the segment….) SEGMENT #3 – Dave Phoenix has an interview with Terri Runnels complaining about the way that Clarke Andrews and Teddy Graham run TPG. MATCH #3 – Rupert Shaw def. Dave Phoenix, Derek Rampant, Zack Bowman, Liberty and Matt Razor SEGMENT #4 – David D. and Tom Danials talk to Terri Runnels about their undefeated streak, and how it is bound to continue tonight MATCH #4 – AVH def. David D. MATCH #5 – Lacey and Eliza Marie vs. Trish Stratus and So Cal Val We get a shot of the fans cheering as “Date With A Night” by the Yeah Yeah Yeahs hits and Lacey emerges to a big “Lacey Sucks!” chant. She shows off her title to the fans who boo her more.) Ring Announcer: The following Diva’s Tag Team contest is scheduled for a one fall. Introducing the Women’s Champion……..Laceeeeeeyyyyyyyy. Gene: One of two women’s champions is not loved by these fans at all. Jesse: She sure isn’t, but she is great to look at. (“International Woman” hits the arena as Eliza Marie comes out to boos as well.) Gene: Neither of these women will have the crowd on their side. Let’s see if that will be a disadvantage. Ring Announcer: And her tag team partner. From Bridgewater Connecticut….Elizaaaa Marieeeee. (Eliza barely enters the ring when “Rich Girl” by Gwen Stefani hits and So Cal Val emerges, by herself this time. She gets a lot of cheers as she slowly walks down to the ring, looking very attractive like always.) Gene: No Neta this time Jesse. Jesse: I heard he is leaving PWE for a while but should be back soon. Ring Announer: And the opponents, first, now residing in Beverly Hills California….So….Cal……Vallllll! (So Cal Val stays on the outside when “Keys To The City” by Lil Kim hits and the 2nd Women’s Champion emerges to a big pop.) Ring Announcer: And introducing her partner, the other Women’s Champion…from Toronto Ontario Canada….Trishhh Stratusss!! (Trish runs down and runs in the ring, under the bottom rope along with So Cal Val and the bell rings.) Gene: We’re ready for action. So Cal Val and Trish bitch slap the hell out of Marie and Lacey. Marie and Lacey leave the ring and wait until So Cal Val goes to the outside. Trish and Marie will start us off. They lock up and Trish hits a few forearms followed by a clothesline. Lacey scrambles to the corner and tags in Eliza Marie who charges Trish. Trish puts out her fott and Eliza hits her face on the 2nd turnbuckle. Trish and So Cal Val laugh at her and Eliza charges Trish who hits a big back body drop. She tags in an eager So Cal Val who knocks down Eliza with a clothesline. So Cal Val poses for the crowd and Eliza charges her but Val saw it coming and landed a big elbow. Gene: Val and Trish are teaching Eliza and Lacey a lesson in wrestling. Jesse: Sure are. This match is gonna end very soon if this keeps up. Eliza tags in Lacey who locks up with Val. Val kicks out and then slaps Lacey across the face. Lacey responds with a quick ddt. Lacey tags in Eliza who stomps at Val until the ref breaks it up. Eliza then hits a few forearms and goes for a ddt but Val reverses it and pushes her down. She tags in Trish who charges Eliza and knocks her down with a clothesline. Lacey enters and also gets one. Trish clears house with arm drags to both. Val gets up and knocks Lacey down and proceeds to throw her into the corner. Trish does the same to Eliza. Gene: They are o a roll…whats this? Val and Trish mount their opponents and deliver a 10 count punch. The fans count with them. After 10, Trish and Val rub their “fun parts” in the faces of Eliza and Lacey. Lacey and Marie are dazed and Lacey walks into the Flapjack Slam while Eliza walks into the Chick Kick. Gene: There it is. The two devastating moves from two beautfiul women. Trish covers Eliza and the ref begins the count 1………………………………………….. 2…………………………………………….. 3…………………………………. Ring Announcer: Here are your winners, So Cal Val and the Womens Champion…Trish Stratus!!! (Trish and Val hug and celebrate their win) Gene: Well, a big, easy win for Trish Stratus and So Cal Val. Jesse: A great divas match there. The future is bright for the Womens Division…also the future is looking hot! SEGMENT #5 – Trish Stratus grabs the microphone and challenges Lacey to a match for both TPG Diva Titles. Lacey accepts the challenge and a catfight ensues. MATCH #6 – Leviathon vs. The Inquisitor (“Bodies” by Drowning Pool rocks the arena as the purple lights engulf the Budweiser Events Center. A set of fireworks erupt and white smoke takes the entrance ramp as “The Insurance Policy” Leviathon slowly walks down the ring.) Ring Announcer: The following is an Internation Championship First Round match, introducing first, from the United Kingdom…..LEVIATHONNNN!! Gene: Here he comes. The very mysterious ‘Insurance Policy’ but he will have some tough ‘Insurance’ ahead of him in The Inquistor. (Leviathon walks up the steps and enters the ring. Suddenly “Master of Puppets” by Mettalica hits and the lights go out. A red smoke surrounds the ramp and ring. The Inquistor slowly walks out and down the ring. He walks up the steps and raises his arms slowly. He brings them down and lightning strikes. It sends Gene and Jesse shouting “WHOA” at the same time. The lights come on, the smoke clears, and the bell rings.) The two lock up and Leviathon takes the early advantage with a snapmare followed up with a sleeper hold. The Inquistor slowly gets up and begins to elbow out. He tosses him into the ropes and hits a shoulder block. Leviathon gets up and runs into a hip toss. The Inquisition continues with a few clubs to the back and sets up for the Hopping Tombstone. Gene: Here it is. It’s over now. The Inquistor hits it?? Hooks the leg. 1…………………………………. 2…………………………. Kickout. Gene: How did Leviathon kick out of that? Jesse: I have no clue but that was incredible. The Inquistor has a confused look on his face but lifts up Leviathon. He hits a few left hands and then connects with a Modified Neckbreaker. The Inquistor begins to choke ‘The Insurance Policy’ with his boot in the corner. The ref breaks it up and then The Inquistor makes another cover. 1……………………………………….. Kickout. Gene: Quick kickout there. The Inquistor tosses Leviathon into the corner and follows with a clothesline. Inquistor does the same in the opposite corner and and follows up with a backbreaker. Inquistor this time doesn’t go for the pin attempt but lifts him up and rams his head into the turnbuckle. Leviathon responds with a thumb to the eye but The Inquistor responds with a big uppercut and he sets up for another Hopping Tombstone. Jesse: Is it time again?? This time, however, Leviathon is able to wriggle out and land a back suplex. Both men are down and but The Inquistor pops back up and is livid. He hits a few stiff right hands and Leviathon slowly starts to bleed. The Inquistor sees this and rams Leviathon’s head into the turnbuckle a few more times. Leviathon is reeling and The Inquistor hits a big ddt. Again, he doesn’t cover and lifts him up. Gene: Smart more here. Not giving Leviathon any time to rest. The Inquistor hits a few more left hands at the damaged area of his opponents head. He lifts up Leviathon by the throat, and just like Eternal, slams his down with a big Chokeslam. Gene: Leviathon just went STRAIGHT……TO…….HELLLLLL!!! The Inquistor signals that the match is done and lifts up a Leviathon who can barely stand. He lifts him up for another Hopping Tombstone, and drops him right onto the center of the mat. Jesse: Another Hopping Tombstone. The Inquistor is advancing. The Inquisor covers his beaten opponent…. 1……………………………….. 2………………………………….. 3…………………………………. Ring Announcer: Here is your winner………..THE INQUISTORRRRR!! Gene: There it is. The Inquistor dominates, scores a Chokeslam and two Hopping Tombstones, the second one getting the pinfall. Jesse: Boy this tournament is looking great and the second round will be even better. (The Inquistor walks slowly to the back as Referees try and help up Leviathon.) SEGMENT #6 – Steve Hart cuts an in-ring promo on his opponent, Scott Steiner. MATCH #7 – Steve Hart def. Scott Steiner with help from Inquisitor SEGMENT #7 – Terri Runnels gets a backstage interview with Eternal on his thoughts on the tournament and his upcoming match. MAIN EVENT – Eternal vs. Matt McCoy (The lights turn out as the arena turns silent and the last rights are recited on the PA. Flames then light up the ring turnbuckles and provide the only light throughout the arena Eternal slowly emerges from the curtain and then walks down to the ring, slowly in a weird trance, gazing at all the fans, like they are freaks of nature. He then climbs into the ring, over the ropes and in the center of the ring, pulling at his hair, and scratching at his arms.) Ring Announcer: The following contest is a first round match in the International Championship Tournament. In the ring, from Parts Unknown, weight in at two hundred and fifty pounds.…Eternallllll!! Gene: Here is a very mysterious man who won his debut in the Battle Royal. But now, he as a greater opportunity to win the International Championship. (Just as Gene finishes, Matt McCoy enters through the curtains and waves at the crowd. The crowd cheers as Matt runs down to the ring and stands on the turnbuckle. He taunts the crowd as the ref rings the bell.) Gene: Here we go! McCoy charges and runs into a big sidewalk slam from Eternal. Jesse: What a move there. That HAD to hurt. Eternal lifts up McCoy and hits a few overhand chops and then follows with short arm clothesline that sends McCoy to the outside. Eternal slowly follows and tosses McCoy into the guard rail. The crowd groans at the noise of McCoy’s back colliding with the rail. Gene: Great move there. That will soften up the back for the finishing Chokeslam. Eternal rolls McCoy back into the ring and hooks the leg. 1……………………………… 2…………………………… Kickout. Gene: Eternal almost won there. McCoy needs to turn this around. Eternal slowly picks up McCoy and tosses him into the turnbuckle. He hits a tilt-a-whirl slam. Eternal slowly continues the assault with a few right hands and hits a HUGE Oklahoma Slam. Again, Eternal hooks the leg. 1………………………………. 2………………………………. Kickout. Jesse: Another near fall. McCoy is done for. Eternal slowly lifts up McCoy and hits a few over hand chops. He tosses McCoy into the corner and follows with a clothesline. McCoy bounces up and Eternal lifts him up for Eternal Damnation but McCoy countered into a big ddt. Gene: What a counter from Matt McCoy. Eternal was about to win it. The ref begins his count to 10. 1………………. 2……………….. 3………………. 4…………………. 5………………….. Both men slowly get up and McCoy takes the advantage with a few left hands and a few chops. He runs into Eternal and knocks him back with a clothesline. McCoy does it again, and a third time and he finally knocks him down. McCoy slowly ascends to the turnbuckle. Gene: What’s he doing? Jesse: He isn’t a high flyer so what is he doing up there? Gene: This could cost him the match. Eternal gets up and McCoy flies off the top but Eternal catches his throat and slams him down! Gene: ETERNAL DAMN-NATION!!!!! Jesse: What a Chokeslam from Eternal! Eternal slowly covers McCoy… 1……………………………………………. 2…………………………………………….. 3……………………………………… (Eternal grabs his hair and stomps up and down, letting out anger.) Ring Announcer: Here is your winner………….ETERNALLLLL! Gene: He advances. Domination from Eternal in this match. Jesse: Is this a foreshadowing for things to come? SEGMENT #8 – Clarke Andrews comes to the ring and announces that TPG International Title match at the Golden Oppurtunity PPV will be a Ladder Match. --------------------------------------------------------- A special thanks to Andy and Fallen Angel for the parts of this weeks show that are up.
