Underground Network Oct 10; Milw WI, Neta/Brandi & Chaos/Dozer Doc — Posted: Oct 9 2005, 10:30 PM Underground Network Milwaukee, WI The Bradley Center October 10, 2005 Seg 9 – Hardcore Champion?! The feed breaks into the programming. The man, holding the Hardcore Championship in front of his face, drops it. Randy Walker looks into the camera. Walker: You see, I’ve been a part of this program for a while now, and it seems I’ve been forgotten. Hell it comes down to the point where I have commandere my own time. Well, as of next week, there will be no more of this bullshit. I am taking my spot on Underground by force, if I have to. I have spoken to Underground management, oh, let’s say not recently! Apparently you have no interest in having me on your damned show. Did you forget I was on your roster? Come on, someone tell me. He looks down at his title, and spits on it. Walker: This is what I think of PWE and it’s championships. Apparently being the TPG Hardcore Champion has held me back. Well, maybe, just maybe not being a part of PWE would be a wise move, huh? You think? I’ve been paying attention to what has happened here tonight on Underground. And, it’s needless to say that I have a match signed for myself next week. He looks back down at the Hardcore Championship. He lifts it. Walker: Well, I’m obviously not in TPG anymore, am I? I’m not really wanted on Underground. Revenge hasn’t called. Maybe someone else has a need for a Hardcore Champion. Regardless of any of that, next week, I am going to be on UG. I will be taking on this Holden character. And, who knows, maybe it will be for this very Hardcore Championship! Neta v Brandi SILVER CROWN ROUND ROBIN MATCH: Tom "Da bomb" Brandi vs. Neta Announcer: Our next match is set for one fall. Introducing first, he weighs in a 289 lbs, from Shenandoah, PA…This is Neta! ["Set It Off" by P.O.D hits the arena and the fans go wild. Neta makes his way through the curtain as like in Randy Orton's entrance, pyros come down from above as Neta raises his arms out in the air. He then makes his way to the ring calmly, generic style and prepares for his match.] Announcer: And his opponent, weighing in at 231 from Philadelphia, PA…Tom Brandi! [The lights go dark and then a swarm of pink and purple strobe lights hit the crowd. Immediately followed by the song "Its not unusual" by Tom Jones hitting the PA System. Five women in evening gowns step down the ring entrance as Tom Brandi finally makes his appearance. He is followed by personal Errand boy, Nikolai Volkoff. As he walks down the ring ramp he gives each women a rose and a kiss on the cheek. The last woman on the ring ramp cant wait and passes out moments before Brandi's embrace. Brandi, proudly, arrogantly struts to the ring. Nikolai is in front of him, cleaning each square inch of ground in front of Brandi so as to avoid getting Tom Brandi's feet dirty. At this point the crowd is swarmed by pink confetti swallowing the arena. A kid in the front row is crying while trying to eat an ice cream, as Brandi passes him by, he takes his ice cream and enjoys his frozen treat as he steps through the ropes into the ring, waiting for yet another match.] Monsoon: Brain, this is a rematch of a few weeks ago. Heenan: I know, Neta better hope for a different result. [Brandi marches to the center of the ring. Neta greets him with a VICIOUS right hand that rocks Brandi back into the ropes. Neta marches over and hurls Brandi to the other side of the ring. Brandi bounces off the ropes, but Neta PLANTS him with a monstrous clothesline. Brandi crashes to the canvas, but springs right back to his feet. Neta reaches down and scoops up Brandi, lifting him over his head in a military style press.] Monsoon: What a display of strength by the Shenandoah Native! [Neta delays for a moment then drops Brandi straight down. Brandi crashes into the canvas, making a sickening thud as he hits the mat. Brandi rolls over for a moment in pain, as Neta refuses to let up. He gives Brandi a kick for good measure while on the canvas, then falls down to get the pin… 1 KICKOUT! Monsoon: WAY! Too soon. [Neta doesn’t pause. He pulls Brandi up to his feet, and grabs him by the hair. He begins to talk smack. He releases Brandi and goes for another monstrous clothesline, however Brandi is able to duck out of the way! DROPKICK! Neta crashes to the canvas. He slowly makes his way to his feet, and when he does, Brandi hurls him to the other side of the ring. BACK BODY DROP! Neta bounces off the canvas.] Monsoon: The Prettiest Man In Wrestling is really starting to gain momentum here! [Neta slowly makes his way to his feet, and when he does, Brandi gives him a shot to the gut…. SPINNING NECK BREAKER!!! Monsoon: This may be it! 1 2 KICKOUT! [Brandi can’t believe it. He shakes his head for a moment, but stays on the assault. He pulls Neta to his feet, but Neta explodes with a clothesline, sending Brandi crashing into the turnbuckle. Brandi staggers forward, Neta scoops him up, and spins around, driving him into the canvas with a Spinebuster.] Monsoon: And just like that, Neta regains control of this match up! [Neta seems groggy, but fights to get to his feet. He reaches his feet, and grabs Brandi by the hair, pulling him to his feet. Neta hooks Brandi in a suplex position and lifts him over his head. He delays for a good long while, letting all the blood drain to the head of Neta…. Waiting…. Waiting… All of a sudden Neta’s right knee buckles! He releases the hold and drops to one knee. Brandi takes advanatage of this, giving a kick to the gut then throwing Neta’s head between his legs… BRANDI GLASS!!!! 1 2 3!!! Announcer: Here is your winner…TOM BRANDI!! [Brandi rolls out of the ring, and raises his arm in victory.] Monsoon: What a win by Brandi! Heenan: True, but Neta is grabbing at his knee. He may have hurt it seriously. [The scene switches back to the ring, where as what Heenan said is true, Neta is grabbing his knee, and from the expression on his face, it doesn’t look good. Refs soon slide into the ring, to check on Neta.] Monsoon: If he re-injured that knee, we may not see Neta for a few weeks. Heenan: True. Seg 10 - The next Champ? [As we fade in, we find Dozer sitting in his locker preparing for his World Title Match. As he tapes his wrist, he looks back on his PWE career. The Dream title sits next to him on the bench. He then looks to his other side, where the bench is empty.] Dozer: That will all change tonight. After all this time, I will finally rise to the top. Finally I will the man. After tonight, everyone will have to acknowledge that I am just as good as I always said I was. I Will do what no one else in the world has ever been able to do. I will take the World Title from Chaos. Voice: Maybe you will. Looks like after all this time, your backstabbing just might finally pay off. [Dozer turns to see a very familiar face standing in the doorway. ] Tex Graham: How long has it been for you Dozer? Two years almost? Hell, the last time that you were this close to the belt.. well, come to think of it, you have never been this close to the belt. Dozer: What are you doing here old man? Tex: Old Man? Why do you people keep calling me that? Hell, I’m only 29 years old. I have a lot of years left ahead of me. And as far as why I am here, lets just say I am a little concerned about your match tonight. Dozer: What… Are you afraid that I might end the career of your little pal Chaos? Tex: Me worried about Chaos? Not in the least. What I am worried about is what it would mean for this company if God for bid you walked out of this building tonight with the World Heavyweight title around your waist. Dozer: It would mean that finally, for the first time ever, Underground has a champion that is… [Tex interrupts] Tex: A Complete Disgrace. [Dozer becomes irate with Tex.] Dozer: What the hell are you talking about? Tex: I am talking about you. I am talking the fact that I personally don’t think you deserve to hold that title. Dozer: And why is that? Tex: Because that title has a history. A legacy. Men like Kurt Angle, ZerO, Myself, Deuce Jackson, and Chaos have all given everything they had to hold that title with honor and dignity. And each and one of those men got that title the hard way. They Earned it. They deserved it. Dozer: I EARNED IT TOO!! Tex: HOW? Tell me what you ever did to earn that title? You attacked your friends and turned you back on them. You used a sledgehammer to put my father in the hospital? Face it, you ignorant S.O.B., you never did a damn thing to earn that belt, or even a shot at it. I see you sitting back here talking about how this is your time. How this is your night. Hell, the only reason you are even getting this match tonight is because Lennex handed you the shot on a silver platter. You didn’t do a damn thing to earn it. Backstabbing and lobbying isn’t what makes you a champion you jack ass. Its like Ric flair always said, the belt does not make the man. The man makes the belt. And if you win tonight, that belt will lose everything that it once stood for. Dozer: Well then I guess you better get ready for the meaning of that belt to change. Tex: You know what, Dozer. In a way, I hope you do win that belt tonight. Because I know first hand that if you do, you will all of a sudden have a very large bulls eye painted right on you chest, and mark my words, people will start taking shots at you faster than you will be able to turn around. Dozer: I think I can take care of myself. Tex: You have no idea what you are in for. But I do. And I will enjoy every moment of it. You see, Dozer, If you do manage to win tonight, before all is said and done all the shit that you have pulled for the last year will all of a sudden come back on you ten fold. And personally, I will sit back and laugh every time it does. [Fade.] Seg 11 - The Champs arrive Backstage, two men sit in front of the television. They’re in a secluded part of the arena. You can see gold belts laying over their shoulders. The camera pans around, to get a look at their faces. The Mask is one of the men. Mask: Let me introduce you to a man that you will soon come to know very, very well. This man is known as Michael Hopkins. He is the World Heavyweight Champion. The UCW Champion that is. What we’re doing here tonight, as you already know, is watching YOUR champion. Alex Miller. Oh, this thing on my shoulder? With that, The Mask taps the championship belt, and looks at it as the camera zooms in on it. UCW North American Championship. Mask: Well, it would be considered the equivalent of your Silver Crown Championship, which as it seems, you have issues even getting someone to want to be the champion. As Mask begins to continue, Hopkins stops him. Hopkins: I don’t have to say much. The reason we’ve come here is simple, and as Mr. Lennex elaborates, everything will become crystal clear to everyone. Let me start with Alex Miller. I’ve watched you for the past four months. I’ve seen a man with a conviction unlike that of anyone I’ve seen in a long time. With that said, it matters not to me who you are, or what you stand for. You claim to be something that you are not. The World Champion. I am that man. Sure, you hold a version of a title that your company holds dear. But as you well know, it’s now my company as well. Well, sort of. Hopkins pulls his belt off his shoulder, and looks at it, before dropping it to his lap. Hopkins: Chaos, you have had a solid run. But, it’s not legitimate. Never has been, and even if by some longshot chance, you do get beat by Dozer, you will lose it. But it won’t stop you from being my target. One Million Dollars. That’s all that needs to be said. Tonight, win or lose, Chaos, your title reign ends. Tonight, Chaos, your career will end. *Main Event* PWE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH © Chaos vs Bulldozer Announcer: Our next match is set for one fall, and is for the PWE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP!!!! Making his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 350 pounds, from Toronto Ontario Canada…he is BULLDOZER!! [The lights go black as we see and hear… This is the BULL DO ZER Suddenly we here concert smashing as the stage is filled with dust. “Bulldozer” blares over the speakers as Bulldozer walks out from behind the back. Bulldozer , who is carrying his sledgehammer. Bulldozer struts down the ramp way making obscene jesters to the fans, threatening them with his sledge...spitting on them whatever. Bulldozer climbs over the top rope and enters the ring. Bulldozer stands in the center of the ring as he lifts his sledgehammer over his head. Bulldozer starts to slam the sledgehammer down as yellow fireworks shoot from the stage as Bulldozer hits the sledgehammer on the canvas the fireworks hit each ring post as the turnbuckles light in a yellow blaze. Bulldozer throws his sledgehammer onto the floor as he awaits for the champion’s arrival.] Announcer: And now, making his way to the ring at this time, he stands 6’8 and now resides in Oslo, Norway…ladies and gentlemen this is the PWE WORLD CHAMPION…CHAOS!!! [The lights go out and "Shoot to Thrill " by ACDC hits the loudspeakers. A red searchlight circles through the arena and finally focuses on the rampway, where Chaos comes up on a platform surrounded by fire.] Monsoon: Brain, Chaos may very well go down, as the fightingest champion on the UnderGround Roster is Lennex has his way. Heenan: If Lennex had his way, Chaos would just go down. [The Champion makes his way down to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope, then proceeds to climb to the middle rope, raising his World Title Belt above his head.] Monsoon: This may prove to be the biggest test thus far for Alex Miller. DING-DING-DING [The two men march to the center of the ring. Camera flashes begin to fill the arena, as the two large men stand face to face, jawing at one another.] Heenan: This in intense! [Chaos lifts back to land the first punch, but Bulldozer dives forward with this head, and delivers a head butt right to the jaw of the champion. Chaos staggers backwards into the ropes, a bit dazed from this initial hit.] Heenan: NOW THAT is the way to use your head! [Bulldozer walks over to the ropes and gives the champion a straight shot to the face.] Monsoon: I do believe, no one in this bussiness delivers that right hand like Bulldozer. Heenan: Chaos won’t argue with you right now. [Bulldozer leans Chaos back into the ropes, then throws the person away like an Irish whip but pulls them back into a spear! Chaos rolls over, grasping his chest after the move.] Monsoon: Bulldozer DRILLS the champion with a monstrous spear. [Bulldozer springs up to his feet, as boos pour down upon the ring. Dozer seems to soak up this reaction, as he walks over to the ropes. He begins to jaw with a fan at ringside, as he climbs to the middle rope. Bulldozer turns around and raises his arms outward soaking up the negative reaction… BODY SPLASH FROM THE MIDDLE ROPE! Heenan: That is three hundred and FIFTY POUNDS coming off the middle rope! Monsoon: This one could be over right here brain…. 1 2 CHAOS GETS A SHOULDER UP! [The crowd roars, as Chaos is able to throw his shoulder off the canvas at the very last moment. Bulldozer lowers his head in disappointment, thinking that may have been enough to finish off the champion. Bulldozer doesn’t dwell long, as he climbs to his feet. He soon pulls the champion to his feet, by the hair of his head. Dozer lifts up Chaos, and drops him with a Manhattan Drop! Chaos staggers for a moment, as Dozer falls back into the ropes, then dives through the air, connecting with a hard clothesline!] Monsoon: You know Brain, I am very impressed with the challenger to this point. [Bulldozer mounts the champion, and begins to unload on him with rights and lefts to the grill of Alex Miller. The champion can do nothing but cover up, as Bulldozer begins to display his power with rights and lefts to the face of Chaos. Bulldozer finally unmounts the champion, pulling him to his feet. Bulldozer connects with a knee to the gut, then connecting with a PUMP HANDLE SLAM! Monsoon: Bulldozer’s Nothing’s Left 1 Heenan: How ironic…because Chaos may not have “Nothing Left” after that move… 2 CHAOS GETS A FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE!!!! [Bulldozer believes he won! He jumps to his feet and throws his hands up in the air. He marches over to the ropes and begins to jaw with the fans at ringside, pointing to his chest, and proclaiming to be the champion. The ref moves over and tells him the count was only a two. Dozer can’t believe it. He says he watched the three count, but the ref points out the foot on the bottom rope. Dozer gives a kick to the leg of Chaos in frustration, knocking the foot off the rope. The ref warns Dozer, who ignores the ref, giving him a shove. ] Monsoon: I have admit, I didn’t see the foot either. [Dozer rubs his eyes for a minute, lost of what to do to put away the champion. Finally, Dozer realizes this is the opportunity to win the championship, so he steps between the ropes and out onto the apron. He then walks over to the turnbuckle and climbs to the top rope.] Heenan: Monsoon, this is three hundred and FIFTY POUNDS coming off that top rope! Monsoon: He Bulldozer connects with this “Steam Roller” it is over. [Bulldozer climbs to the top rope, and once again begins to jaw with the fans at ringside. He then climbs to the top rope, and extends his hands once more outwardly, soaking up the negative crowd reaction. However this reaction soon changes to cheers, as Chaos springs up to his feet, and drives his body into the ropes. This action causes Bulldozer to lose his balance, and crash into the top turnbuckle!] Monsoon: That could be the break the champion needs! [Chaos slowly makes his way over to the ropes. He climbs onto the middle rope, and locks Bulldozer in a suplex position... DOUBLE OR NOTHING!!! [Chaos connects with a T-Bone suplex off the top rope! Bulldozer crashes into the canvas, as does the champion however!] Monsoon: What a move! Heenan: THIS IS WHAT MAKES THE UG GREAT! Monsoon: That it is. Two of the greatest superstars in our industry, giving these fans everything they have, for the World Championship. [Neither man moves.] Monsoon: Like last week, this could be a match of who gets to their feet first. [Bulldozer begins to move, as the champion is still motionless on the canvas.] Heenan: You know Monsoon, this match could headline a pay per view. We are damn lucky to see this match on Underground. Monsoon: Once again, this is what makes the Underground what it is. [Bulldozer finally makes it to his feet. He begins to stagger towards the champion. As he reaches down to pick up Chaos, the champion rolls onto his feet…. Monsoon: Chaos was playing possum…. MASSIVE SPINEBUSTER!! Monsoon: MY GOD THE RING SHOOK ON THAT ONE! [Chaos now springs to his feet, totally energized by the crowd’s reaction. Chaos brings Bulldozer up to his feet. Chaos gives a kick to the gut….PUMP HANDLE SLAM!] Monsoon: Chaos returns the Pump Handle Slam! [Chaos pulls Bulldozer back up to his feet, and delivers a knife edged chop. Bulldozer falls into the ropes. Chaos hurls Bulldozer across the ring…. AND RIGHT INTO THE REF!! The ref flies through the air, and crashes onto the canvas. Bulldozer staggers around after the collision… GAME OVER!!! Monsoon: This one is over!!! 1 2 3 4 5 6 7!!! No REF!!! [Chaos looks down in total disbelief. He crawls over to the ref and tries to wake him up. Chaos climbs to his feet, and begins to shake the ref, trying to revive him.] Monsoon: Chaos has to get the ref up. He has this match up won. Heenan: Tough break for the champion. [Bulldozer rolls over and rolls over onto the apron.] Monsoon: You can tell Bulldozer is still feeling the effects of that move. Heenan: Yeah, I’m still not too sure if Chaos couldn’t get the pin now… [Bulldozer climbs to his feet and spins around the champion… WACK! SLEDGEHAMMER TO THE GRILL!!! Chaos crashes to the canvas, as Bulldozer drops to his knees in exhaustion. He tosses the hammer out of the ring.] Monsoon: WHAT THE HELL!!!! [The ref begins to make his way to his feet. He sees Chaos flat on the canvas, as Bulldozer is climbing to his feet.] Monsoon: This is a travesty! [Dozer grabs Chaos by the head and throws his head between his legs… The Demolisher!!!!!!! 1 Monsoon: No! Don’t let it end like this! 2 Monsoon: Come on! Not like this! 3! Announcer: Here is your winner….AND NEWWWWW WORLD CHAMPION…BULLDOZER!!!! [‘Bulldozer” starts up as the ring bell sounds. The crowd erupts into a chorus of boos as the ref hands Bulldozer the World Title. Dozer is standing on his knees, above the former champion, who’s face is a crimson mask from that shot from the sledgehammer. Bulldozer clutches the World’s Championship.] Heenan: The PWE has a new WORLD CHAMPION!!! Seg 12: An attempt at the bounty. [Bulldozer drops the World Title to the canvas. He stands up and looks down with an evil glare in his eye. He turns his head and spots the sledgehammer. He walks over to the sledgehammer and picks it up.] Monsoon: Oh no! I don’t like the looks of this. [The ref rushes over to stop Dozer, but the big man just shoves the ref to the canvas. Dozer grabs the sledgehammer and chuckles evilly, as he glares at the sledgehammer with almost a lustful look in his eye. He walks over to Chaos, who is turned over, still totally disoriented from that shot to the head. Bulldozer lifts the sledgehammer over his head and RAMS IT INTO THE ARM OF CHAOS! Monsoon: OH MY GOD! QUIT IT! FOR THE LOVE OF GOD! STOP! [Dozer repeatedly drives the sledgehammer into the left arm of Chaos over and over, , as the former champion can do nothing to stop the assault. ] Monsoon: He is going to end the man’s career dammit! Heenan: That is what Lennex wants, isn’t it? Monsoon: I don’t give a damn. Not like this! [Dozer gives one final powerful shot from the sledgehammer into the arm of Chaos, however Dozer is frozen in shock as you hear… ”SLEEP THIS TIME TOOOOONIGHHHT” [The screaming vocals hit and the arena fills with the sound of “Sleepwalking” by Blindside. Green strobes illuminate throughout the arena, and smoke fills the stage, aisle, and ring. Mortis steps out from behind the curtain and makes his way down the aisle.] Monsoon: MORTIS! MORTIS HAS ARRIVED!!! [He stops at the bottom of the ramp, and places his jester staff into the ground, and on cue, lightning strikes the stage behind him. Dozer looks on in total shock. Chaos rolls out of the ring, and falls the floor, in front of Mortis. His face a crimson mask, his arm shattered, as he crawls towards his best friend. Mortis removes his gear under a lone spotlight now and looks down at Chaos. Mortis then slowly raises his head up towards the ring and locks eyes with Dozer. The look on Mortis’ face is one of pure rage. The look on Dozer’s face…pure fear.] Monsoon: May god have mercy on your soul Bulldozer … Your GOD DAMN SOUL! Heenan: I don’t know if Bulldozer is religious, but if he does worship a god, he better call in a favor. [Mortis explodes under the bottom rope and slides into the ring. The lights come back up. Bulldozer goes to delivers a shot from that sledgehammer to Mortis, however Mortis is able to duck out of the way. Mortis delivers a shot right to the grill of Bulldozer, who drops the sledgehammer! Mortis gives another shot to the face of Bulldozer, who is totally disoriented. Mortis falls back into the ropes, soaring through the air, connecting with a FLYING FOREARM SHOT! Bulldozer crashes to the canvas. Mortis crawls over to Bulldozer and begins to whale away at the face of Bulldozer with right and lefts.] Heenan: I promise you Monsoon, this isn’t how Bulldozer, or Lennex envisioned the first few moments after Chaos lost his title. Monsoon: I can promise you it wasn’t the way Bulldozer had envisioned. [Finally UnderGround officials pour out from the backstage area and fill the ring. The physically remove Mortis from a top of the new PWE World Champion. The arena begins to fill with boo birds, however that is quickly cut off by.. SO-SO-SO-Scandalist! [Staci Kiebler walks out onto the entrance ramp as all the attention is directed towards her. Mortis is still trying to free himself, but calms down as she begins to speak.] Kiebler: Congratulations Bulldozer. You have accomplished what John Lennex has wanted to do since the moment he stepped foot into this place. You took the title off of Alex Miller… [Boo.] Kiebler: And your reward? The main event spot at Carnage… [Bulldozer seems proud of his actions, as he chest swells. A small chant of “Go To Hell” starts up.] Kiebler: and you will be defending your title against none other then Chaos’ best friend, and tag team partner…THE PRINCE OF DARKNESS…MORTIS!!! [Bulldozer gives a classic look of shock and disbelief. He slowly turns his head back to Mortis, just in time to see the number one contender flying through the air, over the refs, landing on top of the World’s Champion. Mortis begins to whale away at Bulldozer as the refs attempt to break them up, but have little success in doing so.] Segment 13: A Bounty Collected. (Chaos is seen clawing his way into the backstage area, when he reaches in front of him and grabs hold of a leg. One that’s attached to a nice pair of newly shined dress shoes. He looks up, and there’s Lennex.) Lennex: Are we having a problem, Alex? Looks like you’re not feeling to well. Almost like you’ve dropped about 25 pounds. Where is your title? (Chaos attempts to pull himself up. Lennex takes his other foot, and pushes him down.) Lennex: You know, Alex, I used to do my own dirty work. But, not anymore. Let me introduce you to a couple of guys you’re going to need to know. (Two men appear from behind the curtain. One is The Mask, with the UCW North American championship belt he used last week. The other, Michael Hopkins, his UCW Championship around his waist. He unbuttons it, and hands it to Lennex.) Lennex: Alex, first, before anything, the man to my right, he’s the North American Champion, you know him as The Mask. This other man, I know you’re not familiar with him. This is the UCW World Champion, Michael Hopkins. I purchased UCW for a reason. As much as I’d like to say you’re the only reason…you’re not. But, I’ll let you rot in that for a bit while you’re on your vacation. (Chaos looks up with a questioning look on his face. Lennex smiles, and motions to Mask & Hopkins…) Lennex: Finish him. (The Mask grabs Chaos by the hurt arm, and pulls him up and latches Chaos’ arms with his own as Hopkins lets loose with a flurry of punches, until Chaos is having issues breathing. The Mask shoves Chaos into the arms of Hopkins, and he with quick motion, hits a thunderous belly to belly.) Lennex: I was going to save this until Carnage, but, you’re not the champ any more, I have no reason to wait. (Hopkins takes Chaos’ good arm and places him in an arm bar, while The Mask grabs the bad one and sends it crashing into the concrete wall five times.) Lennex: See, Chaos, you could have taken the easy route all along. But, no, you had to choose the hard route. Now, you’re going to be hurt, and hurt badly. Honestly, I prefer it this way. (Hopkins lets go and pulls Chaos up, and motions to The Mask that he has it. He whips Chaos head first into the wall, and then quickly grabs the injured arm, and runs with it, and snaps it around a steel pole. Chaos hits the ground, as blood begins to flow from the arm.) Lennex: Oh, Alex, a compound fracture….man, that’s going to take quite some time to heal. Are you okay? (The camera focuses in on the fallen former champion. Clutching at the arm, looking at the bone that has punctured the skin, then at Lennex with hatred.) Lennex: Like I care anyway, right? Hopkins..Mask, well done. Very well done. I believe I owe you some money. (The picture stays with Chaos, and the blood pouring, as two medics rush in, and the picture begins to fade.)
