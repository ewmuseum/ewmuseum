Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Chad Dupree Memorial Cup is a recurring tournament held in memory of Chad Dupree, a roleplayer and federation owner. It kept no roster and no home of its own. Two hosts staged it, each on its own platform and each as a tournament of its own: EUWC, on its board and pay-per-view cards, from 2004, and TEAM Wrestling, on its forum at FWrestling.com, from 2006. EUWC sent a team of its own to TEAM’s 2006 Cup; beyond that, the name and the dedication, the pages do not say how the two series relate.

EUWC’s own tournament history lists four winners: Jordan Lockhart over STEALTH in 2004, Rocko Daymon over Michael Lennox in 2005, Nero over Violent K in 2006 and STEALTH over Rocko Daymon in 2007. The 2005 running was announced on EUWC’s board on 29 July 2005 as the second annual, first as an eight-man bracket with all but two places for wrestlers from fellow federations of the WWC, then as sixteen, with competitors from the BWA, SOC and WCCW named beside EUWC’s own, its roleplay posted on the WWC’s forums, its first round on EUWC’s Main Frame and its later rounds at the Cup’s own pay-per-view. The 2006 running (15 October 2006, University Arena, Albuquerque) set four wrestlers from each of EUWC’s two brands, Main Frame and Blackout, against one another in an eight-man bracket. The 2007 running (28 October 2007, United Center, Chicago) was a sixteen-man single-elimination tournament for the vacant EUWC World Heavyweight Championship, and STEALTH’s win made him champion.

TEAM Wrestling and The Mark e-fed newsmagazine announced their own Dupree Cup on 30 July 2006 as a World Cup-style tournament in which teams of four roleplayers represented federations, for a trophy named in his memory that had been donated to TEAM as the prize of its league season. Twelve teams, EUWC’s among them, were drawn into three groups of four; the roleplay was free-form, and a panel of three judged it. New Era of Wrestling (HAL, MWG, Mr. Entertainment and Chaos) beat Message Board Entertainment two matches to one in the final of 9 December 2006, which TEAM billed as the inaugural Dupree Cup; HAL and The Spoiler shared the Most Valuable Wrestler award. TEAM ran it again in 2007, billed as the second year, with stables allowed to send teams of their own. The A-List, entered as Danny Ferguson, Chandler Tsonda, The Illustrious Face-Eater and Mike Wade, beat A1E in a WarGames final at SuperShow VII, posted on 6 February 2008 and billed as first aired on 23 December 2007; Ferguson’s bodyguard Dametreyus Fuquieawytas was in the cage as the team’s fourth man, and Danny Ferguson took the Rob Sampson Memorial Most Valuable Wrestler Trophy.

The hosts number the Cup differently. EUWC’s pay-per-view of 15 October 2006 calls itself the third annual Chad Dupree Memorial Cup, and TEAM’s final of 9 December 2006 calls its own the first; TEAM’s announcement of 25 July 2007 speaks of the Cup coming back “for a second year.” EUWC’s event history heads both its 2006 and its 2007 sections “3rd Annual,” while its card for 28 October 2007 reads “4th Annual.” Each numbering stands as its page gives it. No later staging or closing notice survives; TEAM’s 2007 final is the last the pages show.
