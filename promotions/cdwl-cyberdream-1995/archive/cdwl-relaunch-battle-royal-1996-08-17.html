CDWL — Battle Royal (1996 relaunch)

"CDWL Action, check it out!"

17 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Cyber Dream Wrestling League just held it's first battle royal to determine it's champion. This is a small but tough league. Check out the battle royal below, and get your wrestler in on the action. Mail me at [detail withheld], and I'll send you an application form, a handbook, and CDWL bulletin #1 and your man will be wrestling with these nasty competitors in no time

---------------------------- Welcome to the Cyberdome, home of all Cyber Dream Wrestling League matches. Eleven tough wrestlers signed up by the deadline for the battle royal, and as they make their way towards the ring, I can tell this is going to be an all out Brawl! Some of these wrestlers were around for the first CDWL a year ago, and some are complete rookies to the league. Each one just as tough as the next though it seems, it's really anyone's guess. And the bell sounds...

The Prince of Darkness is the first one over the ropes in this battle royal. Steve Gallagher just gave him a nasty clothesline, dropping him to the floor. On the other end of the ring, look at 'The Shroud' Anthony Alred!! He's displaying his awesome strength here. He's just picked up Omega with one hand. Oh my god!! Look at this, he's now picking up The Barbarian with the other! He's got about 500 pounds off the ground, that's amazing. Alred takes a few running steps and dumps them onto the floor. Wait a minute, The Rebel just snuck up from behind of a posing Alred and dumped him to the floor. He has hit his shoulder pretty hard. Officials are calling for a stretcher. The remaining seven men are battling it out now. Robski has just body slammed Destroyer! And again, now for a third time. Robski is going to the top rope, but Thunderstrike came out of nowhere and kicked Robski off the rope and onto the floor.

This leaves six men now. Destroyer and White Tiger are talking in the far corner — these two know each other from the last time CDWL was running — and both were strong forces. They call The Rebel and Steve Gallagher over for some assistance to dump out Destroyer, but it was a trick: White Tiger just slammed The Rebel's and Gallagher's heads together, and Destroyer and White Tiger throw them into the first row of seats. White Tiger drop kicks Destroyer out of the ring right after.

Meanwhile Wild Bill Hans and Thunderstrike are locked up in another corner. Hans clotheslines Thunderstrike and suplexes him. White Tiger gets in on this action and gives Thunderstrike a leg drop. Wild Bill drops Thunderstrike's neck onto the top rope, and White Tiger gives him a DDT. Hans and White Tiger argue over who gets to dump out the unconscious Thunderstrike; White Tiger allows Hans to do it, then DDTs Hans himself, picks him up, punches him, and throws him into the ropes, catching a boot before landing a suplex. White Tiger goes to the top rope and nails both Hans and Thunderstrike with an elbow, then throws Hans over his shoulder and dumps him on the floor. Thunderstrike is knocked out; White Tiger applies the STF and throws him out too.

The winner of the battle royal, and NEEEEEEWWWWWW CDWL heavyweight champion: White Tiger ---------------------------------- There you have it. Our first battle royal. Do you think your wrestler could match strength with 'The Shroud' Anthony Alred? Do you think he could match wits with Destroyer? Or do you think he could match the all around determination to win that our champion White Tiger has? If you think so, why not sign up and see. Don't miss out. See you in the ring!
Brian [detail withheld]
