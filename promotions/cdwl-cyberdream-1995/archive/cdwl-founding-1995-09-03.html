"Join CDWL. New Exciting League"

3 September 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Join the Cyber Dream Wrestling League. This league is brand new, and gives the handlers more of a chance to get involved. In this league, the handler gives 12 moves. The computer randomly assignes point totals for each move. It then matches you against your opponent, and randomly pits move against move. So handlers will have a system of making their wrestlers 'better' as time goes on. In addition there are many special matches (steel cage, texas death, etc) that the computer can factor in. There will be a newsletter distributed to the managers every week or two, with comments from wrestlers (printed by their handlers) sneak attacks, or whatever the handlers want to send me. More surprises are planned, but this league can only be good if enthused handlers get involved. There will be a heavyweight, and tag team champions immediately. For now I'd ask that handlers limit their numbers to 3 wrestlers a piece, but as the numbers grow, we'll work on expanding that. I'm hoping to have a card a week, plus special cards throughout the year. Once a month, there will be rankings listed and emailed to each handler. So get involved

---------------- To get started I need: Your Name: Your email address Wrestlers name: Height: Weight: 10 Moves: (pick any ten moves you want, but try to vary them as a wrestler with all power moves and no set up moves probably won't fair well)
Specialty Ending move: Is the specialty move a pin or submission move? Manager's Name (if applicable) History on your cyber wrestler (as I'd like to send handlers a list of wrestlers in the league and bio's so they can start looking at potential friends/foes)
---------- Again this can be a very exciting league, with many great ideas, but you need to get involved. The first 20 applicants, are in the championship battle royal, so don't delay.
Send applications to [detail withheld] See you in the ring, Brian
