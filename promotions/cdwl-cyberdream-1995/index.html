Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Cyber Dream Wrestling League introduced itself on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 3 September 1995 as a computer-assisted move-matching league: a handler submitted twelve moves for a wrestler, the computer assigned point totals and pitted move against move, and results ran in a newsletter distributed to managers every week or two. It planned a heavyweight champion and a tag team championship from the start, a card a week plus special cards through the year, and entered its first twenty applicants directly into a championship battle royal.

By 9 September 1995 that battle royal had crowned White Tiger the league's first heavyweight champion. The league then went quiet for a year.

On 17 August 1996 the same operator, Brian, relaunched it out of a venue he called the Cyberdome, with a fresh eleven-man battle royal mixing returning names with rookies. The recap ran it fall by fall: the Prince of Darkness went over the top first, clotheslined out by Steve Gallagher; “The Shroud” Anthony Alred hoisted Omega in one hand and the Barbarian in the other before the Rebel dumped him from behind; Robski body-slammed Destroyer three times before Thunderstrike kicked him off the top rope. White Tiger and Destroyer — “these two know each other from the last time CDWL was running,” the recap says — lured the Rebel and Gallagher in for a double clothesline before turning on each other; White Tiger then ran through Wild Bill Hans and a knocked-out Thunderstrike, STF and all, to win the battle royal and reclaim the CDWL heavyweight championship.

Brian closed the recap advertising an application form, a handbook, and “CDWL bulletin #1” to any new handler who wrote in. The league's last dated trace in the surviving record is a fourth results cycle the following month; whether it ran beyond that, and how or when it closed, is not in the record.
