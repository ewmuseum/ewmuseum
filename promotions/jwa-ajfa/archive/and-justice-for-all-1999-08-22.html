And Justice For All A promo is seen, The faces of the 20 competitiors, flash quickly through the screen. Narrator: "Each of these men have taken different paths to their opprutunity, Each of these men have been awarded a chance at controlling their Destiny, their Glory, their Justice. Who will arise, who will persevere, who will defy the odds and become the land's first World Champion. These men must sacrifice in order to gain what they desire most. The screen is filled with darkness, Then another promo starts up,Mass Carnage and Faith appear on a split screen. Narrator: "Two men, One goal, One's a dedicated student, The other is just beginning his journey. Both will encounter pain, One will become Champion, Who will survive what is thrown at them best? He who does will become what he desires, Champion. Once Again, The screen fills with Darkness, As pictures of Oasis winning the TV title appear. Pictures of him flying through the air. Oasis is holding the title in the air. Then the pictures of Gangrene determination on his face, Gangrene hands in the air after a convincing win. Narrator: "One wants what the other has, One wants to keep what he has. The glory is what they crave, Who will grab it?" The scene, shifts to outside Comiskey Park you see fans lining up to get there seats for the big PPV And Justice For All. As they are chanting, "Justice, Justice" THe screen then switches to inside the stadium as people have filled it with a mass of humanity. Signs are everywhere, One says "Steven can i borrow your Butler?" Another says "The Angel has cometh" A Third says "Desire, I'll be your Anything" Fireworks go off in massive making the screen flash white a few times. Biff Wellington: Ladies and Gentleman, Welcome to the beginning of an Era. Welcome to the first JWA sponsored PPV, This is And Justice For All. Tonight we will crown 3 champions, The World, The Trans-Atlantic, and The Hardcore Titles will all find homes tonight. Also Oasis will defend the TV Title against Gangrene. My partner in crime as always is Fast Eddie, Any Thoughts Eddie? Fast Eddie: Well I know my good friend Leon Wallace is in the house and will present the winner of Hardcore Title match, with the title. That match should be pretty interesting as Faith takes on the Angel, Mass Carnage. As with any big event, Anything can and will happen, No different here in the land of Justice. Well Sweet Lou, Lets get to the opening battel Royal to see where our cards are gonna lay. Sweet Lou: Introducing first, already in the ring standing 5 feet 10 inches, weighing in at 220 pounds and a master of the martial arts. He is the Samurai!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The second man into the ring in this rumble, also at 5' 10", tips the scales at 180 pounds. He is Lucky Rich!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Song 15 is played and Lucky Rich is being escorted by his manager Professor of Live. He is wearing an Armani Suit and is beeing jeered hard by the fans at ringside, The Bell rings, Fast Eddie: and We are off, The two men circle each other. A traditional, collar and elbow, A headlock by Samurai quickly reversed by Lucky Rich by running up the turnbucles and turning it into a bull dog. Lucky Rich on the move again, as he bounces off the ropes and delivers a drop kick. Samurai got caught right under the chin. Lucky Rich picks up Samurai and whips him into the ropes and tries a back drop. Samurai front flips over and nails a crunching Crescent Kick. Biff Wellington: That one really connected. Samurai immeadiately tries to throw Rich over the top. Rich goes over but to only the Canvas. Samurai throws a right blocked by Rich, Rich springboards to the top rope and head scissors Samurai to the mat. Rich follows that up with back flip splash, He instinctively goes for the cover. He must have forgot that this is a battle royal. Anyways He picks Samurai up and Bell goes off meaning the next superstar is on his way to the ring. Livin La Vida Loca hits the PA as Starkid heads his way down to the ring. He is in his unique black tights with white stars on the hips. He is sporting a black mask with a star on it. Fast Eddie: Starkid, leaps onto the apron, springboards and nails a dropkick on Samurai. Rich sees this and tries to clothesline Starkid down but he ducks and nails a spinnig heel kick dropping him to the mat, he then does a forward roll into a fist drop on top of Lucky Rich. He gets to his feet and has his feet swept from underneath him. Samurai picks him up and whips him to the turnbuckle. Biff Wellington:Samurai, follows him but Starkid leaps up an avoids the contact then he delivers a tornado DDT to Samurai. He is really excited and celebrates as Rich drop kicks him from behind. Rich sends his face into the turnbuckle. He follows up with a Russian Leg Sweep. He really starts to stomp away on Starkid. Samurai to his feet as well. He joins in as well. Fast Eddie: Yeah four minutes ago they were bashing each others faces in, Now they are a cohesive unit, makes you wonder. Ain't Wrestling Grand? Back to the action as Samurai picks up Starkid and attempts a powerbomb but Starkid punches at the top and knocks Samurai to the mat. This is amazing action and its just getting warmed up. There's the bell. "The Immortals" by Encounter the Ultimate (MK theme) hits the PA, The crowds to his feet as Wildwing makes his entrance. He walks down the ring confidently as his good friend Darren Ridel follows in tow. Biff Wellington:Wildwing steps into the ring almost casually as Luck Rich lands a monkey flip on Starkid, Samuari a little dazed as Wildwing grabs his head and just simply tosses him out, of the ring. Samuarai a little cagey lands on the apron and points to his head. Wildwing sees this and nails a devastaing Sweet Dreams kick sending Samurai to the rail. Fast Eddie: Amazing, Wildwing has already influenced this match. Lucky Rich comes at him and throws a left blocked by Wildwing who picks up Rich and lands his trademark Destruction Driver. Rich is laying there, and out. Wildwing may have been unlucky Thursday Night, but here in Chicago he's on fire. Starkid charges Wildwing and Wildwing simply ducks down and backdrops him totally out of the ring. Wildwing has eliminated 2 men in 2 minutes. There's the bell. I just wanna Fly is being played as Sugar Ray comes out and is jeered for imitating the popular band. Biff Wellington: Surgar Ray steps into the ring, Lucky Rich comes at Wildwing, Wildwing side steps him and he knocks into Sugar Ray causing both men to go over the top and be eliminated. Wildwing points and smiles to himself as he awaitd the next competitior. Fast Eddie: Pretty Amazing as Wildwing cleared house, Eliminating 4 men rather quickly, looks like glory is gonna have to go through Wildwing tonight. By the way Sugar Ray has the record for shortest stay in the ring. There's the Bell, Ice Ice Baby by Vanilla Ice is played, No one steps out to a raucus boos and Wildwing scratches his head. Biff Wellington: Uh Oh from behind, Polar Bear comes through the crowd he's toting a steel chair. Business is about to pick up. *Crack* You hear that shot is was savagely. Wildwing drops like a sack of potatoes, Polar Bear grins with a sick look on his face. He's proud of what he did. Polar bear kicks the chair out of the ring. Fast Eddie: Polar Bear goes to the top rope. He leaps off and attempts a diving headbutt. Wildwing moves both men are down. The bell rings. "Running With the Devil" by Van Halen hits the PA, The crowd gives a great ovation to Bryan Mercy, the self proclaimed greatest athlete im the wrestling business makes his way down the aisle Biff Wellington: Polar Bear to his feet first and he picks up Wildwing who has a laceration on the top of noggin. Polar Bear, with a couple of rights that stagger the former World Champion, Mercy clam rolls in and taps Polar Bear on the back who turns around with a punch that Mercy blocks. Mercy follows with a right of his own. He leaps up while holding the ropes. Oh my gosh, He head scissored Polar Bear out of the ring. Fast Eddie: That's why he's the greatest athlete in wrestling, including the guy from the left coast. Anyways Mercy advances towards Wildwing as the Bell rings. As we await our next competitor.
