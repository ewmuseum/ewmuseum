Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Roughcut Extreme Wrestling Federation opened on 3 May 1997: “You`ve seen the rest now take a look at the best… if you can`t play with the Big Boys, GET THE HELL OUT.” Roleplays went to its president alone, who then distributed them in a newsletter-style mailing and built angles around them. Two weekly cards ran, Sunday Night Supreme and Wednesday Night WAR, with a monthly pay-per-view and a hotline and chat room for members between cards. Three belts launched with it: a World title, an International title, and a Tag Team title.

Its roster blended two registers. Original applicants like Johnny Star, built openly in the mold of Shawn Michaels down to his finisher's name and his wife Amanda Star as a non-appearing manager, wrestled for characters of their own devising, while its televised cards ran entirely on real, contemporaneous professional wrestlers staged as a dream-card promotion.

Its surviving Saturday Night Hardcore Heaven card, 14 September 1997, had Steve Austin beat Shawn Michaels with a backslide, the Head Hunters beat Kevin Nash and Scott Hall in a no-disqualification tag match that cost Head Hunter #2 a torn bicep and roughly five cards on the shelf, and The Patriot beat Ken Shamrock with the Patriot Slam.

Its WrestleMania, billed as “the biggest E-Wrestling Event EVER” and “THE OFFICIAL E-FED OF DAN SEVERN,” ran 28 September 1997, announced the same day over a Turner-hosted IRC chat room. An eight-man battle royal crowned its own Heavyweight Champion for the first time, The Patriot eliminating Anderson last at 29:31, on a card that also saw Vader win a ten-man number-one-contender's battle royal and Sting escape a cage to beat Raven.

No document past that card survives.
