REWF Sunday Night RESULTS

18 August 2002

http://rewf.cjb.net/ Results are easier to read there. REWF Sunday Night Date AUG/18/2002 Show starts with a recap of the Super Junior-X cup bouts. Standard package, mainly littered with Calypso's high spots, then leading to the finals, and then shows Kenny Psycore's demented grin as he holds a jar of Jerry Malibu's blood, while Malibu is shown on and off in DTS glory with dramatic
end-of-show commentary. REWF commentators begin the show discussing their new world champion, Krax. Color guy complains about how he cheated, and he didn't deserve the honor the belt bestowed upon him. They have a replay shown of Grubb's superkick, which then allowed Krax to score the win. More negative commentary from the color guy.
Match 1 RECKLESS REBEL VS. KOMODO KID Re-match from WAR!. Komodo starts out with a strong flurry of kicks and chops. He gains a decent advantage early on. Komodo goes for a Fujiwara armbar, and Rebel reverses it into a hammerlock. Komodo goes against the ropes and takes an armdrag.
Armdrags back and forth for a while, and Rebel ends the spot with a stiff jumping-spinning back kick to the chest and a pin attempt. Komodo reels back into the ropes. Rebel dropkicks him over and bounds off the other side. He nails Komodo with a diving plancha and both are down.
Komodo staggers to his feet and realizes he needs to do something, so he hops onto the apron, into and Asai moonsault, all in one fluid motion. He rolls Rebel's lifeless body back into the ring, and gets a near 3.
Frustrated, he strays from his usual high-flying style and brutally stomps
Komodo several times. He jumps onto the turnbuckle, and gives the high-sign.
Before he can nail a move, Komodo knocks him on his crotch, facing away from the ring. Komodo hits an Iconoclasm spontaneously, and gets the 3.
KOMODO KID PINS RECKLESS REBEL IN 13 MINUTES AND 47 SECONDS WITH THE ICONOCLASM. BACKSTAGE - Rebel is being interviewed about his loss, when Justine walks up and begins hitting on him. Rebel makes allusions to Justine's sexuality, to which Justine only reacts by coming on stronger.
Match 2 MIKEY LODEN VS. IGOR GRISHKOV Loden pulls some cheap heel heat by insulting the town, and talking trash about random fans. Grishkov hits the ring and says something in Russian, which he then explains was an insult to Loden's mother. Loden replies with an attempted clothesline, but Grishkov ducks and pantses Loden. Loden rolls out of the ring and pulls up his tights, as the crowd pops when he falls now and then.
Loden goes to leave the arena, swearing all the way. Grishkov runs to Loden and nails him in the back of the skull with a forearm. He puts Loden in a headlock and gives him nuggies as he brings him back down to the ring.
Grishkov hits a scoopslam and an elbow drop. He waits for Loden to get up, and hits a heavy-looking dropkick. Grishkov gets up faster than normal to pose. Loden hits a low blow while Grishkov's back is turned, and rolls him up, holding some tights, for the 3.
MIKEY LODEN PINS IGOR GRISHKOV IN 8 MINUTES AND 1 SECOND WITH A SCHOOLBOY PIN. Match 3 MARI-ANN CAROLINA VS. TRISHA NOIR Match starts out as Noir and Carolina talk trash face to face. Carolina goes to slap Noir, but Noir blocks it and delivers a slap of her own. Carolina trips Noir while down, and mounts her. They roll around, exchanging moves, punches, and hair pulling. The ref tries to get in the way, and takes a bump in the process. He gets up almost immediately after the pop has died off.
Noir bounces off the ropes, and clotheslines Carolina. She does it again, and then puts her in a sleeper on the ground. Carolina quickly counters with a jawbreaker, and then goes for a pin. She gets a 2 count. Carolina hits a standing elbow drop on Noir. Noir goes for a jab, but is countered with a jab from Carolina. Trisha ducks the next jab and hits a DDT, to the amazement of the crowd.
She immediately follows up with a half crab, but Carolina reaches the ropes. Noir attempts a powerbomb but is reversed into a hurricanranna pin, for a 3 count, to the overall disdain of the crowd. Carolina adds insult to injury as she spike DDTs Noir after the match has already ended.
MARI-ANN CAROLINA PINS TRISHA NOIR IN 7 MINUTES AND 52 SECONDS WITH A SUPER RANA PIN COMBO. Match 4 SUPER JUNIOR-X TITLE CALYPSO VS. JUSTINE Justine does his usual flamboyant shtick, and Calypso does as everyone else seem to do, and pummels Justine.
Calypso hits a Japanese arm drag, followed by a dropkick to the back. Fast paced stuff here. Justine gets up and turns to face Calypso, and is met with a jumping facebuster.
Justine starts to get up, mumbling incoherently about his makeup being smeared and walks right into a Michinoku Driver II. Calypso gets a 2 count.
He gets up, bounces off the ropes, Justine drops to the floor, Calypso shoots back towards Justine, and Justine grabs Calypso and just floors him with a kiss, followed by a tilt-a-whirl slam.
Calypso, wiping the kiss off his face, gets up and hops over a charging
Justine, who rams himself into the turnbuckle instead of Calypso. Before
Justine can fall down, Calypso nails a superkick to the back of Justine's platinum blonde head.
Calypso goes for the pin, but Justine, knowing his way around the ring, knows to put his foot on the ropes. Ref counts 2 and sees the foot, and breaks up the pin. Calypso, beginning to show some frustration after having to work so much in so little time, coupled with Justine's stubbornness, screws up and allows Justine to nail him with the Diamond Dust, seemingly out of nowhere.
Justine gets a 2 and a half, making Calypso realize this has to be the end. Calypso hits a body scissors into a bulldog, as Justine went for a reverse powerbomb. Calypso does the high-sign and goes up top. High-sign again and does an absolutely PERFECT Phoenix Splash, forcing Justine's tired limbs into the air. 1..2..3! Calypso retains the belt. He celebrates on the turnbuckles as Justine rolls out of the ring. Reckless Rebel runs out with a chair and dropkicks it into the face of Justine for his remarks during
Rebel's backstage segment.
CALYPSO DEF. JUSTINE WITH A PHEONIX SPLASH IN 14 MIN/45 SECONDS TO RETAIN THE SUPER JUNIOR-X TITLE. Show ends with shot of Rebel eying both the Super Junior-X champion and the busted open Justine while walking back to the dressing rooms
