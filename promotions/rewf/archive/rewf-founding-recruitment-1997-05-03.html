Think You Got What It Takes?Join REWF And Prove Yourself...

3 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You`ve seen the rest now take a look at the best. Roughcut Extreme Wrestling Federation is the place to be. This is where ONLY the BEST Role-Players are allowed in. To put it easy, if you can`t play with the Big Boys, GET THE HELL OUT. I`ve been in alot of feds and I have had many feds. But this one is gonna be different. This one is gonna get extremely to the extreme. If you like what you hear keep on reading. Role-Playin... On of my favorite feds I was in was CIW. This place was great, but now its dead. Anyway, I want to bring that kind of format back. You send your role-plays to me and ME only and then I send them out to everyone at the end of the day. I put them in a newsletter type of thing but not really. You must Role-play but as the president I will be making angles and giving pushes to those who deserve it. Cards... We got cards on Sunday and Wednesday. They`re called Sunday Night Supreme and Wednesday Night WAR. To get your matches Role-play. You can send in sneak attacks but you gotta have a reason. As a matter of fact, I would perfer you did send in some sneak attacks to get some angles started. Both cards will have about five matches or more sometimes less. Also, we have PPVs once every month, usually the third week of every month.These are the super cards and usually everyone gets a match here. Features... This is what makes the fed fun and gives members a chance to not worry so much on the compitition but just lets them have fun.We will have a chat room and this place is very important becuase you can go in there whenever you want and I go in there in my free time so that is where you can talk to me.We got a hotline that you can call BUT its only open at a certain time. Don`t worry, its not a short time period but its open as soon as soon as my reporters get any scoops concerning me, you or the fed. I send out a letter saying that the hotlines open and then you can call in. This is good becuase,in a way, it reminds you about the hotline so you don`t miss any of the HOT scoops that we get. We will also have Webpage. More info on the page later. We have letters from the presidents desk that come out every so often that contain up coming matches upcoming PPVs and PPV matches. Info on alot of things will be in here. I`ve heard of a feature of money and it sounds cool. I will probably have it here in a few weeks probabaly even days. We will have more and I mean ALOT more. Belts... More belts will be added as the fed grows which it will but heres what we will start out with. Roughcut Extreme Wrestling Federation Championship World Belt Roughcut Extreme Wrestling Federation Championship International Belt Roughcut Extreme Wrestling Federation Championship Tag-Team Belts Wrestlers... You can make up to three guys, any sort of combintaion. You can have stables but somewhere along the line I am gonna have to start limiting the stables or the fed will fill up with em and then it gets pretty confusing. Its suggested that you make two guys becuase wrestlers do get hurt here in this federation. It doesn`t happen often but it is possible. Don`t be scared, you can still have one guy but just be aware of the possibility of injuries. Application... Name: height: Weight: Hometown: Face/Heel/Neutral: Gimmick If Any: Finisher. Give me the technical name and your wrestlers name for it: Special Moves, 10-50 At least ten. please don`t put any basic moves, put in ones that makes your wrestler special: Stable If Any: Manager If Any: Ring Attire: Favorite match.
