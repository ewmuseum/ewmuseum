Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Association Mondiale de Catch, AMC to its players, was a French-language federation on a Forumactif board in 2020. Its television show, Monday Night Universe, ran an episode a week from the end of May, each posted with a preview card, and its first big event, D-Day, was set for Friday 19 June at 20:00 French time, with M Cube meeting Destroyer, leader of the Hell Legion, for the Universe Championship, and the Hell Legion celebrating Destroyer's title win the following week. The players filed sheets for solo wrestlers and tag teams, wrote promos under Les Aventures, kept in-character blogs and played scenes in set locations such as an Italian restaurant and the toilets; Kevin Cyd ran a political campaign called the Project, General Manager Magic Booya had a big announcement on the main title, and a news item of 4 June reported an anonymous informant's claim that Richard White, head of White Corp, used his network to place relatives on the roster.

On 7 July 2020 the leadership announced that the pay-per-view Hard Times would be the last of the federation before it was put to sleep, and the board's latest post, on 12 July, is a member's advertisement of their own e-fed.
