I. "Prelude"
Welcome to "Twin Cities Weekly", the weekly magazine that provide you with the happenings in the newest and the most anticipated wrestling federation, the TCW. The TCW is the brainchild of [name withheld] and [name withheld] and is based out of Minneapolis, MN---which is the birthplace of professional wrestling. For the time being, the TCW will be a singles-only wrestling federation and we plan on having upwards of thirty wrestlers that must pass through a rigorous application process in order to be accepted. With that in mind, the TCW plans on maintaining a very competitive and regional "feel" to it as the fans of Minneapolis are very "wrestling smart" and will no doubt display their approval or hatred towards wrestlers good and bad.

II. "The Friendly Confines"
The TCW shows will be held at Williams Arena which is located on the campus of the University of Minnesota near downtown Minneapolis. Williams Arena is commonly known as "The Barn" because of it's physical similarities to a oversized steel barn. The capacity of Williams Arena is 13,000 and "The Barn" is famous not only to have the one of the loudest arenas in the United States (due to the "tinny" echoing when things get cooking!), but to allow the fans to be "right on top" of the action.

III. "The Shows"
The TCW will bring you three weekly events. "Twin Cities Weekly", the magazine you are reading, will be delivered to your doorstep every Monday morning. In it you will find up-to-the-minute news, rankings, schedules, articles, columns, results and anything else we can come up with. :P
"Midweek Crisis" will be aired on Wednesday evenings. On "MC", you will find plenty of interviews, six dark matches (match summaries) and one live "main event" match.
"Weekend Crisis" is our big event of the week in which the TCW presents six full matches, and more interviews. "WW" will be shown on Saturday or Sunday depending......
Both shows in the TCW will be incorporating the interviews directly into the shows-- no roleplay packets here!
The TCW will occasionally (approximately once per month) have a Pay Per View which may entail a tour or a special theme.

IV. "The Staff"
Every top-notch federation needs a crack staff to make the wheels turn on a consistent basis. Let's take a look at these fine individuals:

Announcers: Ryan Anderson- Ryan is in his mid-to late twenties. He leans towards the "faces" but does recognizes talent regardless of their alingment. Ryan was born and raised in Minnesota and grew up following the old Vern Gagne run AWA. Filled with numerous "midwestern-isms" like "you betcha'"; "dontcha' know" and eh's,. Ryan is the prototype Minnesotan ...but he remains a excellent student of the sport.
Color Commentator: Larry "The Neck" Franken- Franken is a very rotund individual(a la Dusty Rhodes) that was also born and raised in the Gopher state. Larry wrestled for over thirty years until he was forced to retire after being bodyslammed off of the Nicolley Island Bridge in a "Falls Count Anywhere Match"------he lost. Due to the back injuries sustained, Larry was forced to retire and is fulfilling his contractual obligations to wrestling at the announcer's table. He was and always will be a heel and constantly gets under the skin of his compadre, Ryan Anderson, by calling him "Andy".
Interviewer/Special Hostess: Lindsay Jackson- This stunning and sultry light-skinned African-American woman handles most of the interviews for the TCW and provides the TCW with the always needed "Special Reports" and "Pay-Per-View Updates." She will provide unbiassed and direct feedback and favors neither faces or heels. She is also constantly being flirted with by the wrestlers of the TCW (and elsewhere). Caution is needed here--- her beau is "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond of the NPWA, TVW and LWC. Diamond is known to be a jealous boyfriend and has been known to "protect what is his". Moreover...Her ***BIG** brother is Xavier Lee Jackson of the NLWP.....and we do mean *BIG*.
And now...the referees of the TCW. They are the ones that keep things under control in the TCW.
Barry "The Bifocal" Bennington. An ex-optomotrist that needed to retire from that profession because it got to the point in which the patient were reading the charts to *HIM*. The man may not see well, but he knows every word and every clause of the TCW rulebook.
Bronk Nielson. An ex-wrestler that was banned from the sport for force-feeding a microphone to his opponent (Bronk lost via DQ...a fact that still bothers him today). Needless to say, Bronk will not tolerate any leeway in the ring and, oddly enough, he is a fan favorite. The calls of "BBRROOOOOOOOONNKK!" are constantly heard whenever Nielson refs a match.
Francine "Harley" Hanson. Only in the TCW will you find a female ref that is also a premiere member of the "Hell's Angels". Typically, she outweighs the wrestlers, but she sure is agile for a woman that tips the scales at 280 pounds. When "Harley" calls a match, there is often a contigent of fellow "Hell's Angels" present that cheer her every call....and the wrestle that gives Francine grief..........shouldn't have.
The President of the TCW is Mr. Scott Labat. Mr. Labat, another home grown talent, has been around the world of wrestling for all but six of his thirty two years. Mr. Labat is not only a huge advocate of the sport he calls "the best thing since handi-wipes", but he takes his job as TCW President very seriously and will not hesitate to distribute fines, suspensions and expulsions should a wrestler consistently break the rules, or worse yet, strike a TCW staff member. Mr. Labat will also be making periodic appearances at Williams Arena to view his product.

V. "The Gold"
Ahh yes....the stuff that everybody strives for and the TCW will not disappiont as we offer FOUR different championships:
TCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP-- The creme de la creme of the TCW. This title is far and away the most coveted title the TCW gives to the one individual that truly deserves it. The TCW HW Championship must be defended at least once per month (against one of the top five ranked wrestlers) and at stated Pay Per Views. The champ may challenge whomever he wants outside of the Top 5, but it will not be considered part of the "once/month defense" clause.
TCW INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP-- Another prestigous belt that the TCW offers. This championship typically is held by the "second best wrestler in the TCW". Theoretically, the IC Champion is preparing himself for the HW Belt. The IC Championship must be defended once every other week against one of the Top 10 the TCW has to offer and stated PPVs. Again, the IC Champ may wrestle anyone outside of the Top 10, but that won't be considered part of the aforementioned clause.
TCW TV TITLE-- The TCW TV Title is still a solid piece of precious metal to obtain as it is, actually, the most difficult to retain. This belt must be defended every week against someone from the Top 15 rankings and every PPV. As stated above, this champion may defend his belt against someone outside the Top 15, but it will not fit under the "once a week" clause.
TCW XTREME BELT-- An interesting concept that was brought with the founders. This championship must be defended every other week against ANYBODY on the roster and all stated PPVs. When defended, their must be a stipulation set forth by the champion (i.e.-- Cage Match, Falls Count Anywhere Match, Brass Knuckles Match etc...). This belt is not for the timid.
