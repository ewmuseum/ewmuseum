TCW: The Saga Continues . . .

24 May 1998 the post on Google Groups
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TCW: The Saga Continues...... Well, after several weeks of discussion with myself...[name withheld]...and a few others, the TCW will be returning to the airwaves whether you like it or not.
;-) The structure may be a bit different but, hopefully, the creative angles and writing will remain the same.
Let's talk about specifics, shall we? As we last remembered, the TCW had moved to Canada due to the illegal business dealings of one, [name withheld] and [name withheld]. In Canada, many TCWers took refuge in a small promotion called the MLWA. After several weeks of production, the Canadien Mounties, on an anonymous tip, tracked down the MLWA staff and immediately shut them down.
Now....after three months of hard labor, the group is back. This time, they're in Mineeapolis to stay! Now...let's take a closer look at what to expect from this version of the TCW. [STAFF] What would the TCW be without the lovable team of Ryan Anderson and Larry Franken. Yes, the duo is returning to the airwaves and plan on being as level-headed and unbiassed as they once were. [Yeah......right]
Following Ryan and Andj, and making her return to the media circles is the lovely Lindsey Jackson. Ms. Jackson, sister of the wrestler Xavier Lee
Jackson, will be handling all of the wrestler interviews. This sultry African American lady is known for her frank questions and her ability to draw the truths from even the most rancid wrestler.
Taking control of the ring will be none other than Bifocals Bennington. Like their cousins in the National Basketball Association, Bennington's colleagues are still behind bars due to tax evasion leaving this still-blind fellow to carry all of the officiating duties. And yes...his eyesight is worse than before; however, due to technology today and the laser surgery that he had while in jail himself, occasionally his own lens kick in at just the right moment.
Mr. Xavier Lee Jackson, the burly brother of Lindsey Jackson, takes control of two duties in the TCW. Of course, he'll be controlling the security matters for the TCW and will, without hesitation, rush out and terminate any and all spats that get out of hand.
Moreover, this African American 300 pound wrestler has been asked to take control of the day-to-day operations of the TCW. However, rumor has it that even though Mr. Bakewell and Mr. Hebert are still leary of being seen in the
Twin Cities, they are actually controlling the TCW via Mr. Jackson..... [ARENA] The Armory. This abandoned armory is the new home of the TCW. Once a haven for travelling rodeos; infantry excercises and dirt car races; this facility actually is a smaller version of the popular University of Minnesota's William's Arena. In other words, with it's oval roof, this tan structure looks like an oversized barn.
With a seating capacity of a few hundred people, this venue is much smaller than what the old TCW was accustomed to but when you've been ravaged by the
Internal Revenue Service and the FBI, what can one expect? [ROSTER] This version of the TCW will be more compact. Much like it's beloved cousin, the MLWA, the TCW will house up to a maximum of 18 wrestlers.
[SHOWS] Two of 'em. And for those that remember the old TPW, this will bring back some memories... Midweek Crisis: This show will be released anywhere from Monday evening to Wednesday afternoon. It will include the TCW Ticker [an update] along with the highlights of two matches that had taken place earlier on.
Weekend Wars: Live from The Armory, this show will bring the fans anywhere from two to four live matches. Prior to production, TCW Talks [a roleplay packet] will be released. After the show, the following week's schedule will also be introduced. WW plans on being released either Friday afternoon/evening; Saturday or at the very latest, Sunday afternoon depending on The Armory's bookings. ;-)
[TITLES] TCW Heavyweight Championship- [Ranks 1-5 available to challenge] TCW Minnesota State Championship- [Ranks 1- 10 available to challenge] Yes, there will be a third title added. It will either be a TV Title or a Cruiserweight Title [or Extreme?]. Input and opinions are appreciated. [SUPERCARDS] Yup...we'll have them! Watch the schedule... [WEBPAGE] http://www.execulink/~azrael/TCW/ It's not quite done yet, but you'll get the idea. Expect the final package to be completed within the next week.
[WHEN DO WE START?] Within a week or two....watch your mailboxes for the TCW Ticker..... [OTHER STUFF] Okay...now let's get down to the grit and grime that will really make the TCW run smoothly. Pardon the inpersonal bullet points, but it's easier this
way...:-) -------- Real life staff. [name withheld]- President [name withheld]- Vice- President/webmaster _ Send all correspondence to Kevin [ [detail withheld] ] as he/I will be handling the vast majority of the booking etc... -------- Roleplays etc.. _Deadlines for the next show will be announced after the preceeding show. Don't worry, you'll be given enough time to roleplay. ;-) [usually two/three days] _The only wrestlers that are to roleplay on Midweek Crisis are those wrestling unless you have permission from the President [me!!] first.
_85% of the RP's for Weekend Wars will be sent in the TCW Talks packet. If you'd like to RP within the WW show [in-rings etc...]....email me for
approval, please. :-) Unless otherwise noted, I will respond to your email within six to eight hours [usually sooner].....My motivation for this is to lessen my workload in putting together Weekend Wars.
_If you don't RP for a match....you will lose.....badly....and I have a damn vivid imagination. ;-)
_If you don't RP for two consecutive weeks, you are gone. Period. [Unless, of course, you have an OK from the President (me again!!)
-------------Attacks, strats and other nonsense _There will no attacking of fans, TCW staff or other wrestlers without written approval from the President [hey...that's me again!]. There is a no-tolerance level here.....You will be beat up by Xavier Lee and then booted.
;-) _Swearing.....don't do it. If it can't be said on television, then I don't want to see it in the TCW. [Bleeps] are permitted, though.
_Attacks......yeah...I love them......mail me to get them but don't go overboard. Be realistic and have it make sense.. :-) [Minimal attacks for
Midweek Crisis] _Strats.....[sigh]........I don't like them but realize that they are, occasionally, needed. Make them simple. Make them realistic. Make them SHORT!
;-) -------------General philosophy _I don't envision this version of the TCW to be as intense as it's predecessor. Nor do I want it to be. I'd like the TCW to be much like it's cousin, the MLWA, thus, this will be a rather stress-free environment [I hope]. This means that not all of the matches will be the wild-and-wooley-12k-fiascos......We want to maintain an even-keel.....smartass humor.....highly creative atmosphere around here. :-)
If a show is going to be late [which I hope doesn't happen..], you'll be notified If a match needs to be "darked", it'll be darked with a brief explanation accompanying it.
All-in-all, we plan on having this version of the TCW last a while. Chis and I have plenty of experience in the efedding world and our "real-life"
experiences should enable us to maintain a high level of organization and creativity. However, "real life" comes first. Not only for us...but for you, the handler. If something comes up that will take you away from the TCW...please....just let me know. Believe me, I certainly understand this area of efedding. :-)
Lastly, the TCW will remain small. Period. Moreover, it will, basically, be an invitation-only promotion. OR....the acceptance of a wrester will be made up of a very stringent process. In other words, we won't have a revolving-door here in the TCW. Yes...new blood is good, but I won't have it be the norm.
With the above being said, as creative as I/we may be...any angle ideas that you may have will certainly be appreciated.
Thanks for the time, folks, and as always, any questions, comments or concerns will be appreciated. ;-)
After reading this little note and now knowing what to expect from the TCW....please email me and let me know if you'd still like to participate and
I will gladly send you an application immediately.
Chow!!! [name withheld] [detail withheld]
