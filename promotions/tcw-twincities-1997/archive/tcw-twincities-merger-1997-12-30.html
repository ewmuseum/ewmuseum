TCW — Closure

"TCW and NLWP Merge!!!"

30–31 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Late Breaking News:
A merger of two federations, TCW and NLWP (Northern Lights Wrestling Promotion), was announced today. [name withheld] and [name withheld] of TCW and [name withheld] of NLWP have come to terms and have formed the TMW (Toronto-Minneapolis Wrestling).
Many top stars head the list from both feds: Brody Thunder, Nik Nyce, Doc Toxic, Valtharius the Mad, Bodycount, Kid Canuk, Cowboy Ken Curtis, and many other RSPWF Top 200 wrestlers.
This merger will be beneficial to both organizations with the influx of many talented writers and the ability to put out cards with greater ease. This merger should allow the TMW to be one of the top feds in the new year.
