Eve now dumps Kabuki back into the ring and sets Kabuki to her feet by the turnbuckles. Eve now with a tornado ddt on the wobbly,punch drunk Kabuki! BLOOD ! Kabuki now subjects Eve to a barrage of cops and spinning kicks . Eve staggers as Kabuki, sensing Eves weakness ,ascends the turnbuckles. Kabuki flies through the air to nail Eve with a hurricanranna and NO!!Eve caught Kabuki and powerbombed her !Eve and kabuki are both down!The ref is counting 1-2-3-4-5-kabuki jumps up and ascends the turbuckles!

Eve hooks the leg the ref counts 1--2--NO! Kabuki kicked out!?!? Eve and Kabuki now toe to toe in a brawl that would scare even the likes of a Cactus Jack! Both ladies bust each other open and their faces are a crimson mask of blood as they go at it for 15 violent minutes!

The WINNER and NEW UNITED WRESTLING PROMOTIONS UNIFIED GRAND LADY"S
CHAMPION OF THE WO_O_O_O_ORRRRLLLDDDD! E___E_E_VVVVVEEEEEE!!!"
