"[AD] UWP World Cup Challenge (For Presidents)"

10 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last Month To Enter!
United Wrestling Promotions presents the World Cup Challenge.
All presidents of play-by-mail promotions and e-wrestling federations are invited to participate in this event by sanctioning battle royals in your respective games, and submitting the winners to the finals of the
World Cup Challenge tournament in September.
Presidents only can contact me for more details. United Wrestling
Promotions is a group of play-by-mail and e-wrestling games who work together to enhance fantasy wrestling as a whole. The UWP is always looking for other games to join it's family. Presidents only can contact me if you're interested.
