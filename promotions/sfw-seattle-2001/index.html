The SEWF's own feeder federation, which opened in a Seattle high-school gymnasium in March 2001 under the SEWF's United States Champion as its president, and whose debut card was gatecrashed by an SEWF star claiming a place in its first title tournament. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The SFW opened on a Sunday night in March 2001 in the Ballard High School Gymnasium in Seattle, the feeder federation its parent, the SEWF, had promised in January for rookies it had no room to sign. Its own president was the SEWF's reigning United States Champion, Art Blake, white hair standing straight up, who told the crowd that it had been a long time since Seattle had a promotion of its own and that every wrestler on the card was there to earn a place in the SEWF.

Its first night was called from ringside by Billy Harrison and Samantha Barnes, the latter working the show for college credit and saying so, with Langdon Moore as its field reporter and Thomas Casey, newly named Vice President, promising the fans more of what they wanted. Seven matches followed: Violence over KK Hollywood, Sypher over Chris Johnson, David Dynamite Kidd over Pete Awesome, Konrad over Jubee, and in the main event Jacob Sawyer, the Archangel, over Justin Blade with the Dead Sea DDT.

The card also carried its first casualty and its first intruder. Paul Barker was left unconscious by a German suplex from Vengence and taken to hospital while Vengence swung a chair at the medics, and Jeff Kane, one of the SEWF's own stars, watched from the front row, called the whole roster jokers, pinned Jimmy and Super Cool Dude together after a chair shot, and forced his own name into the hat.

The night closed with that hat: six winners' names and Kane's own slip, four of them drawn for a NorthWest title tournament the following week, Konrad against Kane and Sawyer against Vengence. No later broadcast of the SFW survives. Its names ran on into the parent federation: the SEWF's April card bills Kane and Smoove as wrestlers transplanted from the SFW, Justin Blade reaches the SEWF by the autumn, and the SFW's Vice President became the SEWF's own President that September.
