Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On 10 September 1997 its self-titled owner, Rusty, opened a recruitment burst for the Undisputed World Championship of E-wrestling with a direct challenge to the newsgroup: “U GOT A GREAT WORLD CHAMPION THEN TRY TO WIN THE UNDISPUTED WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP OF E-WRESTLING.” In the same post he credited himself as owner of two further e-feds, ECEW and WC.

He posted twice more over the following days, pointing recruits first to a wbs.net home page and then, two days later, to a GeoCities mirror while asking the newsgroup outright for a match simulator to run the fed on.

Its recruitment ran ten days in all, 10 to 20 September 1997, and its record stops there — no roster, card, or result survives.
