Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This wing portrays Total Nonstop Action, the real wrestling company. Total Non Stop Wrestling Action is a fan federation's fiction about that institution, not a statement of fact about the company or the people it names. The federation's own pages call it TNA, set its show at the iMPACT! Zone in Orlando and bill real wrestlers under their own names; nothing it stages is the real company's history, and none of its belts is the real one.

What survives of its own booking is a news column of three entries. On 8 June 2008 it reports that a Turner executive announced Panda Energy's purchase of World Championship Wrestling, over WCW president Eric Bischoff's protest, and that the merger into TNA took effect at once, with the Big Gold Championship and WCW's contracts, though only a handful of stars had signed, World Champion Mark Jindrak among those slated to jump. On 26 June it reports TNA's first show, at the iMPACT! Zone in Orlando, to a full house, and on 3 July it says TNA will name its first X-Division Champion that week while the World title, in the show's third week, has still not been defended.

Its site was up well before that story, though its news calls the show of June 2008 TNA's first. A capture of its front page from 19 September 2006, headed Welcome to the iMPACT ZONE!, already lists Impact and pay-per-view cards, a roleplay board, a pay-per-view roleplay board, rosters, stables, champions and a title history, and an Extra list that names WCW World Wide; the same menu was copied into its directory again on 14 January 2008, and a capture of its own subdomain from 24 July 2008 carries only its title. The page headed WCW Title Histories that the same directory holds is the WCW fan federation's own, and that federation has a wing of its own: this federation's news has TNA take over WCW's Big Gold title and stars, which is why the two are joined as predecessor and successor. The directory also holds roleplays that belong to other federations and to their characters, not to this wing.
