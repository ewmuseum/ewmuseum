WLC Wrestling: staff positions

29 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Everyone, Im Jonathan Bretmeyer CEO of Bretmeyer Entertainment. Im not looking for wrestlers, but right now I wanna focus on staff members. Here are the following positions im looking to be filled.
President: Not Directly involved in the federation. He does fire, and hire wrestlers. He is to deal with business outside of the efed, such as, making deals, tv coverage, major contracts.
Vice President: He is to update the page, handle wrestlers complaints, make sure the wrestlers are participating, making sure the rules are carried out accordingly.
Creative Director/writer: You don't have to worry about this position because im taking this role. His job is to read the roleplays, work with the wrestlers with storylines an angles. He is to turn the results into the Vice
President at a specific time every week.
HTML Director: this person will be used once every 6 weeks. His or her job will be to create graphics for the weekly shows, or anything else that needs graphics. He is to build a whole new webpage every 2months or 6 weeks or depending on the Vice Presidents Decision.
Executive Vice President: To take the load off of the Vice President, he is to share duties with the Vice President, and he is to take over in the event that the president, or Vice President loose access to the internet, or become sick.
If I the president is missing for more than a week, and I havent sent an email in, the Vice President is to assume my computer is down, or I cant get to one, and he is to take over my duties until I return. The Executive Vice
President must take the place of the Vice President , when the vice president takes the position of president.
We will have meetings every 6 weeks, to make major decisions concerning the federation. Whenever a staff member is missing for more than 3 days, and has not sent in a email, the person directly under that staff member is to take the role until he or she returns.
If You are interested in any of these positions, please contact me at [detail withheld]
