WLC is returning.

19 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey im back again. Are you guys tired of efed presidents making promises they can't keep. I know a few of my former efed presidents making promises as to when the show'll be up. I worked for a guy who would only keep the efed up for one month, and I got tired of it. I looking to make a huge change to the efed world.
Heres my plan. I get 20 wrestlers to join we have our first show on Monday
September 27 or in october. We have our first PPV on OCT 30. Where the world title will be on the line.
We will have one show a week. You guys have until friday to get all of your roleplays in. Then sat and sunday I work on the show. If we have a ppv the roleplays are do on thursday, so I can work on the PPV and Sat and sunday I work on the monday show. See I have enough time to do both shows. We'll have specials every other month when I feel like it. Monday shows are live after ppv's. So after the ppv, you guys roleplayed on thursday for the PPV and
Monday show, well that will count for next monday show as well. So you would've roleplayed for the PPV , monday show and the monday show the following week. So it'll be less work for you, and less work for me.
Im trying to get you guys to understand i am a reliable person. All I need is 20 wrestlers and we'll jump start this thing. When I say the PPV will be up 7:33 et time, i mean it'll be up. Im gonna leave messages like this everyday until people join. I accept any kind of wrestler let they be made up or TV characters(Ex Sting or lex luger) if have a made up wrestler we will have a series of meetings so I can develop angles to fit your character. Well thats it, you wanna join my efed go here
http://www.angelfire.com/pa/wlcwrestling or email me . [detail withheld] the limit of wrestlers in the tournament is 16. later everyone.
