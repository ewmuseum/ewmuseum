A federation blending real-world wrestler personas with its own invented characters across a casket-match feud, closing and relaunching at least once between 1998 and 1999. Sixteen wrestlers are recorded across four served documents naming its roster and card structure. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WLC's surviving record opens on 3 January 1999, its own president recapping title tournaments and feuds already running; no document from before that broadcast survives. Kane held the promotion's own World Heavyweight Championship at the start of this record, and Spector held the Intercontinental title, retaining it by disqualification after his own ally interfered against Hollywood Hogan's challenge.

A four-way match for the World Heavyweight title, pitting Stone Cold Steve Austin, The Rock, Bret Hart, and Kane against each other, ended without a winner when Austin vanished from inside a locked casket in the middle of the match; Bret Hart had already been pulled from the same match earlier after being found unconscious beside a cup of drugged juice.

A separate three-way for the promotion's own Hardcore Championship, Scott Hall and Kevin Nash against Crazy Marc Bonnin and Mick Powers, ended with Hall and Nash turning on their own partners to face off, and closed on Hall raising Nash's hand as the new champion rather than taking the title for himself — the same building, the broadcast notes, where the two had once been tag team champions together years before.

Diamond Dallas Page spent this stretch of the record barred from the building by the promotion's own president, struck by a car in the parking lot, and accused rival Total Torture of holding his own wife hostage; Total Torture's car was later hijacked by an unidentified assailant.

The surviving record runs to 3 November 1999, an ongoing weekly broadcast under what the promotion calls a restarted operation. Nothing records how, or whether, its run ended.
