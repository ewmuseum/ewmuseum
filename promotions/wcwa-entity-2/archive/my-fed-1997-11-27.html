Hello everyone. I jjst wanted to send you some info on my fed. It is really good. Read this and if you like it. E-mail me for an app. We need a lot of good roleplayers. Bye World Championship Wrestling Association (WCWA) Belts: 1.)World Heavyweight Title (Only the best can challenge for this baby) 2.)TV Title (regular old Televison Title) 4.)Cruiserweight/Lightweight Title(for the Rey Misterio's and the Great Sasuke's) 5.)Intercontinental Title (this would be the same as the WWF or WCW Title. And even though it sounds like nothing it is second in line) 6.)Tag Title (even though we have few tag teams) Cards: (Monday) Main Weekly Card: Monday Madness (Friday) Other Weekly Card: Fallout Friday Commentators: Hour 1: Jim Ross Joey Styles Jim Cornette Larry Zybisco Hour 2: Tony Shavonie Mike Teney Jerry Lawler PPV's 1.)January: New Years Eve of Destruction 2.)February: King Of The Ring 3.)March: March Mayhem 4.)April: Rage In The Cage 5.)May: Battle Royal 6.)June: Barly Legal 7.)July:...Beach Blast 8.)August: Wrestlepaloza 9.)September: September Spectacular 10.)October: Halloween Hell 11.)November: Gang Warfair 12.)December: Zero Below Rules: *All of your wrestlers must roleplay at least once a week. *If you don't roleplay and you have a title, you automatically forfiet the title. *If you don't roleplay and you don't have a title, your out of the fed. *If you get injured for more than 3 cards and you have a title, the title is automatically vacated. *If you have the tag titles and you get hurt for more than 3 cards, you get to keep the titles, you just have to substitute a wrestler for the injured wrestler. *One wrestler may not hold more than 2 singles titles at once. But, one wrestler can hold a singles title and a tag title at the same time.
