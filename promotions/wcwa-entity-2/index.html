Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Championship Wrestling Association declared itself “NOW OPEN FOR BUSINESS” on 19 October 1997, a booked, all-real-wrestler federation run on a match simulator rather than judged roleplay alone. Members were allowed two singles wrestlers and a tag team apiece, on a minimum of two cards and one roleplay a week, and the fed's own opening roster call listed the real wrestlers already claimed, grouped by stable: The Kliq (Syxx, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, Shawn Michaels, Scott Hall, Kevin Nash), the Four Horsemen (Ric Flair, Bret Hart, Ken Shamrock, Sting), the Stone Cold Foundation (Steve Austin, Mark Mero, Cactus Jack, Ron Simmons, Ahmed Johnson), Planet Boom (Macho Man, Buff Bagwell, Curt Hennig, Mosh, Thrasher), the Anti-ECW Crew (Sabu, Rob Van Dam, Jerry Lawler, managed by Bill Alfonso), the N.W.O. (Hollywood Hogan, Rick Rude, Alex Wright, the Steiner Brothers), Team Taz (Taz, Sid, Tommy Dreamer, Dean Malenko), the Extreme Gangsta Party (Shane Douglas, New Jack, Kronus), and the E.W.O. (Raven, Sandman).

Its own paperwork laid out a five-belt list — World Heavyweight, TV, Cruiserweight/Lightweight, Intercontinental, and Tag Team — a two-card weekly schedule (Monday Madness, Fallout Friday), and a two-hour broadcast staffed by real commentators split across the hours: Jim Ross and Joey Styles, Jim Cornette and Larry Zbyszko for the first, Tony Schiavone, Mike Tenay, and Jerry Lawler for the second. A twelve-event pay-per-view calendar ran the length of a full year, one a month, from New Year's Eve of Destruction through King of the Ring, March Mayhem, Rage in the Cage, Battle Royal, Barely Legal, Beach Blast, Wrestlepalooza, September Spectacular, Halloween Hell, Gang Warfare, and Zero Below. Its own rules vacated a title on a roleplay lapse or a long injury layoff, and released a wrestler outright for going quiet without one.

No card, result, or title change survives anywhere in the record. The WCWA kept posting recruitment calls into December 1997 — its own president was still advertising the fed, unstructured roster and all, as late as 12 December — but nothing dated afterward is held.
