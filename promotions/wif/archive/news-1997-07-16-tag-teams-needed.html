Tag Teams needed

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. My name is [name withheld]. I am otherwise known as Iron. My wrestler has accomplished much in his career, but he has retired, taking on the task of running an e-wrestling league that was about to die. Since taking this league over, handler satisfaction has improved tremendously with the quality of the matches and organization of the league. The WIF made its emergence into the higher world of e-wrestling in August of 1996 and has been holding strong ever since. Take a look at the WIF Homepage at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/6484/ The WIF closed in February of 1997 because I was too stressed to hold the league any longer. All my spare time was going into keeping the reputation of this league. I closed the league and handed the majority of the handlers over to another league, the HCW, which had just formed. This league was being run by someone who thought running a league would be easy. The league closed but a month after it formed. The handlers are very close in the WIF. It's almost like a family, though they love beating the hell out of each other. I figured that these guys needed a good league to participate in, a league they could call home. The WIF was born again, with new rules and means of gaining victory. Roleplaying is a high-ranking factor in judging the winners of our matches. If you don't roleplay, you will not win. If two guys' roleplaying is too close for me to call, then the match is stuck into a TNM simulator and the winner is judged there. Unfortunately, roleplaying has been the only thing that has plagued the WIF all it's life. The WIF has a few great handlers and wrestlers. Among these men are Lancaster Bomber, Tony "Falcon" Silver, Roundhouse, Dr. Doom, Jaw, Cold Blooded Nitrogen, Andros, Sapphire, and many others. The WIF is issuing a challenge right now to anyone reading this. We invite you to come and see what you've got, if you can match up with the WIF's stars. Mostly we are looking for tag team handlers. Our tag team division has a couple of great handlers, but then nothing. We have about four respectable teams in this division and we need many more. Tag team handlers, come to the WIF and test out the likes of the AWESOME Search and Destroy, the great Masked Bulldog and Steel, and the newcomers, the Tokyo Terrors. If you would like to join the WIF, contact me at [detail withheld] We would love to see more excellent roleplayers in our league as the league is going to start really heating up in the next couple of weeks. Oh, and just some advice.... if you're in more than one other league, it is not advisable that you sign up for the WIF if you want to go far. Being in the WIF takes a lot of time and effort. Good luck and have a nice day. [name withheld] WIF President
