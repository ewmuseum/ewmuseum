Best Match of the Year 1996

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
J.Starr:Evening folks, and welcome to the final WIF main event of 1996. It's been a truly great inaugural year, some teething problems for the fed initially, 3 presidents in the first few months, but now the league has settled down, it's moving smoothly and we have been proud to bring you all the superb WIF action. Two of the league's best in action at tonight's main event. Tobis now coming down the aisle, Doom disrobing in the ring....now he's stepping to the outside! Tobis posing for the crowd, doesn't see him! Ow! big clothesline to the back of the head, Tobis goes down, what a cheap shot! J.Stevens:Obviously Doom saw Tobis' performance against the Bomber and he knows he needs to get the early advantage in order to win. Not exactly ethical..... Starr:...but who cares. Lord, why isn't the Falcon calling tonight. Doom now with a couple of right fists on the prone Tobis, now picks him up and whips him hard into the steel post! Tobis collapses like a ton of bricks. Stevens:250lbs of bricks to be exact. Doom rolls him into the ring, steps over the top rope, grabs Tobis now and whips him to the ropes and hits him with a reverse knife edge on the rebound. Tobis to the canvas once more. Starr:Big cross corner whip from Doom, Tobis hard into the turnbuckle and Doom picks him and hits a twirl a whirl side walk slam. Covers 1...2...kickout. Tobis' getting the left shoulder up. Doom now, with a bodyslam, he's going to the outside.....he's going to the top rope. Leaps.....big vertical splash! covers, hooks both legs, but they roll over with the momentum and a 1 count for Tobis. Stevens:Authoratitive kickout by Doom. Tobis yet to mount any sort of offense. Gets whipped to the ropes and is met with a big boot to the face, goes down....followed by...a missed leg drop, but Doom ramming Tobis' head into the canvas before either of them got up off it. Doom still on the offensive, cross corner whip....followed in....clothesline to the throat, follows with a succession of elbows to the throat. Starr:Doom now raining kneelifts into Tobis' stomach and mid section, really pounding away. Great tactics by Doom, he knows that Tobis has the greater stamina of the two and the longer the match is the more it will favour Tobis, so he's trying to put him away as quickly as possible. Tobis never an easy prey though.Doom with a whip again....follows in....this time Tobis is ready! back body drops onto the steps to the floor!! Stevens: Doom going to the well once to many times, the whip followed by the high impact move, that time Tobis anticipating well. Now it's Doom in a precarious situation as he clears the cobwebs and Tobis is completely focused standing on the top turnbuckle. Leaps....perfect dropkick to the outside!! Doom doesn't know what hit him! Starr:Tobis picks Doom up by the hair, but Doom with a low blow, wristlock and a short arm clothesline, levels Tobis. Stevens:Doom has wrestled a great match thus far, really taking it to Tobis. Tobis' offense being nipped in the bud. Doom with a whip.... Tobis reverses....and Doom crashes into the steel steps! Starr:Tobis follows him, and Doom has got up limping! This could mean trouble for him, Tobis comes up from behind him and slides down to clip the leg, Doom is floored. Tobis now props Doom's left leg onto the steel steps....hitting a succession of elbows, fists and knees on the leg. Now he's going back into the ring, what's this? Stevens:He's going up to the top....he's up on top....leaps....knee drop onto Doom's leg!!! Doom's face searing with pain. Tobis rolls him back in now. Starr:Doom limps to his feet and shoulder blocks Tobis who was on the apron, now sets him up.....side suplex.....no! falls forward and straddles Tobis on the top rope, now clotheslines him off it! and Tobis landing on his neck on the floor! Stevens:Doom proving more than a match for the "legendary" Tobis..... Starr:Let's not take anything away from Tobis. He is one of the greats in this sport and took the WIF champion to the limit, he isn't a man I want to countout before the final bell. Doom on the apron, waiting for Tobis to come to his feet. He does, Doom leaps for a double axe handle.....but Tobis hits him with an elbow to the mid section in mid air! Doom doubles over and Tobis hits Rocker Dropper on the concrete!! Stevens:Tobis now slides back into the ring, runs the ropes....Doom up, moves towards the ring, and Tobis' with a sliding dropkick to the chest and Doom into the security railing! Starr:Tobis stepping to the floor to follow up the attack....but Doom, with a bearhug.....now rams Tobis' back into the steel post.Irish whip....and Tobis crashes over the security railing!! Stevens:Tobis gets up, leaps onto the security railing and hits a flying cross body block off it....but gets caught by Doom....he's got him overhead now.....oh no! throws him headfirst into the steel ring post!!! Starr:Doom rolls Tobis back in, what a performance this has been from Tobis, hurt leg and all, battling on and on to keep the initiative in this the final WIF supercard in 1996. Stevens:Hmmm, well, what a way for these two to celebrate the New Year as Tobis reverses an irish whip and catches Doom with a kneelift to the midsection. Starr:These two will probably spend New Year's day aching all over but they have given us and the assembled fans a really great New Year's present in this fine performance. Tobis now picking Doom up and locking in an abdominal stretch. Stevens:Tobis doing the right thing, slowing down Doom's momentum. Now holding onto the ropes for added leverage, breaks as soon as the ref looks up. He's been working on the legs now using the abdominal stretch to further weaken the lower body, Tobis has great technical expertise and we've seen only a fraction of his reportire so far today, these two still have a lot left in them. Starr:Certainly, both these men among the best the WIF has to offer, Doom now hip tosses his way out of that abdominal stretch, Tobis goes down and immediately returns to the leg with a couple of fists and an elbow, now with a single leg take down as he was getting to his feet. Stevens:Tobis.....with a....boston crab is it? NO!! Slingshot to the turnbuckle! Gets him up on the rebound....almost an atomic drop, but bending Doom's knee back and dropping it onto his own knee to soften it further. Starr:Tobis has the legs once again, this time bending Doom's left leg around his own, almost into a figure four position now jumps up and drives a knee into Doom's lower leg. Doom grimacing in agony. Now Tobis pressing down on Doom's left leg with his own right leg. Stevens:Very tough situation because there is no real way to reverse this hold, it's very difficult to get the free leg across to kick at ones opponent because Tobis has Doom's leg almost at a right angle to his torso, and if Doom tries to get to the ropes pulling Tobis' weight to the ropes is likely to hurt him as much as being in the hold itself. Starr:Well, it seems that is what he has decided on, his face is etched in pain but he's sliding along the canvas in an effort to get to the ropes, Tobis trying to hold on and doing so with some success, but Doom is making progress, is getting closer to the ropes.......and yes, grabs the ropes now, the ref calls for the break. Stevens:Both men getting up Tobis with a kick to the leg, Doom with a big roundhouse right, reverse atomic drop....and a claw hold!!! Clawhold to the stomach! Starr:This is a hold I have not seen in a long, long time. Probably the last time I did see it was "The Spoiler" using it against Tony Atlas. Stevens:Doom obviously a very keen student of the sport, Tobis breaks the hold with an eye rake, leg sweep take down, yanks Doom's leg hard to the side and quickly slaps on a figure four. Good move by Tobis considering the damage already done to Doom's leg, Doom though is moving towards the ropes, I think Tobis was hasty with the move and hasn't got it on properly enabling Doom to escape with some ease. Starr:That's true, but the damage is done, Doom once again limping severely, getting to his feet by holding on to the ropes as Tobis measures him...takes a running start and hits a spin kick which sends them both to the concrete, Tobis however holds onto the top rope and powers his way back inside. Stevens:Tobis runs the ropes.....shoulder block through the ropes! Doom is levelled!!Great move by Tobis who is now raining closed fists on the head of Doom. Tobis now with Doom in a headlock, runs towards the steel post, I think he wants to ram Doom's head into it....but Doom pushes Tobis headfirst into the pole! Doom gets Tobis up...and suplexes him across the security railing!! How much punishment will these two take?! Starr:The question is how much can they take, they'll take as much as is possible, I don't see either of these two backing down from the other. Tobis gets up, takes a running start, leaps onto the guardrail, onto Doom's shoulders and hits a frankensteiner on the floor! What a move! Stevens:Tobis on a roll, Doom back in Tobis follows him, he's got the leg again! This time synches in a Boston Crab! Tobis has it in beautifully, almost sitting across the back of Doom pulling his legs right back, he's been trying to extract a submission from Doom almost since Doom hurt his leg as a result of that whip into the stairs. So far he's been unsuccessful....Doom, attempting to power out now!!!! Unbelievable! A normal man would barely be able to stay conscious, but Doom is fighting back, getting up on his hands and using whatever strength is left in thos powerful legs to fight the technical expertise of Tony "The Assasin" Tobis. Starr:Tobis' grip is weakening, Doom's face contorted with the effort of trying to break this hold, I don't think that Tobis can hold on much longer.....no he can't! the hold is broken and Tobis propelled through the bottom rope to the floor!!!!! Stevens:Doom limps across the ring and rolls beneath the bottom rope, grabs Tobis by the throat! He's picked him up! 250lbs of Tobis suspended in mid air....chokeslam on the concrete! Doom doesn't let go! he's picked him up again in the same motion but this time Tobis breaks it with an elbow between the eyes. Starr:Doom drops Tobis, he's disoriented, turns away from Tobis...who takes a running start...and hits The Assasination!!!!!Great ring presence! Tobis rolls Doom back inside. Superb move from Tobis, the opportunity to hit his finisher presenting itself to Tobis, who saw and seized it, this could be over. Tobis slides in and covers 1...2....no! foot on the ropes!!!!!! Stevens:That's what fatigue does to you. Normally there's no doubt that a veteran like Tobis' would have the presence of mind to pin an opponent in the middle of the ring, or at least hook the legs, that time Tobis' doing neither and Doom lives on. Starr:Tobis whips Doom to the ropes now, puts his head down....too early!...Doomsday DDT!!!!! There was no Assasination, but will it be Doomsday for Tobis? Doom covers 1...2...no!! kickout!!! Stevens:Both men hitting their finishers, neither getting pinned. I don't know what else these two have to throw at each other. Aside from some ring side chairs I suppose, but hopefully it won't come to that. Starr:Doom gets Tobis up....powerbomb....no! frankensteiner by Tobis! how he still has the energy to do that I do not know, Tobis gets up....moves across and gets hold of Doom's legs. Holds them open, now steps across and crosses Doom's legs, uh oh, turns him over! Sting calls it the scorpion deathlock, Bret Hart calls it the sharpshooter, by the look on his face I have a feeling Doom calls it painful!! Stevens:There is no doubt about that, but I do not think that Doom has any intentions of submitting. He has shown tremendous resilience in the face of some very high class submission wrestling from Tobis. He is moving towards the ropes now, fighting off this hold, the crowd here - with no real sympathy for either of these men, but certainly with great respect for their wrestling abilities - completely engrossed in this contest, watching Doom as he inches towards the ropes, the referee keeping a close eye on proceedings as well....Doom grabs the ropes, and the ref calls for a break. We're going to be here all night, Starr, I can feel it. Starr:Neither of these men are in any hurry to join the New Year's celebrations, if it means adding a loss to their record. A truly tremendous display by these two men. One of them will have to be on the losing side, and after this sort of an effort it will be a shame. Stevens:Doom very unsteady on his feet, but hits a chop to the throat before Tobis can take advantage, follows with a back breaker.Doom picks Tobis up gets him in a reverse head....no turns him around and hits with a reverse DDT! the Doomsday DDT didn't work, will this? 1..2...no! Tobis holds on! Starr:Doom with Tobis in his grasp once more, gets him up for the powerbomb, too high! Tobis with a torrent of punches to the head of Doom...Doom deliberately falls backwards....stun gun!! Tobis' clutching his throat in pain. Stevens:Doom grabs Tobis by a handful of hair, again it looks like powerbomb attempt, you've got to give this man an A for persistence. No, piledriver! well executed cover 1....2...kickout, two and a half count that time. Doom dominating Tobis now as he sets him up once more....gets him up.....third time lucky!! powerbomb! Doom not going a for a pin though! Starr:He's probably tired of trying, I think he's going to go for more punishment, twists Tobis' arm in a wristlock, steps over the extended limb, falls to the side, Tobis' shoulders are down, leg hooks his leg 1..2...kickout! What is it going to take to put this man away? Stevens:Superlative performance by both men. Back and forth match, initially Doom was on the offensive, then Tobis' took the offensive with the attack on Doom's leg and now Doom is back in the driving seat. Doom, taking Tobis over to a corner. He's got him in a side headlock and is climbing the ropes. Onto the top turnbuckle, he's picking Tobis up...Good Lord! it look's like....a top rope Razor's Edge!!!! Perfectly executed, devastating move, Doom himself drained with the effort. What a move that was, but can he cover and will it be enough. Doom crawls across, and hooks Tobis' leg the cover, the count 1...2...3!! the victory!!!!!!! Starr:Phenomenal, absolutely phenomenal. What a way to close out the New Year. This match epitomizing the spirit of the WIF. There was no championship at stake, but such is the level of competition, such is the level of pride of these great performers of ours that they are willing to put everything on the line, at each and every WIF card. It has been a great year, this has been the great night, this is John Starr, on behalf of myself and Jack Stevens wishing you all a very Happy New Year. Just before we go, let's here the official word: Announcer:Ladies and Gentlemen, here is your winner...DR.DOOM!!!! (The screen fades as the referee raises Doom's arm in the ring.)
