What you missed on the last WIF card.

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A lot of things happened on the last card. Number 1 we have a new World champion. In a classic encounter Lancaster Bomber defeated Barbwire. The match of the evening though was between Falcon and Roundhouse. A classic battle that could easily have gone either way until Blindsider came down to ringside and clocked Roundhouse with the ring bell. In another classic tag title defense by Search & Destroy, Zac and Da Bomb gave them 27 minutes of action. In other matches, newcomer Harley Davidson pinned Angel. Fangs defeated Alien King by spitting blood in his eyes and stunning him enough to get the pin. Blindsider defeated Stone Cold Da Killa. In upcoming weeks Roundhouse will be getting his chance to get revenge against Blindsider as the match has been signed. This week the fans are going to be treated to a hardcore match as Falcon will be putting his Hardcore title on the line against former world heavyweight champion Barbwire. This match will be no dq, no timelimit anything goes brawl in which the loser must state "I respect you" to the winner. I'm working on getting other matches signed as we speak.
