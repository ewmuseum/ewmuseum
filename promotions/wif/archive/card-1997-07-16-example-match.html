Here is a match that took place in the WIF a couple weeks ago, just to show you what the WIF can do if motivated by the right handlers: ------------------------------------------------------------ Marty Woods: Welcome back, ladies and gentlemen, to the conclusion of tonight's thunderous event! I am Marty Woods alongside veteran WIF commentator Warren Vaughan to present to you tonight's main event, the eight-man tag team match between, the WIF's Dream Team, consisting of Dr. Doom, the Jaw, Roundhouse, and Tony "Falcon" Silver, and the team of the Cold Blooded Crew, Cold Blooded Nitrogen, Cold Blooded Hydrogen, Shock, and Burn! They have already been introduced and are up in the ring right now, all eight of them ready to go! Warren Vaughan: The Cold Blooded Crew has really raised the ire of certain WIF superstars, starting with Dr. Doom, whom Nitrogen attacked after a match with the Falcon a few weeks ago. From there, Maniac Bob worked his magic to create perhaps the greatest team ever formed in wrestling. Though mortal enemies, these four men hold a great respect for each other and for this great league and sport. They feel that there is no place for the Cold Blooded Crew in the whole mix. And they are about to get it on in the ring! MW: What a conclusion to this extraordinary event, I tell you what. To have this match on a card boosts the card's rating ten fold. Here we go! It's the big man Roundhouse in there kicking things off with Cold Blooded Nitrogen, perpetrator of the whole ordeal. Roundhouse runs at him and nails him with a knee to the midsection and backs him up to the ropes, then whips him across the ring, to the ropes, and hits with a BIG boot on Nitrogen's rebound! Nitrogen is stunned but doesn't go down! Nitrogen turns around 180 degrees and now Roundhouse runs the ropes and comes from behind, NAILING a bulldog on Nitrogen! These fans are going absolutely nuts! Listen to them! WV: Roundhouse gets a burst of energy from this crowd and now goes for the tag to his mate, Tony Silver! Falcon climbs to the top turnbuckle and is preparing to leap! He leaps as Nitrogen gets to his feet! Falcon spun in the air, hitting Nitrogen with a flying spinning heel kick! Nitrogen is out like a light on the mat and now Falcon gets up and covers! 1…..2….. kickout by Nitrogen. Nitrogen is not ready to be defeated just yet. He gets to his feet as does Falcon. Quickly, Nitrogen gouges the eyes of Falcon to gain his team's first advantage in the match. And now a European uppercut gives him time to get his act together and go for the tag to Cold Blooded Hydrogen. MW: Hydrogen comes in and hits Falcon with a boot to the midsection and now positions himself to execute his Cold Blooded Stunner, a variation of the jawbreaker!!! Quick and catlike, he smoothly hits one of the most devastating maneuvers in the sport. Falcon is down on the mat, shell shocked. Hydrogen's team is celebrating this move in their corner and the Dream Team look on, hoping for Falcon to regain his consciousness and composure. Hydrogen is following in though. He reaches down and picks Falcon up, now whipping him to the Cold Blooded corner! WV: Look at them! The Cold Blooded Crew are on Falcon like a pack of dogs on a three-legged cat! Hydrogen is drawing the referee's attention away from the action and now the Dream Team corner is going nuts, telling the referee to turn around! Maniac Bob gets up on the ring apron and gets the official's attention! The official is arguing with Bob! Bob now has the power of the Interim President but this official doesn't look too intimidated! Look at them! They are beating the hell out of Falcon! But here comes the Dream Team!!! The whole team just stormed the ring and came to the corner, where they are now brawling with the Cold Blooded Crew!!! Slugging it out in the corner, now the official has lost total control! Bob gets down from the ring apron and the official turns to see complete chaos! MW: Roundhouse is taking out Hydrogen while Doom is going after Shock and Jaw is going after Nitrogen. Burn, who should keep his dominance over Falcon, doesn't and tries to help out Shock in his battle with Doom. Doom is fending off two men there and Roundhouse is taking care of business with Hydrogen! He has him in the corner, pounding away at him. Doom is being backed into another corner now as he is being double-teamed. Doom kicks Shock in the midsection and elbows Burn in the mush! He pounds on the back of Shock with a forearm and now tosses him through the ropes, out of the ring! And now he has breathing room to get out of the corner! He does and hits Burn with a kick to the midsection! WV: WHOA!!! Jaw just launched Nitrogen over the top turnbuckle and to the floor! And now Doom whips Burn to Jaw, who scoops him up on the run! And he crashes down on him with a sidewalk slam!!! Wow!!! Roundhouse hits a slingshot in the corner on Hydrogen, making him smack his head on the steel post! And now Roundhouse hits an atomic drop on Hydrogen, sending HIM over the top rope and to the floor! And Burn voluntarily rolls out of the ring to join his stable. Look at the Dream Team in the ring right now! They are jumping around, getting this amazing crowd going! And they are going nuts! The Cold Blooded Crew are on the outside of the ring trying to get their act together while Falcon is regrouping on the inside. MW: The referee is escorting the Dream Team to their corner right now and Falcon tags to Doom. Doom comes into the ring and waits for one of the Cold Blooded Crew to enter. It doesn't look like any of them want to enter the ring against such a monster as Dr. Doom. He is totally ready to destroy the Cold Blooded Crew and he'll do it single-handedly if he has to! Finally he gets some opposition in the ring with him as Shock enters the ring. Immediately, though, Shock is nailed with a knee to the side of the head by Doom and now Doom is down hammering on Shock, punching and hitting him with forearms! Shock is trying to fight back by trading blows with Doom, but it isn't very effective because Doom is relentless. Doom is going off on him, finally nailing him completely to the mat! And now he is dropping his knee repeatedly on the midsection of Shock, really loosening up his ribs. WV: Doom now takes Shock by the head and pulls him to his feet, avoiding having Shock tag one of his partners as he takes him to the center of the ring, standing toe-to-toe with him. Now Doom runs back to the ropes where he is kicked by one of the Cold Blooded Crew, distracting him a bit but not taking him off of his game plan! He runs at Shock and nails him with a leaping shoulderblock to the midsection! Now he quickly gets up and hits his Doomsday DDT!!! Cover!!! 1…..2…..3!!! Wow! That is a great job of focusing by Doom. He is really focused in this match, not letting that kick he received throw him off of his game plan. He knew what he wanted to do and he executed it perfectly, eliminating a member of the Cold Blooded Crew! MW: Doom is immediately attacked by Nitrogen! But Doom keeping up with his attacker by slugging it out with him, hitting him with shots to the breadbasket! Finally, he senses weakness in his opponent and he nails him with a European uppercut! And now a knee to the midsection! And he hooks the bent Nitrogen with a gutwrench and nails him with a gutwrench suplex, taking him back and down hard to the mat! Doom has tons of momentum here but he is a bit tired and doesn't want to take any chances, so he quickly goes for the tag to Jaw! Jaw is in the ring right now, going right toward Nitrogen, backing the seven-footer into the corner! Wow! Jaw must be very intimidating! No! Nitrogen was playing the scared cat the whole time! He reached up and grabbed Jaw by his tights, pulling him over his head so that Jaw would hit his head on the turnbuckle! Jaw is a bit stunned now and Nitrogen slides behind Jaw, standing up now. WV: Jaw is a bit stunned indeed. He looks like he hit his head on the steel post when he went in. He looks to be busted wide open, bleeding from his forehead. Nitrogen follows in on Jaw now and claws him in that gash which is now clearly visible because of the clawing. Nitrogen rakes it now and moves Jaw to the ropes, now whipping him. Nitrogen puts up his boot for Jaw as he comes in but Jaw ducks Nitrogen's boot and continues running the ropes, though very slowly because he is a big man. Nitrogen turns around and runs at Jaw, hitting him with a clothesline that sends him reeling on the ropes! Jaw is almost about to go over the top rope! Nitrogen runs back to the ropes and comes back quickly at Jaw, going for another clothesline! But Jaw ducked and hit a giant backdrop, sending Nitrogen up and over the top rope, smashing hard on the floor!!! MW: Wow! Did you see the elevation on that backdrop??? Jaw flipped him with authority! He didn't even touch the ropes when he went over. It looked like Nitrogen was put on a catapult and launched! *laughs* Nitrogen is out cold on the floor out there and his teammates are coming over to help him, but are being backed away by the opposing team. These guys mean business! And look at how friendly the Dream Team is. They get Nitrogen to his feet and roll him into the ring. Jaw goes over and tags in Falcon. Now it's Falcon and Nitrogen! WV: Falcon runs over to Nitrogen and drops a leg on the back of his head. Now he turns over and puts on a front facelock. Right now we are seeing the new and improved Falcon working his magic. Falcon has become more of a submission artist of late, reducing the risk he takes when he goes to the top. If the guy is stunned and doesn't know where Falcon is, it's a lot easier for Falcon to pursue his aerial attack. Falcon is currently wearing down his opponent with a front facelock, doing an excellent job of keeping him from reaching the ropes. Falcon sure has been practicing this submission style of late, preparing for his recent battle with Barbwire in that unbelievable submission match. Now Falcon has carried that experience to merge with his regular style of wrestling. And he's doing a good job of executing this style against Nitrogen right here. MW: Nitrogen, being as big as he is, isn't very hard to wear down. Nitrogen isn't making many moves to try to escape this hold. Actually, I'm surprised that Falcon has the hold on such a big man. He needs to get all the way around his arms on this one and he has done so. Not many people could put a front facelock on a guy that is 6'11", 324 pounds, and has a reach like that. WV: But you've got to remember, Marty, that he's been practicing on the 6'9", 300+ pound Sapphire. They are training together and therefore, he is getting good competition and learning how to handle big guys like this. Nitrogen now is making his move. He's to his knees and is moving his uncontrolled arm around the waist of Falcon, moving him back to the corner with the drive in his powerful legs. Falcon is now between a rock and a hard place as he has virtually no way of escaping the pursuit of this monster they call Cold Blooded Nitrogen. MW: Falcon, as you know, is very cunning and I think he can find a way out of this if Nitrogen doesn't make his move quickly. Nitrogen is now backing up as Falcon is forced to release the hold. Nitrogen is playing this very well, escaping the maneuver by grabbing the ropes behind Falcon. Falcon lets go and Nitrogen has Falcon trapped in the corner, but he really doesn't know what to do with Falcon! He is standing there, stunned from the hold that was just applied to him! Falcon sees this advantage and quickly capitalizes! He hops up to sit on the top turnbuckle, leans back and sticks his feet in the air, wrapping them around the head of Nitrogen and taking him over with a flying headscissors, flipping him over the top rope and to the floor! Wow! WV: See, right there, those are the effects of a submission hold applied and then the capitalization by Falcon on the stunned Nitrogen. Now Falcon is again climbing to the top turnbuckle! Oh no! What's he going to do? Nitrogen is laying on the outside, down and out! Falcon leaps and hits a flying elbow smash to the outside! Wow! He had a ton of elevation on that elbow smash right there and a tremendous amount of impact as he hit it on the floor! I don't know if Nitrogen is conscious out there! He's been taking a pounding here! MW: Whoa!!! Look who decided to join the fray on the outside of the ring! The Dream Team are all down on the floor taking their shots at Nitrogen! Jaw just landed a legdrop on the back of his head and Doom is pounding on him with axehandles! Roundhouse is stomping away at him! Falcon is getting in the ring, waiting for his teammates to set Nitrogen up! They all get Nitrogen to his feet and Falcon runs the ropes, coming back, leaping over, and NAILING Nitrogen with a somersault plancha!!! The Dream Team got out of Falcon's way as he lept through the air for that maneuver. Wow! WV: They all begin hammering on him again! The referee, who was at the time distracted by the Cold Blooded Crew on the other side of the ring, now comes to the outside of the ring to see all this carnage. Falcon is setting a pic on the official so he can't get through, but the official is doing everything possible to stop this madness on the outside of the ring. He's in the center of the pile-up right now and nobody sees him. Whoa! Jaw just inadvertently backhanded the official!!! The official goes down and quickly gets back up! And he's pointing at Jaw and now going over to the officials' table! He has just disqualified Jaw! Whoa! MW: The whole Dream Team comes into the ring after the official now to complain at him! They leave Nitrogen on the outside and start a justified bitch-fest. If that official wouldn't have stuck his nose into the ordeal, then Jaw wouldn't have hit him! Maniac Bob is livid on the ring apron, almost falling over the top rope and into the ring! Look at this melee! The referee is now tossing Bob!!! Wow!!! He just gave him the boot from ringside with the movement of his arm and finger in the general direction of the locker room! What the hell is going on here?! WV: I guess this official is tired of all the crap going down in this match and has now tossed Maniac Bob and the Jaw from this match. This is very unfortunate for the Dream Team. They just had a boatload of momentum and now this! The official is really destroying the Dream Team's plans to dominate the Cold Blooded Crew. But it looks like things are settling down at least a little bit as Jaw and Bob are evacuating the area. But look at this! From the back door, Nitrogen travels all the way from where he was at over to the other side of the ring and rolls in on that side to make the tag to Cold Blooded Hydrogen! This has definitely been a positive switch for the Cold Blooded Crew! Now they have a fresh man in the ring and an even match with their opponents. MW: Hydrogen dives into the pile of his opponents who were still having a discussion with the official and starts nailing them with elbows, punches, forearms, knees, and kicks. He is a wild man in there! And now he is focusing his attention on Falcon, the legal man in the match, who has been in there a very long time in this match. He hits him with a couple of knee lifts and then sends him to the ropes. He misses him with a clothesline as Falcon ducks and continues running the ropes. Hydrogen gets a lift on Falcon on his second rebound and falls back, NAILING Falcon with a stun-gun! Wow!!! WV: Did you see Falcon's neck snap back on that one??? Unbelievable! Hydrogen quickly follows in and plants an elbow to the face of Falcon, now covering! 1…..2…..thr-NO! Falcon got his foot up on the bottom rope! Whoa! That was a close one! Falcon nearly lost it right there! But he barely saved it for himself! Now Hydrogen lifts Falcon up by his head and whips him into a corner. He charges and leaps for the avalanche but Falcon moves! Hydrogen braces himself though as Falcon moved! MW: Hydrogen didn't eat turnbuckle, luckily for him. But Falcon has escaped and he is going for the tag to Roundhouse! Roundhouse gets in the ring and comes at Hydrogen head-on, going for a big boot! But Hydrogen ducks the big boot and now sweeps the other leg of Roundhouse! Roundhouse goes down in a heap and now Hydrogen is going to work on that limb of Roundhouse's! He is twisting it with a toehold now. WV: This is quite a sight to see. Two men as big as this and one is going for the other's leg. Hydrogen knows how it feels to get his wheels taken from beneath him and now he is administering that lesson to Roundhouse who, I am sure, knows the feeling all too well by now. Hydrogen is now clamping on a spinning toehold, really putting the pressure on Roundhouse. The problem with putting any kind of submission hold on a big man is that they are so big and have so long of a reach that it is easier for them to reach the ropes to break the hold. Uh-oh! It looks like Hydrogen is going for a figure-four leglock! He has it hooked and quickly backs up! Wow! Hydrogen is hanging out of the ring to put more distance between the ring ropes and Roundhouse! Not to mention that, with Hydrogen hanging there, he is getting more leverage and putting more pressure on the hold. Roundhouse is screaming in agony from the hold, straining to reach the ropes but highly unsuccessful. Hydrogen isn't allowing any leeway. MW: Roundhouse is now sitting up and sliding toward Hydrogen! Look at this! He's using the fact that Hydrogen is hanging out of the ring to his advantage! He continues sliding easily until he reaches the ring ropes under which their legs are locked together! Great move there by Roundhouse, and now the official goes out of the ring to release the hold. WV: Wrestling is not just about what move you put on a guy. That's just the offensive part. Real wrestling skill comes from being able to counter holds and play well defensively. That move right there signified that Roundhouse is an excellent competitor and worthy of being a top contender in the WIF. Now Roundhouse slips down to the outside where the referee has untangled the legs of the two competitors. Whoa! Upon the release of the leglock, Roundhouse went down and nailed Hydrogen with a heart punch as Hydrogen was on his back on the floor! Whoa! Hydrogen came back with a kick to the groin and now Roundhouse is shaken! Burn comes flying off of the ring apron and hits a flying clothesline on Roundhouse! Wow! Now Burn starts pounding on Roundhouse on the floor! And Hydrogen is joining in on the Roundhouse-bashing! The referee is in there to split everything up though, and Burn goes back to the ring apron to stand next to Nitrogen, who is still taking his time in reviving from that beating he took earlier. MW: Hydrogen now grabs Roundhouse's head and goes to slam it into the steel guardrail! Wow! And now he takes Roundhouse and whips him into the ring, smashing his back on the ring apron! And now Hydrogen comes in with a clothesline on the big man as he was laying against the ring apron! I think Roundhouse is busted open, cut on his forehead from that pounding he took into the steel guardrail! Earlier, we saw Jaw get busted open at the hands of the Cold Blooded Crew. Now we see another member of the Dream Team fall with the same fate. Hydrogen now grabs hold of Roundhouse by the throat and hoists him up to chokeslam him on the floor!!! Wow! Roundhouse is out cold! Hydrogen rolls into the ring and is now posing and showing off for the fans, who are responding with a tumult of jeers. WV: The official is in the ring administering his ten-count and look! Roundhouse still hasn't moved! This would be totally devastating for the Dream Team to go down three-to-two in this war! Come on Roundhouse! Get up! MW: He really took a pummelling from that chokeslam on the floor. I don't know if Roundhouse will be able to get up at all, let alone before the ten count! The official is up to seven! Eight! Nine! Ten! Oh no! He calls for the bell! The Dream Team is at a severe disadvantage! WV: This is unbelievable. Just a few minutes ago, the Dream Team had the Cold Blooded Crew in the palm of their hand, with Falcon dominating Nitrogen! But bing bang boom, the Cold Blooded Crew gets one lucky break and now THEY have an advantage in the match. Doom doesn't look intimidated at all as he runs into the ring and attacks Hydrogen from behind with a clip! He took the leg of Hydrogen right out from under him and now he goes up to pound on his head. The fans are going nuts cheering for Doom in the ring against Hydrogen! He is pounding away, punching at the head of Hydrogen! And now he gets to his feet and runs the ropes, coming back to drop an elbow on his nemesis! And he goes back to pounding! MW: Now he gets Hydrogen up to his feet and takes him to the ropes with a whip! And he NAILS a backdrop on Hydrogen's rebound! Doom is getting energized from the cheers of this crowd! He follows in on Hydrogen and gets him to his feet, then moves him back to the corner. There's a cross-ring whip! Hydrogen crashes into the corner! Doom charges and spins to nails a back elbow to the mush of Hydrogen! Wow! Doom going to work in the ring right now! Hydrogen stunned coming out of the corner and Doom gets in front of him and drops down to nail a jawbreaker on Hydrogen! Cover! 1…..2…..3!!! WV: WOW! Doom is a wild man in the ring right now, going off on Hydrogen and perfectly executing a jawbreaker, which just happens to be Hydrogen's finishing maneuver! Doom quickly gets up and turns to hit a kick to the midsection of Burn, who was charging Doom! Nice job of Doom to avoid being attacked from behind by Burn! Doom now perfectly executes a gutwrench suplex and dives back to smack the hand of Tony "Falcon" Silver! Falcon leaps to the top turnbuckle and perches himself, waiting for Burn to get to his feet! Burn gets up and Falcon dives for a cross-body but Burn catches Falcon and nails him with a powerslam!!! Cover!!! 1…..2…..kickout! MW: Falcon barely kicked out of that cover by Burn! Now Burn gets to his feet and hits the tag to Nitrogen! Nitrogen steps into the ring by stepping over the top rope and is now teasing the crowd as he closes in on Falcon. Falcon is groggy getting to his feet, not knowing where he is. He really took a pounding with that powerslam by Burn! Nitrogen is yapping at Falcon now, giving him a little chin music, and now he quickly goes to hit a DDT!!! WOW! He brought that DDT out of nowhere! And now Nitrogen is really giving it to the fans! Nitrogen is calling for the end of Falcon! He raises his hand in the air! Oh no! I think we're going to see the Cold Blooded Bomb! WV: Doom is on the ring apron stomping and getting the fans behind Falcon, trying to give Falcon an adrenaline rush! But Falcon is limp as Nitrogen picks him up! Nitrogen now plants Falcon between his legs and is yelling for the Cold Blooded Bomb! LOOK!!! Falcon with an inside cradle!!! 1…..2…..thr-NO!!! Kickout by Nitrogen just barely! Nitrogen barely escaped the trap of the sneaky Falcon and now is angry and embarrassed because of what just happened! He gets up and starts stomping away at Falcon, kicking him in the back and sides! MW: Falcon is now paying for that move he pulled off earlier! He is being dominated now by Nitrogen! Nitrogen gets Falcon to his feet and throws him into the corner hard! And now he goes into the corner with a hard knee to Falcon's midsection! He hits him again with the knee! And again! Again! He's repeatedly kneeing Falcon in the midsection in the corner and now he has Falcon bent. He gets behind Falcon in the corner and now runs with Falcon's head, trying for a bulldog! No!!! Falcon threw Nitrogen off of him and Nitrogen went crashing into the corner! Falcon quickly tags in Dr. Doom now and goes to the top turnbuckle! What's this??? Doom comes in and gets behind Nitrogen, hoisting him up on his shoulders! It looks like they're going for Search and Destroy's Roadkill maneuver! But Falcon is turned around, facing the crowd! Falcon leaps and WHOA!!! Falcon just hit the hoisted Nitrogen with a moonsault body block up there and Nitrogen comes down hard!!! Doom runs the ropes and Falcon rolls out of the ring! Doom comes back and hits Nitrogen with a splash into a cover! 1…..2…..3!!! WV: WOW!!! The Dream Team really putting pressure on the Cold Blooded Crew! What a move by Falcon and Doom! Great tag teamwork here in this match! Amazing maneuver, I'll tell you what! Perhaps Search and Destroy should practice that one! Unbelievable! Now it's Doom and Falcon against Burn! Burn is the last remaining member of the Cold Blooded Crew! This should be very interesting! Burn reluctantly steps into the ring and looks frightened as he stands in the corner. WHOA!!! Falcon just came from behind Doom, leapfrogged him, and nailed Burn with a splash in the corner without even touching the ground! Falcon now hammering on Burn in the corner and here comes Doom to help with the beating! MW: They are pummelling Burn in the corner now, nailing him with knees, punches, kicks, and forearms! The referee is giving them a five-count and now Falcon backs out of the ordeal and goes to his corner to stand on the ring apron, raising his arm to the crowd as they respond with tons of cheers! Doom is in the corner with Burn, pounding away at him still! Now he takes him out of the corner with a mare and runs from behind the sitting Burn, nailing him with a short bulldog!!! Wow!!! He just snapped the body of Burn right in half, bulldogging him from the sitting position! Burn may have broken his neck there! Wow! WV: Doom putting on quite a performance in the ring tonight, absolutely dominating this match! Now Doom gets Burn to his feet and sends him to the ropes! He catches Burn on the rebound and NAILS him with a spinebuster!!! Great move there by Doom! The crowd is going nuts! Doom circles his hand in the air to call for the end and pulls Burn to his feet! Doom kicks Burn in the midsection and quickly nails the Doomsday DDT!!! He gives a yell and goes down for the cover, hooking the leg! 1….2…..3!!!!! *ding ding ding* MW: The Dream Team has won the match! They won it! Falcon comes into the ring and jumps for Doom to catch him! They are in the ring celebrating the victory now! And here come Roundhouse, Jaw, and Maniac Bob! They are joining with the celebration and the crowd is rocking as well! As they shake hands and congratulate each other on their victory, Burn hobbles to the back. WV: That was a great job of working together by these four men who are big enemies when it comes to normal ring experience. I'm surprised they stayed this focused in the match. Most enemies wouldn't join forces and execute their plan that well, but this Dream Team did and they accomplished the task of wiping out the Cold Blooded Crew. Will the Cold Blooded Crew come back? It will be interesting to see what will happen in the next couple of weeks. MW: This has been a tremendous night of wrestling action and the WIF is proud to have produced this great card. Folks, we hope you've enjoyed tonight's action. We will return next week with another night of great WIF wrestling. For now, this is Marty Woods and Warren Vaughan for the entire WIF production staff saying so long.
