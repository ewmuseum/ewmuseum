World Internet Federation — Founding

""WIF" HAS ITS FIRST EVER WRESTLER! PLEASE JOIN NOW"

23-26 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The fed's own first-ever recruitment post, 23 May 1996:

THE WORLD INTERNET FEDERATION HAS ITS FIRST EVER COMPETITOR! ENIGMA. WHO HAILS FROM BERMUDA. WEIGHT : 275lbs. HEIGHT : 6'6" DON'T BE SHY ENTER YOUR WRESTLERS TODAY!

The full recruitment form, the same day:

I AM STARTING UP A NEW FANTASY WRESTLING FEDERATION AND WOULD LOVE ANYONE TO JOIN WITH SINGLE WRESTLERS OR TAG TEAMS. AT THE MOMENT I WILL BE RUNNING IT BY E-MAIL BUT I HOPE TO HAVE MY WEB PAGE UP AND RUNNING BY THE END OF AUGUST. IF YOU WOULD LIKE TO JOIN JUST E-MAIL THE COMPLETED FORM BELOW. YOUR NAME : E-MAIL ADDRESS : WRESTLERS NAME : HEIGHT : WEIGHT : FAN FAVOURITE/NETURAL/RULE BREAKER : HAILS FROM : ENTRANCE MUSIC : optional MANAGER/VALET/BODYGAURD : optional TEAM NAME : if a tag team FINISHING HOLD/MOVE : include a description if you have named the hold. WHAT TYPE OF WRESTLER IS HE : choose two of the following - Strength, Toughness, Inteligence, Conditioning, Cheating, Brawling, Technical, Mat wrestling, Aerial, Quickness, Team work. FAVOURITE TYPE OF MATCH : optional STYLE : what makes him different? What makes the fans/other wrestlers like him or hate him? MODELED AFTER : optional (pro-wrestler competing at the moment. IF A TAG TEAM : fill the form out like this WEIGHT : 245lbs, 265lbs. THEN JUST E-MAIL IT TO ME AT [detail withheld] AS SOON AS ENOUGH WRESTLERS ARE IN THE FEDERATION, THERE WILL BE A TOURNAMENT OR BATTLE ROYAL TO DECIDE THE CHAMPIONS.

A tag-team addendum, three days later (26 May 1996):

Hello again I forgot to mention that you can send in tag teams also, to [detail withheld], and then send in two singles wrestlers, along with that
send a tag team name and a tag team finisher, and a discription of the tag team finisher. See yeah in the ring!
