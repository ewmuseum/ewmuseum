World Internet Federation — Card Preview

"[WIF] Wendesday Main Event PreVu"

10 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[scene opens on Smiley Davidson standing at a glass desk in fron of a big sign that reads "PreVu"] SD: Hi everybody and welcome to PreVu. I'm your host, Smiley Davidson. here is only one word to describe this weeks card: HUGE! And one of the highlighting matches, the battle of the Juggernauts: Iron versus the Sapphire. [a few stills of the Too Hot To Handle Internet Title match are shopwn on the screen, Davidson narrates] SD: Only a short time ago, the Sapphire was in probably the most hard-fought match of his career, at Too Hot to Handle, when he took the Dark Ninja to the limit. The most important move was probably this one. [picture of Sapphire executing the Blue Death backbreaker on the Dark Ninja is shown] SD: The Dark Ninja was in the Blue Death, which alone may be the most painful hold in the WIF, for almost 5 minutes, screaming in pain, and his manager was ready to thrown in the towel literally. But the Ninja found away to escape and squeaked in a win over the enormous Sapphire. More on that match later. [scene returns to Smiley at the desk] SD: Anyway, after that match, Sapphire was looking for action, so he issued an open contract. That's where Iron comes in, but on the last Main Event, Iron had some trouble of his own. Let me set the scene: wild card tag match. Hitman and the Supersonic versus "Rain Man" Bill Bradley and Iron. Most people didn't know if the Sergeant of Storms and the Ironman could cooperate. Well this speaks for itself: [footage of the Wild Card Tag Match is shown, Davidson still narrates] SD: This is where it starts, Watch carefully. Up till this point in the match it was a real see-saw. >C.Ross:Iron executes a powerbomb now. Hitman in big trouble, grabbing his >back and yelling in pain, The Godfather trying to calm him from the >outside. Whip to the ropes by Iron, reversed by Hitman. > >J.Starr:OH LORD! Iron crashing into Bradley and Bradley getting knocked >off the apron and onto the floor. Hitman crawls across, stretches and tags >Supersonic. > >C.Ross:Iron still with his back turned, looking on aghast. Supersonic runs >in and dropkicks him to the outside. Iron now, picks himself off the floor >and is asking Rain Man if he's okay. Both men now back up on the apron, >Iron steps in. SD: The two seem to have no trouble here but now Iron takes over the Supersonic with a bodyslam and a military press. And as we rejoin the action, Iron is climbing to the top for a moonsault, which is quite a feat for such a big guy. >C.Ross:And hits with a moonsault! Great agility shown by the big man, goes >for a cover, 1-2 What is this?! Rain Man kicking him in the back and >breaking the cover. > >J.Starr:Iron can't believe it, some shoving and finger pointing between >the two. Supersonic getting up behind Iron, Iron look out! > >C.Ross:Roll up, 1-2-3. Its over, iron absolutely disgusted. And look at >this, Rain Man raising The Supersonic's hand and celebrating with him and >Hitman. > >J.Starr: Well, he may be celebrating now but Iron looked livid as he left >the ring and he's not going to take kindly to being double crossed. >Awesome showing by Iron today against both opponents today, I would not >want to be "Rain Man" Bill Bradley after what happened today. > >C.Ross:Yes, Iron looked very good today despite being on the losing side. >However, I'm sure Rain man has a few tricks up his sleeve as well. It'll >be one hell of a feud. [scene cuts back to Smiley Davidson] SD: Iron was really mad about this and then challenged the turncoat Bill Bradley. [cuts to interview with Iron] >Iron: "Rain Man" Bill Bradley. You knew that this day was going to come. >You want a feud? You want to betray Iron? Well, I'll tell you what. I >don't get angry often, but I am pretty upset now. I really hoped that you >had made a turn-around. Obviously, you haven't. Well, let's have a match. >I'd rather settle grudges in the ring, by the rules, instead of cheap moves >like your behind-the-back attacks. The time and place of the match don't >matter because I'm always prepared to wrestle. So go buy an extra pair of >underwear at K-mart because you'll need them after our match. (Sorry, I >just had to say that.) *laughs* Good luck, buddy. [again returns to Smiley] SD: But for an unknown reason, there was no response from Bradley so Iron had some anger to take out on someone. He heard about the open contract of Sapphire's and had this to say. [cuts to another Iron interview] >Iron: Sapphire, you have an open contract? Well, I would like to take that >offer up. Everybody else that I have challenged in this league is not >responding. I enjoyed your match with Dark Ninja. I would like to test >your ability. I have tremendous respect for the rules and for everybody >involved in the league, so you can expect a clean match from me. I just >want an opportunity to prove myself to this league. Just tell me when. >Good luck. Have a nice day. [returns] SD: And, unlike ol' Double B, here's what the Sapphire had to say. [cuts to a Sapphire interview] >(The Sapphire is lifting weights in Mr.Charisma's gym, Mr.Charisma looks on.) > >Mr.Charisma:Iron just closed your open contract. What do you think? > >(Sapphire continues pressing the weights) > >Sapphire: I'll tell you something about Iron. I was watching his wild card >match on T.V at home. I think that is the most impressive debut we've seen >in the W.I.F. Infact I was quite close to asking him to tag with me. Then >I decided I should concentrate on singles. I think Iron is the best man to >accept the challenge you issued. He's almost as big as I am, and has shown >he can really wrestle. Its going to be tough. It'll be a great match, but >the better man will win. I am the better man. > >Iron, you're very good but when you deal with me, you are dealing with the >best. Listen up and listen good, I will beat you. See you in the ring. [returns yet again] SD: So there you have it, the fans are in for one heck of a match. Another match, that will not be wrestling. It will be nothing but a brawl. A street fight. A Brooklyn Street Fight that is. After losing the Steel Cage Match to the Falcon, Fisto Flash and Robo Stone are hungry for something sweet. Like the oh-so sweet taste of revenge. So they challenged the Falcon to a Brooklyn Street Fight, Fisto's specialty. And what did Charisma and the Knight have to say about that? [cut to a Falcon interview] (The Falcon, The Dark Knight and The Sapphire are discussing recent W.I.F happenings with Mr.Charisma. Mr.Charisma sits at the head of the table in his study. The Falcon is to his left, The Sapphire to his right and the Dark Knight faces him.) Mr.Charisma:Fisto Flash wasn't too pleased about what happened at the steel cage match. Falcon:Well, if he thinks I'm around here to please him, he's got another thing coming. A Brooklyn Street Fight? No rules, no ref, well, as much as I hate him Flash seems a man after my own heart. That's just the way I like it. Flash, I'm going to beat you to a pulp, yes, you will lose at you're own game and believe me it won't be pretty. Stone, Flash, Challenge Accepted. [returns] SD: But of course, Stone and Flash never at a loss for words. [cut to Fisto Flash inteview] >Robo Stone: Falcon, we know that you haven't forgotten. How can you??? >When the most brutal force in e-wrestling challenges you to a Street >Fight; YOU CAN'T FORGET!!!!!!! 'Cause ya' know that if ya' DO forget, >Fisto Flash will find ya', and DESTROY ya'!!!!!!!! > >Fisto Flash: FALCON!!!! YA' FREAKIN' BIRD!!!!!! EVERYONE knows >that birds can't fight!!!!! All birds know how to do is to FLY away in >when they're in danger a' bein' hunted. Well, I'm gonna be one a' >those hunters. I'm gonna stalk ya', follow ya', and when I reach ya', >you can only hope to fly away, 'cause I'm gonna put ya', SIX-FEET >UNDER!!!!!! This is MY kind of match!!!!! In MY hometown!!!!! And >rest asure, it'll be MY victory!!!!!!! > >Robo Stone: Brooklyn Street Fight????? Falcon, you're a goner. >HA! HA! HA! HA! HA! > >(Fisto Flash poses) [returns to front desk] SD: I mean who knows what they're gonna use in that one, tables, baseball bats, automobiles, fire extinguishers, man, the list keeps going on and on. And next up is match that just came up out of the blue! Here is the original challenge to the Supersonic. [cut to Headbanger Interview] The Headbanger: I want to challenge you to a casket match on Wednesday night main event. [cut to Supersonic interview] >Supersonic:I accept [returns to Smiley] SD: Talk about cut and dry. They was about as clear as it can be. So the casket match is set. Now onto the tag scene where the four biggies are talking up a storm, no not about Bill Bradley. [cut to Search and Destroy Interview] >Hey Dairy Products Gone Bad we did you two fools inside 1 minute in the PGF. >Now we're here in the WIF we want another match. We will keep our titles!!!! [returns] SD: Wellll, I wouldn't want to be the High Fliers if I'm in the same hemisphere as the Steele's. Anyway, with Search and Destroy looking for a title shot, they get one, not from the `Dairy Boys' but from the High Fliers. And the next match I'll discuss is between the Dragon Master and Enigma. And it looks like Enigma is pretty fired up. [cut to Enigma interview] > "Well now the WIF has finally done it. I have been jobbed for >weeks upon weeks and now it will stop! [cut to Dragon Master Interview] >the dragon master has an open contract match anyone man enough to fight me >hu hu well then bring it on [cut to Enigma interview] Enigma will take on this Dragon Master and beat him! [returns] SD: Well that's the match. But I think everyone should either be staying away from Enigma or battling Enigma. Enigma is really going off at everybody! This next interview has not been responded to and times up. So I guess the Lancaster Bomber is going to have to wait to face Robski and the Hitman. [cut to Lancaster Bomb interview] >Looking through some mail and at the rankings I've just discovered Robski >(Yobski) >and the Hitman are here. >Guess what boys? Now The Lancaster Bomber is too. Title versus title, the Dark Ninja versus Slayer. Here's how it started. [cut to Dark Ninja Interview] >(Scene is Dark Ninja's dressing room. And now a recap of the entire card: ****************************************************************************** Falcon vs. Fisto Flash in a Brooklyn Street Fight ****************************************************************************** Dragon Master vs. Enigma ****************************************************************************** The Headbanger vs. Supersonic in a Casket Match ****************************************************************************** Search and Destroy vs. the High Fliers ****************************************************************************** Iron vs. Sapphire ****************************************************************************** SD: And don't forget about Dark Ninja versus Slayer on Weekend War!
