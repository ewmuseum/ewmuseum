World Internet Federation — Presidential Succession

"WIF's Changes of Command, 1996"

30 May - 12 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

30 May 1996 — a change of command is announced:

I am going to be taking over the WIF June 1st. I need 2 or 3 co-presidents that will help w/ deciding outcomes, writing matches and moderating flashes. Please mail me ASAP if you want to help. First come, first serve.
[name withheld]

3 June 1996 — the new president announces the league's first pay-per-view, to run live in a web chat room:

I am going to allow 10 people to enter the WIF. For these people to be accepted I need at least 2 people to volunteer to join the WIF Board of Directors. We are an up and coming league. We will be having our first PPV under my incumbency as president Friday through a WWW chat room. This will be a breakthrough event for e-wrestling. For more info e-mail me.
[name withheld]

23 June 1996 — a recruiting drive sets the league's schedule at two cards a week:

The W.I.F. is opening its doors to accept 5 new handlers. Starting this week we will have 2 cards per week. There is a special need for tag teams. However if you wish to be a singles wrestler you may. You are allowed 3 wreslters. Please specify if you want a singles, tag or both of the creation sheets. See you soon!!
[name withheld] W.I.F. President [detail withheld]

28 June 1996 — a second claimant to the name reopens under it:

Hello everyone. My name is [name withheld]. I am hear to tell you about my league that I have. It is the WIF, and I run it. I closed the fed 3 weeks ago because knowone was roleplaying. Well, I'm back, and better then ever. I will have 2 cards a week that I will e-mail you. These cards will be Tuesday and Saturday. I don't use TNM to decide the matches, I use a NEW way to decide matches. It is a fair and easy way to decide who wins. The only thing I ask from you is that you ROLEPLAY,
ROLEPLAY, ROLEPLAY!!!!!!!! This is how I decide who wins matches, and it also makes the fed more exciting. If you don't roleplay enough, then I will suspend you for 2 cards. I have been involved with some of the top e-feds around, writing cards and being V.P. Now I have decided to make my own e-fed. I have been involved with the WEW, CWF, AEWA, CWA, and the
EWWA. These are all great feds, and mine will be just as good as them. If you are intrested in joining my fed e-mail me at [detail withheld], and
I will send you a creation sheet. See yeah in the ring!

29 June 1996, the following day:

I am [name withheld], the president of the WIF. I reopened last night and I already have 9 wrestlers. I have Fogie's Friends, and 3 other great wrestlers. I have a uniqe way of decideing the matches and it is a very fair way. Alot of people like this way and say that it may be the best way to decide matches. I am asking anyone to join the WIF. If you are intrested just e-mail me at [detail withheld]. See yeah in the ring.

1 July 1996 — the first claimant disputes the name:

There is already a WIF and I run it. We have been in existence for a couple of months now and are well established. Could you please change the name to avoid confusion before its too late.
[name withheld] President of the W.I.F.

6 July 1996 — the first claimant's own recruiting post takes a jab at the second:

The greatest WIF e-wrestling league on the net is accepting handlers. You are allowed up to 3 wrestlers. Please specify whether you want a singles or tag creation sheet or both. Don't be fooled by imitations.
[name withheld] [detail withheld]

18 July 1996 — the same president recruits again, still running two cards a week:

The WIF is opening its doors again for the next two weeks. If you are interested in joining this great organization mail [detail withheld]. Specify if you want a singles or tag sheet. We have two cards a week and are a very exciting and active league. If you want the best in e-wrestling action come on over!
[name withheld] [detail withheld]

12 October 1996 — a handler, quoting an ad from the Masked Bulldog (11 October), names the WIF's third president:

> [detail withheld] (mikemetz) writes: > For the very best in e-wrestling join the WIF! > Leave a message to [detail withheld] and tell him the Masked Bulldog > sent ya! > > > Masked Bulldog ruler of all of e-wrestling! > > >>>> Guys, Bulldog is right. [name withheld] is the president, and he treats everyone well. Matches are decided by TNM, but matches are written out in length, and are very good. It's exciting. It has to be one of the best leagues around, and has been in existence for several months. Take it from me, Dark Ninja.
