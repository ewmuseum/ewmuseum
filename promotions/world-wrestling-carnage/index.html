Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Wrestling Carnage debuted before a packed St. Paul Civic Center on 12 September 1997, renamed from an earlier working name, World Wrestling Council, within days of its own opening. Commentators Derek Stallion and Jesse “The Golden Boy” Rhodes bickered through the broadcast, and ring announcer Veronica, billed as a former Penthouse Pet of the Year, introduced the main event: Valiant defending the World Championship against Nightstalker, already the United States Champion and the top-ranked contender. The match ran the length of two posts, a week apart, and ended with Nightstalker beating Valiant bloody with a steel chair before Valiant rallied, bulldogging him through a table for the pin as the crowd chanted his own catchword, “VENGEANCE.”

Its next surviving trace is a recruiting post from its own Board of Directors, dated 20 July 1999, naming wrestlers who had passed through by then — Mad Dog Mortimer, Chainsaw Charlie, Frat Boy, Mighty Ryan Kelly, The Hottest Prospect, Street Punk, Larry Gionet, and Joe Snow, “and many more” — and pitching a community message board as a point of pride: “How many feds have a community board?”

No further document survives. What happened between its 1997 debut and that 1999 recruiting post, or after it, isn't in the record.
