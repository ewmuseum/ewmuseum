World Wrestling Carnage debut — WWC Championship match, 13/20 September 1997

13 & 20 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The St. Paul Civic Center in St. Paul, Minnesota is packed far beyond it's capacity Friday night September 12. The reason? World Wrestling Carnage the newest wrestling promotion on the scene!! The fans are chanting at the top of their lungs for their favorite extreme promotion, World Wrestling Carnage. Banners, posters and other WWC merchandise can be seen all over the Civic Center as fans call for blood!! Derek Stallion: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the debut of World Wrestling Carnage!! I'm your play by play commentator for tonight's match along with my color....... Jesse "The Golden Boy" Rhodes: Just a minute you pathetic puke! I'm the star of this show!! I'll introduce my own glorious self." The camera pans over to a man wearing a bright purple jacket with matching fedora hat. His fingers are covered with diamond rings and a gold tooth shines as he smiles. JR: "Hey people, you should be honored that I even consented to appear on this show tonight. After all I am a star on the first magnitude unlike my has been partner over here." DS: "If you don't shut your fat mouth I'm going to pull that gold tooth and shove it up your fat bloated ass!! Now let's go up to Veronica for the ring introductions!!" JR: I won't be treated like this!! I'll get my...... DS: SHUTUP!!!!!!!!! JR: Yes sir Mr Stallion. Right away sir. The male population of the croud roars as the gorgeous Veronica enters the ring. She is a tall blonde with gorgeous tan long legs that slide up into a flat toned tummy and a pair of firm breasts that any other wrestling cheesecake would die for. Topped off with her model good looks it is no wonder that the WWC ring announcer was a former Penthouse Pet of the Year!! She is wearing a tight leather mini skirt outfit with a sleeveless leather vest showing off plenty of cleavage. She swerves her thighs back and forth to emphasize her first sentence. Veronica: Ladies and Gentlemen, lets get ready to really rumble!! The croud is deafening with cheers. Veronica: This match is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit!! Currently coming down the aisle (the fans erupt into boos and jeers) he is the current WWC United States Champion and number one contender for the WWC Championship, here is the Nightstalker!! Boos and jeers continue as a man dressed in black robes with the symbolism of many a dark religion walks slowly to ringside carrying a black candle to light his path. As he enters the ring he removes the hood of his robe to reveal the disfigured face of a madman. He is scarred horribly all across his face. His black hair is a tangled mess that hangs in his eyes which look to be two hollow black circles. His upper body is covered with tattoos burnt into his skin. He wears long ring trunks that cover his legs and wrestling boots both in the darkest black. As the man stares coldly at the croud Veronica scurrys over to the opposite corner to make the next introduction. Veronica: And his opponent for this contest, he is the one and only WWC Champion of the World!! Here is Valiant!! As Veronica hurriedly jumps out of the ring into the arms of a happy ring attendant the fans bust into a ear shattering chant. They call out the word that has become synomynous with the career of Valiant. Fans: VENGENCE!!!!!!!!!! VENGENCE!!!!!!!!!!! VENGENCE!!!!!!!!!! VENGENCE!!!!!!!!!!! A lone man in a grey tattered ringrobe splattered with blood walks slowly to the ring. He to has a scar, although not as bad as the Nightstalker, across his right cheekbone. His black hair lays messily in his face as he walks slowly to the ring. Once inside the ring the word Vengeance in plain white letters can be seen on the back of the robe. Taking the robe off reveals tattered jeans and hightops for wrestling gear. But perhaps the most significant aspect of this man's appearance are his eyes. They are a bright blue and shine with goodness trapped inside a man who has experienced none. DS: Well this is it. The two strongest forces in the WWC. One man having gone through a life of living in the streets of New York city looking for hope he finally found here in the WWC. Valiant is that man. JR: And the man he is about to face, a man born into one of the weathiest families in the nation. A man that chose to embrace himself into the occult world of Death and Darkness and became the most feared and hated wrestler in the WWC. The Nightstalker. DS; There's the bell and they....... Jesse Rhodes: And with the pipes the Nightstalker has that is more than just a normal headlock. Look at Valiant's head!! Its turning purple!! DS: Well I think Valiant's had enough as he tosses Nightstalker off the ropes and nails him with a dropkick right in the face. The Stalker goes over the top rope and to the ring floor!! JR: But he lands on his feet!! What dexterity shown there by Nightstalker!! Here comes Valiant!! The fans go nuts as Valiant hits his next move.... JR: This is a great hold for Nightstalker because he isn't running around like Valiant usually forces his opponents to do. DS: Thats right. He has him grounded for now and that is where Nightstalker wants to keep him so that he can continue with his slow methodical power attack. JR: Valiant is showing signs of life though. He's got to one knee now. Nightstalker had to drop the chinlock and has a less devastating headlock on his opponent. DS: Valiant with an elbow to the ribs, and another and a third. Nightstalker releases the hold!! Valiant bounces himself off the ropes and delivers a flying spin kick to the head!! What a move!! JR: The Nightstalker ain't hurt, he's still standing!! Valiant off the ropes again....oh my god that hurt him!! The fans go nuts as Valiant does a backflip type dive off the ropes and catches Nightstalker in a very quick and devastating Frankensteiner. DS: What a move!! The fans have erupted here!! Now Valiant is going to the top!! Nightstalker stumbles to his feet!! Valiant caught him in a neck wrenching leg scissors takedown from the top rope!! Nightstalker is howling in pain as he clutches his neck!! JR: This is just what Nightstalker wanted to avoid!! These high risk moves that allow Valiant to confuse and cause tremendous amounts of punishment at the same time!! DS: Valiant lives on that top rope!! He's up their again!! Nightstalker is on the canvas clutching his neck in pain!! Frogsplash perfectly executed by the champ!! He goes for the cover!! JR: 1.........2..........NOOOOOO!!!!!! The Nightstalker has to much power as he kicks out with authority!! The powerful legs on Nightstalker saved him that time!! He should be using the high risk offense that he is famous for!! DS: It's simple strategy!! Nightstalker still has a lot of strength in his legs which makes it easier to perform the kind of power moves he wins with. Not to mention kicking out of a pin attempt. This hold weakens the legs and takes that offense away!! JR: I still think he should keep flying around and try to get a quick win with one of his aerial moves!! Nightstalker is to strong for this!! DS: Nightstalker reaches the ropes but the damage is done!! Valiant attacks with a flurry of vicious kicks to the knee of the Nightstalker!! He going nuts!! JR: He's still pounding on that knee with kicks!! This is brutal!! Ref get him off him!! Lay on a count there in the ropes!! Listen to these fans they love this brutality!! DS: The referee finally pulls Valiant off of the Nightstalker. The Nightstalker slowly gets up!! Valiant pushes the ref aside and charges....... JR: HA HA HA HA!!!!!!!! The fans go ballistic as Nightstalker tosses Valiant over the top rope to the concrete floor!! DS: My god he didn't just drop him either he threw him as hard as he could!! This is sick!! What does he think he's doing!! This man is nuts!! JR: And Nightstalker is beating the hell out of Valiant with that steel chair!! Oh what a shot to the head!! Valiant's blood is flying!! He's screaming madly at the top of his lungs!! DS: What the hell is this!! He's gonna kill him!! Get security out here!! JR: We don't need security he threw the chair away!! He's getting a table now!! This is gonna be great!! He's gonna cripple Valiant!! DS: We don't need this!! Nightstalker sets up the table in the ring!! He's going to get Valiant!! He throws Valiant back in!! What is going on!! JR: I told you he's gonna cripple him!! I love it!! He sets Valiant on the top turnbuckle!! Here comes another referee!! Wait a minute no!! DS: Thank god!! Valiant jabs Nightstalker in the eye with his thumb!!
