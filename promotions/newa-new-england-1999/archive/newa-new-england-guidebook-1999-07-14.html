Webpage 6. APPLICATION ========================================= 1. INTRODUCTION a. What is the NEWA? If you take ewrestling seriously. STOP! Close this guidebook, and pretend that that NAWA never even exsisted
2. GENERAL RULES a. Promoting & Booking Matches Everything in the New England Wrestling Association is completely booked, or predetermined, based on rollplays and angles. All matches are decided by our team of bookers.
b. Interviews If you want to do an interview to get your point across, that is fine. All you have to do is send it to
[detail withheld] ! All we ask of you, is to keep them down to a minimum. We here at the NEWA like to focus things more on rollplaying.
c. Strategies If you want something special to happen during a match, send in a strategy. This can be anything from manager interference to sneak attacks. It must be noted, though, that they are not guaranteed to occur 100% of the time. Send all strategies to
[detail withheld] ! d. Managers If you want to bring in a manager to this fed, you are permitted to. However, if he serves no purpose and does nothing, he will be fired. e. Challenges The New England Wrestling Association is all about interaction between it's members. If you want to challenge another member, you must first get in contact with the other handler. If he accepts, then bring it to the attention of the president and he will sign the match.
f. Swearing The NEWA likes to consider itself as more of a traditional fed, so we must insist that if you must swear... do so in moderation, and mask out anything that you can't say on television! Remember, try and use it for *shock value* only!
g. Angles Once again, the New England Wrestling Association is all about member participation. If you want to request an angle for your character, by all means, go for it! Just remember, if it's not humorous, funny, and/or serves no purpose... just forget about it.
h. Rollplaying Ok, what good is an efed with no rollplaying, right? Well, we here at NEWA know that for a fact and stress it as one of the most important aspects of our organization. However, we also realize that people have lives outside of the computer so we do not require novel length rp's every single day. All we ask is, at least one but no more then four, a week. And no one liners please? I don't want to make a minimum length, but will if that comes to the case. Just remember, when rollplaying, think quality not quantity. Also, when submitting your rollplays, you must send them as a standard text (.txt) document, and for the sake of everyone in the fed.. *always spell check before sending*!
3. CARDS a. Tuesday Night Terror This is the big one. *The* card of the New England Wrestling Association, where feuds are begun and titles are won. It is the equivilant of Monday Night
Raw or Monday Night Nitro, and will "air" on Tuesday nights, roughly around
9pm.
b. Friday Night Faceoff This is our secondary card. This card will usually feature more under/mid carders and all matches will be written in description format. It is the equivilant of Heat or Thunder, and will "air" on Friday nights, roughly around
9pm
c. Pay-Per-Views Every two months, the New England Wrestling Association will go on pay tv and offer three hours of awesome wrestling action. These cards I'm referring to are supercards. We offer them ever two months, instead of one, to have a chance to better build up angles and make each card a huge one.
4. TITLES a. North Eastern Championship This is the big one. Whoever possesses this belt is the man of the NEWA. This belt will be defended at every supercard, and at least once a month -- depending on the owner.
b. Why Only One Title? The NEWA only has one title because we are a small fed, right now. Rather then have fifteen titles, so that everyone in the fed can have one, we prefer to keep it low. If more people come, we may make a new one.
5. MISCELLANEOUS a. Character Creation The New England Wrestling Association offers each handler to control two singles wrestlers, or one tag team. That's all. We put this limit, because it's a proven fact that people put more time and effort into a character when they don't have fifteen wrestlers to control. However, if the executive board sees that you can handle it, and it serves a purpose, you will be allowed to bring in a third.
b. Keep In Touch Communication is very important in the world of e-wrestling. We supply the email address of everyone in the fed, so use them. We aren't gonna give you forms to use or whatever, we feel that if you really want something to happen badly, you can get in touch with whoever it may concern. Also, because it is a useful tool and can help everyone stay in touch, we ask you to download a free copy of ICQ (
