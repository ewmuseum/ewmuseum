A regional Usenet e-fed launched in the summer of 1999 restricting itself to a single regional title, built entirely around handler-submitted roleplays compiled by its own booking staff. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The New England Wrestling Association opened its own recruitment with a guidebook, revised 12 July 1999 and posted two days later, pitching itself as a small regional promotion still gathering its first roster: “If you take ewrestling seriously. STOP! Close this guidebook.” It restricted itself to a single title, the North Eastern Championship, deliberately — its own guidebook explaining that with a small roster, spreading the gold across several belts would only cheapen it.

Its own front office split cleanly between the two people actually running it and a separate cast of invented on-air characters: On-Camera Owner Bruce Marcose, Head Commentator Dan Crawfield, Color Commentator Rox Rogers, Ring Announcer Brett Butcher, Interviewer Walter Fox, Head Referee Gavin Mondale, Referee Dennis Walters, and Head of Security Blake Bradley, each listed with their own contact address for handlers to use in running angles through them.

Its own schedule paired a main card, Tuesday Night Terror, with a secondary show, Friday Night Faceoff, building toward a pay-per-view every two months rather than one, “to have a chance to better build up angles and make each card a huge one.”

No card, roster, or title result survives beyond the guidebook itself; the only other surviving document is a single community reply to it, four days later, with no card ever shown to have run.
