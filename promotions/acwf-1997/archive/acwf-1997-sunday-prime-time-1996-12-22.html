ACWF — Weekly Card

ACWF Sunday Prime Time, December 22nd

22 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---------- Forwarded message ---------- Date: Sun, 22 Dec 1996 15:02:57 -0400 From: [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Subject: ACWF Sunday Prime Time, December 22nd (Fade in to these comments from ACWF newcomer David Graves.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ David Graves: Some of you may have seen me wrestle before in some of the bigger leagues like the AEWA, GWF, and PWF. Sometimes in an organzation like those, it's easy to get lost in the shuffle. well, I was looking for a smaller group where I would have a greater opportunity to showcase and develop my skills. Here in the ACWF, I think I've found it. Anyone looking for some good, honest competition can find it with me. Anyone who thinks they're going to get away with cutting corners will something a lot worse...
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (cut to announcers table on site in Tallahasse, Florida.) Darren Ball: Welcome everyone to Prime Time. We'll give you the entire lineup, plus an update on Kyle Spartan later on in the show. But first, we have "The Modern Day Goliath", David Graves in his ACWF debut.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ DAVID GRAVES vs DWIGHT BOLTON ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "Blitzkreig Bop", by the Ramones played, and a 6'9", 275 pound monster came to the ring. Bolton didn't look to pleased to be fighting him, but the fans popped like mad for the veteran of e-wrestling. (Well, compared to most of the ACWF roster, he's a veteran.)
DB: This man looks like he means business! Brett Greene: Let's see if he can wrestle before we fall all over the guy. Clark Cross: Yeah, for all we know he could be a complete loser. This ain't a bodybuilding contest, it's wrestling.
Graves was quick to show that he could wrestle, too, coming right at Bolton with lefts and rights. He was whipped to the ropes, and the big man hit a tilt-a-whirl suplex. Bolton was dumped down in a piledriver, and whipped to the turnbuckle. He stumbled back out, and ducked a charging clothesline, but was caught from behind with a boot to the gut.
Graves continued to display his power with a belly-to-belly suplex that had Bolton seeing stars.
DB: I am impressed. CC: So he looks good against Dwight Bolton, big deal! BG: I think you could take Bolton, Darren. Graves continued the onslaught with a scoop slam, and then nailed an atomic drop. The fans were still popping the guy, as despite his being a big man he showed a very good workrate considering. Graves went to the top, and hit his Gravestone (top rope clothesline.) for an easy three count, even for Scott Ingraham's snail paced three.
WINNER DAVID GRAVES VIA PINFALL DB: A big win for Graves in his debut, and the fans seem to like him too. BG: He can wrestle a bit, but Ithink he relies on the power game too much. CC: This man is in the Iceman mold, but there's only one Iceman. BG: Thank goodness. Speaking of champions, let's hear from my favourite
ACWF champion, Joey Valachi.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "THE CARDIAC KID" JOEY VALACHI: (Looking at his Canadian title) Well, it ain't the UWF World Title, but it'll do for now. And Ricky Reno, what did
I tell you? Had you accepted my offer, I probably would have been fresh for the main event, and easily won the ACWF Title. Then you'd have had a belt, too. Instead, you thought you could beat me in the ring. You've got potential, unlike some of these other losers, but you're nowhere near the level of the Cardiac Kid yet.
Now, as far as Iceman goes, maybe I was wrong. Maybe there's ONE other person in the ACWF who, like me, deserves so much more. Me and
Iceman would have been a classic match, if it weren't for that idiot Reno's inability to see logic, and that buzzcutted moron Kyle Spartan thinking he deserves a spot in the same ring with guys like you and me.
So now, let's see who thinks they can take this belt from me. Stark? Spartan? Reno? Slade? I'm waiting, losers. I get paid whether
I wrestle or not, because unlike you, I was smart enough to get a guaranteed money contract. So I'm in no hurry. Sort it out amongst yourselves. I'll be waiting so far above you that you won't even be able to see me.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ BG: I like that guy more and more very week. A true champion, just like I was. DB: He is right that Valachi vs Iceman would be a classic match, but who's to say we may not see it some day. CC: I'll give the devil his due, and say that Valachi could push Iceman. But he can't beat him. Valachi is no Iceman, mark my words. BG: Whatever you say, Clark. Valachi is twice the champion and ten times the man that back-stabbing Greg Smith ever was.
DB: Before you two get at each other's throats, let's just call the next match. We've got Ian Mantle in action, then an update on Kyle Spartan, who were are pleased to announce has no career threatening injuries.
CC: Damn! We also have the World Champion, "Iceman" Greg Smith, up against Joey Roy of Chalmers Corp.
BG: Who to cheer for? Then the Scholar takes on Brent Slade. same dilemma. DB: And finally, we have the tag team triangle match for the titles. We'll hear from some of the participants later, but for now let's go down to the ring. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ IAN MANTLE vs MITSUHARA YOSHIAKI ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Mitsuhara was the first in the ring, and while trying to show off some martial arts for the fans, he tripped on his own feet. The crowd roared with laughter, while we assume Mitsuhara blushed. (We can't tell because of his mask.) Then Ian Mantle was introduced, but no one answered the call. Jack Donaldson counted to ten, and was then forced to give a forfeit win to the Japanese JTTS.
WINNER MITSUHARA YOSHIAKI VIA FORFEIT DB: Now, where exactly is Ian Mantle? Wasn't he on the plane with all the rest of us? CC: I dunno, I was busy trying to get the stewardesses numbers. DB: They call them flight attendants now, Clark. Brett, did you see him? BG: I haven't seen the guy since Winter Washout. To be honest, he kinda freaks me out. There's something a little... off... about that guy.
DB: What do you mean? BG: I'm not sure, but he's just not quite normal, you know what I mean? DB: I'm not quite sure I do. Well, let's go to investigative reporter David Brennan who has an update on Kyle Spartan. (cut to Brennan standing in front of a Quebec city hospital.) Brennan: I'm standing outside the hospital where Kyle Spartan spent a few days earlier this week after suffering a slight concusion at Winter
Washout. He also has a broken nose, but doctors assure me that Spartan will be ready for his World title shot at Beach Wars this January 19th.
BG: Spartan gets the shot? How'd you know that? Brennan: It's my job to know. CC: And how'd you speak to his doctors? I thought you were in Quebec... Brennan: Among other things, I am very fluent in French. Good day, gentlemen. DB: Merry Christmas, David. BG: Geez, is there anything that guy doesn't know? DB: That's why we hired him. Now, let's move on to the World title defense, as Greg Smith battles "The Ultimate Man", Joey Roy.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "ICEMAN" GREG SMITH vs JOEY ROY (w/Diamond and Randolph Chalmers) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Roy was the first one out of the back, with his usual entourage of manager and valet, and the usual mediocre heel pop, mostly for Chalmers.
The heel pop intensified tenfold, however, when "Bullet With Butterfly Wings" started, and the Iceman walked down to ringside. (I'm beginning to think that his mother boos the screen when he wrestles, that's how over as a heel he is!) He showed off his new hardware, and the fans booed even more, which he just ate up.
DB: And finally, the hunter has become the hunted. BG: What's this, Wrestling Psychology 101? Shut the hell up! CC: You're just angry because Greg Smith is more talented then you could ever be. BG: Please! I could wrestle circles around that big traitor, and so could
Valachi! DB: Just call the match, okay? Smith jumped Roy from behind, and hit him with a bridging back suplex for two. A double underhook suplex followed, and Smith had really gained the early control. He had an Irish whip reversed, but the champ stunned everyone with a rolling reverse cradle for two, showing a lot of technical knowledge.
CC: Everytime you think you've got this guy pegged, he comes up with something new! Smith made a mistake in going for a spinebuster, as Roy was able to reverse it into a swinging neckbreaker. Roy then tried to go toe to toe with Smith, but ended up getting the worst of it. He was whipped to the corner, but moved out of the way of a charging Smith, and back suplexed the champ for two. Smith came right back with a jawbreaker, and then went off the ropes but was tripped by Chalmers. Smith grabbed Chalmers by the neck, and flung him into the ring. Ref Hank Haliburton looked like he was about to call a dq when Roy came running at Iceman, who ducked. RC3 got blasted, and Roy stood over his boss, distraught.
BG: I love seeing Chalmers get hurt! DB: He did deserve it. Iceman spun around the pre-occupied Roy, and booted him in the gut. One
Icepick later, Greg Smith had a successful title defense under his belt.
(No pun intended.) WINNER, AND STILL ACWF WORLD CHAMPION, "ICEMAN" GREG SMITH CC: Yes! Iceman comes through! DB: I have to admit he made it look pretty easy there. BG: Any time a championship match only goes 6 minutes, that's good news for the champion, because they usually have pretty grueling schedules as it is.
DB: Good point, Brett. Next off we have another Chalmers protege, the Scholar, against Brent Slade. CC: Hold on! That's why Ian Mantle no-showed! DB: Why? CC: Because he knew the Scholar would be here. He's afraid! DB: I highly doubt that... BG: Besides, there's lots tougher guys to be afraid of around here than the Scholar. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- THE SCHOLAR (w/Randolph Chalmers IIIrd) vs BRENT SLADE ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Brent Slade was at his old pro-USA bit, but this time he actually got a small pop for it. Note the word small, as people still had bad memories of the "Lex Express". Then Chalmers returned to ringside with the
Scholar, who drew more heat than Slade. (Hell, I'd draw more heat than Slade!) Still, the crowd was not quite as pumped as it might be, until Chalmers grabbed a mic and said: RC3: I just wanted to point something out to all you moronic fans, and especially the snotty American kids. There is no Santa Claus!
(A few of the younger fans started to cry, and Chalmers was rained upon with boos.) DB: What a despicable thing to say! BG: Not to defend the worthless fans, but Chalmers is a scumbag. CC: (Crying) What? No Santa? The Scholar, also not in a very festive mood, started things off with a shoulderblock on the charging Slade. Slade tried his lick again, and this time it was Chalmers' man that fell over. The Scholar was whipped to the buckle, and avalanched. Slade in a rare move left his feet for a cross-body block, but was caught and backbreakered. The Scholar then tossed his patriotic foe to the outside. The Scholar leapt off the apron with a double axhandle, and allowed Chalmers time to get in a few cheap shots as well. Slade was rolled back into the ring and put in a cobra clutch.
BG: I think Slade's in a bit of trouble. DB: Don't count out his resiliency... CC: (still sniffing) Then who eats the cookies? BG: Get over it, Clark! Slade got to the ropes after a few seconds, and hit an armdrag to take the Scholar off his feet. A bulldog kept him there for a while, but then
Slade got cocky and went for an elbow off the second turnbuckle. It works for Bret Hart, but the Best there is he ain't. The Scholar picked him up and piledrove him, and then set up for Chalmers' old figure four. Slade small-packaged through it for a count of two, but the Scholar kept rolling and got two of his own from ref Colin Black.
DB: That was a nice series of counters by both men. BG: In terms of pure wrestling ability, I'd say the Scholar is ahead. CC: Way ahead! I'm so happy he's winning that not even this Santa fiasco can keep me down! The Scholar did however keep Slade down, hitting a flying lariat, and then packaging the American. Black broke the count at 2 when he saw the
Scholar's foot on the ropes. Slde got up first and went for a clothesline, which he missed. Both men collided on the rebound, knocking both to the mat. The Scholar managed to drag his arm over the limp Slade, but it was still only enough for two.
DB: And Slade just won't quit, folks! BG: Although I certainly wish he would. Slade was whipped to the ropes, but nailed a DDT after the Scholar telegraphed that he intended to try a back body drop. Slade hit a spinning elbowdrop that gave him momentum, and he then went off the ropes where he was tripped up by Chalmers. Slade stumbled forward into a Final
Passage, a three count, and a defeat.
WINNER THE SCHOLAR VIA PINFALL DB: And Brent Slade just can't seem to get started off here in the ACWF. CC: Forget about him, the Scholar is the real story. He demolishes Brent
Slade and scares off Miss Ian Mantle all in the same night! What a talent!
BG: He's okay, but he hasn't really had much competition yet. His opponents are a Who's Not list... Yoshiaki, Slade, Spartan...
DB: Kyle Spartan has been deemed the number one contendor for the ACWF World title. BG: And for the life of me I don't know why. To be honest, Ricky Reno deserves it, or maybe Harmon Stark.
DB: well, ladies and gentlemen, we have with us now a man who may very well earn an ACWF title shot one day, the newest entrant to the ACWF, Nick
"The Schooner" McGill! Let's hear what he has to say.
[ A small man walks down the ring. He is wearing blue jeans, and a white shirt that has a Canadian maple leaf on it. He is wearing sunglasses too.
he has short brown hair and also sports a goatee. He steps into the ring and is given the microphone ]
Nick McGill: "Hello everybody! Is this thing on?" [the crowd responds accordingly. Nick takes of the glasses ] "How you all doing?" [again, the crowd responds accordingly with cheers ] "Everybody knows, that I Nick "The Schooner" McGill was fired from UWF by Lance Fredericks. Well,
Lance isn't in charge over there any more... and Paul Reed called me up, and offered a new UWF contract. To which I replied...'Paul, I can not work with UWF, because I plan on being... the next ACWF champion!'" [the crowd pops loudly] "That's right, Nick "The Schooner" McGill is headed into ACWF action. Maybe I'll fit in a little better, and maybe I will get the opportunity to wrestle, the champions, Iceman Greg SMith or Joey
Valachi. EIther man would be a great honor to wrestle, but I'm leaning more or less to Joey Valachi. This week, I have the biggest match of my life and carrer in the RINGLORD 60 man Royal Rumble... but after that!..., Nick McGill is officially in the ACWF... and if I win the
RINGLORD matchup... it is for the ACWF!!!! Yeah!!!" [large crowd pop] "ANd Joey Valachi... you said some not-so-nice things about me a month back... well if we meet in the RINGLORD Valachi, and I know your backstage listening. If we meet in the RINGLORD, I'm going to show you
_why_ they call me the Schooner... because I'll be breezing by you and dumping you over that top rope!!! I may not win it... but damnit... I'm going to try my hardest!!!! Valachi... in RINGLORD _AND_ ACWF, your going to get a taste of Nick McGill!!!"
[Nick McGill leaves as Brenda Stubbort plays by Ashlay MacAsaic. There is a large pop as Nick McGill claps as many hands as he can before heading backstage.]
DB: We certainly wish McGill good luck here in the ACWF, as well as in the big Ringlord tournament.
CC: I don't. DB: Well, most of us do. We'll see his debut next week, but for now we have a triangle match for the tag team titles. Any predictions?
BG: Deadly Intentions were robbed in the tournament, and they're gonna make up for that now. New champions, these guys are just too dominant not to rise to the top.
CC: I agree that we'll see new champs, but I think it will be the High School Toughies. It always helps to have a manager at ringside, especially in title matches.
DB: And I think that the champions always have an advantage. You'll see what I mean when we go over the special rules for this match. Only two men can be in the ring at once, and there must be physical contact before either man can tag out. No count-outs or disqualifications are allowed, and this will be a one-fall match, meaning that whoever gets the first fall wins the titles, regardless of who they pin.
BG: Why does that favour the champions? DB: Because it will probably rule out any 4 on 2 situations, as an alliance between the challengers would be counter productive.
BG: Ah. CC: You have a point there. DB: The only other rule is the special "Outsiders" clause: you cannot win by defeating your own teammate.
BG: But that takes all the fun out of it! DB: Let's get to comments from a few of the teams, and then down to ringside. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hans: "Hey Fronze... how do we show everyone in the ACWF, that we are the coolest guys around?" Fronze: "I don't know Hans...win the ACWF Tag Team Titles?" Hans: "NO!!! We don't have to do anything!!! Everyone _KNOWS_ we are the coolest guys around! Everyone else is just in denial about it!" Fronze: "But the ACWF Tag team titles look mighty nice too, so we want to take the titles from those two losers that upseted us, Lacroix and Sheradin!" Kristee: " Also, those ACWF World Tag Team titles will look great with the green and black wrestling jackets that says how cool you two studs are!"
Hans: "She has a point there ya know. Deadly Intentions, we'll understand if you want to forfeit your spot in the match and save yourself the embarassment of being beaten in by us. You'll still be
semi-cool guys." Fronze: "But of course, since you don't have the jackets, you aren't as cool as we are!" Kristee: "Tonights, we will win those ACWF tag team titles and rename them the Absolute Coolest Wrestlers Forever tag team titles!" Hans: "Uh... yeah.. that's it." ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Cut to a brick wall, completely out of focus. The camera zooms out while focusing, and pans to the left. Bobby Nash and Stevie Ramirez, Deadly
Intentions, each hold in their hands an nWo replica title belt. Nash puts his over his shoulder while Ramirez straps it around his waist.]
BN "Hey look, we're the champs..." SR "Ooh, look, we got gold. Well, come this Sunday, we will have the real thing instead of these replica pieces of garbage that the nWo are peddling."
BN "Guess what Dreamer and LaCroix. You two are dreamin', that's for sure. This little championship dream is comin' to an end, and Stevie and me are your dreamweavers. Sure, you snuck by us in the tournament, but you won't get past us this time."
SR "And as for the High School Toughies, I won't even waste my breath on you two." BN "What the hell, I will.[Nash begins to speak in a valley accent, a bad one at that] Like, Stevie, we're gonna, like, take these loo-oosers out.
Fur sure!" SR "This Sunday, mark it down as DI-Day." [Cut to black immediately.] ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- JEFF LACROIX AND "DREAMER" RANDY SHERIDAN vs HIGH SCHOOL TOUGHIES (w/Krissey) vs DEADLY INTENTIONS FOR THE ACWF TAG TEAM TITLES. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Toughies were the first duo out of the gates, with jackets and all. They felt they were very cool, but no one in the crowd did. Then "Whiplash" by Metallica started up, and so did a heel pop for Bobby Nash and Stevie Ramirez, Deadly Intentions. Finally, the champions walked out to "Dream On", by Aerosmith. Ref Hank Haliburton discussed the rules with all three teams, and it was decided that Stevie Ramirez and Jeff LaCroix would start things out.
DB: So the Toughies decide to sit things out first. That could be a good decision, to stay fresh.
CC: You've gotta be in it to win it. BG: Yeah, but why do the dirty work when someone else can do it for you? DB: Let's just say this could go either way. LaCroix came out fast attempting an enzuigiri, but Ramirez ducked and leveled him with a short-arm clothesline. The champ was whipped to the corner, and then hoisted onto the top turnbuckle. He then crashed right back down as the result of a belly-to-belly superplex. (That's gotta hurt!) Ramirez covered and almost got the quick title win. He decided to take a break while he was ahead, and tagged out to Hans. Hans ran in quickly but was hit by a drop-toe hold. LaCroix whipped Hans to the ropes, but ended up missing on a dropkick. Hans latched on to the champion with a half-boston, but it wasn't quite enough to snare a title victory. He then spinebustered the champ, but had a pin attempt broken up by Bobby Nash.
DB: It doesn't look like we'll have any allegiances here. BG: That's for the better, who needs someone bringing you down? If you can't win cleanly by yourself, cheat. That's what I always say.
CC: Sometimes it helps to have a partner cheat for you though. DB: While these two discuss proper rule-breaking methods, I'll get back to calling the match.
Hans dragged LaCroix to his feet, but had a snap suplex reversed. LaCroix managed to limp over and tag in his partner, Randy Sheridan, while Hans got to Fronze. Sheridan went into "Face With Hot Tag" mode, nailing everything in sight. He dropkicked Hans off the apron, and double headsmashed Deadly Intentions. Oh yeah, he also nailed Fronze with a standing dropkick, and covered for two. He then went upstairs and hit a tremendous splash which looked like it might end the match. Sheridan, however, decided to go upstairs again, this time just missing a spinning legdrop. Fronze crawled over to a corner (he wasn't sure which it was), and reached for the tag. Stevie Ramirez responded, hitting a belly-to-belly suplex, and then a powerslam. Sheridan was in a lot of trouble, and a double clothesline from Deadly Intentions didn't help.
Bobby Nash was now the legal man, as well as the Dreamer. DB: Now all six men have been in this match. BG: Darren Ball, master of the obvious. We can count, moron! Wait, we're in Florida, I forgot. Six comes after five, folks.
DB: We here at the ACWF, especially the legal department, apologize to all our Floridian viewers.
CC: Floridian??? DB: Well, what is it then? Nash then hit a kneeling headbutt on the prone Dreamer, and followed up with a backdrop driver. Nash tried to whip Sheridan off the ropes, but was reversed and inadvertently tripped up by Krissey, who was busy talking to a handsome looking university student in the front row. Nash screamed at her to watch what she was doing, and this brought over the
High School Toughies, who objected to yelling at their "cool" manager. Ramirez came over as well, and the two teams got into a shoving match. During this time, Sheridan managed to tag out to LaCroix, and then both men snuck up behind tha arguing teams, and dumped them over the top rope.
The four men landed in a heap on the floor, while the fans showed their support for the champions.
DB: Way to go, guys! Ha, i love it! BG: You see, that's what having a manager does to you. CC: Come on, how can you stay mad at a girl as pretty as that? I get dizzy just looking at her...
BG: Can I get you another towel, Clark? DB: Please, do you want the FCC on our case again? Bobby Nash slowly got back into the ring, and quickly asserted himself with an elbowsmash from behind on the unsuspecting LaCroix. A Sammartino backbreaker followed, and then Nash went to the top turnbuckle to hit a reverse flying elbowdrop.(!) Nash went for the pin, which was broken up by Hans and Sheridan at about the same time. Nash turned to yell at referee Hank Haliburton when he was hit with a frankensteiner. An Asai moonsault got LaCroix two, but no more. LaCroix then went over to tag out to Sheridan, and Nash decided he'd had enough and tagged in Fronze to soften up the champ. Fronze charged at the Dreamer, but Randy moved and
Fronze ran right into LaCroix, knocking him off the apron. Sheridan turned around to see if he was okay, and Fronze took the opportunity to hit a clothesline. Hans was tagged in, and the Toughies hit their Dream
Spike (think Shaker Heights Spike). It looked bad for Sheridan, but Hans instead of making the cover showed off how "cool" he was for the crowd.
Finally, he covered. 1...2...no! DB: Randy Sheridan showing a lot of resilency here! BG: Hans had him if he hadn't goofed around. CC: Don't worry, the Toughies still have this under control. Hans was shocked that someone had kicked out of the Dream Spike, and wasn't ready when Sheridan whipped him into the corner. He bumped into
Nash there, which apparently constitued a tag. Nash came in only to get blasted by a spin kick, and then be placed in a front facelock. It still wasn't enough to get him to submit, so Sheridan let go. Sheridan then whipped him off the ropes and tried for a back drop, but was early putting his head down and paid for it in the form of a spinning power bomb. Nash went for the cover, and Haliburton counted: 1...2...3!
WINNERS, AND NEW ACWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, DEADLY INTENTIONS Nash and Ramirez quickly ran over to grab their new belts, while Jeff LaCroix went into the ring to console his partner, and check if he was okay. The Toughies, meanwhile, went back quickly to the locker rooms with Krissey, debating over what went wrong.
BG: Yes, we finally have real tag team champs! CC: No! The Toughies were robbed! They shoulda won! DB: I somehow don't see it, Deadly Intentions were just the better team tonight. BG: They always were the better team. DB: That's debatable. Well, folks, we just about out of time this week, but... hold on, who's this?
The curtains opened at the entranceway, to reveal Ian Mantle running down to ringside, and still in civies, right down to the Nikes instead of wrestling boots.
BG: What the heck is he doing here? DB: i don't know, and we don't have the time to find out now. We promise that we'll show you footage of whatever happens here next week, but we're just simply out of time. For Brett Greene and Mr. Excitement Clark Cross, this is Darren Ball saying so long, and tune in next week!
BG: What's going on...? --
