ACWF Sunday Prime Time, 5 January 1997

5 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From: [name withheld], ACWF President: [detail withheld] Subject: ACWF Sunday Prime Time, January 5th. (Fade in to shots of a sea of humanity [no, not Brett.] crowds around the ailseway, as "Semper Fi" plays. Kyle Spartan begins to make his way down to the ring to a very respectable pop.] Darren Ball: Welcome, ladies and gentlemen, to Sunday Prime Time. We're absolutely live, in the semi-tropical locale of San Juan, Peurto Rico. As you can see, we've got the big return of Kyle Spartan, as he comes back to the ring to face Brent Slade. As you know, Spartan is just two weeks away from a World title shot. Clark Cross: Speaking of the world title, champion Greg Smith is here to put the Deep Freeze on the Dreamer. We also have my favs, the High School Toughies in action, and a sort of preview for Beach Wars' tag team title match, as Micheal Samuelson of Hellfire and Damnation takes on Stevie Ramirez of Deadly Intentions. DB: And of course, the big main event is a tag team match, Ricky Reno and the Scholar take on Ian Mantle and Harmon Stark. But for now, let's just get down to the ring and kick off the first Prime Time of 1997! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ KYLE SPARTAN vs BRENT SLADE ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The match started with a tieup and (of all things!) a clean break. Spartan then came back out, and nailed Slade in the gut with a punch. Showing few signs of ring rust, he went off the ropes and hit a bulldog. Spartan then caught up Slade in a rolling reverse cradle for two, but came back up into a side headlock from Slade. Slade followed up with a bulldog of his own, but neglected to cover. Instead, he hit a devastating legdrop. Spartan was hauled to his feet and caught in a Northern Lights suplex that almost cost him the match. After a hasty kickout, Spartan hit an enzuigiri and covered, again only for two. DB: It looks like Spartan is picking up about where he left off. CC: Yup, in boring undercard matches. Spartan, apparently resentful at having his match called boring, decided to liven it up with an attempt for the Spartan Slam, but Slade hit a jawbreaker that stunned the ex-Marine. Slade then went to the high rent district, and hit a Bret Hart like elbowdrop off the second turnbuckle. Slade went upstairs again, but this time took too long in getting there, and paid for it by being belly-to-back superplexed. All that was left in the match was a Spartan Slam, a cover, and Hank Haliburton counting to three. WINNER KYLE SPARTAN VIA PINFALL After the match, Spartan helped revive his fallen foe, and they both walked off to a nice face pop. DB: Now that's sportsmanship. What a contender! CC: What a sap, you mean. He has no killer instinct, he hasn't got a chance against a killer like the "Iceman". Greg Smith will finish the job he started in Quebec, and cripple that lousy private for good. DB: Actually, I don't think Kyle ever mentioned what his rank was... CC: Shaddup. DB: Well, now that we have some time, let's run down a few other things you'll see tonight. Brett Greene is not one of them, as he's up in Canada undergoing negotiations with the UWF. CC: But you've still got me! DB: Super. Well, Ian Mantle and the Scholar have some kind of feud going on between them, but no one's quite sure what it's all about. They meet on opposite sides of a tag match in tonight's main event, and the Scholar has promised us that he'll tell us all of what he's talking about when he mentioned calling the police. CC: We also have my favourites, the High School Toughies! They're up next! DB: But first, let's go to Jennifer Davies-Parker back at the studios for a Beach Wars '97 report. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Cut to the ACWF studio, wher Jennifer Davies-Parker is standing a sand box, wearing a bikini that is going to give the 13 year olds watching some good dreams tonight. She has a microphone in one hand, and a sand shovel in the other.) JDP: Beach Wars '97 is just two weeks away, folks, and the card has just been finalized. We have eight great matches for you to... (Jennifer begins to scoop up some of the sand.) ...dig into. Let's quickly run down the whole card. 1) "Iceman" Greg Smith defends his World title against a man he almost crippled, Kyle Spartan. Can the ex-marine's work ethic bring him the title, or does Spartan become the next victim of the Icepick power bomb? 2) "Cardiac Kid", Joey Valachi, takes on former UWF world champion Youth Gone Wild. Both these men have an extensive title history, although Valachi's Canadian title is not on the line here. Can Valachi prove he belongs in the bigs, or does YGW Wildflyer some modesty into the Cardiac Kid? 3) Deadly Intentions take on Hellfire and Damnation for the Tag Team titles. Will the first title defense be the last for Deadly Intentions, or do prayers go unanswered and title dreams unfulfilled? 4) Ian Mantle takes on archnemisis The Scholar and manager Randolph Chalmers in a handicap match. Can Mantle take on two men at once, or will this be too much? And what is Mantle's secret? 5)David Graves is up against "Mr. Class", Jimmie Blass. Can the unlucky Blass snare an upset win, or will Graves have a Gravestone ready for Blass? 6) Nick "Schooner" McGill makes his ACWF pay per view debut against Joey Roy. Is the Ultimate Man too much for the Maritimer, or does the Schooner sail to victory over a Randolph Chalmers protege? 7) "Highflyer" Harmon Stark tangles with "Slick" Ricky Reno. Can Reno prove himself as the title contender he's been so far, or does Harmon Stark get a jumpstart on ACWF success? 8) The High School Toughies have a date with Saturday Night Fever. Is disco really dead, or can it stage a comeback. And just who IS the ACWF's coolest tag team? JDP: So many questions, and only one way to answer them. Call your local cable company, and order Beach Wars '97, Sunday, January 19th, from Orlando, Florida. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ DB: That should be just every bit as spectacular as Winter Washout, and maybe even more. We've got some new talent in, and we now have the experience at running a pay per view. I'd advise ordering Beach Wars, folks, you won't want to miss it! CC: And I don't want to miss the Toughies! DB: Here we go again... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ HIGH SCHOOL TOUGHIES (w/Christy) vs STETSON BROTHERS ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Toughies were the only ones that had a ring entrance, as the luckless Stetsons just walked to the ring unannounced. Hans and Fronze, however, certainly did announce their arrival, along with announcing their coolness and the coolness of the "Valley Wrestling" jackets. They finally got into the ring after much urging from referee Scott Ingraham. CC: What are his shorts in a bunch for? The match will start soon enough. DB: It's his job, Clark. CC: What's his job? DB: Being a referee, he has to do that. CC: Do what? DB: Start the match! CC: Who has to start the match? DB: No, Who's on first. CC: Huh? Meanwhile, a match was going on. Not much of one, though, as Hans and Fronze ran right out and nailed Jimmy Stetson with a double dropkick. Hans started off as the legal man, and he floored Stetson with a series of stiff elbows. He then quickly tagged to Fronze, and held Stetson to be punched in the ribs, to which Fronze obliged. Fronze whipped Jimmy to the ropes, and backdropped him, then again tagging in Hans. Apparently, they weren't interested in ever letting Jerry Stetson enter the match, as they pancaked Jimmy with the Dream Spike, which is pretty much the same as the old Shaker Heights Spike. Hans covered for an easy three (which Ingraham eventually got up to) and won the match. WINNERS HIGH SCHOOL TOUGHIES VIA PINFALL CC: Yes! DB: Well, the Toughies looked very impressive tonight and... oh look, it's not enough to win, they have to put on some kind of show! That's sick. Ball was referring to what was happening inside the ring, where both Hans and Fronze first double clotheslined Jerry Stetson off the ring apron, and then picked up the Valley Wrestling jackets. Fronze placed one around the battered body of Jimmy Stetson, while Hans yelled out "Now he's as cool as us!". Then, Fronze quickly, but with great care, took the jacket back, leaving Hans to yell "He's not cool anymore!". CC: I love these guys! DB: You would. Well, we're about to show you a big World title match between champion "Iceman" Greg Smith and challenger "Dreamer" Randy Sheridan. This is the first match to come of Smith's open contract to the UWF, but a lot more wrestlers are lining up to take him on, including this man. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ David Graves, the Modern Day Goliath: What ails me, Scholar? After our last match, the better question is what ails YOU. The last view I had of you was of your back and it was retreating further and further into the distance. If you don't have the courage to stay in the ring with an ACWF newcomer, I don't think you'll have what it takes to get any further in this career. Perhaps you've taken too much advice from Mr. Chalmers, whose record in the UWF, I understand, should have been all the encouragement he would need to follow your strategy from this past week. Enough about the past, let's look at the future: Mr. Blass, I don't know who you are, but you are in a very bad position right now. My ACWF PPV is coming soon, and I intend to make a real impression. I'll be doing some pretty extensive research into your ring work between now and then, and I hope for your sake you haven't been doing anything that would anger me as much as the Scholar had. And now the ACWF Champion has an open contract? And he seems to respect and admire Joey Valachi? Curiouser and curiouser. Smith, if no one has else has taken up your offer, I'd be more than happy to solve the ACWF's biggest problem- you. If anyone else who has been here longer and done more than I have in my bried stay, I'll defer to them. But the day will come, Smith, when, like the Scholar, you'll have to choose fight or flight. I'm hoping that others in the ACWF don't follow the Scholar's example. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ DB: Now there's a man that could go somewhere. CC: I won't deny his obvious physical advantages, but I don't think he has the in-ring knowledge to be a champion. DB: Well, that's what experience is for. He certainly knew how to handle the Scholar last week. CC: Hey, shut up about that. Let's talk about how bad Iceman is gonna smoke this Dreamer kid. DB: Don't underestimate Randy Sheridan, he's a former tag team champion. CC: Yeah, for two weeks. Big deal. And besides, they robbed the Toughies for those titles at Winter Washout. DB: Well, LaCroix did pull the tights, I'll give you that. But that's history, we're talking about the present, and a world title defense coming up. CC: Before the match, we'd just like to mention that this is the REAL Greg Smith you'll be seeing here, not some Lance Fredricks' copy of him. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "ICEMAN" GREG SMITH vs "DREAMER" RANDY SHERIDAN (w/Jeff LaCroix) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ "Dream On", by Aerosmith played first, and the "Dreamer" made an appearance to some modest face heat, which he played up on by slapping hands and generally being a nice guy. Then "XYU", by the Smashing Pumpkins played, and crowd reaction turned 180 degrees for the World champion, Greg Smith. He absolutely loved it, holding his World title aloft for the fans to boo him even more. Referee Jack Donaldson was eventually able to get the fans to calm down enough that the match could start. CC: Iceman all the way! DB: How could I have guessed you'd say that? But, Clark was proved wrong in the early going. Smith hit a kick to the thigh, but then when he tried for an early Icepick he was Frankensteinered and covered for two. Sheridan elbowsmashed the bigger Smith, and followed up with a backspin DDT and another two count. Sheridan then went off the ropes and lost a bit of momentum, running right into a big boot from the champion. Smith picked his man up and vertical suplexed him hard to the mat. A legdrop followed, and a cover for two. CC: Just like I said, Smith all the way! DB: Well, Greg is in control at the moment, but don't count out the former tag team champion. Sheridan hadn't quite given up either, as he doged a lariat and hit a dropkick to stagger the big man. Two successive dropkicks almost had him, but Smith stepped away from a fourth, and then hauled Sheridan back up and nailed him with a brainbuster. Instead of covering, Smith whipped the Dreamer to the ropes and hit the most devastating shoulderblock we've ever seen here in the ACWF. A jawbreaker put Sheridan out like a light, and Smith went for the cover. 1...2... no! Smith pulled Sheridan's shoulder off the mat at the last second. Donaldson warned him not to do it again, but Smith just laughed in the face of authority. DB: He could have won, that was uncalled for! CC: What was uncalled for? Sheridan just managed to kick out somehow, that's all. DB: Oh, brother! Apparently, Smith wasn't interested in finishing this one without using his patented Icepick. He quickly did this, and then used one finger to cover Sheridan for three. WINNER, AND STILL ACWF WORLD CHAMPION, "ICEMAN" GREG SMITH DB: The REAL Greg Smith comes up big tonight and... oh no, this is despicable! In the ring, Jeff LaCroix had rolled into the ring to confront the cocky Iceman about what he'd done to his partner. LaCroix got two Icepicks for his trouble, and just for good measure the champ Icepicked Sheridan again too. He then left to astronomical heel heat, while Jack Donaldson attended to the fallen team. DB: What a despicable, horrid act! CC: Somebody got a thesaurus for Christmas! I think that was great! It shows that the Iceman has the killer edge. DB: Oh please, it just shows that he has no respect. In that vein, we bring you comments from Deadly Intentions. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Fade into a basketball court, surrounded by a wire mesh cage. Deadly Intentions are visible on camera, but are off in the distance. Slowly the camera zooms into them, and they also start walking towards the lense. They carry the ACWF tag team titles, and are both wearing DI cutoff shirts.] Bobby Nash "Here we are again, and we've gained some weight." Stevie Ramirez "Some from the holidays, but eight of these pounds from accessories made of gold. The former champs leaned the hard way, and I can't even think of their names. Oh well." [Ramirez begins to hum "Hail to the Chief" while Nash starts talking] BN "Now the ACWF has real tag champions to be proud of. Deadly Intentions is the top team in wrestling, ACWF or anywhere else. This week my partner has a singles match, and they stick him against a Reverend? Samuelson, you won't get any salvation tonight." SR "Bottom line, the time is up for you, Rev. And any teams out there wanting a shot at Deadly Intentions- Step up to the plate." ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ DB: A lot of confidence considering they have to defend those titles against Hellfire and Damnation in two weeks. CC: Oh please, those church sissies can't hold a candle to the best tag team in wrestling today, after the High School Toughies of course. DB: You had a valid point up until that Toughies reference. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ REV. MICHEAL SAMUELSON (on his own for once) vs STEVIE RAMIREZ (ditto) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The good reverend was out first, and seemed a little unsure of himself coming down to the ring without a partner. Stevie Ramirez, however, didn't allow anything but confidence come through in his demeanor once "Whiplash" started to play. He was all business, although he did have some fun taunting a few fans. DB: Who do you see in this one, Clark? CC: It's tough to call two tag specialists going at it in singles, but I'll take Ramirez just because he looks less jittery. Smauelson and Ramirez started things off in a "test of strength", with Samuelson winning until Ramirez booted him in the gut. Samuelson came back strong, no-selling a few punches and then slamming Ramirez down. A backbreaker had the tag champion in more trouble, and then Samuelson went to the second rope and hit a fistdrop. A cover only went for two, however, so Samuelson got frustrated and again whipped Ramirez to the ropes. Ramirez countered by Thesz pressing Samuelson, and then knocking his head off the turnbuckle with a slingshot. Samuelson staggered back towards Ramirez, and was put in a sleeper. However, Micheal Samuelson is not Hulk Hogan, and he did not go out. Instead, he went to the ropes and reversed the move into a sleeper of his own. Not to be outdone, Ramirez jawbreakered his foe to take the advantage back. DB: Back and forth action in these first few minutes, but which man has enough conditioning to go without making that tag they're so accustomed to? CC: Trust me, Stevie Ramirez is in much better shape. He looked it at the moment, as he piledrove Samuelson and covered him for two. He then went to the top rope, but showboated for a bit too long and missed when he finally went for a moonsault. Samuelson got back up quickly and hit a kneeto the back, and then really had Ramirez in trouble with a power bomb. He almost had the win, but the champ just barely got his foot on the ropes. Samuelson picked up Ramirez and whipped him to the ropes, and then both were hit with a double clothesline off the rebound. Ref Scott Ingraham started a count, and got to 6 when Ramirez got back up. No one but Ramirez noticed Bobby Nash beginning to make his way down to the aisle, so Ramirez smartly started to complain about hair pulls. Somehow, this lead to a discussion between Ingraham and Ramirez on the history of professional wrestling, so despite the crowd suddenly yelling "Turn around, ref!", Ingraham didn't notice Nash enter the ring and pick up Samuelson for a jackknife power bomb. However, Samuelson at the last second managed to backdrop Nash, and then tossed him out over the top rope. He then stood waiting for Ramirez, who was just finishing up his chat with Scott Ingraham. DB: Hey, wait a minute! I don't think Ramirez knows it didn't work! He thinks Samuelson has been jack-knifed! CC: No! Come on, Stevie, don't fall into the trap! Ramirez smugly turned around, and was shocked to see Samuelson standing. He didn't have any time to react, however, and he was quickly cradled and held for a three count. WINNER REV. MICHEAL SAMUELSON VIA PINFALL Ramirez was livid over the plan backfiring, so he clipped out Samuelson's knee from behind while the reverend celebrated. Bobby Nash soon reentered the ring, and the two men started to pound on Samuelson's ribs. However, Samuelson's partner Quincy Cameron quickly ran out of the back with a chair, and slid into the ring while Deadly Intentions took the high road and skee-daddled. (Yes, I did just make up that word.) DB: How about that for justice, Deadly Intentions have their own plan backfire on them! CC: There was no gold on the line there, Ramirez and Nash just weren't motivated, that's all. DB: Whatever you say, Clark. Well, we've got an incredible main event lined up for you tonight, as a couple of odd tag team combinations will try to co-exist long enough to get a win. One team is Ian Mantle and Harmon Stark, and they have worked together before, in the Survivor series match at Winter Washout. However, Ricky Reno and The Scholar have never worked together before. Oh, and did we mention that Reno and Stark face each other in two weeks at Beach Wars? And that Mantle and The Scholar have what may be the most heated rivalry in the ACWF today? Well, let's hear from both members of one of the teams, to see if they can co-exist. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Fade in on Slick Ricky Reno who stands in front of a wall picturing the ACWF logo. He has a p.o'ed look, and launches into a speech immediately.] SRR "Well Valachi, you proved why you've got the Canadian title, I'll admit that much. But remember this, you didn't beat me in that match, and I'll be back for the belt later. But tonight My friend the Scholar and I have two guys named Harmon Stark and Ian Mantle. First things first, Harmon Stark? That's not a name, it's a kitchen appliance. My friend, you are going down tonight. And then there's Ian Mantle. How dare you drag down the good name of Mickey Mantle. You are no Mantle, Ian. Wait a minute, I know why you've got the name Mantle. It signifies when you wrestle, because you always lose so badly that your opponent puts your head on his MANTLE. But I digress, and thank you to the Scholar for that word, names mean nothing. When we rip you apart in the ring, you can consider yourself in the presence of the best. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Scholar: "Tell zeal it wants devotion; Tell love it is but lust; Tell time it is but motion; Tell flesh it is but dust: And wish them not reply, For thou must give the lie." - The Lie, Sir Walter Ralegh. Indeed, Ian Mantle does lie when he tells you of grand voyages to Pittsburgh, and gastro-intestinal bouts with Pennsylvania sandwhiches. For where he actually was that day was my homeland, my England. You may ask for proof of these accusations, and surely I have much proof. Upon the door of my mother's home was found a towel, a blood-soaked towel. Who placed it there, prey tell? RC3: Ian Mantle, that's who. We can't quite prove it yet, but with my resources it shouldn't be that hard. Mantle, quit trying to play mind games with my Scholar. It was a nice little trick, and you covered your tracks well, but we are on to your game. I have hired someone who will put an end to cheap tricks like this for good. (Suddenly a smallish man in his early thirties walks into view. He is impeccably dressed, and looks of money.) Man: Allow me to introduce myself. I am Kevin Stone, Mr. Chalmers' new personal legal counsel. It is my responsability to find irrefutable evidence that will prove in a court of law that Mr. Mantle was indeed the party involved in the heinous crime committed upon my clients. We will be pressing charges on counts of trespassing, slander, fraud, and anything else I can find the evidence for, along with a civil suit for property damage and mental restitution. I will take any measures neccesary to find Mr. Mantle guilty, up to and including serving every single ACWF wrestler with a subpeona if I have to. Mr. Mantle, are you familiar with the term plea bargaining? ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ DB: (stammering) Uh... well I... CC: I knew that Mantle was up to something! didn't I uncover all that suspicious information? DB: Wasn't that Brett Greene? CC: Shut up. DB: These are certainly serious allegations if true, but I just can't see Ian Mantle doing anything like that. This is just an attempt by Chalmers to get Ian off his game going into that handicap match. CC: Don't be so sure, Randolph Chalmers wouldn't lie. Plus, that Kevin Stone guy seemed pretty reputable. DB: Please, the guy's a sleaze. He reminds me too much of my ex's divorce lawyer to seem truthful to me. Geez, if this keeps up we'll have more lawyers in the dressing rooms than the Dallas Cowboys do. CC: May I bring up an interesting point? DB: By all means, do. CC: That tape was only played on the outgoing transmission, none of the guys in this upcoming match, or even the fans here in San Juan, saw that tape, and Mantle didn't hear the accusations. DB: Good point, they'll be treating this like just another match, yet if somehow they heard it might throw a hamper in the tag team continuity of BOTH teams. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ IAN MANTLE AND "HIGHFLYER" HARMON STARK vs "SLICK" RICKY RENO AND THE SCHOLAR (w/Randolph Chalmers IIIrd.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Reno and The Scholar showed some solidarity coming out first, and both at the same time, to "Sharp Dressed Man" by ZZ Top. Both men seemed to be willing to work together, and Chalmers had a grin from ear to ear after having accquired for one night the services of Ricky Reno. Then Mantle and Stark also came out to the same entrance (Stark's) and clapped hands with a lot of the fans. The two teams went to their respective corners, and decided that The Scholar would start off against Ian Mantle. DB: It doesn't look like any of the wrestlers, or even the fans, know what just went on concerning the Scholar's wild accusations against Ian Mantle. I don't think The Scholar even knows his comments were aired... CC: This is gonna get REAL interesting. The match quickly got out of control, as Reno attacked Mantle before the bell, and hit him with a chokeslam. A kneeling headbutt really had Mantle in trouble, while The Scholar was busy being hit by a flying forearm from Stark. Stark tossed the Scholar over the top rope, but when he went to help his teammate, Randolph Chalmers got up on the apron and got in his face. Referee Colin Black went over to break up that dispute, while Mantle continued to suffer at the hands of Reno as a result of several kicks to the ribs. Finally, order was restored, and the Scholar re-entered the ring as the legal man. The Scholar nailed a falling splash on Mantle, and covered for two. The Scholar then picked up Mantle to suplex him, but was inside cradled for a slow two. Mantle got up to complain about the count, and was waffled from behind by a roundhouse right. The Scholar then dragged Mantle over to the corner, and rung his head against Reno's boot. Reno was tagged in, and he quickly hit a dragon suplex for two. DB: Look at the tag team continuity on the part of Reno and The Scholar! CC: That's what money can do, it motivates you to work harder. Reno got a little cocky, and struted for the fans for a moment. he paid, in the form of a German suplex for two. Mantle was then able to dive over and make a tag to Harmon Stark. Stark started off hot with a dropkick, and also hit a gutwrench to knock the wind out of his opponent at Beach Wars. Stark then went after the knee of Reno with a Half-boston Crab, but Reno was able to bring his other leg up into the midsection of the Highflyer, and then crawled over to tag in The Scholar. The brainy Britisher with brawn to boot came in trying a hiptoss, but was backslid for two. The Scholar reversed the move, and got a two count of his own after hooking the ropes for leverage. The Scholar then whipped Stark into the corner, and tagged back to Reno. CC: Things are looking up! DB: The moemntum has been very back and forth, and I still don't think these guys know about the sub-plot behind this one... Reno blocked a neck snap, and snap mared Stark over. The Scholar quickly started to get into the ring, so Mantle (ever the dutiful face) stepped in to argue. Colin Black went over to calm him down, while The Scholar choked out Stark with the tag rope. He then held him for Reno to take a few cheapshots, and to add insult to injury, The Scholar slapped his own hand and took over as the "legal" man. The Scholar faceraked Stark, and then went off the ropes. Stark put his head down to early, so The Scholar sunset flipped him for two, but stark kept rolling and got two of his own. Stark paid for it getting back up, by being hit with a short-arm clothesline. DB: Harmon Stark is taking a lot of punishment here. CC: And I'm loving every minute of it! The Scholar continued the barrage with a chokeslam, and covered Stark for (miraculously) only two seconds. He then went off the ropes and tried for a flying lariat, but Stark finally managed a counter attack and hit a standing dropkick. The effort of getting up was too much for the Highflyer, and both men lay out on the canvas. After a few seconds, Stark began to crawl towards his corner, as did The Scholar. the Scholar made it first, and Reno charged into (you guessed it, this only happens in every tag match!) a fresh Ian Mantle, who was just barely tagged in on time. Mantle wailed away on reno after blocking a punch, and then whipped him to the turnbuckle. He came back and was monkey flipped into the other turnbuckle, and pretty much was put out. The Scholar charged in, but Mantle simply backdropped him over the top rope to the floor. Stark managed to tie up the Scholar, while Mantle signalled for his Moonsault Kneedrop. DB: If he hits this, it's all over! CC: No! Someone, do something! Mantle climbed to the top rope, and took a moment to size up how far he'd have to leap. This was all that was needed for Randolph Chalmers, who shoved Mantle off the turnbuckle. Unfortunately for him, however, Colin Black saw it and called for the bell. WINNERS IAN MANTLE AND "HIGHFLYER" HARMON STARK VIA DISQUALIFICATION The Scholar quickly rolled back into the ring to go after the fallen Mantle, while Reno decided to try and help him. harmon Stark would have none of it, as he jumped the Slick One from behind, and the two brawled all the way into the stands, and soon disappeared out an exit. Meanwhile, Chalmers was holding down Mantle as The Scholar dropped a couple of legdrops on him, and then Mantle was picked up and hammered with the Final Passage running powerslam. Finally, enough officials arrived that The Scholar and Chalmers could be taken away, and mantle was attended to in the ring. The crowd gave a loud pop when he got up on his own power. DB: Well, a lot of shocking developments tonight will only be topped by the great action next week. We'll have an update on this whole Ian Mantle-Scholar situation, and we'll have Mantle himself in a non-title match against Canadian champ Joey Valachi. Plus, we'll talk about Beach Wars '97, and we'll have as always a lot of great matches. See you next week! CC: You forgot... "From Clark Cross and Darren Ball, good night." DB: Hey, I can vary my routine once in a while! --
