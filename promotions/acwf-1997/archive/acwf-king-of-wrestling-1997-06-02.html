The 1997 King of Wrestling, 2 June 1997

2 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
____________________________________________ Coming This Summer: | | | 0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0_0 /|_________________________|\ / |_________________________| \ / /|_________________________|\ \ / //The 1997 King Of Wrestling\ \ \ / // Live from \ \ \ / // Sky Dome, Toronto ONT. \ \ \ --------------------------------------- [The screen fades in to a studio with over two dozen monitors in the background with the 1997 King of Wrestling logo on them. A man, approximately 180 pounds, 25 years of age, with a dark blonde mushroom cut sits at a control desk in front of the monitors. The man is a well-known UWF wrestling host, Moe Owens. Moe looks to the camera and speaks.] MO: "Hello all you great wrestling fans out there. For those of you that do not know me, my name is Moe Owens. Some may know me because of my UWF work, but right now I come to you as the official spokesperson for the upcoming spectacular, the 1997 King of Wrestling tournament and battle royal. This is your first notice of the King of Wrestling. Allow me to explain a little about this inter-federation spectacular: The 1997 King of Wrestling is a special Pay Per View extravaganza to be held on Sunday, August 17th live from Sky Dome in Toronto Ontario. It will be a 30 man over the top rope royal rumble to declare the 1997 King of Wrestling. However, there are 60 slots available for wrestlers to sign in to. Now, I am sure you are wondering what the heck I am talking about. 30 wrestlers, but 60 slots? Let me explain. 60 wrestlers will be officially entered into the tournament. There will be a one round single ellimination tournament to be held. The rule is simple: in order to make it to the battle royal, you must first qualify by beating an opponent in a one on one contest! 30 men will be narrowed down from the 60 and will be entered! And in case of double ellimination, a list of reserves will be kept! In July, there will be three nights of singles action, with ten matches each! And these will be aired for free on the RSPWF wrestling network! Now, here is why I am talking to you right now... we need wrestlers! Before I tell you how a wrestler can enter, I will go over the list of names allready entered in the 1997 King of Wrestling tournament... [The monitors show a picture the mentioned wrestler] Our first entrant is a UWF newcomer, Sebastian Jericho. I've seen this guy folks, he is awsome! The second name to be signed in is a legend in e-wrestling. He is a former IIWF World Champion, and from I understand will be stepping out of retirement, Dan Kauffman! I wonder what the IIWF'ers will think of that as he has also wrestled one or two matches for a rival organiazation. The third entrant is one of the most hated men in UWF, a former U.S and World champion, Brett Greene! And from the ACWF, The Mexican competetor known only as Vortex. Also, here is a new list of entrants to the King of Wrestling: Filling the fifth spot is EWA's Magus! What a name! In slot six is Mad Dog Colleti. Following him in slot number seven is another man coming out of retirement, Johny 'Cool' Coles! It'll be interesting to see him back in action. Then in at the slot number eight is 'Badboy' Randy Acorn who is always a threat. And finally, the most recent signing is Mr. Acorn's own enemy, Chris Scottsdale. And that is just the beggining! Well, folks, my time is just about out. Don't forget wrestlers, to sign in to earn the right to be called the 1997 King of Wrestling! Thanks for your time, I am as always, Moe Owens!" More info will be emailed to those interested. TNM will be used, but roleplaying is __STRONGLY__ encouraged and I shall book to those who provide quality roleplaying. Therefore, the golden rule of: good writing ='s better push applies to this. Again, any questions or comments to be sent to: [detail withheld] (Thanks to [name withheld] for the character sheet) Name of Wrestler: Name of Handler: email: Federation wrestler is from: theme music(optional) type of character:(standard TNM styles) finisher: move that finisher ressembles: setup to finisher: weight: (total of 240 points for next three categories) worker: push: stamina: manager(if one): does the manager interfere?: if so, what weapon?: alignment(face/heel/neutral): does he use chairs?: Thank you for your valuable time. Rest assured. it is greatly appreciated. [name withheld] [detail withheld] <---- all info goes to this address [detail withheld] --------------------- Who will be King? ------------------- --
