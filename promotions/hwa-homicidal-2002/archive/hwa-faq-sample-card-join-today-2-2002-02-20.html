What is the HWA? - The Homicidal Wrestling Alliance (HWA) is a brand new, all fake, BCC Email RP Fedeeration. This fed takes place through email, although it is much different then your typical everyday Email Federation, which you will find out later. The HWA was established in February of 2002, by two experienced FedHeads. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 2. Who Runs the HWA? - Like I said above, the HWA was established by two experienced FedHeads. Both have owned feds in the past, and both have been in many different feds. So we know what you guys want and what you are looking for! [name withheld] -- He is the big guy, he is the one that is in charge of making sure everything gets done right and gets done properly, he is the owner that has a small on-screen role. Big Money -- He is the guy who came up with it all. He is the one in charge of making sure everyone has fun. He has a very large on-screen role, and he is the guy every HWA fan loves! ---------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 3. How does the HWA Work? - It's simple.. We are an email fed. Although we know that not everyone likes email feds, we figured this is the way to go. This way you can be as creative as you would like, without having to know HTML, or without having to upload pictures, just so you can include them in your RP's. - BCC... What the heck is BCC? Ahh.. BCC is the type of email federation we have. We know that sometimes, a fed gets started, and you start to send out roleplays, and one person doesn't like your roleplay.. so he goes and be's ignorant by sending Spam mail to everyone. Then someone gets mad, and sends out more Spam, and before you know it.. your caught in the middle of World War III!! Well, we provide a way out.. A BCC fed is where you send your RP's to us, and we send the rps to everyone BCC Style.. which means.. your email address won't be available to the world! ---------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 4. Events - There are two weekly events, Monday Night Mayhem, and Saturday Night Breakdown. - Monday Night Mayhem Monday Night Mayhem is our ' Smackdown' Although it is not as big as our primary card, Breakdown, it could have some interesting plots going on. The Monday Night Mayhem card will be sent out every Monday Night, right after the Previous MNM's results, and RP's are due Sunday's at 8 PM Eastern Time. - Saturday Night Breakdown Saturday Night Breakdown is out primary card, it is our 'RAW IS WAR' it is the biggest weekly event the HWA Holds. Anything can happen on Breakdown, any title can be defended, any type of match can take place. The Saturday Night Breakdown card is sent out every Saturday after the previous SNB's results. The Rp's are due Friday's at 8PM Eastern Time. - Supercard The Pride and Glory of the HWA. Every Month the HWA holds a Supercard, or a Pay-Per-View (PPV). This is the card every wrestlers wants. Grunges will be finished here. Titles will be on the line. Mostly every match has a catch, whether it be a cell, a cage, or maybe the ring is surrounded by fire. Supercards will take the place of a Monday Night Mayhem. Except Supercards will be on Sundays. So in other words, if there is a Supercard on Sunday, there will not be a Mayhem on Monday. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 5.
