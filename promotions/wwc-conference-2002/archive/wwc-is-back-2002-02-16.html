The WWC Is Back!

16 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you tired of time-consuming e-feds that promise big but don't deliver? Are you an experienced e-wrestler looking for a little more competition on the side? Or maybe you're starting off and wondering what all the e-wrestling fuss is all about? Well if you've answered yes to either of the above questions, the World Wrestling Conference is the place for you. In the WWC, you have complete control over whom your wrestler competes against and when. There is no favoritism. All matches are run using the TNM7 wrestling simulator, and the odds of your wrestler winning matches are based on his push, which in return is determined by his ranking. This means that the higher your wrestler is ranked, the better his chances are of winning. Therefore, if you are lower in the rankings, you have a better chance of winning if you compete against an individual closer to you in the rankings. When you win, your push goes up; when you lose, it goes down. So in the WWC, you start from the bottom and you pay your dues to get to the top. You have NOTHING to lose! Check it out! http://www.geocities.com/blouinn/wwc
