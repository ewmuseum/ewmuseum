Yes! The WWC Is Back!

18 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nice, well organized Web site. Here's your chance to win a title... Are you tired of time-consuming e-feds that promise big but don't deliver? Are you an experienced e-wrestler looking for a little more competition on the side? Or maybe you're starting off and wondering what all the e-wrestling fuss is all about? Well if you've answered yes to either of the above questions, the World Wrestling Conference is the place for you. In the WWC, you're not forced to post a multitude of time-consuming roleplays, and we do not play favorites. Here, you will get rewarded by the QUALITY of your roleplays, not their length and frequency, and you don't get ahead by simply knowing the fed head. Strategy will also play a huge part in your success. Whom you fight, and when you do, will determine your success in the WWC. http://www.geocities.com/blouinn/wwc
