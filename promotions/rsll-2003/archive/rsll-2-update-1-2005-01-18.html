<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<< RISING STARS, LIVING LEGENDS 2005 Update 1 groups.yahoo.com/group/rsll >>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Yes, it is back. And if you want to be a part of it, you need to read this and apply by the end of Sunday 23rd January.
After the astounding success of Rising Stars, Living Legends back in the Autumn of 2003, it was only a matter of time before RSLL returned.
This tournament celebrates the new and the old. It highlights the new masters and etches into the heaven the legacy of legends. Last time Langston Crete won the Rising Star tournament and Nick Demola won the Living Legend tournament. Will YOU win this time? Can your character stake the claim of Rising Star or Living Legend?
The definition in this case of RSLL is that a Rising Star is a character who has not been handled in eWrestling for longer than 2 years. A Living Legend is a character who has been handled in eWrestling for longer than 2 years.
We are looking for, ideally, 16 Rising Stars and 16 Living Legends. There may be a repeat of Brothers In Arms tag-team section IF there is sufficient interest.
The format of the tournament is subject to change but is likely to be an elimination tournament format with a first round in both catergories of 8 matches.
This tournament is a little different to other supercards. There is no fed representation, though I'm sure each fed will be pleased to see one of their stars win it. Furthermore, the decision making is mostly abdicated by me to…well, to you, those taking part in this. And unlike last time the voting is now opened up to anyone who is registered on the RSLL yahoogroup. To do this you will have to be registered on Yahoogroups because the voting will be done through the RSLL group. As soon as the promo show is released for each round the voting will open…and I'm hoping you'll vote according to the quality of the promos.
It is the promos/roleplays/flashes that will count in this tournament. The strats aren't going to be revealed to everyone, just to me and any match writers who volunteer. Therefore you'll have to concentrate your efforts on the promos and really use strats to get across any vital character point or spots that wouldn't be covered by your bios.
As for the promos…it's hard to determine what will make a promo that people will love but you'll probably have to aim to get the actual personality of your character across and also generate heat & interest in your character at the same time. Doing promos for tournaments is always tough. I wouldn't make them too long, I wouldn't make them too short. With the people you're up against, you're just going to have to be perfect.
How do you apply? You need to upload your character sheet to p220.ezboard.com/fewrestl...tablefrm29 and then send me the link to your character along with whether you want them to be in Rising Stars or Living Legends.
Submission of interest to be in RSLL2 does not guarentee inclusion. (But I'm not the kind of guy to turn people away). Promos will be sent to me at [detail withheld] You can catch me on IM in the following ways MSN: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] ICQ: [detail withheld] Yahoo: [detail withheld] You can also catch me on XBox, gamertag: [detail withheld] The level of competition involved is very tough. It's a shame that only one can win each tournament, but there can ultimately be only one.
As for matches and match writers…match writers are always appreciated, but those of you that know my match writing, you know I go for short sharp matches, somewhere in the 1000wd region, getting longer as the rounds progress…if you feel you can handle that and want to lend a hand in this historic tournament, email me at [detail withheld]
We didn't have a problem last time. In fact, we had some really great matches! Having said that, if you don't do a promo for your match, you will be defeated in a horrendously embarrassingly quick or painful fashion and that'll save on match writing!
Great stars, great matches, legends and rising stars…are you going to be a part of it? Please pass this on.
