<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<< ____ ____ _ _ | _ \ / ___| | | | | | |_) | \___ \ | | | | | _ < ___) | | |___ | |___ |_| \_\ |____/ |_____| |_____| RISING STARS, LIVING LEGENDS 2003 Update 1 http://groups.yahoo.com/group/rsll >>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Welcome to the first official update for Rising Stars, Living Legends. The response to this tournament has seen phenomenal, so much so that it has doubled in size. The quality of entrants has been of the highest order and it truly is my privilege to be running this.
There was an update sent out yesterday to those signed up for the tournament but a few corrections have had to be made due to accidental omissions and last minute additions, which has resulted in a slight alteration of some matches and the tournament structure.
This tournament celebrates the new and the old, the singles and the tag-teams and having spoken to many involved, whoever wins this will have done so by producing the most sterling work.
Of course, this tournament is a little different to previous supercards. There is no fed representation, though I'm sure each fed will be pleased to see one of their stars win it. Furthermore, the decision making is mostly abdicated by me to…well, to you, those taking part in this. Each participant in this tournament will have a vote on each match, all throughout the tournament. To do this you will have to be registered on Yahoogroups because the voting will be done through the RSLL group. As soon as the promo show is released for each round the voting will open…and I'm hoping you'll vote according to the quality of the promos.
It is the promos/roleplays/flashes that will count in this tournament. The strats aren't going to be revealed to everyone, just to me and any match writers who volunteer. Therefore you'll have to concentrate your efforts on the promos and really use strats to get across any vital character point or spots that wouldn't be covered by your bios.
As for the promos…it's hard to determine what will make a promo that people will love but you'll probably have to aim to get the actual personality of your character across and also generate heat & interest in your character at the same time. Doing promos for tournaments is always tough. I wouldn't make them too long, I wouldn't make them too short. With the people you're up against, you're just going to have to be perfect.
If you want to use an interviewer, use an interviewer from your current fed or you can use long serving interviewer Toxic Shock.
Promos will be sent to me at [detail withheld] You can catch me on IM in the following ways MSN: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] ICQ: [detail withheld] Yahoo: [detail withheld] The level of competition involved is very tough. It's a shame that only one can win each tournament, but there can ultimately be only one.
As for matches and match writers…match writers are always appreciated, but those of you that know my match writing, you know I go for short sharp matches, somewhere in the 1000wd region…if you feel you can handle that and want to lend a hand in this historic tournament, email me at [detail withheld]
Having said that, if you don't do a promo for your match, you will be defeated in a horrendously embarrassingly quick or painful fashion.
Most of the bios have been uploaded into the files section of http://groups.yahoo.com/group/rsll If yours is missing, you can upload it yourself into the relevant section or pass it on to me.
There isn't too much more to say. This is quite straight forward – two singles elimination tournaments and a tag team round robin tournament. 4 nights & 36matches of great action over a 50 day period
– can't want more than that can you?
