[ICWA] Fantasy Booking Federation

2000-05-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ICWA, or the International Championship Wrestling Federation is looking for wrestlers. But before you fill out this application there are a few things you should know about this particular federation.
ICWA, is not an e-fed. You do not roleplay, you don't make angles. I know your probably thinking "Huh? What's the point then?" Well, me and my friend Tha Kingfish have decided that since we're bored, and almost every fed we've tried to build or join crumbles within a month...we were going to just have some fun with a little idea of ours.
We are going to have our own little fantasy booking federation where you create as many wrestlers as you want, and basically hand them over to us. We will create angles, and the like.
There is an interactive part, we will have message boards and polls where you can tell us what you do and don't like about the way we're doing things.
You'll be able to judge the top faces and heels[no voting for your own wrestler], and give us ideas on what we should do next. So, basically you are going to be a fan. Your the ones who tell us how "over" people and angles are.
BUT we do have one minor rule...their will be no complaining about our decisions. You are giving us your wrestler, and doing none of the work so it shouldn't really mean that much when you lose, but if you do think that a losing streak or being a temporary jobber would get to you...don't join.
Now, that doesn't mean everyone is gonna be a jobber. I assure you that every one who takes the time to fill out an application will get their time in the sun at one point or another, but your also more than likely gonna have to pay some dues before you get there.
Anywayz, here's the application. Name - E-mail Address - ICQ # - AOL IM - Character Name(s) - Height - Weight - Hometown - Theme Song - Wrestling Style - Finisher - His/Her gimmick - Physical Description (include a celebrity, person that you have a picture of or wrestler he most closely resemebles, so we can put a photo of him/her on the bio page or show banners) -
Hope to see you in the RING!
