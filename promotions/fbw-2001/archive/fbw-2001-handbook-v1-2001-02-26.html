The Introduction "A letter from Mr. [name withheld]" Welcome,  Somehow you've gone and stumbled onto us here at First Blood Wrestling (FBW). But instead of wasting time making promises that none of you care all that much about, I think I'll just get right into what counts - the promotion!  First
Blood is a brand new booked e-wrestling league run by me, that's right, your's truly, [name withheld]. As a fed-hed and a writer for countless promotions in the past, I know my way around for sure.  What I'm hoping is going to really seperate FBW from other federations though isn't so much me and my experiences in the business as it is you - the handlers.  
 First Blood is going to depend on handlers that are looking just as much to have a good time as they are to see their characters win titles and beat people to a bloody pulp.  Here in FBW we're not so much trying to create a big old roster.. in fact, the roster will probably remain small.. but we're really trying to construct a whole federations filled with superstars that are sure to add excitement to the mix.  Angles and originality reign supreme here.  So if you're at all interested, please continue and really think about filling out the application at the bottom.  Thanks for your time.
 Founder of First Blood Wrestling, Mr. [name withheld] II. How it Started  Back in the late 90's a promotion known as the Underground Wrestling Alliance (uWa) was in its prime. Its leader at the time was the business savvy psycho himself, Mr. Shaun "Sutter" Kain. Kain ran the promotion straight through the roof - and then, some say on purpose, right through the ground. After over a year of some of the best action the sport had ever seen, the Underground
Alliance came to end. But struggling from the ashes of what was left of the uWa came none other then the man who would later be credited with much of the reason why the Underground was such a success. Apparently it was not President
Kain who was the master mind behind the promotion - infact, it was none other then Mr. [name withheld].
 Now [name withheld] has set the coggs in motion for the formation of his wrestling empire. Building a home base in Seatle, Washington, Mr. [name withheld] has openned his doors and is looking to sign some of the top talent in the sport for his newest baby,
First Blood Wrestling. According to [name withheld] himself, "Back when I ran with Kain, we where pushing a product (the uWa) that turned everybody's head.
Then things got stale. The same old shit served up brand new wasn't working anymore. Well, First Blood is going to do a lot more then just turn a few heads - we're going to break some fuckin' necks".
III. Rules and Regulations  1.)  Original characters only!  This should be pretty obvious, but just incase any of you where thinking about it for some unknown reason, I thought
I'd stress it right here.  Though I don't like the term, I won't be accepting anything but "fake" wrestlers into the eWa - the more creative the better.  No
"Stone Cold" Stew Boston or "Big Sexy" Ken Mash please.  2.)  Role plays do NOT, and I repeat, do NOT have to be novels!  As much as I love description and detail, myself, and probably everyone else will agree, you don't have to write over two hundred or some odd lines for it to be good.  As far as I'm concerned, if you can get your point across and make it interesting, you can write yourself a fifty liner.
 3.)  Though I know most people will be a bit shocked - don't worry about role playing ALL the time!  In the gWa we're looking for consistent RPers not people that put out a dozen a week.  One or two RPs per card is PERFECT!
 Just make sure they are GOOD role plays.. solid and creative. Besides, since FBW is run through a flash system (we'll be sending out flash packets about once a week) there is no need to send in fifty.
 4.)  Cuss all you want - just be reasonable.  Absolutely no racial slurs.  5.)  Don't use other wrestlers in your role plays unless you have their consent - same goes for President [name withheld] or Vice President [name withheld].
IV. How to Succeed  First Blood Wrestling is a closed, booked flash fed, which basically means there is a possibility for anyone to walk away with the win as long as they do their part. To break things down as simply as possible, here are the three ways of making sure you rise to the top in FBW..
 - Flashing (role plays): 50% - Angles: 40% - Strategies: 10%  FLASHING Obviously the first major factor in being successful in FBW is participating. And how better to participate then flash?! Flashes, or role plays, are the on-air comments of your wrestler expressing how he feels about an upcoming match, or just whatever is going on in the promotion at the time. Flashes will be sent out in packets about once each card. During the week you'll send your flashes to me and I'll keep REAL good care of them - then, about a day or two before the card, I'll send them all out in a neat little packet for your viewing pleasure.
 ANGLES Angles are just about as golden as flashes are. Being we're looking to have fun here in FBW, angles are going to be what really spices things up. Anything from current feuds or alliances, to what's going on behind the scenes can be considered a possible angle. Those of you who contribute and take ahold of what happens to your wrestler will be rewarded fittingly. Some angles are so powerful they alone can carry a wrestler to a win here and there.
 STRATEGIES Though weighing as much as those mentioned above, strats are the helpful little push to get your character over in a match that is thus far close. If you and your opponent have both been flashing really well and you know things are going to be close, you'll surely went to send in a strategy. Strats are detailed little plans for what moves or spots you'd like to see happen during the match.
Things like a special entrance, or maybe if you want to purposely have your character get DQed - anything! Of course strats are NOT always going to work, but I'll try my best to make them happen.
 V. Weekly Shows and Events  THURSDAY NIGHT TRAUMA TNT, as its been dubbed, is the big weekly card in FBW. Trauma is where all the big matches will take place along with title defenses galore. This is where the First Blood superstars will be battling it out to see who really is the best of the best. Expect about anywhere from five to six scheduled fully written, detailed, commentator style matches - not to mention UNscheduled matches.
 HARDCORE HANGOVER Late late Saturday nights are Hardcore Hangover. Hangover will only feature a few pre-recorded matches and some interviews. Matches for Hangover will be in summary format.
 Supercards or pay per views have not yet been determined for FBW. We'll probably be having one every other month or so, with the first to be held sometime in April or maybe even later March. Supercards will be where all those boiling out feuds end - though some may just begin.

Titles and Crowns  - First Blood Wrestling Heavyweight Championship The FBW Heavyweight Title is the most honored and precious title in the sports to date!  Everyone who's anyone wants to get their hands on a piece of this coveted gold - but only ONE man can hold it and it won't be easy!  The FBW
Title will be defended whenever President [name withheld] declares fit and it will always be defended in BIG matches against the number one contender.
 - FBW Television Championship The TV Title is the second top title in the promotion and even though some people may think second place it the first loser, that is HARDLY the case!
 The TV Championship takes a lot of guts and glory to capture and even more to hold onto.  It will be defended even more so then the Heavyweight belt.
