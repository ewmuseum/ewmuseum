OPENING MATCH!! "SABOTAGE" versus Conway!!] "Sabotage" Taj Collins versus "Slammin'" Steve Conway - Both of these men will be making their OWE debut. Steve Conway is coming straight from the OWE School of Wrestling in Philadelphia. His debut match he will face someone coming to you straight from Australia! Taj Collins has seen the world and wants to make it known he is in the OWE! Who will come out on top? [2. THE "DIRTY" ONE versus "TRUE LIVING LEGEND"!!!] "True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie versus "Dirty" Doyle Woodall - These two men have been at each other's throats since the OWE started. After Blottie caused Doyle his match with Jeff Keenan last week he demanded he get Blottie in the ring. And Blottie was quick to accept since Doyle caused him his debut match as well. These two will go to war. Who will come out on top? The "Dirty" one or the "True Living Legend"? [3. DREAM MATCH!! "2 SMOOTH" versus "SIMPLY GOLDEN!!!] "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife versus "Simply Golden" Jeff Keenan - These two men will collide in what is being billed as a dream match come true. Calvin Peter's righth and man is on a streak here in OWE, but then again so is Keenan. These two men both have other things on their mind but they will go to battle. Who will come out on top? Will Glamour play a role in the match? Or will the man who is stalking SVS make an impact? [4. MAIN EVENT!! 11 MAN BATTLE ROYAL!!
