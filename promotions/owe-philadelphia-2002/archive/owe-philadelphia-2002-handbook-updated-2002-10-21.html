All About OWE] [OWE Origin] On the evening of Sunday Night October 20th, 2002, multi-billionare Alex Smith decided he was going to open a wrestling promotion. He will handle all the business aspects of the promotion. He has called upon his life long friend Calvin Peete to handle the wrestling aspect of the promotion. The OWE is based out of beautiful Philadelphia, PA where they have their wrestling school and their main office complex. The OWE is an international federation coming to you live every week from a different city, sometimes even a different country. [OWE Broadcasts] Every Sunday Night the OWE brings to you Sunday Night Impact. Coming to you live on national television every week from a different arena across the country. Every couple months we venture into Canada, and sometimes over seas. Usually 4-6 matches of non-stop action. Just like the name says, it leaves a big Impact every Sunday. Every Friday Night at Midnight the OWE brings to you "Talk is Cheap." This is where the roster of the OWE tells the world what is on their mind. Uncensored and uncut. Sometimes this show can prove to you that Talk really is Cheap. [OWE Championships] OWE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - This belt is the best this industry has to offer. If you can capture this belt, you can officially say you've done it all in wrestling. Only the strong survive when it comes to this belt. OWE WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP - This belt is also known as the endurance belt. The champion goes out week after week and defend the belt. Defended weekly at Impact and ,like all other titles, at every Pay-Per-View. *When more people come in more titles will be added.* [OWE Pay Per Views] The OWE comes to you live on Pay Per View every other month from usually a stadium, or a bigger arena then where Impact is held. Here is a rundown of the Pay Per View Schedule: February - OWE Broken Hearts April - OWE Crossing the Line June - OWE Blackest Dawn August - OWE Summer Showdown October - OWE Horror Show December - OWE Liberty or Death [3. Meet The Faces] [Calvin Peete] He runs the show. He makes the matches, he makes careers, and sometimes he ruins them. He's the sheriff in town. [Frankie Lobeck] Hired from the now defunct Garden State Wrestling Promotion. Known for calling some of the greatest matches in wrestling history. Hasn't been seen since the final days of GSWP. Now brings back his expertise to the OWE. [Lance Marshall] Hired from the now defunct HHWO. Also hasn't been seen since the day HHWO closed it's doors. Making his comeback to wrestling with the OWE. [Mike Capone] The voice of OWE. The man who announces each wrestler as they make their way down to the ring. [4.
