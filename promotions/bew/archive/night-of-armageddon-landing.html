The BEW Presents...Night of Armageddon LIVE March 20th 1999 (You must have Real Audio to listen to the sounds on this page) A year and 6 months for those of you counting. A year and six months since those words were first uttered "M.o.A Supreme", and now for the first time ever the M.o.A found itself without a home base of operations. For the first time in it's long powerful existence the M.o.A was left out in the cold... Before the WCIWA closed the M.o.A did enjoy tours in many different feds...Many of these feds had become homes to them. The ICWL, M.o.A TF, WiW just to name a few, but surprisingly these federations as well closed up. The WCIWA seems to be all that as left. Then along came the BEW just in the nick of time. For their birth almost directly coincided with the death of the WCIWA. So a few M.o.A'ers fled to this foreign land but still...it wasn't home. They had not truly dominated. Styx had not made his presence felt... Now after 5-months the M.o.A's presence has been felt once more. A new home has been found. Familiar faces to oppose them, and most importantly a corrupt president interested in only the ratings to allow them to run wild. The M.o.A is home...the BEW and they shall become one. And so to christen this blessed event is one night...A Night of Armageddon... Control Center The Event...Aired Finally The Card... Participants... Interview Area
