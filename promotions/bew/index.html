An independently-run 1999 fed, its own full PPV called by its own President, staged as "a joint venture" hosting the Masters of Armageddon stable's own dominance angle before closing and giving way to the Masters' own successor promotion. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

BEW ran under its own President, Michael Merrix, staging Night of Armageddon (20 March 1999) at the Cox Arena in San Diego as "a joint venture" with the Masters of Armageddon (M.o.A) stable — a roaming faction the same held corpus traces back through the WCIWA and several other, since-closed federations before it found this "home." The fed's own commentary frames the whole show as a referendum on whether BEW itself "depends on the M.o.A to survive."

Jamie Williams won the BEW World Heavyweight Title from its reigning champion Devastator, billed the fed's own "header to the main event" — the belt's only documented title change in the held corpus. The night's own untitled main event that follows, Kevin Merrix against Flesh, the M.o.A's own leader, carries no title stakes in either the broadcast or the fed's own card page. The show closes on the M.o.A beating Merrix down after the bell — engineered by a turncoat Greg Eskridge and a cloaked Saleah Ridgers, under Styx's own returning order to "Break him" — before a surprise five-man run-in by his own former allies clears the ring.

A later document held under this same site's own MoA-branded successor (see "Masters of Armageddon") records that "the BEW did eventually close" — this wing's own holdings end with the one PPV served here.

The turn of Greg Eskridge Written by AI, 2026

Greg Eskridge opens the show as its own ring announcer, needling the M.o.A on-mic until they ambush and beat him mid-broadcast — read, on first pass, as a face caught in the crossfire. The main event reveals the angle running the other way: Eskridge delivers the beating that knocks Kevin Merrix unconscious, cuts a lengthy promo taking credit for Merrix's own career ("I made you, Kev"), and strips and gags him with his own jock strap before a cloaked Saleah Ridgers directs the M.o.A's own continued assault. Merrix unmasks Ridgers mid-beatdown; Styx, absent for the rest of the broadcast, returns in person only now, ordering the M.o.A to "Break him" before a five-man save — Eskridge freed alongside Merrix by the same rescue — clears the ring.

Affiliations

Masters of Armageddon (MoA)

Successor to BEW (1999) (1999)
BEW’s own held broadcast (Night of Armageddon, 20 March 1999) frames the show as “a joint venture” between BEW and the M.o.A, with BEW’s own President Merrix calling the show. A separate document states plainly that “the BEW did eventually close” afterward. The same creative circle continued as “MoA Corp,” running Night of Armageddon 2 (2000) independently, its own promotional copy billing it as proving dominance “over a single federation” the year before and “the entire AOWF community” this year. Recorded as two distinct promotions — BEW carried its own independent institution (President, five-title system, venue, commentary team) — rather than one renamed, since no purchase or acquisition language appears in the sources.
