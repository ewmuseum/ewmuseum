Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

DWF advertised itself starting 11 April 1997 with nothing but a boast: “The DWf is the best federation to join! It has things no other federation has to offer!” — no specifics, just an invitation to e-mail and join.

A second recruitment post on 18 April, after the fed had “just got under way,” filled in some detail: two shows a week, four championships, one pay-per-view, and a tournament “just coming up.” It still argued mostly by comparison: “DWF has so much to offer that no [other] fed has too!”

The boast did not go unanswered. Within two days, the operator of an already-running federation demanded to know what, specifically, no other fed offered, and a second federation's own vice president piled on in the same thread, each defending their own promotion's record against DWF's unsupported claim.

No further document survives. Whether DWF ever ran the two-shows-a-week schedule, crowned any of its four titles, or held the promised tournament is not something the record answers; what remains of it is one boast and the argument it started.
