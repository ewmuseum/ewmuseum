A Compuserve-originated e-fed that migrated to Usenet in September 1994 under an in-fiction Japanese-wrestling gimmick, running weekly show transcripts and at least five title lineages through a multi-part 1995 tag-team tournament before this pull's own coverage ends. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Japanese Computer Wrestling Federation began on Compuserve under its own on-air President, Sato Uhara, before migrating to Usenet on 23 September 1994 with a recruitment post pitching “titles to be won here, and much glory and honor to gain” against a roster built around gimmicks like Tiger and the destructive force of JCWF Hammer Quan.

Its Tournament of Honor closed out in April 1995 with a World Tag Team Title change staged inside a full-scale brawl: the reigning champions, the Great Oni and Ninja Blade, lost the belts to the Hillside Stranglers, Thomas DeWitt and Johnathon Moore, after a monstrous outside wrestler called 187 ran in to hand the Stranglers the gold, two more teams — the Wolfpack and the Broad Street Bullies — poured into the ring to settle their own grudges, and Ninja Blade turned on his own partner, piledriving Oni onto the concrete floor as the broadcast cut away.

No closure notice of its own survives; that tournament finale is the last dated document held from the promotion.
