UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #406

I wanna Party!

13 May 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's dressing room at a house show.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Another week has gone by and what do we have to show for it? A new member of this alliance called The Resurrection. Malcolm Kennedy decided to step up with the big boys and forget about Ruff Ryder and now the Prime Alliance is destroyed into little pieces, what more could you want? Nothing. They are ready to take on Stable Wars head on, and take on the Player's Club head on. Resurrection is finally complete, the four key members will bring about the destruction of this vile UWF and bring about the resurrection of what was once great but is now in peril.] [We have seen that even after his loss of the Tag Team Titles, Mike Montgomery gave himself a World Title shot and actually won it. Now that is what I call pathetic. Now their little pal Kevin Starr comes back, like he always does, ready for Stable Wars. Well bring it on, because Digital Mortality, Moraless, Bondage and Malcolm Kennedy are waiting for just that, competition. It seemed like the Player's Club had become stale, not being any real threat for the Resurrection, but now that their numbers are the same as ours, the fun just begins.] [As the weeks pass by and Stable Wars comes closer to being, we see, like every year around SW time, the demise and creation of new stables to participate in this wonderful event. We see one time teams forming, like The Faces of Doom that won two years before... *cough* won, yeah right. That Stable Wars was stolen from the hands of Digital and Joshua Dunn, and everyone knows that. Then last year Jamie Shock decides to ruin Digital's chances of winning by betraying him in the middle of the ring, but that will happen no more. With Moraless at one side, and Kennedy and Bondage in the other, they are a force to be reckoned with, and the Player's Club will know exactly what it feels like to be destroyed completely. Madison Square Garden, will be ground zero for the UWF.] [Now this SAS, our Tag Team champions, Moraless and Digital will be facing none other than the team RTP, Right To Party. Don't get me wrong, there is nothing wrong with wanting to party, hell, we all love to party! But when it comes to Resurrection and the Tag Team Belts, the match will be everything but a party. So Smokey, get ready, because it will be one hell of a ride.] [Now, before I leave so you can have the pleasure of seeing and hearing Digital, I wanted to say something about these morons... 'The Iron Curtain'. Duc and Thai... don't you realize that when you betrayed Digital, you comepletely destroyed and dismantled the Curtain? This thing you are trying to raise from the ashes is not the Iron Curtain, who is this moron you recruited? The Communist? What a pathetic excuse for a Stable. I guess Resurrection will have to drop the Curtain and destroy it once more.] (The scene opens with Digital Mortality and Burton Bell walking around in their dressing room at a house show. He is sitting on the sofa with his tag title belt on his shoulder. He is wearing his usual attire, black cut-off jeans, black shirt and his long red hair tied into a ponytail. He is talking to Burton when a cameraman enters the room without knocking taping for the crowd in the arena to see through the titantron.) (In comes Carsten Jancker to make the interview.) CJ: Digital, how is it going man? DM: Well, I was quite well until you decided to barge in here without my permission, but it's alright. CJ: Well, sorry about that. I wanted to ask you some stuff about this Sunday's SAS. DM: Shoot. CJ: What do you think of your opponents Right To Party? DM: They seem like cool guys. I got nothing wrong with them, they like to party, and so do I. The thing is, I am not going in the ring to lose to them. I might think they are cool guys, but when it comes to these baby, there is nothing anyone can do to seperate me from it, and I'm sure Moraless feels the same way. CJ: So there is no bad blood between you guys? DM: None whatsoever. CJ: Now, I wanted to ask you about The Communist. DM: Oh please, that guy doesn't deserve the time to be discussed about him. He is a lame attempt at resurrecting a stable who's greatness still thrives, but is already dead. The Curtain was the best stable ever in the UWF, and we might even argue that it could have been better than the Youngbloods of old, but sadly, the chance to prove ourselves is long gone. CJ: The Local NVA Company seem confident they can win. DM: Duc and Thai are nothing but fools! It's funny, because they say that I destroyed the Curtain, when it was them who constantly left to Vietnam to fight over there and left me and Dunn here to fend for ourselves. We carried this stable Dunn and I. And I got nothing but the utmost respect for Joshua, I might not like him, might not like the company he keeps, but he is one hell of a competitor and in my book, that counts. CJ: Now, what do you think about Kevin Starr? DM: Well, Kevin is another competitor. He is always in the shadows of that fool Montgomery, and could never rise to the status that I am in. CJ: What about Raid? DM: That fool dared to attack me. He thinks his little Anarchy is going to beat either the Resurrection or the Players Club, then he is dreaming. He will suffer the consequences of his actions very soon. CJ: One mo... DM: I'm done. I will be appearing tomorrow in The Sideshow with Jon Focker, so stay tuned for that! (Digital shoos the reporter and the cameraman from his dressing room.) BB: What are you planning for this Sunday? DM: Nothing... let them make all the moves they want, because in the end, at Stable Wars, we will prevail. (Scene Fades.) OOC-Crappy and short, but got a final on Saturday... can you believe that!?
