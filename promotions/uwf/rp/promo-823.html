Fan #2 Where is the Party

5 Dec 2004

Adien Pryde — Chaos Society Bus

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to inside of the Chaos Society’s bus. The bus itself is dark. The windows are tinted a deep black color, and the interior is red. The bus is also very lavish, almost looking more like a living room instead of a bus, complete with TV, couches, game systems, and training facility. There are some random locations where you can see the red circle with an “A” in the middle of it. The symbol of anarchy, the symbol of the future tag team champions, Adien Pryde and Reo Dai. Joanna and Pryde are sitting on a couch watching the recent promos done by the various wrestlers for UWF’s next PPV, FANdemonium.] Joanna : There is a lot of activity in the UWF the last few days. FANdemonium sure has lit a fire under everyones butt. Everyone is gunning for everyone else. Pryde : It’s a PPV, what do you expect? This is supposed to be what the fans want to see, and the wrestlers want to make sure the fans get what they want. Joanna : This is getting insane. Mike Montgomery is ready to end his career to get in the ring with Clyde and Seymour Almasy is ready to end his life because he thinks his opponent Tsunami Oni is dead. Pryde : Does Dai know that the Commie may be giving the place a visit? Joanna : Yes I called him as soon as I heard about it. Pryde : Commie had a good idea though. Maybe you should wear something more revealing tomorrow. You know, maybe something strapless. [Joanna goes off and backhands Pryde in the face.] Joanna : In you dreams. I will wear to your match the same thing I have ever other match my Black Biker Shorts and Red cut off T-shirt. Nothing less. Pryde : OK I get it. Joanna : What about your TV Title match? You don’t seem to be putting much thought into it. Don’t you want another title reign? Pryde : The TV Title is the bottom of the heap. A Title for the up and comers and I have proven that I am better than that. With Dai back at full strength I am 100% focused on the Tag Titles and nothing will stand in our way. Joanna : At least your partner Oni is back to his old self. I would have hated to have you tag with a stripper. Pryde : That is a relief. Outside of Dai, Oni is the best choice I have for a partner in that mess of a match. Never a bad thing to have the World Champion on your team. Joanna : What about the rest of his stable. You will be up against every one of them in one match or another. Pryde : Well Malcolm Kennedy is in the TV Title match and I have said my peace about that match. But I do have to admit, the other three may make the Venetian Home Rumble 5 pretty damn interesting. Persia is with Killer Dude, Mike Montgomery is with his enemy Clyde, and Jamie Shock is with Kenney Mercury. Anyway you look at it, it is a good bet that that stable will walk away with the title. Joanna : Do you actually think that Oni will actually lock up with any of them? Pryde : Doesn’t matter. Tag team wrestling is what I am best at, and no matter who is my partner, I intend to come out on top. [A voice is heard over the intercom system of the bus.] Drivers Voice : Joanna, Pryde, we are almost at Kazee’s home. He does know we are picking him up doesn’t he? Joanna : I left a message a few days ago but I haven’t heard anything form him. It’s almost like he fell of the face of Winona, I mean the earth. If he doesn’t answer the door he will just have to find his own ride to FANdemonium. Drivers Voice : Will do Joanna. Pryde : What is with him anyways. Every since he joined The Chaos Society he has been in his own little universe. Joanna : I am sure it is some inner conflict that needs to be resolved. Fizzy, Meaner and Kazee need to all sit down and have a serious talk amongst themselves. Pryde : He better do it soon otherwise Clyde is going to kill him. Joanna : His problem. You deal with yours and bring us home the gold. Now turn on “The Real Gilligan’s Island”. [Pryde reaches for the remote control as the screen fades to black.]
