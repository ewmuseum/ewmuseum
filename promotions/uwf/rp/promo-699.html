Consider his marbles spilled

24 Sep 2004

White Wolf — White Wolf's leaky Basement

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
White Wolf The duality of conflict; The man of interest Man: 'He's not insightful! He's insane! He should be anywhere but in a public arena filled with many innocent people to whom his loose mind might do harm!' [His voice echoed through the crowded atmosphere of the room. In the busy police station one man, a lawyer, Dorrin Golddrop pleas his case to the chief of police. Dorrin has no intentions of having UWF charged. However, he feels that White Wolf's current state is far beyond anything we have seen before.] Officer: 'LISTEN! Mr. Golddrop, I understand your concern. I'm just not sympathetic about it. So no.... I refuse to give you government documents on that man's criminal history. Now please leave.' Dorrin: 'But without those documents I have no case.' Officer: 'Why does it bother you so much? What has the man done to you?' Dorrin: 'Personally? Nothing really isolated to me. But I was standing outside a bus station one day...' When suddenly, there he was... White Wolf. Walking down the street! I couldn't believe it. I wouldn't approach him though. Not after all the tabloid stories. In fact, he even looked menacing in person. I remember he was wearing a long black shirt and tight jeans that were ripped and had blood on them. His eyes were black... Probably from being a heroin addict. His face was pale. But I'll never, EVER for get that smile on his face. That sinister grin he sported. Well, you see, he was minding his own business, when people took notice of him. A child (in abouts the age of 13 or 14) was biking towards him and yelled, 'WHitE WoLf!' When the poor child got close to White Wolf, he punched him off the bike... I wanted to help, but I was too scared. Then a crowd of gentlemen in suits tried to detain the monster by hitting him with their briefcases. I don't remember much of this part as I was trying to notice if the child was okay or not. All I remember is that eventually, they took White Wolf down and police took him away. Dorrin: 'Ever since that day, I've been trying to find something to stick to White Wolf to keep him away from the public for a long time.' [The officer hangs his head.] Officer: 'Look... Even if I could give you information, it wouldn't help you... You're fighting a man that has more public arrests then Jim Morrison. He has a religion that precedes law, which states that he can legally use Masculine for recreational purposes. He's famous (or infamous), so he's untouchable. This vendetta you have has to stop or there will be consequences... I see no one good thing that can come from this. Now I'm telling you to stay away from him, and from me!' Dorrin: 'I can't do that... Not after-' Officer (Slamming his fist on the table and pointing at the exit): 'GET OUT BEFORE YOU BRING THIS PLACE TROUBLE! GET OUT NOW! AND TAKE YOUR RECORDER WITH YOU.' [Dorrin Golddrop stares over the desk at the clock. 6:00PM] Dorrin: 'Fine... I'll leave...' [The men leave and head towards a large Ford Bronco. They climb aboard with Dorrin in the front seat. He's putting on some leather driving gloves, and changes his glasses to his sunglasses.] Dorrin: 'Its a result of conflict. The man causes so much conflict he has become a social disease. Everyone seems to fear his presence yet, he seems to snake free of all his past troubles. However, he always falls back into his own shit. Its time someone took a stand. Its time to go meet the Wolf...' --------- *TAP-TAP TAP-TAP TAP-TAP White Wolf: 'This is wonderful... Isn't it? I have quickly become the man of interest in the UWF... wouldn't you agree?' [Tapping his fingers on the table White Wolf seems content. His bald head (yes bald) rests on his hand and his creepy grin smirks across his face. Sitting directly across from the nomadic crazy man is UWF's Larson. He's constantly looking towards an exit.] Larson: 'White Wolf... err, How about that crazy get-up of yours?' [Larson refers to White Wolf's 'new' look. The bald head. The ear piercings. The bondage straps. The white taped hands with bear skin paw gloves. Blue jeans.] White Wolf: 'In my state of mind...' [White Wolf's eyes widen, and he hunches over with a big grin leaning closer and closer to Larson with his hands firmly on the table.] White Wolf: '... attire is the only thing that lets people know that I'm SANE!' [Larson shudders.] Larson: 'But you're covered in bondage... bondage and you're bald with dirty scruff. Charles Manson looked like the Neighborhood Watch Parent compared to you!' [Slams his fist on the table knocking over everything beer bottle and whiskey bottle on the table.] White Wolf: 'Torturing my head is no good idea Larson... They say...' [He pauses and grins again] White Wolf: 'They say I'm crazy. That I could go 'off' at any minute. They tell me to take medication. I never take medication to heal. I would die before I heal with that demon creation.' [Larson takes note of all the pill bottles (open and empty bottles and scattered random pills) that lie on a shelf.] Larson: 'What are those for then?' White Wolf: 'Those? Those are for my craziness. You know they say I'm crazy... That I can go off at any minute. So I have to take medication for it. They say it keeps me balanced.' [Larson begins to tap his fingers anxiously.] Larson: 'Well... ummm. Would you be offended if I told you that you scare me?' [White Wolf jumps out of his chair and launches the table over on the concrete flooring breaking every bottle that was on the table. The table itself is now broke. White Wolf breaths heavy with his jaw clenched and speaks furiously and demonically through his teeth.] White Wolf: 'I'd be delighted...' [Larson is definitely scared. White Wolf pulls his chair up uncomfortably close to Larson's. White Wolf sits down.] Larson: 'Ahem... Yes... Well. So they're throwing you right into the ring. In a TV Title match on your return. What do you think of that?' White Wolf: 'I think I am pleased. But I hate the Irish. And to obtain the TV title on my return would be grand! Grander then being Irish... fucking Irish. I have held that title twice before... My first time... I beat a legend in wrestling to win it. You know for a wrestler who has never held a major title, I have enjoyed many spoils of victory over the top wrestlers.' Larson: 'We know that. Can I ask you something personal? Are you in therapy?' White Wolf: 'My last therapist mysteriously died...' [Larson backs up.] Larson: 'WHAT!? What did you do!?' White Wolf: 'What did I do to who???' Larson: 'You're old therapist?' [White Wolf puts his elbows on his knees and rests his head on his hands and smiles.] White Wolf: 'I never had a therapist silly.' Larson: 'Er ... Whatever.' [Suddenly, the door busts open. Dorrin Golddrop stands in the doorway with a large bat in one hand, and a machete in the other. White Wolf smiles.] White Wolf: 'This looks friendly.' Dorrin: 'Mister Wolf... I'm Dorrin Golddrop... and like Slasher, I am going to be very vigilante tonight and I will take your life.' [Larson steps back and begin to dial the police] White Wolf: 'Just try it pony boy...' [White Wolf gets ready for a bout and they circle the room starring each other down.] Dorrin: 'You... You need to be stopped. For the good of the people, and for the good of your own. Before you go too far.' White Wolf: 'HAHAHAHAHA! Thursday, I will be going farther then you'll ever get in you're whole life pantywaist. You seriously think a tiny little man like yourself can take the wolf instinct in a godly body like mine?' Dorrin: 'That's what the knife and bat are for...' White Wolf: 'Well after I kick your ass with those, you can lend them too Alex Gay so that he has a chance against me...' [Dorrin lunges the knife at White Wolf. White Wolf doges the stab. Dorrin cracks the bat over White Wolf's head. White Wolf drops. Dorrin raises the knife. Dorrin swings the knife towards White Wolf's face. White Wolf rolls out of the way and is back on his feet. He smiles.] White Wolf: 'You're quite the saucy bastard... You almost had me...' [White Wolf dodges another hack. He returns with a blow to Dorrin's face. Dorrin stumbles. White wolf takes advantage and delivers a sidekick to the mans face. He flies back into the cement wall of the basement like room. Dorrin drops the weapons. White Wolf picks up the knife and walks towards Dorrin. He pins Dorrin's hand up against the wall. Oddly, White Wolf places the knife in his pinned hand. White Wolf smiles and stares at him.] White Wolf: 'You shouldn't have come here... Whoever you are.' [Sirens are heard outside. White Wolf laughs and runs his face up the blade in Dorrin's hand. Wolf's blood runs all over Dorrin, and Dorrin shakes with rage. The police bust in and White Wolf hits the ground. Dorrin stands over White Wolf with a bloody knife and dripping in foreign blood.] White Wolf: 'THIS MAN ACOSTED ME! He attempted to kill me several times! Have him arrested!' [The police grab the man and help White Wolf to his feet. White Wolf acts weary like he was beaten terribly.] Larson: 'Ladies and gentlemen... I'm out of here. This guy needs help, and the UWF need to be careful. I think we may have a nuclear bomb in the form of a man... and I'm not talking about Danny Jaye!' [Fade...]
