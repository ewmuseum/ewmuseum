UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #353

Mentok returns~!

date unknown

Mentok the Mindtaker

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guess who’s back? So, have you figure it out yet? ? No? Common’, I’m dying to know here. … MEN-MOTHERFUCKING-TOK! Ring a bell bitches?!? For those of you who don’t know who Mentok is, let me be the first one to say … ARE YOU SERIOUS?! Mentok is the greatest thing to ever hit the planet earth, and he does not mean it figuratively here, he actually is (it’s proven). Mentok is a mind taker, which is why they call him Mentok THE Mind Taker. What does that mean you ask your pitiful self? It means he takes minds, could you not figure that out on your own? It means that this EeeeeeeeViiiiiiiiiL green Alien from a planet you have never heard of can actually break into a person’s mind and control what they do, say and think. You think you are actually reading this out of your own will? You do?!?!?! Ha ha ha ha, pitiful mortals! Mentok is actually controlling your minds through your computer screens and FORCING you to read this. All in all, he is strong, you are not. To make it even more simple, Mentok owns you … he owns you ALL. NOOOOOOOOOOW, onto business, yeah? “Why did Mentok leave the UWF the first time he was there?” is the question you are asking yourself, right? Of course you are, remember … Mind-Taking. Well to answer you stupid little question, he left because he felt like it. He was not amused enough with the antics of mortals trying to beat him down. At first, every thing was funny, he beat one of the roster members with a flying vacuum cleaner, but then, things started getting dull for him. See, when you have the power to do whatever you want with human beings across the globe, why would you stay in a federation? Now, you are telling yourself that the same may happen this time around, that Mentok might just up and leave again. And we ALLLLL know that you are smiling because without Mentok around, your chances at winning matches are much higher, and yes, you are right, Mentok might choose to leave, but not now, for he now has a mission! Yes that’s right monkey boy, a mission! The mission starts in the UWF, but you see, it will not stop there, OH NO~! It will go to the bigger picture when it is done, and when it gets to the bigger picture, then and ONLY then will Mentok leave the Earth and return to his planet, Nebular! Yeah you read right, Nebular, you have never heard of it, and there is a reason for that … it’s because you SUCK! NYAHAHAHAHAHA~! With all the commotion, I still have not told you what master Mentok’s master plan is, have I? Well you see, as a Mind Taker, Mentok has the power to control people and make them his Mind Slaves. On his last passage on Earth Mentok took over the minds of a few wrestlers over in PTC and had so much fun doing it, so as he came back from Nebular in his Intergalactic Space Ship of RULENESS~, he began plotting and scheming like the mastermind at evil that he is, rubbing his hands against one another, nodding his head and laughing to himself. Many thought he was crazy, and all of those people died … Because Mentok is not crazy, he is just UBER EVIIIIIIIL~! And UBER EVIIIIIIIL~! Plot and scheme while rubbing their hands and nodding their heads! Anyway, after having turned the people who thought he was crazy into human chickens with his SUPREME MINDTAKING~!, he sat there and watched them have a cock fight. What would you expect? Would you not want to see two human beings acting like chicken (or like cocks, nyahahahaha) and pecking at each other? It’s actually very amusing and refreshing. After his well deserved recreation minute of rest, Mentok got back to plotting and scheming. His plan is clear and simple, to a Mind Taker … not simple to a human. He will take over UWF with his arsenal of SUPREME MIND-TAKING~!, and then after, he will gradually take control of PTC. What’s Mentok ultimate goal you ask? Let’s ask Mentok that, shall we? - - - - A purple mist began to form itself above the ground. Cue in to Darth Vader’s theme music to add that special EVIIIIIIIIL~! Factor to the precedings. Strobes lights flickered on and off as a audio tape featuring people “ooohing” and “aaaahing” begins to play out. In all evidence, Mentok wants to make one hell of an entrance. The mist continues to fill up the screen’s focus, and the suddenly, everything stops, and the mist begins to drop down to the floor. Only this time, something is standing in the middle of it, only this time, the mist was shrouding the greatest of all Mind Takers. MENTOK~!
