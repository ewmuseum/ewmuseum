Ruff Ryder

( The scene begins out in an open field. Ruff Ryder is standing under a large tree leaning against the trunk with his arms folded wearing a black t-shirt with Prime Alliance in red letters across the chest. He has his usual cocky grin, but is without his PWW North American Championship. The camera zooms in on his face as the Cherokee Thrilla begins to speak. ) Ryder: Notice somethin missing? I do...IT'S MY DAMN BELT! WHAT THE FUCK WAS THAT SHIT ALL ABOUT!! * Ryder calms himself before speaking again * Ryder: How in the hell could I possibly lose that title with the lights out and the ref not even knowin what the hell was goin on? You tell me that, because i'll never get that. I don't know what happened while the lights were off and i'm not even gonna get into it right now, because I got other things to focus on. The first thing is this little fuckin prick Ezra Kazee. You are like a damn fly that is buzzin around my head Ezra, I mean I swat you and shoo you away, but the second I turn around you're right back in my damn face again and it's gettin a little fuckin annoying. You couldn't beat me in the Contest of Champions so what the hell makes you think you can beat me now? Escaped with the title? Are you out of your head son? I beat your ass from one end of the arena to the other. Fuck the title, you were lucky to get out of there alive boy. You ain't on my level, how many times must I remind you of that fact? Why didn't you ask santa for some wrestling skills before you agreed to a match with me, because i'm gonna destroy you bitch. You can't hang with the Cherokee Thrilla. That's right I said the Cherokee Thrilla, because i'm Cherokee motherfucker, I ain't got to explain shit to you or anyone else. I like who I like and if you got a problem with it then why don't you do somethin about it. I'll tell you why, because you can't. You couldn't beat me if I laid on the mat and went to sleep. Just cause I don't put on a damn head dress and go stomp dance in the middle of the ring doesn't make me any less a Native American than the next Cherokee. Fuck you and if you don't like it i'll see your ass in the ring son. * Ryder glares into the camera before switching his focus * Ryder: I bet you're all wondering why i'm out in this field under this big damn tree for my promo huh...well that's why i'm out here, to make you assholes wonder about it. I'm whoopin Ezra's punk ass, and Kennedy is whoopin Dunn's ass, and The Shock is gonna make Mike Montgomery his punk bitch. The Prime Alliance is and always will be top dawgs in this place. Nobody can take us down and anyone that tries will end up six feet under. We are the best in the business and we accept all challenges, because we can't be stopped. Oh and for our NEW champ...enjoy it while you got it. * Ryder smiles at the camera as the scene fades to black *
