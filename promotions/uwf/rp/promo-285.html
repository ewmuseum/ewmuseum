Upset Special

13 Mar 2004

Iron Lung — Backstage

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Upset Special Starring Iron Lung ** The following is a backstage exclusive account of Iron Lung's reactions after losing the Triple Threat match for (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship to The Plague. It may contain some graphic violence and course language, therefore viewer descretion is advised.. ** The stomping of wrestling boots could be heard all the way through the hallways, ringing into our ears and getting closer to us with each passing moment. These were the steps of a confused individual, and an individual who obviously had something on his mind. Perhaps something went wrong, terribly wrong on his part, or perhaps he just wasn't in the mood to be in another show. The stomping kept getting closer, and along with it a faint huffing and muttering of obscenities. Now the plot thickened, as they say in the business. What could possibly be upsetting this person so much that he was yelling loudly, and now damaging public property to boot by pushing down trash cans and such? When the footstep sound finally got close enough, we were able to catch a glimpse of one Iron Lung. So this was the menace to the backstage area, the man who was so ticked off about losing a Triple Threat match to The Plague. That wasn't the only thing he lost in the process, he also lost (T)he (H)ardcore (C)hampionship and his undefeated standing among the stars. This was not going to be a good rest of the night for him, and we weren't exactly happy about having to approach him about it. But, it was our job, and we had to follow through.. As we kept walking closer, we said a little prayer that we would walk out without any type of physical harm. We had caught wind of the savagery characteristics of this man in the past, he almost broke our bones with his harsh grip after we asked what in his mind was a stupid question. We could only imagine what kind of harm he could do to us now. We were stuttering before the words even came out of our mouths, but we had to find out what his reaction was, whether he wanted us to talk to him at that very moment or not. So, with shuttering knees and a crackled voice, we made a bee line to the ex-champion and prepared for the worst when getting ready to ask him about his feelings. .. uh, ex..cuse me, Mir-- Lung? Suddenly, he turned our way, giving us a look that would send shivers down even a deceased's spine. Those flourescent red eyes, they were always filled with such a fireball, and everytime he was upset, they were like bombs and other explosions going off inside. It was amazing, but at the same time, utterly terrifying. He stared at us for what seemed like an eternity, and we just stood there, grasping onto our breath and locking our knees up, shaking like a leaf in the breeze. Was he that intimidating? No, he was just that damn psychotic when he was upset. We review all of the things he has done to us in the past in previous shows, including throwing us into a table and knocking us unconscious numerous times as well with kendo sticks and fists to our temple. We also recall many doors being slammed in our faces. Finally, the glare from his eyes seemed to turn away, and his red eyes seemed to gray out while still looking at us, as if shielded by some extraordinary emotion. His face while remaining as fixed as a stone, seems to chisle a bit of an uneasy frown. We keep walking closer until we are in his full presence, putting the company microphone between the two of us. (Gulp) I... uh -- was just wondering... what you are feeling right now.. having lost THC after only having it for a couple of weeks... His expression went blank, and once again we shuttered with fear and winced as he raised his hand to us. But then, he calmly put his hand on our shoulder and shook his head with discontent, gripping hard but not hard enough to hurt us in any way. He looked into our eyes hard and long, almost as if he were a predator and we were just the prey, standing in his territory and filled with such fear that we couldn't move. It was true, we were paralyzed with the fear of this man, even if he was a calm individual in a normal state. But as of right now, he was far from normal. We could see that he was full of pain and rage as he calmly collected his thoughts while grasping for the right words to say, tapping us on the shoulder with an open palm all the while. And after several moments, he finally stumbled across the words that he would say to the camera, and he would say it knowing that the whole audience was watching. I really don't know what to feel right now Scott. All I have to say is, I obviously didn't put my best effort into this match, and I paid the price for it by losing the title. And I also paid the price by ruining my perfect record, but I suppose nobody is perfect in this business, right Scott? (Stuttering still) No-- no.. nobody is perfect. It's just a shame that I couldn't concentrate on my opponents hard enough to actually defend my title like a true champion would. And now, I have to start all the way at the bottom of the Hardcore chain, almost like living my life all over again. How do you think that makes me feel Scott? I -- I'm... not exactly sure Mr. Lung -- I'll tell you how it makes me feel then, since you obviously don't have a clue, you ignorant punk ass kid. I've been in this business for too long to start over, too long to make an ass out of myself in front of my fans like this. I don't know what was going on in my head, but obviously it was the wrong things, do you catch my drift? I -- I... believe I do Mr. Lung -- All right then shut up and keep listening.. All I have to say is this, and that is that The Plague is a hell of a fighter, and he showed me the definition of hardcore or whatever you want to call it. I got beat at my own game fair and square, but now it's in the past, and now he has my title wrapped around his scrawny little waist. He's walking around with it as if it were a fashion item, thinking it will impress anyone he walks by. But guess what.. It isn't impressing me, and in due time, that belt will be back where it belongs and it will be staying where it belongs for a long time to come. You may have gotten the best of me this time Plague, but I assure you that you haven't heard the last of Iron Lung.. .. Not to switch tracks or anything Mr. Lung --- but.. I have just been informed that next week on Supremacy, you will be facing another tough opponent by the name of Malcolm Kennedy! Do you have any words... for Mr. Kennedy.. At the moment Scott, I have a lot of words for Mr. Kennedy. Because you see, I'm a little ticked off about what happened tonight, and when I get a little ticked off, things are said and poor little innocent reporters like you end up getting their asses stomped to the ground. All I have to say to Malcolm Kennedy is this. He may think he has the advantage over me since I just lost for the first time, he may think that he can make it two in a row now since I'll be so depressed that I won't be able to concentrate or something. Well, he'll have another thing coming, and you can count that as a damn fact. Because this is just the beginning of a new road now, and he will be the first to fall on my way to THC.. So Malcolm Kennedy, the odds are indeed still in my favor. There is nothing and nobody that will stand in my way from getting my title back, and when I am focused, you might as well not even bother to make an appearance in the ring. It will be over in the blink of an eye, I will be walking tall, and you will be lying in a hospital bed. Malcolm, I suggest you stay in your little locker room, and think long and hard before you ever think that you have an upper hand, especially against somebody like me. Because my friend, when you think positively, it only leads to one thing. Your ass on the mat, me pinning you and the referee counting.. I'll let you do the rest Scott.. Iron Lung pumps the microphone into Scott's chest hard, making him slide back a little bit and crouch over, catching the wind in his breath once more. The camera slowly begins to fade away with Iron Lung opening the door to his locker room, and slamming it loudly from behind, leaving Scott coughing in the hallway as the camera goes to black and then to static...
