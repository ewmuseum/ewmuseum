UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #622

Coming from all around

13 Aug 2004

Mr. Meaner — The Forest.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a forest. It is very dark and very dreary. Walking amongst the trees is Mr. Meaner. He is wearing all black, like usual. His face is covered and his expressionless mask is even more so than usual. He walks around, weaving in and out of the trees, almost playing hide and seek with the camera.) Meaner: So, the put me in the Regal Rumble. What a great match that is. A match where there are absolutely no rules what so ever. Just me, Chaos Society, anything we can get our hands on, and 30 some odd jerkoffs that think they are good and tough….. We have a bunch of wannabes in this match. We have a bunch of people who think they are tough and can do anything they want. They think that you can just walk around and be some grand old guy and be well known and successful and all that. Well, you’re all wrong. Which is nothing new…… And there is even more in this one that I am going to walk out with. I can walk out with Rich Rollins TV Title. You know what; it would make it like 3 times now for me to be the TV Champion again. And Rich Rollins isn’t in this anymore. Rich Rollins is tired of this and ready to hang up the trunks. Rich Rollins is ready to go home and sleep and play mommy to his future children, which he will never be having if I have my say….. And there is the past in Tsunami Oni. Listen up old man. Just go home. Just go and take your Viagra and do that slut of your like there is no tomorrow. There is nothing left here for you, and if you come the only thing that will happen is you will end up being hurt. Your hip is bad, just go home and replace it now so you don’t have to worry about your doctor’s bill after I break it….. Killer Dude, please, stop right now. Accept your fate. You are a loser, and that’s all you’ll ever be. Go back and start surfing and start running around with the beach bunnies cause that’s the only wrestling that you are any good at. Just go home. It’s not worth it for you to be here. Save yourself for something greater, and that greater thing is the bottom of the ocean….. Jamie Shock, you need to go back to playing video games because it’s the only thing you can ever win. IN fact, why don’t you invite Killer Dude over? Y’all can have a sausage party with Sara and all those other people and sit there and drink and play video games and watch your fate both ways, because it ain’t going to be any different either way……. And then there is the Communist, the stupidest SOB who ever lived. Now he is beating up random people on a plane. He’s lucky that that plane didn’t go down on him. He’s such a stupid bastard that he can’t even realize that putting that much force onto a plane floor could not only destroy the floor but also send it into a tail spin. Which wouldn’t be a bad thing, I mean then we could keep Soviette….. We also have Jayson Christy or whatever the hell his name is. Wish he knew what he was doing. He claims to be the savior, but the only thing he could ever save is the stuff that comes out from between his legs before it falls into the pot. And he’s not even interested in titles anyway, so just leave, because we don’t want you here….. And there are a bunch of other putz in this thing. All of them with their wants whose dreams are going to be crushed all at once, because they are nothing. They don’t know what they are getting into….. (Mr. Meaner ducks behind a tree.) You don’t know where people are going to come from. (Meaner peeks his head out to the left.) People could come from the left….. (Meaner goes back behind the tree and sticks his head out from the right.) They could come from the right…… (Meaner sticks his head back behind the tree.) (Voice from behind) Or they could come from behind you. (The Camera spins around to see him standing there.) This match is everything that I love. This match is going to be a huge thing. I’m use to sneaking around and making a low profile and striking like a snake. Nobody else is. And this time, nobody else ever will. I’m going to win this Regal Rumble. I’m going to walk out with the TV belt. And I’m going to walk out of Live and Loud with the World Title, and there is nothing you can do about it.) (The camera follows Mr. Meaner out of the forest and watches as he gets into a limo.)
