UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #534

Big PARTAY TIME!!!

13 Jul 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — The Disco.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up with the camera focused on a Disco Ball. The ball spins around for a while and then the camera starts to pan out. As the camera pans out, it is quite obvious that the camera is in a disco, well duh. Soon, as you keep getting down, One can see Fizbane the Fabulous sitting on a chair. Face painted, as always, his staff with the beautiful jewel at his side. He is wearing a neon yellow leisure suit. He has the bling bling hanging from his neck and a matching top hat as well. Sitting to either side of him are Venus and Aphrodite. Fizzy has his arms extended around the girls necks and they are both leaning against him. Both girls are wearing dresses that go down just a little past there knees and knee high boots. Venus’ dress is a blue glittery dress and Aphrodite’s is a multi colored pattern dress.) Fizzy: Well well well, what a NIGHT it was on Sunday. Let me tell you. It was a great night for me. It was a great night for everyone all around. And after the night, it was a great morning. It was one hell of a party……. I got a couple of FIIIIINNNEE sugar plums here. I have a shot to take this party higher and higher in the world. But it’s all cool. It’s all good. It’s going to be one hell of a good time. (Venus and Aphrodite both giggle a little and straighten up, crossing there legs.) Venus: Let me tell you all something out there, this guy knows how to party. Aphrodite: Ain’t that the truth girlfriend. (They both laugh a little.) Fizzy: You girls are so good to me. Let me tell you. Venus: Yeah, and your so much more fun than our previous employer. Aphrodite: All he ever wanted is sex. I got a great libido, but lordy lord, sex can’t come all the time. Fizzy: Sex is good and all, but what’s the point if you can’t have fun. It’s the name of the game. It’s all about the party and all about the dancing, and hugging, and moving to the music. (Fizzy starts dancing with his upper body area. The girls join in.) Fizzy: Yeah they were dancing, and hugging , and moving to the music saying All three: PLAY THAT FUNKY MUSIC WHITE BOY, PLAY THAT FUNKY MUSIC RIGHT. (all three of them start laughing a bit.) Aphrodite: Hey, you know you have a match on Wednesday, right? Fizzy: Sweet. Who do I wrestle. Venus: It’s a battle royal for the number one contender ship for the TV Title. Fizzy: Sweet. We gonna have a PAAAARRRR-TAAAAY on Wednesday night. It’s going to be a night of dancing and grooving. And it’s going to be a night of showing love all around. So who are all these cool cats that are going to be in this party with me? I can tell you right now it’s going to be a fun time. Aphrodite: It’s a newbie battle royal. Lots of newbies and such. Venus: Nothing I’m sure you can’t handle. (Both girls giggle again) Fizzy: Oh yeah, I’ve seen some of these cats promos. I tell you something, they take things way to seriously. That Renegade dude, man it seems like this guy doesn’t know what it means to have fun. As he said, he left cause he wasn’t angry, and now he is angry again. Must be tough to go through life angry all the time and not enjoying the fun part of life. Or the finer parts of life. The partying and the sugarplums…… (The girls giggle again and blush a little) And then there is that communism dude. Now there is a party killer. Communism is all about being stiff and unenjoyable. They sit there and tell you when to work, when to laugh, and what to do all the time. And they sure didn’t believe life being a party. They sure didn’t believe that you could have fun. What a way to cramp a style. What a way to kill a mood….. And who else was there. Oh yeah, Sacrifice. And Fosta. Man, all these heavy dudes. Has anyone around here heard of fun. Has anyone around here ever heard about partying and having a good ol’ time? Venus: Only you. Aphrodite: You are the king of the party. Fizzy; How true how true. Well, this Wednesday, all I have to say is that we are going to have one hell of a party. And after the show on Wednesday, we are going to PAAAARRR-TAAAAYYYY ALL……. NIGHT…… LONG!!!!!! (Fizzy stands up and walks a few steps away from his chair. He stops, turns around, and offers his hand out to Venus and Aphrodite. Aphrodite and Venus both take a hand and Fizzy helps them up to there feet. All of a sudden a train whistle is heard. The song “Locomotion” starts up and the disco lights and ball go into full steam. Aphrodite, Fizzy, and Venus all turn to the left, Fizzy grabs Aphrodite’s hips, and Venus grabs Fizzy’s hips. They start to dance away to the beat of the song. The camera pans upward again to the spinning disco ball, which spins for a few moments. Finally, the scene fades to black..)
