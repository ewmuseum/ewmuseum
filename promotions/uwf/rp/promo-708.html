UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #708

Reunion

9/18 (year unrecorded)

Tsunami Oni — San Francisco Airport

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tsunami Oni is back... at the airport. But this time it's not the Devil that's flying in. Oni waits at the gate for the flight from Japan to arrive. Finally, after what seems like an eternity, the passengers disembark. Among the passengers is a well-dressed older Japanese man. Keen-eyed UWF fans would recognize him as Tsunami Oni's father. Oni: Hey, Dad. Welcome to the States. Mr. Yamada: Good to see you again so soon, son. You're here alone? Oni: Yeah, Shiatsu's been having recurring headaches since the Rumble. She's taking it easy right now. Mr. Yamada: Well, at least it will give the two of time to chat. Father and son pick up Mr. Yamada's baggage and head to the parking lot where Tsunami Oni's Bentley is. Mr. Yamada: Nice car. Oni: One of the rewards of beating the hell out of as many yutzes as I do. The pair get in the car and head towards Oni's building in the heart of San Francisco. Mr. Yamada: As much as I enjoy seeing your exploits in the ring, I was thinking you should be more of part in the family business. Oni: Really? Mr. Yamada: Yes. OmniNational Industries would be an ideal front for us. Plus you have so many connections in the States. Oni: Back to trying to take over the world? Mr. Yamada: Just pull the strings a bit. Oni: Hmmm.. let me think on it. I quit being a supervillain not that long ago. Not sure if I'm ready to become some kind of mobster. Mr. Yamamda: 'Mobster.' Such an ugly word. Oni: What would you prefer? Mr. Yamada: I think 'Businessman' would be more accurate. Oni: Uh-huh. You would. Mr. Yamada: But enough about this. I need to ask you another question. Why have you lied to me? Oni: What? Mr. Yamada: I know about what's happened with Shiatsu. With Montgomery. Tsunami Oni says nothing, just keeps his eyes on the road. Mr. Yamada: Son... Oni: Listen. I don't want to talk about this. I don't know what's happened. I don't know why she's done this to me. All I know is that somehow... this is my fault. I exposed her to this 'life.' I put her in danger. I had her wlatz around like my plaything. And know all that has come back to me. My enemies have lined up against me. My friends have diappeared. And the one person I care about more than anything is finally sick of me and all this shit I go through. An dfor what? Belts? Applause. Myabe that bastard Almasy is right. I do deserve all this. Mr. Yamada: You can't believe that. Oni: Maybe I don't know what to believe right now. Neither man speaks for the rest of the trip to Mr. Yamada's hotel. As the two stand in the lobby waiting to check in, Mr. Yamada turns to his son. Mr. Yamada: Listen, I have faith in you. You are too much of a fighter. Too damn stubborn, like your old man. This manages to get a smile out of Tsunami Oni. Mr. Yamada: You need to deal with this head on. And his Sunday is the perfect time. Show Montgomery and Almasy that you mean business. I sthis Mlacolm Kenndy up to this task? Oni: To tell the truth, I really don't know him well. He wasn't here during my first run. I know he's a definate player, but we'll have to see if I can rely on him. I guess that's why they put him as my partner. Another unknown factor in the equation. Mr. Yamada: Well, you can trust in yourself. and in me. Oni: Thanks. Thanks a lot. Hey, it's your turn. I'll let to settle in for a bit. As his father registers into the hotel, Tsunami Oni walks out of the lobby. Then he stops at the door. Oni: Hey, papa-san... Mr. Yamada: Yes? Oni: Glad you came. Mr. Yamada: Me too, son. Me too.
