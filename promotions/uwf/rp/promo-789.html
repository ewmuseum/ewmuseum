UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #789

What is Clyde on?

24 Nov 2004

Adien Pryde — Locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the locker room of The Chaos Society. Adien Pryde, Reo Dai, and Joanna are watching Clyde’s latest promo on a TV monitor.] “As the air whips past, as the ground races to reach me, no longer do I seek my death with this little hobby of mine. Rather, I revel in it, Adien! I revel in the fact that nothing may stop me, when I do not wish to be stopped. Not gravity, nor useless god, nor you! As once again, I reach the ground and that which does not kill me makes me ever stronger, I smile. As my bones break upon impact, I still manage a laugh. Adien, my brother. Adien, my soulmate. Adien, my victim.....pride truly does come before the fall. “ [Dai turns off the TV.] Pryde : What the fuck is wrong with this guy? He must be on some really good drugs to be acting this badly. Joanna : Right to Party wasn’t even this bad. Pryde : Whatever he is on should affect his performance. It already has shriveled up his balls the way he lets that girl push him around. Joanna : Should be no problem for you. Pryde : I would hope not. Just another Kane wannabe. Take away the foreign objects and he will fall to my superior mat wrestling and with my grapples, I should be able to go toe to toe with him. Dai : You’ve taken wannabe like him many times before. But now you have us in The Chaos Society to back you if you need it. Pryde : Only reason I would need you guy is to make sure those losers that hang around him don’t interfere with me putting Clyde out of his misery once and for all. You say that my prayers to my deity fall on deaf ears. You may be right because… [Pryde pulls out a wad of $100.00 bills from his front pocket and fans them out in front of the camera.] Pryde : …you see, I don’t think that my god has ears, cold hard cash is my deity and it always gets me what I want just like it has gotten me to where I am today, ready to be the next breakout star in the UWF. Get past you and I am on my way to the Contest of Champions, the launching pad to the World Title match. [Pryde stuff the money back into his pocket then motions for Reo Dai and Joanna to leave him along. After they leave, Pryde turns his attention back to the camera.] Pryde : Clyde, you talk about the pain and suffering you have been through and you enjoy putting your opponents in. At Wednesday Night Universal, I intend to end it all for you and put you down and out for good. No man deserves to be in the misery you are in and after out match you will not feel anything anymore. [Pryde turns and switches the TV back on and begins to rewind the tape.] Pryde : I hope you bring you A Game Clyde. I would not want anything less. [Pryde pushes play on the VCR as the Scene fades to black.]
