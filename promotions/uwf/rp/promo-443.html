UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #443

Dead Turtles and Communist

9 Jun 2004

Adien Pryde — New York City

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to on a New York street. It is night time and very few people can be seen except three figures, a 5’6” woman, a 5’10” man and a 7’ monster. They are the Chaos Society, or at least three of the five members. All are wearing the Chaos Society/Anarchy colors of black and red.] Draco Paladine : So here we are, New York, New York. The Big Apple. Why in the hell are we here anyways? I want to take it to those Iron Curtain losers and go to Russia or where ever that ass Takamichi Mori is from. After what they did to you Pryde just to introduce their new member is ballsy, something we would and have done. Pryde : What’s done is done and we came out ahead in the deal. [Pryde pulls back his black trench and reviles around his waist the UWF Television Title.] Pryde : This baby right here, the TV Title. Two titles in two weeks, I would have to say that the Chaos Society is on a roll, and after we win Stable Wars we will have our pick of the rest to go after. Draco : Sunday, Raid and I will soften up the Iron Curtain for whoever they face in the opening round. I know you and Dai are the tag team specialist, but Raid and I will destroy them. The Communist loses his size and strength advantage to me. He hasn’t face another 7’ monster like me, and that little Asian man will get squashed by Raid. Two less communist in the world the better. Pryde : Ah, here we are. [Pryde motions to the ground.] Pryde : The manhole is where the UWF office list as the residence for El Turtlé. El Turtlé, I am calling you out. I don’t want to wait till Sunday to kick your ass, hell, I’ll take on you four brothers to. Joanna : Are you OK, did you take to many blows to the head in your feud with Nick Persia? Pryde : No, I am just giving the UWF fans what they want, turtle soup. [Pryde pulls out a can from his coat with the label “Turtle Poison”.] Pryde : I got this from out own “Evil Exterminator”, Raid. He guarantees to work with one dose. Drac, could you lift off the cover. Draco : No problem. [Draco lifts off and holds it while Pryde walks over to the hole.] Pryde : Sleep good El Turtlé, since you will never wake up. [With that, Pryde pulls the pin on the can and lets it drop as smoke begins to billow from the can. Drac quickly replaces the cover.] Draco : That turtle is getting off easy, Iron Curtain, your ass is mine at Supremacy, and I don’t intend to let you live to see Stable Wars. [The trio begins to walk away as the camera fades to black.]
