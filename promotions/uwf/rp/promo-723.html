Hey, What Smells?

30 Sep 2004

The Communist — A TV Cooking Show

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The scene opens with some cheesey, upbeat music and a sign, 'Cooking With the Communist.' The camera pans down to show the Communist wearing a tall chefs hat with the scythe and hammer on it and an apron that says 'Kiss the Commie' behind a table covered in various foods and cooking utensels. The Communist raises his arms in welcome as the camera zooms in on him: Commie: Welcome Comrads, to Cooking With the Communist! We put the Sauce back in Socialism! Sence this is our first show, we shall be expirementing with some different dishes. Lets start, Shall we? :The Communist pulls up a slab of beef, tossing it on the cutting board. He pulls a rather large knife out, chopping chunks off of the slab: Commie: First we will be having steak! You can't have a good hearty meal without meat. First we will season the chump... I mean the beef with my personal blend, Bitter Defeat. Some lemon and salt to pour into wombs, and some pepper for flavor, and a secret ingredient that only grows in Russia, Native born Whup ass. It's very rare and grows with the sweat and the blood of the working class. Lets add it, shall we? :The Communist shakes on a liberal amount of Bitter Defeat all over the cut slab, and works it in with his fingers: Commie: Next we need to tenderize. :He picks up a small tenderizing mallet: With a job this big, small weapons... I mean tools will never do, it'd take us forever to tenderize all of this. What we need it a bigger hammer. :The Communist picks up his 16lb sledge and beats the slab of beef, leaving cracks in the table, water squirting from broken pipes, tile flying everywhere. He sets the hammer down, smiling at his handywork: Commie: We need to go to a break now. After the capitalist pigs pawn their overprices wears on you, we'll toss our beef in the oven and start on the side dish, We'll be back! :The Camera pans out and the sign, 'Cooking with the Communist' pops up as the scene fades to black. A commercial for the Communist party, and one for a food processor, then we fade back into show, the 'Cooking With the Communist' Sign back up, and the cammera zooms up to The Comminist, the table fixed: Commie: We're back! :he picks up the slab of beef, now looking more like a meat pancake: We're going to toss this in the over and get started on some vegitables. :The Communist tosses the slab on a pan and tosses it in the preheated oven, letting it cook. He takes a head of cabbage and some broccolie and sets them on the table: Commie: Remember, eat healty, green vegitables, not cowardy, yellow ones, like bannana pepers. Only foolish american pigs eat those, right, Mr Meaner? We will be steaming these, to remove all the filth from them that the bourguasis laden them with. But first, we need to chop them up! :The Communist pulls up a crowbar: I don't have any knives that will do the job better than this, it's simple, effective and Cheep. :Commie beats the vegitables into submission, turning them into pulp and tosses them into a pan.: Commie: What? That's all the time we have! I haven't even finished yet! YOU CAPITALIST PIGS! NOT ENOUGH MONEY! :The cammera pans up to the sound of crowbar on skull and the playful opening music as the 'Cooking with the Communist' Sign fades onto the screen and we fade out:
