UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #656

WNU#1 vs McClancy Sean

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A figure stands seemingly alone in his small midtown apartment, stood silently at the window gazing out. Summer was drawing to a close and the party atmosphere around the town was slowly dying, children were coming in from the street earlier, tucked up in their beds that much earlier as the darkness began to engulf the town that little bit earlier. Barbeques, that scented the night air were becoming fewer and fewer. It was the time of year when things did not seem as pleasant as the summer that had just passed, the leaves on the trees were becoming brittle, stale almost as the dull autumn zapped their beautiful green, draining them to a sombre orange, until they gently day and fall to the ground. A single leaf lying amongst its fallen comrades. The figures eyes seem to be gazing out across the horizon through the pane of glass, trying to find something, the nights were a lot darker than they used to be though and it’s a lot harder to see in the dark. “In the beginning the was nothing but darkness and silence, for some that image may prove to be disturbing, they would focus on the fact that it would be completely devoid of life. To myself, it would be bliss. People fear the dark, scared that it may consume them, it consumes us all at sometime but we always survive, it’s our nature.” The voice emanates from the figure that remains silent and steadfast at the window, he slowly slides his hands out of his pockets and wraps them around his own waist, trying to grant himself a sense of comfort and security. From behind the man, the light sound of footsteps can be heard. Though the light in the room is minimal, the outline of a slim woman can briefly be made out, as she steps forwards slowly towards the man. “I understand that John, but I still don’t understand what you are saying. Do you honestly believe that the dark is something that can be a place of bliss, what has happened to you? Five years ago, you were so full of life and energy, you saved me. Yet now you have found yourself, the energy has been drained from you.” The voice from the woman was soft, caring and full of compassion. At the window John did not move, he simply continued to stare forwards, not turning his head to acknowledge the woman “As a race, our primary goals are to pass on our genes, so that mankind shall continue to exist, when it is taken down to base levels that’s all we have, everything else in life is just a bonus, the sunset in the evening, the first flake of snow in winter its all just a pleasant background for the inevitability that we suffer, must suffer.” The woman takes a few tentative steps forwards before wrapping her arms slowly, almost cautiously around the waist of John. Although it may look like a scene the body language is all wrong. The woman’s body is not pressed against John’s, the arms hanging loose, almost limp just enough for them to be acknowledged that they are there. “John, I understand that it has been a long time, too long. If I had known how I felt, if I had known that there was the slightest possibility that you would take me back, I would have come back. You disappeared John, after n4o you just went away, you were never heard of again. It took me a year to track you down after I finally realised you weren’t dead!” The woman’s grip has tightened around John’s waist now, a woman in desperation clinging on to him, with every ounce of strength left in her. He had left her once, choosing not to say goodbye, it would have been too hard. The effects of a sordid love triangle, which not only tore apart relationships, but also tore into the very existence of his old company, could he really allow himself to open his heart to that risk again? John squirmed out of the woman’s grip and turned to face her. Her deep brown eyes barely visible from the small amount of moonlight that had begun to filter into the room. Her name was Louise. They had met five years ago when love drew them together. Her love for another man had torn them apart. A lot had happened in five years and John couldn’t help by smile slightly to himself at her last words. He could well have been dead; after all he had tried to kill himself. No matter how much he tried to deny it, he had died a little that day he woke up in intensive care, when someone attempts to kill themselves they mean it. People who are looking for sympathy or a bit of attention in their lives, those are the ones that leave notes, they’re the ones that threaten it. To be pushed to a true state of despair means you won’t bother letting anyone else know. It will just come upon you, to end it all. “I nearly died.” The slowly seeped out of John’s mouth like water dripping from a tap. Each word was cold, harsh and bitter, the resentment not being shown towards Louise, more himself. Louise took a short step backwards, clasping a hand over her mouth, she knew that John was someone that wouldn’t joke about something like this, when coupled with the tone of his voice, Louise knew instantly that she had missed a large part of something. “I went through a patch where I didn’t care, it was as though I was here yet I was unable to feel anything. The world would sour past me, everyone focusing on his or her own worries, the way it should be. I was never one for sympathies. Yet I couldn’t feel, so I made myself feel.” Slowly John rolls the sleeves of his sweatshirt up to show the extent of his past, the scars, which burden his everyday existence. Louise gazes on unable to pull herself away from the numerous blade marks that lie across his wrists and arms. It is at that moment he rolls his arms over to show the truth to Louise, she had been truthful with him when she arrived on his doorstep before the Regal Rumble; he now had to do the same. The moon had risen higher into the darkness, its silver aura reflecting through the windows illuminating John’s arms. The words REAL and SOON carved deeply into the flesh, scarring him for eternity. Tentatively Louise extends her arm and slowly uncurls her fingers, moving them towards the scarred skin. She looks up at John, a single tear is flowing down her cheek as she fights to control her emotions, running her finger over the words, tracing them attempting to figure out the true meaning behind them. “Two weeks before my suicide attempt” The words shot from John’s mouth before Louise had a chance to ask the question he knew was coming, he wanted to avoid the whole circle that would involve her asking why he would do such a thing, how it occurred. He had told this story too often, yet he was always drawn back to it, he couldn’t let it go. “I have one more thing that I have to show you.” Louise nodded as her bottom lip quivered, there was fear in her eyes, afraid of what John was going to reveal, deep down she knew that there would be a scar, a symbol of when John had given up on life and chosen to end it. It’s not until you see a scar of that magnitude that you realise how graphic life can truly be. John slowly crossed his arms, grabbing the bottom of his sweatshirt, pulling it over his head to reveal his bare chest. For a moment there was silence as Louise’s eyes gazed upon the now healed wound where, John had plunged a knife into himself, where he had felt the blood slowly trickle over this knuckles before staining the grass on the cliff top. The light from the moon was temporarily blocked out by cloud plunging the room into darkness. Through the darkness the two continued to gaze at each other, neither one knowing what to do next, both knowing what they wanted to do though. “I can’t change the past Louise” John spoke, his words no longer bitter, but carried a faint glimmer of hope, that for once something might go right, he stepped forwards and softly but firmly put his hands on Louise’s waist, never pulling his gaze away from hers. “I know you can’t John, neither can I” Louise’s voice barely more than a hushed whisper as she placed her hands upon John’s chest staring up at John before leaning up and kissing him passionately, a kiss that John had longed for, for five long years. The past is something that will always be there. Whether it is the events, which led the world to exist, or events, which have shaped our destiny and led us to where we are. So why do we live in a society where words and phrases such as ‘What if?’ and ‘If only’ are paramount to the ideology, people longing and hoping for the chance to change one significant event, in order to better themselves. It has been said many times that things happen for a reason. No matter how many times people argue it, it’s true. Life isn’t something that is predetermined and we learn from our experiences. The past can’t be changed; all that we can take from the past is to learn from it. Taking John’s hand Louise led him away from the window, making sure to clasp his hand tightly. She couldn’t help but feel guilty that John had gone through all of the pain, all of the suffering on his own. She hadn’t been there when he needed her, but she wasn’t about to let that happen again. For John, he was going to have to take things one-step at a time. No matter what he saw when he looked to the future, his arms, his chest and his mind was constant reminder of the past. Louise’s return was a welcome one, no doubt about that. However in the back of John’s mind were doubts, which would never go away, a chapter, which he thought, was closed had now been reopened. The consequences of which were unknown. That evening was not for doubts or worries, it was to not focus on his upcoming bout with Sean McClancy. The evening was simply for the two of them to make up for lost time, to rekindle the flame that they once had, with complete disregard for anything else. Tonight was a night when the dark didn’t matter and the past met the future.
