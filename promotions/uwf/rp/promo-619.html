Let's get it started

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with the name RUFF RYDER in blood red letters flashing up on a black screen. The screen fades and the image is replaced by that of the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder. He is wearing a red t-shirt with the words 'Cherokee Thrilla' in black letters and his picture underneath. We can only see him from the waist up but there is no doubt he has on black wind pants and he's also wearing a black Kansas City Chiefs hat tipped and turned to one side. He is giving his usual cocky grin as the camera zooms in to record his words. ) Ryder: Hello there wrestling fans. I'm guessing you all know who I am. Everyone around here knows the True Indian Superstar, even little punks like this new guy Jason Christy. I don't know who you think you're tryin to impress by takin verbal shots at the Cherokee Thrilla, but you might want to go over a few tapes of my matches before you start runnin your mouth boy. Regal Rumble or not, I will get ahold of you and when I do you'll really wish you hadn't opened your mouth, because i'll rip off your jaw and stick it somewhere very uncomfortable. Now I get that you're the new guy and you're tryin to make a name for yourself, but a little tip for ya, don't jump in the deep end of the pool if you can't swim. * Ryder fixes his hat and checks his face as if he's lookin in a mirror obviously showing the rookie Christy that he's not worried at all about him before switching topics * Ryder: Now that I got that outta the way i'd like to turn my attention to The Plague. My poor diseased little buddy, you know there was nothin personal about my attack. It was just me being a friend, and had you responded to my many attempts to be your friend you might not have had to worry about me attacking you. See when I consider a person my friend then that means i'm loyal to that friendship, but you up and left me here without so much as a goodbye so I took that as you not wanting to associate with me. That's fine with me, but just remember if you're not with me, you're against me so if you want revenge at the Regal Rumble then you just bring it son. You do what you gotta do, but remember that i'll do what I gotta do as well. Just so you know though, I will be there when you come in at number 60 and i'll be more than happy to toss your infested ass right over the top ropes. Don't get comfortable in the ring, cause you won't be there for long my friend. * Ryder gives a half smile before getting serious about his last subject of the promo * Ryder: Now as for my friend Jamie Shock. I am truely sorry that I caused you to enter number one, but that doesn't mean i'm gonna let you past me in the Rumble. If it comes down to it i'll throw your big ass over the ropes right along with The Plague. Make no mistake about it i'm a competitor first and friendships stop once I step through the ropes. I think the world of ya homie, but nobody is gonna stop me from fightin my way to the top and getting into that Live and Loud main event. It's been too long without Ruff Ryder causing some trouble at the top of the ranks and i'm using the Regal Rumble as my catapult to the top. I'll hop right over the top of ya Jamie, but at the end of the day when it's all said and done...it's nothin personal, just business. ( Ryder smiles as the image fades and is replaced with an image of Ruff Ryder holding the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. )
