Dunn and more Dunn...CORPORAL STYLE

date unknown

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(When last we spoke, the Corporal was busy learning what a video player was. Whether he has figured that out remains to be seen. But what we do know is that there is one man by the name of Joshua Dunn who thinks he has what it takes to be a Corporal Killer. This is absurd we know, however there will always be those who think that they have what it takes to take out the meanest son of a gun in wrestling. But just as Urban Legends sound to good to be true, so are the ill dreamed thoughts of wrestlers just like Joshua Dunn. When last we spoke, the Corporal was addressing some issues that were brought up by his opponent for this weekend. It really wasn't the fact that Joshua Dunn has the mindset in place to have a very unproductive showing against the Corporal. It wasn't the lack luster ambition that Dunn shows towards the Corporal. But it was the fact that he claimed that the Corporal already knew how to work something called a video player. What is a video player? We have heard of a DVD player, a VCR, a film reel player, and even an MP3 player, but just a video player... I still have not a clue. But I am sure that the Corporal now knows. We will find out shortly. In the world of wrestling, many things can happen. And what better ways for things to happen than when an opponent the Corporal is about face, does not even take time to realize that statements the Corporal has quoted in his previous promotion were the words of the freaking OPONENT he is facing! What a shame it is for a training camp to refer to their own comments as being '...a whole heap of buzz words and clips of someone else's promotion... ' Oh no! there the Corporal goes again! As Joshua Dunn's team of trainers would say. But the fact still remains that Dunn and Dunner did in fact make those comments, and should they not be held responsible for their ignorance? The vast majority of wrestling enthusiasts would agree that if the training camp of Joshua Dunn can not handle their failings in an adversaries promotion, they should get out of this business entirely. It simply goes to show you how youth is not more superior than age. It just proves that the youth of today are that much more ignorant than the youth of yesterday. Ah the days of being ignorant boys and girls. The days when wrestlers start attempting to mimic the greatness of Corporal Punishment. Have you seen the similarities in the recent Dunn promotion? Have you noticed how those who attempt to belittle such a great athlete as Corporal Punishment, that they have to copy his unique style of entertainment? It is truly an honor to see those who are not great in wrestling, consider the path that the Corporal has taken to become an icon. It truly is. However, do they not know that they can not beat the Corporal at his own game unless they can surpass the talent of the Corporal. It is obvious to see, this enemy of Corporal Punishment does not have the ability to do this. He just opens up more weaknesses for the Corporal to pounce upon. For instance can the Corporal TRAWL through some of the history of the UWF? What is a trawl? We here at Corporal TV have yet to see such a place as TRAWL. Yet the Dunn team seems to believe this place exists. They have recently said, 'Perhaps if the Corporal, the man who can work a computer but not a video player, were to trawl through some of the history of the UWF ' Now we know that a trawl is a fishing net, but what in the world does this have to do with anything? OH no the Corporal has done it again! He has placed yet another HEAP of buzz words from someone else's promotion! DEAR LORD WHERE will it end! Oh my God! the Sky is falling! Someone give the Dunn team of trainers a hanky to snot upon! They will never stand for this! NEVER! Does the Corporal do his homework? Does he care where Dunn has been in the UWF? Why should he? Dunn has yet to realize that he is about to face not just a person who begs for attention as the longest running IC champion in the UWF... like Dunn has. But he is facing a man who does not realize that the Corporal has HELD the GIWF IC title three times and was the man who held the GIWF IC title MORE than any other wrestler in the history of the GIWF. So why should the Corporal make an issue of Dunn holding a title when no true competition existed in the UWF? Can someone answer this question? The Corporal would never pride himself in admiring a title that has no credibility? Was it not Digital M who defeated Dunn in an IC title match? He allowed someone who has never been able to defeat the Corporal in anything, cause him to lose that little title belt. Should that make the Corporal fear Joshua Dunn? Or maybe we should look at the North American Title history Mr. Dunn. Who holds the most title reigns in that belts history? was it Dunn? Nope.... and why not, because he will never be as great as the Corporal of Punishment. The Corporal has held that title three times as well. Should that be advertised in order to make Dunn look greater than he really is? I didn't think so either. So what it is that the Dunn&Dunner team is really proud of. Is it that there prized wrestler has held the IC title in the UWF the longest... and that is it? Come on boys and girls, we want a real man with real accomplishments. Not a boy who thinks he deserves credibility because he knows how to duck true competition. Dunn is such a veteran that he does not even understand wrestling terminology. Why he does not even know that the term FACE stands for a good guy. Bwahahahahhaa! He thinks the Corporal was calling him a NEW FACE JOBBER! A jobber he may be, but a FACE he is not. What a pity for this nimrod of anal proportions to make such a claim. But that is clearly why he still has the mind of a JOBBER none the less. And although he claims to have been around the block a few times, we do not need to bring up his former career as a male prostitute to the table. What he did before entering the squared circle is off limits as far as the Punishment Camp is concerned. We know that this is a family show and we would never go that far to degrade a fellow wrestler. ... Dunn is a fellow right? ... Ah, we can remember the days together boys and girls. Can we not? Those days when wrestlers knew how to truly confront their adversaries. The days when a promotion was more than a ten second production. Those days are gone. The lack of creativity has left the youth of today. They are boring because they have no vision. But as you will see shortly, there is still one man alive who is more creative than this entire fiasco of the Dunn Team. Camp Punishment has always been proud to bring a solid product to the people of wrestling. And they will continue to do so. Even if this weeks opponent has no creativity to be able to read between the lines. That is where the Corporal shall strike... Or will he? Who knows for sure except the man who dares to step up and be separated from these clowns of wrestling. Is dunn the poster boy for boredom? Will he learn to watch his own material and not get himself confused with someone else? These questions and more will be answered in the next few moments. Because the former THREE time IC champion, and former 3 time NA champion in wrestling is about to enter your presence in a way that will be unique as always. And we want you to be ready for his appearance as well. So without any further delays, we proudly present to you Corporal Punishment... CORPORAL STYLE *The scene opens up outside 235 Crawford Street. A window is open and the sounds of someone yelling in anger can be heard. *In just a mere seconds the camera observes a black box hurling out of that open window. The sound of Corporal Punishment can be heard briefly... Corporal: SCREW THIS!!! THIS THING IS IMPOSSIBLE! *The box lands near a land mine that was placed directly beside a rusty trash can. *the camera quickly fades to black and then returns inside the house to witness the Corporal ripping up a pamphlet that reads 'Video recorder directions'. He throws the debris everywhere as he stomps throughout the house. Finally after a few moments have went by, the Corporal relaxes from the difficult task of learning how to work a video player. Corporal: That stupid piece of equipment reminded me of a Studog Escobar invention. The only thing they were good for was taking up space. And I do not have time to watch a video off of a player that has no clear directions on how to play videos! There were no pictures either! *The Corporal slams his fist down on the coffee table as he shows his frustrations again. Corporal: ...And that stupid Dunn&Dunner idiots made the claim that I knew how to use one of those boxes. Corporal: It goes to show you what they know. Of course I should have known better than to even think the kid knows what he talks about. After all, his only claim to fame is running away from the competition to be deemed as the longest IC champion in the UWF. Anyone could do that. Unlike me, who took every title I have held in the GIWF and the UWF, and placed my title on the line each and every week. That is what a true warrior does. They don't cower in the covers and only throw out a defense when it is mandatory. That is just cheap. *The Corporal pauses for a moment and then dashes outside of the house. *the camera follows the Corporal as he walks up on the porch of his neighbors house, and begins to beat on the door with his fist. Corporal: MISSES SMITH!!!! HELLO THERE!!! ARE YOU HERE!!!! MRS. SMITH!! IT IS YOUR LOVING NEIGHBOR CORPORAL PUNISHMENT! HELLO!!!!! *Within a moment, the sound of a timid old woman can be heard whispering on the other side of the door. Mrs. Smith: Please leave me alone. Corporal: Come on now Grand Maw. It is me. You know you want to talk to me. Now open the door! Mrs. Smith: If you do not leave me alone, I will call the police. Now please leave me alone. I am an old woman. I can not handle this. Corporal: Look old woman. What happened the last time you called the police huh? And how is that old fart doing in the county jail. I heard that he is learning a new game of checkers in the slammer. One that causes him to grease up his back end if he loses. Would you like for me to make a call for him at the jail? Would you like to see him with or without lipstick on? You know I can arrange that to happen. Corporal: So come on Mrs. Smith. Let me speak to you for a moment. I promise I will not harm you or your husband again. In fact, how would you like to take your husband to Florida to retire? I can make that happen you know. So can we talk for a mere moment? *There is a brief pause at the door. ... *then the sounds of locks being opened are heard. Within a few seconds of this, the gray headed elderly woman slowly opens the door. Mrs. Smith: What do you want? Corporal: I was needing some assistance with a video player. Do you know what that is? Mrs. Smith: I have never heard of one. Corporal: Well I have a deal for you. You figure it out and I will send you and your husband to Florida to live forever. I promise my life on it. Can you do this? Mrs. Smith: Why I guess I could... Corporal: EXCELLENT! It is over there beside that trash can. You get it and figure it out, and I will carry out my promise to you. *The Corporal smiles at her and then he takes a sarcastic bow. He turns around and returns to his house and then walks downstairs to the basement where the sight of dried up blood is everywhere. Corporal: Man, Mr. Smith sure did make a mess of things down here. It does though, look lived in. That is a nice touch... the blood and all. *The Corporal prepares to begin his workout with a heavy bag that was stashed back in the far corner of the basement. He starts off with small jabs and uppercuts. Corporal: I never have understood why people don't keep things simple. Everyday somewhere, there is a person who goes out and buys something to make their life easier. Somehow, they think that modern technology equals success. Take for instance this heavy bag... *The Corporal begins to throw a few more body shots it's way. Only he picks up the power in each of his punches. Corporal: A long time ago, this bag was probably considered a new fad. I can almost imagine the excitement it gave off to people who thought that this thing was top of the line boxing equipment. I am sure everyone saw this bag and said, 'Man I wish I had one of those!' Or maybe they said something like this, 'Look at what I just bought fella's! This is a top of the line heavy bag!'. Corporal: You know that it happened that way. At least in some part of the world when this bag first came out. Corporal: But now look at it. It is down in my basement being beat on by the likes of me. Nobody shows any interest in it any more. It still meets it's purpose. It still does it's job. It just does not have any excitement about it any more. That is how people are now a days. Yet, they over look the bag because it appears to be out of date. *The Corporal stops throwing punches on the heavy bag for a moment. He grabs it and re-centers its position before continuing. *He begins to throw even stronger punches towards the old ran down heavy bag. Corporal: Why I bet my opponent this week is doing his training on something more modern than this heavy bag. It might be a Nordic track, or even a tread mill. Why with a click of a button, it will do things electronically that supposedly speeds up the process. This I guess, allows the user to perform his or her task that much faster. Corporal: Sometimes modern technology causes one to be lazy. They want speed over time consuming tasks. All in the name of convenience. Instead of getting the fresh air by walking or running, they jump on a tread mill. Corporal: Instead of doing things the old fashioned yet proven way, they go for the more modern and politically correct way. Why I bet that the majority of people would sell their soul for a piece of modern technology. That is how things are today...PERIOD. *The Corporal now begins to throw power punches onto the heavy bag with force. Each punch causes the house to shake. But the Corporal does not bat an eye as he lands one power shot after another. Corporal: It must be nice to be politically correct. Really nice. I did not have the pleasure of being spoon fed. I had to learn things from the streets. From the military, and from prison. My ways are not the most glamorous.... but they are still my ways. And as long as I am in control of myself, I will never change the way I am. Corporal: Joshua Dunn is about to see why I am so brutal. He is going to find out why they call me the enforcer. Corporal: He wants to think that youth surpasses wisdom. Well let's see what happens when he gets hit with the LETHAL INJECTION! That is where the modern way of doing things meet the old fashioned way of doing things! *The anger in his voice shows his utter frustration at being middle aged. He slams his fists deep into the heavy bag and after several power shots the bag begins to wear down and tear. Corporal: I guess everything has to come to an end someday... ... ... Corporal: Too bad Joshua's day is Sunday! *Out of nowhere, a giant explosion is heard outside of the house. This surprises the Corporal as he ducks underneath the bag for a moment. Standing up after the sound ends. The Corporal quickly runs up the stairs and outside. *Once outside, he observes the rusty trash can rolling down the street. There are black particles everywhere. And then the Corporal sees one of the elderly woman's shoes lying beside of his porch. Corporal: Too bad her end was today. I guess she got a trip to the moon instead of Florida. I need to remind myself to remove some of those mines in the yard. I would hate for someone to step on one of them and get hurt... corporal style!
