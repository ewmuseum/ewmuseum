UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #697

Remembrance

Sept. 11, 2004 (year unrecorded)

Misfit — unknown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Misfit has been silent for some weeks now since battling Kenny Mercury at Terror in Tokyo. He emerged victorious however he was far from unscathed. Cut, bruised, and broken, Misfit limped out to the arena in Tokyo a second time to compete in the Regal Rumble. It was there that Kenny Mercury was able to extract some small amount of revenge by eliminating the King of Pain, but things there are still far from over. Now, Misfit steps into the ring against Ruff Ryder who is a former near-everything in the UWF. Ryder says that the Intercontential champion is beneath him, a gross under estimation by the man who calls himself the Cherokee Thriller. Time and time again he's risen and fallen while Misfit stays relatively constant. Misfit raises the bar for himself night after night without faltering; the fans' own personal thriller. He's gone through everything. He's had his up & downs, highs & lows, triumphs & tragedies. He's been a champion & a challenger, a winner & a loser, an outcast & a martyr. He's mutated & grown far beyond what he was supposed to be; more than anyone ever thought he would be. It's his time. [We fade in on Misfit, standing beneath a red spotlight in an otherwise black room. His flesh still shows fresh cuts, scrapes, and bruises from his epic battle with Kenny Mercury at Terror in Tokyo. The low static hums in the background, audible behind Misfit's heavy breaths.] Misfit: I remember you... I remember our battles... our triumphs and tragedies. Everything over one of these... [Misfit lifts up his Intercontential championship belt briefly.] Misfit: The Television championship; a showcase for the people. We've both moved on to much more presitigous things since then. You've even become a World champion, and I... [Misfit grins.] Misfit: I've been a martyr for my trade; for this entire business. I've stood against Lew Spectre and Sam Gordon... against Kenny Mercury and the shell that Ben Jericho became. I've kept on as myself and earned everything I've got; I shed my blood, sweat, and tears for this business. And you... [Misfit twitches.] Misfit: You've played into every system that presented itself to you. The PWW, the UWF management, even the Knightmare Brigade. Do you crave to be accepted that badly, or do you think that you're using the system to your advantage? Do you really believe that the system would accept you knowing that you planned on using it to get ahead? Of course not. The systems accepted you, knowing that they were in control. [The static in the background switches, as if scanning through radio channels, before settling on a low hiss.] Misfit: You don't understand this, Ryder. You only want to succeed; you want glory and fame. That's not what this business is about. At least, it shouldn't be. I've given everything to the fans... [More channel surfing before the static comes to a loud outburst, almost as if the static is cheering for Misfit.] Misfit: You've sinned against us, Ryder. This Sunday, you will come to atone, and you will atone by my hand. [Misfit brushes his blue-tipped hair out of his eyes, the static surfing back to what it was in the beginning.] Misfit: It's time. Time to unleash my vengence and fury. Time to reclaim my mantle as the King of Pain and step up again to my rightful place. I carry the cross of wrestling on my shoulders, martyring myself for what I believe in. I'm going to open your eyes; the eyes of everyone... and it starts with Ruff Ryder. You'll see... you'll all see. [The scene fades.]
