Pete the PG Tips Chimp — Round 1

date unknown

Pete the PG Tips Chimp

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was on a quest. I knew he was out there … somewhere … and by George, I was going to find him. [In a dark, dirty alley, our protagonist stands, a shadow barely visible in so many other shadows. His face is hidden by the large, black fedora that hangs at an angle … his body hidden by the far-too-large overcoat draped around his small frame.] He’s out there. Pete the PG Tips Chimp. I’ve never met him, but I know I have to. It is my destiny to find him … to help him … to save him! [The vague attempt at a serious opening dies abruptly as a couple walks by the alley way, blowing past our target, and showing our protagonist for the three-foot child that he is. He tips his fedora back, showing a face rocked by anger, worry, and only the slightest hint of puberty.] Mine name is Timmy. Little Timmy, they call me. Once upon a time, my father told me a story … a story of a chimpanzee. A chimpanzee that bites testicles. I don’t know how long ago that was – days, surely – but nothing will stop me now. I will find Pete. I must find Pete. [Reaching into his overcoat, Little Timmy pulls out his first piece of evidence … his best hope at discovering where the almost-mythical former wrestling champion had disappeared to. He pulls out … The Complete Adventures of Curious George.] I’ve been waiting here for at least an hour … I had been certain that Pete would come riding by me on a bicycle, wearing a hilariously oversized suit and a spaceman’s helmet any minute now, but alas, it was beginning to look like literature had lied to be once again. Lied! Just like my father. Lies, lies, always lies … lies had driven me on this adventure, an adventure that could only be finished when Pete had been unleashed on two hundred and [Little Timmy rubs his chin.] I’m not sure I understand, but apparently putting on this fedora and giving myself an inner narrative has allowed me to expound my hidden feelings in long, drawn out soliloquies, and just words I’ve never learned. Odd, but this can not hold me back from my mission. [Shaking his head, Timmy tucks his book back within his overcoat, and pulls his fedora back down.] Pete is out there somewhere. Waiting for him to ride by me on a big bicycle with an electric guitar on his back might have been a good plan, but it’s time to accept that it just won’t happen. Plus, dinner is in two hours, and it’s spaghetti night. My time is running out, it’s time to use Plan B. It’s time to ask … the man in the big yellow hat. [Timmy throws out his arm and spins around, trying to make his overcoat throw outwards, but it was already dragging on the floor anyway, and being stuck in gum doesn’t give you the cool whip-around effect. Timmy shrugs and wanders off.] ------------------------------------- [Roughly forty five seconds later, Little Timmy is standing on a sidewalk, his cute widdle head looking upwards at a tall man wearing a hideous yellow beret. Timmy says nothing, and the little man looks down at him uncomfortably a few times while waiting for the light to change to ‘Walk.’] I had found him! Here he was, the Man in the Yellow Hat … if anyone would know where my hero was, it would be him. [Timmy yanks on the man’s jacket twice, getting his attention.] Little Timmy: “Where’s Pete?” Man in the Yellow Hat: “What?” Little Timmy: “Pete. Give me Pete.” Man in the Yellow Hat: “Shouldn’t you be in school, kid? Why are …” Little Timmy: “I want Pete! Give me Pete!!!!!” Man in the Yellow Hat: “What are you …” This was getting us nowhere. Obviously he wouldn’t talk. Drastic measures had to be taken. [Little Timmy headbutts the Man in the Yellow Hat in the nuts. A woman nearby gasps, but Timmy is a boy on a mission. Once the man has finished crying on the floor, Timmy grabs him by the ankle and begins dragging him into the alleyway. Being eleven and all, it doesn’t happen very quickly. In fact, let’s do one of these …] ------------------------------------- [Five minutes later, the Man in the Yellow Hat is handcuffed to a dumpster. Little Timmy eyes him angrily, his eyes blazing balls under the rim of his fedora. As the Man in the Yellow Hat finally regains consciousness, he begins shaking.] Man in the Yellow Hat: “What in the … ?!” Little Timmy: “If you want to live, you’ll tell me where Pete is!!!” Man in the Yellow Hat: “You’re fucking psychotic!!!!” [Little Timmy holds out a jar.] Little Timmy: “Cussing is naughty. That’ll be a quarter, mister.” Man in the Yellow Hat: “You have to be …” Little Timmy: “QUARTER!!!!” Man in the Yellow Hat: “In my right pocket!!!” [Fishing out a quarter, Timmy drops it into the jar and sets it back down. He goes back to eyeing his captive angrily.] Little Timmy: “I’m sorry about this, but I’m desperate, Mr. Yellow Hat. Dinner time is in an hour and fifty minutes. I’m racing the clock. Please … just tell me where Pete is!” Man in the Yellow Hat: “Why haven’t your parents given you Ritalin? WHY!?” Oh, he was going to be a tough cookie to crack, yes indeed. But I would have my answers. Little Timmy: “You’re making me angry, Mr. Yellow Hat. You wouldn’t like me when I’m angry …” [And just like that, Timmy brought out … the butter knife.] Little Timmy: “Tell me what I want to know! Pete! Where?!?” Man in the Yellow Hat: “I don’t know any Pete!!!!!” Little Timmy: “Lies! Everyone knows Pete! He’s a chimpanzee that wrestles! I mean … duh. It’s not like you forget watching a chimpanzee bite a man in spandex’s left testicle off! I couldn’t forget if I tried!” … burned … burned into my mind’s eye … burned!! Man in the Yellow Hat: “Wait … chimpanzee? I think I saw him at the zoo.” And there it was. I knew he had seen Pete. Finally the pieces of the puzzle were coming together. I had been searching for who knows how long – at least since eleven – and now I had my first big break. The zoo it was. [Little Timmy cocks his fedora to the Man in the Yellow Hat, and turns away. He goes off … off to the zoo.] Man in the Yellow Hat: “Err … how about unlocking me? Kid? Little help here?” ------------------------------------- [Ten minutes and a wiggle through a half-open gate later, Little Timmy had slipped into the local zoo, and was quickly approaching his latest target.] I was getting close, now. I just knew it. [Because dinner was quickly approaching, Little Timmy wastes no time. Within another few minutes, he is standing within the monkey house. With a quick overview, and a sideward glance at a series of apes mooning him with their bright red asses, Timmy nods; he was definitely in the right place. He wouldn’t have to kill the Man in the Yellow Hat after all.] Well, I don’t HAVE to, at least. [Finding the chimpanzees, Little Timmy begins beating against the cage with furious abandon.] Little Timmy: “Pete!!! Pete!!!! I know you’re in there! Say something, Pete!!” [A chimpanzee in the cage stares at Timmy awkwardly. Then he poops. And then he throws it at Timmy, who deftly dodges it.] Little Timmy: “Little chimpanzee, have you seen my hero, Pete the PG Tips Chimp? The Man in the Yellow Hat told me he was here!” Chimpanzee: “…” Little Timmy: “Please … I need to find Pete. He used to be the greatest chimpanzee of them all … a chimpanzee feared across the world! But my daddy told me he had fallen into the cold, bitter embrace of tea addiction, and now I have to find him, save him, and let him know I kind of signed him up for the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament (5!) without telling him. Otherwise he won’t be able to make it to the match, and then this angry Australian will ban him and kick him in the nuts and call him mean names and I can’t let that happen to my hero Pete!!!” Chimpanzee: “…” Little Timmy: “…” Chimpanzee: “…” Little Timmy: “… I’m screwed.” [One little hand goes up, pushing his oversized fedora backwards, revealing Timmy’s little, bare face. As he turns around, one small tear begins falling down his cheek.] I tried, Pete. I did everything I could to find you, but … but … it’s dinner time, and I just couldn’t do it. If only chimpanzees could talk … I just know they’re help me the— “KID!!!! TURN THE FUCK AROUND!!! [Surprised, Little Timmy spins around, almost tripping over the overcoat that’s dragging on concrete. And just like that, Timmy comes face-to-face with … an imprisoned Spiff the Magic Monkey.] Spiff the Magic Monkey: “Yes!!! It’s about fucking time someone came to the fucking zoo again!! I’ve been trapped in this fucking cage for two months, and you’re the first fucking person that’s come by here who isn’t fucking eighty-years-fucking-old!” Little Timmy: “Wait a second … I know you! You’re that retard that hung around the UWF even though no one really thought you were very funny. Spiff, is that you?” Spiff the Magic Monkey: “Fucking yes it’s me! Stop talking and get me the fuck out of here!!” Little Timmy: “No! I’ve been searching long and hard, and I want answers … tell me where Pete the PG Tips Chimp is!” Spiff the Magic Monkey: “Fuck Pete!!! I’m the real monkey!!!” Little Timmy: “Chimpanzees aren’t monkeys, you retard.” Spiff the Magic Monkey: “Fuck!!!” Little Timmy: “Tell me!” Spiff the Magic Monkey: “Pete’s where Pete always fucking is! Fucking unconscious in a fucking bar downtown! The Unending Fucking Tap! Now get me the fuck out of here!” Little Timmy: “Kickass! But no.” Spiff the Magic Monkey: “What!? This isn’t fucking funny! Get me the fuck out of here!” Little Timmy: “Dude, you suck. You’ve always sucked. You sucked when you first started, you sucked when the joke got old, you still suck. If it hadn’t been for that Flaming Monkey Match, no one would give a damn about you, and even that match you were only cool because they were trying to set you on fire and beat each other with your charred body. You’re boring, unrealistic, and you owe my Curse Jar like $250 already, and if you weren’t so boring, unrealistic, and poor, I’d be able to buy a bicycle just because of this one silly little trip, you foul mouthed, annoying, rip-off of Pete. Enjoy being raped by orangutans, you unoriginal twat. I’m off to find my hero!” I could have gone on like that for another fifteen minutes, but gosh darnit … I’m rushing the clock. [And just like that, Little Timmy spins, runs, and charges off in the direction of the Unending Fucking Tap, where his hero is said to be.] Spiff the Magic Monkey: “FUCK!!!!!” [Spiff shakes the bars frantically as a large monkey approaches his rear end with reckless abandon … and we thankfully follow Timmy.] ------------------------------------- Here it is. My long, strange journey has finally come to an end – and with twenty five minutes to spare before dinner, too. I’m coming for you, Petey. [Little Timmy stands in the shadows in front of the dilapidated building that is the Unending Fucking Tap, his overcoat trying to blow in the wind (and would, if he wasn’t standing on the end of it), one small hand holding down his fedora. With a deep breath and a nod, Timmy stalks forward, throws open the doors, and enters.] “OOOO OOOO OOO!!!!!” “Alright! Alright! You’re the champ! Whatever you want!” Wait a second … this seems familiar. [As Little Timmy stands in the middle of the Unending Fucking Tap, Timmy watches as the disappointed bartender begins pouring another cup of tea for the slouched patron at the bar.] “Gosh darnit, Pete…can’t you see what you’re doing to yourself? What you’re doing to your friends? You’re killing us all!” [With a roar, Pete sends his glass flying across the bar, where it shatters against the head of an unconscious drunk. The PG Tips Chimp leaps onto the bar, where he begins backhanding and bitch-slapping the bartender.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: “Ooo ooo ooo ooo aww!!!!!!” Little Timmy: “… Petey?” [And just like that, the tea-drunken outburst comes to an end. Pete turns around slowly, his wittle heart breaking as he sees the horrified child standing in the bar, tears dripping down his little hairless cheeks. Pete can only look through hazy eyes at his own hands as Timmy stares at him.] Little Timmy: “Pete … my dad told me this is what you’d become, but I couldn’t believe it was true. Why, Pete? What’s wrong with you? You were my hero … you were the best! Why, Pete, why!?!” [Pete slumps down, dropping from the bar onto the floor. He sits down on his bare chimp-ass, pulling his hands to his eyes and sobbing.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: (Crying) “Ooo ooo ooo ooo ooo ooo …” [Silently, Little Timmy goes to his hero, picking the little guy up in his own little arms, holding him like a baby as Pete cries.] Little Timmy: “Don’t you worry, Petey … I’m going to help you get over this. I’m going to help you get back your life! We’re going to get you back into wrestling … we’re going to let you bite people’s testicles off for no reason again … we’re going to get you off that addictive devil’s piss known as PG Tips Tea!” [Patting Pete on the back, Little Timmy exits the Unending Fucking Tap with the former wrestling superstar in his arms, still bawling.] Little Timmy: “Oh … and I kind of entered you into some silly wrestling tournament. I mean … just so you know. You kind of have to wrestle tomorrow.” Pete the PG Tips Chimp: “Ooo ooo ooo!?” Little Timmy: “I don’t why a giant, global organization would let me enter you into a tournament … but hey, why start questioning logic now? Now come on … my daddy’s got spaghetti at home, and we’ve only got five minutes to get home for dinner. Hey … can you help me with my Geography homework?” Pete the PG Tips Chimp: “Ooo.” [Fin.]
