UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #533

Ryders turn to cry.

Whenever (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Somewhere

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Malcolm Kennedy versus Ruff Ryder in a steel cage match to end it al. Jamie Shock returns and drew blood from Malcolm. Sounds like Malcolm’s gonna have plenty to talk about. *** [ The scene opens up inside the UWF trainers room. Inside Malcolm Kennedy sits on the table, still wearing the same jeans from the night before, blood stains scattered about them. His bloody t-shirt is hanging on the side of the trash can, and he now wears only a white wife beater. The trainers hands move about Malcolm’s forehead. You hear a loud snip and he pulls his hands back. Six stitches are exposed a quarter of an inch above Malcolm’s right eye. The trainer returns and places two band aids over the top of the stitches.] Trainer: “You know the drill Malcolm, take care of the stitches.” Malcolm Kennedy: “Yeah, Yeah, I know. Do you have any Tylenol?” Trainer: “Yeah, Gimme a second.” [ The trainer walks to the cabinet on the wall. He opens it up and knocks a few bottles around before pulling a bottle of Tylenol.. He hands the bottle to Malcolm followed by a bottle of water. ] [Malcolm pops the top off the Tylenol and turns up the bottle. He crunches up a few pills in his mouth and washes them down with water.] Malcolm Kennedy: “That stupid twat. I mean, who the hell does he think he is? Coming out there interrupting my speech. Fucking Shock. He’ll get what is coming to him. And when he does, he will wish he never even thought of making one last come back. Ill make sure of that. But I gotta take care of Ryder first.” [ Malcolm stands up off the table, and walks out of the room. He slams the door behind him as he goes. He walks down the hall to a bench where his bags sit. He picks them up and continues down the hall. At the end of the hall he comes to a rotating glass door. He walks through to the parking lot. There, waiting on him courtesy of the Valet, is his 1980 Pontiac Trans Am, In factory gold paint with gold wheels and Dayton tires. The exhaust pipes are straight headers that run out behind the front wheels. ] [ Malcolm yanks the keys out of the Valet’s hand and pushes him to the ground. He throws his bags in the trunk and slams it down. The camera watches from over top the car as he goes to sit in, he sees the camera is still around so he stops. He then closes the door and rests his forearms on top of the car. ] Malcolm: “What do you want? You want to hear about the pain and suffering Im gonna put Ruff Ryder through at WNU? You want to hear about the blood Im gonna make pour from his body like a river? The fact is Ryder, you got what you thought you wanted. You got a match with me. You got me in a steel cage. But now that you got me, Deep down you know you don’t want it. Because you know what I can do, and you know what Im gonna do to you inside that cage. You’ve been coming out every time I have a match and sticking your nose where it doesn’t belong. Interfering during the match, and costing me my win. Then you crossed the line at Stable Wars when you interfered during the final match and got me eliminated. And that is what got you in the predicament your in now. Trust me when I say this Ryder, Your in for a whole world of hurt at WNU. This is payback bitch!” “Once they close and lock that cage door, no one is gonna be able to save you. Not even your slap ass butt buddy Jamie Shock. The only person that can do anything to save you is god himself. And Im sure he doesn’t even like your loud mouth” “ Let me ask you something Ryder, what do you think will give way first, Your skin, or the steel cage? Don’t know the answer? Well that’s okay, because your gonna find out at SAS when I rake your forehead back and forth against that cold steel. My goal here Ryder, is to hurt you in every way freaking possible. To make you bleed, to make you scream in pain, and to leave you bloodied and broken in the middle of the ring. And I will do every one of those things wether you like it or not.” [ Malcolm stands up straight and checks the time on a watch that he pulled from his pocket. He the returns to resting his arms on the hood of the car.] “ Ryder, when the Prime Alliance was down, I kept us up. When we were outnumbered, I was the first to step up and take a swing. When people said we were dead, I kept the heart of the Prime Alliance beating. Hell, I was the heart of the Prime Alliance. I carried you and Shocks weight on my shoulders for months and months. Then Shock ran off like the coward he is, and left us to fend for ourselves. And that we did. Well, I did at least. But I still had to carry you every where. You were like a baby. You had to be constantly watched and taken care of by me or you would have died. You would’ve been destroyed by the Players Club. But I never let that happen. I saved your ass night after night, and when I finally realized all you were doing was slowing me down, I dropped you like a bad habit. You took it personal. You made it personal. And making something personal with me, will only get you hurt. You want to step up and jump me every single time I have a match, that is just fine and dandy with me. But after our cage match, after I destroy you, Every time you step out and interfere with a match of mine, Im gonna pay you back for it, TEN FOLD. Im not gonna let your bullshit antics slide anymore. If you don’t want to accept the fact Im better than you, and you wanna try and come out night after night and fuck with me in my matches, Then your gonna get paid back. But I don’t think Ill have to worry about that after WNU. Because Im gonna beat you within an inch of your life, and leave you in the ring for the rest of the KnightMare Brigade to come and clean up off the matt. But if for some reason the beating you get inside that steel cage isn’t enough to make your stupid ass realize not to mess with me, and you keep fucking with me matches, Your gonna regret every minute of it. My goal is to hurt you at WNU Ryder, to completely and totally destroy you. To make you feel pain worse than the first time you and Jamie banged in the backseat of a taxi cab. And if that doesn’t get my point across to you that Im done with you, then Ill have to call for more drastic measures. If I have to do that, your gonna have to watch your back every where you go. I can promise you that.” “My advice to you Ryder, is to come out at WNU and taking your beating like the bitch you are. And let your beef with me die. Because its only gonna lead to more pain for you. And you don’t want that. I promise you.” [ With that, Malcolm swings his door open and drops down into the drivers seat. He turns the key and the engine roars to life. He shifts the car into first and speeds off as the scene fades to black. ]
