UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #298

A poor choice of words

2 Apr 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bright red words appear on the blackened screen, reading “The night after last week’s Supremacy.” The words disappear, and we open on familiar territory: namely, the metal gates leading to Hell’s Carnival. The flames erupting from the statuary are brighter because of the darkness enveloping the scene. Shapes are seen moving in the darkness, and as they reach the light of the flames we see it’s, naturally, the World Champion and his lady, Pyretta Blaze. Blaze: “I still can’t believe you went there in the first place! What were you thinking?” The clown sighs and turns to face her. Beelzebozo: “I told you already—Ryder said he wanted to have it out, once and for all, to settle things between us! Sure, it was stupid to believe him, but that was a chance I couldn’t pass up! Can you blame me?” Blaze: “I suppose not…” Beelzebozo: “Anyway, I didn’t know he was friends with Moraless, or I’d have certainly kept my eye on him. Still , it figures he wouldn’t be man enough to actually WANT to settle things once and for all. He’d rather continue whining and crying and acting like a little chickenshit bitch-ass punk.” Blaze: “So now what?” Beelzebozo: “Nothing. I’m not going to continue playing this stupid game of tit-for-tat. We’ll call this his venting after dropping the ball yet again, and let him fade back into the obscurity he so richly deserves. He’s so clearly unworthy of being a World Title contender that it’s no longer funny. It was amusing to play with him for a while, but now it’s just pathetic. He’s like a child who’s been told he can’t have everything he wants, and so he lashes out with mindless violence and throw tantrums. I say let him; there are more important things to think about.” With that, they go through the gates. We dissolve to the black screen again, and new words appear, reading “Today.” And back to the action, as Pyretta Blaze and the Quiet One are watching something of apparent interest on TV. Unlike most times we see them, they’re wearing simple street clothes. It’s kind of disturbing, seeing a mime wearing blue jeans and a T-shirt. Blaze turns to the mime and points toward the curtains. Blaze: “Go get Beelzebozo while I rewind the tape. I’m sure he’ll want to hear this.” The Quiet One nods and dashes off as our lovable pyro rewinds said tape. She checks her nails as she waits. “At least this guy seems stable and intelligent. It was nice to hear a promo that wasn’t a bunch of swearing and insults. Ryder could learn a few things from this Dunn fella.” She stops the tape and puts on that goofy channel that shows the burning log 24-7. She rests her chin in her hands and smiles dreamily as she stares at the screen. The Quiet One returns, looks at the TV screen, then at Pyretta. He rolls his eyes and seats himself on the couch once more. Beelzebozo, wearing sweat pants and a torn sleeveless shirt, comes in, using a dirty towel to wipe some grease off of his hands. Beelzebozo: “You both realize that if this isn’t important enough to warrant interrupting my work, I’ll have to hurt you, right?” Blaze: “Huh? Oh, hey honey! We thought you might want to see this promo from your opponent.” Beelzebozo: “Why would I care about that?” Blaze: “He’s asking for a favor.” The look of distaste that appears on the clown’s face seems more appropriate for someone being told they’ve been eating fried frog eyes than for someone being asked for a favor. “Hold on, hon. Remember that it’s Dunn, who we don’t actively hate yet.” Beelzebozo: “Oh, very well. Roll tape.” And roll tape she does! Beelzebozo: “Can you get it to the part about me? I could care less about him showing the world that Hooked on Phonics™ worked for him.” Blaze: “Sure thing, babe!” She does so, and stops tape. Dunn’s words can be heard now. JD: “One of these being the return of a certain Vietnamese tag team that I would rather not to mention by name. Their return signifies that all the past is not truly dead and buried, as I would like it. The eerie shadow of the Iron Curtain creeping back under the door. This is another detail to leave for a later date, there is and even bigger fish than the multiple tag team champs to worry about. That fish would be Beezlebozo, and I finally get my ONE ON ONE match against him on renaissance.” Beelzebozo: “He called me a fish! Son of a BITCH.” Blaze: “It’s going to get important here.” Beelzebozo: “A FISH. He’s so dead now.” JD: “I finally get the honour of taking the World Champ on a one way thrill ride through the ring on my own, no distractions, no weapons, no mess, just a good clean fight. That’s what I hope anyway. There isn’t a gimmick to this match, possibly I’m quite glad, but nevertheless I’m upset, as I know how fond of weapons Mr Bozo is, and one connection to my skull means my victory, but he retains the title. I think you can safely see that this is indeed unfair, but instead of imposing a title change on a disqualification, I’ll instead behave quite rashly and appeal to the Clown’s sense of dignity. “ Beelzebozo: “My what now?” Blaze: “Honey, he doesn’t want a stupid “DQ equals title change” stipulation.” Beelzebozo: “Well, I guess that’s pretty cool…” JD: “You may think that’s a mad idea, appealing to the dignity of a clown. There is though a man there; no matter how bitter and twisted, and I know every man has a sense of pride, even if his morals are perverted and shameful. I also know the feeling that you get when you undeniably beat a man in a fair fight. This is what I ask of you Bozo, everyone likes a challenge, having the odds stacked in your favour are never interesting. Then again it is only a suggestion and a matter of pride, could you give up a baseball bat covered in my blood for a nice clean fight because your opponent asked you?” Beelzebozo: “Pause this.” Blaze does so, and the clown begins pacing. “This is unexpected. I realize I know next to nothing of Dunn, outside of his in-ring prowess and his being a Scotch Commie, but this…” He stops, and stares at Blaze, an almost pleading note creeping into his voice. “Dear, he’s appealing to my sense of fair play and honor! Make him stop!” He throws his hands into the air. “What do I do now?!” Blaze: “Well…this may be nuts, and I know how much you love your hardcore wrestling, but maybe you could…indulge him? Don’t hit!” She immediately covers her head with her arms. Beelzebozo, however, doesn’t raise a finger toward her, but again begins pacing. Beelzebozo: “With the Ryder fiasco taking up so much of my time lately, I’d started to think that I was the only wrestler who cared about man-to-man, fairly balanced wrestling. I was afraid everyone here would constantly try and stack the deck against me, to take advantage of my being who I am. “And here’s Dunn. First he compliments me prior to my first match as referee…and now this? “If I didn’t know better I’d swear he actually…had respect for me in some form.” Blaze: “Well, stranger things have happened…” Beelzebozo: “I find myself tempted to actually give him what he wants. I mean, he’s actually asked…NICELY…and it’d give me another chance to prove that I’m the best damn wrestler on the planet, no matter what the circumstances. I can win against all odds, and I can win in a fair fight too.” He stops pacing and nods to himself, apparently deciding that this is the thing to do. Blaze: “There’s a little bit left, but it’s not important.” Beelzebozo: “We might as well finish this.” Blaze: “I don’t know that that’s a good idea…” Beelzebozo: “I want to see it. Play it. Now.” She does, her face tense. JD: “Then again maybe the masses are right and our world champ is indeed a joke not to be taken seriously, and in that case I’m just wasting my time talking about it. But we shall see if I am right or wrong on the matter at a different date.” The clown’s face contorts into a snarl. “I have some other matters to be attending to. So I bid you farewell.” Blaze stops the tape. Beelzebozo: “I KNEW it. I freakin’ KNEW IT. He doesn’t respect me at all! He couldn’t just assume I’d be up for what he asked, oh no. He had to try and goad me into it by calling my character into question. He thinks me weak enough mentally to fall for such an obvious ploy? He throws out a blatant taunt like that and expects me to get all angry and give him what he wants to save my wounded pride, rather than trust I’m man enough to accept his request on my own? How DARE he!? HOW DARE HE!?!” He screams, causing Blaze and the Quiet One to dart to the other side of the room. “Well, he’s not getting that fair contest now! In fact, he’s not even getting a match! Soon as he sets foot into that ring with me I’m taking every foreign object within reach to his smug little head! And considering how big my duffel bag is, that’s a LOT of weaponry!! He’s going to wish he’d kept up his pathetic façade of respect, the rat bastard!” He storms off. Blaze: “Honey, wait…” Beelzebozo: “I KNEW tuning up that chainsaw was a good idea…” And with that, he disappears through the curtains. Blaze and the mime shake their heads, and return to their seats. Blaze sighs sadly. Blaze: “I knew I shouldn’t have shown him the rest of that promo.” The Quiet One pats her shoulder reassuringly as we fade out.
