UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #267

A Laughing Matter

3 Mar 2004

The Plague — Limo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a limo, we see The Plague sitting in the backseat he is dressed in a 'Philadelphia Flyers' shirt and jeans. He is wearing a bandana on his head. He is seen watching a TV screen that is sitting across from him. He is laughing hysterically he is also slapping his hand off his knee repeatedly. The camera pans over to show Robert Pendragon falling into some women's arms. The camera than pans back over to The Plague who is still laughing.] Plague: 'OH MY GOD, that is The UWF Hardcore Champion.' [Tears begin coming from The Plague's eyes, they are runing down his cheek and falling to the floor off his face.] Plague: 'Trust Exercises, what the hell is wrong with this guy?' [Plague tries to stop his laughter but only more comes out and more tears pour from his face. The Plague quickly shuts off the TV and looks into the camera.] Plague: 'Pheewww.' [Plague breathes heavy and wipes the tears from his face.] Plague: 'Hold on, I need to get my self situated here. I mean Mr. Pendragon, hahahahaha, and I thought Iron Lung was bad. Come on man, you are a disgrace to the word Hardcore, wow.' [Plague gets a more serious look on his face as herubs his hands on his face one more time to make sure all the tears are gone.] Plague: 'And I thought hearing Raid giving Gordy a compliment was funny, damn this is what I have been missing. Wow no wonder The UWF put me in this match, these guys need an eye opening experience, or maybe a head opening one. A chair with names on it, now that's original.' [Plague reaches under his seat and pulls out a sledgehammer he than smiles.] Plague: 'Now this may not be original either but it is way more effective for the task at hand. Ol' Sledge, we have had some good times haven't we?' [Plague sits holding the hammer in his hand repeatedly hitting it into his other hand with a psychotic grin on his face.] Plague: 'I have done a lot of damage with this lil' guy right here, boy it brings back a lot of memories.' [Plague closes his eyes and a more sincer smile appears on his face.] Plague: 'Yeah, that was a great time.' [Plague opens his eyes realizing the camera is still on him and repositions himself in the seat and his faces goes back to the more serious look.] Plague: 'Oh yeah, your here, damn my mind wonders sometimes. I mean it has been a while, and I surely can't wait to wear gold one more time.' [Plague places the sledgehammer back under the seat.] Plague: 'No, I highly doubt I will be needing thisto defeat Iron Lung or Raid. But seriously, Ben Jericho, what the hell happened to you? You lost to a pansy boy. Iron Lung, you better get your priorities straight, see Sunday you won't be looking at any beautiful women, and no one will be able to catch you when you fall flat on your face from The Black Death.' [Plague cracks his knuckles.] Plague: 'You see Iron Lung, you may like the ladies, but it seems you ar a lesbian, because frankly I see no balls.' [Plague busts out in laughter again.] Plague: 'Alright, alright, I got to stop now before I rupture something. Sunday is my second match back.' [Plague holds up two fingers.] Plague: 'Count'em, one, two, and I am already in a Title Match, boy Mike Montgomery you surely work fast.' [Plague grins] Plague: 'I don't know what I ever had against you man, must have been the times, I was pretty immature back then, but now I realize true greatness, and I see you going places very soon man, once that head of your is a hundred percent, you will show Seymour what wrestling is all about.' [Plague pours himself a drink, a rum and coke, sixty/forty of course.] Plague: 'You want one?' [Plague offers an empty glass to the cameraman.] Cameraman: 'No thanks.' [Plague shrugs his shoulders] Plague: 'Oh well, just tryin' to be nice.' [Plague takes a drink from his rum and coke and wipes his mouth.] Plague: 'Mmmm, that's some good shit, but not as good as the THC Title being placed around my waist.' [Plague nods his head in confidence.] Plague: 'Now I bet Iron Lung and Raid are gonna be talking about me being overconfident and all that bullshit. About how I don't have any championships right now, yeah that is true Raid, but where's yours? Ouch, I bet you saw that one coming.' [Plague shakes his head and laughs under his breath.] Plague: 'You talk about history, yeah that is what it is, but it is important to know what you are getting yourself into before you start running your mouth. I was, yea the key word is was, but I WAS the Intercontinental Champ, and the Hardcore Champ, and the Shoot Wrestling Champ. What have you won Raid? Absolutely nothing, exactly. I've beaten countless greats here, I've beaten Lew Spectre, I've beaten Gurgles, I've beaten Jamie Shock, I've beaten Joshua Dunn. Who have you beaten? Oh yeah Bruce Gordy.' [Plague again starts with the ever familiar laughing.] Plague: 'I think you get my point. So I am going to leave you with some words of advice. Iron Lung; keep that Title close to you for the next couple of days cause it will be leaving your pathetic little grip on Sunday. Raid; keep running your mouth about how history doesn't mean a thing, cause on Sunday I will make you just that, History.' [Plague places his hand over the lens of the camera.] Plague: 'You can turn that off now.' [With that you see blackness fill the screen.]
