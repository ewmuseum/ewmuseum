UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #235

Time Draweth Near...

date unknown

Seymour Almasy — Apartment of the Almasys.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“I have failed once. I have failed twice. I will not fail a third time.” A depressed Seymour Almasy lies in his bed. There is much for him to be depressed about. He lost his UWF World Title to Beezlebozo. He lost to Joshua Dunn on Mount Gagazet. But, there remains one last chance for the Final Fantasy. This final opportunity is in the form of a tournament. A tournament for the UWF Intercontinental Title. Seymour Almasy: So much sorrow recently. I have been failing. Failure is not a concept I am used to. I despise failure. And so, I must manage to turn around this losing streak. In doing so, I can capture the one prize that has yet eluded me. Almasy smiles, then goes back to his moping . Seymour Almasy: My opponent is a large man. I face Raid. He is an individual with considerable power, size, and strength. He has all of the tools one could need in order to do battle. He trains, however, by fighting roaches. A dismissive wave of the hand seems to indicate Seymour’s opinions of this training method. Seymour Almasy: Roaches are small and piddly! They can’t possibly provide any significant experience points! At least I battle squirrels and rabbits, creatures of significant size and danger to give me good experience! It is a weary Seymour that begins to lean up in his bedroom. Muscles are aching, body tired. Almasy has undergone much trauma, and it shows. Seymour Almasy: You know, sometimes I wonder why I still bother doing this anymore… Aerith Heartilly: Don’t say that! Seymour Almasy: Eh, I know, I know…I now must focus on Raid, however. Aerith Heartilly: What do we know about him? Seymour Almasy: Well, he does have a seemingly powerful poison attack. It’s what he uses primarily to destroy the roaches he meets in combat. Aerith Heartilly: Well, that’ll just mean you’ll need Antidotes. Seymour Almasy: Or a Star Pendant. Those things aren’t too rare, and they prevent poison, so lord knows I have to have one around here somewhere. Aerith Heartilly: Yes, go consult your accessory drawer. Seymour gets up from the bed, and heads over to a drawer, and begins rummaging through it, hands passing over all sorts of rings, necklaces, and charms, before finally settling on one in particular. Seymour Almasy: Ah, here we are, the Star Pendant! This will now keep me safe from Raid’s infernal poison attacks! Aerith Heartilly: Excellent, so there’s nothing else to worry about? Seymour Almasy: Well, Raid also possesses some elemental attacks. Aerith Heartilly: He does? Seymour Almasy: Well, yes. He has a Bug Zapper. It’s kind of like Reno’s Electro-Mag Rod. So, it’s an electric attack. Aerith Heartilly: What else? Seymour Almasy: His Bug Bomb seems to be a fire-elemental attack. It is a devastating tactic that he annihilated Ezra Kazee with a few weeks ago. He even managed to use it to blow up an entire house! It is a power unlike any I’ve ever seen. Aerith Heartilly: So, it would help greatly if you could avoid it? Seymour Almasy: Absolutely it would. Almasy smiles, and nods his head. Seymour Almasy: I just cannot afford to lose this match. I have lost two matches straight for the first time. It has NEVER happened before here in the UWF. Aerith Heartilly: It’s okay, Seymour. You will win this one. Seymour Almasy: I certainly plan on it. If I win this, I move one step closer to obtaining the UWF Intercontinental Title I could not defeat Joshua Dunn for. Aerith Heartilly: What’s the status of that Via Infinito of yours? Seymour Almasy: I don’t know. I may not go through with it. Aerith Heartilly: But why not? Seymour Almasy: Because…I don’t want a shot at the World Title. Aerith Heartilly: You DON’T?!?! Seymour Almasy: No. Aerith, I do not. The will for me here is broken. Aerith Heartilly: But Seymour… Seymour Almasy: I have been the greatest warrior here for some time, Aerith. Every land has eras. Tsunami Oni’s era was ended by Jamie Shock, but neither he nor the chimpanzee has enough time to establish their own eras in turn. I was the man who followed the Oni era. The Seymour era lasted for many fortnights. But in turn, my era was ended by Beezlebozo. Time shall tell if his era shall last. But, for me…my era is done. And as such, it is almost time for me to leave. Aerith Heartilly: No…you love it here, Seymour. You love this place! Seymour Almasy: I do…but that is why I must leave. Before I become too attached. To this land, I am now a declining warrior. A warrior past his prime. I have been figured out, understood. And as such, it is time to go. Aerith Heartilly: When will you leave? Seymour Almasy: After the Renaissance. Then shall I leave the land of the UWF. Aerith Heartilly: I wish it didn’t have to come to this. Seymour Almasy: As do I. But it does. And since it does, I must finish my mission. I must achieve the Grand Slam Championship. And to do so, I must win this tournament. This tournament is what is left for me. Aerith Heartilly: Does this mean we’ll be leaving then? Seymour Almasy: Eventually yes. I will take you to Global Wrestling then. But for now, I still have a month left. A month until my time is up. Aerith Heartilly: One month to achieve your dream. Seymour Almasy: And with so much at stake…with so much to gain and so much to lose, I cannot fail. I must not fail. I will not fail. Aerith Heartilly: I know that you won’t, sweetie. Seymour Almasy: Raid, you face a warrior refocused. A man who has little time here left. A man who can ill-afford to lose again. Aerith Heartilly: As such, you face a man who is very, very dangerous indeed. Seymour Almasy: That I am, Raid. A man who, thanks to his Star Pendant, is immune to your little poison spells. So long as I can avoid the Bug Bomb, I shall be successful, and I shall move on in this tournament. Aerith Heartilly: Raid, prepare yourself. Seymour Almasy: For the Farplane awaits you! (Fin).
