Real good time(Part1)

27 Jun 2004

The Real Deal — Spotlight Central

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now concentrate children. I wouldn't want your mind's to go off track while you are reading the following because that would just be a disgrace. You are all disgraces anyway but we don't want you all digging a hole deeper than you all already have. What is that? Like 10 feet deep already? Wow, what have you people been doing recently. Anyway, the fact is that all of you who are part of the UWF suck complete ass compared to The Real Deal. Now you may think that he's going to come out and say that he's better than all of you because he's the UWF Television Champion. The Real Deal flashes into view for a second. 'Shit to that!' Or else you think that he's going to start talking about how he was the DWO Intercontinental Champion and DWO Extreme Champion at the same time not long before it closed. Once again The Real Deal flashes into view for a second. 'Shit to that!' Or maybe you all think he is going to tell you that he is better than all of you because he was able to destroy Donny Diamond just months before Double D became PTC Global Champion. And again The Real Deal flashes into view for another second. 'Shit to that!' By now you're probably all thinking about where I am going with this and secondly who the hell am I? You do not need to know who I am. All you need to know is what's going on with your future ruler...the King of the newest evil on the earth. A spotlight flicks on to show The Real Deal sitting on a wooden chair inside a small whitewashed room. There are various different patterns all around the walls which look like symbols for medievil beasts. Deal looks around calmly, checking out the room in it's entirety. 'How lovely it is to have some piece for once' he states as his arms move up and behind him as he stretches out fully. He brings his arms back down by his side before bending forward and reaching underneath the wooden seat. He stays prone in that position for a moment before swooping backwards, bringing a strap with him. He holds it up towards the spotlight and it can seen as the UWF Television Title. He brings it close to him and runs the back of his hand along it. 'It has been a long time since I have tasted gold, too long.' The words slither from his lips which make him sound like he is already in love with the championship belt. Though his tone changes dramatically. 'It's a pity that this gold has the taste of vile and disgust' he scowls. 'I have seen better looking monkey asses than this thing!' The UWF TV Title gets removed from the spotlight and thrown to the ground by Deal. 'Is this what that title represents?' he questions. 'Does it mean that once you have it, you are out of the spotlight?' Real Deal stands up and walks over to the Title which is laying a couple of metres away from him. He looks down at it but has no intentions of picking it up. He moves his foot forward and kicks it into the wall. 'That is what I think of the TV Title' he murmered as he moves towards the belt again. This time though he picks it up and grabs it with both hands. With pure rage he keeps it held in his right hand and moves back to throw it. 'And this is what I think of the UWF!' Before he can let go of the title a hand moves across and holds him back. 'Now now my friend...' The gentle voice of a female soothes Deal inside and he relaxes back to his previous state. The female is around five foot, eight inches with short blonde haired which reached her perfect cheekbones. With a fiery passion she looks at Deal, her eyes as deep as the sun. 'Come with me dear, you need to calm down' she says, 'You have been in here for the past two hours, you need some rest' Deal turns to her and gives an evil grin. 'I will precious, I will.' At that the two open a small door in the corner of the room and ascend some steps. The door closes behind them and the room descends into darkness... Nothing is ever what it seems. You may all be thinking that the extract above didn't tell you anything...but it told you alot more than you could imagine...Some of you think The Real Deal is a rookie...think again or you shall be destroyed...
