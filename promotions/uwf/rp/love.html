Love

4 May 2006

Clyde — undated in the source

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the invincible, gazing down upon you. While you quake beneath the strain of mortal existence, I stand atop the mountain, and I laugh, laugh, laugh. I watch as your pitiful masses thrash and screech and attempt to climb, but you shall never reach me. You destroy each other in the attempt, and the scent of your blood rises to greet me, coppery and sweet. Insects, all of you. My mocking chortle becomes thunder, tearing the heavens apart. And as the clouds give way to glorious lightning, tearing down with a sear of ozone and burning you alive, I extend my mighty hand. I reach out for one of you, just one. I let your hope seep in, the misery in your shattered face, abated only by the desire that I, I shall be your savior. And then I see your lips tremble and move, your voice creaking out to say to me, 'Wake up, dipshit.' ..........ah hell. The dirt caked face before me shifts to one familiar. Though while I expect Bethany's expression to be her usual one of rage, oddly, it is not. Rather, she simply stares at me, her head tilted quizzically. If I didn't know better, I'd think she almost looked concerned. This can't be good. And I am proven correct, as the glint of a butcher's knife enters my vision. Before I can react, exquisite pain rips through me as her blade sinks directly into my forehead. Do you have any idea what it feels like to have a piece of cold metal rammed into your brain? All of those things you take for granted, like motor functions, tend to go awry. 'Ew, you nasty bastard,' she shrieked as she twisted the knife, my skull cracking horribly. 'Do we need to get you diapers?' I'm certain you didn't need the visual, but frankly, I didn't need the experience and I don't care what you need. 'You woke me up from a sound sleep and a perfectly pleasant dream of world domination. And I'm certain you had no good reason behind it other than to yell at me about wrestling or something equally stupid. So kindly remove your knife from my frontal lobes and get the hell out of my life.' .........well, that is what I wanted to say. However due to my impromptu lobotomy it came out closer to 'Igymasis, plunky plunky catablith.' 'Are you aware you have a wrestling match coming up?', she inquired as she began to lead me to my feet, pulling on the handle of the knife. 'Pshmablah,' I replied. 'Akamachoi.' 'Yeah, yeah,' said she, boredom apparently already setting in. 'Apparently everyone else has forgotten the retarded fed exists too. Nevertheless, you're in luck. It means you can probably halfass it and come out with a win anyway!' 'I wouldn't count on it,' came another voice. Though I couldn't quite see him through the blur of agony, the ancient creaking of my father sadly couldn't escape my ears. 'Haven't I taught you anything, boy? You don't put effort into a damned thing, and it always comes back to bite you.' I regained just enough control of myself to grab the knife and tug it a bit to the left. This somehow or another cleared my vision enough to see dear old dad, Dark Gimmick sitting on the couch next to Violator. A videogame controller was clutched in one hand, as he pointed at Bethany with the other. 'And personally, I blame her!' 'Me?', Bethany retorted, absent mindedly pulling on the knife and causing me to groan. 'I'm the only one who EVER motivates this sack of crap, while you sit here playing fucking Xbox!' 'Sure, you've motivated him alright,' Dark Gimmick snorted as he returned to his game. 'You've motivated him into being the biggest embarrassment this family's ever seen since Hessian.' 'I am the Violator!', bellowed the Violator, shaking his controller. 'And no, I don't cheat. What makes you think that?' 'Prepare for total violation!' ' Fucker!', exclaimed Bethany, waving her arms and me as well as she refused to let go of the blade handle. 'It was bad enough he's a whiny 2000 year old asshole, but you turned him into a whiny 2000 year old asshole with daddy issues!' 'The hell is that supposed to mean? You're lucky I spend the time I do with him. I don't have to, you know. I'm young, I shouldn't be tied down!' To accentuate this statement, my father tugged up the sleeve of his robe and flexed a skeletal arm. As he grinned however, the bone then cracked and fell onto the couch. '.........ah hell.' 'It's no wonder he's such a fucking mess!', Bethany screeched, glaring at me as I involuntarily drooled. 'I think he's beyond all repair at this point. I should just give up and find some other sugar daddy demon.' 'Yup, you do that,' said Dark Gimmick, picking up his broken arm and placing it back where it was, only to watch it fall again. 'I'm sure there are TONS of them around who want a girl who'll never have any boobs.' At this Bethany paused. She slowly turned her head from me to my father. 'I am the Violator!', the Violator shouted as he dropped his controller and dove behind the couch. 'DIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIE!', bellowed my eight year old lover, finally tugging the knife free from my skull as she leapt towards Dark Gimmick. I crashed limply to the floor, struggling to raise my head. 'I'm the Grim goddamned Reaper,' DG said cockily. 'Redundant much, you MY TEETH! GIVE ME BACK MY TEETH!' Bethany looked to her hand, where a set of dentures were now clutched, freshly removed from my father's bony face. She nodded, then proceeded to repeatedly bash him with them. 'I.........will.......kill.......you........until......I.......somehow.......manage......to......kill.......you.........again!', she screamed, each word punctuated by another strike with the dentures. Finally they broke, causing a rain of teeth to fall upon Violator, who knelt behind the couch with his hands clasped over his head. 'Prepare for total violation!', he moaned and hid once more. Meanwhile Dark Gimmick finally managed to buck Bethany off of him. He then sneered, reaching into his cloak with his remaining arm and producing what looked like a large cartoonish bomb. 'Nowph yoush.....huh?' His blue and glowing eye sockets registered shock as he saw that Bethany had landed on her feet and somehow produced the exact same thing. Both of them stopped momentarily. But only for a moment. Then, with shrieks from the depths of an even deeper hell than the one I live in, they charged. I closed my eyes, willed the darkness to take me, and mercifully faded to black.......
