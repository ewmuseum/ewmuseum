Not Another Jobber!!!

7 Nov 2001

Digital Mortality

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* Last time Digital Mortality was in the ring, he beat the living hell out of Brad Johnson, for the second time, and now he is getting the news that he has to face some jobber named Jack Union! A low life man, who has not stepped in the UWF ring, and if he has, I don't even remember seeing him. The fact is, after losing to Toxic Shock, Digital Mortality has been on the low card fighting jobbers. This is not to the liking of Digital, but what can he do... kick the ass of all those damn jobbers, and that is what he will do. Then he will move on to finally kick the crap out of Toxic. * * Killer Dude, has to defend his TV championship against Toxic Shock, and if he wins, he will have to defend against Digital. After beating all this jobbers he has been booked against, he will get revenge. But not yet, not yet. When the time comes, Toxic Shock will know, and he will remember it, as the worst day of his life. * ** The scene opens in the New EoD locker room, there is a House Show going on, but Digital is not watching it. He is sitting in a chair by the only light, he is looking at the card for Wednesday Night Universal. He throws it into the trash can, and slams his fist into the table. ** DM: "What the hell did I do to deserve this? What did I do? Why do they have to waste my talent and book me against jobbers who are f*ckin mute? This is a f*ckin conspiracy! While Toxic Shock is getting shots at titles, I get shots at kickin the crap out of jobbers... I have beaten Stephen, I have beaten Brad Johnson TWICE, and now I have to fight a guy named Jack Union? What the hell is that?" "This is getting frustrating, I have to fight people that don't speak... what am I supposed to say?" "Well then, the only things I know about this man, is that he is from England, and he thinks English people are the best. Well, you know what, I don't care what you think, I am Boricua hasta la muerte, if you can figure out what that is, which I think you won't. Tomorrow night, you will learn why you have to pass through me to get into the upper echelon." "Toxic Shock, you think because you beat me with a crucifix, that you can beat everyone? You had to use a f*cking lightsaber to get Plague, but you still lost. You are pathetic, I would have cut off his head. I would have cut all of your heads if it were me." "And the last thing I have to say, I better get a good match at Sunday, I don't want to fight another jobber." ** The scene fades to RED. **
