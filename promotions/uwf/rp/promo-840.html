UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #840

Press Release

15 Dec 2004

Pájaro de Presa — Your local newspaper

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DATELINE...CHICAGO... Outside the Universal Wrestling Federation Headquarters, the coalition of luchadores/security guards known as the Masked Monkeys called a press conferance with the promise of a major announcement. Unfortunately, it was not to report that the entire stable was being deported. Instead, spokesman Pajaro de Presa drew attention to a recent tag team tourney conducted by Primetime Central. While they failed to win, the Masked Monkeys team of Pajaro de Presa and White Tiger came in a close and impressive second place. Due to these events, Pajaro de Presa announced that the Masked Moneys would hereby lay claim to the title of 'Greatest Enhancement Talent Tag Team Ever.' He then asked this reporter if she would 'feel the machismo' while he gyrated his hips. When this reporter pointed out her wedding band, Pajaro de Presa stated 'once you've had Chicano, the rest is just guano.' White Tiger then moved his partner aside and offered to answer questions. He replied to one query about the state of Crab Mask Jr. following FANdemonium by stating the burns are healing nicely and he wears a mask anyways. White Tiger had no comment to rumors that the simian civil rights group Gay and Lesbian Association of Monkeys, or GLAM, is protesting their use of the name 'Masked Monkeys' considering none of them are really monkeys, especially the turtle. He also had no comment to a follow-up question inquiring his prediction on how his team would do against actual Evil Monkeys. White Tiger then put his face in his hands and mumbled something about 'I used to get title shots.' A note was handed to White Tiger and he announced a beige Honda Accord was double-parked, so this reporter left the press conferance so she wouldn't get a ticket. (Yeesh, to think I could've been covering a dog show... I never get the choice assignments.)
