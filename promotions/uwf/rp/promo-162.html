UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #162

Facing your own alliance.

Jan 6 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Unknown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---So, the world continues turning. A new year has arrived to find the UWF still running. Just coming off a break after an amazing Contest of Champions that saw PWW North American Champion, And member of The Prime Alliance, Ruff Ruder, come out the overall victor. Beating all he faced. Now in the new year, the UWF returns to their wrestling schedule, beginning this weekend with the first Supremacy of 2004. And what a Supremacy it will be. Two friends, both want nothing more than to win, and will do anything to get it. One, all that is evil in the UWF. Some even consider him mad. The crowbar wheeling Jamie Shock. The other, his stable mate and friend, Malcom Kennedy. Both are battling for a great prize, A shot at the UWF World Heavyweight Title. A great Supremacy indeed.--- (* The scene opens....All that can be seen in Malcom Kennedys face. His skin, freshly shaven. His jet black hair trimmed and gelled to perfection. His blue eyes stare deep into the camera, no true expression on his face. Just standing there, staring blankly into the camera.* ) Malcom Kennedy: “I start my year facing Jamie......” (* Malcom takes a deep breath and sighs as the camera zooms out exposing his entire body, standing with his arms crossed, resting with his back against the walls. He wears a pair of black khaki pants and a white shirt tucked in, underneath a black sports coat. A gold necklace hangs from his neck, a new silver watch rests on his wrist. *) “Shock Vs Kennedy, first supremacy of 2004. If thats not enough, the match is for a shot at the UWF Heavyweight title at Underground. I start my year facing Jamie?....So be it” (* He uncrosses his arms and adjusts his body. *) “Jamie I consider you a good friend, and a great partner. Otherwise I never would have joined The Prime Alliance in the first place. But lets just go ahead and gather up all that our friendship is made up of, and push it to the side for this week. If we go ahead and do it now, it will make things easier when the match comes around. Lets face it, neither of us want to lose a shot at the title. You just came off a loss at the COC for the Heavyweight title, and Im sure your looking for a chance to take the title once again. I can understand that, hell winning the world title has been my main goal since I came to the UWF. So we both know that the other will do whatever it takes to earn that shot.” (* Malcom scratches his neck. *) “You know, We are a lot alike, me and you. We both do whatever we possibly can to get the win. Crowbars, chairs, cheating, and low blows. We go all out, and we are both good at it. You’ve got much more experience than I do, and I know that. I will never quit. You know that. And we BOTH know that wether we are facing our best friend, or our worst enemy, we will break every rule in the book if it turns out in a victory for us. So I expect our match to be nothing short of an all out, no holds barred, brawl. Im sure your fine with that, and I am too.” “So I see no reason to continue complimenting my next opponent.” (* Malcom stands up straight, moving away from the wall. *) “You know Jamie, I take a lot of mental notes when I watch other people fight. So naturally, I’ve got some work done on you. I’ve noticed, in a lot of your matches, that as the match grows longer and longer, you grow more and more desperate. Desperate to win. Desperate to leave the ring. Desperate to get away from the fans that hate you more than any other wrestler in the business. I plan to use that to my advantage. Im gonna drag this match out as long as I possibly can, break you down mentally and physically, and when the time is right, set you up for the big fall. I don’t expect this to be easy at the least, I know you will give me a fair share of the beating, and that you will dish me out a fine helping of pain. But Im ready for everything you’ve got. Your hits, your moves, and yes, your crowbar. So I ask you to bring everything you have to the ring at Supremacy, and make this match one for the ages. So we can show everyone what The Prime Alliance is made up of the best in the business when it comes to wrestling. I know you wont disappoint.” (* Malcom walks off to the right, the camera remains focused straight forward at the wall. The scene fades to black. *)
