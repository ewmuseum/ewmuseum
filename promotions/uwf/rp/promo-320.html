UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #320

Friends and Masks

7 Apr 2004

Ezra Kazee — The streets of LA

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up outside of the NBC television studios. It’s daytime and there are some people bustling around. Standing there is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is standing there in a pair of slacks, a blue Players Club t-shirt, and a jean jacket, his glasses, and a UWF baseball cap. He has very little else with him, just a smile on his face.) Kazee: Good times all around, let me tell you. Good times here, good times there, and good times with that interview I just did on The Tonight Show with Jay Leno. You know, just the normal old promotional stuff and all that good stuff, but Jay, he’s a good friend of mine. Ever since I’ve dropped the entire asshole image, I and he have really gotten to know each other really well. Of course Dave isn’t too fond of that, but hey that’s just the competition between the two of them……. Friendship is a wonderful thing, don’t you agree. A friend is someone who will always be there for you. He knows all of your flaws, all of your shortcomings, and all of your weaknesses, and still cares about you know matter what. There are very few people who can truly be great friends. And fewer yet who would even know what the meaning of friendship would be……. Me, I’m lucky. I’ve got lots of good friends all over the world. I’ve got my friends in the UWF. I’ve got my friends in the former federations I’ve been in. I’ve got my friends in entertainment, I’ve got my friends who are internationally known…… And the thing that makes them my friend is that they don’t pull punches. They are the type of people who don’t hide behind masks. They don’t put on this front or some look at me I’m some big shot thing. They are just regular old people with me. That’s all I ask for from anyone who is my friend. Don’t try to be someone your not, just be yourself. That’s more important than trying to put on some sort of a front that allows no one to see the real you…… Raid, you complain about what has been lost. You complain about how I cost us our friendship. You know what, you may be right. (Kazee looks down at the ground for a moment, and starts to slowly walk down the street.) You may be right Raid; I may have been the one who cost us our friendship. I was a different man then. I was different from who I am now. I am different from what I use to believe and what I use to be about. Things have changed, and that change may have cost us our friendship. (Kazee continues to walk with his head down. People part left and right as he walks down the street.) Raid, at the time, I was going against everything that I love now. I was putting on that front that I hate. I was pretending to be someone who I wasn’t. I was pretending to be a jackass, which I wasn’t……. Mr. Meaner was the dark side of me. Every angry moment, every piece of hatred that was Mr. Meaner. But that isn’t who I am. I am much different from him. I am much different from what he ever was. And that person who I am is more important to me then that. (Kazee stops at a drink stand.) Can I get lemonade please? (The attendant hands Kazee a lemonade and Kazee hands him a $5 before continuing his walk down the street without waiting for his change. Kazee takes a long swig of his drink) You see Raid, there is this old saying. The saying goes “A friend is someone who knows all about you and loves you just the same.” You didn’t know all about me Raid. You only knew about my hatred, my anger. That’s all you ever knew about me. And once I dropped that Mr. Meaner facade, you decided to drop me. Your right, I did put on an act Raid, but you broke the golden rule of friendship…… You also do what you do best Raid, you whine and complain. You know, you’re right, I don’t like the way that I earned my title, but there is not much I can do about it. You can call me a non athlete if you want, but the fact of the matter is is that this body and this person is more of an athlete than most people. And athlete is described by three parts, physical, emotional, and intellectual. Your right, I may be lacking in the physical department, but the fact of the matter is that where I lack in physical, I make up for in the other two categories. And that is what will keep me this title……. Of course I haven’t forgotten about you Moraless. I love how you walk around and tell me how I don’t want to defend the title. How I don’t respect things and how I don’t parade things around. I love how you think you know everything and call me a “simple delusional crackpot bastard who played with children’s toys.” Well, maybe all this is true…… But that is one person’s perspective of it. Much like Raid, you truly don’t know me. You don’t know what I’m about. You don’t know where I’ve been, what I’ve been through, and why I am who I am. It is only then that you may respect my beliefs and desires….. You know, I would be more than willing to defend this belt every chance I had. It doesn’t matter to me at all. Fact of the matter is though is that I am a busy person, and the UWF, in all its great wisdom, decided to send me on a promotional tour after I defeated you for the IC belt Moraless. Why you are even being given this opportunity when you no showed last time is beyond me. Are you going to no show again? Are you going to stand up the fans again and not allow them to have the match they deserve?....... (Kazee throws his cup away) Your right, I don’t parade my belt around with me. There is no reason to. It’s a simple concept that I would expect even you to understand Moraless. It’s called humbleness. It’s called that you don’t brag. I’ve been blessed to be able to hold this title. I’ve been blessed with the ability to accomplish what I have done and be where I am today. I could walk around all my life with it around my waist, but that would seem pretty stupid to me…… Oh, and you say I don’t care about the title either. You complain about me hanging it from the ceiling where it can get scratched and the gold could be tarnished. I think part of this all goes back to my willingness to defend the belt……. You see, I’m not exactly the most hardcore person in the world. I’m not exactly a big fan of ladders. Steel chairs and foreign objects are things that I am not fond of at all. If I had my preference, this would be on an amateur wrestling mat and based on the point system where I have done so well and have the skills that continue to propel me where I am today…… But I am out of my element on this one. This is a TLC match against two men who claim to be hardcore legends. They claim to be in the environment they love. In this match, every advantage that I would have ever had has been taken away from me. I am not home on ladders, with tables, or with chairs. It’s not my cup of tea. But being that I am a fighting champion, and a champion who is more than willing to defend his belt on any given day against anyone during any situation, I signed on for this match because it’s what the fans want, and what better way is there to legitimize myself as a champion than to defeat two self proclaimed hardcore legends in a match they should feel very at home in……. Finally Moraless, you claim you have bigger things and that is why you won’t win the IC title match. Well, that may be, I really don’t know, but fact of the matter is that the match is going to be a great match and a match that will definitely keep the fans on the edge of the seats. That’s what we are here for after all, isn’t it. To keep the fans on the edge of the seats…… So please Moraless, by all means, attend to your bigger things, just don’t let it distract you in the match that you will be having at Renaissance. Let the match be what it should be, a classic, and let everything go the way it should. Oh and as far as your PTC Extreme Championship match goes, I wouldn’t worry about that either, cause much like you won’t win the IC title, you aren’t going to win that either. Oh, also, Monty, please, do not fret. The sickness is a part of everyday life. I just hope that you’re feeling good for the PPV. Even though I know they won’t win, I’m sure they’ll find some reason as to why they didn’t win. Like you were so hopped up on drugs that you didn’t feel any of the moves they did to you. You know that might happen. Of course than again, Right To Party always seems to be that way…… Please, if you will, excuse me for one moment. (Kazee heads into a shop which has a bunch of flowers in the window. The camera doe not follow, just watches him as he walks around the store. He looks around for a moment, before finally deciding on a bouquet of flowers, they being tulips, roses, and sunflowers. He brings the bouquet to the clerk who checks him out and he hands her a slip of paper. The clerk writes something down in a book while Kazee hands her a check. Kazee then walks out of the store.) Sorry about that, I just had to take care of some business. Pryde, Joanna, Anarchy, I know that we have our differences, and we may always have our differences, but please accept my condolences in this time of grief. I have never been good at picking out flowers, but I hope you enjoy the bouquet that I have just sent...... Come Renaissance though, it will be back to business like usual. And I look forward to it. Raid, Moraless, Anarchy, Local NVA, Team AWF, Right To Party, Lustful Lullabys, good luck, cause your going to need it. (With that, Kazee holds out his hand to summon a cab, boards it as the scene fades to black.)
