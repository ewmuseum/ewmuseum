UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #726

Russia Sucks

1 Oct 2004

Mr. Meaner — McDonalds

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up within the friendly confines of a local McDonald’s. However, there is a difference within this McDonald’s. Everything is written in Russian. Yes, my friends, much like everything else that America has, this is a Russian McDonald’s. Standing there is Mr. Meaner, the man in black. There is nothing special going on there. Just him and a bunch of other stuff. There are hamburgers there however. A pile of them.) Mr. Meaner: So you can cook. Big surprise there Communist. I really don’t know what you were trying to prove by that. I mean woopty freaking do. You can cook. Wow. Let’s all just stop the presses because the Communist can cook……. Fact of the matter is that you only proved one thing by your little cooking show there. You only proved one thing in your little episode that I already knew and will continue to know and will continue to tout, especially now that we know that it is true……. You are an even bigger woman than we originally thought. I mean, the signs are all there. You couldn’t keep the woman who you had, who I understand Fizzy has been having a grand ol time with and has been showing her what she missed all these years with you……… Hell, if that slut was so important to you, then she shouldn’t have been sitting near the wrestling ring or sitting in your corner. That bitch should’ve been at home barefoot and pregnant. But don’t worry you stupid piece of crap, Fizzy is working on that. I’m sure that she will be in said position before too long…… It’s no wonder that you haven’t been able to figure that part out about it. It’s no wonder that your precious council is so freaking upset at you. You aren’t even man enough to make a bitch get down flat on her back so you can ram her and make her carry your child. Now we really know the reason for their disappointment. I’m sorry; did I let the cat out o the bag? But I’m here, getting ready for a match that you have no hope in. Getting ready for a match that you couldn’t win if your life depended on it. You think that you can win a prison fight? You think that you could come into my domain and actually defeat me. It isn’t going to happen. You know it and I know it….. And just like you Communist, the food up here sucks. Cold beef stew? God, how can you stand to eat that crap? Thank god that Western Civilization has at least taken over a bit. The only place I seem to be able to get good food is here at McDonald’s. And everyone knows how much McDonald’s food sucks…… Communist. You are but a road block at this point. I am going to destroy you. I’m going to walk all over you and through your pansy ass just so I can get back my THC Title. And there isn’t a damn thing you can do about it. (Scene fades out.)
