UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #424

Charts and Updates

22 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Classroom

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to inside a classroom complete with a science skeleton standing off to the right. In front is standing an easel with a bunch of sheets of paper. On the front sheet is the UWF Sunday Afternoon Supremacy logo on it. After a couple minute wait the door opens and in walks Adien Pryde. He walks directly to the skeleton before he addresses the camera.] Pryde : First off, I have an update on Reo Dai. The doctor told us that he has some torn cartilage in his right knee. [Pryde points to the knee on the skeleton.] Pryde : Right here and here. The estimated time he will be out will be 4-8 weeks. Now you are wonder how that will effect The Chaos Society for Stable Wars. Well we still have the Evil Exterminator Raid, the monster Draco Paladine, and myself. We will have a 4th member by then if is the last thing we do. [Pryde walks over to the easel and removes the first sheet of paper reveling a picture of the new comer Takamichi Mori.] Pryde : First we have Takamichi Mori. We don’t know much about this man other than he was trained by some man named The Green Demon in the Demon Dojo. Who and what the hell they are is anybodies guess but he will learn quick that when you come to the UWF you start with a clean slate and the past means nothing, I found that out. [Pryde tears Takamichi’s page away revealing a picture of The Plague.] Pryde : Now we have the hardcore legend The Plague. Plague is a wildcard here in the UWF. He has won the IC Belt once, and the THC belt three times. Very good credentials but what has he done with his career since he came back, not much, he has been riding his reputation from his previous stints in the UWF. If you ask me he needs to make a change to warrant his high price contract. [Pryde tears away Plague’s page away revealing Malcolm Kennedy.] Pryde : Malcolm Kennedy, what can I say about him? Back stabber, Turncoat, Opportunist, hell, a man after my on heart. Here we have a member of the Prime Alliance turned on his own stable mate to join Resurrection. Him joining Resurrection may be questionable but him laying out Ruff Rider may be the smartest thin he has ever done. Anyone who calls himself the Cherokee Thrilla needs to be left alone. [Pryde tears away yet another page to show the man himself Ruff Rider.] Pryde : And then there is Ruff Rider, the Indian superstar. This man has had more problems keeping friends than any other person since Anarchy arrived here in the UWF. Is it that he constantly talks about himself, or is it that he swears damn near every other word, or maybe is his body odor. He maybe a great wrestler but until he changes things about himself he may never know how great he can be. [Pryde pulls off Riders sheet to show a picture of himself standing in a UWF ring, arms raised in victory.] Pryde : Sundays match may just be my springboard to the top. There is so much main event talent in that ring that whoever wins will have people giving them a serious look at. I can benefit most from the win and will be fighting tooth and nail to get myself the 1-2-3. Takamichi Mori, Plague, Ruff Rider, and Malcolm Kennedy, you better keep your eyes on everybody because if you don’t watch out you may just have the match taken from you. [Pryde pulls off the last sheet to show the Anarchy symbol and walks out of the room. The camera zooms into the symbol and fades to black.]
