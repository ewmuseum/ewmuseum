Far From Pathetic...

4 Mar 2004

Iron Lung — Church

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Far From Pathetic... Starring Iron Lung The slightly faded sound of church bells tolling is the first recognition we know of that this would not be a normal interview with the THC Champion. It would be far from the norm, and perhaps even on the extreme side. Pendragon enjoyed that word, extreme. It had been running through his veins from a very young age. His parents abandoning him on the tough neighborhood's streets, and he having the strength to keep battling with nature and with the people around him, being strong enough to get where he was today. His past wasn't without pain, that's for damn sure. It wasn't without sorrow, it wasn't without reckless thoughts of what could have been if he lived in a healthy home instead of the scum that taught him how to survive. He certainly didn't have a silver spoon, his spoon was plastic and the end was twisted from boots stomping on it. As the bells kept tolling, the scene opened to us. A mist was heavy in the background, it had just rained. You could smell it in the air, that brisk air smell from the rainfall. There wasn't a rainbow, it was all too cloudy, and the wind was starting to caress the tree branches. An all too familiar scene for Pendragon. He knew what it was like to walk in the darkness of the night, shielding himself from the gang violence and hiding from the constant police patrol. This mist was nothing, he had seen worse days. Although he was ashamed to say so, his life was all too many memories of days just like this. How did he ever come to be? Sacrifices were made at points in the road, challenges were laid down in which he gladly accepted. People were lined up to get their shot at him, to try and end his reign. But he would not let it be, he wouldn't lay down for any man, no matter how hard that battered his body in the process. Hardcore? Far from it. This was the man that legends only dreamed to be, the type of character that could strike fear in a man if he told them his life story. It sounded like a horror movie, and that's exactly what it was. Pendragon just lived it one nightmare at a time, hoping to wake up. And when he got that wakeup call at the Lion's Den Dojo, he couldn't wait to have somebody pinch him to make sure that this was indeed real. He trained with the best, only to get into a company that failed thanks to him. More agony, more defeat ran through his body. He was back at ground zero, suffering on the streets. Nobody would take him in for almost two years, and it was the slowest two years of his life. But then, a light at the end of a tunnel. A chance to reconsile and begin a fresh career, and he took it without haste. It felt so sweet to wrap the belt around his waist for the first time, and his skin and bone composure almost led it to slip right off of him. He held it for what seemed like an eternity, before the organization fell to ruins. He would never forget it. You would think that with all this sorrow, he would just give up and make his life end. But no, he knew that he was ready for something special, he knew that he had it in him to keep fighting. That's what he was all of his life, and so he kept battling until he rampaged through all the organizations he could, making a name for himself among the stars everywhere he grazed his boots. The pieces were finally coming together. And he would never look back from that very moment, he would never have to get back to that all too troubled time ever again... Suddenly, we hear the taps of footsteps on pavement. They get closer with every passing moment, making our heart go along with the pace. And then, we see a man in a dark-colored overcoat pacing his way towards the church doors, his head hung low and a brimmed hat over his head. We could not notice who this man was. Walking slowly towards the entrance, he scaled two sets of stairs, ten stairs each to the top. He was all alone as he opened the large wooden doors, decored with a Gargolye on either side on the top of the entry way. They were rested on large pillars, and were meant to scare away any evil spirits that might be around the area. Gregorian hyms filled the air as the man's footsteps kept shifting into the church, and as the camera pans around, we realise again that he is the only one in the place. We look around us, the stained glass windows with images of the Saints on them, and the elegant roof that had been painted from someone who resembled a Renaissance man. Our subject took a deep and grimacing sigh, looking towards the back of the church and into a seperate room where candles were lit to remember the fallen. There was definitely a distinct aura to this place, and spirits were indeed in the presence of this man. He could feel it around him as if someone were watching him, following his every move with precision. Sighing again, he took more steps, heading towards the cornered room. The camera reveals more, as statues of idols have been placed in the corners. On the mantle of this candle collection were pictures of the more recently deceased, and in front of each candle was a small piece of paper with the person's name on it, last name coming first. The man looked upon the candles, all of them lit besides one. He looked at the name, Pendragon...Kneeling down at the provided ottoman, he made no sign of the cross and did not clench his hands in prayer. Rather, he just sat silently, eyes wide open, glaring at the candles around him. I thought I'd never come back here. I always told everyone that my father abandoned me, and that I'd have to reason to even say he existed. But now, I come before you and ask for your guidance. A strange chill entered his spine as he spoke those words. This indeed was Robert Pendragon, at a local church, paying a visit to his father. He hadn't been back here in over ten years, and this would prove to be a moving experience for the young superstar. What could he need guidance for? Was he perhaps afraid of the road that was before him? Was he in doubt of his own abilites for his upcoming match? Or was it that he just needed somebody to talk to, and had nobody else to turn to? We would soon find out.. I'm having a hard time trying to concentrate lately father. It seems that with every victory I uphold, somebody else is looking to shut me down. Even though I didn't know you, I know that if you were here right now talking to me, you'd tell me to keep my head up and keep fighting.. The truth is, sometimes I think I'm not making the best effort to the fans. Sure, they'd follow me to the very end, but sometimes I think I'm not doing the best I can for them. I'm undefeated now, and the THC Champion, but I feel as if it's not enough.. I have guys breathing down my neck everywhere I turn, trying to take advantage of me. They are trying to break my spirits, trying to make me look like I might have lost my touch. They plan to destroy me, and they taunt me for wanting to better my own lifestyle.. I know that if you were here right now, you would say to stand up for myself and keep fighting, no matter what the task. Or maybe that's just what I want to believe that you would say, because you never were supportive of me. You always just carried on with your own life, and never paid any attention to your only son. You abandoned me dad, do you even know what that did to me inside? In a way, I'm angry at you for doing that. I could have learned everything you knew, and grown up exactly the way you wanted me to. But instead, I was brought up by bums and didn't eat for three or four days at a time. It was horrible, but I survived. I think I learned more from them than I might have ever learned from you.. So, I'm here to ask you one thing and one thing only. Do you think I'm doing the right thing, fighting like this? My opponents want me to tear me limb from limb next time I see them, but I know damn well that I won't give them that satisfaction dad. You probably want me to give up, don't you, you selfish bastard? Nothing I did was ever good enough for you, WAS IT?! Quickly standing up, the young Pendragon takes his fathers name from the front of the mantel, putting it inside of the candle holder. Gasping for air and filled with raw and intense emotion, he takes a bamboo stick from the holder and takes a light from another candle. Bringing the stick to the candle wick, Pendragon lights the candle, engulfing his father's name in the process. He then slowly takes the candle out of its holder, looking at it with a grin. His flourescent red eyes look upon it, and then direct toward the brick wall in front of him. And then, he hauled the candle, breaking the glass against the wall with a scream of utter passion. The candle snufs out on the floor, and the church bells toll again as Pendragon breathes heavily, looking at the chaos he has just put forth. So much pain and suffering was running through his body at that very moment, it wasn't hard to pinpoint how he released it in such a fashion. He looks at the camera with an intense passion in his eyes, this was the man we knew best. This was the man that promised to change every place he stepped into for the better. This was the man who had been involved with every hardcore stipulation, even the ones that people couldn't imagine even in their wildest of dreams. I feel so much pain from the past running through me, but it feels so good. I feel like I'm alive again, with a new start to things. Call me crazy, but I missed this feeling from within. The past couple of weeks, I haven't been watching out for myself, and it almost ended up costing me the tag match, along with much respect from the fans around me. I went through soap operas like some sort of old lady, and went through trust class like some sort of self-absorbed freak. But now that it's all over with, I feel like I can accomplish anything. All these criticisms have been floating around me over the weeks. People thinking that I'm not worthy enough to be carrying around the THC title, thinking that maybe I'm a little... emotional right about now to be worried about defending it, maybe even thinking that I'm... a wuss? Well let me tell you all one thing and one thing only, I'm definitely not one of those, I'm far from it. You all talk big, like you were made for Hardcore, and like you were made to wear this title around your waist. Well, let me tell you another thing. This belt was given to me because I deserved it, I didn't go around saying I was the best, I proved it in the ring. I'm still undefeated, I didn't go around bragging about it, I just proved that I'm a force to be reckoned with in the ring. All of this talk is going nowhere, and I just revert to silence, doing what I do best, and that's proving myself worthy to be with this belt and against these types of people. It seems like you are all out for blood, well here's another fact for you to pay attention to. It's hard to get blood from a stone, and even harder to get blood from me. I've bled too much in my life to worry about a little scratch from a chair, I don't even flinch when I see you hoisting that hammer into your palm. You claim to be the best at the stipulation, well then you just come into Supremacy and you prove it to me. Shut up your lips, and step up your technique. I'll be waiting for you when you are ready to start making something of yourself, and when you are ready to stop boasting about how quickly you got to this step. The fire burns within me, can't you see that? It's a fire from rages of my past, and it can't be extinguished, not by anyone. But why should I tell you? I'd rather you experience it first hand for yourselves, on Supremacy, when the three of us do battle. Bring whatever weapons you want to bring, they won't even get a chance to be used. Because the second you turn your back to get them, you will end up just like this candle on the ground. Snuffed out of existence, and placed back at the bottom of the ladder... The scene quietly fades to black as Iron Lung stares into the camera lens, his flourescent red eyes are like fireballs exploding into his opponents' hearts. Will they take the hint that he isn't messing with them anymore? Or will he need to send them another uninvited wakeup call?
