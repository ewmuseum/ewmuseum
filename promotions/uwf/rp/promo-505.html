6 Jul 2004

Angel — Road Side Diner & Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://mysite.wanadoo-members.co.uk/raynus2781/angelrp3.htm For a colourful look on the RP choose the above link :) ----------------------------------------------------------- The scene opens in a courtyard; a tall dark man is standing chatting to another smaller more elderly figure. The elderly man is dressed in a dark long robe oriental in style. The tall dark man in wearing a long black and white clock with intricate symbols on the arms and down the back, the two men seem to be talking to each other about various things. The elderly man walks over to the bench and calls the tall dark man to sit next to him. The elderly man picks out something from his robe and shows it to the tall dark man. Yuni: This thing you see in my hand is known as the Snakes Fang, it is an amulet that is believed to have mystic powers of long life. Simply by wearing it and with a small incantation from myself your life will be made much longer than any living man. We need to you to carry on the legacy, but I must warn you if you are injured and die…you will revive but have little knowledge of your former life or your training. So it is imperative you must not die, if you do you lose all knowledge of you martial arts and also what your task is. The tall dark man sits silent for a few moments scratching the stubble on his face. Dark Man: I accept your offer Yuni; I must continue the legacy of our cause to stop this world falling into the hands of evil. I cannot fail the world depends on it. Yuni: You are most correct, evil cannot be let to cover this world and it cannot be allowed to rule us. It would send this world in a destructive spiral that no one, not even you could turn back. Dark Man: But what if I die? There has to be someway of reminding me of my task. Yuni: There will be, they will live in the shadows keeping watch of you. They too will live as long as you. The dark man nods to the old man, the dark man walks out of the courtyard and into the main building. Just as the dark man leaves a young lady walks up to Yuni. Oni: Do you think the Dark Man can fulfil his task? Yuni: I think he can the world depends on him remembering, if he dies I only hope the person I chosen to watch The Dark Man can train him well again in the our sacred form of Kung Fu. Oni: If he cannot or the watcher dies, we are truly lost. Yuni: No, there is another. [Scene Fades] [Scene Opens] The scene opens in a small roadside diner, Angel is sitting at one of the tables lost in a daydream. Angel holding a coffee mug in has had but has long since gone cold. A waitress walks up to Angel. Waitress: So honey, do you want a refill? Angel still sits silently staring on the coffee cup Waitress: Sir? Angel doesn’t move and so the waitress nudges him. Waitress: Sir? Angel jumps and looks up at the waitress. Angel: What? Oh…sorry I must have been daydreaming…what can I do for you? Waitress: Lost in a pleasant thought eh? I was asking if you wanted a refill. Angel: Oh yeah, sure that would be nice. The waitress replaces the old cup with a new freshly filled one. Waitress: So…was it a pleasant thought? Angel: What was? Waitress: Your daydream. Angel: Yes, I guess it was. The waitress smiles and walks off just as Ayami enters the diner. Ayami notices Angel sitting down by the window and walks over to him. Ayami: Found you at last! I was looking all over for you. Angel smiles Angel: Just fancied to sit and relax before another bout of training with you. Your are a hard trainer I can tell you…was warn out by 2pm yesterday. Ayami: Well as long as you are in shape then you cannot lose. It is imperative that we continue to win. The more we win the higher up that ladder we get, higher up the ladder the closer we get to those people who walk the path of evil. Angel: Those people I must either turn…or destroy. Ayami: I understand it is hard; it would be nice to change people’s evil ways. But unfortunately not all can be changed and they are too far-gone…this being the case you simply must destroy them. Angel: Isn’t that a bit drastic? Ayami puts her hands on Angel’s Ayami: I know lets just hope that situation never does arise. Angel: Anyway, lets move on. I promised to continue that interview so we best make our way to the Gym. Ayami: I think that at the moment is the best course of action. [Scene Fades] [Scene Opens] The scene opens in the UWF Gym, Angel is again sparing with Ayami when the interviewer enters the Gym. Angel notices the interviewer and signals to Ayami to stop. Angel walks over to the interviewer and sits down on one of the benches. Interviewer: Angel, glad you could make it, so ready for more questions? Angel: Yes I am. Interviewer: Excellent, ok well first question what is your response to Adien Pryde’s promotion? Angel: I wasn’t aware he had said anything. Interviewer: Really? Well let me play the little bit he said about you. The interviewer holds up a tape recorder and presses play. “Angel and Green Demon. Heaven and hell. I guess that puts me in purgatory. Why the UWF put me in this match I will never know, but come Wednesday at Underground, I will use these two to start my rampage to the top of this federation. Some would call them stepping stone on my way up but I prefer to call the cannon fodder to be obliterated for others to see how dangerous I can be.” The interviewer stops the tape. Interviewer: So what is your response? Angel: If he thinks I would lie down to let a man of evil win, he has another thing coming. I have to win, it is my destiny and no one is going to stop me fulfilling what I have come here to do. Adien will be pushed aside with little or no thought effort on my behalf. I respect no man who walks the path of evil a man who would rather cheat to win than do it using his own wrestling ability. It sickens me. Interviewer: So you have no doubts that you will win tomorrow? Angel: None at all, I have trained for 4 years and I am trained in the ancient arts of Kung Fu and Tae Kwon Do. Adien is merely taught in the ancient art of cheating, it is most dishonourable. Interviewer: Ok, so after this match what are your plans? Angel: Like I said before, I am going to rise through the ranks and at each match I will either turn those who are evil…or destroy them. I have no alternative to destroy those souls who are truly lost to the evil cause. Interviewer: It sounds like you are almost on some kind of quest. Angel: I am. Interviewer: Well I think that is all I need for now, I might return to see how you are after your match. Until then, I thank you. Angel: It’s been a pleasure. The interviewer stands up and walks out with the cameraman following behind, Ayami walks up to Angel and sits next to him noticing that something is troubling Angel. Ayami: What is the matter? Angel: It’s ok…I just sometimes feel myself getting…overly angry. Ayami: Everyone has anger; it is how we deal with it that matters. You release that anger at someone innocent that makes you evil. You release that anger at someone like Adien it is ok, you cannot hold back your anger because it will explode one day and you may harm someone innocent. You can still remain good…but you must not bottle away your feelings believing those are evil…it is what makes us human. How we deal with our feelings is what makes us either good…or evil. Angel smiles Angel: You explain things wonderfully…how could I ever be without you? Ayami: That is why I am you mentor…I am here to make sure you fulfil your task. I am here to be your wife. Angel kisses Ayami on the cheek. Angel: I know. [Scene Fades]
