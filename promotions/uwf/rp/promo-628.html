UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #628

Who's fault is it?

14 Aug 2004

Ezra Kazee — The Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a work out facility. The facility is fairly empty, and there is very little activity going on. The camera pans around and focuses in on one single solitary individual. That individual is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing long sweats and is sweating profusely. H slows down the treadmill that he is running on to a stop and gets off of it, sitting on a nearby bench with a bottle of water, which he takes a big drink from. He sets the bottle down and looks at the camera.) Kazee: We are just a few days away from Regal Rumble. We are just a few days from one of the biggest events of the year. The entire UWF roster will be participating. The entire UWF roster has a place on this card, and from top to bottom, this looks like a huge event. One that the UWF should be proud of…… The main event of course is the Regal Rumble. The match that everyone is coming to see. The match that everyone is waiting to see who wins, who comes out on top, and who might possibly eliminate “The Renegade” Rich Rollins. Everyone is talking about that match, and well they should….. But there is another match people are talking about. There is another match that people are excited to see and are talking about with as much gusto as anyone in the world can. The match is the first of its kind. The match is going to make a huge difference in somebody’s life. The match is quite possibly the biggest match that I have ever wrestled in my entire career…… The match, of course, is the UWF World Title Match. The match, a 4-way Ultimate Submission in a Cell match. An extremely unique match for an extremely unique situation. To make the match even more unique, the match will have two referees who will be licensed for one night only in Nick Persia and one of the Tropical Storm members. This match is going to be a defining moment for someone……. So let’s be honest here for a moment, shall we? This is my first World Title shot here in the UWF. This is the first time I have ever had the shot at becoming the World Champion. What a great honor this is to be. What a great opportunity it is for me to be in this match…… (Kazee grabs a towel and starts wiping his brow, and takes another drink of water.) I’ve been successful here in the UWF. I’ve held several titles here. I’ve been the TV Champion, I’ve held the IC Champion, and with Mike Montgomery, I’ve held the Tag Team Championship. The North American Championship I came this close to winning, but ran into a buzz saw that was White Wolf. The Contest of Champions I almost one, except for the obstacle that is Ruff Ryder. I have never had a shot at the UWF THC Championship, or the Shoot Title. And yet another one of my biggest accomplishments was being on the winning side with The Players Club in winning Stable Wars. But this is my first World Title Shot…… To say that I’m nervous would be an understatement. The butterflies are flying around so much I can almost taste them. And outside of my training regimen and dietary regimen, I have eaten very little. I’m nervous, and I have good reason to be, don’t I? I am in the ring with some of the greatest competitors to ever grace the UWF ring……. We have Kevin Starr. A great competitor in his own right. He is a living legend who has accomplished some great things in the UWF. He has been up and down these roads and knows what he is doing in the ring. He has held several titles and has been to the big dance on numerous occasions to win the belt. An accomplished competitor who is definitely going to be a tough competitor…… We have Mike Montgomery. Former World Champion, a man who I know quite a bit about. A man who was quite possibly unfairly stripped of his belt thanks to an injury, and deserves a shot to regain it. A man who can make great things happen in that ring and is truly deserving of being back there and making waves again. Another tough competitor who I have all respect for and who could very easily regain the belt…… And then we have Joshua Dunn. The man that I have lost almost all respect for. Along with Kevin Starr, a man who has no scruples and will change things without authority. That World Title match should have never taken place. You changed the rule book. You and Kevin. You two are both great competitors, and don’t get me wrong, it was a great match, but in the end, you had no right to and should be disciplined with extreme prejudice…… (Kazee takes another drink of water and stares off into the distance.) A lot of people are upset because I filed the lawsuit. A lot of people are upset that I would even go to such measures. Fact of the matter is that I was driven to do it. I have had enough of the cheating and enough of the unscrupulous bastards here in this federation. And it starts right at the top with Joshua Dunn. You had no reason to do that. You could have won the title by yourself. But no. You had to go and cheat and lie and make things happen yourself. This is so unlike a Communist……. But it’s ok. You just helped me get exactly what I wanted in this match. You just helped me make this match to my advantage. You helped me make my first ever World Title match the type of match I would want to compete for it in. I mean what a great stipulation for me. Ultimate Submission. Hell in a Cell. No one gets in, no one gets out. And an iron man match where only submissions can count as falls. Even better. Or do I have to remind you of my prowess of wrestling knowledge….. Apparently though you have forgotten about my prowess though Mr. Dunn. Apparently you have forgotten about my back ground in the ways of the amateur and the ways of technicality. I don’t call myself the Technical Spectacle or the Definition of Precision, because I don’t have to. But I have studied this sport inside and out. I have forgotten more moves than most people ever will know, and I’m still learning more. Submission moves are just one area of my repertoire, probably my strongest area…… I could throw a Crippler Cross face on you, or I could put you in the Texas Cloverleaf, or Regal Stretch even. There are Eastern Stretches, Octopuses, and Spikes I can put you in. My knowledge is limitless in this one…… I could even pull out moves like a ring post figure-four, or a sleeper hold using the Microphone card. It’s all legal. And I may have to use it to my advantage. Do not forget about my roots, Mr. Dunn. Do not forget where I came from and where my background is…… (Kazee steps back up onto the treadmill after taking another drink of water.) I’m prepared for a great match, which I’m sure I will have. I am prepared to have a great challenge and I am prepared for this match to be everything that it should be. Grueling, painful, and exhausting. So many adjectives could be used to describe it. But in the end, this match is what I want, and this match is your entire fault Mr. Dunn. Next time, think about the consequences of your actions before you make them. Good Luck, Mr. Dunn, Mr. Starr, and Mr. Montgomery. You’re entering into my world, and you’re going to need it. (Kazee turns on the treadmill as the scene fades to black.)
