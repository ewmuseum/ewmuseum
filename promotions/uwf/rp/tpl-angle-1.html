Trailer Park Luchador — Angle #1

5 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The lights in the arena get really fucking bright. Because turning them off is soooooo 90's.] Larson: “What!? Why ... no! Blinded! Argh!” Rick: “You're going to use this as an excuse to 'accidentally' stumble into Tigera, aren't you?“ Artist: Usher Song: Superstar This is for you, you, my number one This is for you, you, my number one This is for you, you, my number one “Arrrrrrrrrrrrrrriba!!” Larson: “I have a bad, bad feeling about this.” Spotlights, big stage Fifty thousand fans screamin' in a rage Bodyguards and limousines This is the way I see you in my dreams Rick: “Like the one you get before some bad guy comes down and beats up the person in the ring?” Larson: “Like the one you get right before a car accident that kills a carpool to preschool.” Paparazzi flash, hundred pictures all of you Hangin' on my bedroom wall I'm a kid again, I feel like thirteen But I knew since we fell in love Girl I'd be [The crowd sits on their hands, horrified by the music, intrigued by what it means …] [Right up until HE arrives.] [Chorus] I'll be your groupie baby Cuz you are my superstar I'm your number one fan, give me your autograph Sign it right here on my heart Girl I'll be your groupie baby Cuz you are my superstar And as your number one fan I'll do all that I can To show you how super you are [The Trailer Park Luchador comes bouncing out of the backstage area. The loudest groan in wrestling history erupts from the audience.] Front row, there I am Jumpin' and hollerin' waving full fledge Would you notice me, it'll be Drove twelve hours girl just to see Your pretty face one more time Bought my ticket I was first in line This is a metaphor to show how I adore (I adore you) Baby I do [The Luchador throws his arms wide and begins spinning on the top of the entrance ramp.] [Chorus] I'll be your groupie baby Cuz you are my superstar I'm your number one fan, give me your autograph Sign it right here on my heart Girl I'll be your groupie baby Cuz you are my superstar And as your number one fan I'll do all that I can To show you how super you are [The Decimator from the Double-Wide comes sprinting down the rampway toward the ring.] Now you know how I feel You're truly special Your love is legendary to me Without you my life stands still I'll never leave your world Treat you like a diva girl Girl you're one of a kind Cuz they don't make 'em like you anymore So you'll be your fans life I dedicate this to my superstar for all time [As the music cuts, the Trailer Park Luchador spins and bows to the audience.] TPL: “Thank you!!!” [What fans haven’t already left to buy hotdogs groan.] TPL: “Oh screw you guys.” [The Trailer Park Luchador leaps to the top rope, preparing for a high impact move of incredible pain!] Rick: “What is … ?” [The hero of a three block radius in southern Wisconsin turns, squaring up to Tigera, and leaps off!] Rick: “Why is he attacking Tigera?!” [White Tiger is yelling at his masked comrade-in-arms to get down and stop acting like an idiot.] [The Luchador of the Trailer Parks spins … he twirls … he flips! A cascading, flip-flopping, move of incredible pain rains death down upon Tigera in the center of the ring …] [… except that Tigera spins, shoots one open palm forward, and slams it into the diving attacker’s groin.] [The Legend of the Landfill drops like a sack of shit. Tigera shakes her head and starts kicking him.] Larson: “He just can’t do anything right.” Rick: “It would actually be kind of endearing if it wasn’t so sad, you know?” [Tigera has begun using the Luchador’s own hands to slap the piss out of him.] Rick: “What was the point of this? Really?” [White Tiger is stalking up and down the side of the ring, shaking his head as he watches Tigera just pummel her would-be attacker.] Larson: “Guess what, Tiger? This is what happens when you pick your friends based on the first three people who talk to you. Especially when those three were banned from the arena for urinating in the Gatorade …” Rick: “I think we’re getting ready to cut to a commercial break … Tigera seems to be only half-heartedly pummeling him now, and …” [Tigera doesn’t even see the superkick from White Tiger coming.] Rick: “And White Tiger couldn’t stand to see his friend being pummeled anymore! The Masked Security Detail has taken over against the newcomer!” Larson: “But … never mind. I don’t care.” [The Trailer Park Luchador, holding his groin and groaning in agony, still manages to crawl quickly across the floor, grabbing one of Tigera’s arms before she has a chance to recover from the kick. With the quickness of a rat, the Luchador whips out a pair of handcuffs … seconds later, Tigera is desperately trying to pull her arm away from the top rope as the Luchador backs out of reach.] Rick: “He’s … what in the hell is he doing!?” Larson: “This is officially the weakest, worst-performed heel turn in wrestling history.” [The Trailer Park Luchador screams for a microphone to be tossed to him.] Rick: “He’s going to talk.” Larson: “Great. And for an encore, I’m going to shit myself and describe it’s aroma for fifteen minutes.” [A microphone is thrown into the ring. It hits the Luchador in the head, but the Demon of the Doublewide quickly recovers and scoops it up.] TPL: “A-HAH~!” [While the Luchador is still trying to stand up, White Tiger shoves him, screaming out ‘What do you think you’re doing?!’] TPL: “Dude … shhh. Don’t sweat it … it’s a girl! I can beat a girl.” [White Tiger considers hitting him, but he throws his hands in the air and just leaves. The hero of the mobile home community turns his attention to his ‘victim.’] TPL: “Miss Tigera … I am the man, the myth, the … umm … man … the Trailer … Park … Luchador!” [TPL flexes.] TPL: “You might be asking yourself … ‘What is this perfect specimen of manliness; this legend of the wrestling world; this phenomenon with the power of a million suns bothering with little old me?!’ And the answer to that would be …” [TPL frowns.] TPL: “I mean … it’s because I’m a bad guy now! I am the Trailer Park Luchador … and I am the evilest bastard that ever gosh darn lived!” [TPL approaches Tigera, but stays out of arm’s reach.] TPL: “Grrrrrrr!” [Suddenly, the Luchador’s eyes go wide, and he points into the crowd.] TPL: “Holy poop! What is THAT?!” [Tigera stops trying to kick him in the head and turns her attention to the audience. The Luchador dives forward, and slaps another pair of handcuffs on her and the ropes with the eerie speed of a cat!] TPL: “AHA!!! Well guess what, Tigera? I know all about you! And you know what … I don’t like you! You smell, your clothes are all sorts of ugly, and … I mean … the hair? Hello? Pippi Longstocking called, and she wants her … wait …” [The Luchador fishes inside of his tights and pulls out a dozen notecards. He begins desperately shuffling through them, frowns, and throws them over the ropes.] TPL: “Never mind. But like I was saying, I know allllllll about you! And you know what I heard? Big, bad Tigera doesn’t like being touched. That’s so sad. I would never try to invade your person space …” [The Trailer Park Luchador pokes Tigera in the shoulder.] TPL: “Psyche!!! I’m a bad guy now! Touched you, touched you, nanny-nanny-boo-boo!” [The Trailer Park Luchador begins rapidly poking Tigera.] TPL: “Touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch touch …” [Tigera is growling.] TPL: “… touch touch touch touch touch touch tou—“ [Tigera rifles a kick into the Luchador’s testicles. TPL’s eyes roll into the back of his head, showing the whites of his eyes. His knees buckle together. And he passes out.] Rick: “Dear sweet lord in heaven.” Larson: “I swear to Rod … Stee was a better bad guy than him.” Rick: “I mean … well … he’s trying?” Larson: “That’s real encouraging.” Rick: “Well … White Tiger is dragging the Luchador to the back, and the referees are freeing Tigera, so I guess we’re all done here.”
