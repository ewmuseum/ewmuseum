RP#2 for ETD

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s amazing what a small revelation will do, how clear the mind can become, the focus, the grit and the determination. This had happened to John Edwards, yes John Edwards, no longer feeling the need to hide under the moniker, which had kept his identity for four years. Much like the Universal Wrestling Federation John Edwards was going through a resurrection. He had discarded the past, the shackles which it had over him. As far as John was concerned, Moraless was dead. Sure, he would still continue to use the name in the ring, but it wasn’t going to be him. In the past, he avoided using his name, he even considered legally changing it to Moraless, but that didn’t really work out too well. When it came down to it, in the past, John didn’t exist. It was simply a birth name, the dark and tragic past of John had become too much, he wanted to black it out so hid from his existence. The thing is he couldn’t hide now, with the destruction of Moraless, came the rebirth of John Edwards and everything that came with him. It was all very well saying that he was ok, but there was still something that he needed to do, a final step towards normality, if there is such a thing. The cobbles underneath his feet cracked as he walked down the path. His chest felt tight and the air intake was restricted, it didn’t matter he knew he had to do this. It was the one thing remaining. The one thing, which caused him, pain, grief about the past. It was the one thing that he had denied all of these years, blocked out. The bees were buzzing in and around the flowers, which decorated the garden either side of the path. What with it being spring, the assortment of smells and colours were beginning to flourish, beginning their life anew for just a few seasons before hiding from the cold harsh winter. John paused his walk and looked around what could only be described as a small corner of paradise. There was a small pond, fish swimming about in it with a small fountain trickling into it. Directly in front of John though was what he had been looking for and yet hiding from for a long time. Stepping forwards he approached a large yellow rose bush. The smell attacked John’s sinuses and made him smile, memories came flooding back to him, memories of home and his childhood. He knelt silently in front of the rose bush, and bowed his head, his eyes finding something just in front of his knees. Underneath the flowers was a small wooden plaque, no bigger than a piece of A4 paper, a dark mahogany, which whilst showing the effects of time, maintained its beauty. His heart was now beating so rapidly it felt as though it would burst his chest. He felt lost, confused, not knowing how to react. He had blocked it away for so long, not acknowledging the truth. A single solitary tear fell and splashed off the mahogany as John traced his fingers over the engraved words. ‘In loving memory of Sarah and Robert Edwards, taken away in their prime. You will never be forgotten’ John’s head dropped as the tears began to flow like wine. He had forgotten them, forgotten his parents as a result of this person he had become, this Moraless. A character, who he had always hidden behind. He never expected him to do this though; he didn’t want Moraless to cause this. Sniffing deeply, John wiped the tears on the sleeve of his jacket, his eyes bloodshot trying to fight back the tears. Somewhere in the garden a bird began to sing it’s spring song, the wind increased to a light breeze, gently massaging John’s short black hair. It was as though nature had a connection with this time and place, it was as though someone didn’t want John to be sad, not here. This was a place of remembrance that is why John had come here in the first place, to restore some memory, to give a better indication of the past. The tears were a good sign in that respect, the emotion showed that feeling was coming back, thoughts and memories all cascading through John’s brain. “Hi” the words were forced from John’s throat squeaking slightly as he did so, his throat was dry and crying always led to high-pitched levels. “I don’t really know what to say, I… I just know that I have to do something, say something. I… I was so lost. I forgot who I was. I forgot you, but that’s changing I promise I know who I am now. This place is so beautiful, I…I just hope I’m remembered this well when, well when it happens. Who keeps this place so tidy?” “That would be me, John” John was cut off by the sound of a voice he knew well, he didn’t turn around and simply remained sat looking at the plaque below him. James Edwards kneeled down next to John and turned to look at him, his blond hair blowing in the wind as he also lowered to look at the plaque. “It’s hard the first time isn’t it, as an adult I mean. We were bought here as children, but we didn’t really understand it then, not the true significance, it’s true beauty. I’m glad you came.” James spoke, his words were far away from the goofy demeanour that he played in RPW, mostly because this was something that he was deadly serious about. “Yeah, it’s hard it really is, I mean what do I do, what do I say?” spoke John turning to his brother, a man who under the guise of Moraless he had taught himself to hate, his own flesh and blood who had always been there no matter how much of a dick he was to him. “You don’t really have to say anything John, this is a garden of remembrance, its what mum and dad wanted. If you have something to say, you can say it here. A lot of the time I don’t say a thing, it’s just nice to sit and watch the flowers, the fish and just think back. Make sure they aren’t forgotten.” James looked at John sympathetically, he could tell that he was hurting, he placed a reassuring hand on John’s shoulder and squeezed it gently. John smiled and let out a small breath, James had always known how to reassure John, whenever hr was in trouble as a child, it always seemed to be James who would sort it out for him, he had to, there was nobody else around. “I know it must have been hard for you to come here today John, but can I ask, why did you?” I know that you have bigger fish to fry at the moment. A certain Mr McClancy you have to deal with, as well as defending your tag team championship. I mean I’m hardly in a position to criticise your working methods, but shouldn’t you be training or something?” James asked his younger brother, releasing his hand from his shoulder as he did so. John, the artist formally known as Moraless, rose to his feet and turned away from the plaque. He walked towards the small pond, the trickling water soothing his ears and simply stood there gazing into the crystal clear fluid, watching the fish swim effortlessly. “Sean, yeah. That was always going to be a tricky one. I never thought I would be able to get this far, to be able to pose a legitimate challenge to a champion, not the calibre of McClancy anyway. Yet I don’t know, I think I just might be able to do this one. There are two things, which may just swing things in my favour. James had now also raised to his feet, taking care not to disturb any of the flowers, the centrepiece in the garden of remembrance. He moved over to where his younger brother was and began to tend to some lily’s to the side of the pond, James listened as John continued to talk. “Its just that it doesn’t seem like McClancy has paid any attention to exactly what is going on, he doesn’t seem to have followed me, scouted out what I do. He believes that match is a construction yard brawl, it’s much more than that, it’s ACWF. Asphalt, chains, weapons and forklifts. ACWF isn’t just about construction, it carries a legacy behind it. This will be ACWF IV, the previous three held at my old playing grounds of GCW. At the end of the day, if you are able to walk out of ACWF then you have already more than won. Don’t get me wrong, I know that McClancy is tough, tougher than me in fact, but I don’t think he is ready for this. He appears to be taking it far too light heartedly. I mean, Jason O’ Neil barely walked out of one of these matches alive, do I really have a chance. James had turned and stopped pruning at the mention of Jason O’ Neil. It was only now that he realised the severity of the match that John had placed himself in. In RPW, Jason O’Neil was simply put a hard as nails bastard and if he had barely survived…the thoughts started dashing through his head, fearful for his little brother and the damage that he was about to endure. “O’Neil? O’Neil barely got out of one of these, Christ John what were you thinking? Why not just go for a ladder match or hell, that submissions match with Vendetti worked out well for you, why do something this crazy if you don’t think you’re up for it. It’s just a way of shortening your career. John’s eyes remained focused on the fish in the pond, a gust of air sent ripples flying through the water as he slowly raised his head, looked at his brother and smiled. “It’s who I am James, I know that I’m not Moraless anymore, I know where to draw the line. The fact of the matter is though, that I was still him for a long, long time. He has influenced me. This maybe my one shot that I actually get at this title. Should I lose, there is no way its going to be in a bog standard ladder match, a match which has been killed repeatedly. I wanted something special, hard hitting, something that people would be able to look back on in the future and go ‘ACWF IV…damn that was special’. John continued to smile at James who had begun to smile back, James knew that John was always going to be the showman, it had always been that way, time doesn’t really change all that much when you think about it. “And besides” continued John “he doesn’t even know who I am, not really. That’s the other thing that I think will give me the edge. All he really knows about me is that I work for the UWF, other than that I may as well be a blank page to him. I mean get this, McClancy actually thought that people fear me, he actually went and said ‘I’m beginin’ t’ think tha’ Moraless thinks tha’ I’m afraid o’ ‘im.” There was silence, just for a second. John was totally deadpan, as was James. Then it happened. You could see James’ face begin to twitch upwards, as did John’s. Then as though on cue the two brothers laughed, laughed until their lungs were sore. It could have been the horrendous Irish accent that John had just attempted, but the truth was that they were laughing at the comment. “Really? He actually said that, out loud…to other people?” enquired James, who was looking extremely flustered, trying to regain his breath. John simply nodded, the sheer lunacy of the idea, that John would ever think anyone would be scared of him, had become far too much to believe. Moraless was the man who walked tall held his head high. John Edwards just a guy with low self-esteem, he’s scared of wasps for Christ sake. John stopped smiling and looked around the garden once more, it truly was a miraculous thing, this garden had bought together the two brothers, a common bond shared between them, even though it was not the happiest in the world, it was something which connected them. “How do I do it James? How do I tap into Moraless, but at the same time not allow myself to be taken over again. I know that as I am now, I can’t beat McClancy, not confidently anyway. I still want Mory, to be a part of me, but not to complete me.” Spoke John, his brow now furrowed as his mind searches for answers. James smiled once more at his brother, before turning and beginning to walk away from him. “You already have John, if you didn’t have control you wouldn’t be here now, you told me yourself earlier. Moraless will always be a part of you, but you also said you know where to draw the line. When you go out into that match it will be you. Moraless maybe the name headlining the bill, but John Edwards will be the one fighting. You do what you have to do man. I’ll see you soon, I’m not wishing you good luck, because it’s all in your hands. “Wait you’re leaving?” asked John, clearly shocked by the not so grand revelation. “Yeah, have to head back to North Carolina, meeting up with one of your old friends in fact, Lord Skywolf.” James grinned as he spoke, a large beaming smile covered John’s face as his eyes appeared to just light up. “Jobberwolf? Jobberwolf is still around, say hi for me ok,” John said, with a certain amount of bounce in his voice, possibly a little too enthusiastic. “Sure, I’ll say hi…or something like that,” replied James, who waved to his brother and walked up the cobbled path leaving John and the garden alone. John glanced around the garden one more time before returning to the rosebush. He knelt down and read the plaque once more. There he remained, silent, not needing to say a thing. Life would always continue long after John was gone, life would always flourish new generations thrown into this life head first not knowing where to turn, but in essence that’s what life is. If we knew what to do with it, if we knew where we were headed in our existence there would be no fun, life would be dull and monotonous. Destiny is everywhere, you cannot help but face it head on, because that’s destiny, it’s unavoidable and you don’t want to spend your life running away.
