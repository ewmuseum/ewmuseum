UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #239

Don't underestimate CHAOS

25 Feb 2004

Anarchy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to in the ring just after the last SAS card. Anarchy and Joanna there] Joanna: Again Mike Montgomery acts before he thinks. After contacting the makers of the Bug Bomb 2000 and showing them the video of Raids last extermination. They are assuming the responsibility of the fire because Raid did everything according to the instructions. So again Montgomery has caused the UWF money that is didn't need to pay. Raid has been released and is pissed. Now on too the Prime Alliance. Dai: Ruff Rider, I heard you say that we will be cake. You should never underestimate you opponents, especially when you have never seen us in action. Pryde: We have proven many times in other feds that we are the best and we will do it again here. We are Tag-Team specialist and will walk over you. After you we are coming after the gold. Player's Club, you may have weaseled your way into those titles, but we are coming to get them. Dai: Yea, and after Raid captures the THC title we will prove that the Chaos Society is the top stable in all of wrestling, not Prime Alliance or Player's Club, just CHAOS. [Scene fades to black]
