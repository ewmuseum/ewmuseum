UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #479

Stable Wars is Mine!

28 Jun 2004

Kevin Starr — Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Guess who’s got an extra ticket to the Sox-Yanks game the day after Stable Wars?” [We find ourselves in Kevin’s living room where we see the one the only, Kevin Starr wearing a muscle shirt exposing his perfect biceps, and ooohh… look at those deltoids… Perfection!] [Anyway, he’s sitting up on the black leather recliner perfectly placed inside of his parlor waiving the 2 tickets to the Red Sox-Yankees game on Thursday in his best friend Mike Michael’s face.] Kevin: “So you coming?” [Kevin Starr, with the biggest grin in the history of grins on, begins to kiss one side of the ticket; hopefully he's doing it to show the importance of it.] [Mike is still debating the offer however. He’s scheduled for a House Show for some other crappy federation because he wasn’t good enough to get a contract extension with the UWF.] Mike: “I don’t know, man. I’m scheduled for a match the same night as Stable Wars so I won’t even be able to make the trip out.” [Kevin takes his now chapped, yet still perfect lips off of the game ticket in shock. How could any man in America pass up the opportunity to see the Red Sox beat the crap out of the Yankees in their own stadium? It’s Un-American!] Kevin: “…Umm… wh-what?” Mike: “I can’t go, I’ve got a match.” [Kevin’s jaw slowly drops, he starts thinking to himself, wondering if he asked the right question. Wondering if can’t is just a metaphor for can in this case. Wondering why the hell Mike has a match… he sucks…] Kevin: “WHAT?! You don’t understand Mike! This fits perfectly well with our plans for the upcoming week. On Wednesday I’ll win Stable Wars, and then on Thursday we can catch the Red Sox Yankees game. Still giving me enough time to prepare for a match on SAS. It fits in perfectly well with our schedule!” [Kevin pleads with Mike, trying to get him to come with him to New York. However, Kevin’s little speech seemed to have no effect on Mike Michael what-so-ever.] Mike: “No Kevin, your schedule. Not mine. Listen, this match is important to me. I could finally get one of those contract thingies you have. Listen, just get one of your other friends to go.” [Kevin’s jaw slowly begins to drop down forming an ‘O’ shape with his mouth.] Kevin: “Mike you su-“ Mike: “I can’t go! Just get one of your other friends to go. You do have other friends now do you? [Kevin let’s out a nervous laugh as he sits back in his recliner chair so that the foot rest swoops up.] Kevin: “Me… well of course I have other friends, tons in fact. I mean I’ve got so many friends it's not even funny. Mike: “Oh yeah?” [Mike leans in towards Kevin’s direction with an evilish grin on his face.] Mike: “Name one.” Kevin: “Well uh, there’s... there’s Joooee Barkin-gar-buddyish. Yeah, Joe Barkingarbuddyish. He’s from Germany.” Mike: “A REAL one.” Kevin: “Err…” [A bead of sweat starts to run down Kevin’s forehead as he looks into Mike’s determined eyes. Hey, don’t let Mike’s small figure fool you, he would make a great interrogator.] Kevin: “Umm…Mike Montgomery?” Mike: “You haven’t even said a word to Montgomery since he broke his leg! Not even a get well soon card. Nothing. That’s no friend.” [Kevin’s knees begin to shake as his lip quivers up and down. The pressure is beginning to have it’s effect on Kevin. He can’t take it anymore!] Kevin: “OKAY YOU GOT ME! I have no friends! But that doesn’t matter because I don’t need any! I mean look at how far I’ve gotten in the UWF without them! It’s not my fault that I have this perfect bone structure, this beautiful smile, cowlick free hair, a chiseled body, it’s not my fault!” [Kevin finally breaks out under the pressure. Mike on the other hand breaks out in a completely different way. He bursts out with an uncontrollable laughter, rolling back and forth on Kevin’s black leather couch.] [Laughing because this is the first time he’s ever gotten the better end on Starr. I mean, usually it’s Kevin making fun of Mike, but in this case it’s the complete opposite.] [Unfortunately for Mike, his happiness only lasts for a short time as a quick jab to the ribs by Kevin converts his laughter in to coughing.] Kevin: “Shut up Damnit! And let’s never speak of this moment again!’ [Kevin returns his fist to his side and looks over at Mike who is gasping for air after getting the wind knocked out of him.] Mike: *cough cough cough* “Damn it! Did you have to hit me, though! Oww! You should be saving it for Stable Wars!” Kevin: “You know, speaking of Stable Wars. This upcoming Stable Wars will be my 3rd one and I’m not going to let this one slip through my hands, no, not after what happened last year.' '...I remember it as if it happened just yesterday...' [The screen begins to ripple signaling a flashback to Stable Wars 5.] [Unfortunately, no record of Stable Wars 5 can be found on the site... seriously can we update the PPV page?! Couldn't find it under SAS's or from the promo's way back.] [Anyways it went something like this... Kevin Starr led his stable of nobody’s, the EoD, all the way to the Finals where they faced off against Par 4 Stroke 6 WACC, something like that. I think now's a good time to mention that Kevin Starr basically fought alone in this match, the Elements of Destruction were nothing without Kevin Starr. In fact he eliminated every wrestler in the final round!] [Man after man went down to Kevin Starr, until there were just 2 men- err... living things in the ring. Kevin Starr circled the ring, staring straight in to the eyes of a 3 ft tall prime ape. The two clashed for about 7 minutes, Kevin absolutely destroyed the prime ape, DESTROYED!!! Then, out of the corner of his eye, Kevin Starr saw it. He saw his chances of winning the tournament begin to slowly flutter away. He tried to move out of the way, but he couldn't. It's as if his feet were glued to the mat. He was unable to dodge the Firaga~! attack by the interfering Seymour Almasy. The fireball smacked Kevin’s face like a 95 mph fastball from Randy Johnson. Kevin’s knees gave way to the fatigue and pain as he fell to the mat… making it the end of the Cinderella story that was the Elements of Destruction.] [The scene slowly fades back into present day as we see Kevin Starr sitting forward, staring at the floor, going over that night again and again in his mind. If only he could go back and see Seymour running down the ramp, then maybe things would be different.] Kevin: “You know… in less then 30 seconds, I went from feeling the best I ever have in my life, to being so depressed that I was unable to get out of bed for the next 3 days.” Mike: “Don’t worry about it. Don’t let the past get to you, I mean you’re gonna win the Cup anyway in a few days.’ Kevin: “That’s what I said last year, Mike… and the year before that… and that’s what I said in my World Title match, but you know what happened each of those times Mike? I lost. I think I’m starting to realize something Mike. I think my overconfidence is blinding me from the truth, blinding me from the real skills of my opponents. Maybe instead of watching every little detail about myself, I should actually start watching my opponents and trying to learn from their strengths and weaknesses…” [A moment of silence passes after Kevin spills out his feelings… realizing that he should start to think of others instead of himself. Maybe even try to open himself up to the world…] Kevin: “Who am I kidding? I’m Kevin FREAKING STARR! The Greatest UWF SuperStarr of All-Time!” [Kevin begins to laugh at himself, wondering what the hell he was thinking. Mike joins in on the laugh.] Kevin: “I don’t need to do all that crap to win matches. I just need to strut down to the ring, look good, whoop some ass, 1-2-3, Victory!” Mike: “I’ll admit, you had me going there for a sec Kev.” Kevin: “Yeah well, when you’re facing a stable like the Chaos Society, there’s no need to prepare at all! I mean just who the hell are they? I can’t even name one of their member’s off the top of my head, I’d need the Stable Wars card to do that.” [Luckily for Kevin, Mike has a Stable Wars card in his hands as we speak.] Mike: “Well there’s Draco Paladine.” Kevin: “Who?” Mike: “Adien Pryde.” Kevin: “Who?” Mike: “Mystery Partner.” Kevin: “Who?” Mike: “Raid.” Kevin: “Who?” Mike: “Raid? Well he is the IC Champ.” Kevin: “I don’t give a crap who he is, all he is to me is another checkmark in the Win column. As far as I’m concerned this Chaos Society is a joke.” [Mike looks down the list of Stables on the card to find The Iron Curtain.] Mike: “Ah yes, the Iron Curtain.” Kevin: “Skip them… they’re not worth the 30 seconds of time that I’ll waste talking about them.” Mike: “Okay then… Chrono Cross~!” Kevin: “Okay, okay. A little competition never hurt anyone.” “I’ve only had the chance to meet Cpl. Punishment in the ring once in my career and that was way back when I was a rookie in a Triple Threat Match for the THC Title. If I remember correctly, Punishment came out on top of that one, ONLY because I was too involved in beating the crap out of the other guy in that match, who’s name I can’t recall. Apparently, Punishment is a legend here in the GIWF/UWF. But the only thing I see is a washed up old man who’s gone crazy. Seriously Cpl., you’re old news, now why don’t you do something worth your time and join a retirement home. You’re no good anymore, can’t you see through that thick head of yours? And frankly Corporal, I’m not looking forward to having my perfect body being in the same ring as your hairy disgusting THING that you call a torso!” “Then there’s Kenny Mercury, the man who started out Purple Haze. Haze defeated me once, but I remember that day well. My boots got misplaced with someone else’s and I didn’t find that out until midway through the match. See, the boots I was wearing during that match started to give me a blister on the back of my foot. So in fear of making it worse, I just laid down for the 1-2-3. It was well worth the loss though… have you ever tried walking around the house with a blister on your foot? It sucks! Anyway, I think Mercury could make a great wrestler here in the UWF if he would stop using one of MY catch phrases at the beginning of his promos! I AM THE RISING STARR! I was the fist to use it, not YOU! It’s mine! So leave it!” “Finally we have Seymour Almasy. The man who put me on the shelf for over 6 months. Almasy, do you understand how much your carelessness with a sword cost me in my life?! It took MONTHS of constant rehab and training to fully recover from losing the pinky on my right hand!” [Kevin lifts up his right hand to the camera, only 4 fingers and a small stub to were the pinky should be.] “Seymour, do you know how hard it is to use a spoon without a pinky? It took a month to do that right. Or how to tie your shoes or play Cat’s Cradle? What about pinky swearing? Huh? Do you? NO, you don’t! Because you still have all 10 fingers intact. As far as I’m concerned Seymour, that was a cheap shot to maintain your World Title! You knew if you didn’t do something about it that I would win the title.” “See Seymour, at Stable Wars I’m getting even. I’m not only going to break every last bone in your miniscule body, I’m going to ruin your chances at winning Stable Wars, just like you did to me a year ago.” Mike: “And finally, the last stable competing in the tournament, The Resurrection.” Kevin: “The favorite to win Stable Wars they say. They say that The Player’s Club has fallen from that spot after we lost Mike Montgomery. Hello? Are you nimrods forgetting something? Kevin Starr is still here. Still on the Player’s Club. Yep. Listen you retarded clowns known as The Resurrection, I hope that being the favorite of the tournament makes you feel good, because that’s the only thing that you’ll be able to tell your grandchildren, that’s if you have grand children because you’re all a bunch of ugly bastards, that you accomplished at Stable Wars 6, because The Player’s Club is winning the tournament!” “What the hell is The Resurrection anyway? You say you want to build the UWF back up to its former glory? Hell, the only way to do that would be with me standing in a UWF ring with the World Title draped over my shoulder. Then and only then, would all be right with the UWF again.” “I’ll admit though, you guys do have a pretty good team on your hands. I mean, I don’t know much about this Moraless, but I’ve heard some pretty good things about him. Something about being a PTC Extreme Champ or something. Then there’s Bondage I’ve heard plenty of good thin-… wait nevermind, I haven’t heard anything good about him at all.” “I know a bit about the one known as Malcolm Kennedy though. I remember him from his debut last summer and I remember thinking to myself, man, this kid really thinks he’s good enough to be in the same federation as Kevin Starr, pathetic. Fact is this kid has as much talent as my right pinky finger. None… at all, because I don’t physically have one, duh. Anyways, it’s pretty funny to hear this kid talk about Stable Wars though. Thinking that The Resurrection actually has a chance against me in Stable Wars. Honestly, Mac, is it alright if I call you Mac? Until you have had any kind of effect on this federation like I have, then you can talk, but until then, just sit at home and watch people like me win matches.” “Finally, we have Digital Mortality. Ever since I joined this federation he’s been there. We’ve met a countless number of times before in the past, with him getting some victories over me and me getting some over him in tag matches and rumbles. We’ve never met 1 on 1 though and that is something that I look forward to down the line in my UWF career. Hey, it could even happen at Stable Wars. Actually I hope it does happen, I hope that the Stable Wars Trophy comes down to just you and me. The only 2 men left out of the 24 that entered. That way I can enjoy watching blood trickle down from the top of your forehead after I SuperStarr Slam you for the 1-2-3.” “Digital, I know you have talent though, in fact, deep down inside a small part of me respects you. I mean, we’ve both been here since around the time that the UWF was formed back in the days when names like Shawn Eclipse and Deke Aurora headlined the Main Events and filled the seats. Now take a look around Digi, who is still here today going strong from back then? I mean there’s only a select few who’ve been around constantly… there’s you, me, Dunn, Montgomery, and that’s it really. Even when the UWF was going though some rocky times, we stuck with it and are still here today. So I just want to let you know, that when I eliminate you from the tournament, it will be my honor” *camera fades*
