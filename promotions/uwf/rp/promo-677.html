UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #677

dicky who?

7 Sep 2004

Dreammaker — parking lot outside of arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(the scene opens up outside the arena after sas and a losing 1st effort. caring his gym bag and looking low and reading a couple of pages in front of him, dreammaker didnt even notice the photagraphers waiting at the vehicles and walks right into the back of one) camera man: what the .. hey watch out ..were you ... you are the new guy right can i talk to ya? dm: whatever .. cm: how can you explain your loss tonight? dm: boy.. i didnt even see that coming. but they couldnt pin me.. had to pin other ones... but ill teach them .. ill find them and make sure they remmeber me.. Cm: ya, ya , ya it was a great try but what are you reading before you hit me? dm: some personal info about some chump named dicky flint?.. give me a break.. as far as i can tell.. he isnt even liked here.. let alone anywhere.. why should i waste my time on a loser? cm: well maybe you should prove your self to the world that you aint scared of a hated man. (as the camera man finshes his statement he recieves a closeline that knocks him right off his feet) dm: remember this i aint afraid of noone and i will prove it to you and to everyone.. as for dicky.. remember that you can run and you can hide but you always have to sleep.. and when your guard is down.. ill catch you napping and it will be lights out for you. !! (just as dreammaker finishes his statement to the world, other wrestlers start filing out and dreammaker picks up his bad and gets in his car and drives off)
