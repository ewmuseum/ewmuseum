Kenny Mercury — TV Land!

[Kenny Mercury! I chose you! GO~!!!!] [Everything is dark…. Suddenly, red lasers shoot out from the center of the vast nothing, emanating from the center. AND THERE HE IS! The face of Kenny Mercury appears in the center of the UWF loser redneck fan's television screen.] ***WAPSH!*** [Kenny Mercury's arm shoots out in front of him, he points at the viewers.] Mercury: “Listen up, imbeciles! This is none other than the Technical Wizard… The Ring Doctor… The Submission Exhibition… the Shooting Star… THE VERY DEFINITION OF PRECISION… Kenneth Mercury. So listen up, you poop-sickle- licking fuck-tards, because the man with more talent in his right pinky toe than in your whole body has something to say!” “On second thought… maybe I shouldn't say it… Why? BECAUSE, DOOFUS! It SHOULD be already understood. Understood… accepted… and preached… Oh screw it, I'll tell you, though you are soooooo totally unworthy of hearing it.” “Kenny Mercury…. He… I… … is… how should I say it… oh… the pinnacle of wrestling…. superior to everyone that ever was… ever is… and ever will be… the most talented… the most proficient…. The most… the most everything…. Kenny Mercury…. He… I… is an awesome force, unlike any other ever seen…. Or even IMAGINED! Kenny Mercury is a God amongst poops… Poops that really suck… well, everyone is a poop compared to Kenny Mercury… but especially everyone in UWF!” “And…. He… me… will be proving this FACT at FANdemonium, the UWF's latest Pay per View. You see, Kenny Mercury… he… I… have been voted into the Venetian Home Rubmle match, partnered with Jamie Shock. Now, I know that this is a big mistake. I actually won the election for the World Title match, but since all the UWF's fans have bat guano for brains, they left hanging chads on their ballots and Seymour Almasy won the match with Oni by a fluke…. A FLUKE I SAY!” “No matter… with the Contest of Champions just around the corner, I will be easily winning the right to take on the World Champion, whoever it is, soon enough. For now, I will show the UWF what a big mistake they've made by not making the ballot instructions crystal clear to the fat monkeys voting at home. I will defeat each and every one of those douche bags in the VHR and claim the UWF tag team titles…. Only the tag titles, the North American Title, and the World Title have I never wrestled in a match for… this will be my chance at the tag belts, and as always, I will win. You see, I've never been in a match for those titles…. And they are the only ones I haven't won…. Because when I wrestle in a title match... I win the damned belt… Three time TV champ, damn it! THC champ! Intercontinental champ! When you put me in a title match, just like any match, the result is…. Kenny Mercury dissects you with his technical precision, like a surgeon or a high school biology student, except better looking and with more muscles and less acne.” “Now Jamie, I know you are a uni-nutted British tart, but you do have this uncanny ability to weasel your way into getting wins. That is very commendable, for I know that not everyone is so naturally talented and gifted as me, so you gotta do what you gotta do, and Jamie, you have done so many many times over your career. Like when you united the THC and Shoot Wrestling titles… yeah… I hate you for that… you slut… But anyways, you are a weasel… and you are prone to winning, somehow… So, keep it up, damn it, because you better not hold me back! I will NOT, I repeat… WILL NOT be embarrassed by you, when something unfortunate happens, like you get anally raped or get a teste eaten off… and YOU lose the match for ME… It will not happen, understand? Because if it does… Well… you know…. I will be very upset….” “Anywho…. This match is really filled with jobbers…. Mostly just losers who suck…. A LOT… the only ones who have ever amounted to anything aside for myself, obviously, and my partner, Jamie Shock are Tsunami Oni and Seymour Almasy. I've said it before, but I'll say it again, they are the real prize… I will be taking GREAT pleasure in inflicting as much pain and suffering upon their persons and inner-persons as humanly possible. Wait a second… I am much more than some mere human! I shall rephrase that…. * AHEM*…. I will be taking GREAT pleasure in inflicting as much pain and suffering upon their persons and inner-persons as spiritually possible. Yes, that's right, for I am not of this world… I am heaven sent… SENT BY GOD HIMSELF TO RIGHT THE WRONGS OF THE WRESTLING WORLD! FOR FAR TOO LONG NOW STOOOOOOOOOPID IMBECILES HAVE BEEN PRANCING ABOUT IN THIS FEERATION! But now… the savior… the messiah… the GOD-SEND, Kenny Mercury, endowed with the most awesome wrestling power EVER…. Has come down to earth to break each and everyone one of you fart-knockers… you practitioners of douche-baggery at the highest degree… you jobbers in high carders clothing… you…. You… YOU…. YOU NINCOMPOOPS~!!!!” “Yes, Kenny Mercury is here to totally annihilate you Weiner- noses… and the Venetian Home Rumble is just the beginning. Before, I had gone over a few bumps… well, I lost my damn mind… because of that idiot, Misfit… but he's like… dead now…. Or something… SO! The REAL Kenny Mercury is back. THE DOCTOR IS IN, WHORES! And now its time to drop your drawers, turn, and FRIGGIN COUGH, because “The Shooting Star,” “The Submission Exhibition,” “The Technical Wizard”…. And “THE VERY MOTHA FRIGGIN DEFINTION OF PRECISION”… Oh… and “THE GOD-SEND”… KENNY FRIGGIN MERCURY IS BACK IN BUSINESS! And Kenny Mercury…. He… I… am about to go to town on this federation of KONKEY DONGS! So, pay attention, MAGGOTS! Because you might learn something… and who knows, maybe some of my PERFECTION will rub off on you… NOT! HA! SUCKERS!” [Explosion.]
