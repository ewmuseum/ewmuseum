UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #337

Heeeee's baaaaaaack....wait, does anyone really care?

13 Apr 2004

Mike Montgomery — UWF Studios

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in the studios at the Universal Wrestling Federation's head office in Chicago. It's a simple blue background with a single, collapsable, chair in the middle of the scene. The chair is one of those wood/felt numbers that directors seem to really love. Occupying that chair is the UWF's Executive Vice President of Talent Relations, Player's Club Founder, and the newest UWF Commissioner, Mike Montgomery. The Commissioner appears to be very relaxed and contented. He has a bottle of coke in his hand, the tag title on his shoulder, and a big smile on his face. 'Renaissance. It was supposed to be the biggest Pay Per View we'd ever put out. Bigger than Stable Wars, bigger than the Regal Rumble. And you know what? I can say with a great deal of pride that it delivered. From top to bottom it was probably the best Pay Per View I've seen around here in a long time. It was five star match after five star match...well, with the exception of the opening bout. God that was bad.' Mike twists the cap off his coke and takes a nice long swig. He closes his eyes, savoring the feel of the pop as it burns down his throat. 'Once again, the Player's Club came out on top. Okay, we didn't get the World Title, but that's okay. It's not always about getting the win. What people seem to forget, is that this business is about the fans. It's about doing whatever it takes to give the fans exactly what they wanted to see. 'It's about guys like Ezra Kazee. Guys who come out and work their asses off day after day after day, not for the win, and not for the money. They do it because they love what it is that they do. Ezra Kazee comes down to the ring and gives the fans everything he has, he puts his life on the line night after night because he has the passion for this business. I'm a tag team champion because of him. The Player's Club is where it is today because...of...him.' Mike takes another gulp from his coke, then puts the bottle down, 'But then of course there are the guys like Digital Mortality. The Hulk Hogans of the business. Guys who think they're entitled to have everything handed to them on a silver platter, simply because of who they are. Sorry, but it doesn't work that way. The UWF you knew is dead and gone Digi. It's not coming back. And you know what? We're better off for it. 'But Digi, don't be mad at me, don't hate the playah. You know what, you've been gone so long that you've cleary missed out on a thing or two. See, the golden age you're so fond of? I didn't destroy it. It was dead and gone before the Player's Club was even born. You want to lay blame? Lay it at Jamie Shock's feet. 'I had to work hard to bring in the guys people wanted to see because Jamie wouldn't do it. All Jamie wanted to do was shove Ruff Ryder down our throats whether we wanted to see him or not. It was never about the fans for Jamie. It was just about putting his boys over. I was the one who brough Jay Phoenix back. I was the one who convinced Tsunami Oni to come out of retirement for a few shows. *I* was the one who gave the people what they wanted. 'What you need to understand, Digi, is this. You and I want the same thing. We both want to undo the damage that has been done to the UWF. The thing is, I've already made most of those repairs, but you can't accept that. You can't accept that it's my name on the marquee and not yours. You can't accept that the Intercontinental Title is Ezra's and not yours. 'Well you know what Digi. This Sunday, you're going to learn a few things. You're going to learn why Ezra Kazee is the reason the Player's Club has the tag titles. You're going to learn why Ezra Kazee is the MVP of the UWF right now. And you will sure as hell learn why the Common Man is anything but common. 'But you know what Digi? You know what your biggest lesson will be? This isn't your UWF. This isn't the place where you could try and ride the coattails of my career or anybody elses. You're going to learn that this is a UWF where...no matter who you are...no matter what you did in the past...you WILL earn the accolades you are given. The Player's Club is at the top because we fought tooth and nail to get there. The Prime Alliance tried to stop us and we destroyed them in one night. One night, that's all it took to beat three of the biggest stars in the UWF. The Chaos Society thought they could take us. We sent them packing again and again and again. You won't stop us. 'How long do you think it will take Digi? How long until Moraless realizes that he doesn't need you? How long until he sees that you expect him to carry you to the top? How long until he realizes that he surpassed you in skill? Until he realizes that you're trying to ride his coattails to success? When do you think he'll realize that you're nothing but dead weight, holding him back?' Mike picks his coke up, takes another drink, then rests the bottle on the arm of his chair, 'Yes, Sunday will be most educational indeed. You'll be taking a trip down memory lane. You'll be reminded of what it was like to have me beat you day after day after day. But there's going to be one difference. No Hangman's Joke this time. No Eternal Struggle. Are you going the latest victim of one of my Power Plays? Do you get to feel the effects of my Executive Decision? Or maybe...just maybe...this match will go the way all our other tag matches go...Bomb De Commonwealth...pin...1...2...3...your winners, the Player's Club. What's it going to be Digi? Oh, by the way, welcome back. Enjoy your stay because I guarantee it won't last.'
