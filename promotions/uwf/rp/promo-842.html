UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #842

Second Chances part 2

15 Dec 2004

Adien Pryde — Chaos Society Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens where our last scene ends, with Adien Pryde, Reo Dai and Joanna are sitting in their locker room. Pryde and Joanna have just finished talking to 'Snap' Meyer about the state of The Chaos Society and its future and fellow Society member Raid showed to speak his mind to.] Joanna : Can you believe the nerve of that 'Snap' Meyer. He would actually think that Chaos Society would not be together on such a thing as tomorrow's match. Pryde : That's what reporters are supposed to do, stir up the dirt to try to make god news. Shit, let’s give some news if he wants it. [All three Chaos Society members make their way back to the door and open it slowly. Dai gets some rope from a locker before the camera fades out.] ……[The camera starts to pan away and fade in from ‘Snap’ as Raid walks away. Behind ‘Snap’ you can see both Pryde and Dai but they are hunched down at ‘Snaps’ foot level.] Snap;” So till this is ‘Snap’ Meyeeerrrrrrrrrrrr… [As he says that he is quickly snatched up by a rope that was around his legs, placed there by Pryde and Dai. As he hangs there swinging back and forth you can see Pryde, Dai, Joanna and Raid all standing behind him laughing. Raid walks up to him and stops him from swinging.] Raid:” HERE’S A LITTLE CHAOS FOR YA! Maybe next time you’ll believe ME.” [As Raid starts to walk away he gives ‘Snap’ a little slap on the cheek, and ‘Snap’ begins to swing again. As he walks next to the camera man he gives him a shove and the camera man falls into the wall breaking the camera. And thus the scene fades to black.]….. [The camera fades back in with all four members of Chaos Society in the locker room.] Raid : Dude, that was so mean, I loved it. Pryde : Couldn't have done it without you distracting him. Raid : Any time. So what is this meeting about? Joanna : Just need to nail down the plans about shutting out Commie from tomorrows match. Pryde : We decided who is the better man between you and me without any interference from that commie monster. Raid : You know that I am Chaos Society to the bitter end. Pryde : I knew you were the man. Now it will be a true test for us co-founders of the only active stable in the UWF. Joanna : After this Contest of Champions crap, what are you three plans? Pryde : Well assuming I don't win the whole thing, it is all about why we came to the UWF for in the first place. We are going to gets us the Tag Team Championship. Mercury and Shock are great wrestlers, but I can't see them getting along long enough to successfully defend those belts. Dai : Besides, there are few teams here that have the experience as tag wrestlers that we have. [Outside the door can be heard 'Snap' crying for someone to help him down.] Pryde : I think 'Snap' has had enough Chaos for one day, don't you think? [Pryde, Raid, and Joanna get up and make their way out the door. Pryde goes and gets a garbage can as Raid plays some more with the now very red faced UWF interviewer.] Pryde : Time to take out the trash. Would you mind doing us the favor Raid? [Joanna cuts the rope dropping everyone's favorite reporter head first into the garbage can. Raid picks up the can and starts to make his way out the back door.] Pryde : Thanks a lot man, see you tomorrow night. [Pryde waves as he and Joanna go back into the locker room and close the door as the scene fades to black.]
