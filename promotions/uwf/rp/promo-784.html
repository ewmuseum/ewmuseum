UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #784

A Certainty I Envy

11/24 (year unrecorded)

'The Savior' Wes Diable

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Our view opens with a flash of green against a steady grey background. As we pan out slightly we see the frame of a window and the sound of the train becomes audible. The clouds overhead appear threatening. In the seat of the train, we find an old man with a mostly grey head and pale blue eyes staring out of that very window. Upon further examination we notice his priest’s collar visible because his jacket is unzipped to around his chest] [He sighs slightly as he takes a pen to his notebook to the notebook in his lap] It’s been almost a week that I’ve been gone and I’m beginning to meet myself with questions. Is this what was intended of me? How will I find him? What will I do if I manage to locate him? Is there anything I can do? [He brings the top of the pen up to his mouth and bites down slightly almost seeing if the answers come to him now that he has written them down] Each Church I extend my hand to has reached out and welcomed me completely, but each has the same question. What is my purpose? And it’s the one I can’t seem to answer with words merely with the visions of the past I have. [He shakes his head and almost shivers as he thinks] I never had encountered such a rare individual in my life. Its rare that someone who almost pulled valedictorian be one of the most popular students and also one of the biggest trouble-makers, which is perhaps why he didn’t manage to get valedictorian. [He closes his eyes for a moment to paint himself a picture of the past, so that perhaps the words might come a little easier] I’ve never seen a person manage to enter people’s lives and just be such a destructive force... [The train brakes slightly breaking him out of his writing mode, but only for a moment] The counselors came to me many times saying how he hurt this person in such a severe way, whether it was a friend or a girlfriend... I’d set-up a mediation and within ten minutes the problem would be resolved. [He closes the book suddenly as the train comes to a stop] Manasquan... Next stop Point Pleasant! [The priest sat up then leans back in the chair again. He looks towards the book and just tosses it onto the seat next to him] “I should’ve seen it coming...” [With that the priest closes his eyes and we cut away] Voice: Yo Wes, its Matty. [This man’s voice sounds scared and desperate] Wes: Whats wrong? Matty: They raided John’s. [There is a long pause] Matty: Wes... Wes: Shut up! I’m thinking, God damnit! [Matty just can’t seem to keep quiet as he’s beginning to sound on the verge of tears] Matty: What’re we gonna do, man? They fucking got him! [A loud noise in the background apparently a shot of frustration from one of those involved] Wes: Calm down, I can handle this, where is he? Matty: The thirty-sixth. What’re you going to do? [Calm as ever] Wes: What I have to. [A dialtone] [A desk, a lamp, a computer, and a beautiful woman typing away with glasses on. Those and the blouse give Vivian a look, we’ve yet to see. The businesswoman focused and mature, but still so attractive with her dark hair up and perfectly tanned skin. Who could forget the events of last week’s SAS when we seen Vivian seduce both Skankalie and Hot Mask, just in order to give her boyfriend a shot at the back of Mask’s head.. Behind Vivian are large windows covered by white curtains and on both sides of her are large bookshelves. This room we assume is a sort of library or den] [In from offscreen walks Wes, he appears kind of upset as he throws on a zip-up hoodie sweatshirt. His straight dark hair is still growing longer and parted off center, his blue eyes are ice and balls of massive tidal waves. He wears a pair of faded jeans as well] “Well, you look happy.” [Vivian says with a playful grin o n her face, Wes; however, doesn’t return the favor] “Listen, I have to go take care of some business... [Vivian’s look turns serious and she mouths the wored “who”] “John.” [[She shakes her head almost in disgust] “Idiot.” [Wes nods in agreement with her statement] “So could you do me a favor, love?” [Vivian looks up from her paperwork as Wes motions towards the camera] “Can I take off my clothes?” [That grin is so hot because she’s playing, b8ut she’s also half-serious at the same time. This does manage to get Wes to smile a bit] “Ladies is pimps too” [He says as he starts to walk off, as we hear the door shut] “Damn right.” [She goes back to her paperwork for a moment then tosses it down as she apparently forget about the camera for just a moment] “Well, one match deep and he’s already in a tournament that could lead him to a World Titleshot...” [A half-grin as she stands up, she opens a drawer] “Not bad Nicky., eh?” [She pulls out a pack of cigarettes and walks around to the other side of table where she reveals black dress pants] “Or should I say Mr. Intercontinental Champion?” [She leans back against the desk and lights the cigarette] “Well whats it gonna be Nicky, how are you going to go into this one? How are you going to end with your foot in your mouth this week?” [She takes a drag] “Will you do it yourself or will Wes have to do it for you yourself?” [Smoke released as she smiles confidently, its unbelievable how much she reminiscent of Wes in front of the camera] “You should be happy about that little belt you have there, it’s a blessing for someone like yourself. But for Wes, for myself; we wouldn’t waste our time with some third rate belt. We want the big one... I guess you could say size does matter.” [Here that Mask, you’re screwed] “You see when Wes decided to come here, he said he wasn’t going to waste anytime in getting to the top, he said he was going to literally become the savior this sport hasn’t seen in quite some time. And now he’s a few wins from delivering on his promise.” “He doesn’t make a lot of promises, but he never breaks them.” [She ashes onto the ashtray on the right side of the desk] “Nick, its rather unfortunate for you to have drawn Wes in the first round, at least you could’ve offered your dillusional fans or yourself even a bit of hope for this shot at the big time, but either way you would’ve met the same fate” [She smiles slightly as she rest both her hands down onto the table] “Its funny the name of the tournament is the Contest Of Champions and one of UWF’s champions won’t even qualify for it... But shit happens, thats on them.” [She places the cigarette in the ashtray and stands up stretching her arms up in the air, revealing a slight bit of her perfect midsection] “Nicky try not to think about things to hard this week, try not to train so hard, try not to fool yourself. This match is over before it starts; you have no chance. “ [Her arms come back down around her side as she takes a small step towards the camera, we get a headshot] Well, maybe you would if this was Hollywood, but there’s no script here, there’s no perfect ending. This isn’t Philadelphia and you are not Rocky Balboa... You’re Nick Persia and I’m sorry. [She lets out a mocking little girl chuckle. The last image we see is that beautifully sadistic smile] [Cut away]
