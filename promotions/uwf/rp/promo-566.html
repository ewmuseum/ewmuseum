UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #566

Burning A Past

21 Jul 2004

Misfit — Project Mayhem basement

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [A wooden door, once painted white, fills the screen. The wood is old and rotted, the paint dirty and chipped mostly away. With a loud creak, the door slowly swings open to reveal several dirty, busted windows at a distance as the descent down into the basement begins. Several dark, blurred shapes are barely visible as the camera moves at a very slow, dawdling pace down the stairs. A few small moonbeams fall over the walls and several old tools that have been tacked to pegboards.] Voice: I’ve been waiting for you… [The camera stops for a moment, freezing in response to the haunting voice that calls from the basement.] Voice: Don’t stop there… there’s so much to discuss… [Slower than before, the camera begins moving down the stairs again. The journey ends and the camera turns to the left where Misfit stands in the empty basement. The faint glimmer of the moon through the filthy, cracked windows illuminates the pale skin of his face, the rest of his attire hardly visible.] Misfit: I’ve sat silent for weeks now… I’ve barely spoken a word since returning to the UWF… and everyone has tried getting me to talk. I’ve been contacted by Internet sites, trade magazines, talk shows… not even the UWF website has gotten me to speak. [A soft click and the entire basement becomes bleakly lit by an old, yellowed light bulb. Misfit stands above an old wooden chair in what loosely resembles the basement of his Project Mayhem house. The walls have since been crudely plastered with lined notebook paper, words and scribbles scrawled all over them in black & red marker.] Misfit: Like the redecorating? [He scoffs at his own remark, then takes a seat in the chair.] Misfit: Unforgiven and Slasher both destroyed their homes… their pasts… upon returning to the UWF. I have no past… no place that I’ve ever called home… nothing to destroy. They’ve given up everything in returning here, or that’s what some would say. In reality, they’ve given up nothing. They’ve realized that all of the houses and treasures of the world are nothing. All that matters on this spinning shitball of a planet… is this… [Misfit holds out an arm, showcasing with his free hand a large scar across his flesh.] Misfit: Brotherhood… friendship… [He pulls his arm back and crosses them across his chest, leaning back to rest in the chair. His right leg slides forward, covered with the metal protectors over his boiler suit.] Misfit: Everyone knows the past of drowning POOL. Slasher and Unforgiven returned to the UWF to wreak havoc over a year ago. They saw me backstage, the little UWF midcard freak, laid up with a concussion… and they saw something in me. We gave a big “fuck you” to the management, and they brought me into the fold as a third member to the hardcore revolution. We laid out everyone, and we were damn good at it! [Misfit shifts uneasily in his chair, shaking his head slightly and fitting back a scream.] Misfit: Then… Spectre stuck his nose into our business. We had no choice… we had to leave. Collectively the most bankable trio in UWF history: Ben Jericho, the indie sensation who was picked up by the UWF; Slasher, the hardcore demigod; and Misfit… thought talented by some, a hardcore fool by others… but everyone saw the potential in this… [He winces lightly] disturbed… [Through gnashed teeth] little… [He chokes back another scream, speaking at a half-yell] freak! [He breathes in deeply and exhales, calming himself slightly.] Misfit: We all walked out. [He hangs his head down and leans forward, rubbing his hands back and forth on his knees.] Misfit: Then things began falling apart… Slasher left us to return… addiction consumed Ben Jericho… and I was left alone. Isolated, I became the martyr for what we had done. There was no wrestling for me to live on; no smack to get me high… no federation would take a chance on the unbalanced little freak that walked out on his contract… [Madness overtaking him momentarily, he lets loose a flurry of screams.] Misfit: NO ONE!!! No federation wanted an insurance hazard like Misfit. No one would take a business risk who hadn’t proven himself to be a bankable star on his own… I… got… nothing… [Misfit swallows, choking back his anger and frustration again. He runs his hands through his hair and leans back in the chair again.] Misfit: So I went back to the UWF… I went back to pull Slasher out of the vile pit he had come back to… not because I was jealous; not because I was afraid he couldn’t handle himself… but because our friend needed us. Because Ben Jericho was dying. But things got confused… the whole agenda got lost in the shuffle, and I was drug into this battle against Lew Spectre and the UWF management. Stable Wars came, and I was able to lift Ben out of his private hell to declare war on what we loathed. [Misfit brushes the hair out of the left side of his face, then turns to look off into the empty basement.] Misfit: But it was too late… Ben Jericho was already dead. What I brought back was a sad, pathetic shell of a man. He was so mind-fucked; all he cared about was getting even with Slasher for abandoning him. So addicted that he would do anything for that extra dollar to feed his little monster… so addicted that he would stab me in the back, just for a little extra bonus on a new contract. [Misfit runs a finger under his nose, scratching away a little tickle before looking at the camera again.] Misfit: And that, ladies and gentlemen, was the birth of the Black Star Revolution. A whole era of darkness for all of us in drowning POOL. Slasher left again, and I was stuck having to fight my best friend in the business. I was like Lew Spectre’s little chew toy, only there for his amusement whenever he got bored. And then… things just… died. I think you can fill in the rest from what Unforgiven and Slasher have told you, but that’s my side. [He breathes in deep, and then lets the breath go, almost like he’s breathing in a new life.] Misfit: But that’s all in the past now. It’s over, done with, and forgotten. Which brings me to the present; the new drowning POOL revolution. Coming up, we’re faced with a gauntlet; a situation put in place by Richard Hayes, yet another old buddy of the dP. Apparently, he didn’t learn anything last time he fucked with us, otherwise he would’ve just let this be. But, what’s done is done. [Misfit looks off in the distance, through the broken windows, for a moment before scratching his chin and looking back at the camera.] Misfit: I can’t say I mind, really… because it’s given me the chance to get to know Kenny Mercury up-close… and personal. [He lashes forward with his hand, grabbing hold of the camera and pulling it in tight on his face. All of the composure and calm leaves him as he screams at the camera and shakes.] Misfit: Remember me, Kenny? The man you fucked over on the road to glory; the guy you wanted to be, standing at Ben Jericho’s side just so you could share his fame; the man who threw you into a fucking corpse box, just so the Devil himself could set it ablaze?! REMEMBER?! [Misfit shoves the camera backwards as his head twitches. He closes his eyes, peace returning to his expression as he slowly leans back into the chair again.] Misfit: I’m sure you can’t forget. You scarred Unforgiven… and we scarred you. You think you can stand in our way, Kenny? You think this little gauntlet is really going to take those titles away from Unforgiven and Slasher. I think not, Kenny. In fact, only good can come of this… we keep the tag team titles, and add Intercontential gold to the fray. I think you and Hayes have both made a grave mistake here, Kenny… you’ve underestimated us. You figure that, somewhere, there will be weak leak… an easy element to eliminate in the drowning POOL equation. Guess again. Nothing can stop us, Kenny… not you, or Richard Hayes… not Corporal Punishment, Sean McClancy, or any of those Knightmare Brigade morons… NOTHING. [Misfit twitches again, wincing his eyes and gnashing his teeth.] Misfit: See you soon, Kenny… see you real… soon… [Fade to black.]
