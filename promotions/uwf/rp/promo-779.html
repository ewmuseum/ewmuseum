'Eh!' A crisp autumn midday where two worlds collide, as we focus on our speaking subjects - a bright honeymooning couple gazing with intent at one of the most serene wonders of the world. Leaves that have mostly changed from a deep green to visually stimulating blends of yellow and gold litter the area we stand upon, not to mention hordes of fallen pine straw that stays steadfast despite the beeze. The newlyweds stand in a filmworthy pose, with man in a black fleece coat standing behind the woman and cradling her with his arms. The woman takes occasional glances backward to the clean-shaven face of her husband, with the fur lining the hood of her parka somewhat restricting her view. Welcome to the Canadian claim of Niagara Falls, somewhat of a cliche spot for those united in matrimony to vacation, but the soothing charm can melt away even the coldest inhibitions. Birds migrate south overhead in anticipation of a frosty winter that seems to lurk around the corner, and the calming roar of the rushing waters in the near distance cheers them on, but oblivious to nature's unspoken language stands the couple swaying lightly in unison during loving embrace. 'GAY-AYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!' Now we are treated with a bit of a paradigm shift to a not-so-picturesque view of human completion, UWF's hyperextended version of Archie and Edith Bunker...Hot Mask & Skankalie, about twenty feet from the romantic display. It would be an understatement to say that this isn't quite a warm show of affection, although the Czar of Spank appears very comfortable bundled up in a wool petticoat, a brown-and-white plaid scarf wrapped around his neck, black ski pants and brown mukluks...and of course, his always-worn red mask covering his face. 'That is gayer than Gaylord Gayerson on 21 Gay Street in Gaysville, Gay S.A.' Ignoring the incongruous homophobia for a moment, we turn attention to Skankalie, who has a look on her face that Hell has indeed iced over, and she was smack in the middle of it with the devil himself right by her side. She is dressed in what she normally would only wear to ringside...a dresslike low-cut potato sack. Holding herself and forcing shivers to keep herself from freezing, the normal cute expression on her face looks anything but adorable now, as she looks to be on the edge of malice. With only one pink furry mitten to keep her hands warm, she has shoved both into it and furiously rubs them together, keeping the mitten close to her face. 'Oh GOD, NO! Not THAT! EWWWWWWWWW!' Turning our attention back for a moment to the couple nearby, we see that the woman has switched positions and now faces the man with a lovestruck gleam in her eye as they engage in a passionate kiss. A gloved hand reaches out in front of the camera, grabbing the lens fixture and directing it toward the face of Mask. Then, with his same hand, Mask points his finger to his mouth in a bulimic gesture, and expresses his disgust with the sappy scene by faux vomiting. Once the act had been hammed up for about ten seconds, the facial expression is replaced with a trademark scowl from the Genital Giant, as he elaborates. 'Kissing and lovey-dovey crap is officially the dumbest thing in the universe. Everyone knows that the only REAL reason to get married is to have even more excuses to beat the hell out of a woman, so why flower it up with all that mushy stuff? You know what I blame this on? 'We live in a country where it's considered 'ideal' to go to college, get a job, have two cars in your garage, have some pencil-neck dweeb take your order at a fast food restaurant, poop said food out of your ass into a sewer that fantasy mutated turtles live in who fight crime, get married, slam the ham into the vertical smile and have the tubby broad pop out a couple of sniveling little horse dumplings, and pretend your life is oh-so-fucktastic because that's what is expected of us.' Since you can't be here in person, just know that the only distinguishable factor here is that the women have thicker moustaches here. Oh, and the entire country smells like a toilet bowl freshener in a gas station bathroom. Quite nice, if I must say so myself.' 'But don't think I haven't got my complaints about this place either. Also, Canada has the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, and although I've never seen one in person...if the movie 'Dudley Do-Right' was any indication, I'd assume that they're all completely homo,' Mask quips. 'It has it's perks, though...with free health care and all...although since I am genetically flawless, I never get sick or hurt, so the whole thing is pretty much useless for me. Fuck, I might just consider packing up the gear and buying myself a nice little mansion in Saskatchewan, you know...since I'm frightened with the re-election of George W. Bush and all...' Mask doubles over in laughter at his anticdote of sarcasm, muttering something about 'dirty hippies' underneath his breath. Skankalie doesn't appear too amused, however...then again, even the comedic stylings of Rodney Dangerfield probably wouldn't be as entertaining when you're dressed like she is in 30 degree weather. 'But honestly, if you want to know the REAL reason why Canada is so much better than the United States, I'll let all of you lowly plebians out there on the secret...' Casually unfolding it and handing it to Skankalie for her to hold, he points at it sternly and addresses the viewing audience. 'Betsy Ross was a harlot and her vagina smelled like salmon. I just happen to be in Canada at this very moment with an American flag stuck between my ass cheeks, so I'm really doing this on a whim! Okay, let's GO!' As the figure comes closer, a smile spreads across his face. 'Oh...HEY! It's YOU!' 'That's right, Maskerino! It's ME!' the white-haired man says, approaching Mask and carrying an old-school gray boombox on his shoulder. 'Chief PETERHEAD,' they both cry out in unison! The two embrace, with the penis on the Indian's forehead almost poking Mask in the face. 'Watch out there, Bigdicked Hope...that thing'll put an eye out...with it's EYE!' Both the men share raucous laughter with each other, as Peterhead sets down the boombox he was carrying to the ground. As he bends down, he looks up to see Skankalie peering at the abhorration on his forehead. The Chief notices this, and tries to 'peek up her dress,' causing the apparatus to casually sneak up the potato sack. She backs away in horror, very disgusted at this gesture, which only sparks even more laughter from both Mask and the surfer Indian. 'So Mask, I heard the news...you fulfilled your prophecy! You won the Television belt!' Chief Peterhead exclaims, beaming with admiration. 'I sure did, Chief. It was my destiny, like you told me,' Mask remarks, equally sharing in the admiration. 'But hey man, where's the gold at now? I want to see it!' 'Hmmm...' Mask thinks to himself. Got a knock at my door from the Secret Service saying I was under suspicion by the government...of course they're gonna stereotype a guy who has any kind of genetic deformation...so instead of reporting to the Capitol building, I headed up here to take refuge,' he explains.
