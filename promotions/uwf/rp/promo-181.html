UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #181

Getting the job Dunn.

Jan 20th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Grand Central Casino.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<< The first supremacy of the new year starts out perfect for Malcolm Kennedy. After a very hard fought victory over his friend Jamie Shock, Malcolm is now the number one contender for the World Heavyweight Title. Id say Malcolm’s new year couldn’t start any better. So when will Malcolm get his chance to win the belt? Not this Sunday. This Sunday Malcolm will be a part of a 3 match set, best of 3 matches between The Prime Alliance and The Players Club. Who is his opponent? Joshua Dunn. Looks like Malcolm has another tough match ahead of him. >>> *** The scene opens up inside an extremely nice office in Grand Central Casino. An oak desks sits in the middle of the room, the desk chair sits behind it, the chairs back to the camera. Behind the chair are three full sized, one way windows. The chair spins around to face the camera. Sitting in the chair is none other than Malcolm Kennedy. Malcolm wears a black business suite with a white undershirt. His hands are pressed together, palm to palm, and are resting below his chin. He smirks at the camera as he reaches out and picks up a piece of paper off his desk.*** Top Notch: “ It seems as though we will both finally get what we went. The Prime Alliance gets the Players Club, the Players Club gets The Prime Alliance. Best of Three matches. Its funny, because the best three in the UWF, are in the best of three matches. Those three individuals being Jamie, Ryder, and myself. But we will get to that shortly. There is something more important that I want to talk about first.” *** Malcolm sets the paper back on his desk. He slides back from the desk in his chair and begins to recline in his chair. He puts both of his hands behind his head and kicks his feet up on the desk .*** Malcolm Kennedy: “ Jamie, we fought one hell of a match, And you put me through hell. I pulled the win out in the end and won the right to be number one contender. We did what we both said we would, we beat the living shit out of each other. But that match is behind us now, and there are more important things to deal with. The Players club being our main focal point. I assure you once I win that title from Beelzebozo, you will get your shot at the title once again. But for now, lets do what we’ve wanted to do for a long time, and take out those idiots in the Players Club once and for all.” *** Malcolm stands up from the chair and walks to the window. He puts his right hand in his pocket as he scans across the casino floor, watching all the people trying to strike it big. He begins to speak without turning around. *** “ Joshua Dunn. We finally will meet each other one on one. Oh we have had our encounters before, but never man to man. I believe one of our first encounters was when your buddy Mike was bossing you around like a little bitch and telling you to beat me down. Well done my friend. I wont take anything away from you for that though, because lets face it, you’re the current IC champion. And the record that comes with that is pretty damn good. That record will only get you so far though. When it comes time for that bell to ring, all that crap will be thrown out the door. When that bell rings everything else is no longer important. When that bell rings the only thing you have to think about, is how in the hell your gonna beat the man standing across the ring from you. Why? Because that man across from you is going to everything he can to put you down for the three count. Because that man will take you to the limit and beyond. Because that man will do everything in his power to hurt you any way possible. Because that man......Is me. Should that scare you? Absolutely not. Lets face it, being scared gets you nothing, and it doesn’t make for a very good match. The only thing you should be, is prepared. You should be prepared for a war. Prepared to give me everything you have got, and more. You should be prepared to kill me if you have to. That might be the only way you will get my shoulders down for three seconds. *** Malcolm turns around and opens up the middle drawer in his desk. He reaches inside it and pulls out a single rubber band. He pushes the drawer back in and walks around his desk. Resting on the edge of the desk, Malcolm begins to speak again. *** “ Dunn, the human body can be a lot like this little thing here. (Malcolm holds up the rubber band.) The body, just like the rubber band, is plenty flexible. It can stretch and bend. But they both have their limits. There is only so far you can stretch this rubber band, likewise, there is only so far your body can stretch before it snaps. Everyone has a breaking point. Physically, Mentally, at some point everyone can and will break . What’s this got to do with anything? Its simple really. I plan on breaking you at supremacy. I plan on snapping you like a twig. Not only do I want to break your body, I want to break your spirit. I plan on leaving you a shell of the wrestler that you used to be. I am going to beat you so bad that nothing will make sense any more. Up will be down, black will be white, yes will be no. You wont be able to figure out how anyone could do such drastic damage. How I could be so cold hearted. How I could beat you so badly, without having the slightest bit of remorse. When I get done with you, you will cry at night trying to figure out why this happened to you. Why god put you through the hell that was our match. All these questions will ring in your head over and over again as you tremble at night, unable to sleep anymore. *** Malcolm locks his index fingers around two sides of the rubber band and begins to stretch it.*** “ Josh, It wont all happen at once. I will slowly break you apart. Minute by minute, second by second, you will be stretched to the limit. Until you can stretch no further. Then......” *** Malcolm pulls the rubber band tight and quickly tugs it and snaps it in half.*** “I will finish you off with the Jackpot and leave you broken in two, physically and mentally. And you will be left reaching for answers to those questions.” *** Malcolm drops the rubber band to the floor and returns to the window behind his desk. He puts his hands in his pockets as he stares out the windows one again, looking at all the hopefuls betting there money. Malcolm begins to snicker slightly. He turns back to the camera.*** Malcolm Kennedy: “Dunn, your luck has finally run out. At supremacy I will break you.” *** Malcolm turns back to the window as he begins to laugh out loud. He shakes with evil laughter as the scene fades to black.***
