In The Beginning...

27 Jan 2004

Mike Montgomery — The Empty Ericcson Stadium

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In life there are certain things that are inevitable. New York sports teams will try and throw money around to gain championships. The Yankees will succeed, the Rangers will mystify everyone for years with their failure. George Bush will say or do something really really stupid. An underage kid will try and get into a bar with a fake ID. These are things that will always happen over the course of one's life. Somewhere, some time, they happen. And it feels right this time On this crash course we're in the big time Pay no mind to the distant thunder Beauty fills his head with wonder, boy.... These same constants apply to the Universal Wrestling Federation. Cards will make no sense. People get excessive with Shooting Star Presses. Bruce Gordy stages yet another comeback in a desperate bid for respectability. Iron Lung beats a jobber in less than a minute. Somebody turns out the lights on Ruff Ryder...and Joshua Dunn remains the Intercontinental Champion. But there's one more constant. One more thing that must always occur here in the UWF. The Player's Club will always reign supreme. Says it feels right this time Turn around, found new high lights Good day to be alive Sir Good day to be alive, he said..... The Prime Alliance. Jamie Shock, Ruff Ryder, and former Player Malcolm Kennedy. These men came together, perhaps out of convenience, perhaps to ride Jamie Shock's coattails to success, whatever. They strutted through the halls of the UWF's inner sanctum, thinking they were the greatest the company had. The Alliance walked through the UWF, convinced that it was utterly dominant. But then something happened. A groundswell movement finally took shape into a second triumverate. One of the greatest cruiserweights, perhaps the greatest Intercontinental Champion, and one of the greatest TV Champions. These three men had one thing in common. They all knew how the game of professional wrestling was really played. They all recognized the need for a strong mix of political influence and wrestling skill that was second to none. Suddenly the Alliance was legitimately threatened. Ruff Ryder was the only North American champion in UWF history, and Jamie Shock is in the hall of fame. They hid behind crowbars, used their influence to ensure they only had to fight each other, did everything they could to ensure that they never had to work for what they got. They kissed up to ownership in the hopes of taking a trip down easy street. But then the UWF's political landscape changed. The Alliance had been unprepared. At the end of the previous ownership's tenure, the Player's Club's founder, Mike Montgomery, declared war on the Prime Alliance. At stake? The right to be considered the superiour stable in the UWF. The Prime Alliance, the only group that could have possibly stood up to the Player's Club was in trouble. Oh they talked the talk. They went on and on about how they were better than the Club, telling it to anyone who would listen. Then the new era began. Joshua Dunn, in the first battle between the two factions, would defeat Ruff Ryder in a unification North American/Intercontinental Title match. But then the unthinkable the following week. A best of three series. Ezra Kazee took on Ruff Ryder in a re-match of the Contest of Champions finals. 1-0 for the Player's Club. Joshua Dunn took on Malcolm Kennedy. 2-0 for the Player's Club. The third match wasn't needed, but just HAD to be done. It would be Jamie Shock, Comissioner, Hall of Famer, Legend, against Mike Montgomery, Executive VP of Talent Relations. The leaders of both sides would do battle in what was supposed to be a cakewalk for Jamie. Jamie talked often about Montgomery had no business being in the ring with him, how he would remove this cancer from wrestling. Montgomery said simply this: Jamie Shock's time was over and he would fall from grace. Jamie fell. The Prime Alliance is now no more.
