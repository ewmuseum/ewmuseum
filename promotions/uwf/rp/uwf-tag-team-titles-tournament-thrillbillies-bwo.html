UWF Tag Team Titles Tournament, Quarter-Final: The Thrillbillies vs. Bob World Order

date unknown

The Thrillbillies / Bob World Order

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWF Tag Team Titles Tournament – Quarter-Finals The Thrillbillies -v- Bob World Order [Like a FLASH~!, the arena has suddenly gone dark, with neon green lights dancing across the drunken orgy of UWF fans.] Tommy Waters: “Ladies and gentlemen, continuing tonight’s festivities, our next match is a quarter-final match in the Phenomenons’ Tag Team Title Tournament! Introducing first … the Thrillbillies!” [The Thrillbillies come a runnin’’.] Tommy Waters: “And their opponents …” [Suddenly, the camera crew throw wide their UWF gear, revealing themselves as … the bWo~! They dive onto the Thrillbillies, poking at their eyes.] Tommy Waters: “The … err … Bob World Order.” [The Thrillbillies continue to run to the ring, with the Bob World Order hanging on their backs.] *DING DING DING* Rick: “And the match is—“ [The Thrillbillies run at each other … duck … and Bob Charlie and Big Bob Alien’s skulls connect, each man instantly dropping like a sack of peanuts.] Rick: “The Thrillbillies have them down … the Thrillbillies have them down!!!” Larson: “Why are you yelling? It took them … like … five seconds.” Rick: “Thanks.” [Thrillbilly #1 just kind of sits on Bob Charlie, making the cover. Jerry Kohler tries to drop down, winces at the creaking in his knees, and decides to just slap one hand into his open palm.] One! … Two! … Thr—CRASH~! Larson: “What in the name of Friday night blue-balls was THAT?!” [Suddenly~! A Delorean with dice in the mirror and a license plate that says “Cool” slams into the ring.] Larson: “I’m having an 80’s acid flashback here.” [As the Thrillbillies stare stupidly toward the car, the Bob World Order appear … Bob Charlie and Big Bob Alien, bursting out of the car, and striking a pose.] [And in the ring, the … err … the Bob World Order also stares stupidly.] Bob Charlie #2: “Dudes!!! We’ve traveled back in time, because the Bob World Order must not lose this match!” Big Bob Alien #2: “If the Bob World Order doesn’t win this tag team tournament, the world is in serious danger … of becoming un-cool! bWo, attack~!” [The second Bob World Order launches toward the ring and the dumbfounded Thrillbillies.] Rick: “Wait … what?! The Bob World Order has traveled back in time to … to interfere in their own match?! Can they be disqualified?” Larson: “Technically, I don’t think so … but if a black hole opens in the ring, I’m throwing you in.” [Thrillbilly #1 … pokes Bob Charlie #2 in the eye. Thrillbilly #2 slaps Big Bob Alien #2 just as he goes to the top rope, sending the big black man in the bug mask sailing.] [But in comes the first set of Bob World Order! Bob Charlie dropkicks Thrillbilly #2 … and Big Bob Alien comes flying in, sweeping Thrillbilly #1 up in a small package! The bWo goes for the pin!] Rick: “I’m getting a headache.” Larson: “Wait … what’s that?” Rick: “I … I just got word that this match has become, by order of the Phenomenons, a … a “Million, Bajillion Jelly Beans Match.”” [On cue, a billion jelly beans come flying down from the ceiling, pummeling both sets of the Bob World Order, the Thrillbillies, and coating the ringside area in a carpet of multi-colored sugary goodness. Larson opens his mouth and begins eating them as they fall out of the air.] Larson: “Mmmmm … jelly beans. Yum-yum.” [In the ring, Big Bob Alien and Bob Charlie collide, slipping forward on the carpet of jelly beans, and knocking each other out. The Thrillbillies appear happy about this … but in come the second wave! The Bob World Order comes, they charge, and …] *CRASH* [A neon green Delorean rear-ends the other car, and another set of the Bob World Order leap out.] Bob Charlie #3: “STOP THE MATCH~! Bob World Order, you don’t know what you’ve done! Our interference has thrown the time-space-thangamajiggy off course! We’ve doomed the planet~! No tag team titles are worth this! We must be stopped! RAR!” [The Bob World Order (#3) goes flying into the ring, attacking … themselves.] Rick: “…” Larson: “Mmmm … jelly beans.” [A hole rips into the time-space continuum and a fourth set of Bob World Order – older, wiser, but still Bob-rific, come propelling down from the top of the arena.] [As the assault continues, and the Bob World Order continues fighting themselves, one of the Thrillbillies slips on the jelly beans and falls on one of the Bob Charlie’s.] One! Two! Three!!!
