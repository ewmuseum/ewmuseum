UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #664

The Knightmare Begins...

date unknown

Seymour Almasy — #1 for SAS

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why? It was all that Seymour had heard ever since Terror in Tokyo. Why assault Tsunami Oni? Why turn on your loving wife? Why? Why? Why? … Even he couldn’t tell you. But the fact of the matter was that he did. And starting now, he had to move on with his TRUE mission. Obliterating Tsunami Oni, and showing the world he could still be a champion. And if that meant he had to get hardcore…so be it. ------ Seymour Almasy: I swear, this place has gone to crap since I left. In the apartment that he once shared with his wife, Seymour Almasy sits, waving a piece of paper at his new friend Aya. On it is printed his match assignment. Seymour Almasy: Not only does that world domination asshole come back, but the rest of his freaky friends all gather together to form this drowning POOL thing. As if capitalization makes it any better. Aya: Well, they’re probably trying to draw attention to themselves. Seymour Almasy: If that is their mission, Aya, then they have succeeded. The expression on Almasy’s face is beyond his usual tight concentration. His eyes brim over with anger, even hatred. Seymour Almasy: The drowning POOL is made up of odious individuals. I have already spoke of Tsunami Oni, but to fully explain his evil would take weeks. And then, they have Unforgiven, a man who slandered my former wife Laura…and yet now is cheered. It’s not like the heathenous fans know any better, but still! It is an outrage! And lastly, there is that Japanese deathmatch loving, scythe wielding, all-out lunatic Slasher. And, considering the lineup for this week, it is THAT fool who will feel the wrath of the Lord of Destruction first. It was a silly nickname, something Seymour would probably forget in five minutes. But the intent and the focus of his comments would not be forgotten. Aya: He’s a dangerous man. I’ve seen him before, in my native country. He’s done things that would make a serial killer blush. Seymour Almasy: It is amazing the things that one can do in the wrestling ring and be cheered for… He spoke the words, clear disgust oozing through each syllable. For once in his life, he almost had to agree with Kenny Mercury. The THC division wasn’t wrestling. It was barbary. Seymour Almasy: Considering that then, you’d think the fans would cheer what I have in mind for Slasher. I would advise him not to underestimate my ability in a hardcore match. I am, after all, a former THC Champion. Aya: But you won it under 24/7 rules by stealing it from Beezlebozo. Just saying, and all… Seymour Almasy: Fair enough. But I have competed in hardcore environments not even he can imagine. Slasher, I ask you…have you ever stepped onto a raw, unforgiving snow-capped mountain? Have you ever hit another man with a rock? Have you ever been knocked down face first into cold, wet snow, and risked frostbite and gangrene? I have twice, on the slopes of Mount Gagazet. Aya can only shake her head at the melodrama, but Seymour isn’t finished yet. Seymour Almasy: Have you ever stepped into a steel cage, with four razor sharp swords on poles in the corner, all of which are perfectly legal to use? I have. It’s called the Ultimate Battle Arena Deathmatch. So, go have a swordfight with someone, and then you can come back and tell me what hardcore is. Douche. The last word caught Aya off guard. It was a juvenile addition to what had been a strong statement. In that, it was perfectly indicative of who Seymour really was. A child trapped in a man’s body. Aya: Well, you do realize Slasher’s not the only guy in the match, right? Seymour Almasy: Yeah, yeah, it’s UWF. My other two opponents are probably like El Turtle and hardkore. Aya: Well, actually, one of them’s Nick Persia, the longest reigning THC Champion ever. Seymour Almasy: Oh. So, you mean I’m not facing two jobbers? Aya: The other opponent’s Raid. Seymour Almasy: Okay, so I’m facing ONE jobber. That’s an improvement. Usually in the UWF, I face multiple. The expression of disgust on Almasy’s face was evident. This was beneath him, really. Soon, he would defend his honor and tournament reputation against hundreds of other men, the best wrestlers in the world. Now… Seymour Almasy: I’m facing a bunch of fucking vampires. Aya: Just because they like causing pain with weaponry doesn’t mean they should be underestimated. They’re capable…well, the non-Raid ones anyway. Seymour Almasy: Raid and Persia are simply in the wrong place at the wrong time. But Slasher…he shall be shown no mercy. None whatsoever. With a grin, Almasy rubs his hands together. Seymour Almasy: Face your fate, Slasher. The Knightmare Brigade are no fools. They knew damn well what they got when they welcomed me into their fold. Before, even when I was the happy-go-lucky idiot I used to be, I was still one of the most capable athletes in the world. Now, the Brigade has a Seymour Almasy that has yet another deadly quality: aggressiveness. A sneer now comes over Almasy’s face. It is an expression unlike anything Almasy has ever given in his life. Seymour Almasy: Slasher, ultra violence and hardcore are merely words. Look into my eyes, and you will see a man who is not afraid of you, or your weapons. You will see a man focused on domination. Destruction. Even annihilation. For you stand between me and Tsunami Oni. And because of that, you shall be laid to waste. Persia…Raid…you two have the option of getting the hell out of my way. Eyes flashed with fire, scaring even Aya seated a few feet away. Seymour Almasy: And for your own safety, I suggest that you both take that option. (Fin).
