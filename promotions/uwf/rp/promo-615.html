RR: On the Plane

13 Aug 2004

The Communist — On the Plane... duh

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The plane takes off and the Communist kicks back, reading some passages from the Torah recomended by the Rabbi. The buisness man beside him scoots to the far side of his chair, having seen the news report on the two golfers beaten with a sledgehammer by a man bearing a striking resembalance to the person he's sitting next to: Commie: Only a few more hours till I touch down in Tokyo. The Regal Rumble, a chance to get one step closer to the Heavyweight Championship. All that stands between me and it are 60 wrestlers. 60 men, willing to risk body, mind, and soul to get what all of us want. A shot at the Champion. A shot at the belt that matters, the top of the heap. The big gold belt. Can I get it? Of course! I'm the Proletariot Champion! The Hero of the People! I am the Communist! *he chuckles to himself* And the others don't have a chance. :All's quiet for most of the flight. The buisnessman stays on his side of the seat, the stuardess brings some vodka. all is well until two men in ski masks stand up with guns: Hijacker: Everyone stay calm! We're taking this think to Cuba! :The sky marshal pulls his gun, only to take the butt of one hijackers gun in the forehead. He crumples like cheap siding. One hijacker walks up to the cockpit to give instructions to the pilots. The other looks around, keeping his gun on the passangers: Hijacker: No one plays hero, and no one gets killed. :The Communist waits untill the Hijacker passes him, then steps up behind him, stooped over, picks him up from behind and Communist Slams him onto the floor, then elbow drops him for good measure: Commie: Feel the Wrath of the People! :The second hijacker steps out of the cockpit just in time to get speared by the 7' Champion of the People. The Communist kicks the broken, unconsious body of the hijacker: Commie: Pilot, take us to Tokyo. *sighs* So much for a quite flight...
