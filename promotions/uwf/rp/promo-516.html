UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #516

Clearly Focused

July 8 (year unrecorded)

Slasher

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Focus, never before has a term been so rightly used. Focus is something that comes to a person when they have their eyes set on a goal that they wish to achieve. An invidiual can set their sights on a given task, but without the proper focus, that task can seem almost unreachable. For example, a person with paralysis in their legs, getting up to go to the bathroom can be a totally impossible task, but with the proper focus, it can be done. Any given task that a human being sets their mind to can be done, no question about it. Here we have two members of the drowningPool, Slasher and Unforgiven. Two of the most focused men ever to set foot in a wrestling ring. They know what they want, and hell itself wouldn't stop them from getting it. This Sunday, they have a shot at something that had eluded them on their last tag team run. The Tag Team Championships, which just seemed to vanish from them. If it wasn't for the inner politics of a federation, pushing somebody to be something they didn't want to be, the belts would have been around the waists of the Drowning Pool, no question about it. But Unforgiven was forced away, forced to do the bidding of the 'higher ups' in the federation. Doing this left Slasher in a kind of limbo, left him alone, pissed him off. Now, Unforgiven is free of having the politics, now doing what he wants, what he feels is right. The desire between these men to attain their goal is incredible, they both want the belts, and they will do anything to get them. Anyone who gets in their way, well.. I think it is pretty obvious. [The UWF office quickly pops into view, and the UWF production studio also zaps onto the screen. The room is fairly dark, with two chairs sitting in the middle of the room. Rick O' Connor can be seen sitting with a bunch of notes in front of him. The spotlight quickly bursts on and shines on him. The cameraman signals for the camera to start rolling.] Rick: Today UWF fans, we have a treat. We have a superstar who has bridged the gap between the GIWF and the UWF. He started out in the GIWF, known soley as being a 'nutcase'. The most common mold for a character these days, but now he has morphed and changed. Sure he is still a total nutbar, don't get me wrong. But never before have we seen or known a more focused Slasher. Folks, here he is, Slasher! [Slasher emerges from behind the curtains. He is wearing his mask, with his black boiler suit, and Doc Martins. His fingernails are painted black, and his hair is looking very wet and oily. Slasher walks over and puts his face about 2 inches from Ricks. Rick is visably shaken at this and squirms backwards in his chair. The mask of Slasher is mere inches from Rick, as Slasher backs up with a visible smile under his mask. He sits down and then just stares at Rick.] Rick.. um.. alright.. lets get this interview underway. [Slasher still sits motionless staring at Rick. Not moving at all, just breathing extremely slowly, watching the fear on Rick's face.] Rick: Ok.. this is just freaking me out. I don't know if I can do this. [Rick starts to get up when Slasher's right hand darts out, and motions for Rick to sit down.] Slasher: Sit the hell down you ignorant sack of shit. I heard what you said about me.. calling me a nutbar and a generic type of wrestler. You sit down, and you do this interview. I am telling you this once! You insult me EVER.. EVER AGAIN AND I WILL SHOVE YOUR HEAD SO FAR UP YOUR ASS YOU CAN WATCH YOUR FOOD TURN INTO SHIT! [Rick slams himself down is chair, beads of sweat can be seen forming on the forehead of Rick.] Slasher: Now.. where were we little Ricky.. [Rick grabs his papers and starts the interview before Slasher snaps on him again.] Rick: *ahem*.. *cough*.. Ok.. Slasher. Now, firstly how does it feel to be back in the UWF? Slasher: Wonderful.. amazing.. next question. Rick: erm.. What made you comeback? Slasher: What the hell do you think made me come back?? You know as much as anyone, what I love to do. I love to fight, I love to wrestle... I love to hurt people. I enjoy watching somebody suffer after I bash their face in with a steel chair.. ask Bondage how I feel about that. If you can recognize him, I bashed his head in so bad I think he may now look like somebody else. The main reason I came back, was the Tag Team Titles. Something Unforgiven and I need to have, we missed out on them last time, and now that we are back.. we want them more than ever! Rick: One question many people have been asking, what did you attempt to prove last week by burning your house down? Slasher: Proove? PROOVE?? TAKE A WILD GUESS!! Rick:... I am not sure... Slasher: The whole thing was about saying the hell with the past Rick.. the hell with it! It's about the future now! It's about Unforgiven and I finally getting a break in life. Finally getting something we are both longing for. You know and I know, those belts should have been ours last time we were here. If it wasn't for the god damn politics in this place, we would have had it to. People have to step in and make people do stuff the don't want to.. why.. ratings. Well you know what.. fuck ratings.. fuck everyone else in this place. WE are doing what we want.. not want everyone else wants. Those belts are coming to us.. the DP.. the best tag team this federation has ever seen! Rick: What are your feelings towards the current tag champs? Slasher: Digi.. known you a long time.. and don't give two shits if you live or die. Plain and simple.. you could die tomorow and would it affect me.. hell no. Your nothing to me.. a worthless sack of nothing. Your belt is going around my waist.. I don't care if I have to pick it off your rotting corpse.. I will have it. Moraless... same to you. Although I don't know you, and I don't really care if I ever do you know. Your just something in my way to what I want. If I maim you, blind you, I don't care.. just get the hell beat out of you, step aside and give me my title. Plain and simple. Your both dead men as far as I am concerned Rick: This past WNU, we saw an old friend of yours return to your group, as well as another help you. Slasher: Misfit.. one of my real friends in this world. Unforgiven and Misfit are two of my closest friends, and closest allies. I would trust them with my life, and for me to trust is a great statement. I don't usually trust anyone.. at all. These two however, have my trust. Misfit is back with us, where he belongs. He is back with a vengance, and Kenny Mercury is now set for the battle of his life against him. Misfit will destroy him, and leave nothing more than a total wake of destruction in his path. As far as Oni goes, he is a bit different than the rest of us, and I don't know him that well yet I will admit. But I see a fire in him, a burning desire to get what he wants.. and that is something we all share. Desire and focus.. something that the four of us have.. that nobody can take from us. Rick: What about the Knightmare Brigade? Slasher: The KB.. I am going to issue them a verbal warning.. do not interfere with us this Sunday.. do so and you are signing your own death warrant. I will tell you this once, you do anything to our match.. and I will personally see to it that each and every one of you is hospitalized for the rest of your life. Thats a promise, and trust me.. I can more than backup my words! Rick: Ok we have to cut this interview a bit short.. any closing comments for the fans? Slasher: This Sunday.. the belts are coming to us.. where they belong. UWF.. the drowningPool is going to finally get what they want, and after we crush Digi and Moraless.. the rest of you can line yourselves up, only to be knocked the hell down. Your all dominos.. and we are the hand knocking you all over.. and when we knock you over.. if you get back up.. WE HAVE NO PROBLEM KNOCKING YOU BACK DOWN AGAIN!!! AND AGAIN!!! AND AGAIN!!!!!!!!! [Slasher jumps up and lets out a massive roar, Rick looks terrified. Slasher grabs Rick and puts his face near Rick's.] Slasher: THE FACE OF FEAR RICK!!!! THE REST OF THE UWF WILL SEE THIS SAME FACE... AND IT WILL RUN RAMPANT ON THEIR ASSES!!!! [Slasher slams Rick back down into his chair and then storms off the set. Fade... to.. and off shade of black.]
