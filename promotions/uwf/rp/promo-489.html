Visiting the Betrayer -Part 1-

3 Jul 2004

The Real Deal — Outer Reaches of Tribal Village

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deal moves slowly down the little dirt track in the middle of what looks to be a forest. Completely surrounded by trees, Deal does not look to either side as he knows that he needs to make it to his destination as soon as possible. Recently he had started to loose his way. His mind was in torment with a number of things that he hadn't got to clear up earlier in his life and he had decided it was time to clear things up. Walking along slowly Deal enters a small opening in the forest. Making his way in all he can see is a number of vines accompanied by the trees that totally surround the area. With no warning of any kind Deal is somehow locked in by one of the vines and hoisted into the air. The silence was broken only by the rasping of the Real Deal's attempts to stay alive, but he knew inside of him that no matter what had happened, he had definitely alerted the attention of the man he sought.... 'Good evening.' The low voice grumbles from beyond vision and TRD knows exactly who it is. It's mocking tone and air of superiority reminds him why he had disliked this man so much, but the strength was still there, the strength that had served to fuel the love hate relationship between these two allies turned foes. Without a sound, Kaiowas, the Tribal Warrior moves out from the bushes ahead of where TRD is suspended in mid air. Taking a step forward, the moonlight hits the black man's chiselled face, shining off the deep black eyes and glinting off the one thing that this man never left behind. That double bladed staff that had been the downfall of many enemies. Flicking his dreadlocks back over his head. Kaiowas reveals a grimace born of the animosity he felt for this man held a few feet in the air before him. Drawing breath, in a way that only the Real Deal wishes he could right now, he begins to speak. Deal's arms frantically flurry around to find something to help support his huge weight which is now bring him to his untimely demise. His arms drop back down to his sides as he is unable to find support. His face now red and throat beginning to crush he somehow tilts his head to look straight at Kaiowas. His eyes bulge and pulse as if hell itself is about to explode from them. He takes as much of a breath that he can muster and gnarls at Kaiowas. 'Is this anyway to treat a visitor you son of a bitch!? You stand there as always felling that you are the king of all, even God if I could put it that far. You Kaiowas make my mind think of many violent things. I do not know why but your presense makes me feel like killing someone. Now release me you fool!' Kaiowas turns his head upwards to stare back at TRD, and slowly his grimace turns into a smile, and then a laugh, low and derisory.... 'I pity your plight, and your mouth still holds sway over your mind.... But I will not release you.... You have caused me too much trouble already' Kaiowas smiles once more, evilly, and turns, walking away from the clearing, into the darkness from whence he came... 'Unless of course, you have reason to need me, Real Deal...' Deal imagines how a smile would feel right now and it makes him feel alive enough to return a coment to Kaiowas. He watches as the Tribal Warrior begins to disappear and begins to yell out to him.. 'As usual Kaiowas you take the easy way out and walk away. I do not need you and I will never need you but I do need some information. For once in your fickle life are you going to listen for once and stop talking. You are a disgrace to mankind Kaiowas if you walk away now, you are no longer a man...' Kaiowas stops, slightly stung by the impune comment. 'A man, Real Deal? A man? I was once a man, simple as you, angry, bitter, foul mouthed. But the hell's that I have seen, I have experienced, and the things I have done..... I have transgressed humanity. But you..... I had respect for you, you fought well, you pushed me to my limits..... But you still have no respect for me....' Kaiowas turns around and drops his staff to the leafy forest floor. 'Which is why you cannot yet die......' Striding forth, he grabs TRD's boots with both arms, flexing his muscles as he heaves the full body weight of the Real Deal, taking the force away from his neck. Deal gasps wildly to recover every single bit of air back that had left his body before. His head swings back and forth as the blood rushes down through his body causing him to become nausiated to the extreme. He takes a moment and pulls in a number of deep breathes before calmly speaking to his capter... 'I want to set the record straight between us. Last time I saw you was in the DWO, the last match I had was against you in the DWO but after that everything disappeared. You disappeared, SurReal disappeared. You say I have no respect for you Kaiowas? Then maybe you have to think about it a little harder. Back when we fought it was one of the toughest matches of my life and you prevailed, that alone earned you my respect...' Deal lets out a number of rough coughs before turning his head to the side and vomited over the lush grass below him. He wipes his mouth dry with his arm and continues... 'What I want to know Kaiowas is where did you go? You just disappeared off the face of the planet, no word from you to anyone, not even your apprentice SurReal. Then he was the one who disappeared but I have been unable to locate him. What the hell is going on?' Kaiowas tightens his grip on the flailing legs of the Real Deal, intent on keeping him alive, but unable to escape. He looks up and the sickly sight of a man pulled from the edge of existence and sees the strange intense look of inquisitiveness in his deep eyes. Kaiowas sees that this man has lost himself, lost his life, and is trying to piece it back together... Calmer now, Kaiowas continues... 'Of SurReal I have heard nothing....... He betrayed me back when I fought for my living, and I expect he has got what he deserved..... He has been gone from our Tribe ever since he returned to America, he never embraced us like he had to.... I no longer can feel him in this mortal coil anymore. Whether that means he has passed on, or if he has just put up barriers to my searches I do not know.... only know that he is not important anymore.... He is a fragment of a life I left behind..... I disappeared because I still believe I have a higher purpose.... To serve this world without it knowing.... Even you must understand that the life of a supernatural cannot be broadcast weekly on FOX... I only melted into the background... I have not vanished....' Deal bears an evil grin as he looks down at Kaiowas. He had now realised that the two shared atleast one thing in common. That being that SurReal was no longer an issue in either of their lives. Suddenly the sensation of a proper oxygen supply going to Deal's brain forces him into a fit of laughter. He turns his head and stares straight Kaiowas, still laughing and replies... 'After a year of searching I decided to give up on SurReal, he was a nobody anyway, always dragging me and even you down. I am glad to see that he is finally out of the picture and I'm happy now that I was the one who finished him off to put him into retirement. For myself I have no purpose in my life and surely now to live is to die as I have no other option. When I raise up to a higher place my enemy I will see you there and things may change. As for now death is a virtue I must hold true to...' 'You have plans? I underestimated you old enemy, perhaps we may meet again sometime, when the powers of this world must collide....' Kaiowas looks to the sky, and then again at the Real Deal...He holds the glare for a minute straight, all the while slowly lifting the Real Deal higher above his head, muscles rippling with the effort. 'Or maybe we shall not........' Deal laughs and taps Kaiowas on the head before softly whispering to him... 'Just one thing before you go...Like broken glass you'll shatter...with bloodied fists I'll batter...Like a ten ton hammer son...' 'Your fate has been sealed old 'friend', couldn't your final words have been a little more poetic.........?' 'These are my final words...For once I opened my heart to you...For once I lifted up for you...But once again I'll fight to the end against you...but I will always hold my pride true' The quiet before the storm, Kaiowas begins to whisper those three dreaded words......... 'I am Betrayer.......' With that Kaiowas heaves upwards, freeing himself of the grasp he had around TRD's ankles, forcing the bulky man into a freefall with only one ending.....A broken neck. Before he even falls, Kaiowas turns and walks away, stooping to lift his weapon. Whispering to himself as he leaves the Real Deal gasping for his life..... 'I could not allow it..... No one must interfere with my plans, no one may rise to my level without at least dying..... once. I am the Angel of Vengeance and vengeance has been particularily sweet, Real Deal. Enjoy the firey pits of Hell, and make sure 'He' knows I sent you.......' Unbelievably Deal's head lifts up to watch Kaiowas making his way toward the exit of the forest opening. With the force of a thousand asteroids colliding with the surfaces of the earth he retaliates at the betrayer...His eyes once again begin to bulge with a glinting red but this time with extreme anger and hate...A sly grin moves across his face as he exclaims words he believe Kaiowas will never forget... 'My final will must be done and will be done for I am alls Saviour, the one who destroys those who try to place adversary apon others. I am destroyer, destroyer of those who betray and you Kaiowas are The Betrayer. The destroyer targets the betrayer will full lust for suffering and death...feel my presense when you leave here Kaiowas for I will always be with you...' Kaiowas does not stop or even turn to the words of a dying man. He leaves but three words, ringing in the ears of the Real Deal.... 'So be it.........' As Kaiowas leaves, The Real Deal can hear rustling from the bushes, he hears a faint whisper of a female from behind him... 'You may be alls Saviour but for now I'm saving you...' Deal's eyes light up as he hears the arrows flighting through the air. As it connects with the vine Deal drops to the ground. As soon as he lands he passes out. The feet of the female can be seen standing over him. 'You have been through alot dear Real Deal, enjoy your rest, you will need it....' The woman grabs Deal's arm and drags him to cover so that he is safe from anymore harm for the meantime... -To Be Continued-
