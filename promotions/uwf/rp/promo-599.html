UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #599

Tools of the Trade

5 Aug 2004

Mr. Meaner — An empty arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of an empty arena. The only thing present are the rings, the seats, the lights, the walls, the UniversalTron, the garters, the entrance ramp. OK, it’s not as empty as I said it was. Standing at ring side is the man in black. No, I’m not talking about Johnny Cash. I’m talking about Mr. Meaner. He is just standing against the ring, leaning against it.) Mr. Meaner: Now that everything is going according to plan, the UWF will soon be able figure out exactly what I’m all about. And when you figure out what I’m all about, well, you can count your days as numbered……. Of course, then again, for those of you out there who know me, there is no plan. There is only anarchy. There is no itinerary, only chaos. And figuring what I’m all about? If you haven’t figured that out yet, well, then you are the stupidest SOB that I have ever met and have ever wanted to meet on the face of this earth……. We are sitting now on the brink of the Regal Rumble, which is more or less controlled Chaos, and it would seem that we have pissed a few people off. It would seem there are a couple of people who aren’t happy with Chaos Society. And those names are going to have their chance to get back and get revenge with Chaos Society. Those people are the Communist and Angel. But first thing is first. And that is Sunday Afternoon Suicide. And that includes The Communist, Sacrifice, and THC Championship….. What a great match this is for me. No rules, anything goes, just beat the hell out of each other an hope to win. Just an all out fight. A brawl that won’t end until someone quits or is pinned. Can’t get any better than that. Right up my league. I’m use to this. This really is no different than a prison brawl….. We have the Communist, who I don’t even know how he got the belt. I mean communism is about nothing then telling everyone what they are going to do. How you can live in a world of no rules is beyond me. And on SAS, you bastard, you are going to find out exactly what it means to live in a world of no rules…. And Sacrifice, well, the jackass doesn’t even matter to me. He is just another jabroni who couldn’t tell the difference between a wristlock and a wristwatch. Hell, for that matter, in this kind of a match, a wristwatch would be more helpful for you. At least you can use that to do some damage to your opponent. Granted it won’t do you much good when your lying on the ground, but what the hell, your stupid enough to try…… But let’s think of some of the things I can do this week. Let’s think of some of the items I can use to my advantage here, shall we…… (Mr. Meaner picks up a steel chair.) Well, this is an old favorite. Seems that everyone is swinging these around now a days. But it’s still good, and it’s a whole heck of a lot better than Sacrifices wristwatch…… (Mr. Meaner then picks up a cord, probably a microphone cord.) Just think of what can be done with this. I can wrap this around the Communist thick neck and try to choke some sense into the idiot. Maybe he’ll finally realize that he has nothing that he can do to compete with me…… (Mr. Meaner then picks up his lead pipe and slaps it against his hand.) Of course, then you have my favorite. Something that can be swung like a steel chair and do just as much damage, or something you can wrap around your opponents throat and do more damage than a cord. Those are just a few of the things that I can do….. Just think of what you have waiting for you. You two POS don’t stand a chance against me in this match. You two would never stand a chance against me. You might as well just walk down and hand me the belt because that’s what you are basically going to be doing anyway. Besides, if you do that, you save yourself some pain….. In the end, I will be walking out of SAS with the THC Belt. Chaos Society will be with the THC Champion once again in their ranks. And then I will walk into Regal Rumble as THC Champion, where more of the same will happen, and I will walk out with the THC Belt plus a title shot at Live and Loud. For in the end, I won’t matter. I will win. I will accomplish greatness, and I will hurt you. My advice, either don’t show up or reserve a hotel room. (Meaner takes the lead pipe and smashes it straight through the lens of the camera causing it to black out with Meaner’s laughter being heard.)
