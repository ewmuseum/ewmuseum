Regal Rumble RP

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There are certain events for a wrestler, that always makes them excited, the prospect of a certain type of match or event, which makes their stomach tingle with anticipation. In UWF there is no denial that there was one match that every one looked forward to. The Regal Rumble, was more than just a main event match at a pay-per-view, it is more than a shot at the world title. The rumble forced you to do more than you had ever done before; it pushed you to the limits. You were placed into a ring with fifty-nine other men, some of which you may never have faced before. The term survival of the fittest simply didn’t come into play. The fittest wouldn’t survive; in a match of this magnitude no one survives not really. You can’t prepare strategy; you can’t decide who you are going to focus on, for as soon as you began to move towards one opponent another twelve may appear in their place. The regal rumble a place where dreams can be achieved as well as crushed in one fell swoop. In the living room of a small apartment, a large tabby cat is lying on an arm chair, it is staring forwards with a look of disinterest as a body in front of it dashes about frantically. This body of course is John ‘Moraless’ Edwards complete with large black marker pen in his hand and a flip chart in front of him. “This is just impossible, it’s not going to happen!” shouts John as he flicks the over one of the large A1 pieces of paper, revealing a list of all the competitors of the regal rumble. He glances down the names on the piece of paper and begins to slowly but surely circle a large selection of the names. As he does this, the cat, which is sat on, the armchair begins to pad around slightly, stretching as digging his claws deep into the upholstery. John genuinely thought that the cat cared, for some reason the cat always stuck around, despite it attacking John if he got within just a few feet of it. After so much rejection, John had decided that animals were the way to go. Finally John’s had stopped skirting over the paper and he stepped back glancing at the multitude of circles that had suddenly appeared, his head dropped into his hands and he allowed them to slide upwards and through his hair, shaking his head as he did so. “How the hell am I going to be able to do that?” He turned and asked his cat, whom he had lovingly named Humphrey, who by now had, had enough, hissed at John slightly before leaving the armchair and wondered off upstairs. It was true what they said cats were so much more intelligent than dogs, they don’t stick around morons. John turned away from the flip chat and allowed himself to collapse into the chair, his fingers pressed lightly together as he peered over the top of them. His whole idea of drawing circles around those who he felt posed the most potent threat was intended to try and give him some focus, allow him to work out who exactly to avoid, or who to try and eliminate as soon as possible. In reality though it had just made John a worried man, when you didn’t include the mystery entrants and when you didn’t include the three losers from the world title match who were always going to pose a threat, a total of twenty five people, who posed a threat. Twenty-five people who had more than enough ability to take him out of the rumble. So many with a vendetta against him. John was used to rumbles, his whole adventures at PTC extreme champion, had begun with an extreme rumble. Weapons were flying all over the shop, as were bodies and blood. Many people felt that extreme wrestling was no more than a few rest holds before smacking people with chairs or large chunks of wood wrapped in razor wire. It was all good fun really, nothing that a few stitches wouldn’t fix. John sighed deeply his chest expanding as he did so, one name of the flip chart leaped out at him as his eyes were drawn constantly to it. Sean McClancy, the Irish bastard. That wasn’t John’s opinion that was the nickname Sean had given himself. “You are going to try and make me suffer aren’t you” The words flowed very quietly and slowly from John’s lips as his eyes remained focused on Sean’s name. Despite only facing each other once the match was so brutal and memorable that a certain legacy remained with it. Whispers had gone around UWF, wondering when the two would finally face each other again, after Sean McClancy joining the federation and making his mark by destroying John at Stable Wars, with a little help from the Knightmare Brigade. Deep down John was looking forwards to finally being in the same ring as Sean again, the chance to get a bit of retribution for the humiliation he suffered at Stable Wars, however what he was not looking forwards to was being a lone man in there. When Sean had his stable mates around him. John rose to his feet and pulled the flipchart down, placing it in the corner of the room, before moving towards the window, which looked out over the street. It was a rough neighbourhood, but something about it felt like home, you left them alone and they left you alone. It suited John down to the ground. The regal rumble would be the first time that John would have to be in the same ring with Kennedy and Mortality after the split of Resurrection. There was no major announcement on any show. After Bondage’s injury at Stable Wars there was a quiet meeting between the final three members and they had agreed to call time on it. At the end of the day, everyone had their own agendas as of Stable Wars; their mission had been completed. The Resurrection said that through any means possible they wanted to improve UWF, the said that even if they had to burn the federation to the ground and rebuild it, they would. The threats were taken seriously, members of the past came out of the shadows to protect the federation they had once called home, to save the legacy they once new, in the process returning UWF to a golden age. The frown on John’s face showed that he was worried about it, his eyes squinting as he continued his gaze out of the window. Would Digital Mortality and Kennedy work with him in order to eliminate stronger opposition, would there be a Resurrection revival for one night only, or would it be the opposite? Would they attack each other, thinning the field knowing each other’s moves and strategies? It was what the regal rumble was all about, the clichés had been used before but it truly is every man for himself. John turned away from the window and walked through to his kitchen and flicked the kettle onto boil. In times of deep thought, a brew was always crucial. He would simply do what he could at the regal rumble. His time would come eventually, after all his luck couldn’t be bad forever. Outside an attractive looking woman was walking slowly down the street pulling a large black carry case behind her. Her natural beauty set her apart from the surroundings, which looked extremely, drab and run down. She pulls a small piece of paper out of her hand and looks at the writing on it before glancing across the street, her eyes looking over the dilapidated building with a slight look of distain, her agenda however was to see the person inside, not the building. Someone she had not seen in four years. In the kitchen the kettle had not boiled, John poured the boiling water into a mug which had ‘the worlds greatest’ written on the side and finished making his cup of tea, taking a sip from it, feeling the warm drink slide effortlessly down his throat. He would have time for this and would then have to head off to the airport his flight to Tokyo was leaving in a few hours and even though he had booked in early, he still wanted some time to chill out at the airport bar. A knock came from John’s front door, letting out a short sigh he wondered towards the hallway and the front door. He never really liked opening the front door, not round here. The kids round this area were little bastards and he half expected to see a burning bag of crap on his doorstep. Was he going to be in for a shock. As he opened the door his face dropped, it was literally like opening the door to the past. In front of him stood a woman, whose face he would never forget and the body that he would never forget either for that matter. The cup, which he was holding, dropped to the floor and shattered on the wood stained floor below him. There was a moment’s silence. “Louise?” John asked his voice suddenly going extremely high. The past can always come back to haunt you, hell it could come back and be your saviour but when it happens just hours before a match that could determine your future. You have to feel somewhat cheated, as though someone is playing with you somewhere up there.
