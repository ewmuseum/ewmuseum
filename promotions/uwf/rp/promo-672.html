The Knightmare Continues

5 Sep 2004

Seymour Almasy — almasy apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hardcore is not simply a matter of hitting another man over the head with a cookie sheet. Hardcore is a state of being. A frame of mind. A way of life. Men such as Slasher, Raid, and Nick Persia are not truly hardcore. No, not in the least. To understand TRUE hardcore, you must observe Seymour Almasy in his native environment. Levelling up Final Fantasy characters. ---- The hour is late. In the Almasy apartment, all of the lights are out, save for one. This light burns brightly in the game room of the well-furnished apartment. On the couch sits Seymour Almasy. His eyes are bloodshot, and a short growth of black beard covers his face and neck. Adrenaline pumps through his veins, as he shouts at the object of his ire. Seymour Almasy: DIE CURSED MALBORO! SquareEnix’s hit Final Fantasy X plays on Almasy’s screen, as Seymour attempts to control his party in an effort to bring down the creature, Seymour Almasy: Blast it! If this thing dies, I’ll finally have Rikku’s sphere grid maxed! The next few moments are an unintelligible jumble of battle cries, frantic button-pushing combinations, and exhortations of “DIEYOUSONUVABITCHMALBORODIE!”, until finally the creature is reduced to pyre flies, giving Seymour’s team the win. Seymour Almasy: Yes yes YES!! The ecstatic Almasy collapses back to the couch, letting the controller fall from his hands. Seconds later, he is back on his feet, doing some sort of ritualistic prayer to the Final Fantasy gods. His loud stomping and whooping, however, appears to have had a side effect. Aya: What in the name of hell are you doing up at 3:00 AM? Yes, Seymour’s loud revelry has woken up his new companion, Aya. Unlike his ex-wife, Aya does not seem to be so understanding of Seymour’s oddities and quirks. Aya: I mean JESUS! Some people are trying to sleep! Seymour Almasy: I’m sorry, I just got excited, that’s all. The former Japanese prostitute shook her head, and sighed deeply. She hadn’t signed on for this when she had agreed to accompany Seymour back to America. Still though, she couldn’t be mad at him for too long. Almasy was delusional, yes, and even evil now. However, there was one thing that even an evil Seymour Almasy was. Very, very naïve. Aya: It’s okay, Seymour…why are you up this late anyway? Seymour Almasy: Because, Aya, I am preparing for my THC contendership match. Aya: How the hell are you doing that? The shock on her face was evident. She apparently does not realize Almasy’s preparation techniques, and their power….or maybe she thinks he’s a nut. Your call. Seymour Almasy: My opponents all believe that they are hardcore because they can swing a weapon, or withstand great amounts of pain. In that, they are mistaken. Hardcore is a mindset. It is being willing to go to any extreme, pay any price to achieve a goal. Slasher can carve others up with barbed wire, but could he turn his back on a family member if he needed to? Would Slasher betray the Drowning Pool? No. And that is why, despite all of his outward appearances, he is not hardcore, and I am as hardcore as they come. Aya: That’s all fine and dandy, but what the fuck does that have to do with the video game? Seymour Almasy: By staying up with no food or water, and ignoring sleep, I am making self sacrifice in order to achieve the goal of leveling my party up. Just as I will sacrifice anything in order to achieve my professional goals. Aya: Good for you. Although, one of your opponents really doesn’t seem to like your plan to win the match. Seymour Almasy: Opponents generally don’t. The statement is said with a quaint nod, as if to make sure that Aya appreciates its profundity. Aya, however, is not so easily impressed. Aya: Nick Persia’s pretty much ready to kill you. Seymour Almasy: Oh boy, oh boy! A former Player’s Club member, one of the many ex-goons of the Canadian idiot that passes as our reigning UWF World Champion Mike Montgomery, an utter waste of existence, Nick Persia is ready to kill me. ALERT THE NEWS MEDIA! MY LIFE IS IN GRAVE DANGER! Aya: Cut the sarcasm for a moment! I’m not joking, I’m dead serious. He’s pissed. Really, REALLY pissed. Seymour Almasy: Oh. Really? Perhaps I did neglect him a bit in my efforts to explain to Slasher that his health was in grave danger. Even one as great as I am can have an oversight every once in a while… The former Global Champion smirks, and slowly begins to shake his head side to side. Seymour Almasy: But this is not one of those times. Oddly, the Final Fantasy breaks out into applause, clapping slowly at first, and then faster and faster, more maniacally, as giggles escape his lips. Seymour Almasy: I admit it. Nick Persia has beaten me. But then again, lots of people have beaten me. Mike Montgomery’s beaten me. There are many, many men in the wrestling world that can say they’ve defeated Seymour Almasy, and believe me, they use that fact to pad their resume. But, poor delusional Persia, there is a small fact I must enlighten you upon. This match….well, means something. Aya turns away for the moment, as the increasingly megalomaniacal Almasy continues to cackle. Seymour Almasy: Ask the amount of people who have beaten me in a title match…or a contendership match, or a tournament match…and the answer you will receive will be very small indeed. In singles tournament competition, I have lost a grand total of two matches. Two. Out of four tournaments, I have lost two matches. Doesn’t that tell you something, Nick Persia? It should. And since it probably doesn’t due to the fact that your brain isn‘t even the size of a newly born piece of materia, I will inform you of what it should tell you. Aya: I think he probably gets the idea. Seymour Almasy: Maybe, but with Rabites such as him, it is necessary to pound the point home. Aya: I’ll take your word for it. Seymour Almasy: Excellent. Nick Persia, what it SHOULD tell you is that when it comes to the big match, I may as well have taken a Holy War before it, because I am UNTOUCHABLE! It took a PCW World Champion and one of the greatest athletes in our sport, Xavier Kannon, to defeat me in a tournament. You have the credentials of neither. But, to be fair, you do have one very good credential. You’re the longest reigning THC Champion, ever. But, you’re looking at a man who can do you one better. I held the TV Title longer than anyone. Defending it at every show, against all comers. And even more importantly, I have held the UWF World Heavyweight Title, the top prize in this company, longer than anyone. Have you even gotten a SHOT at it yet, Mister Persia? Probably not, but even if you did, you obviously never won it, considering your name isn’t on the list of champions. Arrogance now seems to drip from Almasy’s every pore. He continues to speak, cocky swagger intact, the clear sign of a man who has begun to believe his own hype. Seymour Almasy: I can ask for a shot at every title because I AM SIMPLY GOOD ENOUGH TO! Anyone could make a request such as that, and it would lead to them losing every time out. Me, on the other hand…the UWF head honchos know as it is that I’m too good for this little pisshole of a federation! Why do you think they pay me more than anyone else on the roster to be here? I bring in the publicity, the money…THE MERCHANDISE SALES! Can YOU do that, Persia? Damn STRAIGHT you can’t. If I want a shot at every title in the company, I will damn well get it. And you know what? I’m starting here. With the THC Championship. After I trample you, Slasher, and Raid, I’m going to disembowel that Mr. Meaner character, and pick up the THC Title, and hold it for a year. And along the way, I will get every single other title in this company, and find someone on this roster, difficult as it may be, worthy enough to be my partner, and we will get the tag titles. Aya’s jaw was somewhere between her kneecaps and the floor, as Seymour put an arm around her. Seymour Almasy: This woman here is my only friend in the world right now. All others are enemies. And that means you Raid, you Slasher, and ESPECIALLY you Nick Persia. You wish to challenge the Final Fantasy. You are a brave, brave man…but also a very silly one. Bottom line: the title shot comes back with me this Sunday. And anyone who disputes this fact is living in a fantasy world. (Fin)
