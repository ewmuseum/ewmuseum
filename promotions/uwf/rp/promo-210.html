UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #210

Two fans, one loooooong queue

9/2/2004

Joshua Dunn — UWF meet and greet Monday 9th February 2004

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Hero’s, everyone’s heard about them, everyone has met their own personal hero, be it a sports personality, or just their undying respect for their father, hero’s are mentioned throughout history. But with Hero’s comes villains, and a villain is a lot harder to define, especially in the realm of sports entertainment. In wrestling athletes go from the most beloved to the most hated almost daily, and the greatest hero can turn anyone into a villain just by stepping in the ring against them. This is also true for when the most hated man in sports history steps in with some completely unknown person, they become the hero almost instantly. What happens when both men are completely neutral? Neither one better than the other. It can go either way, and usually does, with the crowd cheering on the man who’s got the upper hand at that moment, and usually the label of the Hero changes hands more often than the TV title. Could this be what we can expect to happen in the Clash? Two monuments of the opposite end of the scale meeting to give meaning to the namesake of the PPV? Or could the tarnished pasts of each individual meaning that the Hero is the victor? Of course I am talking about the Mount Gagazet Death match II Dunn -v- Almasy for the IC title, as which other match up could be so monumental or so intermediate at the same time? But going into the Clash neither man has spoken to the cameras of UWF TV and Dunn has already said how much he’s looking forward to the match up. So what hasn’t been said already?] [The scene opens, and we find ourselves looking at a promotional poster for the Clash PPV, a reflective shine of light passes over it as the camera pans out slightly to bring it into full view, the imagery is quite good, at the top and leading to the bottom are the three singles titles of the UWF in order of reputation, World Title at the top, then the IC title and finally the world title, and super imposed over each is a faint picture of the stars involved, Beezlebozo and Ruff Ryder over the World, Dunn and Almasy over the IC and Jericho and Iron Lung over the THC. Each image of the superstar is looking angrily at their opponent, mostly with their teeth showing in some grimace of hatred, apart from Beezlebozo who has an even more worrying insane smile, reminiscent from the Joker from the batman comics. The words under this image clearly read, “When enemies Clash, Legends are made” and over in the lower right hand corner the UWF logo presides, like on all its produce. Now the camera moves over to the other side of the cameraman. We see several people of either sex standing in an orderly queue to a cubicle somewhere further off, a sign above said cubicle explains that the people are standing to get autographs of the UWF IC Champ Joshua Dunn at yet another meet and greet held every so often by the owners of the UWF, and that most athletes turn up to, but with the recent cuts in the roster, the amount of stalls are dramatically smaller, and larger queue’s have developed. Each member of the queue are talking amongst themselves and are holding some sort of UWF produce, from posters to dolls that they hope will be signed by their favourite star, or just the chance to be next to the man to hold the longest current reign in UWF history, as we can tell from every now and again a camera flash going off at the far end of the queue] [Some of these people are right next to the camera probably unaware that it’s turned on and are starting to talk about the man in question, and the camera picks up every word. Two mates standing side by side each holding a poster, non descript because the camera can only tell so much from a rolled up piece of paper, start to talk to each other] Man1: Wow, I can’t believe we’re finally going to meet Joshua Dunn; this has to be one of those defining moments in my life Man2: Oh yeah you’re right, I really have to ask him what he thinks of Almasy, you know outside of wrestling. Man1: He so obviously hates him; it’s written all over his face isn’t it Man2: Maybe, but I say Dunn’s really an all right guy, you know, he can’t really be a communist, you don’t get any of those outside Cuba, they’re banned aren’t they? Man1: How am I supposed to know? I’m not a close personally friend of him am I? Man2: Oh man how cool would that be, to have someone like Dunn as your friend? Man1: Way cool man, almost as cool as if spider man were real Man2: Oh man yeah. You think he could actually keep his title away from Seymour? I mean Almasy’s had the longest World title reign, and held every title apart from the IC title not many people have beaten him. Man1: He was beaten by a clown man, of course Dunn would beat him, and as for being so close to the Grand slam, Dunn’s pretty close to that two you know, he just needs the Tag titles and the World title, and he must be close to a shot now? I mean how long does somebody need to be in the UWF before you get a world title shot? Man2: Four matches apparently, that’s how many beezle was in before he was put in the ring with Seymour, I think anyway. [One of the guy’s glances ahead and spy’s movement] Man1: Heads up man were moving [They both break off the conversation for a moment to take one single solitary step forward, just that little closer towards Dunn] Man2: What if he is really communist? He looks pretty convincing on the telly doesn’t he? Man1: You think there really is a talking monkey in the UWF? Man2: Pfft! No, monkeys can’t talk Man1: Well there you are then, he won’t be communist Man2: Jees Carl, why didn’t I think on that? Carl: Quite simple Ron, I’m a genius. [The man now known as Carl exaggerates his speech to a poor attempt at a Posh English accent while Ron laughs] Ron: Of course you are Oh lord Carl. [They laugh a while before a quick look of shock motions over Ron’s face] Ron: Christ! I left my pen in the car, I totally forgot Carl: Shut up you dumb ass, Joshua will have his own pen; besides, you need more than a biro to write on a poster Ron: Phew! Scared myself for a minute there, thanks Carl: Yeah well what are friends for? Ron: God the line’s huge, it’ll take us ages to get to the front Carl: Worth the wait if you ask me, by the looks of things Dunn isn’t going anywhere fast Ron: True, how long has he been IC champ? Carl: About 4 or 5 months I think Ron: Jesus, not even Tsunami Oni held it that long Carl: I know, it’s like everyone can’t touch him Ron: What about Seymour? Carl: Of course HE’LL be able to touch him, I mean he was the longest reigning World Champ, that kind of wrestler can’t help but touch people Ron: You make him sound like BBB Carl: Now you mention it they do look kind of alike Ron: Oh yeah, I never saw it before, I do now, but, nah, no nothing like him really Carl: Your right, I was only joking Ron: Thought so… Hold up someone’s moving up front [Sure enough the eagle eyes of Ron have spotted something moving up front, and as they, and everyone else in the queue look on they can see Joshua Dunn moving out from behind the cubical and making his way away from the crowd. He’s met with cheers from the people further back in the crowd who haven’t seen him since they last switched on their TV. But Dunn just walks on not even looking towards them. Behind Dunn another man is walking out from the queue, and he’s dressed like a UWF lackey and is carrying a megaphone, he switches it on and calls out to the crowd] Man: Can I have your attention, your attention please? [This successfully manages to get the crowd, including Carl and Ron to quieten down to listen to what he has to hear] Man: I’m sorry to tell you that Joshua has taken a break for lunch and will be back within the hour, sorry for the inconvenience [This is met with a distasteful reaction from the crowd, consisting mainly of a huge “AAAAAW” from them collectively and breaks down into separate conversations] Ron: Aww man, you can tell why he’s a heel now can’t you Carl: True, now we got another hour to wait Ron: Well I’m not moving; I’m not going to the back of the queue again Carl: Me neither, I don’t think anyone else is either, but if they do, we’ll be closer than ever Ron: Yeah, but what we going to do here for an hour? Carl: I don’t know [They look dejected for a moment before Carl turns around and spots the camera recording him; he nudges Ron in the ribs to get his attention] Carl: We’re on TV man, just there behind us [Ron turns round and also spots the camera, as if it were a natural response he starts jumping up and down waving] Ron: Hey Mom, I’m on TV! [As if it were a signal, near one hundred heads turn round and start shouting at the camera, each grasping for their own five minutes of fame, and with that, the cameraman takes his cue to leave for his lunch break too] [The Scene Fades to Black]
