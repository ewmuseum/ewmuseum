UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #364

A combined Monkey See/Monkey Do - Commie Scott production

24 Apr 2004

Mike Montgomery — Yes, you just read most of this. Deal with it.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Well, isn’t this an interesting situation for our esteemed Commissioner? Tomorrow he will step into the ring with the one and only Beelzebozo for the World Heavyweight title. Not only that, but stablemate Joshua Dunn will face the entire Resurrection by himself in a Gauntlet match…the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. The tag titles have been lost to the Resurrection and nobody’s entirely sure whether or not Montgomery was aware of his title match before today.] [In this combined production, we open the scene in the Commissioners Office, which is currently occupied by the UWF Commissioner himself, Mike Montgomery. He’s sitting in his executive swivel chair looking at some papers. He’s got his feet up on the desk, and is currently lighting up a Cuban cigar. He lights it and lays his Zippo down on the table before drawing in a large plume of smoke and exhaling it in an equally extravagant manner. There is a knock on the door of the office. Montgomery takes another draw on the cigar before opening his mouth to acknowledge the person on the other side of the door] MM: Come in [The door opens slowly, and standing in the doorframe is none other than gauntletee Joshua Dunn. Perhaps now the Promotion will make more sense. Monty lets out a puff of smoke as he swings his legs off the table in a newfound urgency and beckons the leader of the stable he founded into his office] MM: Josh! Come in come in, what an unexpected surprise, come in, have a seat [Dunn enters with an almost profound air about him and sits down on the chair offered, as he does so, Mike leans back and starts to smoke on the Cuban again] JD: Nice office you have here Monty, I can see why you wanted the commissioners job now MM: Mmm, the job does have it’s perks, but as I’ve said before, I was doing the job for most of Jamie’s tenure. I simply felt it was about time that I got the recognition for it. [Dunn grins for a moment, but a solemn look soon replaces it, he’s got more important matters to be attending to] JD: I’ve got to tell you, people are far more sceptical that it was actually me out there challenging the whole of Resurrection to a gauntlet match; you know that people were thinking that I’ve retired? MM: I must say, I’m rather surprised they think I’d let you go that easily. As for this gauntlet match, I must say you have me rather intrigued by this. The entire Resurrection? Think you’re up to something that audacious? [A trademark sideways Grin passes across Dunn’s face] JD: No audacious plan, other than getting the fans on our side [He snorts out a laugh. Montgomery, in turn, grins, perhaps seeing Dunn’s logic.] MM: Ah, but contrary to popular belief, we already have a good portion of the fans on our side. Though more support can certainly never hurt. However, I must admit to some concern. Despite the current situation, I intend to see to it you remain in the World Title picture, and I’ll need you at a hundred percent. JD: Don’t worry about me; I’ve done some things that can make grown men faint. Technical wrestling is a true mans wrestling. Besides, I win, we win, I lose we still win MM: Really? You lose and we still win? Interesting logic. Please, enlighten me. [Dunn grins] JD: Well, I win, that means I’ve taken out an entire three man stable by myself. Resurrection is completely humiliated. I lose all three matches, I’ve still gone through hell to prove something, we’ll have the fans on our side, and we all know where the money comes from for our paychecks. MM: Why Mr. Dunn, I truly had no idea how well you’ve grasped the rules of the game. Sheer brilliance, I am very, very impressed. [Mike takes another puff of the cigar] JD: Hmm, I am far from just your muscle, and you better remember that. MM: Don’t worry Joshua. If you were simply my muscle, I’d never have given you leadership of the Player’s Club. Cigar? [Mike holds up a second cigar from a box on his desk.] JD: Thought you’d never ask [Dunn reaches over and takes the cigar offered to him; he also accepts the light from Monty as he offers it] JD: I didn’t know you smoked [Dunn Puffs on the cigar now also, adding to the new smoky atmosphere of the office] MM: Something about the power of a position like mine. Makes a good Cuban too good to pass up. [Another plume of smoke goes up from both parties] JD: Useful thing the power of your position, lets you give yourself major title shots like the one this SAS. MM: Yes…that. [Mike has practically spit the words out, forcing them into sound.] MM: That…is a bit of a problem. This will sound strange, and I certainly don’t expect you to believe what I’m about to say right off the bat, but I never made that announcement. I don’t know who did, but it wasn’t me. [Dunn’s eyes narrow] JD: You’re right, I don’t believe you MM: Well consider this. When someone comes out to the ring to make an announcement or cut an interview, the microphone is left behind afterward. They don’t take it from the ring with them. The announcement of my world title match comes after my leaving the ring…when I didn’t have the mic. Second, in all the years I have been with this company, I have never had an actual world title match before now. Why would I so blatantly disrespect such an occasion by treating such a glorious opportunity as a mere afterthought? JD: I’ve been through to much to just believe what people tell me, you should know that. I can bend people to my way of thinking, no matter what their state of mind. MM: True. Ask yourself this. What would I have to gain by throwing myself into the title picture now? I wanted the Intercontinental Title, but held off when Ezra got it. The belt was with the Player’s Club and that’s what matters. When you win the World Title, the belt will be with the Player’s Club and that’s what matters. What would I gain? Power? Money? Fame? I already have those. What benefit could I derive from interfering in your world title quest? [Dunn lets out a thoughtful puff] JD: I see your point. Still it’s hard to believe someone would do something like that. But where I come from, your innocent until proven guilty. MM: Excellent. Now let me assure that no matter what happens, whether Beelzebozo wins or I do, there shall indeed be a title shot in your future. And as for whoever was playing around in the audio department, they will certainly pay for that. JD: Yes. But you better be intending in getting the Tag Titles back. We can’t have Resurrection Mocking us like that MM: One thing at a time Joshua. This is but one battle in what looks to be a long, bloody, drawn out war. We’ll have the titles back, of that I have no doubt. For now though, we need to focus on tomorrow. [Another dual puff goes up] JD: I want to make it perfectly clear that I don’t want either you or Ezra near the Gauntlet matches. MM: While I certainly don’t speak for Ezra, so long as it’s one on one, you won’t see my face. I have the utmost faith in you, not to mention my own opportunity to focus on. JD: I’m serious, if I even see you at the top of the ramp, I’m walking. This is non negotiable. MM: Like I said, I won’t be there so long as it’s just one on one. If you and Bondage are in a match and Digital Mortality decides to interfere, I’m coming down. I have to protect my investment. JD: Investment my good eye, you set foot in the arena, I’m gone. Clear? Finito. No more. I don’t care if all three of them decide to come in, I’ve set this gauntlet for myself, not the Players Club. I get through this on my own or not at all. MM: Now let me make myself clear. You Mr. Dunn are a talent. Along with Ezra, you are possibly one of the hottest and fastest rising stars this company has ever seen. The people love you. The people want to see you in the main event, where you belong. That makes you the future of the UWF. That makes you an incredibly important commodity which must be protected. [Mike takes another puff of the cigar, a thought suddenly occurring to him.] MM: Though perhaps this is all a moot point. The Resurrection is in a very precarious position tomorrow. They cannot afford to lose to you, and as you said, you cannot lose. If you win and defeat all three members of the Resurrection by yourself, then their credibility as a force to be reckoned with is completely and utterly destroyed. Can they truly afford that? Would they do themselves more harm than they would you if they interfered? [Dunn grins] JD: I’m glad you see things my way, indeed, whatever the outcome we’re the victors. We will be the people the crowd yearn to see in the spotlight. MM: And that will be the beauty of it. No matter what happens, we are so firmly entrenched in the UWF that we will always be on top. JD: I have to admit; I can’t wait to get my fists into Digital again. There’s so many things left unsaid [With that he massages his knuckles. Mike raises an eyebrow at Dunn.] MM: I suspect that there won’t be much actual talking. [Dunn’s knuckles crack, that’s all the reaction to the question Montgomery gets] MM: Just think if all goes well…the Resurrection humiliated…the World Title under the control of the Player’s Club… [Mike takes a long drag off the cigar, a grin crossing his face as the smoke escapes his lips.] JD: Nice thought isn’t it. [Mike just laughs for a moment before taking another drag.] MM: “I see... the way... you look at me… you envy me, and it’s killing you…” Indeed. Sometimes it’s just good to be a player. JD: Yes you’re right, I do envy you, your going after the title that I would genuinely kill for, one of the very few that I still haven’t held. Still it is good to be a player. If you win you win, and that’s the end of it [He cracks the knuckles of his other hand] MM: Indeed. Just remember, like I said, you will remain #1 contender, and no matter what happens tomorrow, you *will* get a shot. [Dunn grins once more before letting out a satisfied puff of smoke] JD: That would be much appreciated. But I better get ready for tomorrow. I shall be in the Gym if you want to join me [Dunn gets up and opens the door to the office] MM: I’ll see you there in a bit. I’ve just got to get a few things taken care of here. [The door closes behind Dunn, leaving Montgomery alone with his cigar. He closes his eyes and smiles before continuing his song.] MM: “And you know it makes me smile when I look in the mirror, thinkin’ how good it is to be king…” Yes, tomorrow will be a most interesting day indeed.
