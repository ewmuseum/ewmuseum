Ask an Enhancement Talent Luchador

1 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 August 2006 Pajaro de Presa and White Tiger. Some call them the Masked Monkeys. Some call them Los Mucho Macho Mascaras. Some call them to mop something up in the restroom. For years, they have laid down for more sweaty men than Ryan-o's mom. I keed, I keed... Ryan's mom likes to be on top. And now is your chance to ask them anything you want. Pajaro and Tiger have allowed me to act as a go-between the public and themselves. See, masked jobbers can't afford computers and all the local libraries have banned them due to what Pajaro did to their old issues of National Geographic. So ask away! Pajaro de Presa and White Tiger are open for anything... but not in the same way that Ryan-o's mom is. ---- 1 August 2006 Is the Masked Monkey, Pajaro I think it was, still in love with Karina from PRIME? If so has he come any closer to winning her heart since she kicked his head in on a ReVolution show last year? ---- 1 August 2006 [Los Mucho Macho Mascaras are sitting on a bed with a leopard print blanket. Gauzy red drapes hang everywhere.] Pajaro: Hola, amigos! We are coming at you from the bedroom of White Tiger's cousin, Chiquita Chimichanga. She's one of those web cam girls that do naughty things on the internet for money. Chiquita is getting a Brazilian bikini wax, so we have her camera to ourselves! White Tiger: MY cousin? I thought she was YOUR cousin! Pajaro: Are you kidding? Would I have pictures of my own flesh and blood doing obscene things involving a tequila bottle and a chihuaha? White Tiger: Yes, yes you would. Pajaro: Yeah, you're right. White Tiger: So neither of us actually know this chica. Which means we're breaking and entering. Pajaro: So I guess we better answer as many questions as we can before Chiquita comes back and pepper sprays us. White Tiger: Well, a youngster named Dean wants to know if you ever won the heart of Karina Wolfenden. Pajaro: Ah... my sweet Kare-Bear! We had a short but torrid affair after our meeting in PRIME. A very PHYSICAL relationship, if you know what I mean. White Tiger: Are you talking about the PTC tag title match where she smacked you around all over the ring? Pajaro: Some might call it that. I just remember we made a genuine connection. White Tiger: You mean when we got the fluke win and you pinned her? Not exactly the consumation of an epic romance... Pajaro: I consumated... in my tights! White Tiger: Madre dios... please tell me you've done laundry since then. Pajaro: Maybe I should look up Kare-Bear again... White Tiger: And I'll make the appointment with the dentist. And proctologist. Pajaro: Amigo! A car just pulled up! White Tiger: Well, we gotta go. So to answer your question, Dean, Pajaro de Prsea didn't win Karina Wolfenden's heart, but he did touch her boobs. Pajaro: Sweet maracas of fun... [Just then, a female voice screams. Los Mucho Macho Mascaras quickly dive out the nearest window.] ---- 2 August 2006 Q. Last time time I checked the going rate for full service from Ryan-o's mom was twenty bucks. Has it changed since? Q2. And how awesome would it be if you two went to another federation under the identity of the "masked wrestlers" only to reveal yourselves to actually be masked wrestlers? ---- 2 August 2006 [An orange video tape arrives at PTC headquarters. Upon viewing a serious-looking but attractive young woman is standing on a busy street corner with an orange megaphone.] Young woman: In 1987, tobacco company executi-hey! What the hell? [At that moment, Los Mucho Macho Mascaras walk into view. Pajaro de Presa grabs the orange megaphone.] Pajaro: Sorry, chica. We need to borrow your camera. Young woman: Masked men trying to stop the truth! I bet you're from Big Tobacco! White Tiger: More like Big Jabroni... Young woman: You won't stop me from shoing people the truth about smoking! White Tiger: Listen, lady. I don't smoke. But your commercials make me want to buy cigarettes just to piss you guys off. Pajaro: And what's with the dude with the dreads and droopy eyes? I don't think someone that looks totally baked should be in anti-smoking commercials. Maybe you should have chicas with big maracas in them. They could say stuff like "we don't wanna mess these lungs up with smoke!" White Tiger: That's brilliant. Pajaro: Really? White Tiger: No. You're an idiot. Pajaro: Oh. Young woman: You haven't heard the last of me! You can't silence the truth! [She storms off out of view.] Pajaro: Ay yi yi... I hate to see her go, bit I love to watch her walk away! White Tiger: Well, we got business to take care of. We got a letter here from Syko Arsonist... Pajaro: Holy frijoles! What a hottie! Did she get the name Arsonist because she is one muy caliente mamacita? White Tiger: Pajaro... Pajaro: I think I'm in love! Hey, Syko... have you ever been with a Latino man? White Tiger: Pajaro... Pajaro: Once you've had chicano, the rest is just guano! White Tiger: Pajaro! Pajaro: Simmer down, amigo. Maybe she's got a sister. White Tiger: SHE is a HE, Pajaro. Pajaro: What? You talkin' loco, vato. Look at the maracas! White Tiger: That isn't Syko Arsonist. That's just his avatar on PTC's forums. He's a guy named [name withheld]. Pajaro: I... I feel cold... so cold... White Tiger: Maybe this will warm you up. His first question is about Ryan-o's mom. Pajaro: Who's Ryan-o? White Tiger: That janitor that works in the UWF offices. Pajaro: The one that collects My Little Pony dolls? White Tiger: That's him. You know his mother? Pajaro: EVERYBODY knows his mother. White Tiger: So true. Well, to answer if she still goes for twenty bucks, the answer is that she has had to raise her prices. Between inflation, gas prices, and the drop in the real estate market, it know costs thirty dollars for a go at Mrs. [name withheld]. Pajaro: For those same reasons, I've had to raise my jobbing fee. Once I would lose for a small order of stuffed jalapenos and a chocolate shake from Jack-In-The-Box. Now I'll only job for a LARGE order of stuffed jalapenos and an OREO shake. White Tiger: I guess you gotta do what you can to survive. Pajaro: Amen, brother. White Tiger: And his second question is how awesome would it be if we went to another federation as masked wrestlers and revealed ourselves to be masked wrestlers. Pajaro: I've been in in other federtions. I won 4WW's first match ever! White Tiger: A fact that still gives the Childress family night terrors. Well, to answer the question, we can't really go to any other federations. Every single female wrestler got together and put out a collective restraining order against Pajaro after his whole Karina Wolfenden obsession. Pajaro: My sweet Kare-Bear! White Tiger: Shut up before she shows up and hits you. Also, we're luchadores. It is against our tradition to unmask, even if we have another mask under that. Pajaro: I always wear my mask, just like I always wear a condom. White Tiger: Uh... that's nice. Safe sex is important. Pajaro: Not just for sex. Always. I just like the feel of latex on my love rocket. White Tiger: Madre dios... Pajaro: That reminds me, Tiger... can I borrow fifty cents to get a new one from the vending machine in the men's room? I've been wearing this one for about two weeks. [White Tiger is saved from having to answer by the appearance of the young anti-smoking activist and a pair of police officers.] Young woman: There they are! I told you two that you couldn't silence the truth! Pajaro: We don't want to silence it! We just want it to shut up for a little while. White Tiger: Quick! Grab the tape! [Los Mucho Macho Mascaras run towards the camera. There is a crash and then the screen turns to static, thus ending this installment of Ask an Enhancement Talent Luchador.] ---- 3 August 2006 How do you feel about the recent down time for the UWF? Even more importantly what do you think are the chances of the new regime leading UWF into a bigger and brighter future?
