Visiting the Betrayer Part 2 (SAS rp#2)

9 Jul 2004

The Real Deal — Kaiowas' tribal village

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After enjuring a moment of hardship due to Kaiowas, Deal was saved by a mysterious female who had been hiding in the bushes the whole time during their coversation...what will happen next? Deal lies unconcious in a small two man tent rapped in a sleeping bag with a jacket underneath his head for support. The light of the moon shines through the thin green material lighting up the small area. Deal is alone, the female who saved him is no where in sight, there is no sound outside to indicate that she is there also. Beside Deal is another sleeping bag, most likely for the female while she was still present, up on the side of the tent a knife hangs, tied by a small thread onto a loop. At the top and centre of the tent a burnt out lantern swings, softly creaking as it moves from side to side. Welcome to the Jungle Outside the sound of a branch breaking awakens Deal. He groans as his arms move quickly to his neck as he feels pain. At this point his head moves back revealing the laserations to his neck from the vine that nearly cost him his life. It takes a moment, but shudders of pain run all through his body, not only his neck. He shots up, his hands wrapping round his neck, trying to keep the pain away. This only makes it worse though and he lets out an almighty scream of hurt. 'Shhh' For the first time Deal hears the female. 'You don't want that man coming back for you do you?' Deal tries to look around to find out where the girl is but has no joy because his neck has been completely stiffened. He sighs... 'Where am I? and...emmm...why am I not dead?' A quick stiffled laughs is heard from the outside of the tent. 'Because I saved you. Don't you remember?' Deal thinks back for a moment, hate filling his glazed eyes... 'Actually no I don't, last thing I remember was being dropped from a great height by that fuck Kaiowas!' The front of the tent opens up and the woman enters slowly. She sits down on her sleeping bag facing Deal. Her eyes are large with a whirly blue colour circulating the iris. Her face is smooth like a babies bottom and is as good as any man could want. In Deal's eyes she's damn attractive but that is the last thing on his mind at the moment. Deal stares back at her, a grin coming across his face as he is happy to be alive... 'Who are you?' The woman laughs... 'I'm Anita' She smiles and extends her hand towards The Real Deal. He returns the favour and the two shakes hands. Deal grins. 'I take it I owe you a thank you then Anita for saving my life' Anita puts her hand on his shoulder... 'I wasn't going to stand there and watch you hang. Something had to be done so I saved you. Who was that man who attack you anyway?' Deal coughs roughly and blood seeps from his throat and out through his mouth. Anita hands him a cloth and he wipes away the blood from his face. 'That was Kaiowas...The Betrayer...The Tribal Warrior' Anita looks confused. 'Tribal Warrior you say?' Deal sighs and sits up, resting his arms on his knees. 'It's a long story. I'm sure you wouldn't want to know about it' Anita sits back and looks at Deal questionably... 'Ofcourse I want to know about it. That man nearly killed you and deserves to be payed back' Deal shakes his head before looking straight back at Anita... 'More than a year ago myself and my friend Chris entered a wrestling federation known as the DWO. Yes, I'm a wrestler and so is that man Kaiowas.' Deal smirks... 'Back in the DWO myself and Chris joined together as always and formed our team, little did I know though that Kaiowas who was there also was about to change Chris into a Tribal Warrior also. Chris became SurReal, one as bad as Kaiowas but one that still had his old self inside of him. I will tell you this. Kaiowas is not to be messed with, he isn't some jungle man who has never seen the outside world, he isn't some weak man unwise to the ways of the outside world. He knows as much as you and I, but he hides away here, with his tribe.' Anita looks shocked at the fact she is talking to a wrestler and what she has heard of Kaiowas. 'So you are telling me that you came here to seek revenge for your friend Chris?' Deal laughs loudly at Anita's comment and shakes his finger infront of her face... 'Oh no, nothing to do with that. Me and Chris somehow always got along and always were partners. Even when Chris became a Tribal Warrior, Kaiowas and him didn't always get along. I came here to sort things out with Kaiowas, we used to be on the same page then it all changed. I don't know why our hate grew for once another. Our attitudes changed towards one and other. I hated the way he acted and he hated the way I acted.' Anita shakes her head and speaks sternly apon The Real Deal 'So you came here to sort out some ego trip you had last year, how manly!' Deal gives her an evil grin with a glint in his eye before getting rather angry... 'You know nothing of this situation...it's as if you haven't been listening to me. I came here to sort things out, close this chapter of my life and move on, but now it seems like this chapter is going to turn into a whole book. The point where Kaiowas dropped me when I was on the vine, within an inch of life secured that my respect for him left my mind and the hate for him returned with a bloody vengeance. Hate that could destroy all of hell including the thrown of Satan himself!' Anita sits back, not knowing how to responde to what she just heard... 'I'm sorry, I just am confused about this whole situation, but don't worry. I shall help you to deal with this Kaiowas person. I will help you get your revenge on him. For now though I want you to get some rest. You are going to need it soon when we go to find him.' Deal shifts around until he can lie back down onto the jacket he is using for a pillow. WIthin moments he is back to sleep. Noticing that Deal is back to sleep Anita moves out of the tent to the surrounding area. She looks around the cool night before she rushes from the campsite leaving Deal resting without knowledge of where she is going. She moves through the opening where she saved Deal and checks around for the path Kaiowas used as he left the scene. After seeing it she darts forward quietly and enters the path. The forest around her comes alive with birds crying out and snakes slithering underneath her feet. She reaches the end of the path. Her eyes open up wide as she see's the ten foot stone wall directly infront of her. To the left of the wall there is a gate with guard towers on either side of it. She moves back swiftly, making sure that the guards don't see her, she knows she has to use pure stealth. Kaiowas on the other hand had anticipated something like this, but not from someone other than TRD, Kaiowas knows he is a tough nut to crack and did not believe that leaving him hanging there would be the end of him. He had cleared out all the guards from their posts around the wall but had left the torches lit. It was a silent night, great for hunting, for a little bit of aggressive hunting.... Kaiowas himself now kneels, hidden from the light of the moon, his dreadlocks tied back in preparation for any type of conflict. All of a sudden, a warm feeling spreads over Kaiowas' left thigh as a familiar presence appears... His pet jaguar, Rula had nuzzled up against him in the cold, she also sensed there was something amiss. They both could feel it was too silent...... Silent enough for their prey to sneak in........ Silent enough for Kaiowas and Rula to catch them. 'Rula, be at rest until I move.... our opponent has not yet appeared.... they wait, hesitantly, cowardly.' Kaiowas alone though may have thought that this would be an easy catch for him but Anita was as weary as he was. She thought to herself if she was to be going up against Kaiowas, that she would have the advantage. Kaiowas knew nothing of her and how she worked, how she preyed apon others. Anita on the other hand knew alot about Kaiowas from talking to Deal ealier on in the night... Moving silently through the shadows of the perimeter walls, Anita moves herself against the wall so that she can slide across to the gate undetected. She is playing safe by assuming the guards are still in their towers, she believe that if she doesn't work this way she'll end up dead. As she shifts herself along towards the gate she turns her head to look up at one of the guard towers... She stopped abruptly and thought to herself. If there actually someone in the towers looking out onto the surrounding area of the village, would there not be shadows forming from the light coming from the towers. This thought makes her move faster along the wall and within minutes she is beside it.... Anita turns her head to look inside of the village, just like Kaiowas, she can feel that something can't be right. She slowly whispers to herself... 'No guards in the towers, no guards at the gate and the place is completely silent...not a good sign Anita...' She grabs onto a loose part of the wall and begins to pull herself up, using the gate to help her she finally makes it to the top and sits on the foot wide wall. She lies down quickly into the shadows onto of the wall which are produced by the trees and stays completely still so she isn't seen if anyone happens to walk past. All of a sudden a blade bites the cool night air and the glimmer of moonlight blinds Anita. She jumps back shocked a little and when she looks up she finds an extremely intimidating sight. Where she stood mere seconds ago, now was a long curved blade, which had bitten deep into the clay-like earth. Her eyes followed the blade along the wooden shaft and up to the pair of weathered hands that skillfully held it in position. The arms that were attached to it seemed to glint, but when she looked into that dark face, full of shadows and mystery, she could see no eyes..... only a tranquil expression but always surrounded by an aura of aggression. 'And who are you?' Cool as ice and about as calm, Kaiowas the Tribal Warrior opens his eyes and looks at his immediate foe. He does not know what exactly to make of this woman, she was not expected, she was not the Real Deal. Perhaps he had been so weak that he had actually died. For now it mattered not. Kaiowas withdraws his weapon from the ground and takes up an offensive stance towards the young woman, forever piercing her soul with that intense stare. He waits for her to speak, but she does not. Inhaling, he repeats his question. 'I repeat...... who are you?' Anita sits up on the wall and crosses her legs, looking cutely down at the Tribal Warrior. Her puppy dog eyes she pulls at Kaiowas could make the dead weak at the knees. She dismounts the wall and walks over the Kaiowas and rubs her hand down his chest... 'I'm Anita...who are you?' Kaiowas' expression turns to ultimate confusion but he does not let his guard down at all. This woman was trying the oldest trick in the book.... And he had given up all those sort of 'interests' a long time ago... When his power came above all 'fun'. He follows her actions with a look of utter disgrace. Releasing one hand from the staff, he slaps away her intrusive limb with vigour. Taking one step backwards he renews his guard, ever more wary of this woman. 'Do not be impune.... And I feel you know about me. Or else you would not have come here, whore. I will not hesitate to strike you down, no one infiltrates my village without punishment, and no one leaves without my permission. So, humour me before I kill you.....Why are you here, girl?' Anita's head drops and she stares down at the forests floor. She had tried that trick many times before and it had always worked. This time though she had to move onto plan b. Pressurising every part of her face to make the blood rush to it her eyes start to swell up. She falls to her knees and somehow makes herself able to cry. She moves her hands up to her face and sobs to Kaiowas... 'I'm sorry Sir, but I'm lost. I was here with my family but I got lost when darkness fell apon the Jungle. I didn't know how to get back to them, I saw this Village and thought that I could get in. I thought that if I was able to get past this wall I'd be safe for a night. I just didn't want to be alone tonight, alone amoungst the beasts of the trees' Kaiowas smiles, but not because he is happy, but because he sees straight through this young lady. She is no lost fool, there was no family in these woods, but Kaiowas decides to play along. He allows his exterior expression to drop to that of a kind benefactor. He releases the weapon in one hand and reaches out to the 'poor' crying girl... 'Girl, you are safe here....take my hand' Kaiowas offers his hand but makes a quick sweep of the area with his peripheral vision, checking that no one else had snuck in while this foolish would be assassin broke down into tears. 'Follow me, we will get you a warm bed for the night, my tribe will welcome you and we shall search together for your family in the morning.' Anita takes Kaiowas' hand and rises to her feet, her kness now completely covered in dirt from the ground. She moves with him as he enters through the gates. Wtihout him seeing a grin moves across his face though she still pretends to be crying... 'Thank you kind Sir. This means alot to me, how can I ever thank you?' Kaiowas hoped the double cross would pay off, there was more to this girl, and he knew it. Could it be linked to his strange meeting with his old foe, the Real Deal, earlier this evening, or was she just another pretender to the power he held. Realisng that she was speaking to him he turned to her once more. He swore he caught a glimpse of a smile disappearing on her lips, but he let it slip, he would only turn aggressive when she did.... He had to act convinced. 'No need young lady, just delight us with stories from the outside world. We hear you have harnessed the lightning and use it to power motorised horses.... Is this true?' Playing stupid was difficult but he found it entertaining. He had been born into the tribe, he had been born in Rio, a part of a very rich and a very poor city. But this girl did not know in Kaiowas' eyes, if she did then she must be dealt with.... He was sounding her out... What Kaiowas didn't know was that earlier on in the night Deal had told Anita about Kaiowas so she was well scouted on him. She decided to play along though. Both of them knew each other were acting but they didn't know that each other knew that they knew the other was acting. It was a pretty much mind boggling experience if each other would have realised. Anita ponders the question Kaiowas laid to her about the outside world. Joking aboutshe answers, trying to test Kaiowas' defences... 'Yes, infact somehow you are totally correct. Cars are now produced with electric charge built into them and can now produce their own lightning to power the engine, it's a facinating theory and it works very well. It's excellent for the environment.' Kaiowas stops in his tracks, realisation hit him like a ton of bricks as he knew that she was joking about. He now knew that she knew who he was. He know knew that she was definately linked to The Real Deal. Kaiowas nods and gives the best smile he possibly can. Now well beyond the gates and close to the centre of the village, Kaiowas decides it is time to unvail his true self to Anita. He has had enough of her lies and now feels it's time to get rid of her as quickly as possible. He turns to her, placing his hands on her shoulders he gives another quick smile... 'Now we are here.' Anita looks around, only seeing one small building to her left and a hill to her right. 'Where are we?' Kaiowas points to the house but keeps hold on Anita, he stares through her like she isn't even there and turns her towards the hill. 'That building behind you is where you will stay tonight but for now I want to show you something.' Anita thinks nothing of this as Kaiowas moves to her side and they start to climb the hill. Once reaching the top Anita looks around. Unable to see anything a shiver of shock runs up her spine. Without warning Kaiowas swings his staff an knocks her to the ground, she then drifts uncounciousness. Kaiowas lets his staff rest onto the earth and looks apon his prey. 'Tonight, you only have one resting place and that is in eternal sleep.' Meanwhile... While all of this was going on, The Real Deal, as cunning as he is was able to sneak into the village without being acknowledged. He had woken up to find that he was alone and decided to go investigate. His gut told him that Anita had gone to find Kaiowas, and he knew that she would be in danger. Kaiowas somehow did not expect The Real Deal to be turning up anytime soon. After all he had nearly killed the man he hated so deeply. Without knowing of The Real Deal's pressence, Kaiowas stands on the top of the hill looking down upon his village, it's greatness brings a truethful smile to his hardened lips. He breathes in softly, taking in the midnight air to sooth his lungs. Worry is not far away though a Rula appears at his side. Kaiowas knows something is wrong, he knew that his friend would not come to his side so quickly and unexpectadly if nothing was wrong. Kaiowas speeds down the hill, following Rula all the way. He is taken to the far side of the village which from the hill is out of site. Fires rage from two of the buildings and a third begins to flare up as he arrives. He cries out to his fellow villagers to get their attention. As everyone gathers from being awoken he speaks to them all... 'Who has done this to us?' Silence falls amoungst his tribe, none of them knowing who had done this. Kaiowas looks to around, finding that all his tribe were present, none of them tried to escape, none of them could of done this. Rage begins to build in the heart of him as he now knows who has done this...He yells out at the top of his voice and the whole surrounding area is risen by his torment... 'REAL DEAL!!!' Kaiowas, correct in his accusations runs back towards the hill. Using every bit of pace he can muster he makes it back to the point where he left Anita. In the spot she lay he know finds emptiness. He closes his eyes to calm himself before returned back to the village to help put out the fires... Meanwhile... Real Deal, carrying Anita over his right shoulder moves silently away from Kaiowas' village with a huge grin on his face. He knew that revenge had been oh so sweet this time but deep inside him he also knew that Kaiowas would be back to get him. Deal being wise returns to a different camp site which he set up while Anita was away. Further away from the village and in a less open area. He used all the things from the previous site to set up the new one. He drop Anita down slowly, she is still uncouncious so he decides to lift her into the tent and let her get some rest. The lantern he left burning slowly starts to dim as he too lays himself down onto his jacket pillow. Deal looks around to either side to see if any shadows creeped through the trees. Satisfied that noone had followed him he lifts himself up and turns out the lantern...The jungle fades into darkness...
