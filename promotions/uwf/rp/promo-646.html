UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #646

Shocking? I think not.

Today (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Tokyo streets.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Jamie Shock and Malcolm Kennedy will do battle at Terror in Tokyo. One on one, man to man. One winner in a straight wrestling match.*** {Time: 10:06} {Place: The Streets of Tokyo.} {Date: August 16th, 2004} ((The scene opens)) *** The camera opens up in downtown Tokyo, Japan. Cars zoom up and down the busy streets, people crowd the sidewalks hurrying back and forth. The camera turns around from facing the street and focuses on a man propped up against a concrete wall. Its Malcolm Kennedy. Malcolm stands with his back against the wall and one leg up, with the foot of it also against the wall. His arms crossed on his chest. He wears a white sports coat, black pants, black shoes, and a light blue undershirt. A pair of jet black Oakley sunglasses cover his eyes. His hair neatly gelled and his beard neatly trimmed. He smirks when the camera rests on him. He shakes his head as he begins to speak.*** Malcolm Kennedy: “Jamie, your such a fool. You knew that you would have to cross my path if you ever made a return, and you knew it would result in tragedy for you. I tried to warn you. But you wouldn’t listen. So I had to show you. You stepped in the ring with me the night you returned, and I planted you head first into a chair. Then when you still kept running your mouth, I shut you up with a crowbar. Then you and Slasher double team me when I was the referee and you think you got the best of me. You did nothing James.....NOTHING. Im still here, still breathing, and still boiling with hate for you. You disgust me. You make me sick.” “ You think the fact that you want to become a knew man changes anything? You may want to be someone different, but your still the washed up fool who ran away because he lost. Your still the man who ditched the alliance that YOU created, when you realized that I was the one carrying your creation. You may think that this new quest your on changes who you are, but it doesn’t. Your still that arrogant crow bar swinging freak that destroyed Danny Jaye. And your still the little bitch that lost to me one on one in a straight up wrestling match. So what makes you think this time is gonna be any different?” “ Well James, I must be the bearer of bad news. I must be the one to break it to you. This match will be no different. I will beat you just like I did last time. I will tear you apart and leave the pieces in the ring. Jamie................Im gonna make you hurt in a way you’ve never been hurt before. Your gonna regret ever crossing me Jamie, I swear to god your gonna regret it.” *** Malcolm stands up from the wall and walks off down the street. The scene fades to black.***
