UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #563

Job Transition

19 Jul 2004

Jonny Roxx — Detroit, MI

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8:03 A.M. – Donnie Rock’s Apartment(Detroit, MI) [The loud ringing of a phone brings Donnie Rock out of his deep sleep. He opens his eyes and curses very loudly. He throws his cover to the side and moves to a sitting position on the side of his bed. He reaches over to the table beside his bed and picks up the phone.] Donnie: “Hello…” “Yeah, that’s me…” “You want to sign Jonny?” “He starts when?” “I’ll go tell him today! Goodbye.” [Donnie puts the phone back on the hook. He lets out a big sigh of relief and pushes his long black hair back.] Donnie: “Finally we catch a break.” [He looks around his room. It’s a small room, just enough for the bare essentials - a small bed, a television, and mountains of beer bottles. He glances down at the floor and then reaches down for his blue jeans. He slides them on and zips them shut. He grabs a plain, white shirt that had been hanging from a lampshade and puts that on as well. He checks himself in the mirror. Donnie is of average height, but he is lanky and pale. He walks into the next room and opens up the refrigerator. He takes out a bottle of Miller High Life and opens it up. He raises it in the air to make a toast to himself.] Donnie: “To a new start.” [He gulps down the entire bottle and he sets it down on the counter. He walks over to his couch. Before he can do anything, he trips on the couch’s leg and face plants on to the floor where he soon passes out.] 12:43 PM – Donnie Rock’s Apartment [Donnie rolls on to his back and rubs his head. He kicks the couch with all of his might and then clutches his foot in pain. He lifts himself up on the couch and leans back. He rubs his head once again and then walks back to his room. He puts on a pair of socks followed by a pair of black boots. He grabs his keys off of the kitchen counter.] Donnie: “Okay. Lets try this again.” [He exits the room and walks towards the elevator. He pushes the “down” button and waits. The elevator arrives after a few moments and he steps into a crowded space. Once the ride is over everyone hurriedly escapes the horrific smell that was given off by the drunk that occupied. Donnie walks out of the building and his car is parked out front, much to the dismay of the building’s owners. His “pride and joy” is a beat-up, green and brown 1974 Dodge Charger.] Donnie: “There’s my baby.” [Donnie runs his hands over the hood.] Donnie: “We’ve been through a lot together…” [Just as he says that, he runs his hand across some very old paint and it falls to the ground.] Donnie: “and then we’ve been through even more. I just know you’re going to die after I do. Lets get through another day.” [He climbs into the car through the driver’s side window because the door no longer opens and his window is missing. He has no rear-view mirror, a crappy old stereo, but he thinks it worth missing all of that to have his shifter with the eight ball grip. He cranks it up, but it shuts right off. He cranks it again, it struggles, he gives it some gas and there’s a loud bang and it shuts off. He hits the steering wheel, but he has an idea. The car is parked on a slant so he puts it in neutral and lets off the brake. The car slowly gets out onto the street. When the car gets to about twenty miles an hour he cranks it up and it goes.] Donnie: “It’s all in the touch, baby. You’re still alive!” 1:07 PM – Jonny Roxx’s House [Donnie pulls into the driveway of Jonny Roxx. The house is small, but fit for a single guy. Donnie gets out of his car and walks up to the door. He rings the doorbell, but no one comes to the door. He rings it again and still no one answers. He bangs on the door screaming for Jonny, but still just silence. He walks around to the backyard and finds that the bathroom window is open. He climbs through the window and he tumbles to the floor. He picks himself up and walks into Jonny’s room. The alarm next to Jonny is going off, and has been for a long time. Roxx is lying on his bed, fully clothed, and was apparently drinking all night.] Donnie: “What a mess! Who would do this to himself?” [Donnie thinks, and then shakes his head. He shakes Jonny to try and wake him up.] Donnie: “Dude, I’ve got some good news.” Jonny: “Who cares about your big bruise.” Donnie: “No, man. You’ve got a new gig.” [Jonny immediately sobers up and a smile comes to his face.] Jonny: “A gig? My band is ready to get back together? I knew they’d be back.” Donnie: “Not that kind of gig.” Jonny: “I’m trying to put a band together, bro. I can’t have any distractions.” Donnie: “But you can’t go on unless you have some money. When you get some money together you can start a new band.” Jonny: “So what am I supposed to do? Donnie: “It’s a wrestling company called Universal Wrestling Federation.” Jonny: “There’s just one small problem with that, Donnie. Did you forget that I’m not a wrestler? I’m a rock star!” Donnie: “When was your last hit?” Jonny: “Dude, not cool. Good point, though.” Donnie: “I will always be by your side for the music, bro, but we need some money.” Jonny: “What do you mean, we.” Donnie: “I’m going to be your manager. I’ve been your band manager, so I figured I’d make some extra money and be your wrestling manager as well.” Jonny: “Okay, dude. I just need to ask your one thing?” Donnie: “Yeah?” Jonny: “How am I going to learn to wrestle?” Donnie: “I’ve set up some lessons for you at the local gym. It should be fun.” Jonny: “By fun do you mean extremely painful?” Donnie: “More or less. Get ready, we’re going over there now.” Jonny: “Hey, Donnie. Can we take my car?” Donnie: “That’s okay, I guess. Why?” Jonny: “I just wanted to drive today.” Donnie: “You can drive my car.” Jonny(under his breath): “No one can.” Donnie: “Huh?” Jonny: “I said, I’ll get ready now.” Donnie: “I’ll wait outside.” [Donnie exits the room and Jonny walks into his bathroom. Unlike Donnie, Jonny decides to take a shower and wash off the alcoholic odor. Jonny takes his shower and dries off. He puts on a pair of black jeans, some tennis shoes, and a Led Zeppelin t-shirt. He looks at himself in the mirror. Jonny is taller and more muscular than Donnie. He has the look of a hair metal band member. He uses a red bandana to push back his long, light red hair. He strokes his small goatee and then walks outside to meet his friend.] Donnie: “Still got the look, my friend.” Jonny: “No doubt. Now lets get this over with.” 1:42 PM – Stan’s Gym [Jonny pulls his black, 1978 Pontiac Trans Am into the parking lot of Stan’s Gym. Both Jonny and Donnie walks into the place and immediately both see a ring and two men victimizing each other. Jonny attempts to walk out, but Donnie holds him back.] Donnie: “C’mon, man. We really need this job.” Jonny: “There’s got to be a better way to earn money.” Donnie: “Think about it, bro. You know how to move. You’ve done flips and the likes on stage. You have the look. You have the charisma, no man can match your passion. This is it, man. This is where things will pay off.” Jonny: “Okay, but as soon as we have the money we split. I’m a rocker, not a wrestler.” [They enter the ring area and stand ringside to watch the two big men duke it out. The match keeps dragging on and on with boring moves and no technique at all. Finally a man, who appears to be the owner, steps into the ring to stop the match.] Stan: “Jesus Christ, you pansies. Didn’t I teach you anything?” Guy #1: “No. We came in to ask directions to Chicago and you told us to get in the ring and fight.” Stan: “Oh. Just head south and watch the signs.” Guy #2: “Uh, thanks.” [The two guys head out and both Donnie and Jonny enter the ring.] Stan: “What do you want?” Donnie: “I called earlier about a new wrestler, Jonny Roxx.” Stan: “This him? Well….I’ve worked with better. Don’t feel bad, kid. I’ve also worked with much, much worse.” Jonny: “Alright. So where do we begin?” Stan: “How high is your threshold to pain?” Jonny: “I guess it’s about average.” Stan: “That’s going to change.” Jonny: “Wha….whoa!” [Before Jonny can react, Stan grabs his arm and slams him down into an armbar. Jonny tries to escape, but is unable.] Jonny: “What the hell are you doing?” Stan: “Your first lesson. Expect the unexpected. Always be ready. If you’re caught off your guard, you’re done for.” [Stan releases Jonny and they both climb to their feet.] Stan: “I want you here every day at eight in the morning.” Jonny: “How come you get to pick the time?” [Stan gives Jonny a very hard chop across the chest.] Stan: “Because I said so. Get ready. Tomorrow is going to be painful.” [Donnie helps Roxx out of the ring. They both exit the building as the scene fades to black.]
