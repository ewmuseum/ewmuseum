UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #348

UWF Superstars Talks (Good one)

17 Apr 2004

Nick Persia — Studio

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[After a Moraless T-shirt commercials the screen fades into the UWF Press room. Nick Persia is there with Larson, sitting behind a fairly large wooden table considering there are just two people behind it on steel chairs. The director gives the sign that they will go on-air in just three seconds. Three.......two......one....On-air.] Larson: “Welcome Ladies and Gentleman to a new edition of UWF Superstar talks. Tonigh’s guest is the man known as “The Invader”. He is the reigning and defending UWF hardcore Champion and without a doubt one of the most amazing rookies today. He is Nick Persia.” Nick Persia: “Thank you for the kind introduction.” Larson: “No problem. But I have some question for you regarding your status going into this hardcore title match, your feelings about Adien pryde and the brutal attack he gave you and many, many more. So much to ask and just so little time so let’s get started.” [Another commercial airs, this time for Ezra Kazee] Larson: “I’ve got to ask you this question since we all want to know. What do you have up your sleeve for Sunday Afternoon Supremacy?” Nick Persia: “Take a look at me.” [Larson takes a look at him with a confused face, obviously not understanding why he has to look at Nick Persia] Nick Persia: “Can you see it?” Larson: “What can I see? I see a T-shirt and your jeans, but nothing special really.” Nick Persia: “Wrong, wrong, wrong. You are looking at 230 pounds of solid steal and sex appeal.” Larson: “You still didn’t answer my question.” Nick Persia: “My bad. What I am bringing this SAS, Adien Pryde is simply not designed to handle. Are you ready for a rhyme?” Larson: “Go ahead.....” Nick Persia: “Sunday, Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, fully concentrated with my mind on the match Hardcore title on the line, no time for playing, these opponent is a great catch Promos of his are laughable, always about the same old “I accomplished that, look at all the titles that I’ve hold” cut that crap and come with something else Adien you better give the respect that I deserve I’ll strike the hardest, come thundering in the match, you’ll be defeated first Already in mind that you clown will be holding the gold, but this is the truth that’s gotta be told You been there, done that, so it’s time to move a step back Persia always coming full equipped, ready for attack Tell your Doc you need him, stars you’ll be seeing, Look for another way to pay your bills when I retire you in front of the crowd who gives you thrills I’ll be coming doper then fiends who are addicted on extasy pills No lying, neither am I playing, you hear what I am saying? Better hope so for you, on your body I’ll be dropping jewels This man fears no one, so come on with your strategies, none can * beep * with this mastermind, ignored by the fantasies you have so you are blind Look to present, Nick Persia is the future, we will never back down Who are you foolin, you are the weakest link so you’ll be losin Cooking up marvellous * beep *, I am the hungry one, I will give you suprises Sweet, solutions, maybe you need some of them, but it won’t help you Too sweet for defeat? Nah sweaty and you will be beat On my hands you will get defeated, I see a coward when I look into your eyes Only thing you are good at is telling lies, stunned when you see what I am truly capable off, how it feels to be already humiliated, by someone who you considered a fluke? Through the ring you will be shooked, get wrapped up in a paper bag, so that I can smoke you like some good hasj When I’m finished with you , I will be putting out the trash Here is a riddle for you nah more like the truth this sunday night: Clothes lines, Body drop, I am still gonna come out on top. Stop fronting with what you can’t accomplish, at the end of the SAS your waist will have nothing, around it, And that’s for real, you better be prepared to finish what you started punk hasas. Persia ain’t the one to play, you sure don’t belong in mi clasa I’m ready, bring it on like a grown man and don’t back down like a coward Nick Persia is luminous, anything else that comes on my way is simply ludicrous” Larson: “Wow, some harsh comments directed to Adien pryde here.” Nick Persia: “That’s what he asked for. Allow me to give you the meaning of Hardcore.....” Larson: “Yeah, sure. Go ahead......” Nick Persia: “I have a dictionary of the English language! I decided to look up the word 'hardcore' to see if the definition included 'smashing up property and beating up innocents'. Strangely enough, I couldn't find any mention to that. However, I was able to find a couple of definitions of my own! The first one that I found said: 'Going to great lengths' Going to great lengths? That seems to me like an accurate definition! Adien did go to great lengths to do this to me! However, it's not just about me and Pryde! Oh no! This match concerns itself with the Hardcore Title as well! There have been dozens of people who have fought for the hardcore title and have given their hearts and soul for such an honour. The Plague was the first person to hold such an honour, and since then people have tried but only those that are able to give their heart and soul for the cause! There have been such men like Beezlebozo, Seymour Almasy, Ben Jericho and, Iron Lung! It's a title that has caused controversy throughout the entire federation. One such move was made by the UWF when they retired the belt from Gurgles. But the point that I'm trying to make is that such a belt requires a strong heart and avid dedication to go through so much pain and to pull through it all to become the UWF hardcore Champion! And that's what I want to achieve! Now, I'll accept that I'm not the 'extreme type', but do I have to be? Such a title is a landmark in our business! It means so much to be able to put all your passion and your fury into one match to be able to come out the UWF Hardcore Champion; the man who went through it all to get it all! There was, also, another definition in this book under the word 'extreme': 'Something that is as remote of different as possible' To me, that sums up every single superstar in this business. Everyone has their own way of going about things! Some prefer to be technical whilst others will use power moves. Some superstars take to the skies with lucha liba moves, and others prefer submission moves. Each person's individual characteristics and in-ring technique develops a story for every person willing to watch and learn. However, as far as ‘hardcore' is concerned, some of the people I've 'done business with' may consider me to be one of the most extreme they know! Some of them may call me pure evil incarnate, given the treatement I made them endure. I was 'hardcore' when I murdered business men and corporation managers. However, I was always able to swing the law my way, no matter which country I worked in! Do you want to know how? Because in every country across the entire globe...there are flaws!! Just like there is in anything that anyone does! And that's JUST what I'm going to do to Pryde! I'm going to work him like the law! Use him to my advantage when I need to and then just expose his flaws! Adien Pryde is one of the best wrestlers that I have ever known! After not having a single shot at the World Title for over a year, he finally achieves the gold that he deserved! But there's something that you've got to prepare yourself for!' [He held out his hands gesturing to the room around him. He even pointed to his shirt, pointing out the crimsons, the scarlets and the ruby redness of the whole room] Nick Persia: 'Red...is a colour that is dear to my heart! It is a metaphor for a lot of emotions! Love! Anger! Hate! Fury! But out of all the emotions I have ever experienced, the one that is constantly burning in me...is passion! Passion is what keeps me going! Passion is what keeps me alive!! I'll admit that what you did was clever...but it wasn't smart! You've made me angry, and when I get angry, I not only get my own back...I do it in a painful way! So just bear something in mind Pryde, before we step into the ring on Wednesday! I want you to know that if I will not only mentally and physically scar you...but I WILL end your career if I have to! THAT's how much this means to me! You have been warned Adien! Don't make this any harded that it already is for yourself...and I'll see you at the Sunday Afternoon Supremacy show! Larson: “But what about.....”[gets interrupted] Nick Persia: “The truth is TOLD, I’ll retain the GOLD!” Larson: “No, I wasn’t going to ask you about the outcome of the match, but about the comments made by Adien pryde. They claim you fell right into their trap!” Nick Persia: “Remember you asked me about what I had up my sleeve? I remember! So to you and all this non-believers, beware! To my opponent in this match, beware! I will step into this match with a raging fury of desire. A flaming ball of fire wich is feuled by will-power. As a tornado I will demolish anybody in my way and leave nothing behind but battered bodies! I will do whatever it takes to win. Whatever it takes! So let nobody make a mistake about it, I will do all that is needed to come out on top! No friends, no foes, only obstacles! I will jump to the TOP, with my Head DROP!!!” Larson: “I understand where you are coming from with these claims!” Nick Persia: “These aren’t claims it’s as blatant as I am black my friend.” Larson: “What do you think of Adien Pryde’s back-up?” Nick Persia: “He can bring all he wants since it is all Legal. But I’ll give him what I feel i need to bring. The ultimate Fighting machine is ready; I have the experience, intelligence, strength, speed, power and endurance all rolled up into one. And Pryde will be the first to experience the new wrath of The Unstoppable one. Listen to me Larson ‘Before me there was many, After me there will be none, Nick Persia is the One” Larson: “Thank you for your time.” Nick Persia: “No problem at all, larson. No problem at all.” [Screen fades into the new Kenny Mercury Action Figure]
