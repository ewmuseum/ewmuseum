UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #521

And the Brigade comes...

8 Jul 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — Here.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life goes full circle. Once again the Irish Bastard has been forced to find a new home to stay away from the place that he’d be cosidered playing the wrong cards in. And in this one instance he’s returned ‘home’. But not home as it once was. Sean has already made the comments about the UWF. It’s a Shadow of it’s former self. It’s been proven time and again with the likes of Seymor Almasy and a few others running around and saying they’ve held the top accolades here. Originally he one time “Irish Knight” Bres had been one of the men who was a staple of the upper midcard of the original father of the UWF: The GIWF. A federation that many have now come out of the woodwork from. Mr. Meaner (oops, well we won’t go there), Angel, Unforgiven Ben Jericho/Jackson whatever he’s calling himself this week, and Slasher. But another had come from the GIWF too. One that this last week made the biggest impact of the night…. And he did so by siding with an old rival. Cpl. Punishment and Sean McCalncy siding together? They form the new base of the stable once known as now known again to the UWF/GIWF family… The KnightMare Brigade! But now, now it grows by one more. One more as a former member of the Order of the Hammer gets his offer and now he’s accepted, and this is after the men of the UWF decided to book a match between the Ruff Ryder and Sean McClancy. Nay, don’t worry there Ruff, this won’t be a match. It’ll be an acceptance speech for you as Cpl. Punishment, Sean McCalncy, Tom and Patrick McClancy, Annie Leanan and now Ruff Ryder… and could there be more coming soon? How many will join the only force to make amockery of two sets of Stable Wars and prove that they’re still around and still the big dawgs out there? The Brigade is back… Now in two federations, one the younger sibling of the UWF, the one from the same father but a different mother. But this one is formed from the oginal leader of the KnightMare Brigade… Fomer GIWF TV Champion Former GIWF Intercontinental Champion Former GIWF THC Champion Former GIWF North Amer… European Champion Former 5 time SCWF Insanity Champion Former 3 time SCWF Superior Champion, their world level title Former PTC Extreme Champion And now the current, still Breakdown Wrestling World Champion… As he’s never been defeated for that title. And now, we go to the man of the hour… Your BDW World Champion, this makes it #4… “The Irish Bastard” Sean Michael McClancy. We find him standing in fron tof a Video Screen, an image of Ruff Ryder, the Cherokee Thrilla, showing upon it as he’s holding the black shirt with the flaming unicorn that is the symbol of the KnightMare Brigade… Sean, looking a bit scruffy as he’s growning his beard back, but his hair cut into a short cut to keep the long hair from behing a weapon against him. However he’s wearing his own KnightMare Brigade Muscle shirt and a pair of jean shorts. He looks at Ruff Ryder’s promo and smiles a big toothy grin. McClancy: Well, looks like the man knows when he’s given a fokin’ golden opportunity. A hand comes up onto Sean’s shoulder, his current manager and Valet, and the one that will be occampaning all of the KB to the ring, Annie Leanan, “The Beauty of the Wisps”, is standing there in a KB-UWF Babydoll shirt and a short vinyl skirt. Annie: Ya can’na blame ‘im fer it. A few years ago yer brother-in-law saw potential in ‘im. Bu’ does ‘e realize tha’ ‘e’s never left th’ Order? McClancy: The KnightMare Brigade merged with th’ Order in th’ old PWW when Ryder ‘ad ‘is little problem with Midnigh’ Rider, well Midnigh’ Flounder. A man I destroyed. Bu’ under me sister two years ago, th’ KB became th’ Special Forces o’ the Order. We are it’s elite. We are it’s vanguard. Now I’ve gone an’ collected th’ only man t’ ever confound Ian Childress. They’re 3 an’ 3 in official competition. An’ Th’ Cpl took Ian’s Intercontinental an’ North American Titles back in the day. He’s a mean bastard an’ I wanted ‘im t’ join. An’ now I’ve ga’ the most servere force in’ the UWF, this shadow of a federation, wallowin’ in the former glory tha’ it once was. This is nothin’ now. Annie: I know. Look a’ them. They’re basically wights walkin’ around ‘ere. Th’ Resurrection, th’ Player’s Club, who else? Who cares? The KnightMare Brigade ‘as been in now 5 federations, an’ it continues t’ grow strong. IT continues t’ be a dominant force. An’ fer the first time ever, it’s in two federations a’ one time. Sean turns and looks away from the screen. He looks at the raven haired woman. McClancy: An’ this time, it’s time tha’ I’ll na’ be ‘eld down. I’ll na’ be ‘eld t’ bein’ the man who gets stepped on. I’m the man who retired Ace Thunder. I’m th’ man who broke ‘im an’ became th’ True ‘Ardcore Icon. My goals ‘ere include such a thing as Mike Mongomery an’ tha World Title. The only GIWF/UWF belt I”ve never ‘eld. We ‘ave two world champions ‘ere now. I’ve ga’ full right’s t’ carry this belt around. But I know some o’ them will belly ache an’ moan abou’ it all. So if I ‘ave t’ bash a few skulls, First this week we’ll ‘ave a proper induction ceremony fer the’ Cherokee Thrilla t’ come ‘ome t’ th’ Order an’ the Brgade. Then the good Corporal will beat th’ livin’ ‘ell ou’ of Moraless. Then it’s only a matter o’ time befer we’re standin’ there, as the undisputed top o’ the UWF. There is no other then the KnightMare Brigade riogh’ now. No one cares abou’ the fokin’ Players Club. No one cares abou’ th’ fokin’ Resurrection. As of last week, the KnightMare Brigade is the biggest thing ‘ere. An’ we’re ‘ere t’ stay. With that we’ll fade out.
