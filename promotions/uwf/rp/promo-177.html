UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #177

Time goes round

19 Jan 2004

Ezra Kazee — Some sports bar.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a bar. There are not a lot of people, probably being that it is early in the week. It’s a nicer bar as well, probably more like a sports bar, as one can see several banners and pictures on the wall. Sitting at one of the tables is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He has a beer in front of him, yes he drinks, and is watching the television, which has some other federations wrestling show on it. He seems to be rather enthralled into it, but yet pays enough attention at the camera. You can see him reacting once in a while as maybe a spot was blown or something really impressive happened. The camera pans around so that it is looking at him from a side view. He takes a sip of his beer, and looks back up at the television.) Kazee: Well, another week has come and gone, and another week will come and go, and all things come around, again and again and again. It’s kind of funny how the world works that way…… But I don’t know really how that fits in with anything. Maybe we should just talk about wrestling. After all, I’m sure that’s why you are here. I couldn’t think of any other reason why the UWF would spend the money to send a camera out to find me, could you? (The camera shakes no) Kazee: Well, that’s good to know. So what do you wanna know? We had a, well, a very strange match, which we came out on top from. This of course should really be no surprise. I mean, we are talking about a bunch of bunnies vs. the elite group in the UWF today, the Players Club. Now I thought it was suppose to be a six man tag team match. How in the world did that ever turn out to be a 3 vs. 15000 handicapped match? Oh well, it doesn’t matter. Its life and the right team won…… And now we go onto this weekend. The Prime Alliance wants to show their dominance by taking out the Players Club. And this, their first shot in their attempt to make it their. Of course, in their grand wisdom, the bookers decided to give the Prime Alliance a chance, even though they are a bunch of misfits, go against a group of finely tuned athletes in Dunn, Montgomery, and I. But the fans must want to see it, so it will happen…… And it’s going to be a two out of three set of matches. Montgomery will face the “leader” of the Prime Alliance in Jamie Shock. Well, who knows about that, since I really can’t say that there truly is a leader, but Montgomery is the superior athlete in this one, and I have no doubts that he will win……. And then we have Joshua Dunn vs. Malcolm Kennedy. They expect that this is going to be a good match? Will someone get me whatever the bookers are smoking? I’d like to get my hands on some of that. Dunn ought to have no problem with this match. Kennedy, you may put up a fight, but it won’t do you any good…… And then there is my match. Against the self proclaimed Cherokee Thrilla who wouldn’t know a Native American if it bit him in the ass. Let’s be honest about this one, because that’s the only way I know how to be. And in my opinion, you don’t deserve the title of being the Cherokee Thrilla. You turn your back on Native Americans in general by cheering for the Chiefs. That there is an insult in and of itself. And you attitude, your greed, and your absolute disregard for those around you show that you no longer hold the values that a Native American holds dear to ourselves….. (Kazee takes another drink of his beer) This match is about more than just that. This match has that element of revenge in it. This match, you know in your heart, is going to be one of your toughest matches. I took you to the limit at the Contest of Champions. You know that you were lucky to escape that PPV with the title. This match, it’s more about the Prime Alliance vs. the Players Club, and you know that just as much as I do. This match is going to show to people that not only can I give the big dogs a run for their money, this match is going to prove that I deserve to be right there running with the big dogs. And I, Ruff Ryder, am going to prove it by beating you. It doesn’t matter how many times I have to beat you. It doesn’t matter where I have to beat you. Fact of the matter is, I am going to prove it to everyone by beating you…….. Our match, it’s the first one in the series. Start the series off with a bang I guess is what they were thinking. Well, that’s exactly what they are going to get is a bang. They know that the last time we hooked up we tore the roof off of the place. This time, not only will they expect nothing less, but they are going to get just as much, if not more. Last time we met, we had both wrestled two matches already. This time, we will be fresh. We will be ready to go. And the fans are going to get everything they pay for and deserve. And I, to all of my doubters out there, am going to prove……. (Kazee takes the final drink of his beer) Prove that I belong at main event level and that I can and will beat anyone in the UWF because I am that good. Underestimate my size, speed, strength, but don’t underestimate my heart and my knowledge, because that is how I am going to beat you…… Good Luck Prime Alliance, for its time for the Players Club to truly show their dominance, and you’ll need all the luck you can get. (Scene fades to black.)
