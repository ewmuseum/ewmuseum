UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #279

Days of future past

11 Mar 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival/last week's Supremacy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pyretta Blaze is standing in front of the Carnival’s bonfire in a tiny red leather bodysuit and long red boots. Her back is to use as she gazes into the fire, giving us a hell of a look at her spectacular ass. Eventually, she turns and notices the camera. Blaze: “Oh, hey there. Didn’t see you coming. Don’t let that happen again or else I’ll set fire to your head. “Anyway, to business. The clown’s got some things he wants to say in the near future, but he currently training a bit for his upcoming non-title match against Ruff Ryder this weekend. He’ll probably have quite a few things to talk about, some of which will refer to an incident that happened backstage at the last show this past Sunday. “However, this altercation did not air, so in order for you, the viewers at home, to know what the hell he’s talking about, we’ll be showing you said altercation. Enjoy, and we’ll talk to you later. Bye for now!” She smiles and waves as the camera fades to a black screen. We then fade in to the back stage area of last week’s Supremacy. We see Beelzebozo and Pyretta Blaze, hanging out and enjoying a couple of fine carbonated, alcoholic beverages. The clown has traded in his sleeveless trench coat for a ripped, sleeveless referee’s shirt. Beelzebozo: “So did we ever find out WHY I’m doing this?” Blaze: “I guess they just needed a gimmick for this. I dunno. Dunn seems pretty keen on the choice though.” Beelzebozo: “Yeah, because I’m here to keep HIM happy.” He snorts, drains his cup, and tosses it away before continuing. “Still, I have to admit that I appreciate his love for the hardcore wrestling style. So I guess he can’t be as big a loser as Kennedy or Ryder.” Blaze: “Damn, baby, next you’ll be sitting around a campfire toasting marshmallows and singing hymns.” Beelzebozo: “No kidding.” He rolls his eyes. Blaze: “Anyway, I think it’s kinda keen that he trusts your ability to referee the match. I hope you can live up to it.” Beelzebozo: “I don’t have any worries about that. Just be sure to keep an eye out for anyone trying to come down and interfere so I can…have a word with them.” The smile that creeps across his face indicates that the last thing he’d be doing with any potential party-crashers is talking. “But it’s not like I have any biases going into this match. Both men are after my belt. Dunn’s my next scheduled title defense, but Mercury got his stupid ass pinned last week. As far as who wins, I couldn’t care less. I just hope they give me something amusing to watch. Maybe one of them’ll end up bleeding all over the place. THAT’D be entertaining!” Blaze: “Not like Ryder and Kennedy’s little soiree later.” Beelzebozo: “No kidding. Christ, the tag division’s a joke if those two goofs are what passes for a high-quality tag team. I imagine, by virtue of the other teams sucking even more, that they’ll win. Man…I hope some great, dominant team from the UWF’s past shows up and cleans their clocks.” Blaze: “That almost sounds…prophetic.” Beelzebozo: “Eh, what do I know? I doubt we’re all that lucky. Man, I hope the fans don’t all fall asleep before my refereeing debut! Ryder couldn’t put asses in seats if he promised to blow his own worthless head off!” Blaze: “Well, maybe a few…” Beelzebozo: “I’m tryin’ to make a point here, so hush. He couldn’t draw money with a pencil! He couldn’t suck more if he was a Hoover!” Ryder’s voice: “Is that a fact?” Ruff Ryder comes walking up to Beelzebozo and stands directly in front of him. Ryder: “Go on, what was that you were saying? I mean, I’m here now, so why don’t you say it to my face, clown boy?” Beelzebozo: “Gladly, Cherokee Vanilla. You’re a bigger joke than G.W. and half as intelligent. Whoever thought you’d make good championship material was obviously dropped a few times when they were little. Simply put, Ryder…you SUCK.” Ryder: “And yet I beat you.” Beelzebozo rolls his eyes. Beelzebozo: “I’m not goin’ through that song and dance again, little man. Besides, even if you HAD actually beaten me in that tourney, it wouldn’t matter because, when crunch time came, you choked. And don’t start whining about me cheating, Wisenheimer…you were just as eager to start swinging steel as I was. If you’d been so against the way our match went, you should’ve cried to the ref like a little bitch. I ALMOST had respect for you when you let it go, but sure enough, this past week you finally cracked and cried about it.” Ryder: “The only one who’ll be crying is you, when I take that belt from around your pudgy waist.” Beelzebozo: “Are you for real?” He lifts his shirt. “You WISH your abs were this rock-hard. You WISH you could look as good as me.” Ryder: “This from the guy who paints his face?” Beelzebozo: “OOOOOOO!! How original! Truly this battle of wits is over!” He snorts. “I can take this makeup off any time, and still look incredible. Sadly, all the paint in the world couldn’t help you out. I mean, it looks like your neck threw up!” Blaze: “Ugly-ass bastard.” Ryder: “Tell your bitch to watch her mouth before the Cherokee Thrilla closes it for her.” Beelzebozo: “Touch her and you’ll never see the sun rise again.” The two men bore holes into each other with their eyes. Beelzebozo: “I win.” Ryder: “What? What are you talking about?” Beelzebozo: “You blinked. I win.” He giggles. Ryder: “What the hell? God, you’re a moron!” Beelzebozo: 'Awww, what's the matter Ryder? Don't you like...GAMES?' His emphasis on the last word is somewhat ominous. Ryder: 'The only game I want to play is Kick the Clown's Stupid Ass.' Beelzebozo: 'Aw, I don't like that game at all. I much prefer the kind of games Foreigner once sang about.' Ryder: 'Huh? Whatever!' He puts his face right into Beelzebozo’s. “Look, you painted dipshit, I’ve got better things to do than stand here jawing with you. I hope you’re stupid enough to find a partner and come out there tonight so I can beat your worthless ass and show everyone that the True Indian Superstar is the best, and prove that I should be the one wearing the title, not you!” Beelzebozo: “God your breath stinks. I hope you’re not planning on kissing me.” Ryder pulls back from the clown. Ryder: “What the hell are you talking about now you worthless dickhead?!” Beelzebozo: “Look, I know you asked me to lick your asscrack earlier this week, but I keep telling you I’m not into that sort of thing! And for the last time, I DO NOT want to be in one of your gay porno movies! So find someone else to pester!” Ryder: “Quit calling me gay! If I didn’t have a match coming up I’d beat you so badly you’d never be able to wrestle again!! In fact, maybe I will right now! And after that I’ll kick the crap out of your stupid whore!” Beelzebozo’s face stretches into a hideous, insane grin. His voice drops to a whisper. Beelzebozo: “Go on, do it. Call her another name. Say something else about her. I’m BEGGING you, Ryder. Say it.” He steps closer to Ryder, and grits his teeth so loud that the grinding can actually be heard. His voice hisses from behind his clenched teeth. “PLEASE….SAY IT.” Ryder involuntarily takes a step back, then snarls and lashes out, catching the champion’s face with a vicious backhanded slap. Beelzebozo freezes, his expressionless face snapped toward the camera by the blow. Ryder: “I’m sick of this crap! I’ve got more important things to take care of right now! Your time is coming, though, so enjoy that title reign while you can! It’s gonna end soon, at the hands of the Cherokee Thrilla!!” With that, Ryder spins on his heel and walks off. Blaze: “Yeah, that’s right. Move out your five dollar ass before he decides to make change, asshole!” She suddenly spins, beaming, and latches herself onto Beelzebozo’s arm. “Oh WOW…That was so COOL! You defended my honor, risking your personal well-being for me!” The clown does not speak. Blaze: “You’re the best, baby.” She falters, noticing that he’s still not moving, not even to blink or breathe. “Umm…baby?” She tenatively kisses his cheek, causing him to wince slightly. This seems to bring him around. Beelzebozo: “I’m sorry, were you saying something? I suddenly got lost in a fantasy of delicious violence and bloodshed.” Blaze: “I was just thanking you for protecting me, and for being the best.” Beelzebozo: “Well, I AM the World Champ…I have to be the best, right?” Blaze: “Right!” She catches his eyes shifting in the direction that Ryder left. “You going to go pound him down, hon?” Beelzebozo: “No, not now. The last thing he needs is another excuse in case he loses. You know, like last time? I don’t want to give him an easy out.” Blaze: “Well, you could always go out there and take part in their match…” Beelzebozo: “….Eh, we’ll see. Come on, I need another drink. Defending you is thirsty work.” With a laugh, the two of them depart, and the scene fades out.
