UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #185

Into the Darkness

21 Jan 2004

Ezra Kazee — the Darkness

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in complete blackness. There is absolutely no light, absolutely no ability to tell what is going on in the room.) Ezra Kazee: Does this look familiar, Ryder? Does this look like something you’ve seen before? Do you like the way this looks? Maybe this frightens you a little. Maybe this darkness is a reminder of darker days. Maybe this darkness will send you to a memory that you don’t want to remember…… This here, this darkness, this use to be my home. The shadows, the areas without light, they use to bring fear and terror to my opponents, because they didn’t know where I was coming from. This darkness brought me lots of success, inside and out of the ring…… It was in this darkness that I so well used to my advantage to hold several titles. It was in this darkness that I used so well to gain that incredible advantage that so many people have chalked up to psychology. It was within this darkness that my enemies, and there were many, feared so much, that they didn’t go near it…… And it was in this darkness Ryder, which you lost your precious PWW title. It was in this darkness in which Joshua Dunn took everything that you held dear to you. It was in this darkness that you went from the penthouse to the chump house, and it will be in this darkness that you will wonder how you ever lost the advantage you had……. (All of a sudden, there is a flash of light, a very bright blue light almost like the flash of a camera. That engulfs the screen, but you can see the silhouette of Ezra Kazee standing there.) Just like that, Ryder, it can all be over. Just like that you could lose all of your dreams, your respect, and your confidence. Just like that you could be farther down on the food chain than you were before. Just like that Ryder, you could become the hunted instead of the hunter……. You compare me to a fly. A fly that you shoo away and keep coming back in your face. A fly you just can’t get rid of. Is that what I must be, than that is what I will be. The fly is a very crafty animal however. It just can’t be caught. It’s one of the little creatures which are hard to kill, if you can do so at all. It is a creature that will continue to bug you…… Of course this fly, isn’t so much of a fly. This fly is more like a dragon fly. This fly fights back. This fly will take everything you have and keep coming at you fighting and fighting and fighting till it’s all over. This fly will not let up…… You Ryder, have shown a kink in the armor. You are becoming unfocused. You are becoming wild. You are not so sure of yourself without that title. And you seem to have a lot more on your mind than just me…… Will that be the case on Sunday, Ryder, will you become unfocused on the fly you’re swatting and not realize that the fly has turned into a hornet that will sting you where it hurts the most, in your pride? (Again, the flash of light happens. This time, it is a little duller than before. The flash is still blue and still shows off the silhouette of Kazee, but it isn’t as clear, it isn’t as bright, almost as if it were fading out.) You think that I’m out of my head. You wonder how I can say that you barely beat me at Contest of Champions. How long did it take you to beat me? How many near falls did you have to go through. Where was your confidence as I kicked out move after move….. You ask me what makes me think I can beat you now? You ask me what makes me think that you will fall on Sunday? The thing that makes me think that you will fall on Sunday is that your confidence, while it is overflowing, is not anywhere near the hubris the Greeks had when they went into battle. Your confidence is cocky, and it is your cockiness that will be your downfall….. And that is what makes me confidence that I will win. My skill, my ability, my knowledge, improves match by match. And that knowledge and skill are unmatched by any man in this business. The thing that holds me back is my ability. The fact that I am not the strongest, fastest, or biggest man in this business is what holds me back. You, your ability will diminish, your knowledge has never been as great as mine, and neither has your skill. And I know how to counteract that now, and that is exactly what I will do. (For the third time, the light flashes, a little duller once again, still with the same silhouette.) Your nickname is a disgrace to the Native American people such as me. Your attitude is a disgrace to everything we hold near and dear. And your total disregard for other human being will be your downfall….. This match, it isn’t just a match for the Players Clubs true dominance. This match is to teach a measure of a lesson to you as to your disrespect of our people, the Native American people. And this match will knock the sense into you that you need. And when it’s all said and done Ryder, the final image you will see after the light fades out and the count hits three, will be me, standing over you victoriously, and doing to you what should have been done to you long before. (A white light goes on, and the camera is angled up at Kazee who is standing with his arms raised.) This is the last image you’ll see as you realize that the rest of your Prime Alliance will fall. Good Luck Ryder. (Scene fades to black.)
