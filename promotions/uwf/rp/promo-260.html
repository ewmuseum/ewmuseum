UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #260

Plague Speaks, Everyone Listens

1 Mar 2004

The Plague — UWF Arena: Press Conference

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It is a beautiful Monday afternoon on this March 1, 2004. We are in Chicago, Illinois outside the UWF Arena. A stage is set up outside and a few hundred people are sitting in front of this stage. On the stage is a eight foot table and three chairs positioned behind it. A banner behind the table reads 'The Plague.' There are two huge half stack amps on each side of the stage. You can hear the conversations going on in the crowd, none clear enough to make out.] 3 . . . 2 . . . 1 . . . RRRRAAAA HERE WE GO AGAIN! Right now! Let's go! Me and You! Toe to toe! So we can rock! We can roll! Here we go again! [Executive Vice President of Talent Relations Mike Montgomery steps up to the podium. He surveys the crowd a moment before beginning to speak.] Montgomery: Folks, I have a very simple rule. That rule is this, 'Every once in a while, shock the people.' Last night, on behalf of the Player's Club and the UWF, I did exactly that. Last night, I yet again brought a true legend of the game back to the UWF. You see, I found myself in need of someone with this man's...unique...talents. I needed a little insurance...a trouble shooter if you will. He is certainly that, so without further ado... [Music plays through the amps now. The crowd looks on in anticipation.] Montgomery: Please welcome... Run away no where To chicken shit, two face I'm gunna go there The fear I cannot taste You think you got me (you laugh) You're gunna tumble down (you laugh) Keep coming for me (fuck off) I'll drop you on the ground Montgomery: THE PLAGUE! [Some explosions go off from the stage as The Plague emerges from the left side of the stage and begins to walk up the steps and stands and poses for the crowd. He is wearing his patented 'Plague' t-shirt, he is wearing cutoff jeans and black shoes. His hair is ties back in a ponytail and he is wearing shades covering his eyes.] I fuck with no one Until you get into my face Break you down Mercy, I cannot allow Through your face, My fist will plow Watching as your blood pours down Lets do this now [The Plague sits down in the middle of the three chairs and positions the microphone in front of his face. He clears his throat and speaks to the awaiting crowd.] Plague: 'First off this isn't going to be your normal press conference. Here I talk, you listen got it. That means no stupid little questions. I know why you are all here and that is why I am going to just tell you how it is and your going to like it. I first want to thank Mike Montgomery for that kind intoduction, I also want to thank him for the two point five million he deposited into my account.' [Plague than pulls out from under his shirt a 24K Gold necklace that is now dangling from around his neck. Montgomery looks at the necklace and smiles.] Montgomery: 'Nice, glad you enjoyed it.' [Flashes from the hundreds of cameras from the crowd begin going off simultaniously to catch the action on stage.] Plague: 'Yeah but you know I am really looking forward to the rest of the five million you promised me, but we can talk about that later.' [Montgomery nods as Plague holds up three fingers.] Plague: 'Lase night was a great night, I can't get over how it felt to be back in the ring. And the best part is that three little bitches all in the course of one night, all falling victim to my hands. Damn it feels good to be back, and it was even better to do so at The UWF Arena in beautiful Chicago, Illinois.' [The crowd pops.] Plague: 'Pathetic, cheap pops never fail.' [Montgomery looks at Plague and shakes his head. The crowd goes silent.] Plague: 'Finally I can continue without anymore interruptions from the peanut gallery.' Plague: 'Well folks, I know you all witnessed my shocking return last night, and boy was it great. I not only got a victory over Moraless, but I showed Bruce Gordy and Raid what Hardcore is really like. Gordy, you need to stop living off of the EoD and my coattails. The EoD died a long time ago, along with your career. I mean I only took you under my wing back in the GIWF for Stable Wars. I don't know what I was thinking allowing Tommy to convince me you were a good choice. Oh yeah, how is your head feeling.' [Plague and Montgomery high five and laugh.] Plague: 'Thats a good one, I mean maybe not for you Gordy, but damn it was great to see you bleed by my barbed wire bat. Maybe now you will stop with the whole king of hardcore shit and sole survivor of the Elements of Destruction. If it weren't for me there would be no EoD and as of right now there isn't and I swear if you keep bringing up the name there will be a hell of a lot more pain for you to endure, trust me on that one.' [Plague cracks his knuckles and his neck as he continues.] Plague: 'Now onto Moraless, were you surprised? I know you weren't expecting me to be your opponent, and I know you were going into the match all high and mighty thinking you could just walk away with the victory. Well guess again, and boy oh boy I defeated the man who defeated Seymour Almasy, the so-called greatest star ever to step into the UWF, the PTC Champ, what a crock. Seymour you are so pathetic, I mean why don't you except the challenge from my man over here.' [Plague pats Montgomery on the back.] Plague: 'It's not that hard, just say yes.But I am sure you are too worried about your failing career to think about Mike. You are going on a downward spiral my friend, and soon your name will be lost in the sands of time much like Jamie Shock and Lyle WIle and Ice Cold Killa. I mean seriously Seymour, your time is nearly up and you know you don't want to face a worthy opponent, too afraid Mikey over here will end your pathetic little career. But enough of that rant, lets get back to me and my thoughts here.' [Plague points to the third empty chair, Mike Montgomery pulls out a can of Raid and places it on the chair next to him, Plague and Mike begin to laugh.] Plague: 'Mike you are awesome, did I ever tell you that? I mean Raid, what the hell is wrong with you? You beat Bruce Gordy, holy shit what a remarkable accomplishment. And now you are about to embark on a great mission, you are about to seek the THC Title from The Iron Lung. Well it seems that the UWF Booking Committee had a little something else in mind, and that was to make it a three-way, and they added me. What a great way to reward a returnign superstar. I hope you listened closely S-U-P-E-R-S-T-A-R.' [Plague cracks an evil grin] Plague: 'Yes I am a superstar Raid, I am a superstar Lung, take a look at the title history bitches, I am sure you will find some interesting things there. You see I am not some newbie, I can beat Bruce Gordy or Ben Jericho. I am The Plague and frankly Lung, beating Ben in a Table Match is nothing, try going through what I went through to gain my Championships. I have been in every match imaginable. And I can't wait to show you two what Hardcore is all about on Sunday. You can ask anyone in The UWF, past or present and they will tell you that I am the true definition of hardcore. The facts are in the books, read them if you like, or just show up on Sunday and I will give you a real life experience.' [Plague removes his shades. He motions to the camera to come closer.] Plague: 'I'm back baby, and Sunday The THC Title will be mine again, and then I will move on to my next bitch. Who knows, it could be anyone. No one is safe in the UWF. I am back and I hope everyone is ready to be infected.' [Plague stands up.] Run away no where To chicken shit, two face I'm gunna go there The fear I cannot taste You think you got me (you laugh) You're gunna tumble down (you laugh) Keep coming for me (fuck off) I'll drop you on the ground I fuck with no one Until you get into my face Break you down Mercy, I cannot allow Through your face, My fist will plow Watching as your blood pours down Lets do this now [Mike Montgomery gets up and grabs the can of Raid and throws it into the crowd as Plague and Mike exit the stage and the scene fades out to black.]
