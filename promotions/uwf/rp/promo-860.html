The Day After

5 Jan 2005

The Communist — The Floor?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Communist, Champion of the People, the Russian Bear, the Ultimate Weapon of Hardcore, we could really blab on and on about his many myriad titles, but lets get down to buisness. He's passed out on the floor, boxers around his head, vodka bottles strone about, and red party streamers covering the furniture. A blanket covers Commie's lower half, but we can assume that he's not covered very well UNDER the blanket.] Commie: *groooooan* My head feels like a sledgehammer got dropped on it... [He looks over and sees the vodka bottle by his head] Ahhh... THAT'S what happened. The cause and solution of all life's problems. [tosses the bottle aside, a crash is heard elsewhere in the room] You know... I should probably have carpet installed for when this happens again... [A muffled groan is heard under the blanket. The Communist pulls the blanket up and peeks under, his eyes widen and drops the blanket. He peeks again, as if his brain refused to register what he had just seen, then drops it again] Commie: Oh Shit. Soviette: *from under the blanket* I swear, I'm never drinking vodka again... Where am I? *the blanket stirs with movement* Who is this? *more stirring from the blanket, a groan* and why are my knees so sore? Wait a minuit... [Soviette brusts up from under the covers, bare. She looks at Commie, slaps him, then grabs the covers, covering her breasts] Soviette: What did you do to me?! Commie: Well... Umm.. I... Soviette: You took advantage of me! Commie: No! I don't Remember... Soviette: You did so! *she huffs, waiting for her temper to cool* No more vodka. Ever. Commie: Agreed... You know... this isn't ALL bad... Soviette: YES. It is. Well... mostly bad. *She turns to The Communist* NOT A WORD to anyone. Do you Understand? Commie: Of course Soviette: ESPECIALLY not the Council. They'd have me shipped back in an instant. *she slaps his hand which had mysteriously drifted to her thigh* Hands Off. I need a shower. A long, long shower. Commie: Cold Shower? [Soviette stands, leaving the blanket behind] Soviette: Perhapse... going to join me Commie?
