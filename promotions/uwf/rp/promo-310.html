UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #310

Whispers in the dark

6 Apr 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello darkness, my old friend… At length, the voice of the UWF World Champion rises from the abysmal darkness… “What to do, what to do…” Eeeeever so slowly, the Daemonic Clown’s face materializes, bathed in crimson light. The hellish visage stares, seemingly not into the camera, but at some random point beyond it. Save for his lips and the VERY occasional eye momement, it’s as if a mannequin’s head is on the screen, talking to us. A disquieting thought, that. “So the things we know are as follows. Joshua Dunn may or may not be playing a very clever game with us. He may or may not actually want a hardcore match, where the distinct advantage is ours. He may or may not hold us in esteem. And he may or may not be the one who could topple our title reign.” The clown’s face disappears, only to appear in profile on the left side of the screen a moment later. “In other words, we know nothing of the enemy.” It disappears, and reappears in opposite profile on the right side. “No shit, Sherlock. You figure that out by yourself or did you mom have to explain it to you using really small words?” Again he vanishes, and appears in his original place. “Now is not the time to bicker! We must steel ourselves for battle! The problem here is, which battle are we to prepare for? Going to a fight with the wrong weapons and mindset can have disastrous results. Horses to a tank fight, swords to a gun battle, real bullets to a paint gun game…you see the dilemma?” Gone, and on the left again. “One of many, to be sure. But I think we should take them one at a time, don’t you?” And back to the middle. “The main problem, as I see it, is to judge just what the intentions of Mr. Dunn are. At first, he tried to get us into his type of match, a pure contest of wrestler against wrestler—no tricks, no weapons, no stipulations. Nothing wrong with that, to be certain. After the tiresome battle with Ryder it would be a welcome relief. “But then he sought to use our pride against us, a trick that failed. “In our anger we thought to stymie him by merely annihilating him upon our entry into the ring on the fateful night.” The face returns to the right once more. “He seeks to trick us! He wants to destroy us! We must crush him now or suffer!!” Now to the left. “But to do so would be to play into the hands of those who claim we are nothing more than a joke, a merely transitional title holder that is merely there to take up space between the reigns of those truly worthy of the belt! We cannot allow this to transpire! We must defeat him in the ring, no matter what rules are laid out!” To the middle once more. “Now, he seeks to appease us by offering to take us on in a match all too well known to us...a no-rules war. He speaks of being the underdog and wanting to show he can rise to the challenge. He talks of giving the fans what they want.” Another return to the right. “To hell with the fans! They spit on us and laugh in our faces! They do not see the truth! They wish to be blind, so they may continue to mock and despise! What they want is irrelevant!” Again to the left. “But he speaks the truth! The fans are what keeps the federation alive! If they are not entertained, they will put forth their fists, with thumbs pointed to hell itself! And if they want us to battle with Dunn on the field of his choosing, then perhaps we should give it to them… “However, he has powerful allies. Allies who no doubt would love to have one of their number the top man in the company. Allies who would surely conspire against us were there no rules to bar their way. He says he would keep them at bay, but can we trust him to hold his word?” The middle. “It seems he wishes to play with our head, but in what manner? Did he truly misspeak in the past; did he really not want to goad us? Does he really want a good, fair match? “Is he truly prepared to take on the pain we can dish out in a match with nothing to hold us back? Is it a mere attempt to soothe, so that were we to unleash our rage upon him it would be legal, if not justified? “Or is he now twisting the net a little tighter, with promises of bloody glory when what he really wants is what he always wanted—no outside influences, so that we may pit our might against his and see who the better wrestler truly is? “And can he be trusted? He speaks of respect, and being akin to us, despite his acknowledgement of not being able to truly know us. But is it a front, to put us off our guard, only that he and his friends may swoop in and steal the prize from the rightful owner? “What say you? Do we merely destroy him and end this? Do we engage in the bloody delights of a match where rules are not a worry? Or do we give the world an old-fashioned, mano-y-mano wrestling match where the winner is the one who is truly the best, not merely the most violent or cruel?” The right. “Blood is what is needed! They will not respect us, so let them FEAR us! Be it before the bell or after, legal or otherwise, let the crimson joy splash upon the faces of the jeering throngs, so that they may see the light! And let Dunn, too, see that his games have backfired in his face, blowing him into a fate worse than death!” The left. “No, respect can still be gained! Take the first match, just him and us, and let the best wrestler be victorious! Dunn will hold up his end of the bargain, and so should we! We can show the world that none is our equal, and surely they must realize the error of their beliefs and begin to see us as a true champion!” Right. “They will never see! They must have the lessons ground into them until they are too numb to resist! Let the blood flow, the bodies fall, and the screams of the horrified masses be the symphony of our ascension!” Left. “We can still do this the right way! We can show our sense of honor and fair play, and be hailed as the conquering hero!” Right. “Violence is the only answer! Fear is the key!” Left. “Respect and dignity must be our banner that we proudly wave over the fields of battle!” Right. “FEAR!” Left. “RESPECT!” Middle. “WHAT DO I DOOOOO!?!” Shrieking, the face falls back into the darkness. His final wail slowly dies away, and we are left with the silent void we started with. Eventually, Beelzebozo emerges from the dark once more, his entire body visible. The light is no longer red, but white. He is now looking into the camera, and seems amazingly calm. He crosses his arms and speaks. “The die is cast. I have decided. And I shall give you, Mr. Dunn, the benefit of the doubt. “It may come back to haunt me, but I shall give you your original desire, Mr. Dunn. Just you and I in the wrestling ring. No gimmicks or stacked decks, just the two of us relying on our own skills to see us through. You will have to trust in my sense of fair play, and I in yours. However, I think that is something we can both count on. “So meet me in the ring. Bring everything you have inside you, and I shall as well. And we shall see who triumphs.' The light changes to red once more. “But a warning, Mr. Dunn. If this is all a plot to screw me out of the title…the consequences will be swift and horrifying. Not just to you, but to your cadre of compatriots as well. You’ll win the battle…but lose the war. “It is not a threat, but a promise, that I give you. “Prove yourself to be a true man and meet me with nothing up your sleeves, as I shall do. Show the world that you are indeed a worthy contender to the World Title. “Show the world otherwise, and you’ll find that having the last laugh does you precious little good…” He steps into the darkness, but his voice can still be heard. “…when you’re having it in hell.”
