UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #44

Real quick... before the card... you should know the score

Monday, October 06, 2003 (year unrecorded)

White Wolf — Backstage

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lights are bright, the stage is set... But are they ready? Tonight, vital decisions will be made and only certainty about tonight's show is the excitement that will be shared by the fans and alumni alike. Sitting in a chair White Wolf wallows away the hours thinking and rethinking about how he can overcome his two powerful competitors. The dark room has little light and White Wolf is found on an old armchair. Next to the chair is his pal Murdoc, an arctic Wolf. White Wolf's head is blasting a mile a minute, when suddenly his train of though it interrupted by the noise in the hall. He begins to speak aloud for the camera to hear him. White Wolf: 'They're pouring in now... Soon, everyone will be in his or her seats expecting to see an epic show better then the last... But I'm expecting so much more. This match tonight could mean the difference in great and legendary. But first I'll have to get through Chris Stevens to retain my title. I don't see any problems there.' 'But after him... the trouble brews. I have to get past some tough, albeit beatable, competition. I know I can pull it off... but what lies ahead of Digital Mortality and Joshua Dunn?' White Wolf contemplates about outcomes... White Wolf: 'Supposing I progress... I should have more competition. My predictions for the final rounds? Ruff Ryder, Malcolm Kennedy, and this character Mentok...' 'As for Ruff Ryder, he seems to be the kind of man I'd appreciate in a battle. A well-rounded combatant who can talk and walk. He shares my views on the Players Club, and will fight for what he stands for... Courageous and heart filled. But he lacks something. I'm not sure what it is about the guy... but he doesn't seem threatening to my run at this point.' 'Malcolm Kennedy? What can I say about this guy that he doesn't display openly anyway. He backed down from the Players Club because he couldn't take anymore of it... He claims I'm only here to be star because I couldn't do it long ago... which isn't the case. Because I'm here to satisfy a hunger for fighting... but the path to glory grows on a man and I am becoming stronger and more satisfied with my state of mind and strength here in the UWF. And I hate to admit it, but Malcolm was one of the first to give me the kick start I truly needed.' 'Mentok... what can I say about this guy... He cracks me up. I like em. But I'll destroy him in the hour of battle. Mentok... You need your own division... One you can fight an Imp, a Turtle, a random amount of bunnies, a monkey, and worst of all, a couple of communists.' 'But tonight... I'll focus on the present... And the challenges at present are those of Chris Stevens... Digital Mortality... and Joshua Dunn... I've got a long night ahead of me... But I can do this...' Scene fades as White Wolf leaves the room dressed in his ring attire. He's pumped... he's pissed... and he wants that title more then life itself
