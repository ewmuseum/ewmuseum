UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #590

The legal side of life

1 Aug 2004

Ezra Kazee — Shareholders Branch Office.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in the familiar setting of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Sitting at the familiar desk is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a green t-shirt and a black baseball hat. Just plain clothing. There is a phone on the desk and some papers sitting there, along with a bunch of papers. Kazee is sitting there shuffling some papers.) Kazee: Welcome to the Shareholders Branch office. Where yours truly is still in charge and still the manager of it. They haven’t taken it away. Unlike what happen to my good friend Mike Montgomery. You’ll have to excuse me however, I’m awaiting a call. (Shortly, the phone rings) Kazee: UWF Shareholders Branch Office….. (silence) Ah, hey there Vincent, how are you doing today……. (silence) Well that’s good to hear. I’ve been doing ok. But I need you for something…… (silence) No, I don’t need a manager, even though you were one of the best I ever worked with……. (silence) Well, I need a lawyer…… (Silence is heard as Kazee searches for some papers.) Well, here is the deal. I’m filing a lawsuit for breech of contract….. (silence) Well, I’m looking at my contract, which is a very standard contract for the UWF. I also have copies of some other peoples contracts here……. (silence) It’s ok, I have the permission to be looking at them, especially since I am the manager of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. I have the contracts of Joshua Dunn and Kevin Starr…… (Kazee grabs his glasses and puts them on.) Well, here is the deal. Here in the contract that all of us have with the UWF it clearly states that all three of us signed, it clearly states that we must “respect and abide by the UWF rulebook as it is written.” Now I don’t know if you have been paying attention to the UWF lately, but there is video tape evidence that Joshua Dunn and Kevin Starr changed the UWF rulebook with no one’s authority and thus were not abiding by or respecting it….. (There is silence on the phone.) Well, like I said, I need a lawyer, and you’re the best one that I know. (silence) The defendants that I am naming in my lawsuit are the UWF, Patti Preest, Richard Ray Vaughn, Mike Montgomery, Joshua Dunn, and Kevin Starr. As I’ve stated already, it is for Breech of Contract. I have the video tape and all the evidence I need from when Starr and Dunn changed the rule book, and none of these people did anything about it. (silence) Well, I suppose I could name PTC, but they really don’t have anything to do with this. (silence) Monetary amount? Well, um, whatever you think is fitting. My actual end goal is for my championship match to have a stipulation put onto it, but we can put a nice amount of money onto it…… (silence) Great, will see you tonight…… (Kazee hangs up the phone.) Now, let’s see the match. Well, um, not much to really say. I’ve fought along side these men and fought against them. I’ve been on both sides of the fence here and who knows what will happen. Is it a reunion, well no. Mainly because I’m done with the Players Club at this point. See, unlike Dunn and Starr, I respect the rule book, and I abide by it. And I’m not going to sit here and watch as they change the rulebook to their own needs…… Persia, I’m sorry you got involved in this. You are a good man who will go far. But they teamed you with a cheat. They teamed you with someone who wouldn’t know right from wrong if it hit him upside his head. And your also facing the former UWF Tag Team Champions. This match will be a barn burner, but in the end, there will only be one team that reigns supreme, and that will be ours….. Good Luck to you both. You’ll need it. (scene fades to black.)
