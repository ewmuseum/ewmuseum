UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #811

With Both hands and a compass

3 Dec 2004

The Communist — Oh, Here and there.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist lays on the weight bench, Soviette streached across his hands as he benchpresses her. She lays back staying pretty still while she looks over the card for Fandamonium: Soviette: You and JOSH JUSTICE?! Who's assinine idea was that? Commie: The Fans. It was a vote by the fans. Soviette: Good god. You see what happens when you let democracy run things. First Bush, now this. Commie: Atleast they voted me in for the North American Title. Second to the Heavyweight title. Soviette: Bah. Second place is second place. You had a chance to be the contender, but you threw it away. And of course, who can remember you loss to Dreammaker :She smacks the Communist with the file in her hands, he almost drops her on his face: Commie: HEY! calm down Comrad, we will make things right. If I get the NA title, We can make a push for the heavyweight title later. Everything will work out. Trust me. Soviette: I DID trust you and you became a musing idiot who couldn't wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag. Enlightenment. *meh* You can't truly tell me you were happy there, planting flowers and talking about how happy you were. Commie: I felt at peace, sometimes that's better than happiness. Soviette: Fine, you can be as enlightened as you want OUTSIDE of the ring. INSIDE the ring you need to focus. And at Fandamonium, you'll be focusing on Killer Dude and Digital Mortality. I saw Dude's little rant about how he's the future of wrestling and how he's going to wear three belts by the end of Fandamonium. It was really quite cute. Commie: Oh? Cute? Soviette: Of course, he's so insecure, he has to wear his own promotional shirts. *chuckles* And he Rants and raves before the camera, posturing like a peacock, impressed with his own plumage. *sighs* I must say, even you should be able to beat him. Commie: And Digital Mortality? Soviette: A Wash up. He may have been able to take you in his prime, but not now. I'd still be weary of him, but you can definatly win. :The Communist sets Soviette down beside the bench and streaches his arms out, then his neck and back: Commie: Now, about this Tag Match. Where are we wrestling? Soviette: In a Venetian Home Rumble. It's a sort of tradition from what I hear. You fight inside a house. It's Hardcore, You'll feel right at home. No pun intended. Commie: I like it. And I have a partner. Josh Justice? Who I insulted and attacked? The Bourgouasis Dog that dares to stand for the evils of Capitalism! This is not good. I do not trust him. Soviette: Well, you don't very well have a choice. You will fight with Josh Justice, and who knows, you might even win the tag titles. You might leave the arena with two titles around your waist. And then the Council will get off my back. Commie: I'm not concerned about the Council. :Soviette slaps the shit out of the Communist, her eyes filled with fire: Soviette: Well I do! These people have a great deal of power over our lives, and not only have you been cavorting with the Opiate of the Masses, but you've been loosing matches because of it. They will not stand for your insulence any further, if you do not win SOMETHING at this PPV, there will be HELL to pay! You're 'Enlightened' I'm sure you know what hell is! So Unless you want to experience it FIRST HAND I suggest you WHIP YOU ENLIGHTENED ASS INTO SHAPE. :Soviette storms out of the room, knocking a trash can down on the way out:
