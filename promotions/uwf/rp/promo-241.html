UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #241

One enchanted mid-afternoon

26 Feb 2004

Joshua Dunn — A Park

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SAS, It’s coming up close, and what a main event, a randomly placed Tag match, pitting The World Champ and the self proclaimed Definition of Precision against the rumoured challenger for the World Title Belt and the most promising new comer to hit UWF TV since the Great Tsunami Oni. We are of course talking about the match up of Beezlebozo and Kenny Mercury –v– Joshua Dunn and Iron Lung. Strange to see a new comer shoved into a main event match so soon after his debut, but it worked for the demented clown didn’t it? Well nothing much has happened to hype the match so far, not a breath from Iron Lung, not a peep from Mercury, no manic laughter from Beezlebozo, and no words from Dunn. Until now] [The Scene opens in a park, just that, the generic park, no distinguishing features. There’s a playing field lines with birch tree’s, a set of soccer goals, a few kids running around, young and old, and on one green bench where the paint is peeling off out of neglect, a solitary person sits. This person is wearing a light brown, nearly fawn trench coat, wrapped up tightly around him, the belt tied and lapels turned up round the neck. A light breeze ruffles the lapels while the man watches the children at play, not really because he knows any of them, more of a lack of anything else to watch and keep his amusement, a sudden gust of wind whips his hair up in an unruly fashion and makes the lapels flutter harsher. We all know who this must be on the bench, but the cameraman instead goes beyond the seated man and focuses in on a small group of Children playing catch with a football. The kids must all be around Twelve years old and running around mindlessly. Just off to the side of them another child sits alone on the grass, also around twelve years of age, playing with a set of action figures, and is quite enjoying himself. The group of three kids stop their playing and start to converse among themselves, they giggle amongst them and then approach the other kid. The camera man zooms in and watches] [The largest kid of the group steps forward to the lone kid and towers over him, hands on hips he enjoys looming over the other one, filled with a sense of power. He bends down and talks to the other one, while the other two kids peer over the shoulder of the looming one like some sorts of goons. The lone kid, still minds his own business amusing himself with the adventures he’s making up in his head] Looming Kid: Hey Jeeeeaarry, what you doing Jeeeeaarry? [The looming kid seems quite used to turning peoples names into insults in themselves. He puts on a smirk that would be worthy of the sideways grin of Joshua Dunn, but not quite, the “goons” giggle on cue. Quite free of maliciousness the lone kid looks up, quite cheery and bright eyed, and talks back to the looming kid, almost unaware he’s being made fun of] Jerry: Nothing much Frank, you know, just playin’ [The three kids giggle again and “Frank” speaks out again] Frank: Playing with your dolls Jeeeeaarry? [Jerry gives an almost puzzled look and tilts his head slightly] Jerry: Dolls? These are action figures! [Frank and his cronies laugh again] Frank: Sure their Dolls, look, there’s Cindy, Barbie, and Ken! [Frank points to each of the action figures in turn and throws back his head in laughter, and so does his goons, Jerry just shrugs off the insult and corrects franks mistake] Jerry: No Frank, their Action figures, of the UWF guy’s, that’s Seymour Almasy, Danny-Jaye, and this one’s Jamie Shock [Frank seeing he’s not making the impression he hoped tries another tact] Frank: So why you playing with your dolls baby? You not ready for sports baby? Jerry: I’m not a baby; in fact I’m older than you [The goons giggle at frank this time, but shut up quick when Franks glares at them] Frank: Do you…? Do you mind if I have a look at that Seymour Almasy doll? Jerry: …. Sure Frank, just be careful with it it’s my favourite [He hands the action figure to Frank who’s grins is almost as unnerving as that of a sharks, as he snatches the doll and inspects it] Frank: Yeah, is pretty coo… KEEP AWAY! [As if it were some sort of unwritten code the group of three slit apart and start to throw around the action figure to each other as a distraught Jerry gets to his feet and tries to claim back what’s his. The man who seems very interested in all of this watches more closely. The kid Jerry desperately tries to catch the toy in mid air as it’s thrown around, but just always seems to fall short of catching it] Jerry: No fair, give it back Goon1: Huh Huh, KEEP AWAY! Goon2: KEEP AWAY! Frank: KEEP AWAY! Jerry: Give it baaack! [The toys tossed around worse than a salad, and Jerry is still getting no closer to catching it as more shouts of “keep away” are heard, but all of a sudden Frank doesn’t throw away the action figure, but instead extends his hand which is clasping the toy] Frank: Alright Jeeeeaarry Here you go Jerry: Thanks Frank, I knew you weren’t all bad… [But unknown to Jerry one of the goons has dropped on all fours and placed himself directly behind him. Frank near enough punches the doll into Jerry and pushes him back, causing him to tumble backwards over the goon, landing with a soft thud on the grass, the wind knocked out of him, and struggling to breath] Frank: You’re such a wimp Jerry; my mom could whip your ass! Jerry: …Seymour…. would…beat…. you…. Frank: But he isn’t here though is he? [The smug expression of triumph inflates Frank’s head, and his goons inflate it further by laughing at his joke. A shadow appears over them as a person stands between them and the mid afternoon sun, casting a long shadow over them all and a voice rings out one syllable which pitch increases in the way that can even make grown men squeamish sometimes] Recognisable Voice: Nooooooo… [The goons and Frank Turn around slowly and face what seems to them to be a ghost, judging by their expressions, the goons are the first to take a step back behind Frank before legging it across the park, leaving Frank there himself. The camera turns around and looks into the trench coated man, face on, instantly recognised as Joshua Dunn, the Communist Scotsman] JD: …But I am [Frank just stands there gawping at the figure, before summoning up the courage to step towards Dunn in a stupid confidence shared only with Hero’s and complete imbeciles] Frank: And what you gonna do Mr? [Dunn bends down so he can look the kid square in the eyes, and grins, not so much because he’s happy of it, but rather that disconcerting smile we saw on Frank not moments before] JD: You know who I am don’t you? [Caught off guard Frank just finds enough energy to nod. Dunn reaches down and grabs the back of his collar and lifts Frank clear off the ground] JD: I can make grown men cry; just imagine what I could do to you? [Cringing in mid air, frank brings his hands up to his face protectively] Frank: Please, don’t hurt me! [Dunn lowers the kid again] JD: Clear off! [Frank obligingly does, as fast as his under developed legs can carry him] [Awe inspired, Jerry just gawps almost as badly at Joshua as the others and gazes starry eyed at the athlete. Dunn just turns around and starts to walk away, snapping out of the trance, the kid Jerry, gathers up his three action figures and runs after the communist. He walks long side the man for a minute hoping that Joshua will start to speak to him. Their hair and clothes ruffle in the wind but not much else happens. He plucks up the courage to say something] Jerry: Hey, you’re… your Joshua Dunn aren’t you? [There could have been so many better things to say, but when your pumped full of awe, certain muscles like the mind shut down] JD: People call me lots of things Jerry: But that’s one of them… right? [Dunn sighs] JD: Yes it is [A new gleam seems to fill the boy’s eyes as he trots to keep up with the pace of the Scotsman] Jerry: Oh wow, no one will believe this JD: Your probably right [A small silence ensues as Jerry struggles to find something else to say] Jerry: What were you going to do to Frank and his buddies? JD: Nothing Jerry: Nothing? But you did all that stuff… and, and you picked him up and stuff JD: Yes I did, what did you expect me to do? Hit a kid? Jerry: Well… No… but, but why do all that stuff? JD: I don’t like people being picked on Jerry: They weren’t picking on me, they were just hazing me. I’m sure of it JD: Hazing you?… [This makes Dunn glance at the kid, with a look that says, “You are really that naive?”] JD: … Sure they were… word of advice kid Jerry: Yes Mr Dunn? JD: Get rid of the dolls [Jerry stops dead for a moment, but noticing that Joshua is not in the slightest bit worried about this, runs to catch up again] Jerry: But I like my dolls- I mean action figures JD: You’ll learn kid, you’ll take my advice or you’ll learn the hard way [Dunn leaves the park gates, with Jerry trailing behind him, not to far away from the park gates Dunn stops at a convenient car park, and takes out the keys to his black ford car. Undoing the lock at the drivers side he gets in and starts the engine, Jerry just looks on, perched the other side of the drivers door, looking almost dejected before Dunn puts down the electric windows, and Jerry looks slightly happier again, before he realises that Dunn did it solely to get a better view of behind himself. As Dunn manoeuvres the car towards the entrance gate, Jerry plods along side still speaking to Dunn] Jerry: Hey, you’re leaving? JD: Yes kid, I got more important things to do then stand around talking to a kid all day [Almost whimpering Jerry turns around and hangs his head and walks away from the car, his slow pace doesn’t carry him anyplace fast. Dunn looks on, almost out of coincidence as he checks to see that his junction is clear, and sighs, mumbling under his breath and fumbling around in the car] JD: Your getting soft Joshua… HEY KID! [Almost instantaneously Jerry is back at the side of the car looking perkier than ever, looking intently at Dunn] JD: Take this before you regret that too [Dunn flicks out a small card, and Jerry tentatively takes it, like he believes the thing will take his hand off] JD: Now get out of here, you bother me [Dunn rolls up the window and moves out of the car park and off out of the picture, Jerry inspects what he’s given, and so does the camera, it shows that Jerry now holds a UWF Trading card of Joshua Dunn, the kid flips over to view the picture side of the card, revealing, scrawled over the picture of the Communist is Dunn’s signature. The kids eyes gleam as if he’s about to cry, and lifts up his Almasy Doll, and looks it square in the plastic eyes] Jerry: Sorry Seymour, I got a new favourite now [Sticking the signed card into his pocket Jerry runs back into the park, still clutching his action figures, and the camera holds the picture for a moment before fading out to black]
