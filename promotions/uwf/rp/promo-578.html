UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #578

Leaches

27 Jul 2004

Kenny Mercury — UWF Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.] [The UWF locker room. The place where it all begins… and ends. Men go out ready for war… and come back in broken in pieces, shattered. This time, it’s Kenny Mercury. Kenny sits on a bench, leaning forward. His golden locks cover most of his face. Across his lap rests his most treasured possession… The Universal Wrestling Federation’s Intercontinental Championship Title Belt. Laughter emits from under the long wavy hair of the Intercontinental Champion.] “Heh…heh… heheheheheh…..HEHEH-HAHAAHAHAHAHAHA~! HAHAAHAAA~! HA!” [Kenny flings his hair, revealing his face. He stares straight ahead and begins to lean back against the metal locker doors. He doesn’t blink.] Kenny Mercury: “Misfit… heheheh… You little piece of shit. That’s all you are… a piece of shit.” “Do I owe you something? You throw all these accusations against me. That I teamed with Ben Jericho in the Black Star Revolution to get myself in the spotlight… that I used Benji to get a piece of the pie that he had baked after breaking his back night after night since 1999. Bull-freaking-shit. You pieces of crap! You think that’d you know abuse… that you’re been used… You stupid imbecile… you think that you’re some kind of martyr? You don’t know abuse… You think you do… But you don’t know REAL abuse…. Don’t worry though… I’m going to show you it…” “What’s REAL abuse? Real abuse… stepping into the ring with the technical wizard… going toe to toe with the shooting star… having a one on one appointment with the ring doctor… FACING THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION IN HAND TO HAND COMBAT!!! That’s abuse right there… When I’ve got you locked in the Spell-Binder… in the center of the ring…” [Mercury grins sadistically and runs his tongue along his lips.] Kenny Mercury: “Right… where I want you… where there’s no escape… and I am in total control… I have supreme power over your body… I call all the shots… I am the alpha and the omega… I can finish you when I wish… or I can keep the match going… I control how much pain I inflict… and when… I can apply more pressure… making you wish you were dead… I am in control… I own you…” “Black Star Revolution was a phenomenon the likes of which had never been seen, and probably never will be again. Pure technical wrestling at its finest… Too bad Benji had to abandon me. Misfit, you know nothing of the REAL Black Star Revolution… I didn’t use Ben… HE USED ME! His career was in shambles and I was the one way ticket back to the top. Ever since I’ve been in this God forsaken federation, I’ve been the prodigy. I’ve been the one who was destined for greatness, destined to reach the pinnacle of the federation… destined to destroy every obstacle set in my way. Ben’s career was over… he’s a leach… just like now… nothing changes… he’s leaching off of you and Slasher. Maybe you are a martyr… but just for the wrong reasons… no… martyr isn’t the correct term… more like a sheep… a stupid, confused, imbecilic lamb being lead to the slaughter. Fucking nincompoop… I hope you enjoy your demise, because Unforgiven will bring it about, after he’s done with you.” “This Intercontinental Championship… it symbolizes my perfection. All that I’ve fought for… all the sacrifices I’ve made along the way… all the bodies I’ve broken and dreams I’ve shattered… and all my perfect technical prowess… and the unfathomable amount of talent that is my birthright… it’s all right here… within this beautiful chunk of gold and leather. I’ve go down as the greatest Intercontinental champion of all time… past the greats… better than there has ever been. I don’t intend on losing this belt… and I don’t intend on losing to you. Misfit… this is your last day of freedom… come Wednesday…” Heheheheh…..heh… “I’ll make you mine.” [End Transmission.]
