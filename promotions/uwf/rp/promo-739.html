UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #739

Gone Fishin'

2 Oct 2004

Joshua Justice — A lake in Idaho.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s true that beauty is in the eye of the beholder… [The water is calm as the fish swim freely through it.] …vacations are as well. [A few lines and hooks go splashing though the water and the fish scatter.] For some, an enjoyable respite means a five star hotel and room service. For others, it’s a warm beach of white sand. Or a ski hill fresh with new snow. [On top of the water, which is a lake, you can see in the distance two men sitting on a boat fishing.] For Wesley Williams and Joshua Justice It’s a placid lake in Idaho where the chief concern is lack of appropriate bait. [These two half-brothers sit back and relax with their feet up on the edge of the boat. They look calm and collected as they sit with their lines in the water awaiting a nibble.] Wesley: I tell you what, Joshua. There’s perfect, and then there’s perfect. This is perfect. Joshua: What do you mean, ‘perfect and perfect’? How can there be two kinds of perfect? Wesley: Think of it this way. Salma Hayek gets all the pub as havin’ perfect looks, right? Joshua: Salma Hayek? She’s not really my type… Wesley: Dude. You have got to get more plugged in. Anyway, just for the sake of argument, let’s assume she’s perfect. Joshua: Wish I could say I agree… Wesley: Joshua! Joshua: Okay, okay. She’s perfect. Wesley: But me, I say Marilyn is better. So that makes two kinds of perfect. Salma perfect… and Marilyn perfect. Joshua: Gotcha. Perfect and really, really perfect. Perfect, more perfect and most perfect. [Wesley stretches out his arms with a smile on his face.] Wesley: Which is what this is! I am so glad we decided to come back! [Joshua hands Wesley a chocolate candy bar.] Joshua: Me too, Wesley. Last time we were here it was on a work assignment to wrestle in the local gym in a tag team. Now it’s a real vacation. Wesley: For one of us, anyway. You gotta unwind, Mr. Justice. Let your hair down and relax. Joshua: I am relaxed. Wesley: Right. [Joshua jumps to his feet and recasts his line very violently.] Joshua: But we have to be alert! For all we know, Dr. Death could attack at any minute. Or they could find out who bombed my jet a few weeks back. Or… Wesley: Right. [Wesley lowers his hat over his eyes.] Wesley: Ya never know when old Dr. Death will decide to conquer Idaho. Joshua: Or The Communist! What if he’s already taken over Idaho and convinced everyone to turn into a communist and we don’t even know it? [Joshua sits back down with his line sitting in the placid water.] Wesley: Joshua, that is exactly what I’m talkin’ about! For this to be a real vacation, you have to put those thoughts out of yorumind! This is why we shouldn’t have flown down here in your new UWF private jet. Dicky Flint might be trying to get in contact with you right now! Joshua: You really think so, Wesley? [Wesley sits up in his seat and looks at Joshua with a smirk.] Wesley: To warn you about the real threat to peace and security. Cause Dicky Flint would do that. He’s all about keeping the peace. Joshua: Who’s threatening the peace and security? The Communist? Dicky Flint? Slasher? Alex Grey? Seraphiend?! [Wesley leans in towards Joshua.] Wesley: That’s your problem. I know we’re busy right now. I have GTT4 to worry about, PLUS Stormfront over in 4WW. You have a PPV match to win to get a shot at the TV Title in UWF! But right now we are on a mini-vacation and there is a threat. What threatens you is you! You inner self! [Joshua rolls his eyes.] Joshua: Not this again. [Wesley puts his arm around his half-brother’s shoulders.] Wesley: It’s the same problem you always have, bud! Lady troubles! And it’s eatin’ you alive! [Joshua sinks down and starts to pout.] Joshua: I’m fine. Leave me alone. Wesley: Fine?! You’re mopin’ around like a kid whose bike, dog and favorite girl all got run over on the same mornin’! Joshua: I’m taking my next vacation alone. [Wesley back off and points at Joshua.] Wesley: Who do you think you’re kiddin’? You’ve been messed up ever since Meg Connelly let you that note sayin’ she was dumpin’ you.You never got to ask why, an’ that bugs people like us—bad! See, we like things resolved. If you see a bad guy get taken by the authorities, you know you gotta get check the jail to make sure he made it there. What I’m sayin’, Joshua… [Wesley stands up in the boat and shoots his arms out to the side of the boat.] Wesley: …is that you gotta settle thigns with Connelly! Speak your piece! Say your mind and take a shot at getting her back! Joshua: How? She bolted without giving me the chance! [Joshua stands up now.] Wesley: She might say that you had a thousand chances to tell her that you’re a pro-wrestler. Joshua: But—! Wesley: But you lied, Joshua. You hid an essential part of yourself from her. Heck, you didn’t even tell her about your facination with truth and justice. For all she knew, you were a stockbroker! For all she knows, you have a wife and kid somewhere in a trailer like most wrestlers have! Joshua: She… doesn’t trust me? [The line on Wesley’s rod tugs. A fish has taken his bait! Wesley starts to struggle with the fish.] Wesley: Probably the only person in the whole country who feels she can’t trust Joshua Justice. Ain’t that a kick? [Joshua sits down and puts his head down in his hands.] Joshua: Kick in the groin is what it is. Wesley: Not that you asked for any advice… [Wesley pulls up on this fishing pole and the large fish splashes out of the water and back into it.] Joshua: That’s never stopped you before. Wesley: …find her. Use your use of the UWF jet and you can be on her doorstep in an instant. Joshua: Why bother? She doesn’t want me. She couldn’t have been clearer. Wesley: Some people carry their shields so they don’t get hurt. Other wrap ‘em around their heart. Think about it and… WOAH! [Wesley pulls up on the fishing pole and the large fish splashes into the boat. He picks it up and turns towards Joshua with a grin on his face. Joshua is already almost on land, still swimming.] Wesley: I didn’t mean you hadda go now! Monday! When we get back! [In a matter of seconds, the UWF jet sours by making waves in the lake. Wesley holds onto his hat.] Wesley: Geez, I wanted to tell him how Leslie Barda fits into all this first! He’s lost without my guidance!
