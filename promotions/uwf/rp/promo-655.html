UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #655

The Flag, the Nation, The Power

29 Aug 2004

The Communist — The JCR Temple

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist holds the Peoples Flag, the flag of the USSR in his hands, The bright red reflected in his eyes. The Scythe and Hammer burned into his brain. The representation of all he believes in and loves he holds in his hand. He holds it up as the Soviette walks up to him: Soviette: You put on quite a show Sunday. The committee was impressed. A few more matches like that and we'll have their full support again. Commie: You know how much their 'Support' means to me. And I lost. The THC Title slipped through my fingers and I failed. The Jewdio-Communist Revolution has the ability to level this federation, this power is yet untapped. We shall make a show of our power Sunday on Supremacy. We must. We will strike fear into the hearts of our enemies and devastate their confidence in themselves. Soviette: The Committee isn't very concerned about the THC title. They're impressed you formed a powerful stable and set up a devastating ambush for your opponents. However, they're concerend about the theme of the stable. Why Jewdaism? Why not just Communism? Commie: Where was the Committee when I was in jail? Where were they when you were kidnapped? Where? I heard nothing from them when you were gone. No support, no information. I fought with only the help of the Jewish people and the JCR. I fought without them, the only reason I even put it in the name is because the NVA insisted. Otherwise, I could have done without it. :The Rabbi walks up to the Communist and takes the flag out of his hand: Rabbi: Thank you Commie, we'll get this right back to you. :The Rabbi walks away with the flag: Soviette: What is he doing?! Why is he taking the Soviet Flag? That flew over the Kremlin before comming here! Commie: Calm down Soviette. I represent more than Communism now. I am these people's Champion as well. I am the Champion of the PEOPLE. All the People. This Jewish community has accepted me, and I intend to embrace that. I asked a small favor from them regarding the flag, that is all. Soviette: A Small favor? What are you talking about? Commie: Just a small favor. It's nothing to concern yourself over. Soviette: Commie, the Committee will NOT be happy about this. Not at all. You need to watch who's toes you step on. You are treading on very dangerous ground. Commie: I know what I am doing. Soviette: No, you don't. :The Rabbi returns with the flag. Right beside the Scythe and Hammer is the Star of David: Commi: YES! This is what I represent! Communist and Jewdaism! Thank you, Comrad Rabbi. Rabbi: My pleasure. :The Soviette faits dead away:
