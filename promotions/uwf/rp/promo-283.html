UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #283

Difference

12 Mar 2004

Nick Persia — In a room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Screen fades in as we take a look in a big room where a lot of people are talking about a lot of things. Seemingly nobody recognises Nick Persia, and therefore they aren’t realising that they should be honoured to be in his presence. Nik feels like he has to do something about it and draws the attention to stand on the podium. Nobody in the room dares to say anything to The Invader, because they had seemingly seen UWF Sunday Afternoon Supremacy.] Nick Persia: Well, well..... I made my debut. I beated two opponents single handed. I made a statement regarding not only my future but the future of UWF in general. You see, I did my very best to win. And I did. But that’s history. I need to focus all my attention to Chief Bakuf Oninandwa. The advantage of being unknown is now gone. I am not underestimated any more. People know now that I am not a force to be messed with. I showed my dominance to the world. Now, I have to prove that I am here to stay! I am here to make the difference. [Nick Persia is clearly still for a while, he tries to form the words out of his thoughts. The people in the audience stare at him] The difference..yeah...what is difference I hear people wondering in the audience. I will explain what difference here. If I want to be better than my opponent I need not only to kick his ass by physical dominance, but I must be the master of the mind games too. Now that’s the in-ring psychology part which I really didn’t have the chance to come up with yet. Usually I drive my opponent to madness, hoping they are fools and bite the ace. But to my suprise both of my opponents didn’t bite. They did their own game. It wasn’t good enough though, but they showed their faith in themselves. I have to tell you. I was shocked. I was damn suprised. But when I thought about it, I put it in a whole other daylight. If you don’t believe in yourself, who does believe in you then? Indeed, nobody. My point....they are not afraid of my yet. They don’t know yet why my name is The Invader. I proved just a point. My own streak has started...1-0 ort should I say 2-0? It doesn’t matter, all that matter is that I bring down the house. I will not only bring down the house but Chief as well. I never saw him wrestle before which makes it extremely difficult to make a good estimation of his wrestling capabilities. I know he can wrestle, otherwise you won’t be signed by UWF. But how smart is he? Is he like a plumber of is he more like a professor at a University? No offense off course, but there is a difference that cannot be denied by anyone. Now I was driving back from Germany the other day and what did you think? I came up with something great. I came up with a strategy to wrestlewith that nobody has ever witnessed in the history of pro-wrestling. I introduced it on SAS last week, now I will make it a succes. I will capture all titles. And there is nothing you can do about it. Resistance is futile. Do not delay the inevitable. You can’t fight what you can’t stop. What I am brining to the table, you are simply not designed to handle. The pinfall is what you want, bloodbath and massacre is what you will get. I am giving you one chance to lay down for me or else I will kick your ass so badly you’ll look worse than an 100-year old grandpa wrestling in a wheelchair! Did I make myself clear? I hope so, because I don’t want to see you the same mistake as the other two last week. I am too cool to be a fool. I am too sweet for defeat. I am true elite. What are you? A no good lying son of a bitch? I bet you told your momma you would win and kick my ass. That the UWF was scripted? It was not. Everything I do is unscripted. What I do is what I want. My will is LAW to you. If you have a problem with that stand up to me and tell me face to face so I can kick that cowboy ass of yours. What kind of man are you? A man of honour? A man who takes pride in his work? What in the hell are you? I could only wish I knew. But you are so pathetetic nobody cares about you. Wether you win or....wait a minute.....did you ever win? I didn’t see you for like weeks on television. And the last time I saw you you lost BIG TIME. My question to you is do you have the skills to pay the bills? You will answer that question this Sunday. Quite possibly I am in the best shape of my life. I've been thinking like an inventor, Calculating like a politician, Taking bumps like a rubber ball And working out like a demon! I'm ready, DADDY! Ready, willing and able! I'm in the greatest shape of my life! I've never been so well conditioned as I am now. I just took out four guys and still have a lot left, so three opponents is definitly posible! I know that Chief is the bigger man of us. He has a bigger name than I have. He has more fans than I have. But there is a saying that says The bigger they are the harder they fall. It’s true and I am going to prove it to your guys not only this sunday but each and every sunday. I am a constant factor, not o be triedfull with. I am a major league playor who is used to taking things to the extreme. I always like things pushing over the edge and extend some limits. But somehow I never felt at any time in my career that there is a limit to my abilities. I still don’t believe it. I am still conquering and battling on all fronts with one goal in my mind and that is regaining my pretty much faded away honour. I am a star whose shine faded away for a long time but now my star shines bigger than ever. I feel the adrenaline flowing through my veigns again i feel like I can take on the world...alone. [The audience starts to clap and thus creating a great atmosphere in the room] Yes, I am on a rampage. It’s the best thing going now. I am heree to pump the audience. I am here to gain some much needed attention. I am bringing the spotlight to the UWF. You see, the UWF committee did not make a mistake when the signed their biggest star ever. I am the man that will give UWF what it needs. I put on decent fights. You see my victories are not a fluke. It might have been a little bit tainted but I won. All that amtters are results. Some guys say the only thing what counts i entertaining the fans....yeah right....tell that crap to you family but not to us. I should be admired for my courage to battle in place like this. Some people wish they’d go to heaven. Well, hell is heaven compared to UWF. The matches are all pretty stiff to speak. There are no mats just a concrete floor and when we are coughing up blood it’s ours not from a pig. There is no point or whatsoever in telling how good you are. I am good, byt my actions speak for itself. And I am only repeating it for the blind people. You see, I am a man who helps. I have discovered my second me. I saw that I was the greatest Dutch wrestler ever, but Iin Holland there is no real competition nor a real fan base for wrestling so I decided to go the the great USA. Here I travelled a lot, mainly working two shows a night for 20 dollars each. It wasn’t enough to cover our outlay. So we’d do some work in a bar too, consisting of checking people if they purchased a ticket. After a month I left, in the illusion I wouldn’t get a decent job offer here. I went to Japan were I worked a few shows, but the culture was not what I liked. In fact I couldn’t stand all those people talking in a language I coulnd’t understand. I was glad I left there. I worked a year or so in Germany ( of all places) and then I finally went back to USA. Here I could work almost immediately in the UWF. I won my first match, and I am planning on doing the same with my second match. Europeans are known of their warfare. I will show my own warfare this very sunday, to show the true meanign what an invasion is really all about. I am the Dutch Destroyer and I will show how effective I really am. The Silent Assasin will strike again. The Invader moves on. You cocksucking-@sslicking-bitcheating-whoreraised-whitethrash-excuse for a failed abortion-dogfartsniffing-horsecockstroking-piece of shit. You will get your ass wipped and be named as my third victim. You’ll get some permanent marks for free. Be glad I won’t charge you extra. I am mad as hell and I will unleash all my fury on you. Mark my words. There ain’t no half stepping. I am going to shine luminous. Everything else is simply ludricous. [ The audience looks like they had seen Julius Ceaser. Veni, vidi, vici. They are pretty impressed by the performance put up by the fabulous Dutchman]
