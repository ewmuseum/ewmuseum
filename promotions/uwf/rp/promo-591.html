UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #591

A High Stakes Dance

1 Aug 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — Studio Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in the middle of an big empty room. It’s a studio apartment overlooking the skyline of a city at night. Standing there in a orange leisure suit is Fizbane the Fabulous, back turned toward the camera. He is staring out into the skyline. “Gypsies, Tramps, and Thieves” is playing in the back ground.) Fizzy: It’s a party night out there. It’s a party town out there, and the party is just about to get started. That’s all that there is to no. Have I been on a little bit of a down swing? Well, yeah, but it don’t matter, it’s all good. After all, sometimes the party dies down before it goes back to full swing again…… The Fizzy express is running full speed. I’ve got some chickadees and I’ve got some super freaks. I’ve got cool cats and hipsters all over the place, and life couldn’t ever get any better. This is definitely not your local gypsies tramps or thieves here that we are talking about. We are talking about the most out of sight people ever to grace the world of professional wrestling, and we are going to continue to party because that is what life is all about…… And that party will continue on Sunday when I fight Moraless. The mans name is interesting, but even a party animal like myself has his morals. Even a man like myself has to know what is right or wrong….. This man here however, he is really hardcore. He is really on top of everything and is really going to make things happen. This is the former PTC Champion that we are talking about here. This is the former Tag Team champion that we are talking about here. And they put me up against him…… There is going to be a bigger party tonight than I have ever found. All my sweethearts and hard bodies out there are going to be there and we are going to dance and scream and have one hell of a good ol’ time ALL…… NIGHT…… LONG!! I hope Moraless is ready for the party, cause it’s gonna be fun. I’m going to have my beautiful chickadees on my arm. Venus will be there. Aphrodite will be there. Athena will be there. And Freya will be there. And we are going to dance our way into the match tonight, and I’m going to dance my way out of the match tonight, and we are going to dance during the entire match tonight, because that is what we do. And we are going to have a hell of a good time, because that is what this life is all about. Let’s get this party started chickadees. (The four ladies of the Fizzy Express come walking out in different colored dresses and all gather around Fizzy as the scene fades out)
