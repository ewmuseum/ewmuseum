Ruff Ryding Your Mother

Challenge! As loverboy cleans up after a RUFF RIDE he dresses to go to go to his limo. A reporter catches him again for the final updates tonight they say there saving the best for last or at least the most tired person in the UWF after you know. They ask who has Loverboy had the most problems with since he has gotten in the UWF he says no one until a family member of someone didn't payup. He mentions the name. LOVERBOY: Ruff Ryder. All you do is cry and complain you are the best at what ever the hell you do. But the only thing that is being ruff rid is your mom last night anyways after i get that taste out ill get prepared to challenge you anytime. I guess since i haven't been to any feds in awhile and i have not made no money i guess whooping your ass will pay for your mother. Now is there any kind of match that you dream that you could take me in well if the FED would let us go at it I would like to have a match at sunday night supremacy maybe then i could prove that there is no one in this fed better than me. After stepping out into his giant limo and driving away he suddenly stops and rolls down the window the reporters Say: Loverboy says remember Im the best and ill be back here sunday night sumpremacy if RUFF RYDER thinks he can get over the depressing mood of me being his knew daddy. I guess now that im back wrestling all ladies will be out for me that is the reason im called LOVERBOY. Finally he rolls away beating to the bass in the limo! Droping the money that he was paid by ruff ryders mom!
