UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #822

Going Dutch, German & Crazy!

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — UWF Studios

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick Persia is walking in the UWF Head Office. As he walks through the long corridors he’s been greeted by people whom know him. He quickens up his pace, because he needs to be in time for a special UWF Superstar Talk~! Of course, Nick Persia is a bit on the late side, as he always is. He swings a big door open, and he sees a lot of people staring at him. Nick Persia: “I hope I’m on time.” The people look at him like he has done something terribly wrong. The director starts to speak with a not so well hidden tone of disgust: “I think you’ve taken the wrong turn. We’re filming a movie here and you’re interrupting us.” Nick Persia looks at the people in attendance, and waves them goodbye. He quickly turns his back to them and walks out of the door. He opens another door. This time, he’s in the right place, only at the wrong time. A fat ass Thommy look-a-like (or not): “Where have you been? I’ve been waiting on you for over an hour. Luckily I was prepared for this with bringing some Shiatsu pictures with me, but if I didn’t have them with me I would be even more mad, I mean, angry then I am now.” Nick Persia: “Oh right, sorry. I understand where you’re coming from. You’ve got a really though life. No computer access at home, you forgot to pay the bill for Playboy and you’re waiting one hour on me. It must have been an horrible hour, watching at a nude Shiatsu. I feel your pain.” Thommy: “Well, let’s not waste anymore time and let’s get this thing going.” He points at a chair right before the camera. “You can sit there.” Nick Persia: “Yeah, sure, no problem.” Nick walks to the chair and takes his place. The chair is far from uncomfortable, but we must deal with the facts here: it’s UWF, what else can you expect? We’re too lazy to even make up our own matches. That’s why we called this FANdemonium and we say we let the fans decide. Sure, they’ll decide the matches and do all of our work, so we don’t have to come up with superb concepts like a tulip picking contest. Unfortunately, that was the runner up to another great stipulation: The Flaming Monkey Match! For Nick Persia, it did not matter. He would never get used to those silly Americans. Thommy: “Can we start now?” Nick Persia: “I’m ready if you are.” Thommy: “No stupid comments here, mate! Anyway, let me tell you what we are going to do. I’m going to ask you a few questions, and you’ll answer them. Any questions?” Nick Persia: “No.” Thommy gives a few command to the other employees and they immediately take their positions on the set. Thommy: “Welcome for having you here Nick Persia at the newest edition of UWF Superstar Talks. For the past few weeks we haven’t seen the best of you. You lost to the likes of Killer Dude and Wes Diable. How do you feel about that?” Nick Persia: “How do you think I feel? Of course I have been cursing myself time after time. In the past I proved myself to be one of the top guys here in the UWF. I am the one who has beaten legends time after time. My THC Title reign is still the longest reign in the history of the UWF! I’ve been here for quite some time now. I’m now considered as one of the established stars so to speak. Finally, I must confess. At this very time I am losing a few matches to a new guy and Killer Dude! I mean, is this some kind of sick joke pulled by the Devil? But as much as I feel ashamed for this, I need to go on. That’s exactly what I will do. At FANdemonium I will show the UWF and the fans why they call me ‘The Dutch Destroyer’! They’ll see why I am the current UWF Intercontinental champion. You bet they will. You’ll see me going at it. It doesn’t matter against who, but I’ll be there. I’m sure my opponents will bring their all too.” Thommy: “It’s always nice to know that you are going Dutch at UWF’s FANdemonium!” As always, Thommy is in for a lame joke. It isn’t funny, let alone this was the right time for such a joke. Ah well, we can’t all have such an accurate feeling for timing as Nick has. Nick Persia: “In some ways, you could say I am going Dutch. Because I’m bringing the half of the match with me, and my opponent is bring the other half with him. So yeah, in some way we’re really going Dutch. Wes Diable, do you think you’re ready to dance with the Dutch devil? Because I am IN for a great party at FANdemonium. Let’s dance Wes Diable, like you have never done before. Let’s Dance, Wes Diable with flaming monkeys surrounding us. Let’s dance, Wes Diable and show the world the meaning of going Dutch! Let’s do exactly that!” With a sinister look on his face Nick laughs diabolically. Thommy: “Erm, right. You’ve just mentioned your opponent for your Intercontinental Title Match at FANdemonium, ‘The Savior’ Wes Diable. What do you think of this up and coming threat? He’s been making a name for himself as of late, even at your expense.” Nick Persia: “You called it straight down the middle here! He made a name on MY expense. I mean, I feel this guy can become the best of the best if he wants! Just realise, that his will happen over my dead body. As long as I am breathing I will be the top dog. I am the one who has a perfect pay per view record. When the pressure level reaches its top I am dealing the best with it. I never fail when it truly comes down to it. I’ve never lost the final chapter in any rivalry I’ve had. Yes, I might have lost a few battles in the war, but the grand victory was always mine. Always! Wes Diable, you must realise something. FANdemonium isn’t just any event. Flaming Monkeys Match isn’t just any match. Nick Persia isn’t just any opponent. Wes Diable, if you truly are the savoir make damn sure you can save yourself. Because Nick Persia in a Flaming Monkey Match at FANdemonium is a VERY dangerous combination. Do you KNOW what I’ve done to Reo Dai? Do you know what I do to people? Ask that no good Adien Pryde. No one, and I mean no one, can oppose my wrestling skills. Not even once I’ve been outwrestled, or outsmarted. I violate my opponents in every sense of the word. There is no such thing as mercy in my dictionary. You have to realise that I am Nick Persia. I’m the Dutch destroyer for a reason! Did you actually think that it was a fancy nickname with no meaning or history attached to it? Did you really think that? I injured people for less than what you did to me. The ring is my playground. Flaming Monkeys are my toys. You, yes you, you are my very opponent. Are you good enough to be in my playground? We’ll find out soon.” Nick Persia gets that diabolical smile back on his face. He licks his lips with his tongue. Nick Persia: “Am I thirsty! I’ve got a thirst for blood. I want to see it flowing. I want to see you bleeding. I want to see you lying in a pool of blood of your own. You have been getting away from your punishment for far too long. You can’t escape your destiny. No one can. The only nice thing about FANdemonium will be that I will be dealing with you. I will be the last face you see. Remember this face, Wes Diable, for this will be the last thing you will ever see! Know this, come FANdemonium, there will be no salvation. Because I am going DUTCH! There’s nothing you can do about it. You didn’t actually think I was at my best at our last encounter? No, I was ill. But now I truly am at the top of my game. I feel like slapping a bitch in the face. I feel like slapping Wes Diable in the face. You arrogant punk! I’m Nick Persia. I’m the stable mate of Tsunami Oni. He’s the Vivaldi of Violence. Come to think of it, you may call me the Van Gogh of Violence!” Nick Persia is grabbing a knife out of his pocket and is playing with it around his left ear. Nick Persia: “Shall I cut it off? So I can prove I truly listened to you! You’re a priest or something? And a warrior, of course. Is it okay if we call you warrior-Priest? Is that okay with the pope too? I don’t want to make any enemies that live in God’s house. My god, can you imagine how boring that would be? I would be devoting my whole life to a God that doesn’t even exist. I would like to see your face when you realise it is all been a joke in the end. All of these prayers you’ve spoken to the wind. All of this time you could have spent on training, but instead wasted in the church. It’s a same, you know. But to all things must come an end. Your wrestling career must come to an end. You will get killed in that match. Then you’ll see your God. Your God is dead. You and your god are dead! In the end, you will be nothing more than a bad memory. There is a mention of you on a dark page in the history of the UWF. The rest of your worthless existence will fade into the nothingness. There is no God. There is no life after the death. There is only power and I am power.” Nick throws the knife towards the camera. The cameraman ducks away, barely in time to avoid the knife. With his whole body shaking of fear he continues to film. Sorry for the inconvenience, but all people behind a UWF camera are cowards. Nick Persia: “I am aiming for you. I’m out on a blood-quest. Do not see this as a joke, please. For you begin to make terrible mistakes, I can become very sadistically. You look to talk about death so much, don’t you? I don’t like talking about death. Sure, I would laugh if I killed you. Still, there is one thing I like more in this world than seeing you death. I want to see you suffer. I want to see you suffer from the damage I have inflicted on you. You will be facing my darker side. You will be facing my DARK~! Side. Yes, Wes Diable. You will be the first. You’ve crossed the line. At FANdemonium, I’m no Dutch Destoyer anymore! The Dutch Destroyer knows words as mercy, mistakes and peace. But I’m no Dutch destroyer.” Suddenly, the lights dim in the studio. It gets darker, and a glow of light can be seen on Nick’s face. His eyes have turned red, with the blood running out of his eyes over his face. Out of nowhere, a wind gazes through the studio, lifting Nick’s hair up in the sky. Nick Persia: “I………AM………THE……..GER-MAN……DESTROYER~!” Nick Persia is blinking with his eyes for a small moment. Thommy: “What does that mean?” Nick Persia: “Ten points for Thommy! Nobody has any clue how violent I can get now. Instead of a Dutch Destroyer you will see my German Destroyer. Instead of a nice, friendly guy who likes to wrestle you will now see a masochist. I like to see people suffer. It’s in my blood. I’m a child of the Devil. I am born in hell. I am born in Germany. The most cursed place on this whole God green earth. I could have been born anywhere, but of all places I was born in Hannover, Germany. Even growing up in a place of God couldn’t help me. I will always be a child of the Devil. Thommy, do you know what the children of the Devil do?” Thommy (stuttering): “Nnnnnnoooooo” Nick Persia: “No? Don’t worry about it! You’ll find out at first hand what the children of the Devil do! They listen to the demon inside of them. The everlasting voice that keeps on screaming it wants to be fed with blood, like a vampire. Inside me, a pure animalistic beast rages on with fury. It can not be tempted. I shall drop a few hints right here and now so a few people might decide to buy FANdemonium. When I finally have dealth with Wes Diable I will place my picture in his eyes. He’ll remember my face forever! I will staple his cock at his ass! I will let Flaming Monkeys rape him!” Thommy: “Why won’t they pick you? You’re said to be gay after all! Man love and all of that!” Nick Persia: “What? Who said that? That is not true! Anyway, I will keep those monkeys away from me by using Wes Diable’s holy water and my own Chiquita bananas. How does that sound? I think it sounds very good. Smart too, by the way!” Thommy: “So you are going Dutch and German on poor Wes Diable’s ass?” Nick Persia: “Not only I will be going at it. Those Flaming Monkeys will be going at his ass like no other. I’ve watched a few tapes and when they were brought in……WOW! They’re sicko’s. I don’t know which bastard thought this up, but I place my bets on Al Crud. I’ve always know these XUW guys are sore winners! Even when they got lucky, they think they can sabotage our votes by voting for the FANdemonium event.” Thommy: “Maybe they were fans of you…” Nick Persia: “Good thinking there, Thommy Knocker. Next time, keep your tongue behind your teeth before I cut it out with the knife that is laying on the floor! You will get an infection too then! That’s called two-in-one. Just like my game plan for FANdemonium, because that is two-in-one as well. Two matches in one night. Two sides of me in one night. Two victories in one night.” Thommy: “Unless I am mistaken, you will have made nine victims in one night then. So that’s nine-in-one then!” Nick Persia: “I already said two victories in one night. So that’s two-in-one.” Thommy: “But I was talking about victims.” Nick Persia: “And I was talking about ripping that tongue out of your mouth. Beat it. I am right, and you know it. So shut up, and act like you are smart for once. You are starting to look like that punk called Wes Diable you know. I bet you will be saying your prayers in a few minutes too, after I turned up the heat. I am giving you a last warning, I am always right.” A deadly silent falls into the studio. Nick Persia: “What are you waiting for? Continue the damn interview!” Thommy: “You just stated you are also in two matches. One match we have just discussed. The other match is a Venation Home Rumble. You’re set to face a hell of a lot of teammates, but they are randomly thrown together tag teams. So I suggest you give us your opinion on each person who is in the rumble individually!” Nick Persia: “Knock yourself out.” Thommy: “Let’s start with your tag team partner: Killer Dude.” Nick Persia: “Two people in one team! Two-in-one.” Thommy: “What the f*ck do you mean with that?” Nick Persia: “I was right, you know.” Thommy: “Yeah, sure. Just answer the damn question.” Nick Persia: “Killer Dude is somebody I’ve met several times in the ring. I think he’s weed. Because when you erase him, he always comes back! There is nothing you can do about it. It always returns. He’s the lowest of the lowest scum. He’s a punk and he knows it. Look, if he is trying to be a though guy and all of that, you must at least LOOK like one. If I didn’t know any better I’d say he’s an escaped Flaming Monkey when I would see him! I have no choice but to work with him. I’m giving it a go. I’ve always wanted to get the tag team titles. But one joke, and he’s dead. As dead as Wes Diable and as dead as God! Point one finger at me, Killer Dude, and you are history!” Thommy: “You’re starting to repeat yourself here!” Nick Persia: “One, that’s no question. Two, little children must hear everything twice, because they can’t follow sound and movements at the same time. So to make sure they can understand everything, I say things twice.” Thommy: “But I’m no child, I can understand this.” Nick Persia: “Because I said it twice.” Thommy: “But how else should I know you are repeating?” Nick Persia: “You should only ask question. That’s the second time I am saying it. I’ve proven my point.” Thommy takes a look at Nick Persia with a very disturbed expression on his face. Thommy: “Anyway, what’s your opinion on Joshua Justice?” Nick Persia: “He’s been doing nice so far. But he hasn’t had any real opponents. Let’s be honest here: he’s even worse than the Flaming Monkeys. If his legs weren’t attached to his ass he would forget them as well. He’s dumb, fat and an American. He’s no opponent at all. He won’t pose a threat to me, but to the ring. I think it will crash under his weight. But beside that, he’s being a fat ass, such as yourself Thommy!” Thommy suddenly gets a red face. Thommy: “How about his teammate, The Communist?” Nick Persia: “I’m German and he’s Jewish. You figure the math. Since it was in the news Neo-Nazi’s are holding conventions outside of Germany now to avoid getting caught, who knows what can happen in the Home Rumble! Not that I am threatening something, nor am I being against Jews here. It are the cold facts, unfortunately for them. Besides, anyone remember the cold war? Two wars in one match: two in-one!” Thommy: “Fizbane the Fabulous.” Nick Persia: “Next.” Thommy: “That’s no real comment!” Nick Persia: “Well, do you think you’ll actually see him in the match? If it’s the same cameraman I’m afraid Fizbane is eliminated before our cameraman has even turned on the power. Fizbane will be down and out by the time you start filming. But don’t worry, there will be plenty of other things to film!” Thommy: “MY, I mean THE FINAL FANTASY….Seymour Almasy.” Nick Persia: “I think I will outscore Almasy again in our record together. Because his teammate is absolutely worthless. Besides, Almasy has so many opponents in this match, it isn’t even funny anymore! Tsunami Oni, Killer Dude, Jamie Shock, Mike Montgomery and myself all hate him. I despise him. He’s no better than me, although he’s the PTC Global Champion. We both reached the GTT 4 Semi Bracket Finals. I had to pull myself out of the tournament, due to a conflicting travel schedule, but he just plain lost to a Goat! What a loser. I can’t see myself losing to him again.” Thommy: “I don’t feel like going down the list. Just say whatever you want to say about the rest!” Nick Persia: “Rest you say, huh? Be the best, beat the rest! How does that sounds for a slogan? I think it fits the bill! I know there are quite a few top guys in this match! I know I have to look out for sneak attacks from behind! I know I will be surrounded with enemies! But I also know I will come out on top. And no one is going to stop me! All of those people have set a dozen other priorities. Everybody has two matches on that night, and most teams look like shit. But you never know, maybe Mike Montgomery and Clyde will work together. Maybe Jamie Shock will make his presence felt by randomly raping fans! You never know. But in the end, at the grand final, when all things com to an end it will be me standing the tall, with my arms raised in the sky carrying TWO titles. I will walk out the UWF Intercontinental Champion and the new World Tag Team Champion!” Thommy: “Technically speaking, mustn’t that victory count as a half victory?” Nick Persia: “Technically speaking, shut up! FANdemonium was made for me. Hell, the fans WANT me as their champion. I can recognise demon in the title, so I can go German. Of course, I will be going Dutch too, like always. So the party will be complete when I’m coming to the ring this Sunday. Who will be up for the task? Who of all those men will be strong enough to oppose my threat after such gruesome matches they all had. While I am drilling them through the ring, one by one! I don’t care if they are legends or punks, as long as I’m standing in the ring I will always seek to win. I was born a winner, and I will die as a winner. But I will not die at FANdemonium. No, not at all! You want to know why?” Thommy: “Yeah, I do!” Nick Persia: “It isn’t because of those special stipulations. It isn’t because the fans made these matches. It is because I want to win this. I will do anything within my powers to win. After the misery I’ve been through I am not going to give up. Not now. Not now the titles are up for grasps. I’m Nick Persia. I’m both the Dutch Destroyer as the German Destroyer. You will see both of my side. Towards my stable mates I will be friendly, but towards Wes Diable I will be less friendly. I’m the Van Gogh of Violence! All the things I’ve said tonight, they are all true. With all my heart, or a lack of, I am going to beat the hell out of those sons of bitches. They want to see me going at it? They will see me going at it!” Nick Persia points in the air. Nick Persia: “Mark my words, for they are the gospel! Nick Persia has always been a man of his words. I swear that I will win, with anything I have in me. The key to my victory will be a combination of my brains and my body. Like all the previous things it will be two-in-one, once more! Let me make things clear a final time. I’m not fighting this war alone. No, it will be me and the fans! That’s two spirits in one body! Let me say it one more time:…” Thommy: “Two-in-one!” Nick Persia: “I see even you have picked my message.” Thommy: “Pretty good, huh?” Nick Persia: “For a mentally disabled person, yeah.” The scene fades out to the commercials, promoting the FANdemonium Pay Per View event.
