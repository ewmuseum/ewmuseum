UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #369

Shocked?

25th April (year unrecorded)

Jamie Shock — Unknown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jamie Shock. The Hall Of Famer. The Grand Slam Champion. The most hated man in UWF history. Jamie Shock, the man who ends careers. Jamie Shock ... the man who lost to Mike Montgomery. ... The man who lost to Mike ... Montgomery. Mike Montgomery. The very name has been circling 'round Jamie's head. Jamie cant loose to him! Surely, this is Jamie Shock isnt it. This is the man who defeated Tsunami Oni for the World Title isnt it? Jamie immediatly took time off. He had lost 'it'. It was 'it' which made him Hall Of Fame material. The 'it' material was the thing that made him rise above all the others.. he needed the 'it' for his ego, and he lost ... 'it'. He couldnt loose to Mike Montgomery. Who is he afterall? ... Now, Jamie Shock isnt the same. After declining for several months, after letting his body get in worse and worse shape, Jamie quit. He had to. He was turning white with hate. The buisness he once loved was ruining his life. It was making him bitter and twisted.. and the crowbar.. OH how he is obsessed with that bent tool. ... Now, he's changed. He's no longer going to rely on a crowbar to win matches, because he simply wont need to. He's past being a Nemesis in his family. For once in a long time he cares about his legacy, and wants to end on a high.. he doesnt want to be remembered for something he recently was. Shock will return to right the wrongs, and finish the rivalries that have span his career. ... T-Minus 5 weeks.
