2 Mar 2004

Anarchy — Somewhere in Mid West Amaerica

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to a view from the backseat of a SUV. There is a man in both front seats. The man in the driver's seat has shoulder length red hair and is wearing a black baseball hat backward. The man in the passenger seat has military cut black hair, he turns his head and you can now see that it is Adien Pryde.] Pryde: Turn here. Dai: Left or right? Pryde: Right. Well Prime Alliance, you beat us, But it took two finishers to do it was it a lucky win? Maybe, But you did do it so some congratulations is due. [Dai turns left throwing the camera man over to the right side of the vehicle then being shoved hard to the left.] Joanna: Watch it loser, get over to your side. Cameraman: Sorry Joanna. Pryde: We still haven't changed our goal. We will come to get the titles and control the tag-team division. Joanna: There it is. Right over there. [As an arm extends in front of the camera as it points to the left.] Dai: Thanks Joanna. We lost or first match against to UWF established stars. So what. One match, our first match here. All we care about is the end result. That's the reason we are entering the all comers tag match this Sunday. There we plan to prove our team superiority. [You can see out the windshield a mobile home park in what looks to be mid west america.] Joanna: Stop up here. [Dai stops the vehicle. Pryde, Dai, and Joanna get out leaving the cameraman in it by himself.] Pryde: Damnit, you staying in there or getting out to follow us? [The camera goes black. When it comes back on the three figures are walking up the street between two rows of trailer houses.] Dai: This is where I came from. Well not here, but a trailer park just like this one. Yes, my parents weren't rich but we got by. They made me what I am, and I see someone named Trailer Park Luchador running around giving people who live like this a bad name. It just pisses me off. [Dai stops for for a few minutes. He turns to face the camera, a very angry look on his face.] Dai: TPL, I plan on showing you that no one disgraces the people like these, especially when these people are like I was. Anytime, anywhere I will beat the respect these people deserve into you. Be a man. [The three turn a pass buy the cameraman and start walking toward the black SUV. When they get to it Pryde turns and looks right into the camera.] Pryde: Bondage, I see I get to face you Sunday, there you will find out what I do for a living, Wrestle! Not some porn show, not some sick fantasy world, a wrestling match. If people wanted to see what you show them, they would order it on PPV. Nick Persia, you don't know what you’re getting into, my suggestion, stay out of the way. Bondage, get ready for a trip to the hospital, because I plan to rip what you hold dear to you, and I will shove your balls down your throat. Joanna: And keep you whores out of the way or I will send them to the hospital with you. [With that, all three climb into the SUV. Dai starts it up and drives away.] Cameraman: Wait, you forgot me. Damnit, I should have realized this would happen. Where the hell am I? [Camera fades to black]
