UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #733

Live and Loud

date unknown

Nick Persia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in to a movie: Austin Powers: Gold member. Austin is doing his shagadelic kind of thing with his mojo as we can clearly hear him stating: “Oh, Dutch people are evil!”. The TV is turned off by someone who remains sitting in a dark corner.] “There is only so much one man can take. There is only so much a man can do. But now I am starting to believe I have a little problem. Five other man in one little dangerous match on one big pay per view. I mean, we’re talking about a triangle ladder match with three titles on the line at Live and Loud 4!” The man remained sitting in the dark corner although his voice became louder, even so loud you are tend to call it screaming. “I’m talking about five other men. I am talking about a certain Kenny Mercury. I am talking about a guy looking to beat me down with his partner in crime and run a way like a coward. But I know why he attacks me. He fears me! He fears me because he already knows what I can do. I can do so many bad things that a wise men would tell you to pack your stuff and leave. But you can hide and you can run but in the end there is no escape. Only justice.” Nick Persia walks out of the shadows, showing his face. “I’ve tolerated you since the very beginning. Guys like you are always hiding. Behind someone else or a fake injury…it doesn’t matter to me…..because I know that some day, I will beat you. To me it doesn’t matter in what state you are or in which condition but I will beat you. This day, Kenny has come for you. The end of all days. The day that will decide if you win or I win. You can say a lot about that but there is never ever going to be another Live and Loud Four!” Nick Persia smirks in a sinister way. “But you are not the only one! Killer Dude, you have been calling me a lot of things as of late and none of them were positive not true for the matter! But as badly as you are giving me nicknames is exactly as badly as I am going to beat you! No games, no tricks…just you and me. Let’s see what we can do when we give it a little go. I mean, you don’t mind if I hurt you? Just wanted to make sure you are cool with this, because I don’t like upsetting little children. They tend to become very annoying. So I guess you better stay out of my otherwise you will be in for a very rude awakening!” Nick Persia blinks with his eyes for a small moment. “You see, I am really worried about this match! I have watched tapes, studied every counter move you did and still I think something isn’t right. That is this is not a normal match. Nothing will be spared to get a title. We have three chances to get a title. Three small chances. That’s not very much since you know that each time someone grabs a title your chances to get a title diminish a lot!” Nick Persia nods with his head in disagreement. “But not me. I will get a title. Seymour, you can say all you want! But if I say I want the IC title then I’ll take the title about it! All you are going to do about it is watching. If you do decide to get in my way is only making me to give you something you don’t want to experience. That is my very own finisher. You know how deadly it is. You’ve felt it before. When I beat you against all the odds!” Nick Persia gets a diabolic laugh in his face and the sarcasm in his voice can be clearly heard. “As you said Seymour I am the threat in this one. Especially now I dealt with all of you no good son of a bitches. I mean, who now has the strength to oppose me? You, Seymour? Like you said, you need to keep your honour in PTC! Did you really think I had forgotten about that? Did you really think I wouldn’t take a certain advantage of that? Did you really think I wasn’t looking for revenge after our last encounter?” Nick Persia spits on the floor, expressing his anger. “Dream on! I am Nick Persia. Do I need to spell that name for you? I don’t think so. I am your worst nightmare. I am the one who delivered you an astonishing defeat just a few weeks ago! Sure, you are the THC Champion. Sure, you can say all the things in the world that you want to say but in the end there is only one thing that truly matters…….Results. Boy, did I show you some? I beat you. You beat me. The score is even, but I am going to make it 2-1. This will definitely not be our final encounter, but it will be one to remember, Seymour, it will be one to remember!” While he continues talking he takes a few steps. “This will be truly a night to remember! Nick Persia winning his second title. The question is if I shall become a hardcore king, perhaps the dominator of the intercontinental title or become the champion of all North Americans! Maybe I won’t become a champion at all by some miracle. But one thing is for sure, Moraless, and that is that you will get your ass handed to you! Now you can say a lot, no a damn lot but I will not let you win. Hell no!” A nasty diabolical smile appears on the face of the man from Holland. “Don’t think I am overlooking you, Misfit. Because in this match you really miss fit if you catch my drift! What are you doing in the likes of Seymour Almasy, Moraless and myself? The only thing I have been noticing as of late is that you are prone to injuries! And not on my level of course. But then again, who is? You, Killer Dude? Please don’t make me laugh. I need to watch my health!” Nick’s look on his face gets very serious. “I’ve been trough a hell of a lot more than you have been through. A triangle match means nothing to me! Nothing! I can deliver so much more pain! I can inflict so much damage that in the end you will beg for mercy! But before you will do that you will have endured my madness. You wanted this? Then this is what you will get! Live and Loud baby!” Nick starts to stare into the camera. “However some people do not realise what they have gotten themselves into. They talk about kicking ass, but they have no idea what they are talking about. No idea at all. I can say a damn lot about that but I will only say this. Killer Dude, watch your steps before you will regret that your father met your mother! That’s a promise. A damn promise. And it will happen. Because I said so.” Nick gets that nasty smile on his face again. “There is no way you will ever make it. There is no way you can become as great as I am. I am the longest THc Champion. Not you. I beat legends like Almasy and Plague. Not you. I competed with Hall of Famers. I’ve main evented. And what about you? You’ve talked about them. Wow! You’ve been in the same fed! Impressive. But just realise son that you are getting your ass kicked so badly! Man, o, man! There is no way you will win. Not one way. Not one.” Nick starts to walk away as he speaks his last words. “For the five of you I have just one message. Get out whilst you still can. I won’t give you any salvation. I can only offer you eternal suffering. I can give you damnation. But if you stand there in that ring at Live and Loud 4 I will promise you this. You are nothing more to me than an obstacle. By all means, I will remove it. So if your life is precious to you. Stay out of my way! Keep a safe distance.”
