UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #486

Training for Success-#2 SW

30 Jun 2004

Digital Mortality — A gym in Chicago.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We get even closer to the big night, to the night where stars are made and other crumble, to the night were everyone has the chance to be the winners, where everyone has the chance to survive, but as always, only the strong survive. Only the team that has the best cohesive unit will come out victorius, only a team that is not dysfunctional will be able to make it in this business. The only team that can really be called a team, that really believes in what they are doing, that really knows what they are getting themselves into is Resurrection. All other teams are dysfunctional individuals put together with no rhyme or reason, with no vision. Resurrection has a reason to be together, they have a vision of the UWF, they are not together because they need to surround themselves with other talent to be able to survive.] [You take a look at the Player's Club, and the philosophy for their creation is simple, Mike Montgomery knows he can't get anywhere by himself, so he starts recruiting people who are better than him, people with more talent, and people that are promising in the ring and fills them up with promises of glory and success, but in the end fails to deliver. He is supposed to be the leader and where is he? In his house sleeping with the belt around his waist while his minions do his dirty work. It's pathetic, it's pathetic because Montgomery is a mediocre wrestler. If it wasn't for Digital wanting to prove a point, Digital would now have that World Title belt around his shoulder. Who does the Player's Club have that Digital hasn't already beat? Ezra has fallen to Digital, Starr has also fallen to him, Dunn has fallen to him, and Mike has countless times. The only one that Digital hasn't met one on one is Nick Persia. Is that really all the challenge in this Tournament?] [Now, Chrono Cross is another story, they are a stable created for stable's sake. What does Seymour, Corporal and Mercury have in common? Nothing, they are not friends, they are not family, they don't even protect themselves. They are just a group of individuals who thought it would be fun to unite forces and give Stable Wars a shot. Newsflash, it's not gonna happen. Just because they are talented, doesn't mean they have a chance in hell of surviving. Sure they are great when they are fighting by themselves, but they are not team players, thus they will not succed. There is no way they can take this Stable Wars from the grasp of Digital Mortality and Resurrection. Even if they could pass to the second round, it will not be easy for them to defeat Resurrection, or even the Player's Club.] [It is going to be a strenuous night, it will not be a walk in the park, but if Resurrection has their minds on their game, there won't be anyone to stop them. There won't be anything anyone can do about it, because they are the future of the UWF, they are the ones that will keep the soul of the UWF going through these dark ages. They are the only glimmer of light at the end of the tunnel, and they will live up to their names and take this one and put it in the bag to finally put the stake on Montgomery and his Player's Club's heart.] (The scene opens in a gym in Chicago. Digital Mortality and Burton Bell, after going to the taping of the Side Show are gonna train for Stable Wars. Even though Burton is not a UWF wrestler, he is Digital's trainee. Mortality has been training him on the technical side of wrestling, he has been showing him how to make a man tap out in pain, scream in agony when his leg, or arm is pulled out of it's socket. No one has resisted the pain that the Digitalizer brings to the table, and he will teach the same thing to Burton. The gym is a traditional one, the wrestling/boxing ring is in the middle, the sandbags are on the far left, to the right are the weights, and to the right a blue thin mattress used for martial arts training. Everyone is doing their thing, and the place is nothing but full. In the ring, two amateurs to professional wrestling, or sports entertainment are doing their thing in the middle of the ring.) (Digital is wearing black sweat pants and a black shirt, his long red hair is tied in a pony tail, and he has black sunglasses that do not let you see his dark brown eyes. He is carrying a black duffel bag. Burton is wearing his usual blue sweat pants and a white shirt. He also has a duffel bag and a white towel on his shoulder. His black hair is only to the bottom of his nose. He is a little bit taller than Digital, but not as toned. They both sit down on a couple of chairs besides the ring and watch the amateurs in action. There is a blown spot and Digital let's out a small laugh. The two men obviously hear it and completely stop what they were doing. They look at each other and sniff loudly.) Man#1: And who do you think YOU are? Man#2: Yeah, we can kick your ass any day. (They obviously have no idea who they are talking too, and Digital laughes out louder than before. He takes off his shirt and Burton does so too. The two men step back talking to each other. Digital and Burton enters the ring, and Digi clears his throat. The two men angrily turn around towards these two people who have made fun of them and they wanted to take them down. As soon as they turn around, they gasp at they same time and stand there transfixed. Digital smiles.) DM: Well, are we gonna just stand here or are you gonna start kicking my ass now? (They are obviously deeply disturbed about angering one of the most succesful wrestlers in the Chicago area. They obviously backed down and started the apologies.) Man#2: Er... I'm sorry Mr. Digital, sir... Man#1: Yeah... we thought you were just some idiots making fun of us. (Digital's grin went from slight, to a full grin.) DM: I WAS making fun of you. (They both looked at each other and suddenly seemed very lost. They didn't know how to react.) DM: Tell you what. Why don't you leave the ring, watch me and Burt here train. Maybe you could learn not to blow so many spots. Man#2: Yes, sir! (They both climb out of the ring quickly. Burton supresses his laughter and begins to test the ropes.) DM: Okay Burt, let's do this one more time, and no John Wayne heroics this time. (They lock up. Burton grabs Digital's arm and twists it up. Digital quickly reverses and is now behind Burton with his arm locked on his back. Digital then grabs Burton in a side headlock. Burton proceeds to send him to the ropes, as he is coming back, Burton goes for a dropkick, but Digital had hold on to the ropes and Burton falls face down on the mat. Digital quickly goes over to the body of Burton and goes for a surfboard. After a 30 seconds of the hold, he releases him and hits Burton with a leg drop to the back of the neck. Digital picks Burton up and suplexes him, Digi goes for an elbow drop but Burton quickly moves out of the way. He gets up and waits for Digital to get up, as soon as he does so, he hits him with a nice suplex to the face. Digital falls back and ends in the turnbuckle. Burton goes over to him and gets on the second rope and starts to punsh Digital head. After six punches, Digital punches Burton in the face and pushes him to the mat. Digital quickly goes over to the body of Burton and applies a nice triangle hold by grabbing one arm and Burt's head and locking them up with his legs. After a while, he lets go and stands up, helps Burton up, only to throw him to the ropes. Burton ducks the clothesline, goes for a clothesline of his own and Digital ducks it, when Burton bounces back, he is srop toe holed face first into the mat and Digital grabs his leg and applies a modified STF known as the Lynchpin. After a while, Burton taps out.) (Digital gets up and grabs Burton's hand and pulls him up. One of the men from the outside starts to talk to Digital.) Man#1: What happened to the Digitalizer? DM: Nothing happened to it. It's still there and better than ever. Man#1: Does it hurt? DM: Want to try it out? (The guy gets into the ring and positions himself with his back turned to Digital. Digital grabs his arms and ties it around him in a croba clutch, he then suplexes the man and keeps the submission until the man starts to scream and tap out wildly. Digital releases the hold, gets up and helps the man up.) DM: Did it hurt? Man#1: Fuck yeah! DM: You still want to become a wrestler? Man#1: Hell yeah! It's been my dream since I was a kid. DM: Good. We need passion in the ring, purpose. I am afraid it has been lost in the UWF. People like Nick Persia running their mouths, Kevin Starr running his mouth also. Nick Persia thinks he is the last coke in the desert, because he has the THC Title... are you kidding me? What esle has he done? What else can he say that he has done to be able to talk down to me? I haven't seen the IC Title around his waist, or the Tag Titles, or the World title. He has nothing. The THC Title is a joke, it is a mockety of what it should be, just like the World title keeps losing it's prestige every single day Mike Montgomery holds it. Nick Persia is a nobody that will end up as a nobody, he is not Digital Mortality and will never be me. He says that I will fade into nothing, that no one will remember, but he has to understand, that there is a little thing that is called the Hall of Fame, and I am a member of that club for a select few. It comes from years of busting my ass trying to make the UWF a better place, only to see it destroyed by some fools who think they are better than anybody. I feel sick. It's easy to babble, it's easy to talk shit all the time, but if you can't back it up, then it doesn't matter. I have been giving it my all for this federation, three years I have been doing my hardest to rise the ranks, and now that I am on the top some green rookie think he can push me back down? He is sadly mistaken, I will be at the top, and there is nothing you can do about it. BB: Chill out man, when you win Stable Wars, you will have nothing else to look forward in the UWF. DM: True, maybe I can get myself a position backstage in the UWF. Just like Mike needs to do to finally win the World title. It's as easy as that ain't it? Just manipulate a couple of people who are more talented than myself and make then take me to the top, quite easy. People like Joshua Dunn and Ezra Kazee are much more talented than Mike Montgomery, hell, even Adien Pryde is more talented than him! He thinks it is his time, but it isn't, it was never his time. Never the time of Mike Montgomery. BB: What about Corporal Punishment? DM: Corporal is probably the biggest threat I have in this whole event. I remember back when I was just a rookie, when I was IC Champ for the first time, and I was booked against Corporal at Christmas Capers. He won, he beat me for the title. This is the man that I do not have a win over, he is the only one that can fight with me one on one, but that was two years ago, that was long ago and I am a changed man. I would love to have him one on one. BB: You think you can take him? DM: Yeah, if I'm at the top of my game, I am sure I can go one on one with him and probably beat him. He might be more experienced than me, he might be more powerful than me, but I have what it takes to beat him, I have what it takes to defeat him. I have what it takes to take on anyone, it doesn't matter who it is I will do my best, I will give it my all to win this event that I have come so close to winning twice. The last thing I need to accomplish in order to retire doing everything there is to be done. The only thing that has escaped my grasps twice. I will do everything possible to win it. Hell, I would do the impossible to win it! BB: Who do you think is the mystery stable though? DM: Frankly, I really don't care. It could be Killer Dude and the EoD back from the grave, it could be the Outcastz back from the grave, it could be the freaking Youngbloods and I wouldn't give a damn. No one will seperate me from my goal of winning Stable Wars for me and for Resurrection, and for the good of the UWF. Once we win it, the dark ages will be over, the reign of these Player's Club fools will be over and the Golden Age of the UWF will be again. Just like it was once and then was forgotten. This will be a night to remember, a night of to celebrate! Make sure you get that plane ready for our flight to Puerto Rico, we are gonna have us a party. BB: Awesome, but what if you lose? DM: Don't think like that! We must stay positive, if we lose, we'll party anyways! A loss is not something to be stressed about, it will only make you sour in the end of your career. A loss is nothing more than incentive to keep fighting. To keep giving your blood, sweat and tears every single time you are out there in the ring, everytime you are in here. (He points at the mat.) DM: This is what makes a good wrestler great. (The scene fades to black.)
