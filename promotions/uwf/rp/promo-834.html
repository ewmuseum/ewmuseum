UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #834

I am so Pissed off right now.

14 Dec 2004

The Communist — The Power Company

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Communist marches into the power company, sledgehammer in hand, and the Soviette following closely behind] Soviette: Communist, maybe this isn't the best way to handle this... Communist: I think differently Soviette, I beleive this is the BEST way to handle this. [The girl behind the counter continues to file her nails, nigh oblivious to the happenings around her. She blows the dust off her nails and kicks back, her feet on the desk. She looks up at the Communist and Soviette with contempt and aloof] Commie: I DEMAND to see the Manager! I REFUSE to pay 3 hundred dollars for my power! This is bullshit! Girl: Hey hey hey, calm down baby. First thing ya gots to do is sign in on da roster. Then take a seat and wait. Commie: I haven't TIME for your silly roster and Capitalist dogmatic practices! I DEMAND to see the manager! [Soviette signs the roster then pulls the Communist to a seat] Soviette: Come on Commie, lets talk. So, you won the North American Title. Good, Good. The Council is impressed. Commie: You know how much I care about the Council. Soviette: I know, but it is something we need to keep in mind, REGARDLESS of how much you care. They pay us, give us intelegence and information. They are our greatest allys. Commie: Funny, I don't see them in the ring with me. All I see are myself, my opponent and victory. Soviette: And a Commie Claus hat *chuckles* [Commie's eyes narrow a bit at her] Commie: That wasn't funny. Soviette: Yes it was and you know it. Regardless, you've proven to the Council that you have been well worth the effort. Even in not winning the Venetian Home Rumble, you were one of the last three in the contest. And you now hold the second highest title in the Federation. We are proud of your progress. Commie: It's bee 15 minuits. I'm going in. Soviette: Wait! [Commie gets up and walks towards the back. The girl gets up and attempts to stop him] Girl: EH! You can't go back Der! [Soviette pulls the girl down an armbar take down and rolls it into a ground wristlock. The girl, not accustomed to any sort of physical conflict, flops and twitches like a fish out of water, pulling at her arms and screaming. The Proletariot Monster walks into the managers office. The Manager looks up at the now broken door, mouth half full of donut, eyes wide, coffee still steaming] Manager: Mah fumahufame? Commie: I Demand to know why I'm paying a hundred and fifty dollars of Extra power this month! [The manager swallows his donut, takes a sip of coffee and reminds himself that he's the one incharge] Manager: Well sir, you should start by putting the Sledgehammer down. [The Communist swings the hammer over his head and puts the 16 lb Sledgehammer of the Proletariat through the desk of the rich bourguasis skum. The Manager's eyes go wide, he chokes on his coffee and the donuts, along with all the papers, his desk clock and the Hello Kitty paperweight.] Commie: Better? Manager: This is an outrage! Commie: NO! What is an outrage is being charged 5 times more for power then I EVER HAVE! Even in Mother Russa the rates weren't this bad! This is Bullshit! Manager: I'm sure our Information line can hel... Commie: NO! I was on hold for 3 hours! I will not submit to your bourguasis dehumanization! I will not be a voice on a phone line! YOU WILL NOW FEEL THE WRATH OF THE PEOPLE! [Commie grabs the man by the throat, picks him up and chokeslams him through the wreckage that is his desk. He pulls the sledgehammer out from under the man's wrecked body, then drops a copy of his bill on the man's chest] Commie: Here is my bill. I'm sure you can find a solution before I come by tomorrow. [Commie walks out of the office. The manager fights his way to a sitting position] Manager: TOMORROW?!
