(The scene opens up to the camerman (Jeff Johnson) walking down the street towards some very busy businesses. Large groups of people are seen walking in and out of the clubs. Jeff: Well here we are, at one'o'clock in the morning and I am stuck here reporting again for the UWF. Why did I take this job is all I ask? The Lord only knows why people take so much interest in these so called performers. I mean most of them are just disgusting and untalented. But why does my opinion matter anyhow. Just do my job. And that is follow dreammaker and interview him when needed. (stops to look at the camera) Why me? We all know he is up and coming, but damn, HE DOESN'T SLEEP! And my wife isn't very happy about going to some club called, (pauses to read the letter pulled form his pocket) DAYDREAMERS! Sounds like some getnlemens club. (he walks past a group of people to get to the front door and is stopped by a bouncer.) Bouncer : There is a line back there to get in buddy! Jeff: I was told to give you this for my interview. Bouncer: Oh, shit! you are the guy that got his ass kicked the other night by the group. Right? Jeff : Are you gonna let me in or not? Bouncer: Be my guess(the big man laughs and steps to teh side. Another big man pushes open the door and the camera catches a glimspe of a naked, very fine, ass dancing on a table to some very loud bass driven music) Jeff: Oh my God! It is a stip club. My wife is gonna kick my ass worse then those guys did the other night! (looks at teh bouncer) WHERE IN THE BLUE HELL DO I FIND DREAMMAKER!? Bouncer: Relax man, he is in the VIP room to the back. He's in a metting, so go ask his secetery to take your name down. (as the bouncer turns his back to close the door everyone hears him say ' What a loser.' And lets out a laugh) Jeff: Lets get this crap over with. (and he starts running around people with dollars in their hands and very naked girls giving lap dances and serving drinks. ) (in teh back corner of this building we see a beads hanging from the ceiling and stretched to the floor. Do you (Before he could finish) Girl in nurse outfir: My name is Bubbles. Im his assistant. And you can go in Mr. Johnson. He has been waiting for you. Jeff: (kinda of embarrassed) Well thank you (pauses) Bubbles. CAN I ask why you are called that? Bubbles: We all have a name here based on what we are good at. But please dont keep Mr. Dream waiting. (Jeff half smiles, nods his head, and walks towards the door which is half open. Laughter is heard very well as Jeff makes his way in.) (In the room, sitting around a TV is : Dreammaker, Fizbin and some other people not recognized. Standing against the wall we see a large man in the shadows. On the TV we see last weeks PPV:LIVE AND LOUD 4!) Fizzy: (after seeing that pepperoni stick jammed into Doc Cho Song's rectum) Glad that wasnt me. (the room fills with laughter and after the match is over, Dreammaker notices Jeff standing in the door way) (Dreammaker turns of the TV and then the large man in the corner steps foward) Raid: Hey lets watch that scene again! Fizzy: why in the hell would you want to see that pepperoni again? Dreammaker : Sad to say it, but that is probably what it was like when he was with one of my girls last night. (Everyone gives a good laugh) Dreammaker: Hey Bubbles! (she pops her head in and says, 'yes?') Can you find a girl for Raid to go dance with or what ever. Bubbles: Ya, the girls seem to need lots to do tonight. I can get right on that. Dreammaker : But make suure you get one of the tough and bigger ones for him, I dont need any more hospital trips this week. Maybe find the one I was reserving for Little Dicky before he ran off. Like the chump he is. Bubbles: Are you sure? She's a mean one! Dreammaker: Yes! Dammnit! It will keep him busy for the time being. (looks at Raid) Go, have some fun on my account. And remember , As my personal security, you dont put my girls tables or just Bug Bomb any one ok? Raid: But that last girl you gave me was biting me! Fizzy: Thats called four play dumb-dumb. Wow should of stayed a virgin. Dreammaker: YA, oh well, at least he can throw bodies around like no other. (Raid and Bubbles leave the room) Dreammaker: Jeff sit down and join us. We were jsut enjoying ourselves. heres a drink. Jeff: No thanks. I dont drink. Dreammaker: NO, well today you do. IN celebrating you joining us. So drink up. I mean you did put up a good fight the other night. Jeff: I knew i could hold my own, ok , one drink. But start talkin to this camera crew that you seem to have at your beck and call. Dreammaker: Hold on relax, jus thave a drink and let reality takes its toll. Lets talk about the dreams and life of a Mr Justice. (He turns to the TV and starts a movie) Dreammaker: After looking over some info about my next opponent I came accross these promos. (The video shows Justice's promo about working out) Dreammaker: (looks seriously at the camera) Hey everyone a UWF employee lifting weights. Not to mention properly! (Everyone laughs) Think stupid. We all lift weights here. How do you think we have these bodies or these jobs. The people are not impressed with what you can lift or how you do it. It is what you do with it. And how you work with the fan base. People dont pay us to work out. They pay us to kick ass! ( ya! -heard in the backgound) Now lets look at the newest one and stupidest thing yet to date. (Video shows Justice doing his promo at the school ) Dreammaker: Boring. You think you touched those kids. Look at White Wolf. Hes been touched. And look where he is now, just trying to get back on the roster I'm thingking. Hopefully I didnt end his career! (the room fills with laughter) Look at the real world there. Stop dreaming and come back to earth. Those kids were cheering cuz they got out of school and got to sit and listen to you talk instead of some boring old teacher. Didnt you see them run out of the building trying to get ahead of you, not trying to get to you. What would you give to the fans that pay to see the championship defended everynight. Fizzy: WEll he could give a lecture about how bad fighting is. Dreammaker: (laughs at Fizzy's joke) Dreammaker: Lets get an example of what people want. Hey Bubbles, could you come here and pick up my glass of wine (as DReammaker throws it on the floor) (Bubbles walks in and slowly bends over and as she picks up the glass we get a great glory filled view of everything down under) (the group gives some hollas and some woops.) DReammaker: Thank you Bubbles that will be all. And that proves my point. That is what everyone wants . so go pump your muscles somewhere else. Besides (turns around) Did you know i am an ec-body builder? I live this life and I have a body every woman would desire. So who cares about your jym? Fizzy: Why do they call her Bubbles any ways? Dreammaekr: WEll she does this thing with her legs...Hey you getting me off track. (he laughs and then looks at the camera and stands up) Dreammaker: Ya. Look The people have called and they want you to take your crap and 'Justice' way and go fight communism, some veitcong boys or better yet chase some mexican wrestler around the ring! WE dont need you to waste any more of my time. Bottom line is. I am gonna hurt you. Im not gonna let you go till you go to sleep. And then when you do, your dreams will be my reality and you will have to just wake up. Or I'll have to say nighty-night. (the camera fades as: Dreammaker takes off his shirt and flexes, yelling, ' Please everyone dont have a body like mine. ' and Jeff reaches for another drink and slaps Fizzy on the side from laughter. )
