UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #190

The Wood of the Harvest

24 Jan 2004

Ezra Kazee — Ryder's Field

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a very familiar spot. In fact this spot is probably familiar from someone else promo earlier, only from a much farther out view. There is an extremely large field, with a very large tree standing there in it. There is a fire pit off to one side, which looks like it was recently used. There is a log sitting to the outside of the fire pit. If you look closely, you can probably deduce that this is the place where Ruff Ryder shot his last promo. As the camera fades in you can here what sounds like a chainsaw running on high power. The camera starts to zoom in and you can see “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee with the Chainsaw in his hand. He is rapidly cutting through the tree with the chainsaw and has almost completely cut through to the other side, all of a sudden, the blade of the chainsaw can be seen on the other side, and the tree begins to fall of to the opposite side of which Kazee is standing. Kazee shuts off the chainsaw as a large crash can be heard from the felled tree.) Kazee: Part of the thing about being a Native American, which you seem to forget sometimes, is to use only what you need, and treat the world with respect, for it is your mother. You Ruff Ryder don’t know what respect is. Whether you ever will know what respect is, I don’t know….. The reason I’m out here cutting down this tree is, well, winter has set in hard in Minnesota. It’s getting to be extremely cold, and well, although I do have a furnace in my house, there is just something about turning that off and using the stone fireplace that we have in our house. Of course, when spring comes around, I’ll be out here replacing this tree, because that’s just the way things are, and it is part of giving back to our mother, isn’t that right Ryder? (Kazee walks around to the felled tree, turns the chainsaw on again, and starts to cut into it again, this time gutting it into smaller pieces, about 6 inches in length. He cuts several off these pieces, and turns off the chainsaw, he gathers them up, and brings them over to the stump that was left when the tree was felled. He sets one upright on the stump, lays down the chainsaw, and picks up an ax.) Of course, much like this tree, the Prime Alliance too will fall. Much as they talk a good talk, they just keep coming up short, except when they are wrestling each other, but that’s another matter all together. (Kazee swings the ax back, and runs it down through the log, splitting it in half.) You complain about me giving you a lecture on the fly? You’re upset about being educated? Do you have absolutely no reason to want to improve yourself? Do you have absolutely any reason to not want to learn? Yeah, you’re right; I did give you a lecture. Although it was barely scratching on the true nature of the fly. As a man with my educational background, I could give you quite a bit more, but the thing is I don’t want to, because to be honest with you, it would be a waste of both of our times, because a) I would be speaking to a bowl full of Jell-O, and b) Jell-O can’t learn…….. Of course, as I’ve always said though, my education and my knowledge is what helps me succeed. It’s that education which, obviously unmatched by you, gives me the edge against most of the wrestlers here, and allows me to give you the lecture on the fly which you seem to so hate……. (Kazee places another log on the stump, swings the ax back and back down through the log.) Dunn, he may be the strongest member of the Prime Alliance, Montgomery maybe as you put it a piece of shit, but they both are great athletes who have this element of respect that you and your associates seem to have forgotten about. They have an element of loyalty that you and yours have not demonstrated to me. And honestly, they are a whole heck of a lot better company than you are…… Me, no, I’m not Dunn. I don’t claim to be Dunn. I don’t know if I’d want to be Dunn. As a man, he’s a great person, a great partner. As a wrestler, he is ruthless, always on top of his game, but his style is just something that I would not want to use. Just as your style Ryder is one that I don’t want to use. My style fits me fine. I like the way my style has brought me success and will continue to bring me success, present and future success…... I have come this close to defeating Dunn, as I have come so close to defeating you. That small hump that I must get over and I will get over is just a matter of a few hours now. It will end your dominance over me, and also smash the dreams of the Prime Alliance to defeat The Players Club….. (Kazee takes another log yet, sets it on the stump, and runs through it with the ax.) Reflect on this tree. This tree here, which your promos have been shot in front of for the last two times. Like this tree, you will be chopped down. While I’m sure that it will not affect your ego, which honestly would probably survive a nuclear bomb much like the cockroaches would, it will affect your record, and your ability to get back into the title hunt once you have been defeated by Almasay…… It’s almost time, can you feel it, Ryder. Your defeat is close at hand. It is just hours away. You and I as the first match in the main event of the SAS card, you and I going one on one will set the tone of the main event for SAS. This is going to be a great match. Fact of the matter is we are both great athletes, no one will deny that. Fact of the matter is when we step into the ring we are going to give the Commonwealth a hell of a match, but this time the result will change. Last time, you defeated me, all be it it took everything you had, and then some. This time, it will be the other way around. You will be the defeated one, and I will stand in glory…… (Kazee places another log onto the stump. This time he takes out a pocket knife, and starts carving into the top of it. He carves in the initials RR, MK, JS, PA.) Ruff Ryder, Malcolm Kennedy, Jamie Shock, The Prime Alliance, this Sunday, our dominance will be established. The strongest group in the Universal Wrestling Federation will have landed the first blow in what is to be a rivalry, I’m sure, but just like this tree, we shall cut you into pieces, and then, we shall let the Prime Alliance burn within the fires of their own Hell…… Good Luck, you’ll need it. (The camera pan out a little bit and aims straight down on the top of the log. All that can be seen is the letters that were carved into the log. All of a sudden, the ax comes crashing down between the letters, splitting the log in two. The two pieces of wood fall off to the side, as the ax sets there in the stump. Upon further examination of the ax, the letters TPC can be seen. The camera zooms in a little more and the scene fades to black.)
