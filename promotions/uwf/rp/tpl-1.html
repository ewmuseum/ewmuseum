Trailer Park Luchador #1

2 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Sigh …” [The Trailer Park Luchador is sad.] [Why, you ask? The man has a trailer, a kick-ass luchador mask, and complete immunity to being eaten alive by El Turtlé, the Masked Ninja Turtle. The man has it all! What could possibly make a man with everything so sad?] “Humph.” [No, the Trailer Park Luchador is not just sad. He’s actually incredibly depressed.] “…” [No, even that’s not right. To be perfectly honest, the only thing keeping him from hanging himself off the side of his mother’s trailer is wanting to find out why that guy is under the island in Lost, and at the rate they’re going, it’s going to take them another fucking year just so the TPL, the idol of at least three people – though all mentally ill – can finally end it all.] “Oy.” [And so that is the situation we find our Luchador in on a rainy Friday night. He lies on the used, flea market racecar bed his mother had given him for Christmas last year, staring at the ceiling in utter despair. His sacred and very beaten up luchador mask lies on his bare chest; his face, unshaven and unkempt, looks upwards with bored despair. His smiley face boxers do nothing to help the situation.] “I suck.” [There! He said it.] “I suck, I suck, I suck, I suck!!! This isn’t how it was supposed to be!” [And thus begins a silly monologue, started unprompted and completely naturally in an empty bedroom for no reason.] “Man … when I find this super-duper Mexican luchador mask, I thought I was heading straight for the top. Touring professional wrestler … idol of millions … the limousines, the private jets, the prostitutes that said I was so good they wouldn’t even charge for it!” [TPL closes his eyes.] “Instead I’m making minimum wage as a security guard for the UWF, hoping every great once in a while they’ll give me the chance to get my ass kicked by some loser that’s only going to be around for another week. Rod damnit, I’m sick of losing to no-names!” [TPL sticks his tongue out and blows spittle at the ceiling.] “No money. No women. No car. Still living in my mom’s trailer two years after I got into wrestling. Man, even White Tiger and Pajaro left me in the dust, winning belts and crap and here I am waiting for them to get back so we can finish playing Operation and God damnit it zaps me every stinking time and I HATE PLAYING OPERATION!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” [Banging comes from somewhere off screen.] “Stop your screaming right this minute Chris or I’ll make your step father beat you with the TV Guide again!!!!” [And so the TPL sighs.] “And … and … and … you know what? I think I’ve realized something. You know what it is?” [The Trailer Park Luchador turns his head, looking into the eyes of the hideous teddy bear that sits awkwardly on his bed.] “Teddy … I think I’m a … a …” [The TPL gulps noticeably.] “I think I’m a jobber.” [The Luchador from the Trailer Park throws his hands over his face, groaning horribly. And as he does, the eyes of his teddy bear light up, and a harsh voice comes out:] “Of course you’re a jobber, you fucking idiot.” [The Lucha from the Double-Wide screams like a girl as his beloved teddy bear begins talking back.] TPL: “Wha … what?!?!” Teddy: “Have you ever won a match?” TPL: “Of course I have! I mean … I think … one or two … what …?!” Teddy: “Ever won a title?” TPL: “Pajaro used to let me pet his tag titles.” Teddy: “That doesn’t count, you retard.” TPL: “I know.” Teddy: “You’re nothing. You’re the comedic relief … they laugh at you when you walk by. WizHard had more respect in the locker room than you do.” TPL: “That’s not … !” Teddy: “You make me sick. Every fucking night you make me sit here on your bed and listen to your God damn whining. ‘Oh little teddy bear, nobody likes me, I can’t do anything, I’ve never kissed a girl …’” TPL: “I told you that in confidence you little bastard! We pinky swore!!!” Teddy: “I’ve had it! Not even an inanimate teddy bear can take your whining! You’re a loser, you’re a joke … you’re a jobber.” TPL: “It’s true … it’s all true.” Teddy: “And you know what, big buddy?” TPL: “What’s that, little buddy?” Teddy: “Sooner or later, your friends are going to realize you’re a jobber.” TPL: “But they love me …” Teddy: “HELLO! Earth to retard … what do they call that El Turtlé guy? The jobber killer! The guy eats jobbers!” TPL: “But …” Teddy: “Sooner or later, you’re going to turn around, and that giant ninja turtle is going to realize that no matter how good of friends you are … you’re just as big of a jobber as anyone he’s ever had to dispatch. And in that moment, he’s going to eat you.” TPL: “Oh dear Rod, it’s true … what can I do!? Teddy … please! Please … I need your help. You have to tell me what to do.” Teddy: “And what do I get out of the bargain?” TPL: “I … umm …” Teddy: “Look, idiot. If you promise to do what I tell you to do … I can save you, my child.” TPL: “Anything!!!” Teddy: “Good. First, go wash that fucking mask. It reaks.” TPL: “Done!” [The Trailer Park Luchador leaps off the bed, rushing out to find some detergent to watch his super-duper mask of doom.] Teddy: “Idiot.” [And with the TPL gone, and only the teddy bear remaining … the camera begins panning downwards.] [Down to the bottom of the bed … and through the floorboards.] [Past the wheels of the mobile home, and into the dirt.] [Down and down we go for dozens of feet … until we begin to see the concrete.] [And then the room.] [And then one, lone man, with a microphone in hand. One man with a bald head and an aura of evilness that radiates in every direction for miles on end.] [One man with an evil, twirling mustache.] [And Baron Black Scourge laughs. And laughs. And laughs.] [Because … you know … he’s evil like that.]
