UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #480

Fishing for glory.

June 29th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Secret fishing pond.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** No deep thoughts. Stable Wars is at hand and Malcolm Kennedy is ready to speak once more.*** ( The scene opens up on a beautiful mid-summers day. The camera focused on a few clouds in the bright blue sky. As the clouds slowly roll by the sound of footsteps in the grass can be heard. As the camera pans down a large field comes into view. Green, knee high grass and wild flowers cover the field from end to end. The field slopes down hill slightly to the left where a very large pond sits. A wooden dock rests at the edge of the pond. Tied to the dock is a large wooden platform nailed to two pontoon runners. It gently rocks in the ripples of the pond from the wind. Just in front of the dock sits a medium sized fishing boat resting on its trailer. To the left of the boat a small dirt path can be seen leading up to and ending at the dock. The sounds of footsteps can still be heard crunching the tall grass. As the camera pans slightly to the right, the dirt path that led to the dock can be seen. Only the path is not quite as worn down, and the tall grass is still present ) {Crunch} {Crunch} {Crunch} [SMACK! SLAP! SLAP!] “Damn mosquitos. Almost makes fishing not worth the trip.” “Awww, you said the D word. Your nut spose ta say that!” “Yeah I know. Lets pretend you didn’t hear that today okay? Your mom would kill me. And the last thing I need is for my sister to be barking at me because my nephew learned a new word on his fishing trip with Uncle Malcolm.” “I wont tell her if you buy me an ice cream cone on the way home.” “You know Eric, your gonna make one heck of a business man in life. I can tell. You got yourself a deal.” “Yes!” ( Malcolm Kennedy and his young nephew Eric can be seen walking up the half worn path. Each with a fishing pole of their right shoulder, and Malcolm carrying a very large tackle box. The boy wears a pair of blue jeans and a gray UWF t-shirt and a red cap while his uncle wears a pair of ripped up khaki cargo shorts and a white t-shirt with the sleeves cut off and a backwards black hat. ) Eric: “Hey uncle Malcolm? Whose that guy with the camera over there, watching us walk towards him?” Malcolm Kennedy: I swear to god, when the UWF wants a promo, by god they go all out to get one. No one outside of my family knows about this spot. We’ve been fishing here for years. It’s the only place that we can bank on no one keeping anything they catch. And now its on tv! God damn management, they had someone follow me!” Eric: “Awww, you said.........” Malcolm: “Alright Alright Alright, make it 2 damn ice creams. Eric: “......” Malcolm: “Better make it three. Do me a favor Eric. Run on ahead of me and see if you can find any pretty flowers for your mom. Ill catch up to you in a minute. Ok?” Eric: “Good idea. She would love those.” ( The boy runs off past the camera and up the hill. His footsteps slowly trail off until they cant be heard. Malcolm drops his pole on the ground and sits down on top of his tackle box. He looks up into the sky and squints his eyes as he begins to speak. ) Malcolm Kennedy: “People do a lot of strange things to clear their heads. Some go for walks. Some go for a drive. I go fishing with my nephew. With all the build up for Stable Wars 6, I just needed some time away from it all to think about something other than winning. Not because I don’t want to or because I don’t think we will. Mainly, its because Im so confident in myself and the rest of The Resurrection that we will win. I have plenty of faith in myself and my crew. Enough faith that I can sit back and just relax. Or go out and fish with my nephew on a beautiful summers day without worrying. Stable Wars will go just the way we have been saying it will for the past month. We will beat every opponent who stands in front of us. And lay the next block in the rebuilding of the UWF.” ( Malcolm turns his black hat back around. On the front of the hat in gold are the letters WF, which stands for Wake Forest University. With the shade from the bill blocking the sun from his eyes, Malcolm no longer squints. He grabs a piece of grass and pulls it out of the ground. He begins to tear it apart piece by piece in the finger tips of both his hands. As he stares down at the blade of grass he is ripping apart he keeps talking. ) “Everyone in the big match at Stable Wars has something to say. Some kind of bullshit comment to make that they think will make an impact on everyone else. People have been running their mouths off trying to install some kind of false hope in themselves and a little fear in their opponents. But to the Resurrection, that’s all just wasted breath. The fact is, half the UWF roster is in the Stable Wars match up, and they are all so sure of themselves about winning out the whole thing. They have all built their confidences up and told themselves that they can actually win Stable Wars 6. They built up their confidence so much that they have forgotten about the one major thing standing in the middle of their path to glory.........The Resurrection. The Resurrection is what will keep each and every single one of the UWF hopefuls that think they have a shot at winning it all, from doing just that. We have already staked our claim as Stable Wars champions, Now all we have to do is show everyone else what we knew all and said all along. And thats exactly what we are gonna do.” “ Now my history here in the UWF isn’t amazing. I held the TV title two or three times. No big deal. I’ve got a few claims to fame here. I stood up against Mike Montgomery before anyone ever knew my name.........I lost that battle on my own. But I made a name for myself. And I was credited with the reputation of a true fighter. A man with real heart. Someone who would be the first to step into the ring when the odds were stacked against him, and never quit no matter how many times I got knocked down. I handcuffed Spiff The Magic Monkey to the ring post and clobbered him with a chair just to get a THC title shot. Only to be fired at the following show by Mike Montgomery. And as my last match after being fired by Mike, I was forced to face him in a lumberjack match. The lumberjacks? The Players Club, Two Masked Canadians,The HIT, The ASYLUM, and Jobbers galore. All wanting to beat the shit out of me, and guess what? I got my ass whipped like none other. Ill never forget that night. Or the pain I went through. Mentally and Physically.” ( A ripple effect comes into play as Malcolm goes deep into thought. *and he made it seem like this promo wasnt planned!*.) Mike - Malcolm, I bet you're real proud of yourself. I bet you think that by beating Spiff like that, that you hold all the cards. 'Kinda Decent' K.D. - He's got something up his sleeve! Mike - But you made a mistake Malcolm, a very big one. You see Malcolm, when you said that you wouldn't hurt Spiff if I signed your little paper, you made a verbal contract with me. Since it was on national television, I've got thousands of witnesses, so don't even try and deny it. But you know what the best part is? The very second the chair in your hands connected with Spiff, you BROKE THE CONTRACT! [A furious Malcolm Kennedy immediately grabs a microphone if his own.] Malcolm - Montgomery, what the hell are you talking about!? You god damned owe me... Mike - SHUT THE FUCK UP BITCH!!! 'Kinda Decent' K.D. - Wow! I don't think I've ever heard Mike swear! Mike - I owe you absolutely nothing! Zero, ziltch, nada, rien, not a damned thing. Your chance at my THC title shot? GONE! The contract I signed? A useless piece of paper that was NEVER legally binding in the first place. The wonderful lawyers at Duey, Cheatem and Howe have informed me that any contract signed under duress is NOT binding. Oh wait, that reminds me...Mr. Price? [A man in a three piece suit steps out from the back. A black leather briefcase is in his left hand. He climbs into the ring, opens the briefcase, and hands a manilla folder to Malcolm Kennedy.] Lawyer - Mr. Kennedy, you have been served. [As the lawyer climbs back out of the ring, Malcolm Kennedy opens the folder and looks over the papers. An expression of shock and horror quickly overcomes him.] Mike - For the benefit of everyone else here tonight, what Mr. Kennedy's reading right now is a summons to appear in court. He's being sued by Asylum Industries for his actions last week and the emotional trauma those actions caused. Next, he'll find a notice of sale and ownership. The Grand Central Casino and Resort in Reno, Nevada, favorite hangout of one Malcolm Kennedy is now the exclusive property of Asylum Industries. 'Kinda Decent' K.D. - WHAT?! Mike - That's right, Spiff now owns Malcolm's favorite casino. Oh, Spiff says hi, by the way. That and if you ever show up at the Grand Central Resort and Casino again, you will be removed by security, charged with trespassing, and arrested on the spot. Oh, and there's four police officers in your dressing room waiting for you. Once the match is over, you are being taken into custody and charged with aggravated assault, assault causing severe bodily harm, and cruelty to animals. Now, flip to the next page, would you? [Malcolm does so, and his face goes chalk white.] Mike - That's right. It seems you don't quite have enough funds to cover Spiff's medical bills, so your assets have been frozen. You're broke. And, by the way, Spiff's not a wrestler. You assaulted a member of the UWF front office, and we kinda have a zero-tolerance policy against that. So...God I love this...wait a second...lemme just take a deep breath here...*ahem* *ahem* *hmm*...sorry, frog in my throat. Now, as I was saying...YOOOOOU'RE FIRED!!!!! 'Kinda Decent' K.D. - GOOD LORD! What else could Montgomery POSSIBLY do to him?! He's practically taken everything Malcolm Kennedy owns! Now the poor man has no livelyhood! We haven't even started the damned match yet! The Duke of Earl - You know what they say Rick, don't hate the player, hate the game! HA! Mike - That's right. F-I-R-E-D, FIRED! You will no longer receive a UWF paycheck, your travel expenses will not be covered, hell you'll be lucky if security lets you into future shows. Any future match you manage to participate in will be unsanctioned and occuring without the approval of the UWF. If your opponent does not agree to the match, you can't have it. If you insist on following the company from city to city and getting in the ring with guys, well, I ain't gonna stop you. Just remember that security will throw you out of the building the second they lay eyes on you. [Mike finally begins to make his way to the ring as he continues.] Mike - Now obviously we can't deprive these people of a match tonight, can we? So there's going to be a match. A Lumberjack Strap match. A lumberjack strap submission match to be precise. See, I get the sense Malcolm that you're not all that good in submission matches, but I am. Naturally that's why it's a submission match. Now, let's meet the Lumberjacks, shall we? . . . . [Little Tony wraps his strap around his fist and begins hitting Malcolm Kennedy with blows to the kidneys. The HIT grabs a chair, folds it up and calls for Kennedy. Little Tony and Joshua Dunn pick up Malcolm Kennedy and throw him at the HIT. *WHAM* Kennedy flips nearly end over end before hitting the arena floor after the HIT plasters him with a chair shot. The HIT then tosses Kennedy into the ring.] . . . . Joshua Dunn locks Kennedy in the St. Andrew's Crossface as the other lumberjacks descend. Everyone begins whipping Malcolm Kennedy with their straps. The crowd quickly disperses as they see Mike charge across the ring. Dunn keeps the St. Andrew's Crossface locked in as MIke leaps over the top rope and hits the Tranquilizer on Malcolm Kennedy!] 'Kinda Decent' K.D. - DEAR GOD ALMIGHTY NO!! Malcolm Kennedy may have been paralyzed from that! The Duke of Earl - The Human spine's not meant to take that sort of abuse! . . . . ( The ripple effect comes back and Malcolm Kennedy comes back into view. ) Malcolm: “That was the day I learned that strength lies in number here in the UWF. So I made started aligning myself with people. At one time I even sided with the Players Club. But most of my fame came from the Prime Alliance. Me...Ruff Ryder....And the most hated man in the UWF. Jamie Shock. We had our run as the best. But that fell off quickly. Jamie left me and Ryder dead in the water. But before we ever died, I was given my shot for number one contendership for the world title. My opponent though, was my good friend and stable mate. Jamie Shock. My single greatest victory in the UWF came from this match. ( Cue the ripple effect.) Shock goes for a football tackle, but Malcom Kennedy leap frogs over him. Shock spins around, only to meet Kennedy’s fist flying right at him. But Shock ducks it and tries to sweep the legs of Malcom. Malcom hops over the leg sweep and goes for a kick to the temple of Shock. Jamie dives and rolls out of the way and somersaults to his feet. Malcom punches at Shock, who dodges it, grabbing Kennedy’s extended arm, and executing the Whip-Lash!!]Larson: WHIP-LASH! WHIP-LASH! It’s all over for Malcom Kennedy!! [The referee counts as Jamie Shock covers Malcom Kennedy.] One… Two… Thre—KICK OUT!! Larson: WHAT?! Rick: AMAZING! MALCOM KENNEDY KICKED OUT OF THE WHIP-LASH! . . . . [Shock is in total disbelief and tears some of his hair out. He punches Kennedy in the face a few times. Then brings him to his feet and props him on the turnbuckle. Jamie punches Kennedy a few more times, and then climbs the ropes himself. He punches Malcom again. He positions himself for a power bomb off the top rope. He signals to the crowd, who are going nuts at this point, jeering and booing Shock. Some debris hits him in the back.] Larson: He’s going to finish off Malcom Kennedy once and for all!! HAHAHA!! I LOVE IT!! Rick: This is NOT going to be good! Malcom Kennedy’s body can’t take this! This is too much! It’s going to end his career! [Jamie smirks to himself, with that sly and sinister grin of his. He goes for the power bomb, but Malcom Kennedy grabs onto Shock’s boots. Shock elbows Kennedy in the back, and knees him in the face. Kennedy lets go of the boots. Jamie Shock goes for the power bomb, but Malcom grabs onto the boots again, but this time, he taps into his last reserve of energy and back body drops Jamie Shock off the top rope!!! Jamie Shock hits the mat with an earth shattering thud, and Malcom Kennedy. Atop the turnbuckle, dazedly turns around. He points to the sky, and launches himself off the top rope. He lands his finisher, The Jack-pot, a shooting star press into an elbow drop. Shock’s body bounces off the mat as Kennedy’s elbow is driven into his sternum. Malcom’s arm lies across Jamie’s chest.] Larson: GOOD ROD NO!! NO!! [The referee counts.] One… Two… THREE!! The Winner and New #1 Contender for the UWF World Heavyweight Championship: Malcom Kennedy!!! . . . . . ( Malcolm once again comes back into focus.) Malcolm: “I beat Jamie Shock. Fair and square. Not a lot of people in the UWF can truly claim that. At least not fairly. But I did. Of course as most of you might recall, I never got that shot at Beelzebozo for the Heavyweight Championship. Family problems not only kept me out of the ring, They kept me from running my casino for a while. But to me, it wasn’t that big a deal to loose my shot. Because I knew the time would come when I would get another chance to fight for that World title. And after The Resurrection and wins Stable Wars, my eyes will be set on Mike Montgomery and his precious belt, and sooner or later Im gonna take it from him.” ( Malcolm rips the grass one more time and drops it to the ground between his feet. He begins to scratch at his cheek and chin, which are in need of a shave. The stubles of hair make a rough scratchy noise as his fingers run through them. He looks back to the sky. ) “People have told me all my life that I would never amount to anything. That all I ever would be is one big failure. But time and time again I have proved them all wrong. I mean in the business world, Im just one big success story. I created the Grand Central Resort and Casino from nothing. And now its one of the biggest and best places in the state Nevada. Hell the biggest and best to the west of the Mississippi river. In the ring though, Im not quite as big a success as I am in business. Everyone knows who I am, and everyone knows that I never give up and Im a tough son of a bitch. But I don’t have an extensive championship background to back it all up. But now I have a chance to change all that. Now I have a chance to prove to the world that I am a walking Success story. Both in and out of the ring. And at Stable Wars 6, Even if I have to take on every single team by myself as the last member of the Resurrection remaining, by God that is exactly what Ill do. And I will win too!” “ As for Corporal Punishment and Chrono Cross. Corporal you’re a UWF legend. No one can deny you that. Your “Can Do”, or should I say “Can Defeat” attitude has gotten you a lot of places in the UWF and a lot of big wins. But make no mistake about it, your attitude will get you NOWHERE at Stable Wars. You hear me? NO WHERE! You think you can defeat every member of The Resurrection, well you go ahead and try. You may think everyone here in the UWF is in some way “inferior” to you, and that’s just fine with me. You think Im some kind of push over, some kind of a joke? That’s fine as well. You say you can defeat me. Well soon we are gonna find out if thats true. When stable wars comes around my friend, your gonna be left with two choices. Either Put up, or Shut up. I don’t think you can do either.” ( Malcolm looks up past the camera as a voice from the distance can be heard. ) Eric: “Uncle Malcolm, come quick! It’s a frog.” Malcolm: “Ok Eric, Ill be there in a second, just don’t pick it up yet.” Eric: “Ok I wont pick him up. Ill just follow him around.” Malcolm: “Alright, be there in a second.” Eric: “Hey Uncle Malcolmmmmmmmm?” Malcolm: “What is it now Eric?” Eric: “The frog just slithered up and wrapped around my leg.” Malcolm: “The frog did what?....... ( Malcolms voice lowers to a whisper. ) Malcolm: “Jesus christ, the boy just found a snake!” Eric: “UNCLE MALCOLM! THE FROG JUST BIT ME!!!!!” Malcolm: “IM COMING ERIC!” ( Malcolm stands up from the tackle box and grabs his fishing rod. He props it against the shoulder and carries the tackle box in the other hand. As he hurries up the path you can hear him mumble under his breath. ) Malcolm: “I better go ahead and make that four ice creams.” ( Malcolm’s footsteps crunch off into the distance as he hurries off up the path. As his footsteps slowly trail off until they cant be heard, the scene fades to black. )
