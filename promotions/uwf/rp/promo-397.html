UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #397

Nick's Little Secret

8 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Anarchy's SUV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to the back seat of a SUV. Driving it is Reo Dai, riding shotgun is Adien Pryde. Pryde is talking on a cell phone. Let’s listen in on what he has to say…] Pryde : Damn UWF receptionist, she put me on hold. I need to talk to someone in talent relations right now. Dai : What’s so important that you want to talk to Montgomery? I know you can’t stand the guy and neither can I. Pryde : It has to deal with Nick Persia and whether or not he is here in the USA legally or not. I heard though a reliable source that his work visa has expired and he is working here illegally. I need to find out as soon…Yea, Mike I have a question for you. [Pryde pauses while he listens.] Pryde : Yes, I know I have had problems with you and The Players Club and I don’t think that it will change anytime soon but I need to… [Pryde listens some more, obviously getting more frustrated.] Pryde : Yes I understand that, but you are the VP of talent and you were the one to bring in Nick Persia so… [Pryde stops talking again to listen.] Pryde : Damnit, shut-up and listen to me. I need to know if Nick Persia’s work visa is up to date. Your were the one to sign his contract and I demand you check his file to see if it is up to date. [Pryde stops talking and slams the phone on the dash before he puts it up to his ear again.] Pryde : Bastard put me on hold again. Dai : Is he going to check for you though? Pryde : He say’s he is. I hate frickin hold…. Yes, I am still here. [Pryde stops talking again to listen.] Pryde : What do you mean Montgomery doesn’t have time for me so he patched me to you? [Pryde stops talking again to listen.] Pryde : Fine, whatever just as long as I get my answer. Is Persia’s work visa current? [Pryde stops talking again to listen. He gets a big smile on his face.] Pryde : Really, thanks a lot. By the way what are you doing Monday night? [Pryde looks at his phone in shock then turns it off.] Pryde : Bitch hung up on me! [Dai laughs out loud.] Pryde : Get us to the sheriff’s department. I have a little surprise for Nick Persia this Sunday that may have some repercussions on out Stretcher Match. Dai : Sounds good, fill me in. [The scene fades to black.]
