UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #640

What's more important?

15 Aug 2004

Sean 'The Irish Bastard' McClancy — Tokyo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Look at what’s happening here? Tsunami Oni wins his contract back and he then gets booked in a match to ‘close a loose end’ such as once again facing “The Irish Bastard” Sean McClancy. What a friggin’ joke… The man really thinks he can even come close to doing anything here? He thinks he’s got the ability to take out one of the stong favorites to win the Rumble, let alone his match. And how does Oni think he can get ready? By beating up on some midget with a piss poor Irish accent and letting Shiatsu sit on him? I’ve got news for you Oni… You’re not dealing with Fey or Blaise McClancy here. The two ‘light weight or cruiserweight’ McClancy’s aren’t here. Sean is no midget. He’s 6’ 4” and over 260 lbs. If you think fighting a midget is going to get you ready for a man who can tie you up like a preztel, toss your carcass around the ring, or do much worse to you, then you can only be classified as one thing in the wrestling world, a moron. You’re lost Oni. Truly lost. You’re doing too much of the drugs you have peddled, and really need to get clean and sober and wake up. This isn’t a game. This is a freaking way of life for some people, and if you think we’re just going to roll over for your horse shit, then you really are in for a rude awakening. Then again, if it’s usual Drowning Pool stuff that’ll go on, how can Sean really care what happens in this match, as it’s obvious what will happen as soon as Oni is setup for a Celtic Driver, Celtic Cross of if he Kisses that Blarney Stone… Benji and Slasher will be there. The KnightMare Brigade doesn’t need to do that. If Sean can’t handle the man then so be it. It’ll be up to Sean as to how he goes out of it. Then again, this match against Oni holds nothing for him. Oni is only a speed bump and a means of softening him up before the Rumble. Let’s just add in more stress for Sean. The Twins, Thomas and Patrick McClancy, his younger brothers, have a match against DP earlier in the night for the Tag Team Titles. And then Sean gets Oni and then a match that will basically have them all in a match to determine a new contender for the UWF World Title. Sean cares not for Oni. He didn’t come to the UWF to wallow as the bottom, sucking the sand and living like the other flounders. Sean McClancy is a former world champion. Sean McClancy is a CURRENT world champion. The UWF World title is only a step in the right direction, and let’s not forget that TV title also is up for grabs there. Oni holds nothing for him. Oni is worthless. Oni will get him nothing. Oni is not the target. Oni is not what he wants. Oni…. Is NOTHING! The brightly decorated streets of Tokyo is where we now find our first family of many federations: The McClancy’s. Three members are here at the Fukagawa Matsuri, festival of Tomioka Hachimangu Shrine in Koto Ward. “The Irish Bastard” and his two little brothers, all three Wearing black Knightmare Brigade T-shirts. Tthe twinsare also wearing tight jeans, Sean looks to be wearing more “gu”- like pants, loose and comforatable. They stick out like sore thumbs walking through the many shopping stalls that are set up. Nothing like 3 red-haired Irishmen, each in the mid 6’ range, walking together. One of the two twins carries a few bags in his hands. The other one keeps looking at his watch. Sean looks to be trying to barter with a stall owner on a red and black piece of silk folded on the table. A small crowd of young girls have formed behind him, they look to be chattering between themselves pointing at at him, half of them seem to think one thing and the others shaking their heads no seem to think another. As Sean seems to finish what he is doing he turns to walk off and a young man walks right up to him. Young Man: Excuse me. Mr McClancy? Sean looks at the kid and smiles as he is use to being approached in places like this by fans. Sean: Yes? How may I be helpin’ ye? The kid pulls out a small red scroll looking package and hands it to him. Sean opens the small cylinder box and then pulls out the scroll that’s inside. It’s about a foot long and about a half an inch in diameter. It appers to have a proper wax seal upon it. Young Man: I was asked to deliever this to you right away. It just came to the hotel. And the messenger said it was important and that it had to be sent to you with the utmost urgency. Sean doesn’t respond, just looks at the scroll in his hands. Young Man: I’ll be going then good sir. The young man bows to Sean the turns around and scrurries off. The twins run up to see what is going on. Behind them Annie can be seen. Annie: Is tha’ wha’ I think it is? Sean: Tis’ th’ first o’ three, yes. Sean Nervously opens the scroll. Unrolling the piece of thick parchment that is rolled up tightly, breaking the seal in doing so. Taking a deep breath, he starts to read it. The twins step forward to try to read over his shoulder but Annie stops them. Annie: This isn’na yer business, boys. Sean Crumples the paper in his hand then looks at Annie… and nods his head. Sean: it’s been accepted…. So far so good. Yer reports of Lord Ishram goin’ t’ Ireland were good. That was ‘is acknowledgement o’ ‘is support. Annie walks up to him and kisses him on his cheek. She then takes the two younger brothers by their arms. Annie: Let’s give him time to ‘imself, boys. We ‘ave a crowd o’ people tha’ ‘ave been waitin’ t’ get autographs. We migh’ as well do some PR work fer UWF an’ th’ show now. Na’ t’ mention th’ KB. The twins head off with her as they get close to the crowd they get envloped by the bodies of fans of all shapes and sizes. Sean stands there watching Annie and his little brothers, the scroll is a start to a new life, and Annie is one of the few that understands that. She had been on the waiting end of that one a few times herself. He was actually glad to have her near him for this. Plus, she kept him grounded. And with the high profile matches coming up, he neeed that. Sean: Soon this will be over. The matches this week call fer me mind t’ be one hundered percent focused on what’s goin’ on. I can’na afford t’ ‘ave too many distractions this week. There is one tha’ is me biggest distraction, and I can’na stop thinking abou’ ‘er. I was ‘oping these scrolls would help me think abou’ others more then ‘er. But th’ answers are coming faster then I was expectin’, or ‘opeing fer. Oni, will be in tha’ ring, with only one job there, t’ ensure tha’ DP can soften me up befer the Royal Rumble. It’s th’ only way tha’ people like Oni, Slasher an’ Benjamin would even fokin’ ‘ave a chance. Me brothers will be in th’ big match. I’ll be in th’ big match So will ninety percent o’ the roster of the UWF. Stables won’na mean much fer tha’ as it will be a winner takes all situation. So what do I focus on first? Oni or the RR what do I want more…. In his deep thought, he doesn’t see Annie approach him again. Her hand runs up to his face. Instictly he pulls away then stops. His green eyes look into her eyes and he gives her a slight polite smile. Sean: Sorry, didn’na see ye comin’ again. Annie: Yer too uptight me prince, it is time t’ unwind an’ relax. Yer fans are waiting fer ye t’ join yer brothers fer a quick photo shoot. Ye still seem to be a favorite with th’ ladies. Even ‘ere they seem t’ fawn over ye. To bad for them ye’re off the market. Lucky for some of us ye are. Today, distractions are good tomarrow it is back to work. But for now ye are just Sean McClancy, wrestlin’ superstar, an fan favorite. Yer getting ready t’ take one of th’ biggest steps in th’ UWF, becoming the Number one Contender. Meanwhile, I’ll reserch who sent tha’ parcel to those two bafoons so ye, me prince can break the bones o’ everyone as ye see fit. The twins are ‘aving a wee bit o’ fun befer facing Unforgiven an’ ‘is friend. Other then tha’, I wouldn’na worry abou’ Oni, ‘e doesn’na seeem to be all tha’ worried about ye eather. A mistake on ‘is part. Yer a fokin’ world champion already. Ye jus’ need t’ beat some o’ the rest o’ them t’ get a shot a’ addin’ the UWF World Title t’ th’ BDW one. Oni jus’ don’na know ye, tha’s all. Sean: No, ‘e doesn’na. Sean soft eyes turn hard. Sean: I be wantin’ to know who sent tha’ video ou’. The other one I ‘ave seen, an’ t’ be ‘onest, I could’na care. Patti ‘as a past marred by some choices t’ make money when she was startin’ as an actress. Big deal. Me own sister once posed fer a lingerie catalog, na’ t’ much different. Until I know, as far as I’m concerned, Oni an’ tha’ little dog like bitch of his are the ones who sent it ou’. I am na’ goin’ int’ tha’ match t’ win. Bu’ t’ fokin’ urt ‘im. My focus will be on getting’ t’ th’ end o’ th’ Rumble, an then movin’ on th’ one title in this feds past tha’ I never ‘eld. Each person in tha’ Rumble will be the face o’ th’ man, or woman, tha’ sent the video t’ Jaime an’ Miguel. Tha’s a fokin’ promise. I don’na ‘ave t’ play nicely anymore. Sean crushes the scroll and drops it onto the ground, he then turns to start heading towards the Crowd and his little brothers. Annie bends down at her knees and picks up the scroll. She gentley places it in her purse. He stops and stares at her, cocking his head to one side in a questionable manner. Annie: Ye migh’ be wantin’ t’ keep it, fer later. Sometimes men don’na think abou’ such simple things that are meaningful fer us fairer folk. Sean: I’ll trust yer judgement on tha’ one. Annie: Come, let’s go now. Sean: Aye, let’s go. And we fade to green here.
