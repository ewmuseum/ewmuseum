The Proletariot Champion

27 Apr 2004

The Communist — The Conferance Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:the scene fades in with the Communist sitting in his familiar position at his desk with the slade projector at his side and the screen behind him: Commie: Ah yes, now is the time for Communism. And my first conquest on my way to revenge for my People... :he shows the first slide, a picture of Loverboy's face appears on the screen: Commie: is Loverboy. Yes, you will fall at my feet little man. You will beg Me for mercy, the way my people Beg for food in the streets. Your decadant living and American Elitest attitude will no longer be tolerated by the people. :He flips to the next slide, picture of the LA skyline: Commie: You hide in your filth ridden hovel of a town, driving your expensive car around, Flaunting your good fortune to the homeless in the streets. Eating your fancy foods and buying nice things for your girlfriend. Tell me, how will she feel when your pretty face is so destroyed that the doctor will need to resort to Dental records for positive identification? :smiles evily: I know how I'll feel.
