UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #475

The Side Show-#1 SW

28 Jun 2004

Digital Mortality — UWF TV Building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Stable Wars, at last, the greatest event in UWF history is upon us. It is time for one of the UWF's greatest stables to take what is rightfully their, to be the winners of the greatest accolade, Stable Wars Winners. The team of Malcolm Kennedy, Moraless, Bondage the Sex Fiend, and Hall of Famer Digital Mortality will be taking on everyone to become winners, it doesn't matter who is in the way, it doesn't matter who everyone thinks will win, it is of no importance because whoever it is will never do it when they have to face Resurrection. Four of the best UWF wrestlers right now have united and created this unbeatable team. As soon as they arrived, it marked the demise of the Player's Club. The Club lost the Tag Titles, lost the IC Title, and most important of all, they lost their leader. Digital Mortality completely destroyed the leader of the Club Mike Montgomery. Not that they really need him, he is probably the worst of them all.] [There are a couple of other stables that are participating besides Resurrection and the Club. There is Chaos Society, a group of misfits that will not get very far in this business if they don't work harder. The only one that has a promising future is Raid and he hasn't been seen in a long time even though he has the IC Title around his waist. The belt is just losing its prestige every second it is at the waist of that man. Adien Pryde is TV Champ material, but who isn't? If he doesn't improve his performances, he will never be able to hold a belt like the IC Title much less the World Title. Draco Paladine... who the hell is this person? I have never seen him in UWF TV, what the hell is he doin? Is he a ghost? No one has heard of him, and I am willing to bet that we will not hear him for Stable Wars either. Last in thsi stable of nobodies is a mystery partner... isn't that a cliché? Don't they get tired of these Mystery Opponents? Whoever he is, if he hangs around Chaos Society, he must not be that talented.] [Chrono Cross. What can I say, this is a team of talented wrestlers, Kenny Mercury is one of the best technical wrestlers of UWF history, if not the best one, he just needs a little time and he should be up there main eventing. Richard Ray Vaughn, I have no clue who he is. I don't even think he is a wrestler, and I don't think it was a wise move to put in a manager in the place of a wrestler, not a very good idea. Corporal Punichment, this is one of the greatest wrestler of all time, except that this is an old and obsolete version of what once was. Sure he can still wrestle against the likes of Nick Persia and Joshua Dunn, but can he go one on one with Digital Mortality? Can he go one on one with the PTC Extreme Champion Moraless? I am sure that he could have, if he was in his prime, but right now, I doubt it. The last member for this stable is none other than the Final Fantasy himself, the man acclaimed as one of the greatest wrestlers ever to grace a wrestling ring, Seymour Almasy. There is no doubt that he is one of the greats, but can he defeat Digital Mortality when he is in his prime?] [The other stables will be left for another time, but now, we are going live to the UWF studio where Digital Mortality is a special guest of The Side Show with Jon Focker.] (The scene opens inside a very large studio, there is a desk in the right part of the set, and a couple of chairs on the right side. There are a couple of plants scattered about to fill up the set. The words 'Side Show' are shown on the back of the set in white. The man everyone knows as Jon Focker is sitting behind his desk getting his makeup done, the production assistants are all running around getting ready for the show that is about to start. The cameramen all get into their positions, the makeup and hair crew hurry off the set and one of the cameramen begins the countdown.) Cameraman: We're live in ten, nine, eight, seven, six, *mouthing words* fivee, four, three, two, one... (The lights go out and the intro for the show begins, wrestling highlights from the UWF. Then suddenly, there appear some words and a deep freaky voice begins to talk.) Voice: Are you ready? (Chaos Society appear looking at the camera with a look of wanting to destroy something. Raid has the IC Title around his waist, and there is a black shadow representing the mystery partner.) Voice: It is coming. (The Iron Curtain appear looking all funny and goofy. The really small looking Local NVA Company are as always standing together besides Takamichi Mori, and the gigantic Communist on the back, holding the Soviette on his huge arms.) Voice: The most important event in UWF history! (The Mystery Stable appear, but we only see black figures, so we don't know who they are.) Voice: The battle will be fierce. (The Player's Club appear on the screen with a mad look on their face. Kevin Starr is at the far right almost completely turning his back to his stable. He has Nick Persia besides him with his THC Title belt around his waist. Besides him is Ezra Kazee looking very cheery, and Joshua Dunn with a look of determination. Finally, Mike Montgomery holding his World Title belt high in the air with his right hand and looking at the sky.) Voice: Only one team will survive. (Resurrection appear on the screen, Digital Mortality and Moraless in the middle holding their Tag Team title belts and Moraless the PTC Extreme Title belt. Bondage is standing two the right of Digital Mortality and in between them is Spice. Malcolm Kennedy is standing to the left of Moraless and in between them is Sugar. Digital has a look of determination in his eyes that no one will be able to take away.) Voice: The World has already rumbled five times. Let's make it a sixth. (The logo of Stable Wars VI appears on the screen.) Voice: STABLE WARS! (The lights start to flash in different colors and behind the desk is the smiling face of Jon Focker.) Focker: Welcome everyone, to The Side Show! We have a great show for all of you tonday, we will be talking to our guest, former World Champ, two time IC Champ, one time TV Champ, finalist in the last two Stable Wars, Hall of Famer and current Tag Team Champion. A member of Resurrection, Digital Mortality. He will be with us in just a moment. We will now start the show with a quick recap of the events of last Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. (The scene fades and quickly fades back to Digital Mortality in the waiting room for the show along with his friend Burton Bell.) DM: It's been a long time since I've been here in the Side Show. BB: Yeah it has, you know what is funny though? DM: What? BB: How far Jon has made it in this business. DM: That is very true. After hanging around with Plague for so long, I can't believe he still has talent left. Just listening to Plague talk can drain you out of any talent you have ever had. (They both laugh and then start watching the show again. They show a clip of Digital breaking Mike Montgomery's leg.) Focker: These two Stable's have been at each other's throats way before the creation of Resurrection, there is history with all those people in the Club. Mike Montgomery and Digital have been fighting each other since the days of the Iron Curtain and the Asylum. They had a bitter feud then, and they have a bitter feud now, that will not end until the World title is at someone else's waist. Kevin Starr and Digital go back to the days of the Asylum too, and they have battled many times before so they know each other's styles quite well. Ezra Kazee and Nick Persia don't have such a long history with Digital, but they can be sure to excpect the greatest performance from this man. Now, Joshua Dunn and Digital Mortality have the longest history of all of them. They were members of the Iron Curtain, they came close to winning Stable Wars two years ago, and they were teammates even after the demise of the Curtain. Until, Joshua Dunn betrayed Digital by going out and kidnapping Justine, Digital's girlfriend, and turning her against him. Devastated, Digital left the UWF to find himself and to regain control of his life. The news of Justine's death came hard on Digital, he had lost his only soulmate. Now he is here today to show everyone that he still has the ability of defeating so many opponents to finally win Stable Wars. (Back in the waiting room, Digital has a sad look on his face after the mention of Justine. Th door opened and the producer told him to follow her. He stood up, and followed her to the set.) Focker: And here he is now, ladies and gentlemen, a big round of applause for Digital Mortality. (He stood up and waited for Digital to come close. They shook hands and he motioned for him to sit. Digital does so.) Focker: How are you doing Digital? My condolences on your loss. DM: Thanks Jon. I have had time to think on it, and I understand what has happened and I understand that life goes on, and I gotta move on. It was a very hard loss, but that is life. Focker: That is life indeed. Now, let's get on with UWF matters, Stable Wars is this Wednesday, are you ready for it? DM: Of course I'm ready, I have been waiting for this chance for a long time. The chance to win Stable Wars at last, and take down those fools in the Player's Club. They don't understand our mission, they don't understand our resolve, they do not understand what the Resurrection is about, and it will cost them dearly. Focker: What is exactly the Resurrection's mission? DM: It's simple really. Our mission is to take down this sorry excuse for the UWF and then raise it back up to it's original standards. The UWF that was a year or two ago looks at us now and laughs, because the UWF is a joke. Look at our champions and tell me you don't laugh. Focker: Well, Mike Montgomery is a good champion. DM: Are you kidding me? How many times has he defended his belt? One time, and against me. That is the time I broke his leg, because the World title was not as important as seeing him suffer. Seeing his dreams of winning Stable Wars fade. Focker: Why didn't you just win the title from him? DM: Because Resurrection is not about titles. We do not care for these minor accolades, our mission is for the greater good. Let me ask you a question, if you could remove all the sickness in the world, but had to kill one child for it, would you do it? (Thanks to John Travolta for that line. :D) Focker: Of course not! DM: You dissapoint me. You would not end a life to save millions more? What is there to lose? Focker: Well, my morals, it just goes against everything I believe in. DM: Well, we are here for the greater good, if we have to sacrifice the Player's Club and completely destroy them, then that is what we will do. We will sacrifice everyone that gets in our way, no exeptions. Focker: What about this mystery stable you will be facing in the first round of Stable Wars? DM: Well, it doesn't really matter who it is. It could be the EoD, it could be El Turtlé and all those jobbers, it could even be the great Hamish McGrubber himself and I would not care, I would just battle him like I would battle anyone. It is that simple. Focker: You sound very confident. Do you think there will not be any challenge for you? DM: There is always a challenge down the line. Something unexpected that can set you back, but ultimately we will prevail in our mission. Focker: Well, what are you expecting in later rounds? DM: I am expecting to go head to head with the Player's Club so we can finally finish them off. After we win Stable Wars, they will understand what is good for them and the UWF. Focker: Well, that is it for today. I want to thank our guest Digital Mortality for being with us here today. Good luck to you this Wednesday. DM: Thanks Jon. (Digital stands up and shakes Jon's hand. He leaves backstage and Burton is waiting there for him with his bag packed and ready to go.) BB: Time to get to training. DM: Yes... though I wish we were in Puerto Rico... I hate training here. BB: That's life bro. DM: Unfortunately. (They both leave the building and the scene fades to black.)
