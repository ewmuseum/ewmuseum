UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #621

RR:Bang Bang

13 Aug 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of the familiar setting of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Standing outside the office is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing shorts and a t-shirt, and has a gun of some sorts in his hand, probably just a BB gun. He is looking directly at a bookshelf which sports a bunch of action figures. Every action figure is a UWF wrestler, and every UWF wrestler is represented upon the book shelf. Kazee is just standing there with the gun upright balanced against his shoulder.) Kazee: We are a very short time away now. A very short time away from the Regal Rumble. And everyone wants to know exactly what is going to happen. Everyone wants to know who is going to win. For that matter, everyone wants to know exactly who is going to be entered. There are several who we know about, and several that we don’t know about. And no one knows there position, save two men, Jamie Shock and the Plague…… (BANG!!! BANG!!! The Fool and Trailer Park Luchador fall off the bookshelf.) This match, it really isn’t my cup of tea. I’m a mat wrestler. I’m a technician. I know submission and leverage moves. I don’t know power and throwing people over ropes. I know about going catch can wrestling. Move and Countermove, not clinging to ropes and throwing people over them. This is not my match at all…… (BANG!!! BANG!!! El Turtle and El Chupacabra fall.) This match though, this match is more about throwing people over the ropes. This match has so much more on the line than that. This match is a match for every single person to not only put a feather in their hats, but also to gain a shot at the World Heavyweight Championship, and maybe even more. This match gives people to propel their careers to a higher level that very few will ever know or may ever know. This match will give the winner everything they probably could ever want in the world……. (BANG!!! BANG!!!! Lance Rose and Jukei Kutabare fall.) We have “The Renegade” Rich Rollins in this match. This man is so very impressive. He has came in and taken the UWF by storm. His latest promo says that he quit. Are we to believe this? Who knows? If he has, then it is truly sad because this man has so much talent it isn’t even funny. He has come in and taken the UWF TV Title by storm and has been able to do great things with it. He is a talent unto himself with a bright bright future. He also has more on the line. If he gets eliminated, he loses his UWF TV Title to that man. What a huge disadvantage he is at.….. We have Kenny Mercury in here as well. The definition of precision. The current UWF IC Champion, a title that I once held. He is a great competitor. Much like me, he is not known for his brawling or power wrestling. He is however becoming more and more known for it. He is however changing who he is and becoming that brawler. He is becoming an even more dangerous man than he was before. (BANG!!! BANG!!!! Mercury and Rollins fall.) We have tag teams in this thing as well. We have some great tag teams in this match as well. If they can work as a team, they can make out ahead I’m sure. There are so many teams in here that have held the gold and proved to be great teams. And will always be known for being a great team. The Legends, the Local NVA Company. If they are lucky enough to function as a team, what a great advantage. But then what happens at the end when there can only be one winner….. And there are those teams out there who have yet to truly realize their potential. Once again, you have a people who can make themselves a force as a team. But some of these teams may be frustrated. Some of them have gone no where. This could be the opportunity for them to make a change in their career. This could be the opportunity for them to make some significant headway in their career and make a run for something, while becoming a singles wrestler. (BANG!!! BANG!!! BANG!!!! BANG!!! The Legends and the Local NVA Company fall from their perch.) We have some great stables out there too. At least one that has been a thorn in my side. Chaos Society. These people are bad asses, but on top of that, they are great fighters and great power wrestlers. And if they get together, the results could be disastrous for anyone. Adien Pryde is a man who has been in the THC Hunt for some time now, and will continue to make strides. If his tag partner ever gets back, they could definitely make some waves. We have Raid, a former THC Champion and IC Champion. He has beaten some great wrestlers. And he has a lot of potential. He is going to be doing something in this fed….. Chaos Society also has Mr. Meaner, a former TV Champion. This guy is a bad man. One of the baddest men in the federation. He has beaten several of the top wrestlers in this federation. And he is a huge threat in this match, especially if he gets together with Raid. And perhaps the wild card of the group, Fizbane the Fabulous. He’s had some limited success so far, with the exception of with the ladies, but he is definitely a wild card and might surprise a few people….. (BANG!!! BANG!!!! BANG!!! BANG!!!! The four members of Chaos Society fall.) We have some people who will be wrestling twice as well. Kennedy, Shock, Clancy, Mercury. So many wrestlers are going to be winded by the end of the night. These are all great competitors, and they all know what they are in for. But the question is how are they going to be at the end of the night. The question is what kind of shape are they going to be in at the end of the night for the Regal Rumble….. Very rarely do people wrestle twice at a PPV. This is an exception here. This is going to be a huge test for them. And this is going to really prove their worth and how they can overcome anything. IF any of these men can win the Rumble, then they will be definitely among the greatest in the history of the UWF. (BANG!!! BANG!!! BANG!!! BANG!!! Mercury, Kennedy, Shock, and Clancy all fall.) But the greatest question in all of this is one thing. At least to me there is only one question. Will I be in the Regal Rumble? Will I be the one who will be competing for a shot to get back for another shot at the UWF World Championship? Or will I be the man who will be waiting for the winner at Live and Loud….. The greatest question for every wrestler in this match is who will come out of the UWF Championship match into the Regal Rumble. And what shape will they be in. An hour long submission match is going to be grueling and exhausting. In the end, whoever comes out of that on top is going to be great. Whoever comes out on top as the World Champion will have a well deserved win, but those who go on, the testament to their stamina is going to be head and shoulders above everyone. (BANG!!! BANG!!!! BANG!!! Dunn, Montgomery, and Starr all fall. More bangs are heard and more of the action figures fall, leaving only one.) In the end however, there will only be one left standing. Hopefully, if I don’t come out on top in the Championship match, that one who will come out on top will be me. Hopefully, in the end, the World Title will be in the hands of a man who exemplifies sportsmanship and exemplifies everything that a role model should be. In the end, hopefully, that one man on top will be me. (The camera show the last action figure standing is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee.) Good Luck to everyone in the Regal Rumble. (Kazee walks off to the left, where there is a forest, and disappears into the forest.)
