05Aug04 (year unrecorded)

The Communist — The Local Golf Range

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist walks out of the bus into the club house. He looks around, reading the prices on different club sets, some of the bags, some of the shoes, and he is pissed off. He shrugs the sledgehammer off his shoulder and walks over to the front counter. He brings the hammer up and drops the head on the counter: Commie: WHAT KIND OF SICK BOURGOUASIS TRAP HAVE YOU LAIN FOR MY PEOPLE!!!! :The teen at the counter, working his summer job, shrinks behind the counter peering up at the 7' monster with the 16 lbs sledgehammer: Teen: C-c-c-c-c-can I help you s-s-s-s-sir... Commie: Yes, you can. You can rise above you evil masters and take hold of whats yours! Look at this! Children can eat for years on the money you demand to play this worthless elitest game! Hundreds of dollars for your so called 'Qualitly clubs.' Hundreds of dollars for your bags to carry your 'Quality clubs' in. Acres and acres of land WASTED on your Stupid game for rich old farts! :A rich old fart walks in, wearing plaid pants and a barret, walks up followed by his professional caddy carrying his 300 dollar bag, filled with about a thousand dollars worth in clubs. He walks up to the counter and pushes his way around the Communist: Rich Old Fart: What is the meaning of this! I demand this man be removed! He's not a memeber and he's not dressed according to the dress code! I demand :He never finishes the sentence as the Communist takes a full side swing with the sledgehammer and catches the Old Fart in the side, slamming him through the window he's standing next to: Commie: You, bag boy, come with me. :Commie walks out to the first hole. He goes to the golf cart and starts to wail away on it, beating on the seats, the floor, and the clubs in the back. The rich old fart on the hill runs down to find out what's going on and takes a 16 lbs sledgehammer head to the gut. He drops like a sack of hammers. Commie takes the old farts wrists and ties them to the cart using his golf clubs. He tosses the club bag on the gas and watches as the golf cart drives off, dragging the old man behind it. The caddie walks up to the Communist: Caddie: Why are you doing this? Commie: Because, comrad, I have a match Sunday against some evil, evil men. One is a coward and a theif. He and his coward friends stole my manager, and they will ALL pay dearly. The other is a talentless wimp who couldn't wrestle his way out of a paperbag. Caddie: Wait, you're the Communist! I watch you on UWF! I love that show! Who are you fighting? Commie: Mr Meaner and Sacrifice. For the THC title. I will crush them. Meaner thinks he knows hardcore, And SacK rifice thinks he knows how to wrestle. I think I may have to teach them how little they know. Caddie: Hell yah! Take them out man! I'd love to see you keep the title! I'm a Communist too man! I think you're AWESOME! :The Communist pins the Scythe and Hammer on the Caddie's lapel: Commie: Now you're one of us. One of the great revolution. I expect your support when we gather. Caddie: When who gather? Commie: You will see Comrad. You will see. :He swings the hammer at the base of a tree and knocks it over with a single fell blow: Commie: Yes, I am ready. Come Comrad, we must teach the rich old bougruasis that we will no longer take it. Caddie: HELL YAH!
