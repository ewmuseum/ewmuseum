UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #793

Snap Back to Reality, Such as it is

29 Nov 2004

The Communist — The JCR Temple Garden

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist tends his garden. He has found peace in his life, in Jewdaism. He may be losing matches, but atlease he was enlightened and happy. The Rabbi comes walking out, a worried look on his face: Rabbi: Communist? How are you? I'm glad you're making use of the garden, but shouldn't you be training? I mean, you haven't exactly been doing a bang up job in the ring lately. Commie: Ah, Comrad, the ring isn't important. Wrestling is so violent and nonsensical. The Garden is so serene and peaceful. The way the bees and insects interact with the plants, and how the sun shines on them, giving its energy so that we may live. Truly, God is great. Rabbi: Friend Communist, the temple needs money and your Wrestling has been very helpful. We love having you here with us, growing spiritualy, but, truth be told, your unique talents are a part of who you are, and enightenment can be found even in the chaos of the ring. Sometimes we simple need to see our normal enviroments in a new light to find the enlightenment in them. Do you understand. Commie: I appreciate what you're trying to do Rabbi, but I am happy here, surrounded by the peace and the people that I love. All my life I've been a fighter, and now that I'm pulling away from that, I feel more at peace with myself, more confident that there is a God in my life and he cares for me. Rabbi: well... if that is your choice... :The Rabbi walks away and grabs one of the acolytes: Rabbi: Call the Soviette. :An Hour later...: :Soviette walks behind the peaceful, enlightend Communist, carrying a trash can full of fun in one hand, and a folding chair in the other. She drops the trash can with a clatter, getting the attention of the People's Enlightened Hero: Commie: Soviette! Have you joined the temple? :Soviette draws the chair over her head and bounces the steel off The Communist's head, busting him open and droping him into the forget-me-nots. She kicks him while he's down in the dirt: Soviette: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING! You've LOST YOUR MIND! :She Hits him again in the side, the arm, and the thigh: You've pretty much NO SHOWED your last two matches. WAKE UP COMMUNIST! YOU LOST TO DREAMAKER! YOU! The Ultimate Weapon of Hardcore! The Proletariot Champion! :She drops the chair on him and grabs the trash can, dumping the various improvised weapons on the ground: SO, you're enlightened now? Does that mean you don't feel pain? Commie: Well, no, it means OOOF! :Soviette drives a baseball bat into his ribcage: Soviette: We're going back to the Gym. You are going to win at Fandamonium. Or I am going to beat you to death. Go, NOW! :The Communist runs out to the car in the front. The Rabbi walks over to Soviette: Rabbi: So, we have a deal? Soviette: You'll get your cut, now back off. :She walks out to the car and drives away:
