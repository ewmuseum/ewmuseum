UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #785

what???

24 Nov 2004

Dreammaker — a quiet room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene cuts in to see Dreamaker, shirtless and in gym shorts, watching the latest SAS on a large TV. ) DM: Damn !! You scared the shit out of me. (AS he turns around and sees the camera.) Did you ... (reaches over and grabs his Minnesota Twins sleeveless t-shirt. And he starts to put it on) After reviewing some footage on my next opponent, I got bored, so I had to Watch SAS all over again. I mean BWahhh???!!! Man that was almost original. Same with all these matches. Same old slow power. Man, boy do I have to be quick to the punch. Maybe catch him sleeping? (AS he looks at the camera and smiles)!! I got 24 hours to get ready. Now go film some one else. Unless this is all you want. (AS Dreammaker takes a step back and does a pose showing off his muscle) Give me a break. I got shit to do. Gotta go get me some turkey and some Bubbles. (After saying that, A girl's sexy voice is heard) Bubbles: But Mr. Dream, I don't come in a bottle. I'm already open and poured out to be cherished. (AS we see Bubbles walk in the door holding to wine glasses wearing nothing but a towel. Which has a big 'DM' written in the middle of the towel. ) DM: Damn! (looks at the camera.. pauses .. and looks back at Bubbles. I guess its time for my pre game warm up. But first. (The camera follows Dreammakers finger pointing at Bubbles. ) Why dont you go get in the hot tub for some R and R. I'll be right there. (Bubbles walks starts to walk by and..) DM: Oh, is that my towel. Great, can you throw it here! (Bubbles drops the towel and we see the beautiful back side of her as she walks threw the door) (crowd watching promo-cheering!!!!) DM: (looks at the camera) That was for all my loyal fans out there. When you are looking for sports entertainment, you might just need to sit back.. (pause).. relax... (pause) .. and just start dreaming. Cuz dreams are my specialty. (As he turns around he drops his towel enough to see some cheek. Then stops and looks over his shoulder listening to the female crowd members yell. Then pulls up the towel and walks threw the door. ) (A laugh is heard from behind the door) BWAHAHAHAHAHAH!! (the scene fades)
