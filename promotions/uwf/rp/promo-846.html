Intro to Deuce

16 Dec 2004

Deuce — empty room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Deuce stands alone in darkened room; there is nothing in the room except a light hanging down from the ceiling. He stands before the camera with his head hanging low. 'It’s been a long time since I've seen my family. Ever since the day my parents were killed my whole family has disowned me. Do they truly believe that I murdered them? I don't know, but I don't care either! He pauses for a moment to collect his thoughts. 'My life was so much easier before that day, I had hopes and dreams, and I had my whole life before me. Then in an instant, it was all snatched away from me. And for what? For money, that’s what! Yes my parents were rich, but we didn't live like it. I went to public schools, went to the park to play with the neighborhood kids. Yes we had nice things but we weren’t show-offs.' He stops to wipe a tear from his cheek, and collect himself. 'Now I have nothing. It doesn’t bother me though. I've gotten used to having nothing. In prison you have to fight to keep the things you have. When I first got in prison I was not a fighter, so I had nothing. But I soon learned how to fight for what I had. Everything I have today I have fought for.' He now looks up at the camera to reveal his battered face. By no means is he ugly but from the scars it is evident that he has been in many brutal fights. 'I have never had things just handed to me, and this is no different. I intend to fight my way to the top of the ladder. I know it’s not gonna be an easy fight, but I believe it’s worth it. I have been out of prison for five years now, and each and every day has been a struggle. I'm not complaining, the things that I have been through make me who I am. Each day I have become a stronger person, and at the end each day I thank the higher power for the things I have been through.' The camera zooms out as he lowers his head. End secne.
