Halloweiners

Hot Mask — Normaltown, U.S.A.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a dark and spooky night in the quiet suburban paradise smack in the middle of Louisville. A mist quietly rose over the cobblestone walkway, spilling off into the beaten down paved road and covering the empty streetlit paths to houses decorated in orange and white with a mild fog. Ghosts and witches and goblins and kids in Harry Potter costumes (God, that is sooooo last season) littered the township, bringing the haunting hour in full effect! Creepy noises made by moving creatures could be heard in the distance -- dogs barking at the sight of a blinding white moon, owls perched atop branches hunting for prey, motors of minivans carrying kids door-to-door, and the incessant whining of spoiled brats who didn't get as much candy as their peers. Ladies and gentlemen, it's a good old fashioned Hallow's Eve in Normaltown, U.S.A. A cat, seemingly curious off in the distance, spots a couple in the street, parading incognito while holding standard issue plastic pumpkin candy buckets and seemingly plotting out their next move. On closer inspection, we recognize the two as none other than the Ultimate Wrestling Federation's own happy business relationship -- Hot Mask: the Sultan of Self-Semination and his manager, the lovely and severely misguided Skankalie. Hot Mask, decked out in wrestling garb consisting of full-length red tights with an exclamation point over the crotch region, white boots that adorn 'HM' on the sides, and a tattered red T-shirt scratches his head in thought while pointing toward a large white house. 'That one,' he states. Skankalie, mixing a blend of adorable and sexy in a somewhat revealing French pink poodle outfit with painted face and accessories in tact, nods her head. 'Works for me!' Skankalie, well-aware of the deception of Mask's plan to reap candy-a-plenty, seems excited at the mission at hand. 'All righty then,' states Mask, connotating a very serious tone. 'Remember your opening line?' 'Yep,' she responds, repeating the same solemn voice. The two slowly start across the desolate street, with destination set on the large white house coined with several carved pumpkins along the sidewalk path leading up to the green door enclosed by a columned overhang. However, their journey deems interrupted, as the black cat zooms right in front of the couple, giving a ferocious 'mew!' as it passes. Mask is somewhat startled by this turn of events, and immediately squeaks out a yelp of fear, dropping his bucket and clinging tightly to the safety of Skankalie. She gives a smile, and tries to console him. 'It's just a kitty, sweetheart. Did you get a little spooked?' Skankalie smiles as Mask retreats off, feeling somewhat embarrassed. 'No,' Mask replies. 'I just wanted to feel your boobs.' Padding the lie, he begins to rub her chest like a curious child, causing her to feel very uncomfortable. Naturally, the King of Ding-a-ling senses this and laughs it off, squeezing one of the breasts as hard as he can, causing Skankalie to let out a yelp of pain. 'Trick or TEET!' * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * DING-DONG! A somewhat pretty soccer mom-ish type almost immediately opens the door and greets the two twentysomethings with a smile that quickly disperses into shock when she comprehends the age that they appear to be. 'Hello, ma'am. This is my little brother, Jeffrey...and he has something he'd like to say to you,' Skankalie speaks with a charming smile. Grabbing Mask around the shoulder, she nudges him forward. 'Go ahead, Jeffrey!' 'TWICK UR TWEET!' Mask begins to giggle stupidly, slightly bouncing and holding his bucket forward, beckoning for candy. The woman, with an understanding show of warmth, gives a smile as she reaches into a bowl, pulling out a handful of bite-size Butterfinger bars. 'Here's some candy for the cutie poodle...and here's some for the Mexican wrestler!' Mask, biting his tongue and holding back a scowl, promptly interjects. 'NO I AM...HAWT MASKZ!' He adds to the flavor by flexing his muscle in a purposefully ridiculous pose. The woman hopelessly tries to play along, but it's somewhat obvious that she's not exactly a wrestling fan. 'Okay then, Mr. Hot Mask! Arriba, arriba los amigos!' she replies with a laugh. Skankalie, aware of the rising temperature gauge in his bloodstream, thinks quickly and prevents the fire from spreading. 'All right, Jeffrey, what do you say to the nice lady?' Reluctant, Hot Mask spits out a not-so-enthusiastic 'thank you...' to the woman as she closes the door. '...you fucking yeast infection.' Yes, the bitter agony of once again being passed off as something he's not cannot go undaunted, but, as the two begin to walk back down the sidewalk, his mood quickly shifts as he examines the contents of his candy bucket. 'SWEET!' Mask cries. 'BUTTERFINGERS!' However, instead of reaching inside his own bucket, Mask opts for the bucket of Skankalie, which he jerks away from her and pulls out four of the tiny morsels. Unwrapping them in a feverish hurry, he stuffs all four into his mouth and chews them up, smiling at Skankalie to reveal a rather grotesque chocolate-covered mouth. 'Cho-mmmph-licious!' A mouthful of cavity-causing goodies is certainly a Kodak moment, as Skankalie shrugs off the blatant bogarting of her stash and laughs to herself. * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * The next stop seemed a tad bit less exotic than the previous home dwelled in by stereotypical upper-middle class family. The small ranch home, littered with tacky kitsch related to fall -- cardboard turkey cutouts and the like, did not look very Halloween-spirited. However, Mask had gotten his first taste of victory, and like any power-hungry martyr, would be virtually unstoppable on his quest for more sugar. DING-DONG! Seconds pass, and the door of the somewhat smaller house slowly begins to creep ajar. As the mousey white-haired woman quietly peered outside with frames of her horn-rimmed glasses covering most of her face, Mask and Skankalie stood with hopeful glares. HM was already hamming up the Down's persona, having one arm cocked in front with elbow bent at a 45 degree angle. With a squeaky hinge as her accompaniment, the old frail woman slowly opened the door and examined her guests with curiosity. 'Trick or treat!' The couple rang out in unison, with Mask putting some extra emphasis on the syllables. However, it seemed undecipherable as to whether or not she was a bit senile or just wasn't buying the act. Hot Mask then tried to sell it a bit more. 'GUESS HOW OLD I AM I AM THIS MANY, ' Mask stuttered, holding up eight fingers and dropping his bucket of candy in the process. With faux frustration, he bends over slowly and reaches for the handle, bringing the pumpkin back up and sheepishly remarking with a grin, 'I DWOPPED IT!' The woman slowly reaches out her hands to both Skankalie and Hot Mask, somewhat freaking them out. 'God bless the both of you, such beautiful children! Thank you so much for stopping by to see me...no one ever comes to my house on Halloween.' 'I can see why...' Mask interjects under his breath. 'What was that, Sonny?' The lady pressed her ear forward. 'I'm a tad bit hard of hearin' in my old age,' explained the woman in a sweet and soothing voice. 'UM BOOGERS AREN'T SUPPO-SET TO BE EATING!' Horrible cover-up, as Skankalie even shoots him a look of disbelief. 'Oh.' She nods, knowingly. 'Well, I have JUST the treat for you two special and blessed individuals! Here you go...' Granny reaches back, and when she comes once again to the door with a toothless grin crossed over her face, she holds in her hand...two pocket-sized copies of the New Testament. With a satisfied look on her face, she drops the two mini-Bibles into the buckets, and the halo above her head began to glow brighter than the moon above. 'What...the...FUCK?' The halo suddenly disappears as Mask is fuming with anger. 'What in the goddamn crispy crucified CHRIST am I supposed to do with this? Use the thin sheets to wipe my ass with? Lady, you got a LOTTA FUCKING NERVE TO BE...OOOOOOH!' Mask is interrupted by a quick retaliation from the old lady, who at the first mention of the GD-bomb grabbed for the nearest thing possible...which just happened to be a glass Mason jar containing water and her dentures...and clocked him over the head with it, sending him crashing to the grass below. The dentures clung tightly to Mask's nose, and the impact of the blow...causes Mask...to...fall...a...sleeeeeeeppp... * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * Dream sequence? Or just creatively-placed sub-promo? YOU MAKE THE CALL... A dark cavelike room with lighting extremely dim is where Hot Mask finds himself, flat on his ass and rubbing his eyes to adjust to the darkness. 'Owww...wher...where am I?' Quickly dropping his hand to his crotch as a natural instinct, he gives a sigh of relief as he pats the exclamation point. 'Hello, Hot Mask.' 'WHAAAAAAAAAAAA?' Mask quickly jumps to his feet, alarmed by the eerie and echoing voice that he hears in the distance. 'Who's there? Wha? Wher? Whe? Why? Who-who-who-who-who ARE YOU?' 'Hey dudemeister,' a much happier voice responds, as out from the shadows we see the keeper of this voice...who appears to look somewhat similar to the 'surfin'' Native American off that one episode of Saved By The Bell where Zack had to do an oral report on one of his heroes! Remember that one? It's where Zack was getting ready for the state track meet and...eh, you remember. Anyway, the Native American comes out, dressed in pretty typical Indian garb...but something stands out about this man. Actually, a very BIG something stands out about this man... 'You have a...dongersaurus...on your HEAD,' Mask states the obvious. 'Damn bitch put acid in those Butterfingers, I knew it.' 'Whoa, calm down Maskman,' the Indian responds. 'I'm only a dream...an image to send you an important message about the future!' 'Then why do you have a fucking cock on your head?' Mask quips. 'Hey, it's better than having one permanently lodged up my ass!' the Indian responds. The both share a hysterical laugh, as Mask begins to warm up to the individual, curious about the 'future' that he speaks of. 'I'm curious about this 'future' you speak of. What's going to happen to me, Chief Peterhead?' Mask somehow knew the man's name, but the man had not even told him! This shocked Mask, but he brushed it off as he listened to the words of wisdom from Chief Peterhead with patience and awe. 'Hot Mask, you are curious about your future...and I am here to inform you. This week, you have the golden opportunity to win a very coveted prize in the Ultimate Wrestling Federation...the TELEVISION TITLE!' The Indian wings and nudges Mask's arm with glee. 'Okay, maybe the Television title sucks a lot of rancid tit, but Mask...you MUST win it, for the sake of UWF viewers everywhere!' The Indian pauses, allowing Mask to obtain a response. 'But why, Chief Peterhead? Why must I win this rattan piece of tin that I'd rather recycle for three bucks at the local scrap yard? Why must I obtain this litterbox liner?' 'It is simple, my son,' the Chief retorts. 'You are the Great Bigdicked Hope.' 'I am the Great Bigdicked Hope?' 'Yes!' exclaims Chief Peterhead. 'You are the Great Bigdicked Hope!' 'Awesome...but what does it all mean, Chief?' 'It is simple. Although the UWF viewers may not respect you, not unlike the comedic genius of one deceased Jim Varney, people cannot help but admire the way you capture the audience! Let's face it, Mask. You are ratings,' he explains. 'Yeah! I AM ratings!' Mask agrees. 'No one else compares to your grasp of wit and your amazing vocabulary when it comes to synonyms of the female genitalia! You are sexy, talented, and all-around amazing. But WHY the Television title, Mask?' Chief ponders rhetorically. 'I'll tell you why. The Television title is defended on a weekly basis, and if some boring ass masked patriot is holding it, people will end up watching...' 'No, don't say it! DON'T EVEN SAY IT...' Mask cringes. '...Hip Hop Countdown on MTV.' NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOkay you get the picture. 'And even furthermore...if you DON'T win the title on Wednesday Night...even worse than Joshua Justice being champion, the UWF may forever be represented by...' quips Chief, building the suspense. '...a guy who has to pay women to fuck him! Mask, you are the Great Bigdicked Hope, and you must triumph!' 'I must triumph,' Mask repeats to himself, as the image of Chief Peterhead slowly begins to fizzle away in the background. 'I must triumph...must triumph...must...' * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * '...triumph...must...tri...AHHHHHHHHHHH!' Mask awakes from the dream to look up to a disgusting shot of the old woman, standing over him with hands on her hips and revealing the inside of her long skirt. Mask can see the Depends out of the corner of his eye, and immediately leaps up, screaming in terror! 'Get the fuck off my lawn you damn luchadore!' yells the woman with a firey rage. Mask runs away, but not before kicking over a 'Ten Commandments' display in the lawn and stepping on it with full force. Reaching a stop at the road next to Skankalie and almost out of breath, he stumbles over his words... 'Bitch...stole...our...candy...stupid...ass...hole...' 'HEY, MY MOM SAYS NOT TO USE DIRTY WORDS!' What? A little kid, dressed as the white Power Ranger, pipes up next to the couple. Mask looks down, somewhat startled, and grows a huge smile on his face. 'BOOGABOOGABOOGA! I EAT KIDS!' Mask jumps at the little boy, giving creepy hand motions and jumping up and down like a deranged African native. The boy screams in terror, dropping his grocery sack chock full of delicious GOODIES~! and runs off. Mask laughs aloud, picks up the sack, and pulls out a lollipop, unwrapping it with care. 'I am the Great Bigdicked Hope!'
