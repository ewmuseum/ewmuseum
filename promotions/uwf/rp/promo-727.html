UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #727

paying up

1 Oct 2004

Dreammaker — backstage area

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to the backstage area of some arena. Located outside a locker room a cameraman bumps into dreammaker as he is leaving the room). DM: Perfect timing like usual. Follow me please. (the camerman follows dreammaker as they make their way down the hall) DM: Feel free to get all of the wrestlers' pictures on this because it is a great day. (We see many different wrestlers looking and nodding at the camera) Yesterday we got everything we can imagine. I got my match my way. The crowd was loving it and all i can think about is tying white wolf up on his leash to the cage walls. I mean think about it. He belongs in a cage doesn't he? Im just doing society a favor. Hey hold on! (just then raid walks around the corner) Come on over here(Raid walks up close to Dreammaker). Here is your money i owe you. (Raid looks around over his shoulder and then smiles). Come by later and I'll have another one for ya. (Raid smiles and walks away not even saying a single word). (Dreammaker then starts whistling as he walks around some more) IT is gonna be so sweet finally getting my first win and its on PPV. I mean people are gonna pay to see me put White Wolf back on the endangered list, permantly. (Just then Fizzban walks around the corner and DReammaker stops him by waving him down) DM: Fizzy so glad to run into you. Hey nice match teh other night. Beating up on whats his name , Oh ya, Little Dicky! I sure need to run into him again. Any ways i gotta get going, so here is some tickets to a place I own up the road from here. Fizzy: What are they for and why do you care about my matches? DM: Lets just say that I like your approach on life. And these tickets I know you will enjoy. Every body should get something for winng right? (Dreammaker then hands over some tickets to Fizzy and then walks away) Dm: Man it sure feels good giving today. Maybe tomorrow I'll have something for the audience. (He stops and looks at the camera) You can scram now because I have business to attend to.(AS dreamaker looks over his shoulder and we see Raid sitting at a table in the corner of the weight room. Cans of beer on the tablt and some playing cards in his hands. ) The camera fades out as Dreammaker opnes the weight room door and enters the room)
