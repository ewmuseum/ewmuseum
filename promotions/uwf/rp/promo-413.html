UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #413

Last Chance

15 May 2004

Adien Pryde — Empty Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to inside an empty arena. Down by the ring is a stretcher with a man sitting on it. He is wearing black jeans and a red hooded sweatshirt with the hood up. Best guess is that this is Adien Pryde, challenger to The Hardcore Championship, currently held by Nick Persia, this Sunday in a Stretcher Match.] Pryde : So it comes to this Nick Persia, my final shot at your THC, Sunday at Supremacy. For over a month you have held that title now, quite an accomplishment in its own right, longest since Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. [Pryde looks up at the camera.] Pryde : Last Sunday I reveled to the world that you were working here in the USA without a current work visa. Where you are now is anybodies guess. The officials back stage have not seen or heard from you since that day. That means one of two things have happened. One, you were deported back to that country of yours Holland, or two, you are still being held for more questioning. Either way you have not been heard from. [Pryde stands and walks around to the corner where he climbs the steps and climbs into the ring.] Pryde : Now I can understand that you would want to work for this great fed, the UWF, but you need to realize we have laws in this country to protect its citizens so that we have a place of employment. If people keep coming in to this country illegally and take these jobs from us, it will only hurt our country. [Pryde walks around the ring arms stretched out.] Pryde : This is where I do my job, in this very ring, and I take it dead serious. I called those officers to teach you a lesson, a lesson that I will drive home this Sunday. No one makes a fool of Pryde, the UWF, and especially the United States of America. [Pryde pauses to collect his thoughts.] Pryde : A Hardcore Stretcher Match. No pin fall, no submission, the only way to win is by beating your opponent down to the point where they are helpless. Then and only then you can put their bloody, lifeless body on the stretcher and strap them down for the win. I will relish the thought of breaking you and bloodying your Dutch ass to the point where your family won’t recognize you. This is my chance to gain my first gold in this fed and I will not let it slip though my fingers again. [Pryde walks over to the ropes closes to the camera and leans over to stare directly into it and it zooms in.] Pryde : I will take that title from you by any means necessary. [The scene fades to black.]
