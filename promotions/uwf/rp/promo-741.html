UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #741

Saying Goodbye

4 Oct 2004

Joshua Justice — Ohio

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It’s a windy day in Ohio. A woman named Meg Connelly is standing outside on the sidewalk sweeping. She feels like someone is behind her. When she turns around, Joshua Justice is standing there. She turns around and continues to sweep.] Meg: I’ve been expecting you. Frankly, I’m surprised it took so long. Joshua: I didn’t know if I should look for you or not, Meg. And I’ve been… busy. Meg: Joshua busy? Or Joshua Justice busy? Joshua: I wanted to tell you, Meg. But it’s not that easy. Meg: Of course not. [Joshua goes to put his hand on her shoulder, but she turns towards him and scowls.] Meg: We date, get close, try to build a relationship and be honest… But you can’t tell me about your other life because it’s complicated! [She turns completely around and puts her hand on her hip with pure attitude.] Meg: Can’t you see how hard it is to fall in love with someone who hides such an important part of who he is? It’s like dating a guy who turns out to be married or is an ex-con or something! Joshua: Please. Be fair… [Joshua places his hand on her cheek.] Meg: A couple is nothing without trust, Joshua. The simple truth—is that you didn’t trust that I would accept you… [Meg sits down on her steps in front of her house as a tear starts to fall down her cheek.] Joshua: My turn? Meg: Go ahead. Joshua: I have lots of friends who don’t know I’m Joshua Justice, Meg. Justice was created in secrecy and privacy years ago. I wore a mask for the first few years of my career. It’s a secret I don’t share easily because it comes with so much baggage. The responsibility of people knowing that you know who I know I am… [Meg just looks at him confused.] Joshua: I have lots of enemies. Dicky Flint probably wants to put a burning cross on your front step… Meg: …burning cross…? Joshua: Nevermind. [Joshua moves down to console her.] Meg: I’ve thought about this a great deal, Joshua. There’s one question that I’d like to know. One answer that might still give me a reason to hope. Joshua: Go ahead. [Meg looks up to him.] Meg: When you think of yourself, your daily life, who are you wand what you do… Do you picture yourself as just Joshua or Joshua Justice? [Joshua looks at her while he thinks. Then a sorrowful look enters his eyes.] Joshua: I wish… I could give you the answer you’re looking for, Meg. [Meg hugs him and then starts to walk away.] Meg: Thank you for being honest. Joshua: In a way, I’m being honest with myself for the first time in a long time. [Meg picks up the broom that she had dropped.] Meg: I wish you all the best, Joshua. [She turns her back and starts to walk away.] Meg: I’ve always sensed a certain loneliness in you and I hope you conquer that. Instead of Joshua looking for a relationship, maybe it’s Justice who should. After all, that’s who you really are. [Joshua puts his hand on her shoulder.] Joshua: Will you be all right. [Meg rubs his hand with her cheek.] Meg: Yes. I passed the bar and have already opened my law office. I’ll be fine. Joshua: Good. I’m glad to hear it. I’m sure there are lots of people who can use a lawyer who can’t be beat. [She turns around and hugs him.] Meg: You think I’m that good? Joshua: I know so. Take care of yourself, Meg. If you ever need anything, call. Meg: You too. In the meantime… [She pushes off of him.] Meg: …keep the world safe for Democracy, okay? Joshua: Will do. [Meg walks up her steps and opens her front door.] Joshua: Thanks, Meg. Meg: For what? Joshua: For helping me realize who I really am. [Meg closes her door as Joshua turns around and starts to walk down the empty street. He drops the rose that he was holding behind his back this whole time. He starts to mutter just loud enough to hear.] Joshua: Slasher… Alex Grey… Rugg Ryder… Seraphiend… Bryn Jaye… Dicky Flint… I don’t know all of you and I don’t really care who most of you are. All I know is that you are all bad people and deserve to be brought to justice. By my hands at Live And Loud 4, you will all be brought to pure justice. [He looks down at his hands.] Joshua: You will feel pain and run. You will try to get away, but you shall not run. You will be beaten into a bloody pulp. That I promise… If I am to be myself… I must keep my promises… [He continues to walk down the street as the wind blows leaves all around him.]
