He looks around his surroundings. The air feel thick and oppressive, it hurts to even breathe it. Weird, nightmarish figures walk a bizarre landscape of stone. There is heat everywhere, inescapable And worst of all is the unending echoes of millions of empty souls begging and pleading, willing to debase themselves in any foul manner just to rise up from their putrid holes the slightest bit. Oni: Huh. When did I come to Los Angeles? Rising to his feet, Tsunami Oni starts to walk around. He sees a familiar looking gentleman in a suit coming towards him. Oni: Hey… aren’t you Ronald Reagan? Reagan: Aw shucks, you don’t have to call me “Mr. President.” Oni: Uh… I didn’t. Reagan: Oh yeah. Guess you didn’t. Well, call me Ronnie, anyways. Oni: What are you doing in LA? Aren’t you like dead or something? Reagan: Well, son. I hate to tell you this, but this isn’t LA. This is Hell. Oni: Hell? That makes sense. I was wondering how I could be in LA and not seen a Gap or Starbucks yet. Wait a minute… This isn’t LA… you’re dead… damn it! It was that Suzuki-Von Doom Romantic Ramjet 6900, wasn’t it? Reagan: That’s not the one that got me. Oni: Fine, I give up. Reagan: “Bedtime for Bonzo.” Oni: What? Reagan: Well, I guess the Big Guy really hated that movie. Oni: I can see that. Reagan: I really worry about Warren Beatty and Dustin Hoffman when they kick the bucket. Word is He is STILL riled up over “Ishtar.” Oni: At least you don’t seem to be getting tortured too badly. Reagan: Well, I got 666 scorpions right up my wazoo. Hurts like the dickens. Oni: Ouch. So why’d you come talk to me? Reagan: Well, before you get sent to a level of Hell, ol’ Satan has to sentence you. So off we go, little cowpoke! And off they went. It wasn’t long before they reached Satan’s palace of brimstone. It rose from the plain of Hell like… Oni: Hey! That looks just like Sauron’s tower in “Lord of the Rings!” Reagan: Sorry, didn’t see it. Oni: It does! Satan totally ripped off “Lord of the Rings!” Reagan: Well, you might want to keep that tidbit to yourself, young buckeroo. Oni: What did it look like before? The Death Star? Reagan: Shhh… They enter Satan’s palace. Ronald Reagan and Tsunami Oni make their way though caverns and hallways. The sounds of torture echo through the entire structure. It isn’t long before they enter a colossal chamber. All throughout are demons of all sort… fiends, hell hounds, imps, and succubi. Hee hee… “succubi!” What? You’re back again? I wanted to grab some grub in the cafeteria. It’s Salisbury steak day. Hey, could you imagine nailing a chick with wings? Talk about Mile High Club! Get out! Go eat your Salisbury steak! Chill! I’m gone! At the center of this unholy chamber of horrors is a massive iron throne, cover in images most vile. And on this throne sits none other than this Prince of Darkness… Satan. His massive red skin body ripped with diabolic might. His eyes burning with fiendish thoughts unspeakable. Black horns jutting forth from his skull, like they could pierce the very soul of existence. And a tail with a little pointy fork at the end. Satan: Is this the latest soul sent to swell the ranks of the damned? Reagan: Well, you got it right the button there, chief! Satan: Yoshihiro Yamada! Also known as Tsunami Oni! What say ye before you are sentence to your own personal torment to last for all eternity? Oni: Uh, don’t I get like a hearing or a lawyer or something? Satan: Yeah, sure. What the hell. Nothing else going on. The Prince of Darkness snaps his fingers and in a puff of smoke stands a middle-aged man in an outdated suit. Stan: Your lawyer. Oni: Wow! Clarence Darrow! Bet you’re here because of that whole Scopes trail and evolution hubbub. Darrow: Afraid so. Oni: You know, Ronnie’s here because of “Bedtime for Bonzo.” Maybe God just doesn’t like chimps. Hey, is Satan going to get a lawyer? Darrow: The Prince of Lies? He’s what every attorney aspires to be. Satan: Silence! The trial of Tsunami Oni begins now! Reagan! Read the first charge! The former president reads from an ominous looking scroll written in blood. Reagan: Well, it’s extreme cruelty including using a fellow human being as a golf tee and instigating something called “the Evilutionary War.” Oni: I can explain all that. Where’s that card? I always have it… here it is. Tsunami Oni holds a card out to Satan. Satan: You are just wearing a swimsuit. Where did you get that from? Oni: Do you really want to know? Satan: Just tell me what it is. Oni: It’s my Professional Sports Entertainer’s card. It says I’m free from prosecution for events happening in a pro wrestling environment. Darrow: That immunity is not limited to earthly courts. And all those charges occurred under the aegis of the Universal Wrestling Federation. Satan: Damn! You got us there! What’s the next charge, Gipper? Reagan! Aw shucks! I love it when people still call me that! Satan: Just read! Reagan: Well, the next charge is encouraging the worship of false gods. He insists on referring to his fans as “Devil-worshippers.” Satan: Ah ha! Wait a minute? I’m the Devil! Isn’t that copyright infringement? Darrow: Well, I don’t think that counts as a sin. He takes a bamboo staff and wooden bowl out from a basket on the front of the bicycle, then he turns to the Prince of Lies and gives a big, cheerful grin.
