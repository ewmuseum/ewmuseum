My ETD rp#1

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To give it your all, to not hang your head when you have been defeated but to raise your head high, look your opponent in the eye and acknowledge their win, that is what I had always been taught. When you lose it is not about you, no matter how much you hurt in side it is never about you. It was the victor’s time. You could go home and cry yourself to sleep, lick your wounds and return to fight another day, but the winner just has that one chance to celebrate. To rise onto those turnbuckles, thrust their title into the air and when the crowd reaches its crescendo you will know one thing. That you are the best. So why was it that at this very moment in time, all I can think about is how I should react when I fail. I know what the daunting task ahead of me is. A fresh champion who will be more than willing to spill blood in order to keep it, whether it will be mine or his still remains to be seen. I know I have a chance, possibly the best chance that I am every going to get yet still in the back of my mind is that fear. You don’t deserve the shot. You are going to lose. This is your ONLY opportunity. You will lose John… Moraless’ hand stopped moving over the paper, as he dropped the black biro within it on the table. Letting out a brief sigh he rose to his feet and looked at the journal in front of him, his eyes focusing on the last sentence that he had written. The words in front of him were a sure example of his fate, he had determined it himself. Moraless turned away from the book and walked towards the window, staring out at the city below him, the hustle and bustle of life thriving. He pulled the shutters apart giving himself a better view over the city’s landscape. Streetlights illuminating the asphalt below whilst a particularly eerie moon lights the remainder of the night sky. His mind was racing with thoughts as he stared through the glass, a cool breeze blowing his face, feeling almost omnipotent from the viewpoint, anything but that in his mind. Moraless was going to do battle with Sean McClancy, he was going to take his one shot that he had and he was going to lose in a blaze of glory. Moraless paced up and down his study, his eyes transfixed on the journal, no worry within the eyes, simply regret for the past few months his life had been leading up to this moment. In the Universal Wrestling Federation at the moment it seemed as though he could do no wrong, the resurrection was thriving as stable wars approached, a tag team title belt hung high above his dresser, he had the momentum, yet there was something missing. The self-confidence. It had always been a problem, from day one Moraless had always doubted that he could ever amount to anything, even when things were going well. He would pass them off as fluke or blame it on the other competitor’s inability as opposed to his own true ability. Yet he had always tried to hide it. When he was wrestling he oozed a certain charisma that drew the crowd to him, whether it was a love or hate relationship it didn’t matter. He could always wind a crowd round his little finger and pull the right strings. That was all for the crowd though, he was almost as good an actor as a wrestler. Almost. Moraless turned and sat down at the desk once more, taking the pen in his hand, rubbing his hand across his face as the pen slowly began to move across the paper. It happened to me the other day, I was walking down the street. It was nothing special, literally just a normal day. I had gone food shopping and was on the way to the gym when it happened. It was in an alley, I only really caught a glimpse of it out of the corner of my eye, a struggle maybe I thought to myself. I remember stopping in the street turning to watch the incident unfold in the secluded corner of suburbia, three men, surrounding another man, punching him, kicking him. I wanted to move, just leap forwards to join the fray but I couldn’t, my body simply wouldn’t let me. The thoughts were dashing around my head about what would happen to him if I simply turned and walked away, if I had left the man to be beaten, possibly to death. And I still wanted to leave. I know at the end of the day I am a coward. Even though I knew what could happen to the man, I wanted to leave him fearing for myself and what might happen to me if I entered the fray. Does that make me a bad person? Moraless’ pen stopped moving again, his eyes scanning the words he had written, looking for an answer to his questions deep within his mind, focusing intensely, trying to find some truth, whether he wanted to hear it or not. I don’t know. What does it matter anyway, it’s not like I did abandon him in the end, it was just that. I wanted to and it unnerved me you know, the fact that I could be so heartless. He could have been a fan, someone who looked up to guys like me, strived to be like me. How could I have lived with myself if I had simply turned away? I didn’t want to know, I didn’t want to be that person. Which was why it was so hard to walk away. Its true I left him. I took one step forwards and then another, slowly but surely taking steps to make sure that I would not see what was happening behind me, so that I could go to sleep that night and close my eyes, pretend it was all a dream. It was then I heard the scream. I knew straight away someone had seen what I had saw, the cries for help were becoming louder as I began to up my tempo, my walk became brisker as I went to turn the corner, trying desperately to put some sort of distance, between me and the incident. To most people I would probably have looked like a guilty party myself. It was then I caught a brief glimpse of myself, just a small reflection in a pane of glass covering a clothes store, but it was enough to make me stop and just look at myself. I saw my face, the scar that ran down my right cheek to my chin and I realised that I was no longer the man I used to be. Moraless lightly brushed his right hands fingers down his cheek, running along the line of the scar, his eyes closed tight as the memories of the past flooded his mind. It was then I realised why I didn’t want to help the man, the scar on my face. The one true time anyone but myself had inflicted any major pain on myself, it occurred during a mugging. It’s funny how you can spend years, repressing memories only for them to resurface at the times you don’t want them to. As I stood transfixed at the scar I realised for the first time why I hid away from the world, why I wanted solitude. I was scared. I was so very scared of being hurt, whether it was emotionally or physically, the one flick of the blade that had permanently scarred my body had apparently scarred my soul, my very being. I then turned back and headed towards the scene of the crime. I don’t know why, possibly the realisation of why I had turned away in the first place, but adrenaline was coursing through me, it was as though I had come to terms with my past, with who I was. I entered the alleyway, barging my way through a crowd of people who were simply watching as I had done, maybe I wasn’t the strange one after all. No one else would do anything either. I strode into that alley, and I hurt them. I hurt them as they had hurt the defenceless innocent who was bleeding at their very feet. The numbers didn’t seem to matter, they were flustered, shocked at the idea that someone would actually come to the mans aid. Their moves were noticeable from miles away and could be counted so easily I barely had to make any form of offence. Eventually they ran away, the crowd that had gathered was cheering but as I looked down at the man who had been attacked I could not help but loathe myself. Yet this was different, it was no longer me hating myself for no reason, for an incident that occurred years ago that was beyond my control. I loathed myself this time because I had been a coward. Moraless stopped writing and moved his hand across the desk over to a pile of paper sifting through them until he found one letter in particular, his eyes scanning over the text, placing the paper back down on the desk, just next to the journal, Moraless allowed himself a small smile before nodding and continuing to write. His parents sent me a letter, thanking me for my actions. Although when he had left he looked like a bloody mess apparently the wounds were mostly superficial, a lot of cuts and bruises and a few cracked ribs. Nothing that wouldn’t mend with time. The letter said that if I had not decided to risk my own life then their son may not have had his and that I would always have a special place in their hearts. Right at the bottom of the letter though there were four words, which really reached me though, four words that made all the pain go away, the pain of the past, which had haunted me for so long. You saved his life. When I read those words, I broke down and cried. They did not realise it and they probably never would do but their son had actually saved mine. I don’t believe in fate as such but I know that certain things happen for certain reasons. Seventeen years I had lived in a shell within myself fearing that it would always be that way. Until the life of another was put at risk. I know I’m no hero I’m just a guy who was able to do the right thing, I found myself. This is why I’m writing this now, a new beginning. So that when things get rough I can look back on this and realise who I actually am. I could have turned my back on him but I didn’t I came through and that alone will make me stronger than any amount of training will ever do. I am John Edwards a man who no longer needs to hide under the shackles of a fake name. From this moment I begin anew. Moraless turned back the journal a few pages and scanned over the words that he had written. You don’t deserve the shot. You are going to lose. This is your ONLY opportunity. You will lose John… His pen scribbled frantically over the paper before he finished and rose to his feet. Stretching he looked out of the window, the sun was beginning to rise over the landscape, the night had disappeared such as the moon had. It was a new day and a new beginning. A new John Edwards. He then turned and left the room a smile on his face, a natural smile one that was no longer forced, closing the door behind him. The journal remained open however it had been changed the pessimistic words that had been altered or completely erased, unlike actions words can always be changed. You worked hard for this title shot you’ve earned it. Who cares if you lose, as long as you give it your all that’s all you can possibly ask for? This is NOT your ONLY opportunity, you are still considered with high esteem in the division. John…the match will only be decided, by what you do.
