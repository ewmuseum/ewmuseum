Yo quiero El Baianos madre!

23 Oct 2004

Hot Mask — Taco Bell: Look Outside The Bun!

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A rather clean cut fast food dining establishment is our opening setting, which is a rather rare occurrence at any nationwide Taco Bell -- but nonetheless, our camera fades in from black. Walking forward, we see a familiar sight from our previous mountain rendezvous dressed in a pair of Diesel jeans, black Asics, and a black hooded sweatshirt with the logo of grindcore band Pig Destroyer in white lettering across the chest. With sparkly red-and-white mask in tact, UWF newcomer Hot Mask greets the camera with a grab and squeeze of his twig and berries and a 'point-and-shoot' gesture to the lens.] [Next to him is an unfamiliar individual to UWF viewers, a gorgeous young short-haired blond with beautiful dough eyes...wearing a potato sack? Yes fans, this is the slightly misled sweetheart known only by the name of Skankalie, Hot Mask's own personal valet/slave who's reasoning behind her putting up with his consistent abuse remains a mystery to us all.] [The cameraman pulls back a bit, giving us a side view of the order exchange. Behind the counter stands a pimply-faced teen with greasy black hair covered in a standard-issue Taco Bell cap. With a smile, the teen greets the couple.] Taco Bell Employee: 'Welcome to Taco Bell, can I help you?' Hot Mask: 'You can sure as hell try. Tell me, is it true that your Zesty Chicken Border Bowls are made fresh, right when I order?' Taco Bell Employee: 'Yes, sir! It's made with only the freshest of ingredients right when you order!' [Mask gives a small scratch to his chin, and smiles.] Hot Mask: 'Okay then, I'll have a Zesty Chicken...' [Movement from behind the counter reveals a somewhat portly and plump young woman with a plastic bowl clutched in her right hand, ready for action! However, she stops short in Mask's mid-sentence, shooting him a look of confusion.] Hot Mask: '..a Zesty Chi...ick...en...' [The two employees seem perplexed.] Hot Mask: 'A Zesty...' [Skankalie interjects with a sheepish grin on her face. Awww. What a cutie!] Skankalie: 'We'll take two...sorry...' [Mask turns abruptly, bringing the parody to a screeching halt.] Hot Mask: 'The hell you say? Get up out my business, leech woman!' [HM points a sharp elbow to the arm of Skankalie, who winces in pain and appears to have tears welling up at her eyes. Mask gives out a laugh of satisfaction, then pulling with a backhand motion, threatens to slap her. She cowers back, which only makes him laugh harder. Meanwhile, the employees just stare on with complete and utter shock at how much of a jackass this masked jerk is.] Hot Mask: 'We'll take ten tacos. And hey, Tubby...try not to eat the tacos before you put them on the tray, eh?' [This causes the boy at the counter to give off a chuckle. Mask gives him a look, and the look is returned with a 'What?' type of facial expression.] Hot Mask: 'Funny guy, aren't I, Junior? Tell you what, after the natural disaster over there finishes up my food, try not to let any of that dandruff in the pubic mop of yours spill over the Grade F meat. Mask doesn't take kindly to Parmesan on his tacos.' [With a very irritated look, the kid rings up the order hastily, and biting his tongue to have a shot at the next Employee Of The Month award, politely gives Mask his total.] Taco Bell Employee: 'That'll be 8.95, sir.' [Mask hands the kid a ten dollar bill, slipping it to him gingerly.] Hot Mask: 'Keep the change. Buy yourself some Head and Shoulders or somethin'.' ----------------------------------- [SCENE BREAK! Now, at a cute little Taco Bell dine-in table, our camera throws a passing glance at the fat girl taking a standing break while business remains slow. Her eyes are fixated on Mask, giving him a stare of death as we tilt and fill with a shot of HM and Skankalie dining on the taco banquet! In mid crunch, Mask takes a look at the camera and addresses the people with a mouthful of food.] Hot Mask: 'You know, there's not really much point to filming me right now. I'm eating a fucking taco, for Christ's sake. Not exactly 'high ratings' material. Big Ted's Arm is NO WHERE TO BE FOUND...it's not interesting whatsoever. Just a taco. With meat. And cheese. And low-grade lettuce.' [The cameraman nods, along with the camera to simulate audience participation.] Hot Mask: 'Jesus. I hate this place already.' [An interruption! Maybe we are at this random Taco Bell after all, for two kids just happen to walk by and point out our masked fiend and his object of lust dining at the table. The first kid who pipes up, appears to be about the age of 11 and wearing an oversized Limp Bizkit T-shirt with messy blonde hair and braces on his teeth, addresses his friend next to him.] Blonde Kid: 'Look, Tommy...it's one of 'dem masked Mexican immigrant wrestlers!' [His friend, who appears to be slightly older with the same moppy hairdo resting atop his head and sporting baggy jeans and a Slipknot T-shirt, smacks his friend upside the head.] Tommy: 'Saying 'Mexican immigrant' is offensive, you assbutt~! The proper name is 'luchadore.' [to Mask] Sorry about that, Mr. El Luchadore, we aren't like...bigots or something. Billy here is in the retard class at school because he's RETARDED and has mental disorders and is a rejection from society.' [Mask shakes his head.] Hot Mask: 'Whatever. Scram.' [Mask then raises his fist in an angry gesture, but rather than scaring the kids off, they casually maintain their pace and soon disappear off camera. With a gaze to the lens and a scowl, he finishes off the last of his taco and adjusts in his seat to face the camera head on.] Hot Mask: 'You know, it's almost impossible nowadays for a professional masked wrestler with a killer tan to go into a Mexican dining establishment without being stereotyped as a luchadore. Hell, I can't even make a decent fucking entrance without some poonjob automatically shooting his smegma and assuming that I'm going to hit the ring and perform 800 flips in the span of a minute. 'You hear that, viewing audience I could give a rat's ass about? HOT MASK DOES NOT GO 'ARRIBA ARRIBA UNDULE!' Hot Mask doesn't do triple gainer somersault with a half-twist presses, or rama-lama-ding-dong plancha super flying arm-to-leg ultra magic tope suicida splashes. Hot Mask doesn't endorse cruising around in low-riders or working in chicken factories for less than minimum wage, and having 8 brothers in the same wrestling promotion along with me. Hot Mask hates guacamole, and Hot Mask carries the hugest penis alive.' [A scratch of his balls, as Mask presses on with his slightly derogatory monologue.] Hot Mask: 'Stereotypes. The world is full of them. Sure, some of them have their merit and more often than not hold up in real life situations. For example: all women being morons. Look at this one...' [The camera pans to Skankalie, who is merely trying to take the cheese off of her taco. She doesn't like cheese very much. Why she tags around with Mask is still a mystery.] Hot Mask: 'Have you ever seen anything dumber in your life? Of course not. Another...the stereotype that all homosexuals are incredibly annoying. Ever see the show 'Queer Eye For The Straight Guy?' Case rested. 'All boy bands don't hold a candle to the original boy band, New Kids On The Block. Anyone who listens to and enjoys popular music as much as I can tell you that this one is true. 'Let's not also forget that if one has big feet, then the size of their package is also accounted for in this largeness. Some of the more mentionable individuals with big feet...Shaquille O'Neal, Bigfoot, and Hot Mask all have incredibly large liver turners to boot. Mark up another one for stereotypes, people!' [Mask, with hand still on his berries after a hearty scratch, makes even more light of his crotch by pointing towards it. The camera takes note.] Hot Mask: 'But a guy wearing a mask and eating tacos being a luchadore? Give me a break. This one holds about as much credibility as Lyle Wile's reputation in the adult film industry. And speaking of THAT Lavender Boy...' [BINGO. I know you all were looking for that ever-present TRANSITIONAL SENTENCE~! Mask continues his speech, as if this scene bracket never happened!] Hot Mask: 'Being at the young and tender age of 19, I don't claim to be that experienced or knowledgeable in a lot of things. Granted, I sure know my way around a lady's low toupee...but I am not what you would consider a scholar by any means. However, I do consider myself to be somewhat of a porn aficionado...not because I necessarily NEED to watch adult films, but because the plot lines and dialogue are quite intriguing! 'I'd say I've seen about oh...sixty-eight billion skin flicks, and guess what, kids? I don't recall a certain Lyle Wile in ANY of these. Of course, I usually fast-forward through the credits, so he may have had a couple of jobs as a grip or a lotion boy...but eh. 'Now now now. I'm not trying to accuse Lyle of having a false record. I could be wrong, ya know? Ol' Limp Dick Lyle may have starred in plenty of these films. So I'll give him the benefit of the doubt...' [A shrug from Mask.] Hot Mask: 'After all, I try to avoid the 'Dudes Pounding Each Other In The Ass' section of the video store when I frequent.' [Shrill chuckles from the mouth of the madman, as he laughs at his own wisecrack. Unwrapping another taco from the tray that sits in the middle of the table, he takes a huge bite and chews feverishly, while still talking in a muffled tone.] Hot Mask: 'As for the protege of this closet case Ron Jeremy, you probably oughta just skip this match we're having and go to that party in Miami. Hell, I'm all for skipping it with you. Latino ass in my face is certainly a better ideal than having to deal with your Brazilian smell in the ring. Scent's the same, anyway. 'Unfortunately for you, having an arsenal of moves with names I can't pronounce isn't really that intimidating. You being an extra for the movie You Got Served also isn't very impressive. Granted, I will say that I did enjoy the attack on that patriotic skin fluter Joshua Justice, but honestly...what a weak target! 'News flash for you, El Bionic...Bi...sexual...Bi...whatever your name is: I don't sashay around in a pair of Old Glory diapers and an Uncle Sam pubic moustache. Your cute little tornado kicks and soccer-style shenanigans won't work on me. I've played Tekken 3 enough to know how to defend myself against that kind of arsenal! 'There's no hope. Stay away from the Windy City on Sunday afternoon. Go 'party it up', stay home and play with your tiny little nut sack, eat Crunch Berries...whatever. Save yourself the anal-raping that will occur in my UWF debut...' [A pause.] Hot Mask: 'Figuratively speaking, unless Bologna Colonic Wile decides to show up.' [Thanks for the clarification, Mask.] Hot Mask: 'No problem.' [Wait, what?] Hot Mask: '...because you stand about as much chance as my stomach does after I finish my eighth taco.' [CHOMP!] [Fade.]
