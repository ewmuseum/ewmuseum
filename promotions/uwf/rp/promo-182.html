A Week at Lungie's

Iron Lung — Villa Roma apartments

A Week at Lungie's A Week at Lungie’s Starring Robin Leach and Iron Lung Co-starring Lynn the receptionist and Frederick the elevator operator Monday morning (4:00 am), after Supremacy went off the air A television screen turns on before our eyes, and without hesitation, a camera focuses in on Robin Leach. He is with his trademark tuxedo look and glass of champagne, and he perks a sly smile towards our way. His smile always carried the most sparkling set of choppers, and it was always hard to imagine how he kept them that way after being in the business for so long. You would think after so much smiling, his teeth would start to break or his face would stick that way, just like the stories mothers tell their children. But none the less, he perked his eyebrows and adjusted his tie as the camera focused out, looking upon the storied apartment complex known only as the Villa Roma. Hello and welcome to Lifestyles of the Rich and Famous, I’m Robin Leach. Smiling once more as the camera catches Robin from his “good side”, he turns and is ready to introduce the segment. The camera slowly pans backwards to reveal more of the Villa Roma as Robin does a ravishing voiceover. Although the building behind me looks like a rundown apartment complex, and also looks like it has no business in my rich and famous lifestyle, it does hold some great things. One of these great things is that it is the home to perhaps one of the most hardcore or extreme, whatever you want to call him, wrestlers. Taking a short break from his talking, Leach takes a short sip of his champagne before holding it back down to his side with another smile. After a while, these smiles would become quite scary. Perhaps you recognize the off stage name of Robert Alexander Pendragon. But, in case you haven’t been accustomed to saying his name in that way, you might know him more as Iron Lung. He is indeed a fascinating character, and in just a few moments, we’re going to be having a little chat with this newly acquired fighter of the UWF. But first, we have some background information to share on this unique individual. Some elegant piano music accompanies Robin as pictures of Pendragon’s hometown begin to show on the screen. Although we don’t have pictures of Pendragon himself, we have found out that he started his life on the wrong foot, living in this ghetto on the wrong side of Norwalk, Connecticut. It was a side of town that was polluted with gangs and violence, and poor Robert’s only chance of survival was to boldly crawl into a cardboard box, recently dumped in the alley by people who just didn't want to bother recycling a television box. The young Pendragon was taken under the wings of many alleyway bums, and they did their best to raise him up into the man he is today. After he was the tender age of nine, he had learned everything that the street folk knew, including how to survive without eating or drinking for days at a time. But Pendragon knew there had to be more out there, so with a spring in his step and a song in his heart, he left the alleyway and headed to the only place he knew how to get to, down the road. Taking a breath with a slight sound of sighing, Leach sipped at his glass once more. This time, he didn't smile the least bit. He had never heard a story of misery before in all his time on the show, but he didn't know that the misery that Pendragon experienced was what fueled him to keep going. The misery that must have been with Pendragon forever, for it was just after he lead his body down the road that he found the neighborhood children that showed him how to fight on the streets. It was just after he lead his tired soul down the road that he found the Dojo in which he gained the Dojo Championship under Jade’s supervision, the youngest to do so. Leach didn't understand that Pendragon, despite being gruelingly punished and tortured, was a machine. Nothing would stop him, not even the news that his parents abandoned him right after he was born, just as if he were a bastard being born into the world. Nobody wanted him from the beginning. This attention was something that he would never get used to. Looking back upon the apartment complex, Leach sighed again and began moving foreword, getting closer to its main entrance as the camera followed closely behind, much like a scared dog. Who knew that when Pendragon took that road less traveled, that it would end up here, in Villa Roma? For Villa Roma was an apartment complex on the right side of town, far away from the violence and hatred that once destroyed the hope of young children like him. For now, this is our fighter’s new place of rest. Now, we will look inside. The camera keeps following Robin Leach inside of the main entrance of the apartment complex. We immediately look up for any sign of a chandelier on the ceiling, and down for any type of fancy tiling. We look to the sides of us to look for any type of fancy wallpapers or decorations, but all together, we find nothing. All that is in front of us is a reception desk, the more common motel ones with keys hanging off chains. Beside the keys are the common motel cubbyholes, some packed with mail and some empty as Old Mother Hubbard’s infamous cupboard. Well, I suppose that this is the main docking station. I’ll ask the clerk the directions to Mr. Pendragon’s suite, we have reservations so there shouldn't be a security issue. Yes, excuse me miss. A red headed teenage girl swings her head around, chewing bubble gum. She twirls her fingers through her hair while popping a bubble and chewing like a cow. Yah, what can I help you with. We have an appointment with Mr. Pendragon. It’s on the thirteenth floor up, seventh room down. Do you need a room key? No, I believe we’ll manage without one. Suit yourselves, and thanks for coming. He’s been expecting you since Supremacy. Thank you for the quick service. The red headed teenage girl swings her head back to the mail cubbyholes, sorting through the incoming bin while Robin Leach heads to the elevator, pressing the button to go up. It seems that we’re in for a pretty long flight up the elevator, so hopefully there will some music to accompany us on our journey. Even some human company is fine by me, for I hate being alone with just my champagne to hold my own. The elevator doors open, and they reveal a man in a red tinted suit with white buttons and black shoulder pad wear, much like the help at the Ritz Hotel wear. Going up? Yes, to the thirteenth floor if you don’t mind. Oh yes, you’re the ones seeing Mr. Pendragon. He’s been expecting you. So I’ve heard. Well, let’s not keep him waiting shall we. As you wish, Mister Leach. Pushing the button for the thirteenth floor, the elevator doors closed again. We now had a shot of the inside of the elevator, and it was a very basic one indeed. The padding on the walls were white, the rivets not even painted to match. The walls gave off a metallic shine, especially after reflecting off Leach’s almost completely bald dome. A couple of the light bulbs on the ceiling were fused out or completely shattered, but Robin didn't’t seem to mind it. He was already in over his head, and didn't think it could become much worse. But the truth was, he hadn't even started his nightmare in the Villa Roma. For on the thirteenth floor, there was someone waiting for him. That someone was Iron Lung, and he wasn't in the mood for a conversation over caviar and champagne. So, do you know Mister Pendragon formally? Just a couple of hellos and goodbyes you know. But I’ll tell you one thing, he doesn't like to be called mister. All right, I’ll keep that in the back of my mind – - He also doesn't like pointless questions. And he doesn't like when you try to talk about his past, because he likes to look into the future and he sees his past as exactly what it is. He doesn't like comments about his parents, and he sure as hell doesn't like cheap drinks. So if you’re thinking of tossing him the rest of that back washed bottle, you’re gonna wind up with it being smashed over your bald bean, got me? Putting the bottle of champagne in the corner of the elevator, Robin wipes some sweat off his head. The elevator operator gives him a sly smile of his own as the elevator stops, the number thirteen lighting up on the electronic overhead. Thirteenth floor, enjoy your interview. Robin clutched his throat a little bit before gagging out a, “thanks a lot, I’ll try to”. The elevator doors then closed with the operator laughing in an insane manner. Robin Leach gulped hard as he began to walk down the hallway, all the way to the seventh door down, just like he was instructed. Well, here goes nothing folks. It’s time for our impromptu interview with one Iron Lung, right after his debut match on Supremacy with El Turtle. He requested me to interview him at this time, and that’s why I’m out here so late. Who knows what will be in store for yours truly, but we’ll find out when I knock on this very cherry oak door. Extending a clutched fist, Robin knocks on the cherry oak door. A voice gently quakes the door while we hear footsteps, and then the door slowly creaks open, displaying a fluorescent red eye staring out. It’s about damn time you got here. I was waiting for an hour and a half. I’m very sorry Mr. Lung, but finding your way around this town after midnight is a little out of the ordinary. I took the bus back home as soon as my match was over. It took me six and a half hours to get home from the University of Richmond, so that would be around 2:30 that I got back home. I’ve been waiting for you since then. I apologize once more - - Just get your ass in here, and let’s get this over with. Did you bring the compromise? The elevator operator told me you didn't’t like bottles that were already drunken out of. Frederick? Well my friend, looks like you and I both were just shafted. He’s just a drunken hobo, only works here because he’d be deported if he didn't’t state to the government that he was doing something with his life. I see. Oh well, he can have it this time. May we come in? Yeah, I invited you in didn't I? Moving inside of the door, the camera swoops around to see Iron Lung’s famous Lazy Boy recliner as the centerpiece, and a pair of small couches accompany beside it. In front of the recliner and couches is a granite center table, where Lung puts his wrestling magazines and entertainment center remotes. A little more back in the room, there are smaller granite tables on either side of the room. On them are porcelain lamps along with some collectible pictures and autographs from past work places. Robin sits on one of the small couches as the camera continues to roll. The first question on my mind Mr. Lung, of course, is how does it feel to be back in the spotlight? To be honest with you Robin, it doesn't feel like anything. You see, I feel like it’s been there all along. But in an interview with Howard Stern a week or so before you walked away – - Let’s get something straight, I didn't walk away from anything. And I know what I said, about how the crowd’s cheering was like my pulse and without it, I was basically dead. But let me tell you a little secret. All right Mr. Lung, I’ll promise not to tell. I have that special tone of the crowd implanted in my ears for the rest of my natural days. It’s like a deafness, and it will never go away. Well, that is indeed a special trait. It's one of my better traits Robin. Another one of them is being able to burp the alphabet, but I bet that you don't want to stick around for that. I sure don't. Aww, you'd be missing the best part of the night then. Maybe another time. My next question to you is, how do you think you did tonight? Do you think that you performed well for your return to the ring? I feel that I performed well, considering my opponent. Ah yes, the dreaded El Turtle stepped up to your challenge tonight. And next Supremacy, you have to face another returning superstar in Axel Action. Yeah, I've heard of this guy at least. He is a contender, and a favorable match up too. I'm sure you get this question a lot, but how do you prepare for a match? Is there any difference between being the underdog and training for it, and being the favorite and training for it? No, there isn't a difference at all. In fact, I'm always the underdog any way, no matter what match it is. I always see my self as the underdog, and I train likewise. I have a strict training regimen, and I don't reveal it for anyone. How does it feel to be known as a Hardcore Icon? Once again, it feels completely normal to me. Whatever the people want to call me, I'm going to go along with it. If they think I'm horrible, hell, I'll think that I'm horrible too and up my game unil they don't think of me like that any more. It's all about respecting the audience Robin, something that you wouldn't know anything about. I have a slight idea of what you are saying, Mister Lung - - Of course you do, Robin. You always think you have an idea, but you really don't have a clue. You ask me all of these repetitive questions, I thought you were smarter than that. But like usual, your people disappoint me. I apologize Mister -- Call me Mister one more time and I'll break your skull off your neck for you, got it? (Gulps) Got it... You always come here with the same dumb ass set of questions. Why don't you be original already? Don't you read up on my responses from the past? What makes you think this time will be any different? How do I like being the Hardcore Icon, what do I do to prepare, blah blah blah. I -- You nothing, it's always about you isn't it? Well I'll tell you what, you're in my apartment now Robin, and in my apartment, it's my way or you can get your bald ass the hell out of here! Well, I certainly didn't expect that from you. No one ever does cheap ass, now get out! As you wish... Getting up from the couch, Robin Leach slowly paces his way towards the door, taking one final look back. Iron Lung goes to the door, slamming it in Robin's face. Robin catches the wind from the door as he turns around to face the camera, sighing. I'm Robin Leach, and this has been Lifestyles of the Rich and Famous... The camera slowly fades away from Robin, pulling out and gradually wiping to a completely blank screen.
