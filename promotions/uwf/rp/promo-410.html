Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder driving his red and black Chevy Blazer down the streets of Tulsa, Oklahoma. He is talking on a cell phone as he speeds through traffic. Ryder seems suprisingly upbeat compared to the beating he suffered at the hands of Resurrection. He even has that familiar Ryder smile of confidence. ) Ryder: So he's down for the match huh? Exloding turnbuckles and the barbed wired and everything? Well then let's sign it up, because i'm ready to rip Malcolm Kennedy to shreds. I'm gonna take his face and rake it over every piece of barbed wire there is. There won't be a single spot of that cage that isn't dripping with the blood of Resurrection. So book the match and i'll swing by soon to sign the papers. * Ryder hangs up the cell phone and pulls into an empty parking lot. The sign on the building says Gold's Gym. Ryder gets out of the vehicle and he is wearing the red and black colors of the Prime Alliance still. He pulls a back out of the back and walks in through the doors. Once inside he makes his way to an office and sets his bag outside the door. He knocks a couple times and a womanly voice tells him to enter. Ryder walks in and has a seat on a black leather couch as a tall blonde and beautiful woman sets up a camera. * Ryder: Man, you guys don't waste any time huh? Woman: Not on our most important clients. Ryder: Well thank you very much, but i'm not a client. I just work out here, and the boss said I could use your camera equipment to record my promo for a match I got coming up. Woman: You work out here...which makes you a client. Ryder: I guess so, but could we get this moving? I wanna get in a workout before I gotta catch the plane to go to my match. Woman: Sure, begin when you are ready. * Ryder switches to interview mode and begins his promo * Ryder: So I guess i'm booked in a match with The Plague, a man whom I have tremendous respect for. The Plague has fought and beaten the best this business has to offer, but that doesn't frighten me. No, that gets me excited, because I know i'm gonna be in there with a top notch wrestler and an opponent who can truely bring out the best in me. Against The Plague I know that i've got to bring my A game and you can bet that's what is gonna happen. I look forward to a hard fought match, but I think it's a match that I will come out on top. I'm not being cocky, I am just a firm believer in my skills as a wrestler. No matter who wins though, it's gonna be one hell of a match Plague, and I wish you nothin but luck. * Ryder smiles at the camera and then a serious look crawls across his face * Ryder: Now as for the other little thorn in my damn side, Malcolm Kennedy and his little girlfriends. You can all bet on one thing when it comes to Ruff Ryder. I will get to each and every one of you. You see, before the match with Malcolm I had respect for Digital Mortality. I even thought Moraless was a decent human being, but after you bitches all decided you'd jump me because your boy was gettin beat like he stole somethin, you pissed me off. That is your mistake and that's the mistake that is gonna have you all in extreme pain. I might not get you all at once, but you can bet your ass that i'll pick each one of you off little by little and then i'm gonna rip all your hearts out of your throat. You should have killed me when you had the chance, because you'll never get that shot again. Kennedy, I hear you decided to accept my challenge, and let me just tell you straight up...YOU FUCKED UP! I always thought you were arrogant, but I didn't know you were stupid until now. You walked into a match that's gonna end your damn career. You better bring more than the one man army with you to beat me son. You better bring the whole United States army, because i'm not goin into this match to win. I'm goin into this match to beat you to death. I want to see flesh hanging from the barbed wire and I want to see pools of blood all over the arena. You're day is comin Kennedy, and I just want you to remember one thing....you brought this on yourself. ( Ryder stands up and walks out of the office leaving the woman with a wide eyed shocked look on her face. As she is turning off the camera her voice can be heard. ) Woman: What a sick son of a bi... * The scene ends *
