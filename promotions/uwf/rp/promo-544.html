UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #544

The end of the Local NVA

14 Jul 2004

Adien Pryde — Vietnamese Restaurant

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a Vietnamese Restaurant in down town Chicago. Inside we find Pryde, Joanna and an Asian man all sitting at a table.] Man : So what did you want me here for Mr. Pryde and Ms. Joanna? Pryde : Well, you see, we have a match with the Local NVA and we would like you to translate what we have to say into something they can understand. Man : I see, I guess I could do that for you. [Just then a Vietnamese waitress walks up.] Waitress : 我可以得到您任何吗? Man : She asks “Can I get you anything?” Joanna : Just some water. Man : 一些水。 [The waitress bows and leaves.] Pryde : Now I want you to translate this and do it looking into the camera. Man : OK Pryde : Local NVA, Raid and I will be coming to Wednesday Night Underground tonight to destroy you and any communist terrorist you bring with you. Man : 地方NVA, 袭击和我来临到星期三夜地下今晚毁坏您和您带来与您的任一个共产主义恐怖分子。 Pryde : You may be UWF legends but to us in The Chaos Society, you have proven that you are nothing more than want to be communist heroes. Man : 您也许是UWF 传奇但对我们在纷乱社会, 您证明, 您是没什么更多比想要是共产主义英雄。 [Restaurant patrons are starting to take notice.] Pryde : With Raid’s Bug Bomb and my Falcon Driver, we will bury you under the UWF Hall of Fame where you should have stayed. Man : 用袭击的臭虫炸弹和我的猎鹰司机, 我们将埋葬您在UWF 您应该停留了的光荣榜之下。 Pryde : Tonight you can kiss your Vietnamese ass goodbye. [The waitress makes it back to the table and starts to set out the water. Man : 今晚您能亲吻您的越南驴子再见。 [The waitress looks shocked at what she hears and throws the contents of the water glass into the translators face and storms away.] Joanna : You really should watch what you say in the presence of a woman. [The screen fades to black.]
