UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #209

Que Sera Sera

8 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — Training facility

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in a training room. There are lots of weights lying around. The camera pans around and there is a heavy bag that is sitting there, as well as a wrestling ring, and an amateur wrestling mat. The camera continues to pans around and come in on a treadmill. On the treadmill is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a black muscle shirt, along with a pair of black gym shorts. It seems as if he has been running on the treadmill for quite a while as he has quite a sweat built up.) Kazee: Montgomery, your right, we would blow the roof off of any facility that the UWF decides to put is in. You and I are right up there as the best in the business as is our other associate, Mr. Dunn. Thing of the matter is even one of us in a match blows the roof off the building. It’s just a fact of life and the UWF knows about it….. I know the UWF knows that alone that we can blow the roof off of the building and will make ticket sales that much better. I guess the geniuses think that if they pit us against each other it will be that much better. Who knows, now is not the time to question them. They have their reasons, and they have their ways, and now their ways are that they want to destroy the only standing stable left in the UWF, as well as the most dominant force in the UWF…… I don’t like this situation any more than you do, Montgomery. In fact, I hate the idea that I have to face my own stable mate. There are things that just shouldn’t happen, and this is one of them. This is the second time of course that the UWF has done this to me. Clash of the Champions, shortly after I joined with the Players Club, in the first round match at the Clash, it was myself vs. Dunn. The question is why the UWF continues doing this…… The UWF tried it before, probably under the coercion of The Prime Alliance, be they no more, and Dunn and I went into that match with what was a hell of a feud to start with, and in the third match we had against each other. I guess 3 times the charm; because I did finally beat Dunn and went on to represent The Players Club against the Native American turncoat known as Ruff Ryder……. Fact of the matter is that whatever they had in mind backfired, because not only did we tear the roof off the place, but the Players Club became that much stronger. The Players Club as a group overcame whatever the UWF was trying to do and became a much more stable entity in the UWF, as if we weren’t already the most stable entity…… Now, the UWF has done it again, and again, we shall prove them wrong. The Players Club may go at it in the ring, but we will come back stronger than ever before. This is just one of those situations I guess. Take the good with the bad. This is the bad, having to force stable mates into a match against each other. It’s their prerogative, but why they would want to destroy their number one ticket sellers is beyond me….. Montgomery, you claim the match won’t happen this way. Well, if it doesn’t than that would be great. The last thing I want to do is face you in the ring, but it maybe are and there may be nothing that we can do about it. If the case comes that there is nothing we can do about it, then Montgomery, I promise you this right here, right now, no matter what happens in that match we will continue to be strong and united in the Players Club, and I don’t want you to hold anything back……. That’s the way the fans would want it. That’s the way the “experts” would want it. That the way I want it. No reason to go and give a half ass performance. Besides, I’d be too ashamed of myself if I did…… Fact of the matter is, either way; I have my hands full in this match. Not only do I have to keep my eyes and my opponents, I have to keep my eyes on my partner. Kennedy and I didn’t start on the best of terms. After all I did beat him in a qualifying match for the NA Title tournament in my first match back here in the UWF. I don’t think he ever got over it. Of course he did somehow earn a shot at the UWF Title, which I truly don’t understand, but whatever. He also managed to somehow manage to weasel himself back into the Clash of the Champions tournament. Now, he still a thorn in the side of the Players Club. Of course he is teamed up with my good friend, who has been here so long that he deserves a damned title, I just wish it were under different circumstances, like teaming with me…… But on the other end of that, I have Raid to watch out for. I have no idea what is going through his mind. I don’t even know if I want to know, but I’ve spend plenty of time on you. I’ve got other things to focus on, and I have to renew the trust that I had for you when we were running high as Tag Team Champions…… At this point, whatever happens, who knows, but either way, whoever I face, they will need the luck, and if somehow the UWF brass comes to its senses and put me with Montgomery against Raid and Kennedy, well, they’ll really need luck…… Let me be the first to wish you luck, because you truly will need. (Camera fades out)
