Alex Grey — Underground

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight the moon gazes down upon two lovers. Lost in each other on one of the hundreds of rooftops amidst the city they find their own private heaven and a moment to call their own. No words are needed, these kinds of feelings are quite often conveyed without words. A soft breeze caresses bare skin and rebellious strands of hair as the two entangled in each other continue on unaware of anything or anyone else around them. Inside the building where the moonlight cannot be seen a techno beat roars over the speakers, bodies bounce, bump, and grind, sweat and perfume mix and mingle into an intoxicating scent that blurs the senses, young bodies are pushed beyond their limits, club drugs are consumed under the watchful eyes of video cameras. Relationships begin...relationships end. Hearts are broken, some hearts are too young to understand the pain of being broken while some are all too used to the pain, the club is a pulsing beat of human emotion at it’s highest, it is a breeding ground for romance and puppy love. Most of it will be forgotten by tomorrow. While this building full of youth at its pinnacle carries out it’s nightly activities there is a different sort of club buried underneath, the kind of club where it’s best not to be scene entering or leaving. This is Martins club, and for 3 years he’s been running it right under the nose of one of the most popular dance clubs in the city buried far enough under the foundation only a select group know it exists at all, which is just how Martin wants it. The atmosphere is dark, no moonlight let alone daylight ever graces this place, the only lights down here are Neon or Florescent. The “Club drugs” found down here range from Coke to just about anything else you can think of, Martin goes through a great deal of secrecy and trouble to keep this place stocked with whatever the patrons want, when people are under the influence of Martins chemicals they become more spontaneous, more willing to answer questions and make deals, this is where Martin plants his seeds and reaps the rewards of information and money. A long hallway filled is filled with recreational rooms, another one of Martins favorite vices. Gratuitous sex with many anonymous partners. From under these doors moans of pleasure and cries of pain drift through the hallway, these cries will linger on until morning until patrons stumble out blurry eyed with pounding headaches and dry mouths, left to find their own way home to their high paying jobs and wives, while in their wake whore’s and mistresses sleep off a night of debauchery with smiles of satisfaction on their faces. Martin sits on one of many couches soaking it all in, a glass of god knows what in one hand and Kims breast in his other as he fondles her under her shirt, Kim herself has been here several times but doesn’t appear to be at ease with her surroundings yet, deep down she’s still a girl from the country and fast paced city life makes her uncomfortable. I sit across from Martin watching everyone from the rich and powerful the violent and insecure takes pleasure in their vices, in this mix of dirty humanity I feel at home, in this mix of corruption I can almost take my mind off losing the TV title, the strange voice I’ve been hearing....almost. Almost... “Joshua tells me our little problem has been taken care of?” Martin asks gazing over his glass towards me referring to his blackmail problem I nod. “Joshua said it was...quite unpleasant” Martin said putting his glass down “Just what the fuck did you do to him Alex?” “I took advantage of his greed Martin...Joshua brought him to my apartment, I offered him a glass of wine, I convinced him I shared his desire to screw you over...his eyes lit up and we toasted to bringing you down...he drank his wine...I drank mine” “Then what?” Martin asks now only halfheartedly fondling Kim “He finished his wine...and it was too late” “Why?” “Because I put this in his drink...” I reach into my pocket and toss the small packet of powder into his lap “Don’t open that” “What is it?” he asks picking it up and examining the contents through the bag “It’s glass...ground up real fine...he might of seen it clumped at the bottom...maybe...if he hadn’t been such a greedy fuck” “Jesus Alex...” Martin breaths dropping the packet on the table “You’re one sick fuck...” “It got the job done...isn’t that what you wanted? Nice and painful...sliced up his insides real nice...he choked to death on his own blood and in no small amount of pain Martin” Martin takes a large gulp of his drink and sets the glass down empty, returning his attention to Kim he gestures to her, she knows what he means and slides onto the floor, her hand reaching for his zipper. “You did good Alex” Martin closes his eyes and smiles slightly as Kim...gets to work. “Real good...” He hesitates “Alex?” “Yeah?” “Do you trust me?” “No...” “Can I trust you?” “I wouldn’t...” I let my words sink in for a few seconds, letting the silence drive them home like hammer “But you don’t have to trust me to have a working relationship with me Martin, you keep me happy and we’ll never drink wine together...deal?” Martin pales visibly and shudders slightly whether from Kims attention or my thinly veiled threat I can’t tell. “Deal...” he says finally reaching down and steering Kims head via a handful of hair. “Now then...” he says “What about the TV Title?” “What about it?” I ask not really wanting to talk shop right at this particular moment “Do you want it back?” “I suppose I do” “You suppose?” I shrug “I don’t mean a fuck to these people yet Martin, Having that title around my waist didn’t do shit for my popularity, they just tossed me like a bone to a returning legend, I was rusty...he wasn’t” “So you don’t want it back then?” “No...I’d be happy to get it back, but I’d be happier to teach White Wolf a lesson as to what happens when he takes what’s mine...Make an example of him” Martin nods “Glad to hear it Alex...because you’ve got a number one contenders shot at the upcoming PPV...a chance to do just what you want...get your hands on White Wolf...now if you’ll excuse me Alex...” Martin pulled Kim to her feet “I’ve got some other business to attend to” he grinned standing and offering Kim his arm. “Enjoy yourself tonight Alex...take advantage of my hospitality” Without another word he leaves me, he and Kim are joined by another young woman and all three soon vanish into one of the rooms at the end of the hall, what goes on behind their closed door is better left to the imagination. My thoughts turn to the number one contenders match, no matter how many people they throw into the ring it all comes down to who wants it more, can I say I want a shot at that title more then any of them? No I can’t... Can I say I want to make White Wolf choke on his own blood more then anyone they’ll throw into the ring with me? Without a doubt yes. See it’s not about Gold, or who wants it more...It’s not about hype for Live and Loud. It’s about proving a point. No one knows me here, no fans give a damn about Alex Grey. I like that... Carving out a name for myself again, I’m looking forward to it. Because I’ll carve my name out of any dumb son of a bitch they stick into the ring with me. Gazing around the club I realize that I’m too wound up to enjoy the atmosphere, I need to burn off some steam, standing up I grab a nearby bottle and leave the small packet of glass behind, I’ve got plenty more where that came from. Making my way towards the door I run into the only thing that could possibly keep me here. “Hey there...you’re Martins friend right? She’s young... “He’s told us all about you...says you like to get rough” Supple... Dark hair... Curves... “You sound like a challenge Mister Grey” And best of all... “And if there’s anything we like better then a rough customer...it’s a challenge” She has a twin sister What better way to prepare for a match with multiple opponents? “Are you up for it Alex?” “Do you hear me complaining?” I ask with a small smile “Not yet...” she fires back taking me by the hand and leading me towards one of the rooms, her sister follows from behind. Disengaging myself from her hold I caress her long black hair gripping the ends I jerk her head back, the sudden rush of air that escapes her mouth is like a hiss, but the smile doesn’t vanish...it broadens “If you’re thinking you’re going to break me tonight then you’ve got another thing coming honey” “Oh Alex...” she giggles like a school girl as if I’ve just suggested something very naughty “I love it when you talk dirty” with a sudden twist she breaks free of my grip and slaps me across the face, she smiles when the blood rushes to my face and my eyes narrow “What’s wrong? Not used to women fighting back?” she grins wickedly “Let’s go honey...and I’ll show you just how much it bothers me” I growl throwing open the nearest door. “The names Mia” she smiles sauntering into the room “And this is my sister Lia” Shutting the door behind me I lock it and then double lock it, no one is going to be getting out of this room for some time. I turn and find Mia undressing her sister and vice versa, as their clothing falls to the floor and more flesh is bared I begin to see scars, jagged claw marks, faded and not so faded bruises. “You going to join us?” Lia asks raising her eyebrow “Or stand there like and yank it like a Horney little teenager?” My clothes are quick to join the pile, they see my scars, a lifetime of results of a man who enjoys what he does...what follows from there like Martin and Kim...is better left to the imagination.
