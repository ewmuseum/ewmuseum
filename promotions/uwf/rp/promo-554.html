16 Jul 2004

The Communist — Breifing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist is dressed in a red suit coat and pants, unusual to say the least. He's seated at a long table in an empty room. Soviette quietly paces the room and you can see the Red Star with the Scythe and Hammer behind the head of the table. She holds in her hand a thick file with a large red stamp on it, 'Property of the KGB.': Commie: What is this all about? I have to train. My match with Persia is Sunday. Soviette: That is why you are here. We have some interesting information to dispence to you. What do you know about Persia? Commie: He has something I want. He has the THC belt. And he will be relieved of it, promptly. Soviette: No no no. I don't want the propaganda that you've filled your empty skull with. As a person. What do you know about Nick Persia. Don't hurt yourself. Commie: He's a Dutchman. From Holland. Soviette: Born there? Commie: Probably. Soviette:: No. Commie: No? :Soviette slides a piece of paper from the file to The Communist. He reads it, then again. His eyebrows cock ever so slightly: Commie: Hannover, Germany? Why would he call himself the Dutch Destroyer if he were not Dutch. This makes no sence. Soviette: Keep Reading. :The Communist slowly reads over the document, taking in each word, digesting it, and bringing it into his psychie. Soviette continues to pace, looking over the rest of the documents: Soviette: The Son of a Dutch couple, his father was a sailor transporting Persian rugs between Hannover, Germany and Rotterdam, Holland. Persia was born in Hannover. Now, WHY does he never speak of this when he calles himself the Voice? hmmm? Commie: Your mud diggers are doing their job too well. I see nothing here that can help me in my match. Just a German who want to be Dutch. So he wants to wear wooden shoes and eat chocolate in stead of drink beer and be an Arayan. I can't say I blame him. Soviette: Commie, this is important Commie: NO IT ISN'T You Favor brokering, mud slinging and backstabbing isn't what this is about! This is a match of strength and ability between two men. It is a glorious battle. And for nothing less than a Championship. I won't belittle the importance or the honor of this combat with your politicing and rumor mills. Soviette: That is EXACTLY what this is about. Incase you didn't notice Communist, you are getting a shot at a major title. This isn't the low card anymore. For the Comittee, for the State, For the Party, For YOURSELF. You must win this, or we may not be able to recover the backing of the Party. And you know what happens then? You're back to wrestling bears in the Russian Circus for the money people throw at you. And I will return to my dead end desk job with the KGB. Neither of us want that. I suggest you pull yourself together and get ready for this match. Wrestling isn't just fighting. It isn't so simple, and it never was. You need to get in your opponents head, and sometimes that means digging up things in their past. And if these things just HAPPEN to slip out, then SO BE IT. Now, read up and prepare. Soon, you will meet with Persia. And it will be ready, even if I have to force feed the information to you. Commie: Fine. But when I have the title, we will do things my way. :Soviette sighs, rolls her eyes and shrugs: Soviette: Fine. Whatever makes you sleep better at night. :Scene fades as Soviette and the Commie go over the information:
