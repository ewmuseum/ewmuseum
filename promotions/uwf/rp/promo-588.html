UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #588

Tired, but what the hell

date unknown

Mike Montgomery — The office of the UWF Commissioner

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the former world champion and founder of one of the greatest stables seen in a long, long time now back in action, things are going to get interesting. It seems the UWF has moved on, leaving Montgomery behind in the dust. A new commissioner, one Patti Preest has been hired, though the old commissioner was never actually fired. Richard Hayes is running around telling anyone who will listen that he now owns the UWF. Of course, he never made an offer to buy those shares owned by the Player's Club so really he doesn't have complete control. And of course, sadly just one week before Montgomery's return, a new heavyweight champion was crowned in the form of a certainly worthy Joshua Dunn. The problem is that the old champ never got to defend his title. Indeed, what was once the most powerful stable in the federation appeared to be falling apart at the seams. As these thoughts go through Montgomery's mind, he strolls past his...no, Patti's, secretary, headed into the commissioner's office. 'Excuse me sir, but you can't...oh, Mr. Montgomery. I'm sorry but Ms. Preest isn't in right now. Mike simply smiled at the woman, 'That's okay. I'm simply here to reclaim what is rightfully mine.' 'But...you never actually got a notice of termination did you?' Mike's smile grew wider, 'Nope.' Technicalities can be wonderful things as Mike always said. He opened the door and stepped inside. The office had a distinctly feminine feel to it. Montgomery's trophies were gone, his title belts no longer adorning the wall. He walked toward what was once his desk, stepping behind it to stand between it and what was once his chair. In one sweeping motion, he sent the entire desktop's contents sliding over the side of the desk and straight into the garbage can. Mike sat down, put his feet up, leaned back, and closed his eyes. Yes, this would be his office again. He pressed the intercom button, 'Ms. Smith, please have some boxes sent up to my office. It's time for a change in command. And please tell Ms. Preest that the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations will be taking his commissionership back. Send her my thanks and see to it she's given a sizable severance package.' 'Can I assume you're back for good then?' Mike hesitated for a moment. This wasn't the UWF he had known when Digital Mortality broke his leg in a world title match. Friends were now enemies, enemies now potential friends. He would need to re-acclimate himself, get the lay of the land. And what better place start than this Sunday in this reunion tag match? All of this flashed through Mike's mind before he finally said 'Yup, I'm back.'
