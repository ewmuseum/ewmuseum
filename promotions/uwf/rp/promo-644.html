UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #644

In the nick of time

16 Aug 2004

Joshua Dunn — The House of Dunn

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She sat at her desk busily typing away at what was her work; she was calling up stock market prices onto the office computer screen. She was a stock broker, and a damn good one at that. The name plaque on the other side of the desk read Alexia Young. This was indeed the woman that Joshua was infatuated with. Good reason to be as well, even dressed in the grey pinstriped suit she wore, she still looked stunning, reading the information that she wanted to see. UWF Stock had gone up, that was the third time of increase this month alone, it was looking to be a sound investment for anyone. When she told Joshua She was a successful business woman, she hadn’t exactly been lying, but she hadn’t exactly told the truth either, but being a stock broker, she had managed to make a few tidy profits in her time. Her fingers were in too many pies to count, but she never thought toad up her total worth, firstly the math was unappealing, and she had gotten a tidy some from her job anyway and didn’t need to. On their dinner date on that infamous Saturday, infamous to her and her closest friends, who had laughed in her face about her meeting Joshua Dunn, until it had actually happened. She bonded with him immediately after meeting him in the restaurant. He looked as if he hung on every word she said, she was especially impressed how he kept eye contact with her throughout, as many men before had been more interested with another pair of her assets. That knocked up another notch for him on her personal rating that she gave most men she’d been to dinner with. None were quite like Joshua. Perhaps her already high thoughts of him were due for what he did for her in the park that night, the ultimately chivalrous man had saved her from a fate worse than death. Some women she knew counted chivalry along with chauvinistic, she never understood why the two could have been one in the same. Either way, she was so glad to have slipped him her mobile phone, one of her soundest investments yet. When going to the dinner, she had practically thought about what to wear all day, her hair took two ours to get to stand just right, the clothes were her favourites, the smart but casual approach seemed to work, and she was so glad it had, because she would be making a grave mistake if it hadn’t. Having no way to contact him again was the most devastating thing to happen, she almost wished that he had forgotten to return her mobile phone to her at the end of that night. But fortunately she had a back up plan, and it was this that she was acting on at this very moment. There was a knock on her office door, it was not a fancy office, but the face that she was in one and not outside working among the other brokers gave her status among them, not to mention a higher salary. She told them to enter while still looking over the stock prices, Micr*soft didn’t look to be shaking at all, eventually it would drop, but what would cause it she didn’t know. A man also dressed in a suit, but currently without the jacket or the tie, peeked round the door. He entered and shut the door behind him, he had a wad of paper in his hand, she recognised him immediately as one of her closest friends Garry Henderson, she had sent him on a little “Errand” previously, and it looked like his search was fruitful. Her biggest break through was finding the UWF stock contract, her company had handled all the stock dealings of the UWF, especially since they all got their share for being owners. The Todd that had done the underhanded dealings was sacked soon after the news leaked, that kind of publicity was anything but good, but she had heard that he had retired early in Florida, no doubt with the gratitude of one Mr Hayes. The important thing to her in all this was that all dealings with clients were recorded, even to the most trivial phone conversation, and that was her goal, to find the number of one Joshua Dunn. Garry sidled into the office a bit more and flung the wad of paper onto her desk. Garry: There you go, all the dealings we ever had with the Universal Wrestling Federation to Date. I hope you do realise how much Shi- Crap you could get us both into Alexia: Relax Garry, even if it does, I’d never tell anyone who got me the information Why exactly none of this information was kept on the computer database she was unsure of, but it took a long time for Garry to find what she needed, and she was very grateful about it, that was the hard bit done. Now just to hope the man had not left for Tokyo already. Naturally in a flurry of not quite sense, she had booked herself a flight to Tokyo and managed to “Acquire” some tickets, it had cost her a lot, and her boss wasn’t too happy about the short notice for her leave of absence, but it would be worth it, now to see if she could get in contact with Joshua to meet up after the show, it would be like a dream come true… Again She riffled through the papers in front of her and extracted a sheet that needed closer inspection. If it had been Tsunami Oni, or even Jamie Shock for that matter that she was trying to find it would have been a lot harder, the fact that he used his own name for wrestling it made it much easier to find him, there it was in bold print in front of her, details of the Client Joshua Dunn, and yes, including a home telephone number. Her eyes watered with excitement, perhaps he wasn’t so lost to her after all. She looked up at Gary, eyes shining in the light, a smile across her face. Alexia: Thank you Garry, it’s here, it’s really here Garry: As long as he doesn’t mind you calling it should be fine, otherwise… He shrugged dismissively, and that’s exactly what would happen, dismissal. Garry left the office, remembering to close the door behind him, leaving her alone with the telephone. Garry was absolutely right, as usual, as long as he didn’t mind her phoning it was alright. That delayed her response a while she weighed up the argument in her head. What exactly did she have to lose apart from her job? What did she have to gain? What would he think if he received a phone call from her? Despite all the cons, she foolishly picked up the phone and asked for an outside line. Not much time left now *** Not much time left now. Joshua had everything he needed, all his stuff was packed into a suitcase, and ready for the off to Tokyo, at long last. He’d left it far too late to get any decent training in while he was there, but it would have to do. Although he was pleased to see he wasn’t the only person not leaving the country in ample time. But then again, Mike Montgomery made a habit of appearing to do the right thing all the time, he made a career of it, his answers were right no matter what the question, but his views on who would walk out World Champion would soon be scrutinised when the four of them walked into the ring. He wasn’t too sure what condition he would be in for the fight though, insomnia had him in its iron grip, he hadn’t slept much last night again, the bags under his eyes were getting more pronounced every day. And he was starting to feel the ill effects now, especially with the drowsiness he felt from the pills prescribed by the doctor to help him regain some of his lost memories. He just felt rotten through and through. Unable to sleep for thought of the woman lost to him, and drowsy all the time he was awake, from a combination of exhaustion, drowsiness and lack of sleep. He felt more like a Zombie than the UWF World Champion at the moment. And at this rate might give just a good performance in the ring as one. If he didn’t catch enough sleep on the plane, he was probably done for. He’d packed enough for a few days stay in Tokyo after the event, perhaps the rest away from it all would be good for him. Time enough to forget and move on, whether he wanted to or not. Just in case he wasn’t going to get any sleep on the plane, he’d brought the book he was reading at the moment, well he had to do something while in transit, the flight was a long one, and the jet lag would be horrible, one more nail in the coffin. He was driving to the air port himself, he was beginning to think it wasn’t such a great idea, maybe taking up the chauffeur service the UWF offered it’s employee’s was not such a bad idea after all, but he was running a tad late as it was, he was still in plenty of time, but he liked being at places with time to spare, allows for you to rest a little before pressing ahead, that’s why he was so annoyed at booking his flight for Tokyo at the last minute. Too late to call a chauffeur, but he was starting to shake away the cobwebs in his head, the three cups of coffee should help him along until he reached the airport. With everything in check, he picked up his bags, one suitcase and a hold all, and left his house, locking the door on the other side. He placed the bags into the back of the car before entering the driving seat and starting the engine, he was most of the way down the drive before he realised that he’d left his damn passport on the coffee table, cursing his own failing memory, and blaming it also on lack of sleep, he reversed back up the drive and went back into the house. He was in no doubt he probably would forget his head if it wasn’t screwed on, and he probably would attribute that to lake of sleep also. He wasted no time in grabbing his passport from the coffee table and had made it back to the door. Then it happened. The Phone rang. He found it strange, so strange he looked at the device puzzled for a moment, instead of answering it immediately. Everyone new he was leaving today, everyone that might need him anyway. He wasn’t expecting a phone call of any sort before he left, but curiosity befell him and he just had to pick up the phone, lifting the receiver to his ear, he let out the universal greeting of JD: Hello? Female Voice: Joshua? His stomach churned, he felt like he was about to lose the lunch he hadn’t had. He was convinced it could only be one person, but how could this be? He didn’t quite know, but he sure as hell was going to find out JD: Alexia? How did you get this number? Alexia: Don’t yell… I didn’t meant to hit you like this JD: It’s ok really For some strange reason the image of Jamie Shock popped into the forefront of his mind, he had no idea why, but it seemed to fit somehow. He shook off the feeling and Alexia continued Alexia: Please don’t ask where I got the number; I took a huge risk in phoning you. JD: It’s fine Again a flashing image of a smiling Shock, it was quite disconcerting feeling, he didn’t entirely know where the thought was coming from, but trying to tease it out while talking on the phone wasn’t working to well, men weren’t meant for multi tasking. Alexia: I just had to speak to you again; I never gave you my number to get in contact again JD: I never asked you for it, I’m a fool for it too Alexia, well I’ll be make sure to give it to you next time we meet JD: Next time we meet might be a while; I’m heading off to Tokyo as of right now, won’t be back for a while. Alexia: I’m going too JD: What? That he hadn’t expected, maybe she wanted to see him more than he suspected, but he was a little pessimistic about these things. Alexia: I got tickets to the show too; maybe we could meet up after the show. JD: After the show, would you be willing to meet up with an Ex-World Champion? Alexia: Don’t be ridiculous, you know I’ve seen you going far, there’s no way you can lose There it was again, the thought of shock, and now a plane? It was confusing him, why was he thinking of Shock and a plane… or was he remembering? He suddenly got more excited than when he found out who was on the other end of the phone, were those rotten pills the doctor gave him finally making the drowsiness worth it? JD: Listen, I’d LOVE to meet up after the show, just make sure you hang around after the end of the show, I’ll find you, you can count on that. Alexia: Fantastic, I’ll be seeing you soon then. He could almost hear the wink down the phone line, it was strange, it was just what he expected to follow after the statement, and she probably had done it too. JD: Great, well I have a plane to catch now; I’ll see you in Tokyo. Alexia: Bye JD: Bye He replaced the receiver, and looked in awe at the wall opposite, he couldn’t believe it. He still had no idea how she got the number, but he didn’t care, things just seemed to be falling in place once again, with things going right again, he felt relaxed, the niggling feeling at the back of his mind was gone, and he felt immediately exhausted, as if the coffee had all worn off in an instant. He didn’t think he’d need to book for the flight now, he had a more important matter to pursue, and maybe phoning for a chauffeur now wasn’t a bad idea either, he picked up the receiver again. And with the excitement of when he first came to America, he could finally look forward to what was bound to be a fantastic match to be in. And with the glee of finally having one stolen memory back in his mind to be recalled at will, he was definitely looking on the plus side now. After all there’s always hope
