UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #254

Bus Stop (read this one)

Feb 27 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Bus Station

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Usually you read this to kinda update you to this point on what is going on with Malcolm Kennedy. Unfortunately this is really not needed this time. Anarchy has spoken out once again, and once again they will get a response. *** <<< The scene opens up inside a busy bus station. People quickly walking too and fro trying to get where they need to be. The camera pans in circles scanning the station, apparently looking for someone. It pans past the pay phones along the wall, just as they go out of view the camera swings back and stops on a man in a black t-shirt and jeans. A luggage bag hanging from his shoulder, a set of headphones hanging around his neck and connected to a cd player inside the bag. His hair is jet black and spiked. On the back of his shirt in camouflage letters are the words “The One Man Army.” The man hangs up the phone and turns around. The camera focuses in on the front of the shirt. On the front is a picture of Malcolm Kennedy raising his hands while standing on the second turnbuckle in the ring. The picture is wrapped in barbed wire and the words “Top Notch” in camouflage are found above it. The camera pans up and focuses in on the face of the man.......>>> “ How the hell do you guys always find me in places like this?” <<< Malcolm smiles as he adjusts the bag on his shoulder. He walks past the camera and heads towards the door. The camera follows. As he heads outside a man approaches him.>>> Man: “ Uhhh Mr. Kennedy?” Malcolm: “Yeah that’s me.” Man: “Umm Sir, we are almost done with your bus. We just need to do the routine inspection and get it out of the shop. It should be done in ten minutes or so.” Malcolm: “Great, thanks a lot dude. I really appreciate you making this one exception and fix our bus, we never expected to get a flat tire.” Man: “Your welcome Mr Kennedy, we’ll call you when its done.” Malcolm: “Ok, thanks.” <<< The man walks back towards the shop. Malcolm turns and walks in the opposite direction. It follows him until Malcolm finally stops and sits down on a bench. He crosses his right leg over his left and rests both his arms on the back of the bench.>>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ Anarchy, you guys are quick thinkers, I’ll give you that. I mean, we point out the mistakes you are making, for example how I told you that your past in other federations doesn’t mean jack shit, and you come back by saying that you know that, and that your going to win because you’ve been studying us in the ring. Man, I wish I had a referee nearby so he could throw his bullshit flag on that one. I mean seriously guys, wise up will you? You don’t know a damned thing about us. Sure, MAYBE you did, in fact, watch our matches. But if you think for one minute that I am going to believe you studied them and memorized my flaws, then you’re a lot dumber than you appear. You two don’t know crap about me and the way I wrestle. All you got going for you right now is that you know you wish you could be the wrestler I am today.” <<< He blurts out a sarcastic laugh.>>> “You think you know my our flaws?” <<< He shakes his head violently to prove his point.>>> “Boys, the only things you know about us is what you read in the paper or heard in the back. You know the Prime Alliance lost 3 to 0 to the Players Club. That’s it. Other than that, you know zip, zilch, notta, YOU KNOW NOTHING!” “So let me just clear things up.” “First of all, I would like to address you Adien Pryde. Lets clear one thing up real quick. I am TOP NOTCH MALCOLM KENNEDY! I don’t need to know anything about you. I am the fastest rising superstar in the UWF. You think Im gonna waste my time studying up on some little half ass tag team who backs up a disgruntled ass clown of a exterminator? No, No, No, you are sadly mistaken. Yeah, back in the day when you were wrestling in those crappy no name feds with guys who couldn’t tell a Jackknife from a hand job, you might have been a wrestling force. But your in the UWF now. Your in the real world of wrestling now. Those plastic titles you and your buddy carried around wont get you anything but a quick trip to the hospital here. You need to wise up and wise up fast. Your stepping up to Malcolm Kennedy and Ruff Ryder, not Joe Jock the Football Cock and the No Name wonder. Your going to get in the ring with two of the best in the business. I suggest you smarten up real quick or your going to be in for a whole new world of hurt.” “You know, I thought I was bad ass by talking crap to women once. Bad idea. It doesn’t prove crap. Anyone can talk crap to a woman trying to work on the ring. Try stepping to me with that same attitude saying the same crap, and you will end up on the flat of your back before you can blink an eye.” “ You even have the nerve to act the way you do, talk the way you do, give yourself credit the way you do, and call someone else a disgrace to the wrestling industry.” <<< Malcolm shakes with laughter. After a good 25 seconds he settles down.>>> “You, of all people, call someone else a day. Oh man, if that isn’t the pot calling the kettle black. You’re the disgrace buddy, you don’t deserve a chance here in the backyard wrestling business let alone here in the UWF. You need to take a good long look in the mirror and realize what you really are. Just some third rate joke of a tag team who lucked out and found a job with the UWF. But don’t you worry, at SAS this Sunday I will help show the world what you guys really are.” “As for you Mr Dai. For the most part you haven’t dug yourself a hole. What I mean is that you haven’t said a lot. And that is a good thing. Save yourself a lot of pain that way. Just don’t try and get all hard now that I’ve said that and start running your mouth. Otherwise your gonna end up in the same boat as your partner. I think you have realized what your dealing with. A former UWF World Heavyweight champ and UWF North American champ. AND......” <<< Malcolm stands up from the bench as you hear a buss roll up in the background. He gets right up to the lense of the camera.>>> “Your also dealing THEE One Man Army. Anarchy will soon be just a stain on my battlefield. The One Man Army will once again win in battle, and you two will be left with nothing. Ill see you two this Sunday in the ring.” <<< Malcolm takes the headphones that were around his neck and puts them over his ears. He walks past the camera and the camera turns to see him walk up the steps and onto his bus. The doors close and the bus drives off as the scene fades to black.>>>
