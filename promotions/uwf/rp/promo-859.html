A Maskmas Carol (Part Two)

date unknown

Hot Mask

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You've seen them in films like this, so it should be no surprise when we open back up to where we left off...almost. A slightly hazy black-and-white rendering of the living room inside Casa de Cumshoot, with Hot Mask seen fast asleep on his couch, minus the presence of Kenny Mercury. The television set buzzes ever so quietly with the last big of an infomercial for the Flowbee home hairstyling kit. Moments later, after a slow fadeout, an image of an American flag flapping in the wind appears on screen, and the blaring of 'Taps' begins to play in the background. The loud horns are enough to wake and startle the snoozing Mask, who rises to a sitting position in a millisecond. 'Huh? Wha-wha-hm?' With this, a blinding light begins to blast from the screen, causing Hot Mask to scream in terror, not unlike that of a ten year old girl. The light slowly begins to dim as the military evening anthem comes to a stop and the ever-known 'OFF AIR' beep causes a shrill whistle. Mask stares agape in horror in front of him, as the ghostly pale figure stands in between him and television. 'Oh my Mask!' The figure is inspected further, and is now recognized as someone well-acquainted with the Puss Pulverizer. In case the greyscale version of Old Glory tied to a bamboo pole resting on the man's shoulder wasn't enough to throw you off, the facial features of Joshua Justice (in ghastly makeup!) shine through as he stares down at Mask with a mischievous smile, noticing a small wet spot forming in his blue Superman underwear. 'HA! What a pathetic sight!' Justice exclaims. 'A grown man with the mindset and tendencies of a five year old! What do you have to say for yourself?' Mask, still a bit on the borderline of terrified, rubs his eyes to make sure that his imagination isn't just playing a trick on him. 'What do you want from me?' he squeaks, teeth chattering with fear. 'Oh, not a lot. Just...your SOUL!' A clap of thunder is heard, as Justice smiles wickedly. 'EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!' The banshee-like ear piercer echoes out of the mouth of Mask, coupled with a villainous laugh from Josh Justice. 'Just kidding!' he quips, as both the laughter and screeching subside. Justice's attitude quickly undergoes a paradigm shift, as he once again grows serious. 'You are a sick, sick man, Hot Mask. Someone who placates the evils of Joseph Stalin and Adolf Hitler combined!' Mask appears a bit insulted. 'Hey, kiss my grits, you Dick Cheney ass-swab,' he snaps. 'Give me a little bit of credit here!' 'Fine,' Justice agrees. 'Hitler, Stalin, AND Mussolini combined! With a dash of Albert Fish for good measure.' 'That's better,' Mask smiles, face smug with confidence. 'Whatever,' responds the patriotic ghoul, continuing on. 'The fact of the matter is that you, Hot Mask, have several skeletons in your closet that root this problem...and tonight, we are going to deal with these things face to face! Maybe with a fire lit inside your ass, you will succumb to the error of your ways and undergo a change!' Mask scratches his head momentarily, and with perked ears, belts out a rather unexpected response. 'Skeletons in the closet? Fire in my ass? Sounds like the only journey you wanna take me on involves the beef bus driving into the brown tunnel, ya fucking Red, White, and Fag!' He shakes his head unwillingly. 'The Masked Masturbator doesn't switch hit, so I think I'll have to ride the pine...as opposed to you riding my brown star...on this one.' Joshua Justice shakes his head, thumping Mask painfully in the ear. 'Infant.' 'Ow, that hurt, ASS NECK!' Mask cries out, rubbing the fleshy part of his ear. With a staunch tone, Justice calls out to Mask. 'This is NOT an option, you pervert. We are going to take a journey...a journey...to CHRISTMAS PAST...' And with a wave of his flag, the two vanish from the room, making the flashback journey to the most feint memories deep in the corridor of Hot Mask's childhood... * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 'Do you recognize where you are?' the solemn voice of Justice declares, feint in the background where our eyes only distinguish darkness. '...how...how did you do that?' Mask responds meekly. 'Look over there.' The inside of a comfortable and roomy two-story home within the suburban area of Kentucky, we take in our surroundings of the living room. A large and healthy Christmas fur brightening the area, blaring lights circumventing the space and causing an inviting glow to the area. Inside, a man paces back and forth on a rug in front of a seated woman, face buried in her delicate hands as she softly sobs. 'My parents!' Hot Mask exclaims, shielded in the hall off to the side, standing next to Justice. 'Yes, you had quite the life, living in the boyhood American dream. Your parents wanted to give you all the things they never had...and came pretty close, too. Look to your left, Mask. Recognize him?' Mask sullenly peers up the stairwell to the figure of a young boy seated at the top, cautiously looking down below at the scene taking place. Mask gulps as he realizes the mirror image to be young Caleb Mason...himself at the tender age of six. The woven ski mask that he wore to bed each night to scare the monsters away covers his face entirely, as the Super Mario Bros. pajamas clung snugly to his body. HM slowly began to recall this painful Christmas Eve that had been tucked and shoved away in his subconscious for many years. He had heard loud footsteps from his room where he lay awake in anticipation for the morning to come. Hoping to catch a glimpse of Santa, he snuck quietly to the open hall...only to find his parents downstairs. 'No!' Mask yells at the top of his lungs, rushing up the stairs to try and shake his child incarnation. 'Go back to bed! Just go back to bed!' It was to no avail, however, as the child remained responsive, continuing to observe. 'It's Christmas Eve, and you can't even go without hitting that god damn bottle!' the beautiful blonde-haired woman cries in between periodical bouts of sniffling, while her husband continues to pace angrily back and forth. 'So! Who in the fuck are you to tell me what I can and can't do!' he retorts, growing hotter with rage. Hot Mask's mother stands, approaching her husband and cutting off his path of travel. 'Our son is in bed asleep, excited about tomorrow, while all you can think about is YOURSELF! You don't even love him! You don't even love me!' A loud smack is heard that seems to echo across the room, as both Mask and Caleb simultaneously look away, tears welling in both of their eyes'. 'Don't you EVER SAY THAT!' Mr. Mason yells as his wife, who quickly darts past the images of Mask and Justice into the next room. Hot Mask reaches his hand out towards her, but only air is felt as she disappears through their hologram. Meanwhile, the six-year old replica bounds down the staircase, wailing loudly and ferociously pounding his father with his fist (at least as ferociously as a six year old can.) Mr. Mason's eyes sink, as he tries desperately to calm his son down. 'You hit Mommy! You hit Mommy!' 'Whoa...Caleb...whoa...wait!' Mr. Mason says, giving an off-laugh to try and play down the situation. 'Let daddy explain!' Caleb, with rage subsiding, slowly begins to calm down as his father takes to his knees and places his hands on his shoulders. This was a man who had never lashed out at his own child, and cared incredibly for his son's well-being. Although he still remained quite inebriated, he maintained his focus on his upset boy, attempting to reason with him. 'Son,' Mr. Mason starts, 'you are going to be taught several lessons as you get bigger and face new things. Right now, you just saw an important one that should always be taken into consideration when you grow up.' 'Wha-wha-what lesson is that?' Caleb stutters. 'That women don't feel pain, so it's perfectly okay to hit them!' he responds. Joshua Justice shakes his head in complete disgust, as Mask remains blank, continuing to look on. 'But, Dad! Mommy was crying, so she had to have gotten hurt!' 'Well, son...that's ANOTHER lesson to be learned,' he continues. 'Women also like to cry for no reason, that way us men feel pity for them for being so godda...I mean...gosh darn stupid!' Little Caleb looks up to his father, eyes still a bit glazed over as the man's whiskers were a constant reminder that he was a figure to be trusted. 'Son, just remember...I will always love you.' 'I love you too, Daddy!' Caleb says, regaining his composure. The two embrace in a heartwarming display, while Hot Mask and Justice look on. Justice continues to shake his head in disbelief of this skewed scene, and the father and son break away slightly. 'Hey, look!' Mr. Mason states aloud. 'Santa already came, and he left PRESENTS!' Caleb's eyes grow wide with excitement, as he ganders around the tree, observing the bounty left by Jolly Ol' Saint Nick. 'Can I open 'em, please, dad...please, can I?' he bounces, now wide awake on the midnight hour. 'Eh, maybe just one, Caleb...we want to save a bit of surprise for the morning! Here,' Mr. Mason smiles, grabbing a box wrapped in shiny red paper and a green ribbon. 'Open this one!' Caleb tears into the present anxiously, ripping the package apart to reveal one of the essential toys in the 80's for any budding little professional wrestler - the Ultimate Warrior Wrestling Buddy! Caleb coos with delight as he lifts the doll triumphantly over his head and slams it down to the carpeted floor, then straddles over its head and begins to hump its painted face. He and his father laugh with glee as the scene begins to shimmer and fade...right back where we started to the living room quarters of Hot Mask. * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 'All makes sense, doesn't it?' Justice ponders to his UWF comrade, who gingerly takes a seat back onto his couch. 'Sure does,' Mask quips, with a bit of thought in his passing glance. 'Are you going to change your ways?' 'I guess so...' Justice's mouth drops in surprise, as the shock of the effect hits like a two-ton sack of bricks. 'NO WAY! You're kidding, right? You mean that actually WORKED?' 'Well,' Mask replies, 'what's the point in hitting a woman when she can't even feel it? Seems like a waste of time, don't you think?' The shocked expression on Justice's face immediately turns to a look of failure, as he smacks his cheek with disgust. 'Idiot...' he swaggers, slowly making his way back into the television set, disappearing from sight. Hot Mask lets a yawn escape from his mouth as he stretches his arms out, weary and seeking sleep. However, his plight is in vain, as a pair of powder-white hands cover his eyes. 'Guess who?' a female voice giggles, as Mask struggles to pry the hands off his eyes. She reluctantly lets loose as our Masked Savant somersaults backward off the couch, falling on the floor and staring up to the image of a familiar body, also appearing ghostlike as her hair bobs with each adorable laugh. She offers her hands out to help him to his feet. Mask is a bit reluctant, but hoists himself up and stares into the face of his one and only. 'Dad, this one's for you!' He rares back and attempts to slug his ex-girlfriend with a stinging backhand, but upon connection square to her cheek, she merely laughs and shoots him a wink. 'Damn!' Mask steams. 'Old man was right!' Skankalie, crossing her arms over her chest, shakes her finger in a tsk-tsk motion as she smiles at her former love, basking in her power to be unfazed by his strikes. 'My my my, how the tables have turned! Here I am, just little ol' me...unable to let you bully me around. It's sad, really...the potential we could've had.' 'Will you stop babbling already?' Mask remarks, annoyed with her presence. 'Let me guess, you're going to take me on a 'magical journey' to try and 'convince me to change my ways,' right? If anyone should realize that the Mask is one-hundred percent perfection and doesn't need any of that Christmas spirit bah-humbug! Now, either get the hell out of my house, or hey...' Mask smirks, pointing toward his crotch, '...give another for old times' sake!' Skankalie shrugs. 'Funny you should mention that, honey bunny.' She leans toward Mask, who seems a bit mesmerized by her advance, and slowly begins to kiss him passionately. A whirl of smoke fills the room, and in a flash, they vanish into the air... * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 'Welcome to Christmas present!' she quotes, as our scene fades quickly in. An aerial viewpoint gazing down upon a straw shack, where a makeshift chimney blows smoke from the inside fireplace. Seems like quite the potential fire hazard, but luckily for the family inside, government officials really don't care to intervene in this side of town. We zoom forward to the inside, where we are met with the newspaper-covered walls encasing a room containing only a coat rack, a coffee table, and said fireplace. A small black pot hangs over the fire, boiling water inside and causing the steam to lightly saturate the indoor air. Mask and Skankalie go undetected in a corner of the room as the front door opens, revealing the sight of none other than our friend Crotchitt, as he hangs his Jimmy hat on the rack and holds his arms in the air. 'Honey, I'm home!' A portly woman's face is the only distinguishing factor between the two, who are both dressed in paiper mache penis costumes. With a pink apron tied around her, she lumbers over to her husband and the two embrace, nuzzling each others' heads (and I do mean THAT head) in a show of affection. Crotchitt kisses his wife on the shaft, and slowly takes a seat onto a rickety old chair next to the coffee table. 'Where is our son, dear?' Crotchitt states, stretching his arms out in front of him. She staggers back toward the pot, fetching a wooden spoon and slowly stirring the contents while responding. 'He hasn't come home from work yet, love. He should be in any minute!' 'I still say it's an atrocity for a boy of that age to have a job!' Crotchitt says sternly, with a forlorn look on his face. 'Yes, yes, I know,' she responds. 'But, it's not like he's working for that Scrooge of a boss that you have. It's Malcolm Kennedy, and God knows the only hole that Tiny Tim's seen thus far probably consists of inflatable plastic!' 'I just hate it that one fateful day, darling...when Malcolm Kennedy finally DOES get action that's not in the form of his right hand...Tiny Tim will have to perform the most degrading task known to all peni...' 'The Poop Chute!' Both Crotchitt and wife state in unison, shivering with terror. Meanwhile, Mask and Skankalie look on in keen observation. Mask shoots Skankalie a look and shrugs his shoulders. 'Could be worse,' Hot Mask states. 'He could work for Dreammaker and have to spelunk inside a sheep's orifice.' 'Good point,' Skankalie agrees, nodding. Their conversation is stopped by the entrance of their subject, the one and only Tiny Tim...who appears to be about the size of Mask's six-year-old form yet dressed in a similar genitalia costume. He smiles as he hobbles inside the door, assisted by a wooden crutch. 'Hello, parents!' 'Tiny Tim!' Crotchitt quickly rises to greet his son, hurriedly running over to lift him into his arms and kiss him on the cheek. Tiny Tim giggles excitedly as Crotchitt carries him over to a chair and seats him down. Meanwhile, Mrs. Crotchitt has taken a bowl and emptied the contents of the pot inside, bringing it over to the table and setting it in the middle. 'Dinner is served!' she exclaims as she takes a seat across from Tiny Tim and offers him a spoon. Crotchitt smiles warmly as he taps his spoon onto the bowl, gaining his family's attention. 'Family,' he starts, 'let us give thanks to this meal to the wonderful Mr. Mask, who provides me with the salary to have this bountiful feast!' 'Bah!' Mrs. Crotchitt says, with resent in her voice. 'That man is as low as they come! Working you to death for a mere ten shillings a day!' Crotchitt places a hand on his wife's shoulder...or where her shoulder would be...and consoles her. 'Now, now, honey...although Mr. Mask may seem like a cruel man, his intentions are good, and we must always be thankful for keeping hope to this family!' 'Damn right, ungrateful wench,' Mask snaps. 'Hooray for Mr. Hot Mask!' Tiny Tim yelps, as he sticks his spoon inside and fishes out the brown contents, taking a big bite. Hot Mask gazes toward the young dong, with an incredibly sincere look on his face...something very rarely seen. With a smile and a nod, both he and Skankalie observe this family having their Christmas meal...amazed at the unity displayed even taking into account their horrible living conditions. Skankalie places her hand onto Mask's shoulder, and surprisingly enough, he allows her to keep it there as he looks on with awe...as their figures slowly begin to shimmer and fade into the distance...
