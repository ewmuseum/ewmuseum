UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #648

Stop Worrying

8/16/04 11:19 PM (year unrecorded)

Ezra Kazee — The Airport.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of the airport where “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee is sitting. He is wearing a pair of wind pants along with a white t-shirt and a Packers ball cap. He is just watching the people walk back and forth, back and forth, as they rush to catch their planes. Much like the others, he waited to the last minute to books his flight, but he is not as concerned as the others. He is just waiting, relaxing.) Kazee: It’s only hours away now. Can you feel the excitement? Can you feel the intensity? Can you feel the tension? Can you feel it? You can almost cut it with a knife….. Terror in Tokyo. The Regal Rumble. A card that is not soon to be forgotten. From top to bottom this card is going to be one that is going to be memorable. We start with the Double Ladder A-Go-Go Match, end with the Regal Rumble, and in the middle there we have Jamie Shock, Malcolm Kennedy, McClancy, and a tag team championship match…… And we have the World Championship match. We have what many are proclaiming as one of the most innovative and one of the fairest matches to ever grace the wrestling world. Of course, what would they expect from someone who fully believes in sportsmanship and fairness…..? And everyone is off worrying about this and that. Starr hasn’t been heard from in days. Where is he? Why isn’t he around? I’m sure he is training. I’m sure that he is preparing in his own way. It would’ve been nice to have heard from him, but I’m sure that the front office is quite pleased with the hype that myself, Dunn, and Montgomery have given this match….. Speaking of Montgomery, still worrying about your job position and all that stuff I see. I understand that you are upset because you got usurped in your position. I understand that it must tear you up inside. It must absolutely tear you up to know that you are no longer the commissioner. I know it must make you seethe with anger to see Ms. Preest doing your job. But please, don’t worry about it; you have bigger things to worry about….. I’m sure your also worried about your shares in the company. I’m sure they are being taken care of. After all, I do manage the UWF Shareholders Branch office, and I truly have not heard of much movement going on. And being in the position I am, I would know if something was up as I do have access to all of those files…… Of course, you could also be worried about Tropical Storm in this match. Your right, I made a mistake. Tropical Storm is the guest referees. It’s been confusing lately. I’ve been told several things. All I requested was two referees. Who they were didn’t matter to me. Ms. Preest mentioned a couple of names, but I wasn’t really paying attention much past the point of having her say yes to my claim. I had a very capable lawyer there. And I have full faith that everything will be fair. Even with your enforcers…… Just worry about one thing, Monty, and that is the match. I see you got some training in. I see you honed your submission skills. Well that’s good. I am expecting a good match from you. Granted, I know this isn’t your cup of tea, but then again, you are a former world champion and you are right up with the greatest of all time. Just like Joshua Dunn….. And Joshua, you seemed to be a little hung up over a woman. You seem to be losing your edge. This is all fine and dandy. That’s you choice. But this is your championship we are talking about. Do you really want to lose it on your first defense? Do you really want to lose it now? Granted, she is one hell of a woman, but sometime, you have to make sacrifices. Focus, Mr. Dunn, focus…… She seems to be doing a capable job of taking care of your stock as well as whoever is taking care of Monty’s. And she seems to know what she is doing, although I’m a little concerned of her ethics, but whatever works. Just make sure she doesn’t do anything that is going to get you in trouble….... And you seem to be losing sleep and not be in the best of shape. I truly hope that is not the case. One of the reasons I set this match up the way I did is because I wanted everyone to have a fair shake and no excuses. If you come in all tired and out of it, what kind of shape are you going to be in? You won’t have any one to blame but yourself, and I know you are better than this. I know that you are a great competitor who is going to be put up with the rest of the Hall of Famers in the UWF. And I know that you do not want to let anyone down…… (Kazee shifts his position a little.) As has been mentioned time and time again, the Players Club is dead. Monty has made that quite clear. Monty continues to tell everyone he sees about it. There is some confusion about what The Players Club was about. Career Advancement? Sure. Someone to watch a back? Of course. But what was the Players Club really about. It was about one thing and one thing only. It was something that each and everyone of us did every time we went out to the ring. And it is something that we all individually will continue to do…… The Players Club was about going out there in front of the fans of the UWF day in and day out. The Players Club was about going out there and working our butts off every single night. The Players Club was about going out there and giving the fans the best damn show we possibly could. And that is exactly what we did. The fans came to see us. The fans came to see us put on a show that only we could….. The fans knew that when there was a Players Club member on the card, there would be at least one good match. They knew that they would get there money’s worth. They knew that they would walk away satisfied. And the knew that we would give everything we had every time we went out there…… At the Regal Rumble, it will be no different at all. Four Players Club Members, in a match with no interference. A match that is all about wrestling. And a match that can only be described as a match for the ages. The crowd is going to be getting their money’s worth several times on this night. The crowd is going to walk away happy, any way it turns out. Because they are going to see a great match from four of the premiere wrestlers in the UWF today…… In the end, does it matter who wins? Not really. Because who ever comes out on top is going to be a great champion. We all know what we are doing in that ring. We all know where to go and how to operate. And the fans are going to continue to get great matches. The enforcer, the jobs, the stocks, the women, once we are all in that ring, the door locked, and the bell rung, it’s just us four, and nothing else. Fighting it out for the most coveted prize in the UWF. And that prize is going to crown one man as the best in the UWF, and make him the hunted instead of the hunter. The fans get a great match. Our careers are advanced, and the stocks go up. There truly is nothing to worry about except what is in the match and what is at stake. Voice over the Intercom: Northwest flight 769 to Tokyo Japan now boarding. (Kazee looks up, grabs a bag next to him, and stands up.) Kazee: Well, that’s my flight. I will see you all in Japan. Good Luck to you all, and till we meet again, Ja Mata. (Kazee walks off to the gate as the scene fades to black.)
