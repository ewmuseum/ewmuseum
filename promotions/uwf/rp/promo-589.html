Cracking Spines...Smasing Hearts

31 Jul 2004

The Real Deal — Bleakness

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cracking spines...smashing hearts... Love Love can be shown as many forms. You can love a person for who they are or else you can per say love them as a partner. You can love an object or place because it make you feel happy. You can love a hobbie because it makes you feel alive, but you can also love yourself if you're egotistical enough. Back in the past The Real Deal was this egotistical person. He never really cared from anyone but himself. Back when he entered the wrestling world he was so egotistical that he would hardly even acknowledge others, never mind socialise with them at all. His mind was trapped in a conflict which destroyed who was when he was younger. He had become lost in his own self immortality, lost in the greed of power which he had gained in such a short time since starting his profession. He fed on it and it gave him strength. Even outside of the wrestling world, Deal was pretty much the same. Always looking to better himself and not others. He didn't have many friends, two infact. They were SurReal and Baal. They formed a faction known as the Death World Order. It may have been a total rip off of the New World Order but they didn't particularly care. Back then these two people were the only ones that Deal could actually get along with. They shared the same views as he did, drank the same things as he did and on the whole basically wanted to fuck everyone up as much as he did. For years they beat apon people throughout various wrestling federations. Their meanstreak was flawless and never ending, until Baal was sent to prison for murder. This act of violence on the part of Baal struck a nerve in The Real Deal's system. He decided that he was going to open his mind more to the outside world. This left him regretting the past... Regret We can all feel regret at some point in our lives. We feel negative about something or some things that we have done in the past. We figure that it was the wrong thing to do at the time and we simply just regret doing it. After the d.w.o era, The Real Deal lived with alot of regret that hit him after Baal left the group. He realised that he had been a nasty person and that he could be more open to others around him and improve his life. To do this he decided that he would leave the wrestling world and take himself on a journey to set his spirit free from the tyrany that had brought it down to the level it was on. He decided that it was time to become a new Real Deal and make himself a better person. Though sometimes this is easier said than done. His first step was to meet up with his mother, who he saw on occasion and look for the man who claimed to be his father. He needed closure on this issue for he had only met his father once and that was when he was only ten years old. Anger was drilled into his system by his mother when she told him about the abuse that this man had put her through. He would not stand the fact that his father had been beating apon his mother. It was no longer a time to talk to his father, but a time to hurt him...badly. After an extensive search they found the man known as Eric, Deal's father. Eric tried to play it cool and be nice to his son, who he didn't recognise until his wife appeared beside Deal. After realising what was happening, Eric tried to attack his son. This only led to him being taken out by a walking stick and dropped to the ground. This brought alot of joy to The Real Deal and his soul as he had finally gotten a chapter out of his life that had been with him for years. Freedom ran through his body like a wave passing over rocks at the side of the ocean. One chapter finished he would move onto the next... Freedom Always feeling restricted throughout his life, freedom was a new emotion really to flow through the veins of The Real Deal. When he was younger, even though he can't remember, his father would used to hit him and throw him about, even against walls. His mother couldn't do anything about this for she would have felt the wrath of Eric also. When getting older this tormented childhood was probably the main cause that Deal couldn't make many friends. His mind worked different from all the other kids in his school. They all thought he was weird and always asked him what the marks on his arms and legs were....he couldn't tell them. Now finding out that his father would attack him and his mother when he was young let him talk more freely to people about it. Going to clubs throughout the world got him asked many times what had happened to him, now he had no problem in telling anyone. There was a problem with freedom though...it may have let him control his anger alot more and be more independant but without the rest of his team-mate in the d.w.o his ego grew to the size of all three d.w.o members put together. The more he gained, the more he wanted. He would again start to feed and making people hurt and it would give him great pleasure in ending careers. For him, ending a career was great because it would always put a little more spotlight onto him. When the spotlight gets too bright though...what happens? [To Be continued]
