UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #556

Party TIme

16 Jul 2004

Fizbane the Fabulous — Inside of a limo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in the back of a limo. Sitting in the limo is Fizbane the Fabulous. Conspicuous by their absence however are Venus and Aphrodite. Fizzy is wearing a baby blue leisure suit, with his usual cane and his face painted, as your accustomed to see. The music is loud as is usually the case whenever Fizzy’s around. The song is a greatly familiar one for just about everyone. The song is “Ain’t No Mountain High Enough” by Diana Ross. Fizzy is dancing and grooving to the beat.) Fizzy: Hey driver dude? How much longer to will pick up my little chickadees? Driver: Just a little bit longer sir. Fizzy: Ok dude, cool. (Fizzy starts singing and grooving with the music.) Fizzy: My love is alive Deep down in my heart Although we are miles apart If you ever need a helping hand I'll be there on the double just as fast as I can You know that ; they're ain't no mountain high enough, ain't no valley low enough, ain't no river wide enough, to keep me from getting to you babe (The car comes to a stop, and shortly the limo doors open up. In steps Venus on one side, and from the other side is Aphrodite. Venus is wearing a yellow dress, and Aphrodite is wearing a pink dress.) Fizzy: Ow ow, You sugarplums sure are looking hot tonight. (The girls giggle a bit) Fizzy: So are y’all ready to PAAARRR-TAY? Venus: You know it hon. Aphrodite: So where are we going to dance the night away tonight at? Fizzy: I’m not really sure, the driver says he knows where he is going, and I can tell you this. When we get there, we are going to do the Bus Stop. We are going to Hustle. We are going to Hand Jive. And we are going to hope aboard the locomotion. Venus: What about “The Shocker” Dave Storm? Fizzy: Well, if he’s there, he’s more than welcome to show us his moves. I’m sure he’s got some great moves. Aphrodite: I think what she meant is why aren’t you at home studying Dave Storm. Fizzy: Cause that ain’t my style. That ain’t who I am. I party first, and then later, I party harder. Venus: Just thinking that since it has the number one contender ship on the line for the TV Title, you might want to learn a little more about him. Fizzy: You chickadees worry to much sometimes. Look, it’s all good, don’t worry about anything at all. If your worried about my job, I’ve got an iron clad contract to party with the UWF cats as long as I want. And as long as I’m there, you guys are going to be there. You know that. So stop worrying. Aphrodite: But think of the parties you could throw with the TV Title around your waist. Think of all the fun you could have with that belt. Venus: And think of how much more jewelry you could have too. I mean you think you have the bling bling now, wait till after you win that belt. Fizzy: You know, it would be great to have that belt, but it ain’t my style. I got to go with the flow. I gotta be myself. I got to go out there and do what comes naturally and do what I do best. Move and Grove. Aphrodite: I suppose your right. Venus: We just worry about you, ya know. Fizzy: Y’all don’t have to worry about me. With me, it’s always a party. And when I’m partying, you know it’s going to be a party ALL…… NIGHT….. LONG!!!!! (The limo stops. Shortly, the door opens.) Driver: Sir, we are at Studio 54. Fizzy: All righty than, let’s get this party started. (Venus, Aphrodite, and Fizzy all get out of the car as the scene goes to black.)
