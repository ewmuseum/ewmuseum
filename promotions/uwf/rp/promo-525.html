UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #525

Choices

10 Jul 2004

Kenny Mercury — Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.] **BEEP** **BEEP** **BEEP** **BEEP** **BEEP** [The heart monitor’s sensor bounces up on and down with each pulse of Kenny Mercury’s heart. Kenny Mercury lies in a hospital bed, bandages covering his entire body. Suddenly, his body stirs and Kenny sits up in bed slowly. His eye dart about the room quickly, and he glares at his IV filled arms with a puzzled look.] Kenny Mercury: What in the name of precision arm-bars and leg-locks are these? Where am I? What the hecky-naw happened?! [An unrecognizable voice sounds off from out of sight.] Voice: You were burned alive, moron. Kenny Mercury: Burned… alive? Voice: Yes. Burned Alive. Kenny Mercury: Burned….alive. Voice: YES! YOU TWAT! BURNED ALIVE! [Kenny Mercury looks down at his burn wounds, which cover most of his body. He remembers Wednesday Night Underground, he remembers Unforgiven and Slasher, he remembers Tsunami Oni, and most of all, he remembers Misfit and the excruciating pain that Misfit inflicted upon Mercury’s demigod-like body. He remembered how helpless he was against that many foes and how the flames burned him in the casket. He was trapped, defenseless. He choked on the smoke; it burned his eyes and suffocated him… until everything went black. Then, there was nothing. The next thing Kenny remembered was waking up in this very hospital bed, as we witnessed. Kenny was in shock for a moment as he gazed at his injuries… but that shock soon turned to rage. He clenched both his fists as hard as he could and gritted his teeth. His eyes nearly turned red. He again thought of Misfit. It was too much to bear. The IV’s shot out from his veins from the sheer pressure he but on them from squeezing his arm muscles. He leapt from his hospital bed, and with a roar of pain and vengeance, he put his hands under the bed, and flipped it over.] Voice: Whoa! Settle down! [Like an animal, Kenny spun around to see who this voice had been all this time. It was UWF owner, Richard Hayes. Kenny Mercury continued to fume.] Richard Hayes: For Chrissake, Ken, knock it off. I mean, look at you, you’re ridiculous! You’re wearing a God damn gown. [Kenny looked down at himself. Indeed, Mercury was in a fit of rage, ready to kill on a moments notice. Shit, if someone had so much as sneezed or opened a window or something, BAM-BOOM… and they’re dead, but looking at himself… his naked self. Bare-foot…. With only a polka-dotted patient’s gown on… with bandages all over and that hospital wristband around his wrist. Kenny immediately settled down… he was ridiculous… and he hoped to Rod that no one else had seen him in this state of fashion emergency.] Kenny Mercury: Okay…. Okay… I’m simmering… simmering…. Simmering down… Richard Hayes: That’s good. Kenny Mercury: Why are you here? Richard Hayes: Why am I here? I needed to check the health of my associate! My Lieutenant Commissioner. If you were dead or something, I’d need to appoint and new one, wouldn’t I? Plus, you’ve got an Intercontinental Title match this Sunday, and if you were too injured to make it, well… I’d have to find a replacement. There are plenty of able superstars on the roster just DYING to take a shot at that championship. Kenny Mercury: Intercontinental Championship? [Kenny had nearly forgotten about that match, his match for the Intercontinental title against the reigning champion, Raid.] Kenny Mercury: THE INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!! [Kenny grabbed Richard Hayes with both hands around the triceps and shook him.] Kenny Mercury: THE FERAKIN’ INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP!! Richard Hayes: YES! Now don’t touch me EVER again. Kenny Mercury: Sorry… Richard Hayes: Well, though you should be dead… it seems as you’ll be ready for action this Supremacy? Kenny Mercury: Indeed. Richard Hayes: Right, that’s all I really care about. I’m off. See you this Sunday. [And with that, Richard Hayes turned and briskly exited.] Kenny Mercury: Heh… Man… I am quite fucked up right now. [Kenny, again, surveyed the destruction done to his heavenly body.] Kenny Mercury: Misfit, you are one sick fuck-tard, you know that? Quite frankly, I am very disturbed and upset at your recent actions. The path that you have chosen to take… well, in short, it’s fruitless. I am very disappointed in you. No one FORCED you to do this to me, to inflict this hateful damage upon my beautifully perfect body, did they? If they did… If Ben, oh sorry… if UNFORGIVEN or Slashy-Whashy FORCED you do this to me, speak now. And be truthful, I know… sometimes the truth hurts. But tell it to me. Tell me the truth, Misfit. Did they force you to do this… or not? Either way... you had your choice. You see, life is all about choices… And you chose to this to me. You didn’t have to… but you did, and that’s all that really matters to me… is that you, by your own free will or not… YOU ATTACKED ME!!!!! [Kenny rips off his patient’s wristband and throws it to the ground.] Kenny Mercury: And… and… this attack will not go unpunished. You see, for every action, for every choice, there are repercussions. When you chose to do something, something else will occur solely as an effect of your first initial action. Your actions… your gruesome and unheralded attack upon my person will carry repercussions, just like any other action does; it’s no different than any other action. The one difference is… [Kenny begins to unravel his bandages, casting them aside. He tears off the other adhesive ones. His wounds are no where near totally healed, and pieces of his own skin and body are ripped off along with the bandages. Mercury doesn’t even flinch at bit… he doesn’t even blink. He just tosses the bandages aside. Blood oozes from his reopened wounds. Kenny doesn’t….even… flinch.] Kenny Mercury: Heh… The one difference is… these repercussions… these repercussions… heh… these ones… WILL BE SEVERE!! THEY WILL BE PAINFUL AND YOU WILL BEG ME FOR YOUR OWN DEATH, MISFIT!!!! You will plead with me to put you out of your misery. You will beg amidst your screams of agony, to end your life… to take your worthless life. But I won’t. I won’t stop the pain. I, the technical wizard… the definition of precision… will pick your body apart. Precisely targeting those magic little spots that I know will cause you a great pain… the kind of pain that slowly increases as I apply pressure… the kind of pain that multiplies, it builds upon itself, Misfit. Every passing second it gets more and more unbearable, until you’re writhing in agony on the canvass, screaming for me to stop, wishing I would just end it all and take your life. But I continue. I am I MASTER of these maneuvers, Misfit, and you know that. I am the ring doctor, with a PhD in slow physical and psychological torture. And… again… you know that. I am the shooting star… the one destined for the ultimate prize in this federation… and you know that. The one with superior skills and talent, far surpassing anyone in the ENTIRE world of wrestling, let alone the Universal Wrestling Federation… YOU KNOW THIS!! Which only further boggles my mind as to why... WHY IN THE NAME OF ALL THAT SACRED WOULD YOU DO THIS TO ME?!?!?!?! [Kenny rips off his patient’s gown and casts it aside. He stands there, in the hospital, stark raving nude.] Kenny Mercury: WHY WOULD YOU WILLINGLY SIGN YOUR OWN DEAHT WARRANT?!? There is only one explanation, and that is, quite frankly that you are suicidal… you want to kill yourself. You… want… to… DIE! BUT! But… you can’t do it… oh no… you can’t… you’re a coward, Misfit. You’re a coward. You’re frightened…. You’re afraid… you can’t do it yourself… you’re a PANSY! You need ME… KENNY FREAKIN’ MERCURY, THE TECHINCAL WIZARD, THE VERY DEFINITION OF PRECISION TO END YOUR WORTHLESS AND MISERABLE LIFE FOR YOU!!!! But… but… Like I said… I’m going to take you to the edge. I’ll take to you to places you’ve never even dreamed about in your wildest nightmares. I will inflict so much pain upon your body, mind, and soul… that you’ll think that you’ll finally die… but, like I said… it won’t stop. The pain doesn’t stop… IT WILL NEVER STOP, NEVER REST, NEVER CEASE, IT JUST KEEPS ON GETTING STRONGER!!!! And Misfit… as you lie there, clinging to me like the whore that you are… wishing you were dead… remember this… you made this choice… and you have brought this upon yourself. [Kenny Mercury smirks evilly.] Kenny Mercury: And Raid… on Sunday… I use your body… I use you… as an example. An example that needs to be made. An example. What I do to you is but a mere fraction of what I will inflict upon Misfit. I have fought long and hard for years to finally have a chance at the Intercontinental Title… and it shall be mine… oh yes… it shall be mine… and I will have you… as an example. This… I… promise. [Kenny Mercury’s speech comes to an end. He breathes heavily… and falls to his knees, trying to catch his breath. His looks at his wounds… he is naked… He laughs to himself… and the scene slowly fades out to black.] [End Transmission.]
