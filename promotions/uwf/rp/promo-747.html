UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #747

Hot Mask

19 Oct 2004

El Bianno — Stylemeister's Airplane

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens on The Stylemeister's private jet enroute to New York from Sao Paulo. Lyle 'In Style' Wile is introducing one of the millions and millions of Ring Rat Fans into the Mile High Club when The Stylemeister's in-flight Satellite phone rings... ring ring... Lyle: 'God Daminit... sit tight little lady... The Stylemeister will be right back.' ring ring... The Stylemeister climbs off, puts on a robe and answers the phone... Lyle: 'Yeah???' The Stylemeister rolls his eyes... Lyle: 'El Baiano, what did The Stylemeister tell you last time little buddy... I told you not to call me, I'd call you... You know this number is reserved for business, you really got to stop calling me everytime you start to doubt your ability to compete in the UWF.' Lyle walks over to the mini bar and pours himself a drink... Lyle: 'It's a nothing match against a luchadore El Baiano... Why are you so worried???' Lyle takes a drink of 7 and rye as he listens to El Baiano bitch and complain about the match... Lyle: 'What happened to you??? Before I sent you to the USA you were ready, you knew everything you needed to know... Don't tell me that The Stylemeister has wasted the last year and a half training you to be his proxy in the UWF only to have you pull a punk move like this...' The Stylemeister brushes his hair from his face as he listens to El Baiano's response... Lyle: 'Listen punk... The Stylemeister sent you to the UWF because you wanted to kick Gringo ass. I could have left you in Brazil if all you wanted to do was to come to the states to dance and drink and screw... You have a job to do at Supremacy... You have a match against Hot Mask, I know he's not worth getting out of bed, but you got to start somewhere and if the UWF wants you to start out with this Hot Mask punk... you god damn well do it!!!!' The Stylemeister gets red in the face... Lyle: 'The Stylemeister doesn't care if there is a party at some fucking dance club in Miami on Sunday afternoon... You will be at Supremacy, You will be ready, you will be focused... You will destroy Hot Mask or The Stylemeister will destroy you!!!!' The Stylemeister drops the satellite phone and finishes his drink as the scene fades...
