UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #807

working on my dreams

3 Dec 2004

Dreammaker — local theater

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene cuts in to a large dark theater. Which seems to be empty. We see Jeff Johnson, new reporter for teh UWF, walking down the aisle with a huge over flowing bowl of popcorn and a large beer. He seems to be in a hurry as the lights go completely out and a movie seems to start playing. Jeff hustles to the very front and center row, where there seems to be someone large sitting already. ) J: Hey man, sorry I am late. You wouldn't understand the fucking line out there for some thing to drink. (The voice heard is very recognizable. IT's the UWF's very own Dreammaker!) DM: What the fuck ever! We borrow (making quotation marks with hands) this theater to do research on UWF's finest and you worry about beer? We are the only fucking people here! J: Hey! With all the shit you have made me do, this is the least you can do. DM: You know, maybe if you acting like a man, and stop whining, you might get somewhere in life. Instead you just follow me around. Then the UWF will get bored with me and you will have to follow some other 'up-and-coming- star'. So live life a little. Let dreams happen. (Then Dreammaker stands up and yells to the projection room. ) DM: All I see is white screen! Can anybody do anything right around here? (Looks at Jeff) (quietly talking) Man, I am so psyched about this up coming match. My second PPV! (looks at the projection room as a large figure gives a thumbs up) (sitting back down) DM: Think about this Jeff. I will be in my 2nd over all PPV, which I won the first time. Who did I wrestle again? Oh, it doesn't matter. Oh, ya, White Wolf. Since then he still hasn't been the same. I actually really haven't seen him since. But, no-one cares anyways. I got a warm up match for the PPV against Commi. That (makes quotation marks with hands) Rabbi of Pain sure showed me how to play. Boy, glad I stayed up all night preparing for that. He even got his ass kicked by a chick just for losing. You know you hit bottom when women are kicking yor ass. (laughs a little chuckle) Jeff: Ya, you have been wrestling much better lately. Even have a title under your name now. Speaking of titles, how will you .. you.. win this title again agains like..is it.. ya.. 6 other guys? (He then takes a huge drink from his glass.) DM: Thats what this freaking research is all about. ( stands up again) (looking at the back again) WHAT THE FUCK IS TAKING WITH THIS WHITE SCREEN I AM SEEING? (the same large figure is seen looking confused with his hands in the air. ) DM: ( sits back down) That it, that is the last straw. He can't run security, he can't even try not to hurt my girls. He can't even ruin matches by interfering. Shit, the man can't even run a projector. (opens up his cell phone) (It beeps) Bubbles.... (he waits for the beep) yes? where are you? I am almost to the projection room. I am running late. Too many girls have been injured and I actually had to fill in. Sorry for being late> I don't care right now, I got work to do. The club is running great with you in charge. when you get tiem fire the head of security and fine someone else. Okay, I will do that. ( seems like a long silence) (breaking the silence) Jeff: So what about this match? Tell me what you got from these opponents of yours? DM: (looks at his hand like he is counting) First there is Craig Wright and Malcolm Keneddy, who I actually no not enough about. Except that the are a bunch of old timers that can't decide if they actually want to wrestle again. Maybe thats why cats are involved. Cats are lazy and can never make up there minds. Maybe these guys will grab their walkers and come down the ramp and meet us up and coming stars. (He then grabs some of Jeff's popcorn and starts to feed his face) (after spitting a seed at the screen) DM: Damn this is gonna be a long week. Who is next? Jeff: What about Adien Pryde? DM: Who ? (giving a very confused look) Jeff: You know, the leader of Choas Society. The mid carder who wrestling with in this title match also. DM: Wow. A wanna be singles wrestler, who can't make it with out his tag team partner. And i have been watching the other matches and I see that he cant even wrestle a match with out help. NO, I am not going to worry about him. When his big buddies come down to the ring, everyone in the match will more then likely target them because they have no business in any of our lives. Besides who cheating to win the TV title? Give me a break. What next, All out war at the Victory Secrets for who gets the thong? (takes a sip of his soda) DM: Speaking of thongs, Hot Mask, you twelve year old pervert. Man isn't the life of a wrestler fun? How about you think of something other then your junk. An old tale I was told when i was a kid told me about how man who has to talk ' up' his penis, is making up for what he lacks in man power. ( looks at the camera) Give me a break. You got lucky the first time, but now you have to watch your back from all of us. No one is gonna protect you and you huge unit from all the shit you are gonna eat. Writing checks that your ass can't cash is all I see. You aren't amking friends with any one and you will be all alone when there is 6 men looking at you from around the ring. ( just then the screen fades to black and it readds ( from Dream Catchers Theater) (The camera goes back to DReammaker smiling) I love this film. AFter this is over, we can talk about how Hot Mask has to leave his favorite party spot cuz he realized how his girls had bigger units (quotations again) then his. Laughing follows as the scene fades out! (As the Promo ends we hear Jeff sounding surprised and yelling, 'What kind of movie is this, I am gonna need another beer!' )
