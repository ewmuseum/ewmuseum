UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #207

A Proud Past

7 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — Kazee's Trophy Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in a basement area. There is a lot of gold sitting around, be they trophies, belts, or medals. The camera pans over to the right and pans over to “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a black shirt and has a large bandage going across his forehead. He doesn’t look to happy either. In his hand he is holding a belt, not a UWF one, just a belt which is quite a bit smaller than any belt that has been seen on UWF TV, or any other TV wrestling program, but it is yet a belt.) Kazee: Damn it, Damn it, Damn it. I can’t believe that just happened. I can’t believe that a man whom I know so well would attack me for greeting him to where he belongs. What is up with that? And what is up with the booking committee to put us together on the PPV. Are they looking for fireworks? And on top of that, will you look at this card. Raid and I vs. Montgomery and Kennedy? Why isn’t it I and Montgomery vs. Raid and Kennedy? Oh well, it’s not about bitching and moaning. Montgomery and I, we know that this can happen, and it’s life. Stable mates, we sometimes face each other, and its life. Mike, I’ve never faced you to my recollection, but lord knows that when the Players Club is in the ring, all hell is gonna break lose, and it usually spells bad news for someone. And in this case, that someone is going to be either Kennedy or Raid, or both, who knows…… But Raid. Raid, you, what in the world goes through your mind. Yeah, your right, I should’ve known better than to talk to you after a match. Your one of the most intense Sons of a Bitch I have ever known. And you will always be. But damn it Raid, it’s me. You know me. You trained with me. Hell, we’ve been up and down the road with each other……. (Kazee holds up the belt.) Do you remember this Raid? Do you see what I have here? This here, this is the first ever title that either of us held. This is the first ever championship that either of us held. I can remember it all really well. We were wrestling in the Fond Du Loc Armory; we were wrestling for a guy named Jerry. We had been there, in Steel Pro Wrestling, for a couple of months. Then, we get to the arena one day, back when I wrestled as Mr. Meaner. He informed us we had a Title shot. Against a team called Mason-Dixon…… That match was one of our toughest that we had fought to date. Us, two rookies, taking on the Tag Team Champions. And we won that match. We took them to a new height and they never knew what hit them. And that’s when we first started our dominance in the independence up north. That’s when we as a team Raid dominated the Independent seen from Kalamazoo, Michigan to Pierre, South Dakota. It didn’t matter where, it didn’t matter who, it didn’t matter how, we were living as high as you could on the independent scene, and we were Tag Team Champions all over the 5 state regions…… Then, as the history books show, I got myself a big break. I made it to the semi big time, and finally, to the big time here in the UWF. But you know what, I never forgot my friends. I never forgot who helped me get to this level, because I knew that you were just as good as I was, if not better. And ever since I made it here in the UWF, I’ve been lobbying the front offices to get you up here. I’ve been telling everyone who would listen that you are an absolute asset. I knew that you were still struggling down there in the independents and I wanted you to get your shot. When I heard the news they finally signed you I was absolutely elated….. I thought maybe we could get together again like old times. Take the first tag team gold we ever won and make it into even more gold here in the UWF. We could become one of the greatest tag teams ever seen here in the UWF. Up there with the Dragoons and the Phenomenons…… Then, last week, after all I wanted to do was congratulate you on making it here. Congratulate you on where I knew you could finally make it, and what do you do? You Bug Bomb me through a damn table. Yeah, you’re right, I should know how intense you are after a match, but even a friendly word should NOT HAVE SET YOU OFF LIKE THAT……. (Kazee throws the belt to the side) Well, Raid, I’ve been on the phone all night for the past two days with the Prime Alliance begging them not to retaliate on my behalf. Montgomery was pissed. Dunn was up in arms. We had just celebrated our biggest victory to ever happen here in the UWF and you go and ruin it. Make no mistake Raid, I’ve begged on your behalf this time, and I’ve been able to hold the dogs back, so to speak, but next time, you might not be so lucky……. Raid, it’s time that we return back to our old days. Raid, we use to be an unstoppable team, and I know we still have that in us. Things have changed, but we are still the same. You are still the powerhouse I have always known. I am still the technical master that you have always known. Yes, our philosophies have changed, or at least mine has, but that doesn’t make a difference, because my rage is more focused now, and you, your rage has always been on a very one track mind, you just hurt anyone……. Let’s shake off the ring rust Raid, let’s go back to who we use to be. Let’s prove to the world that we are and will be one of the most devastating Tag Teams in the world…… But make no mistake Raid, if you even so much as slap me upside the head, not only will you not leave with the titles, you will not be leaving under your own pressure. As much as we as The Players Club hate the Prime Alliance, and its rubble left over in Ruff Ryder and Montgomery’s partner of Malcolm Kennedy, we will not hesitate to take you out as well. You have been warned Raid, now be sure to understand, or else we will make you understand…… But I know you Raid, you will make the correct choice, and in the end, the UWF Tag Team division is going to need all the luck they can muster. (Scene fades to black.)
