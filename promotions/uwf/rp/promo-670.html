Honour

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — read the rp

[Scene opens with Nick Persia talking on the telephone. It seems that he’s standing in a big hotel room. The room is in top condition, everything perfectly clean. Nick Persia just sits on a chair staring with his eyes to a place in the ground. Suddenly someone knocks on the door. Nick Persia quickly ends the conversation and opens the door.] Nick Persia: “What the hell are you doing here?” [The Camera turns and we see Johnson in front of Persia] Ben Johnson: “I came here to bring you some news.” Nick Persia: “Great! Make it quick.” Ben Johnson: “I just received word that you are supposed to face Seymour Almasy, Slasher and Raid.” Nick Persia stares at Johnson with disbelieve. His mouth falls open. Nick Persia: “After all the torment, they are actually sending me to the butcher?” Ben Johnson: “I forgot to add...it’s for the number one contendership for the THC.” Nick Persia: “Slasher at his top? You mean the Slasher absolutely destroying everything on his path? We aren’t actually talking about him, are we?” A worried expression appears on his face, almost demanding an answer. Ben Johnson: “If you mean the Slasher that became the two time THc Champ, well, yes.” Nick Persia: “Oh my god, I am so doomed. Tell me, what have I done to deserve this? What have I done so I deserved the wrath of God? I have worked all my life for everything I wanted. I never just got anything. NO! I have endured more pain than you could ever imagine! I have let more tears than you can count! I have lost more blood than I posses, I am practically bled dry. And still, after all i have done, I am giving this destiny? I mean, he will hit me with everything he sees. Baseball bats, chairs, barbed wire, tables, ladders, C4, it doesn’t matter to him. So please tell me, what have I done to get in this match?” Ben Johnson: “I think this is a match of former champions!” Nick Persia: “Luckily Raid has been out of action for a while and hasn’t been that impressive after losing his intercontinental title. Maybe he isn’t even interested in this whole match and wants to get his IC title back. As far as Seymour goes, well, I beat him before.” Ben Johnson: “Word has it Seymour Almasy has been demanding title shots, and I was wondering if you could tell us something about that.” Nick Persia: “Well, in every fed you have a Hogan type of guy. He earns big money, wins every title totally undeserved and demands main event matches while he hasn’t done anything to prove his worth. I guess those types will always remain. I think the guy should become more down to earth. After our last confrontation he should have realised I don’t make much jokes. When I say something I mean it. I don’t have the time to make jokes. I am always obligated to stick to my schedule. Always I have to train and the time that i am not training I am travelling or doing other work for UWF like giving interviews. But not once I have been able to talk to the man in control Richard Hayes. Not ever.” Ben Johnson: “So it’s true he wants to have a go at every title?” Nick Persia: “I heard something about it, yes. Absolutely out of control. I have never ever met anyone with such an ego. I mean, sometimes I might talk like I am the big thing, but he is talking like he is superior to us. Obviously somebody must knock some sense into him and who better than Slasher? He will cut him with the barbed wire, adding a nice few scars to Seymour’s collection. He will continue his rage with smashing him down to the mat with a chair until the chair is useless for anything. He will not even attempt to pin Seymour because he just doesn’t see the pleassure in doign that. To top it all of he enjoys seeing somebody suffer. How barbaric can someone get? Disguisting!” Ben Johnson: “The question that pops up in my head if how hardcore are you? After all, you are the longest reigning THC Champion ever.” Nick Persia: “And everybody is making sure that I don’t forget it. Lately people have been calling me Mr. THC. I mean, that’s just unbelieveable. I have got many compliments in my life, but this is a compliment for something I had never wished fore. This goes beyond me. I am a old skoll type of guy. One on one matches, under normal rules. Those matches absolutely rock the house. That is, if both men are doing what they are supposed to do. You need to give it your all to entertain the fans. And I will only use one weapon. My body.” Ben Johnson: “Seymour Almasy is a member of the Knightmare Brigade. Reason I am telling you this is that I can recall you losing to Corporal Punishment.” Nick Persia: “Now you bring me that to my attention I see that I am now able to complete finish off the whole Knightmare Brigade. Sean McClancy and the Formorians have left, and well, I took care of Corporal Punishment already so that leaves me with Almasy. I showed the Brigade that it isn’t all about words, but about deeds. And my deed proved to be very powerful.” Ben Johnson: “But you are forgetting a few things here. Almasy is a two time World Champion. And he is the current PTc Global Champion.” Nick Persia: “Jay Phoenix is right. Jay Phoenix is damn right. When he told the world Almasy was yesterday’s news he spoke the truth. And something that is also the truth is the fact that I do not only like to beat Seymour, but de humiliate him. I am looking for a 2-0 score against him. Two victories in a row and his precious GTT 4 tournament coming up. Can you see the damage I could do to his reputation?” Ben Johnson: “I suppose a lot.” Nick Persia: “I can do more to him than a lot. Do you know what lot means in Dutch? Fate! And I think he should realise that his fate could suffer a whole lot of pain with a whole lot of punishment and more important, emberassement. I can see him falling flat on his mouth after I beat him. All his talks about being the greatest will have no meaning any more. They future I have set for him does not look nice. But hey, I never said I was a nice guy. I offered him respect. I offered him so much yet he refused everything. He signed his own deathwarrant. Not me, not Oni, but it was him and him alone.” Nick Persia shakes his hand. Nick Persia: “It’s sad to see someone talking a shitload of trash while he should be occupied with more important things. I mean, he’s gaming all the time. That’s really sad. A whiz kid he is in every sense of the word. He’s a nerd. He sees complot theories against him everywhere and he thinks he being hold back. But the fact is that he should get real for just once and see how things go in the real world. I mean, it’s not my fault that he never saw anything of the world but what the TV showed him. Probably how he learned to wrestle. Most kids start in the backyard. He started in his bed. In all his youth he never left his room. He spent all his time with gaming, watching TV and sleeping! If he had to eat he yelled to his mum and that bitch delivered his food. When he needs to go he goes, because he is wearing a pamper. I won’t show you, but if Luke Warren ever feels like pulling Seymour’s pants down we would probably see a pamper!” Nick Persia laughs sadistically, with a bitter sound of irony he talks on. Nick Persia: “I think he still lives in the same room. In his own imagination. Becauyse somebody with the size of his ego has a serious ailment, and in all honesty, I don’t think that the best doctors in the world can heal him. And I think he will panic some time soon when Seymour starts to realise that I am too much of an opponent for him. That I am worse than tsunami Oni. Because he might hold one victory over him, because he will get none over me! I swear you this, I will rather commit suicide than live on with the embarressement I wil have if he beats me!” Ben Johnson looks on with a suprised look on his face. Nick Persia: “That’s right, Seymour! I gave you all chance in the world to say something about me. But everytime you do your thing you paint me off like I am a nobody. You need to realise something really quick and that is that I don’t like ego’s and that I don’t like you underestimating me. You might be on your guard for Slasher, but if I catch you one second off guard you will pay for that mistake immediately. I would watch my back if I were you. Oh wait, that’s not really necessary, is it? Because that Japanese whore will probably help you out. If she decides to interfere on your behalf I promise you that she will get a special treatment, even worse than the one Slasher will be giving you. Besides, Japanese people are very proud people. They would rather die with honour than live on without. If a woman, even a bitch like she is, helps you out you will be losing much of your reputation.” Nick Persia spits on the ground. Nick Persia: “Damn right this is about honour. I won’t rest until I beat you. I won’t rest until you suffered a big loss. I would fear for everything you have. Your life, your home, your fan base, your reputation......because my friend you have made the biggest mistake of your life: neglecting me is something you just don’t do. After SAS, you’ll realise why.” Nick Persia slowly turns his head to the reporter. Nick Persia: “And you get out.....NOW!” [The camera man leaves the scene quickly, happy he has bene able to conduct an interview.]
