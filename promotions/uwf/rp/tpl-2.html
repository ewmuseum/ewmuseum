Trailer Park Luchador #2

7 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Lah … lah lah lah lah … lalalalalalala!” [It was a trailer home much like any other trailer home.] [Except, of course, this one houses a morbidly obese woman and her professional wrestler son. So technically, it’s not like very many other homes. But my point still stands. It was a trailer home. Stop arguing semantics.] [And within this home, we are treated to perhaps the most disturbing image in wrestling history.] [The Trailer Park Luchador … hero of the Ozarks … superstar of the mulleted … is in the shower, singing horribly.] “I’ll be your groupie … whatchamacallit … cuz you … something something superstar …” [As if there could be a more horrible image than a malnourished redneck wearing a luchador mask that only barely covers his blonde mullet showering … that very nude redneck singing an Usher song reassures us that it always gets worse.] “Number one fan … lalalala … something autograph …” [Anyway … we’re fast-forwarding now.] [And several minutes later, the Trailer Park Luchador stands glistening, a towel wrapped around his waist, another towel over his shoulders. He steps forward … but then, his eyes go wide, and he gasps!] “Gasp!” [Standing in the doorway of the bathroom of the flipping superstar’s double-wide is none other than … the Teddy Bear. He is propped up against a stack of books, and his eyes glow faintly. With EVIL.] [Suddenly, dawning realization hits the Trailer Park Luchador like a shot. He stands in the middle of the bathroom, dripping water, and pointing at his bear in horror.] TPL: “Wait … I know you. You’re … you’re … !” Teddy: “Yes … I knew it was only a matter of time before you discovered my true identity. I am living death. The very incarnation of evil. The personification of everything that goes bump in the night. Nightmares made reality … the scourge of the world … the …” TPL: “Oh. Never mind. I thought you were David Cross.” Teddy: “…” TPL: “…” Teddy: “…” TPL: “I’m just saying. David Cross is awesome.” Teddy: “I fucking hate you.” TPL: “I get that a lot. Usually it’s a bunch of college kids screaming it while they beat me up and steal the money mom gave me to buy groceries. I should probably stop wearing the mask to Win-Dixie …” Teddy: “Look, you idiot. I’m here for a reason.” TPL: “Because you’re an inanimate stuffed animal with no mobility who only exists in my horribly deluded, self-hating imagination?” Teddy: “Well … yes, actually, I suppose so. But in addition to that, I am here once again to tutor you in how to become … less pathetic.” TPL: “But how? I’ve tried everything!” Teddy: “No, you haven’t. You haven’t yet tried the only thing that can truly make a nobody into a superstar in the wrestling industry …” TPL: “What!? I’ll do anything!” Teddy: “A heel-turn out of absolutely no where. The bad guys get all the love in wrestling, genius.” TPL: “But I’m not a bad guy. I cry every time I watch Waterboy.” Teddy: “Right. You should probably keep that to yourself.” TPL: “But I …” Teddy: “Listen to me, you fool. You are a jobber … you know this, and so does everyone else. A complete change of everything that you are is the only hope you have of not becoming part of that giant turtle freak’s stools, which we both know is the only logical conclusion to your misguided abortion of a career.” TPL: “Yeah. I know.” Teddy: “So what are you going to do about it?” TPL: “Move to Michigan?” Teddy: “… no. You are going to do everything I say. You are going to become my protégé. I am going to tutor you in the ways of evilness … and you shall become the most evil, sadistic, bastard who ever lived!” TPL: “Oh! Oh! Oh! Like the dad on One Tree Hill. Oh man, he is soooooo mean. He’s all like, ‘Oh, you’re not my son anymore, I’m cutting you off, I only love this son cuz he’s my real son and you suck.’ And then it’s all like … ohhhh snap.” Teddy: “…” TPL: “You know what I’m talking about. Eh? Eh? You know what I mean.” Teddy: “Are you going to take the necessary step or not, you tool?” TPL: “Anything to keep El Turtlé from eating me. That’s just nasty.” Teddy: “Good. Your first step in learning how to be a generic evil bastard and succeed in the wrestling world is to find someone you can assault out of the blue, with no provocation, and use as your stepping stone to success and world domination!!! Mwahahahahahaha!” TPL: “Sweet. Who?” [Teddy Bear glares at the Hero of the Big Oaks Mobile Home Park and groans.] Teddy: “Well, normally I’d say to find someone you know you can beat with ease to make yourself look better, but …” TPL: “What?” Teddy: “Right. I’m going to have to look into this. Maybe there’s a woman on the roster … or … a referee … or something.” TPL: “I could sooooo take Jerry Kohler out. BAM!!! Just like that. See how much of a jobber I’d be when that wrinkled punk is all on the floor and … actually, I like Pops. What am I saying?!” Teddy: “Quiet. You have sworn obedience to me, so leave the leg work to me. All you have to do is follow my instructions perfectly, and I will save your career, and give you all of the success you could ever dream of. You will be my foot soldier in this war, my son. My inbred, mulleted foot soldier.” TPL: “You know what, Teddy?” Teddy: “What?” TPL: “You’re my best friend.” Teddy: “Why are you …” TPL: “Come here, Teddy.” [The Trailer Park Luchador grabs his teddy bear and begins hugging him very enthusiastically.] Teddy: “No! What are you … unhand me! Stop that!” TPL: “Best friend!” Teddy: “Stop!” [And so the blossoming friendship of a Luchador and the masochistic figment of his own imagination (who happens to be bent on world domination) was born.] [And of course, roughly twenty feet below this horrifying scene, Baron Black Scourge shrieks as the wet Luchador blows the bear’s circuitry and sends feedback roaring into the Baron’s headset.] Scourge: “You will pay for your shoddy circuitry one day, Sony … one day!!!!!” [Baron Black Scourge shakes his fist with evil condemnation at the sky, as we leave.]
