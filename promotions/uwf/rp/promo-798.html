UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #798

FAN 1: A shot at the TV title

1/12/04

Craig Wright

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Craig Wright is back in effect. The Hot-Shot proves once again that he has still got it in the squared circle with an impressive win over El Baiano at WNU. Its great to win your debut match, OK Hot-Shot may not be at the top of the ladder already but he has opened many peoples eyes including the UWF superstars. Will this comeback be a meer visit again for Craig Wright or will this be the start of a new era. I time where king of the ring will be Craig Wright. Now Craig Wright is in action this Sunday at FANdemonium. On Sunday Hot-Shot will be in a ring full of Tables, Ladders and ..... CATS!! oh and 6 opponents. Why would Craig Wright lower himslef to fight in a match with such silly stipulations. First of all it is the UWF and secondally for the Television title. A title that Craig Wright has got so close to but never actually held. This will be the fourth time that Craig Wright will have the chance and honour to win the title. It's been close but no cigar. ON Sunday Craig Wright will step in the ring with Current television champion Hot Mask, Killer Dude, Dream Maker, Aiden Pryde, Joshua Justice and Malcolm Kennedy. Craig Wright has never stepped in the ring with any of these Superstars meaning theres a lot of homework to be done for the 'Highlight Of the Night' Craig Wright. Thanks to Craig Wright's loyal fans he will have to fight in a Tables Ladder Cats match. The strange fans have always had a soft spot for the Hot-Shot and they prove this by voting the stipulations. How will Craig Wright fight in a ring with such stipulations this indeed is the most challenging match the Craig Wright is goin to have to go through ever in his career. Will it be Worth it. Will the scrathes and bumps and bruises that Wright will recieve in this match really be worth it? Hell Yeah! its worth it, its for the Television title. It seems that the UWF is getting stranger and stranger. We have had L.U.C.A.B.O.T weve also got El Turtle. We have a massive Communist and a Evil Villian as the World Champion. Then we have the Hot-Shot Craig Wright. A complete normal human being living in a not so simple life. Will this effect The Hot-Shot in his match on Sunday or can he keep his cool and walk out the arena as the New television champion for the first time in his career. [The scene opens In Regal Gym opened by the legendary Wilhelm Regal a wrestler famous for his brawling and his tactical knowledge in the ring. A punch bag is seen taking a hard beating from rapid taped fists. The bag is hit powerfully in many directions. After another minute of punching the bag comes to a hault. The camera focuses on the taped up hands, pans out and reveals to the world that it is none other than the taped hands of the 'Highlight of the Night' Craig Wright, The smiling Craig Wright looks over to one of the trainers and recieves a thumbs up. Craig Wright walks over to the water machine gets a drink and begins to speak for the first time after last weeks victory.] Hot-Shot: Wow that was some party last week huh? Craig Wright steps into the UWF arena once again and in his debut match defeats El Baiano. Not only that The Highlight of the Night proves he has still got it. I told you I'm back and ready for action. This week has been a time for joy for me. After partyin with the girls and getting absolutely blitzed on alcohol I recieved a phonecall and got the news that I will be fighting once again for the Television Title. I take this time to thank the fans for voting for me and following me after I ditched you and made you suffer to watching guys like Dream Maker and El Baiaino. Maybe this was why you all made my Television match a Table Ladders and Cats~!! match. I'm not going to take that out on you guys after all this is the UWF anything can happen in the UWF this match is an example of this. [One of the gym trainers walks over to Craig Wright and begins to speak.] Trainer: Ok Craig your lookin damn good. I haven't seen anyone hit that bag like that in a while. Wont need the duster out tonight. If you ask me this time round I think your ready to win the Television Title. Your Upper-body strength has improved dramatically since you last came here. You been on that Steriod package I use to tell you about. They have invented this new drug you can find it anywhere these days such as toothpaste and Vapouring oils its really good stuff you sh..... Hot-Shot: Nah Nah I've not been on the steriods and im certainly not inhaling fumes I've just tooken myself a little bit more serious. The old Craig Wright use to think that a few hours at the gym a week was enough. Not this time around. I've fully committed myself to be someone here in the UWF. I aint goin to be a pushover like I use to be. Trainer: You can say that again Craig. I remember when you were stepping in the ring al happy as if you won the lottery then had your head jumped on like a packet of crisps an angry little kid can't get opened. [Hot-Shot laughs] Hot-Shot: I acknowledge that Tom. I feel in perfect condition now though. I don't care about the money. [There is silence for a second, the Trainer looks at the Hot-Shot with amazement on his face.] Hot-Shot: Well I do care about the money but thats not everything. I want to be remembered here in the UWF for being someone. I don't want to be remembered as the jobber who would talk shit then get whipped corner to corner. I want to be remembered as the greatest ever superstar ever to walk the planet. I can be if I keep focused. This Table Ladders Cats match, weird as it is, is goin to be really important. This will be a very challenging match. Winning this match will be everything to me. I know if I can come out vectorious in this match I can do anything here in the UWF even pull Shiatsu. No but being serious I want that Television title and I want it bad. Everything has been falling into place for me right now. I won my debut match I haven't been as fit as this ever in my life and I have my first title shot on Sunday, winning the title would just round off everything. [Craig Wright walks over to one of the walls and bgind to stretch his lower body. The trainer walks over and helps him balance.] Trainer: Craig, That was a really good performance from you at WNU El Baiano was no challenge really for you was he? Anyway who are you fighting at FANdemonium any real challenges for you here. Hot-Shot: Every match for me is a challenge. Every match is as important as the last one. Sure some of the wrestlers here in the UWF are better than others but hey They are all a challenge. I know that I am better than I use to be and I know that I have a good a chance as the other guys fighting in this Table Ladders Cats match as i doubt this is anyones speciality. I have never stepped into the ring with any of my opponents as of yet. I have to eleimate 6 men for the title. Even though this is the television title it was one of the most challenging. To hold the title is a real honour and I praise Hot-mask for holding it right now but Hot- Mask don't get too attached to that title because on Sunday when it is removed from your waist I will be the owner of the title from that moment. the only thing interrupting this is the other guys in this match. I'm not underestimating anyones ability but I am that bit better than all of you. Killer Dude you have been doing well recently in the UWF, but then Killer I feel I am at the top level of my game. My performance against El Baiano says it all. This match will only be a small sniff at the title because it will be mine. I also say hands off to you Dream Maker. My dreams will never be reality to you. I will achieve things that only can happen in fairy tale stories. I will be Television champion for the first time ever after Sunday. It will be the start of a new era. Dream Maker you have to snap back to reality. I have achieved so much already you just wish you could be like me. I have so much money in the bank that I could feed Ethiopia. I have more cars in my garage than the Toyota garage at the corner of the street. I have had more women in my bedroom than the playboy mansion. This is something you will never have because you don't have the ability to be able to knock me out. [Craig Wright walks over to get another drink. He begins to unwrap the tape of his arms slowly while talking to the camera.] Hot-Shot: I am hungry for success this time round. I am back for good. I have emerged from my cave to actually be a success here in the UWF. I am eager to win the title and thats what I will do. It's nice to see Malcolm Kennedy back in action in the UWF. Kennedy you know what its like to fight in Television championship matches and fight some grueling battles. You havent yet stepped into the ring with me yet though. It could possibly be one of the last matches you will ever fight as I am no pushover no more. My advise to and Joshua is dont bother turning up and save yourself a few bucks. You don't want me hospitalising you in a match you know that you can't win. This Oppurtunity for the title this time I will take serious. I haven't been this hungry for success in a long long time. In fact 4 years. In 2001 I was Regal Rumble runner-up. No one remembers second place though. I'll make sure this time I walk out number 1. I'll prove to all of you that I am the 'Highlight Of The Night' Craig Wright. [Scene Fades]
