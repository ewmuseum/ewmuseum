UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #437

Victory tainted.

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 week ago “Congratulations man” the words came from all around as John Edwards walked back to the arena from the construction yard, the brutal setting for ACWF IV, the battle ground on which he won his new found glory. The PrimeTime Central, extreme title. John nodded at the crowd gathered outside of the UWF arena, he was physically and emotionally tired but he owed them this. It was strange, in the federation they hated him, sure they appreciated that he was more than able to put on a good show, but his persona was what they hated, and they hated how he claimed he was going to destroy the federation, that he would rebuild it from the ashes. Yet when he represented the federation he was welcomed and cheered on. It was as though the entire crowd that attended UWF had forgotten about his misdeeds, forgotten of how he left the federation and for just one night were willing to accept him as their saviour. John reached the back entrance to the arena and opened the door slowly, the masses behind him chanting his name, well his moniker anyway. MOR AL ESS MOR AL ESS. He smiled to himself, through time he had come to realise that Moraless was a part of him, but it was not the whole deal. He was John Edwards but the fans would always know his as Moraless, he didn’t mind anymore, he knew who he was. Turning slowly John dropped to his knees and opened up his kit bag rummaging around in it until he found what he was looking for. His pride and joy, something that was going to get the crowd cheering they had waited for hours to see him, he owed them this. Slowly he pulled out two large straps of leather from his bag, each plated with gold the name ‘Moraless’ engraved on both of them. This was his time and it was sure as hell their time. Thrusting the two title belts into the air, John let out a small cry of triumph. The fans went ballistic as the flashes of their disposable cameras temporarily began to blind John, he didn’t care though. He was bleeding and he was bruised from his battle with Sean McClancy but this made it all worth while. He remained there for a good few minutes the UWF tag team championship and PTC Extreme championship high above him until he finally lowered them and waved to the crowd, he had given them what they wanted and they loved him for it. He turned away and slowly entered the door to the arena, the cries of the crowd drowned out as he entered the arena. The corridors seemed whiter than usual as John slowly but surely waded through them, his eyes blurry, and the neon lights above him seemingly flickering with every step he took. His breath became heavier, causing him to prop himself up against the wall, resting his head against it. He remained there for a few moments, trying to pull himself together, trying to get the oxygen circulating through his body. He knew that something was wrong. Lifting himself from the wall he began to lurch down the corridor once more, a large stain of blood on the white wall where he was resting. He finally reached his destination, the resurrections dressing room; he opened the door slowly and stepped in. Bondage’s ‘special friends’ Sugar and Spice dashed forwards and hugged John tightly, causing him to wince as they did so, the bruising on his body taking another battering. “Congratulations honey, we are here for you tonight if you want to celebrate you know” Sugar whispered in John’s ear, usually the thought of the evening with two nymphomaniacs, especially those as attractive as Sugar and Spice would be nothing but heaven on a platter but he wouldn’t be up for it tonight, not the state he was in. John nodded and smiled after receiving a peck on the cheek from both of them, moving past them to mingle with his stable mates, Malcolm Kennedy, Bondage and Digi all of whom were sat in the dressing room discussing the events of Supremacy. “Don’t worry about it Kennedy, you’re still getting used to things a five way is not exactly a walk in a park now is it” Digital Mortality spoke, Digi had a way of calming things down, it was probably why he had become the leader of The Resurrection, the man who would lead them to victory at stablewars. ”It’s not like we have anything to worry about now, I took Mike out of commission we are good to go, now the one thing that we will have to sort out, is who goes for which title” continued Digi before the room fell quiet. This was always going to be a flashpoint, every wrestler wants to be successful and in the end every wrestler wants just one thing, the world championship. Stepping into the centre of the room John threw his bag down and looked at the extreme belt in his bag, he turned to his fellow members of the resurrection and as he opened his mouth, fell to the floor silently blood still trickling from the back of his head. The Day before Wednesday Night Underground. “Are you sure you’re going to be ok to defend tomorrow, you had us all worried with that passing out thing and the blood URGH I mean just bah” described Digital Mortality as he looked across the ring to John ‘Moraless’ Edwards. The swelling on John had gone down, but the cuts were still there, still healing, still ready to be torn open at any second. John simply nodded towards Digi and smiled. ”Come on, you know that I’m going to be there even if I had to be wheeled to the ring. I want to be in that ring tonight to rub in the fact that I am the extreme champion, that I bested the formorians, second cousin twice removed or whatever the hell relation to Sean they are. Digi I haven’t let you down yet, we share the dream and the vision.” John spoke as he climbed out of the ring, various sweat patches on his attire, giving the indication that they had been training. “I’ll be there tomorrow doing my job, just you make sure you do yours man, we can think of this as a precursor of things to come, of our victory over the players club at StableWars.” And that was all that was said, as John left gym leaving Digital Mortality, with a concerned look on his face, not entirely sure if John was ready for battle.
