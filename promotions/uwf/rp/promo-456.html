UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #456

Shitty, but i can't be assed

18 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Age, it’s an important factor in many people’s lives, but it is not always a good thing, sure experience comes with age, but the Human body isn’t like a fine wine, it deteriorates with age. There comes a point where a man can no longer implement the experience he has accumulated. The oldest tree in the forest may indeed be the strongest, but a sapling will bend and not break. What has all this to do with wrestling? Well if you think to yourself age and wrestling, you instantly think of the dinosaur of the squared circle, Corporal Punishment. Sure the man’s been around the prison block a few more times than everyone else, but he’s an inmate short of a dinner queue. Now faced against one of the more notorious members of the UWF, Joshua Dunn, could the aspiring Granddad stave off the arthritis long enough to attempt for a win? We can only speculate [The Scene opens, we can see the strong wind out of a window, when I say see, I mean we can see various scraps of litter whipping around, and the branches of a tree waving erratically. A reflection on the inside of the glass is barely visible, but we can make out the thoughtful features of one Communist Scotsman, Joshua Dunn. The reflection sighs and turns away from the mirror. The camera pans out a bit and the actual Scotsman can be seen, he wanders over to his communist sofa and flops down. There are only so many things that would make this man act the way that he does, one being the death of a family member, seeing as his past has been stripped away from him, it won’t be that. Another would be deep disappointment. Possibly disappointment at losing his chance at the Tag Titles thanks to Kevin Starr, but there will be other chances, other partners, other champions. But all in due time] [Dunn looks up, he looks just as we speculated, the gasp of air leaving his mouth before he speaks says it all, Why Try] JD: Well it seems I am not the Tag champ, neither could Starr and I take down the Formations, but we did get a draw, which is more than anyone else has. And what next for the Champion of the people, Giant Killer of the UWF? He’s been sanctioned to take Granddad for his weekly exercises. [Dunn shakes his head in disgust] JD: Thing is Corporal Punishment may have done it all, but that was back in the day, something to sit kids on his knee and tell them. “I did that” and they will look on, captivated. Now he’s only a relic of what once was, he can’t stand a chance against the fresh faces here. Youth can and does win over experience. He’s past his expiry date, now it’s time to get his state pension to make his existence bearable until he goes to guard that great big prison in the sky. [He pushes himself off of the couch and goes to the door, opens it and steps through, but before closing it, he sticks his head back through it one last time to say one thing] JD: I’m off to a Gym, even though he is ancient in athletic terms, there is no truth to the saying you can’t teach an old dog new tricks. He can work a video player. [He closes the door and the scene fades]
