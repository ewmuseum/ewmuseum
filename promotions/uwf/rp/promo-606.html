UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #606

Gimmicks and a Plague

7 Aug 2004

Jamie Shock — Home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Almost a week has passed since a measure of revenge was extracted on Malcolm Kennedy after weeks of conflict between himself and Jamie Shock on Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. Jamie was meant to be facing Slasher on Supremacy, with Malcolm Kennedy as the referee. Of course, whoever made the match would have known that it wouldnt have gone smoothly for the competitors. With the official 'bent', Shock resorted to attacking the referee, before Slasher showed a sense of agreement to Jamie's cause with a huge move through the announcers table, destroying Malcolm Kennedy.] [Before Slasher's involvement though, Ruff Ryder entered the arena, re-uniting the entire former Prime Alliance Stable. Both Kennedy and Shock were suprised and confused to whos side Ruff Ryder, a faithful friend of Jamie, was on. It appeared there was no deviation to Ruff Ryder's christmas card list as he proceeded to hand Jamie a crowbar and get a small matter of revenge for the vicious attack he suffered a week ago by Malcolm. Ruff Ryder then layed Kennedy out and left the ring.] [So after weeks of Malcolm getting the upper hand, Shock manages to deflate the bubble of momentum his former stable mate has been inflating, as well as his ego perhaps. Kennedy, dazed, was met in his locker room after the match. Richard Hayes entered, wasnt met with kind words, and in turn set a 'Jamie Shock -v- Malcolm Kennedy' match up for the upcoming Terror In Tokyo Pay per View, with Jamie Shock able to choose the gimmick for the match.] [Richard Hayes's involvement with Shock didnt end there though. He also announced that Jamie would face a returning Plague on this weeks SAS, the winner of which would become the LAST entry of the Regal Rumble in 15 days time. The looser though would be the #1 entry, of course, giving little to no chance of victory.] [As you can imagine, Shock isnt exactly laid back. Knowing he's in a match where the outcome could be an easy ticket to a #1 contenders spot for the World Title in days time has given Jamie sleepless nights. Not only that, but Sarah has returned into Jamie's life, under the premise that Shock is no longer an evil crowbar swinging git, but a generally nice transformed person. But has he really changed?] [We open in Jamie's apartment. The sun is shining, in through an open window, lighting the room to it's extent. We hear the sound of fans in this large modern flat, which hints of money with the wacky modern art scattered over the walls, and the view over a river which couldnt have been cheap. It goes without saying that Shock's career has bought him wealth.] [He lies on a white leather couch, eyes shut, hair blowing in the cold breeze emitted from the fan beside him. He's peaceful - that is, for a few moments, before Sarah decides to shout down his ear.] 'YOU havent changed a BIT!' [Jamie's eyes open. He quickly sits up, gasping.] Sarah: 'No wonder you didnt want me to watch Supremacy. Well Mr, I've just watched the tape, and I saw yours and Ruff Ryder's little show!' [Shock sits up, ready to defend his case. Luckily he knows Sarah as a pushover, and doesnt make much effort to wake up fully.] Shock: 'Listen Sarah, that wasnt planned! It was a matter of revenge. I was thinking of you see...' [Sarah's eyes widen.] Shock: 'I was so mad when Kennedy whacked me with that crowbar again, and again, and again..' [Sarah's eyes narrow.] Sarah: 'Urgh.. the twat!' Shock: 'YES! I know.. see, I needed to get revenge.. I only whacked him once if you remember love. He cant get away with hitting me time and time again, causing permanent scars. I'm twenty three, I'm not even a proper man yet, and if so called wrestlers see that attackers get no up and commence for attacking the Hall Of Famer Jamie Shock brutally they'll jump on the band wagon and use me as a stepping stone.' [Jamie reaches to Sarah's hand. Sarah moves forward and sits on her boyfriend's lap. Jamie smiles.] Shock: 'So see, I needed to teach him a lesson..' Sarah: 'Well I'm still not happy with Ruff Ryder being with you again. I mean, you change when you're with him. I thought you said you'd changed..' [He sighs.] Shock: 'I can keep the same friends. Ryder IS a good friend you know. He sticks up for me when I'm out numbered, he'll agree with whatever I think. He's a decent bloke. I know, I knew him when I was a bit weird and a bit obsessed with being evil, but Sarah, he's adopted to the old me. Other people can change too!' Sarah: '... oh I'm sorry, I just worry you'll go all evil on me again. The last time you did was so unexpected, I should have guessed when you started wearing that Tuxedo all the time.' [For those of you who have forgot, Jamie Shock turned against the fans with 'Mr Tuxedo' - a masked wrestler everybody thought was Ace Thunder. It was shortly after that Jamie started swinging a crowbar into many faces of the UWF roster. Jamie smiles as his own genius.] Shock: 'It was a phase.. you even said yourself I was under achieving. What was I? A former TV Champion at that time, and.. and a tag team champion with Eclipse, who was so damn good he wouldnt have needed me to win, he could go in single handed he was so bloody talented. Every shot I got I was screwed, I was angry and.. well it's over now. I'm back to my old self.' Sarah: 'Yeah, the cocky twat I discovered in a scouse drinking pit' [They both laugh, recalling past times.] Shock: 'Yeah well in that phase I won every-damn-thing. Unfortunatly my reign as [b]the[/b] World Champion will go down as me being a complete..' Sarah: '..tosser..' [He pauses, then continues.] Shock: '..which I am not too happy about.' [He slowly stands up.] Shock: 'That's why princess, I just HAVE to win on Supremacy. If I win, the World Title Shot is mine. I can redeem my legacy!' Sarah: '... but you're against The Plague! - and you have Malcolm Kennedy eager for revenge!' Shock: 'Oh dont worry. Malcolm Kennedy isnt my concern, he's not used to being destroyed like he was on SAS. I doubt he'll even show up to be honest!' [He starts to walk across the room.] Sarah: 'The Plague's eager to wrestle you. You and him have had a rematch waiting to happen for a year or two! Shock: 'The Plague.. phh.. he has ten moves to his name! If it were a hardcore match, I'd actually be concerned. He's one tough cookie. [Jamie walks over to the fridge, and slowly opens it. He pulls out a beer can before shutting the door with his elbow. He opens the can.] Shock: 'But it's not. When wrestling is concerned, he's no better than the average mid carder. I'm better than him. I know it. He's far too predictable in the ring, and out. Fair play to him, he's a good bloke, but wrestling wise he isnt anything tremdously special.' [He takes a drink as Sarah talks.] Sarah: 'Well glad to see you're so confident!' [He pulls the can down from his mouth.] Shock: 'Hey I've spent too long worrying. I know what I'm capable of. I'm not a Grand Slam Champion for nothing you know. Listen - The Plague is like the little hand on a clock; in which you know where it's going next. I know his style, I've wrestled more matches with him than anyone.' [He takes a quick swig of his drink.] Shock: 'Ahh - Malcolm Kennedy is no concern, he wont show up, and if he does, I'll deal with him. He should know that if he ruins this for me I'll make him pay in our match in Tokyo.' Sarah: Hey thats a point! [She interupts.] Sarah: 'Your and his match!' Shock: 'Yeah what about the damn match?' Sarah: 'You havent chose the stipulation! Hayes said to Kennedy on Supremacy that you're choosing the stipulation!' [Jamie intakes some more alcohol before responding.] Shock: 'Ah yeah, I've emailed Hayes. See, I thought long and hard about it. Me and Kennedy.. a match I partly returned just to have. A match where I show him just WHO is boss. A match where I prove everything he has said about me being a 'washed up so and so' wrong. The truth is.. oh, I dont want no stipulation. I want to wrestle him, and as that's the way I want it to be then there will be no stipulation. It will just be a straight forward one on one bout between two rivals.' Sarah: 'Are you sure that's what you want it to be?' Shock: 'Yes I do. By god it will be a great night, I'll defeat Malcolm, then win the Rumble. I know I can do it, I was hot seed for it a year ago.. just.. well, Eclipse was in it, enough said. This year there isnt any big scary names, and the fact I will be coming in at good ol' number 30 means I cant loose.' [He throws the can into the bin.] Shock: 'Right now though.. I guess I have to concentrate on The Plague. I kinda feel sorry by beating him I'll make him be the #1 seed, but to be honest.. he never really stood a chance of winning the rumble anyway did he?' [He jumps back onto the couch. Sarah gets closer and puts her arm around him, snuggling upto him.] Sarah: 'I know you're not so confident really.. just because I'm here..' [As Sarah's eyes close, Jamie's cocky smile retreats into a more serious look. He takes a deep breath before leaning back, resting his head sideways onto the back of the couch. It's obvious he has some doubt.]
