UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #297

Chaos at K-B

2 Apr 2004

Anarchy — K-B Toys

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchy Promo [Scene opens to a street just outside of a K-B Toy store. Inside you can see children of all ages browsing up and down the aisles. The camera man starts toward the door as a mysterious arm reaches in from the side and opens the door for him. Once inside, it is a battle to make his way up the aisles. Ahead you can see 2 familiar people. Pryde and Dai, they are standing in front of what appears to be action figures, in fact they are UWF Pro-Wrestling action figures. They each are holding 2 and shaking their heads.] Dai : Damn, 5 bucks for some pieces of plastic that look like us wrestlers, where the hell is our cut? Pryde : See this? [Pryde points at something on the package] Pryde : Ages 5 and up. Kazee is pushing it there [They both laugh. Pryde looks up at the camera] Pryde : Kazee, I thing you have been taking a few two many bumps to that head. Don’t mater because a Renaissance we will be taking those Tag-Team Titles from you. The way I see it they have 2 main event teams with use and the Players Club, then a few out of touch former great teams and 2 whores. Major cluster frick but, it makes it all the easier for us. Dai : We aren’t sure what the rules are if it is first pin or elimination(Sorry, my computer can’t access the forums), but we are walking out the Tag-Team champs. Then when Raid takes you IC title, the Chaos Society will be well on our way to becoming the premier stable in the UWF. [Pryde picks up a package off the shelf] Pryde : Damn, these guys are fast. They have a Nick Persia doll already. [Pryde throws it over his shoulder and the 2 begin to walk out of the store. Once outside they start walking up the sidewalk.] Pryde : Persia, I told you not to ignore me. I despise people who do that, and you found out what I do to people I despise. You come in, make a couple of TV appearances, have a couple of lucky matches and you think you are the man. Your match with me was a three way and you hit me from behind when I was dealing with the third person in the ring. You pinned me, I will give you that, but when I requested a match to see who the better man was one on one, you blew me off. Well blow me off now. Oh sorry, I guess you can’t with a brace on your neck and a trip to the hospital. I dare you, I just dare you to step in the ring without acknowledging me again. You may be beyond the care of a hospital. [Pryde and Dai walk up to a black Navigator with the plates of ANARCHY on it.] Dai : Kazee, you are right, this is a spendy vehicle and we do have a decent contract here, 25 grand each is a far shot from a 5 million signing bonus that you buddy Montgomery gave Plague when he arrived. With all of us wrestlers being part owner of the UWF, and all contracts coming out of the UWF treasury, we intend to take that 5 million out of your ass Montgomery. No wrestler is worth that much money, especially when his check comes out of all of our pockets. A good place to start with will be taking those gold belts. [Pryde and Dai start to get into the SUV. Pryde looks over his shoulder at the camera and shrugs his shoulders] Pryde : See you at Renaissance, where the Players Club loses everything. ANARCHY will prevail and CHOAS will be on the top. [Pryde closes the door and the SUV drives away as the camera fades to black]
