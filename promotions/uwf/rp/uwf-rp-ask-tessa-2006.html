UWF — Roleplay

Ask Tessa

2 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 March 2006 Following the lead of about a dozen people in attempts to keep the UWF's forum active. I suppose I'll take questions on just about anything. ---- 2 March 2006 How do you plan to handle Steven Caldera’s hostile takeover of UWF? :smiley: ---- 2 March 2006 In a recent Team Baked joint, Bammy stated that 'Seattle Weed tastes like Seymour Almasy's ass, I know this because Tessa Windsor told me.' 1. Is this true? 2. If so, why doesn't he shower before the salad tossing? ---- 2 March 2006 Are you going to win the Total J Cup? ---- 2 March 2006 1. Considering I work with Team Baked in GCW, how badly would you like me to bludgeon them for besmirching our relationship? 2. As a former member of PCW, you've been around the PTC community for a very long time. What's the biggest change you've een between then and now? 3. If I take you shopping tommorow, will you wear the special outfit tommorow night? *hopeful* ---- 2 March 2006 Caldera: I welcome it with open arms, so long as it means I’ll get a raise. :smiley: When it comes to the J-Cup, I wouldn't go so far as to say I'll win. I'd have ot be pretty damn good to get past the likes of Digi and Pajaro's, who has already beaten me once. So I'll leave that as a no, unless I'm at the very very top of my game. Seymour: I think the biggest change I've seen around here is talent. When I first came in it was all about Chryste, Angelo Deville, Xavier Kannon, Digital Mortality, Callisto Bane (though that was short lived after her scandal), and of course you. If you were not familiar with those names, then you were considered under a rock. Now half of those people have dissapered, and while you and Digi are still awesome, now you've gotta compete with the likes of people like Shadow, Dawn McFarlane, Rich Rollins, and Karina Wolfenden. I think the attitude of a lot of people has also changed. When I first came in, I seemed to be surrounded by egomaniacs like Chris Storm and Jason Hughes. I'm talking about people who were midcard level, if even that, seemingly thinking they should be god, when in all honesty they were sub par level. Now it seems people are more intent on proving themselves, other than bitching their way to a title shot, or the top of a fed. That's a change I'm so relieved to see. And yes I'll wear that outfit, even if we don't go shopping. ;) As far as Team Baked, I'll leave that up to Seymour to handle however he feels fit, which by all means is probably a total beatdown. You poor poor boys. ---- 2 March 2006
How do you plan to handle Steven Caldera’s hostile takeover of UWF? :smiley:
Hostile? I’d accept candy as payment for it. :smiley: ---- 2 March 2006 Thanks for the kinds words Tessa. What do you think is the biggest improvement in your work in all these years you've been in this community? ---- 3 March 2006 Hmm, biggest improvement. That's a tough one. I'd probably say I've improved most on the tag front. When I first started out I was definetly intending to be nothing more than a singles wrestler, and that was that. Yet somehow it all started with Erik Erratic back in WIW, wanting me to tag with me. Then Storm, for our big PCW run. From there I kinda built up the knowledge of what I needed and how to balance my partner out, and stangely enough now I'm probably more known from my success in tag teams between Storm, Karina, and now Almasy, than my singles runs. ---- 13 April 2006 Sid Torvald from the PTC Global News. Only one question comes to mind: Will you EVER rid yourself from that egotistical asshole, Chris Storm? From what I've seen, he's holding you back in your career where you can do much better than what you're currently doing. I believe that you can become the UWF World Heavyweight Champion any time you want, but the only problem is that Chris Storm is there, holding you back and demeaning you and your career. What are your thoughts on that? Thank you for your time. ---- 14 April 2006 You've been all around the wrestling environment so to speak. How do you get the businesses to portray you as an individual rather than a half-naked woman to stare at? What do you think can be done to get viewership changed from staring, to actually seeing you as a wrestler and admiring you for your skills? And while we're at it, we all get a little tired of this business. Myself included. So how much longer do you see yourself in this business, and what plans do you have for the future? ---- 15 April 2006 Sid: Kinda a tough one there. Can't say that I've really excelled in the short periods of time where he wasn't around. Then again, same can be said for him being there. I don't really think he affects my performance in the ring, only my mood. As far as getting rid of him, that's a lot easier said then done. As is, the decision as to what our relationship is, is still a work in progress. I can agree that he has his selfish moments, but at the same time I can't deny that he gave me the boost I needed to jumpstart my career. Syko: Truth be told, I feel that the only people that will ever view you as an individual inside of this business, are the people that get to know you as an individual. When people see that you are serious about what you do, it's kinda hard for them to put a stereotype on you. So any comments outside of those I'm close with in the business, I try and take with a grain of salt. It's the same with viewers. I can go out there and plead my case as much as I want, but so long as I'm female I'll probably always be judged more by my looks than by what I actually do in the ring.I can't really do much more than go out there and put on a good match to persuade them away from that stereotype, because I'm certainly not going to go out there dressed like a man just to appease the few that don't want to accept females in wrestling. As far as my future with this business, I can't really say I'm sure at the moment. I've recently signed a deal with GCW to fill for the gap of shows [and pay] between UWF. I'd certainly love to have a good singles run in either UWF or GCW, but outside of that I really don't have any solid goals for the future. Obviously I'd love to win the big one, but I just don't see that happening while I'm a single parent. It's too much to juggle at once. I can't really put an estimate on the time I've got left in the business. While I may be in a male dominated business, I still have my womanly dreams for myself. First and foremost is finally figuring out who I want to spend the rest of my life with, and then going on to the whole marriage and kids thing. That's probably when I'll leave the business. ---- 15 April 2006 Next time I have to carry you, will you stump up some cash for a good lower-back support? ---- 15 April 2006 Nope. You were warned well ahead of time ;) ---- 15 April 2006 I'm going to ask the one thing on everyones mind. Tessa… Do you still fear “The Orginal Pranksta” Fred Grest? :stuck_out_tongue: Note: Nobody needs to take this question more serious then its meant to be. Which is not at all. ---- 15 April 2006 More importantly....Do you still love Taurus Capone? ---- 17 April 2006 For Ms. Windsor. How come you use pink lettering to answer your questions? It’s so…eww… :smiley: ---- 28 July 2006 What, in your opinion is the hardest thing about E-Fedding and how do you personaly deal with it.
