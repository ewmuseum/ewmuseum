UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #384

Phillips Debut (Anyone interested in angle with me read and Kyle Cross too)

3/05/04

Bret Phillips — WNU (PR Promo)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras switch to the back and focus on a black limo pulling into the parking lot. The chauffeur exits the front and talks the long walk towards the back of the limo to open the door for the VIP in the back. The door is opened and a unknown exits the limo with the crowd left wondering who this is. The guy has an excellent build on him and stands in at around 6’4”. He is wearing a smart suite and a Rolex and is very much dressed to impress. He towers over the limo driver and we hear his first words. “Just park somewhere and I’ll be out after I’ve introduced myself to this damn place and remember to keep your pager on so I can tell u when to come round, ok?” “Yes Mr Phillips as you wish” “Good and if you don’t screw up and you might just of got yourself a permanent job” The chauffeur shuts the door and walks back up to the front and re-enters the limo. He pulls off looking for a parking space. Meanwhile this unknown enters the arena with a purpose in his walk and its not long before he bumps into a production member backstage. “Hey, you must be the new guy, how ya doing?” “Who in the blue hell are you? You think you can just wonder up to me and start a conversation? You’re a nobody!” “Well you’re a fucking nobody to asshole learn some man………..” With that Mr Phillips grabs the man by the throat and throws him up against the wall and has a killer look on his face. “LOOK HERE YOU PIECE OF SHIT! Don’t you dare talk to Bret Phillips like that or he will destroy you. I could kill you right now u stupid arrogant piece of shit. I’m not in the mood EVER for people like you who think they are better than me. Let me tell you I may be a nobody right now but soon everybody in this damn federation will know who the hell I am including you. I’M BRET PHILLIPS!” “Shit man I’m sorry” With that Phillips throws the man to the floor and continues his advance to the stage area. He finally gets there and……………………………… 'Time To Play The Game' by Motorhead hits the PA system and after a few seconds Bret Phillips appears from the behind the stage curtain and at the top of the ramp with water bottle in hand. He looks towards the ring with a mean stare and begins to walk with a purpose towards the ring tipping water onto the top of his head as he walks. He reaches the side of the ring and takes a swig of water from the bottle before throwing it into the crowd. Phillips then jumps onto the appron and faces the crowd with his head down and waits for the cue in the music. Phillips then raises his head and arms and spits water vapour into the crowd he then looks at the crowd with a mean stare for a while and spits a little more water vapour out before entering the ring and approaching the far left turnbuckle. Phillips climbs to the second rope and lifts his arms above his head and adopts a mean look on his face. Phillips then drops down from the turnbuckle and demands a mic from ringside and he snatches one from the grapes of the ring announcer. “I’ve had enough of this crap in this damn federation. Everywhere I turn all I see are nobodies holding titles and claiming to be the best. I see people at the top that I can beat with no hands and I see the lower ranks full of absolute bullshit! I signed for this federation because it needs me; it needs me to take it to the top. It needs me to be its antidote and drag it through to the big time. It needs me to rise through the ranks like the pill of crap they are and take they all out with swift ruthlessness and aggression that I can only posses. THIS FEDERATION NEEDS ME! I can see everyone of you fans thinking yeah sure, yeah whatever, yeah I’ve heard all this before but I don’t give a flying crap what u losers think (CROWD GIVE REAL HEAT!) Yeah you have heard it all before and so have I but the difference is I can deliver where others have failed. Why am I so different? Well just look at me, look at my physique: Chest: 48' Arms: 18' Waist: 29' Legs: 25', I’m 6’4” and 264 lbs. I’m 240 pounds of pure muscle and strength. I can bench press 370. It’s not just my physique that makes me the damn best; I am the damn smartest wrestling you’ll ever face in that ring. You’ll never face someone as finely tuned to the wrestling world as me and I’m only 25!” Phillips takes a look at the crowd who are giving him real heat and chanting “asshole” “I don’t ask you to like me I just want you to; no I demand you RESPECT ME! (Crowd go crazy with jeers) I’m going to take this federation by storm and I may start at the bottom but it won’t be long before I the main event stud of this damn place. First I need to start with some gold and I see the tag division as a good place to start. So I offer any singles superstar out here in UWF a chance to win tag gold. You won’t even have to work particularly hard because I could win those damn titles on my own but unfortunately a stipulation is that u need a partner. All I ask is that you don’t get in my way and you’ll be holding gold before long. So to whichever loses hold the tag titles back there then hear my warning: I’m coming for your titles and there is nothing you can do about it. I don’t give a damn who you are. I don’t give a damn if you’re 2 ft nothing, I don’t give a damn if you’re 11ft tall because there is not one opponent I will ever or have ever met that I can’t beat. Heed my warning champs cause providing that owner knows who the talent is around here he’ll give me what I want because he must know its important to keep Bret Phillips damn happy!” Phillips stops to stare at the crowd again that are giving him real heat. “What’s the matter with you people? Do it hurt you to see me? Does it hurt to be half a man when you see me? Does it hurt you that all you bitches want a piece of me? (Crowd sound like they want to kill Phillips except a few females’ screams) Talking of winners I have had my first match booked in this very arena. (Crowd cheers) Shut the hell up assholes I would be anywhere but here for my first match. (Crowd boo loudly) My first match is against some dude called Kyle Cross I believe. You look impressive Cross you’re 6’2” and 238 lbs but guess what I’m 6’4” and 240 lbs and unlike you Cross I am not carrying any excess fat I’m pure muscle, look at me. Tapes of you suggest that you like extreme wrestling with a little submission. Well Cross that’s useless against the greatest ring tactician alive today. You’re extreme style will just see you crash and burn and your submissions what get locked on and hell even if they do I won’t submit damn it. I’ve wrestled idiots like you before in my younger years and they always ask me afterwards: Phillips how did you move out of the way of my 900 degree splash or whatever the hell it is. Phillips how did you manager to escape my finisher? Well it’s simple I’m smart and I use that in the ring and Cross come this Wednesday you don’t stand a chance in hell of beating Bret Phillips the smartest man in wrestling today.” Phillips drops the mic and exits to the back with the crowd giving him real heat. When he gets to the back he whips his mobile phone out and pages the chauffer and continues his advance to the parking lot. The Chauffeur opens the door and Phillips climbs in. The limo driver re-enters the limo and drives off
