Pryde Comes Before the Fall

24 Nov 2004

Clyde — Somewhere out there.....

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I stand gazing down upon a rocky cliff, somewhere in the middle of nowhere. I find it suits me well. You might call this a hobby of mine that I have developed. Yes, even monsters such as I need hobbies.....we all need something to take our minds off of the humdrum of everyday life. As I look upon the endless terrain, the sun beats down upon my brow. And I think of certain things. I think of events in my existence that have led me to this point. My downfall, it always seems, was my own vanity. For eons, I believed foolishly that I could bring about my own demise. In spite of any evidence to the contrary, I made it my focus in life. My focus in life. My death. I chuckle, as a dry wind whips across desert sands, scraping the skin of my face as I stand atop my lofty perch. But as I said, it truly was vain of me. After all....if the fates have decreed that one such as I should be tormented for all of eternity, who am I to argue? ........I'll tell you who I am to argue. I am a God upon this earth. Among the storm of life, I am the rocks upon all which come to be broken. So why should I die? Why should I even attempt to fight such a fate? I will tell you. I won't. Ha....it is amusing.Turns out it just might be true what they say....if you can't beat them, join them. In my case, I choose to simply go along with the tide. Yes, I choose not to allow you peons to defeat me. Oh no....I choose to stick around. You'll never be rid of me, kids. I have focus now. It's a scary thought, isn't it? The cry of a vulture pierces the air, swooping for carrion to feed upon. Circle of life, and all that. Born together, die alone. Lay there in the burning sun, waiting for some scavenger to pick the flesh from your bones. Which brings me to you, Adien Pryde. In this grand circle of life, how did you ever come to cross me? What could have ever possessed you to do such a thing, oh child of man? I know the answer to that one as well as you do, Adien, though the ability to glean the thoughts directly from your pitiful mind may have left me with my latest death. Vanity.That's all it is....pure vanity. You could escape my wrath. You always have a choice in this life, I've found. So why don't you run? Why don't you save yourself? Why? I'll tell you why. Because your ego leads you to believe that you actually have a prayer. Not against me. In life in general. I am here to deliver you from such lies though, Mr. Pryde. Your prayers don't fall upon deaf ears.....no, they don't. For the God who you pray to chooses not to allow them to even reach his pearly gates. I know. I've met the guy. Pryde. Ironic, isn't it? As I stare up into the burning sun and remember the face of a diety you may or may not believe in, you shall soon stare into the same. My face. And I laugh again, as I launch myself off of this rocky cliff. As the air whips past, as the ground races to reach me, no longer do I seek my death with this little hobby of mine. Rather, I revel in it, Adien! I revel in the fact that nothing may stop me, when I do not wish to be stopped. Not gravity, nor useless god, nor you! As once again, I reach the ground and that which does not kill me makes me ever stronger, I smile. As my bones break upon impact, I still manage a laugh. Adien, my brother. Adien, my soulmate. Adien, my victim.....pride truly does come before the fall.
