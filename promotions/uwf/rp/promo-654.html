UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #654

Day 28: The Final Broadcast

28 Aug 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — Tokyo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Day 28 […static…static…static…] […static…static…] […static…] [The camera shakes violently as the view fades in, the result of a frantic attempt to get the handheld camcorder to work. Few details are made plain; the white plaster walls of a room, filled with fuzzy posters for obscure events in a foreign language slowly come into focus, followed by a row of storage lockers. A bench cuts the room in half, and a small curtained-off corner suggests the presence of a not-so-private shower.] [The confines of a small locker room, halfway across the globe in the city they call Tokyo.] [Sitting on the far end of the bench with his forehead in his hands, “The Renegade” Rich Rollins’ mood is apparent even without seeing his face. His shoulders heave in slow, methodical rhythm. His hair hangs unchecked around his face, falling down his bare backside.] [Like the tension in the air before a flash of lightening, The Renegade’s frustration is suffocating.] Lacy: (off screen) You did good, tiger. You did good. [Her words bounce around the tiny room, a hollow sound. Still, Rich Rollins says nothing.] Lacy: (off screen) You could say something, you know. I’m not stupid, Rollins… I know you’re angry. I know you’re upset, but you don’t have to play the role of the silent recluse. People lose, it’s just a way of life. [Silence.] Lacy: (off screen) Damnit Rollins, talk to me! [Slowly, Rollins’ shoulders stop heaving. His head lifts from its perch in his palms, turning ever so slightly toward the camera and its feminine operator. Brushing a disheveled clump of hair behind one ear, Rich Rollins reveals his face in all its hardened glory.] [At least, it would be hardened were it not stained blue. The victim of one of Tsunami Oni’s Evil Blue Mist attacks, The Renegade’s face is covered from chin to hairline in a dark indigo liquid. The consistency is smudged from obvious attempts to wipe it off, but the strange substance was persistent. His eyes are scorching, bloodshot and haggard from the fumes.] [Once eyes of fire, they now reveal the very human, very real person that is Richard Andrew Rollins.] Lacy: (off camera) Does it hurt? [His expression doesn’t change. The hairline fissure that represents his mouth opens amid the blue ocean that his face has become, and his voice resonates with restrained fury.] Renegade: The only thing hurt is my pride. Do… do you really need to be filming this, Lace? I’m not in the mood for a candid interview. Lacy: (off screen) You owe it to the documentary, Rollins. Renegade: Documentary? Lacy: (off screen) Do you remember why you grabbed this camera in the first place? [Rollins’ eyes glaze over for a second, trying to recall the answer to her simple question. Indeed, The Renegade had stolen the camera from the UWF Arena the night be beat Jay Vyper to win the World Television Title. He’d called it his “documentary,” the on-going chronicles of a man on a mission. A man out to break barriers, to shatter records.] [The record held by Seymour Almasy, to be more precise.] Renegade: Yeah, I do. (he looks up at the camera) But it’s done with now, Lacy. Malcolm Kennedy managed to catch me at just the right moment… and now he has the TV title. So put that damned thing away. I don’t feel like being the subject of somebody’s journalistic boredom. Lacy: (off camera) This is your final broadcast, tiger. The book end to one hell of a run with the single hardest title to run with. You know… you’ve still never been beaten one-on-on in the UWF. You’ve still never been pinned. It took fifty-nine men gunning for a one-way ticket to glory to finally knock you off your perch, Rollins. Can’t you be content with that? [Her words strike deep. Had the somber mood in the room not been so thick, the image of a blue-faced Renegade would have been comical. But there was nothing funny about the look in Rollins’ face or the tone in Lacy’s voice, not now.] [Not ever.] [Standing upright, Rollins’ bare broad shoulders spread out like the wings of some terrible bird of prey, his exposed chest glistening with the sweat of spending almost exactly an hour inside the ring. He nods once, acceptance creeping into the pupils of his bloodshot eyes.] Renegade: You’re absolutely right, Lace. Lacy: (off camera) I usually am, tiger. You should listen to me more often. [Rollins smiles at that, an expression that has come to be a rarity on his usually stoic features. The hairline of his mouth begins to move as if to respond…] KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK-KNO… [The furious pounding on his locker room door startles The Renegade, and his smile immediately drops to an agitated frown. Scooping up his sweaty white tank top, Rollins pulls it over his head as he walks toward the door, Lacy following his every move with the unerring eye of the camera.] [Before The Renegade can snag the door handle, it bursts open of its own accord, admitting the biggest Russian on the UWF roster and the two smallest Asians.] [The Communist. Duc Chu Song and Thai Trang. For some reason unbeknownst to him, Rollins can’t help but smile once again as the three other members of the newly-formed Judao Communist Revolution storms into his locker room, each one sporting a red yamakah with the yellow Russian scythe and hammer emblazoned across them draped across their shoulders.] Communist: Comrade, where is your yamakah? Tonight is a night of great celebration! [Rollins absently turns his head toward an open locker, where up until a few hours ago his World Television title had been hanging… next to his own red scythe-and-hammer yamakah. The Communist grins as Duc Chu Song and Thai Trang giggle.] [The Renegade cuts the giggles short with a single searing gaze.] Renegade: What do we possibly have to celebrate? Communist, you lost the THC. I lost my Television title. And to top it all off, all four of us lost the Regal Rumble. So I’ll ask again… what in the hell do we have to celebrate? [The Communist sighs, placing one massive arm across The Renegade’s shoulders.] Communist: Saki, what else? We don’t have much of the Asian juice in Russia, comrade. Our flight doesn’t head back to America until tomorrow… so we should take advantage of Tokyo and her many benefits tonight! Besides… Thai, show him the memo. [Reaching into his pants, Thai Trang produces a crumpled sheet of paper.] Thai Trang: From the boss… Duc Chu Song: It’s the card for Wednesday Night Underground! Thai Trang: I was gonna tell him! ME! Duc Chu Song: Then TELL him already! Thai Trang: You already did! Duc Chu Song: Oh… I guess I did. Oops! [The Renegade snatches the piece of paper from Thai as the two continue to bicker. Spreading it out, his eyes furiously scan the information.] Lacy: (off screen) What does it say? Renegade: It looks like I’ll be facing The Real Deal on Wednesday, one-on-one. Communist: Exactly, comrade. And do you know what this means? [Rollins looks up at the big Russian as Thai Trang and Duc Chu Song quiet down. Now with their full attention, The Communist’s voice becomes that of the dictator, his entire demeanor radiating the ethereal presence of the late Joseph Stalin.] Communist: It is our opportunity to quiet our oppressor’s voice, comrades. Our swift judgment will be known to all, come Underground. The self-proclaimed “Real Deal” may think he has the skill to take on our brother Rich Rollins, but he will find that the power of the Judao Communist Revolution is unstoppable, our resolve unshakable. He will find that our Jewish brethren… Renegade: I’m not Jewish. [The Communist stops mid-sentence, looking down at The Renegade with incredulity. Rollins looks right back, a ridiculous scene considering the not-so-rosy tint of his dark blue skin. Despite himself, The Communist can’t keep from laughing.] Communist: Details, details… Judaism is an ideal, as is communism. And in their holy marriage, nothing shall stand in their way! Duc Chu Song: Don’t you mean our way? Thai Trang: Shut up, let him talk! Duc Chu Song: Don’t tell me to shut up! Thai Trang: Well… I just did! Duc Chu Song: (pause) Touche. [This was far too much for Rollins to handle, especially right now.] [Sticking the wadded-up WNU card in his pocket, The Renegade silences both Local NVA Company members and stops The Communist before another round of rousing propaganda can spill out of his mouth.] Renegade: Look guys… I’m more than capable to taking care of business on Wednesday. But if you don’t mind, I just came within arm’s reach of winning the biggest match of my career, only be toppled over and stripped of my title. I have a lot on my mind, and none of this is helping. Let me take a shower… Duc Chu Song: You need it. Thai Trang: Yeah Renegade, you’re looking a little… BLUE~! [The Local NVA Company giggles.] [The Renegade sneers, and immediately the giggling stops.] Renegade: As I was saying, let me get cleaned up while you guys get ready, and then we’ll hit Tokyo like the multi-cultured band of misfits we are. Agreed? Communist: Agreed. [Thrusting out his hand, The Communist nods at Rollins. Returning the nod, The Renegade thrusts his own hand forward, and the two men grasp one another’s forearms, shaking firmly. Ten seconds later, the only sound in the small locker room is that of the air conditioning thrumming somewhere in the background, leaving Rich Rollins alone once again with the camera and the blonde beauty behind it.] [A wry mischievous twinkling enters Rollins’ eyes as he turns back to the camera.] Renegade: Care to join me? Lacy: (off camera) Was that a proposition? [Rollins chuckles, and for once it‘s a joyful sound.] Renegade: Baby, I’ll show you “the real deal” long before Wednesday ever comes around… […static…static…] […static…] [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
