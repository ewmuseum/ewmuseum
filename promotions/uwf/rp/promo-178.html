UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #178

Errrr...yeah

20 Jan 2004

Moraless — SAS rp 1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It has been a while since we had seen any action from a UWF ring, I mean shit you know what the place is like at the best of times yet 2004 seems to be bringing in a new era, stars are returning, the wrestlers are working and the fans are slowly filling the arenas once more. Two such superstars that are making their return, Moraless and Ben Jericho, the two have never met in the ring before but all that is about to change, as at Supremacy the two are scheduled to go head to head. No-one had seen Moraless in quite a while, the last time he was seen was telling some board member about where the hell he had disappeared to leaving UWF for the best part of eight months, he got half way through a story telling of his attempt to take his own life, to end the suffering that plagued him in one fell swoop. Sadly as these things tend to happen in the world, the exec member fell asleep during the story, so did the cameraman and the sound guy. Well wouldn’t you if you heard some man rambling on for hours about how he tried to kill himself. Christmas had finished, a time for family and happiness so maybe Moraless has had time to reflect on things, maybe he will be all smiles as he speaks, maybe even appear with a little puppy by his side…nah I didn’t think so either. We find Moraless, skulking around the streets, kicking his heels on the dirty gravel. It’s night time again if they weren’t completely fictitious you would almost believe that he was a vampire. You know only appearing at night and hating garlic…well everyone hates garlic don’t they. He holds a sullen look on his face; head hung low just staring absently at the ground ahead of him taking slow plodding steps as though he has no particular place to go. Moraless: So they don’t book me for the first card but then decide I’m good enough to be a match against Jericho what sort of logic is that? Moraless lets out a long lingering sigh; gently pressing against his stomach with his finger wincing slightly as he does so. Lifting his shirt he reveals a large scar, a scar which resulted from the knife wound that he inflicted on himself, ramming the cold steel through his stomach his own flesh and sinew. Moraless: I’m no-where near ready yet, I haven’t been able to train anywhere near to the extent I usually do, my skin is still tender to the touch so god knows what is going to happen if Ben decides to stomp away at it like a bitch in heat…wow that was a bad analogy. Continuing his walk, the skies open and it begins to rain as it almost always does whenever Moraless is out walking, maybe he should walk in the desert could really sort out a few problems there, the rain cascading down Moraless’ face as he lets out a discontent sigh. Moraless: Don’t get me wrong I look at the position of the match, I look at who I’m facing and I know that this could open big doors for me. Ben Jericho is a former world champion here; he’s been around since GIWF. I spent most of my time watching him during my last time here, just someday hoping to be half the man he is. The rain grows heavier now not showing any indication of lightening up. Moraless’ black leather jacket causing the rain to slide off onto his blue jeans which were slowly taking in water and become heavier as well as much more uncomfortable. Moraless: Maybe I should do a sicky, not show to the bout. I don’t know why I’m here at all you know. It’s clear I’m not ready. The doctors said I would be by now if I took it easy but I’m not. Then you have to add on the extra training that I will have to do just to get back to where I was before. Hell what was I then? A nobody, someone who faced Chaos more times than I would care to remember, my only two real bouts against Oni and Mercury I choked. I lost my chance at the THC title and I lost my chance of taking out the then world champion. Moraless comes to a complete stop the rain, still beating down rapidly. To the side of his body he forms a fist, clenching so hard that his knuckles begin to turn a pasty white, clearly feeling the frustration and the anger towards himself coursing through his body. Moraless: But it’s never been in me to quit, it’s not my way. I don’t know if I’m going to beat Jericho. I’m fairly confident that I’m not going to but I’m going to give it a shot. He can split my stomach open and I will keep running at him. He can pin me but eventually I will be back to have a shot at him again. They can feed me to jobbers and arm them with weapons to attack me, mock me and humiliate me in front of the fans. Moraless raises his head and looks possibly a bit too over melodramatically to the sky. Moraless: But I will not quit, because I’m a fighter till the end and I will go down with a bang.
