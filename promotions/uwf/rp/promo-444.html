UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #444

Meh. Heart so isn't in this anymore.

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A man stands alone. It is nightfall; the moon shines down overhead providing a mild amount of light for the land below it. Atop a hill the man stands simply gazing forwards, his eyes focusing on something that doesn’t seem to be there. Maybe it’s some guy on an acid trip, watching the world below him dance under the lunar light. Maybe it is a man contemplating his life, looking down at the town below him trying to work out, where exactly he went wrong. Then again, maybe it’s just a man, someone out on a night stroll, taking a moment to observe the view, maybe thinking of memories past before journeying home to bed, cuddling up to his wife and telling her that he loves her. I could go on, what I’m trying to get at here is that it’s just a man. We are all the same. Have you ever been in the situation, where someone can just talk and talk and talk; yet you don’t care what they are saying? No that’s not right, it’s not so much that you don’t care what they are saying, but more the fact that you can’t hear them. Everyone, the world in general hears the cries of others each day, yet they don’t hear them. Heh, I’m not making much sense am I. We as a people, as a society, don’t listen to others, we can sit and nod at the people, agree with them, even offer them comfort but we don’t hear them. All that we hear is the bear minimal. It’s not that we don’t want to help them, mankind is well known for its compassion…ok so it’s not so well known but it is a feature of ours. If we could, we would give our heart to those that we care about; if it were possible we would take away their pain. The thing that stands in our way however is ourselves. Our pain. The pain the courses through our bodies every single day, making us question our very being, our very purpose. Every single one of us has it, you can try and hide it but deep down no one is truly happy. We live our lives hidden away from the truth; we hide constantly in everyday life. We hide our bodies in cotton and wool; various fabrics to hide our shame, walls surround us everywhere we go, just somewhere else to escape the reality of the world. At the end of the day it doesn’t matter exactly how successful you are, you could have more money and wealth than you could know what to do with it, that doesn’t make you happy the pain will always be there. That is why you don’t hear people; as if you really could hear them; hear what was happening within their souls, the torment would keep you awake at night. The camera pans up, the mans face is covered by shadow as he stands arms crossed he stares forwards, always looking forwards. Its as though he is afraid to look back, he knows that the past is behind him, he knows that things cannot be changed. In the end things will come and go, the world we keep spinning and mankind will keep hiding. The man steps forwards, two golden plates, reflect the moonlight causing it to cascade all around, it now becomes blatantly obvious to anyone and everyone that this man is John Edwards. I don’t care what people think; I don’t care what Ryder thinks. The simple fact of the matter is, that when all is said and done, I’ll be leaving the victor.
