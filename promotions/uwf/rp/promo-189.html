RP 2

23 Jan 2004

Joshua Dunn — Hotel Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Up bringing. It defines a lot about a person, how they interact with other people, how they cope with loss, and how much winning means to them. But what happens when those defining moments are taken away from you, what then? You lose all that makes you what you are, don’t you? Can you be exactly like you are without the same up bringing? Would Al Crud be an alcoholic? Would Danny Jaye be a legend? Would Jamie Shock be such a bum? This is the paradox of Dunn. Striped of his past, left to conform to the needs of a dead team. Dunn is still Dunn right? If he were to snap out of his communist falsity that he believes top be him, would he still be the Dunn we all know now? We can’t tell, because we have nothing to compare it with. Although if he knew himself how he was before, would he want to go back? Would he gladly dismiss something that brought him so much? We cannot say, however some things just remain the same] [The Scene Opens in a hotel room somewhere near the Ericcson stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina, the place that has been pegged for the UWF Superstars to rest in while they are here for SAS, each day spent either with associating with fans or giving press conferences, if the Wrestlers so wish. We how ever are here, through the lens of a camera, to visit the Only Scottish Communist in the UWF Joshua Dunn. And as we can see the room is covered with a mahogany and floral wallpaper, no expense spared for the wrestlers of the UWF. Joshua Dunn is standing in one area of the hotel room, next to a large wardrobe, his bag of clothing and that sits next to it still full, to Dunn this might as well be a motel room for all the difference it would make. He’s wearing, of what we can see, his leather jacket and black jeans. He’s looking out of a window, hands held up around his midsection holding something. What we can’t rightly tell. A hand comes up to his mouth, and we can see a cup filled with what is presumable tea, he replaces the hand to where it was originally and we can hear a “clink” when the cup returns to it’s saucer. After he swallows he inhales deeply letting the air rush out in a contented sigh, and turns around while taking another sip. We can now see he’s wearing a red T-Shirt and also his gold chain around his neck. When he looks up from drinking the tea he notices the camera and lets the cup and saucer he’s holding drop to his midsection along with his arms, which are holding the cup and saucer] JD: Hmm, seems like no one gets some private time around here. Are you here for a few words? [The camera motions up and down signifying a yes, and Dunn lets out another sigh, this one of slight annoyance as he turns around and places the saucer on the writing table behind him and downs the rest of the tea and sets the cup on the saucer which lets out another clink that would have been quiet if it weren’t for the deafening silence in the rest of the room. Dunn pulls out the chair that accompanies the writing desk and angles it so he will face the camera when he sits down] JD: Well, I am here in Charlotte. And what a lovely place it is, I haven’t had a real long time to look around but it seems an all right place. But we all know I’m not here for the town, oh no, we’re here to see one thing and one thing only, the clash between The Prime Alliance and The Players Club. And this is what has made SAS a sell out event already. For this I am very proud, because this main event is giving the fans of the UWF what they rightly deserve. [He smiles briefly before returning to his previous state] JD: Although not everything is smelling of roses, of course everything can’t be, or what a bland world that would be. There is still things that bother me still, like Malcolm Kennedy, who has decided that he is better than me, and hasn’t even faced me once. Sure you have the next chance for the world title, but so does Ruff Ryder. And remember who you went through to get that shot, Jamie Shock, who can’t boast a win over me, likewise with you Malcolm. When you beat me, I’ll change my mind. Until then I still class you as scum, along with Ruff Ryder and your master Jamie Shock. Yes you could call that whining if you like, you manage to call everything else whining. I fail to see how trends in UWF board decisions make me complain, but somehow you find a way. Maybe if you found time to look at promotions instead of just getting the transcripts read to you by some half wit you pay to do so while you swan about in your office of that casino. I’m sure it could manage itself without your constant meddling. But it’s your business and I won’t dictate your policies to you. You might be wise to do the same, as dictating how well your going to do in a match before you even step into the ring with your opponent is equally as foolish, you can study strategies and technique as much as you like but it’s nothing to what experience can teach you. And experience you lack, especially when it comes to me. So you’ve been on the receiving end of the St. Andrews Crossface, that’s one move in a thousand I could pull on you. One move doesn’t decide a match, as you should well know, to Mr. Shock’s utter disappointment you proved that true. Just like your precious Jackpot might not clinch the match. [Dunn shrugs, pouting his bottom lip out for a moment before returning to normal] JD: Of course there’s no saying that it might not. There is a certain random element in the case of wrestling, as you suggested in the most lenient of terms. There is never a guarantee to how a match will go; you know what they say about eggs and baskets. You might do well by heeding your own advice. Prepare for the unexpected, because you might just get it [Dunn gets up and moves out of camera view, the camera turns to the cup and saucer and stays there for a few moments and fades out on that image]
