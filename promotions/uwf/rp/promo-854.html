UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #854

getting ready

1 Jan 2005

Dreammaker — mmmmmm

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A video tape is hand delivered by a man in the brown uniform. A UWF staff member looks pretty confused when he has to sign for a package. After opening it up he sees that it reads. “ Video production” by Dreammakin home videos. ) ‘Great now we have a wrestler who thinks he is somebody and look everyone watch my video. The guard keeps on mumbling under his breath as he goes into production room and pops in the tape. The camera then focuses on the video monitor and it fades to black. Then pops up in bold letters: From a place very far, far away Comes the preparation for his largest matches to date No cheesy male strippers to entertain you No props inserted into you rectal cavities No fans not receiving autographs If you blink you will miss the new era of wrestling (then the screen fades to a field covered with wild flowers of some sort. And the camera follows down what seems like and endless amount of wildflowers. Then out of nowhere a car is seen cruising threw the fields of flowers. The red car slams on its breaks and power slides right in front of the camera crew. We notice that it is a ford focus and out steps Dreammaker. ) (with a large smile he looks at the camera and flexes. Then he points out behind him and laughs. ) DM: America! This is what I give to you. Be proud. The fans have spoken and I will make everyone’s dreams come true. Pay attention cuz I don’t have much time. (he stops to look around) ok. Here we go.. the contest of champions is upon us. My first real test in this federation. They have booked me with that hated Hotmask and another guy. Oh ya, Mr. Persia, don’t worry im not forgetting about you. See I have this all figured out. This match was booked because this fed is afraid that I am too good for singles matches. I haven’t lost in any of my singles matches yet. As a matter of fact I haven’t lost in any singles title matches yet either. ( then the camera man whispers “ That is your one and only match) SHUT UP…. These people don’t care about details…. they want nothing but numbers and statistics. And I haven’t lost a singles match yet. There needs to be two others in the ring to contain me. So you pay attention and realize that I am the wild card here.. I am the newbie. The one no-one cares about. That is my dream. To prove to you all that I am worthy of a title shot for all of the American public. Because I have seen the future. The future is for those who know they can dream. Who have a dream. And the nightmare that some will feel.(pauses) Will be felt… prepare yourselves for a time of change. UWF beware. I am not some dorky chaos member gonna attack you everytime you try to take a shit. Lets just say that the time for change is now.. (pauses to look to side) oh shit(whipered) well that is all for today. Enjoy the tulips. I know you the fans wanted them. (shouting is heard off camera) (and getting louder) (we see a security guard looking at the camera crew) Guard: this is a state park please remove this motor vehicle from the field. DM: do you like the color? Guard: what . hey man I don’t want any fear factory shit going on out here so please leave. DM: what do you drive? (asked really nicely) Guard : who cares.. (pauses) an old truck. No blair witch crap now leave. DM: well I guess so. Here you go. (throws the keys to the guard) I dotn need it any more. And you could sure use it. Now you see you are interrupting my promo for the UWF with you car in my way so move it now. Guard: (looks puzzled and then smiles ) sir no problem. DM: (smiles at the camera and it fades to black)
