UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #430

LOSERS...CORPORAL STYLE

29 May 2004

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(What a great way to get a show running! Wouldn't you say. I mean after all, you were able to see a new era in wrestling begin AND a new way to say good morning Grand ma, how is the weather down there in PUNISHMENT-VILLE. By the looks of the ending of the last house show, I can easily say that the Corporal has officially entered the UWF.) Are there any questions? I am sure there are. After all, we seen a few members of the UWF step up to take talent to the next level. Men like Kenny Mercury, who sees what true talent is in wrestling as well as great taste in music. Seymour Almasy,a man who prides himself in perfection as well as holding every title but one in the UWF. And last but not least Corporal Punishment. The greatest demolisher on the entire planet, and the man who has single handedly placed fear into more hearts than Freddy Kruger, Jason, Michael Myers, and The Plagues grandmother dressed in panties while standing on the corner of 1st and 3rd Street combined. This threesome is breaking all the rules in order to prove a simple point that when it comes to domination in the square circle, only complete unadulterated physical force mixed with sadistic-ness will prevail. And with these three men, Stable Wars is a shoe in for this group. But alas, there is one man missing in this foursome. And that man will remain anonymous until the time is right to shake this federation to it's knees. However, before we can move on, there are a few names that must be mentioned. After all, you just can't have a Corporal Punishment appearance without a little creative expression can you? I did not think so. So with that said, let us begin with a little mouse who is about to be verbally assaulted solely because he is so far out of his league that the little white school bus (with the seat belts) won't even come this far to let this mentally handicapped jobber get a ride back to romper room. Truly thee is better talent than this pathetic person who calls himself Ruff Rider? Tell me if this cowardly loin of a loser isn't waiting in the middle of the street for a truck to run right over him. The world must be laughing their arses off when they saw this guy speak after this weekends card was over. PRIVATE JET... OKLAHOMA... CHEWING ON NUTS!!! GIVE ME A BREAK! This is a family show people! Having born losers flying home with nuts in their mouth is NOT family entertainment! Especially when after the guy has nuts in his mouth, a female CHILD is seen walking up to him solely to remind him to SHOT his PROMO! Does that not give a new term for PRE-EJACULATION! You would think this so called talent could at least have shot his promo in the privacy of his own bed. But instead, he has to grab a mere child to get his nuts off. I guess that is how they do things in the land of Steers and Queers...Oklahoma! What was worse than him having nuts in his mouth, was the fact that he actually thinks he is accomplishing something. He thinks that he actually has the talent to face a mediocre opponent. Tell me that he is not a funny freak. To think that this low card no talent is crying that he wants to face real talent, is like Mother Theresa wanting all the guys to come over her house for a nut sack party. Hello! Do you get the picture?! IT IS NOT GOING TO HAPPEN! He can not even see past his useless career to notice that the single most greatest thing to ever happen in the UWF has just taken place. The UWF has finally gotten a team worthy of federation domination. But still Ruff Rider thinks he has a chance to become more than what his mother insisted he become when he grew up. And that was a street peddler of plastic spatulas. Enough with this idiot. Let us move on. Then there is Loverboy. The guy has a pole dancer saying, 'Hey Boy!' Tell me if that isn't a show of absolute talent! Enough said about this last place jobber. It hurts to think that there will be people who pay to see either of these two nimrods of rectums. But that is what the sport of wrestling is about. You have to have wrestlers to brutally beat up. And these two munchkins are the perfect talent for jobbing in wrestling. At least their family wont go hungry. But relax. Sit down and take a load off. Because it is time for the greatest ENFORCER of wrestling to enter the building and grace you with his presence. So have a beer, put your children away ...Ruff Rider may be watching, and get ready to enter the land of LOCKDOWN because... The Corporal has entered the building!) ***The scene opens up inside of the Corporal's new home. Boxes are piled everywhere as the camera pans each room until it finds the Corporal seated in the kitchen. He is dressed in a pair of camouflaged thermal top and bottoms, with a pair of fluffy slippers in the shape of army boots. Being his typical sleep wear, the Corporal is sipping a cuup of three day old coffee while looking at several blueprints.*** Corporal: By my calculations, I figure it will be five weeks before this entire neighborhood has been abandoned by these pathetic civilians. Looking at the fact that I have yet to receive a homemade apple pie from the little old lady who got taged with that canister the other day, I figure she wont be sending me anything anytime soon. And from the looks outside... ***The Corporal looks out the window in the kitchen and the camera views several signs planted in his front yards telling him to get out of the neighborhood.*** Corporal: My new found neighbors are just a little saddened at my presence. But hey, what else is new. Wait till they see my new project. These blueprints look perfect, and once I begin the reconstruction the Punishment Compound on this site, they are going to really love being around me. But like I said, five weeks and this land will be all mine. ***The Corporal takes a sip of his coffee and then ponders for a moment.*** Corporal: I just love a happy ending, don't you? ***The Corporal chuckles for a moment, and then lays the blueprints of his compound on the table. He stands up and walks into the living room and grabs the morning paper. Taking the paper to his favorite lounge chair, the Corporal sits and beings to look through the morning news.*** Corporal: Well let us see what is happening around the world. There is murder in Iraq, murder in L.A., murder in Florida, murder in Texas, murder in New York, murder in China, Russia, Asia, and even Singapore. The only place there isn't any murder is France... and that is because the only thing the French are good at is licking each others nut sacks. What a bunch of fags! Corporal: Speaking of NUT SACKS, who would of thought that a wrestler in the UWF would actually advertise that they were sucking on nuts? What ever happened to keeping these kind of things in the closet? Ever since the GIWF brought out the Bum Boozler, this sport has went straight to the UK. All of this liberal groin hugging makes me sick! Corporal: Thank God there are only a few Canadians in this federation. I do not think I could take that crap without beating up a few queers and grand mothers... ***The Corporal stops speaking for a moment, and then his eyes light up with excitement.*** Corporal: WAIT! I ALREADY beat down someone's grandmother... I guess then I need to beat up a queer then. Corporal: OK I WILL! Starting with Plague! The man should not be called a disease, he should be called a dental disorder. ***The Corporal beings to shake his head as he reads a few lines in the obituaries.*** Corporal: Why do they give me wrestlers to face that are like the PLAQUE? How many times have I beat this poor soul down? The guy has got to be urinating in his corn flakes at the thought of entering the squared circle with me again. But hat is how it works in these federations. Corporal: You just can not find good opponents now a days. If it was not the Plaque, then I would be beating the BE-JESUS out of a wrestler named after a vibrator without batteries, RUFF RIDER. Or maybe that is the name of a second generation used condom. Either way, he does not deserve the right to be called a wrestler in a federation that I am present in. It is down right un-American to let some like these jokes to walk around parading to be talent. So, I figure I will go out there this week and beat down the Plaque in way that hopefully HIS mother or father will come to ring side and BEG for me to drop kick them instead of their physically retarded boy. I might just take them up on the offer and spare their boy from being utterly destroyed at my hands. Corporal: And then what is left... LOVERBOY? Two words...HELL NO! This guy should be sweeping up after the show instead of participating in this sport. I swear to GOD that if this man ever gets in the ring with me, I will not only kill him but then I will kill myself from the sheer boredom he displays! The guy needs to learn that there are several things wrong with him. FIRST, he is from Oklahoma. Can anyone tell me what if anything has ever came out of Oklahoma that was intimidating? NOTHING! Secondly, he is little boy who already has a problem hanging out in strip clubs while talking to skanks sliding down poles. The only pole a girl slides down when she is talking to me is my little private downstairs. But not this retard of wrestling. I bet he fancies himself more than he does the skank anyway. ***The Corporal stands up shaking his head. He balls the newspaper up and walks outside. Once outside, the Corporal looks around until he sees an elderly couple relaxing on their porch. He strolls over to them, smiles, and then slings the newspaper ball in the elderly mans face. The elderly man falls out of his chair and the elderly woman yells out his name, 'HENRY!' The Corporal looks at the camera and put his sunglasses on.*** Corporal: Like I said...FIVE WEEKS and their gone... CORPORAL STYLE!
