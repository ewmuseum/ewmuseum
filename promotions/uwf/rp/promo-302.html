UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #302

Rev it Up

5 Apr 2004

Iron Lung — Dream, Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rev it Up with Iron Lung and 'Dashing' Dane Davis Previously... 'Dashing' Dane Davis (Trying to console I.L.) Well, if you ask me, this match would be a great way to start your path to being the champion once more. If you win, you'll show the Plague that you are worthy enough to face him once more. And a tie isn't exactly a loss, but it isn't exactly a win either. Overall, you still have a decent record in my opinion.. Iron Lung I guess you are right, I mean I still have the fans on my side, win or lose. The best that I can do is what I always do, just go in there and fight this guy, try my best to come up on top. And most of the time, I do come out on top don't I? 'Dashing' Dane Davis Ya' damn right... Iron Lung (Putting his hand on Dane's shoulder) You know something? You aren't so bad Dane. I tell you what, why don't you come with me to the gym, and I can show you some of my routine? 'Dashing' Dane Davis (Smiling wide) Really, that would be totally sweet! Iron Lung All right then, that's the spirit. Let's hit the road! And now, we bring you ' Rev it Up '.. Foreword The following contains random hilarity, directed towards the expense of the Ruff Ryder rap organization. This is intended souly on the purposes of comedy, and should not be taken to offense by anyone in the reading audience. If you have no taste for pun in creative pieces, perhaps you should take your leave now. Otherwise, enjoy the following work. We slowly fade out from the darkness of the television screen, and in front of us when the scene offically opens, stands a blinking flourescent tube sign that states 'Golds Gym'. While panning around the building, we realize that this structure has been here for a while, as we can tell by the faded look of the bricks, along with the windows slightly hazing over when you looked into them. But, none of the less, we were here for a reason. That reason wasn't to look at the sights, but rather to train, and train hard, for an upcoming match. This was the kind of match that the young superstar Iron Lung, already an ex-THC Champion in the Universal Wrestling Federation, was looking for. This time, it was a matchup against Ruff Ryder for a shot at the Intercontinental Championship. He had one loss against his record as a singles wrestler, his only method of focus now was to think of all his opponents as the man who tossed him that loss, the Plague. He quivered every time he head that name, and swore that in the near future, he would get his chance to draw even, and even go beyond the call of duty and take back the THC title which was stripped from him all too quickly. Everything was quiet for a moment now, as you could hear the wind slowly beginning to pick up, touching the branches of the trees and making them dance in tranceful strides. It was all too peaceful, but that would soon change as the squeeling of tires is heard near the parking lot gravel pits. We sprint with camera in hand to see what was the matter, and as we approach the lot, we see that it is so early in the morning, the one car that has just pulled in, is in fact the only car there. It was either too early, or the gym was just so run down that everyone had forgotten about it, but whatever the case, we were about to find out who.. or what was in the vehicle. We got even closer to the car, and even closer to its occupants. But just as we were about to get right up to its finish, the doors swung open, almost hitting us down to the pavement. When the doors swung open, two men in du rags and wife beaters stepped out, the car shimmering from the bass of the radio, playing this specific song.. Stop... drop... shut 'em down open up shop... ooo... no... that's a Ruff Ryda's role! The two large men nod their heads for a little bit while the song plays on, and then they give each other a slick version of a handshake in which at the end of it, they end up patting each others backs. Suddenly, the first larger man sits inside of the drivers seat, shutting off the car and then stepping back out of it, shutting the door and pushing the button to enable the alarm with a loud beep. The two men nod at each other again and stand there for a moment, and then begin to talk between each other in an odd tone. Large man #1 What up Jamal Large man #2 Yo, Devon Jamal Yo, what we doin' up in herrr, we ain't need 'dis shyt! We all diesel! Devon I know 'dat dogg, but we gotz ta meet J-Dillay herr, 'dats what he said. We'z gonna get us some hot bootleg CD's, 'dem new cuts from fiddy cent an' Joe, and of course 'da bootleg of Walking Tall, 'dat shyt is off 'da chain! Jamal I know bro, 'dat shyt looks hot. But dayam, 'dey takin' foreva to get up in this joint! What are 'dey doin'? I'm 'fraid 'dat dere's 'bout to be some 5-0 up in 'dis shyt any minute! Devon I know son, but lucky fa' me, they ain't gonna find shyt on me! Jamal Dayum, you finished yours already? I had to leave minez in 'da whip! The two nod at each other once more, and as soon as they are finished, we hear the faint honking of a horn and buzzing of a motor. Within moments, two four wheeled ATV's pull up to Jamal and Devon, and they stop on a time, almost seeming like they are going to tip on the front end. But the two riders somehow ease it back down to the ground, winding up the engines a few more times before leaving them to idle. Rider #1 Whatup gangsta's, ma' bad about bein' so late, had a little trouble on da streets. Jamal Dayum yo Jerrod, we was waitin' here fo' -- Jerrod I said ma' bad Jamal, don't be tryin' to trip like 'dat. I tried ma' best to get here on time. Devon Yo Jerrod, who 'dis wit' you, look like a playa' hata' to me. Jerrod Ah yeh, I almost forgot to tell 'ya, 'dis here is Bruce... Jamal Bruce? What in da' hell are you doin' bringin' a whitey up in here? Jerrod Jus' hear me out bro, 'cuz 'dis here is Bruce Li! Bruce (In a barely audible accented tone) Peased to meet you. Devon Why you even here Bruce, we ain't need no kung fu protection! Bruce (Keeping the tone) I don't weely know kung fu. Devon 'Den what good are you? You got some egg rolls for us? Jerrod Just chill, aight Devon. He's just gonna chill wid' us for the day. Devon Aight yo. But jus' so you know, I'm gonna trip if you start talkin' that jibberish ta' me. Bruce I have no clue what you awe tawking about Devon Don't play wid' me foo', you know what I'm goin' on about, 'dat freaky language ya'll speak. Bruce Fweeky language? You don't undastand da words dat awe comin' out of my mouf? Devon I said don't play wid' me, 'dis is your last warnin' before I bust a cap! Jerrod Jus' chill homie, he don't mean no harm. Devon He's buggin' yo, I ain't need nobody playin' round wid' me like 'dat. Bruce You can pway with yoursewf. Suddenly, Devon pulls out a Swiss army knife and holds it in front of Bruce's neck on the ATV, breathing hard as Jerrod and Jamal both try to stop the confrontation. Jerrod pulls Devon away by his waist, while Jamal takes the knife away, clipping it back into the safety. All the while, Devon reaches for his pocket once more, and pulls out a gun! Devon slowly cocks the trigger, getting ready to fire, as Bruce trembles in fear. Both Jerrod and Jamal are already occupied with the knife, and they had no intentions of having their hands blown off by this weapon. It was up to Devon, and up to if he had the courage to pull the trigger. Bruce hoped not, and he had a perfect escape plan, all he needed was enough time to shift the gear in his ATV, and he would be off. But what could distract Devon for that amount of time? Sweat poured down Bruce's forehead as he looked down the barrel of the gun, and then suddenly, a buzzing noise overtook the air. The whole crew looks around without a clue, until the scene goes black and we hear a faint groaning, followed by the playing of the same song from before on the radio. The groaning continues until the scene opens wide, and we can see a figure tossing his covers aside, yawning widely and sitting up on the edge of his bed. With his hair in disarray, we can barely recognize him. As he looks at his clock, he groans again and furiously looks near his bedside with his hands for clothing. Finally, he touches upon a black shirt, and lifting it up to his nose, he checks for any odor. Not noticing any, he throws it over his head, and all the while his phone begins to ring. He is very disoriented as he fights to put his shirt on and gather the phone before the ringing stops, and luckily he would catch the phone on its last tone, pushing the 'talk' button and holding the receiver up to his ear, but far enough away so he wouldn't become deaf if the other talker was to scream. Receiver Hello? Caller Lung, it's Dane.. Iron Lung What? How did you get this number? 'Dashing' Dane Davis You gave it to me after your match, remember? Iron Lung Oh, ok. What do you want? 'Dashing' Dane Davis Forgetful aren't we? We're supposed to go to the gym today! Iron Lung (Looking at his clock) Shit, you're right. You know what? Just meet me at the Golds Gym in about an hour, I have to get ready! 'Dashing' Dane Davis All right, I'll meet you there. Ta! Shaking his head lightly, Iron Lung gets out of bed and mutters 'damn, what a crazy ass dream' before yawning once more. He is slow to get around the room, as he is still trying to shake away the sandman, but none of the less, he arrives at his destination. We wouldn't watch him get his daily routine through with, we would respect his privacy. So, with that being said, the camera fades away on this part, only to pick up briefly when the gym was in sights. End of ' Rev it Up ', but be sure to catch Iron Lung in ' Pump it Up ' for the exciting conclusion!
