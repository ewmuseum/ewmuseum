UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #766

taking time

2 Nov 2004

Dreammaker — in the weight room at the club

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to a very quiet weight room. There are many people working on many different machines. This would seem like an ordinary weight room except we notice that the women are walking in and out of the tanning room with nothing on. Making us beleive that we are in Dreammaker's club) (Jeff Johnson, the UWF's very own reporter and some how a new member of The Dreammaker's passe, is seen in the corner working on a bench press machine. After sweating out a couple more reps he sits up and towels himself off. When he lifts his head and notices the camera crew is focused on him. ) Jeff: Hey now, just cuz I'm working out doesn't mean you can interview me. Dreammaker is over in that crowd of people. Leave me the F alone! (The camera starts moving towards the crowd as we see a shirt less wrestler signing autographs and peoples' shirts) Dreammaker: Hold on everyone! Back off! I willl sign everyones shirt or pic of me if you all just hold on. Remmeber to follow your dreams or someone will come alone and crush them. (As he drops the pen he is holding and flexes for the crowd. The crowd cheers and some pictures are taken.) DREAMMAKER: Now everyone make sure you come watch my three way dance for the TV Title! I will make sure you all get to see this Hot Mask fellow fall asleep right in that ring. And of course Mr. Justice, who fought very well the other night. Maybe I should shake you hand and congraduate you on a very good match. Seems there is some talent on your side. But rememebr that this is my belt and my match to win. (He then looks down and sees a 10 year old boy holding his picture and a pen. Dreammaker then grabs the pen and sighns the picture and motions for Bubbles to walk over) Bubbles: What can I do for you Mr. Dream?? Dreammaker: God, I love it when you say that! Anyways Bubbles, this boy, how did he get into the back of the club? Better yet how did he get into the club at ALL? I would like to find something out today! Bubbles: Well that would be Raid's department. You put him in charge didn't you? Dreammaker: That lazy son-of-a-bitch! Does anyone know where he is? Girl in the crowd: I think he is upstairs. He asked me to join him and 2 very naked girls. Dreamaker: Maybe making him head of security was a bad idea. At least we found something he's good at. Because we know he has limits. (Laughs) (Looking at the camera) Bubbles, please give the boy a t-shirt for being crafty and at least cover his eyes when you take him outside. (Stops and flexes in front of the camera and points at it) Now everyone get ready for the reality to happen. Tomorrow, (dramatic pause) I will show you all who have been sitting around and dreamming about being a champion. And the one (softly pounds his chest) whos been working to be in the reality of the championship. That belt belongs around my waist! Mr. Justice, good luck. You are gonna need it! And Hot Mask, I am sorry this to your sorry ass, but your first time with the Dream isn't gonna be a dream, but a nightmare, a nightmare that I wont let you ever wake up from! (Dreammaker turns around and starts signing autographs again as the camera fades to black.)
