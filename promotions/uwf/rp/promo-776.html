Polka My Eyes Out

9 Nov 2004

Hot Mask — Panthro Studios

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Within every professional wrestler's career, although it may not lie as important as other goals of making a living or to wow audiences with incredible performances, one will often agree that having something that signifies your worth in the wrestling community is a crowning achievement. A gold-plated, shiny piece of metal designed not for the intent of holding one's pants up, but as an accessory to model with pride. What does one gain emotionally when being bestowed this prize? A sense of self-gratification? A virtue of honor in knowing that the hardest aspect is not winning the belt, but defending it night after night? An even more inflated ego than before? An increased amount of ladies combing for a chance to slob the knob? 'What the fuck is this, a goddamned profile for the History Channel?' the distinguishable snotty high-pitched voice of Hot Mask ponders. 'Pardon me, cumsicle, but I'm about to ruin your pseudo-clever narration of rhetoric.' 'Self-gratification? You better believe it, you old fart. I apparently overestimated my chances against the patriotic nobody and the clamoring retard...ya see, I guessed I'd have to use at least four wrestling moves to take the gold...buuuuuuuuuuuuuut hey, it only took three! So, combined with the lack of having to break a sweat and the useful cracked password for Tittysmothered.com, there was DEFINITELY some self-gratification on the horizon! Of course, since it IS the Dongersaurus we're talkin' bout here, I needed a couple hundred...*ahem*...'hired hands,' if you catch the load I'm shootin!' A chuckle from the voice, as Mask presses on, still steaming with pride in his speech. 'A virtue of honor? Puh-LEEEEEEEEEEZE! Who in their right mind would actually look forward to having to touch these sweathogs on a weekly basis? Skin flutists who probably stiffen up just at the opportunity to see the Finest Ass in Sports Entertainment in a pair of spandex tights...yeah, I'm REAL honored. Defending against such 'huge' stars as 'Guy No One Cares About,' 'Masked Guy No One Cares About,' 'Smelly Fat Ass Who People Wish Would Be Hit With Transfer Truck,' and 'Masked Guy No One Cares About Jr.' And it's all for a recycled piece of scrap aluminum that won't even go for 25 bucks on Ebay. And yes,' Mask snorts, 'I've already tried it.' 'Virtue...PSSH,' he continues. 'You can take your virtue, make an ample-sized hole with it, and let me shove my Samwise Gamgee through!' 'Po-ta-toes!' Mask adds, as he quickly transitions to his next response, pausing just briefly to take a breath before continuing. 'And touching base with this whole 'inflated ego' bellegerance that seems pretty damn commonplace to spew from the filthy sewers of Pringles-eating wrestling fans when in mention of the Almighty Peen. Listen, I'm well-aware of the fact that yeah...it might be true that I do put the 'cock' in 'cocky.' It's no revealing Internet-based rumor that I do think quite highly of myself. But hell...take a look at me...' Our lens NOW opens up, revealing the inside of our location, which is a small recording studio switchboard room that's pretty standard issue for those somewhat acquainted with what one would look like. Unpainted plywood walls line the area save for a glass window to our right where we see a six-piece band with members dressed in matching green-and-white Swiss-style clogger outfits. There is a drummer, a bass player, a man holding an accordion, and three individuals covering the horn section. Just to the right is a plainclothed man with a pair of headphones running the board, looking preoccupied with his mixing duties. 'I'm fucking amazing!' We take a look at the one and only King Dingaling himself, Hot Mask in the flesh...and have to stifle laughter, as he is identically dressed in the same outfit as the band in the next room. Green short overalls worn over a white T-shirt, plaid golfer socks with tiny fuzz balls hanging at the top rims, and brown upturned wooden shoes. He poses glamour model style for the camera, and like a sudden inclination, realizes the viewing audience is probably making light of his ridiculous appearance. 'Hey, kiss my polka-loving ass, America. Anyone without a fine appreciation of a truly artistic culture such as this has NO room to scoff at their style of dress. While you idiots buy into the latest trends, Mask is keeping it real...like I always do.' 'Now, as I was saying...I rule and am better than everyone. However, to claim that someone is egotistical and for someone to have infinite factual knowledge are two dicks inside a different vagina,' Mask comments, citing a quite crude and irreprehensible reference. 'Yeah, to the amateur dingleberry, it might seem 'egotistical' to write your own name in on the election ballot for President...but honestly, if you're the most qualified to do something as mundane as run a country, it's not an illusion of grandeur...it's a goddamned right decision!' 'I don't claim to be the 'best' at everything I do. I'm just far better at it than everyone else,' Mask adds. 'Whether it's running, jumping, wrestling, sexing, peeing, farting, smarting, tarting and go-karting...if it's slapping, trapping, lapping, crackin, tackin, and booty-smackin! And I'm not alone in my claims, because my 8,938,476,812 and a half Maskulines will back it up! I R TEH r0x0r! It ain't an ego trip...it's a cold hard fact.' Since he has been on his feet for the majority of the speech, Mask spots a nearby rolling chair to his left, and proceeds to take a seat, adjusting his outfit with a bit of a slouch. Taking a passing glance at the band inside and giving an acknowledging nod, he poises himself to the camera, shooting a confident smile. 'No, you actually haven't reached the set of Making the Band. The only thing 'Puffy' about this is the unhideable bulge in these here trousers,' HM smirks, quickly motioning to his unit with a snap of his right thumb and middle finger. 'We're here at Panthro Studios, laying down the tracks for my new theme song, kiddies. Thankfully, the UWF decided to fork over some investment dough on the Purple-Helmeted Warrior of Love so I won't have to use something as outdated as the Lewis diddy.' 'Don't get me wrong, Maskulines, I have nothing but the utmost respect for the ol' corpse Jerry Lee for composing the number dedicated to the fuel tank of the perfect genetic material...but I need something a little more definitive. However, just leave it to the Hot One to produce something so pleasing...so fucking desirable that it'll more than likely replace the vibrator as a woman's choice for self-pleasure! And since I don't want to pull some sort of Ashlee Simpson on all my adoring fans, I'll be doing REAL vocal tracks! Ahhh,' Mask reflects, 'just imagine my soothing voice over the PA of the UWF Arena night after night. Makes me all tingly inside.' An unrequited shift in his seat causes him to pause, giving off a smug grin. 'Unfortunately for Joshua Justice, my soothing voice will be ringing in his ears not via a loudspeaker, but through the laughter that will ensue after I make him look like the pathetic no-talent hack he is AGAIN on Supremacy!' 'Apparently this moron didn't get the memo that patriotism is soooooo late 2001. Get with the program, ponce. No one cares about the 'American Way,' politics are boring and unimportant considering my eventual takeover of this pathetic planet, and Bruce Springstein sucks. It's the truth, kiddies...The Boss is about as outdated as Dreammaker's mom's skidmarked underwear, and if I ever hear some broad ever talk about the way his ass looks in that video AGAIN, I'm going to impale myself on Ron Jeremy's unit.' Mask shudders at this statement. 'Born in the U.S.A.? Congratulations, you're special.' 'Joshua Justice. A week of holding my shiny scrap is REALLLLLL impressive, let me tell ya. But you know what, Josh? Since you obviously don't have a pisser's chance downwind of putting my incredibly huge shoulders to the mat, I'm going to offer a bit of consolation for your efforts.' Hot Mask laughs, while crossing his left hand over his heart in somewhat of a mocking gesture to the patriotic wrestling hero, while continuing with his proposal. 'You're obviously a man who can appreciate his Constitutional freedoms, as well as his dire responsibility of an American citizen to make your voice be heard. I'm more than positive that you're a voting kind of guy, JJ, so let me invite you to...*ahem*...choose or lose...for our upcoming match. Well...that might be a bit misphrased,' Mask boasts, 'you're going to lose, anyway.' 'Express yourself! For I will give you an option, and buddy...it's your call. YOUR decision. You see, you can either show up for our little rendezvous and accept the complete murderalization that will ensue, resulting in utter humiliation and disgrace...or...' A smirk. '...you can stay home. Check the track record, Junior. A 10 second battle resulting in a small package. A 30 second battle involving a couple of piledrivers. Wanna push that envelope and try to last...oh, say, 31 seconds? Justice, you are simply about as challenging as making Jenna Jameson climax nine-hundred consecutive times in a single seating.' 'Yeah, that might be hard for some, but you're looking at the Sultan of the Seafood Diner here!' Laughing, Mask tugs downward on his headpiece for a bit of readjustment, and finishes things up, anxious to get back to work on his new theme. 'Either way, Captain America...bring your Patriot missiles, bring your twisted foreign policies, and feel free to utilize your second amendment rights on Supremacy. But, the fact of the matter is, Josh...' 'This,' he snickers, making quotation marks with his fingers, ''head of the oval office' will not...and I repeat WILL NOT...be impeached.'
