UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #432

Chaos Vs Order

29 May 2004

The Communist — On TV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:the scene opens with the old Soviet Flag waving proudly in the background, the gold Scythe and Hammer Displayed for the whole world: Announcer: What can YOU do to better your Country? Be a Communist! :Scene shifts to a lady being mugged in a dark alley, crying as the evil mugger, wearing the anarchy symbol on his ragged t-shirt takes her purse from her: Announcer: Inferior systems of government, like Anarchy and Democracy, lead to chaos. Would you feel safe in a world like this? :the word Safe come up, covered by a red circle/slash: Of course not! :sence shift, men in red uniforms policing the safe streets, while loving couples walk safely among them, happy and prosperous: Announcer: Your life could be like this, Safe, happy, knowing that your government cares. Communism Cares. :the words COMMUNISM CARES come up on the screen: :scene shift, the Communist standing proudly before the Soviet Flag, TV title straped firmly around his waist: Commie: I want YOU! :points at the screen: To Be A Communist! Announcer: Paid for by the Central Committee, Communist Party. ------------------------------------------------ :after the commercial shoot the poor interviewer from last time comes up to the Communist: Interviewer: Umm... Sir? Mr Communist? Commie: Ahh! Comrad! Friend in Communism :Commie warmly puts his arm over the interviewers shoulder: How may I help you? Interviewer: Well, Communist, How do you feel about winning the TV title? Commie: It is Wonderful! :laughs: Finally, the Respect I deserve! Winning this title showes I have the power and the abllity necessary to make it in the UWF. I will continue to dominate in the federation untill the day I leave. And I promise, that will be no time soon. Interviewer: Do you think you're up to the challenge of defending the title every show? That is alot of matches. Commie: I will take on as many people as it takes. Every Show, Twice every show, THREE times a day! I will eat contenders for Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner! My people never backed down from the USA and I will never back down from ANYONE who wants a shot at this belt. Interviewer: What about Pryde? Some say this is going to be the hardest match you've had yet. Commie: He will fall. He represents a chaotic mindset. Order will always overcome those who chose Chaos. The Iron Curtain represents this Order. Our Strength is in our ability to strike as one. In Union. Soviet Union Interviewer: Well, thank you Communist. it's been most enlightening. Commie: Of course :he pins a small Scythe and Hammer on the Interviewer: There you go, now you're one of us.
