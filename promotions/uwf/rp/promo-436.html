UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #436

The Great Sleeping Bear

2 Jun 2004

The Communist — Backstage WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist sits on the bench at the locker room, focused, ready, TV title sitting in his lap. He stares intently at it, the words engraved into the metal surface. The glint off the edge. He knows, he must defend his title today, no matter what the odds, no matter the cost, No matter what. This is the moment of truth, to fail, or to fly. No other options exist.: Commie: Adien, I'm not sorry for what I'm about to do to your body. I'm not sorry that you won't be walking from the ring. I'm not sorry that you might not be making it to Stable Wars. You, however, will be sorry. You'll be sorry you woke the Great Red Bear. You'll be sorry you decided that the Communist looked like an easy target. You'll be sorry you're not a Communist. You see, Adien, Anarchy isn't a system, it's the lack there of. Communism is about control, about fellowship, about knowing what will happen. I know what will happen. I will walk into that ring the TV Champion, and I will leave the TV Champion. It's that simple. You will walk in a representative of Chaos, and you will leave on a streacher. I do not kid Adien. I don't make threats. I only tell you, pieces of you will remain in that ring. Large pieces. :The communist takes a deep drink of his water, spills the rest on his face, he afixes the TV Title around his waist, clasping each button in turn, adjusting, and finaly leaving it be when he's comfortable: Commie: This title is not going anywhere. You've fought quite a few people Adien. Have you ever fought a bear? The only way to win is to lay like you're dead and pray he doesn't maul you. I'm afraid, the same tatci won't work for you. :the Theme from Hunt for Red October plays in the background: Commie: It's time. Prepare to be mauled Adien. I hope you didn't have any plans for after the match. :the Communist stands, proud and tall, and walks out to the ring, prepared for Victory:
