UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #339

The Resurrection Part 2

15 Apr 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's beach house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Time and time again it has been proven that people don't just rise to the top when they want. They have to go through hell to get where they are, and once they are there, they don't want to go back down. Well, contrary to what Mike Motgomery says, Digital fought long and hard to where he is was before he left. Digital Mortality is a Hall of Famer, something that not many people have achieved, and he did not get there by being overconfident or cocky. He got there fighting, he was never a favored by the management, so he had to make his own way through the ranks by force. Everytime he was getting somewhere, everytime he was getting a push, he was thrown into the ring against Luca the Imp, Deke Aurora, and Corporal Punishment. He stood his ground though. He kept going and going when everyone thought he could go no further. He made it though. He made his dreams come true, and now it might seem like cockyness, but his confidence never left him.] [It is through Digital and many people of that time that the UWF became what it was, the best damn federation in Primetime Central, hell, the whole world! It was filled with fresh new talent that was certainly going places, and it was filled with the legends of the era. Even people like Killer Dude, who even though they were criticised as being the jobbers of the era, gave their all day in and day out to try and make it to the top. That was when the top mattered. The UWF is still in decay, the pieces from that era are falling, and that is when Mike Montgomery saw his chance to make himself better than what he actually is. He is now Commissioner of the UWF, but it's funny, because he never really did anything to deserve it.] [Mike Montgomery was never really anything. He had the Asylum, which was a bunch of idiots being manipulated by him. Countless times he and his Asylum tried to get in the way of the Iron Curtain, but he never did, excuse me, he never could. The Curtain was not going to be pushed by lowlifes who had nothing better to do. Some of the greatest names ever were members of the Iron Curtain, Digital Mortality, The Local NVA Company, Joshua Dunn, Deke Aurora, Alexander Sebastian Saunders III, Lacuna Debris, even Mike Montgomery! Yes, he called Digital and begged for the Iron Curtain to let him join. We, such good people that we were, let him join, and that was the demise of the Curtain. Mike Montgomery the Master Manipulator.] (The scene opens with Digital Mortality and Burton Bell standing in his trophy room at his beautiful house in Isla Verde, Puerto Rico. He is looking at some old pictures of himself, remembering the old times, the memories. They are laughing when they look at a certain picture, then they seem to see one that was not such a good memory and Digital frowns. The room has a huge plasma TV, and to it's right and left, the belts and awards he has recieved over the years. On the left wall though, there is a crystal case and in it the coveted Hall of Fame trophy. The walls are covered with old pictures. Digital beckons the camera to come closer and leads him to a picture of Digital Mortality holding Killer Dude and Bruce Gordy's hand up in victory over their fallen foe The Plague. That was the time when Digital helped Killer beat Plague in a cage match in Digital's debut at Global Warming III.) DM: This was so long ago. That is how I started in the GIWF, uniting with probably the worst stable in GIWF history, but one that gave the fans so many great individual wrestlers. The Elements of Destruction was a mess! The only thing I got from them was, direction. I started as a guy who used to destroy buildings with my computer.(Digital laughs) Damn was that a crappy gimmick or what!? But it's no worry, we all have a crappy gimmick at least once. The important thing is where we are now. Ah, the old GIWF days. It's true Kazee, Gurgles was reigning supreme, but you are confusing Golden Ages. The GIWF was it's own golden age, I am talking about the UWF. Yes, I admit you are a very different person from the last time I saw you, at least personality wise. Physically wise we will see. You seem to think that just because I say that you are the same as years ago, that I will be overconfident. I know you have skill, and I respect your wrestling abilities, but you are just in our way. You are in the way of the true future of the UWF. It is going to be a bright future, but you want to keep following the Master Manipulator Mike Montgomery around. Such a waste of talent. I know you don't remember the Curtain, mainly because you were not there at that time. Ask Dunn, he knows how we took it to the next level, so never talk to me about teamwork. You see now? I am not stuck in the past, I AM the past. And together with the Present and now the Future, we will turn the UWF around and make it better than ever. I don't really care about the tag titles Kazee. What I care is about sending a message to everyone on the roster, that you are either with us or against us. I am very sure they will get that message. BB: Yo Digi, check this picture out! (Digital goes over to Burton, who shows him the picture. They both burst out laughing. Digital takes it and goes over to the camera.) DM: This is me, when I joined the GIWA...... HAHAHAHA! Oh my god, was I that stupid back then? Even though when I joined the GIWA I won my first IC Title, it was pathetic joining Gold E. Fish. You see Ezra, I did not have everything goven to me, I fought for everything. I will only suggest to you, that you put the Digital toys away... BB: Erm... action figures. DM: Um... right, put the Digital action figures away and get ready for the real thing. I will not be as easy to beat as them. (Digital goes over to his 2002 Wrestler of the Year award.) DM: This was given to me after that great year that was 2002. I was surprised to get it, but I did. It's funny how so many egos inflate over such a trivial thing. Mike, you are right about one thing, I am not thinking I can come here and have whatever I want, I know I have to fight for it, like I did long ago. It doesn't matter, because you know that what I'll do is destroy the whole roster. Mike, there are no accolades that need to be earn, because I have had them all. I am not here for the gold, you don't seem to understand that, it is about the UWF. When you understand that it is not about gold but about the UWF, you will truly become a good wrestler/commissioner. You see, there you go again trying to manipulate everything to your advantage. You have always been one sneaky bastard. Not me, I always confront things head on. Moraless understands what our mission is, I am not manipulating him, this was his decision, why would I use him if I already had all I wanted? That is the reason I left the UWF in the first place, there was nothing else for me to do. You know what really makes me laugh though Mike? You saying how you beat me day in and day out. You seem to have a very short memory. You should check what you say before talking Mike. The Curtain defeated you time and time again. So much defeat came your way that you begged me to join the Curtain. You don't remember those little details do you? No, of course not. The truth hurts doesn't it? You know something Mike, I never really blamed you for the demise of the real UWF. What bothers me is that in the real UWF you were nothing. You were no one. Suddenly you become Commissioner, or you get some other position in management and suddenly almost every piece of gold is in the Player's Club's hands. I know you care nothing about the UWF, you only care about yourself and your little Player's Club. You only care about talking backstage and giving your people the pushes and the title shots. You are nothing but a cheap imitation of Jamie Shock. He wanted to drown us with Ryder, but you want to drown us with yourself. At least Jamie was nice enough to keep his ugly mug out of it. When you name the people who have tried to take the power away from you, I don't see anyone worth even fighting with. The Chaos Society? Who are those? You have to understand Mike, that the tides are turning, we are nothing like The Prime Alliance and The Chaos Society. You have never fought against an enemy as powerful as Moraless and myself. Remember my words Mike, the Resurrection has already begun. We are the Past, Present, and Future of the UWF, and you will be proven wrong come Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. On SAS the joke will be on you. (Scene fades)
