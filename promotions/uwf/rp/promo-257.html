Longest...promo...EVER!!

28 Feb 2004

Mike Montgomery — A Halifax Bar

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Respect. Respect is one of the most important things in a wrestler's life. When a wrestler has received the respect of his peers...of the fans...he knows he's done well over the course of his career. In the ring, it is the respect and trust between two wrestlers that creates the needed feeling of safety in the ring. Seymour Almasy has earned respect, both from his peers and from the fans. The things Seymour has accomplished in the ring are things that may never be seen again. Seymour is a true legend in this business and I respect him for that. To date, the biggest honor of my career was to face him in a fatal four way match back at Live and Loud Three. But of course that can't compare to the honor of being able to say that I faced him one on one. Win or lose, that would be something I could look back on with pride. I have nothing but admiration and, most importantly respect for him. Who he is...the things he's done. I respect all of my opponents. Everyone from Bruce Gordy to Raid to Ruff Ryder, right up to Jamie Shock. I respect them not for who they are, nor what they've done in the past, but for what they can do. I respect them because it is that respect that keeps me on my guard. It is that respect that helps remind me that if I'm not careful, I can be put to the mat for those three seconds by anyone, any time, anywhere. Regrettably, there are still those who don't recognize the value of respect. Jamie Shock didn't. He made the mistake of not giving me my due in the ring. Jamie did not respect his opponent and therefore he lost everything. His career is no more, he lost the power he had as Commissioner, and his Prime Alliance is no more. Ruff Ryder respects no one and this is his biggest failing. True, he has held the World Title...for all of ten seconds, but what else has he done? It is a mistake to not respect someone you've never cleanly beaten. It is his ego, his unwavering conviction that he is unbeatable, that having a stable will put him at the top of the pecking order, that is his biggest weakness. That will always be his downfall. Finally, one cannot forget Moraless. The enigma. A man who had already earned the respect of both the fans and his fellow wrestlers, yet decided to throw it all away. What can he possibly gain from helping re-form the Prime Alliance? With his public declaration of leaving for RPW, Moraless has put the new Prime Alliance on life support right off the bat. These people's actions can benefit no one, and that's why, in the end, they'll fail.
