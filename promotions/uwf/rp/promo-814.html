UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #814

Dig Your Grave

4 Dec 2004

Clyde — Oak Park Cemetery

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I stand in the pouring rain, the chill of the night biting at my flesh, but the fury of the storm cannot compare to the wailing of my soul. A shovel in hand, I dig ever onward into the cold ground, dirt flying over my mighty shoulder. Further I travel downward, in every sense of the word, as I stand in this cemetery and dig, dig the grave. Nothing may stop me now. My pain, Montgomery! My pain will eat you alive. Don't you want to partake of it? Wouldn't you love to know the sensation of such agony, that it would shatter your very mind and soul to feel but a moment of it, before I take the life from your body? Digging. Soon it will be deep enough to to prevent escape, to erase cowardice in an eternal grip of earth. Our grave, Montgomery. We were born into it. You and me. Stare into my wide, chaos stricken eye, and travel with me. Relive with me that which I relive for each and every moment of all time. Can you see her there? My darling Bethany, dancing the madcap frolic of one so far into a state of denial as to no longer even BE in denial. Her lie became truth, through sheer force of will. She wasn't always like that, you see. Once upon a time, she was almost a normal little girl. At one point, I would have told you that I knew nothing about her past. The time before she met me, that is....that would have been a lie. I am, after all, Satan. Beelzebub. The Prince of Lies, walking this earth. There is little that I do not know. There is much that I choose to lie to myself about, though. In that way, I didn't even know myself until very recently, when my own last veneers of sanity left me, blowing away into a tornado that was as a blissful and perfect scream. You of course know our origin. How she began following me about one day, back when I didn't quite know who I was myself. How we met in a church, and how she asked why I held the fire in my hand the way that I did. What you don't know is that before that..... 'STOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOP!' ........oh god, not now. 'You son of a bitch!', shrieks the object of my affection, the rain beating down upon her in the darkness of the night. 'What the hell do you think you're doing!?' 'I'm digging a grave,' I reply as sarcastically as demonically possible, while flinging a shovel full of dirt in her general direction. Sadly, it doesn't quite make it. 'Ehhhhhhhhhhhhhh, digging a grave,' she replies in an even more sarcastic tone. 'I know you're digging a grave. You know that's not what I mean!' 'Then what,' I sigh, 'do you mean? Please, enlighten me.' 'I mean you're about to give away my origin on a cheapass promo against Mike Montgomery!' I pause, as the thunder claps and lightning flashes overhead. I stare at her blankly. '........why, you are right. I am, aren't I?' With a shrug, I toss the shovel over my shoulder, letting it impale itself vertically in the ground. 'Yes you are!', yells Bethany, flailing her arms madly. 'And look at this scenery! A cemetery! Digging a grave!? God, how fucking generic can you get, moron!?' 'I rather liked it,' I say to her, somewhat hurt. 'It fit my mood at the time, and.....' 'Nobody cares about your pissy ass mood swings!', she bellows in return, producing one of her blades and clonking me over the head with it. 'I bet you were going to end this promo with something all dramatic and symbolic and crap, like burying yourself in the grave at the end too!' 'Wrong!', I answer her quickly. 'I was going to end it by burying myself in the gr.....' Again, I stare at her. 'Well that's not the point. The point is, it was going to be a very large and dramatic tale of your origin, and.....' 'AND YOU ARE NOT GIVING AWAY MY ORIGIN FOR A MATCH WITH MIKE FUCKING MONTGOMERY! NOT IN A GENERIC-ASS CEMETERY, AND NOT WHEN YOU HAVE SUCH A CHEESY-ASS ENDING SUCH AS THAT ONE PLANNED OUT AHEAD OF TIME!' 'But what if I lose to him?,' answereth I in the most puppy dog voice I can muster. 'I'd never be able to live it down.' 'Who cares if you lose to him?', Bethany replies, whipping out another blade and waving both of them theatrically in the air. 'It's me we're talking about! Me me ME!' 'But.....this promo can't be more than two pages long as is. It's a pay per view, we really.....' 'SHUT UP AND LET'S GET HOME SO YOU CAN PLEASURE ME BY LICKING MY COMBAT BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOTS!' I sigh once more, my head hanging low. 'Yes dear.' Thoroughly beaten, I sulk towards her. And then, I glance up. Reaching forward, my hands lock about a pair of forms, who I pull from the gloom. 'Scourge! Violator!' 'Yeah, boss?', Scourge answers. 'I am the Violator!', Violator answers as well. 'We need to kill some time in this promo. Get over there and kill some time!' With a shove into both of their backs, I propel them back towards the grave site before stomping out of view. For a long moment, they merely stand there. Then, they look at each other. And then.....they stand there. And finally, they begin to dance. 'Let's all go to the lobby, let's all go to the lobby. Let's all go to the lobby, and get ourselves some snacks!' 'I am the Violator!' Both men then strike poses, though to what I do not know, nor do I care. Quickly I emerge again, racing up and pushing both of my associates as hard as I can, knocking them into the open grave. 'Why you gotta be like that man!?' 'Prepare for total violation!!!!!' 'PLEASURE! BOOTS! PLEASURE!!!!!' And as I sigh once more, I make my way back into the eternal darkness......
