UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #817

Table For Two

4 Dec 2004

Hot Mask — Various

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bottom of the Mountain - 8:30 AM: A blanket of snow as far as the eyes can stretch blankets the ground, with the horizon broken into several polygraphic-like lines as a result of the mountainous terrain. Our panoramic view of this scene will provide to be the most expensive shot of the usually low-budget Hot Mask promotional videos, so enjoy it while it lasts, folks. Like something from a Jerry Bruckheimer film, we circle around the remote area of the Himalayas, peaks of the gargantuan rock structures stretching to the sky and wind blowing with ferocity that causes clods of snow and dirt to tumble off the centerpiece of the scene. This mountain, one which laughs in the face of those daring enough to try and scale it, is where we zoom in to the bottom level. Here, the determined Hot Mask, dressed in an orange snowsuit and thick black climbing boots, stands poised to conquer, with pick in a gloved left hand and bungee cord gripped firmly in the right. Thanks to the magic of overdubbing, we observe his first vertical steps while hearing his voice (which is edited to sound deeper and more heroic, somewhat like Mr. Moviefone) in the background. 'For the week, I have sought knowledge in successfully overcoming the most retarded gimmick match in the universe.' Mask rubs the face of the mountain, exhaling a visible breath from his uncovered mouth. He rests his body weight against it for a moment, checking its durability. 'I have dealt with several individuals on my quest. Mormons, who's annoyance and nonsensical ways far surpasses even that of a Dreammaker interview.' Taking a grappling hook from his side, he stretches part of it out, checking for any loose ends on the rope. Staring with intent at a nearby ledge above, he observes it and with all of his might, throws the hook in the air to catch. 'I lost my lunch in an encounter to better cope with felines at a drag club. The only useful information I find...Craig Wright fucks guys...something that's already pretty much common knowledge.' The hook hangs onto a protruding rock. Mask pulls on it to remove all slack, and prepares to climb. 'Ha-ha! Craig Wright has sex with men who dress like women. And doesn't deny it.' However, his climb will not be possible on this ledge, as the rock loosens, and Mask's jerking only causes it to come tumbling down, landing just short of putting him out of his misery. 'Both of these quests proven futile. It has been my learning experience to discover the true righteous path...that I must search the highest possible pinnacle of ridiculousness in order to win such a match as silly as a Tables...Ladders...and CATS match!' Slightly peeved, Hot Mask finds a ledge a bit higher off of the ground. He slings the hook up to it, allowing it to graze the area, and pulls tight. Checking things twice to assure that no more falling projectiles will tumble from the sky, he begins his ascent. 'I must seek him. I will find him. And nothing will stop me...' About five feet up in the air, Mask struggles with his carabiner and tilts himself forward a bit, deploring a gigantic fart from his butt. 'Heh heh heh! I bet your mouth was all open and shit right then.' Dramatic action music then replaces the words of Hot Mask, as we pan out to another wide-open shot of a figure scaling the huge mountain that we'll go ahead and infer is the Towering Inferno of Titanic Testicles, reaching closer and closer to the top. Although it *might* not really be him but just borrowed clips from various educational videos...who really cares? You get to see a pretty mountain and some dude climbing it in various screenshots switching back and forth! I mean, how awesome is THAT? ... Oh, shut up. * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 'The Peak of The Mountain' - 12:05 PM We find ourselves smack dab inside of an oval cavern, apparently on top of the large mountain just scaled by our valiant hero, Hot Mask. Lit up by various torches placed strategically around the circumference of the area, the heavily-clothed Mask stands with back turned to the camera, arms at his sides, and breathing heavily due to his monstrous climb. Although he appears weary, he spares no words as he mutters out a greeting. 'You...you really DO exist!' Moving forward, we gaze upon the veneration and excitement that Mask's eyes are filled with. A large red throw rug sits in between Mask and his object of address, and sitting adjacent from it is none other than the 10th Wonder Of The World... 'The piece of broken table that won the Hardcore Championship!' The piece of table stays still in response. Mask seats himself at his current position, Indian-style, as he continues. 'I have searched long and hard for you...almost three hours...and now I understand full well why...it is truly an honor to be in your presence!' Hot Mask exclaims with awe. The table responds, in a voice that can only be described as the teacher's voice from the Charlie Brown movies. 'Waaaah waaah waaah, waaaaaah waaaaaah waaaaah waaaaah waaaah! Waaah?' 'Yes, I do have a question for you, which is why I have come all this way to seek your expertise,' Mask responds. 'I must know...' A brief pause as Mask gathers the courage to ask his question. '...why must we be tortured by the management of the UWF to be forced to watch Dreammaker interviews? It seems sick and inane, but I know that there must be some higher purpose!' The table ponders lightly, showing no signs of emotion whatsoever. 'Waaaaah waah waaaaah waaaah waah wah, wahhh; waaaah, waaaah, waaaaah, WAAAAAAH.' Mask, who's mouth is agape in disbelief, nods his head. 'Oh my Mask! It all makes sense to me now!' The piece of broken table grunts softly in reverence. 'Waaaaah wah waaaaah waaaah?' it asks. 'I do have one more concern, O' Mighty One. Since you are in fact all-knowing, I don't have to explain my current dilemma in defending my gaudy scrap of tin at FANdemonium...but, I must know...how can I best use the materials to gain the upper advantage in my match? Please, enlighten me!' 'Waaaaaah...' starts the table, '...waaah wah waaah waaah wah, waaaah waaah syphilis waaah wah. Waaaah waaaah wah wah WAH wah WAH wah WAAAAAH!' 'I see,' Mask states in thought. 'Thank you so much, Mister Table...' The Der Furher of Fuck is abruptly interrupted by the table. 'Waaaah wah,' it interjects. A huge smile grows across the face of the Masked Masturbator. 'Wow...I mean...Misses Table...' Thanks to the incredible art of video editing, we hear a harp play in the background as a stream of cartoon hearts and singing birds begin to flutter around the skull of Mask, as we zoom in to get a full head shot. The view shifts to the piece of the broken table, and animated images of log cabins and bottles of Aunt Jemima's maple syrup are spinning at a dancy pace around the top of the perforated edge of its base. Bringing the view back out, we see the two glaring into each others' eyes (figuratively), and the camera slowly fades... # They say we're young and we don't know # # We won't find out until we grow # Hot Mask, dressed in a white long-sleeved shirt and a pair of green tights, resembling something out of a movie based on the Middle Ages, skips with delight from one end of a grassy meadow plain. The piece of the table, which has a piece of rope attached to it, 'bounces' along the meadow towards Hot Mask. The camera work is a bit shoddy, as you can see a pair of legs and Nikes just behind the table in the form of the operator making it move. The two eventually meet in the middle, and Mask and the piece of table roll around in the grass, with Mask laughing lovingly and affectionately. # Well I don't know if all that's true # # 'Cause you got me, and baby I got you # The scene cuts out and into an empty swimming pool, with the piece of table bobbing in and out of the water. Hot Mask comes off the side with a huge cannonball, drenching the wood and almost causing it to sink to the bottom. Mask then playfully splashes it, giggling like a little schoolgirl. # Babe # # I got you babe # # I got you babe # We then are taken to a carnival scene, where Mask and the table are rested on a bench just next to a food stand. Hot Mask has a big spindle of cotton candy that he eats, and with two fingers, takes a chunk off and 'feeds' it to the piece of table. The cotton candy falls, and Mask seductively cleans her 'mouth' off with a napkin. # They say our love won't pay the rent # # Before it's earned, our money's all been spent # Still at the carnival, we see Hot Mask feverishly throw a softball at a trio of bottles resting upon a tree stump. With eloquence, he pitches it right into the middle and knocks over the bottles! The man behind the stand hands Hot Mask an oversized novelty pencil, which peeves our hero (because oversized novelty pencils are lame), so he takes the pencil and proceeds to break it over the man's back. # I guess that's so, we don't have a pot # # But at least I'm sure of all the things we got # Mask and the table both sit together on a park bench, with the piece of broken table atop Mask's lap. The two notice an old woman walking down the path with the assistance of a cane. Mask sneers, and throws the piece of table in the lady's path, causing her to trip and fall straight to the ground. Hot Mask hurriedly grabs the piece of table off the dirt and brushes her off, and both begin to double over in laughter at their practical joke. # Babe # # I got you babe # # I got you babe # Sitting on the bed of Mask's humble abode in his newly-purchased brick home are the two lovebirds. Mask is dressed in a pair of black jockey shorts, and is busy caressing the surface of the broken table. He leans in and kisses her. Meanwhile, Skankalie stands in the far corner, dressed in a revealing black teddy, with her hands on her hips and a look of disgust across her face. Mask doesn't seem to care. # I got.....you babe! # * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 'The Morning After' - 9:35 AM With a yawn, Hot Mask rolls out of his bed, making sure not to wake the snoozing piece of the broken table that lays next to him. However, upon putting his feet to the ground, he notices that Skankalie is still sleeping there, seemingly uninterrupted by her uncomfortable conditions. Mask dangles his feet in her face and chuckles to himself, tempted almost to step on her as he stands up but a striking pain in his crotch causes him to scurry to the bathroom. 'Oh please oh please oh please I can't afford this right now...please no,' he whispers to himself, closing the restroom door behind him. Flipping on the light switch, he opens the elastic waistband and peers down inside. 'Aw, MAN! I got a splinter!' Fiddling down there, he breathes a sigh of relief as it seems that he has retrieved the splinter from off of his Dongersaurus. 'Guess that's what I get for fucking a carpenter's dream, eh?' Mask gazes into the mirror with a smile on his face, admiring his defined body. Taking note of the cameraman behind him in the reflection, he waves into the mirror. 'Hey, Steve.' 'Yo,' Steve responds, a thumbs-up sign given as a returned greeting. Mask turns to face the camera, not bothering to remove his hand from inside of his jockey shorts. 'Guess it's time for the moment everyone's been waiting for. I know all of you Irish muttons are gonna prematurely ejaculate if I don't provide a few 'thoughts' of my opponents in this upcoming match. I feel as if I owe you a favor to provide a least a bit of gripping wit, since it's sure as fuck apparent that you aren't going to get it from Craig Wright.' Mask takes a deep breath, preparing himself for a long train of insults in order to make you smile and/or laugh out loud. 'FARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRT~!' screams the butt of Mask, as he turns it toward the camera and waves the stench in Steve's general direction. You can almost see stink lines protruding, and Steve begins to choke and tears start to well up in his eyes. 'Damn, man!' Steve cries out, trying to shade himself from the stench. His waving proves futile, however...and suddenly, our view switches to the ground, as we go barreling toward the tiled floor of the bathroom! SMASH! Black.
