UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #196

Elimination

27 Jan 2004

Raid — Turner Field

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a wide shot of the of the WNU ring in Turner Field Atlanta, Georgia. You then see a shadow cast onto the ring from an unknown figure that looms on the entry way. The figure starts to walk down to the ring and upon reaching it he climbs into the ring. You then realize the man is Raid.) (Raid:) It has been clear thus far that my sole purpose in life was to destroy the pests of the world, it was to eliminate that which was considered a pest. Now that I have changed careers it seems ironic that my task seems all to similar, it feels like something I have done before. As a matter of fact it IS something I have done before. The only thing that has changed it that which is to be eliminated. On Feburary 4th I will eliminate that which stands in my way. I will eliminate the pests known as Iron Lung, Axle Action and Moraless. These men think they have what it takes to win but let me tell you, these three men are going to fall victim to one of the most leathal poisons known to the UWF. These men are going to suffer just like everything else that has tasted the poison. If they are lucky they will fall quickly because the longer they fight it the more torment and pain they will have to endure. That is if I let them fall quickly, for I love to watch pests suffer before they are put out of their misery. In the end the result is always the same. The poison known as RAID takes control and the victims fall helplessly in defeat. (A large cloud of smoke has begun to form around Raid and the camera begins to pan out and fade to black.)
