UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #485

F U N00BS

TODAY, Bitch (year unrecorded)

Kenny Mercury — Log Cabin

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Stable Wars: The pinnacle event of co-operative team wrestling. Six four-man teams enter… Only one survives. No… they aren’t 6’4’’, that’s six teams of four. And one of those teams, you know the one featuring KENNY MERCURY… well, that one is going to win. Why? Because not only is it made up of THE BEST OF THE BEST! With superior attributes in strength, power, cunning, speed, and precision… but Kenny Mercury is UNSTOPPABLE! He’s a shooting star… the technical wizard… THE VERY DEFINTION of precision. Really, he is, look it up.] [The starry night hangs up in the sky in America’s wooded Midwest. Smoke billows up out from a chimney of a small log cabin, nestled within the trees. Candles light the inside and their warm glow flicker upon a wooden table. Inside, a bear skin rug covers the ground, at the foot of the fireplace. Suddenly, a shiny black boot plants itself on the rug. It’s the boot of the shooting star himself, KENNY MERCURY!] Kenny Mercury: This beast... This monster… This sinister incantation of Mother nature’s fury and wrath… 300 pounds of pure power… this grizzly bear, is nothing more than a cute little carpet in this, my summer cabin. And where did the Shooting Star of the UWF come across such a fine piece of rug-etry? No, I didn’t buy it at some gypsy flee market that most of the UWF were conceived and delivered. I, Kenny Mercury, slew this beast myself, out in the Upper Michigan wilderness. I was strolling through the wilderness, seeking out some wrestling competition from the local lumberjacks and Sasquatch when the 12 foot grizzly jumped me from behind! But I got the best of him. You see, with my impeccable technical wrestling skills, I locked the gnarled brute into the dreaded Spell Binder! And as I applied pressure, the creature yelped in pain… and then… CRACK! It’s spinal cord was turned into Figgie pudding. You see… I am not only the shooting star… the ring doctor… the definition of precision…. The technical… and the sultan of submission… but I am… THE BEAST SLAYER~!!!!!!!! [Mercury flexes his biceps and pauses for a few moments… and pauses… and pauses…] [AND PAUSES…. Okay, he’s done.] Kenny Mercury: So, UWF, BEWARE OF THE BEAST SLAYER~! Haha! So what’s on the Beast Slayer’s agenda tonight? What else but STABLE WARS SIX! Nothing… nothing else, that’s what else. You see, last year, was a dismal effort on the part of my stable, The Youngbloods. Danny Jaye… Slasher… and MAX DAMAGE? What a crock... A first class wrestler like me stuck with an ape in chain mail, a freak on a leash, and a retarded loser ass-hat in Max Damage. Was Lyle Wile in there? I can’t even remember… Oh well, the point is I was SCREWED OVER by having a non-talent banana bunch of imbeciles for teammates. But this year… this year… heh heh heh… this year is different. *AHEM* Kenny Mercury: This year, I have assembled the GREATEST STABLE EVER THOUGHT OF EVER! With me at the fore-front, we shall be unstoppable. Corporal Punishment… the backbone, stronger than an Abrams tank and even more lethal. He’s the intimidator, the enforcer, THE RAVAGER… The camouflaged crusader! The electric elicitor. A teaming of he and I would be enough to win Stable Wars alone… but no.... WE HAVE MORE! Seymour Almasy… though a crack-baby, obviously, is very dangerous in the ring, and knows how to win. And after that… There’s…um… Richard Ray Vaughn… So we couldn’t get Lew Spectre… so sue me. HE BEAT THE FREAKING G’VNOR FOR CHRISSAKE~!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kenny Mercury: Yeah, you pieces of soggy coleslaw… We’re coming for you... and we’re going to OWN YOU ALL. See you tomorrow. Hoes. [End Transmission.]
