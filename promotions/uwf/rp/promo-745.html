Dreammaker — out side the arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with Dreammaker walking out the back door of the arena with no shirt on and a brand new belt arcoss his right shoulder) DM: Man it feels good to win! Damn! Did everyone see how that cage just shut White Wolf down. He didnt even stand a chance, I beat the living snot out of him. Probably back at his den licking his wounds or just playing with himself. Who cares, (looks around the corner of the building to make sure no one is around) ( starts to walk at a fast pace to his car) Looks like the coast is clear. (Jus then some fans start to yell his direction and crowd around him before he can get into his car) Fans: Sign some stuff for us!!! (DReammaker grabs his pen and starts signing and smiling and thanking for the congrats on the first big win) 'So how does it feel to be the champ?' yells one of the fans. Dm: It sounds good. I like the sound of this. The champ! It feels good. To good to be true, kinda like im dreaming and I dont want to wake up. But you know who needs to wake up?? Mr. Justice! Who needs to feel a little wake up call. You may have survived the battle royal against all odd, which let me be the first to congraduate you. But now you are in my world and let me tell you. Your dreams dont come true here. Im the champ, and i cant be pinned. So how are you gonna beat me. I have yet to unleash my manuevers live on tv yet. No one has seen me at my best. I have watched your tapes already and I'll continue to watch them this weekend. So get ready cuz im ready. And when i'm finished with you, It will be all over after you go 'Nighty-Night!'. (Just then a bunch of cars speed up to the crowd which scatters and out jumps a bunch of large body building type figures. Dreammaker tosses the belt on the hood of the car flexes for the guys coming towards him. There is a pause as everyone looks at everyone and then the threee guys put there arms around Dreammaker and congaduate him.) Guy #1: Lets go celebrate the night away at my gym. I have some drinks and lots of weights. Dm: Well not to late cuz i got a match tomorrow! #1: Never stopped you before! DM: And it wont stop me now ( as he high fives the guys and tehy all get into their vehicles and start to drive away) (scene fades)
