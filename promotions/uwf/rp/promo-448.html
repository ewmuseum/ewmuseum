Being politically correct has it's downfall. But luckily, there are still some real men out in the world who do not get offended easy. At one time, a man could say what was on his mind. He could call another man a name, and the other man would simply confront the other man. A fight might have started from the words, but afterwards, both men would wipe the dirt from their clothing and walk away as if nothing ever happened. That would never happen in today's day and age. When a person says something may be offensive to another human being, they have to cry and whine about it and get the legal system involved. What a pity. One person is offended with prayer in school, and what happens, they ban praying in school. One minority stands up on opposition to the 10 Commandments, and what happens? No more 10 Commandments in the courtroom. Now if a woman is employed at your job, you can not tell that she has a nice rack or tell her she looks nice, or she may be offended to the point that you lose your job if she complains. What ever happened to people simply fighting the battles themselves? What ever happened to embracing the American way of waiting for someone to come out of their work after making a comment that you did not like, and drilling him in the back of the head with a pipe or bat? That was true judgment. That is true freedom. Thank goodness that there still some places in this world that you can still live up to the old American way for dealing with problems. Like the UWF for instance. It is still pure. It still allows men to settle their differences in a violent way without getting politically correct. At least for now. After all, look at some of the talent we have in this federation... Names like Nick Persia. Tell me that you don't think of a B rated Sinbad movie. Or Ruff Ryder... does that remind you of a two dollar porno supporting actor who takes it up the rear for a chance to give another man a ORAL EXAM from the meat flute? Then we can not forget Loverboy. The leading role for that same two dollar porno. Moraless???? Excuse me, but is there not a requirement to have at least a shred of creativity when using a stage name? With a name like that, no wonder the guy lives in Manchester England... he is probably the one who wrote the two dollar porno for Persia and Loverboy. After all, he is from MAN-CHEST-HAIR. The list goes on and on. What ever happened to the HEEL in wrestling? Is the Corporal the only real evil in this federation? Only time will tell. But until others decide to step up and be accounted for, the Corporal of Punishment will gladly hold that proud title of HEEL. And he will proudly broadcast to the entire world that he loves to hate others. He loves to verbally assault others. And he will gladly continue his assault on those who are weaker than he is. After all, that is the American way, and there is no other greater American ALIVE that can live the American dream the way it was originally intended EXCEPT it be the Corporal of Punishment. With that said, let me proudly introduce to you the one and only Enforcer of professional wrestling. And do not be surprised if he does not make your grandmother cry, because the CORPORAL has entered the building... CORPORAL STYLE! Just a few hours remain before Corporal Punishment enters the ring to face a man who calls himself Nick Persia. At first glimpse it would look like the Corporal would actually have a contest on his hands. After all the opponent known as Nick Persia has a defining win/loss record. At least that is what his bio page states. But it appears that it was just something that looks good on paper because with just a few hours left to go, the man known as Nicky P. is hiding somewhere. The Corporal on the other hand, is ready for battle. He has already started brutal work out in preparation for Sundays event. He mentally prepared to destroy this myth known by the name of NICKY, and in a few hours, Nicky will probably be crying in a corner with his thumb in his mouth. This scene opens up in the basement of 235 Crawford street. The basement is dark, and the Corporal stands in front of a homemade heavy bag. The Corporal, dressed in basic fleece warm-up pants with black tee-shirt and black wrestling shoes, stands there slamming his fists into the bag repeatedly. Each time, the Corporal makes contact with the heavy bag, the sound of a person gasping in pain can be heard. After close examination of the heavy bag, you can see that it is really not a heavy bag at all. Instead it is an elderly man chained feet first from the ceiling. His hands and feet are bound and his mouth is taped shut. Blood is running out of almost every orphas of his body. The man, dressed only in a pair of boxing trunks, is clearly terrified and injured. Blow after blow, the man convulses in pain. The Corporal continues to slug away at the human heavy bag until it, the bag, goes completely limp. The Corporal then stops throwing punches and stares at the bloody corpse. Corporal: Don't quit on me now old man. I still have one more hour of this to go. You would think you could suck it up for an entire training session. The Corporal walks over to a 5 gallon bucket filled with vinegar and water. Kneeling down, he takes a nearby cup and fills it with the mixture. Looking towards the old man who appears to be unconscious, he throws the vinegar water in the elderly mans face. The elderly man suddenly begins to shriek as the vinegar touches his open wounds. His body begins to convulse from the pain brought on by the vinegar based water. Corporal: THAT'S MORE LIKE IT! Finally you are alive again.... NOW... WHERE WAS I? The Corporal returns to the human heavy bag and being pounding away. Body blow after body blow is heard as the elderly man can be heard gasping for air. This continues for approximately 15 seconds, and then the telephone begins to ring. Corporal: GO FIGURE! In disgust, the Corporal throws his leather gloves to the side, and un-latches the elderly man from the ceiling. A loud thud is heard as the Corporal briefly looks down at the bludgeoned elderly man and shrugs. Corporal: Fine. Go ahead and get up. It is obvious that I am not going to get to finish this episode. So you can leave. Just clean the blood off the floor before you go. I would hate to have to call you in for a second opinion Mr. Smith. The Corporal grabs a towel that hangs in the doorway. He walks up the basements stairway and finally answers the phone. Corporal: WHAT! This better be good! Telephone: ... Corporal: So you still haven't heard from that cowardly opponent I face tonight? Telephone: ... Corporal: That is fine I guess. I mean if the kid thought this was a picnic, he is sure going to be surprised. It amazes me how these guys just run away from real competition. The kid supposedly had a pretty good record. He looked like I was going to get to play with his carcass for at least five minutes before leaving him in the ring for dead. But it looks like this kid will be about a 15 second slaughter. Telephone: .... ! Corporal: Yep. You would think that there would be stronger opposition for me any style. But that is not the case. This will be the second beat down in a month that I virtually destroyed an idiot in wrestling tights. It just goes to show you how wrestling has changed from old school to this new modern day crap! Next thing you know, there will be men floating to the ring, and computers will do all the fighting for them! I really mean this Lew, this day and age of wrestling is a sad one. There is no way that these guys who call themselves wrestlers, could make it 2 months in wrestling OLD SCHOOL way. Not one of them! Telephone: ..., ... ... ... Corporal: You are right. Listen, I can not talk right now. You just make sure everything is ready for Stable Wars. I have to go and see if this idiot has cleaned the blood of my basement floor yet. Telephone:....... ..... .. ....... ... ? Corporal: Yes, I said blood. Well, you know how I like to practice old school style... Well I needed an old school heavy bag. One that could really inform my on whether my punches are effective or not. And so, I remembered seeing this old man who acted scared to death of me. I befriended him and offered to play him a game of checkers. He agreed, and that was that. Telephone: ... ... .... ........? Corporal: Of course we played a game of checkers. I lost. What can I say. Well I better get down there and see what the old fart is doing. Bye. The Corporal hangs up the phone and begins to walk back down to the basement. with his back facing the camera, the camera yells out a few last words as he disappears down the steps. Corporal: Where were me... OH YEAH! I believe I had just got finished KINGING YOU! NOW HOW IS THAT BLOOD COMING UP? Don't take it personal. Just take it... CORPORAL STYLE!
