UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #802

FAN 2: Late Night with Davin Smith

1/12/04

Craig Wright — SECC studios, UK

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4 Days in counting till Craig Wright will step in the ring with the immature Pizza-face Hot-Mask, We know he is pizza face why else would he wear the mask? Dream Maker the man who wishes he could have what the Hot-Shot has, Joshua Justice a man that will have to fight 2 matches a wrestler who thinks he is actually capable of defeating the 'Highlight Of The Night'. It's all talk but no action these days in the UWF. Any man can talk the talk but can they walk the walk. Craig Wright brings some old school back into the business after all he has been hear wrestling and in spirit here in the UWF for more than 4 years. Craig Wright fought in the GIWF a place where legends entertained and 100% dedication was needed to become even the Television Champion. A lot has changed now and the Hot-Shot is going to give the title to a more realistic champion, back to a wrestler who still knows what is what and a competitor who hasn't lost the plot..yet, Craig Wright has 3 other opponents in his match, Killer Dude a man who also knows what it takes to fight in the GIWF and also Malcolm Kennedy. Is there a weak link in this match? Aiden Pryde will certainly have his hands full in a match full of of skillfull wrestlers and the Hot-Shot Craig Wright a man eager to get his claws on the UWF Television. Has the Hot-Shot trained enough to finally win the Television Title. Right now The 'Highlight Of The Night' takes time off to talk to his fans. If it wasn't for them he may have never got the title shot. [The scene opens in a Brittish Talkshow the host of the show is none other than Davin Smith a legendary comedian wrestler in the 70s. The guest of the show is none other than the 'Highlight Of The Night' Craig Wright. The show is just about to go on air and the fans are ready to applaud as Davin Stands to his feet] Smith: Hello folks and welcome to Fight Night. Im your host and referee for tonight Davin Smith. What a night we have for you. Not only has the Dancing Clown recovered from severe food poisoning but we have a special guest on tonight's show. You may remember him in the late 80s if you still watched Brittish wrestling then. He is none other than The Bad-Shot, the Hot-ass, The Fright Of The Night' weighing at a weight of 276 Ibs and hails from Grangemouth, Scotland. I introduce to you Craig Wright. [ The lights flash on the mini stage and David Bowie's Magic Dance hits the small arena. There stands Craig wright at the foot of the stage and walks over to the mini wrestling ring. He shows the fans his right bicep gives it a kiss and then takes his seat next to Davin Smith.] Smith: Welcome to the show Craig Wright how is it going. Your looking buff. Nice ass! Hot-Shot: hehe the famous Davin Smith. The man who could fool any referee and inflict pain in opponents bodies where no ther person could. The biggest cheat in the game. Your looky not so good. Is that your real hair or just a wig.Anyway I am the 'Highlight Of The Night' and you can call me Hot-Shot! if you wish. Smith: Yeah ..... anyway so Hot-Shot your back in the wrestling business and you have your first title shot already don't you feel that it is come to early for you. Hot-Shot: Well yes it has come early but I am in top form this is my match. I would never have got this match if it wasn't for all of you. I thank you all for voting me in the match. Hey nice t-shirt there. [Hot-Shot winks at the little kid at the front who has a massive smile on his face and coke all over a Hot-Shot Craig Wright t-shirt. Craig Wright talks more] Hot-Shot: Ive been working really hard for this match. I have went through so much intense training you would think I am a machine. There no way I will let any punk put me down on the mat in pin me. Not only do I have to put up with fighting with 5 wrestlers including Dream Maker but I also have to deal with Cats flying all over the place which reminds me I have to go see the doctor and see if I am actually allergic to them or not. Damn!! Smith: Has much changed now than maybe two years ago in the UWF? I mean is the atmosphere different? What about the buzz of the fans calling you a jobber, does it still give you the adrenaline rush that you need to compete with? Hot-Shot: You are a really funny guy. Ok yes I maybe was a bit of a jobber back in the days. This time round it is a different story. Go on laugh away ill give you 10 seconds to laugh until your heart is content go on. Smith: Can't man got to watch the air time. Maybe later at the break. Hot-Shot: This time round I will be the Highlight of the night nothing will be able to stop me. Not a sad bitch of champion Hot-Mask will be able to stop me. No Killer Dude no Malcolm Kennedy no one will be able to stop the powerfull Craig Wright. If you really think I am jobber Davin why dont we ask the fans. He little kid at the front, Whos your favourite wrestler. [ Craig Wright puts the mic to the ice cream covered face of the kid with the Hot-Shot t-shirt with the coke down it.] Little Kid: CRAIG WIGHT CRAIG WIGHT CRAIG WIGHT!!! Hot-Shot: Hey there, thats a nice top it highlights your beautiful eyes. Have you got a boyfriend? [The sexy women at the front giggles and blushes.] Women: No Hot-Shot I don't have a boyfriend. You know you have always been my favourite wrestler Craig. Hot-Shot: Well how about I show you some moves later on after the show how about that. Anyway you see Smith not everyone thinks I am a jobber hey you at the back whos your favourite wrestler. Guy at back: My like totally favourite wrestler man is like thee Killer Dude man hes the Dude and he's like going to kick butt on Sunday night and win the Television Title dude. Hot-Shot:..... er....... [Craig Wright runs up to the Dude and whispers something into his ear.] Hot-Shot: Hey man, give me a break don't embarass me man this is live on television. [Craig Wright whispers something to the guy then walks out of the crowd and enters the mini ring to be he is welcomed with a smiling Davin Smith] Smith: Well not everyone can be a fan Hot-Shot. Hot-Shot: Nah I think he had something stuck in his throat try asking him. Smith: Well whos your favourite wrestler? The Guy: Eh thee Hot-Shot man all the way he totally like rocks, i think he is the greatest wrestler of all time man. Hot-Mask better watch out man the Hot-Shot is on fire. [Craig Wright sarcastically smiles at Smith, Smith looks at him and sighs and then moves on with the show.] Smith: Moving on Hot-Shot. How are you going to approach this match, There are 6 opponents but not only that you have to fight in the ring full tables, ladders and CATS. I mean back in my day the craziest you could get was someone coming into the ring with a chair and getting themselves disqualified. I mean now we have Flaming Monkeys and every match you could think of. What are you going to do. Hot-Shot: I'll approach this match the same as everyone else. No one knows what to expect as none of my opponents have fought in a table ladders and cats match. Only I will go into the match with a different train of thought. You see Success drives me to win matches unlike many of the superstars in the UWF. I wan't to be someone. I want children to fight over who wants to be the 'Highlight of The Night' when they are having wrestling matches. I want the television Title to go round the waist of something that will respect it and know what its all about to be the champion. Ok I haven't won a title yet but I believe my time has come. if 6 men are put in my way of the title then so be it. If table ladders and cats are put in my way then so be that to because i will walk out winner no matter how much work I have to go through. Its time Craig Wright proves that the words coming out his mouth are for real this Sunday you will all see. All of you will see. Brown: Well I have go to thank you for coming in it has been a real pleasure. I wish you the best of luck in your match. Hot-Shot: I dont need luck. Look at me. I haven't seen anyone in this shape in a long time. Brown: Has it been that long since I wrestled. Hot-Shot: Anyway thanks for having me Brown. Ive got one more thing to say to my opponents. Bring all that you have got thats if you turn up. You probably would be better of at home and watching. I will tear all of you apart to get the Title round my waist. Brown: Good Fight Good Night. Hey Craig you want to hit the pub theres a great bar just round the corner. The Guy: Hey man can I get that 100 bucks you said you would give me dude. Hot-Shot: EAT SHIT! The Guy: You like promised me Dude give me money. In fact fuck you. Killer Dude is going to dance all over you on Sunday man you'll see. [Craig Wright takes out a cat from under the table he swings it round his head by its tail and throws it into the direction of the defenceless Killer Dude fan. The cat mauls the screaming fan. The fans go wild and applaud his ability of throwing cats. The Hot-Shot then takes the sexy female from the front by the hand and walks backstage with her whispering in her ear as she giggles away. The scene fades to the FANdemonium logo with the Hot-Shot at the front of it.]
