date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. The Cherokee Thrilla is sitting in his black leather recliner wearing a red Kansas City Chiefs t-shirt with a black Oklahoma Sooners cap turned to the side and black wind pants. He is obviously in interview mode and quickly begins to get straight to the point. ) Ryder: So I guess i've got alot of explaining to do to alot of people around the UWF. Well i'm gonna get to the bottom of all that mess today...right now. * Ryder takes in a deep breath, ready to face the music * Ryder: First off with Jamie Shock let me just say that I get what you want. I get the fact that you wanna restore your honor, but I just want you to remember that you chose this path. I'm willing to let you have your space and stay away, but just remember that when the time comes and trust me it will come that you need me...don't expect to see me come running down that entrance ramp. I helped you get the revenge you needed against Malcom Kennedy and now my debt is paid so i'll let you travel down your path to the World Title alone. I consider you a friend and always will, but you've made it quite clear that you don't need me to watch your back anymore and I get your message loud and clear. Good luck to you dawg. * Ryder pops his neck as he shifts moods * Ryder: Now as for the KB. I understand you guys are pissed and you have every reason to be. Sean, you and Jamie are far from friends, but that doesn't mean that he's not my friend. The man had my back when nobody else did and that's not something I just turn my back on and forget. I want to continue to whip ass in the KB, but you have to do what you have to do. If you choose to drop me and storm the ring after my match and make an example then so be it, you do what you gotta do, but don't think i'm gonna change who I am for anybody. If anything my helping Jamie should prove to you that i'm extremely loyal to the company I keep. I'm down for the KB for as long as you allow me to be in it, but i'm not gonna appologize for helping my friend Jamie Shock. That's just not Ruff Ryder's style, and if there's one thing you can count on it's that I don't change for nobody. So you take me as I am or drop me and we'll go from there. * Ryder takes another deep breath and his calm expression turns into a menacing glare * Ryder: Now as for the punk ass fuckin losers that i'm gonna be facin in the Regal Rumble, all I can say is may God have mercy on your soul, because I sure as hell will be showing no mercy on your body. I don't care who is standing in front of me, because i'm gonna make you wish you'd looked into the eyes of a different man. I'll beat the fuck out of you until i'm through playin around and then i'll end you. Don't think that just cause the True Indian Superstar has been out for a couple of weeks or so that i've gone soft. I'm always focused and ready to rumble. I don't care where the match is or what time of day or night because i'm always in the mood to fight. Friend or foe, whoever is put in front of me will pay the price. I'm in it to win it and there ain't nobody that's gonna rain on my parade. I'm the best thing that ever happened to a pair of wrestling boots and I don't let up for nobody. You fight me, and you'll understand why I am the True Indian Superstar and why all the ladies love the Cherokee Thrilla. I'm the man that makes your wives and girlfriends wet their panties and put them in the mood to go home and show you fat ass losers a good time. If it wasn't for them lookin at me on a nightly basis they wouldn't have the stomach to put up with you bastards much less be in the same room with you. So bring the noise, because Ruff Ryder is ready for a fight and i'm takin on all comers. ( Ryder's glare is now replaced by a cocky grin as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
