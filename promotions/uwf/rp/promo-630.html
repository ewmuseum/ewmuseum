UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #630

Snips and snails...

14 Aug 2004

Tsunami Oni — Tokyo, Japan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tokyo. Japan. Earth. Milky Way. Third Dimension. Tsunami Oni, that dashing Devil from the Deep Blue Sea, is an a small cafe, enjoying a late breakfast with the sultry Shiatsu. Why a late breakfast? Because they spent most of the morning gettin' their freak on! Awwww, yeah! Gonna make ya say 'uhhhhhhhhh!' Shiatsu: Damn! I need some more OJ! Gotta some fluids I need refilled... Oni: Ahhhh, such a lovely day! Just then, a UPS man arrives. UPS man: Salami Oni? Oni: That's TSUNAMI Oni... clown-shoe... UPS man: Sorry, somebody has lousy handwriting. Anyhoo, the desk clerk at your hotel said you were here. I got some boxes for ya. Oni: Really? Fine, I take them. UPS man: All right, Bob! Unload the packages! Four boxes, eack larger than the next, are kicked out the back of the UPS truck. UPS man: Here ya go, pal! Shiatsu: What are Amercians doing working for UPS in Tokyo? UPS man: Hey, I don't tell ya how to do your job, sweetheart! The UPS truck leaves. Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu stare at the boxes. Shiatsu: So what the hell do you think all this is? Oni: Hmmm... they came from Modesto... Shiatsu: You don't think... Oni: Sweet amaba jamma, no! And at that very moment , three of the boxes burts open, revealing three young boys with blue hair, make-up, and devil horns, just like Tsunami Oni. One has a wide mouth, one looks cranky, and the other is a rather hefty young lad. Shiatsu: The Lil' Devils! Gabby, Crabby, and Flabby! Gabby: Hi Mister Oni hi shiatsu lookat us now we look like you again we used to wear masks like you but now you don't wear a mask so we don't wear masks but we got the blue hair and white make-ip and devilhorns which don't stay on as good as yours I tried to use super glue but my mom wouldn't let and we couldn't find way cool green contacts like did you know for a long time we thought that your eyes just glowed by Crabby's dad told us they were contacts and we asked for them for Christmas except for Crrabby who's Jewish and asked for them for Hannukah but December is a long way of and - OW! You hit me! Crabby: Ya bet yer butt I did! Flabby: Hey, you gonna eat that, Mister Oni? Oni: No, go ahead, Flabby. I've somehow lost my appetite. So what are you kids doing here? Gabby: We wanted to see you at the Regal Rumble 'cuz this is your first real match back and we wanted to see you beat that Sean McClancy guy I don't know why he calls himself the Irish B-word I called him that and my mom put soap in my mouth even though I told her that's what he calls himself and she almost grounded me from watching UWF TV but I promised never to say the B-word again and OW! Stop hitting me! Crabby: Then quit yer yappin'! Shiatsu: So you guys mailed yourselves here to see the Regal Rumble? Crabby: Ya bet yer sweet patootie, doll-face! Flabby: Sweet potato? I like yams! Crabby: I said 'patootie!' Some of that lard in yer butt has got in yer ears! Oni: I'm almost afraid to ask, but what's in that biggest box there? On cue, the final box busts open and there stands a being looking a lot like the old-school Tsunami Oni except... not quite right. Bizarro Oni: Good-bye! Oni: Damn. Gabby: Our moms and dads said we could come to Japan as long as we had adult supervision and at first I thought they meant something like X-ray vision and I was like how are we supposed to get something like that but then my dad explained he meant something like a babysiiter so we thunk and thunk and thunk and we couldn't thin of anybody that would go with us to Japan to see you but then we found Bizarro Oni sleepsing in a tree and said there' an adult even though he's an imperfectly cloned adult and as so here we are and OW! Will you stop with the hitting!?! Crabby: Will you shut yer pie-hole!?! Bizarro Oni: Why me only one in mask? Oni: I've evolved. I've changed. No more of that schtick for me. Bizarro Oni: No more conquer the world? Oni: No more. I'm a serious competitor now. Bizarro Oni: Waaaaaahhhhh! How me be imperfect clone of of serious competitor? Where fun in that? Shiatsu: Uhhhhh... well, the opposite of a serious competitor would be a not-serious competitor. Oni: Do... not.. give.. him... ideas. Bizarro Oni: Yippee! Me life still got meaning! Me stil got place in grand scheme of things! Oni: Damn! Shiatsu: Sorry, I just couldn't bear to see him so sad. Oni: Sucker. Flabby: Sucker? I want a sucker! Tootsie pop! Gabby: So we're all here so we should go have fun like mahe go that Disneyland they have here or watch sumos wrestlers or maybe go see Godzilla or- OW! I haven't even said that much! Crabby: There ain't no Godzilla! Flabby: Huh? What about Mothra? Crabby: Nope. Gabby: Rodan? Crabby: Nope. Bizarro Oni: Smog Monster? Crabby: Nope. And no Monster Zero or Mecha-Godzilla or Gammera! No giant monster, period! And at that moment, a mob of Tokyo citizens screams and runs down the street. Some stop and point benhind him! Shiatsu: Oh my freakin' lord! What the hell is that!?! It's a collosal snail, right in the middle of downtown Tokyo! Bizarro Oni: That am... Es-KARRRRRGH-o! Oni: Damn. Damn. Damn. Shiatsu: That thing will destroy the city! Eventually. That puppy is slow. It certainly is. The mob still runs from Es-KARRRRRGH-o even though the mtant snal is travelling at about six inches an hour. Gabby: Hey Mister Oni you're not a bad guy anymore well you weren't really bad you always seemed a pretty swell guy to us but now you're not a supervillain anymore so maybe you could be a superhero and save the day and then we could all be your sidekick like a bunch of Robins or would that e a flock of Robins and- OW! Oni: Thank you, Crabby. Crabby: No problem-o, Mister Oni. Bizarro Oni: Me no wanna die! Oni: You won't die! You just have to walk away from that thing! Hell, Ants can get out of it's way! Bizarro Oni: Save us! Tsunami Oni looks at the faces of his greatest fans and one retarded clone. Oni: If I save the day, will you leave me alone for the rest of the time in Japan? Bizarro Oni: Anything! Flabby: I'm too young to die! If haven't tried all the food in the world yet! Oni: Fine. Acting swiftly, Tsunami Oni runs across the street to Jum-jum's Oversized Novelty Store. Oni: Got any giant salt shakers? Jum-jum: Sure do. Just got some in. Oni: Great. I'll take one. Jum-jum: That's be $299.99. Oni: Whyisn't that in yen? This is Tokyo. Jum-jum: I don't know how to convert dollars to yen. Oni: Whatever. Take plastic? Jum-jum: Got picture ID? Oni: Sure. Jum-jum: Hey! You got a mask on in this photo! How do I know it's you? Oni: Well... A blast of EVIL BLUE MIST~! brings the transaction to a close. Oni grabs the giant salt shaker and runs out the door. Carrying the seven-foot shaker on his back, Oni goes to a building right by where Es-KARRRRRGH-o is reighing excrutiatingly slow terror on Tokyo. Oni: This is so stupid. I thought I was passed this. Well, the sooner I get this done... Taking a deep breath, Tsunami Oni runs up the fire escape of the building with his giant salt shaker. Reaching the top he stares down at the mammoth mollusk. Oni: I did say this was stupd, right? With that less than enthusiastic battle-cry, Tsunami Oni leaps from the roof! He lands on the head of Es-KARRRRRGH-o and starts shaking salt onto the flesh of the monster! Es-KARRRGH-o thrashes around as his body dissolves, but Oni hang on like a champion bronco buster, shaking salt the whole time! Before too long, Es-KARRRRRGH-o is no more, just shell and Oni stands triumphant wtih an empty giant salt shaker, covered in snail goo. Oni: My Rod, melted mutant mollusk never comes out. So the city was saved, Tsunami Oni was a hero, and the Lil' Devils and Bizarro Oni left him alone... for a little while.
