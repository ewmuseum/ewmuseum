UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #379

Pride

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — On the road

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Persia is driving towards the arena in his E Type Jag; Larson is sitting beside him in the passenger seat. Both men are out of their wrestling kit and are instead dressed casually. Nick Persia in jeans and a T-shirt with a vivid and colourful logo splashed across the chest. Larson in contrast is looking a lot more formal, wearing a blue short sleeved shirt, which is unbuttoned at the collar, and a pair of neat looking black trousers.] Nick Persia (Annoyed): “I can’t believe it cost me €800 to get the bumper fixed, I mean €800 for one little bumper, who’d have thought it’d cost so much?” Larson: “It’s a classic car, what were you expecting, €5 for a new bumper and €20 to have it fitted? Besides maybe this will teach you not to do something so stupid again?” [He takes a long look at the man driving the car, then shakes his head and mutters under his breath] Larson: “There’s always a first time.” Nick Persia: “How was I to know that the bumper was so fragile. I thought it would be able to take it easy?” Larson: “I don’t know why you’re complaining so much, it’s not like you can’t afford the repairs. So lighten up already will you, cause all this moaning is getting boring.” Nick Persia: “All right, all right, I’ll stop already. But if I’m so boring, why are you here? After all I only asked you to come with me, you could have gone back to your business instead of coming to the Arena with me.” Larson: “I know, and while running your own business is cool, to tell the truth I was starting to get a bit bored. Too much of the same stuff day in day out, and you know how much I hate that kind of thing?” Nick Persia: “Yeah, but I’m a bit worried about my match with Draco Paladine.” Larson: “And why is that?” Nick Persia: “I don’t know what to expect. I saw the guy wrestling once, and he lost without having a chance to win once, although he was outnumbered. So I’m not too keen on this guy. Hell, I don’t know why they even put me in a match with this guy. But I will go in that ring and make sure that I will be on the delevering end and that Chaos Society will be on the receiving end. You see, for the past occasions I always managed to pick up the win. I was always the man to pick up the win. But Now for the first time, I am the underdog. I am the underdog, because I don’t know who I am tagging with. So i’m going into this match thinking this is a handicap match, and depending what the guy does I will see if my predicament is true.” Larson: “Look at it the optimistic way, you might get the chance to kick three butts instead of two. So nothing but advantages for you.” Nick Persia: “You always look at the bright side of life. Of course you are right. I’m a proven winner while they are nothing more than pathetic wannabe’s. They want to be like me. I’m their dream. I’m like their Idol. So you geniuses had better put your heads together and think of a ploy to stop the inevitable. It’ll be a complete waste of time though, because at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy all of your hopes and dreams are going to come crashing down. I don’t care about what personal feelings you have for each other. I don’t care if you’ve got scores to settle. But I do care about my honour and my reputation. In all sincere I will go in as a champion and I walk out as the same champion with the same dignity. A champion can not lose in a single match, even though it is a non-title match. However, this is going to be something completely different. This is a tag team match, consisting of one champion and a newbie. Of course I shall do everything in my powers, but the things that bothers me since day one this match was announced is the fact I don’t know the guy. Do I wanna know him? Maybe it is some pervert calling children to him if they bow over for an ice cream or something. Hell I do nto fear him, so what am I worrying about. In the worst scenario he doesn’t show up, but I don’t care as long as I get Adien pryde. Adien Pryde, we go a long way back. Ever since you had to put your nose in my business I have been suffering from your interferences. But each time I proved I was just ready for it, and I handled it professionally. I made sure that I did what I had to. So I fulfilled my obligations towards the fans. How about you Adien? [A Moment of Silence follows] You have been nothing but talks. You wasn’t even able to win the television championship. No, it was your stable mate who won it. What a devaluation of such a prestigious title. If I was the President I would have stripped him of the title, immediately. However, I must focus myself on that what matters. That what matters is me, and the fans. The fans who support me, and despise you. Eever wondered why that is, Adien? You make them sick to their stomach by your low class acts. You never proved your worth in a honest way. The way it should be. The way I took. The reason I am more succesfull than you. It doesn’t matter what you do, the fact to the matter is I am the better man. You won’t stand a chance. But this time something will be different from all our other battles. [Another moment of silence follows] I will beat it into your thick skull that you are not in my league. You see, you take yourself too seriously. You think you are hardcore, but you pass out when you see a barbed wire bat. Just because you fear it that much. I think you will receive a heart attack when you realise I could easily smash it into that ugly little face of yours. Beware of my abilties, and beware of my sickness. You’ve been talking about going to the edge of things, but you don’t even know that I don’t have an edge. I can go whereever I want, whenever I want. Just because I can, just because I want too. Since you want to take me to your edge, I say go ahead. I damn sure beat you again. When I will let your head CRUSH I will get the RUSH.” Larson: “What about his buddies?” Nick Persia: “They don’t matter. Because when Adien is in the ring I will kick his ass so hard that he won’t even be able to tag them in. He will be seeing stars, with the main star being me. I’m just being the man with the plan.” Larson: “Which plan?” Nick Persia: “If I would reveal it, it would not do it justice. So will keep the suprise for SAS. But there will be suprises enough even for me. Adien pryde, do you take pride in what you do? Because I damn sure do. I will take pride at your expense. Your time is up. Your time is over. I will be famous world wide. Wrestling is like playing cards; if you don't have a good partner, you better have a good hand. I have one. I’ll see you at SAS.” [Screen fades into the new Digital Morality T-Shirt commercial]
