UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #769

I Drink Whiskey Instead Of Water

3 Nov 2004

'The Savior' Wes Diable

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Outside a church on a day, in which the clouds appear to be more menacing then the devil himself. They lurk in the sky dark in color; waiting for their chance to pour down a burst of water from the heavens] [The church is a pale orange color and is made of stone. The steps leading up to are concrete and on each side of the steps is a ramp. There are three sets of double doors colored dark brown across the front of the Church. A tower rests on top with a bell inside of it] [The sign next to the church reads Holy Counsel Church and is surrounded by many different types of flowers, that are dying with the autumn air] [Cut away] [Inside the Church we find row after row of pews leading up to a beautiful alter. The alter has a table set on it with a wonderful white and purple cloth; above and behind it rests a giant crucifix with Jesus Christ on it. Slightly above that and along the sides a silk, gold drapery hangs leading down into a group of lovely flower arrangements. On both sides of the alter sit a series of candles for people to light for a prayer. As we move forward we spot, someone who appears to be a priest knelt down in front of one] [The priest is fairly old, probably in his late fifties as we approach. His dark hair turned grey and his hands shake as he prays. He lips the word to his prayer] [He looks up makes the sign of the cross and then rises to his feet] “Father” [He turns to find a middle-aged woman with short curly red hair standing there with a duffle bag in her hand. She wears a dark red suit pants and jacket that help hide her robust figure] “Yes, Marlene” [She brings the bag over and places it onto the pew closest to him. She appears confused as she approaches him] “I found your note and the bag... Obviously, you haven’t left yet. [He shrugs] “Well, you know, before I do this I wanted to get all the prayer in I could. [[He turns towards the cross] “Lord knows, I’m going to need it” [She looks at him completely lost] “For what? Why are you leaving?” [He turns back to her with a smile of regret. He reaches out with his arms and gives the woman a hug. She goes to speak again, but he puts his finger over her lips. He gives her a sign of the cross, she closes her eyes and lips the words “Amen.”] “I’ll be back someday, this is part of my vocation... I have to do this.” [She nods as she manages to somewhat understand. The priest grabs the duffle bag and throws it over his shoulder. The woman stands there in silence and watches the priest as he walks off through the doors and into the pouring rain of the sky outside] [Cut away] “You want a shot baby?” [A male voice asks, in a seductive manner over the somewhat quieted blaring music in the background] “Maybe in a little bit... I’m going to have a little fun first.” [We hear a quick kissing sound] “I’ll watch.” [We fade into find Wes Diable and his girlfriend Vivian sitting on a blue couch in front a glass table. Well actually Vivian now is standing up. She wears a low-cut tank top style shirt and a black skirt that comes up just high enough to keep us interested without spoiling the fun completely. Her tan skin appears even darker in this dimly lit room. Her dark hair falls straight back. She’s got a devilish grin on her face as she starts to walk away from Wes. Wes gives her a slight tap on the behind, soliciting a look and a smile] “I’ll be by in a bit” [Now that Vivian is gone we can focus on Wes. His dark hair parted to the side almost comes down over his blue eyes. He wears a black buttoned-down shirt with a pair of faded jeans. He leans forward watching Vivian walk away then looks down at the table at the drink in front of him, which is dark colored, probably whiskey. After taking a quick drink he leans back on couch and puts his feet up. Lights blare behind him, which we assume is also where the music is coming from] “Damn I was starting to feel bad for the UWF faithful...” [He reaches into his right pants pocket and pulls out a pack of cigarettes, which he taps on as he sits] “I was beginning to think maybe they just signed me to look pretty. Or that they didn’t want me to step in the ring and embarrass the so-called talent that calls this place home.” [He shrugs his shoulders as he takes one out and reaches into his breast pocket for a lighter] “But I guess I wasn’t THAT impressive in my tryout...” [As he lights the cigarette, he shoots us a grin and shakes his head. As to say that he doesn’t think that was the case. He puts the lighter away and leans forward with his elbows on his knees] “Anyway, my first test...” [He coughs slightly pretending as though it was from the cigarette, but we get the meaning] “Excuse me... My first opponent is The Plague.” [Another smirk from the arrogant young man] “How lucky you are my friend? How grateful you should be? The tears of joy you should shed... You get to be the first of many, the one that will be remembered as the first to fall in the path of the most illustrious career this no-so-spectacular sport has ever seen.” [He stands up taking another drag once he does, he walks around the table] “Think about it right now... Years from now you can tell your children that you were the first name that ever got mentioned by Wes Diable. People will want to shake your hand just for being in the ring with me, you know?” [We follow him as walks from this private area passed a bar on the right. He gives a quick wave to the bartender, who is a young man, who nods in return] “You were the first name I spoke on my way to greatness.” [He turns to the camera walks backwards for a moment] “Am I coming off as arrogant?” [He smirks as he takes another drag then continues to walk towards a railing. He leans against the railing] “Seriously my friend, I could go on and on with reasons as to why I am better then you... Hell, I could go on and on with reasons as to why I am better then anyone in this talent-forsaken place.” [He turns from facing the camera to out into the crowd of people below dancing away in the haze] “But I’m not going bore the masses with tireless chit-chat about the facts I’ll prove in due time. Basically come SAS, there won’t be any doubt as to who the best is... Who should believe... And who will be saved” [We now focus in with the camera to find Vivian out in the middle of the dancefloor, embarrassing two males, who appear to either have had a bit too much to drink or are overwhelmed by her. Her hips move like the create the music and the look on her face is of pure elation, she truly has mastered the art of manipulation] [Cut away]
