UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #535

A Day at the Gym

12 Jul 2004

'The Shocker' Dave Storm — Local Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(( The scene opens up infront of a large facility somewhere in a city. The camera finds a black escalade roaming through the streets near the facility, and instantly we know the car belongs to 'The Shocker' Dave Storm. He cadillac pulls up to a handicapped parking spot and out bolts Dave, seemingly in a pissed off mood. He opens the back and throws his black gym bag out of the back and closes the doors. He heads to the enterance of the building. When he gets in it is revealed that the building is a gym. He walks past the front desk and heads to a large room with two couches and a tv, almost like a dressing room. Inside is Kevin Lander, Dave's personnal trainer.) Kevin Lander:: 'Hey, what's up?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Not much, how you doin?' Kevin Lander:: 'Good.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Alright, let's get started, i gotta go soon.' (The dou headed out of the room to the weightroom-machine room. The two men are riding on the treadmill when the cameras pick them back up.) Dave Storm:: 'So, I got my first match in UWF.' Kevin Lander:: 'Awesome!When and against who?' Dave Storm:: 'Wednesday night on Wednesday Night Underground, and it's a battle royal for newcomers. I'm one of ten vying for the number one contendership to the UWF Television Title.' Kevin:: 'Nice. Good luck.' Dave Storm:: 'Cool. I better win this thing. I gotta show everyone at UWF who the hell I am, and why they should all fear me.' Kevin Lander:: 'Why should they fear you?' Dave Storm:: 'You'll see. Everyone will this Wednesday night.' Kevin Lander:: 'Oh..can I find out now?' Dave Storm:: 'Well to do that, I'd have to kick your ass.' Kevin Lander:: 'Why don't you just give me a quick verbal explanation?' Dave Storm:: 'It means I'm going to kick some major ass in the UWF and noone's gonna stop me. Not Moraless, not Corpral Punishment, not Jukei Kutabare.' Kevin Lander:: 'Jukei Kutabare?' Dave Storm:: 'He's one of the newer guys who's ass I'm going to kick in my first match. Him and Sacrafice, Green Demon and a few others will all be lying on the floor on at ringside when they crown me the TV title Number one contender.' Kevin Lander:: 'Ohh...Gotcha.' Dave Storm:: ' You see, Kevin, Although I have yet to step into the UWF squared circle, I know that I'm already a head and shoulders above the nine others I will defeat on Wednesday Night. Soon enough my name will be up there with the so-called greats of UWF. But I've already surpassed them all in my prior feats, which is why my main prize here is the Universal Wrestling Federation World Championship. That gold strap is the one thing im vying for, the one thing I'm in this for, and nothing and noone is EVER going to stop me.' Kevin Lander:: 'Well if you keep in shape your looking on the upside.' Dave Storm:: 'Of course I am. I already am. I am unstoppable. And they will all figure that out once I step into the ring on Wednesday Night Underground. I will prove myself to the entire UWF and make them all realize that I have arrived. I am excellence at its finest and that will be common knowledge soon enough. By Thursday everyone will know that the next Television champion's name is 'The Shocker' Dave Storm. Jay Vyper, who the hell is he?' Kevin Lander:: 'I hoped you'd know.' Dave Storm:: 'He's the Television Champion.' Kevin Lander:: 'Never heard of him.' Dave Storm:: 'Exactly. But once I beat his ass everyone will know his name. Like a great man once said, Kevin, 'I'm gonna make him famous'.' Kevin Lander:: 'Make who famous? I think I'm missing something here? Dave Storm:: 'Aghh, nevermind. I'll explain It to you later.' Kevin Lander:: 'Okay.' Dave Storm:: 'Alright, let's go lift weight's or something.' Kevin Lander:: ' Sure.' (With that the two men slow down their machines and take their bags to the weight room. As they do that the scene slowly fades.)
