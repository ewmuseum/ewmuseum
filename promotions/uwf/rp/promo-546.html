UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #546

Battles of a Royal Nature

14 Jul 2004

'The Renegade' Rich Rollins — WNU Arena Parking Lot

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The arena parking lot is mostly empty, with a few sparse clusters of rented vehicles here and there. Some of the wrestlers have already arrived this early in the afternoon, and the ring crew has just begun to set up shop somewhere inside the building. Hefting his athletic duffel bag over one shoulder, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins starts walking across the sticky black asphalt of the barren lot, his eyes fixed squarely on the arena building itself.] [From the back of a parked van a few rows over, a camera crew starts unpacking equipment onto a dolly for loading. Noticing The Renegade walking alone toward the building, one of the crewman snatches a hand-held camera unit and yells after him.] Cameraman: Hey Rollins, wait up! [The Renegade stops and turns, his expression two parts quizzical, one part annoyed. The cameraman jogs across the handful of empty parking rows to meet him, flipping the camera on and pointing it at Rollins.] Cameraman: Mind if I get an interview, Rollins? I remember you from Global Championship Wrestling back in the day, and was excited when I heard you joined the roster here in the UWF. Got a minute? Renegade: For you, I’ve got two. [Grinning widely, the cameraman sets the camera comfortably on his shoulder and shrugs.] Cameraman: So, do you feel prepared to return to the ring tonight after two years of retirement? [The Renegade frowns, shifting his duffel from one shoulder to the next, staring the camera dead-on. His voice solomn and intense, he responds, his words chosen carefuly and methodically.] Renegade: In this business, “retirement” is just a fancy word for “extended vacation.” These two hands … (he spreads them, emphasizing his point) … are more than eager to pick up their former profession. And tonight, nine other newcomers are going to repeat the same mistake that my opponents in the past made. Cameraman: And what mistake is that? Renegade: Showing up in the first damn place. [Letting the duffel bag fall to the asphalt, Rich Rollins focuses his stare into the camera lens, boreing holes into the cameraman’s head beyond like a white hot torch. Deadly serious, The Renegade continues.] Renegade: This is to Jukie Kutabare, The Green Demon, and Fosta: you can’t hold what you can’t catch, believe me on that. To Justin Morris, Nick Nitro, and Draco Paladine… you can’t catch what you can’t beat, and I’m talkin’ about yours truly. And to Sacrifice, Dave Storm and Fizbane the Fabulous… you can’t beat what you can’t earn. And while I’m around, there ain’t not way you’re gonna earn a shot at the Television title. I damn sure promise you that. Cameraman: And what about the Television Champion, Jay Vyper? [Bending over to snatch his duffel bag back up, The Renegade averts his gaze from the camera, breaking the intensity like a chissle on cracked ice. Turning to look back over at the arena, he shrugs.] Renegade: I’ll cross that bridge when I get to it. Whether it be Jay Vyper or Richard Ray Vaughn who looks at me from across the ring this Sunday, the outcome remains the same. (his attention snaps back to the camera) I’m going to win my first UWF championship within my first week of employment. And that’s not a promise… that’s a truth. Period. [Switching the camera off and slinging it off his shoulder, the cameraman smiles and reaches out to shake The Renegade’s hand. Rich Rollins stares at the offered hand, but doesn’t take it. His smile fading, the cameraman clears his throat and runs the hand through his shock of brown hair, taking a few steps back.] Cameraman: Well then… good luck, Rollins. Welcome to the UWF. Renegade: We’ll see what kind of a welcome I get. [With that, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins starts the long walk across the scorching sticky asphalt parking lot, leaving the cameraman to sweat by himself.] [From the heat of the sun or from Rollins’ attention, The Renegade can’t decide.] [Fade to black.] ~Fin~
