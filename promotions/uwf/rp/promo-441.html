UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #441

Moraless......piece of cake

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder lying on his couch resting from his long flight home. He is in deep sleep when there is a knock on the door that causes him to jump up. He springs to his feet wearing his black shorts and red Gangsta Nation t-shirt. He cautiously walks to the door and looks through the peephole. He relaxes after realizing who is at his door and opens it. Standing in the doorway, holding a camera, is the UWF cameraman. He is smiling as Ryder stares him up and down. ) Ryder: And just what the fuck do you want? Cameraman: Ain't it obvious? Ryder: Don't be a smartass. What the hell are you doin here? Cameraman: Here to shoot a promo. Ryder: I can see that dipshit, but what the hell do I have to promo for? Cameraman: You've got a match on Supremacy against Moraless. * Ryder throws his head back and starts laughing * Ryder: Moraless? That pissant got his panties in a bunch about that little beating I threw him huh? Wait a second, Supremacy? Did I fuckin sleep through Stable Wars or something? Cameraman: No, it's been pushed back two weeks....didn't you hear that? Ryder: Yea I heard it asshole, I just find it amusing to act like i'm out of the loop on shit. I swear the UWF doesn't hold any kind of scholastic standards in their hiring do they? Cameraman: Whatever. Ryder: Yea, whatever, just get your ass in here and turn on your stupid camera. * The man walks in and sets up his camera as Ryder prepares to shoot his latest promo for the upcoming match against Moraless * Ryder: So, Moraless got a little offended by the stompin I gave him huh? That's ok with me, because you see i've gotten to him and Digital now. Maybe once I hit the rest of you Ressurection bitches you'll understand that i'm not fuckin playin around when I say I want Malcom Kennedy in the ring. I don't care what I have to do to make the match happen, but you can bet your ass I will have my revenge for what happened in the Prime Alliance. Moraless, I had no beef with you before, I even thought you were an ok wrestler, but you stuck your nose in my business and you paid for it. You wanna settle this in the ring? I got no problems with that, because i'll just stomp you into dirt anyways. * Ryder gives the camera a cocky grin * Ryder: Moraless, you might be a tag champ or ptc champ or whatever the hell you are, but that don't impress or intimidate me, because I am a former World Champ. I've been to the top of the mountain, and though it might have been a short stay at the top I can still say I held the top spot. You might just make it there one day too, but I can guarantee you this boy, it'll never be by walkin over the Cherokee Thrilla. You will not use me as a stepping stone to fame, because i'm a stone that will rip your fuckin leg off and beat you to death with it. Your skills are weak and i've already found a way to pick you apart in the ring so you can bet your ass i'm ready for this match. Bring whatever you need to the ring because no matter who or what you got with you...it'll never be enough. You can't stop me Moraless, and at Supremacy i'm gonna prove that fact to you. See you soon. * Ryder winks at the camera and the cameraman stops recording. He gets up to shake Ryder's hand, but Ryder just tells him * Ryder: Get the hell outta here. ( The man scurries out the front door nearly tripping on the steps of Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. Ryder just stands in the doorway laughing as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
