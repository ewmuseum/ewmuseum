UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #184

The Cursed Roleplay

21 Jan 2004

Mike Montgomery — The Halifax Waterfront

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For a change of pace, our promo opens along the Halifax Waterfront. We see Mike Montgomery, Executive VP of Talent Relationships and a man who doesn't want to be a full time wrestler anymore, yet keeps getting signed. Mike walks along the waterfront, his path lit by street lamps during this starless night. The cords from earphones can be seen extending from his ears deep into his jacket. The music plays loud, Fuel's 'Won't Back Down.' He is a man clearly lost in his own thoughts. Yeah! Yeah! You know, I'd always thought our esteemed comissioner was a smart man. I always figured him for someone who understood how the game was played. I must say it really was quite the humbling experience, being shown just how wrong I was. I know what darkness means (and the void you learned from me) The isolation steams (So I think it wants to bleed) The echoes in my brain (All the things you said to me) You took my everything Now I'm coming for you! I watched your last promo Jamie, and that was when it hit me. You're no smart man, no brilliant strategist. You're a child, Jamie. A spoiled, self-centered, little brat who can't stand the simple fact that he has to share the sandbox. I'll give you credit though. Credit because you continue to amaze me. It amazes me that you have the nerve, the balls...(well in your case 'ball') to call yourself the most successful UWF wrestler ever. No sane person in the business today would put themselves ahead of a Hamish McGrubber, a Curt Raines or a Lew Spectre. I certainly wouldn't. I would be the first to admit that any one of them could take me out in less than a second. But then of course you're no sane man. Wait...now that I think about it, you're not even a man. I won't back down I will not bow I've come to bring you hell I can’t forget Things you did I've come to bring you hell You continue to amaze me Jamie, simply because you actually believe that have something to do with the UWF's success. You forget Jamie. You forget that Lew Spectre had already built the UWF when you took on your 'job' as commissioner. You were a cog in Lew's machine, but then you got greedy. You made the mistake of removing Lew. The cog believed itself to be better than the machine. A cog without a machine, much like a token commissioner, is useless. The shadows that you see (In the places that you sleep) Are memories of me (Better pray your soul to keep) The truth behind your eyes (You know the thing you never see) Your darkest little lies I'm coming for you! You want credit for other people's work, which no one would consider acceptable. What exactly have you done to merit all the praise that you think you deserve? What superstars have you brought in? You gave us the lottery. A failed one time gimmick that just didn't work. Who should be impressed by that? Can you claim to have brought Jay Phoenix back? Were you the one who surprised the entire wrestling world with the return of the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea? Did you bring in new fans who wanted to see what would happen? No. You have never done your job. A smile slowly creeps across Mike's face as the next thought enters his mind... I won't back down I will not bow I've come to bring you hell I can’t forget Things you did I've come to bring you hell Was that really disgust you felt when Spiff made his return? Are you sure it wasn't perhaps...fear? Shock even? Did the sight of a monkey in the same ring as you send a shiver down your spine when some small part of your subconscious mind asked if you were about to lose the other raisin you call a testicle? Or is it perhaps that you're jealous? Jealous that thanks to my seizing the unique opportunity of having a super-intelligent monkey on my side that I have more power and wealth than you could ever dream of? Why am I still breathing? Why am I still a part of this business? Shall I educate you, dear James? Shall I provide you insight with how the wrestling world truly works? You see, it's about the people. The people decide who stays and who goes, who rises to the top of the card, and who never sees TV time. I am here because the people decided that they want me here. So long as the people want me here, I will stay here. There isn't a damned thing you can do about it. Not only have you truly done nothing with your power, your alliance, but you even claim to have done things that clearly never happened! Eliminated the Bunnies? Oh no my dear James. They're still alive and hopping. You and your boy Ruff Ryder did what you have always done. Absolutely nothing. But I will say this for you. You are indeed royalty. Useless, lacking in actual authority, and kept around strictly for show. Yes, I should think that 'royalty' does indeed describe you. But before you lecture anyone about professionalism, don't you think it would be a wise idea to actually acquire some? Hiding behind crowbars and incompetent thugs, yet you believe yourself to be professional? The shadows that you see (In the places that you sleep) Are memories of me (Better pray your soul to keep) The truth behind your eyes (You know the thing you never see) Your darkest little lies I'm coming for you! It is indeed an honor to step into the ring with you, though. An honor because I am given the opportunity to further my own career, at your expense. You see, the Player's Club is about seizing opportunity. This Sunday I am afforded a tremendous opportunity. One that I most assuredly will capitalize on. All the scars that never heal All the wounds that will not seal I will not forget the day These memories never fall I won't back down I will not bow I've come to bring you hell I can’t forget Things you did I've come to bring you hell Perhaps you'll need to educate me as well. Should I be scared of your crowbar? Should I feel threatened? If you seek to threaten me then I'm afraid you'll have to do better than that. I've survived the...ministrations...of Corporal Punishment. If you think a crowbar can compare to the very thought of having a live cattleprod shoved up your ass, then you are sadly mistaken. I still wake up at night in a cold sweat thanks to that experience. You don't intimidate me that way Jamie. What you will do though, is experience something you've been heading toward for a long, long time. Your fall from grace. I've come to bring you hell I've come to bring you hell I've come to bring you hell I've come to bring you hell...
