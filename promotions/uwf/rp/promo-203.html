UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #203

Super Bowl Blitz

2 Feb 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival and Raleigh, N.C.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 31st, 2004. 3 p.m. We open upon Beelzebozo, Pyretta Blaze, and the Quiet One standing just outside the gates that lead to Hell’s Carnival. The two men are sorting through a duffle bag as Pyretta stands nearby, shaking her head. Beelzebozo: “All right, let’s see here…bats, bricks, barbed wire…and the beer’s in the cooler, right?” The mime nods. “Great, let’s go then.” Blaze: “I’m sorry, but can you explain this plan to me one more time? It’s still not making sense to me.” Beelzebozo: “It’s simple. We’re going to North Carolina for the Super Bowl.” Blaze: “But it’s in Texas.” Beelzebozo: “Yes, but neither team’s from Texas. Carolina is.” Blaze: “OK, and we’re picking this instead of New England why?” Beelzebozo: “Because if Carolina actually wins the game, there shall be rioting in the streets. And if they lose, there shall still be rioting in the streets.” Blaze: “You don’t know that.” Beelzebozo: “It’s part of American sports, dear. The cities that host the teams in any final playoff are required to have postgame riots.” Blaze: “That happens in other countries, not just America. I mean, what about soccer riots?” Beelzebozo: “Ah, but theirs happen in the actual stadium, often during the game. We respect our arenas enough to wait until we leave before we break stuff.’ Blaze: “Ah. OK then.” Beelzebozo: “So, are we ready to go?” Blaze: “I guess. This just seems like a weird way to spend a weekend. So where in Carolina are we going?” Beelzebozo: “Raleigh. Big city, bigger riot.” And with that, the three of them walk offscreen. We fade to black. Feb. 1, 2004 10 p.m. Our three intrepid psychos are standing under an awning in heavy winter coats and gloves, as freezing drizzle falls around them. Blaze looks pissed. Blaze: “Good call o fearless leader.” Beelzebozo: “SHUT. UP.” Blaze: “No one riots in winter storm conditions, you know.” Beelzebozo: “I know that NOW. Who the hell knew the weather’d turn like this? I thought we got all our winter storm crap out of the way last week!” Blaze: “Another fine mess you’ve gotten us into.” Beelzebozo: “You know, this doesn’t have to put a damper on our night out.” Blaze: “The hell with that. Look, I’m going back to the hotel. You and the mime can loot and pillage all you want; I need a hot bath.” And with that, she leaves. Beelzebozo: “Her loss. Come on, Quiet One. Let’s riot!” With that, the clown drops the duffle bag, lifts a brick out of it, and tosses it through the window behind him. Feb. 1, 2004 11:19 p.m. The two men are sitting dejectedly on the curb of a street. Broken glass and strewn trash lies around them. Several alarms are heard going off. The drizzle has turned into snow. Beelzebozo: “I can’t believe the cops haven’t shown up. No one else came out to help us riot, or to even see what was going on. This sucks.” The mime nods. “Well, to hell with this worthless city. I’ve pillaged in better dumps than this. Let’s go, mime.” As the two men stand, an obviously inebriated man stumbles by. Drunk: “Whas you guysh doin’?” Beelzebozo: “Waiting for hypothermia to set in, numbnuts. What’s it look like we’re doing!?” The drunk man looks around for a moment. Drunk: “Looksh lick a riot.” Beelzebozo: “Well, that’s what it was SUPPOSED to be. A Super Bowl riot. But apparently it was called on account of weather.” Drunk: “I wanna riot!” Beelzebozo: “For real, man??” The drunkard nods vigorously. “Sweet ass! Well, grab a weapon and go to town!” The drunk man leans down to pick up a brick, nearly toppling over in the process. He manages to heft the brick, leans back, and then promptly drops it on the mime’s left foot. The mime, to his credit, manages to hop around in completely mute agony. Drunk: “PILLAGE!!! WHOOOOO!!!” Beelzebozo: “You stupid drunken bastard, you crippled my mime!” Drunk: “That’sh the dangersh of riting!” He begins puking uncontrollably. Beelzebozo: “Oh, to hell with this.” He picks up a baseball bat and, as the drunk regains his composure, cracks the man upside the head. The drunk flies three feet back and lands in a heap in the gutter. The clown begins to yell at the unconscious man, punctuating his speech with blows from the bat. “We let you join our riot CRACK and this is how WHACK you repay us!? THUD By maiming my mime!? CRUNCH Here’s your goddamn pillage bitch!!” Eventually, the clown stops, leaving the bleeding man lying in a broken mess. The Quiet One hobbles back onto the scene. “You OK?” The mime gingerly nods. “Good. Let’s get out of here. This is the worst riot ever.” The two men depart the scene. Feb. 2, 2004 1:09 a.m. The three are lounging in a posh-looking hotel room. Blaze rubs Beelzebozo’s shoulders. Blaze: “Well, I’m sorry the riot didn’t pan out the way you intended, dear.” Beelzebozo: “Meh. I’ll live I guess.” Blaze: “Cheer up! Look at this great hotel we found! At least we can enjoy the rest of our night!” Beelzebozo: “I guess. Still…my first riot, and it went right into the crapper.” Blaze: “Honey, there will be other riots.” Beelzebozo: “I guess you’re right. Still, I really wanted this to go as planned, and I’m still steamed it did not. So, I’m going to be taking it out on my next opponent.” He looks into the camera. “That’s right, Malcolm X, I’m coming for you you bastard.” Blaze: “Kennedy’s been dropped. He’ll get a shot in the future, but he couldn’t make it to the show this week, so they’ve got a replacement.” Beelzebozo: “Replacement?! What the hell! I should be getting a win by forfeit! If Kennedy’s so scared of another beating at the hands of the World Champion, then he shouldn’t have tried for a shot at me in the first place!” Blaze: “To be fair, he thought he was getting a shot at Seymour at the time.” Beelzebozo: “Well, that IS true. I mean, you think you’ve got some goofy video game nut as your opponent, and suddenly it turns out you’re facing the Clown from Hell. I guess I could understand his feet suddenly getting so cold. Fair enough. Who’s this replacement then?” Blaze: “The other guy with the title shot. Ruff Ryder.” Beelzebozo: “Really now?...Hmmm…I might actually enjoy this. Ever since he kicked my mime’s ass I’ve been dying to get my hands on him. So now I have the chance, sooner than expected? And at a time that I’m filled with rage at my abortion of a riot? Excellent.” He rubs his hands together. “Mr. Ryder…you’re in for the ride of your life. A ride straight to Hell, courtesy of its most dangerous resident. Me. You think you have what it takes to beat me, just because you won some goofy-ass tournament? Well, think about this—if I’d been in that tournament, as I should have been, you never would have gotten your chance at this belt. So get ready for some massive pain and suffering, and then…as you’re lying on the mat, staring at the rafters, you’ll see me standing over you, ready to pin you and send you to the Home for Infinite Losers. “Sleep tight, Ryder…you’re going to be my first victim as World Champion of the UWF. Because no matter how good you think you are…no matter how hard you try… Beelzebozo will have the Last Laugh.” And with that, we fade to black.
