UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #673

Tonights the night

5 Oct 2004

Adien Pryde — SAS Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens outside the arena for tonight Sunday Night Supremacy. The crowds have gathered hours before the show to catch a glimpse of the UWF superstars as they enter the building. The crowd cheer as wrestlers like Tsunami Oni and Nick Persia enter and boo as Seymour Almasy and The Formorians push their way through the crowds. As a black Navigator pulls up, everyone have a mixed reaction because it is time for some Chaos. Out of the passenger door steps Adien Pryde dressed in jeans and a t-shirt in Chaos Society colors of black and red. Behind him climbing out of the back seat is Joanna in her black biker shorts and red Anarchy sweat shirt. Reo Dai is the last to climb out of the driver seat making sure the stage help knows to not scratch the SUV when he goes to park it.] Fan : Pryde, Any comments about tonight’s match? Pryde : I have already said my piece about Oni but that piece of shit Plague. You think that just because I am new to the UWF that I don’t have experience? You have another thing coming. I have been wrestling for 9 years all over this world. I have been in 4 feds in the USA and 1 in Japan. I have held Tag–Team Titles with 2 different men and have also held 3 different singles titles. But do you know the difference between you and me. I am loyal to the one federation that I am currently wrestling in where as you break contracts and go where the money is. Why you UWF fans continue to cheer him after what he has done to them is beyond me. Fan : He beat Jamie Shock to become the final entry in The Rumble. Are you intimidated at all by that feat? Pryde : Hell no, the only reason he won was because Ruff Ryder is to stupid to know when he is helping his stable mate and hurting him and gave Plague the win by DQ. How he can think that a win that way is worthy of praise is beyond me. As far as final entry to the Regal Rumble, you came in last and still didn’t win where our opponent Oni cam in at 18 and won the whole thing. I survived 20 entrants and had a THC match earlier in the night. As far as I am concerned, I made a bigger impact at the PPV than you did. Get off of your high horse Plague and come back to reality. You were a great THC champion, but the key work “were”. Go back to Japan where your name means something because here all you are is a has bin. Fan : Dai, any comment on the rumored lawsuit against you from Snap Meyer? Dai : He has no chance at winning, it is all in the video footage, and you can clearly see that he walked into my foot that just happened to be there. He can blame me all he wants but he just needs to be more careful and look where he is going. Fan : Joanna, is it true that Anarchy will be going after the Tag belts. Joanna : Given time, our first concern is to see Pryde go as far as he can go with this singles run. When he is satisfied that he has gone as far as he can go at this time we will set our sights at the Tag Team gold. Till then Dai is accepting singles matches to work off what little ring rust he may have. No further questions, we have a match to prepare for. [With that, Anarchy turn and walk in through the back door to the arena and the camera fades to black.]
