“The winner and the moving onto the next round John Edwards” the announcer cried as John Edwards, through his arms into the air with triumph. His earlier doubts and fears had proved to not have a solid foundation, as he had somehow managed to succeed over the former Primetime Central Global Champion and a man called A-Poc, whom he had greatly underestimated. In the moment’s exhilaration, he had forgotten the main reason that he had won. However the truth soon dawned upon him, the second he began to walk up the entrance ramp, just a simple glance down at his forearms were a tell tale sign, there was no-way that simple adrenaline would cause veins to bulge so wildly out of skin. The drugs were still in his system, his win was tainted. John’s head dropped slightly as he took the final few steps up the entrance ramp and through the curtain. At least backstage he would have some time to think, contemplate his next move, a bit of solitude and all would be well. “Congratulations sir, now if you would just like to accompany me for a few moments and we’ll let you get some rest before the next match” The voice emanated from a man wearing a white coat, although he was smiling as he spoke the words the simple image of a man in white, scared John. Men in white coats usually meant one of two things. A high school student messing around in the science lab or a doctor and this didn’t look like a student. “Can I ask why I have to follow you” John enquired, the pitch of his voice rising rapidly as he spoke, the nerves nearly causing him to physically shake. John suddenly realised about his arms, the veins practically exploding out of the skin. Quickly he moved them behind his back, or at least as best he could. He was not prepared to lose this victory on a technicality. Not because of that bastard Kyle Stone who had drugged him before the last bout. “Its just procedure sir, nothing to be worried about. Everyone that advances in the tournament just has to give us a simple urine sample to make sure that no foul play is afoot.” The doctor said extending his arm and indicating the way. “If you’ll follow me please” Reluctantly John nodded and began to follow the doctor, his head hung low and dragging his feet a long the floor. A lot like a small child who had just been told that he couldn’t have desert. John knew what was coming, without a shadow of a doubt he was going to be eliminated from the competition, that was only the start though. His whole career would be bought into question. The effects of this were going to be long lasting and have a chain effect. Four years worth of accomplishments could effectively be wiped out, simply by one spiked meal. “Ok here we are” The doctor said his voice breaking John’s train of thought, who turned and paid close attention to him. “If you can just provide us a sample in this, then we will get back to you in about an hour. Absolutely nothing to worry about. Then you will be facing Ryutaro Jiro in the second round in roughly two hours.” The doctor said was handing over a small plastic container, obviously intended for the urine sample. John turned and entered the door that the doctor had opened, it was just a small cube really, a bench occupying the room and nothing else. Sitting down on the cold wooden bench John, undid his fly and held the container between his legs waiting for the inevitable. Jiro was someone quite like A-Poc in the respect that John didn’t really know who he was. He knew that he was involved with PRIME another federation which featured under the PrimeTime Central umbrella but other than that Jiro may as well be a blank slate to him. The fact of the matter was though that Jiro, like John had just beaten two other contestants. That in itself was enough to set off the warning signs in John’s head, not that it mattered really. As soon as the small pot of urine he was now holding in his hands was tested. His life would be over. Carefully putting the lid on the container, making sure not to spill any, John proceeded to do up his fly and leave the room, passing the container full of yellow fluid to the doctor, who took it and placed it in a clear envelope before sealing it. “How long did you say I had before my match?” Asked John as he began to walk away from the doctor heading towards the exit of the arena. “Two hours, but don’t you want to use the time to get a rub down or something relax?” Questioned the doctor; obviously confused at the course of action that John was about to take in leaving the arena. “No, there is someone I have to see, I need to sort something out.” Spoke John, the determination and anger in his voice was apparent causing the doctor take a cautionary step backwards. John took two small steps before dashing forwards, slamming his way through the arena door and into the night of Columbus. Two Hours Before The Second Round Have you ever run so fast that it felt like your legs were going to fall off? That’s how fast John was running. He was feeling physically and mentally drained after a long gruelling battle with A-Poc and Jesse Jamester. It had not been easy in the slightest, if truth were told he should have lost, but that was something he could not admit to. The drug test would sort that one out, there was only one option left to him. He had to find the rat named Kyle Stone and get him to confess, something, which could save his career, but would by no means be an easy task. Reaching inside of his black leather jackets pocket, John pulled out Kyle Stones card which he had received after the meal, an address was firmly etched around the gold leaf writing, without a doubt Kyle was a show off, but worse than that he was manipulative. He walked around with airs and graces, talking you up to the world, yet when it suited him he could cut you down. John despised men like this his whole life it was people like Kyle that had made John despise mankind. The type of person who John would openly walk up to in the street and hit, just for bringing society down to a much lower level. People like that weren’t men. They were parasites. Glancing over the business card John found what he was looking for, an address for Stone. For all John knew it could be a fake, even if it was legitimate the chances were that he would have flown the coup a long time ago, knowing what he did to John, the pain and suffering that he had or at least would cause him. Even still he had to try. The address it self wasn’t too far from the Captain’s Landing restaurant where the two had met earlier in the day, he had passed it on the taxi journey to the arena and was able to trace back the journey as he ran toward his destination, people around him glanced at the crazy man running through their streets. The anonymity was quite a nice feeling for John, anyone who was a fan of wrestling had already got tickets to the arena, to anyone else he would simply be seen as a man out on an evening run, just another face in the crowd. It was handy really because once the results of his urine test came through, that’s all he would ever be, just another man. One Hour and Forty-Five Minutes Before The Second Round John now stood outside of his target. Kyle stones office was on the second floor and sure enough there was a light on. Emotions began to flood through him, he had not thought of what the outcome would be once he got there. It was simple rage and revenge that had pushed him forwards, ignoring the physical pain that he was enduring, now though the pain was all too real. He had been battered and bruised in the battle just a few minutes before in a gruelling match up, he had just run a fair distance for fifteen minutes and now he was going to confront the man that in theory could have ruined his life. John took a few more steps towards the door, if he outstretched his hand he could have turned the doorknob and entered, but he didn’t. Something was holding him back. Through the rage and anger, he hadn’t actually thought about what he was going to do. He knew that he needed a confession out of him that would allow him the biggest chance of progression. If John could prove that he had no idea about the drugging, then the panel may take pity on him. His heart wanted to walk in there and knock him out though. His emotions were torn and he didn’t know what to do. Reaching out he clasped the doorknob in his hands and turned it slowly, hearing it click open as he did so. John’s heart was pounding as he stepped into the darkened foyer, a staircase directly in front of him as he stepped forwards and began to climb it. With each step his breath grew quicker, his pulse rose rapidly until it felt as though his heart was going to burst through his chest. This continued until he came across a door with the gold plaque across it the name. Kyle Stone engraved within it. John’s eyes fell into a glare as he saw the name, the anger he had felt suddenly became magnified as he slammed open the door and stormed into the room, finding Kyle Stone sat silently across the room behind a large desk, looking over some papers. Upon hearing the smash of the door Kyle looked up slowly and calmly, glanced at John for a split second before returning to his paperwork. “Ah I was expecting you John, I hear the match went well. Congratulations” The words flowed from Stone’s mouth smoothly with him showing absolutely no remorse for what he had done. John clenched his fist attempting to control his rage, but to no avail. “You bastard!” John lurched forwards and swung for Stone connecting with a swinging right to his jaw. John stood, fists clenched breathing deeply through gritted teeth as Stone simply rubbed his jaw, which was already beginning to glow red. “Is that anyway to thank the person that helped you beat A-Poc and Jesse Jamester?” asked Stone, John simply glared at Stone, he was a slimy human being and there was no-way he would let him talk to him like that. John lurched forwards again, this time however he was restrained from behind. John glanced over his shoulder only to be met by a large fist flying towards his face. The second fist flew into John’s stomach, causing him to fold over like an accordion as the hands released him and let him fall to the floor. Stone stepped out from behind the desk and stood over John, who was clutching his stomach, curled up in a near foetal position on the floor. “John, I never did introduce you to Charlie Temple did I? He’s, how do I best describe him. He’s someone that I pay to hurt people that annoy me. He’s the one standing behind you right now, the one that just hit you.” Spoke Kyle as he paced up and down in front of John. John turned his head around to indeed see a tall, broad shouldered man behind him with arms the size of tree trunks. Strangely Charlie simply waved at John before glancing around the room. “Charlie never was one for the thinking, he is one of the nicest boys you will ever meet, and he’s just not all that bright.” Stone said stopping his pacing to sit on the edge of his desk looking down at John, always paying attention to be a level above him. Always keeping the power. John pulled himself up to a kneeling position before rising to his feet, Charlie stepped forwards fists clenched ready to take out John but Stone simply shook his head allowing John to raise to his feet. “Why did you do it Kyle? Why did you drug me?” Asked John, the urge to leap forwards and throttle him was almost overbearing, he knew in his physical state though he would be no match for the simpleton goliath behind him. Kyle stroked his chin slightly before staring towards the ceiling. “To help you John, you may not believe this but I know you. Everything about you is printed upon your body. The way you walk, your head hung low. The way you didn’t look me in the eyes at dinner. You doubted yourself, which was to be your downfall. I helped you to overcome this.” Kyle said with no change in tone, he firmly believed what he was saying. In his twisted little mind Stone actually thought that by drugging John he was helping him. “No!” Screamed John, getting right in Stone’s face “You didn’t help me overcome it. I did that months ago, you simply poisoned me and not only put my match future in jeopardy, but also my past. Don’t you understand that there will be stories about this? How all of my years as a wrestler were a sham as I was using performance enhancing drugs? That without them I would be nothing? No. You were just thinking about lining your pocket you had not care for me. You didn’t give two shits about what happened to me. So now you are going to come with me and explain how all of this is your fault.” John spat out a few shards of spittle sprayed over Kyle as John glanced over his shoulder at Charlie. “I don’t care I have to go through that to get you there either. Kyle sat silent for a moment before glancing down at his watch. Looking up over the shoulder of John he simply nodded at Charlie who grinned and began to bound towards John. John span around and swung for Charlie who simply caught his arm mid-swing and bought his fist down across the top of John’s head. The room began to spin for John as he tried to remain on his feet. He felt flustered as his eyesight turned and faded to a grey, before falling to the floor. As John fell Charlie knelt down and caught him, not allowing him to hit his head on the floor. Kyle nodded towards Charlie and the two of them left. Charlie cradling John like an infant, leaving the building entering the night of Columbus once more the warm air whipping around them. In front of the building, a large black estate car was parked, obviously belonging to Kyle Stone. “Kyle, why couldn’t we just tell him the truth?” Asked Charlie as he waited for Stone to unlock the car in front of them. “He wouldn’t understand not yet. I hope you didn’t hit him too hard, he’s got another match tonight.” Replied Kyle as he unlocked the door and opened the back door. Allowing Charlie to carefully place John in the back, fastening his seat belt as he did so. “Yeah he’ll be awake soon. I barely stunned him. Lets get him back to the arena” replied Charlie as he entered the car and it drove off into the night, headed towards the second round matches of the tournament. Twenty Minutes Before The Second Round “Wake up John” the words, echoed through John’s head as he roused from his fist induced slumber. Slowly he opened his eyes and sat up. His vision was blurry and his head was pounding as he finally came too he saw Kyle Stone and Charlie Temple sat opposite him in the room. Immediately John stood up and tried to run, however as he rose he stumbled forwards, prompting Charlie to dash forwards and catch him once more, before placing him back on the bed slowly. “Take it easy John, you’ve only just recovered. Seems Charlie hit you a bit harder than he meant to” Kyle spoke before shooting a glance at Charlie, who shrugged and looked apologetic. John considered making a move again but his head was still buzzing too much to bother. Instead he simply sat there glaring across at the two of them. “John I know that you aren’t happy and that we can’t really explain our actions but I hope this may make things easier” Kyle said as he stood up and leaned forwards handing John a slip of paper. “We will leave you to get some rest now. Kyle and Charlie stood up and left the room, leaving John sat alone. A headache the size of Manhattan throbbing through his skull. He took the piece of paper in his hands and stared intently at it, his eyesight still not one hundred percent yet. This is to confirm that John Edwards complied with the rules of the wrestle god tournament and passed all necessary tests to compete in the second round. John sat in a state of shock; he didn’t understand what had just happened. It didn’t make sense. A knock at the door made John jump slightly as he stood up and shakily walked towards the door. As he opened the door, it revealed a small crewmember at the arena. “Mr Edwards are you ready? You are scheduled to face Ryutaro Jiro in five minutes.” Spoke the man. John nodded and walked out of the door following the crewmember. In all of the excitement, John had nearly forgotten that he had another match tonight. Ryutaro Jiro a man whom he knew little about. As he neared the arena he could hear the fans once more, hyped at what was about to happen. The weak links had been eliminated earlier on in the evening; all that was left was those who wanted to win the most. At the end of the evening only sixteen people would remain. “You ready to go John?” the sound guy who had dealt with him in the first round enquired as John reached the top of the entrance ramp, just behind the black curtain. John turned and nodded as ‘Somewhere I Belong’ once again blasted through the arena sending the crowd into frenzy as he stepped out through the curtain and walked down the entrance ramp. In the back of his mind he couldn’t help but wonder how he had passed the drugs test. He couldn’t begin to comprehend what had happened in the past twenty-four hours. That didn’t matter though as the blood pumped around his body once more, his body ached but he locked it out, because at the end of the evening, only sixteen people would remain. John Edwards would be one of them.
