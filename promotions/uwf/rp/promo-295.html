UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #295

Let's do this

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder sitting behind a desk with the Prime Alliance flag hanging behind him. He is sporting a black and red Kansas City Chiefs hat turned backwards with a black Prime Alliance t-shirt. He has his usual cocky grin showing as the camera pans in closer to record his promo. ) Ryder: Well well here we are. The Pay Per View is upon us and yet something is missing from the Main Event. Let's see now, what could that possibly be? I mean you have Beezlebozo, the UWF World Champion and you have the number one contender in Joshua Dunn so the match should break records all over the world. It won't do that though, because the damn ticketseller is thrown into a fuckin match with Iron motherfuckin Lung! Now i'm not tryin to run the little prick down, but what the hell? I'm a former World Champion and I am a member of the Prime Alliance. I deserve better than this shit. This guy is mid-card at best. This is all just a bunch of bullshit and if i'm not in a World Title match as soon as this Pay Per View is over I will kill the World Champion, whoever he is, and the number 1..2..and 3 contenders. I'm tired of this place playing keep away with that damn title. Either give me the fuckin shot I deserve or come right out and tell me that it won't happen so that I can stop tryin to win the son of a bitch. I realize that this place doesn't wanna sell tickets and make money, which is why they do whatever is possible to keep myself and Malcolm Kennedy behind the curtains. This is getting ridiculous, and i'm not gonna put up with this crap any longer. * Ryder rolls his eyes at having to mention his opponent for the Pay Per View * Ryder: Now as for the so called future star of the UWF...the up and comer of the week Iron Lung. Look Lungie, I got no problems with you proving your worth and showing everyone that you've been held down for way too long, but do it on someone else's time, because quite frankly I don't have the patience to deal with you. You aren't on my level and it's very obvious. You couldn't beat me on my worst day so why not just cancel the match and let me get on with what I must do...capture the World Title and bring it to the Prime Alliance camp. You can be the future of the UWF all you want, but we are the present. Our time is now, and in case you were in a coma for a long time you'd realize that the real future of the UWF is the One Man Army Malcolm Kennedy. He is the man that will take this piece of trash that we call a federation and make it into something respectable. Without Malcolm Kennedy and Ruff Ryder this place is a shithole. I mean you have a fuckin stupid clown as the World Champion, what kind of stupid shit is that? No wonder people laugh at us and write us off as a non threat. Well you can bet your ass that the Prime Alliance is gonna restore the UWF to greatness, no matter how much I hate this shitbox. So in closing Lungie...be ready for a long day, because at the Pay Per View...I OWN YOUR ASS! ( Ryder laughs into the camera as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
