UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #433

Time is TICKING...CORPORAL STYLE

30 May 2004

Corporal Punishment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Life in the fast lane is never a winning ingredient in life...unless you are driving a tank, then you can pretty much do what ever you want. And that is where the Corporal comes in. You can't stop him, you can't even slow him down. He just looks at the one he wants to destroy and then he simply destroys the poor soul. It does not matter if you retaliate, or pursue him. Because the Corporal just analyzes his opponent in a way that you puny average people can not understand. His mind is far too sinister for the weak at heart to fathom. And when it comes to retribution, there is no one else on this Earth that can administer punishment and despair like Corporal Punishment. In the last week, the Corporal has knocked out two elderly people, verbally assaulted several wrestlers in the UWF, and made a treaty with two men for Stable Wars. And yet, there is not one man big enough to step up and confront the Corporal eye to eye. What a sad day it is for wrestling. Are all the real men gone? Is the Corporal's presence that horrific to those who are in this federation? Does this mean that the champions in this federation are just going to place their tails in-between their legs when the Corporal calls them out? Who knows. The Corporal loves this type of attention lackfulness. This means that MARTIAL LAW has already begun. But none the less, the Corporal will show his power and sheer sadistic-ness with his first opponent. The PLAQUE WILL BE DESTROYED. End of discussion. There will not be a simple one... two... three count. There will be a submission and a brutal beating afterwards. And the reason there will be a beating, is because... the Corporal CAN! End of discussion. Ruff Ryder, a walking porno device remains speechless, and so does the lovebug. I would have used capital letters with his name but he does not even deserve that. The opponent Plaque already knows his fate, so we end that story before it begins. But never fear my friends of pain and misfortune. The Corporal is about to speak and I am sure you all are waiting to hear from him. So throw anyone who can not hear the word PENIS out of the room. Lock the doors and cover the windows. Because the CORPORAL has now entered the building...) ***(The scene opens up with the Corporal entering the structure where he will face the wrestler Plague this week. The smell of sweat is already present as the workers do their best to make the facility presentable for the local camera crews and visitors, who will travel several hundred miles to witness the return of Corporal Punishment. ***Already there are Punish-ites gathering at the arena. These devout Corporal fans come days in advance, just to get a glimpse of the Corporal. These fan's are unlike the average wrestling enthusiast. The Corporal fans are conditioned to be soldiers for the Corporals cause. They crave for the opportunity to defend his name. Several brawls break out in the average Corporal bout. This is because while the Corporal verbally assaults his victim, the Punish-ites verbally assault those that cheer for the Corporal's opponent. As you can see, this makes the arena dangerous for any person who enters a wrestling match where the Corporal is on the card. ***The Corporal walks up to the squared circle, and with his right arm brushing against the canvass, he begins to walk around the outside of the ring. Corporal: It is almost time. Corporal: In a few days, this ring will be filled with the blood of my latest victim... Corporal: The crowd will chant his name, and my loyal FANS will create total mayhem in the stands... Corporal: Then the crowd will lower their voices as they watch my opponent drop to his knees in exhaustion... Corporal: The Cobra Clutch will be applied, and the opponent will go to sleep. ***The Corporal stops, and turns away from the ring and toward the seats where the audience will be seated. Corporal: AND THEN THE FUN WILL BEGIN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Corporal: I WILL DEFEAT THE PLAQUE! HE WILL FALL TO ME!! AND HE WILL RECEIVE THE LETHAL INJECTION IN A WAY THAT WILL EITHER CAUSE HIM TO RETIRE OR IT WILL CAUSE A MEMBER OF HIS FAMILY TO DIE!! Corporal: THAT IS WHAT WILL TRANSPIRE! WHY? ...BECAUSE I CAN!!!! ***A few fans of the Corporal can be heard in the background chanting his name as he turns back towards the ring and begins walking around it in the same manner as he started. Corporal: It is a pity the man who I will be facing, has run to the depths of his cowardly existence. Instead of attempting to fight and defend himself, he chooses to run and hide. It is a typical story that happens all to often in my career. My opponent talks a big talk before the match is signed, and then after the green light is pushed, they run off in fear like a deer about to be spot lighted. It is a shame, really it is. But what can you do? The wrestlers claim that they are important. They say that they are the greatest thing since barbed wire and dental floss. But when it comes to earning their keep, they do this to me. Corporal: Oh well. That is what makes my job so easy. I just go in the ring and physically abuse my opponent. And trust me when I say that the Plaque is going to be abused. His blood will fill this ring and I will wipe it up on a handkerchief and place it in my trophy room... Corporal: He will meet Jesus at my hands, and I will send him to either the hospital or the morgue. Whichever is closer. And then the UWF will at least start to ponder the future. For if there are no real warriors in this federation, think of how Stable Wars will be at my hands. ***Corporal Punishment begins to chuckle at his comments. Corporal: WHO CAN STOP ME AND MY BAND OF MORTAL PIRATES! THE DAYS OF LOCKDOWN HAVE BEGUN AND IT FEELS GOOD TO BE ME!!! Corporal: ... Corporal: ... Corporal: ... Corporal: And bad to be PLAGUE... CORPORAL STYLE!
