UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #221

Back On The Road To Glory

13 Feb 2004

Kenny Mercury — UWF TOWERS, CHICAGO, IL

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Begin Transmission.] [It was just your normal sunny afternoon at the UWF Chicago Towers. The sun blazed brightly in the sky, poking its brilliant rays through the fluffy white clouds. Too bad it was negative 30 degrees out…] [Inside, there strolled a man. A man of business… A man of power… A man… of confidence and great talent. A man known to the world as… KENNY MERCURY~!!!] [Kenny stolled (As I stated before, he was strolling… he’s good at strolling, almost as good as he is at wrestling) on up to a water fountain. You know, the kind with the water jug on top. Yeah, that kind. Kenny eyed it…] Kenny: Dixie cup dispenser… excellent… [Mercury reached over and pull out a little wax paper Dixie cup.] Kenny: And now, to quench my thirst… [Kenny went to the spout to pull down the little tab-thingy and get some water… but wait… there is not just one spout… THERE ARE TWO!!] Kenny: What the? Two? What kind of Bull…ogna is this? What does it mean? Why? WHY?! [Kenny scratched his head.] Kenny: Morons… why in Rod’s name would we need two spouts? People are such imbeciles… [Kenny glares at the spouts.] Kenny: Rod damn it… Ok… there’s a blue one, and a white one… Well… water is blue, but the white could be taken as transparent, which water is too… GAH! I JUST DON’T KNOW!!! [Mercury collapses to his knees, looking up at the ceiling and reaching out his arms to the heavens.] Kenny: WILL SOMEBODY NOT STOP THIS MADNESS!?!?!? [Mercury takes a few deep breaths.] Kenny: Fine I just don’t care anymore! I’m just going to drink BOTH and MIX them! I JUST DON’T CARE!!! [Mercury stands up, lunges forward at the water jug, and presses down on the blue tab. He fills it half way then finishes filling it with the white tab. Kenny Mercury downs the Dixie cup of water in one big gulp, swinging his head back, getting gravity to help him.] Kenny: *ahhhhhh* A little warm for my tastes, but refreshing none-the-less… Idiots, making two spouts for no reason. It’s pointless… POINTLESS! Just like my match this Sunday at The Clash. I have to wrestle someone named “????” What the heck? That’s the lamest name since Mr. Powerful. Look, Mr. ????, I don’t know who you are, what you want, what you smell like, or what kind of car you drive, but I DO know one thing. [Mercury holds up is index finger.] Kenny: And that one thing is… that you have no chance against the shooting star… the technical wizard… THE DEFINITION OF PRECISION! You have no chance, and I mean it, NO CHANCE against Kenny Mercury! Watch this… [Mercury grabs a new Dixie cup in his hand.] Kenny: This… is you. [Kenny smashes the small, measly Dixie cup by clenching a fist around it.] Kenny: This is you when I get my hands on you! BAHAHAHA!! You’ll be nothing more than a smashed cup, with cute little flowers on it, and oh look! Little butterflies and bunnies on it too! … … … … GAH! YOU’LL BE SMASHED! SMASHED I SAY! [Kenny tosses the cup over his shoulder.] Kenny: For far too long, I have been suppressed, but now… now… I have returned to the UWF, with rejuvenated power. I will rule the federation with my un-surpassed technical skills. So, if Mr. ???? thinks he’s going to pull a fast one on me, he’s wrong… DEAD WRONG! Why? Because! BAM! WHAM! SLAM! And he taps out, just like that. No one can escape me, no one ever… I mean, EVER, escaped the Spell-Binder. It will break your little friggin’ mystery man back. And don’t think that you’re going to try and confuse me with all those question marks that’ll most likely be all over your wrestling tights! OH NO! Not only am I a master of the mat, a ring technician and the king tactician… I am a master of only seeing what I want to see, only hearing what I want to hear, and only knowing what I want to know. So, Mr. ????, there’s no way you could EVER confuse someone like me, because I just cannot be done! You try, fine, I don’t care, go ahead and try it! It will only open you for attack, which will mean that I will have you on the mat, crying like a baby and tapping out just a few seconds faster than if you didn’t try to confuse me with your lame-o in ring get-up and jobberlicious name. So wait… maybe you SHOULD try and confuse me, because then it will just be over quicker and you won’t have to suffer for as long of a period of time, how does that sound? [Mercury lets loose his signature smirk and leans back on the water jug, with his elbow resting on top of it. He shakes his head and laughs to himself, trying to hold it back, but failing…] Kenny: Man… Who would have THUNK it, huh? That my glorious return… My long awaited and LONG PROPHESIZED step to the top, the very tip-top, of the federation. This is going to be the best times in the history of the Universal Wrestling Federation, the era of Kenny Mercury, and it all begins on Sunday. So, Mr. ????, whoever you are… you slug… you dirty douche… Are you ready to become a part of history? Are you ready to be the first stepping stone on the path of glory? The path of glory in which I will walk upon… I and I alone… NO ONE ELSE CAN! It’s basically my driveway… Mr. ???? is like my front porch, the genesis of my driveway to glory. And there’s a big fence around it too, so losers like Mike Montgomery and Joshua Dunn can’t sneak in and steal cabbage and stuff… Friggin’ jack-ilacs… But, Mr. ???? it’s all the same, you’re just another nameless, literally, competitor who is about to be totally and completely annihilated. It’s losers like you that make me look like a bully… but you won’t receive any mercy… NONE… and no cabbage either, you friggin’ Ass-hat nincompoop. So, I’ll see you Sunday, AND NO FUNNY STUFF!!!! [Kenny Mercury turns away from the camera (no he wasn’t just talking to himself that whole time) and storms away, laughing confidently to himself. He swipes about two dozen Dixie cups as he passes.] [End Transmission.]
