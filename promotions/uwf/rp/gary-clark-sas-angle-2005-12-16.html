UWF (2001–07) Roleplay

Gary Clark's SAS angle

16 Dec 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An angle written by the handler of Gary Clark, e-mailed to the UWF's booker on 16 December 2005 for the Sunday Afternoon Supremacy of 18 December 2005; this is the e-mail, from the Janitor's own mail archive, donated on 9 October 2026. The sender’s address is marked.

Written for Sunday Afternoon Supremacy — 18 Dec 2005, whose card does not carry it. Served whole, as written.

---------- Forwarded message --------- From: Gary Clark [detail withheld] Date: Fri, Dec 16, 2005 at 9:28 AM Subject: SAS ANGLE MONKEY! Angle for SAS if possible please, Ryan-o! If possible towards the beginning of the card, if not you probably want to edit the first line of commentary out ;) [The lights in the Arena fade down to a dark glow, until the only lights that remain on are the Emergency Exit lights, and a glowing VideoTron. An eerie silence in the crowd sparks off a few people's cellular phones as they try, aimlessly, to throw light on the arena.] 1#: We're only minutes into this show, and it seems we have a power outage… 2#: This sort of thing means we have a visitor! [After a few more seconds of anticipation, a white light fills the VideoTron screen before a bolt of black lightening strikes down the centre of the screen. As it hits the bottom, the first few notes of "High Rise" by Ladytron are piped into the arena. Dense and propulsive, the beat fills the arena before the lights at the top of the arena begin to strobe.] 1#: Look away now if you don't like strobe lighting. 2#: Ngggggggggggg. "Black sun rising, new day dawning, I'm defying… I'm destroying" [The crowd, everyone single one of them, have turned to look at the entrance ramp.] "We are on the same high, you and I. Open on the same page, no sunrise." [Stepping down the ramp, a face from the past… someone who hasn't been seen in four years. The Perennial Underdog. The Thrillseeker…" 2#: Is that who I think it is? 1#: It is… I think. It's… 2#: Gary Clark? 1#: One of the most successful GIWF stars of all time… he's come home to the company who started his career. "Tender talons… close horizons… Masquerading… Black sun rising." "We are on the same high, you and I. Open on the same page, no sunrise." [Clark walks down the aisle, dressed in a white t-shirt with a black lightening strike across the front and a pair of faded black jeans. As he reaches the ring, he rolls under the bottom rope and leaps to his feet. The crowd cheer for the former GIWF star.] "Silent movers, up and towards them. Perfect timing, hello morning." [Standing in each of the corners, and raising both arms, Clark laps up the applause. Finally asking for a microphone, Clark requests the music to be killed.] Clark: UWF! Say hello… to the nice guy! [The crowd cheer wildly.] 1#: He certainly has a way with the fans. Clark: Four long years and a lot of backstory. That's what stands between the last time you saw me in a UWF ring and today. There'll be plenty of time for us to go down that path and there'll be plenty of time around the ole campfire to tell the tales, but at the moment, hello. [Clark dampens down the crowds enthusiasm, waving his arms horizontally… suggesting quiet.] Clark: The GIWF brought me to this business. It's fitting that after such a long spell away that one of the most successful GIWF superstars in it's history has returned to his spiritual home. It may be different in name, and it may be different in location… But this is Home. Something here, however, doesn't feel right. It is deeply, deeply concerning. The UWF has a sort of cancer burying itself in the hearts of the competitors. Complacency is that cancer. I've spent the last few weeks catching up, and it makes disappointing viewing, I can tell you. [The crowd murmurs some discontent at this statement.] Clark: You see, where I come from… winning a championship title means something. It meant something to hold the GIWF Television Title up in the air for the very first time, and every single other time I lifted it in the air. The way that the current champions regard their titles here makes me want to take my own life with a spatula. Foster Nackedy, TV Champion. Who? The Insert Name Here championship… which hair-brained hootaninny made that decision? And more importantly, the World title. Killer Dude, World Heavy-fucking-weight Champion? Has the world taken leave of it's senses? [Pacing around the ring slightly, Clark holds the microphone in his right hand. He comes to a halt, resting on the top rope.] Clark: One of the reasons I've returned is to fulfil a promise. I made a promise which I fully intend on keeping. In January 2000, I stepped into a GIWF ring for the first time at the age of 23. In my eighteen months in the GIWF, I held the Television Title three times. I held the Intercontinental Title. I held the THC title, and arguably should never have lost it on a technicality. I was one of the longest North American champions in GIWF HISTORY! I even held one half of the World Tag Team Championships. But the one piece of the puzzle that still stands unfinished is the World Heavyweight Championship. I intend on laying that curse to rest. That nightmare will be put to rest. But first things first, UWF. There's a ladder to be climbed, and I fully intend on climbing it rung, by rung, by rung, by fucking rung. I'm from the old school of thinking. You climb the ladder and you earn your chance. You don't just buy your chance. You don't go for that leapfrogging bullshit. 1#: The bottom of the pile. He's starting from the bottom of the pile! Clark: I will advance through the ranks. I've gone through too much not to. The Clark New Year begins here and now – no quarter given and certainly no quarter asked. And when people wake up and smell the coffee, their dreams will become nightmares. It starts here. Now. If anyone thinks I can't do it, I invite them to try and stop me. And then they'll realise that they should… Say Goodnight… to the Black and White! [Throwing the microphone down to the ground, Gary Clark raises his right arm before sliding out of the ring and marching back to the locker room area with "High Rise" blaring out around the arena.]
