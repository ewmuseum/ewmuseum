UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #585

Holy Fijoles!

7/30 (year unrecorded)

White Tiger — A trailer somewhere in California

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Somewhere in California there is a trailer. Once belonging to a legendary UWF Superstar, it's now the home of that elite force for security... the Masked Monkeys. And one of those Masked Monkeys is White Tiger, the young luchador with lots of heart. And that's pretty much is all he has, other than a losing record. Right now he is sitting in the trailer's small table as his dear Uncle Oscar makes omelettes for breakfast. 'My Rod!' exclaims the Tiger, 'How long does El Turtle take in the shower?' 'Well,' Uncle Oscar replies, 'He is a turtle, you know.' 'Si, tio,' White Tiger responds, 'But he uses all the hot water! And the singing! How much 'Ninja Rap' must I take?' 'Just be happy you have a place to live and a job with security,' says Uncle Oscar. 'The only thing more disappointing than your UWF career in you movie career.' 'Hey, now!' White Tiger says defensively, 'I had soem tough opposition when I was here, I did all right in Japan. And my movie career wasn't my fault. If that director from Mucha Macho Movies would check the birth certificates of his starlets before getting them on the casting couch, the studio would still be in business and I would be a star!' Uncle Oscar mereley shakes his head and continues making omelettes. Suddenly, the phone rings and White Tiger answers it. 'Hola,' he says. 'Uh huh... uh huh... uh huh... this Sunday? Of course! Gracias, Senor Hayes!' White Tiger hangs up the phone and begins jumping up and down, which shakes the whole trailer. 'I got a match! I got a match! Woo-hoo!' 'I'm so happy for you, nephew,' says Uncle Oscar. 'But calm down. You're going to make me spill the omelttes!' 'Sorry,' White Tiger says as he simmers down a bit. 'So who is your opponent?' asks Uncle Oscar. 'Well.. it's Sacrifice,' he answers nervously. Uncle Oscar turns pale. 'That means...' 'Yes,' says White Tiger with a gulp. 'I'm gonna be... SACRIFICED~!' Cue ominous music.
