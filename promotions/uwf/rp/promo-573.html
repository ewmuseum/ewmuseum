UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #573

Gold Gold everywhere

25 Jul 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of the familiar lodgings of the UWF Shareholders branch office. The office seems pretty bare, although there is some newness to it. If you look closely at it, the walls are covered with a gold foil. Not just one wall, all the walls. Also, the “desk” is, and everything else is gold foiled. Sitting in the gold foiled chair is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee, who is also gold foiled, except for his face. Not even Golddust has this much gold around him. There is absolutely nothing in the office that isn’t gold. Kazee looks at the camera with a chaotic look on his face.) Kazee: Gold, it’s what every athlete in the UWF wants. It’s what everyone is here to compete for. For some of us, it’s a dream we realize. For others, it’s a dream that is full of empty promises. Gold, for myself, has never been an important part of this sport, or an important part of what drives me in this sport……. To say that I haven’t had the success of winning gold, everyone would know that to be a lie. To say that I haven’t had the taste of success, as well as of defeat would be so wrong it isn’t even funny. I’ve had great success when it comes to gold. And I have had some complete disappointments when it comes to losing gold…… Recent times will show you how the gold has been there and has been a big part of my success recently. It was just earlier this year that two of us in the Players Club held almost all of the gold. It was just recently that Mike Montgomery as the World Champion, myself as the Intercontinental Champion, were also carrying around Tag Team Gold as well. It was a good time for me and Monty. It was a good time for the Players Club…… Then, it all started to fall apart. Monty and I lost the tag gold to Digimon and Moraless. It was a hard fought match, and in the end, we just couldn’t finish off those two. Then, I lost my IC Gold to Raid in the Tower of Doom. Shocked and dismayed weren’t the exact feelings going through my head, but to say I was disappointed is an understatement. Then, the final blow came when Monty was stripped of his World Title…… But now, the Players Club is back on top. We’ve won Stable Wars. We’ve recruited the THC Champion into our ranks, and just recently, Joshua Dunn captured the World Title for the Players Club once more, in an impressive match over Kevin Starr. The Players Club is back in the title hunt, and two of us have yet to use our Stable Wars prizes…… Saturday, I get the chance to team up again with a World Champion. It’s not Mike Montgomery however. It’s Joshua Dunn in this case. It’s the Communist Scott that I get to team with. This man is one of the greatest competitors that I have ever known. This man is the emphasis of what a competitor should be. This man almost single handedly won Stable Wars for The Players Club……. And the team we face is not an unfamiliar one. Malcom Kennedy is a man I’ve faced on several occasions now. How many is this man. 5? And my record against you is absolutely stellar. In fact, you could say it’s flawless. 4-0 against you so far, and things are only looking up in my favor for Sunday. I have the best in the business at my side, and we are only looking up at the way things are going.;;;;;; And then we have Digimon. Now yeah, you are equally as flawless against me as I am against Kennedy. But times have changed, and things are going to continue to change. And as the old saying goes, even the sun shines on a dogs behind. This Sunday, that’s exactly what is going to happen. The sun is going to be shining bright, and it’s going to be so bright that I gotta wear shades. (Kazee puts a pair of very dark sunglasses on, and then Kazee turns on a light that. It reflects all over the place and is almost to bright for the camera to see through anything.) Sunday, we are going to rumble. Sunday, we are going to have a match of epic proportions. And in the end, it will be the Players Club which once again proves its dominance…… Good Luck Mr. Kennedy and Digimon, cause the Players Club is on a runaway train that is heading right for you, and neither of you are wearing an S on your chest. (Scene fades to black)
