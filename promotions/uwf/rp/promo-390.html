UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #390

New Rivals and Old Nemesis

4 May 2004

Anarchy — Raid's extermination business

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to outside of Raid’s extermination business. In the parking lot is parked a black SUV with four individuals standing around it talking amongst themselves. They are Pryde, Dai, Joanna and the big man Draco Paladine.] Camera Man : Joanna, you revealed your fourth member for stable wars this last Sunday at Supremacy, why would you get the man who betrayed you and Pryde in the past and what makes you think that he won’t do it again? [Paladine starts to step forward to say something then Joanna stops him.] Joanna : We have had that discussion with Drac… [Joanna pokes Paladine in the chest.] Joanna : …and we feel that we can trust him. After stable wars we will be behind him 100% with whatever he decides to do. CM : Dai, what about the attack on you and Drac this last Sunday, they appear to be the new comers Robb Daniels and Jefferson. Any word for them? Dai : These two come in bragging about title rains in other feds and how they are going to become big starts right away and become champions in no time. I don’t think it is going to happen. CM : But they feel that it has an importance here. Doesn’t that intimidate you? Dai : Titles from other feds don’t mater here, we found that out. As far as intimidating us, we could care less what they have held elseware and no one else here cares either. What matters is what you do here in the UWF. We plan to teach them a lesson in the ring tomorrow and let you see what Total Anarchy is all about and send them back to Persia like the lap dogs they are. CM : There has been no official connection between them three so how can you say that they have anything to do with him? [Pryde steps forward] Pryde : One can only assume that he brought them in for the attack since no other surprise showed up that night. Whether they are still his lackeys I don’t know but we will crush them no matter what. CM : Speaking of teams, what ever happened to Anarchy the tag team? Lately it has been all about you Pryde and your feud with Nick Persia. Where are you headed now? Pryde : Dai knows… [Pryde pats Dai on the back.] Pryde : I am always here to go after the tag belts, this thing with Persia is personal. CM : So the your not after his belt? Pryde : Hell Ya I’m after his belt. Just because it’s personal doesn’t mean I won’t take that precious belt from him. I want to prove to him that I defiantly don’t see him as the teacher and me as the student and taking THC from him will prove that I need no teacher when it comes to wrestling. I am as technically sound as anyone in the UWF with the exception to a few, and if I get another match with that Dutch loser I will prove that. CM : So are you throwing out a challenge to Nick Persia? Pryde : Damn right, and I feel so confident that I can take that belt from him that if I lose I will leave Nick Persia and his hardcore championship alone, no ifs, and, or buts about it. CM : That is quite a statement, are you sure you want to do that? Pryde : Yes, but with one additional stipulation. CM : I got to hear this an added stip to an already hardcore match. Pryde : I will only do it if the stip of a Stretcher Match is added. The first to get his opponent on a stretcher and strap him down wins. Joanna : So Nick Persia, are you man enough to defend your strap in a Stretcher Match? My man Pryde here is giving you the chance of a life time to get him out of your hair, but are you man enough to do it? CM : What a challenge, any final statements? [Dai steps forward and stares right into the lens.] Dai : Robb Daniels and Jefferson, prepare to feel Total Anarchy. [The scene fades to black.]
