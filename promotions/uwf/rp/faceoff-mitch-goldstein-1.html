FaceOff, Part One

date unknown

Mitch Goldstein

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"If a Jew doesn't make Kiddush, then the non-Jew will make Havdalah for him." - R' Chaim of Volozhin Grrrrrr. [One year old, our hero lies on his back, his feet slowly kicking into the air, giggling as the fan overhead spins ‘round and ‘round.] They’re responsible for what happens now. They are! [Suddenly the big, round face of a woman bounces into view, and the giggling stops.] “Mitchie Mitchie Mitchie!!” [Mitch Goldstein tries to kick away from the round woman, but it does no good. She’s on him in seconds, poking him in the stomach, pinching his cheeks, and terrorizing the poor child.] “Oy! His toes are still so small, Abraham!” [Our hero tries desperately to pull himself away, but the round woman grabs his legs, holding him in place, and lifting his foot high into the air sound the man with way-too-much-facial-hair can see.] “And would you look at that tiny shmekel! Oh, Abraham, what is wrong with our boy’s shmekel?” [The man with far too much facial hair reaches over, lifting up the baby’s diaper and groaning.] “Voden? He gets it from your side of the family, Judith!” [A vase goes flying across our hero’s line of sight, crashing far out of view with a horrible shatter.] “Shvayg!!!!” [As hands go flying past Mitch’s line of sight, we rocket forward in time.] Again and again they pushed me. They never expected I would turn on them! They just go “Oooo little Mitchie let’s make fun of him and give him crappy gifts and he’ll never do anything” well that’s chazeray! OY! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Now six years old, Mitch Goldstein sits with hope dancing in his innocent eyes, smiling up at the lit candles on the table. The round woman once more comes into view.] “Oh, Mitchie! Happy Chanukah!” [Mitch Goldstein looks up, smiling at the round woman that had given him so much grief. Until... !] “Here’s your dreidel.” [Mitch Goldstein’s smile dies as the round woman hands him the little wooden top.] “That’s a good boy. Just don’t let it run over your shmekel.” [The round woman pats him on the head.] “…” [Mitch Goldstein shakes uncontrollably as he tries to crush the dreidel in his hands.] A dreidel!? They give me a dreidel!?! I’m six f’ing years old!!!! Dreidel’s suck!!!!!! <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Our hero now thirteen years old, we come face to face with… a large wooden door.] [Nothing moves. No sound is heard. Until the round woman comes flying into view, pounding on the door.] “MITCHIE!!! Mitchie!!! What are you doing in there!? You’ve been in there for a half hour, Mitchie!!!!” [She keeps pounding.] “Nothing, mother! I had to take a very large dreck!!!” [She keeps pounding.] “You’d better not be shlepping your shmekel, Mitch Goldstein!!! Don’t make me get your father up here!!!!” [Mitch pounds back.] “GO AWAY GO AWAY GO AWAY!!!!!!” ...oh that was so embarassing. <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Now 18, our hero is a man… an angry, angry young man.] “They drove me to this!!! You hear me?! YOU!!!!!” [The nanny looks up from her bagel.] “Me, Mitch?” [Mitch Goldstein turns around, annoyed.] “No, not you, Miss Frannie. I was having an emotional soliloquy, gosh darnit.” [The nanny looks back down, taking a bite of her bagel and going back to the paper.] “Very good, Mitchie.” [Mitch Goldstein raises his fist, shaking it with utter rage.] “They mocked me far too much… they pushed me… they prodded me… they drove me to what I must do!!! Mitch Goldstein is no more!!!!” [Mitch grabs a torn black sheet with holes cut into it and yanks it down over his face. The stretchy piece at the base keeps it tight around his throat. Quickly and suddenly, he slams his yarmulke on top of his head. With his gear in place, Mitch strikes a sudden pose, looking like a fool.] “There is only… THE RAVING JEWISH DESTROYER NOW!!!!!!!!!” [The RJD throws wide the front door, but makes sure to pick up the lunch the nanny left for him first.] “Look out world… the Raving Jewish Destroyer is among you!!! First the wrestling world… then… I don’t know! But I’ll think of something! RAR!!!!!!!!” [Mitch leaps through the doorway.] “AND THERE IS NOTHING WRONG WITH MY SHMEKEL!!!!!!!!!!” [The Raving Jewish Destroyer charges through the door, darting across the front lawn. Two minutes later, his dead sprint comes to a stop. He carefully picks out his keys, fumbles with them for a few seconds, and THROWS! open the front gate.] [Then he turns around, carefully closes and locks it, and resumes his sprint.] [The nanny shrugs.] “Very good, dear. Remember to wear clean underwear.” “RAAAAARRRRRRRR!!!!!!!” [And so the Raving Jewish Destroyer was unleashed on an unsuspecting world.] .... .... <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Ahem." [The sound of a pencil tap-tap-tapping against a clipboard.] "Mitch..." [And now, a scene very much different from the others, as a skinny Mitch Goldstein lies on a long, red couch, a stereotypical psychiatrist sitting over him in his own chair, glasses tilted downwards slightly.] "Please, call me the Raving Jewish Destroyer, Doc." [The therapist winces.] "Right. About that, Mr... Raving Jewish Destroyer; you've told me quite a bit about your background, but I don't think we're getting quite to the heart of the matter. You unresolved issues with your family notwithstanding... exactly what do you hope to gain from entering a wrestling tournament?" [The RJD tilts his head back.] "Pupik?" "Mitch, I believe you just said 'belly button' and put a question mark at the end of it." "Oh... yeah. My Yiddish is meshugala, doc. And it's still the Raving Jewish Destroyer." "Right... nevertheless, Mr. Destroyer, if you don't mind me saying so... you're not the most intimidating of physical specimens." [The RJD shakes his head, still staring up at the ceiling.] "In fact, I believe you weigh quite a bit less than my wife. You are simply not in an impressive physical condition. You have absolutely no training for this sort of thing. And now you have taken it upon yourself to enter into a demanding physical competition that will test your every limit, with quite large and well-trained opponents, in contests that will most likely leave you physically crippled... and all for less money than I am charging your mother for this intervention. Even this woman you told me about ... this Miranda you will be competing with..." [The RJD smiles.] "Miranda Big-Bazoongas! OY!" [The therapist nods.] "Yes, her. Even a woman such as her is so physically superior to you, not to mention better trained, that you may very well be putting your life in danger simply for... for some foolish, adolescent crusade. Mr. Destroyer... Do you not see it as possible that you are simply acting out in fear over your impending departure from the home you've known all of your life, and that you've adopted this... alter-ego in an effort to lash out against over-bearing parents and to deal with that fear?" <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "And what did you do then, Mr. Raving Jewish Destroyer?" ["The Raving Jewish Destroyer" Mitch Goldstein smiles down upon the small child that eagerly stares up at him, his face showing the tremendous curiosity in the story and awe for the mysteriously masked man under it.] "I kicked him in the shmekel, obviously! Which, I believe you will learn, is the answer to most of life's problems." [The child nods happily, storing this for future reference.] "So now what, Mr. Jewish Destroyer? Are we really going to a wrestling show?!" [The RJD strikes a demented pose in the middle of the sidewalk, throwing an arm into the air, and staring out dementedly. Other pedestrians give him a wide berth.] "We are, Little Timmy... we are! Because the dreidle has spun, Little Timmy... and when the dreidle spins, that means destruction for the gentiles! Plus, I have a righteous wrestling costume, and I'm going to get to touch boobies." [Little Timmy nods happily.] "Man, Mr. Destroyer. I can't wait until I'm big so I can wrestle and touch boobies and kick people in the shmekel." [The Raving Jewish Destroyer begins once more walking down the sidewalk with his young accomplice.] "It's a dream worth having, Little Timmy, and never let anyone tell you any differently. Now, come! To the Jew-Cave!!!!!" [The Raving Jewish Destroyer and Little Timmy go running down the sidewalk, leaving us behind, no doubt preparing for the challenge of the RJD's life.] … [WHAT will young Mitch Goldstein do against blatantly superior competition?] [WHY would Mitch Goldstein go into a match with one of the only opponents in the tournament that doesn't have a shmekel for him to kick?] [WHO is Little Timmy, and why was he just randomly thrown in there at the end?] [WHAT will the Raving Jewish Destroyer tell the cops when they wonder why a man with panty hose on his head is wondering around the city with a child he intends to bring to his Jew-Cave?] [All those questions will be answered NEXT TIME!!! Same Jew time, same Jew channel. Assuming Mitch doesn't just get his heiny kicked right out of the gate.] [But do not fear, kids! Because win or lose, the Raving Jewish Destroyer WILL touch boobies.] [And there's really not much more to life than that.] [Fin.]
