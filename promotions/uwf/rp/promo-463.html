(untitled)

date unknown

Jay Vyper

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Angry shouts and staccato bursts of weapons fire filled the hallway behind them, but The new UCATA6 recruits, A.K.A. Team X didn't slow for a moment. Their pursuers had yet to realize that fire was not being returned, that the operatives who had assassinated the so-called president of EI Malojo were on the run. Those few seconds were precious. “Go, go!” Vyper yelled, and nodded toward the wide palace stairwell. Once again, he scanned the hall down which they'd run. Still no sign of pursuit. A quick glance at the stairs confirmed that the other three members of his team-Vlad, Stratus, and Jade- were heading for the roof of the estate. It wasn't really a palace. He'd seen palaces. But in a tiny Central American nation whose gross national product was probably less than the average American made in a year, it was as close to a palace as even a tyrannical dictator was likely to get. Not that it mattered. The master of the house, EI Malojo's dictator and one of the primary figures in international drug trading, was dead. Vlad had made absolutely certain of that before the guards had discovered them. Now they were headed for their dust off point in high gear. None of them could be traced back to the Agency if captured, but they'd all prefer to avoid it if at all possible. Down the hall, guards cursed in Spanish and poked their heads around the corner, finally realizing their leader's assassins were escaping. Without hesitation, they swarmed around the comer, nearly falling over one another for a chance to become a hero, to avenge the death of a man they'd all hated. Jerks, Vyper thought, and then stepped out from the shadow of the arched doorway across from the wide stairwell. In the heartbeat when the guards were too stunned by his sudden appearance to react, he hefted the iron weight in his hands and hurled a pair of Czech RG34 grenades along the hallway toward the guards. The EI Malojan troops barely had time to cry out in alarm and scramble for cover when the double explosion brought much of the hallway down around them. Several of them were thrown through windows or along the hall. Even before the grenade detonated, Vyper had reached the stairwell and taken the first two steps in one stride. He stumbled slightly as the concussive blast propelled him forward. Two more steps, and he tossed a British number seventy-seven white phosphorus grenade over his shoulder, filling the stairwell with fire and smoke in his wake. An agonized voice shouted up from below in sneering Spanish. “You are heading for the roof, you fools!” the voice roared. “Your escape is cut off, now. It is only a matter of time before you are captured. Then you will be at our mercy, and you will suffer greatly before you are allowed to die!” He was a soldier, true, yet Vyper could not help but wince at the man's words. There was no real pleasure in war for him. But even small wars had to have their soldiers. And soldiering was good for him; it meant he could let the savagery out of his heart in controlled bursts. Even better, he could do it in the service of the free world. Vyper-codename: The Major-was haunted by the beast within, a bloodthirsty creature that cried out for battle. The Major was a warrior in search of a war. His teammates each had their own story, their own background, much of which they hid from one another. Jeremy Stratus, a.k.a Stryker, had been a freedom fighter in East Germany before entering the world of espionage. Once, he had told Vyper that he'd agreed to join UCATA6 because he believed they could do some good. Vyper had been meaning to ask him if he'd changed his mind. The other two members of the team were more enigmatic. Beast-whose real name was Vlad Abendroth, though he seemed at times to prefer the codename-was a vicious s.o.b. who took great pleasure in killing, and would use any excuse to do so. It chilled Vyper to watch him, at times, for Vlad's butchery echoed the dreadful urges in Vyper's own heart. “Vyper, move it!” Stratus roared from above. “Dustoff's in less than thirty!” There was a clattering of bullets far below as he turned up the last flight of stairs to the roof. Reinforcements, and those who'd survived the grenades. Vyper had a 9MM in an armpit holster, and a Walther MPL on a leather holster slung across his shoulder, but he didn’t return fire. There was no purpose. The mission was done. Escape was the only goal now. He vaulted the last few steps and saw the shattered window just ahead. Team X had made its own door out to the roof. They waited for him there, and he had to wonder how long it would be before the EI Malojans got a chopper in the air. Glass crunched underfoot as he stepped through the broken window and onto the roof of the palace. “Aw, and here I was hopin' we'd be able to leave the runt behind,” Beast said. “Any time you think you're ready, Vlad,” Vyper said with a growl. Vlad only smiled. The man, at about seven foot three, definitely towered over Vyper. Even Stratus, at just over six feet, was a midget by comparison. But height was not as much an asset as his skill, strength, speed, and savagery. Then the fourth member of the team finally spoke up. Jade, a.k.a Vixen was the quietest of them, and in her way the most secretive. Even Vyper, who had been her lover for more than a year, knew very little about her. “Ignore him, Vyper,” she said, and he was soothed by the Chinese woman's words, and by the sight of her chocolate eyes and the breeze whipping her hair across her face. “You tell 'im, squaw,'' Vlad barked, his tone mocking. “He don't need to know about us just yet.” Vyper felt his upper lip begin to curl into a snarl, and pushed the animal back down inside him. “Leave it be,” Vixen whispered. “One 0' these days,” Vyper said grimly, shaking his head. Then Vlad's head shot up. “We got company.” Vyper listened, and knew that Beast was right. The guards were making plenty of noise coming up the stairs. They were terrified. Vyper could smell it on them. “I don't suppose anybody's got any explosives left?” Vixen asked. “Stryker, what about you? Any of those thermite grenades you love so much?” No reply. Team X uttered a collective sigh and reached for its weapons. Vyper brought the Walther MPL up on its tether, holding the nine-millimeter submachine gun by its grip, and planting the folding stock against his shoulder. “Where the hell is Phoenix?” Stratus grumbled behind him. “Speak of the devil,” an amused voice declared, “and she shall appear.' The speaker was a slender black haired woman of medium height, wearing mirrored sunglasses and a cowboy hat, despite the fact that it was nearly midnight Veronica Gurovich, their extraction woman. “About time,” Vyper said. “Why don't you get us outta here?” “Your wish is” Phoenix pointed the almost invisible helicopter on the far corner of the roof the air began to shimmer around them all. The sound of the blades increased instantly. “-my command,” Phoenix finished. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “Next time, Phoenix, try to be on time,” Vlad whispered with menace, as they all took their seats. Phoenix ignored him. She had always had very little tolerance for Vlad. Out of all the UCATA6 teams she has worked with, he was definitely the worst recruit. She didn’t even know what country was sad enough to let him represent them. Veronica Gurovich had once been the head of Russian recruiting, but had since become more involved with the missions. She was never really good at the whole war scene so she choose to be the pickup/drop off lady. It kept her happy. When Phoenix stood unsteadily, Stratus moved away from the wall where he'd been leaning. “Listen, I need some downtime,” he-said. “Can we deal with the debrief in the morning?” “No,” Phoenix replied curtly. “We don't even have time for a debrief. You're on your way to East Germany in ninety minutes.” Vlad and Vyper said nothing. They had nowhere else to go. “You promised us three days' leave,” Vixen reminded Phoenix. “Not this time, boys and girls,” Phoenix declared. Stratus grumbled, stared at Vyper, who shrugged almost imperceptibly in response. “It better be good, Veronica,” Stratus snapped. Vyper smiled thinly. Poor kid actually thinks he can do the kind of work Team X does and still have some kind of normal life. He really is a dreamer. “I think you'll enjoy this one, Sergeant Stratus,” Phoenix said archly. “You get to go home.” Stratus raised an eyebrow. “East Berlin?” Vyper asked. “Hooray, a vacation behind the Iron Curtain,” Vlad growled. “Let's kill us some commies.” “What's the mission?” Vixen asked. “Save the free world,” Phoenix replied. “I could be sleeping right now,” Stratus complained. “I'm not kidding,” Phoenix protested, and the tone of her voice made Vyper take notice. She really wasn't kidding. The idea was profoundly disturbing: the fate of the world could rest on the actions of a group of borderline--or in the case of Vlad, complete-psychotics like Team X. “A pair of KGB agents have come into possession of some very sensitive information,” Phoenix explained. “They stole a data disk that contains the locations and codes of America's entire nuclear arsenal. In the wrong hands, it could mean the nuclear devastation of the United States.” “Folks shouldn't ought to leave that kind 0' thing layin' around,' Vlad grunted. “So we snatch the disk back,” Stratus said, obviously trying to hurry the proceedings along. “And terminate the KGB agents?” Vlad asked, a broad smile on his face. “If necessary,” Phoenix replied. Beast grinned even wider.
