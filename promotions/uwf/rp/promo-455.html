UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #455

Breaking in the new blood.

17 Jun 2004

Adien Pryde — Alley in Florida

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Scene opens to a dark alley of what could possibly be Orlando, Florida. Walking up the alley toward the camera are two figures, a man and a woman, both of which are wearing long trench coats with the collars up to shield against the drizzle that is falling. As they get nearer three figures, a woman and two men, step out of the shadows and bar their way.] Woman 1 : So how did it go? Woman 2 : Real good. He didn’t fall for the first part but these two sure put the scare into him. He calls himself The Real Deal, but I didn’t see anything other than REAL fright in him. Woman 1 : Thanks, here is the money I promised you. [Woman 1 hands woman 2 an envelope.] Woman 1 : As for you two, you better get back, drill starts at o5:oo hours and if your currant Drill Sergeant is anything like I was then he will see the lack of sleep in your eyes. [The two men salute woman 1 and then they and woman 2 walk down the alley away from the camera as the other two figures walk toward the camera. As they leave the shadows of the alley you can finally see that they are none other than Adien Pryde and Joanna.] Joanna : See Pryde, having the military background that we do has it’s privileges. Pryde : It looks like this newcomer will be no challenge for the new TV Champ. If he can be so easily distracted by a good looking woman than he stands no chance in the ring with me. Hell, his only other match in the UWF is against that loser Loverboy and that was probably only because the poor man was tired from a full night of, well you know. Joanna : Sex, damn boy, when it comes to talking about things like that you are terrible. Didn’t I beat the shyness out of you back in boot camp. Pryde : Yes you did but that was 16 years ago and, hell never mind. So did your sources get any info on what experience this guy has. I mean, all we saw this last Sunday was some brawling and a few power moves. Joanna : Nope, he seems to be relatively new to the sport which means you will have no problem taking him out. Pryde : Like I thought. [Pryde turns and looks straight into the camera.] Pryde : Real Deal, you want to play with the big boys here in the UWF. You must have impressed someone to get a title match after only on win but that means nothing to me. Sunday, you will come to the ring with a 1 and 0 record but I intend to make you leave with a 1 and 1. See you Sunday. [The scene fades to black]
