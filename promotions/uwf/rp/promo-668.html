UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #668

Bring It

6 Sep 2004

The Plague — Philadelphia

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[So it is time once again for The Plague to step into The Main Event. Plague couldn’t achieve Regal Rumble victory, but this Sunday he has a chance to defeat a hall of famer, a former World Champ. One of, if not the best UWF Superstar in the short history. Oh yeah, there is some other ass that thinks he is worthy of being in the ring with Tsunami Oni and Plague. But anyway, the scene opens up with The Plague at the airport in Philly. He is just stepping out of the gate and heading to the luggage area. He is wearing a UWF shirt and shorts as well as his boots and a backwards hat on his head. A UWF camera crew walks up and stops Plague.] Interviewer: “Plague, it is good to see you back, ready for a huge battle at Supremacy.” Plague: “ Yeah, I am really looking forward to stepping in the ring with Tsunami Oni and Mr. Pryde. Oni, you know I have a ton of respect for you, even though you did Cheat your way to the top. But hey, you got to do what you got to do to get to the top right? Pride, you have no idea what your talking about buddy.” [Plague cracks his knuckles and gets an evil look in his eyes.] Plague: “You say I just come and go from this organization, well your right. But I have been here since the beginning. You ever hear of experience bitch.. You know what it’s like to be a legend in this sport, oh wait, no you don’t. I’ve been here since the beginning, since the GIWF, that means I got a little stroke here Pryde. Hayes knows I am a worthy competitor. He knows what I am capable of doing, and Pryde, you don’t want to get on my bad side. You can ask some of the greats in this sport. Ask Jamie Shock, ask Josh Dunn, two former World Champs, ask anyone who stepped into the ring with me and they’ll tell you what I am capable of doing.” [Plague walks over and grabs his luggage.] Plague: “Adian Pryde, this kid thinks running his mouth will get him somewhere. You never got into the ring with me Pryde. You think I didn’t achieve shit, well did you see me defeat Jamie Shock to become the final entry in The Rumble? I may not have won, but I proved to the world I still got it, and you better not forget that Pryde. [Plague walks away from the luggage area and about to head out of the airport.] Plague: No, I’m not done yet. I got a lot to get off my chest tonight, one day before I get into the ring facing Tsunami Oni, finally a chance to show to the world I can defeat The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. A chance to prove that I am not just some washed up bum trying to appear and re-appear as I please and jumping into the spotlight. I feel belong in the spotlight, I just need to prove to myself I can stand with the big playas here in The UWF. Which if you look at it, I basically fought, or defeated everybody in this Fed. Plus defeated legends of this ring that no longer share their presence here in this ring anymore. [Plague seems really pissed with the accusations of Pryde.] Plague: Pryde, you really think that your Chaos Society is anything here in The UWF. I can take you any day of the week and make you my bitch, plus I can take on any of your other Chaos Society assholes too. I don’t need a stable. I been there and done that. I am a loner and that’s the way I like it right now. I want to prove not only to myself but also to The UWF and the world that I am a true champion, not some fluke. I know in my heart that I can achieve the goal of World Champ, all I need is that little push of confidence and I feel that Sunday is that day. Oni, a true champion, yeah right, maybe in all of the UWF followers’ eyes. I see a paper champ, a false champ, a man who used sexual exploitation in order to keep his success rising up and up. Shiatsu, now doubt is one hot bitch but come on, if she wants a swift ass beating, I’ll gladly give it to her in a way Oni could never give it. [Plague cracks a grin and licks his lips.] Plague: “I’m really looking forward to get back into the ring on Sunday, I mean I can prove to some punk ass who thinks he knows The UWF what I’m all about, and then I can finally defeat Tsunami Oni and work my way back into The World Title hunt. I haven’t done shit? Ha, I’ll show you what I’m all about Pryde. Cause if you want to start something, I’m all about putting and end to it. I’m not afraid to end a career you know. I’ll crack a sledgehammer across your face if you want. But I really don’t think you want that do you. So Pryde, I suggest you just sit back and take your beating like the bitch you are.” [Plague heads out of the airport and again puts his luggage down. No limo this time Hayes? Oh well, I don’t need that shit. I know I’m the man and I’ll prove it in the coming weeks you just watch. This Sunday will be only the beginning. Until Sunday bitches. Don’t be afraid, it’s only The Plague.” [Plague hails a cab and steps in. He waves to the camera crew and the cab drives away.]
