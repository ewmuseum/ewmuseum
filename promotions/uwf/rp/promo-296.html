UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #296

World Title here we come

1/4/2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Renaissance is here, well near enough, The PPV is going to be big, as every member of the UWF has been booking into this monster pile of Talent, and near misses. But the biggest story of the night is Joshua Dunn -v- Beezlebozo the psychotic clown of a World Champion for the World Title. The Number One contender for the World Title Belt finally gets his shot at the Federations most prestigious award. Even after his minor set back of losing to Moraless last SAS, Dunn must be raring to have a go at the World Champion who has been Quiet as of late] [The Scene Opens, we find ourselves in the dressing room of Joshua Dunn, the camera looks over the room of the star, and the patterned furniture instantly recognises the communist theme of the place, the red walls and sofa and the coffee table sporting the hammer and sickle design. On the sofa, legs raised over the far arm and leaning against the opposite corner reading a book is the communist in question Joshua Dunn. Dunn’s wearing his red T-shirt stretched over his highly muscled upper half and black jeans filled also. The camera closes in on Dunn and catches the glint of the chain Joshua has around his neck, the emblem of communism clearly visible against the red of his shirt. In Dunn’s hands, he is studiously reading a book, the exact title can’t be seen but two words dominate the drab brown cover, Psychological Warfare. He turns the page and begins to read again before he looks up and notices the camera. When he does he slaps the books shut with one hand creating a nice punctuated sound with it before laying it down on the table. He rolls around taking his feet to the floor so he can face the camera better and sits hunched forwards] JD: I was just enjoying a bit of light reading there The human brain is one of the organs we use the least, it’s nice to indulge it every now and again. Some certain fellows would benefit from that, as they seem to use theirs even less than the average person. Ruff Ryder would be one of those people. I still will hold true that your paycheque will lose a few zeros if you even think in interfering in the Main event. Why can’t you understand that you blew your chance at the World title on so many different occasions? I have never complained so much in my entire career for what you complain about after every loss. Your probably believe your bullet proof too. [At this point he sits back and places both arms over the back of the sofa and waves his right hand dismissively while looking upward slightly] JD: But you are beneath my concern at the moment. I do indeed have bigger fish to fry. [He looks back at the camera a more solemn look on his face if that’s possible for the eternally sober faced Dunn] JD: One of these being the return of a certain Vietnamese tag team that I would rather not to mention by name. Their return signifies that all the past is not truly dead and buried, as I would like it. The eerie shadow of the Iron Curtain creeping back under the door. This is another detail to leave for a later date, there is and even bigger fish than the multiple tag team champs to worry about. That fish would be Beezlebozo, and I finally get my ONE ON ONE match against him on renaissance. [He grins that grin that only Dunn could use without frightening small children] JD: I finally get the honour of taking the World Champ on a one way thrill ride through the ring on my own, no distractions, no weapons, no mess, just a good clean fight. That’s what I hope anyway. There isn’t a gimmick to this match, possibly I’m quite glad, but nevertheless I’m upset, as I know how fond of weapons Mr Bozo is, and one connection to my skull means my victory, but he retains the title. I think you can safely see that this is indeed unfair, but instead of imposing a title change on a disqualification, I’ll instead behave quite rashly and appeal to the Clown’s sense of dignity. [Dunn looks at the camera for a few moments, head slightly cocked] JD: You may think that’s a mad idea, appealing to the dignity of a clown. There is though a man there; no matter how bitter and twisted, and I know every man has a sense of pride, even if his morals are perverted and shameful. I also know the feeling that you get when you undeniably beat a man in a fair fight. This is what I ask of you Bozo, everyone likes a challenge, having the odds stacked in your favour are never interesting. Then again it is only a suggestion and a matter of pride, could you give up a baseball bat covered in my blood for a nice clean fight because your opponent asked you? [He repositions himself again sitting forward again] JD: Then again maybe the masses are right and our world champ is indeed a joke not to be taken seriously, and in that case I’m just wasting my time talking about it. But we shall see if I am right or wrong on the matter at a different date. I have some other matters to be attending to. So I bid you farewell [Dunn throws his legs back onto the sofa and picks up the book again and continues from where he left off] [The scene fades to black]
