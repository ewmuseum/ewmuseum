UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #797

Bunnies Rule, Seymour Drools

30 Nov 2004

Bunny Army of Doom — Alexander Castle

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“…and so that is how I convinced Chris Storm to be my opponent for this fraud election” Laguna boosted proudly, arms on hips with chest sticking out. His think chin stood out like Yao Ming at home. “Hey wait a minute!” Ward shouted as he shot to his feet. “I was the one telling the story.” Indeed he had, though Laguna was about to explain just how he came to telling the end. “Well you see if our handler waited so long to roleplay again that I forgot who was telling the story.” Yeah I’m lazy, so what are you going to do fire me? I wasn’t even hired! I just like stalking you people and posting random roleplays. Bite me doughboy. Ok I’m finished, time for more storyline development. In the highest tower of Alexander castle the trio of Kiros, Ward, and their leader Laguna discuss matters at hand. The past few weeks have been a troublesome time. The army of man-eating bunnies has become even more agitated and continues to show signs of revolution. In an attempt to dissolve these feelings it was decided to hold a fraud election. With doing so it was their intent to make the bunnies believe they again support Laguna by having him run against an opponent so undesirable that there would be no possible way Laguna could lose. That opponent turned out to be none other than the self proclaimed “Last Great Technical Wrestler” and walking Ironman, Chris Storm. The question was raised “but wasn’t Laguna and Chris childhood friends?” Yes they in fact were childhood friends. Truth be told Chris was one of Laguna’s only friends. Being an idiotic annoyance does tend to hurt your social life. It was not until Laguna joined the service life that he became friends with Kiros and Ward, who are currently his only friends. So again the question remained “why did Chris agree to run against Laguna if they were friends?” Understand that though he can be an egoistical bastard Chris does understand that he is not well liked. There is almost no actual chance he could win this election. Each of the three in the room had their own opinions on the matter. “I still don’t like it” Kiros voiced. His opinion against this election had been stated since the beginning. Though he was against it his loyalty to Laguna forced him to continue to play a part in it. “That man is cruel, bitter, and seems to have a complete hatred for anything not a reflection of his own self.” Ward cut in, “that is what makes him so perfect for the position. This week and up until the election we will be running anti-Storm campaigns on UWF. Once the troops see the footage of all the things he has done they will come back to Laguna begging for him to lead them again.” It sounded like a solid plan but Kiros still had his doubts. “I don’t know, above all else the man is shrewd. He’s managed to use some important people and embarrass others. I just have a feeling there is something he plans to gain out of all of this.” Tapping his chin Kiros looked out of the arch window. Below them were the courtyard and tents. With the stench of treason strong the air looked ominously grey. “He is a friend doing a friend a favor” Laguna said, “nothing more”. Words spoken with more force were never heard. To Laguna, Chris was still a good friend and someone he trusted dearly. Chris was how Laguna even broke into the wrestling business. For him to betray him now in his darkest hour would be unthinkable. He wanted, nay, needed his fellow comrades to understand his situation and continued to support Chris Storm as a worthy opponent. Ward joined Kiros at the window and looked out with him. Below them they could see the troops now enjoying down time in the courtyard. There seemed to be more down time as of late. The soldiers were losing their disciple and training, a situation which only served to push them further toward the edge of the sword. Placing his large hand on Kiros’ shoulder, Ward spoke to him. “I know you are leery of Mr. Storm but rest assure that he has our best interest at heart.” Over and over Kiros heard these words but now like many other times they did not breach the doubt in his head. Ward stepped back and gave Kiros a reassuring smile. “Come on now, what does Chris have to even gain from winning the election? Oh he becomes the new leader of the Bunny Army of DOOM~! Chris prides himself on being a serious technical wrestler. It wouldn’t look right for him to preach about the greatness of a suplex while he has man-eating bunnies surrounding him.” Ward’s tone took a sharp change. No longer were the words informal and heart-felt but fun and encouraging. Kiros finally started to cave, “you’re right, I guess” he stated. Shoulders slumped; Kiros turned and looked at the two smiling faces of his friends. A soft sigh escaped him but he soon adorned his own smile. “I am too pessimistic for my own good. The plan seems fool proof.” “And we’ve got the fool right here to prove it!” Ward shouted as he wrapped a thick tree trunk like arm around Laguna’s neck and pulled him. Swinging his other arm upward Ward playfully hit Laguna on the top of the head and rubbed his balled up fist into his raven black hair. Laguna pushed free and fixed his hair. Placing both hands on the ends of his opened jacket he gave it a tug down showing two thumbs up. “Don’t forget who the commander here is. I’m not a fool; I’m your next President!” Laugher proceeded and the three friends headed down to the mess hall for a hearty meal. The day drew on turning to night. The black cloak of darkness covered the footsteps of plotting figures. Something was indeed a foot in the camp of the Bunny Army of DOOM~!
