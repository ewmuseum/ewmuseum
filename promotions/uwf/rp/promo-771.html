UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #771

Fate

Today (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — Unknown

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick Persia is once again champion in every sense of the word. He is a defending champion. He is a fighting champion. He deserved every bit of his championship gold. Nobody else has had the THC Title for so long as he did. And the reason for all of this? The Dutch Destroyer. Once upon a time ago he was told the magical words: “The world’s your oyster”. That’s without a shadow of a doubt the truth. He has the world in his back pocket now. Finally the best of the best, he’s ready to go at it against Killer Dude. Where did I hear that name before? You’re right, he was the boyfriend of that faggot named Kenny Mercury. Now he is all alone in a place full of enemies. Nick Persia is definitely Killer Dude’s enemy. After weeks and weeks of terrorising the Dutch Destroyer Nick Persia finally gets the chance to even the score. That’s exactly what he will do. Nick Persia will even the score. Nick Persia: “So long I’ve been waiting for this one chance. This one single chance I would get on gaining my revenge. My revenge for all the suffering. I’ve bled because of you. I’ve lost some matches because of you. And now I have the chance of making you pay for every single act you did. Not only will you pay for what you have done to me, but you will pay what you have done to the UWF also.” That was definitely true. People are short of memory. But all the sneak attacks have been taking its toll on Nick Persia. He was sick of it, very sick. Nick Persia: “Last time you might have found your way out of alive of our confrontation, but then again I was pretty occupied with other people as well, and I had to leave the match. However, that is all gone now. Any hope you had on survival is now GONE. Did you really think I forgot about you? Did you really think I forgot about that chair shot? Just so you know, I didn’t forget. I didn’t forget about you or your acts.” Nick Persia remembered himself of the sneak attacks of Kenny Mercury and Killer Dude together, also known as The BlackStar Revolution. Nick Persia: “You were able to get away with it time after time, but that time is now ended. Face it, you have run out of luck. Your days are numbered. Because I am looking for revenge. I don’t care whether you are going to bring you’ve had a match before this, I’m still going to hit you as hard as you can. Because I have no mercy for you. I pity you.” We should all pity him. The destiny Nick Persia laid out for him looked far from nice. Nick Persia: “Apocalypse Now, that’s basically what I have in store for you. Do you know what the Apocalypse is? It’s the dramatic ending of the world as described in the bible. But you could also call it Ragnarok I guess. The battle of the Gods that says that the end of all Gods will come and give new, peaceful life to this world. Do you believe? If you do, start praying.” His doom was already set in motion. There was nothing Killer Dude could do now, his fate is sealed. Nick Persia: “You can say what ever you want. You can claim anything you want. You can say you lost due to some nasty tricks, but we all know the truth. You failed in your attempts to stop us. For that, you are no more than an helpless ant. Your days have gone down. I already told you how your pathetic life would end. I will give you a warrior’s death quick and swiftly. But do not think you won’t suffer. This night UWF will be stained with YOUR blood.” Nick Persia hated this man; Killer Dude. He would do anything to make an end to his career. Nick Persia: “I really regret the fact that you have to face Clyde first, because I wouldn’t love anything more than publicly humiliate you. May the whole world see it. May the whole lockerroom see it. People are still thinking I am a joke, some kind of fool. I didn’t get so far because of luck, or because I have so much money but because of pure skill. That’s what most of you lack. I don’t see you all reaching the GTT 4 Bracket Finals!” There was another who did, Seymour Almasy. He was an obstacle, but he would be dealt with on a later time. And Angelo Deville, whose fate is now in doubt with the rumours concerning Rich Rollins. Nick Persia: “I didn’t see you reach anything. You did not climb anything besides Kenny mercury’s back! You are nothing more than the lowest of the lowest. You are lesser than scum. You make me sick to my stomach. You’re like a disease to the UWF. I will cure the UWF. I will get rid of you. I will defeat you. I will beat you, and when I do that I will make sure you’re days can be expressed in days. Finally, we’ll understand one another, Killer Dude.” He would teach his sorry ass until he wished he didn’t have an ass because of all the pain. Nick Persia: “I know what you are wondering now. What’s his trick? My trick is nothing more than something you have already met before. Yes, it’s my infamous Dutch destroyer. You will feel my rather. My pain will become your pain. Your plan will back fire. I will make it 2-0. You are going down. But don’t despair, since you are not the only one. In the future they will say the following about my finisher: “Is this how the mighty have fallen?” We’d all be obligated to say yes. So don’t worry and sleep tight. Bring anything you have. At least I will.” [With a sinister look in his eyes Nick Persia starts to walk away from the scene and the cameras fade out to the commercials.]
