UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #166

The Debut of the legend of El Chupacabra

8 Jan 2003

Mike Montgomery — Mexico. Luchadores are involved, so it has to be Mexico.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So if you weren't paying attention when you first opened this promo, here's the deal: You're seeing the first promo from the man who thinks he's the greatest thing to hit the UWF. No, I'm not talking about Ruff Ryder... Jamie Shock? No. Malcolm Kennedy? No! Is Lew Spectre FINALLY coming back? NO!! Dammit, I'm talking about a *real* legend! The man Mexican wrestlers everywhere look up to, he can make or break a career (usually just his own), he is truly a God amongst men, a legend amongst legends, he is EL CHUPACABRA!! Who? El Chupacabra. Greatest wrestler to emerge from the city of Punta Del Este. Who else is from there? Okay, so he's the only wrestler to emerge from the party city of Punta Del Este. Can I continue? Fine, but I don't get how he's a legend since I've never heard of him. Let's be honest. Just about the only people who'd have seen his previous TV work spend most of their time with Natalie Portman blow up dolls. Now if you don't mind... Mmm...Natalie Portman... Oy. Alright, I'll continue this for the benefit of those with an IQ above that of a T.W.A.T viewer, let's continue this promo. Now as I was trying to say, we now mark the debut of the great El Chupacabra. Here's the deal, we're at a bar known as 'Lucha-Libations.' Okay the name sounds lame, but it's a good play on words. Let's get to the fun... Our masked marvel sidles up to the bar and waits for the bartender to come over. And waits...and waits...and...well you get the idea. 'Bartender!' He finally shouts. The barkeep, (I don't know why I'm calling him a barkeep since this isn't british, but who cares) who conveniently looks exactly like Cheech in the last two El Mariachi films (if you want creativity, go watch a Beelzebozo promo okay?), finally bothers to at least look towards El Chupacabra. 'What do you want essai?' he asks. 'Do you not know who I am?!' 'Frankly no. What do you want to drink?' 'You have no heard of me? I am a God among men! I am the greatest Luchadore ever! I am a legend among legends! I am...' (We're going to skip ahead because we both know you already read this stuff earlier) '...the great El Chup...' Clearly exasperated, the bartender finally walks over, slams his hands down on the bar and glares at El Chupacabra, '...I'll have a tequila.' El Chupacabra meekly finishes. Rolling off a string of hispanic curses that we won't repeat for the benefit of sensitive ears (and the simple fact I don't actually *know* any hispanic curses), the bartender leaves to fetch his drink. 'El Chupacabra cannot understand this. He is a true legend of the sport of kings! Tales of his conquests should have spread far and wide! Women want to love me! Men want to be me! But clearly I am not heard of! Perhaps when I make my debut against the Fool in the UWF, the legend shall finally spread!' The bartender returns with a tequilla bottle, a lime, salt and a shot glass. He slams the bottle on the bar (violent bartender, isn't he?) and asks 'Shouldn't you be training for the match or something?' 'Train? What are you talking about? El Chupacabra does not need to train! He does not need to practice! For he is EL CHUPACABRA! None can stand before the legend of El Chupacabra! He is greatness personified, truly legendious' (hey, this is one story teller not against gimmick infringement), 'men want to be him, women want him!' The bartender picks up El Chupacabra and throws him across the room. 'You shall pay for your insolence! None may lay a hand on the legend of El Chupacabra!' He mutters before losing consciousness.
