UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #597

The Japanese Interview (Entire)

4 Aug 2004

The Plague — Japan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera pans around showing off the scenic views of Tokyo.] Voice: 'Hello there UWF, Welcome to Japan. It seems you are coming to our great country, and let me be the first to say I hope you enjoy your stay.' [Camera continues to pan around until you see a Japanese man wearing a business suit, glasses and a tie, he has black hair.] Voice: 'I am Akio Takahashi, and I am a journalist for Pro Japan Wrestling Magazine and we are putting together a article on The UWF coming to Japan.' [Standing in the parking lot of the arena.] Akio Takahashi: 'Yes, UWF and Japan, hopefully a relationship that will not be a one night stand. Tonight is a special day too, I am actually about to film my first TV Show on Japanese TV, and it is a wrestling show. And tonight we will be the first episode all about The UWF.' [Akio Takahashi begins to walk towards the Dome. There is a figure standing by a big billboard promoting UWF's Upcoming Pay-Per-View, Terror in Tokyo.] Akio Takahashi: 'Look a fan, lets go get some words from him.' [As the camera gets closer, the figure can be seen with long black hair, in a ponytail, a black shirt with a Japanese Symbol on it, cutoff camo shorts, black sneakers. Akio approaches the figure and taps him on the shoulder.] Akio Takahashi: 'Excuse me sir you know UWF?' Person: 'You could say that.' [Person turns around slowly. It's Plague, who hasn't changed too much he just looks a lot happier.] Akio Takahashi: 'You looking forward to this event sir?' [Plague standing with a huge smile on his face.] Plague: 'Hell's Yeah!! Aren't you?' [Fades out.] ------------------------------------------------------------ Unseen Footage: During Second Match Akio Takahashi: 'Well I'm Akido Takahashi and we're filming and this is actually being sent to UWF. We are doing show about UWF in Japan. I'm sure they'd enjoy hearing from a fan.' [Plague laughs and waves to the camera.] Plague: 'Oh really ironic huh?' Akio Takahashi: 'What you mean.' Plague: 'Well...' [Plague waves again.] Plague: 'Hi everyone watching surprized to see me again? Akio I wrestle in The UWF. I just wasn't happy. I was making others happy but not me. I just needed to get out of America.' Akio Takahashi: 'Wow UWF superstar in Japan what timing. Let's go into the arena.' [Walking backstage at TokyoDome.] Plague: 'So you get UWF here in Japan? I never even tried to find it.' Akio Takahashi: 'Actually we started getting shows after hearing that The UWF was coming here.' [Plague shakes his head.] Plague: 'Wow I can't believe it. I haven't even been following it since I left.' Akio Takahashi: 'World Champ Josh Dunn.' [Plague laughs in a shocked/non-believing way.] Plague: 'Wow that Commie bastard. Congrats Dunn you deserve it. At least now they have a World Champ I beat.' Akio Takahashi: 'Rumble in Japan too.' [Plague grins.] Plague: 'Now that brings back memories. Akio I think I might just have to get tickets for that show.' Akio Takahashi: 'Great show indeed.' [Reach curtain leading to the ring.] [End Unseen Footage.] ------------------------------------------------------------ [Footage for SAS.] [Akio and Plague at curtain just before heading to ring.] Plague: 'Wow it's so quiet. I'm so use to hearing this place packed checing out some Japanese Wrestling. It's such a privalige to be backstage at this legendary arena.' Akio Takahashi: 'Well it gets better.' [Akio and Plague emerge from the backstage area to a packed arena. The ring has a table and a chairs set up in the middle.] Akio Takahashi: 'We weren't expecting anyh guests tonight but we changed it since we ran into you.' [Plague and Akio walk down to the ring and step in. The capacity crowd is cheering a tear falls down Plague's face. Plague sits down and wipes the tear from his face.] Plague: 'A little emotional Akio.' [Camera shows the packed crowd cheering.] Akio Takahashi: 'Ladies and gentleman from The UWF it's The Plague.' [The cheering is deafening.] Plague: 'Thank you Japan thanks very much.' [Fades out.] ------------------------------------------------------------ Unseen Footage: During Third Match [Plague sitting along side Akio the crowd still cheering.] Plague: 'Japan is great. I came here a few months back and getting away from it all feels even better. I'm more relaxed and even training in a Dojo now to spice up my style.' Akio Takahashi: 'Well Plague you are UWF star please tell us a little about yourself.' [Plague gets red and sighs.] Plague: 'Ok well I am twenty-five and I started wrestling in The GIWF in 2001. I was TV Champ. I worked my ass off there as well being in a bunch of grueling matches. My career was about to go somewhere when the company just died. No more career no nothing. And that's really all I like to talk about. Those days are behind me now. It hit me hard being a young athelete just coming up in the game. A few huge matches some crazy feuds. But that all ended and here I am now.' Akio Takahashi: 'How bout The UWF?' Plague: 'The UWF was great. Hell it is probably still great. I have to say that they have got the best talent.' [Plague nods his head and smiles.] Plague: 'Yeah even with Dunn as Champ I bet it's still the same ol' UWF. And that is a reason why I am here and not in America. It's the same ol' bull shit there. Here it is great. No Bull shit in Japan.' Akio Takahashi: 'Thanks Plague glad you enjoy it here.' Plague: 'Yeah especially the ladies know what I'm sayin Akio.' Akio Takahashi: 'True Plague Japanese women very attractive.' Plague: 'So The Regal Rumble is in Japan too. I can't believe it. There gonna make a killing here. All that Yen.' [Plague smiles.] Plague: 'Great if anyone deserves it it's The UWF. Finally they're getting some recognition. So how bout a clip Akio you all want to see The Plague in action Japan?' [Crowd cheers.] Plague: 'That's right studio it better be a good one to.' [Back to Plague who is holding up three fingers.] Plague: 'And that's when I won my third Hardcore Title.' [Crowd cheers.] Plague: 'That's right I am great.' [Cuts back to Hayes.] [Hayes is seen with a pissed off look on his face.] Hayes: 'That's right Plague you were great. The key word there Plague is were great.' [Back to screen.] Akio Takahashi: 'You leaving UWF why? It seemed you were going far you almost won World Title.' [Plague chuckles at the statement. You can see Plague's temper rising.] Plague: 'Funny you brought that up Akio.' [Plague clears his throat.] Plague: 'Akio I like you man. You have been on my good side the whole interview. But you do need to learn what to and not to say. If this was The UWF and an announcer said some shit like that he would get clocked. But you I'll tell you a little something. I wasn't ready to take that leap Akio.' [Plague takes a deep breath.] Plague:'Here goes. I wanted the spotlight Akio. I wanted it bad but I couldn't grasp it. Anyone who knows anything about my stint in The UWF could tell you that. I had plenty of huge matches and I was in my handful of Main Events. It's pressure Akio plain and simple. The pressure to perform twice a week. Being a huge star having no privacy will get to you. Sometimes you just got to get away. My feud with Tsunami Oni was great but it could have been so much more. No World Title for Plague. I choked Akio. It's hard for me to say this but it is true. Now I might just be done. But on the other hand someday down the line you might see Plague in a wrestling ring one more time. One person I'd like to get back in the ring with would be Tsunami Oni or Jamie Shock. But both of them are gone so that scratches that.' [Scene fades out.] ------------------------------------------------------------ Unseen Footage: The Rest. [Plague and Akio talking following WNU Footage.] Akio Takahashi: 'So ever think about wrestling in Japan.' [Plague looks happy with that question.] Plague: 'Akio I love Japan. And I would love to show Japan what I am made of but I haven't wrestled in over two months. And my last displays were terrible. I lost to Nick Persia damn.' [Plague shakes head with a dissappointed look on his face.] Plague: 'I love wrestling. I'd be honored to wrestle in Japan but not now. I'm just not ready.' Akio Takahashi: 'So leaving UWF was that stress related?' [Plague shakes head.] Plague: 'No there was a lot of different factors rolled all into one giant decision to leave. I mean they had Corporal Punishment coming back. Mike Montgomery was Champ. Stable Wars was coming up and I wasn't going to be in it. I was nowhere fighting rookies for a title I won countless times. I don't even know why. I just thought that was what everyone wanted to see. So I did it. I beat the rookies won the title for a third time. I shut up all the non-believers in The UWF. That's all I needed to do for me.' Akio Takahashi: 'What Plague do now?' Plague: 'Well I am doing motion capturing for an upcoming wrestling game here in Japan. It is all ficticious wrestlers. Basically I am doing all the moves for the creators it is real fun.' Akio Takahashi: 'You enjoy it?' [Plague shrugs his shoulders.] Plague: 'It brings in the Yen if that's what you mean.' [Plague laughs jokingly.] Plague: 'This whole experience in Japan is quite a learning period for me. A kind of self-discovering experience. No phone no contacts. I mean I check voicemail which is in Philly but that's all. Mostly family leaves important messages for me. They realize what I am going through right now.' [Plague sits looking around the arena at all the people.] Plague: 'It truely is amazing Akio. The Japanese reallylove wrestling.' [Akio smiles.] Akio Takahashi: 'We sure do Plague. And that is why we are looking forward to August 15th. The UWF in Japan. The Regal Rumble more American Wrestling coming over to Japan.' Plague: 'Yeah the rumble is great. I never won it but it is a great event. A shot at the World Title at the UWF's premeire event Live and Loud. Last year I got involved with the World Title Match but wasn't wrestling for it. The Rumble is great cause anyone can win it. People will do whatever it takes to get that shot trust me I know. Wrestling can end careers mine almost ended numerous times. It's rough Akio.' Akio Takahashi: 'Plague since this is being sent to UWF Headquarters in Chicago do you want to say any last words to your former collegues?' [Plague smiles.] Plague: 'Yeah I guess I might see you in Japan. Maybe we can hang out and have some drinks. Shoot the shit and stuff. Maybe I'll be back. But for now enjoy life. Don't let the bull shit get you down. Later America.' [Akio thanks Plague. They shake hands and the crowd cheers as the scene fades out.]
