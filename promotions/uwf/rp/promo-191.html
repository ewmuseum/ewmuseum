UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #191

Iron Lung is about to burst.

24 Jan 2004

Axel Action — Hospital

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****The scene opens up in a local hospital.Everything is white.The ceiling,the walls,the tile on the floor.All white.The only thing that's not white are the scrubs that the doctors are wearing as they're rushing a patient to the ER.The head doctor of the group pulls the face mask off,revealing himself as none other than Axel Action.He looks into the camera.**** Axel:You know,this is quite an experience.I come here to cut a promo for my match tomorrow night,and I end up getting to participate in a surgery.I don't know what the hell I'm doing,so I'm just going to do what I know best. ****Axel walks inside the ER and walks over to the operating table.He asks for an air pump.He inserts it into the guy's lung.He pumps air into it until it bursts.**** Axel:Shit!I killed him!Oh well...Just tell his family that his lung exploded.Speaking of lungs,I believe my match this Sunday is against some douchebag named Iron Lung.I mean,does he really have an iron lung,or is he just some guy who thinks he IS an iron lung?No matter.He'll end up like all the other guys who have challenged me.They will end up being pinned 1,2,3 in the middle of the ring.He's going to end up like this guy's lung that I destroyed... ****Axel starts to take off the medical scrubs,and he tosses them in the trash bin.He exits the ER.**** Axel:Man,I'll tell you something...You don't want to get in the ring with me.I'll make you hurt in places you didn't even know you had.But you'll find that out soon enough.And as for everyone else,you know that chicks go whack for Action Packed!!! ****The scene fades to black as Axel cracks a smile while looking into the camera.****
