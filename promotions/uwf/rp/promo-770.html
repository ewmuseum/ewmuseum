UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #770

Killer Dude Fishing

4 Nov 2004

Killer Dude — Can you guess?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in to a screen filled with green grass, and a hilly area. The camera pulls out, showing that it is indeed the side of a hill in the countryside of Wisconsin. The camera pans to the right and comes across a creek, which the camera follows until it reaches the bank of the creek.... and a human's foot with a piece of string tied around his big toe. We zoom out to reveal that the foot belongs to the one, the only, Killer Dude. Killer Dude lays back on the bed of the creek, wearing a fishing cap, and a bunch of fishing gear.) Killer Dude: I've gotta tell you Punk, I don't really think that the fish are biting today. (The camera zooms out, about a foot more, and it shows Killer Dude's best friend, Punk Rock, lying a little closer to the creek, with once again, a string tied around his right big toe. His string is also leading into the creek, as he is obviously trying to catch a fish with his toe.) Killer Dude: This is the life, ain't it? A sunny day in the countryside, a nice breeze, the birds are chirping...... Punk Rock: Yeah, ain't it? (Killer Dude sits up and spits the straw out) Killer Dude: Oh, who the fuck am I kidding? This sucks about as much dick as a man in jail! All I have been thinking about since Live and Loud is getting my hands on certain men, and smashing their heads! Three of them if you want to keep track! First, it's Nick Persia, then it's Seymour Almasy, and then out of nowhere, here comes fuckin' CLYDE!? Where the fuck did he come from? And all I can do is sit around in the fuckin' country side of fuckin' Wisconsin, counting the days till Universal so I can get my hands on all three of you assholes! What's going on, really? Where the fuck did Clyde come from? When did he come back from the gates of hell? First, he has the gall to come out during MY match, and cost me the THC Title, and then he has the balls to join up with fuckin' SEYMOUR ALMASY!? (The camera zooms in on Killer Dude, who removes his hat, and throws it on to the ground) Killer Dude: You just fucked with the wrong man, Clyde! Now, I'm just counting the days until I can get my hands on you, Clyde, til I can rip your fuckin' eyes out and shove them down your fuckin' throat, you little bitch! I won't let you beat me again! And now, moving on to those god damn fools, Nick Persia and Seymour Almasy. Yes, I'll admit, at Live and Loud, you both got extremely lucky and pinned me. I'll admit to that! But the way that you did that Seymour, was extremely unfair damnit! And now.... hold on a sec.... (Killer Dude gets up and goes over to Punk Rock, as the string tied to his toe is beginning to twitch) Killer Dude: Well done, Punk! I think you might've caught a big one! (Killer Dude grabs the string and reels it in. After a few moments, he gets to the end of the string, revealing a hook with only a half eaten bit of bait on it) Killer Dude + Punk Rock: DAMNIT! Killer Dude: Bad luck there, Punk. Better luck next time. Punk Rock: Fuck you, man. (Killer Dude turns back to the camera) Killer Dude: Clyde, Nick Persia, Seymour Almasy, trust me fools.... I won't make the same mistake I made at Live and Loud! I will not let you guys get away from me. I will make you pay. And it all begins at Universal, when I take every single one of you're titles! (Killer Dude picks up his hat and walks off the scene as the camera fades to snow)
