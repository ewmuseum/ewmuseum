UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #240

Malcolm Kennedy

Feb 25th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Locker Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Another heartbreaker. How can one man continue to go on match after match, let down after let down, defeat after defeat? Some consider it impossible, But Malcolm Kennedy puts all of that to rest. At supremacy Ezra Kazee beat Malcolm for the third time and advanced in the intercontinental title tournament. Malcolm can not be pleased at all. *** <<< The scene opens up in a dimly lit locker room. At the other side of the room the figure of a man can be seen sitting on a bench. One single lit hangs above him, slightly rocking back and forth. The camera zooms in to see that the man wears a pair of dark blue Adidas wind pants with white stripes. The man also wears a black hooded sweatshirt. His head in his hands, his jet black hair matted and tangled. He sighs. >>> Man: “ I blew it. I had my chance to get what I wanted, and I blew it. Plain and simple.” “On top of that, you come waltzing back in before my match and try and reform what all fell apart? I mean I respect what your trying to do, but why before my match? How am I supposed to focus with that on my mind? Screw it, I cant blame you though. I take complete responsibility for what happened, and Ill just have to move on.” <<< The man lifts his head from his hands and his face is revealed. Malcolm Kennedy’s face can be seen. His scratchy beard poorly shaven, bags under his eyes, his face plainly expressing his lack of rest. He coughs a few times >>> Malcolm Kennedy: “ And where does this all leave me now? With a tag team match against these retard anarchists and a partner that has made an offer of reform. Not exactly what I had in mind. You see, almost a month ago a good friend of mine got up and walked out of the UWF. Just up and went on his way. Now I don’t know why Jamie did what he did, but he’s gone either way. As soon as Jamie left, bam Ruff Ryder goes solo. Gives up all hope, and loose’s his mind a few weeks later. And all that freaking time, where is Malcolm Kennedy? Right here doing what he loves day in day out, week after week, not complaining about a thing. Then last week before a HUGE match with Ezra Kazee, Ruff, you come walking back into my career. Offering me a chance to reform the Prime Alliance. Throwing a shirt on the ropes and just walking back out the door without hesitating. Just one month before you were saying screw the Prime Alliance. You said screw all the stables, that you were going solo. And I had no problem with that, I just let it all slide out of my mind and stayed focused as best I could on my match week after week. I’ve become my own One Man Army. The SOLO destruction crew. And still prooving that I can run with the best of them. Im earning the respect I’ve wanted, and the respect I’ve deserved. And now, Ding Dong, you show up wanting to team back up. I just don’t know If I can do that man, I don’t know if that is what I need.....” <<< He puts his head back in his hands. >>> “ Am I saying no? Absolutely not. But the fact of the matter is, I wont know anything for a while, and it surely wont be before SAS. So lets just focus on our match for now, the finer details we can work out later. This match will help prove to me just how serious you are about it all.” <<< He sighs loudly. >>> “Which brings me to my opponents at SAS.” “Anarchy” <<< His tone is one of disgust. >>> “ You guys sit there and tell Ruff Ryder not to underestimate you, because that will cost him the match. Then you turn right back around and say that it will be a cake walk for you two. That you wont have the slightest problem beating Ryder and myself. WHOA WHOA WHOA!!!” <<< Malcolm jumps to his feet.>>> “Do you not realize just who you are dealing with? I am the fastest rising superstar in the history of the UWF. And Ruff Ryder is a world title contender. And you two jerk-offs think that your gonna beat us, bam!, just like that? You talk about all the titles you two held together in other feds, and how many teams you beat in the past.” “WAKE UP CALL!” “ Oh come on guys! You cant be serious. Need I remind you that you are in the UWF? I mean, I think its really time you realize that your past don’t mean jack squat here. I mean you can sit there and pat your back about how good you two USED to be, but fellas, that was then, this is now.” “ You talk about being the next tag champions like you already have me and Ryder beat. You need to realize that yeah sure, maybe in those other feds you two were somebody, that you two were true contenders, but now, here in the UWF, you two are nothing more than a couple of push overs. Just a pair of two bit, no talent, wanna be wrestlers who got enough sympathy from the UWF that the UWF was willing to hire them just so they could get payed to get their ass kicked night in, night out. Fellas, this Sunday at Supremacy, you two will get a big time reality check. You two will learn just what you are dealing with here in the UWF. And just what the Top Notch Malcolm Kennedy is truly all about.” <<< Malcolm starts to walk off and hesitates. He opens his bag and pulls out the Prime Alliance shirt Ryder offered him. >>> “Ryder, time will only tell. Lets just get this match over with first. Then we can talk.” <<< Malcolm drops the shirt on the bench and zips up his bag. He then walks off as the light continues to slightly sway back and forth. The scene fades to black. >>>
