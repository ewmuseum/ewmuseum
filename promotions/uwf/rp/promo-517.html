UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #517

Time is Running Out

8 Jul 2004

Misfit — not applicable

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Time is Running Out Artist: Muse Album: Absolution [White fog begins to swirl in amongst the darkness, bringing with it a low, repetitive, digital hum. Cymbal clicks and reverse drum beats filter in through the hum, creating an almost poppy but undeniably dark rhythm. A small, black & white image of a dark figure sitting beneath a window fades into the lower-right side of the screen as a final click gives way to the lyrics.] I think I'm drowning... Asphyxiating... [The figure pushes himself to his feet, walking across the screen through the fog. As his face becomes the only thing visible, a sadistic smile crosses his lips. With most of his veiled in shadow, only the smile and a dark-bagged eye are visible but the face is clear enough to recognize; it is Misfit.] I wanna break the spell That you've created [Fog overtakes the screen again for a moment before we see another image of Misfit, sitting on a wooden chair in the old basement. The image is from one of his early UWF promos, and we see him raise a knife and slice it through his arm.] You're something beautiful... [After the previous lyrics have been sung, the image of Misfit in the chair is quickly replaced by an image of him standing in a UWF ring. He wears a sick smile with the look of a psychopath in his eyes, standing over a fallen foe who has words carved into his chest. Blood drips from the last letter as Misfit stands over him, knife in hand.] A contradiction... [That image is again replaced, this time with Misfit backdropping Digital Mortality into a dumpster upon his return to the UWF and debut with drowningPOOL. Quick flashes of the scene are shown to the music as Misfit, Slasher, and Unforgiven dismantle Digimon.] I wanna play the game... I want the friction... [Lew Spectre stands at the top of the entry way, microphone in hand with Misfit in the ring.] You... will be... The death... of me... [Misfit is shown giving an Evenflow DDT to Lew Spectre at the Regal Rumble, one of his final appearances with the company before disappearing.] Yeah, you... will be... The death... of me... [Ben Jericho stands in the ring, shaking hands with Kenny Mercury. Lew Spectre stands behind them, a satisfied smile on his face.] Bury it... [Ben Jericho and Kenny Mercury attack Misfit in a parking lot. Kenny delivers the first blow, then Misfit looks up at Jericho execting help. Jericho then begins raining down punches on Misfit, blood pouring from his head as he hits the concrete.] I won't let you bury it... [Jericho drives a screwdriver into Misfit's flesh.] I won't let you smother it... [Misfit returns the favor, driving a screwdriver into Jericho's shoulder.] I won't let you murder it... [Several quick flashes of Misfit and Jericho battling it out come through the fog, showing their fued from the time when Jericho joined up with Mercury in the Black Star Revolution, turning his back on Misfit. Jericho delivers the Unforgiven DDT mk. II to Misfit just as the heavy guitars kick into the song.] Our time is running out... [Quick visuals of Lew Spectre's actions in his vendetta against Misfit come through the fog, including Spectre forcing Misfit to team with him in a Tag Team Title match against BSR.] Our time is running out... [BSR dismantle Misfit as Spectre looks on from the corner.] You can't push it underground [Misfit screams in pain, trapped in Mercury's Spellbinder.] We can't stop it screaming out [Misfit nails Spectre with Stigmata, who joins Mercury and Jericho unconcious on the mat.] I wanted freedom... [Spectre and Misfit stand side-by-side, each holding a tag team title belt.] But I'm restricted [Misfit has Jericho on his knees, begging for mercy. Both are bloodied and beaten, and Misfit extends his hand to help Jericho up.] I tried to give you up... [Jericho delivers a low blow to Misfit.] But I'm addicted [Images of Misfit being beaten and brutalized flash quickly through the fog.] Now that you know I'm trapped... Sense of elation... [Misfit stands at the top of the entry ramp, a tag team title belt lying at his feet.] You'll never dream of breaking this fixation... [A few images of Misfit wandering through forests, sitting in dark corners shaking, and looking around confused in the middle of an open field show through the fog.] You... will squeeze... the life... out of me... [A 'highlight reel' of footage from dP's appearance on WNU shows through the fog, ending with Misfit delivering A Fire Inside to Kenny Mercury as the vocalist sings the last line.] Bury it... I won't let you bury it... I won't let you smother it... I won't let you murder it... [Seemingly hundreds of images flash through the fog of Misfit in action across the globe. He delivers a shooting star press from inside the ring, over the ropes, to an opponent on the outside in Japan. He delivers Stigmata to an opponent onto a bed of tacks during a death match in Singapore.] Our time is running out... Our time is running out... You can't push it underground We can't stop it screaming out [Misfit sits alone in the basement, his head resting in his hands.] How did it come to this? [Fog fills the screen again for a moment, moving and swirling violently as the chaos of the song plays. As the pianos kick in, the images fade into the picture again. Clips of Misfit training intercut with clips of him slowly going further and further towards the edge as he sits alone play.] You... will suck... the life... out of me... [The song continues to play as Misfit is shown truding through wastelands, clips of his career flashing every so often. Bury it... I won't let you bury it... I won't let you smother it... I won't let you murder it... [Misfit comes to the end of the wastelands, falling to his knees and looking up at the sky. The fog again overtakes the screen.] Our time is running out... Our time is running out... You can't push it underground We can't stop it screaming out [Misfit stands in the center of a completely black room, wearing the attire he was seen in at WNU.] How did it come to this? [The fog quickly overpowers the imagery, wiping everything away before fading to black. A heavily distorted and reverbed voice echoes through the darkness.] Welcome back... I hope you enjoy my nightmare...
