RR: Changed Man?

13 Aug 2004

Jamie Shock — Many places

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Sunday, Jamie Shock faces one of the most gruelling events in the UWF: the Regal Rumble. The biggest match in wrestling history, which features 60 men, will determine the number one contender for the UWF World Heavyweight Championship. That contender will get his/her shot at the title at Live And Loud, the ‘super bowl’ of the UWF’s pay per view calendar. The wrestling world is anticipating the Rumble. It was named the match of the year in the 2003 awards ceremony. It’s the one chance to see every single UWF roster member in action, colliding against … well, everyone. We today though for a short while will look at Jamie Shock. He is no stranger to the Regal Rumble. Everyone had high hopes for the then fan favourite. Unfortunately Jamie got tossed over the top rope soon after he entered, shattering his Rumble hopes. In that Regal Rumble though, Shawn Eclipse, probably the greatest PWW wrestler ever, dominated everyone, knocking them down like dominos. It was at that pay per view, where Lewis Spectre decided that this iron man, for the sake of the other roster members, had to be stopped. Eclipse was beaten and the title was stolen from him, ending his short-lived UWF career. Jamie Shock lost an ally, and a friend. The fact that if you win the Rumble you face the champion for the title is insignificant to most wrestlers. The thing that makes most wrestlers want to win, and in Jamie’s case this is true, is that the match takes place in the main event of Live And Loud. Shock doesn’t want to be remembered for his last reign, he was bitter, twisted, and evil. He wasn’t a man; he was a scheming coward that after a short while was so used to outside interference to help him pick up the win that he, in the end, lost to Petey The Chimp in the famous match which lost him almost all his dignity and pride. The match where he lost the title wasn’t good on a wrestling level, not by far. It was Maurice, Petey, and Shock in a triple threat where Jamie decided to beat Maurice senseless with the chair enabling Petey to sneak up behind Shock and perform a low blow type move. But lets not go into that. So if you take that into account, and also how bad Shock was in the ring at that time, it’s no wonder he wants to redeem his legacy. It’s ironic that Jamie won the title at the ‘Legacies’ UWF Pay Per View, where he defeated Tsunami Oni and his old rival The Plague, as it marked the start of where such a good legacy tumbled into the gutter. For Jamie, this rumble means everything. How people see him in the future almost depends on it. ---------/*~*\--------- The scene opens in a dark room. The room is small, and square shaped, with white walls and a blue carpet. The window isn’t visible, as a thick blue blind has been wound down, blocking any possible light from outside entering the room. The room has few items: a leather chair, a table, and a computer on the table, with the main hard drive beneath. This is Jamie Shock’s study, although judging on the action in the room today, it’s doubtful any work goes on in this place at all. We see Jamie Shock sat in front of the computer. The flashing lights from the screen light up his face, thus showing his clenched teeth, his bulging eyes, and a single drop of sweat rolling down his cheek. The drop flies off his face as he launches his head to his side, letting out a grunt. He focuses back onto the screen. His fingers are bashing onto the keyboard, shaking the entire desk. The attention turns to the screen. We see three figures in UWF ring. It becomes apparent this is the 2003 summer hit of ‘UWF: The Game’ which came in limited supply to special UWF associates. On the screen, Corporal Punishment grabs Tsunami Oni by the throat, and launches him sideways into the turnbuckle. Oni crashes against the corner and falls to his rear, dazed. Three birds dance around his head. Jamie Shock jumps onto Corporal Punishments back, wrapping his arms around his head. Punishment flicks Shock over in a snap mere with ease. Jamie bounces across the ring. The Cpl. Taunts to the crowd. At the top of the screen, the Corporal’s ‘Injection’ Metre reaches full capacity. He grabs Jamie Shock and pulls him to his feet. Shock is stunned. ‘L-O-C-K-D-O-W-N T-I-M-E’ moves horizontally along the screen as The Corporal applies the lethal injection. In the room, Jamie bashes the keyboard, using his fist and arm as a club. “BASTARD!” he screams, “BLOODY FUCKING BASTARD!” he screams again. The keyboard starts to bounce towards the edge of the desk. He sits back in his chair, before moving the keyboard into the centre of the desk, retaining it’s original poise in front of him. On the screen, Corporal Punishment releases Shock. Jamie falls to his knees and onto the mat in a star shape. His body is flashing, symbolising he can be eliminated. The Corporal moves towards Jamie, but is surprisingly taken down by Tsunami Oni! In the room, Jamie jumps forward. The impossible has become possible, as for once Shock is thankful of the actions of the Devil From The Deep Blue Sea … so what if it’s a form of the devil as a 200-polygon man. On the screen, Corporal Punishment is thrown into the corner. Oni runs at him and jumps onto his head, before flipping back and tossing the Corporal into the centre of the ring; monkey tossing him! Oni quickly gets up and kicks the Corporal. Jamie Shock is still laid out in a star. The Corporal slowly gets up, where Tsunami is waiting for him. The Corporal though kicks Oni in the gut, and lifts him up into a gorilla press slam. The screen shakes as Jamie Shock whacks the keyboard in another reality. Tsunami Oni is thrown of the top rope. His name bar disappears from the bottom left of the screen, leaving just the Corporals, and Shock’s at the left and the right of our view. In the room, Jamie moans as his middle finger of his right hand begins to ache, as he presses the up arrow repeatedly. On the screen, Jamie Shock gets up. The tapping of the keyboard stops. Jamie Shock sprints at the Corporal, who has nowhere to go – so ducks, and flips Jamie over the top rope. “AHHHHHH BASTARD FUCKING BASTART CORPORAL BASTARD!” Jamie cries, as his head falls onto the keyboard beneath the view of Corporal Punishment raising both hands into the air. Shock turns off the screen and then turns off the computer by pressing the button on the hard drive. He slowly gets up, mentally, and physically drained. He leaves the room. ---------/*~*\--------- Sarah has seen all of Jamie’s adult life. She met him first when she was fourteen. He was celebrating his fifteenth year on the planet, and was throwing a party at his house while his parents were away in Scotland for the weekend. He had told his mates to, if they wished, bring their girlfriends, and hopefully their friends. He was a single boy coming to the end of puberty, and he wanted action. Sarah was the friend of Tommy’s girlfriend. Tommy was a friend of Jamie. The door opens to the Pederson household. Jamie Pederson is Jamie Shock’s real name. The smiling brit welcomed Tommy into the house, whose hand joined the one of his girl. “Alrite mate?” Jamie said, trying to make his yet-to-break voice as low as possible. Tommy nodded and proceeded to drag his girlfriend through the ‘yet to be plastered’ hall. Jamie took a look back onto the open street. That’s where he first caught a glance of her. He soaking air rose up in the wind, which brought the typically English rain through the door and onto Jamie. He didn’t mind though. She was so attractive; she could have got a job at Louis’s behind the counter. That’s just how glamorous she was. “Are you going to bloody let me in?” she roared. He slowly looked down. Holey hell – a mini skirt. Her tanned legs seemed to go on forever. Her right foot slowly rose off the welcome mat and … kicked him. “OW!” screamed Jamie, hopping backwards holding his shin. She forced her way past him. Jamie, bent over, slowly looked up as she walked past and into his living room. He stumbled to the stairs. “Holey hell I haven’t washed this morning!” he said, a little too loud, as he ran up the stairs. He almost proceeded to bring his parents bedroom door off its hinges as he forced his way into the room. He stopped dead in his tracks as his eyes bulged at the sight in front of him. Tommy and his girlfriend slowly turn their heads to Jamie. Jamie slowly walked backwards and shut the door. “Bloody hell, they waste no time do they,” he muttered, as he walked backwards into the bathroom. Knowing there was nobody down stairs to keep this girl company, he knew he had to be quick. He had no time to wash, and at that age didn’t know how much he’d sweat in the company of a girl he liked, so thought he didn’t need to. He grabbed the small bottle of ‘joop’ besides the bath. He quickly picked it up and poured it into his hands, before rubbing it on his wrists and on his neck. He moves over and looks at himself in the mirror. He gives a deep breath, before leaving the bathroom. Back in the living room, Sarah is bored. Her friend and Tommy have run upstairs to explore each other’s joy departments and she feels left out. That and the host of this so-called ‘party’ looked like a ballet dancer. Wait … no he looked kind of cute. “Oh there you are!” Sarah said as she looked at Jamie, the top of his shirt soaking with ‘joop’. He closed the door behind him and sat into the chair. Sarah yawned, a very bad sign. “How can you have a party with four people? Two of which only came to use your bed … Mr man?” Jamie smiled. “It’s Johnny, Johnny Stud,” said Jamie, smiling as he tried to look cool. Sarah stared blankly at ‘Stud’ in front of her. “More people are coming later, you’re an hour early … luckily for that time you have me all alone.” He said. Sarah sighed as she shook her head. “Oh really. Well Mr ‘Stud’ you cant get too close as I’m not available.” Jamie leant back on his chair. “I’m going out with Luke Hewit. I’m sure you’ve heard of him sometime.” This was terrible. Not only did he now smell like a girl as of all the ‘joop’ he’d put on, but this girl in front of him, who Jamie was already crazy over, was firstly treating him like a nobody, and secondly going out with the leader of a gang which did nothing but cause trouble on the estate. “He’s coming around in a minute,” she said. Jamie put his hands over his head. Luke Hewit, the ‘ardest boy in the neighbourhood was coming into his house. Jamie took his hands down and looked at the new TV his parents recently purchased. He then turned to Sarah. “Wait a second, he’s eighteen! – And you’re fourteen!” Sarah looked at Jamie blankly. His point was a good one, but it wasn’t wise arguing with the girlfriend of Luke Hewit, and he was due round in a minute, or even less. Jamie realised that he had to do something, or else before he knew it this so called party would turn into a disaster. It was a bad idea, he realised that now. Stupid Tommy, inviting the girlfriend of Luke Hewit to his house “Right, ok, that’s it.” Said Jamie, slowly standing up. “OUT!” He pointed to the door. Sarah’s eyes widened. Of course, Jamie still didn’t know her name. “You’re telling ME, to get out?” she said, slowly. Jamie couldn’t believe he was doing what he was doing, this girl, and this attractive beautiful girl … like he had a chance; he had to get her out so she wouldn’t bring Luke Hewit in. Luke Hewit: what a stupid name anyway. “Come on, out out out!” he said, grabbing her by the hand and forcing her out of the room, and dragging her down the hallway to the open door he had forgot to close. “Hey hunny!” said Sarah. Jamie stopped, and stared at Sarah. He shrugged. “What did you say?” said Jamie. He then knew. He couldn’t see the reflection in the mirror in front of him. Luke Hewit slowly walked into the hall. Jamie slowly turned to the eighteen year old, quickly releasing his grip from Sarah. Luke slowly shut the door behind him. Jamie smiled. “You!” said Hewit, slowly approaching Jamie. “What you doin’ touchin’ me lass?” he roared at Jamie, who regretted putting that ‘joop’ on even more now. Sarah shook her head. “Luke, honestly, he just wanted me to leave. He hasn’t done anything, and we haven’t. What, you think I’d do something with him? Oh don’t be stupid” Sarah said, defending and insulting Jamie at the same time. Luke slowly turned to Sarah. Sarah took a few steps back. “Shut up bitch” said Luke, before grabbing Sarah and pulling her towards him. Sarah screamed as Luke grabbed her by the neck. “Now what the hell have you two been doing behind MY back?” Jamie was angry. He was angry this beautiful girl was going out with such a paranoid loser, and that this loser looked like a woman beater. The first ounce of what we’d see in the future form of Jamie Shock made an appearance on that day. “Right. That’s it, … … … fuck off,” said Jamie, calmly. Luke slowly let go of Sarah and approached the host of this so-called party. “Did I just say that?” said Jamie, quickly turning round and sprinting up the stairs. Luke burst after him. ---------/*~*\--------- The Prime Alliance’s locker room. Jamie Shock and Ruff Ryder laugh at their antics on that night. They both hold two flamethrowers, strapped over their shoulders. It’s that infamous night where the Bunny Army Of DOOM~! Were torched, and turned to char grill. “Wow! That was amazing” Ruff Ryder said, smiling happily. Jamie, out of breath, nods. “Good god, I haven’t had so much fun since throwing former PWW roster members into dumpsters. God, who thought setting fire to Bunnies, would be so cool. “Erm, Jamie” said Sarah, stood in the corner of the room. “Can we have a word please?” Ruff Ryder shakes his head. “The look on Seymour Almasy’s face. He was like ‘what the fuck! That’s my opponent’ haha!” and Laguna! She was like “Oh my poor little pets!” Oh god, that’ll teach ‘em. “Excuse me, Jamie?!” said Sarah, annoyed nobody is taking any attention to her. Jamie laughs at Ryder. “Aye. Well, we set them straight. Comedic characters in the main event? No thank you. Seymour’s next on the list. Sarah walked over to Jamie. “For fucks sake listen to me!” There is a moment’s silence. “Looks like your slut needs you. I’ll just go grab a beer” Ruff Ryder said, laughing. Jamie laughed too, and nodded. Sarah scowled. “What do you want bitch?” said Jamie. ---------/*~*\--------- Jamie’s eyes had remained open for some time. He had been staring at Sarah for some time as she lay next to him. The clock shows 4:00am. Shock rolls over and looks up at the ceiling. The apartment himself and Sarah were staying in had a slanted ceiling, with a huge window stretching almost all the way across. For the three or so days they had been staying in Tokyo, as soon as Jamie left the hospital because of the vicious attack Kennedy gave him on SAS, prior to The Regal Rumble, Jamie hardly went to sleep. Instead he stayed awake and looked out at the city. He looked at the skyscrapers especially, behind which is the arena. The arena where he has a chance to redeem what he was. The only thing that matters at this time is how he will be remembered. It scares him when he thinks he could be remembered as some twisted.. “Prat.. What a prat I was” he slowly says, a voice hardly coming out. “I have to get to that championship again, I just have to. What else will I be remembered as then?!” He takes a deep breath as he recalls previous actions. “A … a bloody freak who could only win because of interference, a bloody freak who bullied, lied, and cheated. Urgh!” he moans. Jamie rolls back onto his side, looking at Sarah as she sleeps beside him. “I’m sorry for what I did to you my love,” he says as he slowly moves over and gives her a small kiss on the cheek. “I have changed. Honest.” ---------/*~*\--------- We’re in a park. It is days after Jamie’s so called ‘party’. Few changes have happened since that day, most probably because he hasn’t dared to go out. The reason: Luke Hewit. Jamie tried to escape that day, but instead got pulled down the stairs as he tried to run off, and got a black eye for his troubles. Sarah? Luke dragged her away. Jamie decided to do something about it. Jamie challenged Luke to a fight, which, as a thirteen year old would do knowing he’s going to face an eighteen year old, he’s regretting. Stupid parents, thinking he could be responsible if they went away for a weekend. Of course, Jamie could have let the Luke incident be forgot, but he’s still wound up about Sarah. Infact, he still doesn’t know her name, and she thinks he’s called Johnny Stud … that’s if she chose to believe that. No, Jamie’s angry. He’s obsessed with that girl he saw. “I’m a fool,” he muttered to himself. He waits. The time is 9.02pm. If this fight lasts longer than ten minutes, he’ll be in late for tea, and it’s Spaghetti Bolognaise tonight. “Well hello there” said a voice from behind. Jamie slowly turned around. It was Luke. “I can’t believe you’re doing this. Who the fuck do you think you are? This is just sad!” Jamie smiles. He doesn’t mean to, he just can’t think of anything else to do. “I’ll make this quick” said Luke, which comes as a good relief to Jamie, moments before the eighteen year old cracks Jamie in the jaw with a fierce right hand, sending Jamie to the floor in a moment. A moment for Luke is a lifetime for Jamie. He falls back, the pain not having time to set into his brain. Jamie crashes against the grass, luckily a soft landing from such a hard blow. Luke stands above him, seeming proud. What a sad git, he’s just hit someone two thirds of his age, what a loser. Of course, at the current moment that loser is in perfect position to inflict more pain, so Jamie wisely said nothing. He can’t say anything anyway, as his jaw has locked up. Before Luke inflicted more damage, a distant voice called out to him. Jamie can’t hear anything, it seems like the throbbing pain in his jaw has blocked his senses. “LUKE!” screams Sarah, as she approaches Jamie’s opponent. “Oh my god, you actually did it. WHY?! For gods sake he’s my age, and you’ve just knocked him out.” She continues, “What type of boy are you?!” She looks down at Jamie. Jamie’s eyes widen as he sees her. Luke frowns as he grabs Sarah. “Listen you slag, I bloody do not back down from challenges, you hear me?! I can’t help it if this nobhead decides to challenge him, that’s his mistake.” “Get off her,” said a voice from the ground. Luke and Sarah slowly look down at Jamie. Hewit smiles. “Oh yeah kid, what are you going to do? You put up less of a fight than cake being cut.” He said. “Now don’t tempt me to finish you off punk” Sarah tried to escape from Luke’s grasp but he wouldn’t let go. That is, until Jamie raised his leg into Luke’s groin. “OOO! You git!” said Luke, releasing Sarah and bending down. Jamie jumped up. Luke slowly turned to him. Jamie kicked Luke in the head, and then punched him in the stomach. No effect. Jamie dived to the floor as a fist flew at him. Once on the floor, Jamie knew he was in trouble. He brought every ounce of strength into his legs as he swiped them back into Luke’s ankle. Luke stumbled back and fell over. “Go Stud!” said Sarah. Luke sat up, and raised an eyebrow. Jamie leapt into the air and splashed onto Luke, causing him to scowl before he threw Jamie off him. As Luke got up, Jamie quickly slid under his legs, grabbing onto an arm in the process. Luke stood with his arm under his legs. Jamie then places one foot onto the back of Luke, forcing him to the floor, before splashing down onto Luke’s back and then grabbing his feet. Sarah watched in amazement as her former ‘hard’ boyfriend was almost reduced to tears as Jamie locked in an STF. “Hahaha” said Jamie as Luke waved his arms madly. After the ‘fight’, Jamie took Sarah home. Sarah’s dad turned out to be an ex wrestler, and the rest is history. As for Luke, well.. Actually, he got worse and ended up robbing a bank, but for the sake of this story, lets presume he’s a changed man. Does this seem a bit far-fetched? Well it happened … well, around those lines anyway. ---------/*~*\--------- “Jamie?” said Sarah, as she slowly woke up, turning to her side to realise nobody was there. She slowly sat up, the Tokyo city greeting her. Her boyfriend sits on the end of the bed, looking out at the city. “What’s wrong Jamie? Did you get no sleep again?” she asks, obviously concerned. Jamie slowly turns to her. He has two small bags under his eyes. He smiles as he feels the warmth of his awoken love for Sarah. “… I was just thinking, that’s all, well, that and waiting for the gym to open” He slowly stands up. “I’ll be off now, it’s almost nine. I’ll go do a bit of exercise and then take you out to lunch. I wont be too long love.” He grabs a training bag from beside the bed and heads out of the room. Sarah thinks to herself, and closes her eyes. “He has changed,” she says. ---------/*~*\--------- On Sunday, Jamie Shock faces 60 men. Although being the first entry into the Regal Rumble, he obviously still has hope. Although, what this could lead to isn’t certain. Any man, no matter how talented, would still be nervous going into the rumble as the first entry. Has Jamie Shock got some tricks up his sleeve to eliminate people with ease? – New friends perhaps? Or is he just trying to conceal the doubt. Obviously, we wont know until Sunday. Thank you for reading.
