WNG - Killer Dude has the match in the bag

6 Nov 2001

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killer Dude Built to Last The scene opens in Denver, Colorado where Killer Dude lives. Killer Dude is sitting on a bench outside of his house. Digital Mortality arrives in shot riding a horse. He is wearing a yellow cowboy style outfit. Killer Dude: Yes Digital, it looks like I have my match set up against Toxic Shock, and this pleases me. At first I thought that he was going to chicken out, but Clark set the match up before he could reply, but I am sure that he won't bother. And, earlier I was watching a Toxic Shock interview from before Sunday Afternoon Supremacy, but he never mentioned anything about his upcoming match with me. Could this be that he knows that I'm going to win? That he knows that he is going to have to cheat because that's the only chance in hell he'll have of winning? But you know what, I really don't give a damn, cause I can only look upwards and onwards, to our match at WNU. A scream is heard and as the camera turns around, you see Digital Mortality hanging by one foot, on an angry horse which is dragging him along the mud. He eventually breaks free and walks over to Killer Dude and procedes to wipe the mud off of his face. Digital Mortality: Stupid horse! Are you ready to go to training? Killer Dude: Yes! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The scene opens again at a UWF House Show in Denver. Killer Dude is standing behind the entrance curtain, looking out into the small stadium. In the ring a tag team match is taking place. Digital Mortality: Now, watch as Killer Dude does his training. Killer Dude's music hits and he runs into the ring. Killer Dude kicks one of the guys in the stomach Killer Dude executes a DDT on his opponent Killer Dude grabs another wrestler and headbutts him Killer Dude executes a one handed chokeslam on him Killer Dude then executes the Inverted Splash on one of the wrestlers. Killer Dude slams his opponent into the corner Killer Dude executes a Tornado DDT on him Killer Dude executes the Killer Smash on the wrestler Killer Dude goes for a quadruple pin The referee counts 1, 2, 3 Killer Dude recieves a standing ovation. Killer Dude's music hits again and he leaves the ring and returns to Digi's car. The scene cuts to a commercial.
