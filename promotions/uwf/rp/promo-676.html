UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #676

TV Title Part One

Sept 6 (year unrecorded)

Alex Grey — Home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Alex....” “Time to wake up... “But before you do...there’s something I want you to ponder for me” “I want you to ponder...just what life means to you” (Ringing...) “Put some thought into this Alex...I’m going to quiz you on it later” (Ringing...) “Now wake up...Martin’s on the phone” (Ringing...) —The Land of the Waking— Ringing.... The phone reaches into the darkness of sleep and drags me kicking and screaming back to the land of the waking, for all the painful memories and horrible dreams I have every night you’d think waking up every morning would be a blessing rather then a chore. But getting up in the morning was an exercise in discipline... Ringing... “Screw off...” The phone didn’t heed my grumbles, nor did it hear the venom in my voice. Reaching for the phone I battled the small collections of aches and pains that woke up with me every morning like an old lover who just couldn’t take the hint and leave before the sun came up, These aches and pains were accompanied by a pain that had become all too familiar to me even before I’d accumulated my first real wrestling injuries... Ringing... A hangover... Knocking over a beer can in my quest to reach the phone I didn’t have to look on the bedside table or on the floor to know there would undoubtedly be 4 or 5 empties to accompany the one I’d knocked over. Damn Martin for getting me hooked on this imported shit... Ringing... “Alright! ALRIGHT!” I growled finally reaching the phone and debating Jerking the cord out of the wall just to fade back into the blissful silence of sleep. Instead against my better judgement I picked up the receiver “Martin...what the hell do you want?” “Alex?” Martins voice sounded puzzled on the other end of the line, he sounded close which meant he was probably outside in his limo “How did you know it was me?” “I...” the question caught me off guard...how did I know? “Who else would it be?” I recovered lamely “I don’t know...” Martin countered “Maybe one of any hundred women you’ve bedded over the years calling and claiming you owe child support?” God he was in a disgustingly good mood this morning “Martin...” “Yeah?” “This conversation?” “What about it?” “Does it have a fucking point?” “It does...” he wasn’t put off by the tone of my voice “So let’s get to it!” I growled into the receiver squinting against the bright sunlight that spilled into my room “Anyone ever tell you that you’re no fun in the morning when your hangover?” Martin taunted over the airwaves “All the time...” I said contemplating going for my gun I kept in the drawer and taking a few lead pills to get rid of this hangover...or Martin. “Alright...” Martin said “I’ve got some great news for you...but we can’t talk about it over the phone...get dressed and meet me outside in ten minutes” “Twenty minutes...” “Get out of bed you lush” “Kiss my ass Smalls...I’ve got no purpose, what else can I do besides enjoy the luxuries of imported beer and high priced women” “You’ve got a purpose now Alex...It’s called the UWF...this binge drinking shit stops today, from now on you hit the gym.” Slowly a smile crept over my face, Martins voice may not have been the first thing I wanted to wake up to every morning, but when Martin had good news for me it was usually worth getting up for. “I’ll be down in thirty minutes...” “You said twenty Mi...” I hung up the phone cutting him off, With a chuckle I swung my feet out over the bed, My hangover vanishing and being replaced by adrenaline, the thought of having a purpose again...stepping into the ring again where I hadn’t been in front of a live crowd for almost a year and a half... Damn it felt good. Life was finally looking up. Heading for the shower I found myself pondering life... And just what exactly it meant to me... Funny pondering that... I didn’t usually think about such things.
