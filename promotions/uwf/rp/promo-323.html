UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #323

Dante and Lover Boy (RP#1 For SAS Match)

11/04/04

Gregory — Minneapolis, USA

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The scene opens up in Gregory's locker room, about 24 hours before his match against Dante and Loverboy at SAS. The scary-eyed Gregory is sitting down with his eyes closed on a blue chair, and after about 5 seconds or so, he slowly begins to lift his head up and looks at the camera with his fierce eyes standing out straight away. He begins to talk with a slightly growly voice.* Gregory: 'With great power, comes great responsibility.' That should be remembered not just as a famous film saying, but as a real life saying. Explain? Ok. Take me for example. I go by this saying. Take a look at my body, my figure and then think about it. I obviously have GREAT power over others, but do I take it for granted? No. I take it as a blessing, I take it with responsibility, that simple enough for you to remember? You see, unlike others, I have an advantage in life. You see those posh, snobby englishmen walking the roads of our country in their 100% cotton suits, and they look at us poorer people and think they are bigger and better than we could ever be. But they don't realise that they're just another face in the crowd, another qualified worker, 1 in a million who is helping to run this city. But I am the better man. I have power, a puny little englishmen with top grades in school cannot have power, he has a big head. But, as you can see, so do I. I am better than those englishmen, I am better than most people. I am not afraid to show it. Throw me a stipulation, sure, throw me a legend, big deal, But what you can't forget is that I will always win. Somehow, some way, I win. And again, unlike most others, I will do it without cheating! I hate stables, I hate tag teams, I hate managers, I hate girlfriends. I work alone. I always will. I always have done. *Gregory laughs, and he begins to speak once more* Gregory: I just had a thought. It's kind of funny when you think about it properly. In the last two weeks, I have gone straight from the top of the EWF to the bottom, rookie pile of the UWF. And now I know it for sure. I WILL get to the top of the WWA. I WILL prove to the owners I am worth the contract. And all you other rookies can watch me rapidly climbing the UWF Ladder, you can watch me at the top playing the big wrestlers. El Spastico, Kenny Mercury, those sort of guys. They are all good wrestlers, yes. But there is one thing about SAS I do not understand. Who in the blue hell is Loverboy? *Gregory smiles at the camera for a while, clearly amused with his own joke, and then he chuckles a bit, 3 seconds pass, and he chuckles a bit more. He begins to speak about 10 seconds after.* Gregory: Loverboy. Loverboy, Loverboy, Loverboy. Nope. No matter how much you say that name it doesn't seem to get any more serious. How can you have a name like Loverboy and then still not wake up every morning wanting to die. I mean imagine it, decent wrestlers coming to the ring and then the announcer says Loverboy is making his way to the ring. You would have thought the ring announcer is taking the piss out of you! *Gregory's expression rapidly changes, and he starts to talk about his other opponent, Dante.* Gregory: Dante, you're very dumb, you have to admit. You think that you're going to be a big superstar in a couple of months time. But you're not. After I beat you, you are going to have to put up with being a full-time losing wrestler. Yes. I can just imagine the 2004 awards. 'And the winner of the loser of the year award.......Dante!' And here's another one. 'And the winner of the best uprising superstar of the year award........Gregory!' You see? You can't win. With my athleticism barely anyone will be able to scrape a victory. You take a look at my muscles (camera goes down to his muscles) and then you imagine yours. You can see the difference. The difference is the truth. I have muscles. You have tensed Fat. Or is someone taking steroids? Maybe. Gregory: But, Dante, I don't care whether you might cry when I beat you to a pulp, I don't care how happy you would be if you won. What I care about is me. While I am wrestling, it's just me and my unfortunate opponent/s. Gregory: There's a word some wrestlers here will be familiar with. Then again some of you may not know of this word. The word is risk, spelt R...I...S...K. I like taking risks, Dante. If you're down, I would jump from say 25 feet in the air just to hit you, just to finish you off. You see, I am completely different to you, and most other wrestlers. If I had the tiniest amount of energy and blood left, and it was me against you in the ring, I would use those tiny bits of blood and energy just to have the satisfaction of punching you around the face, the satisfaction of finishing you off with a spear. *Gregory stares at the wall for a while, the camera stays exactly where it is, waiting for Gregory's mind to return. It does. He begins to speak once more.* Gregory: Control. That's a great word, if you ask me. I have control over the vast amount of anger I hide inside me. I can do whatever I want with it. I can let it all out at once, I can let it out gradually, and i'll tell you one thing it gives me a hell of a lot of adrenalin. Punching someone's lights out is a thrill to me. Loverboy, this is what I am trying to say. I was born for wrestling. You were not. If I didn't get paid to do it, I would still wrestle. You would have a moan and try to get a 'good' job. Well, that's all I've got to say to an ass like you. *The cameraman hears this, but he does not move. He stays there, keeping the camera on Gregory.* Gregory: Didn't you hear me? I've got some training to day if you don't mind, maggot. *The cameraman walks out of the locker room and goes to interview Dante and Loverboy, but before we see the interview, the screen fades to black and goes to a commercial about Gregory t-shirts.*
