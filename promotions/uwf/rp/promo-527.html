Rendezvous With the Contact

1979 (year unrecorded)

Jay Vyper — East Germany

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It wasn't a heavy rain that came out of the gray German sky, not the type that might have sent anyone on the street running for cover, but a light, chilly drizzle that only served to make the day that much more miserable. Like many wild animals, Vyper didn't really like to be wet. It put him in a foul temper as if he needed anything else to give this op a nasty little sense of foreboding. Stratus was at point and Jade batting cleanup. Vyper and Vlad strode side by side, talking in low voices in German, just enough to create a semblance of normalcy among men trying very hard to look like common laborers. Jade kept back a bit, her gypsy clothing drew a little attention, but none of it the suspicious kind. 'You an' the squaw gettin' on pretty good, eh ?' Vlad whispered. Vyper frowned. “Auf Deutsch, Vlad' he snarled. With a cruel smile, the only kind at his command, Beast repeated himself in German. Or, at least, he tried. There was no direct German translation for 'squaw,' and Vlad couldn't manage the same tone of lecherous disdain in another language. The Major told him it was none of his business, but Vlad wasn't about to let it go. 'Let me know if things don't work out,' he said in German. 'I wouldn't mind a taste of that.' His guts burned with anger, but Vyper laughed heartily bent over slightly, and clapped a hand to Vlad's back as if they were the best of friends. 'One of these days,' Vyper said in German, menace informing his tone despite the smile on his face, 'you're going to go too far.' Beast smiled back, stopped walking to stare down into Vyper’s eyes. He snarled. 'You have no idea.' The moment lasted longer than it ought to have, considering they were trying to stay inconspicuous. It ended only when Stryker backtracked to step between them. 'I don't know about you two,' he said, 'but I would dearly love a beer.' Vyper looked up, keeping the forced plastic smile on his face, and was about to tear into Stryker when he realized what his teammate had meant. They had reached their initial destination. Just ahead on the left side of the street was a glass and wood fronted building with BUEHAREST RESTAURANT engraved in German on a sign in front. It was a nicer establishment than, they ought to be going into dressed like this, but there was nothing to be done for it now. Across the street from the restaurant was a big, ugly, ancient Wartburg truck--a model manufactured in East Germany--whose broad-shouldered driver wore a shiny leather apron and was busily unloading beer kegs from the back. 'Vlad,' Vyper said, 'hang back, keep an eye on the door. If we're in trouble, you'll know it. We don't want any rude surprises while we're inside.' Beast nodded, eyes narrowed and filled with danger. But he didn't argue. He did exactly what Vyper told him to do. Vyper was field command on this op, after all. And, for the moment at least, Vlad was willing to be the good soldier. As long as that involved bloody murder. Vyper and Stratus moved to the front door of the restaurant and looked inside. They went in and each of them stepped to one side to allow Jade in after them. Jade walked straight ahead, finding a table near the back. While anyone interested in, watching them followed Jade's progress across the room, Vyper and Stratus scanned the interior. It was almost a full house, which surprised Vyper because he'd figured East Germans didn't have the time or the money to be sitting around inside a restaurant tavern in the middle of the day. On the other hand, he figured, those that did have some spare time didn't have a whole lot else to occupy it. They went to join Jade at the table, sat down noisily, as if they were laborers who'd finished their work for the day. Vyper's German was passable if he didn't draw too much attention to himself. But Stratus was German, so he did most of the talking in public. Fortunately, they didn't have to wait long. Vyper was halfway through a drink when they were joined by a thin, plain woman whose dour features were reflected in the severity of her short, black hair. It had been recently shorn, and was all practicality. In the picture they'd been shown of their German contact, the woman had been younger, more attractive, with long and flowing hair. But it was a hard world, and it wore some people down all too quickly. 'Nice to see you again, Fraulein Haupt,' Stryker said in German, as though the two were old friends, though none of them had ever met the woman before. 'And you, Herr Nord,' the woman replied, and Vyper raised his eyebrows. She'd used Styker's real name, which wasn't a good idea, not at all. Of course, Nord had to be a relatively common surrname, but still it wasn't professional. Fraulein Haupt ordered herself a Marzenbier and then turned to smile at Jade and Vyper. 'Wie geht es Ihnen?' she asked, an inquiry as to their well-being. But her eyes were on Vyper, then. Greeting him. Noticing, Admiring him. Had she been more beautiful, he might have wondered about that. He'd been in love with some extraordinary women in his long life, and incredibly enough, some of them had loved him in return. But Vyper knew that at first glance he was only barely handsome. His face was too hard, chiseled, and his hair was wild. And, truth be told, he always wore a mask. But here was Fraulein Haupt, checking him out. It made him decidedly uncomfortable for several reasons, not the least of which was the proximity of Jade, whom he loved with the same passion he felt for the wilderness of his homeland. He and Vixen belonged together, as far as Vyper was concerned. Still, there was something about that look in Fraulein Haupt's eyes. A mischief that danced there as she looked at him, a playful seductiveness which belied her severe exterior. 'Gut, danke,' Stryker said. Vyper realized that he'd never returned Fraulein Haupt's greeting. He felt more than a little foolish. It wasn't like him at all to be so distracted. Not at all. He frowned slightly and looked at their contact more closely. There was something not quite right about her, and he wondered what it could be. Before they had drained their steins, Fraulein Haupt had suggested they move to her rooms nearby where they could speak more freely. A moment later, they were following her out of the tavern. On the street, Vyper didn't see Vlad anywhere, which was good. Vlad wasn't supposed to be seen right about then. He was supposed to watch. Still, The Major knew he hadn't gone far. Beast had a scent he would never forget, and never miss even in a crowd. No, Vlad was nearby. Despite his misgivings about the man, if there was trouble, having Beast around was always a good idea. Vyper almost smiled at the thought. Even if he wasn't good for anything else, Vlad was a pretty big target. Soldiers were likely to shoot at him before they did anyone else. Which would be a crying shame, if you had any affection for homicidal lunatics. Fraulein Haupt led the way, arm in arm with Stratus as if they were lovers. Vyper and Jade walked side by side as well, but they did not link arms. Even if they were out on the town in Paris together, it just wasn't the kind of gesture they would have shared. That sort of innocence was lost to them, and not easy to feign, even for those in the business of espionage. Stratus still retained some of it, and perhaps that was why it seemed to come to him so naturally. But Fraulein Haupt? Vyper had to wonder. Even as he considered this, she glanced over her shoulder at him and began to make excuses for the mess they would find in her rooms. Her bedroom, particularly, was a disaster. When she said this, she winked at Vyper very purposefully. He ignored her, but felt Jade stiffen at his side. He couldn't help feeling a bit of pleasure at Jade's reaction. When they were on an op, she was so coldly professional that he was frequently forced to wonder what she really felt. But here it was. And it made him painfully aware of how dangerous their lives were. It was a danger they had lived with from the beginning; a cruel, harsh thing that would never truly allow them to love without reservation. Enough of that romantic crap, Vyper thought. They had a job to do. They followed Fraulein Haupt into the rat hole where she had rented rooms. The fat old man at the front desk raised his eyes as they walked in, but averted them just as quickly when Haupt slid a pile of deutschemarks across the desk. Cash. It wasn't a guaranteed insurance policy, but it was fairly reliable. As she held the door for them, Fraulein Haupt smiled knowingly at Vyper. He'd had enough. He let his upper lip rise in a kind of silent snarl, and the woman reacted instantly, her face registering, first surprise, then anger. The hell with her. 'Just do the job,' he wanted to say. But he didn't. He'd done enough. 'Where are they?' he asked her in English, but quietly. Vyper didn't think there were unseen listeners. He didn't hear or see anyone within the rooms or outside the door. Well, except for Vlad and he had to wonder how Beast had gotten past the fat old desk jockey downstairs. 'They split up yesterday,' Fraulein Haupt answered. 'I don't know when they'll be back together, but you should wait. I don't know which of them has the disk.' A soft knock at the door caught her attention. It was the cadence of 'Shave and a haircut,' without the 'two bits' at the end. It was Vlad. But not an emergency, or he wouldn't have bothered to knock. So what was he doing? Fraulein Haupt pulled a Walther Polizei Pistole Kriminal semi auto from under her skirt, but Vyper held up his hand. 'A friend of ours,' he said, and the irony of the word friend wasn't lost on him. 'Let's see what news he's got.' Stratus opened the door to admit Vlad, who met Vyper's questioning gaze with a hard stare. He looked at each of them, then walked slowly across the room to Fraulein Haupt. He sniffed at her, the way a dog might sniff a stranger. The woman's eyes were wide, but Vyper sensed that she was more anxious than afraid, an odd reaction to Beast's even odder behavior. 'Vlad?' Vyper asked. 'What are you doing, Beast?' Jade wondered aloud. 'Have you lost your…' Then Vlad started to laugh, almost genuine laughter kind of tainted amusement. He reached behind Fraulein Haupt, and gave her a pinch, to which she reacted not at all. Again Vyper was surprised at her lack of reaction at this entire weird scene. 'Good to see you again, darlin'' Vlad said, and offered a final chuckle before the mask of the killer once again fell across his face. 'Beast, what's goin' on here?' Vyper demanded. 'This mission's got enough bad omens without you freakin' out.' Vlad looked at him. 'Ah, Jay. She pulled one over on you, runt. She try to pick you up? Girl can twist a man a hundred ways just with a glance, can't you, Raven?' He looked at Fraulein Haupt, who only stared at Vyper. 'Aw, come on,' Vlad said, growing frustrated now. 'This ain't Fraulein Haupt!' Vyper stared at him, then at their contact. She looked exactly like the pictures they'd seen of her, with the exception of her hair. So, unless she had a twin…. 'The Haupt babe is probably dead in an alley or a dumpster by now,' Vlad claimed. 'I knew this one was wrong when I followed you all into the building. Might not even have noticed if I wasn't lookin' for somethin' out o' place. But only one woman I ever met smells like this lady here. And her name ain't Haupt… 'It's Raven Monolov.' Jay was about to speak again. Then the woman just changed. One moment, Fraulein Haupt was standing in front of them. Then She peeled off a very impressive mask showing someone totally different. Her face was stunning, her hair a flaming red. Her eyes were blue, but that mischievous quality never left them. She was a militant that much was obvious. Nobody in the room questioned it either. Raven glared at Vlad. 'So nice to see you again, Abendroth,' she said, voice dripping with venom. 'Yeah, babe, it's a thrill for me, too,' Vlad replied with just as much enthusiasm. 'So now we know your name,' Jade said, moving in closer to Raven, taking control of the room. But Vyper wanted to caution her, tell her not to be so sure of herself. There was a danger that radiated from the red haired disguiser (as the military called their best under cover operatives). A quality not unlike the waves of cruelty that came off Vlad every time he stepped in a room. 'Before you all get crazy,' Raven said, 'I'll answer the questions I know you have.' 'You're right,' Stratus said grimly. 'You will.' 'Ooooh, Stratus gets tough,' Vlad taunted. 'Raven, I think he likes you.' Both Raven and Stratus glared at Vlad, but he only chuckled to himself. 'I killed your contact,' she said, so bluntly that Vyper was taken aback. 'You. ..' Stratus began, but Raven cut him off. 'She was a double,' the woman said quickly. 'You should thank me. If I hadn't killed her, you'd all be dead by now. 'I wasn't lying about the Zhevakovs,' she continued. 'I know where the husband, Grigorii, is staying. But I don't think he has the disk. And we're not the only ones looking for them. Until the wife, Katrina, shows up again, there's nothing we can do.' 'What is this 'we'?' Jade sneered. Raven smiled. 'Jealous, honey?' Jade said nothing. 'You need my help,' Raven said. 'Haupt is dead, and would have turned you in anyway. You want to know where Grigorii Zhevakov is? I can show you. We get the disk, I'll take a copy, that's all I ask.' 'Who are you workin' for?' Vyper asked, and watched her face carefully. 'Not the KGB,' Raven replied. 'That's all you really need to know.' Vyper felt them all staring at him, waiting for a decision. He was field commander on this op. It was up to him. He could throw it out to a vote, but that wasn't the way covert ops were played. 'All right,' he said at last, and ignored the looks of astonishment on the faces of his teammates. Even Vlad looked stunned. 'Well, let's go, then,' raven said easily, and turned to leave the room. The others were still staring at him. Vlad started to open his mouth, to say something to ask him what he thought he was doing. .., 'Stay with her, no matter what,' he told Vlad. Beast grinned, and followed Raven out. 'You don't mean to let her have a copy of that disk?' Stratus asked, obviously disgusted at the thought. With good reason. It didn't matter who Raven was working for. The Agency wouldn't want anyone having the information on that disk. Anyone. It was the kind of information people killed for on a regular basis. The kind of information they might all have to kill for before this op was over. 'No,' Vyper said, once he was sure Raven and Vlad were out of earshot. 'No, I don't.'
