UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #371

Mindtake that!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins outside of Ruff Ryder's home. He is sitting on his front porch with what's left of his black baseball bat in his hand as he stares at the camera with a cocky grin. He looks the bat over and then tosses it to the side as he chuckles to himself a bit. ) Ryder: Hey Mentok....got wood? * Ryder starts laughing * Ryder: No seriously dawg, you were tryin to mindtake my boy Malcolm and we can't be havin none of that in the Prime Alliance. You don't think it's gonna be that easy do you? I mean seriously? We are the best this company has ever seen, and i'm sorry to tell ya son, but you ain't shit. Don't think that you even had a chance at beating Malcolm Kennedy with that mindtaking shit. You're ass is lucky they dq'd my boy for that beating or your psychic friends ass would be laid up in some hospital bed bleeding to death. You did get the win though, and for that I must apologize to my partner. Malcolm, I didn't think that senile old bastard of a ref would give the match to Mentok and I apologize. I hope you're not pissed. As for you though Mentok, you got part two of the Prime Alliance and son you're gonna wish you'd never met either one of us when we get done with you. Fuck the rest of these punk ass stables, because we are the best and we will prove that night in and night out. Malcolm Kennedy has his eyes set on Mike Montgomery, the new champ, and you better believe that when Malcolm Kennedy sees something he wants...he gets it. * Ryder starts to get that intense look on his face as he leans forward in his chair * Ryder: Montgomery, you just made a huge mistake by winning that World Title, because now you have reignited that old flame that is known as the Prime Alliance vs Players Club feud. You have something that we want and you can bet your ass that we'll be coming to get it. Ezra, Dunn, sexpot, don't matter who it is...you're all goin down. Hell four on two? We'll take those odds, because we'll dominate from start to finish and take home all the pride and the World Title because you got lucky the first time. The first time we were cocky and we got ourselves beat, but this time we aren't underestimating you at all...and that means trouble for you. You better spend all the time you can with that World Title, because it's coming home to the Prime Alliance, and whether it's me or the one man army makes no difference. The Prime Alliance is poised for a return to greatness...believe that. * Ryder changes gears and the cocky smirk returns to his face as he sits back in his chair * Ryder: Now back to my so-called opponent Mentok the goatraper or whatever your name is. Who do you think you're kidding? I mean you can't take people's minds! You're just some dumb jackass that painted himself green. You are a pathetic excuse for the talent that the UWF has on it's roster. You'd better watch out, because the PWW failed to reopen it's doors and if the kind of talent that they recruit is on it's way here...you're a dead man. The PWW only employs the best and if those guys end up here, this place is in for a shake up. You won't make it till then though i'm afraid Mentok, because at SAS you're dead before you hit the mat. I'm gonna stomp you all around the ring and punish you all over the arena. You're gonna be begging me to kill you when you feel the pain that I can bring. I am the True Indian Superstar and you're gonna find out just what that means when I drive you into the mat son. You think that baseball bat hurt, well believe me, you haven't even begun to experience pain...but you will. * Ryder grins and winks before changing subjects again * Ryder: On a side note, I understand that our former leader is returning. Jamie Shock comin back to right the wrongs huh. Well the first wrong he needs to right is apologizing to the Prime Alliance for running out on us like he did. That was bullshit and he knows it. The man didn't even give us a goodbye or anything and now he thinks he can just waltz back in and not say sorry? Fuck that! If he wants to make things right then i'm all for that, but I think that me and the one man army deserve an explanation. See you in five Shock. ( Ryder leans back in his chair and kicks his feet up as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
