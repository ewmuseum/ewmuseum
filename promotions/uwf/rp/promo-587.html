UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #587

Guess who's back!

12 Jul 2004

Nick Persia — UWF headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Player’s Club is falling apart. That’s what they are telling me. Now with the return of Mike Montgomery we are the mightiest stable in the whole UWF. No, that’s not true. We are the UWF. I mean, just look at the facts. The last three people to have competed for the UWF World Title are Mike Montgomery, Kevin Starr and Joshua Dunn. Total Dominance. A display of power. And if the rumour is true Ezra might be challenging for the title as well. But let’s not get ahead of ourselves here.] [Scene opens at the UWF headquarters and we see Nick Persia walking out of the office of the Uwf owner, Richard Hayes. Coincidentally Larson walks in the corridor and notices that Nick persia is walking out of the office. This has gained larson’s attention and he wants to know what Hayes said to Nick and walks towards him to ask some questions.] Larson: “Long time, no see. How are you doing?” Nick Persia replies calmly “I am doing fine, thanks. How are you?” Larson: “Not bad, thanks. But what were you doing in Richard hayes’ office?” Nick Persia smiles at first, but then looks with the intense look in his eyes into Larson’s eyes. Larson feels slightly intimidated by this, but, of course, acts like everything is normal. Nick Persia: “I was listening to his speech.” Larson: “Really? What was the speech about?” Nick Persia: “About how the best time to give a flower some water. No, he talked about my lack of interviews and that it was in my interest of doing those interviews since they give me more name recognition and more fame thus more money so I shouldn’t look at it as more work, but as more money. But I already have enough money and to be honest with you I really have other problems I have to deal with.” Larson: “Like the Player’s club. Because it seems to me that they are showing some friction. Not everybody is on the same page, so it appears.” Nick Persia: “Not everybody has enough personality to whistand greed. However it’s in the nature of a human being to be greedy. Even I have the desire to use my shot for a title before I am ready to take that next big step. I was learned to have some patience and keep my emotions under control. Sometimes you need to sacrifice yourself for the greater good, whatever that might be. You see, in the end I always get what I want. But nothing comes cheap so I do whatever I have to do. And I can assure you that patience can be a very powerful weapon.” Larson: “But ambition is a powerful weapon as well.” Nick Persia: “Ambition is just one step away from greed.” Larson: “But you still didn’t answer my question.” Nick Persia: “No, I didn’t. The Player’s Club isn’t falling apart.” Larson: “I heard very reliable sources stating it is.” Nick Persia: “Then I suggest you check again because it appears to me that I would know better since I am an actual member of the club.” Larson: “But you weren’t present at the show.” Nick Persia: “I wasn’t but did you really think I only see them at shows? Besides we are real friends, not a group supporting each other backs under the theme “I watch your back if you watch mine”. We are a tight unit. My mum always said you aren’t real friends if you haven’t got a fight every now and then.” Larson: ‘So you will go out there to kick some ass?” Nick Persia: “I will indeed. You look suprised. But let me tell you this, i always want to win. Especially with the two losses in a row I suffered recently. People musn’t think of me as a joke or a one hit wonder. No, i am Nick Persia. I am for real. I am here to stay. I am here to get what belongs to me. But everything must be put into perspective. The interest of the Club gets prior my own personal interests. Because in the long run I will have more benefit serving the Player’s Club. Not only do I have friends for life but I also know how to deal with the people around you in a proper fashion. Let’s not forget I always deal with people with respect and dignity. Unlike a few other people who hang around this place. I won’t say any names but when I say the army should be a sign of discipline I know for sure we have a man who deserted from the army in our presence.” Larson: “You aren’t talking about Corporal again, are you? Get over with it.” Nick Persia: “After I humiliated him, sure. But for now I will not bother him in his activities. But rather sooner than later he will feel my wrath. I shall do what the army failed to do: teaching him some discipline. I will give him a personal message in the near future. Sdo keep your eyes peeled and your ears open. Otherwise you are in for a rude awakening. I never rest, my dear friend.” Larson: “But in the near future you will have to fight your fellow stable mates. How do you feel about that?” Nick Persia: “Sure, i am having second thoughts about fighting them. But sometimes you need to pur your personal feelings aside. The fans would love to see a match between the true top dogs here. Sure, I won’t pull off as much tricks as I would do against other opponents. Sure, we will be a bit more friendly. But I am in this match to win. So I will hit him with anything I have. I will do whatever I can within my limits. I do not carry hatred nor envy against them. I know they are all going for the world title. But like I told you patience pays. And when opportunity comes I will seize it. But that’s the future. I need to worry about the here and now. I will have to worry about Sunday.” Larson: “You have tagged with Dunn before so that will give you some edge and he’s the world champion.” Nick Persia: “But Mike and Ezra have tagged before as well. But, yes, it’s true what you said. The thing I worry about most is the savage I have caused in the match. I do not hope I wil create some bad blood. I do not wish to turn my best friends into my worst enemies.” Larson: “Although the saying goes ‘Keep your friends close but your enemies even closer’!” Nick Persia: “Not everything people say is true. Just take your reliable source for an example! Come Sunday, I will be ready. See you there, larson.” Nick walks away but them seems to remember something and turns around. Nick Persia: “And say something nice about me for a change.” [Scene fades out with a confused Larson who watches Persia leave first, but then leaves himself after he regained his senses.]
