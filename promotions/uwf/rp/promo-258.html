UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #258

One More TIme

1 Mar 2004

Ezra Kazee — Outside a church

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in front of a large white church. There are stairs that lead up to the green doors of the church. Overtop of the door is a large stained glass window with a wooden cross on top of it. As you look farther up, there is also a large cross that sits on top of the church’s roof. Inside, music can be heard and singing is also heard. Shortly thereafter, people start to file out of the church. Men and Women, young and old, fat and thin, all wearing their Sunday finest. Soon, almost everyone has filed out of the church and shortly there after, appearing at the doorway with an older gentleman is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing black slacks, a black shirt with a black tie, and a green sports jacket. The man next to him is the minister, wearing a blue robe with a red sash.) Kazee: What a great service that was, Rev. Orde. I wish I had the time to come more often but with my busy schedule and all. Rev. Orde: Do not worry, Ezra. All people have their own calling and you are doing what God has called you to do. Good Luck in your future matches and may God Bless you. Kazee: Thank you reverend. (The two men shake hands. Rev. Orde walks back into the church and closes the doors behind him. Kazee starts to walk down the stairs as the camera follows him. He pulls the lapels of his sports coat.) Kazee: Well well well, what can I say? Guess who is once again one victory away from Intercontinental Gold. Guess who has yet another shot at the IC belt. Most men don’t get this many shots in one life time. I will now have my fourth opportunity to win this belt. Three times wasn’t the charm, but the fourth time. Yeah, I think it’s time now…… My previous record, like I said, when going for this belt isn’t exactly sterling. But let’s consider who I faced. I faced Lyle “In Style” Wile. I took him to the absolute limit. I had the belt. I had the Jailhouse Break locked in on this guy, twice. First time for 30 seconds, the second time for a minute. Do you know how that move feels even after one second, let alone 30 seconds, or even a minute? I got to give him props. He fought through it……. The last two times, I faced “The Communist Scotsman” Joshua Dunn. I took the fight to him. I brought everything I had to him and he still got the best of me. Not disappointed at all. He won both times, be it the second time under some sketchy circumstances, but he won both times. Now he is on to bigger and better things. And soon he will be walking away with the UWF World Title because he is that good……. (Kazee starts walking down the street, away from the church.) Two of the greatest IC Champions of all time is who I have faced for the belt. Both times I came within eyelashes of beating them. Both times I took them to their limits. And both times I came up just that eyelash short of walking out of the building with the belt. This time will be different…….. This time it was a tournament. This time I had to defeat three people to win the title. The challenges are on par and some of the greatest challenges I have ever faced. Macolm Kennedy, you were the first one. And after a hard fought match, I came out on top. Not only establishing my continued dominance over you, but also The Players Club continued dominance over The Prime Alliance…… Next, it was that sick freak known as Bondage Sex Fiend or whatever his name his. Once again, a hard fought match, but unfortunately for the newcomer, I was just a little bit more hungry, a little bit more wanting. And I walked out with my shot at the title….. This time, in the final match for the IC belt, I face yet another Prime Alliance member. I must say that something has got to be done to stop the Prime Alliance from breeding like rabbits. I mean c’mon, one person leaves and another comes in. It’s like a giant revolving door. Anyone can join and leave at anytime….. But I digress. This time, the Prime Alliance stands in my way of the IC belt in the form of Moraless... This time, the stepping stone to the IC belt shall be another stepping stone in the dominance of the Players Club over the Prime Alliance. This time, I will not be denied……. Moraless, I have never faced you before. You have never faced me. But fact of the matter is that it won’t matter. My fire is burning bright. My desire is deeper than ever, and my hunger for IC gold is greater than any other competitor here. Your friend Macolm Kennedy claims he is the fastest rising star here in the UWF. My only question is if that’s the case, what in the world does that make me. Within two months, not only will I have gone from no gold to Tag Team and IC Gold, but I’ll have acquired my dream….. Are you ready to deal with the fire, the heart, the desire that I have coming after you. Are you ready to face “the most knowledgeable” wrestler in all of the UWF. Are you ready to face things you’ve never faced before? Are you up to the challenge of IC Gold……? This is an unfamiliar spot for you Moraless. That will work against you. The man you fight against will make your disadvantage even greater. Your Prime Alliance, they won’t help you. Your friends, they can’t stop me, and neither can you. On Sunday night, not only do I plan on walking out with yet another victory over the Prime Alliance, I also plan on walking out with the IC title….. Good Luck, Moraless, you’ll need it. (Scene fades to black.)
