Bondage Sex Fiend

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a Tuesday Afternoon and the clouds had just defeated the sun in domination of the sky. The murky gray sky gave reflection upon a choppy lake and on the banks sits a man, skipping rocks on the water. Beside him sits a decent sized gym bag. The dark haired and brown-eyed man looks to have no future or no past. He tosses one more rock into the water as a car speeds by from the interstate highway on the top of the pond bank. 'So what is left for me?' The man quietly questions staring out into the gray pond, 'I have nothing left for me. A mediocre wrestler with nothing but a catchy gimmick going for him. I've wrestled under this name for six months and while there is little recognition, what is there is needed. I can't continue a wrestling career, there has to be some change or else I am done. Bondage the Sex Fiend... who the hell was a kidding.' The man stops and shakes his head reaching over to his bag and unzipping it. He digs into the bag and pulls out a black mask which he unfolds and places two fingers into the eye holes. 'I can't believe that people believed it this long and the success I do have is barely enough to live off of. I'd rather have a lobotomy then a libido the size of Bondage's. He's nothing like me and no one knows the true me in the wrestling world. I have always put this face out to the world and try to compensate for my lack of wrestling ability. I was trained for a few weeks, using my 2 years of high school wrestling as a step up, but that can only go so far.' The man, obviously Bondage the Sex Fiend is staring into the mask. 'Bondage is a part of me, Bondage is me but not at the same time. I have been Bondage for 6 months and while I know the fans want to see more of me, it's hard to keep up this act. My life has fallen apart ever since I became Bondage, my fiancé has become sketchy and openly hates the idea of me being around escorts that were overly paid to wrestle with me.' Bondage takes a deep sigh and then puts the mask away and rests his elbows on his lower thigh, and rests his head in his hands. As he talks the sort of muffled echo extends into his voice because of his head hiding, 'And after all of this, after being put up against Starr and others, where I haven't been trying, I have to wrestle at Stable Wars. Honestly any other federation would have let me go, they would have said, you're not the type of wrestler we want so leave. But UWF has seen something in me and while they have not shown it, they let me stick around and try to reach my full potential and once I do I will be on the top of my game. But I have had enough of this all, at Stable Wars I am going to show my full potential and take all this built up rage out on everybody. This will be Bondage's Great Come Back; I am going to show the world and UWF that I deserve a lot more than the TV Title. To do this, I am going to single handedly take down at least one full team and I will not pull punches because I want my shot at the World title. Kennedy, Digi, Moraless, they have all had some kind of success while I am the rookie of the group. I am the one man that can't get a single ounce of recognition given to me but I must fight for every single bit I can get. The winning team of Stable Wars gets their member's choice of titles, I am going to get my pick, I am going to get first pick and take the World title. The title is vacant; Digi didn't do his job but went beyond the call and broke Montgomery’s leg. No one has seen that gimp for a while and while I may not be the best wrestler and I may not have a gimmick, but just a name, I know I can beat any and every wrestler in UWF, just give me the chance.' Bondage stands up and grabs the open bag, climbing up the pond bank and heads to his black Cadilliac El Dorado. He tosses the bag into the passenger window, and swings around the front of the car to the driver's side and opens the door, and plops down into the car.
