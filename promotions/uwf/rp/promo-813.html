UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #813

waiting till its my turn

3 Dec 2004

Dreammaker — at teh theater

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to Dreammaker and UWF's own reporter, Jeff Johnson laughing at the screen in front of them as it goes to black..) Jeff: Well, that was entertaining. Man. I didn't think you hooking up your satelite dish to this theater would be worth it. BUT, THIS FUCKING ROCKS! What do you ahve to say to those wonderful UWF moments we just watched? Dreammaker: What do I have to say? Man, can you believe it? First we got that wanna porn star checking out the dudes at his favorite club. Then we have cat flinging at a battered cat shelter. I mean a battered cat shelter, can you beleive the originality in it? Jeff: You do mean cat catapulting right? I mean it was very entertaining. Dreammaker: It doesn't matter what you call it, I laughed my ass off watching it. I don't know much about this Craig Write fella. But what can I say except that he has a sick sense of humor. And he sure likes to target Hot Mask. Jeff, write that down for me. Jeff: No problem . (opens his phone and we hear it beeb and he says) Bubbles. Can you grab another beer for me and for me and The Dream? (phone beeps) No problem. Tell Mr. Dream that I will be down stairs when I finsih doing pay roll for the club. (Jeff puts the phone away) Dreammaker: Good call. At least you can say thank you. I mean for my sake and all. (stops to look at the camera) Mr. Wright, take a look around you. Nobody cares about you feline fetish. How it gets you going so you can keep up with the club hotties. Trust me, it takes a real man to keep up with those professionals. (grins and stands up from his chair) The bottom line is the fact that no matter how you prepare for this match, no matter what you do while you wait, and no matter where you go in your fancy limo. I will be still here waiting for you to prove yourself to the millions of UWF fans, that you can stand up with the big boys and entertain the world. Because when the smoke clears, the cats are shaved and thrown into the crowd, it will all come down to the man that stood tall and took it all. So keep dreaming, beacause as of this next PPV, you will be in my world. Where dreams become reality and your dreams won't even be heard over the roar of the fans as they hold my hand high over all of your hands. (Just then Bubbles comes out the door and down the aisle. She is hurrying with 2 large cups over flowing with quickly filled beer. ) Bubbles: (talking as she walks) Sorry guys, just so much paper work to be down for the business. (she hands the cups to the guys) Jeff: Boy sure like my head! (ovbiously sarcasticly said) Bubbles: like I said, sorry! (as she puts her hands on her hips to show disgust) Jeff: Is that the new ring you were talking about Dreammaker? Dreammaker : yup, girl damn sure desserves it after dating me now for 10 years this week. Maybe I'll even marry her some day. Jeff: ( admiring that rock) Marry her, damn should invest into something then. Well, at least that stupid AIDS joke by Hot Mask or Ht Shot is realy retarded huh? Dreammaker: Wait a minute. You mean they are different people. DAmn !! These wannable are a dime a dozen. ( everyone laughs as Bubbles lets out something like a yelp) Dreammaker: What the? What is wrong.... ( as he looks towards the floor and there is Bubbles bending over lookin frantically for something) Bubbles: I just took it off to show Jeff and damn I drop it. Ok here it is! (AS she stand up she hits the bottom of Dreammakers glass and spill beer all over the front of his shirt and all over her head.) Dreammaker: Son of a Bitch! ( AS he takes of his shirt) (Dreammaker then stops taking his shirt off with it hanging from his left arm and looks up at the camera. He then smiles and stands up straight and flexes those world class weight lifting muscles for the world to see.) Dreammaker: Well at least if there are actaully any women fans left out there then at least tehy got something to cheer about. Hey Jeff check this out) (DReammaker then gives a mean look and points at the camera as to look tough. ) DM: Now you all folks listen here. I am tough. Believe me. No one is talking about me beacause they are afraid of me. I am the man that no one wants to fight cuz i am tough and mean. I even punch camera men. (after laughing and doing a little bicep flex, dreammaker, looks back at the camera) DM: Look MR. Killer Dude. Belive it or not. The reason no one is talking about you, (puts his hands togther as if gonna whisper) (instead yells ) NO ONE CARES! See in your world we all fear you and hope not to wrestle you. But in reality, you are an over the hill, hillbilly. I mean, I am more worried about what Justice will do to this world after reading his promos. Or are they fantasy movies. I don;t understand any one these days. (he then stops and looks over at Bubbles) DM: Come on hun, lets go get this paper work done. Jeff, good work. Go home to your wife. If she is still there. She might be drooling over some Hot man or gambling away her life in Wisconsin or some foreign contry filled with super heros. (they all laugh ) (the scene fades away to everyone walking up the aisle of the dark theater, smiling and preparing for this weekend of Fun. This weekend prepared for the fans.)
