23 May 2004

Nick Persia — Lockerrooms

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A lot of things have happened. In the past few weeks we saw Nick Persia and Adien pryde feuding week after week. Now, this brutal feud has ende thanks to a stipulation added to the match that if Pryde would lose he wouldn’t stick his nose in Persia’s business again. Nick Persia has further established himself a worthy Hardcore Champion after defending in a Stretcher Match and taking out Reo Dai. Now he has become member of The Player’s Club too.] [Scene opens at the Arena which will feature Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. He gets a welcome from Rick, on ehalf of the commentators duo. They walk to a lockerroom and they knock on the door. No responso, so they enter it.} Rick: “You wanted to show me something?” Nick Persia: “Yes, but be patient with me here, since I first need to check if it’s here.” [Nick checks out the room, but nothing out of the extraordinary] Nick: “Ah yes, it’s here” Rick: “What? What’s here?” Nick: “They have the tape so I can tape in my hands. You see, I wanted to show you something. In this room I always prepare for battle. I am always 4 hours before the actual event happens at the crime scene. Because I take my work very seriously. And as I showed last week, it payed off.” Rick: “We already know that. Is that all?” Nick: “I am sure you wished, but no. Follow me!” [They walk out of Nick’s lockerroom and they step into another lockerroom] Rick: “But this isn’t your lockerroom! This is...this is Digital Morality’s lockerroom.” Nick: “Relax, dude. The guy isn’t even here yet although he has a world title match scheduled. But let’s take a look at what he has in here.” [He takes a look in the closet, but nothing special is found.] Nick: “I can’t find anything here that says me he has actual done any preperation. And let me save you some time, he didn’t do any mental preperation, damn it he doesn’t even know what the word contains.” Rick: “Why is he scheduled to face Mike Montgomery then?” Nick: “Just because he is challenging for the PTC Global Title. And I wish him the best of luck, but that is because he is from UWF. Tonight, on the other hand, I hope he gets his ass kicked by Mike Montgomery. Since it will be The Player’s club having all the gold come next week.” Rick: “You heard about the plans of the Formorians?” Nick: “Yes, I did. They are just in and already have those kind of plans. What do you think we are? We aren’t a charity foundation. We won’t help you set up any career. Because they only thing we do with careers is ending it.” Rick: “Are you speaking for your fellow stable mate Joshua Dunn as well?” Nick: “Oh, yes. Because we didn’t tag doesn’t mean we didn’t meet. And let me tell you that the connection bewteen all TPC members is very good, if not perfect. You see, we have what all other teams lack. That is the titles. I mean after Mike Montgomery retained his title, and after Joshua Dunn and I beat Lustfully Lullaby and The formorians we will be the ones challenging Digital Morality for the tag team championships. And after Moraless won the PTC Extreme Title and Digital Morality lost the World Title match we will prove them that we are the best tag team and that come Stable Wars The Player’s Club will have all the gold. I don’t even see a reason why we are participating in Stable Wars. We can’t win anything.” Rick: “That is if you win this match, if you even get the shot and if you beat the tag team champs.” Nick: “Are you doubting me here, Rick? Let me tell you something about Moraless and.....no, wait a minute......” Rick: “What’s wrong?” Nick: “Is Moraless in the building?” Rick: “No.” Nick: “Good, now where was I? I remember.....Moraless can be challenging for the PTC Extreme Title, but he will never be the true hardcore or extreme icon if he didn’t beat me. And to be honest......I don’t expect that chicken to do so.” Rick: “But shall we get out of this lockerroom now?” Nick: “No! I am showing to you that this guy isn’t even here. And it wouldn’t suprise me if he doesn’t show up at all. Just like all those other low lives who are set to face one of the five. One of the five greatest men to ever grace the UWF. And I am not just talking about the ring, but all the successes we made possible were possible due to the fact that The Player’s Club is so strict in accepting new members. Now I was asked to join, and the rest is history.” [Some noice is heard at the end of the hallway and Nick Persia looks at hsi watch.] Nick: “You know what? Let’s go because we have a busy schedule ahead of us.” Rick: “Urhm..........right.” [They leave the lockerroom, but before they do Nick Persia checked the corridor. Nobody was seen so they stepped out quick and pretended like they nothing happened.] Rick: “How do you feel about your opponents?” Nick: “I feel sorry for them.” Rick: “That’s all? Because you are acting all cocky here.” Nick: “Am I? Give me one reason not to be! I beat opponent after oponent and I can surely take on the Formorians. The identical twins will get an identical beating. They are legends in Ireland? Well, I am en enigma in Holland. And after I kicked their asses I will be the nightmare of Ireland.” Rick: “We need to go now, we’ve run out of time.” [Scene fades out to the commercials]
