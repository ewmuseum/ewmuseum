UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #179

Oh please..

20th January (year unrecorded)

Jamie Shock — Jamie's hotel

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Jamie Shock has always lost VERY narrowly to his first opponent after Christmas. This year he had an excuse - he has had a broken foot. Stepping into a ring with 'top notch' Malcolm Kennedy is quite unfortunate, and was just that for Jamie Shock. You see, Malcolm is classed as a 'friend' to Jamie Shock, and with that in mind, Shock's method of winning matches isn’t a method he'd do on such a friend. Shock wins matches with a crowbar, as many cheap shots as possible, and as many devastating blows as he can achieve in a short space of time. Unfortunately, Jamie couldn’t introduce a crowbar to Malcolm's head. By the time Shock realized it was time to resort to the old methods of putting an opponent away, it was too late. Malcolm was already coming back, and what a come back it was. An elbow drop right into the cold heart of Jamie Shock, almost piercing him with the impact. With that done, it was only a matter of seconds before the inevitable occurred. Jamie Shock was defeated, for the 3rd year running after Christmas.] [You'd think that Jamie Shock would have learnt that training, even over Christmas, is a crucial necessity for a wrestler. But no, despite his three and a half year career, he still hasn’t grasped that simple fact. And he payed for it. Big time. White Wolf beat him, and now Malcolm Kennedy has. Two opponents, in all honesty, that Jamie Shock would prefer to beat. Malcolm Kennedy now though has the ability to boast that he's beat the best wrestler in the UWF. Some may argue that fact, but it's as true as the sky is indeed blue. To add insult to injury, Malcolm Kennedy is now the #1 contender for the World Title because he beat Jamie.] [Jamie Shock is one pissed off wrestler. Surprise you it may, but it's not because of Malcolm Kennedy beating him for the #1 contenders shot, against the new UWF World Champion. What is it then? - Jamie will soon tell you.] [We open in Jamie Shock's flat. A cold, dark place. We're in the same room as it seems we're always 'forced' into. The room with the desk. The room with the chair. The room with the lamp on the desk. The typical, plain, room. Jamie Shock is in the chair, ready to begin. A dynamic light shines at him from the lamp on the desk in front of him, which he rests his hands on. Shock wears a dusty, black tuxedo, and a black top hat. His face is worn and torn - you would think that if Jamie happened to step into the wind, the dry skin would be blown off, like sand, and all that would be remaining is a skull. A sick, sadistic smile for once isn't present, but instead an enraged frown, which causes his top hat to be tilted forward slightly. He begins.] Shock: 'Why is Mike Montgomery still breathing?' [He leans back slightly, rolling his deep eyes as he grits his teeth.] Shock: 'I have no idea. It's a mystery. It should be the ninth wonder of the world. Why.. WHY is that useless, over-rated, shite still living?! If it's one person I despise, it's him. That man is ruining this federation with his fucking unprofessional antics. He's making a mockery of everything I DID to build this federation up to where it is today.' 'Fuck you Montgomery.' 'I know you're going to be watching. Let me just tell you, that you are the most over-rated wrestler in the entire history of this business. You're nothing but a lower carder who for some reason unknown to man managed to get an ounce of power. I'm the power behind this federation, I'm the turbines which keep this federation going, believe you and me you're nothing. NOTHING! I squinted with the crowd as 'Spiff The Magic Monkey' made his appearance again. You know why we all squinted, and sighed in disbelief? Because of embarrassment. How the hell can people take you seriously when you pretend to have a talking monkey as a best friend and an ally? How? Please tell me, because it's driving me mad.' 'You shouldn’t even have the fuckin' privilege to wrestle me. I'm royalty when you're concerned. You're scum. S-C-U-M. SCUM that's managed to 'book' his way to the top because some twat gave you power. I'll let it be known again that you are an embarrassment. Nobody likes you Mike. Everyone in the backstage thinks you're nothing, NOTHING but a giant kid who thinks he's a big shot. The way you strut your stuff, flashing your money around.. all the time talking to a monkey. I eliminated the Bunnies Montgomery. Do you have any idea why I and Ruff Ryder did that Mike? Do you know why? Let me tell you - because they were a disgrace and were getting people to think of the federation as a joke. People thought the federation was loosing fans and needed some new gimmick to spice it up!' 'The fact is, dick, that I saved this federation, as I have done in times before. I saved this federation from becoming a joke, a disgrace, a mockery. Just when the federation has managed to regain that respect from the wrestling community, Mr. Wise Balls decides to bring a bloody monkey into the picture. My blood boils when I think of everything this federation is.. and then think of the man destroying it internally.' 'Montgomery, you're wrecking this federation. You don’t see it, your head is too far in your bowels. Pull it out, suffer some piles, then while you're on the toilet pick up a god damned wrestling magazine and read what people have to say. This federation is one of the best professional federations in the world. P-R-O-F-E-S-S-I-O-N-A-L you twat. You're not professional Mike, you're beyond a joke. I feel sorry for myself when I think of the respect I wasted on Ezra Kazee and Joshua Dunn. Respect they earned through achievements. Unfortunately, they lost everything.. respect, dignity, everything.. when they joined Mike's little club.' 'How far do you guys lick Mike's back guys? The fact that Ezra Kazee simply regarded my match on Sunday as an easy win for Montgomery, with no doubt.. because 'he's a superior athlete' gives me the image of a puppet. Quite frankly, Montgomery must be controlling you like a mere hand puppet to say something like that. Ezra, do you know who I am? Do you know that I am the most successful UWF wrestler of all time? because I am, oh yes.' 'The fact that Ezra is aligned with Montgomery is one of the many reasons you should learn about people before you do things with them. If Ezra Kazee, a wrestling veteran of GIWF fame, had taken.. at least half an hour to find out just who the hell Mike Montgomery was, he wouldn’t have touched him with a barge pole. He would have declined Mike's offer and left him, knowing that he is indeed nothing but a laughing stop in the UWF. Believe me, unpopular I may be, I still listen to people's opinions.' 'Joshua.. mate.. fellow Brit.. you have no fucking excuse you Scottish twat. Oh you can think you're all everything you want to be by aligning yourself with Montgomery and Kazee, but at the end of the day, you're an added line to the joke. You could say that you're what makes the poor joke, actually funny. You're a disgrace to that fine mountainous country of Scotland, Dunn. I mean - aligning yourself with someone who steals WWE catchphrases? Come on, if I imagined anyone to have any sense, I thought it would be you. It shows I wouldn’t make a good psychiatrist.' [The Grand Slam Champion adjusts his tie before he turns onto another subject.] 'I've said all I want to say about them though for the time being. I know of the matches on the card for SAS, and although I dont think that Montgomery is main event worthy, as he seems to do, I am quite looking forward to doing something about this unfunny joke. It's old. 'Knock Knock's' live for ever.. but unfortunatly for three individuals, one certain character doesnt.' 'Mike, I look forward to beating you in more ways than one. Some people call me an artist.. they say I create works of arts. They say, that instead of a paintbrush, I use a crowbar. Blood is my paint. Now if that were true, I'm positive I create masterpieces.' 'I call my next piece, the Bloody Monkey' [With that, Jamie Shock pulls a crowbar out from underneath the desk, and with a huge amount of force, slams it infront of him onto the table. We end on that.]
