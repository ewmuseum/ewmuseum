Pete the PG Tips Chimp — Round 2

Pete the PG Tips Chimp

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Lights!] [Camera!] [Action!] [As graphics that would have earned a C in high school, we’re welcomed with a giant, swirling ‘10’ … welcomed into the loving embrace of the nightly news.] Generic Female Newscaster: "Hello and welcome to WTAJ News Channel 10, your home for news and events here in Altoona, Pennsylvania!" Generic Male Newscaster: "Yes indeed, nameless blonde to my right! In tonight’s news … a new FDA study shows that a common soft drink consumed by many people in our viewing area, that you are likely drinking right this second, will not only kill you, your children, your pets, and burn down your home … but it will also desecrate the graves of your relatives, and ruin their chances at heaven! Stay tuned for what drink that is!" Generic Female Newscaster: "Also, a verified national security threat has landed here in Altoona. By all accounts, a large nuclear device is within a very populated building right here in Altoona … what building is it? We’ll tell you shortly." Generic Male Newscaster: "And on the lighter side of the news … by recent reporters, the world’s first, and hopefully only, wrestling chimpanzee has apparently moved into a small home here in Altoona. Pete the PG Tips Chimp, a former member of the much-protested Universal Wrestling Federation, as been the subject of PETA protests for years … but he looks so gosh darn cute when he drinks tea, I don’t think anyone could protest his new residence here in our wonderful city." Generic Female Newscaster: "The only question now is … with this little furry instrument of destruction in our home town, just what is his plan?" [Dun dun dun!] ----------------------------------------------- [Pete the PG Tips Chimp strolls through the kitchen of Little Timmy’s small home, wearing only a pair of tiny Superman tightie-whities, with a glass in his tiny little hand.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: " *burp* " [And with that … Pete scratches his ass and wanders off.] ------------------------------------------------ [In another part of the house … Little Timmy has come face to face with his father. The old man stalks across the little bedroom (decorated with oh-so-many UWF posters … and, of course, a signed photo of the Raving Jewish Destroyer, the only one ever given or desired that wasn’t on a debit card receipt), while Timmy sits at the foot of his bed, staring up with a crooked smile on his wittle face.] Dad: "I don’t even understand how you managed to find him!" Little Timmy: "Well …" Dad: "For the love of Rod, Timmy … he’s a trained athlete! A vicious, destructive force of nature!" Little Timmy: "No! Pete is my friend!" Dad: "He drank every piece of tea in the house before we managed to dry him out!" Little Timmy: "Pft." Dad: "There’s a testicle on the kitchen counter!!!" Little Timmy: "Err … yeah. That was John Adams’. He brought it back for me as a thank you for getting him back into wrestling. I think? He kind of spit it at me. But he sounded happy when he went "ooo ooo ooo!", at least." Dad: "And do I even want to know about THAT?!" [Timmy’s dad points accusingly to the corner of the room … where a very depressed older man in a suit coat with a large yellow hat is chained to the side of the bed.] Man in the Yellow Hat: "For the love of Christ, are you ALL retarded?! LET ME GO!!!!" [Timmy shrugs.] Little Timmy: "You never know when a guy in a yellow hat will be needed when you’re managing a chimpanzee." Dad: "How could you possibly be managing Pete? You’re twelve years old!" Little Timmy: "Pete’s my friend." Dad: "I just … I want that monster out of my damn house!" Little Timmy: "Fine! [And with that … the little one darts off.] ---------------------------------------------- [Downstairs … Pete the PG Tips Chimp is locked into a deathly iron staring contest with a kitten.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "…" Kitten: "…" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "…" Kitten: "…" [My apologies. Pete’s apparently just very bored.] ----------------------------------------------- [Marching down the staircase, Little Timmy silently fumes about his father.] Man! My dad is a giant poopy head. I just don’t understand the issue. Sure, Pete is a wild animal, ruthlessly ripped from his natural habitat, forced to both test and be a spokesman for an evil British tea company, only to steal away to pursue his love of participating in a vaguely homosexual sporting contest where he bites the left testicle off of any who stand in his path for utter domination of the world, all for the insane devotion of thousands and thousands of drunken retards … but that’s not the point! [Little Timmy stomps down hard.] He never lets me have anything my way! This is just like that time I brought home the runaway puppy. Man, I loved that little guy so much … but he poops in the house one time, and all of a sudden he’s gone, my dad has a new hat, and we’re not allowed to eat Chinese food anymore. I hate it! I hate him! [Timmy growls.] And you know what … wait a second, these stairs sure got long. [Finishing his inner-narrative, Little Timmy hops over the last three steps, and spins around through the kitchen door.] [… where he can only find Pete the PG Tips Chimp and the kitten, locked in their timeless struggle of wills.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "…" Kitten: "…" [Little Timmy can only stare, wondering what could possibly be happening. If only he could hear Pete’s own inner-narrative …] Oooo ooo ooo ooo. Tea. Ooo ooo oo. [… then he would understand it all.] [Nevertheless, the battle would be short-lived … Little Timmy’s eyes go wide in horror as he spies the time on the microwave.] Little Timmy: "PETE!!!!!" [Pete the PG Tips Chimp is ripped out of his eternal struggle, leaping seven feet into the air. He grabs onto the hanging light in the kitchen, crying out in fright.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "OOO OOO OOO OOO AWWW OOOO OOO AWW!!!!" [Pete shakes a disapproving finger at Little Timmy. The kitten seems to be smiling. Timmy, however, only points at the microwave.] Little Timmy: "Pete … holy poop, we only have an hour and a half until your next tournament match!!! My dad was yelling at me, and … and … we have to get to that match! You’re going to no-show!" [Pete the PG Tips Chimp … points one hairy chimpanzee middle finger at the sky in Timmy’s direction.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo." Little Timmy: "Pete … please! You know what that Aussie bastard will do to you if you no-show! We have to get to the arena!" [The chimpanzee finger wags silently.] Little Timmy: "Please, Pete … everyone was so happy when you made your triumphant return in the first round, and you kicked that John Adams guy’s butt! You can’t let your fans down!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo." Little Timmy: "But … what about your opponent, Pete?" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo?" Little Timmy: "Didn’t they tell you? You’re facing Blaise McClancy!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Pft." Little Timmy: "No, Petey … the McClancy’s are from the UK!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo?!" Little Timmy: "Cross my heart, hope to die! You know what that means, Petey …" [Petey’s eyes cloud over as a horrible flashback runs through his little chimp mind.] ----------------------------------------------------- [Eight years ago, a tiny Pete the PG Tips Chimp is strapped to a chair, his every appendage tied down. Standing over him … the evil Baron Von Schugenhouser!] BVS: "Ve have vays of vaking you talk, vittle chimpanzee!" [And just like that, the evil Baron pulls out … a pitcher of PG Tips Tea.] BVS: "Tell us vhat ve vant to know, Pete! Tell us or the vea gets vit!!!!" [Pete lashes about futiley, but won’t give up his secrets.] BVS: "Then so ve it!" [And just like that, the pitcher is tipped, it’s contents rushing to certain doom … and just as the first drop hits the ground …] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!" ----------------------------------------------------- [Back in the present, Little Timmy blinks.] Little Timmy: "What in the hell did that have to do with anything?" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "OOOO OOO OOO OOOO OOO!!!!!!!" Little Timmy: "Alright! Alright! I’m sorry! It must have been horrible!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo." Little Timmy: "But that’s exactly why you have to do this, Pete! You’re not a little chimpanzee anymore … but what about all of those other little chimpanzees that can’t fight for themselves?" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo?" Little Timmy: "Yes … the ones with teeth that aren’t sharp enough to bite off testicles." Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "OOO OOO!!" Little Timmy: "I swear, Pete! The McClancy’s … own PG Tips Tea Company!!!" [Cue the ominous music, as Pete can only cry out …] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO OOO OOO OOO!!!!!" [Pete the PG Tips Chimp falls to the floor, where he hugs Timmy’s leg.] Little Timmy: "I swear it’s true, Pete. The McClancy’s are an evil British clan that have run the tea syndicate for years. They are the natural enemies to your kind. They are the ones who ripped you from your family!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Grrrrrrrrr!" Little Timmy: "I mean … either that or they’re a crazy Irish wrestling group that takes things way too seriously and just happen to all look like baboons, but … seriously, I think the tea syndicate is more realistic and likely, Pete." [Petey nods with a renewed sense of purpose.] Little Timmy: "Excellent. I just know you’re going to rock, Pete … you’re going to avenge your family, rescue millions of little chimpanzees these evil McClancy punks have used for their sinister PG Tips advertising purposes, and you’re going to advance in the tournament to boot!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo!" Little Timmy: "We’ve still got time … all we need to do is get you into your wrestling gear …" [Pete quickly yanks off his Superman underwear, standing in his furry nakedness.] Little Timmy: "Excellent. So that’s done … now all we have to do is get to the arena. I’ll just take my dad’s car, and … jumping Jehosephat!!!!" [Timmy cries out as he looks out the front door window. On their lawn … area massive contingent of reporters, cameramen, gaping onlookers, and utterly hammered UWF fans, desperately looking for a glimpse of their hero. WTAJ Altoona has arrived at Timmy’s home … how can they possibly make the deadline with this mess blocking their only exit?] Little Timmy: "The back door?" [Quiet, child! Be realistic!] Little Timmy: "Sorry." [Pete pounds his little chimp fists against the door.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo ooo ooo ooo ooo!!! GRRRRR!" [Suddenly, inspiration strikes our tiny hero. The human one.] Little Timmy: "Pete … I have a plan! We can still make it to the tournament … but only if we’re quick! Just wait right here for one second …" ---------------------------------------------------------- 12:15 AM EST … Forty Five Minutes until the deadline. [On the outside, some random reporter is sipping a warm glass of tea. His cameraman stands by, filming the tiny house nonstop.] Reporter: "Dude … Altoona sucks." Cameraman: "Yep." Reporter: "Seriously, dude. I mean … look at us! I have a Master’s degree in journalism … do you know the sort of student loans I have to pay off because of that? And what girl in college wants to hump a journalism major?" Cameraman: "I wouldn’t. You know … if I was a girl … and in college … and …" Reporter: "And now look at me! Six years of college, and I’m standing on the lawn of a drunk redneck’s house hoping to get an interview with a WRESTLING CHIMPANZEE!!!!!" Cameraman: "How is that even possible? I mean … how do you teach a chimpanzee to wrestle? Ridiculous!" [The cameraman picks his nose.] Cameraman: "All I know is Core would never let a chimpanzee wrestle. That’s why they’re the best. If you aren’t a middle aged man with a massive ego and a generic heel gimmick, Core won’t ever accept you. Best. Fed. Ever!" Reporter: "Whatever. I’m going to shoot myself tonight. Interviewing a chimpanzee would be the lowest point in my life … if it wasn’t for the fact that the chimpanzee won’t even GIVE me an interview. I’m beneath a chimpanzee? Christ!" [As the two continue their meaningless bickering, we spy our heroes spying on them from beneath the news van.] Little Timmy: "We need their van if we’re going to get to the arena so you can avenge your family, Pete … you know what you have to do." [As the bickering continues … Pete rises behind them to his full height of two feet three inches. His movement is silent … nearly ghostlike. His face is painted with various shades of green and black, completely camouflaged … around his neck, a necklace lined with dried, shriveled testicles. Pete the PG Tips Chimp has become more than death incarnate … he’s become a Vietnamese-era chimpanzee of pain!] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "OOOOO OOOO OOO OOO OOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!" [With an uncanny speed, Pete the PG Tips Chimp is five feet into the air before the reporter can even turn around. He doesn’t even have a chance to scream before Pete is ripping into his face, biting his nose, clawing at his eyeballs. It’s a horrible, furry scene of terror. The cameraman cries out.] Cameraman: "No!" [The cameraman rears back, swinging his camera with all his might … but Pete is a blazing ball of fur with vengeance in his heart! He dives away, leaving the camera to go crashing into the reporter’s face.] Cameraman: "Holy shit!!!" [Seconds later, Pete the PG Tips Chimp’s face is buried in the cameraman’s groin … a sickening rip, a horrified scream, and then the crumpled mass of a man falls to the ground, crying as he curls into the fetal position.] Little Timmy: "Yes!!!" [Timmy, likewise painted, slides out from under the van. Pete spits the torn testicle off onto the ground with disgust. But then … his tiny chimp eyes spy the tea the reporter had been drinking, and he leaps over to the spilled glass, desperately trying to lick any remaining tea off the grass before it can all seep into the ground.] Little Timmy: "Pete! No! We have to go!" [Timmy throws all of his strength into yanking Pete away. Petey gets in a few more licks, but finally he’s willing to go.] Little Timmy: "Come, Pete … we have to go! To the chimp-mobile!" [And so they dart into the van.] -------------------------------------------- 12:30 PM EST … Thirty Minutes Until the Deadline. [Timmy’s father looks around the kitchen strangely, pondering over the missing child, vanished chimp-wrestler, and strange water-paint set lying out.] Dad: "Timmy? You here?" [No response.] Dad: "Tim? Where have you gone to?" [His child missing, Timmy’s dad comes to the only solution he can think of. He picks up the telephone …] Dad: "Awesome. Kid’s gone … time to call the hooker." [And we go.] ----------------------------------------------- 12:35 PM EST … Twenty Five Minutes Until the Deadline. [Little Timmy is sitting on top of the unconscious body of the reporter, using him as a booster seat. Pete the PG Tips Chimp hops into the floorboard to work the pedal. Timmy fiddles with the keys … and the van roars into life!] Little Timmy: "Yay! We’re on our way, Petey!" [Suddenly … a pack of UWF fans comes charging towards the van.] UWF Fan #1: "Oh my God … that guy’s missing a testicle! Pete must be in the … *hiccup* … van!!! PETEY!!!" Little Timmy: "Oh sweet Bobby no … Petey, floor it!!!" [The van comes flying out of park, driving straight at the pack of drunken fans.] Little Timmy: "Die wrestling geeks! DIE~!!!" [Wrestling fans scatter in every direction as the WJAJ News Van comes barreling through the driveway, heading for freedom.] Little Timmy: "Kick butt. We’re free at last, Pete … and we’ve still got twenty one minutes to spare to get to the arena!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo ooo ooo?" Little Timmy: "Never mind how any of this is possible. Just drive, Pete … for the love of all those poor chimpanzee children the evil McClancy’s will kidnap, torture, rape, and test on, drive I say!!!" [And so they do. And while nine men, women, and children die on their strange, death-defying, adventure through the mean streets, never slowing down for even one moment as Pete sits on the gas pedal … a quickly approaching deadline means none of said adventure can be described herein. Probably for the best. Timmy wouldn’t survive well in prison. And Pete would likely only be placed in a zoo, where he would lead his fellow animals in an uprising against their evil zookeepers, bringing the end of humanity in a furry blaze … and that would really help none of us at all.] ------------------------------------------------------ 12:55 PM EST … Five Minutes Until the Deadline. [Finally, with only minutes to spare, the WJAJ News Van crashes into Kenny Mercury’s limousine, ending their horrible, horrible journey to the GTT arena in … Whosawhatsawherevers-ville. With a cry, Pete the PG Tips Chimp leaps out of the van onto the concrete outside, beats his tiny chimpanzee chest, and lets out a war cry.] Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "RAR!!!!!!!!!!!" [Petey begins to dart off towards the arena, ready to seek out and obliterate the evil McClancy tea syndicate single-handedly … but Little Timmy leaps out of the van, calls out, and stops him in mid-stride.] Little Timmy: "Oh! Pete!" Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo ooo ooo?" Little Timmy: "I just wanted to give you the only advice my father ever gave me before your big match." Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "Ooo!" Little Timmy: "Winning isn’t everything, Petey … but if you lose, we’re selling all of your toys and giving you to that mean Chinese guy with the wandering eye. Oh, and your mother was a drunken whore." Pete the PG Tips Chimp: "…" Little Timmy: "Wait a second … that wasn’t inspiring at all! My dad’s a butthole!" [Pete can only nod in silent agreement.] Little Timmy: "You know what … poop it, Pete. I … I just want you to know … I …" [A tear silently courses down Timmy’s tender face.] Little Timmy: "I love you Petey!!!" [Timmy throws his arms around his little chimpanzee friend.] Little Timmy: "Now go out there and beat the snot out of that Irish punk. Good luck!!!" [And with that … Pete the PG Tips Chimp walks off to face his destiny. A destiny that will, win or lose, involve the removal of a large Irishman’s testicle.] [Fin.]
