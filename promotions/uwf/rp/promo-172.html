UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #172

THC is Back

TodaY (year unrecorded)

Bruce Gordy — The House of Pain

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Thunder strikes the ground. The blackness covers the sky. A raven calls from a dead tree. Lightning flashes and shows the outline of a shadowy figure. It is Bruce Gordy.) Bruce Gordy: I have returned. And now, I come to take what is rightfully mine. I am the most hardcore wrestler of all time, and the H.I.T. can't say nothing about that. He's going down, and I'm the one bringing him there. The hardcore revolution has begun, you can't stop me. THC is back and its face is mine. The face of... THE INNOVATOR OF PAIN!!! Bruce Gordy: UWF... you can run, but you can't hide. The Element is after you. Soon, my wrath will be felt. Be ware, for this is only the beginning! (A lightning bolt streaks across the sky, and Bruce Gordy vanishes into thin air.) (FADE TO BLACK!)
