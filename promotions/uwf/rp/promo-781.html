UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #781

Overly Qualified (1)

22 Nov 2004

Seymour Almasy — Almasy Apartment

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life sucks sometimes. Fact. A fact that Seymour Almasy knows quite well. In the space of two weeks, he lost his fourth round match in GTT4, the UWF Intercontinental and North American Titles, and found out that he would have to soon forfeit his half of the tag team titles. But not all was bad. The PTC Global Championship was his once again. And with the Contest of Champions looming on the horizon, he had a chance to get back on the right tournament track as well… …Against the man who took the North American Championship from him. If you thought Seymour was mad before, just wait. Just…wait. ------ It was another night at home for Seymour. Going out to drink on a Monday night was tacky, and besides, he’d already gone out Saturday night. Thus, Seymour’s Monday evening was spent not unlike many other young men would spend it: planted on the couch, with takeout pizza and a two-liter bottle of soda on the coffee table, getting ready to watch Monday Night Football in his apartment. Seymour Almasy: Yevon, will they get the Pistons/Pacers brawl off the news? I swear, it’s been the only thing on ESPN since Friday night… A surprisingly ordinary statement from Almasy, right? Ever since divorce from Laura, he had started to become more and more of an ordinary young adult, who just happened to be a professional wrestler. Seymour Almasy: A pizza pie, some soda, and football. What else could I ask for? The rhetorical question, to Seymour at least, clearly had an answer. Seymour Almasy: I can think of at least one thing, and that’s a win over Killer Dude in the qualifying round of the Contest of Champions. Two weeks ago, Mike Montgomery took it upon himself to attack Clyde and myself Clyde escaped with his title. Sadly, I wasn’t so fortunate. A look of utter rage crossed Seymour’s face, as he picked up a piece of pizza, angrily chomping down on the slice, before washing it down with a swig of soda. Seymour Almasy: So now, I have to live with the fact that I lost the North American Title to a man who’s been nominate twice for the ever-prestigious UWF Jobber of the Year award. All because of Mike Montgomery and his steel chair. But you know what? I’ll worry about Mike later. Right now, the only thing I’m focused on is Killer Dude. He shook his head, and banged his fist on the table, nearly upending his cup of soda. The events of October and November had, for the most part, made this fall the worst season of Seymour’s career. Seymour Almasy: As far as I’m concerned, Dude, you’re walking around with something that belongs to me. But that’s not what matters to me when I step into the ring with you this Sunday afternoon on Supremacy. This Sunday is about beating you so soundly that I make it absolutely evident that the only way someone like you could ever beat me was with the aid of that piece of shit Montgomery. You humiliated me two weeks ago. By defeating me, you have stained my pristine record forever. I can’t erase the fact that you defeated me, but by annihilating you, I can reduce that loss to the answer to a trivia question in UWF Trivial Pursuit. The ESPN pregame show continued on Almasy’s big-screen television, as Seymour shook with rage. Seymour Almasy: I have never been in the Contest of Champions. When it’s happened, I’ve always either been out of the fed, or the holder of the World Title at the time. But make no mistake about it…this thing is tailor made for me. A tournament for the best of the best. After the qualifying rounds, it’s a one night tournament, with eight men. To win it, you have to win three matches in one night. For someone with my stamina and skills…I’ll win this thing in a walkover. Despite his arrogance, however, there is more than a bit of nervousness under the surface. After all, no one gave Craig Goat a chance in hell against him, and it’s Goat in the GTT4 Semi-Finals, not Seymour. Seymour Almasy: Despite having absolutely no wrestling talent to speak of, I’m not going to let up on you, Killer Dude. I’m not going to show you any mercy whatsoever. Once again, the Final Fantasy chomps angrily into his pizza. Seymour Almasy: None… Another chomp. Seymour Almasy: At… Another. Seymour Almasy: ALL. A final chomp, finishing the pizza up to the crust. Unlike many people, Seymour actually bothered to eat the crust, washing it down with several healthy drinks of soda before speaking again. Seymour Almasy: You see, I have a lot to lose. One loss to Killer Dude is terrible enough. Two would be absolutely and utterly horrific. I cannot allow another loss to this…embarrassment. And to put it in terms that even Killer Dude can understand, I WILL NOT ALLOW IT TO HAPPEN AGAIN. I simply will not. This month, my nearly year-long streak of a perfect record in tournaments came to an end at the hands of a man with practically no wrestling experience, and that was STILL less embarrassing than losing to Killer Dude in the first round of the Contest of Champions would be! Almasy reached out, to switch over to ABC, just as the Monday Night Football opening began to play. He tried to relax, tried to forget about wrestling for the moment, but Killer Dude refused to leave his mind. Seymour Almasy: Come Sunday, I will be able to put the humiliating events of last week behind me. I will pin Killer Dude’s shoulders to the canvas. And I will advance on to the main portion of the Contest of Champions, to be joined by seven of the UWF’s best. And even then, none of them will be able to stand before me. I’ve been to the top of the mountain, and I’ve been thrown off that mountain. Now, it’s time to go back up again. And there is NO ONE in the Universal Wrestling Federation who can stop me. If I have to beat everyone on the roster to prove it, so be it. It starts with Killer Dude, and it will end at The Clash in January, when I will once again raise the UWF World Heavyweight Championship overhead for all to see. As the Chiefs kick off to the Patriots, Seymour places another piece of pizza on his paper plate, and pours himself a new cup of soda. Once again, he is calm, tranquil, no different from any other young man his age. Seymour Almasy: And anyone who could possibly think otherwise? He smiles invitingly, as if thinking that no one could doubt him. Seymour Almasy: They’re living in a fantasy world. With that, Almasy said no more, watching the football game intently, enjoying his Monday evening. For tomorrow, he would be back to training. Back to figuring out how to end Killer Dude… (Fin)
