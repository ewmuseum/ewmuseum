Hell Fire (Match Stipulations)

22 Jul 2004

Sacrifice — Home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with Sacrifice trying not to lose his temper after being killed on Final Fantasy then suddenly a flash of light blinds the camera when the scene finally comes back the screen to the t.v has been busted. The camera slowly turns around to see Sacrifice going behind his bar and grabbing a beer. Sacrifice>> Do you want a beer? Then he pops the cap and begins to drink down his beer after he finishes his beer he grabs another and heads outside and lays in the hammock finally he turns over a turns up the stereo and begins to dance to the cha cha slide before the song is over he is setting down writing ideals for his next match. Sacrifice>> Lets see they've got me in this match with The Real Bitch what kind of a match is it. Then he glances back at the roster and sees that the match has no stipulation to it and smiles. Sacrifice>> A cage no its to easy for him to escape and run a coffin match no to easy for me to trip into it Got it a Hell Fire Match! A pit of fire first person to throw their opponent into the fire wins the match! Then he begins to write down the match on a slip and sends it through the mail the camera glances at the letter and realizes the address UWF and turns the camera back on to Sacrifice were he continues to drink his beer and listen to the radio. Finally the scene fades to black with hells bells playin on the radio.
