UWF (2001–07) Promo

Al Crud Revives the North American Title

circa Oct 2003

Al Crud — circa Oct 2003

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sets upThe North American Title Tournament that crowns a champion at Live and Loud 3 — the tournament's semi-final, Mentok the Mindtaker vs. Ruff Ryder, is served on that same card.
"Sooooo ... let's get right down to business then, eh dudes?" [Al farts, but continues on like a trooper.] "I know what you all are thinkin' ... you're goin', 'Hey Al, how are you going to save Live and Loud? You don't know nothin!' But you're wrong ... cuz the Crudster knows all, baby! The Crudster knows exactly what the PPV's missin', cuz it's exactly what he's wanted back since realized it ..." [Al looks around at the crowd and smiles.] "Where's Crud's belt!? "Everyone knows the Flatulent Fighter made it into the big time in the GIWF ... it was the beginning of the legarthic legacy! Al Crud went into battle, and he was throwin' fools over this rope, and over that rope, and over this rope, and over that rope ..." [Crud runs around the ring, pointing to each rope as he talks.] "And that's how your buddy Al became the Smelly Superstar ... by winning the North American Championship!" Rick: "He's not ..." Larson: "Crud isn't exactly good at beating around the bush ... I think it's obvious he is." Crud: "And that's why your buddy Al brought you ... THIS!" [Al Crud reaches into his bag, snatching out ...] *North American title image* [Al smiles stupidly as he holds the belt.] Crud: "If this is gonna be the Crudster's home, it needs a bit more history to spruce it up ... so it just makes sense to have the Cruder's very own title back! So now we got's to find us a new champeen, huh?" [Al farts loudly.] Crud: "So Crud was thinkin' about how he could do the match ... and he was doing this, and he was doing that ... he was a shuckin' and a jivin' ... first he was just gonna give it to himself to hold up his pants, but it didn't fit ... so then the Crudster was gonna give it to the first person who brought him a beer, but then he remembered he's rich now so he can get all the beer he wants ... then he was gonna give it to the first chick who slept with him ..." [Al's head drops.] Crud: "Anyway, that was like six weeks ago, so I gave up and just decided to have that one smart dude think up somethin'. So we're's gonna have us a fun tournament thingie ... cuz next week, we're gonna have twelve dudes go into the ring in the fastest tournament I think we could do. And those twelve dudes are gonna be in FOUR Triple Threat matches ... and the winners of those matches are gonna go to Live and Loud 3, where's they's gonna fight each other in a mini-tournament. Two matches, and the winners fight in the finals to crowd Al Crud's personal North American Champion!" [Al farts to the cheers of the crowd again.] Crud: "And who's gonna be fightin'? Well Crud looked around, and by God the Crudster found the best dudes and dudettes he could find! "First we got Ruff Ryder ... I don't really know why, cuz that dude pisses me off, but watcha gonna do. "Then there's Digimon Mortally ... he's kinda cool, I guess. "And we got Joshua Dunn ... cuz whoever wins that IC title match is gonna get the chance to be a double champ! "And there's Jay Quickness ... dude's got talent, even if Crud don't know nothin' he's sayin'. "And Laura Whittle ... cuz she's like evil and stuff, but those kids are so cute Al couldn't forget her. "Don't forget El Turtlé~!, cuz he makes me giggle, and the Crudster likes to giggle. "Then there's that Kenny Mercury guy ... he's kind of a jerk, but he's good. "And White Wolf, cuz the wOlF kicks ass! "And how about Killer Dude ... he makes me laugh too. "And Malcolm Kennedy ... he kicked Montgomery's ass, so he's okay in my book. Although hurtin' monkeys is NOT cool. "And Mike Mo--" [Al stumbles backwards, his eyes swimming.] "I'm sorry, Mike Montgomery sucks. I meant to say Mentok the Mindtaker, the greatest athlete in professional wrestling history." [Al pops back to life.] "And ... that's ... like, everyone, right?" [Al starts countin' on his fingers and toes.] "Oh! And the twelf ... twelve? Whatever ... that last dude's Ezra Kazee." Rick: "The Common Man?!" Larson: "I guess that's as good a way as any to announce a guy's return." Crud: "Cuz Ezra kicks ass, and his name rules! Ezzzzzra ... Eeeeeeeezzzzzzzzzzzzra! Just rocks. "Anywho ... there you go, guys. My PPV's gonna rule!" [Crud drops the microphone to his ass, lets loose a roar, and drops it to the mat, heading out of the ring.]
