UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #434

Realizing a Dream

6/1 (year unrecorded)

Kevin Starr

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'What is the meaning of this!' [An enraged Kevin Starr comes storming out of his hotel bedroom suite, in downtown... wherever WNU is taking place. His temper rises as he tightens his grip on the freshly sharpened pencil in his right hand.] “Damnit!” 'What happened?' [A new voice is heard, a voice that most of you probably don't recognize. This was the voice of Mike Michael, a good friend of Kevin Starr's.] [Mike watches Kevin Starr from his seated position on a black leather couch, as Kevin stomps across the room, glaring down at the paper he holds in his left hand. Kevin positions himself next to Mike and flops down on to the couch, not taking his beat red eyes off of the paper in front of him.] Kevin: 'What is the meaning of this?!' Mike: '... Oh, that must be the card. Well listen, at least you g-' [Kevin interrupts Mike because well... Mike's opinion means nothing to Kevin Starr.] Kevin: “I mean, what the HELL is the meaning of this?!” Mike: “Listen, the Tag Ti- Kevin: 'No seriously, what's the meaning of this?' Mike: 'Err.. what?' Kevin: 'It's this freaking crossword puzzle! The clue is 'A pronoun used to refer to the person or thing present, nearby, or just mentioned'. Is that what 'this' means?' I think it's 'this', but i'm not 100% sure. Mike: '...Wait... have you seen the card yet?' [Kevin jots down a few more random letters in the crossword puzzle... he’s concentrating an unusual amount for himself on this puzzle… must be a pretty important puzzle. After a few more silent seconds, Mike’s words finally register inside of Kevin Starr’s enormous brain.] Kevin: “You mean the one for WNU? Nah, it’s not that important anyway. I’m not even on WNU. Now shh… What’s a 4 letter word for snatch?” Mike: “Umm, Kevin… you really should grab yourself a WNU card cause you’re on it.” [Kevin begins to poke his forehead with the eraser of his pencil, muttering to himself.] Kevin: “… A 4 letter word for snatch… catch?... no that’s 5 letters… hmm… wait a minute! By Rod! What did you just say Mike?!” Mike: “You’re on the WNU card.” Kevin: “No, before that.” Mike: “Err… I think it was You really should grab yo-“ Kevin: “THAT’S IT!” [Mike ponders to himself, scratching his head.] Mike: “What’s it? Kevin: “Grab. That’s a 4 letter word for snatch. Nice job Mike.” [Kevin Starr enters the word in it’s correct slot after decoding the clue. Kevin smiles at Mike and gives him a great big wink… the friendly type of wink. Mike smashes his hand on his forehead and then slowly slides it down his face in frustration.] Mike: “Damnit, did you hear what I said? You’re on the WNU card! You are teaming up with Joshua Dunn for a shot at the Tag-Team Championship!” [Kevin Starr is in the middle of writing in another word when he hears Mike’s statement. The words quickly rush into Kevin’s brain, after hearing the words teaming up and Joshua Dunn in the same sentence, Kevin starts flipping out! His body temperature starts to rise, his eyebrows and mouth begin to twitch, his nose starts to flare, his eyes erupt in flames, he tightens his grip on the pencil causing the lead tip to break off under the pressure, ripping a huge hole through the crossword puzzle that he worked so hard on!] Kevin: “There is NO WAY IN HELL I’m TEAMING UP WITH THAT SMELLY BASTARD!” [Kevin throws his now broken pencil to the floor so fast that the tip actually pierces into the ground and gets stuck standing up. He grabs the paper as well and throws that full speed towards the wall, but he didn’t crumple it up, so the paper just kind of slowly fluttered to the ground 3 inches in front of Kevin Starr.] Mike: “But it’s for the Tag-Team Championship! Think about it, you’ll have gold around your waist again and that’s all that really matters. Kevin: “But it’s Joshua-FREAKING-DUNN! He made me poke a hole through that crossword puzzle! I’ve been working on that piece of crap for 3 hours! Mike: “I know but…” Kevin: “Listen Mike, I can not team up with the man that kidnapped my nephew just 9 months ago, putting my entire family through major stress for that one month that he was gone. And do you know why he did that? Why he kidnapped David? Not for revenge, not because I called him some sort of name, but because he wanted a shot at my Intercontinental Title. Think about it… he puts an 8 year old boy’s life in danger, for a title shot. Am I the only one that sees the problem there?” Mike: “Trust me Kev, he’s changed since you left. He’s a new guy. Kevin: “You know what happened last time I trusted you Mike? I ended up with food poisoning for a week! I’m through trusting you Damnit! Mike: “Just think of it though…” [Mike starts to crack a feint smile, waving his hands around trying to imagine some sort of mental picture.] Mike: “Kevin Starr… Tag-Team Champion of the World… making him just the 2nd man to hold the Television, the Shoot, the THC, the IC, and the Tag Titles in UWF History!” [Kevin Starr’s expression changes dramatically. He raises his face towards the sky, his eyes light up and begin to water up, his frown changes into a slight grin, his lips begin to shutter as he speaks silently, but we can tell what he is trying to say by lip reading… “Yes…yes… yes”] Kevin: “You know… that doesn’t sound so bad after all.” Mike: “And then after you win Stable Wars and then go on to win the World Championship, making you the 2nd ever Grand Slam Champion of the UWF.” [Kevin begins to nod his head up and down realizing the truth, a tear begins to mold in the corner of his right eye as it slowly begins it’s journey, slowly sliding down his cheek.] Kevin: “My rod Mike… you’re a genius! I need to win this match. Even if I have to team up with Joshua Dumb. Mike: “Damn right!” Kevin: “I mean, I’m the Greatest UWF Superstarr of All-Time! I have to be the Grand Slam Champ at some point! The only thing stopping me right now is Joshua Dunn. I don’t know how he’s going to react in our Tag Match. I know it doesn’t matter though, because I can take both Virtual Reality and More-or-less with both hands tied behind my back without Dunn. This match will be nothing.” Mike: “Err… yeah?” [Kevin glares over at Mike, cocking his eyebrow.] Kevin: “What was that hesitation for?” Mike: “Nothing it’s just… having Dunn on your team is a great asset. Especially when you’re facing a Hall of Famer, you shouldn’t let having Dunn go to waste.” Kevin: “What are you saying? That I can’t win without Dunn? Because I can… oh I know I can!” [Mike rolls his eyes.] Mike: “Yes… of course you can. But still, in order to win, you’re going to need to work as a team, as a unit.” Kevin: “Well I’m willing to work as a team, I don’t know about little Joshy though. As long as he doesn’t get out of line during the match I’m willing to work together… and as long as he spends a few more hours in that gym he was just in, to, you know, gain some actual muscle and lose all that fat, then I’ll be willing to work together. Because I don’t team up with just anybody, I can’t have some fat, smelly, slow, Scottish guy standing next to my muscular, chiseled, lean, fast body. Just doesn’t happen like that. So Josh, as long as you don’t spend more then 5 minutes in the ring at a time, get in my way, steal my moves, then we’ll be the next Tag-Team Champions of the world! [camera fades]
