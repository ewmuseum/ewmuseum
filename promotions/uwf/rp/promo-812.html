Sitting Down with Killer Dude

3 Dec 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(This episode in the saga of Killer Dude opens once again with his logo filling the entire screen. The scene then opens to a shot in the lobby of a Green Bay hotel. The hotel can be seen to be a fancy joint with marble floors and gold trim that outlines the walls. The glass doors at the front of the hotel slide open and Killer Dude walks into the lobby. He is wearing the same attire that he was the last time we saw him. As Killer Dude continues through the lobby, he spots another cameraman sitting down on a couch. Seeing a chance to cut another promo, Killer Dude approaches him) Killer Dude: Who would've thought it? For once in my life, I'm actually glad to see one of your kind. (Nervously, the cameraman looks up at Killer Dude) Cameraman: You're kidding, right? Killer Dude: No man. I mean it. On my way here, I had a chance to see more of my opponents' promos and I've got some things to get off of my chest. I'll be right back. (With those last words, Killer Dude walks around the corner, and takes his cell phone out of his pocket. He flips the phone open, presses a button and raises the phone slowly to his ear. After a few moments, Killer Dude begins speaking into the phone, and we are left to assume that someone picked up on the other line.) Killer Dude: Yo Punk, what's up? I'll be up in just a few minutes. Got a promo to cut, ya dig? See you in a few. Peace out. (Killer Dude hits another button, and closes the phone, ending his conversation with, apparently, Punk Rock. He places the phone carefully back onto a strap that is coming out of his warm-up pants, turns back around the corner, and sits down on a couch opposite where the cameraman is sitting) Killer Dude: Is that camera ready? Cameraman: Yes. Killer Dude: Good work. All right, count me down. (The cameraman starts the count at five, and Killer Dude begins to prepare. As the cameraman reaches zero, Killer Dude looks directly into the camera) Killer Dude: Today, I'm going to give a lesson in decision making. I've heard from a lot of personnel in this federation that the decisions that you make will affect your entire career and life. Well, no shit sherlock. What the hell do you people want for that obvious observation? A fucking cookie?! Do you think that you people are fucking geniuses because you have figured out the meaning of decision making? Well, the people I am facing this Sunday at Fandemonium must not, because the fans were stupid enough to vote them into a match with me, a rising superstar in this federation. (Killer Dude pauses for a moment and adjusts how he is sitting on the couch.) Killer Dude: You see, the fans abandoned you guys when they voted for you to appear in a match with me. Their voting will unfortunately be the cause of your last act in this federation. It is one that will leave you wondering for the rest of your lives why you didn't choose a different path. It will leave you wondering why you didn't choose to be the people your parents wanted you to be. It will leave you wondering why you didn't take the easy way out just this one time. Because you better believe me when I say that you all will have ample time after the matches you have with me at Fandemonium to contemplate the answers to the questions I have stated. Why, you ask? Because a road through me, is one that will not have a prosperous end for you. In fact, you could really say that a road through me is a dead end. (Killer Dude tilts his head back and smiles to himself) Killer Dude: It doesn't matter what you people say or do, because nothing will change these facts which I have stated today. I can't believe that it hasn't sunk in to you people yet. Your backgrounds don't matter. Neither do the months you spend in rehab after countless knee injuries. I don't give two shits about these things, because now, they don't matter. In fact, the only thing that matters is me. I am the one the people have voted for the most. I am the one people came to see. The sooner you all realize that, the better off you will be. (Killer Dude draws himself closer to the camera) Killer Dude: I just don't see why you people are denying the inevitable. Why do you people find it so hard to face the facts? Each time I hear you people talk, your stories change. Make up your damn minds, jackasses. Stop trying to talk your way out of a situation. It'll never work, all of your backtracking is proving nothing, and all it's doing is pissing me off even more than before. You people and all of your beliefs are nothing but a barrel of bullshit, and that is the only true fact behind this matter. It is people like all of you that ruin my buisiness, and a business it is. If you all are still trying to fool yourselves into thinking it's not, well then I already have the advantage over all of you. You all are on the ropes already and the matches haven't even started yet. I've ripped through far better than you all on my worst day, and on Sunday, I will be at my best, guaranteed. (Killer Dude sits back and crosses his left leg over his right.) Killer Dude: Do I place myself above you all? You damn right, I do. The only reason I do this is that I am above ALL of you. You all are a disgrace to the people who should be at the top. You are all handed chances on a silver platter, yet all you do is push them aside as if they were nothing more than a torn t-shirt. I do not see any of my opponents as a threat to me, because in my opinion, they are not. I see them for what they are and nothing more. I see them as wastes of god given talent. I see them as wastes of space, and most importantly, they are all wastes of my time. (Killer Dude pauses for a moment, pondering something.) Killer Dude: You people, my opponents, are not worth my time. Which actually leads me to wonder why I'm still talking about you all. It leaves me to wonder why I'm spending so much time in front of this camera when I can do much more better things with my time. I guess I could attribute it to the fact that I hate you all. Now I know for a fact that people love you all, seeing as how they voted for you to appear in matches with me, but I'm just gonna have to dissapoint all the fans in the arena on Sunday, because they will not get what they want. After I am through with you all, I will have walken out with three titles on my waist, and around my shoulders. (Killer Dude uncrosses his legs and draws in closer to the camera once again) Killer Dude: You see, you all have pushed me from my plateau. You have all inspired me to climb even higher up the mountain. You have re-lit the fire that burns in my soul. A fire that had dimmed to nothing as I became an injury-prone hack that was complacent about everything he did. I thank you all for that. I thank you for that, however I promise that I will not fail like I did in my last three turns with this company. Oh no, I will burn you all with that fire and I will make sure that there is nothing left when I'm done with you. (As he calms down slowly, Killer Dude continues) Killer Dude: I will not turn away what the fans want to see. I do not have the place. In fact, I will accept what they want with great pleasure. Pleasure in the fact that in one night, I can take out almost 3/4's of the people in this federation. Pleasure in the fact that I will make you all squeal in pain, as the fans watch on wishing they could do what I do. Pleasure in the sight of watching you all fall and grovel at my feet. Begging me to end the pain, once and for all. Your dreams will not save you. They will give you no reprieve in your hour of despair. For the only thing that can give that to you is me. And i will do it, when I'm tired of listening to your screams. (Killer Dude sits back down on the couch) Killer Dude: So, my opponents, do what you want. Compare me to the wars of the past. Do whatever it takes to prepare yourselves for the battles ahead. I want you all to be as comfortable as possible before you face me, because for you all there will be no allied forces. There will be no mistakes. No, for you all, all you have is me. Me, and a world of pain and suffering. A world that you will never return from. So sleep well, rest up before the battles ahead. You'll all need it. (Killer Dude tilts his head back, taking his eyes off the camera) Killer Dude: I am not laughing at you all. Normally, as you all know, I would be, but not this time. This time, I actually feel sorry for you all. Sorry for the fact that I'm going to do to you what you truly deserve to have done to you. Sorry for the lonesome fact that when the pain comes to an end, your sense of reality will be shattered into little fragmented pieces. It will happen people, and once it does there is no return to your earlier state of mind. (Killer Dude returns his attention to the camera) Killer Dude: As for me being the one that has everything to lose, I don't know where you all get that idea from. Don't get me wrong, I am the North American Champion. I have no doubts in my ability to end you all. So, let's just go over what happens if I lose. What exactly do I have to lose? Tell me people, what do I lose? Am I gonna be disgraced if I lose to Digital Mortality, one of the greatest world champions our sport has ever known? Is it gonna look bad if I get taken out by a legend in the wrestling business, Kenny Mercury. Answer my questions people, as you all seem to know the answers. What do I have to lose? I'll tell you exactly what I have to lose. Nothing! If anything, it will give me a much needed release. A release from all of you and your psycho babble bullshit that is the only thing that you seem to be able to spew out of your pathetic mouths. So if anything, I stand to gain from a loss don't I? There is nothing that you people can do to me. There are only things that I can do to you. Things that I will take immense joy in doing. Things that you will never forget. Things that you will never recover from. In closing, I am about to show you all new realms of pain. Pain that no one has ever witnessed before in their lives. I will show you all what true hatred really feels like. I will send your minds into a blur as you try to understand what level of hatred allows a man to be so sadistic and so cruel as I will be to all of you. I will leave you questioning why you made the decisions that you have made. SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH! (Killer Dude sits back and sighs, and the screen fades out to his logo. As the logo fades, the screen fades to black)
