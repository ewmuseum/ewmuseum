UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #580

What Was Will Be Again

27 Jul 2004

Mr. Meaner — Somewhere gloomy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The “man in black” is standing there, staring directly at the camera. It would be nice to know what he is thinking, or be even able to look into his eyes, but this is Mr. Meaner and you ain’t getting a look at him easily. He is sitting on a chair, and the room is very stale. It’s brick, a gray brick. Quite boring.) Mr. Meaner; Well, so my former enemy becomes my friend. Angel, who has been victimized by the Chaos Society turns to his former ally for help. How ironic. First he wants to turn me, then he comes to the meanest SOB for help. Whatever works, I guess. But the fact of the matter is that I am not really worried about Ayami, or Angel, or anyone for that matter. I’m just going to go out there and win, because that is what I do best. Kick ass and win…… The team we face is quite interesting however. I see that Adien Pryde is there. Without his former tag team partner in Reo Dai. How sad. All alone. Anarchy is half off, so to speak, so that must mean that there is some control there. And what an interesting name for a stupid POS. PRYDE. Oh look at me, I’m Pryde. I’m so full of myself that it doesn’t even matter. Well you can take your freaking pryde and shove it straight up your ass. Cause if you don’t, then I will….. And then you have your tag team partner in Raid. Yeah, Raid, I remember you you jack ass. You always were nothing, and never will be anything. Yeah, so you held the IC Championship. Big deal. I could’ve beaten that wuss Kazee. That really isn’t a big accomplishment. You’ve become absolutely nothing without me around. Is that what this is all about. Letting yourself stagnate for the world to see, because your doing a hell of a good job doing it…….. Let’s be honest here. For being called Chaos Society, you really have no idea what Chaos is, do you. You really don’t know what it means to be chaotic. You really don’t know how to cause chaos or make waves so that you will be noticed. I mean when Anarchy was together, they never got a title shot. Raid’s only IC Title shot was given to him by a fool, and the THC title was the same way. So why don’t y’all just quit calling yourself Chaos Society now. Why don’t you go and call yourself the Order Society. Or the Pansy society. Because you don’t know what Chaos is…… But don’t’ worry. I’ll help you. I’ll show you what it means to have Chaos. I’ll show you what it means to take a situation and get people to notice you. You go ahead and slap those women around. You go ahead and slap the little girls around and take your orders from a leather clad bimbo. Wednesday night, you’ll be facing something you can’t handle….. You’ll be facing a reuniting of Angel and myself. You’ll be facing a reuniting of the New Era. You’ll be facing the past and the future of the UWF…. And in the end, the Chaos Society shall be just that, in Chaos. (Scene fades to black)
