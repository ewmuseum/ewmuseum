Ain't Ready Fo Tha Stuntin!

15 Dec 2004

Hot Mask — Out and About in Gary, IN

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC Note: In order to garnish the full effect of this promo, you may want to check out http://stuntaz.cjb.net before reading. Trust me. When we last left our hero, the effervescent and impeccable giant-cocked wonderboy known as Hot Mask, a mid-day cyber romp through the stalking paradise known as AOL Instant Messenger was halted by a familiar voice over the phone. Immediately dropping his present activity, Mask quickly darted for a shower and prepared himself for a few long-time friends who were unexpectedly progressing toward the area and planned on paying him a much-needed visit. Seated on the couch in anticipation for their arrival, we take a gander at the Self-Fellating Fandango looking so fresh and so clean clean geared out in a pair of baggy Sean John jeans, classic white Reeboks, and a large red New Jersey Devils hockey jersey. A sparkling gold chain dangles from around his neck, fastening together from the back and adorning a platinum circle-and-pointed arrow 'male' symbol towards the bottom loop. Twiddling his thumbs with excitement, he is not left waiting much longer, as the sound of hellaciously loud and booming speakers can be heard pulling into the driveway of the Casa de Cumshoot. Mask giggles with glee as he leaps off of his couch, almost knocking over a green lava lamp sitting innocently on the table stand. 'Asshole,' the lava lamp whispers underneath its breath, making sure Mask doesn't hear him. No more repeats of what happened to that piece of table. Mask darts to his front door and flings it open, with a rush of cold air causing him to shiver. However, a warming glow pulsates through Hot Mask's skin as he peers out toward the blue Chevrolet Tahoe parked in the driveway. 'WHAAAAASUP MASSSSSSK?' a voice shouts, climbing out of the front passenger side of the automobile. The call is matched together with a young man, appearing to be about Hot Mask's age. His perfectly-gelled blonde hair is partially hidden behind a red Nike visor tilted to the side, and out steps the individual also sporting a pair of red windbreaker pants and a white wife beater. 'Da FLAME in da HOUSSSSE!' Mask responds, rushing over to greet the individual. The man, apparently called 'Da Flame,' forms a fake gun with his finger and points it at Hot Mask in a show of thug love. 'What it be, H.M.? We up in dis BITCH!' Da Flame exclaims, offering his hand out to Hot Mask. Mask and Da Flame proceed to do one of those 'cool' hand-slap handshakes, and the two hug each other in a completely hetero and cool way. 'It's so good to see you, man!' Hot Mask yelps. He points to Da Flame's forearm, admonishing the handiwork. 'I see you finally got your tattoo!' 'Yeah dawg,' Flame explains, 'da moms and pops finally caved in and signed da release form, yo! I be straight stackin da ladies wid dis pimp shit glazed on my fo'arm! Hey,' he notices, 'I'm feelin dat bling, dawg!' Mask holds up his necklace to be examined closer by Da Flame. The two mumble back and forth, not noticing another individual casually sneaking up behind him. Decked out in a light blue snow jacket, light blue Adidas visor facing forward, and a pair of black parachute pants, he creeps behind Da Flame and Hot Mask. 'BOOOO, HOMEY!' Mask turns around, startled at first, but a smile crosses his face as he gazes into the familiar complexion of the man who puts the 'cold' in Icy Hot Stuntaz. 'Freeze! How the fuck are ya, man? You look...' 'Older?' Freeze finishes, scratching his peach fuzz with a smile. 'Well, I wasn't gonna say older...but...' 'Yea yea YEAH, homey G! I be lookin straight up like a mothafuckin P-I-M-P fo shee, in tha two-g! Straight growin a phiggity-phiggity-PHAT biggity-biggity-BEARD!' Freeze continues his smile, pointing out the stubble that just barely grazes his parallel jawline. 'Oh yeah, I do see it!' Mask lies, giving reassurance to the rather preteen-looking Freeze. One thing does puzzle Hot Mask at the present, however. 'Where's Blade?' 'Open tha dough, dawg.' Da Flame points to the back passenger door. Mask opens it accordingly only to find a rather pathetic image of the acne-covered face of the Stunta known as Blade, cuddled up with a teddy bear and sucking his thumb with content as he breathes and makes the soft sounds of a deep sleep. 'Been dozin-dozin since we left Philly, what what?' 'Hey, wake up, man!' Mask coaxes, tapping Blade on the shoulder. He murmurs, and slowly begins to come to, giving a smile to our wrestling friend. 'M to tha A-S-K! What it is?' Blade mumbles, slowly lifting himself up. 'Have a good nap?' HM inquires with a sense of playfulness. 'Tru,' is his only response, wiping his eyes sleepily. 'Yo, I hates ta be rushin, but we gotsta go STRAIGHT BALLIN on some fly honeys fo our show tonight, dawgs. Maskizzle, get that ass in there, 'cause we 'bout to ROLLLLLLL OUT!' Da Flame commands, opening up the passenger-side door and clamoring inside. Hot Mask nods and obliges, allowing Blade a moment to scoot over as he follows suit and climbs in. 'Bitches ain't ready fo tha STUNTINNNNNNN!' Freeze yells, cranking the SUV and switching gears to reverse. 'ICY HOT STUNTINNNNNNNNN!' the trio calls out in unison, as they pull away into the road. * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * Clover Heights Middle School. Home of the Roaring Clovers. The original suggestor of the team mascot, we assume, was probably picked on a good bit when he himself was in junior high. The SUV is parked at an empty space in the lot just outside of the school. With the ringing of the exit bell just five minutes prior to the crew's arrival, the members of the Stuntaz are scattered in different positions, promoting their upcoming gig later that night at the local YMCA. Attention first to Blade, who after a rigorous burst of energy from a 20 ounce bottle of Jolt Cola, is hyping the Great White Rap Game to a group of girls dressed in green-and-white marching band uniforms. 'Ya'll ladies gonna show up, tho?' he asks, with a gleam in his eye and a bling in his chain. 'Um...tee hee,' a rather homely looking black-haired girl speaks for the group, 'we've got practice until 6 and I'd have to ask my mom first...' Meanwhile, Freeze is once again putting pleasure before business, spotting a blonde wearing green warm-up pants underneath a white cheerleading skirt. She appears quite smitten with him, as he explains to her one of the most difficult challenges that he has ever had to face. 'So grandpops was all like, 'you GOTSTA get a job!' and I was all like, 'yo, dawg, BITCHES dig DITCHES while da FREEZE make his RICHES!' and he was all up in my face sayin shit like 'you gonna be grounded if you say that dirty word again', and I was all like, 'yo I don't gotta do what you say because you ain't no boss of me, foo,' and he was all like, 'get out of my house!' so I put my fly gear in my duffel bag and busted straight rubber out his driveway in my icy blue Ford Ranger!' But, the reservations of Da Flame and Hot Mask, long-time childhood comrades, are noted in quiet conversation next to the SUV. 'So what been dippin and trippin, dawg?' Da Flame quips, straining his faux hip-hop accent. 'How's tha wrestling biz treatin ya?' 'Meh,' Mask responds, a bit reserved, 'it's alright. Company pops huge boner for a 185 pound kid who runs the table, mediocre paycheck results. I've got a match Wednesday against a Scot who I will more than likely destroy...and then, I'll allow some lucky young trifling hobag nibble the Golf Ball Gonads as a reward.' 'Tiiiiiiiiight,' Da Flame acknowledges, nodding his head with understanding. 'But what happened to that one lil' nug you were chiefin with...Skankalie or what not?' Mask shakes his head and sighs. 'Placed boot to ass for running around with another woman.' Da Flame perks up. 'A woman? Damn! Why aren't you throwin the game on a menage a trois fo dat shit?' 'Believe me, I've tried,' Mask smiles. 'But the chickenhead she runs with must have brainwashed her by hypnotizing her brain with the wave of his smaller-than-life unit.' He then gazes out into the sky, in a movie-like pose, offering this often-expressed anecdote. 'Broads are fucking crazy, man.' 'Preachin to tha converted, yo. You gonna try and get the goose back to tha golden egg or what?' 'It'd be nice, bro,' Mask quotes. 'I sure miss having someone around the house to beat when I get angry.' Da Flame shakes his head in disagreement. 'Dat's where you goin wrong, kid. Bitches be wisin' up to tha game, playa. You can't play tha role of the pimp so long into thangs. Sometimes ya just gotta lay it all out dere on da table, puttin tha suki suki and that sweet poetry on her.' 'That shit's gay,' Mask responds with intellect. 'Yeaaaaaaa, I know the sap can lick my wet nap, but dawg...I ain't sayin you can't bend the broad over every once in a while to have her smack tha bass...but follow that shit up by tellin' her how beautiful she is or somethin!' Da Flame, always recognized as a brilliant philosopher, places his hand on Mask's shoulder as the Hot One gives his words some thought. 'I always thought the best way to say 'I love you' was to offer her your underwear to wipe her eye off after shooting a load in it. I guess it's gonna take more than that, huh?' 'Now you gettin it!' Da Flame smiles, exclaiming with wonder. 'Maybe I'll get her back,' Mask bodes with confidence. 'Anyway, enough of my goddamn problems...how've you been?' Da Flame gives a sigh of discomfort at this quesiton. 'I 'unno, man...shit's alright, I guess. Still SERVIN FOOLS on da tennis courts in college and it pays tha bills, but man...I don't wanna go to no school to work in some office 'n shit! I got blessed with tha rap game, but man...dis shit...' he motions, pointing towards Blade and Freeze, '...just ain't takin off like I expected it to. Thinkin about bustin solo, ya know? Lettin me be me and all dat dere.' 'I dunno if I agree with that,' Mask says. 'The Icy Hot Stuntaz are the BOMB! You're like my favorite rap group ever...even better than Ill Mitch! But hey, I'm no record producer, so don't take my word for it.' 'Dat's tha thing, I think we bring tha ICY ICY HEAT too, but tha record companies obviously don't think so. I dropped that demo EVERYWHERE, dawg...ain't gettin no phone calls on the celly celly cell, ain't gettin no letters in the mailly mailly mail...nothin! I shoulda just become a wrestler like you, dawg. You gotsa nice house...ain't as big as my folks'...but it's nice. I'm sure you be livin dat shit up, fo sho!' 'Uh...I guess,' Mask lies, trying to keep the same facial expression in tact. 'But fo real, I just gotta keep doin what I do. The Lord gon' provide, straight up!' Da Flame hopes, shooting for his dreams. The presence of both Blade and Freeze halt the conversation, as they excitedly tell their experiences on the promotion of the show. 'Got straight FLY bitches ridin' tha piece tonight, dawgs. And one of 'em's fourteen!' Blade beams, ego rising with each breath. 'Yeah, dawg...and check dis shit out,' Freeze jumps, sticking his hand out for Flame and Hot Mask to see. A telephone number, written in bright pink pen adorns his palm as he giggles excitedly. 'She comin to the show tonight?' Flame asks. 'Oh...oh...fuck man...I forgot about dat.' 'That's straight, dawg...you can just ring dat bitch up!' Flame reassures, slapping Freeze on the shoulder. 'Man...call her? Shoo, I dunno man...' The adolescence of Freeze begins to break through as he suddenly grows nervous. 'Whateva dawg,' Blade remarks. 'Let's trail out of dis bitch!' A smile from Hot Mask spreads across his face as the three Icy Hot Stuntaz join one another in unison just before boarding the vehicle and hitting the road... 'YO YO YO! HERE WE GO! KICKIN DIS FLOW! LIKE PISSIN IN DA SNOW!'
