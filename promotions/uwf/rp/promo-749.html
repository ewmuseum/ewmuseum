The Eternal Cycle

20 Oct 2004

Clyde

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time marches onward, onward, and things never seem to change. In spite of variations in circumstances, the general pattern always remains the same. Here in a rather dingy hotel, an air of mustiness and decay greets us as we look upon a bored looking receptionist, his face resting upon his hand. Receptionist: (Glancing up) Keep in down, will ya!? With a tired sigh, the employee of this dismal abode fetches a broom from the floor next to him. Poking the end of it against the low ceiling above his head, white flakes of debris rain downward upon him, as we move on. Up a flight of stairs and around, to the closed door of room 205. A thud rings forth, lightly at first, then more forceful from the inside of the room. The door shakes, shuddering beneath the force of the blows, again and again. And then, just as quickly as it began, the assault stops. From the small space beneath the door, the head of a rat appears, struggling to pull its tiny body through. After a mighty struggle it does so, darting forth and glancing all around in terror. The door bursts open behind the rodent, smacking violently against the grimy hotel wall. The poor creature leaps, and after a glance of pure mousey horror at the opened portal, runs for its life down the hallway as a figure emerges from the room. Scourge: What's wrong? I thought you wanted a real man! Catching a glimpse of the fleeing animal, the bestiality loving freak quickly gives chase, struggling to hold his pants up with one hand. He passes by another figure in the hallway, who shrugs. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Turning his attention, the Violator focuses upon a vending machine before him. His eyes focused with determination, he raises a dollar bill and inserts it into the slot. The machine buzzes, devouring his money. And, just as quickly, the machine spits it right back out again. Violator: ......I am the Violator! Grunting, Violator rips the dollar free, smoothing it out against the front of the vending machine. Again he presses it into the slot....and again, his money is rejected. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Yet again the Violator pushes his money forth. Again the bill is spit out. Then again. Then again. Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!!!! Furiously, our mentally challenged avenger shakes the machine, various pieces of candy goodness rattling from just beyond his reach. As his fists pound futilely against solid plastic, we move onward to the room behind him. Through the door, and to a sight we so often have seen before in our travels. A little girl, shrieking incoherently with a wickedly large knife in her hand. And a beleaguered demon, repeatedly slamming his head against a window. Clyde: (Slamming) Why.....me? Why....me!? Bethany: Oh will you stop your whining!? Things aren't that bad! The eternal demon known only as Clyde thrusts his face against the glass once more, his features smooshing into it. Clyde: Not that bad. You've condemned me to hell! Bethany: You say that every time I ruin your life. Get some new material! Nonchalantly, Bethany raises her blade and bonks Clyde upon the head with the handle, a meaty 'thunk' ringing forth. Not surprisingly, he pays little notice. Clyde: This is different. You dragged me away from both Heaven and Maui.... Bethany: (Sighing) Here we go again. Clyde: Heaven and Maui. From a paradise of fluffy white clouds and a somehow superior paradise of sun, sand, alcohol and beautiful women massaging my buttocks..... Bethany: What!? You never mentioned that part! Clyde: (Ignoring her) Back to this silly wrestling business! What the hell is it with you and wrestling, anyway? We're immortal demons, and the best thing you can find to do with our time is have me WRESTLE? Bethany: It's the only place you'd be mildly socially acceptable. Clyde: Bah! And teamed with Seymour Almasy? Really now, kiddo, was that the BEST you could do? Bethany: Sadly, yes. Clyde: Bah, bah, BAH! Each consecutive 'bah' from the burned man is punctuated by another slam of his face into the window. Bethany: Will you stop that!? You're getting loser marks all over the glass! The hellspawned eight year old once again clobbers her immortal lover over the head with the butt of her knife, causing him to moan and clutch his skull. Bethany: (Filing her nails) Just keep yourself busy and collect paychecks. With any luck, the fed'll die just like every fed I sucker you into, then I can blow all of your money on shoes and cutlery. Then we can start all over again! Clyde turns, snatching the knife away from Bethany. He begins ramming the blade into his head and neck, the wounds closing almost as quickly as he can inflict them. Bethany, for her part, merely shrugs and reaches into her dress, producing another knife from her seemingly never ending supply of them. Bethany: (Resuming her filing) Crybaby! And as an eternally damned monster continues to rend and tear his own flesh, we mercifully fade to black.....
