UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #627

And Now a Message from Our Sponsers

14 Aug 2004

The Communist — Transmitted Directly to your TV

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The National Anthem of the USSR plays in the background, The Communist standing proudly before the boldly waving Flag of the USSR, THC title over his shoulder: Announcer: And now a message from your THC Champion, The Communist. :The screen shifts to The Communist sitting at a desk, the THC title spread out before him and a slide projector at his side. Behind him, the screen is pulled down: Commie: Good Morning, Comrads. Today I sit before you to reply to the accusations made against me. Yes, I beat the bourguasis out of some old Golfers. :The Communist presses the button and the first slide comes up, a picture of the broken body of the old fart he sledgehammered through a window: Commie: Yes. I beat the racism out of some idiot skinheads. :He presses the button and the second slide shows the Communist performing the Communist Slam on one of the skinheads at the temple: Commie: And yes, I put some terror in some terrorists. :The next slide is of him beating on the hijackers on the plane.: Commie: I'm rather proud of that last one. You see, Mr Meaner, I have the balls to be the Hardcore Champion. Thats why I beat Nick Persia, thats why I beat on the old Golfers, thats why I'm going to beat on you. You can't beat me because you don't have the balls. You don't have the fierce fighting spirit of a champion, and a THC champion must have more fighting spirit than anyone, even the Heavyweight Champion. Why, you may ask, why do I say that the THC requires more spirit? Well, how many Heavyweight Chapionship matches end with a Communist Slam onto a mat covered in broken glass and thumb tacks? How many Intercontenental Championship matches end in leg drop off a ladder through a table? How many TV title matches end in a chair shot delivered to a face made bloody by repeated bludgonings against a ring post? Not many, I assure you. And yet, on any given match, I will be facing all of these things. From glass tables, to steel chairs. From hundred foot drops to rope assisted submission moves. And not only do I have to deal it out, I have to take it. :The Communist stands to his full, intimidating, 7', 380 lbs.: Commie: I have become a tank. A T-72 Main Battle Tank. My ability to deal and take damage is unmatched in this federation. I am the ultimate weapon of Hardcore, and I will show you in Tokyo. Bring your friends, Bring your weapons. And find your balls, because you've been missing them for a while. Because an army of policemen can't save you. And all the medical miracles in the world won't put your face back together after I've mangled it. And all the shit you talk, you had better be able to back up. Because The Red Army's Perfect Weapon is here. It's Commie Time Meaner. :The Communist takes a moment to compose himself: Commie: And to the rest of you, remember, Communism is your friend. Thank you, and have a great day. :The Scythe and Hammer pop up on the screen: This message paid for by the Central Committee, Communist Party.
