UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #661

Look, Ma... I'm on TV!

9/1 (year unrecorded)

Pájaro de Presa — Los Banos, CA

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Los Banos, California. A town not well-know for anything other than a really ufortunate name, But it does have one claim to fame. Because in this town is a Jack-in-the-Box. And this Jack-in-the-Box has a dumpster. And this dumpster has a visitor. A man in a white mask. He knocks on the side of the dumpster. The lid flies open and out leaps UWF's Rudo- with Attitude-o, Pajaro de Presa. Pajaro: Albert Einstein! My hero! White Tiger: Pajaro, it's me. White Tiger. Pajaro: Oh yeah.. what's shaking, amigo? Want some stuffed jalapenos? They were just thrown out today! White Tiger turns a little green. White Tiger: Uh, no thanks. Listen I got something important to talk to you about. You have a match Sunday. You can become TV champion. The Bird of Prey looks a little confused. Pajaro: TV champ? You mean drag queens? I gotta wear a dress in this match. Ok, I'll do it! I got one here someplace! Leaping back into the dumpster, Pajaro de Presa starts rumaging for a dress. Pajaro: Hmmm... where did it go? Here it is! It's a blue one! Hey! What's this stain? Huh. Tastes salty. White Tiger bangs on the dumpster again. White Tiger: Pajaro, you don't get it. It's a match for the Television championship. You have a chance to bring gold to the Masked Monkeys! Pajaro de Presa pokes his head out of the dumpster. Pajaro: Televison champion? But I ain't got a television. The manager won't let me run a cord into the resteraunt. White Tiger is looking frustrated. White Tiger: Listen, you don't have to own a televison. You just have to beat the champ and win the titl. Pajaro: I don't need a televison to be Televison champion? White Tiger: Right. Pajaro: And I don't have to wear a dress? White Tiger: Right. Pajaro: But I can still wear one if I want? White Tiger: Sure. Whatever. Pajaro: Sweet! See ya Sunday! Pajaro de Presa closes the lid to his dumpster. White Tiger realizes this has gone on way too long for a Masked Monkey promo and goes home.
