UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #280

House Encounter.

March 11 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Malcolms House

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Another SAS, another win for Malcolm Kennedy and The Prime Alliance. Apparently he keeps his word. So after an extremely weird match, a long night of partying, and a full day of wrest, where is Malcolm Kennedy? After 4 hours in the gym this morning, his day is open for wrest and relaxation. Or so he thought. Sounds like its time for another UWF camera crew to do their job, and ruin someone’s day.*** <<<The scene opens up in a white driveway that leads up to very big house. Kids can be heard laughing and screaming in the background of the small rich development that Malcolm Kennedy lives in. A very loud roar can be heard as the camera man spins around. The camera stops to see a silver streak come flying up the road only to power slide into the white driveway in which the camera stands. It screeches to a halt inches from the camera man. The loud engine revs a couple times as the straight headers roar from the 898 horsepower 350 cubic inch Twin Turbo V-8 engine. Finally the roar comes to a stop as the car is cut off. The driver door swings open and a man steps out. He wears a red t-shirt and baggy blue jeans. On the back of the shirt reads the words “Step to the One Man Army”. The man turns and the front of the shirt can be seen. The words “The Prime Alliance” in white run across the chest. Its Malcolm Kennedy. His black hair is gelled neatly and a pair of black Oakleys cover his eyes. He pushes the sunglasses up onto his forehead as his eyes squint as a man walks past the camera with a mic. He stops maybe a half inch from Malcolm side, almost hugging him.
