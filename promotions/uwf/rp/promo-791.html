UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #791

Destiny Unfolds!

27 Nov 2004

Killer Dude

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens on a shot of Killer Dude standing in front of an archive of tapes and videos as he thumbs through each one slowly. He looks around his surroundings, and then begins to look at the various posters of his acheivements. Quietly, he opens a book and reads through it. The camera zooms in, and you can see that it is a history of everything he has done in the UWF. His eyes then begin to wander from place to place on the shelf, seemingly looking at what has happened in his life. He then turns and walks away from the book shelves. He stops at another shelf, which is barely filled with various tapes of his opponents. He taps a tape called 'Kenny Mercury. He taps the tape, turns and looks at another tape, which reads 'Miniature Golf Match, 2003'. Killer Dude shakes his head as he remembers that upsetting loss, turns to the camera, walks to a small desk nearby and places three tapes on it, with names 'Nick Persia', 'Clyde', and 'Seymour Almasy' on them. Killer Dude spreads the tape across the desk and raises his gaze to the camera, ready to speak) Killer Dude: Seymour Almasy, it seems that our paths have crossed once again. This time it is not for the North American Title. This time, it is a tournament of Champions, a test of pride, and what could possibly be the beginning of a new era in the UWF. Seymour Almasy, once again it seems that you are underestimating my ability in the ring. You still seem to think that I am an easy defeat. Well, guess what, junior. You're dead wrong. Unfortunately for you, I have dealt with you, and those like you before. You can ignore my words, you can do what you will. I just hope you will remember that I will do whatever I have to do to handle you, and your supposed 'vengeance'. You know what Seymour, I've been in hell for most of my life. Let's see if I can elevate your guilt from your own personal hell. Your personal hell, as you say it, would be to lose to me twice. Well, guess what Seymour? The fire still burns within, and that spells your doom. I will bring with me everything I've got, and in the end, you will be buried under the overwhelming aspects of your personal hell. Hehehehehe! (Killer Dude grabs a tape named 'Gurgles' and holds it in his hand, looking at it) Killer Dude: The most violent confrontation I've ever had, was against this man, this clown, this abomination named Gurgles. This man was violence incarnate. He and I went through hell and back, just torturing the hell out of each other, beating each other to within an inch of our lives. We pushed the limits of the GIWF. The limits that any normal man wouldn't have been able to exceed. Seymour, do you honestly think that you can take me to the limits, like Gurgles did before. No man has ever taken me to these limits since him. I've severely punished my opponents, and every time, I've been able to get up and walk away. Let's see if you can get up and walk away tomorrow, or will you have to be carried on to a stretcher when I'm done with you. (Killer Dude pushes the 'Gurgles' tape to the side and picks up the tape named 'Kenny Mercury') Killer Dude: Kenny Mercury thought that I was an easy mark. Made the mistake of attacking my heavily damaged knee, time and time again. In the end, it was I who came out victorious, and took his THC Title in the process. This in effect, was the beginning of the downward spiral of Kenny Mercury. He was the never the same after this happened. Where is he now? He is out of wrestling because of psychotic individuals such as myself. Let that be a thought to you Seymour? Just how far do you think that I'm willing to go to beat you? (Killer Dude slides that tape to the side, and picks up yet another tape. The camera zooms in on the name, and it read 'Nick Persia') Killer Dude: I have defeated this man twice since I came back to the UWF. This is the one man in this fed right now, who had the ability to stand up to me, and bring the same extreme level to each one of our encounters. He and I have established the upper echelon of what it means to be extreme in the UWF. Killer Dude: What will happen, Seymour, if, no, WHEN I beat you for the second time in as many meetings. What will you do? Will you leave this glorious federation filled with shame, because you could not defeat the lowly Killer Dude? I do not see this happening. I see it as a new beginning for you. Seymour, by the end of Supremacy, you will know what it is like to humiliated to the fullest extent. Can you handle what I'm going to do to you? Can you even possibly imagine the hell that I'm gonna unleash upon your pathetic body? Killer Dude: The game is getting ready to begin Seymour. The only question that remains is are you ready to play? Are you prepared for a changing of the guard? It's time to get ready, because I am coming Seymour. I am coming with one thing on my mind. I will hurt you Seymour. You better hope that I am in a good mood tomorrow. There is only one thing that you need worry about Almasy. What will I do this time? Killer Dude: The future has come sooner than you expected, Seymour Almasy. It comes in the form of the Hardcore Legend. It comes in the form of Killer Dude. And with that being said, there's only one thing left to say. SEE YOU IN HELL BITCH!!!!!!!!! (Killer Dude turns away from the camera, walks over to a light switch, and flips it off. The camera fades to static)
