CoC Round 1

- (year unrecorded)

Nick Persia — UWF Headquarters

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick Persia made the score even. Nick Persia did what many held for impossible: he brought down the Savior in Wes Diable. He pushed him back into his place. He showed him that he was nothing more than a rookie that got lucky. But now, after he had put things back in place, he has the opportunity of getting to the second round in Contest of Champions. Normally anyone would have been out of the tournament, but Nick Persia got a very exceptional chance. A rematch to qualify himself to the second round of Contest of Champions. This time, he has to face Joshua Justice. The new rookie in UWF with loads of talent. The question is if his talent could outclass Nick’s experience and power. The scene fades into Nick Persia talking to Thommy again. Thommy’s t-shirt looks really great because it says ‘The Grand Poobah of red Hot Monkey Love’. Behond the next we see a picture of a … monkey. What else did you expect? Freak. Thommy: Did you know our interview from last week was being watched? I received fanmail, so I was hoping to do yet another interview with you. Nick Persia: Is that true? Thommy: One hundred percent. Nick Persia: I didn’t expect anything else. Because I always guarantee to be shocking. Thommy: You mean you let the Nazi’s invade the UWF? Nick Persia: I never let anyone invade the UWF, but myself. Let me clear somethings up. I was never the one who ordered these Nazi’s to come to the VHR~! No, that wasn’t me. Perhaps it was Eric Cameron or his partner, but certainly not me. Thommy: That’s strange, because they were greeting you. Nick Persia: No….they were greeting Eric Cameron! Thommy: LIES! I’ve got a tape of VHR, so I can... Nick Persia: SHUT UP! You can’t have a tape of VHR yet, because it hasn’t been released yet. Or are you telling me you have an illegal copy of UWF property in your house…. Thommy: Of course not! Erm, I wanted to show that tape to prove you are right of course. Nick Persia: Of course. Thommy: But now another challenge awaits you at this week’s UWF show. You are facing Joshua Justice in your qualifing match for CoC. Nick Persia: Yeah, and I’m looking forward to meet him. This is like David versus Goliath. Holland versus The United States. Let’s see if I have what it takes to reach the second round. Suddenly, Jamie Fletch interrupts Nick Persia, who was just getting to his greatness. And he has greatness. Jamie Fletch: Sir, can I have your autograph? Thommy: Whose? Mine or his? Jamie Fletch: You’re so funny. Nick Perisa’s of course. I’m your biggest fan EVER! Nick Persia: Whoa. You must have balls to say that. Jamie Fletch: One ball actually. Thommy: Whuahahaha….you’re the infamous Jamie ‘One Ball’ Fletch, aren’t you…. Jamie Fletch: Yes, I am. Thommy: Mwuhahaha, you’re like the stupidest fan we have ever seen in the UWF. At the last fan meeting you knew fewer questions than Pete the Chimp. Moron…… Jamie Fletch looked really sad, because it was the words were all true. Nick Persia: Don’t worry, there are famous wrestlers who have one ball too. Jamie Fletch: Really? Nick Persia: Really! I’ve seen it you know. I’m not only talking about Jamie Shock you know. You won’t get an autograph, because I have to train before I defeat JJ. No kidding. With that, Nick Persia walks away (Ran out of time, sorry)
