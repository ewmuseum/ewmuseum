UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #365

Fluke? Ha!!

24 Apr 2004

The Plague — Park

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens up in park, it is a beautiful Saturday afternoon, you can see a group of children playing on some swings, and some more going down slides and playing around on monkeybars. The camera pans around to show The Plague sitting on a bench watching the kids enjoy the day. The Plague is wearing a black tanktop and shorts and a pair of black shoes. He is wearing a black bandana on his head and a pair of sunglasses covering his eyes. He cracks a smile and begins to talk in a low monotone voice.] Plague: 'Fluke? Iron Lung, you really don't know who you are dealing with do you? I mean you saying how me defeating you for The UWF Hardcore Title was a fluke. I can assure you that it was no fluke Iron Lung.' [A frisbee is seen flying across The Plague's face. Plague gets up and picks up the frisbee. He than flings it to a group of four people two guys and two girls. One of the guys catches the frisbee.] Guy: 'Thanks man.' [Plague nods and waves.] Plague: 'No problem.' [Plague shakes his head and smiles.] Plague: 'There, my good deed of the day.' [Plague cracks his knuckles and flips his glasses up so now you can see his eyes. He starts to talk again now with a bit more aggression in his voice.] Plague: 'Iron Lung, tomorrow there will be no good deeds done, well not by me that's for sure. You want to run your mouth about cursing me. You had absolutely, hold on, let me repeat that for you, you had absolutely nothing to so with me losing the THC Title to Nick Persia. I give the man credit, he did what he had to do in order to secure victory, and that is what it takes to be Hardcore Champion.' [Plague gets up.] Plague: 'Walk with me mr. cameraman, lets explore some of natures works of art.' [Plague starts walking away from the bench, away from the playground, away from the kids. The camera follows him into the woods, The Plague stops and turns around leaning up against a giant tree.] Plague: 'This is where the interview will continue, and most likely end, I just didn't want to scar any innocent ears with what I about to say. And it all pertains to Iron Lung. You see bitch, you are all hellbent onregaining The Hardcore Title, you see that's all fine with me. But you see you are going to have to really impress the UWF booking committee, and beating me will really help you out. But if you think I'm gonna allow you to get back into the Title race that easily, you really don't know me very well.' [Plague laughs.] Plague: 'You see, I am really not to concerned with The Hardcore Title, I proved what I had to upon my return, I won it in my second match, and I did it in a three-way. So Iron Lung, you can go on with your hunt for THC, but you will have to go through me. I'm shooting for bigger and better things in The UWF now, I see a man by the name of Digital Mortality is back. Congrats I guess should be given to you. But a tag wrestler, come on. But I guess you did everything, but that still won't stop me from having a few more run-ins with you down the line. I still remember what you did to me.' [Plague shakes his head and refocuses on his match] Plague: 'Iron Lung, oh yeah, your just another worthless piece of shit I need to give a good long lesson in hardcore too. Nick Persia I underestimated, but hey maybe he can bring some honor back to THC, much like I have. Iron Lung see you in the ring tomorrow, you better bring your big guns cause you are gonna need them.' [Plague walks silently deeper into the woods as the scene fades out to black.]
