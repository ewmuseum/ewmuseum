UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #238

The Alliance is back!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins with Ruff Ryder sitting behind an oak desk with his feet kicked up wearing a red & black Prime Alliance t-shirt. Behind him on the wall is a Prime Alliance flag. Ryder has his usual cocky grin as he begins to speak. ) Ryder: Is it true? * Ryder grins * Ryder: Can it be? * He chuckles to himself * Ryder: Hell yes to both questions! We're back, and we're gonna be badder than ever! After a brief silence the Prime Alliance is back and we're here to stay. No punk ass fuckin losers like the Player's Club can keep us down. We suffered a minor setback to those assholes, but now we're ready to take back what is rightfully ours, and that is...THE WHOLE FUCKIN SHOW! We own the UWF and it's about time we start taking it back piece by piece until there isn't a single person left who even remembers the Player's Club or what they represent. No Jamie Shock? Who the hell cares! He can take his ball and go home if he wants, but that isn't gonna stop the Alliance from doing what it set out to do in the first damn place and that's ridding the UWF of scum like the Player's Club. I don't care what it takes, because we will be victorious. It all starts with the tag team match that myself and Malcom Kennedy have coming up. That's gonna be a fuckin cakewalk, and then we'll get into business full swing. Montgomery, you got a taste of it son, but that brick to the skull wasn't nothin compared to the beating you've got comin your way. * Ryder looks up as if he's remembering the brick to the head that he dropped Mike Montgomery with * Ryder: Fuck the IC title tournament, I got screwed out of that, but i'm not bitter, because I know that this sex maniac or whatever he likes to call himself will cross paths with me again, and when it happens he had better pray to God that I don't rip his damn throat out and feed it to my rottweiler. * Ryder gets a more serious look on his face * Ryder: Now as for my good friend Malcom Kennedy, I have yet to hear your response to my offer. I hope you're not thinking of turning me down. I mean, I know you weren't in the Alliance for very long, but trust me you were the future of the Prime Alliance and still are. Just like I had to grab the reins when that pussy Jamie Shock left, someday I won't be able to wrestle anymore and when that day comes you'll be the one to bring the Alliance forward. I hope that you will come home where you belong. I can't do it without you and I don't want to do it without you. * Ryder takes a drink of water before continuing * Ryder: Now as for the tag team opponents, what are they called again? Anarchy? Whatever the hell you wanna call yourselves..don't show up. It's that simple, stay at home on your sofa where you're nice and comfortable and that way you can save everyone the trouble of watching you stink up the arena and Malcom and me can get to more pressing business like who the next member of the Prime Alliance might be. That's right, we may just do a little recruiting on Supremacy. You two punks don't have to worry though, because you're not even bein considered. You don't have what it takes to be aligned with the greatest. We only take talented wrestlers and you are definately not qualified. You both haven't even shown me anything worth getting worked up about. I mean usually, if I think I need it i'll train hard for a match, but for you two bums i'm not even gonna lift a finger. You can't beat a team like mine and Malcom's so don't even step through the ropes or we'll kill you. That's all. * Ryder smiles big for the camera again * Ryder: Malcom, I hope you're with me dawg. I know you want the Player's Club just as bad as I do, so come on homie, let's do this. * The scene fades to black and comes to an end *
