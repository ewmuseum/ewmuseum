UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #662

My shot at the big time.

1 Oct 2004

Adien Pryde — Japan Airport

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to inside a private jet. Flight attendants and other flight crew can be seen hurrying around getting ready for take off. To one side the members of The Chaos Society, Adien Pryde, Reo Dai, Joanna, Raid and Mr. Meaner, are talking about what just happened this last weekend.] Pryde : Thank god we are finally getting out of this god forsaken country. I can’t get over how hard it is to get a damn T-bone steak. Dai : Tell me about it. I am not a fan of Japanese food and the only American food I could find was McDonalds and that is not good for training. Pryde : Soon we will be back in good old America where this Sunday I get my shot at the main event. A main event against Hall of Famer Tsunami Oni and legend in his own mind and I’m sure to be future hall of famer himself The Plague. Dai : Shiatsu, now there is someone we should have kidnapped. She is H.O.T. [Joanna jabs Dai in the ribs with her elbow.] Dai : Oww, well she is. Joanna : That is beside the point, Adien here has a real chance at making the UWF officials and that asshole Richard Hayes take notice. This could be a real boost for The Chaos Society. With Meaner holding the THC and Raid soon to be back after IC race once he takes care of some things when we get back to the states. Throw Anarchy back into the Tag Title hunt and when Pryde wins Sunday over these two legends, he may be looking at a singles title shot in the near future. To me, that makes The Chaos Society one of the top stable going in the UWF right now. [Pryde looks into the camera.} Pryde : Tsunami Oni, The Demon from the Deep Blue Sea, what ever he calls himself. The winner of the 2004 Regal Rumble and receiver of an ass kicking. Hope you will be on you’re ‘A’ game Sunday, I want you to have no excesses when I win the match. Plague, don’t think I am forgetting about you. You come and go as you please in this fed and expect to start where you left off. Well, I plan to make sure that it won’t happen again. You go off to Japan to wrestle while you are still under UWF contract only to jump back on ship thinking you are the man again. Joanna : He does know his way around in the ring. He is a multi time champ. Pryde : Was, was a multi time champ just like Oni and both are ready for retirement if you ask me. Plague hasn’t done shit since his return and all Tsunami Oni really is good for is bringing Shiatsu to the ring, and Dai, she is hot. Nothing against you Joanna, you still can kick her ass. [Joanna shrugs.] Dai : And a great ass it is. [Joanna back hands Dai.] Pryde : This Sunday is my day. My day to prove to the world that I can hang with the big boys of the UWF, and they don’t come any bigger than you Oni. I can’t wait to lock the Sibko Stretcher on you and when I hear you scream I will pick you up and drive your skull to the mat with the Falcon Driver. Joanna : Oh, Shiatsu, stick your nose where it don’t belong, and I will be sure to take it off. Now get out of here, we need to discuses some things in private. [As the camera back away it fades to black.]
