UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #152

Moraless — SAS vs Rollins

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sound of rain can be heard as it lashes against a window pain, smashing into it before seeping down the smooth surface, landing on the concrete below. As the rain continues to hammer down a flash of lightening illuminates the sky, it is only mid afternoon but the clouds have caused a temporary nighttime. A bird flies across the horizon before landing in a tree, burying itself deep within its nest and sheltering from the downpour underneath its wing. Below the tree and just outside of a typical suburban house John Edwards can be seen. He leans backwards and gazes towards the sky as the rain continues to pour down drenching him, the water seeping into his Jeans and t-shirt increasing the weight of them substantially. The truth was that John liked the rain; he liked the way that it cascaded down his messy brown hair, before it dripped onto his face. The effect was cleansing as though it allowed the thoughts in his mind to just flow away, it laid his soul to rest. Ultimately the rain let him know that he was alive. When the rain made contact with his skin a tingle a life shot through his very being, suddenly the air seemed cleaner, fresher almost and his heart began to beat with reason. The door to the house opens, a short and attractive brunette peers around the corner of the door, her eyes glancing over John, they are full of worry and concern. John had changed in the years that she had been away and some of his knew habits worried her. “John you’ll catch your death of cold honey, come in and I’ll make you a nice drink of hot chocolate.” Louise mentioned as she ducked back inside as the rain picked up, some of the larger beads making slight dents in the house’s front garden. “I will be in, in a bit honey, come out here for a second. I’ve just had a thought.” John spoke; his eyes remained transfixed on the clouds that loomed overhead, only blinking occasionally when a particularly large bead splashed his eye. Louise looked out at the downpour and begrudgingly stepped out, hardly in rain attire. He silk dressing gown clung to her body as the water began to drench her. As she neared John he put an arm round her and turned to kiss her lightly. “For so long I was angry at the world. The world that failed to recognise me Laura. For so long it felt that I would simply talk and nobody would listen to me. They always heard me, but never listened. Not really, not to what I had to say.” The words flowed freely, however the streams of water that thundered down around them were drowning the low tone of his voice out. Louise pulled herself tighter into John’s grasp, nuzzling her face into John’s chest. “My mum always used to tell me, don’t worry son, everyone has a special plan in life, a plan that is predetermined. I always believed her, I guess I had to. I had to have something to believe in. Then she left. Leaving myself and James to live in this harsh world alone. I embraced the darkness, thinking that the shadows offered me some sort of solace, somewhere where I wouldn’t be disturbed.” John pulled himself away from Louise and stepped further into the garden, extending his arms and tilting his head backwards, once more the rain cascading down his face as he smiled. “It got to the stage where I could walk down the street and not even be noticed. It was as though, the world was revolving around me, and no one even gave me a second glance. You can always hear their conversations but you never feel connected. I had given up on mankind, then you came back Louise.” John lowered his arms and extended them to Louise whose face was beaming as she slowly walked towards him and locked in a passionate embrace. “John come in now, lets get you dried off. You won’t be much use in the ring Sunday if you are sneezing all over the place” Louise almost purred the words to John as she rested her hand on John’s chest. John took Louise’s hand as she led him away from the storm that continued to assault the town. A few minutes later, looking decidedly drier and clutching a cup of tea, John is sat in the bedroom of his house, glancing down at a photograph, which is clutched in his other hand. The photo shows an image of the GCW locker room three years ago. GCW was one of those federations that always remained in the limelight, producing stars without even trying. Jason O’Neil, Steven Caldera, Moraless, Payne and Rich Rollins. It could possibly have been fate that bought Rollins to the UWF, to start a match that should have happened three years ago. The difference now was that Rollins was considered the rookie of the federation, in comparison to Moraless anyway. Rollins was correct, this match was three years in the making at the end of the day it was always fun to face someone that he knew, the added edge of being able to work out their next moves, of course after three years his memory was more than a little hazy. A pair of arms wrapped themselves around John from behind as Louise lightly kissed the back of his neck, resting her head on John’s shoulder. “Who are those guys?” Asked Louise indicating to the photograph. “Just some old friends, three years ago I worked for this company, I’m actually facing him Sunday.” John pointed at the image of Rich Rollins, who was smiling back at him. “Seems like an ok guy, think you can take him?” Louise questioned before lying down on the bed, drying her hair with a fluffy green towel. “I honestly don’t know, its been a while since I really spoke to him, plus he’s been making waves through the federation, either way it’ll be fun.” Spoke John putting down the photo on the bed and leaning across to cuddle Louise, who in turn returned the hug lovingly. “Thank you for making me feel again, thank you for saving me.”
