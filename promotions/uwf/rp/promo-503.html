UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #503

The TV title Again

5 Jul 2004

The Communist — The Peoples Training Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:The Communist lays under a weight bench, pressing weighs in excess of 700 lbs. Soviette walks over and sits on his stomach: Commie: What do you want Soviette?! I'm busy! Soviette: Change of plans. The UWF has seen fit to give you another chance at the TV title. Jay Viper is the Current holder. :she opens the file in her hands: US Military trained. Here on some special project or another. Not very much information. :huff: So much for soviet intellegence. Commie: He is not a problem. :continues lifting: I will take the title from him and leave him laying in his own bodily fluids. It is as simple as that. Soviette: No it isn't Communist. This is why you continue to lose. We need a plan. We need him to play by our rules. He needs to feel the bitter cold of the Russian winter. He needs to know the anger of our people, and that won't happen if he defeats you. Commie: Then what do you propose, Comrad? That I slink around like a snake, making friends with the Bourguasis Bastards that have corrupted this country? Never. I will defeat him by my own hands. That is the way I have always done it. I will not insult my heritage by whoring myself out to the Bourguasis and their politic games. Soviette: I said to do no such thing. We need no friends here. We have the Committee. You wanted to take the Hardcore Title, yes? Commie: Yes Soviette: So you need to get the attention of the UWF. Tell them that you can be hardcore. Show them Commie: What are you talking about? Soviette: A Hardcore TV Title match. Commie: hmmm... Soviette: Think about it. You can demolish this Viper person, Regain the TV title, and set yourself up to take the Hardcore Title in one fell swoop. A work of brilliance if I do say so myself. Commie: Excecllent. I like the way you think Comrad. I will regain the TV Title and the Confidence of the CCCP at the same time Soviette: Yes. Yes you will
