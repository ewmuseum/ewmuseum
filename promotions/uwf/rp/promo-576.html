UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #576

The start of my evil run.

25 Jul 2004

Adien Pryde — Unknown abandoned building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to Adien Pryde standing in a room of what appears to be an abandoned building. He is wearing the Chaos Society colors of a red t-shirt and black jeans and is standing in front of a banner with a black and white yin yang symbol on it. Only this symbol has a large red Anarchy mark in the middle. Pryde : So it has come to this. The Chaos has gotten so bad for the UWF that they are sending the most holy of good guys and the most evil of bad men after us. Hey Angel, we can see what your beef with us is, we got your woman, but you know what, it was your fault. You are the one calling me evil, and I guess it just went to my head. It got me to thinking as to what evil I had done to deserve the name calling. I wondered if it was the attacks from behind I have done to Kazee and Nick Persia. No, everyone seems to be doing ambushes now, so it couldn't be that. Then I thought that maybe it was taking out my former partner and stable mate Draco Paladine, but then thought no, he deserved it. I just couldn't figure out what it could be. [A slap and a muffled scream can be heard from behind the banner can be heard.] Pryde : Then it came to me. I wasn't Evil, yet. You saw something in me, something that I was capable of. That is when I decided to do something that you were oh so capable of doing in you past, a kidnapping. But not just take anyone, I would take the most important person in you life, the woman that you worked so hard for to make your comeback for, Ayami. [Pryde tears down the banner to reveal Ayami sitting bound and gagged sitting in a chair, blood trickling down her cheek with Joanna standing in front of her, blood glistening on the metal studs of her falconer glove.] Pryde : - Ayami is your heart and soul. Without her you are nothing, and on Wednesday, Raid and I will finish what was started in our last encounter, the end of you short lived comeback. Pryde : Mr. Meaner, Raid has told us a lot about you, and I have much respect for you. I have no arguments with you other you being the tag partner of my enemy. The front office decided to play a cruel joke on you by teaming you with your opponent from last week. When you and I meet in the ring, I will show you nothing but the respect you deserve. [Ayami begins to struggle again which Joanna brings to a stop with another backhand slap across the other side of her face causing blood to flow.] Pryde : Get a good look at her now Angel. Joanna likes her work and you never know when she will get carried away. [Both Pryde and Joanna begin to laugh as the screen fades to black.]
