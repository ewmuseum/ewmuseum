UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #462

Winners don't do drugs....intentionally.

date unknown

Moraless

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know how sometimes, things just seem like a good idea? For example to take part in something and then realise that, maybe, just maybe you shouldn’t have done it. The only problem is there is no-way for us to know what is ever going to happen. You could be walking down the road one day, and be run over by a bus. It’s an old cliché I know but its still true, it still holds merit. Oh well something’s were just meant to happen I suppose. 12 Hours Before The Match. A loud knocking echoed through John’s room, he tried to ignore it though, resting his head under the covers and covering it with a pillow. He didn’t want to get up, simple as that. He hadn’t slept too well the night before and had been kept awake by the noisy couple in the room next to his till the early hours. What he had to do tonight would be hard enough, without being deprived of sleep as well. The knocking occurred again causing John to let out a small mumble of discontent as he pulled the pillow tighter down trying to block out the noise once more. In twelve hours he would be facing Jesse Jamester and someone known as A-Poc in some sort of tournament. John didn’t exactly know what he had signed up for and had just been dragged along by his stable mate Digital Mortality. At the drawing of the brackets he had pulled Jesse Jamester and he knew that meant trouble. “Sir, I have to clean the room!” shouted a voice from behind the wooden door, which reverberated through the spacious room, drilling into the mind of John Edwards. John pulled the pillows off of his head and proceeded to poke his head out from under the covers. “Don’t you think it’s a bit rude to clean the room while the occupants still in it?” John shouted back, trying to remain civil but the frustration in his voice was clearly seeping through. There was a moment’s silence, as the words must have seeped through to the cleaner’s brain. “Ah yeah you’re right, sorry didn’t think about that. Enjoy your rest.” Shouted the cleaner as John could hear her walk down the corridor, continuing her rounds. John stared at the ceiling for a few moments and shook his head, bemused, before returning back under the covers. In twelve short hours he would be entering the ring with a man he knew everything about and a man, he knew nothing about. That was the general consensus going around those involved in the tournament. Vegas had given Jamester odds of 6/4 to win the bout with John close behind on 11/2 A-Poc though was considered a rank outsider though, with no-one seemingly willing to put any money on him. Who could blame them really? Jesse Jamester was a former global champion at Primetime Central, the governing body that maintained John’s and Jesse’s feds, whilst John himself was their extreme champion another highly converted belt within the company. John wasn’t about to let himself fall into the trap that just because he had not heard of A-Poc he would dismiss him. He had done that before and it had cost him. John rolled over and pulled up the covers once more, he would just do what he always did. His best, there was no point worrying about it, what was going to happen, was going to happen. There is no way to change destiny, besides it was twelve hours away, no point worrying just yet. 8 Hours Before The Match. The sound of a mobile phone ringing broke John out of his slumber, a small trail of drool followed him as he lifted his head off of his pillow. Groggily he stepped out of his bed wearing nothing but his boxer shorts and stumbled across the room, following the sound of the universally annoying Nokia ringtone. A warm breeze blew in from the window, soothing John somewhat as he fumbled around the small dresser in his room and clicked a button on the phone resulting in turning it to speakerphone as he walked towards the en-suite shower. “John, baby! You don’t know me but do I have a proposition for you” a high pitched male voice shouted down the phone, causing John to jump somewhat, not expecting a voice of that magnitude at the time. He didn’t reply and merely stepped into the shower, slipping out of his boxer shorts before turning it on. “John, the names Kyle Stone and I want to represent you through this tournament, you are one of those that I deem capable of making a shocking impact here in Ohio. I want to help you promote yourself, get into the public eye. Put John Edwards on the map.” Stone shouted down the mouthpiece, or at least it sounded like it. John simply continued to bathe, he was taking in the words, but in all fairness he had heard them all before. So many times agents came along offering the world on a plate, only to turn out to be money, leeching parasites, but he always heard them out. It was only fair. The fact of the matter was though; he didn’t really need anyone to look out for him. He had done fine on his own, or at least with a small amount of guidance from his stable mates. At the end of the day he was one of the top draws in his federation, ranked reasonably highly, in the upper echelons of Primetime Central and was carrying enough gold to satisfy him at the moment. “John, I know you are a busy man, I know that you will have heard this before, but at least let me explain my situation. I don’t want to do this over the phone, I’ve got dinner reservations at a place called Captains Landing, its not too far from your hotel, great little Thai place. If you are even remotely interested come and meet me there. Oh the dinner is on me too.” Kyle Stone continued to scream down the phone, before hanging up. John was in no major rush, taking the time to relax and enjoy the shower. One thing was for certain though; John was not about to pass up a free meal, not in a town where he had no idea of anything. Columbus was brand new to him and besides; he would need to build up his strength. He had a match in eight hours. 6 Hours Before The Match. “John, I’m glad you decided to come” Kyle said as he approached John with his hand extended. To John’s surprise Kyle was quite a small man and didn’t seem to shout at all. John had stepped into the Captains Landing and had his senses attacked on all fronts, from the smell to the sights it was a wonderful little restaurant. ”Well I always listen to business offers, you never know when a good one might come up. I just want you to know though; I doubt I will be here long. I have a match tonight as I’m sure you are aware” John spoke, he had made eye contact but there was very little interest in his voice as a waiter came over and showed the two to their table. “Basically I’ll cut right to the chase, if you allow me to be your agent. I can promise lucrative deals for you. In fact I have one commercial lined up that would pull you in around fifty thousand dollars for two days work.” Kyle spoke with a large smile on his face; he then turned to the waiter and gave his order, calmly and collectedly. John’s jaw had dropped. His wage in UWF, along with the bonuses that he received from Primetime central was enough to live on, but only just. John wasn’t one of these people that wrestled because it was his life. He did it because it was a job, a talent that he had found and was actually reasonably good at. “Fifty-thousand?” questioned John, just making sure that he had heard the figure correctly. Kyle simply nodded towards John, continuing to smile; casually taking a sip of the glass of water that was in front of him. “That figure is likely to rise too, well depending on how you do tonight that is” Kyle said, his voice was extremely calm and collected, the look in John’s eye was basically letting Kyle know that he had him. John quietly sipped his water, his eyes never leaving Kyle’s. Fifty thousand pounds would come in very handy; recently UWF had been going through quite a rough patch his position was looking in doubt. With a cool fifty thousand, he could live for quite a while comfortably. The problem was though, that he didn’t like agents. He never had done. For four years he had worked independently letting his action in the ring do the talking. “John, you are on the brink of something golden here. The matches that you will be apart of tonight won’t just increase your money, but it will thrust you into the big time. We all know how nervy you get during tournaments, its echoed in the way you wrestle. You see everyone else as someone who is capable of beating you, never trusting in your own ability. When you win tonight, you will become a part of history, as you carve your path towards becoming a god.” The words flowed from Kyle as though he had been practising them for sometime. John continued to look him in the eyes, he was a strong believer that the eyes were the gateway to the soul, from where John was sat, and no lies were being told. “You’re food sirs” an interrupting waiter mentioned as he placed two plates full of Thai cuisine in front of John and Kyle. John looked at the waiter somewhat confused, he hadn’t even ordered yet, how had the waiter known, or decided for that matter what to bring him? John glanced across at Kyle who smiled and nodded in reply. “I hope you don’t mind me taking liberties in ordering for you, its just that you seemed quite bemused when you entered, it looked like you didn’t know what was going on at all. I got you something nice trust me.” Kyle smiled as John looked at the dish in front of him; it was true he had never had Thai food. “Do you mind if I go freshen up?” Enquired John as he pulled his seat away from the table and rose to his feet. Kyle nodded and pointed him in the direction of the rest room, smiling constantly until John was out of sight. At which point he reached into his inside jacket pocket and pulled out a small bottle, full of tablets. Pouring two from the bottle, he swiftly entered them into John’s food, hiding them from immediate view, before putting the bottle into John’s jacket, which he had left resting on the back of the chair. Then with a sickening smile, he began to eat away at his meal. Waiting for John to return. In the rest room, John splashed water over his face and stared at himself in the mirror. It un-nerved him somewhat hearing what he always knew from someone else. He always looked at others plus sides as opposed to their weaknesses. Pulling a paper towel from the dispenser next to him, John dabbed it over his face, drying it in the process. He knew what he had to do, Kyle was not only promising money, he was also offering a way to progress John as a person. He could see his faults, but did not attack them, merely offering advice and ways to better himself. John had made his decision, he was going to accept Kyle’s offer. Striding out of the restroom John sat down, directly opposite from Kyle who was still smiling and tucking into his meal. John nodded in the acknowledgement that Kyle had started without him, if the situation had been reversed; John knew he would have done the same. He sat down silently and took a large fork full of food and entered into his mouth, chewing happily much to the delight of Kyle. “Wow, this is good stuff” Mumbled John as shards of food flew across the room, narrowly missing the ever smiling Kyle. “So, do we have a deal Mr Edwards” Kyle said, the first time that his voice had entered a more serious tone. “Sure, we have a deal,” replied John as he swallowed the food in his mouth and extended his hand to Kyle across the table. 4 Hours Before The Match. The deal had been done; Kyle Stone was now the official representative of John Edwards. John didn’t have time to consider that though, the time until the evening of the tournament was getting shorter. Through the window of the taxi, John could see a number of people walking towards the arena. Yet something inside John felt a lot different. He was buzzing, adrenaline was flowing through his body and his mind was clear. He felt ready, he felt strong. Most of all though, he felt he could win, something that hadn’t happened in a good two years. “So, I’m guessing you’re here for that big ol tournament happening at the arena tonight” The taxi driver blurted out of no-where, causing John to jump with a start, breaking him out of his trance and directing his attention forwards. “Yep, you got the nail on the head there.” Spoke John, he hated small talk, especially in taxis. It was probably just the confined space and the not having anything in common with the driver, it happened all the time. There was silence for a moment, an awkward one at that before John broke it with the universal conversation starter for taxi journeys. “So, have you been busy?” Said John and let himself recline in his seat a little as he waited for the barrage that was bound to come from the taxi drivers mouth in the next few minutes. “Hell yeah, all you wrestler types have been cruising back and forth to gyms, restaurants etcetera.” The taxi driver continued but John didn’t really care. He felt odd. The world around him seemed to be moving faster than usual, he felt more aware of everything. John pulled himself up and winded down the window and stuck his head out. The warm air hitting his face as the taxi drove through the streets of Columbus, the wind blowing through his hair. He truly felt alive. More than alive, he felt strong. Clenching his fists his knuckles turned white, he knew that in a little under four hours he would be fighting. Not fighting for his life, it never got that serious, but fighting for his career. This tournament would make or break John. As Kyle had said, it would make him a god amongst men. John glanced down at his wrists and noticed something, his veins were bulging, like nothing he’d ever seen before, apart from in films and comics, right before some dude turned into a hulking monster. Maybe it was coincidence, maybe it was fate but at that very moment, John’s mobile phone rang. Reaching into his pocket, he pulled out not his mobile phone, but a small glass bottle. A number of pills inside. John stared blankly at the bottle; he had never seen it before, let alone the pills. He placed them on the leather seat in the taxi and proceeded to pull out his mobile phone. It turned out it wasn’t a call, simply a text message. Clicking the buttons frantically as he always did, John eventually read the message. His eyes skimming over the text on the screen. You should be feeling it about now; lets just call it a little insurance. Good luck for tonight not that you’ll need it now. Kyle. John glanced at the phone, then at the bottle of pills on the seat beside him. He stared down at his bulging veins on his forearm. It was all adding up, the feeling of well-being, the feeling of power. It wasn’t a natural feeling. “Driver, get me to my hotel and step on it.” Spoke John, a great deal of urgency in his voice. He had been drugged 2 Hours Before The Match. The sound of retching filled John’s small hotel room. John hunched up over the toilet bowl, his fingers ramming down his throat, forcing himself to vomit, quite violently as well. He had been at it for half an hour. His throat ached, his stomach ached and his fingers were covered in saliva and mucus. He wanted to make sure that it was all out of his system. The thought didn’t occur to him that the damage had already been done. He was still feeling the same buzz as in the taxi except he now felt stronger. It was only instinct telling him to get this poison out of his system. “How could that son of a bitch do that to me” John spoke, his voice echoing off the porcelain of the toilet. “Doesn’t he trust in me, doesn’t he think I can get the job done.” John slammed his fist into the tiled floor. A solid crunching sound could be hurt, with no reaction from John, who proceeded to wretch again, a small amount of fluid flowing into the bowl as he did so. “I can’t feel a thing…” John spoke, tears began to run down his cheek, dribbling off of his chin and causing ripples as they fell into the toilet. “What the hell am I going to do?” 0 Hours Before The Match. “John, Jesse, A-Poc. You’re up next,” shouted one of the workers from within the arena. John turned his head in the direction of the call and sighed. He hadn’t fully returned to normal, he didn’t know if he had the mental strength to go through with it. What if he did win? What if he did score the win over A-Poc and Jesse? It wouldn’t be through his, own abilities, but through something else, something that wasn’t him. He knew what he had to do. “John where the hell are you? We’re waiting!” The cry was louder this time; causing John to push himself off the wall he was leaning on and trudge towards the curtain. His head was hung low, barely wanting to look forwards. Someone else had blown his shot at the big time; he had to do the honest thing. He had to confess. As he walked forwards the noise from the crowd became louder, a different kind of buzz ran through John, the adrenaline rush that he always got when he heard the crowd. As he finally reached the curtain, he allowed himself to peek through, surveying the crowd and his two opponents already in the ring. “John? Are you ready?” the voice carried urgency as the stagehand looked up at John pleadingly. His eyes glanced out to the crowd once more. What he was about to do was stupid, it would more than likely get him into a lot of trouble, but this was his playground and he was not going to let anyone take that away from him. “I’m ready” spoke John, his fists clenched as he breathed deeply and turned to face the curtain. The stagehand gave the thumbs up to the sound technician as ‘Somewhere I Belong’ belted out through the arena and John stepped through the curtain, against his better judgement.
