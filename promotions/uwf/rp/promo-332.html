UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #332

The Next Step

12 Apr 2004

Anarchy — UWF house show in mid-west America

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to the UWF ring after a house show in mid-west America. The Chaos Society stands in the ring watching something on the Universal-tron, the camera turns to see what they are watching.] [Nick Persia spits blood out of his mouth to the ground. Nick Persia: “Be careful what you wish for Adien Pryde because by God you are going to get it. I’m out to get my revenge. You have a target at yoru back. I think you aimed a little bit too high Adien. You should have finished what you started. Don’t leave living people like they are death. I promise you Pryde, there is no hope for you left. God has forsaken you, and punishment has just come along the way.” Nick Persia signs Pryde is death with his thumb and neck.] [The Universal-tron goes black and the camera turns to face The Chaos Society] Joanna : Nick, Nick, Nick. You have fallen into our trap. We have pushed you over the edge and with that and your injuries you are ripe for the picking. Pryde : I warned you and warned you to not ignore me and I made good my promise. You are lucky that all you got out of it was a hospital trip and coughing up blood. You having the belt is just the icing on the cake. I have talked this over with the rest of The Chaos Society and they have OKed it but this will force me to put my tag career on hold to concentrate on taking that Hard Core belt from you and run with it far as long as I can. Raid will work singles matches as well as tag with Dai, but we still will be shooting for those belts. [Pryde stops and turns to Dai and Raid, they both nod in agreement. Pryde steps back and allows Dai to step forward.] Dai : Looks like the powers that be in the UWF have me tagging with Raid. We are facing Ruff Ryder & Malcolm Kennedy, neither of which are tag specialist. With my Tag experience and Raids power and skill I don’t see any reason why we can’t come out on top. SAS will only propel The Chaos Society to the top and get the UWF “Commissioner” to realize that this stable can and will bring the UWF the TV viewers and the PPV revenue to bring the fed to heights it has never seen. Joanna : The Chaos Society is a premier stable, and if people could keep thing fair and not abuse the power that they have taken and not given, we could prove that. That is all we ask is a even playing field and we can prove it. [Pryde makes his way to the front again. He is holding a object that may look familiar to the many of the UWF viewers that watched the PPV] Pryde : Persia, does this look familiar? [Pryde holds out the lead pipe from Persia’s Renaissance match] Pryde : Oh, I’m sorry, you didn’t see it, Let me introduce you to, Lead Pipe meet Nick Persia, Persia meet Lead Pipe. Don’t worry, you will see him again at SAS since it is a Hard Core match. Anything goes and it will. You will probably even see this. [Pryde motions toward the Universal-tron and the camera follows. Up on it is played a clip from Renaissance.] [Pryde puts Persia’s head between his legs and lifts him into a Razor’s Edge position over his shoulders. Moves over and climbs up on the chair facing away from the table.] Rick : This can’t be good for Persia. [With one smooth motion Pryde flips Persia over his shoulders wrapping his right arm around Persia’s head driving him through the table in a Single Arm DDT like maneuver.] Larson : Oh, My, Rod, that was a perfectly good table.] Pryde : Tables are legal, and I intend to use them. Persia, this started a respect thing, you made it much, much more. Have a wonderful week, and I will see you Sunday. [Scene fades to black as The Chaos Society leave the ring]
