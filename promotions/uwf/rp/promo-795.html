FAN~!1: Hot Watersports

29 Nov 2004

Tsunami Oni — On top of the OmnNational Industries building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ah, it’s good to be the king. All right, maybe not an actual king, but an obscenely wealthy wrestling champion. Close enough. Because that’s exactly what Tsunami Oni is. UWF World freakin’ champion. Maybe he isn’t a king, but he sure knows how to live like one. For instance, look where he lives. A penthouse suite on top of the headquarters of his own corporation, OmniNational Industries. A building in one of the most beautiful cities in the world, San Francisco. Sure, some naysayers might say if Tsunami Oni’s so great, why doesn’t he live in a mansion? Simple. Because mansions have lots of extra rooms. That gives “long-lost cousins” and “old high school buddies” an excuse to drop by and stay for weeks on end. No, the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea is waaaaaay too smart for that. He stays far above the rabble, enjoying the privacy. The privacy that allows him to be doing what he is now, kicking back in his hot tub on the roof. Ah, just what the Vivaldi of Violence needs… some relaxation before FANdemonium. He’s earned it. First, Tsunami Oni wins the World title from Mike Montgomery in a viscous, and some say controversial, match. But Oni showed why he’s the one with the gold by defending it a mere week later against Ezra Kazee, a match that was a very decisive victory for the Devil. And a week after that win, he faces one hell of battle. He teams up with Adien Pryde to take part in the UWF’s most famous match… the Venetian Home Rumble. And if that wasn’t enough, Tsunami Oni takes on perhaps his greatest rival, “the Final Fantasy” Seymour Almasy, in the first ever FANdemonium~! Audience Participation match with his World championship title on the line. These aren’t exactly matches you can train for, people. What’s he supposed to do, break into people’s houses? Start brawlson top of bar counters in sports pubs? OK, so neither of those is actually beneath Tsunami Oni, but they’re not gonna happen. Instead he is going to focus his body and his spirit and his mind. That is the way of a true warrior. Hence, the hot tub. That and it’s an excuse to have Shiatsu in a skimpy bikini. How skimpy is this bikini? Well, it certainly leaves nothing to the imagination. But let’s imagine anyways! Shiatsu in a bikini doing jumping jacks. Shiatsu in a bikini touching her toes. Shiatsu in a bikini doing the splits. Mmmmm… Shiatsu… OK, focus. So Shiatsu in her thong bikini... what? You didn’t know it was a thong? Yeah, it is. Ain’t it great? Damn it! Concentrate! So Shiatsu in her thong bikini steps out onto the roof. It’s a chilly night. And you know what that means. Two pointy little nip- Shiatsu: Hey, pervo! Go smack that thing and come back when you can do your job right! Damn! We really oughtta get a gay voice-over. I’ll be good. Shiatsu: Whatever. Let’s get going on this. It’s a chilly night. Shiatsu: Brrrrr… Thanks. So Shiatsu scampers over and slides into the hot tub, sitting across from Tsunami Oni. Shiatsu: Oooo… nice and warm in here… She gives Tsunami Oni a wicked smile. He raises a single eyebrow. Shiatsu: Bet I can make it warmer… Oni: Oh really? And how will you do that? Shiatsu: Like this… Staring into the eyes of Tsunami On, Shiatsu puts both her arms underwater. A moment later she raises them up. In one hand dangles her bikini top and in the other the bottom. Wait... that means she’s nekkid! My Rod, Shiatsu is nekkid! Shiatsu: Dude, chill! You can’t see anything with all the bubbles and steam! But... but… you’re nekkid! Shiatsu: I’ve been naked before. Remember the Paddle on a Pole match? That was almost two years ago. And it was only a glimpse at the end. Shiatsu: Buddy, you really need a woman. I know… … … … … What? Shiatsu: We’re working on something here! Oh yeah. So Shiatsu sets down both halves of her… my Rod… bikini next to the hot tub and keeps staring into Tsunami Oni’s eyes. Oni: Hmmm… it really doesn’t feel any warmer to me. Maybe if you were closer to me. Body heat. Shiatsu: Body heat, you say? Oni: That might work. So Shiatsu slides on over and presses her wet… nude… body against Tsunami Oni, THAT LUCKY BASTARD. Oni: Now the temperature is rising. Shiatsu: Oooo… that doesn’t seem to be the only thing, Mister World champ. Shiatsu leans in and plants a long, deep kiss on the mouth of THAT BLUE-HAIRED FREAK THAT DOESN’T DESERVE HER. Shiatsu: Gee, I don’t seem to doing a good job of helping you relax. This muscle here is soooo tense. Maybe I should massage it? I hate him. I really hate him. Oni: You’re so bad. Shiatsu: I know. That’s why you love me. Oni: You bet your ass. The two kiss again. Get a freakin’ room. Shiatsu: We’ve been through a lot lately, haven’t we? Oni: Yes we have. The reunion with my father. Your injury. Mike Montgomery’s mind games. Shiatsu: Yeah, but we got through all that. And we’re stronger and more in love than ever. Jesus H. Christ! Is Danielle Steele doing your dialogue? I’m taking a coffee break. Call when the soap opera is over. Oni: I’ve never gotten the chance to make Almasy pay for what happened to you. But I will at FANdemonium. It’s like the fans gave me an early Christmas present. Shiatsu: They gave us all one. I want to see you kick his scrawny ass too. Hell, the fans wanna see you do it! And a few of them might wanna help out too. Maybe that’s why it’s Audience Participation. Oni: Either that or they’re a bunch of sociopathic drunks. Shiatsu: Ya got a point there. So you feel pretty good about the match against that clown shoe? Oni: Of course. Why do you ask? Shiatsu: It’s just… I don’t know. Sweetie, this isn’t any Seymour you’ve faced before. This is funny, clueless Seymour with no clue about real world. This isn’t the bad guy wannabe you palled around with and stole cats with. This guy is one sick fuck. Not just because of what he did to me. Hell, he dumped his own wife, the only woman who ever loved him, by beating her half to death. And then bringing in monsters like Clyde and Deville. Seymour’s like some deranged little kid. Whatever he can’t have, he wants to destroy. Like the UWF. Like you. Oni: Like me? Shiatsu: It’s like this, sweetie. When you were running around in that mask and coming up with one wild scheme after another, you were the most hated man around. Hell, people loved to hate you. Then you got your head on straight. You decided what was really important, what really mattered to you. And now the people cheer for you. Meanwhile, Seymour’s bounced around from delusion to delusion until he was finally spit out into the real world. And it’s a place he was totally unprepared for. So he’s going to make it all go away. And at the top of the list is you. You know how he resents you. He says it’s because of how the fans love you now. I think it’s really because you’ve moved beyond the world of fantasy and emerged even better. And Seymour has no idea how to do the same thing because he has no idea who he actuall is. And that is why he’s so damn dangerous. Oni: I can handle dangerous. Shiatsu: This isn’t one of the random thugs out there who just wanna hit or get hit to feel something. Seymour is obsessed with you. He wants to destroy you. And he’s gonna be locked in a cage with you. One of you will be taking a really bad fall. I don’t want it to be you, sweetie. Oni: It won’t be. Remember, I’m Tsunami Oni and this is my world. Like you said, I know what matters to me. There’s no more dreams of “world domination” distracting me. When I’m in that cage up the air, I will be focused on one thing… seeing Seymour Almasy fall. I will be fighting him. He will be fighting all of his demons... and the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. The odds are quite stacked against him. And then I can go back to what’s most important to me. Shiatsu: The World championship? Oni: No, silly… you. Shiatsu: I know. I just wanted to hear you say it. Is the Hallmark moment over? Oh, Rod damn, they’re kissing again! And it’s not one of those kisses from porno flicks that happen right when the music starts to tell you the good stuff is coming up. It’s one of those kisses that make teenage girls in movie theaters go “Awwwwww…” Shiatsu: Hold on... lemme go it something… Shiatsu gets out of the hot tub and… HOLY CATS! SHIATSU IS OUT OF THE HOT TUB! She’s nekkid, Rod dammit! What? Why is there so much steam? I can’t anything! Noooo! God hates me! Shiatsu: OK, I’m back. Oni: What is that thing? Shiatsu, WHO IS STILL NEKKID EVEN THOUGH THERE’S STEAM EVERYWHERE I KNOW SHE’S NEKKID, is hold a large phallic contraption that looks frightening, yet somehow appealing… Shiatsu: This is the new Suzuki-Von Doom Romance Ramjet 6900! It’s the most sophisticated vibrator ever made! This one is just the beta tester, but I know someone. Wanna try it out? Oni: Uh, as intriguing as that sounds, I’m not sure if that thing’s safe enough to be used in water. Shiatsu: I guess your right. It still has testing to go through. Somewhat sad, Shiatsu turns to put the Suzuki-Von Doom Romantic Ramjet 6900 back. But then her bare foot steps in a puddle of water! Shiatsu slips and the Suzuki-Von Doom Romantic Ramjet 6900 flies though the air and lands in the hot tubs! Sparks start flying as Tsunami Oni shakes! And NOT like a Polaroid picture! Shiatsu: Sweetie! The sparks die down and Shiatsu runs over to the unmoving body of the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea, his blue hair standing on end. Well, even more on end. Shiatsu: Oh my God! I-I can’t feel a pulse! I think he’s dead! YES! She’s all mine know! What? What do you mean this is “To Be Continued?” Oni’s dead! No more promos! Ah, I knew I couldn’t be that lucky…
