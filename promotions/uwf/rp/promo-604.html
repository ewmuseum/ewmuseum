UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #604

The Arrival

7 Aug 2004

The Plague — Philadelphia, Pa

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens at the Philadelphia International Airport. It is packed with people. There are camera crews and security staff all over the place. Out of the crowd of people you can see Plague walking towards the camera holding two bags. His hair is down over his face, he is wearing a Terrel Owens Jersey and black jeans. As he walks toward the camera he slowly lifts his head and sees the crowd and the cameras and smiles. Up walks a wan in a business suit and walks up to Plague.] Man: 'Hey you are coming with us, there is a ride already waiting for you.' [Plague grins.] Plague: 'Really? That's great.' [They walk right past the groups of people.] Guy in crowd: 'Plague, good to see you back man, Kick some ass on Sunday.' [Plague looks back and raises his hand in the air and nods.] Man: 'Richard Hayes sent a limo for your arrival, just to show they are no hard feelings about what you did.' [They arrive at the door of the ariport and there is a limo outside.] Plague: 'He sent that!!??' [Plague gets a huge grin on his face.] Man: 'No . . . .' [They exit the airport and the man points down behind the limo to reveal an even bigger and longer limo a hummer converted into a limo.] Plague: 'Holy Shit man!!' [Plague's jaw drops almost to the ground.] Plague: 'Ok, well it seems that Mr. Jamie Shock is going to get his ass kick on Sunday, if that is what Mr. Hayes wants I mean. I really don't know what is going down now days in the UWF. I am just back cause of that damn contract, I was hoping they'd forget about that. But oh well, I am back and that is all that matters now.' [They walk up to the limo the driver steps out and opens the back door. Plague throws his bags into the limo and the driver closes the door. He than goes and opens another door two down from the back door and opens it. Plague steps in and sits down. A few seconds later the other door opens an in sits the man in the business suit.] Man: 'This is pretty sweet isn't it? Oh yeah I'm Chris, on of Mr. Hayes' assisitants.' [Plague extends his hand out and shakes Chris' hand.] Plague: 'Nice to meet you. So where are we going.' Chris: 'Well we are going to take you to your place so you can do what you got to do, then we are off to Chicago, to do business.' [Plague grins again.] Plague: 'Ahhhh yes, business, I remember what that was like.' Chris: 'So you and Jamie Shock, again stepping into the ring. I bet you are hyped.' [Plague shakes his head and sighs.] Plague: 'Oh yeah I am exstatic. I mean I said I would like to face him sometime down the road, but not my first match back damn. But I mean we had some vicious encounters. And I really don't want to go down that road again. Sledgehammers and lightsabers and all other types of crazy shit. This stuff can take its toll. I have been battered and broken and a lot of it has to do with feuding with Shock.' Chris: 'But this is a huge match, the winner gets number thirty in the Regal Rumble, and you know what that could mean?' [Plague interrupts.] Plague: 'Yeah, me finally winning The World Title. How great would that be to return in that kind of fashion huh. I already beat Dunn, and I feel I can do it again. I just need to lose the rust. Hopfully I can get enough of it off by Sunday so I don't make a fool out of myself.' Chris: 'I don't think that will be a problem. I mean you are a legend here, I guess if I can call you that, you've been here since the beginning like Hayes said, so I mean you know how to handle yourself.' [Plague shrugs his shoulders.] Plague: 'I guess I mean I do itend to choke up around the big moments but this time I am going all out, this could be my last chance to get somewhere. I have accomplished everything, except the holy grail, The World Title.' [Plague stops and thinks for a moment about that Title.] Plague: 'I mean Jamie Shock was World Champ, Mike Montgomery, Josh Dunn, I beat them all why am I not Champ? Cause I can't beat Tsunami Oni or Digital Mortality, that is a crock, I can beat them, I just need to focus this time around. I need to make sure nothing distracts me this time. No clique, no nothing, I'm going at this on alone, it is time for a change in The UWF. I think Hayes will know what I mean when I say this.' Chris: 'Yeah, Hayes is a visionary it seems to me, he is working everyone hard each and every night. He wants to put on the best show for the crowd, and bringing you back will definatly do that. Did you see that crowd at the airport, now just imagine tomorrow at Supremacy when you step through the backstage and step back into a UWF ring. All the new fans who may have never got to see a Shock vs. Plague match live, well now they can.' [Plague has his finger up to his temple thinking as Chris speaks.] Plague: 'You know, you are right, I need to come out there and do what I do best, and that is kick ass and take names. I mean Ground Zero, that was one of my shining moments I feel, winning The THC Title for the first time and doing it great fashion. I don't know what Shock has up his sleeve but I guess all I can say is bring it Jamie, just like old times.' [Plague clenches his fist.] Plague: 'I guess I hear that Shock and Hayes are at each other as well as Mercury being thrown into the mix. At the Rumble it is Shock going against Mercury and Shock picks the match type. Now I know Shock he is one sick bastard. And you really want him to pick the match. You must be insane Mercury, or stupid. I guess I'd go with stupid, but I am lost here right now. I need to be filled in when I get back. I guess there are communists again, why does history have to repeat itself. I mean communism is dead, get over it. The Vietnamese tried that those crazy powder throwing freaks, now someone else is trying to do it. And what is this about Tsunami Oni?' Chris: 'He is back, but he got deported or something back to Japan.' [Plague smiles.] Plague: 'Too bad I didn't see him when I was there, we could have had a nice chat.' [The limo stops.] Driver: 'Alright we are at the destination Plague, we will be waiting out here for you to get back, so hurry up we got a nice trip to Chicago to prepare for.' [Plague stretches.] Plague: 'Alright I'll be back, but no more camera, I am not in a talking mood, I just want to relax and get metally prepared for my return. See you tomorrow Shock, can't wait.' [Plague gets out of the limo and the scene fades out to black.]
