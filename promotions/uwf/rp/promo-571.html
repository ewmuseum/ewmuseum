Judgement Day [Part 2]

24 Jul 2004

The Real Deal — Evil Realm

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A little bit of background knowledge needs to be brought to those who ever want to talk smack about The Real Deal. Not many people in the UWF ever considered the DWO nevermind The Real Deal when it was around. When Deal entered UWF he was shown no respect at all, epecially by loverboy and now his son Sacrifice. 'Who the hell does this guy think he is anyway?' Deal snarls through the pitch black, nowhere to be seen' Deal had somehow got a copy of Sacrifice doing his little cha cha slide and setting the stipulations to the match. He was confused by how his match stipulation named...Straight to Hell...had been turned into some monstrousity by this moron... 'What kind of moron changes the name of a stipulation just to make it his own? I am really confused as to why Sacrifice wants to inherit such a great punishment that he'll never forget' The anger coming from The Real Deal fills the darkness shrouding where he is. Shortly after the echo of Deal's voice fades, two eyes open up. The colour of them is bright red and with closer inspection it seems as though the fires of hell burn within the iris' that surround the pupils. Deal speaks with anger and frustration... 'Why must I waste my time with these fools. In my personal opinion I think I could win the match without going to the ring on Sunday night. Problem is that I have to stand up and walk to that ring and show myself off to the world, beat the hell out of Sacrifice for two minutes and then walk off. It's pathetic....UWF's pathetic. It wasn't often that Deal got this angry...angry enough for his eyes to be pure red. He just wasn't liking the fact that just because Sacrifice was bitching to him that he had to go and throw him in a pit of fire. Candles begin to light up on either side of The Real Deal, lighting the stretch of land that he is on. He sits relaxed, his legs stretched out and his arms resting upon them. His eyes still beam with red horror as he sits completely still, not moving a muscle. He shouts out to whatever or whoever is infront of him... 'Elifor!' His voice echoes throughout the expansive area in which he is seated. Slowly the sky turns a bright red which illuminates Deal's face. To the right of him the head of the dragon that he had previously seen comes into view. He turns and greets it by stroking it's firey mouth. The dragon sets its head upon the ground and a woman, dressed in all black slowly walks down the neck of it. It was Elifor... Elifor was the personification of beauty...but she was also damn evil. These two are normally linked together so this was no surprise to Deal. Elifor steps off the dragon's neck and moves towards The Real Deal. She grabs his hand and motions him to stand. He does this and receives a kiss to each cheek. Elifor speaks out, her voice soothing and calm... 'It is good to see you Real Deal. I feel we have made the right decision in choosing you as the one who will destroy all' Deal grins at Elifor. 'I thank you Elifor but I must ask, what are we doing here?' Elifor pulls Deal towards the dragon and the both stand infront of it looking into nothingness... 'Real Deal, this is where you become the ruler of evil...' As they stand the dragon pulls it's head back and without warning flames both Elifor and Deal with complete hellfire. When the breath of fire ends, the two are no-where to be seen and everything remotely close to the flames is destroyed. -To Be Continued-
