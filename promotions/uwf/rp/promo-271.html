UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #271

Overconfident? Nope, The Truth

4 Mar 2004

The Plague — Penthouse Suite

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up at in a hotel room, the room is huge. There is a table in the center that has a tray full of food. A cheese steak, fries, a salad and a two liter bottle of Pepsi. There is a chandelier hanging above the table. The blinds are white and they are closed blocking the windows and the sunlight. You can see the end of the bed in the left corner of the room. In the right corner there is a couch. Sitting on the couch is The Plague, he is wearing shorts and a tank top, his hair is tied back and he is staring at a television. He is rubbing his chin, it looks as if he is deep thought. The camera goes around the table where the food is sitting and focuses on Plague who looks up at the camera and points his finger.] Plague: 'Hold on ok?' [You hear the faded voice of Iron Lung on the TV, Plague cracks a smile. He than shuts off the TV and stands up. He walks past the cameraman and heads toward the table. He grabs a handful of fries and shoves them into his mouth. He than pours himself a glass of Pepsi. He chugs about half of the glass. He takes a napkin and wipes his face. He picks up the cheese steak and takes a bite out of it. He than places it back down and heads back to the couch carrying the tray with him. The camera is follow the whole thing. Plague again wipes his mouth and crosses his arms over his chest.] Plague: 'I am in the middle of my meal but I can still find time to chat with The UWF.' [Plague takes another bite out of his cheese steak and than takes another drink.] Plague: 'I guess this THC Match is a lot more serious than I thought. Maybe I underestimated young Pendragon, or maybe I have just not looked deep enough into this match up.' [Plague looks at the cheese steak and picks it up he than tosses it into a nearby trashcan.] Plague: 'Nothing like a Philly cheese steak, what the hell were they thinking calling this a Philly cheese steak, God, I should sue for false advertisement. But I guess you can't win'em all. And on the note of winning, maybe Raid thinks I am overconfident, and I called it, I knew he was going to spit that bullshit, but yeah, I guess some people are just to predictable. But what wasn't predictable was Iron Lung. wow, let me tel you something, you really showed your true colors in your last promo. You shocked me, and that is something that many people have accomplished in my career.' [Plague grabs some more fries and eats them. He finishes his drink and leans back on the couch stretching his legs out on the leg rest in front of him.] Plague: 'I guess I should be a better man and not laugh at another man's misfortune. I could joke about you being unloved by your father, I could rip on the fact you were abandoned, but I am not going to go that route today. I would a few years ago, but not now. You see, you showed you were the better man, your father hurt you, and basically you stepped up and said to him, you' were still my dad and that's what matters most.' [Plague wipes an invisible tear away from his eye and smiles.] Plague: 'Oh boo woo.' [Plague starts laughing.] Plague: 'Seriously Iron Lung, your dad is dead, he can't help you now, he isn't going to come out on Sunday and help you hold onto your sacred THC Title. You see this isn't a movie, this is real life. You can talk to the dead all you want Pendragon, but when all is said and done, you will fall victim to The Infection and you will be tapping in the center of the ring. And the conclusion is, I will be walking out of Supremacy The New UWF THC Champion. Period.' [Plague gets up and walks over to the mini fridge that is sitting on the side of the bed, he opens it and grabs a bottle of Grey Goose Vodka. He walks back over and sits down. He takes his glass and pours the vodka about half way in the glass. He than grabs the two liter bottle of Pepsi and fills the other half of the glass. He takes a drink.] Plague: 'Ahhhh, now that is living. Once again, thanks to Mike Montgomery for this Penthouse suite and a full mini bar, man it is great living in luxury, and there will be more where that comes from after I am carrying the THC Title once again.' [Plague takes another drink.] Plague: 'Now I am no Al Crud, but I do like my share of drinks once in a while. And on Sunday after I win, the drinks will taste that much sweeter.' [Plague shrugs his shoulders.] Plague: 'I guess I just can't bring myself down, I know I will win, nothing is stopping me on my mission to once again reign supreme as a Hardcore Icon in The UWF. Straight out of Philly.' [Plague pounds his fist on his chest and nods his head.] Plague: 'You see Iron Lung and Raid, Hardcore is in my blood, and trust I have spilt enough blood iand hardcore in the UWF. I damn near died in a lot of my matches, I have had hundreds of stitches, and you know what I will probably have hundreds more, and you know what, thats great. I want that Hardcore Title and I will do whatever it takes to get it. It was mine first, and it will be mine again. Raid you talk about winning battle royals to gain THC Shots, well back in The GIWF I accomplished the same thing, so in a way you and I are similar. And now we are both going after Lung. And Lung you have everything to lose my friend. And Raid your right, Lung doesn't have to be pinned to lose his Title, sucks to be him huh? But do you really want to be the one to fall victim to The Black Death or The Infection to cost Lung his Title? Cause I know I sure as hell am not getting pinned. And you know I am not allowing Lung to Pin you that is for sure.' [Plague is glowing with confidence as you hear a knock coming from his door. Plague gets up and heads towards the door. He opens it.] Plague: 'Ahh yes the tapes I have asked for thanks kind sir, here is something for your troubles.' [Plague hands the man a hundred and closes the door.] Plague: 'Well time to do my studying, By Sunday I will know more about Iron Lung and Raid than they know about themselves. Overconfident? Ha, please, you may call it that, but I call it truth. Take it for what it is worth Lung and Raid, but when all is said and done, you will look back at what I have been saying this past week and say 'damn he was right the whole time, I guess he really is as good as he says.' See you Sunday, shine that THC Title up for me Iron Lung, cause it is coming home with The Plague. See you in the ring.' [Plague walks over to the couch and sits down, he pulls out the tapes labeled 'Raid Matches' and 'Iron Lung' and places the Raid on in the VCR. He stretches out again and hits play. He crosses his arms and watches as the scene fades out to black.]
