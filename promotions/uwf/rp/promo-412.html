UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #412

COW-A-BACK!

15 May 2004

El Turtlé — NY Sewers

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
El Turtlé: 'What are you gonna do about it dude?' The best damn wrestler to ever step foot in a UWF ring's voice is heard. Who could it be? Well I'm pretty sure its EL TURTLé~! The voice echos a bit and the smelly old New York Sewers open up the scene. There are papers scattered on the ground with varying headlining, like TITANIC SINKS and HITLER AND ELVIS SMOKING POT IN CUBA! You know the usual stuff. Then the roll of wheels and cement sounds and awakes the rats sending them scattering in different directions. El Turtlé: 'COWABUNGA DUDE!' El Turtlé skates by going up and down the circular sewer system, the camera turns to see his shell as the board buckles underneath El Turtlé and gets caught on a pizza box sending El Turtlé face first into the pavement. El Turtlé: 'Totally bogus dude. Primera mi tv breakso y nowo this...o.' El Turtlé opens the box of pizza and sees a half eaten slice of pizza with something green on it and a mouse nibbling on the box. El Turtlé: 'Awesome dude.' El Turtlé smacks the mouse and than grabs the piece of pizza and with his free hand tries to whip of the green mold. El Turtlé: 'Waste not want not, hombre.' Right then as the pizza is only a few centimeters, because I had a ruler, in front of El Turtlé's mouth the wheels of a skateboard are heard again. And than some shouting then four more turtles come out of no where and either get tripped up on El Turtlé or get around him but stop to confront him. Raphael: 'Well if it is the little runt of the group... How's it going Giovanni?' Leonardo: 'Raph, go easy on him. I swear ever since those new action figures you think that you are perfect. Raphael: 'It's not my fault that your doll only had one eye.' Donatello: 'Hey man what's that?' Donatello points to El Turtlé's hand in which the pizza still rests. El Turtlé: 'Nothing. Really dudes it's nothing.' Donatello: 'So what I am seeing things?' Raphael: 'Fess up runt. Otherwise I'm going to tell Splinter that you've been holding out on us. We're a TEAM remember.' Michaelangelo: 'Just let have it alright guys.' Raphael: 'No way ever since he joined that UWF he has been better than us. Prancing around wrestling other people. While we are out there fighting crime. I think we all have to tell him where to go.' Leonardo: 'No one is telling anyone to go anywhere dudes. Look money has been tight after the 3rd movie but what is there that we can do?' Donatello: 'Alright well didn't we have something important to do until numnuts here interrupted us.' El Turtlé: 'I could really go for a fresh pizza... you know with the extra cheese and pepperonis! I wonder what April is up to? Meanwhile, April O'Neal is getting gangbanged by four random men that are wearing The Foot outfits. Donatello: 'I don't want to know.' Raphael: 'Yeah April is a slut.' Leonardo: 'Alright let's go guys. Gio you want to come bro?' El Turtlé: 'No way dude, that path is totally bodacious anyway dudes. The four other turtles, that are no longer Teenage, skate off and before El Turtlé can eat the pizza Rapheal snatches it and skates off. El Turtlé looks at the camera and grins. El Turtlé: 'What dudes? You got a problem? Those bros, they don't understand how totally awesome I truely am. I mean all they like to do is skateboard and eat pizza... totally bogus. But you know what is bogus dudes, the fact that everyone is asking me why I attacked Bret Phillips... I mean I'm like DUDES, I AM THE BODACIOUS JOBBER KILLER! I take out losers who are bogus, and I have ended many a career... many a career. So I attacked that dude because he was a jobber dudes. He is a jobber and he should be totally taken out of UWF limb by limb. I mean I am awesome because I destroyed him... I am totally going to be ending some more careers bros by just crashing uncool dudes. It was so radical to see the look on Phillips' face when he was like, 'Uhhh...' and then I nailed him with my shell... I LOVE BEING A TURTLE! UWF THE MOST RADICAL, BODACIOUS, AWESOME JOBBER KILLER IS BACK! COWABUNGA!' End.
