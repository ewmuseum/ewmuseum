In the Gym...

19 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — in the Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So the Corporal has said his piece before the nurses gave him his afternoon pills and sat him down for his mid afternoon nap. Well enough said, the corporal seems to think that a whole heap of buzz words and clips of someone else’s promotion to bulk out his own mean he has a valid point? The entire promotion has the air of a senile old man jabbering something to sound intellectual one last time. Perhaps if the Corporal, the man who can work a computer but not a video player, were to trawl through some of the history of the UWF he would find out who exactly he is facing rather than who he thinks he’s facing. Perhaps after a good rest and in the morning after he’s forgotten all what he said yesterday, he’s get up, get another video camera and say everything he already did all over again. Where did Corporal Punishment get to without the help of a cattle prod? Despite everything the old man still has dismissed Dunn as a new face jobber upstart; well that’s his own problem. Dunn’s also been around the block a couple of times, if the Corporal even bothered to look around anywhere in the UWF site, he’d find out that Dunn is the longest reigning Intercontinental Champion, and undefeated there of. Sometimes it takes a while for elderly brains to work up to speed, and this is definitely evident in the case of Mr Punishment. *** [The scene opens and we can here scattered grunts and sounds of fists hitting something soft. What we see is the roof area and rafters of a warehouse. Sun shines in through the small windows at the top, glinting off the dust particles swirling about in the rafters creating beams of sunlight. The camera moves slowly down to ground level we see that even though there’s brilliant sunshine outside it’s still dank and gloomy inside the building. Two or three people are scattered around various gym equipment, including treadmills rowing machines and bench presses. A large dust covered clock on one wall keeps the time, showing that in Franks all night gym; it is 3 in the afternoon. Although there are a few people here today, the camera moves past all of them and makes to a guy with his back to the camera laying into a punch bag with a steady rhythm, the vest he’s wearing giving us a clue to who he is, even if it is only very slight. The phrase on the back of the vest reads “Communist Scotsman”. And yes, the man wearing the vest is indeed Joshua Dunn. The camera moves round to the side of Dunn, and we can see that there are large heavy bags under his eyes, and that, quite oddly his eyes are shut. Our attention gets diverted from the self blinded Dunn for a moment when a middle aged balding man walks through into the gym area wheeling a trolley in front of him. He has towels slung over most of the corners, and one over his shoulder, and on the trolley itself are half a dozen plastic bottles of clear liquid. He stops in the middle of the people using the equipment, and moves the towel on his shoulder revealing a shirt displaying that he is an employee of Franks all night gym, and a name tag telling us that he is that Frank.] Frank: Water anyone? [An almost unanimous sigh of relief goes up as they all abandon their equipment and head over to the trolley, all that is except Joshua Dunn who is still rhythmically pummelling the punch bag. As everyone else helps themselves to a bottle and a towel, frank looks over to Dunn and picks up a water bottle and a towel and heads over to him. He is about to lay a hand on his shoulder but thinks better about it, instead he takes a step back and simply calls to Dunn] Frank: Another all night training session? [Dunn’s fists still pumps out blow after blow to the bag, but his eyes do open and he takes a sudden influx of breath, as he realises that all 283lbs of himself is quivering from exhaustion. Instead of trying to find somewhere to sit down, he simply lowers his fists and turns to Frank, the sweat glistening over his body, and hair matted into an unattractive mesh. He takes the bottle from Frank and takes a long drink emptying half of the bottle and mops his brow with the towel Frank hands to him] Frank: You shouldn’t over stretch yourself you know [Dunn takes another swig from the bottle and looks back to Frank, almost appalled at what he’s suggesting] JD: It’s only Corporal Punishment; the man’s more contradictive than Ruff Ryder for Christ’s sake Frank: He’s only around the same age as me, and I could still give you a run for your money if I tried JD: Don’t make me prove a point Frank, just let me get going with my training. Even though I should beat down the Corp for calling me Kevin Starr, I don’t want to make him need a hip replacement now. [He slings the towel over his own shoulder and makes his way over to an unused treadmill and powers it up, drapes the towel over the handle, sets down the bottle of water and cranks up the speed to 5mph, a nice healthy speed. Frank wanders up behind him, Dunn simply carries on jogging, leaving Frank to carry the conversation onwards] Frank: He called you Kevin Starr? JD: He called me a spotty faced jobber, so it’s much in the same thing [Another short period of silence while Frank thinks on that last statement, eventually giving himself a little chuckle] Frank: He’s getting to you isn’t he JD: Starr? Frank: No Corporal Punishment JD: What makes you say that? [Frank moves round to the front of the treadmill and leans on the hand bar, a small grin on his face] Frank: Last time you had an entire night session in here was when you went up against Digital Mortality and Moraless for the tag titles [Dunn just gives Frank a long hard look from his place in the treadmill] JD: Maybe this is something I should be doing more often, that’s all Frank: Whatever you say Josh JD: Even though, I am getting to him. You can always tell when a man bases an entire promotion about what you said in yours. He bought a video player to prove a point. And a bad point at that, being unable to work such a simple thing like a video player is not something to be proud of. Neither is using another mans promotion to make yours seem larger. He’s scraping the bottom of the barrel in this one. He can tell it, and like many people, he tries to bluff his way through it. Frank: Uh huh JD: Do I get to get on with my training yet? [Frank stops leaning on the treadmill bar and take a step back holding his hands up defensively] Frank: Sorry, I’ll leave you to it JD: good, you do that [Frank takes himself away and over to the other people in the gym, who have gotten back to their own training. Dunn cranks up the speed of the treadmill a few more notches. Frank pulls one of the more burly men aside fro a bench press, and whispers something into his ear, and points towards Dunn. A little hesitant at first, the man eventually walks towards Dunn from behind and stops to look back at Frank to make sure that he’s supposed to do this. Frank urges him on. The man turns back to Dunn, and reluctantly grabs him around the waist and German suplexes him off of the treadmill, Dunn thankfully misses a stack of dumbbell weights and lands on a padded mat. This action prompts Dunn to get swiftly to his feet and look around startled] Frank: See, your reactions are more sluggish, go home and get some rest, it’ll do you good [Dunn looks around a bit more, looking far angrier than you might expect. When he realises what just happened, he grabs a sports bag with his belongings in it and leaves the gym, giving Frank a bit of an evil glare, shutting the door forcefully behind him] [A few seconds pass where everyone is just deadly silent, watching the door, expecting him to come bursting back in. but Frank breaks the trance by clapping his hands, grabbing their attention] Frank: Come on Guy’s get back to it, I’m sure you don’t want to pay for just standing around like that [And so the Scene Fades, and some more light shed on the Mystery that is Joshua Dunn]
