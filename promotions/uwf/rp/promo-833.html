UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #833

Chaos in the Forecast(edit)

14 Dec 2004

Raid — Chaos Society Locker room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A hand reaches out and grabs the camera lens and aims it toward the body attached to it.] Raid: Is there any problem Adien? Pryde: No problems Raid, these losers were just leaving. ‘Snap’: We were? Joanna: We were waiting for you. Get in here. Raid: As soon as I am done here. [Joanna closes the door as Raid approaches ‘Snap’ Meyer.] Snap: “So Raid how do you feel about having to face you stable mate in you first match back in the UWF?” Raid: “Did you just here anything that Pryde just said! Yea he’s my stable partner, but we’re here to wrestle and if it means that we have to wrestle each other than so be it; because in the end we both know that the better man, no matter who it is, will win!” Snap:” Do you have any thing to say about Kazee’s condition?” Raid: “All that I have to say is that that big bastard of a demon had better watch his UN holy ass, because NO ONE! And I mean NO ONE! Puts one of my best friends in to the hospital and gets away with it. Who the fuck, do those two think they are!” Snap:” Those are some pretty bold words to be saying about a demon!” Raid: “Demon my ass. If he wants to see Hell on earth than you tell that son-of-a-bitch I’m right here waiting for him. And he had better bring some back up with him.” Snap:” Well he is the THC champ you know. And speaking of champs you are a former T.V champ and I.C champ, now that you back in the ring, what do you have your sights set on?” Raid: “My sights have always been the same. They are the reasons I eat, sleep, breathe, and bleed. The thing I desire the most is total and utter chaos. When I step into the ring I only have one thing on my mind, and that is to inflict as much pain on my opponent as I can. And the easiest way to do that is with chaos, I mean look around at the world today, every time you turn on your television set you see chaos; and what does this chaos bring? It brings pain. So if it happens that someone who holds a title gets in my way I will just make them apart of the debris that is leftover after the chaos of Raid has passed.” Snap:” Wow, nuff said. So this chaos thing you speak of when are we gonna witness it, or have we already?” Raid:” This CHAOS thing is always around us, and in us I mean myself, Pryde, Dai and Joanna, we are the heart and soul of chaos. Like right now there is chaos going on and you don’t even know it, and you probably won’t even notice it until WE make it clear. There is always something going on that leads to chaos. Take CoC for example. The events that happen there are just another step towards total chaos. A big steeping stone if you will. Events have already been set in motion that will bring about chaos at CoC and those things that happen there will start another event that will bring about chaos. For us this is a way of life, it’s what we do. If we’re not bringing about panic, disorder and destruction then we are planning our next big move to set things into motion that will lead into chaos.” Snap: “All right, I see what you mean!?! So what about the Communist? How do you feel about having to wrestle him?” Raid:” He will be a test no doubt. He is one of the biggest guys in the locker room, but that doesn’t mean he’s gonna win. In a match against either one of Pryde or myself, I would give him a little more that a standing chance to win, but with both Pryde and my self he’s only gonna be dust in the tornado of chaos.” Snap:” Chaos, yea sure! Well there you have it folks, looks like the forecast for the next few days is gonna be chaos and more chaos, so they say. It seems to me that the Chaos Society has something up there sleeves but only time will tell.” [The camera starts to pan away from snap as Raid walks away. Behind Snap you can see both Pryde and Dai but they are hunched down at Snaps foot level.] Snap;” So till this is ‘Snap’ Meyeeerrrrrrrrrrrr… [As he says that he is quickly snatched up by a rope that was around his legs, placed there by Pryde and Dai. As he hangs there swinging back and forth you can see Pryde, Dai, Joanna and Raid all standing behind him laughing. Raid walks up to him and stops him from swinging.] Raid:” HERE’S A LITTLE CHAOS FOR YA! Maybe next time you’ll believe ME.” [As Raid starts to walk away he gives Snap a little slap on the cheek, and Snap begins to swing again. As he walks next to the camera man he gives him a shove and the camera man falls into the wall breaking the camera. And thus the scene fades to black.]
