UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #249

Sunday and Beyond

27 Feb 2004

Anarchy — The arena for sundays card

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to the UWF ring as it is being set up for this Sunday’s card. The camera is zoomed out wide. Ring crew are running around getting everything ready, dropping things, bumping into each other, the usual for this motley bunch. Inside the ring are 2 men, sitting on opposite turnbuckles. On one side sits a muscular man of about 5'10', black hair cut in military style. He wears a black leather jacket and black jeans. This is the man named Adien Pryde] Pryde: First off, Ruff Rider, you are right. We have never faced you, and may lose to you but you see, we have seen your matches, which may or may not impress us, but we have studied your tapes and know your flaws. On the other hand, you 2 know nothing about us. That my good sir… [Pryde snickers} Pryde: That is where we have the advantage. We may be overlooking the Prime Alliance, but guess what… [Pryde waits a few seconds] Pryde: Times up, can’t wait to long, other wise your mind may wander. You don’t have the titles. When we came here to the UWF, we had 2 things in mind, back up Raid in his problems with the Player’s Club and capturing the Tag-Team Titles. You two are just an obstacle, a stepping stone. We want the Gold. [A voice comes from the other side of the ring. As the camera pans to the over, you see a man in black jeans, a black duster and a black wide rimmed hat with his head lowered] Dai: That’s right; we will take on all comers. Any combination, any singles match. We will climb to the top over whoever gets in the way. [Dai slowly raises his head and peers straight into the camera with his deep blue eyes, long red hair draped over he shoulders] Dai: And I mean all comers. Seymour Almasy, Bruce Gordy, Bondage the S… Pryde: That’s right… [Camera man turns around to catch Pryde jumping down from the turnbuckle] Pryde: Bondage, you are a disgrace to the wrestling industry. I have been in this business for 10 years, and you do a dishonor to all of the wresting greats who used ring work, not shock value to climb the ranks. After this tag match we have on Sunday, you are my #1 target to give a wrestling reality check to. We prefer tag matches, but you are the exception. Dai: Kazee and Montgomery don’t think we haven’t forgotten about you two. You have what we came for, and we will take it, but first we need to get the small business out of the way. The way I see it is Montgomery is getting way too much of a big head with his power. You may have the skills, and you may have the back-up, but your I AM THE MAN of the UWF is getting me sick. Push people too far and they may just push back, like through a table. Just a little heads up. [Pryde and Dai walk toward the camera] Pryde: Prime Alliance, we will see you on Sunday. Dai: Everyone else, hunting season is open. [A woman in black biker shorts and red hooded sweatshirt can be seen enter through the door on the far side of the arena. She has long brown hair tied back in a pony tail.] Joanna: Pryde, Dai. The car is here, lets get g… [A stage crew member bumps into here with a ringside railing] Crew Member: Oh sorry miss, but I didn’t see you. Joanna: Didn’t see me? [Joanna grabs something from behind her back.] Joanna: DIDN’T SEE ME? [She starts to put on her left hand what seems to be a falconer’s glove but with what seems to be metal studs. As this is taking place Pryde, Dai and the camera man start climbing out of the ring. The crew member set down the railing and starts to back up waiving his hands in protest.] Crew Member: Honest Miss, I didn’t mean anything, I mind was wander to my date tonight. Joanna: Your date tonight, I know you crew members are dim, but at least you could pay attention to you work. When you pay attention you learn new and better ways to do your job. Crew Member: Yes Miss, I will pay attention, anything you say. Joanna: When I was a drill sergeant, there was one lesson I taught all of my recruits and that is Dai? [The crew member looks nervously over his shoulder at the approaching wresters] Dai: Joanna is no Miss. [As the crew member turns to face Joanna, she nails him with a left jab to the jaw. The crew member falls to the ground. As Joanna starts to mount him and punish him some more.] Joanna: You will never take your eyes off of your work, and you will never let you attention be diverted. [Blood starts to flow as Pryde begins to pull Joanna off the now unconscious worker. Dai turns and puts his hand over the camera lens.] Dai: Shows over, go film your footage elsewhere. [Scene fades to black]
