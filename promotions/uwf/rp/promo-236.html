UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #236

Psychological What?

23 Feb 2004

Ezra Kazee — Outside a porn store

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in front of the Triple X Book Store. The front of the building is read, and the doors are set inside of a little cut out into the building. Jogging in place in front of them is “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a pair of black sweatpants, black sweatshirt, and a black stocking cap. Around his waist he is wearing the UWF Tag Team Titles. Around both shoulders he is wearing what look to be very heavy weights. The camera pans out and it appears that he is also wearing very heavy weights around his ankles. He has worked up quite a sweat. He stops, just looking into the camera.) Kazee: Well, Mr. Kennedy, looks like your friend is back. Looks like the Prime Alliance wants to make a come back. Goody for them. Looks like the Native American wannabe has offered you to come back to were you think you belong. But hey, he’s right. Why not go to the second best stable in the UWF. It’s a great opportunity for you. Granted, that y’all will never be as big as the Players Club, but why not surround yourself with those you think you can trust…… But the fact remains, Mr. Kennedy, I continue to sweep our series. It’s now up to 3-0. I now hold a singles victory over you. I now hold a triple threat victory over you. And I now hold a Tag Team match victory over you. I think it’s pretty safe to say that while even the sun shines on a dog’s ass some days, it hasn’t shined on yours in a long time, at least when I’m concerned……. (Kazee pulls a bottle of water out of his fanny pack, takes a long drink, and puts it back.) Also, it looks like my “rival” Raid has well, decided to make a move of sorts. He’s decided that his residence isn’t good enough I guess and is now living at the big house, with Bubba, playing Chutes without the Ladders. Don’t worry Raid; we were only doing what was right. I mean you did destroy that sweet old lady’s house, which was going to cost the UWF plenty according to the receipts I’ve seen, so something had to be done...….. But I’m sure that your friends Anarchy can more than afford to bail you out. I’m sure that they’re “HUGE” salary they made while they were “dominating” the WWA is more than enough to bail you out, find you a decent lawyer, and keep you out of jail. Then y’all can go back to playing bad ass exterminators since y’all love to play it so much. And you can go after your little hardcore title, and your little hardcore matches, and watch your pals Anarchy, the “great” athletes they are, job over and over and over again to The Players Club….. But that’s enough of you guys. Now onto my opponent for Sunday. The Bondage Sex Fiend or something like that. I’m not even sure I know what to expect out of this. I mean look at these guys they are quite, well, disgusting. Now, yes, I’ll admit, I was a little allured by them at first, but after I’ve seen their smut around the UWF, I must admit that I am sick of it……. This isn’t the first time I’ve faced a sex addict. The first one was named Lyle Wile. You put him to shame. You guys are sick, and well, this is the UWF, and the “W” stand for wrestling, which is completely different from what you guys are doing. Your type of wrestling, I’ll be honest, I enjoy doing, but that’s at home, with my wife, behind closed doors. Not out in public in the middle of a wrestling ring. Not only have you dropped our rating from Y14 to NC-17, which I thought was almost impossible to do being that this is a television show, there are several sponsors who are backing out of their contracts with the UWF…… If that is what y’all were planning to accomplish, then you’ve done it. But I would hope there is more to it than this. Your smut, it doesn’t belong in a wrestling ring. It belongs in places like this……. (Kazee points behind him at the store) I have worked my entire life to be where I am today. I am an amateur wrestling champion. I am a multi time champion in the UWF, GIWF, FFA, MWF, and several other feds. I have worked too hard to make wrestling and to take wrestling to wear it is today to have it ruined by the likes of you……. And the Intercontinental Title will not belong to you either. You are not the type of representatives we want for the UWF. You are not the type of representatives we want anywhere but a triple x store of some sort. And I am not going to be held back from the IC title again by a sex freak like I was in my first run here in the UWF. I will not let the likes of you represent the UWF, because it is not appropriate…… Y’all can come out and say how I’m sex deprived, how I need a hug, need this, need that. I’ll tell you what, it’s all crap. I don’t need anything but to defeat you and make my way to the finals of this tournament. Your first match here, it was impressive, I will give you that. You beat a top notch wrestler. You are obviously an accomplished athlete who hides behind the psychology of sex, a psychology that your talents do not need…….. All your psychological warfare, you go ahead and use it. I’ve faced it before. I am not phased by psychological warfare. I’m just going to come at you as hard and as best as I possibly can, because that’s what got me were I am today. My heart, my blood, my sweat, you will not be able to overcome these, because they are my advantage. My knowledge is my advantage. And you, your psychology, while it may be your advantage, does not work against me…… Good Luck, Sex Fiend, you’ll need it. (Kazee starts jogging in place again. After a few minutes, he starts jogging to the left. Soon he is out of sight and the camera fades to black.)
