UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #594

The Green Demon

3 Aug 2004

Green Demon — Japan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We are treated to a video montage of the great UWF/NGPW feud of 2002. We see multiple stills of Demon taking down numerous members of the UWF roster of yesterday and finally we see a picture of the Green Demon, current day:: The Green Demon, live and via satellite from Japan! I never thought in a great while that I would be again working with UWF, let alone helping to train some of their young prospects, but things change; management, roles in our lives, attitudes. Here we are 2 years later and UWF is thriving once more and working side by side with my very own training grounds. I get this call from the UWF management; since Mori is leaving we were wondering if you could work in UWF till his return. I said well why the hell not, I feel I need to accomplish a little more in my career in the U.S. So this week I take on this “Shocker” guy. I can see he is a gifted young man who wishes to give me a run for my money. Well I am afraid “Shocker” your gonna be hard pressed to find any mercy from this masked man. You see as I am a respected figure in the pro wrestling community, I’m also a mean son of a bitch who doesn’t even show his beloved students any mercy. I play hardball and I don’t play any other way. I’m gonna stretch you, chop the living hell out of you and drop you straight down for a 3 count or submission. That’s how I’ve been operating for over 12 years now and what makes you think that I’m gonna start changing for you? I’m not, I’m gonna show you what the Demon Dojo is all about. Hard asses and hard beatings from me. I’ll see you in the ring “Shocker” ::We then come back to a visual of the Demon Dojo logo::
