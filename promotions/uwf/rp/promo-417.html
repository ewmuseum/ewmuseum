UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #417

The Who?

20 May 2004

Joshua Dunn — Outdoor Cafe

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Who? The not so infamous Formorions have made their return to UWF soil, against the Sex crazed duo of Bondage the Sex fiend and Nymphomaniac. But that’s not all. Oh no, that would make something a little too easy for the obscure new comers, they face a third team. The team of Joshua Dunn and Nick Persia. These two could be considered major assets to the UWF Roster. They both have had a fruitful career, holding ample title belts, making their names house hold. But have we ever seen them in a team before? No we have not. Then again the most unlikely duos have held the tag team title belt. And not so long ago, against both Kenny Mercury and the estranged Beezlebozo, Dunn paired up with Iron Lung to defeat them. They had no team experience but pulled off the win. Maybe Dunn’s just a natural team player. Then again the champion of the people wouldn’t want to disappoint would they?] [The scene opens in an open-air café. Around a circular square several tables with parasols are arranged and the sun shines down upon the occupants of the café. There’s a strangely continental Europe feel to the place. Perhaps it’s the cobbled surface that the tables adorn, or maybe just that it is an outdoor café. Whatever the reason we see much bustle, laughter, jest and lots more happy people with happy faces. The camera plunges into the joyous gathering, to a table situated near he outskirts of the café boundary, shown with the cobbles being replaced by the more practical but less elegant tarmac. Sitting on his own, a cup of tea and saucer perched on the table, reading the days newspaper is Joshua Dunn. Wearing red t-shirt and black jeans. The weather too hot to permit his leather jacket, it’s slung over the back of the chair he’s sitting on. He whacks out the paper so it stays standing firm as he reaches with one hand to the teacup raising it to his mouth while still reading the newspaper. He takes a sip of the steaming beverage, slurping it quietly before setting it back down in the saucer, almost transfixed by the story in the newspaper] JD: So… [He flings down the paper in almost disgust, seeing the headline for the first time we can read it as “The Rebirth of the Curtain” obviously a story on the newly communist ranks of the rejuvenated Iron Curtain. Containing one quarter of its original members, the others of which have either moved on, been forgotten about, or like one troubled individual has been struggling to reclaim what that stable life stole from him] JD: …It’s back. [An exasperated sigh escapes his half opened mouth, almost appearing as a wheeze. Dunn recalls the torment, the misguidedness that he now can see when he reviews those past months under the heavy flag of the curtain. No stable survives after stable wars. No matter what they try, it is as inevitable as the end of the universe itself. Nothing lasts forever; the good die young and the evil keep on living. Like the phoenix, dreams of rebirth are just that, dreams. Past glory cannot be re-attained by rash inductions of new blood. Numbers never outweighs experience] JD: Just another thing to worry about. That’s the last thing I need. Why did it come back? Why must it haunt me? [Still not looking at the camera, Dunn seems to just be sorting out the junk in his head verbally. In fact, seeing as he is speaking so softly, perhaps he doesn’t want it to be heard at all. Weakness isn’t something you should shout about, unless it’s a ploy] JD: Maybe I should show them what it’s like to be locked out of your past. Taken away from everything you know. I cannot remember my family, am I married? I don’t know, everything that made me, me have been locked away, just so they could gain a quick advantage in the numbers game. I shall not forget what they did to me, they shall get their fair share of payback in due time. [His fist clenches and starts to rise up in anger. Dunn reaches to it with his other hand and lowers it, taking a soothing breath of air to calm his frustration] JD: OK, it’s fine [Another soothing breath] JD: Just remember, you’re better than they are, eventually you’ll regain yourself. Until then, just remember… Remember what they did, and wait, your in the perfect position for vengeance, all you need is the timing. Strike the blow with the crowd on your side; you have almost everything in place. [He lifts up the teacup again and takes an elongated mouthful, taking doubly long to let the caffeine dribble down his gullet. Seeming more composed now than he did a few moments ago, he sets down the cup in the saucer and goes to pick up the paper again. His eyes fall on the listing of the card for SAS. Reading down through the list his eyes fix on the World Title Match] JD: Not the Main event? Monty wont be too pleased with that. But with a Player’s Club member in the main event he wont be too sore about it. He better make sure he’s ready for Digi though. I may not like the man, but he knows how to wrestle, I can’t hold that against him. [Skimming through the next section of the listings he finds something that catches his eye] JD: The Who? Hmm, perhaps a bit of research on my part wouldn’t go amiss. If they’re as seasoned as this says, I might have my work cut out for me. Formorians… What an odd name, I wonder what it means… [He ponders over it for a moment, stroking his chin before shrugging it off and returning to the newspaper] JD: Lustful Lullaby. Hah. Bondage really doesn’t know what he’s doing in a team. His teammate might make a half decent wrestler if she didn’t have sex on the brain all the time. Well one mans trash is another mans treasure. It’ll take half the equation of the match away. [He looks on quite oddly at the paper, almost not understanding what’s going on] JD: Well, that I wasn’t expecting. Nick Persia? Why Nick Persia? I thought I’d have been teamed with Starr. Then again management never knew what’s going on, on the ground floor of the business. So Nick Persia it is. He reminds me of myself all those years ago. Here, alone, in a strange country, not like his native European nation. Everything to prove and nothing to lose. I’ve a feeling I’m going to like the Dutchman. [His face breaks out in a broad smile, he lifts up the cup to drink again and the scene fades out as he turns the page to see the football scores of last nights local game] [The Scene Fades to Black]
