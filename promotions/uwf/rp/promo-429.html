UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #429

Time to pull my weight

29 May 2004

Joshua Dunn — Franks All Night Gym

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cheering. Everyone loves to here it, the sound of people wiling you to go on and conquer over all odds. It’s the sound revered from Roman times, which can make or break a champions career. The thing that the Resurrection has to remember is that the good guys always win. No matter what the odds. Some things never change] Perhaps this is what drives the force behind Joshua Dunn. Now he has found his way to a place he has never seen since the days before the first Iron Curtain. He has found the way into the hearts of millions. Every household in America, and across some other countries as well, tune in on their TV sets to see the Communist Scotsman take on impossible odds, just to give the people what they want. A champion people can believe in, one that can rise up and take command of the helm, lead what and entire federation stands for to the brink knowing full well that it could make or break it, for him, his stable mates and of course, the people at home and in the stands. Stable Wars has escalated beyond mere factions now. Now it seems that a war of giants has fallen to the mortals’ squared circle. A conflict reminiscing back to the time of the Second World War. The stage is set for two powers to battle it out, one fighting for a new world order, while the other battles feverishly to try and keep things as they have been and hopefully ever will be. The People do not like change. Be it technophobes or counter revolutionaries, someone will try to stem the tide of change for better or worse. This time the fight is all or nothing, come stable wars, the UWF will be saved or face the Revolution. And stopping the revolution? A band of those that swore to give you the best damned entertainment around. Mike Montgomery, founder of the Players Club, Commissioner of the UWF and World Title Holder. Ezra Kazee, former IC Champion, sportsman and gentleman, his morals are at an impeccably high standard. Dutch Wrestler extraordinaire, and the new comer to make the biggest splash in the UWF Nick Persia. Kevin Starr, the last inductee to the Stable, Cocky, arrogant, and pig headed. Unlikely to be somewhat of a people’s hero, but then again he is one of the people notorious for fantastic wrestling matches that make the UWF what it is. Then of course, it is Joshua Dunn, notorious Giant killer and showman. His past misdemeanours are enough to doubt his alignment, but over 3 million weekly viewers cannot be wrong in their judgement can they? *** The Wind Howls. It’s a wet and dreary night as rain files down from the heavens in nice orderly companies of water droplets, each one making it on of those nights it might be better to be at home curled up in front of the telly with someone you love. Then again one person doesn’t have a luxury of any near by loved ones. Being away from his family, in a new land that he has barely scratched the surface of because of his work. Lightning crashes around, lighting up the dingy surroundings for a brief minute or two, showing glimpses of what we are here for. One flash and accompanying thunder crack reveals a street with inadequate lighting, buildings built an age ago making this look more of the 1920’s period rather than the modern day USA. Another followed swiftly by yet another, the thunder rolling along the clouds enough to make people sit up and take notice. Illuminated in the second flash is, what seems to be, a warehouse door way with the sign that reads, “Frank’s All Night Gym”. Thanks to the modern day media techniques, superseded over the noise of the storm is the sound of exertion, followed by the rhythmic beating of a padded bag suspended from the ceiling with a chain. On the third flash we find ourselves inside the building, looking down on the scene, it seems the outside isn’t the only period piece of architecture. The poor lighting is give by a series of bulbs suspended from three feet of electrical wire with a lampshade covered in period dust to boot. This place looks as if it hasn’t changed much since the time it was actually used as a warehouse. Although more recent training equipment is scattered throughout the large open plan building, including a wrestling ring that doesn’t look like it has been used since being take out of the box. Not surprising that the place seems as derelict as you would expect it to be on a night like this, the rain bounces of the roof and hits the windows which are far too high up to actually let people see out from, or even let proper light in. There is however one person in view at this time, and finally we can place the origin of the sound we heard outside the building. Standing alone, pounding away at a punch bag suspended from a suspended beam hung from the roof by a pair of heave duty chains. Sweat glistens on his upper body, in another flash of lightning, the sweat stain down the front of his vest can be clearly seen before the room is plunged back into artificial twilight. The man throws more punches at the punch bag, managing to make it swing on its chain each and every time it’s hit. Of course we can immediately identify the training man as the Communist Scotsman, Joshua Dunn. What is going through his mind at the moment? It’s a valid question, one several people have asked, even after leaving the Curtain, he hasn’t managed to shake the communist persona forced upon him. Then again, seeing as he won his first major title after joining the band of communists, who would want to drop something that has given him so much, for only taking a little of his personality away from him. Perhaps not his choice at all. Still dangling on the chain around his neck is the little golden emblem of the Hammer and Sickle. A contradiction in terms, a capitalistic item, displaying the communists system for equality among men. The irony of the item lost on Dunn, at least for now as he continues to lay into the punch bag, starting to pant a bit, but still relentlessly soldiering on, not stopping till he’s satisfied, and right now, who would be satisfied? The biggest fight in his career yet, excluding his fight for the World title. Stable Wars, yet again he gets the chance to carve his name into its gold plating. Soon, but not quite yet, there’s one last stop for this freight train before it’s final destination. Wednesday Night Underground Behind him a figure appears, his more aged features lost in the gloom. Evident only briefly when the lightning brightens the surroundings. He approaches slowly carrying a towel and a plastic bottle filled with water. His approach is met with the twitch of Dunn’s ear, but he continues on regardless. The man stands behind him for a while just watching him, and Dunn watching him from the corner of his eye, refusing to stop punishing the bag, even though he’s starting to tire, as if the man was draining his energy just by observing him. Not quite sure how to approach the loose cannon, he simply reaches out with his free arm and clasping Dunn’s shoulder. Dunn spins round brandishing a fist that otherwise would have landed on the punch bag. The man soon backs back Man: Hey now, put that away Dunn only stands completely still for a while, just as he stopped, hand raised and everything, taking a few breaths to regain composure as well as his breath. The built up fire and resentment saps away in those few moments. He takes one last intake of the musky air before lowering his fist and sitting down cross-legged on the safety mat. The man also takes a breath, but one of relief. He tosses Dunn the towel, shortly after tossing him the bottle too and he sits down on the near by bench press. Man: You ok? You look like your exhausted Dunn pants for a while, taking in great gulps of the water before looking up to the man. A convenient lightning strike illuminates his tired features for a time. Dunn: I’m fine Frank, you worry too much The newly named Frank gives him a knowing look, one that speaks volumes, one shouting that he has known several other people say exactly the same thing and be completely wrong, you can never worry enough. Frank: You know you’ve been here since eight and it’s now… He slides up his shirtsleeve to reveal that he didn’t put his watch on this morning Frank: … Late This lets something just as foreign as Crowd cheering for him pass over his face, a smile. Granted the mouth only turned up at the corners, but beggars cannot be choosers. Dunn: Your sign does say all night Gym though. Frank: Your point being? Dunn: I’m going to be here for some time yet This time it’s Frank that lets out a weak smile. Dunn uses this brief silence to take another healthy swig from the water bottle. Perhaps it is time to take a breather after all. Since when has a little rest hurt anyone? Frank: I’ve seen your type around here before, avid that there’s always room for improvement. You should take it a little easier that’s all I’m saying Dunn rubs his forehead with the palm of his hand, wiping the sweat off and flinging it to the dusty floor. He then props up his head with that hand. Maybe I do need a rest. There’s always tomorrow I suppose. Dunn: I know my limits Frank: I know that, but rest is just as good for you as a work out is. Dunn gives Frank a dismissive look Dunn: I know that, you’re talking to me like I’m an amateur Frank: Is there something bothering you? I’ve seen several people come in here and try to fight out their anxiety like that. Joshua gets up and sits down on another bench press opposite Frank. His body suddenly feeling ten times heavier, he slumps down on the bench almost like a drunkard and slumps his shoulders forward to balance himself, holding his head up to face Frank. A couple of beads of sweat drip off his face and hit the floor, now not heated by the warmth of motion, he starts to feel very cold, he drapes the towel around his exposed shoulders and looks towards frank again. Frank: I have been keeping up with the UWF you know, I’m perfectly aware about the situation leading up to stable wars. Your worried about the Resurrection aren’t you? Joshua looks confused for a moment, not entirely sure he just heard what Frank just said. Perhaps it would be best if I just went home Dunn: Hah. I don’t think so. They don’t even know who the leader of the Players Club is. There is a reason that Monty refers to himself as the founder of TPC and not the leader. Frank: Then what is bothering you? Joshua grins again before dropping his head, shaking it back and forth Dunn: You know I’m glad I was part of a stable where each member pulled its weight. No one riding the coat tails to success Frank: Was? You still are aren’t you? Dunn: Technically, except we now have a coat rider in our midst, all you have to do is look at Kevin Starr to see that he wants an easy journey to the top. A smile passes over his face, another sorrowful one. He’s the weak link here, I know it. We’re dying before we even reach our prime Dunn: Someone has to pull their weight, and he won’t if he can help it. A small silence emerges; the patter of the rain on the windows becomes almost deafening. Another lightning flash floods the place with light for a spell, before fading back to the murky light of the warehouse Frank: So you want me to get the bench weights then? Joshua looks at him with the eyes that a boy would view his father with. All I want to do is go home. Dunn: That would be a help thanks. Frank gets up and leaves Dunn alone to lie out on the bench press and try to catch a few precious moments of rest before setting the training bar up a notch. I hope all this is worth it
