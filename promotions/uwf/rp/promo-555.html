UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #555

Turning What

16 Jul 2004

Mr. Meaner — A random Alley

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a dark alley. There are building on either side of it, duh. How many of you reading this would be surprised if these were tall brick building even. Anyway, there is a dumpster on one side, and trashcans on the other side. A rather typical alley. There is a man who is lying at the end of the alley looking rather ragged. All of a suddenly a dark figure is seen walking into the alley. He is wearing all black, and a mask. If you couldn’t guess, I’ll let you know that this is Mr. Meaner. He is walking with a purpose into the alley. He sees the bum at the end of the alley.) MM: Hey jack@$$!! (The bum continues to sleep.) Wake up you idiot. (still sleeping. Mr. Meaner walks up to the bum and kicks him straight away right in the gut. The bum lets out a gasp of air and wakes up with a startle.) Bum: Hey, what was that for? MM: I’m trying to do something in here. I suggest you leave now. Bum: And if I don’t? MM: Well, if you don’t want to leave, I guess I really don’t have any choices than, do I. Bum: That’s right mister. Now I’m going to lay right here. (Mr. Meaner turns around. You can almost sense that he is smiling underneath the mask as he pulls a lead pipe out of his pants. He swiftly turns around and nails the bum in the gut with it.) MM: I said I didn’t have a choice. That doesn’t mean you get to lay here, that means I get to kick you’re @$$. (The bum starts trying to crawl away. He looks terrified as Mr. Meaner starts to stalk him. Meaner finally decides that he is done playing and grabs him by the back of his neck and lifts him up.) MM: Do you even know who I am? Bum: Not a clue. MM: That’s probably a good thing. I wouldn’t want you squealing to anyone. (With that, Meaner picks him up and decides to use his head as a battering ram, smashing it into one of the buildings. He laugh as the bum’s body goes limp. He then turns around, and runs full speed into the other building. He then carries him over to the dumpster and unceremoniously dumps him into it.) MM: Well now that that is taken care of, I have this little matter of my match on Sunday, don’t I. I have some jerk off named Angel who seems to think that he is some do-gooder who is going to do anything he wants. He talks about how he made me Ezra Kazee. How he made me denounce my evil and become good. What a freak this guy is. How I could ever be as nice as that freaking pansy Kazee I have no idea. But you go ahead and claim it all you want…….. You say your going to turn me. Well, I don’t know exactly what you are going to try to do to me, but turning me definitely sounds like it isn’t anything I want to be involved in. And turn, you imbecile is such an ambiguous term as it is anyway. What kind of turning are you planning to do? Let’s see, according to Dungeons and Dragons Players handbook, which seems to fit you more than anything being that your trying to be some sort of paladin or some crap like that, turning is a special ability that clerics have that will either destroy undead or make them run away. Well, you may be some sort of a cleric, but I’m sure as hell ain’t undead. I haven’t the taste for human flesh and I can’t pass through solid objects. Although, that last ability would be quite helpful sometimes….. Let’s see, you could try and make me change directions. I honestly don’t know why you’d want me to do that though. Why would you care if I went left or right, up or down. Maybe that’s not the kind of turning your thinking of either…... Unless you are planning on turning me around because we sure as hell know that that b---h Ayami ain’t putting out for you. Although, maybe she would for a real man like myself. You are quite fruity, if you know what I mean Angel, and to be around a hot slut like that for this long and not have done anything to her really makes me wonder. I tell you what, if you need help in knowing what to do to that c--t, I’ll rent you a video or two, that oughtta help……. Oh, wait, I got it. You want me to turn good. Is that what it is. You want to turn my philosophies and beliefs so that I will join you and fight for good. Well, that’s not going to happen. I’ve got better things to do. You save your preaching and your moaning for someone else, cause I don’t want to hear it. I’m going to go on doing what I do best. Hurting people. And making sure that I’m hurting them to the point where it just doesn’t matter what they think anymore, cause they aren’t going to get back into the ring…… And that is exactly what I’m going to do to you Angel. I’m going to beat you and beat you and beat you til you can’t take anymore, and then I’m going to beat you some more. You think that you had it tough before. Drinking and doing drugs, and all that stuff, you ain’t had anything bad you piece of crap. This Sunday, you are going to feel what it means to be hurt. (A honking is heard.) MM: Now you’ll have to excuse me you peon. I have other engagements to attend to than sitting here and talking to the morons in the audience. I actually have work to do. (Meaner walks past the camera and into a limo that has pulled up and is waiting for him. The camera then fades to black.)
