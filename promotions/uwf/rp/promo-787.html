UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #787

ARRIBA~!

24 Nov 2004

Trailer Park Luchador — The Trailer Parks of Wisconsin

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“I am the Trailer Park Luchador~!” A flash of light goes by the screen. “ARRIBA~!” With a crash, the Trailer Park Luchador corkscrews off the top of his mobile home, slamming into the bed left out on the ground with a cry and a puff of dust. On the ground, the head Luchador of the white trash shakes his head from side to side, clearing the cobwebs. “Aye yay yay. How was that?” The camera swivels sideways, focusing in on the giant green monstrosity that stands near a wooden fence, silently picking at his humongous teeth with a sawed off baseball bat. For he is El Turtlé … scourge of the jobber underworld, possessor of the biggest mouth in professional wrestling, and the mutant ninja master of … !~THE MASKED MONKEYS~! Anywho, El Turtlé just shakes his head at him and gives a thumbs down. El Turtlé: “Dude … you suck.” The Trailer Park Luchador, master of his domain or not, can only hang his head in shame. TPL: “Dude … seriously?” El Turtlé: “Seriously.” TPL: “Weak, man.” El Turtlé: “Cowabunga.” TPL: “Man! I’ve got to go up against Hot Mask on Wednesday Night Underground … dude! Television Title match! After the other Monkeys lost the Tag Titles, I could … I could bring back the belts! My first title reign! DUDE!” El Turtlé: “True. But you know what?” TPL: “What?” El Turtlé: “You still suck.” TPL: “Harsh, dude.” El Turtlé: “Dude … I know. But you know what’s really harsh?” TPL: “What?” El Turtlé: “I still haven’t crapped out White Wolf.” TPL: “Man, I so didn’t need to know that.” El Turtlé: “It’s … oh man. You couldn’t begin to …” TPL: “LALALALALALALA NOT LISTENING LALALALALALA.” El Turtlé: “Man. This promo’s weak, Lucha man.” TPL: “I know.” El Turtlé: “Why are we even taping it?” TPL: “We didn’t have any other idea what to do.” El Turtlé: “So what are you going to do for WNU?” TPL: “Cheat?” El Turtlé: “Dude! NO! Masked Monkeys can’t cheat!” TPL: “Damnit. I know.” El Turtlé: “COWABUNGA~!” TPL: “Alright … it’s time to break out the Super Duper Twisty Luchador Flashback From Hell Thingie! That’ll win the Television Title for sure!” El Turtlé: “What’s that?” TPL: “I’m not sure yet.” El Turtlé: “Dude.” TPL: “I know. Shut off the camera so we can make something up.” El Turtlé: “You think they’re going to realize we just did this because Hot Mask didn’t promo yet?” TPL: “Not until you said that, idiot.” El Turtlé: “Sorry. White Wolf just keeps kicking, and it’s …” TPL: “LALALALALALALA.” El Turtlé: “Alright, alright, I’ll turn off the camera. COWABUNGA!” And the camera cuts out. Because that’s what Hot Mask gets for not RPing.
