Old Ties

Sept 07/04 (year unrecorded)

Roy Lewis — Las Vegas Hotel

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was the time he proposed to her, under a tree in the Japanese hills. At least that’s what he wished it could have been. In reality, cruel reality, it was in some cheesy Asian restaurant in downtown Toronto. He remembered so clearly, she didn’t want to go. She wanted to stay home and cook chicken. He stopped thinking about it, other memories were surfacing. The blinding flash, the burning heat, the smell of gasoline mixed with charred flesh. Roy was snapped awake, he didn’t want to think about it but he did anyway. She wasn’t the only one to parish in that explosion. So did her sister (who was in the car with her) and 5 bystanders. An entire family out on a walk, three of them children. He swore he’d find the fucker who did it but he never did. After the funeral he turned in his letter of resignation and secluded himself. He didn’t know how but he knew that he was supposed to be that car with her; he should by all rights be dead. But you not dead are you, that’s what matters. He had fallen asleep on a chair, in his hotel room. He had a half-empty bottle of Smirnoff clutched to his chest, and he had left the T.V. on. He had been watching a hockey game but now it was showing an infomercial for a hair growth formula. He looked at his watch, 4:00, had only been asleep for a few hours. He decided he may as well get up, since he wasn’t going to get any more sleep tonight, He tried to stand and suddenly a he felt a sharp pain in his temples. “Fucking Hangover” He muttered as he threw the bottle of vodka against the closest wall. It shattered into pieces and he swore to himself for being so stupid. No matter, UWF had paid for the room and would pay for that too. You could end the pain, just point it to head and pull the trigger. He had the gun under his bed, just in case, and he could end it all right now. Then he decided no, he still had shit to do. He was moving to shut off the T.V. when there was a bang on the door. He looked up and watched the door rock with a second bang as it was hit. “Hold on a sec” He called, as he hit the switch on the T.V. He walked over to the door and reached for the lock, but then stopped as a thought crossed his mind. He had many enemies that did not where he lived, but he was on national television the other night. Could some-one have seen that and tracked him down to this hotel? Unlikely but he wasn’t going to take any chances. He ran back to his room, reached under his bed, and produced the 9mm. He kept it loaded, and checked it to make sure he had a round chambered. He flicked the safety off and went back to the door. He held the gun tight, with his finger on the trigger he called out to the stranger on the other side of the four inches of wood. “Who is it?” There was a slight silence broken only by a gruff ‘Ha’. “Open the door you little Dickhead I’m freezing my balls off out here” Roy grinned to himself, that’s a voice he hadn’t heard in a long time, yet one he was glad still to. He flicked the safety back on the gun, shoved it into the waistband of his pants, and unlocked the door. He opened it, muttered a hello, and nodded at the old man standing in the hall. He was shivering as he walked past Roy into the room, not responding to the greeting. He looked around the hotel room, focusing on the shards of glass in the corner with the word Smirnoff on one of the larger pieces, and shook his head. Pierce Campbell, his uncle’s best friend and the man who trained Roy, looked disgusted, and from him that was usually considered a compliment. “So this is where they stick you boys now a-days, huh?” “Yeah” Roy responded as he shut the door, walked back into the bedroom and stuck the gun under the bed. “Jesus Christ” Pierce said and shook his head “You loss a match and you get but up in first class, I got to see what kind of idiots run this fed.” “Saw that did ya? First of all, the guys that run this league to their job pretty fucking well. And second I didn’t lose…” He dropped back into the chair. “I drew” “Just as bad, you looked terrible; you could have mopped the floor with that guy if you had fought smart.” “Unless you didn’t notice, I’ve looked like shit the last two years.” “Ah, yes” He responding while looking out the window and staring into the Vegas skyline. “The ‘accident’, terrible shame that was.” He had no emotion in his voice, and Roy would have ignored it if Pierce had kept his mouth shut. “Just as well, she was making you weak.” Roy was on his feet in a second, and in another he had Pierce by the throat and pushed up against the wall. “YOU EVER SAY ANYTHING ABOUT HER AGAIN I COLLECT YOUR SKIN, YOU GET ME OLD MAN?” He said nothing but just stared into Roy’s eyes which were filled with hate. Roy did not let his grip loosen until a bang on the two snapped him back to attention. “WHAT!” He shouted, his voice quivering with anger. He didn’t have time to deal with this right now. There was no response, except a second bang. “Give me a fucking second!” As he shouted the words the aggressive knocking had stopped and now some-one was punching the door. The four inches of wood lasted about four seconds before, it burst into splinters and flew inward. “Jesus Christ!” Roy shouted as he ducked and a chunk of door barely missed him, before hitting the wall beside Pierce. Roy looked up to see a dark figure standing in the doorway. He was about to dive into the bedroom, swearing to himself for putting the gun away when the figure stepped into view. “Holy shit, you fuck. Ever hear of patience?” Roy said as SKuLL walked into the hotel room. “This fucking hotel is going to have my head.” “I’m not here to chat about fucking hotels, Lewis. I’m gonna make this quick” SKuLL stood face to face with Roy, and being a few inches taller had to look at him. “You and I have a Match, on WNU, Tag to be exact” Roy sighed in relieve. “That it? Fuck I thought you finally snapped for a second there.” He began laughing but stopped when he realized SKuLL wasn’t smiling. “What?” “Listen to me; you little fuck, if it wasn’t for the fact that I have a match with you. I’d strike you down where you fucking stand. Lucky for you though I’m not that mean of a guy…sometimes. And since we have a history, but I want to make sure you understand me, I don’t need your help. So just stay out of my way.” “I was wrong you have fucking snapped, I’ll see you on WNU, now get the fuck out of my hotel room.” SKuLL turned and began to walk away. “Oh…” Roy said with a grin “Your paying for that door” SKuLL stopped in the doorway and shot a glance over his shoulder, before walking away. Pierce, who had been standing by watching this finally managed to find the words he was looking for. “I never liked that guy.”
