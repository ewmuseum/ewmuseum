Fw: Can you send this please?

7 Nov 2001

an in-character “Pro Wrestling Illustrated” dirt-sheet report, bylined Bill Haugland

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Living Highlight. Those words were last seen several months ago, as Johnny Diamondz had decimated the UcW owner, Pure Playa, to finally be released from his contract, and ended a year old rivalry between the two. No one has either seen or heard from him, but the rumors began to spread. He was rumored to be involved in PCW's Diamond in the Rough tournament, but no one was leaking any information about that. Any organization will love to have Jaydee as an asset to their company. In such a short career, he had amassed so much out of the wrestling industry, but he still failed to make it to the big one. The plans for that will be changed if the latest rumor is true. There is a lot of speculation that Jaydee had already signed a contract with the UWF. Apparently, nobody's talking in the front office, but several UWF mainstays are to beleive to be very angry about this, for Johnny's ego is uncontrollable, has been known to create problems in the back, and has very little respect for authority. But, this will promote both Johnny D. and the UWF, being it was almost 6 months that anybody has heard from "The Living Highlight". When more of this shocking revelation becomes available, we will pass the information to you, the fans, but until then, this is Bill Haugland for Pro Wrestling Illustrated
