UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #635

The Answers

16 Aug 2004

Ezra Kazee — UWF Shareholders Branch Office.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens inside of the UWF Shareholders Branch Office. Sitting at the desk is of course “The Common Man” Ezra Kazee. He is wearing a green shirt with a black hat. The room is fairly bland as isn’t normally the case. This time there is only a lamp and a phone and some other stuff you’d usually expect to find in an office building. Kazee looks directly into the camera.) Kazee: Well, nice to see you joined the party their Monty. Although I don’t know why the UWF decided to show all three of your promos almost all in a row, but whatever works I guess. The UWF has very rarely been known for making a ton of smart decisions, but hey, it’s the fed I love…… But it seems that everyone is confused as to who I am exactly. Am I the ultimate sportsman? Am I the person who wants everything to be in my favor? Am I the cheap shot artist who attacks champions from behind just to get a title shot? Or am I the guy who will sue the UWF just to get my way…… Yes, I am the ultimate sportsman. I decided to start fighting fire with fire, your right. I have done some things I shouldn’t have done lately. Like trying to help Persia win his match, or trying to help Montgomery win the world title. Because everyone else seems to be interfering so I figured since everyone else is already interfering in my matches just to get themselves over, it was time that I start doing the same thing…… Yes, I am the person who wants everything to be in my favor. I mean, don’t we all want everything to be in our own favor? Don’t we want everything to go our way, don’t we want everything to give us the advantage and always make things better for us in the long run. It is part of human nature after all…….. Yes, I am the guy who attacks a champion from behind to get my title shot. I think it sent a pretty clear message to everyone that I was no longer part of the Players Club. I think it was a pretty clear message that I was displeased with the champion’s actions as of late. And I think it was a pretty clear way of me making sure that I don’t break rule 13.1 or whatever it may be which says that two people in the same stable can not go after the same title,,,,,,, Yes, I’m the guy who will sue to get my way. I mean, after all, sometimes things have to be taken to measures of a higher level in order to make ones point. And there had been so much rule breaking and contract breeching going on that the UWF wouldn’t enforce that something needed to be done, and I did it. So you want to get pissed at me for it, so be it. Just make sure you are getting pissed at the right people. This isn’t about me, this is about the fans and making sure they aren’t cheated out of anything…… The Players Club, as Montgomery has said, is dead. It had been a fun run. It truly had been. We were the best out there. And we proved it night after night after night. And every fan out there knew that the fans were coming to see us. The fans were paying their hard earned money to see The Players Club because we were the premiere wrestlers in the UWF…… But it is done now. It is dead. And whose fault is that? I can only pick out two individuals for who have destroyed the Players Club. I can only pick out two individuals who have lead to the destruction of it. And it sure as hell ain’t Monty. It ain’t Persia, and it ain’t me. I can only point to two people. Joshua Dunn and Kevin Starr. You see, I wouldn’t have done what I had done if it weren’t for your actions. I wouldn’t have done what I had done if it weren’t for your absolute disregard for authority and decided to change the rulebook on your own accord……. A lot of things I can overlook. Like a foreign object here or there, or interference, but when you go that far and change the rulebook, then you have gone too far in my opinion. You have taken things to a level they don’t need to go. Your complete disregard for the rules and the rulebook of the UWF has lead to the downfall of the Players Club and of the premiere stable in the UWF……. See, Monty is also only partially right when he declares why I joined the Players Club. Yeah, I did do it partially for career advancement. I admit it; I was toiling around in the depths of the abyss. But the bigger reason I joined The Players Club is because I needed someone to watch my back. I needed a group to help me and make sure that I was treated fair, and that if someone did try to jump me from behind, I had someone to help me out. And it worked great for a while…… But those other areas that Monty claims were fouls that I didn’t call, well, it’s a nice spin he puts on things, isn’t it. But let’s be honest here. Before everyone forgets what was happening, Mike Montgomery was the UWF Commissioner at the time, and therefore, according to the rulebook, at least the section that Dunn and Starr didn’t unilaterally decide to change, it says that he has the authority to make and change matches as he sees fit. It also must be remembered that the fans were not being hurt in these instances either. For there is that disclaimer of the card being subject to change……. The Clash which you changed in order to put me and you on the same side in the tag team championship match. You said it yourself Mike, you were the commissioner. You were the person who could make those changes. And thus you did so. So you expect me to cry foul when someone was actually following the rulebook? Sometimes in the UWF people actually follow the rule book. I know, it’s amazing, but sometimes it actually happens…… Of course then you say something about the tournament that I won for the IC Title. Well, once again, it’s true that you were the leaders of the Players Club, but you continue to forget to mention that you were also the UWF Commissioner at the time. Maybe this will help remind you….. (Kazee holds up a packet of paper. The top of the paper states “Contract for UWF Intercontinental Championship Tournament.” Kazee flips through the pages of the document to the last page were all the wrestlers signatures are. Kazee then points out one signature in particular. The camera zooms in and focuses. The signature is that of Mike Montgomery’s right on the line of the UWF Commissioner.) How quickly you forget. How quickly you try and spin things Monty. Next time, maybe you should tell the whole truth instead of just part of it. It works so much better. If you ever want to get the respect you so deserve then maybe you should try it. It works a lot better, especially if you are going to hold the position you do.….. (Kazee places the packet of paper down.) Of course I haven’t forgotten about you Joshua. Your right, you have gone the extra distance in your matches. You have been able to go through hell and back to come back and win, and you have made a career at humiliating people at their own game. Unfortunately, for you, in this match, you can not humiliate anyone at their own game. See, I purposely made the stipulation the way it was. It really doesn’t favor anyone, nor does it really disfavor anyone…….. At least three of us have proven the ability to use submission moves competently. At least three of us have used submission moves to our advantage. There is a cell involved, so there will be no interference. And there are two referees, which makes absolutely certain that not one submission will be missed…… But let’s talk about you going the distance, shall we? I too know what it means to go the distance. You do forget that I am a former amateur wrestling champion. You forget that I have competed at high levels and in very grueling situations. I mean, maybe we just need to take a look at the differences here, shall we? Amateur wrestling has three periods, 3 minutes, 2 minutes, and 2 minutes. There is absolutely no rest in between those periods, and if you rest even once during the match, you might as well consider yourself pinned. It is a game of endurance, skill and reaction that doesn’t allow for rest periods or rest moves….. Professional Wrestling has matches with basically no time limit and no periods. It is a game of endurance, skill, reaction, and pain. But the difference between the two is that there are moves that are designed for nothing but rest. There are moves that allow the aggressor to rest while putting all of his weight on his opponent. The ability for one to rest in a professional wrestling match is much greater than in an amateur wrestling match….. There are a lot of similarities, but there are a lot of differences too. Which one is tougher, I don’t know, nor may anyone ever know, but the fact of it is that they both have their advantages and disadvantages. The winner can be determined in a heart beat, a matter of only a few seconds. Or in the case of this match, the winner will be decided in one hour of submissions. This match will truly prove the better man…… In the end, who will be the better man? Who knows? You are all great competitors. You are all people who I would love to step in the ring with as an ally or foe on any given day. It does sadden me the circumstances that have brought the match about. I thought that Dunn and Starr were better than this, but so be it, it is still a great honor and one I plan to live to my fullest. And in the end, the winner will have truly deserved the honor. And in the end, that man can not only declare himself as the undisputed UWF World Champion, but also the master of submission wrestling….. Good luck to y’all. We are all going to need it in this match. (Scene fades to black.)
