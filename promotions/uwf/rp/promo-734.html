UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #734

Superstar

4 Oct 2004

Tsunami Oni — San Francisco

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tsunami Oni has been all over this world. Japan. Mexico. The White House. With his fortune and connections, he could be anyplace on the eve of UWF's biggest event of the year... Live and Loud. Oni is, after all, in the main event. A chance to for the biggest brass ring of them all... the World championship. Atitle that the Vivaldi of Violence has held before. And like his first shot, Oni earned it with blood and sweat. First, he won the Contest of Champions to get his chance for the big dance. This time it was beating every other man to win the Regal Rumble. So one day before Live and Loud, why do we find Tsunami Oni sitting alone in a dingy bar on karaoke night? The other patrons give him the occasional glance and whisper amongst themselves. This is a star in their midst, an icon. The've seen him on TV, doing things thought impossible by anyone not named Peter Parker. Yet here he is, not looking like the legend he is. Instead, he appears... sad. A strange way to be for a money with the money, the fame, the main event on a show that will be seen by millions. But that's because they don't really know Tsunami Oni. Of course, Tsunami Oni loves the fame and the glory. That's why he got into this business in the first place... to hear the awe of the crowds as he flew through the air. But reality has made him crash to the earth. Mike Montgomery has the most importmant thing in the world to him. No, it's not the World championship belt. It's Shiatsu. Shiatsu. Some call her his valet. Or his manager. But the truth of it is, Shiatsu is his partner. She is as important to the success of Tsunami Oni as blue mist or dragonranas. And just because of frying pans to the skull or distracting opponents with a few well-timed wiggles. Shiatsu kept Oni focused, on target to success. She was the one that would always be honest with him. She was by his side from the very beginning. Except now, she isn't. She left him. She left him for Mike Montgomery, of all people. But Tsunami Oni doesn't blame either of them, he blames himself. He knows why she left. He exposed her to danger night after night. He forced her to tag along with all his ludicrous schemes. It was all about Oni, now Oni is all there is. Alone. Just like he is alone in this bar. The woman onstage finishes her song. Slowly, Tsunami Oni gets out of his seat and walks to the stage. Seeing the man considered the best mic worker in the business so apprehensive about being on a microphone seems bizarre. Oni doesn't acknowledge the polite applause or even take his eyes from the floor. He doesn't even make a sound until the music starts. Tsunami Oni walks off the stage before the music ends. He pays no attention the the other patrons of the bar, just keeps walking until he's out the door. The people murmur about what they've seen, but none can understand it. Tsunami Oni gets into his Bentley and drives off into the San Francisco night. He has no destination, just keeps driving. The street glistens with water from a light evening rain. The streetlights stand like sentries against the darkness. Oni notices nothing, he just keeps driving. He doesn't notice that he's crossed into Oakland. The only thing he does notice is that he's gotten hungry. While in a not-too-pleasant neighborhood, he spots a McDonald's. Oni pulls his car into the drive-thru. As he waits, Tsunami Oni stares blankly out his window. He sees a young man taking the trash out to the dumpster. A pair of rough character step out from behind the dumpster. Oni sees words are being exchanged, then one of the thugs shoves the McDonald's worker. The other thug tags him with a chap shot. Both are laying into the young man brutally. Tsunami Oniknows it isn't his business. He know he shouldn't get involved. But right now, he just feels like hitting something. Steeping out of his Bentley, Oni walks quickly to the scene. Wasting no time, he punches one thug in the back of the neck, then follows with a knee to the temple. As he falls, his accomplice takes a swing at Oni. The Vivaldi of Violence catches his wrist, then grabs his elbow. A quick application of pressure and there's the sick sound of a bone sanpping. Cradling his arm, the bawling thug runs off. His pal stumbls to his feet, then takes off in the same direction. Tsunai oni helps the bloodied young man up. Young man: Thanks, man. I thought I was... Holy! You... you're Tsunami Oni! Oh man! You're my idol! Oni: Well, there's no accounting for taste, but I'm flattered. Young man; Dude, I'm serious! I dig all the Japenese guy, but you're the bomb! That's how you took those mofos out! Oni: What was the deal here, anyways? Young man: My pops owns this McDonald's. He would always kick the hood like those two out and wouldn't let them near the place. I guess they didn't like that much, 'cause he got shot during a robbery. Oni: Damn. Sorry to hear that. Young man: I t didn't kill kim, but he's pretty tore up. Plus he's got alot of bills. I dropped out of college to help out, just like I did in high school. With my pops gone, the gang-bangers think they can run the place over. I try to do my part, but you saw I ain't doing so hot. Oni: You dropped out of college for this? Young man: Yeah, I had to take care of the fam, ya know? Gotta remeber what's important. Oni: It seems pretty hopeless. Young man: Maybe, but ya gotta fight for what ya care about. The words resonate within Tsunami Oni. Here's a guy risking life and limb for the people he cares abou. And what about Oni? He mopes around for weeks and sings sappy songs. The Devil from the Deep Blue Sea gives the young man a smile. Oni: Tell you what. I'll call some... associates of my father's. They excel at persuasion. Young man: Lawyers? Oni: Yakuza. Young man: Man, I don';t wanna trade one bunch of thugs for another. No offense. Oni: Don't worry. They'll just take care of your unwmanted visitors, the n disappear. But don't worry, if any of these schmucks show their faces again, they's be... reprimanded. Young man: Thans, bro! Oni: And I'll tell you what. I'll help train you to defend yourself. Young man: No way! Oni: I can't be coming back here. Bad for the digestion. Young man. Man, there's no way I can repay you. Oni: Actually, you already did. Young man: Huh? Oni: Never mind. What's your name, anyways. Young man: Michael Donaldson. But everybody calls me Mickey D. Oni: All right, Mickey. This is my card. See me later this week, all right? Mickey D: A'ight! And thanks again! Tsunami Oni heads back to his Bentley. Deciding that fast food maybe isn't thebest choice, he backs out and starts heading back to San Francisco and home. As he drives into the night, the Devil smiles. Mickey D was right. You have to fight about what you care about. And at Live and Loud, Mike Montgomery is going to find out just how important Shiatsu and the UWF World championship mean to Tsunami Oni!
