UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #483

Some well needed rest?

29 Jun 2004

Joshua Dunn — Joshua Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It was dark, Joshua could hardly see what was in front of him, and it was like looking out into starless space from the windscreen of a car. Then appearing out of the darkness was a rabid dog, foaming at the mouth and eyes red like fire. It’s coat so indistinguishable from the blackness around it, it was as if it was the darkness had came alive with the soul purpose to cause him harm. Suddenly grasping onto the steering wheel Dunn threw it right, but the car turned left. The dog was still in front of him. He turned the wheel to the left, the car turned to the right, the dog was now on the bonnet of the car, but it dissipated, leaving Joshua Staring at a tree as it came ever so fast into the centre of his vision, he heard the collision before he felt it] *** [In his Dressing Room Joshua awoke with a jolt, his arms splaying away from their previous crossed position, and legs springing apart. He was sitting on the sofa, and in his hand was the card for the stable wars PPV. A cold shiver ran down his back, but he came to his senses quickly. It had been a dream. He raised his free hand to wipe the cold sweat from his head. When it reached his forehead, it found the healed scar across his brow. That’s right, that had happened early yesterday morning. He dragged himself into a sitting position and his eyes fell onto the piece of paper in his hand. Yes, he had taken it upon himself to look through the card for once. But today was a blur. He couldn’t think straight, the wound on his head thumping intensely; he was convinced he could hear knocking] [He could hear knocking] [He looked up, trying to gain focus on the door on the far away wall, yes, he could hear knocking, and he could also hear a voice] Voice: Mr Dunn? Mr Dunn, can you hear me? [Joshua pushed himself up from the couch, managing to stay on his feet on the second try and stumbled over to the door, opening it slowly to reveal the panic stricken face of Jack Kipling on the other side. Jack: Oh my Lord, I was so worried, I thought you were dead or seriously wounded at least [He finally takes time to look over Joshua’s muddy clothes that he failed to change since the crash. Jack’s eyes focused on the wound on his forehead and the look of panic returned with a vengeance] Jack: Oh no, you are hurt, sit down, let me find a doctor… [Jack starts to wildly pat around his own pockets and plunges one hand into his jeans pocket producing his cell phone and starts to jab at the key pad. Joshua places one of his thick set hands onto Jack’s arm and pulls the phone away from his dialling finger; Jack’s concentration slowly comes away from the phone and rests on the easing eyes of Dunn. He then places the phone back into his pocket and lets his hands fall to his sides] JD: Do you want to come in? [Ushering him in, he guides Jack to an armchair and heads over to his rooms cooler] JD: Drink? Jack: No thanks [Dunn still reaches into the cooler and produces an energy drink in a clear plastic bottle, he sits back down on the sofa and cracks open the seal on the cap] Jack: How can you be so calm? You have a huge gash on your head [Dunn calmly takes a swig of the drink before answering, later he wasn’t sure if it was the cool liquid or the caffeine that calmed his throbbing headache] JD: I’ve had worse injuries in wrestling matches, I’ll be absolutely fine Jack: But… but… JD: But nothing, trust me, I’ve been in this business long enough to know when I’m ok to wrestle, and I’d never pass up the chance to go into Stable wars. [The two locked stares for a moment, Dunn’s one of assurance and fortitude, Jack’s one of vague comprehension] Jack: You don’t have concussion or anything? You’re absolutely fine? JD: A bit of a head ache and some dizzy spells, but it’ll pass; I’ve seen the like before. Nothing to worry about [Jack seemed sceptical of this, but coming out unscathed from a car crash is more than enough to turn your views rather pessimistic] Jack: If you’re sure… JD: I am [It was at that point that the room decided to start spinning, Joshua took another swig from the bottle and it seemed to settle the dispute for the moment… the throbbing sensation still present, but it was manageable. Everything should be well and good before Stable Wars starts. He didn’t realise it himself, but he had been staring into space for some time, and was brought back to reality with a bump] Jack: Mr. Dunn? JD: I’m fine, trust me on this [He couldn’t pull out now, not on such short notice, Nick and Ezra were counting on his support in the tournament, and Kevin too probably, although he would never admit it on camera. Mike left him in charge for a reason, and what kind of leader would cause the entire stable to fall flat after all this time at the top?] [His eyes glanced down at the card for stable wars again, he pitied the people reduced to having to play at the build up instead of being in the tournament, and most of them could be pretty decent wrestlers if they tried, although some had no hope in hell. Although he was aware of all the stables involved, he didn’t know the first round match ups. He found it too much of a coincidence to see that none of the more favoured stables didn’t draw to fight each other in the first round, it reeked of the same fair judgement of the Jamie Shock Lottery. He surprised himself that he could remember that, all his memories seemed to be swimming in a soup at the moment, finding it hard to piece together the events of the crash.] [He could remember swerving to avoid a dog and heading into the tree’s, and he could remember hitting the tree and spinning and even slamming into a second tree, but the moments in between were hazy at best] Jack: Is this how you treat all your guests? [His train of thought pulled back to the here and now, Dunn realised how rude he had been, feeling slightly embarrassed, but refusing to lose face he simply smiled towards jack before replying] JD: You know, I hardly have guests these days. I do apologise. Jack: No offence taken Mr Dunn. It’s just every time you go quite I’m convinced I’m not going to hear you speak again. JD: You’re far too pessimistic, that’s your problem Jack: I’m just a little shaken from the crash, that’s all [Dunn snorted shaking his head so slightly it slipped Jack’s notice; he only gave a little chuckle of his own. Taking the brunt of the collision, Dunn wondered how he was feeling himself if Jack was “A little Shaken”] JD: It’s only expected. Your first car crash? [Jack nodded his head in agreement, not able to find the right words to vocalise the response as a simple yes didn’t seem to fit the bill] JD: Well that makes two of us, and I for one don’t want to have to go through that again Jack: You’re right, that’s one experience I don’t want repeated JD: Well it was your first day in a new job, which means things can only get better right? Jack: I suppose, they can’t get much worse than that JD: That’s the spirit, you been given another assignment for the day? Jack: No, I’ve been given the day off JD: Then what you doing around here then? Go on, go out get some air, you have a wife? Jack: A girlfriend yes JD: Then take her out, make a day of it, I would have thought something like a car crash would have made you realise how precious she is to you? [Jack’s glum face brightens up again, a beaming smile now across his face] Jack: You’re right again, in fact I’ll do that right now… [He gets up and makes a break for the door, pausing only to look behind him once] Jack: Bye [And he was gone, Dunn let out a sigh of relief sprawling himself across the sofa again] JD: I thought he would never leave [He stared at the piece of paper again; he wasn’t entirely sure why he found it so fascinating, but there was something about the mystery stable that just screamed at him “buy”. The head ache was getting worse again, but at least the tunnel vision was gone. He let out an enormous yawn, opening his mouth so far that it felt like the corners were going to rip, and before he knew it he was fast asleep again] *** [It was dark, Joshua could hardly see what was in front of him, and it was like looking out into starless space from the windscreen of a car. Then appearing out of the darkness was a rabid dog, foaming at the mouth and eyes red like fire. It’s coat so indistinguishable from the blackness around it, it was as if it was the darkness had came alive with the soul purpose to cause him harm. Suddenly grasping onto the steering wheel Dunn threw it right, but the car turned left. The dog was still in front of him. He turned the wheel to the left, the car turned to the right, the dog was now on the bonnet of the car, but it dissipated, leaving Joshua Staring at a tree as it came ever so fast into the centre of his vision, he heard the collision before he felt it]
