UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #767

Continuing the World's Worst Political Parody

Today (year unrecorded)

Bunny Army of Doom — Alexander Castle

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Your troops are discussing revolting. You find yourself with your back up against the wall. A friend advises you to attempt an election in hopes to win back the support of your people. The election is meant to be rigged and you know it. You must find a challenger to face you that is so unlikely to be voted for that they can’t help but elect you. So who do you choose? “Next!” Loud and rough, like sandpaper against skin, that was the voice of Kiros screaming as he threw a head shot behind his head. Behind him was a small pile of head shots of various people. Each person is a possible candidate for the opponent to face Laguna. In the middle of the room in the mist of a frantic walk, Laguna shuffled through various pictures of different men. He pulled one of the pictures to the front, “here is one!” Laguna exclaimed. “He cheated on his wife and lied in court. In fact he was nearly impeached!” Laguna held out the picture for Kiros to explain. Looking it over once quickly Kiros threw it behind him into the pile, “won’t work, the man does have too much charisma and people over 50 think he’s very attractive. Plus he plays the saxophone; no way are you beating someone who can play a musical instrument.” Shuffling through the headshots again Laguna comes across another possible candidate. “How about him, he killed his chances of winning another election after his famous last words.” Handing the picture over to Kiros, Kiros took one look at the head shot and yelled out in horror “DYYYYYYAAAAAA~!!” The picture flew into the air out of his grasp as Kiros fell backwards out of his chair. He tumbles over back onto the floor and stopped face down on the cold stone floor. Laguna walked over and looked down at him. “You ok?” he asked, wondering what just happened. Before Laguna could bother to help his fallen commander the door swung open. Storming in as if he was coming out from a blizzard, Ward bust into the room. “I’ve got the perfect candidate” he shouted out while throwing his arms up. Kiros tilted his head back so that he could look up from the floor to Ward, who stood a few feet back from him. Laguna turned his attention from helping Kiros back up to Ward. “And just who is he?” asked Laguna. “He’s perfect. The man is egoistical beyond belief. He’s a jackass to everyone he knows and anyone willing to get within ear range. He can’t control his temper and seems to have a talent for getting in trouble. He’s known to have used drugs in his past and has a criminal record. The man beat the only long term girlfriend he ever had and was even a former porn star. He is violent, crude, foul, and disrespectful to anyone he knows. There is no way any person with half a brain cell will ever vote for him” Ward went on, still leaving the true identity of the opponent a secret. Why did Ward leave the identity a secret? Because the author is trying to build suspense, damnit! On that note I end the promo randomly. HA! Now you must wait until the next time I bother to write a roleplay to find out who the opponent is! Mwhahahaha the torture! Wait? What do you mean you don’t care? Well fine be like that, see if I care. Wait don’t go! I care! I CARE! NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!
