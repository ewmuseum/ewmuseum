UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #372

Origins of a resurrection.

date unknown

Moraless — SAS#1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I had faced him before, the man who stood before me. His name was Iron Lung, a man who fought for two federations at once. In one corner you have the Lights Out Wrestling federation, a federation that recently rose from the ashes and was once again attempting to flourish into the greatness it was once able to claim. In the other corner the Universal Wrestling Federation, my home, thing I had to destroy in order to save it. My quest was one that would bring me up against many nay Sayers, those who thought that my methods were too extreme. They were the fools. The ones who failed to understand the severity of the situation. The Universal Wrestling Federation was dying, they could try to hide the facts all they wanted, but it didn’t change the truth. The truth that we had seen with our own eyes. So Iron Lung would prove to be the next block ahead of me, I use the word block as I stumbled there before, losing the opportunity to become the hardcore champion. I would not make the same mistakes again, before I was without focus I was simply arriving at the arena and then coming back to my home, not realising the company that I belonged to falling around me. Until the resurrection Digital Morality, the past of UWF. The former world champion and hall of famer. The man who stood by my side at Renaissance and declared that we would bring about the change to make the Universal Wrestling Federation great once more. The Universal Wrestling Federation once stood as a giant among wrestling companies, destroying all of those their path wherever they decided to roam, yet in the past year we had seen in drop and degrade to something shallow and laughable and we were no longer feared. We were going to put the fear back. We were going to show the world what the Universal Wrestling Federation was about. Three months ago there was a war brewing between Rush Pro Wrestling and ourselves, when Seymour Almasy was still the Global Champion of PrimeTime Central, when he was still a threat to anybody and representing us. I stood tall in the ring with the rest of my co-workers and do you know what we did? We declared shenanigans. Fucking Shenanigans. It all started from then for me, the hate, and the realisation that something had to be done. Naturally UWF did nothing it was me who spilt the blood of Rush Pro Wrestling, their golden boy Chris Storm fell to his knees and bowed before me as I smashed a chair against his skull splitting it open. It was then I learnt that I was the one with the power. Although my so-called co-workers had stood with me in the ring, they dared not venture from the safety of it. They wouldn’t attack or push their cause with me so in the end Rush Pro Wrestling did the only thing it could do. It laughed at us. They sat back and watched our valiant stand crumble, as we were too afraid as a collective to do anything. As they laughed it became clear to me the way to do things, was not through words. Words are soft they are easily forgotten but actions are hard, like a sledgehammer to the skull. The actions that I would take would determine the fate of the Universal Wrestling Federation. One way or another. Until the resurrection. Bondage The Sex Fiend, the future of UWF. A man climbing the ranks quicker than anyone in the federation at present including myself. What he does in his own time is down to him we all have secrets that we hold close to ourselves. That wasn’t important though, he is a man who will claim what is rightfully his in time. At Supremacy the unit would finally be completed. The four men who would stand tall and bring about the new age of Universal Wrestling, the age where once again we would be feared, we would dominate all that we were involved in. No longer would tournaments enter the second round without a member of our roster present. No longer would idle threats be made, nor we, become the butts of their jokes any longer. Iron Lung maybe ahead of me this Sunday but that truly does not matter, it could be anyone. Raid, Malcom Kennedy even our new world champion Mike Montgomery. It doesn’t matter, as eventually they will all have to conform, individually we may be weak but as a unit we are unbreakable. The tag team titles demonstrate that. As will our victory at Stable Wars. For when our final member reveals himself at Supremacy, there will be no force in the Universal Wrestling Federation who could possibly dream of stopping us. Not the Chaos Society. Not the Prime Alliance. And not the Players Club.
