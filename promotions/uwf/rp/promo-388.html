UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #388

StableWars is a Fluke

4 May 2004

Nick Persia — Amsterdam Arena

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Screen fades in as we see Nick Persia on the airfield. He holds on a cab and he steps in. They appear to be in Amsterdam as wee see the Amsterdam Arena, a huge stadium in Europe. He steps out and Nick persia pays the cab driver. He walks towards the building and gets a warm welcome from a receptionist. He is guided to a business room. There he is welcombed by John Ruiters.] John Ruiters: “It’s such a pleasure to meet you.” Nick Persia: “That feeling is mutual. But let’s go down to bussiness shall we?” John Ruiters: “Of course, please follow me.” [They walk towards the bar, where a few other people are busy with doing other business as everybody is calling and reading papers at the same time. They take place at the bar and John orders a drink for both of them] John Ruiters: “Well, what a match last week.” Nick Persia: “I knew it was going to happen, and I warned them. I saw it coming. I know Why they did it so I understand what they did. But you see, they should have left me for dead. They thought I was, but I am not. As you can see, I’m sitting in front of you. So I promise you that Chaos Society is in for the ride of their life. They are probably thinking they made a good move. And after the 4-1 beat down I’m sur ethey felt pretty confident of their chances of winning Stable wars. However there arose one problem. They are with four men, I am on my own. You tell me, does this neccesarily mean I will lose the war? Let me answer this for you. No. I am a lone wolf. I know why Draco joined. Probably why all men join groups, to have that back-up that guarantees the win. I never had that back-up, never needed it. While the pack might seem stronger, they’re also more dependant of the group. They can’t catch a prey alone. The lone wolf, catches a pack of prey…alone. They will think by now that I am their prey. But it takes more than a little powerbomb to take me out. Many have tried that way, most of them discovered that you can only destroy this creature one way: through years of hard work, bloody brawls and mind games.” John Ruiters: “But you didn’t seem prepared though.” Nick Persia: “Not really John, because I did extensively study their work. They aren’t what you would call a “ring general”. They are competent with a few moves, can do damage if you’re not looking, but they’re more like little puppies, playful and nice. I, however, am a lone wolf waiting for my prey, and they decided to wander off into the woods and expose themselves to the danger of a true carnivore!” John Ruiters: “Aren’t you disappointed with this loss?” Nick Persia: “Remember what I said about everyone losing from time to time, no matter what? The same applies. I am a man of honour at heart John I seek the greatest challenge. I do not whine and complain when I lose, it is an inevitability across your career. I never complain when people use underhand tactics on me, in fact I stand up and applaud them for the initiative, and same frame of mind. It isn’t unfair to fight with whatever it takes, its brains John plain and simple. Only losers complain about fairness. Well they should wake up, because life is far from fair to anyone. Hardcore is my middle name!! Guys, you have no idea who you messed with, what beast you unleashed last week at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. You have no idea how I’ve left opponents bloodied, battered and beaten behind me in rings all over the world. How I’ve fought above shark pools dozens of times, needed a total of 674 stitches throughout my career, bled from every vein in my body until I was practically dry. Even Holy Jesus Christ hasn’t even suffered as much as I have throughout my entire career. Yet you, Pryde, and your cronies think that you’re better than me? That you can just screw good ol’ Nick over? It isn’t THAT easy my friend. I’ll make sure that every bone in your body will be broken, I’ll make sure you’ll have so much pain that you’d wish you where in Hell, where it’s a lot nicer. You know Pryde, I really don’t get why you did it, you screwed a man who has lived by the ten commandments all his life, doing good wherever he went. I even founded an orphanage! What for good deeds have you done Pryde? If you haven’t realised it by now, you won’t stand a chance. Look I am not mad at you. You must see me as the teacher, and yourselves as Students. Students are there to learn. They learn from their master. I’m your master. So in some way and form I have the feeling I can’t destroy them. After all, how could we blame them. Students can make mistakes. That’s why they’re called students. But the teacher is there to correct them. And believe me, I will.” John Ruiters: “But what about Mike Montgomery, the World Heavyweight Champion, calling to the aid?” Nick Persia: “I don’t know why he came, and I don’t know what was going on so I don’t know what went through his mind. For details you should ask him.” John Ruiters: “It has nothing to do with Stable Wars, or has it?” Nick Persia: “Look, as I have been just stating: I am a lone wolf. I catch my prey alone. I am a loner, John. But that way I’m sinured nobody turns on me, nobody will double cross me. And this week was the living proof that I was right...AGAIN. You see, Chaos Society want to creat Anarchy. But the problem is I know that they don’t want Anarchy. They want to be on top, but in the world of Anarchy there is no top. Everybody would be equal. Everybody would have the same rights as well as obligations. I can state that it will never happen. I think everybody can figure why. The legacy of the World Title, would be even big as the legacy of the Hardcore Championship. So in their eyes, I would be equal to Mike Montgomery. So I must admit I’m honoured. In fact, they have officially crowned me the chosen one.” John Ruiters: “How do you mean?” Nick Persia: “If I am chosen by them of all people, that would mean I am just that bit different. I wouldn’t dare to say better, but in some way I seperate myself from the rest. But we can conclude one thing. That is that they are nothing more than a couple of no-good lying SOB’s. And what if they would get a shot at my Hardcore title? Then they would be in for a rude awakening. Reality check: Only one of you guys can be the Hardcore Champion. If one of your guys would be the hardcore Champion your faction would might hold the title, but wrestling is still an individual sport, and this is not individualism. So they are betraying the fans here. In fact, the entire UWF but me is betraying the fans here. You see, Stable wars is the one event that is the disgrace to the business. And I’m sad to find out that all hope depends on me. But at the same time I’m glad, because I know it will be in good hands. Let the wrestlers participating in Stable Wars go ahead, but we already know what will happen. Guys will cross each other over, the one betraying on each other, the one after the other. New rivalries will come to life, old rivalries will come back to life again. But in some way Stable Wars has become stale. Sure, it’s a classic. But we all know that the contest is a fluke. You will never get credit for but your stable will. Tell me, how could you be considered as one of the top guys in the business, as you didn’t pick up the win all alone and all by yourself? The answer is simple, that is just not possible. So without further ado, Stable Wars is a fluke. The Winners of this ‘prestigious’ event are a fluke. All people who are particpating are losers. Let me tell you why. They are going in there with four men. All hoping to screw on each other, they’ll do anything if they just think for split second they can be the winner. This is THE event where people get screwed by their so called friends. I think the winners are the worst, because they played the game the dirtiest. This is great entertainment, but it’s just not wrestling. And I am in a wrestling federation, and I am not in a soap. Therefore there is little to no chance I will participate. Therefore I have no clue why Mike Montgomery helped me out. Of course, I am thankful. But I don’t understand why myself. Everything in UWF I considere to be a riddle, with some mystery. Everything is a secret here, and telling rumours is even normal as breathing. You would almost say it is a primary need here in the UWF.” John Ruiters: “And what about your match at Sunday Afternoon Supremacy?” Nick Persia: “There is a point in any major undertaking, where the amount of time, effort, money, and in some cases blood, sweat and tears, that have been poured into the undertaking has grown so great that the people involved become almost irretrievably bound into the task. Withdrawing or giving up becomes no longer an option, so much of themselves have been put into the task that they become determined to see it through, no matter the final costs. Any sacrifice will be made to make it a success, even if the effort involved is completely out of proportion to the value of the task itself. People will drive themselves too, and in a few special cases right past, impossible limits in order to make it work. It ceases to be an investment, or a project and become a point of honour, a ceaseless battle between an implacably hostile universe and the human will. And the funny thing is that given the enormous and limitless resources available too it, the human will seems to win out one time in three. This might not be logical, or even sensible, but this kind of do or die attitude is very human. A certain inability to distance yourself from the events that you are involved in, and be able deal with it rationally. Nick Persia has now reached this point of no return. Prior to this it was merely important to him to succeed, now it’s not important, it’s vital, a matter of life and death that the heavily tattooed and massively muscled individual in question wins the competition. Nothing less than a total outright victory for himself will satisfy him. Defeat is not an option now, it’s not even something that he can contemplate considering. Draco Paladine will be crushed or he will die trying.” John Ruiters: “How do you feel about your opponents?” Nick Persia: “How do I feel about them? Let me put this straight for them. What they have done if unforgiveable. What I will do is history in the making. You see, they might think I will only go for the three count. But this time I will see no horizon as I will try to end their careers. You see, I feel there should be a age cut off. Sometimes you see this when you are reading on the internet, and you are visiting a forum and you see it to your right side ‘This topic is older than the age cut off.’. We should have something like this in the UWF too, for wrestlers. Some people are way too old. They are slower than I could anybody imagine to be. So let’s end them out of their misery, with a legitimate excuse to get rid of them once and for all. And since a few weeks, we have ‘this age cut offer’. That’s me. I volunteered to do so. In a war, everything is allowed. And I will grab every opportunity I get with two hands. I do whatever it takes to win, breaking the rules, dirty tactics anything. Dirty tactics are considered dirty by their recipient though John, to anyone else it is clear to see that is clever tactical thought. I have always had a policy of not underestimating my opponents, it is how I reached the career heights I have. I may have bragged about how I would beat Draco and other lesser wrestlers, but that is because I know even if they are at their best, at their top of their game there is no chance they could better me. Pryde is one of the few exceptions to that. If you underestimate a person’s abilities that is when you get caught off guard and submit yourself to a beating and a lost match. If you still lose without underestimating your opponents and still doing your best, you can be sure that you lost by chance or were simply bettered. It is better to limit the chances that you will lose John, don’t you agree?” John Ruiters: “I agree, but you sounds very respectful towards wrestling and your opponents as well here.” Nick Persia: “This match is something I will relish in regardless of the outcome. As always with my career here it is victory and titles secondary, the enjoyment of the matches is primary. It’s not the fans or the staff interests that concern me, or any sense of achievement for myself in terms of titles or victories. It was and always will be about the fight John, the adrenaline rush you get in that squared circle, the opportunity to show the world what you are truly capable of. To inflict pain and suffering on to your enemies and enjoy the hurt you lay upon them. This is what I enjoy about my “job” John. To shut all the mouths that spew out crap about how you are not capable of victory, that you are long dead and buried. Oh how all my actions have shown how wrong those critics truly are. We are shutting them up and shutting them down one by one. We have a long list of enemies and people always want to step in your way, well let them because I take pleasure in demolishing them down to the ground, it is what I live for, to fight. It is what I do best. Draco Paladine will not go down easily but I will make sure he is a shattered man before I give up the ghost and long after the breath has been taken from my lungs. There is a constant fire inside me, a burning desire. It is something I can control most of the time, but when I lose my cool and step into the ring something changes and I become what you have so witnessed so many times now. I lose that control, and it feels liberating and I enjoy it as the fire consumes me and turns me into the hate exuding force I become on the apron.” John Ruiters: “You didn’t make a mention about Dante yet.” Nick Persia: “: You want one? You’ll get one. He f*cked up. How’s that for an explanation?” John Ruiters: “Wow, that’s kind of rude actually.” Nick Persia: “I can be all nice about him, but that would feel like I screwed him over. Of course, anything I do can be classified as tactics. That’s not how I want to live my life.” John Ruiters: “How do you feel about your tag team partner, the man you beat to become THC Champion, The Plague?” Nick Persia: “You’re either with me or against me. Either way, I will get what I want.” John Ruiters: “I think you are underestimating your opponents!!” Nick Persia: “No the problem is you show too much faith in them…” Coach: No I don’t!! Nick Persia: Draco and Dante will be taken apart by the hands of Nick Persia...Team Double D shall be wiped from the face of the earth…Nick Persia will cover the land of a great darkness…Team D2 and their short coorperation will be the first to go…next who knows…all I can tell you John is that silence will be spreading like a plague…village to village…city to city…country to country…no more running… John Ruiters: “Draco and Dante will give one hundred percent against you, they won’t want another team fighting against them… Nick Persia: “Team Dumb and Dumberer can give two hundred percent…it won’t make a difference; compared to the might of Nick Persia they have no answer…time is ticking away for them...the final judgement is at hand…Nick Persia will conquer…” John Ruiters: “But…” Nick Persia: “Their footsteps are doomed…the moment they step into the ring with us they will be given no reprieve…slaughter is what the crowd will see…it is hopeless for them…D and D will soon be hearing the everlasting silence…everlasting darkness…eternal sleep…against the might of Nick Persia there can be no victor…” [Nick Persia stays silent for a moment] Nick Persia: “Me and The Plague are just so much better than Dante and Draco....It’s not even funny. You see… Pryde might be smart… But I’m smarter… I can see through his ways and plans… I’m always one step in front of him… The only difference is… I don’t let him know I’m on to his game and plans… Let him believe he has outsmarted us all… Let him believe his plan is bulletproof… Let’s give him the false sense of supremacy… Let him have his fun thinking he’s on the verge of winning the UWF Hardcore Title from me… I’ll put him back into his place and put him back down to earth… Do you know the serie Ed, Edd and Eddy?” John Ruiters: “Eerhmm, no.......” Nick Persia: “Too bad, because they make an excellent comparison to Reo Dai, Raid and Pryde. The one follows the other in their stupid plans. They are soulmates. The same with them. But they are so stupid to bother other people with their own shortcomings. Now they will pay the prize.” John Ruiters: “Any last comments?” Nick Persia: “You see, they may be talented performers. But they are not nearly as talented as the multi-talented, multi-champion, multi-millionaire, multi-Persia-Girls, wrestling-living-and-breathing-match-maker-and breaker, called the Nick Persia! If they think their frame will be strong enough to tangle with me, he's in for a little surprise! When that bell rings, he will think he's in a roller-coaster. He will go up and down, from left to right and upside down. Because this will be a ride he will remember, as I'm the roller-coaster-tycoon, BABY! I will bounce him around that ring, like a basketball and dribble him all over that arena if I have to. But I will make that match-winning-slamdunk, when I Persia Drop him to the mat!” [Screen fades into the commercials, for a Plague T-Shirt]
