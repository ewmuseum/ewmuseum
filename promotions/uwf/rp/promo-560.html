Sacrifice — After WNU

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens with sacrifice leaving the ring cussing every breath of the way then he makes his way back to the back locker room were the three finalist are celebrating their victory over the rest of the newbies involved. Sacrifice turns to them and all the music and people stop and turn to him. Sacrifice leaves the room he then begins to notice someone is following him through the hallway he then stops and suddenly turns around to find the little girl whos notebook he autographed earlier then notices a frown on the little girls face. Sacrifice>> Whats a matter? The little girl>> I really wanted you to win that match tonight and get your chance at the title. Sacrifice>> Well this is what happen but get ready ill get my shot at one point maybe a year or maybe tommorow you never know. The little girl wipes the tears from her face and begins to have a little smile. The little girl>> I hope you get a shot at the title soon. Sacrifice>> since you are my biggest fan how about we go to the backstage party together and you can see everyone and maybe get some more autographs. The little girl jumps with excitment and grabs sacrifices hand and tug on him to make him go faster down the hall. Upon entering the party they begin to play disco fever and you see a disco ball lower from the ceiling fizbane was the first to join to the dance floor followed by alot of girls the little girl laughs with excitment and leaves sacrifice to begin to collect autographs. Finally everyone is on the dance floor and sacrifice is still standing alone. until voices begin to say. Voices>> come on join in the fun it will lloosin you up a bit your to intense. Sacrifice laughs and finally jumps in the dance and laughs out loud and tells everyone thnaks for being so nice to him and he smiles and ladies begin to join him and the scene fades out.
