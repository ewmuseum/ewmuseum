UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #377

The aftermath... a new champion born

29 Apr 2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's Dressing Room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some times great risks must be taken for the greater good. Some times the ends really do justify the means. Not every loss is a defeat… but how does all this apply to Joshua Dunn or the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet. Well it’s quite simple really, if you think about it. The Great risk… that would be hoping for a clean fight from the Resurrection and trusting that his stable mates wouldn’t intervene what ever was happening. As with all goals, not all are met. The Ends? Well that would be that Dunn now has proven his word, and shown how foolish Resurrection, not he, are and managing to equate a draw from them after receiving a beating in the very first match. How could one man possibly take on a whole Stable and come out in a Stale mate? This questions the ability of said Stable. Not every loss a defeat… Of course going against three men consecutively, no one expects them to win all the matches. But what has happened is that Dunn has given the crowd something that the federation hasn’t seen as a whole for now, and that is proper Sports entertainment. The fans like the Underdog; the man who stacks the odds against him on purpose is what they like. This man has overcome odds, has been the under dog from the minute he first stepped into the federation, he has come back and given the Fans a thrill ride of a career to follow, ups, downs, intrigue and a dark history. All makes for a Fan favourite. And a Fan Favourite becomes a People’s Champion, and a people’s champion IS the best champion to be So what if he’s communist That’s right, Dunn IS now the Fan’s #1. Name one other that the people would rather see as world champion. Name one other who doesn’t interfere in matches. The mere sight of the man spurs the crowd into a cheering frenzy now, despite past misdemeanours. He takes what the fans want and spits it out ten fold, like an entertainment magnifier. Mt. Gagazet, Ladder cage match, Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet… even trying to set the odds against himself in his first World Title Shot. All points mean one thing; the man is obviously up for a challenge Or just plain crazy Simply said, Dunn’s unpredictable, and is sure to entertain, and the masses love to be entertained Why else be in the Business [The Scene Opens in the dressing room of the Scottish Communist, the TRUE Communist, the GREATEST Scotsman in the UWF. And yes sitting within the communistic décor of the room lays the master of manipulation and technical wrestler that is Joshua Dunn. He’s wearing his red T-Shirt and black Jeans, you’d never be expecting him to be wearing anything else, and it’s what Dunn is in essence. The pretence of unoriginality has may times caught an opponent off guard, expecting him to go through the same boring procedure over and over again, even though that’s nothing like real Wrestling. Real wrestling involves thinking on your toes and being able to roll with the punches, Dunn has done this and more, the man to have the longest title reign in the federation ever, along with having the most title defences because of that, and resigned undefeated IC champion after the Mt. Gagazet death match and denying the former World Champion Seymour Almasy from the Grand Slam. A small glint of gold is visible from he chain he wears around his neck, and on his arm, over the elbow is a bandage support, and a large band-aid on his forehead. The people’s Champion sits only looking at the camera, knowing exactly what the fans of the World want to hear] JD: That’s what I call a Wrestling match [And so the masses are appeased and Dunn can now assert his physical dominance along with the impression he gives in the ring when all the Crowd are chanting and screaming his name, there’s no doubt in their mind where Dunn should be right now instead of some commissioner] [Dunn pushes himself up from the sofa, and takes a stand that lets him look as powerful as if he’d just been pumped full of steroids and focusing drugs, an intimidating stance I can tell you] JD: One man Army… if Malcolm Kennedy hadn’t violated that handle I’d have earned it on last SAS. I only managed a draw yes, but that was after being beaten down by the entire Resurrection. A Draw? They should be ashamed. No one can deny that I did it on my own, without the help of anyone else. Even Resurrection couldn’t first pick up a win without ensuring that the Communist Scotsman was beaten and bloody. Makes you think about their function of a Stable [Dunn closes his eyes for a moment, taking in a long deep breath; obviously the painkillers sitting on the coffee table are not enough to keep the pain at bay. With the punishment he went through, thinking anything otherwise would be ridiculous. One man can’t go through three without some kind of self-hurt. Dunn manages to subside the pain and composes himself before continuing] JD: But they do seem to be one step ahead of the game. I have no doubt that the special Guest referee they have in store for Monty and Ezra on SAS is indeed their fourth member. Who, it remains to be seen, but we do not compromise quality with short-term benefits. We will once again be known as the Stable that people fear the name of. We are the piece de la resistance of the entire federation. And we only strengthen our status with over coming impossible odds. Numbers means nothing to us. [An eye twitches, and Dunn’s hand flies to the bandage on his arm, holding it tight, suppressing a yelp of pain, however great a person is does not mean they can come out unscathed from any match] JD: But first I face Plague, who Monty Calls the Player’s Club troubleshooter, well what problem is he trouble shooting? I can’t see the logic behind this match up, but I never have, and never will back down from a challenge, no matter how idiotic, no matter how superfluous, no matter how suicidal. Once I’ve accepted there’s no turning back, my word is stronger than the toughest metal alloy. I was annoyed at the appearance of Ezra during the Dunn Exhibition Gauntlet, but as he did no valuable damage, I have no problem with it; I shall just call it a misunderstanding and be done with it. But I shan’t expect it to happen again. We are the UWF Elite, and who would turn their back on the greatest superstars in the business? Only Idiots… Idiots and Anarchists. And those are our opponents for Stable wars? An idiotic pair of dreamers, a group of people who want to raise the federation that pays them and a group that falls into both categories? Fools But first Plague shall be shown who the people want to win, they are the customers, and the customers are always right [He’s right you know] [The Scene Fades to Black on Dunn's pain supressing face]
