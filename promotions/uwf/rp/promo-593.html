UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #593

It's been a while.

date unknown

Moraless — RP#1 WNU vs Unforgiven/Real Deal

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A fist slams hard into a punch bag, the power being transferred from fist to bag almost seamlessly, sending it careening forwards, before being bought to a stop by a white-coated man. The man gave a sagely nod of approval before scribbling in the unreadable scrawl that doctors often did, on a large clipboard before crossing the room and sitting back down on his desk, flicking through the numerous pieces of paper. A taped up hand appears in view, until it draws back to show a heavily scarred arm, continuing to do so to reveal the facial features of John ‘Moraless’ Edwards. We haven’t seen John, since the events of Supremacy, which was both a time for joy, as well as a time for pain. Supremacy had cemented the resurrection as the most dominant tag team ever, in terms of length of time holding the belts. However, the returning drowning pool had put a dampener on that, little did they know that in that one move, they had changed the shape of the Universal Wrestling Federation. “Ok John, I’ve got to say that I think you are going to be ok. The strength in your arm has returned, your back, well you know the damage that you have done to that in the past so it will never be perfect, BUT…” the doctor paused as he gazed across the room at John who had just put on his plain white t-shirt, poking his head out of the top of it. Dr Jones had been John’s physician for as long as he could or cared to remember, he hated seeing John’s back slowly become brittle, one day it would give out, both of them knew that. Neither would ever say it. John nodded at Dr Jones, acknowledging what I was about to say, much to the relief of the doctor whose seating position adjusted into a much more open stance. His eyes crossed the room and met the returned gaze of John who had remained quiet for the duration of the physical, focusing purely on the tasks at hand. His time off had gotten to him, made him feel inadequate, he loathed the fact that the events of Stable Wars had cost him his Extreme Championship belt, left him unable to defend and ultimately being forced to drop the title. “I don’t have to say that I don’t think you should be returning to the ring period do I John?” questioned Dr Jones, totally breaking John’s train of thought as he slowly glanced up from a furrowed brow. His eyes told the doctor everything he needed to know and everything he expected. John had unfinished business plenty of it. “I’m sorry doc but you know I have to finish this chapter in my life. Things are slowly falling into place. You know I am a firm believer in the idea that everything happens for a reason, that the actions we take and not the decisions we make determine our destiny.” The words flowed from John’s mouth with the greatest of ease, he had practiced those lines many times, running through their true meaning within his mind. He didn’t really fully understand any of it; he had read it on an inspirational poster once. It always sounded nice when he was trying to be philosophical. John rose to his feet and stretched across the desk, extending his hand. As he did so John winced slightly as the discs in his back slid around almost freely, trapping nerves causing him to clench his teeth to fight through the pain. Dr Jones shook John’s hand lightly a look of deep regret on his face. Ultimately he had the power to stop John fighting, but he knew that without his career. He had nothing. Roughly fifteen minutes had passed and nothing had occurred other than a walk home for John, the weather had been pleasant in the past few days, the sun beating down for hours on end with light wispy clouds doing nothing to hide the suns rays. John hated the sun; he had always been a night owl for a start and had incorporated that into his persona, using it to his advantage in the early days, skulking around in misty alleys to give an air of mysticism. The truth behind the matter was if he stayed out in the sun for longer than ten minutes his wrestler name would have been ‘The Amazing Lobster Boy’. Turning the corner at the end of the street John continued to amble along, the summer bought out a lethargic feel around the small town he called home. The children could not be bothered to mug old ladies anymore and simply sat on street corners drinking bottles of hobo strength cider. Some things never did change, UWF however had. When John returned to UWF he and Digital Mortality had set out for one goal, to bring UWF back to the glory it once had. The means did not matter to them, they simply wanted the UWF to be as successful as it used to be, and the crowd hated them for it. The fans that had grown content with lack lustre performances and shows, which were as well put together as a broken vase. Yet in a few short months the changes were immense. Ticket sales had increased two hundred percent. Once more arenas were being sold out and the roster was star packed as it once was, the likes of Oni and Shock returning along with the Drowning Pool, all memories of the past suddenly arriving back to bring UWF back to glory and it had succeeded. Resurrection had been a success; yet something didn’t sit too well with John, not well at all. Having reached his small apartment building, he turned the key gently in the lock, jiggling the key as he did so. The lock was rusty and needed a bit of encouragement. He entered the corridor and ignored the letters on the mat in front of him, they would only be offering him a lifetime of credit card debt anyway, he turned and opened the wooden door on the right of the corridor, it slowly creaked open as he entered his living room. Alerted by the noise a pair of ears perked up from a small armchair positioned opposite as a small tabby cat stretched and walked slowly across the carpet before swiping at John with its claw before wondering off upstairs. John winced in pain as blood began to trickle down from just above his ankle. John loved the cat with all his heart, but the cat just didn’t seem to like him. Sighing deeply, John moved slowly towards the mantle piece and the mirror that rested above it. Just a few weeks ago the mantle piece was the home to his two titles, its funny how things change so quickly, instead of lacquered cases contained leather and gold, the was now a simple mantle piece with dust collecting on it. There was one person he blamed for all of this, one person whose jealousy and bitter rage had led to John’s downfall. That person was Sean McClancy. John’s fist clenched as his mind was cast back to stable wars and the Knightmare Brigades relentless assault on The Resurrection, after they had been town to pieces by the Drowning Pool. Sooner or later Sean and John would face again, there was too much bitterness between them for it to simply be left. The heads in the offices at UWF had already spoken to John about it, would he be ok with the situation should it come up where they were placed in a situation where they were to work together. Naturally John had said yes, saying that everything would be fine and it would be a great show. John however was a great actor and he knew as well as Sean did that when they met again it would be devastation. The last time it took a piledriver onto solid concrete to stop Sean, would anything be able to stop him this time, when revenge was on the cards. John shook his head slightly and let himself flop lifelessly into his armchair, picking up the television remote next to him, clicking the television onto some random music channel, music to accompany his thoughts. The problem with John was that he was always looking towards the future and never the present and the extreme present represented the main event at Underground. He was as physically ready as he could have hoped to be and his mind was focused on the task at hand, every win and every loss prepared a path for him. The path led straight to his target. Sean McClancy. John ‘Moraless’ Edwards was back and it was only a matter of time, till the UWF went up with a bang.
