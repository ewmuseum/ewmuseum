UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #374

This mind isn't for the taking

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins on the front porch of Ruff Ryder once again. He seems to not have moved since the last interview except to get the ice chest full of beer that is now sitting beside him. He has his feet propped up on the ice chest and is wearing black shorts with a red 'Prime Alliance' t-shirt and a black hat turned backwards. He looks slowly at the camera holding a beer in his right hand. ) Ryder: Back so soon? Oh I guess that's cause I called ya'll here to do an interview huh. Oh well I just got a little bored and figured i'd tell Mentok how much of a bitch he was some more. Seein as how he hasn't bothered to respond to my earlier promo and all. How does he expect to win a damn match when he won't even talk some trash with the true indian superstar? It's all the same to me though, because i'm gonna whip his ass no matter if he talks or not. He can start spoutin his mind stuff all he wants, but it ain't gonna change the outcome of the match, because when it's all said and done the winner will always be the Cherokee Thrilla Ruff Ryder...me. * Ryder takes a drink of beer and continues * Ryder: I always come out on top and you're gonna see that Mentok. You can't stop me, you can only hope to slow me down a bit. I'm like a tank that won't stop. I just keep on coming and then I blow you out of the ring. Just try and stop me, because i'm gonna enjoy beating the hell out of you. * Ryder throws his empty beer bottle over the side of the porch before crackin open another * Ryder: I'm back to true form and i'm not gonna be taking any shit from anyone, especially those bitches from the Players Club. Those idiots think that just cause they got the win over us once that it's gonna happen all the time well that's horseshit. We will be the victors sooner than they think and you can bet it's gonna sting. We're gonna rid the UWF of these sorry ass stables that can't run with the big dawgs too. Like this stable that Moraless has, now don't get me wrong, I like Digital. I fought against him and with him and he's a hell of a fighter, but that don't mean I won't run him into the ground if his little faction tries to get in my way. The one man army and me got something goin and we're gonna step on any and everybody who gets in our way until we reach the top. We are on a mission and it's a mission that we will complete...by any means necessary! ( Ryder glares into the camera as the scene fades to black and comes to an end. )
