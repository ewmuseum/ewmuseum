UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #293

too little too late

28 Mar 2004

Joshua Dunn — Dunn's House

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Perhaps too little too late, but somewhere in the immenseness of the city, we have finally tracked down Joshua Dunn, The AWOL UWF Star has been missing for some time, missing in the fact that he has been able to avoid the prying eye of the UWF for a time. With a main event against Moraless this isn’t the time for Joshua to be doing something else. What has he been doing for the past week or so, we’re not entirely sure, no one is. There has to be a reason, there’s always a reason] [The Scene Opens, we’re in Joshua’s Home. Well, not exactly his home, but we are on his property. As the blackness fades away we can see a royal blue wooden door, no number, just the word “Hame” as the name of the house, the camera is focused on the cast iron door knocker, shaped in the form of a hammer and sickle. Anything to do with Joshua Dunn is never away from the vivid symbolism of his enforced communist beliefs. A hand comes out from the left hand side of the camera and used the doorknocker, a very loud bang made as the heavy iron knocker hits the wooden door. The camera pans out a bit to see who the arm belongs to, it belongs to George Thomson, UWF reporter and perhaps the only Close friend of Joshua Dunn in the business. He’s dressed in his blue suit, a lighter shade than that of the door, and he seems to have gotten rid of the neck brace from the last time we saw him] [After a short pause of waiting, the door swings back and reveals the man we’ve been waiting to see, Joshua Dunn. His eyes bore into the camera staring him in the face, He’s wearing black jeans and a red T-Shirt, but as he looks over to the other man standing at his door, the anger of being disturbed melts away. Replacing the icy glare a wry smile creeps across his face before it too melts away into a vague blank look that occupies his face between anger and intense calculation. He and George exchange looks for a moment before George finally breaks the silence] GT: Hello Joshua [Dunn slowly nods his head] JD: George GT: It’s been a while JD: It has [Another small silence ensues before George breaks it again] GT: Mind if we have a few words? JD: Not at all [With that said Dunn moves to the outside of the door and shuts it behind him. He leans against the door and folds his arms, muscles tensing] GT: You’ve been a bit quiet lately on UWF TV JD: I have a reason for needing to be on air? GT: Well you do have a main event match on SAS… have you been evading our cameramen? JD: You know I don’t like you people coming to my house GT: Even so, I think you have a little bit of explaining to do, I mean you’ve never been this quiet before a match… Why now? [Dunn adjusts his position so he appears taller in the shot, arm muscles tightening defensively, with this happening George takes a step back, in full knowledge of what Dunn can do when he’s made angry, that left him several months in hospital, in an almost full body cast, back in the days when Dunn was the NVA’s puppet. Just newly losing his neck brace and back to full health, George doesn’t want to spend another lengthy period in hospital] JD: I’ve had some more… important matters that have shifted priorities slightly GT: What matters? [This time Dunn actually pushes himself up from the wall with his back to stand at his full menacing height of 6 foot 4. This time the cameraman takes a step backwards, giving himself plenty time for a head start if Dunn decides to lash out, when his patients with reporters and the media finally wear out] JD: Family matters, which I’d rather… not… talk… about… [He pauses for a moment] JD: OK? GT: Of course, of course. Aren’t you even the slightest bit anxious about your main event match? I mean it’s against Moraless, a man who himself could soon be on the path for the world title soon, don’t you want to say a few things about him? A few things to make him irate? Irritated? Distracted? [Dunn’s arm muscles sag as he relaxes once more; an inaudible sigh of relief passes both George and the cameraman about that fact. Dunn leans against the door again] JD: Yes, usually I would try and distract my opponents wouldn’t I… [Dunn smiles briefly, as if looking back on the “good old days”, before continuing] JD: But I have been keeping an eye on him, He earns respect. He earns respect by showing respect. I can show such a man respect. But he’s also distracted already, as shown by his last promo, although I haven’t been on air, I have been paying attention to him, and why try to distract a man who is already distracted. His brother is causing him grief, it may give me the advantage I need to win, but now it could be too little to late… I need time alone to think about… matters… [With that Dunn unfolds his arms and goes back inside of the house, leaving George and the cameraman, almost disappointed by Joshua’s poor showing… a sign of things to come maybe? George comes into the front of the camera] GT: This is George Thomson for UWF TV, signing off [The Scene Fades through Black to the UWF logo]
