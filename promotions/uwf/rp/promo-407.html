UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #407

My turn.

Today (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Office

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* How many people doubt Malcolm Kennedy can beat Mike Montgomery at SAS? So many that you would get sick trying to count them all. But that doesn’t matter. Not this time. Malcolm now has his chance to prove to the entire UWF that he is World Title material. Mike Montgomery and the Players Club have gotten the best of Malcolm so many times that you cant count them all on fingers and toes. But that doesn’t either. Malcolm has his chance to prove that he is, in fact, one of the best in the UWF, And no one short of god can stop him now.* (Open Scene.) “ Listen, If he wants the turnbuckles to explode, by god give it to him. He wants the cage covered in barbed wire, give it to him. If he wants me inside that cage with him, then for gods sake, give it to him. Book the freaking match. Ill be more than happy to settle this with Ryder once and for all.” [Click] (The sound of a cell phone closing is heard.) (The scene fades into a familiar office. A man stands staring through the blinds of a very large one way window. He looks out onto the floor below, watching the people make and break their lives at the discretion of lady luck. He puts his right hand in his pocket and continues to stare out the window. His left hand raises to his lips carrying a cigar. He puffs on it and lowers his hand again. The suit he wears is black.) “ Its time we get what we both want Mike. A chance to tear each other apart in the ring. In front of all those people. Those ‘fans’ of yours that love you so much. Those people that we go out in front of every night to entertain. Those people that consider you THEIR champion. But we all know the truth, those people don’t meant shit. That belt around your waist is what everyone cares about. The fans, the ones who could never cut it but dream every day about being the champion. The refs who were never big enough to fight, who wish they had someone count to three for them and put that title over their shoulder. And most of all the entire roster. That belt is the reason every one of those guys are here. Wether they admit it or not, that belts the reason they show up every Wednesday and Saturday. And Im gonna prove to every man in the back at SAS that I am very much worthy of carrying that title. Im gonna put you down for the count.” (Turning to face the camera. The man in the black suit spins around. He wears a light blue undershirt and has a rose in his chest pocket. He pulls the rose from his pocket with his right hand and smells. He drops it on the desk and continues.) Malcolm Kennedy: “Its you and me Mike. One on one, man to man. Tooth for a tooth, eye for an eye. And Im gonna come out on top.” (Malcolm smiles as he smashes his cigar into the ash tray. Leaving the butt in the tray he sits down in his chair behind his desk.) “ Every time we get in the ring, you throw your power around and lay the boots to me. And I come back every time. You try and get rid of me, you fire me, you leave me in the ring waiting for a stretcher every time, and I come back all the time. But this time, Your not the only one with someone by your side to help you out against me. You can rest assured that the Resurrection will be ready and waiting for you to try and pull something on me.” “That little beating you got when I was special referee is nothing compared to what I will do at SAS.” (Malcolm leans back in the chair. He locks his hands behind his head and relaxes.) “The Players Club will soon be exactly like the Prime Alliance is now. NOTHING! Soon Mike, you, Dunn, Kazee, and your newest addition known as Kevin Starr, will be left in shambles.” “Kevin Starr? Pfft. That was your big surprise. The man who left for god knows how many months a injured pinky? That was the shocking announcement? Kevin played the big leagues with Almasy and didn’t get the job done. He wont be able to do it with the Players Club at his side either.” “The Resurrection is blazing through the UWF. Doing what we promised from day one. Destroying the UWF so that we can rebuild it like it used to be, like its SUPPOSED to be.” “Mike Montgomery, you are part of the reason we are going to rebuild this fed. When we are done, you wont abuse your power any more. You will no longer win the title shots you give to yourself. You will no longer be at the top of the pecking order, or a part of the pecking order for that matter. Those fans in the stands may love you, they may scream your name and jump for joy. But you wont have that much longer. Your time at the top is drawing to a close and soon the Resurrection will be at the top. Come hell or High water.” (Malcolm stands back up and walks to the window and returns to staring out the window.) (The scene fades back to black.) *End*
