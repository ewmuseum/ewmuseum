UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #349

Memory Lane

17 Apr 2004

Mike Montgomery — A UWF Production truck.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As we begin this promo from our brand spankin' new Commissioner, we do something that hasn't been done in several years. We open our little scene inside a Universal Wrestling Federation Production Truck. Everything's set up for tomorrow's Supremacy Card where, for reasons that have yet to be figured out, the Player's Club will face the team of Digital Mortality and Moraless in a UWF Tag Team Title match. Nobody's entirely sure how or why those two got a shot without fighting in a single match to, ya know, earn it, but let's be honest, Monty and Ezra have pretty much gone through EVERY OTHER TEAM ON THE ROSTER! I mean really, did the Administration really have any alternative? So anyway, we find Monty sitting in one of the producer's chairs, tilted back slightly and his eyes are closed as though he's trying to put his finger on something. Interestingly enough, that's exactly what he's doing. 'So I'm watching Digimon's promos, and something he said jogged a memory. It wasn't the blatant lie about begging to join the Iron Curtain. That was a simple matter of the Devil you know vs. the Devil you don't. Besides, as I recall, Digimon got himself booted out of the Curtain for that one. No no, that lie wasn't it. 'It wasn't that beautiful line about how Digimon fought long and hard to get where he is...I mean was. Key word there is was. Wrestling, much like it's fans, is more about 'what have you done for me lately.' The answer to that is, in Digimon's case, not a damn thing. Now of course he'll go on and on about having beaten me day in and day out, singlehandedly destroying the Asylum...and that's when it hits me. See, it was Digimon's ranting about that which reminded me of something. Something from one of my old promos...' Mike pulls a tape off a shelf. He slides the cassette into one of truck's many VCR's and flicks a couple switches before hitting play. Thai: But of course, he is a communist after all. Mike and Spiff both fall to the floor as they burst out laughing. Mike slowly pulls himself back up, tears streaming down his face. Mike Montgomery: They...they don't know do they? Spiff: I guess not. Instead of trying to ride on your coattails, Digimon's been riding on theirs. He's been claiming credit for their win pretty much since the NVA left. Spiff-O-Matic, roll archival footage. Spiff-O-Matic: rolling. DM: 'Everyone knows that I went in some kind of a downhill after losing the IC Title, but that really doesn't matter anymore, I have gotten my revenge, I beat three of the Ass-ylum guys almost by myself. The NVA were just there to support me, and as you can see, I didn't fail them. Mike Montgomery: But wait...there's more. That is what has been done to the Ass-ylum, Digital Mortality has put a dagger in their hearts, he, almost single handedly defeated three of the members of the Ass-ylum, they were just no match for a stroger and more physically violent Digital Mortality. Jared Crafter, Mike Montgomery and Mysterious Matt Jones have all gone home with another loss on their record, but if they love this sport just as Digital does, they will bounce back. Spiff: Of course there's this little gem. Note how he seems to have forgotten by now that the NVA was even in the arena for that one. DM: 'Not yet. I want to know what he thinks about this match. I have everything to gain and nothing to lose, but I think he is planning something bigger. I think he and Corporal are going to attack me, but what the hell, I really don’t know. The only thing I do know is that if he does, I’m going after him and I’m going to destroy him.' BB: 'Just like you did to the Ass-ylum.' Mike Montgomery: Personally, I love this one. He's stupid enough to actually say that he beat us single handedly. There is no question that Plague is a great wrestler, but in his downfall, he will be kicked down by Digital and buried in the ground. After yes, single-handedly beating The Ass-ylum, he will destroy the Plague. No one will be able to stop Digital, he is going wild and he is starting a streak no one will be able to stop him. Spiff: Now you'd think we were done discrediting Digimon, but no. We're just as good at beating you over the head with a point as the NVA is. Mike Montgomery has been flapping his mouth about how some powder was used in the match, the only thing is, how the hell could he know if he was unconcious in the ring? That was a clean win and if he tries to evade the fact that Digital alone beat three other wrestlers, then he is crazy after all. Because he knows that Digital was the better man, he was much better than those three fools with singing clowns. Mike Montgomery: Real easy to evade the fact that Digital alone beat three other wrestlers, seeing as how it never happened and all. DM: 'Hello, they couldn’t even beat me!' BB: 'Yeah, they do suck, three against technically one.' Spiff: So Duck Chow Mein, Tai Chi Chwan, consider this a favor. Your boy, Digimon, can't be trusted at all. He doesn't acknowledge the simple fact that you beat us for him. He couldn't give a rat's ass about you. The scene fades back to Montgomery as he takes the tape out. Monty replaces it in the truck's video library before retrieving another tape. 'See, here's the thing. Digital Mortality is what we call a glory hog. That promo clip you just saw is from after a 6-man tag match between the Asylum and the Iron Curtain. So the NVA pulls out it's good old white powder of Doom, cheats and wins the match. You know, same way they always did. But then our friend Digi comes out and tries to take all the credit for himself. Digital Mortality decides to claim that he beat the Asylum by himself. Never happened that way, but who cares right? It's not like people believe in things like continuity and truth...oh wait...they do don't they? 'Now of course if I'm Moraless, I'm pretty skeptical right now. I mean, I'm not showing the actual match, so why should he believe me? Well what can I say, I'd show hte match if it weren't for the simple fact that there are bookshelves bigger than our match library. Thusly, I work with what I got,' Mike pops another tape into the machines, 'let's revisit the past a little more, shall we?' Mike Montgomery: Man, that's gotta be driving Digimon nuts. The NVA helps him beat us, and I still get higher profile matches than he does. This is beautiful. What else we got? Spiff: The rumble's on before the Danny/Digimon match. Mike Montgomery bursts out laughing at the mention of the rumble. He's clearly loving every second of the way things are going. Mike Montgomery: And so basically I'm on the Pay Per View before Digimon, AND after him. Spiff: Basically. Mike Montgomery: Man, losing to Digimon must be good for a guy's career or something. The scene fades back to Montgomery. He furiously fiddles with some controls, 'Dammit, that wasn't the one I wanted. Oh well, Does help me with another point that I was going to get to later anyway. See, yeah we lost to the Iron Curtain on a regular basis. But what you seem to forget Digi, is that it takes two to tango. You needed us. Simple as that. You needed us, because otherwise there was nobody to make you look good. With nobody to make you look good, the people don't care about you. Now I could go on about how you pretty much owe us for all the success you had. I mean, the Asylum launched Tsunami Oni, it launched Kevin Starr, and in a way it launched you into the main even to Digi. I could go on, but I don't think there'd be much point. Now let's see what else Memory Lane has in store for us, shall we?' Mike pops a third tape into the machine as the scene fades out... Spiff: You see the promos Digital Mortality is putting out? Mike Montgomery: Nope, I'm kinda hoping that if I ignore him, he'll go away. Spiff: He says he took out the Asylum single handed. Montgomery falls off the couch and hits the floor, bursting out laughing. Montgomery slowly pulls himself back onto the chair, gasping for breath Mike Montgomery: You're kidding right? Spiff: Nope. Says he made Crafter tap to the Digitalizer. I dunno what match he was in, but didn't you guys get caught by that white powder stuff the NVA loved so much? Mike Montgomery: Yeah, that was the match I was fighting. Well, payback's a bitch and so's Digital. But until then, let's ignore the little git. Maybe the wannabe really will go away. 'You see, there you go again, taking credit for other people's work. Moraless, let me explain something to you. That's the type of thing you have to look forward to. Like I said earlier, Digimon's a glory boy. Let's say, just for the heck of it, that you guys manage to beat us on Sunday. Here's the thing. I know exactly what will happen in Digital Mortality's first promo following that match. He'll come out here and try to convince everyone that he won the match on his own, that he didn't get help from anyone. I mean, when he's stealing the thunder of one of the greatest tag teams in UWF history...okay I beat hte NVA by myself at Renaissance, but that's beside the point...my point is that if he's stealing the thunder of somebody like the NVA, do you really think he'll show any kind of gratitude to you? 'In fact, let me give you this friendly warning. Unlike Digital Mortality, I don't care about the past. He's focused almost entirely on it. That's not good for someone trying to get ahead. His goal is solely to recapture his glory days. He's got a lot of trouble letting things go.' Montgomery: God dammit. I hate Hollywood. Do we really have to watch this video of Digital Jackass'? I mean my God, I thought the Corporal was repetitive. Spiff: 'Fraid so. It's in your contract somewhere. You know that he's still going on about that IC title match that you cost him back in...what was it, January? Montgomery: You mean that one where I jumped the Corporal, and I was doing the whole masked Canadian thing? Spiff: Yeah, that one. Montgomery: Good grief. That's...that's sad. That was six months ago! Man, I'd completely forgotten about that! Hell, I think everybody else did. 'You see, it's like I said. He can't let go. Digital Mortality is convinced that he is A) the same guy he was all those months ago and B) that I'm the same guy I was all those months ago. Now Moraless, you know the guy I am now. And you know that a damned fine talent like yourself couldn't beat me without the help of Ruff Ryder and a brick. So if you, the more talented of the two, can't beat me one on one...and I'm quite comfortable in saying that Digital couldn't beat Ezra on Ezra's worst day, do you really think the two of you have a chance? 'See, truth be told Moraless, I like you. Believe it or not, I actually do respect you. That determination you were going on about, that's what I like most about you. I watch you go down to the ring, and I know that no matter what happens, the people will be getting a damned fine show. But what you need to understand is this. I have no intention of driving any kind of a wedge between you and Digital Mortality. There's a very simple reason for this. I won't have to. To me Moraless, you're money in the bank. You're gold...a veritable money making machine. To Digital Mortality, you're nothing but a means to an end. You're just a tool in Digimon's quest to recapture his glory days. This whole Resurrection thing isn't about bringing the UWF back to it's glory days. Never was. I mean hell, we're already there. What this is about, is an aging veteran trying to resurrect his career at the expense of new blood. That's what we are Moraless, the new blood. Ezra, Me, you, we are the wave of the future. Digital Mortality is just a dinosaur who can't accept the inevitability of change. 'Don't you think it a bit strange that he would have you come straight after me? Why not start with the Chaos Society? Or the Prime Alliance? Why not build yourselves up with a winning record before making the power grab. Do a little research Moraless. Go through the joke we call a video library here and learn a little something about your partner. Here, why don't I do you a favor and get you started? Here's a clip from an old 'Ask Dr. Stupid'.' Spiff: Well put Doctor. Our next question here is from little Kimmy in Memramcook New Brunswick. Kimmy says 'Dear Dr. Stupid. Is Digital Mortality obsessed with Mike Montgomery?' Wizzleteets: A truly excellent question Kimmy, and in response, yes. The little Digimon is very much obsessed with me...err...Mike, with Mike. You see, Digital Mortality is what we call a 'seasonal jobber.' He comes in, attacks someone in an effort to revive his long stalled career, gets beaten senseless, and disappears again. Digital Mortality has attempted to use Mr. Montgomery on several occasions in the past to get shots at title belts that he has never earned. The little Digimon has attempted to use Mr. Montgomery as a stepping stone in his career on numerous occasions, and unfortunately he still does not understand that it doesn't work. Digital Mortality is an idiot and has to move on with his life or he'll be come the next Barry Horowitz. 'Digital Mortality has always tried to use me to build up his career. If I was given a title shot, he immediately demanded to be in the match to. It never failed. Anything I earned, he decided he deserved. And now here we are with me running the show, having gained all the power that Digital Mortality would LOVE to have. All of a sudden I'm more powerful, more popular than he could ever hope to be, and it's driving him crazy. That's where you come in. Means to an end buddy. Means to an end. 'Like I said, I have no intention of driving any kind of a wedge between you two. Digital Mortality will be more than happy to do that for me. He will use you and then lose you. You're only good to him for as long as he needs you, and right now he needs you. You don't need him. He...needs...you. 'But I will say this. Ezra's right. You should join us. Do I honestly expect you to? Frankly no. That would be rather naive of me. What I expect is this. I expect you to swear loyalty to Digimon over and over again. I expect you to sit by and watch title shot after title shot pass you by because you're too busy helping Digimon to accept any shots of your own. You'll stand by him, second fiddle as he soaks up all the glory...glory that should be yours. And then, when he decides he doesn't need you, Digimon will bury you....and then you'll wise up, beat the hell out of him, and end up in the World Title picture or something. 'Frankly Moraless, as far as tag partners go...you can do better than Digital Mortality....hell you can do better than just about anybody on this roster. But either way, let me leave you with this one last clip...' Thai: Can't forget that... What about Mike Montgomery? Duc: Well, over a month ago the great NVA faced off against Mike Montgomery and Digital Mortality. Needless to say, Monty and his partner were easily crushed below the wait of the overwhelming superiority of the Local NVA Company. If Mike couldn't have beat the NVA then, what makes him think he can now? The NVA are unbeatable. End of story. Montgomery: Overwhelming superiority? You guys have never tagged with Digital Mortality! The words 'handicap match' mean anything to you? 'That's the thing about the past Digi, it can come back to bite you.'
