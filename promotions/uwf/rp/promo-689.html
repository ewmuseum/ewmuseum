UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #689

TV Title TWO

Sept 9 (year unrecorded)

Alex Grey — Limo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Martin’s limo still waits for me 30 minutes later when I step out the door. For the annoying little annoying bastard he can be Martin is gifted with seemingly unlimited patience, his news must have been important... Granted important news didn’t always mean good news... I still remembered Martin bailing me out jail to participate in the xCw, for me it was a get out of jail free card...a shot at my mortal enemy Lestatt Knight, and a chance to make more then my fair share of cash. And true to Martin’s promise all three things went off according to plan...with two big exceptions. One being Jeff Wylde... My “Brother” from GCW, we’d been part of the Revolution with Jason O’Neil and his mysterious brother Bryan Mayhem, to make a long story short The Revolution went to shit and GCW closed, everyone went their own way except for Jeff...who continued to live in the past, when he heard I’d joined xCw he came sniffing around like a shark who smelled blood. And there was plenty of blood to go around... More then enough blood for 3 people...which is where the second big exception came in. Moraless... When GCW closed he’d been nothing but a little punk, he’d won the TV title as the fed closed their doors and he was left there holding a title that didn’t mean shit to anyone...maybe he saw us fighting and we dredged up the past for him, whatever the reasons the four of us spent our weeks making each others lives hell. The xCw President was a sick man...he loved the sight of us tearing strips of each others hide off week after week, so he set us up for a Four corners of hell match, to this day I don’t recall the exact stipulations of the match...Boiling Tar and Broken glass were two big ones...but sadly the match never happened, xCw closed its doors right before the event was to take place. I continued to work for Martin after that...But history just seemed to repeat itself, Martins ideas were brilliant but the feds kept crumbling, HWC where I held the World Title Briefly...DWA Where I held their World Championship belt...closed...closed...closed...fucking closed. Martin could pick em but they never seemed to last, the only thing that had stayed strong was my undying hatred for Lestatt Knight, From GCW to DWA he was a constant thorn in my side, and though I hadn’t seen him in almost a year I still bore the initials he’d carved into my arm... L.K. A keepsake from a man I wanted to remember absolutely nothing about... I hadn’t seen Lestatt in almost a year...but whenever Martin popped up with a new job it seemed our paths were destined to clash again, as I approached the limo I silently hoped that whatever this UWF was...that it wouldn’t involve Knight...just this once let Knight go his way and me go mine. –The UWF– “Bout time...” Martin muttered as I entered the limo “Sorry to keep you waiting” “No...You’re not...I think you get off on keeping me waiting” “True on both counts” I confessed “Now...down to business...what do you have for me? And why should I sign the contract I know you’re going to stick in my face?” I knew Martin too well, watching him shuffle through a briefcase by his side I could only imagine what he had in store for me. “Here” he said handing me the usual thick stack of papers I’d come to dread and associate with getting hired onto a federation “Look it over” “Martin...” I said “You know I don’t understand shit about these things, that’s why you handle the contracts...why don’t you just sum it up for me” For a moment Martin looked offended, as if summing up that giant stack of papers was too much work, but then he remembered that summing up such things was his specialty and he smiled. “Look for as long as we’ve both been in this business we’ve been stuck in mediocre federations...HWC ring a bell?” “Sadly yes” “DWA?” “Yes...” “I’ve watched you for a long time Alex...You’ve been like a mule trying to get that carrot that’s been dangling in front of his face...that carrot was Lestatt Knight” “I’ll remind you...” I said folding my arms “That you encouraged me to paint the walls with Knights blood...you never tried to stop me” “Alex...lets not dredge up the past” Martin said with a dismissing wave “The point of this contract is to leave the past behind...no Lestatt Knight I promise” This intrigued me “Keep going...” I said “The UWF is a chance for you to start over without the shadow of Knight hanging over you, a chance for both of us to perform on a bigger stage...shit Alex the UWF is a bridge to the PTC! One of the biggest organizations in the business, this is a chance for the big time...don’t you want another chance?” The answer was a resounding yes, but just for a moment a small voice in the back of my head spoke up, chances are it was the voice of reason, something I’d done my hardest to push away years ago. What if you did quit right now? Walk away from it all? Take your money and retire to some quiet island somewhere, live out your days drinking something alcoholic out of a coconut shell and find a nice young Hawaiian mistress to fool around with... Go a step deeper...what if you put aside all those urges and tendencies, you’ve done some things you’re not proud of...you know the ring brings those out in you, so does Martin... Common sense provided a good argument, but in the end my own judgement won over, I’d sign Martins contract, damn it I’d make a deal with the devil to get back into that ring, standing on the sidelines “I do...” “Good...then I think you know what to do?” Taking the pen he offered I signed my name in the appropriate space, there was no fire, no brimstone, no maniacal laughter...Just Martins smile. Just like it’d had been so many times before. “Excellent” Martin said putting the contract away “This is your opponent” he said handing me a folder “His name is Kyle Cross...a Hardcore Legend in Japan...the UWF higher ups were drooling all over themselves to give this guy a contract, and when he showed up he won the TV title with little difficulty” “Thing is the TV title has to be defended every show, and it happens to be on the line this week when you fight him, if you can beat this guy and take this title it will be a one hell of a way to debut...if you can hold onto it long enough you’ll impress the right people” He made it sound so simple...but dangling a title in my face was always a mistake, Titles meant something in wrestling, they were an ego booster, and any sensible wrestler wanted that kind of ego boost, I wanted that title...right then and there I made up my mind, It was the first step to my new career, one without Lestatt Knight or the shadows of the past. I wanted that title... And I would do anything to get my hands on it... Anything... Martin recognized the look in my eyes, I think it unsettled him and pleased him at the same time. “Alright...” he said “We’ve lit the fuse...now it’s time to get to the gym and get you reacquainted with something called Exercise ...not that you’re out of shape” he added raising his hands as I glared. So thus it began, I found myself being transported via limo to Martins privately run gym. Visions of gold dancing in my head... Just like the old days.
