UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #612

The Beginning

11 Aug 2004

The Plague — Hotel in Tokyo

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens in The Plague's hotel room in Tokyo, Japan. He is sitting at the bar which is in his room. He is on the stole, you can see he has on black jeans. no shoes or socks, A black UWF shirt, his hair is tied back. He turns around in his chair and notices the camera. He grins, does a shot of Sake, and stands up wiping his mouth. He walks to the camera smiling the whole way. Plague seems very happy and relaxed. He starts nodding his head and he cracks his knuckles. He stops and you can see a bed behind him and an open window showing a view of the moring sky over Tokyo.] Plague: 'Well Jamie, looks like you lost, again. It's sad isn't it. I can be gone for months and still come back and beat you. Hey it may not have been my best victory, but number sixty is better than one. And Ryder, you'll get your repayment soon I gladly appreciate your kindness, I know you missed me and all, but the gift you gave me on Sunday, well, almost makes me shed a tear or two.' [Plague brings a finger up to his eye and wipes away an imaginary tear.] Plague: 'You see Ryder, I am hoping your still in the Rumble when I eventually emerge from the back. And it won't be a surprrise or random. No one will be shocked when I walk out from backstage at number sixty oh no. The whole arena will know, all of Japan will know, all of the whole goddamn world will know, it will be me, The Plague.' [Plague smiles, he seems really relaxed and happy.] Plague: 'Here in Japan, I feel at home. I see that Tsunami Oni is on his way here as well. I would really love to see Shiatsu again, it's been a while since I seen that hot ass. And Tsunami, you know it's true so you can't get mad. I mean look at Supremacy and that will prove my point.' [Plague licks his lips and grins.] Plague: 'I see how determined you are to recapture the Gold Tsunami, but come on man, give it up, can't you see your washed up, I mean you had to fight some really bad wrestlers on Sunday, minus Mercury, and that's not saying much. But you had to do all that just to get back into The UWF, look at your credentials, you shouldn't have to prove yourself. But me, I just happened to be at the right place at the right time, Japan, and Hayes saw me and said he wanted, actually needed me to come back to help make The UWF better, and look I'm back. I know you'll stand the test of time until I emerge into the Rumble, yup last, and I'll be sure to shatter your dream like you did to mine a while back.' [Plague clenches his fist.] Plague: 'Shock, we still are not finished, not by a long shot. But like you said this is a mission and like you I am going about it alone, and it seems our paths will cross again, possibly to determine a World Champ, maybe before that is all decided, who knows, but I felt the intensity in our match, and I am ready. Too bad you'll be long gone by the time I get out. Damn all the worthy opponents will not be there when I come out. And I thought this Rumble would be hard.' [Plague walks over to the bar in the hotel room and grabs some sake and drinks it.] Plague: 'This shit is amazing.' [Plague wipes his mouth.] Plague: 'Once again I am put in this situation, the very situation I was running from before. Damn One week and look where I am at. I am not even three seconds away from victory, just a throw of a body over a rope. And hell, I don't even have to worry about fifty-nine other guys, you all can fight over the bull shit yourselves. I'll be happy watching the bodies hit the floor. God I can't stand that phrase, but it will be true.' [Plague walks back from the bar and sits on the bed.] Plague: 'I'm number sixty, the last entry into the Regal Rumble, I guess that makes me the favorite don't it? Wow! I am so close to victory it is not even funny. Well it is funny, funny to see all the losers, and boy will it be a lot of losers, oh about fifty-nine of them. Hahahaha, boy I can't believe I ever left this behind.' [Plague folds his ands and bows his head.] Plague: 'You know UWF, you really don't know what I am going to do. I don't even know anymore. I mean I could very well come out and win the Rumble and go on and get that World Title Shot. Or I could fuck around and just crack some skulls like the old days. But I know one thing, I am more focused than ever before. I'm ready to once again prove why I was here since the beginning and almost reinvent myself to a new group of young talent. I really and taking a liking to this Dicky Flint, this guy has guts. But hey controversy gets you exposure and winning matches helps a lot too you know. This guy has potential, just as long as he doesn't decided to start preaching his white power shit to me, I'll knock his racist head off, but he can do his business, just stay out of mine. I'm beyond you buddy, I got a quest for Gold, I want that World Title, and if I can just keep going along this straight track and not get derailed, I will be seeing Josh Dunn, or whoever walks away as Champ in Japan, at Live and Loud.' [Plague brings a finger to his temple.] Plague: 'Well I better get training, I got a big night to look forward to on Wednesday. One week away, the intensity starts now. I'm ready, are you? [Scene fades out to black.]
