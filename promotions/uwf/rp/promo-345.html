UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #345

Extermination 101

16 Apr 2004

Raid — School building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens and you see Raid sitting backwards on a steel chair. He is wearing a white button up shirt, black pants and a black tie. He also has some papers in his hands and he is shuffling through them. He briefly looks up and sees the camera man, and then goes back to the papers.) Raid (without looking up) “Its about damn time you showed up! I got better things to do than to sit around and wait for your sorry ass! And now that you have made me wait I’m gonna make you wait, or if you would like you could sit in on my class and actually increase your knowledge, if that is even possibly.” (Raid gets up off of the chair and starts to walk out of the room. The camera man pauses for a second, and then follows Raid. As the camera man walks out of the door you can see Raid holding the door of a room. On the door you see “Extermination 101”. As the camera man walks into the room you can see that there is 10-15 people sitting behind tables. On the white board is written “Basics of Extermination”.) Raid “ Alright, shut up! Today were gonna get some real world experience. In the rooms next to us I have placed several mice, one of which is caller Ryder the other being Kennedy. Both of which have to be eliminated. The only thing I am going to require is that there lifeless corpses be brought back to me once you have expelled the life from their bodies. You can exterminate them any way you want to, but just remember I give bonus points for creativity. Now get the fuck out and kill those damn pests.” (With that all of the students scurry out of the room leaving only Raid and the camera man behind. Raid walks over to his desk and sits down. He begins to read through some of the papers on his desk. After a minute or so he realizes that the camera man is still there. He goes back to reading the papers ,and without looking up he says) Raid “ I suppose you want my to cut my promo now huh? Well I guess I could they should be awhile.” (He stands up and begins to walk around the room. As he nears the windows he sits on a desk and looks out the window and begins to talk.) Raid “First of all I want to say good luck to Pryde. Although Perisa is no match for someone of Prydes talent, the match is still going to be a hard fought one, but in the end Pryde WILL come out victorious. As for Dai and my match it is going to be one hell of a match. We have to face two of the UWF’s premiere superstars. HA! They wish they were superstars! Those two are no more than Jack and Will wanna bees. Those two couldn’t wrestle there way out of a pool filled with KY jelly. Skill? Yea they got skill but its to bad those skills of meat packing won’t help them this Sunday.” (Just as he says this the door of the room bursts open and in runs two of his students. In there hands they have the mice known as Ryder and Kennedy, both of which are militated beyond belief. They run over to the desk and set them down. Raid has a surprised look on his face, but he is definitely not excited.) Raid “So how did you get them?” (The two students both grin and hold up baseball bats. The bats are very bloody, in fact they obviously have more blood on them than is contained in two small mice.) Raid “Very creative! But why is there so much blood on them? You only killed the two mice, right?” Student #1 “Well you see, we kinda had sort of a problem with the students. So we kinda, umm, how should I say this, knocked the rest of the class out. I hope this wont affect our grade.” Raid “Affect you grade! Your damn right its gonna affect your grades! You both get an “A”. You two are the most creative students I have ever had. God I wish I would have thought of that when I was going through extermination school. Damn you two, ha you guys are great. Now get outta here, take the rest of the day off. Anyway where was I. This Sunday Ryder and Kennedy had better hope me and Dai go easier on them than my two “STAR” students went on the rest of my class today. If their lucky we’ll stop the slaughter before they fall into bloody piles. This Sunday the massacre will begin, and it won’t stop until the Chaos Society holds the tag team titles. So, to whoever holds the tag titles after this Sunday, Watch out cause we’re hunting for ya.” (With that Raid throws the two dead mice against the window and walks out the door. The camera slowly zooms in on the bloody streaks on the window and follows it down to the mice laying in a bloody pile on the floor. The scene fades to black.)
