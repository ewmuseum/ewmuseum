UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #504

Stable Wars Aftermath

5 Jul 2004

Adien Pryde — Local Sports Bar

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a what appears to be a sports bar. There are dozens of people milling around the various locations and in various stages of drunkenness. Over on the far side is a group of people at a table watching a baseball game on a big screen TV. To the left is another group, these 5 are quite a bit more drunk, they are playing one if those trivia type of games over the TV monitors, but it appears to be a drinking variation.] [Now to the right there is a large crowd gathered around a man at the bar. Lots of questions are flying from bar patron's toward the person in the middle of the group. That man is answering as many of the questions as he can.] Man One : So, what is your proudest moment in pro wrestling? It has to be winning the TV Title from a veteran like El Turtle. Pryde : Hell no. Stable Wars is by far the best time I have. Here take a look. Hey Jim, do you have that tape from the PPV? Jim the Bartender : Ya Adien, let me fast forward to your match. [The camera looks up at a TV that is now showing the Chaos Society vs. The Players Club match.] -----(Draco holds on for the pin 1.………………………..2.………….Persia comes in and makes the save. The referee turns and admonishes Persia. Persia is arguing with the referee for some reason. Draco picks Kazee up and drags him into the corner where Pryde and Raid both reach down and grab two steel chairs. Draco hold onto Kazee as Raid and Pryde nail Kazee with a Con-Chair-To type maneuver as the referees back is still turned. Kazee crumbles to the mat.)----- [The crowd gasp .] Pryde : Yea that had to hurt. -----[Adien Pryde kicks Joshua Dunn in the back of the knee as Dunn falls to one knee. Almost as soon as he falls though, Joshua sends a fist straight towards Adien’s gut. Adien doubles over as Joshua Dunn gets to his feet and lifts up Adien on to his shoulders.] Rick: He could be going for it…! Larson: The Tenn Dunn Salute! [Josh sets up Adien for the Tenn Dunn salute! The crowd gets ready to chant along with Dunn and ONE! TW-NO! Pryde wiggled his way out of the move!] Rick: PRYDE COUNTERED! [Pryde lands on his feet behind Dunn, Dunn turns around and walks straight in to The Falcon Driver~!] Larson: He hit his finishing move on Dunn! Dunn’s gone! YES! [Adien has no time to make the pin however as Kevin Starr charges towards him. Adien gets up just before Starr and kicks him in the shin with his steel toe boot… hard… Kevin begins to hop up and down on one leg in the middle of the ring rubbing his shin with his hands. Adien quickly clotheslines Starr to the ground.] Larson: Ahaha! That fool Starr! He’s gonna have a bruise there for weeks! Rick: Well the shin is a very sensitive spot Larson. Larson: I don’t care, Starr sucks. [Adien quickly turns to see Nick Persia sending a closed right fist his way! Adien Pryde ducks under it and tries to clothesline Persia again, but this time Persia’s ready and ducks under it. Adien turns around as Persia hits with a right, and another, and another! Persia’s on a roll! Adien is forced back towards the rope again. Persia goes to whip him into the ropes, but Adien holds on to the ropes and pulls Persia towards him. Adien crouches down and then lifts Persia up and over the ropes to the outside. Persia slams the mat hard on the outside.] Rick: Adien Pryde is on a roll here! He’s single handedly taking down The Player’s Club! Larson: He still gonna lose though. [Pryde walks over to Joshua who’s holding his head in pain from The Falcon Driver. Pryde goes to lift him up but Josh lifts his hand up and rakes the eyes of Adien Pryde. Pryde staggers back, blinded from the rake.] Rick: A veteran move there by Joshua Dunn. [Josh quickly jumps up, obviously faking that his head hurt, and runs towards Adien who’s grabbing his eyes… but Adien to was faking the injury as he surprises Josh and quickly steps over to the side, out of the way of Josh’s fist, and grabs Josh’s upper body, stopping him in his tracks as he hits the Side Effect!] Rick: What a counter!------ Pryde : See here, this is what I am talking about. At any time, any member of The Chaos Society can kick any member of The Players Clubs ass like here, hell, I am kicking Persia's, Starr's, and Dunn's ass all by myself. These guys think they are all great wrestlers when one man, ME, can do that to them. -----[Adien gets up and walks over to Dunn for the pin, but just as he bends down, BOOM~! Kevin Starr out of nowhere hits him with the Starr Struck!] Rick: SPEAR! KEVIN STARR HIT THE STARR STRUCK! Larson: …what the hell is with him and lame names? Honestly… [Kevin Starr rolls on top of Adien and makes the pin One Two Three! [Adien Pryde is eliminated!]----- [Boos erupt from the gathered crowd.] Pryde : Yea I know. But as you can see, the numbers finally became to much for me and that eliminated me. I don't know about you but to me, they got lucky. [Jim stops the tape and turns on the same baseball game the other TV is on.] Man Two : So Pryde, what you going to do about The Players Club. They get shots at any titles they want but I think you just proved the you and the rest of The Chaos Society are the better stable. Pryde : They aren’t worth the trouble. Let them have their title shots. Everyone can see that they are coming apart at the seams. Starr and Dunn can't stand each other and Kazee has been prone to injury since he lost his title to Raid 'The Evil Exterminator'. I need to look toward my immediate future. Pryde : Angel and Green Demon. Heaven and hell. I guess that puts me in purgatory. Why the UWF put me in this match I will never know, but come Wednesday at Underground, I will use these two to start my rampage to the top of this federation. Some would call them stepping stone on my way up but I prefer to call the cannon fodder to be obliterated for others to see how dangerous I can be. Pryde : Hey Jim, a round for everyone here. I'm buying. Crowd : Yay, Pryde Pryde Pryde. [The scene fades to black]
