UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #192

THC IS MINE!

27 Jan 2004

Bruce Gordy — The Hardcore Grocery Store

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Scene opens up at a grocery store. Suddenly, a nerdy clerk is thrown across a check out counter and into a shelf. Bruce Gordy walks up into the camera-shot.) Bruce Gordy: Now I'm not the one checking out... you are! You know why? Because I have finally done what I've always wanted to do in my entire career. That one goal, that I have now achieved, was to win the THC title. I had tried numerous times over my career in GIWF and UWF, and now I finally have it, my precious THC title belt. And now, there's already people who want to take it away from me. (The clerk starts to get up, he's a whussy and Gordy grabs him by the thraot and picks him up and gets all in his face.) Bruce Gordy: Do you know what I do to those you try and take things from me? That's right, I show them pain! (Gordy gorilla press slams the grocer through a cardboard cut out of the jolly green giant.) Bruce Gordy: You are not worthy to be beaten up by the UWF THC champion, The Innovator of Pain. I could innovate pain on you right now, but you're just a nerdy clerk boy. go mop up isle 4, you slutty kid. (The kid leaves.) Bruce Gordy: Now, to busyness. Beelzebozo whats to start something with the SOLE SURVIVOR OF THE GREATEST STABLE OF ALL TIME... Then let him come, I am not afraid. I'm laughing, clwons are funny, not scary, you want to see fear? Look into the face of the Innovator of Pain and see the tormented soul of an outcast who will reak havok on your body mind and soul and destory your very being all the way down to your clown kidneys. I am the sole suvivor, the element of destruction, and I will take on anyone, even Beelzebozo the wrestler with the stupidest name ever in the dictionary of the united nations. This title is mine fair and square. bring it on, i hear youre really Gurgles, well if thats so, then even better. You see Gurgles you never gave me a world title shot in GIwf because you were scared of me, but now, now ther's no escaping. (Bruce Gordy takes a paper grocery bag and rips it in half.) Bruce Gordy: Do you see the power? Can you feel it? This is the pain of the Bruce Gordy. Ben Jericho , you will feel this two. Sleep tight, I don't even have to talk about you to beat you. Sleep tight.
