UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #243

Anarchy goes down!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. Ryder is sitting in his black leather recliner watching the recent promos from the UWF, which is as close to studying anyone's moves that he will be doing for this upcoming match, becaue Anarchy doesn't deserve his time. He stops the tape after a few seconds and a look of disgust comes across his face. He throws down the remote and turns toward the camera. ) Ryder: Is that it? That's all you two idiots have to say? Please! You two aren't even halfway good enough to face a team like Ruff Ryder and Malcom Kennedy. We are gonna abuse and embarass you both, but hey if you wanna get punked out on national television that's your call. Make no mistake about it though, you are gonna get destroyed if you show up for this match. I don't care what you've done in other places and I don't give a damn what titles you've won, because you're lookin at a former World Champion, and a soon to be two time World Champion, because i'm taking that belt back in the near future. * Ryder shows the camera his famous cocky grin * Ryder: I hope that you two don't seriously think that you can stand in the ring toe to toe with us and survive. I mean come on, we are the best the UWF has to offer, and you two are just common scrubs that they put in front of us. Scheduling a match with you is like giving us the night off, because we kill you in two seconds flat. Oh well I guess that will be good enough. Onto the next part of my promo. * Ryder goes into serious interview mode * Ryder: Let me explain something to you Malcom. I didn't disband the Prime Alliance, Jamie Shock did that. He walked out on us and left us high and dry. It fucked me up in a pretty bad way, because the man was my best friend. I had no desire to form any stables after that, but I realized that if we let the Player's Club and assholes like em run this place then we'd live to regret it. Think about it, you're their sworn enemy, as am I, do you think they'll give us title shots? Do you think they'll allow us to get where we want to be? We owned the UWF once before and I think it's time to bring the place back home to the Prime Alliance. I know it's a tough decision for you, as you've established yourself as the one man army, but it's your decision to make. If you don't come home then i'll completely understand and no hard feelings will come your way, but I really hope you'll join me in my climb back to UWF superiority. The Prime Alliance will come out on top once again, I hop you're apart of it my friend. I will be waiting for your answer. ( Ryder's serious look continues as the scene fades out and the Prime Alliance logo is shown )
