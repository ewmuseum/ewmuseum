UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #632

Women and weaknesses

14 Aug 2004

Mr. Meaner — Prison Cell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside of a prison cell, at least it seems that way. Sitting there on the bench is Mr. Meaner. All in black as always. He is just sitting there with a ring of keys which he is spinning on his arm. He also has several pictures of girls on his wall. All over the place. And they are all familiar. Soviette, Ayami, and Joanna. Mr. Meaner moves to the side so that you can see all of them.) Mr. Meaner: So you’re going to try and explain all the reasons you shouldn’t be in jail, are you Communist? Wow, how stupid is that. You might as well face your destiny. You are going to be in Jail and there isn’t anything you can do about it. But I’ll talk to my friends in the DA office. Maybe they can cut you a deal. I mean you are going to need it….. Face the facts dude, you beat on some people. And while I do admire you for it, you need to separate the difference from the wrestling world to the real world. In the wrestling world, you can hit people with sledgehammers and chair and glass tables. In the real world, you can’t. It’s called assault and battery, and it’ll get you some time. But your to stupid to realize that, aren’t you bastard? And Sunday, on PPV, in Tokyo, you can bet that they are all going to be used against you. You can bet that I will be more than happy to introduce them all to you in full force. You and that do gooder Angel should be making appointments to get splinters and glass removed from you carcasses, because you are going to have plenty of it…… You guys are doing it in the name of love and mentor ship. You guys are doing it for women. Isn’t that so sweet. All over a girl. You stupid f**ks. A woman will keep you down. You’re weak because you need the woman to motivate you. Wow. Maybe you should think about dumping the sluts because that’s all they are. The only position they truly know well is flat on their back…… And that makes you weak. That makes you worthless. That makes you more panty waist then every single competitor out here. And that is your downfall. That is what is going to lose you the match. That is what is going to lose you the THC Title. And that is what is going to lose you your edge in any match you will ever have…… But don’t feel bad. You really shouldn’t. I mean this is the THC Championship we are talking about her. It’s a belt that has eluded me all these years even though I would be perfect for it. Just think, I can use anything I want whenever I want and just beat on them with no rules. My cup of tea right there. So at least you’ll know that you lost the THC Championship to a future hall of famer, you piece of scum……. At RR, I’ll be able to win the THC Championship, the TV Championship, and the RR and go on to win the World Championship at Live and Loud. Think about it everyone. Mr. Meaner will single handily hold every title there is to hold in the UWF, because you all are wusses and I am just that damn good…… Angle, Communist, go away. This isn’t good for you. And if you can’t go away, well, just do it anyway, because we really don’t want to see you get hurt. (scene fades to black.)
