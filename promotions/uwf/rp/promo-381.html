UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #381

Flames of Glory

1 May 2004

Raid — Parking Lot

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens and you see a man standing in a vacant lot, but you see him from above. There are some street lights that are on but it is overall dark. In the midst of the darkness you see a gleam of light and the light seems to be coming from the man’s shoulder. As the camera zooms in on the man it is apparent that the man is none other than the T.V Champ, Raid. He looks up into the camera and he has a sinister look on his face, but the look soon morphs into a smile. The T.V title is draped over his shoulder and he is playing with a Zippo tm. Lighter.] “When I began my as an exterminator I felt as though I could accomplish something. Truthfully I had no idea what I was to accomplish, but none the less I gave it my all and succeeded.” [You see Raid begin to walk around the lot, still from an overhead view. He walks over to one of the light poles and picks up a medium sized red container. He then walks back into the middle of the lot.] “So then I asked myself, ‘what more could I do, what else could I accomplish?’, and the answer came to me one night while I was watching Television. I was to become a wrestler! So for the next two years of my life I busted my ass on the independent circuit. While there I met a man who changed my outlook on life, that man was none other than Mr. Meaner! He showed me how ruthless and brutal I could be. He showed me that the extermination business is very similar to the wrestling industry. In both you have to eliminate your problems by any means necessary.” [Raid takes the cap off of the red jug and begins to walk around the parking lot.] “This was my key to success. With him by my side there was no obstacle I couldn’t over come. Then one day the backstabbing bastard went to the big leagues, leaving me in his dust. This, however, did not stop me from climbing to the top of the independents. After realizing that the pests of the minors were no longer a challenge, I made it my goal to take revenge on the man who I once called my friend.” [Raid is now making a big circle in the lot, still the can.] “My desire for revenge is now burning hotter than ever. I am getting close to striking at the heart of my enemy, and when I get my chance at him, my burning rage will just add more fuel to the fire!! And he will feel the heat of the flames that are burning inside me.” [Raid has now made a full circle in the lot, but he continues to walk.] “But before I can reach that goal I have other pests to deal with. One of which is a man called Dante. Now I don’t know much about this man, save for the fact that he thinks he is worthy of MY T.V title! Unfortunately for you Dante there isn’t a man alive who could take this title away from me. I am willing to walk through the fires of Hell to keep this belt, so don’t think that a pest like you has even an ounce of a chance to win it. I am looking forward to the massacre that is going to take place this Sunday at SAS, when I face you.” [Raid now is back in the center of the lot, and he is again playing with a lighter.] “Pryde! Kill the lights!” [As he says that all of the street lights explode, and the parking lot is pitch black except for the flame of the lighter that Raid is holding.] “Dante, this Sunday your dreams of being the T.V champ are going to go up….” [He then drops the lighter and the ground bursts into flames. The fire begins to spread away from him and the camera pans out. Raid then starts laughing chaotically as you see a giant flaming anarchy symbol on the ground. The scene fades to black.]
