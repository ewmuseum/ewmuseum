The Lonesome Hotel

16 Jan 2004

Iron Lung

The Lonesome Hotel There comes a time in everyone’s life where they have to stop and look at themselves in the mirror. Sometimes, they don’t even know what they look like any longer. Their old age shows through wrinkles, their reputation with the common folk shows through black eyes and broken noses. But the one thing nobody can catch coming in the mirror, is the reaper behind them. It can’t be stopped, time will keep beating on without us. It’s just a fact of life, and some can’t come to realize that the world doesn’t revolve around them, until it’s too late. My belief was always that there was no death, and that we were all dead already. This was just a dream, not a reality. This was just a manipulation of our minds, a world full of war and hatred towards each other, all because of sins our predecessors committed. Now I looked in the mirror this fateful night, and I saw my own reflection. Eyes of fire were staring back at my chiseled face, the face of a warrior. I hadn’t looked this dismantled since I left the world I was born into, only to come to this limelight. This business took the hearts of many men and women, and all that was left was the black ashes. Was there no room left for a kind-hearted individual? If he or she were to cross the boundaries, would their soul be taken too? What would the future hold for the one who dared trespass against our creators will? Time would only tell. But for now, I, the weary traveler, was settled in for a one night stay at… The Lonesome Hotel You would think that I would have arrived in my accustomed dismal weather, but the moon did shine fully the night I pulled up to my lodging. And when I first pulled up to this hotel, I could have sworn that it was the exact same picture that I had seen in the traveling brochure. It was the hotel that the areas own lovely lounge singer, Sidney Timmons, used. Sidney was known throughout the county as having one of the most angelic of voices. Of course, not knowing anything about the art of lounge singing, I thought maybe I would catch a glimpse of this potential damsel. After checking into the hotel and receiving my room key (room #713, my favorite), I strolled to the lounge with a soft tap in my step. I sat down in a lounge sofa, it was just as comfortable as what I was used to in my room. Within minutes, I was completely relaxed, and the company around me wasn’t too bad either. And then, as I was just about to order a club soda, there she was. She stepped out in a stunning midnight blue dress, complete with glitter all around it. My jaw almost dropped as I saw her golden locks and the most elegant pair of, get this, greenish-hazel eyes. I felt like the cartoon wolf with my tongue hitting the ground, but I quickly had to regain my composure as I took my club soda. I had almost spilt it all over my lap, and that wouldn’t have been exactly the best impression for yours truly. It was true. From the moment she parted her lips, she carried a canary-like tune that echoed across the room, giving me pins and needles all down my arms. I didn’t know if it was just because I had stepped in from the sub-freezing temperatures, or because I was experiencing the chance of love at first sight. I had to know more about her. So, when the show was over and everyone had given her the appropriate curtain call, I approached her swiftly. I didn’t follow her backstage, but rather gently tapped her on her shoulder before she was to exit. Her smile lit up the room, and I couldn’t help but smile back and stumble for my words as she gave a slight giggle for my efforts… Hey Hi there stranger, haven’t seen the likes of you around here lately. That’s because I just checked in. But I all ready feel like I’m part of the family. Aw, that’s sweet of you to say. You have the most magnificent voice, have you ever tried to bring it out for millions to hear? Hmm, haven’t thought about it really. I sort of get nervous around this small crowd of ten, I couldn’t imagine doing it with millions watching me! Well, if it’s any consolation to you, I think you’d fit in great. Aw, thank you so much. That’s sweet of you to say. Do you say that to all the lounge singers? I certainly don’t ma’am, you’re the only one that I’ve approached. Hmm, you seem like you’re quite the ladies man. Why don’t you take a seat right where you were, and I’ll go change into something more comfortable. Then, I’ll come back and we can share a drink, my treat. That sounds great to me. All right then, I’ll be right back. Don’t miss me too much. I’ll try not to. It felt like I was waiting for her for hours, and the time couldn’t come quickly enough to see her again. But suddenly, I saw her amazing figure come towards me once more, somehow looking even more amazing in a white halter top and washed out low riding jeans. She sat right next to me, her hands inches away from my legs, and I couldn’t help but give a ravishing smile. So, you look awfully familiar. Do you get that a lot? Indeed I do, but it’s because I am awfully familiar. It feels like that I’ve known you forever, but I don’t even know your name. My name is Robert, but everyone calls me Rob. Well, it is always a pleasure to meet a fan Robert. To be honest with you Miss Timmons, I only became a fan a minute ago. You’re just too funny, and also charming mister… Pendragon, Robert Alexander Pendragon to be exact. Hmm, I still say that you look very familiar. But, enough riff raff, shall we have a toast? Why Miss Timmons, you have read my mind. With a snap of Sidney’s fingers, a waiter quickly made his way over to our seats. Yes Miss Timmons, what will you drink this evening. I think tonight, I’ll have a little variety in my life. What champagne do you suggest? Perhaps the new Brut Imperial Rose, it’s a French champagne from Moet & Chandon. That sounds like an excellent choice Martine. Please do provide us with two glasses. Surely Miss Timmons, it will come right away. Martine rushed back to service Sidney, and while he was gone she couldn’t help but start to giggle a little bit while looking at me. What’s wrong? You look like you’re shaking in your boots. Don’t be nervous, I don’t bite that hard. Giving me a quick wink, I just had to smile and chuckle a little too. Sorry, it’s just that I haven’t been with such delightful company in a very long time. Hmm, well maybe after this delightful company, we can go back to my room and I will be glad to give you a private showing. If the champagne is good, then you’ve got yourself a deal Miss Timmons. Mm good, I like the way you think Mr. Pendragon. She came closer to me and put her hand on my cheek, rubbing it gently. I was putty in her hands, and just as she was about to kiss me, Martine had arrived and put the bottle of Brut Imperial Rose on the table. He then put two glasses on the table with a smile, before tipping his hat and leaving once more. Martine is a good friend of mine, there’s never a charge when he’s around. That sounds like my kind of friend. He is a great friend, but not great with words or with company. You on the other hand… I would say the same for you Miss Timmons. Well then, here’s to a fun filled night. Yes, here’s to a fun filled night. We lightly tapped our glasses together. And yes, the champagne was delicious. It was like I was in a field of wild strawberries, or something. After we were done with our glasses, we were feeling quite warm. Taking my hand into hers, Sidney quickly pulled me up and escorted me to her hotel suite. And, you know the rest of the story. Or perhaps you can leave it up to your imagination. I will tell you one thing though, this definitely wasn’t a one night stay at… The Lonesome Hotel
