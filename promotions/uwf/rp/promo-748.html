Your New Best Friend...

20 Oct 2004

'The Savior' Wes Daible

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Eyes open. Eyes close. Eyes open. Eyes close] [Inbetween the blinks we can only see the back of someone apparently staring in a mirror, but yet we cannot nseem to get a clear look at the front of him. His hair is long and dark, almost jet black, but looks to be slicked back nicely. He's a got a pair of jeans on, somewhat faded as is the fashion and a pair of black shoes on his feet, the brand is unidentified. A black windbreaker guards his upper body, which fills it out fairly nicely. From this distance we can assume his height to be about six foot two, give or take an inch] [Eyes open. Eyes close.] [Eyes open.] 'Richard!' [This man has now given us a full view of his front. He looks to be caucasian with a very attractive face hidden behind some slight facial hair. His chin is somewhat pointed, but nothing you would notice unless you were looking for it. The structure of his face is symetric and making him appear very attractive. Blue irises are invaded by slight red viens on each side] 'Yes sir!' [With a smile that lights the room, but yet one that leads us to assumptions about what is about to go on] 'Are the dogs ready?' [Richard is a short man of about five foot two, once again give or take an inch. He wears a black suit with a bow tie around his neck. His age is tough to identify because he has such a young face, but we would guess either middle-aged or slightly younger. His brown hair is cut short and parted down the middle, his face combined with the hair bear resembalance to the typical English schoolboy. His eyes are a hazel shade show the submissive nature of his work] 'Yes sir.' 'Fetch them for me, will you?' [Richard nods quickly before hurrying off] 'Certainly sir.' [This man finally manages to find the time to acknowledge the camera and does so only passing as he digs into his pockets really quickly, he nods when he feels something in them and then begins to walk past us. Our cameraman stumbles slightly, but is able to give us a continued shot of the man walking through, what is apparently his home] [The hallway we were just in was somewhat compact with the mirror in the corner where it turns to the left and leads to combination of three doors, one of which is obviously due to the structure a closet. The walls are painted an off-white shade and a circular light hangs overhead] [The dining room is next, painted an light blue color with a large glass table shaped much in the style that a wooden table would be. Its impressive to say the least... There is a wall unit that is mohogany sitting on the far wall and a tea cart on the oppoisite side] [And into the living room, which is colored a light green with a fireplace on the wall to the right. A wall unit displaying all sorts of strange figurines sits on both sides of the faireplace. Another furniture unit houses a flat screen TV with surround sound and a large sterio system on the level above it. The couch wraps around a coffee table until it reaches a set of stairs, in between the coffee table and television unit is where we find this man. He turns to us] 'It really is so hard to find good help these days.' [He says in smart-aleck tone with a grin forming on his face. This man's grin just reeks of something, like the grin on the face of the guy at the bar who just stole your prized piece of ass within a matter of words after you wasted all your money on her drinks... Yeah that sums it up...] [Almost on cue, Richard enters with two very large German Sheperds leading the way. Such proud, beautiful animals, but yet vicious because of the unfamilarity with the cameraman. They are almost identical except the one on our left has black running all around his back, while the one on the right only has it down his spinal area] [Richard walks them over or they walk Richard over to the man. The dogs seem to have eased their aggression for some reason, we're guessing its that unspoken language between a dog and their owner] 'My apologies sir, but I must ask a question.' [Handing over the leashes. The man nods as to say 'what is it'] 'Do you happen to know what I should make for Miss Vivian's ten o'clock breakfast?' [He ponders for a moment] 'Cheerios.' [Walking away with the satire of that comment reeking up hios path as he leaves the servant standing there. The face on the servant is admirable, for he remains stern despite the humiliation] [Thats the last we will see Richard though as we go out the front door, right next to the wall unit. Once outside the man reaches into his pocket and busts out the pack of cigarettes he was searching for before. The dogs stay amazingly quiet and still as he does this] [Lights it and takes a drag. Then starts to walk down the steps and down the path] 'I must apologize for my rudeness' [Half-heartedly] 'My name is Wes Diable and I'm your new best friend' [Taking another drag as we turn right onto the surburban street] 'Why you may ask am I your new best friend? Why?' [A chuckle] 'Simply put the once proud tradition of this business has eroded away faster then Jesus on the cross' [Wow.] [Another drag like he doesn't even realize how many people he's insulted] 'And I'm the one thats going to become your savoir, your new bright star. The pedestal is there waiting for me, calling to me. And whether or not you would like to admit to yourself, I am the one who is set to take that throne. I've got everything you could want...' [He turns to the camera holding his arms out with a giant smile on his famous, cigarette in mouth. This guy has got to be kididng himself, right? Well, at least he's got one person convinced] 'I'm not one those poor souls that have been in the business for far too long, who I think should've joined the dinosaurs in extinction by now. I'm not one of those wind-bags, who could put to sleep a hyper-active eight year old with their clips. I'm not those that have tried to revive their past glory for the sake of this dying business nor am I the failures of the present, who could just never reach the pinnacle that I was born to take' [The dogs stop to do their thing and he takes another drag] 'Now you may ask what're my credentials? Where have I been? And what right do I have to make these claims?' [Removing the cigarette and he releases the smoke like a fire breathing dragon, his ablaza with a confidence that could make Yao Ming feel small] 'I am who am.' [He pauses for this comment just to sink in. The Bible freaks are getting their shotguns ready to hunt this guy down. Fear not Wes, this location is unknown... But I really don't he'd be afraid to begin with] 'I am otherworldly in prowess and ability. Which if you don't believe... You will. Whether its on the videotape, in the ring, or hell outside this very house. I'll make you believe it, I'll make you live the experience of a lifetime, I'll make you recite my words back to me...' [There is a rising seriousness in his tone, which was formerly just a friendly talk and joke has now apparently become a proclamation of greatness. His pace also quickens as the dogs are done and they start walking again] 'Then when you look up at me, I'll smile and you'll understand that... YOU HAD NO IDEA WHO YOU WERE FUCKING WITH!' [A drag of the cigarette and a momentary pause] 'But I digress for I don't want to say too much, there are things you will see from me that you won't believe... But in time you may find yourself changing your faith, changing your address, changing your hairstyle, changing even your name! Just based on what you experience through that television set, in the arena, and in that ring...' [One more seemingly trademark smile that reeks of a man that knows how to start a fire without a matches. A man who knows more then he lets on] 'You will be saved' [End transmission]
