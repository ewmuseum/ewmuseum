UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #626

... to the tag titles!

14 Aug 2004

The Legends — It's a location

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Static.] [Static.] 'Why isnt this bloody thing working?' [Static.] 'You're the Legend, you fix it' [Static.] [Static.] [We see two faces staring blankly into the camera, Luke Warren and Lance Rose infact. They both smile as they see the flashing red light on the camera.] Luke: It's working! We've done it! [We cut to static.] 'Bugger!' [Static.] [Static.] [We see the two men again. They are whacking the camera as it is on the table. Lance Rose stops Luke from whacking the camera again, before he kneels down so we see his face on the screen.] Lance: Wait! It's working again! [Rose slowly backs away and sits on the floor.] Luke: Oh you have to be joking. I'm not spending minutes of my life sat on the floor for a stupid camera. Lance: Just sit down my friend. This wont take long. [Warren slowly sits down next to his tag team partner.] Lance: Hello. I am Lance Rose, a hardcore champion, a shoot wrestling champion, and a tag team champion. On Sunday, we, myself and Luke Warren, The Legends, plan to win the latter for a second time when we face two other UWF tag teams, the drowning rats and the Formadios. Luke: What silly names. Lance: Please join us for a moments silence to thank Mr Richard Hayes for hiring us again, and giving us this oppertunity to reclaim former glory. [Rose stops as Warren takes over.] Luke: It's not as if we need it though. See, the trouble with the other tag teams in the UWF, and wrestlers too, is that they take their jobs too seriously. Now, an championship is used, lets be honest here, as an ego boost. It's something wrestlers win when they want to go on perade. It's something wrestlers win, to show off. Lance: We're different. Luke: See, we dont use titles to show off and use them as a way to say 'I'm better than you' to the other wrestlers in the back like others. Oh no, we use them as.. titles come with tv time. Lance: As we are role models, we need public attention. Being a champion of the UWF guarantees public attention, obviously. It's also said that champions get the fans respect.. although it's not been true in the past you never know when that might change. See, as role models, we need titles to communicate with the fans. To our 'flock'. Luke: The fans of the UWF are all sheep, and they go around the basis of their lives being told what to do. So how about, for once, some responsible decent good men tell them what to do, in the interest of making this planet a better place for everyone. [They both smile.] Lance: Public interest is in our hearts ladies and gentlemen. At the end of the day we're not wrestlers, we're just two kind Legends who are out to help the common society. We care, honest. Luke: That's right, we do. Unfortunatly winning the tag team titles seems vital for us to communicate with the people we wish to 'save'. Without titles, get this; we're apparently nothing but losers. In this society, two perfect specimins of the human race are called l-o-s-e-r-s. It hurts. It hurts our reputation and it hurts, it hurts inside ladies and gentlemen. Lance: But when we're hurt, the result hurts everyone else. Things could be catastrohpic if we werent the hard men god made us to be. Oh no. Luckily, we are. We can take blows. We've learnt to live against the elements. [Rose turns to Luke, who clasps his hands together.] Luke: We're going out on Sunday to earn the respect teams in the past stole from us. Richard Hayes has changed his mind about us, soon everyone else will too. We're back, and we're going to make a difference ladies and gentlemen. The Legends, the executers of the Rose Perfume and the Peanut Skinner, will earn the respect of you people at home, and once again shall lay down what is right and wrong. Lance: In a week or two, whether you like it or not, the arena crowd will be joining hands, and uniting, all singing together ... our names. Because, drowning and formians, we are Legends. You two teams are common scum. Withdraw from the match or be a pawn in our little game of role model chess. [They both stand up.] Both: Legendious~! [Static.] [Static.] 'I think that went pretty well..'
