UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #361

Short on ideas, but gotta get the ball rolling...

24 Apr 2004

Mike Montgomery — The UWF Restaurant

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike Montgomery sits alone in the UWF Restaurant, across the street from UWF Headquarters in Chicago. It's the post SAS part, various wrestlers can be seen milling about the establishment. Montgomery, however, sits at a table, staring at the far wall where various pieces of UWF hardware is displayed. Trophies, costumes of former wrestlers, packets of the ~~WHITE POWDER OF DEATH DOOM AND DESTRUCTION~~ and title belts dating back to the days of the IWF. A placard over the GIWF belts reads 'On loan from Asylum Industries,' the company became owners of those pieces of UWF history after buying them at a 'Gimmick Garage Sale.' Montgomery's gaze, though, appears focused on the UWF belts. Open doors so I walk inside Close my eyes Find my place to hide And I shake as I take it in Let the show begin I remember, late last year, getting that invite for the Universal Title Tournament. There was no real reaso nfor me to enter, but I figured what the hell? Show the world what I can do. So I signed up. Before I knew what was happening, I was facing Jay Phoenix in the first round. I couldn't believe it. Jay Phoenix...a legend...a hall of famer...practically a wrestling God. A man who was light years ahead of me on the wrestling ladder. I was a mid-carder at best, yet he could headline any show he wanted to. Didn't matter though because when that bell rang, there I was staring across the ring at a living legend. Once the match started, all those thoughts were forgotten. It was me against another man, fighting for supremacy. We went at each other tooth and nail, no quarter asked, none given. In the end, Jay prevailed, but it didn't matter. I could hold my head high knowing that he had to earn the victory. I had given everything I had for what I thought was the biggest match of my career. Open my eyes Just to have them close again Well on my way But on my way to where I've been Back in December, almost as soon as I got back from the Universal Title Tournament, I started one of my own. A tournament to crown the greatest cruiserweight in UWF History. In the second round I faced Jay Phoenix yet again. Again we came at each other with everything we had. Hell, we really didn't know any other way to go. It ended up a time limit draw and I thought that was the biggest match of my career. Then came the tournament finals. I wasn't facing one legend, but three. Jay Phoenix, Seymour Almasy...believed to be the greatest champion in UWF History, and the Devil from the Deep Blue Sea, Tsunami Oni...said to be the greatest superstar the UWF has ever known. I didn't win then, but I really didn't need to. Sometimes just getting into the ring and giving your best effort is enough. That was exactly what I did, and that match was considered one of the greatest matches seen in a long, long time. A career maker. I thought *that* was the biggest match of my career. It swallows me As it takes me in it's fog I twist away As I give this world a nod Then came this past January. Jamie Shock, Malcolm Kennedy and Ruff Ryder. They claimed to be the most powerful stable in UWF History. As founder of the Player's Club, I couldn't allow that claim to stand. It was a best of three series. Ryder and Kennedy quickly fell to my fellow players Ezra Kazee and Joshua Dunn. Then came the main event. Me against Jamie Shock, a man was probably, with maybe the exception of Tsunami Oni, one of the best heels the UWF had ever known. If I won, it would be a clean sweep. If Shock won, well, the Alliance wouldn't have been completely embarassed. The bell rang, and once again I was fighting for my life in the biggest match of my career. But this time...this time things would be different. I still remember the echo of Jamie Shock's body hitting the mat, the arena deathly quiet as everyone stared in, well, shock. Open doors so I walk inside Close my eyes Find my place to hide And I shake as I take it in It wasn't supposed to happen that way. Everyone said I wasn't in Jamie's league. Everyone expected Jamie to take me out with his crowbar and pick up the easy win. He's a hall of famer they said. He's the best in the company they said. That night they didn't have anything to say. All they could do was stare in awe as I stood over the unconcsious form of our then Commissioner. But I wasn't done yet. I had a promise to deliver, a promise of Jamie's Fall From Grace. Slowly, the crowd turned. I could hear the cheers building up for me as I climbed to the top rope. When I leapt into the air, flashbulbs went off and the crowd roared. I landed, driving the air from Jamie's lungs, and three seconds later picking up the biggest victory I'd ever had. The Prime Alliance was no more and Jamie Shock's career ended that night. Ended at my hands. I was sure *that* was the biggest match of my career. Let the show begin Open my eyes Just to have them close once again Don't want control As it takes me down and down and down again Then came the Clash. Raid and Ezra Kazee against Malcolm Kennedy and I. With that booking, I saw something from the Administration I hadn't seen in a very, very long time. Fear. They feared the Player's Club. It didn't matter though. With my victory over Jamie Shock, I had something I hadn't truly had in a long time. Power. I wouldn't allow them to break us up. Ezra and I teamed up, and in the end, I had something I hadn't had in a long, long time. Gold. Is that the moon Or just a light that lights this dead end street? Is that you there Or just another demon that I meet? Now I'm the one on top. I'm the one with the power, the influence. I only have one thing more to do. I still can't believe it though. I've always known that losing to Digital Mortality was good for my career. I've never understood that, but I've always known it. During our first go around, if I lost to him, I'd be main eventing the next week's show...or I'd have a shot at the TV title...or my two Shoot Title runs? They both came after losses to Digital Mortality. But this...never in a million years would I have expected this. The higher you are The farther you fall The longer the walk The farther you crawl My body my temple This temple it tilts Step into the house that Jack built A shot at the World...Heavyweight...Title. That's something I have almost never had. Back in the days of the Wimp and Chimp Connection Mark 4 Stroke 9, the Administration tried to break us up. They put me in a match against Pete the PG Tips Chimp. It was non-title and I refused to fight it. Then, the day of the show, the title went on the line. I still refused. I walked out to the ring, laid down, and let Petey have the 1..2..3. Then I announced that I was suing the company for breach of contract. I always figured that one day I'd get another shot and now here it is. The higher you are The farther you fall The longer the walk The farther you crawl My body my temple This temple it tilts Yes this is the house that Jack built No stablemates to have to fight, nothing to keep me from walking into that ring and going after our champion with everything I have. But this is Beelzebozo. The psychotic clown. The man who may or may not be Gurgles, one of the most ruthless, sadistic, cruel, and just plain evil clowns to ever set foot in a ring. But Beelzebozo has one claim to fame, aside from having the World Title. One thing that sets him apart from every other man and woman on the roster right now. He ended the legend of Seymour Almasy. Beelzebozo is the man who took the world title from the greatest champion in UWF History. Seymour was never the same after that match. He lost just about every match he had after that before finally fading away into the night. Open doors as I walk inside Swallow me so the pain subsides And I shake as I take this in Let the show begin I challenged Seymour to a match. It was to be a Halifax Beer Brawl. My very own signature match. I wanted to put myself up against the very best. As far as I was concerned, Seymour still WAS the very best. He disappeared before I could get my match. My challenge went completely ignored. But that's okay because now, now I have something better. I have a match against a man who IS the best. The man who beat Seymour Almasy. I've always wanted to put myself up against the very best, now I get to do that. I get to face the absolute best in the business. The higher you are The farther you fall The longer the walk The farther you crawl My body my temple This temple it tilts Yes this is the house that Jack built There are those out there who would say this is just a political ploy. That I used my position as Commissioner to give myself the shot. I've had the power to give myself that title shot for months. Long before I took the post as Commissioner. So why didn't I give myself the shot then? Because I didn't want to. I have too much respect for the world title and what it represents. I went down that road once though. It was back when I first broke into the business as a part of Carnage Extreme Hardcore Wrestling. I shot to the top of that company faster than you can say 'Triple H.' I had it all. The World Title, the ear of the owner, everything. Then it happened. I got greedy. I put myself into a match for control of the company. I won it and pushed myself to the moon. A month later the company went broke and shut down. I can never allow myself to do anything like that again. I have far too much respect for this company and the people it employs for that. The higher you are The farther you fall The longer the walk The farther you crawl My body my temple This temple it tilts 'Yes I am I am I am' I dunno what's going to happen tomorrow. But I know this. I will be coming down to that ring with everything I've got. I know that I will not stop until either I'm unconscious or the new World Champion. Finally...I know that Beelzebozo is in for the fight of his life. I won't accept anything less than that either from myself or from him. Open my eyes It swallows me Is that you there I twist away Of course there's always going to be those who don't want me to succeed. Tomorrow will be no different. The remnants of the Prime Alliance...the Resurrection...the Chaos Society...all of them would love nothing better than to see me fall at the hands of the clown. I can't allow that to happen. If I lose to Beelzebozo because he was the better man, I can accept that. The better man will have won and there can be no disputing that. However, if I lose because of some idiot with a brick...or a chair...that I can't accept. I won't lose the world title, not that way. Maybe I can make it Hell in a Cell. Nice thing about being Commish is that I have that kind of authority. Whatever happens, I need to do whatever I can to keep this one on one. After all, this will be the biggest match of my career. Mike stands up and walks over to the trophy case. His hand brushes over the section containing a replica of the UWF World Heavyweight Title. Light reflects off the name plate, which currently says Beelzebozo. Mike looks at the nameplate and smiles about what might yet be... Away Away Away
