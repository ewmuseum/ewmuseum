Maintaining the High Quality in Spectre Rp's for almost 3 whole Weeks!!!!!!!!! Man this one Rocks!!!!!!

6 Nov 2001

Corky Spectre

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in the back of a motor home travelling along a highway between shows.... Corky Spectre is in the back setting up his X-Box Gaming system to play the Newly Released "GIWF/PWW Inter-Fed Rumble"..... Corky starts up the game, waits through the credits and then begins the process of selecting his favorite wrestler, Lew Spectre.... And then choses his opponent for his first match.... Corky choses the ever popular "Celeste" character from the PWW wrestlers. As Corky is about to begin crushing Celeste.... The game freezes, and then fades away to be replaced with the following message.... "Sometimes life gets in the way.... We Apologize. But due to circumstances beyond our control, we couldn't finish programming this game in time for it's release date, so we released what we had finished. Thank You for choosing another fine product from Talesmith Productions." Corky looks and the screen in disbelief. Corky: "Rod Damnit!!!!!!!" Corky shakes his head, picks up the game box and reads it.... Corky: "Rod Damnit!!!!!!! I should have known not to buy into anything that is even remotely associated with the PWW or Talesmith Productions.... Nothing is ever what it seems with those guys!!!!!! Guess what my daddy always said about the PWW is true.... They had some stupid Hardkore guy as their Main Eventer for cripe's sake so they can't be that good..." Corky looks around the back of the motor home for something to do..... Standing up in the corner is his trusty 6 string guitar... bought for him on his nineth birthday by his daddy. Corky walks over to it.... Corky: "Hello my old friend.... I've come to talk to you again...." Corky picks up his guitar and starts strumming.... A familiar Old Tune called Cats in the Cradle.... Corky: "To you it must seem that I arrived just the other day, I came to the world in the usual way. But there were planes to catch, and bills to pay. I learned to walk while Lew was away. And I was talking 'fore you knew it, and as I grew, I'd say, "I'm gonna be like you, dad. You know I'm gonna be like you." And the cat's in the cradle and the silver spoon, A boy named Corky, his dad named Lew. "When you coming home, dad?" "I don't know when, But we'll get together then. You know we'll have a good time then." I bet it seems like I just turned ten the other day. saying, "Thanks for the ball, dad, come on let's play. Can you teach me to throw?" you said, "Not today, I got a lot to do." I said, "That's ok." And I walked away, my smile never dimmmed, I said, "I'm gonna be like him, yeah. You know I'm gonna be like him." And the cat's in the cradle and the silver spoon, A boy named Corky and his dad named Lew. "When you coming home, dad?" "I don't know when, But we'll get together then. You know we'll have a good time then." Remember when I came from college that cool winter day, So much like a man you just had to say, "Son, I'm proud of you. Can you sit for a while?" I shook his head, and I said with a smile, "What I'd really like, dad, is to borrow the car keys. See you later. Can I have them please?" And the cat's in the cradle and the silver spoon, A boy named Corky and his dad named Lew. "When you coming home, son?" "I don't know when, But we'll get together then, dad. You know we'll have a good time then." Corky continues to strum his guitar.... changes up the cords.... Corky: "I Wanna Rock.... ROCK!!!!!! I Want to Rock!!!!!!!!!" The scene fades.....
