UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #273

Don't get between my mom and me!

4 Mar 2004

Anarchy — Outside Pryde's Appartment Building

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens with Adien Pryde rushing out of an apartment building. He is wearing black jeans, black button down shirt and a red baseball hat. He is struggling to put on a jacket while carrying a duffle bag.] Pryde: Dude, this is no a good time. My mother is on her way right now to Abbot Northwestern Hospital for emergency surgery and I need to hurry up and get there. [He opens the back door of a black SUV and throws the bag into the back seat of it and slams the door.] Pryde: I said I don't have time for this. [He shoves his way past the camera man.] Camera Man: But I need to find out about Sunday Afternoon Supremacy. [Pryde turns quickly to face the man. He looks to be very pissed off.] Pryde: Look asshole, I don't have time for this but I will give you a comment. [Pryde nails the camera man in the face. As the camera falls, it stays recording all be it sideways.] Pryde: Yes Anarchy will be there. [You hear what sounds like a head hitting a car door.] Pryde: Bondage and that other nobody will be taken care of allowing me to move onto bigger and better things. [You see what seems t be Pryde kicking the man in the gut.] Pryde: Yes we will be entering the ALL COMERS challenge and win it, proving that we are the superior tag-team tacticians. Camera Man: No, no, please no [You see the man land on his back on the cement side walk like he was power bombed] Pryde: Sorry dude, but like I said. I don't have time for this. [The sound of a car door slamming shut, the sound of an engine starting, the sound of tires screeching, the moan of a camera man. Scene fades to black]
