The Comeback (short)

6 Nov 2001

Deke Aurora

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
template He's And He's STILL The First Ever UWF World Heavyweight Champion. For days and counting. [The camera opens up in the basement training facility of Deke Aurora. As a change of pace, he is not sparring. He's not working out with weights, or performing some martial arts exercises. He's not even working on the punching bag. Instead, he just sits on his old couch, staring intently at the camera. He wears just a pair of jeans, no shirt. His ribs are no longer taped, but he seems to react with pain as he speaks.] Deke: I bet you've wondered where I've been this past few days. I bet you've been wondering why I haven't been gracing your television screens with my presence. I'll be honest. I was hurt. A man isn't in top shape after being jumped twice and battling in a ladder match . . . all in the same week. Not even if that man happens to be the World Champion. And after all the events of Ground Zero, after risking my career for a match, I needed a break. Heh, a lot happened in that ring, didn't it? New champions crowned, crippled robots, old "friends" returning . . . but you know what? None of that crap matters. The only thing that matters is that I am still the UWF World Heavyweight Champion. There were a lot of fools out there who thought I was going to lose. Plague was among them. But the skeptics have been silenced, and once again, I have proven myself to be the pinnacle of wrestling talent. But perhaps I'm getting a little bit too cocky. Perhaps my title defense meant virtually nothing. After all, all that I did was defeat two men who barely qualify to be in the IC title ranks. All I did was fend off a typically half-assed attempt from Danny Jaye and an unusually (even for him) half-assed attempt from Blade. Oh well. I can be happy. Now these two suckers have finally been knocked off my victory platform for good. Hell, they aren't even in the federation anymore, for all I know. No longer do I have to put up with their constant babble about who is the "true" number one contender. They can bitch about who is the number one contender to the TV title for all I care. I'm moving on. Moving on to find some real opposition. And after the PPV, I think we all know who THAT'S going to be. Ben Jackson. Ben Jericho. The Ayatollah of Rock and Rollah, and whatever else he can come up with. My sometimes friend, other times enemy. Sometimes tag team partner, other times puroresu opponent. A man who I've schooled in the ways of true fighting, a man who has incorporated much of my style into his own. I have to hand it to Luca. He's a lot of things - a dickwad, an asshole, a smarmy little prick - but he's not dumb. I've never said he was. And certainly, with LUCABOT out of commission, he's made a wise choice for a back-up hired gun. Because he picked the one man who knows me better than any other. The man who used to be my stable mate, the man who, apart from maybe Lyle Wile, has stood across from me in the ring more than anyone. A man I've spent countless hours training with, sparring with, inventing moves and testing them out with. In short, Ben Jericho has the best chance of anticipating what I will do in that ring. But that carries a price with it. Because Jericho may know me, but just as importantly, I know Jericho. Hell, I once fought him full steam for an hour. In all these years, I've learned a great deal about one of the most bewildering characters the wrestling world has ever seen. And above all, I know that Jericho has the power to symbolize all that I love about wrestling . . . and all that I hate about it. Jericho can be hardcore, or he can fight like a man. Let's hope he chooses the latter path this go-around. Because despite all I've said about him in the past years, I respect the man. And finally, it looks like I will have a REAL match. I guess we'll find out soon enough. At WNU, I will take on Jackson. And for the hell of it, they've thrown Plague into the mix. Oooh, the hardcore champion. What a waste of everyone's time. Here we were, poised to see a great match between Jericho and myself. And we've ruined it with hardcore garbage, we've demoted a possible classic into your run-of-the-mill jobberfest. They've even thrown in a "mystery opponent," the most trite of all sports entertainment clichés. Whatever. I don't give a damn. All that matters is, I'm the best. I've proven it time and again. And I will prove it once again on Wednesday. [Fade to this:]
