The Road/Flight to the Regal Rumble

14 Aug 2004

Dicky Flint — a plane in the sky

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[alright, the scene starts with dicky seated in his spot on the plane. the mood of the scene is somewhat awkward, not so much for dicky but for the other people aboard the plane. its not every flight you have a kkk grand wizard aboard, especially in his get-up.] -as the people stare at dicky in an offended manner, dicky, completely oblivious to the stares, pulls out a news letter/magazing thing from his little carry-on bag. the news letter/magazine this is titled 'The Ku Klux Kronicles' (im so running that phrase into the ground...ku klux klutch, ku klux kronicles, ku klux kerosine, ku klux kadaver....hehe, yeah) dicky skims over the front page...the head line reads, 'gasoline prices are on the rise, what does this mean?!?!' dicky to himself:um...oh man, dude, the gasoline prices are putting a strain on the number of houses and crosses we can burn!!! i mean, we already cut back, like a lot, and the gas prices just keep rising!! this is so uncool. -dicky is all the while getting more and more concerned and worked up over the matter. more and more people are concentrating on dicky, this time not only for his attire, but for the change in his temperment. dickys voice is steadily getting louder:dude, what is going on?!?! like for every good thing that happens to me, something bad happens and vice versa...i win my first match, which wasnt even that hard, then i find out i cant bring emanuel to fuckin tokyo-i find out im in the regal rumble, my second match, such a prestigious match, now i find out its going to cost more to burn some goddamn crosses!!! this is rediculous, i can handle this, what next?!?! -as dicky finishes his little rant the plane lands, and the other passengers quickly exit the plane, as dicky slowly gets off the plane and looks around at his surroundings he is mortified dicky:oh....my.....god...oh..my god, ohmygod, where the fuck am i??!?! am i in like china?!?!? oh man, dude, tokyo, i thought that was in california, oh man, well this explians the 10 hour flight.......all i see is yellow!!!! -dicky now is convinced something good has to happen out of this......whether this means at the regal rumble, who knows?!?!
