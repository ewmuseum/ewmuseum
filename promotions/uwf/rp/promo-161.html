UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #161

A word from our VP

21 Dec 2003

Mike Montgomery — The Office of the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As with virtually every promo done this year, we open in the office of the Executive Vice President of Talent Relations, and creator of the Player's Club, Mike Montgomery. While he is no longer in active competition, some things don't change. Ruff Ryder is still running his mouth, Jamie Shock's still gone, and Malcolm Kennedy is still doing nothing with his career. On the other side of hte coin, Joshua Dunn is continuing one of the greatest intercontinental title runs in history, and the Player's Club thrives under his guidance. Now the addition of Ezra Kazee gives the Club the greatest Intercontinental Champion, one of the greatest Television champions, and one of the greatest cruiserweights in UWF history. And with the last episode of Supremacy, the challenge has been thrown down. The Player's Club is going to war. Meanwhile, our Executive VP does have a job to perform. Sitting in front of him is a masked latino who is applying for a UWF contract. Mike looks the wrestler up and down, eyebrow raised slightly in an expression that borders on utter disgust. 'And exactly what..are you supposed to be?' The man quickly rises to his feet, a tone of rage in his voice, 'You dare claim ignorance of the might of El Chupacabra?! El Chupacabra is legend! El Chupacabra is considered a God amongst luchadores! He is...' '...three seconds from being thrown out of my office. Sit down. Now what in the hell makes you think you have anywhere near enough talent to join the UWF.' 'Have enough talent? El Chupacabra IS talent! He is excitement! He is charisma!' 'Shut...up.' Mike orders. 'Please, explain your gimmick.' 'Gimmick? El Chupacabra needs no gimmick! He is the greatest! Men tremble at the mention of El Chupacabra's name! Women swoon at the sight of him! Children...' 'You are...by far...the most irritating man I have ever met...' Montgomery glances over the contract, an idea suddenly coming to him, 'however...your..talent..is certainly undeniable. More importantly, you're willing to work for little to no pay. You, my dear Chupacabra, have yourself a contract.' The luchadore known as El Chupacabra bolts to his feet, pumping Montgomery's arm enthusiastically, 'El Chupacabra thanks you! He shall ensure that you do not regret this decision!!' 'Get out of my office. Now.' The Uruguan superstar quickly nods and runs from the office. Mike rubs his temps in exasperation. 'By far one of the most irritating...grating...annoying people I have ever met...and he will be appearing every single week on Commissioner Shock's precious Sunday Afternoon Supremacy for 6 months. I love it when a plan comes together.' Mike chuckles to himself as he envisions what El Chupacabra will do to the ratings of Supremacy. 'Now, we really will have to do something about Ruff Ryder's monopolization of the North American title. True Indian Superstar my ass. What kind of champion doesn't defend his title? Something's going to have to be done about that. Ezra vs. Ryder should be fun. I think that'll be good for the first Supremacy after the holidays.' Mike's phone begins ringing. He picks it up, listening intently for a moment. 'Really? He is? Excellent. Thank you Doctor.' After hanging up, Mike looks at the phone for a moment with a bemused expression, 'Malcolm, your worst nightmare is about to come true.'
