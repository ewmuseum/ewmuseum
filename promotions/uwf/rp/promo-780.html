World's Worst Political Parody pt. 3

Todayish (year unrecorded)

Bunny Army of Doom — Like I paid attention

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the last adventure, aka poorly wrote roleplay, Ward was just about to reveal the identity of Laguna’s opponent for the December election. Before Ward could say the name the adventure had came to a close leaving the reader on the edge of their seat waiting to see what will happen next (actually they didn’t care and just read Oni’s roleplay because Oni is funnier than me *DAMN YOU ONI!*). Will the identity of Laguna’s opponent for the election finally be revealed? Probably, I mean I need to reveal the identity before December anyways. “Wow I can’t believe you got him to agree to run against me, there is no way I can lose now!” Laguna shouted with joy as he skipped down the hall. Oops, I jumped ahead too far. Let me back track for a minute here. Standing tall in the doorway Ward looked in to see his worried leader and friend, Laguna, waiting to hear just who Ward had convinced to run against him in what easily was the most important election in his life. Ward, however, enjoyed the moments of power he had over the man that for the rest of the time held the power over him. “I found him while I was performing recon at one of the UWF shows.” Bringing them back with him, Ward starts to indulge his friends in the tale of just how he discovers this opponent that represents dishonor and treachery. The place is the greatest city in the world; the Big Apple. The site is a dirty, hole in the wall bar where the lights are either half broken or half working depending on your view of the world. Regardless of your view though, inside is people drinking away theirs. Bottle after bottle destroys brain cells as the men and women inside simply chose to forget their worries. Pulling down his hat to better cover his face Ward gets ready to slip into the bar. As he approaches the door, a loud shout stops him dead in his track. “Like I knew she was your wife when I slept with her!” That was the wrong thing to say. Ward starts to run into the bar to see what was happening but the window to his side shatters to pieces and flying through the shards is a body. Without a moment wasted, Ward is at his side. “Are you alright?” he asks. Much to Ward’s surprise the man pushes him aside and gets back to his feet. A bit of now red glass falls from this man’s body. “Oh fucking hell, no one throws me out of a bar! Come back here mother fucker!” With those fighting words, he marches through the doors. Ward stands back in shock. He wants to enter the bar and see what was happening but within seconds he does not even need to bother. Through already broken glass, soaring over the broken pieces of glass is another body. He tumbles on the tough concrete outside the bar until he comes to a rolling stop just short of being in the street. Shortly there after, the man originally tossed through the window walks out of the door clapping his hands together as if knocking off dirt that did not exist. Flipping his hair the man turns and gazes his soft blue eyes at Ward, gentle eyes for a not so gentle man. “What the fuck are you staring at?” the man demands of Ward, snapping at him. The suddenness and harsh tone takes Ward by surprise. He looks around at the empty scene surrounding him. Under the cover of the street lamp, the poor man and husband of an unfaithful wife still lays in paid. Ward turns his eyes back to the man before him. His lips almost in a tremble he speaks “you’re bleeding.” “And?” the man says before turning to the person he sent out the window. With a swift motion of his foot he kicks him square in the side. After the vicious shot to the ribs, he looks at Ward. “Who are you?” Ward finally asks. Though he spoke the words, he already knew the answer. He has been searching for this person. Instead of replying, the man starts to walk off. Ward quickly hurries up and catches up with him before he can get too far away. Once he joins the man his question is finally answered, “I’m Chris Storm, what do you want?” Ward already suspects as much. If he were not so nervous, he would smile. “Chris, I have a deal to make with you. How would you like to run for a position in government?” A sly grin appears on Ward’s lips. His mind is working at the speed of a thousand gears thinking of ways to talk Chris into accepting the offer. Will he though? Tune in next time when I have more time to write during class!
