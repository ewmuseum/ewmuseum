Communism 101

25 Apr 2004

The Communist — In a small conference room

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:scene opens on the Communist sitting at the head of a small table, a slide projector on the desk pointing at a pulldown screen behind him: Commie: Good Evening. As I'm sure you all know, I'm The Communist, Champion of the Working Man and dire enemy of the bourguosis. I am here to educate your small minds on the history of my proud and strong country. :The Communist hits a button on his control and the first slide pops up. Its a map of the former USSR: Commie: You see, once Russia was a proud country, and the people worked hard, not for your worthless ugly green paper, but for the satasfaction that their work helped the rest of their people. The farmers made food, not for money, but so others could eat. The military did not fight for glory, but for the spread of our superior way of life to all people, so they may be free from the oppression of the corperate evil that now spreads like a cancer over this world. :He presses the slide button and a map of the now split russia appears on the screen: Commie: That all changed when we gained our so called 'Freedom'. :He calles another slide up, one of Russian people lined around the block to get food from a Corprate fast food place: Commie: Your Corprations are bleeding my people dry. We wait in lines miles long to taste your damned 'Fast food'. Your 'Freedom' has destroyed my people and now, I will destroy you. From bottom to top, all of you will feel the wrath of the People. I swear on memory of Lenin and the will of Great Mother Russia.
