UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #396

The real Big Dawg

May 6th (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — Back yard.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Resurrection has taken on its 4th member. A man bound for glory. The world title is his goal. Malcolm Kennedy jumped ship at SAS from the Prime Alliance to The Resurrection. Now after ditching the man he once sided with, he must face Ruff Ryder in the ring at the next SAS. We all know Ryder had plenty to say. You can hear his bullshit rants from miles away, and feel the hot air pour from his mouth from the other side of the planet. But now its Malcolm’s turn. Now its time to hear how its really gonna be at SAS. How everything will turn out in the long run.* (The scene opens up in the back yard of Malcolm Kennedy’s house. A fire burns in a barrel as Malcolm stands next to it. Dropping pieces of cardboard and other sorts of trash into it. He turns to see the camera. After taking notice to the camera, he goes back to work, speaking as he does.) Malcolm Kennedy: “The flames ignited when Moraless and Digital Mortality aligned just a few weeks ago. The fire grew when the addition of Bondage arrived. And now the fire is out of control in the UWF since the addition of yours truly. The UWF will be burned down to the very foundation. And rebuilt by The Resurrection.” “Who can stop us? The Players club? I don’t think so. Not this time. You gentlemen have had your run at the top of the ladder, and now its time we tip that ladder over and put the Resurrection at the top. Yeah Mike Montgomery may be the Heavyweight Champ, but that wont last too long. He’s a marked man and soon enough he will fall flat on his face. And someone will pull his precious title from his waist and walk off with ease. If I have any say about it, you can rest assured that person will be me.” “But there are more pressing matters to attend to.” ( Malcolm’s face twists in disgusts.) “Ruff Ryder.” ( Malcolm picks a shirt up from the ground. The words Prime Alliance run across it in red. He drops the shirt in the flames. ) “Ryder, its safe to say we know each other pretty damn well. You ran along side me for along time. I dragged you into the big time. I made the Prime Alliance function when Jamie flaked off. You can say that you made us, but the only thing you did was beg me to come back. So I did, and made the Prime Alliance a true force. You did nothing. I was the one who made us keep on running, and I was the one who put the dagger through the heart of the Prime Alliance. Now that that’s done, your next on my agenda, and you will fall all to easily.” ( He picks up a duffle bag and unzips it. He pours out the contents which appear to be all of his old Prime Alliance gear. The fire engulfs them and soon ash is all that is left of them. ) “ So you wanna come at me with the same strategy you use on the rookies? Ryder you know better than that. Your not that dumb. That in your face trash talk wont do you any good here. I’ve seen what you can do in the ring, and I know that when push comes to shove, your nothing but hot air. You can keep on running your mouth though. Because I know that’s your style and that’s all you know how to do. But trust me Ryder, you wont beat me at SAS. Im too good, and your too stupid to realize it. You think the fact you beat Mentok makes you better than me? HAHAHA! Lets go over some key points about that match. Take notes, and if you have any questions at the end Ill help you remove to foot from your mouth.” ( Malcolm turns to focus completely on the camera. Counting the points one after another on each finger.) “One. I lost to Mentok by a disqualification. Who got me disqualified? I believe it was the baseball swinging Cherokee Thrilla.” “Two. You Broke a baseball bat over the back of Mentoks head. Jesus man, even Axel Action could come out and beat Mentok just one week after that!” “Three. You pulled the match out of your ass because Mentoks a freaking idiot. He’s freaking nuttier than squirrel turds. And you barely beat him. If he wasn’t so busy dancing around, you would have been down and out for good.” “You pat yourself on the back way too much for beating that idiot. Im a totally different story. The fact is, you can bring all you have in you to the ring and unleash all hell on me, and your still gonna end up with your shoulders pinned for the three count. Its just a simple fact, Im better than you. Always will be. But I want you to keep telling yourself that your gonna win, it makes it feel that much more embarrassing when you lose at SAS. Ryder this Sunday we are gonna battle it out. If you want to turn it into a war instead, thats just fine with me. This can be the beginning or the end. You can choose which one you really want. Right now you may think you want to go to war with me, but after I beat you at SAS, you’ll be singing a different tune. Im sure of it.” ( Malcolm drops the duffle bag into the fire and the fire roars back to life. The camera zooms in on the flames as Malcolm walks off and the scene fades to black. )
