What up boyz!!!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Ya'll can't deny it!!!! ** ( We see a shot of an all too familiar cabin in a wooded area of Locust Grove Oklahoma. ) ** I'm a f*ckin Ryder!!! ** ( The camera slowly moves toward the door and just as it is directly in front of the door it swings open and a large indian man wearing black wind pants and a red t-shirt with Cherokee Thrilla across the chest in black letters. The man stands leaning against the doorway with a very familiar y grin on his face. It is the man you all love to , the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder. ) Ryder: Now I know what you are all thinkin but you're wrong. Ol' Ryder ain't back in the UWF just yet. I've just been away for awhile and thought i'd send ya'll a little promo to see what's been goin on since I left. I know all you homo s had to have missed me while I was gone so I just figured i'd let ya'll see my arrogant face once again. Now i'm not real sure what my status is in this God forsaken place but i'd kinda like to find out. Maybe i'm fired or retired or whatever they wanna put it but i'd like for the person runnin this hell hole to maybe give a native a call and let me know where I stand. Not sayin i'd come back to this dump, but hey you never know. I might want to jump back through those ropes and beat someone's head in just for old time's sake. Well for now i'll just sign off and say to all the friends that I had get at me sometime and for the boss holla at ya boy. As for the rest of you Cherokee Thrilla rs... you all!!! and to all a good night! ( Ryder throws his head back and laughs hysterically as the camera pans out and the scene fades to black. )
