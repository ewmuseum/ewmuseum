Beginnings

14 Sep 2001

Feng Huang

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A billowing mass of smoke is visible as the scene fades into existence. The smoke masks all from sight, but within it’s hidden centre breathing can be heard, getting slightly louder, as if getting closer. A figure emerges from the smoke, it’s tendrils clinging onto him as if fingers reaching out for one last caress before they lose their grip and he steps fully out of the smoke to stand revealed. The man seems to stand at just shy of six feet in height, and those his weight can not be told he can be seen to be quite muscled, his shoulders and arms thick with corded muscles that show through the tight lycra material that covers the majority of his body. His face is covered in the black material that also covers him from his neck down fully of all of his left side, right down to his feet, leaving only his right arm bare, deeply tanned skin visible here, but nowhere else. While the majority of the material is black, there are swathes of color visible on it, starting at his top left shoulder, and working down to form a belt like circle around his waist, before continuing down his right leg three bands of red, orange and yellow, linking together like swirls of flame or air. A simple Chinese symbol, also in the three colors, is on the mask, and covers from the top to the place where a mouth would be, and it is a translation of the wearer’s name. Feng Huang. From this mask comes a long straight ponytail, the hair jet black, that hangs almost to his waist. As the smoke stays billowed in the background, the figure stands with an almost palpable air of confidence, as he says nothing, but instead seems to be looking directly at us. Then he speaks, the mask muffling his voice to some extent, but it is audible enough to make out his accented words clearly. “There are many things that people in the UWF will want to know about me. They will want to know who I am. They will want to know where I came from. They will want to know my pedigree.” The figure, without looking around, slowly walks backwards into the smoke, his form becoming covered and slowly disappearing from sight as his words fade out. “ That will all be revealed in time, for now just know that I am Feng Huang, and that my reason for being here is simple “ The scene fades on his last words. “ To triumph ”
