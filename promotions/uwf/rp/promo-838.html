UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #838

Mexicans, Tacos, and Jamie Shock

14 Dec 2004

Duke Williams — Chicago, Illinois

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s a seasonally warm Chicago night with temperatures in the forties. Walking down the street is the culturally out of place wrestling icon “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. A plum of smoke surrounds his head as he puffs on his Churchill type cigar. The Ostrich skin boots wear on the hard concrete of the city sidewalk as Duke pounds along. Somewhere between biker, hippie, and cowboy the Malice Man draws attention wearing boots, worn blue jeans, a t-shirt covered by a leather vest, and of coarse a black beaver Stetson cowboy hat wrapped with a dead flamingo. Despite it being late in the evening Duke has on chrome shades that reflect the city scenery and a neon sign that shines like a bacon in the light to a lost ship, “Taco Cabaña”. Mexican food after a late night of drinking are as synonymous as the Bears and Ditka’s mustache. With out even thinking about it Duke reaches for the handle on the door and enters the all-Mexican establishment. A mariachi band is playing “Good Night Irene” which doesn’t register right with his ears but again you’ll put up with a lot of strange things for a few tacos after a night of hard drinking. Duke who stands 6’3 with out boots on sticks out in a sea of short Mexican guys. One ugly hombre sitting in the corner booth stares down Duke. The pox marks on his face and ugly scares are highlighted by his gold teeth and big ass Mexican mustache. Like a scene in a Johnny Rodriguez movie the ugly Mexican speaks and when he does everything from the music to the cooking stops. Ugly Mexican: ‘Whatca want Holmes?’ In what seems like slow motion four big Mexican guy’s who stand about 5’5” start moving in towards Duke. The Malice Man takes off his sunglasses and looks hard into the ugly man’s eyes. Neither man breaks stare as both wait for the next mans move like a pro chess game. Duke: ‘I came here to dance, eat a few taco, and buy you a tequila hoss.’ Not in the mood to take on a restaurant of Mexicans at three in the morning, in Chicago, Duke takes the role of pacifist. Ten years ago he would have flipped a table and gone to swinging but at 45 with bad knees sometimes it just isn’t worth it. The ugly Mexican nods at Duke as the music kicks back on and the world around him goes back to business as usual. Duke nods back and orders up a drink for ugly and his gang as he dances over to the small dance floor. He starts dancing James Brown style with a cute Chicano girl as it seems like the whole place is staring at him. The Malice Man gives the girl a pat on the ass and dances over to an empty table where he sits down. The place looks clean enough to pass for a gas station bathroom which is generally a good sign the food is going to be greasy and good…tonight. A waitress who looks tired and worn out from a late night of work walks over to Duke. Despite being in her fifties and rode hard her face still has a certain charm to it. A charm that Duke finds attractive but then again that could be the alcohol talking. Waitress: ‘What can I get you sweetheart?’ Duke: ‘The energy of these kids and your phone number.’ The Malice Man may be slower physically but he still has that quick wit that made him a star. The waitress blushes a little as she whips out her order pad showing no real interest in the comment. Duke: ‘Ok, give me ten taco’s and a bottle of tequila.’ Waitress: ‘I don’t think that’s going to help you get that energy or my phone number.’ The waitress winks as she walks away to pass the order on to the cook. What she doesn’t know is liquor and Duke are like Popeye and spinach. Duke’s mind wonders off into deep thought as he tries to remember if he left out a dish of food for his cat Guthrie. That thought turns into a pondering about if his cat would like a taco or not. The waitress makes a “drop and run” as she hands Duke the bottle of tequila. Duke twists off the cap and immediately takes a swig forgetting all about his cat. Out of nowhere Duke whips out his cell phone and listens to his voice mails. Duke: ‘Well holy hog shit hoss!’ It turns out Duke is going to wrestler for the first time in four month this Tuesday for UWF. Excitement turns to cold sweat as nerves kick in. An emotion Duke hasn’t felt in a long time. Duke: ‘Where the hell are my wresling tight?’ The Malice Man ponders out loud as even more thoughts enter his tiny mind. He puts all his concerns to rest as he chugs down some more white tequila. The ugly Mexican and two of his thugs wonder over and help themselves to a seat at the table Duke is occupying. Duke: ‘Have a seat Hoss.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘Thanks for your O-K amigo.’ The three Mexicans laugh like hyenas at the zoo. Ugly Mexican: ‘Are you that wrestling guy?’ Duke: ‘I’m just a ramblin, scrambling, cowboy trying to keep his mind untangled.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘So you are him.’ Duke: ‘The Malice Man Duke Williams glad to make your acquaintance.’ The three men shake hands as the kick back a little more laid back sipping tequila and chatting. Ugly Mexican: ‘So you’re going to wrestle again?’ Duke: ‘I want to buy one of them big screen plasma TV’s but there expensive as hell so I figure I knock the hell out of a few little jerks, make some cash, and disappear again.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘Who you got first?’ Duke: ‘According to my manager I have some brash punk named Jamie Shock.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘What’s so shocking about him?’ Duke: ‘Besides he swears a lot and tells stupid stories nothing. I guess that’s just his name. Ugly Mexican: ‘Are you going to kick his ass?’ The Malice Man chuckles as the old waitress brings out his tacos. Duke starts to scarf them down as he chats with the ugly Mexican and his two thug friends. Duke: ‘Do fat chick make you horney when your drunk? God damn right I’m going to beat him I got nothing better to do that night except drink, get drunk, then beat him down like a bad cook in a trailer park.’ Unimportant Thug 1: ‘Isn’t Jamie Shock a Brit?’ Duke: ‘I reckon if you say he is Hoss, then he is. I think I remember watching one of his promo’s and jack this guy is about as funny as David Spade after Farley died.’ The four men laugh at how unfunny David Spade is. Duke sloppily fills up everybody’s glass full of tequila as he drinks down another shot and chases it with a taco. Duke: ‘Look this jive ass crooked tooth asshole ain’t got nothing on me. I was dancing around rings while this guy was sucking on his mother’s things. I’m the only cowboy left in this town and come high noon Shock is going to get a lesson in culture.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘What about his lady friend.’ Duke: ‘Right now that young lady is like Tom Hanks and riding on the Polar Express. It’s cold baby and frigid but if she wants to warm up the Malice Express is making a stop in Shocktown. You see once you do it Malice Style you forget everything before that point. A new beginning for a frigid women. That’s what I can offer her but if she’s set on the shock stick then so be it but she should know he’s out of batteries.’ Ugly Mexican: ‘Damn that’s cold.’ Duke: ‘Not as cold as there bedroom. You see I’m an American and I’m proud. Even if I go down I’m going to go down swigging. It reminds me of a story. A Frenchman, an Englishman and a New Yorker were exploring the jungle and were captured by a fierce tribe. As they sit in a hut, awaiting their fate, the chief comes to them and says, 'The bad news is that now that we've caught you, we're going to kill you, and then use your skins to build a canoe. The good news is that you get to choose how you die.' The Frenchman says, 'I take ze poison.' The chief gives him some poison, the Frenchman says, 'Vive la France!' and drinks it down. The Englishman says, 'A pistol for me, please.' The chief gives him a pistol, he points it at his head, says, 'God save the queen!' and blows his brains out. The New Yorker says, 'Gimme a fork.' The chief is puzzled, but he shrugs and gives him a fork. The New Yorker takes the fork and starts jabbing himself all over-the stomach, the sides, the chest, everywhere. There's blood gushing out all over, it's horrible. The chief is appalled, and screams, 'What are you doing???' The New Yorker looks at the chief and says, 'So much for your canoe, asshole!' So you see we Americans and Mexican Americans fight till the end.’ Three of the four men laugh one of them doesn’t. Unimportant Mexican Thug 2: ‘Man I heard that joke in the internet.’ Duke: ‘Yea well when you’ve been running a character as long as me you have to dip in all the resources. Look the brass tacks is this Jamie Fox er Shock is going to the first of many to be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND till I can afford a plasma TV. That’s the honky tonk truth hoss.’ DW =w=
