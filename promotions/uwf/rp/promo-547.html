UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #547

A Little Pick-up Game

July 13/14 2004 (year unrecorded)

'The Shocker' Dave Storm — A basketball court at a schoolyard

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up in a schoolyard. Being summer, there are only two cars in the parkinglot, a black cadillac escalade and a silver porsche. They are parked near the basketball courts, where two men are playing a pick up game, each dressed in proper playing attire. Soon after a few minutes of playing, the final point is scored and each man takes a rest. The men are revealed to be 'The Shocker' Dave Storm and his close friend and former tag team partner,'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Good Game, man.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Yeah, you too.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'One more?' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Wiliams:: 'I don't know man, I'm getting too old for this.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'C'mon, last one.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Hold on, let's wait. I still need to catch my breath.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Man, no wonder you retired.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: ''Cause of my knee.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'No, 'cause you couldn't keep up with me anymore.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Willaims:: 'Uh, sure. Keep telling your self that buddy.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'What, you know its true!' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Hey, I'm not that old!' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'thirty three and can't run. Your body's sure aged.' 'Dr Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Are you calling me old?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, if that's how you want to put it.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Willaims:: 'Your not too young yourself, buddy.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'We'll see about that tomorrow night.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Why? What's tomorrow night?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'My first match in UWF.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Ohh..against who?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'A buncha people. It's like a rookie battle royal against nine others, or something like that.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Willaims:: 'That's awesome.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah it is. If I win I get a shot at the TV title, if i come in third or second i get another shot at the number one contendership.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Dat chill yo.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, whatever.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'What? Isn't that what you kids say these days?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Sure. Anyway, about my match. Some of the guys are okay, considering they're all young rookies. One of em' 's a chinese karate freak, and one calls himself Sacrified. But another, the weirdest of the bunch, calls himself fizzy and hangs out with girls named Aphrodite and Venus and thinks he's in the seventies.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Willaims:: 'I had a dream i was a white seventies pimp before.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, you told me. Actually, I'm trying to forget.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Willaims:: 'ohh..Whatever.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'There is one guy that may present a challenge though.' 'Dr Xtreme' Dave Williams: 'Just one of em?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Oh, shut up.' 'Dr Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Whatever, Mr. 'I can beat Everyone'.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Ahh, screw you. You were the same way.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Atleast I can back it up.' (Dave Storm sprints over to the edge of the court and picks up the basketball. He walks to the middle of the court and chest passes the ball to Williams, who is a bit off guard when he catches it. He begins to dribble and they play a little during the conversation.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Bring it buddy.' 'Dr Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Alright, watch yourself though, young man.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, whatever. Anyway, that guy I was talking about..Rich Rollins. He calls himself the Renegade.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Hey, didn't I use they name in VWFU?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yes, yes you did. And then a that guy used it in ICWA or ICWF or KIWF of whatever the hell they called it, right after you become the Xtremist.' 'Dr Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Ahh, the good ol' days.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, less then four years ago.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Time blurs when you get old.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Your not even that old.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'So What? I'm getting ready for twenty years down the road when i can't act young anymore.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Wow, you really are a loser now.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: Now, I've always been like this!' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Thanks for reminding me.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Uh, no problem bro.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'So yeah, about the Rollins dude.' (Williams goes infor a layup and passes the ball to Storm as he replies.) 'Dr, Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'What about him?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Damn, I think your hearings on it's way out.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'No, you said something about him being in your match.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah. He's the best guy there, the only man other than me with a shot. Aside from maybe that karate kid, if he can understand english and show up. But that so-called 'Renegade' dude, he's like a chicago kid who thinks he has the whole city on his shoulders. I don't blame him. After all, that city hasn't won shit in seven years. I mean, thats not a long time but seriously, who watches the Bulls anymore. It's all about the Cubbies now. So thats like ninety six years. That's a helluvah long time. And He may be what the Chicagonians are looking for, but not when he has to cross path's with me. Sorry, It's just not happening. The kid has a shot.(shoots basketball from three-point line.)But when he's facing me,(The shoot sinks in the net) He just won't score.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Who would want to score with you?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'You are such a faggot. I feel bad for your kids.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'I feel bad for them too, because you're there uncle.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'I don't have time to argue with a thirty three year old who has the mental age of a six year old.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Like whatever, talk to the hand!' (Dave Williams says, sarcastically, sounding like a six-year old girl.) 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Man, I'm whiped.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Me too. I gotta get home to watch my sox in the all-star game tonight.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Too bad you'll be seeing alot more of MY yankees.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Yeah, striking out and making errors that might cost my sox home field advantage.' 'Dr.Xtreme' Dave Williams:: 'Ahh, I don't have time to argue with a Red Sox fan.' (The two men go over to a bench set up in between their cars. They wipe eachother down with towels and get ready to get in their cars.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Alright, see you later man.' 'Dr. Xtreme' Dave Williams: 'You go get em tomorrow night, man.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Thanks, will do.' (( Dave Williams speeds off in his porsche quickly. Right as Dave Storm starts his cadillac escalade, he see's a white tv camera crew van in his rear view mirror.) 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Owwwwhh that damn Focker.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'What the crap do you want??' Jon Focker:: 'Uh, an interview.' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'No shit. Let's hurry it up though, I gotta go soon. Make this quick.' Jon Focker:: 'Well, Mr. Storm, now that you know more about your oponents tomorrow night, could you discuss your match?' 'The Shocker' Dave Storm:: 'Sure I'll discuss my match with you. What is there to say? Tomorrow night I will debuet against nine other mofos in a battle royal to determine the TV title's number one contender. I'm matching up against a chinese freak, a poor kid from chicago, a man who thinks he's sacraficing his oponents, and a man who calls himself Fizzy. And those are the best of the bunch. Sound's like a lot of work, Focker! I hope I make it out alive! Haha, now that I think about it, none of those men even come close to me. Not even CLOSE. Maybe that Dick Rollers guy or that Kutabare kid, but other than that, 'The Shocker' will reign victorious on Wednesday Night Underground.'' (With that, 'The Shocker' drives away in his black cadillac leaving Jon Focker right next to where his car was parked.)
