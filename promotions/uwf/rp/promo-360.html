UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #360

Hotel

April 23 (year unrecorded)

Malcolm Kennedy — A hotel room dumbass.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“ “ ” “ *Promising a victory no matter who the opponent is, Malcolm Kennedy has proven he is confident. But is that enough to really get him the win? There are still so many who believe that he’s nothing more than hot air. Stupid twats. They’ll see. They’ll all see soon enough. Malcolm Kennedy is the real deal and should not be taken lightly.* “ “ “ “ “Six hours in the airport for a three hour flight? This has gotta change! I fly way too much to spend all my time waiting for my flight instead of actually flying on it.” “ “ ” “ { Malcolm slammed his brief case on the bed. Dropping his luggage to the floor he made his way to the complimentary bottle of champagne and meal left for him by the room service. } “ “ ” “ “Its hard to travel from the same house in Reno to the ring in a different town every week. But I love my jobs too much to give it all up.” “ “ ” “ { POP } “ “ ” “ { The cork from the champagne bottle shot across the room, bounced off the wall, and landed on the couch. He filled up his glass with the bubbling champagne and continued on. } “ “ ” “ “The weekend is now upon us and my mystery match is at hand” “ “ ” “ (He drank from the glass.) “ “ ” ““Ahhh..” “ “ ” “ (The sigh of relief signaled that the champagne had quenched the young mans thirst.) “ “ ” “ “I don’t know why Im standing here in front of this stupid camera right now. My thoughts are still the same. The guarantee of victory is still there. I haven’t changed the way I feel about anything. At SAS I will walk down to the ring, wait for out mystery guest, listen for the bell to ring, and proceed to kick some ass. Nothings changed. Nothing at all.” “ “ ” “ { He shook his head as he sat the glass down.} (It still didn’t make any sense to Malcolm as to why he is still in this match. Why was he still stuck facing an opponent he doesn’t want to waste his time on. An opponent that is still unknown. An opponent that no one has heard from since the SAS card was released. ) { The table shook as he slammed his fist down on the surface of it. He sat down in the tables matching chair. } “ “ ” ““The simple fact is that no one gives a shit who this mystery opponent is. Not me, not anyone. If Mike Montgomery and the UWF executives want to play these stupid games and keep me down, that’s fine. Because Ill get them back. Soon they will realize that I am the real deal and then it will be far too late. I will tear this damn company down if this shit continues. Im one of the absolute best in the UWF. I know I am. Give me my chance and I will beat every single wrestler they’ve got to offer. This isn’t some game. Im as serious as a heart attack. I walked out there and earned my shot at the heavyweight champion a couple months back when I pinned Jamie Shock for the three count. That following Friday, my wife totals her car and I cant make the show. I cant fight for the title I’ve always wanted to have. Ever since then, I’ve been the sixth man of the UWF. I got the skills to be the best, But I only get used every so often.” “ “ ” “ { He kicked the opposing chair of the table. } (He’d show them. He would teach the UWF a lesson they soon wouldn’t forget. Things are gonna change for Malcolm Kennedy, Just give them time to fall into place. Time to take shape. } “ “ ” ““Soon enough Im gonna get exactly what I want. Exactly what I DESERVE! I may need some help along the way, But I’ll just have to cross that bridge when I get there.” “ “ ” “ ( He wanted to start tearing the UWF down right away. But he knew he couldn’t just yet. Ruff Ryder needed to be there by his side. The onslaught would be pro-longed.) “ “ ” “ “Ill get to the top of the UWF one way or another. Ill stack the bodies high, Wade through the blood of my fallen opponents, and Ill pour everything I have into that ring to get where I deserve. I have got a thirst for glory, and that heavyweight championship is the only thing that can quench it. At SAS I will beat any man that finds himself walking down that aisle looking to prove something. I will do everything I can to win that match, and win that match I will.” “ “ ” “{ He stood up from the table and pulled the tie loose from his neck. He then threw off the coat and unbuttoned to top two buttons of his shirt. } ( He knows what he must do at SAS, and what he must do for everything after SAS. The time is at hand and Malcolm Kennedy will get recognized once again. The world title will be put around his waist and it will be put there very soon. }
