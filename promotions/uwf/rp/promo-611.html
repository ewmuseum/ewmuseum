UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #611

Irish Eyes Ain't Smilin'

11 Aug 2004

Tsunami Oni — A pub near the UWF Arena in Chicago

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When we last saw Tsunami Oni, he had just survived the Masked Monkey gauntlet and was awarded a new UWF contract! But there is no rest for the wicked awesome, for he is no only in the Regal Rumble, but has a rematch against Sean McClancy! Right now, though, Oni and Shiatsu are still in Chicago following SAS. They've decided to go celebrate and have headed to a nearby pub called Shenanigan's. This was several hours and even more drinks ago. Oni: I am da champeen, my frennnnsssss... an' I'll keep on fightin' 'til da end! Shiatsu: Ya ain't no champeen! Oni: Uh huh! Shiatsu: Nuh uh! Oni: Uh huh! Shiatsu: Nun uh! Oni: I'm champeen o' chmapeens, 'member? Shiatsu: Champeen at kissin' my butt! Oni: Dat too... The pub is crowded and many people are looking at Tsunami Oni and Shiatsu. Oni does stand out with his blue hair and devil horns and Shiatsu, well, she's smokin' hot. Plus they're really freakin' loud. Drunks. Oni: We goin' home, baby! Shiatsu: Ta San Frisco? Oni: No! Home! Japan! Shiatsu: Um, but I'm from Fresno... Oni: Whatevah.We're gonna go ta Japan an' I'm gonna win da Regal Rumble an' I'm gonna beat da poop outta Sean McCancy... Mancy... Ancy... DA IRISH BASTARD! Everybody at the bar turns to look at the very loud, very intoxicated Devil from the Deep Blue Sea. The bartender walks over to him and leans close. Bartender: You might want to watch yourself. This is an Irish pub. Oni: Not my fault! It's what he calls hisself! Bartender: Well, just watch yourself all the same. Oni: Yeesh, dat guy's a dick! Bartender: I'm still standing right here, sir. Oni: I knew dat! Dick! The bartender just shakes his head and walks away to help another patron. Tsunami Oni downs his drink and unsteadily gets off his stool. Oni: Gotta hit da porcelain! Shiatsu: Okey-dokey... Tsunami Oni stumbles his way through the crowded room, showing none of his legendary agility. He stumbles into something. Oni: Owwwwieeee! The Vivaldi of Violence looks down and see a red-headed midget glaring up at him. Oni: Holy sheet! It's a freakin' leprechaun! Midget: I ain't a leprechaun, ya daft bastard! Oni: You wouldn't be Lucky, would ya? Midget: See for yourself, mate! The midget unzips his pants and whips out a disproportionately large part of his anatomy. Oni: Whoah! Dere's ladies here! An' Shiatsu! The midget tucks it back away and zips back up. Midget: Now why don't you just get your drunk arse out of this pub? Oni: What're you gonna do? Slap me with yer lucky charms there? Midget: I was thinking more about your charms! With that, the midget gives Tsunami Oni an uppercut to the genitals. Oni groans and bends over. Shiatsu sees this from the bar. Shiatsu: Hey! No breakin' my toys! Running across the floor, Shiatsu tackles the midget. As she crashes to the floor on the wee man, her skirt flies up. Three guesses on who's not wearing panties... Shiatsu: Take dat and dat and dat! Shiatsu slaps away at the midget, who really isn't fighting back much. Hell, he's got a hot Asian chick going commando sitting on him and her drunken slaps don't exactly hurt. Shiatsu stops for a moment when she feels something poking her. She looks at the midget's crotch and sees the source. Shiatsu: What da hell? Whoever said dere's no snakes in Ireland never saw dis! Holy shillelagh! Just then, a large hand clamps down on Tsunami Oni's shoulder. The Devil turns and sees a very large red-headed man staring down at him. Large man: Get your lassie off my big brother! Oni: I don' got a collie! Large man: Your woman! Oni: Oh! Well, I really don' tink yer bruddah minds. Hell, I can see from here evertin's upright! The large man growls and and lifts Oni up by the throat with one hand. With the other he makes a fist. He pulls back, ready to pummel the already semi-conscious Vivaldi of Violence. Just then, Oni lets out a huge belch. Oni: BUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRRRP~!!! But this is no mere belch. This is a belch combining several hours of heavy drinking with the EVIL BLUE MIST~! The large man drops Oni and clutches at his face in pain! Large man: My eyes! My eyes! Midget: My little brother! My little brother! The midget shoves Shiatsu off of him and stands up quickly. But he had forgotten the state he was in and collapses back to the floor, hunched over in agony. Oni runs over and pulls Shiatsu up. Oni: C'mon, time ta make like babies an' head out! And with that our drunken duo stumbles out into the Chicago streets...
