Trailer Park Luchador #3

29 Oct 2005

Trailer Park Luchador

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“An inter-whatsa?” [Standing in the doorway to his mother’s trailer, his feet bare, smiley face sweatpants his only piece of clothing, and a newly cleaned magical luchador mask that allows him to flip-flop all over the known universe … is the Trailer Park Luchador.] “An intervention, ‘Luchador!” [The other players in tonight’s festivities … the Artists Formerly Known as the Masked Monkeys … or the Masked Security Force … or whatever they’re technically known as now. White Tiger has taken the lead, standing in the doorway and pointing an accusing finger. Pajaro de Presa stands nearby, trying to calm down an agitated Plucky the Cockfighting Duck. On the lawn, El Turtlé is eating a pink, plastic flamingo, while Crab Mask, Jr. and El Chupacabra try to look as inconspicuous as possible so no one will have to remember they exist.] TPL: “A whosa-whatsa!?” Tiger: “We’re staging an intervention! This has gone on long enough!” TPL: “Oh! Why didn’t you just say so? Come on in! You guys want Coca-Cola and candy? I have TONS of candy.” [As the Rudos With Attitude-o lead the Masked Army into the Luchador’s mobile home, we cut away.] -------- [Several minutes later, the Trailer Park Luchador is passing out Cokes and bite-size Kit-Kat bars to his old friends. The Rudos are sitting at the small table … the ‘Mask and Chupacabra behind them. El Turtlé is trying to use his massively oversized fingers to work the tiny remote control of the black-and-white television.] TPL: “Alright, dudes … what’s this about again.” [White Tiger points an accusing finger.] Tiger: “You’re evil!!!” TPL: “What!? No!!” [Pajaro de Presa shakes his head.] Presa: “No, amigo! It’s true! You’ve gone completely loco, and we’re here to set you to rights!” [Plucky, the Cockfighting Duck, flaps his wings frantically, quacking like a … err … mad duck.] Presa: “SEE!? Even Plucky knows you’re nutso!” [The Hero of the NRA gasps.] TPL: “But … but … but … !” El Turtlé: “Totally! You’re mean, and you hurt a baby seal, and … and … and it made El Turtlé cry, dude!” Presa: “Plus … this thing you have going on with Tigera is just ending up with us getting our butts kicked every week! She kicks so hard. It hurt my fanny, amigo.” Tiger: “You must be stopped, Chris! It’s for your own good … we don’t know what’s wrong with you, or why you’ve gone crazy …” Presa: “Lolo.” Tiger: “Whatever.” [Crab Mask, Jr., and El Chupacabra glare condemningly at their brethren.] Tiger: “We don’t know what’s wrong with you, but you’re got to be stopped … it’s for your own good!” TPL: “But … but … but … !” Presa: “We thought your momma raised you better than this. Tsk, tsk, tsk.” Tiger: “Speaking of which … where is your mom?” TPL: “Oh … uh …” [In the “master bedroom” of the TPL’s trailer …] [The massive blob that is Chris’ mother is sitting on the smashed remains of what used to be a chair. Her hands are still handcuffed behind her, and about five socks are jammed into her mouth, muffling her.] “Mphfjkjldkflajdl;jafljkaf!!! MPHSHAW!!!!!!!!!!!!” [… and so is the mother of the great Trailer Park Luchador.] TPL: “She’s around here somewhere?” [The ‘Luchador shakes his head frantically.] TPL: “PLUS! You guys have it all wrong! I’m not REALLY evil … I’m just pretending to be evil!” Presa: “Dude!” Tiger: “That makes … like … no sense.” El Turtlé: “Totally.” TPL: “No, no, no … I have it all worked out. See … I, like, never win matches. I was really, really sucking, and I was a …” [The Man with the Mullet glares at El Turtlé, the Jobber Killer.] TPL: “I mean … I … uh … needed something to do to get my career working! And he said turning into a bad guy would do it! So … see! I’m still your buddy … but I have to be evil on TV so people will care about me!” Presa: “Oh. You should have just said that in the first place.” Tiger: “I … wait! Who said turning into a bad guy would do it?” TPL: “That’s what I wanted to show you!” [The Luchador spins around, throws open the bread cabinet, and yanks out … the Teddy Bear!] TPL: “This is my Teddy Bear! He’s been guiding me on how to be evil on TV.” Tiger: “…” Presa: “…” El Turtlé: “… dude …” TPL: “What?” Presa: “Teddy Bears don’t talk, esé!” Plucky: “Quack.” Presa: “See! Plucky knows … inanimate objects can’t tell you how to further your wrestling career. That’s just silly.” [The Luchador shakes his head frantically.] TPL: “Tell them, Teddy! Tell them what you told me … !” [The Teddy Bear … sits in the Luchador’s arms, unmoving.] Plucky: “Quack.” Tiger: “Well … you’re completely lost your fucking mind.” El Turtlé: “Totally.” Tiger: “But at least you’re not evil, so I guess we’re cool.” TPL: “Kickass.” [The ‘Luchador frowns.] TPL: “Err … you guys are going to be there to help me with Tigera, right?” [The Monkeys trade uncomfortable glances.] TPL: “RIGHT?!?” Tiger: “See you at the show!” [And with that, the Masked Army grabs all of the ‘Luchador’s candy they can carry and file out.] [Suddenly, the Bear’s eyes light up, and he quietly whispers to his masked compatriot.] Teddy Bear: “Now!” [The Trailer Park Luchador nods quietly to the Teddy Bear, before turning around.] TPL: “Oh! Crab Mask!” [Crab Mask, Jr. spins around, smiling.] Crab: “Que … ? [The Trailer Park Luchador … BLASTS Crab Mask, Jr. over the head with the biggest bag of Reese’s Pieces ever. The ‘Mask drops like a sack of crap at the Mulleted Wonder’s bare feet.] TPL: “Muahahahahahahahaha!” [The Bear’s eyes glow … with evil!] Teddy Bear: “Yes! Now stash him away. The next stage of our plan has finally come into being … muahahahhahaha!’ TPL: “Muahahahahaha!” Teddy Bear: “Muahahahaha!” TPL: “Muahahahahaha!” Teddy Bear: “Muahahahaha!” TPL: “Muahahahaha—” Teddy Bear: “God damnit, just do it.” [The Luchador shrugs and begins dragging Crab Mask, Jr. by his little crab-shaped booties, beginning Stage 2.] [To Be Continued!] [Muahahahaha!]
