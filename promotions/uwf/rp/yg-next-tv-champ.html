(WNU) Next TV Champ

6 Nov 2001

Toxic Shock

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TOXIC SHOCK #1 "...Killer Dude you didn't deserve that title one bit, you were put in the ring with rookies..." Toxic Shock lost to the Plague on Sunday in a hardcore title match, but Toxic Shock did hurt the Plague, he chopped 1 inch of his Torso off, making his seriously bleed. Then someone out the audiance handed him a weapon and then after being beaten with that Toxic Shock recieved The Black Death which knocked him out cold like. The Plague was going to get with the Whiplash but luck beat him I'm afraid, anyway it was a good match for the state we're in. Also here's some good news, Toxic Shock is going one on one with Killer Dude for the Television Championship on Wednesday. Good news for the SHock as he will get a belt round his waist. Digital Mortality on the other hand is facing jobbers, on Wednesday he's facing Jack Union! I really hope he gets beat by Jack, that'll be so funny and will cripple the EoD [The Scene opens in a small shed type room, Toxic Shock is on the phone to someone, he hangs up the phone when he realises the camera is filming. Toxic Shock turns towards the camera...] Toxic Shock: Before I talk about trash (Killer Dude) I will start with a quick note. Plague, you are one hard son of a b****. I can't believe you were still standing after I sliced 1 whole inch of your torso with my lightsaber I recieved from some dodgy geezer dude. Plague keep in mind that I could of done more damage than what I did with that saber, you could be walking back with only one arm right now, but I had to defend myself, you did have a sledgehammer after all and if I never had the saber my skull would be cracked open, so sorry.. Digital Mortality, you're facing the best of the best, the on only known as Tox... Jack Union! Ha, if you loose to him Digimon you can loose to anybody. I admit the UWF isn't giving you a break. Not that you deserve one or anything as you really do suck. You can moan all you want that you lost to me even though you had the match won but at the end of the day.. I came out on top, I nailed you with a crucifix and pinned you on the ground. Now my advise is before you challenge Toxic Shock for another match you go back to your home, the dustbin on Brown Street, stay there a little while, then step out of your bin and bang your head against the wall, until you realise that you can't beat the Acid Bull. Because if you don't you'll get your ass kicked again and again and again, until I'll end your career and then you'll come to your senses too late and.. The next Pay Per View will rock the goldfish's bowl! Killer Dude -v- Toxic Shock, weapon filled cage match, if I win the EoD will be finished, if Killer Dude wins he can.. regain his title I suppose, because everyone knows I will beat that sorry dude and pin him in the middle of the canvas. Killer Dude, it seems that you have a problem with your speech, well infact you always have, the problem is when you speak crap 24/7. At the moment there is only one cure, the Whiplash! I'll knock your head so hard against Gods White Ring that you'll suddenly turn straight. Now if that ain't a gift of mine what is? Killer Dude you didn't deserve that title one bit, you were put in the ring with rookies. Python should of won that match easy. Unlike you Killer Dude I will never ever loose my title. Blade may have done 50 days, but I will double, no treble that! That's nothing to what I can do punk! Also I sent a message to Killer Dude and I want Sarah! If I don't get Sarah on Wednesday I'll end your career. I have plenty of battery left in my LightSaber I assure you! And I have been training with it, I love the damn thing, hahaha Oh well, I'll leave. I'll let other people have chances to send out promos now, it's just me me me all the time... [The Scene Fades as Toxic Shock picks up the phone and a number on the pad]
