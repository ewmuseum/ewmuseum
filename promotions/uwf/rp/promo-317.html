UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #317

Well, Dunn...

7 Apr 2004

Beezlebozo — Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Beelzebozo is exiting the gates of Hell’s Carnival. His face is a mask of determination and focus. Blaze’s voice: “Wait hon!” He stops, and turns as Pyretta Blaze appears behind the gates, dragging a huge duffel bag. She drops it and pants for breath before speaking. Blaze: “You forgot your bag!” Beelzebozo: “I won’t be needing it, dear.” Blaze: “You what? Are you out of your…hold on, bad choice of words…” Beelzebozo: “I won’t be assaulting Dunn before the match after all. I will be having, I hope, a good clean match with him. The way it should be.” Blaze: “What the hell’ve you been smoking??” Beelzebozo: “We came to a decision and decided to do things the old-fashioned way. At least, I think we did.” Blaze: “So I dragged this goddamn thing out here for nothing!?” She kicks the gate in frustration. “You could’ve told me sooner!” Beelzebozo: “I didn’t think it would come up.” Blaze: “So what brought on this sudden burst of nobility?” Beelzebozo: “Joint decision by the committee. We decided it’d be better to try and earn the respect of everyone than just make them fear or hate us. If it doesn’t work, hey, we can always bushwhack the next pretender to the throne.” Blaze: “Riiiiight…well, whatever makes you happy I guess.” Beelzebozo: “That’s a very good motto to live by.” Blaze: “Anyway, are we ready to go?” Beelzebozo: “Go on ahead. You have a phone on you? You can get the Quiet One to take the bag back for us.” Blaze: “Yeah, I brought my cell. But I hate calling him. You’re never sure he’s listening…the phone picks up, you talk, and then he hangs up.” Beelzebozo: “You expect him to chit-chat?” Blaze: “Of course not. But it’s still creepy.” Beelzebozo: “Well, do it anyway. I need to address my opponent one last time.” Blaze: “OK sweetie!” She smooches his cheek as she walks by. The clown briefly smiles, then returns his gaze to the camera. Beelzebozo: “So, in a matter of hours we shall finally come to our much-awaited match, Dunn. Hopefully we’ll be able to give the crowd a match for the ages, one that will be talked about for years to come. A match that will redefine wrestling as a pure athletic competition. At least, that’s what I hope we get. Knowing this place, any number of jackasses could run in and spoil the fun. But I guess we can only try. “Are you really ready for what you’re in for, I wonder? You’ve been very busy talking to the fans and reading your books, but have you really prepared yourself for our match? It’s not going to be a match like any other you’ve had, I assure you. I know you went through Mt. Gagazet, but that was against a wrestler who, while talented, was no match for me. I’m going to come at you like a wild elephant, ready and willing to stomp you into the dirt without a thought. You’re going to experience pain and suffering on a level you never thought possible, and if you’re not ready you will fall, screaming in agony and defeat. “I honestly hope you are. I want you to provide me with a worthy challenge, Joshua Dunn. I want you to take me to my limits, for I shall surely take you to yours. “I shall see you soon. I hope you don’t end up a waste of my time…or worse yet, a devious little snake like so many others here seem to be. “But no matter who you turn out to be, rest assured… “I will have the Last Laugh.” Blaze reappears, looking a bit dazed. Blaze: “What in the nine circles of hell was that all about!? You about had me puking with all that friendly, honorable, ‘may the best man win’ crap!” Beelzebozo: “I’m…not sure. I think I’ve spent too much time thinking about this match with Dunn. “Apparently his appeal to my sense of honor and fair play was more successful than any of us realized?...” Blaze: “Will it wear off soon? Beelzebozo: “I certainly freakin’ hope so! Maybe we should go kick some kids on the way to the show. Or beat hookers with bats. Or watch The Passion of the Christ—that’d get the blood flowin’, in more ways than one.” Blaze: “You’ve got some real problems, you know that?” Beelzebozo: “Why else do you love me?” Blaze: “Mmm…good point.” They embrace, and with that the two depart.
