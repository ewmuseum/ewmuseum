UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #744

Justice Fried

16 Oct 2004

Lyle Wile — Santos Brazil

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens in the Stylemeister's penthouse suite in a luxury Condo development on the beach in Santos, Brazil. Lyle 'in Style' Wile is laying in bed with a number of stunningly beautiful Brazilian beauties... The telephone rings... ring... ring... ring... The Stylemeister stirs.... ring... ring... ring... Lyle: 'SHIT!!!! Who's calling me at this hour????' ring... ring... ring... The Stylemeister untangles himself from the ladies in his bed, gets up, puts on his house coat and stretches... ring... ring... ring... Lyle: 'This better be good...' The Stylemeister reaches for the telephone and picks it up... Lyle: 'OI!! What the fuck do you want?' The Stylemeister walks out on to his bedroom balcony that overlooks the beautiful beach in Santos... It's 7am and already there are thousands of people at the beach. Lyle: 'Calm down El Baiano... speak slower... I can't understand a freaking word you are saying... No... No... Don't worry about Joshua Justice, he can't get your green card revoked... Yes you are going to be able to stay in the usa, little Joshie Justice is all smoke and mirrors, there ain't a god damned thing he can do about what happened last night at Live and Lame 4' The Stylemeister is joined out on the balcony by a couple of the absolutely stunning beauties that shared his bed the previous night. Lyle: 'Remember why we chose this Justice kid... Think El Baiano... Sure I know that he looks a little intimidating, but once you take away that red white and blue rag he wraps himself in... He's just another mark, another stepping stone on the way to your destiny El Baiano.' The Stylemeister grasps the fabulous ass (or bunda) of one of the beauties standing beside him and smiles... Lyle: 'Do you remember where I found you dude??? Living in that filthy run down shack in that stinking favela... Do you remember what you said to me when I brought your sister home after the filming of the 77th instalment of 'World of Ass'? Buddy... it was classic, no one had ever challenged The Stylemeister like you had... You were hungry, you wanted blood and you trained relentlessly until The Stylemeister told you that you were ready. Don't loose sight of why I sent you to the UWF.' The Stylemeister signals to one of the gatinhas to get him a drink... Lyle: 'The Stylemeister knows exactly what will happen now El Baiano... Little Joshie Justice will most likely do a little dance, sing a little song, and then whine relentlessly about being the one chosen as your initial stepping stone... He'll moan and complain like a little American Bitch about truth, justice and the Pathetic Puny Puke way. He'll wrap himself up in his little American Flag, say that you are evil and must be stopped... He'll go on and on and on... He'll evoke the name of God, and say God Bless America and no one else... He'll babble incoherently that America itself was attacked at Live and Lame 4... He'll proclaim that he's a war hero and the cousin of some fat, overweight, past her prime Hollywood Starlet that everyone has forgotten... And do you know what??? The fans of the UWF will eat it all up and support him because they all lack the intellectual ability to see just how pathetic Little Joshie Justice is...' The Stylemeister is handed his drink, takes a sip and continues without missing a beat... Lyle: 'And remember El Baiano... You are fighting for the moral good. You are lashing out at the personification of evil himself. Little Joshie Justice represents everything that is wrong with the United States of America... He is the Whore Mongering American Sex Tourist that travels to Rio de Janeiro twice a year to have his way with underage girls... He is the clean cut kid from Utah that comes to Sao Paulo to spread the word of Mormon and annoy the population with his 'Gee awh shucks' and Skippy peanut butter... He represents a people that cannot walk softly outside their own borders... He is your target El Baiano and you will destroy him!!!!!' The Stylemeister spits off his balcony and watches his spit travel through the air until it is out of sight... Lyle: 'Now... El Baiano I want you to start preparing to destroy Justice... and one more thing, don't call me... The Stylemeister will call you...' The Stylemeister hands the telephone to one of his Brazilian beauties... Lyle: 'He just needed a little pep talk...' Brazilian Beauty #24: 'Stylemeister lets go to the beach...' Lyle: 'In a little while... for now why don't you girls do a little nude sun tanning up here...' The scene fades...
