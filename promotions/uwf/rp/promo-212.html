UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #212

The Clash is on!

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins on the porch of Ruff Ryder's lakeside Oklahoma home. The cameraman knocks on the door but there is no answer. He knocks again and once again nothing. Just as he steps off the porch and is about to pack up his camera to leave he notices something fall off the roof. He looks up to see a leg dangling off the edge of the roof. As he steps back and points the camera upward we can see none other than the True Indian Superstar Ruff Ryder. This isn't the same cocky, sure of himself Ryder that we are used to. His clothes are torn and his hair is a mess. He just stares at the ground while his leg swings back and forth. The cameraman stays focused on him a few seconds before finally asking him. ) Cameraman: Mr. Ryder, are you ok? * Ryder stares at the ground as if he doesn't even know the man is there * Cameraman: They told me to come and record a promo for you...you do know that you have a match right? * Ryder slowly raises his eyes and looks at the cameraman who takes a couple steps backward * Cameraman: Are you shooting a promo today or not? Ryder: Why? Camera man: Why? What the hell do you mean why? Ryder: Why! WHY WHY WHY!! WHY ME!! WHAT THE HELL DID I EVER DO TO YOU!!! * The cameraman freaks as Ryder hurls himself off the roof and crashes to the ground. He lays in a heap not moving at all as the cameraman slowly approaches him * Cameraman: Are you ok? Dude what is your fuckin problem! * Ryder starts to twitch. His whole body goes into convulsions as if he's goin into some kind of seizure, but he stops suddenly and springs to his feet with a sadistic smile on his face * Ryder: Problem? I HAVE NO PROBLEM! You're my problem, Jamie Shock is my problem...THE ENTIRE WORLD IS MY DAMN PROBLEM!! What are you doin on my property boy? Cameraman: I just told you like 5 seconds ago that i'm here to record your promo for the UWF. Ryder: Then get to recording son! Cameraman: I am recording, but you aren't sayin anything about your match with Beezlebozo. Ryder: Screw him! I beat him a long time ago. He doesn't even deserve to wrestle me again...I'm the Cherokee Manilla. * The cameraman gives Ryder a confused look * Cameraman: What? Ryder The Cherokee Manilla that's me! Cameraman: What the hell? Manilla? Like the folder? Ryder: Yes! I am the folder of good and evil!! * Ryder falls straight backward onto his back and laughs hysterically, before rapidly getting back to his feet * Ryder: What's it to you? Cameraman: Dude you are screwed in the head I swear! Ryder: What do you care? Nobody cares anymore. Jamie Shock said he cared, but where is he now? He's nowhere!! He left on the first thing smoking and left me behind! I don't matter to him and you don't matter to him. Beezlebozo doesn't matter to me and nobody matters to the True Indian Superstar!! * Ryder clotheslines the cameraman who drops the camera, but it's still recording so it catches a sideways view of Ryder stomping the life out of him. Ryder then turns his head toward the camera with a crazed smile before walking over and grabbing it. He mounts it on the cameraman's car and then steps back to get into view * Ryder: This is Ruff Ryder with a hard hitting documentary entitled...Jamie Shock...where are you now? The Shock has disappeared leaving many to believe he was gang raped by a herd of elephants last Tuesday at the quickie-mart on eleventh and crenshaw. Our reporter is on the scene and we'll go to him after this commercial break. * Ryder stands in front of the camera with a blank stare for a few minutes before realizing there is no commercial break and he then goes into normal Ryder interview mode * Ryder: What the hell? Where did this camera come from? What's this fat gorilla looking bastard doin on my front lawn? What smells like ass? I must have blacked out again. That's been happening alot lately, but let me assure you that I know who and what i'm gonna be killing at The Clash and that is Beezlebozo. You stupid clown wannabe, you're gonna wish you were squirtin kids in the face with a toy flower at a birthday party when I get through with you. I'm gonna make you regret the day you were ever born. I done beat your punk ass once, and i'll be more than happy to do it again. I own you boy, and until you realize that fact i'm just gonna have to hand you beatin after beating and hopefully you'll die soon. You can't stand up to what I bring to the table. I am a better World Champion than you could ever be, and now that I don't have that jackass Jamie Shock holding me back i'm badder than ever. How dare that son of a bitch leave me behind like that. Who needs him! He can go do whatever he wants, because I stand alone now and if anyone's got a problem with that they can come tell me to my face. Bozo, you're a dead man if you show up to that match. You've had the title for a little while now, why not just hand it over. It'll make it that much easier and it'll avoid a painful ride in an ambulance for you. Then I can get on to business and whoopin whoever keeps turnin out the damn lights during my matches. If I find you, you're dead. Who knows, maybe it was you Bozo, and if it was you better give your soul to God, because your ass belongs to me. Get ready to feel a new kind of pain at The Clash. I'm takin somethin home that night, either my title....OR YOUR HEAD! ( Ryder starts to laugh as he looks down at his torn shirt that says Prime Alliance and has pictures of Ryder, Jamie Shock, and Malcom Kennedy before he starts to twitch and then he turns and sprints toward his house slamming into the door face first knocking him unconcious. He lays there bleeding as the scene ends. )
