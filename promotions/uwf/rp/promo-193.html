UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #193

Low road

date unknown

Ruff Ryder

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The scene begins inside of Ruff Ryder's dressing room following Supremacy. He is sitting indian style in the middle of the floor with broken glass scattered around him. A television is laying face down and smoking behind him, a couch is over turned to his right and just in front of him a blood stained mirror with a spiderweb break surrounding a fist sized hole is on the floor. Ryder has his head hung low as blood trickles off his knuckles. He slowly raises his head not even looking in the direction of the cameraman who has just come inside. ) Ryder: This is bullshit! * He grabs his bottle of water that was between his legs and hurls it across the room * Ryder: We weren't supposed to lose like that!! He wasn't supposed to leave me here alone! * The cameraman can be heard clearing his throat as if to let Ryder know of him being in the room. Ryder slowly looks his direction * Ryder: What the fuck do you want?! Cameraman: I uhh... Ryder: I don't wanna hear it! Get the fuck out of here right now. * The Cameraman starts to leave but Ryder's words stop him in his tracks * Ryder: Go on leave, just like everyone else. I mean I looked up to the guy the entire time I was in the UWF and he just up and walks the fuck out without so much as a goodbye or kiss my ass? He goes out and gets monkey slapped by Mike Montgomery and then just walks out the doors forever? What kind of fucked up dumbshit is that? Everything I ever believe is bullshit right now and I don't know what the hell to do about it. On top of that I got some asshole runnin around flippin lights off on me and screwing me out of matches. How am I supposed to take that? * He looks up at the cameraman as if he's waiting for a response, but before the cameraman can utter a word he cuts him off * Ryder: I'll tell you how, I ain't gonna take that shit. Fuck the Prime Alliance and fuck anyone who gets in my way from now on. It's my time from now on, and from here on out it's all about Ruff Ryder. My time is now....and it's a time to go...SOLO! ( Ryder has a demonic psychotic just snapped evil look in his eyes as he's lost his only friend and the stable he loved all in one night. Where will the Cherokee Thrilla go from here? Only time will tell. )
