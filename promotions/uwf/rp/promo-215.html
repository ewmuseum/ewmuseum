UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #215

Intelligent paranoia

11 Feb 2004

Beezlebozo — UWF headquarters/Hell's Carnival

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again, we open on…er, no, wait. We’re actually opening up on the corporate offices of the UWF, tall and gleaming in the harsh winter sunlight. The World Champion and his entourage are seen exiting the building. Beelzebozo can be seen gesticulating wildly, with Pyretta Blaze making soothing gestures. As they approach the camera, their voices become audible… Beelzebozo: “This is such utter crap! Who the hell do those bastards think they are?” Blaze: “The, um, owners of the company?” Beelzebozo: “I give a dead rat’s mouldy, decaying ass. How DARE they pull this kind of garbage with me! I’m their freakin’ World Champion! They think they can just waltz in and throw stipulations into matches willy-nilly, nice as you please!?” Blaze: “Technically they can.” Beelzebozo: “It’s a goddamn conspiracy, and we all know it! I’m not done with this, not by a long shot!” With that, the three walk past the camera. We fade to the enormous, seemingly eternal fire that burns in Hell’s Carnival. Blaze is dancing around in a gleeful frenzy, her voluptuous body barely contained within a red leather halter top and matching (VERY) short shorts. The Quiet One and Beelzebozo, wearing their traditional clothes but wearing matching sleeveless trench coats over them, are dragging mannequins dressed in business suits. The Daemonic Clown stands them one by one before the fire. Beelzebozo: “All right, you corporate sons of bitches, who wants to go first?” He stands expectantly. “What’s that? I can’t hear you, please speak up!” He puts his hand to his ear. “No explanations, eh? Your lives hang in the damn balance, and not one of you has the balls to speak? Don’t any of you want to tell me what you’re thinking? How sad.” He begins to quickly pace before the mannequins, his hands clasped tightly behind his back, his coat flapping behind him. “All right, I’LL tell you what you were thinking. You, until recently, had a deranged but fan-beloved World Champion, one Seymour Almasy. He carried the belt for a long time, and brought in a lot of fans and cash. Well, all must eventually fall, and fall he did…but not to an established superstar, one you could milk for every red cent you could bleed from him.” He suddenly stops, and turns to stare from one unmoving mannequin face to another. “No, instead your champion was dethroned by a newcomer, one that came out of nowhere and amazed the world with his ferocious wrestling skills. One you don’t know much about; one you no doubt dislike due to his being an insane clown along with being a professional wrestler. And most importantly, one you’re unsure of how to market to the sheep-like masses of fans that watch your programming week after week and attend your shows. “Needless to say, this caused you some consternation. How can we make money off of this guy? He seems quite dangerous, and is perhaps the most skillful wrestler we’ve seen in a long while. I mean, he claimed the World Title for himself within a month of appearing on the scene, a feat nearly unheard of in the wrestling world. Worse, he doesn’t give a damn about being ‘family-friendly’ or toeing the company line. He does what he wants, where he wants, and woe to anyone else who gets in his way.” His pacing beings anew. “So, what did you do? You gave a title shot to one Ruff Ryder. Granted, he’d earned it in one long night of tournament competition.” He snickers. “Never mind the fact that I had been replaced with an inferior wrestler due to illness…no offense, Quiet One.” The mime smiles, obviously not taking any. “Which, of course, means that had I been there I would have won that tournament. But, I wasn’t, and Ryder won. So now he’s got a title shot. It should’ve been against your cash cow, but instead you’ve got a mad bull on your hands. “You are afraid of what might happen come the PPV. You have seen just how dominant the current World Champ was against the previous one, and you know that there’s not a chance in hell of this upstart punk winning, and therefore securing your retirement funds. So, you start to think of possible stipulations you could add to the match. Perhaps no-DQ? No, this Beelzebozo character seems all too willing to wrestle in the hardcore style. That won’t do. This fact eliminates a lot of options—falls count anywhere, street fights, and so on.” He stops once more, his face contorting into a bitter sneer. “So you decide to hedge your bets, and ask THE CHALLENGER to come up with a stipulation!! Not the CHAMPION, who by all rights SHOULD be the one to come up with any possible stips, but the challenger, who can then craft the match to insure that he has more than one means of winning. “And sure enough, the spineless, craven little bastard comes up with a doozy. Should the match end by count-out or DQ on my part, the title changes hands and goes to the challenger!” The clown pauses, and nods to the Quiet One. The mime disappears offscreen, and the clown goes back to his pacing and ranting. “And there was much rejoicing in the corporate offices then, wasn’t there? Because now everything was set! If Ryder, by some miracle, EARNED the right to wear the belt, well, that’d be great! But there isn’t a chance of that, is there? Not with me holding the belt. So, now, with this stipulation added, the options suddenly open up. Perhaps a mysterious intruder will come to the ring and attack me from behind, dragging me outside and leaving me unconscious on the floor just long enough for me to be counted out. Perhaps Ryder himself, devious little bitch that he is, will sneak in a weapon, crack the clown over the head, and toss him out for the ten count. Maybe someone will run in…and then attack Ryder himself, making it look like I had something to do with it, and therefore I’m disqualified and stripped of the title. “Oh, it was all very slick, wasn’t it? The corporate whore and his suit-wearing pimps had come up with a very nice little trap for that nasty clown holding the belt, hadn’t they? “Of course, that doesn’t take into account one thing…” The Quiet One reappears, holding a large metal pipe in his hand. He walks up behind Beelzebozo and swiftly swings the pipe into the back of the clown’s head. Beelzebozo stumbles and falls to his knees. Pyretta Blaze begins counting. By the time she gets to three, the clown’s on his feet again. The mime hits him again, and this time follows up with several more shots to the back and head. The clown collapses to the ground, bleeding, and the mime steps back, his pipe stained with crimson. Blaze counts again. But before she can get further than four, the clown stirs…and by seven, he’s up again, dazed but steady. A sadistic grin crosses his face. “…and that is the resilience of their champion.” Blaze begins to laugh delightedly, and the mime tosses the pipe aside and stands back, a smile on his lips. The clown cackles, and looks directly into the camera. “See boys, it’s going to take a lot more than some lame sneak attack to lay me out long enough for a count-out loss. And I’ll have my friends backstage, just outside the ramp, making sure no one runs out to try and inferfere. So don’t try anything like that either. “And in case you try and blackmail me into leaving the ringside area and getting counted out, I’ll just let you know right now…my two pals here are under no impressions about what I’ll do if they get in trouble. They know damn well I’ll leave them to their fate until the match is over. Sure, once the bell rings, I’ll be on the hunt for those that dared raise a hand against them and I won’t rest until they’re six feet under. But until that bell sounds, I’m in it for the match and nothing else.” He turns back to the mannequins. “So, understand this right now, gents—there won’t be any shenanigans due to this stipulation. I will make sure of it. Oh, I suppose there’s a SLIGHT chance that Ryder merely asked for this rule because he’s scared crapless of me and what I could do to him, and wants to save his own worthless hide. But I seriously doubt it. He’s a weasel, that one, and he’s right at home with you people.” He turns to the camera once more. “Ryder, I know you’re out there. You listen, and listen good. You want my belt, you had better DAMN well earn the right to wear it. All right? You even THINK of sneaking in the back door…something I’m sure you’re good at, considering those movies you star in…and I’ll track you down and cut your filthy throat. And I don’t mean that figuratively. “Be a man, Ryder. Live up to the victory you had in that Clash of Champions tournament. Beat me clean, and be a champion people can look up to with respect. You know, like me.” His benign smile turns positively evil. “Oh, and you guys who run this federation? It’s time for an object lesson.” With a roar, Beelzebozo turns, grabs the nearest mannequin and hurls it into the flames as Blaze squeals and jumps up and down. Soon, all of the mannequins are roasting in the fire. The clown turns back and snarls. “See that? If I somehow lose this title match to Ryder fair and square, and he earns that belt, then fair play to him and I say no more about it. “BUT…if this stipulation turns out to be his ace in the hole, and he wins this match in any sort of underhanded hijinx…you know, acting like the miserable bitch he is instead of the man he SHOULD be…I swear to all that is unholy and hate-filled that you people in the offices of the UWF will end up in the fire with the dummies. IF you’re lucky. Do you understand me?” Spittle flies from his mouth as he begins to yell. “IF RYDER WINS BY ANYTHING OTHER THAN FAIR MEANS, YOU WILL ALL DIE! AND RYDER WILL BE THE LAST TO GO, AND I’LL CRUCIFY HIM FOR HIS SINS!! DO YOU HEAR ME?! DO YOU GODDAMN HEAR ME!?” With a shriek, he runs at the camera. His fist rushes up toward the lens, and the scene goes black an instant after his fist connects.
