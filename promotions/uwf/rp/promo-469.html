UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #469

Stable Wars in the Evil Empire?!

26 Jun 2004

Kevin Starr — Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stable Wars creeps closer and closer now as wrestlers from the Low Cards of WNU to the wrestlers of Main Event SAS prepare for what will be a test in teamwork and in endurance. Each day leading up to the event mainly consists of hours upon hours of physical training, watching tape, and in Kevin Starr's case, combing his hair in to perfection. What for? All of them, each and every man on the UWF roster... well, except for those nerds competing for the T.V. Title, they're all chasing a dream. A dream only few have been able to make reality. The dream, to become Stable Wars Champion. Stable Wars is the definition of trust. You need to trust your team mates that they will give it their 110% to win the Trophy. You need to trust them that they will have your back, that they will not screw you over and leave halfway through the tourney, that they will be there by your side, bloodied and beaten in the end when it matters most. If you can fully trust your team mates, then only good things can lie ahead. The Player's Club, however, are a little unstable when it comes to trust. Though most of the members get along, Kevin Starr and Joshua Dunn have been unable to forget about the past. In recent weeks we've seen the two get face to face in a heated battle of words, fortunately for the Player's Club chances for this tournament, they were able to control their tempers just in time before a fist may have been thrown. The heat between the two has obviously had an effect on The Player's Club as they have been on a downfall recently. Ezra lost the IC gold, Starr and Dunn lost the chance to bring Tag Team gold back to the Player's Club, Kevin Starr has also been pretty silent over these past 2 weeks. Hopefully, The Player's Club's luck will change now that Stable Wars is approaching and it appears that Kevin Starr's back up to tip top shape. “What the hell!!!” [Kevin Starr yells these 3 words inside his apartment complex in downtown Boston. He’s in his living room, which is actually picked up, washed down, and vacuumed for once, sitting back on his black leather recliner. He’s wearing his new fitted authentic Boston Red Sox hat, autographed by the great Red Sox player, Bronson Arroyo. The fitted hat goes perfectly well with his new David Ortiz Home Jersey, stitched and all. Down in front of him is the card for the UWF’s upcoming PPV, Stable Wars VI.] [Sitting next to Kevin Starr, is Mike Michael, laying comfortably on Kevin’s black leather couch, because the only furniture inside of Kevin’s house is made of black leather. He’s way too concentrated on the freshly opened bag of Smart Food laying in his lap then to reply to what Kevin said.] Kevin: “Mike, have you seen this Stable Wars card yet? It’s preposterous! [Kevin, obviously unhappy with something on the card, watches Mike munch down on a couple more of the pasty white popcorn.] Mike: *CRUNCH CRUNCH CRUNCH* “Of corefufhk ive hafcd” [Kevin hates it when people talk with a mouth full of food. HATES it. He finds it very appalling and unattractive. His eyes twitch like crazy whenever he sees someone attempt to speak English, only to have piles and piles of mushy, food come spewing out of the person’s mouth... when Kevin witnesses this, it’s not uncommon to find him throwing up in the bathroom for hours at the disgusting sight.] Kevin: “Chew your food rod Damnit! What the fuck that’s disgusting! Ugh! Eww!” [Mike quickly swallows down the crunched up Smart Food in his mouth in fear of the much bigger Kevin Starr. He must’ve forgotten the 3 Rules You Must Go By In Order to Be In Kevin’s House which is hung up on his front door, so there’s NO reason you should have forgotten them… #1 No Smelly People #2 No Ugly People #3 No TALKING WITH FOOD IN YOUR MOUTH!] Mike: *ahem* “Of course I have seen the card.” Kevin: “And did you notice anything… retarded about the card?” [Mike wonders what Kevin’s talking about, bringing his hand up to his chin in deep thought.] Mike: “No.” [Kevin’s eyebrows rise in disbelief.] Kevin: “Are you serious? How could you not notice this!” Mike: “Notice what?” Kevin: “The PPV, Stable Wars VI, maybe the third bi- no SECOND biggest event of the year is being held at Madison Square Garden! In New York!” [The half empty Smart Food bag comes flying off his lap, popcorn going everywhere as Mike quickly jumps up onto the couch.] Mike: “You mean… New York?” Kevin: “Yeah.” Mike: “Where the… *gulp* New York Yankees call home?” Kevin: “I think that’s the place, yup.” Mike: “What the hell were they thinking! NO! You can’t have the second best event of the year in a crummy place like that! Listen, Kevin don’t go, it’s not worth it.” [Mike looks deep into Kevin’s eyes, worried for his friends life.] Kevin: “I have to Mike… I have to. I mean, sure the citizens of New York, are fat, smelly, ugly, dismembered, and big nosed. And sure Donald Trump and George Steinbrenner live in New York, where they combine to make up the two ugliest people alive! And sure the New York Yankees play there. But that doesn’t mean that all people from New York are dirty, disgusting, and dirty. There’s still a few good souls left living there.” [Mike scratches his head, a good person from New York? Impossible!] Mike: “Like who?” Kevin: “Well there’s Conan O’Brien. The funniest guy in America. He’s from Framingham you know, and even though he’s lived in New York for a good part of his life, he hasn’t let those bastards brain wash him in to becoming one of those… those.. things!” Mike: “What do you mean by… “things”? Kevin: “…Yankee Fans…” Mike: *gasp!* [Kevin puts a hand on Mike’s shoulder, trying to comfort him.] Kevin: “Don’t worry Mike… he still manages to be a great Red Sox fan, despite living in the area where Yankee fans prowl the streets!” [Mike let’s out a big sigh in relief.] Mike: “Thank god.” Kevin: “And don’t worry about me Mike, I’ll make it out alive. Those animals won’t be able to convert me into one of their own.” Mike: “B-B-Bu-But why can’t they just have the event somewhere better like… Fenway Park?” Kevin: “Probably because George Steinbrenner gave them an unheard of amount of money to come to New York. Just like he did to Gary Sheffield, Kevin Brown, and A-Rod.” Mike: “Bastard.” Kevin: “But let’s forget about where Stable Wars is taking place, now is a time for partying and celebration! Stable Wars is in just 4 days! 4 days till I finally raise that trophy above my head. 4 days till I pick what ever title I want In the UWF to throw over my shoulder.” [The two’s mood changes quickly as the matter of Stable Wars is brought up.] Mike: “Are you sure you can do it though… The Player’s Club has been really sucky as of late and you guys have been on a losing streak.” Kevin: “Nonsense… those were just practice matches. I wasn’t even trying, just trying to… you know, try some new moves. But now there is no way I can lose. I swear if I were in a handicap match against the entire UWF roster, I’d still come out on top, just to show you how good I feel right now.” Mike: “I know but then there’s… Joshua Dunn.” Kevin: “…Yeah well… I’ve been able to set my anger aside the past 2 weeks and hopefully he won’t do anything stupid to ruin that streak going into Stable Wars. Like I said before Mike, as long as Joshy Woshy doesn’t get out of line, then The Player’s Club will win Stable Wars.” Mike: “So you think you can do it? Even with Joshua Dunn on your team… the man who kidnapped you nephew jus-“ [Kevin quickly interrupts Mike.] Kevin: “Damnit Mike will you stop being a negative Nancy! Seriously, all you do is bring up the bad parts about the past. Stop asking me those stupid questions [Doing his best Mike impression] “Kevin, do you think you can win” “I know but…” “Kevin are you absolutely sure you can win” “But what about so and so” SHUT UP MIKE AND STOP BEING A NEGATIVE NANCY! [Mike cowers in fear on the couch as Kevin screams at his direction. Kevin’s always had a loud voice. The volume of his voice can make even the strongest man break down in tears.] Kevin: “You know Mike, a great man once said “You better lose yourself… in the moment, you own it, you better never let if go. Cause you only have one shot. Do not miss your chance to blow. Cause opportunity comes once in a lifetime.” And I like to live my life by that quote, so Stable Wars VI, is as good as mine!” *scene fades*
