UWF (2001–07) Universal Promos — #438

The Silence Before the Storm

2 Jun 2004

Digital Mortality — Digital's Beach house

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The UWF is in shambles, a World Champion that is also the Comissioner and is bent of putting everyone else down while he gives himself title shots and he gives titles shots to those around himself. His precious Player's Club, Joshua Dunn, Ezra Kazee, Kevin Starr, and Nick Persia. Pathetitc little morons. They think that with the power of the Commish they will be able to take down Resurrection? They think they can take down the greatest stable of all time? The team of Moraless, Malcolm Kennedy, Bondage the Sex Fiend and Digital Mortality? Are they out of their minds? I think they are.] [That is why Digital Mortality had to take care of Mike Montgomery. He had a chance of taking the belt away from that corrupt animal but he didn't. The belt meant nothing to him, the destruction of the Player's Club meant everything, and what a way to destroy the Club than starting with it's leader. The other ones are nothing if they are leaderless, they can't stand in the ring with Resurrection. Almost all of them need Mike to make them better, because Mike is avarage at the highest. Guys like Joshua Dunn don't need Mike, they can be alone whenever they want and still be succesful, that is why I don't understand them.] [Now on Wednesday the Tag Champs Moraless and Digital Mortality take on the representatives of the Player's Club, Kevin Starr and Joshua Dunn. Digital has a history with both, and it will be one hell of a match, but you will see that in the end Digital and Moraless will be able to destroy Dunn and Starr, because when you put two great wrestlers with one great and the other below average, the two great ones always come out on top. That is what will happen on Wednesday night, then we move on to the greatest spectacle in UWF history, Stable Wars.] [This Stable Wars will be one of the best in it's history, and it's all thanks to Resurrection. Bondage, Kennedy, Moraless and Digital will tear the competition apart, it doesn't matter who it is. Seymour Almasy, Corporal Punishment, Nick Persia, Anarchy, it doesn't really matter because Digital Mortality will do his best to destroy all of these people in the ring so that he and Resurrection can hold the Stable Wars trophy and be a part of history being made. It doesn't matter who it is, Jay Phoenix could come back, Tsunami Oni, even Lew Spectre and Resurrection would still destroy their opposition.] (The scene opens with Digital and Burton sitting around in Digital's beach house drinking some beer. They are both wearing their usual attire and they both have beers on their hands. Digital has his tag title belt on his shoulder and his hair is tied up in the usual ponytail. The day is a little dark and there is rain sure to follow, and the wind is howling through the windows.) DM: Do you see the sky? We know something is coming up because of it's color, because it's not normal for it to be in a dark grey, and that is why people get ready. They make their plans for the day knowing that something is coming up this day. The rain will be too much for them to go to the beach and so on so forth. It's our nature to prepare ourselves for what is coming, it is something that everyone does, even the animals. Bears prepare for winter by setting up their little cave for hybernation. Chipmunks collect every food they can and store it to survive the winter. BB: What's going on with you? Going all philosophical on me now? (Digital keeps staring at the dark grey clouds for a moment more and the sits back on his chair and looks at Burton.) DM: It's not philosophy. It's fact. What I'm trying to show you, is that we must prepare when we see an change in climate, we don't know what might happen. It is the same with wrestling events. When a show as big as Stable Wars comes around, we must prepare ourselves for even the weirdest thing that could happen. The thing is, the Player's Club are so full of themselves that they actually believe they will win this without competition, they believe that we will just be there to stand around and watch them win it all. They won't win without us giving them the fight of their lives. They will have to take care of all of us to be able to survive in Stable Wars, and I am sure we won't be the only ones giving them the fight of their lives. I am sure the Anarchy stable will be looking to give it their all too, and all those other stable that are formed at the last minute, like the Curtain, or should I say cheap imitation of the Curtain. BB: I get what you are saying, but shouldn't you be concentrating on the match you have tonight for the Tag Team Titles against Joshua Dunn and Kevin Starr? DM: That match doesn't matter, they could take the belts for what I care. My sight is on the biggest prize, the Stable Wars trophy. That is the most important part of my career, the only thing I haven't accomplished, and I will finally do it this year. This year it will be the year of Digital as 2002 was. Not only the year of Digital, but the year of Resurrection. Moraless won the PTC Extreme Title, and I'm sure he will set his sights on the THC Title now. I am sure he can beat Nick Persia any day. I still don't know what title I will be going for, maybe I won't go for any title, because to me, the important thing is being called Stable Wars winner. BB: You don't plan to get any title shot? Are you mad? DM: Which one should I go for? I have held them all except THC, and I'm sure with Moraless going for it, he has it already won, plus I could get a title shot any time I want it. Like I said before, it is not about titles, but about the UWF. About destroying the filth that is the UWF now and resurrecting it from the ashes into the fed that once was. The great UWF, where people like Tsunami Oni, Jay Phoenix, Danny Jaye, Shawn Eclipse were champions, the age where I was no one and I had job day in and day out until I got the respect I deserved and won the IC Title. BB: And you lost it to the guy that recently came back, Corporal Punishment. DM: Yes indeed I did. I'm not ashamed of it though. I am not ashamed of losing to Corporal Punishment, I would be ashamed if I lost the title to Raid though. Like Ezra Kazee did... pathetic. Is it that Ezra that we must face at Stable Wars? I am getting my hopes up! BB: What about Jushua Dunn? DM: Everyone knows that I have the utmost respect for Joshua Dunn. I might not like who he hangs around with, but we were a team for so long that I respect the man, becaus ehe is a hard worker and because he takes no bullshit, and that is what the UWF needs. Too bad he has to walk around with Mike Montgomery... wait... Mike Montgomery can't walk now! (They both laugh.) DM: Mike, you thought you could take Resurrection down did ya? Well, you got what was coming to ya, you had been asking for an ass-kicking for so long now. I destroyed you Mike, like I have done all those times before, and like I could do any day of the week. I could have taken that little title for myself, but belts are not important to me, what was important to me was the destruction of the Player's Club, and it has already started. We will finish it at Stable Wars. BB: What about Kevin? He seems to think he can beat you tonight for the Tag belts. DM: Kevin Starr is a fool. He thinks he is this great thing, but he has never shown it, he has never showed us that he is worthy. At least not me. He has been dragged down and taken down by so many people, and he has been so loyal to Mike it's disgusting. I am not really scared of Kevin, I know he has some skills, but I think we can still take em. BB: Even with John having all those cuts and bruises? He lost a lot of blood on that Extreme Title Match. DM: He can do it, I am sure he can. He is not the Extreme Champ for nothing, he is not in Resurrection for nothing. If I didn't think he was capable of destroying his body only to come back the next week and destroying his body again for an ideal, I wouldn't be here tonight, and the greatest stable ever formed wouldn't exist. I respect the hell out of the man, and I am glad that he is my partner, and tonight, we will arrive at the arena and show the Player's Club what the strom is going to be like. This is the silence before the storm, I just hope the Club can prepare themselves for what is to come or there will be hell to pay. We are ready. The Resurrection is ready. Stable Wars, here we come. (Scene fades.)
